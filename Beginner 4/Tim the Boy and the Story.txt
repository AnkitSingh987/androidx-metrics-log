Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy shield. He loved his shield very much. One day, he saw a big spoiled fruit on the ground. He knew it was not good to eat. Tim saw his friend, Sam, walking near the fruit. Tim said, "Sam, don't touch the fruit! It's spoiled!" Sam did not listen. He picked up the fruit and threw it. The fruit broke and the juice started to spray. Tim used his shield to block the spray. He told Sam, "We should not play with spoiled things. It's not safe." Sam said, "You're right, Tim. I'm sorry." From that day on, they both learned to be careful and to listen to each other. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends when they try to keep you safe.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Tim was very happy to play outside. He saw a big, thick tree and wanted to play hide and seek with his mom. Tim ran to the tree and hid behind it. He peeked out from behind the tree to see if his mom could find him. Tim's mom started to look for him, but she could not find him right away. She looked behind the slide and under the bench, but Tim was not there. Tim giggled as he peeked out from behind the tree again. His mom heard the giggle and smiled. She knew where Tim was hiding now. Just then, a big plane flew in the sky above them. Tim and his mom looked up and saw the plane. They waved at the plane as it flew by. Tim's mom found him behind the thick tree and they both laughed. They continued to play hide and seek, and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a wide volcano. The volcano was very big and tall. It lived in a land far, far away. Many plants and trees grew near the volcano. They were happy and green. One day, a small seed fell near the volcano. The seed wanted to grow big and strong like the other plants. It tried very hard to grow, but it was tough. The other plants told the seed not to worry. They said that the volcano would help the seed grow. Then, something unexpected happened. The volcano started to shake and rumble. The plants and trees were scared. They thought the volcano would hurt them. But the volcano did not hurt them. Instead, it gave the seed a big, warm hug. The seed felt the love from the volcano and started to grow. The seed grew and grew. It became a big, strong tree. The other plants and trees were so happy. They thanked the volcano for helping the seed grow. The volcano smiled and said, "I will always be here to help you all grow." And from that day on, the wide volcano, the big tree, and all the plants and trees lived happily ever after. They grew together, and the volcano always took care of them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. In their backyard, there was a well. Tim liked to play near the well, but his mom always said, "Be careful, Tim! Don't spoil your clothes or fall in the well." One day, Tim saw a frog near the well. The frog looked serious. Tim asked the frog, "Why are you so serious?" The frog said, "I am sad because I lost my toy in the well. Can you help me find it?" Tim wanted to help the frog, but he remembered what his mom said about the well. Tim went to his mom and told her about the frog and its lost toy. His mom said, "Okay, Tim. I will help you and the frog, but you must promise to be very careful near the well." Tim promised to be careful. They went outside and used a long stick to get the toy from the well. The frog was very happy and thanked Tim and his mom. From that day on, Tim and the frog became good friends, and Tim always remembered to be careful near the well.
