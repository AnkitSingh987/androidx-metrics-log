Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside under the sun. One day, it was very hot, but Lily was glad because she could play in her pool. As Lily played in the pool, a grumpy frog hopped by. The frog did not like the hot sun. He would complain, "It's too hot! I don't like the sun!" But Lily just smiled and splashed in her pool. After a while, Lily felt sorry for the frog. She thought of a way to make him feel better. She took a big leaf and held it over the frog to give him some shade. The frog stopped complaining, and they both played happily under the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. She loved to play chess with her dad every Saturday morning. Ann would always pick a hidden spot to set up the chess pieces. This made it a fun game for Ann and her dad. One Saturday morning, Ann was getting frustrated because her dad had won the game three times in a row. Ann wanted to win, so she decided to hide one of the chess pieces to try to win the game. When her dad came back from getting breakfast for Ann, he said, "Ready for our game, Ann?" Ann looked away, feeling guilty. Her dad noticed the hidden piece and said, "I know you want to win Ann, but we should always forgive each other. I forgive you, Ann, now let's finish our game." Ann felt relieved and hugged her dad. She said, "Thank you, Dad. I forgive you too." The two of them swept up the pieces, ready to start their next game.

One day, a big lion with a loud roar lived in the forest. He liked to roar very much. All the animals were scared of him. The lion had a friend, a small bird. The bird could sing a clear note. The bird was not scared of the lion. One day, the lion and the bird had a talk. The lion said, "I can roar louder than you can sing." The bird said, "I can sing a clear note that everyone likes." The lion and the bird decided to have a contest to see who was the best. All the animals came to watch the contest. The lion roared very loud. The animals were scared. Then, the bird sang a clear note. The animals liked the bird's song. The lion was sad because the animals liked the bird's song more than his roar. The bird said to the lion, "Don't be sad. We can both be friends and make the forest a happy place." The lion agreed and they both became best friends.

One day, Tom and his mom went to the sea. They wanted to play in the water and have fun. Before they left, they had to pack. They put toys, food, and clothes in a big bag. Tom was very happy. At the sea, they played with the wet sand and water. They built a big sand house. But then, something unexpected happened. A small crab came out of the sand house. Tom was surprised and a little scared. Tom's mom said, "Don't be scared. The crab wants to play too!" So, Tom and the crab played together. They became friends. At the end of the day, they said goodbye to the crab and went home. Tom was very happy and couldn't wait to come back to the sea again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a modest girl named Lily. She had a big heart and loved to help others. One day, she saw a man who seemed sad. He sat on a bench near the park. Lily wanted to cheer him up. "Hi, I am Lily. Why are you sad?" she asked. The man looked at her and said, "I lost my job. I did work, but now I don't." Lily thought for a moment and said, "I can help you find work. Let's look together." They walked and talked. Soon, they found a shop. The shop needed help. The man said thank you to Lily. He was happy now. Lily felt good too. She knew that helping others is always a good thing to do.
