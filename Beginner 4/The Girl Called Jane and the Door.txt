Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Jane and the Door

Once upon a time there was a girl called Jane. She was only three years old and had a lot to learn. One day, Jane was in her garden playing when the doorbell rang loudly. Jane ran up to the door to answer it. When she opened the door, she saw a big grouchy man. "Hey there little one," he said in a rude voice. He started to stare at Jane's neck for a few seconds. Jane didn't know what to do and she felt scared. The man noticed how scared Jane was and said, "I'm sorry if I frightened you. I was just looking at your necklace. It is very beautiful." Jane's necklace was made of shells with every colour of the rainbow. The man smiled and told her, "Remember, being polite is always the right way to talk to others. No matter how rude they may act, it is important to be kind in return." Jane thanked the man and watched him leave. That day, Jane learnt an important lesson. She knew that no matter how rude people might be, being kind and polite was the best way to behave.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim and his sister, Sue. They loved to play together. One sunny day, they went outside to play with their ball. Tim threw the ball to Sue, but she was too tired to catch it. "Oh no!" Sue said. The ball hit a big stick and the stick broke. They were both very sad. "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to break it," Sue said. Tim hugged his sister and said, "It's okay, we can fix it." They found some tape and put the stick back together. Now, they were both happy and continued to play with their ball, being careful not to break anything again.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball, a bike and a kite. They have fun in the sun. One day, the sky is grey. It starts to rain. Tom and Sue run to the big tree. They sit under the tree and wait for the rain to stop. "Rain, rain, go away, come again another day," they sing. But the rain does not go away. It gets darker and darker. Tom and Sue are scared. "I don't like the dark," Sue says. "It is spooky. I want to go home." "Me too," Tom says. "But we have to be tough. We have to learn how to be brave in the dark. Maybe we can tell each other stories to make us feel better." "OK," Sue says. "What kind of stories?" "Happy stories," Tom says. "Stories about rainbows, and stars, and fairies, and dragons." "Dragons?" Sue says. "But dragons are scary." "Not all dragons," Tom says. "Some dragons are nice. They can fly and breathe fire and help people. I have a story about a nice dragon. Do you want to hear it?" "Yes, please," Sue says. Tom starts to tell Sue a story about a nice dragon. He makes the story funny and exciting. Sue listens and smiles. She forgets about the dark and the rain. She feels warm and safe with Tom. They tell each other more stories until the rain stops and the sun comes out. They see a rainbow in the sky. They are happy. They pick up their ball, their bike and their kite. They run to their moms and dads. They hug them and tell them about their stories. They are not scared of the dark anymore. They have learned how to be brave and how to have fun. They are tough. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful dog named Spot. Spot liked to be quiet and think about things. One day, Spot saw a big ball in the yard. He thought to himself, "I want to play with that ball." Spot went to his friend, a little bird named Tweet. Spot asked Tweet, "Do you want to play with the ball too?" Tweet said, "Yes, let's play together!" So, they both decided to play with the big ball. As they played, Spot had an idea. He thought, "If we push the ball near the tree, something fun might happen!" So, they pushed the ball near the tree. Suddenly, a squirrel jumped out and joined them in their game! They all laughed and played together, and it was a very fun day.
