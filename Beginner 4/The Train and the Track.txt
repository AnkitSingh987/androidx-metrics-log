Title: The Train and the Track

Once upon a time, there was a little train. The train loved to run on its track. One day, the train saw that the track was part. The train was very worried. It did not know what to do. The train saw a big tree near the track. With a strong push, the tree fell and joined the part track. The train was happy and went on its way. But, the train did not know that a big surprise was waiting for it. As the train went on, it saw a big cake on the track. The train was very surprised. The cake was for the train's birthday! All the train's friends were there. They all had a great time. The train learned that even when you are worried, good things can happen.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wealthy lady. She had a lot of pretty dishes. One day, she got a new dish. It was not like the others. It was plain and not pretty. The wealthy lady said, "I hate this dish. It is not pretty like my others." She put the plain dish in a box and forgot about it. The other pretty dishes were sad for the plain dish. One day, the wealthy lady had a big dinner. She needed one more dish. She found the plain dish in the box. She used it and everyone liked it. The plain dish was strong and did not break. The wealthy lady said, "I was wrong. This plain dish is good too." The moral of the story is: Do not judge something by how it looks.

Once upon a time, there was a rat. The rat was small and had thick fur. The rat lived in a big house with a girl named Lisa. One day, the rat wanted to play with Lisa. But he did not know how to talk to her. He tried and tried, but he could not manage to say anything. Then, he had an idea. He would make a sign with a stick and some mud. The rat wrote "Hi Lisa" in the mud with the stick. Lisa saw the sign and smiled. She said, "Hi, little rat! Do you want to play with me?" The rat nodded and they played all day. From that day on, Lisa and the rat were best friends. They managed to find new ways to talk and play together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy painter. He loved to paint all day. One day, while painting, he felt pain. He had a boo-boo on his hand. He was sad because he could not paint anymore. He went to his friend, a kind doctor. The doctor looked at the painter's boo-boo. "You suffer from a small cut," said the doctor. "I will help you feel better." The doctor put a band-aid on the painter's hand. The hairy painter was happy. He said, "Thank you, doctor!" The doctor smiled and said, "You're welcome! Now you can paint again." The painter went home and painted a big, beautiful picture for his friend, the doctor. They were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big wish. She wanted to lift a very heavy rock in her yard. Sue tried to lift the rock, but it was too heavy. She asked her mom for help, but her mom said, "That rock is too big for you." One day, Sue saw a scary bug under the rock. She screamed, "Mom, help! There's a scary bug!" Her mom came running and saw the bug. She was not scared. She said, "That's not a scary bug, Sue. That's a friendly bug!" Sue looked at the bug again. It was not so scary anymore. The bug said, "I can help you lift the rock if you help me find my family." Sue agreed. The bug helped Sue lift the heavy rock, and they found the bug's family. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the pool with her mom. The pool was big and had lots of water. Lucy was excited to swim and play in the water. Lucy saw a boy named Tim by the pool. He looked sad. Lucy asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim sighed and said, "I lost my original toy boat. I can't find it." Lucy wanted to help Tim find his boat. Lucy and Tim looked around the pool. They saw many toys, but none of them were Tim's original boat. Finally, Lucy found the boat under a chair. Tim was so happy! They played with the boat and had fun in the pool all day.
