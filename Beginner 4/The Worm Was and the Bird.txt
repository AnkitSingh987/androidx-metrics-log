Category: Beginner
Title: The Worm Was and the Bird

One day, a little worm was in the dirt. The worm was good at finding food. It liked to eat leaves and small things. The worm was very happy. A big bird saw the worm. The bird wanted to eat the worm. The bird flew down and tried to catch the worm. The worm saw the bird and was scared. A kind bug saw the worm was in trouble. The bug whispered to the worm, "Hide in the dirt!" The worm went deep into the dirt. The bird couldn't find the worm. The worm was safe and said "Thank you" to the bug. They became good friends.

Lila and Ben were bored. They had no toys to play with. All their toys were in boxes. Mom and Dad were moving to a new house. "Can we decorate something?" Lila asked. She liked to make things pretty. "Sure, but what?" Ben said. He looked around the room. He saw a lizard on the windowsill. It was green and brown. It had a long tail. "Look, a lizard!" Ben said. He ran to the window. He tried to catch the lizard. "Wait, Ben, don't scare it!" Lila said. She followed him. She wanted to see the lizard too. The lizard saw Ben and Lila. It was scared. It ran away. It hid behind a box. "Where did it go?" Ben said. He looked for the lizard. "Maybe it's in the box," Lila said. She opened the box. She found some crayons and stickers. "Look, we can decorate the box!" Lila said. She smiled. She took some crayons and stickers. "Good idea!" Ben said. He took some too. He helped Lila decorate the box. They made the box colorful and shiny. They drew flowers and stars and hearts. They put stickers of animals and letters and shapes. They were happy. They had fun. They forgot about the lizard. The lizard peeked out from behind the box. It saw the decorated box. It liked the box. It crawled into the box. It was cozy and warm. It felt safe. It fell asleep. Lila and Ben finished decorating the box. They admired their work. "Wow, we made a beautiful box!" Lila said. "Yeah, we did!" Ben said. They heard a soft snore. They looked inside the box. They saw the lizard. "Look, the lizard is in the box!" Lila said. "It likes our box!" Ben said. They were surprised. They were happy. They had a new friend. They decided to keep the lizard. They named it Lizzy. They showed Mom and Dad their decorated box and their lizard. Mom and Dad smiled. They said they could keep Lizzy if they took good care of her. Lila and Ben agreed. They said they would feed Lizzy and give her water and love. They hugged Lizzy. Lizzy licked them. They were not bored anymore. They had a new house, a new toy and a new pet. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a mysterious box. It had many shapes on it. One day, a little boy named Tim found it. He wanted to open it, but his mom said, "No, Tim. I don't permit you to open the box." Later, Tim met his friend, Sue. He showed her the box. Sue said, "Wow! What's inside?" Tim said, "I don't know. My mom didn't permit me to open it." Sue looked at the shapes and said, "Let's ask the wise old man. He knows many things." The wise old man looked at the box and said, "This box has a secret. If you open it, you must be ready for what's inside." Tim and Sue thought about it. They decided to wait until they were older and wiser. Years later, Tim and Sue remembered the mysterious box. They went to the wise old man and said, "We are ready now." The wise old man smiled and said, "You have learned patience and wisdom. You may open the box." Inside, they found a note that said, "You have learned the value of waiting for the right time. This is the greatest gift." And so, Tim and Sue learned that sometimes it's better to wait and be wise. The mysterious box taught them an important lesson.
