Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Orange

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play with her toys and color with her orange crayons. One day, Mia found a big piece of cardboard in her room. She was very happy and wanted to make something fun with it. Mia decided to make a big orange sun with her crayons on the cardboard. She drew a big circle and colored it all orange. Then, she showed her mom and dad her beautiful sun. They were very proud of her. Later, Mia's dad was cooking in the kitchen. He was not careful and something started to burn. Mia smelled the smoke and told her mom and dad. They quickly put out the fire and everyone was safe. Mia's orange sun on the cardboard was a little burnt, but she was happy that her family was okay.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a regular room with a big window. The window had a pretty curtain. The curtain was blue and had stars on it. Tim liked to look at the curtain every day. One day, Tim saw a tiny bug on the curtain. The bug was green and had wings. Tim said, "Hi, little bug! What are you doing on my curtain?" The bug looked at Tim and started to fly. Tim wanted to follow the bug and see where it went. Tim followed the bug around his room. The bug flew up, down, and all around. Tim laughed as he followed the bug. The bug landed on Tim's toy car. Tim said, "Do you want to play with me, little bug?" The bug nodded its head, and they played together all day long. And from that day on, Tim and the bug became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. He hopped in the grass, he hopped on the path, and he hopped in his home. One day, while Bob was hopping, he saw a big house with a tall chimney. The chimney was so brilliant that the sun made it shine. Bob thought it would be fun to hop near the chimney. Bob hopped closer to the chimney and started to hop around it. He hopped and hopped until he got tired. Then, he sat down near the brilliant chimney, happy with his day of hopping. And from that day on, Bob would always visit the brilliant chimney to hop and play.

One day, a little girl named Mia and her dog, Max, went on a boat. They wanted to find a new place to play. The sun was hot, and the water was cool. Mia saw an island far away. "Let's go there, Max!" she said. Max barked, and they went to the island. When they got to the island, they saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a big table with sweet treats. Mia and Max could not believe their eyes. "Wow, Max! Look at all the yummy food!" Mia said. Max wagged his tail and barked. Mia and Max entered the island and ate the sweet treats. They played and laughed all day. They found new friends like a bird and a cat. The island was their new favorite place. At the end of the day, they went back to their boat and waved goodbye to their new friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a hose in his yard. He was very happy and felt lucky. He played with the hose, making water come out. He made a big puddle and splashed in it. A small bird saw the water and flew down. The bird was very thirsty. It drank some water from the puddle. "Thank you, Tim!" said the bird. Tim felt good that he could help the bird. Just then, a big wind came and blew the hose away. Tim was sad. But the bird said, "Don't be sad, Tim. You were lucky to find the hose and help me. Now, you can find more things to make you happy." Tim learned that being kind to others can bring happiness.
