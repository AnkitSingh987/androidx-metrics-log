Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and the Ball

Jack had a new toy. It was a bright, shiny ball. It had a string on it. The string was clear and very long. Jack liked to keep the string all nice and neat. He kept it in a neat bundle in his pocket. He also kept it from getting tangled up. Every day he would take it out and play with it. He liked to practice throwing and catching it. He kept the string clear and tidy from day to day. He was very proud to have such a wonderful toy.

One day, Tom went for a jog in the park. He saw a shiny coin on the ground. He picked it up and felt happy. Tom wanted to show the coin to his friend, Sue. Tom ran to Sue's house and knocked on the door. Sue opened the door and Tom said, "Look, I found a coin!" Sue was anxious because she had lost her coin in the park. She asked, "Is that my coin?" Tom didn't know, so they both went back to the park. They looked around and found more coins. Sue was happy to find her coin and Tom was happy to help his friend. They both went home with big smiles on their faces.

Sam and Mia liked to play in the park. They saw many things there: birds, flowers, swings, slides. One day, they saw a frog near the pond. The frog was green and wet. It jumped and croaked. "Look, a frog!" Sam said. "Can we catch it?" "OK, but be careful," Mia said. "Frogs are slippery." They ran after the frog. The frog was fast. It hopped from one place to another. Sam and Mia tried to grab it, but they missed. They laughed and chased it more. The frog came to a big rock. It jumped on the rock. Sam and Mia followed it. They climbed on the rock too. The frog looked at them. It opened its mouth and said: "Ribbit! Leave me alone! I am a normal frog. I do not want to play with you. Go away!" Sam and Mia were surprised. They did not know frogs could talk. They felt scared and sorry. They said: "Sorry, frog. We did not mean to bother you. We just wanted to see you. You are very cool." The frog said: "Too late. You made me angry. Now you will pay. I have a magic power. I can stretch you like rubber. Watch this!" The frog stretched its tongue. It was very long and sticky. It wrapped around Sam and Mia. It pulled them close to the frog. The frog smiled and said: "Now you are mine. I will eat you for lunch. You are not cool. You are fools." Sam and Mia screamed. They tried to get free, but they could not. The frog's tongue was too strong. They cried and wished they had left the frog alone. They learned a hard lesson. They never played with frogs again. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a good boy named Tim. Tim had a shiny diamond that he found in the woods. He loved his diamond very much. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. They became friends and liked to play together. They would often discuss the pretty diamond and where it came from. They thought it was very special. As they talked about the diamond, they noticed a map hidden inside it. The map showed them the way to a big treasure chest. Tim and Sue followed the map and found the treasure. They were so happy to have found it together. From that day on, they shared the treasure and their friendship grew even stronger.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird named Bobo. Bobo was very special because he had many colors on his feathers. He lived in a big hedge with his friends. One day, Bobo and his friend, Dodo, were playing near the hedge. Dodo said, "Bobo, let's play hide and seek!" Bobo agreed and started to count. Dodo quickly hid in the hedge. While Bobo was looking for Dodo, he saw a big, juicy worm. Bobo knew he shouldn't eat it because it belonged to Mr. Owl. But Bobo couldn't resist and ate the worm. When Mr. Owl found out, he was very mad. He said, "Bobo, you ate my worm! I will punish you by not letting you play with your friends for two days." Bobo was sad, but he knew he had to learn from his mistake. After two days, Bobo promised never to take something that wasn't his again. He and Dodo played happily ever after, and Bobo always remembered to be a good friend.
