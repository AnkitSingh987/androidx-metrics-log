Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Boy

A boy and his mom go to a quiet restaurant. They sit at a big table. The boy is happy. He wants to get ice cream. The mom says, "We eat dinner first, then you get ice cream." They eat dinner and talk. The boy is very hungry. He eats all his food. The mom is happy. She says, "Good job! Now you can get ice cream." But then, something funny happens. A big dog comes into the restaurant. The dog wants ice cream too! The boy and the dog look at each other. The boy says, "Do you want ice cream?" The dog wags its tail. The boy and the dog get ice cream and share it. They are both happy and full.

Once there was a little girl named Anna. She had a weird glove. The glove had many colors. She liked to wear it all the time. One day, she went to play in the park with her friends. In the park, Anna saw a boy named Tom. Tom looked at her glove and said, "That's a weird glove." Anna felt sad. She started to wonder if her glove was too weird. But then, she remembered that her grandma gave her the glove. It was special to her. Anna said to Tom, "My glove may be weird, but it is special to me." Tom thought about it and said, "You're right. I'm sorry for making you sad." They played together and had fun. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and not judge them by how they look.

Mummy was bored one day, so she decided to do something creative. She decided to explain motion. Mummy smiled at her three year old daughter. "Listen carefully and I will explain motion," said Mummy. The little girl nodded her head and listened intently. Mummy held up a ball and moved it slowly through the air. "Look at the ball. I can move it with my hand. That's motion. Do you understand what I mean, darling?" The little girl thought for a moment and then smiled. "Yes, I understand. Motion is when something moves!" she said. Mummy laughed and gave her daughter a hug. "That's very good! You are so creative!" She said. The little girl beamed with pride and hugged mummy back. And so, mummy had successfully explained motion to her daughter!

Once upon a time, there was a big car. It was very organized. The car had a seat for a boy and a seat for a girl. The boy and girl liked to go on rides in the car. One day, the car went zoom down the road. The boy and girl laughed as they went fast. They saw a big tree and a cute dog. The dog ran after the car and barked. At the end of the day, the car stopped. The boy and girl got out of their seats. They said "thank you" to the car for the fun ride. They went inside their house and had a good night's sleep.

Once upon a time, a chubby cat named Tom wandered around the forest. He was looking for his friend, a little bird named Sue. Tom was sad because he could not find Sue anywhere. In the forest, Tom met a skeleton. The skeleton said, "I know where Sue is. She is in a big tree. But, you must play with me first." Tom did not like the skeleton, but he wanted to find Sue. Tom and the skeleton played a game. Tom won the game and the skeleton showed him the big tree. Tom found Sue and they were happy. They said goodbye to the skeleton and went home together.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. They enjoy the swings, the slide and the sandbox. They also like to look for bugs and flowers. One day, they see a big boot in the grass. It is brown and dirty. They wonder who left it there. They go closer to look at it. "Maybe there is something inside," Tom says. He puts his hand in the boot. He feels something soft and wet. He pulls it out. It is a rat! The rat is angry. It bites Tom's hand. Tom screams. He drops the rat. The rat runs away. Sue is fearful. She sees blood on Tom's hand. She cries. She runs to find help. Tom is hurt. He is scared. He wishes he never touched the boot. He thinks it is a bad day. He hopes he will be okay.
