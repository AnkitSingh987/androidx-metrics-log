Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Mia and the Hill

Tim and Mia were best friends. They liked to play with their bicycles in the park. One day, they saw a big hill. Tim wanted to go down the hill very fast. Mia was scared. "Come on, Mia, it will be fun!" Tim said. "Don't be a baby!" Mia did not want to be a baby. She wanted to be brave like Tim. She followed him to the top of the hill. Tim smiled and said, "Ready, set, go!" He pushed his bicycle and zoomed down the hill. Mia pushed her bicycle too, but she was not ready. She felt the wind in her face and the ground under her wheels. She felt dizzy. She tried to stop, but she could not. She lost her balance and fell off her bicycle. She scraped her knee and cried. Tim heard her cry and stopped. He ran back to her and saw her on the ground. He felt sorry for her. He helped her get up and hugged her. "I'm sorry, Mia, I should not have made you go down the hill. It was too hard for you. You should have remained at the bottom and waited for me. Are you okay?" He asked. Mia nodded and wiped her tears. She hugged him back and said, "It's okay, Tim, I forgive you. You are still my best friend. But next time, let's play something else, okay?" Tim agreed and said, "Okay, let's play something else. How about we go to the swings?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, let's go to the swings. They are fun and not dizzy." They took their bicycles and walked to the swings. They learned to be careful and respect each other's feelings. They had a good time and remained best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bobo. Bobo lived in a forest near a small river. One day, Bobo felt very hot, so he went to the river to find a cooler place to rest. As Bobo walked near the river, he saw a small bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was flying near a dangerous cliff. Bobo knew that the cliff was not safe, so he said, "Chirpy, don't go near the cliff. It is dangerous!" Chirpy listened to Bobo's order and flew away from the cliff. They both found a cooler spot under a big tree. They sat down and talked. Bobo and Chirpy became good friends. They learned that it is important to listen to others and stay away from dangerous places.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He liked to cook. Tim was very serious when he cooked. One day, he wanted to make a big cake. He put all the things in a big bowl. He mixed and mixed. Tim went to find his big boot. He wanted to use the boot to make a cake shape. He put the cake mix in the boot. He put the boot in the oven. The cake started to grow and grow. Then, something funny happened. The cake in the boot came to life! It jumped out of the oven. It was a big, happy cake. Tim was not serious anymore. He laughed and played with his new cake friend. They had so much fun together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy got a mysterious box. She did not know who sent it. She was very excited to open the box. She thought, "What is inside?" When Lucy opened the box, she found a pretty sweater. It was red and blue with a big star on it. She liked it very much. She put on the sweater and showed it to her mom and dad. They liked it too. Lucy wore the sweater every day. She played with her friends and they liked her sweater too. Lucy was happy to receive the mysterious sweater. She felt special and loved.

Once upon a time, there was a thief named Tom. Tom liked to take things that did not belong to him. One day, he saw a thick book in a house. He wanted the book, so he went inside the house to take it. When Tom got the book, he saw a big toy next to it. He thought the toy was very fun, so he decided to replace the book with the toy. He took the toy and left the book in the house. Later, Tom found out that the toy was magic! The toy could talk and it told Tom that it did not like being a thief. The magic toy helped Tom change his ways and they became good friends. In the end, Tom decided to give back all the things he took and never be a thief again.
