Category: Beginner
Title: Jenny the Girl and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jenny. She was three years old and loved to go on adventures. Today, she decided to explore the dark forest right by her house. She was a bit scared but really excited too. Jenny looked all over the forest, finding interesting things like tall trees and bright flowers. Then, she heard something small and loud coming from a nearby pond. Jenny sneaked closer and saw a lot of small shrimp jumping around in the water. She was amazed and wanted to catch one. But it was getting late and Jenny knew she had to go home soon. So she decided to come back another day and try to catch a shrimp. She said goodbye to the pond and began the walk home. Before Jenny knew it, she was out of the dark forest and back at her house. It had been such a fun adventure that day, Jenny couldn't wait to come back soon and try catching a shrimp.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Mark. Mark loved to play in the big field near his home. One day, Mark found some corn in the field. He thought it was a fun toy to play with. Mark played with the corn all day. He would throw it up in the air and catch it. He would run around the field with the corn in his mouth. Mark was very happy with his new toy. At the end of the day, Mark took the corn home. He showed it to his family and they all thought it was a great toy too. They all played with the corn together and had a lot of fun. And from that day on, Mark and his family would always play with the corn in the big field.

A long time ago, a cat and a dog lived in a big house. They liked to play all day. One day, they were playing with a red ball in the yard. "Let's rest," said the cat, "I'm tired." The dog nodded, and they lay down under a big tree. They closed their eyes and went to sleep. While they were sleeping, a small bird came and took the red ball. The bird flew far away with the ball. When the cat and the dog woke up, they saw that the ball was gone. "Where is our ball?" asked the dog. They looked everywhere, but they could not find it. They were very sad. Then, they saw the small bird flying back with the ball. The bird dropped the ball in front of them. The cat and the dog were very happy. They played with the red ball again and thanked the small bird. From that day on, they all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth stone. The stone lived in a park with many friends. The stone's best friend was a little dog named Spot. One day, Spot found a toy that looked like a stick. It was a laser, but Spot did not know that. Spot picked up the laser with his mouth and started to play with it. He liked to bite the laser because it was fun. As Spot played and bit the laser, it made pretty lights. The smooth stone and Spot's other friends saw the lights and came to play too. They all had a great time playing with the laser and the smooth stone. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a funny cat named Tom went to a lake. He saw a row of ducks swimming. He wanted to swim with them. Tom called out to the ducks, "Can you teach me how to swim?" The ducks laughed and said, "Yes, we can teach you!" So, Tom tried to swim with the ducks. They showed him how to kick his legs and move his arms. But Tom was not very good at it. As Tom tried to swim, a big fish jumped out of the water. The fish said, "I can help you swim better!" The fish held Tom's tail and pulled him through the water. Tom was so happy and surprised! He swam with the ducks and the fish all day.
