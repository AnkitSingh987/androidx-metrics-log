Category: Beginner
Title: The Nosy Bird and the Rock

One day, a nosy little bird went to the river. The bird saw a big crocodile. The crocodile was sleeping. The bird wanted to play with the crocodile. The bird went close to the crocodile and said, "Hello, Mr. Crocodile! Let's play!" The bird disturbed the crocodile. The crocodile woke up. The crocodile was not happy. The crocodile said, "Go away, little bird! I don't want to play. I want to sleep." The bird was sad, but it flew away to find a new friend. The bird found a big rock. The bird said, "Hello, Mr. Rock! Let's play!" But the rock did not say anything. The bird was confused. Then, the rock moved! The rock was not a rock. It was the crocodile! The crocodile smiled and said, "I am sorry, little bird. I was not nice before. Let's play together now." The bird was happy and they played all day.

One day, a jet was flying high in the sky. The jet loved to go fast and make big loops. The jet had a friend named Bird. Bird loved to fly with the jet and sing happy songs. One day, while they were flying, Bird said, "Jet, let's try a new trick!" Jet agreed and they tried to do a spin. But, as they spun, Jet started to slip. Bird saw Jet slip and said, "Oh no! Be careful, Jet!" Jet tried to stop slipping, but it was too late. Jet fell down and landed on the ground with a big thud. "Ouch! That hurt!" cried Jet. Bird flew down to help Jet. Together, they found a way to get Jet back up in the sky. Jet was very thankful for Bird's help and they continued to fly and have fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was nervous because he wanted to make his mom a special lunch, but he did not know how to cook. He found a menu with yummy food on it and wanted to make something from it. Tim tried to make a sandwich but he struggled. He asked his big sister, Sue, for help. "Sue, can you help me make a sandwich?" Sue said, "Sure, Tim. I will help you." Together, they made a yummy sandwich for their mom. Mom was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tim and Sue! You worked together and made a great lunch!" Tim and Sue learned that when they work together, they can do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to taste new foods. One day, she saw a big cake with a new style. It had pink and blue colors on it. She was very excited to taste it. Sue took a big bite of the cake. But, she did not like the taste. She felt a little ashamed because she did not want to hurt the feelings of the person who made the cake. So, she did not say anything about the taste. Later, Sue's mom saw the cake and tasted it too. She also did not like the taste. Sue's mom told the person who made the cake. They all laughed and tried to make a new cake with a different style. This time, the cake tasted very good and everyone was happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his friend Sue went to the beach to play. They saw a lot of sand and thought it was a good place to build a big sandcastle. They started to build the castle with their hands and small shovels. They were very happy. Tim said, "Sue, let's add more sand to make the castle taller!" Sue agreed and they both added more sand to their castle. They worked together and made the castle very big and strong. As they finished their sandcastle, a nice man walked by and said, "Wow, that's a very good sandcastle you made!" Tim and Sue smiled and thanked the man. They were very proud of their work and had a fun day at the beach.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a snake in his yard. The snake was green and long. Tim was scared, but his mom said, "Don't worry, it's a harmless snake." Tim and the snake became friends. They played together every day. The snake would attach itself to a tree and Tim would try to catch it. They had a lot of fun. One day, the snake said, "Tim, I have to go to my snake family now." Tim was sad, but he knew the snake had to go. They said goodbye and the snake left. Tim knew he would miss his harmless snake friend.
