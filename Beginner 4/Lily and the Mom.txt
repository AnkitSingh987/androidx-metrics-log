Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Mom

Lily wanted to play with her new doll, but she had to take a bath first. She did not like baths. They made her wet and cold. She tried to run away, but Mom caught her and put her in the tub. Lily was frustrated. She cried and splashed and made a mess. Mom washed Lily's hair and body with soap and water. She said, "You have to be clean, Lily. It's good for you." Lily did not care. She wanted to get out. She tried to grab the towel, but it was too far. She stretched and reached, but she could not gain it. She was more frustrated. She screamed and kicked and made more mess. Mom was not happy. She said, "Stop it, Lily. You are being naughty. You have to calm down." She took the towel and wrapped Lily in it. She lifted her out of the tub and dried her off. She said, "There, you are done. Now you can play with your doll." Lily was still frustrated. She did not want the towel. She wanted to be free. She wriggled and squirmed and made the towel fall. Mom sighed. She said, "Lily, you are making this hard. You have to cooperate. You have to wear clothes." She picked up the towel and Lily and took them to the bedroom. She put Lily on the bed and tried to dress her. Lily did not want clothes. She wanted to play. She pushed and pulled and made the clothes fall. Mom was tired. She said, "Lily, you are being silly. You have to listen. You have to be nice." She hugged Lily and kissed her. She said, "I love you, Lily. But you have to behave. You have to follow the rules." Lily looked at Mom. She saw that Mom was sad. She felt sorry. She said, "I love you, Mom. I'm sorry. I'll be good." She smiled and hugged Mom back. She said, "Can I play with my doll now?" Mom smiled and nodded. She said, "Yes, you can. But first, let me put on your clothes." Lily agreed. She let Mom put on her clothes. She was not frustrated anymore. She was happy. She said, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best." Mom said, "You are welcome, Lily. You are the best, too." They hugged and kissed again. Then they went to the living room, where Lily played with her doll and Mom watched her with love.

One day, a little girl named Sue was very hungry. She knew it was time for lunch. Sue looked and looked, but she could not find her lunch. She started to feel sad. Sue's big sister, Jane, saw her and wanted to help. Jane said, "Let's look together." They looked in the kitchen, but it was not there. They looked in the living room, but it was not there. Finally, they looked in the bedroom. There, under Sue's bed, Jane found a box. She opened the box and revealed Sue's lunch. Sue was so happy to see her lunch. She thanked Jane for helping her. They both sat down and ate their lunch together. Sue felt calm and happy again. The two sisters enjoyed their time and promised to help each other always.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, important job to do. Her mom asked her to wash the dishes and put them in the cabinet. Lily was very excited to help. Lily started to wash the dishes. She washed a plate, a cup, and a spoon. Then, she opened the cabinet to put them away. But when she opened the cabinet, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse jumped out and ran across the room! Lily was surprised, but she knew what to do. She put the dishes away in the cabinet and closed the door. Then, she went to find her mom to tell her about the mouse. Her mom thanked her for doing the important job and for telling her about the mouse. They both laughed and went to catch the little mouse together.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little fish named Fin. Fin loved to play with his friends in the sea. They would swim and dive all day long. One day, Fin found a pair of scissors on the sea floor. He did not know what they were, but he thought they looked fun. He showed them to his friends, and they all wanted to play with the scissors too. Fin's mom saw them playing with the scissors and told them to be careful. She said that scissors could be sharp and hurt them. Fin and his friends listened to his mom and put the scissors back where they found them. They went back to diving and swimming, happy and safe.
