Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He lived in a big, clean ocean. Fin loved to swim and play with his friends all day long. One day, Fin saw a sad crab named Carl. Fin swam up to Carl and asked, "Why are you sad?" Carl said, "I lost my way home and I miss my family." Fin wanted to comfort Carl, so he said, "Don't worry, I will help you find your home." Fin and Carl swam together, asking other ocean friends for help. They met a wise old turtle who knew where Carl's home was. The turtle showed them the way, and soon, Carl was back with his family. Carl was so happy and thanked Fin for his help. From that day on, Fin and Carl became the best of friends, and they played together in the clean ocean every day.

Once there was a little boy named Jamie. Jamie was very adventurous and he loved to explore. One day, Jamie was playing in his backyard when he found a match. It was a special match and Jamie thought it looked very shiny. Jamie wanted to pay for the match. He didn't have any money, so he offered to do some errands like cleaning his room and helping with the dishes. Jamie's mom thought it was a great idea, so she agreed to help him pay for the match. Jamie was so happy that he had been able to pay for the match. It made Jamie feel even more adventurous, and now he had a special item of his own that he had paid for. He put the match in a special safe place and would look at it often. He was so proud of himself that he had been able to pay for the match.

One day, a polite cat named Tom wanted to succeed in finding a big red switch. The big red switch could turn on a light that would make everyone happy. Tom asked his friend, a dog named Max, to help him. "Max, can you help me find the big red switch?" Tom asked. "Yes, Tom! I will help you!" Max said with a big smile. They looked high and low for the switch, but they could not find it. They were very sad. Just when they were about to give up, a little bird flew down and said, "I know where the big red switch is!" Tom and Max were so happy. They followed the bird and found the switch. They turned on the light, and everyone was happy. The moral of the story is that asking for help can lead to success, and being polite makes friends who can help you.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The little bug liked to crawl. He crawled on the ground and on the walls. He liked to crawl fast. One day, the little bug went outside. It was very cold. The little bug crawled on a big rock. He wanted to get warm. He crawled fast to get warm faster. But the cold wind blew, and it made the little bug colder. He wished he could find a warm place to hide. The little bug saw a big, warm house. He crawled fast to the house. He wanted to go inside, but the door was closed. The little bug was very sad. He crawled fast around the house, but he could not find a way in. The cold wind blew, and the little bug was colder and colder. The little bug could not find a warm place, and the cold wind made him too cold to crawl. The little bug did not have a happy ending.

One day, a big cat named Tim went to play outside. The sky was dark, but Tim did not care. He liked to jump and run in the grass. He saw a spicy red pepper on the ground and thought it would be fun to eat it. Tim took a bite of the spicy red pepper. His mouth felt very hot! He ran to find water to drink. As he drank, he heard a loud thunder noise. Tim was scared and wanted to go home. Just as Tim was about to run home, he saw the thunder was not from the sky, but from a big drum. A little girl was playing the drum and having fun. Tim was not scared anymore. He went to play with the little girl, and they had a great day together.
