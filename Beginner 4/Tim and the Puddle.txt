Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the rain. One rainy day, Tim saw a big puddle. The puddle was very wet. Tim believed he could jump over it. Tim backed up to get ready. He ran fast and jumped high. His motion was like a frog. But Tim's foot went into the wet puddle. Splash! Tim laughed and tried again. Tim practiced his motion many times. Each time, he got better and better. Finally, Tim jumped over the wet puddle. He was so happy. Tim believed he could do it, and he did!

Once upon a time, in a nice jungle, there lived a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to play and jump from tree to tree with his friends. One day, Momo saw a big, red fruit on a high branch. He wanted to taste it but he was scared. Momo asked his friend, Lulu the bird, "Should I eat the fruit?" Lulu said, "I don't know. It may not be good." But Momo did not listen. He climbed up the tree and ate the fruit. It tasted very bad! Momo felt sick and said, "I regret eating that fruit." His friends helped him feel better. Momo learned that he should listen to his friends and not eat things he does not know. From that day, Momo and his friends played happily in the nice jungle.

One day, a little frog named Tim saw a big purple ball. He wanted to jump on it to see how high he could go. Tim asked his friend, Sam the snail, "Can I jump on the purple ball?" Sam said, "I don't know, but I saw a message on the ball." Tim and Sam went to look at the message. The message said, "Jump on me and you will go high." Tim was so happy. He jumped on the purple ball, and it made him go very high. Sam watched from the ground, and he was happy for his friend. When Tim came back down, they both laughed and played with the purple ball all day long.

Sara and Ben like to play with paints. They make pictures of flowers, trees, and animals. They have fun mixing colors and making shapes. But sometimes they make a mess. Paint gets on their hands, faces, and clothes. Paint also gets on the bench where they sit and on the floor. "Uh-oh," Sara says. "Mom will be angry. We have to clean up." Ben nods. He looks for a cloth to wipe the paint. But he can't find one. All the cloths are dirty or wet. "What can we do?" he asks. Sara thinks. She is clever. She has an idea. "Look," she says. "We can use these." She points to some paper towels on the shelf. "They are soft and dry. We can wipe the paint with them." Ben smiles. He thinks Sara is clever too. He takes some paper towels and helps Sara. They wipe the paint from the bench, the floor, and themselves. They throw the dirty paper towels in the trash. They finish just in time. Mom comes in and sees them. "Wow," she says. "You made such beautiful pictures. And you cleaned up so well. You are very clever and responsible." Sara and Ben beam. They hug Mom and show her their pictures. They are proud of their work and their cleverness.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to gather rocks and sticks in his yard. One day, he found a big, yummy steak on the ground. He was very proud of his find. Tim showed the steak to his friend, Sam. "Look what I found!" Tim said. Sam wanted the steak too. "I want it!" Sam said. This made Tim sad. They both wanted the steak, but there was only one. Tim and Sam decided to share the steak. They cut it in half and ate it together. They were both happy and proud. From that day on, they always shared their finds with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her box of letters. One day, she felt confused because she could not match the letters to make words. Lucy asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Let's try to match the letters together!" They started to match the letters and soon, Lucy was not confused anymore. In the end, Lucy and her mom matched all the letters to make many words. They were happy and had a lot of fun. Lucy learned how to match letters and was not confused anymore.
