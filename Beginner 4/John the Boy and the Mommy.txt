Category: Beginner
Title: John the Boy and the Mommy

Once there was a little boy named John. John's mommy and daddy wanted him to try new things. One day they said to John, "Let's try some cauliflower." John said, "No, thank you!" John's mommy said, "John, if you don't try it, you'll never know if you like it or not. You should at least try it before you say no." John saw that his mommy was right so he said, "Okay, mommy." He took one bite and surprisingly he liked the cauliflower! He said, "Yummy!" John's mommy said, "See John, something unknown can become something known if you just give it a chance." This experience taught John an important moral: never be afraid to try something new. It could even turn out to be something you like!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy arrow. The arrow was not pretty. It was ugly. But Tim loved his arrow very much. He played with it every day. One night, Tim saw his arrow glow in the dark. It was a magic arrow! Tim was very happy. He showed his mom the glowing arrow. "Look, mom! My arrow glows!" Tim said. His mom was surprised and smiled at him. Tim and his mom played with the glowing arrow all night. They had so much fun. The ugly arrow made them very happy. And from that day on, Tim knew that even ugly things can be special and make people happy.

Tom and Mia are twins. They like to play in their bedroom. They have many toys and books and pictures. They also have a big window that shows the sky. One day, they see a bird outside the window. The bird is blue and yellow and has a long tail. It flies very high and fast. It soars in the air like a plane. "Wow, look at the bird!" Tom says. "It is so pretty and light. I wish I could soar like that." "Me too," Mia says. "Maybe we can pretend. Let's get our capes and masks. We can be superheroes and soar in our bedroom." Tom and Mia put on their capes and masks. They are red and blue and have stars on them. They run and jump on their beds. They make sounds like the bird and the plane. They pretend to soar in their bedroom. They have a lot of fun. They laugh and smile and feel happy. They forget about the bird and the window. They only see each other and their toys and books and pictures. They are superheroes and they soar in their bedroom.

Once upon a time, there was a normal cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a nice family. Tom liked to store his toys under the big chair. One day, Tom saw a new toy. He wanted to play with it. Tom asked his friend, the dog named Max, "Can I play with the new toy?" Max said, "Yes, but you must store it under the big chair when you are done." Tom played with the new toy all day. He had so much fun! But then, he forgot what Max had told him. He left the toy on the floor and went to sleep. The next day, Tom looked for his new toy. He could not find it anywhere. Then, he remembered what Max had said. Tom went to the big chair and saw his new toy, safe and sound. From that day on, Tom always stored his toys under the big chair.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Tom. Tom loved to catch fish. One day, he went to the sea with a sail. He wanted to catch big fish. Tom met a small fish named Fin. Fin said, "Please don't catch me, Tom! I can help you." Tom thought about it and said, "Okay, Fin. Let's catch big fish together." They sailed far away and saw a big fish. Tom tried to catch it, but the big fish was too strong. Suddenly, the big fish turned into a big, friendly whale! The whale said, "Thank you for not catching Fin. I will help you catch even bigger fish!" And they all became good friends.
