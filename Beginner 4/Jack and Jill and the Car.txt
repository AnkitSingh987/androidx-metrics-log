Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and Jill and the Car

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They both loved to play with toy cars. One day Jack had an idea. He asked Jill, “Let’s put fuel in a yellow toy car and see what happens.” Jill thought it was a great idea and they both went to get the fuel. They took a can of yellow fuel and put it in the car. At first, nothing happened. But then, suddenly, the car started to move. It was so amazing! They were both so excited that they decided to play a race. Jack removed the fuel from one of their cars so they would both have a fair start. Jack said to Jill, “Ready, set, go!” They both pushed their cars forward and raced across the room. Jack's car won the race but it took a lot of effort. Both Jack and Jill had lots of fun playing together. The End!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a new toy in her room. It was a small, soft object. She didn't know what it was, but she liked it very much. Jane played with the object all day. She was not sure what it could do, but she had fun with it. When it was time for bed, Jane felt tired. She knew she needed to sleep. She took the object with her to bed, and held it tight. While Jane slept, the object slept too. They were both very happy. The next day, Jane showed the object to her friends. They all played together and had a great day. Jane and the object were never apart, and they were always happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She dreamed of going on a journey but it seemed so far away. She had never been this far from home before and she was feeling scared. The little girl looked at the rock beside her. It stood so tall and seemed so distant. She wanted to find the courage to keep walking but she was having trouble. Just then, the little girl heard a voice. "What's wrong?" it said. She looked up and saw a friendly dog. "I'm scared to go on a journey," she said. The dog smiled at her. "It's okay to be scared. You can take the journey step by step," he said. The little girl felt brave and she started walking. She dreamed of reaching her distant destination, and with each step, that dream became a reality.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big hat. He was very excited. He put the hat on his head and went outside to play. Tim saw his friend, Sam, and they both started to run and jump with joy. As they played, something unexpected happened. Tim felt a strange power in the hat. Suddenly, he could zoom very fast. He zoomed here and there, and Sam laughed as he tried to catch Tim. They were having so much fun. Then, the big hat fell off Tim's head. The zoom power was gone. Tim and Sam were surprised, but they still had fun. They took turns wearing the hat and zooming around. They played until the sun went down, and they went home happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big earthquake. It shook the ground and made the trees dance. The animals were scared and ran to hide. In a small house, a little girl named Amy lived with her mom and her dog, Spot. One day, Amy saw her mom wipe a plate clean. She wanted to help. "Can I wipe too, Mom?" she asked. Her mom said, "Of course, Amy! You can help me wipe the plates." Amy felt happy to help her mom. She wiped and wiped, making the plates shiny and clean. Spot, the dog, was jealous. He wanted to help too. So, he took a cloth in his mouth and tried to wipe a plate. But, oh no! He dropped it, and it broke into pieces. Amy and her mom looked at Spot. They were surprised but could not be mad at him. They laughed and said, "It's okay, Spot. You can help us in other ways." And they all cleaned up the mess together.
