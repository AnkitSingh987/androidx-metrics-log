Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Intelligent Dog and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Max. Max was very good at fixing things. One day, Max saw a broken tap in the park. The tap was very sad because it could not give water to the flowers. Max wanted to help the tap. Max knew that he needed a tool to repair the tap. He went on a long walk to find the tool. On his way, he met a wise old cat. The cat told Max where to find the tool. Max was very happy and went to the place the cat told him about. Max found the tool and went back to the park. He used the tool to fix the tap. The tap was very happy because it could give water to the flowers again. The flowers grew big and beautiful. Everyone in the park thanked Max for his help. Max was proud that he could repair the tap and help the flowers grow.

Once upon a time, there was a high tree in a park. Under the tree, there was grass. Two birds lived in the tree. One bird was red and the other bird was blue. One day, the red bird and the blue bird had a quarrel. The red bird said, "This grass is mine!" The blue bird said, "No, this grass is mine!" They were not happy. A little girl came to the park. She saw the birds and said, "Why do you quarrel? You can both play on the grass." The birds stopped quarreling. They played on the grass together and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived with her mommy and daddy in a small house. One day, Lily's mommy was cooking on the stove. But, the stove was broken. The food did not cook. Lily's mommy said, "Oh no, the stove is broken! How will we complete our dinner?" Lily wanted to help. She thought and thought. Then, she had an idea. "I know!" said Lily. "We can use the sun to cook our food!" She took the food outside and put it in a special box that used the sun's heat. The sun cooked the food and made it very yummy. Lily's mommy was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Lily! You helped complete our dinner!" They all sat down and ate the yummy food that Lily helped cook. From that day on, they used the sun to cook whenever the stove was broken. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a popular little boy named Tim. Tim had a big screen in his house. He liked to watch fun shows on the screen with his friends. Tim was very happy because all his friends liked to come to his house. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. They wanted to watch a show on the big screen. But, when they tried to turn it on, the screen did not work. Tim and Sue were sad. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's try to push the button harder!" So, they both pushed the button together. The screen turned on! Tim and Sue were very happy. They watched their favorite show and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a crazy dog named Bob. Bob loved to play and jump all day. One day, Bob saw a yummy sandwich on a big rock. Bob wanted to eat the sandwich. He tried to point at the sandwich to show his friend, the little bird. The little bird saw the sandwich too. They both wanted to eat it. Bob and the little bird ran to the sandwich. They both tried to eat it at the same time. They pulled and pulled. The sandwich broke into two parts. Bob and the little bird fell down. They were sad because the sandwich was now all dirty and they could not eat it.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat sandwiches. One sunny day, Tim made a big sandwich with bread, cheese, and ham. As Tim walked outside to eat his sandwich, he saw his friend, Sam. "Hi Sam!" said Tim. "Look at my big sandwich!" Sam laughed and said, "That's a big sandwich, Tim! Can I have a bite?" Tim smiled and said, "Of course, Sam!" As they walked to the park, Tim stumbled on a rock. His sandwich fell on the ground. Tim and Sam felt sad. But then, Sam had an idea. "Let's make a new sandwich, Tim!" So, Tim and Sam went back to the charming little house and made a new sandwich to share. They were happy and ate the sandwich together.
