Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Sweet Girl and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She was very excited because today was her first day at school. Lily put on her pretty dress and her new shoes. She felt happy because she knew she belonged at school with her new friends. When Lily got to school, she saw a big, colorful building. She held her mommy's hand as they walked inside. Inside, there were lots of children playing and laughing. Lily's teacher, Miss Jane, had a warm smile and said, "Welcome, Lily! You belong here with us!" Lily had a great day at school. She played with blocks, drew pictures, and sang songs. She even made a new friend named Timmy. They shared a sweet apple during snack time. At the end of the day, Lily knew she belonged at her new school. And she couldn't wait to go back the next day.

---

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her colorful bookshelf. She wanted to read a fun book. She saw a big book on the top shelf. She tried to reach it, but it was too high. Sue called her friend Sam. "Help me get the book, please," she said. Sam came over and used a chair to reach the book. But as he pulled the book, something unexpected happened. The bookshelf started to shake! The bookshelf fell down with a loud crash. Books and toys were everywhere. Sue and Sam were scared. They tried to put the bookshelf back up, but it was broken. Sue's mom came in and saw the mess. She was not happy. Sue and Sam had to clean up the mess and throw away the broken bookshelf. They were sad and learned to be careful when getting books from high places.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Tom. He liked to play with his friend, a little mouse named Jerry. They lived in a small house with a big yard. One sunny day, Tom and Jerry went outside to play. They ran around the yard, laughing and having fun. Tom saw a big ball on the side of the yard. He had an idea for a new game. Tom poked the ball with his paw and it rolled towards Jerry. Jerry saw the ball coming and got ready to poke it back. He used his tiny hands to give the ball a big push. The ball went back to Tom, and they took turns poking the ball to each other. They played this game all day, enjoying the sun and each other's company. Tom and Jerry were the best of friends.

Lily had a big head. She liked to wear hats and bows and ribbons on her head. She had many hats and bows and ribbons in her room. She wanted to try them all. One day, she found a new hat in her closet. It was green and had a flower on it. Lily put it on her head and looked in the mirror. She smiled. She liked the hat. She went to show her mom. But something strange happened. As she walked, she felt her head grow bigger and bigger. The hat became too small and tight. Lily felt her head push against the hat. She tried to take it off, but it was stuck. Lily ran to her mom and cried. "Mom, mom, help me! My head is growing! The hat is crazy!" Her mom saw her and gasped. She grabbed Lily and hugged her. She saw a tag on the hat. It said: "Magic hat. Do not wear. Will make your head grow." Her mom quickly cut the hat off Lily's head with scissors. Lily felt her head shrink back to normal. She was relieved and happy. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, mom. I love you. I don't like the hat. It was crazy." Her mom smiled and said, "I love you too, Lily. I'm sorry you found the hat. It was a joke from your uncle. He likes to play tricks. He didn't know it would make your head grow. He will say sorry to you." Lily nodded and said, "It's okay, mom. I forgive him. But I don't want any more magic hats. I like my head the way it is."
