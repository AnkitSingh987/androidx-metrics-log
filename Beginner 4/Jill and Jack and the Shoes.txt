Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and Jack and the Shoes

Jill and Jack like to dance. They dance in the living room, in the kitchen, and in the garden. They dance to music, to songs, and to their own sounds. They have fun when they dance. One day, they go to visit their grandma. Grandma is elderly. She has white hair, glasses, and a cane. She sits on a big chair and smiles at them. She says, "Hello, my dear grandchildren. I'm happy to see you. Do you want to dance with me?" Jill and Jack are surprised. They think grandma is too old to dance. They say, "Grandma, you can't dance. You are elderly. You need your cane. You might fall and hurt yourself." Grandma laughs. She says, "Don't worry, my dears. I can still dance. I just need to change my shoes. I have special shoes for dancing. They are shiny, red, and have heels. They make me feel young and happy. Wait here, I'll be right back." Grandma goes to her bedroom and changes her shoes. She comes back with her shiny, red shoes and her cane. She says, "Now, I'm ready to dance. Come on, let's put on some music. I'll show you how I used to dance when I was young." Jill and Jack are curious. They follow grandma to the living room. They turn on the radio and find a song that grandma likes. Grandma starts to dance with her cane. She moves her feet, her hips, and her arms. She twirls, spins, and taps. She smiles and sings along. Jill and Jack are amazed. They see that grandma can dance very well. They see that grandma is happy and lively. They join her and dance with her. They learn some of her moves and try to copy them. They laugh and clap. They have fun when they dance with grandma. They learn that elderly people can still dance. They learn that dancing can change how you feel. They love their grandma and her shiny, red shoes.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with crayons. One day, Fluffy found a big box of crayons under a tree. Fluffy's friend, Buddy the dog, came over to play. "Look, Buddy! I found crayons!" said Fluffy. Buddy was happy too. They both started to draw on the ground with the crayons. They made a big sun, a house, and some flowers. After they were done, Fluffy and Buddy saw that they had crayons all over their paws. "Oh no, we need to remove the crayon from our paws," said Buddy. So, they went to a nearby pond and washed their paws clean. They were happy and clean again, ready to play another day.

Once upon a time, a little red bird lived in a big tree. The tree had many leaves that made a nice shade. The bird liked to sit in the shade when it was hot outside. One day, the bird saw a small ant walking on the ground. The ant looked worried. The bird asked, "Why are you worried, little ant?" The ant said, "I need to find shade. The sun is too hot for me." The kind red bird wanted to help the ant. The bird said, "Come with me. I know a good place with shade." The ant was happy and followed the bird. They went to the big tree and sat in the cool shade. The ant was not worried anymore. They became good friends and played together every day under the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion named Leo. Leo was very strong and had an adorable little cub named Lucy. One day, Leo found a long spear in the forest. He thought it would be fun to play with it. Leo and Lucy went to the river to play with the spear. They saw their friend, the big bird named Ben. Ben was good at flying and carrying things. Leo asked Ben, "Can you help us deliver this spear to our den?" Ben said, "Yes, I can help you." Ben took the spear in his beak and flew high in the sky. Leo and Lucy watched as Ben delivered the spear to their den. When they got home, they found the spear waiting for them. They played with it all day and had lots of fun.
