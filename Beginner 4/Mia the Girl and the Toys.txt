Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a hidden village, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to display her toys for everyone to see. One day, Mia met a boy named Tim who also lived in the village. Tim was sad because he could not find his toys. "Where are your toys?" Mia asked Tim. "I don't know," Tim replied, "I think someone took them." Mia wanted to help Tim find his toys, so they both started to look for them. They looked and looked, but they could not find the toys. As they were looking, Mia saw a big box near her house. She had never seen the box before. Mia and Tim opened the box and found all of Tim's toys inside! It turned out that Mia's little brother had taken the toys and put them in the box. Mia and Tim were happy to find the toys, and they played together all day.

Once there was a delicate flower on a big stage. Everyone in the audience loved the flower, so much so that they would all yell with joy when the flower was shown. One day, however, a strong wind began to blow. The delicate flower was blown away from the stage, into the crowd of people. Everyone tried to grab the flower, but the wind was too strong. The flower eventually flew out of the crowd, never to be seen again. Everyone felt very sad, and realized that sometimes even the most delicate things can be taken away from us if we don't take care of them. Moral of the story: take care of things that are special to us, because they can be taken away in an instant.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy boat in the big tub. One day, he saw a distant tiny bubble in the water. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to make more bubbles. He took a cup and began to pour water into the tub. As he poured, more and more bubbles appeared. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. Tim played with the bubbles and his toy boat for a long time. He watched the bubbles pop and the boat float on the water. Tim had so much fun. At the end of the day, he was a very happy boy.

Ben likes gum. He likes to chew it and make bubbles. He has many kinds of gum in his pocket. He has red gum, green gum, yellow gum and blue gum. He likes to share his gum with his friends. One day, he goes to the park with his mom. He sees a girl on the swing. She looks sad. Ben wants to make her happy. He walks to her and says, "Hi, I am Ben. Do you want some gum?" The girl looks at him and says, "No, thank you. I don't like gum. It is sticky and bad for my teeth." Ben is surprised. He thinks gum is fun and good. He says, "But gum is yummy and cool. You can chew it and make bubbles. Look, I have many colors of gum. You can choose one." The girl shakes her head and says, "No, thank you. I don't want any gum. I have a serious problem. I lost my teddy bear. He is my best friend. I don't know where he is. I am very sad." Ben feels sorry for the girl. He knows how it feels to lose something you love. He says, "I am sorry. I know how you feel. I lost my ball once. It was my favorite toy. I was very sad too. But then I found it under my bed. Maybe your teddy bear is somewhere near. Do you want me to help you look for him?" The girl smiles a little and says, "Yes, please. That would be nice of you. Thank you, Ben." Ben says, "You are welcome. Come on, let's go. Maybe he is in the sandbox or behind the slide. And if we don't find him, I can give you a hug and make you feel better. And maybe you can try some gum. I recommend the blue one. It is the best." The girl nods and says, "OK, Ben. You are very kind. Maybe I will try some gum later. But first, let's find my teddy bear." They hold hands and walk to the sandbox. They look for the teddy bear. They hope they will find him soon. They are happy to have a new friend.
