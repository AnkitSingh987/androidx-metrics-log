Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Dog

Tim and Lily were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they found a big bone in the grass. It was white and hard and had holes in it. "What is this?" Tim asked. "I think it is a skull," Lily said. "Maybe it was from a dinosaur or a dragon." "Wow, can we keep it?" Tim said. "Sure, let's take it home and show our moms," Lily said. They picked up the skull and ran to their houses. But on the way, they met a big dog. The dog was friendly and wagged its tail. It smelled the skull and wanted to play with it. "Hey, that's ours!" Tim shouted. "Go away, dog!" Lily said. But the dog did not listen. It jumped and grabbed the skull with its teeth. It ran away with it and chewed it. "Come back, dog!" Tim and Lily cried. They chased the dog, but it was too fast. It spoiled their skull and broke it into pieces. Tim and Lily were sad. They had lost their skull and their treasure. "Maybe we can find another one," Tim said. "Maybe," Lily said. "But we still have each other." They hugged and went back to the park. They hoped to find a new skull, or something else to play with. They were still friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom wanted to be successful. He thought that if he wore his lucky pants, he would be successful. So, he wore his lucky pants every day. One day, Tom saw a big hill. He wanted to climb the hill to show he was successful. He started to climb the hill. But then, something unexpected happened. Tom's lucky pants got stuck on a branch! Tom did not want to surrender. He kept trying to climb the hill. But his pants were still stuck. He tried and tried, but he could not get his pants free. Tom was very sad. He had to surrender. He could not be successful without his lucky pants. So, he went home with his pants still stuck on the branch. Tom was not successful, and this story has a bad ending.

One day, a gray cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and decided to rub against it. While he was rubbing, he heard a loud noise. Tom got scared and ran away. Tom ran to his friend, a dog named Max. Max asked, "What's wrong, Tom?" Tom said, "I heard a big noise when I rubbed the tree. I am scared." Max wanted to help Tom, so they went back to the tree together. When they got to the tree, they saw a police car with its lights on. The police officer came out and said, "Don't be scared, Tom and Max. The noise was just my car. I was driving by and saw you rubbing the tree. I stopped to say hello." Tom and Max felt better and laughed. The unexpected noise was not scary after all.

Sam is a big truck. He likes to load things and go places. He helps people with his work. One day, he has to load medicine for the hospital. The medicine is very important. It can make sick people feel better. Sam goes to the warehouse. He sees many boxes of medicine. He says to the man, "I can load all the boxes. I am a strong truck." The man smiles and says, "Thank you, Sam. You are a good truck. But be careful. The boxes are heavy and fragile. You have to load them gently and securely." Sam nods and starts to load the boxes. He uses his forks and his straps. He is careful not to drop or shake the boxes. He counts the boxes as he loads them. He says, "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten. I have loaded ten boxes of medicine. I am ready to go." Sam drives to the hospital. He is happy and proud. He says, "I have loaded medicine for the hospital. I have done a good job. I hope the sick people feel better soon." He arrives at the hospital. He sees a nurse waiting for him. She says, "Hello, Sam. You are a fast and strong truck. We need the medicine urgently. Thank you for bringing it to us." Sam unloads the boxes. He gives them to the nurse. He says, "You are welcome. I am glad to help. The medicine is very important. Please take good care of the sick people." The nurse smiles and says, "We will, Sam. You are a kind and helpful truck. We appreciate your work. Have a nice day." Sam waves goodbye and drives away. He feels happy and proud. He says, "I have unloaded medicine for the hospital. I have done a good job. I am a big, strong, and good truck." He looks for his next work. He likes to load things and go places. He helps people with his work.
