Category: Beginner
Title: Ella the Lively Elephant and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a lively elephant named Ella. She had a big trunk that she used to pick up things. One day, Ella found a pretty hat. She wanted to wear it on her head. Ella went to her friend, the monkey, and said, "Look at my new hat! I want to wear it." The monkey said, "It's lovely, but it's too small for you." Ella didn't listen and tried to wear the hat anyway. She put the hat on her trunk and tried to put it on her head. But the hat was too small and it got stuck on her trunk. Ella was very sad and cried. She couldn't get the hat off and her trunk hurt. The hat was not a good idea after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big piano in her house. She loved to play the piano every day. The piano made pretty sounds when she played it. One day, Lily saw that her piano was dirty. She wanted her piano to be clean and pretty again. So, she asked her mom for help. Her mom gave her a soft cloth to clean the piano. Lily cleaned the piano with the cloth. She worked hard to make it clean. When she was done, the piano was not dirty anymore. It was shiny and pretty again. Lily played the piano and it sounded even better now. She was very happy.

---

One day, Tom and Tim were playing in their room. They saw a big lamp on the table. Tom wanted to touch the lamp, but Tim said, "No, don't touch it!" Tom did not listen. He touched the lamp and it fell on the floor. The lamp broke and Tom felt guilty. He knew he did something wrong. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's fold a paper lamp to fix it!" So they both started folding paper to make a new lamp. They worked hard and made a pretty paper lamp. They showed the paper lamp to their mom. She saw the broken lamp and knew what happened. But she was happy that they tried to fix it. She hugged them and said, "Next time, listen to each other and be careful." Tom and Tim learned their lesson. They promised to listen and be more careful in the future. They put the paper lamp on the table and played nicely together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw an old man sitting on a seat. The old man looked sad. Tim wanted to help him feel better. "Hi, old man," Tim said. "Why are you sad?" The old man looked at Tim and said, "I lost my hat. I loved that hat so much." Tim wanted to help the old man find his hat. They looked all over the park, but they could not find it. Then, they saw a little girl wearing a hat that looked like the old man's hat. Tim asked the girl, "Did you find that hat in the park?" The girl said, "Yes, I found it on the ground." Tim asked her to give it back to the old man. The girl said, "No, I like this hat. I will keep it." Tim and the old man were sad. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew the hat off the girl's head and back to the old man. The old man was so happy to have his hat back. He thanked Tim for helping him. Tim felt proud and happy. The old man, Tim, and the girl all played in the park together, and they became good friends.

One day, a beautiful bunny named Bella went to play in the sunny field. She loved to hop high and fast. As she hopped, she saw a big puddle of oil on the ground. Bella did not know what it was, so she hopped closer to look. A friendly bird saw Bella and flew down to her. "Hello, Bella! Be careful, that is oil," said the bird. "It is not good to hop in." Bella looked at the oil and said, "Thank you, bird. I will not hop in it." Bella and the bird played all day, hopping and flying in the field. They had so much fun together. When it was time to go home, Bella said, "Goodbye, bird! Let's play again soon!" The bird smiled and flew away, and Bella hopped back home, happy and safe.
