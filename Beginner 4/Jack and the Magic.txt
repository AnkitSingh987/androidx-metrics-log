Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a 3 year old named Jack. He was a fair lad, with a shock of bright yellow hair. One day, he decided to go explore his house. He had never been in the door at the end of the hallway before. "Who lives there?" he asked his mother. "Oh, that's the magic room," she said, with a smile. "Go and find out!" So Jack went to the door, and despite being a bit scared, he opened it. Inside was a wonderful room filled with magic. The walls were painted with stars, and glittered with gold. Jack was amazed, but he couldn't help feel that something was missing. He looked around and saw a note taped to the wall. It said: "Come back tomorrow when the sun is high, and the magic will be even fairer!" The next day, Jack returned to the room. Sure enough, something had happened - the stars seemed to have grown bigger, and even more glittered with gold. Jack smiled and laughed, knowing that his mother had been right. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a fancy red bag with a big zip. He loved to carry his toys in the bag. One day, he decided to go to the park with his friends. At the park, Tim felt the urge to open his bag and show his friends the toys inside. But his mom told him to wait. She said, "Tim, you will get a surprise if you wait." Tim was curious and decided to listen to his mom. After playing with his friends, Tim opened his fancy red bag. To his surprise, there was a big, shiny toy car in the bag! His mom had put it there for him. Tim was so happy and thanked his mom. He learned that waiting can bring good things.

Once there was a little boy named Max. He was only three years old and he loved art. He loved to draw pictures and hang them up around his house. His mum and dad loved it too. One day, Max asked his mum if he could have a special box of art supplies. His mum smiled and said yes and gave him a big cardboard box filled with crayons, markers and paper. Max was so excited that he couldn't wait to get started! He pulled out a piece of paper and began to draw a picture. He was so lost in his picture that he forgot to tidy up his supplies. His mum came into his room and saw the mess. She didn't say anything but just smiled at him and said, "Let me help you tidy up, Max." Max thanked his mum and said, "Next time I'll try to be more careless." His mum smiled and said, "I know you will."

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She was full of energy, and very restless! Every day, Lucy would play hide and seek around her house. One day, she wanted to hide on the top shelf, but she was too small to reach it. She begged her mom to help her. Her mom said, "It's too high, sweetheart. I can't help you." Lucy looked sad and replied, "But I trust you mom! Please help me get on the shelf!" Her mom smiled and said, "Ok, as long as you are careful. Hold onto my hand and I'll help you get up." Lucy grasped her mom's hand and stepped onto the shelf. Her mom said, "Be careful there, ok?" Lucy smiled brightly and said, "I will Mom! I trust you!" And from then on, Lucy and her mom trusted each other. They had a bond that nothing could break.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. He liked to sleep all day and play all night. One morning, Tom did not want to wake up. His friend, a little bird named Sue, flew to his window. "Tom, wake up! It's time to play," Sue said. But Tom did not want to wake up. He was too sleepy. Sue tried to wake him up again. "Tom, please wake up," she said. This time, Tom woke up, but he was not happy. Tom started to complain, "Sue, why did you wake me up? I was having a nice sleep." Sue felt sad but said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I just wanted to play with you. Let's go outside and have fun." Tom and Sue went outside and played all day. They ran, jumped, and had a great time. At the end of the day, Tom was happy that Sue woke him up. They both had so much fun, and Tom was not sleepy anymore.
