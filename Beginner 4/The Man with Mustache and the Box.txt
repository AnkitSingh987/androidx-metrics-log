Category: Beginner
Title: The Man with Mustache and the Box

One day, a man with a big mustache walked to the park. He saw a little boy playing with an empty box. The boy was sad because his box was empty and he had no toys. The man with the mustache said, "Why are you sad, little boy?" The boy looked up and said, "I have no toys and my box is empty." The man with the mustache wanted to help the boy. He saw a bug on the ground and slapped it with his hand. He picked up the bug and put it in the empty box. The boy's face lit up and he said, "Thank you, Mr. Mustache! Now my box is not empty!" From that day on, the boy and the man with the mustache became friends, and the boy was never sad again.

Once upon a time, there was a young cat named Bob. Bob loved to stretch his legs and jump high in the air. He would play all day with his friends, running and jumping around. One day, a new cat named Tim came to play. Tim was very good at stretching and jumping too. Bob started to feel jealous because Tim could jump higher than him. Bob wanted to be the best jumper. Bob practiced stretching every day to jump higher. He stretched and stretched, and soon he could jump just as high as Tim. Bob was happy, and he learned that he didn't need to be jealous. He and Tim became best friends, and they played together, jumping and stretching all day long.

Sam was anxious because it was his turn to zip on the zip line. He wanted to do it but he was scared too. He started trembling and his mum gave him a big hug. Mum said it was okay to be scared and she showed him how to do it. She zipped down the zip line first. It looked fun, so Sam got ready. He took a deep breath and pushed off the platform. As he moved faster and faster, his fear split and changed into excitement. When Sam got to the end, he couldn't believe how much fun he had! He was so happy that he did it that he asked if he could go again.

Jake was a little boy who loved to play outside. One day, he went out in the garden and saw a huge pile of dust. He was excited and ran towards it, ready to kick it and make a big mess. When Jake reached the pile, he lifted his foot to kick it. But just as he was about to do so, he heard a voice. "Why don't you just leave it alone?" said the voice. Jake was scared. He turned around and saw a huge frog staring at him. "What should I do?" Jake asked in a scared voice. "Just sweep it away with a broom," the frog said. So Jake grabbed the broom and slowly swept the dust away. When he was done, he thanked the frog for helping him and ran off to continue playing in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a white dog. This dog was very independent. He liked to play and run all by himself. One day, the dog went to the park to play. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As the white dog tried to climb the tree, he heard a funny sound. He looked up and saw a bird. The bird was laughing at him. The dog felt silly but continued to try to climb the tree. The dog finally climbed the tree, but something unexpected happened. The branch he was on broke and he fell down. The white dog was sad and hurt. The bird stopped laughing and flew away. The dog learned that maybe he should not do everything alone.

One day, a gray cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a house with a big window. Inside, he saw a sharp knife and a piece of toast on a table. The toast looked very yummy. Tom wanted to eat the toast, but he didn't know how to get inside the house. Tom asked a bird, "How can I get inside the house?" The bird said, "I can help you. I will open the door for you." The bird flew to the door and opened it for Tom. Tom was very happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" Then, Tom went inside the house to get the toast. But when Tom got to the table, the toast was gone! He looked around and saw a mouse eating the toast. Tom was sad, but the mouse said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I was hungry too. Let's share the toast." Tom smiled and said, "Okay, let's share it." So, Tom and the mouse became friends and shared the yummy toast.
