Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bubbles

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with bubbles. One sunny day, Lily went outside to blow some bubbles with her favorite bubble wand. As she blew the bubbles, one big bubble floated up into the sky. But then, the bubble wand broke. Lily frowned, because she could not make more bubbles. She felt sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a nice boy named Tom saw Lily and came to help. He had a new bubble wand with him. They both blew lots of bubbles together and had lots of fun. The broken wand didn't make Lily frown anymore, because she had a new friend to play with.

One day, Anna and Ben went to the park with their mom. They saw a big slide and ran to play on it. They had a lot of fun going up and down the slide. But then, the sky became dark and it started to rain. "Oh no, we have to go home!" mom said. "Come on, Anna and Ben, let's hurry!" Anna and Ben followed mom to the car. But on the way, they saw a big puddle. They wanted to jump in it and splash. They stopped and looked at mom. "Can we jump in the puddle, mom?" Anna asked. "No, you can't, Anna. You will get wet and cold. And we don't have an umbrella." mom said. "But we like puddles, mom. They are fun." Ben said. "I know, Ben. But we have to go home now. Maybe we can play in the puddles another day." mom said. Anna and Ben were sad. They wanted to play in the puddles. But they realized that mom was right. They would get wet and cold. And they didn't have an umbrella. They decided to listen to mom and go to the car. "OK, mom. We will go home." Anna said. "OK, mom. We will go home." Ben said. They got in the car and mom drove them home. They were still a little sad. But they hoped that the sun would come out soon. And maybe, they would find a big umbrella. Then, they could play in the puddles again.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Tiny. Tiny loved to wander around the park near his home. One sunny day, while wandering, he found a bone. Tiny was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Kitty. Tiny went to Kitty's house and said, "Look, Kitty! I found a bone!" Kitty, a small cat, looked at the bone and said, "Wow, that's a big bone, Tiny!" Tiny and Kitty decided to play with the bone together. They played and played, having lots of fun. After a while, they got tired and lay down under a tree. Tiny and Kitty were very happy they found the bone and could share it. They promised to always play and wander together, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to visit his grandma. Every time he went to see her, he wore his favorite vest. It was red and blue and very warm. One day, Tim and his grandma went for a walk in the park. Tim was very careful as he walked. He did not want to fall or get his vest dirty. They saw ducks in the pond and flowers in the grass. After their walk, Tim and his grandma went back to her house. They had a yummy snack and talked about their day. Tim was happy that he had been careful and his vest was still clean. He couldn't wait to visit his grandma again.

Lily likes to find things. She finds toys, books, rocks, and bugs. She puts them in a big box in her room. She is happy when she finds new things. One day, Lily finds a shiny thing in the grass. It is round and has a hole in the middle. It looks like a ring. Lily thinks it is pretty. She puts it on her finger. She feels proud. Lily runs to show her mom. But her mom is not home. She is at work. Lily is sad. She looks for her dad. But her dad is not home. He is at the store. Lily is lonely. Lily goes to her room. She wants to play with her box of things. But she cannot open the box. The shiny thing is too big for her finger. It is stuck. It hurts. Lily cries. Lily waits for her mom and dad. She is quiet. She is dull. She is scared. She does not like the shiny thing anymore. She wants to take it off. But she cannot. She is stuck. She is unhappy.
