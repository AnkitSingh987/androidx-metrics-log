Category: Beginner
Title: Abi the Girl and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Abi. Abi was three years old and had a very special toy. Her toy was made of shiny metal and it was one of Abi's most prized possessions. One day, Abi's friend Jenny came over to visit. She saw Abi's toy and wanted to play with it. Abi didn't want anyone to touch her toy, so she told Jenny no. Jenny was mad, so she pretended that the toy was hers. She said it was very valuable and she wasn't going to let Abi have it. Abi disagreed with Jenny and said the toy was really hers. Jenny still wouldn't listen, so Abi knew she had to be sneaky. Abi made a fake toy. It looked just like her real one, but it wasn't made of metal. She said it was hers and that she didn't want Jenny to have it either. Abi and Jenny argued for a bit, but eventually Jenny gave up. She saw that Abi was not going to give up and that the toy really was hers. Abi was so happy that she was able to keep her toy!

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They like to swing on the big swings. They go up and down, up and down. They feel happy and free. "Look, Sara, look!" Ben says. He points to the ground. "There are many ants there. They are so small and busy. They carry things to their home." Sara jumps off the swing and runs to see the ants. She bends down and watches them. They are black and shiny. They have six legs and two feelers. They walk in a line. "Wow, they are amazing!" Sara says. "Can we touch them?" Ben shakes his head. "No, we can't. They might bite us. And they are disgusting. They eat dirty things. Yuck!" Sara makes a face. "You are right. They are disgusting. Let's go back to the swing. It is more fun." They run back to the swing and hop on. They swing higher and higher. They forget about the ants. They laugh and sing. They enjoy the sun and the breeze. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named John and Joanne. One day, they left their homes and went to the garage. Joanne took out the car and said "Let's go!" John paused and said, "Wait a minute, let's get something to eat first." Joanne nodded and said, "We can get ice cream." John thought for a moment and said, "Let's get something bigger. I know a generous person who will give us a big box of snacks." So the two went to the generous person's house and asked for the snacks. The generous person was happy to give them and the two friends happily ate the snacks. After that, they went back to the garage and drove off in the car. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. He lived in a small house with his cat, Sam. Tom liked to watch television, and Sam liked to play with his toys. One day, Tom saw a big parade on the television. He saw people waving and having fun. Tom wanted to join the parade too. So, he put on his hat and went outside with Sam. Tom and Sam waved at everyone they saw. The people in the parade waved back. They were all very happy. Tom and Sam had a great day at the parade. When they went back home, they watched the parade on the television together.

One day, a green rat met a big lion. The lion was very hungry. The rat was scared. The lion said, "I will eat you, little rat!" The green rat did not want to be eaten. The green rat had an idea. He said to the lion, "Wait! If you let me go, I will help you find more food." The lion thought about it and said, "Okay, little rat. Show me where the food is." The green rat took the lion to a big tree with lots of fruit. The lion was happy and started to eat the fruit. The green rat said, "Now you have food and you don't need to eat me." The lion agreed and let the green rat go. They both became friends and the lion never tried to eat the green rat again.
