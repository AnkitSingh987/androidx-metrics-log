Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there was a man named Tim. Tim liked to sell things. He sold toys, balls, and even hats. Everyone in the town liked Tim. One day, a little girl named Sue came to Tim's store. Sue wanted to buy a toy. She asked Tim, "Can I buy a toy, please?" Tim smiled and said, "Of course! Pick any toy you like." Sue picked a toy car and gave Tim some money. She was very happy with her new toy. But then, a big man came to the store. He was not happy. He told Tim, "You can't sell toys here. It's against the law." Tim was sad. He didn't want to break the law. So, he stopped selling toys. But Sue had an idea. She talked to the big man and asked, "Can Tim sell toys if he follows the law?" The big man thought about it and said, "Yes, if he follows the law, he can sell toys." Tim was happy again. He followed the law and continued to sell toys. Everyone in the quiet little town was happy, too. And Sue played with her toy car every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very foolish boy. One day, he had to go to school. His mom gave him lunch in a bag. She told him to hold the bag tight and not to lose it. On his way to school, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. He put his lunch down and started to climb. But he forgot to hold his lunch bag. A dog came and took his lunch away. Tim felt sad when he saw the dog with his lunch. He knew he should have held the bag tight. Next time, he promised to hold his things and not be foolish. And he never lost his lunch again.

Once upon a time, there was a big red engine. The engine had a door that was always closed. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the engine and wanted to look inside. He walked up to the engine and said, "I want to open the door and see what's inside!" Tim tried to open the door, but it was stuck. He pulled and pulled, but the door wouldn't open. A wise old man saw Tim and said, "Little boy, do not be foolish. The door will not open today, but it will open when the time is right." Tim was sad but listened to the wise old man. He went home and waited. The next day, Tim went back to the engine. He tried to open the door again, and this time, it opened! Inside, he found a fun play area with lots of toys and games. Tim was so happy that he had waited for the right time to open the door. He played with the toys and games all day long. And from that day on, Tim always remembered the wise old man's words and never acted foolish again.

Once upon a time there was a persistent little girl called Jane. Jane loved picnics and she would drop everything she was doing so she could go on picnics. One day, her mom asked her "would you like to go on a picnic Jane?" and Jane shouted "YES!" so loudly that her mom couldn't help but smile. So off they went to the nearby park for a picnic. Jane sat in the grass and put down her favourite teddy bear. Then she started to drop things. She dropped her spoon in the grass, she dropped her juice cup and then she dropped her sandwich! Mama asked Jane, "What are you doing Jane?" Jane smiled and said, "I'm persistent! I'm setting up the picnic!" Mama laughed and said, "Well you sure are persistent, I'll give you that!" And so Jane went on to have a wonderful picnic with her family. It was the perfect day.

Once upon a time, there was a pale boy named Tom. Tom loved to be outside and play with his friends. They liked to run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Tom heard music coming from a big tree. The music made him feel happy. He wanted to find out where the music was coming from, so he went to the tree and looked up. In the tree, he saw a small bird singing a beautiful song. Tom smiled and started to dance to the bird's music. His friends saw him and came to dance too. They all had a lot of fun dancing to the bird's song, and they were all very happy.
