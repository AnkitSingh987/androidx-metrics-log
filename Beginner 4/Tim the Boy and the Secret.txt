Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Secret

One day, a boy named Tim found a file. He did not know what it was, so he decided to examine it. He saw many papers inside the file. Tim became very impatient because he wanted to know what was in the papers. He went to his mom and asked, "Mom, what is this?" His mom looked at the file and said, "Oh, this is a file with papers inside. Let's look at them together." As they looked at the papers, they found a map. The map showed a secret path to a hidden park. Tim and his mom were very excited. They followed the map and found the park. They played there all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a loud dog named Spot. He lived in a small house with a big fireplace. Spot liked to twist and play near the fireplace. He had many friends, but his best friend was a cat named Fluffy. One day, Spot and Fluffy were playing near the fireplace. They were twisting and jumping when something unexpected happened. A big bird came down the fireplace! The bird was very big and very loud. Spot and Fluffy were scared, but they wanted to help the big bird. Spot and Fluffy worked together to help the big bird. They twisted and pulled, and finally, the big bird was free. The big bird was so happy that it sang a loud song. Spot and Fluffy were happy too. They had a new friend, and they all played and twisted together near the fireplace. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, there was a little bear. The little bear loved to play all day. He would run, jump, and climb trees with his friends. But when the sun went down, the little bear would get very tired and needed to sleep. One day, the little bear's mom gave him a tiny pill to help him sleep deep. She said, "Take this before bed, and you will have the best sleep ever." The little bear was excited to try the pill and see if it would help him sleep better. That night, the little bear took the pill, and he fell into a deep sleep. He dreamed of playing with his friends in the forest, and they all had so much fun. When the little bear woke up the next morning, he felt so good and rested. He thanked his mom for the pill and went off to play with his friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very hungry. He went to look for food. Max saw a big mine. He thought there might be food inside the mine. Max went into the mine. He walked and walked. Then, something unexpected happened. Max saw a big cat. The big cat said, "This is my mine. You cannot have food here." Max was scared, but he did not want to surrender. He had an idea. Max said to the big cat, "If you give me food, I will be your friend." The big cat thought about it. Then, the big cat said, "Okay, let's be friends and share the food." Max and the big cat ate food together. Max was not hungry anymore, and they both were happy.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys. Lily has a soft bear. Tom has a hard truck. One day, they go to the park with their toys. "Can I play with your bear?" Tom asks Lily. "No, it is my bear. You have your truck," Lily says. "But I want your bear. It is soft and nice. My truck is hard and loud," Tom says. Lily does not want to share. She hugs her bear. Tom is sad and angry. He thinks of a plan. Tom waits for Lily to look away. Then he grabs her bear and runs. Lily sees him and shouts. "Give me back my bear! It is mine!" Lily says. Tom does not listen. He runs faster. He does not see a big rock on the ground. He trips and falls. The bear flies out of his hand. Lily runs after him. Tom hurts his leg. He cries. Lily gets her bear. She is happy. But she sees Tom crying. She feels sorry for him. "Are you okay?" Lily asks Tom. "No, my leg hurts. And I have no toy," Tom says. Lily thinks for a moment. Then she does something nice. She gives Tom her bear. "Here, you can play with my bear. It is soft and nice. I can play with your truck. It is hard and loud," Lily says. Tom stops crying. He smiles. He takes the bear and hugs it. "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend," Tom says. Lily smiles too. She takes the truck and makes noises. "You are welcome, Tom. You are a good friend too," Lily says. They play together with their toys. They are happy. They share. They are friends.
