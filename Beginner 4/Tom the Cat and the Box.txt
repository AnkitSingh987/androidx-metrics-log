Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his ball. One day, Tom saw a big box in the room. He was very curious. Tom went to the box and gave it a little poke. "What's inside?" he thought. The box moved a little. Tom jumped back, but then he heard a small voice from inside the box. "Let me out, please!" said the voice. Tom pushed the box and it made a turn. The box opened and out came a tiny mouse named Jerry. "Thank you for helping me!" said Jerry. Tom and Jerry became good friends and played with the ball together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a toy that could shrink. When she said "zip", the toy would get very small. Sue loved her toy because she could take it with her when she went to visit her grandma. One day, Sue went to the store with her mom. She saw a cheap toy that she really wanted. Her mom said she could buy it with her own money. Sue was so happy! She put her shrinking toy in her pocket and bought the cheap toy. When Sue got home, she wanted to play with both toys. She said "zip" and her shrinking toy got big again. Sue played with her toys all day. She was very happy to have a new friend for her shrinking toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to get mail from her friends. One day, she got a story in the mail. The story was about a little dog who liked to play. Sue read the story to her little brother, Tim. They both liked the story a lot. They wanted to play like the little dog in the story. So, they went outside to play in the yard. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the story away. Sue and Tim were very sad. They looked and looked, but they could not find the story. The story was gone forever, and Sue and Tim were sad that they could not read it again.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They played in the park, and Spot jumped high to catch a ball. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. As they walked, they saw a small hole in the ground. Tim said, "What's that, Spot?" Spot sniffed the hole and barked. Tim looked closer and saw something underground. He called his friend, Sue, to show her. Sue came running and looked at the hole. She said, "Wow, Tim! That's a secret underground room!" They both got excited and decided to explore. Tim and Sue climbed down into the room and found a big treasure chest. They opened it and saw shiny gold coins and pretty jewels. Tim and Sue showed the treasure to their friends and shared it with everyone. They all played together in the park with their new toys. And Spot, the dog, continued to jump high and catch the ball, happy to be with his friends.

One day, a little girl named Amy was in her room. She was playing with her toys. She had a big box of toys. It was very messy. She wanted to sort them. She started to sort her toys into small piles. She put all the cars in one pile and all the dolls in another pile. She felt happy when she saw her toys in neat piles. Amy's mom came into the room with a big empty box. She said, "Amy, let's put all your toys in this box. Then, we can put the box in the living room." Amy liked the idea. She took a napkin and wiped the toys clean. Then, she put them in the empty box. She was excited to play with her toys in the living room. Later that day, Amy's dad came home. He saw the box of toys in the living room. He said, "Wow, Amy! You did a great job sorting your toys!" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, Daddy! I sorted them and cleaned them with a napkin!" Her dad was very proud of her. They spent the rest of the day playing with the toys in the living room.
