Category: Beginner
Title: Molly and the Blanket

Molly was a three year old and she wanted to play. She got out of bed and ran outside. She saw a big blanket on the grass. She ran over, picked it up and shouted, "Blanket! I want to play with the blanket!" Molly and her friend, Jack, started playing with the blanket. They pretended it was a boat and played a game of pirates. Suddenly, Jack shouted, "We need water for our boat!" Molly was very excited and said, "Let's find some!" The two children ran to the river. They gathered up some water and shouted, "Water! It's available!" Molly and Jack took the water back to the blanket. They filled up the blanket with the water, and now they could really play pirates. They had so much fun!

Once upon a time there were two groups of friends. The first group was big, and the second group was small. One day, the small group was playing and having lots of fun. But suddenly, one of the members became sad. She started to cry. The big group was a bit scared. They didn't know what to do. Then the leader of the big group stepped forward. She found a way to make the small group smile again. She called out, "Let's share!" The big group shared their toys, games and stories with the small group. The small group was very happy and so were the big group. The big group was proud of their leader. They saw how she spread happiness by sharing. The small group learned that when you share, it can make everyone feel calm and happy. The end.

On a bright morning, Jack had a very exciting mission. He had to unpack his new toy box! As he opened the box, he was amazed at all the fun things that were inside. He saw a modern gemstone inside, a jewel made of bright colors! "Wow," he exclaimed, "what's this?" Jack's mom smiled. "That's your new jewel!" she said. "It's a very special toy. You can look at it and learn all about the different colors and shapes it has." Jack was so excited he began to unpack the rest of the toy box right away. He found all kinds of modern gadgets and trinkets, each one brighter than the last. Jack had a great day exploring all his new treasures.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw a cat with a brush in its mouth. The cat looked impatient. Amy wanted to help the cat, so she sat down next to it. "Hi cat, why do you have a brush?" Amy asked. The cat looked at her and meowed. It was trying to tell her something. Amy thought the cat wanted her to brush its fur. So, she took the brush and started brushing the cat. As she brushed the cat, something unexpected happened. The cat started to talk! "Thank you, Amy! I am a magic cat. I could not talk until someone brushed me," the cat said. Amy was very surprised, but happy she could help. Now, Amy and the magic cat were good friends and had lots of fun together.

Mia and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars and blocks. One day, they went to the park with their moms. They saw a big slide and ran to it. Mia climbed up the ladder and slid down. She laughed and said, "That was fun!" Sam nodded and followed her. He slid down and smiled. He said, "Let's do it again!" They climbed up and slid down many times. They were having a lot of fun. But then, they saw a boy who was bigger and meaner than them. He came to the slide and pushed them away. He said, "This is my slide. Go away!" Mia and Sam felt scared and sad. They did not want to fight. They looked at their moms, who were sitting on a bench. Their moms saw what was happening and got up. They walked to the slide and said, "Stop that. You can't push other kids. You have to share and be nice." The boy did not listen. He said, "No, I won't. This is my slide. You can't tell me what to do." He tried to push Mia and Sam again, but their moms stopped him. They said, "Yes, we can. You are being rude and mean. You have to leave now." The boy felt angry and uncomfortable. He did not like to be told what to do. He said, "Fine, I don't care. I don't want to play with you anyway." He ran away to another part of the park. Mia and Sam felt relieved and happy. They hugged their moms and said, "Thank you. You are the best." Their moms hugged them back and said, "You're welcome. We love you." They touched their cheeks and said, "Now, do you want to play some more?" Mia and Sam nodded and said, "Yes, please!" They climbed up the slide and slid down again. They laughed and said, "This is our slide. We can share and be nice." They played until it was time to go home. They had a lot of fun.
