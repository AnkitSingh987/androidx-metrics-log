Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a pet dog named Zip. Tim loved to feed Zip every day. They were very happy and felt lucky to have each other. One day, Tim and Zip went to the park to play. They saw a big tree and decided to rest under it. While they were sitting, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and took Zip's food! Tim and Zip were very sad. They didn't know what to do. They thought they were lucky, but now they felt unlucky. They started to walk home with no food for Zip. But then, another surprise happened! A kind lady saw Tim and Zip looking sad. She asked them what was wrong. Tim told her about the big bird that took Zip's food. The kind lady smiled and gave them a big bag of food for Zip. Tim and Zip were so happy and felt lucky again. They thanked the lady and went home to enjoy their new food.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little lion. The little lion was very hungry. He wanted to eat something tasty. He walked and walked, looking for food. One day, the little lion found a small bird. The bird was hurt and could not fly. The little lion was happy. He thought, "I can eat this bird, and it will be tasty." But the bird looked at the lion and said, "Please, don't eat me. I will pray for you to find more tasty food." The little lion thought for a moment. He decided to let the bird go. The bird was so happy and prayed for the lion to find tasty food. The lion felt good inside. The next day, the little lion found a big pile of tasty food. He was so happy! He remembered the bird and knew that the bird's prayer had helped him. The little lion was not hungry anymore. He learned that being kind can bring good things.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown dog. He had a long tail. He liked to play in the park. One day, the little brown dog went to the park. He saw a big tree. He wanted to chase his tail under the tree. He ran around and around. While he was running, he did not see a hole in the ground. The little brown dog started to fall. His tail got stuck in the hole. He was sad and scared. But then, a nice girl came and helped the little brown dog. She pulled him out of the hole. The little brown dog was so happy. They played together in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he saw his friend Tom with a big, shiny, new toy car. Tim felt envious of Tom's new car, and he wanted one too. Tim went home and asked his mom, "Mom, can I order a big, shiny toy car like Tom's?" His mom said, "Sure, but first, you need to search for your old toys and give them away. This will teach you to share and be kind." So, Tim searched his room and found many old toys he didn't play with anymore. He gave them away to other kids who didn't have many toys. After that, his mom ordered the big, shiny toy car for him. When the new car arrived, Tim showed it to Tom. Tom said, "Wow, your car is so cool! I wish I had one like that." Tim smiled and said, "You can play with my car too. We can share and have fun together!" The moral of the story is that sharing and being kind to others can make you happy and bring you good things.

Once upon a time there was a special little girl who was very gifted. The girl was excited to go on a special adventure and so she went looking for it. The girl went for a walk into the forest and as she came to a clearing she saw something shiny - it was steel! The girl thought it was a treasure from far away. So she took it and followed a path that lead her to a big castle. There she saw lots of different people and machines all looking at her. She didn't know what to do and felt a bit scared. But then the people started to smile at her and the king came to talk. He said that he could see she was so very gifted and had brought him the steel. He thanked her and told her that it was a very special gift. The little girl was so happy, and the King gave her a big hug and said she could come back any time. The girl thanked him and went on her way, looking back at the castle with a big smile.
