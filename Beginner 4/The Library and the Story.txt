Category: Beginner
Title: The Library and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a very pretty library. Many people liked to go there to read books. One day, a man came to the library. He wanted to sell some of his old books. The library lady thought the books were nice, so she bought them. She put them on the shelves for everyone to read. The people in the town were very happy to see new books. But soon, the people found out that the books had bad words in them. They were not happy anymore. The pretty library was not a nice place to be. The library lady was sad. She learned that she should be more careful when buying books. The moral of the story is to always check what you buy before putting it in a special place like the library.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a drive with her dad. They drove to a big green park. In the park, Lily saw a lot of wet grass from the rain. She liked to play on the wet grass because it felt nice and cool on her feet. Lily saw a small plant in the wet grass. She went to look at it. It was a little herb. She picked it up and smelled it. It smelled very nice. Lily wanted to show her dad the herb, but when she turned around, her dad was gone! Lily was scared at first. Then, she saw her dad hiding behind a big tree. He was playing a game with her! Lily laughed and ran to her dad. They played hide and seek with the herb in the wet grass. It was a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to write. He would write all day with his big crayon. One day, Tim saw his dad write something called a "license". Tim wanted to write one too. Tim asked his dad, "Can I write a license?" His dad smiled and said, "Sure, you can try." Tim was very happy. He took his big crayon and a piece of paper. He tried to write like his dad. Tim showed his license to his friends. His friends were envious. They wanted to write a license too. So, they all got their crayons and papers. They had a fun day writing licenses together. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little ant was walking in the park. The ant was very intelligent. The ant saw a big cake on a table. The ant wanted to eat the cake. But the cake was too high for the ant to reach. The ant thought, "How can I get to the cake?" The ant saw a boy named Tim. The ant walked up to Tim and said, "Hello, can you help me get the cake?" Tim looked at the ant and said, "Yes, I can help you, but we have to delay. My mom is coming soon." The ant agreed to wait. When Tim's mom came, she saw the ant and Tim. She was surprised and said, "Wow, an intelligent ant! Let's help the ant get the cake." Tim's mom lifted the ant up to the cake. The ant was very happy and thanked Tim and his mom. The ant shared the cake with Tim and his mom, and they all became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim had sunglasses. The sunglasses were not bright. They were dull. Tim liked his sunglasses very much. He wore them every day. Tim had a friend named Sue. Sue did not have sunglasses. She wanted sunglasses like Tim. Tim did not mind. He said, "We can share my sunglasses, Sue." So, Tim and Sue took turns wearing the dull sunglasses. They played in the sun all day. They were very happy. And they did not mind sharing the sunglasses at all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her fragile toy house. One day, Sue's mommy had to go to work. She told Sue to stay home and play. While mommy was at work, Sue played with her toy house. She carefully opened the door and said, "Hello, Mr. Bear! Do you want to stay in my house?" Mr. Bear nodded and went inside. Sue was very happy that Mr. Bear stayed in her toy house. She played all day until her mommy came back from work. Sue showed her mommy how Mr. Bear stayed in the house. Mommy smiled and said, "Good job, Sue! You took care of your fragile toy house and Mr. Bear."
