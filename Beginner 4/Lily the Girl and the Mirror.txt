Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Mirror

One day, a little girl named Lily found a funny mirror in her room. She did not know where it came from. Lily loved to play with the funny mirror because it made her face look silly. She would make a big smile and the mirror would make it look even bigger. Lily showed the funny mirror to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! This mirror is so funny. Peek at it and see!" Tom peeked at the mirror and saw his face look very silly too. They both laughed and laughed at their funny faces. But then, something unexpected happened. When they looked away from the mirror, their faces stayed funny! Lily's smile was still big and Tom's eyes were still wide. They started to worry. "What do we do now?" asked Tom. Lily thought for a moment and said, "Let's peek at the mirror one more time." They peeked at the mirror again and their faces went back to normal. They were so happy and decided to only use the funny mirror for special times.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play in the park. She saw a big, hard rock that she wanted to climb. Lucy's mom said, "Promise me you will be careful." Lucy said, "I promise, Mom!" Lucy started to climb the rock. She was having fun and felt very happy. Suddenly, the rock started to move! It was not a rock, but a big turtle! The turtle was moving very slowly, making a funny motion. The turtle said, "Hello, Lucy! I am Tommy the Turtle. I was sleeping, but your climbing woke me up. Let's be friends and play together!" Lucy was surprised but happy. She and Tommy played all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Every day, Tim would go to the park and play with his friends. Today, Tim wanted to show his friends something special. In the park, Tim found an ancient rock. It was big and round. "Look what I found!" Tim said to his friends. "Wow!" they said. "What can this rock do?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's see if it can yield something!" Tim and his friends pushed the rock together. Suddenly, the rock started to shake and roll. It opened up and out came a big, friendly dinosaur! The dinosaur said, "Thank you for freeing me! I've been stuck in that rock for a very long time!" Tim and his friends were so surprised! They played with the dinosaur all day long. At the end of the day, the dinosaur said, "I must go back to my home now. But I'll always remember the fun we had today!" Tim and his friends waved goodbye and knew they had a very special story to tell.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like the dark. He always wanted the light to be on. One night, Tim went to bed with the light on. In the middle of the night, something unexpected happened. Tim's mom came in and shut the light off. Tim woke up and saw that the light was off. He felt scared, but he was a brave boy. He thought he could be brave like his big sister. Tim got out of bed and went to the kitchen. He saw a big, disgusting bug on the floor. Tim was not scared of the bug. He thought the bug was funny. He picked up the big bug and put it in a jar. Then, he went back to his room and turned the light back on. With the light on, Tim looked at the bug in the jar. The bug was not a bug at all! It was a tiny, friendly fairy. The fairy said, "Thank you for saving me, Tim!" Tim was so happy that he had been brave. He let the fairy go, and she turned his room into a magical place with lots of twinkling lights. Tim was never scared of the dark again.

Lily loved waffles. She liked to eat them with butter and honey. Every morning, her mom would bring her a waffle for breakfast. Lily would smile and say, "Thank you, mom. I love waffles." One day, Lily's mom had to go to work early. She told Lily, "I'm sorry, honey. I don't have time to make you a waffle today. But I left some cereal and milk for you in the kitchen. You can eat that instead." Lily was sad. She did not like cereal. She wanted a waffle. Lily went to the kitchen and saw the cereal and milk. She did not want to eat them. She looked around and saw a box of frozen waffles in the freezer. She had an idea. She took out a frozen waffle and put it in the toaster. She waited for it to pop up. She thought it would be warm and crispy like her mom's waffles. But when the toaster popped, the waffle was still cold and hard. Lily did not know how to use the toaster. She tried to bite the waffle, but it hurt her teeth. She spit it out and cried. She wished her mom was here to bring her a waffle. She heard a knock on the door. She wiped her tears and opened it. It was her dad. He had come home from work early. He saw Lily's sad face and the frozen waffle on the plate. He asked, "What's wrong, sweetie? Where's your mom?" Lily told him what happened. She said, "Mom had to go to work. She left me cereal and milk. I don't like cereal. I wanted a waffle. I found a waffle in the freezer. I put it in the toaster. But it did not work. The waffle is cold and hard. It hurts my teeth. I want a waffle, dad. A warm and crispy waffle." Her dad hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I know you love waffles. But frozen waffles are not the same as mom's waffles. They need to be cooked longer and with more heat. You can't eat them like that. They are rare. And rare waffles are not good. But don't worry. I have a surprise for you. Come with me." He took her hand and led her to the car. He drove her to a place where they made waffles. He ordered two waffles with butter and honey. He gave one to Lily and said, "Here you go, Lily. A warm and crispy waffle. Just like mom's. I hope you like it." Lily took a bite and smiled. She said, "Thank you, dad. I love waffles. And I love you."
