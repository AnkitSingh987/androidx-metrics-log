Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to read books. He had a hidden spot in the garden where he would sit and read. Max had a hurt heel, so he could not run and play with the other dogs. One day, Max found a magic book in his hidden spot. The book said that it could make wishes come true. Max wished for his hurt heel to be better so he could play with his friends. He read the magic words out loud and hoped it would work. The next day, Max woke up and his heel was all better! He was so happy. Max ran outside to play with his friends. They had so much fun, and Max was able to run and play just like the other dogs. The magic book had made his wish come true!

One day, a boy named Tim found a hidden soap. The soap was in a box under his bed. Tim was very happy. He loved soap because it made his hands clean and smelled good. Tim took the soap to his friend, Sam. He said, "Look, Sam! I found hidden soap! Let's act like we are washing our hands." They both pretended to wash their hands with the soap. They laughed and had fun. Later, Tim and Sam showed the hidden soap to their friends. They all acted like they were washing their hands. They had a great time playing with the hidden soap.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play with cubes. They have many cubes of different colors and sizes. They can make houses, towers, cars and animals with their cubes. One day, Lila and Tom decide to make a big cube with all their cubes. They work hard and stack the cubes on top of each other. They make a cube that is bigger than them. They are very proud of their cube. "Look at our cube!" Lila says. "It is the best cube ever!" "Yes, it is!" Tom agrees. "Can we go inside the cube?" "Sure, let's go!" Lila says. They crawl inside the cube. It is dark and cozy. They feel safe and happy. They close their eyes and dream. They dream of flying in the sky, swimming in the ocean, exploring the jungle and visiting the moon. They have fun in their dreams. They see many things and meet many friends. They wake up and smile. They are glad. They hug each other and say: "That was a good dream!" "Yes, it was! Let's do it again!" They close their eyes and dream some more. They are happy in their cube.

One day, a little fish was swimming in the sea. The fish saw a big boat. The boat was mixing the water. The fish was scared. The fish swam to the boat and said, "Please stop mixing the water. I am sorry, but it is hard for me to swim." The man on the boat said, "I am sorry too, little fish. I did not know I was making it hard for you." The man stopped the boat. Then, something unexpected happened. The fish grew very big and said, "Thank you for being kind. I am a magic fish. Because you were sorry and stopped, I will give you a wish." The man wished for a new boat that did not mix the water. The fish made the wish come true. The man and the fish were both happy. The man learned to always be kind and think of others.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom loved to sign his name on big papers. One day, Tom heard a loud sound. It was thunder! Tom was scared of the thunder, so he ran to his small, cozy house. He hid under his bed and waited for the thunder to stop. Tom's cat, Fluffy, came to keep him company. They both stayed under the bed, feeling safe and warm. Soon, the thunder went away and the sun came out. Tom and Fluffy came out from under the bed. They were happy the thunder was gone. Tom went back outside and signed his name on a big paper. The town was peaceful once again, and Tom and Fluffy lived happily ever after.
