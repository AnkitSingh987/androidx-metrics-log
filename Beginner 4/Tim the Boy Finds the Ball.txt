Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a round ball on the grass. Tim picked up the ball and started to play with it. He was very happy. As Tim played, he observed a girl sitting alone on a bench. She looked sad and ashamed. Tim wanted to make her feel better. So, he went to the girl and said, "Hi, do you want to play with me and my round ball?" The girl looked at Tim and smiled. She said, "Yes, I would love to play with you." They played together and had a lot of fun. The girl was not sad and ashamed anymore. Tim was happy that he made a new friend.

John was a clever boy who liked to dream. One day, he watched as his dad put on a special zip. John wanted one too, so he asked where it came from. Dad told him it was from a special shop, so John closed his eyes and dreamed of going. The next day, Dad and John went to the special shop. It was filled with different kinds of zips in lots of bright colours! John chose a red one and he was so excited. He couldn't wait to show off his new zip to his friends. John wore his zip every day and it made him feel clever. He would tell all of his friends that his Dad got it from the special shop. He was so proud of it. John loved his zip and it made him happy. He still dreams of going back to that special shop someday.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a big lion. The lion had many friends because he was very popular. All the animals loved him because he was kind and always ready to help. One day, the lion heard a tiny voice. It was a little rat. The rat said, "Help me, please! I'm stuck in a hole." The lion bent down and gently pulled the rat out of the hole. The rat was so happy and said, "Thank you, lion! You are so kind." The next day, the lion was caught in a big net. He roared loudly for help. All the animals heard him, but they were too small to help. Then, the little rat came and saw the lion. The rat said, "Don't worry, lion! I will help you." The rat used his sharp teeth to cut the net. The lion was free, and he roared with joy. From that day on, the lion and the rat were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a large, round ball. He loved to play with his ball every day. One day, while Tim was playing with his ball, a big, friendly dog came up to him. The dog wagged its tail and barked at Tim. Tim wanted to talk to the dog, so he said, "Hi, dog! My name is Tim. Do you want to play with my ball?" The dog barked again and nodded its head. Tim threw the large ball, and the dog ran to catch it. They played together all day, and they became best friends. Tim and the dog talked and laughed as they played with the ball. They were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy skull that he liked a lot. Tim would take the skull with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big seat on the playground. Tim wanted to put his skull on the seat, so he climbed up and put it there. Tim was happy to see his skull on the big seat. A girl named Sue saw Tim and his skull. Sue felt envious because she didn't have a toy like that. She asked Tim if she could play with the skull too. Tim said yes, and they played together with the skull on the big seat. They became good friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a mommy who had a lovely lime. Mommy loved her lime, but one day she thought of a great idea. She decided to lend her lovely lime to her neighbor. Mommy told her neighbor to take good care of the lime. The neighbor said she would do so. She was filled with joy and decided to use the lime to make a yummy treat. The neighbor cut up the lime and squeezed out fresh juice to make a delicious limeade. The neighbor thanked Mommy for lending her the lovely lime. Mommy smiled and said, "You're welcome." The neighbor enjoyed the refreshing limeade, while Mommy was happy knowing her lovely lime was being put to good use.
