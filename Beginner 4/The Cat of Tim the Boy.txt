Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to find a treat. He went outside and saw a big tree. Under the tree, he saw a small box. Tim was very happy and wanted to see what was inside the box. He tried to open the box, but it was hard. So, he used a stick to poke it. The box opened and a small cat jumped out! Tim was surprised and didn't expect to see a cat. The cat looked happy and started to play with Tim. Tim and the cat played all day. When it was time to go home, Tim found a treat in his pocket. He gave it to the cat as a thank you for playing with him. The cat was happy and they became best friends. The treat was not for Tim, but for his new friend, the cat.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to test his new equipment. He had a big red ball and a small blue ball. He was so excited to play with them. Tim went to the park with his mom to test his balls. In the park, Tim met a big dog. The dog was very big and loud. Tim was frightened. He said to his mom, "Mom, I am scared of the dog." But his mom told him not to worry. "The dog is nice, Tim. Just play with your balls and have fun." Tim tried to play with his balls, but the big dog came closer. The dog took the big red ball in his mouth and ran away. Tim was very sad and cried. His mom hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. We will get you a new ball." But Tim knew that his big red ball was gone forever, and he was still frightened of the big dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was a selfish girl. She did not like to share her toys with her friends. One day, Amy went to the park with her mom. At the park, there were many kids playing and having fun. Amy saw a nice seat on a swing. She ran to the swing and sat on it. A boy named Tim came to her and said, "Can I play on the swing too?" Amy did not want to share the swing. She said, "No, this is my swing. You cannot play on it." Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Amy off the swing. She fell on the ground and started to cry. Tim, the boy, came to help her. He said, "It's okay. We can share the swing and play together." Amy learned that sharing is good and not to be selfish. From that day on, Amy and Tim became best friends and always shared their toys and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a big parade. All the animals were there. They all march together. They were having so much fun. The sun was shining and everyone was happy. At the end of the parade, there was a big table with treats. There was chocolate on the table. The animals loved chocolate. They all wanted to eat it. They all took a piece and started to eat. But, oh no! The chocolate was disgusting. The animals made funny faces. They didn't like it at all. They all laughed at the silly chocolate. The parade was still fun, even with the disgusting chocolate.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. He hopped in the sun, and he hopped in the rain. Hop, hop, hop, went Bob the bunny. One day, Bob found a dirty plant. The plant was sad because it was dirty. Bob wanted to help the plant. So, he hopped to the river to get some water. He hopped back to the plant with the water in his mouth. Bob gave the water to the plant. The plant was happy and started to grow. It was not dirty anymore. Bob was happy too. He hopped around the plant, and they became friends. Now, Bob and the plant hop and play together every day.
