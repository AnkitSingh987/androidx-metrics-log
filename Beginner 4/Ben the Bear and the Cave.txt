Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bear and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a little bear named Ben. He lived in a warm cave with his mom and dad. They loved to play together and eat yummy food. One day, Ben went to sleep in his cozy bed. The next morning, Ben woke up and felt a cold wind. He felt pain in his head. He did not like this. He was sad and did not want to play. His mom and dad saw him and wanted to help. Mom and dad bear hugged him tight to make him warm. They gave him warm soup to eat. Soon, the pain was gone and Ben felt better. They played all day and were happy again.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in the garden. They see many flowers and bugs. They see a bee on a rose. Tom wants to touch the bee. "Look, Lily, a bee!" Tom says. He runs to the rose. "Tom, no!" Lily says. "Bees can sting. They do not like to be touched. You must behave." But Tom does not listen. He reaches for the bee. The bee flies away. Tom follows the bee. He is fast. Lily is scared. She runs after Tom. She is slow. She calls for Mom. "Mom, help! Tom is chasing a bee!" Mom hears Lily. She comes to the garden. She sees Tom and Lily. She sees the bee. "Tom, stop!" Mom says. "You are being silly. The bee will hurt you. Come here, now." Tom stops. He sees Mom. He is sorry. He runs to Mom. He hugs Mom. "I am sorry, Mom," Tom says. "I wanted to see the bee. I did not behave." Mom hugs Tom. She is glad he is safe. She is not angry. "It is okay, Tom," Mom says. "I know you are curious. But you must be careful. Bees are not toys. They are living things. They have feelings. They need respect." Tom nods. He understands. He looks at the bee. The bee is on another flower. It is busy. "Can I say sorry to the bee?" Tom asks. Mom smiles. She is proud of Tom. "Yes, you can," Mom says. "But you must be quiet and slow. Do not get too close. Just wave and say sorry." Tom does what Mom says. He walks to the flower. He is quiet and slow. He waves to the bee. He says sorry. The bee sees Tom. It buzzes. It does not sting. It flies away. Tom smiles. He feels better. He goes back to Mom and Lily. "Thank you, Mom," Tom says. "I learned something today. I will behave." Mom kisses Tom. She kisses Lily. She is happy. "You are welcome, Tom," Mom says. "You are a good boy. And you, Lily, you are a good girl. You tried to help your brother. You are both brave and kind. I love you both." Tom and Lily hug Mom. They love Mom. They are happy. They play in the garden. They see more flowers and bugs. They do not chase the bees. They behave.

Once upon a time there was a huge flea. He liked to peek around the world to look for fun things to do. One day he peeked into a farmer's vegetable patch and saw lots of yummy carrots. He was so excited! He jumped in and began to eat as many carrots as he could. The farmer soon saw the flea and he was not happy. He chased him away, saying that he mustn't take things that don't belong to him. The flea was scared and ran as fast as he could. Later the flea learned a valuable lesson. He realized that he should not take things without permission, no matter how tempting they might seem. He also knew that he should always ask permission first. The huge flea vowed to never forget this lesson, and everyone lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He loved to listen to all kinds of music. Whenever music was played, he would dance with so much joy. One day, his Mommy scolded him and said, "Jack, stop spoiling your music!" Jack was very sad, so he stopped moving and listened carefully. He asked, "What does spoil mean?" Mommy lovingly explained, "Spoiling means destroying. Music should be enjoyed with respect. You can dance with joy but it should never be done in a fierce way, because it may hurt someone or damage something else." Jack learned an important lesson that day. He understood that he should enjoy music with joy and respect, never spoiling it.
