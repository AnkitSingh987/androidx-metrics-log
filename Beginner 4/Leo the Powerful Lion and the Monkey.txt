Category: Beginner
Title: Leo the Powerful Lion and the Monkey

Once upon a time, there was a powerful lion named Leo. Leo lived in the jungle with his friends. One day, Leo decided to go on a trip. He needed to pack his bag with food and toys. Leo asked his friend, the monkey, to help him. "Monkey, can you help me pack my bag?" Leo asked. "I need food and toys for my trip." Monkey agreed and helped Leo pack his bag. They put bananas, apples, and oranges on one side of the bag. Then, they put Leo's favorite toys on the other side of the bag. When they were done, Leo said, "Thank you, Monkey! You are a good friend." Monkey smiled and said, "You're welcome, Leo. Have a fun trip!" Leo waved goodbye to Monkey and went on his trip, with his bag full of food and toys.

One day, a little boy named Sam was skipping down the road. He spotted a beautiful white rose growing by the side of the road. He decided to pick it and take it home with him. He said to himself, “I will give this rose to Mommy. She will be so happy!” When he got to his house, he saw that Mommy was upset. She was crying. Sam felt sad and asked, “Why are you upset, Mommy?” Mommy said, “I am upset because I lost my ring. I have looked and looked and I can’t find it.” Sam said, “I can help you. I found this pretty rose and it made me think of you. Here, take it and it will make you happy.” Mommy smiled and said, “Oh, Sam, thank you! That rose is so beautiful, it did make me happy. Let’s go and look for the ring together now.” They went back outside and together, they looked for the ring. After some searching, they found the ring and Mommy was very happy. Sam was so happy he skipped back home with Mommy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a favorite mitten. The mitten was red and very soft. Sue was proud of her mitten. One day, Sue played a game. She liked to imagine things. She imagined her mitten could talk. The mitten said, "Hello, Sue! Let's be friends!" Sue laughed and played with her mitten. Sue and her mitten had fun together. They played outside and inside. Sue was happy with her mitten. At night, Sue and her mitten went to bed. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a lazy dog named Max. Max liked to sleep all day under the big tree. He did not care about playing with his friends. One day, a little bird named Sue came to sit on Max's shoulder. Sue said, "Max, why are you so lazy? Let's play together and have fun!" Max opened his eyes and looked at Sue. He thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, Sue, let's play." Max and Sue played all day in the forest. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Max learned that it is fun to play with friends and not be lazy. From that day on, Max cared about playing and having fun with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small sheep. The small sheep lived on a farm with other animals. The small sheep liked to play with his friends, the cow and the pig. One day, a nice man came to the farm. The nice man wanted to buy the small sheep. The small sheep was happy to go with the nice man. The nice man took the small sheep to his new home. In the new home, the small sheep met a little girl. The little girl loved the small sheep very much. They played together every day. The small sheep was very happy in his new home with the little girl. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big, serious dog named Max found a box. The box had many things to decorate with. Max had an idea. He wanted to make his dog house pretty. Max was very excited. Max took the things from the box. He put them on his dog house. He put a red thing on his lip. Max looked funny. Max's friend, a small cat named Lily, saw Max. Lily laughed at Max. Max did not know why. Later, Max looked in a mirror. He saw the red thing on his lip. Max laughed too. He took the red thing off his lip. Max and Lily played with the things from the box. They had a fun day decorating Max's dog house together.
