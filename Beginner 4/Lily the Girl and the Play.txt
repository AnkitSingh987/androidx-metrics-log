Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Play

One day, a little girl named Lily went to school. She was very excited because today was the day her teacher, Miss Jane, would mention who would be in the school play. Lily wanted to be in the play so much. She thought it would be perfect for her. In the morning, Miss Jane said, "I have some big news. I will tell you who will be in the play later today." All day, Lily and her friends talked about the play. They all wanted to be a part of it. Lily said, "I hope I get to be in the play. I have been practicing at home." At the end of the day, Miss Jane told the class who would be in the play. Lily was so happy when she heard her name. She got a big part in the play. Her friend Tom said, "You will be perfect for the part. I am happy for you." Lily went home and told her mom and dad. They were very proud of her. They knew she would do a great job in the play. Lily practiced every day with Miss Jane to learn her part. She was so happy that her teacher picked her for the play.

Once there was a patient little dog. He liked to rush around and play in the sun. One day he saw a shiny wire laying in the grass. He thought it would be fun to play with, so he ran to it. He chewed and pulled and tugged, but it wasn't coming undone. He kept trying, being very patient. Finally after lots of rushing and coaxing, he managed to make the wire into a nice ball. He was very proud and ran around with it in his mouth. He had so much fun with his new wire ball.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a dog named Spot. Spot was a noisy dog. He liked to bark all day and all night. His owner, Tom, would always say, "Spot, please be quiet!" One sunny day, Spot saw a big cat. He wanted to chase it. But Tom did not allow Spot to do that. Spot was sad and asked, "Why can't I chase the cat?" Tom replied, "If you chase the cat, you will scare it. You need to be kind to other animals." Spot thought about what Tom said. He decided to be a good dog and not chase the cat. The cat became Spot's friend. They played together every day. Spot learned that being kind and quiet made more friends than being noisy. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a wet dog. The dog was wet because it was playing in a puddle. Lily wanted to help the dog, so she took it home. At home, Lily gave the dog a bath. She used a nice-smelling perfume soap. The dog liked the smell too. Lily and the dog played in the water and had fun. Then, Lily turned off the water and dried the dog. Lily and the dog became good friends. They played together every day. The dog was happy to be clean and smell good. And Lily was happy to have a new friend.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk in the woods. She saw a big, rough tree with a trunk so wide she could not hug it. Sue thought the tree was very old and wise. She wanted to talk to the tree and ask it questions. "Hello, tree," Sue said. "How old are you?" The tree did not answer, but Sue could see that it had many rings on its trunk. She knew that each ring meant one year. The tree was very old indeed. A little bird came and sat on the tree's branch. "Hello, bird," Sue said. "Can you tell me about this tree?" The bird chirped and said, "This tree has seen many things. It has seen the sun rise and set, and it has seen the stars at night. It has been here for a very long time, and it is a friend to all the animals in the woods." Sue smiled and thanked the bird. She knew that the tree was special, and she would always remember the rough trunk that she could not hug.
