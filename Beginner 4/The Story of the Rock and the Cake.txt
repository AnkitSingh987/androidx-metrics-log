Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Rock and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy rock. The rock was sad because no one wanted to play with it. One day, a little girl named Amy saw the rock and wanted to play. Amy said, "Hi, rock! Do you want to play with me?" The rock was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" Amy tried to lift the rock, but it was too heavy. She thought, "I will pinch the rock and see if it can move." When she pinched the rock, something unexpected happened. The rock turned into a big, tasty cake! Amy was so surprised and said, "Wow, you are not a rock, you are a cake!" The cake said, "Yes, I was a rock, but your pinch turned me into a cake. Now we can have a taste and share with our friends!" Amy and the cake had a fun day sharing with all their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. It was a perfect rock. It was round and smooth. The rock was in a park where kids liked to play. One day, a little boy saw the rock. He wanted to touch it. He walked to the rock and put his hand on it. The rock felt nice and warm. The boy was happy. The boy went to the park every day to touch the rock. He told his friends about the perfect rock. They all liked to touch it too. They had fun playing with the rock. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys. One day, she saw her mom using a needle to sew a button on her shirt. Sue asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "This is a needle, dear. I use it to fix clothes. But be patient, it can be sharp and we don't want you to get hurt." Later, Sue was playing with her friend Tom. Tom had a toy with a loose button. Sue said, "I can help fix that! My mom showed me a needle." Tom was happy and thanked Sue for mentioning it. Sue and Tom went to Sue's mom. They were patient as she helped them sew the button. They learned how to use a needle safely and had fun fixing the toy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a kind girl named Sue. Sue loved to serve food to her family every evening. One clear evening, Sue's friend, Tom, came to visit her. Sue said, "Hi Tom! I am making food for my family. Do you want to help me serve the food?" Tom said, "Yes! I want to help you." So, Sue and Tom served the yummy food to Sue's family together. After they ate, Sue's mom said, "Thank you, Sue and Tom, for serving the food. It was very good!" Sue and Tom felt happy. They learned that helping others can make them feel good too. And from that day, they always helped others when they could.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to travel with his toy train. One day, Tom found a whistle in his toy box. He was very happy and started to blow the whistle. Tom took his train and whistle outside to play. He met his friend, Sue. "Look at my whistle!" said Tom. Sue looked at the whistle and said, "It's so cool! Can I try?" Tom gave the whistle to Sue, but when she blew it, the sound was bitter and not fun. Suddenly, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird had heard the bitter sound of the whistle. The bird took the whistle from Sue and flew away. Tom and Sue were sad, but they learned that some things are better to be left alone. They played with the toy train and had fun together without the whistle.

Mia likes to eat waffles for breakfast. She likes them with butter and honey. She likes to cut them into small pieces and make shapes with them. Sometimes she makes a star, sometimes a heart, sometimes a flower. One day, Mia's mom says they are going to a fancy place for breakfast. Mia is excited. She puts on her pretty dress and her shiny shoes. She hopes they have waffles at the fancy place. She loves waffles. They get in the car and drive to the fancy place. It is a big building with a lot of windows. They go inside and sit at a table with a white cloth and a vase of flowers. Mia's mom says they have to wait for the waiter to come and take their order. Mia looks at the menu. She sees a lot of words she does not know. She does not see waffles. Mia feels sad. She wants waffles. She asks her mom if they have waffles. Her mom says no, they do not have waffles. They have pancakes, eggs, bacon, toast, and fruit. Mia does not like any of those things. She only likes waffles. Mia starts to cry. She does not want to eat anything else. She wants waffles. She does not like the fancy place. She wants to go home. Her mom tries to calm her down. She says they can go home and make waffles later. She says they have to remain here for a little while and be polite. She says maybe Mia can try something new and like it. Mia does not listen. She cries louder. She does not want to remain here. She does not want to try something new. She does not want to be polite. She wants waffles. She loves waffles. She does not love the fancy place. She hates the fancy place. She wants to go home. Now.
