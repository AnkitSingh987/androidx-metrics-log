Title: Lily and Max and the Cake

Lily and Max were twins who loved to share everything. They shared their toys, their clothes, and their bed. But there was one thing they did not like to share: their food. One day, their mom made them a big cake for their birthday. It was incredible. It had chocolate and cream and sprinkles on top. Lily and Max could not wait to eat it. "Can I have the first slice?" Lily asked. "No, I want the first slice!" Max said. They both reached for the knife and tried to cut the cake. But they pulled too hard and the knife slipped. The cake split in half and fell on the floor. "Oh no!" Lily and Max cried. Their mom heard the noise and came to the kitchen. She saw the mess and shook her head. "I'm sorry, mom," Lily and Max said. "It's okay, sweeties," their mom said. "Accidents happen. But you know, you don't have to fight over the cake. There is enough for both of you. Why don't you split it in half and share it?" Lily and Max looked at each other and nodded. They picked up the cake pieces and put them on two plates. They each gave half of their slice to the other. "Happy birthday, Lily," Max said. "Happy birthday, Max," Lily said. They smiled and hugged each other. Then they ate their cake and enjoyed it. It was still incredible.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play sport. His favorite sport was to kick a ball. He played with his friends every day after school. They had so much fun together. One day, Tim could not find his ball. He was sad. He wanted to play with his friends. So, Tim asked his friend, Sue, if he could borrow her ball. Sue said yes. She was a very nice friend. Tim and his friends played with Sue's ball. The sun was shining. It was a perfect day. They laughed and kicked the ball all afternoon. Tim was happy he could borrow the ball. They all had a great time playing their favorite sport.

Once there was a little green bird who really wanted to fly. He flapped his wings and tried really hard, but he just couldn't get off the ground. But then one day he saw a mosquito. It was buzzing around in the air and the little green bird thought 'I can do that too!' So he flapped his wings harder and harder until finally he was soaring above the clouds. The little green bird was so happy and he swooped and soared through the sky for hours. He even saw other flying creatures like eagles and seagulls, but none of them could fly as fast as him. The little green bird stayed up in the air all afternoon and, when the sun started to set, he flew back down to where he started. He couldn't wait to tell his friends all about his adventure. From then on, the little green bird flew every day and he was never happier.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called John. He was three years old and loved to play pretend. One day he was playing in the garden when he saw an old, thin cat. John squealed with delight and ran to the cat. He asked the cat, “What are you doing here?” The cat replied, ”I have come to play with you. Would you like to pretend to be a cat like me? We can go on adventures together.” John was so excited and gave the cat a big hug. Then he got up and started to pretend to be a cat. He ran around the garden meowing and chasing after old leaves. John and the cat had lots of fun pretending to be cats together. At the end of the day, the old, thin cat said goodbye and went on his way. John waved goodbye to his new friend and couldn't wait to pretend again.

One day, a little boy named Tom was excited to search for his toy car. He looked under his bed, but it was not there. He looked in his toy box, but it was not there either. Tom felt sad because he could not find his toy car. Then, Tom saw a cord on the floor. He thought maybe the cord would lead to his toy car. He followed the cord, and it went behind the big chair. Tom was sure his toy car was there. But when he looked behind the chair, he found a big, red ball instead of his toy car. Tom was surprised and happy to find the ball, but he still wanted to find his toy car. Tom played with the big, red ball for a while, but he did not forget about his toy car. He went to search again, and this time he found it! It was hiding under a big pillow on the couch. Tom was so excited to have both his toy car and the big, red ball to play with.
