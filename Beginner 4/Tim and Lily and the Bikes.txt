Title: Tim and Lily and the Bikes

Tim and Lily are friends. They live next to each other. They like to play with their bikes. They have red bikes. They are fast bikes. One day, they decide to have a race. They go to the park. They find a long path. They say, "Ready, set, go!" They start to speed on their bikes. They feel the wind on their faces. They laugh and shout. Tim is ahead of Lily. He thinks he will win. He looks back at Lily. He smiles. But he does not see a big rock on the path. He hits the rock. He falls off his bike. He hurts his knee. He cries. Lily sees Tim fall. She stops her bike. She runs to Tim. She says, "Are you okay, Tim?" She helps him get up. She hugs him. She says, "You are lucky. It is not a bad cut. You will be fine." Tim says, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend. You are the winner. You are faster than me." He smiles. He says, "Let's go home. We can put a band-aid on my knee. We can have some juice and cookies. We can play with our bikes again tomorrow." Lily nods. She says, "Okay, Tim. That sounds good." They walk home. They are happy. They are still friends.

Once upon a time, there was a slow lawyer. He lived in a small house with his cat, Tom. The lawyer liked to help people with their problems. One day, the lawyer and Tom went for a walk. They saw a big tree. Tom wanted to climb the tree, so the lawyer leaned on the tree to help Tom. Tom climbed the tree and saw many birds. The birds sang beautiful songs. At the end of the day, the slow lawyer and Tom went back home. They were happy because they had a fun day. The lawyer leaned on his chair and smiled. Tom sat on his lap, and they both went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was an old tree in a big park. The tree had been there for a very long time. It was friends with all the animals that lived in the park. The tree was very happy. One day, a big truck came to the park. The truck had a lot of soil in it. The truck driver wanted to put the soil near the old tree. The animals that lived in the park did not like this. They did not want the truck to disturb their friend, the old tree. The animals got together and made a plan. They talked to the truck driver and told him about their friend, the old tree. The truck driver listened to the animals and decided not to disturb the old tree. He took the soil to another part of the park. The old tree and the animals were very happy. They thanked the truck driver for not disturbing their friend, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. Sally loved grapes. One day, she found a big, sour grape in her garden. She thought it was very special. Sally wanted to marry the sour grape. She put on a pretty dress and asked the grape to be her friend. The grape said yes, and they had a fun party. Sally and the sour grape lived happily ever after. They danced, played, and ate grapes together every day. And they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little white shrimp. The shrimp lived in a big blue sea. The shrimp had many friends. They liked to play together. One day, a big fish came. The fish wanted to eat the shrimp. The shrimp was scared. The shrimp's friends wanted to help. They tried to prevent the fish from eating the shrimp. They swam fast and hid behind rocks. But the fish was too big and too fast. It found the shrimp and ate it. The shrimp's friends were very sad. They missed their friend, the white shrimp. The sea was not the same without the little white shrimp.

Lily liked to bounce. She bounced on her bed, on the sofa, on the ball. She bounced everywhere. One day, she saw a mouse in the garden. The mouse was small and brown and had a long tail. Lily wanted to play with the mouse. She ran after it, bouncing on the grass. "Hello, mouse!" Lily said. "Do you want to bounce with me?" The mouse was scared of Lily. He did not want to bounce. He wanted to hide. He ran to a hole under a big tree. Lily followed him. She bounced to the hole and looked inside. "Come out, mouse!" Lily said. "Don't be afraid. I am nice. I have a cheese for you." The mouse smelled the cheese. He was hungry. He peeked out of the hole. He saw Lily holding a yellow cheese. He also saw something else. He saw a big cat behind Lily. The cat was black and had green eyes. The cat was not nice. The cat wanted to eat the mouse. "Look out, Lily!" the mouse said. "There is a cat behind you!" Lily turned around. She saw the cat. She was surprised. She dropped the cheese. The cat jumped on the cheese. The mouse grabbed the cheese and ran back to the hole. He was safe. He was happy. He had a cheese. "Thank you, mouse!" Lily said. "You are very creative. You saved me from the cat. You are my friend." The mouse smiled. He said, "You are welcome, Lily. You are very kind. You gave me a cheese. You are my friend too." Lily and the mouse became friends. They played together in the garden. Sometimes they bounced. Sometimes they did not. They had fun. They were happy.
