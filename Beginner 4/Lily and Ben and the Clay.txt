Title: Lily and Ben and the Clay

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with clay. They make animals and shapes with clay. They have fun. One day, they have a big box of clay. They want to share the clay. But they do not agree on how to share. Lily wants to have more clay than Ben. Ben wants to have the same clay as Lily. They start to fight. They pull and push the clay. They make a mess. Mom sees the mess. She is not happy. She tells Lily and Ben to stop fighting. She tells them to clean the mess. She tells them to say sorry to each other. She tells them to share the clay nicely. Lily and Ben feel bad. They do not want to make Mom angry. They do not want to lose their friend. They say sorry to each other. They clean the mess. They share the clay nicely. They make animals and shapes with clay. They have fun.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play together in the park. One day, they see a big truck with many balloons. The truck stops near them and a man comes out. "Hello, kids! Do you want a balloon?" the man asks. "Yes, please!" Anna and Ben say. The man gives them each a balloon. Anna gets a pink one and Ben gets a blue one. They are very happy. "Thank you, mister!" they say. "You're welcome. Have fun!" the man says. He goes back to the truck and drives away. Anna and Ben hold their balloons and run to the swings. They swing high and low. They imagine they are flying in the sky with their balloons. "Look, Anna! I can see the clouds!" Ben says. "Me too, Ben! And I can see the birds!" Anna says. They laugh and enjoy the breeze. They think their balloons are very attractive. They make the park more colorful and fun. They swing for a long time. Then they hear their moms calling them. "Anna! Ben! It's time to go home!" their moms say. They stop swinging and jump off. They run to their moms with their balloons. "Did you have fun, kids?" their moms ask. "Yes, mom! We got balloons from a nice man!" Anna and Ben say. They show their moms their balloons. Their moms smile and hug them. "That's wonderful, kids! But remember, don't talk to strangers, okay?" their moms say. "Okay, mom!" Anna and Ben say. They say goodbye to each other and go home with their moms. They hold their balloons tight. They don't want to lose them. They wait for the next day to play together again. They imagine what they will do with their balloons. They are very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot. The parrot lived in a big tree. The parrot liked to talk a lot. One day, the parrot saw a tasty fruit on a branch. The parrot wanted to eat the fruit. The parrot tried to get the fruit, but it was too high. The parrot had to think. The parrot thought and thought. Then, the parrot had an idea. The parrot jumped and flapped its wings. The parrot could fly! The parrot was happy. The parrot flew up to the tasty fruit. The parrot took a bite. But, the fruit was not a fruit! It was a ball. The parrot was surprised. The parrot laughed and laughed. The parrot played with the ball. The parrot had fun.
