Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very restless. He always wanted to play and run around. One day, his mom said they were going to the grocery store to buy food. Tim was excited because he loved to go shopping with his mom. At the grocery store, Tim saw a big, red apple. He asked his mom, "What is this?" His mom took the time to explain, "That is an apple, Tim. We eat apples because they are yummy and good for us." Tim was happy to learn about the apple and wanted to buy it. As they walked through the store, Tim's mom explained about all the different foods they saw. Tim liked learning about the food and was not restless anymore. When they were done shopping, Tim and his mom went home with their groceries. Tim was excited to eat the yummy apple and share his new knowledge with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a reliable lawyer named Bob. Bob was very good at his job and always helped people. He had a big office where he worked every day. One day, Bob needed to push a heavy box. The box was full of important papers. He tried to push it, but it was too hard for him. Bob needed help. Bob's friend, Tim, came to help. Tim was strong and could push the heavy box. Together, they pushed the box to its place. Bob was happy and thanked Tim. They were a great team.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat was very independent. It liked to go on trips all by itself. It would float on the water, feeling the sun on its sail and the wind in its face. It was a very happy boat. One day, the boat saw a big fish. The fish was blue and shiny. The boat started to wonder where the fish was going. So, it decided to follow the fish. The fish swam fast, but the boat was faster. They went on a fun adventure together. After a while, the fish and the boat became good friends. They played in the water and had lots of fun. The boat was very happy to have a friend. They stayed together and had many more adventures in the big, wide sea.

Once upon a time, there was a big cliff near a small town. Many people lived in the town. They were all very nice. One day, a man needed to deliver a box to his friend on the other side of the cliff. But, he did not know how to get there. The man asked for help. He said, "Is there anyone available to help me deliver this box?" A little girl heard him and said, "I can help you!" The man was happy. So, the little girl and the man went on a walk to find a way around the cliff. They walked and walked until they found a safe path. The little girl and the man went around the cliff and delivered the box to the man's friend. The friend was very happy. The little girl and the man went back to the town. Everyone was proud of them for helping each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a cart. He loved to play with his cart all day long. One day, Tim saw a big hill. He wanted to push his cart down the hill. Tim pushed his cart down the hill. The cart went very fast. But then, the cart hit a rock. The cart broke. Tim felt bad. He didn't know what to do. Tim's friend, Sam, came over. Sam saw the broken cart. He said, "Don't feel bad, Tim. We can fix it." Tim and Sam worked together to fix the cart. When they were done, Tim's mom saw the fixed cart. She was so happy, she clapped her hands. She said, "Good job, boys!" Tim and Sam smiled and played with the cart again.

"Once upon a time, there was a little fox. It had soft fur that was the brightest red you ever did see! The fox was feeling very helpless and it just wanted someone to help it. A kind little girl came by and saw the fox. She saw how helpless it was and said, "I want to help you!" The fox looked up with eyes as big as saucers, and it nodded its head to say 'yes'. The little girl smiled and she started to gather some food for the fox. She helped the fox to feel better and she pet it gently so that it felt safe. The fox thanked the little girl and then it nudged her hand with its soft fur. It was a way of saying, "thank you!" The little girl smiled and stroked the fox's fur. After that, the fox felt happy, warm and safe. The girl and the fox stayed friends, and every time the fox saw the little girl, it would nod it's head to say, "Hello friend!"
