Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She felt lonely because she had no one to play with. She saw a big, pretty tree and decided to sit under it. As she sat there, she opened her lunch box and found a tuna sandwich. Suddenly, a funny bunny came by, hopping and skipping. "Hi, I'm Skip," said the bunny. "Do you want to play with me?" Lily was very happy and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Skip!" As they played, Lily shared her tuna sandwich with Skip. But then, something unexpected happened. Skip started to grow bigger and bigger! Soon, he was as big as the tree! "Wow, Skip! You're so big now!" said Lily. They laughed and played together, and Lily never felt lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy. He was only three years old. He wanted to play in the park, but his mum said no. The boy was frightened so he shouted. His mum said he had to stay home and learn. But the boy wanted to play. He shouted again, and his mum was mad. She shouted too and told him to do his learning. Then, she went out. The boy was alone and frightened. He peered out the window, wishing he could go outside. He shouted one last time but no one answered. When his mum came back, she was madder still. She shouted at the boy and told him he couldn't play in the park anymore. The boy felt sad, but he had to be a good boy and obey his mum. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow duck. The duck loved playing with his friends in the pond near his house. One day, he swam over to the other side of the pond, and he saw something sparkly. He got closer and found a yellow jewel! He was so excited and he wanted to show his friends. But when he tried to pick it up, it started to tear. He was sad that he had broken it. He tried to put the pieces back together, but he couldn't. He looked all around, but he couldn't find the other pieces and he started to cry. But then, his friends came and put their feathers together. The feathers were yellow too, and they built a yellow nest with the feathers. The little duck put the jewel inside the nest and smiled. He was happy that he could play with his friends and keep the yellow jewel safe. The little duck and his friends all lived happily ever after!

One day, a polite cat named Kitty went for a walk. She had a rag with her. The rag was her favorite toy. She liked to play with it all the time. While walking, Kitty met a dog named Spot. Spot saw the rag and wanted to play too. Kitty said, "Hi Spot! Do you want to play with my rag?" Spot said, "Yes, please!" They played with the rag for a long time. They had a lot of fun. But then, Kitty had to go home. She said, "Bye, Spot! I will miss you!" Spot said, "I will miss you too, Kitty!" Kitty went home with her rag. She was happy that she had a new friend. Now she could play with Spot and the rag every day.

Anna likes to swing on the big swing in the park. She can go very high and feel the wind in her hair. She laughs and sings as she swings. One day, she sees a new girl on the small swing. The girl is sad and does not talk. Anna wants to be her friend. She stops swinging and goes to the girl. "Hi, I am Anna. What is your name?" she asks. The girl looks up and says, "I am Mia. I am new here. I miss my old home and my friends." Anna says, "I am sorry. That is hard. Do you want to swing with me? The big swing is fun." Mia nods and smiles a little. She gets off the small swing and follows Anna to the big swing. Anna helps her get on and pushes her gently. "Wow, this is fun!" Mia says. "Thank you, Anna." Anna says, "You are welcome, Mia. I am happy you like it. Do you want to be my friend?" Mia says, "Yes, I do. You are very nice, Anna." They swing together and talk. They find out they like the same things. They like pink, cats, and ice cream. They also have the same phone. It is small and fit in their pockets. They show each other their phone and the pictures they have. They laugh and hug. They are happy. They have made a new friend.
