Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Storm

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a small tree with his family. One day, a big storm came to their home. The wind blew so hard that Tim's tree started to shake. Tim's mom said, "Tim, stay close and call if you need help!" Tim was scared, but he listened to his mom. The storm was very loud, and it made the tree bend. When the storm was over, Tim saw that some of his friends' homes were broken. Tim felt sorry for his friends. He said, "I am sorry your homes are broken. Can I help you fix them?" And so, Tim and his friends worked together to fix their homes. They were happy to have each other's help.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved candy so much. One day, he saw a big jar of candy on the top shelf in the kitchen. Tim had an idea. He would get the candy when his mom was not looking. Tim found a chair and put it by the shelf. He stood on the chair and reached up high. He could almost touch the jar. Tim jumped and got the jar. But, the jar was very heavy. Tim's hands twist and the jar fell down. The jar hit the ground and broke into many pieces. Candy and broken glass were all over the floor. Tim was sad and scared. He knew he should have asked for help. When Mom came in, she saw the mess. She was not mad but told Tim to be careful next time. They cleaned up together and Mom gave Tim a piece of candy. Tim learned to ask for help when he needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit. The rabbit lived in a deep hole in the woods. The rabbit had many friends, like birds and bugs. One day, the rabbit heard a loud noise. The rabbit began to wonder what the noise was. The rabbit asked his friends if they knew what the noise was. The birds said, "We don't know, but we can fly up high and see." The bugs said, "We don't know, but we can crawl on the ground and listen." The rabbit waited for his friends to find out. The birds flew up high and saw a big tree falling down. They told the rabbit, "A big tree is falling down. That is the loud noise!" The rabbit and his friends all ran away from the falling tree. They were safe and happy. The rabbit was glad he had friends to help him when he wondered about things.

One day, a lovely girl named Lily went to a fun party. At the party, there were many balloons. Lily loved balloons so much. She saw a big, red balloon that she wanted to play with. But her mom said, "Wait, Lily. We will play with it later." Lily tried to resist playing with the big, red balloon. She played with other toys and ate yummy cake. But she always looked at the big, red balloon. She knew she had to be patient and listen to her mom. Finally, it was time for a big surprise. Lily's mom said, "Now we can play with the big, red balloon!" Lily was so happy. The big, red balloon was filled with small, fun toys for all the kids at the party. When they popped the balloon, toys fell out for everyone to play with. Lily knew she did a good job waiting, and she had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a sweet squirrel named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends all day long. They would climb trees, run around, and laugh together. One day, Tim found a big acorn on the ground. He was so happy! He wanted to show his friends his new acorn. So, he picked it up and ran to find them. When he found his friends, he said, "Look at my big acorn!" Tim's friend, a bird named Sam, had an idea. "Let's play a game with the acorn!" he said. They decided to play a game where they would shoot the acorn into a hole in a tree. They took turns trying to shoot the acorn into the hole. Tim, Sam, and their friends had so much fun playing with the acorn all day long.
