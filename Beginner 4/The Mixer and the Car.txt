Category: Beginner
Title: The Mixer and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a big mixer. The mixer was not normal. It could talk and roar! The mixer lived in a small house with a little girl and her mom. One day, the little girl was playing with her toy car. The mixer wanted to play too. So, the mixer roared, "Let's play together!" The little girl jumped and said, "You can talk and roar? That's not normal!" The little girl and the mixer played all day. They had so much fun. The mixer roared with joy. At the end of the day, the little girl hugged the mixer and said, "I'm happy you're not normal. You're my best friend." And they lived happily ever after.

Sara loves to play with her pink ball. She likes to throw it high in the air and catch it. She likes to roll it on the grass and kick it. She likes to bounce it on the pitch and make it go fast. But one day, her ball got a hole. She did not see the sharp rock on the pitch. When she bounced her ball, it hit the rock and made a hiss. The air came out of the ball and it went flat. Sara was very sad. She did not want to lose her pink ball. She ran to her dad and showed him her ball. She asked him to repair it. Her dad looked at the hole and shook his head. He said it was too big to repair. He said he was sorry, but she needed a new ball. Sara cried. She did not want a new ball. She wanted her pink ball. Her dad hugged her and said he had an idea. He took her to the garage and gave her some paint. He said she could paint anything she wanted on a new ball. He said it would be her special ball. Sara thought for a moment. She decided to paint a pink heart on the new ball. She said it would remind her of her old ball and her dad. She painted the heart carefully and waited for it to dry. Then she thanked her dad and ran to the pitch. She bounced her new ball and smiled. It was not the same as her pink ball, but it was still fun. She threw it high in the air and caught it. She rolled it on the grass and kicked it. She bounced it on the pitch and made it go fast. She was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a nation. It was a nice place, with happy people who smiled a lot. But then something crazy happened! Evil monsters came in and started to destroy everything. The houses, the roads, even the gardens! People were so scared and sad. But then one brave hero arrived and fought off the monsters! He drove them away and the nation was safe again. Everyone was happy and said thank you to the brave hero. Everyone lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, there was a cool pirate named Bob. Bob had a big ship and a big hat. He also had a skull on his flag. Bob liked to sail on the sea and find treasure. One day, Bob met another pirate named Tom. Tom had a small ship and a small hat. Tom wanted to be friends with Bob. He said, "Let's invite each other to our ships and share our treasure." Bob did not like the idea. He wanted all the treasure for himself. So, he said, "No, I will not invite you to my ship. I will take your treasure." Tom was sad and angry. Tom and Bob had a big fight with their ships. They shot big balls at each other's ships. In the end, both ships broke and sank. All the treasure was lost in the sea, and Bob and Tom had nothing left.

One morning a bright sun rose in the sky. As the sun rays touched the lake, the swan woke up. She felt very happy that it was a beautiful day. The swan started swimming around the lake. She was so graceful and her feathers seemed to sparkle in the sun. Everything around her was so bright and beautiful. The swan looked up in the sky and saw a big white cloud. It looked like a castle and the swan was curious to explore. She started to flap her wings and flew towards the majestic cloud. She flew around the cloud several times, before she flew back down to the lake. As she touched the surface of the lake she said goodbye and went back to sleep.
