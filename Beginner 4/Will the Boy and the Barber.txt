Category: Beginner
Title: Will the Boy and the Barber

Once there was a chubby little boy named Will. He loved going to the barber with his dad. One day, Will and his dad went to the barber and Will was so excited. The barber was funny and he made Will laugh. When they came back the next week, Will expected the barber to make him laugh again. The barber had a surprise for him. He gave Will a toy plane and said, "You can fly it around the room!" Will laughed and took the plane. At the next visit, the barber had another surprise. He gave Will two tickets to a special show. He said, "It's your birthday present!" The barber winked, and Will laughed. Will and his dad had a wonderful time at the show. After the show, Will told the barber thank you and laughed. The barber said, "It's my pleasure." Will and his dad went back to the barber every week, and with each visit the barber always brought a special surprise. Will's dad said, "The barber is sure good to you!" Will laughed, and said, "He sure is!"

Once upon a time, a long, long train lived in a small town. The train loved to go fast and make steam. The train had many friends, like the birds and the trees. They would often discuss fun things to do together. One day, the train saw a big hill. The train thought, "I want to go up that hill!" The train told the birds and the trees about the big hill. They all discussed how the train could go up the hill. The birds said, "You will need a lot of steam to go up the hill!" The next day, the train got ready to go up the hill. It made a lot of steam and went very fast. The train remembered what the birds said, so it made even more steam. The train went up the hill and reached the top! The train was so happy and thanked the birds and the trees for their help. They all celebrated the train's big climb and had a fun day together.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He was very excited because he loved to play with his toys. He had cars, dolls, and his favorite toy, a big dinosaur. Tim liked to make the dinosaur walk and roar like a real one. As Tim played, he noticed something on the floor. It was a small, shiny rock. He picked it up and looked at it. It was very pretty. He thought maybe his dinosaur would like the rock too. So, he put the rock next to his dinosaur and pretended they were friends. Tim and his dinosaur played with the rock all day. They had so much fun together. Tim was very happy he found the rock and made a new friend for his dinosaur. And the dinosaur was happy too, with its new friend, the shiny rock.

Once upon a time, there was a plant. The plant was not high. It wanted to be high. So, it tried to grow every day. One day, the plant met a bird. The bird said, "I can help you succeed. I can take you high in the sky." The plant was happy. It wanted to go high with the bird. The bird took the plant in its beak. They went high in the sky. The plant was so happy. But then, something bad happened. The bird dropped the plant. The plant fell down, down, down. It did not succeed to be high. The plant was sad.

One day, a boy named Tim found an unusual helmet. It was big and shiny. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam!" said Tim. "I found a big helmet. It is not like other helmets." Sam was excited. "Wow, Tim! That is a very different helmet. Can I try it on?" Tim said, "Yes, you can try it on, Sam." When Sam put on the helmet, it made a lock sound. They could not take it off. Tim and Sam were scared. They ran to Tim's mom for help. Tim's mom saw the helmet and said, "Don't worry, boys. I know how to unlock it." She pushed a button on the helmet, and it came off. Tim and Sam were happy. They thanked Tim's mom and played with the unusual helmet all day.
