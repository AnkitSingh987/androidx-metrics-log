Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his red ball. He would throw it high up in the sky and watch it come back down. The sky was very clear that day. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He was so excited to play with his red ball. He gave the ball a big throw to start his playtime. But this time, the ball went too far away. Tim was sad because he might lose his ball. Tim looked and looked for his red ball. He asked his friends to help him find it. After a while, they saw the red ball near a big tree. Tim was so happy that he didn't lose his ball. He said thank you to his friends and they all played together with the red ball under the clear sky.

Johny was walking in the park one hot day. He noticed a big eraser lying on the grass. It was too big for him to carry, but he decided to stand and stare at it for some time. Johny heard a soft voice behind him. "Is that your epaser?" The voice said. Johny turned around and saw an old man there. The man was wearing a big hat. Johny shook his head. "No, it's not mine," he said. The old man smiled. "Ah, that's too bad! I was hopping you found it." Johny couldn't understand what the old man meant. "If that was your eraser, it would have saved you a lot of trouble!" The old man said. Johny didn't understand. The old man said he meant it was a special eraser - a magic eraser. He told Johny that if he touched the eraser it would take away anything that made him scared or hurt. Johny thought this sounded too good to be true. He stepped forward to touch it… but as soon as his finger made contact with the eraser, a big gust of hot wind blew it away. Johny was sad and disappointed. He turned to the old man for an explanation, but the old man was gone. He had vanished. Johny never saw him again.

One day, a thin cat named Sam saw a faucet. The faucet was not on, but Sam wanted to play with the water. He tried to act like a person and turn the faucet on with his paw. But he could not do it. A little girl named Lucy saw Sam trying. She said, "Do you need help, Sam?" Sam meowed and nodded his head. Lucy turned on the faucet for Sam, and water came out. Sam was happy and played with the water. After playing, Sam and Lucy were both wet. They laughed and had fun. Sam was glad he had a friend like Lucy to help him. They played together every day.

One day, a boy and a girl went to the beach. They wanted to play a game. They decided to race to the water. The boy said, "I will win!" The girl said, "No, I will win!" They started to run, but the boy tripped on a clear ball. The girl stopped and helped him up. They both laughed and walked to the water together. They did not care who won. Just then, a big wave came and splashed them. They were both very wet, but they laughed even more. The clear ball had made a new game for them. They played with the ball, and had fun all day at the beach.

Lily and Ben were playing with their dolls in the garden. They liked to pretend that their dolls were their friends and they had tea parties and adventures with them. Lily had a pink doll with long hair and a dress. Ben had a blue doll with short hair and a hat. One day, a new girl moved in next door. Her name was Mia and she had a yellow doll with curly hair and glasses. She saw Lily and Ben playing and wanted to join them. She knocked on the fence and said, "Hello, can I play with you?" Lily and Ben looked at her and smiled. They said, "Yes, you can play with us. We are having a tea party. What is your doll's name?" Mia said, "My doll's name is Sunny. She is very smart and funny. She likes to read books and tell jokes." Lily and Ben said, "Wow, Sunny sounds very nice. Our dolls are called Rosie and Max. They like to dance and sing. Come, we have some cookies and juice for you and Sunny. Welcome to our garden." Mia and Sunny climbed over the fence and joined Lily and Ben. They sat on a blanket and shared their cookies and juice. They talked and laughed and played with their dolls. They had a lot of fun. But then, Mia said something that made Lily and Ben sad. She said, "You know, Sunny is not a real doll. She is a fake doll. She is made of plastic and cloth. She cannot talk or move or feel. She is just a toy. I only pretend that she is my friend because I have no real friends." Lily and Ben looked at Mia and said, "But Mia, we are your real friends. We like you and Sunny. We don't care if your doll is fake or not. We care about you and your memory. You can always play with us and our dolls. We are happy to have you here." Mia looked at Lily and Ben and felt a warm feeling in her heart. She said, "Thank you, Lily and Ben. You are very kind and sweet. You are my real friends. And Sunny is my real friend too. She is not fake to me. She is my memory of my grandma. She gave me Sunny before she went to heaven. She told me to always smile and be brave. She told me to find good friends like you." Lily and Ben hugged Mia and said, "We are sorry about your grandma. She sounds very nice. We are glad that you have Sunny to remember her. We are glad that you found us. We love you and Sunny. You are welcome to our garden anytime."
