Title: The Bird and the Clouds

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. His wings were so big that he could not wait to try them out. He was ready to fly. One sunny morning he said to his Mom, "I'm ready to count the clouds!" She smiled at him and said, "You can try. But don't forget to come back soon!" He spread out his wings and started to fly. Higher and higher he went, counting the clouds as he went along. Soon, he was so high that he could see all the way across the sky. He flew very fast. He looped and twirled and did all kinds of tricks. But then, it was time to come back home. He opened his wings and started to fly back down to the ground. Finally, he reached the ground and said, "I counted all the clouds!" His Mom was happy and hugged him tight. The little bird had had a wonderful adventure and couldn't wait to go back and count the clouds again.

Once upon a time, there was a little wolf. The wolf liked to stare at interesting things. One day, he saw a big, red ball. The ball was very interesting to the wolf. The wolf wanted to play with the ball, so he went closer. He stared at the ball and it moved. The wolf was happy and jumped around the ball. He wanted to play all day with the big, red ball. But soon, the sky turned dark and it started to rain. The wolf was sad because the ball was getting wet. He tried to take the ball home, but it was too big. The rain made the ball slippery and it rolled away. The little wolf tried to find the ball, but he couldn't. The wolf was very sad and he never saw the interesting ball again.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. He was so fat that he could not jump on the big chair. One day, Tom saw a little bug on the floor. He wanted to catch the bug with his finger. But the bug was too fast for Tom. Tom said, "I will get you, little bug!" The bug just laughed and said, "You are too fat to catch me, Tom!" This made Tom very sad. He wanted to prove the bug wrong. Tom had an idea. He found a wet cloth and began to wipe the floor. As he wiped, the floor became slippery. The little bug could not run fast on the slippery floor. Tom used his finger to catch the bug. "Ha! I got you!" said Tom. The little bug was surprised. "You are smart, Tom. I'm sorry I called you fat," said the bug. Tom let the bug go, and they became friends. From that day on, Tom and the bug played together, and Tom learned that being fat did not mean he could not do fun things.

Once upon a time there was a little boy, who wanted to go for a swim. He asked his mum if he could go to the pool, but she said that he had to take care of his chores first. The little boy sighed, but he concsented. He tidied his room and made sure it was clean. Then he started to mow the lawn, but the lawnmower stopped working. The little boy was quite angry and said, "I don't understand why this happened". His mum said, "You must learn to mind your temper. Sort your problems before getting mad". The little boy went to his dad and asked him to help him fix the lawnmower. Dad patiently helped fix the mower and they went to the pool and enjoyed a nice swim. The next day, the little boy's dad showed him the navy, their soldiers and ships. He reminded the little boy to always mind his language and behaviour, as navy had to always be self-disciplined, respectful and clean. That day, the little boy realised the importance of following the rules, keeping clean and minding his actions.

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. He would hop in the grass, in the park, and all around town. People liked to watch him hop, as it made them happy. One day, Bob found a pattern on the ground. It looked like a game. He decided to hop on the pattern. He hopped on the first spot, then the second, and so on. People came to watch him hop on the pattern. They clapped and cheered for him. Bob was very happy. But then, a big dog saw Bob hopping on the pattern. The dog wanted to play too. The dog ran to the pattern and tried to hop like Bob. But the dog was too big and clumsy. He stepped on Bob by accident. Bob was hurt, and he couldn't hop anymore. The people were sad, and the dog felt bad for hurting Bob.
