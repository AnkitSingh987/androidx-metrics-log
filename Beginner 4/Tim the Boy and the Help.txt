Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Help

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big box near his house. Tim was confused. He did not know what was in the box. He thought it was a mystery. Tim wanted to find out what was inside the box. He tried to open it, but it was too hard. He shouted for help. His friend, Sam, came running to see what was wrong. Sam tried to help Tim open the box, but they could not do it. They shouted for more help. More friends came to help Tim and Sam. They all tried to open the box, but it was still too hard. The box shook and made a loud noise. Everyone was scared and ran away. The mystery was never solved, and the big box stayed closed forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big sun. The sun loved to shine very bright. It made everything look good and happy. In a small town, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy was a good girl who liked to help others. One day, there was a race in the town. The prize was a big, shiny ball. Amy wanted to win the prize. She ran very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. A little cat came into the race. The cat was lost and scared. Amy stopped running to help the cat. She picked it up and started to walk. Everyone passed Amy, but she did not care. She wanted to help the cat. In the end, Amy did not win the race. But the people in the town saw how good she was. They gave her the prize anyway. Amy was very happy. She had a new friend and a shiny prize.

One day, a fat lion was walking in the forest. He was very hungry and wanted to find food. He saw a small house and went inside. The house had a big lock on the door, but the lion was able to open it. Inside the house, there was a little cat. The cat was scared of the fat lion. The lion wanted to eat the cat, but the cat had an idea. The cat said, "If you don't eat me, I will help you find more food." The fat lion agreed, and they went to look for food together. They found a big tree with lots of fruit. The lion and the cat ate the fruit and became friends. They went back to the house and the cat locked the door, so they could both be safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a thick cable. The cable was very long and strong. It lived in a big forest with many trees. The cable was friends with all the animals in the forest. One day, the cable heard a call. It was a small bird. The bird was in trouble. It was stuck in a tree. The cable wanted to help the bird. So, it moved and moved until it reached the tree. The cable helped the bird get free. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird was not a bird at all! It was a butterfly. The butterfly said thank you to the cable. The cable was happy to help a new friend.

Ben and Lily love to play outside. They like to run, jump, swing and slide. They have a lot of fun with their toys and their dog, Spot. One day, they see dark clouds in the sky. They hear a loud boom. It is thunder. They feel drops of water on their faces. It is rain. They are sad. They do not want to go inside. They want to play more. But their mom calls them. She says they have to come in. She says it is not safe to play in the storm. She says she has a surprise for them. Ben and Lily are curious. They wonder what the surprise is. They rush to the door. They do not want to miss it. They go inside and dry off. They hug their mom. She smiles and points to the window. She says, "Look, there is a rainbow!" Ben and Lily look. They see a big, bright, colorful arc in the sky. It is beautiful. It has red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet. They are amazed. They have never seen a rainbow before. They thank their mom for the surprise. They say they love her. She says she loves them too. She says they can go back outside when the rain stops. She says the rainbow is a sign of hope and promise. She says she is always there for them. She says she is dependable. Ben and Lily nod. They know their mom is dependable. They know she always keeps her word. They are happy. They hug their mom again. They wait for the rain to stop. They hope to see another rainbow.
