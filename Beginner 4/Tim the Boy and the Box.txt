Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Box

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, empty box. Tim wanted to play with the box. He thought, "I can bury this box in the sand." So, he started to dig a big hole. As Tim was digging, he found a snake. The snake said, "Hi, I am Sam the Snake. Can you help me find my home?" Tim was surprised but wanted to help. So, he stopped digging and went with Sam the Snake. They looked and looked for Sam's home. Then, they found a twisty tree. Sam said, "This is my home! Thank you, Tim!" Tim was happy to help. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy was only 3 years old, but she was very brave. One day, she saw a cage on the ground with a little bird inside it. Daisy wanted to help the bird, so she opened the cage and the bird flew away. Daisy was so happy. She had been successful in helping the bird, and she danced around with glee. But then night came and the bird couldn't find its way home. Daisy was worried, so she comforted it with a hug. The bird was so grateful that it snuggled up close to Daisy and they slept together throughout the night. In the morning, the bird found its way home. Daisy hugged it goodbye and watched it fly away with a smile on her face. She had been successful in helping the bird and she was so happy that she had been able to give it comfort.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He loved to make it go fast and pretend he was a cop. He felt lucky to have his toy car. As Tim played, he saw a big dog come into his yard. The dog looked lost and scared. Tim wanted to help the dog, so he said, "Hello, dog! I am a cop. Do you need help?" The dog wagged its tail and barked. Tim knew the dog was saying yes. So, Tim and the dog went to find the dog's home. They walked and walked until they found a house with a picture of the dog on it. Tim said, "This is your home, dog!" The dog barked happily and went inside. Tim felt lucky that he could help the dog. He went back to his yard and played with his toy car, feeling like a real cop.

The toddler went outside. He was feeling calm and saw a bright white bird. He smiled and nodded to it. But suddenly, the sky got dark. The toddler felt scared and the white bird flew away. Rain started pouring down and the wind started to blow. The toddler didn’t know what to do. But then he saw a big tree and he ran to it. The big tree kept him calm and safe from the wind. He nodded at the tree in thanks. He waited for the storm to pass and when it finally did, the sun was shining again. The white bird returned and the toddler smiled and nodded. The bird flew up and the toddler went back home. He was happy and calm.

John had a very difficult problem. He didn't know how he was going to support himself and his family. John sat at the kitchen table, holding his head in his hands, when his wife came in. "What's wrong my love?" She asked. John looked up. "I don't know how to support us." His wife thought for a moment, then grabbed a bowl from the cupboard. She filled it with some coins and put it on the table. "This money should help you," she said. John was surprised. He gave her a hug. "That's so kind of you. Thank-you!" He was grateful that his wife had supported him at such a difficult time.

Once upon a time, there was a wild bird named Tim. Tim was not happy in his cage. He wanted to escape and fly free in the sky. He talked to his friend, the patient cat named Sam. "I want to escape and fly," Tim said. Sam listened and thought of a plan. One day, the door of the cage was open. Tim thought this was his chance to escape. He flew out of the cage and into the sky. He was so happy to be free. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a big storm coming. He was scared and didn't know what to do. He missed his safe cage. Sam saw Tim in trouble and called out, "Tim, come back! I will help you." Tim flew back to Sam and they went inside the cage together. They were safe from the storm. From that day on, Tim and Sam were the best of friends, and Tim was happy to have a safe home.
