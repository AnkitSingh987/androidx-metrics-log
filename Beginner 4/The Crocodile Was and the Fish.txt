Category: Beginner
Title: The Crocodile Was and the Fish

One day, a big crocodile was near a small pond. He was very useful because he helped his friends. He could touch things with his long nose and tail. He liked to play with his friends, the fish, the frog, and the bird. The crocodile said, "Let's play a game! I will touch things, and you have to guess what they are." The fish, the frog, and the bird all said, "Yes! Let's play!" The crocodile touched a rock, and the bird guessed it right. Then, he touched a leaf, and the frog guessed it. It was fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The crocodile touched a big box that was in the pond. "What is that?" asked the fish. The crocodile did not know. They all looked at the big box. It was a surprise. They opened the box, and inside were many toys for them to play with. The crocodile, the fish, the frog, and the bird played with the toys all day. They were so happy to have found the big box. The crocodile said, "This was the best game ever!" And all his friends agreed.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big box of toys. He liked to keep his toys organized. He had a place for each toy, so he could find them easily. One day, Tim got a new toy. It was a bar of soap. The soap was not like his other toys. It was not soft or round. It was sharp on the edges. Tim did not know where to put the soap in his organized toy box. Tim's mom came into his room and saw the soap. She said, "Oh, Tim! The soap is for washing, not for playing." Tim learned that some things are not toys. He put the sharp soap in the bathroom, where it belonged. Now, Tim's toys were organized again, and he could play happily.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to mix colors on paper. One day, it was a gloomy day outside. The sky was gray and the wind was blowing. Tim was inside his house, looking for something fun to do. Tim found some paper and paint. He thought, "I will mix colors to make a pretty picture!" Tim mixed red and blue and made purple. He mixed yellow and blue and made green. He was very happy with his new colors. Tim's mom saw him mixing colors and said, "What a great idea, Tim! Let's hang your pretty picture on the wall." Tim smiled, and they hung the picture up. The gloomy day became brighter inside the house because of Tim's colorful painting.

Once upon a time, there was a little otter named Ollie. Ollie loved to play in the water all day. One day, Ollie saw a big fish and wanted to catch it. But the big fish was too fast for Ollie. Ollie felt ashamed that he could not catch the big fish. The next day, Ollie saw the big fish again. This time, Ollie thought of a plan. He would ask his friend, the bird, to help him catch the big fish. The bird agreed to help Ollie. They worked together and caught the big fish. But then, something unexpected happened. Ollie's mom saw them with the big fish and said, "You should not catch big fish. They are not for us to eat." Ollie's mom punished Ollie by making him let the big fish go. Ollie felt sad but he learned that he should listen to his mom. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents because they know what is best for you.

One day, a little boy named Mark went for a walk. He saw a small light in the sky. It was very pretty. Mark wanted to show his friend, Sue. He ran to find her. "Look, Sue! Look up!" said Mark. "See the light?" Sue looked up and saw the light too. "Wow! It's so pretty!" said Sue. They were both very happy. But then, the light started to get bigger and bigger. They were upset. What was happening? The big light came down to the ground. It was a big, shiny ball! Mark and Sue were not upset anymore. They were very happy. They played with the big, shiny ball all day. The light in the sky was a fun surprise for them.
