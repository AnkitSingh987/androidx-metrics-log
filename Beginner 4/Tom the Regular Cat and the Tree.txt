Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Regular Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a regular cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy and sleep in the sun. One day, Tom saw a letter on the ground. He picked it up with his paw and looked at it. It was a letter from a little girl who needed help. Tom knew he had to rescue the little girl. He ran and ran until he found her. The little girl was stuck in a tree. Tom climbed up the tree and helped the girl get down. They both were very happy. After the rescue, the little girl hugged Tom and said "thank you." Tom felt very proud. From that day on, Tom and the little girl were best friends. They played together and had lots of fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent. He loved to play with his toys and learn new things. One day, Tim saw a beautiful glass on the table. It was shiny and full of juice. He wanted to drink the juice, but the glass was too high for him to reach. Tim thought, "I need help to get the glass." He asked his big sister, Sue. Sue said, "I will help you, but first, let's get a chair to stand on." Tim and Sue found a chair and put it near the table. Tim stood on the chair and tried to reach the glass again. This time, he could touch it, but it was still a little too far away. Sue had an idea. She said, "Tim, I will hold you up, and you can grab the glass." Tim smiled and said, "Okay!" Sue lifted Tim up, and he reached for the glass. He finally got it and drank the juice. Tim was happy, and Sue was proud of her intelligent little brother. They knew that if they worked together, they could do anything.

Jim was looking at a beautiful flower. He admired its pretty colours. He thought it was the most wonderful flower he had ever seen. Suddenly, he heard a voice behind him. He turned around and saw a little girl. She was three years old. The little girl was looking at the flower too. She said, "Pretty flower!" Jim was so pleased that he smiled at her. He said, "Yes, it's very nice!" The little girl smiled back and said, "Can I touch it?" Jim was a bit worried. He said, "Sorry, you can only look at it, you can't pick it." The little girl looked disappointed. "Oh," she said. Jim felt sorry for her. He said, "I know - let's find another flower that you can pick." The little girl smiled again and they went off to find a flower she could admire and pick.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat sausages. One day, his mom made a new kind of sausage. It was not the original sausage Tim liked. It was different. Tim saw the sausage and had a question. He asked his mom, "What is this?" His mom said, "It is a new sausage. Try it, you might like it." Tim was not sure, but he wanted to try. He took a bite of the new sausage. It was good! Tim liked the new sausage too. He was happy that he tried it. Now, Tim liked both the original and the new sausage. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cave. It was a light cave. In the cave, there was a little boy. The little boy liked to work. He worked with rocks and sticks. One day, a big dog came to the cave. The dog wanted the boy's sticks. The boy did not like that. The boy and the dog did not know what to do. They both wanted the sticks. The little boy had an idea. He would work with the dog. They would share the sticks. The dog liked this idea. They worked together and played with the sticks. The cave was happy and light.
