Category: Beginner
Title: The Patient Boy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a patient chubby boy. He loved to walk around his room. One day his mom asked him if he wanted to go outside. He jumped up in excitement and ran to the door. Mom: "Can you be a patient and put your shoes on first before we walk outside?" Boy: "Yes, Mommy." The boy put on his shoes and started walking with his mom. They both enjoyed their walk outside. The boy admired all the trees, flowers and birds around his house. When they got back, the boy was tired from all the walking, but happy and content. His mom gave him a hug and said, "You were such a patient and chubby little boy today."

Once there was a dad who liked going for a walk in the park. Every day, he would take his 3 year old daughter with him. He wanted to show her all the animals in the park so one day he stopped and pointed to a tall tree. There, high up in the leaves, was a monkey. The little girl was so excited. She wanted to meet the monkey and give him a hug. But dad was careful. He told the girl, "We can't give the monkey a hug. He could hurt us." The little girl nodded, understanding that she should be careful. Then dad took her hand and started to walk away. But then, the monkey started to move. He leapt from branch to branch, following the dad and daughter. He wanted to join them on their walk! The dad was amazed and the daughter was so happy. She got to give the monkey a hug after all. They all had a wonderful time walking in the park together.

Lily and Ben went to the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and giraffes. But their favorite animal was the otter. The otter was brown and furry, and it liked to swim and play in the water. "Look, mom, the otter is sliding on its belly!" Lily said, pointing at the pool. "That looks like fun, doesn't it?" mom said, smiling. "Can we slide too, mom?" Ben asked. "Not here, Ben. The pool is for the otter, not for us. But maybe we can find a slide somewhere else in the zoo," mom said. They walked around the zoo, looking for a slide. They saw a big blue slide near the penguin house. It looked like a big wave. "Wow, mom, look at that slide!" Lily said, excited. "That looks like a great slide, Lily. Do you want to try it?" mom asked. "Yes, yes, yes!" Lily said, nodding. "What about you, Ben?" mom asked. Ben looked at the slide. He felt a little scared. It was very high and fast. "I don't know, mom. It looks scary," Ben said, shaking his head. Mom hugged Ben. "It's okay, Ben. You don't have to slide if you don't want to. You can watch Lily and me, or you can look at the penguins. They are very cute and funny," mom said. Ben looked at the penguins. They were black and white, and they waddled and splashed in the water. They made funny noises. "Okay, mom. I will look at the penguins. Maybe they will make me laugh," Ben said. Mom kissed Ben. "That's a good idea, Ben. I'm sure they will. And maybe later, you can try the slide if you feel like it. No pressure, okay?" mom said. "Okay, mom. Have fun, Lily!" Ben said. Lily ran to the slide. She climbed up the stairs and sat on the edge. She looked at mom and smiled. "Are you ready, Lily?" mom asked. "Yes, mom. I'm ready. This is going to be awesome!" Lily said. She pushed herself off the edge and slid down the blue slide. She felt the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. She screamed with joy. "Wheeeeee!" Lily said. She reached the bottom and splashed into a small pool of water. She got up and shook herself off. She looked at mom and Ben. "That was so fun, mom! That was the best slide ever! You have to try it, mom!" Lily said. She looked at Ben. "And you too, Ben! You will love it, I promise! It's not scary, it's fun! Come on, Ben, please!" Lily said. She ran to Ben and took his hand. She pulled him towards the slide. "Come on, Ben, please! Trust me, it's fun! I recommend it!" Lily said. Ben looked at Lily. He saw her happy face and sparkling eyes. He felt her warm hand and gentle pull. He looked at the slide. He felt a little curious. Maybe it was fun. Maybe he could try it. Just once. "Okay, Lily. Okay. I will try it. Just once," Ben said. Lily cheered. "Yay, Ben! Yay! You are brave, Ben! You are awesome, Ben! Come on, let's go!" Lily said. She dragged Ben to the slide. Mom followed them, smiling. She was proud of both of them. She was happy to see them have fun. She was glad they were siblings. She loved them very much.
