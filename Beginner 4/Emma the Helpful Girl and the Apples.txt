Category: Beginner
Title: Emma the Helpful Girl and the Apples

Once upon a time, there was a helpful little girl named Emma. She was always helping her mom around the house. Every day, she would go to the store to pick up groceries. One day, her mom had a special task for her. She asked Emma to buy some apples. Emma had never bought anything in the store before, so she was a little nervous. As she reached the apples, she remembered her mom's instructions. She needed to look at the price. She looked around and found a sign with the price of the apples. She was so proud of herself for remembering. When Emma reached the checkout, the shopkeeper asked if she needed some help. Emma replied, "No, thank you. I remember the price and can do this myself." The shopkeeper smiled and said, "Wow, you must be a very helpful little girl." Emma blushed and nodded. The shopkeeper handed her the apples. As she walked away, she was very proud of herself for being helpful.

One day, a little boy found a small hole in his yard. He thought it was interesting. He looked inside and saw a little mole. The mole was sad because he lost his home. The boy wanted to help the mole. The boy thought of a plan. He would replace the mole's home with a new one. He found a big box and put soft things inside. He showed the mole the new home. The mole was happy and went inside. Every day, the boy and the mole played together. They became best friends. The boy was happy that he could help the mole. The mole was happy to have a new home and a friend.

Once there was a big, slow cat named Tom. Tom had a funny mustache. He liked to play with his friend, a little bird named Sue. They lived in a nice, green park. One sunny day, Tom and Sue met to discuss a game they wanted to play. Sue said, "Tom, let's play hide and seek!" Tom thought it was a great idea. They both loved to play games together. Tom started to count, "One, two, three!" Sue flew to find a hiding spot. Tom looked for Sue, but his mustache made it hard to see. After a while, Tom found Sue hiding in a tree. They laughed and played all day long.

One day, Ben and Lily were playing with their toy cars in the park. They made them zoom on the grass and the sand. They had a lot of fun. But then, a big boy came and saw their cars. He wanted them for himself. He was a thief. He ran and grabbed Ben's car and Lily's car. He laughed and said, "These are mine now!" Ben and Lily were very sad and angry. They shouted, "No, they are not! Give them back!" They chased the big boy, but he was too fast. He zoomed away with their cars. But the big boy was foolish. He did not see the big rock in his way. He tripped and fell. The cars flew out of his hands and landed on the grass. Ben and Lily ran and picked them up. They were happy to have their cars back. The big boy was hurt and sorry. He said, "I'm sorry, I was wrong. Can you forgive me?" Ben and Lily looked at him. They decided to be kind. They said, "Yes, we forgive you. But don't be a thief again. It's not nice." They gave him a band-aid for his knee and a hug. The big boy smiled and said, "Thank you. You are good friends." They all played together with their cars. They made them zoom on the grass and the sand. They had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a restless duck. He lived near a big pond. The duck liked to swim all day. One day, the duck met a frog. The frog was green and liked to jump. The duck and the frog began to quarrel. "I can swim better than you," said the duck. "No, I can jump better than you," said the frog. They decided to have a race. The duck swam fast, and the frog jumped high. In the end, both of them were tired. They stopped quarreling and became friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.
