Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Charming Dog and the Ball

One day, a charming little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big ball in the park. Max wanted to play with the ball, so he tried to kick it with his leg. But, Max was a dog, and he did not know how to kick. He tried and tried, but the ball did not move. A little girl named Lucy saw Max trying to kick the ball. She laughed and said, "Silly dog, you can't kick the ball with your leg. But, I can help you play with it." Lucy kicked the ball and Max ran after it. They played together and had lots of fun. After a while, Max and Lucy were tired. They sat under a tree and Lucy said, "Max, you are a charming dog. I am happy we played together." Max wagged his tail and licked Lucy's face. They became best friends and played in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy dog named Max. Max loved to eat sausages. One day, Max saw a big sausage on the table. He wanted to eat it, but his mom said, "No, Max! That is not for you." Max was sad, but he had an idea. He would wait until his mom was not looking, and then he would bite the sausage. Max knew he had to be sneaky. He watched his mom and waited for the right time. Finally, his mom went to the other room. Max jumped up on the table and bit the sausage. It was so yummy! But then, his mom came back and saw what Max had done. She was not happy. Max knew he should have listened to his mom. From that day on, Max was never bossy again, and he always listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tim saw a big, shiny toy car. He wanted to play with it. He asked his friend, "Can I borrow your car?" His friend said, "Yes, but be alert and take care of it." Tim played with the car, but he was not alert. He pushed the car too hard and it broke. Tim was sad, and his friend was mad. The car could not be fixed. Tim learned that he must be careful when he borrows things from his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to pretend he was at the zoo. He liked to play with his toy animals and make them talk to each other. His favorite toy was an orange lion, which he named Leo. One day, Tim took his toys to the park. He found a big tree and pretended it was the zoo. He put Leo and his other toy animals under the tree. Tim was so happy playing with his toys and making them talk to each other. But then, it started to rain. Tim had to go home fast. He forgot to take Leo and his other toys with him. The rain made the toys wet and dirty. Leo, the orange lion, was lost forever. Tim was very sad and missed his favorite toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. She looked everywhere in her room but could not find it. Lily's mom saw her and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I can't find my toy, and it makes me sad." Her mom gave her a hug and said, "Let's look for it together." They looked in the kitchen, the living room, and even the yard. But they could not find the toy. Just then, their dog, Spot, came running in with the toy in his mouth! He had been playing with it outside. Lily was so happy and hugged Spot. She said, "Thank you, Spot! You are the best dog ever!" And from that day on, Lily and Spot were always together, and they were never sad again.

Sara loves to play with her mom's jewelry. She likes to put on the shiny rings and necklaces and pretend she is a princess. One day, she finds a purple bracelet in her mom's drawer. It has a big stone that sparkles like a star. Sara puts on the bracelet and feels very happy. She runs around the house, waving her arm and making zoom sounds. She zooms to the kitchen, where her dad is making lunch. He smiles and says, "Wow, Sara, you look very pretty with that bracelet. Where did you get it?" Sara says, "It's mom's. She let me borrow it. It's purple and it makes me zoom." She zooms to the living room, where her brother is watching TV. He looks at her and says, "Hey, Sara, that's mom's special bracelet. You can't play with it. It's very expensive and fragile. You might break it or lose it." Sara says, "No, it's mine. Mom gave it to me. It's purple and it makes me zoom." She zooms to her bedroom, where she has her dolls and toys. She puts the bracelet on her favorite doll and says, "Now you are a princess too. You can zoom with me." But then, she hears her mom coming home. She hears her mom say, "Hello, everyone. I'm back from the store. I have a surprise for you." Sara feels scared. She knows her mom will be angry if she sees her with the bracelet. She quickly takes it off the doll and tries to hide it under her pillow. But it's too late. Her mom comes into her room and sees the bracelet on her bed. Her mom says, "Sara, what are you doing with my bracelet? This is not a toy. This is a very special gift from your grandma. She gave it to me before she went to heaven. It's very old and precious. You could have damaged it or lost it. How did you get it?" Sara feels very sorry. She says, "I'm sorry, mom. I found it in your drawer. It's purple and it makes me zoom. I wanted to be a princess like you. I didn't mean to hurt it. Please don't be mad at me." Her mom hugs her and says, "I'm not mad at you, Sara. I'm glad you like the bracelet. It's very beautiful, just like you. But you have to be careful with it. It's not a toy. It's a family treasure. Maybe when you are older, I can let you wear it for a special occasion. But for now, you have to put it back in my drawer. And you can play with your own jewelry. You have many pretty ones that are purple and make you zoom." Sara nods and says, "Okay, mom. I'm sorry. I love you." She gives her mom the bracelet and kisses her. Then she goes to her drawer and picks out her own purple necklace and earrings. She puts them on and says, "Look, mom. I'm a princess too. And I can zoom." Her mom smiles and says, "Yes, you are, Sara. And you can zoom. But don't zoom too fast. You might bump into something. Be careful and have fun." Sara says, "Okay, mom. Thank you. I love you." She zooms out of her room, happy and proud. She zooms to the kitchen, where her dad and brother are waiting for the surprise. She zooms to the living room, where her mom has a big cake with candles. She zooms to the table, where her family sings happy birthday to her. She zooms to the cake, where she makes a wish and blows out the candles. She zooms to her mom, where she hugs her and says, "This is the best day ever. Thank you, mom. You are the best mom ever."
