Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing outside in the mud. His clothes got filthy. Tim saw a big pepper plant in his garden. He wanted to pick a pepper to eat. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, I must warn you. Do not eat the pepper. It is too hot for you." Tim listened to his mom and did not eat the pepper. Later, Tim helped his mom wash his filthy clothes. They laughed and had fun together. Tim was happy that he listened to his mom's warning.

Jack went to the theater with his mom and dad. He was so excited! He saw a big crowd of people and lots of shiny lights. He saw popcorn and soda and candy. Everything looked so fine. Jack and his family went in to the theater. It was very big and dark. He saw a big screen and lots of comfy chairs with big pillows to sit on. He knew he was going to have a good time. After the show, Jack felt tired but happy. He liked what he saw. He told his mom and dad that he liked the theater very much. His mom said they had to go home but they could come back soon. Jack said he was ready to come back and see another show.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a selfish boy named Tom. He did not like to share his toys with other kids. One day, Tom found a pretty flame toy. He was very happy and wanted to keep it all to himself. Tom took his new flame toy to the park. He saw his friend, Lily. Lily was sitting on a seat, feeling sad. "Why are you sad, Lily?" asked Tom. "I lost my favorite toy," she replied. Tom knew that the flame toy was Lily's, but he did not want to give it back. Just then, a big wind came and blew the flame toy out of Tom's hand. It landed back on the seat next to Lily. Tom felt bad for being selfish. "I'm sorry, Lily. I found your toy and I should have given it back," he said. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. Let's play together and share our toys." From that day on, Tom learned to share and they all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy was a harmless bird who liked to play in the rain. One day, it started to rain, and Timmy was very happy. Timmy went outside to play in the rain. He jumped in puddles and splashed water on his wings. As he played, he saw a big, juicy worm on the ground. Timmy was hungry, so he decided to eat the worm. Timmy ate the worm and felt very full. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. Timmy went back to his nest, feeling happy and warm. He had a great day playing in the rain and eating a tasty worm.

One day, a jolly man had a big van. He liked to print fun things for kids. He had a big machine in his van to print. He went to the park to give the kids their fun things. At the park, the kids were happy to see the jolly man and his van. They all wanted the fun things he printed. But then, something unexpected happened. The jolly man's big machine stopped working. The kids were sad and the jolly man was sad too. Then, a little dog came to the park. The dog could print fun things too! The jolly man and the kids were very surprised. The little dog printed fun things for all the kids. Everyone was happy again. The jolly man and the little dog became good friends and they went to many parks together to make kids happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his dog, Sam. They liked to play in the park. The sun was shining, but the air was bitter cold. Tim wore a big coat to stay warm. Sam didn't mind the cold, he just wanted to play. As they walked in the park, Sam saw a big pile of leaves. Sam jumped into the pile and leaves went everywhere. Tim laughed and threw more leaves on Sam. Suddenly, Sam stopped playing and looked at the leaves. Tim shrugged and asked, "What's wrong, Sam?" Sam started to dig in the leaves. Tim watched Sam dig and then he saw something shiny. It was a key! Tim picked it up and looked at it. He didn't know what it was for, but he thought it was cool. They kept playing and forgot about the key. Later, they went home and showed Mom the key. She looked at it and smiled. "This is the key to my old toy box!" she said. The toy box had been lost for many years. Tim and Sam helped Mom find the toy box in the attic. When they opened it, they found lots of fun toys to play with. The fall day ended with a big surprise and lots of fun for Tim and Sam.
