Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick ball. This ball loved to roll all day long. It would roll in the grass, on the road, and even in the mud. The ball was very happy when it was rolling. One day, the ball had a dream. In the dream, it saw a beautiful hill. The ball knew that it would be so much fun to roll down that hill. It wanted to find the hill and roll down it all day long. The next day, the ball went to look for the hill. It rolled and rolled, searching for the hill from its dream. After a long time, it found the hill! The ball was so happy. It rolled down the hill, laughing and having fun, just like in its dream.

Once upon a time there was a boy who wanted to ride his bike. Every day he would get his bike and ride it around the yard. One day he decided to go on an adventure. He rode his bike until he got to the top of a big hill. When he got there he looked around and it became tough to go down. He started to go down the hill and it was much faster than going up. He had to ride fast and it was very scary! But he didn't give up, he kept riding and made it all the way down to the bottom. When he finally reached the bottom it was almost noon. He decided it was a good time to have a snack and rest. So he put his bike back in the yard and ran inside for something to eat. The boy felt so proud of himself for being so tough and riding his bike all the way to the top of the hill. He had accomplished his adventure and was very excited to tell all of his friends about it.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the big store with her mom. They needed to go up to the top floor. Mia saw a big box with doors. Her mom said, "That is an elevator. It will take us up fast." Mia and her mom went inside the elevator. The doors closed and they went up very fast. Mia felt funny in her tummy. She laughed and said, "This is fun, Mommy!" At the top floor, they met Mia's friend, Ben. Ben's mom had a big cake. Mia's mom said, "We invite you to our house for a party." Ben and his mom said yes. They all went back to the elevator. This time, Ben and Mia laughed together as they went down fast. At home, they had a great party with the big cake.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a big ball on the ground. It was not an easy ball to play with. It was very heavy. Tom tried to pick it up, but he could not. So, he just pushed it around the park. Tom pushed the ball into the woods. He heard a funny noise and saw a little green man. The green man said, "Please pause, Tom. That is not a ball. That is my planet." Tom was surprised! He did not know that he was playing with a planet. The green man told Tom that his planet needed to go back to the sky. Tom helped the green man lift the planet. Together, they put it back in the sky. The green man thanked Tom and went back to his planet. Tom went home and told his mom the story. She did not believe him, but Tom knew it was true.

Once upon a time, there was a big ostrich named Ollie. Ollie was very enthusiastic and loved to show his friends new things. One day, Ollie met a small bird named Benny. Ollie said, "Hi, Benny! I want to show you something fun!" Benny was excited and said, "What is it, Ollie?" Ollie took Benny to a big tree with lots of pretty leaves. Ollie and Benny played under the tree, and Ollie showed Benny how to jump high and catch the leaves. They laughed and had a great time. Benny was happy that Ollie showed him the fun game. At the end of the day, Benny said, "Thank you, Ollie, for showing me the fun game. You are a good friend." Ollie smiled and said, "You're welcome, Benny. I'm glad you had fun. Let's play together again soon!" And from that day on, Ollie and Benny were the best of friends.
