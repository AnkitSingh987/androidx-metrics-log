Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Pilot and the Storm

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tom. Tom loved to fly his big plane in the sky. He had a job that was difficult, but he was very good at it. One day, Tom was flying his plane when he saw a big storm. His friend, Lisa, was on the ground. She saw the storm too. Lisa called Tom on the radio to warn him. She said, "Tom, be careful! There is a big storm coming!" Tom listened to Lisa's warning and turned his plane around. He flew away from the storm and landed safely on the ground. Tom was happy that his friend Lisa warned him. They knew that flying in a storm is difficult and dangerous. From that day on, Tom always listened to his friends when they warned him about something.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a hot day, so they brought a big hat and cold drinks. Tim's mom had a camera to take pictures of Tim playing. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. They played with a twisty toy snake. "Let's twist the snake and make it dance!" said Tim. Sue laughed and twisted the snake too. Then, something unexpected happened. A real snake came out of the bushes! Tim and Sue were scared, but Tim's mom took a picture with her camera. The real snake saw the twisty toy snake and thought it was a friend. It twisted around the toy snake and went back into the bushes. Tim, Sue, and Tim's mom laughed and went home with a fun story to tell.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a bit cold, so Tim's mom gave him a jacket to wear. Tim put on the jacket and they went to play. At the park, Tim saw a girl who looked sad. She was cold and did not have a jacket. Tim went to her and said, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I am cold and I don't have a jacket." Tim thought for a moment and then took off his jacket. He gave it to the girl and said, "Here, you can have my jacket. Now you won't be cold." The girl smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Just then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew away the bad clouds. The sun came out and it was not cold anymore. Tim and the girl laughed and played in the warm sun. They were happy because they had made a new friend and the day was not cold anymore.

One day, a charming little fish lived in a stream. The fish liked to swim and play with his friends. The fish was very small, but he wanted to grow big and strong like his dad. One day, the little fish met a frog. The frog said, "I can help you grow big and strong. Just follow me to my home." The little fish was very happy and said, "Thank you, frog! I will follow you." The frog took the little fish to a big pond. The fish started to swim and play in the pond. But then, something unexpected happened. The fish did not grow big and strong. Instead, the frog ate the little fish. The little fish should have stayed in the stream with his friends.

Lily and Max were friends who liked to play with toys. One day, they found a microscope in the attic. It was a big tool that made small things look big. They wanted to see what things looked like under the microscope. Lily said, "Let me go first. I want to see this flower." She put the flower on the slide and looked through the eye-piece. Max said, "No, I want to go first. I want to see this bug." He tried to grab the microscope from Lily. They disagreed. They pulled and pushed the microscope. They did not share. They were not careful. The microscope fell off the table and broke. The glass shattered and the metal bent. The flower and the bug flew away. Lily and Max cried. They were sad and sorry. They had ruined the microscope. They had no more fun. They had a bad ending.
