Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy car that could speed very fast. Lily loved her toy car and played with it every day. She would make it go fast and then stop. She would laugh and clap her hands. One day, Lily lost her comb. She needed it to brush her hair. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. So, she decided to use her toy car to help her look for the comb. She made the car speed all around the room, under the bed, and behind the chair. As the toy car went fast, it made a loud noise. The loud noise scared the cat, and the cat jumped high in the air. When the cat jumped, the comb fell out from under the cat. Lily saw the comb and picked it up. She was so happy to find her comb, and she thanked her toy car for helping her.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucy. She was three years old and loved playing with her toys. One day, Lucy was playing near the machine in the living room when she heard a loud noise. She saw that the machine was still on and she was scared. She felt guilty because she thought she was the one who had left it on. Mommy came in and saw the machine was still on. She looked at Lucy and said: "Did you shut the machine off, Lucy?" Lucy was scared and was very quiet. Mommy said: "When you turn something off that you're not supposed to turn on, it's important to tell the truth." Lucy said: "I'm sorry, I forgot to shut the machine off." Mommy smiled and said: "It's alright Lucy, we all make mistakes. The important thing is to tell the truth and we can figure out what to do together." The moral of the story is that it's important to always tell the truth, even if it's something small. And if you make a mistake, it's never too late to admit it and take responsibility for it.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play catch with his friends. They would throw a light, red ball to each other and laugh as they played. One day, they decided to have a game to see who could catch the ball the most times. The person who caught the ball the most would get a big win. All of Tim's friends were very excited and wanted to be the best at catch. The game started and Tim tried his best to catch the ball every time it came to him. His friends were also trying very hard. "I will catch it!" said Tim's friend, Sam. "No, I will!" said another friend, Lily. In the end, Tim caught the ball the most times and got the big win. All of his friends clapped and cheered for him. They were all happy and continued to play catch, because they loved the game and being together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a kite in the sky. It was red and blue. Tim wanted to fly a kite too. He asked his mom to buy him one. She said yes and they went to the store. At the store, Tim picked a big, yellow kite. They went back to the park. Tim held the kite and ran. The wind helped the kite go up, up, up! Tim's mom told him to be alert and watch where he was going. As Tim ran, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue wanted to fly a kite too, but she did not have one. Tim was kind and let Sue pass the kite to him. They took turns flying the kite. They had a fun day at the park with the big, yellow kite.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful girl named Lily. She had a red cap that she loved to wear. One day, she went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. In the park, Lily saw her friend, Tim. Tim was playing with a toy car. "Hi, Lily!" said Tim. "Do you want to play with me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" They played together until they were tired. Lily looked at the sky and saw that it was getting dark. "Tim, I have to go home now. It's time to finish playing," she said. Tim nodded and said, "Okay, let's go home." They put on their caps and walked home, happy after a fun day in the park.
