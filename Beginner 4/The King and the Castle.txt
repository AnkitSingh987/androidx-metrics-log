Category: Beginner
Title: The King and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a kind king who lived in a big castle. The king had many friends who would come to play with him. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. The king loved his castle and his friends. One sunny day, while they were playing, a big rain cloud came and it started to rain. Everyone ran inside the castle to stay dry. The rain made the castle dirty and wet. The kind king wanted to clean it up, so he started to wipe the floors and walls. As the king wiped the walls, he found a secret door he never saw before. He opened the door and saw a room full of toys and treats. The kind king and his friends were so happy! They played with the toys and ate the treats. From that day on, the king always wiped the walls, hoping to find more secret doors.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They loved to play together all day. One day, Tim and Sue found a big sink in the kitchen. They wanted to attach a toy boat to the sink, so they could play with it in the water. Sue found some string and helped Tim attach the boat to the sink. They filled the sink with water and put the boat in. The boat floated and they had so much fun playing together. Tim and Sue were very happy with their smart idea. They played with the boat in the sink all day long.

Maggie was walking down the street wearing her pretty skirt. She loved swishing it around when she walked. Suddenly, something scared her. She stopped and blinked. What was it? Maggie saw a big dog walking toward her. She knew the dog would never hurt her, but it still looked scary. Maggie blinked again. The dog came closer and closer until it was right in front of her. Maggie said to the dog, "Hello, doggy. What's your name?" The dog replied, "My name is Woof!" Maggie smiled and patted the dog's head. She wasn't scared anymore. She thanked the dog and continued walking down the street, swishing her skirt as she went.

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. The car had four round wheels. One day, the car went for a slow ride in the park. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. In the park, the car met a little boy. The boy saw the car and said, "Hey, car! Why are you so slow?" The car said, "I like to go slow so I can see all the pretty things in the park." The boy thought about it and said, "That's nice, car. Can I go for a ride with you?" So, the boy and the car went for a slow ride together. They saw pretty flowers and green trees. Then, they saw a big rock in the road. The boy pointed at the rock and said, "Look, car! A big rock! Let's go around it." The car went around the rock and they kept going slow. The boy learned that going slow can be good because you can see more things. The car and the boy had a fun day in the park, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a little mushroom. It was unknown to many. It lived near a big tree. The mushroom was very shy and did not talk to anyone. One day, a little girl named Sally came to the forest. She saw the unknown mushroom and thought it was very pretty. She wanted to take it home. So, she tried to remove it from the ground. But the mushroom did not want to leave the forest. It asked Sally to let it stay. Sally felt sad, but she understood. She left the mushroom in the forest and went home. From that day, Sally and the mushroom became good friends. They played together in the forest every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very intelligent. He loved to play at home with his family. One day, Max saw a big ball outside and wanted to play with it. He was so excited that he ran out of his home. Max started to chase the big ball. He ran and ran, but the ball kept going away from him. Max was so busy chasing the ball that he didn't see how far he was from home. He looked around and couldn't see his home anymore. Max was lost and felt very sad. Max tried to find his home, but he couldn't. He walked and walked, but he didn't know where to go. As it got dark, Max became more and more scared. He missed his family and his home. In the end, Max couldn't find his way back home, and he was all alone in the dark.
