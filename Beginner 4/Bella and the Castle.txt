Category: Beginner
Title: Bella and the Castle

Bella and her mom went to the beach. Bella loved the sand and played in it. She filled a bucket with sand and made a sand castle. Her mom watched with a smile on her face. But then, a wave came and washed the sand castle away. Bella was sad. She started to cry and demanded that her mom make the sand castle again. Her mom said she was sorry, but she couldn't. Bella was ashamed and ran away. Bella went to the sand and asked it why it had taken away her sand castle. She felt angry and demanded that the sand make her sand castle back. But the sand couldn't do anything. Bella felt sad and ashamed. She slowly went back to her mom and apologized for demanding that she do something that was impossible. Her mom was so proud of her and showed her how to make a better sand castle.

Once upon a time, there was a little pony named Poppy. Poppy lived in a calm, green field with her friends. They liked to run and play all day long. One day, Poppy hurt her leg while playing. She was sad and couldn't run with her friends. Her friends gathered around her and tried to make her feel better. Poppy's mom came to help. She knew how to heal Poppy's leg. She put a soft bandage on it and told Poppy to rest. Poppy listened to her mom and stayed calm. After a few days, Poppy's leg was all better. She was so happy to run and play with her friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird who loved to whistle. His whistles were very loud and happy, and everyone in the forest loved to hear them! One day, the big bird was feeling very sad. He sat on his branch and didn't whistle at all. The other animals in the forest wondered why he was so sad. Then, a helpful little mouse came up and asked the bird why he was sad. The bird said he was sad because he couldn't find his nest. He had been looking for it all morning but he couldn't find it anywhere. The mouse felt sad for the bird and decided to help him. She ran all around the forest looking for a nest, and when she found one, she called the bird over. He lay in the nest and was so happy - it was exactly what he was looking for! The bird thanked the helpful mouse for finding his nest, and then started to whistle again. His happy whistles filled the entire forest with joy!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big tree in his yard. The tree had many prunes. Tim loved to eat prunes. But Tim was selfish. He did not like to share his prunes with his friends. One day, Tim's friend Sam came to play. Sam saw the prunes and wanted some. Tim dared Sam to climb the tree and get the prunes. Sam was scared, but he wanted to try. Tim watched as Sam started to climb. Sam climbed up and up. He got the prunes and started to come down. Tim was happy to see Sam get the prunes. But then, Sam fell! Tim was scared for his friend. Sam was okay, but he was sad. Tim felt bad for being selfish. He learned that sharing is good. From that day on, Tim shared his prunes with all his friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a child named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim tried to climb the tree, but he was slow. A bird on a branch saw Tim and said, "Why are you slow?" Tim just gave a shrug and said, "I don't know, but I want to climb the tree." The bird flew down and helped Tim climb the tree. Together, they went up the tree. At the top, they looked around and saw many fun things. They saw other kids playing, dogs running, and the sun shining. Tim said to the bird, "Thank you for helping me climb the tree. It's so nice up here." The bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm happy to help you." Tim and the bird became good friends. They played in the tree every day. And even though Tim was slow, he was happy because he had a friend to help him.
