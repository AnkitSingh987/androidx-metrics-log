Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cop and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a nice cop named Tom. He had a big, blue car. Tom loved to help people in the town. One sunny day, Tom saw a little boy with an expensive toy car. The boy was sad because he had no friends to play with. Tom wanted to help the boy. Tom said, "Can I join you and play with your car?" The boy smiled and said, "Yes, please!" They played together all day and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat liked to sail on the wide blue sea. The sun was hot, and the wind blew soft. The boat was very happy. One day, a big, hard rock was in the boat's way. The boat did not see the rock. It hit the rock with a loud "Bump!" The boat was sad and scared. It did not want to sail anymore. A kind bird saw the boat. The bird said, "Do not be sad, little boat. I will help you." The bird pushed the boat away from the hard rock. The boat was happy again. It sailed on the wide blue sea with the kind bird as its friend. They had fun in the sun together.

One day, a man with a big mustache went for a walk. He saw a dry tree and thought it looked sad. He wanted to help the tree, so he went to find some water. "I will make you happy again," he told the tree. The man found a big bucket of water. But, he had to walk backward to carry it. So, he reversed all the way back to the tree. He was very careful not to spill the water. When he got back to the tree, he poured the water on it. The tree became happy and green again. The man with the mustache smiled. "I told you I would help you," he said. The tree and the man became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a shiny floor. The sun would come in and shine on the floor every day. It made the room look very pretty. In the room lived a cat named Whiskers. Whiskers was a healthy cat with soft fur. He liked to play on the shiny floor. He would chase his ball and slide all around. One day, Whiskers found a new friend. It was a little mouse named Squeaky. Whiskers and Squeaky played on the shiny floor together. They had so much fun, and they became the best of friends.

One sunny day, a little girl named Lily found a big purple couch outside her house. The couch was soft and bouncy. Lily was so happy and wanted to play on it. Lily jumped on the couch and said, "Couch, can you spring me high up in the sky?" The couch heard her and started to spring. Lily went up and down, laughing and giggling. Lily's friend, Tom, came over and saw her playing on the purple couch. He asked, "Can I play on the couch too?" Lily said, "Yes, come and jump with me!" They both jumped and sprang high in the sky, having lots of fun together.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Joe. Joe loved to play with his blocks. He would play so silly and laugh so much! One day while Joe was playing, he heard a strange noise. He didn't know where the noise was coming from! He looked around the room but did not see anything. Joe decided to investigate. He picked up all of his blocks and put them near his ear. He heard the noise again! It was coming from the blocks! Joe was so excited. He made a silly tower with the blocks and the noise became louder. The more blocks he added and the higher the tower got, the louder the noise got too. Joe was so happy to find out that the noise came from his blocks!

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The owl had a big face with big eyes. The owl lived in a tree near a pond. The pond had water for all the animals to drink. One day, a little bunny came to the pond to drink some water. The bunny saw the wise old owl and said, "Hi, Mr. Owl! I am thirsty. Can I drink some water from the pond?" The wise old owl smiled and said, "Yes, little bunny. You can drink the water. The pond is for all of us." The little bunny drank the water and felt happy. The wise old owl watched the bunny and smiled. The other animals came to the pond to drink water too. They all became friends and played together. The wise old owl was happy to see everyone sharing and being kind to each other.
