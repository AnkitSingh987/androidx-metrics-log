Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Dog

One day, a little cat wanted to climb. The cat saw a big steel pole. The cat thought, "I can climb that!" So, the cat started to climb the pole. The cat climbed up, up, up. As the cat climbed, a fierce dog saw the cat. The dog said, "Hey cat, come down! I want to play!" The cat did not want to play with the fierce dog. The cat was scared. So, the cat climbed higher, higher, higher. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog started to climb the pole too! The cat was very surprised. The dog climbed up, up, up. When they both reached the top, the cat and dog looked at each other. The dog said, "I'm not so fierce. I just want to be friends." The cat smiled and said, "Okay, let's be friends." And from that day on, the cat and dog were best friends.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who was scared. He had hurt his foot and it was very sore. One day his mom helped him into the van and they drove to visit a special doctor. The doctor was very nice and she smiled at the boy. She made him feel better right away. The doctor healed his foot with special medicine and gentle touches. She put a bandage on his foot and he felt very brave. He thanked the doctor for healing him and went happily back to the van with his mom. They drove back home and the little boy was no longer scared. He was no longer hurt either. He was happy and full of joy. His mom hugged him and said she was proud of him for being brave. The little boy smiled and felt very proud. He knew that the doctor had helped him heal.

Once upon a time, in a wide green field, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her best friend, Olive the cat. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One sunny day, Lily and Olive were playing near a big tree. Olive saw a butterfly and tried to catch it. She jumped and jumped, but the butterfly flew higher and higher. Olive climbed up the tree to reach it. But when she looked down, she got scared. She was too high up and didn't know how to get down. Lily saw Olive was scared and said, "Don't worry, Olive! I'll help you!" But the tree was too tall for Lily to climb. Lily began to weep because she didn't know how to help her friend. Then, a kind bird flew by and saw Lily crying. The bird asked, "Why are you weeping, little girl?" Lily pointed to Olive in the tree and said, "My friend is stuck and I can't help her." The kind bird flew up to Olive and told her, "Hold onto my back, and I'll fly you down." Olive held on tight, and the bird flew her safely to the ground. Lily hugged Olive, and they both thanked the bird. From that day on, they played happily together, always careful not to climb too high.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of a big, scary tree near his house. Every day, at noon, he would look out the window and see the scary tree. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, don't be scared. Go give the scary tree a kiss, and you will see it's not so bad." Tim was very scared, but he wanted to be brave like his mom. Tim went outside and walked up to the scary tree. He closed his eyes and gave the tree a kiss. But when he opened his eyes, he saw a big, scary face on the tree. The tree said, "Boo!" and Tim ran away, crying. The scary tree was even scarier than he thought.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Tim found a shiny coin on the ground. He was very happy and wanted to show it to his friends. Tim ran to his friends and said, "Look what I found! A shiny coin!" His friends were excited too. They wanted to play a game with the coin. They decided to play a game of catch with the coin. Tim threw the coin high in the air and his friends tried to catch it. But the coin went too high and too far. It fell into a deep hole. Tim and his friends tried to get the coin out, but they were not strong enough. They were very sad. The game had to end, and they never saw the shiny coin again.
