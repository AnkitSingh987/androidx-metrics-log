Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Sun

One day, a funny dog named Max found a big, purple ball. He loved to play with it. Max would run, jump, and catch the ball all day long. He was very happy. Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, came to his house. She saw Max playing with the purple ball. "Wow, Max! That ball is so big and purple! Can I play with you?" Lily asked. Max wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's play!" They had a great time playing with the purple ball. Max would catch it and Lily would watch and clap her hands. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Max and Lily were tired but happy. They both knew they had a fun day with the big, purple ball.

Once upon a time, there was a tall girl named Susie. She was playing in the park with her friends when she suddenly spotted something sparkly in the grass. Curiously, Susie looked closer and saw it was a stunning veil! It must have been lost by somebody, she thought. "I'm going to find the owner of this," she said to her friends, "so I can give it back." But her friends didn't think that was a good idea. "Just keep it," they said. "It's too pretty to just give away." Susie disagreed, so the friends decided to play a game to decide who should win the veil. Susie wanted to take the veil to the owner, and her friends wanted to keep it. The friends all took turns trying to solve a riddle. The one who got it right would win the veil. After a few tries, Susie solved it correctly! "I won!" she said, proudly. She put on the veil and ran to the nearby store to find its owner. The storekeeper helped Susie find the veil's owner, and the two were very happy to be reunited with it. Susie felt wonderful for helping out somebody else and she was never happier.

One day, a boy named Tim found a hose in his yard. He wanted to play with it. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the hose?" His mom said, "Yes, but first you must complete putting away your toys." Tim did what his mom said. He put away all his toys. Then, he went outside to play with the hose. He turned on the water and the hose became mighty. It was so strong that it pushed Tim to the ground! Suddenly, a big dog came running to Tim. The dog saw the hose and started to play with it. Tim and the dog became friends. They played with the mighty hose all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim found a stick in the park. He liked the stick and thought it was a reliable friend. Tim and the stick played together all day. One time, Tim saw a big ball rolling towards a little dog. Tim wanted to prevent the ball from hitting the dog. So, he used his reliable stick to stop the ball. The dog was safe and happy. Tim and the stick became heroes in the park. Everyone liked them. They played and helped others every day. Tim and the stick were always reliable friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a clumsy boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside, but he would often fall and bump into things. One day, while playing in the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. "Hi, I'm Sue," said the girl. "I like the warm season. What's your name?" "Hi, I'm Tim," he replied. "I like the warm season too. I like to play ball." They decided to play ball together. Tim tried to strike the ball with a stick, but he missed and fell down. Sue helped him up and they both laughed. Tim and Sue played ball all day and had lots of fun. They became good friends and played together every day. The warm season was their favorite time to play, and they were always happy when they were together.

One day, a little gray otter named Ollie was playing by the river. He liked to swim and splash in the water. Ollie had many friends, but today he was all alone. Ollie saw a big shadow on the water. He was scared. "Who is there?" he asked. "Don't scare me, please!" The shadow moved and a big, friendly frog jumped out. "Hi, Ollie!" said the frog. "I'm sorry I scared you. I'm Fred the frog. Want to play?" Ollie was happy to meet Fred. They played in the water all day long. They swam, jumped, and laughed together. Ollie was not scared anymore. He had a new friend and they had so much fun.
