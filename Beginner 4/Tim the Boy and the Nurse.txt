Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Nurse

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a cold, icy day. Tim saw a big dog. The dog looked sad. Tim went to the dog and asked, "Why are you sad?" The dog said, "I am lost. I can't find my home." Tim wanted to help the dog. He said, "I will help you find your home." They walked and walked. Then, they saw a lady. She was a nurse. The nurse saw the dog and said, "Oh, that's my dog! Thank you for finding him!" The nurse was happy. Tim felt good for helping the dog and the nurse. The nurse explained, "I thought I lost my dog forever. You are a good boy, Tim."

Once upon a time, there was a fair pilot named Sam. He had a special pistol he remembered getting for his birthday. It was itchy and green and he loved it. Every day, Sam practiced shooting targets with the pistol. He was able to hit them every time and soon, he was the best shooter in the whole town. People often asked Sam to go to their fairs. He was happy to do so and always hit every target they pointed out. Everyone thought he was really fair because he was so good. His friends and family were very proud of Sam. They all remembered his special pistol had been the beginning of his success. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat. The cat had a friend, a little red bird. One day, the purple cat went to visit the little red bird. When the purple cat got to the bird's house, the little red bird was very happy. They played and laughed all day. After playing, they got hungry. The little red bird said, "Let's have some tea!" So, the purple cat and the little red bird sat down and had tea. They drank from small cups and ate yummy cookies. They talked and laughed some more. Then, it was time for the purple cat to go home. The purple cat said goodbye to the little red bird and went home. They promised to visit each other again soon. And they lived happily ever after, always having fun and drinking tea together.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. Lily had a tutor named Mr. Bear. Mr. Bear was big, brown, and very nice. He helped Lily learn her ABCs and 123s. One day, Lily and Mr. Bear went on a trip. They had to pack their bags. Lily put her clothes, toys, and books in her bag. Mr. Bear helped her pack her bag. They were very excited to go on their trip. When they got to the trip place, Lily and Mr. Bear played in the park, ate yummy food, and read books together. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, they packed their bags again and said goodbye to the trip place. Lily and Mr. Bear were happy they went on the trip, and they could not wait to go on another one soon.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to hop on the squares on the ground. The squares were different colors: red, blue, yellow and green. Ben and Lily tried to hop on all the colors. "Look, Lily, I can hop on four squares at once!" Ben said. He jumped with his legs wide and landed on a red, a blue, a yellow and a green square. "Wow, Ben, you are good at hopping!" Lily said. She tried to do the same, but she fell down and scraped her knee. She started to cry. Ben ran to Lily and helped her up. He saw blood on her knee and felt worried. He did not like to see Lily hurt. "Don't cry, Lily, it's okay. I have a band-aid in my backpack. Let me put it on your knee. It will make it better." Ben said. He took out a band-aid with a picture of a bunny on it and stuck it on Lily's knee. "Thank you, Ben, you are very nice. The band-aid is pretty. Does it have a name?" Lily asked. She stopped crying and smiled. "Yes, it does. It's called Hoppy. He likes to hop, just like us. He can be your friend." Ben said. "I like Hoppy. He makes me happy. Can we hop some more?" Lily asked. "Sure, we can. But be careful, okay? We don't want to hurt ourselves again." Ben said. "Okay, I will. Come on, let's hop on the squares!" Lily said. They held hands and hopped on the squares. They had fun and forgot about the scrape. They were happy.
