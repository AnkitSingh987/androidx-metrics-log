Category: Beginner
Title: The Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat was going to a port. The port was far away. The boat had many things on it. The boat had toys, food, and clothes. One day, the boat was troubled. The boat was sad. The boat needed help. A little fish saw the boat. The little fish wanted to care for the boat. The little fish went to the boat. The little fish said, "I will help you, boat." The boat was happy. The little fish pushed the boat. The boat went to the port. The people at the port were happy. The little fish cared for the boat, and the boat was not troubled anymore.

Once there was a little boy and his mom. One day, he decided he wanted to learn how to dance. His mom was so proud of him for wanting to try something new and she said yes. The first day, the boy was a bit nervous. He was learning how to do the steps and it was harder than he thought. But he kept practicing and his mom and dad watched with a big smile. Soon enough, the boy had it! He was so proud of himself. He fit the moves perfectly and was now a real dancer. Every day the boy went to dance class and he got better and better. He was so happy to show off his new moves. His mom and dad watched him every time and were so proud. The boy kept practicing and practicing until he was the best dancer he knew! Everyone was so impressed and said he was amazing. With a big smile, the boy knew he was the best dancer and was so proud.

Anna was a little girl who liked to play with her dolls and her teddy bear. She had a big bed with pink sheets and a soft pillow. Every night, she would hug her teddy bear and say good night to her dolls. She liked to dream of happy things, like flowers and rainbows and candy. But one night, she had a bad dream. She dreamed that a big monster with sharp teeth and claws was chasing her in a dark forest. She ran and ran, but the monster was faster. She tried to scream, but no one heard her. She was very scared and sad. She woke up with a loud cry and stumbled out of her bed. She ran to her door and opened it. She saw a light in the hall. It was her mommy, who heard her cry. She ran to her mommy and hugged her tight. "Mommy, mommy, I had a nightmare!" she sobbed. "Oh, my poor baby, what did you dream?" her mommy asked, stroking her hair. "A big monster was after me, and I couldn't get away!" Anna said. "That sounds very scary, but it was only a dream, sweetie. The monster is not real, and it can't hurt you. You are safe with me, and I love you very much," her mommy said. She carried Anna back to her room and tucked her in. She kissed her forehead and said, "Do you want me to stay with you until you fall asleep?" "Yes, please, mommy," Anna said. Her mommy smiled and sat on the edge of her bed. She sang a soft lullaby and held her hand. Anna felt calm and warm. She looked at her teddy bear and her dolls. They looked charming and friendly. She smiled and closed her eyes. She dreamed of happy things again.

One day, a happy dog named Max went for a walk with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They walked to the park and saw a big, red ball. Max loved to play with balls, so he picked it up with his mouth. Max said to Lily, "Look, I got the ball!" Lily laughed and said, "Good job, Max! Let's play!" Max dropped the ball and Lily kicked it. They played catch and had lots of fun. While they were playing, Max smelled something funny. He sniffed the air and said, "Yuck! It's gas!" Lily looked around and saw a car nearby. The car was making the gas smell. They decided to move away from the car and keep playing. Max and Lily played until they were tired and went back home, happy and smiling.
