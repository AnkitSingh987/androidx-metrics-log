Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Nosy Dog and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Max. Max loved to sniff everything. He would sniff the flowers, the grass, and even the air. One day, Max met a big truck. The truck had a job to supply gas to the cars. Max went up to the truck and said, "Hi, I am Max. What do you do?" The truck replied, "Hello Max, I am Tom. I supply gas to cars so they can go vroom vroom." Max was very curious and wanted to see how Tom did his job. Tom said, "Max, you can watch, but don't be too nosy. Gas is not for sniffing." Max agreed and watched Tom supply gas to the cars. Max learned that being nosy is not always good. He promised to be more careful when sniffing around. Max and Tom became good friends and helped each other out. And they lived happily ever after.

Tim and Anna were hungry. They wanted a snack. They went to the kitchen and looked for something to eat. "What do you want, Tim?" Anna asked. "I want a cookie," Tim said. "Me too," Anna said. "But Mom said we have to eat something healthy first." They opened the fridge and saw some green celery. Anna liked celery. She took one and bit it. It was crunchy and fresh. "Try some, Tim," Anna said. "It's good." Tim made a face. He did not like celery. He thought it was bitter and boring. "No, thank you," Tim said. "I want something else." He saw some cheese and ham. He took them and made a sandwich. He ate it quickly. "Can I have a cookie now?" Tim asked. Anna shook her head. She was still eating her celery. "No, Tim," Anna said. "You have to eat some celery too. Mom said so." Tim sighed. He did not want to argue with Anna. She was older and smarter. He took a small piece of celery and put it in his mouth. He chewed it slowly. He did not like it at all. "Yuck," Tim said. "This is not good." Anna smiled. She had an idea. "Maybe you will like it better with some peanut butter," Anna said. "Mom said peanut butter is healthy too. And it is yummy." She took a jar of peanut butter and a knife. She spread some peanut butter on Tim's celery. She gave it to him. "Here, try this," Anna said. Tim looked at the celery. It was still green, but it had some brown stuff on it. He was curious. He took a bite. He was surprised. It was sweet and nutty. He liked it. "Mmm," Tim said. "This is good." Anna was happy. She gave him another piece. "See, Tim," Anna said. "Celery is not so bad. You just have to try new things." Tim nodded. He ate the celery with peanut butter. He finished it. He felt full and proud. "Can I have a cookie now?" Tim asked. Anna nodded. She took two cookies from the jar. She gave one to Tim. They ate the cookies together. They were delicious. "Thank you, Anna," Tim said. "You are a good sister." Anna hugged him. She was glad. "You are welcome, Tim," Anna said. "You are a good brother."

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a delicate leg. He had to be careful when he played with his friends. One day, Spot was playing with his best friend, a cat named Fluffy. They were running in the park. Spot had to care for his delicate leg. He did not want to hurt it. But then, Spot saw a big ball. He loved to play with balls. He forgot to care for his leg. Spot ran fast to get the ball. His leg did not hurt. Spot was happy. From that day on, Spot and Fluffy played together every day, and Spot learned to care for his leg while having fun.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to the park. They had a red ball to play with. They liked to share the ball and take turns. The ball had an easy handle, so they could hold it well. While they were playing, a big wind came. The wind took the ball away. Tim and Sue were sad. They did not know what to do. They wanted their ball back. Then, a nice dog came to them. The dog had the ball in its mouth! The dog wanted to play too. Tim and Sue were happy. They did not expect the dog to bring the ball. Now, they could all share the ball and play together.
