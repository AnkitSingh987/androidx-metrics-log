Category: Beginner
Title: The Lady and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a nice elderly lady who loved roses. She had a big rose garden with many pretty roses. Every day, she would water them and take care of them. She was very happy. One day, she saw a small rose tremble in the wind. She was worried the wind would hurt the rose. So, she took the rose inside her house to keep it safe. The elderly lady put the rose in a big jar with water. The next day, the lady woke up and saw that the rose was now a beautiful fairy! The fairy thanked the elderly lady for saving her. The fairy gave the lady a wish. The lady wished for a friend to help her take care of the roses. The fairy became the lady's friend and they took care of the roses together. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others, because you never know when something unexpected will happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a hoop that she loved to play with every day. One day, she could not find her hoop. She was very sad. Lily's mom said, "Remember, we put it in the toy box. It is important to put your toys away when you are done playing." Lily looked in the toy box and found her hoop. She was very happy. Lily played with her hoop outside. She saw her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Can I play with your hoop too?" Lily said, "Yes, we can play together." They played with the hoop all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little cactus. Every day, the cactus would go on walks and play in the sun. But sometimes, it would also get very lonely. One day while out on its walk, the cactus came across a broken toy. It was so sad, so the cactus decided to take the toy with it and make it better. The cactus was determined to get its new friend back to good working order. Every day the cactus worked on the toy. Finally, one day the cactus had finished patching up its new friend. But then, it had a thought: what if it doesn't work? The cactus shrugged, not knowing what else it could do. Yet, when the cactus pressed the toy, it lit up! The toy worked, and the cactus was so happy. From then on, the cactus and its new toy friend were inseparable.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a new boy. He is sitting on a bench. He has a big hat and a book. "Who is he?" Tom asks Mia. "I don't know. Let's go and say hello," Mia says. They walk to the bench. Mia smiles and says, "Hi, I'm Mia. This is Tom. What's your name?" The boy looks up. He says, "I'm Leo. I'm new here. I just moved with my mom and dad." "Nice to meet you, Leo. Do you want to play with us?" Tom asks. Leo shakes his head. He says, "No, thank you. I like to read. Reading is fun." Tom and Mia are surprised. They like to read, too, but they also like to run and jump and slide. They think reading is boring. "Reading is not fun. Playing is fun. Come on, Leo. The park has a lot of things to do. There is a swing and a slide and a sandbox and a seesaw," Mia says. Leo says, "No, thank you. I like to read. Reading is quiet. Playing is noisy. I don't like noisy things. They hurt my ears." Tom and Mia are angry. They think Leo is rude. They don't want to be friends with him. They say, "Fine. Stay here. We don't care. We will go and play. Bye, Leo." They run away. Leo watches them go. He feels sad. He wishes he had friends. He likes to read, but he also likes to talk and laugh and share. He doesn't like to be alone. He looks at the sun. The sun is bright and warm. The sun is his friend. He says, "Hello, sun. Thank you for being here. Can you help me find friends?" The sun smiles. The sun says, "Of course, Leo. I will help you. You are a good boy. You just need to be brave and kind. Sometimes, you have to try new things. Sometimes, you have to listen to others. Sometimes, you have to compromise. That's how you make friends." Leo nods. He says, "Thank you, sun. You are very wise. I will try to be brave and kind. I will try to try new things. I will try to listen to others. I will try to compromise. I hope I can make friends." The sun says, "You're welcome, Leo. I'm sure you can. Just be yourself. And don't forget to smile. Smiling is the best way to introduce yourself."
