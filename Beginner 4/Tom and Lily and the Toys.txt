Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Toys

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play together. One day, they decide to play restaurant. Tom is the chef and Lily is the waitress. Tom makes some food with his toys. He puts them on a big plate. He calls Lily. "Lily, come and serve the food!" he says. Lily comes with a tray. She puts the plate on the tray. She smiles. "I will serve the food to our guests," she says. She walks to the table where their teddy bears are sitting. She puts the plate in front of them. "Here you go, teddy bears. Enjoy your food," she says. But then, she sees something on the plate. It is a test. A test with many questions and answers. Lily is surprised. She looks at Tom. "Tom, why did you put a test on the plate?" she asks. Tom laughs. He says, "I wanted to see if you are honest, Lily. If you are honest, you will tell the teddy bears that this is not food. This is a test. If you are not honest, you will pretend that this is food and try to make them eat it." Lily thinks for a moment. She knows that lying is bad. She wants to be honest. She says, "Tom, you are silly. This is not food. This is a test. Teddy bears cannot eat a test. They cannot read a test. They only want to hug and cuddle." Tom stops laughing. He says, "You are right, Lily. You are honest. I am sorry for tricking you. Let's make some real food for the teddy bears. They must be hungry." Lily nods. She says, "OK, Tom. Let's make some real food. And let's eat some too. I am hungry too." They hug and say sorry. They make some real food with their toys. They serve the food to the teddy bears and to themselves. They have a lot of fun. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and many toys. One sunny day, Tom went for a walk by the river. He saw a little boy named Tim playing with a ball near the water. Suddenly, Tim's ball rolled into the river. Tim was sad and started to cry. Tom saw this and said, "Don't cry, I will help you get your ball back." Tom hurried to find a long stick to reach the ball in the river. Tom used the stick and got the ball out of the river. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! You are very kind." Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Be careful when you play near the river." From that day on, Tom and Tim became good friends and played together by the river every day.

Once upon a time there was a mummy and a daddy who were in search of a new adventure. One day, they came across a large library. On the noticeboard was a sign that said, "Library closed! The books have all rotted!" Mummy said, "That's silly! Why would all the books rot?" Daddy replied, "Yes, it does seem strange. Let's go and take a closer look." So, mummy and daddy opened the door and found that all the books were covered with mold and half of them were ripped in shreds. Mummy said, "Oh dear! Someone must have been very careless. We should take better care of our books." Daddy said, "You are right. We can't let our books rot. We must look after them." Mummy and daddy learned an important lesson that day- that taking care of books is important and we should never let them rot.

One day, a farmer named Tom wanted to organize his wheat. He had a lot of wheat to put away in his big red barn. He asked his friend Sam to help him. "Sam, can you help me organize my wheat?" Tom asked. Sam said, "Yes, I can help you, Tom!" They both started to put the wheat in the barn. They were very happy to work together. As they were organizing the wheat, they found some that looked different. It was spoiled wheat! They were sad because they couldn't use it. But then, a little mouse came out and said, "I can eat the spoiled wheat!" Tom and Sam were surprised that the mouse could talk, but they were happy that the spoiled wheat could be used. The mouse ate the spoiled wheat and they all became friends.
