Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Frog and the Grass

Once upon a time, in a great big jungle, there lived a little frog named Tim. Tim loved to hop all day long. He hopped over rocks, he hopped over logs, and he hopped through the tall green grass. One sunny day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a small bird with bright blue feathers. Sam also liked to hop, just like Tim. They decided to hop together through the great big jungle. As they hopped along, they saw many other animals who lived in the jungle. They saw a big lion, a tall giraffe, and a fast cheetah. Tim and Sam had so much fun hopping together, they became best friends. And from that day on, they hopped and played in the great big jungle, happy as can be.

Ben likes to write. He writes with his crayons on paper. He writes his name, his mom's name, and his dog's name. He also likes to draw stars and planets. He wants to go to space one day. One night, Ben sees a bright light in the sky. He runs to the window and looks up. He sees a big star with a long tail. He thinks it is a comet. He is very excited. He calls his mom. "Mom, mom, look! A comet!" he shouts. Mom comes to the window and smiles. She hugs Ben and says, "Yes, that is a comet, Ben. It is very rare and beautiful. Do you know what a comet is?" Ben shakes his head. He wants to know more. "A comet is a big ball of ice and dust that travels around the sun. Sometimes, it comes close to the earth and we can see it. The tail is made of gas and dust that the sun pushes away from the comet. The comet is very rough and cold, but the tail is very bright and hot," mom explains. Ben listens and nods. He thinks the comet is amazing. He wants to write about it. He grabs his crayons and paper and runs to the table. He writes "comet" and draws a big star with a long tail. He shows his mom. "Look, mom, I wrote about the comet!" he says proudly. Mom looks at his writing and drawing and claps her hands. She says, "Wow, Ben, you are a very good writer and artist. I love your comet. Can I keep it?" Ben smiles and says, "Yes, mom, you can keep it. It is for you. I love you, mom." Mom hugs Ben and says, "I love you too, Ben. You are my star."

Once upon a time, a little bird named Bluey lived in a small nest high up in a tree. Bluey liked to fly fast, but his friend, Snaily the snail, was very slow. They liked to play together and tell each other stories. One sunny day, Bluey saw Snaily looking sad. "What's wrong, Snaily?" Bluey asked. "I want to see your nest, but I am too slow to climb the tree," Snaily said with a sigh. Bluey had an idea. He picked up Snaily in his beak and flew up to the nest. Snaily was so happy to see Bluey's home. They spent the day telling stories and laughing in the nest. Even though Snaily was slow, they still had lots of fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a man with a big, warm coat. The man had a cone in his hand. Tim was curious and asked, "What's in the cone?" The man smiled and said, "I will reveal it soon. Just wait and see." Tim was excited and waited to see what was inside the cone. After a while, the man opened the cone. Inside, there was a big, scary bug! The bug jumped out and scared Tim. He cried and ran away from the park. The man laughed, but Tim was sad. The day did not end well for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The frog felt miserable because he had no friends. He wanted to find a friend to play with. One day, he saw a big box. He decided to unpack the box to see what was inside. Inside the box, the frog found a small, yellow ball. He thought, "Maybe I can use this ball to make a friend." So, the frog took the ball and went to the park. At the park, the frog saw a little girl. He hopped over to her and said, "Hi, do you want to play with me and my ball?" The little girl looked at the frog and said, "No, I don't want to play with you. You are just a green frog." The frog felt very sad and miserable. He went back to the box with his yellow ball, all alone. He never found a friend to play with, and he stayed miserable forever.
