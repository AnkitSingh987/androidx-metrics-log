Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play in her garden. One day, she found a small squash. Sue was very happy. Sue wanted to show her mom the squash, so she ran inside. But she saw her mom hang a picture on the wall. Sue thought it would be fun to hang the squash too. She found a string and tied it around the squash. Sue tried to hang the squash on the wall, but it was too heavy. The squash fell down and broke. Sue was very sad. She learned that not everything can hang like a picture. The moral of the story is to be careful with things that are not meant to be hung.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom had a job to do. He had to deliver a jug of milk to his friend, Sam the dog. Tom put the jug in a cart and went on his way. The sky was dark, and it looked like it might rain soon. As Tom walked, he saw a big puddle of mud. He thought, "I must be careful not to get the jug filthy." Tom went around the puddle, but he heard a loud noise. It was a big truck! The truck splashed mud all over Tom and the jug. Now, the jug was filthy. Tom was sad, but he knew what to do. He found a clean cloth and wiped the jug clean. Then, he continued on his way to deliver the jug to Sam. When Tom got to Sam's house, Sam was very happy. Tom had delivered the jug, and it was not filthy anymore. They both laughed and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a nice angel. She had big wings and a pretty smile. The nice angel liked to play with her friends in the sky. One day, the nice angel saw a big pool of water. She wanted to try something new and fun. So, she asked her friends to join her. They all got ready to dive into the water. The nice angel counted, "One, two, three!" and they all dove into the pool. The water felt cool and fun. They laughed and played all day. The nice angel was happy she tried something new with her friends. They all had a great time, and they promised to dive and play in the water again soon.

One day, a big bird with an enormous wing wanted to fly high in the sky. The bird wore a pretty apron with many colors. It loved to wear the apron while flying. The bird had a friend, a small bug. The bug liked to sit on the bird's back and go for a ride. The big bird said, "Bug, do you want to fly with me today?" The bug said, "Yes, bird! I love to fly with you." So, the big bird and the small bug started to fly high in the sky. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard, and the apron flew off the big bird. The bird and the bug looked down and saw a little girl holding the apron. The little girl said, "Is this your apron, bird?" The big bird said, "Yes, thank you!" The little girl gave the apron back, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird. The bird had a pretty egg. She wanted to keep her egg safe. So, she asked her friend, the honest fox, to watch it for her. The fox said, "Okay, I will keep your egg safe." The bird went away to find food. The fox sat by the egg. He started to feel hungry. He looked at the egg and thought, "This egg looks tasty." The fox ate the egg. When the bird came back, she asked, "Where is my egg?" The fox said, "I am sorry. I ate it." The bird was very sad and angry. The moral of the story is to not trust someone who may want what you have. The bird thought the fox was honest, but he was not. The bird lost her egg because she trusted the wrong friend.
