Title: Lily the Girl and the Pen

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to write a letter to her friend. She needed a pen to write the letter. So, she went to the shop to buy a pen. The shop was open, and she saw many pens inside. In the shop, Lily met a boy named Tom. Tom wanted the same pen as Lily, but there was only one left. They both looked at each other and said, "I want the pen!" They did not know what to do. Lily thought for a moment and said, "Tom, let's share the pen. You can use it first to write, and then I will use it." Tom agreed, and they both were happy. They learned that sharing is a good thing and made a new friend.

One dark night, Jim saw a crane stretching its long neck towards the moon. He was so excited and wanted to get closer, so he followed it. When they arrived at the pond, Jim asked the crane why it was stretching. The crane replied, "I am stretching because I want to touch the moon. I can't reach it yet, but I'm almost there! Jim said, "That's so cool! Can I help you reach the moon?" The crane replied, "Of course. I need a few more stretches. Can you help me?" So, Jim and the crane stretched their necks together while they stared at the moon. After a few minutes, the crane finally touched the moon and said, "I did it! Thank you for helping me, Jim." Jim smiled and said, "No problem. We did it together!"

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Tom. Tom lived near a big factory. He loved to play with his friends, the mice. One day, Tom and the mice were playing a game. They were teasing each other and having lots of fun. Tom said, "You can't catch me!" The mice laughed and said, "We will try!" They ran and played all day long. They went inside the big factory to play hide and seek. The factory was full of boxes and machines, which made it a fun place to play. As they played, something unexpected happened. The machines in the factory started to make noise. Tom and the mice were scared. They didn't know what to do. Then, a big, friendly robot came out of the factory. The robot said, "Don't be scared. I just want to play too!" Tom and the mice were happy. They played with the robot and had even more fun than before.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends. One day, while Spot was playing, he saw a big pile of lumber. Spot was curious and went to look at the lumber. As Spot got closer, he heard a tiny voice calling for help. "Help me, please!" said the voice. Spot looked around and saw a little bird stuck under the lumber. The bird was scared and couldn't get out. Spot wanted to rescue the bird, so he started to move the lumber. Spot tried very hard to rescue the bird, but the lumber was too heavy. Spot called his friends to help him. They all tried to move the lumber together, but it was still too hard. The bird was still stuck and couldn't get out. In the end, Spot and his friends couldn't rescue the bird. They were very sad and went back to their homes. The bird stayed stuck under the lumber, and Spot wished he could have helped.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty lion named Leo. He was big and strong. One day, Leo met a little bird named Bella. Bella was looking for her friend, the wise old owl. Leo asked Bella, "Why do you need the owl?" Bella said, "I hope to find a big book called a dictionary. It has many words in it. I want to learn new words to talk to my friends." Leo wanted to help Bella. They went to the wise old owl's tree. The owl said, "I have the dictionary. Let's learn new words together." They all sat down and started to read the big book. Leo, Bella, and the wise old owl became good friends, and they learned many new words.
