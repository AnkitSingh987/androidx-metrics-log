Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hat

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. It was a hot day, so he put on his hat and ran to the park. There, he saw his friend, Sue. Sue was playing with a big, icy ball. Tim thought it was very cool. "Hi, Sue!" Tim said. "Can I play with your icy ball?" "Of course, Tim!" Sue replied. "But we need to stay in the shade, or the ball will melt." Tim and Sue played with the icy ball under a big tree. They laughed and had fun, staying cool in the shade. The icy ball did not melt because they stayed away from the hot sun. Soon, it was time for Tim to go home. He said goodbye to Sue and the icy ball. Tim was happy that he got to play with his friend and stay cool on such a hot day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was playing with his toy car when he heard a loud noise. Tim looked around and saw a small bird with a broken wing. The bird was angry because it was hurt. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he went to his mom. His mom gave him some medicine for the bird. Tim took the medicine and went back to the bird. He put the medicine on the bird's wing, and the bird felt better. The bird was so happy that it started to fly again. But then, the bird saw a piece of paper on the ground. It wanted to play with Tim, so it tried to tear the paper. Tim laughed and played with the bird. They both had a fun day together.

Once there was a worm whose name was Stubbs. Stubbs was feeling very tired, so he decided to take a nap under a rock in the garden. But while he was sleeping, a bird saw the little worm! Stubbs was so scared that he tried to escape; he quickly wriggled out from under the rock. But the bird was quick too and chased him! Stubbs knew he had to hide, so he squirmed deep into the ground. When he was safe, Stubbs heard the bird say, “Where did the little worm go?” Stubbs was so relieved that he had escaped. He stayed very quiet until the bird flew away. Then Stubbs said to himself, “That was close! I need to get some rest because I am so tired.” So Stubbs crawled back to the rock and happily fell asleep. He slept until morning and then he went out to look for food. From then on, when Stubbs felt tired he always remembered to escape and hide somewhere safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big red stove. It was in a small kitchen. The stove was thick and strong. It loved to help cook food for a nice family. One day, the family wanted to make a cake. Mom put all the cake things in a big bowl. She mixed and mixed. Then, she put the mix in a round pan. Mom put the pan on the stove. She said, "Stove, please make a yummy cake for us." But, the stove did not want to make a cake. It wanted to do something new. So, the stove did a reverse. It took the cake mix out of the pan. It put the mix back in the big bowl. The family was very surprised. The family laughed and laughed. They thought it was funny. Mom said, "Okay, stove, we will try something else." They made cookies instead. The stove was happy to help. The family and the stove had a fun day. They all enjoyed the yummy cookies.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They saw a big, expensive toy car. Tim wanted it so much. "Mom, can I get the toy car, please?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Not today, Tim. Maybe next time." The next day, Tim and his mom went to the park. Tim saw a small trap on the ground. He didn't know what it was for. He thought it was a toy. Tim picked up the trap and took it home. In the evening, Tim's dad came home. He saw the trap and said, "Wow, Tim! This trap can catch a big animal!" Tim was surprised. He didn't know the trap was for catching animals. He felt sad and didn't want to hurt any animals. Tim and his dad took the trap back to the park. They put it in a safe place, so no animals would get hurt. Tim's dad said, "Good job, Tim. You saved the animals today." The next day, Tim's mom took him back to the store. She bought the expensive toy car for him. "You did a good thing yesterday, Tim. You deserve this toy," she said. Tim felt happy and played with his new toy car all day.
