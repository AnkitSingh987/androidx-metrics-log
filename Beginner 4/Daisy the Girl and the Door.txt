Category: Beginner
Title: Daisy the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy had an oven and she wanted it to bloom. With her mom's help, Daisy gathered some dough and set the oven to bake. After a few minutes Daisy opened the oven door. Inside was a funny sight - bread shaped like funny animals! Daisy smiled and cheered. "Let's take them outside," said Daisy's mom. So together, the two of them carried the funny loaves of bread outside. Daisy smelled the bread and said, "They smell so good! I can't wait to eat them!" They found a picnic blanket and some funny plates. But when they looked around, there was no butter anywhere to be found. Daisy's mom thought for a minute and said, "That's okay! Butter or not, this bread looks yummy. Let's sit down and eat them! So the two of them enjoyed their funny bread and had lots of fun. Daisy couldn't believe she had made such yummy bread all by herself. And she was so proud that her oven had bloomed just like she wanted it to.

One day, a little cat named Mimi went to the park to play. She loved to run, jump, and chase bugs. While playing, she saw a big can near a tree. Mimi went to look at the can. She examined it and saw that it had a picture of a fish on it. Mimi knew it was tuna, her favorite food. Mimi wanted to eat the tuna, but she couldn't open the can. She felt sorry that she couldn't eat it. Then, a little boy named Tim saw Mimi and the can of tuna. Tim wanted to help Mimi, so he went to his mom and asked for help. His mom had a tool to open the can. Tim and his mom went back to Mimi and opened the can of tuna for her. Mimi was very happy and ate the tuna. She thanked Tim and his mom for helping her. After eating, Mimi played with Tim in the park. They became good friends and had a fun day together.

Anna and Ben were hungry. They smelled something good from the kitchen. They ran to see what it was. Mom was cooking sausages for lunch. They looked brown and crispy. "Can we have some, Mom?" Anna asked. "Not yet, sweetie. They are still hot. You have to wait a little bit," Mom said. Anna and Ben nodded. They sat on the chairs and waited. They watched the sausages sizzle in the pan. They heard them pop and crackle. They smelled them and their mouths watered. They thought they would be delicious. Mom turned off the stove and took the pan. She put it on the table. She got some plates and forks. She was about to serve the sausages when the phone rang. "Sorry, kids. I have to answer this. It might be important. Don't touch the sausages, okay? They are very hot. I'll be right back," Mom said. She went to the living room and picked up the phone. She said hello and started to talk. Anna and Ben looked at each other. They looked at the sausages. They looked at Mom. She was not looking at them. She was talking and talking. She did not mention the sausages. Anna and Ben were very hungry. They could not wait any longer. They reached for the sausages. They grabbed them with their hands. They bit them with their teeth. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" they cried. The sausages were very hot. They burned their hands and mouths. They dropped the sausages and started to cry. Mom heard them and ran to the kitchen. She saw them and the sausages. She was angry and worried. "What did you do, kids? I told you not to touch the sausages. They are very hot. You could have hurt yourselves. Why didn't you listen to me?" Mom said. Anna and Ben sobbed. They were sorry and in pain. They did not get to eat the sausages. They did not taste delicious. They tasted awful. They had a bad ending.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big tree with red apples. Lily and Ben want to eat some apples. "Can we take some apples?" Lily asks Ben. "Yes, but we have to be careful. The tree belongs to Mr. Brown. He is not nice. He does not like children," Ben says. They look around and see no one. They climb the tree and take some apples. They are happy and hungry. But then they hear a loud bark. It is Mr. Brown's dog. He is big and black. He is loyal to Mr. Brown. He sees Lily and Ben in the tree. He runs to the tree and barks. "Who is there? Who is stealing my apples?" Mr. Brown shouts. He comes out of his house. He sees Lily and Ben. He is angry. "Get down from there! You are naughty children! I will call your parents!" he yells. Lily and Ben are scared. They drop their apples and climb down. They run away from the park. They do not look back. They learn a lesson. They should not take things that are not theirs. They should respect other people's property. They should be honest and kind.
