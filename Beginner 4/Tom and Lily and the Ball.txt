Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Ball

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they missed the ball and it rolled away. They ran after the ball and laughed. One time, the ball rolled too far and went to a man. The man was very hairy. He had hair on his face, his arms, his legs, and his chest. He wore a big hat and a coat. He picked up the ball and looked at Tom and Lily. "Hello, little ones. Do you want your ball back?" he asked. "Yes, please. That is our ball. We were playing with it," Tom said. "Well, you have to pay me for it. This is a nice ball. I like it. How much money do you have?" the man said. Tom and Lily did not have any money. They only had their ball. They did not know the man. He was a stranger. They felt scared. "No, we don't have to pay you. That is our ball. You have to give it back. You are not nice. You are mean," Lily said. She ran to the man and tried to grab the ball. The man pushed her away. He laughed. "Ha, ha, ha. You can't have it. It is mine now. Go away, little girl. You are too weak," he said. Tom was angry. He did not like the man. He did not like how he treated Lily. He wanted to help his sister. He looked around and saw a big stick. He picked up the stick and ran to the man. He hit the man with the stick. The man dropped the ball and cried. "Ow, ow, ow. That hurts. Stop it, little boy. You are too strong," he said. Tom and Lily took their ball and ran away. They did not look back. They did not want to see the man again. They found their mom and dad and told them what happened. Their mom and dad hugged them and said they were brave. They said they did the right thing. They said they should never talk to strangers or pay them for their things. They said they loved them. Tom and Lily felt happy. They played with their ball again. They smiled.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a raven. The raven had a cold. His nose felt icy. One day, he flew to find food. He saw a big red apple on the ground. He wanted to eat it. As the raven tried to pick up the apple, he sneezed. The apple rolled away. The raven chased the apple. It rolled and rolled. The raven sneezed again. The apple rolled faster. The apple rolled into a hole. The raven looked inside. He saw many apples. The hole was full of apples! The raven was happy. He ate the apples and sneezed no more.

Once upon a time, there was a big parade. Many people came to watch. They saw a big band march down the street. They played loud music for everyone to hear. The people clapped and danced as the band played. In a store close by, there was an expensive ornament. It was very pretty. The store owner put it in the window so people could see it. The ornament sparkled in the sun. Everyone who saw it loved it very much. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the store. It saw the ornament and thought it was a shiny toy. The bird picked up the ornament and flew away with it. The store owner was very surprised. He did not know what to do. But everyone laughed and clapped. They thought it was part of the parade. The store owner smiled too. He knew that sometimes, surprises can be fun.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Tiny. Tiny loved to play and laugh with his friends. One day, Tiny and his friends went to the park to do some exercise. While they were playing, a big cat came up to them. The cat said, "I want to play too!" Tiny's friends were scared, but Tiny said, "Okay, you can play with us!" So, the cat joined them, and they all did exercise together. After a while, the big cat got tired and wanted to go home. Tiny and his friends said goodbye. But when they looked around, they saw that the cat had taken all their toys! Tiny and his friends were very sad. They learned that not everyone can be trusted, even if they laugh and play with you.
