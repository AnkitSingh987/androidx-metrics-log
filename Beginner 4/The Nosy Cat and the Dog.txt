Category: Beginner
Title: The Nosy Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little cat. The cat liked to watch people. One day, the cat saw a big pot. The pot had steam coming out. The cat wanted to know what was inside. The cat asked a dog, "What is in the pot?" The dog said, "I don't know. But I heard it is very hot. Do not touch it." The cat did not listen. The cat wanted to see what was inside. The cat went to the pot and said, "I demand to see what is inside!" The cat jumped up and looked inside the pot. The steam was too hot, and the cat got hurt. The cat should have listened to the dog.

Lily liked to dance. She danced in the morning, she danced in the afternoon, and she danced at night. She danced with her dolls, she danced with her dog, and she danced with her mom and dad. Dancing made her happy. One day, Lily had to go to the toilet. She ran to the bathroom, but she saw a big spider on the wall. She was scared of spiders. She screamed and ran away. She did not want to go to the toilet anymore. Her mom heard her scream and came to see what was wrong. She saw the spider and said, "Don't worry, Lily. It's just a little spider. It won't hurt you. You can be brave and go to the toilet. I'll be right here with you." Lily looked at her mom and then at the spider. She wanted to be brave, but she was still scared. She said, "Can I dance first?" Her mom smiled and said, "Sure, you can dance first. Dancing can help you feel better. But then you have to go to the toilet, okay?" Lily nodded and started to dance. She danced around the bathroom, she danced on the rug, and she danced near the spider. She danced until she felt less scared. Then she said, "Okay, mom. I'm ready to go to the toilet." She went to the toilet and flushed it. She washed her hands and dried them. She looked at the spider and said, "Bye, spider. You can stay on the wall. I'm not scared of you anymore. I'm brave." Her mom hugged her and said, "You are very brave, Lily. And you are a great dancer. I'm proud of you." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, mom. Can we dance some more?" Her mom said, "Of course, we can dance some more. Let's go to the living room and dance with dad and the dog." They went to the living room and danced together. They had fun and laughed. Lily was happy. She liked to dance.

One day, a big dog named Buddy found a shiny diamond in the park. He thought it was a crazy toy to play with. Buddy picked up the diamond with his mouth and ran around the park, wagging his tail. Buddy saw his friend, a little girl named Lily. He wanted to show her the diamond. So, he ran to her as fast as he could. "Hurry, Lily! Look what I found!" Buddy barked. Lily looked at the diamond and smiled. "Wow, Buddy! That's a pretty diamond!" she said. Lily and Buddy played with the diamond all day. They threw it, chased it, and even hid it in the sand. They had so much fun with their crazy, shiny diamond. At the end of the day, they put the diamond back where they found it, so another friend could find it and have fun too.

Once upon a time, there was a youth named Tim. Tim was very impatient. One day, he found a big seed. He wanted to see it grow into a big tree fast. So, he went to the park and found a spot to bury the seed. Tim came back to the park every day. He wanted to see the tree grow. But the tree did not grow fast. Tim was sad and wanted to give up. Then, one day, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a little green plant poking out of the ground. He was so happy and danced around it. Tim learned that good things take time. He came to the park every day to take care of the little plant. As days went by, the plant grew into a big, strong tree. Tim was not impatient anymore. He knew that if he waited and took care of things, they would grow big and strong. And that was the moral of the story: patience is important.
