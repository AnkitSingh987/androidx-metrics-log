Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Park

One day, a shy cat named Tom made a plan. He wanted to find his favorite toy, a small red ball. He lost it a long time ago, but he had a memory of where it could be. He thought it was in the big park near his house. Tom went to the park and looked everywhere for his red ball. He looked under the trees, in the bushes, and near the swings. But he could not find it. He felt sad and thought his plan did not work. Just then, something unexpected happened. A little girl named Lucy saw Tom looking sad. She walked up to him and showed him a red ball she found. She asked if it was his. Tom was so happy! He did not know that Lucy had found his ball. His plan worked after all, and he made a new friend. They played with the red ball together and had lots of fun.

Lily loved to paint. She had many colors and brushes and papers. She liked to paint flowers and animals and rainbows. She was very happy when she painted. One day, she wanted to paint with her friend Ben. But Ben was sick. He had a fever and a cough and he stayed in bed. Lily felt sad for Ben. She wanted to make him feel better. She asked her mom, "Can I paint something for Ben?" Her mom said, "That's very kind of you, Lily. What do you want to paint?" Lily thought for a moment. She said, "I want to paint soup." Her mom smiled. She said, "Soup? Why soup?" Lily said, "Because soup is warm and yummy and good for sick people. Maybe if Ben sees my soup painting, he will want to eat some soup and he will get well soon." Her mom said, "That's a very compassionate idea, Lily. Compassionate means you care about others and want to help them. Let's go and paint some soup." Lily and her mom went to the kitchen and found a pot of soup. Lily looked at the soup and saw carrots and potatoes and noodles and chicken. She said, "This soup looks delicious. Can I have some?" Her mom said, "Of course, you can. But first, let's paint it." They took a big paper and some paints and brushes. Lily painted the pot and the soup and the spoon. She used orange and yellow and brown and white. She made the soup look very tasty. When she finished, she showed her mom. Her mom said, "Wow, Lily, you are a very good painter. Your soup painting looks very real. I'm sure Ben will love it." They put the painting in a bag and went to Ben's house. They knocked on the door and Ben's mom opened it. She said, "Hello, Lily and Lily's mom. Thank you for coming to visit Ben. He is feeling a little better, but he is still in bed." They went to Ben's room and saw him lying under a blanket. He looked pale and tired. He said, "Hi, Lily. Hi, Lily's mom. What did you bring me?" Lily said, "I brought you a painting, Ben. A painting of soup." She took out the painting and showed it to Ben. Ben's eyes widened. He said, "Wow, Lily, that's amazing. You painted soup? It looks so good. I can almost smell it." Lily said, "I painted it for you, Ben. Because I know you like soup and soup is good for you. I hope you like it." Ben smiled. He said, "I love it, Lily. Thank you so much. You are a very good friend. And a very good painter." He reached out and hugged Lily. Lily hugged him back. She said, "You're welcome, Ben. I'm glad you like it. I hope you get well soon." Ben's mom and Lily's mom smiled. They said, "You are both very compassionate children. Compassionate means you care about others and want to help them. We are very proud of you." They gave them some real soup and some cookies. They ate and talked and laughed. Lily and Ben felt happy. They learned that painting can be a way of showing love and kindness. And that soup can be a way of healing and sharing. And that compassion can be a way of making friends and making the world a better place.
