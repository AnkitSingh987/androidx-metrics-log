Category: Beginner
Title: The Volcano and the Town

Once upon a time, there was a big volcano. The volcano was near a small town. In the town, there were many people who felt helpless. They were scared of the volcano. One day, the volcano started to increase. It got bigger and bigger. The people in the town did not know what to do. They needed help. A brave girl named Lily decided to help. She went to the volcano and talked to it. She asked it to stop being so big. The volcano listened to Lily and stopped increasing. The people in the town were happy and no longer felt helpless. They all thanked Lily for her help.

One day, a flexible snake went for a walk. The snake was very happy. He had pretty, shiny skin. The snake liked to play in the sun. He liked to open his mouth and say hello to the bugs. While the snake was playing, he met a big frog. The frog was sad. He could not open his mouth to eat bugs. The snake wanted to help the frog. He had an idea. The snake used his flexible body to open the frog's mouth. The frog was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. The frog was not a frog. He was a prince! The prince said thank you to the snake. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken named Chicky. Chicky was a very graceful chicken who loved to dance and play with her friends. One day, Chicky and her friends were playing in the yard when they saw a big, red ball. "Look at that big ball!" said Chicky to her friends. They all ran to the big ball and started to play with it. They kicked the ball high into the air and laughed as they chased it around. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. The big ball started to float up into the sky! Chicky and her friends looked up and saw a big bird holding the ball in its beak. The bird laughed and said, "I wanted to play too!" Chicky and her friends all laughed together and played with the big bird and the big ball all day long.

Lily liked to climb. She climbed on chairs, on tables, on beds, on sofas. She climbed on everything she could find. She wanted to see what was up high and have fun. One day, she saw a big door in the hall. She had never opened it before. She wondered what was behind it. She climbed on a chair and reached for the handle. It was shiny and round. She turned it and pushed the door. She saw stairs. They went up and up and up. She was curious. She wanted to go up the stairs. She left the chair and the door and started to climb the stairs. She counted them as she went. One, two, three, four, five... She reached the top of the stairs. She saw another door. It was small and dark. She was a little scared. She did not know what was behind it. She thought maybe it was a monster or a ghost. She wanted to go back. But she was also brave. She wanted to find out. She reached for the handle. It was cold and hard. She turned it and opened the door. She saw a room. It was full of toys. There were dolls, cars, blocks, books, puzzles, and more. There was a window with a nice view. There was a bed with a soft blanket. There was no monster or ghost. It was a nice room. It was her big sister's room. She had gone to school. Lily smiled. She had found a new place to play. She climbed on the bed and picked up a doll. She was happy. She was brave. She had climbed the stairs.

Once upon a time, there was a little player named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball outside. One day, he felt uncomfortable because his shoes were too tight. He sat down on the grass and took off his shoes. A big bird saw Tim and flew down to him. The bird said, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim looked at the bird and said, "My shoes are too tight, and I feel uncomfortable." The bird had an idea and told Tim, "I will help you. I can urge you to try new shoes that are not tight." So, the bird and Tim went to the shoe store. In the store, they found many shoes for Tim to try. Tim tried on a pair of shoes that felt good and did not make him uncomfortable. He looked at the bird and said, "Thank you for urging me to find new shoes. Now I can play with my ball again." The bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Have fun playing!" Tim and the bird became good friends, and they played together every day. Tim was happy because he had new shoes, and he did not feel uncomfortable anymore.
