Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Valley

One day, a little boy named Tim was walking in a light, green valley. He saw a big tree with many red apples. Tim wanted to pick some apples to take home. He started to pick the apples one by one. A little bird came and asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim said, "I am picking apples to take home." The bird said, "Please leave some apples for us too. We need them to eat." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, I will leave some apples for you and your friends." He did not finish picking all the apples. He left some for the little bird and its friends. Tim went home with his apples and shared them with his family. They were happy and proud of Tim for thinking about the little birds. The moral of the story is to always think about others and share what you have.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to her grandma's house. Grandma had an old cat named Mr. Whiskers. Lucy liked to play with Mr. Whiskers. She would stare at him as he slept on his soft bed. "Grandma, can I give Mr. Whiskers a snack?" asked Lucy. Grandma smiled and said, "Yes, dear. But first, put a napkin on your lap so you don't get crumbs on you." Lucy put the napkin on her lap and fed Mr. Whiskers a small treat. They played and laughed all day. When it was time to go home, Lucy gave Mr. Whiskers a big hug. "Bye, Mr. Whiskers! I'll come play again soon," she said.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. She loved to play with her toys and have fun all day. One day, Lily wanted to gather all her toys and take them upstairs to play in her room. She picked up her dolls, cars, and blocks and carried them up the steps. But when she got to the top, she heard a strange noise. Lily felt scared and didn't know what to do. She called out to her dog, Max. He ran upstairs to be with her. Together, they looked around and found that the noise was just the wind blowing a tree branch against the window. Lily wasn't scared anymore, and she played with her toys upstairs with Max by her side. And they had a great day together.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a microphone. She was very happy. She wanted to write a song to sing with the microphone. Mia asked her friend Tom to help her. "Tom, will you help me write a song?" she asked. Tom was feeling miserable that day. He did not want to help Mia. He said, "No, I do not want to help you write a song." Mia was sad, but she did not give up. She thought, "I will write the song by myself." Mia worked hard to write her song. When she was done, she showed it to Tom. Tom saw how happy Mia was and felt bad for not helping her. He said, "I am sorry, Mia. I will help you sing the song." They sang the song together with the microphone, and Tom was not miserable anymore.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to study with her friend, Tim. They sat under a big tree with their books. Lily had a fancy bag with her. She said, "I have some food in my bag for us to eat later." After they studied for a bit, they got hungry. Lily opened her fancy bag to get the food. But, oh no! The food was gone! Lily and Tim were very sad. They did not know what happened to their food. Just then, a little squirrel came down from the tree. It had a big smile and some food in its paws. The squirrel said, "I took your food by mistake. I am sorry. Here, you can have it back." Lily and Tim were happy and thanked the squirrel. They all sat under the tree, ate the food, and studied together.
