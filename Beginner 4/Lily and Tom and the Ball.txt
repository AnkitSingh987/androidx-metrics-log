Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Ball

Lily and Tom were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and run after it. The ball was yellow and shiny and bounced high. But then a big dog came and saw the ball. The dog wanted to play too, but he did not know how to share. He ran and grabbed the ball in his mouth and ran away. Lily and Tom were very sad. They tried to chase the dog, but he was too fast and too enormous. "Give us back our ball!" Lily shouted. "That is our ball, not yours!" But the dog did not listen. He ran to the other side of the park and started to chew the ball. The ball made a loud pop and broke. The dog spat out the pieces and ran away again. Lily and Tom saw their ball was ruined. They felt very angry and very sad. They started to weep. They wept so loud that their mom heard them. She came and hugged them and asked what was wrong. "A big dog took our ball and broke it!" Tom sobbed. "It was our favorite ball!" Mom felt sorry for them. She wiped their tears and said, "I know, that was very mean of the dog. But don't worry, we can get you a new ball. A better ball. A ball that no dog can break. How about that?" Lily and Tom stopped weeping and looked at mom. They smiled a little and nodded. They liked the idea of a new ball. A better ball. A ball that no dog can break. "Thank you, mom!" they said. "You are the best mom!" Mom smiled and kissed them. She took them by the hand and walked to the store. They saw many balls there. Red balls, blue balls, green balls. But they chose a yellow ball. A shiny yellow ball that bounced high. They liked yellow balls. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a warm, soft carpet in a big room. A little boy named Tim and his friend, a lion named Leo, loved to play on the carpet. They would run and jump and sometimes, Leo would roar. One day, while they were playing, Leo said, "Tim, I can roar very loud. Do you want to hear?" Tim nodded and Leo roared as loud as he could. The roar was so loud that it made the carpet start to move! The carpet began to fly! Tim and Leo held on tight as they flew up into the sky. They were surprised but also very happy. They flew higher and higher, laughing and roaring together. And that is how Tim and Leo found out that their warm, soft carpet was also a magic flying carpet.

Once upon a time, there was a big owl who lived in a tree. The owl was very frustrated because he could not roar like a lion. He wanted to roar so that all the animals in the forest would hear him. One day, the owl met a little lion cub. The lion cub was sad because he could not fly like the owl. The owl and the lion cub became friends and they decided to help each other. The owl would teach the lion cub how to fly and the lion cub would teach the owl how to roar. They practiced every day. The owl tried to roar and the lion cub tried to fly. After a while, the owl finally roared and the lion cub flew a little bit. They were so happy that they could do what they wanted. The animals in the forest heard the owl's roar and saw the lion cub fly, and they all clapped and cheered. The owl and the lion cub were happy because they learned new things and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little mouse. The mouse was very kind and compassionate. He loved to help his friends in need. He had many friends, like the cat, the dog, and the bird. One day, the mouse saw the bird weep. The bird was sad because he lost his nest. The compassionate mouse wanted to help the bird. So, he went to the cat and the dog and asked them to help too. Together, the mouse, the cat, and the dog worked hard to make a new nest for the bird. The bird stopped weeping and was very happy. The compassionate mouse felt good that he could help his friend. From that day on, they all lived happily together in the small house.
