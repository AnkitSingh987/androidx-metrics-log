Category: Beginner
Title: Bob and the Window

Bob was playing in his yellow swimming pool. He was splashing around and having lots of fun. He tried to do a backstroke, but he wasn't very good at it yet. Suddenly, his mom called to him from the kitchen window. "Bob, time to stop swimming, it's dinner time." Bob didn't want to stop, he wanted to keep playing, but his mom kept calling. Bob called back to his mom. "No, I want to swim. Please let me keep playing." But his mom was very firm. "No, Bob, it's time to stop swimming." Bob was frustrated. He was so close to mastering his backstroke and now he had to stop. He slowly got out of the pool and went inside for dinner. But all night, he couldn't stop thinking about swimming in his yellow pool.

Once upon a time, a little bird named Tim wanted to find a new home. He flew around and saw a nice lawn. It looked like a good place to settle. Tim was very happy. Tim was careful when he built his nest on the lawn. He used soft sticks and leaves. His new home was cozy and warm. Tim liked his new home a lot. One day, a big wind came and blew Tim's nest away. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He built a new nest on the lawn. Tim was even more careful this time. Now, Tim and his new home were safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play football. Tim had a big, dependable friend named Sam. They played football together every day. One day, Tim got hurt while playing. "Ouch!" he cried. Sam came to help him. "Don't worry, Tim. You will heal soon," Sam said. Tim was sad because he could not play. But he knew Sam was right. After a few days, Tim felt better. His hurt was healed. Tim and Sam played football again. They were happy. They played together, and Tim knew he had a dependable friend in Sam.

Once upon a time, there was a cute cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with a big bookshelf. She loved to play and jump around all day. One day, Kitty was playing near the bookshelf. She jumped high and hit the books. The books started to scatter on the floor. Kitty was scared and didn't know what to do. Just then, a little mouse came out from behind the books. Kitty thought the mouse would be scared, but the mouse was not scared. The mouse said, "Hi, I'm Mousy. Let's be friends and put the books back together." Kitty and Mousy became best friends and they fixed the bookshelf. They played and had fun together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that could clap. The toy was harmless and made Tim happy. Tim liked to play with the clapping toy all day. One day, Tim saw a big screen in the living room. He wanted the clapping toy to see the screen too. So, Tim put the toy near the screen and they watched together. The screen showed pretty colors and happy songs. The clapping toy and Tim clapped along with the songs. Tim and the clapping toy had lots of fun watching the screen. They clapped, laughed, and played together. At the end of the day, Tim was very tired. He hugged the harmless clapping toy and went to sleep, dreaming of more fun days with his toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big, soft teddy bear named Ted. Sue loved Ted very much. Every night, she gave him a big hug before going to sleep. One day, Sue was playing with Ted in the park. A bad boy named Tom came over. He wanted to play with Ted too. Tom pinched Ted's arm and made him fall on the ground. Sue was very sad. Sue's mom saw what happened and came to help. She told Tom that it was not nice to pinch and make others sad. Tom said sorry to Sue and promised to be good. Sue gave Ted a big hug and they all played together happily.
