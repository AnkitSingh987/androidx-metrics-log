Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Treasure

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play near the creek. They would throw stones, catch frogs and look for shells. One day, they found a big sack near the water. It was heavy and wet. "Let's see what's inside!" Lily said. "Maybe it's a treasure!" Ben said. They opened the sack and saw something shiny. It was a lot of coins and jewels. They were very happy and excited. "Wow, look at this! We are rich!" Lily said. "Let's take it home and share it!" Ben said. They tried to lift the sack, but it was too heavy for them. They decided to drag it along the ground. They did not see the man who was hiding behind a tree. He was the one who had stolen the coins and jewels from a house. He was very angry and mean. He ran after Lily and Ben and shouted at them. "Hey, you little thieves! Give me back my sack!" Lily and Ben were scared and tried to run away. But the man was faster and stronger. He caught them and grabbed the sack. He pushed them into the creek and laughed. "Ha ha ha! This is what you get for being nosy! Enjoy your swim!" Lily and Ben cried and splashed in the water. They were cold and wet and hurt. They could not get out of the creek. They wished they had never found the sack. It was not fair.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride the subway with his mom. One day, they went on the subway to go to the park. Tim was very excited and wanted to go fast. But his mom told him to be careful. As they rode the subway, Tim saw a big red button. He wanted to push it. His mom said, "No, Tim. Don't push that button. It will make the subway quit." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. They had a fun day at the park. On their way back home, they got on the subway again. Tim was very tired. He saw the big red button again. This time, he was careful and did not push it. Just then, a man slipped and almost fell on the button. But Tim stopped him and saved the day. Everyone clapped for Tim, and he felt very happy.

One day, little Jenny was walking outside in her garden with her pet dog, Rover. Jenny wanted to play with her rubber ball, so she threw it up into the air. But when the ball came back down, Rover was so excited that he grabbed it in his teeth and ran off with it! Jenny felt very sad, because she couldn't run as fast as Rover. But then her mom came up with a plan. She told Jenny to stand in front of Rover and hold out her hand. When Rover came close, she should surrender the ball. At first, Jenny was worried because she didn't want to give up the rubber ball. But when Rover came near, she held out her hand and Rover put the ball in her hand. Jenny was so surprised that she laughed out loud. She had surrendered to Rover, but it turned out that Rover had surrendered to her! Jenny smiled and hugged Rover, happy that he had given her the rubber ball back. Jenny now knew that even the littlest things can be the most special.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the thick forest. He loved the forest because it was a fun place to play. As he walked, he saw a little bird with a broken wing. The bird had a fall from a tree and was very sad. Tim wanted to help the bird. He said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you." Tim picked up the bird and took it to his mom. She knew how to restore broken things. His mom put a bandage on the bird's wing. After a few days, the bird felt better. Tim and his mom went to the forest to let the bird go. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim and mom, for helping me." The bird flew away, happy and strong. Tim felt good because he helped a friend.
