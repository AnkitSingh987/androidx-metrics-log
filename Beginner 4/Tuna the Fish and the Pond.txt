Category: Beginner
Title: Tuna the Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Tuna. Tuna was orange and lived in a big pond. Tuna had many friends, but his best friend was a frog named Freddy. They liked to play and swim together. One day, Tuna and Freddy saw a big, round thing in the pond. They did not know what it was. Tuna said, "Let's push it out of the pond!" But they struggle to move the big, round thing. It was too heavy for them. Then, a big bird came and picked up the big, round thing. It was an egg! The big bird said, "Thank you for finding my egg!" Tuna and Freddy were happy they could help. They went back to playing and swimming, happy that they had a new friend in the big bird.

Once upon a time, there was a wide tree in a big park. A little bird lived in the tree. The bird had a nest with eggs. The bird was happy and sang every day. One day, a loud dog came to the park. The dog barked and ran near the tree. The little bird said, "Please, don't disturb my eggs." The dog stopped barking and said, "I am sorry. I will not disturb your eggs." The bird and the dog became friends. They played in the park for a week. The bird sang and the dog wagged its tail. They were happy and had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish who loved to nap. One day, the fish swam close to the shore. The sun was shining, and the fish felt warm and dry. The little fish thought it was a good time to take a nap. While the fish was napping, a big wave came and pushed the fish onto the shore. The little fish woke up, and it was very dry. The fish was scared, but it saw a kind bird nearby. The bird saw the fish and wanted to help. The bird picked up the fish in its beak and flew back to the water. The fish was happy and wet again. The bird and the fish became best friends, and they played together every day. And they both learned that naps are best taken in safe places.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a sign. The sign said, "Raise your hand to get a healthy snack." Tim wanted a healthy snack, so he raised his hand high. A girl named Lily saw Tim raise his hand. She wanted a healthy snack too. But there was only one snack left. "I want the snack!" Lily said. "No, I want it!" Tim said. They both wanted the snack, but there was only one. Tim and Lily talked to each other. "Let's share the snack," said Tim. "That's a good idea," said Lily. They both raised their hands together and got the healthy snack. They shared the snack and were happy. The conflict was over, and they became friends.

Robbie wanted to play with the model car that his dad had bought. He kept asking to play with it but his dad said he had to wait until after dinner. Robbie was disappointed but he knew his dad was the boss so he went to play with his blocks instead. After dinner, Robbie was so excited to finally play with his dad's model car. He could tell it was really fancy and he couldn't believe his dad had bought it. However, when he asked to play with it, his dad said they would do it the next day. Robbie was frustrated because he wanted to play with the model car now! His dad just smiled and said, "We'll play with it tomorrow. It will be more fun if you have to wait a little longer." Robbie thought this was a silly idea so he argued that he wanted to play now but his dad was determined to make him wait. Robbie huffed and said, "Fine. I'll have to delay my fun until tomorrow." The next day, Robbie was so excited to finally play with the model car. His dad let him drive it around the room and he was so happy that the silly delay was worth it.
