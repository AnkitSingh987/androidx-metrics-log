Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Park

Lily and her mom went to the park. Lily liked the park. She saw many birds and flowers and swings. She ran and played with her mom. She felt happy. "Mom, can I do the slide?" Lily asked. "Sure, honey. But be careful, okay?" Mom said. Lily nodded and ran to the slide. She climbed up the ladder and sat on the top. She looked down and saw a big purse on the ground. It was red and shiny and had many things inside. Lily was curious. "Mom, look! A purse!" Lily shouted. Mom looked and saw the purse too. She frowned and walked to the slide. "Lily, don't touch that purse. It's not ours. Someone might be looking for it," Mom said. "But Mom, it's so pretty. And lively. It has a bell and a flower and a book and a ball," Lily said. "I know, honey. But those things belong to someone else. Maybe they need them. How would you feel if you lost your purse?" Mom asked. Lily thought for a moment. She had a small purse that her grandma gave her. It had a picture of a cat and a candy and a sticker and a coin. She liked her purse very much. She would be sad if she lost it. "I would feel bad, Mom. I'm sorry. I won't touch the purse," Lily said. Mom smiled and hugged Lily. "That's very good, honey. You are very kind and smart. Now, let's go and see if we can find the owner of the purse. Maybe they are still in the park," Mom said. Lily agreed and slid down the slide. She held Mom's hand and they walked around the park. They looked for someone who might have lost the purse. They hoped they could make them happy.

Once there was a reindeer named Bobo. He was very comfortable in his home in the forest. Every morning he hopped out of bed and went for a walk. On one special morning, Bobo came across the most wonderful decorations in the clearing. He was so excited that he decided to decorate his home! He hung lights and bells from the trees and put a colourful ribbon on the door. But when Bobo finished, he noticed that the decorations looked very bad. He was so disappointed that he ran away and never came back.

One day, a little cat named Tom wanted to perform a dance. He practiced his dance all day and all night. Tom wanted to show his dance to his friends on a regular night. One night, Tom went to the park to perform his dance. But his friend, a big dog named Max, was there too. Max wanted to play and did not want to watch Tom dance. Tom was sad and did not know what to do. Tom thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He asked Max to dance with him. Max agreed, and they both performed the dance together. All their friends came to watch and had fun. Tom and Max were happy, and they danced all night long.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He had a lively shirt. The shirt was red and blue. Tim loved his shirt very much. One day, Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sam. Sam said, "I like your shirt, Tim!" Tim said, "Thank you, Sam! I like your hat!" They played and had fun. At the end of the day, Tim and Sam were tired. They said goodbye to each other. Tim went home and told his mom about his fun day. He was happy to have a lively shirt and a good friend like Sam.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a shiny bulb hanging on it. Tim asked his mom, "What is that bulb for?" His mom, being patient, said, "I don't know, let's find out." They walked closer to the tree and saw a little girl standing there. Tim asked the girl, "Do you know what the bulb is for?" The girl smiled and said, "Yes, I do. When you touch the bulb, it will answer a question you have." Tim was excited and touched the bulb. He asked, "What will I be when I grow up?" The bulb started to glow and said, "You will be a doctor." Tim and his mom were very surprised. The little girl then touched the bulb and asked, "Will I ever meet a real princess?" The bulb glowed again and said, "Yes, you will." The girl was so happy. Then, the bulb fell off the tree and broke into pieces. Tim, his mom, and the little girl looked at each other and laughed. They knew that the magic bulb had given them a special moment they would never forget.
