Category: Beginner
Title: True the Boy and the Store

One day, a boy named True went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some food for dinner. True was very excited because he loved going to the store with his mom. At the store, True saw a man being rude to the store lady. The man said, "I don't like this food. I want better food!" True felt sad for the store lady. He wanted to help her. True went to the man and said, "Please don't be rude. The store lady is nice." The man looked at True and said sorry. True's mom was proud of him. They bought their food and went home. True learned that being kind is important.

Lily liked to play with her dad's cables. He had many cables of different colors and shapes. He used them for his work, but sometimes he let Lily match them. She had to find two cables that were the same and plug them together. One day, Lily saw a new cable on the table. It was shiny and had a star on it. She wanted to match it with another cable, but she could not find one. She asked her dad, "What is this cable for?" Her dad smiled and said, "This is a special cable, Lily. It is for my famous friend. He is coming to visit us today. He is a singer and he needs this cable for his microphone." Lily was curious. She liked to sing, too. She wondered who the famous friend was. She hoped he would sing with her. Later, the doorbell rang. Lily ran to open it. She was surprised to see a man with long hair and a guitar. He smiled and said, "Hello, Lily. I'm your dad's friend. Do you know who I am?" Lily looked at him and then at the cable in her hand. She recognized the star on it. She gasped and said, "You are the star singer! I see you on TV! You have the same cable as me!" The man laughed and said, "That's right, Lily. I'm the star singer. And you have a very good eye. You matched the cable with me. Do you want to sing with me?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, please!" She gave him the cable and they went to the living room. Her dad plugged the cable into the microphone and the speaker. The star singer and Lily sang a song together. They had a lot of fun. Lily was happy. She had matched the cable with the famous friend.

One day, a little mouse named Tim saw a big piece of cheese. He wanted to eat it, but he needed to make sure it was safe. So, Tim decided to observe the cheese for a while. He looked at it from far away and saw that no one was around. He thought it was safe to get closer. As Tim got closer to the cheese, he met a friendly bird named Sue. Sue also saw the cheese and wanted some too. Tim asked Sue, "Do you think it's safe to eat the cheese?" Sue said, "I don't know, let's observe it together." So Tim and Sue watched the cheese very carefully. After a while, they saw that no one was coming to take the cheese away. They both felt it was safe to eat. Tim and Sue shared the cheese and became good friends. They were happy that they observed the cheese and made sure it was safe to eat.

Once upon a time, there was a flexible cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball all day. He had a friend, a dog named Max. They lived in a big house with their family. One day, Tom and Max found a mystery box in the garden. They did not know what was inside. Tom said, "Max, let's open the box!" Max agreed, and they opened the box together. Inside, they found a big red button. Tom and Max looked at the button and wondered what it did. Tom said, "I will push the button." When he pushed the button, the ball started to increase in size! It got bigger and bigger until it was as big as the house. Max and Tom were so surprised. They played with the big ball all day long. They had so much fun, and they were happy they found the mystery box.
