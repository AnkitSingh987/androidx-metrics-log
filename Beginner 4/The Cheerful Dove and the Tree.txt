Category: Beginner
Title: The Cheerful Dove and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful dove. The dove liked to fly and play with her friends. She had many friends, like the cat and the dog. They all played together in the sun. One day, the dove saw a big tree. She wanted to sit on the tree and blink her eyes. So, the dove flew up to the tree and sat on a branch. She blinked her eyes and looked around. It was fun to be up so high. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the branch. The cheerful dove lost her balance and fell down. She tried to fly, but it was too late. The dove got hurt and could not play with her friends anymore. The cat and the dog were very sad.

One day, a big red ball was in the mud. A boy saw the ball and wanted to play with it. The boy tried to push the ball out of the mud, but it was very hard. The ball did not want to move. A rude dog came to the boy. The dog liked to play in the mud too. The boy did not want the dog to play with the ball. The boy thought the dog would make the ball more dirty. But the dog wanted to help the boy. The dog started to push the ball with the boy. They pushed and pushed, and the ball moved. The ball was out of the mud! The boy and the dog were happy. They played with the ball together. The boy learned that the rude dog was not so bad after all. They became good friends.

Once there was a sad gold bird. She picked up a pebble and squeezed it tight in her beak. Suddenly, a voice called out to her. "Why are you so sad?" asked a friendly yellow bird. The sad gold bird looked up. "I want to fly, but my wings are too small," she said sadly. The yellow bird smiled. "Let me help you," he said. He flew up in the sky and gathered a small piece of rainbow. He pinched it in his beak and brought it back down to the ground. He squeezed it softly, and opened his beak. The gold bird gasped in surprise. Suddenly her wings had grown to be as big and bright and beautiful as a rainbow. The yellow bird smiled again. "Fly, little gold bird," he said encouragingly. The gold bird flapped her new wings, and flew away into the sky. She was no longer sad.

One day, a cheerful little girl named Lucy went with her mom to the grocery store. They needed to buy food for their home. Lucy loved going to the grocery store because she could help her mom pick out the fruits and vegetables. While they were shopping, Lucy saw a group of ants marching in a line. She was very excited and said, "Look, Mom! The ants are marching!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, they are! They are working together to find food, just like we are." Lucy and her mom finished shopping and went home. They put away the groceries and Lucy couldn't stop talking about the marching ants. That night, as Lucy went to bed, she felt happy that she could help her mom and learn about the ants too.

Lila and Tom like to play with marbles. They have many marbles of different colors and sizes. They roll them on the floor and make them go fast or slow. Sometimes they make a circle with the marbles and try to hit them with another marble. One day, they find a new marble in their toy box. It is very light and shiny. It looks like a star. Lila and Tom want to play with it. They take it out of the box and roll it on the floor. It makes a bright trail behind it. They think it is magic. But the marble is not magic. It is a sparkler. It is a thing that grown-ups use to make fire and light. Lila and Tom do not know that. They keep playing with it. They do not see that the sparkler is getting smaller and hotter. They do not hear that it is making a hissing sound. Soon, the sparkler is very small and very hot. It burns Lila's hand. She drops it on the floor. It burns the carpet. She screams. Tom screams. They run away from the fire. They call for mom and dad. Mom and dad come and see the fire. They are angry and scared. They use water to put out the fire. They check Lila's hand. It is red and sore. They put some cream on it. They hug Lila and Tom. They tell them to never play with sparklers. They tell them to only play with marbles that are safe and fun. Lila and Tom say they are sorry. They say they will not play with sparklers again. They say they will only play with marbles that are light and shiny.
