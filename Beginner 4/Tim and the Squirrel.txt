Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Squirrel

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog. He liked to run in the park. It was a sunny day and he was very happy. The park was big, with green grass and tall trees. Tim was very careful not to trip on the rocks. As Tim jogged, he saw a squirrel. The squirrel was playing with a stick. Tim wanted to play too, so he picked up a stick. He pretended to shoot with the stick like it was a toy gun. "Pew, pew!" Tim said, laughing. The squirrel liked the game and played with Tim. They were both very happy. They played all day in the park. Tim learned to be careful when he jogged and played. The squirrel became his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a doctor. He was a very selfish doctor. He did not like to help others. One day, a magic bug came to him. The bug could glow in the dark. The doctor was amazed. The bug said, "I will help you, but you must promise to be nice and help others." The doctor thought about it. He decided to be nice and help others. The next day, he went to the park. He saw a little girl who was sad. Her toy was stuck in a tree. The doctor used a stick to get the toy. The girl was happy. The doctor felt good. At night, the magic bug glowed for the doctor. The doctor was so happy. He had learned to be nice and help others. Now, he had a glowing bug friend too. The doctor and the bug lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a black cat named Boo. Boo liked to play with his friends. One day, Boo's friend, a bird named Tweet, came to visit him. Tweet told Boo about a new game they could play, but Boo had never heard of it before. Tweet said, "I will mention the game to our other friends, and we can play together." Boo was excited and asked if the game was available to play now. Tweet said, "Yes, the game is available. We just need to find a ball." So, Boo and Tweet went to find a ball. They looked everywhere, but they could not find one. Boo was sad because he wanted to play the new game. Just then, another friend, a dog named Barky, came to see what was going on. Barky saw that Boo and Tweet were sad. Barky asked, "What's wrong?" Boo and Tweet told Barky about the new game and how they could not find a ball. Barky smiled and said, "I have a ball we can use!" Barky went to get the ball, and soon, all three friends were playing the new game together. They had lots of fun and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun all day. One day, Spot found a big surf near the water. Spot wanted to perform a trick on the surf. He jumped on it and started to ride the waves. All his friends clapped and cheered for Spot. In the end, Spot performed the best trick and won a prize. He was so happy and shared his prize with all his friends. They all had a great day at the beach.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to make a spicy soup. He went to his mom and asked, "Mom, can I make soup?" His mom said, "Yes, I permit you to make soup, but be careful." Tim got a pot and put water in it. He put the pot on the stove and turned it on. He got some spicy peppers from the garden and put them in the soup. Tim was excited for the soup to start cooking. As the soup started to cook, Tim's dog, Spot, came into the kitchen. Spot smelled the soup and wanted to try it. When Tim was not looking, Spot jumped up and tasted the soup. Spot did not expect the soup to be so spicy! He barked and ran around the room. Tim laughed and said, "Silly Spot! Spicy soup is not for dogs!"
