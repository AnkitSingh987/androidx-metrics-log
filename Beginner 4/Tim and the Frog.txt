Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Frog

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. In the park, he saw a toy yacht near the pond. Tim wanted to play with the yacht, so he picked it up and put it in the water. As the yacht sailed on the pond, it suddenly sprang into the air! A talking frog jumped out of the water and landed on the yacht. "Hello, Tim!" said the frog. "I am a magic frog, and this is my magic yacht." Tim was very surprised. "Wow, a talking frog!" he said. "Can you do more magic?" The frog smiled and said, "Sure! I can make something sour taste sweet." The frog found a sour lemon and gave it to Tim. When Tim took a bite, it tasted like sweet candy! From that day on, Tim and the magic frog played together in the park, having many fun adventures with the magic yacht.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very independent. She liked to do things by herself. One day, Mia wanted to dress up all by herself. She went to her room and picked out her clothes. Mia's room had a small light. The light was not very bright, but Mia could see her clothes. She tried to put on her shirt, but it was hard. Mia tried again and again. She started to feel sad because she could not do it. Then, Mia's mom came into the room. She saw Mia was sad and asked her what was wrong. Mia told her mom she wanted to dress up by herself, but she couldn't. Her mom gave her a big hug and said, "It's okay, Mia. We can do it together." Mia smiled, and with her mom's help, she was able to dress up.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch TV all day. He thought TV was very good. He did not play outside with his friends. He only wanted to watch TV. One day, Tim's mom said, "No more TV. You need to play outside." She took the TV and locked it in a big box. Tim was very sad. He did not want to play outside. He wanted to watch his good TV. Tim tried to open the box, but it was locked. He could not watch TV. He did not learn to play outside. He was sad all the time. The moral of the story is to listen to your mom and learn to play outside.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tom and Joe. Tom wanted to borrow Joe's car, but Joe said he couldn't because it was too scary. Tom wanted to go fast in the car, but Joe said it was dangerous to go that fast. Tom was disappointed and asked his friend why. Joe said it was because he wasn't allowed to drive too fast because it was unsafe. Tom got an idea. He asked Joe if he could borrow his bike instead. Joe said yes, but he warned Tom to be careful because bikes go fast too. Tom said he would be careful, so Joe let him have the bike. Tom was glad he could go fast, but he was also a little scared. He peddled hard and went faster and faster. He was going so fast it felt like he was flying. When Tom saw how far he had gone, he got scared. He realized how fast he was going and that he needing to slow down. So Tom slowed down and eventually went home safe and sound.

Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Max. Max loved to drive his little red car. Every day, he would drive around the town and wave to his friends. One day, Max had a very special mission. He wanted to find a big cake for his friend's birthday. It was a big year for his friend, turning five years old. Max knew he had to find the best cake ever. Max drove his car to the cake shop. He looked at all the cakes, but he wanted something even more special. He asked the baker to make a cake with his friend's favorite toy on it. When the cake was ready, Max drove it to his friend's house. Everyone at the party was so happy, and Max felt proud to be such a clever cat.
