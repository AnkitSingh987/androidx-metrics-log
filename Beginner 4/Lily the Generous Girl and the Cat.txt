Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Generous Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a generous girl named Lily. She liked to visit her friend, Stitch, who was a cute little cat. They played together every day and were very happy. One day, while they were playing, Stitch got hurt. Lily saw that her friend needed help. She knew what to do. She got a band-aid and put it on Stitch's boo-boo. Stitch felt better right away. Lily was always kind to her friends. She liked to share her toys and her snacks. All the animals in the neighborhood loved to visit Lily and Stitch. They had many fun days together, and everyone was happy.

Once there was a man who was lost in the forest. He had been walking for hours, but he couldn't find his way out. Suddenly, he saw a deep pit in the ground. He tried to climb in but he was too hurt. He decided he would have to crawl out. He had to move slowly, but he was determined to get out. As he crawled, he could see that the walls of the pit were getting closer. He was almost there when he hit a snag. His foot got stuck and he couldn't move. He was so scared he shouted for help. Fortunately, a passerby heard his shouts. He came to the man's rescue and helped him out of the pit. He was so relieved to be free from the deep pit that he hugged the passerby and thanked them. The man was so very thankful that he found his way back to safety. He knew he had come close to danger, but now he was safe, and that was all that mattered.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Tom. Tom liked to look at everything and ask questions. One day, he saw a big red ball in the yard. He wanted to play with it, but he did not know how. Tom asked his friend, the dog, "How do you play with the ball?" The dog said, "I can show you. I can push it with my nose and chase it." Tom watched the dog play and thought it looked fun. He tried to manage playing with the ball like the dog, but it was hard for him because he was a cat. Just when Tom was about to give up, a little bird flew down and said, "You can play with the ball too, Tom. You just need to use your paws." Tom tried using his paws and found it was much easier. The nosy cat, the dog, and the bird all played with the big red ball together and had a great day.

One day, a kind girl named Amy went to the park. She brought a big cooler with her. Inside the cooler, there were yummy snacks and cold drinks. Amy wanted to share with her friends. At the park, Amy saw her friend, Tom. Tom was sad because he was hot and thirsty. Amy said, "Tom, would you like a cold drink? I have some in my cooler." Tom's face lit up and he said, "Yes, please, Amy!" Amy offered Tom a cold drink from her cooler. They both sat under a big tree and enjoyed their snacks and drinks. Tom was very happy, and he thanked Amy for being so kind. They played together all day and had lots of fun at the park.

One day, Mark and his friend, Cat, went to play in the park. Mark was very happy. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Cat was scared, but Mark said, "Don't worry, I will be careful." Mark started to climb the tree. He went higher and higher. Cat looked up and saw Mark near the top. Then, Mark slipped and fell. He hit the ground with a thud. Cat ran to Mark and saw that he was hurt. Cat was very sorry for Mark. Cat hugged Mark and said, "I will help you." They walked back home, with Cat helping Mark all the way. Mark was still hurt, but he felt better because of his friend's hug. From that day, Mark and Cat knew that they would always be there for each other.
