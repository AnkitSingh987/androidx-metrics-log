Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a big, rough bear named Ben. Ben lived in a deep, dark forest. He had many friends, like the little rabbits and the nice birds. One day, Ben saw a bright light in the sky. It was lightning! It made a loud noise, and Ben was scared. He ran to his friends for help. The rabbits and birds told him not to worry, the lightning would vanish soon. As they all waited together, the lightning did vanish. The sky was blue again, and the sun was shining. Ben was happy, and he thanked his friends for helping him feel better. They all played and laughed together for the rest of the day.

Lily and Ben like to play with their toys. They have a big case with many toys inside. They can make cars, houses, animals and more. They have fun with their toys every day. One day, Lily and Ben want to play with their toys outside. They take their case and go to the garden. They find a nice spot under a tree. They open their case and start to make things. They make a car, a house, a dog and a cat. They are happy. But then, it starts to rain. The rain is very wet and cold. Lily and Ben are sad. They do not want their toys to get wet. They do not want their case to get dirty. They want to go inside. They hurry to pick up their toys and put them back in their case. But the case is too heavy. They cannot carry it by themselves. They see Mommy coming. Mommy is helpful. She sees Lily and Ben and their case. She says, "Do not worry, I will help you." She takes the case and holds it with one hand. She takes Lily and Ben and holds them with the other hand. She takes them inside. She is strong. Inside, Mommy wipes the case with a cloth. She wipes the toys with another cloth. She makes them dry and clean. She says, "Your toys are safe now. You can play with them again later." She gives Lily and Ben a hug and a kiss. She says, "You are good children. You took care of your toys. I am proud of you." Lily and Ben smile. They say, "Thank you, Mommy. You are the best." They love Mommy. They love their toys. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. The tree was very tall and could see a lot. It had a great view of the whole park. Many animals lived in the tree, like birds and squirrels. One day, a little bird told the tree a secret. The bird said, "I know where the best food is in the park." The tree listened and remembered the secret. The tree knew that one day, it could help someone with this secret. Later, a hungry squirrel came to the tree. The squirrel said, "I am so hungry, but I cannot find any food." The tree remembered the secret the bird had told it. The tree said, "I know where the best food is. Go to the big bush near the pond." The squirrel went to the big bush near the pond and found lots of food. The squirrel was very happy and thanked the tall tree. From that day on, the animals in the park knew the tall tree was very wise and could help them.

One day, a helpful boy named Tim went to the station with his mom. They wanted to take a train to visit grandma. Tim saw a big train with lots of people. He was very excited to get on the train. While they were waiting, Tim saw a little girl who was sad. She had a toy that she could not squeeze. "Why are you sad?" Tim asked the girl. "I want to squeeze my toy, but it's too hard," she said. Tim wanted to help the girl. He took the toy and tried to squeeze it. With a little bit of help from his mom, they made the toy soft. The girl was happy, and they all got on the train together. Tim felt good because he was helpful.
