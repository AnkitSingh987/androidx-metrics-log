Category: Beginner
Title: Spike and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball named Spike. Spike lived in a modern house with his friend, a small, blue ball named Bounce. They loved to play and roll around all day. One sunny day, Spike and Bounce went outside to play in the yard. They rolled and bounced on the soft, green grass. They were having so much fun. But then, Spike hit a rock and stumbled. He rolled and rolled until he bumped into a tree. Bounce came to help Spike. They both laughed and went back to their modern house. They were tired but happy. From that day on, they always looked out for rocks when they played outside. And they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Jen are twins. They like to play hide and seek in the big house. One day, they play when Mom and Dad are not home. They are very quiet so no one will hear them. Tom counts to ten and then looks for Jen. He looks in the kitchen, the living room, the bathroom, but he cannot find her. He goes upstairs and sees a shoe on the floor. It is Jen's shoe. He thinks she is hiding in the closet. He opens the door and says, "I found you!" But Jen is not in the closet. There is a big hole in the floor. Tom sees Jen at the bottom of the hole. She is not moving. She is hurt. Tom screams and cries. He runs to the phone and calls Mom and Dad. He tells them what happened. He is very scared and sad. Mom and Dad come home fast. They see the hole and Jen. They call the doctor. They try to help Jen. But it is too late. Jen is gone. Tom and Jen will never play hide and seek again. Tom wishes he never found the shoe. He wishes he never opened the door. He wishes he never played in the big house. He wishes he had Jen back.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely boy named Tom. He had no friends to play with. One day, Tom found a big red ball in his yard. He wanted to play with it. He gave the ball a big push and it rolled away. As Tom chased the ball, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue was wearing a pretty shirt with flowers on it. Tom asked Sue if she wanted to play with the big red ball. Sue said yes, and together they pushed the ball back and forth. Tom and Sue played with the big red ball all day. They laughed and had fun. Tom was not lonely anymore. He found a new friend in Sue. They played with the big red ball every day and were very happy.

One day, a grumpy cat named Tom watched his favorite cartoon on TV. He liked the cartoon a lot, but he thought he could improve it. He wanted to make the cartoon funnier and more fun to watch. Tom had an idea. He went to find his friend, a happy dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, let's make our own cartoon. We can make it better and more fun!" Max liked the idea and they started to work on their cartoon. As they worked, something unexpected happened. A funny bird named Lucy joined them. Lucy was very good at making funny sounds and faces. With Lucy's help, their cartoon became even better than they ever thought it could be. Tom was not grumpy anymore, and they all laughed and had fun making the best cartoon ever.

One day, a playful dog named Max went to the beach. Max liked to run and play in the sand. He liked to chase birds and jump in the water. Max had a lot of fun at the beach. While Max was playing, he found a pretty shell. The shell was small and had many colors. Max picked up the shell with his mouth. He wanted to show the shell to his friend, Sam. Max ran to Sam and showed him the shell. Sam liked the shell too. They both played with the shell and had a fun day at the beach. Max and Sam were happy they found the shell and could do something fun together.
