Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tooth

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a tooth that was not like the others. This tooth was brilliant and shiny. Tim liked his special tooth very much. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's brilliant tooth and said, "Wow, your tooth is so shiny! How did it become like that?" Tim smiled and said, "I don't know, but I like it a lot!" But then, one day, Tim ate a big, hard candy. His brilliant tooth broke! Tim was very sad. Sue saw Tim and asked, "What happened to your shiny tooth?" Tim said, "I ate a hard candy and now my tooth is gone." Sue felt bad for Tim, but there was nothing she could do. From that day on, Tim was always careful with his teeth, but his brilliant tooth was gone forever.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the farm with her mom. They wanted to buy some oats to make yummy cookies. Amy saw a bald man with a big hat. The bald man was the farmer. He had a big smile and said, "Hi, Amy! Are you here to get some oats?" "Yes," said Amy. "I want to make cookies with my mom." The farmer told her to listen to the sound of the wind in the oat field. "When you hear the wind, you'll know the oats are ready," he said. Amy and her mom walked to the oat field. They listened very carefully. Soon, they heard the wind blowing through the oats. "Mom, I hear it!" Amy said excitedly. They picked the oats and thanked the farmer. At home, Amy and her mom made the best cookies ever. Amy knew that listening to the wind in the oat field was the secret to finding the best oats. And the bald farmer with the big hat was always there to help them. From that day on, every time they made cookies, they thought of the kind farmer and his special advice.

Once upon a time, there was a car named Tim. Tim was a tough car. He liked to drive fast and far. One day, Tim went to the fuel place to get some fuel. He needed fuel to go fast. While getting fuel, Tim saw another car. The car was big and red. Tim said, "Hi, I am Tim!" The big red car said, "Hi, I am Sam!" They were happy to meet each other. They decided to race to see who was the fastest car. Tim and Sam started to race. They went very fast. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. A big tree fell on the road. Tim and Sam had to stop. They were scared, but then they saw a little bird on the tree. The bird said, "Thank you for stopping! You saved my life!" Tim and Sam were happy they could help. They became friends with the bird and forgot about the race. They learned that being kind was more important than being fast.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an incredible vest. It was red, blue, and yellow. He loved to wear it every day. One day, Tim went to the park. He saw a big crab. The crab was very close to him. Tim wanted to touch the crab. But the crab tried to pinch him! Tim was scared and ran away. Tim went back to his mom. He told her about the crab. His mom said, "Be careful, crabs can pinch!" Tim was happy he had his incredible vest. It made him feel brave.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the field to play. He was very enthusiastic to throw a ball with his friends. They were all happy to play together. While they were playing, a little dog came running into the field. The dog picked up the ball in its mouth and started to run away. Tim and his friends were surprised and didn't know what to do. Tim had an idea. He picked up a stick and threw it far away. The dog saw the stick, dropped the ball, and ran after the stick. Tim and his friends got their ball back and continued to play. They were happy that the dog wanted to play too, and they all had fun together.
