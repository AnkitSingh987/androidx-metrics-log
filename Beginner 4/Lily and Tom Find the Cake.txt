Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom Find the Cake

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play together. Today, they want to make a surprise for Lily's mom. They prepare a cake in the kitchen. They mix flour, eggs, milk, and sugar in a big bowl. They pour the batter in a pan. They put the pan in the oven. "Shh, we have to be quiet," Lily says. "Mom is in the living room. She does not know we are making a cake." "OK, I will be quiet," Tom says. "But how will we know when the cake is ready?" "We will smell it," Lily says. "When it smells sweet and yummy, it is ready." They wait for a while. They smell something good. They open the oven. The cake is golden and fluffy. They take it out carefully. They put it on a plate. They decorate it with soft whipped cream and colorful sprinkles. "Wow, it looks so good," Tom says. "Can we eat some?" "Not yet," Lily says. "We have to wait for mom. It is her surprise. Come on, let's hide behind the door." They carry the plate to the door. They hide behind it. They hear mom coming. They whisper to each other. "Surprise!" they shout as mom opens the door. They show her the cake. Mom smiles and hugs them. "Oh, thank you, my sweeties," mom says. "You made me a cake. How thoughtful of you. It looks delicious. Did you do it all by yourselves?" "Yes, we did," Lily and Tom say proudly. "We prepared it for you. We love you, mom." "I love you too, my darlings," mom says. "You are the best. Come on, let's eat some cake together."

Once upon a time, there was a nice witch who lived in a small house. She loved to cook and taste her food. One day, she made a big pot of soup. She wanted to share her soup with her friends in the forest. The witch went to her friend, the helpless bunny. She said, "Bunny, do you want to taste my soup?" The bunny was very happy and said, "Yes, please!" The bunny tasted the soup and loved it. The witch was happy to see her friend enjoy the soup. But then, something unexpected happened. The bunny turned into a big, strong lion! The witch and the lion were both surprised. The witch realized that her soup had magic in it. The lion thanked the witch and said, "Now I am not helpless anymore. Thank you for your magic soup!" The witch and the lion became best friends and shared the magic soup with all their friends in the forest.

Lily and Sam liked to play in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two buttons for eyes. They were very happy with their snowman. But then they saw a big red sled. They wanted to ride on it. They ran to the sled and tried to fit on it. But it was too small for both of them. Lily pushed Sam off the sled. Sam pushed Lily off the sled. They started to fight. The snowman saw them fighting. He felt sad. He wanted them to be friends. He rolled down the hill and bumped into the sled. The sled broke into pieces. Lily and Sam stopped fighting. They saw the broken sled and the snowman. They felt sorry. They ran to the snowman and hugged him. They said they were sorry for breaking the sled and for fighting. They said they liked the snowman and each other. The snowman smiled. He was happy again. Lily and Sam were tired. They decided to go home. They said goodbye to the snowman and thanked him for being their friend. They walked home holding hands. They were happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple dog. The dog had a spot on his back. The spot was very special to him. The dog loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, it started to rain. The dog did not want to go inside. He wanted to play in the rain. He jumped in a puddle and got all wet. The dog was so happy to soak in the water. After the rain stopped, the purple dog went home. He was wet and had so much fun. His spot was still there, and he was happy. The dog knew he would play in the rain again soon.
