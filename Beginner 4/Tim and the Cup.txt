Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to tell stories to his friends. One day, Tim and his friends went to the park to play. While they were playing, they found a magic coffee cup. The coffee cup was smooth and shiny. Tim and his friends picked it up and looked at it. They knew it was special. Tim decided to tell a story about the magic coffee cup. He said that the coffee cup could make all their wishes come true. Tim and his friends took turns making wishes, and they all had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a gray rat. The gray rat liked to organize his things. He had a little room where he kept all his toys and food. One day, the gray rat met a big cat. The cat said, "Hello, little rat. What are you doing?" The gray rat replied, "I am organizing my room. Do you want to help me?" The cat thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I will help you." The gray rat and the big cat worked together to organize the room. But when they finished, the big cat was very hungry. The cat looked at the gray rat and said, "I am sorry, little rat, but I am very hungry." Then, the big cat ate the gray rat.

Once upon a time, there was a big black dog named Sam. Sam liked to watch the kids play at the park. He would sit on the grass and wag his tail. Sam was a very good dog. One day, Sam saw the kids playing with some equipment. They were playing with a ball, a bat, and a glove. Sam wanted to play with the kids and their equipment too. He barked and ran to them. The kids saw Sam and laughed. They let Sam play with them. They threw the ball, and Sam ran to get it. The kids clapped and cheered for Sam. They all had fun playing together at the park.

One day, Tim and Sue found a fancy map. The map was very pretty with fun drawings. It suggested where to find a big, shiny treasure. They were very excited and wanted to find it. Tim and Sue walked and walked, following the map. They saw a big tree and a little pond. Then, they found a big, red X on the ground. They thought the treasure was there. They dug and dug, but there was no treasure. Suddenly, a big bird came down and took the fancy map. Tim and Sue were sad. They thought the bird took their treasure. But then, they saw the bird put the map in a nest. Inside the nest, they found many shiny things. The bird was the treasure keeper!

Once upon a time there was a special doll. Her name was Zippy. Zippy had bright blue eyes and she liked to dress up in fancy clothes. One day Zippy and her friend decided to go for a swim. They grabbed their towels and ran to the beach. When they got to the water Zippy was a bit scared. The water looked so deep and blue. But then her friend held out her hand and said “Come on, let’s zip in!” Zippy smiled and took her friend’s hand. Together they jumped into the deep water. They played and laughed and splashed about. It was so much fun! Finally, when they were tired, they climbed out of the water and dried off in the sunshine. Zippy and her friend were happy and they promised to go swimming together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a broken leg. He was sad because he could not play with his friends. One day, Spot went for a walk with his mom. They went to the park. Spot wanted to find something to make him happy. He looked and looked. Then, Spot saw a big, red ball. He hopped over to it and started to play. Spot was happy. His broken leg did not hurt anymore. Spot and his mom went home, and he played with the big, red ball all day.
