Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Leaves

One day, a boy named Tim saw a huge pile of leaves. He wanted to play in it. So, he took his rake and started to gather more leaves. He rushed to put all the leaves in one big spot. As he was working, his friend Sam came by. "What are you doing?" Sam asked. "I am making a big pile of leaves to jump in," Tim said. Sam thought it was a fun idea, so he helped Tim rake the leaves. When they were done, they had a huge pile of leaves. They were so excited to jump in. But as they rushed to jump, they did not see the big rock hiding in the leaves. They both jumped and landed on the rock. "Ouch!" they cried. They were hurt and sad. The fun day ended with a bad ending.

One day, a colorful bird named Bob wanted to make a cake. He had a big bowl to mix all the things. He put in flour, eggs, and milk. Then, he began to mix them all together. "Mix, mix, mix!" sang Bob as he mixed the cake mix. While he mixed, he saw a strip of paper on the table. The strip had a fun game on it. It said, "Find the hidden toy!" Bob stopped mixing and started to look for the toy. He looked under the table, behind the door, and in the plants. Then, he saw something shiny near the cake mix. "Wow! I found the toy!" said Bob. But when he picked it up, he saw it was not a toy. It was a spoon! The spoon had fallen into the cake mix when he was mixing. Bob laughed and said, "That was a fun game, but now I need to finish my cake!"

Once upon a time there was a grumpy man who lived in a big house. He had lots of things to do but he didn't have any nails to fix them. One day he went outside and saw a little bunny in the garden. The bunny said, "Good morning, grumpy man! I can help you with lots of nails." The man said, "Why thank you, little bunny, that would be great!" So the little bunny hopped off and brought back lots of nails to help the grumpy man. The man then used the nails to fix all his things. The bunny hopped away and waved goodbye. The man said, "Thank you so much for the nails, little bunny!" And they both went on their merry way.

Once upon a time there was an old man who had an exciting adventure. He was walking down the street one day when he heard a noise. He paused and noticed a bright light in the sky. He paused again and watched as an old spacecraft flew down. The old man was so excited he followed the spacecraft. The old man came to a big clear space in the woods. He paused and saw the spacecraft parked in the middle. He slowly walked up to it and saw a door. He paused for a minute before he opened the door. Inside the spaceship was a man. The old man smiled and said, "Hey!" The man in the spaceship looked surprised. He said, "Hello, who are you?" The old man replied, "I'm here for an adventure!" The man smiled, asked the old man to come in, and the two of them talked until the sun went down. They had a wonderful adventure together.

Once upon a time, there was a barber named Tim. He loved to cut hair. Tim's shop was cheap, so many people came to see him. One day, a big man came to Tim's shop. The big man wanted a hair cut too. While Tim was cutting the big man's hair, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and started to destroy the barber shop. The wind blew very hard, and the walls shook. Tim and the big man were scared. But then, the big man stood up and blew back at the wind. The wind stopped and the barber shop was safe. Tim was very happy. He gave the big man a free hair cut. They became good friends, and the barber shop was never destroyed again.
