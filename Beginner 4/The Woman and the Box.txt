Category: Beginner
Title: The Woman and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a woman who loved to shine. She had a big, open house with many windows. The sun would shine through the windows and make her house very bright. The woman was very happy. One day, the woman saw a big box outside her open door. She was very curious. She thought maybe there was a gift inside the box. She decided to bring the box into her house. The woman opened the box, and a big wind blew out. The wind was so strong that it closed all the windows and doors. The house was now dark, and the woman could not make it shine again. She was very sad. The woman learned to be careful with things she did not know.

Mama and Lilly were walking in the park. It was sunny and warm. Mama said, "Look at the blue sky, Lilly! Isn't it pretty?" Then they saw a big tree with branches reaching up high. The branches had some thin, green strips hanging from them. Mama looked up at the strips. She said, "These are leaves, Lilly. Leaves provide us with shade and food. They also make our world more beautiful." Lilly smiled. Then she noticed some strips on the ground that were yellow and brown. She asked, "What are those strips, Mama?" Mama replied, "Those strips are dangerous. They come from the tree, but they can be harmful if you touch them. We should stay away from them." Lilly nodded and said, "Okay, Mama." They looked at the leaves one more time and then kept walking. Lilly liked what the leaves provided.

Once upon a time, there was a reliable tree. The tree was big and strong. It lived in a nice forest with many friends. The tree was always there to help when someone needed it. One day, the wind started to blow hard. The tree tried to stay strong, but then it heard a crack. A big branch fell down. The tree was sad. It did not want to lose its branch. The tree's friends wanted to help it feel better. They decided to change the tree into something new. The tree's friends worked together to make the tree into a fun swing. The tree was happy again. It was still reliable and could help its friends have fun.

Lily was playing with her toys in her room. She liked to make them talk and have fun. She had a doll, a bear, a car and a sock. The sock was her favorite. She liked to squeeze it and pretend it was a soft animal. One day, her mom came in and said, "Lily, it's time to clean up your room. You have too many toys. You need to give some away to other kids who don't have any." Lily felt sad. She did not want to give away her toys. She loved them all. She looked at her sock and hugged it. "Mom, can I keep my sock?" she asked. "It's not really a toy. It's just a sock. I need it to keep my foot warm." Mom smiled and said, "Of course you can keep your sock, honey. But you have to choose some other toys to give away. How about the car? You don't play with it much." Lily thought for a moment. She did not like the car very much. It was hard and noisy. She said, "Okay, mom. I will give away the car. But can I keep the doll and the bear?" Mom nodded and said, "Yes, you can keep the doll and the bear. They are your friends. But you have to be grateful for what you have, Lily. Some kids don't have any toys at all. They would be happy to have the car." Lily understood. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I'm grateful for my toys. And I'm grateful for you. I love you, mom." Mom hugged Lily and said, "I love you too, Lily. You are a good girl. Now let's put the car in this bag and take it to the charity shop. Maybe someone will squeeze it and love it like you love your sock."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go on walks with his mom and dad. They would walk in the park, by the lake, and sometimes in the woods. One day, while they were walking, Tim found a big rock. The rock was not pretty or special, but Tim liked it because it was cheap. He did not have to pay for it. He picked it up and took it home. In his room, Tim put the rock on his shelf. He looked at it every day. Tim's mom told him, "You can make the rock special by giving it love and care." So, Tim painted the rock with bright colors and made it pretty. He even gave it a name: Rocky. One day, Tim's friend Sarah came over to play. Sarah saw Rocky and laughed. She said, "That's just a cheap rock. It's not a real toy." Tim felt sad, but he remembered what his mom said. He told Sarah, "Rocky is special to me because I gave it love and care." Sarah thought about it and said, "You're right, Tim. It's not about how much something costs. It's about the love we give to it." They played with Rocky together, and from that day on, they both learned that the most important thing is the love and care we give, not how much something costs. And they all lived happily ever after.
