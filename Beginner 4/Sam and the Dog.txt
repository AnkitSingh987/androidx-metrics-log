Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Dog

Once upon a time, a very hairy dog named Sam wanted to travel to find a new toy. Sam liked to read books and his favorite was a novel about a brave dog who went on adventures. He wanted to be like the dog in the novel. One day, Sam saw a big hill and thought his toy was on the other side. He tried to go up the hill, but it was too steep. Sam was sad because he could not climb it. He sat down and thought about how he could reach the other side. Sam saw a big stick and had an idea. He pushed the stick up the hill and used it to help him climb. It was hard, but Sam kept trying. Finally, Sam reached the top of the hill and saw his new toy on the other side. He was very happy and proud that he did not give up. The hairy dog traveled back home with his new toy and continued to read his novel, dreaming of more adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a nice nurse named Sue. Sue wore a pink dress every day. She liked to help people feel better. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the hospital. Lily was sad because she was sick. Sue, the nurse, took care of Lily. She gave her a pink blanket and a hug. Lily started to feel better. Lily began to admire Sue. She wanted to be a nurse too, so she could help people like Sue. When Lily grew up, she wore a pink dress and became a nurse. She was happy to help others, just like Sue.

Sam and Ben are brothers. They like to play with their toys. But sometimes they fight over them. One day, they both want the same car. They pull and pull, but no one lets go. "Give me the car!" Sam says. "No, it's mine!" Ben says. They pull harder and harder, until the car splits in two. They both fall back and cry. Mom hears them and comes to see what is wrong. "What happened here?" Mom asks. "They split the car!" Sam says. "He split the car!" Ben says. Mom is not happy. She picks up the broken car and looks at them. "You both split the car. You both were bad. Now you have to go to jail." Mom says. She takes them to their room and closes the door. She says they have to stay there until they say sorry and share. Sam and Ben sit on their beds and feel sad. They look at the car and wish they did not break it. They look at each other and feel sorry. They say sorry and hug. They decide to share their toys and play nicely. They knock on the door and call Mom. "Mom, we are sorry. We want to come out of jail." Sam says. "Mom, we will share our toys. We want to play with you." Ben says. Mom hears them and smiles. She opens the door and hugs them. She says she is proud of them. She gives them a soft blanket and a new car. They say thank you and play with the car together. They are happy and Mom is happy.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a tiny acorn. The acorn was not bright, but dull. All the other acorns in the tree were having fun, but the dull acorn felt sad. One day, the wind blew and the dull acorn began to fall. As it fell, it saw a little squirrel on the ground. The squirrel looked at the acorn and smiled. The acorn felt happy for the first time. When the acorn reached the ground, the squirrel picked it up. The squirrel and the acorn became best friends. They played together every day. The dull acorn was not sad anymore. It had found a friend who loved it just the way it was.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to print a picture. She found a lime in the kitchen. She thought it was a great idea to use the lime to make her picture. She was very happy. Lily took the lime and pressed it on a paper. It made a green mark. She did it again and again. Soon, she had a great picture of a tree made with lime prints. Lily showed her mom the great lime print tree. Her mom loved it and put it on the fridge. Lily was proud of her lime tree and smiled every time she saw it.
