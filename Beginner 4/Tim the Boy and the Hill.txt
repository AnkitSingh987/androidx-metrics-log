Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and a dog named Spot. They lived near a big hill with a distant tree on top. Tim liked to play near the tree with his toys. One day, Tim and Spot went up the hill to play. Tim saw a big switch on the ground. He didn't know what it did. Tim asked Spot, "What does this switch do?" Spot barked and wagged his tail, but he didn't know either. Tim decided to pass the switch to his dad. He picked it up and ran down the hill with Spot. When they got home, Tim showed the switch to his dad. His dad smiled and said, "This switch is for our lights at home!" Tim was happy that he found the switch, and they all played together until bedtime.

Once upon a time, there was a fast rabbit. The rabbit liked to run and play. One day, the rabbit met a lawyer. The lawyer was a big bear. The bear wore a suit and carried a big bag. The rabbit said, "Hello, Mr. Bear Lawyer! What do you have in your big bag?" The bear replied, "I have many things to give to my friends." The rabbit was very happy. "Can you give me something too?" asked the rabbit. The bear smiled and said, "Of course!" He looked in his big bag and found a beautiful, shiny toy car. He gave the toy car to the rabbit. The rabbit was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mr. Bear Lawyer!" They played together all day and became good friends.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to whistle. He whistled all day long. One day, Tim saw a wide rocket. It was very big and tall. Tim wanted to go inside the rocket. Tim went into the rocket. He saw many big buttons. Tim whistled a happy song. He pushed a big red button. The rocket started to shake. Tim was scared, but he kept whistling. The rocket went up into the sky. Tim looked out the window. He saw his home get smaller and smaller. Tim was sad. He wanted to go back home. But the rocket went far away. Tim never saw his home again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. It was an icy day, but he wanted to play outside. Tim saw a big tree and heard a whisper. He walked closer to the tree to find out who was talking. Tim saw a small bird on a branch, and the bird said, "Help me, please! My wing is stuck in the tree." Tim wanted to help the bird, but he was too short to reach it. He looked around and found a long staff on the ground. Tim picked up the staff and used it to free the bird's wing. The bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim and the bird played in the park until the sun went down, and they both went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hairy dog named Spot. Spot had so much hair that he could not see. One day, Spot's owner, a kind girl named Lisa, decided to give him a haircut. "Spot, you need a haircut," Lisa said. Spot was happy to get a haircut. Lisa took out her scissors and carefully cut Spot's hair. After the haircut, Spot could see again! He was very happy. Spot wanted to thank Lisa, so he decided to run and fetch her favorite toy. He ran fast and got the toy for her. Lisa was so happy and proud of Spot. They played together all day long. The moral of the story is to always help your friends, and they will help you too.

One day, a girl named Sally wanted to create a book. She had a hidden spot under her bed where she kept her special things. In her hidden spot, she found paper and a pen. Sally started to create her book. She drew a cat, a dog, and a big tree. Her little brother, Tim, came in and said, "What are you doing?" Sally said, "I am making a book. But it is a secret, so don't tell anyone!" Tim wanted to help, but Sally said no. Tim was sad and took the book when Sally was not looking. Sally looked everywhere for her book, but could not find it. She was very upset. Then, Sally found her book in Tim's room. But now, the book had more drawings! Tim had drawn a funny clown and a big sun. Sally saw this and smiled. She said, "Tim, let's create the book together!" And they did, making the best book ever.
