Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Mighty Triangle and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a land of shapes, lived a mighty triangle named Tim. Tim was very strong and always helped his friends. One day, while walking in the park, Tim saw a little circle named Cindy. Cindy was sad because her ball was stuck in a tree. Tim said, "Don't worry, Cindy. I will help you get your ball." With his mighty strength, Tim pushed the tree gently. The ball fell down, and Cindy was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim! You are so kind and strong!" From that day on, Tim and Cindy became best friends. They played together every day and helped each other when they needed it. All the other shapes saw how happy Tim and Cindy were and learned that helping and thanking each other made everyone feel good. The moral of the story is: always help your friends and say thank you when someone helps you.

One day, a little boy named Tim was walking with his mom. They saw a big building. Tim looked at the building and said, "Mom, what is that building?" His mom said, "That is a school, Tim. When you are big, you will go there and learn many things." Tim was excited to go to school. But, his friend Sam said, "School is stupid. I don't want to go." Tim did not know what to think. On the first day of school, Tim was scared. But, he met a nice girl named Sue. Sue said, "School is fun! We can be friends and learn together." Tim was happy. He learned that school was not stupid. He had fun and made many friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She liked to skip down the street. She saw a weird looking cat. The cat looked sad and hungry. Sue wanted to help the cat. Sue went home to get a bowl. She filled the bowl with cat food. She took the bowl back to the weird cat. The cat was happy to see the food. It ate the food very fast. But then, a big dog came. The dog wanted the cat's food. Sue was scared. She did not want the cat to be hurt. Sue picked up the cat and ran away. The dog did not follow them. Sue and the cat were safe. They went home and became best friends.

Once upon a time there was a bossy little girl. Her name was Amy. One day, Amy was feeling hot and grumpy. Her mom was helping her to feel better. She said, “Amy, we need to check your temperature. I’m going to check it with a thermometer.” Amy was not happy about this. She started to yell, “No! I don’t like thermometers!” Her mom was gentle and calm. She said, “Calm down, Amy. I won’t put the thermometer in your mouth, I’m just going to put it in your ear. It won’t hurt.” Amy stopped yelling and relaxed. Her mom put the thermometer in her ear and it made a funny beep sound. The beep sounded like a glad voice. Amy smiled. Her mom said, “See, you don’t have a fever. You just needed some rest. Now you can play in the yard.” Amy was the bossiest girl ever, but she was also brave and smart. She listened to her mom and did as she said. Amy ran outside and felt much better.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a clever little bird. This bird loved to whistle. Every day, the bird would fly around and whistle to all her friends. One day, while flying to the west, the bird met a big, friendly bear. The bear said, "Hello, little bird! I like your whistle. Can you teach me how to whistle too?" The clever bird was happy to help her new friend. So, the little bird and the big bear spent the whole day together. The bird would whistle, and the bear would try to whistle too. After some time, the bear learned to whistle just like the clever little bird. They were both very happy and became best friends. From that day on, they would whistle and play together in the west every day.
