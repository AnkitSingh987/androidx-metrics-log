Category: Beginner
Title: Bella the Beautiful Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bird named Bella. Bella loved to fly high in the sky and sing sweet songs. One day, Bella saw a screen on a window. The screen was open, so she flew inside the house to explore. Inside the house, Bella saw many fun things. She saw a big, yellow ball and a soft, red rug. Bella was having a great time playing with all the toys. Then, Bella heard a voice say, "Please, can you help me?" Bella looked around and saw a small, green frog named Fred. Fred was trapped in a glass jar and could not get out. Bella felt sad for Fred and wanted to help. She tried to open the jar, but her wings were too weak. Just then, a friendly cat named Charlie came into the room. Bella asked Charlie, "Please, can you help us open the jar?" Charlie was a kind cat, and he agreed to help. With a big push, Charlie opened the jar and set Fred free. Bella, Fred, and Charlie became very good friends. They played together every day, and Bella even taught Fred how to sing. The three friends had lots of fun, and they always remembered to help each other when they needed it.

Tom likes to play in the garden. He likes to dig and run and look at bugs. One day, he sees a big spider on a web. The spider is black and hairy and has many legs. Tom is not scared. He thinks the spider is cool. He goes closer to the spider and says hello. The spider does not say anything. It just moves on the web. Tom thinks the spider is shy. He asks the spider if it wants to be his friend. The spider does not answer. It just makes a little sound. Tom waits for the spider to talk. He is patient and kind. He tells the spider his name and what he likes to do. He tells the spider about his toys and his dog and his mom. The spider listens to Tom. It does not move much. After a while, Tom asks the spider again if it wants to be his friend. He says he will be nice and gentle and share his things. The spider looks at Tom. It nods its head. Tom is happy. He smiles and says thank you. He has a new friend. He and the spider play together in the garden. They have fun.

Anna likes to eat beans. She likes them a lot. She likes them with rice, with bread, with cheese, with anything. She always asks her mom for beans when she is hungry. One day, Anna comes home from school. She is very hungry. She runs to the kitchen and looks for beans. But she cannot find any beans. She sees carrots, potatoes, tomatoes, but no beans. She gets frustrated. She asks her mom, "Mom, where are the beans?" Mom says, "Sorry, Anna, we ran out of beans. We have to go to the store and get some more. Do you want to eat something else?" Anna shakes her head. She does not want anything else. She only wants beans. She says, "No, mom, I want beans. I want beans now!" Mom says, "Anna, you have to be patient. We cannot get beans right now. We have to wait until tomorrow. Why don't you try some carrots? They are good for you." Anna does not listen. She gets angry. She stomps her feet. She cries. She says, "No, no, no! I don't want carrots! I want beans! Beans, beans, beans!" Mom sighs. She does not know what to do. She tries to calm Anna down. She says, "Anna, please stop crying. Crying will not get you beans. You have to be nice and polite. Maybe we can get some beans from the neighbor. Do you want to go and ask?" Anna stops crying. She thinks. She likes the neighbor. She likes her dog. She likes her cookies. Maybe she has some beans. She nods. She says, "Okay, mom, let's go and ask." Mom smiles. She takes Anna's hand. They go to the neighbor's house. They knock on the door. The neighbor opens the door. She sees Anna and mom. She says, "Hello, Anna and mom. What can I do for you?" Mom says, "Hello, neighbor. We have a little problem. Anna is very hungry and she only wants to eat beans. But we don't have any beans at home. Do you have some beans that we can borrow?" The neighbor says, "Sure, I have some beans. I was going to make some soup with them. But I can share some with you. How many beans do you need?" Anna says, "A lot of beans. A big bowl of beans. Please, please, please!" The neighbor laughs. She says, "Okay, okay, okay. Come in, come in, come in. I will get you some beans. And maybe some cookies, too. Do you like cookies?" Anna says, "Yes, I like cookies. But I like beans more. Thank you, thank you, thank you!" The neighbor gives Anna and mom some beans and some cookies. Anna is very happy. She hugs the neighbor. She says, "You are the best neighbor ever. I love you. And I love your beans." Mom says, "Thank you, neighbor. You are very kind. We will bring back some beans tomorrow. And maybe some cake, too. Do you like cake?" The neighbor says, "Yes, I like cake. But I like you more. You are very welcome. Come back anytime." Anna and mom go back home. Anna eats her beans and her cookies. She is very full. She is very happy. She says, "Mom, I love you. And I love your beans." Mom says, "I love you, too, Anna. And I love your smile."
