Category: Beginner
Title: Tommy and the Garden

Tommy went to the beach with his mum. He was so excited to go for a swim and play in the sand. When he arrived, he saw a big pile of pebbles. He wanted to take one home. “Mum, can I pack a pebble in my special bag?” Tommy asked. “No Tommy, it wouldn’t be fair to the other children,” replied his mum. “It would be very selfish to take something away from the beach that doesn’t belong to you.” Tommy felt sad and walked away. When he was gone, his mum took out a bag and slowly started to pack pebbles. “Mum!” Tommy yelled, “That’s not fair.” His mum smiled and said, “Don’t be selfish, Tommy. I’m just packing a few pebbles for our garden, not for me.” Tommy felt better, and together they packed a few pebbles in his bag. A few pebbles for his garden, and a few more for his special bag.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big landscape with green grass, trees, and a blue sky. Tim was so happy to be outside. He wanted to get a pretty flower for his mom. Tim saw a beautiful red flower under a tree. He tried to get it, but it was too high for him to reach. He was frustrated. He said, "I want to get this flower, but I can't reach it." A friendly bird heard Tim and wanted to help. The bird said, "Don't be sad, Tim. I can help you get the flower." The bird flew up and picked the flower with its beak. Then, the bird gave the flower to Tim. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" He took the flower home and gave it to his mom. She was so happy and gave Tim a big hug. They both smiled and enjoyed the pretty landscape together.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max was a foolish dog who loved to eat dessert. One day, Max saw a big cake on the table. He wanted to eat it all by himself. Max jumped up and down, trying to reach the cake. A cat named Lily saw him and said, "Max, you can't eat that cake! It's not for you!" Max didn't listen to Lily. He kept trying to get the cake. Finally, Max's owner arrived and saw what Max was doing. She said, "No, Max! That cake is for my party!" Max felt sad and stopped jumping. He knew he had been foolish. His owner gave him a small treat instead, and Max learned not to eat things that were not for him.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim's mom asked him to choose a new knob for their door. Tim was very excited to help. Tim and his mom went to the store. There were many knobs to choose from. Some were big, some were small, some were smooth, and some were rough. Tim looked at all the knobs carefully. He wanted to find the perfect one for their door. Finally, Tim saw a rough knob that he liked. It was not too big and not too small. He told his mom, "I choose this one!" They bought the rough knob and went home. Tim's dad put the new knob on the door. Tim was so proud of his choice, and his mom and dad were happy too.

Billy was excited. Today was his birthday. He was going to celebrate with his friends. He ran around the house, laughing and jumping. Billy's parents were in the kitchen. "Where's my cake?" asked Billy. "It's in the oven," said his mom. Billy clapped his hands. He could hardly wait. In the living room, his dad had set up a funny looking lamp. It was shaped like a rabbit. "What's that silly lamp?" asked Billy. "It's for your birthday," said his dad. Billy smiled. His friends arrived and they all celebrated together. They played with their funny rabbit lamp, cheered and laughed. It was the best birthday ever.
