Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Helpful Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small room, there was a helpful boy named Tim. He had a desk where he liked to draw and color. Tim had a little sister named Amy. Amy liked to play with her toys and sometimes made a big mess. One day, Tim was at his desk drawing a beautiful picture. Amy came in and started to play with her toys. She accidentally knocked over Tim's colors and they fell on the floor. Tim was upset and asked Amy to be careful. Amy felt sad and didn't know what to do. Tim saw her sad face and decided to explain how to be careful. He showed her how to pick up the colors and put them back on the desk. Amy listened and learned from her helpful big brother. Now, Tim and Amy played together in the room without any problems. They both took care of their things and helped each other. They became the best of friends and always had fun together. And Tim's desk was always neat and tidy.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing in his room. He heard a mysterious noise coming from the closet. It scared him a little. He waited a minute and then opened the closet door to see what was inside. Inside, he found a small, talking cat. The cat said, "Hi, I am a magic cat. I need your help to find my toy." Tim was surprised to hear the cat talk, but he wanted to help. They both started to search for the cat's toy. As they looked, the cat and Tim talked. Tim asked, "Why is your toy so important?" The cat said, "It is special to me. It was a gift from my best friend." They searched high and low, but could not find the toy. Just when they were about to give up, Tim's little sister came in with the cat's toy. She had taken it to play with. The cat was happy to have the toy back, and Tim was glad he could help his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to look at the stars at night. She wanted to learn about the universe. Lucy was very proud of her big book about stars. One day, Lucy got a letter in the mail. She was so happy to receive it. The letter said that she could go to a special place to learn more about the universe. Lucy was very excited. Lucy went to the special place with her mom and dad. They saw big things that could look at the stars. Lucy learned more about the universe. She felt very proud and happy. Lucy knew that she would always love the stars and the universe.

Lila wanted to tie her shoes by herself. She learned how to make a loop and a cross. But she did not know how to make a knot. She tried and tried, but her loops always came undone. She felt frustrated and angry. She threw her shoes on the floor and cried. "I can't do it! I can't do it!" she shouted. Her mom came to her room and saw her shoes and her tears. She hugged Lila and said, "It's okay, sweetie. Tying shoes is hard. You are doing a good job. Do you want me to help you?" Lila nodded and sniffed. She wiped her eyes and picked up her shoes. She gave one to her mom and kept one for herself. Her mom showed her how to make a knot. She said, "You have to pull the loops tight and tuck one under the other. Then you have a knot. See?" Lila saw the knot on her mom's shoe. She copied what her mom did on her own shoe. She pulled and tucked and made a knot. She smiled and said, "I did it! I did it!" Her mom clapped and said, "You did it! You are so smart and brave. I'm proud of you." Lila felt happy and proud. She thanked her mom and put on her shoes. She ran to the door and said, "Let's go to the park!" Her mom smiled and said, "Let's go!" They held hands and walked to the park. Lila showed her knot to everyone she met. She felt like she could do anything.
