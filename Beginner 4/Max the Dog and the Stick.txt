Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a happy dog with a fierce love for playing outside. One sunny day, Max went to the park to play with his friends. While playing, Max felt an itch on his back. He tried to scratch it, but he could not reach it. Max asked his friends for help. They tried to help Max, but they could not reach the itch either. Finally, Max had an idea. He found a big stick and managed to scratch the itch with it. Max was so happy that he could play again. He and his friends played all day long, and Max did not feel the itch anymore.

Billy always liked to help his mom around the house. He was only three, but he was dependable for a kid his age. One day, he heard his mom yell for help in the kitchen. "Grab the lung and put it in the cupboard," she yelled. Billy ran to the kitchen. He grabbed the lung and put it in the cupboard. His mom was so happy and gave him a big hug. Billy loved to make his mom happy. He thought to himself, "I can be very dependable and make my mom proud." He felt proud and happy with himself. The next day, his mom was still saying she needed help in the kitchen. So Billy ran in to help her again. He grabbed the lung and put it in the cupboard. His mom thanked him, gave him a hug and praised him for being so dependable. Billy smiled and said, "You can always count on me, mom!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very impatient. He did not like to wait for anything. One day, Tim went outside to play. He saw a drain on the ground. Tim wanted to examine it. Tim looked at the drain. It was big and dark. He wanted to see what was inside. So, Tim put his hand in the drain. He felt something wet and slimy. Tim was scared, but he wanted to know what it was. Tim pulled the wet and slimy thing out of the drain. It was a big, angry frog! The frog jumped on Tim's face. Tim screamed and tried to get the frog off. But the frog would not let go. Tim cried and ran home. The frog stayed on his face. It was a bad day for Tim.

Once upon a time, a little cat named Kitty lived in a small house. Kitty loved to play all day. One sunny day, Kitty saw a perfect tree to climb. The tree was big and had many branches. Kitty was very happy and wanted to climb it. As Kitty started to climb the tree, she leaned on a branch to go higher. Suddenly, a bird named Birdie came out of the tree. Birdie was scared and said, "Please don't climb my tree! My nest is up here, and I don't want it to break." Kitty felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Birdie. I didn't know your nest was here." Birdie and Kitty became friends. They played together under the tree. But one day, a big dog came to the tree. The dog wanted to catch Kitty and Birdie. Kitty and Birdie needed to escape. They had an idea. Birdie flew up high and called the dog. The dog looked up and tried to jump. While the dog was busy, Kitty ran away to her house. Birdie flew away too. They were safe. Kitty and Birdie were happy. They knew they could always help each other. From that day on, they were best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy king. He lived in a big castle. The king had many pretty things. One day, a bad man came to the castle. He took the king's pretty things. The king was very sad. He told his people to find the bad man. The people found the bad man. They took him to the king. The king said, "Lock him in the jail." They locked the bad man in the jail. The king got his pretty things back. The king was happy again.
