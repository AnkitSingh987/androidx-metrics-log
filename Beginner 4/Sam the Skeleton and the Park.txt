Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Skeleton and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a skeleton named Sam. Sam liked to run fast. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Sam and his friends decided to have a race. They wanted to see who was the fastest. As they started the race, Sam ran very fast. He was far ahead of his friends. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. Sam's bones began to separate. His friends stopped running and looked at Sam in surprise. Sam's friends quickly put his bones back together. They were happy that Sam was okay. Then, they all decided to play a different game. They learned that sometimes it's better to play together than to race apart.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play with dolls and cars. One day, Sara sees a new doll in the shop. It has long hair and a pink dress. Sara wants the doll very much. But Sara has no money. She asks her mom for money, but her mom says no. She says the doll is too cheap. It will break soon. Sara is sad. She thinks of a plan. She goes to the shop with Tom. She tells Tom to take a car and play with it. Tom likes cars. He takes a car and plays with it. Sara takes the doll and puts it in her bag. She thinks no one will see her. But the shop man sees her. He is angry. He runs after Sara and Tom. He says they are bad. He says they must give back the car and the doll. He says they must say sorry. Sara and Tom are scared. They give back the car and the doll. They say sorry. They run away. They cry. They learn a lesson. They never take things that are not theirs. They know it is wrong.

Once upon a time, there was a big ocean. In the ocean, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin was a fair fish who liked to help others. One day, Fin saw a big boat on the ocean. The boat had food on it. Fin thought, "I can serve food to my friends in the ocean!" He swam fast to the boat. Fin took the food and swam back to his friends. He served the food to all his friends in the ocean. They were very happy. Fin felt good because he was a fair and helpful fish. They all lived happily in the big ocean.

Once upon a time, there was a big white dog. The dog was very unusual because he had two tails. One day, the dog went for a walk in the park. He was very happy and wagged both his tails. In the park, the dog saw a big tree. He wanted to play close to the tree. Suddenly, a little cat jumped out from behind the tree. The cat was surprised to see the dog with two tails. The cat and the dog became friends. They played together all day. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat's tail got stuck to the dog's tail. Now, the dog had one tail and the cat had two tails. They were both very happy and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a vendor. The vendor sold many things. He had a big, soft chair that was very comfortable. People liked to sit on the chair and feel happy. One day, the vendor found a seed. He planted the seed in a pot. He gave the seed water and sun. He waited for it to grow. Soon, a little plant came out of the ground. The vendor was very happy. The plant grew bigger and bigger. Then, one day, it started to bloom. Pretty flowers came out. They were red, yellow, and blue. The vendor put the pot with the flowers next to the comfortable chair. Now, when people sat in the chair, they could see the pretty flowers too. Everyone in the town was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy skull that he loved to play with. One day, his friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the skull and thought it was cool. "Tom, can I borrow your skull?" Sam asked. Tom was a little scared to let Sam play with his skull, but he didn't want to be a foolish friend. So, he said, "Okay, Sam, you can borrow my skull. But please be careful with it." Sam was happy and played with the skull. They had lots of fun. But then, Sam dropped the skull, and it broke. Tom was sad, but Sam said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I didn't mean to break your skull." Tom forgave Sam because he knew it was an accident. In the end, Tom's mom helped them fix the skull with some glue. Tom and Sam learned that it's okay to borrow things, but they must be careful with them. They also learned that accidents happen, and it's important to forgive friends.
