Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Loyal Dog and the Lake

Once upon a time, in a big forest, lived a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day long with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They would run, jump, and laugh together. Every day, they would watch the beautiful sunset together. One day, while they were playing near a big lake, Tim saw a pretty flower. He wanted to pick it for his mom. But as he reached for it, he slipped and fell into the lake. Tim could not swim, and he was very scared. "Help! Save me, Spot!" Tim cried out. Spot, the loyal dog, heard his friend and quickly jumped into the lake. He swam to Tim and grabbed his shirt with his teeth. Spot pulled Tim back to the shore, saving him from the water. "Thank you, Spot! You saved me!" Tim hugged his loyal friend. They were both very happy and grateful. From that day on, they were even closer friends. And every day, they continued to watch the beautiful sunset together.

Once there was a dependable elephant. He had a trunk which he loved to use for all kinds of things. Every day he used his trunk to get food and pick up sticks. One day he went into the forest to get some treats. He went really far and after a while he started to get tired. Luckily he came to a pond with lots of delicious fruit. He got busy picking the fruit and put it in his trunk. Then he started on his way back home. By the time he got home it was already dark. He was happy to have his dependable trunk which was so strong he could make it all the way home. He ate his treats and then went to sleep.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. There is a big bridge in the park. The bridge goes over a river. Anna and Ben like to walk on the bridge and look at the water. One day, they see a boat on the river. The boat is slow and big. It has a flag and a horn. Anna and Ben wave to the boat. They hope the boat will wave back. "Hello, boat!" Anna shouts. "Can you see us?" The boat does not wave. It does not shout. It only makes a loud noise. Ben covers his ears. "Brrr! That is a scary noise!" Ben says. "Why does the boat do that?" Anna thinks for a moment. She remembers what her mom told her. "The boat says hello with its horn," Anna says. "It is a different way to talk. Maybe the boat is shy and does not like to wave." Ben nods. He understands. He smiles at Anna. "Then let's say hello with our horns, too!" Ben says. He makes a funny face and pretends to have a horn. He blows air through his mouth. "Beep beep!" Ben says. Anna laughs. She does the same. "Beep beep!" Anna says. The boat hears them. It makes another loud noise. It sounds like a happy noise. Anna and Ben think the boat likes them. They wave again. They feel happy, too. They have a new friend.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to play near the train tracks. They saw a big rail that was very useful for the trains to ride on. Tim and Spot liked to watch the trains go by every day. Tim had a ball that he would throw for Spot to catch. They played and played, having lots of fun. One day, while they were playing, Tim threw the ball a little too far. It went over the rail and got stuck in a bush. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and picked up the ball in its beak. Tim and Spot were very surprised. The bird flew away with the ball, and they thought it was gone forever. But then, the bird came back and dropped the ball right in front of Tim and Spot. They were so happy to have their ball back! They thanked the bird and continued to play, being more careful not to throw the ball too close to the rail again.
