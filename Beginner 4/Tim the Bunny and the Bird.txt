Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bunny and the Bird

One day, a little bunny named Tim went for a walk. Tim liked to spring and jump all around. The sun was shining and it was a light and happy day. Tim felt very happy too. On his walk, Tim found a newspaper. The newspaper had many fun pictures on it. Tim liked to look at the pictures and make up stories about them. He saw a picture of a bird, a cat, and a big ball. Tim thought the bird and cat were friends playing with the ball. Tim took the newspaper home to show his mom. His mom liked the newspaper too. They sat together and looked at the pictures. They made up stories about the pictures and laughed a lot. It was a fun day for Tim and his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He wanted to find some food to eat. He walked and walked until he found a big church. The church had a big door. Tim opened the door and went inside. Inside the church, Tim met a kind man named Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown saw that Tim was hungry. He said, "You look hungry, Tim. Let me show you where to find some food." Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Mr. Brown." But there was a problem. A big dog was guarding the food. The dog did not want Tim to eat the food. Tim was scared. Mr. Brown said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." Mr. Brown talked to the dog and made it go away. Tim and Mr. Brown ate the food together. They were not hungry anymore. They became good friends and lived happily ever after.

Tom loves his motorcycle. It is red and shiny and fast. He likes to ride it on the road and in the park. He feels happy and free when he rides his motorcycle. One day, Tom sees a big hill in the park. He wants to go up the hill and see what is on the other side. He thinks he is very clever and brave. He rides his motorcycle faster and faster towards the hill. But the hill is too steep and too high for his motorcycle. When he reaches the top, his motorcycle cannot go any further. It stops and falls down. Tom falls down too. He hurts his knee and his elbow. He cries and cries. A nice lady sees Tom and his motorcycle. She runs to help him. She picks him up and hugs him. She says, "Don't cry, Tom. You are okay. You just had a little accident. Your motorcycle is okay too. It just needs a little fix." She takes Tom and his motorcycle to her car. She puts a band-aid on his knee and his elbow. She says, "You are a very good boy, Tom. You are not afraid of trying new things. But you have to be careful too. Some hills are too big for motorcycles. Next time, you can use a spring. A spring is a thing that makes you jump high. You can put a spring under your motorcycle and then you can fly over the hill. That would be fun and safe." Tom smiles and says, "Thank you, lady. You are very nice and clever. I like your idea. I will try a spring next time. Can I be your friend?" The lady smiles and says, "Of course, Tom. You can be my friend. And I can be your friend. Friends help each other and have fun together. Do you want to go to my house and have some cookies and milk?" Tom nods and says, "Yes, please. I like cookies and milk. And I like you. You are the best friend ever." They drive to the lady's house and have a good time. Tom forgets about his pain and his accident. He is happy and grateful. He learns a new word: spring. He thinks it is a very cool word. He can't wait to try it with his motorcycle. He thinks he will have more adventures and more friends. He thinks life is good.

One day, a brilliant sun was shining in the sky. Tim and his friend, Sam, played outside. They liked to make big towers with their toy blocks. They built and built until the tower was very tall. Suddenly, the ground shook. It was an earthquake! The blocks fell down and the tower was no more. Tim and Sam looked at each other with big eyes. Tim said, "Let's add more blocks to make the tower strong!" They picked up the blocks and started to build again. They added more and more blocks until the tower was tall and strong. The earthquake could not knock it down this time. They smiled and high-fived each other, happy with their new tower.
