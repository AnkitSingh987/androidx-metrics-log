Category: Beginner
Title: Mimi the Purple Cat and the Ball

One day, a purple cat named Mimi saw a glass. The glass was on a table. Mimi wanted to play with the glass. So, Mimi jumped on the table and started to spin the glass. The glass went round and round. It was fun! Mimi's friend, a dog named Bobo, came into the room. Bobo saw Mimi spinning the glass. "Mimi, stop! The glass can fall and break!" said Bobo. But Mimi did not listen. She kept spinning the glass. Bobo was worried. He did not want the glass to break. So, Bobo jumped on the table too. He tried to stop the glass from spinning. Mimi and Bobo both tried to spin the glass their way. They were not happy. Finally, the glass stopped spinning. Mimi and Bobo looked at each other. "I am sorry," said Mimi. "I did not want to make you sad." Bobo smiled. "It's okay, Mimi. Let's play with a ball instead," he said. And so, Mimi and Bobo played with a ball and had lots of fun.

Mum and Dad went out to buy some groceries and Jack, the three year old, was at home. He was so excited because they had decided to buy him a notebook. As soon as they left, Jack started running to his room to grab the notebook. He was so happy that he wanted to show off his new notebook. He grabbed his garden hose and started to spray the newly bought notebook with the water from the hose. He thought that it would be fun to give it a wash. But Jack soon realised that he had made a mistake. The water had made his notebook wet and he started to cry. His tears tasted bitter and he felt so sad. When Mum and Dad came back and saw the situation, they knew how to solve it. Dad took out a tissue and wiped the notebook with it while Mum dried it with a towel. Jack was so relieved and with a smile, he hugged his notebook tight.

Once upon a time, in a big gray palace, there lived a nice king and queen. They had a little girl who liked to play with her toys. One day, the king said, "I will bring you a new toy, my dear." The next day, the king went to find the new toy for his girl. He looked and looked but could not find one. Then, he saw a small gray cat. The king thought, "This cat can be a good friend for my girl." So, he brought the cat to the palace. The little girl was so happy to see the cat. They played and had fun all day. But at night, something unexpected happened. The gray cat turned into a beautiful fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for being my friend. I will make your palace more colorful." With a wave of her wand, the gray palace turned into a bright, happy place. The king, queen, and little girl were amazed and lived happily ever after.

The dog was very excited. He wanted to race. He looked at the tire, wondering if it was the right one. His friends cheered him on. He took a deep breath and went to the start line. The race began and the dog ran as fast as he could. He made it to the tire first! His friends cheered and cheered! He was so happy! But soon, he became tired. His legs were sore and he was panting. He needed to rest. He lay down next to the tire and soon fell fast asleep. He had won the race and was so tired. When he woke up, his friends were still by his side. They laughed and hugged him. He was so proud of himself for winning the race!

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with dolls and cars. One day, Anna brings a new sweater to school. It is pink and soft. She is very happy. "Look at my sweater!" Anna says to Ben. "It is good and pretty. Do you like it?" Ben nods. He likes Anna's sweater. He wants to touch it. He reaches out his hand. But Anna does not want him to touch it. "No, Ben!" Anna says. "Do not touch my sweater! It is mine. You might make it dirty or tear it." Ben feels sad and angry. He does not understand why Anna is so mean. He thinks Anna is not a good friend. He turns away and plays with his car. Anna sees Ben's face. She feels sorry. She knows she was not nice to Ben. She likes Ben. He is her friend. She wants to make him happy. "Ben, I am sorry," Anna says. "I was wrong. You can touch my sweater. It is soft, right? Do you forgive me?" Ben looks at Anna. He sees her smile. He hears her words. He feels better. He forgives Anna. He smiles back. "Yes, Anna, I forgive you," Ben says. "You are a good friend. I like your sweater. It is pretty. Can we play together?" Anna nods. She is happy. She hugs Ben. They play with dolls and cars. They share their toys. They are friends again.
