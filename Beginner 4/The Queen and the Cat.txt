Category: Beginner
Title: The Queen and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a shy queen. She lived in a big castle with her cat. The queen liked to wear pretty dresses and play with her cat. She was very nice to everyone, but she was scared to talk to people. One day, the queen went to the park with her cat. She saw a little girl on a swing. The little girl was sad. The queen wanted to help, so she walked up to the little girl. The queen was shy, so she just stared at the little girl. The little girl looked at the queen and smiled. The queen smiled back. They started to play together on the swing. The queen was not shy anymore. They became friends and played in the park every day. The queen and the little girl were very happy.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park after school. One day, it was very cold and windy. Lily had a warm jacket, but Ben did not. He only had a thin sweater. "Ben, are you cold?" Lily asked. "A little," Ben said. "But I'm okay." Lily felt sorry for Ben. She wanted to share her jacket with him. She whispered in his ear, "Ben, do you want to wear my jacket? We can take turns." Ben smiled. He liked Lily. He whispered back, "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind." They took off Lily's jacket and put it on Ben. He felt warm and cozy. They ran to the swings and had fun. But then, a big boy came. He was mean and rude. He saw Ben's jacket and liked it. He wanted to take it. "Hey, that's a nice jacket," he said. "Give it to me." Ben was scared. He said, "No, this is Lily's jacket. She let me wear it." The big boy did not care. He grabbed Ben's arm and pulled the jacket off him. He threw Ben to the ground and ran away with the jacket. Lily and Ben were very sad. They cried and hugged each other. They went to look for the big boy, but they could not find him. He was gone with the jacket. They went home and told their moms what happened. Their moms were angry and sad. They said, "We are sorry, Lily and Ben. You did a good thing, but some people are not nice. They take what is not theirs. You have to be careful and watch out for them." Lily and Ben learned a lesson. They learned that sharing is good, but sometimes it can be dangerous. They learned that not everyone is kind and fair. They learned that they have to protect themselves and their friends. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved animals and wanted to go on a safari. She wanted to see lions, elephants, and giraffes. One day, her mom and dad took her on a safari trip. On the safari, Mia saw many animals. She saw a big lion and a baby elephant. But then, she saw a foolish monkey. The monkey was playing with a snake. Mia knew that was not safe. She started to cry. Her mom and dad saw her cry and asked her what was wrong. Mia pointed at the monkey and the snake. They told the safari man, and he helped the monkey. The monkey was safe, and Mia was happy.

One day, a boy and his dog went to a restaurant. The boy was hungry and the dog was obedient. They sat at a table and looked at the food. A man came to their table and said, "No dogs allowed!" The boy did not want to leave his dog, so he shrugged and they left the restaurant. They walked and walked, but could not find another place to eat. The boy was very sad and hungry. The dog was sad too. They went home with empty tummies and the day was not good.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear named Bo. Bo lived in a big forest. Winter was coming and it was very cold. Bo needed to find a warm place to sleep. Bo saw a small cave with an open door. He walked to the door and said, "Hello? Can I come in?" A small voice from inside said, "Yes, come in, Bo!" It was a tiny squirrel named Sue. Sue was also looking for a warm place to sleep. Bo and Sue struggled to make the cave cozy. They found soft leaves and warm grass to make a bed. They closed the open door with big sticks. Together, they made the cave a warm and safe place to sleep during the cold winter.
