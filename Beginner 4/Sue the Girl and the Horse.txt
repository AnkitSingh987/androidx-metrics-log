Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Horse

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a box that was tight shut. Sue wanted to open the box, but she could not. She was sad. She knew that the box had a wild thing inside that could help her. One day, Sue met a big, strong man. She asked him to open the box for her. The man tried very hard, but he could not open it too. He told Sue that the wild thing inside the box was very strong, and it would open the box when it was ready. Sue waited and waited. She knew that the wild thing would help her. Then one day, the box started to shake. Sue was excited! The wild thing was ready to come out. The box opened, and out came a wild, strong horse. The horse helped Sue with her problems and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Buddy. He loved to dig holes in the ground. One day, he found a tool, a small spade, near his house. Buddy decided to use the tool to dig a big hole. As Buddy was digging, he saw a little bird. The bird looked sad. Buddy paused his digging and asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I lost my home. It was in a tree that fell down." Buddy wanted to help the bird find a new home. Buddy used the tool to dig a hole for the bird to live in. The bird was so happy and thanked Buddy. They became good friends. From that day on, Buddy learned that it is good to help others when they are in need. And the bird learned that even when things are bad, there is always hope for something good to happen.

One day, a little frog named Tim went to play in the park. He loved to hop and jump all around. As he hopped, he saw a big whip on the ground. Tim was curious and wanted to know what it was. He hopped over to another little frog named Sue. Tim asked, "What is this thing?" Sue looked at the whip and said, "I don't know, but it looks fun!" They both decided to play with the whip. As they played, they found a sour lemon under a tree. Tim said, "Let's taste it!" They both took a bite and made a funny face. "Yuck! It's so sour!" said Sue. They laughed and continued to play with the whip, hopping and having fun all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was eager to go to the store with his mom. He loved to see all the toys and food there. One day, they went to the store together. At the store, Tim saw a nice lady. The lady had a big plate of cookies. She said, "Hi Tim! Would you like a cookie?" Tim looked at his mom, and she nodded. The lady offered Tim a cookie, and he took one with a big smile. Tim and his mom walked around the store some more. They picked up some apples, milk, and bread. Tim held his mom's hand as they went to pay for their things. After they left the store, Tim said, "Thank you, Mom, for taking me to the store. I had fun!" His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad you had fun!"

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious big shoe in the park. It was so big that all the kids could play in it. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to the park to play with her friends. Lily and her friends decided to step inside the big shoe. They laughed and played, having a lot of fun. Suddenly, the shoe started to move! It walked all around the park with the kids inside. The kids were surprised and a bit scared, but they held on tight. After a while, the shoe stopped moving. The kids stepped out and saw that they had helped clean the park by picking up trash with the shoe. They learned that working together can make a big difference. From that day on, Lily and her friends always helped to keep the park clean and had fun doing it.
