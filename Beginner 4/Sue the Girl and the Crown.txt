Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Crown

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a wish. She wanted to dress up like a princess. She told her mom and dad about her wish. They smiled and said, "We will help you." Sue's mom found a pretty dress. It was pink and had sparkles. Sue's dad found a shiny crown. Sue put on the dress and the crown. She looked in the mirror and smiled. She was a princess now! But Sue wanted to be a tough princess. So, she put on big boots and a cape too. She played in the yard, running and jumping. She was a tough princess who could do anything. Sue was happy, and her wish came true.

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a shiny swing set. The swing set was near a noisy track. Every day, kids would come to the park and play on the swings. They would laugh and have fun. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went to the park. They saw the noisy track and the shiny swing set. They ran to the swings and sat down. Tim said, "Let's swing high, Sue!" Sue nodded and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They began to swing back and forth. They swung higher and higher. They could hear the noisy track as the big cars went by. But they didn't mind. They just kept swinging and laughing. They had so much fun that day. They didn't want to leave the park. But when the sun went down, they knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye to the swings and the noisy track and walked home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a wild puppy named Spot. Spot was very happy and loved to run and play. He lived in a big, green field with his family. One day, Spot saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with the ball, but it was stuck in a tree. Spot tried to jump and get the ball, but he was too small. He asked his mom for help. She said, "Let me try, Spot." Spot's mom jumped high and got the ball for him. Spot was so happy! He played with the ball all day long. He ran and jumped and had so much fun. At the end of the day, Spot was very tired. He went back to his family and they all lay down to sleep. Spot dreamed of more fun days with his big, red ball. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a human who had a cool bike. The human would ride the bike every day. One day, the human looked out of the window and saw a 3 year old child. The 3 year old child was excited to see the bike and said, "Can I ride it too? Please!" The human smiled and said, "Sure, you can ride it!" The 3 year old climbed onto the bike and the human held onto the back of the bike. They rode around the park and the 3 year old was having so much fun. The 3 year old said, "This is so cool! Let's do it again tomorrow!" The human smiled and said, "Okay, let's do it again tomorrow!" The 3 year old rode the bike again the next day and it was even more fun than the day before. The 3 year old had an amazing time and was sad when it was time to say goodbye to the cool bike.

One day, a boy named Tim went to see an opera with his mom. The opera had singing and music. Tim liked it a lot. At the opera, a big lady sang very loud. Tim thought she had a fit voice for the opera. In the middle of the opera, a cat jumped on the stage. The cat started to sing too. "Meow, meow!" said the cat. Everyone was surprised. The cat was not supposed to be in the opera. The big lady laughed and said, "This opera now includes a cat!" The cat kept singing with the big lady. Everyone clapped and cheered. The cat and the big lady became friends. They sang together in many operas. People loved the cat and the big lady. Tim was happy that he saw the opera with the singing cat.
