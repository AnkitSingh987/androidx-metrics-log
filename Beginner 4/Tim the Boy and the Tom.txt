Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tom

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked down the regular path they always took. Tim saw his friend, Tom, playing with a toy truck in the park. Tim asked Tom, "Can I play with you?" Tom said, "Yes, let's load the truck with rocks!" They picked up little rocks and loaded them into the toy truck, having lots of fun. After a while, Tim's mom said, "Time to go home, Tim." Tim said bye to Tom and they walked back home. Tim was happy he got to play with his friend on their regular walk.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little gray bunny. The bunny loved to play and hop around. One day, the gray bunny saw a big ball. The ball was red and shiny. The gray bunny wanted to play with the ball. He tried to pass the ball to his friend, the brown squirrel. But the gray bunny did not know how to pass the ball well. The ball went too far and hit a tree. The gray bunny felt ashamed. The brown squirrel saw the gray bunny was sad. He said, "It's okay, gray bunny. We can learn to pass the ball better together." The gray bunny smiled and they both played and passed the ball all day. The gray bunny was not ashamed anymore, and they had lots of fun.

Sara and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with many vines hanging from it. Sara wanted to swing on the vines like a monkey. "Look, Tom, let's swing on the vines!" Sara said. "No, Sara, that is too dangerous. We might fall and hurt ourselves. And Mommy said not to go near the tree. It is not ours," Tom said. Sara did not listen to Tom. She was selfish and wanted to have fun. She ran to the tree and grabbed a vine. She pulled it hard and swung in the air. "Yay, this is so fun! Come on, Tom, don't be scared. I dare you to swing with me!" Sara shouted. Tom was scared, but he did not want to look like a baby. He followed Sara and took another vine. He tried to swing like Sara, but he was not strong enough. The vine broke and Tom fell to the ground. He cried out loud and his knee was bleeding. Sara stopped swinging and ran to Tom. She saw that he was hurt and felt sorry. She hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Tom, I was selfish and stupid. I should have listened to you. Are you okay?" Tom was still crying, but he hugged Sara back. He said, "It's okay, Sara, I forgive you. But let's not swing on the vines again. It is too dangerous and not ours. Let's go find Mommy and get a band-aid." Sara and Tom walked to their Mommy, who was sitting on a bench. She saw that Tom was hurt and Sara was sad. She asked them what happened and they told her the truth. She was angry, but also glad that they were honest. She gave them a band-aid and a hug. She said, "I love you, Sara and Tom, but you have to be careful and respectful. The tree and the vines belong to someone else. You could have hurt yourselves or damaged the tree. Next time, don't be selfish and don't dare each other to do something bad. Okay?" Sara and Tom nodded and said, "Okay, Mommy, we're sorry. We love you too." Mommy smiled and said, "Let's go home and have some cookies. And maybe we can read a book about monkeys and vines. How does that sound?" Sara and Tom smiled and said, "That sounds great, Mommy. Thank you." They held Mommy's hand and walked home. They learned their lesson and never swung on the vines again.

Once upon a time, there were two twins named Tim and Tom. They liked to march around their house. They were always alert and listened to their mom and dad. One day, Tim and Tom were marching in their room when they heard a noise. They stopped and looked around. "What's that?" asked Tim. "I don't know," said Tom. They were very alert and wanted to find out what made the noise. They marched down the hall and saw their cat playing with a toy. "Oh, it's just our cat," said Tim. "Let's keep marching," said Tom. So, the twins continued to march around their house, happy and alert, as they played together.
