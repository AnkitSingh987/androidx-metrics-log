Category: Beginner
Title: Daisy the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy who wanted a friend. One day, Daisy looked around outside and saw a friendly dog. The dog wagged its tail and smiled at Daisy. Daisy was so excited to meet the friendly dog. She said, "Hi there! I'm Daisy. What's your name?" The dog replied, "My name is Bowser. Nice to meet you Daisy!" Daisy was so happy to meet Bowser. She asked, "Will you be my friend?" Bowser looked around and said, "Sure! Let's play together!" So Daisy and Bowser ran around, played tag, and had a lot of fun. Then Daisy wanted to introduce Bowser to her parents. She put on her special clothes, patted Bowser's head, and said, "Let's go meet my folks!" Daisy's mom and dad were so surprised to meet Bowser. They said, "He's so friendly!" Daisy smiled and said, "My new best friend!" From then on, Daisy and Bowser were always together, playing and having lots of fun. The end!

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim and his dependable dog, Spot, went on a vacation. They went to a big farm where they could play and listen to the birds sing. Tim was very happy to be on vacation with his best friend, Spot. One day, while they were playing near the big red barn, Tim heard a tiny voice say, "Please, help me!" Tim stopped and listened. He looked down and saw a small bug stuck in a spider's web. "Oh no!" said Tim, "I will help you!" Tim carefully took the bug out of the web. The bug was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome! I am glad I could help you." Then Tim and Spot went back to playing on their fun vacation.

Anna and Ben went to the mall with Mom. They liked the mall. It had many shops and toys and people. Mom said they could pick one toy each. Anna and Ben smiled. Anna saw a brown bear. It was soft and big and had a red bow. Anna hugged the bear. She wanted the bear. She showed Mom the bear. Mom smiled and said yes. Anna was happy. Ben saw a brown car. It was shiny and fast and had four wheels. Ben pushed the car. He wanted the car. He showed Mom the car. Mom smiled and said yes. Ben was happy. Anna and Ben paid for their toys. They thanked Mom. They walked to the car. They held their toys. They smiled. Mom smiled. They had a good day at the mall.

One day, a girl named Sue went to a party. She wore a pretty dress and a veil. She was very happy. At the party, there was a lot of food and many friends to play with. Sue was having a great time. Sue saw her friend Tim. Tim was kneeling on the ground, trying to clean a big mess. "What happened?" Sue asked. "I dropped my cake," Tim said with a sad face. Sue wanted to help Tim, so they both cleaned the mess together. Just when they finished cleaning, a big dog ran into the party. The dog was so happy and excited. It jumped and played with everyone. Then, the dog saw Sue's veil and thought it was a toy. It grabbed the veil and ran away. Sue and Tim laughed and ran after the dog. They all played together, and the party became even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a brush. He liked to use the brush to make his hair look nice. Tom was very good at brushing his hair. He was successful at it. One day, Tom went to the store with his mom. They had to pay for the things they bought. Tom saw a girl with messy hair. He thought she needed help. Tom said, "I can help you. I have a brush. I am successful at brushing hair." The girl smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom." But then, something unexpected happened. The girl's hair turned into a big, happy dog! The dog licked Tom's face. Tom was surprised, but he liked the dog. They all laughed and played together.
