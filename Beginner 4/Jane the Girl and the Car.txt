Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She had a big toy box in her room. Jane loved her toys very much. One day, she saw a new toy in her box. It was a car with a handle. She was very happy and wanted to play with it. Jane started to play with her new car. She pushed the car by the handle and it went fast. She was so happy. But then, she started to feel anxious. It was time for her mom to come home. Jane wanted to show her mom the new car. Jane put the car with the handle back in the toy box. She waited for her mom to come home. When her mom came home, Jane showed her the car. Her mom smiled and said, "I knew you would find it! I put it there for you." Jane was happy and played with the car all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was an honest girl who always told the truth. One day, she went for a walk with her mom. As they walked, Sue saw something shiny on the ground. She knelt down and picked it up. It was a penny! Sue was very happy and showed the penny to her mom. Her mom said, "Good job, Sue! You found a penny." Sue took the penny home and put it in her piggy bank. She knew that being honest and telling the truth was important. And now, she had a shiny penny to remind her of that.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to an opera with his mom. The opera was a big show with singing and music. Tim liked the music and the pretty costumes. But he did not like to sit still for a long time. He wanted to run and play. During a mild and quiet song, Tim stood up and said, "Mom, can I run outside? I don't want to sit anymore." His mom smiled and said, "Okay, Tim, but be careful and come back soon." Tim went outside and saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help her. They both started to look for the toy. They ran around the opera house until they found it under a bush. Sue was very happy and thanked Tim for his help. Tim learned that helping others can be more fun than just playing by himself. He felt good about being kind to Sue. When he went back inside to watch the opera with his mom, he was happy and sat still for the rest of the show. The moral of the story is that helping others can bring happiness and joy.

One day, a busy bee named Buzz was flying around. He saw a pretty bird playing a flute. The sound was so nice, Buzz wanted to try it too. "Can I play the flute too?" asked Buzz. The bird said, "Sure, but be careful. It's easy to tear." Buzz tried to play the flute, but it was hard. He blew too hard and the flute made a tear. "Oh no!" said Buzz. "I broke your flute. I'm sorry." The bird was sad, but said, "It's okay. We can fix it together." They found some tape and fixed the flute. Then, they played music and had fun all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse was very lucky. He had a big house with lots of food. But one day, the door to his house got locked. The mouse could not get in. The mouse tried to unlock the door. He pushed and pulled, but the door did not open. The mouse was sad. He wanted to go inside his house. Then, the mouse saw a key. The key was shiny and pretty. The mouse picked up the key and put it in the door. The door unlocked! The mouse was so happy. He went inside his house and ate all the food. The lucky mouse lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She loved to wear her pretty skirt. One sunny day, Sally went outside to play with her friends. They played with a big, round ball. As they played, the ball fell into a big puddle. Sally's skirt got all wet and muddy. "Oh no! My skirt is all dirty now," said Sally. Her friend, Tom, said, "Let's go home and soak it in water to make it clean again." Sally and Tom went home and put the skirt in a tub of water. They watched the dirt come off the skirt. After it was clean, Sally's mom gave them tasty cookies as a treat. "Thank you, Mom!" said Sally and Tom. Sally learned that it's okay to get dirty while playing, but it's important to clean up afterward. And she also learned that friends are always there to help each other.
