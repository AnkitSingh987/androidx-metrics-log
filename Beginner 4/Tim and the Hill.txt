Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with clay. One day, he made a clay dog. He named it Max. Tim and Max would go on fun walks together. One day, Tim and Max went to a big park. They saw a dangerous hill. Tim wanted to go up the hill, but Max stopped him. Max knew it was dangerous, so they went to play in the grass instead. Tim and Max had a great time playing in the park. They were happy they did not go up the dangerous hill. When it was time to go home, Tim thanked Max for keeping him safe. They went home and played with more clay.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She was very lucky because she found a pretty rock. She wanted to show her mom the rock. But first, she needed to wash it. So, Lucy went to the pond to wash her rock. As Lucy washed the rock, she heard a voice. It said, "Thank you for washing me!" Lucy looked around and saw a little frog. The frog said, "I was stuck to the rock and couldn't move. You saved me!" Lucy was happy to help the frog. She said, "You're welcome, Mr. Frog!" The frog was very thankful and jumped away. Lucy felt lucky to have made a new friend at the park. She couldn't wait to tell her mom about her adventure.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia had a pretty basket. She loved to keep her toys in it. One day, it was very hot and the sun was harsh. Mia went for a walk with her basket. As she walked, she saw a big tree. She thought it would be a good place to rest. She put her basket down and sat under the tree. Suddenly, a bird flew down and took her basket away. Mia was sad and cried. But then, she saw the bird come back. The bird did not have her basket, but it had a big, shiny toy. The bird gave the toy to Mia. She was so happy! The bird had taken her basket, but it gave her a new, special toy. Mia smiled and played with her new toy under the tree.

Once upon a time there was an adventurous little girl called Abbie. She wanted to explore the world, so she decided to buy some gear and go on an adventure. Abbie went to the store and bought some gear: she got a hat, a backpack and a map. Abbie was very excited but a bit anxious too. She wanted to set off straight away. So Abbie started to put on all her gear. She was wearing her hat, her back pack and she had the map tucked into her pocket. Suddenly, Abbie heard her mom calling her to come inside. Abbie stopped and said "Oh no, I have to part with my gear". Abbie's mom smiled and said, "It's ok Abbie, you can go back out to explore tomorrow". Abbie smiled, said goodbye to her mum and ran off, excited for her next adventure.

One day, a thin cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big fence. Tom wanted to test if he could jump over the fence. He tried and tried, but he could not jump over it. Tom saw a dog named Max. Max was big and strong. Tom asked Max, "Can you help me jump over the fence?" Max said, "Yes, I can help you. But first, you must give me your food." Tom did not want to give Max his food. But he wanted to jump over the fence. So he gave Max his food. Max helped Tom jump over the fence. But on the other side of the fence, there was no food. Tom was hungry and sad. The moral of the story is: do not give away what you need for something you want.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a man selling strawberries. They want to buy some, but they have no money. "Can we have some strawberries, please?" Tom asks the man. "Sorry, kids, you have to pay for them. They are two coins for one cup," the man says. Tom and Anna are sad. They have no coins. They look around and see a lady sitting on a bench. She has a big bag of coins. She is reading a book. "Maybe she can help us," Anna says. They go to the lady and say hello. The lady smiles and puts down her book. "Hello, kids. What can I do for you?" she asks. "We want to buy some strawberries, but we have no money. Can you give us some coins, please?" Tom says. The lady looks at the man and the strawberries. She is compassionate. She likes to help people. "Sure, I can give you some coins. How many do you need?" she says. "We need four coins. Two for me and two for Anna," Tom says. The lady gives them four coins. They say thank you and run to the man. They buy two cups of strawberries and share them. They are happy and delicious. The lady watches them and smiles. She is glad to see them enjoy the strawberries. She picks up her book and continues reading. She is also happy.
