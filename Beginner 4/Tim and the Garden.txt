Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the dirt. One day, he found a small, red tomato in the garden. The tomato was filthy because it was covered in dirt. Tim wanted to eat the tomato, but he knew he had to clean it first. Tim asked his mom if he could eat the tomato. His mom said, "Yes, but you have to wash it first." Tim went to the sink and washed the filthy tomato. He used soap and water to make it clean. After washing the tomato, Tim was allowed to eat it. Tim learned that it is important to clean things before eating them. He knew that if he ate the filthy tomato, he might get sick. The moral of the story is to always wash your food before eating it, so you stay healthy and strong.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy top that he loved to play with. It was his favorite toy. Tim would spin the top every day and watch it go round and round. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a big top that he wanted. Tim's mom said he needed to pay for it with his own money. Tim was sad because he didn't have any money. He decided to save his money until he could buy the big top. Tim did chores to earn money. He helped his mom clean and put away toys. After some days, Tim had enough money to buy the big top. But when he went to the store, he saw a candy that he wanted too. Tim bought the candy instead of the top. When he tasted the candy, it was bitter! Tim was sad because he didn't like the bitter candy and he still didn't have the big top. He learned that he should think more before he spends his money.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She loved playing with her toys. One day, her dad said, "Let's go for a ride in the vehicle!" So they went to the garage and the little girl was very excited. She saw the vehicle and asked, "Can I mark it?" Dad said, "Yes, but be careful and only use a little bit of paint". The little girl then got some paint and carefully marked the vehicle. She made a big smiley face and added some flowers. When she was done, Dad said, "That looks great!" The little girl was so proud and happy. She and Dad went for a ride in the vehicle and they both had a great time. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a bright little train. This train loved to go fast. It went up and down hills, and all around the town. The train needed coal to go fast. Coal was black and dirty, but it made the train strong. One day, the bright little train wanted to finish a big race. The race was very long, and the train knew it would need a lot of coal. So, the train went to the coal place and got lots of coal. The day of the race came, and the bright little train was ready. It went very fast, up and down hills, and all around the town. The train used all the coal it got. At last, the train finished the big race. Everyone was so happy, and the bright little train was very proud.

One day, Spot the dog went to the park to play. He saw his friend, Ben the cat. Ben said, "Let's play a game. Who can run the fastest?" Spot was excited and said, "Yes, let's play!" They started to run. Spot was light on his feet and ran very fast. Ben tried to catch up, but Spot was too quick. Soon, Spot won the race. He was so happy and said, "I won! I am the fastest!" Just then, a small bird flew down from a tree. The bird said, "I saw you win, Spot. But can you beat me in a race?" Spot and Ben looked at each other, surprised. They never thought a bird would want to race them. Spot said, "I will try to win again!" They all raced, and the bird flew so fast that Spot and Ben could not catch up. The bird won the race. Spot and Ben were not sad. They clapped for the bird and said, "You are very fast too! Let's all play together!" From that day on, Spot, Ben, and the bird became best friends and played at the park every day.
