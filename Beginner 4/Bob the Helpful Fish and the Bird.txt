Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Helpful Fish and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a helpful fish named Bob. Bob loved to surf on the big waves in the sea. He had many friends who liked to surf too. They had so much fun together. One day, while Bob and his friends were surfing, a big bird came and took Bob away. Bob was scared and sad. He wanted to return to his friends and surf again. The big bird took Bob to a far away place. But then, something unexpected happened. The big bird was helpful and kind. It knew that Bob wanted to return to his friends. So, the big bird took Bob back to the sea. Bob was so happy to be back with his friends. They all surfed together and had a great time. And they even made a new friend, the helpful big bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog, but he had a heavy toy. Spot hoped he could find a friend to play with his toy. He went to the park to look for a friend. At the park, Spot saw a big cat named Fluffy. Spot asked Fluffy, "Do you want to play with my heavy toy?" Fluffy said, "Yes, I want to play, but I don't like to fight. Can we play without a fight?" Spot agreed, and they started to play together. As they played, Spot and Fluffy became good friends. They played every day and never had a fight. Spot was happy that his hope came true, and he found a friend to play with his heavy toy. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim lived in a small town. Tim loved Wednesdays because it was the day his mom took him to the big park. The park was always busy with kids playing and having fun. Tim was excited for the day to come. On Wednesday, Tim and his mom went to the park. When they got there, Tim saw a big tent. He asked his mom, "Can I enter the tent?" His mom said, "Sure, but be careful." Tim knew something fun was waiting inside the tent. As Tim entered the tent, he saw a big surprise. It was a puppet show! All the kids from the park were there too. They laughed and clapped as the puppets danced and told funny stories. Tim had the best Wednesday ever, just like he knew he would.

Tom and Jen were playing in the park. They liked to climb on the equipment and slide down the slides. They saw a big truck with a crane and a shovel. It was digging a hole in the ground. "Wow, look at that!" Tom said. "It is so big and loud!" "Can we go and see it?" Jen asked. "OK, but be careful. It is not for kids," Tom said. They ran across the grass and got closer to the truck. They saw a man in a yellow hat and a blue shirt. He was holding a walkie-talkie and talking to someone. "Hello, sir," Tom said. "What are you doing?" The man looked at them and frowned. He was not happy to see them. "Go away, kids. This is not a place for you. This is a rare and important job. We are digging for dinosaur bones," he said. "Dinosaur bones?" Jen said. "Like in the books?" "Yes, like in the books. But you can't touch them. They are very old and fragile. Now go back to the equipment and play there," the man said. Tom and Jen were curious. They wanted to see the dinosaur bones. They waited until the man was busy with his walkie-talkie and sneaked behind the truck. They saw a big pile of dirt and rocks. They saw something shiny and white sticking out of the dirt. "Look, Tom, I think that is a bone!" Jen said. She reached out her hand and tried to grab it. But the bone was stuck and did not move. She pulled harder and harder. "Jen, stop! You might break it!" Tom said. But Jen did not listen. She pulled with all her strength. Suddenly, the bone came loose and flew out of the dirt. It hit the man in the face and made a loud noise. "Ow!" the man yelled. "Who did that?" He turned around and saw Tom and Jen. He was very angry. He ran towards them and raised his hand. He was going to slap them. Tom and Jen were scared. They knew they did something bad. They ran away as fast as they could. They ran back to the equipment and hid behind the slide. "Tom, I'm sorry. I did not mean to hurt the man," Jen said. "It's OK, Jen. I'm sorry too. We should not have gone near the truck," Tom said. They hugged each other and hoped the man would not find them. They learned their lesson. They decided to stay away from the rare and important job and play with the equipment instead.
