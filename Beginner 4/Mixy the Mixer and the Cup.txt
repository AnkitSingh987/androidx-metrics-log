Category: Beginner
Title: Mixy the Mixer and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little mixer named Mixy. Mixy loved to mix things up. One day, Mixy was hanging out with his friends, Cup and Spoon. They were having a lot of fun playing in the kitchen. But Mixy started to feel worried. He thought he was not as good at mixing as he used to be. He didn't want his friends to know, so he tried to mix even harder. Mixy mixed and mixed, but he still felt worried. Cup and Spoon saw that Mixy was worried, so they talked to him. They told Mixy that they loved him just the way he was, and that it was okay if he couldn't mix as fast as before. Mixy felt happy again, and they all played together in the kitchen, having fun and laughing.

Mommy and Daddy took Jane to the park. Jane saw a man with a bald head. She pointed and asked Daddy what it was. Daddy told Jane it was a bald head. Jane thought it was funny and she started to laugh. The man stared at Jane. Jane didn't know what to do, so she hid behind Daddy. Daddy put his hand on her shoulder and said it was ok. Jane slowly looked up at the man. The man smiled at Jane, and then he said hello. Jane smiled and waved back. She felt brave and said hello back. Daddy laughed and told her she had said hello to the bald man. Jane giggled and asked if she could pet the bald man's head. Daddy laughed and said no. But he said Jane could pet his bald head instead. Jane smiled and pet Daddy's bald head. She liked the funny feeling of the smooth, bald head. It was much better than the hairy heads she had seen before. Jane smiled atDaddy and said she thought bald heads were funny. Daddy laughed and said it was ok to make jokes about bald heads. But he also said it was important to be respectful of other people. Jane nodded and said she would remember.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the doctor with her mom. The doctor said, "Sue, we need to take an x-ray of your arm." Sue looked at the big gray machine and felt a little scared. Her mom held her hand and said, "Don't worry, Sue. The x-ray will help the doctor see if your arm is okay. It will be very fast." Sue felt better with her mom close to her. The doctor took the x-ray and showed it to Sue and her mom. "Look, your arm is all good!" he said with a smile. Sue was happy and thanked the doctor. She couldn't wait to go home and tell her friends about the big gray x-ray machine.

One day, a nice cat named Tom saw a big tower. Tom wanted to climb the tower to see the view. He thought it would be fun to be up high. So, he started to climb the tower. Tom saw a bird on the tower. The bird said, "Hurry, Tom! If you reach the top, you can see the whole town!" Tom hurried up the tower, climbing faster and faster. He was excited to see everything from up high. Finally, Tom reached the top of the tower. He looked around and saw the whole town. It was so nice and pretty! He was happy that he hurried up the tower. Tom and the bird sat at the top, looking at the town and enjoying their new view.

One day, a little boy went for a walk in the wild. He saw a big tree and decided to look up. He saw a little bird in the tree. The bird was sad because it could not fly. The little boy wanted to help the bird. The little boy had an idea. He ran home and got a soft blanket. He went back to the wild and climbed the tree. He put the blanket under the bird and helped it down. The bird was grateful and let the boy pet it. As the boy pet the bird, something unexpected happened. The bird started to grow bigger and bigger. Soon, the bird was big enough to carry the boy. The bird took the boy for a ride in the sky. The boy had so much fun and was happy he had helped the bird.
