Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Smelly Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a smelly cat named Tom. Tom loved to sing. He would sing all day long. One day, he heard about a big show called "opera". Tom wanted to sing in the opera, so he went to the big city. In the city, Tom met a kind dog named Max. Max liked to help others. Max saw that Tom was lost and wanted to help. Max said, "I will take you to the opera!" Tom was so happy. They walked together to the big building where the opera was held. Before the opera started, Tom smelled something bad. He followed the smell and found a little bird trapped in a cage. Tom knew he had to rescue the bird. He opened the cage and set the bird free. The bird was so happy and thanked Tom. The bird flew away and told everyone about Tom's good deed. Tom and Max went back to watch the opera, and everyone cheered for Tom, the smelly hero.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Max. Max had a purple ball that he loved very much. One day, Max saw a big tree with a long string. He thought it would be fun to tie his purple ball to the string. Max asked his friend, a little boy named Tim, to help him. Tim said, "Okay, Max! Let's tie your purple ball to the string." They tied the ball to the string and Max started to play with it. He was very happy. But then, the wind blew very hard. The purple ball went up, up, up into the sky. Max and Tim tried to catch it, but they couldn't. The purple ball was gone forever. Max was very sad. The moral of the story is to be careful with your favorite things, or you might lose them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big bed in her room. She loved to jump on her bed and play with her toys. The bed was very soft and mild. Sue felt happy when she was on her bed. One day, Sue's mom told her to go to bed early. Sue was playing with her toys and she forgot what her mom said. She played and played until it was very late. The moon was up in the sky and her toys were all around her bed. Sue's mom came into her room and saw her still playing. She reminded Sue that she forgot to go to bed early. Sue felt sorry and put her toys away. She climbed into her soft, mild bed and closed her eyes. Sue's mom kissed her goodnight and she fell asleep, dreaming of more fun days to come.

Once upon a time, in a far away place, there was a little girl. Her name was Jane. One day she was walking along a beach. The sand was soft and warm, and the shoreline was ancient. Jane was looking for something interesting. Suddenly she saw a twist in the sand. She picked it up and ran back to the shore. Once there she held it up and shouted, "Look what I found!" But her words were met with silence. Jane looked around but no one was there. Just then, a mysterious voice spoke from the ancient shore. "That's an old twist," the voice said. Jane was startled. She looked around, but still saw nothing. "Who are you?" She asked. "I am the sand," replied the voice. Jane smiled. She thanked the voice and carefully put the twist back where she had found it. Then, she ran off down the shore.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird. This bird loved to eat. Every day, the bird would fly and look for food. The bird liked the sight of big, juicy bugs to eat. One day, the blue bird saw a big bug on a leaf. The bird was very happy. The bird flew down to the leaf to eat the bug. The bug tried to hide, but the bird was too fast. The blue bird ate the bug and it was very yummy. The bird was full and happy. The bird flew back to its nest and took a nap. The bird had a good day and a good meal.
