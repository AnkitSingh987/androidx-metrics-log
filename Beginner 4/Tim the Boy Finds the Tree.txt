Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the park. He saw a very old tree with many twigs. Tim wanted to test how strong the twigs were. He pulled on one twig and it broke off. "Oh no!" Tim said, "I broke the twig." Just then, a small bird flew down and landed on the ground next to Tim. The bird looked at the broken twig and said, "Hi, I'm Birdy. That twig was my home. Can you help me fix it?" Tim felt sad for breaking the bird's home. He said, "I'm sorry, Birdy. I will help you." Tim and Birdy looked for a new twig to fix Birdy's home. They found a big, strong twig and put it back on the old tree. Suddenly, the tree started to talk! "Thank you for helping my friend Birdy," said the tree. Tim and Birdy were very surprised. They didn't know trees could talk! The tree was happy and gave Tim and Birdy a big hug. From that day on, Tim, Birdy, and the old tree became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Anna. Anna was a bossy girl. She liked to tell her friends what to do. One day, Anna found a bell in her toy box. She loved the sound it made when she shook it. Anna took the bell to the park to play with her friends. She said, "I am the boss. When I ring the bell, you must do what I say." Her friends did not like this, but they wanted to play with the bell too. One friend, Tim, said, "Let's take turns being the boss." Anna did not want to share, but she agreed. They all played together, ringing the bell and taking turns being bossy. In the end, they learned that sharing and taking turns made playtime more fun for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a small desk in his room. The desk was dirty, but Tim liked it very much. He would draw and play with his toys on the desk every day. One day, Tim lost his favorite toy car. He was very sad. He looked and looked for the car, but he could not find it. Tim thought that maybe the car was under his dirty desk. He looked under the desk, but the car was not there. Tim was so sad that he could not find his toy car. He looked everywhere in his room, but the car was gone. Tim cried and cried, but the car did not come back. In the end, Tim never found his car, and he was sad for a very long time.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little dog. They were best friends. They lived in a small house with a nice yard. The cat and the dog liked to play all day. One day, something happened. The cat found a box on the left side of the yard. The box was full of food. But the food was spoiled. The cat and the dog did not like the smell. The cat and the dog decided to throw the spoiled food away. They put the food in the trash. Then, they went back to play in the yard. They were happy because the bad smell was gone. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Bob. Bob loved dinosaurs. He wanted to have his own dinosaur, so he made a plan. Bob asked Mommy if he could have a dinosaur. "That's a big plan," Mommy said. But Bob was very persistent and kept asking. Finally, Mommy said, "Ok, if you can make it happen, you can have a dinosaur." Bob was so excited! He called all his friends and told them the plan. Everyone was excited. Bob went to the store and looked for a dinosaur. He looked and looked and looked. Then, after a long time, Bob found the perfect dinosaur! Bob was so proud of himself for finding the dinosaur. He couldn't wait to show it to Mommy and his friends. Everyone was happy that Bob's plan worked out.
