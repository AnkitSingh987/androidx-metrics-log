Category: Beginner
Title: The Green Frog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The frog loved to eat pizza. One day, he found a big pizza in the park. The frog was very happy. He wanted to stay and eat the pizza. But his mom said, "No, don't stay. We have to go home." The frog did not listen to his mom. He stayed and ate the pizza. But the pizza was bad. The green frog got sick. He was sad and wished he had listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, in an ancient forest, there lived a little rabbit with soft fur. This rabbit had a magic trick. He could vanish! He loved to play hide and seek with his friends. One day, the rabbit was playing with his friend, the squirrel. "Let's play hide and seek!" said the rabbit. "Okay!" said the squirrel. The rabbit closed his eyes and started to count. The squirrel ran to hide. When the rabbit finished counting, he opened his eyes and said, "Ready or not, here I come!" The rabbit looked and looked, but he could not find the squirrel. Then, he had an idea. He would use his magic trick to vanish and surprise the squirrel. The rabbit closed his eyes and vanished. But, something unexpected happened. The rabbit could not un-vanish! He was invisible forever. The squirrel finally came out of hiding and called, "You win, rabbit! I can't find you!" The rabbit tried to answer, but his voice was also gone. The squirrel never knew that the rabbit was right next to him, feeling sad. From that day on, the rabbit learned that magic tricks can sometimes have a twist.

Sam liked to play with his toy cars. He made them zoom on the floor and on the sofa. He had many cars of different colors and sizes. He liked to pretend they were racing or going on adventures. One day, he saw a new boy in the park. The boy had a big red car that looked very fast. Sam wanted to play with him, but he was shy. He did not know how to say hello. He watched the boy from far away, and wished he had a car like that. The boy saw Sam and smiled. He waved his hand and said, "Hi, I'm Ben. Do you like cars? Do you want to join me?" He held out his red car to Sam. Sam felt nervous, but he also felt curious. He liked cars, and he wanted to join Ben. He took a deep breath and walked over to him. He said, "Hi, I'm Sam. I like cars too. Can I play with you?" Ben nodded and said, "Sure, you can play with me. You can have my red car. It's very fast and loud. It can zoom like a rocket. Do you have a car too?" Sam nodded and showed him his blue car. It was smaller than Ben's, but it was shiny and smooth. He said, "This is my blue car. It's very quiet and smooth. It can zoom like a bird." Ben and Sam smiled at each other. They decided to play together. They made their cars zoom on the grass and on the slide. They had fun and became friends. They learned that they had different cars, but they liked the same things. They learned that it was good to join and share with others. They learned that it was brave to say hello and make new friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a flexible tree. The tree could bend and move with the wind. One day, a big storm came to the village. The wind was very strong and the rain was heavy. The tree was scared of the storm. The wind would slap its branches and make the tree shake. The other trees in the village were strong, but they could not move like the flexible tree. They were scared too. The storm was very bad, and some of the strong trees broke. But the flexible tree did not break. It could bend and move with the wind. When the storm was over, the flexible tree was still standing. The other trees were happy that the flexible tree was okay. They learned that being flexible can be helpful in a storm.
