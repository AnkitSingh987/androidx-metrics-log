Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Castle

Lily and Ben were playing in the sand. They had a big bucket and a small shovel. They made a hill and a hole. They were very jolly. "Look, Lily, I can hide the shovel in the hole!" Ben said. He put the shovel in the hole and covered it with sand. "Where is the shovel, Ben?" Lily asked. She looked around the sand. "It is a secret, Lily. You have to find it!" Ben said. He smiled. Lily tried to find the shovel. She dug with her hands. She moved the sand. She could not find the shovel. "Ben, this is not fun. Tell me where the shovel is!" Lily said. She was cross. "No, Lily, you have to find it. It is a game!" Ben said. He laughed. Lily was very cross. She grabbed the bucket and poured sand on Ben's head. "Hey, Lily, that is not nice!" Ben said. He was angry. They both started to cry. They were not jolly anymore. Mom came and saw them crying. She asked them what was wrong. "Ben hid the shovel and I could not find it!" Lily said. "Lily poured sand on my head!" Ben said. Mom hugged them and said, "I am sorry, my loves. Hiding and pouring sand are not good games. They can hurt your feelings and your eyes. Why don't you play something else? You can share the bucket and the shovel and make a castle or a cake. That would be more fun and jolly." Lily and Ben said sorry to each other. They wiped their tears and their sand. They agreed to play nicely. They shared the bucket and the shovel and made a castle and a cake. They were jolly again.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat named Kitty. She lived in a small room with her best friend, a little girl named Sue. They loved to play together all day long. One day, while playing in the room, Kitty saw a tiny mouse. Kitty wanted to catch the mouse, so she kneeled down and got ready to pounce. "Kitty, be nice to the mouse!" said Sue. Kitty looked at Sue and decided not to chase the mouse. Instead, Kitty and Sue kneeled down and watched the mouse play. They laughed and clapped as the mouse ran around the room. The mouse saw them and knew they were kind. So, it came closer and played with them. From that day on, Kitty, Sue, and the mouse became good friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a woman who was getting ready to go on a trip. She carefully packed her suitcase with all the things she would need while she was away. "I'm making sure everything is safe and secure," she said to herself. To make sure she was really ready, the woman asked her three year old daughter, who was watching nearby. "I'm all packed up. Can you check and make sure I'm not forgetting anything?" The little girl looked into the suitcase and then checked her mother's pocket. "You have everything you need, Mommy," she said. The woman smiled, gave her daughter a big hug, and said, "Let's go have an adventure!"

One day, a calm cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big rock and a small pizza on the ground. Tim was very hungry, so he ate the pizza. It was yummy! Then, a dog named Sam came by. He saw the rock and said, "Hey, Tim! Let's play with this rock." Tim and Sam played with the rock, rolling it back and forth. They had so much fun! But, the rock started to shake! It was not a rock, but a big egg! The egg broke and a baby bird came out. Tim and Sam were surprised, but they became friends with the bird. Together, they all played and had a great day.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big, soft bed and thought it would be a nice place for a nap. He jumped on the bed and started to wrap himself in a warm blanket. Tom was happy and ready for his nap. Then, a dog named Max came into the room. Max saw Tom on the bed and said, "Hey, that's my bed! You can't nap there!" Tom looked at Max and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know. I just wanted a warm nap." Max thought about it and said, "Okay, you can take a nap, but don't be rude and take up all the space." As Tom and Max were napping, they heard a loud noise. They woke up and saw a big, friendly bear in the room. The bear said, "Hi, I'm Ben! I heard you two talking and I wanted to join your nap." Tom and Max were surprised but happy to make a new friend. They all moved over to make room for Ben, and the three of them took a nice, warm nap together.
