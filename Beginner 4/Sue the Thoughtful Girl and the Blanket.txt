Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Thoughtful Girl and the Blanket

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Sue. Sue loved to study her books and learn new things. She had a special blanket that she always used when she studied. It was her lucky blanket. One sunny day, Sue was sitting under a big tree with her blanket, studying her books. Suddenly, a little bird flew down and took her blanket. Sue was very sad and did not know what to do. She looked up at the tree and saw the bird with her blanket. Sue decided to climb the tree to get her blanket back. When she reached the top, she saw the bird had made a nest with her blanket. Inside the nest were three baby birds who were cold. Sue felt sorry for the baby birds and thought that they needed the blanket more than she did. So, she let the bird keep her blanket for the baby birds to stay warm. Sue climbed down the tree and went home. She told her mom about the baby birds and the blanket. Her mom was proud of Sue for being so thoughtful. They went to the store and bought a new blanket for Sue to use when she studied. And from that day on, Sue and the little bird became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with her toys in her dad's office. One day, Lily was playing with her toy car when she felt uncomfortable. She wanted to scream but she didn't want to bother her dad. Lily went to her mom and said, "Mom, I feel bad. My tummy hurts." Her mom gave her a hug and said, "Let's go tell your dad." So, Lily and her mom went to the office. When they got to the office, Lily said, "Dad, I feel bad." Her dad looked at her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Let's go sit down and rest." So, Lily, her mom, and her dad all sat down and took a break. Soon, Lily felt better and went back to playing with her toys.

Once upon a time, there was a wide sky. In the sky, there were many stars. One little star wanted to complete a big jump from one side of the sky to the other side. The little star was very brave. The little star tried to jump, but it was too far. The other stars saw the little star and wanted to help. They all held hands and made a long line. The little star was very happy. The little star jumped again. This time, with the help of the other stars, the little star could complete the big jump. All the stars in the wide sky were very happy and they all danced together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his uncle. One day, they went to the park to play. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim was very happy. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it very fast. His uncle said, "Be careful, Tim. It's not safe." But Tim did not listen. He started to climb the tree. The tree was very tall and the branches were very high. Tim was almost at the top when he felt the branch under his feet start to shake. He got scared and tried to hold on tight. But it was too late. Tim began to fall. His uncle tried to catch him, but he was not fast enough. Tim fell to the ground and got hurt. His uncle felt very sad. They both wished they had listened to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big war between two groups of ants. One group of ants was red, and the other group was black. They were always fighting over food and space. They did not like each other at all. One day, a little red ant named Tim was out looking for food. He found a big, dead bug. Tim was so happy! He started to carry the bug back to his home. On his way, he met a little black ant named Sam. Sam was very hungry and saw Tim with the bug. "Can I have some of your bug?" Sam asked Tim. Tim thought for a moment and realized that they did not have to fight. They could share the bug and be friends. So, Tim said, "Yes, we can share the bug, and we can be friends!" From that day on, the red ants and the black ants stopped fighting. They worked together and shared their food. They realized that being friends was much better than being at war. And they all lived happily ever after.
