Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. They brought a huge basket full of toys to play with. In the basket, Mia found her favorite toy, a big ball. She also found some sand toys, like a bucket and a shovel. Mia was very happy. Mia saw a boy named Tim playing in the sand. He had a fun toy that could mix sand and water. Mia asked Tim, "Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play together!" They took turns using the toy to mix the sand and water. It was so much fun. After they played in the sand, Mia and Tim decided to play with the big ball from the huge basket. They kicked the ball back and forth, laughing and having a great time. When it was time to go home, Mia and Tim said goodbye and promised to play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had big ears that could hear very well. One cold day, Max went outside to play with his friend, a little bird named Lily. Max and Lily were playing when Max started to shiver. "Brrr, I am cold," Max said. "My ear is cold too!" Lily wanted to help Max, so she thought of an idea. "Wait here," Lily told Max. She flew away and came back with a small, important hat. She put the hat on Max's head to cover his ears. Max stopped shivering and smiled. "Thank you, Lily! Now my ears are warm, and we can keep playing." And they played happily all day long.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to visit a shop with her mom. They walked to the shop holding hands. Lucy was very happy because she loved to visit shops with her mom. When they got to the shop, Lucy saw a toy she wanted. But her mom said, "No, not today." This made Lucy upset. She wanted the toy so much, but her mom said they could not buy it. Lucy was still upset when they left the shop. Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't be upset. We will come back and visit the shop another day." Lucy felt a little better and went home with her mom, looking forward to the next time they could visit the shop together.

Tom and Amy were friends who liked to play with cars. They had many cars of different colors and sizes. One day, Tom got a new car from his mom. It was a shiny silver car that looked very fast and cool. "Wow, look at my new car!" Tom said, showing it to Amy. "It is the best car ever!" Amy looked at the car and felt sad. She did not have a silver car. She only had a red car, a blue car, and a yellow car. She wanted a silver car too. "Can I play with your silver car?" Amy asked Tom. "No, you can't!" Tom said. "It is my car and I don't want to share it. You can play with your own cars." Amy felt hurt and angry. She did not like Tom's answer. She thought he was being mean and selfish. She did not want to play with him anymore. She turned away and started to play with her red car. She pretended it was a fire truck and made siren noises. Tom saw Amy playing with her red car and felt curious. He liked fire trucks too. He wanted to join her and play together. "Can I play with your red car?" Tom asked Amy. "No, you can't!" Amy said. "It is my car and I don't want to share it. You can play with your own car." Tom felt hurt and angry. He did not like Amy's answer. He thought she was being mean and selfish. He did not want to play with her anymore. He turned away and started to play with his silver car. He pretended it was a rocket and made zooming noises. But soon, he felt bored and lonely. He realized that playing with his silver car was not as fun as playing with Amy. He missed her and wanted to be friends again. He looked at Amy and saw that she was bored and lonely too. She realized that playing with her red car was not as fun as playing with Tom. She missed him and wanted to be friends again. They both felt sorry for what they had said and done. They wanted to say sorry and make up. They walked towards each other and smiled. "I'm sorry, Tom," Amy said. "I was wrong to be angry at you. Your silver car is very nice and I'm happy for you. Can we share our cars and play together?" "I'm sorry, Amy," Tom said. "I was wrong to be selfish with you. Your red car is very cool and I like it. Can we share our cars and play together?" They hugged and said yes. They learned to accept each other's cars and be happy with what they had. They learned that being different is not bad, but good. They learned that sharing is caring and that friendship is more important than things. They played with their cars and had a lot of fun. They were friends again.
