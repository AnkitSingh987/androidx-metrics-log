Category: Beginner
Title: The Its Shadow on Ground and the Cat

One day, a big cat saw its shadow on the ground. The cat thought the shadow was very impressive. It liked to look at its shadow and play with it. The cat would jump and run, and the shadow would do the same. The cat was very happy. The cat wanted to catch its shadow, so it ran very fast. But the shadow was always just out of reach. The cat tried and tried, but it could not catch the shadow. The cat was very tired, but it did not give up. Then, the cat saw a puddle of water on the ground. It did not see the water and ran into it. The cat slipped on the water and fell down. The cat was wet and sad. But when it looked up, it saw its shadow again. The cat smiled and knew that it could still have fun with its shadow, even if it could not catch it.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter. The hunter liked to look for things. He had a big dog to help him. They went to the woods to observe the animals. One day, the hunter and the dog saw a big, difficult hill. They wanted to go up the hill. The hunter tried to climb, but it was very hard. The dog tried too, but it was also hard for the dog. At last, they got to the top of the hill. They were very happy. They observed many animals from the top. The hunter and the dog went home and had a good sleep. They had a fun day in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved ice-cream more than anything else. One day, she had a big idea. She thought, "I want to grow an ice-cream tree!" So, Mia went outside and planted a dry ice-cream cone in the ground. She watered it every day and hoped for the best. After a week, Mia saw a tiny tree start to grow. She was so happy! She told her mom, "Look, my ice-cream tree is growing!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's nice, dear." Mia couldn't wait for the tree to grow big and make ice-cream for her. One day, Mia saw something on her tree. It was not ice-cream, but a big, scary bug! The bug was eating her tree. Mia was very sad and cried. She yelled at the bug, "Go away! You are not nice!" But the bug did not listen. It kept eating and eating until there was no tree left. Mia's dream of having an ice-cream tree was gone. And the bad bug went away, looking for more trees to eat.

Once upon a time, there was a little furry dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to play outside in the sun. One day, Buddy saw a big house and wanted to go inside. He wanted to stay there for a while. Buddy asked the man who lived in the house if he could stay. The man said, "I will permit you to stay, but you must not go into the room at the end of the hall." Buddy was happy and went into the house. Buddy was good and did not go into the room for many days. But one day, he could not stop thinking about the room. He opened the door and went inside. In the room, there was a big, angry cat. The cat did not like Buddy and chased him out of the house. Buddy was sad and could not stay in the big house anymore.

Lily and Ben are at the park with Mom. They see a big cart with many toys in it. The cart belongs to Sam, who is playing with his friends. "Look, Mom, toys!" Lily says. She runs to the cart and tries to reach a doll with long hair. "Stop, Lily, those are not our toys," Mom says. "They belong to Sam. You have to ask him first." But Lily does not listen. She pulls the doll from the cart and hugs it. The doll is dirty and has a hole in its dress. "Hey, that's my doll!" Sam says. He runs to Lily and tries to take the doll back. "Give it to me, now!" "No, it's mine!" Lily says. She holds the doll tight and cries. "Let go, you are hurting her!" Sam says. He pulls harder and makes the hole bigger. "Stop, both of you!" Mom says. She comes to them and takes the doll from their hands. "This is not how you play. Lily, you have to say sorry to Sam. You cannot take his toys without asking. And Sam, you have to be gentle with your toys. You cannot rip them apart." Lily and Sam look at the doll and feel bad. They say sorry to each other and hug. Mom gives the doll back to Sam and helps him fix the hole. She tells Lily and Ben to play with their own toys or share nicely with others. They nod and smile. They learn a lesson and have fun at the park.
