Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big closet in his room. In the closet, he kept his toys and clothes. Tim's room was far away from his mom and dad's room, so he felt distant. One day, Tim found a pair of scissors. He thought it would be fun to cut some paper. He took the scissors and some paper and went to his closet. He sat down and started to cut the paper into many shapes. As he was cutting, his mom came into the room. She saw Tim with the scissors and said, "Tim, be careful with the scissors. They can hurt you." Tim looked at his mom and said, "Okay, Mom. I will be careful." Tim put the scissors away and showed his mom the shapes he had cut. They both smiled and cleaned up the closet together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a very important job. She had to rescue her toy bear, Mr. Mint, who was lost in the big park. One day, Lucy saw a big tree near the swings. She thought, "Maybe Mr. Mint is hiding there!" She went to look for him. But he was not there. Lucy felt sad but did not give up. The next day, Lucy saw a big bush near the slide. She thought, "Maybe Mr. Mint is hiding there!" She went to look for him. And there he was! Lucy was so happy to rescue her toy bear, Mr. Mint. They went home and had a big hug.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a happy girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends and help her family. One sunny day, Lily found a pretty ball in the park. She wanted to keep it, but her mom said, "We must find the owner and give it back." Lily and her mom went around the village, asking if anyone lost a ball. At last, they found a sad boy named Tom. He said, "That's my ball! I thought it was gone forever!" Lily gave the ball back to Tom, and he was very happy. The truth was, Lily wanted to keep the ball, but she knew it was right to give it back. The village people saw how Lily helped Tom and were very proud of her. They learned that being honest and kind can restore happiness to others. From that day on, everyone in the village tried their best to be like Lily and share the truth. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, enormous tree. This tree had many pretty patterns on its bark. A little boy named Tim and his best friend, a squirrel named Sam, loved to play near the tree. One sunny day, Tim and Sam found a big, red ball under the tree. "Wow! Look at this ball!" said Tim. "Let's share it and play together!" Sam nodded his head and they both started to play with the ball. As they played, they noticed the sun made pretty patterns on the ground through the leaves of the enormous tree. They laughed and had lots of fun sharing the ball and playing under the tree with the pretty patterns.

Once upon a time, there was a big king. He had a smooth, shiny crown. He wore it every day. One day, he went for a walk. The sun was very bright. The king met a small girl. She said, "Hi, King! Your crown is too shiny. I can't see. Can you shut your eyes?" The king shut his eyes. The girl took his smooth, shiny crown and ran away. The king opened his eyes. His crown was gone! He was sad. He looked for the girl. Then, he saw her. She was giving the crown to a poor boy. The boy was very happy. The king smiled. The girl was nice. She just wanted to make the boy happy. The king let the boy keep the crown.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to create things with her hands. One day, she made a veil out of soft cloth. She thought it was very pretty. Sue showed her veil to her friend, Tom. Tom said, "What a nice veil, Sue! Can I try it on?" Sue said, "Sure, Tom!" When Tom put on the veil, he felt fearful. He didn't know why, but he was very scared. Just then, a small bird flew by and took the veil from Tom's head. The bird flew away with the veil. Tom was not fearful anymore. Sue and Tom laughed and said, "That was unexpected!" They played together, happy that the veil was gone.
