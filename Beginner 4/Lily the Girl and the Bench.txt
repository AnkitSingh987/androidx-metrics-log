Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bench

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a distant bench under a big tree. Lily walked to the bench and sat down. She blinked her eyes and saw a tiny bug. "Hello, bug," said Lily. The bug blinked back at her. "Let's be friends," she said. The bug nodded and they played together. They had fun in the sun. After a while, Lily's mom called her. "Lily, it's time to go home!" Lily looked at the bug and said, "Bye, friend. See you soon!" The bug blinked goodbye. The next day, Lily came back to the bench. She saw the bug again. They played and laughed all day. Lily learned that even the smallest friends can bring big joy. And every time they blinked, they knew they would be friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red bus. Every day, the bus would drive down the street and pick up kids to take them to school. The bus driver, Mr. Brown, was a very nice man. He always said "hello" to the kids when they got on the bus. One day, a new kid named Tommy got on the bus. Mr. Brown said, "Hi, Tommy! Welcome to our regular bus!" Tommy was very happy to be on the bus. He sat next to a girl named Lily. They talked and laughed all the way to school. But then, something unexpected happened. The bus turned into a giant ice cream truck! Mr. Brown said, "Surprise, kids! Today, we are going to the ice cream factory instead of school!" The kids were so excited. They all got to pick their favorite ice cream flavors and had a great day. And from that day on, Tommy and Lily became best friends.

Tom and Sam were playing with a big box in the yard. They pretended it was a spaceship and made noises like zoom and beep. They had a lot of fun. Then Tom saw a lighter on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Look, Sam, I have a flame. It is impressive. It can make things burn." Sam was curious and wanted to see the flame. He said, "Let me have it, Tom. I want to try it too." Tom said, "No, it is mine. You can't have it." He did not want to share the lighter. Sam was angry and tried to grab the lighter from Tom. They fought and pulled and pushed. The lighter fell out of Tom's hand and landed on the box. The box caught fire and made a big flame. Tom and Sam were scared and screamed. They ran away from the box. The fire was too big and too hot. It spread to the grass and the trees. It made a lot of smoke and noise. The neighbors saw the fire and called the firemen. The firemen came with a big truck and a hose. They sprayed water on the fire and put it out. They were not happy with Tom and Sam. They scolded them and told them to never play with fire again. Tom and Sam were very sorry and very sad. They had ruined their box and their yard. They had made a big mess and a big trouble. They had no more fun. They wished they had never found the lighter. They learned a hard lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely little dog named Ben. Ben lived in a small house with his friend, Sam. Sam was a kind boy who loved to play with Ben. They were very happy together. One day, Sam went to the store and bought a pastry. It was a sweet treat that he wanted to share with Ben. When they got home, Sam put the pastry on the table. Ben looked at the pastry and started to demand it. He barked and jumped, showing Sam that he wanted it too. Sam laughed and broke the pastry into two pieces. He gave one piece to Ben and kept the other for himself. They sat down and ate the sweet treat together. Ben was no longer lonely, and they both enjoyed the pastry. From that day on, Ben and Sam shared many more treats and had lots of fun together.
