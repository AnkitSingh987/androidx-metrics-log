Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tap

One day, a little boy named Tim found a tap. The tap was in his yard. He was very happy. Tim wanted to show the tap to his friend, Sara. He thought, "I will record a video of the tap for Sara." Tim used his mom's phone to record the tap. He said, "Look, Sara! I found a tap!" After, he sent the video to Sara. She was excited to see it. She replied, "Wow, Tim! That's a cool tap!" The next day, Sara came to Tim's house to see the tap. They played with the tap, turning it on and off. They were very grateful for the fun they had. They knew the tap was special. And they were right, because the tap was magic.

One day, a girl named Mary went to school. She wore her nice uniform. In her class, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was careless. He liked to play and not listen. In art class, they had to stir paint. The teacher said, "Be careful when you stir the paint." Mary listened, but Tom did not. He stirred the paint fast and wild. The paint went everywhere! "Oh no, my uniform!" cried Mary. Tom felt bad. He said, "I'm sorry, Mary. I will be more careful next time." They cleaned up the paint together. From that day on, Tom was never careless again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to worry about things. She would worry about the sun, the rain, and even the wind. One day, her mom gave her a lecture about not worrying so much. She said, "Sue, you need to be useful and help others instead of worrying all the time." Sue thought about what her mom said and decided to be useful. She went to the park to help her friends. She saw her friend Tim with a big, heavy bag. Sue helped Tim carry the bag to the other side of the park. Sue felt good about being useful. As they walked, a big gust of wind came and blew the bag open. Out came lots of balloons! It was a surprise party for Sue! Her friends had planned it to show her that she didn't need to worry so much. Sue learned that being useful and helping others made her feel much better than worrying all the time.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a doll and Ben had a car. They were having fun, but they also made a big mess. There were toys, papers, and crayons everywhere. "Look, Mom is coming!" Lily said. "We have to clean up!" But Ben was lazy. He did not want to clean up. He wanted to keep playing with his car. "No, I don't want to!" Ben said. "You do it!" Lily was angry. She did not like Ben's attitude. She wanted to teach him a lesson. "Fine, I will do it!" Lily said. "But you have to help me with one thing." "What is it?" Ben asked. Lily smiled. She had a plan. She picked up a pin from the floor and hid it in her hand. "Here, take this cloth and wipe the table," Lily said. "It is dirty." Ben took the cloth and walked to the table. He did not see the pin in Lily's hand. He started to wipe the table. "Ouch!" Ben cried. He felt a sharp pain in his finger. He dropped the cloth and looked at his hand. He saw a drop of blood and the pin. "Lily, you tricked me!" Ben said. "You put a pin in the cloth!" Lily laughed. She thought it was funny. "That's what you get for being lazy!" Lily said. "Now you have to clean up and put a band-aid on your finger!" Ben was not happy. He realized that Lily was right. He was lazy and mean. He felt sorry for what he did. "I'm sorry, Lily," Ben said. "You are a good sister. Can you help me with the band-aid?" Lily nodded. She was not angry anymore. She was a good sister too. She helped Ben with the band-aid and then they cleaned up together. They learned their lesson and hugged each other. Mom was proud of them.
