Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Max

One day, a little boy named Tim had a fun event at his house. He invited all his friends to play games and eat yummy treats. Tim was very happy because he loved spending time with his friends. During the event, Tim's friends wanted to play a game called "reverse". In this game, they had to walk and talk backward. Everyone was laughing and having a great time. They were all very good at playing "reverse". Tim's dog, Max, watched the fun game. Max was an obedient dog and always listened to Tim. When Tim asked Max to try playing "reverse", Max did his best to walk and bark backward. Tim and his friends clapped and cheered for Max. They all had a great day at the event.

One day, a little girl named Jane went out to play. She wore her favorite white dress with gray dots. Jane loved her dress very much. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. It was a perfect day to play outside. While Jane was playing, she saw a small gray cat. The cat looked sad and cold. Jane thought, "Maybe the cat needs a dress too." She went inside her house and found a small piece of cloth. Jane made a tiny dress for the cat. "Here, kitty," said Jane. "This is for you. Now you can be warm and pretty like me." The cat put on the tiny dress and smiled. Jane and the cat played together all day. The cat was very happy, and Jane felt good for helping her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Eliza. She loved to play in the garden, and today she had a great idea. Eliza wanted to build a boat. She got some sticks and a big rock and built the boat. Then, Eliza put yummy things in the boat like cakes and sweets. She also added a toy bear. Eliza was so happy she jumped up and down. Suddenly, she saw her big brother and he was very angry. He wanted to destroy Eliza's boat. Eliza was very sad but she had a great idea. She asked her big brother to help her and together they rebuilt the boat. Now Eliza's boat was even better and the yummy things were safe. Eliza was so happy and she smiled. She and her brother had lots of fun playing with the boat in the garden all day. They even shared the yummy things they had put in the boat.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with a big ball in the park. They take turns to roll the ball and run after it. One day, they see a big hill in the park. They want to roll the ball down the hill and see how far it goes. They run to the top of the hill with the ball. "Are you ready?" Lily asks Ben. "Yes, I am ready!" Ben says. They push the ball with their hands and watch it roll down the hill. The ball goes very fast and very far. It rolls past the trees, the flowers, and the pond. It rolls all the way to the other side of the park. "Wow, look at that!" Lily says. "That is amazing!" Ben says. They run down the hill to get the ball. They are very happy and excited. They see a man with a dog near the ball. The man picks up the ball and smiles at them. "Hello, kids. Is this your ball?" the man asks. "Yes, it is our ball. We rolled it down the hill. It went very far!" Lily says. "That is very impressive. You are very lucky. This is a special ball. It has a point inside. If you shake it, it will tell you something." the man says. He shakes the ball and shows them the point. The point has words on it. It says: "You are smart and brave." "Wow, that is cool!" Ben says. "Can we try?" Lily asks. "Sure, you can try. Here you go." the man says. He gives them the ball. Lily and Ben shake the ball and look at the point. The point says: "You are kind and fun." They smile and hug each other. They thank the man and his dog. They play with the ball some more. They are very lucky. They have a special ball and a special friend.
