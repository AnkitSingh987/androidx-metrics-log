Category: Beginner
Title: A Tale of the Cat

One day, a cat and a dog sat under a big tree. They were very comfortable. They began to discuss what they liked to drink. The cat said, "I like milk." The dog said, "I like water." As they talked, a big bird flew down from the tree. The bird said, "I heard you talking about water. I know where there is a lot of water." The cat and the dog were very surprised. They did not know that the bird could talk. The bird took them to a big pool of water. The dog was very happy. He jumped in and started to play. The cat did not like the water. She just watched the dog play. Then, the bird did something unexpected. It brought a bowl of milk for the cat. Both the cat and the dog were happy and thanked the bird for the surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a little blue bird. The bird was very proud of its blue feathers. It lived in a big tree with other birds. The other birds had many colors, but the little blue bird liked its blue the most. One day, the little blue bird saw a big red bird. The red bird said, "I dare you to fly up to the top of the tree." The blue bird was scared, but it wanted to show the red bird that it was brave. So, the blue bird tried to fly up to the top of the tree. The wind was strong, and the blue bird had a hard time flying. But it kept trying and trying. At last, the blue bird made it to the top of the tree. All the other birds cheered. The little blue bird was so proud and happy. It showed the big red bird that it was brave and strong. And from that day, the little blue bird was not scared to fly high up in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She lived in a big castle. The princess was very sad because her days were dull. She wanted a friend to play with. One day, she went to the park. She saw a little girl. The little girl was watching the birds. The princess went to the girl and said, "Hi, I am a princess. Do you want to play with me?" The little girl said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" They played together and watched the birds. They became good friends. The princess was not sad anymore. They had fun every day.

Mum and Dad were in the living room. Dad was explaining something. "Look at that small bull in the field," said Dad. Mum looked out of the window. "What a cute little bull," Mum said. "It's not just cute," Dad said, "It shows us how very brave animals can be, even if they are small." Dad explained that the small bull was eating the grass and had been very brave as it had walked away from the big bulls. Mum smiled and said, "I'm so proud of that little bull, aren't you?" Dad agreed and explained that all animals deserve our respect, no matter how small.

It was a dark night. Little Sam was walking in the woods and he heard a noise. Suddenly he saw a snake! He was scared, but he decided not to move. He watched the snake carefully. "Hey, little snake," he said. "What are you doing here?" But the snake didn't answer. Suddenly, Sam felt a warm feeling in his heart. He knew he had to help the snake. He gently picked the snake up and carried it to a nearby lake. Then Sam put the snake in the lake and he watched as it swam away. Sam smiled. He knew that he had helped heal the snake. He had made it feel safe again. He put his hands together and said a little prayer. Then he turned to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little car with a thin wheel. The car loved to zoom around the town. One day, the car saw a big hill and wanted to go up. As the car went up the hill, it felt something was not right. The thin wheel started to wobble. The car knew it needed to fix the wheel soon. The car found a friend to help fix the wheel. Together, they made the thin wheel strong. Now, the car could go up the big hill without any wobble. The car was very happy and zoomed around the town again.
