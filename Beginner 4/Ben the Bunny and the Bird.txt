Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Bird

One day, a little bunny named Ben went out to play. The sunshine was perfect for a fun day. Ben hopped in the green grass and saw a big tree. He wanted to climb the tree and see what he could find. As Ben climbed the tree, he saw a little bird stuck in a net. The bird looked sad and scared. "Help me, please," said the bird. Ben wanted to help his new friend escape. Ben used his sharp teeth to bite the net. The net broke, and the bird was free. "Thank you, Ben," said the bird. They both played in the perfect sunshine all day long. They were very happy and became best friends.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy who loved to play outside. She was three years old and had a big garden to explore. One day she couldn't find her toy and started tob cry. Then a doctor appeared. He was kind and had a big smile on his face. The doctor looked around and saw the toy under the bush. He bent down and lifted the toy up. He was happy to give Lucy her toy back. The doctor said, "Let's go inside and wash it so it will be clean again." So they went to wash the toy and when they were done, it was sparkling clean. Lucy was so happy to have her toy back and smiled at the doctor. She thanked him for helping her and hugged him. The kind doctor just smiled and said," You're welcome. Now go and have fun with your toy." So Lucy went out to the garden to play. She had a wonderful day playing with her toy.

One day, a big bird and a small fish were friends. The big bird liked to dive into the water. The small fish liked to play in the clay. They were very happy. One day, the big bird said, "I want to show you how to dive. It is very fun!" The small fish said, "Okay, let's try!" So the big bird helped the small fish to dive. They had a lot of fun! But then, they found a big box in the clay. The big bird and the small fish opened the box. Inside the box, there were many useful things for their friends. They were very happy and took the box to share with their friends. The big bird and the small fish learned that sharing is good and makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a tough boy who liked to play outside. One day, Tim found a flashlight in his room. He was very happy and wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look, I have a flashlight!" Sam was excited and said, "Let's play with it in the dark!" So, they went to a small room and turned off the light. They played with the flashlight, making shadows on the wall. But then, the flashlight stopped working. Tim and Sam were sad. They tried to fix it, but they couldn't. Tim had to return the broken flashlight to his room. They couldn't play with the flashlight anymore, and the fun day ended in a bad way.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to organize his toys. He had many toys, but his favorite toy was a clean, metal car. He played with it all the time. One day, Tim could not find his metal car. He looked everywhere, but it was gone. He was very sad. His mom and dad helped him look for the car, but they could not find it either. They all felt sad and did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's little sister, Sue, walked into the room with the clean, metal car. She had taken it to play with and forgot to put it back. Tim was so happy to see his car again! He thanked Sue and gave her a big hug. From that day on, Tim made sure to share his toys with Sue, and they played together happily.
