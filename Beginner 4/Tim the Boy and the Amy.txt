Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Amy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a small, red heart toy. He loved to throw his heart toy up in the air and catch it. One day, while playing, Tim met a girl named Amy. Amy had a big, black ball. They decided to play together. Tim threw his heart toy to Amy and she threw her black ball to Tim. They played catch and had lots of fun. But soon, they began to argue. Tim wanted to play with his heart toy more, but Amy wanted to play with her black ball more. They could not agree on what to play with. Tim and Amy decided to take turns. First, they played with the heart toy and then with the black ball. They learned to share and play together. In the end, Tim and Amy became good friends. They knew that sharing was the best way to have fun and keep everyone happy.

One day, a little dog named Spot found a buckle on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to show his friend, a cat named Kitty. "Look, Kitty! I found a buckle!" said Spot. Kitty looked at the buckle and thought it was very pretty. Spot and Kitty wanted to find something to do with the buckle. They saw a big tree and decided to climb it. When they got to the top, they found a yummy fruit. They picked the fruit and used the buckle to hold it. As they climbed down the tree, they saw a big flower. The flower could spray water! Spot and Kitty played with the flower, laughing as it sprayed water on them. They shared the yummy fruit and enjoyed their fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a little house near the woods, there lived a girl named Sally. She loved to play ball on the pitch near her house. One day, Sally found a little box in her room. She did not know what was inside it. Sally asked her mom, "What is in this little box?" Her mom said, "You will find out when you can count to ten." Sally was excited and started to learn how to count. Every day, Sally practiced counting. She counted her toys, her fingers, and the trees on the pitch. Finally, she could count to ten! She ran to her mom and said, "Mom, I can count to ten now! Can I open the little box?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can." Sally opened the little box and found a shiny new ball inside. She was so happy! Now she could play ball on the pitch with her friends. Sally learned that counting was fun and useful. From that day on, she loved to count everything she saw.

Once upon a time, there was a nurse named Jane. Jane was an ordinary nurse who helped people every day. One day, Jane saw a big box. The box had a note on it. The note said, "Do not open until tomorrow." Jane was very curious about the box. She wanted to know what was inside. She thought about opening it, but she remembered the note said to wait until tomorrow. So, she decided to let the box sit for one more day. The next day, Jane opened the box. Inside the box, she found a big cake and a note that said, "Surprise! You are the best nurse ever!" Jane was so happy she had waited to open the box. She shared the cake with all her friends and they all had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Sam. Sam liked to travel. He would walk and run all day long. One day, Sam saw a big hill. He wanted to climb it. Sam was so excited! Sam started to climb the hill. But then, he fell and hurt his knee. It was very bad. Sam cried and cried. He wanted his mom and dad to help him. But they were too far away. Sam felt sad and alone. A deaf dog came by and saw Sam. The dog wanted to help. But the dog could not hear Sam's cries. The dog walked away. Sam stayed on the hill with his hurt knee. He was sad and scared. The end.
