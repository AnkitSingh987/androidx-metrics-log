Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Slide

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to slide. He put on his shoes and went outside to the park. He saw a big slide and ran to it. Tim was very happy to slide down the big slide. As he slid down, he saw something on the ground. It was a paper with many words on it. Tim picked it up and read it. It was an exam. Tim did not like exams. He thought they were disgusting. He wanted to play, not take an exam. But then, he had an idea. He would make the exam fun. He drew funny pictures on the paper and laughed. He made the exam into a game. Just then, Tim's mom came to the park. She saw him playing with the exam paper. She was surprised because she thought Tim did not like exams. Tim showed her the funny pictures he drew. Mom laughed and said, "You made the disgusting exam fun!" Tim and his mom slid down the slide together, happy that they had a fun day at the park.

Once there was a lime that shone brightly in the sun. Its pale green skin sparkled with the light of day. Everyone loved the lime's shine, but nobody wanted it because it was spoiled. The little lime felt sad. It sat there sadly, wondering why it was alone. But then one day, something amazing happened. A little girl came by and saw the lime. Her eyes lit up when she saw it sparkle in the sunlight. She quickly grabbed it and held it close. The little girl declared that the lime was the most beautiful thing she had ever seen. She promised to love and care for it forever. The lime was so happy it shone even brighter! Ever since that day, the lime and the little girl have been the best of friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big box of paint. He wanted to paint a pretty picture for his mom. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, do you want to paint with me?" Tim said. Sam smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's paint together." Tim and Sam went to a safe place in the yard to paint. They put a big paper on the ground. Then, they opened the paint box. There were many colors: red, blue, yellow, and more. Sam took a brush and started to paint. Tim took a cup of water and filled it. They dipped their brushes in the water and then in the paint. They painted a big sun, a blue sky, and a green tree. They also painted a house and some flowers. They were happy with their picture. "Look, Tim! Our picture is so nice!" Sam said. Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Sam! My mom will love it!" They let the paint dry and then gave the picture to Tim's mom. She was very happy and hung it on the wall.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a very glad dog. He loved to play with his ball and run in the yard. One day, Spot saw a door. The door was open just a little bit. Spot wanted to see what was on the other side, so he pushed the door and went through. Spot found a big park with lots of grass and trees. He was so happy to escape and run around. He played with his ball and met new friends. At the end of the day, Spot went back through the door and was glad to be home.

One day, a yummy apple sat on a stage. It was a big, red apple. A little girl named Lucy saw the apple. She wanted to eat it. She said, "I want to eat that yummy apple!" Lucy climbed on the stage to get the apple. Her friend Tom saw her and said, "Lucy, be careful! The apple is not good to eat. It will rot soon!" Lucy looked at the apple again. She saw that it was starting to rot. She said, "Thank you, Tom! I will not eat the apple." Tom and Lucy left the stage. They went to find another yummy snack to eat. They were happy to have each other as friends.
