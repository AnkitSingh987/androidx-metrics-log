Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Fish and the Dance

One day, a colorful jellyfish wanted to dance. She lived in the sea with her friends. Her friends were a fish, a crab, and a starfish. They all liked to play together. The jellyfish said, "Let's have a dance party!" The fish said, "Yes, I love to dance!" The crab said, "I can dance too!" But the starfish was sad. He said, "I can't dance. I don't know how." The colorful jellyfish had an idea. She said, "We can teach you how to dance!" They all practiced dancing together. The starfish tried very hard to learn how to dance. He was still sad because he could not dance as well as his friends. At the dance party, the starfish was still sad. He said, "I can't dance like you." The colorful jellyfish said, "That's okay. We all dance in our own way." The starfish smiled and started to dance his own dance. All the sea friends danced together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to pretend and play with her crayons. One day, she saw a distant star in the sky and had a big idea. Lily took her crayons and drew a beautiful picture of the star. She showed it to her friends and they all wanted to pretend to go to the distant star together. They made a big rocket out of boxes and pretended to fly to the star. When they got to the star, they met a kind alien who taught them how to share and be nice to each other. They all played together and had so much fun. They learned that sharing and being kind to others is what makes everyone happy. And so, they took the lesson back to their home and shared it with everyone they knew.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear. He was very glad. He liked to keep his toys in a big box. One day, he saw a big red ball outside his house. He wanted to play with it and keep it in his box. The little bear played with the big red ball all day. He was very tired, but very happy. He knew it was time to go home and put the ball in his box. But, when he got home, the box was gone! He was very sad. The little bear looked and looked for his box. He asked his friends if they had seen it. They all said no. Then, he saw a big hill. He thought maybe his box was on the other side. He walked up the hill and found his box! The little bear was so glad. He put the big red ball in the box and went to sleep.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a funny frog named Fred. Fred loved to play with his friends. Every day, they would meet at the park and have lots of fun. One sunny day, Fred saw his friend Sam the squirrel. "Let's play!" said Fred. Sam agreed, but he had to wait for his little sister first. So, they sat on a rock and waited. While they waited, they watched the birds fly and the bugs crawl. Finally, Sam's little sister came. They were ready to play. Fred, Sam, and Sam's sister played a game of tag. Fred would spring high into the air to tag his friends. They all laughed and had a great time. "Tag, you're it!" said Fred as he tagged Sam. They played until the sun went down. As they said goodbye, Fred knew he had a great day with his friends. He couldn't wait to play with them again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, in a big yellow house, there lived a huge dog named Max. Max was a happy dog who loved to play with his ball. One day, Max saw a little cat named Lucy playing with a toy. Max wanted to play with Lucy, so he said, "Lucy, can I play with you and your toy?" Lucy looked at Max and said, "No, Max. You are too huge and might break my toy." Max was sad and went back to his ball. Later, Max saw Lucy stuck in a tree. He wanted to help, but he was scared that Lucy would punish him for being huge. Max decided to help anyway. He stood on his back legs and let Lucy climb on him. Together, they got out of the tree. Lucy said, "Thank you, Max. I'm sorry for not letting you play before." From that day on, Max and Lucy were best friends, and they played together every day.
