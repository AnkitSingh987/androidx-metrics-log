Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Quiet Boy and the Drawing

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw and color with his crayons. One day, Tim found a big eraser on the table. He picked it up and looked at it. Tim wanted to play a game with the eraser. He thought of a fun game called "Number Eraser". In this game, Tim would say a number and then erase that many things in his drawing. Tim started to play the game. First, Tim said the number two. He erased two trees in his drawing. Then, he said the number three. He erased three flowers. Tim had so much fun playing "Number Eraser" with his big eraser. He played until his drawing was all gone.

One day, a ball and a slide were in the park. The ball was red and the slide was blue. The ball wanted to roll down the slide. The ball said to the slide, "Can I roll down you?" The slide said, "Yes, you can roll down me. It will be fun!" The ball went up the slide and started to roll down. The ball went fast and laughed, "Whee! This is cool!" The ball rolled all the way to the bottom of the slide. The ball was very happy. A little girl came to the park and saw the ball and the slide. She wanted to play too. She climbed up the slide and went down just like the ball. The girl laughed and said, "This is cool!" The ball and the slide were happy to play with the little girl. They all had fun together.

Once there was a man that had a dirty wet shirt. He was keen to wear it to a special event, so he put it on. Unfortunately the shirt was too big for him! He went to ask his friend for help. “Can you help me shrink my shirt?” he asked. His friend smiled and said, “Yes. Put it in the dryer and let it spin around for a few minutes. Then it should be a better fit!” The man thanked his friend and did as he said. He put his wet and dirty shirt into the dryer and watched it spin. When the shirt came out it looked much better. It had shrunk enough so that it fit him perfectly! The man was really happy and he wore his shirt to the event. He looked very smart and he was delighted with his new look.

Tom and Mia are twins. They like to help Mom in the kitchen. Today, Mom is making soup for lunch. She tells Tom and Mia to measure some water and put it in the pot. Tom and Mia each take a cup and fill it with water. But Tom wants to put more water than Mia. He says his cup is bigger and he can measure better. Mia says no, they have to measure the same or the soup will be too thin or too thick. They start to argue and pull the cups. The cups slip from their hands and splash water everywhere. The water spills on the stove and makes a loud hiss. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what happened. She sees the mess and the water on the fire. She turns off the stove and scolds Tom and Mia. She says they are not careful and they could get hurt. Tom and Mia feel sorry and sad. They say they are sorry to Mom and to each other. They help Mom clean up the water and the cups. Mom says it's okay, they can still make soup. She gives them new cups and shows them how to measure the right amount of water. She says they have to work together and listen to each other. Tom and Mia nod and smile. They measure the water and put it in the pot. They also help Mom add some carrots, potatoes, and chicken. They stir the soup and smell the yummy aroma. Mom says the soup is almost ready. She tells Tom and Mia to set the table and call Dad and Baby Sam. Tom and Mia run to the dining room and get the plates, spoons, and napkins. They also get some bread and butter. They call Dad and Baby Sam and tell them lunch is ready. They all sit down and enjoy the hot soup. Mom says the soup is delicious and thanks Tom and Mia for their help. Tom and Mia say they are happy to help and they like soup. They hug Mom and say they love her. Mom hugs them back and says she loves them too. They eat and talk and laugh. They don't mind that it's dark and rainy outside. They have soup and love inside.
