Category: Beginner
Title: The Tree with Root and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tree with a big root. The tree was very tall and strong. One day, a crazy bird came and sat on a branch. The bird started to shake the tree with all its might. As the bird shook the tree, the big root started to move. The other animals in the forest saw this and were very surprised. They had never seen a root move like that before. They all gathered around to watch the crazy bird and the moving root. Then, something unexpected happened. The big root lifted up from the ground and turned into a magic door. The animals were so excited and curious. They all went through the magic door and found a beautiful new world full of fun and adventure. The crazy bird had shown them a secret place they never knew existed.

Lila and Sam went to the beach with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and the water. They also liked to look for shells. Shells were pretty and shiny and had different shapes and colors. "Look, Sam, I found a big shell!" Lila said, holding a pink and white shell in her hand. "It has stripes and spots and a hole. Do you want to see?" "Yes, please!" Sam said, running to his sister. He took the shell and looked at it closely. He put it to his ear and listened. "I can hear the sea!" he said, smiling. "That's cool!" Lila said. "Can I hear it too?" "Sure!" Sam said, giving the shell back to Lila. She put it to her ear and heard the waves and the wind. They decided to keep the shell and look for more. They found many shells, some small and some big, some round and some pointy, some smooth and some rough. They put them in a bucket and showed them to Mom and Dad. "Wow, you found some beautiful shells!" Mom said, admiring their collection. "You can make a necklace or a picture with them. Or you can just keep them as a souvenir." "I want to make a necklace!" Lila said. "Can you help me, Mom?" "Of course, sweetie!" Mom said. "We need some string and a needle. Do you know what string and needle are?" "Yes, I do!" Lila said. "String is long and thin and flexible. You can tie it and twist it and bend it. Needle is small and sharp and metal. You can poke it and sew it and make holes." "That's right!" Mom said. "You are very smart, Lila. Let's go and find some string and needle and make a necklace with your shells." "Yay!" Lila said, clapping her hands. She picked up her bucket and followed Mom. "What about you, Sam?" Dad asked. "Do you want to make something with your shells?" "No, thank you, Dad," Sam said. "I want to play with my shells. I can pretend they are animals or cars or houses. I can make stories with them and have fun." "That's fine, Sam," Dad said. "You are very creative, Sam. Let's go and play with your shells." "Yay!" Sam said, picking up his bucket and following Dad. Lila and Sam had a great day at the beach with their shells. They learned new words and made new things. They were happy and proud of their shells.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to be organized. He had a big box for his toys, a small box for his books, and a tiny box for his shoes. One day, Tim went for a walk and found a temple. The temple was big and old. Tim went inside the temple and saw a cat. The cat was shivering. "Brrr, I am cold," said the cat. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he looked around the temple for something warm. Tim found a big, soft blanket. He picked it up and brought it to the cat. "Here, cat, this will keep you warm," said Tim. The cat stopped shivering and smiled at Tim. "Thank you, Tim," said the cat. But then, a strong wind blew through the temple. The wind was so strong that it knocked Tim and the cat down. The blanket flew away, and the cat started to shiver again. Tim tried to catch the blanket, but he couldn't. The wind was too strong. Tim and the cat were sad and cold. The end.
