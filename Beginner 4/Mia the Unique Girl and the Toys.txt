Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Unique Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a unique little girl named Mia. She had a big room with lots of toys. Mia loved to play with her toys all day. One day, Mia found a big sheet in her room. She was very happy and thought of a fun game to play. Mia decided to make a fort with the sheet. She put the sheet on top of her bed and used her toys to hold it down. Mia crawled under the sheet and felt like she was in her own special world. She played and played until she got tired. Then, Mia took a nap under her sheet fort. While Mia was napping, her mommy came into her room. She wanted to lock the door so Mia could sleep without noise. But when Mia's mommy saw the sheet fort, she thought it was so special that she wanted to join Mia. So, instead of locking the door, Mia's mommy crawled under the sheet fort and took a nap with Mia. When Mia woke up, she was so happy to see her mommy there. They both laughed and played together under the sheet fort for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy who loved to play in her backyard. One sunny day, she found a purple ball under a big tree. She picked it up and started to play with it. Amy was so happy to have found the ball. As Amy was playing, a little boy named Tim came into the backyard. Tim saw the purple ball and said, "Hey, that's my ball!" Amy didn't want to give the ball back, and they both started to cry. Amy's mom heard them crying and came outside to help. She asked, "What's wrong?" Tim said, "Amy has my purple ball." Amy's mom told her, "Amy, you need to give the ball back to Tim. It's his ball." Amy listened to her mom and gave the ball back to Tim. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Amy." Amy felt sad, but then Tim asked, "Do you want to play with the ball together?" Amy's face lit up, and she said, "Yes, please!" So, Amy and Tim played with the purple ball in the backyard, and they both had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful owl who lived in a big tree. The owl had many friends in the forest. One day, a little bird came to visit the owl. "Hello, Mr. Owl," said the little bird. "Can you teach me how to shake my wings like you do?" The owl was happy to help. He flapped his wings up and down, showing the little bird how to shake them. The little bird tried to copy the owl, shaking his wings too. Soon, all the animals in the forest were shaking their wings and tails, having a fun dance party. The cheerful owl was proud to see everyone so happy. And that is how the owl and his friends spent their days, shaking and dancing together in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a small, smelly fish. The fish lived in a tank. The tank was in a big room with other tanks. The fish had many friends in the other tanks. One day, the fish saw a new toy in the tank. The toy was big and fun. The fish wanted to play with the toy. But the other fish in the tank did not want to play. The small fish insist to play with the toy. At last, the other fish agreed to play with the small fish and the toy. They all had lots of fun. They played all day. The small fish was very happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ella. Ella went to the park to play. When she got there, she saw a white bunny. She smiled and asked the bunny to come closer. The Bunny hopped over and sat with Ella. She laughed and said, "Let me tell you a joke!" The bunny smiled. Ella told the bunny a funny joke and they both laughed. Then, Ella asked the bunny to come play with her. The bunny hopped around and they had fun all day. The End.
