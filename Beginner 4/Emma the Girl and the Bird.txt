Category: Beginner
Title: Emma the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Emma was playing outside. She loved to imagine she was a big bird. She would spread her arms and run around, feeling the gentle wind on her face. From her view, she could see the big blue sky and the green grass. As Emma played, she saw a little boy named Tim sitting under a tree. Tim looked sad. Emma walked over and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim looked up and said, "I can't imagine like you can, Emma. I want to play too." Emma thought for a moment and then smiled. "Close your eyes, Tim," she said. Tim closed his eyes, and Emma began to tell him a story. She told him about a big bird that could fly high in the sky. As she spoke, Tim began to smile. Suddenly, a gentle breeze blew, and Tim felt like he was flying with the big bird. He opened his eyes and saw that he and Emma were floating in the air! They laughed and played, soaring high in the sky. From their new view, they could see the whole world below. When they landed, Tim hugged Emma and said, "Thank you, Emma! Now I can imagine too!" From that day on, Emma and Tim played together, imagining all kinds of adventures. And they always remembered that sometimes, the most unexpected things can happen when you use your imagination.

One day, a big storm came to a town. The wind blew hard and the rain fell fast. A little boy named Tim was scared. He saw his friend, a bird named Sam, outside his window. Sam was wet and cold. Tim opened the window and said, "Sam, come in. I offer you a warm place to stay." Sam said, "Thank you, Tim." Sam came in and they played together. They had fun and forgot about the storm. But then, a rude cat named Max came into the room. Max saw Sam and wanted to eat him. Tim tried to stop Max, but Max was too fast. Max ate Sam and ran away. Tim was very sad and cried. The storm outside was still bad, and now Tim had lost his friend too.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to step in puddles. One day, he saw a distant puddle near a big wreck. The wreck was an old car that did not work. Tim wanted to step in the puddle, but his mom said, "No, Tim, don't go near the wreck." Tim did not listen to his mom. He ran to the distant puddle by the wreck. As he stepped in the puddle, he heard a loud noise. The wreck started to move! Tim was scared, and he tried to run away. But it was too late. The wreck fell on the puddle, and Tim got wet and hurt. His mom came to help him, but she was sad. Tim learned that he should have listened to his mom.

Once there was a stubborn little boy, who loved playing outside. One day, he was running and playing around in the park. He was running so fast and laughed joyfully. Suddenly he fell on the ground and lost his hold on the ball he was playing with. The ball rolled to a wall and a hook stuck out of the wall. The little boy was curious and decided to try touching the hook, but it was too high for him to reach. He was very stubborn and did not want to give up. He decided to climb the wall to try and get to the hook. He climbed and climbed until he was at the top. He was so happy that he could finally touch the hook. He was just about to grab it, but he slipped and suddenly fell down. He bumped his head on the way and felt very sad. The stubborn little boy had been too eager to get what he wanted and it ended with a bad result. He had been too stubborn and it cost him dearly. He learnt a valuable lesson that day about being too eager.

Lila and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big bird with a long neck and a yellow beak. It is a duck. The duck is in a cage. Lila and Ben feel sorry for the duck. "Hello, duck," Lila says. "Why are you in a cage?" "Quack, quack," the duck says. "I want to escape. I want to swim in the pond and fly with my friends. But the man who owns me does not let me go. He keeps me here for people to look at." "That is not fair," Ben says. "You should be free. We will help you escape. We will open the cage and you can run away." Lila and Ben look around. They do not see the man who owns the duck. They sneak to the cage and push the door. The door opens. The duck is happy. He flaps his wings and jumps out of the cage. "Thank you, thank you," the duck says. "You are very kind. Now I can go back to my home. Goodbye, Lila and Ben." The duck runs to the pond. He splashes into the water and swims away. He is wet, but he does not mind. He is free. He sees his friends and joins them. They quack and fly together. Lila and Ben watch the duck. They are happy too. They have done a good thing. They wave to the duck and smile. They go back to playing in the park.
