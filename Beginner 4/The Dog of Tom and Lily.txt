Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog of Tom and Lily

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play catch with a ball. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They find a big space to throw the ball. "Tom, catch!" Lily says, and tosses the ball to Tom. Tom runs and catches the ball. He smiles and throws it back to Lily. "Lily, catch!" Tom says, and watches the ball fly in the air. But Lily does not catch the ball. The ball goes past Lily and hits a dog. The dog barks and chases the ball. "Uh-oh!" Lily says, and runs after the dog. Tom runs after Lily. The dog runs faster and faster. The dog does not want to give the ball back. "Stop, dog, stop!" Tom and Lily shout. But the dog does not stop. The dog runs out of the park and into the street. Tom and Lily stop at the edge of the park. They are scared. The street has many cars and trucks. They are loud and fast. "Mom, mom, help!" Tom and Lily cry. Their moms hear them and come quickly. They see the dog and the ball in the street. They are calm and smart. They wait for the cars and trucks to stop. Then they cross the street and get the dog and the ball. "Are you okay, Tom and Lily?" their moms ask. Tom and Lily nod. They are okay, but they are sad. They lost their ball for a long time. "Sorry, dog, sorry," Tom and Lily say to the dog. The dog wags its tail. The dog is sorry, too. The dog likes the ball, but the dog likes Tom and Lily more. "Here, you can have the ball," Tom says, and gives the ball to the dog. The dog is happy. The dog licks Tom and Lily. "Thank you, Tom, thank you," the dog says. The dog is nice. The dog is a friend. Tom and Lily smile. They are happy, too. They have a new friend. They hug the dog. "Let's go back to the park," Lily says. "We can play catch with the dog." "Okay," Tom says. "That sounds fun." They go back to the park with their moms and the dog. They play catch with the ball. They have a good day. They are friends for a year.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom and a little mouse named Jerry. They lived in a house near a lumber pile. Tom was always trying to catch Jerry, but Jerry was very fast. One day, Tom felt uncomfortable because he had a tummy ache. He was sitting by the lumber pile, holding his belly. Jerry saw Tom and felt sorry for him. "Tom, are you okay?" asked Jerry. Tom did not want to ignore Jerry, so he said, "My tummy hurts, Jerry." Jerry wanted to help Tom feel better. He found a soft pillow and gave it to Tom. "Here, Tom. This will make you feel better," said Jerry. Tom smiled and thanked Jerry. From that day on, they became good friends and played together near the lumber pile.

Once upon a time, there was a human named Mark. Mark was always alert and liked to help his friends. One day, Mark went for a walk in the park with his dog, Spot. As they walked, they saw a big box. Mark and Spot went to look at the box. They were very curious. Inside the box, they found a magic wand. Mark picked up the wand and waved it around. Suddenly, something unexpected happened! Mark and Spot switched places. Now, Mark was the dog and Spot was the human. They were both very surprised. They laughed and played in the park, trying to figure out how to switch back.

Once there was a little boy who had a great idea. He wanted to act like his daddy. So, he put on his daddy's hat and pretended to speak on his phone like daddy did. Daddy was not impressed and told him to stop acting. But the little boy didn't listen. He kept acting like his daddy and becoming more and more dull. He would follow daddy around the house and copy everything he did. Daddy finally got fed up and yelled at him. “I said I don't want you acting like me! Stop it now!” The little boy felt scared and ran away to his bedroom. He stayed in there until the evening coming out only to have dinner with his family. The little boy never acted like his daddy again. He had learned his lesson and also learned that his daddy was not as dull as he had originally thought.
