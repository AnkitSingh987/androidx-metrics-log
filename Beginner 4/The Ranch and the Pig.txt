Category: Beginner
Title: The Ranch and the Pig

Once upon a time, there was a ranch. On the ranch, there were many animals. The animals liked to play and talk to each other. One day, it was very icy outside. The animals stayed inside their warm homes. In one home, there was a cow and a pig. The cow said to the pig, "Brrr, it is so icy today. I am glad we are inside where it is warm." The pig agreed, "Yes, it is too cold to play outside today." Later, the sun came out, and the icy ground started to melt. The animals were happy they could go outside again. But they had to be careful. The sun was very hot, and it could burn their skin. They played in the shade and had a fun day at the ranch.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a very important hammer. He loved to build things with it. One day, he wanted to make a birdhouse for his new bird friend. Tim looked everywhere for his hammer, but he could not find it. He was sad. He had lost his important hammer. He asked his mom, "Mom, have you seen my hammer?" His mom said, "No, Tim, I have not seen your hammer. Did you look in your toy box?" Tim looked in his toy box, and there it was! His important hammer was hiding under a big red ball. Tim was very happy to find his hammer. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" Then, he went outside and built a beautiful birdhouse for his bird friend. They were both very happy.

Ben liked to play in the garden. He liked to look for bugs and flowers and rocks. One day, he saw something new. It was a lizard. The lizard was green and had a long tail. Ben wanted to be friends with the lizard. He moved slowly and quietly. He reached out his hand. The lizard did not run away. It looked at Ben with its small eyes. Ben smiled. He touched the lizard gently. The lizard felt warm and scaly. Ben was happy. He had gained a new friend. He picked up the lizard and put it in his pocket. He ran to his mom. He wanted to show her his friend. He said, "Mom, look! I found a lizard! He is my friend. Can I keep him?" Mom looked at Ben and the lizard. She was surprised. She said, "Wow, Ben, you are gifted. You have a special way with animals. But you know, lizards are wild. They need to live in their own home. They need to eat bugs and drink water and sunbathe. They are not pets. You have to let him go." Ben felt sad. He liked the lizard. He wanted to keep him. But he also wanted the lizard to be happy. He said, "OK, Mom. I understand. I will let him go. But can I visit him sometimes?" Mom nodded. She said, "Of course, Ben. You can visit him in the garden. But remember, he is not yours. He is free. You have to respect him and his space. And maybe, he will respect you and your friendship." Ben hugged his mom. He said, "Thank you, Mom. You are right. He is free. He is not mine. He is my friend." He took the lizard out of his pocket. He kissed him gently. He said, "Goodbye, friend. I will see you soon. Be happy." He put the lizard on the ground. The lizard looked at Ben. He nodded. He ran away. Ben waved. He felt proud. He had gained a new friend. And he had learned a new lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy bear that he loved a lot. He would care for the bear every day. Tim would take the bear with him everywhere he went. One cold day, Tim and his bear went to the park. Tim played and played, but then he got very hungry. He left his bear on a bench and went to find some food. When he came back, he could not find his bear. Tim looked everywhere for his bear. He was very sad. His arm was cold without his bear to hold. He cried and cried, but he could not find his bear. Tim went home without his bear, and he was very sad.
