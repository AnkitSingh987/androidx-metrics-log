Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Frog

One day, a little girl named Lily was walking to the post. She had a letter to send to her friend. On her way, she saw a frog sitting by a pond. The frog said, "Hi, Lily! If you kiss me, I will turn into a prince!" Lily thought about it, but she wanted to send her letter first. Lily went to the post and found out there were no more stamps available. She felt sad because she couldn't send her letter. She went back to the frog and said, "I will kiss you now." So she kissed the frog, but nothing happened. The frog just smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. I am not really a prince, but I wanted to make you feel better." Lily learned that sometimes, it's the little things, like a kiss or a kind word, that can make someone happy. Even though she couldn't send her letter, she was glad she could make the frog smile. And in the end, Lily made a new friend.

One day, a little girl named Mia was very excited. She wanted to mail a letter to her friend, Tim. Mia put the letter in a big blue box near her house. She was happy that her friend would get her letter soon. The next day, Mia was playing in the park. She ran very fast and fell down. "Ouch!" she cried. She hurt her knee. Her mommy came to help her. "It's okay, Mia. Let's put a bandage on your knee," her mommy said. Mia felt better with the bandage on her knee. A few days later, Mia got a letter from Tim. He was excited to get her letter too. They both liked getting mail from each other. Mia's knee was better now, and she was happy to have a friend like Tim.

One day, a boy named Tim and his mom went to the beach. Tim was very excite to play in the sand and surf. He saw a cheap, colorful toy boat at the store and asked his mom if he could buy it. "Mom, can I have this cheap boat for the surf?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, you can have the boat. But be careful when you play in the surf." Tim took his new boat to the water. He watched as the waves made the boat go up and down. Soon, a girl named Sue came by and saw Tim's boat. "Wow! I like your boat," Sue said. "Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play together!" They had a lot of fun playing in the surf with the cheap boat. They became good friends and promised to play together again next time they went to the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a little red wagon. It was a fit wagon, not too big and not too small. The wagon had four round wheels and a long handle. It loved to help people carry things from one place to another. One sunny day, the wagon went to the park. In the park, it saw a big, green tree. Under the tree, there was a small, brown dog. The dog was sad because it had a hurt leg. The wagon wanted to help the dog, so it went close to the dog and said, "Hi, I am the little red wagon. I can help you." The dog looked at the wagon and wagged its tail. The wagon said, "Let me introduce myself. I am a fit wagon and I can carry you to your home." The dog was happy and jumped into the wagon. The little red wagon carried the dog all the way to its home. The dog's family was very thankful to the little red wagon. From that day on, the little red wagon and the small, brown dog became best friends. They played together every day and went on many fun adventures. The wagon was always happy to help the dog and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a girl. She liked to wear a red hat. She had a pet cow. The cow liked to eat oats. They were very good friends. One day, they went for a walk. They saw a deep hole. They did not know what was in the hole. The cow was very scared. The girl said, "Do not worry, I will help you." The girl got a big rope. She put the rope in the hole. The cow pulled the rope. The girl held the cow. They pulled and pulled. Out of the hole came a big, happy frog! The frog said, "Thank you for helping me!" The girl and the cow were very surprised. They did not know frogs could talk. The frog became their friend. They all played together and ate oats. They were very happy.
