Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Furry Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a cold winter, there was a furry cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play in the snow. She would jump and run and have lots of fun. One day, Kitty saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it. Kitty stretched her legs and jumped very high. She climbed up the tree and saw a big, round bird's nest. Inside the nest, Kitty found a little bird. The bird was cold and scared. Kitty felt sad for the bird. She stretched her furry body around the bird to keep it warm. They became good friends and played together all winter long.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim dared to go near the old temple. The temple was very big and old. Tim was a stubborn boy and did not listen to his mom. His mom told him not to go near the temple, but he went anyway. As Tim walked near the temple, he saw a big, funny-looking bird. The bird said, "Hi! I am a talking bird. I live in this temple." Tim was very surprised. He did not know that birds could talk. The talking bird said, "I know you are stubborn, but I can help you. If you promise not to come near the temple again, I will give you a magic feather." Tim agreed and the bird gave him the feather. From that day on, Tim never went near the temple again and he became a good listener.

One day, a thin cat and a big dog were in a park. They liked to play together. The cat was good at singing songs, and the dog liked to listen. A bird heard the cat's song and got mad. The bird thought it was too loud. The bird wanted the cat to stop singing. The bird rushed to the cat and said, "Stop your song now!" The cat did not want to stop singing. The dog did not want the cat to stop either. They both rushed to the bird and said, "We like the song. It makes us happy." The bird thought about it and said, "Let's all sing together." So, the cat, dog, and bird sang a new song, and they all were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a nice duck. The duck liked to swim in a pond every day. The pond was big and had a lot of water for the duck to play in. The duck had fun when it was time to swim. The water in the pond was cool and nice for the duck. One day, the duck met a frog. The frog seemed very happy. The frog liked to jump and play in the pond too. The duck and the frog became good friends. They liked to swim and play together in the nice pond. The duck and the frog were very happy. They played in the pond every day. The duck and the frog had a lot of fun. They swam and jumped in the nice pond. The duck and the frog were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big, mysterious tent. It was a special tent, because it could change colors. Tim loved his tent very much. One day, a man came to Tim's town. He wanted to sell things. The man saw Tim's tent and said, "Wow! That is a great tent! Can I sell it for you?" Tim thought about it and said, "Yes, you can sell my tent." The man sold the tent, but he did not give Tim any money. Tim was very sad. He missed his tent and felt bad for letting it go. Tim's mom saw him crying and said, "Tim, it's okay. We can learn from this. Next time, be careful who you trust." From that day on, Tim learned to be careful with his things and who he trusts. And he never forgot the lesson his mom taught him.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a brown jacket that he loved very much. Tim wore the jacket every day, even when it was hot outside. One day, Tim saw a big puddle of mud. He thought it would be fun to jump in it. So, he did! But, when he got out of the puddle, his brown jacket was very dirty. Tim was sad and knew he needed to clean his jacket. Tim went home and asked his mom to help him clean the jacket. His mom said, "I will clean it, but you must promise to be more careful next time." Tim said, "I promise!" The next day, Tim wore his clean brown jacket again. He saw another puddle of mud, but this time, he remembered his promise and walked around it. Tim and his jacket stayed clean and happy.
