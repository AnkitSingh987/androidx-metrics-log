Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun. One day, he went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and it was a good day. While walking, Spot saw a big red ball. He thought it would be fun to find out who it belonged to. So, he picked up the ball in his mouth and started to look for its owner. In his search, Spot met a little girl named Sue. Sue was so happy to find her ball. She thanked Spot and gave him a big hug. From that day on, Spot and Sue became the best of friends. They had lots of fun together and made many good memories.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He had a big boat. The sailor loved to go on wild trips in the water. One day, the sailor saw a big fish. The fish was very big and very wild. The sailor wanted to catch the fish. The sailor tried to catch the wild fish, but it was too fast. The fish swam away and the sailor was sad. He wanted to find the fish again. So, the sailor went on a long trip to look for the wild fish. He looked and looked, but he could not find the fish. Then, something strange happened. The wild fish came back! But this time, the fish was not wild anymore. It was a nice fish. The fish wanted to be friends with the sailor. The sailor was very happy. They played together in the water. And the big, wild fish did not vanish again.

Once upon a time, there was a little purple bird named Bobo. Bobo was a fearful bird. He was scared of many things. Bobo had a friend named Gogo. Gogo was a big, strong bird. One day, Bobo and Gogo were playing in the park. They were having lots of fun. Suddenly, Bobo saw a big shadow on the ground. He was scared. He thought it was a big monster. Bobo said, "Gogo, help! I am scared!" Gogo looked up and saw it was just a cloud. Gogo said, "Don't be scared, Bobo. It's just a cloud. It can't scare you." Bobo felt better. They played together all day long. From that day on, Bobo was not scared of the big shadows anymore.

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim was weak and small. He liked to climb trees and play outside. One day, Tim saw a big subway train. He wanted to ride it, but his mom said, "No, Tim. You are too little." Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He would climb the fence and go to the subway by himself. The fence was high, but Tim was good at climbing. He went over the fence and saw the subway train. Tim got on the train and it started to move fast. He was scared and wanted his mom. The train went into a dark tunnel and stopped. Tim was all alone and cried. He should have listened to his mom.

One day, a chubby cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw a well with water inside. Kitty wanted to look at the water, but she was scared. She thought, "I need to balance on the edge to see the water." Kitty tried to balance on the edge of the well. She put one paw in front of the other and moved very slow. She said out loud, "I can do it. I can balance." A little bird heard Kitty and came to help. The bird said, "Hi, Kitty! I can help you balance. Hold on to me." Kitty held on to the bird and they looked at the water together. Kitty was happy and thanked the bird. They became good friends and played all day.

Sam and Mia were playing with their toy trucks in the yard. They liked to load them with rocks, sticks and leaves and pretend they were builders. Sam had a big red truck and Mia had a small yellow one. "Look, Mia, I found some metal!" Sam said, pointing to a shiny piece of tin on the ground. "Let's load it on my truck and take it to the dump." "Okay, Sam, but be careful. Metal is sharp and heavy," Mia said, helping him lift the tin. They put it on the back of Sam's truck and pushed it along the dirt. They came to a hill and Sam wanted to go down fast. He ran behind his truck and gave it a big push. "Whee!" he shouted, as his truck zoomed down the hill. But Sam was clumsy and tripped on a stone. He fell and scraped his knee. He cried out in pain and watched his truck roll away. The metal slid off the truck and hit a fence. It made a loud bang and a dent. Mia ran after Sam and hugged him. "Are you okay, Sam?" she asked, looking at his knee. "That was a bad fall." Sam sniffed and nodded. "I'm okay, Mia. But look at my truck. And the metal. And the fence. I made a big mess." Mia looked and saw the damage. She felt sorry for Sam, but also a little angry. "Sam, you were too fast and reckless. You should have been more careful. Metal is not a good thing to load on your truck. It can hurt you and other things." Sam felt ashamed and sorry. He apologized to Mia and the fence. He picked up his truck and the metal. He decided to load his truck with softer things, like flowers and feathers. He learned his lesson.
