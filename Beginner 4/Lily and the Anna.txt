Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Anna

Lily had a big doll that she loved very much. Her name was Anna. Lily played with Anna every day. She gave her hugs and kisses, and dressed her in pretty clothes. One day, Lily was playing with Anna in the garden. She saw a butterfly and ran after it. She forgot to hold Anna's hand. Anna fell on the ground and her arm broke. Lily was very sad. She cried and cried. Lily's mom heard her cry and came to see what was wrong. She saw Anna's broken arm and felt sorry for Lily. She said, "Don't worry, Lily. We can repair Anna. I have some glue and some tape. We can fix her arm and make her as good as new." Lily's mom took Anna and Lily inside the house. She found some glue and some tape in a drawer. She put some glue on Anna's arm and stuck it back together. Then she wrapped some tape around it to make it stronger. She said, "There, Anna is repaired. She just needs some rest and some love. Can you give her that, Lily?" Lily nodded and smiled. She hugged Anna and said, "I'm sorry, Anna. I love you very much. You are my best friend." She thanked her mom for repairing Anna. She took Anna to her bed and tucked her in. She kissed her good night and said, "Sleep well, Anna. Tomorrow we will play again."

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a soft, fluffy dog named Max. They loved to play all day. One day, Tim's mom gave him a notebook. It was a gift for being a good boy. Tim was very happy. He wanted to show Max his new notebook. He said, "Max, this is my notebook." Max wagged his tail and barked. Tim knew Max liked the notebook too. The next day, Tim took Max to the park to play. They met a little girl named Lily. Tim wanted Max to meet Lily. He said, "Lily, I want to introduce you to Max." Max wagged his tail and barked. Lily smiled and said, "Hi Max! Nice to meet you!" They all played together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a sunny park, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his red ball. He would run and jump all day. Spot was a very useful dog. He liked to help his friends. One hot day, Spot saw a tiny bird stuck in a tree. The bird was scared and needed help. Spot knew he had to rescue the bird. He thought of a plan. He would use his red ball to save the bird. Spot threw the red ball at the tree. The bird was free and flew away. Spot and the bird were very happy. They played together under the shade of the big tree. Spot was a hero, and he made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play in her room. One day, Sue was very careless and broke her toy. She felt sad and wanted to find a new toy to play with. Sue went to the cupboard to discover a new toy. She opened the cupboard and saw many toys. She picked a toy car and said, "Wow! I found a new toy car to play with!" Sue played with the toy car all day. She was very happy. Sue learned to be more careful with her toys. She knew that if she was careless again, she might break her new toy car. And she didn't want that to happen.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful daddy and his little girl. They loved to play games together. One day, they decided to play a new game called "match". Daddy showed the little girl some cards. Each card had a picture on it. There were two cards with a cat, two cards with a dog, and two cards with a ball. Daddy said, "We need to find the cards that match. If we find the cat cards, we win!" The little girl was so excited to play. She picked up one card and saw a cat. Then, she picked up another card, and it was a dog. "Oops!" she said, and put the cards back down. Daddy smiled and said, "It's okay, try again!" The little girl tried again. This time, she found the two cat cards that matched! Daddy clapped his hands and said, "You did it! You found the match!" They both laughed and played the game again and again. The cheerful daddy and his little girl had so much fun playing the match game together.
