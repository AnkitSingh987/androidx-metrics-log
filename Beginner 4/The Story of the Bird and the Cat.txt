Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. They loved to play with a big board. One day, a new friend, a bird, came to play with them. The cat and the dog were happy to have a new friend, but they were also a little jealous. They wanted to play with the board by themselves. The cat had an idea to hide the board from the bird. The dog agreed. They hid the board under a big tree. The next day, when the bird came to play, they all looked for the board, but could not find it. The bird was sad and flew away. Later, the cat and the dog felt bad for what they did. They wanted to unite and be friends with the bird again. They found the board and brought it back. The bird came back too, but with a big surprise. The bird had found another board! Now, they all had a board to play with, and they were all happy. The cat, the dog, and the bird played together every day, and they were never jealous again.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful boy named Tim. Tim had a red wagon. He loved to use his wagon to help people. One day, he saw an old lady with many bags. She looked tired. Tim went to the old lady and said, "I can help you with my wagon. I promise!" The old lady smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. You are very helpful." They put the bags in the wagon and walked together. As they walked, a dog jumped out from behind a tree. The dog was very big and it scared Tim. But the dog only wanted to play. The old lady laughed and said, "This is my dog, Max. He likes to play." Tim, the old lady, and Max all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a naughty boy who liked to play tricks on his friends. One day, Tom found a white sheet and had an idea. "I will dress like a ghost and scare my friends!" he thought. Tom put the sheet over his head and went to find his friends. He saw them playing in the park and went to scare them. As he came closer, he heard his friends talking. They were wondering what was making a strange noise in the bushes. Tom thought it was a good time to scare them. But as Tom jumped out to scare his friends, something unexpected happened. A real ghost came out of the bushes! The ghost said, "Boo!" Tom and his friends were so scared that they all ran away. The real ghost laughed and said, "It's fun to play tricks too!" From that day on, Tom was not naughty anymore and learned to be kind to his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jim. Jim loved to play. everywhere he went, he would play. One day, Jim decided to go to the store. When he got there, he paused. He was not sure what to do or what to get. Then he remembered his mom's order. She told him to get some candy. Jim was glad. He knew exactly what to do now and he started to look for the candy. Everywhere he looked, he saw different colors and shapes. It made him so happy. Finally, he found the candy and put it in his bag. He hurried out of the store, smiling. He had followed his mom's order and it made him glad.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play together. But sometimes they fight over toys. Today they fight over a pretty doll. Anna says, "It is mine!" Ben says, "No, it is mine!" Mom hears them and comes to the room. She sees them pulling the doll. She says, "Stop! You are hurting the doll. And you are hurting each other. You need to share. Or you need to separate." Anna and Ben do not want to share. They want the pretty doll. Mom says, "Okay, then you need to separate. Anna, you go to the couch. Ben, you go to the bed. You can play with other toys. But you cannot play with the doll." Anna and Ben are sad. They go to the couch and the bed. They play with other toys. But they do not have fun. They miss the pretty doll. And they miss each other. After a while, they look at each other. They feel sorry. They say, "I am sorry. I love you." They hug and kiss. They go to Mom and say, "We are sorry. We love you. Can we play with the pretty doll together?" Mom smiles and says, "Yes, you can. I am proud of you. You are good twins. You can play with the pretty doll together. But you need to be gentle and kind. And you need to take turns." Anna and Ben are happy. They go to the couch and play with the pretty doll. They are gentle and kind. They take turns. They have fun. They are good twins.
