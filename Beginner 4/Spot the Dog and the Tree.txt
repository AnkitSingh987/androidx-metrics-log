Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very happy dog who loved to play and run. One day, Spot went for a walk and met a big, incredible tree. He had never seen such a big tree before. Spot wanted to climb the tree, but he was too small. Then, Spot saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was the owner of the tree. Tim said, "Hi Spot, do you want to play with me?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, I want to play!" Tim and Spot played all day by the incredible tree. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. As the sun went down, Spot and Tim were tired. They sat under the big tree and smiled. Spot learned that it is better to play with friends than to try to do things alone. The moral of the story is: friends make everything more fun.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their toy cars. They liked to zoom them on the grass and make loud noises. But then, Lily's car hit a rock and broke. The wheel came off and the car could not move. "Oh no!" Lily cried. "My car is broken! I can't play anymore!" Ben saw that Lily was sad and wanted to help. He picked up the wheel and the car and said, "Don't worry, Lily. I can fix your car. I can restore it and make it work again." "Really?" Lily asked. "How can you do that?" Ben smiled and said, "I have some glue in my backpack. I can glue the wheel back on the car. Then it will be good as new. Come on, let's go to the bench and do it." Lily and Ben went to the bench and Ben took out his glue. He carefully glued the wheel on the car and waited for it to dry. Then he gave the car back to Lily and said, "Here you go, Lily. Your car is restored. Try it and see." Lily took the car and zoomed it on the grass. It worked perfectly. She was so happy that she hugged Ben and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are the best friend ever. You restored my car and made me happy." Ben hugged her back and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm glad you're happy. You are my best friend too. And you know what? I have a treat for you. I have some cookies in my backpack. Do you want some?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, please. I love cookies. You are so jolly and nice." Ben and Lily shared the cookies and ate them with joy. They were jolly and happy. They played with their cars until it was time to go home. They had a wonderful day in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a toy named Bobo. Bobo was a funny toy with a big switch on his back. When Mia pressed the switch, Bobo would say something. Mia loved to play with Bobo every day. One day, Mia saw an unknown toy in her room. It was shiny and new. Mia wanted to play with the new toy, but she was scared. She did not know what the unknown toy would do. Mia looked at Bobo and said, "Bobo, I am scared. What should I do?" Bobo said, "It is okay to be scared, but we can learn about new things together." Mia and Bobo went to the unknown toy. They looked at it and found a switch, just like Bobo's. Mia pressed the switch and the unknown toy said, "Hello, I am Lulu. I am here to be your friend." Mia smiled and said, "Hello, Lulu. I am Mia, and this is Bobo." From that day on, Mia, Bobo, and Lulu played together and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is that it is okay to be scared of new things, but we can learn and make new friends when we face our fears.

Lila and Tom were playing with their crayons. They liked to draw many things with different colors. Lila drew a big sun with yellow and orange. Tom drew a green frog with spots. "Look at my frog!" Tom said. "He is very clever. He can jump high and catch flies." "Wow, your frog is nice!" Lila said. "But my sun is brighter. It can make everything warm and happy." They showed each other their drawings and smiled. They were proud of their work. But then they heard their mom calling them. "Lila, Tom, it's time to return the crayons and clean up. We have to go to the park soon." Lila and Tom did not want to stop drawing. They wanted to make more pictures with their colors. They looked at each other and had an idea. "Mom, can we take the crayons to the park?" Lila asked. "We can draw on the paper there." "Please, mom, please!" Tom begged. "We will be careful and return them later." Mom thought for a moment. She saw how much they loved their crayons and how creative they were. She smiled and nodded. "Okay, you can take the crayons to the park. But you have to promise to return them when we come back home. And don't lose them or make a mess." "Yay, thank you, mom!" Lila and Tom said. They hugged their mom and put their crayons and paper in a bag. They were happy and excited. They ran to the car and buckled up. They went to the park and had fun drawing with their colors. They made more suns and frogs and other things. They were clever and careful. They did not lose or break any crayons. They returned them to their mom when they came back home. Mom was proud of them and gave them a kiss. They put their drawings on the fridge and admired them. They had a good day.
