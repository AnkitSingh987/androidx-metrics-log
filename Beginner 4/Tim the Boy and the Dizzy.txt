Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dizzy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, he felt very dizzy. His mom saw him and said, "Oh no, we need to go to the hospital!" At the hospital, the nice doctor looked at Tim. The doctor said, "You are dizzy because you played too much. You need to rest." Tim's mom said, "I will not allow you to play too much anymore." Tim went home and took a long nap. When he woke up, he did not feel dizzy anymore. Tim promised his mom that he would not play too much again. From then on, Tim played just enough and was always happy.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big bush with pretty red flowers. Tim was glad because he loved flowers. He picked one flower from the bush to take home. As Tim walked, he met a small bird. The bird was sad because it had lost its nest. Tim wanted to help the bird. He said, "Do you want my flower? You can use it to make a new nest." The bird looked at the flower and said, "Yes, I accept your flower. Thank you, Tim!" The bird took the flower and flew away. Tim felt happy that he could help his new friend. Tim went back to the bush to pick another flower for his home. As he picked the flower, he saw the bird again. The bird had made a new nest with the flower. Tim and the bird were both very glad. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Bob. Bob loved to paint, but his paintings were boring. He wanted to make them fun and pretty. One day, a little girl named Sally saw Bob painting. She said, "Hi, Bob! Can I help you paint?" Bob thought about it and then said, "Yes, I will allow you to help me paint." Sally was very happy. She took a brush and started to paint. She used many colors like red, blue, and yellow. The painting became very pretty and not boring at all. Bob and Sally smiled and had a lot of fun painting together.

One day, a brave cat named Tom saw a big sack. He was very curious to know what was inside the sack. He called his friend, a little mouse named Tim. "Tim, come look at this sack!" said Tom. Tim came running and they both looked at the sack. They tried to open it, but it was very heavy. They knew that they had to work together to open it. They tried and tried, and finally, the sack opened. Inside the sack, they found many colorful balls. They were so happy and started to play with the balls. But then, something unexpected happened. The balls began to bounce on their own! Tom and Tim were surprised but they kept playing with the bouncing balls, laughing and having fun. And from that day on, they became the best of friends.

Tom was sad. He had a bad dream. He saw a big monster with sharp teeth and red eyes. The monster chased him and wanted to eat him. Tom woke up and cried. Mom heard Tom cry. She ran to his room and turned on the light. She saw Tom on his bed, shaking and sobbing. She hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tom. It was just a dream. The monster is not real. You are safe with me." Tom hugged Mom back and said, "But it was so scary, Mom. I don't want to see the monster again. Can you stay with me?" Mom nodded and said, "Of course, Tom. I will stay with you until you fall asleep. Do you want some water?" Tom nodded and said, "Yes, please, Mom." Mom took Tom to the bathroom and turned on the faucet. She filled a cup with water and gave it to Tom. Tom drank the water and felt better. He said, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best." Mom smiled and said, "You are welcome, Tom. You are my mighty boy. You are brave and strong. You can face any dream. I love you." Mom took Tom back to his room and tucked him in. She kissed him and said, "Good night, Tom. Sweet dreams." Tom smiled and said, "Good night, Mom. I love you too." Mom turned off the light and stayed with Tom until he fell asleep. She comforted him and sang him a lullaby. She was proud of her mighty boy.
