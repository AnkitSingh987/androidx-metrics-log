Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Loyal Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved his family very much. He had a pretty collar that his family gave him. The collar was blue and had his name on it. One day, Spot's family went to the park. They allowed Spot to come with them. He was very happy. Spot played with his family and had so much fun. He ran, jumped, and wagged his tail. As they were playing, Spot's collar fell off. He didn't know what to do. But because he was a loyal dog, he stayed close to his family. They saw the collar on the ground and put it back on Spot. Spot was so happy to have his collar back, and his family was happy to have their loyal dog. They all went home and had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a land far to the east, there was a long road. On this road lived a little boy named Tim and his dog, Max. They loved to play and have fun together all day. But Tim had a big sister named Sue, who did not like to play with them. She would always say, "I hate playing with you and Max!" One day, Tim and Max found a big box on the long road. They were very excited and ran to tell Sue about it. Sue, thinking that the box might have something fun for her, decided to join them. They all went back to the box, and Sue helped Tim and Max open it. Inside the box was a big, beautiful kite. They all looked at it and knew it would be so much fun to fly. So, they took the kite to a big hill and started to play with it. As they played, Sue realized that she actually loved playing with Tim and Max. From that day on, Sue never said she hated playing with them again. And the three of them had many more fun days together, flying their kite on the long road to the east.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a new toy for Lily. In the store, Lily saw a beautiful, graceful toy bird. It had pretty wings and could sing. Lily looked at the bird and knew she wanted it. Her mom said, "Okay, we can buy the bird, but you must take good care of it." Lily was very happy and could not wait to play with her new toy. At home, Lily played with her new bird all day. She loved how it could sing and fly. But Lily forgot to put the bird away when she went to bed. That night, there was a big storm. The wind blew the window open, and the bird flew away. Lily was very sad when she woke up and saw the bird was gone. Lily looked for her bird everywhere. She asked her friends if they had seen it. One day, she saw the bird in a tree. It was singing a beautiful song. Lily knew she had to be careful to catch the bird. She climbed the tree slowly and reached for the bird. Just as she was about to grab it, the bird started to fly away. But Lily was fast, and she caught the bird. She was so happy to have her bird back. From that day on, Lily always made sure to take good care of her toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his ball on the porch. He loved to bounce the ball up and down. One day, Tim saw a big bug on the porch. He was scared of the bug and thought it was stupid. Tim wanted the bug to go away. He tried to slap the bug with his hand. But the bug was too fast. It moved away before Tim could slap it. Tim was sad and mad. He did not want the bug on his porch. Tim had an idea. He used his ball to slap the bug. He threw the ball at the bug and it worked! The bug flew away, and Tim was happy. He could play on the porch again without the stupid bug.
