Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Mechanic and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tom. Tom liked to fix cars and make them run well. One day, Tom was worried because he had to perform in a big show. In the show, he had to fix a car in front of many people. Tom practiced fixing cars every day. He wanted to do a good job at the show. His friends and family told him not to be worried. They said, "You are a great mechanic. You can do it!" The day of the show came. Tom was still worried, but he knew he had to try his best. He went on stage and started to fix the car. Everyone watched him work. When he was done, the car worked perfectly! The people clapped and cheered for Tom. He was no longer worried and was very happy.

Mike was playing in the park. He was holding a toy drum. Suddenly, he heard a voice. It was Grandma. She said, "Mike, tie the drum to the tree so it won't get away." Mike looked for a strong string. He wanted something reliable so he could tie the drum and it wouldn't come apart. He found some in the grass, so he grabbed it and carefully tied it to the tree. Grandma looked pleased. She said, "Good job, Mike. That's a very reliable knot you tied. Now you can play with the drum safely." Mike said, "Yay! I'm so happy that my drum is safe!"

Lily and Tom were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to make up stories with their dolls and cars. Lily had a pink doll that she called Anna, and Tom had a blue car that he called Max. "Anna and Max are friends," Lily said. "They go to the park and have fun." "No, they are not friends," Tom said. "They are enemies. Max is faster and stronger than Anna. He can knock her down and make her cry." Tom zoomed his car towards Lily's doll and hit her hard. Lily's doll fell on the floor. Lily was angry. She picked up her doll and hugged her. "Stop it, Tom!" Lily shouted. "You are mean. Anna is nice. She does not like to fight. She likes to share and help." "I don't care," Tom said. "I like to fight. It is fun. Max is the best. He can do whatever he wants." Tom zoomed his car again and hit Lily's doll again. Lily was very angry. She threw her doll at Tom's car and made them both crash. Tom was angry too. He grabbed his car and threw it at Lily. Lily dodged and ran away. "Mommy, mommy!" Lily cried. "Tom is hurting me and Anna. He is bad. He does not trust me." Lily ran upstairs to find her mommy. She hoped her mommy would make Tom stop and say sorry. She loved her mommy. She trusted her mommy. Her mommy always made her feel safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved cocoa. He would drink it every day. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, are you ready for some cocoa?" Tim was very happy and said, "Yes, Mom! I am ready!" Mom made the cocoa and gave it to Tim. He took a big sip, but he did not recognize the taste. It was not his favorite cocoa. Tim looked at his mom and said, "Mom, this is not my cocoa." Mom smiled and said, "I know, Tim. I made a new kind of cocoa for you to try." Just then, a little bird flew into their house. The bird said, "Hello, Tim! I heard you have new cocoa. Can I try it too?" Tim and his mom were very surprised. They did not know birds could talk! Tim shared his cocoa with the bird, and they all became good friends. From that day on, they all enjoyed different kinds of cocoa together.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a purple toy in her room. It was a small, boring toy with nothing special about it. Mia didn't know where it came from or why it was there. She showed it to her mom and asked, "Mom, can I attach this purple toy to my bag?" Her mom said, "Sure, Mia. You can do that." So Mia attached the purple toy to her bag and took it with her everywhere she went. One day, while Mia was playing at the park, something unexpected happened. The purple toy started to move! It wasn't a boring toy after all. Mia was very surprised and happy. From that day on, Mia and the purple toy had lots of fun together, and they became the best of friends.
