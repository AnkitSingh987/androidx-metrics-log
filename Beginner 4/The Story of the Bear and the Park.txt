Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Park

Once upon a time, a little alligator was upset. He was sad because he could not carry his big toy. He wanted to play with his toy in the park. So, he asked his friend, a strong bear, to help him. The bear was happy to help. He carried the big toy for the alligator. They walked to the park together. The alligator was not upset anymore. He was very happy to play with his toy and his friend. But then, it started to rain. The alligator and the bear got wet. They were cold and upset. They tried to carry the toy back home, but it was too heavy. The toy got wet and broke. The alligator and the bear were very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a mole. The mole lived in a hole. The hole was in a big yard. The mole had many friends in the yard. They played all day. One day, a bird told the mole about a magic tree. The bird said, "If you believe, the tree will give you a gift." The mole wanted to see the tree. The mole went to the tree with the bird. They both believed. The tree gave the mole a gift. The gift was a shiny rock. The mole was very happy. Some of the mole's friends were jealous. They wanted a shiny rock too. But the mole shared the shiny rock with everyone. They all played with the shiny rock and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, he felt very miserable. He did not want to play with his toys. He did not want to eat his food. He did not want to go outside. So, he sat in his room and looked around. Then, Tim saw a book on the floor. He picked it up and started to read. The book was about a big, scary monster. The monster made everyone scared. Tim read the book and felt even more miserable. Tim's mom came into the room and said, "What's wrong, Tim? Why are you so sad?" Tim showed her the book and said, "This book is scary. I don't like it." His mom took the book away, but Tim still felt sad. He went to bed that night, still feeling miserable.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a small red heart on the ground. She was confused. She did not know what it was. She picked it up and looked at it. Sue saw her friend, Tim, playing with a toy car. She walked to Tim and asked, "Tim, do you know what this is? Please tell me." Tim looked at the red heart and said, "I don't know, Sue. Let's ask our teacher." They went to their teacher, Miss Brown, and showed her the red heart. Miss Brown smiled and said, "This is a heart, kids. It means love and care. You should give it to someone you love." Sue thought about it and decided to give the heart to her mom. When she gave the heart to her mom, her mom was so happy and gave her a big hug. The little red heart made everyone feel loved and happy.

One day, a helpful cat named Tom saw a big box. The box was very heavy. Tom wanted to know how heavy it was. He thought, "I need to weigh this box." Tom asked his friend, a strong figure named Max, to help him weigh the box. Max was very big and could lift heavy things. Tom and Max tried to lift the box together. They lifted and lifted, but the box was still too heavy. Finally, Tom had a good idea. He found a big stick and put it under the box. Then, Tom and Max pushed the stick and the box went up. They weighed the box and it was very heavy. Tom and Max were happy that they worked together. The helpful cat and the strong figure were a great team.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a naughty boy named Tim. He loved to play tricks on people. One day, he went to the market with his mommy. At the market, Tim saw a man selling big red apples. He thought it would be fun to play a trick on the man. So, he sneaked behind the man and shut the apple box very loudly. The man jumped and said, "Who did that?" Tim laughed and said, "It was me!" The man looked at Tim and said, "You are a naughty boy. Please don't do that again." Tim's mommy heard what happened and took him home. At home, Tim's mommy told him that being naughty is not nice. She said, "You should be kind to others and not play tricks." Tim thought about it and decided to be a good boy. The next day, he went back to the market and said sorry to the man. From that day on, Tim was a good boy and made many friends in the town.
