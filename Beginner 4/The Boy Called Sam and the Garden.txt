Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy Called Sam and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Sam. He was always so happy and loved to explore with his family. One day he was playing in the garden, when his mummy and daddy asked if he wanted to go on a trip. Sam was so excited and said yes! They packed their bags and headed off in the car. Sam was having so much fun, watching all the different things they passed by. There were fields and cows and trees. His mummy and daddy said their trip would take them somewhere special. Once they arrived, Sam couldn't believe his eyes. He was at a lovely beach! He spent the whole day running around and collecting seashells. He loved watching the waves crashing against the shore and feeling the sand between his toes. Later on, mummy and daddy showed Sam a beautiful sunset. All the different colours lit up the sky in a magical way. As dusk spread, they shared a picnic beside the sea and watched the stars appear. Sam couldn't wait to tell all his friends how wonderful his trip had been.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a rare toy in her drawer. It was her favorite toy. One day, she could not find it. She was very sad. Sue asked her friend, Tom, "Do you understand where my toy is?" Tom said, "No, I do not understand." They looked and looked for the toy, but they could not find it. Then, a big wind came and blew the drawer away. Sue and Tom were very scared. They tried to hold the drawer, but it was too strong. The wind took the drawer far away. Sue and Tom cried, and they never saw the rare toy again.

One day, a little fish named Fin went for a swim. He swam in the big water. He saw a shark. The shark was big and had many teeth. The shark swam up to Fin. Fin was scared. "Hello, little fish," said the shark. "I am not mean. I want to be your friend. Let's play a game." Fin thought for a moment. "Okay, we can play," said Fin. "Let's see who can swim through the tight rocks first." The shark and Fin swam very fast. They went through the tight rocks. It was hard for the shark. The shark got stuck in the rocks. Fin swam back to help the shark. "Thank you, little fish," said the shark. "You are a good friend." From that day, Fin and the shark were best friends. They played together in the big water and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Mia saw a shadow on her wall. It was big and looked like a monster. Mia was scared and called her mom. "Mom, there is a big shadow on my wall!" she said. Her mom came and looked at the shadow. "Don't worry, Mia. It's just your toy bear. Let's change its place," her mom said. Mia's mom moved the toy bear and the shadow changed. It was not scary anymore. Mia was happy and thanked her mom. But then, Mia saw her spoiled apple on the floor. It was all brown and yucky. Mia knew she shouldn't eat it. The next day, Mia saw another shadow on her wall. This time, she was not scared. She remembered what her mom said and changed the place of her toy. The shadow was not scary anymore. Mia learned that shadows can change and not be scary. She also learned not to eat spoiled food like the brown apple.

It was a dark night, and Jack was walking in the woods. He was looking for his favorite animal, the fox. He had his flashlight ready as he walked further and further into the wilderness. Suddenly, Jack heard a voice. “Hey kid, what are you doing out here?” It was an old man with a big bald head. Jack was a bit scared so he didn’t answer. “Come on, don’t be scared. I won’t hurt you. But if you don’t answer me, I’ll have to take your flashlight.” Jack felt scared but he bravely asked, “Please, don’t take my flashlight. I need it to find my fox.” The old man looked surprised and said, “I didn’t know you loved the fox. Well, if you promise to take extra good care of the fox, I won’t take your flashlight.” Jack smiled and agreed. The old man smiled and patted Jack on the head and said, “Go on and be careful.” Jack thanked the old man and went off looking for his fox. He knew that the old man had understood his love for the fox and had given him a chance. As Jack walked through the woods, he shined his flashlight and looked for his fox.
