Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Book

Lily liked to go to the park with her mom. She liked to play on the swings, slide down the slide, and run on the grass. But her favorite thing was to look at the big statue in the middle of the park. It was a statue of a man with a hat and a coat, holding a book. Lily was curious about the statue. She wanted to know who he was and what he was doing. One day, Lily asked her mom if she could go closer to the statue. Her mom said yes, but she had to be careful and not touch it. Lily nodded and ran to the statue. She looked at his face, his hat, his coat, and his book. She wondered what he was reading. She wished she could read too. She leaned forward to see the words on the book. But as she did, she lost her balance and fell. She grabbed the book to stop herself, but it was too heavy. The book came off the statue's hand and landed on the ground with a loud thud. Lily was scared. She had broken the statue. She started to cry. Her mom heard the noise and ran to her. She saw the book on the ground and the statue without a hand. She was shocked. She hugged Lily and told her it was okay. She said they had to tell someone what happened. She said they needed to say sorry and fix the statue. Lily nodded and wiped her tears. She felt bad for breaking the statue. She hoped he was not angry with her. She hoped they could make him whole again. She learned a lesson that day. She learned to be careful and not touch things that were not hers. She learned to respect the statue and his book. She learned to be curious, but not too curious.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird wanted to feel what it would be like to soar. So one day, the bird decided to take its first flight. The bird was so excited but also a little nervous. It wanted to be brave. The bird's friend, a big, grey bird said, “Don’t be serious and have some fun! Just flap your wings and soar!” The little bird listened to its friend and was ready to try! It flapped its wings and soared up, up, and away! It felt so free, like it could stay in the air forever! The bird's friend said, “That was so brave and amazing! You should now come down and take a break!” Since the little bird was so happy and proud, it decided to listen and rest. After a while, it was ready to soar up again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big red box. It was full of yummy chocolate. A boy named Tim and a girl named Lily wanted to eat the chocolate. They had a fun game to play. They would sit in a circle and pass the red box of chocolate to each other. One day, they were passing the red box to each other. They were laughing and having fun. Suddenly, the red box fell on the floor. The chocolate was gone! Tim and Lily looked around, but they could not find it. Then, they saw a little puppy. The puppy had the chocolate in its mouth! Tim and Lily were surprised. The puppy wagged its tail and gave the chocolate back to them. They laughed and played with the puppy. Now, they had a new friend to pass the red box of chocolate with. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a fake phone in her toy box. She liked to play with it a lot. She would pretend to talk to her friends and family. One day, while playing with her fake phone, she heard a voice from it. "Hello, Sue! I am your new friend, Mr. Phone," it said. Sue was very surprised but happy to have a new friend. Mr. Phone told her to follow him to a big tree in the park. Sue did as she was told and followed the voice from the fake phone. When they got to the tree, Mr. Phone said, "Now, give me all your toys and I will give you a big surprise." Sue thought about it and then said, "No, Mr. Phone. That is not nice. I will not give you my toys." Then, the fake phone stopped talking and fell on the ground. Sue was sad that her new friend was not a good friend. In the end, Sue learned that not everyone you meet is a good friend. She knew that she had to be careful with who she trusts. She went back home and played with her real friends and her toys, knowing that she made the right choice.
