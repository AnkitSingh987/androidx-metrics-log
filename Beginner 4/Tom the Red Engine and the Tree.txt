Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Red Engine and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big red engine named Tom. Tom was very fit and strong. He loved to help people and teach them new things. One day, Tom met a little blue engine named Tim. Tim was not as fit as Tom and wanted to learn how to be strong. Tom said to Tim, "I can teach you how to be fit and strong like me." Tim was very happy and said, "Yes, please teach me!" So, Tom started teaching Tim how to be a fit engine. They practiced every day, and Tim was getting better and better. One day, while they were practicing, a big tree fell on the train tracks. Tom knew this was the perfect time for Tim to show what he had learned. Tom said, "Tim, you can do it. You are now fit and strong." Tim pushed the tree off the tracks with all his strength. Everyone cheered for Tim, and he was very proud. Tom was happy that he could teach Tim to be a fit and strong engine. And they continued to help and teach others together.

One day, Jack and his mom were playing outside in the backyard. Jack was chasing his shadow across the yard. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise and his shadow quickly disappeared. Jack shouted to his mom, "Where did my shadow go?" Mom replied, "It looks like it's gone. Shadows can be dangerous sometimes." Jack became scared. He didn't like the thought of shadow being dangerous. He started to look around the yard for it. He pushed his hands through the tall grass, but didn't find it. He scanned the trees, but the shadow was nowhere to be seen. Jack started to cry. "Mommy, I want my shadow back!" He shouted. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Jack. Shadows come and go. I'm sure it'll come back when it's ready." Jack looked around one last time and then decided to go inside. With a little bit of hope, he waited for his shadow to come back.

One day, a cat named True saw a big, red ball. True wanted to play with the ball, so she tried to catch it. But the ball was tough to hold, and it would slip from her paws every time. Then, a dog named Max came by. Max saw True and asked, "What are you doing?" True said, "I want to play with this ball, but it keeps slipping." Max said, "Let's play together. I will help you catch the ball." True and Max played together, and they both held the ball. They did not let it slip. They had a lot of fun playing with the ball. True learned that when you work with friends, tough things can be easy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and eat yummy snacks. One day, he went to the store with his mom to buy some food. While they were at the store, Tim saw something on the shelf. It was a bottle with a terrible-looking picture on it. Tim asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom said, "That is poison, Tim. We do not touch poison. It can make us very sick." Tim listened to his mom and did not touch the poison. They bought their food and went home. Tim was happy he listened to his mom and stayed safe. They had a good day together, and Tim learned a very important lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round face on a wall. The face was very sad. It wanted to be pretty like the other faces on the wall. One day, a little girl came to the wall. She saw the big, round face and said, "Why are you sad, big face?" The face said, "I want to be pretty like the other faces. Can you help me?" The little girl said, "Yes, I can help you. I will decorate you." The little girl found some paint and started to decorate the big, round face. She painted a big, red nose, two big, blue eyes, and a big, green mouth. But the face still looked sad. The little girl said, "I tried to make you pretty, but you still look sad." The big, round face said, "Thank you for trying, but I am still not pretty like the other faces." The little girl left, and the big, round face stayed sad on the wall.
