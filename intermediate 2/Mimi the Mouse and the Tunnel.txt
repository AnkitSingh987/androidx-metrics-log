Category: Intermediate
Title: Mimi the Mouse and the Tunnel

One day, a little mouse named Mimi wanted to find some cheese. She knew that there was a big house with many people and food, but it was very far and dangerous. She decided to go through the underground, where there were many tunnels and holes. She hoped to find a safe way to the house. As she was walking, she saw a big rat blocking her path. The rat was rude and mean. He said, "This is my tunnel. You cannot pass. Go away, little mouse, or I will bite you." Mimi was scared, but she was also brave. She said, "Please, Mr. Rat, let me pass. I only want to find some cheese. I am hungry and I will not bother you." The rat laughed and said, "Cheese? You want cheese? I have some cheese, but you cannot have it. It is mine, all mine. If you want cheese, you have to serve me. You have to do what I say. You have to be my slave." Mimi did not like that. She did not want to serve the rat. She wanted to be free and happy. She said, "No, Mr. Rat, I will not serve you. You are rude and greedy. You do not share and you do not care. I will find another way to the house. Goodbye, Mr. Rat." The rat was angry and tried to catch Mimi, but she was fast and smart. She ran away and found another tunnel. She followed it and soon she saw a light. She had reached the house. She smelled the cheese and saw a big piece on a table. She climbed up and took a bite. It was delicious and she was happy. She had found some cheese and she had not served the rat. She was proud of herself and she thanked the underground for helping her.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a battery on the floor. He thought it was very important. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sue. He ran to her house and knocked on the door. "Look, Sue! I found an important battery!" Tim said, showing her the battery. Sue's eyes grew big. "Wow, Tim! That's so cool!" she said. They played with the battery, but then, something unexpected happened. The battery started to wiggle and jump! The battery was trying to escape! Tim and Sue tried to catch it, but it was too fast. They chased the battery all around the house, laughing and having fun. Finally, they caught the battery and put it back in its place. Tim and Sue learned that sometimes, even important things can be silly and fun. They promised to always help each other when something unexpected happens, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a big stick in his yard. He loved to play with it. He would pretend it was a horse and ride it around the yard. He would say, "Giddy up, horsey!" as he rode. One day, Tim's friends came to play. They saw Tim riding the stick and laughed. Tim felt embarrassed. He didn't want his friends to laugh at him. So, he put the stick down and played with his friends instead. Later, Tim's friends saw the stick and wanted to try riding it too. They took turns pretending it was a horse. They all had so much fun! Tim felt happy and not embarrassed anymore. They all played together, riding the stick horse and laughing.

Tom and Lily are sister and brother. They like to play in the garden. One day, they find a big red apple on a tree. They want to eat it. It looks tasty. Tom tries to reach the apple, but he is too small. Lily is bigger. She climbs on a rock and grabs the apple. She is happy. She says, "I got it! I got it!" But the apple is not alone. There is a bee on it. The bee is angry. It does not want to share. It stings Lily on her hand. Lily screams. It hurts. The bee is sharp. Tom hears Lily. He runs to her. He sees the bee and the apple. He is scared. He says, "Oh no! Oh no!" He hugs Lily. He says, "I'm sorry. I'm sorry." He throws the apple away. He does not want it anymore. He wants Lily to feel better. Lily cries. She says, "Ow! Ow!" She shows Tom her hand. It is red and swollen. Tom says, "Let's go to mom. She will help you. She will make it better." They hold hands and run to the house. They leave the apple and the bee behind. They do not care about them. They only care about each other. They are sister and brother. They love each other.
