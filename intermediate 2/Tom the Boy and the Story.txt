Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to hear stories. Every night, his mom would tell him a story before bed. Tom loved the stories his mom told him. One day, Tom felt worried. He thought that there were no more stories for his mom to tell. He did not want to go to bed without a story. Tom asked his mom, "Are there more stories?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tom. There are many more stories. I will tell you a new story tonight." Tom was happy and not worried anymore. He went to bed and listened to his mom tell a new story. Tom loved the story and fell asleep with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to read big books with many pages. She had a favorite book with a white cover. One day, while she was reading her book, a strong wind blew through her window. The wind took one page from her book and blew it far away. Amy was very sad. She wanted to restore her book and find the missing page. She put on her shoes and went outside to look for the page. She looked under the trees and behind the bushes, but she could not find the page anywhere. Just when Amy was about to give up, she saw a little bird. The bird was holding the white page in its beak! The bird flew down and gave the page back to Amy. The little girl was so happy! She thanked the bird and went back home. Amy put the page back in her book and it was restored. From that day on, Amy and the bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a white dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and run all day. One day, Spot found a big box. The box was closed. Spot wanted to see what was inside the box. He tried to open it, but it was hard. Spot did not give up. He tried and tried to open the box. Then, the box started to shake. Spot was scared, but he did not run away. Finally, the box opened. Inside the box, there was a test. The test had many toys and treats. Spot was very happy. Spot started to play with the toys and eat the treats. Then, something unexpected happened. The toys and treats came to life! Spot was surprised, but he was not scared. He played with the toys and treats, and they all became friends. They had fun together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very anxious because he wanted to win a big race at school. He practiced running every day, but he was still scared. One day, Tim met a wise old man who told him about a special law. The old man said, "If you try your best and never give up, you will always win in your heart." Tim thought about this law and decided to try his best in the race. The day of the race came, and Tim was very nervous. He remembered the old man's words and tried his best. Tim ran as fast as he could and finished the race. He did not win the race, but he was proud of himself for trying his best. Tim's mom said, "I am so proud of you for trying your best! You may not have won the race, but you won in your heart." Tim smiled and knew that the old man was right. The moral of the story is to always try your best and never give up, because winning is not always about coming in first place.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play together, but sometimes they fight. Today, they want to go to the gym with Mom. The gym has a big room with toys and mats and balls. Tom and Lily love to jump and run and roll in the gym. But there is a problem. Tom wants to play with the red ball, and Lily wants to play with the red ball too. They both grab the ball and pull. They shout and cry. "Mine! Mine!" Tom says. "No, mine! Mine!" Lily says. Mom hears them and comes to the gym. She sees Tom and Lily fighting over the ball. She is not happy. "Tom, Lily, stop that!" Mom says. "You are being selfish. You have to share the ball. There are other toys you can play with. And there are other children who want to play too." But Tom and Lily do not listen. They insist on having the red ball. They do not want to share. They do not care about the other toys or the other children. Mom shakes her head. She takes the red ball away from Tom and Lily. She gives it to a little boy who is waiting for a turn. The little boy smiles and says thank you. He runs to the mat and bounces the ball. Tom and Lily are very angry. They pout and cross their arms. They do not want to play with anything else. They do not want to say sorry. They do not have fun in the gym. Mom hopes that Tom and Lily will learn to be more kind and generous. She hopes that they will make some friends in the gym. She hopes that they will have fun next time.
