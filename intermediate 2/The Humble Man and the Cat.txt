Category: Intermediate
Title: The Humble Man and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a humble man. He lived in a small house. He had a big heart. He loved to help others. One day, the humble man saw a cat stuck in a tree. He knew he had to help. He began to rush to the tree. He ran very fast. When he got to the tree, he climbed up. He saved the cat. The cat was happy. The humble man felt good. Everyone loved the humble man.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to bake with his mom. They baked cakes, cookies, and pies. Tim's favorite thing to bake was a big cake with lots of colors. One day, Tim and his mom baked a cake with red, blue, and green. They mixed the colors together and it looked very nice. Tim was so happy to see the colors on the cake. He could not wait to eat it. Before they could eat the cake, a fierce dog came into their house. The dog was big and loud. Tim and his mom were scared. But then, the dog saw the colorful cake and wanted to eat it too. Tim and his mom gave the dog a piece of cake, and the dog became their friend. They all ate the cake together and had a fun day.

One day, a girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As she tried to climb, she had a little stumble. But she got up and tried again. She made it to the top of the tree and saw something shiny. Lucy reached out and found an invitation. It was an invitation to a party. She was very happy and climbed down the tree. She ran to her friend Tom's house to tell him about the party. "Tom, I found an invitation to a party in the tree!" Lucy said. Tom was excited too. They both got ready for the party. The sky was clear and the sun was shining. They went to the party and had a lot of fun with their friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a tiny toy that he loved very much. It was a small gold car. He played with it every day. One day, Tom wanted to play with his gold car outside. But his mom did not permit him. She said, "No, Tom, you cannot play with your car outside today. It is too wet and muddy." Tom was sad, but he listened to his mom. The next day, the sun was shining, and the ground was dry. Tom's mom said, "Now you can play with your gold car outside." Tom was very happy. He played with his small gold car all day, and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a slow snail named Sam. Sam was always worried about being too slow. He saw other animals run and play, but he could only move very slowly. One day, Sam met a friendly turtle named Tim. Tim told Sam not to worry about being slow. "We can be slow together," Tim said. So, Sam and Tim became best friends. Sam and Tim played together every day. They had fun, even though they were slow. They learned that being slow was not a bad thing. Sam did not worry anymore, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a spicy skeleton named Skelly. Skelly lived in a small house with his best friend, a little dog named Spot. They were very happy together. Every night, they would sit and pray before going to bed. One day, Skelly and Spot went to the park to play. They saw a big boy named Tom. Tom was sad. Skelly asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I lost my toy. It is my favorite toy." Skelly and Spot wanted to help Tom find his toy. They all looked for the toy together. They looked under the trees, near the swings, and by the slide. Finally, Spot found the toy near the sandbox. Tom was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Skelly and Spot! You are my new friends." They all played together for the rest of the day. When it was time to go home, they prayed together and said, "Thank you for our new friend."
