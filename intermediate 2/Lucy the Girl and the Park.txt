Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lucy. She had a big bag of marbles. They were all very pretty and shiny. Lucy loved to play with her marbles every day. One sunny day, Lucy went to the park with her marbles. She found a nice, dry spot to sit and play. She poured her marbles out on the ground and started to play with them. A little boy named Tim saw Lucy playing with her marbles and wanted to play too. "Can I play with you?" Tim asked. Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with me." They played with the marbles together and had a lot of fun. They shared the pretty marbles and made new friends. They played until the sun went down and it was time to go home. Lucy and Tim put all the marbles back into the big bag. They said goodbye to each other and went back to their homes. From that day on, Lucy and Tim played with the marbles together every day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very crazy dog. He loved to run and jump all day long. One day, Spot hurt his leg while playing. Spot's owner took him to a nice lady who was a patient. She helped animals feel better. Spot had to remain very still so the nice lady could look at his leg. Spot tried to be a good dog and remain still. It was hard for him because he was a crazy dog. But he knew the nice lady was helping him. After some time, Spot's leg felt better, and he could run and jump again. Spot was very happy, and he learned to be more careful when playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim went to the park to play. While at the park, he heard music. It was a band! They were playing loud and happy songs. Tim wanted to meet the band. Tim walked up to the band and said, "Hi! I like your music!" The band was happy to meet Tim too. But then, a big dog came running up. The dog knocked over the band's drums. The band was sad and the dog looked guilty. The dog said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to do that." Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's all work together to fix the drums!" So Tim, the dog, and the band worked together. They fixed the drums and the band played happy music again. Everyone was friends and they all played in the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside in the sun. One day, while Spot was playing, a stranger came by. The stranger wanted to play with Spot, but Spot did not know the stranger. Spot tried to keep away from the stranger, but the stranger was very persistent. The stranger kept following Spot, trying to play. Spot did not want to play with the stranger, so he ran to his house. Spot's mom saw what was happening and came outside. She told the stranger to go away. The stranger left, and Spot was happy. Spot learned that it was important to keep away from strangers, and he played safely from that day on.

Once upon a time, there was a mother and her little boy. The mother worked hard every day to pay for food and toys for her son. The little boy loved his mother very much and wanted to help her. One day, the little boy decided to sell his toys to help his mother pay for food. He went to the market and tried to sell his toys. But no one wanted to buy them. The little boy felt sad and went back home. The mother saw her son sad and asked him what was wrong. The little boy told her about trying to sell his toys to help her. The mother hugged her son and said she loved him, but she still had to work hard to pay for things. They both felt sad, but they knew they had each other.
