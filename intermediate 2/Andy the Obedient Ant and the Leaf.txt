Category: Intermediate
Title: Andy the Obedient Ant and the Leaf

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there lived an obedient ant named Andy. Andy always listened to his mommy and daddy. He worked hard and played with his friends. But one day, Andy met another ant named Billy. Andy and Billy did not agree on who could play on the big leaf. They started to quarrel. They yelled and pushed each other. They did not listen or share. Their mommies and daddies told them to stop, but they did not. Because they did not listen, a big wind came and blew the leaf away. Andy and Billy fell to the ground and got hurt. They cried and felt sad. The other ants learned from Andy and Billy's mistakes. They knew that quarreling is bad and sharing is good.

Once there was a lonely dog named Spot. Spot loved to dance. Every day, Spot would dance in his yard. But he wanted a friend to dance with him. One day, Spot found a gum under a tree. He thought it was a toy and played with it. Spot had an idea. He went to the park with the gum. He put the gum on the ground and started to dance around it. He hoped that someone would see him dancing and want to join him. Soon, a little girl named Lucy saw Spot dancing. She thought it was funny and wanted to dance too. Lucy ran to Spot and they danced together. Spot was not lonely anymore. The gum had helped him find a friend. Now, Spot and Lucy dance together every day, and they are both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play near the big water. One day, he saw a small yacht. The yacht was pretty and had a big sail. Tim wanted to go on the yacht and have fun. Tim asked his mom if he could go on the yacht. His mom said yes, but he had to be careful. So, Tim went on the yacht with his toy boat. He played and had a lot of fun. The wind made the yacht go fast on the big water. After a while, Tim saw that his toy boat was separate from the yacht. He was sad. But his mom came and helped him get the toy boat back. Tim was happy again. He played on the yacht until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very nervous because it was his first day at a new class. He did not know anyone there, and he was scared. But Tim's mom said, "Don't worry, you will make new friends and have fun." In the class, Tim met a girl named Lily. Lily said, "Hi, I'm Lily! Let's be friends and keep each other company." Tim was happy to have a new friend. They played together, and Tim was not nervous anymore. One day, Lily told Tim, "Let's go outside and play by the big tree." But Tim knew that they were not allowed to go there. Tim said, "No, we should not go there." Lily went alone, and she did not come back. Tim was sad and nervous again because he lost his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a slow train. It liked to paint its tracks with pretty colors. One day, it met a little boy named Tim. Tim loved trains and colors too. "Hi, slow train! Can I paint with you?" Tim asked. The slow train was happy and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's paint together!" So, Tim and the slow train painted the tracks with red, blue, and yellow. They had a fun day painting and playing. And from that day on, Tim and the slow train were best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box with many toys. But his most favorite toy was a shiny razor. He loved to pretend it was a magic wand. One day, Tim's mommy told him not to play with the razor. She said it could hurt him. But Tim did not listen. He kept playing with the razor, waving it like a magic wand. This was a deep mistake. Later, Tim tripped and fell while playing. The razor cut his hand. Tim cried and cried. He felt regret for not listening to his mommy. His mommy came, took the razor away, and hugged him. Tim learned to always listen to his mommy.
