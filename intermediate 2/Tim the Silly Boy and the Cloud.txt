Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Silly Boy and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a silly boy named Tim. Tim loved to play baseball. One day, Tim saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was dark and reminded him of rain. Tim thought, "I hope it does not rain on my baseball game." Tim went to play baseball with his friends. They all had fun and laughed a lot. The big cloud was still in the sky, but it did not rain yet. Tim's mom called him and reminded him to come home soon. Tim said bye to his friends and went home. When Tim got home, it started to rain. The rain was very big and loud. Tim was happy that he played baseball before the rain. He thought, "The big cloud reminded me that rain was coming. I am glad I played baseball with my friends before the rain came."

One day, a bird and a cat had a big fight. The bird was calm, but the cat was mad. They both wanted a pretty feather they found on the ground. The bird said, "I want the feather because it is like mine." The cat said, "No, I want the feather because it is soft and nice." They fought and fought, but no one could get the feather. Then, a little girl saw them. She said, "Why are you fighting? You can both have the feather if you share." The bird and the cat stopped fighting. They looked at each other and smiled. They both said, "Thank you, little girl." They took the feather and played together, happy and calm.

Anna was a little daughter who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite one was Lily. Lily had long hair, a pink dress and a smile on her face. Anna carried Lily everywhere she went, even to bed. One day, Anna and her mommy went to the park. Anna brought Lily with her, of course. She wanted to show Lily the swings, the slide and the flowers. Mommy said, "Be careful, Anna. Don't lose Lily. She is very special to you." Anna nodded and said, "I know, mommy. I love Lily. I will hold her tight." She ran to the swings and put Lily on her lap. She pushed the swing with her feet and felt the wind in her hair. She giggled and said, "Lily, do you like the swing? It is fun!" But then, something bad happened. Anna swung too high and Lily slipped from her lap. Lily flew in the air and landed on the grass. Anna screamed and jumped off the swing. She ran to Lily and picked her up. She was worried. She looked at Lily and saw that her dress was dirty and her hair was tangled. She also saw a tear on Lily's cheek. Anna cried and hugged Lily. She said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I'm sorry. I didn't mean to hurt you. Please don't be sad. Please don't be broken." She kissed Lily and rubbed her cheek. Mommy heard Anna's cry and came to her. She saw Anna and Lily and said, "Oh, Anna. What happened?" She took Lily from Anna and checked her. She said, "Don't worry, Anna. Lily is not broken. She is just a little dirty and messy. We can clean her and fix her. She will be fine." Anna sniffed and said, "Really, mommy? Lily will be fine?" She looked at Lily and hoped that mommy was right. Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, Anna. Lily will be fine. She is a strong doll. And she knows that you love her very much. Come on, let's go home and make Lily look pretty again." Anna nodded and said, "Okay, mommy. Thank you, mommy. I love you and Lily." She carried Lily in her arms and followed mommy to the car. She felt better and hoped that Lily did too.

John and his dad were walking in the woods. They stopped in front of a big tree, and John asked his dad, "What is this Daddy?" His dad looked at the tree, smiled and explained. "This tree is special. It has an old spirit living inside it. He's the spirit of the woods, and he's been here for a very long time." John was full of questions: "Is the spirit furry?" His dad laughed and got down on one knee so he could look John in the eye. He said, "Yes, the spirit is furry, and he lives inside the tree. He has been watching over the woods for many years, and he's been guarding it and keeping it safe." John was so excited to know that the furry spirit was looking after them. John waved at the trees and shouted, "Hi, spirit!" Then he and his dad continued on their way.
