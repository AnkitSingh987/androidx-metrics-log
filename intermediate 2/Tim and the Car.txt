Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Car

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. One day, he took his toy car to the park to play. He found a nice stand to put his car on. He was very happy. Tim pushed his car and it went speed, speed, speed! The car went very fast. It went so fast that it hit a little girl named Sue. Sue fell down and began to cry. Tim felt very sorry. He knew he should not have made his car go so fast near other kids. Tim went to Sue and said, "I am sorry, Sue. I did not mean to hurt you." Sue stopped crying and smiled. They became good friends and played together in the park. Tim learned to be more careful when playing with his toy car.

Once there lived two friends named Jack and Jane. They had always loved to visit each other and always enjoyed their cheerful visits. One day, Jack noticed something was wrong with Jane. He asked her what was the matter but she didn't say anything, only shook her head sadly. Jack was really worried so he asked her if he could come and visit her. But Jane said "No" and shook her head again. Jack asked if he could help in any way but Jane said nothing and gave him a sad look. So, Jack tried his best to cheer Jane up but she just stayed silent. He said goodbye sadly and started to walk away. Suddenly he noticed that Jane was in tears. He felt really bad for her and went back to her, but it was too late. Jane told him she was too sad and had decided to leave. Jack felt so sad and said goodbye again. After that, they never visited each other again.

Tommy loved going to the park. One day, he was so excited to go! He went with his Mum and Dad, who were talking and didn't pay much attention to him. But then he saw something in the grass. It was a big orange! He walked to it, and picked it up. He showed it to his parents, and said "Look! clever Tommy pick orange?" His Parents were so surprised. They said, "Yes, clever Tommy, you picked the orange!" Tommy was so proud of himself. He ran around the park, showing all his friends the orange he had found. They were all so impressed. Suddenly, he slipped and fell, and he hurt his toe. He started to cry, but his Dad quickly picked him up and gave him a hug. He said, "Don't worry Tommy, that's why you need to be careful okay? You can still be clever, just be a bit more careful next time." Tommy nodded. He was happy with the reminder. He smiled and said, "Okay, clever Tommy, be careful next time."

One day, a big, hairy dog went to the store. He had a long shadow. The dog wanted to buy a toy. He walked and walked in the store. Then, the dog saw a cat. The cat was funny. The cat had a short shadow. The cat wanted to play with the dog. They played in the store. But, the dog's shadow was not a shadow! It was a big, black blanket. The cat and the dog were happy. They played with the blanket and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Spot saw a big pumpkin in the park. It was so big and round. Spot wanted to play with the pumpkin. Spot tried to push the pumpkin with his nose, but it was too heavy. He called his friends to help him. His friends, a cat and a bird, came to play with the pumpkin too. They all pushed and pushed, and the pumpkin started to roll. They rolled the pumpkin all the way to their home. Spot's mom saw the pumpkin and was very happy. She said, "Good job, Spot! You receive a treat for finding this big pumpkin." Spot and his friends were so happy. They ate their treats and played with the pumpkin all day long.
