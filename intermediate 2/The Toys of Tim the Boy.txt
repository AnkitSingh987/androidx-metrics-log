Category: Intermediate
Title: The Toys of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy barrel. He loved to put all his toys in it. One day, Tim could not find his favorite toy car. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Tim felt frustrated. Tim asked his mom for help. They looked inside the barrel, under his bed, and in the closet. But they could not find the toy car. Tim started to weep. He was very sad and wanted his toy car back. Tim's mom had an idea. She asked Tim to tidy up his room. As Tim picked up his toys, he found his toy car under a pillow. Tim was so happy! He hugged his mom and put all his toys back in the barrel. From that day on, Tim always kept his toys in the barrel so he would not lose them again.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who loved to chew. Everywhere he went he was always chewing. One day he saw a model car on the shelf. He wanted to chew on it. The little boy wanted to take it down and chew. His mum said no, but the little boy didn't listen. He took the model down and started chewing it. The model car was very delicate and started to break apart. The little boy's mum was very angry. She said, "I told you not to chew the model. Now it is all broken and it is so bad". The little boy cried and was very upset. He knew he had done a bad thing and he felt so sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, his mom asked him to help her raise the big pot. Tim was very patient and tried his best to lift the pot. Inside the pot was some meat for dinner. Tim's dog, Max, saw the meat and wanted some too. Max jumped up and tried to grab the meat from the pot. Tim was surprised and let go of the pot. The meat fell out and Max ate it all up! Tim was sad because the meat was gone, but his mom just laughed. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. We have more meat in the fridge. You did a good job raising the pot, and Max was just hungry." Tim and his mom cooked the new meat together, and they all had a yummy dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy boat that he loved to play with. One day, it rained a lot and there was a big flood in Tim's backyard. Tim was sad because he could not go outside to play. He saw the flood and thought it would be fun to put his toy boat in the water. The water was harsh and moved very fast. Tim put his boat in the water and watched it go. But the boat went too fast and Tim could not catch it. He started to cry because he did not want to lose his boat. Tim's mom came outside and saw what happened. She hugged Tim and told him not to worry. They went inside and had a snack. Tim felt better knowing his mom was there to help him.

Jimmy and his mommy were walking through the park when it started to rain. His mommy opened her umbrella and watched Jimmy as he jumped in the puddles. Jimmy stopped to take a closer look at something. It was another umbrella, but it looked fake. "Mommy! What is this?", asked Jimmy. "It's an old, fake umbrella," said his mommy. Jimmy was convinced that it was magical. "Can I keep it and replace my umbrella?", he asked excitedly. His mommy smiled and said, "Sure, Jimmy. You can replace your umbrella with the fake one." Jimmy was very happy and ran around the park twirling his new umbrella. He was so excited that he didn't even mind that it was raining!

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia wore her pretty sunglasses because it was sunny outside. They had a fine time playing on the swings and slides. While playing, Mia saw a cute dog. She went to pet the dog and forgot her sunglasses on the bench. Then, Mia and her mom went back home. At home, Mia looked for her sunglasses but could not find them. She told her mom and they remembered they left them at the park. They went back to the park and found the sunglasses on the bench. Mia was happy to have her sunglasses again and they went back home, feeling fine.
