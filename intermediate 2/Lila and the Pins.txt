Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and the Pins

Lila liked to play with pins. She had a big box of pins in different colors and shapes. She put pins on her clothes, on her dolls, on her books, and on anything she could find. She thought pins made everything look pretty and fun. But Lila was ignorant. She did not know that pins could hurt. She did not know that pins could poke her skin, or make her bleed, or get stuck in her eyes. She did not know that pins could make her sick, or make her friends angry, or make her mom sad. One day, Lila found a new pin. It was shiny and round and had a star on it. She loved it so much that she wanted to put it on her nose. She thought it would make her look like a fairy. She did not listen to her mom, who told her to be careful and not to put pins on her face. She did not listen to her dad, who told her to put the pin away and play with something else. She did not listen to her brother, who told her to stop being silly and ignorant. She put the pin on her nose and smiled. She felt like a fairy. She ran to the mirror to see how she looked. But when she got there, she saw something terrible. The pin had poked her nose and made it bleed. The blood was red and wet and dripped on her shirt. She felt a sharp pain and a big fear. She screamed and cried and wished she had not put the pin on her nose. Her mom and dad and brother heard her scream and ran to her. They saw the pin and the blood and the tears. They felt sorry and angry and scared. They took the pin off her nose and cleaned the wound. They hugged her and kissed her and told her to be brave. They told her to never play with pins again. They told her to learn from her mistake and not to be ignorant. Lila nodded and sobbed and said she was sorry. She learned a hard lesson that day. She learned that pins could hurt. She learned that ignorance could hurt. She learned that sometimes, pretty things are not worth the pain.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird named Sunny. Sunny lived in a big tree with his family. They loved to fly and play together. One day, Sunny's mom told him to stay close to the tree while she went to find food. Sunny was a good bird and tried to stay close, but he saw a pretty butterfly and wanted to play. He forgot what his mom said and flew far from the tree. Suddenly, he saw a big cat! Sunny was in shock. He was scared and didn't know how to get back home. Sunny closed his eyes and remembered his mom's words to stay close. He tried to find his way back and finally saw the big tree. When he got home, his mom hugged him tight. Sunny learned that it is important to listen and stay close to his family to stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy rat named Tom. Tom lived in a small hole in a big house. He did not like to play with other rats. He always said, "I want to be alone." One day, a happy rat named Sue came to Tom's hole. Sue said, "Hi, Tom! Do you want to play with me?" Tom was grumpy and said, "No! I want to be alone." Sue was sad, but she had an idea. She found a big, yummy cheese and brought it to Tom. She said, "Look, Tom! I have cheese. Let's share and be friends." Tom saw the cheese and his eyes got big. He smiled and said, "Okay, Sue. I will share the cheese and be your friend." From that day on, Tom was not grumpy anymore. He and Sue played together, shared food, and had fun. They were the best of friends, and everyone was happy. And that is the story of Tom the grumpy rat, who learned to be happy and make friends.
