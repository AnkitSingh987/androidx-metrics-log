Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Cake

Sara and Ben are friends who like to pick berries in the forest. They have a basket full of red and blue berries. They want to sell them to buy some candy. They go to the market and see a lady who sells cakes. They ask her if she wants to buy some berries. The lady is polite and says yes. She gives them some money and takes some berries. "Thank you, lady," Sara and Ben say. "You are very nice." They see another man who sells toys. They ask him if he wants to buy some berries. The man is rude and says no. He says their berries are bad and small. He tells them to go away. "Go away, kids," the man says. "You are very annoying." Sara and Ben are sad and angry. They still have some berries left. They want to throw them at the man. But then they remember the lady who was polite and nice. They think she might want more berries. They go back to her and offer her the rest of their berries. The lady is happy and says yes. She gives them more money and a cake. "Thank you, kids," the lady says. "You are very kind." Sara and Ben are happy and proud. They have enough money to buy some candy and a toy. They also have a cake to share. They learn that being polite and kind is better than being rude and mean. They also learn that their berries are good and valuable. They decide to pick more berries next time and sell them to nice people.

Lily was a wealthy girl who lived in a big house with many toys. She liked to play with her dolls and dress them in pretty clothes. She also liked to spring on her bed and make it bounce. One day, Lily wanted to play with her new doll that had long hair and a pink dress. She took it to her bathroom, where she had a toilet and a sink. She wanted to make her doll's hair wet and shiny, so she put it under the sink and turned on the water. But the water was too strong and it pushed the doll out of Lily's hands. The doll flew across the bathroom and landed in the toilet. Lily was very scared and sad. She ran to the toilet and tried to get her doll out, but it was too deep and she could not reach it. She started to cry and call for her mom. Her mom heard her and came to the bathroom. She saw Lily crying and the doll in the toilet. She hugged Lily and said, "Don't worry, sweetie, I will get your doll out. It's just a toy, it's not hurt." She used a stick to fish the doll out of the toilet and dried it with a towel. She gave it back to Lily and said, "See, your doll is fine. She just had a little adventure. But next time, be careful with the water and the toilet, okay?" Lily nodded and said, "Okay, mom. Thank you for saving my doll. I love you and I love my doll." She hugged her mom and her doll and smiled. She was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play outside. One day, Spot saw a big hill near his home. He wanted to climb the hill and see what was on the other side. Spot started to climb the hill. It was a cold day and the wind made him shiver. As he climbed, he saw a small puddle. The water in the puddle was frozen. Spot thought, "Wow, it's so cold that the water can freeze!" When Spot reached the top of the hill, he looked down and saw a beautiful park. He was so happy to find a new place to play. Spot ran down the hill to the park and played all day. He didn't feel cold anymore because he was having so much fun. And that's how Spot found his new favorite place to play.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He was very thirsty. He saw a tap and wanted to get some water. But Tim was careless. He turned the tap too fast and water went everywhere! "Oh no!" said Tim. "I made a big mess!" Just then, a kind squirrel came up to Tim. "Don't worry," said the squirrel. "I can help you." The squirrel turned the tap slowly and supplied water to Tim. Tim was happy and drank the water. But then, something unexpected happened. The tap started to talk! "Thank you for being careful," said the tap. "I don't like it when people are careless with me." Tim and the squirrel were very surprised. They promised to always be careful with the tap. And they all became good friends.
