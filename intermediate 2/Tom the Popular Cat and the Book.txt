Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Popular Cat and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a popular cat named Tom. Tom loved to deliver things to his friends. One day, he found a pretty pattern in a book. He wanted to show his friends the pattern. Tom went to his friend Sue, a little bird. He wanted to show her the pattern. But Sue was sad. She lost her favorite toy. Tom wanted to help Sue find her toy. Together, Tom and Sue looked for the toy. They looked up, down, and all around. At last, they found the toy under a big tree. Sue was happy, and Tom showed her the pattern. They both smiled and went to play with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a small house with a kind lady. The kind lady liked to drink coffee every morning. Fluffy liked to lean on the lady's leg when she drank her coffee. One day, Fluffy saw a big, red ball in the yard. Fluffy wanted to play with the ball. The kind lady saw Fluffy and the ball. She put down her coffee and went outside to play with Fluffy. They played with the red ball all day. Fluffy was very happy. The kind lady was happy too. When they were tired, they went back inside the house. The kind lady drank her coffee, and Fluffy leaned on her leg. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to grow plants in her garden. One day, Lily found a small print of a tree in a book. She wanted to grow a tree just like the one in the print. Lily planted a seed in her garden and took care of it every day. She gave it water and made sure it had lots of sun. But one day, she saw that her tree was not growing. It looked hurt and sad. Lily was worried about her tree. Then, Lily remembered the print of the tree in her book. She looked at the print again and saw that the tree was by a big rock. Lily found a big rock and put it next to her tree. The next day, her tree started to grow! It grew big and strong, just like the tree in the print. Lily and her tree were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a nice little house, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and tie her shoes. One day, she went outside to play in the park. At the park, she met a boy named Tom. They played together and had fun. Tom said, "I like your shoes! Can you show me how to tie mine?" Sue smiled and said, "Sure! I can help you." They sat down, and Sue showed Tom how to tie his shoes. After a while, it was time for Sue and Tom to go home. They said goodbye and walked away. But as Sue walked home, she felt something was not right. There was silence all around her. She looked down and saw that her shoes were untied. Sue tried to tie them, but she could not remember how to do it. She tripped and fell, and her day ended with a bad feeling.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a great hurricane. The wind blew hard, and the rain fell heavy. Everyone was scared and stayed inside their homes. One day, a little girl named Lucy went outside to play after the hurricane. She saw a small, wet kitten shivering under a tree. Lucy felt sad for the kitten and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." She took the kitten inside her house to get warm and dry. Lucy decided to bathe the kitten to make it clean. She filled a tub with warm water and gently washed the kitten. The kitten purred happily. After the bath, Lucy wrapped the kitten in a soft towel and gave it some food. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Jane was in her room. She saw a big trophy on her shelf. She was very proud of it. Jane won the trophy from a race. But she felt dizzy that day. Jane's friend, Tom, came to play. He saw the trophy and said, "Wow! That's a big trophy! How did you win it?" Jane told him about the race and how she felt dizzy. Tom said, "You must be very strong to win even when you felt bad." Later, they had a snack. Jane gave Tom a cookie to taste. Tom loved it and said, "Yum! This is the best cookie ever!" Jane smiled and said, "Thank you. My mom made them for me." The two friends had a fun day together. They learned that even when things are hard, you can still do your best. And sharing with friends makes everything better.
