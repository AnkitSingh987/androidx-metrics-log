Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa and the Ball

Lisa liked to look at herself in the mirror. She thought she was pretty and smart. She had long brown hair and big blue eyes. She liked to wear her pink dress and her shiny shoes. She wanted to show everyone how nice she was. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. She saw a girl playing with a ball. The girl had short blond hair and green eyes. She wore a yellow shirt and jeans. Lisa thought the girl was ugly and dumb. She wanted to take her ball and make her cry. She ran to the girl and said, "Give me your ball. It belongs to me. You are ugly and dumb. You don't belong here." The girl looked scared and sad. She said, "No, please. This is my ball. My grandma gave it to me. It is special. You are mean and rude. You don't belong here." Lisa did not listen. She grabbed the ball and pushed the girl to the ground. She laughed and ran away. She looked at herself in the mirror. She thought she was happy and proud. She did not see the tears in her eyes and the dirt on her face. But then, something bad happened. A big dog saw Lisa and the ball. The dog liked balls. He wanted to play with the ball. He ran to Lisa and barked. He jumped on her and bit her. He took the ball and ran away. Lisa screamed and cried. She was hurt and scared. She looked at herself in the mirror. She thought she was ugly and dumb. She wished she was compassionate and kind. She wished she had not been mean to the girl. She wished she had a friend.

Once there was a bear and a rabbit who were best friends. They loved to play together every day. One day the bear wanted to test the rabbit's eye. The bear asked the rabbit to close his eyes tight and to try to count to ten, without opening them. The rabbit closed his eyes and started counting. The bear moved around the room and asked: "Where am I?". The rabbit guessed wrong every time! He guessed that the bear was in the kitchen, but he was wrong. Finally, the rabbit opened his eyes and saw the bear. The bear was very happy and brought the rabbit a tasty treat. It was a special cookie with pink frosting, made just for the rabbit! The rabbit loved the cookie and thanked the bear. They hugged each other and went off to play.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lisa. She was 3 years old and loved to play with her friends. One day, the sky was lit up by lots of colourful firework displays. Lisa asked her dad what was making those beautiful colours and he explained they were called fireworks. Lisa was very excited and wanted to watch the fireworks. But her dad said they were too loud and it wasn't safe to stand too close. Lisa was disappointed but agreed to watch them from a safe distance. Suddenly, Lisa heard a loud noise. It was so loud that it made her jump and the noise disturbed the peaceful night. Lisa noticed there was a very bright firework that was suddenly available for everyone to see. She was so excited and jumped up and down with glee. She was so happy to be able to watch the colourful firework. When the show was over, Lisa turned to her dad and said “That was such a nice show, can we come back next time too?” Her dad smiled and said yes. Lisa was so excited she eagerly waited for the next show.

One day, a duck went to the pond. The water was clear and he felt happy. He liked to swim and play with his friends. They liked to splash and quack all day. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. But today, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the water! The duck was scared and his friends were too. They did not know what to do. The big fish said, "Do not be scared, I just want to play too!" The duck and his friends felt better. They played with the big fish all day. They had lots of fun and made a new friend. The pond was a happy place for everyone.
