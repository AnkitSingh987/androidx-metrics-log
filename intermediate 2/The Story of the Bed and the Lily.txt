Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bed and the Lily

Once upon a time, there was a big, weird bed. This bed could talk and walk. It lived in a house with a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily asked the bed, "Why are you so weird?" The bed replied, "I am weird because I can talk and walk. But I enjoy being your friend and helping you sleep at night." Lily and the bed played all day. They enjoyed fun games like hide and seek. When it was time for bed, Lily would climb on the weird bed and they would share stories until Lily fell asleep. The weird bed was happy to be Lily's friend and they enjoyed their time together.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly girl named Lilly. Lilly lived in a big building with an elevator. She liked to ride the elevator up and down, and say "hi" to all her friends. One day, Lilly found a small bird outside. The bird was hungry. Lilly wanted to help the bird, so she decided to feed it. She took the bird in her hands and went inside the building. Lilly and the bird went on the elevator to go up to her home. On the way up, they saw many friends who smiled at the bird. When they got to Lilly's home, she fed the bird some yummy seeds. The bird was happy and full. Lilly felt good that she could help her new friend.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Thomas and Rosa. Thomas had a magical horn. All he needed to do was blow it, and he could create anything he wanted. "Thomas, can you create some candy for me?", Rosa asked. Thomas was so excited. He grabbed the horn, and blew as hard as he could. Suddenly a big pile of candy appeared in front of them. Rosa was very happy and she reached out to grab a piece but something wasn't right. The candy was very rough and tasted bitter. Rosa was a bit scared but Thomas said it was okay. He said he would blow the horn again and create some more candy. But this time, nothing happened - the horn was broken! Thomas was very sad and Rosa was disappointed. She said the bad candy had ruined their fun, and the two friends sadly went home.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the yard with their toys. One day, Lily got a new toy. It was a big doll with a pink dress and long hair. Lily was very happy with her new toy. Tom saw Lily's new toy and he felt envious. He wanted a big doll too. He asked Lily if he could play with her doll, but Lily said no. She said it was her doll and she did not want to share. Tom was angry and sad. He thought Lily was not a good friend. Tom decided to take Lily's doll when she was not looking. He waited until Lily went inside the house to get some water. Then he ran to the yard and grabbed the doll. He ran away with the doll to his house. He thought he was very clever. But Lily saw Tom take her doll. She ran after him and shouted, "Tom, give me back my doll! That is not yours, that is mine!" She was very mad and sad. She thought Tom was not a good friend. Tom did not listen to Lily. He ran inside his house and locked the door. He thought he was safe with the doll. But he was wrong. The doll was not a normal doll. It was a magic doll. And it did not like to be stolen. The doll started to move and talk. It said, "Tom, you are a bad boy. You took me from Lily. You made her cry. You are not my friend. You are my enemy. And I will make you pay." The doll opened its mouth and showed its sharp teeth. It bit Tom on the arm and made him scream. The doll did not stop. It bit him again and again. Tom was very scared and hurt. He wished he never took the doll. He wished he was a good friend to Lily. But it was too late. The doll did not stop. The end.
