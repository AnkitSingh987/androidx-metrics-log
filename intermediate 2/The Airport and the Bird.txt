Category: Intermediate
Title: The Airport and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big airport. In the airport, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a brave boy. He was going on a trip with his mom and dad. At the airport, Tim saw a big plane. He was very excited. He said, "Mom, look at the big plane! I want to go on it!" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. We will go on the plane soon. Just relax and wait." As they waited, Tim saw a bird near the airport. The bird was scared. Tim walked up to the bird and said, "Don't be scared, bird. I am brave, and you can be brave too." The bird flew away, feeling better. Finally, it was time to go on the plane. Tim held his mom's hand and said, "Mom, I am brave. Let's go on the big plane." They all went on the plane and had a fun trip. And Tim learned that being brave can help others feel brave too.

Once there was a little girl called Jane. She had her own toy car and loved playing with it very much. One day she drove the car to the nearby drain. The drain was very sour and smelly. Jane was curious and wanted to know what it was. She looked inside to take a peek, and saw lots of slimy green frogs. She laughed as she watched them hop away. Jane wanted to make the drain less sour, so she got a pail of warm and soapy water, and poured it into the drain. Then the frogs and smell went away, and the drain was nice and clean. Jane was very proud of herself for cleaning the drain and she was happy that her own special drain was no longer sour.

Once upon a time, in a lively pond, there was a little fish named Lily. Lily loved to swim and play with her friends all day long. One day, Lily saw a big rock near the pond. She thought it would be fun to jump over the rock and explore the other side. But when Lily tried to jump, she could not make it over the rock. She was sad and her friends tried to help her. They all pushed and pulled, but the rock would not move. Lily's friends told her to relax and not to worry. They said they would find a way to help her. The next day, Lily's friends found a long stick. They put the stick on the rock and made a bridge for Lily to cross. Lily was so happy and thankful to her friends. She walked over the stick bridge and finally got to the other side of the pond. Together, they all played and had a great time.

Jeff was a small boy. He was only three years old. One day he went to the mill with his dad. He was so excited because he had never been there before. When they arrived at the mill, Jeff wanted to jump. He ran around and around, bouncing and jumping as high as he could. His dad just watched and smiled. After a while, Jeff tired himself out, so they started to walk back home. On their way out of the mill, Jeff stopped and asked his dad if he could jump one more time. His dad said yes, and Jeff jumped and jumped with joy. He was so happy that he could feel the wind on his face. When they finally arrived home, Jeff snuggled in his bed and fell into a deep sleep. His day at the mill was a small but wonderful adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a fair girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the earth. She liked to dig and find bugs. One day, she found a big piece of paper on the ground. Lily picked up the paper and said, "I know! I will fold this paper into a fun toy." So, she folded the paper into a pretty butterfly. She was very happy with her toy. Lily showed her butterfly to her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Wow, that's a nice butterfly! Can you teach me to fold one too?" So, Lily taught Tom how to fold a butterfly. They played with their butterflies all day long, and they had lots of fun.
