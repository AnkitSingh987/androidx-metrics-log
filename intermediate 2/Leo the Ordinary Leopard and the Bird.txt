Category: Intermediate
Title: Leo the Ordinary Leopard and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived an ordinary leopard named Leo. Leo had many friends, like the birds, the monkeys, and the rabbits. Every day, Leo would play with his friends in the forest. One sunny day, while Leo was resting under a tree, a little bird came flying to him. The bird said, "Leo, come! I found something fun for us to play with!" Leo jumped up and followed his friend. They came to a big, open space where all the animals were gathered. In the middle, there was a big, bouncy ball. Leo and his friends had so much fun playing with the ball, taking turns to push and chase it. They laughed and played until the sun went down. As the sky turned dark, Leo said, "Thank you, little bird, for inviting me to play today. We should do this again soon!" The little bird agreed, and all the animals went back to their homes, happy and tired from their fun day. From that day on, Leo and his friends would often come together to play with the big, bouncy ball. And they all lived happily in the big forest, sharing many more fun and exciting days.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her pet dog, Max. Lily loved to play outside with Max and go on fun adventures. One sunny day, Lily and Max found a big horn in the woods. "Wow, look at this horn, Max!" said Lily. Max barked happily. They decided to take the horn back to their house to show mom and dad. When they got home, Lily's mom was making lunch. "Mom, look what we found!" said Lily, showing her the horn. Her mom smiled and said, "That's a very special horn, Lily. You and Max are so brave for finding it." After they ate their lunch, they played with the horn all day long.

One day, a girl named Sue found a purple ball. She wondered where it came from. She asked her friend, Tom, "Do you know who lost this ball?" Tom did not know. They decided to find the owner together. Sue and Tom went to the park. They saw a club of kids playing. They asked the kids, "Did you lose a purple ball?" The kids said, "No, we didn't." Sue and Tom were sad. They wanted to find the owner. Then, something unexpected happened. A dog came running to them. The dog took the purple ball and ran away. Sue and Tom laughed. They understood that the ball belonged to the dog. They were happy they found the owner.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat radish from the garden. One day, he found a big radish. It was so big, he thought it would feed him for days. Bob started to dig and dig to get the big radish out. But, as he dug, he saw something funny. The radish started to shake and wiggle. Bob was scared, but he kept digging. Then, the big radish jumped out of the ground! The big radish was not a radish at all. It was a friendly monster who had been hiding under the ground. The monster said, "Thank you, Bob, for not trying to destroy me." Bob and the monster became friends and played together every day. And the monster helped Bob find more radish to eat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big box of chalk. The chalk was many colors. Tim loved to draw with his chalk. One day, Tim wanted to display his drawings. He found a heavy rock in his yard. Tim used the rock to hold up his drawings. He drew a sun, a tree, and a cat with his chalk. Tim's friends came to see his display. They liked his drawings. They all clapped and smiled. Tim felt happy. They all played together and drew with the chalk.

Once upon a time, there was a soldier named Tim. Tim liked to swing on the big tree in his yard. One day, while swinging, he saw a small, sour fruit on the ground. He thought to himself, "I will keep this sour fruit for later." The next day, Tim went to play with his friend, Sam. They played with toy soldiers and had a lot of fun. Tim remembered the sour fruit he found and thought it would be fun to share it with Sam. He went to his bag and took it out. Tim said, "Look what I found, Sam! Let's try this sour fruit together." Sam was excited to try the sour fruit with Tim. They both took a bite and made funny faces because it was so sour! They laughed and laughed. From that day on, Tim and Sam always looked for more sour fruits to share while they played with their toy soldiers and swung on the big tree.
