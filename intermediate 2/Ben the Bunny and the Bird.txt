Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play all day. One day, Ben saw a shiny mineral on the ground. He thought it was very pretty. Ben took the mineral and put it in his little hole for safe keeping. Ben had a friend named Sam, the squirrel. Sam saw Ben with the mineral and wanted to eat it. But Ben told Sam, "No, we can't eat it. We must keep it safe in my hole." Sam agreed and they played together all day. The next day, a big bird saw the mineral in Ben's hole. The bird wanted to take it away. Ben and Sam knew they had to keep the mineral safe. They hid the mineral in a secret place where the bird could not find it. The bird went away, and Ben and Sam were happy they had kept the mineral safe. From that day on, they knew they must always keep their special things safe from those who might want to take them away.

Once upon a time, in a dark forest, there was a lot of mist. It was hard to see. In the forest, there was a little girl named Lily. She was lost and wanted to find her way home. Lily saw a big tree and went to it. She asked the tree, "Can you help me find my way home?" The tree said, "I can't, but I know who can help you." The tree told her to look for a kind bird who lived in the forest. Lily walked and walked until she found the bird. She said, "Please, bird, can you help me find my way home?" The bird agreed to help her. The bird flew up high and looked around. It saw Lily's house and said, "Follow me!" Lily followed the bird through the dark, misty forest. At last, they reached Lily's house. Lily was so happy to be home. She thanked the bird and gave it a big hug. From that day on, Lily and the bird were the best of friends. And the dark, misty forest didn't seem so scary anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a huge tooth. It was so big that it could not fit in a mouth. One day, a nice man came to deliver it to a special place. He put the tooth in a big truck and started to drive. On the way, something unexpected happened. The huge tooth jumped out of the truck! It started to roll down a hill. The nice man ran after it, but the tooth was too fast. It rolled all the way to a big park where kids were playing. The kids saw the huge tooth and had a great idea. They decided to use it as a slide! The nice man saw the kids having fun and thought it was a good place for the tooth. So, the huge tooth stayed in the park, making kids happy every day.

One day, Peter was feeling very miserable. He had no friends to play with and he was stuck in his room all day. He looked out the window and saw some children playing. He wanted to join them, but he was too shy. Suddenly, his mother came in with a big stick and said "What can I do to make you happy, Peter? Are you feeling lonely?" Peter said, "Yes, I want to go out and play with other children, but I don't know how I will make friends." His mother smiled and said, "I have an idea. You can use this stick to draw lines in the sand. Each line could be a new friend, and the more lines, the more friends you will have! Do you want to try it?" Peter was so excited and he ran outside with the stick. He started to draw lines in the sand and soon he had a lot of friends! He started to feel less miserable, as the number of friends increased. The End.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a box. The box was big and full of many things. She saw a lot of crayons inside. Sue was very happy. She loved to be creative and draw with crayons. Sue took the crayons and began to fill a paper with pretty colors. She drew a big sun and a blue sky. She drew a house with a red roof. Sue knew she was making a special drawing. She was excited to show it to her mom and dad. When Sue was done, she ran to her mom and dad. She showed them the drawing she made with the crayons. They were so proud of her creative work. Sue's mom hung the drawing on the fridge. Sue smiled big, knowing her crayons helped her make something special.
