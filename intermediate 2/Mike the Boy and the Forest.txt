Category: Intermediate
Title: Mike the Boy and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Mike. He was three years old, and he was very brave. He went for a walk in a forest one day and he was having fun. Suddenly, he heard a loud voice from behind a tree. It was a loud, scary voice. Mike was terrified and he didn't know what to do. He stayed still and listened to the voice. The voice said, "I've got an important message for you, Mike". Mike was very frightened, but he answered. He said, "What is it?" The voice said, "You must be brave, Mike. There's a big danger coming, and you must tell everyone so they can be safe". Mike was so scared, but he knew he had to do it. He said, "I'll tell everyone, I promise". The voice said, "Good boy. Now go, before it's too late!" Mike ran back home as fast as he could and he told everyone the message. Everyone was very scared, but they all worked together to be safe. And that's the story of how Mike saved everyone with a brave message.

Sara and Tom like to jog with Mom and Dad. They jog in the park, where there are many trees and flowers. Sometimes they see birds and squirrels. Sara and Tom like to jog fast and laugh. One day, they jog to a big bridge. The bridge goes over a river. The river is blue and shiny. Sara and Tom want to cross the bridge. They ask Mom and Dad. "Can we go over the bridge?" Sara says. "Yes, we can. But be careful. The bridge is high and long." Dad says. "OK. We will hold your hands." Tom says. They jog over the bridge. They feel the wind and the sun. The wind is cool and the sun is warm. They look at the river and the sky. They see a boat and a cloud. They are happy. They jog back to the park. They drink some water and eat some snacks. They thank Mom and Dad for the fun jog. "We love to jog with you." Sara says. "Me too. Jogging is good for us." Dad says. They hug and smile. They jog home together.

One day, a little dog named Sam was playing with his toy. He was very happy. But then, he saw a big cat named Tom. Tom was not happy. He was very tired and wanted to take a nap. Sam said, "Hi, Tom! Do you want to play with me?" Tom looked at Sam and said, "No, Sam. I am very tired. I need to take a nap. Can you please be quiet?" Sam felt sorry. He did not want to make Tom sad. So, he decided to yield and let Tom take his nap. Sam went to another room and played with his toy quietly. After a while, Tom woke up from his nap. He was feeling much better. He went to Sam and said, "Thank you for being quiet, Sam. I had a good nap. Now we can play together!" Sam was very happy, and they played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was playing with her toy telephone in her room. One day, she saw a real telephone on the table. She wanted to play with it, but her mom said, "No, Lucy. That is not a toy." Later, Lucy's mom went to the store. Lucy was alone and saw the real telephone again. She was frightened, but she wanted to play with it. She picked it up and pressed the buttons. Suddenly, the telephone made a loud noise. Lucy was very scared. Her mom returned from the store and saw Lucy crying. She asked, "What happened, Lucy?" Lucy told her mom about the loud noise. Her mom hugged her and said, "That's okay, Lucy. But remember, we must listen when we are told not to touch something." Lucy learned to always listen to her mom and not to touch things that are not for playing.

Once upon a time, in a small world, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a big, round, blue ball that she loved to play with. One day, while playing with her ball, it rolled into an unknown place. Lily was very sad and didn't know what to do. She asked her friend, Tom, "Can you help me get my ball back?" Tom said, "Yes, I will help you." So, they went together to find the ball. They walked and walked until they saw a big, dark cave. They thought the ball might be inside. Lily and Tom were scared, but they wanted to get the ball back. They went into the cave and saw many strange things. They heard a loud sound and saw a big monster! The monster said, "I have your ball, but I won't give it back." Lily and Tom were very sad and ran away. They never got the ball back, and the monster sent them away from his cave.
