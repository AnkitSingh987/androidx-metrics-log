Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bald Man and the Lion

One day, a bald man named Tim went to the zoo. He saw a big lion in a cage. The lion looked sad. Tim wanted to help the lion feel better. He went to the store and brought a big ball for the lion to play with. "Here you go, Mr. Lion," said Tim, giving the ball to the lion. The lion played with the ball and felt very happy. But then, the lion saw a chance to escape. He jumped out of the cage and ran away. "Oh no!" said Tim. "I didn't want the lion to escape." The lion ran around the zoo, but he didn't want to leave. He just wanted to play with Tim and the ball. So, the lion came back to Tim. "Thank you for the ball," said the lion. "I am sorry I scared you." Tim and the lion became friends. They played with the ball every day, and the lion never tried to escape again.

Once there was a lady who was walking down a dirt path. It was a dirty path because it had not been used in a long time. The lady wanted to gain something, so she kept walking. Then, she saw a big mud puddle. It looked really dirty, so the lady stepped around it. But, it had something special in it! A shiny penny was laying in the mud. So, the lady gained the penny. She was very happy because it was a special penny. She cleaned it off and left the puddle with a smile on her face. She hadn't expected to gain something that day, but it made her very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red fire truck. The fire truck had a job to put out fires. One day, the fire truck went to a house with a crazy, big fire. The fire truck started to put out the fire with water. As the fire truck put out the fire, it saw something behind the fire. The fire truck kept putting out the fire until it could see what was behind it. The fire truck revealed a big, shiny treasure box. The fire truck was so happy to find the treasure box. It took the treasure box to the fire station to show all its friends. The fire truck and its friends opened the treasure box, and inside, they found lots of yummy ice cream! They all had a big ice cream party and had so much fun.

Jack was frustrated. He wanted to get to the bathroom, but his mom said he had to wait. He couldn't wait any longer, so Jack jumped from his chair, and ran to the bathroom. Jack smiled when he got there. He was happy he was able to get there so fast. But as soon as he opened the door, he found it was already full! His mom was in there taking a bath. Jack was frustrated again. He didn't know what he should do. He wanted to take a bath too, but he couldn't go in while his mom was in there. Suddenly he had an idea. He decided to wait outside the bathroom until his mom finished. At last the bathroom door opened, and Jack ran inside. His mom had finished her bath. Quickly, Jack got in and started to play. He was so happy to finally have his turn in the bathroom.

One day, a sweet little bird hurt its wing. It could not fly anymore. It needed help to heal. The bird looked for a shelter to rest and feel better. In the woods, the bird found a big tree. The tree said, "Hello, little bird. You can stay in my branches and heal your wing." The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, big tree! You are very kind." While the bird was healing, a big wind came. The wind blew the tree down, and the bird had to find a new shelter. But the wind also blew away the hurt from the bird's wing. The bird could fly again! The bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, wind! You helped me heal my wing." The wind said, "You're welcome, little bird!" And they all lived happily ever after.
