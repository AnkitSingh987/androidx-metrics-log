Category: Intermediate
Title: The Prince and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a prince. The prince was weak. He could not run or jump like the other kids. One day, the prince saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with the ball. The prince tried to pick up the ball, but it was too big. He tried to push it, but it was too heavy. The prince started to cry. A little girl saw him cry and came to help. She said, "Don't cry! I can help you." The girl was very strong. She picked up the ball and gave it to the prince. The prince smiled and said, "Thank you!" They played with the ball together. They had so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball tore! The air went out of the ball, and it became flat. The prince and the girl laughed. They did not need the ball to have fun. They played together all day, and the prince did not feel weak anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She had a green avocado that she loved very much. One day, she decided to play a game with her avocado. Anna went to her friend, a patient dog named Max. "Max, let's play!" said Anna. "I will bury the avocado, and you must find it." Max wagged his tail, happy to play with Anna. Anna dug a hole with her hands and put the avocado inside. She covered it with dirt and said, "Okay, Max, go find it!" Max sniffed around and soon found the avocado. Anna clapped her hands and said, "Good job, Max!" They played this game all day, and they were very happy.

Once there was a dog named Tom. Tom was an independent dog who liked to go on walks alone. One day, Tom saw a big, tall tree. He wanted to measure how tall it was. So, he tried to climb it. When Tom was up in the tree, he started to tremble. He was scared because it was very high. He tried to climb down, but he could not. Tom was stuck in the tree. No one came to help Tom. He stayed in the tree, scared and trembling. The sun went down, and it got dark. Tom was sad and alone. This was a bad day for Tom.

Once upon a time, there was a big forest in the east. In this forest, there was a hidden tree house. A little boy named Tim lived in the tree house. Tim had a toy bear called Bobo. Tim and Bobo loved to play together. One sunny day, Tim and Bobo decided to march around the forest. They marched and marched, singing a fun song. "March, march, march, we go, Bobo and Tim, we're friends, you know!" Tim said to Bobo, "I love marching with you!" As they marched, they met a friendly squirrel. The squirrel said, "Hi, Tim and Bobo! Can I march with you?" Tim and Bobo were happy to have a new friend. They all marched together, singing and laughing, until the sun went down. Then, they went back to the hidden tree house and had a big sleepover.

Lily likes to print. She has a big box of paper and many colors. She prints with her hands, with stamps, with stickers. She prints flowers, stars, hearts, and more. She prints on the paper, on the wall, on the floor. One day, Lily finds a key. It is shiny and has a funny shape. She wonders what it can open. She tries it on the door, on the drawer, on the box. But it does not fit. She thinks maybe it is a magic key. She prints a big circle on the paper and puts the key in the middle. She says a magic word and waits. Nothing happens. Lily is sad. She thinks the key is broken. She throws it away and prints something else. But then, she hears a noise. She looks at the paper and sees the circle moving. It is a hole. And from the hole, a lively mouse pops out. It has a red bow on its head and a cheese in its mouth. It winks at Lily and runs away. Lily is surprised and happy. She laughs and claps. She thinks the key is very special. She prints more circles and puts the key in each one. She says the magic word and waits. More lively mice come out. They have different colors and clothes and things. They dance and sing and play with Lily. Lily has a lot of fun. She thinks the key is the best thing she ever found.
