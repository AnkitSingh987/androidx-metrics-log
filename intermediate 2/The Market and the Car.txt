Category: Intermediate
Title: The Market and the Car

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a market. At this market, many people came to buy and sell things. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the market with his mom. They wanted to buy a rare toy that Tim saw in a shop. At the market, Tim found the rare toy. It was a shiny car. He was very happy. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have the car, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but you have to win a game first." Tim was excited to play the game. The game was to find a ball hidden in the market. Tim looked around and found the ball under a table. He won the game! Tim's mom bought him the rare car. They went home, and Tim played with his new car all day. He was very happy that he won the game and got the rare car.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a man who had to deliver a toy gun to a little boy. The man was sometimes foolish, but he loved to help people. So, he put the toy gun in a big bag and started to walk to the little boy's house. On the way, the man met a dog. The dog was very friendly and wanted to play. The foolish man thought it would be fun to play with the dog, so he took the toy gun out of the bag. He threw the toy gun, and the dog ran after it. They played for a long time, and the man forgot about delivering the toy gun to the little boy. Suddenly, the dog picked up the toy gun and ran away. The man was very sad and worried. He did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog came back with the toy gun and dropped it at the man's feet. The man was so happy! He thanked the dog and continued his journey to deliver the toy gun to the little boy. The little boy was very happy to get his toy, and the man learned not to be so foolish next time.

One day, a smelly cow named Daisy was walking in the field. She liked to step on the soft grass. The sun was shining, and Daisy was happy. Daisy saw a big red ball. She wanted to play with it. So, she walked to the ball and gave it a big push. The ball rolled away very fast. Daisy ran and ran to catch the ball. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball changed into a big red balloon! Daisy was surprised. She had never seen a balloon before. She stepped back and looked at the balloon. It was not a ball, but Daisy thought it was still fun to play with. So, Daisy and the red balloon became friends, and they played in the field all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to climb trees in his big yard. One day, he saw a tall tree with a zip at the top. Tim really wanted to see what the zip was for. Tim started to climb the tree. The bark felt a little harsh on his hands, but he kept going. He wanted to reach the zip at the top. As he climbed higher, he saw his friend, Sam, playing in the yard next door. "Hi, Sam!" Tim called out. "I found a zip in this tree! I want to see what it does!" Sam looked up and said, "Be careful, Tim! I'll watch you from here." Tim finally reached the top and grabbed the zip. He pulled it, and a big, colorful flag came out! It was like magic. Tim felt so happy and proud of his climb. He waved the flag for Sam to see, and they both laughed and played the rest of the day.

Once upon a time there was a puppy named Max. He was very obedient, and always obeyed his mummy. One day Max decided that he wanted to explore and play in the park. Mummy said, "Be careful and obedient Max! You must not fall on the ground or get lost." Max was very excited and he ran off to the park. When he got there, he saw some other puppies playing and having lots of fun. He flew from one side of the park to the other, jumping and running. Max was playing so much, he didn't realise he was too far away from his mummy. Suddenly, he stumbled and fell on the ground. He was scared, because he knew he was not supposed to do that! Mummy ran towards him, and Max was very happy to see her. She said, "You are ok Max, but you must always stay obedient and careful." Max agreed, and from that day on he followed his mummy's rules and never fell again.
