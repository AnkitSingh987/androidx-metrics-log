Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Slide and the Garden

One day, a naughty little girl found a needle in her garden. It was shiny and silver, and she wanted it. But it was too slippery for her to pick up! She was worried she'd never be able to have it. Just then, a magical fairy appeared! She said she'd help the girl get the needle. She waved her wand and the needle began to slide across the ground, towards the little girl. She was so happy that she picked it up with her tiny hands and smiled. The fairy was pleased with the girl's excitement and told her she could keep the needle. She said it would be useful if the little girl ever needed to sew something. Then the fairy disappeared in a poof of sparkles. The little girl was about to put the needle away when she decided to slide it across the grass for a little while. She made patterns in the dirt and played with it for hours! What a great find!

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Lily. She was a good girl, but sometimes she did things she should not do. One day, her mom told her not to touch the cheap vase on the table. It was easy to break and they did not have money to buy a new one. Lily wanted to listen to her mom, but she could not stop thinking about the vase. She thought it was so pretty. When her mom went to the store, Lily went to the table and touched the vase. It fell and broke into many pieces. When her mom came home, she saw the broken vase and said, "Lily, did you touch the vase?" Lily was sad and said, "Yes, mom. I'm sorry." Her mom said, "I will have to punish you. You cannot watch TV for two days." Lily learned her lesson and never touched things she was not supposed to again.

Once upon a time, there was a big iron pot. It sat in a kitchen with many other pots and pans. One day, the iron pot said to the other pots, "I am the best pot here. I can cook the best food." A little pan heard this and said, "You are foolish! You may be big, but I can cook food too. Let's see who can make the best food." So, they decided to have a cook-off. The big iron pot and the little pan started to cook. They put food in themselves and waited for the food to cook. As the heat got higher, the big iron pot started to rise like a balloon. The little pan looked up and saw the iron pot floating away. The little pan laughed and said, "You may be big, but you are not the best. You are just a big, foolish, floating pot!" And that is how the little pan showed the big iron pot that being big is not always the best.

One day, a band of four friends played music in the park. They played drums, a big guitar, and a small guitar. Everyone liked their music and danced to it. The friends were very happy. A spoiled boy named Tim saw the band playing. He wanted to play too, but he did not know how. Tim said, "Stop! I want to play with you!" The band did not want to stop, but they let Tim try. Tim tried to play the drums, but he made a very bad sound. Everyone stopped dancing. Then, a dog came and took the drumstick from Tim. The dog played the drums better than Tim! Everyone laughed and started dancing again. The band and Tim learned that even a dog can play music.

One day, a young dog was very hungry. He looked for food everywhere, but he could not find any. He saw a big house with a nice garden. He thought there might be food there. He jumped over the fence and ran into the garden. He smelled something good. He followed his nose and found a bowl of food on the porch. It was for the cat who lived in the house. The dog was very happy. He ate the food very fast. The cat saw the dog eating her food. She was very angry. She ran to the porch and hissed at the dog. The dog was scared. He ran away from the cat and hid under a bush. The cat looked for the dog, but she could not find him. She went back to the porch and saw that her food was gone. She was very sad. She lied down on the porch and cried. The dog felt bad for the cat. He came out from under the bush and walked to the porch. He had a bone in his mouth. He gave the bone to the cat. The cat was surprised. She sniffed the bone and liked it. She thanked the dog and shared the bone with him. The dog and the cat became friends. They ate the bone together and played in the garden. They were not hungry or angry anymore. They were happy.
