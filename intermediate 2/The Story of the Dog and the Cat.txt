Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Cat

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing in the park. They liked to talk and play together. They were very good friends. The big dog was always honest with the little cat. They liked to discuss many things. Today, they wanted to discuss the value of being nice to others. The big dog said, "When we are nice, we make others happy." The little cat agreed and said, "Yes, being nice is very important." So, the big dog and the little cat played and talked. They were honest and nice to each other. They learned the value of being good friends. And they were very happy.

---

One day, a big dog and a little cat went on a walk. They saw a subway. The big dog said, "I want to go on the subway." The little cat was envious. The little cat wanted to go on the subway too. The big dog and the little cat went on the subway. They saw many people. The people said, "Hello, big dog and little cat!" The big dog and the little cat replied, "Hello, people!" After the ride, the big dog and the little cat left the subway. They were happy. They played in the park all day. The big dog and the little cat were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a queen who lived in a big castle. She had a pet cat named Kitty. One day, the queen found a small, ordinary plant in her garden. She decided to raise it and make it grow big and strong. The queen took care of the plant every day. She gave it water and put it in the sun. One day, the plant grew so big that it started to talk! "Thank you, Queen!" said the plant. "Now I am strong and tall!" But the plant was not nice. It grew bigger and bigger, and it started to hurt the other plants in the garden. The queen asked the plant to stop, but it did not listen. The plant took over the whole garden, and the queen was very sad. In the end, the queen had to leave her castle because the big, bad plant would not go away.

Once upon a time there were two friends, a purple mixer and a little girl. The little girl was sad and she wanted to cry. She said to the purple mixer, "I feel sad and I want to cry." The purple mixer was kind and wanted to help. He said to the little girl, "Don't cry. We can mix things together to make something fun." The little girl smiled and said, "OK, let's mix together my purple toys." And so they did. They mixed together purple dinosaurs, purple cars and purple dolls. The little girl laughed with joy. She said, "Thank you purple mixer, now I'm happy and I don't want to cry anymore." The purple mixer was happy too. He said, "Let's make more purple mixes together." The little girl and the purple mixer laughed and played together all day.

One day, a little cat named Kitty found a box of powder in the kitchen. She didn't know what it was, but she wanted to play with it. So, Kitty tried to move the box to her play area. But the box was too heavy for her. Kitty's friend, Doggy, saw her trying to move the box. He asked, "What are you doing, Kitty?" Kitty replied, "I want to play with this powder, but the box is too heavy." Doggy decided to help Kitty move the box to her play area. Together, they pushed and pulled the box until it was in the play area. Kitty opened the box and the powder went everywhere! It made a terrible mess. They both laughed and played in the powder, knowing they would have to clean up later.

Once upon a time, there was a little comet. The comet was very fast and bright. It loved to display its beautiful tail in the sky at night. All the stars and the moon loved to watch the comet. One night, the comet felt a little anxious. It was going to display its tail in a big show for all the stars and the moon. The comet wanted to make sure it looked its best. The comet raced across the sky, making its tail shine bright. The stars and the moon clapped and cheered. The comet was so happy it did a good job. From that day on, the comet was never anxious again.
