Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Hill

One day, an elderly cat and a little dog were walking in the park. They saw a big hill. The cat said, "Let's go up the hill and see the sight from there!" The dog agreed. As they walked up the hill, they saw a big bird. The bird was very mean. The dog was scared, but the cat had an idea. The cat said, "Let's suggest to the bird to be nice to us. Maybe, it will let us pass." The elderly cat and the little dog went to the bird and said, "Please, be nice to us. We just want to see the sight from the top of the hill." But the bird did not listen. It was very angry and chased them away. The cat and the dog ran down the hill. They were very sad. They could not see the sight from the top of the hill. The moral of the story is that sometimes, even if you try to be nice, not everyone will be nice back.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. Sue loved the park because she could play with her friends and feel the texture of the sand on her hands. Today, Sue saw a big dog that she had never seen before. She wanted to pet the dog, but her mom said no. Sue felt sad and a little guilty because she knew she should listen to her mom. Sue played with her friends near the big tree. The big dog was still there, watching them. Suddenly, the big dog ran towards Sue and her friends. They all screamed very loud. Sue's mom heard the screams and ran to help them. But then, something unexpected happened. The big dog stopped running and picked up a ball that was near Sue's feet. The dog just wanted to play with them! Sue's mom laughed and told Sue it was okay to play with the dog. Sue felt happy and not guilty anymore. They all played with the dog and had a great day at the park.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a journal. The journal was amazing. It was full of colors and pictures. Lily wanted to make a story in the journal. Lily's mom said, "What do you want to make?" Lily said, "I want to make a story about a big, big cake." Lily's mom smiled and said, "That sounds fun!" They started to make the story together. In the story, a little dog named Spot found the big, big cake. Spot said, "Wow! This cake is so big! I want to share it with my friends." Spot called all his friends to come and eat the cake. They all had a great time and were very happy. At the end of the story, Lily and her mom drew a picture of Spot and his friends eating the big, big cake. The amazing journal was even more amazing now because it had Lily's story in it.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a microphone in his toy box. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was a magic wand. He waved it around and pretended to do magic. Tim showed his mom the microphone and said, "Look, Mom! I found a magic wand!" His mom smiled and said, "That's not a magic wand, Tim. That's a microphone. You can use it to sing or talk, and it makes your voice loud." Tim was surprised. He never knew he had a microphone. He wanted to try it out, so he asked his mom to help him. She turned it on, and Tim started to sing his favorite song. His voice was so loud and clear, and he was very happy. Just then, Tim's dad came home from work. He heard Tim singing and thought it was a surprise party for him. He walked into the room with a big smile on his face, ready to join the fun. Tim and his mom looked at each other, and they both laughed. They did not plan a surprise party, but they decided to make it one anyway. Tim's dad had a hard day at work, and the surprise made him feel much better.
