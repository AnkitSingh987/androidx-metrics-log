Category: Intermediate
Title: Pandy the Panda and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a panda named Pandy. Pandy was a very dirty panda. He loved to play in the mud and roll around in the dirt all day long. One day, Pandy wanted to go play with his friends, but his mom said, "Pandy, you must clean up first. You cannot delay it any longer." Pandy was sad but he knew his mom was right. So, Pandy went to the river and washed away all the dirt from his fur. He felt so clean and happy. Now, Pandy could go play with his friends without any delay. They all had a great time playing together in the big green forest.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful town. In the town, there was a big wall. The wall was very tall and strong. It kept the town safe and happy. People who lived in the town liked the wall a lot. One day, a small boy found a door in the wall. The door had a lock on it. The boy was very curious. He wanted to know what was behind the door. He tried to open the lock, but it was too hard for him. He asked his friends to help him, but they could not open it too. Then, one day, a kind cat came to the town. The cat knew how to open the lock. The cat opened the door, and the people saw what was behind it. It was a beautiful garden with lots of flowers and trees. The people were very happy and surprised. They played in the garden and thanked the cat. From that day on, the town was even more peaceful and happy.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little squirrel named Sammy. Sammy loved to play with his friends and find acorns to eat. One day, Sammy found a big, smooth acorn under a tall tree. He was very happy and excited. Sammy wanted to share the acorn with his best friend, Timmy, another squirrel who lived nearby. So, Sammy and Timmy decided to split the acorn in half. They each took one half of the acorn and started to eat it. As they were eating, something unexpected happened. Inside the acorn, they found a shiny, little stone. Sammy and Timmy were very surprised. They decided to keep the stone and show it to their other friends. The shiny stone made all their friends happy too. The moral of the story is that sharing can bring happiness and surprises to everyone.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with clay. They make animals and shapes with clay. They are careful not to break them. One day, they find a new clay. It is white and soft. They wonder what it is. They take some and roll it in their hands. "Look, I can make a whistle with this clay!" Tom says. He makes a hole in the clay and blows. He makes a loud sound. "Whoo! Whoo!" Mia wants to try too. She makes a whistle with her clay. She blows. She makes a soft sound. "Whee! Whee!" They laugh and whistle together. They have fun with the new clay. But then, they hear a voice. It is Mom. She is angry. "Tom! Mia! What are you doing with my flour? That is not clay! That is for baking bread! You made a mess!" Tom and Mia are scared. They drop their whistles. They say sorry to Mom. Mom is not happy. She tells them to clean up. She says they can play with clay later. But not with flour. Tom and Mia are sad. They like the new clay. They like to whistle. But they know they did wrong. They help Mom to clean up. They hope Mom will forgive them. They hope they can play with clay again.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a lonely jellyfish named Jellie. Jellie had no friends because all the fish were scared of her. She felt sad and wanted to make friends. One day, Jellie met a small fish named Fin. Fin was not scared of Jellie. Fin said, "Hi Jellie! Why are you so lonely?" Jellie replied, "I want friends, but everyone is scared of me." Fin had an idea. "Let's dress you up so you won't look scary anymore!" Fin helped Jellie dress up in a funny outfit. Jellie wore a big hat and a colorful scarf. They went to a fish party together. At first, the other fish were scared. But then, they saw that Jellie was not scary at all. They laughed and played together. Suddenly, a big shark came to the party. All the fish were scared and swam away. But the shark was not there to eat them. He only wanted to join the fun. The shark said, "I am sorry I scared you. I just want to be friends too." Jellie and Fin invited the shark to play with them. From that day on, Jellie, Fin, the shark, and all the other fish were friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. And Jellie was never lonely again.
