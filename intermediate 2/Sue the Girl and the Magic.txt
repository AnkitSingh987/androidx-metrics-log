Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty dress. She liked to wear it all the time. One day, Sue went to play outside with her friends. They were all having a fun time. While they were playing, Sue found a sharp rock. She didn't know it was sharp. She picked it up and it made a hole in her dress. Sue was sad and felt weak. She didn't want her dress to be broken. Then, a nice lady saw Sue and came to help her. The lady had a magic needle. She fixed Sue's dress with the needle, and it was like new again! Sue was so happy and thanked the lady. The lady told Sue to be careful with sharp things. From that day on, Sue always remembered to be careful and watch out for sharp things when she played.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous tree in a big forest. In the tree, there was a small bird who could make a magic spell. One day, a mean cat saw the bird and wanted to catch it. The cat climbed the tree to get the bird. The bird saw the cat and got scared. It said, "Please do not catch me, cat! I can make a spell to help you be a happy cat." The cat thought about it and said, "Okay, bird. Make the spell and I will not catch you." The bird tried to make the spell, but it was too scared. The spell did not work. The cat got mad and caught the bird. A nice dog saw what happened and tried to rescue the bird. The dog barked and jumped, but the tree was too high. The cat and the bird were still in the tree. The cat ate the bird, and the dog could not rescue it. The dog was very sad and went back to its home. The cat stayed in the tree, still a mean cat, and the bird was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog. His name was Buddy. Buddy liked to play in the park with his friends. One day, Buddy found a bottle of perfume. He did not know what it was. Buddy took the perfume to his friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily knew what it was. She told Buddy that it was perfume and that it smelled nice. Buddy wanted to smell nice too, so he asked Lily to help him. Lily put some perfume on Buddy's hairy fur. Now, Buddy smelled very nice. All his friends in the park liked the way he smelled. Buddy was very happy. He thanked Lily for helping him. They played together in the park all day, and Buddy smelled nice the whole time.

One day, a girl named Ana found a letter. The letter was big and had her name on it. She was very happy to see it. She thought it was from her friend, Ben. Ana opened the letter and saw that it was fake. The letter said that Ben did something funny and it made Ana laugh. She wanted to tell everyone about it. But when she told her friends, they did not believe her. They said that Ben would never do that. Ana felt embarrassed. Later, Ana found out that her little brother wrote the fake letter. He wanted to play a trick on her. Ana was not mad. She thought it was funny and they both laughed. From that day on, Ana and her brother played tricks on each other, but they always made sure no one got embarrassed.

One day, a bunny named Spring was hopping in the forest. He saw something shiny on the ground. It was a razor. Spring didn't know what it was, but he thought it looked fun. He picked it up with his soft paws and hopped away. Spring met his friend, a squirrel named Nutty. Nutty saw the razor and said, "Be careful, Spring! That thing can hurt you." Spring didn't want to get hurt, so he gave the razor to Nutty. Nutty knew how to be safe with it. Later, Spring and Nutty went to a big tree. They found some delicious red apples. Spring and Nutty shared the apples and had a fun day. They were happy they could be careful and still have fun with their new shiny thing.
