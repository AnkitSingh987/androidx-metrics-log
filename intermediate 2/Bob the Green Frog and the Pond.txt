Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Green Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Bob. Bob lived in a small pond. One day, Bob found a big, round onion near his pond. He picked it up and thought, "I will return this onion to its home." Bob hopped and hopped until he found a big garden. In the garden, there were many onions just like the one he found. Bob knew this was the onion's home. He put the onion back with its friends. As Bob was about to leave, a kind old man saw him. The man was happy that Bob returned the onion. To thank him, the old man gave Bob a big, juicy fly to eat. Bob was very happy and hopped back to his pond with a full belly.

One day, a little cat named Bob was very tired. He went to the park to play with his friends. He saw a rail near the swings. Bob thought it looked fun to climb on. But first, he let out a big yawn. He was not ready to play yet. His friend, a small dog named Max, saw the yawn and knew Bob was tired. Max said, "Bob, let's play later. You look tired now." Bob nodded and went home to take a nap. He was excited to play on the rail later when he was ready. Bob took a long nap and woke up feeling ready to play. He went back to the park and saw Max waiting by the rail. Max said, "Now you look ready!" Bob smiled and climbed on the rail. He had so much fun playing with Max. The nap helped him have the energy to play and enjoy the day.

Once there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loves to drink his apple juice. One day, he and his parents went to get his hair cut. Tom was a bit worried, but they assured him he would love it. At the hairdresser's, the hairdresser asked Tom what he wanted. Tom asked if they could make his hair light, like the sun. The hairdresser was happy and started to cut Tom's hair, making it just like Tom asked. When Tom looked in the mirror, he was so excited. He thought his hair looked just like the sun. He thanked the hairdresser and hugged his parents. On their way home, Tom and his parents stopped at the park, and Tom's mom got him a big cup of apple juice. Tom drank it all up and smiled. He loved his new haircut and the light, sweet taste of the apple juice.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was playing outside in her yard. She saw a distant tree and she wanted to go see it. She walked and walked until she was close to the tree. Under the tree, something shiny appeared. Mia looked closer and saw it was a thermometer. She picked it up and saw it had numbers on it. Mia was happy to find the thermometer. Mia took the thermometer home and showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Good job, Mia!" Mia put the thermometer on the wall. Now, she could see how warm or cold it was every day. Mia and her mom smiled, happy with their new thermometer.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to read the newspaper every day. Tom liked to sit next to her and watch. One day, Sue got a big box in the mail. She was very excited to unpack it. Tom was curious too. He wanted to see what was inside. As Sue opened the box, Tom jumped in and started to play with the newspaper that was inside. He loved the crinkly sound it made. Sue laughed as Tom played with the newspaper. She took out a new toy from the box. It was a soft, colorful ball. Tom saw the ball and stopped playing with the newspaper. He wasn't grumpy anymore. Sue threw the ball, and Tom chased it. They played together all day and had lots of fun.
