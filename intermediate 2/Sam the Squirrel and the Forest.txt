Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to wander around the woods, playing with his friends and looking for food. He was always very hungry because he was a growing squirrel. One day, while Sam was wandering, he found a shiny screw on the ground. He thought it was a toy, so he picked it up and showed it to his friends. They all wanted to play with the screw, but they didn't know how to share. They started to fight over the screw, and Sam felt sad. Sam's mommy saw them fighting and came over to help. She told the little squirrels that the screw was not a toy, but something people use to fix things. She taught them how to share and be kind to each other. The little squirrels learned that sharing is important, and they all became better friends. From that day on, they always shared their toys and food, and never fought again.

Once upon a time, there was a little train named Toot. Toot loved to go fast and make steam. One day, Toot was going up a big hill. He was going very fast and making lots of steam. The steam was white and puffy, like a cloud. Toot saw his friend, Birdie, sitting on a tree branch. Birdie looked upset. Toot stopped and asked, "Why are you upset, Birdie?" Birdie said, "I lost my nest. I can't find it." Toot wanted to help his friend. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. Toot said, "I will help you find your nest." Toot and Birdie went all around, looking for the nest. They asked other animals if they knew where the nest was. Finally, they found the nest on a big rock. Birdie was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Toot!" Toot smiled and said, "You're welcome, Birdie!" Then Toot went back to making steam and going fast, happy that he could help his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat. The fat cat lived in a small house with a nice girl. The girl loved to play with the fat cat every day. They had lots of fun together. One day, the girl wanted to drink some milk. She took a mug from the shelf. But the mug was too high for her. She tried to pull it down, but it was hard. The fat cat saw the girl and wanted to help her. The fat cat jumped up and used its paw to pull the mug down. The girl was very happy. She poured some milk into the mug and shared it with the fat cat. They both drank the milk and smiled. The girl and the fat cat were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house. In this house, lived a cat and a dog. They liked to play all day. They were good friends. One day, it was very cold. The cat and the dog started to shiver. They needed to get warm. The cat had an idea. "Let's go sit by the stove!" said the cat. They went to the stove and sat down. The stove was warm and nice. Soon, the cat and the dog stopped shivering. They were happy and warm by the stove. They played and had fun all day in their tidy little house.

Once upon a time, there was a big space where many small animals lived. They were always polite to each other. One day, a big, scary monster came to their space. The small animals were very scared. A tiny mouse said, "We need to unite to make the monster go away!" So all the small animals joined together. They made a big line and walked up to the monster. They said, "Please go away! We don't want you here." The monster looked at the small animals and started to cry. "I am sorry," the monster said. "I didn't mean to scare you. I just wanted to be friends." The small animals felt bad for the monster. They decided to be friends with the monster and unite to make their space a happy place for everyone. The small animals and the monster became good friends. They learned that when they unite and are polite, they can do big things. They also learned that even big, scary monsters can be nice friends too. And they all lived happily in their space.
