Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Swings

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a doll that she loved very much. Lily was always polite and kind to her doll, and they played together every day. One day, Lily and her doll went to the park. They played on the swings and slides. Lily was having so much fun with her doll. Suddenly, the doll started to talk! Lily was very surprised. She did not understand how her doll could talk. The doll said, "Thank you, Lily, for being so polite and kind to me. Because you have been so nice, I will give you a special gift." The doll waved her hand, and a big ice cream cone appeared. Lily was so happy and ate the ice cream with her talking doll. They played and laughed together all day long.

One day, a creative boy named Tim went to the park. He had a big wheel that he liked to ride. Tim was very happy when he rode his wheel. He would lean to the left and lean to the right as he went around the park. At the park, Tim saw a big hill. He wanted to ride his wheel down the hill very fast. Tim started to go down the hill. As he went faster, he leaned to the left and leaned to the right. He was having so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. A little dog ran in front of Tim's wheel. Tim didn't want to hit the dog, so he leaned to the right very fast. The wheel turned and Tim fell off. But don't worry! Tim was okay, and the little dog was happy too. They both became friends and played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had a friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to lean on the tree when he was tired. The tree was always there for Tim to rest. One day, Tim saw a bird in the tree. The bird was so small and cute. He looked at the bird with wonder. The bird sang a happy song for Tim. Tim smiled and clapped his hands. Tim wanted to give the bird a gift. He was a generous boy. He gave the bird some bread from his lunch. The bird was very happy. Tim, the bird, and the tree became best friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He liked to go for walks with his dog, Max. One sunny day, they went to the park to play. At the park, Tom and Max met a new friend, a girl named Sue. Sue had a big red ball. They played with the ball, laughing and having fun. Max was very careful not to pop the ball with his teeth. After playing, Tom, Max, and Sue were tired. They sat under a tree to rest. Near the tree, there was a gas station. They watched cars come and go, filling up with gas. They waved to the people driving the cars. It was a happy day for Tom, Max, and Sue.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little blue bird named Bluey. Bluey was very important because he was the only blue bird in the forest. All the other birds were green, red, and yellow. They all liked Bluey and wanted to be his friend. One day, Bluey met a tiny mouse named Mimi. Mimi was shy and liked to whisper when she talked. Bluey asked her, "Do you want to be friends?" Mimi whispered, "Yes, I do! But I am shy, so I talk quietly." Bluey and Mimi played together every day. They had so much fun. They liked to whisper to each other when they played hide and seek. The other birds watched them and wanted to play too. Bluey told them, "You can play with us, but you have to whisper like Mimi." All the birds learned to whisper when they played with Bluey and Mimi. The forest was a very happy place. Bluey, Mimi, and their friends played together all the time, and they whispered and laughed every day.
