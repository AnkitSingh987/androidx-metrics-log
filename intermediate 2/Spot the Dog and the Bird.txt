Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a sad little dog named Spot. Spot had a leather ball that he loved to play with. One day, he lost his ball and could not find it anywhere. This made Spot very sad. Spot's friend, a bird named Tweet, saw how sad Spot was. Tweet wanted to help find the leather ball. They looked high and low, but they could not find it. This made Spot even more sad. Then, Tweet had an idea. Tweet reminded Spot that they could ask their other friends for help. So, they asked the cat, the squirrel, and the rabbit. Together, they found the leather ball under a big tree. Spot was so happy, and he was not sad anymore. They all played together with the leather ball, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. In the field, there were many flowers. One day, a rare, pretty flower grew in the field. The other flowers liked the rare flower. They wanted to play with it. The rare flower had a special job. It could spread its seeds to make more flowers. The wind helped the flower spread its seeds. The seeds flew through the air and landed in the field. The other flowers were excited. They knew something special was going to happen. After a few days, the seeds grew into more rare flowers. The field was full of pretty, rare flowers. The other flowers were very happy. They played with the rare flowers all day. The field became the most beautiful field ever.

Anna and Ben are friends who live on a farm. They like to help the farmer with his animals and plants. One day, they see the farmer fixing his tractor. He is holding a big tool and a small part. "Hello, farmer," Anna says. "What are you doing?" "Hello, Anna and Ben," the farmer says. "I am replacing a part of my tractor. It is broken and I need a new one." "Can we help you?" Ben asks. "Sure, you can help me," the farmer says. "You are very intelligent and curious. You can hold this tool for me and watch how I replace the part." Anna and Ben are happy to help the farmer. They hold the tool and watch him carefully. They see how he takes out the broken part and puts in the new one. They hear how the tractor makes a different sound. "Wow, you did it!" Anna says. "Yes, I did," the farmer says. "Thank you for your help. You are very good helpers. Do you want to ride the tractor with me?" "Yes, please!" Ben says. The farmer smiles and lifts them up to the tractor. They sit next to him and feel the wind in their hair. They see the animals and the plants on the farm. They are having fun and learning new things. They are glad to be friends with the farmer.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. This bird loved to fly around and play with its friends. One day, the bird saw an arrow on the ground. The bird picked up the arrow with its beak and wanted to complete a task. The bird thought of a game. It would fly up high and drop the arrow. Then, its friends would try to catch the arrow before it hit the ground. The bird and its friends played the game all day long, having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. When the bird dropped the arrow, it landed in a big, soft pile of leaves. The arrow was gone! The bird and its friends looked and looked, but they could not find it. They were sad, but then they saw a shiny, new arrow in the pile of leaves. They were happy again, and they played with the new arrow all day long.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a brave little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to jump from tree to tree and play with his friends. One day, while Sam was playing, he saw a big fall from a tree. He heard a cry for help. Sam went to see who it was. It was his friend, Tim the bird. Tim said, "I fell from my nest and I can't get back up. Can you help me?" Sam wanted to help his friend, so he thought of a plan. Sam told Tim, "Hold on to my tail, and I will climb back up the tree with you." Tim held on tight, and Sam started to climb. It was not easy, but Sam was brave. They reached the nest, and Tim was safe. Tim said, "Thank you, Sam! You are a brave friend!" They played together all day, happy to be together again.
