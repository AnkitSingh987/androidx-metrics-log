Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to pretend he was a big crane. He would lift his arms and make noises like a crane. One day, Tim saw a big, delicious cake on the table. He knew it was for his mom's birthday. Tim went to his mom and said, "Can I have some cake?" His mom smiled and said, "Not yet, Tim. We have to wait for the party tonight. But if you help me set the table, you can have a little piece later." Tim was excited to help his mom. He pretended to be a crane and picked up plates and cups with his arms. He put them on the table, making crane noises as he did. His mom laughed and said, "You're such a good helper, Tim!" When the party started, Tim was very happy. He had helped his mom and now it was time for cake. His mom cut a big piece for herself and a little piece for Tim. She said, "Here you go, my little crane. You earned this delicious cake!" Tim ate his cake and smiled. He was proud to be a helpful crane. And he knew that pretending to be a crane made his mom happy too.

Once upon a time there was a little critter who was very lucky. He lived in a small home in the woods. One day the critter decided to go exploring and find some new friends. He ventured deep into the forest and soon he heard a strange noise. He was curious and wanted to investigate. As he made his way to the source of the noise, he did not know his steps were approaching a trap. A wise old critter had warned him about traps in the forest. But the little critter did not listen. Before he knew it, the trap was sprung and the little critter was stuck! Luckily, thanks to his luck, the critter was able to escape the trap. He realized he should have listened to the wise old critter’s warning and vowed to never forget it.

One day, a little bug named Blink was feeling upset. He lost his favorite toy, a shiny screw. Blink looked everywhere for it. He searched under the big rock, in the tall grass, and near the small pond. He could not find it anywhere. Blink asked his friends to help him look for the screw. They all looked high and low. They looked in the trees and under the leaves. They even asked the big fish in the pond if they had seen it. But no one could find the screw. Just when Blink was about to give up, he saw something shiny under a flower. He went closer and found his screw! Blink was so happy that he jumped up and down. His friends were happy too. They all went back to their homes, and Blink played with his screw all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug who loved to crawl on the earth. The bug liked to play with his friends, the ants. They would crawl around and play all day long. One day, the bug heard a loud noise. He asked his ant friend, "What is that loud sound?" The ant said, "It is a big truck! It is very loud, and it is coming this way!" The bug and the ants were scared. They did not want the loud truck to hurt them. So they crawled fast to a safe place under a big rock. There they waited until the loud truck passed by. When the truck was gone, the bug and the ants came out from under the rock. They were happy that they were safe. They crawled back to their homes and played until it was time to sleep. The bug and the ants were very brave, and they learned how to stay safe when they heard a loud noise.

Molly was so angry. Her best friend Tom had taken her pan without asking. Molly ran to find Tom and shouted, "Why did you take my pan?" Tom looked down. He said, "I wanted to make dinner for everyone." Molly said, "But you could have asked!" Tom said, "I’m sorry. I should have asked. I won’t do it again. Please forgive me." Molly thought for a moment. She knew it was wrong of Tom to take the pan, but she also knew that he was sorry. Finally, she smiled and said, "I forgive you." Tom smiled back. "Thank you," he said. They hugged and all the anger was gone.
