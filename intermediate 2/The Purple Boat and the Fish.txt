Category: Intermediate
Title: The Purple Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, in a land full of colors, there was a big purple boat. This boat was part of the navy. The navy kept the sea safe and sound for everyone. One day, a little fish named Finny saw the purple boat. Finny swam up to the boat and said, "Hello, big purple boat! You are so pretty!" The boat said, "Thank you, little fish! I am part of the navy. We help keep the sea safe for all the fish like you." Finny thought for a moment and said, "I want to help too! What can I do?" The big purple boat smiled and said, "You can show love and care to all the sea friends. That is a good way to help." Finny learned to value all his sea friends. He shared his food, played with them, and helped when they were sad. All the sea friends were happy because of Finny's love and care. The moral of the story is to always value and care for your friends. It helps to make the world a happier place for everyone.

One day, a yellow bird was flying in the wind. The bird saw a big tree and sat on a branch. The bird was happy and sang a pretty song. A boy with a toy gun came to the tree. He saw the yellow bird and said, "I will shoot you, bird!" The bird was scared and flew away fast. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the boy's toy gun away. The yellow bird came back and said, "Now we can be friends!" The boy smiled and they played together in the wind.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play outside. One day, Tim saw a brilliant red bag on the ground. He picked it up and saw that it was full of popcorn. "Yummy!" he said. Tim went to his friend, Amy. "Look what I found!" he said to her. "Let's share the popcorn and eat it together!" Amy was happy and they sat down to eat the popcorn. But when they opened the bag, something unexpected happened. Out of the bag jumped a little, talking squirrel! "Thank you for opening the bag!" said the squirrel. "I was stuck inside with all the popcorn!" Tim and Amy were surprised but they laughed. The squirrel became their new friend and they all played and ran together. And of course, they shared the popcorn too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat yogurt every day. One day, his mom gave him a delicate cup of yogurt. She told him to be careful and not to spill it. Tim wanted to eat his yogurt with a spoon. But he couldn't find one. He saw a knife and thought, "I can cut my yogurt with this!" He tried to cut the yogurt, but it was too soft. The yogurt spilled all over the table. Tim felt sad and his mom came to help. She said, "Next time, ask for help when you need it." Tim learned that it's okay to ask for help when he doesn't know what to do. And from that day on, Tim always asked for help when he needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane who was three years old. Jane loved to explore her house. One day, she went to the living room with a box of matches. She saw the dark curtains on the window and wanted to know what was behind it. She lit one of the matches, and peered inside. It was so dark that she couldn't see anything. "Hello?" she called out, but no one answered. Jane felt a bit scared. She wanted to find out what was inside the curtain, but was too scared to move closer. Suddenly, Jane heard a voice. "Don't worry," it said. "I'm just a little mouse who lives in the darkness behind the curtains." Jane was relieved to hear the friendly voice. She lit another match and peered behind the curtain. To her surprise, she saw the tiny mouse! They talked for a bit, until Jane's mom called her away. Jane thanked the mouse for being her friend and waved goodbye. Then she left to explore her house some more.
