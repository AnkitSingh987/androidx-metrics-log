Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was adventurous and loved to play outside. One day, she found a rod in her yard. She thought it was a magic wand, so she picked it up and started to play with it. Mia's friend Tom came over to play, and he saw Mia with the rod. He said, "That's not a magic wand, it's just a stick!" Mia disagreed and said, "No, it's a magic wand, and I can do magic with it!" Tom didn't believe her, but he wanted to play too. They both played with the magic rod, taking turns to wave it around and make wishes. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The rod started to glow, and their wishes came true! Tom's toy car became real, and Mia's doll started to talk. They were both surprised and happy. From that day on, they believed in the magic rod and had many more fun and adventurous times together.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw a big cat. The cat was fearful. Amy wanted to give the cat a hug, but the cat was scared. Amy said, "Don't be scared, kitty. I want to be your friend." The cat looked at Amy and started to trust her. Amy walked closer to the cat and gave it a big hug. The cat felt happy. Just then, a big lion came to the park. The lion let out a loud roar. Amy and the cat were scared, but they stayed together. The lion saw that they were friends and didn't want to hurt them. So, the lion went away, and Amy and the cat played in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim was a very fearful dog. He was scared of big sounds and new things. One day, Tim saw a newspaper on the floor. He had never seen one before, so he was scared of it. Tim's friend, a wise old cat named Bella, saw Tim looking at the newspaper. She wanted to help him not be scared. So, Bella went up to Tim and whispered in his ear, "Don't be scared, Tim. The newspaper is not scary. It is just paper with words and pictures." Tim listened to Bella and decided to be brave. He went closer to the newspaper and sniffed it. He realized that it was not scary at all. In fact, he even found it fun to play with. Tim felt happy and proud that he faced his fear. From that day on, Tim was not as fearful as before. He learned that sometimes new things can be fun and not scary. The moral of the story is: don't be afraid to try new things, as they might not be as scary as you think.

Lila and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with their toys and make up stories. One day, they decided to go on a journey with their toy car and their toy boat. They packed some snacks and some water in a bag and put it in the car. "Are you ready, Ben?" Lila asked. "Yes, I am, Lila. Let's go!" Ben said. They pushed the car along the floor, making vroom-vroom noises. They pretended they were driving to the sea, where they would sail with their boat. They had a lot of fun on the way, seeing different things and animals. But when they reached the sea, they saw that their boat had a hole in it. Water was coming out of it. "Oh no, our boat is broken!" Lila cried. "Don't worry, Lila. I can fix it!" Ben said. He took out some tape and some paper from the bag and wrapped them around the hole. He worked very hard and very fast. "There, it's fixed!" Ben said proudly. "Wow, Ben, you are so smart and so kind!" Lila said. They put the boat in the water and climbed on it. They sailed away, waving to the fish and the birds. They had a silly journey, laughing and singing and telling jokes. They were very happy. They were best friends.
