Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Park

One sunny day, Tom went to the park with his mom. Tom saw an ice-cream truck and got very excited. He asked his mom, "Can I have ice-cream, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have one." Tom was so happy! He ran to the truck and picked a big ice-cream cone. But as he was eating it, the ice-cream began to melt. It dripped down his hands and made a big mess. Tom felt frustrated because he didn't want to waste his treat. Then, his mom came to help. She took a napkin and cut it into small pieces. She gave them to Tom and said, "Here, use these to clean your hands." Tom wiped his hands and felt better. He enjoyed the rest of his ice-cream without any more drips.

Lily and her mom went to the big store to buy some new clothes. Lily liked the store because it had many colors and toys. But she did not like the elevator. The elevator was a big box that moved up and down. It made a loud noise and sometimes it shook. Lily was scared of the elevator. When they got to the store, mom said, "We have to take the elevator to the second floor. Come on, Lily, it's not scary. It's just like a ride." Lily shook her head. She did not want to go in the elevator. She held mom's hand very tight. She said, "No, mom, no. I don't like the elevator. It's bad. It's disgusting." Mom smiled and hugged Lily. She said, "It's okay, Lily. I'm here with you. The elevator is not bad. It's not disgusting. It's just a box that helps us go up and down. It's very fast and easy. Look, there are other people in the elevator. They are not scared. They are happy. Do you want to be happy too?" Lily looked at the other people. They were smiling and talking. They did not look scared. They looked happy. Lily felt a little better. She said, "Okay, mom. I'll try. But can you hold me?" Mom said, "Of course, Lily. I'll hold you and comfort you. You are very brave. I'm proud of you." Mom and Lily went in the elevator. Mom held Lily and comforted her. Lily closed her eyes and counted to ten. She felt mom's warm hug and heard mom's soft voice. She felt less scared. She opened her eyes and saw that they were on the second floor. The elevator stopped and the door opened. Mom said, "See, Lily? We did it. We took the elevator. It was not so bad, was it?" Lily smiled and said, "No, mom. It was not so bad. It was not disgusting. It was just a ride. Can we do it again?" Mom laughed and said, "Sure, Lily. We can do it again. But first, let's go and buy some new clothes. You deserve a treat for being so brave." Lily and mom walked out of the elevator. They were happy. They had a fun day at the big store.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very anxious because he got an invitation to a big party. He did not know what to wear or what to bring. Spot went to his friend, the cat named Fluffy. Spot said, "I need help for the party. I am scared." Fluffy was a good friend and wanted to help Spot. Fluffy said, "Do not worry, Spot. We will find something nice for you to wear and bring to the party." Spot and Fluffy looked in the house for something to wear. They found a pretty hat and a nice bow. Spot put them on and felt happy. They also found a big ball to bring to the party. Spot was not anxious anymore. At the party, all the other animals were happy to see Spot. They liked his hat and bow. They also liked the big ball. Spot played with his friends and had lots of fun. He was glad he got the invitation and went to the party with Fluffy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a hook on it. Tim looked at the hook and thought, "I wonder what it's for." A stubborn bird sat on a branch near the hook. The bird looked at Tim and said, "Do you want to know what the hook is for?" Tim nodded his head yes. The bird said, "It is for hanging things. But be careful, something unexpected might happen." Tim found a hat and hung it on the hook. Suddenly, the tree began to shake. The hat turned into a big, colorful ball. Tim was so happy and surprised. He played with the ball all day and made new friends at the park.
