Category: Intermediate
Title: The Light Shelf and the Toys

Once upon a time there was a light shelf. All the toys and books sat on the shelf nicely. One day, a little girl named Susie wanted to balance some of her toys and books on the shelf. Susie's mom said, "Be careful, Susie. Balance everything nicely on the shelf so it doesn't fall off." Susie tried her best to balance the books and toys on the shelf so nothing would fall off the sides. Susie's dad watched her. He said, "You are doing a great job, Susie! Keep it up." Susie smiled and continued balancing her things on the shelf. After a few minutes of work, Susie had managed to balance all her things on the shelf. Susie's mom said, "Great job, Susie! You did a light balance and now all your toys are nicely on the shelf." Susie beamed with pride and hugged her mom and dad. She was so happy that she had managed to balance all her things on the shelf.

Once upon a time, there was an old cat. The old cat liked to slap his tail on the ground. One day, the old cat went for a walk in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As the old cat climbed the tree, he saw a bird. The bird looked very tired. The old cat wanted to help the tired bird. He thought the bird needed a massage. So, the old cat started to massage the bird with his paws. The bird started to feel better after the massage. But then, something unexpected happened! The bird was not really a bird. It was a butterfly! The butterfly thanked the old cat for the massage and flew away. The old cat was happy that he could help the butterfly. And from that day, the old cat and the butterfly became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small place, there lived a brave little girl named Lily. She had a big heart and was always ready to help others. Lily loved to celebrate with her friends and family. One day, Lily and her friend Tom went to the park. They saw a lost kitten who was scared and alone. Lily knew she had to be brave to help the kitten. She walked up to the kitten slowly and gave it a gentle pat. The kitten began to trust Lily. Lily and Tom took the kitten home and gave it some food and water. They were so happy to have helped the kitten. That night, they decided to celebrate their brave act with a small party. Everyone in the place came to have fun and dance. Lily and Tom felt proud of their bravery and knew they had made a new friend in the little kitten.

One day, a peaceful cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big cross near the path. Tom was curious and went closer to the cross. As he got closer, he saw a small dog named Max. Max said, "Hi Tom, do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to play!" They played near the cross and had a lot of fun. But then, a mean bird named Sam came and stole Max's toy bone. Max was sad and said, "Tom, Sam stole my toy bone!" Tom wanted to help his friend. So, Tom and Max went to find Sam, the bird who stole the toy bone. They found Sam on a tree branch. Tom said, "Sam, give Max his toy bone back!" Sam looked at them and said, "I'm sorry, I will give it back." Sam gave the toy bone back to Max. Tom, Max, and Sam all learned that stealing is not good. They all became friends and played together near the big cross. They had a fun and peaceful day.

One day, a big dog and a little cat went to a food place. The big dog was very careful when he walked. He did not want to step on the little cat. They sat at a table and looked at the food list. The big dog said, "I want to order a big bone." The little cat said, "I want to order a small fish." They told the food person what they wanted. They ate their food and were very happy. When they were done, they walked to the exit. The big dog said, "Be careful, little cat. Watch your step." The little cat said, "Thank you, big dog." They left the food place and went back to their home.
