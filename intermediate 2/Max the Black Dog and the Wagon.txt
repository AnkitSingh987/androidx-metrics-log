Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Black Dog and the Wagon

One day, a big black dog named Max went to the store. He had an order to carry home for his friend, the cat. The cat was sick and could not go out. Max was happy to help his friend. On the way home, Max saw a little girl. She was crying. "Why are you sad?" Max asked. The little girl said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Max wanted to help her, but he had to carry the order home first. Max had an idea. He put the order on the girl's toy wagon. Now, Max could carry the order and help the girl find her toy. They looked everywhere and found the toy under a bush. The girl was so happy, she gave Max a big hug. Max felt proud that he could help his friend and the girl.

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She wanted to visit her Grandma, who lived far away. So, she packed up her bag and set off on a journey. After a long trip, she arrived at her Grandma's house. The girl was so excited and ran up to her Grandma's front door. She knocked on the door and after a few minutes, Grandma answered. The girl said, "Grandma! I'm here to visit you! Can I come in?" Grandma smiled and said, "Of course, sweetheart. Come on in." The girl walked in to findGrandma sitting in her living room with a fan blowing in the corner. She asked, "Grandma, why is that fan on?" Grandma laughed softly and said, "It's to keep me feeling cool, my dear. I need to stay patient in this heat." The girl nodded and smiled. She sat down with Grandma and they talked and laughed together the whole day. At the end of the day, the girl said goodbye to her Grandma and promised to visit again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to mail a letter to his friend, Sam. Tim was very excited to mail his letter, but he did not know how to do it. He felt ignorant about mailing letters. One day, Tim met a talking glass. The glass said, "Hello, Tim! I can help you mail your letter to Sam." Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, glass! I don't know how to mail a letter." The glass showed Tim how to put the letter in an envelope and where to find the mailbox. Tim put a stamp on the envelope and dropped it in the mailbox. He was so proud of himself for mailing the letter. Tim thanked the glass again and went home to wait for Sam's reply.

Once upon a time, there was a little horse named Hope. She lived in a stable with her mom and dad. Hope was a happy horse, but sometimes she felt scared of the dark. One day, a scary big storm came to their home. The wind blew hard and the rain fell down. Hope was very scared. Her mom and dad told her not to worry, but Hope still felt scared. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the stable to hide from the storm. The bird was not scared at all. Hope saw the bird and thought, "If the little bird is not scared, then I can be brave too!" So, Hope was not scared anymore. She played with the bird until the storm went away. From that day on, Hope was never scared of the dark again.

One day, a cute little cat went for a walk. The cat found a big box. She wanted to open it. She tried and tried, but she could not open it. The cat felt sad. A minute later, a big dog came. The dog saw the cat and wanted to help. The dog tried to open the box. The dog was strong and opened the box. The cat was very happy. Inside the box, they found many toys. The cat and the dog played with the toys together. They became good friends. The moral of the story is that we can do more when we help each other.
