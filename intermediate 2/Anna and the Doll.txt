Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Doll

Anna likes to play with her doll. Her doll has a white dress and long hair. Anna makes her doll talk and dance and sing. She has fun with her doll. One day, Anna falls down and hurts her knee. It is red and sore. She cries and runs to her mom. Her mom hugs her and puts a band-aid on her knee. She says, "It's okay, Anna. Your knee will get better soon. You are brave and strong." Anna feels better, but she still wants to stay with her mom. She does not want to play with her doll. She says, "Mom, can I stay with you? I don't want to leave you." Her mom smiles and says, "Of course, Anna. You can stay with me. I love you very much. But your doll might miss you. She likes to play with you too. Why don't you bring her here and we can all stay together?" Anna thinks for a moment. She loves her mom and she loves her doll. She says, "Okay, mom. I will bring my doll. She can stay with us too. She has a white dress and long hair. She is my friend." Anna goes to get her doll and brings her to her mom. They all sit on the couch and cuddle. Anna feels happy and safe. She says, "Mom, you are the best. And so is my doll. We are a family."

Once upon a time, in a friendly town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to imagine and play. One day, he wanted to play a new sport with his friends. Tim went to his friend, Sam, and said, "Let's play a new sport! It will be fun!" Sam was excited and called more friends to play. They all met at the park and started to play a game with a ball. As they played, something unexpected happened. A big, colorful bird flew down and took their ball! The friends were sad, but then the bird said, "I want to play too!" Tim and his friends were surprised but happy. They all played the new sport together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a gray elephant named Elly. She loved to dance. She hopped, skipped and twirled all around her home, the jungle. The other animals would cheer her on, watching and clapping. One day, Elly had an idea. She wanted to go on a journey, to find other animals that loved to dance too! Elly asked her mom if she could go. Her mom said, “Yes, Elly, but you must be careful.” Elly packed her bag full of yummy snacks, and started off on her journey. She traipsed through the woods and across the plains, meeting other animals along the way that loved to dance. Whenever Elly saw them, she would jump, jig and spin. Each night, Elly would find a place to sleep, usually under the stars. In the morning, she would hop out of bed and keep exploring. Eventually, Elly came back home to the jungle. She had made so many friends and had lots of great memories. Now, when Elly dances, she can’t help but think of her wonderful journey.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to build things with his toy blocks. One day, he decided to build something very big and impressive. He wanted to make a castle! Tim worked very hard to build his castle. He used all of his blocks to make it tall and strong. When he was done, he was very happy with his castle. But, Tim thought that his castle needed something else. He needed a sword to protect it from bad guys! So, Tim went to his toy box and found a big, shiny sword. It was the perfect sword for his castle. He played with his sword and castle all day long, fighting off the bad guys and keeping his castle safe. At the end of the day, Tim was very tired but happy. His impressive castle and sword had made his day a great adventure.

Once upon a time there were two friends called Jilly and Jack. They were playing together in the park near their house. Jack spotted a thick tree and had an idea. He asked Jilly to help him organize a game. "Let's pretend this tree is a monster and we have to climb it without it catching us," said Jack. Jilly was very excited. She ran around the tree and put her hands on its trunk. She put her leg up and started to climb the tree. Jack watched as Jilly climbed up the thick tree. He was very impressed. "You're so brave!" he said. Jilly smiled and kept going until she reached the very top. Jack then decided to try and climb the tree. He put his leg up on the trunk, but he was not as strong as Jilly. He tried again and again, but he just couldn't get to the top. Jilly smiled and offered to help Jack. She reached down, grabbed Jack's hands and pulled him up. Jack and Jilly cheered when they both made it to the top. They had organized a fun game and worked together to get to the top of the thick tree.
