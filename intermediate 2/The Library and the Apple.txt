Category: Intermediate
Title: The Library and the Apple

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a library. The library was full of colorful books. It was a place where kids loved to go and read. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the library. She found a smooth, red apple on a table. She picked up the apple and took a big bite. The apple tasted sweet and juicy. Lily was very happy. As she ate the apple, Lily read a book about a funny dog. She laughed and smiled. The library was her favorite place to be. And the smooth, red apple made her day even better.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big dream. She wanted to make the world pretty. But there was an ugly monster who did not like pretty things. The monster wanted to stop Sue's dream. Sue was sad but she did not mind. She knew she could make the world pretty if she tried hard. So, she picked up her paint and brush and went to the park. She painted the trees, flowers, and grass in bright colors. The park looked very pretty now. The ugly monster saw the pretty park and was mad. But when he saw how happy Sue was, he started to change his mind. He wanted to be happy too. So, the monster asked Sue if he could help her make the world pretty. Sue said yes, and they became good friends. They worked together to make the world pretty, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big march. Lots of people were walking and talking. They stopped by a hidden place. When everyone was around, one brave person touched the hidden spot. Everyone was so happy and surprised! Then, magic sparkled out of the hidden spot. Bright rainbows filled the sky and they continued marching to the next spot with joy and excitement. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a big, fat dragon. His name was Dino. He lived in a cave on top of a hill. Dino had many friends, but his best friend was a little boy named Tim. They loved to play together. One day, Dino and Tim were playing with a ball. Dino said, "Tim, let's play catch!" Tim threw the ball high in the sky. Dino tried to catch it, but he was too fat. The ball dropped on the ground. They both laughed. Every day, Dino and Tim played together. They had so much fun. They never wanted to stop. And they were always happy because they were best friends.

One day, a boy named Tom found a long key. He did not know what the key was for. Tom went to his friend, Sue, to ask her about the key. Tom said, "Sue, I found a long key. Do you know what it is for?" Sue did not know, but she wanted to help Tom find out. They went to a big door with a lock. Tom put the key in the lock and turned it. The door opened. Inside the room, they saw a big box. Tom and Sue were very happy. But when they opened the box, a loud yell came out. It scared them a lot. They ran out of the room and the door closed. They never found out what was in the box.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside under the big, bright sun. One day, Tim saw a big cloud hiding the sun. Tim felt sad because he wanted to play outside. Tim had a question. He asked his mom, "Why is the sun hiding behind the cloud?" His mom said, "Sometimes the sun needs to rest, and the clouds help the sun take a break. But don't worry, the sun will come back soon." The next day, the sun came back, and Tim was very happy. He played outside all day with his friends. They all had lots of fun. They knew that even if the sun went away for a little while, it would always come back to make their days bright and happy again. And they were never scared of the terrible clouds anymore.
