Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Anna. She loved to play with her ball. One day, she lost her ball in the tall grass. She was very sad. Anna met a boy named Tom. Tom saw Anna's sad face and asked, "Why are you sad?" Anna told Tom about her lost ball. Tom wanted to help Anna find her ball. They looked and looked for the ball. Finally, they found the ball near a big tree. Tom gave the ball to Anna. "Here, take this pass to the park. We can play and improve our ball game," Tom said. Anna was so happy. They went to the park and played all day. Anna and Tom became good friends.

One day, Tom was walking along a lake when he thought he saw something in the water. He peered closer and saw a fish! He didn't have a fishing rod, so he decided to make a spear. He remembered his dad showing him how to make a spear before, though he had never tried it himself. He found a sharp stick and scraped it against a rock to make it pointy. With the help of some string, a piece of wood, and an old shoe lace, Tom assembled his very own spear. Tom was so enthusiastic about his new spear that he decided to catch a fish. As Tom stood at the edge of the lake, he realised the fish had gone. Tom was very disappointed and threw down the spear in frustration. Suddenly, the fish reappeared and Tom leapt forward, thrusting his spear into the water. He was able to provide some food for dinner! Tom was so proud of himself for providing food for his family and he was also very proud of his spear!

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing in the park. They saw a long rope with a big knot in the middle. The dog said, "I want to play with the knot!" The cat said, "No, I want to play with it!" They started to fight over the rope. Then, a disgusting bug came and sat on the knot. The dog and the cat stopped fighting and looked at the bug. They both said, "Yuck! We don't want to play with the knot now." The bug laughed and said, "Now I can play with the knot all by myself!" But suddenly, the knot came undone, and the bug fell to the ground. The dog and the cat laughed at the bug. They decided to lie down in the sun and forget about the knot. The bug flew away, and the dog and the cat became friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and many toys. Tom loved to look at the sky. He liked the big, blue sky with white clouds. He liked the sun and the moon too. One day, Tom found a little bird. The bird was lost and did not belong in his house. The bird was sad and wanted to go back to the sky. Tom wanted to help the bird be happy again. Tom took the bird outside and let it fly into the sky. The bird was happy to be back where it belonged. Tom was happy too. He knew he had done a good thing. From that day on, Tom and the bird were friends. They played together under the big, blue sky.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy cat named Lily. She lived in a small house with her friend, a funny dog named Max. They liked to play and joke with each other every day. They were very happy. One day, Lily found a shiny coin. She showed it to Max and said, "Look, I found something of value!" Max looked at the coin and smiled. They decided to buy a big toy to share. They went to the toy store and picked out a big, bouncy ball. They couldn't wait to play with it. Lily and Max played with the ball all day, but they didn't know how to share. They fought over the ball and it made them sad. In the end, the ball popped and they couldn't play with it anymore. They learned that fighting over things of value can lead to a bad ending.
