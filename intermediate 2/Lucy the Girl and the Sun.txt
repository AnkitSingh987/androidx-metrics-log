Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Sun

One day, a girl named Lucy went to visit her grandma for a holiday. She was very excited. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Grandma's house was big and had a lot of trees around it. Lucy liked to play in the garden with her dog, Spot. On the holiday, Lucy and Spot were playing in the garden when they found a big hole. They looked inside and saw a bitter cold land with snow and ice. Lucy and Spot were surprised. They decided to go in and see what was there. They put on warm clothes and jumped into the hole. In the bitter cold land, they met a snowman who could talk. The snowman told them that they were in a magic place. He said that they could have one wish before they went back home. Lucy thought hard and wished for the cold land to become warm and sunny. Suddenly, the snow and ice melted, and the bitter cold land turned into a beautiful beach. Lucy, Spot, and the snowman played all day. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye to their new friend and jumped back into the hole. They were happy they had a fun and unexpected holiday adventure.

Ben and Mia are twins. They go to school every day. They wear the same uniform: a blue shirt and a red skirt or pants. They like their uniform. It makes them look like a team. One day, it is very hot. The sun is bright and the air is sticky. Ben and Mia feel sweaty and tired. They want to drink water and eat ice cream. They walk home with their mom. They see a truck with a big sign. It says "Ice Cream". "Mom, can we have ice cream?" Ben and Mia ask. They point to the truck. "Sure, why not?" Mom says. She smiles and takes out some money. She buys two cones for Ben and Mia. One is chocolate and one is vanilla. They are cold and sweet. Ben and Mia lick their ice cream. They feel happy and cool. But the ice cream starts to melt. It drips on their hands and their uniform. It makes stains and spots. Ben and Mia try to eat faster. But they can't. The ice cream melts too much. "Oh no, look at our uniform!" Ben and Mia say. They are sad and messy. They don't look like a team anymore. They look like a mess. "Don't worry, it's okay," Mom says. She takes out some napkins and wipes their hands and faces. She says they can wash their uniform at home. She says they can still be a team. She hugs them and kisses them. Ben and Mia feel better. They love their mom. They love their ice cream. They don't mind their uniform. They are still twins. They are still a team.

One day, a dependable boy named Tim went to play outside. He found a big stick and thought it would be fun to pretend it was a bow. Tim wanted to shoot something with his pretend bow and arrow. He looked around and saw an envelope on the ground. Tim picked it up and put it on a tree. He stood back, pulled his pretend bow, and said, "I will shoot the envelope!" Tim aimed and shouted, "Shoot!" He pretended to shoot the envelope with his stick bow. The wind blew and the envelope fell off the tree. Tim was so happy! He picked up the envelope and ran to show his mom.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gray cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her best friend, a little girl named Lucy. They played all day long and always had a big smile on their faces. One day, while they were playing outside, it started to rain. Fluffy wanted to protect Lucy from getting wet. So, they ran under a big tree to stay dry. They waited and waited until the rain stopped. When the rain was gone, Fluffy and Lucy went back to playing. They played in the sun, and their smiles were bigger than ever. They knew they would always protect each other and be the best of friends.
