Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boat and the Help

One day, a big boat went to a port. The boat had many things to do there. The boat was very compassionate and loved to help others. In the port, there were many little boats that needed help. The big boat saw a little boat that was sad. The big boat asked, "Why are you sad, little boat?" The little boat said, "I am too small to do my job. I need help." The big boat wanted to help the little boat. The big boat said, "I will help you do your job, little boat." The little boat was happy. They worked together to do the job at the port. After they finished the job, the little boat said, "Thank you, big boat! You are very compassionate and kind." The big boat was happy to help. They became good friends and always helped each other do their jobs at the port.

One day, a big, rough dog named Spot went to play with his friends. They played a fun game called "spin the bone." Spot was good at spinning the bone with his mouth. His friends liked to watch him spin the bone fast and far. Today, when Spot spun the bone, something unexpected happened. The bone flew high into the sky and landed in a big tree. Spot and his friends looked up and saw the bone stuck in the tree. They all tried to jump and reach the bone, but they could not get it. Just then, a small bird flew by and saw the bone. The bird flew up and grabbed the bone with its mouth. The bird spun around and dropped the bone down to the ground. Spot and his friends were happy and surprised. They thanked the bird and continued to play their game. The small bird showed them that even little friends can help in big ways.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Ella. Ella was a patient girl, which meant that she was good at waiting and being still. Today Ella was going to the doctor and she was a bit fearful. The doctor's office was a big building and Ella felt small when she entered. The doctor was nice but he did a lot of calling. He called for help and for tools. He asked Ella to stay very still. Ella sat very still in the doctor's office and she listened to all the calling. Then she felt a prick in her arm. It made her jump and she started to cry. The doctor tried to soothe her but it was no use. Ella started to feel even more fearful. Suddenly the doctor stopped calling. He looked very worried and started to shake his head. Ella asked the doctor what was wrong, but he said nothing. He quickly took off his white coat and ran away. Ella felt terrible. She was all alone in the doctor's office and she was very scared. She never found out what had happened. But she went home feeling so sad and so fearful. Horrible things can happen at the doctor's office!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his red ball. He would bounce it all day long. One day, Tim saw a big tree with a shiny axe next to it. Tim knew that the axe was useful for cutting the tree, but he couldn't use it because he was too small. Tim had an idea. He thought that if he could bounce his red ball very high, he could make the tree fall down. So, he tried and tried, but the ball could not make the tree fall. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He asked his dad for help. His dad was big and strong, and he could use the useful axe to cut the tree. Together, Tim and his dad cut the tree. Tim learned that it is good to ask for help when you need it. He also learned that being big and strong is not the only way to be useful. Sometimes, a little red ball can be just as helpful. And they all lived happily ever after.
