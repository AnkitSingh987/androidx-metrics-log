Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to mix things in her kitchen. One day, she wanted to make a cake. Mia needed to use the right number of things to make the cake. She asked her mom for help. Mia and her mom started to mix the cake. They put in one egg, two cups of flour, and three spoons of sugar. But then, Mia's little brother Max came into the kitchen. Max wanted to help, but he was too small. Max took a big spoon and mixed the cake too fast. The cake mix went all over the kitchen! Mia and her mom were sad. The cake mix was spoiled. They had to clean up the big mess. Max said sorry to Mia and her mom. Mia's mom said it was okay. They all worked together to clean up. Then, they made a new cake with the right number of things. The new cake was yummy and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Sue wore her new blouse to play. It was a pretty blouse, and she was very happy. Sue was playing with her friends when she saw a big rock. She wanted to jump on it, but she was careless. Sue did not look where she was going and she did a stumble. She fell down and her blouse got dirty. Sue was sad and cried. Sue's mom came and helped her up. She told Sue that next time, she should be more careful when playing. Sue learned her lesson and promised to be more careful. The moral of the story is to always watch where you are going and not be careless.

Once upon a time, on a big round planet, there lived a tiny ant. This tiny ant had an enormous friend, an elephant. They liked to play together every day. The tiny ant would climb on the elephant's back, and they would go on fun trips. One day, something unexpected happened. The sky turned dark, and a big rain cloud came. The rain started to fall, and the tiny ant was scared. The enormous elephant saw his friend was scared, so he used his big ears to cover the ant and keep him dry. But then, the rain stopped, and the sun came out. The tiny ant looked up and saw a beautiful rainbow in the sky. The ant and the elephant were happy again. They knew that even when scary things happen, good things can come after. And they continued to play and have fun, under the big rainbow on their round planet.

Once upon a time, there was a generous boy named Tim. Tim loved to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim found a big pipe in his yard. The pipe was very long and had two separate parts. Tim had a great idea. He decided to use the pipe to make a fun game for his friends. He put one part of the pipe on one side of his yard and the other part on the other side. Tim then put a ball in the pipe and watched it roll from one side to the other. Tim's friends came over and they all played with the pipe. They laughed and had a great time. Tim was happy that he could share his new toy with his friends. They all said "thank you" to Tim for being so generous. And they played with the pipe until the sun went down.

Lila and Ben were twins who lived on a farm. They liked to help their dad with the animals and the crops. One day, their dad told them he had to deliver some corn to the market. He said he would take the mule, because the mule was strong and could carry a lot of corn. "Can we go with you, dad?" Lila asked. "Sorry, kids, not today. The road to the market is dangerous. There are big trucks and loud horns and fast cars. You have to stay here and watch the farm with mom," dad said. Lila and Ben were sad. They wanted to see the market and the mule. They waved goodbye to their dad and the mule as they left. They decided to play in the barn. They found some hay and some buckets and some ropes. They pretended they were delivering corn to the market, too. They put some hay in the buckets and tied them to a rope. They dragged the rope around the barn, making mule noises. "Come on, mule, let's go to the market!" Ben said. "Watch out, mule, there are dangerous trucks and horns and cars!" Lila said. They had fun playing until they heard their mom calling them for lunch. They left the rope and the buckets in the barn and ran to the house. They ate some soup and bread and cheese. They told their mom about their game. She smiled and said they were very creative. They asked their mom when their dad and the mule would come back. She said they would be back soon. They waited and waited. They looked out the window and saw the sun setting. They wondered if their dad and the mule were okay. Then they heard a familiar sound. It was the mule, braying and snorting. They ran outside and saw their dad and the mule coming up the road. They had a big smile on their face and a lot of corn on their back. Lila and Ben hugged their dad and the mule. They were happy to see them. They helped their dad unload the corn and put it in the barn. They asked their dad how the market was. He said it was busy and noisy and colorful. He said he sold all the corn and got some money and some treats for them. He gave them each a candy and a toy. They thanked their dad and the mule. They said they were proud of them. They went inside and had some dinner and some cake. They told their mom about their dad's and the mule's adventure. She said they were brave and smart. They went to bed and cuddled with their toys. They dreamed of the market and the mule. They were happy.
