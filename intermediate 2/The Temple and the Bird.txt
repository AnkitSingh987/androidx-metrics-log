Category: Intermediate
Title: The Temple and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big temple. The temple was very old and filthy. A kind man named Tom lived near the temple. He wanted to help make the temple clean and beautiful again. One day, Tom met a talking bird named Polly. Tom told Polly, "I need help to clean the temple. Will you support me?" Polly agreed, "Yes, I will support you, Tom!" Together, Tom and Polly worked hard to clean the temple. Tom washed the walls, while Polly picked up all the dirt with her beak. Soon, the temple was clean and shining. Everyone in the land was happy and thankful to Tom and Polly for their help.

Once upon a time, there was a gym where all the kids loved to play. In the gym, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy liked to be careful when she played with her friends. One day, a new boy named Tim came to the gym. He did not understand how to play the games. Lucy saw that Tim was sad. She said, "Do you want to play with me? I can help you understand the games." Tim smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Lucy was careful and showed Tim how to play the games. They had a lot of fun together. All the kids in the gym became friends with Tim. The moral of the story is: Be kind and help others understand new things. It is important to be careful and patient when teaching someone new.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with many leaves. They wanted to climb the tree and see what was on top. "Look, Tom, there is something shiny on that branch!" Lily said. "Maybe it is gold!" "Let's go and get it!" Tom said. They climbed the tree carefully and reached the branch. They saw a small bell hanging from a string. It was made of gold and it had a pretty sound. "Wow, this is so attractive!" Lily said. "Can we take it home?" "I don't know, Lily. Maybe it belongs to someone else." Tom said. "Maybe it is a fairy's bell." Just then, they heard a voice from below. It was a man with a big hat and a long coat. He looked angry. "Hey, you kids! Get down from there! That is my bell! I put it there to scare away the birds!" he shouted. Lily and Tom were scared. They dropped the bell and climbed down quickly. They ran away from the park and did not look back. They learned a lesson that day. They should not take things that are not theirs, even if they are attractive. And they should be careful of strangers in the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She had a fine hat on her head and was out for a walk. As she walked, she saw a lot of smoke in the sky. She asked her mom what it was and her mom said, "That's a fire. Let's go have a look!". They walked over to the fire and saw a lot of people. The mom asked one of them what was going on. The man said, "We're getting ready to have a big party! Would you like to join us?" The little girl nodded yes and the man said, "Great! Let us know if you need any help. Everything is almost ready!" The little girl helped the man set up the decorations and soon the party was ready. Everyone was having lots of fun. The little girl danced, ate cake and laughed with her friends. After the party, the man thanked the little girl for helping. He said, "You did a really fine job! Thank you so much." The little girl smiled and nodded in agreement. Then she and her mom made the journey home, and the little girl thought about all the fun she had at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to look out the window every day. He would watch the birds fly and the cars go by. One day, he saw a big, red ball outside his window. Tim really wanted to play with the big, red ball. He went outside and saw his friend, Sue. "Sue, do you want to play with the big, red ball?" Tim asked. Sue said, "Yes, let's play!" They had a lot of fun kicking the ball, but then something unexpected happened. The ball went too high and it went through the window! They both felt uncomfortable because they knew they did something wrong. Tim and Sue went inside to tell Tim's mom. "Mom, we are sorry. We did not mean to break the window," Tim said. Tim's mom was not mad. She said, "It's okay. I am glad you told me. We can fix the window together." Tim and Sue learned that it is important to tell the truth, even when they miss and make a mistake.
