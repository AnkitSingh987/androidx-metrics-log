Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Secret

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars on his bed. One day, he found a new sheet with a picture of a big car on it. Tim was so happy! He ran to his mom and asked if he could put the new sheet on his bed. His mom said yes, but told Tim to be careful because the sheet was fragile. Tim set the new sheet on his bed, making sure to be extra gentle. He placed his toy cars on the sheet and started to play. As he was playing, something unexpected happened. The big car on the sheet started to move! Tim was so surprised and excited. The big car on the sheet was a magic car! It could talk and play with Tim. They had so much fun racing around the room. But then, the magic car told Tim a secret. It said that it could only play for one day. After that, it would turn back into a picture on the sheet. Tim was sad, but he understood. He played with the magic car until bedtime, and then they said goodbye. The next morning, the big car was back on the sheet, just like before. Tim smiled and knew he would always remember his special day with the magic car.

Once upon a time, there was a calm little girl named Lily. She loved to play in her garden with her best friend, a small orange cat named Sunny. They would run and laugh all day long. One day, Lily found a pretty flower that was about to bloom. She was so excited to see the flower open up. She showed Sunny the flower and they waited for it to bloom. They sat and watched, hoping it would happen soon. But as they waited, dark clouds came and it started to rain. The rain made the flower wet and it did not bloom. Lily and Sunny were sad. They went inside and could not see the pretty flower bloom. The day ended with Lily and Sunny feeling sad and the flower stayed closed.

Once upon a time, there was a team of animals. They all lived on a farm. The team had a big dog, a little cat, and a tiny mouse. They all loved to play and have fun. They were very fit because they played all day. One day, the team wanted to raise a big flag. They wanted everyone to see how strong and fit they were. The dog, the cat, and the mouse worked together to lift the heavy flag. They pushed and pulled and tried very hard. But the flag was too heavy for them. The team tried and tried, but they could not raise the flag. They were very sad. The dog, the cat, and the mouse felt tired and weak. They went back to the farm and did not play anymore. The team was not happy, and the farm was quiet.

Ben likes to write. He writes his name, his mom's name, and his dog's name. He writes on paper, on the wall, and on the sidewalk. He writes with crayons, with markers, and with chalk. One day, Ben goes to the park with his mom and his dog. He sees a big tower made of blocks. He wants to write on it. He runs to the tower with his chalk. He writes "Ben" on one block. He writes "Mom" on another block. He writes "Dog" on another block. "Wow, Ben, you are very enthusiastic about writing!" his mom says. "But you should ask before you write on other people's things." Ben looks around. He sees a girl and a boy. They are frowning. They are holding more blocks. They are the ones who made the tower. "Sorry," Ben says. "I like your tower. Can I help you make it bigger?" The girl and the boy nod. They smile. They give Ben some blocks. They all work together to make the tower taller and stronger. They write their names on the top block. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly bottle. The bottle was sad because it was all alone. It hoped to find a friend to play with. One day, the smelly bottle saw a little girl. The girl was playing with her ball. The bottle hoped the girl would be its friend. So, the bottle rolled over to the girl. The girl saw the smelly bottle and said, "You are smelly, but I will be your friend." The bottle was so happy. The girl and the smelly bottle played together all day. And they were friends forever.
