Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Toys

Lily and Tom are brother and sister. They like to play with their toys in their room. But sometimes they make a big mess and do not put their toys away. One day, Mom came to their room and said, "Lily and Tom, you need to change your room. It is not tidy at all. Look at all the toys on the floor and on the bed. You have to put them in the box or on the shelf. If you do not change your room, you will not get a treat after dinner." Lily and Tom did not want to miss their treat. They loved ice cream and cookies. They said, "Sorry, Mom. We will change our room. We will make it tidy. Please help us." Mom smiled and said, "Okay, I will help you. But you have to do most of the work. It is your room and your toys. You have to learn to take care of them. That is part of being a family. We help each other and we keep our home nice and clean." Lily and Tom nodded and started to pick up their toys. They put them in the box or on the shelf. They also made their bed and threw away the trash. Mom helped them a little bit. Soon, their room was tidy and neat. Mom said, "Well done, Lily and Tom. You changed your room. It looks much better. I am proud of you. You are good children. Now you can have your treat. Let's go to the kitchen and get some ice cream and cookies." Lily and Tom hugged Mom and said, "Thank you, Mom. We love you. We love our family. We will try to keep our room tidy from now on." They went to the kitchen and enjoyed their treat. They were happy and so was Mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken named Chip. Chip lived on a farm with many other animals. Chip was a very good and kind chicken. He had many friends on the farm. One day, Chip saw a big tree with a yummy red apple on it. Chip wanted to get the apple but he was too small. So, he asked his friend, Cow, to help him. Cow said, "I can help you, Chip. Let's lean on the tree together." Chip and Cow leaned on the tree. The tree shook and the yummy red apple fell down. Chip was so happy and said, "Thank you, Cow! We did it together because we are good friends." From that day on, Chip and Cow became the best of friends and they always helped each other. They played together and shared many yummy apples. And they lived happily on the farm.

Mummy and baby were in the kitchen. Mummy was cooking a big pot. Baby saw the pepper and was curious. Baby: "Mummy, what is this?" Mummy: "That, baby, is pepper." Baby: "What is pepper?" Mummy: "It makes food yummy." Baby was feeling clever. She knew that she could put some pepper in the pot. So she carefully picked up the pepper and tried to slip it into the pot. But the pepper slipped and baby gasped. Mummy saw what had happened. She smiled and said, "That was very clever of you, baby. But it was too big. Next time, use a little bit." Baby nodded and said, "Yes, Mummy." Then she carefully put some pepper into the pot and watched the big pot cook.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a pair of pants that he loved very much. These pants were special because they were colorful and had his favorite animal on them. One day, Tim wanted to wear his pants to play outside. But his mom said, "No, you can't wear those pants today. You have to wear your other pants." Tim felt sad and a little fearful because he did not like his other pants. He asked his mom to please permit him to wear his favorite pants. His mom thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, you can wear your favorite pants, but you must be careful not to get them dirty." Tim was so happy! He put on his pants and went outside to play. He had a great day playing with his friends and was careful not to get his pants dirty. When he came home, his mom was proud of him for being so careful with his favorite pants.
