Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Green Frog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Tom. He loved to play sport with his friends. They would play catch and run in the park. One day, Tom saw a big green ball. He could not believe it. He wanted to play with the ball. Tom asked his friends to imagine how fun it would be to play with the big green ball. Tom and his friends started to play with the big green ball. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The big green ball was not a ball. It was a big green monster! The big green monster was not happy. He did not like being played with. The monster chased Tom and his friends away. They were sad and scared. They could not play with the big green ball anymore. And that was the end of their fun day.

One day, a brave little girl named Jane wanted to bake a cake. She put on her apron and went to the kitchen. She saw her mom using a hammer and nail to fix a chair. Jane thought the nail looked interesting. Jane said, "Mom, can I use the nail to make my cake?" Her mom smiled and said, "No, Jane. Nails are not for baking. Let's use a spoon instead." Jane started to bake her cake with a spoon. She mixed and mixed. When the cake was done, her mom put it in the oven. They waited for the cake to be ready. Jane was so excited! When the cake was ready, Jane and her mom took it out of the oven. They saw something shiny in the cake. It was the nail! Jane's mom had put the nail in the cake by mistake. They laughed and laughed. Jane's mom said, "Well, I guess we used the nail after all!" Jane and her mom made a new cake without the nail. They shared the cake with their family and everyone loved it. Jane was happy she could bake a cake and be a brave helper in the kitchen.

Once there was a little boy and he wanted to play outside in the park. His mom said no, as it was very dark outside. He tried to plead but his mom wouldn't listen. Just then a guard came up to the boy's house and shouted, "Come out." The little boy was scared and stayed inside. The guard cursed and said, "You must suffer!" The little boy had no choice so he followed the guard. The guard took the little boy far away from his home. The little boy was so scared but he tried to be brave. When they reached the guard's house the little boy thought he was finally safe, but he was wrong. The guard locked the little boy in a room and said, "You must suffer forever." The little boy was so unhappy and he couldn't fight back. The guard took away the little boy's happiness and all he could feel now was suffering.

Once upon a time, there was a strong octopus. She had eight long arms and loved swimming in the sea. She was always very brave. But one day, she felt a bit weak. She couldn't understand why she felt that way. She questioned what was wrong with her. One of her friends, a wise jellyfish, offered some advice. She said, “You need to add something to your life, something new.” The octopus pondered for a moment. Then, she had an idea. She said, “I think I will add something special to my life. It will be fun yet challenging.” The jellyfish smiled. She said, “That's a good idea. I know it will make you stronger.” The octopus was excited. The octopus decided to add a new hobby. With dedication, determination and hard work, she soon felt less weak. She swam round the ocean with a smile. A smile that showed she learned that to stay strong, she had to add something new.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max was a dull dog, but he liked to help people. One day, Max went to a lab. The lab was a place where people did work. In the lab, Max met a man named Tom. Tom was trying to complete a puzzle. Tom looked sad because the puzzle was hard. Max wanted to help Tom. Max said, "Let me help you." Tom and Max worked together on the puzzle. They put the pieces in the right spots. Soon, they were done. Tom was happy, and Max was happy too. They had completed the puzzle together. From that day on, Max and Tom were the best of friends.
