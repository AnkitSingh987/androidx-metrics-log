Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Adorable Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived an adorable little girl named Lily. She had a big smile that made everyone happy. Lily loved to play outside with her friends in the sun. One day, at noon, Lily saw a sad boy sitting alone. She walked up to him and said, "Hi, I'm Lily. Why are you sad?" The boy looked at her and said, "I'm Tom. I'm new here and I have no friends to play with." Lily smiled and said, "Well, now you have a friend! Let's play together!" Tom's face lit up and they played all day long. When it was time to go home, Tom said, "Thank you, Lily. Your smile made me feel happy." From that day on, Lily and Tom were best friends. They learned that a smile can make others happy and help make new friends. And they always remembered to share their smiles with everyone they met.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his dog, Max. They went to the park to play with Tim's toys. Tim had a lot of stuff to play with, like a ball, a toy car, and a kite. Max liked to chase the ball and bring it back to Tim. While they were playing, Tim saw a broken toy on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Oh no! This toy is broken. I want to fix it." Tim tried to fix the broken toy, but he couldn't. He was sad and asked Max, "What should we do?" Max barked and wagged his tail, but he didn't know what to do either. Tim thought maybe his mom could help, so they walked back home. When they got there, Tim showed the broken toy to his mom. She tried to fix it, but it was too broken. Tim was very sad that the toy couldn't be fixed. He said, "I wish I could make it all better, but I can't." His mom hugged him and said, "Sometimes things stay broken, and it's okay to be sad about it." The day ended with Tim feeling sad about the broken toy. He wished he could have fixed it, but he knew that sometimes things just stay broken.

Mandy and Andy were two siblings who were visiting their local park. It was a very hot day, so they decided to have a rest on the bench. As the siblings sat there, the sky slowly changed to a beautiful sunset. Suddenly, Mandy had an idea. She looked at Andy excitedly and said, "Let's watch a cartoon on our phone." Andy agreed and the siblings settled down, each taking out their phones to watch their favourite cartoons. The siblings watched the cartoon until it finished playing. By then, both Mandy and Andy were feeling tired and sleepy. Mandy looked around the park before pointing to the swings. "Let's swing on the swings until we are sleepy," she said. So, the two siblings made their way to the swings, each taking a seat before pushing off. They kept swinging until both Mandy and Andy started feeling drowsy. Mandy stretched her arms above her head. "Let's go home now," she said. So the two siblings put away their phones and slowly made their way out of the park. As they walked, Mandy looked back at the park and said, "What a fun day we have had!"

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia liked to play and listen to the sounds around her. She heard a bird singing and a dog barking. Mia's mom told her to stay close while she sat on a bench. Mia saw a big tiger hiding behind a tree. The tiger said, "Hi, Mia! I want to be successful and make friends. Will you help me?" Mia was not scared. She wanted to help the tiger. She said, "Yes, I will help you." Mia took the tiger to her mom. Her mom was very surprised because she thought tigers were only in books. The tiger told Mia's mom about wanting to be successful and make friends. Mia's mom smiled and said, "You can be our friend!" The tiger was happy and played with Mia and her mom all day.
