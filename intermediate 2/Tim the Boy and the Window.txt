Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Window

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to build things with his toy blocks. He built tall towers, big houses, and even a tiny city. Tim was very organized when he built his city. He put the blocks in neat rows and made sure each block was in the right place. One day, while Tim was building, he heard a loud noise outside. He looked out the window and saw a big dog. The dog was running around and making a mess. Tim was scared that the dog would come and knock down his city. The dog was a threat to his organized city. Tim thought of a plan to keep his city safe. He went outside and called the dog over. The dog wagged its tail and came to Tim. Tim took the dog to a new spot in the yard and showed the dog how to build with sticks. The dog loved it! Now, the dog had its own place to build and did not bother Tim's city. Tim's organized city was safe from the threat, and they both had fun building together.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to make toast for her mom. Sue's mom was always reliable and made her breakfast every day. Today, Sue wanted to do something nice for her mom. Sue put the bread in the toaster and pushed the button down. She waited and waited. Then, she heard her mom coming. Sue said to her dog, Max, "Hurry, Max! We need to get the toast ready!" Max wagged his tail and watched the toaster. When the toast popped up, Sue put butter on it. She put the toast on a plate and gave it to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue! This is the best toast ever!" Sue felt happy that she could help her reliable mom.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Patty wanted to go outside and play with her friends. But it was a chilly day and Patty had to get bundled up before she could leave! She put on her scarf, her hat and her mittens and still she shivered. She went outside and found that all of her friends were playing a game. They were competing to see who could jump the highest on the trampoline! Patty wanted to join in, so she raced over and took her turn. When it was time for a snack break, Patty's friends all headed over to the kitchen. Patty followed them and noticed a big bottle of something called "sauce". Everyone put some of the sauce on their food and then started munching. Patty put some sauce on her food too and soon she was eating and laughing with her friends. What a fun day Patty had! Her friends were competitive, but they all worked together to have a good time. And, even though it was cold, Patty was never too cold because she was having so much fun!

Once upon a time, there was a happy boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his toys. One day, Tim found a bottle of lotion. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it was fun to play with. Tim went to his friend, Sally, and said, "Look what I found! Let's play with it!" Sally looked at the lotion and said, "Okay, let's complete our game first." So, they played their game and had lots of fun. After they finished, they started to play with the lotion. They put the lotion on their hands and faces. It felt nice and soft. But soon, their skin started to hurt. They didn't know that the lotion was not good for them. Tim and Sally cried because they were in pain. Their moms came and took them home, but they were still sad and hurt. The lotion was not fun after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play in the kitchen with her mom. One day, she found a big, sour lemon. Sue wanted to make lemon juice, so she asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Okay, let's use a teaspoon to get the juice out." Sue and her mom started to squeeze the lemon. They got a lot of juice. Sue wanted to make the juice sweet, so she asked her mom to add sugar. Her mom said, "Let's melt the sugar in the juice." They put the juice and sugar in a pot and heated it up. The juice got very hot. Sue and her mom forgot to watch the pot. The juice melted the teaspoon! The pot got too hot and burned the juice. Sue and her mom were sad because they couldn't drink the lemon juice. The end.
