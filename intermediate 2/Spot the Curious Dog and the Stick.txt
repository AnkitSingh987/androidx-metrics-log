Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Curious Dog and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a curious little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and search for things in the park. One sunny day, Spot went to the park with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue and Spot played a game of hide and seek. Sue would hide, and Spot would search for her. They played and played, and Spot always found Sue. They were both very happy. Then, Sue had an idea. She told Spot to search for a big stick. Spot looked and looked, but he could not find one. So, he asked a big tree to yield a stick for him. The tree shook its branches, and a big stick fell down. Spot took the stick to Sue, and they played fetch all day long.

Once there was a rare spider. He perched on the highest tree branch to watch the world go by. One day, a 3 year old girl noticed him. She started to climb up the tree to get a closer look. "Hello, spider," she said. The spider waved one of his legs at her. She giggled in delight. "What are you doing up here?" she asked. The spider replied, "I've become really rare and so I like to look out from the highest place I can find, to see what's happening down there." The little girl nodded. "That's really cool," she said. The spider thanked her for understanding and for taking the time to talk to him. They said goodbye before the little girl climbed down the tree again. From the top of the tree, the spider watched her. He realised that even though he was rare, it was still possible to make friends.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a little girl named Poppy. Poppy loved to play with her friends, the bugs. One day, she saw a tiny bug crawl on the ground. It was a spoiled bug because it did not want to share its food. Poppy said to the bug, "Why don't you share your food with your friends?" The bug looked at Poppy and said, "I don't want to share because I want all the food for myself." Poppy felt sad for the bug's friends who were hungry. Poppy had an idea. She showed the spoiled bug how much fun it was to share with friends. They played games, laughed, and shared their food. The bug learned that sharing was good and started to share its food with its friends. From that day on, the spoiled bug was not spoiled anymore. It shared its food and played with its friends. Poppy was happy to see the bug change and be a good friend to others. And they all lived happily in the big garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to read her big, thick book. The book had many pages with fun pictures and stories. Sue would sit and read her book every day. One day, Sue met a new friend named Tim. Tim saw Sue with her thick book and asked, "What is your book's name?" Sue smiled and said, "My book's name is 'Fun Stories for Kids'! It has many pages with great stories. Do you want to read it with me?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, I would love to!" So, Sue and Tim sat down under a big tree and read the thick book together. They turned the pages and laughed at the funny stories. They had a great time, and from that day on, Sue and Tim became best friends. They loved to read and play together, and they shared many adventures with their big, thick book.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play the flute. She would play it every day and make sweet sounds. All the people in the village liked to hear her play. One day, Lily found a big, dead tree. She thought it would be a good place to sit and play her flute. But when she played her flute near the tree, something unexpected happened. The tree started to shake and make strange noises. Lily was scared, but she kept playing her flute. Then, the tree began to change. It wasn't a dead tree anymore! It turned into a big, happy tree with lots of green leaves. The tree could dance and sing along with Lily's flute. The whole village came to see the magic tree and Lily's flute. They all danced together and lived happily ever after.
