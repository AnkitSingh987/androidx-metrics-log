Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Clock

One day, a little dog named Spot went out to play. He was very happy and excited to find new things. Spot liked to dig in the dirt and look for fun stuff. While he was digging, Spot found an alarm clock. It was big and red. Spot was anxious because he had never seen an alarm before. He didn't know what it was, so he took it to his friend, Cat. Cat knew a lot of things and could help him. Spot showed the alarm to Cat. Cat said, "This is an alarm! It makes a loud noise to wake you up." Spot was surprised. He didn't know that alarms could do that. They played with the alarm and had fun. Then, the alarm went off and made a big noise. Spot and Cat laughed and laughed. The alarm wasn't scary after all.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the farm with his mom. They saw many animals and plants. Tim saw wheat growing in the field. It was tall and golden. Tim thought it was incredible. Tim's mom said, "We can pick some wheat and take it home." Tim was very happy. He picked up a lot of wheat and put it in a big bag. Then, they heard a voice. It was a little bird. "Please, can you allow me to have some wheat too?" asked the bird. Tim and his mom were surprised. They did not know that birds could talk! But they were kind, so they said, "Yes, you can have some wheat." The bird took some wheat and flew away. Suddenly, the bird changed into a fairy! She said, "Thank you for being kind. I will give you a wish." Tim and his mom were amazed. They wished for more incredible days like this one.

Lila and Ben were playing with their toys in the backyard. They had a lot of fun things to do, like making sand castles, riding bikes, and blowing bubbles. But they wanted to try something new and original. "Let's make a whip!" Lila said. "We can attach a rope to a stick and swing it around." "That sounds cool!" Ben agreed. "But where can we find a rope and a stick?" They looked around and saw a clothesline with some clothespins and a broom leaning against the wall. They ran over and took what they needed. "Here, you hold the stick and I'll tie the rope to it," Lila said. She made a knot and then pulled the rope to make sure it was tight. "OK, now let's see how it works," Ben said. He swung the stick and the rope in the air, making a whooshing sound. "Wow, it works!" Lila exclaimed. "Can I try it too?" "Sure, here you go," Ben said, handing her the whip. Lila took it and swung it as well, feeling very proud of their invention. They took turns playing with the whip, pretending to be cowboys and cowgirls, until they heard their mom calling them for lunch. "Time to eat, kids!" Mom said. "What have you been doing?" "We made a whip!" Lila and Ben said together, showing her their toy. "A whip? How did you make that?" Mom asked, surprised. "We attached a rope to a stick," Lila explained. "It was our original idea." Mom smiled and praised them for their creativity. "That's very clever of you," she said. "But be careful not to hurt yourselves or the clothes with it, OK?" "OK, Mom," Lila and Ben said. They put the whip away and ran inside to wash their hands and eat their lunch. They were happy and hungry after their fun adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely boy named Tim. He had no friends to play with. One day, he found a big screen in his house. Tim did not know what the screen was for. He touched the screen and something unexpected happened. The screen talked to Tim! It said, "Hi Tim, I am your friend. Let's play together!" Tim was so happy to gain a new friend. They played games on the screen and laughed together. Tim was not lonely anymore. One day, Tim's mom saw him playing with the screen. She said, "Tim, you need to go outside and play with other kids too." Tim listened to his mom and went outside. He met a girl named Sue. They became friends and played together every day. Tim was happy to have friends both on the screen and outside.
