Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Surf

Lily liked to surf. She had a big board and a wet suit. She went to the beach with her dad. He helped her catch the waves. She felt happy and brave. One day, a big wave came. Lily wanted to surf it. She paddled hard and stood up. But the wave was too fast and too strong. It pushed Lily off her board. She fell into the water. She could not see or breathe. She was scared. Lily's dad saw her fall. He swam to her. He grabbed her and pulled her up. He hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. I'm here. You're safe." He took her back to the shore. Lily was wet and cold. She was also embarrassed. She saw other surfers and people on the beach. They looked at her. Some of them laughed. Lily felt like a baby. She started to cry. She did not want to surf anymore. She wanted to go home. She hated the beach. She hated the wave. She hated herself.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing outside. She saw the sun shine very bright. She felt a little uncomfortable because it was very hot. Mia thought, "I need to find a way to cool down." Mia had an idea. She went inside her house to find her mom. She asked, "Mom, can we make a cold dessert to eat?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, let's make ice cream!" Mia was so happy and clapped her hands. They made the ice cream and put it in the freezer. Mia waited and waited. Finally, it was time to eat the ice cream! Mia and her mom sat outside under a big tree. The ice cream was very cold and yummy. Mia felt much better and not uncomfortable anymore. The sun still shined, but Mia had found a fun way to stay cool.

Once there was a leopard who needed to find something fun. The leopard had no idea what would make her happy. So she set off on an adventure to find something funny. As she walked, she heard the birds chirping and the bees buzzing. They all sounded so happy. But still, the leopard didn't feel any better. Finally, she reached a tall tree. At the top was a funny monkey. When the leopard saw the monkey, she started to giggle. The monkey was so silly and goofy. She knew this was just what she needed to make her happy. So the leopard and the monkey started playing. They laughed and giggled all day. By the time night came, the leopard was so happy. She had found something fun.

Once upon a time, there was a rat. The rat lived in a big house. The rat liked to play with the toys in the house. The toys were easy to play with. The rat was happy. One day, the rat met a cat. The cat wanted to play with the rat. But the rat did not want to play with the cat. They disagree on playing together. The rat was scared of the cat. The cat was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came into the house. The dog wanted to catch the cat. The rat decided to help the cat. The rat and the cat became friends. They worked together to hide from the dog. The dog went away. The rat and the cat were safe. The rat and the cat learned that it is good to be friends and help each other. They played together every day. They were happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys all day. His room was always messy. One day, his mom told him to clean his room. Tim did not like to clean, but he wanted his mom to be happy. While Tim was cleaning, he found an open box under his bed. Inside the box, there was a big red button. Tim was curious and pressed the button. Suddenly, his toys came to life! The toys started to clean the room for him. Tim was very surprised but happy. As the toys cleaned the room, they found another open box. This box had a green button inside. Tim pressed the green button, and the toys went back to being normal toys. Now, Tim's room was clean, and his mom was happy. Tim learned that cleaning can be fun and sometimes lead to unexpected surprises.
