Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Carl

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin was an independent fish who liked to swim in the ocean. One day, the tide came in and pushed Fin close to the shore. As Fin swam near the shore, he met a crab named Carl. Carl said, "Hello, Fin! Be careful not to bite the rocks!" Fin replied, "Thank you, Carl! I will be careful." They became friends and played together in the water. The tide went out and they said goodbye. Fin swam back into the deep ocean, happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a colorful bird. The bird liked to fly and play with his friends. One day, the bird saw a shiny thing in the dust. It was a key. The bird thought it was pretty and wanted to borrow it. The bird took the key to his friend, the mouse. The mouse said, "This key is not ours. We should give it back." But the bird did not listen. He wanted to keep the key and play with it. So, the bird and the mouse played with the key all day long. As the sun went down, the owner of the key came looking for it. It was a big, angry dog. The dog saw the bird and the mouse with his key and got very mad. The bird and the mouse were scared. They tried to run away, but the dog caught them. The dog was so mad that he did not let the bird and the mouse go. The bird and the mouse learned that they should not borrow things that do not belong to them.

Once upon a time, there was a little red wagon. The red wagon was in the yard. It loved to play with the kids. One day, the red wagon saw a big, shiny thing in the sky. It was not the sun. The red wagon wanted to look at it, but it had to blink. It was too bright. The red wagon told the kids about the shiny thing. The kids wanted to find it too. They all went on a trip to find the shiny thing. The red wagon was very happy. They walked and walked, but they did not find the shiny thing. The red wagon was sad. Then, one day, the shiny thing came back. It was closer this time. The red wagon and the kids ran to it. They found a big, shiny rock. The rock was from the sky. The red wagon and the kids were very happy. They put the rock in the red wagon and took it home. The red wagon was so proud to have the shiny rock from the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little computer. The computer was fragile and needed to be careful. It lived in a house with a girl named Lily. One day, Lily wanted to dance. She asked the computer, "Can you play music for me to dance?" The computer said, "Yes, I can play music!" So, the computer played a fun song and Lily started to dance. As Lily danced, she was very careful not to bump the fragile computer. They had a great time together. The computer played more songs and Lily danced and danced. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous piano. It was in a big room. A little girl named Amy loved to play it. She would set on the chair and play fun songs. The piano made her happy. One day, Amy went to play the piano. She set on the chair and started to play. But the chair was old and not good. It began to break. Amy was sad but kept playing. Then, the chair broke. Amy fell down and cried. The enormous piano was quiet. She could not play anymore. The end was bad and Amy was sad.

Once there was a boy. He had a friend who was a bird. Together they went down to the park. When they arrived, the boy saw a big bench. He went over and examined it. It was painted yellow, and it was very heavy. The boy turned to his bird friend and asked, "Can you turn this bench around?" His friend replied, "No, it’s too heavy. I can’t do it by myself." The boy looked around and saw some other children playing nearby. He went over to them and said, "Do you know how to turn this bench around? We need help." The children agreed to help. They all moved together and turned the heavy bench. The boy thanked the children, and he and his bird friend went on their way.
