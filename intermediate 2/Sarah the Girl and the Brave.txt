Category: Intermediate
Title: Sarah the Girl and the Brave

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sarah. She was 3 years old and very brave. One day she went to the doctor's office with her parents. She was feeling a little frightened, but she was brave. The doctor said they were going to do an x-ray, so Sarah went into a big room with all sorts of machines. She felt a bit scared, but she was brave. Just then, a bright light appeared! It was very bright and Sarah was frightened. The doctor told Sarah to stay still. He said the x-ray would help him see inside her body. She was still feeling a little scared, but she was brave. After the x-ray was done, Sarah saw the doctor looking at the pictures. He said she was all okay and he showed her the x-rays. She smiled - she was very brave!

Once upon a time, in a small, modern house, there lived a big, happy family. They loved to play together and share their toys. They always had peace in their home. One day, Mom said, "Kids, we will split the big cake for dessert tonight. But first, you must clean your rooms." The kids were excited and went to clean their rooms. As they were cleaning, the littlest one found a magic stone. He showed it to his brother and sister. They didn't know what it was, but it looked fun. They started to fight over who could hold it. Suddenly, the magic stone made a loud noise, and it split the house in two! The kids were scared and called for Mom. "Mom, help! The magic stone split our house!" Mom came running and saw the big mess. She was very sad and said, "Oh no, our peace is gone." Now, the family had to live in a broken house. They learned that fighting over things could cause bad things to happen. They promised to share and be kind, but their house was never the same again.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy soap. Sally saw a big soap with a happy face on it. She wanted it, so her mom said, "Okay, you can lift it and put it in the cart." As they walked around the store, Sally started to talk to the soap. "Hi, Mr. Soap! I'm happy to take you home with me!" she said. But Mr. Soap looked sad. Sally asked, "Why are you sad, Mr. Soap?" Mr. Soap said, "I'm sad because I don't want to leave my soap friends at the store." Just then, something unexpected happened. All the other soaps in the store started to sing a happy song. They sang, "Don't be sad, Mr. Soap! We will all go to new homes and make new friends." Hearing the song, Mr. Soap smiled and wasn't sad anymore. Sally was happy too. They went home, and Mr. Soap made friends with all the toys in Sally's bath. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a pretty girl named Lily went to the kitchen. She wanted to make a yummy muffin. She put all the things she needed in a big bowl. She mixed and mixed until it was ready. Lily's mom helped her put the muffin in the hot oven. After some time, they smelled something. "Oh no!" said Mom. "The muffin is starting to burn!" They opened the oven and saw the muffin was too dark. Mom and Lily took the muffin out of the oven. They were sad but then they laughed. "It's okay," said Lily. "We can try to make another muffin later." They ate the burnt muffin together and it still tasted good.

One day, a big vehicle was on the road. It was red and shiny. It went fast. The people in the vehicle were happy. They were going to the park. They wanted to play. At the park, they saw a little girl. She was crying. "My leg is hurt," she said. They wanted to help her. They asked her, "What happened?" The little girl said, "I twist my leg when I play." Then, a big wind came. The wind was strong. It picked up the vehicle and took it away. The people and the little girl were very surprised. They did not know what to do. The wind put the vehicle in a big tree. The people climbed the tree and got the vehicle back. They were happy again. They took the little girl to her mom. Her mom said, "Thank you for helping." They all played together at the park.
