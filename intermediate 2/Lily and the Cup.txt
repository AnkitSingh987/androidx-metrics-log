Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play at the park. One day, Lily found a cup. The cup was flexible. She thought it was a fun toy to play with. Lily took the cup to the park with her. She showed all her friends the flexible cup. They played catch with it. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A bird flew down and took the flexible cup away. Lily and her friends were sad. They looked for the bird and the cup. They found the bird in a tree. The bird was using the flexible cup as a nest for her eggs. Lily and her friends were happy to see the cup being used in a new way. They played at the park together and made new friends with the bird and her babies.

Once upon a time there was a farmer. He lived on his own in a very beautiful landscape. He had many healthy fruit trees that he sold for money. Eating the fruit also made him very healthy. One day the farmer found a friend, a little baby goat. The goat was very happy in his beautiful landscape and he liked to munch on the fruit from the trees. The farmer was so happy to have the goat that he decided to keep him forever. The goat ate lots of fruit from the trees and he soon became very big and strong. The farmer wanted to give the goat a special treat so he sold some of his fruit trees to buy him a big bag of hay. The goat loved the hay, he was healthy and very happy. He played in the beautiful landscape with the farmer and they were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a white boat. It liked to sail on the big blue sea. One day, the boat met a girl. The girl liked to play with makeup. The boat and the girl became friends. The boat told the girl, "I want to sail far away to find a big treasure." The girl said, "I will help you. I will use my makeup to make a map." So, the girl used her makeup to draw a map on the boat's sail. The map showed where the treasure was. The boat and the girl sailed far away. They followed the map on the sail. At last, they found the treasure. It was a big box full of makeup. The girl was very happy. The boat and the girl sailed back home with their treasure. They played with the makeup and had lots of fun.

One day, a small cat named Mimi went for a walk. She saw a mask on the ground. Mimi picked it up and put it in her bag. She thought it might help her later. Mimi walked more and saw a big dog. The dog was sad. Mimi asked, "Why are you sad?" The dog said, "I lost my mask. I need it for my party tonight." Mimi thought about the mask in her bag. Mimi said, "I can help!" She took the mask out of her bag and gave it to the dog. The dog was happy. They went to the party together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a furry cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to play with her ball and eat yummy butter. One day, Mimi saw a big jar of butter on the table. She wanted to take it and eat it all. "Meow," said Mimi to her friend, the little bird. "I want to take the butter from the table. Can you help me?" The little bird thought for a moment and said, "Okay, Mimi. I will help you. But we must be quick and quiet." Mimi and the little bird worked together to take the butter from the table. Mimi jumped high, and the little bird flew up to push the jar. The jar fell to the floor and broke. The butter was all over the floor, and Mimi was very happy. But then, Mimi's mom came into the room. She saw the mess and said, "Mimi, what have you done? You should not take things that are not yours." Mimi felt sad and said, "I am sorry, Mom. I will not take things again." From that day on, Mimi learned to ask for help and not to take things that were not hers. She and the little bird stayed good friends and played together every day. And sometimes, Mimi's mom would give her a little butter as a treat.
