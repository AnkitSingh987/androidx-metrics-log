Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hippo Was Playing and the Bird

One day, a little hippo was playing near the water. He was feeling very tired and wanted to take a nap. So, he found a soft spot under a big tree and lay down to sleep. While the hippo was napping, a tiny bird flew by and saw him. The bird felt scared because she had never seen a hippo before. She flew closer to the hippo and said, "Hello, big animal! Are you a monster? You look so big and scary!" The hippo woke up and saw the frightened bird. He smiled and said, "No, I am not a monster. I am a hippo. I was just taking a nap. Do not be scared of me." The tiny bird felt better and sat down next to the hippo. They became friends and played together until the sun went down. From that day on, the bird was not frightened of the hippo anymore, and they had many fun days together.

Once, there was a balloon. It was blue and round, floating in the air. It was so cool! Two friends saw it and they ran over to it. One of they said, "Let's get it!" The other friend said, "Yes! We can play with it." So, they both grabbed the balloon and started to play. They ran around, laughing and having fun. After a while, the balloon got dirty. One of the friends said, "We should clean the balloon." The other one said, "Yes, let's do it!" Then, they both began to clean the balloon. They used their hands and lots of water to make it look nice and clean. And when they were done, they had the coolest balloon ever! They thanked each other, hugged and had a big party with their colorful balloon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to polish her toy car. She would make it shine and sparkle. One day, her mom made a pie. Lucy was very excited to eat the pie. It smelled so good! Lucy's mom put the pie on the table to cool down. Lucy wanted to help, so she started to polish the pie. She thought it would make the pie taste even better. But the pie did not shine like her toy car. Lucy was confused. Her mom saw what Lucy was doing and laughed. She told Lucy that pies don't need to be polished. They are already yummy. Lucy laughed too and they both enjoyed the pie together. And from that day on, Lucy only polished her toy car.

Tom and Lily are playing with their toys in the living room. Tom has a car and Lily has a doll. They like to make noises and pretend they are driving or talking. "Vroom, vroom, my car is fast and red!" Tom says. "My doll is pretty and soft, she has a dress and a hat!" Lily says. They see a rubber band on the floor. It is smooth and stretchy. They are curious about it. "What is this?" Tom asks, picking it up. "It is a rubber, you can make things with it," Lily says. "Like what?" Tom asks. Lily thinks for a moment. She remembers what her mom told her once. "You can make a bracelet, or a ball, or a slingshot," she says. "How do you make a slingshot?" Tom asks. Lily tries to explain. She finds a stick and a rock. She ties the rubber band to the stick. She puts the rock in the middle of the rubber band. She pulls the rubber band back and lets it go. "Wow, that is cool!" Tom says. The rock flies across the room and hits a vase. The vase falls and breaks. They hear their mom coming. "Uh-oh, we are in trouble!" Lily says. They hide behind the couch and hope their mom will not find them.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was tired from playing all day. He put on his uniform to go to school. On his way, he saw a big ball. Tom loved to play with balls. Tom said to the ball, "Let's roll and have fun!" The ball did not talk back, but Tom pushed it and they started to roll together. They rolled and rolled, laughing and having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball stopped rolling and started to talk! The ball said, "Thank you, Tom, for playing with me. I was lonely and now I am happy!" Tom was surprised but happy to have a new friend. They played together every day after that.
