Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

One day, a boy named Tim walked in the gloomy woods. He saw a big tree with a pattern on it. The pattern looked like a face. Tim was scared but he wanted to know more. Tim asked the tree, "Why do you have a face?" The tree said, "I am a magic tree. I can talk! But I am sad because my leaves rot and fall off." Tim wanted to help the tree. He said, "I will find a way to make you happy." Tim left the woods and asked his mom for help. His mom gave him a big bag of seeds. Tim went back to the magic tree and said, "I have seeds for you. When your leaves rot, new ones will grow." The tree was happy and thanked Tim. From that day on, the tree and Tim were best friends.

Tom is a little boy who likes to learn new things. He goes to school with his friends and listens to his teacher. His teacher gives lectures about animals, colors, numbers and letters. Tom likes to raise his hand and answer questions. One day, his teacher tells them they will learn about plants. She shows them pictures of flowers, trees, grass and fruits. She asks them to name the colors and shapes they see. Tom knows many colors and shapes. He says red, yellow, blue, green, orange, purple, pink, white, black, brown, circle, square, triangle, star and heart. Then, his teacher takes them outside to the garden. She says they will learn more about plants by looking at them. She tells them to be careful and not to touch or pick the plants. She says some plants have thorns, spikes, or poison that can hurt them. She shows them how to open their eyes and observe the plants. She asks them to find the different parts of the plants, like the roots, the stem, the leaves, the petals and the seeds. Tom is very curious and excited. He opens his eyes wide and looks at the plants. He sees a rose with red petals and green leaves. He sees a sunflower with yellow petals and a brown center. He sees a cactus with spikes and a pink flower. He sees a carrot with orange roots and green leaves. He sees a tomato with red fruits and green stems. He learns many new words and facts about plants. He thanks his teacher for the lecture and the garden visit. He says he likes to learn about plants. He says he wants to grow his own plants at home. His teacher smiles and says he can do that. She gives him a small pot, some soil, some seeds and some water. She tells him to plant the seeds, water them and put them in a sunny place. She says he will see them grow and change. Tom is very happy and eager to try. He says he will take good care of his plants and learn more about them. He says he loves school and his teacher.

Once upon a time, there was a new lawyer named Tom. He liked to help people. One day, Tom heard a loud cry. He went to see what was wrong. Tom saw a little girl named Lucy. She was sad because she lost her toy. Tom said, "Don't cry, Lucy. I will help you find your toy." They looked everywhere for the toy. They looked in the park, but it was not there. They looked near the big tree, but it was not there. Then, they heard a sound in the bushes. They found a big dog with the toy in its mouth. The dog ran away with the toy, and they could not catch it. Lucy cried again, and Tom felt sad because he could not help her.

One day, a cheerful dog named Spot found a chain in the park. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his best friend, a little girl named Sue. Spot wanted to play with the chain, so he showed it to Sue. "Let's play with the chain!" Spot insisted. Sue agreed and they started to play a game. They took turns pulling the chain to see who could pull it the longest. They had a lot of fun playing with the chain, but they didn't know that something special was about to happen. As they played, a big wind came and blew the chain high into the sky. Spot and Sue watched as the chain flew away. They were sad that their game was over, but then, the chain came back down and landed in a tree. When the chain landed, it made a loud noise and a hidden door in the tree opened. Spot and Sue looked at each other, excited about their new discovery. They went inside the hidden door and found a secret room filled with toys. They played together all day, happy that the chain had led them to this special place.
