Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Box

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big brown machine in his yard. He did not know what it was, but he wanted to find out. He looked at the machine and saw a seat, wheels, and a button. He thought, "Maybe I can drive this machine." Tim sat on the seat and pushed the button. The brown machine started to move. He was driving it! Tim drove the machine around his yard and saw his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam! I can drive this brown machine!" said Tim. Sam smiled and said, "Wow, Tim! That's fun! Can I try?" Tim let Sam drive the brown machine, and they had a great time. After a while, they found out the machine could dig holes too! They dug a big hole and found a box with toys inside. The brown machine was a fun surprise for both of them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a pet cricket named Jim. Jim was a reliable friend. Tom did not like to be alone, so he always took Jim with him. One day, Tom and Jim went to the park. They saw a big boy named Sam. Sam was not nice. He said, "I hate crickets!" Tom got scared. He knew that something bad might happen to Jim. So, Tom and Jim went home. The next day, Tom and Jim went to the park again. They saw Sam again. But this time, Sam was sad. He had lost his toy. Tom wanted to help. He asked Jim to find the toy. Jim was a reliable cricket, so he found it. Sam was happy. He said, "Thank you, Tom and Jim. I don't hate crickets anymore." Tom and Jim had a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally had a big toy box with many toys. She loved her toys very much. Her favorite toy was a doll named Lucy. Lucy had an incredible dress that Sally liked to wrap around her. One day, Sally's mom told her to clean her room. But Sally did not want to clean her room. She wanted to play with her toys. So, she played with Lucy and did not clean her room. When her mom saw the messy room, she was not happy. Sally's mom said that Sally must be punished for not cleaning her room. She told Sally that she could not play with Lucy for one whole day. Sally was sad, but she knew she should have listened to her mom. The next day, Sally cleaned her room and promised to always listen to her mom.

Once upon a time, a purple bird lived in a big tree. The bird loved to sing and play all day long. Every morning, the sun would come up and the bird would sing a happy song to comfort all the other animals in the forest. One day, the sun did not come up. The sky was dark and the animals were scared. The purple bird tried to sing a happy song, but it was hard to comfort the animals without the sun. The bird decided to fly up high to find the sun. As the purple bird flew higher and higher, it saw something strange. The sun was hiding behind a big cloud! The bird had an idea. It flew around the cloud and pushed it away. The sun came out and all the animals were happy again. The purple bird sang its happy song and everyone knew that they could always count on their friend, the purple bird, to comfort them.

Once upon a time, there was a lady who had a smooth, round ball. She loved to play with the ball every day. She would bounce the ball in her yard, and it made her very happy. One day, the smooth ball got away from the lady. It bounced and bounced down the street. The lady ran after the ball, but it was too fast for her. She was sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a nice dog saw the lady and the bouncing ball. The dog ran fast and caught the ball in its mouth. The dog brought the smooth ball back to the lady. She was so happy and thanked the dog. From that day on, the lady and the dog played together, bouncing the ball and having lots of fun.
