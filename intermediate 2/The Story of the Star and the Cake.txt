Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Star and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a calm sky with many stars. One star was very special. The star was yellow and shiny. It loved to watch the people below. One day, the star saw a girl. The girl was playing with a big bowl. She was mixing something. The star wanted to know what the girl was doing. So, the star went down to the girl to see. The girl was mixing a cake. She saw the star and smiled. She asked the star if it wanted to help her mix the cake. The star was happy and helped the girl. They mixed the cake together and had a fun day.

---

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a calm little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch the automobiles go by his house. One day, a red automobile arrived in front of his house. The door opened, and out came his friend, Sam. "Hi, Tim!" said Sam. "Want to play?" "Yes!" said Tim, and they played all day. As the sun went down, Sam's mom called, "Time to go home, Sam!" Sam got back into the red automobile, and they drove away. Tim waved goodbye, excited for the next time Sam would arrive to play again.

---

Tom was walking through the woods and he saw a little girl. She was standing near a deep, dark well. "What are you doing here?" Tom asked. The little girl smiled and nodded. Tom stepped closer to get a better look and noticed the little girl was throwing stones into the well. He asked her why she was doing it. The little girl said, "I am trying to make a wish. I heard that if you make a wish and throw a stone in the well, your wish will come true!" Tom smiled and nodded. He remembered hearing the same story when he was a child. The little girl looked fierce, she launched the stone with full force into the well and boldly said "I wish for all the fishes to swim free!" Tom nodded and said "That is a very good wish!" and they both walked away.

Once upon a time, there was a thin helicopter. It could fly high in the sky. It was very good at helping people. One day, the helicopter was flying and saw a broken bike. The helicopter wanted to help. The helicopter went down to the ground. It used its tools to repair the bike. The bike was fixed and looked like new. The little boy who owned the bike was very happy. He said "Thank you, helicopter!" The thin helicopter felt proud. It knew that it could help people and repair things. It flew back up into the sky, looking for more people to help. And everyone loved the helpful helicopter.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a hose in the yard. Tim wanted to do something fun with the hose. He thought and thought about what he could do. Tim had an idea. He asked his friend Sam to come over. "Hey Sam, want to play with the hose?" Sam said, "Yes, let's do it!" They turned on the water and started to play. They sprayed the water in the air and laughed as it rained down on them. But then, Tim's mom came outside. She looked worried. "Boys, be careful with the hose!" she said. Tim and Sam stopped playing for a moment. They didn't want Tim's mom to be worried. They promised to be careful and keep having fun. And that's what they did.

Once upon a time, there was a little figure. The figure was very good and liked to help. It lived in a small house with a big tree outside. One day, the figure went outside to play. It saw a cat stuck in the tree. The cat said, "Help me, please!" The good figure wanted to help the cat. The figure thought of a plan. It found a big box and put it under the tree. The cat jumped down onto the box. The cat said, "Thank you!" The good figure was happy to help, and they became friends.
