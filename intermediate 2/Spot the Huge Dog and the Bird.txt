Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Huge Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Spot. Spot lived in a big house with his friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim and Spot liked to play together every day. One day, Tim and Spot found a big box. Tim said, "Let's print a picture on the box." So, they printed a picture of a big, happy sun. They were very proud of their picture. Later, Spot saw a little bird trying to escape from a small cage. Spot said, "Let's help the bird!" Tim and Spot opened the cage and the bird flew away. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim and Spot!" And they all played together in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She was a very nice girl, but sometimes she could be a little rude. One day, Lily saw a pretty veil in a shop. She really wanted that veil. Lily asked her mom if she could have the veil. Her mom said no because it was too much money. Lily was sad and angry. She did not want to listen to her mom. She wanted the veil very much. Lily decided to take the veil without asking. She was very rude to the shop man. She ran away with the veil. The shop man was very sad. In the end, Lily got in big trouble and had to give the veil back. She was not happy and had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a big squash. The squash lived in a garden. It was a brilliant squash. It was very big and very round. All the kids loved the squash. One day, the squash heard a ring. The squash did not know what the ring was. It looked around the garden. The squash saw a small bell. The small bell was making the ring sound. The squash liked the sound. The squash and the small bell became friends. They played together in the garden. But one day, the squash found out that the small bell was a magic bell. The magic bell could make the squash talk. The squash was very happy. Now, it could talk to all the kids who loved it. And they all lived happily in the garden.

Once upon a time, in a large sky, there was a comet. The comet was very big and bright. It moved fast across the sky. Every day, the comet would pass by the Earth. The people on Earth liked to watch it. They would point and say, "Look! There's the comet!" One day, a little boy named Tim saw the comet. Tim began to worry. He thought that the large comet might hit the Earth. He told his mom and dad about his worry. They told him not to worry, but Tim still felt scared. Tim decided to talk to his friend, the wise old owl. The owl listened to Tim's worry. He told Tim that the comet was just a part of the sky and would not hurt the Earth. The owl said, "Do not worry about things you cannot change. Worrying will not make them go away." Tim felt better after talking to the owl. He learned that worrying about things he could not change was not helpful. From that day on, Tim would watch the comet and enjoy its beauty without worrying. The moral of the story is: do not worry about things you cannot change.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a kind witch. She loved to polish her big, smooth stone. The witch used the stone to make magic spells. One day, a little boy came to her house. He was sad because he lost his toy. The witch wanted to help the boy. She took her smooth stone and started to polish it. As she polished the stone, she said a magic spell. In a flash, the boy's toy appeared! The boy was very happy. He thanked the witch and went home to play with his toy. From that day on, the witch and the boy became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little room, there was a candle. The candle was very small and had a twist in the middle. It wanted to shine bright and light up the room. One day, a girl named Lucy came into the room. She saw the twisted candle and thought it was special. Lucy wanted to help the candle shine. She went to find a match to light the candle. Lucy lit the twisted candle and the room became bright. The candle was so happy to shine for Lucy. They played and laughed together in the quiet room, and the little twisted candle felt important and loved.
