Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Puzzle

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with shapes. She had a toy box full of different shapes like circles, squares, and triangles. One day, Lily found a puzzle with many shapes. She wanted to solve the puzzle, but it was very hard. Lily tried and tried, but she could not solve it. She felt sad and wanted to give up. Then, Lily's mom came and saw the spoiled puzzle. She told Lily not to give up and to keep trying. Lily listened to her mom and tried again. This time, she solved the puzzle! Lily was so happy, and her mom was proud of her. They both celebrated with a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved the color purple. One day, she found a big box of purple toys. She was so happy! In the box, there were real toys and fake toys. Sue wanted to separate the real toys from the fake toys. So, she put the real toys in one pile and the fake toys in another pile. After Sue separated the toys, she played with the real purple toys all day. She had so much fun! Sue learned that even though some toys were fake, she could still enjoy her favorite color. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who loved to paint. He painted all day and all night. One day, he wanted to paint a big tree. But he needed help to get the paint to the tip of the tree. He asked his friend, the tall man, to help him. The tall man said, "I will help you if you give me some of your paint." The wealthy man did not want to share his paint, so he said no. The tall man was sad and went away. The wealthy man tried to paint the tree by himself, but he could not reach the tip. He thought about his friend and felt bad. He went to find the tall man and said, "I am sorry. I will share my paint with you. Will you help me now?" The tall man was happy and said yes. They painted the tree together, and it was the most beautiful tree in the land.

Once upon a time, there was a pale monkey. The monkey liked to tie things. One day, the monkey was in the park. He saw a boy with a big red ball. The monkey wanted to play with the ball too. The boy saw the monkey and got scared. He let go of the ball and it rolled away. The monkey ran after the ball. He wanted to tie it to a tree so it would not roll away again. The monkey tied the ball to the tree with a long string. Just then, a girl came to the park. She saw the monkey and the ball tied to the tree. She laughed and clapped her hands. The monkey and the girl played with the ball together. The boy came back and saw them playing. He was not scared anymore. The monkey, the girl, and the boy all became friends and played together every day. And the pale monkey was happy to tie things for his new friends.

One day, a little rat named Remy was playing hide and seek with his friend, Tom the cat. Remy was very good at hiding, but Tom was very careful when looking for him. Remy found a big box and decided to hide inside. He waited and waited for Tom to find him. Tom looked high and low for Remy. He asked a bird, "Have you seen Remy the rat?" The bird said, "No, but I will help you look for him." They looked in the grass, under the rocks, and behind the trees. Then, Tom saw the big box and thought Remy might be inside. Tom opened the box to reveal Remy, who was giggling. "Found you!" said Tom. Remy laughed and said, "You are really good at this game, Tom!" They played hide and seek all day, having lots of fun together.
