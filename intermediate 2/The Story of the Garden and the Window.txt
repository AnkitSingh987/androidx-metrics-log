Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Garden and the Window

Once there was a small girl who was very deaf. She couldn't hear very well, so she felt alone and scared. One day she decided to settle in a small cell. It was all her own so she felt safe and calm. The cell was cozy and had lots of soft blankets. She would spend many hours there, feeling safe and happy. Every day she would look out the window and admire the garden. One day she heard some new sounds. They were birds, who had come to visit the garden. Even though she couldn't hear them very well, she enjoyed watching them. She felt so happy that she had found her special place in the cell. Even though she was deaf, she was still able to enjoy the beauty of the world outside her cell. She settled in her cell, content and happy.

Lily loved to decorate. She liked to put stickers, ribbons and glitter on everything. She wanted to make her room look like a princess castle. She had a big poster of a princess on her wall. She thought the princess was very pretty and kind. One day, Lily found a can of paint in the garage. It was pink, her favorite color. She thought it would be fun to decorate her poster with the paint. She took the can and a brush and went to her room. She did not ask her mom or dad, because they were busy. She opened the can and dipped the brush in the paint. She started to paint over the princess's face, hair and dress. She thought she was making the princess more beautiful. She did not see that the paint was dripping on the floor, the bed and the wall. She did not hear her mom calling her name. Soon, her mom came to her room and saw the mess. She was very angry and sad. She shouted at Lily and took the can and the brush away. She told Lily that she had ruined the poster, the carpet and the furniture. She said that Lily had to clean up everything and that she could not decorate anything for a long time. Lily started to cry. She did not understand why her mom was so mad. She thought she was doing something good. She did not like the paint anymore. She wished she had never found it. She wished she could have the princess poster back. She felt very sorry and bad.

Once upon a time, there was a player. This player liked to run and play all day long. The player had a ball that he would kick and throw. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, the player was very tired. He had played so much that he needed to rest. He sat under a big tree and closed his eyes. The player could hear the birds sing and feel the wind blow. A little girl came to the park and saw the tired player. She asked if he wanted to play with her. The player said, "I am too tired to play right now." The little girl smiled and said, "That's okay. We can play later when you are not tired." The player smiled and thanked the girl. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, a soft bunny and a small soldier lived in a big forest. The soft bunny loved to hop and play all day. The small soldier was a toy, but he could talk and walk. One day, the soft bunny saw the small soldier sitting under a tree. "Hi, soldier!" said the soft bunny. "Do you want to play with me?" The small soldier smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" They played a fun game called "Twist and Jump." The soft bunny would twist and the small soldier would jump. They laughed and played all day. They became the best of friends and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a sleepy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big red house. Tom was curious and went inside the house. In the house, he found a little girl named Lucy. Lucy said, "Tom, I need help. I can't find my toy. Can you solve this problem?" Tom nodded and started to look for the toy. They looked in the rooms and under the beds, but they could not find the toy. Suddenly, they heard a noise from the kitchen. They went to see what it was. They saw a dog named Max, and he had Lucy's toy. Max said, "I'm sorry, I took your toy. I just wanted to play." Lucy smiled and said, "That's okay, Max. Let's all play together!" So Tom, Lucy, and Max played all day in the big red house.
