Category: Intermediate
Title: The Spark and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little spark. The spark lived in a big forest with many trees. The spark was always hungry. One day, the spark met a big tree. The tree said, "Hi, little spark! What do you want?" The spark said, "I am hungry. Can you help me?" The tree felt sad for the spark and said, "Yes, I can help you." The tree let the spark eat some of its leaves. The spark started to burn the leaves. The leaves turned into smoke and went up into the sky. The spark felt happy and not hungry anymore. The spark said, "Thank you, big tree, for helping me. Now, I am not hungry." The tree smiled and said, "You're welcome, little spark. I am happy to help you." From that day on, the spark and the tree were good friends. They lived happily in the big forest and helped each other when they needed it.

Lila and Tom were friends who liked to play music. Lila had a drum and Tom had a guitar. They would bang and strum and make a lot of noise. They did not care about rhythm, they just wanted to have fun. One day, they saw a poster for a talent show at the park. It said, "Show your skills and shine on stage! Win a prize!" Lila and Tom wanted to win the prize, so they decided to enter the talent show. They thought they were the best musicians in the world. They went to the park and saw many other kids with different talents. Some could sing, some could dance, some could do magic tricks. Lila and Tom felt nervous, but they did not practice. They thought they did not need to. When it was their turn, they went on stage and started to play their music. But they did not sound good. They were out of tune, out of sync, and out of control. The other kids laughed at them and booed them. Lila and Tom felt sad and ashamed. They ran off the stage and hid behind a tree. A kind lady came to them and said, "Don't be sad, kids. You have a lot of energy and passion, but you need to work on your rhythm. Rhythm is the beat that makes music sound nice and smooth. If you practice and listen to each other, you can improve your rhythm and play better music." Lila and Tom looked at each other and nodded. They thanked the lady and asked her for some tips. She showed them how to tap their feet, clap their hands, and count to four. She also gave them some smelly cheese to eat, because she said it was good for the voice. Lila and Tom ate the cheese and practiced their rhythm. They found out that playing music with rhythm was more fun and easier than playing without it. They also sounded better and felt happier. The next day, they went back to the park and asked the talent show organizer if they could play again. He agreed and gave them another chance. They went on stage and played their music with rhythm. The other kids cheered and clapped for them. Lila and Tom felt proud and happy. They won the prize, which was a big cake. They shared it with the kind lady and the other kids. They learned that rhythm is important for music and for friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship was very sleepy. It liked to rest on the water all day long. One day, a little bird came to the ship. The bird said, "Hello, sleepy ship! Why do you rest so much?" The ship replied, "I like to rest because it makes me feel good." The bird and the ship became friends. They played together every day. The bird liked to fly around the ship, and the ship liked to watch the bird. They were very happy. And when the sun went down, the sleepy ship would rest, and the little bird would rest too.

Once upon a time, there was a fast dog named Spot. Spot loved to race with his friends in the big park. One day, the sun was setting in the west, and Spot saw a big, red ball. Spot was very alert and wanted to play with the ball. He raced to the ball and pushed it with his nose. The ball rolled far away, and Spot chased it. He was having so much fun. Then, Spot's friend, a small cat named Fluffy, came to play. Fluffy was not as fast as Spot, but she wanted to race too. Spot learned to play slow so Fluffy could have fun. They played together and had a great time. The moral of the story is that being kind and playing together is more fun than winning a race.
