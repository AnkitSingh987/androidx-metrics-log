Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Ride and the Bike

Once upon a time there was a little girl. Every day she would ride on her bike by herself. She felt so independent and happy. One day, she noticed a leak coming from her bike. She stopped and said to herself, "Oh no! That's not good!" She looked around and realized she was far away from home. She started to get sad, but then she saw a kind-looking man. He asked her what was wrong. She told him about the leak and her worry that she wouldn't be able to ride back home. The man said he could help. She was so excited. He took the bike and fixed the leak. Now the girl could ride again. She thanked the man and happily rode back home.

One day, in the warm spring sun, a little rabbit named Remy and a squirrel named Sam were playing in the forest. They were best friends and loved to play together. They liked to jump, run, and climb trees. Remy wore a tight red scarf around his neck that his mom made for him. Remy asked Sam, "Do you want to climb the big tree today?" Sam was scared of the big tree, but he did not want to tell Remy. So, Sam said, "No, I want to play with my toys." Remy was sad but did not refuse his friend's wish. They went to Sam's house to play with his toys. As they played, Remy noticed that Sam looked sad. Remy asked, "Why are you sad, Sam?" Sam finally told Remy that he was scared of the big tree. Remy gave Sam a big hug and said, "It's okay to be scared. We can play here, and I will always be your friend." Sam smiled, and they continued to play together in the warm spring sun.

Once upon a time there was a girl who liked to play with her friends. One day they decided to make a present for their mom. They split the work. One friend painted a pretty picture. Another one found a delicious flower. And the last friend made a nice card. On the weekend their mom was so happy when she opened the gift! She said it was the best present ever. She also said the flower was especially delicious! The friends were very proud and happy. They had made a wonderful gift that made their mom smile.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia was adventurous and loved to play outside. One sunny day, Mia was playing in her room when she heard a sound from the living room. She decided to go and see what it was. Mia tiptoed to the living room and peeked around the corner. She saw her mom watching TV. Mia wanted to watch TV too, but her mom had told her it was time to play, not watch TV. Mia thought about it and decided to be a good girl and listen to her mom. Mia went back to her room and played with her toys. She had so much fun that she forgot about the TV. Later, her mom came in and gave her a big hug. She told Mia that she was proud of her for making a good choice. Mia felt happy and knew that listening to her mom was the right thing to do. And that is the story of how Mia learned the importance of obeying her mom and not watching TV when it was time to play.

Once upon a time there were two children, Jack and Jane. They wanted to go for a ride so they went to the park. Jane saw a zoom and wanted to go for a ride. She asked Jack, "Will you ride with me? Please?" Jack said, "Yes, let's go!" So they hopped on the zoom and it felt so good. The zoom went around in circles and they couldn't stop laughing. The wheels spun round and round, faster and faster. They were having fun! Suddenly, they felt a dull sound. The zoom stopped spinning. Jack and Jane got off and looked around. Then they saw the sun setting in the sky. Jack said, "Let's go." And so they did. Jack and Jane walked home together, holding hands and eating ice cream. They had a great time riding the zoom!
