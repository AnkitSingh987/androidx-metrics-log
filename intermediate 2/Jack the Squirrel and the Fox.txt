Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Squirrel and the Fox

Once upon a time there was a little squirrel named Jack. He was very small and had a big, fluffy tail. One day, he decided to go on a big adventure. Jack set off on a journey and in the forest he found a dark acorn. He was very excited and decided to try spinning it like a top. He tried and tried but he couldn't spin it. Just then, an old fox shouted, "What are you doing?!" Jack quickly replied, "I'm trying to spin this dark acorn like a top!" The old fox shook his head and said, " No, little one, you must use your hands and feet to spin it!" Jack looked confused but he followed the fox's instructions and soon he was spinning the acorn like a top. The old fox was very impressed and said,"Well done, little one!" Jack was so proud of himself and happy that his adventure had a happy ending. He skipped back home happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little flame. The flame lived in a big forest. The flame had no friends, so it was very sad. One day, a bird came to the forest. The bird saw the flame and said, "Hi flame, do you want to talk?" The flame was very grateful and said, "Yes, I want to talk. I am very lonely." As they talked, the wind blew very hard. The flame grew bigger and bigger. The bird said, "Oh no, the forest is in danger!" The flame was sad and said, "I don't want to hurt the forest." But the wind kept blowing and the flame got even bigger. The forest started to burn. All the animals ran away. The bird and the flame were very sad. They could not save the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a polite little girl named Mia. She loved to play in the earth, making mud pies and digging holes. One day, while she was playing, she found a shiny, round rock. Mia thought it was very pretty, so she picked it up and showed it to her mom. Her mom looked at the rock and said, "Mia, this is a magic rock. When you need something, just ask the rock and it will help you." Mia was very happy and took the rock with her everywhere she went. One day, Mia was playing with her friends and they needed a ball to play a game. Mia remembered her magic rock and asked it for a ball. Suddenly, the rock turned into a big, bouncy ball! All of Mia's friends were very surprised and happy. They played with the ball all day long. When they were done, Mia said "thank you" to the rock and it turned back into a shiny, round rock. From that day on, Mia always remembered to be polite and ask for help when she needed it.

Tom likes to watch his dad work on cars. His dad is a mechanic. He fixes cars that are broken or make loud noises. Tom thinks his dad is very smart and strong. One day, Tom and his dad go to the garage. There are many cars there. Some are shiny and new, some are old and dirty. Tom sees a big red car that has a dent on the side. He asks his dad, "What happened to this car?" His dad says, "This car had an accident. Someone hit it with another car. It was very scary and loud. But don't worry, I can make it better. I can fix the dent and the lights and the wheels. Then the car will be happy and safe again." Tom nods. He watches his dad take out some tools and start to work on the car. He sees his dad hammer and drill and screw and paint. He hears his dad hum a song. He smells the oil and the metal and the paint. He feels proud of his dad. After a while, the car looks like new. It is shiny and red and has no dent. Tom's dad says, "All done. See, Tom, I fixed the car. Now it is peaceful and quiet. The owner will be very happy. Do you like it?" Tom smiles. He says, "Yes, dad, I like it. You are the best mechanic. Can I help you with the next car?" His dad hugs him. He says, "Sure, Tom, you can help me. You are my best helper. I love you, son." They walk to the next car, holding hands. They are happy and peaceful. They like to watch and work on cars together.
