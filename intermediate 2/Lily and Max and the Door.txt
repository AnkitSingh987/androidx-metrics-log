Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Max and the Door

Lily and Max were playing in the yard when they saw a big van stop in front of their house. The van had a picture of a flower and the word "fragile" on it. Lily and Max wondered what was inside the van. "Maybe it has toys or candy," Max said. "Or maybe it has a new bike or a doll," Lily said. They ran to the van and tried to peek inside, but the door was locked. They knocked on the door, but no one answered. They heard a voice from the other side of the house. "Hey, kids, stay away from the van!" the voice said. It was their dad. He came around the corner with a big box in his arms. He looked tired and sweaty. "Dad, what's in the van?" Lily and Max asked. "It's a surprise for mom," dad said. "She's coming home from the hospital today. She had a baby, remember?" Lily and Max nodded. They were excited to meet their new brother or sister. "What's in the box?" Max asked. "It's a vase with flowers for mom," dad said. "It's very fragile, so be careful. Don't touch it or it might break." "Can we help you carry it?" Lily asked. "Sure, but you have to be very gentle and slow," dad said. He gave each of them a corner of the box and they walked together to the door. "Mom will be so happy to see you and the flowers," dad said. "And I have something to tell you. You have a new sister. Her name is Rose." Lily and Max smiled. They couldn't wait to see mom and Rose. They hoped they would like the flowers. They were glad dad told them the surprise.

One day, a boy named Tim had a zip on his bag. The zip was very important because it kept all his toys inside. Tim loved his toys and played with them every day. One morning, Tim could not find his bag. He asked his mom, "Where is my bag with the zip?" His mom did not know. So, Tim asked his dad, "Where is my bag with the zip?" His dad did not know either. Tim was sad, but he was also persistent. He asked his dog, "Do you know where my bag with the zip is?" The dog did not reply, but Tim did not give up. Finally, Tim asked his baby sister, "Do you know where my bag with the zip is?" His baby sister pointed to the closet. Tim opened the closet and found his bag with the zip. He was very happy and said, "Thank you!" to his baby sister. Tim learned that being persistent can help you find what you are looking for.

Once upon a time there was a town. It was very powerful. Everyone in town was so proud of it. One day, a man came to town. He had a sharp tool. He wanted to use it to cut something in town. The people in the town were scared. They didn't know what the man wanted to cut. But then the man used the tool to cut down a tree. He used it to make the town look even better. Everyone was so happy when they saw the tree was gone! The man had used his powerful tool to make the town look better. Everyone was so pleased with the result. They thanked him for making the town so nice. The end.

There was a tall man named Bob. He was a lawyer. One day, he was walking to work. He saw a big tree. Bob wanted to lean on the tree. A little cat was near the tree. The cat said, "Hi, tall man! Can you help me?" Bob was surprised. He did not know cats could talk. But he wanted to help the cat. Bob asked, "What do you need?" The cat said, "I want to be a lawyer like you!" Bob smiled. He took the cat to work. They became a great team. And they helped many people together.

One day at the farm, Sarah was very excited. A pony had been delivered to the farm. She ran to the stall to get a closer look. "How modest and beautiful you are," she said to the pony. Sarah wanted to take the pony for a ride, but her mum had other ideas. "No, Sarah," said Mum. "The pony needs to get used to the farm before you can ride it." Sarah was disappointed. She wanted to ride the pony now but she knew her mum was right. "OK Mum," she said sadly. Over the next few days Sarah cared for the pony, feeding and brushing it. She was so excited when she was finally allowed to take it out for a ride. She had worked so hard and it was so worth it. Finally she could experience the thrill of riding her own pony!
