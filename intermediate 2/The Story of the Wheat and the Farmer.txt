Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Wheat and the Farmer

Once upon a time there was a farmer with a wheat field. Each day his wheat grew bigger and bigger. But one terrible day, the farmer forgot to take care of his wheat! The wheat got very dry and died. The farmer was very sad and wished he remembered to water the wheat. The farmer still went to the wheat field everyday and looked at the wheat. He always felt sad. But then one day, the farmer remembered something. He remembered he had planted some wheat seeds a long time ago! The farmer got very happy and dug into the ground. Sure enough, he found the wheat seeds, and he planted them. The farmer took such good care of the new wheat. He watered it everyday, and soon the wheat grew to be very big and strong. The farmer was so happy, he smiled every time he looked at his healthy wheat. He never forgot to take care of it again.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to support his family by doing many things. One day, Max heard about an opera. He thought it would be fun to go with his family. Max and his family went to the opera. They listened to the music and watched the people sing. Max liked the opera, but he felt a little tired. So, he went outside to rest. While Max was outside, he saw a big truck. The truck was going very fast. Max tried to support his family by stopping the truck, but he was too small. The truck hit Max, and he was very hurt. Max's family was sad, and the opera was not fun anymore.

Sam and Mia liked to play in the garage. They had many toys and games there. But sometimes they fought over them. One day, they wanted to play with the same car. Sam grabbed it first, but Mia pulled it away. "Give it back!" Sam shouted. "It's mine!" "No, it's mine!" Mia yelled. "You always take the best things!" They tugged and tugged until the car broke in two. They both looked at the pieces and started to cry. Mom heard the noise and came to the garage. She saw the broken car and the sad kids. She was not happy. "What happened here?" she asked. "Why did you break the car?" Sam and Mia pointed at each other and blamed each other. Mom shook her head. "That's enough. You need to learn to share and be nice. You are not playing in the garage anymore. You are going to your rooms. Now." Sam and Mia did not want to go to their rooms. They wanted to play in the garage. But they knew Mom was serious. They said sorry to each other and to Mom. They picked up the pieces of the car and walked to their rooms. They felt bad for breaking the car. They wished they had not fought over it. They wondered if they could fix it later. They hoped Mom would let them play in the garage again. They decided to quit fighting and be better friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat man. He was very big and round. He loved to eat and play all day. One day, the fat man found a magic hat in his yard. It was a very small hat, but he wanted to wear it on his head. The fat man put the magic hat on his head. All of a sudden, he started to shrink! He got smaller and smaller until he was the size of a toy. The fat man was scared and did not know what to do. He wanted to be big again. Then, a tiny bug came to help the fat man. The bug told him to take off the magic hat and say a special word. The fat man did what the bug said, and he grew back to his big size. The fat man was so happy, he gave the bug a big hug. They became best friends and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Max. Max loved to play. He would play with his ball, his bone, and his friends. Max lived in a big house with a big yard. He was a very happy dog. One day, Max was playing with his ball. He ran fast and jumped high. Suddenly, Max felt a pain in his leg. He stopped playing and sat down. Max was sad because he could not play with his ball anymore. Max's owner saw him sitting in the yard. She knew Max was hurt. She took Max to the doctor. The doctor looked at Max's leg and said it would be better soon. Max was happy to hear this. After a few days, Max's leg was better, and he could play again. Max and his friends played all day long. They were all very happy.
