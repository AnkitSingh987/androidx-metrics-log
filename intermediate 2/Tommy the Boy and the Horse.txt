Category: Intermediate
Title: Tommy the Boy and the Horse

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tommy. Tommy loved horses and wanted to do something special for them. He had an idea. Tommy wanted to bake a special meal for the horses. He worked hard to make the best meal he could. He went to the store and bought special ingredients to make the meal. He mixed them all together and put them in the oven and baked them. When the food was finally finished cooking, Tommy was so excited. He took the meal to the horse stable and set it out for the horses to eat. But when the horses were about to eat, something unusual happened. The horses didn't like the meal. They sniffed it, then turned away refusing to eat it. Tommy was very sad. He had worked so hard on the meal and the horses didn't like it after all. Tommy sadly went home and never made another unusual meal for the horses again.

Tom and Lily wanted to make a snowman. They put on their warm coats, hats, gloves and boots. They went outside and saw a lot of snow. They rolled big balls of snow and stacked them on top of each other. They found a carrot, a scarf, a hat and some stones. They used them to make the snowman's nose, neck, head and eyes. They were very happy with their snowman. They gave him a name: Bob. They said hello to Bob and hugged him. They wanted to play with him some more. But then they heard a loud roar. They looked up and saw a fierce bear. The bear was hungry and angry. He saw Bob and thought he was a big ice snack. He ran towards Bob and tried to bite him. Tom and Lily were scared. They did not want the bear to eat Bob. They ran to their house and looked for something to help. They saw their dad's shovel. They grabbed it and ran back outside. They shouted at the bear and waved the shovel. The bear was surprised and stopped. He saw Tom and Lily and thought they were too noisy and brave. He did not like them. He decided to leave Bob alone and find another snack. He turned around and ran away. Tom and Lily were relieved. They had saved Bob from the bear. They were very brave and smart. They went to Bob and checked if he was okay. He was okay. He still had his nose, neck, head and eyes. He smiled at them. They smiled back. They had completed their snowman. They were very proud of him. They played with him until it was time to go inside. They said goodbye to Bob and promised to come back tomorrow. They went inside and told their mom and dad what had happened. Their mom and dad hugged them and praised them. They had a hot chocolate and a cookie. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a man who liked to work with leather. He was very good at it. He made bags, shoes, and belts for people in the town. He was an independent man and liked to do things by himself. One day, a little girl came to the man's shop. She wanted a new bag made of leather. The man was happy to help her. He worked hard to make the best bag for her. When the bag was ready, the little girl was very happy. She thanked the man and went home with her new leather bag. The man felt good about his work and was proud to be independent. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy and his mom went to a cold museum. The boy saw big things and small things. He saw old things and new things. He felt happy in the museum. The boy saw a big fish. It was very dry. He said to his mom, "Mom, why is the fish dry?" His mom said, "It is dry because it is old and not in water." The boy wanted to help the fish. Then, something unexpected happened. The fish started to talk! The fish said, "Thank you for caring, but I am happy being dry. I can tell stories to kids like you." The boy and his mom were surprised but happy to hear the fish talk. They all became good friends.
