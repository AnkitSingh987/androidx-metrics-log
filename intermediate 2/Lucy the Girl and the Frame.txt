Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Frame

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a big, pretty frame on her wall. Inside the frame was a picture of her family. She loved to look at the picture and feel happy. One day, Lucy's friend, Tom, came to play with her. They played with toys and had a lot of fun. But then, Tom accidentally broke the frame. The glass shattered, and the picture fell to the floor. Lucy started to cry because she felt bad. Lucy's mom heard the noise and came to see what happened. She saw the broken frame and the picture on the floor. Lucy's mom hugged her and said, "Don't feel bad, Lucy. We can fix the frame." They picked up the pieces and put the picture in a new frame. Lucy felt happy again, and she and Tom continued to play.

Mommy was busy in the kitchen when her little girl, Lucy, came in. Lucy was feeling a bit sad, so Mommy said, "Let's give each other a big hug." The two of them hugged and Lucy felt much better. Mommy said, "Let's have a loop race." The two of them ran around the living room together with their arms up in the air, making a big loop. It was very exciting! Next, they went into the garden. It was quite dry, so Mommy put a big blue bucket down on the ground. Inside the bucket there were some leaves and pieces of wood. Mommy said, "Let's use these to make a loop." The two of them used the leaves and pieces of wood to make a big loop shape. When they finished, they stepped inside the loop and hugged each other again. Mommy said, "That was a lot of fun! Let's do it again some time." Lucy nodded and smiled. She felt so much better now. Mommy had given her a big hug and they had made a dry, loopy race together. She couldn't think of a better way to spend the day!

Once upon a time there was a bear. The bear was feeling very lucky. He was about to explore a dark tunnel. The bear was excited and started to part. He parted some grass and then parted some trees. Then he came to the entrance of the tunnel. He took a deep breath and stepped inside the tunnel. It was dark and musty. He could feel a cool breeze coming from the end of the tunnel. He kept walking and walking until he came to the end. When he looked around, he saw all sorts of shiny treasures! He was so lucky. The bear gathered up all the treasures and ran back out of the tunnel. On his way home he couldn’t help but think about his lucky day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. He had a big box of toys. Tim liked to play with his toys and make creative games. He would build tall towers and make funny animals with his blocks. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came to play with him. They wanted to play with the blocks. Sue had an idea to build a bridge, but Tim wanted to build a castle. They did not know what to do. They started to disagree, because they both liked their own idea. Then, Tim had a new word to share with Sue. "Let's build a castle with a bridge," he said. Sue thought it was a great idea. They worked together and built a big, beautiful castle with a bridge. They were both happy and proud of their creative work.

Lily and Tom were playing in the kitchen. They saw a big box on the table. It had a shiny paper and a bow. "Let's open it!" Lily said. She grabbed a knife from the drawer. "No, no, no!" Tom said. "That is not for us. That is for Mommy. And the knife is sharp. It can hurt you." "But I want to see what is inside. Maybe it is a toy or a book or a cake." Lily said. She tried to cut the paper with the knife. Tom ran to the door and called for Mommy. "Mommy, Mommy, come quick! Lily has a knife and she is opening your box!" Mommy came into the kitchen and saw Lily with the knife and the box. She was angry and scared. She took the knife from Lily and put it away. She looked at the box and saw a hole in the paper. "Oh, Lily, what have you done? This is a very special gift from Grandma. She made it for me. It is a scarf. She wrapped it with love and care. And you ruined it with the knife." Mommy said. Lily felt bad. She did not mean to make Mommy sad. She saw the scarf in the box. It was red and blue and green. It had flowers and stars and hearts. It was very pretty. But it was also very smelly. "Mommy, why is the scarf smelly?" Lily asked. Mommy sniffed the scarf and made a face. "Oh, no. The scarf is smelly because Grandma has a cat. And the cat likes to sleep on the scarf. And the cat is not very clean. He has fleas and dirt and hair. And he pees on the scarf. That is why it is smelly." Mommy said. Lily and Tom made a face too. They did not like the smelly scarf. They did not want to touch it or wear it. "Mommy, can we throw away the smelly scarf?" Lily asked. Mommy thought for a moment. She loved Grandma and she knew Grandma loved her. She did not want to hurt Grandma's feelings. But she also did not want to keep the smelly scarf. "Maybe we can wash the smelly scarf and make it clean and nice. And then we can wrap it again and send it back to Grandma. And we can say thank you and we love you. And we can ask her to make us a new scarf without the cat." Mommy said. Lily and Tom nodded. They liked that idea. They helped Mommy wash the smelly scarf and dry it and wrap it. They wrote a card for Grandma and put it in the box. They hoped Grandma would understand and not be mad. They also hoped Grandma would make them a new scarf that was not smelly.
