Category: Intermediate
Title: It and the Cake

It was a beautiful day, the sun was warm and bright, and Alex was excited to play with his toys in the garden. He found an old medal on the ground. It looked very shiny and special. Alex picked up the medal and asked his mummy, "What is this?" Mummy smiled and said, "That's a special medal. It is something that people who do something great get." Alex was so excited. He put the medal around his neck and ran off to tell his friends. When Alex told them about his medal, his friends were so excited for him. They said, "That's so cool! We should bake you a medal cake to celebrate". Alex's face lit up with happiness. Soon the friends were baking a cake – lots of warm, fluffy layers with chocolate shavings in the shape of the medal. It was the best day ever! Alex enjoyed the cake with his friends, and the medal around his neck made him feel very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was enthusiastic and loved to play games with his friends. One day, Max saw a big race in the park. He was excited and wanted to win. Max went to his friend, Cat. "Cat, will you help me win the race?" he asked. Cat said, "Yes, Max! I will help you. Let's practice running to the left and to the right." So Max and Cat practiced every day. The day of the race came. Max, Cat, and all their friends were there. Max remembered what Cat taught him. He ran fast, turning left and right. Max won the race! His friends cheered for him. Max was happy, and he thanked Cat for helping him win.

Ben and Lily were adventurous kids. They liked to create things with their toys and tools. One day, they found a big box in the garage. They decided to make a mule out of the box and some other things. They cut holes for the eyes and the mouth. They used a broom for the tail and some rope for the ears. They painted the box brown and black. They were very proud of their mule. "Let's ride the mule!" Ben said. "We can go to the park and show everyone!" "OK!" Lily said. "But we have to be careful. The mule is not very strong." They put the mule on a wagon and pulled it to the park. They saw some other kids playing there. They wanted to show them their mule. "Look at our mule!" Ben shouted. "We made it ourselves!" The other kids laughed. They thought the mule was silly and ugly. They made fun of Ben and Lily. "That's not a mule!" one kid said. "That's a box with a broom!" "That's the worst mule I ever saw!" another kid said. "It can't even walk or talk!" Ben and Lily felt sad and angry. They didn't like the other kids. They wanted to go home. "Come on, Lily," Ben said. "Let's take our mule and leave." They tried to pull the wagon, but it was too heavy. The mule was too big and bulky. The wheels got stuck in the mud. The rope broke. The broom fell off. The box ripped. Their mule was ruined. They couldn't fix it. They couldn't take it home. They had to leave it in the park. They cried. They wished they never made the mule. They wished they never came to the park. They wished they were more careful. They learned a hard lesson. They learned that creating things is fun, but not always easy. They learned that not everyone likes their creations. They learned that they have to be respectful and responsible. They walked home, holding hands. They hoped to create something better next time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside. One sunny day, she set out to find a beautiful rainbow that she saw from her window. Sue was very happy and excited. As Sue walked, she saw a big hill. She climbed the hill, but when she got to the top, she felt dizzy. She sat down and took a deep breath. As she looked around, she saw the rainbow had moved. It was now on the other side of the hill. Sue was sad, but she decided to keep going. Sue walked and walked, but the rainbow kept moving away from her. She was tired and wanted to give up. Then, she saw her mom, who had been looking for her. Her mom told her that sometimes, things we want are not easy to get. The rainbow taught Sue to never give up, even when she felt dizzy or sad. Sue learned that hard work and not giving up are important, and she was happy to have her mom by her side.
