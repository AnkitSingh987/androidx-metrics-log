Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sue and the Playing

One day, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue were playing a game. They wanted to see who could get the highest score. They were both very good at the game, but Tim was feeling helpless because Sue was winning. Tim said, "Sue, I don't want to play anymore. I surrender." Sue looked at Tim and said, "Don't give up, Tim! We can keep playing together and help each other get better." So, Tim and Sue played the game together and helped each other. They both got high scores and were happy. They learned that playing together and helping each other was more fun than trying to win alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy who loved to whistle. One day, Tim wanted to take a bath in his bathtub. He filled it with warm water and lots of bubbles. As Tim played in the bathtub, he heard a tiny voice. It was a little duck. The duck said, "Hi, Tim! Can I play with you?" Tim was very fair and liked to share, so he said, "Yes, duck, you can play with me!" Tim and the duck played in the bathtub together. Tim whistled a happy song, and the duck quacked along. They had so much fun playing with the bubbles and splashing water. After a while, it was time to get out. Tim and the duck said goodbye and promised to play again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a pen that he loved very much. He used it to draw and write every day. Tim took it with him everywhere he went. One hot day, Tim went outside to play. He was having so much fun that he forgot his pen on the ground. The sun was shining bright and it started to melt the pen. The pen became scared because it did not want to disappear. Tim saw the scared pen and picked it up. He took it inside and put it in a cool place to help it feel better. The pen stopped melting and was very happy. Tim promised to take better care of his pen, and they had many more happy days together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a store with his mom. At the store, he saw a film. The film had a big, red car on it. Tim thought it was interesting. He asked his mom, "Can I have this film, please?" His mom looked at the film and said, "Yes, you can have it. But first, you must help me pass the food to put in our cart." Tim was happy. He helped his mom pass the food. They passed apples, bananas, and milk. After they finished, his mom let him put the film in the cart. When they got home, Tim and his mom watched the interesting film together. They saw the big, red car go fast and do fun tricks. Tim loved the film and was glad he could help his mom at the store.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom liked to climb big rocks. One day, while climbing, Tom found a pretty gem. Tom was very happy with his gem. He showed it to his friends. They all liked the gem too. They wanted to find more gems. So, Tom and his friends went on a hunt. They climbed rocks and looked all around. They found more gems and shared them with each other. They all had fun and were very happy.

Sam was a stubborn boy. He did not like to share his toys or listen to his mom. One day, he found a big shovel in the garage. He wanted to dig a hole in the backyard and find treasure. He did not ask his mom if he could use the shovel. He just took it and ran outside. He dug and dug and dug. He made a big hole in the ground. He did not find any treasure, but he did not stop digging. He wanted to go deeper and deeper. He did not hear his mom calling him for lunch. He did not see the dark clouds in the sky. He did not notice the hole was getting too big and too deep. Suddenly, the ground under him gave way. He fell into the hole with a thud. He was scared and hurt. He cried for help, but no one heard him. He was underground and alone. He wished he had listened to his mom and shared his toys. He wished he had not been so stubborn. His mom was looking for him. She saw the shovel and the hole in the backyard. She was worried and angry. She ran to the hole and looked down. She saw Sam at the bottom, covered in dirt and tears. She called his name and told him to stay calm. She ran to get a rope and a ladder. She brought them to the hole and threw the rope down. She told Sam to grab the rope and climb up. She helped him out of the hole and hugged him. She was glad he was safe, but she was also very mad. She scolded him for taking the shovel without asking, for digging a dangerous hole, and for not answering her calls. She told him he could have been hurt or lost. She told him he had to learn to be more careful and respectful. She told him he had to apologize and clean up the mess. She told him he had to share his toys with his sister and play nicely. She told him he had to be less stubborn and more obedient. Sam felt sorry and ashamed. He said he was sorry and he hugged his mom. He said he loved her and he would try to be better. He learned his lesson. He learned that being stubborn can lead to trouble and that being obedient can lead to happiness. He learned that his mom loved him and wanted the best for him. He learned that digging holes is not as fun as finding treasure.
