Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Jill and the Window

Once upon a time there were two best friends called Jack and Jill. They were always together, even when it was dark outside. One day Jack and Jill were playing outside, when suddenly it started to freeze! They shivered and knew they had to go inside. Jack's house was on one side and Jill's was on the other side, so they said goodbye and ran towards their homes. When Jack got to his house it was already dark and he was so cold he couldn't think straight. As he opened the door he remembered he forgot to close the window! Jack was so scared, he knew he would get into trouble and ran back outside to close the window. By the time Jack got back outside, it was already too late. The window was frozen shut and Jack couldn't open it. He started to cry and shiver, he knew he was in a lot of trouble. The moral of the story is: You should always close the window when it's dark and cold outside, otherwise bad things can happen!

One day, a thoughtful cat named Tom saw a big parade. The parade had many people, big balloons, and loud music. Tom liked the parade very much. He danced and clapped his paws to the music. Tom saw a little girl with a big ice cream cone. The ice cream looked so good. Tom walked up to the girl and said, "Can I have some ice cream, please?" The little girl was kind and gave Tom a small lick of her ice cream. After that, a big dog named Max came to the parade. Max saw Tom and the little girl with the ice cream. Max was not nice. He went to the girl and said, "Give me your ice cream!" That was Max demanding the ice cream. The little girl was scared and started to cry. Tom did not like Max being mean to the little girl. He went to Max and said, "You should be nice and not demand things." Max felt bad and said sorry to the little girl. From that day on, Max was nice to everyone at the parade.

Once upon a time, there was a big pond. It was a nice pond with many fish and frogs. Near the pond, there was a rough path that went through the woods. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the pond. He wanted to play with the fish and frogs. His mom warned him, "Be careful near the pond, Tim. The path is rough and you might fall." Tim listened to his mom and walked slowly on the rough path. He saw the fish and frogs in the pond. They were happy and splashing in the water. Tim played near the pond all day. He was careful and did not fall on the rough path. When it was time to go home, he said goodbye to the fish and frogs. His mom was proud of him for being safe. They went home and Tim told his dad about his fun day at the pond.

Once upon a time, in a small humble house, there lived a little boy named Tim and his mom. They loved to bake muffins together. One day, Tim and his mom made some yummy muffins. They were very happy. But then, Tim and his mom had a quarrel. "I want the big muffin!" said Tim. "No, I want the big muffin!" said his mom. They were both very sad. Just then, a little mouse came into the kitchen. He saw the big muffin and took it away. Tim and his mom saw the mouse and laughed. "Let's share the other muffins," said Tim. His mom agreed, and they were both happy again. They learned that sharing is better than quarreling.

Once upon a time, there was an amazing band. They loved to play and sing together. One day, they went to a big park to play for all the kids. While they were playing, a big dog came running. It wanted to fight with the band. The kids got scared and the band stopped playing. They didn't know what to do. But then, something unexpected happened. A small cat came and stood in front of the dog. The cat and the dog looked at each other. The dog stopped being mean and started to play with the cat. The kids and the band were happy again. They all played and sang together, and it was a fun day.
