Category: Intermediate
Title: Georgie and the Bowl

One day, Georgie and his mommy had a special day. They went to the store and bought lots of different nuts. They came home with different kinds of nuts, like peanuts and walnuts. Mommy said that it was a day to celebrate. When they got home, mommy and Georgie put all the nuts in a big bowl. Then they took out lots of other items – cups, plates and spoons. They used the spoons to take the nuts and put them on the plates. Georgie couldn't wait to taste all the different nuts. Mommy smiled and said that the peanuts would be really soft. Then Georgie put a soft peanut in his mouth and it tasted really good! He was so happy that he clapped his hands and shouted out loud. Mommy laughed, and they both celebrated the day with lots of smiles and cheers. They had a great time eating all the different kinds of nuts and celebrating special moments with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom liked to smile a lot. He was a very happy cat. One day, Tom went outside to play in the sun. He saw a hose in the yard. Tom thought it would be fun to play with the hose. He walked over and started to bite it. The hose wiggled and made funny noises. Tom laughed and smiled even more. In the end, Tom had a great day playing with the hose. He went inside to eat and take a nap. Tom was a happy fat cat who always found fun things to do.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a run in the park. He loved to run because it made him feel great. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. It was a beautiful day. As Tim was running, he heard a loud alarm. He looked around and saw a big red fire truck. The fire truck was going to put out a fire. Tim wanted to help, so he ran after the fire truck. But then, something unexpected happened. The fire truck stopped and the fireman got out. There was no fire! The fireman had just come to the park to give out ice cream to all the kids. Tim was happy and enjoyed his ice cream with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to walk and play with his friends. One day, Max saw a small cat with a hurt shoulder. The cat was sad and could not walk fine. Max wanted to help the cat feel better. Max had an idea. He went to find his friend, a kind bird named Sue. Sue was very smart and knew how to help the cat. Sue told Max to find a soft leaf and some water. Max did what Sue said and brought the leaf and water back to the cat. Sue put the leaf on the cat's hurt shoulder and gave her some water to drink. Soon, the cat felt better and could walk fine. The cat was happy and thanked Max and Sue for their help. Max learned that helping others is a good thing to do. From that day on, Max, Sue, and the cat became best friends and always helped each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his toy car when he felt uncomfortable. He knew he needed to go to the toilet. So, he ran to the bathroom. In the bathroom, Tim saw the toilet paper roll was empty. He called out to his mom, "Mom, there's no paper!" His mom heard him and came to the bathroom with a new roll of toilet paper. She said, "Here you go, Tim." Tim tried to put the new roll on the holder, but it was too high for him. He jumped and tried to pull it down, but he couldn't reach. His mom saw him struggling and said, "Let me help you, Tim." She pulled the holder down and put the new roll on it. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Now, Tim could use the toilet and feel comfortable again.
