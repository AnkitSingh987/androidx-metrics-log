Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wise Owl and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a wise old owl. The owl had a long tail and big eyes. The owl knew many things and had a lot of wisdom. The owl liked to stand on a big tree branch and look at the world. One day, a little bird came to the owl. The little bird was sad because it lost its way. The owl used its wisdom to help the little bird. The owl said, "I will stand on this long branch and look for your home." The owl stood on the long branch and looked around. It saw the little bird's home far away. The owl told the little bird where to go. The little bird was very happy and thanked the wise old owl. The little bird flew back to its home and they all lived happily ever after.

Billy was a 3 year old boy who was always very polite. He was always very nice to the people around him and he was always holding the door open. One day, as he was happily walking to the park, he noticed a pair of sunglasses on the ground. He stopped and bent down to pick them up. Billy put on the sunglasses and immediately felt very cool. He asked his mom, "Where did these sunglasses come from?" Mom replied, "They must belong to someone else. Let's take them to the park so we can give them back." On the way to the park, they met a dog. The dog saw the glasses and wanted them so he jumped up and tried to bite them off Billy's face. Billy was scared, but he remained very polite. Mom said, "No dog! That won't be nice!" The dog sat down in front of them and looked sad. Billy said to the dog, "It's okay. You can have the sunglasses. You can wear them and look cool like me!" The dog was overjoyed and ran around in circles. Billy and his mom laughed and the dog kept the sunglasses. Billy and his mom were so proud of how polite he had been.

Once upon a time, in a small yard, there was a big tree. The tree had many leaves. One day, a little boy named Tim came to play in the yard. He saw the tree and thought it was important. Tim wanted to take care of the tree. One day, Tim saw a leaf on the tree that was starting to rot. He was sad and did not want the tree to be sick. He asked his mom, "Mom, why is the leaf rotting?" His mom said, "Tim, sometimes leaves get old and rot. It is okay. The tree will still be strong and grow new leaves." Tim felt better and kept playing in the yard. He knew the tree was important and would be okay. The tree and Tim became good friends, and they were happy in the yard together.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse lived in a big house. The house was very loud. The mouse did not like the loud house. One day, the mouse found a big box. The box had a small hole. The mouse went inside the hole. Inside the box, the mouse found a lot of food. The mouse was very happy. He knew he would gain a lot of food in this box. The mouse ate and ate. But then, something unexpected happened. The loud noise stopped. The mouse went outside the box. He saw that the big house was now quiet. The mouse was happy. The loud house was not loud anymore. The mouse had a quiet home and a lot of food.

Sue and her mum were walking through the forest. It was a huge forest and Sue was scared. "Mum, why are we walking through the forest?" asked Sue. "We have to turn here," said Mum. "We are going to visit Auntie Jane." Sue was surprised. She had heard stories about Auntie Jane. People said she lived in a dark house and suffered all the time. Sue's legs started to shake. But when they arrived, Auntie Jane was nothing like the scary stories. She welcomed them with warm hugs and gave them big smiles. Suddenly, a loud noise came from outside. Auntie Jane said: "Don't worry. It's just the neighbour's huge truck. He will be gone soon." Sue was relieved. Auntie Jane was kind and full of laughs, unlike the stories. Sue smiled, happy that her aunt didn't suffer anymore.
