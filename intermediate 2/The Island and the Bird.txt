Category: Intermediate
Title: The Island and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a small island in the middle of the sea. On this island, there were many pretty colors. The sun was bright and the grass was green. The flowers were red, blue, and yellow. One day, a big black bird came to the island. It liked to touch everything it saw. When the black bird touched the flowers, they turned black too. The island was not as pretty as before. The animals on the island were sad. Soon, the black bird saw how sad the animals were. It felt bad and wanted to help. The bird asked the animals what it could do. They told the bird to touch the flowers again, but this time to think of the pretty colors. The black bird did as they said, and the flowers turned back to red, blue, and yellow. The animals were happy again. The black bird learned that it is important to think about how our actions can make others feel.

One day, a big sun was in the sky. It was very hot. A boy named Tim went outside to play. He wanted to have a fun day. Tim's mom gave him a bag. The bag included toys and a delicious snack. Tim played with his toys in the park. He was very happy. Then, a big cloud covered the sun. Tim felt cold. He opened his bag to get his snack. But, the snack was gone! Tim was sad. Then, a small dog came to Tim. The dog had Tim's snack in its mouth. The dog wanted to play with Tim. Tim was surprised! He played with the dog and shared his snack. They became best friends. The day was fun again.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little gate. Every day, a small boy named Tim would enter the gate to play. He loved to run, jump, and laugh with his friends. One sunny day, Tim saw a big, red apple on a tree. It looked delicious. He wanted to share it with his friends. But, the apple was too high for him to reach. Tim thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. He asked his friends to help him. They all worked together, and they got the apple. Tim and his friends shared the delicious apple. They were all happy. The moral of the story is that when we share and work together, we can do big things.

Once upon a time, there was a red bird named Bobo. Bobo lived in a cold place with lots of snow. One day, he found a small igloo made of ice. Bobo was hungry and could not resist looking inside the igloo. Inside, he saw a big, yummy cake. It was a special treat for someone's birthday. Bobo knew the cake was not his, but he could not resist. He took a small bite, then another, and soon the cake was gone. Bobo felt bad for eating the cake, so he decided to make a new one. He found some flour, eggs, and sugar, and made the best cake ever. The birthday boy was happy, and Bobo learned to resist taking things that were not his.

One day, a boy named Tim had a soft toy and a stamp. He liked to play with them. Tim's friend, Sue, came to play with him. They had fun with the soft toy and the stamp. "Can I have the stamp?" Sue asked. Tim said, "Yes, you can have it." They took turns with the stamp. They stamped it on paper. Then, a big wind came. It blew the stamp away. Tim and Sue were sad. They looked for the stamp, but they could not find it. The wind took it far away. They could not play with the stamp anymore.

One day, a dancer named Lily went to play. She was not a normal dancer. She could jump very high and spin very fast. All the kids wanted to be her friend. But Lily was not happy. A little boy named Tim wanted to be her friend too. He said, "Lily, can I play with you?" But Lily refused. She was sad because she thought no one liked her for who she was. They only liked her because she was a special dancer. One day, Tim saw Lily sitting alone. He went to her and said, "I don't want to be your friend because you are a dancer. I want to be your friend because you are nice." Lily smiled and they played together. From that day on, Lily and Tim were best friends.
