Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Reliable Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a reliable dog named Spot. Spot was a good dog who always did what he was told. He lived in a small house with a nice family. The family loved Spot very much. One day, the family went to the park to play. Spot heard them mention a new word. The word was "fetch." Spot was curious and wanted to learn what "fetch" meant. He wagged his tail and waited for the family to show him. At the park, the family threw a ball and said the word "fetch" again. Spot knew what to do. He ran fast, got the ball, and brought it back to the family. They were so happy and proud of their reliable dog Spot. They played fetch all day long and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little white dog named Spot. He loved to play with shapes. One day, Spot found a big box full of shapes. "Wow!" he said, "I want to understand these shapes!" Spot started to play with a round shape. He rolled it and watched it go. It went very far. "This shape is fun," he said. But then, he saw another shape. It was a square. He tried to roll it, but it did not go far. "Hmm," he thought, "I don't understand this shape." Later, Spot's friend, a little girl named Lily, came to play. "I can help you understand shapes," she said. They played with the shapes together. Lily showed Spot how to stack the square shapes to make a tower. Just when they finished making the tower, something unexpected happened. The round shape that Spot rolled earlier came back and hit the tower. The tower fell down with a big crash! Spot and Lily looked at each other and laughed. Now, they both understood the shapes and had fun playing together.

Sara liked to bounce on the board in the park. It was fun to go up and down and feel the wind in her hair. She bounced with her friends, Lily and Ben, and they laughed and sang. One day, a big boy came to the park. He was older and taller than Sara and her friends. He said he wanted to bounce on the board too. He pushed Sara and Ben off the board and jumped on it. He bounced so hard that the board made a loud noise. Sara and Ben were scared and ran away. Lily was still on the board. She was anxious and wanted to get off, but the big boy did not let her. Sara and Ben did not know what to do. They wanted to help Lily, but they were afraid of the big boy. They looked around and saw a man. He was wearing a blue shirt and a badge. He was a park ranger. He saw what was happening and walked over to the board. He told the big boy to stop bouncing and get off the board. He said he was being rude and mean to the other children. He said he had to share and play nicely, or he would have to leave the park. The big boy was angry and tried to argue, but the park ranger was firm and calm. He helped Lily off the board and asked her if she was okay. She nodded and smiled. She was happy to be free. She hugged Sara and Ben and thanked them for being her friends. They hugged her back and said they were sorry for running away. They said they were glad she was safe. The park ranger told the big boy to apologize to the other children. He said he was sorry, but he did not sound sincere. He walked away, looking sulky and ashamed. The park ranger told Sara, Lily and Ben that they could bounce on the board again, but they had to take turns and be careful. He said he would watch them and make sure no one bothered them. He said he was proud of them for being brave and kind. He said he hoped they had fun. Sara, Lily and Ben smiled and thanked the park ranger. They climbed on the board and bounced together. They felt happy and relieved. They forgot about the big boy and enjoyed the sun and the breeze. They bounced and laughed and sang. They had fun.
