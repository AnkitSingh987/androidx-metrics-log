Category: Intermediate
Title: The Reliable Train and the Stop

Once upon a time, there was a reliable train. This train was always on time. Everyone liked the train because it was so good. One day, the train forgot something. It forgot to stop at a station. The people at the station were sad. They wanted to go on the train, but it did not stop for them. The train felt bad for forgetting. It said sorry to the people. The next day, the train did not forget to stop. It stopped at every station. The people were happy again. And the reliable train never forgot to stop again.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom liked to eat delicious cake. One day, he saw a cake on the table. Tom wanted to eat it. He said, "Yum, I want to eat this cake." Tom jumped on the table and started to eat the cake. He used a teaspoon to eat the cake. He was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A little mouse named Jerry came out of a hole. Jerry saw Tom eating the cake. Jerry said, "Stop, Tom! That is my cake!" Tom was surprised. He did not know the cake was for Jerry. Tom felt bad. He said, "I am sorry, Jerry. I did not know this was your cake." Jerry smiled and said, "It's okay, Tom. We can share the cake." So Tom and Jerry shared the delicious cake. They became friends and were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big park. The park had many zigzag paths. The people who lived in the town liked to walk on the paths. They liked how the paths were organized. The paths helped them not get lost. One sunny day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. They walked on the zigzag paths. Mia liked to run and play on the paths. Her mom told her to remain close. Mia did not want to get lost. Mia and her mom had a fun day at the park. They played on the swings and the slide. They had a picnic on the grass. Then they walked on the zigzag paths one more time before going home. They were happy they remained close and did not get lost.

---

Lila and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They had a lot of fun on the swings, the slide and the sandbox. One day, they saw a little bird on the ground. It was hurt and could not fly. Lila and Ben felt sad for the bird. They wanted to help it. They ran to the bird and gently picked it up. They wrapped it in a soft cloth and carried it to a bench. They gave it some water and some bread crumbs. They stroked its feathers and talked to it softly. The bird felt better and started to chirp. Lila and Ben smiled and hugged each other. They were happy that they could help the bird. They decided to take the bird to the vet. They asked their moms to drive them there. They said goodbye to the bird and thanked the vet for taking care of it. They hoped the bird would get well soon and fly again. Lila and Ben were compassionate heroes. They loved animals and wanted to help them. They were also good friends who always stuck together. They never wanted to part.

One day, a bossy cat named Tom was walking on the dry ground. He liked to tell all the other animals what to do. Tom saw a little bird named Tim and said, "Tim, you must find me some water to drink!" Tim was scared of the bossy cat, so he tried to find water. But the ground was so dry, there was no water to be found. Tim came back and said, "Tom, I can't find any water. The ground is too dry." Suddenly, there was a big earthquake! The ground shook and all the animals were scared. Tom and Tim tried to run away but they fell into a big crack in the ground. They were stuck and couldn't get out. In the end, the bossy cat and the little bird stayed stuck in the crack. They were sad because they could not get out. The other animals felt bad for them, but they knew that sometimes bad things happen.
