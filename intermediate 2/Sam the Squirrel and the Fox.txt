Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Fox

Once upon a time, there was a squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find nuts and lay them in his home. One day, Sam found a big nut under a tree. He was very happy. Sam met a bird named Bob. Bob saw the nut and said, "Can I have that nut, Sam?" Sam thought for a moment and said, "No, this nut is for me." Bob felt sad but flew away. A foolish fox saw Sam and wanted the nut too. The fox tried to take the nut, but Sam was too fast. Sam ran to his home and lay the nut inside. The fox went away, and Sam was safe. Sam and his big nut lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a very anxious boy. One day, his mom told him they were going to the museum. Tim was excited but also scared because he had never been to a museum before. In the museum, Tim saw many things. He saw big animals and small animals. He saw old things and new things. Then, Tim saw a man who had a box. The man said, "I need to deliver this box to the other side of the museum. Can you help me, Tim?" Tim was scared but wanted to help. He took the box and walked with the man. They talked and laughed as they walked. When they got to the other side, the man said, "Thank you, Tim! You are very brave and kind." Tim felt happy and not scared anymore. He learned that helping others can make you feel good and less anxious. From that day on, Tim was always ready to help others and visit new places. And that is the story of how Tim learned the value of helping others and facing his fears.

One day, a bald man named Tom went on a trip. He walked and walked until he saw a big river. Tom was very hot and sweaty, so he decided to wash his face in the river. As he washed his face, a fish swam up and said, "Hello, Tom! If you help me, I will give you a wish." Tom was surprised but said, "Okay, what do you need?" The fish said, "I am lost. Can you help me find my family?" Tom agreed to help the fish. Together, they looked for the fish's family. After a long time, they found them! The fish was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! Now, what is your wish?" Tom thought and said, "I wish to have hair on my head." The fish granted his wish, and Tom had hair again! The moral of the story is to always help others, and good things will happen to you. And, you never know who you might meet on a trip!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys. Her favorite toy was a soft, yellow triangle. She liked to squeeze it and hug it all day long. One sunny day, Lily went outside to play with her triangle. She found the perfect spot under a big tree. She sat down and began to play. She would throw the triangle up in the air and catch it. Lily loved the way it felt when she squeezed it in her hands. As she played, a small, friendly dog came up to Lily. The dog liked the yellow triangle too. They played together, taking turns to squeeze the triangle. After a while, they got tired and lay down under the tree. Lily and the dog hugged the soft triangle and took a nap. They were very happy to have found the perfect toy to play with together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The dog had an impressive trick. He could sneeze very big sneezes. One day, the dog wanted to show his friends his trick. He went to the park with his friends. The dog said, "Watch me sneeze!" He took a deep breath and sneezed. His friends jumped back an inch. They were so surprised by the big sneeze. The friends clapped and laughed. They all had fun playing in the park. The little dog was happy he could make his friends smile with his sneeze trick. They all played until it was time to go home.
