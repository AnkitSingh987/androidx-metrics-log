Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and the Cookie

Ben liked to chew on things. He chewed on his toys, his clothes, his fingers, and sometimes even his books. His mom and dad told him not to chew on things that were not food, but he did not listen. He liked the way chewing made his teeth feel. One day, he saw a new thing on the table. It was brown and round and smelled good. He thought it was a cookie. He climbed on a chair and reached for it. He took a big bite and chewed hard. But it was not a cookie. It was a candle. It was hot and waxy and bitter. Ben spit it out and cried. His mouth hurt and his tongue was sore. He felt sick and scared. His mom and dad heard him and ran to the table. They saw the candle and the bite mark and the wax on the floor. They were angry and worried. They took Ben to the sink and rinsed his mouth. They checked his teeth and his throat. They hugged him and told him he was lucky he did not get hurt worse. They also told him he had to learn his lesson. He had to stop chewing on things that were not food. He had to ask before he took anything from the table. He had to be careful and smart. Ben nodded and said he was sorry. He did not want to chew on candles or anything else that was not food. He wanted to chew on something mild and sweet and soft. He wanted a cookie. His mom and dad smiled and gave him one. He chewed it slowly and happily. He learned his lesson.

One day, a little boy named Timmy went to the park with his mom. Timmy saw a big firework on the ground. He wanted to play with it, but his mom said, "No, Timmy, that's dangerous!" Timmy was sad, but he still wanted the firework. He said to his mom, "Please, can I have it?" His mom insisted, "No, it's not safe!" Then, a dog came running to the park. The dog saw the firework and picked it up with his mouth. Timmy and his mom were scared. But then, the dog put the firework in a safe place where no one could touch it. Timmy learned that it's important to listen to his mom because she knows what's best for him. And they both thanked the dog for keeping them safe.

On Sunday, Tommy went to the park with his mom. It was a very hot day, so they decided to jog. Tommy began jogging around the park, making loud sounds as he ran. Mom: "Come on, Tommy! Don't jog so loud!" Tommy kept running but he kept making lots of noise. Seeing his mom struggle to keep up, he jackknifed back and put his hands around his mouth. Tommy: "Mommy, watch this!" Tommy began jogging again, but this time he made the loudest sound ever. He was so proud of himself! Mom: "That was too loud, but very impressive! Let's keep jogging!" They did, and eventually they finished their jog around the park. It was a wonderful Sunday morning together!

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box. The box was closed. Tim thought, "I want to open it!" He tried to open the box, but it was too hard. He asked his mom for help. She said, "It's not time to open the box yet. It is important to wait." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. He waited and waited. A few days later, his mom said, "Now it's time to open the box!" Tim was very excited. He opened the box with his mom's help. Inside the box, there was a big, beautiful toy car! Tim was so happy. He knew waiting was important. Now, he could play with his new toy car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a house with her mommy, daddy, and her cat, Mr. Whiskers. Lily and Mr. Whiskers loved to play together. They would run up and down the stairs all day. One day, Lily wanted to measure the stairs. She thought it would be a fun game. She asked her daddy for help. Daddy gave her a small ruler to use. Lily and Mr. Whiskers started at the bottom of the stairs. They counted each stair as they went up. They were very careful not to fall. When they got to the top, Lily felt scared. She had never been so high up before. Mr. Whiskers was scared too. But they knew they could be brave together. They held hands and walked back down the stairs. When they got to the bottom, they were so happy. They had measured the stairs and faced their fear. Lily and Mr. Whiskers hugged each other and continued to play.
