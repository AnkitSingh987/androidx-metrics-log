Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pet cat named Fluffy. They both loved to play together. One day, Sue and Fluffy went to the park to play. At the park, Sue saw her friend Tom. Tom was a happy boy who liked to skip. Sue and Tom started to skip together. Fluffy, the pet cat, watched them and wanted to join in, so she skipped too. They all laughed and had fun. Suddenly, a big dog came running towards them. Fluffy was frightened and ran away. Sue and Tom were scared too, but they wanted to help Fluffy. They looked for Fluffy and found her hiding behind a tree. They all hugged and went back home, safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to ride his red bike all day long. One day, he saw a sign in the park. The sign said, "Bike race today! Win a prize!" Tim was excited and wanted to win the prize. Before the race, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue had a flexible blue bike. Sue said, "Good luck, Tim! Let's have fun!" Tim smiled and said, "Good luck to you too, Sue!" They both got ready to race. The race began, and Tim and Sue rode as fast as they could. During the race, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the prize away! All the kids stopped riding and looked at the flying prize. Tim and Sue had an idea. They rode their bikes together, jumped high, and caught the prize! They won the race and shared the prize. They became best friends and rode their bikes happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who lived in a big house. He had a big yard with lots of toys. One day, he found a big piece of cardboard in his yard. He thought of a fun idea and decided to make a big slide with the cardboard. He put the cardboard on a small hill in his yard. The wealthy man called his friends to come and play on the slide. They all took turns sliding down the cardboard. They laughed and had a lot of fun together. But then, it started to rain. The wealthy man did not want the rain to prevent them from playing. So, he brought out a big umbrella to keep the cardboard slide dry. Now, they could all keep playing and sliding down the cardboard, even in the rain. The wealthy man and his friends played all day and had the best time.

Once upon a time, there was a big sneeze. The sneeze was so big, it blew off a veil from a girl's head. The girl was angry. She wanted to find out who sneezed. She looked around and saw a cat. The cat sneezed again. The girl knew the cat was the one who sneezed. She was angry at the cat. She wanted her veil back. The cat was sorry. It did not mean to sneeze. The cat tried to help the girl find her veil. They looked and looked. Finally, they found the veil. The girl was happy. She was not angry at the cat anymore. They became friends and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a beetle who didn't like to listen. One day, the beetle was not being very thoughtful and broke some rules. The beetle's mom was very mad. She decided to punish the beetle for not being thoughtful. So the beetle had to sit in a corner for a few minutes and think about what he did wrong. He felt very sorry and vowed to be more thoughtful next time. The beetle's mom was very pleased and hugged her little beetle tightly. From that day forward, the beetle was always thoughtful and always followed the rules.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat was very flexible. It could turn and bend in the water. One day, the boat met a big fish. "Hi, boat!" said the big fish. "Can you turn and bend like me?" The boat nodded and showed the big fish how it could turn and bend. They played together in the water. As they were playing, a big wave came. The wave picked up the boat and the big fish. But the flexible boat could turn and bend, so it did not break. The big fish was surprised and happy. "Thank you, boat, for showing me how to turn and bend!" said the big fish. And they became best friends forever.
