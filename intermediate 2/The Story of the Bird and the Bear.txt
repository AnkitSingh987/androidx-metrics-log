Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Bear

One day, a pretty bird wanted to print a picture of her home. Her home was an igloo. She loved her igloo very much. She wanted to show all her friends her pretty igloo. But, the pretty bird did not know how to print. She asked her friend, the big bear, for help. The big bear did not know how to print either. They were both sad. They wanted to find a way to print the picture of the pretty igloo. Then, they met a smart frog. The smart frog knew how to print. The frog helped the pretty bird and the big bear print the picture of the igloo. They were all very happy. The pretty bird showed her friends the picture of her pretty igloo.

Mum was sitting in her chair when she heard a clumsily bumping noise coming from her bedroom. She went to investigate, expecting to find her three year old daughter Emily. Mum discovered Emily trying to open a jar on the floor. 'What are you trying to do, sweetheart?' asked Mum. Emily looked up at her. 'I wanted to help with the cooking, but I can't open the jar.' Mum knelt down and smiled comfortingly. 'You don't need to do that, sweetheart. Why don't you go and play while I finish the cooking.' Emily bit her lip. 'But I wanted to help.' Mum put her arm around Emily's shoulders. 'It's kind of you to want to help, but some jobs are too tricky for little ones. Why don't you play and we can help cook together later?' Emily reluctantly agreed and scampered off, leaving Mum to open the stubborn jar. When Mum was done, she sat with Emily for a while and watched her play. Emily soon forgot about her clumsiness and Mum smiled, relieved her daughter was at ease again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named James. He was three years old. James loved to play with blocks and build. He also loved to dream. One day, James and his mom went on a long walk. They were walking through the forest when James spotted something very big in the distance. "Mommy, what is it?" he asked. "That is an enormous tree," she said. James was amazed and said, "I want to build something as big as that tree!" "That is a very big dream, James," his mom said. "But with hard work, you can do it!" So James went back to the forest every day to play with blocks. He worked on his dream and built things. Pretty soon, he had built something as big as the enormous tree. James had learned a valuable lesson: with hard work, dreams can come true.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to admire things. She liked to look at pretty things and say, "Wow, that is fine!" One day, Sue found a piece of paper on the ground. She picked it up and saw a picture of a cat. The cat was very fine. Sue liked the cat a lot. She showed the picture to her friend, Tim. Tim liked the cat, too. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat in the picture started to move! It jumped out of the paper and became a real cat. Sue and Tim were so surprised. They laughed and played with the cat all day. The cat was their new friend, and they had lots of fun together.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play together in their room. They have many toys and books and games. But sometimes they make a big mess and do not clean up. One day, Mom comes to their room and sees the mess. She is not happy. She says, "Sara and Ben, you have to pick up your toys and books and games. You cannot leave them on the floor and on the bed. It is not safe and not nice. You have to put them in the boxes and on the shelves. Then you can have a jolly snack and read a story with me." Sara and Ben do not want to pick up their toys and books and games. They want to keep playing. They say, "Mom, we are having fun. We do not want to stop. Can we pick up later?" Mom shakes her head. She says, "No, you have to pick up now. It is time to tidy up. If you pick up quickly, you can have more time to play later. And you will feel good when your room is neat and cozy. Come on, I will help you." Sara and Ben sigh. They do not like to pick up, but they love Mom and they want a jolly snack and a story. They start to pick up their toys and books and games. They put them in the boxes and on the shelves. They make their bed and fluff their pillows. They work together and help each other. Soon, their room is clean and tidy. Mom smiles. She says, "Well done, Sara and Ben. You did a great job. I am proud of you. Now you can have a jolly snack and a story with me. What do you want to eat? What do you want to read?" Sara and Ben are happy. They say, "We want cookies and milk. We want to read the book about the jolly monkey. Thank you, Mom. We love you." Mom hugs them. She says, "I love you too. Come on, let's go to the kitchen and then to the couch. We will have a jolly time together."
