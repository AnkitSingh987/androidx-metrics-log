Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Boy and the Lamp

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little boy who enjoyed playing with his lamp. It was yellow and he liked to turn it on and off. One day, the boy's grandma came for a visit. "Oh my! What a chubby little boy!" she said, tickling him. The little boy smiled and laughed. "What are you playing with?" she asked, nodding to the lamp. "Lamp!" said the boy happily. "Let's enjoy the lamp together." Grandma said, turning the lamp on and off together and they enjoyed watching the light twinkle in the room. The little boy laughed and smiled. He was so happy, he gave Grandma a big hug and off they went, to enjoy something else together.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Mia. She loved to play on the beach and find pretty shells. One sunny day, she went to the beach with her mom and dad. At the beach, Mia found lots of shells. She was very excited. She wanted to sort the shells by their size and color. She put big shells in one pile and small shells in another pile. Mia's mom and dad watched her and smiled. But then, a big wave came and washed away all the shells that Mia had sorted. Mia was very sad. Her mom and dad tried to make her feel better, but the shells were gone. They went back home, and Mia did not have her pretty shells anymore.

One day, a little frog named Tim wanted to join the big march. All the animals were going to walk together in a long line. Tim was very excited. He wanted to hop, hop, hop all the way. As the march started, Tim hopped happily. But soon, he hopped on the tail of a big dog named Max. Max yelled, "Ouch! Who did that?" Tim felt guilty. He knew he should say sorry, but he was scared. He hid behind a small bush. Max looked around and saw Tim hiding. He walked over and said, "Did you hop on my tail?" Tim nodded his head and said, "I am sorry, Max. I did not mean to. I just wanted to hop in the march." Max smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. Just be careful next time." Tim and Max became friends. They walked together in the march. Tim hopped carefully and Max wagged his tail. They had a lot of fun and were happy to be part of the big march.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken named Chip. Chip was a restless chicken. He would run, jump, and play all day long. One day, Chip met a big chicken named Charlie. Charlie was reading a book under a tree. "Hi, Charlie! What are you doing?" asked Chip. Charlie looked up from his book and said, "I am reading a book, Chip. It is a fun thing to do when you want to rest." Chip was curious. He wanted to learn how to read too. So, Charlie helped Chip learn how to read. They read books together under the tree every day. Chip became less restless because he enjoyed reading books with his new friend, Charlie. Now, Chip and Charlie read books and play together. They are very happy chickens. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a big toy car. He loved his car very much. Every day, he played with his toy car. But one day, the car looked dirty. Tom felt uncomfortable. Tom wanted to make his car clean and shiny again. He asked his mom for help. Mom gave him a cream to polish the car. Tom was very happy. He took the cream and started to polish his car. Tom worked hard to polish his car. After some time, the car was clean and shiny again. Tom was very happy. He showed his clean car to his mom. Mom was proud of Tom. They played together with the car, and they had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Sam. Sam lived in a small house with a girl named Sue. Sue loved Sam very much. They played together every day. One day, Sam found a shiny crystal under the bed. He didn't know what it was, but it was very pretty. He played with the crystal all day. He pushed it with his paws and watched it sparkle. Sue saw Sam playing with the crystal and wanted to play too. She tried to take the crystal, but Sam didn't want to share. He started to yell, "Meow! Meow!" Sue was sad, but then she had an idea. She found another crystal, and they both played together, happy as can be.
