Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog. The dog liked to play with a little boy. They were best friends. Every day, they played together in the park. They ran, jumped, and laughed. One day, the little boy went on a trip with his family. The happy dog missed the boy very much. He waited at the park every day, hoping the boy would come back to play. Finally, the little boy came back from his trip. The happy dog saw him at the park and ran to him. They hugged and played all day long. The happy dog did not miss the boy anymore. They were best friends forever.

---

Lily and Tom are friends. They live next to each other. They like to play outside when the sun is bright. They have a lot of fun. One day, they see a big box in front of Lily's house. They are curious. They want to know what is inside. They go to the box and try to open it. "Maybe it is a toy," Lily says. "Maybe it is a cake," Tom says. They pull and push the box, but it does not open. They hear a noise from inside. They are scared. "Who is there?" Lily asks. "Boo!" a voice says. Lily and Tom scream and run away. They see Lily's dad laughing. He comes out of the box. "It was me!" he says. "I was joking. I wanted to surprise you." Lily and Tom are angry at first. They think Lily's dad is mean. But then they see he is smiling. He is not mean. He is funny. They laugh too. They forgive Lily's dad. They hug him. "Can we play with the box?" Lily asks. "Sure," Lily's dad says. "But be careful. There might be more jokes inside." Lily and Tom are happy. They play with the box. They make jokes too. They have more fun.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a hidden bench. It was a special bench where kids would sit and play. The bench was behind tall trees, so not many people could see it. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park to play. They ran and played catch with a red ball. After a while, Tim got tired and wanted to sit down. Spot sniffed around and found the hidden bench. Tim and Spot sat on the bench, and they saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim and Spot wanted to help. They all looked for the toy together. They passed the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. At last, they found the toy near the hidden bench. Sue was very happy, and they all played together on the bench. They became good friends and went to the park to play every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. He was three years old, and he loved to explore outside. On one sunny morning, Sam went outside and saw a big bush. He had never noticed the bush before, but he was curious. "What's in there?" Sam asked his mom. His mom smiled and answered, "It's a very special bush. You should explore it, if you would like." Sam was excited to find out what was inside the bush. He went over and carefully removed the bush leaves. To Sam's surprise, he found an open hole! He looked inside the hole and saw a special surprise. It was a little puppy, who was scared and all alone. Sam gasped and said, "Mom look! A puppy!" His mom smiled and said, "It looks like the bush was hiding a special puppy just for you, Sam!" He happily held the puppy in his arms and smiled. Sam knew his life had changed forever. That day, he had removed the bush and opened a new world of possibilities.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a swing and ran to it. Lucy loved to swing high in the sky. She felt so comfortable on the swing, like she was flying with the birds. Lucy's mom called out, "Lucy, it's time to go home!" Lucy stopped swinging and walked to her mom. They went home to their cozy house. In the kitchen, Lucy's mom turned on the stove to cook dinner. The stove made the room warm and nice. Lucy sat at the table and said, "Mom, I love swinging at the park. Can we go again tomorrow?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lucy. We can go to the park and swing again tomorrow." Lucy was very happy and enjoyed her dinner. She couldn't wait for the next day to swing again.
