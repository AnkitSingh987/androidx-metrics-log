Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Amy and the Car

Tom and Amy were playing with a toy car. The car could make noises and move by itself. It was very fun. But then the car stopped. It did not make noises or move anymore. "Oh no, the car is broken!" Amy said. She was sad. "Maybe it needs a new battery," Tom said. He was smart. He knew how to fix things. "What is a battery?" Amy asked. "A battery is a thing that gives power to the car. It is inside the car. We need to open the car and change the battery," Tom explained. He got a screwdriver from his dad's toolbox. He opened the car and took out the old battery. He put in a new battery from his mom's drawer. He closed the car and turned it on. "Look, the car is working again!" Tom said. He was happy. "Yay, you fixed it! You are very good, Tom!" Amy said. She was happy too. They played with the car some more. They did not quit until it was time for dinner. They thanked their mom and dad for the batteries. They put the car and the screwdriver away. They washed their hands and sat at the table. They ate their food and talked about their fun day. They were good kids.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a delicate doll. The doll had a pretty dress, big eyes, and a little lip. Lily loved her doll very much. One day, Lily and her doll went to the park. Lily wanted to play on the swings. But, her doll was too small to sit on the swing. Lily was sad. She wanted her doll to play too. Lily had an idea. She managed to put her doll on her lap. Then, she held her doll tight. They swung together, up and down, up and down. Lily and her doll had lots of fun. They played all day and went home happy.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They loved to play together, but one day, Sue felt hot and did not want to play. Sue's mom came with a thermometer and put it in Sue's mouth. Tom did not understand what the thermometer was for. Sue's mom looked at the thermometer and said, "Sue, you have a fever. You need to rest." Tom was sad but he wanted to help his friend. He sat by Sue's side while she slept. When Sue woke up, she felt better. She hugged Tom and said, "Thank you for staying with me." Tom was happy that Sue was better, and they played together again.

Little Tommy was very excited because he was going out for dinner with his family. He couldn't wait to order from the menu. He asked his mom what they were having. She replied, "You'll have to wait and see, Tommy. Take a look at the menu when it comes." When the waiter arrived, Little Tommy eagerly opened the menu. He asked his mom, "Can I have the famous dish?" His mom smiled. She knew exactly what her son was talking about. She replied, "Yes, of course." The waiter nodded and wrote the order down. When the food arrived, Little Tommy was excited to see the famous dish. He couldn't believe it was in front of him! He exclaimed, "I can't wait to try it! It looks so good." His mom smiled and replied, "I'm glad you like it, Tommy. Enjoy it!" Little Tommy smiled and chowed down on his delicious dinner. He was happy to have tried something so famous.

Lily and her mom moved to a new house. It was big and old and had many rooms. Lily was excited to see her new room. She wanted to unpack her toys and books and make it cozy. "Come on, daughter, let's go upstairs and find your room," mom said. She carried a big box with Lily's name on it. Lily followed her, holding her teddy bear. They walked up the stairs and looked at the doors. One door had a sign that said "Lily's room". Mom opened it and smiled. "Here we are, this is your room. It has a big window and a nice bed. Do you like it?" Lily nodded. She liked the window. She could see the garden and the trees. She ran to the bed and jumped on it. It was soft and bouncy. She giggled. "This is fun, mom!" Mom put the box on the floor and opened it. She took out some of Lily's toys and books and put them on the shelves. She saw a small, wrapped package at the bottom of the box. She did not remember packing it. It looked mysterious. She picked it up and gave it to Lily. "Look, daughter, this is for you. I wonder what it is. Do you want to open it?" Lily took the package and shook it. It made a jingly sound. She was curious. She tore the paper and opened the box. Inside was a shiny necklace with a star pendant. It sparkled in the light. Lily gasped. "Wow, mom, it's so pretty! Where did it come from?" Mom shrugged. She did not know. She looked at the paper and saw a note. It said: "To Lily, from your fairy godmother. Wear this necklace and make a wish. It will come true. Love, F. G." Mom and Lily looked at each other. They were surprised and happy. They did not know they had a fairy godmother. They hugged and thanked her. Lily put on the necklace and felt a warm glow. She closed her eyes and made a wish. She wished for a new friend in her new house. She opened her eyes and smiled. She felt something was going to happen. She did not know what, but she was ready. She had a mysterious necklace and a fairy godmother. She was going to have an adventure.
