Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to show her friends her new toy. She thought it would be fun to have a party. So, she asked her mom, "Can I have a party, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but we need to wait for a dry day, so we can play outside." Mia was so happy. She waited for the sun to come out. Finally, the day was dry and sunny. Mia and her mom got the yard ready for the party. They put out balloons, snacks, and games. When her friends came, Mia showed them her new toy. They all loved it and took turns playing with it. They played games, ate snacks, and had a great time at the party. At the end of the day, Mia was very tired but happy that she had a fun party with her friends.

Once upon a time, a quiet little boy named Tim found a small piece of coal. He picked it up and looked at it. It was black and dirty. Tim blinked his eyes and saw a tiny bug near the coal. "Hello, little bug," said Tim. "Do you want to be my friend?" The tiny bug blinked its eyes too and said, "Yes, I would like to be your friend, Tim." Tim and the tiny bug played all day. They had lots of fun together. Tim showed the bug his toys and they shared a yummy snack. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Goodbye, little bug. I hope to see you again soon." The tiny bug blinked its eyes and said, "Goodbye, Tim. I had a great day with you. I hope to see you again too." And so, Tim went back to his quiet home, happy to have made a new friend. He put the coal in a special place, so he would always remember the tiny bug.

Jack and Sally were adventurous friends who loved to explore together. One day, they were walking in the woods when they saw an old light bulb hung from a tree. They were so curious about it and wondered what it was for. Sally said, "let's see if we can get it down!" Jack agreed and they both grabbed one end of the bulb. It was so heavy, but with a lot of effort, they manage to pull it off. When they looked at the bulb, Jack said, "We should take this home!" But Sally didn't think that was a good idea and wanted to quit. She said it was too dangerous and they should just leave it alone. Jack didn't want to give up, so the two of them argued for a while. Finally, Sally said, "Okay, let's take it home. But let's be careful. I'm sure it has some kind of an adventure ahead for us!" Jack quickly agreed, and the two of them held hands and carried the light bulb home to explore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big sign with an unknown word on it. Tim did not know what the sign said, but he knew it was important. Tim wanted to read the sign, but he struggled. He tried and tried, but he could not read the unknown word. He felt sad and wanted to know what the sign said. Then, Tim's friend, Lily, came along. She saw Tim struggling and asked if she could help. Together, they looked at the sign and tried to read the unknown word. Finally, they figured it out! The sign said "park." They were so happy and went to play in the park together.

Once upon a time two brothers named Alex and Tom lived together in a big house. They were very clever and loved to have adventures. One day, Alex dared Tom to go fight in a war. Tom said, "I don't want to fight in a war, I'm scared!" Alex replied, "Don't be scared, Tom. I'll be brave for you!" Tom thought for a minute and decided to accept the dare. He found a big suit of armor and got on his brave horse. Alex said, "Good luck, Tom. I will wait here for you to come back!" Tom went off to the war and fought bravely. He was so clever that he won every battle and even came home with a prize. When Tom got back to the house he was happy to see that Alex was still waiting for him. Alex said, "Welcome home, Tom! I knew you could do it!" They hugged each other and were happy to be back together again.
