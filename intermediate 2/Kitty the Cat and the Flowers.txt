Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Kitty. Kitty was very weak and needed help from her friends. One day, Kitty wanted to go on a trip with her best friend, Doggy. Doggy said, "Kitty, I will remind you to be careful on our trip because you are weak." Kitty and Doggy went on their trip. They walked slowly and looked at the pretty trees and flowers. They played with a ball and had a lot of fun. Kitty was happy that Doggy was there to help her. At the end of the trip, Kitty said, "Thank you, Doggy, for reminding me to be careful. I had a great time!" Doggy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Kitty. I'm glad we could go on this trip together." And they went home, happy and tired from their fun day.

Once upon a time, in a wide river, there was a little red kayak. The kayak liked to float and play with the fish and ducks. Every day, the sun would shine and the water would sparkle. One day, the little red kayak saw a big frog. The frog said, "Hello, kayak! I wonder what is on the other side of the river." The kayak thought this was a fun idea. So, the frog hopped into the kayak and they went to see. The kayak and the frog went across the wide river. They saw trees, flowers, and more fish. When they got to the other side, they found a big, green field. They played there all day and had so much fun. Then, they went back to the river and said goodbye. The little red kayak was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Bob. Bob loved to play by the sea. One day, he saw a big surf. He wanted to try it too. Bob met a nice fish named Tim. Tim said, "Hi, Bob! Do you want to surf with me?" Bob said, "Yes, Tim! I want to surf too. But I don't know how." Tim said, "Don't worry, Bob. I will help you." They went to the surf together. Bob was scared, but Tim helped him. They had fun in the surf. Bob and Tim surfed all day. They were very happy. Bob said, "Thank you, Tim. I had fun!" Tim said, "You're welcome, Bob. Let's surf again soon!" From that day on, Bob and Tim were best friends. They surfed together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a lively town, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her friends, and they always had fun together. One day, Mia found a letter on the ground. It was a pretty letter with many colors. Mia showed the letter to her friends, but they wanted to keep playing. So Mia put the letter in her pocket and played with her friends. When it was time to go home, Mia wanted to show her mom the letter. But when she looked in her pocket, the letter was gone. It had vanished! Mia felt sad, but her mom told her a secret. "When you find something that doesn't belong to you, it's best to leave it where you found it or give it to a grown-up." Mia learned a valuable lesson that day. And from then on, she always made sure to do the right thing.

One hot day, a big elephant named Ella was walking with her friend, a small mouse named Mimi. They were both very thirsty. They saw a lake and ran to it. Ella used her long trunk to fill it with water. She drank and felt better. Mimi tried to drink, but she was too small to reach the water. "Ella, can you help me?" Mimi asked. Ella smiled and said, "Of course, Mimi!" She used her trunk to fill it with water again and gave it to Mimi. Mimi drank and felt better too. They both played and had fun. The sun started to set, and they went home. Ella and Mimi learned that helping each other is important. They were happy to have each other as friends. The moral of the story is to always help your friends when they need it.
