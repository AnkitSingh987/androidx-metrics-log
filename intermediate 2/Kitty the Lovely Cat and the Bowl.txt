Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Lovely Cat and the Bowl

Once upon a time, there was a lovely cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to eat butter. One day, she found a big jar of butter in the kitchen. Kitty was very happy and wanted to eat all the butter. Kitty tried to fill her little bowl with the butter. But the jar was too high for her. She jumped and jumped, but she could not reach it. Kitty was sad, but she did not give up. She had an idea to use a chair to get to the jar. Kitty pushed the chair to the jar and climbed up. She was so close to the butter now. But as she tried to get the butter, the chair wobbled and Kitty fell down. The jar of butter fell too, and broke on the floor. Kitty was hurt and there was butter everywhere. She did not get to eat the butter and her day was not good.

Once upon a time, there was a small cabin in a big forest. A nice family lived there: a mom, a dad, and a little girl. They were very happy in their cabin. The trees around the cabin were very big and great. One day, the little girl found a big rock near the cabin. She had an idea. She wanted to play with the rock. So, she pushed the rock with all her might. The rock began to move and rock back and forth. The little girl laughed and clapped her hands. She loved her new rock friend. She played with the rock every day, making it rock back and forth. The great trees in the forest watched them play and smiled. And the little girl, her family, and the big rock lived happily ever after.

Mummy and Daddy had to go to work. They put their belts on and said goodbye to their little boy. But the little boy didn't like being left alone. He was very pale and sad. Mummy looked at the little boy and said: "Don't worry. I have a surprise for you!" The little boy looked up at Mummy, his eyes suddenly brighter. "What is it?" he asked. Mummy smiled and said, "I'm going to get you a toy. Would you like to come with me to pick it out?" The little boy beamed with joy. He put on his belt and together they went to the store. When they arrived, he saw so many toys. He quickly picked out a small truck with big wheels. His eyes were sparkling with excitement. Mummy was pleased and gave him a big hug. As they walked home, the little boy waved goodbye to all the toys in the store. He couldn't wait to play with his new truck.

Once upon a time, there was a little mixer. It loved to mix things up all day. It lived in a big kitchen with lots of other things. One day, the mixer felt guilty. It had mixed up all the food and made a big mess. The mixer wanted to fix the mess before mom saw it. So, it tried to clean up. But then, something unexpected happened. The mixer vanished! It was gone! The mixer was now in a new place. It was a magic land with lots of colors and fun things to do. In this magic land, the mixer met a nice fairy. The fairy saw that the mixer was sad. She asked, "Why are you sad, little mixer?" The mixer told the fairy about the mess it made. The fairy smiled and said, "Don't worry, I can help you!" With a wave of her wand, the fairy made the mess vanish too. The mixer was so happy! It thanked the fairy and went back to the kitchen. From that day on, the mixer was more careful when mixing things up. And sometimes, it even went back to visit the fairy in the magic land.

Tom and Lily are brother and sister. They live in a big house with their mom and dad. They like to play in the yard and look at the flowers and the birds. One day, they see a black cat in the yard. The cat is thin and scared. It runs away when they try to pet it. Tom and Lily feel sorry for the cat. They want to help it. They ask their mom if they can give the cat some food and water. Mom says yes, but they have to be careful and not scare the cat. She provides them with a bowl of milk and some bread. Tom and Lily put the bowl near the fence, where they saw the cat. They wait and watch from the window. After a while, the cat comes back. It sees the bowl and sniffs it. It drinks the milk and eats the bread. It looks happier and less scared. Tom and Lily smile and clap. The next day, they do the same thing. They provide the cat with more food and water. The cat comes back again and eats and drinks. It also lets Tom and Lily pet it a little. It purrs and rubs against their hands. Tom and Lily decide to name the cat Blacky. They ask their mom and dad if they can keep Blacky as their pet. Mom and dad say yes, but they have to take good care of Blacky. They have to feed it, clean it, and love it. Tom and Lily are very happy. They have a new friend. Blacky is happy too. It has a new home. It likes to sleep in Tom and Lily's room and play with their toys. It is not thin and scared anymore. It is fat and happy. Tom, Lily, and Blacky are a family. They live in a big house with their mom and dad. They like to play in the yard and look at the flowers and the birds.
