Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe the Boy and the Track

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Joe. Joe was three years old and he had a big dream of becoming a fisherman one day. He enjoyed watching the fishermen work their boats in the harbour. One day, Joe was walking along the harbour when he saw something that made him stop in his tracks. He saw an impressive tuna jumping out of the water again and again. It was so impressive that Joe just had to watch it. Joe enjoyed watching the impressive tuna so much that he lost track of time and his mom soon came to take him home. Joe's mom said, "What have you been doing here for so long, Joe?" Joe replied, "I was watching the tuna bounce and I wanted to be a fisherman like them one day." Joe's mom smiled and said, "That's a wonderful dream, Joe. You should always follow your dreams no matter how hard it may seem." Joe learned an important lesson that day. Whenever you see something impressive and inspiring, it's always a good idea to take the time to watch it. That way, it may help you when you are trying to reach your biggest dreams.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree. Under the tree, he saw a little cat. The cat looked sad. Tim said, "Hi, cat! Why are you sad?" The cat looked at Tim and said, "I am sad because I lost my toy. It is a red ball. Can you help me find it?" Tim said, "Yes, I will help you." They looked and looked for the red ball, but they could not find it. Then, they saw a big dog. The dog had the red ball in his mouth. Tim said, "Hey, dog! That is the cat's ball. Can you give it back?" The dog looked at them and said, "I am sorry. I did not know it was yours. Here you go." The dog gave the ball back to the cat. The cat was happy, and they all played together. The sad cat was sad no more, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a quiet town, there lived a brave cat named Tom. Tom loved to put on a big hat and go on adventures. One day, he saw a little bird stuck in a tree. The bird was scared and couldn't fly away. Tom said, "Don't worry, little bird, I will help you!" He climbed up the tree and helped the bird. As Tom put the bird down on the ground, the bird said, "Thank you, brave cat! You are very kind." But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to grow bigger and bigger! It turned out that the bird was a magic bird. The bird said, "Tom, you have been so brave and kind. I will give you a wish. What do you want?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "I wish for everyone in the quiet town to be happy and kind, just like me." The magic bird smiled and granted Tom's wish. From that day on, everyone in the quiet town was happy and kind to each other. The moral of the story is: be brave and kind, and good things will come your way.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly comet. The comet liked to fly through the sky at night. It would make big loops and shine bright for everyone to see. One day, the comet decided to step down from the sky to visit the Earth. It wanted to make friends and play with the kids. So, it waited until nighttime and then gently stepped down to the ground. When the kids saw the jolly comet, they were so happy. They played games and danced with it all night long. The comet was very glad it stepped down to Earth. After the fun night, it stepped back into the sky, and the kids waved goodbye. They knew the jolly comet would always be there, shining bright in the night sky.

Once upon a time, there was a poor doctor named Tom. He lived in a small house and helped people when they were sick. He was very kind and everyone liked him. One day, Tom met a beautiful girl named Lily. They became good friends and liked to talk to each other. Lily asked Tom, "Do you want to marry me?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I want to marry you." They had a big party to celebrate their love. Everyone in the town came to the party and had lots of fun. Tom and Lily were very happy. From that day on, they lived together and helped each other. And they were never poor again.
