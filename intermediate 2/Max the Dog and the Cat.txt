Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a very angry dog. He would bark and jump all the time. One day, Max saw a big cat. The cat was sitting on a wall. Max wanted to play with the cat, but the cat did not want to play. Max asked the cat to play, but the cat said no. Max asked again, but the cat still said no. The cat did not want to tell Max the truth. The cat refused to play with Max because he was scared of the angry dog. After some time, Max went away. The cat was happy that Max was gone. The cat knew that the truth was that Max was just a little dog who wanted to play. But the cat was still scared. And so, Max and the cat never became friends.

One day, a group of animals was playing in the park. They were very creative and liked to make up new games. They played and laughed all day long. The sun was shining, and the grass was green. As they played, a big wind came and made the leaves fall from the trees. The animals thought this was fun and started to catch the falling leaves. They jumped and ran, trying to grab as many leaves as they could. But then, something unexpected happened. A little mouse, who was very good at catching leaves, saw a big leaf falling from the sky. He ran to catch it, but when he got close, he saw that it was not a leaf at all! It was a butterfly! The butterfly had been hiding in the trees, watching the animals play. The mouse and the butterfly became friends, and they all played together in the park.

Once upon a time, in a pretty little house, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and have fun. One day, she saw her mom pay for a big box. Sue was very curious about the box. "Mom, what is in the box?" asked Sue. Her mom smiled and said, "It is a surprise for you, Sue. But first, you have to be a good girl and help me clean the house." Sue was excited and helped her mom clean the house. She was very good and did not make a mess. When they were done, her mom gave her the big box. Sue opened it and found a new toy inside. She was so happy and learned that being good and helping others can bring nice surprises.

Once upon a time in a small town, there was a powerful baker named Tom. Tom loved to bake big yummy cakes for all his friends. One day, Tom decided to take a walk on the path near his house. While walking, he met a little girl named Sue. "Hi, I'm Sue!" she said. "What do you do?" "Hi Sue, I'm Tom. I like to bake cakes," Tom replied with a big smile. Sue's eyes lit up. "I love cakes! Can you bake one for me and my friends?" "Of course!" said Tom. They walked back to Tom's house, and he baked a big, yummy cake for Sue and her friends. Everyone was very happy and enjoyed the cake. From that day on, Tom and Sue became best friends, and they always shared their love for baking together.

Once upon a time, there was a big whale who lived in the sea. The whale was very sad and miserable. He wanted to make friends, but all the other sea animals were scared of him because he was so big. One day, a small fish saw the miserable whale and swam up to him. "Hello, Mr. Whale," said the fish. "Why are you so sad?" The whale told the fish that he was lonely and wanted to make friends. The fish had an idea. "Why don't you jump and play with us? Everyone will see how fun you are!" The whale thought it was a great idea. He took a deep breath and jumped high into the air. The other sea animals saw the whale jump and thought it was amazing. They all wanted to be friends with the whale. The whale was no longer miserable, and he had many friends to play with in the sea.
