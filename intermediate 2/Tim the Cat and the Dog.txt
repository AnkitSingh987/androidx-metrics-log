Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends, but he also liked to learn new things. One day, Tim decided he wanted to teach his friends how to read a map. So, Tim went to his friend Sam, the dog. Tim said, "Sam, I want to teach you to read a map. It will be fun!" Sam wagged his tail and said, "Okay, Tim! Let's learn together." Tim and Sam sat down with a big map that showed all the places they could go. Tim pointed to the map and said, "This is where we are. We can go here, here, and here." Sam looked at the map and barked happily. They took turns pointing at the map and saying where they wanted to go. After a while, Tim and Sam knew how to read the map. They were so proud of themselves. They decided to go on an adventure using the map to find new places to play. They had a great time and made many new friends along the way. From that day on, Tim and Sam always used a map when they went on adventures. They loved learning new things and teaching their friends too. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. One day, while playing, he realized that his toy car had no string to pull it. So, Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, my car needs a string to pull." His mom smiled and gave him a long, colorful string. Tim was very happy. Tim tied the string to his toy car and pulled it all around the house. He played with his car all day long, and the whole town could hear his happy laughter.

One day, a big pumpkin and a little pumpkin were in a garden. The big pumpkin was very fit and strong. The little pumpkin wanted to be like the big pumpkin. The little pumpkin said, "Can you teach me how to be fit like you?" The big pumpkin smiled and said, "Yes, I will help you. First, we need to joke and laugh. It will make us happy and strong." So, the big pumpkin and the little pumpkin joked and laughed all day. They had a lot of fun. The little pumpkin started to feel fit and strong, just like the big pumpkin. They were happy pumpkins in the garden.

Once upon a time, a bald man named Bob walked in the park. He heard a sound. It was a cricket. The cricket was green and small. Bob liked the cricket. "Hello, cricket!" said Bob. The cricket said, "Hello, bald man!" They talked and laughed. They became friends. Bob and the cricket played all day. Then, a big bird appeared. The bird was hungry. It saw the cricket. The bird flew down and took the cricket away. Bob was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, a curious little girl named Lily went to her grandma's house. Grandma had a big garden with many plants. Lily saw a big, round thing on the ground. It was a squash! Lily asked her grandma, "What is this big thing?" Grandma said, "That is a squash, dear. We can eat it for dinner." Lily was very excited to learn how to cook the squash. Grandma showed Lily how to cut the squash into small pieces. Then, they put the pieces in a pot to cook. They waited for the squash to become soft. When it was ready, they ate the squash together. It was very yummy! Lily was happy to learn something new with her grandma.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and read books. But they also like to eat chocolate. Sometimes, they sneak into the kitchen and take some chocolate from the jar. They know they should not do that, but they cannot resist. One day, their mom finds out. She sees the chocolate on their faces and hands. She is not happy. She says, "Anna and Ben, did you take chocolate from the jar without asking me?" Anna and Ben look at each other. They are scared. They do not want to lie, but they also do not want to get in trouble. They do not know what to say. Then, Ben decides to be brave. He says, "Yes, mom, we did. We are sorry. We like chocolate very much, but we know we should ask you first. Can you forgive us?" Mom is surprised. She is still not happy, but she is also proud of Ben for telling the truth. She says, "Thank you, Ben, for being honest. That is very good of you. I forgive you, but you have to promise me not to take chocolate without asking me again. And you have to share it with your sister." Ben nods. He says, "I promise, mom. And I will share it with Anna. She is my twin and my friend." Mom smiles. She says, "That is very nice of you, Ben. And you, Anna, do you have something to say?" Anna nods. She says, "I am sorry, too, mom. And thank you, Ben, for sharing with me. You are my twin and my friend, too." Mom hugs them both. She says, "I love you, Anna and Ben. You are my twins and my friends, too. Now, let's go and wash your faces and hands. And maybe, later, we can have some chocolate together. But only if you ask me nicely."
