Category: Intermediate
Title: Molly and the Dish

Molly was scared. She was packing her dish in her pink bag. She had to leave home. She took the dish and put it in the bag. Then, she zipped it up tightly. There was something inside the bag that made Molly feel a little bit better. She looked around her room. Everything felt strange and new. But, Molly kept packing her things. She put her favourite toy into the bag and zipped it up tight. The last thing she did was pack her dish. She put it in the bag, gave it a big hug and off she went. Molly was scared but she was also excited to go on her new adventure.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. Lucy saw a big sign with many shapes on it. She was very excited and wanted to play with the shapes. Lucy was a little impatient, so she asked her mom, "Mom, can I play with the shapes now?" Her mom said, "Yes, Lucy, you can play with the shapes. But first, we need to read the sign to know how to play." Lucy and her mom read the sign together. The sign said to put the shapes in the right place. Lucy started to play with the shapes. She put the round shape in the round hole, the square shape in the square hole, and the triangle shape in the triangle hole. Lucy was happy and proud of herself. She said, "Mom, I did it! I put all the shapes in the right place!" Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Lucy! You did it all by yourself. I am so proud of you!" Lucy and her mom played with the shapes for a long time. They had so much fun at the park that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, his mom asked him to put an onion in a big pot. "Tim, can you please put this onion in the pot?" his mom asked. Tim took the onion and put it in the pot. He felt so proud of himself. Later, Tim's mom showed him an ancient book. The book was very old and had many stories inside. Tim's mom read him a story about a magical onion that could make people happy. "Wow, mom! Can our onion do that too?" Tim asked with excitement. His mom smiled and said, "Maybe, Tim. Let's cook the onion and see what happens." They cooked the onion together, and when they ate it, they felt so happy. Tim loved helping his mom and learning about the ancient book. And from that day on, they always cooked onions together.

One day, a poor man was walking down a path. He was very sad. He had a staff in his hand to help him walk. As he walked, he saw a big tree. He sat under the tree to rest. A boy came to the tree too. The boy saw the man and asked, "Why are you sad?" The man said, "I am poor and I have no food." The boy felt bad for the man. He said, "I will pass you some food from my bag." The boy gave the man some food. The man was happy and ate the food. But then, a big wind came. The wind was very strong. The tree fell down on the man and the boy. The man and the boy were hurt by the tree. They both cried. The food was gone too. The poor man was still sad, and now he was hurt too. The boy was sad because he could not help the man. The day was not a good day for them.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird named Sunny. Sunny loved to display his beautiful wings. One day, he saw a pretty curtain hanging in a window. "Hello, curtain!" said Sunny. "You are so pretty. Can we be friends?" The curtain did not talk, but Sunny knew that they were friends. They played together every day. Sunny would fly and dance around the curtain. The curtain would move in the wind and play with Sunny. One day, it was very windy. The curtain blew away from the window. Sunny was sad, but he flew after it. He caught the curtain and brought it back to the window. The curtain was happy to be home. Sunny and the curtain were best friends forever.
