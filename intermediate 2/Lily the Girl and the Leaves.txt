Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Leaves

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a toy fork that she loved to play with. One sunny day, Lily decided to take her toy fork outside to play. While she was playing, she met a boy named Tim. Tim was angry because he lost his toy. Lily wanted to help him, so she asked, "Can we look for your toy together?" Tim agreed, and they started to search for his toy. As they were looking, they saw a small fire burning some leaves. They knew it was not safe, so they called for help. A grown-up came and put out the fire. Tim found his toy near the fire. He was so happy, and he thanked Lily for helping him. From that day on, Lily and Tim became best friends and played together with their toys every day.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and many toys. Tom had a journal. In the journal, he wrote about his day. One day, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tom, can I see your journal?" Tom said, "Yes, here it is." Sue read the journal and saw a page that said, "I own a secret toy room." Sue was excited. She asked, "Tom, can I see the secret toy room?" Tom said, "Yes, let's go!" But when they went to the room, all the toys were gone! Tom and Sue were sad, but they had fun playing with the toys in the other rooms.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy some pepper for dinner. Sally was very excited because she loved to help her mom cook. At the store, Sally saw a man with a sign. The sign had a picture of a dog on it. Sally asked the man, "What is the sign for?" The man said, "I lost my dog. Can you help me find him?" Sally wanted to help, so she told her mom about the man and his dog. Sally, her mom, and the man looked for the dog. They walked and walked until they felt dizzy. Then, they saw the dog, but he was not alone. The dog had found a big bag of pepper! Sally and the man were so happy. They thanked the dog and took him and the pepper home. That night, they all had a yummy dinner together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat corn. Every day, he would ask his mom for corn. But one day, his mom said, "No more corn, Tim. We need to save it for later." Tim was sad because he wanted corn very much. In the village, there was a big, mean man named Bob. Bob had a lot of corn. He would not allow anyone to have his corn. Tim was very sad and tired of not having corn. He decided to ask Bob for some corn. But Bob said, "No, Tim! You cannot have my corn!" Tim was very sad and went home. His mom saw how sad he was and decided to talk to Bob. She said, "Please, Bob, can Tim have some corn? He loves it so much." Bob thought about it and said, "Okay, Tim can have some corn." Tim's mom went home and gave Tim the corn. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little red square. It was a safe square, and it lived in a big box with other shapes. One day, the square wanted to make a new friend. The square met a blue circle. The square said, "Hi, I am a square. Let me introduce you to my friends." The circle was happy and went with the square to meet its friends. When they reached the box, something unexpected happened. The blue circle turned into a square too! Now, they were all squares and could play together safely. The red square and the blue square became best friends forever.
