Category: Intermediate
Title: Tommy and the Sun

Tommy loved to play outside. One day, he was having so much fun that he didn't even notice when the sun started to zoom down in the sky. When his mom saw that it was getting dark, she said, “It’s time to go inside, Tommy.” Tommy was sad and asked, “Can I stay a little longer?” Mom smiled and said, “I’ll make you a deal – if you make me a joke, you can stay for five more minutes.” Tommy thought for a moment and then said, “Why did the bee cross the road? So he could go to the attractive flowers!” His mom laughed and said, “That was a great joke! You can stay for five more minutes.” Tommy was so happy that he stayed and played until it was dark. He ran inside and felt proud of himself for making mom laugh with his joke.

Once upon a time, there was a fat baby. She was a happy baby and loved to giggle and jump around. One day, she saw a big thing and asked her mom what it was. Her mom just shrugged and said she didn't know. The fat baby shrugged too, as she couldn't understand what her mom was saying. The fat baby kept exploring and found a little box in a corner of the room. She opened it carefully and there was a tiny toy inside. She picked it up and shook it, and suddenly the toy winked and smiled at her! The fat baby was so excited! She hugged the toy and couldn't stop smiling. Her mom just shrugged, but she was happy too. The fat baby played with her new toy for a long time. She loved it and refused to let it go. The mom just shrugged, and said she'd have to take care of it forever. The fat baby nodded, and hugged her toy tight.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Jane. She was three years old and loved to dream. One day, Jane went to visit her grandma. Jane was very excited to see her, as they didn't visit often. As they talked, grandma mentioned that Jane should follow her dreams. Jane smiled and nodded. Grandma said that if she tried hard enough, she could make her dreams come true. Jane was sure she could do it! The next day, Jane was feeling a bit hurt. She remembered what grandma had mentioned about following her dreams, but she felt like she couldn't do it. Grandma had also said that trying hard is important, so Jane decided to take her advice and try her best. After a few days, Jane realized she had done it! She had made her dream come true and it was all thanks to her grandma's advice. Jane was so happy. She now knew that she could do anything if she just tried hard enough.

One day, a little blue cat went for a walk. The cat liked to look at the pretty trees and flowers. The sun was bright and the sky was blue, just like the cat. The blue cat saw a big dog. The dog had a hurt paw. The cat wanted to help the dog. So, the cat found a soft leaf and put it on the dog's paw. The dog was happy and wagged its tail. The blue cat and the dog became friends. They played together all day. They looked at the pretty trees and flowers, and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big, popular tree. It was near a house with a porch. Many kids liked to play near the tree. They would laugh, run, and have fun. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to play near the tree. He saw a big box on the porch. Tim was very curious. He wanted to know what was inside the box. Tim opened the box and found a big, red ball. He started to play with it. The ball bounced very high, and it went into the tree. Tim tried to remember how to get the ball down. He thought, and thought, and then he got an idea. Tim got a long stick and poked the ball. The ball fell down, but something else fell too! It was a big bag of candy. All the kids were so happy and surprised. They shared the candy and played with the red ball all day long.
