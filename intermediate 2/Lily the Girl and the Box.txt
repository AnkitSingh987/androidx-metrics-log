Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to play dress up with her fancy clothes. Lily had a big box full of dresses, hats, and shoes. She liked to wear them and pretend she was a princess. One day, Lily found a small bag of powder in her dress up box. The powder was shiny and pretty. She thought it would be fun to put the powder on her face, like her mommy did with her makeup. Lily put on her favorite fancy dress and sprinkled the powder on her cheeks. She felt like a real princess! She danced and twirled around her room, laughing and smiling. Her mommy came in and saw Lily all dressed up. She hugged Lily and said, "You look so beautiful, my little princess!"

One day, a big dog and a small cat were talking. They wanted to see who could cook the best food. They were competitive. The dog said, "I can cook better than you!" The cat said, "No, I can cook better than you!" They decided to have a cook-off. The dog cooked a big bone, and the cat cooked a small fish. As they cooked, a little boy came by. He saw a shiny stone on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to the dog and the cat. The stone was magic! The boy said, "This stone can make any food taste good!" The dog and the cat stopped cooking. They wanted the magic stone. The boy said, "You can both use the stone, but you must be friends and not fight." The dog and the cat agreed. They cooked together and their food tasted very good. They were happy and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a creative boy named Tim. He loved to build things with his toy blocks. One day, he wanted to make the biggest tower ever. Tim started to build and build. He was very happy. Tim's mom gave him juice to drink. He put the juice next to his tower. Tim kept building higher and higher. The tower was almost as tall as him! He was very proud. But then, Tim bumped the tower by accident. The tower started to shake. It fell down and knocked over the juice. The juice spilled all over the floor. Tim was sad. His tower was gone and the floor was wet.

One day, a little squirrel went out to find food. The squirrel was very hungry. He looked up and down, left and right, to see where he could find some nuts. As the squirrel walked, he saw a big tree with lots of nuts on it. The squirrel was happy and started to climb the tree. But when he got to the top, he saw a big bird. The squirrel was scared of the big bird. The squirrel quickly ran down the tree and went to a safe place. He was still hungry, but he was not scared anymore. The squirrel saw another tree with nuts and went there. This time, there was no big bird. The squirrel ate the nuts and was happy.

One day, Jack was out in his garden. He wanted to play with his toy engine. He ran to his shed to get it. He was so excited when he found it. It was brilliant! He carefully picked it up and hugged it. Jack went back outside and sat at his wooden table. He always loved playing with it. He pretended he was driving the engine. Suddenly, Jack saw a large truck pass by in the street. It had a big, shiny engine. He watched as it drove by. Jack ran to his mum. He said, "Mum, mum, did you see how brilliant that engine was?! Can I have a truck like that when I'm older?" His Mum smiled and said, "Maybe one day, Jack. Maybe one day!"

One day, a sailor went to the sea. He was a careless sailor. He did not look where he went. He saw a big fish in the water. The fish said, "I will scare you." The sailor said, "You cannot scare me." The fish got mad. The fish and the sailor began to fight. They pushed and hit each other. The sailor did not want to lose. Then, a big wave came. The wave was very big and strong. It pushed the sailor and the fish away. The sailor was scared now. The fish was scared too. The big wave took them far away from their homes. In the end, the careless sailor and the big fish were lost at sea. They could not find their way back home. They were both very sad and scared. They wished they had not fought with each other.
