Category: Intermediate
Title: Molly and the Shoes

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Molly. She was three year old and loved to play outside. One day, while she was playing, Molly found a puddle of muddy water. She stepped into it and the mud made her shoes very dirty. Molly looked around and noticed steam coming up from the ground. She was so excited and ran over to it. Molly stood in awe at the sight of the steam. She said, "Wow! That's so cool! What's that steam coming out of the ground?" Suddenly, there was a loud noise and the steam started to spring out of the ground. Molly was so surprised and jumped back. Molly was amazed. She laughed as the steam spiraled up into the sky. The dirt on Molly's shoes had all turned to mud by now. But she was too busy enjoying the sight of the steam to care. And eventually, Molly had to go home but she would remember the steam for a long time.

Once upon a time, there was a white dog named Spot. Spot liked to play in the yard. One day, Spot saw a big, shiny thing. It was made of iron. Spot wanted to bite the iron thing but knew he should resist. Spot ran to his friend, a little girl named Sue. "Sue," said Spot, "I found a big, shiny thing. I want to bite it, but I resist. What should I do?" Sue thought for a moment and said, "Let's go see the shiny thing together." When Spot and Sue went to the yard, the shiny iron thing was gone! Instead, there was a big, white cat. The cat looked at Spot and said, "I was hiding in the iron thing, but now I am free. Thank you for not biting it, Spot." Spot and Sue were surprised, but happy to have a new friend.

Lily liked to fix things with her dad. She had a small tool box with a hammer, a screwdriver and some nails. She also had a cap that her dad gave her. It was red and blue and had a star on it. She wore it every time she helped her dad. One day, they were fixing a chair that had a broken leg. Lily held the leg while her dad nailed it back. She was very careful not to hit her fingers. "Good job, Lily," her dad said. "You are a great helper." "Thank you, dad," Lily said. "I like to fix things with you. Can we fix something else?" Her dad looked around and saw a teddy bear on the floor. It had a rip on its ear and some stuffing was coming out. "How about we fix this teddy bear?" he asked. "It looks like it needs some love." Lily nodded and picked up the teddy bear. She felt its soft fur and hugged it. "Poor teddy," she said. "It must hurt." Her dad took a needle and some thread from his pocket. He showed Lily how to sew the rip. He let her pull the thread gently. "Be careful not to poke yourself," he warned. Lily was very careful. She followed her dad's instructions and soon the teddy bear's ear was fixed. She put the cap on its head and smiled. "There, teddy," she said. "Now you are all better. And you have a cap like me." Her dad smiled too and hugged her. "You are very kind, Lily," he said. "And you are a very good fixer. I'm proud of you." Lily felt happy. She loved her dad and her teddy bear. She loved to fix things with her cap. She felt very soft and warm.

Once upon a time, there was a silly girl called Lucy. One day, Lucy was feeling very angry and wanted to tell her mum. She could not say the words, so she slapped her mum instead. Mum was so shocked that she started to cry. Lucy did not know what to do. She was very sorry and hugged her mum. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise coming from outside. They ran out and saw their neighbour was very angry. He was shouting about the news. He was saying it was very stupid. Lucy decided to help. She took her mum's hand and walked over to the neighbour. She looked him in the eyes and said "It's okay". The neighbour smiled and stopped shouting. Lucy looked up at her mum and smiled. They both knew that it was best to be kind and listen to each other's news.
