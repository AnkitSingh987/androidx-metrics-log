Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Stone

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. One day, while playing in his yard, he found a stone. The stone was not pretty. It was difficult to see the good in it. But, Tim wanted to keep the stone. Tim wanted to improve the stone. He tried to make it pretty. He asked his mom for help. She said, "Wash the stone, Tim." So, Tim washed the stone with water. The stone became a little bit better. Next, Tim painted the stone with his favorite colors. He painted it red, blue, and yellow. The stone looked nice now. Tim was happy. He put the stone on his table. Now, the difficult stone was a pretty stone. Tim learned that he can improve things with some work and love.

Once there was a girl named Mia. She liked to wear her favorite outfit, a pink dress with lots of sparkles. One day, she was playing in the garden and she got too close to the fire. She felt the heat and it burned the dress! She was so ashamed. Mia ran to her mom and showed her what happened. Her mom shook her head and said, "Mia, why did you wear that dress so close to the fire?" Mia hung her head and felt sad. Mia was too embarrassed to show her friends her ruined dress. She decided to use her blanket to cover up the holes. At school, Mia was wearing her blanket and all the kids laughed at her. She wanted to run away but she didn't. She kept her head up and smiled even though her stomach was fluttering. Mia mustered up her courage and showed her friends the blanket. Then, she pointed her chin up and told them she was not ashamed. They all smiled and in their hearts, they knew Mia was brave. They gave her a big hug to show her they understood. Mia was so happy she smiled so big that her mouth hurt! Mia learned a very important lesson that day, she should never be ashamed of mistakes. They are part of life and they teach us to be brave.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little dog. They were very good friends. One day, they saw a big party. It was a marriage party. The cat and the dog wanted to go to the party. The cat said, "We should be quiet. We don't want to disturb the people at the marriage." The dog agreed and they both walked to the party. They saw a big cake and many people dancing. While they were watching, a harmless bug flew into the cake. The cat and the dog did not know what to do. The dog said, "We must save the cake!" The cat jumped and caught the bug. Everyone was surprised, but they were happy that the cat and the dog saved the cake. The cat and the dog became the heroes of the marriage party.

One sunny Tuesday, Tim and his old dog, Spot, went for a walk. The sun was shining bright in the sky. They went to the park to play. "Spot, go fetch the ball!" said Tim. Spot ran fast and got the ball. They played for a long time. Tim saw the sun shine on the pond. The water sparkled like gold. As they walked home, Tim saw his friend Sue. "Hi Sue, did you see the sun shine today?" he asked. Sue smiled and said, "Yes, it was so pretty!" Tim and Spot went home, happy from their day in the sun. They couldn't wait for another sunny day to play outside.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to a museum with her mom and dad. They saw many things like big animals, old toys, and pretty rocks. Lily loved the museum and wanted to see everything. In one room, there was a big dinosaur. Lily felt frightened when she saw it. She was scared it would come to life and chase her. Her mom and dad told her, "Don't worry, it's not real. It can't hurt you." But Lily still felt scared. Later, Lily thought about the big dinosaur and felt regret. She wished she had been brave and not scared. Next time they went to the museum, Lily promised herself she would be brave and have fun. And she did!
