Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was an elderly dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with a big yard. Every day, Max would play in the yard and get his ball. He loved to play fetch with his family. One sunny day, Max saw a big bird fly to the west. Max was curious and wanted to see where the bird was going. So, Max decided to follow the bird. He ran and ran, always looking up at the sky to see where the bird was flying. As Max went further west, he met new friends. He played with a little cat and a friendly rabbit. They all had fun together, and Max forgot about the bird. When it was time to go home, Max's new friends helped him find the way back to his house. Max was happy to be home, and he had a great adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to watch his dad chop wood with a big axe. He would sit and watch all day long. Tim wanted to be like his dad, but he was too small to use the axe. His dad always told him to be careful and never touch the axe. One day, Tim's dad was troubled. He had to go to the store and left Tim at home. Before he left, he told Tim not to touch the axe. Tim promised he would not touch it. But as soon as his dad was gone, Tim thought about how much he wanted to be like his dad. Tim went to the axe and picked it up. It was very heavy for him, but he tried to chop the wood like his dad. He swung the axe and missed the wood. The axe hit his foot and it hurt a lot. Tim was sad and scared. When his dad came home, he saw what had happened and was very upset. Tim learned that he should always listen to his dad and not touch things he was told not to touch.

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam lived in a big tree with his family. They liked to play and eat nuts all day. One day, Sam found a small seed on the ground. He asked his mom, "What is this?" She said, "That is a seed. If you plant it in the ground, it will grow into a tree." Sam was excited and wanted to see the tree grow. Sam dug a hole and put the seed in it. He covered it with dirt and watered it every day. He waited and waited. One day, Sam saw a tiny green thing coming out of the ground. "Look, Mom!" he said. "The tree is growing!" Sam's mom was proud of him. They watched the little tree grow bigger and bigger, and Sam knew he had done something very special.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty cat named Tom. Tom was strong and fast. He liked to help people. One day, Tom had a job to deliver a cord to his friend, Ann. Tom took the cord and went to Ann's house. On the way, he saw a little bird. The bird was sad because it lost its nest. Tom said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you find your nest." Tom and the bird looked for the nest. They found it on a tall tree. Tom used the cord to climb the tree. He put the nest back on the branch. The bird was very happy. Finally, Tom went to Ann's house and gave her the cord. Ann was happy too. Tom felt good because he helped his friends. He went back home and took a long nap.

Once upon a time, a little bunny was hopping through a dark, ancient forest. Suddenly, he heard a meow in the distance. He stopped and listened. The meow grew closer and closer. He hopped a little further and saw a beautiful, old cat. The cat looked ancient, and it meowed again, almost like it was talking to the bunny. The bunny hopped closer and asked, "Do you need help, kitty?" The cat paused and meowed again. The little bunny smiled and said, "OK, I'll help you cross the forest, if you want." The bunny and cat hopped together through the dark forest. When they reached the other side, the bunny hugged the ancient cat goodbye. He smiled and watched it hop away. The bunny never expected that the cat was a magical creature, sent to lead him to a mysterious, ancient adventure. But that’s a different story altogether.
