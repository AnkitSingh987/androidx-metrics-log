Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Lion

One sunny day, Tom and his mom decided to have an adventurous trip. They wanted to drive to the zoo. Tom was very excited because he loved animals. On their way to the zoo, Tom saw many big cars and colorful trees. He was so happy. He said, "Mom, I can't wait to see the animals!" His mom smiled and said, "We will be there soon, Tom." When they got to the zoo, Tom saw many animals. He saw a big lion, a tall giraffe, and a funny monkey. Tom and his mom had a great time at the zoo. They went home tired but happy.

One day, a fish named Fin wanted to swim in the calm sea. Fin loved to swim fast and make big loops in the water. He saw a boat with a long rope that had a knot. The knot was big and strong. Fin saw a crab named Carl near the knot. Fin said, "Hi Carl! Do you want to swim with me?" Carl the crab said, "Yes, Fin! I love to swim too!" So, Fin and Carl swam together in the calm sea. They swam around the big knot and played all day. They were very happy. The sun was warm, and the water was calm. Fin and Carl had lots of fun swimming and playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a map that he loved. One day, Tim went out to play with his map. He wanted to find a big hill to rise up and see the world. As Tim walked, he met a big, fierce dog. The dog was angry and wanted the map. Tim was scared, but he had an idea. He threw the map up in the air, and the dog looked up too. When the dog looked up, Tim gave the dog a big hug. The dog felt the love and became nice. The dog helped Tim rise up the hill. They looked at the world together and became best friends. The dog was not fierce anymore, and they had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. Lisa was a troubled girl because she lost her toy cat. She was very sad and did not know where to look for it. One day, Lisa saw a big star in the sky. The star was shining very bright. Lisa said, "Star, can you help me find my toy cat?" The star said, "Yes, Lisa, I will help you." The star took Lisa on a journey to find her toy cat. After a while, they found the toy cat under a big tree. Lisa was so happy! The star said, "I have a reward for you, Lisa, because you were brave and did not give up." The star gave Lisa a shiny new toy cat to play with. Lisa thanked the star and went back home with her two toy cats. From that day on, Lisa was not troubled anymore. She played with her toy cats and always remembered the shining star that helped her.

One day, a little bee was flying in the wide sky. The bee was looking for pretty flowers to get sweet food. The bee liked to fly high and fast. The bee saw a big tree with many flowers. The bee called to its friends, "Come here, friends! I found a big tree with many flowers!" The bee's friends heard the call and came to the big tree. Together, the bee and its friends got sweet food from the flowers. They were very happy. They called it a good day and went back to their home. The wide sky was full of their happy buzz.

Lily was very happy today. She was going to a party with her friends. She had a special costume to wear. It was a fairy costume with wings and a wand. She liked to pretend she was a fairy and do magic. She got in the car with her mom and dad. They drove to the party. Lily saw many other cars and people. She saw some of her friends. They had costumes too. She saw a pirate, a princess, a lion, and a clown. They all looked very fun. Lily and her parents arrived at the party. They got out of the car and walked to the door. Lily rang the bell. The door opened. It was Ben, the birthday boy. He had an ordinary costume. He was wearing a shirt and pants and a hat. Lily was confused. Why did he not have a fun costume? "Hi, Lily!" Ben said. "Welcome to my party! I like your costume. It is very pretty." "Thank you, Ben," Lily said. "But why are you wearing an ordinary costume? Don't you want to be something else?" Ben smiled. He took off his hat. He had a surprise. He had a big red wig on his head. He was really a clown! "Surprise!" Ben said. "I am a clown! I wanted to trick you and make you laugh. Do you like my costume now?" Lily laughed. She liked Ben's costume. It was very funny. She gave him a hug. They went inside to join the other friends. They had a lot of fun at the party. They played games, ate cake, and sang songs. Lily was glad she came. She liked wearing her fairy costume and seeing all the other costumes. She liked Ben's ordinary costume the best. It was a good surprise.
