Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bench and the Park

Once upon a time, in a charming little park, there was a small bench. On sunny days, kids would play near the bench, and their moms and dads would sit and watch. One day, a little boy named Tim and his mom came to the park. Tim saw the bench and said, "Mommy, can we sit on the bench?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's sit down and watch the other kids play." As they sat on the bench, Tim saw a big leaf on the bench. He wanted to remove it, so he picked it up and threw it away. His mom said, "Good job, Tim! You helped make the bench clean for others to sit on." Tim felt happy and proud. He played with his friends at the park and had a great day. From that day on, Tim always made sure to keep the charming little bench clean and tidy for everyone to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat rice every day. Tim was very enthusiastic about helping his mom cook the rice. One day, Tim's mom told him that they would make a big surprise with the rice. Tim was so excited that he could not wait. The next day, Tim and his mom went to the store. They bought a big bag of rice. Tim tried to lift the bag, but it was too heavy. His mom helped him lift the bag and they took it home. Tim knew that the surprise was going to be very big and fun. Finally, it was time for the big surprise. Tim's mom cooked the rice and made it into a big rice mountain. Tim was so happy to see it. He invited all his friends to come over and they all ate the rice mountain together. The surprise was a big success and Tim felt proud that he helped his mom make it.

Julia was playing with her toys in the backyard. Suddenly, she heard a noise. She looked up and saw a tiny, friendly creature. It had wings and colourful makeup on its face. Julia gasped, surprised. "Who are you?" she asked. The creature smiled. "I'm a fairy," she said. "I own this garden. Can I be your friend?" Julia smiled. "Yes please!" The fairy and Julia played together for the rest of the day. They talked, laughed and explored the garden. When it was time to go, Julia and the fairy said goodbye. Years later, Julia still remembered her special day with the friendly fairy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a loyal dog named Skip. Skip loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. Every day, they would skip and hold hands in the park. One day, while they were playing, they saw a big, dark cloud in the sky. Tim looked at the cloud and felt scared. But Skip, being a loyal dog, stayed by his side. They decided to go back home before it started to rain. As they walked home, the dark cloud got closer and closer. Suddenly, the rain came pouring down. Tim and Skip tried to run, but they slipped and fell into a deep puddle. They couldn't get out, and the rain kept coming. In the end, Tim and Skip were very sad and wet.

Once upon a time, there was a little baby named Tim. Tim was very obedient. He always stayed in his crib when his mom told him to. One day, Tim saw a big, colorful ball hanging from the ceiling. "Mom, can I play with the ball?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but only if you stay in your crib." Tim was so happy! He jumped and reached for the ball, but it was too high. He tried and tried, but he just couldn't get it. Tim felt sad. Then, a friendly bird flew into the room. The bird saw Tim and the ball. The bird picked up the ball with its beak and dropped it into Tim's crib. Tim was so surprised and happy! He played with the ball all day. The moral of the story is that good things come to those who are obedient and patient.
