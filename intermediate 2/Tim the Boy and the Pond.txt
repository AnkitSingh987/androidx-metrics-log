Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pond

One day, a boy named Tim went on a trip with his mom and dad. They went to a big park with lots of trees, grass, and a big pond. Tim was very happy to be there. At the pond, Tim saw a man with a box. The man opened the box and released some little ducks into the water. Tim clapped his hands and said, "Look, mom! The man let the ducks go swim!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, the ducks are happy now." After playing in the park, Tim and his family had a yummy picnic. They ate sandwiches, fruits, and cookies. Tim said, "This is the best trip ever!" His mom and dad laughed and agreed. They all had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear called Riley. She was a small, cuddly creature who was always full of energy. One night, Riley went outside to find a shelter to sleep in. She went to her favourite tree, but soon noticed it was a bit too cold. Riley then looked around and found a hollow log, so she quickly snuggled inside and closed her eyes. While she was sleeping, Riley heard someone talking to her. It was a sweet old lady. She said “Good evening, little one! What are you doing out here in the cold?” Riley opened her eyes and said “I’m just looking for a safe place to sleep.” The old lady smiled and said “Well, this is a good spot for you. It’s nice and warm. I’ll be on my way now. Sweet dreams!” Riley smiled and thanked the old lady before settling in for the night. From then on, Riley could always count on the old lady's shelter when she needed a place to sleep.

Once upon a time there were two friends called Lily and Jack. They both wanted to play chess together. Lily asked her mum if she could permit them to play. All of a sudden, an old strong man appeared from behind a tree. He asked to join them, and said he would teach them how to play! Lily's mum was worried, but decided to permit them. When they sat down, the old man showed them the pieces and explained the rules of the game. Eventually, Lily and Jack were getting better and better at chess. The old man was very proud - they had become strong chess players. The old man said goodbye and thanked the two friends. They thanked him back and then went off to play more chess. They now played it all the time, and it was lots of fun!

Ben and Lily liked to play near the old mill. It was a big house with a wheel that turned in the water. Sometimes they saw birds and fish and frogs there. They also liked to look for shiny things in the dirt. One day, they found a small box with a lock. It was rusty and dirty, but they thought it might have something good inside. They tried to open it, but it was too hard. "Maybe we need a key," Ben said. "Or maybe we can break it," Lily said. They looked for a rock or a stick, but they could not find anything. They were about to give up, when they heard a voice. "Hello, children. What are you doing here?" They turned around and saw an old man with a long beard and a hat. He was holding a cane and a bag. He smiled at them. "We found this box, sir. But we can't open it," Ben said. "Let me see," the old man said. He took the box and looked at it. He nodded and said, "This is a very special box. It belongs to me. I left it here a long time ago, when I worked at the mill. It has a gift inside, for someone who is gifted." "Who is gifted, sir?" Lily asked. "Someone who can do something very well, or has a special talent," the old man said. He looked at Ben and Lily and said, "Do you have any gifts, children?" Ben and Lily thought for a moment. Ben liked to draw and paint. He could make beautiful pictures of the mill and the animals. Lily liked to sing and dance. She could make lovely sounds and move gracefully. "I can draw and paint, sir," Ben said. "I can sing and dance, sir," Lily said. The old man smiled and said, "Then you are both gifted, children. And I have a gift for you. Here, take this key. It will open the box. But you have to promise me something. You have to share the gift with each other, and with anyone who needs it. Can you do that?" Ben and Lily nodded and said, "Yes, sir. We promise." The old man gave them the key and said, "Good. Now, open the box and see what's inside." Ben and Lily used the key and opened the box. They gasped and smiled. Inside the box, there were many things. There were crayons and paints and brushes and paper for Ben. There were ribbons and bells and feathers and flowers for Lily. There were also candies and cookies and chocolates for both of them. "Wow! This is amazing!" Ben said. "Thank you, sir! Thank you so much!" Lily said. They looked at the old man, but he was gone. They only saw his hat and his cane and his bag. They were lying on the ground, near the mill. They picked them up and wiped them with their hands. They saw a note on the bag. It said: "Dear children, I hope you enjoy your gifts. They are from the mill fairy. He likes to help gifted children like you. He also likes to play tricks. He turned me into an old man, so I could give you the box. But now he has turned me back into a bird. I have to fly away, but I will always remember you. Be kind, be happy, be gifted. Love, the mill fairy." Ben and Lily looked at the sky and saw a bird flying away. It was blue and yellow and red. It sang a beautiful song. They waved and said, "Goodbye, mill fairy! Thank you for the gifts!" They hugged each other and laughed. They decided to use their gifts and make something for the mill fairy. They also decided to share their gifts with their friends and family. They were very happy. They knew they had met a very special friend. They knew they had a very special day.
