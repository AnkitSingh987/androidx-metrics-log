Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Grumpy Cat and the Door

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to play with the other cats. He liked to sleep all day. One day, Tom's friend, a happy dog named Max, called him on a video. "Hi, Tom!" said Max. "Do you want to play outside with me?" Tom looked at the video and said, "No, I am grumpy. I want to sleep." But Max did not give up. He went to Tom's house and knocked on the door. "Please, Tom, let's play!" Max said. Tom opened the door and saw the sunshine. He decided to give it a try. They played and had fun. Tom was not grumpy anymore.

Once upon a time there was a kind puppy. He was very happy and loved to play. One day he found a long rope and he wanted to attach it to something. So he looked around and eventually he found a big tree in the forest. He ran over to it and quickly attached the rope. After that he had so much fun! He pulled the rope, jumped and ran around the tree until he was exhausted. Finally the kind puppy laid down in the grass and happily fell asleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. Her name was Lily. Lily liked to play hide and seek with her friends. They played in the park where it was safe. One day, Lily found a new hiding spot. It was behind a big tree. She thought it was a great place to hide. Her friends looked and looked, but they could not find her. Lily felt happy and safe in her hiding spot. After a while, Lily heard her name. It was her mom calling her. She came out from her hiding spot and ran to her mom. Lily told her mom about the fun game of hide and seek. Her mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you had fun and stayed safe." They went home together, and Lily couldn't wait to play hide and seek again.

Once upon a time, there was a nice little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys in his room. One day, Tim's mom told him to clean up his room and put his toys in the closet. Tim did not like to clean up, but he knew it was important. Tim started to pick up his toys and put them in the closet. He found a jacket with a zip on the floor. Tim put on the jacket and zipped it up. He felt warm and happy in his nice jacket. Then, he continued to put the toys in the closet. When Tim was done, his room was clean and his toys were in the closet. Tim's mom came in and saw the nice, clean room. She was very happy and gave Tim a big hug. Tim felt proud of his work and knew that keeping his room clean was a good thing to do.

Once there was a girl who had a name but no one knew what it was. She was all alone and had no friends. One day while walking around she heard a sound that sounded like a bell ringing. She got happy and ran to find the source of the sound. She followed the ringing sound until she came to an old house. She opened the door and saw a strange figure. The figure asked her what her name was but she didn't know. The figure then said that if she didn't know her name he would have to take her away with him. The girl tried to run away but she couldn't. The figure grabbed her and took her away to an unknown place where she was never seen again.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Ben. He was three years old and always loved to count. He had a white bookcase in his bedroom and he would spend hours counting the books on the shelves. One day his mommy asked him to go help her count some stuff, so he went with her. When they got in the kitchen, Mommy asked Ben to count the eggs in the basket. Ben was so excited and quickly began counting. Daddy came in the kitchen and said to Ben, "No! You can't count those eggs! They are for dinner!" Ben was so disappointed and started to cry. After dinner Ben asked Daddy, "Can I count the books on my bookcase?" Daddy told him that he was too tired because of work and to go to bed. Ben was really sad so he went to his room. As he looked at the white bookcase he sadly said, "No more counting for me." And from that day on, Ben never counted anything again.
