Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to explore and today she was going to explore the backyard. As she went out the door, she noticed something shiny under the garden hose. When she bent down closer, she saw it was a big red ribbon. Lucy wanted to show the ribbon to her mom, so she grabbed the ribbon and started to tie it around her waist. But then the garden hose started to shake and something strange happened. The garden hose snaked out of the ground and started to wrap itself around Lucy. She tried to open the hose but it was too tight and she couldn't get away. Lucy started to panic but she kept on trying. Finally she opened the garden hose but it was too late. The hose kept her from running away and it eventually squeezed her until she couldn't breath. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a girl named Sue sitting on the grass. Sue was weeping. Tim asked, "Why are you weeping, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my toy. It was my favorite toy." Tim wanted to help Sue. He was an honest boy. So, they looked for the toy together. They looked under a big tree, but the toy was not there. Then, they looked near the flowers, but the toy was not there either. Suddenly, they saw a dog running with a toy in its mouth. It was Sue's toy! The dog gave the toy back to Sue, and she was very happy. Tim and Sue thanked the dog and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy pipe. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. He took his pipe with him. At the park, Tim saw a new kid. The new kid looked sad and alone. Tim went to the new kid and said, "Hi, do you want to play with me and my friends? We will welcome you!" The new kid smiled and said, "Yes, thank you!" They all played together and had fun. While playing, Tim's pipe fell on the ground and became empty. Tim was sad. But then, the new kid found a big bag of fun toys to share with everyone. Tim learned that sharing and welcoming new friends can bring more fun and happiness. And that sometimes, an empty pipe can lead to a full heart.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily had a doll. She loved her doll very much. It was her best friend. They played together every day. One day, Lily and her family went to the park. Lily brought her doll with her. They played near a tree. Lily saw a butterfly and ran to look. When she came back, her doll was not there. She was sad and looked everywhere. Lily saw something far away. She walked to it and saw it was her doll. She knew it was her doll. She was happy again. They went home and played more games. And they were never far apart again.

One day, a friendly cat went for a walk. She saw a big tree and had an urge to climb it. So, she went up the tree and found a nice spot to sit. The friendly cat looked around and saw a big bird on a branch. "Hi, bird! Want to play?" the cat asked. The bird said, "Okay, let's play hide and seek." The cat closed her eyes and started to count. The bird flew away to hide. While the cat was counting, a big wind came and started to shake the tree. The cat felt scared and wanted to go down. But she was stuck! The wind began to strike the tree harder, and the cat felt more scared. Just then, the bird came back with a big surprise. It was a long rope! The bird said, "Hold on, cat! I will help you!" The cat held the rope, and the bird helped her get down from the tree. They were now best friends and played together every day.
