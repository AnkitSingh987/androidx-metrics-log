Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Food

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to jump and play. One day, Spot saw some food on the table. He was hungry, so he jumped up to get it. Spot was fearful, but he wanted the food very much. Spot jumped and jumped, trying to reach the food. He was so close! Finally, he jumped very high and got the food. Spot was happy and started to eat the food. It tasted so good! But then, Spot began to feel sick. His tummy hurt and he didn't want to play or jump anymore. Spot's mom found out that he ate the food and was sad. She told Spot that the food was not for him. Spot learned a lesson, but he still felt very sick. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy car. One day, Tim saw a door with a lock. He was very curious about what was behind the door. Tim tried to push the door, but it was hard to open. He thought and thought about how to open the door. Then, he found a key under a mat. Tim was very happy and excited to use the key. Tim put the key in the lock and turned it. The door opened, and he went inside. But, oh no! A big, mean dog was behind the door. The dog barked and chased Tim. Tim was very scared and ran away. He learned not to open doors with locks without asking for help.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lilly. She had a magic screw. One day, Lilly found out that the screw could make things shrink. Lilly felt tired, but she wanted to play with her toys. She thought, "I can make my bed shrink, so I can play more!" So, she used the magic screw to make her bed very small. But now, Lilly was too big for her bed! She felt sad. She used the magic screw again to make her bed big. Lilly learned that it is good to rest when you are tired. She went to sleep and had happy dreams.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was a pupil at a small school. She had a big, red ball that she loved to play with. One day, the ball became dull because she played with it too much. Amy was sad. Amy's mom saw her sad face and had an idea. She found a pretty ribbon and decided to tie it around the ball. Amy watched as her mom tied the ribbon into a beautiful bow. The ribbon made the dull ball look nice again. Now, Amy and her friends played with the ball at school every day. They loved how the ribbon made the ball look special. The other pupils wanted to tie ribbons around their balls too. And they all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jane. She awoke one bright morning and asked her mom, "What is today?" Her mom answered, "Today is a beautiful day." Jane smiled, happy with the answer. She opened her window and looked up at the light sky. She saw birds flying and asked, "Where are they going?" Her mom replied, "They are going to get food for their babies." Jane was curious and questioned, "What do they eat?" Her mom answered, "They eat little insects and seeds. Now go get dressed, it's a new day." Jane smiled and quickly put on her clothes. She skipped outside and picked a bright flower. She admired the light color and asked her mom, "What kind of flower is this?" Her mom smiled and answered, "That is a daffodil." Jane was happy and cheered. She twirled around in the morning light and shouted, "Today is a beautiful day!"

Tim and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. They saw a big hole under a tree. They wondered what was inside. "Let's go and look," Tim said. He was brave and curious. "No, don't go," Lily said. She was scared and careful. "It might be dangerous. There might be a rat or a snake or a spider." Tim did not listen. He decided to go and look. He crawled to the hole and peeked inside. He saw something furry and brown. It had a long tail and sharp teeth. It was a rat! The rat saw Tim and hissed. It was angry and hungry. It jumped out of the hole and ran towards Tim. Tim screamed and ran away. He was sorry and afraid. Lily heard Tim and ran to him. She hugged him and comforted him. She was glad and relieved. "Are you okay?" she asked. "Yes, I'm okay," Tim said. "But I was wrong. You were right. The hole was dangerous. There was a rat. It was scary and mean." "I'm sorry you saw a rat," Lily said. "But I'm happy you are safe. Next time, let's stay away from holes. They are not fun. They are trouble." Tim nodded and agreed. He learned his lesson. He thanked Lily for being his friend. They decided to play somewhere else. They found a sandbox and a ball. They had fun and forgot about the rat.
