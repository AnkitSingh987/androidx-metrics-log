Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Hat

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a toy dog named Stitch. Stitch was a fake dog, but Lily loved him very much. They played together every day. One day, Lily's mom said they were going to celebrate her birthday. Lily was very happy. She wanted Stitch to be a part of her big day. She put a party hat on Stitch and they played games together. When it was time to eat cake, Lily made sure Stitch had a piece too. They had a great time celebrating together. All of Lily's friends liked Stitch too. At the end of the day, Lily gave Stitch a big hug and thanked him for being her best friend.

---

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a furry dog playing with a ball. Mia wanted to play too. She went to the dog and said, "Hi dog, can I play with you?" The dog was happy and wagged its tail. Mia threw the ball and the dog ran to catch it. They played for a long time. Mia's mom called her and said, "Mia, it's time to go home." Mia was sad but she knew it was time to leave. Before they left, Mia's mom gave her a big lollipop. Mia was so happy. But the furry dog jumped up and tried to eat the lollipop. Mia said, "No dog, this is mine!" The dog didn't listen and the lollipop fell on the ground. The lollipop was spoiled. Mia was sad, but the dog licked her face to say sorry. Mia laughed and forgave the dog. She knew they would play again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Bobby. Bobby loved to crawl all around the big garden. One day, Bobby saw a high building near the garden. He had never seen such a big building before. Bobby thought, "I want to crawl up that high building!" So, he started to crawl up the building. It was not easy, but Bobby was brave and strong. He crawled and crawled until he was very high up. When Bobby reached the top of the high building, he saw the whole garden from up there. He felt so happy and proud. Then, he slowly crawled back down to the garden. Bobby couldn't wait to tell his friends about his big adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a big clock in the middle of the town. Everyone used the clock to know the time. The clock was always available for everyone to see. It was a very important clock. One day, a little girl named Sally saw a small bug near the clock. The bug was lost and needed help. Sally asked the bug, "Do you need help, little bug?" The bug nodded and said, "Yes, please. I want to go home." Sally decided to lead the bug home. They walked together, and the bug showed her the way. They walked past the big clock and many other things. Finally, they reached the bug's home. The bug was very happy and thanked Sally. Sally felt good for helping the bug, and they became good friends.

One day, a man named Mark put on his suit. He was very tall and his suit was high on his legs. Mark wanted to go to a party with his friends. He was very excited to see them and play games. At the party, Mark played a game with his friends. They had to jump high and touch the sky. Mark jumped the highest because of his long legs. Everyone was happy and clapped for him. Then, something unexpected happened. Mark's suit started to grow! It grew so big that it covered all his friends. They all laughed and played under the big suit. It was like a fun tent. Mark and his friends had the best time at the party.

John and his mom were walking in the park. John saw a lemon tree and he said, "Look mommy, a lemon tree!" John's mom said, "Yes, and they look so juicy and bright!" John was excited and he asked, "Can I spray one?" John's mom said, "Sure, go ahead and try it." John put his hand under the lemon and he sprayed. He smiled and said, "That was fun!" Then John looked around and he saw other children playing in the park. John said, "They all look so happy and I want to play too." John's mom said, "You can feel a bit envious, but try to be happy for them too." John nodded and said, "I will try my best." John and his mom continued their walk in the park and had a great day.
