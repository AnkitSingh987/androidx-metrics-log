Category: Intermediate
Title: The Orange Snake and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived an orange snake. The snake was very long and liked to play in the sun. One day, a small boy came to the forest to play. The boy saw the orange snake and wanted to be friends. The boy went near the snake and asked a question. He said, "Hello, snake. Can we play together?" The snake looked at the boy and thought about it. The snake said, "Yes, we can play. Let's play hide and seek." The boy and the snake played hide and seek for a long time. They had a lot of fun. But then, the snake started to feel very hungry. The snake looked at the boy and thought the boy would make a good meal. The snake opened its big mouth and ate the boy. The boy was gone, and the snake was not hungry anymore. The orange snake went back to play in the sun, but it was not happy. It missed the boy and wished they could still be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was not an ordinary girl. She loved to paint. One day, she found a big box. She did not know what was in it. She thought, "I will guess what is in the box." Sue thought and thought. She guessed that there was paint in the box. She was very excited. She opened the box and found a lot of paint. She was very happy. She thought, "I will paint something fun today." Sue started to paint. She painted a big sun and a blue sky. She used all the paint from the box. Then, something unexpected happened. The paint on her paper started to move. The sun and sky came alive! Sue was very surprised. The sun and sky were not ordinary paint. They were magic paint! Sue played with the sun and sky. She had lots of fun. She knew that she was not an ordinary girl. She was a girl with magic paint. From that day on, Sue painted many more magic things. And she lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was ready for a big race at school. He wanted to win a vacation for his family. Tim had been practicing every day, running as fast as he could. The day of the race came, and all the kids lined up. Tim was excited and a little scared. The teacher said, "Ready, set, go!" and all the kids started to run. Tim ran as fast as he could, his little legs moving quickly. Tim saw the finish line and knew he was close to winning. He ran faster and faster. Finally, he crossed the finish line and won the race! Tim's family was so happy and proud of him. They got ready for the vacation Tim won, and they had the best time together.

One day, a tired little girl named Lily was walking with her mom. They were going to the store to buy a new dress. Lily loved to dress up in fun clothes. She liked fashion very much. At the store, Lily saw a beautiful dress with flowers on it. She wanted to try it on, but she felt too tired. Her mom said, "Lily, I encourage you to try on the dress. You will look so pretty in it." Lily put on the dress and looked in the mirror. She felt happy and not tired anymore. She said, "Mom, I love this dress! Thank you for encouraging me to try it on." Her mom smiled and they bought the dress. They went home and Lily showed her new dress to her friends, who all loved it too.

One day, a pretty cat and a dog were playing near a mine. They liked to act like they were digging for gold. The cat said, "I want to find gold in the mine." The dog said, "No, I want to find gold." The pretty cat and dog started to fight. They both wanted the gold in the mine. They acted mean to each other. The cat said, "The gold is mine!" The dog said, "No, it is mine!" The pretty cat and dog were so busy fighting, they did not see the big hole in the ground. They both fell into the hole. They could not get out. They were sad because they did not find gold and were stuck in the hole. The end.
