Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bee and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little bee. The bee lived in a hive. The hive was her home. She had many friends in the hive. They all worked together. One day, the little bee found a big flower. The flower was very pretty. The bee wanted to get some juice from the flower. She knew the juice would be very good. So, she decided to squeeze the flower. The juice came out and the bee was happy. The bee took the juice back to the hive. All her friends loved the juice. They said it was the best juice ever. It was like an expensive gift. The little bee and her friends were very happy. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bald king. He lived in a big castle. One day, he saw the sun shine very bright. The king was very happy. He liked when the sun shined. The king went outside to play. He met a little boy. The boy said, "Hi, King! I like your bald head. It shines like the sun." The king laughed and said, "Thank you, little boy. I like the sun too." They played all day under the shining sun. The king and the boy had so much fun. When the sun went down, they said goodbye. The bald king went back to his castle, happy and tired.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom and a fast dog named Max were playing in the park. Tom liked to sleep and Max liked to zoom around. They were best friends. Tom said, "Max, I have an opinion. I think we should race to the big tree." Max agreed and they got ready to race. Tom was sure he would win because he had a secret plan. As the race began, Max zoomed ahead. Tom yawned and slowly started to walk. But then, he saw a big box near the path. Tom hid inside the box and waited for Max to come back. Max reached the big tree and wondered where Tom was. He zoomed back to find his friend. When Max passed the box, Tom jumped out and said, "Boo!" Max was surprised and Tom laughed. In the end, Tom's secret plan worked, and they both had fun. They played in the park until the sun went down, happy to be best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a woman who liked to chew on icy things. One day, she went to the park with her friend. They sat down on a bench and started to talk. "Look what I have," said the woman. She took out an icy treat and began to chew on it. "Yummy!" she said. Her friend looked at her and wanted to try some too. Just then, a big bird flew down and took the icy treat from the woman's hand. The woman and her friend were very surprised. They laughed and said, "Silly bird! Now we have to get more icy treats!" So, they went to buy more treats and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a modest collar with his name on it. One day, Spot met a pretty dog named Daisy. Spot and Daisy liked to play together. One sunny day, Spot said to Daisy, "I want to marry you. We can be a happy dog family." Daisy wagged her tail and said, "Yes, Spot! Let's get married and be together." So, Spot and Daisy wore their nice collars and had a small party. All their friends came to see them marry. They played, ate yummy food, and had a lot of fun. Spot and Daisy were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a fake penguin toy that she loved very much. Lucy took her penguin with her everywhere she went. She liked to pretend that she owned a real penguin. One sunny day, Lucy and her family went to the park. Lucy played with her fake penguin on the grass. Suddenly, a real penguin waddled up to Lucy! She was very surprised. Lucy thought her toy had turned into a real penguin! Lucy showed the real penguin to her mom and dad. They laughed and told her that the penguin had escaped from the zoo nearby. The zookeepers came and took the penguin back to the zoo. Lucy was happy to see a real penguin, but she still loved her fake penguin toy.
