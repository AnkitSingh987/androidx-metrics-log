Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong animal named Bob. Bob had a big horn on his head. He liked to play games with his friends. Bob was very good at games, so he was competitive. One day, Bob and his friends played a new game. They had to fold a paper into a fun shape. Bob tried to fold the paper with his horn. But it was hard. His friends laughed at him. Bob felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird named Tim saw Bob and wanted to help. Tim used his beak to fold the paper for Bob. They made a beautiful shape together. Bob was happy, and he learned that it was okay to ask for help. From that day on, Bob and Tim were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there lived a lazy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to sleep all day and play all night. One sunny day, Fluffy met a new friend, a little bird named Chirpy. "Hi, I'm Chirpy!" said the bird. "Do you want to play with me?" Fluffy was not excited to play. "No, thank you. I'm too lazy to play," said Fluffy. But Chirpy did not give up. The next day, Chirpy came back with a fun ball. "Look, Fluffy! This ball will excite you!" Chirpy said. Fluffy looked at the ball and started to feel excited. Fluffy and Chirpy played with the ball all day long. From that day on, Fluffy was not lazy anymore. Fluffy and Chirpy played together every day, and they became the best of friends. The nation was happy to see Fluffy and Chirpy playing and having fun together. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a pretty bird sat on a tree. The bird saw a box under the tree. The bird wanted to peek inside the box. The bird made a promise to itself. "I will only look, not take," it said. The pretty bird flew down and peeked in the box. Inside, there were many pretty things. There were shiny rocks, soft toys, and yummy food. The bird was happy but did not take anything. It kept its promise. Then, something unexpected happened. The box started to shake and out came a big, friendly dog. The dog said, "Thank you for not taking anything from my box!" The pretty bird and the dog became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a girl named Sally went to the mall with her mom. Sally loved the mall. It had many toys and games. Sally was happy to be there. At the mall, Sally saw a big, red ball. She wanted to play with it. But another boy named Tim was playing with it too. Sally asked Tim, "Can I play with the ball too?" Tim said, "No, it's mine!" Sally was sad and started to cry. Sally's mom heard her cry and came to help. She said, "Tim, it's nice to share. Let Sally play with the ball too." Tim thought about it and felt sorry. He said, "Okay, Sally can play with the ball too." Sally stopped crying and both kids played with the ball together. They had fun at the mall and became friends.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play together. Today they are in the park. They have a ball and a bike. "Ben, do you want to go to school?" Lily asks. "Yes, I do. School is fun. We learn new things and play with other kids," Ben says. "What do you learn at school?" Lily wants to know. "We learn letters and numbers and colors and shapes. We also sing songs and draw pictures and read stories. Do you like those things?" Ben asks. "Yes, I do. I like them a lot. But I don't go to school yet. I stay at home with Mommy. She teaches me some things too. But I want to go to school like you," Lily says. "Maybe you can go to school next year. Then we can be in the same class. That would be nice," Ben says. "Yes, that would be nice. But I have to be tidy first. Mommy says I have to tidy my room and my toys before I can go to school. She says school is for tidy kids. Are you tidy, Ben?" Lily asks. "Sometimes I am, sometimes I'm not. I try to be tidy, but sometimes I forget. I have to tidy my bed and my clothes and my books every day. Teacher says we have to be tidy at school too. She says tidy kids are smart kids. Do you want to be smart, Lily?" Ben asks. "Yes, I do. I want to be smart and tidy and go to school. I think I will tidy my room and my toys today. Then Mommy will be happy and let me go to school soon. Will you help me, Ben?" Lily asks. "Sure, I will help you, Lily. We can tidy together. Then we can play again. Tidying can be fun too, if we do it with a friend," Ben says. They smile and hug. Then they go to Lily's house to tidy her room and her toys. They are good friends. They like to discuss things. They are excited to go to school.
