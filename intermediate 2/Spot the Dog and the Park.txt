Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run in the park. Every day, he would wag his tail and wait for his friend, Sam. Sam was a little boy who liked to play with Spot. One sunny day, Sam and Spot went to the park to play. They played catch, and Spot would follow Sam wherever he went. They were very happy. After playing, Sam and Spot sat under a big tree. They shared a yummy snack and laughed together. Soon, it was time to go home. Sam and Spot walked back, excited to play again in the park the next day.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. They had a big garden with many flowers and trees. One day, they saw a new flower that was very big and pink. It had not been there before. "Wow, look at that flower!" Ben said. "It is so pretty and bright. I wonder what it is." Lily nodded. She wanted to touch the flower, but it was too high. She tried to jump, but she could not reach it. "Maybe we can get a ladder," she said. They ran to the shed and found a small ladder. They carried it to the flower and put it against the tree. Ben climbed up first and reached for the flower. But as soon as he touched it, something moved inside. "Ouch!" Ben cried. "Something bit me!" He let go of the flower and fell off the ladder. Lily ran to help him. She saw a small cut on his finger. She also saw two big eyes and a beak poking out of the flower. "It is not a flower!" Lily said. "It is an owl!" The owl looked at them and hooted. It was not happy that they had disturbed its nest. It flapped its wings and flew away. Ben and Lily watched the owl disappear in the sky. They felt sorry for bothering it, but they also felt amazed. They had never seen an owl before. "That was not boring at all," Ben said. "That was exciting!" Lily agreed. She hugged her brother and said, "Let's go and tell Mom and Dad what we saw. Maybe they will know more about owls." They ran to the house, holding hands. They did not notice that the other flowers in the garden had started to bloom. They were too busy talking about their adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a very good girl who always tried to behave. She loved to play with her toys and listen to the bell in the town square. One day, Mia went to the town square with her mom. They saw a pretty dress that would fit Mia just right. Mia asked her mom, "Can I have the dress, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can have the dress because you always behave." Mia was very happy to have the new dress. She wore it to a party and danced to the sound of the bell. Everyone said Mia looked very nice in her dress. Mia had a fun day and remembered to always behave.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very popular. He had many friends. Tim loved to play with his friends every day. They played with balls, toy cars, and dolls. Tim was always happy. One day, Tim got a new toy. It was a bag with a zip. Tim wanted to show it to his friends. He said, "Look at my new toy! It has a zip!" His friends looked at the toy and liked it too. They all wanted to play with the toy. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's add our toys to the bag with the zip!" All the friends added their toys to the bag. They zipped it up and took turns carrying it. They played together all day. Tim and his friends were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot liked to jog every day. He would jog up and down the street. Spot was a good dog. One day, while Spot was jogging, he saw a big sign. The sign said, "No dogs can jog here. It is the law." Spot did not know what the law was, but he stopped jogging. Spot was sad. He did not want to break the law. So, he walked up and down the street instead. But then, something bad happened. A big cat jumped out and scared Spot. Spot ran away fast, but the cat was faster. Spot knew he had to do something unexpected. He decided to break the law and started to jog again. The cat was surprised and could not catch Spot anymore. In the end, Spot learned that sometimes, it is not bad to break the law if it helps you. But, he still felt sad because he knew he did something wrong.
