Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Sam and the Hill

One day, Lily and Sam went to the park with their mom. They saw a lot of snow on the ground. They wanted to play with the snow. They made snowballs and threw them at each other. They had fun. Then, they saw a big hill. They wanted to slide down the hill. They found a big plastic bag. They put the bag on the snow. They sat on the bag. They pushed the bag with their feet. The bag started to slide down the hill. But the bag was too fast. It went past the end of the hill. It went into the street. A car was coming. The car honked. The car stopped. The driver got out. He was angry. He yelled at Lily and Sam. He said they were bad. He said they could have hurt themselves and him. Lily and Sam were scared. They got off the bag. They ran to their mom. They said they were sorry. They said they did not mean to go into the street. They said they just wanted to have fun. Their mom hugged them. She said she was glad they were okay. She said they should be careful. She said they should not slide on the street. She said they should stay on the hill. She said she loved them. Lily and Sam felt embarrassed. They said they loved their mom too. They said they would not do it again. They learned their lesson. They gained some wisdom. They went back to the hill. They played with the snow. They had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and eat. His favorite food was sausage. One day, while Spot was playing in the park, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Spot wanted to help Sue. He said, "I recommend we look for your toy together." Sue smiled and they started to search. They looked under the slide, behind the swings, and in the sandbox. Finally, they found the toy by the big tree. Sue was very happy and thanked Spot. Sue's mom gave Spot a sausage as a thank you for helping her daughter. Spot and Sue became good friends. They learned that helping others can make you feel happy and good inside. And from that day on, they always played together and helped each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a mysterious big box. Many animals saw it, but no one knew what was inside. One day, a little rabbit named Tim decided to search for the key to open the big box. He thought it would be fun to find out what was inside. Tim asked his friends, the birds, to help him look for the key. They all searched high and low in the forest. After a long search, they found the key under a big rock. Tim was very happy and excited. He couldn't wait to pull open the big box and see the surprise inside. When Tim put the key in the lock and pulled open the big box, a big scary monster jumped out! All the animals in the forest were very scared and ran away. Tim was sad because he didn't listen to the other animals who told him not to open the mysterious big box. The moral of the story is to always listen to others and not to let curiosity lead you to bad things.

Once upon a time, there was a band of animals who loved to play music. One day, they wanted to prepare for a big show. They practiced their songs and got their instruments ready. The band had a cat, a dog, and a bird. The cat played the drums, the dog played the guitar, and the bird sang. They were very happy and excited to play for everyone. Before the show, the cat said, "Let's make sure we are comfortable on stage." The dog agreed and said, "Yes, let's make sure we have a good time." The bird said, "I am ready to sing my heart out!" The band played their music and everyone clapped and danced. The animals had a great time and felt very comfortable on stage. After the show, they all said, "We did a great job preparing for our big show!"
