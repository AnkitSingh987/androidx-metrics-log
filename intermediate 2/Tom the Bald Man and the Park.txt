Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom loved to play outside. One sunny day, Tom went to the park to play with his friends. While Tom was playing, he felt an itch on his head. He scratched and scratched, but the itch would not go away. Then, his friend, Sally, came over to talk to him. "Tom, why are you scratching your head?" Sally asked. "I have an itch," Tom said. "Let me help you," Sally said. She looked at Tom's head and saw a little bug. Sally carefully took the bug off of Tom's head. Tom was so happy that his itch was gone. He said, "Thank you, Sally!" Now, Tom and Sally could play together without any itches. They played all day long and had so much fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toys and help her mom. One day, her mom asked her to help her cook. Amy was very happy and said, "Yes, I will help you, Mom!" Amy's mom told her to add some light sugar to the cake mix. Amy added the sugar and mixed it well. They baked the cake and waited for a week for Amy's dad's birthday. When the day came, Amy was very excited to give her dad the cake. At the birthday party, Amy's dad took a bite of the cake and said, "Wow! This is the best cake I have ever had. Thank you, Amy and Mom!" Amy felt very proud and happy. She learned that helping others can make you feel good and bring joy to everyone.

Lily and Ben are playing in the garden. They like to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. Lily finds a big worm. She shows it to Ben. Ben says, "Wow, that is a long worm. How many inches is it?" Lily does not know what an inch is. She asks Ben, "What is an inch?" Ben says, "An inch is a little bit of a ruler. Like this." He shows her his finger. "One finger is about one inch. Two fingers are two inches. And so on." Lily tries to measure the worm with her fingers. She says, "This worm is four inches. No, five inches. No, six inches. It is moving too much. I cannot measure it." Ben says, "Maybe we can use something else to measure it. Something that does not move. Like a stick. Or a leaf. Or a rock." Lily likes that idea. She looks around for something useful. She sees a stick. She picks it up. She says, "This stick is good. It is longer than the worm. We can use it to measure the worm." But as she turns around, she sees that the worm is gone. It has crawled away. Lily says, "Oh no, where is the worm? We lost it. We have to find it." Ben says, "Hurry, hurry, let's look for it. Maybe it is under that flower. Or behind that tree. Or in that hole." Lily and Ben hurry to look for the worm. They hope they can find it and measure it. They think worms are very interesting. And they want to learn more about inches.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a nice lady. She had a big house that was very organized. Everything was in its right place. She loved to keep her home clean and tidy. One day, while the lady was in her garden, a naughty bird came to tease her. The bird would fly close to her and then fly away, making her laugh. The lady liked the bird's funny tricks and they became friends. Every day, the bird would come to visit the lady and tease her. They would play and laugh together in the organized garden. The lady and the bird were very happy and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Jazz. Jazz liked to step and dance all day long. She was very happy. One day, Jazz went for a walk. She stepped and danced through the forest. She met a big dog who liked to step and dance too. They became friends and danced together. But, the big dog was not nice. He wanted to dance alone. So, the big dog pushed Jazz away. Jazz was sad and went back home. The big dog danced alone and Jazz never danced with him again.
