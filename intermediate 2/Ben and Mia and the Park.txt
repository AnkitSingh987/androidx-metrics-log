Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Park

Ben and Mia love to go to the park. They like to play on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They also like to look at the animals in the zoo. They want to see the panda, but it is always sleeping. One day, they go to the park with their mom. They run to the zoo and look for the panda. They see a big sign that says "Panda is sick. Do not come close." But Ben and Mia do not read the sign. They climb over the fence and go to the panda's cage. The panda is lying on the ground. It has no fur. It is bald. It looks very sad and tired. Ben and Mia feel sorry for the panda. They want to pet it and make it happy. They go near the panda and reach out their hands. But the panda is not happy. It is angry and scared. It thinks Ben and Mia are bad people who want to hurt it. It growls and bites their hands. Ben and Mia scream and cry. Their mom hears them and runs to the cage. She sees the blood and the panda. She is very scared and angry too. She calls for help. The zoo people come and take Ben and Mia to the hospital. They have to get stitches and shots. They are in a lot of pain. The panda has to go to another zoo. It is too sick and dangerous to stay here. Ben and Mia are very sorry. They wish they had read the sign and stayed away from the panda. They learn a hard lesson. They will never go to the park again.

Once upon a time, there was a little beetle. The beetle was very lucky. It had many friends in the big garden. One day, the beetle was playing with its friends. They were all very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and took the lucky beetle away. The beetle was very scared. It started to cry. The beetle's friends were sad too. They wanted to help their friend. The big bird took the lucky beetle to its nest. But the bird did not want to hurt the beetle. The bird just wanted a friend to play with. The beetle stopped crying and played with the bird. They became good friends. And so, the lucky beetle was even luckier because it made a new friend in the big bird.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy had a fragile nest made of twigs. He lived there with his family. They were happy, but sometimes Timmy would complain about the wind. One day, a big wind came and blew one of the twigs from Timmy's nest. Timmy was sad and scared. He didn't want his nest to break. So, he flew around the forest to find a new twig to fix his nest. Timmy found a twig and brought it back to his nest. He fixed the nest and it was not fragile anymore. Timmy and his family were safe and happy again. Timmy learned that complaining doesn't help, but doing something to fix the problem does. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to gather rocks and put them in a big pile. One day, he found a shiny key in the pile. Tim was very happy and showed it to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "I want the key, too!" Tim was stubborn and did not want to share. They both wanted the key and did not know what to do. They decided to ask their mom for help. Mom said, "Why don't you both take turns with the key? Tim can have it today, and Sam can have it tomorrow." Tim and Sam agreed and were happy again. They learned that sharing was the best way to solve their problem.

One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw her friend, Tom, on the way. Tom was holding a newspaper and looked upset. Lily decided to skip to Tom and find out what was wrong. "Hi Tom, why are you upset?" Lily asked. Tom showed her the newspaper and said, "I can't read this. I wanted to know what it says, but it's too hard." Lily wanted to help Tom feel better. She had an idea. "Let's play a game instead! We can skip and have fun. Maybe we can ask someone to read the newspaper later." Tom liked the idea. They put the newspaper down and started to skip together. They skipped all the way to the park, laughing and having fun. They forgot all about the newspaper and Tom wasn't upset anymore.
