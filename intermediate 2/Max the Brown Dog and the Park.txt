Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Brown Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little brown dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball in the park. One day, Max saw a shiny buckle on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to show his friend, Tim. "Look, Tim!" barked Max, "I found a buckle!" Tim, a tall boy with a big smile, was very surprised. He said, "Wow, Max! That's a nice buckle. Let's see if we can find what it belongs to." Together, Max and Tim looked all around the park. They met a girl named Lily who had lost her buckle. "Is this your buckle?" asked Tim. Lily's face lit up with joy. "Yes! Thank you for finding it," she said. Max and Tim felt very happy that they could help their new friend. They all played together in the park for the rest of the day. And from that day on, Max, Tim, and Lily were the best of friends, always ready to help and surprise each other with new fun adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat, but he did not like bitter things. One day, his mom made him a big plate with lots of food. Tim was very happy and started to eat. As he was eating, Tim saw a bitter green thing on his plate. He did not want to eat it, but his mom said, "You need to finish all your food." Tim was sad, but he wanted to make his mom happy. So, he closed his eyes and ate the bitter green thing very fast. Just as Tim finished the bitter green thing, something unexpected happened. His mom smiled and gave him a big surprise. It was a sweet, yummy cake! Tim was so happy that he finished his food, even the bitter green thing. From that day on, Tim learned that sometimes you need to eat bitter things to get to the sweet ones.

Once upon a time, there was a small file. The file was unknown to everyone, but it held a very special number. One day, a 3 year old found the file. The 3 year old wanted to open the file and see what was inside, so she gave it a try. She opened it up, and to her delight, she found a beautiful number inside. It was a very special number - it was unique, so the 3 year old knew she had found something special. She decided she would take the file and the number with her wherever she went, so she could always show it to her friends. The 3 year old never knew why the number was so special or why it was in that file. But that didn't matter to her, because she knew just how special the number was.

Once upon a time, there was a humble boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim was a good boy who always helped others. One day, Tim met a fairy in the park. The fairy said, "I can send you a wish. What do you want?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I want to help more people." The fairy smiled and said, "Your wish will come true." The next day, Tim found a magic bag. When he opened it, he saw many toys inside. Tim gave the toys to his friends and they were very happy. The fairy had sent the wish to Tim and he became the happiest boy in the world.

Sara and Ben were playing in the park with their toys. They liked to make noises and pretend they were cars and planes. They had a lot of fun and laughed a lot. Then they saw their parent coming to pick them up. Sara and Ben ran to their parent and gave them a big hug. They were happy to see them and wanted to show them their toys. "Look, parent, this is my car. It can go fast and make beep beep sounds," Sara said. "And this is my plane. It can fly high and make zoom zoom sounds," Ben said. Their parent smiled and said, "Wow, those are very nice toys. You are very clever and creative. I'm proud of you." Sara and Ben felt warm and happy. They loved their parent and their toys. But then they saw some other kids from their preschool. They were older and bigger than Sara and Ben. They were playing with a ball and a bike. They looked at Sara and Ben and their toys and laughed. "Ha ha, look at those babies. They still play with silly toys. They are so childish. They need to hug their parent all the time. How embarrassing," one of they said. Sara and Ben felt sad and angry. They didn't like the other kids. They thought they were mean and rude. They didn't think their toys were silly. They didn't think they were babies. They didn't think they needed to be embarrassed. They looked at their parent and said, "Parent, we don't like those kids. They are not nice. They make fun of us and our toys. They hurt our feelings. Can we go home?" Their parent hugged them and said, "I'm sorry, Sara and Ben. Those kids are not nice. They should not make fun of you and your toys. You don't have to be embarrassed. You are not babies. You are wonderful and smart. You can play with whatever toys you like. They make you happy and that's what matters. I love you and I'm always here for you. Let's go home and have some cookies." Sara and Ben smiled and said, "Thank you, parent. We love you too. You are the best. Let's go home and have some cookies." They held their parent's hand and their toys and walked away. They didn't care about the other kids. They were happy and proud. They had each other and their parent and their toys. And they were looking forward to having some cookies.
