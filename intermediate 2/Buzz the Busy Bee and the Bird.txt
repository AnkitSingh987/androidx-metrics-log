Category: Intermediate
Title: Buzz the Busy Bee and the Bird

One day, a busy bee named Buzz was flying around. He saw his friend, Ant, pass by. Ant looked very sad. Buzz wanted to help Ant feel better. "Hi Ant!" said Buzz. "Why are you sad?" "I lost my alarm," said Ant. "I need it to wake up on time." Buzz and Ant looked for the alarm together. They looked under rocks, on top of leaves, and behind flowers. It was hard to find the alarm. Then, they saw a big bird. The bird had the alarm in its beak! Buzz and Ant were happy to find the alarm, but the bird did not want to give it back. The bird flew away with the alarm, and Buzz and Ant could not catch it. Buzz and Ant were very sad. They did not get the alarm back. They learned that they should be careful with their things, so they do not lose them again.

Once upon a time there was a wealthy man called Tom who loved his mug. Every morning he'd take it out of the cupboard and pour himself a steaming hot cup of coffee. Then he'd rise from his chair, mug in hand, and admire the beautiful garden outside his window. One morning, shortly after Tom had risen from his chair, he heard a voice calling for help. He saw that one of the birds from the garden had flown into his house and was now stuck between a bookshelf and a wall. Tom set his mug down and quickly tried to help the bird. He said "Don't worry, little birdie! I'm here to help you." The bird was so grateful that it sang a beautiful song to Tom and rose up above his head. Tom thanked the bird and watched it fly away. Then he picked up his mug and took a sip of the warm coffee. He smiled as he looked back at the garden outside, feeling truly blessed to be a wealthy man.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a glove on a seat. Tim was very happy to find the glove. He put the glove on his hand and went to show his mom. His mom was busy in the kitchen. Tim was impatient. He wanted to show her the glove right away. He jumped up and down and said, "Mom, look at my glove!" His mom smiled and looked at the glove. She said, "That's a nice glove, Tim. But it's not your glove. We need to find the person who lost it." So, Tim and his mom went outside to look for the person who lost the glove. They found a little girl who was sad because she lost her glove. Tim gave the glove back to the girl, and she was very happy. Tim felt good because he helped someone.

Once upon a time, there was a little dirty beetle. He lived in a tiny house in the woods. He had a lot of friends like birds, ants, and squirrels. One sunny day, the dirty beetle went for a walk. While walking, the dirty beetle met a big bird. The big bird said, "Hi, little dirty beetle! What are you doing today?" The dirty beetle replied, "I am going for a walk to see my friends. I wonder where they are." The big bird said, "I saw your friends near the big tree. I can help you find them." The dirty beetle was happy and said, "Thank you, big bird!" They went to the big tree together and found all of the dirty beetle's friends. The dirty beetle and his friends played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. As the sun went down, the dirty beetle said goodbye to his friends and went back to his tiny house. He was tired but happy after a fun day with his friends.

One day, a girl named Lily went to a big tree. Under the tree, she saw a lot of fruit. She was glad. She wanted to eat the fruit. Lily lay on the grass to think of a way to get the fruit. A big bird came and took some fruit from the tree. Lily had an idea. She asked the bird, "Can you help me get some fruit?" The bird said, "Yes, I can help you." The bird got some fruit and gave it to Lily. But then, something unexpected happened. The fruit turned into pretty flowers! Lily and the bird were surprised. They looked at the flowers and smiled. They were happy with the pretty flowers.
