Category: Intermediate
Title: The Musician and the Song

Once upon a time there was a musician. He loved to play his guitar. One day, he was playing and singing so loudly that a 3 year old girl named Lucy overheard the music and stopped to listen. When he was done, the musician saw Lucy and smiled. He said to her, “Did you like my song?” Lucy nodded her head yes, and yawned. The musician laughed and said, “It was too noisy, wasn’t it?” Lucy nodded again and smiled. She asked, “Will you play again?” The musician smiled and said, “Of course I will!” So, he played another song, but this time he played more softly. Lucy was happy because it wasn’t so noisy. She smiled and began to dance to the music. The musician smiled and they both enjoyed the music together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. He loved to play outside and have fun with his friends. One day, while playing in the park, he saw a big, harsh rock. He wanted to move it. "Resist, Tim!" his friend Sam said. "It's too big for you!" But Tim didn't listen. He pushed and pushed the harsh rock, but it didn't move. Suddenly, the rock started to shake and it opened up. To Tim's surprise, there was a small, friendly dog inside the rock! The dog jumped out and licked Tim's face. Tim and Sam laughed and played with the dog. They decided to name him Rocky. From that day on, Tim, Sam, and Rocky were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk with her dog, Max. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Lily loved to play outside with Max, and today was a perfect day for it. As they walked, Lily saw a big puddle. She wanted Max to jump in it with her. Lily was a bit bossy, so she pointed at the puddle and said, "Max, jump!" Max wagged his tail and jumped in the puddle with Lily. They both laughed and splashed in the water. After playing in the puddle, Lily and Max were very wet and muddy. They walked back home, leaving little wet footprints behind them. When they got home, Lily's mom saw them and said, "You two need a bath!" Lily and Max had a bath, and they were clean again. They had a fun day playing in the puddle.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks and make towers. One day, they go to the park and see a big map of the world. The map has many colors and shapes. Anna points to a shape and says, "This is my nation. It is red and has a star. It is the best nation." Ben looks at the map and finds another shape. He says, "This is my nation. It is blue and has a circle. It is the best nation." Anna and Ben start to argue. They say, "No, my nation is better. Your nation is silly. My nation has more people. Your nation has more water." They get angry and push each other. They knock down the map and break it. The map falls into pieces. Anna and Ben feel sad and sorry. They say, "Oh no, we broke the map. We were bad friends. How can we fix it?" They look around and see a man. The man is a teacher. He has a glue and a tape. He says, "Hello, children. I see you had a fight. Do you want to fix the map?" Anna and Ben nod and say, "Yes, please. We want to fix the map. We want to be good friends." The teacher smiles and says, "That's good. Come, I will help you. You can learn about the different nations and how they are all important and special. You can also learn how to share and respect each other. You don't have to be jealous of other nations. You can be proud of your own nation and also curious about other nations." Anna and Ben agree and say, "Thank you, teacher. You are very nice. We will fix the map and we will fix our friendship." They take the glue and the tape and start to put the map back together. They also say sorry to each other and hug. They feel happy and friendly. They learn and play with the map. They make new friends with other children who come to the park. They have fun and enjoy the day.
