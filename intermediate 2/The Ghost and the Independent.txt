Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ghost and the Independent

Once upon a time, there was a little ghost who wanted to be independent. But she was too small to do everything by herself. So every day, she needed help from her friends. One day, she decided that she wanted to attach something to her tail. She asked her friends to help her and they found a very pretty ribbon. With their help, she was able to attach the ribbon to her tail. The little ghost was so happy! Now she was more independent and could do things for herself. She sang and danced around, showing off her new ribbon. Everybody was so happy for her! The little ghost was never scared to be independent anymore. She and her friends would always play together and help each other. She loved feeling so independent and free!

Lily and Ben are playing in the garden. They have a big bowl of water and some toys. They pretend the toys are fish and sharks. Lily has a blue fish and Ben has a red shark. "Look, my shark is hungry. He wants to eat your fish!" Ben says. He makes his shark swim fast towards Lily's fish. "No, no, no! My fish is too smart. He can swim away from your shark!" Lily says. She moves her fish around the bowl. But Ben's shark is faster. He catches Lily's fish and bites it. Lily's fish stops moving. "Oh no! You made my fish dead!" Lily cries. She takes her fish out of the water and hugs it. "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to make your fish dead. It was just a game." Ben says. He feels bad. "Can you make my fish alive again?" Lily asks. She looks at Ben with big eyes. Ben thinks hard. He has an idea. "Maybe we can make your fish alive again. We can use magic!" He says. He finds a stick and waves it over the fish. "Abracadabra! Fish, be alive!" He says. Lily watches. She sees her fish wiggle a little. She smiles. "Wow! You did it! You made my fish alive again!" She says. She puts her fish back in the water and plays with it. Ben smiles too. He is happy that Lily is happy. They play with their fish and shark in the bowl. They are careful not to make them dead again. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a lady. She lived in a normal house with her cat. The lady and her cat liked to play with a big red ball. One day, the lady wanted to increase the fun they had. So, she got more balls. Now, they had a blue ball, a yellow ball, and a green ball too. The lady and her cat played with the balls every day. They were very happy. The cat liked the blue ball best. The lady liked the yellow ball. They had so much fun in their normal house. And their fun increased a lot with the new balls.

Tom was sick. He had a hot head and a sore throat. He did not want to play or eat. He only wanted to sleep. Mom put a thermometer in his mouth to see how hot he was. She looked at the numbers and said, "Oh, Tom, you have a fever. You need to rest and drink water." Tom did not like the thermometer. It was hard and cold and made him gag. He wanted to spit it out, but Mom said, "No, Tom, you have to keep it in for a minute. It will tell us how to help you get better." Tom frowned and shut his mouth. He waited for the minute to be over. Mom took the thermometer out and smiled. She said, "Good job, Tom. You are very brave. Your fever is not too high. You just need some medicine and a hug." She gave him a spoonful of syrup and a kiss. She said, "This will make you feel better. And look, I have something amazing for you." She showed him a book with colorful pictures and funny animals. She said, "This is a new story. Do you want me to read it to you?" Tom nodded. He liked stories. He snuggled with Mom on the couch and listened to her voice. She read him the story of a lion who lost his roar, a monkey who found a banana, and a giraffe who made a friend. Tom laughed and asked questions. He forgot about his hot head and his sore throat. He felt happy and warm. He said, "Thank you, Mom. You are amazing." Mom hugged him and said, "You are welcome, Tom. You are amazing too."
