Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Mom

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in their big house. They have many toys and books and games. But their favorite thing is to rush up and down the stair. The stair is long and has many steps. They pretend they are climbing a mountain or flying a plane or chasing a monster. One day, they hear a loud noise from the kitchen. It sounds like a crash and a bang. They wonder what it is. They rush to the stair and start to go down. But they are too fast and they slip and fall. They bump their heads and knees and elbows. They cry and shout. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tom says. "That hurts!" "Mom, mom, mom!" Lily says. "Help us!" Mom hears them and comes to the stair. She sees them lying on the floor. She is worried and scared. She runs to them and hugs them. She checks if they are hurt. She kisses their boo-boos and wipes their tears. "Are you okay, my loves?" Mom says. "What happened?" "We heard a noise and we rushed to the stair and we fell and we hurt ourselves," Tom says. "We wanted to see what it was and we were too fast and we slipped and we bumped and we cried," Lily says. Mom smiles and says, "I'm sorry, my loves. The noise was me. I dropped a pot and a pan. It was an accident. I'm glad you are not badly hurt. But you have to be careful on the stair. It is long and steep and slippery. You have to go slow and hold the rail. Do you understand?" Tom and Lily nod and say, "Yes, mom. We understand. We are sorry. We love you." Mom says, "I love you too, my loves. Now, let's go to the kitchen and have some cookies and milk. How does that sound?" Tom and Lily smile and say, "That sounds good, mom. Thank you." They hold Mom's hand and go to the kitchen. They are happy and safe. They eat their cookies and drink their milk. They forget their pain and their fear. They laugh and talk and play. They are good twins. They are good children. They are Mom's loves.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk and play outside. One day, he found a big helmet in his yard. It was red and shiny. Tim was very happy. Tim put the helmet on his head and went for a walk. He felt like a hero in his new helmet. He saw a cat stuck in a tree. Tim was grateful he had his helmet on because it gave him the courage to help the cat. Tim climbed the tree and saved the cat. The cat was grateful to Tim for helping her. Tim knew that his helmet had made him brave. He walked home with a big smile on his face, ready for more adventures.

Timmy loved his bicycle. It was red and shiny and had a bell that went ding-ding. He got it for his birthday from his mom and dad. They said it was his original bicycle, because it was the first one he ever had. One day, Timmy wanted to ride his bicycle to the park. He asked his mom if he could go. She said yes, but he had to wear his helmet and be careful. She also said he had to remind her when he came back, because she had a surprise for him. Timmy wondered what the surprise was. He said okay and put on his helmet. He rode his bicycle to the park and had a lot of fun. He saw his friends and played with them. He went on the swings and the slide and the seesaw. He forgot about the time and the surprise. He was having too much fun. Then he heard a loud noise. It was a big truck that was driving by the park. It was very noisy and scary. Timmy looked at the truck and saw something that made him gasp. It was his bicycle! The truck had his bicycle on the back, along with other things. Timmy realized that he had left his bicycle outside the park gate, and someone had taken it and put it on the truck. Timmy ran after the truck, but it was too fast. He shouted and cried, but no one heard him. He lost his bicycle, his original bicycle, and he didn't know what to do. He felt very sad and angry. He walked back to his house, with tears in his eyes. He didn't want to see his mom or his surprise. He just wanted his bicycle back. He opened the door and saw his mom waiting for him. She smiled and said, "Timmy, I have a surprise for you. Look what I found on the street. It's your bicycle!" Timmy couldn't believe his eyes. It was his bicycle, his red and shiny and ding-ding bicycle. His mom had seen the truck and followed it. She had seen the driver stop and unload some things. She had seen his bicycle and recognized it. She had taken it back and brought it home. Timmy was so happy and relieved. He hugged his mom and thanked her. He said he was sorry for forgetting to remind her. He said he loved her and his bicycle. He said it was the best surprise ever. His mom said she loved him too and was glad he was safe. She said he had learned a lesson. He had to be careful with his things and not leave them unattended. She said he had to be more responsible with his original bicycle. Timmy said he understood and he would. He said he wanted to ride his bicycle again. His mom said he could, but he had to wear his helmet and be careful. Timmy said okay and put on his helmet. He rode his bicycle to the park and had a lot of fun. He didn't forget about the time or the surprise. He was happy and grateful. He had his bicycle, his original bicycle, and he loved it.
