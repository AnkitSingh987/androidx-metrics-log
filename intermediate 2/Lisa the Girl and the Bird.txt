Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Lisa went for a walk in the park. She saw a light bird fly by and it made her very happy. The bird landed on the ground and picked up a small pebble in its beak. Lisa was curious about the pebble and wanted to know more. Lisa asked her mom to explain what the bird was doing with the pebble. Her mom said that the bird was taking the pebble to its nest. Lisa thought that was interesting and wanted to see the bird's nest. Her mom said they could look for it together. They walked around the park and finally found the bird's nest up in a tree. The light bird was there with the pebble. It dropped the pebble into the nest and flew away. Lisa and her mom watched as the bird brought more pebbles to the nest. Lisa now understood why the bird needed the pebbles and was happy she had learned something new.

John was a restless boy. He always wanted to do something and his parents were always telling him not to get up to mischief. Today he saw a big shiny vase. He couldn't help himself, he had to open it. When he opened it, a big cloud of smoke came out, and a very scary monster jumped out. John was so scared he screamed and ran away. The monster stayed in the room, looking around and growling. The vase had been a trap, and now the monster was loose! John had made a very bad decision and he was so scared he couldn't even run away.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very alert and loved to play all day. One day, Spot found a bag of powder in the kitchen. He thought it was a toy, so he played with it. The powder went everywhere! It was a big mess. Spot's friend, a little cat named Fluffy, saw the mess and was upset. Fluffy told Spot that he should not play with things that were not his toys. Spot felt sad and said, "I am sorry, Fluffy. Please forgive me." Fluffy forgave Spot, but they both knew they had to clean up the mess. As they were cleaning, Spot and Fluffy did not see that the powder had gone into the oven. When Mommy turned on the oven to cook dinner, the powder caught fire. The fire grew big and scary. Spot and Fluffy were very scared and ran outside. In the end, their home was gone. Spot and Fluffy learned a hard lesson: never play with things that are not toys, and always listen to your friends when they tell you something is wrong.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very patient. She always waited for her turn to play with toys and never cried if she had to wait. One day, Sue and her mom went to the zoo to see the animals. They were very excited to see the big lion. At the zoo, Sue and her mom sat down to have a meal. They had yummy sandwiches and juice. While they were eating, Sue saw a big door with a sign that said "Do not open." Sue was curious but remembered to be patient. After their meal, Sue and her mom went to see the lion. But the lion was not in his home! They looked around and saw that the big door was open. The lion had shut the door after he got out! Sue and her mom were scared, but then they saw the lion eating their sandwiches. The lion just wanted a meal too! They all laughed and the zookeeper put the lion back in his home. From that day on, Sue knew that being patient was very important.

Tim and Jen are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom. One day, they see a big tree with green balls on it. Mom says they are limes. Tim and Jen want to try them. They ask mom if they can pick some limes. Mom says yes, but be careful. The limes are sour and can make your face funny. Tim and Jen laugh. They think it will be fun to make funny faces. They run to the tree and pick some limes. They each take a bite. They make a big mistake. The limes are very sour. They make their mouths hurt. They spit out the limes and cry. Mom hears them and runs to them. She asks what is wrong. Tim and Jen say the limes are bad. They hurt their mouths. They are scared. Mom hugs them and says it is okay. The limes are not bad. They are just sour. They are not for eating. They are for making juice or putting in water. Mom gives them some water to drink. She says it will make their mouths feel better. She says they are brave for trying something new. She says they are safe and she loves them. Tim and Jen drink the water and feel better. They smile and say they love mom too. They say they will not eat limes again. They will only eat sweet fruits. Mom says that is a good idea. They all go home and have some apples.
