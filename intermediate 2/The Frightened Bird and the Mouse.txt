Category: Intermediate
Title: The Frightened Bird and the Mouse

Once upon a time there was a frightened bird. He was very scared because there was a missile near his nest. He didn't know what to do. Then, a little mouse came hopping by. He asked the bird why he was so sad. The bird said, "I'm fearful of the missile near my nest. I'm afraid it will hurt me!" The little mouse said, "Don't worry my friend. I'll help you!" The mouse hopped up to the missile, gave it a big kiss, then quickly hopped away. The bird was amazed. He saw that the missile was no longer there. The mouse had made it disappear. The bird was so happy that he flew down and gave the mouse a big hug and a kiss. From then on, the bird and the mouse were the best of friends. They helped each other whenever they were in need.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to dance. One day, Mia found a big, wide box in her room. The box had a lock on it. Mia needed a key to open the lock. She looked all around her room for the key. Mia asked her mom, "Mom, do you know where the key is?" Her mom said, "No, but maybe it is in the living room." Mia looked in the living room and found the key under a chair. She was so happy! Mia ran back to her room to open the wide box. When Mia opened the box, she found a pair of shiny red shoes. She put them on and started to dance. But something unexpected happened! The shoes made her dance faster and faster. Mia could not stop dancing! She cried, "Help me, Mom!" Her mom grabbed the key and locked the shoes back in the wide box. Mia stopped dancing and hugged her mom. They knew they had to be careful with the magic shoes.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a friendly cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his friends, the birds, and the ducks. They would run, jump, and swim all day long. Tom was very happy. One day, while playing, Tom heard a loud scream. He looked around and saw a little girl holding her ear. She was crying. Tom wanted to help her, so he went to her and said, "Don't cry. What's wrong?" The little girl told Tom, "My ear hurts." Tom knew what to do. He found a soft leaf and gently rubbed it on the girl's ear. Soon, the girl stopped crying and smiled at Tom. They became friends and played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a colorful forest, there lived a small bird named Chirpy. Chirpy had a sharp beak and loved to sing. Every day, Chirpy would fly around the forest and sing to his friends. One day, Chirpy met a new friend, a firefly named Glow. Glow had a light that would shine very bright at night. Chirpy asked Glow, "Can you teach me how to shine like you do at night?" Glow said, "I can't teach you to shine like me, but I can show you how to use your sharp beak to make pretty things." So Glow and Chirpy worked together to make colorful shapes out of leaves and twigs. At night, Glow's light would shine on the colorful shapes, making the forest look even more beautiful. Chirpy and Glow became the best of friends, and they spent their days making the forest a happy place for everyone.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia had a big idea. She wanted to split a big watermelon for her friends. She thought it would be fun to share. Mia told all her friends about the idea. They were all very excited. They went to the big hill near their homes. Mia brought the big watermelon with her. It was very heavy, but she was strong. When they got to the top of the hill, Mia split the watermelon in half. All her friends were happy. They ate the watermelon and laughed. The quiet town was filled with joy. Mia's idea made everyone happy.
