Category: Intermediate
Title: The Magic Cat and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a magic cat. The cat could read. She liked to read big books. The cat had a busy day. She read books all day long. One day, the magic cat found a new book. She was very happy. The book looked fun. The cat started to read the new book. The book was about a big dog. The cat read the book very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The big dog from the book came out of the book! The dog was not nice. The dog chased the magic cat away. The cat could not read any more books.

John was a three-year-old with a big heart. He lived with his mom and liked helping out wherever he could. Today, his mom was in a rush to get ready for her work. She was starting up the grill to make breakfast. “I'm so sorry, John," she said, panting. "I'm going to be late this morning. Be a good boy while I'm gone." John felt a little sad that his mom couldn't stay to play. "It's okay," he said, honestly. John decided to come to the aid of his mom. He walked over to the grill and started to fiddle with it. His mom watched with surprise, but also a little bit of worry. "John, be careful with the grill," she cautioned. John's mom was starting to worry, but Johns' honest attempt to help was heart-warming. He was determined to fix the grill and make his mom proud. John swiftly worked and soon enough, he had healed the grill. His mom couldn't believe it. "You did it, John!" she exclaimed. John smiled proudly at his mom and she gave him a big hug and lots of kisses. This day was going to be alright!

Once upon a time there was a boy called Tom. Tom was very lonely because he didn't have any friends. Tom's mom noticed this and wanted him to be happy. So she put Tom in the garden and told him to practice his exercise. Tom got so excited because he always liked to move his body. But after some time, Tom started to get tired of exercising. He wished he had someone to play with him instead. He felt so lonely and bored. He stopped doing his exercises, and started to feel very unhappy. Tom's mom noticed this and got worried. She tried to make him smile but it didn't work. Tom wished he had someone to cheer him up, but nobody was around. So he carried on feeling really lonely and sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mike. He was a curious little boy and loved to explore. One day Mike decided to go on an adventure. He walked into the forest and soon found something interesting. It was a large, open plant. Mike was very excited, and he wanted to gain it. He said, "I sure want this plant. It looks cool!" But then he noticed a small sign next to it that said, "This plant belongs to the forest, please don't take it." Mike was disappointed but he knew he had to do the right thing. He said, "I will follow the sign and leave the plant here. That's what is best for the forest." So Mike left the plant behind, and he gained something much more special - the feeling of being kind to the environment.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big chair in her room. She was confused. She did not know where the chair came from. Sue dared to sit on the chair. "Who are you?" asked the chair. Sue was scared. She did not know that chairs could talk. "I am Sue," she said. "Who are you?" "I am a magic chair," said the chair. "I can take you to fun places if you dare to sit on me." Sue dared to sit on the chair again. The chair took her to a big park with many slides and swings. Sue had a lot of fun. When she was tired, the chair took her back home. Sue was happy. She thanked the chair for the fun day. The chair said, "You're welcome. I will always be here if you dare to have more fun."
