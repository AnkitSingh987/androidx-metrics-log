Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Stick and the Ball

One day, a big round ball and a small cut stick were playing in the park. They liked to roll and jump around. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. The big round ball was very good at rolling, but the small cut stick was not. The small cut stick felt sad. The big round ball saw the small cut stick feeling sad and said, "Why are you sad, my friend?" The small cut stick replied, "I am not good at rolling like you. I wish I could roll as well as you do." The big round ball thought for a moment and said, "Let's play a fair game where we both can have fun." So, the big round ball and the small cut stick played a new game. They took turns jumping over a little rock. The small cut stick was very good at jumping, and the big round ball was not. They both laughed and had fun playing the new game. In the end, the big round ball and the small cut stick learned that it is important to play fair games where everyone can have fun. They also learned that everyone is good at different things, and that is what makes playing together so special.

One day, a girl named Lily went on a picnic with her mom and dad. They went to a big park with lots of green grass and pretty flowers. Lily was very excited because it was not an ordinary day. Today, they would eat yummy food outside and play fun games. As they sat down on a soft blanket, Lily's mom reminded her, "Lily, don't forget to wash your hands before we eat." Lily listened to her mom and washed her hands with clean water. Then they all started to eat the tasty sandwiches and sweet fruits that they brought for the picnic. After they ate, Lily's dad said, "Let's play a game of catch!" They took turns throwing a small, red ball to each other. Lily laughed and ran around the park with her mom and dad. They had a great time at the picnic, and it was a day that Lily would always remember.

Once upon a time, there was a small room called the chamber. In this chamber, there was a big box. The box had a job to supply toys to all the kids in the town. Every day, the box would give toys to the kids, and they would play and be happy. One day, a boy named Tim went to the chamber. He wanted to get a toy from the big box. But when he opened the box, he found a terrible monster inside! The monster looked very scary and said, "Roar! I am a terrible monster, and I will take all the toys!" Tim was very scared, but he had an idea. He said to the monster, "You may be terrible, but I know you want to play too. If you promise not to take the toys, we can all play together." The monster thought about it and agreed. The terrible monster became friends with Tim and all the kids in the town. They all played together, and the big box continued to supply toys for everyone. The chamber was now a happy place where everyone, even the monster, could have fun and play.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there was a big horse. The horse was very proud. He liked to step high and walk fast. He thought he was the best animal on the farm. One day, a little bird came to the farm. The bird said, "I can fly high and fast, too!" The proud horse did not like this. He said, "I can step higher and faster than you!" So, the horse and the bird had a race. The race began, and the horse stepped high and fast. But he did not see a big hole in the ground. The proud horse fell into the hole. The little bird flew high and fast, and won the race. The horse was sad and not proud anymore.

One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. They saw a small house. The house had a loose nail on the door. Tim's mom said, "That nail is not useful like that. We should fix it." Tim and his mom went to find a hammer. They saw a big tree. Behind the tree, there was a big bear. The bear said, "I can help you fix the nail. I have a hammer." Tim and his mom were surprised. They did not know bears could talk. The bear helped fix the nail on the door. Tim's mom said, "Thank you, bear!" Tim gave the bear a big hug. The bear said, "You're welcome! I am happy to help." From that day on, Tim, his mom, and the bear became good friends.
