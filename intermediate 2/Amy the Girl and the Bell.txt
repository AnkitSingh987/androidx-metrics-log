Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bell

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She liked to play in the wide park near her house. One day, she heard a bell. It made a pretty sound, so Amy wanted to follow it. Amy followed the bell sound into the woods. She walked and walked until she found a little house. The door was open, so Amy went inside to see where the bell sound came from. Inside the house, there was a big, mean wolf. The wolf saw Amy and wanted to eat her. The bell sound was a trick. Amy was very scared and could not run away. The wolf ate poor little Amy, and she was gone forever.

Lila and Ben were playing in the jungle. They liked to look for animals and plants. They saw a big snake, a red bird and a yellow flower. They were happy and curious. They found a cave and went inside. It was dark and cold. They used a flashlight to see. They saw many rocks and bats. They were scared and excited. "Look, Ben, look!" Lila said. She pointed to a shiny thing on the wall. It was a crystal. It sparkled like a star. "It is so pretty!" "Wow, Lila, wow!" Ben said. He wanted to touch it. He reached out his hand. "Can I have it?" "Be careful, Ben, be careful!" Lila said. She knew the crystal was fragile. It could break easily. "It is not a toy!" But Ben did not listen. He grabbed the crystal. It was sharp and heavy. He dropped it on the floor. It shattered into pieces. He hurt his hand. He cried. "Ben, Ben, are you okay?" Lila said. She ran to him. She hugged him. She saw the blood on his hand. She was sorry and worried. "I'm sorry, Lila, I'm sorry!" Ben said. He sobbed. He felt bad. He ruined the crystal. He hurt himself. "It was so shiny!" Lila and Ben left the cave. They went back to the jungle. They found their mom and dad. They told them what happened. They got a bandage and a hug. They learned a lesson. They said sorry to the crystal. They said thank you to the jungle. They said I love you to each other. They were still happy and curious.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite object. It was an ugly, old toy. He loved his ugly toy very much. One night, Tim had a dream. In his dream, his ugly toy came to life! The toy was not ugly anymore. It was big and pretty. The toy played with Tim, and they had lots of fun. When Tim woke up, he looked at his ugly toy. It was still small and old. But Tim knew that in his dreams, the toy was big, pretty, and fun. So, he loved his ugly toy even more.

Jack was a little boy who liked to jump. He would jump over the tall rocks, over the yellow flowers and even over the puddles of water. But today Jack was feeling scared. It was getting dark and he didn't know what to do. He was alone and he felt nervous. Jack heard someone talking to him from behind a big tree. "Hey little boy, why don't you come jump with me?", the voice said. Jack was scared, but he stepped out from behind the tree and saw it was a pretty bird. "I'm scared of the dark," Jack said. "That's okay little one," the bird said. "Come and jump with me, it'll make you feel better." Jack smiled and stepped forward and the bird took off. Jack followed the bird, jumping and twirling in the air. After a while, Jack forgot all his worries and the dark seemed to disappear and he felt happy. When the sun started coming up, Jack realised he was back home. He thanked the bird for helping him have such a pretty jump.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max lived in a small house with his family. Max loved to run and play in the yard. He had strong legs that allowed him to jump high and run fast. Max was very happy. One day, Max saw a big, dangerous snake in the yard. The snake hissed and showed its sharp teeth. Max knew that he should not go near the snake because it could hurt him. He barked loudly to tell his family about the dangerous snake. Max's family heard his bark and came outside. They saw the dangerous snake and knew they had to help. Max's dad got a long stick and carefully moved the snake away from the house. Max and his family were safe. They thanked Max for being brave and protecting them.
