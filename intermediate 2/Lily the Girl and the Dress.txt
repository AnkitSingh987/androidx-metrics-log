Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Dress

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. She had many clothes in her room. One day, her mom bought her a new dress. Lily loved her new dress and wanted to show it to her friend, Sam. Lily went to Sam's house and said, "Look at my new dress! Do you like it?" Sam looked at Lily's dress and smiled. "Yes, I admire your dress. It is very pretty!" said Sam. Lily felt happy that her friend liked her new dress. The two friends played outside and had lots of fun. They laughed and ran in the sun. At the end of the day, Lily thanked her mom for the new dress. She knew that she had a great day with her friend Sam, and she loved her new clothes.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a shiny coin that he loved very much. One day, while playing in the park, he met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the coin and thought it was very pretty. "Can I see your coin?" Sue asked Tim. Tim was scared that Sue might take his coin, so he said, "No, I don't want to allow you to see it." Sue felt terrible and sad. She didn't want to make Tim upset. Later, Tim saw Sue crying and felt bad. He went to her and said, "I'm sorry, you can see my coin." Sue smiled and thanked Tim. They became good friends and played together every day. And Tim learned that sharing can make others happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a graceful cat named Kitty. She loved to play with her friends and eat candy. One day, Kitty could not remember where she put her candy. Kitty looked everywhere for her candy. She asked her friends if they knew where her candy was. Her friends did not know. Kitty was sad. She wanted her candy back. Then, Kitty saw her friend, the dog named Buddy, eating candy. Buddy had found Kitty's candy! Kitty asked Buddy to give her candy back. Buddy said sorry and gave the candy back to Kitty. They both shared the candy and played together. They were happy again.

Once upon a nice day, Jack had an idea. It was a sweet idea and he wanted to put it into action. He asked his friend Susie to help him plan the idea. Susie was excited and said, "Yes, let's do it!" Jack was so happy and he thanked her. Together they thought about how to make it happen. They talked about what supplies they needed and how much time it would take. They figured it out and then wrote the plan on paper. Once the plan was finished, the two friends started getting everything ready. Jack and Susie worked together and when it was done, their idea had come to life. It was sweet. They smiled and hugged each other. The sweet idea had worked out great! They were very proud of themselves.

Once there was a mommy and a little boy. The little boy was three years old and was very scared of the couch in the living room. The couch was very big and the floor was always dirty. One day, when the little boy was playing in the living room, he saw something strange behind the couch. He got scared and ran to his mommy. "Mommy, there is something scary behind the couch," he said. His mommy went to look at the couch and told him not to worry. "It's just some dirt, it won't hurt you," she said. The little boy felt better, but he still felt scared of the couch. So his mommy helped him to clean it and when it was all nice and clean, the little boy was no longer scared.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited. He had a red wagon. He loved to play with his wagon every day. One day, Tim was playing with his wagon in the park. He saw a big hill. Tim thought it would be fun to ride his wagon down the hill. He was so excited! Tim got in his wagon at the top of the hill. He started to go down the hill. The wagon went faster and faster. Tim was scared. At the bottom of the hill, there was a big tree. The wagon hit the tree. Tim was sad and cried. His red wagon was broken.
