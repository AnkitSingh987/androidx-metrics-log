Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite toy car that he loved to play with every day. One day, while he was playing, his toy car broke. Tim was very sad. Tim's uncle saw him crying and asked what was wrong. Tim showed his broken toy car to his uncle. His uncle was very smart and knew how to repair things. He took the toy car and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will fix your car." While his uncle was fixing the toy car, Tim's mom gave him a spicy snack to eat. Tim liked spicy food, and it made him feel better. Soon, his uncle came back with the repaired toy car. Tim was very happy and hugged his uncle to say thank you. Tim learned that when something is broken, you can ask for help to fix it. He also learned that being kind and thankful to others is important. And that's the end of the story.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, red ball. One day, she went to the park to play with her ball. She saw a small, green bug. She wanted to examine it. So, she looked at the bug very closely. The bug was very pretty and had tiny wings. Sue's friend, Tim, came to the park too. He saw Sue looking at the bug. He said, "What are you doing, Sue?" Sue said, "I am looking at this bug. It is green and has wings." Tim looked at the bug too. He thought it was interesting. After playing with the bug, Sue and Tim went to play with the ball. They kicked it back and forth. Then, Sue remembered she had to take a pill. It was a small, round pill. Sue put the pill in her mouth. It was very bitter. She made a funny face. Tim laughed at her funny face. They played at the park until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little bird named Tim. Tim was very good at flying with great speed. He loved to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. One day, while Tim was flying fast, he saw a sharp stone on the ground. Tim's friend, Sam, was playing near the stone. Tim was worried that Sam might get hurt. So, he flew down quickly to talk to Sam. "Sam, be careful! There is a sharp stone near you," said Tim. Sam looked at the stone and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." They both flew away to play somewhere else, safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Spot. Spot loved to jog in the park with his owner, Tim. They were very happy together. Tim had a photo of Spot in his room, and he looked at it every day. One sunny day, while Spot and Tim were jogging in the park, they saw a little girl crying. She had lost her favorite toy. Spot wanted to help, so he used his nose to find the toy. He jogged and jogged, and finally, he found the toy under a big tree. Spot brought the toy back to the little girl, and she was so happy. Tim took a photo of Spot with the girl and her toy. The unexpected thing was that Spot became friends with the little girl, and they all played together in the park every day. The moral of the story is that being kind and helpful can make new friends and bring happiness to others.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily who liked to act. She would dress up in different fashion and pretend to be someone else. One day, she wore a pretty dress and acted like a princess. Her dog, Max, would watch and play along with her. Lily's friend, Tim, came over to play. "Do you have any dress-up clothes for me?" Tim asked. Lily said, "Yes, I have some available. Let's act like we are in a castle!" So, they dressed up and played together, having lots of fun. As they acted, something unexpected happened. Max found a real crown hidden in the dress-up box! Lily and Tim were very surprised. They showed the crown to Lily's mom, who told them it was just a toy. But Lily and Tim knew it was magic, and they played with it all day long, feeling like real royalty.
