Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She wanted to play and have fun. Sue was a good girl who always listened to her mom and dad. At the park, she saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As she climbed, she felt happy and excited. While Sue was in the tree, she saw something in the leaves. She looked closely and discovered a small pet! It was a little cat. The cat was scared and stuck in the tree. Sue felt bad for the cat and wanted to help it get down. She held the cat tight and climbed down the tree. Sue took the cat home to show her mom and dad. They were surprised but happy to see the cat. They thought it was a good idea to keep the cat as their pet. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat's real owner came to their door. The owner was looking for the cat and saw it with Sue. Sue felt sad but knew she had to give the cat back. The owner was so happy and thanked Sue for being a good girl. Sue learned that helping others can make you feel good inside, even when you have to say goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a little flea named Tim. Tim lived in a tight space in a big house. He liked to jump and play all day. One day, Tim found a long string. He thought it was fun to play with. Tim wanted to measure how high he could jump with the string. He asked his friend, Bee, to help him. Bee said, "We can use this string to see how high you jump. I will hold it for you." Tim was happy and excited to try. Tim jumped and jumped as high as he could. The string showed he jumped very high! He was so proud of himself. Tim and Bee played with the string all day, measuring and jumping. They had a lot of fun together. And from that day on, Tim knew he was a great jumper.

Once there was an ancient candle. Everyone said this candle was very special. One day, a young boy wanted to see the candle. He said to his mom, "can I see the candle?". His mother said "yes, but be careful". The boy went to the ancient candle and he looked at it. He reached out and tried to touch it. Suddenly, the candle started to get brighter and brighter. The little boy stepped back in surprise. Suddenly, the ancient candle exploded! It was very loud and the little boy was scared. He ran back to his mom and said, "Mommy, the candle exploded!" His mother was sad and said, "I told you to be careful." The little boy was very upset and he cried. Everyone knew that the ancient candle was very special and now it was gone. They all said it was a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a modest cart. It was not big or fancy. The cart was happy being small. It lived with a nice man. The man and the cart would keep things in it and go to the market. One day, the man put a big box in the cart. The cart was excited to see what was inside. They went to the market, and the man opened the box. Out came a lot of balloons. The cart thought the balloons were pretty. The man tied the balloons to the cart. The cart felt very special. But then, something unexpected happened. The balloons lifted the cart up into the sky! The cart was scared, but it saw the man running after it. The man was able to grab the cart and bring it back down. The cart was happy to be safe and back with the man. From that day on, the cart and the man were more careful with balloons.

One day, a curious little girl named Lily went to the kitchen. She wanted to help her mom make a cake. Lily's mom said, "Okay, you can help me stir the mix." Lily took a big spoon and started to stir. She stirred and stirred until the mix was smooth. Her mom gave her a measure of sugar to put in the mix. Lily poured the sugar in and stirred some more. Then, Lily's mom said, "Now we need to put the cake in the oven." She opened the oven door and put the cake inside. Lily watched as the cake baked. She was excited to see how it would turn out. After a while, the cake was done. Lily's mom took it out of the oven and let it cool. When it was cool, they put frosting on it. Lily was so proud of the cake she helped make. Finally, it was time to eat the cake. Lily's family all enjoyed the cake and told her she did a great job. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you! I had fun stirring and helping mom make the cake."
