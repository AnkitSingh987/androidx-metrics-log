Category: Intermediate
Title: Finn the Fish and the Magic

Once upon a time, in a clear blue sea, there lived a small fish named Finn. Finn loved to swim with the tide and play with his friends. One day, while playing, Finn hurt his fin on a sharp rock. He felt sad because he could not swim fast anymore. Finn's friends wanted to help him heal. They swam around the sea, looking for something that could make Finn's fin better. While they were searching, they found a big, shiny pearl. It was so pretty and bright that they thought it must have magic powers. The fish brought the pearl to Finn and touched it to his hurt fin. Suddenly, something unexpected happened! The pearl turned into a magical sea fairy. The fairy waved her wand, and Finn's fin was healed. From that day on, Finn and his friends played happily in the tide, and they knew that magic could be found in the most surprising places.

Once upon a time, there was a wild bird. The bird could fly very high in the sky. It liked to do tricks while it was up there. The bird was happy and loved to play. One day, the wild bird met a small cat. The cat looked up at the bird and wanted to play too. The bird flew down from the high tree and they played together. They had a lot of fun and became good friends. Every day, the bird would fly high and do tricks for the cat. The cat would laugh and clap its paws. They played together all the time and were very happy. The wild bird and the small cat were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived near a big pond. She loved to play by the pond every day. One sunny day, she saw a fish in the pond. The fish was sad. Lily asked the fish, "Why are you sad, fish?" The fish said, "I am hungry. I cannot find any food." Lily wanted to help the fish. She went to her home and took some bread. Lily came back to the pond and gave the bread to the fish. The fish ate the bread and was very happy. The fish said, "Thank you, Lily. I am grateful for your help." Lily smiled and felt happy that she could help the fish. From that day on, Lily and the fish became good friends. They played together by the pond every day. The fish would yield to Lily's wishes and they had a lot of fun. Lily and the fish were always grateful for their friendship.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car with a cable. He loved to pull the cable and watch the car go fast. One day, Tim saw a different car that was bigger and faster. He wanted to play with it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Can I play with your car?" Sam said, "Sure, but you have to pull the cable and release it to make it go fast." Tim was excited to try the new car. He pulled the cable and released it, but the car did not move. Tim was sad and confused. He tried again and again, but the car still did not move. Then, Sam showed Tim how to do it right. Tim watched and learned. He pulled the cable and released it just like Sam. This time, the car went fast! Tim was happy and played with the different car all day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little bird named Bree. Bree loved to fly high and raise her wings. She liked to watch the sun come up every morning. One day, Bree met a new friend, a small bug named Tiny. Tiny could not fly, but he was very useful. He could help Bree find food on the ground. Bree and Tiny liked to play and talk together. Bree said, "Tiny, let's watch the sun come up together!" Tiny was happy and said, "Yes, Bree! I would love that!" So, Bree and Tiny sat on a branch and watched the sun come up. They were very happy to be friends.
