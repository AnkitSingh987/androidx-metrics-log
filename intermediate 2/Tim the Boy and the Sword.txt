Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sword

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy sword. He liked to play with it a lot. One day, he wanted to show his sword to his friend, Sam. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look at my sword!" Sam was confused. He did not know what a sword was. Tim tried to display the sword to Sam, but Sam still did not understand. Tim decided to teach Sam how to play with the sword. They played together and had lots of fun. Now, Sam knew what a sword was and they both enjoyed playing with it. They were happy friends.

Lila was very enthusiastic about going to the farm with her mom and dad. She loved to see the animals, especially the horses. She wanted to pet them and feed them carrots. She also liked to watch the farmer use a whip to make the horses run fast and pull the cart. One day, when they arrived at the farm, Lila saw a new horse. It was white and had a long mane and tail. It looked very pretty and friendly. Lila wondered what its name was and if it liked carrots too. She asked her mom if she could go and say hello to the horse. Her mom said yes, but told her to be careful and not to go too close to the whip. She said the whip was not a toy and it could hurt the horse or Lila if she touched it. Lila nodded and walked slowly towards the horse. She held out a carrot in her hand and smiled. The horse saw Lila and neighed softly. It walked towards her and sniffed the carrot. It liked the smell and took a bite. Lila giggled and stroked the horse's nose. She felt its warm breath and soft fur. She asked the horse what its name was, but the horse only neighed again. Lila decided to call the horse Snowy, because it was white like snow. She told Snowy that she was her new friend and that she would come back to visit him again. She gave him another carrot and a hug. Snowy nuzzled her and licked her cheek. Lila was very happy and thanked Snowy for being so nice. She ran back to her mom and dad and told them all about Snowy. They smiled and said they were glad she had fun. They said it was time to go home, but they would come back to the farm soon. Lila waved goodbye to Snowy and the other horses. She hoped they would remember her and be happy too. She got in the car and looked out the window. She wondered what Snowy was doing and if he missed her. She decided to draw a picture of him when she got home. She loved Snowy and the farm very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to play in the park. One day, he saw a tunnel near the trees. Max was curious and wanted to see what was inside. As Max walked into the tunnel, he felt the ground become wet. "Oh no!" he said, "I might slip!" Max was careful and took slow steps. He did not want to fall. He knew it was serious to be safe. At the end of the tunnel, Max met a new friend, a small cat named Luna. Luna said, "Hi Max! Be careful, the ground is wet, and you might slip." Max smiled and said, "Thank you, Luna! I will be careful." They played together in the park and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb. One sunny day, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. But, his parent said, "No, Tim. It's too high." Tim felt troubled. He wanted to show his parent that he could climb the big tree. So, when his parent was not looking, Tim started to climb the tree. He climbed higher and higher. Suddenly, Tim got scared. The tree was very high! He called for his parent. His parent came running and helped Tim down. Tim learned to listen to his parent and not to climb too high. They hugged, and Tim was happy.
