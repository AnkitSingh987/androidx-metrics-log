Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa and the Scarf

Lisa liked to learn new things. She learned how to count, how to sing, and how to draw. She also learned how to knit. She wanted to make a scarf for her mom. She asked her dad to buy her some yarn. He said yes, but he only gave her a little money. He said yarn was expensive. Lisa went to the store with her dad. She saw many colors of yarn. She liked the red one, the blue one, and the purple one. But they were all too expensive. She only had enough money for the white one. She was sad, but she bought it anyway. She thought her mom would still like it. Lisa worked hard on her scarf. She knitted every day after school. She made it long and warm. She was proud of her scarf. She wrapped it in a nice paper and put a bow on it. She waited for her mom to come home. When her mom came home, Lisa ran to her and gave her the scarf. She said, "Mom, I made this for you. I learned how to knit. I hope you like it." Her mom smiled and hugged her. She said, "Lisa, this is the most beautiful scarf I ever saw. I love it. Thank you so much. You are very clever and kind." Lisa was happy. She did not care that her scarf was white. She cared that her mom liked it. She learned that it was not the color or the price that mattered. It was the love that mattered. She learned a good lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a kind little pig named Bob. One day, Bob entered a meadow full of flowers and sunshine. Everything was lovely, until a mean fox came trotting along. Bob had never seen a fox before, and was not sure what to do. The fox started to move closer, and Bob got scared. Suddenly, he ran away as fast as his little legs could carry him. The fox tried to catch up, but all its chasing was no good - Bob was too quick and soon it was far away. When Bob reached the safety of his barn, he realized he was very lucky. He thanked the kind farmer who had saved him and vowed to never again enter the meadow alone.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a pretty bandage on the ground. She thought it belonged to someone, but she didn't know who. Mia decided to keep the bandage and see if she could find the person it belonged to later. Mia went to the park to play with her friends. While playing, her friend Sam tripped and fell. "Ouch!" Sam cried, "My knee hurts!" Mia remembered the bandage she found earlier. She thought it would help her friend feel better. Mia took out the pretty bandage and put it on Sam's knee. "Thank you, Mia!" Sam said, smiling. "You're a good friend." Mia was happy that she could help her friend. The bandage she found now belonged to someone who needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and many toys. One day, Tom saw a small boy named Sam. Sam was sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, Sam?" Sam said, "My toy is dirty." Tom wanted to help Sam. He took a cloth and started to wipe the toy. As he wiped, the toy grew an inch. It was a surprise! Tom and Sam looked at the toy in wonder. Tom wiped the toy again, and it grew another inch. Sam was happy now. They played with the big toy together. Soon, they became best friends. Tom learned that sharing made him happier than having a lot of things.

Once upon a time, there was a person named Tim. Tim liked to display his toys on a big shelf. He had many toys like cars, dolls, and blocks. Tim was very happy when he saw his toys all lined up. One day, Tim found a broken toy car. It had a missing wheel and was very sad. Tim wanted to help the broken car. He searched for a new wheel to fix the car and make it happy. Tim found a wheel that was just right for the broken car. He fixed the car and put it on the shelf with the other toys. Now, the car was not broken anymore. It was happy to be with its friends. Tim was happy too, as he looked at his toys all together on the big shelf.
