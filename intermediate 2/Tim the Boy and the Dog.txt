Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dog

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to play outside. He saw the sun rise and knew it was time to go out. He put on his shoes and ran to the park. At the park, Tim found a big ball. He kicked it and it rolled under a tree. Tim looked under the tree and saw a little girl named Sue. She was sad because she lost her ball. Tim said, "I found your ball!" Sue was happy and wanted to play with Tim. But then, a big dog came and took the ball. Tim and Sue were sad. They wanted the ball back. They ran after the dog and said, "Please give our ball back!" The dog listened and gave the ball back. Tim and Sue were happy again and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lily. Lily liked to help her mom. One day, Lily's mom asked her to load the soap in the washing machine. Lily was happy to help. Lily put the soap in the washing machine and started it. She was excited to see the clothes get clean. But something unexpected happened! The washing machine started to make big bubbles. The bubbles grew and grew. They filled the whole room! Lily was scared, but she knew what to do. She turned off the washing machine and the bubbles stopped. Lily's mom came in and saw the mess. She was not mad. She laughed and said, "Good job, Lily! Now we know not to use too much soap!" They cleaned up the bubbles together and had fun.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Alice. Alice liked to play in the garden. One day she went outside and noticed a hose. She was happy to see it. Alice said, "Mama, look, a hose!" Alice's mama said, "Yes, sweetie, you can use the hose to water the plants." So Alice grabbed the hose, went over to the flowers, and started to water them. The flowers felt very grateful and cheered for Alice. Alice said, "What was that?" Mama laughed and said, "That was the flowers saying thank you for watering them." Alice smiled and said, "That was nice!" Alice then put down the hose and looked at all of the happy flowers. She felt very grateful for the hose and for being able to help.

Lily liked to poke things. She poked her toys, her books, and her cat. But her favorite thing to poke was the tomato. The tomato was red and round and soft. It made a funny sound when Lily poked it. One day, Lily saw a new tomato in the kitchen. It was bigger and greener than the other tomatoes. Lily wanted to poke it. She climbed on a chair and reached for the tomato. But the tomato was not a tomato. It was a balloon. The balloon popped when Lily poked it. It made a loud noise and scared Lily. She fell off the chair and started to cry. Her mom came and hugged her. She said, "It's okay, Lily. It was just a balloon. You didn't know. But don't poke things that are not yours." Lily said she was sorry. She asked her mom, "Can I have a real tomato?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can. You can have a charming tomato. It's red and round and soft. And it won't pop when you poke it." Lily was happy. She thanked her mom and took the charming tomato. She poked it gently and giggled. She liked to poke things. But she learned to poke only the right things.

Anna and Ben love to go to the park with Mom. They like to play on the swings, the seesaw and the slide. The slide is smooth and shiny. Anna and Ben take turns to climb up the ladder and slide down fast. Whee! One day, they see a big truck at the park. It has a picture of popcorn on the side. Mom says it is a popcorn truck. It makes popcorn for people to eat. Anna and Ben are curious. They want to try some popcorn. Mom buys a bag of popcorn from the truck. She gives some to Anna and Ben. They say thank you and take a bite. The popcorn is crunchy and salty and yummy. They like it a lot. They share it with Mom. They finish the popcorn and throw the bag in the trash. Then they run back to the slide. They slide down again and again. They laugh and have fun. They are happy. They love the park and the popcorn.
