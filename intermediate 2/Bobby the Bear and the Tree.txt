Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobby the Bear and the Tree

One day, a little bear named Bobby was ready to find some honey. He walked and walked through the forest until he saw a big tree. The tree had a hole in it, and Bobby knew that bees lived there. He knew bees made honey, so he wanted to get some. Bobby tried to climb the tree, but it was very hard. He did not quit. He kept trying and trying. Finally, he made it to the hole where the bees lived. He said, "Hello, bees! Can I please have some honey?" The bees buzzed and said, "Yes, Bobby, you can have some honey. But you must be careful not to take too much. We need it too." Bobby was happy and took a little bit of honey. He thanked the bees and climbed down the tree. Bobby went home and shared the honey with his family. They all enjoyed the sweet taste. Bobby was proud that he did not quit and was able to find the honey. His family was happy that he was ready to try something new.

Once upon a time, there was a small insect named Buzz. Buzz was very good at flying. He had a friend named Hoppy. Hoppy was a bunny who wanted to learn how to fly too. Buzz wanted to teach Hoppy how to fly. One day, Buzz and Hoppy went to a big hill. Buzz told Hoppy, "We will start here. I will teach you to fly from this hill." Hoppy was very excited. He hopped up and down, ready to fly like Buzz. Hoppy tried to fly many times, but he could not. Buzz did not give up. He kept teaching Hoppy. Then, Hoppy had an idea. He found a small stick and tied it to his back. Buzz helped Hoppy run down the hill with the stick. Hoppy jumped, and he started to fly! Buzz and Hoppy were so happy. They flew together all day long.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a picture. The picture showed a big, compassionate dog. The dog had kind eyes and a big smile. Amy liked the picture a lot. She wanted to put the picture on her wall. Amy asked her mom, "Can I put this on my wall?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." So, Amy put the picture on her wall. Every day, she would look at the picture and smile. One day, Amy heard a bark outside her window. She looked out and saw the same big, compassionate dog from the picture! Amy was so happy and surprised. The dog had jumped out of the picture and was now her new friend. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a weird toy. It was a talking pan. The pan could talk and cook. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came to play. "Welcome, Sue!" said the pan. "I can make yummy food for you!" Sue was scared of the talking pan. She did not want to play with Tim and the pan. Sue wanted to go home. Tim was sad. He did not want Sue to go home. Tim said, "Please, Sue, do not be scared. The pan is nice." Sue thought about it. Then, she said, "Okay, I will stay and play." The pan made them yummy food. Sue was not scared anymore. Tim and Sue played and had fun. They became best friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big rope hanging from a tree. They wanted to swing on it. "Can I go first?" Lila asked. "OK, but be careful," Ben said. Lila ran to the rope and grabbed it. She swung back and forth, feeling the wind in her hair. She laughed and shouted, "This is fun!" Ben clapped his hands. He was happy for Lila. He said, "You are very good at swinging. I applaud you!" Lila smiled and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are very kind. Do you want to try?" Ben nodded. He let go of Lila's hand and took the rope. He swung as high as he could. He felt brave and free. Lila cheered for Ben. She said, "You are amazing at swinging. I applaud you!" Ben blushed. He was modest. He said, "Thank you, Lila. You are very nice. Do you want to swing together?" Lila nodded. They held hands and swung on the rope. They felt happy and proud. They applauded each other.
