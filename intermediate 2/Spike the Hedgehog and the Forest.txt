Category: Intermediate
Title: Spike the Hedgehog and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a little hedgehog named Spike. Spike loved to play and act like a brave hero. One sunny day, he went on an adventure in the forest with his best friend, a small squirrel named Nutty. As they played, Spike and Nutty saw a big, scary shadow. Spike decided to act brave, like the hero he always pretended to be. He looked at Nutty and said, "Don't be scared, I will protect you!" Nutty felt safe with his friend and nodded. Spike and Nutty went closer to the big shadow. They found out it was just a big, friendly bear named Ben. Ben was not scary at all! He laughed and said, "I'm not scary, I'm just big!" Spike, Nutty, and Ben became friends and played together in the wild forest, having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a purple rat. The rat was very nice and liked to play with the little boy who lived there. They were best friends. The little boy's name was Tim. One day, Tim and the rat were playing in the garden. They saw a big cat. The cat wanted to catch the rat. Tim said, "No, cat! You cannot catch my friend!" Tim ordered the cat to go away. The cat listened to Tim and left. The purple rat was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Tim, for saving me!" Tim and the rat played more and had lots of fun. They were always together and took care of each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red cap that he loved very much. He wore it every day when he went outside to play. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam looked at Tim's red cap and felt envious. He wished he had a cap just like Tim's. Sam said to Tim, "I like your red cap. Can I wear it?" Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, you can wear it today." Tim permitted Sam to wear his red cap. They played together all day, and Sam was very happy. When it was time to go home, Sam gave the cap back to Tim. Tim was glad to have his red cap back, and they both went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time there was a family who were all very happy together. One day they wanted to have a special day and so they decided to count to see who could reach the highest number. Mum said, "Let's count together and use our numbers to see who gets the most!" So everybody began to count. Dad got to 5, Mum got to 10 and their 3-year-old daughter got to 15! Everyone was so proud of her and clapped with joy. The daughter said, "I'm better at counting - I'm brilliant!" Mum said, "No need to boast - just be proud of yourself!" But then a judge appeared. He said, "I can count even higher! I'm brilliant, too!" So the judge started counting and reached 70! Everyone was so impressed. The judge said, "I'm the best at counting - I'm brilliant!" Everyone cheered and agreed that the judge was right. The day ended with a big, happy count.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. She loved to play with her toy, a red ball. One day, Mia found a hidden box in her room. She opened the box and saw a pretty veil inside. Mia put the veil on her head and went to show her mom. "Look, Mom," said Mia, "I found a veil!" Her mom was surprised. "Mia, that's a magic veil! It can record what you say and sing." Mia was so happy. She wore the veil and sang her favorite song. The veil recorded her song. But then, her brother Max came and took the veil. He wanted to play with it too. "No, Max! It's mine!" Mia cried. But Max didn't listen. He put the veil on and started to sing a silly song. The veil recorded Max's song too. Mia was sad, but then something unexpected happened. The veil started to sing both Mia's favorite song and Max's silly song at the same time! Mia and Max laughed and laughed. They both learned to share the magic veil and played together happily.
