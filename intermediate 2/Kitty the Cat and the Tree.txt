Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Tree

One day, a small cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Kitty was light and fast, so she went up the tree quickly. She was very happy at the top. She could see her home and her friends below. Kitty saw her friend, Doggy, and called out, "Doggy, come see me up here!" Doggy looked up and saw Kitty. He wanted to go up the tree too, but he was scared. Doggy said, "Kitty, I want to come up, but I am scared to climb." Kitty thought for a moment and said, "I will help you, just take a turn and try." Doggy took a deep breath and started to climb. He was slow, but he was trying. Kitty peeked down from the top and cheered for him. Doggy was almost there when he got scared again. He looked down and saw how high he was. He started to cry. Kitty knew she had to help her friend. She climbed down to be with Doggy. She said, "Don't worry, Doggy, we can go down together." They both went down the tree, taking turns and helping each other. When they reached the ground, they were both happy and safe. From that day on, Kitty and Doggy knew they could face their fears together. They became the best of friends and always helped each other when they were scared. They knew that with a little help and a turn, they could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a kind tutor. The tutor liked to help little kids learn. The tutor had a big, safe room for the kids to play and learn in. One day, a little girl named Lisa was playing with her ball in the safe room. The ball was going too fast and was about to hit the wall. The tutor saw this and wanted to prevent it. The tutor ran and stopped the ball before it could hit the wall. Lisa was happy and said, "Thank you, tutor!" The tutor smiled and said, "Always be careful, Lisa. I will keep you safe."

Mommy was measuring something on the table. She smiled and said to her 3 year old daughter, "Honey, I'm measuring the texture of the fabric. Don't you think it looks nice?" The daughter went over and felt the fabric with her little hands. She smiled and said, "It's so soft and nice, Mommy! I love it." Mommy smiled and said, "That's why I'm measuring it – to make sure it's just the right texture for our new dress. She continued to measure the fabric and soon finished. She smiled and said, "Now it's perfect." The daughter smiled and said, "I can't wait to see the dress, Mommy." Mommy hugged her daughter and said, "Me too, sweetheart. Me too!"

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toys. She had a fake baby doll that she loved very much. She liked to pretend she was a nurse and take care of her baby. Lily's mom called her and said, "Lily, come here! You have a surprise to receive." Lily ran to her mom and saw a big box. She opened the box and inside was a new nurse dress and a hat. She was so happy! Lily put on her new nurse dress and hat. She went back to her baby doll and said, "Now I am a real nurse!" She took care of her baby all day, giving it food and hugs. Lily loved being a nurse with her fake baby, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a wild cat named Tom. Tom liked to play near a stream. The stream had cool water and many fish. Tom loved to catch the fish and eat them. One day, Tom saw a big fish in the stream. He wanted to catch it. Tom jumped into the water and tried to slap the fish with his paw. But the fish was too fast. Tom tried again and again, but the fish kept swimming away. Finally, Tom was able to slap the fish and catch it. He was so happy! Tom took the fish to his home and shared it with his friends. They all enjoyed the tasty fish. From that day on, Tom and his friends played near the stream and caught many more fish.
