Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Rope

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, long rope that she loved to play with. One day, Lily wanted to measure the rope to see how long it was. She knew it was important to find out. Lily asked her friend, Tim, to help her. "Tim, will you help me measure my rope?" she asked. Tim said, "Yes, I will help you, Lily!" They both held the rope and started to measure it. They counted out loud, "One, two, three, four, five!" At the end, they found out that the rope was five steps long. Lily and Tim were very happy. They knew it was important to measure the rope, and now they could play with it even more. They played all day, jumping and running with the rope, and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a person named Tom. He lived in a small house near a big tree. Tom was a frightened person. He was scared of loud noises and big animals. One day, Tom went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big bear. The bear said, "Hi, Tom! Do you want to play with me?" Tom was very scared. He wanted to ignore the bear and run away. But, he thought that maybe the bear was nice. So, Tom said, "Okay, we can play together." They played hide and seek. The bear hid behind a tree and Tom looked for him. When Tom found the bear, he saw that the bear was not big and scary. The bear was small and friendly! From that day on, Tom and the bear were best friends. They played together every day. Tom was not frightened anymore. He learned that sometimes, things are not as scary as they seem.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an amazing toy saw. He loved to play with it all day long. One day, his mom asked him to help her clean up the house. Tim didn't want to stop playing, so he thought of a plan to delay the cleaning. Tim hid his saw under his bed and told his mom that he couldn't find it. His mom looked for the saw everywhere, but she couldn't find it. Tim was happy because he could play with his toys a little longer. But soon, he felt sad that he lied to his mom. Later, Tim decided to tell his mom the truth. He showed her the saw under his bed. His mom was happy that Tim was honest. She told him that it's always better to tell the truth, even if it's hard. Tim learned the importance of being honest and not to delay things. From that day on, he always told the truth and helped his mom without any delays.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Binky. Binky loved to hop and play with his friends in the big green field. One day, Binky saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with the ball, so he hopped over to it. When Binky got to the ball, he saw a note on it. The note said, "To Binky, from your friend." Binky was so happy to receive a gift from his friend! He knew his friend wanted him to have fun with the ball. So, Binky started to play with the ball. He kicked it, rolled it, and even bounced it on his head! As Binky played with the ball, he started to feel tired. He needed some rest. He looked around and saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was soft grass. Binky thought it was the perfect place to rest. He hopped over to the tree and lay down on the soft grass. Binky closed his eyes and took a deep breath. He smelled the sweet smell of flowers and felt the soft grass on his fur. Binky was so happy and cozy. He knew he had the best day ever, all thanks to his friend and the big red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man who had a big umbrella. He loved his umbrella very much. One day, it rained, and the rich man took his umbrella outside to keep dry. He walked and walked, and soon he met a little girl. "Hi, can I share your umbrella?" the little girl asked. The rich man said yes, and they walked together. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. The rich man and the little girl were happy. But the umbrella was dirty from the rain. The rich man wanted to wash it. So, he took a cloth and some water, and he washed the umbrella until it was clean. The little girl helped him. When they were done, the umbrella was shiny and new again. The rich man and the little girl laughed and played with the clean umbrella. And they became good friends.
