Category: Intermediate
Title: The Her Mom and the Sandy

Sandy walked into her room and saw her mom. Her mom smiled, and said, "I have something for you." Sandy was excited, and hopped up and down. Her mom opened the drawer and pulled out a soft, fuzzy sock. Sandy clapped and said, "Sock! Is for me?" "Yes," her mom replied. She was so thoughtful. Sandy grabbed the sock and held it close to her chest. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Mommy!" But when Sandy tried to put the sock on, it was too small. She tried again and again but it was still too small. Her mom looked sadly at Sandy. "You can put it away now, and I'll get you something else." Sandy sighed and closed the drawer. She was sad that her mom had been so thoughtful but the sock didn't fit.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She lived on a farm with her mom, dad, and her pet cow, Moo. They had lots of hay to feed Moo and the other animals on the farm. One day, Lily found a hole in the fence where the hay was kept. She saw Moo eating the hay through the hole. Lily said, "Oh no, Moo! You can't eat all the hay!" Moo looked at her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was very hungry." Lily decided to fix the fence. She asked her mom and dad for help. They all worked together to fix the hole. When they were done, Lily said, "There, now Moo can't eat all the hay!" Moo thanked Lily and promised not to eat the hay through the fence anymore. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a cold bedroom. He did not like his cold bedroom, so he told his mom. His mom wanted to help him. One day, Tim and his mom went to the store to buy something to make the bedroom warm. They saw a big, soft blanket. Tim liked the blanket. His mom bought the blanket for him. When they got home, Tim put the blanket on his bed in his bedroom. That night, Tim slept in his warm bed. He was happy and no longer cold in his bedroom. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy phone that he loved to play with. He would call his friends and talk to them for a long time. One day, Tim saw some money on the table. He wanted to buy candy with it. Tim's mom saw him take the money. She said, "Tim, that is not your money. You cannot take it to buy candy." Tim was sad and wanted the candy so much. He knew his mom was right, but he still wanted the candy. Tim thought of a plan. He would call his friend, Sam, and ask for some candy. Sam came over with a big bag of candy. They ate the candy together. Some candy was sweet, and some was sour. Tim and Sam had a great time. In the end, Tim learned that it is better to ask for help instead of taking things that are not his.

James and his mum were in the garden. The sun was shining and there were lots of orange flowers. James was so excited and he wanted to talk. 'Mummy, look at the orange flowers,' said James. Mummy smiled and said, 'Yes, they are so beautiful James.' Then, James heard a terrible noise. He started to cry. Mummy explained, 'That noise is called thunder, it's ok James, don't be scared.' James and his mum talked a lot about the noise and soon James stopped crying. The moral of the story is that it's ok to be scared sometimes, but talking to someone you trust can help those scary feelings go away.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a different cushion. This cushion was not like the others. It was big and had many colors. Tim liked this cushion a lot. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. They played with toys and had fun. Then, Sam saw the different cushion. He said, "Wow, what a fun cushion! Can I jump on it?" Tim said, "Yes, but be careful." Sam jumped on the cushion, but he jumped too hard. The cushion went "pop" and all the colors came out. They both looked at the cushion and saw that it was destroyed. They were sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Tim's mom came in and saw the destroyed cushion. She said, "Don't worry, boys. I can fix it." She put all the colors back in the cushion and sewed it up. Tim and Sam were happy to see the different cushion all fixed. They said, "Thank you, mom!" and promised to be more careful next time.
