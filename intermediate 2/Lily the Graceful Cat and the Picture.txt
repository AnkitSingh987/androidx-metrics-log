Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Graceful Cat and the Picture

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lily. She lived in a small house with her best friend, a little girl named Anna. They loved to play together all day long. One day, Lily was playing with her toy near a picture on the wall. The picture was very special to Anna and her family. But Lily did not know that. She jumped high to catch her toy and accidentally hit the picture. The picture fell down and broke. Anna saw the broken picture and was sad. But she knew Lily did not mean to break it. She gave Lily a big hug and said, "It's okay, Lily. We can fix the picture together." And they did. From that day on, Lily was more careful when she played near the special picture.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run around. One day, Max saw a stream near his home. The stream made Max excite. He wanted to play in the water. Max jumped into the stream and splashed around. He had so much fun. But then, Max saw a fish in the water. The fish looked sad. Max felt guilty because he thought he made the fish sad with his splashing. Max stopped splashing and gently played with the fish. The fish started to smile and play too. Max and the fish became good friends. They played in the stream every day and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a pretty picture in a frame. She wanted to display the frame on her wall. Lucy was happy. One day, a big, loud dog came into Lucy's room. The dog saw the frame and was frightened. The dog said, "Woof, woof! I am scared of the frame!" The frightened dog ran around the room. He bumped into the wall. The frame fell down and broke. Lucy was sad. The end.

Sara liked to step on the grass and feel it tickle her feet. She liked to see the plants that grew in the garden. She liked the red ones and the yellow ones and the purple ones. She liked to smell them and touch them gently. One day, Sara saw a new plant in the garden. It was big and green and had a funny shape. It looked like a cup with a lid. Sara was very nosy. She wanted to know what was inside the plant. She stepped closer and closer to the plant. She reached out her hand to lift the lid. But the plant was not a plant. It was a trap. It was a hungry plant that liked to eat bugs and sometimes bigger things. When Sara touched the lid, the plant snapped shut. It caught Sara's hand and tried to pull her in. Sara screamed and cried. She was scared and hurt. Mama heard Sara's scream and ran to the garden. She saw the plant and Sara's hand. She was angry and scared too. She grabbed a stick and hit the plant hard. She hit it again and again until the plant opened its mouth and let go of Sara's hand. She picked up Sara and hugged her tight. She checked her hand and saw it was red and sore. She took her inside and put some cream and a bandage on it. She kissed her hand and told her it would be okay. Sara learned a lesson that day. She learned that some plants are not nice and some plants are dangerous. She learned that she should not be too nosy and touch things she did not know. She learned that Mama loved her and would always help her. She said sorry to Mama and thanked her for saving her. She said she would be more careful in the garden. She still liked the plants, but she did not step too close to the big green one anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in the big forest near his house. One day, he saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird was very sad. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he picked it up and took it home. At home, Tim took care of the bird. The bird was very hungry, so it would demand food all the time. Tim fed the bird and made it feel better. The bird was very happy and became Tim's friend. They played together every day. The bird was very persistent and kept trying to fly. One day, the bird's wing was all better. Tim knew the bird needed to go back to the forest. So, he took the bird back to the big forest and let it go. The bird flew high in the sky and said "thank you" to Tim. They were both very happy.
