Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Ella and the Friend

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Ella who loved to play. One day, her mum came to her and said “Ella, I want to invite you for a sleepover next week!” Ella smiled and said “It sounds fine, I would love it!” The following week, Ella was so excited and couldn’t wait for her sleepover. She put on her favourite pink dress and went to her friend’s house. When she arrived, her friend greeted her with a big hug. They had a wonderful time together, playing games, chatting and eating yummy snacks. At night, it was time for Ella to go back to her house but her friend asked “Can you please stay for the night?” Ella smiled and agreed. They both laughed and had lots of fun. They even managed to stay up until late. The next morning, Ella woke up feeling so happy. Everything was so fine and she felt like she had a great week. She thanked her friend for inviting her and said goodbye before heading home.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop and play all day long. One day, Bob went to the park to meet his friend, a bird named Sue. They hoped to have a fun day together. As they played, they saw a big box. They didn't know what was inside. Bob and Sue looked at each other and smiled. They wanted to see the surprise inside the box. They hoped it would be a toy or a treat. Bob and Sue opened the box, and out jumped a frog named Tim! They didn't expect a new friend to be inside the box. Tim was a funny frog who loved to play too. Bob, Sue, and Tim played together all day long. They had a great day full of laughter and fun. The surprise in the box made their day even better.

Once upon a time, there was a man. This man was tough. He had a big dog. The man and the dog lived in a small house. They were happy. One day, a big wind came. It blew and blew. The man's house was not strong. The wind blew the house down. The man and the dog were sad. The tough man knew he had to own a new house. He and the dog worked hard. They built a new house. This house was strong. The wind could not blow it down. The man and the dog were happy again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a mint. It was a green and sweet mint. He wanted to find a match for his mint, so he went to ask his friends. Tim went to his best friend, Sam. Sam had a red mint. "No, this is not a match," Tim said. He went to his friend, Liz. Liz had a blue mint. "No, this is not a match," Tim said again. He felt bad because he could not find a match for his green mint. Then, Tim saw a big, green tree. "This is a match!" Tim said, happy. He put his green mint under the tree. But a big wind came and blew the mint away. Tim was sad because he lost his mint and did not find a real match. The end was bad for Tim.

Once, there was a little boy who loved to rock. Everyday, he would rock back and forth, back and forth in his rocking chair. One day, he decided to have an adventure outside. As he rocked off, he heard a voice. It said, "Be careful, there is something rude ahead". He thought it was strange but he kept on going. The little boy eventually came to a pond. A beautiful image was rocking in the water. He was mesmerized and thought it must be very special. He wanted to see it up close, so he started to rock into the water. But he was wrong. As he rocked closer, the image changed and became more rude. He realized the voice was warning him all along. He quickly rocked back, away from the sight. The little boy learned an important lesson that day. If a voice tells you something is rude, it's important to listen. He never forgot that, and he always listened to the voice.
