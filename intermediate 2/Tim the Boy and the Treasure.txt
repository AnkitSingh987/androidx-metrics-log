Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Treasure

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to search for hidden things. One day, he found a map in his room. The map showed the way to a big treasure box. Tim showed the map to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow, I wish I had a map like that. I am envious." Tim and Sam decided to follow the map together. They walked, and walked, and finally, they found a big tree with a big hole. Tim looked inside the hole and saw the treasure box. He was so happy! Tim and Sam opened the box and found many toys inside. They shared the toys and played together all day. Tim was glad he could share the treasure with his friend, and Sam was no longer envious.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Tom. Tom was three years old. One day, he went to the park. He saw a big pile of marbles. He asked his mum, "Can I play with them?" His mum said, "Yes, but be careful!" Tom picked up a few marbles. He rolled them around on the ground. It was easy to play with them. He laughed and ran around with the marbles in his hand. Suddenly, he heard a voice. He looked up and saw a little girl. She said, "Those are my marbles. Give them back!" Tom was surprised. He thought they were his marbles. He replied, "I don't have your marbles. I found them on the ground." The girl nodded and said, "They're mine. I dropped them earlier. Can you help me find them?" Tom smiled. He said, "Yes! I can help you. It'll be easy." He started to look for the marbles. Soon, he had found them all. The little girl was very pleased. She thanked Tom and gave him one of the marbles. Tom was so happy. He thanked her back and took the marble home.

Once upon a time, there was a restless squirrel. His name was Toby and he lived in a big tree in the forest. One day he saw something sparkly and shiny on the ground. He followed it, and it lead him to a big squash. Toby was so excited. He ran around the squash, hopping and jumping. Suddenly, the squash started moving. Toby was a bit scared but he still kept following it. The squash led Toby to a pond and Toby was so happy that he jumped into the water. He swam around the pond, feeling free and restless. Toby thanked the squash for leading him to such a beautiful place. He promised to visit again soon.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who had a mild day. She wanted something to cheer her up so she decided to take a walk outside. As she was walking, she heard a ringing sound. She looked around to see where it was coming from. Suddenly, she saw a ghost hovering above her. She smiled and said, "Hello, ghost!" Then the ghost smiled back and said, "Hello. I'm here to bring you some happiness." As the ghost waved its hand, the ringing suddenly became louder and the little girl's day became filled with joy. Then she waved goodbye to the ghost and ran back home. She knew that day was a mild one but she was sure it was one never to forget.

One day, a little girl named Amy went with her mom to the park. Amy was very excited because she loved the park. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Her mom said, "Be careful, Amy!" Amy climbed the tree and saw a brilliant, shiny thing on a branch. It was a saw! She was excited and wanted to show her mom. Amy carefully climbed down with the saw in her hand. She showed the saw to her mom. Her mom was happy and said, "Great job, Amy! Let's take it home and put it in our toolbox." Amy was excited and felt proud. They walked home, holding hands and smiling.

Lily and Max were playing with their sand toys on the beach. They wanted to make a big castle with towers and walls and a flag. They worked hard and used their buckets and shovels and molds. They were happy and proud of their castle. But then a big wave came and washed away their castle. Lily and Max were very upset. They cried and stamped their feet. They said it was not fair and they wanted their castle back. "Look, look!" said a voice. It was their friend Sam, who was walking by with his dog. "What happened to your castle?" "A wave took it away!" said Lily and Max. "We worked so hard and now it's gone!" "That's too bad," said Sam. "But don't be sad. You can make another one. You can succeed if you try again. Here, I'll help you. And my dog can be the guard." Sam gave Lily and Max a hug and a smile. He took out his own sand toys and joined them. They started to build a new castle, bigger and better than before. They used shells and stones and seaweed to decorate it. They put a palm leaf on top as a flag. They were happy and proud of their castle again. They thanked Sam for his help and his friendship. They played with his dog and had fun. They learned that sometimes things go wrong, but they can always try again and succeed. They also learned that having friends makes everything better.
