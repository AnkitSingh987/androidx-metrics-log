Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lily went out to search for her lost toy. She walked slow and looked all around. Her face was sad because she loved her toy so much. Lily met a big, friendly dog. The dog said, "Why is your face sad, Lily?" Lily told the dog, "I lost my toy and I am searching for it." The dog said, "I can help you search!" Together, they searched all around. The dog looked under a big tree and found Lily's toy. Lily's face was happy now. She said, "Thank you, dog!" They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia saw a boy with a big red ball. The boy looked sad because he had no one to play with. Mia wanted to make the boy happy. Mia asked her mom if she could give the boy a hug. Her mom said yes, so Mia walked up to the boy and gave him a big hug. The boy smiled and asked Mia if she wanted to play with the ball. They played together all day, and Mia felt very proud that she had made a new friend. At the end of the day, Mia and the boy were tired but happy. They said goodbye and Mia went home with her mom. That night, Mia told her dad about her new friend and how proud she was that she had made him happy. Her dad gave her a hug and told her she was a very kind girl.

Once there was a little girl named Sophie who loved wearing her favourite shirt. It was her favourite because it had an ocean blue colour her parents had bought for her birthday. She wore it every day and always felt very special in it. One day, Sophie went to the park with her friends. Everyone was playing happily together and having a lot of fun. Later, the group was joined by a wealthy girl who had a very pretty dress on. When the wealthy girl saw Sophie's shirt, she started to laugh and said, "You must be so embarrassed wearing something like that!" Sophie was confused. She looked down at her shirt and was suddenly ashamed. But then she reminded herself that everyone has different kinds of clothes and surely no one should be made to feel bad about what they wear. She replied to the wealthy girl, "My shirt may be old and simple, but I love it because it makes me feel special. I'm not embarrassed about my shirt and I think it's much better for everyone to accept the way we all dress differently." The wealthy girl smiled and thanked Sophie for the words. From that day on, she always chose to be kind and mindful of how she made others feel. The moral of this story is that we should never judge someone based on their clothing or style as it is a reflection of their personality. Instead, we should choose to be kind, accepting and understanding of differences

Lily likes to wear tops with flowers on them. She has many tops in her closet. She wants to pick one for today. She asks her mom to help her. "Mom, can you help me wear a top?" Lily says. "Sure, Lily. What top do you want to wear?" Mom says. Lily looks at her closet. She sees a red top, a blue top, a yellow top, and a pink top. All of them have flowers on them. She likes all of them. She does not know which one to choose. "Mom, I like all of them. Which one is easy to wear?" Lily says. Mom smiles. She picks up the pink top. She says, "This one is easy to wear, Lily. It has buttons on the front. You can open and close them by yourself. Do you want to try it?" Lily nods. She takes the pink top from Mom. She opens the buttons. She puts her head and arms through the top. She closes the buttons. She looks at herself in the mirror. She sees the flowers on her top. She smiles. "I like this top, Mom. It is easy to wear and it is pretty. Thank you for helping me, Mom." Lily says. "You're welcome, Lily. You look very nice in your pink top. Are you ready to go?" Mom says. Lily nods. She hugs Mom. She says, "Yes, Mom. I am ready to go. Let's go!" They hold hands. They walk out of the door. They go to the park. Lily has fun in her pink top. She sees other kids wearing tops with flowers on them. She says hello to them. She makes new friends. She is happy.
