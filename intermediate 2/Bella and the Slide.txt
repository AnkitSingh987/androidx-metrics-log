Category: Intermediate
Title: Bella and the Slide

Bella was so excited. She had been invited to her first ever birthday party! She was going to the park and her best friend Annie was going to be there too. When they arrived, the park was full of children. Everyone was playing games and celebrating. Bella felt so happy. Bella and Annie decided to go to the slide. As they reached the top, Bella noticed something strange. It was a pale video camera! She asked Annie what it was for. “Oh, that's for my birthday surprise,” said Annie. “My dad is going to make a video of all of us playing together. It’s going to be so much fun!” Bella was so excited that she started to climb up again. As she got back to the top, the slide suddenly started to move. Everyone was screaming as it quickly went down the steep hill. Bella was scared but suddenly Annie grabbed her hand. At the bottom, everyone was ok but full of joy. Annie's dad quickly started recording a video of all of them celebrating and laughing together. Bella thought the slide must have been part of Annie's surprise! It was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a big, comfortable bed that she loved to sleep in. Every day, she would play with her toys and have fun. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, I need to measure your bed. I want to buy a new sheet for it." Lily was excited and permitted her mom to do it. Her mom took a big ruler and measured the bed. It was very long and wide. Lily watched her mom and clapped her hands with joy. The next day, Lily's mom came home with a beautiful new sheet for the bed. Lily helped her mom put it on the bed. It was soft and pretty. That night, Lily slept in her comfortable bed with the new sheet, and she had the sweetest dreams.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a small hut. It was not fancy, but she liked it. Lily went inside the hut to look around. In the hut, Lily found a box. She opened the box and saw many things that match. There were socks, shoes, and even hats that match. Lily put on a hat and shoes that match. She felt happy. Just then, a little mouse came out from a hole in the hut. The mouse said, "Hello! I like your hat and shoes. They match!" Lily was surprised. She did not know that mice could talk. The mouse and Lily became friends and played together all day.

Lily and Ben are playing with a big ball in the park. They like to kick the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they miss the ball and it rolls away. Then they run after it and laugh. One time, the ball rolls too far and goes into the bushes. Lily and Ben follow it, but they cannot find it. They look and look, but the ball is gone. They feel sad and want to go home. "Where is our ball?" Lily asks. "I don't know. Maybe someone took it," Ben says. They see a man walking with a dog. The dog has something in its mouth. It is their ball! "Hey, that's our ball!" Lily shouts. The man hears her and looks at them. He smiles and says, "Sorry, I didn't know. My dog likes to play with balls. He found this one in the bushes. Do you want it back?" "Yes, please!" Ben says. The man tells his dog to drop the ball. The dog obeys, but not before making a big knot in the ball with his teeth. The ball is wet and dirty and has a hole in it. "Here you go, kids. I'm sorry the ball is not good anymore. It was a nice ball," the man says. He sends the ball to Lily and Ben. They catch it and look at it. It is not a nice ball anymore. It is a bad ball. It is wet and dirty and has a hole and a knot. "Thank you, mister. But we don't want this ball anymore. It is a bad ball," Lily says. She throws the ball away. It lands on the dry grass and does not bounce. Lily and Ben turn around and walk away. They are still sad and want to go home. They decide to play with something else next time. Maybe a kite or a doll or a car. Something that dogs don't like to play with. Something that doesn't get wet and dirty and have holes and knots. Something that is a good thing.
