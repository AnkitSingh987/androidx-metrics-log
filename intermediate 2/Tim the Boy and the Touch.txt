Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Touch

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play with his smooth blocks. One day, he built a big tower with his blocks. He was very happy. Tim wanted to touch the top of the tower. He reached up high, but he could not touch it. He jumped and jumped, but still could not touch the top. He felt sad. Then, a little bird flew by and saw Tim. The bird wanted to help. The bird picked up a block and put it on the top of the tower. Now, Tim could touch the top. Tim was very happy, and he and the bird played with the blocks together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Lily and her friends were playing near a big tree. They saw a little bug on the tree. The bug was only one inch long. Lily's mom told her to always behave and be safe when playing. Lily remembered her mom's words and decided to be careful with the little bug. She didn't want to hurt it. Lily and her friends watched the bug as it moved up the tree. They were very gentle and didn't touch the bug. They wanted the bug to feel safe. In the end, the little bug made it to the top of the tree. Lily and her friends were happy they behaved and kept the bug safe. They learned that being kind and gentle to all creatures is important. And that's how Lily and her friends learned to always behave and keep things safe when they play.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a pastry. Amy saw a big pastry that was very sweet. She asked her mom, "Can we get this one, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can get that one." They took the pastry home and put it on the table. Amy's mom said, "Now we can relax and enjoy our pastry." But when they looked at the table, the pastry was gone! They were very surprised. They looked everywhere but could not find the pastry. Then, they heard a noise from the living room. They went to see what it was. They saw their dog, Spot, eating the pastry! Spot had taken it when they were not looking. Amy's mom said, "Oh no, Spot ate our pastry! But don't worry, we can go back to the store and get another one. There are more available." Amy and her mom laughed and went back to the store to buy a new pastry.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the jungle near his home. One day, while playing, he saw a big rock. Tim was nervous, but he wanted to climb the rock. Tim started to climb the rock. Suddenly, he heard a voice. "Hello, can you help me?" said a small bird. The bird was stuck in a tree. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the bird. Tim climbed down from the rock and went to the tree. He helped the bird get free. The bird was very happy. "Thank you, Tim!" said the bird. Tim smiled and they both played in the jungle. They became good friends and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She really liked to explore new places and see what kind of fun things she could find. One day she decided to explore a new forest - she just knew she was going to find something special! As Lucy wandered through the forest, she found a big pear tree. She was so excited to see it and asked her mom if they could pick the pears. Her mom said yes and soon Lucy was picking pears from the tree. But then something strange happened. When Lucy reached for one of the pears, a voice said "Surprise!" Lucy was so surprised that she dropped the pear. When she looked up, she saw a tiny elf standing on the branch! He was wearing a fake hat and a fake beard, and he was laughing. He said, “I was waiting to surprise you! You should come back to the forest often - you never know what you might find!” Lucy smiled and waved goodbye to the elf. She put the pear in her basket and went home, excited to tell her mom all about her surprise adventure.
