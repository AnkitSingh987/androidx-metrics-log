Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Bear

Tom and Lily liked to play in the garden. They had a big tree with a swing and a slide. They also had a lot of flowers and bugs to look at. One day, they saw a big box under the tree. It had a bow and a tag that said "For Tom and Lily". They ran to the box and opened it. Inside, they found a big teddy bear. It was soft and brown and had a red scarf. They hugged the teddy bear and thanked their mom and dad for the gift. They decided to take the teddy bear to the swing and the slide. They wrapped the scarf around the teddy bear's neck and put it on the swing. They pushed the swing and the teddy bear flew in the air. They laughed and clapped. Then, they took the teddy bear to the slide. They wrapped the scarf around the teddy bear's waist and put it on the slide. They slid down with the teddy bear and landed on the grass. They giggled and rolled. But then, they saw a big dog coming to the garden. It was black and hairy and had sharp teeth. It barked and growled and ran to the teddy bear. It grabbed the teddy bear by the scarf and shook it hard. It tore the teddy bear apart and threw it on the ground. It bit the teddy bear's head and ripped off its fur. It left the teddy bear bald and broken. Tom and Lily screamed and cried. They ran to their mom and dad and told them what happened. Their mom and dad were angry and sad. They scolded the dog and chased it away. They picked up the teddy bear and tried to fix it. But it was too late. The teddy bear was ruined. Tom and Lily were very sad. They loved the teddy bear and wanted to play with it. But now, they had no teddy bear. They had only a bald and broken toy. They wished they had never opened the box. They wished they had never seen the dog. They wished they had never wrapped the scarf around the teddy bear. They wished they had a happy ending. But they did not. They had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a zebra named Zara. Zara lived in a big field with her friends. They liked to play all day. One day, Zara found a cheap toy. The toy was small and red. She showed it to her friends. Her friends did not know what the toy was. They looked at it and said, "What is this?" Zara did not know either. She just gave a shrug. They all laughed and decided to play with the toy. They played with the toy for many days. They had lots of fun. They would throw it, kick it, and roll it around. Zara and her friends loved their new cheap toy. And even though they did not know what it was, they were happy to play with it together.

Once upon a time there was a tall painter. The painter loved to paint pictures for everyone. They would make beautiful works of art with paint, paper and lots of bright colors. One day, the painter went to a special party. At the party everyone was eating treats and having fun. But the tall painter couldn't remember anything from the party. The next morning, the painter woke up with a big feeling of sadness. They were trying to remember why they were so sad, but the memory was blurry. The painter eventually remembered that at the party, their friends had made fun of their tall painting. The painter was very hurt. But the painter was determined to carry on anyway. They found happiness in their painting and made lots of beautiful pictures. The painter never forgot the pain of that day, but they also remembered the joy of painting.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat was very hungry. It wanted to find some meat to eat. It looked for meat all day. The sun was hot and the cat was tired. The cat saw a perfect tree. It was big and had lots of shade. The cat sat under the tree. It felt good to rest. The cat let out a big yawn. It was sleepy from looking for meat. While the cat was resting, it saw a bird. The bird had some meat in its beak. The cat jumped up and chased the bird. The bird dropped the meat. The cat was very happy. It ate the meat and felt full. The cat went back to the tree and had a nice nap.
