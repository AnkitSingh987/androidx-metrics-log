Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Nosy Monkey and the Banana

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little monkey named Bobo. He loved to see what everyone was doing in the jungle. One day, he saw a bright yellow thing hanging from a tree. It was a banana! Bobo was very excited to see the banana. He climbed the tree and picked the banana. He wanted to show all his friends. So, he went to find his friend, the big elephant. The elephant was happy to see the banana too. In the end, Bobo shared the banana with his friend, the big elephant. They both enjoyed eating the yummy banana. From that day on, Bobo and the big elephant looked for more bananas together. They had lots of fun and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friend, a dog named Sam. One sunny day, Tim and Sam went to the park to play. They ran and jumped, having lots of fun. As they played, Tim saw a book on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Look, Sam! A book!" Sam wagged his tail and said, "Let's read it!" They sat down under a big tree and opened the book. As Tim read the book out loud, a bug flew into his nose. "Achoo!" Tim sneezed. Sam laughed and said, "Bless you, Tim!" They continued to read the book and had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a musician named Tom. Tom was a little selfish. He only liked to play his music for himself. One day, a sad girl named Jane came to Tom's house. "Please play music for me," Jane asked. "I am sad and want to hear happy music." "No!" said Tom. "I only play music for myself. Go away!" Jane started to cry. Her tears made Tom feel bad. He thought about how his music could make her happy. He decided to not be selfish anymore. "Okay, I will play music for you," Tom said. Jane stopped crying and smiled. Tom played happy music and they danced together. From that day on, Tom shared his music with everyone and was not selfish anymore.

One morning, a little cat went for a walk. The sun was up and the day was mild. The cat was happy to play outside. After a while, the cat got tired. It wanted to rest under a big tree. The tree was nice and cool. The cat lay down and closed its eyes. But, a mean dog came by. The dog saw the cat resting. It barked very loud and scared the cat. The cat ran away and never came back. The cat's day was not fun anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy man. He lived in a small house in the woods. He had a pet dog that he loved very much. One day, the hairy man needed wood for his fire. So, he took his axe and went into the woods. He saw a big tree and thought it would provide good wood for his fire. He used his axe to cut the tree. After he cut the tree, the hairy man and his dog went back to their house. They were happy because they had wood for their fire. They sat by the warm fire and had a nice time.

One day, a purple cat went for a walk. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. The purple cat was happy. While walking, the purple cat saw a thief. The thief took a toy from a little boy. The little boy was sad and cried. The purple cat did not like this. The purple cat yelled, "Stop, thief!" The thief got scared and gave the toy back to the little boy. The little boy was happy again, and the purple cat went back to its walk.

Once there was a clown who loved to cover things. He was very good at it. He would cover his face with paint, and he would cover his car with funny stickers. The clown was very persistent. When he wanted to do something, he would keep trying and trying. One day, the clown saw a big box. He thought it would be fun to cover it with a pretty cloth. He put the cloth on the box and smiled. But then, he heard a small sound from inside the box. It was a little kitten. The kitten was scared because it was dark inside the box. The clown felt bad for the kitten. He took the cloth off the box and let the kitten out. The kitten was happy to see the light, but it was still scared of the clown. The clown tried to be nice to the kitten, but the kitten didn't like him. The clown was sad. He wished he had never covered the box.
