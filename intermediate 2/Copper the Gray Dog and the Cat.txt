Category: Intermediate
Title: Copper the Gray Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big gray dog named Copper. He loved to play and run all day. One day, while playing in the park, Copper saw a small cat stuck in a tree. Copper wanted to help the cat, so he thought of a plan. He knew he could sneeze very hard. He decided to sneeze at the tree to make the cat fall onto the soft grass. Copper took a deep breath and sneezed with all his might. The cat didn't fall from the tree, but something unexpected happened. The sneeze was so strong that it blew away all the leaves from the tree. Now, the cat could see the way down and climbed down by itself. The cat was happy and thanked Copper. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little chick named Charlie found a big egg in the yard. He was very careful when he touched it. He did not want to break the egg. Charlie's friend, Lucy the bunny, hopped over. She saw the egg and said, "Wow, that's a big egg! Let's encourage it to hatch!" Together, they sat by the egg and talked to it. "Come out, little one! We want to meet you!" said Charlie. "We will be your friends!" said Lucy. After a while, the egg started to crack. Charlie and Lucy were so excited! A baby bird came out of the egg. They were so happy to meet their new friend. They all played together in the yard, and Charlie and Lucy were always careful with the baby bird.

Once upon a time, there was a big group of animals. They lived in the forest. The group had a big, strong lion, a tall, impressive giraffe, and a small, quick rabbit. One day, the lion said, "I want to be the boss of the group." The other animals did not like this. They did not want the lion to tell them what to do. But the lion was very big and strong, so they were afraid to say no. The rabbit had an idea. He said to the lion, "If you can catch me, you can be the boss." The lion laughed and thought it would be easy. But the rabbit was very fast and smart. The lion could not catch him, no matter how hard he tried. In the end, the lion said, "Okay, rabbit, you win. I will not be the boss." The group of animals was happy. They all played together and had fun. And they learned that being a good friend is more important than being the boss.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot. The pilot liked to move his big plane up and down in the sky. He had a lot of fun flying the plane every day. One day, the pilot saw a big bird in the sky. The bird was very close to the plane. The pilot was frightened. He did not want the bird to hit the plane. The pilot moved the plane up and down to get away from the bird. The bird flew away and the pilot was safe. The pilot was happy and not frightened anymore. He flew his plane back home and told everyone about the big bird.

One day, a cat named Tom went to play with his friends. He felt dizzy because he played for a long time. His mom told him to bring lotion to help him feel better. Tom put the lotion in his bag and went to find his friends. Tom found his friends, a dog and a bird, in the park. They were playing with a ball. Tom told them about the lotion his mom gave him. They all wanted to try it. So, Tom opened the bag and took out the lotion. But, the lotion was magic! When they put it on, they all changed. The cat turned into a dog, the dog turned into a bird, and the bird turned into a cat. They all laughed and played together, happy with their new selves. The magic lotion made their day even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny seed. The seed was not happy. It was in a field with lots of wheat. The seed wanted to be big and tall like the wheat. It felt very small and dead. One day, a happy bird came to the field. The bird saw the tiny seed and wanted to help. The bird said, "I will encourage you to grow big and tall like the wheat." The seed felt happy and started to try to grow. The bird sang a song every day for the seed. The seed listened and felt strong. It grew and grew. One day, the seed was big and tall like the wheat. The seed was now alive and happy, just like the bird said it would be.
