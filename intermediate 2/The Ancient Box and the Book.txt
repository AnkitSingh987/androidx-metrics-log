Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ancient Box and the Book

Once upon a time, there was an ancient box. A boy found it in his house. The box was big and old. The boy was very happy. He wanted to open the box and see what was inside. The boy asked his mom to let him open the box. Mom said, "No, it is not for you." But the boy did not listen. He opened the box when mom was not looking. Inside the box, there was a book. The book had a language that the boy did not know. The boy tried to read the book. But he could not understand the language. The language was very old. The boy was sad. He put the book back in the box. The box was not fun. The boy went to bed with a sad face. The end.

Mama and Papa were in the kitchen baking cookies. Papa was busy mixing together the ingredients, and Mama was carefully stirring a bowl of melted butter. Suddenly, Mama stopped and said, "Oh, no. I forgot the sugar!" Papa looked troubled. He said, "That's a problem. We can't make our cookies without sugar." But Mama had an idea. She said, "Let's go to my grandpa's house. I bet he's got some sugar there." So they drove off in the car. When they arrived, they heard a gentle, beautiful glow coming from inside. They opened the door and saw Grandpa holding a big bag of sugar. He said, "I was just thinking that you were coming and I had the feeling you might need this." Mama and Papa were so happy, and the cookies baked up perfectly! The kitchen filled with the delicious smell of fresh-baked cookies. Everyone was happy, and the trouble was gone.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They wanted to spend time outside and play. Tim was an obedient boy and always listened to his mom. In the park, there were many trees with pretty leaves. Tim liked to pick up the leaves and look at them. While they were playing, Tim found a big green leaf. He showed it to his mom and she said it was very nice. Tim wanted to keep the leaf, so he put it in his pocket. After that, they played some more and then went home. At home, Tim took the leaf out of his pocket. But something unexpected happened! The leaf started to move and grow. It turned into a friendly bug. The bug said "thank you" to Tim for taking him home. Tim was very surprised, but he was happy to have a new friend. They played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl went for a walk. She saw her old neighbor, Mr. Fox, in his yard. Mr. Fox was very nice and always smiled at her. The little girl liked Mr. Fox. As the little girl walked, she saw a banana on the ground. She did not want to slip, so she picked it up. She thought Mr. Fox might like the banana, so she went to give it to him. When the little girl gave the banana to Mr. Fox, he started to dance! It was a big surprise. The little girl laughed and clapped her hands. She did not know Mr. Fox could dance so well. From that day on, they were good friends and danced together all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot liked to chew on things. One day, he found a knife in the kitchen. Spot did not know that the knife was sharp and could hurt him. Spot started to chew on the knife. His mom saw him and got scared. She knew that the knife was not safe for Spot to chew on. She wanted to take the knife away from him, but she did not want him to get hurt. So, she called Spot's dad. He came into the kitchen and saw Spot with the knife. He knew just what to do. He grabbed a big, soft toy and gave it to Spot. Spot saw the toy and let go of the knife to chew on the toy instead. Spot's mom and dad were happy that he was safe, and Spot was happy to have a new toy to chew on.
