Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Animal and the Horn

Once upon a time, there was a big animal with a long horn. The horn was not nice. It was terrible. The animal was sad because it wanted a pretty horn. One day, the animal met a small bug. The bug said, "I can help you. I can polish your horn." The animal was happy. The bug started to polish the horn. It worked very hard. After some time, the horn was shiny and pretty. The animal was not sad anymore. It was very happy. The bug and the animal became good friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. He loved to run and play all day. Spot was very happy. One day, Spot found a silver ball in the park. He was so excited! He wanted to have the ball and play with it all the time. So, Spot took the ball home with him. Spot and the silver ball were the best of friends. They played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

---

Once upon a time, there was a big, incredible tree. In the tree, there was a little house. In this house, there lived a small bird named Tim. Tim was a very happy bird. He liked to sing and play with his friends all day long. One day, Tim was getting ready to close his door to go play outside. But, he saw something very strange. There was a big box near his house! Tim had never seen a box like this before. It had a note on it that said "order for Tim the bird". Tim was very excited and opened the box. Inside, he found a big, shiny key. Tim looked around and saw a little door at the bottom of the tree. He had never seen this door before. He used the key to open the door. When the door opened, Tim found a room full of toys and treats! It was a secret playroom just for him. Tim was so happy and could not believe his luck. From that day on, Tim and his friends had the most fun in the secret playroom. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. The cat and the dog were very happy. Suddenly, a fine bowl appeared on the ground. The cat said, "Look, a bowl! Let's see what's inside." The dog wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's look!" The cat and the dog looked inside the bowl. They saw some yummy food. The cat said, "Let's eat!" The dog agreed, and they both ate the food. After they finished, the cat and the dog were very full and happy. They played in the yard some more and had a great day.

---

One day, a boy named Tim found a needle. It was shiny and small. He wanted to tie it to a string. He thought it would be fun to play with. Tim asked his mom for some string. She gave him a long, blue string. Tim tied the needle to the string. He started to play with it. He swung it around and around. Tim was having so much fun. He didn't want to share with his little sister, Lily. Tim was being selfish. Lily was sad. She wanted to play with the needle and string too. Tim saw Lily's tears and felt bad. He decided to share with her. They played with the needle and string together. They were both happy and had lots of fun.

---

One day, a boy named Tim had a red wagon. He loved his wagon very much. Tim would play with his wagon every day. He would put toys in it and send it down the hill. The wagon went fast and made Tim happy. One cold day, Tim went outside to play with his wagon. But, his friend Sam was there. Sam said, "I want to play with the wagon too!" Tim did not like this. He wanted to play with his wagon alone. This made Tim sad. Tim thought for a moment. Then he said, "We can take turns with the wagon. You can send it down the hill, and then I can send it down the hill." Sam liked this idea. They took turns and played with the wagon all day. Tim and Sam were happy and became good friends.
