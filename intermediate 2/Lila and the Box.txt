Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and the Box

Lila loves jazz. She likes to listen to the music with her dad. They have a big box that plays jazz. Lila calls it the jazz box. Sometimes, Lila asks her dad to play jazz for her. She says, "Dad, can we listen to jazz, please?" She requests jazz. One day, Lila and her dad go to the park. They see a band playing jazz. They have drums, a guitar, a saxophone, and a trumpet. Lila is very happy. She wants to dance to the jazz. She pulls her dad's hand and says, "Dad, can we dance, please?" She requests to dance. Her dad smiles and says, "Sure, Lila, let's dance." They join the other people who are dancing to the jazz. Lila feels the beat and the rhythm. She moves her feet and her arms. She spins and jumps. She has fun. She is alert to the jazz. The band plays many songs. Lila and her dad dance to all of them. They clap and cheer for the band. The band says, "Thank you, thank you, you are a great audience." They request applause. Lila and her dad are tired but happy. They hug and say, "We love jazz, we love dancing, we love each other." They request a hug. They go home and listen to more jazz on the jazz box. They fall asleep to the sound of jazz. They dream of jazz.

One day, a little girl named Lily and her dog, Max, went for a walk. The sun was shining and the sky was clear. They walked down the street and saw a tiny bird on the ground. The bird looked lost. Lily said, "Hi little bird, are you lost? Max and I can help you find your way home." The bird chirped and hopped onto Lily's hand. Lily put the bird in her pocket to keep it safe. Lily and Max followed the bird's chirps to find its home. They walked past big trees and colorful flowers. The bird chirped louder when they got closer to its home. Finally, they found the bird's family in a small nest. The bird's family was so happy to see their lost little one. They chirped and sang a happy song. Lily said, "Bye little bird, have fun with your family." Lily and Max walked back home, feeling happy that they helped the little bird.

Once upon a time there was a bald man. He was very special because he knew how to create things. Every day, he would create magical things from his workshop. One day, he had an idea for a special project. He wanted to create a pearl from an oyster. He asked all his friends for help to find an oyster, but no one knew where to get one. But one day, something special happened. A little girl came to the man's door and handed him an oyster. When the man opened the oyster, he found a beautiful pearl inside! The man was so happy that he could finally create his pearl. He was so proud of his work. The little girl was so happy that she had helped the man create his pearl! They both celebrated the man's success. From then on, the bald man was known for being able to create beautiful and magical things. He created so many wonderful things, but the pearl he made from the oyster was always his favorite.

One day, a boy named Tim and his mom went for a walk. Tim had a map that he thought was useful. He wanted to show his mom that he could read the map. They walked and walked until they got to a big tree. "Mom, the map says we have to go this way," Tim said, pointing to the path on the left. "Are you sure, Tim?" his mom asked. Tim insisted, "Yes, I am sure. Let's go this way." They walked down the path and saw a little girl crying. She was lost. Tim and his mom asked if she wanted help. The little girl said, "Yes, please. I don't know how to go home." Tim looked at his map and saw the way to the girl's house. They walked together, and soon they were at her house. The little girl was happy and thanked Tim and his mom. Tim's mom was proud of him for using the map to help someone. The moral of the story is that being helpful to others is a good thing to do.
