Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very anxious about her birthday party. She wanted everything to be perfect. She had a big cup filled with her favorite drink, apple juice. She also had a pretty cake with her name on it. When her friends came to the party, Lily said, "Let's part the cake and share it with everyone!" Her friends were excited and gathered around the table. They all wanted a piece of the yummy cake. Lily's mom helped her part the cake into small pieces. Each friend got a piece of cake and a cup of apple juice. They all said, "Thank you, Lily!" They had a great time at the party, and Lily was not anxious anymore.

Tom is a dog. He likes to play and run and bark. He has an owner who loves him. His owner gives him food and water and hugs. His owner also gives him a bath when he is smelly. One day, Tom sees a big cake on the table. It looks yummy and sweet. He wants to eat it. He thinks his owner will not mind. He jumps on a chair and then on the table. He tries to reach the cake with his mouth. But his owner sees him. He is angry and sad. He says, "No, Tom! Bad dog! That cake is for my birthday. You are not supposed to eat it. You are very naughty and smelly. Go to your bed and stay there." Tom feels sorry and ashamed. He knows he did a bad thing. He jumps off the table and goes to his bed. He does not want to play or run or bark. He wants to say sorry to his owner. After a while, his owner comes to him. He is not angry anymore. He says, "Tom, I forgive you. You are still my dog and I love you. But you have to learn to obey and share. The cake is not only for me, but for you and our friends too. If you wait, you will get a slice. And you will also get a bath, because you are very smelly." Tom wags his tail and licks his owner's face. He is happy and grateful. He learns his lesson. He decides to be a good dog and not to steal food again. He also decides to let his owner wash him, even if he does not like it. He knows it is good for him and his owner. The moral of the story is: Do not be greedy and selfish. Respect what belongs to others. Say sorry when you make a mistake. And be clean and tidy.

Once upon a time, there was a woman named Sue. Sue was very cool. She had a big dog named Spot. Sue and Spot liked to play in the park. One sunny day, Sue and Spot were playing with a ball. Spot would run and catch the ball, then bring it back to Sue. They played for a long time, and they were both very tired. Sue yawned and said, "I am sleepy, Spot." Spot yawned too and said, "Me too, Sue. Let's go home and rest." So, they walked back to their small house and took a long nap. They both had happy dreams about playing in the park. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big puddle on the ground. Tim jumped in the puddle and got all wet. He laughed and said, "I love puddles!" Tim walked more and saw a broken toy on the ground. He picked it up and said, "I will fix this toy when I get home." He put the toy in his pocket and kept walking. The next day, Tim woke up and saw that it was very cold outside. He looked out the window and saw the big puddle from yesterday. But now, the puddle was all freeze! Tim put on his coat and ran outside. He slid on the frozen puddle and laughed. He said, "I love when puddles freeze!" Then, he remembered the broken toy in his pocket. Tim went back inside to fix the toy and play with it all day.
