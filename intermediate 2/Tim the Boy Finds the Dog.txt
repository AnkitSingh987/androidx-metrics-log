Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Dog

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a cold day, so Tim wore his warm coat. As they walked, Tim saw a big dog. The dog was running fast and playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with the dog, but he did not know if the dog was friendly. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the dog?" His mom said, "Let's pause and watch the dog for a moment. We don't know if it's safe." As they paused, a girl came running to the dog. She was the dog's owner. The girl said, "My dog is very nice, but he is ignorant. He does not know how to play with little kids." Tim was sad, but he understood. He waved goodbye to the dog and the girl, and continued to walk in the park with his mom.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the pool with her mom. Sue loved to swim and play in the water. She saw a big ball in the pool and wanted to grab it. Sue asked her mom, "Can I grab the ball, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be nice to the other kids." So Sue went to the pool and grabbed the ball. She played with it, but she didn't share with the other kids. The other kids were miserable because they wanted to play too. Then, a big wind came and blew the ball away from Sue. She was sad and cried. A kind boy found the ball and gave it back to Sue. He said, "Let's all play together!" Sue learned that sharing is good and playing together is more fun. From that day on, Sue shared her toys and made many friends at the pool.

Mumma and Jessie were walking through the park together. Jessie asked, "What's that bright, red thing over there, Mumma?" Mumma smiled and said, "That's called a poppy, sweetheart. Poppies are very special. We see them often at this time of year. Look, there's lots of them." Jessie squealed in delight as they walked through the field of poppies. Red petals were everywhere and it felt like they had stepped into a world of magic. The next day, Mumma took Jessie to the beach. When they got to the beach, they saw some ordinary people holding flags. Mumma turned to Jessie and asked if she knew what was happening. Jessie shook her head. "It's a parade," Mumma said. "Everyone is waving flags to celebrate something important." Jessie watched in awe as the people waved their flags. Suddenly, a sea of red formed in her vision as everyone waved theirpoppy flags. Mumma had taught her about poppies and she understood what was happening that day. They were celebrating the heroes who gave their lives in service of their country. Jessie happily waved her poppy flag with the others in the parade. She felt proud to be a part of the celebration.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved very much. It needed a battery to go fast. One day, the car stopped moving. Tim was sad. He knew he needed to care for his car and find a new battery. Tim asked his mom for help. His mom was busy, but Tim was persistent. He kept asking until his mom said yes. They went to the store and got a new battery. Tim was so happy, he thought his car would go fast again. When they got home, Tim put the new battery in the car. But the car still didn't move. Tim was confused. His mom looked at the car and found a small rock stuck in the wheel. She removed the rock, and the car started moving fast again. Tim learned that sometimes, the problem might not be what you think it is. The moral of the story is to always check for other problems and not to give up.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a weak, small bird on the ground. The bird could not fly because its wing was hurt. Mia wanted to help the bird, so she picked it up very carefully. She held the bird close to her waist so it would feel safe. Mia walked to her mom and showed her the weak bird. Her mom said they should take it home and help it feel better. Mia was happy she could help the bird. They went home and put the bird in a warm, soft bed. After a few days, the bird started to feel better. Its wing was not hurt anymore. Mia knew it was time to let the bird go back to its home in the sky. She picked up the bird and took it outside. She said goodbye to the bird and let it fly away. Mia felt happy that she could help the weak bird get better.
