Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna the Girl and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She loved to wear her red hat. One cold day, Anna felt the urge to go outside and play. But her mom told her to put on her warm coat and boots first. Anna didn't want to listen and ran outside without them. As Anna played outside, she started to feel very cold. Her hands and feet were freezing. She thought about her mom's words and felt sad for not listening. Anna decided to go back inside and wear her warm coat and boots. With her warm coat and boots on, Anna went outside again. This time, she felt warm and happy. She played all day and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents, as they know what is best for you.

Once upon a time, a big cat and a little dog lived in a house. They did not like each other. Every day, they would have a fight. The cat would lay on the dog's bed, and the dog would get mad. One day, the dog was very patient. He waited and waited for the cat to leave his bed. But the cat did not go away. The dog got very, very mad. He wanted to have a big fight with the cat. The fight was loud and bad. The dog and the cat made a big mess in the house. They broke things and hurt each other. In the end, the dog and the cat were both very sad. They had no bed to lay on and no one to play with.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a cave and wanted to join his friends inside. As he walked, he saw a fine, big rock near the cave. He thought it was fun to play with. Tim called out to his friends, "Hey, come play with this fine rock!" His friends came out of the cave and they all started to play. They had a lot of fun with the rock, laughing and playing together. But then, something unexpected happened. The rock started to move! It was not a rock at all, but a big, angry bear. The bear chased Tim and his friends away from the cave. They were very scared and sad. They could not play in the cave anymore because the bear stayed there.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved the color orange. He had an orange ball, an orange shirt, and even an orange cat. One day, Tim saw a big orange box outside his house. He felt a lot of wonder about what could be inside. Tim wanted to unpack the box, so he asked his mom for help. They opened the box together. Inside, Tim found a big orange toy. He was so happy and played with the toy all day long. But then, the orange toy broke. Tim was very sad. He tried to fix it, but he could not. The toy was broken forever. Tim learned that not everything that looks fun can last forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a careless boy who never looked after his things. One day, Tim lost his favorite vest. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not find it. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom told him to be more careful with his things. She said, "If you take care of your things, they will stay with you for a long time." Tim listened to his mom and decided to change. He started to put his toys away after playing and folded his clothes neatly. One day, Tim found his favorite vest under his bed. He was so happy! He put it on and went to show his mom. His mom was proud of him for being careful now. She gave him a big hug and everyone around them started to applaud. Tim learned that it is important to take care of his things.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to rush and play all day. One day, Max saw a bright light in the sky. He wanted to find out what it was. Max ran to his friend, Cat. "Cat, do you see the light?" Max asked. Cat looked up and said, "Yes, I see it too! Let's go find it." So, Max and Cat rushed together to find the light. They ran and ran until they got very tired. The light was still far away. Max and Cat couldn't reach the light. They were sad and had to go back home. The light stayed in the sky, but Max and Cat never found out what it was.
