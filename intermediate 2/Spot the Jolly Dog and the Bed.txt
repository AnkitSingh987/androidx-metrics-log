Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Jolly Dog and the Bed

Once upon a time, there was a jolly dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day long. He lived in a big house with a big yard. Spot was a very happy dog. One day, Spot noticed something new in his yard. It was a big, soft bed. Spot thought it was a luxury to have such a nice bed. He jumped on it and took a long nap. When he woke up, he felt very happy and rested. Spot spent his days playing and napping on his new bed. He was so jolly that all the other animals in the yard wanted to be his friend. They all played together and had a great time. And every night, Spot slept on his luxury bed, dreaming of all the fun he would have the next day.

Anna and Ben are at the park with Mom. They see a big fountain with water that goes up and down. Anna likes the fountain. She runs to it and puts her hand in the water. It is cold and wet. "Anna, be careful!" Mom says. "Don't get too close to the fountain. You might fall in." Anna nods. She knows Mom is right. She steps back from the fountain and looks at it. She sees a gray bird on the edge of the fountain. It is a pigeon. It drinks some water and then flies away. "Look, Ben!" Anna says to her brother. "A bird!" Ben looks at the fountain. He sees another pigeon. It is brown and white. It has a shiny green neck. It is pretty. "Can I feed the bird?" Ben asks Mom. Mom nods. She takes out some bread from her bag and gives a piece to Ben. Ben breaks the bread into small bits and throws them to the pigeon. The pigeon pecks at the bread and makes a cooing sound. "Thank you, Ben!" the pigeon seems to say. Ben smiles. He likes the pigeon. He likes the fountain. He likes the park. He is happy.

John was a young boy. He was very chubby and always looking for fun. One day, John decided to go on an adventure outside. He ran to the park, feeling excited and happy. When he arrived at the park, John noticed something shiny peeking out from the ground. He quickly ran over, and saw that it was a coin! John was delighted. He squatted down and used both hands to pick up the coin. He showed it to his mom who was standing nearby. "Wow, you discovered a coin!" exclaimed his mom. John smiled and held up the shiny coin, proudly. He was so happy and excited to have found it. From then on, he always kept his eyes open while exploring outside.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big orange store. The store had many things that people needed. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They were there to buy things they needed for their home. In the store, Lily saw a big box. It had the word "order" on it. Lily wanted to lift the box, but it was too heavy for her. She asked her mom for help. Her mom told her that it was not nice to touch other people's things. The box was someone else's order, and they should not touch it. Lily listened to her mom and did not touch the box. They found the things they needed and went home. Lily learned that it is important to respect other people's things and not to touch them without asking. And that is the story of the orange store and the big box with the word "order" on it.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named Tim. Tim loved to go to the market with his mom. They would buy food and toys. One sunny day, they went to the market together. At the market, Tim saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it. He picked it up and threw it high in the air. The ball went very far and hit a big pile of apples. The apples fell down and went everywhere. A nice lady at the market saw what happened. She was not mad. She started to laugh and clap. She said, "That was a good slap, Tim!" Tim was happy that the lady was not mad. Tim, his mom, and the nice lady picked up the apples and put them back. They all became good friends and went to the market together every week.
