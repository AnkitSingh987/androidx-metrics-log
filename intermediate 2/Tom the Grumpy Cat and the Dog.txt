Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Grumpy Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. He lived near a gym. Every day, he saw people go to the gym to play and have fun. One day, Tom wanted to play too. He went to the gym and tried to jump and run like the people. But he was still grumpy. Tom wanted to be happy like them. Tom had an idea. He wrote a letter to his friend, Sam the dog. He asked Sam to come play with him at the gym. Tom put the letter in the mailbox to mail it. Soon, Sam came to play with Tom. They had so much fun at the gym, and Tom was not grumpy anymore.

Lily was a little girl who liked to speak a lot. She liked to speak to her mom, her dad, her dog, and her toys. She also liked to speak to the mailman, who came every day with a big bag of mail. One day, Lily saw the mailman coming down the street. She ran to the door and opened it. She wanted to speak to him and see what mail he had. "Hello, mailman!" Lily said. "Do you have mail for me?" The mailman smiled and looked in his bag. He took out a letter and a package. He gave the letter to Lily and said, "This is for you, Lily. It's from your grandma. She loves you very much." Lily was happy. She liked to get mail from her grandma. She thanked the mailman and opened the letter. It had a picture of her grandma and a sticker of a flower. Lily put the sticker on her shirt and smiled. "What about the package?" Lily asked. "Who is it for?" The mailman looked at the package and frowned. He said, "This is for your dad, Lily. But it's terrible. It's from the bad people who want his money. They say if he doesn't give them money, they will hurt him." Lily was scared. She didn't like the bad people. She didn't want them to hurt her dad. She said, "No, mailman! You can't give that package to my dad! It's terrible! You have to take it back!" The mailman shook his head and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I can't do that. I have to deliver the mail. It's my job. But don't worry. Your dad is smart and brave. He will know what to do. He will call the police and they will catch the bad people." Lily didn't like the mailman's answer. She didn't want the terrible package in her house. She grabbed the package from the mailman's hand and threw it on the ground. She said, "No, no, no! Go away, mailman! Go away, terrible package! I don't want you here!" The mailman was surprised and angry. He said, "Hey, Lily! That's not nice! You can't do that! You have to respect the mail! You have to say sorry!" Lily didn't say sorry. She ran inside and slammed the door. She was mad at the mailman and the terrible package. She hugged her letter from her grandma and her dog. She said, "I don't like the mailman. I don't like the terrible package. I only like the mail from my grandma. She is nice. She doesn't want to hurt my dad."

Once upon a time, there was a prince who lived in a big castle. The prince had a rare toy that he loved very much. One day, he met another prince from a different castle. They played together, but soon they began to quarrel over the rare toy. "Why do you want my toy?" asked the first prince. "I have never seen a toy like this before," said the second prince. "Can we share it?" The first prince thought about it and then agreed to share the toy with his new friend. As they played together, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and took the rare toy in its beak. The two princes were surprised and looked at each other. They decided to work together to get the toy back. They became good friends and never quarreled again.

Lily was a little girl who loved to draw. She liked to use many colors, but her favorite was yellow. She drew yellow suns, yellow flowers, yellow stars, and yellow birds. She wanted to make her drawings look real and pretty. But Lily had a problem. She could not see very well. She had to squint her eyes to see the lines and shapes on the paper. Sometimes she mixed up the colors or went outside the edges. She felt sad and frustrated. One day, her mom took her to the eye doctor. The eye doctor looked at her eyes and said, "Lily, you need glasses. They will help you see better. They will improve your sight." He showed her some glasses with different frames. Lily saw one pair that was yellow. She liked them very much. She put them on and looked around. Everything was clearer and brighter. She smiled. "Mom, can I have these glasses?" she asked. "Of course, sweetie. They look lovely on you. And they will help you with your drawings," her mom said. Lily was very happy. She thanked the eye doctor and hugged her mom. She could not wait to go home and draw with her new glasses. She knew her drawings would be more real and pretty. She loved her yellow glasses. They improved her sight.
