Category: Intermediate
Title: The Car and the Roof

One day, a big car went down the road. The car was high up and had big wheels. The car was fast and needed fuel. The car saw a store with fuel and stopped. The car said, "Give me fuel, please!" The store said, "Okay, but first you must help me. My roof is broken and I need help to fix it." The car was strong and wanted to help the store. The car helped the store fix the roof. The store was happy and gave the car fuel. The car said, "Thank you!" The car drove away fast and happy, and the store was happy too.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to play and hop around all day. One day, Remy found a big, soft pillow under a tree. He thought it would be perfect for a nap. Remy took the pillow and squeezed it tight. He was so excited for his nap later. He played with his friends all morning, but he couldn't stop thinking about the big, soft pillow. He knew it would make his nap the best nap ever. Finally, it was time for Remy's nap. He went back to the big, soft pillow under the tree. He squeezed it one more time and then laid down. Remy had the best nap of his life on the big, soft pillow. When he woke up, he felt happy and full of energy. Remy knew that the big, soft pillow had given him the best nap ever, just like he thought it would.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a mirror in her room. Lucy liked to look at herself in the mirror and make funny faces. One day, Lucy saw a tight dress in the store. She wanted to try it on. Her mom said, "No, it's too tight." But Lucy could not resist. She tried on the tight dress and it was hard to put on. Lucy went home and looked in the mirror. She saw that the tight dress was not comfortable. She took it off and put on her favorite loose dress. Lucy was happy and played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly high in the sky. It was sad because it wanted to play with its friends. The bird sat in silence, hoping its wing would heal soon. One sunny day, a kind girl found the bird. She wanted to help the bird feel better. The girl took the bird home and put a soft bandage on its wing. Every day, the girl fed the bird and gave it water. After some days, the bird's wing began to heal. The bird was very happy. It could fly high in the sky again. The bird thanked the girl for her help. They played together in the warm sun. The bird was no longer sad and the silence was gone. They both had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a strong girl named Sally. She loved to play with her cardboard toys. One day, she made a big cardboard house. Sally wanted to tell her friend, Tim, about her new cardboard house. She thought of a fun idea. She would mail him a letter. So, she drew a picture of her house on a piece of paper. Sally put the letter in an envelope and went to the mailbox. She put the letter inside and closed the mailbox door. She was happy that she could tell Tim about her house. Soon, Tim got the letter in the mail. He was so happy to see Sally's cardboard house. He wanted to play with her. So, he went to her house and they played together all day. Sally and Tim had a lot of fun in the cardboard house. They were strong friends who loved to play together. And they always mailed each other letters about their fun days.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird who loved to soar high in the sky. He was a very careless bird and always forgot where his home was. One day, while he was flying, he saw a kitchen through a window. The bird was hungry, so he decided to go inside. In the kitchen, the bird found a big plate of yummy food. He ate and ate until his tummy was full. Suddenly, the door opened, and a little girl walked in. The bird was scared, but the little girl was not. She smiled and said, "Hello, bird. I see you found my food." The bird felt bad for eating the little girl's food. But the girl just laughed and said, "It's okay, bird. I have a surprise for you." She opened a door, and the bird saw a room full of other birds just like him. The little girl said, "This is a home for birds who get lost. You can stay here and make friends." The careless bird was happy to have a new home and friends who loved to soar in the sky just like him.
