Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Proud Bird and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a proud bird named Tim. Tim had many friends in the forest. He liked to fly high in the sky and play with them. One day, Tim saw a big fire in the forest. The fire was getting bigger and bigger. Tim knew he had to help his friends. Tim flew close to the fire. He saw a lot of ash on the ground. The ash was from the trees that the fire burned. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help his friends. He thought of a plan to stop the fire from increasing. Tim called all his bird friends. They all worked together to bring water from the river. They dropped the water on the fire. After some time, the fire stopped increasing. The forest was safe again. Tim and his friends were very proud of themselves. They saved the forest and all the animals living there.

Once upon a time, there was a generous reindeer. This reindeer loved to give gifts to his friends. He would always find the best toys and treats to share. His friends were very happy when they got these gifts. One day, the reindeer heard about a big party. This party was going to excite everyone in the forest. All the animals were going to be there. The reindeer wanted to go and bring gifts for all his friends. At the party, the reindeer gave each friend a gift. They all opened their gifts and were very excited. They played with their new toys and ate the yummy treats. The reindeer was happy to see his friends so excited. And they all had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, in a small room, there was a big bookshelf. The bookshelf had many books. One day, a little boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the bookshelf and was very happy. Tim loved to read books. Tim took a book from the bookshelf. He sat on the floor and spread the book open. The pages of the book were very dry. Tim's mom came into the room and saw him reading. She said, "Tim, you are a good boy for reading your book." Tim smiled and said, "Mom, I love to read. The bookshelf has so many books for me to read." Tim's mom was happy too. They spent the day reading books together. The end.

One day, a soft cat named Sam went to the beach. He saw a boat with a big sail. Sam wanted to go on the boat, but his friend Max, the dog, was there to warn him. "Sam, don't go on the boat!" Max said. "It is not safe for cats like you!" But Sam did not listen. He jumped on the boat and the wind blew the sail. The boat went far away from the beach. Max was sad and worried. Then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the water and pushed the boat back to the beach! Sam was safe and Max was happy. They both learned to listen to each other's warnings.

One day, a little red bird was very hungry. It saw a sandwich on the ground. The red bird was very happy to find it. It wanted to eat the sandwich right away. But a big cat saw the sandwich too. The cat wanted to eat the sandwich before the red bird could. So, the cat started to rush to the sandwich. The red bird saw the cat and knew it had to be fast. The red bird rushed faster and faster to the sandwich. It got there before the big cat. The red bird picked up the sandwich and flew away. The big cat was sad, but the red bird was happy to have its tasty sandwich.

Once there was an old man who could not walk so fast anymore. One day, he was walking along a path and saw a young boy. The boy was running and was going quite fast. The old man smiled and said, "Well done, young man! You are going very fast!" The boy stopped running and said, "Do you think so?" The old man replied, "Yes! I think you're doing very well. Keep it up! I encourage you to keep running and to see how fast you can go!" The boy smiled and kept running. He kept going faster and faster as he ran down the path. The old man watched and cheered him on, encouraging him to go as fast as he could. The boy smiled and kept running until he was out of sight. The old man smiled, happy to see someone else enjoying the feeling of speed.
