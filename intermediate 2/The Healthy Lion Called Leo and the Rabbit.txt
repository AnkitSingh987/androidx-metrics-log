Category: Intermediate
Title: The Healthy Lion Called Leo and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a healthy lion called Leo. He had a big brain and was always very clever. He roamed around the jungle looking for food, roaring very loud. One day, he spotted a little rabbit that he wanted to eat. He started chasing the rabbit, roaring at it. The rabbit was scared and started shouting: “Please don't eat me! I'm too small!” Leo stopped and said: “Don't worry! I'm healthy and want to stay that way. I don't eat rabbits, I just like the roar.” The rabbit was so happy that he hopped away into the jungle, while Leo kept roaring!

Once upon a time, there was a big box. It was a mystery box. No one knew what was inside. The box was very heavy. People wanted to know what could weigh so much. One day, a small boy found the box. He tried to open it, but it was broken. The boy was sad. He wanted to solve the mystery. He asked his friends for help. They all tried to fix the broken box. Finally, the box opened! Inside, there were many toys. The toys were heavy, and that's why the box weighed so much. The mystery was solved. The boy and his friends were very happy. They played with the toys all day long.

Ben and Lily liked to play in the big room with many toys. They saw a cord hanging from the ceiling. It looked like a fun thing to climb. "Let's climb the cord and see what is up there!" Ben said. "OK, but be careful. It might be dangerous," Lily said. They took turns to climb the cord. Ben went first. He climbed and climbed until he reached the top. He saw a big light bulb that made the room bright. "Wow, look at this!" he shouted. Lily wanted to see too. She climbed after Ben. She was not as good at climbing as Ben. She got tired and slipped. She fell down and hit her head on the floor. "Ow, ow, ow!" she cried. Ben heard Lily cry. He looked down and saw her lying on the floor. He got scared and tried to climb down. But he was too high and the cord was too thin. He lost his grip and fell too. He landed on top of Lily. "Oof, oof, oof!" he groaned. The cord broke and the light bulb fell. It smashed on the floor and made a loud noise. The room became dark. Mom and Dad heard the noise and ran to the big room. They saw Ben and Lily on the floor, covered in blood and glass. They were not moving. Mom and Dad screamed and cried. They called for help. But it was too late. Ben and Lily were gone.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy. He was in a hospital because he was sick. He had lots of friends who came to visit him. They brought him lots of toys and funny stories. The boy tried to be strong and not cry, but some days were harder than others. But the boy remained brave and determined. His friends and family came to the hospital to cheer him up. They sang songs and danced with him. The boy was so happy to have them there. The boy stayed in the hospital, but everyday he became more and more strong. Finally, after a few weeks, the doctor said he was well enough to go home. The boy was so happy that he was finally strong enough to go home. He said goodbye to his friends and family and went home to his parents. They were so proud of him for being so strong throughout his time in the hospital.

Once upon a time, there was a lawyer who lived in a mysterious forest. One day, she felt like she needed to go out and act. So she put on her cape, grabbed her briefcase and went out. When she arrived in a nearby clearing, she encountered two kids who were playing with a ball. The lawyer asked them "Do you want to play?" The kids were very excited, so they started playing with the lawyer. They had lots of fun and the lawyer even showed them some neat tricks. After some time, the lawyer said goodbye and started to walk out of the clearing. As she was about to leave, one of the kids asked her "Are you a mysterious lawyer?" The lawyer smiled and said "Yes, I am a mysterious lawyer". With that, she gave the kids a final wave and went on her way.
