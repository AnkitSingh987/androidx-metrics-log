Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fat Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat. The cat liked to sit in the sun and felt very happy there. One day, the Fat Cat saw a jar filled with tasty treats. The Cat approached the jar eagerly and tried to take the treats, but the jar was too high and too heavy to reach. The Fat Cat sat back and thought about how it could get to the treats. Suddenly, an idea popped into its head! The Cat took a long deep breath, and then jumped as high as it could. With one giant leap, the Fat Cat's paw was able to reach the jar and knock it down! The Fat Cat sat back and happily enjoyed its treats.

Once upon a time there was a humble pet named Bob. Bob loved to play in the garden and often spent sunny days outside exploring the world. Everyone in the neighbourhood knew Bob because he was so friendly and outgoing. But even though Bob belonged to the neighbourhood, he felt lonely and wished for a family to belong to. One day, a 3-year-old child named Abigail saw Bob playing in the garden and decided to adopt him as her own pet. "Would you like to come and live with me?" Abigail asked him. Bob was overjoyed and leaped with joy. He knew he now belonged to someone who would love and care for him. Abigail and Bob shared many happy days playing together and Bob no longer felt lonely. Abigail was so proud of her humble pet and he was a happy addition to the family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Bob. Bob was a very eager boat who loved to sail on the sea. One sunny day, Bob met a big boat named Betty. "Hi, Betty! Let's go sail together!" said Bob. Betty smiled and said, "Okay, Bob, let's go!" Bob and Betty sailed together all day long. They had so much fun, but soon Bob started to feel tired. "Betty, I need to rest now," said Bob. Betty nodded and said, "Let's go to the port and rest together." So, Bob and Betty went to the port and rested side by side. They talked and laughed until the sun went down. The next day, they were ready to sail again. The moral of the story is that it's important to take time to rest and enjoy the company of friends.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and find worms and stones. They had a big bucket and a small shovel each. They were having fun. Then Tom had an idea. He said to Lily, "Let's bury your head in the sand. It will be funny." Lily was not sure. She said, "Will it hurt? Will I be able to breathe?" Tom said, "No, it will not hurt. You can breathe. I will leave a hole for your nose. Trust me. It will be fun." Lily agreed. She lay down on the ground and closed her eyes. Tom started to shovel sand over her head. He made a big pile. He left a hole for her nose. He said, "There. You are buried. How do you feel?" Lily said, "I feel weird. I can't see anything. I can hear you, but it sounds muffled. I don't like it." Tom said, "Don't worry. I will dig you out soon. Just stay still." He went to get his bucket. He wanted to put some water on the sand to make it harder. He thought it would be more funny. But then he saw a butterfly. It was yellow and black. It was very pretty. He forgot about Lily. He ran after the butterfly. He wanted to catch it. He left his bucket and shovel behind. Lily waited for Tom. She started to feel scared. She wanted to get out of the sand. She tried to move her head, but it was stuck. She tried to call Tom, but he did not hear her. She started to cry. She cried for a long time. She felt alone and cold and uncomfortable. She wished Tom would come back. She wished she never agreed to bury her head in the sand. She wished she could see the sun and the flowers and the birds. She wished she could play with Tom again. She wished she could go home.
