Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. She loved to paint pretty pictures with her colorful paints. One day, Lily decided to visit her friend, Tom, who lived nearby. When Lily got to Tom's house, she saw that he was sad. "Why are you sad, Tom?" she asked. Tom said, "I don't know how to paint pretty pictures like you, Lily." Lily wanted to help Tom feel better, so she had an idea. Lily said, "I will teach you how to paint, Tom!" Tom smiled, and they began to paint together. Lily was a compassionate friend, and she showed Tom how to make pretty pictures with his paints. Soon, Tom was happy and proud of his paintings. From that day on, Lily and Tom loved to paint together and visit each other often.

Once upon a time, in a rare land, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved ice. He would play with ice all day. One day, he saw a big hill of ice. He wanted to gain the top of the hill. Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "I like ice too!" They played together and became friends. Tim and Sue worked together to climb the ice hill. As they climbed, they talked and laughed. Tim said, "I love ice!" Sue said, "Me too!" They reached the top of the ice hill. They were so happy that they gained the top. They slid down the ice hill and played until the sun went down.

One day, a thin cat named Sam saw a big clock on the wall. He wanted to poke it. But his friend, a dog named Max, said, "No, Sam! Do not poke the clock!" Sam did not listen to Max. He jumped up to poke the clock. But when he poked it, the clock fell down. It broke into pieces. Sam was sad and scared. Max was not happy with Sam. He said, "I told you not to poke the clock!" But Sam said, "I am sorry, Max." They both worked together to fix the broken clock. And they learned to listen to each other.

One day, a little girl named Sue had a test at school. She was very scared because she did not study. Sue looked at the test sheet and saw many questions she did not know. She felt terrible. Sue's friend, Tim, studied a lot for the test. He knew all the answers on the sheet. Tim saw that Sue was sad and did not know what to do. Tim whispered to Sue, "Next time, study before the test." After school, Sue went home and told her mom about the test. Her mom said, "It is important to study and learn new things." Sue understood the lesson and promised to study next time. She knew that studying would make her feel better and not terrible during a test.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim felt lonely one day. He did not know what to do. He wanted to play with someone. Tim went to his mother and said, "Mom, I feel lonely. Can you play with me?" His mother looked at him and smiled. She said, "Tim, why don't you go outside and look for a friend to play with?" Tim went outside and looked around. He saw a little girl who looked lonely too. Tim walked up to her and said, "Hi, I am Tim. Do you want to play with me?" The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, I am Lisa. Let's play together!" From that day on, Tim and Lisa played together and were never lonely again. Tim learned that when you feel lonely, you can always look for a friend to share the fun. And that was the moral of the story: when you feel lonely, look for a friend and you won't feel lonely anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a toy. It was a very attractive toy. It was a toy that could grow. The toy was in a small box. One day, a little girl found the toy. She was so happy. She played with the toy all day. Then, something unexpected happened. The toy started to grow! It grew and grew until it was as big as the little girl. The little girl was surprised, but she still loved her toy. She played with it even more. They had so much fun. Then, one day, the toy stopped growing. It was small again. The little girl was sad, but she still loved her toy. They played together forever.
