Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Naughty Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a naughty dog named Max. Max loved to run and play all day long. One sunny day, Max went to the park to have fun. At the park, Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was playing with a big ball. Max wanted to play too. So, Max ran to Lily and said, "Can I play with you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" As they played, Max tried to kick the ball but he did not see a big rock in front of him. Max stumbled on the rock and fell down. The ball went flying far away. Max and Lily were sad because they lost the ball. But then, something unexpected happened. A kind bird saw the ball and brought it back to Max and Lily. Max and Lily were so happy! They thanked the bird and kept playing together. From that day on, Max was not naughty anymore and always looked where he was going.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toy cat, Tom. One day, Sue found a long, pretty thread. She thought it would be fun to play with the thread and Tom. Sue tied the thread to Tom's tail and started to run around. Tom followed her, and they both ran in circles. Sue felt dizzy from running so much. She sat down to rest. While Sue was resting, Tom saw a real cat. The real cat thought the thread was fun too! It started to play with the thread, and Tom followed. Sue saw them and was surprised! She didn't mind, though. Sue, Tom, and the real cat all played together with the thread and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big forest. A little squirrel lived in the tree. The squirrel had a friend, a small bird. They liked to play and talk together. One day, the bird said to the squirrel, "Let's mix nuts and seeds for our food today." So they did. They mixed the nuts and seeds and shared the food. It was yummy! As they ate, they saw other animals in the forest. Some animals were not sharing and were fighting. The squirrel and the bird felt sad. They knew it was better to share and stay together as friends. The tall tree was a happy home for them because they shared and cared for each other. The other animals learned from them and started to share too. And the forest became a happier place for everyone.

Mama took a black firework out of its box. She looked at it and said, "oh, this is a special one. It's going to be very loud." Mama put the firework in the corner of the room and said, "let's make sure we stay away from it, okay?" Jack nodded. He asked, "Will it go off soon?" Mama said, "Yes, it will. When we hear it, we'll all need to lock our hands together and close our eyes." Jack was excited as Mama lit the firework. He held her hands tight and closed his eyes. When the firework went off, it was loud and bright. Jack opened his eyes and saw a shower of sparks. They were all different colours, but the sparks were mostly black. "Wow, Mama," said Jack. "That was amazing!" Mama smiled and said, "Yes it was! Let's do it again next year."

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play and learn new things. One day, Kitty met a big dog named Max. Max said, "I can teach you how to play fetch." Kitty was excited and said, "Yes, please teach me!" They played fetch all day long. Max would throw the ball, and Kitty would run to catch it. Kitty was having so much fun and learned how to play fetch very well. The next day, Kitty wanted to surprise Max. So, she found a small ball and hid it behind her back. When Max came to play, Kitty said, "Guess what I have!" Max thought it was a big bone, but Kitty showed him the small ball. Max was so surprised and happy! They played fetch together again, and Kitty was proud that she could surprise her big friend Max.
