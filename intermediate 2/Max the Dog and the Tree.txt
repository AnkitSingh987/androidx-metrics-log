Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Tree

One day, a dog named Max went for a jog in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. Max was very happy. Max met a cat named Lily. She was sitting under the tree. Max said, "Hi, Lily! Do you want to jog with me?" Lily said, "No, thank you. I like to stay here and watch the birds." Max didn't know what birds were, but he kept jogging. He was ignorant about birds, but he didn't mind. He just wanted to have fun. Max jogged around the park and saw many new things. He saw a pond, some flowers, and a big rock. Max was tired, so he went back to the tree where Lily was. Max and Lily talked about their day. Max told Lily about the pond, the flowers, and the big rock. Lily told Max about the birds. They both had a good day, and they were happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his blocks. One day, Tim wanted to build a big tower with his blocks. He tried very hard to make it tall, but the blocks kept falling down. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw Tim was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to build a big tower, but it's too hard." Sue wanted to help Tim feel better. She had an idea to help him heal his sad heart. Sue said, "Let's build the tower together!" Tim and Sue worked together to build the big tower. They tried hard and did not give up. Soon, the tower was very tall, and Tim was happy. They learned that working together can help heal and make hard things easier.

Danny was walking in the park when he spotted a beautiful butterfly. He thought it was the most beautiful thing he had ever seen, so he decided to follow it. "Where are you going?", he asked the butterfly. Suddenly, a big gust of wind blew, and Danny lost his grip on the butterfly. It flew up into the sky and out of sight. Danny felt hurt, but he still kept looking for the butterfly. He wanted to find it again, so he went searching through the trees. Danny came across a hungry bird. It wasn't happy to see him, and it asked him a question. "Do you have any butter to share?", the bird said. Danny didn't have any butter, so he said no. The bird became angry and snatched Danny's shoe. Danny tried to get it back, but the bird flew away with it. Danny felt so sad and hurt. He had wanted to find the butterfly, but he had lost his shoe. He sighed and trudged away, never to find the butterfly again.

Lily was a tall girl who liked to ask many things. She always asked her mom and dad what, why, how and when. She wanted to know everything about the world. One night, Lily could not sleep. She heard a strange noise outside her window. She got out of her bed and walked to the window. She saw a big, dark shape in the garden. She was scared, but also curious. She opened the window and asked, "Who are you? What are you doing here?" The shape turned around and looked at her. It was a big, black bear. It had sharp teeth and claws. It growled and said, "I am hungry. I am looking for food. And you look tasty." Lily screamed and tried to close the window, but it was too late. The bear jumped and grabbed her with its paws. It dragged her out of the window and into the night. Lily's mom and dad heard her scream, but they could not help her. The bear was too fast and too strong. It took Lily away and ate her. Lily never asked anything again.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to play golf with his friends. One day, Spot wanted to invite his best friend, a cat named Whiskers, to play golf with him. "Whiskers, come play golf with me!" Spot said. Whiskers looked at the golf ball and said, "No, I don't like golf. I like to chase mice." This made Spot sad, and there was a problem. Spot really wanted to play with Whiskers. So, he thought of a fun idea. "What if we play a game where we hit the mice with the golf ball?" Spot asked. Whiskers thought it was a fun idea, and they played together. They had a great time, and the problem was solved. From that day, Spot and Whiskers played together and always found a way to have fun.
