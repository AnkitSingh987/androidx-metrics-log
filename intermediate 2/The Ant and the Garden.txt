Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ant and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there lived a little ant. The ant had many friends, but he felt a little stupid sometimes. One day, he met a big ladybug who wanted to be his friend. The ladybug said, "Hi, little ant! I want to help protect you from danger. Will you be my friend?" The ant was very happy and said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend!" Together, they played in the garden and had lots of fun. The ladybug showed the ant how to protect their food from the rain. The ant felt less stupid when he was with his new friend. They were the best of friends and lived happily in the big green garden.

Once upon a time, there was a normal car. The car liked to travel far. One day, the car met a big truck. The truck was very big and strong. The car said, "Hello, big truck! I like to travel far. Do you like to travel too?" The truck said, "Yes, I like to travel. But I need lots of gas to go far." The car and the truck became friends. They went to the gas place together. The car got a little gas. The truck got lots of gas. They were happy to travel together. The car and the truck traveled far and saw many things. They saw trees, houses, and animals. They had a lot of fun. And they were always happy to find more gas to keep going.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw something unusual in the sky. It was a small, bright light. He thought it might be a planet. Tim was very curious and wanted to know more about it. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, I see something unusual in the sky. Can you help me?" His mom looked at the sky and saw the bright light too. She knew that Tim was excited, so she requested a book about planets from the library. When they got the book, Tim and his mom sat down to read it together. They learned about many different planets. Tim liked the colorful pictures of the planets in the book. He pointed at the bright light in the sky and said, "I think that's the planet Mars!" His mom smiled and said, "You might be right, Tim. It's amazing that you found a planet all by yourself!" Tim felt very happy and proud. He loved learning about the unusual planet and couldn't wait to tell his friends at school.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big bug. The bug was green and slimy. Tim said, "Ew, that bug is disgusting!" His mom nodded and said, "Yes, it is." Tim and his mom walked away from the bug. They saw a car go zoom! Tim laughed and said, "Mom, look at the car go zoom!" His mom nodded and said, "Yes, it's very fast!" Then, something unexpected happened. The disgusting bug jumped on the car that went zoom! The bug said, "I want to go zoom too!" Tim and his mom were very surprised. They laughed and nodded. The bug went zoom with the car, and they all had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree in a forest. A little boy named Tim and a tiny bird named Blue lived near the tree. They were best friends and loved to play together. One day, Tim and Blue were playing near the tree when they saw a big sign. The sign said, "This is the office of Mr. Owl. Do not make noise." Tim and Blue were curious, so they went to the office. Inside, they saw Mr. Owl working on his papers. Tim said, "Hi, Mr. Owl!" Mr. Owl looked at them and said, "You made noise in my office! I must punish you." Tim and Blue were scared, but they said sorry to Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl thought for a moment and then said, "I forgive you. Just be quiet next time." Tim and Blue learned to be quiet near Mr. Owl's office. They played together in the forest and had many fun adventures. And they always remembered to be quiet when they were near the thick tree where Mr. Owl worked.
