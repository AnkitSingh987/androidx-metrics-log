Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Nosy Boy and the Mud

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a nosy little boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect things. One day, he decided to collect mud. Tim went outside and saw his friend, Sue. "Sue, let's collect mud together!" he said. Sue thought it was a fun idea, so they both started to dig and collect mud in their buckets. As they were collecting mud, a nosy dog named Spot came by. Spot saw the mud and jumped in it. Tim and Sue laughed as Spot played in the mud. "Silly Spot!" they said. After playing in the mud, they took their buckets full of mud and made a big mud castle. Tim, Sue, and Spot had a great day collecting mud and playing together. The end.

One day, a little red car named Zoomy loved to zoom around the town. He was very fast and liked to play with his friends. But sometimes, Zoomy zoomed too fast and made other cars feel scared. One day, Zoomy saw two big cars having a fight. They were yelling and bumping each other. Zoomy wanted to help, so he went to them and said, "Please don't fight. We can all play together and have fun!" The big cars looked at Zoomy and then at each other. They felt guilty for fighting and making Zoomy sad. They said, "You are right, Zoomy. We should not fight. Let's all play together and have fun." So, Zoomy and the big cars played together and had a great day. They raced and laughed, and no one was scared or had a fight. Zoomy learned that being kind and helping others was better than zooming too fast and scaring his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to run fast. One day, she won a beautiful trophy in a race. She was so happy. Lily ran home to show her mom the beautiful trophy. "Mom, look what I got!" she said. Her mom was so proud. "Good job, Lily!" her mom said. "Let's put the trophy on the shelf." They put the trophy on the shelf. It was shiny and pretty. Lily's friends came to see the trophy. "Wow, it's so beautiful!" they said. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you! I ran very fast to win it." Lily's trophy made her very happy. She looked at it every day and remembered how fast she ran. Lily knew she could do anything if she tried hard. And she promised to always do her best.

Ben and Mia are twins. They like to play together. They have many toys in their room. They are very organized. They put their toys in boxes and bags. They do not like mess. One day, their mom says, "We are going to visit grandma and grandpa. They have a big surprise for you. But first, you need to wrap some gifts for them. Can you do that?" Ben and Mia are excited. They love surprises. They also love to wrap gifts. They say, "Yes, mom, we can do that!" Mom gives them some paper, tape, and ribbons. She also gives them two books, one for grandma and one for grandpa. Ben and Mia go to their room. They close the door. They do not want mom to see their gifts. They work hard. They cut the paper. They tape the paper around the books. They tie the ribbons. They make bows. They are very careful. They do not tear the paper. They do not waste the tape. They do not tangle the ribbons. They finish their gifts. They are very proud. They look at their gifts. They are very pretty. They say, "Grandma and grandpa will love them!" They open the door. They see mom. She is smiling. She says, "You did a great job, Ben and Mia! You are very organized. You are very creative. You are very sweet. Now, let's go to the car. It is getting dark. We do not want to be late." Ben and Mia grab their gifts. They follow mom. They go to the car. They buckle their seat belts. They are ready to go. They are very happy. They wonder what the big surprise is. They hope it is something fun. They hope it is something they can play with. They hope it is something they can share.
