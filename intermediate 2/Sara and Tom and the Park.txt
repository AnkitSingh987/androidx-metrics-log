Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Park

Sara and Tom went to the park with Mom. They saw many things: trees, flowers, birds, swings, slides. They also saw a big statue of a man on a horse. The statue was gray and still. "Who is that?" Sara asked Mom. "That is a hero. He helped many people a long time ago. He was very brave and kind," Mom said. Sara and Tom looked at the statue. They wanted to be brave and kind too. "Can we say hello to him?" Tom asked. "Sure, you can. But be careful, don't climb on him. He is very old and fragile," Mom said. Sara and Tom walked up to the statue. They smiled and waved at him. "Hello, hero. Thank you for helping people. You are very good," Sara said. "Yes, thank you. We are grateful for you. You are very strong," Tom said. The statue did not move or talk. But Sara and Tom felt happy. They felt like they made a new friend. They hugged each other and Mom. They were a brave and kind family.

Mummy was very busy in the kitchen. She was cutting vegetables and mixing them. 3 year old Bobby was curious and asked mummy what she was doing. Mummy explained that she was making dinner. Bobby asked what she was going to make and mummy said she was making something special with the vegetables. Bobby then saw something orange in the kitchen. It looked like a cage. He asked mummy what it was and she explained it was a cage for chicken. It was a special kind of cage that allows the chickens to roam around outside. Bobby asked if the chickens were inside the cage. Mummy said no, but she had left something behind for the chickens. Bobby then asked why the cage was so hot. Mummy explained that it was hot because the sun had been shining on it all day. Bobby asked if the chickens were inside the cage and mummy said that they were not. She told Bobby that the chickens were free to roam around in the garden.

Lila and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big pond with many fish and shrimp. They wanted to catch some shrimp to take home. "Look, Tom, there are so many shrimp in the water. Let's get a net and catch them," Lila said. "OK, Lila, but we have to be careful. The pond is deep and the shrimp are fast," Tom said. They found a net and went to the edge of the pond. They tried to scoop up some shrimp, but it was difficult. The shrimp were small and slippery, and they jumped out of the net. Lila and Tom got wet and muddy, but they did not give up. "Come on, Lila, we can do it. We just have to try harder," Tom said. "I know, Tom, but I'm tired and hungry. Maybe we should go home and have a snack," Lila said. But Tom did not listen. He wanted to catch more shrimp. He saw a big shrimp near a rock and ran to get it. He did not see that the rock was slippery and the water was deep. He slipped and fell into the pond. He cried for help. "Lila, help me, I can't swim!" Tom shouted. Lila heard Tom and ran to him. She threw him the net and pulled him out of the pond. She was scared and angry. "Tom, you were silly and greedy. You did not listen to me. You could have drowned. You are lucky I was here to save you," Lila said. Tom was sorry and ashamed. He hugged Lila and thanked her. "Lila, you are right. I was silly and greedy. I did not listen to you. I could have drowned. You are a good friend. I'm sorry I made you worry," Tom said. They hugged and made up. They decided to leave the shrimp alone and go home and have a snack. They learned that it is better to be careful and listen to your friends than to be silly and greedy.

One day, a wealthy man named Bob had a big rubber ball. He loved to play with it in the park. He would throw it and watch it bounce high. One sunny day, Bob went to the park with his rubber ball. At the park, Bob saw a little girl named Sue. Sue looked sad. Bob asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my ball and I can't find it." Bob felt bad for Sue and wanted to help her. Bob had an idea. He gave his rubber ball to Sue and said, "You can have my ball." Sue was so happy and they played together. Later, Sue found her lost ball and returned it to Bob. They both learned that sharing makes everyone happy.
