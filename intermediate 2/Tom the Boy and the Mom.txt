Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom felt gloomy one day, because it was raining outside. He could not go and play with his friends. Tom's mom saw him feeling sad. She said, "Tom, let's spend time together and make something fun." Tom's face lit up with a big smile. He liked the idea of making something with his mom. Mom took out a big, colorful cloth. She said, "Let's make a tent with this cloth!" They put the cloth over some chairs and made a cozy tent. Tom and his mom spent the whole day playing in the tent, and Tom was not gloomy anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little rabbit. The little rabbit had a big friend, the bear. They liked to play together. One day, they played a game of zigzag. The rabbit would hop in a zigzag way, and the bear would try to follow. The bear was impatient because he could not keep up with the rabbit. He wanted to win the game. The rabbit saw the bear was sad, so he said, "Listen, friend, we can play a new game." The bear agreed and they played hide and seek. The bear was happy now, and they played together all day long.

One day, a very foolish man was walking down the street with an iron in his hand. He was showing it to everyone he saw. A little boy was watching him from the other side of the street. He thought it was really strange that the man was showing off the iron, so he decided to follow him. The boy followed the man for a long time until they reached a big shop. Then the man went inside the shop, but the boy was too small to go in. He waited outside and kept watching. Suddenly, the man came out of the shop with a big smile on his face. He was showing everyone his new, shiny iron! The little boy was amazed and asked where he got it. The man proudly said he had bought it from the shop. The foolish man then went away, but the boy was so excited about his new discovery that he ran off to tell all his friends!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a blue uniform that he wore every day. One day, he went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree and decided to have a picnic under it. Tim's mom said, "Tim, why don't you try some of this delicious apple?" Tim took a bite and his eyes grew wide. "Yum!" he said, "This is the best apple ever!" Just then, a big wind came and blew Tim's uniform up into the tree. Tim was scared, but his mom encouraged him. "You can do it, Tim!" she said. Tim was brave and climbed the tree. When he got to the top, he saw a bird wearing his uniform! The bird said, "I'm sorry, I thought it was a cozy nest." Tim laughed and took his uniform back. He climbed back down and his mom gave him a big hug. They had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely dog named Spot. Spot lived in a big yard with a fence. The fence was very tall. Spot could not see the other dogs. Spot felt sad. One day, Spot saw a ball. The ball was on the other side of the fence. Spot wanted the ball. He hoped to get the ball. Spot tried to jump over the fence. But Spot was too small. He could not jump high enough. Then, a big dog named Max came. Max saw Spot and the ball. Max wanted to help Spot. Max jumped over the fence and got the ball for Spot. Spot was happy. Now, Spot and Max played together. Spot was not lonely anymore.

Sam and Mia liked to play golf with their dad. They had a big bag with many sticks and balls. Each stick and ball was different. They were unique. One day, they went to a new place to play golf. It had a big gate with a lock. Dad had a key to unlock the gate. He said, "This is a special place. We have to be careful and quiet here." Sam and Mia followed dad inside. They saw many animals. There were deer, rabbits, birds, and squirrels. They were not afraid of people. They came close to Sam and Mia. Sam and Mia wanted to pet them, but dad said, "No, we have to leave them alone. They are wild animals. They live here. We are guests." Sam and Mia nodded. They understood. They took out their sticks and balls. They tried to hit the balls into the holes. It was hard, but fun. Sometimes, the animals watched them. Sometimes, they ran away. When they finished playing, they put their sticks and balls back in the bag. They thanked the animals for letting them play. They said goodbye to them. They went back to the gate. Dad locked the gate with his key. He said, "That was a unique golf game. Did you enjoy it?" Sam and Mia smiled. They said, "Yes, dad. We loved it. Can we come back again?" Dad hugged them. He said, "Maybe, if we are lucky. This is a secret place. We have to keep it safe and clean. We have to respect the animals. They are our friends." Sam and Mia agreed. They held dad's hand. They walked to the car. They drove home. They dreamed of the unique golf place and the animals. They were happy.
