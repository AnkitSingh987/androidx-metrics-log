Category: Intermediate
Title: Chicky the Chicken and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken named Chicky. Chicky lived on a farm with many other animals. Chicky was very fragile and small, but she had a big heart. One day, Chicky saw a big apple tree with yummy apples. Chicky wanted to get an apple but the tree was too tall. Chicky asked her friends for help. The big cow tried to reach the apples, but she could not yield any apples from the tree. Then, Chicky had an idea. She asked all her friends to help. They all stood on each other and made a tall tower. Chicky climbed to the top and finally, they could yield the apples. Chicky and her friends were so happy to eat the yummy apples together.

Once upon a time, there was a very special girl called Sue. Sue was an amazing designer. She was always creating something special with her toys. One day, Sue decided to design a telephone out of a cardboard box. She decorated it with crayons, markers and glitter glue. She couldn't wait to try out her invention! Sue's mom saw what Sue had made and said, "That looks amazing Sue! What does it do?" Sue replied, "It's a telephone. You can talk to people on it!" Sue's mom was very impressed. She said, "That's great! Let's call Grandma and show her your invention!" Sue and her mom called Grandma on Sue's amazing telephone and they were all so excited to show her. Grandma was amazed at what Sue had made. She said, "That's incredible, Sue! What a great invention!" Sue was so happy to show her family her invention and they all congratulated her on such a wonderful design.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a rake. He liked to help his mom and dad fix the yard. One day, Tim was not careful. He left his rake on the ground. A dog named Spot came to play with Tim. Spot did not see the rake. Spot stepped on the rake and it hit him. Spot was sad and cried. Tim felt bad and wanted to make Spot feel better. Tim had an idea! He found a toy to make Spot happy. Spot played with the toy and forgot about the rake. Tim learned to be careful with his rake. Now, Tim and Spot play together and have fun. And the rake? Tim always puts it away when he is done.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy bat. He liked to strike the ball with the bat. One day, Tim found a bottle of glue. He did not know what it was for. He thought it was a toy. Tim played with the glue and his bat. He put the glue on the bat and struck the ball. The ball stuck to the bat! Tim tried to pull the ball off, but it was stuck. He was sad. He thought his bat was dead. Then, Tim's mom came to help. She knew how to fix the bat. She put warm water on the bat and the ball. The glue became soft, and the ball came off. Tim was so happy! His bat was not dead! Now, Tim knew not to play with glue.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful fireman named Jim. He loved to help people and put out fires. Jim had a big red truck that went very fast. He wore a special hat and coat to keep him safe. One day, there was a big fire in a house. Jim and his friends jumped into the truck and went to the house very fast. When they got there, they saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared of the fire. Jim knew what to do. He told his friends to put out the fire while he saved the cat. Jim stood tall on the ladder and climbed up to the cat. He held the cat tight and brought it down to the ground. The cat was so happy to be safe. Jim and his friends put out the fire and everyone was safe. The people in the town were very proud of their powerful fireman.
