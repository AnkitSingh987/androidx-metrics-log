Category: Intermediate
Title: The Grumpy Deer and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy deer. He lived in a big forest. He had no friends because he was always sad and mad. One day, the deer met a frog. The frog could spring high into the air. The frog said, "Let's play!" The deer did not want to play. But the frog kept trying to make the deer happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The frog did a big spring and landed on the deer's head. The deer was not mad. He started to laugh! The deer and the frog became best friends. They played and had fun every day. The grumpy deer was not grumpy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to drink water every day. He knew it was good for him and made him feel strong. One day, Tim saw his friend, Ben, who looked tired and weak. "Ben, why do you look so tired?" asked Tim. Ben replied, "I don't know, Tim. I just don't feel good." Tim thought for a moment and asked, "Do you drink water every day like me?" Ben shook his head and said, "No, I don't like water." Tim told Ben, "Drinking water is easy and helps you feel better. You should try it!" Ben listened to Tim and started to drink water every day too. After some time, Ben felt stronger and full of energy. Now, Tim and Ben play together without feeling tired. They learned that drinking water helps them feel good and carry their weight when they play. They made a promise to always drink water and stay strong.

Once upon a time there was a friendly bear. He loved to dream about the special mist that hung low in the sky. He would stand on a mountain top and close his eyes, letting the mist tickle his nose and fill up his lungs with the sweet smell of adventure. One day, the friendly bear was sitting in the mist when he saw something out of the corner of his eye. He turned around and saw a pretty bird, with shimmering colors of blue and yellow. He smiled at the bird and it flew back to its nest. The friendly bear and the bird became best friends. Every day, they would play and explore together in the mist. One day, when they were playing together, the bird showed the bear how to dream. The bear smiled as he was able to spin stories and create a world of his own. He felt so lucky to call the bird his friend. From then on, they continued to dream happy dreams while they played together in the mist.

Once upon a time, in an ancient house, there lived a cat named Tom. Tom had a toy with a veil. He loved to play with it all day. One day, Tom saw a big tree. He wanted to attach his veil toy to the tree. So, he climbed the tree with the veil in his mouth. When he reached the top, he attached the veil to a branch. But Tom was not careful. He fell from the tree and got hurt. The veil stayed in the tree and Tom could not play with it anymore. Tom was sad and the toy was lost.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little house. It was a happy house with a big yard. A girl named Amy lived in the house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. One day, Amy found a big pumpkin in the yard. She was very excited. But the pumpkin was old and starting to rot. Amy didn't know what to do. Amy's mom and dad told her that they could make a new pumpkin grow. They took the rotting pumpkin and put it in the ground. After some time, a new, cute pumpkin grew in the yard. Amy and her family were very happy, and their house was filled with love and laughter.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little dog named Tim. Tim was scared to play with the other dogs in the park. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, you need to go make new friends." Tim went to the park and saw a big dog named Max. Max was playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play too, but he was too shy. Then, Max saw Tim and said, "Come play with me!" Tim took a deep breath and went to play with Max. They had fun playing with the ball. After a while, all the other dogs came to watch them play. They liked how Tim and Max played together. At the end, all the dogs started to clap their paws. They were happy for Tim and Max. Tim felt proud and not so shy anymore. From that day on, Tim and Max played together in the park every day, and Tim was never shy again.
