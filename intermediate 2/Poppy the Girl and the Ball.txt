Category: Intermediate
Title: Poppy the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Poppy went for a walk. She saw a sad boy sitting on the grass. Poppy was a compassionate girl, so she went to talk to him. "Why are you sad?" she asked. The boy said, "I lost my ball. It was my favorite toy." Poppy wanted to help him feel better. They looked everywhere for the ball, but they could not find it. They were about to give up when something unexpected happened. A dog came running up to them with the ball in its mouth. The dog had found the ball and wanted to play! Poppy and the boy laughed and played with the dog. They passed the ball back and forth, having so much fun. The boy was not sad anymore. The moral of the story is to always help others when they are sad or need help. You never know what unexpected things might happen when you show kindness and compassion to others.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl called Susan. She was 3 years old and went to a very fun playgroup. Her favourite thing about playgroup was the games, where she made lots of new friends. Today, Susan was very troubled as she had been left at the school gates, and no one had come to collect her. Unsure of what to do, she began to weep. Suddenly, a voice from behind said, "Don't cry little one, everything will be alright." Susan quickly turned to see a tall lady smiling at her. "My name is Miss Jones," she said. "I am the playgroup teacher. Welcome to the school." Susan quickly ran to Miss Jones, who hugged her tightly. Miss Jones then said, "We can go inside now, it's time to head towards the exit." The two of them walked through the school, hand in hand. Susan felt much calmer now that she was with someone. They waved to all the other children, who welcomed them. Eventually, they arrived at the exit, and made their way out of school. Susan was relieved; she knew that everything would be alright.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to throw the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they missed and the ball rolled away. They ran after it and laughed. One day, they saw a big hill near the park. They wondered what was on the other side. They decided to climb the hill and find out. They left their ball at the bottom and started to walk up. The hill was steep and hard to climb. They held hands and helped each other. When they reached the top, they gasped. They saw a giant! The giant was very tall and hairy. He had a big nose and big ears. He was holding a big stick. He looked angry and impatient. He saw Lily and Ben and roared. "Who are you? What are you doing here? This is my hill!" the giant shouted. Lily and Ben were scared. They wanted to run away, but they were too high. They wished they had their ball. They remembered how they liked to play with it. "Please, Mr. Giant, we are sorry. We just wanted to see what was on the other side. We didn't mean to bother you. We are Lily and Ben. We like to play with our ball. Do you like to play with balls?" Lily asked. The giant frowned. He looked at his stick. He looked at Lily and Ben. He looked at the ball at the bottom of the hill. He thought for a moment. Then he smiled. "I like to play with balls. But I don't have any. Can I play with your ball?" the giant asked. Lily and Ben nodded. They were happy that the giant was not angry anymore. They hoped he would be nice and gentle. "OK, Mr. Giant, you can play with our ball. But you have to be careful. Don't throw it too hard or too far. And don't break it. It's our favorite ball." Ben said. The giant agreed. He raised his stick and threw it away. He picked up the ball and threw it to Lily and Ben. They caught it and threw it back. They played catch with the giant. They had fun. They became friends. They learned that giants can be nice too.
