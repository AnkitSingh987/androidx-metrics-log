Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rocket

One day, a little boy named Tim found a toy rocket. It was red and shiny. He wanted to play with it, so he went to his friend, Sue. "Look, I have a rocket!" said Tim. Sue was very excited. They went outside to play. Tim put the rocket on the ground. He pulled the zip on the back of the rocket. The rocket went up, up, up into the sky. They both looked up and said, "Wow!" They were so happy to see the rocket fly high. But then, the rocket came down very fast. Tim and Sue were scared. They ran away from the rocket. It landed on the ground with a big thud. They went back to look at the rocket. It was okay! They laughed and played with the rocket all day.

Once upon a time, there was a smart lady who wanted to create something special. She decided to make a pumpkin! She went out to the garden and picked one just the right size. She brought it inside and started to make her creation. First she cut the top off and took the insides out. Then she found some colored paints and carefully painted the pumpkin orange. She was so excited when it was finished! The next day, she placed her pumpkin on the front steps for everyone to see. Everyone thought it looked so cute and friendly! They all cheered for the smart lady, and that made her very happy. The pumpkin stayed on the front steps, bringing smiles to everyone who saw it. The lady was very proud of her creation and she thanked the pumpkin for making her look very smart!

One day, a gray cat named Blink walked in a line with his friends. They were going to the park to play. Blink was very happy. He liked to play with his friends. At the park, they saw a big gray box. They did not know what was in the box. Blink was brave and went to the box. He blinked his eyes and pushed the box. The box moved and made a loud noise. All of a sudden, the box opened and many colorful balls came out! Blink and his friends were very surprised. They played with the balls all day and had lots of fun. The gray box was not scary after all. It was a fun surprise for Blink and his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a modest pigeon. He was not too big or too small. The pigeon liked to walk around the park. He had many friends there. One day, the pigeon went for a walk. He saw a little boy. The little boy had bread. The pigeon liked bread. The little boy saw the pigeon and smiled. He threw some bread for the pigeon to eat. The pigeon was very happy. He ate the bread and walked back to his friends. They all played together in the park. The pigeon was happy to have friends and share his bread with them. Everyone was happy in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to watch the sun rise every morning. One day, he woke up very early and felt impatient. He wanted to see the sun right away. So, he ran to his mom and said, "Mom, can we go see the sun rise now?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's go." They walked to the big hill near their house. Tim carried a glass of juice with him. He wanted to drink it while watching the sun. They sat on the hill, and Tim sipped his juice. He was very excited. Soon, the sun started to rise. It looked like a big, bright ball in the sky. Tim and his mom watched it together. They were very happy. And that's how Tim learned that sometimes, it's good to be a little patient.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wise old doctor. He was very good at helping people heal when they were hurt. One day, a little boy named Timmy came to the doctor with a cut on his leg. "Doctor, I was playing near a wreck and I fell down," said Timmy. "Can you help me heal my leg?" The wise doctor smiled and said, "Of course, Timmy. Let me take a look." He gently cleaned Timmy's cut and put a bandage on it. "There you go, all better!" Timmy smiled and thanked the wise doctor. From that day on, Timmy learned to be more careful when playing near a wreck. He also told all his friends about the wise doctor who helped him heal. And they all lived happily ever after.
