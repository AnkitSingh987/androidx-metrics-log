Category: Intermediate
Title: The Sleepy Castle and the Sun

Once upon a time there was a sleepy castle. In the castle lived a dragon. The dragon loved to blink in the sun. One day, the dragon woke up feeling extra sleepy. He decided to go for a wander around the castle grounds. As the dragon blinked in the warm sun, he felt more and more awake. Before he knew it, the dragon was wide awake and looking around. He saw a beautiful pond at the edge of the castle. He jumped in and started to play. He splashed around, getting water all over himself. The dragon laughed as he blinked and splashed in the water. He had so much fun that he hardly noticed when night came. Soon enough though, the dragon was feeling sleepy again. He hopped out of the pond and stumbled back to the castle. He yawned and opened the door, ready to go to bed and blink in the morning sun.

Ben and Mia were twins. They liked to play together, but sometimes they got bored. One day, they found a big jar of sugar in the kitchen. They had an idea. "Let's change the sugar into something else," Ben said. "Like what?" Mia asked. "Like candy, or sand, or snow," Ben said. They took some spoons and bowls and went to the backyard. They poured some sugar into the bowls and added water, dirt, and leaves. They stirred and stirred, but nothing changed. "This is boring," Mia said. "The sugar is still sugar." "Maybe we need more time," Ben said. "Let's leave them here and come back later." They ran inside to find something else to do. They forgot about the bowls of sugar. A few hours later, their mom came home. She saw the bowls of sugar in the backyard. She was angry. "Who did this?" she shouted. "This is a waste of sugar and a mess to clean up!" She went inside to find Ben and Mia. They were playing with their toys in their room. They heard their mom's voice and looked at each other. "Uh-oh," they said. "We are in trouble."

One day, Jack was walking in the park when he heard a sneeze. He looked around and saw a blueberry bush. Jack was surprised because he had never seen a blueberry bush in the park before. He decided to go see it. When he got closer, he saw a little girl who was very weak. She was sneezing and her face was very red. "Are you OK?" Jack asked her. "Yes," she replied, "I'm allergic to blueberries!" Jack thought about it for a minute. He had a bag full of blueberry snacks in his pocket. He offered her one, and she smiled. She ate it and immediately felt better. Jack had saved the day! He knew she was weak from the sneezing, but he never expected it was caused by blueberries. He was glad he had the snacks, and they both had a great day playing at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to sell unique things. One day, Sue made a special salad. She wanted to sell the salad at the big park. At the park, many people came to buy the salad. Everyone loved it. But then, a big wind came. It blew the salad away! Sue was sad and the people were sad too. Sue learned that sometimes things go wrong. But she didn't give up. Sue made more salad and sold it again. Everyone was happy. The moral of the story is to never give up, even when things go wrong.

Lily and Tom are playing with their toys in the living room. Lily has a brown teddy bear that she loves very much. Tom has a red car that he likes to zoom around. They are having fun and making noises. Suddenly, they hear a loud knock on the door. They stop playing and look at each other. Who is it? They wonder. They hear their mom say, "Who are you? What do you want?" They hear a man say, "I am here to fix the TV. Let me in." They hear their mom say, "No, you are not. Go away. I did not call you." Lily and Tom are scared. They think the man is a thief. They want to help their mom. They run to the door and shout, "Go away, thief! We do not want you here! You are bad!" They hear the man say, "Shut up, kids! I will take your toys if you do not be quiet!" They hear their mom say, "Leave now or I will call the police!" Lily and Tom are brave. They do not be quiet. They keep shouting, "Go away, thief! Go away, thief!" They hear the man say, "Fine, I will go. But I will be back!" They hear him run away. They hear their mom say, "Thank you, Lily and Tom. You are very smart and strong. You helped me scare away the thief. I am proud of you." Lily and Tom are happy. They hug their mom and say, "We love you, mom. You are very smart and strong too. You protected us from the thief. We are proud of you." They go back to the living room and play with their toys. Lily holds her brown teddy bear and says, "You are safe, teddy. The thief did not take you." Tom holds his red car and says, "You are fast, car. The thief could not catch you." They smile and laugh. They are safe and happy.
