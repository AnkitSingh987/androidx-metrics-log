Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mouse and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse liked to pretend he was big and strong. One day, the mouse saw a wet cat. The wet cat was very sad. The cat said, "I don't like being wet. I want to be dry." The mouse wanted to help, but he was too small. The mouse thought, "I need to pretend to be big and strong to help the cat." So, the mouse pretended to be big and strong. He found a dry place for the cat. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, mouse!" The mouse was happy too, because he could help the cat even if he was small.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who arrived in a small village. All the people of the village greeted him warmly and welcomed him. "Welcome, old man!" the villagers said. "What brings you to our village?" The old man smiled and replied, "I have come here to help people who are in trouble. Can you tell me where I can find them?" The village leader stepped forward. "Follow me," he said. "I will take you to a family who needs help." The old man and the village leader arrived at a small house. Inside, they found a family with two small children. The parents were very scared. "We are so worried," said the mother. "Our daughter has been very sick and we don't know what to do." The old man looked at the little girl and smiled. He began to tell them a story. Soon, the little girl was laughing and her parents were relieved. "Thank you so much!" the parents said. The old man smiled and replied, "It's my pleasure. Troubles can always be solved!" And with that, he left for the next village.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. One day, Tim saw a path in the woods. He wanted to see where the path went. So, he started to walk on the path. As Tim walked, he saw a big, foolish dog. The dog was barking loudly at him. Tim was scared. He did not know what to do. He thought the dog might be mean. So, he decided to be brave and keep walking on the path. Tim walked past the foolish dog. The dog stopped barking and started to follow Tim. They walked together on the path. Soon, they became good friends. Tim and the dog played in the woods and had fun. They were happy they met on the path.

One day, Lisa wanted to try something new. She decided to put on some makeup. She had never done that before and it felt a bit strange. But she thought it felt kind of comfortable too. She put on some lipstick and some mascara, and felt like a grown up. She was so proud and wanted to show everyone. But then she kicked the cat, who then jumped away in surprise. The cat's mom wasn't happy. She scolded Lisa for her bad behaviour and made her wash off all her makeup. Lisa was so sad that she had been stupid and didn't follow the rules. That night she stayed in bed, feeling sad and alone. She learned an important lesson that day, and knew she never wanted to feel that way again.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big field of daisies. They ran to the field and picked some flowers. "Look, Lily, I found a very attractive daisy for you!" Ben said, holding a yellow and white flower. "It is bright and pretty, like you!" "Thank you, Ben, you are very kind!" Lily said, smiling. "I have a gift for you, too. Do you see this daisy? It has a purple center. It is rare and special, like you!" They gave each other the daisies and hugged. They felt happy and warm. "Did you know, Ben, that daisies can make wishes come true?" Lily asked. "Really? How?" Ben asked. "Well, you have to believe very hard in your wish, and then blow on the daisy. The petals will fly away and carry your wish to the sky." Lily explained. "That sounds fun! Let's try it!" Ben said. They closed their eyes and thought of their wishes. Lily wished for more daisies to grow in the park. Ben wished for Lily to be his friend forever. Then they opened their eyes and blew on the daisies. The petals flew away in the wind, like little stars. Lily and Ben watched them and hoped their wishes would come true. They held hands and walked back to the park. They felt happy and warm. They believed in their daisies and their friendship.
