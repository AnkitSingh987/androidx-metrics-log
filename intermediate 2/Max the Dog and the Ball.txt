Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a chubby dog named Max. Max liked to play with his toy ball. One day, Max's ball broke into many pieces. Max was very sad. Max's friend, a smart cat named Lily, had an idea. She said, "Let's use glue to fix your ball!" Max was happy. They found some glue and started to number the pieces to put them back together. But, oh no! Max and Lily used too much glue. The ball was sticky and they could not play with it. Max and Lily learned to be more careful next time. They went to find a new ball to play with, and Max was happy again.

One sunny day, a little girl named Lily found a jolly pebble. The pebble was round and smooth. It had a big smile on it. Lily loved the pebble and wanted to take it home. On her way home, Lily met her friend Tom. Tom saw the pebble and said, "Wow, that's a nice pebble! Can I have it?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "No, but we can bury it together and make a wish." Lily and Tom dug a small hole in the ground and buried the jolly pebble. They closed their eyes and made a wish. Then, they covered the hole with dirt and patted it down. A few days later, Lily and Tom went back to the spot where they buried the pebble. To their surprise, they found a beautiful flower growing there. They were very happy and learned that sharing can bring joy and make good things happen.

Once upon a time, there was a thief named Tom. Tom was not nice. He liked to take things that were not his. One day, Tom saw a big, red ball. He wanted the ball very much. Tom went to the little boy who had the ball. "Can I have your ball?" Tom asked. The little boy said, "No, it is my ball." Tom felt guilty, but he took the ball anyway. The little boy was sad. He told his mom about the thief. The mom called the police. They found Tom and took the ball back. Tom was put in jail. He felt very guilty and never got the ball he wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bunny named Bob. Bob had a big bag full of toys. He liked to play with his toys all day. But his room was always messy. His mom asked him to clean his room. One day, Bob decided to organize his toys. He put all the toys in the big bag. He put the cars together, the dolls together, and the balls together. Bob felt happy because his room was clean. Bob's friends came to play. They saw the clean room and the big bag. They all played with the toys and had fun. They learned that when they organize, they can find their toys easily and have more fun. The moral of the story is: when we keep things clean and organized, we can have more fun with our friends.

Clara was a very patient three year old girl. Her mom always encouraged her to work hard and do her best. But one day, when Clara tried to do the same, she just couldn't learn the trick she was trying to learn. "Oh, Mommy, I just can't do it!" Clara cried. "That's ok," her mom said as she hugged her. "You just need more practice." But Clara had been practicing for a long time, and she was getting frustrated. "No, I need to get it today!" Clara said. Her mom tried to calm her down, but Clara just kept trying the same trick over and over again. Finally, her mom got so frustrated that she shouted at her. "Stop it, Clara! You need to be more patient!" Clara was so embarrassed and sorry. She ran upstairs to her room and cried. Her mom regretted it, but it was too late. Clara had already been hurt by her mom's words. She was sad and discouraged and it took a long time for her to feel better.

Once upon a time, there was a cabin in the woods. It was not a big cabin, but it was a nice place to live. A girl named Lily and her dog, Max, lived in the cabin. They liked to play in the woods and have fun. One day, Lily and Max went for a walk in the woods. They saw a huge tree. It was so big! They wanted to see what would happen if they climbed it. So, they started to climb the huge tree. It was a lot of fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The huge tree started to talk! It said, "Hello, Lily and Max! I am a magic tree! I can make your cabin bigger if you want." Lily and Max were very surprised. They said, "Yes, please!" The magic tree made their cabin bigger. Now, Lily and Max had a big cabin to live in. They were so happy and played in their big cabin all day.
