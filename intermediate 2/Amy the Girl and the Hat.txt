Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved the color pink. One day, Amy found a pink hat in her room. She was very happy and put it on her head. She wanted to show her friends her new pink hat. But outside, it was a gloomy day. The sky was gray and it was raining. Amy was sad because she could not go out to play with her friends. She did not want her pink hat to get wet in the rain. Amy had an idea. She asked her mom for a big umbrella. Her mom gave her the umbrella and Amy put it up. With the umbrella, she went outside to find her friends. They all loved her pink hat and played together under the umbrella. The gloomy day became a fun day for Amy and her friends.

One day, a boy named Tim had a red wagon. He liked to pull his wagon all around the yard. The sun was high in the sky, and it was a fun day. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and called her to play with him. Sue came over and said, "Let's put our toys in the wagon and pull it together!" They put their toys in the wagon and began to pull it. They were having a great time, but then they saw dark clouds coming. They knew it would rain soon. Tim and Sue decided to pull the wagon back home before the rain came. They pulled and pulled, and made it home just in time. The rain started to fall, but they were safe and dry inside. They looked at each other and smiled. "Thank you for helping me, Sue," said Tim. "We got our toys home safe and dry because we worked together!" The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can do things faster and better.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house. The man was very big and strong, but his wife was small and fearful. One day, they went for a walk in the woods. The wife was scared, but the man held her hand. They saw a big rock and decided to sit and rest. The wife was still scared, but the man said, "Don't worry, I will protect you." They sat on the rock and looked at the trees and the sky. The wife started to feel better. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. The wife was scared again, but the man was brave. He stood up and saw a big, friendly dog. The dog came to them and the wife was not scared anymore. They all played together and had a fun day. The wife learned that sometimes things that seem scary can actually be nice.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a dry card on the floor. He picked it up and saw a picture of a ball on it. Tim showed the card to his friend, Lily. "Look, Lily! I found a card with a ball!" Tim said. Lily looked at the card and smiled. "Let's play ball," she said. Tim and Lily went outside to play. They found a ball and took turns to strike it. They laughed and had fun. Then, they saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. They talked about the dry card and the fun they had striking the ball. The end.

One day, a little dog named Max found a long chain in the park. He was very proud of his find. Max wanted to show it to his best friend, a cat named Luna. So, Max picked up the chain in his mouth and went to find Luna. When Max found Luna, he said, "Look, Luna! I found a chain!" Luna looked at the chain and said, "Wow, Max! It's very long and nice!" They played with the chain all day long, swinging it and running around with it. But then, something unexpected happened. While they were playing, they lost the chain! Max and Luna looked everywhere for it. They were sad because they lost their new toy. Then, they heard a voice say, "Is this your chain?" They turned around and saw a big, friendly bear holding their chain. Max and Luna were so happy to have their chain back, and they thanked the bear. From that day on, Max, Luna, and the bear became best friends and played together all the time.
