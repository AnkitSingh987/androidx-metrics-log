Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Fish and the Rock

One day, a little fish named Tim was swimming in the sea. He saw a big, heavy rock with a shiny scale on top. Tim wanted to learn how to put the scale on his body. He swam to his mom and asked, "Mom, can you teach me how to put the scale on my body?" Mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's go to the heavy rock and I will show you." They swam to the rock and Mom showed Tim how to put the scale on his body. Tim was so happy! Just then, a big wave came and pushed the heavy rock away. Tim and Mom were surprised! Under the rock, they found a treasure chest. Tim and Mom opened the chest and found many more shiny scales inside. They put the scales on their bodies and became the most beautiful fish in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a girl who wanted music. She wanted to hear music that shone and sparkled. So she went outside and it was dark. But then, she looked up and she saw something beautiful. There were lights in the sky! They twinkled and shone like little stars. She looked and listened and soon heard music coming from the lights! The music was beautiful. She wanted to dance and sing along, so she did! She danced and sang and the lights danced with her. It was a wonderful night of music, lights and fun. And the girl was so happy!

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play outside. She liked to run, jump, and laugh with her friends. Today, she wanted to discover something new, so she went to explore the big tree at the end of her yard. As Mia looked around the tree, she saw something in the tall grass. It was a doll! The doll was small and harmless, with a big smile on its face. Mia was so happy to find the doll that she picked it up and hugged it tight. Mia ran to show her mom the doll she found. But when she got to the house, her mom started to laugh. "Mia, that's not a doll!" she said. Mia looked closer and saw that it was not a doll, but a funny-shaped rock! The rock had a painted face that looked like a doll. Mia laughed too, and decided to keep her new friend, the harmless rock doll, as a special treasure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a troubled kid because he always wanted to play with his friends, but his mom said no. She wanted him to stay inside and look at the screen. Tim didn't like the screen, but he had to listen to his mom. One day, while Tim was looking at the screen, he saw a big bird outside his window. The bird looked at Tim and said, "Why are you always inside? You should come out and play with me!" Tim was very surprised that the bird could talk. He said, "I want to play, but my mom says I have to look at the screen." The bird thought for a moment and then said, "You should believe in yourself, Tim. If you tell your mom how you feel, maybe she will let you play outside." Tim thought that was a good idea, so he went to his mom and said, "Mom, I don't like looking at the screen all day. I want to play outside with my friends and the big bird." His mom listened to Tim and said, "Okay, Tim, you can play outside for a little while. But be careful and stay close to the house." Tim was so happy that he could play outside. He ran out the door and played with the big bird and his friends, and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong wall. The wall was very old and had been there for a long, long time. Many people liked the wall because it kept them safe. One day, a little girl named Lucy walked up to the wall. She wanted to play on the other side. Lucy said, "Wall, let me go to the other side, please." But the wall did not say anything. Lucy thought the wall must be deaf. So, she said it louder, "Wall, let me go to the other side, please!" The wall still did not say anything. Lucy was sad, but she did not give up. She demanded, "Wall, let me go to the other side right now!" This time, a door opened in the wall. Lucy was very happy. She went through the door and played with her friends on the other side. They all had a lot of fun, and the wall kept them safe and happy.
