Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and the Car

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk with her mom. They saw a big store with many things on display. Amy was very excited to see all the colorful objects. "Mom, can I pick an object to buy?" asked Amy. Her mom said, "Yes, but only one thing." Amy looked around the store, trying to find the best object to take home. She saw a toy car, a ball, and a pretty dress. Amy was ignorant about what to choose. Amy's mom said, "Look, there's a surprise box! You don't know what's inside until you open it." Amy remembered her mom talking about surprise boxes before, so she felt happy to pick it. She took the box home and opened it. Inside, she found a toy car, a ball, and a pretty dress. Amy was so happy and thanked her mom for helping her choose the best object.

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat. The cat liked to play with a red ball. One day, the ball rolled under a big tree. Under the tree, the cat heard a voice. It was a tiny bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly. The cat wanted to help the bird. The cat and the bird received help from a kind dog. The dog found a soft nest for the bird. The bird was happy and learned to fly. The cat, the bird, and the dog played together and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat was nervous because it had never gone to sail before. One day, the boat decided to be brave and sail on the water. As the boat started to sail, the wind blew strong. The boat went fast on the water. The boat was so happy to be sailing and playing with the wind. The boat was not nervous anymore. Then, something unexpected happened. The wind stopped blowing. The boat was sad because it could not sail without the wind. But soon, the wind came back and the boat learned to be patient. The boat and the wind became best friends, and they sailed happily together. The moral of the story is to be brave and patient, and good things will come.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run around. One day, Max went to a big room where a man was giving a lecture. Max was very excited and impatient to see what was going on. As Max sat and listened, he felt a tickle in his nose. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. Max let out a big sneeze. "Achoo!" he sneezed. Everyone in the room looked at Max. The man giving the lecture stopped talking and said, "Bless you, Max!" Max was surprised that the man knew his name. The man smiled and said, "I am a magic man, and I can talk to animals. Because you sneezed, you have set me free from a spell. Now, I can go back to my family." Max was very happy to help the magic man. From that day on, Max and the magic man became best friends. They played and had fun together. And Max learned that even a little sneeze could make a big difference.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to perform a dance for her mom. She put on her pretty dress and her dancing shoes. Lily was excited to make her mom smile. Lily started to dance, but she forgot the steps. She felt frustrated and stopped dancing. Her mom saw her and asked, "What's wrong, Lily?" Lily said, "I can't remember the steps, Mom." Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay. Just have fun and dance from your heart." Lily smiled and started to dance again. This time, she didn't worry about the steps. She danced with joy and made her mom smile.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys on the couch. One day, they find a big book on the couch. It has many pictures and words. "What is this?" Lily asks. "It is a book," Ben says. "Mommy reads books to us." "Can we read it?" Lily says. Ben nods. He opens the book. He sees a picture of a star. He points to it. "Look, Lily, this is a star. It is light and shiny. It is in the sky at night." "Wow, a star!" Lily says. "How does it shine?" Ben thinks. He remembers what Mommy told him. "Mommy said stars are like big balls of fire. They are very far away. They make light for us to see." "Fire? Like a candle?" Lily says. "Yes, like a candle, but bigger and hotter," Ben says. Lily and Ben look at the star. They are amazed. They want to learn more. They turn the page. They see a picture of a moon. "What is this?" Lily asks. Ben smiles. He is happy to explain. He likes to share what he knows with his sister. "This is a moon. It is also in the sky at night. It is not light by itself. It gets light from the sun." "The sun? What is the sun?" Lily says. Ben looks at the window. He sees the sun shining outside. "The sun is the biggest and brightest star. It is in the sky in the day. It gives us light and warmth. It makes the moon and the stars shine too." Lily and Ben are curious. They want to see the sun and the moon and the stars. They ask Mommy if they can go outside. Mommy says yes. She is proud of her twins. They are smart and eager to learn. She takes them outside. She shows them the sun and the moon and the stars. She explains more things to them. Lily and Ben listen and ask questions. They have fun. They love to learn. They love their Mommy. They love their book. They love their couch. They love each other.
