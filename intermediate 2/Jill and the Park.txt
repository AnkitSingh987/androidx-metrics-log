Category: Intermediate
Title: Jill and the Park

Jill had never been to the park before, so she asked her father if they could go. Her father said yes and together they walked to the park. It was noon, but Jill did not know what that meant. She had never heard the word before. Once they got to the park, it was empty and looked boring. Jill was disappointed, she wanted to play and make friends with the other kids. But all the kids had already gone home for lunch, so she was stuck there alone with her dad. Jill asked her father why it was so empty and boring. He said, "It is noon, so all the kids have to go home and eat lunch with their parents". Jill felt sad that she did not know this before. From then on, Jill resolved to remember what noon was. She vowed to never go to the park again when it was noon, so she could have fun and make friends. But it was too late. The park was always boring when it was noon and Jill's dreams of having fun were crushed.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a dog named Spot. Sally and Spot were best friends. They played together every day. Spot was an obedient dog, and Sally loved him very much. One day, Sally found a big box. Inside the box, there was a funny image of a man with a hat. The man had a big bow on his hat. Sally laughed and showed the image to Spot. "Look, Spot! The man has a big bow!" she said. Suddenly, the man in the image came to life! He jumped out of the box and said, "Hello, Sally and Spot! I am the Bow Man. I heard you laughing, and I wanted to play too!" Sally and Spot were surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend. They all played together and had lots of fun. And from that day on, Sally, Spot, and the Bow Man were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big lab where he made toys for kids. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the lab. Tim asked Tom, "Can I come in and see the toys?" Tom said, "I will permit you to come in, but you must be good and not touch anything." In the lab, Tim saw many fun toys. He wanted to play with them, but he remembered Tom's words. So, he just looked at the toys. Tom saw that Tim was a good boy. He said, "You can pick one toy to take home." Tim was very happy and picked his favorite toy. Tim went home with his new toy and told his friends about the wealthy man and his lab. They all wanted to visit the lab too, and Tom permitted them to come in and see the toys.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Max. Max loved to play and run all day. He had many friends in the park. One day, Max and his friends were playing with a ball. They would pass the ball to each other with their noses. Max was very good at this game. He was fast and strong. His friends liked to watch him play. Suddenly, a little bird landed on Max's shoulder. The bird was lost and needed help. Max and his friends worked together to find the bird's home. They were all very happy when they found it. The bird thanked Max and his friends, and they all played together until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big sister named Lily. One day, Amy and Lily were playing in the park. They got very dirty. Their clothes had mud on them. Amy started to talk to Lily. "We will go on vacation soon. We can't be dirty," said Amy. Lily said, "You're right, Amy. We need to be clean for vacation." The next day, Amy and Lily took a bath. They washed their clothes too. They were very clean now. They were ready for vacation. On vacation, Amy and Lily played on the beach. They made sand castles and swam in the water. They were happy they were clean when they started their vacation. But now, they were dirty again from playing. They laughed and said, "It's okay to be dirty when we're having fun!"
