Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in her garden with her friends. One day, a foolish bird flew into the garden and saw a beautiful flower. The bird said, "Hello, pretty flower! I like you." The flower bowed and said, "Thank you, silly bird. I like you too!" They played together all day, laughing and having fun. The bird sang songs and the flower danced in the wind. When it was time to say goodbye, the bird and the flower gave each other a big hug. Lily watched them and smiled. She knew that even the most foolish bird could find a friend in a kind flower. And from that day on, the garden was filled with even more joy and laughter.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, lived in a white house. They were very good friends. Every day, they played in the yard and had lots of fun. One day, Tim's mom cooked a big steak for dinner. Tim and Spot smelled the yummy food and their tummies growled. Tim said, "Spot, I want to eat the steak! Do you want some too?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, I want steak too!" Tim's mom cut the steak into small pieces for Tim and Spot. They both ate the tasty steak and were very happy. After dinner, they went outside to play again. Tim and Spot loved living in their white house and having yummy food together.

Once upon a time, there was a fast shark named Mark. Mark loved to swim in the big blue sea. He had many friends, like the little fish and the big whale. One day, Mark and his friends saw a big ship. They wanted to play with the ship, but they were scared. Mark had an idea. He told his friends to wait and see what he would do. Mark swam very fast and jumped high out of the water. He made a big splash near the ship. The people on the ship saw Mark and smiled. They knew the fast shark just wanted to play. So, they played with Mark and his friends all day long. And they all had lots of fun in the big blue sea.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful cat named Tom. Tom liked to organize his toys. One day, he found a new toy. It was a page from a book. Tom was very happy. Tom played with the page all day. He put it with his other toys. He liked to look at it. But, one day, the page was gone. Tom was sad. He looked for the page everywhere. Then, Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was playing with the page! Tom was mad. But, Jerry said, "I can help you organize your toys, Tom!" Tom thought about it. Then, he said yes. Tom and Jerry became friends. They organized the toys together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with yarn. One day, Tom saw a big ball of yarn in the living room. It was red, blue, and yellow. He was so happy! Tom wanted to play with the yarn right away. But, his mom said, "Wait, Tom. We need to delay your playtime. First, we must organize your toys." Tom was sad, but he helped his mom. They put all the toys in their right places. Everything looked organized and neat. After they finished, Tom's mom said, "Good job, Tom! Now you can play with the yarn." Tom played with the yarn all day. He rolled it, tossed it, and had so much fun. And because he had waited and helped his mom, playing with the yarn was even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with furniture. In the room, there was a chair, a table, and a bed. The room was not pretty. So, a girl named Lily wanted to make it look better. Lily had an idea. She wanted to decorate the room. She got some paint and a brush. She painted the chair, the table, and the bed with bright colors. The colors made her happy. When she was done, the room looked impressive. The furniture was pretty now. Lily was happy and proud of her work. Everyone who saw the room liked it too. They all lived happily ever after.
