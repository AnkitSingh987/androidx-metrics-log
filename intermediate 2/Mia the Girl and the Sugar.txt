Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Sugar

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to make a cake. She asked her mom if she could use the sugar. Her mom said, "I permit you to use the sugar, but only a little." Mia was happy and started to make her cake. She put in the sugar, and then some more. She thought the more sugar, the better the cake would be. When her mom saw how much sugar she used, she looked gloomy. When the cake was done, Mia and her mom tried it. It was so sweet! They both laughed, and Mia learned that too much sugar is not always good. From then on, Mia only used a little sugar when she made a cake.

One day, a boy named Tim decided to build an igloo. He wanted it to be the best igloo ever. Tim thought that if he used expensive things, it would be really nice. So, he asked his mom for some money to buy the things he needed. Tim went to the store with his mom. He bought ice and snow to make the igloo. It was a lot of work, but Tim was happy. He knew his igloo would be the best one ever because he used expensive things to make it. When the igloo was done, Tim showed it to his friends. They all liked it and played inside. Tim was very proud of his igloo. He learned that even though he used expensive things, it was his hard work that made it the best.

Once upon a time, there was a king who was very tired. He was so tired one day that he started to sneeze and couldn't stop. His maid saw it and said, "Oh my, your majesty, why are you sneezing so much?" The king answered, "I'm just very tired." The maid was sorry for the king and told him to get some rest. So the king went to his bed and laid down. He closed his eyes, and soon he was fast asleep. As he slept, the sneezing stopped and the king was feeling better. The next day, the king was no longer tired and the maid was so happy. "Your king is feeling better," she said. The king smiled and said, "Yes, I am no longer tired. Thank you for your help." The maid was very happy to hear that the king was feeling much better. She smiled and said, "It's my pleasure, your majesty."

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben liked to run on the track in the woods. He was a very fast bunny. All the other animals liked to watch him run. Ben was a brave bunny. One day, Ben was running on the track when he saw a big rock. He was scared, but he was brave, so he tried to jump over it. He tried and tried, but he could not jump over the rock. Ben felt sad and wanted to quit. His friend, Sam the squirrel, saw Ben and came to help. Sam told Ben, "Don't quit! You are a brave bunny. You can do it!" Ben listened to Sam and tried again. This time, he jumped over the rock! All the animals cheered for Ben. Ben was happy he did not quit. He learned that even when things are hard, you should not give up. Ben was a brave bunny, and he was a good friend to Sam. The moral of the story is to never quit and always try your best.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple ball. It was round like a circle. It belonged to a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his purple ball every day. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over. Sue saw the purple ball and asked, "Can I borrow your ball, Tim?" Tim said, "Yes, but be careful. It might roll away." Sue promised to be careful and they started to play. They played and played, but then, the purple ball rolled away! It went down the hill and far away. Tim and Sue were sad, but they remembered Tim's warning. They looked for the ball and found it near a tree. They were happy again and played with the ball all day long.
