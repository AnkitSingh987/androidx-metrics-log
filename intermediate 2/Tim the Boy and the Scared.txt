Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Scared

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim was scared of the dark. Every night, when he went to bed, he thought he saw scary things in his room. One day, Tim's mom had an idea. She encouraged him to be brave. "Tim," she said, "when you feel scared, just hold onto your doorknob. It will help you feel safe." Tim liked this idea and decided to try it. That night, when it was time for bed, Tim held onto his doorknob. He said to himself, "I am brave. I am not scared." And guess what? It worked! Tim was not scared anymore. He was happy and slept well every night.

Anna and Ben like to play with yarn. They have many colors of yarn: red, blue, yellow, green, and more. They like to make things with yarn, like hats, scarves, and dolls. One day, they decide to try something new. They want to mix the yarn colors and make a rainbow. They take a big bowl and put some yarn in it. Then they use a spoon to mix the yarn. "Look, Anna, I am mixing red and yellow. What color do I get?" Ben asks. "You get orange, Ben. Orange is a pretty color. Can I mix blue and yellow?" Anna says. "Sure, Anna. You can mix blue and yellow. What color do you get?" Ben says. "I get green, Ben. Green is a nice color. Can we mix more colors?" Anna says. "Yes, Anna. We can mix more colors. Let's see what we get." Ben says. They keep mixing the yarn colors until they have a big pile of rainbow yarn. They are very happy with their work. They think they are successful. "Wow, Anna, we made a rainbow. It is so beautiful. We are very good at mixing yarn." Ben says. "Yes, Ben, we are. We are very creative. We can make anything with yarn." Anna says. They hug each other and smile. They love playing with yarn. They love mixing colors. They love making rainbows.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and look at funny pictures in his big book. One day, Tim found a new book on the shelf. It was a big, red book with lots of pictures inside. Tim opened the book and saw a funny picture of a cat wearing a hat. He laughed and showed it to his mom. "Look, mom! This cat is so funny!" Tim said. His mom smiled and told him to keep looking. She knew there was a surprise at the end of the book. Tim kept looking at the funny pictures. He saw a dog with big glasses and a bird with a long, twisty tail. He was so excited to see what the surprise was. At last, Tim reached the end of the book. He took a peek at the last page and saw a picture of himself wearing a big, silly hat! Tim laughed and laughed. "Mom, it's me!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "I put that picture in there just for you, Tim. I knew you would love it!" Tim hugged his mom and they both laughed at the funny picture together.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty vendor. He sold many things like toys, balls, and yummy treats. Every day, kids would come to his store and buy fun things to play with. One sunny day, a little boy came to the vendor's store. He saw a funny toy that made him laugh. The vendor saw the boy laugh and felt happy. He knew that the toy would make the little boy very happy. The boy took the funny toy home and showed it to his friends. They all laughed and played with the toy together. The mighty vendor was glad that he could make the kids happy with his store. And they all had a fun and happy day.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to cook. One day, she wanted to make a cake for her mom. Sue got a big pan and put it on the table. As she was mixing the cake mix, her little brother Tom came into the room. Tom was very curious and wanted to help. Sue said, "Be careful, Tom!" But Tom did not listen. He tried to hold the pan, but it was too heavy for him. He dropped the pan on the floor, and the cake mix went everywhere. Sue was sad, but she knew that Tom did not mean to drop the pan. They cleaned up the mess together and started again. This time, Sue let Tom help her in a safer way. They made the cake together, and it was the best cake ever. The moral of the story is: always be careful and listen to others when they give you advice.
