Category: Intermediate
Title: Zoom the Lonely Car and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a lonely car named Zoom. Zoom had no friends to play with. He would drive around the town all by himself, looking for someone to play with. One sunny day, Zoom saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and could not get down. Zoom wanted to help and save the cat. He thought and thought about how he could save the cat. Zoom had an idea! He drove very fast and made a big jump. He reached the tree and saved the cat! The cat was happy and thanked Zoom. From that day on, Zoom and the cat became best friends, and Zoom was not lonely anymore. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a fast spider. The fast spider loved to make cobwebs. One day, the fast spider decided to prepare a big cobweb near the window. The fast spider worked hard to prepare the cobweb. It was the biggest cobweb in the house. The fast spider was very happy with the big cobweb. Soon, the sun started to set, and the fast spider went to sleep. The next day, the fast spider woke up and saw that the big cobweb was gone. The fast spider was sad, but it knew it could make another big cobweb. So, the fast spider began to prepare a new cobweb, and it was even bigger and better than the first one.

One day, a kind girl named Lily found a mitten on the ground. She picked it up and looked around. "Who lost this mitten?" she asked. No one answered, so she decided to keep it safe until she found its owner. Lily walked to the park with the mitten. She saw her friend, Tim, playing with a ball. "Hi, Tim! Did you lose a mitten?" she asked. Tim shook his head and said, "No, but let's find who did!" They looked around the park, but they could not find the owner. As they walked home, a little bird flew down to them. The bird looked cold and sad. Lily had an idea! She took the mitten and folded it to make a warm bed for the bird. The bird was so happy and thanked Lily and Tim. They all became good friends and played together every day. And that's how the lost mitten found a new home.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big box of stamps in their dad's office. They thought the stamps were pretty and fun. They wanted to stick them on their faces, hands and clothes. "Look, I have a flower on my nose!" Lily said, laughing. "And I have a lion on my arm!" Ben said, roaring. They were very jolly and did not hear their dad coming. Their dad was not jolly when he saw them. He was angry and sad. He had warned them not to touch his stamps. The stamps were very special and expensive. He had collected them for a long time. He wanted to show them to his friends. "Who did this?" he asked, pointing at the stamps. Lily and Ben looked at each other. They knew they were in trouble. They felt sorry and scared. "We did, dad. We're sorry. We didn't know," they said in a small voice. Their dad sighed. He was not happy, but he loved them. He decided to teach them a lesson. "You have to pay for the stamps you used. You have to do chores for a week. No TV, no games, no candy. Do you understand?" he said. Lily and Ben nodded. They understood. They learned their lesson. They never touched their dad's stamps again.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a fan. The fan was big and loved to talk. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw the fan and said "Hi, fan!" The fan was happy to talk to Lily. "Hi, Lily! I am a fan. I can help you stay cool on a hot day," said the fan. Lily smiled and they became friends. The fan told Lily that one day, she would need its help. Days went by, and Lily and the fan talked every day. They had lots of fun. The fan loved to hear Lily's stories and Lily liked to hear the fan's stories too. The fan always reminded Lily that it was there to help her. One hot day, Lily was very hot and sweaty. She remembered what the fan had told her. "Fan, can you help me stay cool today?" she asked. The fan was happy to help. It started to spin and made a nice, cool breeze for Lily. She felt much better and was very thankful for her friend, the fan.
