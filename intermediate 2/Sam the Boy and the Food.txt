Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Boy and the Food

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. Sam lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Sam saw smoke coming from the kitchen. His mom was cooking, but the food was burning. It smelled disgusting. Sam's dad saw the smoke too. He quickly went to the kitchen and turned off the stove. He told Sam's mom to replace the burnt food with something else. They needed to make a new meal for dinner because the burnt food was not good to eat. Sam's mom listened to his dad and replaced the disgusting burnt food with yummy pasta. Sam and his family sat down together and enjoyed their dinner. They were happy that they could replace the bad food with something tasty. And Sam learned that it's important to pay attention when cooking, so the food doesn't turn into smoke and smell disgusting.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a tiger. The tiger was very tired. He had played all day with his friends. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. But now, it was time for the tiger to go home and rest. On his way home, the tired tiger met a big bear. The bear was not happy. He wanted to quarrel with the tiger. The bear said, "This is my path. You cannot walk here." The tired tiger did not want to fight. He just wanted to go home and sleep. So, the tired tiger said, "I am sorry, bear. I did not know this was your path. I will find another way to go home." The bear saw that the tiger was very tired. He felt bad for wanting to quarrel. The bear said, "You can use my path, tired tiger. I hope you have a good sleep." The tired tiger thanked the bear and walked home. That night, the tired tiger had a good sleep and dreamed of playing with his friends again.

Once upon a time there was a little gray bird. The bird was very sad because he had gotten stuck in a tree with no way of getting down. Luckily, a kind man saw the little bird in trouble and wanted to rescue him. The man ran to his house and came back with an apron. He held the apron out to the little bird. The bird hopped onto the corner of the apron and the man carried him back down to the ground carefully. The little bird was so grateful to be free and thanked the kind man with a happy chirp. Now the little bird can fly around and explore, safe and free in the sky!

One day, a lonely cat went for a walk. It saw a sign near a big tree. The sign had a picture of a cat and a plastic toy. The cat wanted to find the toy, so it walked and walked. The cat found the plastic toy under the tree. It was very happy. But, when the cat played with the toy, something unexpected happened. The toy started to move and talk! The toy was not a toy at all! It was a new cat friend. The lonely cat was not lonely anymore. They played together and became best friends. The sign was magic and helped the cat find a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big fat cat named Tom. Tom liked to play in the park. One day, Tom saw a shiny jewel under a tree. He wanted to take it home. A small bird saw Tom and the jewel. The bird tried to warn Tom. "No, Tom! Don't take the jewel! It's not yours," said the bird. But Tom did not listen. He took the jewel and ran away. Soon, Tom got lost. He felt sad and scared. The bird found Tom and said, "I told you not to take the jewel." Tom gave the jewel back to the bird. The bird helped Tom find his way home. Tom learned to listen and never took things that were not his again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One hot day, he saw a big ice cream truck. The ice cream truck had many colors and played a fun song. Tim knew it was time for a sweet treat. Tim said to the ice cream man, "I want a big ice cream cone, please!" The ice cream man smiled and said, "Okay, Tim! Are you ready for your ice cream?" Tim's eyes grew wide and he said, "Yes, I am ready!" Tim got his big ice cream cone and started to eat it. But it was so hot outside that the ice cream began to melt. Tim laughed and said, "Oh no, my ice cream is melting!" He quickly ate the rest of his ice cream before it all melted away. The end.
