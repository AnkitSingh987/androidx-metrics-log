Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Purple Pirate and the Ship

Once upon a time, there was a purple pirate named Bob. Bob lived on a big ship with his friends. They liked to find treasure and play games all day. One day, Bob's ship had a hole. It needed to be fixed. Bob and his friends worked together to fix the hole. They used wood and a big hammer. They hit the wood until the hole was gone. After they fixed the ship, they found a big treasure chest. They opened it and saw shiny gold coins inside. They were so happy! They danced and sang songs all day. And from that day on, the purple pirate Bob and his friends had lots of fun on their fixed ship.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. They saw a big, round melon. The cat said, "I want to eat the melon." The dog said, "I want to eat it too." They thought of a creative idea to share the melon. The cat and dog decided to unite and work together. They pushed the melon to a tree. They climbed the tree and jumped on the melon. The melon broke into two pieces. They were happy to share the melon. But, when they looked at the melon pieces, they saw a surprise. Inside the melon, there were many small toys. The cat and dog were very happy. They played with the toys and ate the melon. They learned that working together is fun and can bring good surprises.

Sam liked to listen to stories. His mom read him stories every night before bed. He liked stories about animals, and magic, and adventures. He had many books with pictures and words. One night, mom read him a story about a rabbit and a turtle. The rabbit was very fast, and the turtle was very slow. The rabbit was mean to the turtle, and said he could beat him in a race. The turtle said he would try, and they made a bet. Sam was excited to hear what would happen. Mom turned the page, and showed Sam a picture of the rabbit and the turtle at the start of the race. The rabbit ran very fast, and left the turtle behind. He was so sure he would win, he decided to take a nap under a tree. The turtle kept walking, and did not give up. He passed the sleeping rabbit, and reached the finish line. He won the race, and the bet. Sam was happy for the turtle, and thought the rabbit was silly. Mom closed the book, and said it was time for bed. She kissed Sam on his skin, and tucked him in. She said good night, and turned off the light. Sam closed his eyes, and thought about the story. He wondered if he could be like the turtle, and do hard things. But he did not sleep well. He had a bad dream. He dreamed he was the rabbit, and he was running from a big fox. The fox was hungry, and wanted to eat him. He ran and ran, but he could not find a place to hide. He saw a tree, and thought he could climb it. But the tree was too high, and he could not reach the branches. He was scared, and upset. He woke up, and cried. He wanted his mom. He called her name, and she came. She heard him cry, and came to his room. She saw him shaking, and hugged him. She asked him what was wrong, and he told her his dream. She said it was only a dream, and it was not real. She said he was safe, and she loved him. She read him another story, a happy story, and sang him a song. He felt better, and calmed down. He thanked her, and said he loved her too. He fell asleep, and dreamed of nice things.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big box. She did not know what was in it. It was a mystery. Mia was very curious. She wanted to open the box and see what was inside. Mia was too small to open the box by herself. She felt anxious. She needed help. So, she went to her big brother, Tom. She asked him, "Can you lend me your help to open the box?" Tom said, "Yes, I will help you." Together, Mia and Tom opened the big box. They found many fun toys and games inside. They played with the toys and had a very good time. Mia was happy that Tom lent her his help. The mystery was solved, and they were not anxious anymore.
