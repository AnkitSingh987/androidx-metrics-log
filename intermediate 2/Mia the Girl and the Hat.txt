Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Hat

Once upon a time, in a land with many seasons, there lived a little girl named Mia. She loved to dress up in pretty clothes. Every season, she would wear something new and fun. One day, during a wild and windy season, Mia decided to go outside and play. She put on her favorite dress and her big hat. She felt very happy and excited. She ran around, laughing and playing with the leaves that blew in the wind. As the wild winds blew, Mia's hat flew off her head. She chased after it, giggling and having fun. At last, she caught her hat and put it back on her head. Mia went back home, tired but happy from her adventure in the wild season.

One day, a little gray cat named Tiny needed clay to make a toy. She went to the store to buy some clay. At the store, she saw a big dog who also wanted clay. The big dog took all the clay and left none for Tiny. Tiny was sad and didn't know what to do. Tiny had an idea. She went to the park and found a big pile of clay. She was so happy! Tiny started to make her toy, but then something unexpected happened. The clay started to move! It was not just clay, but a gray clay monster! The clay monster was not mean, but just wanted to play. Tiny and the monster played together and had lots of fun. They made many toys with the clay. In the end, Tiny and the gray clay monster became best friends, and they shared the clay with the big dog too. Everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a huge lion. The lion had a big, loud roar. One day, the lion was walking around when he saw a small, pretty angel. The angel was lost and scared. The huge lion wanted to help her. So, he roared very loud to call all his friends in the forest. The birds, the rabbits, and the other animals came running. The animals helped the angel find her way back home. The angel was happy and thanked the huge lion and his friends. From that day, the lion and the angel became best friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a brush on the floor. She was very happy because she could use it to brush her doll's hair. She went to her room and started to play. Sue's mom came in and said, "Sue, you cannot use that brush. It is dirty and not safe for you. I do not permit you to use it." Sue felt sad because she couldn't brush her doll's hair. She put the brush down and went to find something else to play with. While Sue was playing, her dad came into the room with a big smile. He had a surprise for her. He gave her a new, clean brush! Sue was so happy and said, "Thank you, Dad!" She could now brush her doll's hair without worrying. The conflict was over, and Sue and her doll had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, a big radish lived in a garden. It was very big and red. One day, a girl saw the radish and wanted to eat it. She tried to pull it out. But the radish was too big and strong. The girl was confused. She did not know what to do. The girl asked her friends to help her. They all came and tried to pull the radish out. But the radish was still too big and strong. Everyone was confused. They did not know what to do. Then, a big storm came to the garden. The wind blew and the rain fell. The radish was scared. The storm pulled the radish out of the ground. The girl and her friends were sad. They could not eat the big radish now. The storm took it away. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, it was very hot and Tim felt very thirsty. He wanted to drink something cool to make him feel better. Tim went to his mom and asked for a cool drink. His mom gave him a glass of vegetable juice. She said it would make him feel better and strong. Tim drank the vegetable juice and felt cool inside. But then, Tim's friends came to his house to play. They saw the vegetable juice and didn't like it. They said it was yucky and didn't want to play with Tim anymore. Tim felt sad and alone because his friends didn't want to play with him.
