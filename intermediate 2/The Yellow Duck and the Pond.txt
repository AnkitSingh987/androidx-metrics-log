Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow Duck and the Pond

Once there was a yellow duck who lived near a pond. Every day, the duck paddled around the pond and splashed in the water. He loved being outdoors, bathing in the sun. One day, the duck spotted something in the pond. It looked like a bright, round object. He quacked loudly and paddled to see what it was. It was a yellow ball! The duck paddled closer and grabbed the ball between his little feet. Suddenly, a big duck swam out from under a big rock. It quacked angrily and rushed towards the little duck. The little duck panicked and threw the ball as hard as he could back into the water. The big duck acted in a fierce manner, quacking and flapping its wings. The little duck was so scared that it hopped out of the pond and cried loudly! It was so terrified of the big duck that it never returned to the pond. The little duck's outing ended in a bad way. It had to say goodbye to its favourite swimming spot, and it never got to play with the yellow ball.

Lily was a girl who liked to play with dolls and sing songs. She had many friends at school and they always had fun together. Lily was popular because she was kind and shared her toys. One day, Lily saw a new girl in her class. Her name was Anna and she looked sad. She did not have any friends and she sat alone at lunch. Lily wanted to help Anna and make her happy. "Hi, Anna. I am Lily. Do you want to play with me?" Lily asked. Anna looked up and smiled. "Yes, please. I am new here and I don't know anyone. Thank you, Lily." Lily and Anna played with dolls and sang songs. They had a lot in common and they became friends. Lily introduced Anna to her other friends and they welcomed her. Anna was not sad anymore and she was grateful to Lily. Lily and Anna were happy and popular. They played with dolls and sang songs every day. They were kind and shared their toys. They were the best of friends.

One day, a small cat named Kitty went for a walk. Kitty saw a bin and wanted to play with it. She pushed the bin and it rolled down the street. Kitty laughed and chased after the bin. As Kitty played, the sun started to melt an ice cream cone on the ground. A boy named Tim saw the melting ice cream and felt sad. He said, "My ice cream is melting. I wish I could eat it." Kitty heard Tim and wanted to help. She rolled the bin over to Tim and said, "Put your ice cream in the bin. It will not melt on the ground." Tim put his ice cream in the bin and smiled. He said, "Thank you, Kitty." Kitty and Tim became good friends. They learned that helping others can make you feel happy and good inside.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big pond with many fish. The fish are bright and shiny. Lily and Tom want to swim with the fish. They take off their shoes and socks. They roll up their pants. They hold hands and walk into the water. The water is cold and wet. The fish swim around them. Lily and Tom laugh and splash. They try to touch the fish, but the fish are fast and smart. After a while, Lily and Tom are hungry. They go back to the shore. They dry their feet with a towel. They put on their shoes and socks. They find a bench and sit down. They eat their sandwiches and apples. They share their food with some ducks. The ducks are happy and quack. Lily and Tom are happy too. They had fun swimming with the fish. They hug and say goodbye. They go home with their moms. They tell them about their adventure. They can't wait to go back to the pond.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived an elderly man. He was old but very wise. Everyone in the village would admire him. One sunny day, a little boy went to the old man's house. He asked the elderly man, "Why do people admire you so much?" The old man smiled and said, "I will tell you a secret. But first, you must do something for me." The little boy agreed. The old man asked the boy to bring him a big, red apple. The boy went to the tree and saw the apple was very high. He tried and tried but could not reach it. Then, a tall girl came by and saw the boy. She helped him get the apple. The boy thanked the girl and took the apple to the old man. The elderly man took the apple and said, "The secret is to always help others. When we help others, they will admire us." The boy learned the moral value and promised to help others too. And from that day on, the little boy became admired just like the old man.
