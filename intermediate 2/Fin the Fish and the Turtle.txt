Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in the big ocean. He loved to swim and play with his friends. One day, Fin saw something new in the water. He wanted to know what it was, so he swam closer to examine it. As Fin got closer, he started to feel anxious. He had never seen something like this before. It was big and round with many colors. Fin's friends came to look too. They all wondered what this thing could be. Fin and his friends decided to push the big round thing to their wise old friend, Mr. Turtle. Mr. Turtle looked at it and smiled. He told them it was a beach ball from the land. Fin and his friends were happy to learn something new. They played with the beach ball all day, and their anxious feelings went away. They had so much fun in the big ocean.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They loved to play together. Tim was always fair when he played with Spot. He would throw the ball and Spot would run to get it. They were very happy. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue had a cat named Fluffy. Tim asked, "Can Fluffy play with us?" Sue said, "Yes, but Fluffy doesn't like to run. She likes to behave and sit." Tim and Sue played with Spot and Fluffy. They all had fun. Then, they heard a loud voice. It was a big bird! The bird said, "I want to play too!" Tim, Sue, Spot, and Fluffy were surprised. They never heard a bird talk before. The bird played with them and they all had more fun. The bird was fair and nice. They all became good friends.

One day, Tim and Sue went to a park. They saw a big fountain. The water went up and down. Tim said, "Look, Sue! The fountain is so pretty!" Sue looked at the fountain and said, "Yes, it is! What can we do with the fountain?" Tim thought for a moment. He said, "Let's play a game! We can see who can throw a stone into the fountain." They found some small stones and started to play. They were very alert, watching the stones go into the water. A duck came to the fountain. It was a friendly duck. Sue said, "Hi, duck! Do you want to play with us?" The duck quacked and jumped into the fountain. Tim and Sue laughed and clapped. They had a fun day at the park with the duck and the big fountain.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside, but today it was raining. He looked out the window and saw big raindrops falling from the sky. Tim put on his raincoat and boots, and he went outside. He saw a little red car and decided to drive it around in the rain. As he drove, he splashed through puddles and laughed. Tim saw a little girl named Sue playing in the rain too. Sue asked, "Can I drive your car?" Tim said, "Yes, let's drive together!" They took turns driving the car, splashing in puddles, and having fun in the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Tom. Tom liked to serve food to his animal friends. One day, Tom wanted to make a big cake for a party. Tom needed to measure all the things for the cake. He used a big cup to measure the sugar, the milk, and the flour. But Tom was clumsy, and he dropped the cup. The cup broke, and Tom was sad. Tom's friend, a smart bird named Sue, saw Tom was sad. Sue had an idea! She told Tom to use his paws to measure. Tom tried it, and it worked! They made the cake together. At the party, Tom served the cake, and everyone loved it. The twist was that Tom didn't need a cup to measure, and he became the best baker in the forest.

Lily liked to watch the pigeons in the park. They had gray and white feathers and made funny noises. Sometimes they flew away and sometimes they came back. Lily wondered where they went. One day, Lily saw a man with a big bag. He took out some letters and gave them to some pigeons. The pigeons had little rings on their legs. The man said, "Fly, my pigeons, fly! You are my mail birds. You take the letters to far places." Lily was curious. She ran to the man and asked, "What are you doing? Why do you give letters to pigeons?" The man smiled and said, "Hello, little girl. I am a pigeon keeper. I train my pigeons to carry messages. They are very smart and fast. They can find their way to any place I tell them. They are my friends and helpers." Lily thought that was very interesting. She said, "Can I see your pigeons? Can I touch them? Can I give them a letter?" The man nodded and said, "Sure, you can. But be gentle and quiet. They are shy and gentle too. And if you want to give them a letter, you have to write something nice and short. They can only carry small papers." Lily was happy. She took a piece of paper and a crayon from her backpack. She wrote, "Hi, I am Lily. I like pigeons. You are cool. Bye." She folded the paper and gave it to the man. The man put it in a small envelope and tied it to a pigeon's leg. He said, "This pigeon is called Ruby. She is very brave and loyal. She will take your letter to my friend in another town. He will write back to you and send it with another pigeon. Do you want to see her fly?" Lily said, "Yes, please!" She watched as the man threw Ruby into the air. Ruby flapped her wings and soared into the sky. She looked like a tiny dot in the blue. Lily waved and said, "Bye, Ruby! Thank you for taking my letter!" The man said, "You are welcome, Lily. You are a very nice and polite girl. I hope you get a reply soon. Maybe you can come back and visit me and my pigeons again." Lily said, "I would love that. You and your pigeons are very interesting. Thank you for showing me your mail birds." The man said, "You are welcome, Lily. Have a good day." Lily said, "You too." She ran back to her mom and told her everything. She was very excited and happy. She hoped to see Ruby and the other pigeons again. She wondered what her letter said. She wondered what the other town was like. She learned something new and made a new friend. She had a great day.
