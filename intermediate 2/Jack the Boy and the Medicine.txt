Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Boy and the Medicine

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He had a big heart and was always cheerful. One day, Jack was feeling a bit sick, so his parents gave him some medicine. "Take the medicine, Jack!" said his dad. Jack frowned. He didn't want to take the medicine. "It'll make you feel better, Jack," said his mum. She encouraged him with a big smile. Jack took one sip of the medicine and soon his smile returned. "Thanks for encouraging me, Mum," said Jack. "Anytime, Jack," said his mum, still smiling. Jack felt much better after taking the medicine. He was cheerful again and ready to have some more fun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a man who could create useful things. He liked to make things from steel. One day, he made a big steel box. He thought it would be good for holding toys. A little boy saw the box and wanted to play with it. He opened the box and found a small steel key inside. The little boy was very curious about the key. He showed it to the man and asked, "What does this key do?" The man was surprised. He didn't remember making the key. They looked around and saw a small steel door. The little boy used the key to open the door. Inside, they found a room full of toys! The man and the little boy were very happy. They played with the toys all day and had lots of fun. The steel box was more useful than they thought.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big ball on the grass. It was red and blue and yellow. "Let's kick the ball!" Lily said. "OK!" Ben said. They took turns to kick the ball. They kicked it high and low and far and near. They had fun. Then they saw something in the sky. It was a rainbow. It had many colors. It was bright and beautiful. "Wow! Look at the rainbow!" Ben said. "It's so pretty!" Lily said. They ran to the rainbow. They wanted to touch it. But it was too far away. They tried to kick the ball to the rainbow. But the ball could not reach it. They felt sad. Then they heard a voice. It was a fairy. She was flying near the rainbow. She had wings and a wand. "Hello, children!" the fairy said. "Why are you sad?" "We want to touch the rainbow, but we can't," Lily said. The fairy smiled. She waved her wand. She said some magic words. "Zim zam zoom! I give you a gift. You can kick the ball to the rainbow. But you have to be careful. The ball is very powerful. It can break things. Only kick it once. Then it will come back to you." Lily and Ben were happy. They thanked the fairy. They kicked the ball to the rainbow. The ball flew in the air. It touched the rainbow. It made a big splash. The rainbow sparkled. Lily and Ben clapped their hands. They saw the ball coming back to them. They caught it. They looked at the fairy. She winked at them. She flew away. Lily and Ben hugged each other. They were glad. They had a magical day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He loved to explore and play all day. One day, while he was playing outside his house, he looked up and saw a window. He had never seen one before, so he was very excited. He wanted to look through the window. He stood on tip toes and peeked in. As he looked inside he saw a big, furry dog. He had never seen such a big dog before, and he was very frightened. He ran away from the window very quickly. When he got back to his house, he told his parents about the window and the big dog. They laughed and said the dog was very friendly. The boy was still a bit frightened but was excited to get to know the dog. The next day, he walked back to the window with his parents. He was a bit scared but also excited, and the dog was still there. He made friends with the dog, and his parents said it was safe to pet him. The little boy was no longer frightened. Every day he excitedly looked out of his window for the big, furry dog. He had a new friend!
