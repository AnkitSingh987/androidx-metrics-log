Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Ball

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. They had a big ball that they could kick and throw and catch. One day, they decided to go for a walk with their ball in the park. They saw many things on their way: trees, flowers, birds, and a pond. They came to a bridge that crossed the pond. They wanted to see the fish and the ducks in the water. They leaned over the railing and looked down. Tom held the ball in his hand. He saw a big fish with a shiny tail. He pointed at it and said, "Look, Lily, a fish!" But as he pointed, he dropped the ball. It bounced on the bridge and rolled into the pond. Tom and Lily gasped. They watched as the ball sank into the water and disappeared. They felt very sad. They had lost their ball. They tried to find a way to get their ball back. They looked for a stick or a net or a boat. But they could not find anything. They walked along the pond, hoping to see their ball again. But they only saw a trail of bubbles where the ball had gone. They walked back to the bridge, feeling very unhappy. They wished they had not dropped the ball. They wished they had not gone to the pond. They wished they had stayed at home and played with their ball. They did not feel cheerful anymore. They felt like crying. They had no ball to play with. They had a bad day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a dog named Max. Max was big and loved to shake. He would shake his tail when he was happy or when he wanted to play. Max had a lot of love for his family. One day, Max was feeling impatient. He wanted to play with his family, but they were all busy. So, Max went out to the yard to play by himself. He saw a ball and started to shake it. As he shook the ball, something unexpected happened. The ball started to shake too! Max was surprised, but he loved this new friend. The ball shook and danced with Max all day long. They played and had so much fun together. Max learned that even when things don't go as planned, you can still find love and friendship in unexpected places.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little car named Zoomy. Zoomy loved to zoom around the town, making people smile. One day, Zoomy saw a race track and thought it would be fun to join the race. Zoomy went to the race track and met a big car named Biggie. Biggie said, "I am the fastest car here. No one can beat me." Zoomy wanted to try and win, so they both got ready to race. The race started and Zoomy and Biggie zoomed as fast as they could. Zoomy was very fast and soon passed Biggie. At the end of the race, Zoomy won and got the victory. Biggie said, "Wow, you are very fast! Good job, Zoomy!" They both became friends and zoomed around the town together.

Once upon a time, there was a blue chicken. The chicken lived on a farm with other animals. The chicken liked to play with his friends, the cow and the pig. One day, the blue chicken found a magic hat. When he put the hat on, he could change! He changed into a big, blue dog. The cow and the pig were very surprised. They did not know it was the chicken. The blue chicken, now a dog, played with the cow and the pig. They had lots of fun. But then, the magic hat fell off. The blue chicken changed back. The cow and the pig were happy to see their friend again. They all laughed and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a poor boy named Tim. Tim was hot all the time because he did not have a fan. He wished for a fan to make him feel cool. One day, Tim saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. He thought the tree could provide shade to help him feel cool. Tim went to the tree and sat under it. The shade from the tree made Tim feel a little cooler. He was very happy. But then, dark clouds came and covered the sky. Tim did not know what was going to happen next. Suddenly, it started to rain very hard. Tim got very wet and cold. The tree could not provide enough shelter for him anymore. He missed his wish for a fan. Tim ran back home, shivering and sad. The tree could not help him stay cool and dry after all.
