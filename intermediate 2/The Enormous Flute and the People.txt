Category: Intermediate
Title: The Enormous Flute and the People

Once upon a time, there was an enormous flute. It was the most beautiful flute in the world. Everywhere it went, people admired it and wanted to take a closer look. One day while the flute was out, it was stolen. The people were very sad and nobody knew what had happened. The thief was very sneaky and nobody could number the flute. The police searched and searched, but they could not find it. The thief had hidden it very well. Eventually the police gave up and the flute was gone forever. The people had to accept that the enormous flute was never coming back. This was a very sad ending indeed.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful forest, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a best friend, a cute bunny named Hoppy. They played together every day. One day, Hoppy got lost in the forest. Lily looked for him and called out, "Hoppy, where are you?" Hoppy heard her and cried, "Lily, help me! I'm stuck!" Lily found Hoppy caught in a bush. She saw a big brush nearby and had an idea. Lily used the brush to rescue Hoppy from the bush. She brushed away the leaves and branches. Hoppy was free! He said, "Thank you, Lily! You saved me!" They hugged and went back to playing in the beautiful forest.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a red ball. She loved to play with it. She would throw the ball, kick the ball, and let the ball roll down the hill. Amy had so much fun with her red ball. One day, while playing with her red ball, Amy fell down. She hurt her knee and it was bleeding. Amy's mom came running to help her. She cleaned Amy's knee and put a bandage on it. The bandage was red, just like Amy's ball. Amy's mom told her to be more careful when playing. Amy promised she would. She let her knee get better and then went back to playing with her red ball. Now, Amy always remembers to be careful when playing, and she keeps a red bandage with her, just in case.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park with their mom. They saw a big truck with a loud siren and a long hose. It was a fire truck. The fire truck stopped near a house that had smoke and fire. Ben and Lily wanted to see what was happening. "Stay here with me," mom said. "The fire truck is here to help. The fire is very hot and dangerous. We need to be safe and far away." But Ben and Lily were curious. They ran behind a tree and peeked at the house. They saw some men in yellow suits and helmets. They had masks on their faces and axes in their hands. They were firemen. They went inside the house with the hose. They were brave. Then Ben and Lily heard a loud bark. It was a dog. The dog was in the house. It was scared and trapped. It ran to the window and looked out. It had a wide mouth and big eyes. It wanted to get out. "Look, mom, a dog!" Ben and Lily shouted. They felt sorry for the dog. They wanted to help. "Stay back, kids," one of the firemen said. He saw the dog too. He put down his axe and ran to the window. He broke the glass with his hand. He reached for the dog. The dog was happy. It licked his face. The fireman lifted the dog and carried it outside. He knelt down and put the dog on the ground. He checked if the dog was hurt. The dog was fine. It wagged its tail and barked. "Good boy, good boy," the fireman said. He gave the dog a hug. Ben and Lily clapped their hands. They were glad the dog was safe. They ran to the fireman and the dog. They asked if they could pet the dog. "Sure, kids, but be gentle," the fireman said. He smiled. He was kind. Ben and Lily knelt down and stroked the dog's fur. The dog licked their faces too. They laughed. They made a new friend. Mom came over and thanked the fireman. She was proud of him. She was also proud of Ben and Lily. She hugged them and said they were good kids. They all watched as the fire truck put out the fire. The house was saved. The dog's owner came and thanked the firemen too. He was happy to see his dog. He thanked Ben and Lily for caring. They all said goodbye and went their ways. Ben and Lily waved to the fire truck as it drove away. They had a fun and exciting day. They learned a lot. They learned about fire trucks, firemen, and dogs. They learned about danger, bravery, and kindness. They learned to kneel and to care.
