Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Helpful Sheep and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a helpful sheep named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to help all of her friends on the farm. One day, while Fluffy was helping her friend, the cow, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and said, "I need help! I lost my nest!" Fluffy wanted to help the bird, so she asked, "Where did you last see your nest?" The bird replied, "I saw it near the big tree." Fluffy and the bird went to the big tree to look for the nest. When they got to the tree, they saw a surprise. The nest was not lost! The bird's nest was on Fluffy's head the whole time! The bird and Fluffy laughed and laughed. Fluffy was happy to help her new friend, and they played together all day.

One day, a little dog named Sam was sad. He was sad because he had no friends to play with. Sam decided to go out and look for new friends. He walked and walked, hoping to find someone to play with. On his walk, Sam met a big cat named Max. Max was patient and nice. Sam asked Max if he wanted to be friends. Max said yes, and they played together all day. They played with a ball and ran in the grass. But then, it started to rain. Sam and Max were sad because they could not play outside anymore. They decided to go home. Sam said bye to his new friend Max and went back to his house. But when he got home, he was still sad. Sam missed his friend Max and wished they could play together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a cat. This cat was very ordinary. It had soft fur, small ears, and a long tail. Every day, the cat would sit by the window and look outside. One day, while the cat was sitting by the window, it saw a big tree. The tree had many branches and leaves. The cat wanted to climb the tree. It knew that one day, it would go outside and climb that tree. Days went by, and the cat kept sitting by the window, looking at the tree. Then, one sunny day, the door was open. The cat saw its chance and went outside. It ran to the tree and started to climb. Up and up it went, higher and higher. At the top of the tree, the cat found a big, comfy nest. The cat was so happy it had climbed the tree. It sat in the nest, feeling proud and looking at its home from high up. The ordinary cat had an extraordinary adventure, all because it never stopped dreaming about that tree.

One day, Joey was playing in the living room. He saw a modern chair that he had never seen before. He ran up to the chair, excited to try it out. But his mom said, "Joey, ignore the chair. We can't sit in it, it's for decoration only". Joey didn't understand why he couldn't sit in the chair. He asked his mom why, but she just kept saying that he had to ignore it. Even though Joey didn't understand, he did what his mom said and ignored the modern chair. Later that day, his dad came home and said, "Why is this beautiful, modern chair just sitting here? It's perfect to sit in!" His mom replied, "Joey wanted to sit in it, but I told him to ignore it". After his mom finished explaining, his dad smiled and said to Joey, "Come on, let's take a break and sit in the modern chair together". Joey was so excited and quickly ran over to sit in the modern chair with his dad. After sitting in the chair, Joey understood why his mom asked him to ignore it, it was just for looking at, not sitting in!

Lily and Ben are going to the park with Mom. They are very happy. They put on their coats and shoes. Mom helps them get in the car. She says, "You have to buckle your seat belts. It is safe and good." Lily and Ben find their buckles. They try to snap them together. It is hard. Lily says, "Mom, can you help me? My buckle is stuck." Mom says, "Sure, sweetie. Let me see." She reaches over and fixes Lily's buckle. She says, "There you go. Good job, Lily." Ben says, "Mom, can you help me too? My buckle is too tight." Mom says, "Of course, honey. Let me loosen it a bit." She pulls on the strap and makes Ben's buckle more comfortable. She says, "There you go. Good job, Ben." Lily and Ben smile. They say, "Thank you, Mom. We love you." Mom says, "I love you too, my darlings. You are so pretty and smart." She starts the car and drives to the park. Lily and Ben are excited. They can't wait to play on the swings and slides. They are going to have a lot of fun.
