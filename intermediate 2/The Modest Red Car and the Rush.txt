Category: Intermediate
Title: The Modest Red Car and the Rush

Once upon a time, there was a modest red car. The car had four big, round tires. It liked to rush down the street, going very fast. The car went "vroom, vroom" as it moved. One day, the car had a flat tire. It could not rush anymore. The car was very sad. It needed help to fix the tire. A kind man saw the car and came to help. He took off the flat tire and put on a new one. The car was so happy! It could rush down the street again. The car said "thank you" to the man and drove away with a big smile.

Once there was a penny that wanted to dress up. So it followed a scary path. Along the way it saw darkness, howling winds and strange noises. Eventually, the penny made it to a big wardrobe. But it couldn't find any clothes to dress in. So the penny sat sad and alone in the wardrobe until the moon came out that night. When it did, the penny turned into a ghost. It realized it would never dress up ever again.

Mia and Leo like to pretend. They pretend to be pirates, doctors, and astronauts. Yesterday, they pretended to be chefs. They used pots, pans, and spoons from the kitchen. They also used some food from the fridge. "Look, I made a cake!" Mia said, holding a plate with bread, cheese, and ketchup. "Do you want some?" "No, thank you," Leo said, making a face. "I made soup. It has carrots, milk, and sugar. Try it!" Mia took a sip of the soup. She spit it out. "Yuck! That's not soup, that's gross!" she said, throwing the plate on the floor. Leo got angry. He threw his pot on the floor too. The pot was big and heavy. It broke the floor. It made a loud noise. Mom and Dad heard the noise. They came to the kitchen. They saw the mess. They saw the broken floor. They were very mad. "Mia and Leo, what did you do?" Mom asked, her voice stern. "We were pretending to be chefs," Mia said, looking down. "That's not pretending, that's wasting food and breaking things!" Dad said, his voice loud. "Do you know how expensive the floor is? Do you know how hard we work to buy food?" Mia and Leo felt bad. They said sorry. But Mom and Dad were still mad. They said Mia and Leo had to clean up the mess. They also said Mia and Leo had to go to bed early. They could not watch TV or read books. They could not pretend anymore. Mia and Leo cried. They did not like the bad ending. They wished they had pretended something else.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little star named Twinkle. Twinkle was a friendly star who loved to join her friends, the birds and animals, in their fun games. One sunny day, Twinkle saw her friends, the bunny and the squirrel, playing hide and seek. She wanted to join them, so she asked, "Can I play too?" The bunny and the squirrel were happy to have Twinkle join their game. They played all day, laughing and having fun. When it was time for Twinkle to go back to the sky, she said goodbye to her friends. As the night came, Twinkle shined brightly in the sky, and her green forest friends looked up at her, smiling. They couldn't wait to play together again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to design. She would draw on paper all day. One day, she decided to design something for her foot. Sue got some pretty paint and began to make attractive patterns on her foot. She used red, blue, and yellow. Her foot looked very nice. She showed her mom and dad, and they liked it too. Sue's friends saw her foot and wanted pretty feet too. So, Sue helped them design their feet. They all had fun and their feet looked great. They played together, showing off their attractive feet, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside. One day, he saw smoke in the sky. Spot was curious. Spot followed the smoke and saw a small fire. The fire was burning some sticks. Spot was scared, but he wanted to help. He ran to the water and got a big drink. He went back to the fire and put it out with the water. Spot was very happy. He had stopped the fire from burning more things. The smoke was gone, and everything was safe. Spot went home and played with his friends. They were all proud of him for being so brave.
