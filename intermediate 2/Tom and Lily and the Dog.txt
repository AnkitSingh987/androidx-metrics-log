Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Dog

Tom and Lily like to play games. They have a lot of fun games, like hide and seek, tag, and hopscotch. But their favorite game is lemon race. They each get a lemon and a spoon. They put the lemon on the spoon and hold it in their mouth. Then they try to run to the other side of the yard without dropping the lemon. The first one to get there wins. One day, they were playing lemon race with their friend Ben. Ben had a big, wide smile. He said he was very good at lemon race. He said he could win every time. Tom and Lily wanted to see if he was right. They got ready to start the race. They said, "One, two, three, go!" They all ran as fast as they could. They tried not to drop their lemons. But it was hard. The lemons were round and slippery. Tom dropped his lemon after a few steps. He said, "Oh no!" and picked it up. Lily dropped her lemon in the middle of the yard. She said, "Oops!" and picked it up. Ben kept running. He did not drop his lemon. He was almost at the end. He said, "Ha ha! I'm going to win!" But then, something happened. A dog came out of nowhere. The dog saw the lemons. The dog liked lemons. The dog ran after the lemons. The dog jumped on Ben. The dog knocked the lemon off his spoon. The dog grabbed the lemon in his mouth. The dog ran away with the lemon. Ben said, "Hey! That's my lemon!" Tom and Lily caught up with Ben. They saw the dog with the lemon. They laughed. They said, "Sorry, Ben. You did not win. The dog won." Ben was sad. He said, "That's not fair. The dog cheated." Tom and Lily said, "It's okay, Ben. It's just a game. We can play again. We have more lemons." Ben said, "Okay. But next time, I will win." They all smiled. They went to get more lemons. They played lemon race again. They had fun.

Lily liked to create things with her crayons and paper. She made pictures of her family, her friends, and her toys. She was very proud of her creations and showed them to everyone. One day, she wanted to create something bigger and more colorful. She found some paint and brushes in the garage and decided to use them. She did not ask her mom or dad, because she thought they would say no. She went to her room and started to paint on the walls. She made flowers, stars, and rainbows. She was having fun and did not notice the time. She did not hear the alarm that told her it was dinner time. Her mom came to her room and saw the messy paint all over the walls. She was very angry and sad. She said to Lily, "What have you done? You made a big mess and you did not listen to the alarm. You have to clean this up and say sorry. You cannot use paint without permission. Paint is not for walls, it is for paper." Lily felt bad and realized she made a mistake. She said to her mom, "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to create something beautiful, but I did not think about the rules. I will help you clean the mess and I will not use paint again without asking. I love you, mom." Her mom hugged her and said, "I love you too, Lily. I'm glad you like to create, but you have to be careful and respectful. Next time, you can use crayons and paper, or we can find some other materials that are safe and fun. Now, let's go have dinner and then we can clean together." The moral of the story is: Creating is good, but you have to follow the rules and respect your parents and your things.

Once upon a time, there was a big tank. The tank was very strong. It could go through anything. The tank lived in a town with many people. The people were scared of the tank because it was terrible. One day, the tank went to play with the other toys. The toys were scared of the tank. They did not want to play with it. The tank was sad. It did not want to be terrible. The tank wanted to be nice. The tank decided to help the people. It would not destroy things anymore. The tank helped the people build new houses. It helped them move big things. The people were happy. They were not scared of the tank anymore. The tank was not terrible. It was a good friend.
