Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Makeup

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to play with makeup. One day, she saw her mom's makeup and wanted to use it. She asked her mom, "Can I use your makeup, please?" Her mom said, "No, Lily, it is not for you. Promise me you will not touch it." Lily wanted to win a game with her friends. They were playing a competitive dress-up game. Lily thought if she used her mom's makeup, she would look the best and win. She forgot her promise and used the makeup anyway. When her mom came back, she saw Lily with makeup all over her face. Her mom was very sad and upset. Lily did not win the game, and she broke her promise. Her friends did not want to play with her anymore. Lily felt bad for not keeping her promise and for making her mom sad.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big wheel that kids could play on. Tim felt nervous because he had never played on a wheel before. His mom held his hand and said, "It's okay, I will help you." Tim climbed on the wheel with his mom's help. He started to spin the wheel slowly. As the wheel went faster, Tim began to feel happy. He was not nervous anymore. He yelled, "Mom, look at me! I am spinning the wheel!" Tim's mom smiled and clapped her hands. She was proud of Tim for trying something new. They played on the wheel together until it was time to go home. Tim felt brave and couldn't wait to come back to the park to play on the wheel again.

Once upon a time, there was a bright green frog. The frog lived in a small pond. The pond was his home. The frog liked to jump and play in the water all day. One day, a little girl came to the pond. She saw the bright frog and wanted to play with him. The frog was shy, but he wanted to be her friend too. The girl asked the frog, "Why are you so bright?" The frog tried to explain. He said, "I am bright so that my friends can see me when we play together." The little girl smiled and understood. She was happy to know why the frog was so bright. From that day on, the little girl and the bright frog became best friends. They played together at the pond every day. The frog was happy to have a friend who understood him, and the little girl loved her new bright frog friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy who liked to do things very fast. Every day she would run around her Mom and Dad's house as fast as she could. One day, Mom and Dad were watching Lucy and noticed that she was being very naughty. She was going way too fast for them to keep up with her. Dad said, "Lucy, you must behave and slow down." But Lucy didn't want to slow down. She wanted to keep running around as fast as she could. So, Dad and Mom decided to give her a speed limit. "Whenever you are in the house, you must only move at the speed of a red light," said Dad. "But, Dad," Lucy complained. "Moving at a red light speed is like being stuck in slow-motion!" Mom and Dad had to laugh at that. But they still made her promise to behave. And ever since that day, Lucy was very good and remembered to keep her speed to a red light's pace.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia thought her days were very boring. She wanted to see something fun and new. One day, Mia heard a loud noise. "Boom!" It was a firework! Mia had never seen a firework before. She ran outside to look at the pretty colors in the sky. Mia said, "Wow! It's so pretty!" Suddenly, Mia started to panic. She didn't know where her mom was. She looked around, but she couldn't see her mom. Mia began to cry. "Mommy, where are you?" she called. Then, Mia's mom came running to her. "I'm here, Mia!" her mom said. Mia hugged her mom tightly. They watched the fireworks together. Mia's day was not boring anymore.
