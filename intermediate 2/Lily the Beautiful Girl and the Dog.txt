Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Beautiful Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful girl named Lily. She loved to wear a pretty bracelet on her arm. One day, she went for a walk in the park. In the park, Lily met a boy named Tom. Tom saw her bracelet and said, "Wow, your bracelet is very nice!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, I like it too!" They played together and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, scary dog came and dared to take Lily's bracelet! Lily and Tom were scared. But Tom was brave. He said, "No, dog! Give it back!" The dog listened to Tom and gave the bracelet back. Lily was so happy and thanked Tom. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a new red car. The car loved to race. It would race all day with its friends. They raced in the sun and they raced in the rain. The new red car was very fast. One day, the new red car saw a big box. The box had a big bow on it. The car did not know what was in the box. It was a surprise! The new red car wanted to see the surprise. The new red car raced to the box. It raced as fast as it could. When it got to the box, the car opened the box. Inside the box was a new friend! It was a blue car. The new red car and the blue car raced together all day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a long bathtub in a big house. It was very long and shiny. Every day, the little girl who lived in the house would fill the bathtub with water and play with her toy boats. One day, a thirsty bird flew into the house. It saw the long bathtub full of water and wanted to drink. The bird sat on the side of the bathtub and started to drink the water. The little girl saw the bird and smiled. The next day, the little girl filled the bathtub again. She put a small cup of water on the side just for the bird. The bird came back, saw the cup, and drank from it. The bird was very happy and sang a beautiful song for the little girl. They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a strong cat named Tom went to the fridge. He wanted a snack. He saw a big bowl of water inside the fridge. Tom thought, "I can soak my toy in this water!" Tom took the bowl out and put his toy in the water. The toy got all wet. He was very happy. Then, Tom's friend, a dog named Max, came over. Max saw the wet toy and said, "Why is your toy wet?" Tom said, "I soaked it in the water from the fridge." Max looked at Tom and said, "That's not water, that's milk!" Tom was surprised. He didn't know it was milk. They both laughed and played with the wet toy.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the garage. Inside, they found an icy puddle. They wanted to play in it, but it was too cold. "Let's wash it away," said Tom. Sue agreed, so they ran to get a mop. With the mop, they began to wash the icy puddle. The water was cold, but they were having fun. They laughed and splashed. "This is fun!" said Sue. Tom nodded and smiled. But as they washed, the icy puddle grew bigger and bigger. Soon, the whole garage floor was slippery. They tried to stop, but it was too late. Tom and Sue slipped and fell. They both started to cry. The garage was a big mess, and they were wet and cold. The fun day had turned into a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house. In this house, there lived a girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to include her friends in everything she did. One day, Lucy and her friends went to the park. They saw a big tree with lots of fruits. They found a pear on the ground. Lucy said, "Let's share the pear and eat it together." They all sat down and Lucy cut the pear into pieces. Each friend got a piece of the pear. They ate the pear and it was very yummy. Lucy and her friends were happy to include everyone and share the tasty pear. They played together in the park all day and had lots of fun.
