Category: Intermediate
Title: Jimmy and the Bird

Jimmy went outside and saw his truck. It was bright red, and it made him very happy. He got in the truck and pretended to be a driver. He made 'brrrrr' noises to make it sound like the truck was driving. Suddenly, Jimmy saw a dead bird on the ground. He got out of the truck and pointed to it. "Mommy, look! A dead bird,"said Jimmy. "Yes, honey," said Mommy. She hugged him and said, "We don't have to pretend this bird is alive. We can be sad for him, but he isn't coming back." Jimmy nodded and got back into his truck. He pretended to drive away, far away, to a happier place.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to go for a walk every day. He felt very lucky to have a big park near his house. Max loved to run, jump, and play in the park. One sunny day, Max went for a walk in the park. He saw a big ball and ran to play with it. But Max started to struggle to push the ball. It was much bigger and heavier than he thought. Then, something unexpected happened. The big ball opened up and out came a new friend, a little cat named Luna. Max was so surprised! Luna had been hiding inside the ball. Max and Luna played together and became best friends. From that day on, Max felt even luckier to have a new friend to walk and play with in the park.

One day, a boy named Tom found a broken toy. It was a little car. Tom wanted to fix it. He asked his mom, "Can you help me fix the car?" His mom said, "Yes, but you have to pay me with a hug." Tom hugged his mom, and they began to fix the car. As they fixed it, they found a message inside the car. The message said, "I am a magic car. If you fix me, I will give you a big surprise." Tom and his mom were very excited. They worked hard and fixed the car. When they were done, Tom said, "Magic car, please give us the big surprise." The car started to shake and then it turned into a big monster! The monster was not nice. It took Tom's toys and broke them. Tom and his mom were sad. They wished they never found the magic car.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a magical toy bunny named Bun-Bun. Bun-Bun had a soft voice and could talk to Lucy. Lucy loved to play with Bun-Bun every day. One sunny day, Lucy and Bun-Bun were playing in the park. A jealous boy named Tim saw them and wanted to play with Bun-Bun too. Tim took Bun-Bun away from Lucy when she was not looking. Lucy was sad and could not find her friend. Lucy looked everywhere for Bun-Bun. Then, she heard Bun-Bun's voice calling her name. She found Tim holding Bun-Bun. Lucy was brave and asked Tim to give Bun-Bun back. Tim felt bad and said sorry. Suddenly, Bun-Bun used magic and vanished. Lucy and Tim were surprised! In the end, Bun-Bun came back to Lucy, and they all played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She had a big room with a bookcase full of books. Jane loved to gaze at the bookcase and dream about the stories inside the books. One day, Jane's friend, Tom, came to play. Tom saw the bookcase and felt envious. He wished he had a bookcase like Jane's. Jane saw Tom looking at the bookcase and had an idea. Jane said, "Let's read some books together!" Tom was happy and they spent the whole day reading and sharing stories. From that day on, Jane and Tom always read books and shared their love for stories.

Once upon a time, there was a proud dog named Max. Max loved to play and run all day with his friends. They would chase each other and roll in the grass, having lots of fun. One day, Max found a skull in the park. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it was a cool toy. Max picked it up in his mouth and showed it to his friends. They all liked the skull and took turns playing with it. After a long day of playing, Max and his friends were very tired. They found a big tree and decided to rest under it. Max put the skull next to him and lay down. They all had a nice nap, dreaming about more fun days to come.
