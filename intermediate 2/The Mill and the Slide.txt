Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mill and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a friendly mill. The mill had a big slide. Many kids loved to play on the slide every day. They would laugh and have fun together. One sunny day, the kids went to the mill to play. They saw a big box at the top of the slide. The kids were surprised. They did not know what was in the box. They climbed up to see. When they opened the box, they found a big, friendly dog. The dog was happy to see the kids. The dog played with the kids and went down the slide too. It was a fun day for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to climb trees. One day, she saw a big tree in the park. She wanted to go up the tree and see what was there. Kitty used her smart brain to imagine how to climb the tree. She thought, "I will jump and grab the branch. Then, I will pull myself up." Kitty did just that. Up, up, up she went until she was at the very top. From the top of the tree, Kitty saw her friends playing below. They saw her and said, "Wow, Kitty is so smart! She can climb the tree and see everything!" Kitty felt happy and proud. She knew that using her smart brain to imagine and plan helped her do something amazing. The moral of the story is: Use your smart brain to imagine and plan, and you can do great things.

One day, a little boy went to the beach. He saw a big, ugly jellyfish. The jellyfish was sad because it was so ugly. The little boy wanted to help the jellyfish. The boy had an idea to fill a bucket with water. He put the jellyfish in the bucket. The jellyfish was happy to be in the water. The boy took the jellyfish to a new home where it could be happy. In the new home, the jellyfish met other jellyfish. They did not think the jellyfish was ugly. They played and had fun in the water. The little boy was happy that he helped the jellyfish find new friends.

One day, a little bee was flying around. He was a lucky bee. He had a big hive with all his friends. The bee loved to find flowers and bring food back to his hive. The little bee saw a big flower. He did not recognize it. But he thought it was a good flower. So, he went to the flower to get some food. He was very happy. But the big flower was not a good flower. It was a bad flower. The little bee got stuck in the flower. He could not go back to his hive. The little bee was not lucky anymore.

Anna and Ben are twins. They love to play with their toys and their dog, Spot. Spot is a big, brown dog with a wagging tail. He likes to run and jump and lick their faces. One day, Anna and Ben hear their mom call them from the kitchen. "Time for dinner, kids!" she says. They put away their toys and run to the table. They see their dad sitting there. He is bald and has a big smile. He works at a hospital and helps sick people. "What's for dinner, mom?" Anna asks. "We have chicken, rice, and carrots. Your favorite!" mom says. "Yummy!" Ben says. He loves chicken and rice. He also loves carrots, because they are crunchy and orange. Anna and Ben sit next to their dad and mom. They say a prayer and thank God for their food and their family. Then they start to eat. They talk about their day and what they learned and what they did. They laugh and have fun. Spot sits under the table and waits for some scraps. He loves dinner time too, because he gets to eat some chicken and rice and carrots. He also loves Anna and Ben and their mom and dad. He is a happy dog. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Tom. Tom loved to play in the backyard. One day, Tom saw a big red ball in the backyard. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. Tom went to his friend, a dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, I found a big red ball! Let's play with it in the backyard." Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's play!" When they went to the backyard, they saw a little girl holding the big red ball. The little girl said, "This is my ball. I will release it, and you can play with it too!" She let the ball go, and it flew up into the sky! Tom and Max were very surprised. They jumped and tried to catch the ball, but it was too high. The little girl laughed and said, "Don't worry, it will come back down soon!" Tom, Max, and the little girl played with the big red ball all day in the backyard. They had so much fun and became good friends. And they all learned that sometimes, unexpected things can bring people together.
