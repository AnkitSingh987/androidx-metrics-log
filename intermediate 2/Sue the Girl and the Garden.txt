Title: Sue the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue lived on a farm with her mom and dad. They had a big garden where they grew many things to eat. One day, Sue looked at the garden and saw that the earth was very dry. She wanted to make it wet so the plants could grow. Sue went to her mom and said, "Mom, the earth is dry. Can we make it wet?" Her mom looked at the sky and said, "Yes, Sue. We need rain to make the earth wet. Let's do a rain dance and hope the rain comes." Sue and her mom danced in the garden, hoping for rain. They danced and danced, but the rain did not come. The sun shone brighter and the earth became even more dry. The plants in the garden started to die. One day, Sue's dad came home and saw the dying plants. He was sad and said, "Oh no, our garden is dying. We need rain to make the earth wet and help the plants grow." Sue and her mom looked at each other and knew that they had tried their best. The rain never came and the garden died. Sue, her mom, and her dad were very sad. They wished they could make the earth wet, but sometimes things just don't go the way we want them to.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house with their dog. One day, the man wanted to measure how tall his wife was. He thought it would be a fun game to play. The man said, "Stand up, wife. I will measure you with this stick." The wife laughed and said, "You are a little bit crazy, but okay!" She stood very straight, and the man measured her with the stick. The dog barked and wagged its tail, happy to see them play. After they measured, they all went outside to play in the sun. The man, the wife, and the dog had a great day. They laughed and ran around together. They were a happy family.

Emily was a chubby three-year-old girl. She liked to play in the garden and explore the world around her. One day, when Emily was in the garden, she found a puddle. She was so excited that she kneeled down and put her hands in the puddle. She cupped the water in her hands and made little splashes on the ground. The water felt very nice and Emily was so happy. She stayed in the garden for hours and played with the puddle. She splashed around and laughed until she was very tired. Finally, Emily kneeled down and said goodbye to the puddle. She waved goodbye and looked forward to coming back and playing in the puddle again.

One day, a little dog named Spot wanted to play. He saw his friend, the big cat named Fluffy. Spot ran to Fluffy and said, "Welcome, Fluffy! Let's play!" Fluffy looked at Spot and said, "No, I don't want to play. You are rude!" Spot felt sad, but he wanted to make Fluffy happy. Spot had an idea. He found a bottle that could spray water. He sprayed the water on Fluffy. Fluffy laughed and said, "That is fun! Now I want to play with you!" Spot and Fluffy played together all day. They were very happy. And from that day on, they were always nice to each other.
