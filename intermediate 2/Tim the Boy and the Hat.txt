Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hat

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw an old man sitting on a bench. The old man looked sad. Tim wanted to make him happy. Tim asked his mom if he could ride his bike to the old man. His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim rode his bike to the old man and said, "Hi! Welcome to the park! Do you want to play with me?" The old man smiled and said, "Thank you, but I am too old to play. I like to watch kids have fun." Just then, a big wind came and blew Tim's hat off his head. The old man was quick! He caught the hat before it flew away. Tim was surprised and said, "Wow! You are fast!" The old man laughed and said, "Maybe I am not too old to play after all." They played together and had a great day. The moral of the story is that you can always find a way to make someone feel welcome and happy, no matter how old they are.

One day, a little chicken was walking in the yard. It was a sunny day, and the chicken was happy. The chicken saw a big tree and wanted to play under it. But then, a bitter wind blew, and the chicken felt cold. The chicken met a friend, a little duck. The duck said, "Why are you cold, chicken? Let's play together and warm up." The chicken agreed, and they played under the big tree. As they played, the wind stopped, and they felt warm again. But then, something unexpected happened! A big dog came, and he wanted to play too. The dog said, "Can I join you?" The chicken and the duck were scared at first, but they decided to let the dog play. They all played together and had a lot of fun. The dog helped delay the wind by standing in front of the chicken and the duck. They all became good friends and played together every day, even on bitter days.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Max saw a gift near a tree. He was very happy and wanted to see what was inside the gift. Max opened the gift and found a magic toy. The magic toy could make things freeze. Max thought it was fun and wanted to show his friends. So, Max took the magic toy to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Max showed his friends the magic toy. They all took turns making things freeze. They froze a ball, a flower, and even a puddle. Max and his friends had so much fun with the magic toy. At the end of the day, they all went home happy and tired.

Once upon a time, there was a dull, grey rocket. The rocket wanted to follow a bright, shiny star in the sky. The rocket was very happy when it could see the star. One day, the dull rocket saw a big, fast missile. The missile was going to the same shiny star. The rocket was sad because it could not follow the star as fast as the missile. But the shiny star saw the dull rocket and the fast missile. The star liked the rocket more because it was kind and slow. So, the star came down to meet the rocket, and they became best friends. The rocket was very happy and the missile went away.

The little girl wanted to make her mommy happy. She saw a shiny metal bowl on the counter and went to grab it. She ran back to her mommy hoping she would be pleased. But as soon as she saw the bowl, she got mad. She told the little girl in a stern voice, "Why did you take my steel bowl?" The little girl felt very guilty. She knew she shouldn't have taken it. She didn't want to make her mommy mad. She softly told her mommy, "I'm sorry. I wanted to fill it with happy things for you." Her mommy smiled and said, "That's so sweet, my dear. Next time, just ask me first, okay?" The little girl was relieved and nodded. Then she ran off to pick some flowers to fill the steel bowl with. She wanted it to be full of happy things.
