Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wise Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a wise old boat. The boat lived in a harbor. The harbor was the boat's home. The boat knew many things. One day, a little fish swam up to the wise old boat. The fish said, "Hello, wise boat! Can you help me? I am lost." The wise old boat said, "Yes, little fish. I know the way." The wise old boat told the little fish where to swim. The little fish said, "Thank you, wise boat! You are very kind." The wise old boat was happy to help. The little fish found its way home. The wise old boat and the little fish became good friends. They played and talked in the harbor every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play outside. But today the weather is bad. It is cold and wet. They cannot go to the park. "Let's play a game," Sue says. "I will say a word and you will repeat it. Then you will say a word and I will repeat it. OK?" "OK," Tom says. "You start." "Ancient," Sue says. "Ancient," Tom repeats. "Tree," Tom says. "Tree," Sue repeats. "Cloud," Sue says. "Cloud," Tom repeats. "Rain," Tom says. "Rain," Sue repeats. They keep playing the game. They say many words. They repeat each other. They have fun. But then they hear a loud noise. It is thunder. They see a bright light. It is lightning. They are scared. "Let's go inside," Tom says. "Yes, let's go inside," Sue says. They run to the house. They are safe and warm. They hug each other. "Maybe tomorrow the weather will be good," Tom says. "Maybe," Sue says. "But we can still play the game inside." They smile. They are happy. They are friends.

One day, Tom and his mom were in the room. Tom saw a big clock on the wall. He asked, "Mom, what is that?" His mom said, "That is a clock, Tom. It tells us the time." Tom looked at the clock and tried to think. He wanted to know what time it was. He saw the big hand and the little hand. He thought it was clear, but he was not sure. He said, "Mom, I think it is two o'clock." Just then, something unexpected happened. The big clock fell off the wall! Tom and his mom were very surprised. They looked at the clock on the floor. The hands were not moving anymore. Tom's mom said, "Oh no, the clock is broken. We will have to fix it." Tom was sad, but he knew they could fix the clock together. He said, "It's okay, Mom. We will fix it and then I can learn the time again."

Once upon a time, there was an elderly turkey. Every day, the turkey went to the farm to say hello to his friends. The young animals on the farm would always try to beat the elderly turkey in a race. One day, the animals said, "Let's have a race!" The turkey did not answer, but he was happy inside. He did not expect to win, but he wanted to try. The other animals started the race, but the elderly turkey was slow. Suddenly, the turkey remembered something important. He stopped and shouted, "Wait! Wait! I have a plan!" He told them, "If I win, everyone will get a nice treat!" The animals were excited and waited for the turkey to start the race. He ran with all his might, faster than the other animals. After a long race, the turkey was the winner! The animals cheered. They hugged the elderly turkey and gave him his special treat. From then on, they all agreed that the elderly turkey should always win the race!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Sally. Sally loved to play dress-up. She liked to wear pretty clothes and try new fashion. One day, Sally found a big, stupid hat. She thought it would be fun to wear it. Sally went to the park with the big, stupid hat on her head. She saw her friend, Tim. Tim looked at Sally and said, "That hat is very big and looks a little stupid. Does it belong to you?" Sally laughed and said, "Yes, it belongs to me. I found it and thought it was funny. I like to try new fashion, even if it looks a little stupid." Tim laughed too, and they played together in the park, wearing the big, stupid hat.
