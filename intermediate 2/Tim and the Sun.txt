Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy made of metal. He loved his toy very much. He played with it all the time. One day, Tim's toy got wet. He wanted to make it dry. He asked his mom, "Mom, can you help me make my toy dry?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. We can put it in the sun. The sun will make it dry." But it was a difficult day for Tim. The sun did not come out. It was a cloudy day. Tim's toy did not get dry. Tim was sad. His metal toy got rusty and he could not play with it anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there was a stable. In the stable, there were two best friends: a big horse named Max and a small mouse named Sam. One day, Max and Sam were playing in the stable. Max said, "Sam, let's imagine we can fly!" Sam liked the idea. They closed their eyes and imagined they were flying high in the sky. As they imagined, they saw big clouds and pretty birds. They felt happy and free. When they opened their eyes, they were still in the stable, but they had a fun adventure in their minds. They laughed and played some more, knowing they could always imagine new adventures together.

One day, a boy named Tom was watching his favorite cartoon on TV. He liked it because it was not ordinary. It had funny animals that could talk and play with each other. Tom laughed and clapped his hands as he watched. Tom's little sister, Sue, walked into the room. She wanted to watch her own cartoon. She said, "Tom, I want to watch my show now!" Tom shook his head and said, "No, Sue! I am watching my show!" Tom and Sue began to quarrel. They both wanted to watch their own cartoons, but there was only one TV. Their mom heard the noise and came into the room. She said, "Stop fighting, you two! We can watch one show now, and the other show later." Tom and Sue agreed. They watched Tom's cartoon first, and then Sue's cartoon. They even laughed together and had fun watching both shows. From that day on, they learned to share the TV and enjoy each other's favorite cartoons.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a kind man named Tom. Tom liked to pray every day. He had a big staff that he always carried with him. Tom was eager to help people in his town. One sunny day, Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a small cat stuck in it. Tom wanted to help the cat. He used his staff to reach the cat. But, the cat was scared and jumped onto Tom's head. Tom was surprised! Tom tried to stay calm and pray. The cat, feeling safe, climbed down from Tom's head. It was not a cat, but a little girl! She had magic and could change into a cat. The little girl thanked Tom for helping her. They became good friends, and Tom was very happy.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful garden, there was a big fence. On one side of the fence, there was a cat named Whiskers. On the other side, there was a dog named Buddy. One day, Whiskers saw Buddy and said, "I can jump on the fence, but you can't!" Whiskers teased Buddy and jumped on the fence. Buddy felt sad and said, "Why are you teasing me? Let's be friends instead." Whiskers felt bad and jumped down from the fence. They both played together in the beautiful garden and became best friends. They never teased each other again and always had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was an old man. He was wise, creative and he had worked as a staff for many years. One day, he decided that it was time for him to move on to new things. But he soon began to regret his decision. He missed his staff and the creative things he used to do. He realised that it was important to be creative and he wished he could go back. But it was too late. He had already said goodbye to his staff and moved on. He knew that he would always regret his decision. The end!
