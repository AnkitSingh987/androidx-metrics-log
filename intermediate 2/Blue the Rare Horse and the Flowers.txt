Category: Intermediate
Title: Blue the Rare Horse and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a rare horse named Blue. Blue was a very big and pretty horse. One day, Blue was walking in the forest. He saw many nice flowers. He wanted to pick them. Blue saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was playing near the flowers. Blue said, "Hi Lily, can you help me pick the flowers?" Lily said, "Yes, Blue! I will help you." Lily and Blue picked many flowers together. They had a lot of fun. They made a big pile of flowers. Blue was happy, and so was Lily. They became very good friends. And they played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a soft pillow that he loved very much. He would always hug it when he went to bed. One day, Tim could not find his pillow. He looked all around his room. He felt angry because he could not sleep without it. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, where is my pillow?" His mom said, "Let's look for it together." They looked in the living room, the kitchen, and the bathroom, but they could not find it. Tim was very sad. Finally, they went to the backyard. There, they saw Tim's dog, Spot. Spot had the pillow! He was using it as a bed. Tim's mom said, "Spot, you need to give the pillow back to Tim." Spot looked at them and gave the pillow back. Tim set the pillow back on his bed and hugged it tight. He was happy again, and now he could sleep well.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Alice. She was very thoughtful and liked to think about many things. One day when she was playing in the park, she saw an old man sitting on a bench. Alice walked up to him and said hello. The man smiled and said hello back. They started talking and Alice asked the man what kind of memories he had. The old man thought for a while and then told her all about his childhood memories. Alice listened to the old man carefully and thought about how lovely it was to hear such special memories. She looked around and noticed that all the other children were playing together. Alice thanked the old man for sharing all his memories with her and ran off to join the other children. She thought about the old man and remembered to be kind and thoughtful.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had many pears. The pears were spicy. One day, a boy named Tim went to the tree. He wanted to eat a pear. Tim tried to get a pear from the tree. He could not. Tim was sad. Then, a big wind came. The wind made the pears fall. Tim was happy. He picked up a pear and took a big bite. But the spicy pear was not good. Tim did not like it. Then, a little bird came. The bird loved the spicy pear. The bird ate the pear with Tim. They became good friends.

Lily liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna. Anna had long hair and a pink dress. Lily liked to brush Anna's hair and make her look pretty. One day, Lily saw a big candle on the table. It had a bright flame and smelled nice. Lily wanted to see if Anna liked the candle too. She picked up Anna and brought her close to the flame. But the flame was very hot and it burned Anna's hair. Lily screamed and dropped Anna on the floor. Mama heard Lily scream and ran to see what happened. She saw Anna on the floor with her hair on fire. She quickly put out the fire with a wet cloth. She was angry and scared. She picked up Lily and said, "Lily, you should never play with fire. It is very dangerous. It can burn you and your toys. Look what you did to Anna. Her hair is gone and her dress is dirty. She also has a cut on her face from the floor. You hurt Anna very much." Lily felt very sorry and sad. She cried and hugged Mama. She said, "I'm sorry, Mama. I didn't mean to hurt Anna. I just wanted to show her the candle. I love Anna. Can you fix her?" Mama sighed and hugged Lily back. She said, "I know you love Anna, Lily. And I love you. But you have to be careful and listen to me. Fire is not a toy. It can hurt you and others. I will try to fix Anna, but she will never look the same. You have to be gentle with her and take good care of her. Can you do that?" Lily nodded and wiped her tears. She said, "Yes, Mama. I will be gentle and take good care of Anna. And I will never play with fire again. I promise." Mama smiled and kissed Lily. She said, "That's a good girl. Now let's go and see what we can do for Anna." She took Lily and Anna to the bathroom and started to clean and mend them. Lily hoped that Anna would forgive her and still be her friend. She learned a hard lesson that day. Fire can burn and hurt.
