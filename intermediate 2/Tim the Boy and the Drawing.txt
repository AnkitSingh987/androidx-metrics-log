Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drawing

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to make a print of its leaves. He took out his paper and crayons to start drawing. While Tim was drawing, the sky turned dark. It started to rain, and Tim began to cry. His print was getting wet, and he did not want it to be ruined. He looked around for a place to hide from the rain. Tim saw a small cave and ran inside. He waited for the rain to stop, holding his print close to him. When the rain stopped, and the sky was blue again, Tim went back to his tree. He finished his print with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day. He lived in a big pond with his fish friends. They all had fun playing together. One day, Fin found a square rock at the bottom of the pond. It was a very special rock. Fin showed his friends the square rock. They all wanted to touch it. "Let's play a game," said Fin. "I will hide the rock, and you all have to find it." His friends agreed, and they all swam away to hide their eyes. Fin hid the square rock behind a big plant. Then he called his friends. "Ready or not, here I come!" they all said. They swam around the pond, looking for the square rock. Finally, one of Fin's friends found it. "I found it!" she shouted. All the fish swam to her and cheered. The fish took turns hiding the square rock and finding it. They played the game all day long. The sun started to set, and the fish were tired. "That was so much fun," said Fin. "Let's play again tomorrow!" All the fish agreed, and they swam home to rest for another day of fun.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man. He lived in a small house. The man had a telephone. He loved to talk with his friends on the telephone. One day, the man went outside to play. He forgot to lock his door. A bad man came and saw the door was open. He went inside the house. The bad man wanted to steal the telephone. The elderly man came back home. He saw the bad man with his telephone. The elderly man was sad. The bad man ran away with the telephone. The man never saw his telephone again.

Once upon a time, a patient giant lived in a big forest. He was very tall and strong. The giant liked to help his little animal friends. He would raise them up high so they could see the whole forest. The little animals loved the giant and played with him all day. One day, the giant found a small egg. He knew that he had to take care of it. The giant was very patient and waited for the egg to crack. After a while, a little bird came out of the egg. The giant was very happy and took care of the bird. The giant and the bird were very good friends. But, one day, the bird wanted to fly high in the sky. The giant knew that the bird had to learn to fly on its own. He raised the bird up high and let it go. The bird tried to fly, but it fell down and got hurt. The giant felt very sad. The moral of the story is that sometimes, even when we try to help, we must let others learn on their own.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a magazine on the table. He wanted to hide it so he could look at it later. He ran to his room and put the magazine under his bed. In the afternoon, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They played with cars and blocks. Then, Tim said, "I have a mighty secret. Want to see?" Sam nodded and followed Tim to his room. Tim showed Sam the magazine he had hidden. They sat on the floor and looked at the pictures together. They saw a mighty lion and a big elephant. They had fun talking about the animals they saw. They felt like mighty explorers on an adventure.
