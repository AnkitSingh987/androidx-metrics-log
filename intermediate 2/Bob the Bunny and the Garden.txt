Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bunny and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a beautiful green garden, there lived a small bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat. His favorite food was a potato. Every day, he would hop around the garden and find potatoes to chew. One sunny day, as Bob was chewing a big potato, he met a new friend. Her name was Lily, and she was a pretty butterfly. Lily asked Bob, "What are you eating?" Bob replied, "I am eating a potato. It is very tasty. Do you want to try?" Lily tried a small bite of the potato, but she didn't like it. She said, "Thank you, Bob, but I like to eat flowers. They are beautiful and sweet." So, Bob and Lily spent the day together in the garden. Bob chewed on his potato, and Lily sipped nectar from the beautiful flowers. From that day on, Bob and Lily were the best of friends. They played together in the garden every day. Bob learned that there were many different things to eat, and Lily learned that it was fun to share with a friend. And they lived happily ever after, enjoying their days in the beautiful garden, eating potatoes and flowers, and sharing their love for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky boy named Tim. He had a big football that he loved to play with. Every day, Tim would go outside and kick his football around the yard. One sunny day, Tim found a small, soft cat. The cat was lost and scared. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he rubbed its head gently. The cat started to purr and feel happy. Tim knew he had made a new friend. Tim and the cat played with the lucky football all day. They had so much fun together. When the sun went down, Tim took the cat home. Now, Tim and the cat play with the football every day, and they are the best of friends.

Anna and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big slide and run to it. Anna goes first. She slides down fast and laughs. Ben waits for his turn. He is excited. But then he sees a big cloud in the sky. It is dark and scary. It makes a loud noise. Ben is afraid. He thinks it is a monster. He runs to Anna and hugs her. "Anna, Anna, help me! The monster is coming!" he cries. Anna looks up and sees the cloud. She knows it is not a monster. It is just rain. She smiles and hugs Ben back. "Don't worry, Ben. It is not a monster. It is rain. Rain is water from the sky. It makes the flowers and the grass grow. It is good," she says. "But it is loud and wet. I don't like it. It will hurt us," Ben says. "No, it won't hurt us. It will make us wet, but we can dry. Look, I have a big hat. We can cover our heads with it. Then we will be warm and dry. Come on, let's go under the tree. The tree will also cover us. Then we can watch the rain and the birds. The birds are lively. They sing and fly in the rain. They are not afraid. They are happy," Anna says. She puts her hat on Ben's head and holds his hand. They run to the tree and sit under it. They cover their heads with the hat and watch the rain and the birds. They see the raindrops sparkle and the birds dance. They hear the rain patter and the birds chirp. They feel the hat and the tree protect them. They are not afraid anymore. They are happy too. They smile and giggle. They are having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a hero named Tim. Tim was not like other heroes. He did not have a bright cape or a big smile. He was a bit dull, but he had a big heart. One day, Tim met a cat stuck in a tree. The cat cried, "Help me, please!" Tim wanted to help, so he tried to stretch his arms to reach the cat. But his arms were too short. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's legs began to stretch! They grew longer and longer until he could stand on the ground and reach the cat. Tim saved the cat and became friends with it. From that day on, Tim learned that even if he was dull, he could still be a hero by helping others. And the cat learned that it's okay to ask for help when you need it. They both lived happily ever after, and everyone in the town loved Tim, the stretching hero.
