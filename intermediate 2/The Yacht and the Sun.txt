Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yacht and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a pretty yacht. The yacht was blue and white. It liked to sail on the water. The sun made the yacht happy. One day, the yacht wanted a friend. It asked a man to help. The man said, "Let's build a friend for the yacht." They started to build a new yacht. They built a pretty red and white yacht. The two yachts were very happy. They sailed together on the water. They played and laughed. They were best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big couch in his house. He liked to sit on the couch and eat his yummy snack. His mom would always put his snack on a small plate for him. One day, Tim saw a big bug on the couch. He was scared and called his mom. "Mommy, there is a big bug on the couch!" he said. His mom came to see the bug and told Tim, "Don't worry, I will take it away." But Tim's mom was too late. The bug had already eaten Tim's yummy snack. Tim was very sad and cried. He didn't have any more yummy snacks to eat.

Once upon a time, a girl named Lily loved to rock in her chair. She liked to play with her toy violin. One day, she found a big violin in her room. She was very happy and started to play it. She played it all day and all night. One morning, a little bird came to her window. The bird was sorry because he was lost. He heard Lily's violin and wanted to sing with her. Lily and the bird played music together. They had so much fun, and they were very loud. But then, something strange happened. The big violin started to rock on its own! Lily and the bird were scared. They stopped playing and looked at the rocking violin. Suddenly, the violin stopped rocking and started to play music all by itself! Lily and the bird were not scared anymore. They danced and played with the magic violin, and they all became best friends.

Joey was out for a walk in the park. He looked up at the sky and hummed a little rhythm as he walked. He was feeling a bit sleepy, so he decided to take a nap. He lay down in the soft grass and closed his eyes. Suddenly, he heard a voice calling his name. It was a kind, gentle voice. Joey opened his eyes and saw an old man standing over him. The old man smiled and said, “Joey, I want to examine something with you. Would you help me?” Joey stood up and nodded yes. The old man then said, “I want to examine how the rhythm of this park makes you feel.” He waved his hand towards the tree and stream nearby. Joey enjoyed walking around and feeling the rhythm of the park. When he was finished, the old man said, “You’ve done a great job, Joey. You have made me happy. Now you can go back to being sleepy.” Joey smiled and thanked the old man before lying back down and falling asleep. He had enjoyed helping the old man and the rhythm of the park reminded him of a lullaby.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom needed to go to the store, so he called a taxi. The taxi came, and Tom got in. On the way to the store, the taxi hit a big bump. Tom's hat fell off, and everyone saw his bald head. Tom felt embarrass because he did not like people to see his bald head. He quickly put his hat back on. When Tom got to the store, he bought a new hat. He wore it all the time, so no one would see his bald head again. Tom was happy, and he took the taxi back home.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the park. She saw a pretty red rose on the ground. She picked it up and felt very comfortable holding it. Lily knew that the rose was special. Lily met a talking frog named Fred. "Hello, Lily," said the frog. "I lost my rose. Have you seen it?" Lily looked at the rose in her hand and thought about what to do. She knew she had to give the rose back to Fred. So, she replied, "Yes, Fred. I found your rose." Fred was very happy to get his rose back. He told Lily that the rose had magic powers. As a thank you, Fred let Lily make one wish. Lily thought about it and wished for a new friend to play with. Suddenly, a cute puppy appeared! Lily and the puppy played all day long and became best friends.
