Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Shoes

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, were ready to play outside. They put on their shoes and ran out the door. The sun was shining, and the grass was green. Tim and Spot loved to run and play together. As they were running, Tim saw a big pool of water. He looked at Spot and said, "Let's jump in the water!" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's do it!" They both ran and jumped into the water. They splashed and laughed, having so much fun. After playing in the water, Tim and Spot were tired. They walked back to their house, all wet and happy. Tim's mom saw them and said, "You two look like you had fun!" Tim and Spot smiled and nodded. They had the best day playing and running in the water.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He had a pretty vase. The vase was in his house. The bald man liked flowers a lot. He put flowers in the vase every day. One day, the bald man saw a bug on the flowers. He did not like the bug. He got a spray bottle. He filled it with water. The bald man wanted to spray the bug away. He sprayed the bug with water. The bug flew away. The bald man was happy. His flowers were safe. The bald man and the vase lived happily ever after.

Jenny glanced out the window of the subway, watching the trees and houses blur by. She was on her way home with her mom, who held her hand tight. Jenny smiled and said, "Mama, I'm going to bring you a present when I get home!" Her mom smiled and said, "Oh wow! That sounds so nice!" Jenny continued to look out the window as the subway raced fast along the track. Suddenly, there was a loud noise and Jenny jolted in her seat. The subway was slowing quickly. Mama gasped and said, "Oh no! We're stuck! I guess we have to stay here until someone brings us a new subway." Jenny frowned, disappointed. Suddenly, the subway began moving again, even faster than before. Excited, Jenny said, "Yay, I'm glad they brought us a new subway!" But moments later, the subway crashed into a tree at the edge of the track. The door opened, and a firefighter gently took Jenny's hand and said, "Let's go! We have to take you to the hospital right away." Mama had tears in her eyes. Jenny never got to bring her the present.

Tom and Lily are brother and sister. They like to travel with their mom and dad. They go to many places and see many things. They have a big car that can fit all their bags and toys. One day, they travel to a farm. They see cows, sheep, chickens and horses. They also see a big barn. Tom and Lily want to explore the barn. They ask their mom and dad if they can go inside. "Be careful, kids," mom says. "The barn is old and dusty. There may be sharp things inside." "OK, mom," Tom and Lily say. They hold hands and walk to the barn. They open the door and see a lot of hay, tools and boxes. They think it is very interesting. They find a ladder that goes to the loft. They want to climb the ladder and see what is up there. They start to climb, but Lily stops. She feels something hurt her foot. She looks down and sees a nail sticking out of the wood. She cries out loud. "Ow, ow, ow! Tom, help me! There is a nail in my foot!" Tom hears Lily and runs back to her. He sees the nail and tries to pull it out. He is scared and worried. He calls for mom and dad. "Mom, dad, come quick! Lily has a nail in her foot!" Mom and dad hear Tom and run to the barn. They see Lily and the nail. They are shocked and sad. They hug Lily and tell her it will be OK. They take her to the car and drive to the doctor. The doctor takes out the nail and puts a bandage on Lily's foot. He gives her a sticker and a lollipop. He tells her she is brave and strong. Lily feels better. She thanks the doctor and smiles. She hugs Tom and says sorry for making him scared. Tom hugs her back and says he loves her. Mom and dad are proud of them. They say they are sorry for the nail and the barn. They say they will find a safer place to explore next time. They all go back to the car and travel home. They are happy to be together.
