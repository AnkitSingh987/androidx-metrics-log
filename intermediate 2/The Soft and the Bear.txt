Category: Intermediate
Title: The Soft and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a soft, big bear. The bear liked to play in the woods. One day, the bear found a big weight. It was so heavy! The bear wanted to scare his friends with the weight. The bear took the weight and went to find his friends. First, he met a small bird. The bear said, "Look at this heavy weight! I will scare you with it!" But the bird did not get scared. The bird just laughed. The bear felt sad. He tried to scare his other friends with the weight, but no one was scared. Then, something unexpected happened. The heavy weight broke into pieces. Inside the weight, there were many soft toys! All the animals were so happy. They played with the soft toys all day long. The bear learned that it was more fun to play with friends than to scare them.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They found a nice spot to sit and put their soft, big towel on the sand. Tim was so excited to play in the water and build sand castles. Tim's mom said, "Tim, let's sit on the towel and eat our lunch before we play." They both sat on the soft towel and ate yummy sandwiches. Tim saw a little girl playing nearby and wanted to be friends. Tim walked over to the little girl and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Do you want to play with me?" The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, I'm Lily. Let's build a big sand castle together!" Tim and Lily played all day, and when it was time to go home, they sat on the soft towel one more time to say goodbye. They promised to play together again soon.

---

One day, a big cat and a small dog were in the kitchen. The big cat saw a mug on the table. The cat said to the dog, "Look at that impressive mug! It's so big and shiny. I want to squeeze it." The small dog said, "I want to try too!" So, they both tried to squeeze the big, impressive mug. But, they were not strong enough to move it. Just then, a tiny ant walked by. The ant said, "I can help you squeeze that impressive mug." The big cat and small dog laughed. "You are too small," they said. But the tiny ant was strong. With a big push, the ant squeezed the impressive mug. It moved! The big cat and small dog were very surprised. They thanked the tiny ant and learned that even small friends can do big things.

Sara and Ben were twins who loved to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big ball that was blue and shiny. They liked to roll it, bounce it, and kick it. Sometimes they pretended it was a planet in the universe. One day, they decided to weigh their ball. They found a scale in the bathroom and put the ball on it. The scale said the ball was five pounds. Sara and Ben were amazed. They wondered how much the whole universe weighed. They asked their mom, who was reading a book in the living room. "Mom, how much does the universe weigh?" they said. Mom smiled and put down her book. She was a charming lady who always had time for her children. She hugged them and said, "That's a very good question, my dears. But the universe is so big and so vast that we can't weigh it with a scale. It has countless stars and planets and moons and comets and asteroids and dust and gas and light and dark. It's more than we can imagine." Sara and Ben looked at each other. They were confused. How could something be so big and so heavy that they couldn't measure it? Mom saw their puzzled faces and said, "Don't worry, my loves. You don't need to know how much the universe weighs to enjoy it. You can look at the sky at night and see the beautiful stars. You can learn about the different planets and what they are like. You can dream about exploring the universe someday. The universe is a wonderful gift that we can appreciate and respect, even if we can't weigh it." Sara and Ben nodded. They understood. They thanked their mom and gave her a kiss. Then they took their ball and went outside to play. They still loved their ball, but they also loved the universe. They learned that some things are more valuable than their weight.
