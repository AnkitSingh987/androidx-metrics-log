Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pigeon and the Mark

Once upon a time, there was a little pigeon. The pigeon had a mark on its wing. The mark was harmless, but it made the pigeon special. One day, the pigeon went to play with its friends. They saw the mark and said, "Wow, that's a cool mark!" The pigeon felt happy and proud of its mark. In the end, the pigeon and its friends played all day. They had lots of fun. The pigeon with the mark was always happy and loved by all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He was very grumpy. He did not like to play with his toys or smile. One day, the grumpy boy met a small bug. The bug told the boy, "If you believe, you can be happy." The boy looked at the bug and thought about what it said. The next day, the boy tried to believe. He played with his toys and started to smile. He was not grumpy anymore. The boy and the bug became good friends and they played together every day.

Once there was a boy, and he was only three years old. He had a lot of toy horses and one day he decided to take them to a stable. When he arrived, it was very peaceful. He saw many animals and he even said hello to them. The boy then began to play with his horses. He threw them around and galloped them around the stable. He played until he was tired and the peaceful stable welcomed him and made him feel very happy. When the sun set, the boy took his horses and went home, very satisfied with his day. He knew that the stable was special and he would come back soon.

Once upon a time there was a small girl named Amy. She liked to explore the world around her. One day while she was out, she came across a small novel. She stepped closer and saw that it was a story about a wild adventure. "What is this?" Amy asked. "It's a novel," said a friendly voice. A friendly fairy had appeared and was smiling at Amy. "What's a novel?" asked Amy. "A novel is a story," said the fairy. The fairy encouraged Amy to step into the book and begin the wild adventure. Amy was hesitant but the fairy was so kind and friendly that she decided to take a chance. With a deep breath she stepped into the novel. The pages of the book began to swirl around her and suddenly she found herself on a wild adventure!

One day, a little girl named Sue went outside to play. It started to rain, but Sue did not care. She liked to play in the rain. She saw a weird bug under a leaf. The bug was green and had big eyes. Sue wanted to show her mom the bug. Sue ran inside and said, "Mom, come see this weird bug I found in the rain!" Her mom put on her coat and went outside with Sue. They both looked at the bug under the leaf. Sue's mom said, "That is a weird bug! Let's wrap it up and show your dad." Sue and her mom found a small box and wrapped the weird bug in a soft cloth. They went inside and showed the bug to Sue's dad. He looked at the bug and said, "Wow! What a weird bug you found in the rain, Sue!" They all laughed and Sue felt happy that she found the weird bug.

Sam and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. They found a big beetle with a shiny buckle on its back. It was green and blue and red. "Wow, look at this!" Sam said. "It's so pretty!" "Can I see?" Lily asked. She reached out her hand to touch the beetle. But the beetle did not like that. It made a loud hiss and opened its wings. The buckle on its back started to glow. It was very bright and scary. "Ah!" Sam and Lily screamed. They ran away from the beetle. They hid behind a tree and hugged each other. They were shaking and crying. "Are you okay?" Sam asked Lily. "Yes, I'm okay. Are you?" Lily asked Sam. "Yes, I'm okay too. I'm sorry I scared you," Sam said. "I'm sorry too. I didn't mean to make the beetle angry," Lily said. They looked at each other and smiled. They were honest friends. They said sorry when they did something wrong. They heard a buzzing sound. They peeked from behind the tree. The beetle was flying away. The glow on its back faded. "Bye bye, beetle," Sam and Lily said. "You are very pretty, but please don't hiss at us again." They went back to their game. They looked for other bugs and flowers. They had fun in the garden.
