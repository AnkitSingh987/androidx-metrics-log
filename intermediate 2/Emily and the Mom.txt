Category: Intermediate
Title: Emily and the Mom

Emily was a very cheerful girl. She loved to smile, and the world seemed brighter when she did. One day her mom said, "Emily, let's make a candle!" "Yay!" Emily jumped with excitement. They gathered the supplies and got to work. "Here you go!" said Mom, handing her a yellow candle. Emily beamed with happiness. "Let's light it!" Mom suggested. Emily smiled, and nodded. They lit the candle, and the room suddenly felt so cozy and warm. "Making this candle was our special moment," Mom said with a smile. Emily smiled back and gave her mom a big hug. This was the best day!

Once upon a time, in a big, yellow house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a round, bouncy ball that she loved to play with every day. It was her favorite toy, and she would take it with her wherever she went. One day, Lily and her ball went to the park to play. She threw the ball high in the sky, and it would come back down for her to catch. But then, the ball got stuck in a tree! Lily was very sad and didn't know what to do. A kind bird saw Lily and wanted to help. The bird flew up to the tree and tried to push the ball out. After trying many times, the bird did not quit. Finally, the round ball fell out of the tree and back to Lily. She was so happy, and she thanked the bird for not quitting. From that day on, Lily and the bird became best friends, and they played together with the yellow ball every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big firework show. Everyone was excited to see the bright lights in the sky. But, there was a small, helpless cat who was scared of the loud noises. One day, before the firework show, the cat heard some kids talking. They said the fireworks would be very loud and bright. The cat knew it had to find a safe place to hide from the noise. The cat started to hurry and look for a quiet place. It tried to hide under a tree, but it was not safe enough. Then, it tried to hide in a box, but it was too small. Finally, the cat found a big, quiet house to hide in. When the fireworks started, the cat was safe and happy in the big, quiet house. It could see the bright lights, but the loud noises didn't scare it anymore. The cat had found the perfect place to enjoy the show. From that day on, the cat knew that whenever there were fireworks, it could always find a safe place to hide. And, it was never scared of the loud noises again.

Once upon a time, in a small house near the west side of town, there lived a cat named Tom and a dog named Max. They were best friends who loved to play together. One day, they decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. They were both very fit and strong, so they knew it would be a fun race. "Ready, set, go!" Tom said, and they started running. They ran and ran, laughing and having a great time. But they did not know that they were about to disturb someone very important. As they raced, they came across a big tree. Behind the tree, a little bird was building her nest. She was very busy and did not want to be disturbed. But Tom and Max were making a lot of noise as they ran, and the little bird got very upset. "Please stop!" the little bird cried. "You are disturbing my work!" Tom and Max felt bad for making the little bird upset, so they stopped their race and helped her build her nest. In the end, they all became friends and had a great time together. And from that day on, they never disturbed the little bird again.

Tim and Sue wanted to play outside. They put on their coats and hats and ran to the park. They saw a big hole in the ground. It was a pit. "Let's go see what's in the pit!" Tim said. "No, Tim, it's too dark and deep. We might fall in and get hurt," Sue said. But Tim did not listen. He was curious and brave. He walked to the edge of the pit and looked down. He saw something shiny and round. It was a ball. "Look, Sue, a ball! I want it!" Tim said. He reached out his hand to grab the ball. But he lost his balance and slipped. He fell into the pit with a loud scream. Sue was scared. She ran to the pit and called for Tim. But she could not see him or hear him. She did not know what to do. She cried and cried. Soon, a man came by. He heard Sue crying and saw the pit. He asked her what happened. She told him about Tim and the ball. The man was sad and angry. He said, "You should not have played near the pit. It is dangerous and dirty. There are rats and snakes and trash in there. Your brother might be dead or hurt. You are bad children." He did not help Sue or Tim. He walked away and left them alone. Sue was scared and sorry. She wished she had stopped Tim from going to the pit. She wished they had played somewhere else. She wished they had gone home.
