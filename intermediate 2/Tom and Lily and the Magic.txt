Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Magic

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play in the garden. One day, they found a jar under a big tree. The jar was tall and shiny, and had a lid with a star on it. "Wow, look at this!" Tom said. "Maybe there is something inside." "Let's open it and see," Lily said. They twisted the lid, but it was very tight. They pulled and pushed, but it did not move. "Maybe it is magic," Tom said. "Maybe we have to say a magic word." "Like what?" Lily asked. "Like... believe!" Tom said. He closed his eyes and said, "Believe, believe, believe!" Suddenly, the lid popped open, and a cloud of smoke came out. The twins coughed and blinked, and then they saw a strange creature in the jar. It had a long nose, a curly tail, and wings. "Hello, hello, hello!" the creature said. "I am a genie, and I grant you three wishes. Thank you for freeing me from the jar." Tom and Lily gasped. They could not believe their eyes. They had found a real genie! "What do you wish for?" the genie asked. Tom and Lily looked at each other, and then they smiled. They had so many ideas. They were going to have a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a favorite toy. It was a rubber ball. She played with it every day. The ball was very comfortable to hold. It made her happy. One day, Sue and her friend Tom were playing with the ball. They threw it back and forth. They laughed and had fun. Then, Sue threw the ball high into the air. Tom tried to catch it, but he missed. The ball went into the bushes. Tom said, "Oops! I missed the ball. Let's go find it!" Sue and Tom looked in the bushes. They looked under leaves and behind rocks. But they could not find the ball. Sue was sad. She missed her rubber ball. Tom said, "Don't worry, Sue. We will find it." They kept looking. At last, they found the ball under a big tree. Sue was happy again. She gave Tom a big hug. They played with the ball and had more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to create things. One day, he found a big melon in the garden. He was very happy and had an idea. Tom wanted to make a funny face on the melon. He took a marker and drew a big smile and two eyes. But then, his little sister, Sue, came running and bumped into him. The marker slipped and made a big line on the melon's face. Tom was ashamed. Tom tried to fix the melon's face, but it only got worse. He felt very bad. Later, his mom came and saw the melon. She was not happy. She asked Tom to throw the melon away. Tom was very sad and learned to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his favorite toy, a brush. He would carry it everywhere he went. One day, Buddy saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she had lost her brush. Buddy wanted to help Lily. Buddy said, "Don't be sad, Lily. I will help you find your brush." They looked high and low, but they could not find it. Then, something unexpected happened. Buddy found a big box. He went inside the box and found lots of brushes. Lily was so happy to see all the brushes. She said, "Thank you, Buddy! You are the best dog ever!" But then, they heard a noise. It was a cat. The cat said, "Please don't disturb my brushes. I collect them." Buddy and Lily were surprised. They did not know that cats could like brushes too. Buddy decided to give his brush to the cat. The cat was happy and became friends with Buddy and Lily. From that day on, they all played together and shared their brushes. They were the best of friends and never disturbed each other's things again.
