Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Story and the Man

Once upon a time, there was a big building called a university. In this place, people learned many things. There was a kind man who worked there. Everyone called him the compassionate man. One day, the compassionate man saw a little girl who was lost. She was crying. He wanted to help her. So, he gave her a big hug and said, "Don't cry. I will help you find your mom." They walked and walked, but they could not find her mom. The compassionate man was very sad. He wanted to make the little girl happy. So, he gave her a toy to play with. But still, they could not find her mom. In the end, the little girl and the compassionate man were both very sad. They never found her mom. The moral of the story is that even kind people can't always fix everything. Sometimes, bad things happen, and we must learn to be strong.

One day, a little zebra named Zara was playing in the warm sun. She liked to crawl on the ground and pretend she was a tiny bug. As she was crawling, she saw a big rock in her way. She tried to crawl over it, but it was too high. Zara was sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a friendly bird named Benny flew by and saw Zara. "Why are you sad?" he asked. Zara told him about the big rock and how she couldn't crawl over it. Benny thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! Let's work together and move the rock." Zara and Benny worked hard to move the big rock. With Benny's help, Zara was able to crawl over the rock and keep playing. Zara was very happy and thanked Benny for his help. They both learned that working together can solve problems. From that day on, they became best friends and always helped each other.

One day, a big impressive cat named Tom found a bottle of ink. Tom was a curious cat, so he played with the bottle. He pushed it with his paw, and the ink spilled all over the floor. It made a big, messy puddle. Tom's friend, a little bird named Sue, flew in and saw the mess. She said, "Oh no, Tom! What did you do?" Tom felt sad and said, "I was playing and made a big mess. I don't know how to clean it up." Sue had an idea. She told Tom to bow down and use his tail to clean the ink. Tom did what Sue said and bowed down. He swished his tail in the ink puddle, and it started to clean up the mess. Tom and Sue worked together to clean the whole floor. When they were done, Tom said, "Thank you, Sue, for helping me clean the ink." Sue smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom. Next time, be careful when you play with new things." Tom agreed, and they both went outside to play in the sun.

Lily liked to study books with pictures and words. She wanted to learn many things. She had a big book about animals that she loved. She looked at the book every day. One day, Lily saw a big envelope in the mail. It had her name on it. She was very happy. She opened the envelope and saw a letter and a sticker. The letter said, "Dear Lily, I am your aunt Sue. I live far away. I miss you very much. I hope you like this sticker. It is a lion. Lions are brave and strong. You are brave and strong too. Love, Aunt Sue." Lily smiled. She liked her aunt Sue. She liked the sticker. It was a lion. She wanted to put the sticker on her book about animals. She ran to her room with the letter and the sticker. But Lily was careless. She did not see her little brother Tommy on the floor. He was playing with his cars. Lily tripped over Tommy and fell down. She dropped the letter and the sticker. Tommy saw the sticker and grabbed it. He liked the lion too. He put the sticker on his car. Lily cried. She was hurt and angry. She wanted her sticker back. She shouted at Tommy, "Give me my sticker! It is mine! Aunt Sue gave it to me!" Tommy shouted back, "No! It is mine! I like it! It is a lion!" They fought over the sticker. They pulled and tore it. The sticker ripped in half. They both cried. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Lily and Tommy crying and the sticker ripped. She saw the letter on the floor. She was sad and mad. She said, "Lily, Tommy, what did you do? This was a nice letter and a nice sticker from your aunt Sue. She sent them to you because she loves you. You were careless and selfish. You ruined the letter and the sticker. You hurt each other. You should be ashamed." Lily and Tommy felt ashamed. They said sorry to Mom and to each other. They hugged and said sorry to Aunt Sue too. But it was too late. The letter and the sticker were ruined. They could not fix them. They could not study the lion anymore. They learned a hard lesson. They wished they had been more careful and kind.
