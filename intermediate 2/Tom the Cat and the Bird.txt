Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Bird

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. Tom was a fierce cat. He liked to chase birds and play with his friends. While he was walking, he saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to climb the tree. As Tom climbed the tree, he saw a bird. The bird had a name too. Her name was Sally. Tom wanted to chase Sally, but he realized something. Sally was not scared of him. She was not like the other birds. Tom and Sally became friends. They played together in the tree. It was unexpected, but Tom was happy. He learned that being fierce was not always good. Sometimes, it is better to be kind and make new friends.

One day, a little dove was playing in the thick forest. The dove liked to hide and look for things. It saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to hide there. "Let's play hide and look!" the dove said to its friend, a small squirrel. The squirrel was excited and said, "Okay, I will look for you!" The dove hid behind the tree, and the squirrel started to look. The squirrel looked and looked, but could not find the dove. The squirrel was sad, but it did not give up. Then, the squirrel saw a clue. It saw white feathers on the ground near the big tree. The squirrel followed the feathers and found the dove. "I found you!" said the squirrel, happy. The dove and the squirrel laughed and played hide and look all day long.

Sherri and her father were in their small and cozy living room. Little Sherri was so excited to have her dad at home, she wanted to show him something. "Daddy, look!" shouted Sherri. She pointed at a sparkly sign that hung on their wall. "That's really nice," said her dad. "Where did you find it?" Sherri beamed. "My nosy neighbor helped me make it. We used glitter and glue, and I added some sparkles. We spent all day making it!" "It's beautiful," said her dad. "I'm sure your neighbor was very kind to you." Sherri nodded and smiled. "Yes, she was. She taught me how to sign it, too!" "That's wonderful," said her dad, squeezing her in a warm hug. Sherri knew that she would never forget the time she had spent with her nosy neighbor, making a special sparkly sign.

Once upon a time, there was a big board. On the board, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. They liked to play on the board every day. The board was very fun for them. One day, the cat and the dog had a quarrel. The cat wanted to play with a ball, but the dog wanted to play with a bone. They both felt sour and did not want to play together anymore. After some time, the cat and the dog decided to stop their quarrel. They both played with the ball and the bone on the board. They were happy again and they learned to share. The cat and the dog stayed friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a nurse named Jane. Jane liked to decorate her house with pretty things. One day, she found a big, bitter fruit in the market. "This will look nice in my house," she said. So she bought the fruit and took it home. The next day, Jane's friend, Tom, came to visit. "What is that?" he asked, pointing at the bitter fruit. "It's a fruit I found at the market," said Jane. "I used it to decorate my house." Tom looked at the fruit and said, "Be careful, Jane. I heard that fruit can be bad for you." A few days later, Jane was very hungry. She looked at the bitter fruit and thought, "Maybe I can eat it." So she took a big bite. Suddenly, she felt very sick. Jane called Tom for help. "I ate the fruit, and now I feel sick," she said. Tom rushed to help Jane, but it was too late. The bitter fruit was very bad for her, and Jane became very sick. She could not be a nurse anymore. Jane was sad that she did not listen to Tom's warning.
