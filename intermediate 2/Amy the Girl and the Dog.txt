Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Amy. She had a cute dog named Max. Max was a bit nervous, but he loved to play with Amy. One day, Amy wanted to take Max for a walk. She put a leash on Max and was ready to go. Amy and Max went to the park. Max was very happy to be outside. They played on the grass and had lots of fun. Amy knew Max was nervous, so she held the leash tight to keep Max safe. Max felt better because Amy was there to lead him. As they walked, Max saw a big dog. He was scared, but Amy held the leash and told him not to worry. Max trusted Amy and stayed by her side. They walked back home, and Max was not scared anymore. The moral of the story is that when we trust and help each other, we can overcome our fears.

Once there was a big truck. He liked to make loud sounds. One day he wanted to make a nice sound so he decided to load his truck. He drove away and started loading all the nice things he found. He found lots of fascinating things and loaded them onto his truck. He was very careful and soon his truck was full. Then he heard the most amazing sound. It was so nice and he couldn't stop smiling. He liked it so much that he decided to keep driving and loading all the nice things he found. Away he drove, loading and making the most lovely sounds. The truck kept driving, finding more nice things and loading them onto his truck. Soon he had filled his truck with lots of nice things and the most lovely sound was coming out of it. It was the best sound he had ever heard and he was so happy. He drove home and unloaded all the nice things he had found and the wonderful sound kept playing. Everyone was amazed by the nice sound and the nice things he had found.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max went out to learn something amazing today. He walked and walked until he met a big cat. The cat was very tall and had a big smile. Max wanted to be friends with the cat. "Hi, cat! Can you teach me something amazing today?" asked Max. The cat nodded and said, "Yes, I can. Follow me, and I will show you." Max was very happy and followed the cat to a big tree. The cat told Max to climb the tree. Max tried and tried, but he could not climb. The cat laughed at Max and said, "You are not amazing like me." Max was sad and went back home. The next day, Max saw the cat stuck in a hole. Max wanted to help, but he remembered the cat's mean words. Max walked away and left the cat in the hole. The cat was sad and learned that being mean to others is not good. The moral of the story is, always be kind to others, even if they are not kind to you.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toys and eat delicious food. One day, Amy found a rag on the floor. She picked it up and looked at it. It was a pretty rag with many colors. Amy wanted to play with the rag, so she took it to her room. She knelt down on the floor and put the rag on her toy horse. The toy horse looked very happy with the colorful rag on its back. Amy clapped her hands and laughed. Amy's mommy came into the room and saw the rag on the toy horse. She smiled and said, "What a nice rag you found, Amy!" They both played with the toy horse and the rag for a long time. They had lots of fun, and later, they ate some delicious cookies together.

Sara and Tom were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat, a scarf and a carrot nose. They were very happy. But then, the sun came out and it was very warm. The snowman started to melt. His hat fell off, his scarf got wet and his nose dropped. Sara and Tom were very sad. "Look, our snowman is gone!" Sara cried. "Maybe we can make another one," Tom said. But there was not enough snow left. They looked around and saw a bottle of shampoo on the porch. Tom had an idea. "Let's use this to make a snowman!" he said. He poured some shampoo on the ground and shaped it into a ball. He put another ball on top and a smaller one for the head. He found some sticks for the arms and some stones for the eyes and mouth. "See, we have a new snowman!" he said. But Sara did not like it. She thought it was ugly and smelly. "That's not a snowman, that's a shampoo man!" she said. She kicked the shampoo man and it fell apart. The shampoo splashed everywhere. Tom was very angry. "Why did you do that?" he shouted. "Because it was not a real snowman!" she shouted back. They started to fight and push each other. They got shampoo on their clothes and hair. They were very messy. Their mom came out and saw them. She was not happy. "What are you doing?" she asked. "We were making a snowman, but Sara ruined it!" Tom said. "No, he ruined it! He used shampoo!" Sara said. Mom shook her head. She took them inside and gave them a bath. She washed the shampoo off their hair and clothes. She was not graceful. She was stern. She told them to say sorry to each other and to share their toys. She told them to be nice and not to waste shampoo. She told them to wait for more snow to make a real snowman. Sara and Tom felt bad. They said sorry to each other and hugged. They promised to be good and to play together. They hoped for more snow soon. They still loved snowmen.
