Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Stan and the Door

Once upon a time there was a brave boy called Stan. Stan had a wide room filled with fun things. One day, he found a curious arrow lying under the bed. He picked it up and started to examine the object. Stan noticed the arrow had colourful feathers at one end. He turned it around and saw that the other end was sharp and dangerous! Suddenly, Stan heard someone knock on the door. It was his friend, Sophie. "What are you doing, Stan?" asked Sophie. Stan smiled. He proudly held the arrow up in the air and said, "I'm examining an arrow!" Sophie gasped. She thought it was amazing. The two children laughed at Stan's brave discovery and vowed to remain best friends forever!

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy town. In this town, there lived a little boy named Tom. Tom was very kind and liked to help others. One day, a big tornado came to the town. Everyone was scared and did not know what to do. Tom wanted to help, so he went to his friend, the wind. He asked the wind to blow the tornado away. The wind listened to Tom and blew very hard. The tornado went away and the town was safe again. All the people in the town were happy. They said, "Thank you, Tom, for saving our town!" Tom felt good because he helped his friends. The sleepy town was safe and everyone could sleep well again.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a mysterious box under a tree. The box had a symbol on it. The symbol was a big star. Tim was excited to find out what was inside the box. He took the box home and went to bed. In the morning, he could not wait to wake up and open the box. When he woke up, the box was gone! Tim felt sad and looked all around the house for the box. Then, Tim saw his little sister, Sue, playing with the box. She had opened it and found a funny hat inside. Tim was happy that the box was not lost. He put on the hat and played with Sue all day. The mysterious box made them both smile and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to help her mom in the kitchen. They would mix food together and make yummy treats. One day, Sue found an old cookbook on the shelf. It had many recipes for her and her mom to try. Sue and her mom picked a recipe from the old cookbook. They got all the food they needed and started to mix it together. Sue was so happy to help her mom. When they were done, they put the food in the oven to cook. They waited and waited until it was ready. Finally, the food was done. Sue and her mom took it out of the oven and let it cool. They tasted the food, and it was very good. Sue learned that even old things can be useful and fun. The moral of the story is to not judge something by its age, as old things can still have value.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I climb the tree?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim started to climb the tree. As he climbed, he felt his skin get itchy. He was upset, but he kept climbing. He did not know why his skin was itchy. When he got to the top, he saw a big, red ball stuck in the branches. "Wow!" Tim said. "I found a ball!" Tim climbed down the tree with the ball. He showed his mom the ball and told her about his itchy skin. His mom looked at the tree and said, "Oh no, that tree has itchy leaves. Let's go home and wash your skin." Tim was happy he found the ball, but he wished he knew about the itchy leaves before he climbed the tree.
