Category: Intermediate
Title: The Plane Was and the Cloud

One day, a little plane was in the sky. The plane was not like other planes. It was a crazy plane. It liked to fly up and down, round and round. The people in the plane were happy and laughed a lot. The crazy plane saw a big cloud. It wanted to fly through the cloud. But the cloud was very big. The plane had to struggle to fly through it. The people in the plane held on tight. At last, the crazy plane came out of the cloud. It was tired but happy. The people in the plane clapped their hands. They loved the crazy plane and its fun flying. They all went home with big smiles on their faces.

The children were playing in the park when one of them, a brave girl, dared the others to win a race. All were eager to accept the challenge. The little girl was very confident in her abilities, believing she was the most intelligent of the group. She quickly went to the starting line and when the race began, she slowly crept ahead. As the others lagged further and further behind, the girl's parents watched proudly. But as the girl pushed her limits, her foot slipped on a stone. She tumbled down, scraping her knee. In a second, the other children caught up with her. The parents quickly ran to the girl, but it was too late. The others had already passed her, and she had lost the race. Still, the brave girl was not easily discouraged. Her parents hugged her and congratulated her for trying her best, but the girl was filled with sadness and tears began to stream down her face.

Once upon a time, there was a group of animals who lived in a big forest. They were all friends and played together. One day, they found a big blue ball near the river. They were all very happy and started to play with it. But soon, they began to fight over who would get to play with the ball. The animals were not happy anymore and stopped being friends. A wise old owl saw what was happening and decided to help the group. He told them that they needed to change and learn to share the blue ball. The owl showed them how to take turns and play together, so everyone could have fun with the ball. The animals listened and started to change. Slowly, the group of animals became friends again. They learned that sharing and taking turns made them all happier. The blue ball brought them back together, and they played in the big forest every day. The wise old owl was happy to see the change in the group, and the animals learned a very important lesson: sharing is caring.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tim. Tim loved to play football. One day, he decided to invite his friends to play football with him. Tim and his friends played football all day. They had a lot of fun. The sun was shining, and everyone was happy. But then, it started to rain. The rain was very heavy, and it made the grass wet and slippery. Tim tried to keep playing, but he slipped on the wet grass. He fell down and hurt his leg. His friends were sad, and they had to stop playing football. Tim had to go home and rest. The day did not end well for Tim and his friends.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big red apple on a tree. They want to eat it. "Can you reach it?" Anna asks Ben. Ben shakes his head. "No, it is too high. We need a stick." They look around and find a long stick. Ben holds the stick and tries to poke the apple. The apple falls down and rolls away. "Quick, get it!" Anna says. They run after the apple. But a dog sees the apple too. The dog barks and chases the apple. "Oh no, the dog wants our apple!" Ben says. They reach the apple before the dog. Anna picks up the apple and holds it tight. The dog jumps and tries to bite the apple. "Go away, dog!" Anna shouts. The dog stops and looks at them. He wags his tail and licks his lips. He wants to be friends. Anna and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry for the dog. He is hungry. "Maybe we can share the apple," Anna says. "OK, let's share," Ben says. They break the apple in half. They give one half to the dog. The dog eats it happily. He thanks them with a lick. They eat the other half. It is juicy and tasty. They smile and say, "Yum!" They make a new friend. The dog follows them to the park. They play together. They have fun. The end.
