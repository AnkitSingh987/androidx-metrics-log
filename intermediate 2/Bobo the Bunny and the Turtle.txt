Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Bunny and the Turtle

Once upon a time, in a sleepy land, there lived a bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to play and hop all day long. He had many friends like Timmy the turtle, Sammy the squirrel, and Dolly the duck. One day, Bobo and his friends were going to play at the park. But Timmy the turtle was very slow. Bobo and his friends had to delay their playtime to wait for Timmy. They did not want to leave him behind. As they waited for Timmy, they sang songs and played games. When Timmy finally arrived, they were so happy. They played at the park all day long. At the end of the day, they were all very sleepy. They went home and had sweet dreams.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play and run all day. She would zoom around the house, chasing her toys and jumping on the couch. One sunny day, Fluffy went outside to play in the yard. She saw a big tree with lots of leaves. Fluffy wanted to climb the tree, so she ran and jumped. She climbed up and up until she reached a big branch. Fluffy was very tired from all the climbing. She decided to rest on the branch. As she lay there, she looked at the sky and the clouds. Fluffy felt happy and sleepy. Soon, she closed her eyes and took a nap on the branch. When she woke up, it was time to go home. Fluffy climbed down the tree and went back inside the house, ready for more fun tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very good dog. He loved to play outside and find the perfect ball. One day, Spot saw a big red ball in the park. He thought it was the perfect ball to play with. Spot ran to the ball and picked it up. A little girl saw Spot and said, "Hey, that's my ball! I value it a lot!" Spot didn't understand, so he kept playing with the ball. The little girl was sad and ran to her mom. The mom came over and took the ball from Spot. She said, "Bad dog! You should not take things that are not yours." Spot felt bad and was sad. The little girl and her mom left the park with the ball, and Spot was all alone.

Once upon a time, there was a big ancient tree. It was very, very old. The tree had many friends who lived near it. One day, a little boy came to visit the tree. He loved the tree and wanted to give it a big hug. The little boy was sad because his toy was spoiled. His toy was a small car that could not move anymore. The tree saw the little boy was sad and wanted to help him. The tree told the wind to blow, and the wind blew a new toy car to the little boy. The little boy was so happy to get a new toy car. He gave the ancient tree a big hug to say thank you. The tree was happy to help the little boy. From that day on, the little boy and the tree became best friends. They played together every day, and the tree never let the little boy's toys spoil again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat pasta with his favorite red sauce. His mom would make it for him every week. One day, Tim's mom got very weak and could not make the red sauce. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim made a promise to his mom that he would make the red sauce for her. He went to the kitchen and tried his best. But the sauce was not good. Tim was sad and thought he had let his mom down. Just then, his big sister came in and saw Tim's sad face. She asked what was wrong and Tim told her about his promise. His sister smiled and said she would help him. They worked together and made the most delicious red sauce ever. Tim's mom was so happy and proud of them. The family enjoyed the pasta together, and Tim learned that sometimes, help can come from unexpected places.
