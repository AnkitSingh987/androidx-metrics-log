Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy that could increase in size. Tim loved his toy very much. One day, Tim saw a symbol on his toy. It was a smiley face. Tim showed his toy to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "My toy has a symbol too, but it's a sad face." Tim said, "Don't be ashamed, Sam. We can play together with our toys." Tim and Sam played together, and their toys became big. They laughed and had a fun time. They were happy that their toys had symbols, and they were not ashamed anymore.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly cat named Velvet. Velvet was a very kind and gentle cat who lived in a small house with her friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play together and were very happy. One day, Velvet began to worry. She couldn't find her favorite toy, a small, soft ball. She looked everywhere in the house but it was nowhere to be found. "Lily," Velvet meowed, "have you seen my ball?" Lily shook her head and said, "No, Velvet. I can't find it either, but don't worry, we will look together." Velvet and Lily searched high and low, but they still couldn't find the ball. They were both very sad. Just then, Lily's mom came into the room with a big smile on her face. "Look what I found in the laundry!" she said, holding up the missing ball. Velvet and Lily were overjoyed and thanked Lily's mom. They played happily with the ball for the rest of the day, and Velvet never had to worry about her favorite toy again.

Tim and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to make noise and pretend they were animals. Tim was a lion and Lily was a monkey. They roared and chattered and jumped on the couch. "Stop it, you two!" Mom said from the kitchen. "You are making too much noise and you are going to break something!" But Tim and Lily did not listen. They kept playing and having fun. They did not see the vase on the table. The vase was very pretty and shiny. Mom liked it a lot. It was a gift from Grandma. Tim and Lily bumped into the table and the vase fell down. It broke into many pieces. They heard a loud crash and saw the mess. They were scared and shocked. They looked at each other with guilty eyes. "What was that?" Mom said, coming into the living room. She saw the broken vase and the toys. She was very angry and sad. She clapped her hands and shouted. "Tim and Lily, what have you done? You broke my vase! You are very naughty and careless! Go to your room right now! No more toys for you today!" Tim and Lily started to cry. They said they were sorry, but Mom did not listen. She sent them to their room and cleaned up the mess. Tim and Lily felt bad. They wished they had listened to Mom and played quietly. They learned their lesson, but it was too late. They broke Mom's vase and Mom's heart.

One day, a little cat named Kitty was very hungry. She wanted to find a meal to eat. She saw a harmless bug on a leaf. Kitty thought, "I can eat this bug for my meal!" So, she got ready to jump and catch the bug. Just as Kitty was about to jump, a big bird named Bobby zoomed down from the sky. Bobby said, "Wait, Kitty! That bug is my friend. Please don't eat him!" Kitty was surprised and stopped. She did not want to hurt Bobby's friend. Kitty and Bobby talked and became friends too. Bobby wanted to help Kitty find a meal. They looked and looked, and then they found a big bowl of cat food. Kitty was so happy, and she ate her meal. From that day on, Kitty, Bobby, and the harmless bug were best friends. They played together and zoomed around the garden every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy made of gold. He loved it very much. One day, he took it to the park to play. At the park, he met a big boy named Max. Max saw Tim's gold toy and wanted it. He said, "Can I play with your gold toy? I don't have any toys available." Tim was kind and said, "Yes, you can play with it for a little while." Max took the toy and started to tease Tim. He said, "I have your gold toy now, and I won't give it back!" Tim was sad and asked, "Please, can I have my toy back?" But Max just laughed and ran away with the toy. Tim cried and went home. He told his mom what happened. She hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. We can try to find another toy for you." But Tim knew he would never find a gold toy like that again. And he never saw Max or his gold toy again. The end.
