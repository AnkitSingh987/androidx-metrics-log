Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and the Hands

Amy was a 3 year old girl. One day she went to the beach with her family. She was so excited to see the big blue sea. When Amy arrived, she saw many people in the water and she saw them moving their hands in the air. Amy was confused. Amy's mom saw that she was confused and explained to her that those people were waving their hands. Amy was excited to learn something new. She wanted to try so her mom held her hand and taught her how to wave her hands. Amy waved her hands and she laughed. She had so much fun learning how to wave at the beach! Every time she saw people in the water, she waved to them and they waved back. It made her feel so happy!

One day, a little girl named Sally went for a walk. The sky was gloomy and gray, but she was happy. She saw a big tree with a knot on it. The knot looked like a funny face. Sally laughed and clapped her hands. Sally kept walking and saw a little squirrel. The squirrel had a knot in its tail. The squirrel tried to fix the knot, but it could not. Sally went to the squirrel and helped it. She untied the knot and the squirrel was very happy. The squirrel clapped its little paws and danced. The sky started to clear up and the sun came out. Sally and the squirrel played and clapped their hands and paws. They were very happy. The gloomy day turned into a bright and sunny day. Sally and the squirrel became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Lily. She liked to help her mom and dad. One cold day, Lily's mom asked her to find her mittens. Lily said, "I will find them, Mom!" Lily looked in her room and found one mitten. She looked in the living room and found the other mitten. She was very happy. Lily ran to her mom and said, "Mom, I found your mittens!" Her mom was happy too. She said, "Thank you, Lily! You are so helpful." Lily's mom asked her to add the mittens to the warm clothes pile. Lily did as her mom asked. She added the mittens to the pile. That night, Lily's mom and dad told her a fun story before bed. They were proud of their helpful girl. Lily was happy she could help her mom and dad. She knew she would help them more in the future.

One day, a little mole was playing in the dirt. He was harmless and liked to make new friends. He saw a big rock and thought it would be fun to play with. A big bird came down and said, "Mole, that is my rock. You can't play with it." The mole was sad, but he had an idea. "Let's have a race, and the one who wins can play with the rock." The bird agreed, and they started the race. The mole ran as fast as he could, and the bird flew high in the sky. They both tried very hard to win. In the end, the mole won the race. The bird was surprised but said, "You won, so you can play with the rock." The mole and the bird became friends and played with the rock together. They learned that sharing was more fun than fighting.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little pine tree. The pine tree had many friends. One day, a squirrel came up to the pine tree and said, "Can I live in your branches? I need a home." The pine tree nodded and said, "Yes, you can live here. I have space available for you." The squirrel was happy and thanked the pine tree. Later, a bird came to the pine tree and asked, "Can I build a nest in your branches?" The pine tree nodded again and said, "Yes, you can build a nest here. I have more space available for you too." The bird was happy and thanked the pine tree. Soon, the pine tree was full of friends who lived together happily.
