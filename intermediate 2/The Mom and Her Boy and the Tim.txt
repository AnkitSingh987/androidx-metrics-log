Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mom and Her Boy and the Tim

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a mom and her little boy, Tim. One day, Tim found a rare toy under his bed. It was a thermometer. Tim was very happy and wanted to play with it. Mom saw Tim with the thermometer and said, "Tim, don't play with that. It can break. I warn you, it's not a toy." But Tim didn't listen to mom. He took the thermometer and went outside to play with his friends. While playing, Tim dropped the thermometer, and it broke. Tim was sad and scared. He went back home to tell mom what happened. Mom was not happy. She said, "I told you not to play with it. Now it's broken, and we can't use it when you're sick." Tim felt bad and learned to listen to mom's warnings.

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside when the sun was shining. She would run and jump under the big trees and watch the sun shine through the leaves. One day, while Lily was playing, a big cloud came and covered the sun. The forest became dark and cold. Lily wanted the sun to shine again, but she didn't know how to make the cloud go away. She felt sad and missed the warm sun. Lily decided to ask her animal friends for help. The birds, squirrels, and rabbits all worked together to blow the big cloud away. They huffed and puffed, and finally, the cloud moved away from the sun. The sun started to shine again, making the wild forest bright and warm. Lily was happy, and she thanked her friends for helping her. They all played together in the sunshine, and the forest was full of joy.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to bake a cake. She asked her mom for help, but her mom was busy. So, Lily decided to bake the cake all by herself. She put flour, sugar, and eggs in a big bowl and mixed them together. While Lily was baking, there was a big storm outside. The lightning was bright and loud. Lily was scared, but she wanted to finish her cake. She put the cake in the oven and waited. When the cake was done, Lily saw that it was not pretty. It was big and lumpy, and it looked expensive because it used a lot of ingredients. Lily was sad and thought her mom would be mad. But her mom saw how hard Lily tried and gave her a hug. They ate the cake together, and it tasted very good.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to see the big red ball that was always there. When he got there, he saw his friend, Sue. Sue was sad. Tim asked her, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I can't find my toy. I heard a sound and when I looked, it was gone." They both started to look for the toy. They looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and near the swings. But they could not find the toy. Just then, a little dog came running up to them. The dog had Sue's toy in its mouth! The dog dropped the toy at their feet and wagged its tail. Sue was happy to see her toy again. Tim and Sue played with the dog and had lots of fun. The unexpected surprise made their day even better.

Tommy was miserable. He wanted to cook with Mommy, but she said he was too little. She said he could only watch and stir sometimes. Tommy did not like that. He wanted to cut and mix and taste. He wanted to make something yummy for Daddy. He saw a shiny star on the counter. It was a cookie cutter. Mommy used it to make star cookies for Christmas. Tommy had an idea. He took the star and ran to his room. He found some play-doh and rolled it flat. He used the star to cut out many star shapes. He put them on a plate and smiled. He had made star cookies for Daddy. He went to the kitchen and showed Mommy his star cookies. Mommy looked surprised. She said, "Tommy, what did you do? You can't cook with play-doh. It's not food. It's for playing." Tommy felt sad. He said, "But I wanted to cook for Daddy. I wanted to make him happy. I used the star like you." Mommy hugged Tommy. She said, "I'm sorry, Tommy. I know you wanted to help. But play-doh is not safe to eat. It can make you sick. You can cook with me when you are bigger. For now, you can help me decorate the real star cookies. We can make them colorful and sparkly. Daddy will love them." Tommy nodded. He gave Mommy the star cookie cutter. He said, "Okay, Mommy. I will help you. Can I have a star cookie too?" Mommy smiled. She said, "Of course, Tommy. You can have a star cookie. You are my little star."
