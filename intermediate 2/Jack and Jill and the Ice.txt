Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Jill and the Ice

Once upon a time there were two good friends, Jack and Jill. They were so excited to play together! Suddenly Jack noticed that his favourite toy was broken. He was very sad. Jill noticed and said, “Let’s fix it!” So Jack and Jill both grabbed a screwdriver. With a few twists and turns, they managed to fix the toy! Jack was very happy and to celebrate, he asked Jill, “Let’s get some ice cream!” Jill nodded in agreement and they walked to the ice cream shop. Once there, Jack pointed to the softest, yummiest looking ice-cream. Jill said, “That looks good! Let’s get it.” So they both enjoyed the soft ice cream and giggled together. The end.

Once upon a time, there a was a Grandpa. He was very clever. One day, Grandpa was walking in the park when he heard a little voice. It said, "Hello! What is your name?" Grandpa looked around and saw a little kid. He smiled and said, "My name is Grandpa and who are you?" The little kid answered, "My name is Sarah. I just arrived here and I don't know anyone." Grandpa was very clever and he knew how to make Sarah happy. He said, "Let's go for a walk together Sarah, and I'll show you around." Sarah was very excited and they both walked off together, chatting and getting to know each other. They had so much fun together that when it was time for Sarah to go home, she said goodbye to Grandpa and promised to come back to the park again soon.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She had a big bag of candy. Sue loved candy. She also had a silly hat on her head. The hat was very funny. At the park, Sue saw a big tree. She wanted to hug the tree. But when she hugged the tree, something unexpected happened. The tree was not a tree at all! It was a big, friendly bear in disguise. The bear was also silly. He liked Sue's hat and candy. They both laughed and shared the candy. Sue and the bear became good friends. They played in the park all day, and Sue learned that sometimes, things are not what they seem.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he went to the park with his mom. There, he saw a big gate. Behind the gate, there was a playground with swings and slides. Tim asked his mom, "Can we go inside the gate, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but you must stay close to me." Tim was very happy. They went inside the gate and he played on the swings and slides. He played for a long time until he was very tired. Tim's mom saw that he was tired. She said, "Tim, it's time to go home now." Tim didn't want to leave, so he asked his mom to repeat what she said. His mom said again, "It's time to go home now." Tim knew he was tired, so he agreed to go home. They walked out of the gate and went home. Tim had a fun day at the park, but he was ready for a nap.

Once upon a time, in a wide green field, there lived a big pink cow. The cow loved to chew on the soft green grass all day long. One day, while chewing, the cow met a little yellow bird. "Hi, cow!" said the bird. "Why do you chew on the grass all day?" The cow smiled and said, "I chew the grass because it makes me big and strong. It also helps the grass grow back even better!" The little bird thought about it and decided to try some grass too. The bird took a tiny bite and chewed it. The bird did not like it at all. "Yuck!" said the bird, "I don't like grass. I like to eat seeds and bugs instead." The cow laughed and said, "That's okay, little bird. We all like different things, and that's what makes us special." So, the cow kept chewing grass, and the bird ate seeds and bugs. They both grew big and strong in their own way. The moral of the story is that it's okay to be different and like different things.
