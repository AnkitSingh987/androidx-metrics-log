Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Smart Dog and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max was very intelligent. He loved to play and help his family. One day, Max saw a little girl crying. She had lost her toy. Max wanted to help her. He looked and looked until he found the toy. The little girl was so happy! She gave Max a big hug. Max felt good because he valued helping others. The little girl's mom was proud of Max. She wanted to give him a reward. She gave Max a big bone to say thank you. Max was happy and wagged his tail. He learned that being kind and helpful is important.

Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Kitty. Kitty had a friend, a little dog named Spot. One day, Spot did not feel good. He was sad and did not want to play. Kitty wanted to help Spot feel better. Kitty thought and thought. She had an idea! She went to find medicine to make Spot feel good. Kitty found the medicine and brought it to Spot. She said, "Here, Spot. This will help you feel good." Spot took the medicine from Kitty. He seemed to feel better already. Spot said, "Thank you, Kitty. You are a clever and good friend." They played together all day and were happy.

---

One day, a unique leaf fell from a tree. It was not like the other leaves. This leaf was big and red. It was very pretty. The wind blew the leaf far away from the tree. The leaf met a little girl. The girl saw the leaf and was very happy. She picked up the leaf and shouted, "Look! I found a special leaf!" The girl showed the leaf to her friends. They all liked the leaf too. The girl took the leaf home. She put the leaf in her room. Every day, she looked at the leaf and smiled. The unique leaf made her very happy. And the leaf was happy too, because it had a friend.

---

Once upon a time, there was a big raven. The raven was very sad and miserable. He had no friends to play with. One day, he went to the park to find a friend. At the park, he saw a little girl. The little girl was playing with a ball. The raven wanted to play too. So, he went to the girl and asked, "Can I play with you?" The little girl was surprised to see a talking raven. She thought for a moment and then replied, "Yes, you can play with me." The raven and the little girl played for a long time. They had so much fun together. The raven was not miserable anymore. He had found a friend. From that day on, the raven and the little girl became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly family. They wanted to go on a picnic. They packed a big basket with yummy food. They were very happy. The family went to the park. They found a nice spot under a big tree. They removed the food from the basket. They had sandwiches, fruits, and cookies. They laughed and shared the food. After the picnic, they played games. They ran and jumped. They had a lot of fun. The family went home with big smiles. They loved their jolly picnic day.

---

One day, a cool cat named Tim was walking in the park. He saw a little girl named Sue crying. Tim went to Sue and asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my toy. It does not belong to me now." Tim wanted to help Sue find her toy. They looked everywhere in the park. Suddenly, there was a small earthquake. The ground shook and they were scared. After the earthquake, they saw Sue's toy on the ground. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! My toy belongs to me again!" Tim and Sue became good friends and played together every day. They were happy and had lots of fun.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily and her dog, Max, went to play outside. They took a big, soft blanket to sit on. Lily was very thoughtful and always took care of Max. They went to the park and put the blanket on the grass. As they sat down, a little boy named Timmy came running. "Hi, can I play with you?" he asked. Lily said, "Yes, come sit on the blanket with us." They all played and had fun. Then, they saw a big, round ball coming towards them. They all got up and started to rush away from the ball. But the ball hit the blanket and it flew up into the air. "Oh no! Our blanket!" Lily cried. Just then, a big gust of wind blew the blanket back down, and it landed right on top of them like a tent. They all laughed and played under the blanket tent until it was time to go home.
