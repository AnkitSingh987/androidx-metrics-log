Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Toys

One day, a cat named Tom was very messy. He did not like to clean. His room was full of toys and clothes. His mom told him to clean it up, but Tom did not want to. He wanted to play and act like a superhero. Tom's mom had a plan. She said if Tom cleaned his room, she would give him a massage. Tom loved massages, so he started to clean. He put away his toys and clothes. It took a long time, but Tom did not give up. He wanted that massage. When Tom was done, his room was clean. His mom was happy and gave him a massage. Tom felt good and learned that cleaning up was not so bad. He also learned that if he did what his mom said, he would get a nice treat. From that day on, Tom tried to keep his room clean and not be so messy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a small tree with a big, green olive on it. Tim felt hungry and wanted to eat the olive. He was a bit fearful because he had never seen such a big olive before. But he was brave, and he picked the olive from the tree. As he held the olive, it started to glow! Tim was very surprised. He looked around and saw a small bug on the ground. The bug said, "Hello, I am a magic bug. I made the olive glow. If you give me the olive, I will give you a wish." Tim thought for a moment, and then he said, "I wish for a big, fun, and happy party with all my friends." The magic bug smiled and took the glowing olive. Suddenly, there was a big party with lots of food, games, and friends. Tim was so happy and had the best day ever. And he knew that sometimes, even when you are fearful, good things can happen.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hairy mechanic named Tom. Tom loved to fix things. He had a big, friendly dog named Spot. Tom and Spot were best friends. They played together and had a lot of fun. One day, Tom and Spot saw a big, scary cat. The cat was mean and liked to fight. Tom knew that Spot did not like to fight. He told Spot, "Be careful, Spot. The cat is not nice." The next day, Spot was playing outside. The mean cat came and tried to fight with Spot. Spot remembered what Tom said and ran away. The cat chased Spot, but Spot was too fast. Spot ran back to Tom, who was fixing a car. Tom saw the cat and knew that Spot was in trouble. He picked up a big stick and scared the cat away. Spot was safe, and Tom and Spot were happy. They knew they could always protect each other.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Ann. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. Ann loved to play outside, eat ice cream, and draw pictures. One hot day, Ann and her mom went to a new ice cream shop. They looked at the menu on the wall. There were so many yummy things to pick! Ann chose a big ice cream cone with three scoops. Her mom got a small one with two scoops. As they sat outside, the sun was very warm. Ann's ice cream began to melt. It dripped down her hand, but she didn't care. She just laughed and licked the drips. Ann and her mom enjoyed their sweet ice cream and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a busy bee named Buzz. Buzz loved to fly around and help his friends. One day, his friend, a big ant named Tony, needed help. Tony asked Buzz to deliver a gift to his friend, who lived far away. Buzz took the gift and flew high up in the sky. He saw the earth below him, with many trees, flowers, and animals. Buzz was happy to help his friend and see the beautiful earth at the same time. Buzz found Tony's friend and gave him the gift. The friend was so happy and thanked Buzz for his help. Buzz felt proud that he could help his friends and deliver the gift. He flew back home, ready for his next busy day helping others.
