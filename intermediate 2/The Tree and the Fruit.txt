Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tree and the Fruit

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had lots of fruit. The fruit was very yummy. A boy and a girl liked to eat the fruit from the tree. One day, the boy said, "We should remember to pick more fruit." The girl said, "Yes, let's pick the big fruit at the top of the tree." They climbed the tree to get the fruit. They picked and ate the fruit. But they forgot to remember to share the fruit with their friends. Their friends were sad. The boy and the girl felt bad. The big tree had no more fruit. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play a sport called ball. She would throw the ball, kick the ball, and catch the ball. Lily had many balls, and she was a bit spoiled because she always wanted more. One sunny day, Lily's mom told her to accept a new friend named Tim to play ball with her. Lily was not happy. She did not want to share her sport with Tim. But her mom said she must learn to play with others. Lily and Tim started to play ball. At first, Lily did not pass the ball to Tim. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim showed Lily a new way to play ball! It was so much fun! Lily and Tim laughed and played together. In the end, Lily was happy to accept Tim as her new friend, and they played ball every day.

Once upon a time, there was a hero named Tom. Tom was a sweet boy who lived in a small town. He always helped others and did good things. Tom had a big heart and was very kind. One day, Tom saw a little bird that could not fly. The bird was sad and needed help. Tom wanted to help the bird, so he took it home. He fed the bird and allowed it to rest in a warm place. After a few days, the bird felt better and could fly again. The bird was very happy and thanked Tom for his help. Tom learned that being a hero means helping others when they need it. The moral of the story is to be kind and help others, just like Tom.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with her toys. One day, she saw a shiny ornament on the tree. It was very pretty. Lily's mom said, "Lily, don't touch the ornament. It can break." But Lily did not listen. She thought it was silly to stop playing with the ornament. So, she took it off the tree and played with it. Later, Lily's cat saw the ornament and wanted to play too. The cat jumped and hit the ornament. It fell on the floor and broke. Lily was very sad. She should have listened to her mom. Now, the pretty ornament was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a judge who lived in a small, dirty house. Every day, the judge would put on her robe and go to work. One day, when the judge arrived at her job, she noticed her house was dirtier than usual. The judge went back home to find out why the house was so dirty. As soon as she opened the door, she saw a little three year old child putting mud all over the walls. "My house! What are you doing?" exclaimed the judge. The little three year old looked up with a smile. "Playing!" The judge laughed and shook her head. "No, we can't put mud all over the walls. Let's find something else to play with." The judge took the little three year old's hand and showed him some toys she had. The three year old was mesmerised and smiled at the judge. The judge smiled back. "That's much better than putting mud on the walls!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to learn about history. He liked to hear stories about kings, queens, and big castles. But sometimes, Tim was careless when playing with his toys. One day, Tim was playing with his favorite toy car. He was so excited that he forgot to be careful. He pushed the car too hard and it broke. Tim was very sad. He knew he had to be more careful next time. Tim wanted to fix his toy car. He asked his mommy for help. They worked together and tried their best. They did not give up. At last, they succeed in fixing the toy car. Tim was so happy, and he promised to be more careful when playing. He learned that being careful is important, and he could still enjoy his history stories with his fixed toy car.
