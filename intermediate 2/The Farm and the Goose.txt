Category: Intermediate
Title: The Farm and the Goose

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm lived an elderly goose. The goose was very kind and loved to help others. One day, the farmer needed to measure his farm. He asked the goose, "Can you help me measure the farm?" The goose said, "Yes, I can help you!" So, the elderly goose and the farmer walked around the farm. They measured the farm together. The farmer was very happy with the goose's help. They became good friends and lived happily ever after.

Sara and Tom went to a big place to eat with Mom and Dad. They sat at a table with a cloth and a candle. A man came to their table. He had a black suit and a white thing on his neck. He smiled and gave them a paper with words and pictures. He said, "This is the menu. You can choose what you want to eat and drink. Do you need help?" Sara looked at the menu. She saw a picture of a pizza with cheese and tomatoes. She liked pizza. She pointed at the picture and said, "I want this, please." Tom looked at the menu. He saw a picture of a burger with meat and lettuce. He liked burgers. He pointed at the picture and said, "I want this, please." Mom and Dad looked at the menu. They saw many things they liked. They told the man what they wanted. The man wrote down their choices and said, "Thank you. I will bring your food soon." The man left and went to the kitchen. Sara and Tom waited for their food. They were hungry. They saw a woman playing a big violin on a stage. She had long hair and a red dress. She made beautiful sounds with the violin. Sara and Tom liked the sounds. They clapped their hands and said, "Wow!" The woman heard them and smiled. She finished playing and bowed to the people. The people clapped their hands and said, "Bravo!" The woman bowed again and left the stage. Sara and Tom wanted to talk to the woman. They asked Mom and Dad if they could go to her. Mom and Dad said, "Yes, but be careful and polite." Sara and Tom got up from their table and walked to the woman. She was sitting on a chair with her violin. She saw them and smiled. She said, "Hello, little ones. Did you like my music?" Sara and Tom nodded and said, "Yes, we liked it very much. You are very good. Can we touch your violin?" The woman said, "Sure, you can touch it gently. But you have to be quiet, because I am deaf. I cannot hear your words. I can only see your lips and feel your hands." Sara and Tom were surprised. They did not know what deaf meant. They asked, "What is deaf?" The woman said, "Deaf means I cannot hear anything. I was born like this. But I can still play music, because I can feel the vibrations and the rhythm. Music is my language." Sara and Tom touched the violin and felt the strings and the wood. They felt the vibrations and the rhythm. They said, "That is amazing. We are sorry you cannot hear us. But we are happy you can play music. Music is beautiful." The woman said, "Thank you. You are very kind. What are your names?" Sara and Tom told her their names. They asked her name. She said, "My name is Anna. I am happy to meet you." Sara and Tom said, "We are happy to meet you too, Anna. You are our friend." Anna said, "You are my friends too. Do you want to learn some signs?" Sara and Tom said, "Yes, please. What are signs?" Anna said, "Signs are how I talk with my hands. I can show you some words and letters. Do you want to try?" Sara and Tom said, "Yes, we want to try. That sounds fun." Anna showed them how to make signs with their hands. She taught them how to say hello, goodbye, thank you, and their names. Sara and Tom learned fast. They liked the signs. They said, "This is cool. Thank you, Anna." Anna said, "You are welcome, Sara and Tom. You are very smart. I like you." Sara and Tom said, "We like you too, Anna. You are very nice. Can we hug you?" Anna said, "Of course, you can hug me. I like hugs." Sara and Tom hugged Anna. They felt her warmth and her heartbeat. They said, "We love you, Anna." Anna said, "I love you too, Sara and Tom. You are very sweet." They smiled and hugged again. They felt happy and proud. They learned something new and made a new friend. They said, "You are the best, Anna." Anna said, "You are the best, Sara and Tom."
