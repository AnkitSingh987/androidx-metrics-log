Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bird

One day, a small girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a huge blueberry bush. Lily's mom said, "Stay here, I will get a bag to pick some blueberries." Lily was very happy. While Lily stayed by the blueberry bush, she saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, I have a secret about this bush." Lily was very excited. "What is the secret?" she asked. The bird whispered, "One of the blueberries is magic." Lily started to look for the magic blueberry. She picked many blueberries, but they all looked the same. Then, she found one that was bigger and shinier than the rest. She knew it was the magic one. When her mom came back, Lily showed her the special blueberry. They both smiled and knew that something fun would happen.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a cricket jumping around. The cricket looked crazy, jumping here and there. Tim thought it was funny. Tim said to the cricket, "Why are you jumping so crazy?" The cricket said, "I am looking for my friend. She is lost." Tim wanted to help. He said, "I will help you find your friend." They walked and walked. Then, Tim saw something on the ground. He stumbled on a rock. He looked down and saw the lost friend. The cricket was so happy. They both jumped around like crazy crickets.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. The sun was warm on his fur. He saw a big tree and went to sniff it. Max felt happy and wagged his tail. Suddenly, Max felt an itch on his leg. He tried to scratch it, but the itch did not go away. Max started to feel sad. He wanted the itch to stop. Max saw his friend, a bird named Tim. Max shouted, "Tim, help me! I have an itch!" Tim flew down and used his beak to scratch Max's leg. The itch went away. Max felt happy again. He thanked Tim and they played together under the warm sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to run fast in the park with her friends. One day, Sue found a big ball of thread in her room. Sue thought it would be fun to play with the thread. Sue tried to pull the thread out of the ball, but it was a struggle. The thread was stuck! Sue pulled and pulled, but the thread would not come out. Sue was sad, but she did not give up. She kept trying to pull the thread out. Finally, after trying very hard, Sue got the thread out of the ball. Sue was very happy! She took the thread to the park and ran fast with her friends. They all had lots of fun playing with the thread.

Once upon a time, there was a big red jeep. The jeep liked to drive on rough roads. It went up and down the hills. It was very fun. One day, the jeep met a nice man. The man liked to cook. He made yummy food. The jeep wanted to help the man cook. So, the man and the jeep cooked together. They made a big, yummy meal. They shared the food with their friends. Everyone was happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She was three years old and liked to play in her room. Every night, when she was getting ready to go to sleep, she went to her wardrobe and opened the door. Inside the wardrobe lived a little mouse. Jane always said hello to the mouse. “Hi there, Mr Mouse,” she would say, giving him a big smile. The mouse was very restless. One evening, he mentioned to Jane that he wanted to get out and explore. "Please, can I come out?" he asked. Jane thought it was a great idea. She opened the door wider so that the mouse could come out and run around. The mouse was so excited. He ran around the room, exploring every corner. Jane watched him with a smile on her face. That night Jane and the mouse were both very sleepy when it was time for bed. So off they went, until the morning when they would play again.
