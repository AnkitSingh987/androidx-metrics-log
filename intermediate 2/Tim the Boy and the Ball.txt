Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big, red ball. He loved to play with his ball all day. One day, Tim saw a big bag in the park. The bag had a picture of a star on it. Tim thought the bag was very important. Tim wanted to put his ball in the important bag. But the bag was high up in a tree. Tim tried to jump, but he could not reach the bag. Then, he had a great idea. He would shoot his ball at the bag to make it fall down. Tim took a step back and aimed his ball at the bag. He threw the ball as hard as he could. The ball went up, up, up and hit the bag. The important bag fell down from the tree. Tim was so happy. Now, he could put his ball in the important bag and take it home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big plan for the day. Lily wanted to go to the park and play with her new toy. But when she arrived at the park, Lily was sad. Someone else was playing with her new toy! Lily was very unhappy. She didn't like that someone else was playing with her toy. She tried to be brave and asked the other child if he would share the toy. But, the other child said no. Suddenly, Lily had a new plan. She decided to make a new toy with things she found in the park. Lily found some sticks, leaves, and stones. She put them together to make a new toy. The other child saw Lily's new toy and wanted to play with it too. So, Lily and the other child played together with the new toy. They had lots of fun, and Lily was so happy!

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a brilliant, big hill. Tim wanted to go up the hill and see what would happen. So, he started to walk up the hill. As Tim went up the hill, he met a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tim! What are you doing?" Tim said, "I am going up the hill. I want to see what will happen. Do you want to come with me?" Sue said, "Yes, I want to come too!" Tim and Sue walked up the hill together. When they got to the top, they saw a big, beautiful rainbow. They were so happy they went up the hill. They sat and looked at the rainbow. They knew that something good can happen when you try new things.

Lucy was playing in the garden when she suddenly heard a loud noise. She looked up and saw a big dog standing on its hind legs. Lucy was scared and started to run, but she stepped on the dog's heel. The dog yelped and Lucy felt very embarrassed. She didn't know what to do so she lay down on the ground. The dog looked surprised and stopped barking. Lucy was still too embarrassed to move, so they both lay there until some other people came to the garden and scare the dog away. Lucy was relieved, but she was still very embarrassed. She went home and tried to forget the incident.

Once upon a time, there was a goose. The goose had many friends. They liked to play together. One day, the goose found a yummy treat. It was a big, red apple. The goose wanted to eat the apple. But the goose remembered to mind his friends. He thought, "I should share this yummy apple with my friends." So, the goose called all his friends. They came running to see the apple. The goose shared the yummy apple with his friends. They all took turns to eat. Everyone was happy. They said, "Thank you, goose!" The goose was happy too. He knew that sharing was good. The moral of the story is to always share with your friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a fair lady. She had a pretty house with a big garden. The lady loved to grow flowers in her garden. Every day, she would water them and sing to them. One sunny day, while the lady was watering her flowers, she heard a tiny voice. "Peek-a-boo!" the voice said. The lady looked around, but she could not see anyone. She kept watering her flowers, but she heard the voice again. "Peek-a-boo!" it said. The lady looked down and saw a little bug. The bug was playing peek-a-boo with her! "Hello, little bug," the lady said. "Do you like my flowers?" The bug nodded and said, "Yes, they are very pretty. Can I live here too?" The fair lady smiled and said, "Of course, you can stay in my garden and play peek-a-boo with me every day." And so, the lady and the little bug became friends and lived happily ever after.
