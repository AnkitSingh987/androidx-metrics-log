Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Jacket and the Lake

Once there was a boy who loved adventures. He always liked to explore new places and try new things. One day, his parents gave him a special jacket. It was bright red and he loved it. He couldn't wait to go outside and show off his new jacket. He went on so many adventures. He explored in forests and climbed mountains. He even went swimming in the lake. Every time he went on an adventure he wore his bright red jacket with pride. It kept him warm and safe no matter where he went. He was so proud of his jacket and he felt like the most adventurous person ever. Everywhere he went, people gave him compliments on his jacket. He loved that his jacket made him feel special and brave. He was so thankful to his parents for giving him this special jacket.

---

Once there was a snowman called Sam. He was made by two children who loved him very much. Sam was special because he was made from cheap snow. His eyes were made from coal, his mouth from a string and his hat from a stick. The children often talked to him. One day the younger child said to him, 'Sam, do you love snow?' Sam smiled and said, 'Yes, I love snow very much. It's very cold but I like it!' The children laughed and hugged him. They kissed him on the head and said, 'We love you too, Sam!' Sam smiled and said, 'I love you too, children!' He stayed there with them in the snow all day, enjoying their company.

---

One day, a fast rabbit and a slow turtle had a race. The sun was very hot, and the rabbit was tired. The rabbit saw a big tree and said, "I will sit in the shade and rest. The turtle is slow, and I can still win." The turtle kept walking. He did not stop. The rabbit fell asleep under the tree. The wind blew, and a delicate leaf fell on the rabbit's nose. The rabbit woke up and saw the turtle was almost at the finish line. The rabbit ran fast, but the turtle won the race. The rabbit said, "You are slow, but you did not stop. I am fast, but I stopped. You won because you did not give up." The rabbit learned that it is important to keep going, even if you are slow.

Once upon a time, there was a little owl. The owl liked to relax. He sat in his tree every day. The owl was always available to help his friends. One day, a little bird came to the owl. The bird was sad. The owl asked, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I lost my mom." The owl wanted to help the bird. So, they both looked for the bird's mom. They looked up and down. They looked left and right. Soon, they found the bird's mom. The bird was happy. The owl was happy too. They all went back to the tree to relax together. The end.

One day, Andy was outside in the park playing. He saw a fair and rushed over to join in the fun. There were so many games and rides! Suddenly, Andy felt a whoosh of wind and saw a colourful fall before him. It looked like a giant rainbow with different coloured leaves on the ground. He reached out and tried to pick them up. Andy's mom chuckled and said; "Those are the leaves falling from the trees. It's so fair!" Andy smiled at his mom and leapt into the sky like a kite and yelled; "Mom, watch me rush". He ran around the park twirling and spinning, feeling the wind on his face, letting the leaves rush around him. When he was done he asked his mom; "Can we come back tomorrow to play again?" His mom smiled and said; "Of course we can, it's only fair we come back." Andy grinned, and they both rushed off home, hand in hand.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia saw a swing and wanted to play on it. She asked her mom, "Can I please play on the swing?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can play on the swing." Mia ran to the swing and saw that it was empty. She was very happy. She sat on the swing and started to swing back and forth. She was having a lot of fun. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the swing very high. Mia was scared but then, the wind stopped and the swing came back down. Mia was safe and happy again. She told her mom, "That was fun, but I want to go home now." They went home, and Mia told her friends about her adventure on the swing.
