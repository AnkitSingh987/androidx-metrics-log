Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Rat and the Cat

One day, a rat named Tom was playing in the park. Tom saw a dull, old log and had an idea. He wanted to balance on the log like a tightrope walker. Tom thought it would be fun, and he could show his friends. Tom climbed on the log and tried to balance. He wobbled and almost fell, but he kept trying. His friend, Cat, saw him and asked, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom replied, "I'm trying to balance on this log. I want to show our friends." Cat decided to help Tom. Together, they practiced balancing on the log. After a while, they could both balance very well. Their friends saw them and clapped. Tom and Cat were happy. They learned that if you work together and help each other, you can do great things.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and jump all day long. He had a navy blue ball that he liked to chase in the park. One day, Spot's ball rolled far away. Spot ran and ran to find it. He looked under a tree and behind a big rock, but he could not find his navy blue ball. Then, a kind bird saw Spot and wanted to help. The bird picked up the navy blue ball in its beak and flew back to Spot. The bird gave the ball to Spot and he was very happy. Spot returned home with his navy blue ball, ready to play again.

Once upon a time there was a happy frog called Jeff. Jeff was always looking out for his friends and enjoyed nothing more than playing with them. One day Jeff and his friends were playing in the pond, when they heard a loud noise. They all looked around, but they could not see anything. Suddenly, a big crack split open in the middle of the pond and Jeff was very scared. His friend Hazel said "Don't worry Jeff, I know just what to do. Follow me!" She led Jeff and his friends over to a lily pad and said "Put your hands together like this and rub them round and round. It'll make the crack go away!" So they did, and sure enough, the crack began to slowly close. Jeff was so relieved and he was so happy that he hugged Hazel. They all jumped back into the pond, happy to be playing together again.

Once upon a time, there was a small gray bug. The bug liked to crawl on the ground. One day, the bug found a shiny knife in the grass. The knife was very big for the little bug. The bug met a friendly bird. The bird said, "Hi, little bug! What are you doing with that big knife?" The bug said, "I found it in the grass. I want to take it home." The bird helped the bug carry the knife. They were a good team. The bug crawled and the bird flew. They took the knife to the bug's home. The bug and the bird were happy and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big sky, there was a helpful comet. The comet loved to work hard and help others. It had a bright tail that lit up the dark night. All the stars and the moon were happy to have the comet as a friend. One day, the comet saw a little star who was sad. The star was too small to shine bright. The helpful comet wanted to work and make the little star happy. So, the comet went close to the star and shared its bright tail. Now, the little star could shine bright too! The little star was very happy and thanked the helpful comet. All the other stars and the moon saw what the comet did. They learned that working together and helping each other made everyone happy. The moral of the story is to be helpful and work together to make others happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, patient cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with bubbles. One sunny day, Tom went outside to play with his favorite bubble toy. Tom's friend, a little girl named Sue, came to play with him. Sue blew the bubbles, and Tom tried to catch them. They had so much fun! Tom jumped and ran, trying to break the bubbles with his paw. But then, Tom saw a very big bubble. He wanted to break it, but he was patient. He waited for the right moment. When the big bubble came close, Tom jumped high and broke it with a big smile. Sue clapped and laughed, and they continued to play with bubbles all day long.
