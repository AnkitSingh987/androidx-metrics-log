Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Brown Dog and the Lucy

Once upon a time, there was a small brown dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat rice. One day, he saw a big bag of rice in the kitchen. He wanted to eat it all, but he needed help. Spot went to his friend, a little girl named Lucy. He said, "Lucy, can you help me eat the rice?" Lucy did not understand what Spot wanted. She asked, "What do you want, Spot?" Spot barked and jumped. Lucy laughed and said, "Oh, I understand now!" Lucy and Spot went to the kitchen. They ate the rice together. The rice was yummy and made them both very happy. They played and laughed all day. From that day on, Lucy and Spot always helped each other and had lots of fun.

One day, Tim went to a big stadium with his dad. They were very excite to watch a fun game. The stadium was full of happy people. Tim saw his friend, Sam, in the crowd. They both smiled and waved at each other. At half-time, Tim's dad bought him a big, red balloon. Tim loved his balloon and held it tight. Suddenly, a strong wind blew and Tim's balloon flew away. Tim was very sad and his face turned red. He felt embarrassed. Sam saw Tim's red face and came over. "Don't worry, Tim," Sam said. "I have a blue balloon. We can share it." Tim smiled and felt better. They both held the blue balloon and enjoyed the rest of the game together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was scared of the dark. One night, she heard a noise outside her window. She hid under her bed, hoping to feel safe. Amy's big brother, Tim, heard her cry. He came into her room and asked, "Why are you scared, Amy?" She told him about the noise she heard outside. Tim looked out the window but saw nothing. "It's okay, Amy. I will spend the night with you, so you feel safe," he said. Amy and Tim spent the night together in her room. They talked and laughed, and soon, Amy was not scared anymore. They both fell asleep, feeling safe and happy. The next morning, they found out the noise was just a friendly cat playing outside. From that day on, Amy was never scared of the dark again.

One sunny Sunday, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a new toy car that could zip very fast. He loved to play with it and watch it go. But Tim was a little selfish and didn't want to share his toy car with anyone. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's toy car and asked, "Can I play with your car too?" Tim said, "No, it's mine!" and zipped the car away from Sue. Sue was sad, but she didn't say anything. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running and took Tim's toy car in its mouth. The dog zipped away very fast. Tim and Sue ran after the dog, but they couldn't catch it. Tim was sad and started to cry. Sue said, "Don't worry, we can find it together!" Tim learned that sharing and helping each other is better than being selfish.

Tom and Anna are friends. They go to the same school. They wear the same uniform. It is blue and white. They like their uniform. It is lovely. One day, Tom and Anna are playing in the playground. They see a big boy. He is not from their school. He wears a different uniform. It is red and black. He looks mean. The big boy comes to Tom and Anna. He says, "Your uniform is ugly. My uniform is better. Give me your uniform or I will hit you." Tom and Anna are scared. They do not want to give their uniform. They do not want to be hit. They say, "No, go away. Our uniform is lovely. Your uniform is bad. Leave us alone." The big boy is angry. He tries to grab Tom's uniform. Tom does not let him. He slaps the big boy's hand. The big boy cries. He runs away. Tom and Anna are happy. They hug each other. They say, "We are brave. We are strong. We are friends. We love our uniform."
