Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ball and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a pretty ball. The ball was red and shiny. It liked to roll all day long. It would roll in the grass, on the road, and even in the house. The ball was very happy when it rolled. One day, the ball met a new friend. The friend was a long neck dinosaur. The dinosaur's name was Dina. Dina liked to watch the ball roll. She would stretch her long neck to see the ball roll far away. Dina and the ball had lots of fun together. Dina would use her long neck to help the ball roll even faster. The pretty ball would roll up and down hills, and Dina would run beside it. They would play all day long. The ball and Dina were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a young boy called Johnny was in his bedroom playing with his toys. He noticed something strange on his bed. It was a sack filled with sheets. Johnny was confused. He had never seen this sack before. He ran to his mother and asked her about the sack. His mother said it was a surprise from his grandmother. Johnny was so excited. He opened the sack and found lots of sheets inside. He felt so happy! Johnny loved his new sheets and spent all day playing with them. He wrapped himself up like a mummy and pretended to be a detective. He also made a fort out of the sheets and sailed across the ocean. But Johnny was a bit ignorant. He left the sack lying around the house, and before he knew it, the sack disappeared. Johnny was so sad! He never saw the sack again and he was sure his grandmother would be angry. But his mother just smiled and said that some things are meant to be a surprise.

Once upon a time, there was an old tree. The old tree had a big crack. A little boy saw the crack and wanted to explain it to his friends. The boy and his friends went to the old tree. They wanted to see the crack. They were all very excited. But when they got there, the crack was gone! They were all very surprised. Then, a wise bird came and said, "The old tree heard you and fixed its crack because it felt sad." The children learned that they should be kind to everyone, even trees. The old tree became their friend, and they played happily ever after.

Tom is a cat who likes to observe. He likes to watch the birds, the bugs, and the fish in the pond. He thinks they are fun and pretty. He wants to play with them, but they are too fast or too far. One day, Tom sees a big fish in the pond. It has a long tail and shiny scales. Tom thinks it is the most beautiful fish he has ever seen. He wants to catch it and make it his friend. He sneaks closer to the pond, hiding behind the grass and the rocks. The fish does not see Tom. It swims around the pond, flicking its tail. Tom waits for the right moment. He jumps out of his hiding place and pounces on the fish. But the fish is too smart. It dives into the deep water, where Tom cannot reach. Tom lands in the pond with a splash. He gets wet and cold. He feels silly and sad. The fish comes out of the water and looks at Tom. It sees that Tom is not a bad cat, just a curious one. It feels sorry for Tom. It swims closer to Tom and says, "Hello, cat. I am Finn. Do you want to be my friend?" Tom is surprised and happy. He says, "Hello, fish. I am Tom. Yes, I want to be your friend. You are very pretty and clever. I am sorry I tried to catch you. I just wanted to play with you." Finn smiles and says, "It's okay, Tom. I forgive you. You can play with me, but not in the water. The water is too deep for you. You can watch me from the shore, or we can talk and sing. I like to talk and sing. Do you?" Tom nods and says, "I do. I like to observe and learn. You can teach me about the pond and the fish. I can teach you about the land and the cat. We can be different and still be friends." Finn and Tom become friends. They play and talk and sing every day. They observe each other and learn from each other. They are happy and grateful. They have a long tail and a deep bond.
