Category: Intermediate
Title: The Queen and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there was a queen. She was very sleepy. One day, she went to her garden to take a nap. She saw a big tree with a nice shade. The queen lay down under the tree and fell asleep. A little bird came and saw the queen sleeping. The bird wanted to record the queen's snore. It was a funny sound. The bird flew to its nest and told its friend, "I will record the queen's snore. It is so funny!" But when the bird tried to record the queen, she woke up. The queen was not happy. She asked the bird, "Why did you wake me up?" The bird said, "I wanted to record your snore. I am sorry." The queen was sad and said, "Please, do not record me. I need to sleep." The bird felt bad and wanted to help the queen. The bird sang a sweet song to make the queen sleepy again. The queen smiled and said, "Thank you, little bird." She fell asleep, and the bird did not record her snore. They both were happy, and the queen had a good nap.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a train station. Many people would come and go from the station every day. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the station with her mom. Lucy had a red ball that she loved to play with. Her mom told her to be careful not to lose her ball. Lucy and her mom waited for their train. While they waited, Lucy played with her red ball. She would throw it up in the air and catch it. But one time, Lucy threw her ball too high. She could not catch it, and it rolled away. Lucy was sad and worried that she would lose her ball. Lucy's mom saw the ball rolling away and quickly ran to get it. She brought it back to Lucy just as the train arrived. Lucy was so happy that her ball was not lost. They got on the train and found seats. Lucy held her ball tightly, making sure it was safe. She knew that her mom would always be there to help her when she needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Max. Max loved to tell his friends what to do. One day, Max saw his friends gather around a big tree. Max walked up to his friends and said, "Hey, let me see what you're looking at!" Max's friends moved aside, and Max saw a big red mug stuck in the tree. Max said, "I want that mug! Let's work together to get it down." Max's friends nodded and started to help. One friend climbed the tree, while the others held onto the friend's legs. They tried and tried, but the mug was still stuck. Finally, Max had an idea. He told his friends to shake the tree. They all shook the tree together, and the red mug fell down. Max caught the mug and said, "We did it! Thank you, friends!" From that day on, Max learned to be nicer to his friends. They all played and gathered together, happy as can be. And Max always shared his big red mug with everyone.

Harry had a bracelet. It was very special because it belonged to his mom. He liked to look at it and do special tricks with it. One day, he decided to show his dad the bracelet. Unfortunately, his dad thought it was stupid. He started to blink rapidly, trying not to cry. His dad said he had to get rid of it. So, Harry reluctantly threw it in the landfill. That night, he couldn't sleep, so he decided to go find it. But when he got to the landfill, he realized that it must be buried deep. He started to cry, feeling so sad and stupid. He knew he'd never see his special bracelet again.

Once upon a time there was a young boy. His name was Jay. He had a special weapon that his father had given him. Every day Jay would go out into the garden and play with his weapon. One day a neighbour saw him playing and came up to him. “Good morning, Jay!” said the neighbour. “What are you playing?” Jay proudly held up his weapon. “I am playing with my special weapon.” The neighbour smiled. “That’s wonderful, Jay. The best thing to do is to relax and have fun.” Jay smiled and nodded. He enjoyed relaxing with his special weapon. He was so young, but already knew how important it was to take time to relax. The End.
