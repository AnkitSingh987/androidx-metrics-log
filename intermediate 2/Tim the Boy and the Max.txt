Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Max

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because he was going to school for the first time. He had a big smile on his face and a new bag on his back. But, he also felt a little scared because he didn't know what it would be like. At school, Tim met a nice girl named Sue. They played together and had lots of fun. One day, they saw a broken toy on the ground. Tim and Sue looked at each other, wondering who it belonged to. They decided to keep it safe so they could give it back to the owner later. The next day, Tim and Sue found the owner of the broken toy. It was their friend, Max. Max was sad because his toy was broken. Tim and Sue wanted to help Max feel better. They took a cloth and began to wipe the toy clean. Max was so happy to see his friends help him. He knew that his new friends at school would always be there for him. And from that day on, Tim, Sue, and Max were the best of friends.

One day, a toy car was broken. The boy who owned the car was sad. He wanted to repair the car. He asked his dad for help. His dad said they needed an x-ray to see what was wrong. The boy and his dad went to the doctor. The doctor took an x-ray of the toy car. The x-ray showed that a small part inside the car was broken. The boy was frustrated because he could not fix it by himself. The dad tried to repair the car, but it was too hard. They could not find a new part to fix it. The boy was very sad. The toy car stayed broken and the boy could not play with it anymore. The boy learned that sometimes, things cannot be fixed.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there lived a fancy queen. She had a pretty dress and a shiny crown. The queen liked to hang pretty things on the walls of her castle. One day, the queen met a little girl. The little girl said, "Hello, queen! I have a pretty picture for you." The queen smiled and said, "Thank you! I will hang it on my wall." The queen took the picture and hung it on her wall. The little girl was very happy. They became good friends and played together in the castle. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a small ball near the drain. He wanted to get the ball, but he felt tired. Tim asked his friend, Sue, "Can you help me get the ball?" Sue looked at the drain and said, "I don't know. It looks scary." Tim felt sad, but he really wanted the ball. So, he decided to try to get it himself. Sue just shrugged and watched. Tim reached for the ball, but he slipped and fell into the drain. Sue screamed and ran to find help. But it was too late. Tim was gone, and the ball was still near the drain.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, named Sam and Sarah. Sam and Sarah were always up for an adventure. One day, they decided to have an adventure in the woods. As they walked around in the woods, they found a small clearing and they decided to take a closer look. In the middle of the clearing, they saw lots of little marks on the ground. Sam and Sarah then knew they had work to do. They started to work together to figure out what the marks were. They studied the marks and then Sarah figured out that they were made by little bugs. Sam and Sarah were so excited! They kept looking at the marks until they couldn't find anymore. At the end of their adventure, Sam and Sarah were very proud of their work. They were happy to see how small the marks were and how they made an interesting discovery. They had a great adventure and learned a lot about nature.
