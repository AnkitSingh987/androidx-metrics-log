Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to kick a ball. One day, he saw a big waste bin near his house. He thought it was funny and wanted to play near it. Tim said to his friend, "Let's kick the ball near the waste bin!" His friend said, "No, Tim. It is not healthy to play near waste." But Tim did not listen. He thought it would be fun. Tim kicked the ball and it went into the waste bin. He was sad and wanted his ball back. But when he looked inside, he saw a cat! The cat was stuck in the waste bin. Tim helped the cat out and took it home. His friend was right, playing near waste was not healthy, but Tim was happy he saved the cat.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a smelly frog named Fred. Fred had a long staff. He used the staff to help him hop from one place to another. Fred was not happy because he could feel how smelly he was. He wanted to be clean and nice like his friends. One day, Fred met a nice bird named Betty. Betty said, "Fred, you are smelly! Let me help you get clean." Fred was happy and said, "Thank you, Betty! I want to be clean like you." So, Betty took Fred to a big pond where they could both splash and play. They played in the water for a long time. Fred used his staff to make big splashes. Betty used her wings to make big splashes too. They laughed and had so much fun. When they were done playing, Fred was clean and did not feel smelly anymore. Fred and Betty became best friends. They played together every day. Fred was happy because he was clean and had a good friend. And they lived happily ever after in the big forest.

One day, a clumsy girl named Lily went to play in the park. She liked to run and jump, but sometimes she would fall. Lily saw a little boy named Tim playing with a ball. She wanted to play with him, so she asked, "Can I play with you?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with me!" They played together and had lots of fun. But then, Lily tripped and fell on the ball. The ball popped and made a loud noise. Lily was scared and started to cry. Tim saw how sad Lily was and wanted to help. He replied, "Don't worry, Lily. We can still have fun without the ball." Tim showed her how to play tag and hide-and-seek. They played all day and became good friends. The unexpected thing was that Lily learned it's okay to be clumsy, and that true friends will still like you for who you are.

Dylan and his mommy were out in the garden. Dylan was peek-a-booing behind the big tree. He heard something and saw big dog peeking at him. The dog said in a bossy voice, “Who are you?” Dylan answered, “My name is Dylan. This is my mommy and this is our garden.” The dog said, “This garden is mine. You must run away!” Dylan was scared and he ran to his mommy. His mommy said, “No, this garden is not yours. We are here to play and have fun.” The bossy dog still looked scary but he said, “Okay, then let’s all play together!” They had lots of fun playing hide and seek. Dylan and the dog played tag and chased each other around the garden. At the end of the day, they all said goodbye and Dylan and his mommy went back inside.

Once upon a time, there was a large, round ball. It loved to spin all day long. It spun so fast that it made everyone smile. One day, the ball met a funny cartoon cat. The cat was very happy to see the ball. "Hello, big ball!" said the cartoon cat. "I like how you spin. Can you spin with me?" "Of course!" replied the large ball. "Let's spin together!" So, the large ball and the cartoon cat started to spin. They spun and spun, laughing and having fun. As they spun, more friends came to join them. There were dogs, birds, and even a little mouse. Everyone was spinning together, enjoying their time. Soon, the whole town was spinning with the large ball and the cartoon cat. They all laughed and played, making new friends. The spinning made everyone feel happy and loved. And from that day on, the large ball, the cartoon cat, and all their friends would meet to spin and have fun together. They learned that spinning together made them all happy and brought them closer as friends.
