Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Taxi and the Park

One day, a taxi was driving down the street. The taxi was yellow and big. The taxi driver was happy and liked to paint. The taxi driver saw a man who wanted a ride. The man got in the taxi and said, "I want to go to the spicy food shop." The taxi driver knew where it was and drove the man there. When they got to the spicy food shop, the taxi driver said, "I will wait for you." The man went inside and got some spicy food. Then, the taxi driver and the man went to a park. The taxi driver took out his paint and painted a picture of the spicy food and the taxi. They had a fun day!

Once there was a gentle little girl named Anna who loved buttons. Everywhere she went there were buttons! On her clothes, in her books and even on Mommy's dresser. One day, Anna asked her Mom if she could own a button. Her Mom said of course, so she gave Anna the beautiful blue button that she had found in her garden. Anna was so excited and couldn't wait to put the button on her shirt. Mommy smiled and said, "Now, Anna, be very gentle when you put the button on your shirt." Anna was careful and was so proud that she had her very own button. The next day, Anna showed her friends the button on her shirt and told them that it was hers. They all oohed and aahed at the beautiful blue button and Anna smiled with pride.

One day, a gifted dog named Max went to the park. Max could wave his paw like a person. He loved to show his friends his trick. Max saw a big tree and went to sit under it. Max waved to all his friends as they walked by. A little boy named Tim saw Max and came over to say hi. Tim said, "Hi Max! I like how you wave. Can you teach me?" Max nodded and waved his paw. Tim waved his hand back. They were both happy and laughed. Max saw a man with some meat. Max wanted the meat, so he waved to the man. But the man did not give Max the meat. Max was sad. Tim said, "Max, you can't have everything you want. It's good to share." Max learned that it's nice to share and not take everything. And Max and Tim played in the park, happy together.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Kay. They were walking along and Jack told Kay about a game he had seen. "Let's measure our steps," Jack said, "Whoever takes the biggest steps wins!" Kay was excited and was ready to begin. She took a big step and Jack measured it with a stick. "My step was so big! I'm so proud of myself," Kay said. But Jack said, "Wait, there's still one more step to take." Kay was curious, so she asked what it was. "You have to take a step with only your heel!" Jack said. Kay was worried because this was harder than she had expected. But she was brave and tried it. She stepped on her heel and Jack measured it with his stick. To Kay's surprise, it was the biggest step of them all! She had won the game and she was proud. "Now I know that nothing is too hard to measure!" Kay said.

One day, a lovely cat named Zip wanted to prepare a big party for all his friends. He was so excited! He started to make a list of all the things he needed for the party. He wanted to have balloons, cake, and games. As Zip was getting everything ready, his friend, a little bird named Pip, came to see what he was doing. "Zip, what are you doing?" asked Pip. Zip told Pip about the party he was preparing. Pip was worried. "Zip, I can't fly high enough to reach the balloons you want to put up. I won't be able to play with them." Zip felt sad. He didn't want his friend Pip to feel left out of the fun. So, Zip thought and thought about how to fix the problem. Then, he had a great idea! He decided to put some balloons lower, so Pip could reach them too. Pip was very happy and thanked Zip. The day of the party came, and all the friends had a great time. They played games, ate cake, and had fun with the balloons. Zip and Pip were so happy that everyone could enjoy the party together.
