Category: Intermediate
Title: Timmy and the Car

Timmy loves animals. He wants to be a veterinarian when he grows up. He likes to play with his toy animals and pretend he is helping them. He has a dog, a cat, a rabbit and a turtle. One day, he decides to take his turtle for a walk. He puts a leash on his turtle and goes outside. He sees his friend Lily across the street. She has a ball. He wants to play with her. "Hi, Lily!" he shouts. "Can I play with you?" "Hi, Timmy!" she shouts back. "Sure, you can play with me. But what is that?" She points at his turtle. Timmy smiles. "This is my turtle. His name is Speedy. He is very slow, but he is very smart. He likes to walk with me." Lily laughs. She thinks Timmy's turtle is funny. "That is a funny turtle, Timmy. But how can he walk with you? He is so slow. He will not catch the ball." Timmy says, "He can walk with me. He just needs some time. He will catch the ball. You will see." He lets go of the leash and throws the ball. He runs after it. He hopes Speedy will follow him. But Speedy does not follow him. He stops and looks around. He sees a big car coming. He is scared. He hides in his shell. The car does not see Speedy. It does not stop. It does not yield. It goes very fast. It is very loud. Timmy hears the car. He turns around. He sees Speedy in the middle of the street. He is very worried. He wants to save him. He runs back to Speedy. He tries to pick him up. But the car is too close. It is too dangerous. Lily sees Timmy. She is very scared. She screams. "Timmy, stop! The car is coming! Come back!" Timmy hears Lily. He listens to her. He knows she is right. He leaves Speedy. He runs back to the sidewalk. He is safe. The car passes by. It does not hit Speedy. It does not hit Timmy. It does not hit anything. It goes away. Timmy and Lily are relieved. They hug each other. They are glad they are okay. They look at Speedy. He is still in his shell. He is still in the street. He is not okay. Timmy cries. He loves Speedy. He wants to help him. He says, "Speedy, please come out. Please be okay. I am sorry. I should not have left you." Lily says, "Don't cry, Timmy. We will help Speedy. We will call a veterinarian. He will make Speedy better. He will make him happy." She takes out her phone. She calls her mom. She tells her what happened. She asks her to come and bring a veterinarian. Her mom says, "Don't worry, Lily. I will come. I will bring a veterinarian. He will help Speedy. He will help Timmy. Stay there. Be careful. I love you." She hangs up. She gets in her car. She drives to them. She brings a veterinarian. The veterinarian is very nice. He has a big bag. He has many tools. He goes to Speedy. He picks him up. He checks him. He says, "Don't worry, Timmy. Speedy is fine. He is just scared. He will come out soon. He will be happy again." He puts Speedy in his bag. He says, "I will take Speedy to my clinic. I will give him some water and some food. I will make him comfortable. He will be back soon. You can visit him anytime." He smiles at Timmy. He says, "You are a good boy, Timmy. You love animals. You want to be a veterinarian. You are brave. You are smart. You are kind." He hugs Timmy. He says, "Thank you for taking care of Speedy. Thank you for being his friend. He is lucky to have you." He gets in his car. He drives away. He takes Speedy with him. Timmy smiles. He is happy. He says, "Thank you, veterinarian. Thank you, Lily. Thank you, mom. You are all very nice. You are all very kind. You are all my friends." He hugs Lily. He hugs his mom. He says, "I love you all. I love Speedy. I hope he is okay. I hope he is happy. I will see him soon." He goes home. He plays with his other animals. He waits for Speedy. He is happy. He is a veterinarian.
