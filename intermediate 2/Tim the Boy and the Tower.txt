Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tower

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to build towers with his toy blocks. He was very careful when he built his towers. He wanted them to be tall and strong. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Tim showed Sam his tower. "Look at my tower, Sam! I built it very careful," said Tim. Sam was amazed. "Wow, Tim! Your tower is so tall!" But then, their dog, Spot, ran into the room. Spot was very excited and wagged his tail. His tail hit the tower and scattered the blocks all over the floor. Tim and Sam were sad. Together, Tim and Sam picked up the blocks and started to build a new tower. They were careful, just like before. Their new tower was even taller and stronger than the first one. And they made sure to keep Spot away from their tower this time.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to prepare a yummy snack. He went to the fridge to get some fruit. He saw apples, bananas, and grapes. Tim picked the grapes because they were his favorite. While he was eating the grapes, he heard a funny noise. It came from the fridge! Tim was scared but curious. He opened the fridge door to see what was making the noise. To Tim's surprise, he found a little frog inside the fridge! The frog had been eating the fruit too. Tim laughed and decided to share his grapes with the frog. They both enjoyed the yummy snack together. And from that day on, Tim and the frog became best friends.

Once there was a wild button. It was so shiny and bright! But it didn't do anything. One day, a little girl saw it. She wanted to make it special. So, she filled it with all kinds of colors. Red, yellow, blue and green! When she was finished, it looked so pretty! She showed it to all of her friends, and they all clapped. It was the happiest button ever! It made everyone smile. The wild button was filled with all kinds of happiness. Everywhere the little girl went, she took the button with her. It filled the world with joy.

Sara was sad. She did not have any friends to play with. She saw a big dog in the park. The dog was red and had a long tail. Sara liked the dog. She wanted to pet him. But Sara was scared. She did not know if the dog was nice or mean. She did not know if the dog would bite her or lick her. She hid behind a tree and watched the dog. The dog saw Sara. He wagged his tail and ran to her. He sniffed her and licked her face. He was friendly. Sara smiled. She put her thumb in her mouth. She felt safe. Sara petted the dog. She hugged him and gave him a name. She called him Rex. Rex liked Sara. He barked and jumped. He wanted to play with her. Sara and Rex played in the park. They ran and chased and rolled. They had fun. Sara was happy. She had a friend. She trusted Rex. Rex trusted Sara. They were best friends.
