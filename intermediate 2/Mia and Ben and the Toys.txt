Title: Mia and Ben and the Toys

Mia and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with their toys together. One day, they found a shiny coin on the floor. It was a value. They both wanted it. "Give me the value!" Mia said. "No, it is mine!" Ben said. They pulled and pushed the value. They were angry and loud. Then, the value flew out of their hands and rolled under the sofa. "Oh no!" Mia and Ben said. They tried to reach the value, but it was too far. They looked at each other. They felt sad and sorry. "I am sorry, Mia. I was not gentle with you. Can you forgive me?" Ben said. "I am sorry, Ben. I was not gentle with you. I forgive you. Can you forgive me?" Mia said. They hugged and smiled. They were friends again. "Let's forget the value. We have each other. That is more important." Mia said. "Yes, you are right. We have each other. That is more important." Ben said. They went to play with their toys again. They were happy and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved playing dress up. Whenever she wanted to play, she would get out a big cloth from her closet. She loved to swirl the thick cloth around her body and make the fabric look like a dress. Once day, her mom showed her how to serve using the cloth. She patiently watched as her mom folded the cloth into neat squares. Whenever she wanted to play dress up, she used the cloth to serve her imaginary guests. She would pretend to be a waiter and offer her guests snacks and tea. The girl was very patient with her dress up game. She took her time folding the cloth and explaining the rules of her game to her imaginary guests. Everytime she played dress up, she learned something new about how to use the cloth. Soon, the little girl became a pro at playing make believe. She loved when she got to serve with the cloth and imagined herself as a real waiter with a real restaurant. She was so happy to make her own special dress up world!

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a clumsy little bear named Ben. Ben loved to play with his toy sword. One day, he went on an adventure to find a friend to play with. As Ben walked through the forest, he met a bunny named Bella. "Hi, Bella! Do you want to play with me?" Ben asked. "I have a sword and we can play knights!" Bella said, "Yes, I want to play!" So, Ben and Bella played knights in the forest. They shook their swords in the air and pretended to fight dragons. But then, Ben shook his sword too hard and it flew out of his hand. The sword landed in a tree. Ben was sad, but Bella had an idea. She hopped up and used her strong legs to jump high into the tree. She grabbed the sword and gave it back to Ben. Ben was so happy to have his sword back. He thanked Bella for her help. From that day on, Ben and Bella became best friends, and they played knights in the forest every day.

One day, a big bear and a small rabbit were talking. The bear said, "I want to have an amazing race with you." The rabbit thought for a bit and then said, "Why don't we suggest a war of running instead?" The bear agreed, and they decided to start the race the next day. As they started the race, the rabbit ran very fast. The bear was slow, but he tried his best to catch up. Suddenly, a little bird flew by and saw the race. The bird wanted to help the bear, so it whispered a secret plan in the bear's ear. The next morning, the rabbit was surprised to see the bear had won the race. The bear told the rabbit, "The little bird helped me by flying me to the finish line." The rabbit was happy for the bear but also learned that friends can help you achieve amazing things.
