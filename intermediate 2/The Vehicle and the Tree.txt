Category: Intermediate
Title: The Vehicle and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big vehicle. It was red and shiny. The vehicle had four wheels and could go very fast. A nice man named Tom would lead the vehicle on many trips. The vehicle liked going on trips with Tom. One day, Tom and the vehicle went on a long trip. The sun was hot and bright. The vehicle started to feel uncomfortable. It wanted to stop and rest. But Tom did not know the vehicle was uncomfortable. The vehicle saw a big tree with lots of shade. It thought the shade would make it feel better. So, the vehicle stopped under the tree. Tom was surprised but understood. They both rested under the tree. After some time, the vehicle felt better. Tom and the vehicle continued their trip, happy and comfortable.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. They have a lot of toys, but their favorite toy is a twisty snake. The twisty snake is long and colorful. They can twist it into different shapes and make it look like other animals. One day, Anna and Ben are playing with the twisty snake in the park. They see a group of kids playing with a ball. The kids are having fun and laughing. Anna and Ben want to join them. "Can we play with you?" Anna asks the kids. "Sure, you can play with us," one of the kids says. "But you have to share your twisty snake. It looks cool." Anna and Ben look at each other. They don't want to share their twisty snake. They love their twisty snake. It is their favorite toy. "No, we can't share our twisty snake," Ben says. "It is ours. We can play with it by ourselves." The kids shrug and go back to playing with the ball. Anna and Ben go back to playing with the twisty snake. They twist it into a circle, a star, and a heart. But soon, they get bored. They see the kids playing with the ball. The kids are still having fun and laughing. Anna and Ben want to have fun and laugh too. "Maybe we should share our twisty snake," Anna says. "Maybe the kids will let us play with the ball too. Maybe we can make new friends." Ben thinks for a minute. He likes the twisty snake, but he also likes the ball. He also likes to make new friends. "Okay, let's share our twisty snake," Ben says. "But only for a little while. Then we can have it back." Anna and Ben go to the kids and offer them their twisty snake. The kids are happy and thank them. They take turns twisting the snake and playing with the ball. Anna and Ben have fun and laugh too. They make new friends. They learn that sharing is good. Sharing makes them popular. Sharing makes them happy.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful boy named Tim. Tim loved to paint. He had many colors to make fun pictures. His favorite thing to paint was the sun. He liked to paint the sun with a big smile. One day, Tim wanted to paint outside. He needed a cord to hang his pictures. He asked his mom for help. She gave him a long cord. They went to the yard and tied the cord to two trees. Tim painted many pictures. He painted the sun, the grass, and the sky. He hung his pictures on the cord. They danced in the wind. Tim was very happy. His mom and dad were proud of him. They all had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bug. The bug loved to crawl all day. One day, the bug saw a big napkin on the ground. The bug thought, "Wow, that napkin looks fun!" The bug crawled on the napkin and met another bug. The new bug said, "Hi, I like to crawl too! Let's be friends and crawl on this napkin together." The unique bug was happy to have a new friend. They crawled and played on the napkin all day. When they got tired, they took a break and talked. "I had fun crawling with you," said the unique bug. "Me too," said the new bug. They smiled and knew they would be friends forever.
