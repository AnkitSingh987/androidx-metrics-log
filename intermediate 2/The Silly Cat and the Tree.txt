Category: Intermediate
Title: The Silly Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat. The silly cat loved to play. One day, it found a mitten. The cat was very happy. The cat played with the mitten all day. It would jump and run with the mitten. The cat was having so much fun. It loved its new toy. But one day, the cat played too hard. The mitten got stuck in a tree. The cat tried to get the mitten down, but it could not. The cat was very sad. It lost its mitten forever.

Once upon a time a small child named Timmy was walking in the park. He had his basket full of treats. One of them was a big bar of chocolate. It looked so yummy that he wanted to share it with his friends. As Timmy kept walking, he saw two of his friends. They both had different treats in their baskets. Timmy shouted, “Hi! I have chocolate. Do you want some?" His friends said “Yes!” with big smiles. Timmy split the big bar of chocolate into three pieces and gave one to each of his friends. All three of them shared the different treats and laughed together. Timmy was so happy that he could share his chocolate with his friends. He smiled, and then they all went back to the park to play some games.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was very anxious. She wanted to find a new book to read. So, she went to the big shelf full of books. Lucy saw many books on the shelf. She looked and looked until she saw a book that she liked. It was a novel. She was happy to find it. Lucy reached up high to grab the novel. She pulled it down and started to read. The story was fun and made her smile. Lucy loved her new book and read it all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy bricks. Tim would build tall towers, big houses, and long roads with his bricks. One day, Tim's mom asked him to separate his bricks by color. She wanted to help him learn about colors. Tim thought this was a fun game. He put the red bricks in one pile, the blue bricks in another pile, and the yellow bricks in a third pile. When Tim finished separating his bricks, his mom was very happy. She gave Tim a big hug and said, "Good job, Tim!" Tim felt proud and happy. Now, he could build even better things with his bricks because he knew all about colors.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat toast in the morning. One day, she wanted to make toast all by herself. She felt proud and was excited to show her mom. Sue put the bread in the toaster and pushed the button. She waited for the toast to pop up. When it did, she heard a loud "snap!" Sue was scared and called her mom, "Mommy, the toaster made a big noise!" Her mom came and looked at the toaster. She saw that the "snap" was just the toast popping up. She laughed and said, "Sue, that's what happens when the toast is ready. It's okay!" Sue felt better and was proud of her first toast. They both enjoyed the toast together, and Sue felt very happy.

One day, a bird wanted to fly. The bird had different colors. It was red, blue, and green. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. It liked to see all the things below. The bird saw a peach tree. The tree had many peaches. The bird wanted to eat a peach. It flew down to the tree. It picked a big, juicy peach with its beak. The bird was happy with the peach. It flew back up into the sky. The bird ate the peach while it flew. The peach was sweet and yummy. The bird was glad it found the peach tree.

Once upon a time, there was a popular girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends in the park. One day, Lily found a rose. It was very pretty and red. Lily showed the rose to her friend, Tom. "Look at my rose! It's so pretty!" she said. Tom looked at the rose and said, "Wow! It's so nice!" They played with the rose all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The rose started to shrink! It got smaller and smaller until it was gone. Lily and Tom were sad, but they learned that even if something goes away, they still had fun together.
