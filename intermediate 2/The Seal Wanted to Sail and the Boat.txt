Category: Intermediate
Title: The Seal Wanted to Sail and the Boat

One day, a seal wanted to sail. He found a small boat near the sea. The seal was very happy. He jumped into the boat and started to sail. The sun was high in the sky. The seal liked to feel the wind on his face. As the seal sailed, he saw a big fish. The big fish said, "Hello, seal! Where are you going?" The seal said, "I am sailing in my boat. I want to see the world." The big fish said, "Be careful, seal. The sea can be a scary place." The seal did not listen to the big fish. He sailed and sailed. The wind got strong, and the waves got high. The seal was scared. He did not know what to do. The boat went up and down in the big waves. The seal wished he had listened to the big fish. The high waves were too much for the small boat. The boat flipped over, and the seal fell into the water. The seal could not sail anymore. He was sad and scared, and he wished he had stayed close to home.

Once upon a time, there was a polite little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play with her toys and sing songs. One day, Lily was playing in her room when she heard a noise. "What is that?" she asked her mom. "I don't know," her mom said. "Let's go see." They walked to the living room and saw a big box. Lily's dad said, "I found this outside. Let's open it." Inside the box was a talking bird! The bird said, "Hello! I am here to tell you a truth." Lily, her mom, and her dad were all very surprised. They did not want to disturb the bird, so they listened carefully. The bird said, "The truth is, I am a magic bird! I can grant you one wish." Lily thought about it and said, "I wish for a big, fun playground in our backyard!" The bird nodded and flew away. The next day, Lily looked out her window and saw a big, fun playground in her backyard! She was so happy and played on it every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She had a dream to become successful. She wanted to be the best at smelling things with her nose. Her nose was very good at finding things. One day, Jane went to the park to practice smelling with her nose. She closed her eyes and took a big sniff. She could smell the flowers, the grass, and even the yummy ice cream that a nearby boy was eating. Jane practiced every day, and soon she became very good at using her nose. People would ask her to help them find things they had lost, like their toys or their pets. Jane was happy to help, and she became very successful. Everyone loved her, and she was proud of her special nose.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with red apples on it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I pick an apple, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can pick one." Tim picked a big red apple and took a big bite. But suddenly, he felt his stomach hurt. He looked at his mom with a helpless face and said, "Mommy, my stomach hurts." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." Just then, a funny squirrel jumped down from the tree. The squirrel picked up the apple Tim dropped and took a bite. The squirrel's face turned red and it started to dance! Tim and his mom laughed. Tim's stomach didn't hurt anymore. They all learned that laughter can make you feel better.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. He loved to eat and was always eager to try new foods. One day, Tim and his mom went to a new restaurant to have lunch. At the restaurant, Tim's mom gave him a menu to look at. Tim was very excited and said, "Mom, I want to eat everything on the menu!" His mom laughed and said, "You can't eat everything, Tim. Pick one thing you want to try." As Tim was looking at the menu, he accidentally spilled his drink. The menu got all wet, and the words started to fade away. Tim was sad and said, "Mom, I destroyed the menu." His mom smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can ask the waiter for a new one." The waiter brought a new menu, and Tim picked his favorite food to eat. They enjoyed their lunch, and Tim learned to be more careful with the menu. From that day on, Tim was still eager to try new foods, but he also made sure to take good care of the menus.
