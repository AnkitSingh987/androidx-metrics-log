Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. His name was Joe. He had a garden. He loved to watch his seeds grow and see what things he could make. One day, Joe woke up feeling very tired. He had worked so hard in the garden the day before, that he was too tired to even stand. He decided to lay in the sun and rest. He closed his eyes and fell asleep. Suddenly, Joe felt something touching his arm. He opened his eyes and saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hello Joe! Why are you so tired?" Joe told the bird that he had worked too hard in the garden. The bird said, "I have an idea. Why don't you watch your garden grow? It's very calming, and it will make you feel better." Joe smiled, and thanked the bird. He watched his garden grow and slowly all his tiredness went away.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. It was cold outside, so they wore big coats to keep warm. They walked to the park where Tim saw a big tree with little prunes on it. Tim wanted to get a prune, but they were too high up. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim. You might fall if you try to get one." Tim looked at the tree and thought of a plan. Tim found a long stick and used it to reach the prunes. He was very happy when he got one. But then, he saw a little bird who looked cold and hungry. Tim gave the bird the prune he got from the tree. The bird was so happy, it sang a song for Tim.

One day, a small cat named Kitty felt very embarrassed. Her body was covered in mud after she fell into a puddle. She wanted to clean herself, but she didn't know how. Kitty saw her friend, a kind dog named Doggy. She asked, "Doggy, can you help me clean my body? I am very embarrassed." Doggy welcomed Kitty to his home and tried to help her clean the mud off her body. They tried to use water, but it didn't work. They tried to use a towel, but it didn't work either. Kitty was still covered in mud and felt even more embarrassed. In the end, Kitty stayed dirty and sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to whistle. Every day, he would sit on a tree branch and whistle a happy tune. One day, Bobo saw a tap. The tap had water coming out of it. Bobo was thirsty, so he flew down to the tap. But when he tried to drink the water, it was harsh. It was not good to drink. Bobo was sad, but he did not give up. He flew to another tap and tried the water there. This time, the water was good. He drank and drank until he was not thirsty anymore. Then, Bobo flew back to his tree and whistled a happy tune again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a rod. Tim wanted to play with it. He tried to use the rod to lead his toy car. But the car did not move. Tim felt frustrated. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to lead my toy car with the rod, but it won't move!" Tim's mom thought for a moment. Then she said, "Why don't you try to lead your dog, Spot, with the rod instead?" Tim liked the idea. He took the rod and used it to lead Spot. Spot followed the rod happily. Tim was not frustrated anymore. He had fun leading Spot around the yard with the rod.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a shiny buckle on the ground. He knew it was important. Tim wanted to show it to his friend, Sally. So, he raised the buckle high in the air and ran to her house. When he got there, he said, "Sally, look what I found! It's a shiny buckle!" Sally looked at the buckle and said, "Wow, Tim! That is important. We should show it to our teacher." So, Tim and Sally went to their teacher with the important buckle. They raised it up to show her. The teacher smiled and said, "Good job, Tim and Sally! You found something very important. This buckle belongs to our friend, Joe. Let's give it back to him." Tim and Sally were happy to help. They raised the buckle one more time and gave it to Joe. Joe was very thankful to have his important buckle back. Tim and Sally felt proud that they could help their friend.
