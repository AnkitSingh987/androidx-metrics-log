Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sue and the Rock

One day, Tom and Sue went to gather rocks. They found a big iron rock. It was very heavy. Tom said, "Wow, this rock is special!" They took the iron rock home. They showed it to their mom. She said, "This rock will make us healthy. We can use it to cook food." One day, they cooked food on the iron rock. The food tasted very good. They ate it and felt strong. But then, a magic thing happened. The iron rock started to talk! The iron rock said, "Thank you for using me. I was lonely. Now I have friends." Tom, Sue, and their mom were happy. They all became friends with the iron rock. They lived happily together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big lemon tree in her yard. Every day, she would go and look at the lemons. They were yellow and smelled good. Lucy loved her lemon tree very much. One day, Lucy saw a clear, big box near the lemon tree. She asked her mom, "What is the big box for?" Her mom said, "We will use the box to protect the lemons from the rain and wind." Lucy was happy that the lemons would be safe. She helped her mom put the clear box over the lemon tree. Now, the lemons were protected from the bad weather. Lucy and her mom smiled as they looked at the lemon tree, knowing that it would grow big and strong. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom are playing in the park. They see a big slide. Lily wants to go on the slide. Tom is scared. "Come on, Tom. It is fun!" Lily says. "No, Lily. It is too high. I will fall." Tom says. Lily goes on the slide. She slides down fast. She laughs. Tom watches her. "See, Tom? It is not scary. It is light and smooth." Lily says. Lily goes on the slide again. But this time, she slides too fast. She falls off the slide. She lands in a puddle of water. She is wet and cold. "Help, Tom! Help!" Lily cries. Tom runs to Lily. He helps her get up. He hugs her. "Are you okay, Lily?" Tom asks. "I am okay, Tom. But I am wet and cold. And the water is dirty." Lily says. "Let's go home, Lily. Mom will give us dry clothes and hot soup." Tom says. "Okay, Tom. Thank you for helping me. You are a good friend." Lily says. They hold hands and walk home. They are happy. They learned that slides are fun, but sometimes they can make you fall. And that water can be nice, but sometimes it can be dirty. And that light can be bright, but sometimes it can be too fast.

Once, there was a girl named Amy. She had a candle that she liked very much. The candle was reliable because it gave light every night. One night, Amy's friend, Ben, came to visit her. They sat and gazed at the candle together. "Look at the pretty light," said Ben. "Yes, it's my favorite candle," said Amy. They liked to watch the candle burn because it made them feel warm and happy. But one day, Amy and Ben played too close to the candle. They forgot to be careful. The candle fell and started a fire. Amy and Ben were very scared. The fire grew bigger and they could not stop it. In the end, their homes were burned down. The moral of the story is to always be careful when playing near fire.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a noisy bird. The bird liked to fly around and make loud sounds. All the people in the village could hear the bird every day. One day, a brave boy found an arrow on the ground. He decided to make the bird quiet. He did not want the bird to be noisy anymore. The boy went to find the bird with the arrow in his hand. When the boy found the bird, he did not use the arrow. Instead, he talked to the bird. The boy demanded, "Please, bird, be quiet!" The bird listened to the boy, and it became quiet. The village was happy, and the boy and the bird became friends.
