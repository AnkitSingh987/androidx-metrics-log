Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rock and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little rock. This rock had a special mineral inside it. Every day, the sun would rise and shine on the rock, making it feel warm and happy. One day, a small bird came to the rock. The bird was very impatient because it wanted to find a friend to play with. The bird saw the rock and thought it was a fun toy. So, the bird started to play with the rock, pushing it around and rolling it on the ground. As the bird played with the rock, the special mineral inside the rock started to glow. The rock began to rise, and it turned into a beautiful, shiny stone. The bird and the shiny stone became best friends, and they played together in the forest every day. And they were never impatient again.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big pile of leaves on the ground. They ran to the pile and jumped in it. They laughed and threw leaves at each other. "Let's dress up as animals with the leaves!" Tom said. He put some leaves on his head and said, "I am a lion, roar!" "I am a bird, tweet!" Lily said. She put some leaves on her arms and pretended to fly. They had fun dressing up as different animals. But then they saw a small kitten on the ground. It was weak and cold. It looked sad and scared. "Look, a kitten!" Lily said. She ran to the kitten and picked it up. She cuddled it and said, "Don't worry, we will help you." Tom followed her and said, "Yes, we will. Let's take it home and give it some food and water." They wrapped the kitten in some leaves and carried it home. They showed it to their mom and dad. They said they found it on the ground and it was weak. They asked if they could keep it. Mom and dad smiled and said, "You are very kind and caring. You did the right thing by helping the kitten. Yes, you can keep it. But you have to take good care of it." Tom and Lily were happy. They named the kitten Leafy. They gave it some food and water and a warm bed. They played with it and loved it. Leafy was happy too. It purred and licked them. The moral of the story is: Be kind and helpful to those who are weak and in need. You will make them happy and yourself happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy. His toy was a ring with a strip on it. Spot would run and jump to catch the ring in his mouth. One day, Spot met a new friend named Max. Max was a big dog who also loved to play. They both wanted to play with the ring. Spot and Max were competitive, they both wanted to be the best at catching the ring. Spot and Max took turns trying to catch the ring. They would run and jump, trying to catch it in their mouths. Spot was fast, but Max was big and strong. In the end, they both learned that playing together was more fun than trying to be the best. They became best friends and played with the ring every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a fake toy key in a drawer. He thought it was very cool. Tim showed the key to his friend, Sue. Sue said, "Wow, what a cool key! What can it open?" Tim and Sue went to the park to play. They tried to open many things with the fake key, but nothing would open. They were sad. Then, something unexpected happened. They found a tiny door on a tree. Tim put the fake key in the door, and it opened! They were so surprised. Inside the tree, they found a small room with more fake keys. They played and had fun all day with the new fake keys they found.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Davey. Davey and his mum were walking in the park. They stopped to watch the birds when they saw a man with a scary weapon. Davey was afraid and he wanted to leave quickly. Mum, who was very wise, knew they had to be careful. She put her arm around Davey and said, "Davey, we must leave now." Davey followed his mum away from the man. He was sad and scared. Mum looked at Davey and said, "Remember Davey, it's not always nice to leave things. Weapons can be very hard and can hurt people. We must always be kind and gentle." Davey understood what his mum was saying and nodded. From this day forward, Davey always tried to be kind and gentle. Even when things were hard, he knew it was important to be nice to others.
