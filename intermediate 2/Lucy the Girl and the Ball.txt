Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She was an honest girl who always told the truth. Lucy loved to play with her ball in the pitch near her house. She would run and kick the ball every day. One day, while Lucy was playing, a boy named Tim came to the pitch. Tim was nice and they had fun playing together. They became good friends and played in the pitch every day. As they grew up, Lucy and Tim fell in love. They decided to marry and have a big party. All their friends and family came to celebrate. They were very happy and continued to be honest and play in the pitch together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a high tree. One day, Tim saw a big red ball on the ground. He wanted to play with the ball, so he flew down from the tree. Tim played with the ball, but he did not know if he should take the ball back to his high tree. He had to decide what to do. Tim thought about it and decided to ask his friend, Sam the squirrel, for help. Sam the squirrel saw Tim and the big red ball. He told Tim that it was okay to take the ball back to the high tree. So, Tim and Sam played with the ball together. They had a lot of fun, and Tim was happy that he decided to ask for help.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to school. It was time for lunch. Tim had a sandwich, an apple, and a cookie in his lunch box. He sat down with his friend, Sam, and they started to eat. "Yummy," said Sam, "I love my lunch." Tim looked at his sandwich and felt embarrassed. His sandwich was much smaller than Sam's. He didn't want Sam to see his small sandwich. Just then, a little bird flew into the lunch room. It landed on Tim's table and looked at the small sandwich. The bird took a tiny bite and flew away. Tim and Sam laughed. Tim didn't feel embarrassed anymore. He shared his small sandwich with Sam, and they both enjoyed their lunch. The bird had made their day very special.

Once upon a time, in a busy sea, there was a small island. On this island, there lived a kind bird named Blue. Blue had a special power. He could make things disappear. One day, Blue met a new friend named Red. Red was a small fish who lived in the sea. Blue said to Red, "I can make things disappear. Do you want to see?" Red was excited and said, "Yes, please show me!" Blue found a big rock and said, "Watch this!" He used his special power and the rock disappeared. Red was very happy and clapped his fins. They played all day, making things disappear and then bringing them back. Later, a big wave came and took Red far away from the island. Blue was sad and missed his friend. He remembered his special power and thought, "I can make things disappear, maybe I can bring Red back!" Blue used his special power and, just like that, Red appeared next to him. They were so happy to be together again. From that day on, Blue and Red played together on the island and had many fun adventures.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a rare shiny coin on the ground. He picked it up and decided to use the money to buy a new toy. Tim went to the toy store with his mom. There were many toys to choose from, but Tim wanted a toy car. He found a red car that he liked very much. Tim showed the rare shiny coin to his mom and said, "I want to use this money to buy the car." His mom smiled and said, "Okay, Tim. Let's buy the car with your rare coin." They went to the store man and gave him the shiny coin. The man was happy to see the rare coin and gave Tim the red car. Tim was very happy to have a new toy and played with it all day long. And that's how Tim learned to use money to buy things he liked.
