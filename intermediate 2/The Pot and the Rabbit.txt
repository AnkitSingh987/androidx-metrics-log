Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pot and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a big pot. It was very popular with all the little animals. They loved to play under it. One day, a kind rabbit decided to make a yummy soup for all her friends. Rabbit started to gather her favorite vegetables. She put them in the pot with some water. Then, she began to stir the soup. The smell was so good! All her friends came to see what she was making. When the soup was ready, Rabbit called all her friends to come eat. They sat under the big pot and shared the tasty soup. They were so happy and full. From that day on, they loved to play under the pot even more.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very curious. He liked to look at everything and ask questions. One day, Tim heard a noise in the woods. He wanted to know what it was. Tim walked into the woods and saw a big bear. The bear looked at Tim and said, "Who are you?" Tim said, "I am Tim. I heard a noise and I was curious." The bear said, "I made the noise. I am hungry." Tim was scared. He wanted to go home. But the bear was faster. The bear ate Tim. The end.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to travel. She went to her dad and said, "Daddy, I want to travel far away!" Her dad smiled and said, "Okay, but first, you must be honest and tell me where you want to go." Lucy thought for a moment and said, "I want to go to the moon!" Her dad laughed and said, "That's very far! But let's start with something closer. How about we travel to the desk in the living room?" Lucy and her dad walked to the desk. On the desk, there was a big box. Lucy asked, "Daddy, what's in the box?" Her dad said, "This is a surprise for being honest about where you want to travel." Lucy opened the box and found a toy rocket inside. Her dad said, "Now you can travel to the moon with your toy rocket!" Lucy was so happy and played with her rocket all day, traveling to the moon and back in her imagination.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very hungry dog. He loved to eat all day. One day, Spot saw a big cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was not nice. Fluffy was a threat to Spot because he was big and scary. Spot wanted to show he was not scared of Fluffy. Spot did something silly. He tried to take Fluffy's food. This made Fluffy very mad. Spot felt embarrass because Fluffy laughed at him. Spot ran away and hid. Later, Spot saw Fluffy again. This time, Spot did not try to take Fluffy's food. Instead, Spot shared his food with Fluffy. Fluffy was happy and became nice. Spot learned that it is better to share and be kind. This made Spot a better dog.

Mia is a young cat who lives with her owner, Lily. Lily loves Mia very much and gives her food, water, toys and hugs. Mia likes to play with Lily and cuddle with her on the sofa. One day, Lily brings home a new toy for Mia. It is a small mouse that squeaks when Mia squeezes it. Mia is very curious and excited. She runs to the toy and pounces on it. She bites it and shakes it and squeezes it hard. The toy makes a loud noise and Mia is happy. Lily watches Mia and smiles. She says, "You like your new toy, Mia? It is a mouse. Mice are animals that cats like to chase and catch. But this mouse is not real. It is a toy. You can play with it, but you cannot eat it." Mia looks at Lily and meows. She does not understand all the words, but she knows that Lily is talking to her. She likes Lily's voice and her smell. She drops the toy and runs to Lily. She jumps on her lap and rubs her head against her chest. She purrs and licks Lily's hand. Lily hugs Mia and says, "You are a good cat, Mia. I love you very much. You are my best friend. Do you love me too?" Mia nods and snuggles with Lily. She squeezes Lily gently and says, "Meow."
