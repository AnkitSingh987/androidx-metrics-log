Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and Lily and the Car

Sam and Lily were playing with their toy cars in the living room. They liked to pretend they were driving on the road and going to different places. Sam had a blue car and Lily had a pink car. "Beep beep! Move out of the way, Lily!" Sam said as he zoomed his car past Lily's car. "Hey, that's not fair, Sam! You bumped into my car!" Lily said as she chased after Sam's car. They both drove their cars around the sofa, the table, and the chair, making loud noises and laughing. But then, they saw Mom coming into the room with a frown on her face. "Sam and Lily, what are you doing? You are making too much noise and mess! You need to settle down and play quietly. And where are your licenses?" Mom asked. "Licenses?" Sam and Lily said together, looking confused. "Yes, licenses. You need a license to drive a car, even a toy car. It's a card that shows you know how to drive safely and follow the rules. Do you have your licenses?" Mom asked. Sam and Lily shook their heads. They didn't have any licenses. They didn't even know what they were. "Then you can't drive your cars anymore. You have to put them away and find something else to play with. Maybe you can draw some licenses and practice driving on paper. But first, you have to say sorry to each other for bumping and shouting." Mom said. Sam and Lily looked at each other and felt sorry. They didn't mean to upset Mom or each other. They put their cars away and hugged each other. "Sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to bump into your car." Sam said. "Sorry, Sam. I didn't mean to chase after your car." Lily said. "It's okay. We can still be friends and play together." Sam said. "Okay. Let's go and draw some licenses and learn how to drive better." Lily said. They took some paper and crayons and went to the table. They drew some cards with their names and pictures of their cars. They showed them to Mom and asked her to teach them some rules of driving. Mom smiled and helped them. They learned how to stop, go, turn, and share the road. They had fun and played quietly. They were good drivers and good children.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big box. They pretended the box was a factory that made different things. Sometimes they made cars, sometimes they made cakes, sometimes they made animals. One day, they decided to make a robot. They used some paper, some tape, some crayons, and some buttons. They worked hard and soon they had a robot with a head, a body, and two arms. But they forgot to make legs. "Oh no, our robot has no legs!" Lily said. "How can he walk?" "Don't worry, I have an idea," Tom said. He took two long socks and taped them to the robot's body. "These are his legs. They can stretch and bend." "Wow, you are so smart, Tom!" Lily said. "Let's try it." They put the robot on the floor and pushed a button. The robot started to move. His sock legs stretched and bent as he walked. He made funny noises like beep and boop. Tom and Lily laughed and clapped. They were very happy with their robot. They played with him all day, until it was time for dinner. "Bye, robot," they said. "We will play with you again tomorrow." They put the robot back in the box and went to the kitchen. They did not see the robot stretch his sock legs and peek out of the box. He wanted to play more. He was a smart robot.

Anna liked to play in the garden with her dog, Spot. Spot was a reliable friend. He always came when Anna called him and he never bit or scratched her. He liked to chase balls and sticks and dig holes in the dirt. One sunny day, Anna and Spot were in the garden. Anna threw a ball for Spot to fetch. Spot ran after the ball and caught it in his mouth. He brought it back to Anna and wagged his tail. Anna threw the ball again and again. Spot was very happy. After a while, Anna and Spot were tired. They lay down on the grass and looked at the sky. They saw clouds and birds and a big yellow sun. Anna yawned and stretched her arms and legs. She felt sleepy. "Spot, do you want to take a nap?" Anna asked. Spot barked and licked Anna's face. He stretched his paws and curled up next to Anna. He felt warm and cozy. Anna smiled and hugged Spot. She closed her eyes and fell asleep. She dreamed of playing in the garden with her reliable friend.
