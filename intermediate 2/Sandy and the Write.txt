Category: Intermediate
Title: Sandy and the Write

Sandy loved playing with pens. She had a collection of at least a dozen different coloured ones! She was so excited to start to learn how to write. Sandy's mum was so happy for her. She said, “Let’s go to the store to get you your own pen and notepad.” When they arrived, Sandy saw so many different pens that she didn’t know which one to choose. She picked the sour coloured one. Back at home, Sandy started to learn how to write. She wrote the letter ‘A’ and felt so proud. The next day, when she picked up her pen, it didn’t write anymore. Sandy's dreams of learning to write had vanished. She was so sad and felt like she had failed. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play at the park. He saw a big swing and wanted to try it. Tim was a bit scared, but he wanted to be brave. A girl named Sue was on the swing next to Tim. She saw that he was scared. Sue said, "Don't be scared, Tim. I will help you." Tim replied, "Thank you, Sue." They both started to swing together. Tim felt happy and not scared anymore. The sky became dark and the wind blew hard. Tim and Sue didn't see the big storm coming. They kept swinging higher and higher. The wind pushed Tim off the swing and he fell. Tim got hurt and cried. Sue felt sad and scared too. They both wished they had gone home before the storm.

Lily loved her dog Spot. Spot was big and brown and liked to play with Lily. They ran and jumped and rolled in the grass. Lily was happy with Spot. One day, Spot ran away from Lily. He saw a cat and wanted to chase it. He ran across the street and did not look for cars. A car hit Spot and he was hurt. Lily saw Spot and cried. She ran to him and hugged him. She was scared for Spot. Lily's mom came and saw what happened. She was sad and worried too. She put Spot in the car and drove to the veterinarian. The veterinarian was a nice person who helped animals. She took Spot to a room and looked at him. She said Spot had a broken leg and needed a cast. She gave Spot some medicine to make him feel better. Lily waited outside the room with her mom. She was sad and hoped Spot would be okay. She prayed for Spot and asked God to help him. She wanted to see Spot and hug him again. The veterinarian came out of the room and smiled. She said Spot was brave and strong. She said he would be fine, but he needed to rest and heal. She said Lily could see him now. She unlocked the door and let Lily and her mom in. Lily saw Spot on a bed with a white cast on his leg. He wagged his tail and licked Lily's face. Lily was happy and hugged Spot. She said she loved him and was sorry he was hurt. She said she would take good care of him. Spot barked and licked her again. He was happy too. He loved Lily and was glad she was there.

John was very excited when the bright day arrived. He was getting ready to board a bus for the first time. John was wearing a pale shirt, he felt special. When he entered the bus, the driver revealed a big smile. John looked around the bus and was surprised to see so many people. He sat down in his seat, his eyes wide open. There were many colors around him. He liked to touch the seat and looked out of the window. The bus started with a rumble. It felt like the whole world was shaking and getting closer to John. He kept looking at things outside, but everything was moving so fast. The ride ended soon enough and the driver revealed that it was their stop. John happily rushed out of the bus, full of stories to tell his family and friends. He was very happy to have taken the first bus ride that day.
