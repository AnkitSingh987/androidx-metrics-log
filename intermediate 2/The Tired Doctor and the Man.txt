Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tired Doctor and the Man

Once upon a time, there was a tired doctor. He worked all day and all night. He wanted to rest, but he had to help many people. One day, the doctor met a funny man. The man loved to joke. He made everyone laugh. The doctor liked the funny man. They became good friends. One day, the funny man got sick. The tired doctor helped him. But then, something unexpected happened. The funny man told a joke, and the doctor laughed so hard that he felt better! Now, the doctor was not tired anymore. The funny man's jokes helped the doctor feel happy and strong again. And they lived happily ever after, sharing jokes and helping people together.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. He had a brother named Sam. One day, Tim and Sam went to play outside. They loved to run and jump in the park. While playing, Sam fell down and started to cry. He felt helpless. Tim asked, "What's wrong, Sam?" Sam said, "I fell and now I suffer." Tim wanted to help his brother feel better. Tim hugged Sam and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." He helped Sam stand up and they walked home together. Tim made sure Sam was okay. They were happy to be brothers who cared for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird with a hard wing. The bird could not fly like the other birds. One day, the bird was sad and fell asleep under a tree. In the morning, the sun began to shine. The sun saw the little bird and said, "Wake up, little bird! I will help you fix your hard wing." The bird woke up and looked at the sun. "Really? Can you help me?" asked the bird. The sun smiled and shone warm light on the bird's hard wing. Slowly, the wing became soft and strong. The little bird flapped its wings and started to fly. "Thank you, sun!" the bird said. From that day on, the bird flew happily with its friends in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a trumpet. He was very enthusiastic when he played it. He would squeeze the trumpet and make a loud noise. One day, Tim saw a big, round ball in the park. He thought it was a fun toy to play with. He squeezed the ball like he squeezed his trumpet. But the ball did not make a noise. Tim was sad. Tim tried to squeeze the ball harder. He wanted it to make a sound like his trumpet. Suddenly, the ball popped! Tim was very sad. Now, he had no ball to play with. He went home and played his trumpet, but he was not enthusiastic anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On top of the hill, there was a house. In the house, there lived a selfish man. He did not like to share his things with others. One day, a nice man came to deliver a box to the selfish man. The nice man knocked on the door, but the selfish man did not want to open it. He thought the box was for him, and he wanted to keep it. But then, something unexpected happened. The box started to shake and make noise. The selfish man got scared and opened the door. The nice man opened the box and out jumped a happy dog! The dog was not for the selfish man, but for the nice man. The selfish man learned that sharing can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had many pretty leaves. One day, the wind blew and the leaves spread all over the ground. A small boy named Tim saw the leaves. He wanted to play with them. He jumped into the leaves on the left side. Tim laughed and was very happy. Tim's mom watched him play. She was patient and smiled. She knew Tim loved the leaves. They played together until the sun went down.

Once there was a boy. He was so confused. He couldn't figure out what to do. He saw a cupboard and ran to it. He sped right up to it and opened the door. Inside he saw an amazing toy. He was so excited. He reached in and grabbed it. But suddenly he heard a loud noise. He looked around and saw a big truck coming! He was so confused and tried to run away. But it was too late; the truck was going way too fast. The truck hit him and he got really hurt. He was so sad and his toy was broken. He never got to play with it.
