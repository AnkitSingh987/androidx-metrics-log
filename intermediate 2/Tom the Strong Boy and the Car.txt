Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Strong Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a strong boy named Tom. He had a favorite toy, a little red car. Tom played with his car all day long. One day, Tom's little sister, Sue, wanted to play with the red car too. Tom didn't want to share, and he said, "No, it's mine! You can't hold it!" Sue began to cry. Tom felt worry in his heart. Tom's mommy saw what happened and said, "Tom, you should share your toys with Sue. It's nice to share." Tom thought about it and decided to let Sue hold the car. They both played with the red car and had lots of fun. Tom's worry went away, and they were happy.

Once there was a lovely little girl named Jane. She loved exploring and playing. One day, she found a big drain near her house. She was excited to explore it. Jane went down into the drain. But then, something embarrassing happened - she started to cry! She was scared and didn't know how to get out. Then, a kind neighbour came by. She looked down the drain, saw Jane, and brought her back up, holding her close. Jane felt embarrassed, but then felt the neighbour's arms around her and knew that she was safe. The neighbour brought Jane home and hugged her. Then she told Jane that she loves her very much. Jane smiled and felt so much better - she'd had a scary experience, but knew she was so very loved.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a messy room. The room was full of toys, clothes, and even some ash from the fireplace. Every day, the little boy who lived in the house would play in the messy room, but he never cleaned up. One day, a small toy named Timmy decided to speak. Timmy was tired of the messy room and the ash all around. He called all the other toys together and said, "We need to help clean this room. It's too messy and dirty with ash!" The toys agreed and started to clean up the room. They put away the clothes, the toys, and even cleaned up the ash from the fireplace. When the little boy came back, he was so happy to see his room clean. From that day on, the little boy always cleaned his room and never let it become messy with ash again. And Timmy the toy never had to speak again.

Tom is a bad boy. He likes to take things that are not his. One day, he sees a fat man in the park. The fat man has a wallet in his pocket. Tom wants the wallet. He thinks it has money and candy inside. Tom waits for the fat man to sit on a bench. Then he runs behind him and lifts the wallet from his pocket. He is very quick and quiet. He does not say sorry. He runs away with the wallet. But the fat man is not stupid. He feels his pocket and sees that his wallet is gone. He is very angry. He gets up and chases Tom. He is faster than Tom. He catches him and grabs the wallet back. He yells at Tom and tells him he is a thief. He calls the police. The police come and take Tom away. They put him in a car and drive him to a place where bad boys go. Tom is very scared and sad. He wishes he did not take the wallet. He wishes he was good. But it is too late. He has to stay in the place for a long time. He has no money and no candy. He has no friends and no fun. He has a bad ending.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a pretty sack. She opened the sack and saw a big, yummy peach inside. Mia was very happy and wanted to eat the peach. Mia showed the peach to her friend Tom. Tom said, "Wow, that is a big peach! Can I have some too?" Mia said, "Yes, we can share the peach." They were excited to eat the peach together. But when they opened the sack again, the peach was gone! Instead, there were many small peaches inside. Mia and Tom were surprised but happy. They shared the small peaches and had a fun day.
