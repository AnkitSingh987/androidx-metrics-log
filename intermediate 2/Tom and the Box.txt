Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Box

One sunny day, a funny cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with lots of shade. Tom thought it was a good place to rest. He lay down under the tree and took a nap. When Tom woke up, he saw a small box next to him. It was locked. Tom was curious and wanted to see what was inside. He saw a key on the ground and thought, "Maybe this key can unlock the box!" Tom picked up the key and said, "Let's try to unlock the box!" He put the key in the lock, turned it, and the box opened. Inside the box was a big, colorful ball. Tom was so happy and surprised! He played with the ball all day long in the shade, laughing and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zoom. Zoom was a lucky car because he had a big mark on his side. This mark made him go very fast. Zoom loved to race with his friends. One day, Zoom saw a big hill. He wanted to go down the hill very fast. He thought he would be the best at going down the hill because of his lucky mark. So, Zoom went to the top of the hill and got ready to race down. Zoom started to go down the hill. He went faster and faster. But then, Zoom hit a big rock. He went up into the air and landed very hard. Zoom was sad and hurt. His lucky mark did not help him this time.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a bottle of glue. He thought it would be fun to use the glue on his toys. Tim saw his friend, Sally, outside. He called her, "Sally, come! I have glue. We can play with it." Sally was excited and came running. They started to put glue on their toys, but they used too much. The toys were all sticky and messy. They had spoiled their toys. Just then, Tim's mom came in and saw the mess. She was not happy. But, she had an idea. She said, "Don't worry, kids. I have more toys available for you to play with. But remember, don't spoil them with glue again." Tim and Sally were surprised and happy. They promised to be more careful and played with their new toys happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a small shelter made of toy blocks. He liked to balance his toy car on the blocks. One day, he saw an expensive toy car in the store. It was big and shiny. Tim wanted the car so much. Tim asked his mom for the expensive car. She said no, but Tim was sad. He went to his shelter and played with his old toy car. He was still thinking about the big shiny car. Then, something unexpected happened! Tim's toy car started to grow! It became as big and shiny as the expensive car in the store. Tim was so happy! He didn't need the expensive car anymore. He played with his big shiny car in his shelter all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to collect pretty rocks. She would go outside and look for the most colorful rocks she could find. She had a big box where she kept all her rocks. One day, Amy wore her favorite vest. It was a pretty vest with lots of colors. While she was playing outside, she found a very sad, lonely rock. It was all by itself and needed a friend. Amy decided to take the sad rock home with her. Amy put the sad rock in her vest pocket and took it to her room. She showed the rock her big box of rocks. The sad rock was so happy to have new friends. From that day on, the sad rock was not sad anymore. Amy and her rock friends lived happily ever after.
