Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Polite Pilot and the Plane

Once upon a time, there was a polite pilot named Tim. Tim liked to fly his big plane up in the sky. He loved to take people from one place to another. Tim was always nice to everyone he met. One day, Tim wanted to organize a fun day for all his friends. He invited them to his big plane. "Come to my plane," he said. "We will have a great time!" All his friends were very excited and happy. When the day came, Tim and his friends played games and ate yummy food inside the big plane. They laughed and talked all day long. Tim was so happy to see his friends having fun. At the end of the day, everyone thanked Tim for the amazing time they had. Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome! I'm so glad you all had fun!"

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim had a nest in a big tree. He loved to swing on the tree branches all day long. One day, Tim felt very hungry. He flew away from his nest to look for food. He saw some yummy worms on the ground and ate them. After eating, Tim went back to his nest and swung on the branches again. He was so happy that he was not hungry anymore. And from that day on, Tim always found food when he was hungry and loved swinging on the branches near his nest.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant named Tim. Tim had a big job to do. He had to drive a tight leaf to his ant hill. The leaf was very big and heavy for him, but he knew he had to do it. On his way, Tim met a big bug. The big bug wanted the leaf, too. The big bug tried to take the leaf away from Tim. Tim was scared, but he did not give up. He held on to the tight leaf and did not let the big bug have it. In the end, Tim made it to the ant hill with the tight leaf. All his ant friends were happy and proud of him. Tim learned that even if you are small and scared, you can still do big things if you try hard and do not give up.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a pretty bracelet that she loved very much. She wore it every day and showed it to all her friends. Lily was a very honest girl and always told the truth. One day, Lily lost her bracelet. She looked everywhere and tried very hard to find it. She asked her friends if they had seen it, but they all said no. Lily was very sad and didn't know what to do. Then, her little brother, Tim, came to her with a big smile. He had found Lily's bracelet! But, the unexpected thing was that Tim had made the bracelet even prettier. He had added more colors to it, making it look like a rainbow. Lily was so happy and thanked Tim for his help. From that day on, they both shared the bracelet and were always honest with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. A tiny bug lived under the rock. The bug had a friend named Judge. Judge was a big, strong ant. They liked to play together. One day, the bug found a magic leaf. The leaf could make things shrink. The bug showed the leaf to Judge. They thought it would be fun to make the rock shrink. They touched the leaf to the rock, and it got smaller! But then, something unexpected happened. The tiny bug started to grow bigger! Judge and the bug were very surprised. Now, the bug was as big as Judge. They laughed and played together, happy with their new sizes.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom loved to build with blocks. One day, Tom found a big box of blocks in his room. He was very happy and started to build a tall tower. As Tom was building, his friend, Sam the dog, came into the room. Sam looked at the tower and said, "Wow, Tom! That's a big tower you're building!" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I love to build with my blocks." But then, the wind blew through the window. It was a harsh wind that made Tom and Sam cold. The wind blew so hard that it knocked over Tom's tower. The blocks fell down and made a loud noise. Tom was sad, but Sam said, "Don't worry, Tom. We can build a new tower together." So, Tom and Sam built a new tower, and they were both very happy.
