Category: Intermediate
Title: The Strong Ant and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a strong ant. This ant was an insect. The ant had many friends. They all lived together in a big ant hill. One day, the strong ant met a small bug. The small bug was sad. The strong ant asked, "Why are you sad?" The small bug said, "I am not strong like you. I cannot carry big things." The strong ant wanted to help. They said, "Do not complain. We can be friends. I will help you carry big things." The small bug was happy. They became friends and played together. From that day on, the strong ant and the small bug were always together. They helped each other and had lots of fun. And the small bug never complained again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside with his friends. One day, while playing, Spot saw a big sign. The sign said, "Do not play on the grass." But Spot did not understand the sign and played on the grass anyway. Soon, a big man came and saw Spot playing on the grass. The man was not happy. He told Spot's mom that Spot had not followed the sign. Spot's mom was sad and told Spot he was going to be punished. Spot felt ashamed because he made his mom sad. Spot's mom made him stay inside for the rest of the day as his punishment. Spot learned his lesson and promised to always follow the signs from now on. He never wanted to make his mom sad again. And from that day, Spot always played where he was allowed to play.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady who loved to take walks by the seashore. One day, she felt especially lonely so she decided to take a stroll to the beach. When she arrived, she spotted a little girl playing in the sand. The elderly woman sighed with delight and strolled closer. The little girl smiled at her with her bright blue eyes. "Hello, who are you?" said the little girl. "My name is Bonnie," the elderly lady replied. "I'm here to enjoy the sunshine." The little girl nodded eagerly. "My name is Remy and my pupils are blue, just like the sea!" The elderly lady smiled. She and Remy talked and shared stories as they walked up and down the beach. Every now and then, Bonnie would sigh with contentment. At the end of the day, they said their goodbyes and went their separate ways. Bonnie was heartsick but happy she'd met Remy. It had been the most wonderful day!

One day, a dog and a cat meet. They want to have a race. The dog is big and strong. The cat is small and fast. They want to see who is faster. The dog says, "Let's race to the big tree." The cat says, "Okay, I will win." They start the race. The dog runs fast, but the cat runs faster. They both try very hard. As they race, they see a fancy hat on the ground. They both want the hat. They run faster and faster. But they do not see the big hole in the ground. The dog and the cat fall into the hole. They do not get the fancy hat. The race has a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a red fox. The red fox was very fast and very smart. One day, while the red fox was playing, he saw a hunter. The hunter was looking for animals to catch. The red fox knew he should run away, but he decided to ignore the hunter and keep playing. The hunter saw the red fox and started to follow him. The red fox did not know the hunter was behind him. He kept playing and having fun. The hunter was getting closer and closer, but the red fox still did not know. At last, the hunter caught the red fox. The red fox was very sad. He wished he had not ignored the hunter when he first saw him. If he had run away, he would still be free and happy in the big forest.
