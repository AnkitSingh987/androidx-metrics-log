Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a playful squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to play in the tall pine trees. He would jump from branch to branch, having so much fun. One sunny day, Sam met a new friend, a bird named Bob. Bob was sitting on a sign near the pine tree. The sign said, "No nuts here." Sam said to Bob, "Hi, I am Sam. Do you want to play with me?" Bob said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Sam!" Sam and Bob played together all day long. They jumped and flew from tree to tree, laughing and having fun. They became the best of friends. And every day, they met by the sign to play in the pine trees, making each other happy.

Once upon a time, there was a large bear. The bear was very hungry. He wanted to find some honey to eat. He walked and walked, looking for honey. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. The bear thought, "Maybe there is honey in that tree." The bear climbed the tree to observe what was inside. He saw a beehive with lots of honey. The bear was very happy. He started to eat the honey from the beehive. But he did not see the bees coming back to their home. The bees saw the bear eating their honey. They were very angry. They started to sting the bear. The bear was hurt and sad. He fell from the tree and could not get up. He did not get to eat more honey, and he was still hungry. The bear learned that taking honey from the bees was not a good idea.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing with her toy bear named Ted. She loved to poke Ted's ear and make him giggle. "Hee hee, that tickles!" said Ted. Amy and Ted decided to have a tea party. They set up a table with cups and plates. Amy's mom made them some delicious cookies. They were having a great time. "Yummy!" said Ted, "These cookies are so delicious!" Just then, a big gust of wind blew through the window. It knocked over the table and all the cookies fell on the floor. Amy and Ted were sad. But then, a real bear came in through the window! The bear saw the cookies on the floor and started to eat them. "Wow!" said Amy, "I didn't know real bears like cookies too!" The real bear finished eating the cookies and smiled at Amy and Ted. "Thank you for the delicious cookies!" said the bear. Then, the bear went back into the woods, leaving Amy and Ted with a big surprise and a fun story to tell.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable cat named Tuna. Tuna loved to play in the park with his friends. Every day, they would run, jump, and have fun together. Tuna was always there to help his friends when they needed him. One sunny day, Tuna and his friends went to the park. They were playing hide and seek. Tuna was very good at this game, and he found all his friends quickly. But when it was Tuna's turn to hide, something unexpected happened. Tuna found a big box to hide in. But when he jumped inside, the box started to move! It was not a box, but a big truck! The truck took Tuna far away from the park. Tuna's friends were very worried when they could not find him. But they knew Tuna was dependable and would find a way back. And he did! Tuna jumped out of the truck and ran all the way back to the park. His friends were so happy to see him, and they all played together again.

One day, a little deer went to the river to bathe. The water was cool and nice. The deer liked to play and splash in the water. As the deer was playing, it started to feel dizzy. It did not know why. The deer saw a big frog sitting on a rock. The deer asked the frog, "Why do I feel dizzy?" The frog said, "Maybe you played too much in the water. You should rest for a while." So the deer went to lay down under a big tree. After some time, the deer felt better. It was not dizzy anymore. The deer thanked the frog and went back to the river to play again. But this time, the deer made sure to take breaks and not play too much.
