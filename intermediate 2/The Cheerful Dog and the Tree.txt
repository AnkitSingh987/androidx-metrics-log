Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cheerful Dog and the Tree

One day, a cheerful dog went for a walk. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. The dog liked to play and run in the park. In the park, the dog saw a big tree. It seemed like a fun tree to play by. The dog ran to the tree and saw a stand with toys on it. There were balls and ropes to play with. The cheerful dog picked up a ball and played with it. The dog was very happy. It played by the tree and the stand all day long. The dog had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little dog named Spot. Spot liked to stay at home with his family. He was too shy to go outside and play with the other dogs. Spot's family loved him very much. One day, Spot's family decided to buy a toy for him. They went to the store and bought a big red ball. Spot was very happy to see the ball. He wagged his tail and barked with joy. Spot's family took him and the red ball to the park. At first, Spot was shy and did not want to play. But soon, he saw other dogs playing with their toys too. Spot started to play with his red ball and made new friends. He was not shy anymore. From that day on, Spot loved to go to the park and play with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little kid named Tim. Tim was a happy boy who loved to play with his toys. One day, he decided to gather all of his toys and take them to the park. He put all his toys in a big bag and walked to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big, terrible monster. It was made of sand and it was very scary. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. He took out his toys and started to play with them. The monster watched Tim and saw how much fun he was having. Soon, the terrible monster wanted to play too. Tim shared his toys with the monster and they played together all day. The monster was not so terrible after all. They became good friends and had lots of fun at the park. And from that day on, Tim and the monster played together every time Tim went to the park.

Jenny was very reliable. She always did her best to help her Mom and Dad. Today, Jenny was going to enjoy a fun day outside with her family. When they arrived at the park, Jenny was excited. She grabbed her sister, Mae, and ran over to the swing set. Jenny loved the side-to-side movement the swings created. Every time they went higher, she giggled and smiled. After playing on the swings, Jenny and Mae ran over to the playground. There were tall slides and spinning wheels to enjoy. Every time Jenny went down a slide, her Mom and Dad cheered and clapped. Finally, it was time to go home. Jenny was sad, but she knew she would be able to come back to the park again. Before getting in the car, she hugged her Mom and Dad and thanked them for such a fun and reliable day.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. It had a long fence around it. In the farm, there was a hen. The hen was very comfortable. She liked to lay eggs near the fence. One day, the hen met a cat. The cat said, "I saw a big nest near the fence. Did you lay eggs there?" The hen said, "Yes, I did. I like to lay eggs near the fence." The next day, the cat came back. He said, "I found a big egg near the fence. It is very big!" The hen was happy. She knew it was her egg. She went to the fence and saw the big egg. She was very proud of her big egg.
