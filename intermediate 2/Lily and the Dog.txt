Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a funny girl named Lily. She loved to celebrate her birthday with her friends. This year, she was turning five, and she was very excited about her big day. On the day of her birthday, Lily and her friends played games and ate cake. They had so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. A big box with the number five on it appeared in the middle of the room. Everyone was surprised and curious. Lily opened the big box, and out jumped a little dog! The little dog had a note that said, "I am your new friend. Let's celebrate together!" Everyone laughed and played with the little dog. It was a very funny and happy day for Lily and her friends.

---

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and watch TV. One day, they see a large truck on the TV. The truck is red and shiny. It has a big horn and a long ladder. "Wow, look at that truck!" Lily says. "I want a truck like that!" "Me too!" Ben says. "Let's ask Mommy for a truck!" They run to the kitchen, where Mommy is making lunch. They shout and jump. "Mommy, Mommy, we want a truck!" Lily demands. "A truck? What kind of truck?" Mommy asks. "A large truck, like on the TV!" Ben says. "It has a horn and a ladder!" Mommy smiles. She has some news for them. "Guess what, kids? Daddy has a surprise for you. He is coming home soon with a truck!" Lily and Ben are very happy. They clap and cheer. "A truck, a truck, we get a truck!" they sing. They run to the window and wait for Daddy. They see him drive up in a large truck. It is red and shiny. It has a big horn and a long ladder. "Daddy, Daddy, you have a truck!" Lily and Ben shout. They run outside and hug Daddy. He lifts them up and puts them in the truck. "Hello, my little firefighters!" he says. "Do you want to see how the truck works?" "Yes, yes, yes!" Lily and Ben say. Daddy shows them how to honk the horn, raise the ladder, and spray the water. Lily and Ben have a lot of fun. They thank Daddy for the truck. They love their new truck. It is the best surprise ever.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy bear named Bobo. Bobo was big and had a lot of weight. He lived in a forest with many trees. Bobo loved to stand on his two legs and try to dance. But because he was clumsy, he would often fall down. One sunny day, Bobo was trying to dance near a big tree. He was standing on his two legs and moving around. Suddenly, he tripped on a rock and fell on the tree. The tree started to shake, and then, something unexpected happened. A big pile of yummy honey fell from the tree! It turns out, there was a beehive hiding in the tree. The bees were not happy, but they flew away to find a new home. Bobo was so excited to find the honey. He ate and ate until his tummy was full. From that day on, Bobo was not sad about being clumsy anymore. He knew that sometimes, good things can happen when you least expect them.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a purple boat. He wanted to use it to row on the lake. He asked his friend, Sam, to lend him the boat. Sam said, "Yes, you can use my boat, but be careful!" Tim was happy and took the boat to the lake. He started to row, but he saw a big fish. The big fish was angry and wanted to eat the boat. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. The big fish jumped, and the boat flipped. Tim fell into the water. He tried to swim, but he couldn't. Sam saw what happened and tried to help, but it was too late. The big fish ate the purple boat, and Tim was very sad.
