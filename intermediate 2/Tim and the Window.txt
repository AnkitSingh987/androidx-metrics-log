Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Window

Once upon a time, in an ancient house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy weapon, a small sword. One day, Tim was playing in his room when he started to shiver. It was very cold outside. Tim's mom came into the room and said, "Why are you shivering, Tim? Let's put on your warm clothes." Tim put on his warm clothes and stopped shivering. He went back to playing with his toy weapon. Suddenly, Tim heard a noise outside. He looked out the window and saw a big, friendly dog. The dog was holding Tim's toy weapon in its mouth. The dog had found the toy outside and brought it back to Tim. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, dog!" They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He had a big boat. The sailor liked to lean on his boat and look at the water. One day, the sailor saw a fish. The fish was sorry because it was lost. The sailor wanted to help the fish. He leaned down and picked up the fish. The fish was happy. The sailor and the fish became friends. They played all day on the boat. But then, the sky got dark. The wind blew hard. The boat leaned too much. The sailor and the fish fell into the water. They could not swim back to the boat. The sailor and the fish were very sorry. They stayed in the water and could not go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very excited because today was his birthday. He knew that his friends would come to his house to play with him. Spot's best friend, a cat named Fluffy, came to the door. Spot said, "Come in, Fluffy! Let's play!" But when Fluffy walked in, she had a big sneeze. "Achoo!" she sneezed. Spot looked at Fluffy and said, "Why did you sneeze?" Fluffy looked sad and said, "I don't know. I sneeze when I'm near you, Spot." Spot was sad too. He wanted to play with Fluffy, but he didn't want her to sneeze all the time. So, they tried to find a way to play without making Fluffy sneeze. They decided to play outside, where there was more open space. Fluffy was excited to play in the open air. They played and laughed, and Fluffy didn't sneeze anymore. Spot and Fluffy were happy that they could play together without any sneezes. And they had the best birthday party ever.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a deaf boy named Tom. He could not hear, but he was very smart and kind. Tom had a big sack that he carried with him everywhere. He loved to fill it with things he found on his walks. One day, Tom found a soft and pretty blanket in the woods. It was a luxury blanket, the kind that kings and queens have. He picked it up and put it in his sack. Then, he saw an old lady who looked cold and sad. Tom knew he had to help her. He walked up to her and said, "Hello, I found this nice blanket. You look cold, do you want it?" The old lady smiled and said, "Oh, thank you, Tom! You are so kind!" Tom gave her the luxury blanket from his sack, and she wrapped it around herself. She was warm and happy, and Tom felt good for helping someone in need.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily and her mom. Lily loved to eat tasty food. One day, Lily's mom made a yummy cake. She wanted to show it to Lily. She said, "Lily, come here and look!" Lily ran to her mom and saw the big, tasty cake. "Wow, Mom! This cake looks so good!" Lily said. Her mom smiled and said, "We can't eat it yet, we have to wait for Dad to come home." Lily and her mom did fun things together while they waited for Dad. They played with toys, read books, and sang songs. When Dad came home, they all sat down and ate the tasty cake. It was the best cake they ever had.
