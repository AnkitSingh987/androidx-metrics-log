Category: Intermediate
Title: The House and the Family

Once upon a time, there was a small house. In the house, there was no furniture. The family who lived there was upset. They wanted to sit and sleep, but they had no place to do it. One day, a kind man came to the house. He saw that the family was upset. He said, "You need furniture to be happy." The kind man went away and came back with a chair, a table, and a bed. The family was so happy! The family learned that they should be kind to others, just like the kind man. They also learned that they only need simple things to be happy. The family was not upset anymore. They were very thankful for the kind man and the furniture he gave them.

One day, a fireman was walking down the street. He liked to march when he walked. His big boots made loud sounds on the ground. People could hear him coming from far away. The fireman saw a big cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and did not know how to get down. The fireman wanted to help the cat, but he was scared too. The loud sounds from his boots made the cat more scared. The fireman had an idea. He took off his big boots and walked very quietly to the tree. He climbed up and helped the cat come down. The cat was happy and the fireman was proud. From that day on, the fireman and the cat were best friends.

One day, a little pigeon was flying in the sky. The pigeon was very poor. He had no food to eat. The pigeon was sad. The pigeon saw a door. The door was shut. The pigeon wanted to open the door. He thought there might be food behind the door. The pigeon tried to push the door, but he could not open it. A kind girl saw the poor pigeon. She asked, "Why are you sad, little pigeon?" The pigeon said, "I am hungry, and I cannot open the door." The girl felt bad for the pigeon. She opened the door for him. The pigeon found food inside. The pigeon was happy, and the girl was happy too. They became good friends.

One day, a little fish named Bob was swimming near a big reef. Bob was a nosy fish who liked to know what was happening with his friends. He saw his friend, Tim the turtle, sitting on a rock. Tim looked sad. Bob swam to Tim and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my toy in the reef and I can't find it." Bob wanted to help, so he said, "Let's pray that we find your toy." As they prayed, a big wave came and moved the rocks on the reef. They saw something shiny under the rocks. It was Tim's toy! But, there was also a big, scary shark sleeping next to it. Bob and Tim were scared, but they knew they had to get the toy. They swam very quietly and grabbed the toy without waking the shark. They swam away fast and were so happy they found the toy. Tim thanked Bob and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. This rock had a big nose. The rock was very popular. All the kids liked to play with the rock. One day, a little girl came to play with the rock. She touched the big nose. Suddenly, the rock started to move! The kids were very surprised. They did not know that the rock could move. The rock stood up and smiled. It was not a rock! It was a big, friendly giant! The giant became friends with the kids. They all played together and had lots of fun. The giant with the big nose was very happy to have new friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a wise old owl. The owl had a lot of wisdom. He liked to remind his friends how to be fit and strong. One day, he saw a little rabbit who looked sad. "Hello, little rabbit," said the owl. "Why are you so sad?" "I want to be fit and strong like my friends, but I don't know how," said the little rabbit. The owl smiled and said, "I can remind you how to be fit. Just eat good food and play with your friends every day." The little rabbit listened to the owl's wisdom. He ate good food and played with his friends. Soon, he became fit and strong. He was very happy and thanked the owl for his help. From that day on, the rabbit and the owl became best friends.
