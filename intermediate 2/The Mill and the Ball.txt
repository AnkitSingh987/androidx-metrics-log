Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mill and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big mill. The mill was where people made flour. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the mill. He was very glad because he loved to see how the mill worked. It was a sunny day and Tim was happy. As Tim played near the mill, he found a big ball. He liked to squeeze the ball. It was fun and soft. But then, something unexpected happened. When Tim squeezed the ball, it made a loud noise. Tim was surprised and a little scared. Tim squeezed the ball again, and it made the same noise. He started to laugh. The noise was funny. It was not a ball, it was a toy that made noise when you squeezed it. Tim was happy and played with his new toy all day long.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends named Bob and Jeff. They enjoyed spending time together discussing all kinds of things. One day, Bob caught a flea in his yard. He looked at the little critter, feeling curious. "Hey Jeff, come take a look at this!" Bob said cheerfully. Jeff came over and said, "Wow, what is that?" Bob explained, "It's a flea. I found it in my backyard." Jeff examined it and said, "It looks so yummy, can I eat it?" Bob replied, "No, Jeff! You must never eat something that you don't know. We should always make sure we know what we eat, so that we stay safe and healthy." Moral of the story: Knowing what you eat is always important.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was an unusual tree. This tree had big spikes on it. No one knew why the tree was like that. All the other trees were smooth and nice. One day, a little girl named Mia walked close to the tree. She saw a small bird on one of the spikes. The bird looked sad. Mia asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I am stuck on this spike. I can't fly away." Mia wanted to help the bird. She got a stick and gently pushed the bird off the spike. The bird was free and happy. It said, "Thank you, Mia!" The bird flew away, and Mia felt good. From that day on, Mia and the bird were best friends. They played together every day, and the unusual tree with spikes was not so scary anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little dog named Bow. Bow loved to play and run in the park. One day, Bow saw a big pastry on the ground. It was round and looked very yummy. Bow wanted to eat the pastry. Bow took the pastry in his mouth and ran to his best friend, Kitty. Kitty was a small cat who liked to play with Bow. Bow showed Kitty the pastry. Kitty thought it looked yummy too. They decided to share the pastry. Bow and Kitty sat under a big tree and ate the pastry together. They both liked it very much. After they finished, they played and ran around the park. They had a lot of fun that day. Bow and Kitty were very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big tree and wanted to count how many leaves were on it. He started counting, "One, two, three..." but there were too many leaves. While counting, Tim found a stick on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Wow, this is a great weapon!" He started to play with the stick, pretending to be a brave knight fighting a dragon. Tim's friend, Amy, came to the park too. She saw Tim playing with the stick and asked, "Can I play with your weapon?" Tim was an honest boy and said, "Yes, you can play with it, but we have to take turns." They played together, fighting dragons and having fun.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. She saw a big blue ball on the grass. She asked her mom, "Can I play with the blue ball?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Amy played with the blue ball for a while. Then, she saw a boy named Tim. Tim had a big jar of jelly. Amy asked Tim, "Can I have some jelly?" Tim said, "Yes, but only a little." Amy and Tim sat under a tree and ate the jelly. It was yummy. They laughed and talked. After they finished eating, they played with the blue ball together. They had a fun day at the park.
