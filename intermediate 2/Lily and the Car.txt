Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Car

One day, a little girl named Lily was watching her favorite show on the screen. She was very happy. But then, her mom told her it was time to turn off the screen and go outside to play. Lily did not want to go outside. She was stubborn and wanted to keep watching her show. Lily's mom sighed. She knew Lily loved her show, but it was a nice day outside. She had an idea. "Lily, let's take your toy car and play outside," her mom said. Lily thought about it and decided to give it a try. Outside, Lily and her mom played with the toy car. They had so much fun! Lily forgot all about the screen and her show. She was happy to play outside with her mom. And her mom was happy that Lily was not stubborn anymore.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a sign that says "Do not whistle". They do not know what whistle means. "Ben, what is whistle?" Sara asks. "I do not know. Maybe it is a sound," Ben says. They try to make sounds with their mouths. They blow air and make funny noises. They laugh and have fun. A man walks by. He hears Sara and Ben. He is angry. He points at the sign. "Stop that! You are not allowed to whistle here. It is too loud. It hurts my ears," he says. Sara and Ben are scared. They do not want to make the man angry. They stop making sounds. "Sorry, sir. We do not know what whistle is. We are just playing," Sara says. The man looks at them. He sees that they are little. He feels sorry for them. He smiles a little. "Whistle is a sound you make with your lips. Like this," he says. He shows them how to whistle. He makes a soft and high sound. Sara and Ben are amazed. They try to whistle too. They make low and hard sounds. They do not sound like the man. The man laughs. He is not angry anymore. He likes Sara and Ben. "You are very cute. But you need to practice more. And do not whistle here. It is a quiet place. Go to another place where you can whistle loud and have fun," he says. He gives them a candy each. He walks away. Sara and Ben are happy. They have a new sound to play with. They have a candy to eat. They run to another place where they can whistle and laugh. They do not see the sign anymore.

One day, a dog and a cat were playing outside. The sun was very hot, and the ground was dry. The dog and the cat were looking for fun things to do. They found a box of matches on the ground. The dog said, "Let's discuss what we can do with these matches." The cat said, "We can use them to make a fire." The dog was not sure if that was a good idea. He was worried that the dry ground could catch fire too. The cat wanted to play with the matches, but the dog did not. They started to argue. The dog said, "It is too dangerous!" The cat said, "But it will be fun!" They could not agree on what to do with the matches. Finally, the dog and the cat decided to ask a wise old owl for help. The owl listened to their problem and said, "It is not safe to play with matches. The dry ground can catch fire, and that would be very bad." The dog and the cat listened to the owl and decided not to play with the matches. Instead, they found a ball to play with. They had fun playing together, and they were glad they listened to the wise old owl.

Once upon a time there lived a mouse called Miri. One day, Miri found a chest in her home. She was so excited and wanted to keep all her favourite things in the chest. Miri asked her mum, “Mum, can this chest be mine?” Her mum replied, “Yes, of course. But you have to be reliable and take care of it. Can you do that?” Miri thought for a moment and said, “Yes, I can take care of it!” Miri’s mum was pleased and said, “If you need any help, just tell me. It is important to always discuss things with parents before making a decision.” Miri nodded understandingly and took good care of her chest. Miri brought many wonderful things in her chest, such as her favourite toys, snacks, books and more. She also shared her chest with her friends so that everyone could enjoy. The moral of this story is to always discuss things with parents before making a decision. In this way, parents can help you make reliable decisions.
