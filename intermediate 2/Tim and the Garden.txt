Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Garden

A little boy named Tim had a tiny seed. He wanted it to grow big and strong. So, he planted it in the ground and watered it every day. One day, Tim saw a net on the ground. He did not know what it was for. He asked his mom, "What is this net for?" His mom said, "It is for catching unknown things that might come to our garden." As days passed, the seed started to grow. It grew bigger and bigger. Tim was very happy. But one day, when he went to see his plant, it was gone! He was sad and asked his mom, "Where is my plant?" His mom pointed at the net. Tim saw the plant inside the net. It was not a normal plant! It had big eyes and a mouth! The plant said, "Hi, Tim! Thank you for helping me grow!" Tim was surprised but happy. He made a new friend, the talking plant.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They played in the sand and swam in the water. Tim noticed a big black shell by the water. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a big black shell!" said Tim. His mom smiled and said, "That's a great find, Tim! Let's put it in our bag to take home." Just as they were about to put the shell in the bag, they heard a tiny voice. "Please don't take me away from my home!" said the voice. Tim and his mom looked around, but they didn't see anyone. Then they noticed the black shell was moving! A little crab came out of the shell. "I live in this shell, and I don't want to leave the beach," said the crab. Tim and his mom were surprised but understood. They put the shell back on the sand and watched the crab go back inside. From that day on, Tim always checked if someone lived in the shells he found at the beach. And he made a new friend, the little crab who lived in the big black shell.

One day, Sam and Tim were playing with their toy tanks. They both loved their tanks very much. They liked to have races to see who had the faster tank. They were very competitive. Sam said, "My tank is the best! It can go so fast!" Tim replied, "No, my tank is better! It can go even faster!" Then, their mom came and said, "Boys, why don't you share your tanks and play together instead of racing?" Sam and Tim thought about it and decided to try sharing. They took turns playing with each other's tank and found out that it was more fun when they shared. They were not competitive anymore and enjoyed playing together. And from that day on, Sam and Tim always shared their toys and played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toys and imagine big adventures. One day, Lucy found a roll of tape in her toy box. She thought it was a very special tape. Lucy had a friend named Tom. Tom was a very generous boy, always sharing his toys and snacks. Lucy went to Tom's house to play. She brought her special tape with her. "Look, Tom! I found this tape," Lucy said. "Let's imagine a big adventure!" Together, Lucy and Tom used the tape to make a big castle on the floor. They pretended to be king and queen of the castle. They had a fun day playing and sharing their toys. And they learned that with a little imagination and a roll of tape, they could have the best adventures ever.

Dad was taking his daughter to the park. She was very excited, clapping her hands and saying, "Yay!" On the way there, they passed by a loud construction site. Dad looked at his daughter and said, "It's a bit noisy here, isn't it? Why don't we find a quieter place?" Once they got to the park they heard birds chirping and kids laughing. Dad and his daughter went on the slides and swings. Dad asked her, "Isn't it nice here? It's much quieter than the construction site." His daughter smiled and said, "Yes, I like it here!" The moral of the story is that it's important to try and find quieter places and activities as we can learn from them. By being in a quieter environment, we can concentrate and be peaceful.
