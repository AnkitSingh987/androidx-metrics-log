Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Squirrel

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the park. He saw a big tree with a lot of fruit. Tim wanted to get the fruit, but his hand was too weak to climb the tree. He felt sad and sat down. A kind squirrel saw Tim and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "I want the fruit, but my hand is too weak to climb the tree." The squirrel wanted to help Tim. So, the squirrel climbed the tree and gave Tim the fruit. The tree yielded many fruits for Tim. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, squirrel!" They both ate the fruit together and became good friends. From that day on, Tim and the squirrel always played together in the park.

Tim and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. They did not want to disturb Mom, who was working on her laptop at the table. But then, they heard a loud knock on the door. They ran to see who it was. They saw a big box with their names on it. It was a gift from Grandma and Grandpa! "Wow, what is it?" Tim asked. "Let's open it and see!" Lily said. They tore the paper and opened the box. Inside, they found two costumes: a dinosaur and a fairy. They were so happy and excited. They put on the costumes and looked at each other. "You look like a scary dinosaur, Tim!" Lily said. "You look like a pretty fairy, Lily!" Tim said. They hugged and thanked Grandma and Grandpa on the phone. Then, they went back to the living room to play with their new costumes. They roared and flew and had more fun. They did not disturb Mom, who smiled and watched them from the table. She was glad that the costumes fit them well and that they were having a good time. She loved her children very much.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lily. She loved to play with clay. One sunny day, Lily decided to bake something with her clay. Lily rolled the clay into a big ball. She pressed it flat and made a pretty shape. She was very happy with her work. Then, Lily put the clay in the sun to bake. After a while, the clay was hard and ready. Lily showed her baked clay to her friends. They all clapped and cheered. Lily felt proud and happy. From that day on, Lily and her friends loved to bake with clay together.

One day, a hairy dog named Max went for a walk. Max liked to count his steps. He wanted to gain a big number today. Max saw a little girl named Amy. Amy was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Amy?" Amy said, "I lost my ball. Can you help me find it?" Max nodded and they went to look for the ball together. They looked up and down, and soon Max saw the ball. It was in a tree. Max jumped and got the ball for Amy. Amy was happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" Max was happy too because he had gained a big number of steps and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Kitty. Kitty was a serious cat who liked to help others. One day, Kitty saw a bird with a hurt wing. The bird was sad and could not fly. Kitty said to the bird, "Don't worry, I will help you heal." Kitty found soft leaves and put them on the bird's wing. The bird felt better and said, "Thank you, Kitty!" After a few days, the bird's wing was all better. The bird could fly again! Kitty and the bird became best friends. They played together every day, and Kitty was always there to help when the bird needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tim. Tim was very sad because he had no food to eat. He wanted to pay for some food, but he had no money. One day, he saw a big dog named Sam who had a lot of food. Tim wanted to be friends with Sam so they could share the food. Tim went to Sam and asked if they could be friends. Sam said yes, but only if Tim would help him with a problem. The problem was that Sam had a big box, and he could not open it. Tim was scared of Sam, but he wanted the food, so he agreed to help. Tim tried to open the box, but it was very hard. Then, Tim had an idea. He used his thin tail to pick the lock on the box. To their surprise, the box opened and the result was amazing. Inside the box, there was a lot of money! Now, Tim and Sam could pay for all the food they wanted. They became best friends and always shared their food and money. The thin cat and the big dog lived happily ever after.
