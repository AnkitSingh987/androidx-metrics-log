Category: Intermediate
Title: The Monkey Was Playing and the Bird

One day, a little monkey was playing in the trees. He was happy and having fun. But then, he saw something strange. His favorite toy had vanished! The poor monkey was sad and didn't know what to do. He asked his friend, the bird, "Have you seen my toy?" The bird shook her head and said, "No, I have not seen it. Let's go look for it together." So, the monkey and the bird went to search for the toy. As they looked, they saw a big cat. The cat had the monkey's toy! The monkey and the bird were scared, but they knew they had to get the toy back. They made a plan to get the toy from the cat. The bird flew up high and made a loud noise. The cat looked up at the bird, and the monkey quickly grabbed his toy. The cat was mad, but the monkey and the bird were too fast. They ran away and the cat couldn't catch them. In the end, the monkey and the bird were happy. The monkey had his toy back, and they both had a new friend. They played together in the trees, and the poor monkey was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. He had a toy car with a big handle. He loved to push the car around his room. The handle was easy for him to hold. It was his favorite toy. One day, Tim lost his toy car. He looked in his room, under the bed, and in the toy box, but he could not find it. Tim was very sad. He sat on his bed and started to cry. His mom came in and saw him crying. She asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told his mom about his lost toy car. She said, "Let's pray and ask for help to find it." Tim and his mom closed their eyes and prayed. After they prayed, Tim felt warm and happy inside. They looked for the toy car together. They found it behind the big chair in the living room. Tim was so happy to have his toy car back. He thanked his mom and gave her a big hug. From that day on, Tim always prayed when he needed help.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to take naps in his small, cozy bed. But one day, Fluffy saw a big, dangerous-looking cat outside his house. Fluffy was scared, but he wanted to show the cat that he was brave. So, Fluffy went outside to play with the dangerous cat. The cat looked at Fluffy and started to laugh. Fluffy felt embarrass, but he still wanted to show the cat that he was not scared. He barked and tried to chase the cat, but the cat was too fast for him. Fluffy was getting tired and wanted to go back to his cozy bed for a nap. As Fluffy was running back to his house, he tripped and fell into a big hole. The cat laughed even more at Fluffy. Fluffy was very sad and stuck in the hole. He wished he had listened to his mommy and stayed inside for a nap. Now, Fluffy was in a big trouble, and he couldn't get out of the hole.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play with his daddy. But daddy was very busy working on his big computer. Tim felt sad because he wanted to play with daddy. Tim had an idea. He thought if he could make daddy laugh, maybe daddy would stop working and play with him. So, Tim decided to slap his own face to make a funny sound. Daddy looked at Tim and smiled, but he did not stop working. Tim tried to slap his face again, but this time he slapped too hard. It hurt a lot and Tim started to cry. Daddy stopped working and hugged Tim. He said, "I'm sorry I was too busy to play with you, but you should not hurt yourself to get my attention." Tim learned that hurting himself was not the right way to solve his problems.
