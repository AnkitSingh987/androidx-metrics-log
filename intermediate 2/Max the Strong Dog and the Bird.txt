Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Strong Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max had a toy motor that he loved to play with. He would run and push the motor all around the yard. One day, a big wind came and blew the motor far away. Max was sad because he lost his favorite toy. Max went on a journey to find his motor. He walked and walked until he saw a big hill. At the top of the hill, there was a big bird who had taken his motor. The bird was very big and strong, but Max was brave. He knew he had to save his motor from the big bird. Max climbed the hill and faced the big bird. He barked loudly and showed that he was strong too. The big bird was scared of Max and let go of the motor. Max was happy to save his toy! He took the motor back home and played with it all day long.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl. She had a big brother and they used to play together all the time. One day they were playing a game and her brother stepped on a small doll and it got broken. The little girl became very sad and started to cry. Her brother felt so sorry for making her cry. "Don't be sad, little sister" her brother said. "Imagine if the doll wasn't broken. What would you do?" The little girl stopped crying and thought for a moment. "I would play with my dolls, and I would give you one to play with too!" Her brother smiled and gave her a big hug. "Let's do that next time, okay?" The little girl nodded and hugged him back, happy to have a brother who cared for her. From that day forward, every time they played, they imagined that the doll was never broken.

Once upon a time, there was a little red car. The car was sad because it could not go. It needed fuel to go, but it had none. The car sat in the yard, wishing it could go on a ride. One day, a girl named Lucy came to the yard. She saw the sad car and wanted to help. Lucy said, "I will fix you, little car!" She went to find some fuel to make the car go. Lucy found fuel and put it in the car. The car was so happy! It could go now. Lucy and the car went for a long ride. They were both very happy. From that day, the car was never sad again.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Jenny and Lee. Jenny really wanted to create something special, so they decided to make a plan. "Let's have a race. We can pick a far away place as our finish line," Lee suggested. Jenny was so excited. They raced and raced, but Lee was always far in the distance. Finally, they reached the finish line. Jenny had won! Lee cheered, "You did it! You created a victory!" Jenny was so proud of her accomplishment. She had created a win, and it felt amazing. The friends hugged, happy to share this special moment. The end.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and make mud pies. They found a big jug in the shed. It was old and dirty. They did not know what was inside. "Let's open it!" said Ben. He tried to pull the lid, but it was stuck. He asked Lily to help him. They pulled and pulled, but the lid did not move. "Maybe we should tell mom," said Lily. She was scared of the unknown jug. What if it had something bad inside? What if it was a trap? "No, let's keep it a secret," said Ben. He was curious and brave. He wanted to see what was inside. He had an idea. He ran to the hose and turned it on. He sprayed water on the jug. The water made the dirt go away. The jug became shiny and bright. It had pictures and words on it. But they were unknown pictures and words. They did not look like anything Lily and Ben had seen before. The water also made the lid loose. It popped open with a loud noise. Lily and Ben jumped back. They saw something come out of the jug. It was not water or mud. It was not a toy or a snack. It was not a bug or a flower. It was something unknown. It was small and round and shiny. It had wings and eyes and a mouth. It looked at Lily and Ben and smiled. "Hello," it said. "Thank you for freeing me. I am a fairy. I have been trapped in this jug for a long time. You are very kind and brave. I will grant you each a wish. What do you want?" Lily and Ben were amazed. They had never seen a fairy before. They had never had a wish before. They looked at each other and smiled. They knew what they wanted. They wanted to tell mom.
