Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved her notebook. She would draw and write in it every day. She believed that her notebook was magic. One day, Lily found a dead bug in her room. She was sad for the bug. She drew a picture of the bug in her notebook. Then, something unexpected happened. The bug came back to life! It was not dead anymore. Lily was so happy. She knew her notebook was magic. From then on, she drew and wrote about good things in her notebook. And sometimes, those good things came true.

---

One day, a dancer named Lily lived in a small town. She was different from the other dancers because she liked to drop her shoes and dance with her bare feet. The other dancers did not like this and would laugh at her. Lily felt sad. One day, a new dancer named Mia came to town. She saw Lily drop her shoes and dance with her feet. Mia thought it was fun and wanted to try it too. She said, "Lily, can you teach me how to dance like that?" Lily smiled and taught Mia how to dance without shoes. The other dancers saw Mia and Lily dancing together and having fun. They stopped laughing and wanted to learn too. Soon, all the dancers in the town were dropping their shoes and dancing with their feet. They all became friends and danced together, happy that they could be different and still have fun.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the gym with his mom. Tim was very excited. He loved to play and jump on the big mats. When they got to the gym, there were many other kids there too. Tim saw a big ball and ran to it. He wanted to play with it. A girl named Lily was there too. She also wanted to play with the ball. Tim said, "Let's play with the ball together!" Lily agreed and they started to play. But then, Tim and Lily started to fight over the ball. They both wanted it all to themselves. They began to scream and cry. Their moms came over and tried to calm them down, but it was too late. The gym teacher said that they had to leave. Tim and Lily were very sad that they could not play anymore. Their fun day at the gym ended badly because they did not share.

---

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and swing. They saw a big oak tree with many leaves. They wanted to climb it. "Can we go up?" Ben asked Lily. "Yes, let's go!" Lily said. They ran to the tree and found a low branch. They helped each other to get on it. They felt very happy. They looked around and saw many things. They saw birds and squirrels and flowers. They saw the sun and the clouds and the sky. They saw other children and their moms and dads. "Wow, this is so nice!" Ben said. "Yes, it is!" Lily said. "Thank you, tree, for letting us climb you!" They decided to say thank you to the tree. They got down from the branch and stood in front of the tree. They bowed to the tree and said, "Thank you, tree, for being so big and strong and green!" The tree did not say anything, but it rustled its leaves. Ben and Lily thought the tree was happy too. They felt very grateful. They hugged the tree and then each other. They smiled and ran back to play.

Once upon a time, there was a proud driver named Tom. He had a big red car. Tom loved to drive his car all around town. One day, Tom saw a play in the park. He wanted to act in the play too. So, he put on a funny hat and went on stage. He acted like a silly cat and made the kids laugh. In the end, Tom was very happy. He was a proud driver and a funny actor. He drove home with a big smile on his face.
