Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hockey. He played hockey every day with his friends. They had so much fun! But Tim's room was not tidy. It was very messy. His toys and hockey things were all over the floor. One day, Tim's mom told him to change. She said, "Tim, you need to be tidy. You must clean your room before you play hockey." Tim did not want to clean his room. He just wanted to play hockey. But Tim's mom was right. He needed to change and be tidy. So Tim cleaned his room. He put all his toys and hockey things away. His room was now very tidy. Tim's mom was happy. Tim was happy too. He could play hockey with his friends and have a tidy room. The moral of the story is: it's good to change and be tidy.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with his ball. One day, Fluffy saw a big box. He wanted to lift the box to find his ball. Fluffy tried very hard, but the box was too heavy. Then, Fluffy met a new friend, a bird named Chirpy. Chirpy spoke a different language, but they still understood each other. They both wanted to help each other and have fun together. Fluffy and Chirpy decided to work together to lift the big box. With Fluffy pushing and Chirpy flying, they lifted the box. Under the box, they found Fluffy's ball! They were so happy and played with the ball all day. Fluffy and Chirpy became best friends, even if they spoke different languages.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a group of ants. They were very organized. They always walked in a straight line. One day, they had to pass a big rock to get to their food. As the ants passed the rock, a little bird saw them. The bird thought it would be fun to play a trick on the ants. So, the bird moved the rock to a new spot. Now, the ants' line was not straight. The ants were confused, but they did not give up. They worked together and found a new way to pass the rock. They made a new straight line. The bird saw this and felt bad for playing the trick. The bird learned that it is not nice to mess up other's hard work. The moral of the story is: it is important to be kind and respect the hard work of others.

Once upon a time, in a small house lived a family. The family had a mum, dad and their little daughter. One day, the family saw a white pill appear in the garden. Dad and Mum smiled at each other; they knew what the pill was for. Dad asked the little girl, "can you be gentle and pick up the pill?" The little girl smiled and carefully took the pill. Mum said "Let's go inside, I'll make it into something special for dinner tonight.” The little girl, dad and mum all went inside and Mum began to cook the gentle pill. They all had a wonderful dinner, and all went to bed feeling happy and thankful for the little pill that had appeared in their garden!

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. The sky was dull and grey, but Tim did not care. He liked to play with his toy cars under the big tree. As Tim played, he found a shiny rock. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was special. He took the rock to his mom and asked, "Mom, is this rock of value?" His mom looked at the rock and smiled. She said, "It is just a rock, but if you like it, then it is of value to you." The next day, Tim took the rock to show his friends. They all laughed and said it was just a dull rock. Tim felt sad and put the rock in his pocket. Just then, a big wind came, and it started to rain. All the kids ran home, but Tim's rock began to glow. The rain stopped, and the sun came out, making the day bright and happy. Tim's rock was not just a dull rock; it was a magic rock that could change the weather! From that day on, Tim and his friends knew that even small things could have great value.
