Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dirty Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a dirty little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the mud. One day, Spot found a big ball with a handle. Spot was very happy and wanted to play with the ball all day long. Spot took the ball with the handle and started to run around the yard. As he played, the ball got dirtier and dirtier. Spot didn't care, he just wanted to play and have fun. The more Spot played with the ball, the more his love for the ball increased. After a long day of playing, Spot's mom called him inside. She saw how dirty he and the ball were. She gave Spot a bath and cleaned the ball with the handle. Spot and the ball were now clean and ready for another day of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to play in the woods near her house. One day, she saw a big mushroom. The mushroom was red with white dots. Lily thought it was pretty. Lily said to the mushroom, "Hi, Mr. Mushroom! Do you want to play with me?" The mushroom did not talk back, but Lily pretended it said yes. They played all day. The sun was hot and Lily was thirsty. She wanted water to drink. Lily found a puddle and tried to soak water with her hands to drink. But the water was dirty and not good for her. Lily got sick from the bad water. She went home and her mommy took care of her. Lily learned not to drink dirty water again.

One day, a fast cat named Tom went for a walk. Tom saw a big tree and decided to climb it. When he got to the top, he saw a bird. The bird said, "Hi! Do you want to play a quiz game with me?" Tom said, "Yes! I love quiz games!" The bird started to ask questions. Tom was very good at the quiz. He answered all the questions right. Then, the bird asked, "What makes a loud ring sound?" Tom thought hard. He did not know the answer. Suddenly, he heard a loud ring sound. It was a big bell on the tree! The bird laughed and said, "That was the answer! The bell rings!" Tom laughed too. He did not expect the bell to ring at that moment. They both had fun playing the quiz game and became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a beautiful bird called Steve. He loved to fly high and far as he could in the sky. One day, Steve was flying and spotted a rare reward beneath him. It was long and golden, and sparkled brightly in the sun. He stopped and hovered for a moment, before deciding to pass the reward to his friends. He flew back to the tree where his friends were waiting. They were so excited! "What is it?" they chirped in unison. Steve smiled. "It's a rare reward. One of us will get to have it." The birds all looked at each other, eager to find out who the lucky winner would be. Then Steve said he knew just the right way to find out. The birds agreed, and with a few shakes of his tail, Steve passed the reward around. Each bird took a turn until finally one of them found the surprise inside the reward - a sweet, juicy bug! Everyone cheered in delight and thanked Steve for his kindness. That's why Steve was the most beloved bird in the sky. He always rewarded his friends with a rare treat and made them feel special.

Once upon a time, in a unique forest, there lived a big bear and a little rabbit. They were very good friends. They played together, ate together, and helped each other. One day, while playing near the river, they saw a big bird. The bird looked like a threat. The bird said, "I will take all your food supply if you don't give me something special." The bear and the rabbit were scared. They thought hard about what to give the bird. The rabbit had an idea. He said to the bear, "Let's give the bird a big hug. A hug is special and unique." The bear agreed. They went to the bird and said, "We will give you a big hug. It is special and unique." The bird was surprised and happy. The bear and the rabbit gave the bird a big hug. The bird was so happy that he didn't take their food supply. He became their friend too. They all played together, ate together, and helped each other. The moral of the story is that sometimes a simple act of kindness can change a threat into a friend.
