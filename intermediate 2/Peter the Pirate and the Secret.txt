Category: Intermediate
Title: Peter the Pirate and the Secret

Once upon a time, there was a pirate named Peter. He had an old, hidden yacht that he kept in a secret cove. One day, he decided to take his yacht out to sea. Peter looked around. Everything seemed very still and quiet. Suddenly, he heard somebody calling out! "Peter, why don't you come join me for a sail?" It was his friend Jack, who also had a yacht. "OK!" said Peter. They set sail and soon arrived in a beautiful bay. They quickly saw a school of dolphins swimming by. "Let's release the sail," said Jack. Peter and Jack released the sail and the wind started to blow. Both yachts glided across the water and the dolphins followed them. "It's so peaceful here," said Jack. "Yes, it's amazing," agreed Peter. The two friends smiled and they sailed until the sun set. It had been a wonderful day and they all enjoyed it. Peter and Jack returned home with wonderful memories of their hidden adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside. One day, he found a small seed. Tom wanted to see the seed grow. He put the seed in his pocket and went home. At home, Tom took the seed out of his pocket. He put it in a pot with dirt. He gave it water and put it in the sun. Tom waited and waited. The seed started to grow. It grew bigger and bigger. Tom was very happy. One day, the plant grew too big for the pot. It was very uncomfortable. Tom knew he had to do something. He took the plant outside and put it in the ground. The plant was happy and grew even more. Tom loved his big plant and took care of it every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch and learn. One day, he went to a fun place where he saw many kids playing games. They were all trying to win and be the best. Tim wanted to join them. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was good at the games. She said, "Hi Tim! Do you want to play with us?" Tim said, "Yes, I want to play too!" They played a game where they had to find a hidden toy. Tim watched Sue and learned from her. Then, Tim found a special toy. It had magic! When he touched it, he could see where all the hidden toys were. Tim and Sue won the game. They were so happy. They played more games and had a great day.

One sunny day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big structure with a swing. Mia was very happy and ran to the swing. She sat on it and started to swing back and forth, higher and higher. A small, fragile bird flew by and landed on the swing next to Mia. The bird said, "Hello, Mia! Can I swing with you?" Mia was surprised to hear the bird talk, but she smiled and said, "Yes, of course!" They both swung together, laughing and having fun. Suddenly, the bird started to grow bigger and bigger! Mia couldn't believe her eyes. The big bird looked at Mia and said, "Thank you for being my friend. I am a magic bird, and now I can fly again!" With a big smile, the magic bird flew high into the sky, and Mia waved goodbye.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a summer day. The sun was shining, and the kids were playing outside. They loved summer because they could play all day long. They liked to play in the dirt and get filthy. Their clothes would be very dirty, but they didn't care. One day, a big girl named Lily decided to organize a game for all the kids. She wanted to make a fun race. The kids would run, jump, and even crawl in the dirt. They were all excited to play the new game. They knew they would get filthy, but they didn't mind. The race started, and all the kids ran as fast as they could. They jumped over logs and crawled through the dirt. At the end of the race, they were all filthy and happy. They laughed and played, and Lily was proud of the fun game she organized. The kids had a great summer day, and they couldn't wait to play again.
