Category: Intermediate
Title: The Beautiful Crystal and the Tom

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a beautiful crystal. It was very shiny and everyone loved it. But one day, it became dirty and not shiny anymore. The people in the village were sad. A stubborn boy named Tom wanted to help. He said, "I can wash the crystal and make it shiny again!" But the other kids in the village didn't believe Tom could do it. They said, "You are too small, Tom. You can't wash the crystal." Tom didn't listen to them. He went to the crystal with a bucket of water and a soft cloth. He started to wash the crystal. It was hard work, but Tom didn't give up. He washed and washed until the crystal was shiny again. The people in the village were so happy. They thanked Tom for his hard work. The other kids said, "We were wrong, Tom. You are not too small. You did a great job!" From that day on, the village knew that even a small boy like Tom could do big things.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play in the big field near his home. One day, while playing in the field, he saw a big tree with a small, scared cat named Fluffy up on a branch. "Fluffy," said Spot, "why are you up there? It's fun down here in the field!" Fluffy looked down at Spot and said, "I was minding my own business, but a big bird scared me. Now, I'm too scared to come down." Spot wanted to help Fluffy. He thought and thought, and then he had an idea. "I will go get my friend, Sam. He is very tall and can help you come down," said Spot. So, Spot ran to get his friend Sam, who was a tall giraffe. Sam came to the big tree and helped Fluffy come down. Fluffy was so happy to be back on the ground. From that day on, Spot, Fluffy, and Sam became best friends and played together every day in the big field.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lily. She loved to zip her coat when it was cold outside. One day, she went to the park with her mom. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. At the park, she saw a squirrel. The squirrel looked hungry. Lily remembered she had a cookie in her pocket. She took out the cookie and said, "Do you want a cookie, Mr. Squirrel?" The squirrel came closer and took the cookie from Lily's hand. It was so happy! Lily and the squirrel became good friends. They played together in the park until it was time to go home. Lily zipped her coat and waved goodbye to her new friend.

Once upon a time there was a lovely fairy called Lola. Lola loved to measure things. One day Lola was measuring the sky. She used her magic measuring tape to measure from one cloud to the other. Just then she heard a voice saying: "Hello, Lola. What are you doing?" Lola was surprised to see a little girl in a pink dress. She smiled and said, "Hi! I am measuring the sky." The little girl looked up and said, "Oh! That looks fun. Can I help?" Lola laughed and said, "Of course! Just hold one end of the measuring tape while I measure from cloud to cloud." The little girl did as she was told and soon they finished. They stood back and admired their work. "That was lovely!" said the little girl. Lola smiled. "Yes, it was! You did a great job measuring with me." The little girl was so happy she gave Lola a big hug and thanked her. "Anytime!," replied Lola. "That was fun!" And they both flew off into the sky, happy and laughing. The end.

John and his mom were out walking in the park. He saw some kids playing on a swing set and he wanted to join them. He asked his mom if he could play and she said yes. John excitedly ran over to the swing set. He saw the kids had all kinds of gear like helmets and knee pads. He asked his mom what they were doing. She said they were wearing safety gear to make sure they were safe while they were playing. John thought this was strange, but Mom said it was just a normal thing for safety. He agreed and put his helmet on. Then he hopped onto the swing and started having lots of fun. He asked his mom to push him on the swings and she did. John had so much fun that day. He was so happy he got to play on the swing set with his friends while feeling safe and normal in his gear.
