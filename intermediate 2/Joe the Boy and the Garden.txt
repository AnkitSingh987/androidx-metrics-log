Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe the Boy and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a boy named Joe. He was three years old and playing in the garden. Joe wanted to pick some flowers, so he went to the garden. He saw some pretty yellow and red strips. Joe was very grateful and decided to pick them. He started picking the strips and was almost done, when suddenly his mom saw him. She started getting mad at Joe. Joe's mom said, "Joe, you can't take those strips, they're not yours." She said it in a very stern voice. Joe felt sad and tried to explain, but his mom didn't listen. Instead, she told him to place the strips back in the garden. Joe felt scared and put the strips in the garden. Joe was so sad that he didn't feel grateful anymore. He had made a mistake and it had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. In the room, there was a big frame. The frame had a pretty picture of a party. The party was to celebrate a birthday. A little girl named Lily lived in the house with the messy room. Lily saw the frame and wanted to celebrate, too. She put on a party hat and started to dance and sing. Lily's mommy and daddy came to see what was happening. They saw the messy room and the frame. They decided to help Lily clean up and celebrate her pretend party. They all had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle sailor named Tom. He had a big boat that he loved to sail on the blue sea. Tom was very kind and always helped others when they needed it. One sunny day, Tom saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Tom wanted to help the bird, so he picked it up and said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will take care of you and prevent you from getting hurt more." The little bird was happy and thanked Tom. Tom took the bird with him on his boat. They sailed together and became good friends. Tom helped the bird get better, and soon the bird could fly again. The bird stayed with Tom and they had many fun adventures on the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin liked to play near the shore. One day, Fin saw the sun rise. It was big and bright. Fin felt happy and wanted to tell his friend, Sam the crab. Fin swam to Sam and said, "Sam, come see the sun rise! It is so pretty!" Sam was a serious crab, but he liked pretty things too. So, Sam and Fin went to the shore to see the sun rise together. As they watched the sun rise, they saw the sky turn many colors. It was beautiful. Sam smiled and said, "Fin, thank you for showing me this. I like it a lot!" They both enjoyed the sun rise and played by the shore all day.

Once upon a time, there was a wild cat. This cat was not nice. One day, the cat saw a little mouse near a big church. The mouse was very scared. The wild cat said, "Mouse, I will eat you!" The little mouse cried, "Please, wild cat, do not eat me! Let's be friends!" The mouse wanted the cat to accept being friends. But the wild cat did not want to be friends. The cat chased the mouse around the church. They ran and ran. The mouse was very tired. In the end, the wild cat caught the little mouse. The mouse was very sad, and the wild cat was not nice.

One day, a little cat named Kitty felt very thirsty. She saw a big cup of milk on the table. Kitty wanted to drink the milk, but the table was too high for her to reach. She felt frustrated. She looked around and saw a soft cushion on the floor. Kitty had an idea. She pushed the cushion with her paws to the table. She jumped on the cushion and then onto the table. Now she could drink the milk. She was so happy! Kitty's friend, Doggy, came into the room. He saw Kitty drinking the milk. Doggy said, "Kitty, you are so smart! You used the cushion to reach the milk." Kitty smiled and said, "Yes, I did! Now I am not thirsty anymore." They both laughed and played together all day.
