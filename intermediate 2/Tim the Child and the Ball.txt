Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Child and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little child named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big ball near the tree. He wanted to play with it. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim! The ball might come too fast." But Tim did not listen. He ran to the ball and kicked it hard. The ball went up high in the sky. As the ball came down, Tim's friend Ben ran to catch it. "I got it!" Ben shouted. Tim and Ben laughed and played with the ball all day. They were happy and had lots of fun.

---

One day, a big van came to the park. It was a food van. A man and his dog, Tim, went to the van to eat. The man said, "I want a hot dog, please." The dog, Tim, wanted a hot dog too. The man gave Tim a hot dog, but Tim was very hungry. He saw another hot dog on the table. Tim jumped up and ate the hot dog very fast. The man said, "Oh no, Tim! That was not your hot dog." Tim felt embarrassed. He knew he made a mistake. The man said, "It's okay, Tim. We will say sorry and give them a new hot dog." They said sorry and everyone was happy again. Tim learned not to eat other people's food.

Once upon a time, there was a polite little girl named Anna. She loved to eat oranges. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. While they were playing, Anna saw a big orange on the ground. She paused and picked it up. She wanted to share it with her friends. She said, "Look, I found a big orange! Let's share it." Her friends were happy and they all sat down to eat the orange together. They took turns and said, "Thank you" to Anna. They all enjoyed the sweet orange and continued to play in the park.

---

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was an ordinary boy who liked to play and have fun. One day, Tom was playing with his ball and accidentally broke his mom's cup. The cup fell on the floor and broke into pieces. Tom felt very sad. Tom wanted to repair the cup, so he asked his dad for help. His dad said, "Don't worry, Tom. We can fix the cup together." They found some glue and carefully put the pieces of the cup back together. It took a long time, but they did it. When the cup was fixed, Tom's mom saw the repaired cup and was very happy. She gave Tom a big hug and said, "Thank you for fixing my cup." Tom felt proud and happy that he could help his mom. From that day on, Tom was more careful when he played near his mom's things.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted girl. She loved to help people, and always wanted to fix things when they were broken. One day, she heard that a friend was very sick, and so she decided to make them some medicine. She used her special gifts to gather the right ingredients and mix them together in just the right way. She worked hard and fixed the medicine exactly right. When she brought it to her friend, the medicine was a success! The friend was feeling much better in no time. The girl was very proud of her work, and learned a valuable lesson - no problem is too hard to fix if you use your gifts. The moral of the story is that you should always use your special gifts to help out those in need. Together we can fix anything!

Once there was a little girl named Cara. She liked to explore the world around her. One day she found something small and shiny on the kitchen counter. It seemed like lotion! She was so excited and quickly picked it up. Cara decided to try it out. She squeezed the bottle and put some lotion on her fingers. The lotion was thick and smelled nice. She rubbed it on her arms and it felt smooth. She liked it a lot! Cara then wanted to share the lotion with her friends. She put some in a small container and shared it with her friends. Everyone was so happy and agreed that the lotion seemed like the best thing ever!
