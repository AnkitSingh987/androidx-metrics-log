Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Mia and the Sun

Tom and Mia are friends who like to play with snow. They make a big snowman in the park with a hat, a scarf and a carrot nose. They are very happy with their snowman and give him a name: Bob. But the next day, the sun is very bright and warm. Tom and Mia go to the park to see Bob, but they are sad. Bob is not big anymore. He is small and wet. His hat and scarf are on the ground. His carrot nose is gone. He is melting. "Who did this to Bob?" Tom asks. "He was our friend." "Nobody did this, Tom," Mia says. "It is the sun. The sun makes the snow melt. It is not Bob's fault. It is not our fault. It is just what happens." "But I don't want Bob to melt. I want him to stay with us. Can we save him?" Tom says. Mia thinks for a moment. She has an idea. "Maybe we can. Come on, let's get a bucket and a shovel. We can scoop up some snow and put it in the bucket. Then we can take it home and put it in the freezer. That way, Bob will not melt anymore. He will be cold and hard." Tom likes Mia's idea. He nods and smiles. They run to get a bucket and a shovel. They work hard to fill the bucket with snow. They carry it home and put it in the freezer. They hope that Bob will be happy there. They are proud of themselves. They think they have saved Bob from melting. They do not know that they have made a lot of waste. They do not know that their parents will be angry when they find a bucket of snow in the freezer. They do not know that Bob will not be the same as before. They are poor snowman savers. But they are good friends.

It was a sunny day, and young Jack was quite excited. He had a notebook in his hands and was looking at the pages. His Mum looked over to him and smiled. "What have you there, Jack?" She asked. Jack clutched the notebook tight. He replied, "My notebook!" Mum nodded and smiled. "What do you have in it?" Jack opened the notebook and flipped through the pale pages. Then he looked up and said, "I want to draw something special." Mum smiled. "What do you want to draw, sweetheart?" Jack ran his fingers over the paper. "A rainbow!" Mum grabbed some crayons and handed them to Jack. He grinned, and started to draw. It was beautiful, with the bright pinks and purples. Suddenly, Jack started to cry. Mum hugged him. "What's wrong, Jack?" "I tore my notebook," he sniffled. Mum gave him a concerned look. She said, "It's okay, sweetheart. We can just tape it back together." Jack looked up with a watery smile and nodded. Mum took the notebook and carefully put it back together. She gave it back to Jack and he smiled. Jack hugged his Mum. "Thank you!"

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a red cap. He loved his cap very much. He wore it every day. One day, Tom went to the park. He saw a big ball. The ball was very hard. Tom kicked the hard ball. The ball flew high in the sky. Tom's cap fell off his head. A girl named Sue saw Tom's cap. She picked it up. Sue said, "Tom, I found your cap." Tom was happy. He said, "Thank you, Sue. You are very nice." Tom and Sue played with the hard ball. They served the ball to each other. They had fun. At the end of the day, Tom wore his red cap and went home.

It was a sunny day, and Lilly was playing outside in her garden with her dolls. She was very happy. Suddenly, her mum came into the garden and demanded that she come inside. Lilly felt a shock of surprise, but did as she was told. When she entered the house she saw that an attractive person had arrived, and was already inside. The person introduced herself as Mary, and told her that she was a long lost aunt. "Where have you been?" asked Lilly. Mary explained that she had gone travelling and had only just returned. Lilly and Mary talked and played until it was time to go to bed. Lilly was happy to discover she had an aunt, and was excited to learn more about her. The next day, Mary took Lilly to the park so they could get to know each other better. As they played, Lilly could feel the same shock she had felt when she first saw her aunt. It was as if she had known all along that something special was going to happen. At the end of the day, Lilly was happy to know her aunt and excited for more adventures in the future.
