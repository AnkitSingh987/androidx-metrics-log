Category: Intermediate
Title: The Messy Volcano and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a messy volcano. The volcano had a lot of rocks and dirt all around it. People in the town did not like the messy volcano because it made their town dirty. One day, a man came to the town. He wanted to sell a big cleaning tool to fix the messy volcano. He said, "I can sell you this tool to make your volcano clean!" The people in the town were happy and bought the tool from the man. They tried to clean the volcano, but they did not know how to use the tool right. The volcano got mad and made a big boom. The town was now even messier than before. The people were sad. The moral of the story is: always learn how to use a tool before you try to fix a problem.

Once upon a time, there was a fish who lived in a reef. The fish was blue and loved to swim fast. Every day, the fish would rush around the reef, playing with his friends. One day, the fish saw a big, tired shark. The shark was sad because he was too slow to play with the fish. The fish wanted to help the tired shark feel better. The fish had an idea. He told all his friends to slow down and play with the tired shark. They all swam slowly and had fun together. The shark was happy, and the reef was a better place for everyone.

Once there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom liked to talk to his friend, a dog named Max. They played on the ground every day. One day, Tom said to Max, "I am sad. I lost my toy." Max wanted to help. "Let's find your toy," he said. They looked on the ground for the toy. They looked near the trees and by the flowers. They found the toy on the ground by a big rock. Tom was happy. "Thank you, Max!" he said. They played with the toy all day.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tim and Tom. Tim liked to play and have fun, while Tom liked to work and kept busy. One day, they were arguing over which activity they should do. Tim wanted to play, while Tom wanted to work. "Let's fight and decide," said Tim. Tom grabbed a stick, and so did Tim. They went to the ground to fight. But soon, Tim got bored. He started to dance and sing, and laughed instead of fighting. Tom couldn't believe it. He got angry and threw his stick away. "If you don't want to work and fight, then why are you even here?" said Tom. Tim just smiled. "Sometimes the best thing about a fight is the fun that can follow."

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a blue kayak. One day, he saw a race for kids with kayaks. Tim wanted to win the race. On the day of the race, all the kids were ready. They got in their kayaks. Tim saw his friend, Lily. Lily said, "Let's have fun and try to win!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's do it!" The race started, and they paddled fast. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, bitter lemon fell from the sky into the water. It made the water taste funny. All the kids stopped paddling to look at the lemon. Tim and Lily looked at each other and laughed. They paddled faster and passed the other kids. In the end, Tim and Lily won the race together. They were so happy and shared the big prize.

One day, a famous lady wanted to provide pretty jewelry to her friends. She had many necklaces, rings, and bracelets. The lady was very happy to share her jewelry with her friends. But, a naughty dog took the jewelry away. The lady was sad and said, "Oh no! My jewelry is gone. What will I do now?" Her friends wanted to help her. They looked for the dog everywhere. Then, they found the dog. The dog was not naughty at all! The dog wanted to give the jewelry to a sad girl who had none. The famous lady was happy and said, "That is very kind, dog. Let's all provide jewelry to those who need it." And they all became good friends.
