Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Anna and the Basket

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play outside in the sun. They have a big ball and a small bike. They share their toys and have fun. One day, they see a big hole in the earth. They wonder what is inside. They go near the hole and look. They see something shiny and round. It looks like a big coin. "Wow, what is that?" Tom asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is a treasure," Anna says. They want to touch it, but the hole is too deep. They need something to reach it. They look around and see a long stick. Tom picks up the stick and tries to poke the shiny thing. He misses and drops the stick in the hole. "Oh no, now we can't get it back," Anna says. They are sad and sit on the grass. They don't know what to do. They think the shiny thing is very special and they want to have it. Then, they hear a voice from the hole. It is a small and squeaky voice. It says, "Hello, can you help me? I am stuck in this hole and I can't get out. I am not a coin, I am a button. I fell from a spaceship and I need to go back to my planet." Tom and Anna are surprised and scared. They look at each other and don't know what to say. They have never heard a talking button before. They think it is not ordinary. "Who are you?" Tom asks. "I am Zee, a space button. I can fly and talk and do many things. But I need to find my spaceship. It is somewhere on this earth. Can you help me?" Zee says. Tom and Anna are curious and excited. They want to help Zee and see his spaceship. They think it is very cool. "OK, we will help you. But how can we get you out of the hole?" Anna asks. Zee says, "You can use your bike. It has a seat and a basket. You can put me in the basket and ride to the hill. There, you will see a big rock. Behind the rock, there is my spaceship. It is hidden and waiting for me. Please hurry, I don't have much time." Tom and Anna agree and run to get their bike. They put Zee in the basket and seat on the bike. They pedal fast and go to the hill. They find the rock and the spaceship. It is shiny and round and has many buttons. Zee says, "Thank you, you are very kind and brave. You are my friends. I will never forget you. Goodbye." He jumps out of the basket and flies to the spaceship. He opens a door and goes inside. The spaceship makes a loud noise and flies away. Tom and Anna wave and watch. They are happy and amazed. They have seen a space button and a spaceship. They think it is not ordinary. They think it is the best day ever.

One day, a girl named Lily and a boy named Tom went to the lake. They saw a boat. They wanted to enjoy the boat ride. So, they got in the boat and started to row. As they rowed, a fish jumped out of the water. The fish said, "Hello, I am a spicy fish. Do you want to be friends?" Lily and Tom said, "Yes, let's be friends." But the spicy fish was not a good friend. It made the water hot. Lily and Tom did not enjoy the boat ride. They rowed back to the shore and left the spicy fish in the lake. The boat ride was not fun, and they were sad.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Jim and Sam. Jim was a very intelligent monkey who loved to explore. One day, Jim and Sam were walking through the woods when they stumbled upon a large closet. Excited, Jim grabbed the doorknob and said to Sam, “Let’s see what’s inside.” “No, Jim!” shouted Sam. “It might be too dangerous. I don’t think we should have a look.” But Jim was too curious, so he opened the door anyway. Inside, there were shiny stars, rainbows and lots of sweets. Jim smiled and said, “Wow! We have to get some of these for ourselves!” Sam, feeling less scared, got out a big bag and both the friends began to collect the sweets. Suddenly, Jim spotted something else in the closet. It was a sparkling crown. With his intelligence, he understood that it must have belonged to a king or a queen. Jim said to Sam, “We should keep this crown safe and secret. No one else must know that we have it.” Sam agreed, and they both quickly hid the crown back inside the closet before setting off again to explore the woods!
