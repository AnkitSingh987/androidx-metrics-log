Category: Intermediate
Title: The Unique Rhinoceros and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a unique rhinoceros. His name was Rino. He was different because he had a very long horn on his nose. All the other animals liked Rino, but they did not have a long horn like him. Rino felt special. One day, Rino found a big open space in the forest. It was a nice place to lie down and rest. Rino told all his friends about the open space. They all wanted to see it and lie down there too. Rino knew he had found something great for everyone. The next day, all the animals went to the open space. They all took turns lying down and resting. They were happy and thanked Rino for finding the special place. Rino was very proud of himself. In the end, Rino's unique horn and big heart brought happiness to all his friends in the forest.

One day, a thin cat named Tom found a big rock in his yard. He saw a horn on top of the rock. Tom was curious and wanted to play with the horn. Tom said to his friend, a dog named Max, "Look at the horn on the rock! Let's play with it!" Max wagged his tail and agreed. They both walked to the rock and tried to take the horn off the top. Suddenly, the rock started to rock back and forth. Tom and Max were scared, but they did not run away. A friendly frog jumped off the rock and said, "Hi! I'm Fred, the frog. I live under this rock. Do you want to play with me?" Tom and Max smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They all played with the horn and had a fun day. The thin cat, the dog, and the frog became good friends and played together every day.

Lily and Ben wanted to buy a gift for Mom. They had some coins in their piggy bank. They walked to the store with Dad. It was cold and windy. They shivered in their coats. At the store, they saw many things. They saw toys, books, flowers, and candy. They wanted to find something nice for Mom. They looked at the prices. Some things were too much. They only had a little money. They felt anxious. They did not want to go home empty-handed. Then they saw a scarf. It was soft and warm. It had Mom's favorite colors. They checked the price. It was just enough for their coins. They smiled and hugged. They took the scarf to the cashier. They paid with their coins. They thanked the cashier. They wrapped the scarf in paper. They ran back to Dad. They showed him their gift. He was proud of them. He said Mom would love it. They nodded and grinned. They stopped shivering. They felt happy. They could not wait to give Mom the gift. They hoped she would like it. They knew they did their best.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty butterfly. She lived in a big garden with lots of flowers and grass. She liked to play with her friends, the birds and the bees. One day, the pretty butterfly saw her friends prepare a party. They were very happy. They put some food on the grass for everyone to eat. The butterfly wanted to help, so she flew around the garden to find more food. The pretty butterfly found some yummy nectar from the flowers. She brought it to the grass where the party was. All her friends were happy, and they had a great time at the party. They played and ate together on the pretty grass. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to help his owner, a kind lady named Sue. They were best friends and did everything together. One day, Sue decided to cook a big meal for her friends. Max wanted to help, so he looked around for something to use. He found a big spoon, which he thought looked like a weapon. Max picked up the spoon with his mouth and brought it to Sue. Sue laughed and thanked Max for his help. She used the spoon to cook a delicious meal for her friends. Max wagged his tail and felt proud to help his best friend. They had a fun day together, and Max knew he was the most loyal dog in the world.
