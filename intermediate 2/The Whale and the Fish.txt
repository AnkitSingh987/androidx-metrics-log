Category: Intermediate
Title: The Whale and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big whale. The whale was very playful. He liked to swim and jump in the water. One day, the whale was feeling tired. He thought, "I need to rest." The whale found a quiet spot to rest. While he was resting, he heard a soft sound. The sound was coming from a small fish. The small fish was lost and needed help. The playful whale wanted to help the small fish. The whale and the small fish swam together. They had fun and played in the water. Then, they found the small fish's family. The small fish was happy. The whale was happy too. But, the small fish was not a fish! It was a baby dolphin. The whale had made a new friend. They played and swam together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to fix things. One day, he found a deep hole in his yard. He did not know what was in the hole, but he wanted to fix it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I fix the deep hole?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim got a big bag of dirt and a shovel. He put the dirt in the hole until it was not deep anymore. After Tim fixed the hole, he found a letter on the ground. The letter was from a bug who lived in the hole. The bug said, "Thank you for fixing my home. You are a good friend." Tim was happy he could help the bug and make a new friend.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to decorate. She had lots of pretty things to choose from. Today was special because she found a navy blue ribbon. It was the perfect color for her project. The girl got to work right away. She put the navy ribbon all around her room. It made it look so pretty and comfortable! Everywhere she looked, she saw the bright blue decorations. When she was finished, she stepped back and admired her work. She was so happy with how it looked! She felt so special and proud. The girl smiled and decided to go tell her mom. She wanted her to see how beautiful her room was. She was so excited, she ran all the way there! When the mom saw the room, she was so amazed. She said, "My little girl is so clever! This room looks so wonderful!" The girl smiled so big. She was so happy to hear her mom's compliments. And that is how the little girl decorated her room with the navy ribbon. She was so proud of herself and was having so much fun!

One day, a gray cat named Tom found a long rope. Tom had a fun idea. He went to his friend, a dog named Max, and said, "Let's play with this rope! We can jump and run with it!" Max was happy and agreed to play with Tom. As they played with the rope, they saw a big tree. Tom suggested, "Let's tie the rope to the tree and swing!" Max liked the idea, so they tied the rope to a strong branch. When they started to swing, something unexpected happened. The branch broke, and they fell on a soft pile of leaves. They were surprised but not hurt. They laughed and played in the leaves. Even though their plan didn't work, they still had a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, a little rabbit and a small squirrel were very good friends. They loved to play together. One day, they found a gentle bird who could not fly. The bird was sad. The rabbit said, "Let's help the bird fly again." The squirrel agreed. They thought and thought, and then they had an idea. They would use a bow to shoot the bird up in the sky. The rabbit held the bow and the squirrel put the gentle bird on it. They counted, "One, two, three!" and then the rabbit let go. The bird went up and up, and then it started to fly! "Thank you, friends!" the bird said. The rabbit and the squirrel were very happy. They all played together in the big forest, and they were the best of friends.
