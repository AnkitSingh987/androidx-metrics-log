Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bunny and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, in a big, dull forest, there lived a little bunny. The little bunny loved to play with his friends, but one day, his friends had to leave. They went to a new forest far away. The little bunny was sad and missed his friends. One day, the little bunny went to look for his friends. He hopped and hopped, but he could not find them. He was very tired, so he sat under a tree to rest. As he sat, he heard a small sound. It was a tiny squirrel. The squirrel was sad too, because he had no friends. The little bunny and the tiny squirrel started to play together. They had so much fun! After a while, the little bunny's friends came back. They had not left forever, they just went on a trip. The result was that the little bunny now had more friends to play with. The little bunny, the tiny squirrel, and the other friends played together every day, and they were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a frog. This frog was not like other frogs. He was tough. He lived in a big pond with his friends. They liked to play and swim all day. One day, the tough frog saw a line of ants. The ants were walking in a straight line. They were carrying food. The frog wanted to be like the ants. He wanted to order his friends to do things too. So, the tough frog told his friends to line up. They listened to him because he was tough. They walked in a line, just like the ants. They had fun playing follow the leader. And from that day on, the frog and his friends would play this game and have lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a group of friends who wanted to play a game. They were all very ready. So, they decided to pay for the game. First, they got a bag of coins. Then, they shared them into three even groups. Everyone had the same amount of coins. Next, they chose a game that they wanted to play. They paid with the coins to buy the game. When the game was ready, they all started to play. They had lots of fun playing the game together. They smiled and laughed a lot! At the end of the day, they were all very happy. They had paid for fun and were very glad that they had done it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to wander in the big park near her house. One day, she saw a small, fearful dog. The dog had a dirty rag in its mouth. Lily wanted to help the dog. Lily said, "Hi dog! Why are you fearful? Can I help you?" The dog looked at her and wagged its tail. It dropped the rag on the ground. Lily picked up the rag and saw that it was a map. Lily and the dog followed the map. They walked and walked until they found a big, pretty house. Inside the house, there was a kind lady. She said, "Thank you for finding my dog and my map! You are very nice." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome!" The lady gave Lily a big hug, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a white rat. He was sitting in his cage and listening to all the sounds outside. One day, he heard a strange sound that he did not recognize. He was curious, so he tried to repeat the sound. Sure enough, he was able to make the same sound! The rat was so proud of himself. He hugged his cage and started to repeat the sound a few more times. Suddenly, a big wolf appeared at the window and heard the rat’s sound. The wolf started to laugh. The rat asked the wolf, “Why do you laugh?” The wolf replied, “It’s not wise to repeat something that you don’t understand. You can end up in trouble if you don’t know what you are repeating.” The rat thought about the wolf’s words, and he understood the moral of the story. From then on, he made sure to only say or do things that he knew and understood.
