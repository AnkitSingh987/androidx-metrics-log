Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Jolly Cat and the Ball

One day, a jolly cat named Max went for a walk. He was very happy because it was a sunny day. Max liked to play outside and feel the warm sun on his fur. As Max walked, he saw a big red ball. This made him very excite. Max ran to the ball and used his waist to push it. The ball rolled and Max chased it, laughing. Max played with the ball all day. He was very tired but happy. Max went home to his warm bed, ready for more fun the next day.

One day, a little girl named Jane went for a walk in the park. She saw a dark club house under a big tree. Jane felt excited and wanted to see what was inside. As she walked closer, she heard a voice from inside the club house. A boy named Tom said, "Hi! Do you want to come in and play?" Jane said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" So, she went into the dark club house. Inside, they played with toys and had fun. But when Jane tried to leave, she did not see a toy on the floor. She slipped on the toy and fell down. Tom quickly helped her up and said, "Be careful! We should put the toys away so we don't slip again." Jane agreed, and they both cleaned up the club house together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to collect pretty pictures. She had a big box full of them. Lily had a friend named Sam who also loved to collect pictures. They both liked to show each other what they found. One day, Lily and Sam became competitive. They both wanted to have the most pictures. They started to argue about who had more. They were not happy anymore. They did not want to share their pictures with each other. Then, Lily's mom told them a story about two friends who shared everything. She said that when friends share, they are happier. Lily and Sam learned that it is better to share and have fun together. They stopped being competitive and started to share their pictures again. They were happy friends once more.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy bear named Ben. Lily and Ben were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Lily found a mirror. She looked at the mirror and saw Ben in it. "Look, Ben!" she said, "I can see you in the mirror!" Ben looked at the mirror too. He saw Lily and himself. They both thought it was fun. Lily wanted to share the mirror with her friend, Sam. Sam was a harmless bug who lived in the garden. Lily showed the mirror to Sam. "Look, Sam!" she said, "You can see yourself in the mirror!" Sam looked at the mirror and saw himself. He was very happy to share the mirror with Lily and Ben. They all played together and had a great day.

Tom was a cat who liked to search for meat. He did not like to eat dry food or milk. He wanted to eat birds or mice or fish. But it was hard to find meat in the city. There were many cars and dogs and people who scared him. One day, Tom saw a big truck with a picture of a cow on it. He thought there might be meat inside. He followed the truck to a distant place where there were many buildings and noises. He saw the truck stop and open its back. He smelled meat. He was very hungry. Tom ran to the truck and jumped inside. He saw many boxes and bags of meat. He was very happy. He bit one of the bags and tore it open. He tasted meat. He was very satisfied. But then he heard a loud voice. It was a man who worked for the truck. He saw Tom and was very angry. He shouted and grabbed a stick. He hit Tom with the stick. He hurt Tom. He threw Tom out of the truck. He closed the truck and drove away. Tom was very sad and scared. He had meat in his mouth, but he also had blood and pain. He ran away from the distant place. He wished he had stayed at home. He learned that searching for meat was not always good. He decided to eat dry food and milk from now on. He hoped to find a kind friend who would give him some.
