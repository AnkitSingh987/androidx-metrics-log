Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and the Rock

Once there was a man who liked to spend time near the beach. He had a big muscle in his arm, so he could do lots of things. He was very flexible. One day, he was trying to pick up a large rock. He used his muscle, but it wasn't strong enough. He felt his arm getting sore and eventually he had to give up and put the rock back on the ground. He was worried that he had used his muscle too much and he was sad that he couldn't move the rock. He tried to stay flexible, but his sore muscles wouldn't let him. He didn't know what to do, so he had to spend more and more time resting until his arm felt better. In the end, he had to leave the beach without having moved the rock.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim felt miserable one day because he could not find his favorite toy. He looked everywhere in his room but could not find it. His mom said, "Tim, why don't you fold your clothes and put them away? Maybe you will find your toy." So, Tim started to fold his clothes. As he was folding, he found his toy hiding under a shirt. He was so happy! Later, Tim was hungry and went to the fridge. He saw a big cake inside. He wanted to eat it, but he remembered how folding his clothes helped him find his toy. So, he closed the fridge and went to ask his mom if he could have a piece of cake. His mom said, "Yes, you can have a piece because you were a good boy and folded your clothes." The moral of the story is that doing what you are told can lead to good things.

One day, a big cat and a small dog met in the park. They both wanted to play with the same ball. The cat said, "Let's trade. You give me the ball, and I'll give you my tight hat." The dog thought about it and said, "Okay, let's trade." They traded, and the dog wore the tight hat. The hat was so tight that it made the dog's head hurt. The dog did not like the trade, and he started to cry. The big cat heard the dog cry and felt bad. The cat said, "I'm sorry I disturbed you. Let's trade back." The dog agreed, and they traded back. The dog was happy to have his ball again, and the cat was happy to have his hat back. They both played together in the park, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red house. In the house, there was a big TV. The TV had many fun colors and sounds. A happy family lived in the house. They liked to watch the big TV together. One day, the sun set and it was time for bed. The family turned off the big TV. They said good night to each other. They went to their rooms and got into their beds. The next morning, the sun came up. The family woke up and had breakfast. After they ate, they went to the big TV. They turned it on and watched their favorite show. They laughed and clapped. They were happy to be together in their big, red house.

One day, a tidy otter named Ollie lived near a big river. He loved to play in the water and have fun with his friends. Ollie was very good at making things look nice and clean. Ollie found a big thing that could print. He wanted to print a picture of his friends. He pushed a button and the big thing started to print. But, instead of a picture, it printed a big mess! Ollie was very sad. Then, Ollie had an idea. He took the big mess and made it into a fun game for his friends. They all played and had a great time. Ollie learned that even when things don't go as planned, you can still have fun.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Pete and Paul. They liked to play together in the park every single day. One day, they decided to make some clay figures. Pete made a beautiful clay elephant, but then Paul got angry and threw his clay away. Pete said, "Why are you angry? Let me help you make a new one." But Paul said, "No, it's too late, I don't want to make one anymore." Then Pete had an idea. He said, "Let's send a message to everyone that it's never too late to start over. If you are angry, just take a deep breath and try again!" So Pete and Paul gathered some clay and started to make new figures. They ended up making a lot of beautiful figures. The moral of the story is that anger won't help you make something beautiful, but cooperation and patience will. So next time when you're angry, take a deep breath and send a message of hope and understanding.
