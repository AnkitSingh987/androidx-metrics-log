Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Play

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to play games with his friends. His friends liked Max because he was reliable. He was always there to play with them. One day, Max and his friends wanted to play a game. But they could not agree on what game to play. Some wanted to play ball, and some wanted to play tag. They started to argue, and they could not find a way to part. Max had an idea. He said, "Let's play a new game!" Max told his friends that they could play a game where they all took turns. They would play ball first, and then they would play tag. Everyone liked Max's idea, and they all agreed to play the new game. The friends played the games and had lots of fun. They were happy that Max found a way to part the argument. Max was a reliable friend, and they knew they could always count on him to help them when they needed it. And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Jack and Jill. They were best friends and shared everything. One day, Jack and Jill were playing with a ball. Suddenly, Jack wanted to keep it for himself and so he tried to take it from Jill. This made Jill very mad and she started to quarrel with Jack. She shouted "That's my ball, stop taking it away from me!". Jack then shouted back, "No! It's my ball, I saw it first!". Jill was so angry, she then pushed Jack, who fell to the ground and hurt his leg. He started crying in pain. Jill felt so bad, she ran away and cried by herself. Suddenly, her mom came and saw her crying. Jill's mom knew what Jack and Jill had been fighting about. She hugged Jill and said, "Jill, quarrel is never the answer - the honest thing to do is to share with your friend. That's how friendship grows and both of you will be happy". Jill learned an important lesson that day and she promised to always be a fair and honest friend.

Once upon a time, there was a humble dog named Spot. Spot had a big itch on his back. He tried to scratch it, but he could not reach it. Spot felt sad because the itch would not go away. One day, Spot met a cat named Whiskers. Whiskers saw Spot's sad face and asked, "Why are you sad, Spot?" Spot told Whiskers about his itch that he could not scratch. Whiskers wanted to help Spot feel better. Whiskers used her paw to scratch Spot's itch. Spot felt so happy and started to smile. He said, "Thank you, Whiskers!" Spot and Whiskers became good friends and played together every day. And whenever Spot had an itch, Whiskers was there to help.

Once upon a time, there was a big cookie. The cookie was very sad because it was broken into three pieces. The three pieces were not friends. They thought they were too different from each other. One piece was round, one was square, and the third one was a funny shape. They all thought the other pieces were stupid. One day, a little boy found the broken cookie pieces. He said, "Hey, cookie pieces, why are you not friends? You all taste yummy, and you all come from the same cookie. You should unite and be one big cookie again!" The cookie pieces looked at each other and thought, "Maybe the little boy is right. We all taste yummy, and we all come from the same cookie. Maybe we should be friends and unite." So, the cookie pieces decided to unite and become one big cookie again. They were happy to be friends and no longer thought the other pieces were stupid. The little boy smiled and said, "Now you are a big, yummy cookie again! I am glad you are friends." From that day on, the cookie pieces stayed united and were always happy. They learned that even though they were different shapes, they were still part of the same cookie. And they all lived happily ever after.
