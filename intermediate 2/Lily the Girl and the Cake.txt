Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very happy because today was her birthday. She put on her favorite dress and ran to the kitchen. Her mom and dad were there with a big cake. "Happy birthday, Lily!" they said. Lily saw her little friend, Bunny, sitting on the floor. She knelt down and said, "Bunny, today is my birthday! Let's have fun!" Bunny did not say anything, but Lily knew Bunny was happy too. But then, something unexpected happened. Bunny started to talk! "Happy birthday, Lily!" said Bunny. Lily was very surprised. She never knew that Bunny could talk. From that day on, Lily and Bunny talked and played together every day.

One day, a little cricket was very sad. He felt boring because he had no friends to play with. He walked and walked, looking for a friend to hug and play with. The cricket saw a big cat. He thought, "Maybe the cat will be my friend." He walked up to the cat and said, "Hi, cat! Will you be my friend?" The cat looked at the cricket and smiled. The cricket was happy and thought he found a friend. But the cat was not a nice friend. He hugged the cricket too tight. The cricket tried to get away, but the cat would not let go. In the end, the cricket was not happy, and the cat was not a good friend.

One day, a big dog named Sam was playing in the mud. He loved to jump and roll in the mud. The mud was wet and made him very dirty. Sam was having so much fun. But then, Sam started to feel troubled. He felt like he was getting smaller. He looked at his legs and saw that they were shrinking. Sam was scared and didn't know what to do. Sam ran to his friend, a wise old cat named Max. Max knew what to do. He told Sam to take a bath in the clean water. Sam did as Max said, and soon he was big again. Sam was happy and thanked Max. From that day on, Sam stayed away from the mud.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his ball in the house. His mom told him, "Tim, don't play with the ball inside. It's difficult to fix things if they break." But Tim did not listen. He wanted to keep playing, so he ignored his mom. Tim threw the ball up, and it hit the ceiling. The ceiling had a big light hanging from it. The light started to shake. Tim saw the light shaking, but he did not care. He wanted to keep playing, so he threw the ball again. This time, the ball hit the light very hard. The light fell down and broke into many pieces. Tim's mom was very sad and angry. She told Tim, "Now we have no light, and it will be dark at night." Tim felt bad for not listening to his mom, and he learned that ignoring is not good.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a vendor named Tom. He sold fruits to everyone. Tom was a kind and harmless man. He always smiled and shared his fruits with the children. One day, Tom thought of a plan to increase the fruits he sold. He planted more fruit trees in his garden. Day by day, the trees grew and gave more fruits. Tom was very happy. Soon, everyone in the town came to buy fruits from Tom. They loved his sweet fruits. The more fruits Tom sold, the more he shared with the children. Everyone learned that being kind and sharing can make you happy. And that was the moral of the story.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a round ball. The round ball was very sad and ashamed. It was ashamed because it could not roll fast like the other balls. The other balls would laugh and play, but the round ball would just sit and watch. One day, a kind girl named Sue saw the round ball. Sue knew that the round ball was sad and wanted to help. Sue had an idea. She set the round ball on a small hill. The round ball was scared, but Sue said, "Do not be afraid. I am here with you." As the round ball started to roll down the hill, it went faster and faster. The round ball was so happy! It could roll as fast as the other balls. The other balls saw the round ball and stopped laughing. They all played together, and the round ball was no longer ashamed.
