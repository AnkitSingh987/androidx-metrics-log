Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Baby and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a baby named Tim. Tim was a stubborn baby who did not like to listen to his mom and dad. He would always make a mess and not clean up after himself. One day, Tim's mom told him to put away his toys. But Tim was stubborn and did not want to listen. He kept playing and did not put away his toys. His mom got mad and decided to punish him. She told Tim that he could not play with his toys for the rest of the day. Tim was sad and cried, but he learned his lesson. From that day on, he was not stubborn and always listened to his mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Lily. She loved to draw with her colorful chalk. Every day, she would go outside and draw pretty pictures on the sidewalk. One sunny day, Lily found a big box of chalk. She was so happy! Lily decided to bury the chalk in her backyard, like a treasure. She dug a small hole, put the chalk inside, and covered it with dirt. The next day, Lily went to dig up her chalk treasure. She found the spot where she buried it and started to dig. When she found the box, she was so excited! She took the chalk out and drew a big, beautiful rainbow on the sidewalk for everyone to see.

Tom and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys in the living room. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big yacht. The yacht is blue and white, and it has a sail and a wheel. Tom and Mia pretend they are sailors and go on adventures with the yacht. One day, Mom tells them to tidy up their toys. She says they have to put them in the big box in the corner. Tom and Mia do not want to tidy up. They want to keep playing with the yacht. They say no to Mom. Mom is not happy. She says they have to listen to her and do what she asks. Tom and Mia start to argue. Tom says he wants to play with the yacht more. Mia says she wants to play with the yacht more. They both pull the yacht and try to take it from each other. The yacht breaks. The sail falls off and the wheel comes off. Tom and Mia are sad. They cry. They wish they had listened to Mom and tidied up their toys. Mom hears them crying. She comes to the living room and sees the broken yacht. She feels sorry for Tom and Mia. She hugs them and says it is okay. She says they can fix the yacht together. She says they have to be careful with their toys and share them. She says they have to request things nicely and not fight. Tom and Mia say they are sorry. They say they love Mom and the yacht. They help Mom to fix the yacht and tidy up their toys. They learn their lesson. They are happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a fancy owl sitting on a tree branch. The owl had big eyes and pretty feathers. Tim was very happy to see the owl. Tim said, "Hi, owl! I am Tim. What is your name?" The owl said, "Hello, Tim. I am Ollie the Owl." Tim and Ollie became friends. They liked to talk and play together in the park. One day, Tim wanted to introduce Ollie to his friend, Sally. Tim said, "Sally, this is Ollie the fancy owl. Ollie, this is my friend Sally." Sally and Ollie said hello to each other. They all played together and had a lot of fun.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy vegetables for dinner. Mia was a good helper, so she picked up a big bag of vegetables and put it in the cart. On the way home, Mia was scared. She thought she saw a big monster hiding behind a tree. When they got home, Mia's mom started to cook. She took the vegetables out of the bag and split them into different bowls. Mia was still scared of the monster she saw, so she told her mom. "Mom, I saw a big monster outside!" Her mom looked at her and said, "Don't be scared, Mia. There are no monsters here." Just then, the doorbell rang. Mia and her mom went to see who it was. When they opened the door, they saw a big, green, friendly-looking monster! He was holding the vegetables that Mia's mom had split. The monster said, "Hi, I'm Veggie! I saw you drop some vegetables, so I picked them up for you." Mia and her mom were surprised, but they thanked Veggie. From that day on, Mia was not scared of monsters anymore because she knew they could be friendly too.
