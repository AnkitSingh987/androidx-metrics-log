Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Pig

Lily was a little pig who loved to sing. She sang every day on the farm, but she was very humble. She did not think she was better than the other animals. She just liked to make them happy with her songs. One day, a man came to the farm. He was looking for a star for his big show. He heard Lily singing and he was amazed. He said, "You have a wonderful voice, little pig. Do you want to come with me and perform in front of many people?" Lily was scared and excited. She had never left the farm before. She asked the farmer if she could go. The farmer was kind and proud. He said, "You are a very special pig, Lily. You can go if you want, but remember where you came from and who loves you." Lily thanked the farmer and hugged him. She said goodbye to the other animals and got in the man's car. She was nervous and curious. She wondered what the big show would be like. The man took Lily to a big city. There were many lights and noises and people. Lily felt small and lost. The man took her to a big stage. There were many seats and a big curtain. The man said, "This is where you will perform, little pig. You will sing for all these people and they will clap and cheer for you. You will be a star." Lily looked at the stage and felt afraid. She missed the farm and the farmer and the other animals. She did not want to be a star. She wanted to be a humble pig who sang for her friends. She said to the man, "I'm sorry, but I don't want to perform. I want to go back to the farm. Please take me back." The man was angry and sad. He said, "But you have such a wonderful voice, little pig. You are wasting your talent. You could be famous and rich and happy." Lily said, "I don't need fame or money or happiness. I have all I need on the farm. I have love and peace and joy. Please take me back." The man saw that Lily was sincere and brave. He said, "OK, little pig. I will take you back. But I hope you will sing again someday. You have a gift that should be shared." Lily smiled and thanked the man. She got in his car and he drove her back to the farm. She was happy and relieved. She ran to the farmer and hugged him. She said, "I'm sorry I left, but I'm glad I'm back. I love you and the farm and the other animals. I don't want to be a star. I just want to be a pig who sings." The farmer smiled and hugged her back. He said, "I'm glad you're back, too, Lily. I love you and I'm proud of you. You are a very special pig, but you are also a very wise pig. You know what matters in life. And you can still sing for us anytime you want. We love your songs." Lily was happy and grateful. She sang a song for the farmer and the other animals. They clapped and cheered for her. She was not a star, but she was a performer. And she was a humble pig who loved to sing.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was a very happy cat who loved to play with his friends. One day, Fluffy found a big potato in the garden. He was so excited and wanted to show it to his friends. Fluffy ran to his friend, the dog named Spot. Spot was playing with a ball. Fluffy yelled, "Look, Spot! I found a big potato!" Spot looked at the potato and said, "Wow, that's a big one!" They both laughed and played with the potato in the garden. After playing, Fluffy and Spot were very hungry. They decided to share the potato with their other friends. They found a nice place in the garden and ate the potato together. All the animals were happy and full. The moral of the story is that sharing with friends makes everyone happy.
