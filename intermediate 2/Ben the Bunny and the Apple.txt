Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Apple

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to eat yummy food. One day, he saw a big, red apple high up in a tree. Ben wanted to eat the apple, but he was too small to reach it. Ben asked his friend, Tim the squirrel, for help. Tim said, "I can carry the apple down for you, but you must be careful. If you drop it, it will fall and be gone forever." Ben promised to be careful, so Tim climbed the tree and carried the apple down for him. Ben was so excited to eat the yummy apple, but he forgot to be careful. He dropped the apple and it started to roll away. As it rolled, it went faster and faster. Ben tried to catch it, but he tripped and fell. The apple rolled into a deep, dark hole, and it was gone forever. Ben was sad because he lost the yummy apple and never got to taste it.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to a store with her mom. They were looking for a chair for Sally's room. Sally saw a big, modern chair that was very pretty. She said, "Mom, I want that chair!" Her mom said, "We can save money to buy the chair, Sally. But first, let's go home and clean your room to make space for it." Sally and her mom went home and cleaned her room. They put away all her toys and made her bed. Sally was excited about the new chair. She said, "I can't wait to sit on the chair and read my books!" A few days later, Sally's mom took her back to the store. They had saved enough money to buy the modern chair. Sally was so happy when they brought the chair home. She sat on it and read her favorite book. The chair was perfect for her room, and she loved it very much.

Alice was ready to eat a grape, but her mom told her to delay. She started to get frustrated. She wanted to eat the grape now! She tried to explain to her mom that she was ready. But her mom shook her head and said, "no, we have to wait". Alice pouted and asked why. Her mom said that eating the grape too early might give Alice a tummy ache. Alice knew that wasn't fun, so she reluctantly agreed to delay eating the grape. A few minutes later, her mom said it was time for the grape. Alice was so excited, she couldn't wait to eat it. When she finally had the grape in her hand, she was smiling from ear to ear!

Once upon a time, there was a big lawn. On the lawn, there were two friends, a dog and a cat. The dog loved to run and play on the lawn. The cat liked to watch the dog play. One day, the cat saw the dog find a big, red ball. The dog was very happy. The cat felt jealous. The cat wanted a ball too. The cat looked and looked but could not find a ball. The next day, the dog saw the cat was sad. The dog wanted to help the cat. So, the dog went and found another big, red ball. The dog gave the ball to the cat. The cat was very happy. Now, the dog and the cat played together on the lawn with their big, red balls.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a fun time playing on the swings and slides. After playing, they sat down to eat their lunch. Tim's mom gave him a sandwich with jam. "Mom, I don't like jam," Tim complained. But his mom said, "Try it, it might be interesting." Tim took a bite of the sandwich and to his surprise, it was interesting! It was not just any jam, it was magic jam. When he ate it, he could talk to the animals in the park. A bird flew down and said, "Hi Tim! Do you want to play?" Tim was so happy and played with the bird and other animals. From that day on, Tim never complained about jam again.
