Title: Mona and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a very special little girl. Her name was Mona and she was very gifted. She loved to play and have fun. One day, Mona and her friends went to the park to play together. They had so much fun! They played on the swings and climbed on the monkey bars. They even made a made a giant sandcastle. After a while, Mona started to get really tired. So her friends all gave her a big hug and they went home. Mona was so happy. She had so much fun playing with her friends. Mona loves to play and she knows that her giftedness will bring her lots of joy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a pretty dress that she loved very much. Mia wanted to wear her dress to her friend's birthday party. On the way to the party, Mia saw a big puddle. She wanted to jump in it, but she was careful not to get her dress dirty. But then, her friend Tom came running and splashed mud on her dress. Mia was very sad. Mia's mom told her to believe that everything would be okay. She helped Mia clean her dress before the party. Mia learned that being careful is good, but accidents happen, and it's important to believe that things can be fixed.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to skip and play all day. He had a clean room with a crib where he slept at night. One day, Tim saw a little bird outside his window. The bird was so pretty and it could skip too! Tim wanted to play with the bird, so he went outside. He skipped and the bird skipped too. They had so much fun playing together. When it was time to go to bed, Tim went back to his clean room. He looked at his crib and had an idea. He opened the window and the little bird flew in. Tim and the bird slept in the crib together, happy and tired from all their skipping.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big march. Many people came to march. They had a leader, and his name was Tom. Tom was a tall man with a big voice. He wanted everyone to be happy. One day, a small boy named Tim wanted to join the march. Tim was very excited to march with Tom and the people. But, some people in the march were angry. They did not want Tim to march with them because he was too small. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. He went to Tom and told him about the angry people. Tom was not happy to hear this. He told the angry people that everyone can march, even Tim. The angry people listened to Tom and let Tim join the march. Everyone was happy again, and they all marched together with big smiles on their faces.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play together. Sometimes they play with dolls, sometimes they play with cars, sometimes they play with books. They are very intelligent. They know many things. One day, they find a big box in the garage. They open it and see many old things. They see clothes, shoes, hats, and toys. They also see a picture of a lady. She has long, curly hair and a pretty dress. She looks like their grandma. "Who is this lady?" Anna asks. "That is our great-grandma," Ben says. "She was grandma's mom. She died a long time ago." "Where is she now?" Anna asks. "She is in heaven," Ben says. "She is with God and the angels. They are happy and peaceful." "Can we talk to her?" Anna asks. "Yes, we can," Ben says. "We can pray to her. We can say hello and tell her we love her. We can also ask her to bless us and help us." "How do we pray?" Anna asks. "We close our eyes and fold our hands," Ben says. "We say 'Dear God and great-grandma, thank you for everything. We are your children. We love you and miss you. Please watch over us and guide us. Amen.'" Anna and Ben do what Ben says. They close their eyes and fold their hands. They pray to God and their great-grandma. They feel warm and calm in their hearts. They smile and hug each other. They put the picture back in the box. They take some toys and clothes and go to their room. They play and have fun. They are happy and grateful. They are intelligent and loving.
