Title: The Red Car and the Blue

Once upon a time, there was a little red car. The car loved to go fast. One day, the car saw a new friend. The friend was a big blue car. The little red car wanted to welcome the big blue car. The big blue car was very happy. They played together all day. The little red car was very alert. The big blue car was very strong. They raced down the road, laughing and having fun. At the end of the day, the little red car and the big blue car were tired. They said goodbye and went home. The little red car was happy to have a new friend. The big blue car was happy too. They knew they would play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He was an independent kid who liked to play on his own. One day, he went to the park to play. While at the park, Tom saw a zigzag path. He decided to walk on it. As he walked, he met a big dog. The dog looked at Tom and said, "Do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "No, I want to play alone." The dog felt sad and walked away. Tom continued walking on the zigzag path. Soon, it started to get dark, and Tom felt scared. He began to weep and called out for the dog, but the dog was gone. Tom was all alone in the dark, and he could not find his way home.

Once upon a time there was a wet rabbit. He was very hungry so he decided to go out and gather some food. He hopped through the woods and found some tasty meat that he could eat. He hopped back to his little burrow and eagerly gathered the meat up in his furry paws. The rabbit took a big bite and the meat was so yummy. He ate the whole thing in seconds. After he finished he realized he was still hungry. He hopped out of his burrow once more and began to gather more food. This time he found some juicy berries. He gathered them up and hopped back, eating them all with delight. The rabbit was so pleased, he filled his tummy and had plenty of energy for the day. He hopped back into his little burrow, safe and happy for the night. And the end!

Once upon a time there was a truck that could drive fast. Its name was True and it was very powerful. True loved to drive around and explore the world. Everywhere it went, adults and children were amazed. One day, True needed to drive to the store to get special tools, but it was too far away. Suddenly, a small three year old boy came up and said, "Let me help!" True thought to itself, "How can this little boy help me?" But he decided to let him try. The boy hopped in and said, "I'll help you drive!" True was skeptical, but it allowed him to steer the wheel. The boy was surprisingly powerful, and he lead True all the way to the store. When they arrived, they were both happy. True was so impressed with the little boy's help that it take the time to give him a special gift. The boy who drove True was very happy. He said "Thank you, True! I will never forget your kindness." And he never did.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play together. One day, they go to the market with their moms. The market has many things to see and buy. Anna and Ben see a stall with toys. They want to play with the toys. "Mom, can I borrow a toy?" Anna asks her mom. "Sure, honey, but you have to give it back when we leave, okay?" her mom says. "Mom, can I borrow a toy too?" Ben asks his mom. "No, Ben, you have to buy a toy if you want one. We don't borrow things from strangers," his mom says. Anna picks a modest toy. It is a small car. She likes the color and the shape. She plays with it happily. Ben picks a big toy. It is a robot. He likes the sound and the light. He plays with it loudly. "Ben, can I see your toy?" Anna asks Ben. "No, Anna, this is my toy. You have a boring toy. Go away," Ben says. Anna feels sad. She goes back to her mom. Her mom hugs her and tells her that her toy is nice and that she is a good girl for borrowing and not taking. "Anna, can I see your toy?" Ben asks Anna. "Why do you want to see my toy? You said it was boring," Anna says. "Because my toy is broken. It stopped making sound and light. I want to play with your toy," Ben says. Anna thinks for a moment. She remembers that Ben was mean to her. She wants to say no. But she also remembers that her mom taught her to share and be kind. She decides to let Ben see her toy. "Okay, Ben, you can see my toy. But you have to be careful and give it back when we leave, okay?" Anna says. "Okay, Anna, thank you. You are a nice friend," Ben says. Anna and Ben play with the toy together. They have fun. They learn that borrowing is better than taking, and that modest is better than greedy. They also learn to be polite and generous to each other. Their moms are proud of them. They buy them ice cream as a treat. Anna and Ben smile and hug. They are happy.
