Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Door

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big aeroplane in the sky. Tim really wanted to spend time with the aeroplane. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the aeroplane?" His mom said, "No, Tim. The aeroplane is too far away. We can't play with it." But Tim was very persistent. He kept asking his mom to let him play with the aeroplane. Then, something unexpected happened. The big aeroplane in the sky started to get smaller and smaller. It came down and landed in the park. The door opened, and a little girl came out. She said, "Hi, I'm Amy. My dad made this aeroplane just for me to play with. Do you want to play with us?" Tim was so happy! He and Amy spent the whole day playing with the aeroplane in the park. Tim learned that being persistent can sometimes make your dreams come true.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. It was an icy cold day, so he wore his big coat and hat. As he walked, he liked to listen to the sounds around him. He heard birds singing and dogs barking. Tim saw his friend, Sue, playing in the park. "Hi, Sue!" he said. "Do you want to play with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played on the swings and the slide, laughing and having fun. As they played, Tim saw an ashtray on a bench. He didn't know what it was, so he asked Sue. "That's an ashtray," she said. "People put their yucky stuff in there." Tim nodded and said, "We should not touch it." They continued to play and enjoy the icy day, listening to the sounds around them.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a chubby bird named Bobo. Bobo had a small nest that he made with sticks and leaves. He loved his nest very much. One day, while Bobo was flying, he saw a long, shiny thing on the ground. "What is this?" he asked his friend, the squirrel. The squirrel said, "It's a ribbon. You can pull it to make it longer." Bobo thought it was pretty, so he pulled the ribbon and took it to his nest. Bobo put the ribbon in his nest, but it was too long. So, he asked his friend, the squirrel, to help him. They both pulled the ribbon until it was the right size for the nest. Now, Bobo's nest was even prettier with the shiny ribbon. Bobo said, "Thank you, squirrel, for helping me pull the ribbon. My nest is very pretty now." The squirrel was happy to help, and they played together in the big tree for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. In the field, there was a lot of wheat. The wheat was tall and yellow. The wheat was very old. It was ancient wheat. One day, a small cat came to the field. The cat was very tired. The cat saw the wheat and thought it would be a nice place to rest. So, the cat went to the wheat and sat down. The cat was very happy to sit in the ancient wheat. As the cat sat in the wheat, a little bird flew by. The bird saw the cat and said, "Hello, cat! Do you like sitting in the ancient wheat?" The cat smiled and said, "Yes, I love it here. It is very nice and cozy." The bird and the cat became friends and played in the wheat field every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to travel with her mom and dad. One day, they went to a far away land. Lily was very happy. In this land, they found a big cocoa tree. Lily and her mom picked cocoa from the tree. They wanted to make a yummy drink. Lily's friend, Tim, saw them and he was envious. He wanted cocoa too. Tim asked if he could have some cocoa. Lily said, "Yes, you can have some." They all made the cocoa drink together. They drank it and it was very good. In the end, they all were happy and not envious anymore.
