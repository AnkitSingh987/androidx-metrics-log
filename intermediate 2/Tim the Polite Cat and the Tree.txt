Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Polite Cat and the Tree

One day, a polite cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big green thing. It was a cactus. Tim thought the cactus was a tree. He wanted to say "hello" to the tree. "Hello, tree!" said Tim. The cactus did not talk back. Tim was sad. He wanted to have a friend. Then, something unexpected happened. The cactus began to shake. It started to reveal a face! The cactus was not a tree, but a new friend for Tim. "Hello, Tim!" said the cactus. Tim was happy. Now he had a friend to talk to.

Once upon a time, there was a tired little bear. He had worked so hard all day and now he needed to relax. He looked for the perfect spot and finally he found it - a cozy bed, high up in a tree. But when he tried to lay down, he noticed something - the bed was too small for him! The bear was very frustrated, but then he heard an old wise owl nearby. “You have worked hard today,” said the owl. “But to rest you must find a bed that is the right size.” The bear thanked the owl and set off to look for the right bed. When he finally found it, he was so happy - and perfect size! He flopped onto it and found the best sleep he had ever had. The moral of the story is that even when we are tired, if we take the time to find the right thing, it can bring us the rest and relaxation that we need.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy he loved very much. The toy was a small car. Tim took the car everywhere he went. He felt safe with his car. One day, Tim went to play with his friends. They all wanted to play with Tim's car. But Tim didn't want to share. He wanted to keep his car safe. His friends were sad and didn't want to play with Tim anymore. Tim's mom saw what happened. She told Tim that sharing is a good thing. Tim thought about it and decided to share his car with his friends. They all played together and had a lot of fun. Tim learned the value of sharing and being a good friend.

Once upon a time there was a black box. It was very heavy. Whenever someone wanted to know how heavy it was, they had to measure it. Everyone wanted to know how heavy it was, so every day different people tried to measure it. They would push and pull it around and use a ruler to try and figure out the measurement. Everyone tried their best, but no one could measure the black box. Then one day, a small 3-year-old girl came up to the box. She said “I can measure it!” Everyone laughed at her because she was so small. But the little girl was sure she could do it. She found some friends to help her. Together, they all pushed and pulled the box. They used the ruler to measure and, eventually, they discovered how heavy the black box really was! Everyone was so surprised that the little girl and her friends had solved the mystery of the heavy black box. From then on, they all agreed that the little girl was a great measurer!

One day, a woman named Sue went for a walk. She saw a frog jump up and down. Sue wanted to jump too, but she couldn't jump as high as the frog. She felt jealous of the frog's big jumps. Sue said to the frog, "I wish I could jump like you." The frog looked at Sue and said, "I can help you jump high, but you need to help me with something first." Sue agreed to help the frog. Together, they went to a big tree. The frog asked Sue to help him climb the tree to get a special leaf. Sue helped the frog, and he got the leaf. Then, the frog gave the leaf to Sue and said, "Eat this, and you will jump high." Sue ate the leaf, and suddenly, she could jump very high! Now, Sue and the frog could jump together, and they became good friends.
