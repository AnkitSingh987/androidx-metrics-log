Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a gift for making people happy. Lucy loved to serve her friends and family. She was always there to help when someone was sad or helpless. One day, Lucy saw a sad cat in her yard. The cat looked helpless. Lucy said, "Hi, cat! I want to serve you. Let me help you." The cat looked at Lucy and meowed. Lucy took the cat inside her house and gave it some food and water. The cat was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lucy, for your gift of help." Lucy smiled and said, "You are welcome, cat. I am here to serve my friends." The cat and Lucy became the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who was scared. The bird was scared because it could not hang on the tree like the other birds. The little bird tried and tried, but it just could not hang. One day, the little bird saw a big tree in nature. The little bird thought, "Maybe I can hang on this big tree." The little bird went to the big tree and tried to hang. But the big tree had a mean cat. The mean cat did not like the little bird. The little bird was very scared of the mean cat. But the little bird wanted to hang on the tree so much. So, the little bird asked the other birds for help. The other birds came and chased the mean cat away. Now, the little bird was not scared anymore. The little bird learned how to hang on the tree with the help of the other birds. And they all lived happily.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his football. He was very happy when he played with it. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his football. As Tim played, a big dog came running. The dog wanted to play too! The dog took Tim's football in its mouth and ran away. Tim was sad and yelled, "Stop!" But the dog did not stop. Tim chased the dog to get his football back. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog found another football in the park. The dog dropped Tim's football and took the new one. Tim got his football back and was happy again. Now, Tim and the dog had their own footballs to play with. They played together and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy goose. He lived near a big slide in the park. The goose did not like when kids came to play on the slide. One day, a little boy came to the park. He saw the slide and wanted to play. The grumpy goose saw the boy and said, "No slide for you!" The little boy was sad, but he had an idea. He found some bread and gave it to the grumpy goose. The goose ate the bread and started to feel happy. The boy and the goose played on the slide together all day. From that day on, the grumpy goose was not grumpy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little cat named Lily. She lived in a small house with her best friend, a girl named Emma. Every day, they played together and had lots of fun. One day, Emma's mom made a yummy cake. She put cream on the top, and it looked so good. Emma and Lily wanted to share the cake. But they didn't know how to cut it. So, they decided to ask their friend, a wise old owl, for help. "Please, Mr. Owl, help us cut the cake," said Emma. "We want to share it." The owl thought for a moment and then said, "Why don't you both eat the cake together, at the same time? That way, you can share it, and no one has to cut it." Emma and Lily liked this idea. They were ready to eat the cake when something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse jumped out of the cake! Emma, Lily, and the wise old owl were very surprised. The mouse said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to scare you. I just wanted a little taste of the cream." They all laughed and decided to share the cake with the mouse too. They all settled down and enjoyed the cake together, making a new friend in the process.
