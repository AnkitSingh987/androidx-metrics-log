Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, he found a big, dull stick in his yard. He picked it up and started to play with it. Tim saw his friend, Sam, close by. He ran to Sam and said, "Look at my big stick!" Sam looked at the stick and said, "Wow! It's so big and dull!" They played with the stick all day long. As the sun went down, Tim and Sam were tired. They put the big stick on the ground and said goodbye. Tim went home and told his mom about his fun day. He couldn't wait to play with the stick again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big blue sea. His best friend was a big cow named Moo. Moo lived on the land near the water. One day, Fin and Moo wanted to play together. Moo had an idea. She took some milk from her big body and poured it in the water. The milk made the water white and it was easier for Moo to see Fin. Fin and Moo played and laughed all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The milk turned the water into a big, white cloud. Fin and Moo were surprised. They didn't know what to do. Suddenly, the big, white cloud started to rain. The rain washed away the milk from the water. The sea became blue again. Fin and Moo learned that they could still be friends even if they lived in different places. They were happy and continued to play together every day.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly cat. The cat lived in a small house with a nice lady. The nice lady loved to make pie. One day, the nice lady made a big, sweet pie. The nice lady wanted to send the pie to her friend. She put the pie in a box and gave it to the ugly cat. "Take this pie to my friend's house," she said. The ugly cat nodded and left with the box. The ugly cat walked and walked until it found the friend's house. The friend was very happy to see the pie. She thanked the ugly cat and shared the pie with it. They both enjoyed the sweet pie and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy bear named Ben. Ben was very old and filthy. Tim's mom said, "We should clean Ben." But Tim made a promise to Ben that he would never let him be washed. One day, Tim and Ben went to play outside. They found a big tree with a hole in it. Tim thought it would be fun to hide Ben in the hole. He told Ben, "I value you, so I will come back and get you later." Tim went to play with his friends and forgot about Ben. As the sun went down, Tim remembered his promise to Ben. He ran back to the tree and found Ben still in the hole. Ben was even more filthy now, but Tim was happy to have him back. Tim learned the value of keeping promises and loved Ben even more.

Once upon a time there was a patient bear. He was walking down the path one day when he saw something impressive. The bear was surprised and couldn't help but stop and look. Suddenly, a bird flew by and warned the bear, "Careful! That thing is not safe!" The bear was startled, but he listened to the wise bird and took a step back. The bear was very patient and decided to investigate carefully. He wanted to know what the impressive thing was. After a while, he figured out that it was a pot of honey. He was so excited and began to lick the sweet honey. The bird came back and saw the bear licking the honey. The bird warned the bear again, "Be careful, you could make yourself ill from eating too much. Too much of a good thing isn't good for you!" The bear realized that the bird was right and thanked it for its wise words. From then on, the bear was patient and he never ate too much honey.
