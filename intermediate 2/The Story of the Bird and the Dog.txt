Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Dog

One day, a cat and a dog were playing. The cat told a joke to the dog. The dog laughed so hard. They were having fun. But then, a big bird came. The bird was difficult. It did not like the joke. The bird was mad. The cat said to the bird, "Why do you disturb our fun?" The bird said, "I don't like the joke. It is not nice." The cat and the dog were sad. They did not want the bird to be mad. They wanted to play and be happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog told a new joke. This time, the bird laughed too. The cat, the dog, and the bird were all happy. They played together and had fun. The difficult bird was not mad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a silly little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her friends in the park. One day, Fluffy was playing with her best friend, a little dog named Spot. Spot said, "Fluffy, can you supply me with a fun game to play?" Fluffy thought for a moment and then said, "Let's play 'I Spy' with our eyes!" Spot was excited and agreed to play the game. Fluffy started the game by saying, "I spy with my little eye, something green!" Spot looked around and saw a big tree. "Is it the tree?" Spot asked. "Yes!" Fluffy said, clapping her paws. They played the game for a long time, laughing and having fun. And from that day on, Fluffy and Spot played 'I Spy' whenever they met at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a family who loved to taste new foods. One day, they saw a big, purple fruit at the store. They had never seen a fruit like this before. They were very excited to try it. "Let's taste this purple fruit!" said Mom. "I wonder what it will taste like." Dad, brother, and sister all agreed. They took the fruit home and cut it into pieces. Everyone took a bite at the same time. "Yum!" said Dad. "This purple fruit is sweet and juicy." Mom, brother, and sister all smiled and nodded. They were happy to find a new fruit that the whole family liked. From then on, they always looked for new and fun things to taste together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, Tim was playing with his car, but he needed a cable to make it go fast. He looked and looked, but he could not find the cable. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam had a cable that would make Tim's car go fast. But Sam was selfish and did not want to share his cable with Tim. Tim was sad because he could not make his car go fast. Later, Sam saw how sad Tim was and felt bad. He decided to share his cable with Tim. They both played with the car, and it went very fast. Tim was happy, and Sam learned that it is not good to be selfish. From that day, they never forget to share their toys and have fun together.

One day, John was looking at the ceiling. It was black. Suddenly, he heard a noise outside his window. He turned around and saw a giant bird on the roof. It was singing and hopping. John wondered what the bird was doing. He slowly opened the window and asked, "What are you doing?" The bird replied, "I am matching! Can you see the black shapes on the ceiling? I am jumping from one to the other." John was amazed. He asked, "Can I join you?" The bird said, "Yes, of course! Jump from one shape to the other like I do." John found the game very fun. He was matching with the bird for hours! When it was time to go inside, John thanked the bird for the fun time and ran inside. He would never forget his fun afternoon with the bird matching on the black ceiling.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy had an amazing toy called Zero. Zero could serve her food and play with her. They had a lot of fun together. One day, Amy and Zero went to the park. They played catch and had a picnic. Amy said, "Zero, you are my best friend! Please serve me a sandwich." Zero gave her a sandwich and they ate together. But then, a big dog came to the park. The dog saw Zero and thought it was a toy to play with. The dog took Zero away from Amy. Amy was very sad and cried. She could not find Zero again. The end.
