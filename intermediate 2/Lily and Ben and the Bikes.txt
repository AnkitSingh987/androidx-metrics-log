Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Bikes

Lily and Ben were playing with their bikes in the park. They liked to ride fast and make loud noises. They saw a big hill and wanted to go up. They pedaled hard and felt the wind in their hair. "Look at me, I'm the fastest!" Ben shouted. "No, I'm the fastest!" Lily said. They reached the top of the hill and stopped. They saw a beautiful view of the city and the sky. They smiled and high-fived each other. "Wow, this is so pretty!" Lily said. "Yeah, it is!" Ben said. They heard a loud honk behind them. They turned and saw a big car with a rude man inside. He was angry and red. "Get out of the way, you little brats!" he yelled. "This is not a place for bikes, this is a road for cars!" He honked again and again. He scared Lily and Ben. They felt sad and scared. "Hey, that's not nice!" Ben said. "We're just playing, we're not hurting anyone!" "Yeah, you're rude!" Lily said. "We have wheels too, we can be here!" The rude man did not listen. He drove closer and closer to them. He wanted to make them move. Lily and Ben did not know what to do. They did not want to go down the hill, it was too steep and fast. They did not want to stay, the rude man was too mean and loud. They looked at each other and had an idea. They held hands and lifted their bikes. They used their wheels as wings. They felt a gust of wind and rose in the air. They flew over the rude man and his car. They laughed and cheered. "Bye bye, rude man!" they said. "We're flying, we're free!" They flew away to find a new place to play. They left the rude man behind, fuming and honking. They did not care. They were happy and brave. They had wheels and they could rise.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily lived in a small house. One day, she found a fake toy phone in her room. She played with it all day, talking and pretending to answer calls. She had so much fun with her new toy. One sunny day, while playing with her fake phone, something strange happened. When she said "hello," someone answered! Lily was very surprised. She put the phone to her lip and said "hello" again. This time, a soft voice said "hello" back. Lily was so excited that she ran to her mom and dad. She told them about the voice in her fake phone. Her mom and dad listened and smiled. They had a secret. They bought a real phone that looked like a toy for Lily. They wanted to surprise her. Lily was so happy, and she talked to her new friend on the phone every day.

Once there was a little girl. She lived in a small house with her mom. She was three years old and loved to jog around the garden. One day her mom said, "Honey, I promise I will take you to the park." This made the little girl so excited. When it was time to go to the park, the little girl started jogging around the house. As she was jogging, it started raining. She felt scared and stopped jogging. "Mom," she said, "It's raining. Will you still take me to the park?" "Yes," replied her mom. "I promised, so I will keep my promise." The little girl hugged her mom and smiled. The rain stopped and she started jogging around the house again, excited about her trip to the park.

Anna loved bananas. She ate one every day after lunch. But today, she saw a big bunch of bananas on the kitchen table. They looked so yellow and sweet. She wanted to eat them all. She looked around. Mommy was in the living room, reading a book. Daddy was in the garden, watering the flowers. No one would see her. She tiptoed to the table and reached for a banana. She peeled it and took a big bite. It was delicious. She wanted another one. She peeled another banana and ate it. And another one. And another one. She did not stop until she ate all the bananas. She felt very full and happy. But then, she heard Mommy's voice. "Anna, where are you? It's time for lunch!" Anna felt a pang in her tummy. She realized what she had done. She had eaten all the bananas. There were none left for Mommy and Daddy. And for her lunch. She felt very guilty. She hid behind the table and hoped Mommy would not find her. But Mommy came into the kitchen and saw the empty banana peels on the floor. She frowned and looked around. "Anna, what have you done?" she asked. Anna peeked from behind the table and saw Mommy's angry face. She knew she was in trouble. She came out slowly and hung her head. "I'm sorry, Mommy," she said. "I ate all the bananas. I was hungry." Mommy sighed and shook her head. She picked up Anna and hugged her. "I know you love bananas, Anna, but you can't eat them all. You have to share with others. And you have to eat other things, too. Bananas are good for you, but too many can make you sick." She put Anna down and examined her tummy. It was round and hard. "Does your tummy hurt, Anna?" she asked. Anna nodded. She felt a little sick. Mommy smiled and kissed her forehead. "Come on, let's go to the couch. I'll make you some tea and toast. And maybe later, we can go to the store and buy some more bananas. But only if you promise to share them, okay?" Anna nodded again. She felt a little better. "Okay, Mommy," she said. "I promise. I love you." "I love you, too, Anna," Mommy said. "Now, let's go."
