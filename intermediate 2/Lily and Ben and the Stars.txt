Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Stars

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play dress-up with Lily's mom's fancy clothes and jewels. Lily's mom lets them use her big hats, shiny necklaces, and pretty rings. They pretend to be kings and queens, or pirates, or movie stars. One day, they find a new jewel in Lily's mom's box. It is a big, red, sparkly jewel. It looks like a ruby. Lily and Ben are very excited. They want to try it on. "Can I wear it first?" Lily asks. "No, I want to wear it first!" Ben says. They both reach for the jewel. They pull and tug. They do not share. The jewel falls on the floor. It breaks into many pieces. Lily and Ben are sad. They cry. Lily's mom hears them. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees the broken jewel. She is not angry. She hugs Lily and Ben. "It's okay, my loves. It was not a real jewel. It was just a toy. I bought it for you to play with. But you have to be careful and gentle with your toys. And you have to share with your friends. Do you understand?" she says. Lily and Ben nod. They say they are sorry. They hug each other. They pick up the pieces of the jewel. They make a new game. They pretend the pieces are magic stones. They enjoy playing with them. They are happy again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved winter. Lily liked to play in the snow and make original snowmen. She would give them funny hats and big smiles. One day, Lily wanted to make a new friend. She decided to make a snowman. She rolled a big snowball for the body and a smaller one for the head. She found two sticks for the arms and a carrot for the nose. Lily was very happy with her new snowman friend. They played all day in the winter snow. When it was time to go inside, Lily said goodbye to her snowman. She knew she would see him again tomorrow for more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sarah. She liked to play outside with her friends. One day, Sarah's friends came over and asked her to come with them to the park. The park had a colorful switch down by the trees. Sarah's friends said the switch could be used to make things wrap around them like a cocoon. Sarah was intrigued and said she wanted to try it out. Sarah's friends reached out and pulled the switch. As soon as they did, colorful ribbons shot out from the switch and wrapped them up! Sarah couldn't believe her eyes. She had so much fun and wanted to do it again. "Let's wrap each other up one more time" said Sarah. Her friends agreed, and everyone grabbed the switch and it wrapped them up again, just like before. Sarah enjoyed wrapping up with her friends and quickly realized that colorful switch had made the park ten times more fun!

Once upon a time, there was a little gray mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a big house with his family. One day, Tim found a small helmet in the house. He put the helmet on his head and felt very brave. Tim heard that there was a big cat living in the house too. He wanted to see the cat, but he was scared. Tim thought that with his helmet, he could escape from the cat if he needed to. So, he put on his helmet and went to look for the cat. Tim found the cat sleeping in a room. He was very quiet, but the cat woke up and saw him. The cat tried to catch Tim, but Tim was fast. He ran and ran, and with his helmet, he was able to escape. Tim went back to his family, and they were all happy and safe. And from that day on, Tim always wore his helmet when he went on adventures.

Tom and his wife Ann went to the park with their dog. They liked to walk and play with their dog. They saw many things in the park: trees, flowers, birds, and other people. One day, they saw a man with a big nose and a mean face. He had an ugly hat and an ugly coat. He also had a dog, but his dog was very small and dirty. The man looked at Tom and Ann and said, "What are you looking at? Go away, or I will make you sorry!" Tom and Ann were scared of the man. They did not want to fight with him. They tried to walk away, but the man followed them. He said, "You think you are better than me, huh? You think you have a nice dog and a nice wife, huh? Well, you are wrong! Your dog is stupid and your wife is ugly!" Tom and Ann were very angry and sad. They did not like the man's words. They loved their dog and their wife. They thought they were very nice and pretty. They wanted the man to stop talking and leave them alone. They noticed a policeman near the park. He was wearing a blue uniform and a shiny badge. He had a big smile and a friendly voice. He saw Tom and Ann and their dog. He said, "Hello, what a lovely day! What a lovely dog! What a lovely wife!" Tom and Ann were happy to see the policeman. They ran to him and told him about the man. They said, "He is very mean and rude. He has an ugly hat and an ugly coat. He has an ugly dog and an ugly face. He said bad things about our dog and our wife. He made us scared and sad. Can you help us?" The policeman nodded and said, "Of course, I can help you. You did the right thing to come to me. You do not have to listen to the man. He is wrong and he is lying. Your dog is smart and your wife is beautiful. You are very lucky and very kind. I will talk to the man and make him go away. You can enjoy the park and have fun." Tom and Ann thanked the policeman. They hugged their dog and their wife. They felt safe and happy. They said, "You are very nice and helpful. You have a nice uniform and a nice badge. You have a nice smile and a nice voice. You said good things about our dog and our wife. You made us happy and proud. Thank you very much!" The policeman smiled and said, "You are very welcome. I am glad to help you. You are very brave and polite. You have a nice dog and a nice wife. You have a nice park and a nice day. Have a good time!" Tom and Ann and their dog walked away from the man. They did not look at him anymore. They looked at the trees, the flowers, the birds, and the other people. They played and laughed and had fun. They loved their dog and their wife. They loved the park and the day. They were very happy.
