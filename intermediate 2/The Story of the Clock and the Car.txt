Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Clock and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a big, round clock. It lived on a wall in a small house. The clock was always happy, and it liked to help people know the time. But one day, the clock got angry. The clock was angry because it saw a little boy playing with a toy car. The boy was having fun, but he was not looking at the clock. The clock wanted the boy to look at it and know the time. So, the clock decided to do something unexpected. The clock jumped off the wall and started to dance. The little boy saw the dancing clock and laughed. He stopped playing with his toy car and looked at the clock. The boy saw the time and said, "Oh, it's time for dinner!" The clock felt happy again, and it went back to its place on the wall. The boy never forgot to look at the clock again.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lily. Lily was young and loved playing with her red ball. One sunny day, Lily went outside to play with her ball in her green yard. As she played, she saw a young cat walk by. The cat was small and had pretty, soft fur. Lily wanted to play with the cat, so she picked up her ball and walked over to it. "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" Lily asked. The cat looked at Lily and nodded. Lily was very happy. She threw the ball up in the air, and the cat jumped to catch it. But the cat missed the ball and dropped it on the ground. They both laughed and played together all day long.

One day, Tim and Sue were playing in the park. Tim found a toy pistol on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to Sue. Sue looked at it and said, "I am scared of that pistol." Tim wanted to make Sue feel better. So, he put the pistol down and gave her a big kiss on the cheek. Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. I am not scared now." They played together all day. They did not play with the pistol. They had fun and were happy. At the end of the day, they went home and told their mom about their day. Mom was glad they had fun and stayed safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable tree. A little boy named Tim loved to play under the tree. One sunny day, Tim saw a tiny beetle on the ground. Tim kneeled down to look at the beetle. He said, "Hi, beetle! Do you want to be my friend?" The beetle nodded its little head. Tim smiled and knew they would be best friends. Later, Tim and the beetle played hide and seek. Tim hid behind the dependable tree. The beetle found him every time. They laughed and played all day under the tree.

Once there was this fisherman who went fishing in a pond. He was a bit nervous but he had to go as he wanted to feed his family. He packed his gear, put on his waders and made his way to the pond. The fisherman had no luck that day. He didn't catch anything. He was feeling a bit sad but he still decided to stay a little longer. The fisherman watied by the pond until he heard a noise. He saw some little fish jumping up into the sky! The fisherman was so excited! He was so happy that he was able to provide food for his family. He learned a valuable lesson that day - there is always something to value. The fisherman's nervousness had been worth it. The day had been a success after all!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his original toy car. It was made of shiny metal and had four big wheels. Tim took his car everywhere he went. One day, Tim's mom asked him to bring his toy car to the park. Tim was very happy. He put his metal car in his pocket and ran to the park. There, he met his friend, Lily. Lily liked Tim's car and wanted to play with it too. Together, Tim and Lily played with the metal car. They made it go fast and slow, up and down the hills. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim put his original toy car back in his pocket. He was glad he could bring it to the park and share it with his friend.
