Title: Lily the Girl and the Cat

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a cat. The cat had a guitar. Lily thought it was funny. She wanted to follow the cat. "Hi cat, can I follow you?" Lily asked. The cat said, "Yes, but I am going to find healthy food. Do you like healthy food?" Lily said, "Yes, I like healthy food too!" Lily and the cat walked and walked. They found a big tree. The tree had a door. The cat opened the door with the guitar. Inside the tree, there were many healthy foods. Lily and the cat were happy. They ate and played music with the guitar. But then, the tree started to talk! The tree said, "Thank you for playing music and eating healthy food. I am a magic tree. I will give you a wish." Lily and the cat were very surprised. They wished for more healthy food and more fun days together. The magic tree made their wish come true.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had to go with his mom and dad to the airport. He did not like the airport. It was big, loud, and full of people. Tim felt scared. At the airport, Tim's mom put a tight hat on his head. Tim did not like the hat. It was too tight and made him feel bad. He said, "Mom, I hate this hat. Can I take it off?" His mom said, "No, Tim. You must keep it on." Tim, his mom, and his dad got on a big plane. The plane was very loud, and Tim was scared. The tight hat made him feel even worse. The plane took off, and Tim started to cry. His mom and dad tried to help him, but Tim just cried louder. The plane ride was very long, and Tim cried the whole time. When they finally got off the plane, Tim was still sad and scared.

Once upon a time, there was a chicken named Bob. Bob was very excited to travel. He wanted to see new places and meet new friends. One day, Bob started his journey. He walked and walked, and soon he saw a big hill. He thought, "I will go up the hill and see what's on the other side." So, Bob climbed the hill. When he reached the top, he saw a big, shiny egg. It was so shiny that Bob couldn't believe his eyes. Bob thought, "This egg must be very special. I will take it with me on my travels." So, Bob carried the egg with him. Then, something unexpected happened. The egg started to shake and crack. Bob was surprised and a little scared. The egg cracked open, and out came a baby dragon! The dragon was friendly and wanted to be friends with Bob. Now, Bob had a new friend to travel with him on his journey. They were both very excited to see the world together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big brother named Tom. They liked to play together in their house. One day, Tom found some shiny money. He said, "Sue, look at this attractive money I found!" Sue smiled and said, "Wow, very pretty! What can we buy with it?" Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's go to the store and buy some candy." So, Tom and Sue went outside. Tom held Sue's hand and lead her to the store. At the store, they bought lots of yummy candy with their money. They shared the candy with their friends and had a fun day. Tom and Sue were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat strawberries. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom to pick some strawberries. They found a big strawberry bush with lots of red, juicy strawberries. Tim was very happy to see the strawberries but then he saw a big bug on the bush. Tim started to panic. He was scared of the bug and didn't want it to eat his strawberries. His mom told him not to worry, the bug was not going to hurt him or the strawberries. Tim listened to his mom and picked the strawberries. He felt much better and not miserable anymore. They went home and ate the strawberries together. It was a fun day at the park.
