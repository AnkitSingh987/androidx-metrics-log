Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Car and the Bus

Once upon a time, there was a big, yellow bus. The bus was independent and liked to go on trips all by itself. One day, the bus decided to go on a very long trip to see new places. On the way, the bus met a little red car. The little red car said, "Hi, big yellow bus! Where are you going?" The bus replied, "I am going on a long trip to see new places all by myself." The little red car wanted to go too, but the bus said no. The bus wanted to be independent. As the bus kept going, it started to get dark. The bus did not know where it was. Suddenly, the bus saw a big, scary monster. The monster said, "Surrender, big yellow bus, or I will eat you!" The bus was scared and did not know what to do. The bus wished it had let the little red car come along. In the end, the bus had to surrender to the monster. The big yellow bus was not so independent anymore, and it was a bad ending for the bus.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play soccer with his friends. They would play every day and try to score a goal. One day, while they were playing, Tim kicked the ball too hard and it flew into a tree. The ball popped and everyone was sad. Tim felt ashamed that he broke the ball. He wanted to make it right. So, he went to his dad and asked for help. His dad said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can repair the ball together." They found some tape and fixed the ball. The next day, Tim and his friends played soccer again. Tim was more careful when he kicked the ball. He scored a goal and everyone was happy. Tim learned that it's okay to make mistakes, as long as you try to fix them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a mild pet cat named Max. They loved to play together. One day, Tim felt the urge to teach Max a new trick. He wanted Max to jump high. Tim tried and tried to make Max jump high. Max did not succeed at first. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He kept trying to help Max learn the new trick. Max wanted to make Tim happy, so he tried harder too. Then, something unexpected happened. Max jumped so high that he touched the sky! Tim was so happy and surprised. Max had learned the new trick and they both succeeded. They played and laughed all day, and Tim knew that with a little urge, anything was possible.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Max was playing with his ball when he felt something on his nose. It was a little mosquito. Max did not like the mosquito. He tried to shake it off, but the mosquito stayed on his nose. Max felt upset, so he went to his friend, a big tree. The tree was always there to help Max when he needed it. The tree saw the mosquito on Max's nose and knew what to do. The tree moved its branch and gently tapped Max's nose. The mosquito flew away, and Max felt happy again. Max was thankful for his loyal friend, the tree, and they played together all day long.

One day, there was a silly little boy who wanted to play in the puddle. He jumped into the wet puddle and splashed around. The boy's mother saw her son in the puddle and scolded him. "What are you doing?" she said. The boy replied, "I wanted to own the puddle". The mother said, "No, you cannot own the puddle". The little boy crossed his arms and said, "Yes I can!" The mother said sternly, "No, you must out of the puddle now". The little boy was so angry that he started to cry. Sadly, he got out of the puddle, and his dream of owning it faded away.
