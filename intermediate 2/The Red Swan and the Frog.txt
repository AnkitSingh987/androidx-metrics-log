Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Swan and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a red swan. It lived in a big pond with other swans. The red swan liked to play with its friends all day. They would swim and splash in the water. The red swan was very happy. One day, the red swan forgot where its home was. It looked around and could not find the big pond. The red swan felt sad and scared. It did not know what to do. So, it walked and walked, trying to find its way back home. As the red swan walked, it met a nice frog. The frog saw that the red swan was sad. The frog asked, "Why are you sad?" The red swan said, "I forgot where my home is." The nice frog knew where the big pond was. So, the frog helped the red swan find its way back home. When they got to the big pond, the red swan was so happy. It thanked the frog and played with its friends again. From that day on, the red swan never forgot where its home was.

Tom and Lily were playing soccer in the park. They liked to kick the ball and run after it. They had a goal made of sticks and a net. They took turns to serve the ball and try to score. "Serve me the ball, Lily!" Tom shouted. He was ready to kick it hard. "OK, Tom!" Lily said. She rolled the ball to him with her foot. She ran to the goal to stop him. Tom kicked the ball with all his might. It flew in the air and landed in the net. He scored! "Yay, Tom! You scored!" Lily clapped her hands. She was jolly for him. "Thank you, Lily! You are a good friend!" Tom said. He was jolly too. They hugged and smiled. They were happy to play soccer together. They served the ball again and again. They scored many times. They had fun.

Once there was a little girl named Amy. She had a photo of her and her friend, Tim. They were very happy in the photo. One day, Tim did something terrible. He took Amy's toy and did not give it back. Amy was very sad. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, Tim took my toy and did not give it back. What should I do?" Her mom said, "You should speak to Tim and tell him how you feel." So, Amy went to Tim and said, "Tim, I am sad because you took my toy. Can you please give it back?" Tim said, "I am sorry, Amy. I did not know it made you sad. Here is your toy." Amy was happy and thanked Tim. They played together and were good friends again. The moral of the story is to always speak up if someone does something that makes you sad.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Aja and Bill. Aja was a hairy cross that was always looking for an adventure. One day they decided to go on a walk. As they were walking along, Bill said to Aja, "Do you see how the bushes are getting bigger and bigger? They are increasing every day!" Aja was fascinated by the idea. She said, "Let's go and explore all the hairy crosses so we can see how much they have increased." The two friends ran towards the crosses, eager to find out what had changed. They noticed that the crosses were getting bigger and bigger. Bill said, "This is amazing! It looks like the hairy crosses have increased over time!" Aja smiled and said, "I'm so happy that we decided to go on an adventure today. Now we know that the crosses have increased, just like Bill said".

One day, Sam was feeling very glad. He stepped his way to the end of the street and saw a big, brown mule. Sam was very excited. He stepped closer to get a better look. The mule had long, wavy fur all over its body. Sam was glad he could see this beautiful animal and he stepped back a little to admire it. He saw the mule move its long tail and step around in a large circle. Sam was so happy he could see the mule and kept smiling. He waved goodbye to the mule and stepped back home. The whole way, he kept thinking about the mule, and was still feeling very glad.
