Category: Intermediate
Title: Dennis and the Mommy

Dennis was a sleepy boy who was ready for bed. He said goodnight to his mommy and she tucked him into bed. He rolled over and snuggled into his seat. As he began to fall asleep, he heard a strange noise. A loud "thump" from the other room! He was worried, so he called for his mommy. His mommy rushed into the room and said, "It's alright, everything is fine." But Dennis couldn't help but feel nervous. He asked if something fell and his mommy said, "Yes, I dropped something." She looked at Dennis and said, "Don't worry, it's all taken care of." The next day Dennis and his mommy were in the kitchen when Dennis saw something on the floor. He pointed to it and said, "What's that?" His mommy smiled and said, "Oh, that's just the thing I dropped last night. I'm so glad I didn't break it!" Dennis felt relieved, knowing that he was right to be worried. He smiled at his mommy and said, "I'm glad nothing was broken," as he slowly started to get sleepy again.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to have a race. He asked his friend, Sam, to race with him. They went to a warm, sunny park to run. At the start of the race, Tim and Sam ran as fast as they could. They both tried to manage to win. Tim was in the front, and Sam was close behind. They were almost at the end of the race. Just then, a little dog came running up. The dog was very fast! It ran right past Tim and Sam. The dog won the race! Tim and Sam were surprised, but they laughed and clapped for the dog. They had fun in the warm park, even if they did not win the race.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with many tasty apples. Tim wanted to pick some apples to take home, but the apples were too high up. Tim's mom had an idea. She took a seat from their car and put it under the tree. She said, "Tim, stand on the seat to pick the apples." Tim stood on the seat and picked many tasty apples. He was very happy. Then, Tim saw a squirrel on the tree. The squirrel wanted an apple too. Tim said, "Here, squirrel! Have a tasty apple!" He held out an apple to the squirrel. The squirrel took the apple and ran away. Tim and his mom laughed and went home with their apples. They displayed their apples in a big bowl on the kitchen table.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very independent. She liked to do things all by herself. One day, Sue wanted to buy a new toy. She knew she had to save her money to get it. Sue did chores around the house to earn money. She helped her mom and dad every day. One day, when Sue was helping her dad, he said, "Soon, there will be snow, and you can help me with the snow too!" Sue was excited to help with the snow and earn more money. Finally, the day came when there was snow on the ground. Sue helped her dad clear the snow. She worked very hard. After they were done, her dad gave her some money. Sue was so happy! She had saved enough money to buy her new toy. Sue learned that being independent and working hard made her feel proud and happy.

Lila and Ben were playing in the hut they made with sticks and leaves. They liked to pretend it was their home and they were the king and queen of the forest. They had a basket of berries and nuts for their lunch. "Can I have some berries, please?" Ben asked Lila. "Sure, here you go," Lila said, giving him some. "But don't eat them all, we have to save some for later." Ben nodded, but he was very hungry. He ate his berries quickly and then reached for more. Lila saw him and stopped him. "Hey, that's not fair! You already had your share!" Lila said, pulling the basket away. "But I want more! They are so yummy!" Ben said, trying to grab the basket back. "No, you can't have more! You have to resist!" Lila said, holding the basket tightly. Ben felt frustrated. He wanted more berries and he didn't like Lila telling him what to do. He pushed Lila and made her fall. Lila dropped the basket and the berries spilled on the ground. "Look what you did!" Lila cried, getting up. "You ruined our lunch!" Ben felt sorry. He didn't mean to hurt Lila or waste the berries. He looked at Lila and saw tears in her eyes. "I'm sorry, Lila. I was greedy and mean. Can you forgive me?" Ben said, picking up some berries and offering them to Lila. Lila sniffed and wiped her eyes. She saw that Ben was sorry and he wanted to share. She smiled and took some berries from him. "It's okay, Ben. I forgive you. But next time, you have to resist your hunger and be fair, okay?" Lila said, hugging Ben. "Okay, Lila. I will. And next time, I will help you make the hut better, too," Ben said, hugging Lila back. They ate the rest of the berries and nuts together and then played some more in their hut. They were happy and they learned to resist and share.
