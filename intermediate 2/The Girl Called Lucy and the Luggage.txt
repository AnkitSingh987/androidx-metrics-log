Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Lucy and the Luggage

Once there was a little girl called Lucy. She was three years old. One day she had to go on her first big trip. She was very excited and asked her mum if she could take her honest luggage. Mum said yes and told Lucy to make sure she minded it. Lucy said that she would. At the airport, they got on the big plane and Lucy held her luggage tightly. She was so happy to be going on her first trip. When she got off the plane, she saw lots of people in the airport. It was very busy and she was worried about losing her luggage. Still, she minded her honest luggage very carefully and finally got to her holiday destination. It was a wonderful adventure and she remembered to mind her luggage all the way.

Once upon a time, there was a fat farmer. He had a big farm with many animals. The fat farmer loved to touch his soft animals. He had a big pig, a little cat, and a funny dog. They were all very happy on the farm. One day, the fat farmer went to the barn to touch his big pig. But the big pig was not there! The fat farmer was sad and looked for the big pig everywhere. He asked the little cat and the funny dog, but they did not know where the big pig was. Then, the fat farmer heard a loud noise from his house. He ran to his house and saw the big pig in his kitchen! The big pig was not a pig at all. It was a big balloon! The fat farmer laughed and laughed. The little cat and the funny dog laughed too. They all played with the big balloon and had a great day.

One day, a pretty cat saw a big box on the stair. The cat was very happy. She wanted to fill the box with her toys. She ran to get her ball, her doll, and her car. The cat put the toys in the box. She said, "This is fun! I love to fill the box." Then, she heard a voice. It was a little mouse. The mouse said, "Can I play too?" The cat was surprised. She did not see the mouse before. But she said, "Yes, you can play. Let's fill the box together." The mouse got his toys too. They filled the box with more toys. They played all day. They became good friends. The cat and the mouse learned that even if they were different, they could still have fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to kick a ball and play with his friends. One sunny day, Tim saw a big tree with a round, green thing on it. Tim had never seen this before. He thought it was a ball. Tim said, "I am going to kick that ball!" He kicked the green thing, but it was not a ball. It was a pear. The pear fell to the ground and Tim picked it up. He looked at the pear and said, "This is not a ball. What is it?" An old lady walked by and saw Tim with the pear. She said, "That is a pear, my dear. It is not a ball. You can eat it." Tim did not know what a pear was. He was ignorant. But he thanked the old lady and took a bite. The pear was sweet and juicy. Tim learned something new that day and shared the pear with his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Lily. She had a silver toy that she loved a lot. One day, Lily saw that her toy was dirty, so she decided to clean it. While she was cleaning her toy, she saw an unknown little bug. The bug looked at Lily and said, "Hello, I am Buggy. Can you help me clean my home too?" Lily was surprised but she wanted to help her new friend. Lily and Buggy went to clean Buggy's home. When they finished cleaning, Buggy's home turned into a beautiful castle. Buggy said, "Thank you, Lily! You helped me find my true home." Lily was happy she could help, and they became best friends forever.
