Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and Ben and the Water

Once upon a time there were two friends, Sam and Ben. They loved to go outside to share and play together. One day they were running around in the mud when suddenly Sam saw a big leak. "Look, Ben!" Sam shouted, pointing at it. "Oh wow! That's so filthy," replied Ben. "What if we share it?" suggested Sam. Ben scrunched up his nose in disgust. "That is not a good idea," he said. But Sam was already squeezing water out of the leak. "It's fun!" he said, splashing it all over. Ben laughed, and soon they both were happily playing in the dirty water. At last when they were all wet and muddy, Ben said, "This has been the most fun we've had in a long time!" Sam agreed, giving Ben a high five. And even though the water was filthy, they both had a really great time!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a guard named Tom. He would work all day to keep the town safe. Tom was very hungry after working so hard. He would go home and eat a big meal. One day, while Tom was working, he saw a little boy named Sam. Sam was hungry too. Sam asked Tom, "Can you help me find some food? I am very hungry." Tom felt sad for Sam and wanted to help him. Tom and Sam went to a big tree with lots of fruits. They both picked fruits and ate them. They were not hungry anymore. They became good friends and always helped each other. The people in the town learned from Tom and Sam's friendship. They also started helping each other. The moral of the story is: "Helping others is a good thing to do."

Once upon a time, there was a poor couple who had been married for many years. They were very unhappy, as they could barely afford the basic necessities. One day, the husband looked at his wife and said, “I’ve had enough!” With that, he packed his belongings and replaced the wife with a younger woman. The poor wife was so distraught that she could barely call up her courage. Yet, as the days passed, she slowly began to realize that her life could still be full of love and joy. She had more time for herself, and started to feel a new level of contentment. At first the new relationship seemed stable. Yet, over time, the couple felt that something was missing from the marriage. As time went on, it became clear what the couple was lacking - true understanding and unconditional love. The husband eventually realized that his initial decision to replace his old wife had been a mistake. He saw her for all the things he was missing in his new relationship and decided to ask for her forgiveness and come home. The wife bravely opened her arms to the husband upon his return, proving that all was not lost, even in their difficult and poor situation. As they embraced, they both felt that they were finally getting back what they had been missing the most in their marriage: love.

Anna and Ben are playing with their toys. Anna has a doll with a red bow in her hair. Ben has a car with a blue bow on the roof. They like to make their toys talk to each other. "Hello, I am Anna's doll. I have a red bow. Do you like it?" Anna makes her doll say. "Hello, I am Ben's car. I have a blue bow. It is very nice." Ben makes his car say. They make their toys smile and nod at each other. They are having fun. But then, a big dog comes into the room. The dog sees the toys and thinks they are for him. He runs towards them and grabs the doll in his mouth. He shakes it and tears the red bow off. "Hey, that is my doll! Give it back!" Anna shouts. She tries to get her doll from the dog. The dog growls and runs away with the doll. He drops the car on the floor. The blue bow falls off too. "Hey, that is my car! He broke it!" Ben cries. He picks up his car and sees the broken bow. Anna and Ben are very sad. They look at their toys and see their faces. The doll and the car look sad too. They have no bows. They hug their toys and say sorry. They wish they had new bows for them. Then, they see their mom. She has a basket with some clothes. She sees their sad faces and asks what is wrong. "The dog took our toys and broke our bows!" Anna and Ben say. "Oh, I am sorry. That is not nice. But I have an idea. Look, I have some small bows here. They are for your clothes, but you can use them for your toys." Mom says. She shows them some small bows of different colors. They are very pretty. "Wow, thank you, mom!" Anna and Ben say. They choose some small bows for their toys. Anna picks a pink bow for her doll. Ben picks a green bow for his car. They tie the bows on their toys' hair and roof. They make their toys say thank you too. "Thank you, mom. You are very kind. We like our new bows." They make their toys say. They smile and hug their mom. They are happy again. They play with their toys and make them talk to each other. They say nice things about their bows. "Hello, I am Anna's doll. I have a pink bow. It is very pretty." Anna makes her doll say. "Hello, I am Ben's car. I have a green bow. It is very cool." Ben makes his car say. They make their toys smile and nod at each other. They are having fun.
