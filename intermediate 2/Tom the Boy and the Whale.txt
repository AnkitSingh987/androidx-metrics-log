Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Whale

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a brave boy who always wanted to go on adventures. One day, he decided to go on a new journey. He built a raft and set off in the sea. As he travelled, he met a friendly whale. The whale was very kind and took Tom to a beautiful island. Tom was amazed by the wonderful island, but he soon realized he was very tired from his journey. At this moment, Tom remembered something his Grandma told him: "When you are tired, take a rest and you will feel fit and ready to go again." Tom followed his Grandma's advice and he had a nap. He woke up feeling fit, and he continued his journey. The moral of this story is that resting will make you feel fit and ready to go on more adventures!

Lily loved roses. She had a rose garden in her backyard. She watered them every day and sang to them. She liked how they smelled and looked. One day, a big storm came. It was very loud and scary. The wind blew hard and the rain poured down. Lily hid under her bed with her teddy bear. She hoped her roses were okay. When the storm was over, Lily ran outside to see her garden. She was sad. Many of her roses were broken and bent. She cried and hugged her teddy bear. "Mommy, mommy, look at my roses! They are ruined!" she sobbed. Mommy came and hugged Lily. She looked at the garden and smiled. "Don't worry, sweetie. We can repair your roses. We can cut off the bad parts and plant new ones. They will grow back soon. Roses are very strong and dependable. They can survive storms and still be beautiful." "Really, mommy? Can we do that?" Lily asked. "Of course, honey. Come on, let's get the scissors and the shovel. We can do it together. It will be fun and we will learn something new." Lily wiped her tears and smiled. She followed mommy to the garden. She was happy. She learned that roses are dependable and that she can repair things that are broken. She also learned that mommy is dependable and that she can help her when she is sad. She thanked mommy and gave her a big hug. She loved roses and she loved mommy.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park on Tuesday. Tuesday is a sunny day. They run and jump and laugh. Anna sees a big slide. She wants to go on the slide. She runs to the slide. But Ben is there first. He climbs up the ladder. "Ben, can I go on the slide?" Anna asks. "No, I want to go first. You have to wait," Ben says. "But I want to go now. It is my turn," Anna says. They both want to go on the slide. They do not yield. They push and pull. They are not happy. A lady sees them. She is Ben's mom. She comes to the slide. "Ben, Anna, what are you doing?" she asks. "We want to go on the slide. But he/she does not let me," they say. The lady shakes her head. She is not happy. "Ben, Anna, you have to share. Sharing is good. Sharing is healthy. If you share, you can both have fun. Do you understand?" she says. Ben and Anna nod. They understand. They are sorry. "OK, mom. We will share. We will yield. Can we go on the slide now?" they ask. "Yes, you can. But take turns. And be careful. And have fun," she says. Ben and Anna smile. They hug. They are friends. They take turns on the slide. They have fun. They are happy. It is a good Tuesday.

Lily loved to read books with her mom before bed. She liked to look at the pictures and hear the stories. Mom would point to the words and say them out loud. Lily would try to repeat them and learn new words. One night, they were reading a book about a bunny who went on an adventure. The bunny met many friends and saw many things. Lily was very curious and asked mom many questions. "What is that?" "Why did he do that?" "Where is he going?" Mom answered patiently and smiled. On one page, the bunny found a big carrot. He was very hungry and wanted to eat it. But he saw a mouse who was also hungry and had no food. The bunny decided to share the carrot with the mouse. They became friends and ate the carrot together. Lily thought that was very nice of the bunny. She said, "Mom, I want to taste the carrot too. Can I have a bite?" Mom laughed and said, "No, sweetie, you can't eat the book. It's not food. It's paper. It doesn't taste good. It tastes bad." Lily didn't believe mom. She wanted to see for herself. She leaned over and took a bite of the page. She quickly spat it out and made a face. "Yuck! Mom, you were right. The page tastes bad. It tastes like dirt and glue. I don't like it." Mom hugged Lily and said, "I'm sorry, honey. I know you were curious. But you should never eat things that are not food. They can make you sick. You should only eat things that mom or dad give you. OK?" Lily nodded and said, "OK, mom. I'm sorry. I won't eat the book again. Can we finish the story?" Mom said, "Of course, my love. Let's see what happens to the bunny next." They turned the page and continued to read. Lily felt sleepy and snuggled with mom. She dreamed of carrots and bunnies and friends.
