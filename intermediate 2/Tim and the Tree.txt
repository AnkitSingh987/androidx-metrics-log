Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Tree

One sunny day, Tim the bunny went for a walk. Tim saw a fork on the ground. He thought it was mighty big. He wanted to show his friend, Sam the squirrel. Tim hopped and jumped to Sam's tree. He called out, "Sam, come see what I found!" Sam came down from the tree and looked at the fork. "Wow, that is a mighty big fork!" said Sam. Tim and Sam decided to play with the fork. They took turns jumping over it. They laughed and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they put the mighty fork away and went home to rest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to count. She would count anything she saw. One day, Sue found a delicate flower in her yard. It was the most beautiful flower she had ever seen. Sue wanted to count the petals on the flower, but she didn't want to hurt it. So, she counted very carefully. One, two, three, four, five. There were five petals on the flower. Just then, Sue saw a little bug on the flower. She had not seen it yet. The bug was also counting the petals. They both laughed and counted together. Sue made a new friend that day, and they counted many more things together.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a tube while playing outside. He picked it up and looked inside. It was empty. Tim was upset because he wanted to find something fun in the tube. He walked to his friend, Sally, and showed her the tube. Sally said, "Tim, we can put something fun in the tube!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sally! Let's do it!" They found some colorful balls and put them in the tube. They shook it and laughed as the balls made funny noises. Tim was not upset anymore. He thanked Sally for helping him have fun with the tube. They played with the tube all day and were very happy.

The little bird looked for a safe place to make her nest. She flew near a tree at the edge of the forest. Sadly, the tree was dead. Nevertheless, the bird decided to rest there for a while. Suddenly, the bird stopped flapping her wings and froze. She heard some noises from behind the tree. It was a little rabbit! The rabbit said, “What are you doing here? This is not a good place to make a nest. You should move away!” The bird replied, “This tree is dead, so I thought I could rest here for a while. I'm just looking for the right place.” The rabbit said, “Don’t stay here! There might be other creatures that want to hurt you. Stop looking for this place and fly somewhere else!” The bird took the rabbit’s advice and flew away. She found a safe spot in a nearby tree and started to make her nest there. The bird was grateful to the rabbit for warning her. She soon settled safely in her new home.

Once upon a time, there was a big pie. A little girl named Sue wanted to eat the pie. Sue's mom said, "You can eat the pie after you kiss me." Sue kissed her mom, but when she went to eat the pie, a rude boy named Tom took it. "That's my pie!" Sue cried. Tom laughed and said, "No, it's mine!" Sue was sad, but then something unexpected happened. A big dog came and took the pie from Tom. The dog ate the pie very fast. Tom and Sue watched the dog eat the pie. They both laughed and became friends. They learned that sharing is better than being rude.

One day, a nosy little girl went to the park. She saw a dancer in a pretty dress. The dancer spun around and around. The little girl wanted to observe the dancer and learn how to dance too. As the little girl watched, a big wind came. It blew the dancer's hat right off her head. The dancer tried to catch the hat, but it flew away. The little girl felt sad for the dancer. Then, something unexpected happened. A dog with a waggy tail ran up and caught the hat in his mouth. The dog brought the hat back to the dancer. The dancer was so happy! She thanked the dog and gave him a hug. The little girl learned that even nosy kids can make new friends at the park.
