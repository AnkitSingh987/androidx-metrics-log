Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red House and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a red house. In this house, lived a little girl named Lily and her mom. They loved to play and laugh together. One day, while they were playing outside, they found a big rock. Lily's mom said, "Let's see what's inside!" They took turns hitting the rock with a stick. After a while, the rock broke and out came a shiny gem! "Wow!" said Lily, "It's so pretty!" Her mom agreed, "Yes, it's a beautiful gem!" They decided to keep the gem in their red house. Every day, they would look at it and remember the fun they had when they found it. And they lived happily ever after.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play together. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big slide. They are eager to try it. "Let's go, Lila!" Ben says. He runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. "Wait for me, Ben!" Lila says. She follows him. She is a little scared of the slide. It is very high and steep. They reach the top of the slide. Ben sits down and pushes himself. He slides down fast. He laughs and shouts. "Whee! This is fun!" Lila sits down too. She holds the sides of the slide. She slides down slowly. She does not laugh. She feels dizzy and scared. "Ahh! This is not fun!" Ben waits for her at the bottom of the slide. He sees her face. She looks sad. He wants to cheer her up. He has an idea. "Hey, Lila, do you want to play reverse?" he asks. "Reverse? What is that?" Lila asks. "It is a game. We do things backwards. Like this." Ben stands up and walks backwards. He makes funny noises. "Beep, beep, beep!" Lila smiles. She thinks it is funny. She stands up and walks backwards too. She makes funny noises. "Boop, boop, boop!" They walk backwards to the swings. They sit on them and swing backwards. They walk backwards to the sandbox. They dig holes and fill them with sand. They walk backwards to the grass. They lie down and look at the sky. "Look, Lila, there is a cloud that looks like a yoga!" Ben says. "A yoga? What is that?" Lila asks. "It is a thing that mom does. She bends and stretches and breathes. Like this." Ben gets up and does a yoga pose. He puts his hands on the ground and lifts his legs in the air. He looks like a triangle. Lila giggles. She gets up and does a yoga pose too. She puts her hands on her knees and sticks her tongue out. She looks like a lion. They do more yoga poses. They have fun. They forget about the slide. They are happy. They are friends.

One day, Tom went to play in his backyard. He saw a dirty bird. He wanted to record the bird singing. Tom ran inside to get his toy phone. When he came back, the bird was gone. Tom was sad. He looked around the backyard. He wanted to find the bird. Then, he saw something unexpected. There was a big, shiny egg on the ground. Tom picked up the egg. It started to crack. A baby bird came out! The baby bird was the dirty bird's baby. The dirty bird came back and sang a happy song. Tom recorded the song and they all played together in the backyard.

Once upon a time, there was a small musician. He loved to play music for his friends. He played a small drum and made everyone happy. One day, the small musician wanted to play music for more people. He needed more things to make music. So, he asked his friends to supply him with more things. They gave him a small guitar, a small piano, and a small flute. With his new things, the small musician played for many people. They all danced and clapped. The small musician was very happy. He thanked his friends for helping him. They all played music together and had a great time.

Lily and Tom were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make soup with water and toys. Lily had a big red pot and Tom had a small blue pot. Lily was happy with her pot, but Tom was envious. He wanted the big red pot too. "Can I have your pot, Lily?" Tom asked. "No, it's mine. You have your own pot," Lily said. "But your pot is bigger and nicer. I want it," Tom said. He tried to grab the pot from Lily, but she held it tight. "Stop it, Tom. You are being mean. Mommy will be angry if you take my pot," Lily said. She tried to warn Tom, but he did not listen. He pulled harder and harder, until the pot slipped from Lily's hands and fell on the floor. The water and toys spilled everywhere. The pot made a loud noise. Mommy heard the noise and came to the kitchen. "What happened here?" Mommy asked. She saw the mess and the pot on the floor. She saw Lily and Tom looking scared and sad. "Lily and Tom, you know you are not supposed to play with the pots. They are not toys. They are for cooking. You could have hurt yourselves or broken something. Why did you do this?" Mommy asked. Lily and Tom did not know what to say. They felt sorry and ashamed. They knew they had done something wrong. "We are sorry, Mommy. We were playing and Tom wanted my pot and we fought and the pot fell," Lily said. Mommy sighed. She was not happy, but she was not very angry either. She knew Lily and Tom were still learning and sometimes made mistakes. "Playing is good, but fighting is bad. You should share and be nice to each other. You should listen when someone warns you. You should not be envious of what someone else has. You should be happy with what you have. Do you understand?" Mommy said. Lily and Tom nodded. They understood. They hugged Mommy and said they were sorry again. They helped Mommy clean up the mess and put the pots away. They promised to play nicely and not touch the pots again. Mommy smiled and kissed them. She was glad they learned their lesson. She loved them very much.
