Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Clumsy Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a little chamber with her family. She loved to play with her ball, but she would always bump into things because she was clumsy. One day, Kitty's ball rolled into a dark corner of the chamber. Kitty was scared, but she wanted her ball back. She tried to be brave and managed to reach the dark corner. As she touched the ball, something unexpected happened. The wall in the dark corner moved, and it showed a secret room! Kitty was very surprised. She went into the secret room and found a big, shiny ball. Kitty played with the shiny ball and had lots of fun. She was not clumsy in the secret room. When her family saw the secret room, they were happy too. They all played together and had a great time.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys in their room. Tim has a big truck that is red and blue. Sam has a small car that is yellow and green. They make noises and pretend to drive on the road. One day, they see a new toy in the store. It is a colorful staff that can make sounds and lights. Tim and Sam both want the staff. They ask their mom to buy it for them. "Mom, can we have the staff, please?" they say. Mom looks at the staff and says, "No, boys, you have enough toys already. And this staff is too expensive. Let's go home." Tim and Sam are sad and angry. They start to cry and shout. They do not want to go home. They want the staff. "Stop, stop, stop!" Mom says. "You are making a lot of noise. You are being rude and naughty. You have to listen to me. Come on, let's go." Mom takes their hands and leads them out of the store. Tim and Sam still cry and shout. They do not like Mom. They do not like the store. They only like the staff.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big, red comb. Mia liked to comb her long, pretty hair. Every day, she felt comfortable sitting on her chair and combing her hair. One day, Mia noticed something strange. When she combed her hair, it changed color! Her hair turned blue, then green, and even pink! Mia was very surprised. She didn't know how this could happen. Mia showed her mom the magic comb. Her mom laughed and said, "I bought you a special comb for your birthday. It makes your hair change colors for a short time, just for fun!" Mia was happy and excited. She loved her magic comb and couldn't wait to show her friends at school.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful fish named Fin. Fin loved to surf the waves in the big blue sea. One sunny day, while Fin was surfing, he met a new friend. His new friend was a small crab named Snap. "Hi, I'm Snap," said the crab. "Do you want to play with me?" Fin smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Snap!" As they played near the shore, Snap saw a big, tasty leaf. Snap wanted to eat the leaf, but it was too high for him to reach. Fin had an idea. He swam up to the leaf and used his teeth to bite it off the tree. Then, Fin brought the leaf down to Snap. "Thank you, Fin!" said Snap, happily munching on the leaf. "You are a great friend!" Fin and Snap played and surfed together all day long. They had so much fun and became the best of friends. And from that day on, Fin and Snap always helped each other and shared many more beautiful days together in the big blue sea.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate butterfly. The butterfly loved to fly in a zigzag way. One day, the butterfly saw a big, red ball. The butterfly wanted to play with the ball. It flew near the ball and touched it. But, the ball started to roll away. The butterfly did not want to spoil the fun, so it chased the ball. As the butterfly chased the ball, it met a tiny ant. The ant was trying to roll the ball too. The butterfly and the ant became friends. They played with the ball together, and they both had lots of fun. The butterfly learned that sometimes, something unexpected can lead to a new friend.
