Category: Intermediate
Title: Chirpy the Chair and the Strong

Once upon a time, there was a chair named Chirpy. Chirpy was not a popular chair. He was old and shaky. He wanted to improve and be a strong chair. One day, Chirpy met a big, strong chair named Brawny. Chirpy asked, "How can I improve and be like you?" Brawny said, "You must eat good food and do chair exercises every day." Chirpy listened to Brawny. He ate good food and did chair exercises. Soon, Chirpy became a strong chair too. All the other chairs liked Chirpy now. Chirpy was a popular chair and very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On this farm lived a girl named Lily and her big, healthy cow named Moo. Moo was Lily's best friend. They played together every day. One sunny day, Lily said to Moo, "Moo, I will feed you now." She gave Moo some yummy food. Moo ate the food and was very happy. They played for a minute, then Moo said, "Moo moo, thank you, Lily." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, Moo. I love you." They played some more and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Lily and Moo went to sleep, ready for another fun day on the farm.

One day, a hunter went to the woods. He was an intelligent man. He looked for animals to catch. He saw a big rabbit. The rabbit saw the hunter too. They looked at each other. The rabbit said, "Please don't catch me!" The hunter thought about it. He wanted to catch the rabbit, but he also wanted to be kind. He said, "If you can run very fast, I won't catch you." The rabbit was happy. It knew it could run fast. The hunter and the rabbit started to race. The rabbit ran as fast as it could. The hunter tried to catch up, but he could not. The hunter shouted, "You win, rabbit! I won't catch you!" The rabbit was very happy. The hunter went home, and the rabbit went to its family. They were all safe and happy.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. They sat on the soft grass near a big tree. Amy's mom gave her a yummy sandwich to eat. Amy took a bite and smiled. She loved the taste of the sandwich. While eating, Amy saw a small bug on the ground. She wondered where it was going. The bug walked in circles and looked dizzy. Amy giggled as she watched the bug spin around. After eating her sandwich, Amy felt dizzy too. She spun around like the bug and laughed. Her mom joined her and they spun together. They had a fun day at the park.

One day, a little turkey named Tim was walking in the farm. Tim was an attractive turkey with big, bright feathers. He liked to play with his friends, the pig and the cow. Tim had a plan to play a fun game with his friends. He went to the pig and the cow and said, "Let's play hide and seek today!" The pig and the cow were excited and said, "Yes, let's play!" So, they started to play the game. Tim closed his eyes and counted to ten while the pig and the cow hid. When Tim opened his eyes, he looked around the farm to find his friends. He found the pig hiding behind a big tree and the cow hiding in the tall grass. After playing for a while, they all got tired and sat down to rest. They were happy that they had so much fun together. Tim's plan to play hide and seek had made their day very special.

Lila and Ben were playing with blocks in the garden. They liked to build towers and castles and bridges. Sometimes they pretended to be kings and queens and knights. "Look at my castle!" Lila said. "It has a big gate and a flag and a moat. It is the best castle ever!" Ben looked at her castle and felt sad. His castle was smaller and had no flag or moat. He wanted to have a castle like Lila's. He thought she was very rich and had more blocks than him. He said, "Your castle is not so good. It is too big and it will fall down. My castle is better. It is strong and safe and cozy." Lila was angry. She did not like Ben's words. She thought he was mean and jealous. She said, "No, your castle is not better. It is too small and it will break. My castle is better. It is beautiful and grand and fun." They started to argue and shout. They did not want to play together anymore. They wanted to have the best castle and the most blocks. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. They turned around and saw a firework in the sky. It made a bright spark and a boom. It was very pretty and scary. They stopped arguing and looked at the firework. They felt amazed and curious. They forgot about their castles and blocks. They said together, "Wow! What is that?" A voice replied, "That is a firework. It is for the festival. Do you want to see more?" They looked and saw a man with a hat and a coat. He had a box with more fireworks. He smiled and waved at them. They nodded and ran to him. They wanted to see more fireworks. They thought they were very rich and had more sparks than anyone.
