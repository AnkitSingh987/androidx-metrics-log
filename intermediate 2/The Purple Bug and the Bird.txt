Category: Intermediate
Title: The Purple Bug and the Bird

One day, a purple bug went out to find some food. It liked to eat soil. The purple bug walked and walked until it found a big pile of soil. The bug was very happy and started to eat. As the purple bug ate the soil, it saw a big bird up in the sky. The bird saw the bug too and wanted to eat it. The purple bug was scared and hid under the soil. The bird came close but could not find the bug. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird saw the soil and started to eat it too! The purple bug and the bird both liked to eat soil. They became friends and ate soil together. From that day on, the purple bug was not scared of the bird anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind and honest boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw and make prints of his drawings. He would share his prints with all his friends, and they loved them too. One day, Tim found a broken toy car in the park. He knew that the toy car belonged to his friend, Sam. Tim wanted to help his friend, so he decided to restore the toy car. He worked hard to fix it, and soon it looked like new again. Tim gave the restored toy car back to Sam, who was very happy. Sam thanked Tim for being an honest friend and for fixing his toy. All the other kids in the town learned from Tim's actions, and they too started being honest and helpful to each other. The town became a better place, and everyone lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is: always be honest and help others when you can.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sarah. She was three years old, and she had a very special tire. It was a big, pink tire that she loved to play with. One day, Sarah decided to take her tire outside and show it to her friends. She said to them, "Hey, look at this tire! It's pink!" Her friends were amazed! They had never seen such a beautiful tire before. They asked Sarah, "Where did you get it?" Sarah said, "I found it in the park. It was just lying there all alone, so I picked it up". Sarah's friends asked her, "What are you going to do with it?" "I'm going to tell my mom all about it," Sarah said. So she took the tire back home and told her mom all about the pink tire she had found. Her mom was so proud of her for finding such a special tire. The End.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a round ball. The ball was part of a band. The band had many balls, all different colors. The band would roll and play together every day. One day, a careless ball was rolling too fast. The ball did not see the big hole in the ground. The other balls in the band tried to warn the careless ball, but it was too late. The careless ball fell into the hole and could not get out. The band of balls knew they had to help their friend. They all rolled together and made a long line. The first ball in the line went into the hole and the careless ball climbed on top. One by one, the balls in the line helped each other out of the hole. Finally, the careless ball was safe. From that day on, the careless ball learned to be more careful when rolling and playing in the park.

One day, a messy dog named Spot saw a cup. The cup was on a table. Spot wanted the cup, but he was too short to reach it. He jumped and jumped, but he could not get the cup. Spot's friend, a bird named Tweet, saw him trying to get the cup. Tweet asked, "Why do you shout, Spot?" Spot said, "I want the cup, but I can't reach it!" Tweet wanted to help Spot, so he flew up to the table and got the cup for him. Spot was very happy to have the cup. He and Tweet played with the cup all day. They had so much fun together. In the end, they were both messy but they did not care. They were happy to be friends and share the cup.
