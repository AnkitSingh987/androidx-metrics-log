Category: Intermediate
Title: Amber the Girl and the Sleep

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amber. Amber was a very stubborn girl. Every night, Amber's parents would tell her to go to sleep. But no matter how hard they tried, Amber was not going to sleep. One night, Amber had a brilliant idea. Instead of following her parent's orders, she would just stay awake all night. She snuck downstairs and opened the cupboard. Amber found some yummy treats and she was ready to eat them all. Just as Amber was about to take a bite, her parents burst in. "What are you doing?" they shouted. Amber sheepishly said, "I didn't want to go to sleep, so I thought I could stay up and eat some treats." Her parents smiled at Amber. They were happy that she was being so stubborn. But they also knew that it was bedtime, so they said, "We know you don't want to sleep, but it's very important for you. So let's all cuddle up in bed and get some sleep." Amber agreed and the family all snuggled up together in bed. After a few minutes, Amber finally fell asleep. And from that day onwards, Amber was always in bed when it was time to go to sleep.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big, bad cloud in the sky. He was scared because he saw lightning. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, there is lightning outside!" His mom replied, "Don't worry, Tim. We are safe inside our house." Tim and his mom sat by the window and watched the lightning. They saw it light up the sky. Tim's mom told him that sometimes, lightning can be bad, but it can also be beautiful. As they watched the lightning, Tim's mom said, "See, Tim? It's not so bad when we are together." Tim smiled and replied, "Yes, Mom. I feel safe with you." And they continued to watch the lightning until it was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cars and trucks. One day, Tim saw his mom using scissors to cut paper. He thought it looked fun and wanted to try it too. Tim found some scissors and started to cut paper. He heard a voice behind him say, "Tim, what are you doing?" It was his mom. She looked at him with a guilty face. Tim knew he should have asked for help before using the scissors. His mom said, "Tim, scissors can be sharp and dangerous. You should always ask for help before using them." Tim felt guilty and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I will ask for help next time." His mom hugged him and they cut paper together, making fun shapes and having a good time.

Anna and Ben love to play in the snow. They make snowballs and snowmen and snow angels. One day, they see a big pile of snow near the park. They wonder what it is. "Let's go and see!" Anna says. "OK!" Ben says. They run to the pile of snow and find a hole in it. They peek inside and see a small room made of snow. It is an igloo! "Wow!" Anna says. "Someone made an igloo!" "Can we go inside?" Ben asks. They crawl into the igloo and look around. They see a blanket, a lantern, and some books. They also see a picture of an old man with a white beard and a furry hat. "Who is he?" Anna asks. "Maybe he lives here," Ben says. They hear a voice outside the igloo. It is the old man! "Hello, hello! Who is in my igloo?" he says. Anna and Ben are scared. They hide under the blanket. "Please don't be mad," Anna says. "We just wanted to see your igloo," Ben says. The old man opens the hole and sees them. He smiles. "Don't be afraid, little ones. I'm not mad. I'm happy to have visitors. I made this igloo for fun. I love the snow. Do you love the snow too?" he says. Anna and Ben nod. "Yes, we love the snow!" they say. The old man invites them to sit with him. He tells them stories about his life in the north, where he used to live in a real igloo. He shows them his books and his lantern. He gives them hot chocolate and cookies. Anna and Ben like the old man. He is kind and funny. They listen to his stories and ask him questions. They forget about the time. They have a lot of fun in the igloo.
