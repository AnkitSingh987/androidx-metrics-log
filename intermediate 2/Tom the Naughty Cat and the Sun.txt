Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Naughty Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a naughty cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with cords. One day, Tom found a long cord in the house. The next day, when the sun was up, Tom tried to wake his friend, the dog named Max. Tom took the cord and tickled Max's nose. Max woke up and saw the cord. He wanted to play too! Tom and Max played with the cord all day. They had so much fun! They learned that even though Tom was sometimes naughty, they could still be good friends and have a great time together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very upset because her friends went on a holiday without her. Lily's mom saw her sad face and wanted to help her feel better. One day, Lily's mom had a fun idea. She said, "Let's make our own holiday at home!" Lily's eyes lit up with excitement. They made yummy food, played games, and even danced together. Lily's mom encouraged her to have fun and forget about being upset. But then, something unexpected happened. Lily's friends came back from their holiday and surprised her with a big gift. They said, "We're sorry we left you, Lily. We wanted to bring you something special!" Lily was so happy and learned that even when things don't go as planned, good things can still happen.

Once upon a time there was a pig who liked to travel. He had many adventures and was always so happy. One day he decided to travel to a clear river. It was so pretty, he just had to take a look. When he arrived, he saw many fish swimming around and the water shimmered under the sun. He thought it was so exciting! He splashed around and had loads of fun, laughing and jumping about. When it was time to go, he said goodbye to the fish and the sparkling river. He had had such a nice time, he was sad to leave. But he was sure he'd be back again soon!

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in a park. A little boy named Sam liked to play near the tree. One day, Sam saw a bird in the tree. The bird seemed sad and distant. Sam wanted to help the bird. Sam asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I lost my heart. I cannot find it." Sam wanted to help the bird find its heart. They looked near the tree, under the leaves, and in the grass. They could not find the heart. Then, something unexpected happened! A squirrel came down from the tree with a shiny red heart. The squirrel said, "I found this heart. Is it yours, bird?" The bird was so happy! The bird's heart was found, and they all played together near the big tree.

One day, a furry cat named Kitty wanted to join a club. She saw a dog club and thought it would be fun. Kitty went to the dog club and asked, "Can I join your club?" The dogs looked at her and said, "You are a cat. You cannot join our club. You must be a dog." Kitty was sad but she wanted to succeed. She thought of a plan to join the club. Kitty found a furry dog costume and put it on. She went back to the dog club and said, "Woof! I am a dog. Can I join your club now?" The dogs looked at her and said, "You look like a dog, but you must do a dog thing to join our club." Kitty thought hard and then she chased her tail. The dogs laughed and said, "You did a dog thing. You can join our club now." Kitty was happy. She succeeded in joining the dog club and made many new friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his dad. They wanted to choose a toy. Tim saw a big red motorcycle. He liked it a lot. He asked his dad, "Can I have the motorcycle, please?" His dad looked at the toy and said, "Yes, it looks harmless. You can have it." Tim was very happy. He took the motorcycle home and played with it all day. Later, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw the motorcycle and said, "Wow! That's a cool toy!" They played with the motorcycle together, taking turns and having fun. They knew it was harmless, so they shared and laughed all day.
