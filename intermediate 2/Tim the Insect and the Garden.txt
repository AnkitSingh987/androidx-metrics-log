Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Insect and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little insect named Tim. Tim was very small and had six legs. He lived in a big garden with many flowers and trees. Tim liked to play with his friends, the ants, and the bees. One day, Tim felt embarrassed because he could not find his food. He looked under the flowers and behind the trees, but he could not find it. Tim knew he needed help to find his food. He asked his friends, the ants, and the bees to help him look for it. They all looked for Tim's food together. They looked high and low, near and far. Finally, they found Tim's food under a big rock. Tim was so happy and thanked his friends for helping him. He was not embarrassed anymore because he knew that sometimes, everyone needs help from their friends. And they all played happily in the garden together.

One day, a boy named Tim had a cool game. He loved to play it all day. But one day, the game broke. Tim was very sad. He wanted to repair the game. Tim asked his mom for help. She looked at the game and said, "I think I can fix it." Tim was happy. They worked together to repair the game. After some time, the game was fixed. But when Tim played the game, something unexpected happened. The game was not the same anymore. Now, it was even more fun! Tim was so happy that his mom helped him repair the game. They played the new, cool game together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a deaf dog, but he was very good at understanding what his friends said with their eyes and body. Spot had many friends who loved to play with him. One day, Spot and his friends went on a picnic. They brought lots of yummy food like apples, bananas, and sandwiches. Spot's friends told him not to touch the food until everyone was ready to eat. Spot tried his best to wait, but the food smelled so good! When it was finally time to eat, Spot's friends said, "Okay, Spot! Now you can touch the food!" Spot was so happy, he wagged his tail and ate the yummy food with his friends. They all had a great day together, and Spot learned that waiting can be hard, but it's worth it when you get to share with your friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Ann. Ann was only three years old, but she was very brave. One day, Ann went walking in the forest with her mom. They were picking apples when Ann heard a loud noise. It was a big bear! Ann started to rush away. The bear was running after her! Ann was so scared. She felt weak. Her mom saw her and screamed. The bear stopped and turned towards Ann's mom. Ann quickly grabbed an apple from the tree and threw it at the bear's head. The apple was filled with jam and the bear was so surprised that it ran away. Ann was happy she was safe. She gave the jam-filled apple to her mom as a present. Her mom was so proud of how brave Ann was. They hugged each other and rushed home to tell the story to Ann's dad.

Tom and Lily liked to play with their toys in the living room. They had many toys, like cars, dolls, blocks, and animals. But they did not like to organize their toys after they played. They left them on the floor, on the couch, on the table, and everywhere. Their mom was not happy with them. She said, "You have to organize your toys, or you will lose them. And you will make a mess for everyone." One day, mom brought home a new toy for them. It was a statue of a lion. It was big and shiny and looked very real. Tom and Lily were very excited. They wanted to play with the statue. They put it on the floor and pretended it was their pet. They gave it a name, Leo, and fed it pretend food. They had a lot of fun with Leo. But they forgot to organize their other toys. They left them all over the living room, as usual. Mom came back from work and saw the messy living room. She was very angry. She said, "How many times do I have to tell you to organize your toys? Look at this mess! You are very naughty children. And what is this statue doing here? It is not a toy. It is a gift for your aunt. She is coming to visit tomorrow. You have to give it back to me right now." Tom and Lily did not want to give back Leo. They loved Leo. They said, "No, mom, please. Leo is our friend. We want to keep him. He is our toy. Please, mom, please." Mom did not listen to them. She said, "No, you cannot keep Leo. He is not yours. He is your aunt's. And you have to learn to organize your toys, or you will have no toys at all. I am taking Leo away, and I am putting all your toys in a box. You will not get them back until you show me you can be responsible and tidy." Mom took Leo and put him in a bag. She also took all their toys and put them in a big box. She locked the box and put it in the closet. She said, "You can only have your toys back when you clean up the living room and apologize to me and your aunt. Now go to your room and think about what you did." Tom and Lily cried and cried. They had no toys and no Leo. They felt very sad and sorry. They wished they had organized their toys and listened to their mom. They learned a hard lesson. The end.
