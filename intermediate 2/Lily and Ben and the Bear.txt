Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Bear

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big hill. They want to climb the hill and see what is on the other side. "Let's go, Lily!" Ben says. "Maybe we can find something cool!" "OK, Ben!" Lily says. "But we have to be careful. Mom said there are bears in the woods." They start to climb the hill. They are happy and excited. They do not see the signs that say "Danger! Keep out!" When they reach the top of the hill, they hear a loud roar. They turn around and see a big, brown bear. The bear is angry and hungry. It runs towards them. "Run, Lily, run!" Ben shouts. "The bear wants to eat us!" They try to run, but the bear is faster. It catches them and bites them. They scream and cry, but no one can hear them. The bear drags them to its den and eats them. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a generous boy. He liked to help people. One day, Tim saw a big drain. The drain was full of water. Tim wanted to help. Tim had an idea. He would measure the water. He asked his friend, Sam, "Will you help me measure the water?" Sam said, "Yes, I will help you." They used a big cup to measure the water in the drain. But, they were not careful. The water in the drain started to move fast. Tim and Sam were scared. The water took them away. They learned to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a polite train named Tom. Tom loved to help people. He would carry them from one place to another. He would always smile and say "hello" to everyone. One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to go to her grandma's house. She went to Tom and said, "Can you please take me to grandma's house?" Tom was happy to help. He said, "Of course, hop on!" Lily made a request and Tom was glad to do it. As Tom started to move, he made steam come out of his top. The steam helped him go faster. Lily was excited and waved to her friends as they went by. They reached grandma's house quickly. Lily thanked Tom and gave him a big hug. Tom was happy to help and went back to the village to help more people.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog. He had a red collar, and everyone thought it was the prettiest collar! One day, the dog was walking around and he noticed a new store. He had never seen it before and was curious, so he decided to go in. As he walked around inside, he started to get tired. He thought to himself, "What a fun store, I wish I could stay longer!" Just then, a customer noticed the cute little dog. She said to him, "What a cute pup! Are you going to store something?". The pup replied, "No, I just came in to look around!" The customer couldn't help but smile at the pup's enthusiasm for the store. She said, "Well, why don't you stay a bit longer? I'm sure the store owner wouldn't mind!" The pup gladly agreed, and stayed for a while longer. He enjoyed looking at all the different items in the store, and eventually went home with a smile on his face and his red collar shining brightly.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird. The bird liked to sing with a happy voice. Every day, the bird would sing and play with its friends. One day, something strange happened. The bird's voice started to change. The bird was sad because it could not sing like before. The bird's friends did not know what to do. Then, a wise old owl came to help. The owl said, "Do not be sad, little yellow bird. Your new voice is special." The bird tried to sing again, and it was true! The new voice was even more beautiful than before. The bird and its friends were happy and they all sang together.
