Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bug and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The bug liked to crawl all day long. One day, the bug saw a bright flower. The flower was very pretty. The bug wanted to crawl to the flower. As the bug crawled closer, it saw a spike on the flower. The bug felt scared. It did not want to get hurt by the spike. The bug thought and thought. It had an idea to crawl around the spike. The bug crawled around the spike very carefully. It reached the bright flower and was very happy. The bug played on the flower all day long. The bug learned that it could be safe and have fun if it was careful.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He lived on a farm with his mommy and daddy. Jack wanted to ride his bike, but it was broken. So Jack asked his mommy if he could ride the horse on the farm. She said yes, so Jack was very happy. He went to the stable and saw the horse. The horse was white and Jack thought it was so big! He was a bit scared, but his daddy said it was ok. Jack got on the horse and rode around the farm. He laughed and had so much fun. After a while, Jack was a bit tired but happy. He got off the horse and gave it a big hug. The end.

Once there was a little girl called Daisy. Daisy was just three years old and she loved to spin round and round. One day, Daisy noticed that she couldn't find her favourite shade. She asked her mum, "Where's my shade?". But her mum said, "I don't know, I think it must have gone away". Daisy started to cry and said "But I need my shade!" Suddenly, Daisy's dad came in carrying a flexible shade. He said to Daisy, "I found this shade outside and it looks perfect for you. Will you give it a try?". Daisy's eyes lit up and she decided to take the flexible shade. She said "Thank you Daddy, I love it!" Daisy was so excited that she started to spin round and round again, this time with the flexible shade. She was so happy that she found her perfect shade that nothing bad could spoil it. The End!

One day, a little boy named Tim had to go to his tutor. Tim did not want to go. He wanted to play with his toys. But his mom said, "You must go, Tim." At the tutor, Tim had to learn. He did not like it. He wanted to go home. But then, the tutor showed him a fun game. Tim played the game and learned. He was happy. In the end, Tim was grateful for the tutor. He learned to play and learn at the same time. The conflict was gone. The moral is that sometimes we have to do things we don't want to do, but we can still find joy in them.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Bob. Bob was a very generous man. He loved to help people and make them happy. One day, Bob had a big idea. He wanted to do something special for his friends. Bob thought of a surprise for his friends. He made a big cake with lots of colors. The cake had red, blue, and yellow on it. He put it in a big box and went to his friends' house. When Bob got to his friends' house, he knocked on the door. His friends opened the door and saw the big box. They were so happy and surprised! They all ate the cake together and had a great time. Bob was happy that he could make his friends smile with his generous surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a little house. In the house lived a nice family: mom, dad, and their little boy, Tim. They were very happy. One day, Tim saw some smoke outside. He was scared and told his mom and dad. Mom and dad were serious. They said, "We must close all the doors and windows!" Tim helped them close everything. They were safe inside the house. But the smoke kept coming closer. They did not know what to do. Then, a big red truck came to their house. The truck had a long hose that could spray water. The truck sprayed water on the smoke, and it went away. The family was so happy! They thanked the truck and the people who helped. The unexpected thing was that the smoke was from a big birthday cake for Tim. His parents had planned a surprise party for him. Tim and his family had a great time at the party.
