Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Mighty Horse and the Farm

Once upon a time, in a big, big farm, there was a mighty horse named Tom. Tom was very strong and he loved to help the farmer. One day, the farmer asked Tom to gather hay from the field. "Tom, can you help me gather hay today?" asked the farmer. Tom said, "Yes, I will help you!" So, they went to the field and started to gather the hay. Tom was very happy to help. As they were gathering hay, a big storm came. It was very dark and windy. The farmer and Tom were scared. They tried to gather the hay faster, but the wind was too strong. The hay blew away in the wind. "Oh no, Tom! The hay is gone!" cried the farmer. Tom was sad too. They went back to the farm, feeling very sad. They did not have any hay for the animals to eat. The next day, the animals were hungry. Tom and the farmer were still sad. They knew they had to find more hay for the animals. But the storm had made it very hard to find hay. It was a bad ending for Tom and the farmer.

One day, a little boy named Tim went out to play. He had a lot of fun playing with his ball. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. But soon, the sky became dark. Tim looked up and saw big gray clouds. He knew it was time to go home. As he started to walk home, he heard a loud yell. Tim was scared. He looked around and saw a small dog. The dog was lost and wanted to find his home too. Tim knew he had to help the dog. Tim and the dog walked together, looking for their homes. They were scared of the dark, but they knew they had to be brave. After a while, they found Tim's home. Tim's mom was waiting for him. She was happy to see him and the dog. Tim's mom helped the dog find his home too. In the end, everyone was safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked Lily to make a sweet treat for their guest, Mr. Bear. Lily thought and thought about what to make. She remembered that Mr. Bear loved coffee. So, she decided to make sweet coffee for him. She managed to find all the things she needed: coffee, sugar, and milk. Lily mixed the coffee, sugar, and milk in a big cup. She tasted it and said, "Yum! This is so sweet!" When Mr. Bear came over, Lily gave him the sweet coffee. Mr. Bear took a sip and smiled. He said, "Thank you, Lily! This is the best sweet coffee I have ever had!" Lily felt so happy that she could make her friend smile.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a pretty rose in the garden. The rose was red and smelled very nice. Lily wanted to show her friend, Tom, the beautiful rose. Lily called Tom and said, "Come see the rose! It is so pretty!" Tom came running to see the rose. He looked at the rose and said, "Wow, it is very pretty!" Lily and Tom went to the kitchen to have a snack. They ate some delicious apples. They talked about the pretty rose and how much they liked it. They were very happy to have seen the rose and to have shared the delicious apples together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. It was very comfortable to play with. A little girl named Emma loved to play with the ball every day. One day, while Emma was playing with the ball, a big wind came. It blew the ball far away. Emma was sad and waited a minute, hoping the ball would come back. But the ball did not come back. Instead, a big, mean dog found the ball. The dog wanted to destroy the ball. It bit the ball and the ball popped. The ball was gone forever, and Emma was very sad.
