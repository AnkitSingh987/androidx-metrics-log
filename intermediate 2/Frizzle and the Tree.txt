Category: Intermediate
Title: Frizzle and the Tree

Once upon a time, there lived a young fox called Frizzle. He was very modest and quite shy. He believed that he could not do many things as he was too small. One day, Frizzle decided to have a go at climbing the tallest tree in his forest. He was very nervous, but he was determined to try. As he climbed up the tree, he stumbled and fell. Frizzle was ready to give up when he realised that his friends had been watching him the whole time. They cheered him on and encouraged him to carry on. He felt courageous and climbed back up the tree. Finally, he reached the top and looked down in triumph. His friends cheered and clapped for him - it was a victorious moment! The moral of this story is that we should always encourage others. With some encouragement from our friends, we can do great things that we never thought we could.

Lily was a patient in a big hospital. She had to stay in a bed with wires and tubes. She did not like it. She wanted to go home and play with her toys and her dog. One day, she saw a bird outside her window. It was blue and yellow and very pretty. It sang a happy song. Lily wished she could be like the bird and fly away. She looked around and saw that no one was watching. She pulled off the wires and tubes and got out of the bed. She put on her shoes and coat and opened the door. She ran down the hall, looking for the way out. "Hey, where are you going?" a nurse shouted. She tried to catch Lily, but Lily was too fast. She dodged and weaved and found the stairs. She ran down the stairs and saw the exit. She pushed the door and ran outside. She felt the sun and the wind on her face. She saw the bird on a tree. It looked at her and sang again. "Hello, bird," Lily said. "You are different from the other birds. You are very pretty. Can I be your friend?" The bird nodded and flew down to Lily. It landed on her shoulder and nuzzled her cheek. Lily smiled and hugged the bird. "I escaped from the hospital," she said. "I don't want to go back. I want to be with you." The bird nodded again and flew up in the air. It circled around Lily and called to her. "Come with me," it said. "I will show you the world." Lily nodded and followed the bird. She ran and jumped and laughed. She felt free and happy. She did not look back.

Once upon a time, there was a gray engine. The engine liked to go fast and make loud noises. It lived in a big garage with other engines. They were all different colors, like red, blue, and yellow. One day, the gray engine saw a little boy with a toy car. The little boy wanted to play with the gray engine, but he was too small. The gray engine had an idea. It would share its toy car with the little boy so they could play together. The gray engine and the little boy played with the toy car all day long. They laughed and had a lot of fun. The other engines saw them playing and wanted to join in. So, they all shared their toy cars with the other kids. Everyone was happy and had a great time playing together. And the gray engine learned that sharing is a good thing to do.

One day, a boy named Tim had a bucket. He liked to fill it with sand and make sand castles. He was a patient boy who would listen to his mom and dad. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue wanted to play with Tim's bucket, but Tim did not want to share. They both got mad and did not play together. Tim's mom saw them and told them to listen. She said, "You need to share the bucket and play together." Tim and Sue listened to Tim's mom. They shared the bucket and made a big sand castle. They were happy and became good friends.
