Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Box

One day, a small cat named Tom went out to play. The sun was up, and the wind was gentle. Tom saw a big box and wanted to lift it. He tried and tried, but the box was too heavy for him. He felt sad and sat down. Soon, the sky turned dark, and a storm was coming. Tom knew he had to go home, but the box was in his way. He pushed the box, but it did not move. He felt scared and worried. Just then, a big, friendly dog named Max came to help. Max saw Tom's problem and wanted to help. Max used his strong legs to lift the box. It moved! Tom was so happy and surprised. He thanked Max, and they both went home before the storm came. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a cold place, there was a penguin. The penguin was very clean. He liked to play with his friends. One day, the penguin heard a loud noise. It scared him. The penguin looked around for his friends. He could not find them. He was sad. The penguin wanted to find his friends. He walked and walked, but he could not find them. The loud noise came back. The penguin was scared again. The penguin saw a big ice wall. He thought his friends were behind it. He pushed and pushed the ice wall. It was hard. But the penguin did not give up. He kept pushing. The ice wall broke. The penguin found his friends. They were happy to see him. The penguin and his friends played together. They were not scared of the loud noise anymore. They were happy and clean.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. Many people would come to watch games there. One day, a wise old owl named Ollie lived near the stadium. Ollie loved to watch the games, too. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the stadium. He was lost and didn't know where to go. He saw Ollie and asked, "Can you help me find my mom?" Ollie said, "Yes, I will help you." Ollie and Tim looked around the stadium. They asked people if they saw Tim's mom. Finally, they found her. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Ollie, for helping me!" Ollie was glad he could help. From that day on, Tim and Ollie became good friends and watched many games together at the stadium.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a small bird in a trap. The bird was sad and scared. Tim felt bad for the bird and wanted to help. He was a compassionate boy. Tim said, "Don't worry, bird. I will help you." He opened the trap and the bird flew out. The bird was very happy and thanked Tim. "Thank you for saving me," said the bird. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird changed into a magic fairy! The fairy said, "You were kind to me, so I will give you a wish." Tim thought for a moment and wished for a new friend to play with. The fairy waved her wand, and a new friend appeared. Tim and his new friend played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would sit on a tree and sing beautiful songs. All the animals in the forest would stop and watch the bird sing. They loved to listen to the bird's creative songs. One day, while the bird was singing, a big wind came and blew the bird's nest away. The bird was very sad and stopped singing. All the animals in the forest missed the bird's songs and wanted to help. They all worked together to build a new nest for the bird. The bird was so happy to see the new nest that the animals had made. The bird started to sing again, but this time, something unexpected happened. The bird's songs were even more beautiful than before. The animals in the forest learned that when they help each other, good things can happen. And they all lived happily ever after, listening to the bird's beautiful songs.
