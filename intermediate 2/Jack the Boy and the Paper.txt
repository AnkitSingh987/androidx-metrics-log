Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Boy and the Paper

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He loved playing with paper. One day he was happily playing when he noticed a picture on the paper. It was a beautiful print. He wanted to show it to Mommy. So he grabbed the paper but then he heard a rip. Jack looked down, and he saw the paper had a tear in it! He was so frustrated. He wanted to show Mommy his print, but he couldn't because of the tear. Jack started to cry. Then Mommy came running in. She saw the tear and asked what had happened. Jack explained with a few tears that he wanted to show her the print, but he made the tear. Mommy smiled and picked up the torn paper. She told Jack, “Don’t worry, Jack. I can help.” Then Mommy put some tape on the paper and fixed the tear. Then Jack showed Mommy the print, and she was very happy. From then on, Jack took care of paper and was careful not to tear anything.

One day, Tom and his mom went to buy a red ball. They walked to the big store. On the way, they saw lots of traffic. There were many cars, buses, and trucks. Tom saw a red bus and said, "Look, Mom! A big red bus!" When they got to the store, Tom found the red ball. He was very happy. He told his mom, "I love this red ball! Can we buy it?" His mom said, "Yes, we can buy it." But, when they went to pay, the red ball was gone! Tom was sad. He asked his mom, "Where is my red ball?" Then, they heard a noise. A dog was playing with the red ball! The dog was happy and wagged its tail. Tom's mom said, "The dog likes the red ball too. Let's buy another ball for you and give this one to the dog." Tom agreed and they all went home happy.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly bird called Jimmy the sparrow. Jimmy loved to collect twigs and leaves to make his nest. Every day, Jimmy collected the things he needed for his nest. He was very careful about the twigs and leaves he chose. One day, Jimmy was so pleased with his new nest. It was beautiful and cozy! Jimmy was proud of his work and was jolly every day. He looked after his nest and enjoyed flying around while looking for things to add to it. Jimmy even made friends with other birds who had nests nearby. They enjoyed playing together and helping each other collect things they needed to make their nests even cozier. Jimmy and his friends were so happy with their nests that they decided to make a competition. They wanted to see who could make the most beautiful, jolly nest in their neighbourhood. Jimmy and his friends worked hard for a few weeks and then showed each other their nests. Everyone agreed that Jimmy had made the most jolly, beautiful nest of them all! Everyone was very proud of him and Jimmy was very proud of himself too!

Once upon a time, there was a young dog named Max. Max had a long tail that he liked to chase. One day, Max saw a cat and wondered if he could be friends with it. So, Max walked up to the cat and said, "Hi, I am Max. Do you want to play with me?" The cat looked at Max and said, "I am not sure, Max. My tail is short, and I cannot chase it like you do. What can we play together?" Max thought for a moment and then said, "We can play catch with a ball. I will show you how." So, Max and the cat played catch and had lots of fun. As they played, other animals saw them and wanted to join in. Soon, there were many animals playing together. They all learned that even though they were different, they could still have fun together. The moral of the story is that we can be friends with others even if they are not like us.

One day, a kind girl named Mia found a big book. The book had many pages. Mia liked to look at the pages and see all the fun pictures. She saw a page with a cat and a dog playing. Mia showed the page to her friend Tom. "Look, Tom," she said. "This page has a cat and a dog. They are playing together. Do you want to play with me like the cat and dog?" Tom looked at the page and smiled. "Yes, Mia. I want to play with you. But can we play with my ball too?" Tom asked. Mia thought for a moment and then said, "I insist we play with your ball too. It will be fun like the cat and dog on the page." So, Mia and Tom played together with the ball, just like the cat and dog in the big book. They had lots of fun and were very happy.
