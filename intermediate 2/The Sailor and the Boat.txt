Category: Intermediate
Title: The Sailor and the Boat

Once there was a sailor who had never been to sea before. His friends wanted to introduce him to something really fun, so they took him out on a boat. The sailor was so excited to see what it was like! When the sailor got out on the water, he quickly noticed how big and blue it looked, and how the sun glittered off the top. He felt so peaceful and relaxed. Then he heard a splash and saw something bright and orange to the side of the boat. It was a school of fish! The sailor was mesmerised as the fish glided and splashed around him. Eventually his friends called him back to the boat and handed him a bowl of their special seafood stew. It smelled so tasty and the sailor couldn't wait to try it. He had lots of fun eating it and talking about all the amazing things he had seen. The sailor was so glad that his friends had introduced him to the sea. He enjoyed it so much, he couldn't wait to come back and explore again and again.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was eager to go outside and play. He put on his leather shoes and asked his mom, "Can I go out and play now?" His mom said, "Yes, but don't go too far." Tim went to the park and saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it, but it was stuck in a tree. Tim tried to get it, but he couldn't reach. A man came and said, "I can help you get the ball. Let me try." Tim was happy and let the man try. The man got the ball and gave it to Tim. They played for a while, but then it was time to go home. Tim took the ball and said bye to the man. When he got home, his mom was sad. She said, "That ball was not yours, Tim. You have to give it back." Tim was sad too, and he knew he had to do the right thing.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big car. The car was red and shiny. Tim wanted to touch the car. He asked his mom, "Can I touch the car?" His mom said, "No, Tim. It is difficult to touch the car. It is not ours." But Tim did not listen. He ran to the car and touched it. A man saw Tim touch the car. The man was angry. He shouted, "You should not touch my car!" Tim was sad and scared. He knew he did something wrong.

Once upon a time, little Jack was feeling lazy on a summer day. He didn't want to play outside or help his mom with house chores. Just then, he heard his mom calling from the kitchen, asking him to come help her light a candle. Jack’s mom said: “Come here, Jack! I need your help to light this candle.” Jack replied: “But I don't feel like doing it!” His mom smiled and said: “It’s okay, Jack, I understand how you feel. But if you help me light this candle, you will learn how to do something nice for others and how to be a helpful person.” Jack smiled and said: “Okay, Mommy! I will do it.” Jack got up and helped his mom light the candle. After they finished, Jack asked his mom why the candle was so important. She replied: “Lighting a candle is a special way to show our appreciation and to show how much we care for one another.” Jack nodded his head and thought to himself. From then on, he promised himself to always be helpful, no matter how lazy he felt.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty flower. It was pale and wanted to bloom. The flower lived in a big garden with many other flowers. They all loved to play and talk. One day, a boy came to the garden. He had a big can of paint. The flower said, "Hello, boy! What is that?" The boy said, "This is paint. I will paint you so you can be pretty like the other flowers." The flower was happy and waited for the boy to paint it. The other flowers watched and whispered. They knew the boy was not good at painting. The boy started to paint the flower. But he did not paint it well. The flower did not look pretty. It looked very bad. The flower cried and the other flowers felt sad. The flower never got to bloom. It stayed pale and sad. The boy never came back to the garden. The other flowers learned to be happy with how they looked.
