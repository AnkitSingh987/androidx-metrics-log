Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Joe and the Tree

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Joe. They were playing in the woods when Joe found a screw on the ground. "What is this?" Joe asked. "It's a screw," Jack said. "Let's see what we can do with it!" Jack and Joe carefully took the screw and put it in a tree. Then, Jack got out some water and poured it on the screw. The water made the screw dry very fast. When the screw was dry, the two friends decided to see what the screw could do. Jack used a stick to turn the screw, and soon they had a brand-new door in the tree. Now they had a place to play that was very peaceful. Jack and Joe smiled. They were so happy with their new door! From then on, they could play inside the tree whenever they wanted to make sure they had a peaceful place to call their own.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful dog named Max. Max had a key to his favorite toy box. One day, Max could not find his key. He looked everywhere. Max was sad. Max saw his friend, Cat. Cat wanted to help Max find his key. They both looked everywhere. They struggled to find it. Then, they saw a bird with the key in its beak. Max and Cat were surprised. They did not expect the bird to have the key. The bird flew away with the key. Max and Cat chased the bird. They wanted the key back. The bird dropped the key in a pond. Max and Cat were happy. They jumped in and got the key. Max and Cat were now best friends. They played with the toys together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, thick melon on the ground. Tim wanted to help his mom carry the melon home. So, they both picked it up and walked back to their house. When they got home, they put the melon on the table. Tim's mom went to the kitchen to get a knife to cut the melon. But when she was gone, the melon started to shake. Tim was scared, but he stayed to see what would happen. To his surprise, the melon broke open and out came a small, cute dog! Tim was so happy and couldn't believe it. He named the dog Melon and they became best friends. From that day on, Tim and Melon always helped each other and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bobo. He lived in a safe place near a valley. Bobo loved to play all day with his friends. They would run, jump, and yield to each other as they played their games. One sunny day, Bobo and his friends were playing near the valley. They were having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and took one of Bobo's friends away! Bobo and his friends were very scared. Bobo knew he had to save his friend. So, he bravely hopped into the valley. As he hopped, he saw the big bird had put his friend down. But, the big bird was not mean after all! It was just playing a game. The big bird gave Bobo's friend back, and they all played together. From that day on, they were all friends and played happily in the safe valley.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to go for a walk with her mom. One day, they went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Lily was very happy. While they were walking, Lily saw a pretty box on the ground. She wanted to take it home. So, her mom helped her pick it up. They decided to wrap the box with a pretty ribbon when they got home. When they got home, they found a ribbon and wrapped the box. Lily was very excited to open it. But when she opened the box, she found a big mess inside. The mess was sticky and smelly. It made Lily and her mom sad because it was not a nice surprise. The end.
