Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Puzzle

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to solve puzzles. She had a big book of puzzles that she would work on every day. One day, Amy found a new puzzle in her book. It had a picture with an unknown symbol. Amy had never seen this symbol before. She was very excited to solve the puzzle with the unknown symbol. Amy thought hard and looked at the symbol. She asked her mom and dad for help. They all worked together to solve the puzzle. In the end, they found out that the unknown symbol meant "love." Amy was very happy that she solved the puzzle and learned a new symbol.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a small garden where she planted a bulb. Every day, she would lead her dog, Max, to the garden to look at the bulb. They both hoped that one day, it would grow into a beautiful flower. One hot day, Lily and Max went to the garden to see if the bulb had grown. They saw a tiny green shoot coming out of the ground! They were so happy. They knew that the hot sun had helped the bulb grow. As the days went by, the shoot grew bigger and bigger. Lily and Max continued to lead each other to the garden to watch the flower grow. Finally, the flower bloomed into a big, bright, and beautiful flower. Lily and Max were very proud of their flower, and they knew that their love and care had helped it grow big and strong.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a bean. He showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a bean!" he said. His mom smiled and told him to plant it in the ground. So, Tim dug a hole and put the bean in it. The next day, Tim went to see if his bean had grown. But the bean was gone! It had vanished! Tim was very upset. He looked around and saw a small bird. "Did you take my bean?" he asked. The bird shook its head, "No, I did not take your bean." Just then, the ground shook, and a big beanstalk grew right in front of Tim. It was taller than the house! Tim was so happy that his bean had not vanished after all. He climbed the beanstalk and found a whole new world full of fun and play. And he was not upset anymore.

One day, a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Sue were going to a hotel. They needed to pack their bags. Sue was glad. She was going to help pack her bag. At the hotel, Sue saw a big dog. The dog's owner said, "Hi, I'm Tom. This is my dog, Max." Sue said, "Hi, I'm Sue. I like Max!" Tom said, "Max likes you too. He has a surprise for you later." Sue played with Max all day. Then, it was time for the surprise. Tom said, "Close your eyes, Sue." When Sue opened her eyes, Max had a big bag. It was full of toys! Sue was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Max!" Sue, Max, and Tom played with the toys for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a small bird named Toby. Toby lived in a big forest with many trees. He had a lot of friends, like other birds, squirrels, and rabbits. The forest was an incredible place with pretty flowers and yummy fruits. One day, Toby decided to find a new place to settle and make a new home. He flew all around the forest, looking at different trees. He saw a tall tree with lots of leaves and thought it would be a great place to live. Toby worked hard to build a nest in the tall tree. He gathered sticks and leaves to make it cozy. When the nest was ready, Toby settled in his new home. He was very happy and loved his new view of the incredible forest.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to create art with her paws. She would dip her finger in paint and draw on the ground. One day, Kitty met a new friend, a dog named Buddy. "Hi, I'm Buddy. What are you doing?" asked Buddy. Kitty said, "I'm making art with my finger. Do you want to try?" Buddy was excited and said, "Yes, I want to create art too!" Together, Kitty and Buddy dipped their fingers in paint and made beautiful pictures on the ground. They were both very happy and proud of their art. The brave little cat and her new friend, the dog, had lots of fun creating art together. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.
