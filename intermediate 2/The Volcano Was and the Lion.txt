Category: Intermediate
Title: The Volcano Was and the Lion

One day, a big volcano was in the forest. The volcano was not a regular mountain. It was very hot and sometimes it would roar. The animals in the forest were scared. They did not like the loud roar. A brave lion lived in the forest. He wanted to help the animals. He went to the volcano to see why it roared. The lion saw that the volcano was angry. The lion asked the volcano, "Why are you so mad?" The volcano told the lion, "I am mad because I have no friends. Nobody wants to be near me because I roar." The lion felt sad for the volcano. He said, "I will be your friend if you stop roaring." The volcano was happy to have a friend. It stopped roaring and the animals in the forest were not scared anymore.

Lily liked to play with yarn. She had many colors of yarn in her box. She made things with yarn, like hats and scarves and dolls. She used a big needle to poke the yarn and make loops. One day, Lily found a new yarn in her box. It was different from the other yarns. It was fuzzy and soft and shiny. It was pink and purple and blue. Lily wanted to make something with the new yarn. She took the big needle and tried to poke the yarn. But the new yarn was hard to poke. It was too fuzzy and soft and shiny. The needle got stuck in the yarn. Lily pulled and pulled, but the needle did not come out. The yarn got tangled and messy. Lily felt sad and mad. She did not like the new yarn. She threw the new yarn and the needle on the floor. She went to her mom and cried. Her mom hugged her and asked her what was wrong. Lily told her about the new yarn and the needle. Her mom said, "I'm sorry, Lily. That yarn is not for poking. It is for brushing. It is a special yarn for making hair for dolls. Let me show you how to use it." Her mom took Lily back to her box. She picked up the new yarn and the needle. She untangled the yarn and brushed it with a comb. She made a long braid with the yarn. She took one of Lily's dolls and sewed the braid to its head. She gave the doll to Lily and said, "Look, Lily. You have a new doll with beautiful hair. Do you like it?" Lily looked at the doll. It had pink and purple and blue hair. It was fuzzy and soft and shiny. It was different from the other dolls. Lily smiled and hugged the doll. She said, "Thank you, mom. I like it. It is a special doll. Can I make more hair with the new yarn?" Her mom nodded and said, "Yes, you can. But remember, this yarn is for brushing, not for poking. Do you understand?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, mom. I understand. This yarn is for brushing, not for poking. I will be careful with it." She took the new yarn and the comb and went to her box. She started to make more hair for her dolls. She was happy and proud. She liked the new yarn.

One day, a brown dog named Spike went to the store with his mom. Spike liked the store because it had many fun things to look at. His mom wanted to buy food for dinner, so they walked down the big aisles together. Spike saw a toy he liked. It was a big, red ball. He asked his mom if he could have it. His mom said yes, but only if he was a good boy at the store. Spike was very happy and held the ball tight. When they finished shopping, Spike and his mom went home. Spike played with his new red ball all day. He was very happy that he had been a good boy at the store. And his mom was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to paint. One day, she went outside to paint a pretty landscape. The trees were green, and the sky was blue. Lily was very happy. As she painted, a lively squirrel came by. The squirrel said, "Hi, Lily! What are you doing?" Lily replied, "I am painting a pretty landscape. Do you want to watch?" The squirrel said, "Yes, I would love to watch!" Lily and the squirrel had fun painting the landscape. But then, it started to rain. The rain made the paint run and the pretty landscape was not pretty anymore. Lily and the squirrel were sad. They went inside and the bad rain ruined their fun day.
