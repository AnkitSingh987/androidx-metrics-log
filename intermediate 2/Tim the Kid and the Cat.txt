Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Kid and the Cat

One day, a cute kid named Tim went for a walk. He liked to march like a big soldier. Tim marched up and down the street with a big smile on his face. As Tim marched, he saw a little cat. The cat was stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he stopped marching and tried to think. He got an idea! Tim started to march again, but this time, he marched around the tree. As he marched, the tree began to dance! The tree shook and the cat fell into Tim's arms. The cat was happy and safe. Tim learned that his march could do more than just make him happy, it could help others too.

Once upon a time, there was a crazy frog. The frog loved to swim all day. He lived in a big pond with many fish friends. One day, the frog and his fish friends had a swim race. They all swam very fast. The frog was very happy because he won the race. After the race, the frog and his fish friends played in the pond. They swam and jumped and had lots of fun. The crazy frog was very happy with his friends.

Once upon a time, a little cat named Kitty liked to travel. One day, Kitty went on a walk and saw a round ball. The ball was red and very big. Kitty wanted to play with the round ball. Kitty pushed the round ball with her paw. The ball rolled and rolled. Kitty was proud of how fast the ball went. She chased the ball and pushed it again. Kitty and the ball went far away. At the end of the day, Kitty was tired but happy. She had traveled far with the round ball. Kitty went home, proud of her fun day. She could not wait to play with the round ball again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a lot of clothes, but she thought they were all boring. She wanted to find new clothes that were fun and pretty. One day, Sue saw a pretty dress in a tree. She wanted it so much! Sue tried to get the dress from the tree. She climbed up the tree very carefully. But as she climbed higher, she started to feel scared. Sue's hands slipped, and she began to fall. She closed her eyes, scared of hitting the ground. But, Sue did not hit the ground. Instead, she fell right into a big pile of clothes. These clothes were not boring at all! They were fun, pretty, and just what Sue wanted. She was so happy! From that day on, Sue loved her new clothes and always remembered her adventure in the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pretty red brush. Sue loved to brush her long hair every day. One day, Sue could not find her brush. She felt anxious. Sue asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my red brush?" Her mom said, "No, but let's look for it together." They looked in the bedroom, the bathroom, and the living room. They still could not find the brush. Finally, they went to the kitchen. There, on the table, Sue saw her red brush. It was set next to her dad's big cup. Sue was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Mom!" Then, Sue took her brush and happily brushed her long hair.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who went to the beach. He loved playing in the warm sand and wading in the salty water. He felt so excited when he saw the shore nearby. After playing for a while, the little boy got tired and felt ready to go back home. He slowly made his way towards the shore, but the waves were too big! He felt very uncomfortable and worried about how he was going to get back. Suddenly, he spotted a boat nearby. He waved his arms in the air and shouted, “Help me, please!” The boat came closer and closer until it reached the little boy. The kind people in the boat helped him get on board and took him back safely to the shore. The little boy thanked the kind people and ran back home to his family. From that day onwards, he was always careful and only played in the water when it was calm.
