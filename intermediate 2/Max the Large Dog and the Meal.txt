Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Large Dog and the Meal

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Max. Max liked to eat a lot. Every day, Max would have a big meal with his family. Max's family loved him, but Max did not always behave. One day, Max saw a big meal on the table. It was not time to eat yet, but Max was very hungry. Max thought, "I want that meal now!" Max did not behave, and he jumped on the table to eat the meal. Max ate all the food on the table. His family came into the room and saw the mess. They were very sad. Max felt bad too. Because Max did not behave, he had to go outside and could not play with his family.

One day, a boy named Tim saw his neighbor, Mr. Smith. Mr. Smith was angry. He was looking at his broken fence. Tim wanted to help, so he went to Mr. Smith. He asked, "Why do you feel angry?" Mr. Smith said, "My fence is broken. I do not know who did it." Tim thought about it. He remembered seeing a big ball near the fence the day before. Tim told Mr. Smith about the ball. They went to find it. They found the ball in the park. A girl named Sue was playing with it. Tim and Mr. Smith talked to Sue. Sue said she broke the fence by accident. She was sorry. Mr. Smith was not angry anymore. They all fixed the fence together. The moral of the story is to always tell the truth and help others when they need it.

One day, a big red ambulance was driving down the road. Inside the ambulance, there were two helpers, named Tim and Sue. They were going to perform a show for kids at the park. They had tasty treats and fun games planned for everyone. As they got to the park, Tim and Sue started to perform their show. They sang, danced, and played games with the kids. Everyone was having a great time. Then, one of the kids said, "I want a tasty treat, please!" Tim went to the back of the ambulance to get the tasty treats. But, oh no! The tasty treats were all gone! Tim and Sue did not know what to do. The kids were sad and wanted their treats. Just then, a big, friendly dog came running to the park. The dog had a big bag in his mouth. The dog dropped the bag in front of Tim and Sue. They opened the bag and found all the tasty treats inside! The dog had found the treats and brought them back. Everyone was so happy and they all enjoyed the tasty treats and the show.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. She loved to sing and play with her toys. One day, her mom gave her a special gift. It was a microphone! Mia was so happy. Mia took her microphone everywhere she went. She would sing for her friends and family. One day, she met a boy named Tim. Tim was shy and didn't talk much. Mia thought it was important for Tim to sing too. She said, "Tim, do you want to try my microphone?" Tim was scared, but Mia was kind. She helped him hold the microphone. "Squeeze it tight," she said. Tim squeezed the microphone and started to sing. His voice was soft, but it was beautiful. Mia and Tim sang together, and they became best friends. From that day on, Mia and Tim would sing together all the time. They learned that singing made them happy and that sharing the microphone was important. They knew that sometimes, all you need is a friend to help you be brave and try new things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her flashlight. She would shine it all around her room and make fun shapes on the walls. One day, Sue's flashlight stopped working. She was sad and did not know what to do. Sue's mom saw her sad face and knew she had to help. She found some new batteries and put them in the flashlight. Sue's mom also gave her a spoiled apple to eat. Sue did not like the spoiled apple, so she threw it away. That night, Sue took her flashlight to bed with her. She used it to look at her book about animals. Sue covered her head with a blanket to make a secret fort. She felt happy and safe in her fort with her flashlight. Sue fell asleep with a big smile on her face.
