Category: Intermediate
Title: Olive the Graceful Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Olive. Olive lived in a big tree with her family. She loved to fly and play with her friends. One sunny day, Olive and her friends set out to find a new place to play. They flew over the green grass and the blue sky. Olive was so happy to be with her friends. At last, they found a beautiful park to play in. Olive and her friends played all day long. When the sun went down, they set off to go back home. Olive felt tired but happy after a fun day with her friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a store with his mom. He saw a man trying to sell toys. The man was confused because no one wanted to buy his toys. Tim felt the urge to help the man. Tim said, "Mom, can we help the man sell his toys?" His mom agreed and they started to think of a plan. They decided to tell all their friends about the toys. Soon, many people came to the store. They saw the toys and wanted to buy them. Tim's friends said, "Wow, these toys are so cool!" The man was happy because he could sell his toys. In the end, the man thanked Tim and his mom for their help. Tim felt proud that he could help someone in need. The man was no longer confused and he had a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a big, reliable dog named Max. Max loved to cook. He lived with a little girl named Lily. They lived in a small house near a big pine tree. One day, Max said to Lily, "Let's cook a yummy meal!" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Max! Let's cook!" They went to the kitchen and cooked a big pot of soup. They put in carrots, peas, and potatoes. Max and Lily cooked and cooked until the soup was ready. When the soup was done, Max and Lily sat under the big pine tree. They ate their soup and talked about their day. They were happy and full. Max and Lily loved to cook and eat together. And they knew they could always rely on each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to meet new friends. As he walked, he saw a nail on the ground. Tim was a careless boy, so he didn't see the nail and stepped on it. "Ouch!" he cried. A kind girl named Sue heard Tim's cry and ran to help him. "What's wrong?" she asked. Tim said, "I stepped on a nail and it hurts!" Sue looked at Tim's foot and saw the nail. She carefully pulled it out and said, "You need to be more careful, Tim." Tim and Sue became good friends. They promised to always help each other and to never be careless again. They played together every day and had lots of fun. And Tim never stepped on a nail again.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful old lady. She was always very thoughtful when helping her friends. One day, she was walking through the forest when she saw a young rabbit. The rabbit was lost and couldn't find its way home. The old lady was very kind. She asked the rabbit, "Where do you want to go?" The rabbit said, "I want to go home." The old lady used her helpful skills to guide the rabbit home. She knew how to use the sun, the trees and the rocks to help show the way. At last, the old lady and the rabbit arrived at the rabbit's home. The rabbit said a big thank you to the old lady and then hopped inside. The old lady looked up at the sky and smiled, thankful that she could help the rabbit. Little did she know that the rabbit's home was right in the middle of the forest she had already been walking through. The helpful old lady had used her thoughtful skills to guide the rabbit home, right where she had been walking in the first place.
