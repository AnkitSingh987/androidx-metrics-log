Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim lived near the shore, where the water met the sand. He loved to play on the shore and look for pretty shells. One day, while playing, Tim found a very big and special shell. It was the most expensive shell he had ever seen. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sam, the expensive shell. But Sam lived far, far away. Tim had an idea. He would mail the shell to Sam, so Sam could see it too. Tim put the shell in a box, wrote Sam's name on it, and took it to the mail place. After a few days, Sam got the box in the mail. He opened it and saw the expensive shell. Sam was so happy and called Tim to say thank you. Tim was glad that he could share the special shell with his friend. They both knew that sharing made their friendship even stronger.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with blocks. They make towers and houses and cars with the blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. One day, they find a new block. It is big and shiny and cool. It is a square. Tom and Mia have never seen a square block before. They want to play with it. "Can I have the square?" Tom asks. "No, I want the square!" Mia says. They both reach for the square. They pull and tug and push. The square falls to the floor. It makes a loud noise. Tom and Mia are scared. They look at the square. It is broken. It has a crack in the middle. Tom and Mia are sad. They like the square. They do not want it to be broken. "I'm sorry, Tom," Mia says. "I'm sorry, Mia," Tom says. They hug each other. They are still friends. They do not want to fight. They look at the square again. They see something inside the crack. It is a surprise. It is a sticker. It has a picture of a heart. It says "I love you". Tom and Mia smile. They think the square is nice. They think the sticker is nice. They think they are nice. They kiss the sticker. They kiss the square. They kiss each other. They are happy. They have a cool square. They have a cool sticker. They have a cool friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big forest. In the forest, there was a shiny crystal. The crystal was very pretty. Everyone in the forest liked the crystal. They wanted to touch it, but they knew it was dangerous. One day, a little bird saw the crystal. The bird wanted to fill its nest with the crystal's shiny pieces. The bird thought it would make the nest pretty. But the bird's mommy told it not to touch the crystal. It was dangerous, and the bird could get hurt. The bird listened to its mommy and did not touch the crystal. Instead, it found pretty leaves and soft grass to fill its nest. The bird's nest was safe and cozy. The bird was happy, and its mommy was proud. They lived happily in their cozy nest, and the dangerous crystal stayed in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Mark. Mark was an obedient dog. He liked to play with his toy case. One day, Mark saw a big ball in the park. He wanted to play with the big ball, but the big ball was far away. Mark's owner told him to stay near the toy case. Mark wanted to be obedient, but the big ball was so fun. He ran to the big ball, leaving the toy case behind. The sky became dark, and Mark felt scared. Mark played with the big ball, but soon he wanted to go back to his toy case. He tried to find his way back, but it was too dark. Mark was lost and couldn't find his toy case or his owner. He was very sad and missed his home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was three years old and loved exploring. One day, she decided to take a walk in her garden. She saw many wonderful and original things. There were trees with beautiful leaves, colourful flowers, a fluffy bunny and a big spot. What was in the spot? Lucy was curious and wanted to find out. She asked her mom: "Can I go explore the spot?" Her mom smiled and said: "Yes, of course. But don't forget to stay close". So Lucy went to the spot and she saw lots of amazing things there - she saw pretty stones, squishy mud, a funny-looking bug and many other things. Lucy was so happy with all the fun things she found. She collected the original stones and brought them back home. She put them in a special box, made with love by her dad, and was very proud of her collection. The end.
