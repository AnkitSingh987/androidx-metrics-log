Category: Intermediate
Title: Mr the Popular Toy and the Duck

Once upon a time, there was a popular toy named Mr. Duck. Mr. Duck loved to play in the bathtub. Every day, he would wait for his best friend, a little girl named Lily, to take him to the bathroom. One day, Lily did not want to take a bath. She wanted to play outside. Mr. Duck did not like this. He wanted to go to the bathtub. So, Mr. Duck insist to Lily that they go to the bathroom. He said, "Please, Lily, let's go to the bathtub." Lily saw Mr. Duck's sad face and agreed to take a bath. She filled the bathtub with warm water and bubbles. Mr. Duck was so happy! They played in the bathtub for a long time. After the bath, Lily and Mr. Duck went outside to play. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a comfortable home with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. They all loved each other very much. One day, Lily and Spot went outside to play in the sun. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. As they played, Lily saw a big tree with lots of leaves. She said to Spot, "Let's go close to the tree and play under it!" Spot barked happily, and they ran to the tree. They played under the tree for a long time, feeling cool and happy. Soon, it was time to go back home. Lily's mom called out, "Lily, Spot, it's time to come in!" Lily and Spot ran back to their comfortable home. They were tired from playing, but they had so much fun. Lily gave Spot a big hug, and they both went inside to eat dinner with their family.

Once upon a time, in a perfect little world, there lived a kind bird named Bluey. Bluey loved to fly high in the sky and sing sweet songs. Everyone in the world loved Bluey. One day, Bluey met a sad frog named Greeny. Greeny could not jump high, and he felt bad. Bluey said, "Don't be sad, Greeny. We can be friends, and I will help you jump high." Greeny smiled and felt happy. Bluey and Greeny played together every day. Bluey helped Greeny jump high, and Greeny taught Bluey how to swim. They had lots of fun, and they were the best of friends. In the end, they learned that helping each other made their world even more perfect. And the moral of the story is: when we help our friends, we make the world a better place.

Milly packed her bag. Inside the pack was a cherry. The cherry was cold. She put the cherry back in the bag and zipped it closed. She put the bag over her shoulder and went outside. Milly looked at the cherry. It was so cold - she didn't know what to do! She decided to put it in the sun. Milly sat in the garden with her bag by her side. After a while, she felt something in her bag. It was warm! The cherry was warm now. Milly felt happy. She smiled and ate the warm cherry. It was delicious!

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat was fast and liked to run. The dog liked to tell jokes. They were good friends. The cat saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. But the dog wanted to prevent the cat from getting the ball. The dog thought it was a joke and wanted to make the cat laugh. The cat ran fast to get the ball, but the dog was in the way. The cat tried to go around the dog, but the dog kept stopping the cat. The cat was sad and the dog saw this. The dog understood that the cat really wanted the ball. The dog moved out of the way and let the cat get the ball. The cat was happy and they played together with the ball. They were good friends again.

Lily liked to play with her toys, but she did not like to put them away. She always left them on the floor, on the bed, or in the closet. Her mom and dad told her many times to clean her room, but she did not listen. She said she was too busy or too tired. One day, Lily's mom had a surprise for her. She said she bought her a new toy and hid it in her room. Lily was very happy and ran to her room to find it. She looked under the bed, in the closet, and on the shelves, but she could not find it. She was about to give up, when she saw a drawer that was slightly open. She thought the toy might be inside. She pulled the drawer open, but instead of a toy, she saw a lot of her old toys that she had forgotten about. They fell out of the drawer and made a big mess on the floor. Lily was not happy. She was dizzy from looking so much. She wished she had put her toys away. Her mom came to her room and saw the mess. She said, "Lily, I'm sorry, but there is no new toy. I wanted to surprise you and show you how many toys you already have. But you never put them away, so you don't appreciate them. You should be thankful for what you have and take care of it. That is the real surprise." Lily felt sorry for being messy and ungrateful. She hugged her mom and said, "I'm sorry, mom. You are right. I have many toys and I love them. I will put them away and play with them more. Thank you for the surprise. I learned a lesson today." Lily and her mom cleaned the room together and had fun playing with the old toys. Lily was happy and proud of her room. She decided to surprise her mom and dad by keeping it clean from then on.
