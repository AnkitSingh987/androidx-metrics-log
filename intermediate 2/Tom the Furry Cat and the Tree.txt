Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Furry Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. He was very soft and loved to play with his friends. One day, Tom went to a big tree where all the animals gathered to play games. They were having fun, and Tom was excited to join them. A wise old owl, who was the judge of the games, said, "Today, we will play the 'squeeze through the hoop' game!" All the animals lined up to try. One by one, they jumped and squeezed through the hoop. It was Tom's turn, and he was very nervous. Tom tried to squeeze through the hoop, but he was too furry and got stuck! His friend, a little mouse named Jerry, saw him and said, "Don't worry, Tom! I will help you!" Jerry pushed and pulled until Tom was free. Everyone cheered, and Tom was so happy. The wise old owl then said, "Tom and Jerry, you both win the game! You showed us the importance of teamwork and helping each other." The animals clapped and cheered for Tom and Jerry, who became the best of friends. And from that day on, they always played and helped each other, no matter what the game was.

One day, a big bird named Tom was flying in the sky. He saw a distant store on the ground. He wanted to see what was inside the store. So, Tom flew down and went in. Inside the store, Tom met a small cat named Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tom! What are you looking for?" Tom said, "I don't know. I just saw the store from the sky and wanted to see what was inside." As they talked, a big wind blew the store door open. The wind was so strong that it picked up Tom and Sue and took them high into the sky. They were scared but held on tight to each other. The wind stopped, and they found themselves in a new, far away place. Tom and Sue became best friends and had many fun adventures together in their new home.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Tom. He was very good at helping people. One day, Tom was walking when he heard a little girl crying. He went to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "My toy is stuck in the tree, and I can't get it." Tom decided to help her. He climbed the tree and got the toy for her. The little girl was very happy and thanked Tom. Later, Tom saw a big emergency. A dog was stuck in a hole, and he could not get out. Tom decided to help the dog too. He found a long stick and put it in the hole. The dog climbed out using the stick. But the dog was not just any dog; it was the famous dog named Max! Max thanked Tom and said, "You are very kind. Let's be friends!" And from that day on, Tom and Max were best friends, and they helped people together.

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there was a wise turkey. This turkey was very smart and all the other animals liked him. He had many friends on the farm. One day, a naughty goat came to the farm. The goat liked to tease the animals. He would run and jump, making fun of the others. The wise turkey did not like this. The wise turkey had a plan. He talked to his friends and they all agreed to help. When the goat came to tease again, they all stood together. The goat could not tease them anymore. From that day on, the farm was a happy place for all.

Lily and Tom were playing in the store. They liked to look at the toys and the clothes and the books. They saw a big mirror on the wall. They ran to the mirror and made funny faces. "Look at me, I am a lion!" Tom said, and roared. "Look at me, I am a princess!" Lily said, and smiled. They laughed and laughed. Then they saw something shiny on the floor. It was a necklace with a big stone. Lily picked it up and put it on. "Wow, you are so pretty!" Tom said. "Thank you, this is my magic necklace!" Lily said. She looked at the mirror and saw the stone sparkle. She felt happy. But then a lady came to the store. She looked angry. She saw Lily and Tom and the necklace. "Hey, you two, what are you doing with my necklace?" she said. She took the necklace from Lily and looked at it. "This is not my necklace, this is a fake one!" she said. She threw the necklace on the floor and left. Lily and Tom felt sad. They liked the necklace. They did not know it was fake. They looked at the mirror and saw their faces. They did not smile. They decided to go home and play with their real toys. They did not like the store anymore.
