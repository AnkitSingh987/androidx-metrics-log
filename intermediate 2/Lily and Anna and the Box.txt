Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Anna and the Box

Lily and Anna are best friends. They like to play together in the park. One day, they see a big box near the swings. They are curious and want to see what is inside. They open the box and find many dresses of different colors and shapes. They are happy and excited. They want to try them on and pretend to be princesses. "Look, Anna, this dress is pink and has flowers. It is so pretty. Do you want to wear it?" Lily suggests. "Yes, Lily, I like it. And you can wear this dress. It is blue and has stars. It is so shiny. You will look like a fairy." Anna suggests. They put on the dresses and start to dance and sing. They feel like they are in a magic land. They have so much fun. But then, they hear a loud voice. It is the voice of a man. He sounds angry and confused. "Hey, you two, what are you doing with my dresses? These are not for you. These are for the theater. Give them back to me right now!" He shouts. Lily and Anna are scared and confused. They do not know who the man is. They do not know what theater is. They think they did something bad. They quickly take off the dresses and put them back in the box. They run away from the man and the box. They hold each other's hands and look for their moms. They are sorry and sad. They do not want to play with the dresses anymore. They just want to be safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a tough dog named Max. Max liked to talk a lot. One day, Max walked to the park to play with his friends. While playing, Max heard a sound. It was a sound he had never heard before. He followed the sound and found an opera show. Max was very curious. He wanted to join the opera. But the other animals at the opera thought he was too tough and loud to sing with them. Max was sad, but he didn't give up. He practiced singing every day, and soon he got better. One day, the opera animals heard Max singing. They were surprised by how good he was. They asked Max to join their show. Max was very happy. The opera animals learned that they should not judge others by how they look. And Max learned that if you work hard, you can do anything.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a tight network of friends. They liked to play together every day. One day, they wanted to set up a big game in the park. "Let's set up the game over there," said Tom. So, they all went to the park and started to set up the game. They were very happy and excited. But, while they were setting up the game, a big wind came and blew everything away. The tight network of friends was sad. They did not know what to do. Then, a little bird came and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." The little bird flew high in the sky and found all the things that the wind blew away. The tight network of friends was very happy and thankful. They set up the game again and played all day. From that day on, the little bird became a part of their tight network, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a loud dog named Max. Max liked to play and run all day. He was very happy and loved to bark. One day, Max met a small cat named Lily. Lily was very quiet and liked to study. She would sit and look at her toys for a long time. Max asked Lily, "Why do you study your toys? Let's play and have fun!" Lily said, "I like to learn about them. It's fun too!" Max and Lily decided to play together. They played with a ball that had weight. Max would throw the ball and Lily would try to catch it. They had a lot of fun and became good friends.
