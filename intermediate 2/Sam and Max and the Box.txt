Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and Max and the Box

Sam and Max were brothers who liked to play in the yard. They had a big pile of junk that their dad said he would throw away soon. The junk had old toys, boxes, cans, and other things. Sam and Max liked to pull things out of the junk and pretend they were treasure. One day, Sam and Max were feeling restless. They wanted to find something new and exciting in the junk. They pulled and pulled, but they only found more junk. They were about to give up, when Sam saw something shiny at the bottom of the pile. He pulled harder and harder, until he freed it from the junk. It was a metal box with a lock and a key. Sam and Max were curious. They wondered what was inside the box. They tried the key, but it did not fit. They tried to open the lock with a can opener, but it did not work. They tried to smash the box with a rock, but it did not break. They were frustrated and angry. They wanted to see what was in the box. They decided to take the box to their dad and ask him to open it. They carried the box to the house, but they did not see their dad. They saw a note on the door. It said: "Boys, I had to go to the store. I'll be back soon. Don't touch the junk. It's dangerous. Especially the metal box. It's a bomb. Love, Dad."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Joe who loved to play. One day, he decided to play catch outside with his friend. Joe was so excited that he ran and grabbed his favorite sock. His friend was amazed at the amazing sock - it was so flexible! Joe threw the sock to his friend, and his friend caught it with ease. After that, they kept playing catch and having lots of fun. Joe and his friend noticed that with each throw and catch they were getting better and better. By the end of the day, Joe and his friend were so happy. Their game of catch had made them both better at catching things. Joe's friend soon realized that it was the flexible sock that helped them the most. It taught him a valuable lesson: if you want to succeed, you have to start with the right tools. With the right tools, you can catch almost anything.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was eager to learn new things. One day, Tim saw his dad playing with a chess set. He wanted to touch the chess pieces. "Can I touch the chess?" Tim asked his dad. "Yes, you can," his dad said with a smile. Tim picked up a chess piece and looked at it. It was a funny shape. Suddenly, the chess piece started to talk! "Hello, Tim!" it said. Tim was surprised. He didn't know chess could talk. The chess piece told Tim stories about the chess world. Tim listened and laughed. In the end, the chess piece said, "Now, it's time for me to go back to the chess set." Tim put the chess piece back on the board. He knew he had found a new friend in the chess set.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to make friends with everyone in the forest. One sunny day, Remy hopped over to a big, round melon. He thought the melon was a new friend! "Hello, Melon!" Remy said. "Do you want to play with me?" But the melon did not say anything. Remy did not know that the melon was just a fruit and not a friend. Remy tried to make the melon laugh. He made funny faces and danced around. But the melon still did not say anything. Remy felt sad. Then, a kind squirrel named Sally came over. "Hi, Remy! What are you doing?" she asked. Remy told Sally about the melon. Sally laughed and said, "Silly Remy, that's not a friend. That's a fruit we can eat!" Remy and Sally shared the yummy melon. Remy learned that melons were not friends, but they could still make him happy. From that day on, Remy and Sally were the best of friends and had many fun adventures together.
