Category: Intermediate
Title: The Animal and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small animal. The animal was very happy and liked to play with his friends. One day, while the animal was playing, he started to run very fast. But, he did not see a big rock and he did a stumble. The animal fell down and felt sad. A kind bird saw the animal and flew down to help. The bird said, "Do not run too fast, little animal. It is not safe to run without looking where you are going. You can get hurt." The animal listened to the kind bird and said, "Thank you, I will be more careful." From that day on, the animal always looked where he was going and did not run too fast. He felt safe and happy. The animal and the bird became good friends. They played together in the forest and had lots of fun. The animal learned that it is important to be careful and safe when playing.

Once upon a time, there was a big, soft pillow. This pillow lived in a room with two little kids, named Tom and Sue. Tom and Sue loved to play with the pillow. They liked to throw it, jump on it, and sleep on it. But sometimes, Tom and Sue would disagree on how to play with the pillow. One day, Tom wanted to play a game where he threw the pillow up in the air. Sue wanted to play a game where they made a bed for their toys with the pillow. They could not agree, so they decided to play both games at the same time. But they were careless, and did not think about what could happen. As Tom threw the pillow up in the air, Sue put her toys on the floor to make the bed. The pillow came down and landed on the toys. The toys broke, and Sue was sad. Tom and Sue learned that they should not be careless and should not disagree when playing with the pillow. From that day on, they took turns playing with the pillow and were more careful.

One day, a nice dog named Max went to a lab. In the lab, there were many things to play with. Max was very happy to be there. Max saw a big red button. He wanted to press it. A man in the lab said, "Max, do not press that button! It will order something bad!" Max did not listen. He pressed the button. The lab went dark. Max heard a loud noise. A big box came out of the wall. Max was scared. The box opened and a big monster came out. The monster was not nice. Max and the man ran away, but the monster chased them. They could not go back to the lab.

One day, a boy named Tim went to his mom. He asked her, "Mom, what is for lunch?" His mom said, "We are having healthy food, Tim." Tim was happy because he liked healthy food. Tim saw a big bowl of sauce on the table. He asked, "Mom, can I have some sauce?" His mom answered, "Yes, you can have some sauce." Tim took a spoon and put some sauce on his food. Tim and his mom sat down to eat. They talked and laughed. Tim said, "The sauce is very tasty, Mom." His mom smiled and said, "I am glad you like it, Tim." They both enjoyed their healthy lunch together.

Once upon a time, there was a big bush. It had many leaves that spread wide. In the bush, there lived a bossy bird named Ben. Ben liked to tell all the other birds what to do. One day, a new bird named Tim came to the bush. Tim wanted to play with the other birds. But bossy Ben said, "No, you can't play with us. You must do what I say!" Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He asked the other birds, "Do you like bossy Ben telling you what to do?" The birds said, "No, we don't like it." So, Tim and the other birds decided to play together without bossy Ben. Bossy Ben saw the birds playing and having fun. He felt sad and lonely. Ben said, "I am sorry for being bossy. Can I play too?" Tim and the other birds said, "Yes, but you must be nice." From that day on, bossy Ben learned to be nice and play with the other birds. The bush was a happy place for all the birds to live and play together.
