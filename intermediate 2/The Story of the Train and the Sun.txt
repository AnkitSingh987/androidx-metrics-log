Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Train and the Sun

One day, a polite train was going down the track. It was a hot day, and the sun was shining bright. The train was happy to take people from one place to another. As the train went on, it started to feel too warm. "Oh no," said the train, "I feel like I might melt!" The train knew it had to find a way to cool down, but it did not know how. Just then, a big cloud came and covered the sun. The train felt cooler and did not melt. The people on the train were happy and thanked the cloud. The train was able to keep going and everyone was safe.

Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was a dependable girl who always kept her promises. One day, she told her friends that she would find the biggest stick in the forest. Lucy walked and walked, looking for the perfect stick. She saw many sticks, but they were too small or too thin. Then, she saw a big, strong stick on the ground. Lucy picked it up and tried to snap it, but it was too strong. She knew this was the perfect stick. Lucy took the big stick back to her friends. They were so happy and proud of her. They knew they could trust Lucy because she was a dependable friend who kept her promises. From that day on, they all played together with the big stick, and they were the best of friends.

Once there was a big red armchair. The armchair was in a room with many other chairs. But the red armchair was special. It was big and soft, and everyone liked to sit in it. The other chairs were a little bit sad because no one wanted to sit in them. One day, a little girl came into the room. She saw the big red armchair and wanted to sit in it. But when she tried to sit down, she fell on the floor. She felt embarrassed. She looked around and saw the other chairs. They were not as big and soft as the red armchair, but they were still nice. The little girl decided to sit in one of the other chairs. She felt happy and comfortable. The other chairs were happy too because someone finally sat in them. The big red armchair remained empty, but it was not sad. It knew that it was still special, and it was happy that the other chairs had a friend now.

Once upon a time, there was a weak little bird named Tim. Tim did not like the rain. He was too weak to fly when it rained. One day, it began to rain. Tim was sad because he could not go out and play with his friends. Tim had an idea. He found a big leaf and tried to attach it to himself. He thought the leaf could help him fly in the rain. He tried and tried, but the leaf would not stay on him. Tim was very sad. Just when Tim was about to give up, a big wind came. The wind picked up the leaf and attached it to Tim's back. Tim was so happy! He flew up into the sky with the leaf on his back. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. Tim's friends saw him flying with the leaf, and they all cheered. Tim had lots of fun flying with his new leaf friend. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to chew on things. One day, he found a stone in his yard. He thought it would be fun to chew on the stone. So, he picked it up with his mouth and started to chew. As Bobo chewed on the stone, he felt a terrible pain in his teeth. He did not like the pain, but he kept chewing on the stone. He wanted to see what was inside the stone. Maybe there was something good to eat, he thought. But the more Bobo chewed, the more terrible the pain became. His teeth started to hurt very badly. In the end, Bobo's teeth broke because of chewing on the stone. Poor Bobo could not chew on anything anymore, and he was very sad.
