Category: Intermediate
Title: The Colorful Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. This bird loved to sing and fly in the sky. One day, the bird saw a big tree. The tree had many pretty flowers. The bird wanted to make the tree its home. The bird went to the tree and said, "Hello, tree! Can I live with you?" The tree was very happy to have a new friend. The tree said, "Yes, bird, you are welcome to live with me. Let's be friends and enjoy our days together." So, the colorful bird made a nest in the tree. Every day, they would enjoy the sun and the wind. The bird sang happy songs, and the tree danced with its leaves. They were the best of friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree was so big and old that it started to lean. One day, a little bird came to sit on the tree. The bird was sad because it could not find any friends. The little bird had an urge to find a friend. It looked around and saw a dead log. The bird thought maybe there was a friend inside. The bird flew down and started to peck at the log. It pecked and pecked, but no friend came out. Just then, a squirrel came by. The squirrel saw the bird and asked, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I want a friend." The squirrel smiled and said, "I will be your friend." From that day on, the bird and the squirrel were best friends. They played together under the big leaning tree, and they were never lonely again.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went to the museum with his mom. Tim loved to see the big dinosaur bones. He was very happy. At the museum, Tim saw a big dinosaur bone. He wanted to touch it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I touch the bone?" His mom said, "No, Tim. We must not touch the bones. They are very old and can break." Tim did not touch the bone, but he leaned close to see it better. He saw a sign that said, "Eating healthy food helps us grow strong like the dinosaurs." Tim thought about the healthy food he ate at home. When they went home, Tim told his dad about the museum and the sign. His dad said, "That's right, Tim. Eating healthy food is important for us to grow strong and be like the dinosaurs." The moral of the story is to listen to your parents and eat healthy food to grow strong.

One day, a nice cat and a funny dog went to play. The cat said, "Let's joke and laugh." The dog agreed and they started to tell jokes. They laughed and played all day. The cat said, "My back hurts. Can you give me a massage?" The dog said, "Yes, I can do that." The dog gave the cat a massage. The cat felt better and they played some more. They saw a big, rare bird in the sky. The bird was not nice. It came down and took the cat away. The dog was sad and cried. The cat and dog could not play anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a friend named Tom. They liked to play together. One day, they saw a weird cat. The cat was big and had funny colors. Sue and Tom wanted to see where the cat would go. The cat walked and Sue and Tom followed it. The cat lead them to a big tree. Under the tree, there were many other cats. The cats were having a party. It was a special party called a marriage. The cats were happy and dancing. Sue and Tom thought the cat marriage was fun. They danced with the cats too. After the party, Sue and Tom went home. They told their moms and dads about the weird cat and the fun marriage. From that day, Sue and Tom always remembered the cat party.

One day, a little girl named Lily was eating her food on a plate. She was very happy because she loved her plate. It had her favorite color, pink, and a picture of a cute cat on it. She ate her food and smiled. Suddenly, her plate fell on the floor and broke into pieces. Lily felt very sad and began to cry. Her mom heard her and came to see what happened. She saw the broken plate and the poor little girl crying. Lily's mom hugged her and said, "Don't cry, my dear. We can get you a new plate." They went to the store and found a new plate with a cute dog on it. Lily loved her new plate and stopped crying. She was happy again and ate her food on her new plate.
