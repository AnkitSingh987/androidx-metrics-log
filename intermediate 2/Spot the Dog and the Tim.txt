Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a loyal dog. He always smiled and played with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They had a lot of fun together. One day, Tim and Spot saw a big water leak near their house. The water was everywhere! Tim and Spot did not know what to do. They tried to stop the leak, but it was too big. Tim and Spot were sad because they could not stop the leak. The water reached their house and made a big mess. Tim and Spot had to leave their home. They were not able to fix the leak, and they could not smile anymore.

George was a jolly little boy who loved to explore. One day he found an object that he had never seen before. He looked at it and nodded his head. He couldn't tell what it was, so he decided to ask his mom. "Mommy, what is this?" asked George as he showed the object to his mom. His mom smiled at him and said, "It's a toy, George! Why don't you try playing with it?" George nodded and began to explore the object. It was fun and he soon was having lots of jolly play with it. He even went and showed his friends and they all began to play with it together. George and his friends had so much fun that the object quickly became their favorite thing to play with. As George picked it up and looked at it, he nodded his head and smiled.

Once upon a time, there was snow everywhere! It was all around the house, and there was a lot of it! Every day, the children were eager to lift the snow and make a big snowman. With their shovels, they would scoop up the white snow and pile it up into a tower. They had so much fun! When the snowman was finished, the children were so proud! They made sure to give him a nice hat and two buttons for eyes. Then, they went back in the house, eager for more fun with the snow.

Jack was a happy 3-year-old boy. He smiled all the time, even when he was sad. One day he was playing in his room when he saw something shiny on the floor. He bent down and picked it up. It felt like a letter. He opened it and saw that it was broken. Jack was sad. He had wanted to see what was inside the letter. He put it back down and went to find his mommy. He showed her the letter and she smiled. She said, "that's a special letter". Then she took it to the kitchen and carefully fixed it with tape. As soon as it was fixed, she put it in an envelope and gave it to Jack with a hug. He opened the envelope and there was a photo inside of his mommy and daddy when they were younger. Jack was so excited. He smiled and hugged his mommy. He now had a special letter that reminded him that his mommy and daddy had once been young and happy, just like him.

Tom and Lily were playing with crayons. They liked to draw and color on paper. Tom had a blue crayon and Lily had a red crayon. They were happy. But then Tom saw Lily's red crayon. He wanted it. He said, "Give me your red crayon. I want to draw a fire truck." Lily said, "No, this is my red crayon. You have a blue crayon. You can draw a sky or a sea." Tom was stubborn. He did not listen to Lily. He grabbed her red crayon and ran away. Lily was sad and angry. She chased Tom and tried to get her crayon back. They ran around the room, shouting and pulling the crayon. The crayon broke in half. Tom and Lily both fell down. They looked at the broken crayon. They felt sorry. They said, "We are sorry. We should not have fought over the crayon. We should have shared and played nicely." They hugged and made up. They used the broken crayon to draw a rainbow. They learned to be kind and not stubborn. They were happy again.
