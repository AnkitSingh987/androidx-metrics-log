Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Door

Ben and Mia were hungry. They wanted to eat something yummy. They looked in the kitchen for food. They saw a big pot on the stove. They opened the lid and smelled the steam. "What is this?" Ben asked. "It is rice," Mia said. "Mommy cooked it for dinner." Ben and Mia liked rice. They took two spoons and scooped some rice into their bowls. They sat at the table and ate the rice. It was warm and soft and tasty. "Can we have more?" Ben asked. "Yes, we can," Mia said. "But we have to leave some for Mommy and Daddy." Ben and Mia were happy. They ate more rice until they were full. They discovered a new food that they loved. They were glad that Mommy cooked it for them. They washed their bowls and spoons and put them in the sink. They heard the door open and saw Mommy and Daddy come home. "Hi, kids," Mommy said. "Did you have a good day?" "Yes, we did," Ben and Mia said. "We ate rice!" Mommy and Daddy smiled. They hugged Ben and Mia and kissed them. "We are glad you liked the rice," Daddy said. "It is from a faraway place called China. We can learn more about it later." Ben and Mia nodded. They were curious and excited. They wanted to discover more things from faraway places. They were glad that Mommy and Daddy were home. They were a happy family.

Once upon a time, there was a warm sandwich. The sandwich was on a plate. It was alone in the kitchen. The sandwich wanted to play with someone. So, it called out, "Come, come, let's play!" A little mouse heard the sandwich. The mouse came into the kitchen. The mouse saw the warm sandwich. The mouse said, "Hello, sandwich! I will play with you." The sandwich was happy. They played together all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The sandwich started to get cold. The mouse said, "Don't worry, sandwich. I will warm you up!" The mouse ran and got a warm blanket. The mouse wrapped the sandwich in the blanket. The sandwich was warm again. They were happy and played all day.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big rock. The rock had a shiny mineral inside. Tim wanted to take the rock home. But the rock was too heavy. He tried and tried, but he could not lift it up. Then, a spicy smell appeared. Tim looked around and saw a big red pepper. The pepper was magic. It said, "I can help you lift the rock if you eat me." Tim was scared, but he wanted the shiny mineral. So, he ate the spicy pepper. Suddenly, Tim was strong. He lifted the rock with the shiny mineral. But when he got home, the rock was not heavy anymore. The spicy pepper made everything light for Tim. Now, Tim could play with the rock and show his friends the shiny mineral.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were playing in the park. They liked to play with their stuff, like balls and toys. The big dog, named Max, said to the small cat, named Lily, "Let's play with this ball, but we must be careful not to destroy it." As they played, a bad bird flew down from the sky. The bird wanted to destroy their stuff, because it was a very bad bird. The bird took the ball from Max and Lily and flew away. Max and Lily were sad. Max and Lily went to find the bad bird to get their ball back. They looked everywhere, but they could not find the bird. Then, they saw the bird in a tree. The bird said, "I will give the ball back if you promise to play with me too." Max and Lily agreed, but the bird was not nice. The bird destroyed the ball and all their stuff. Max, Lily, and the bad bird were all sad because they had no more stuff to play with.

Lily and Ben like to collect things. They collect rocks, leaves, stickers, and shells. One day, they go for a walk with Mom and Dad. They see a big billboard on the road. It has a picture of a blue car and some words. "Look, Mom, a blue car!" Lily says. "Yes, it's a blue car. Do you like it?" Mom asks. "I like it. It's shiny and pretty. Can we collect it?" Lily says. Mom laughs. "No, we can't collect it. It's too big and it belongs to someone else. We can only collect small things that we find or get." "Oh, okay." Lily says. She feels a little sad. Ben sees a blue flower on the grass. He picks it up and gives it to Lily. "Here, Lily. A blue flower for you. You can collect it. It's small and pretty too." Lily smiles. She takes the flower and smells it. "Thank you, Ben. You are very nice. I like the flower. It smells good." They hug each other and continue to walk with Mom and Dad. They see more things on the way. They collect some more rocks, leaves, stickers, and shells. They have fun and they are happy.
