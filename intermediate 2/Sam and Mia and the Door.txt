Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and Mia and the Door

Sam and Mia liked to watch TV after dinner. They watched funny shows with animals and people. They laughed and clapped their hands. But one night, the TV showed something different. It showed a scary movie with monsters and ghosts. Sam and Mia did not like it. They felt frightened. "Mommy, Daddy, turn it off!" Sam cried. "We don't want to watch this!" But Mommy and Daddy were not in the living room. They were in the kitchen, talking. They did not hear Sam and Mia. The TV kept showing the scary movie. The monsters and ghosts came closer and closer to the screen. They made loud noises and ugly faces. "Help, help!" Mia screamed. "They are going to get us!" Sam and Mia ran to the door. They wanted to go to the kitchen and find Mommy and Daddy. But the door was locked. They could not open it. They banged on the door and shouted. But Mommy and Daddy did not come. They were too busy talking. The TV kept showing the scary movie. The monsters and ghosts reached out of the screen. They grabbed Sam and Mia by their hair and pulled them into the TV. Sam and Mia screamed and cried. But no one heard them. No one helped them. The TV swallowed them up. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with paint. One day, he found a big, red paint can. He was very happy. Tim wanted to paint a picture. He put his hand in the paint can and felt something pinch his finger. "Ouch!" said Tim. A tiny, green bug was in the paint can. The bug was filthy from the paint. Tim took the bug out of the paint can. He washed the bug with water. The bug became clean and thanked Tim. They became friends and painted a big, beautiful picture together.

Once upon a time, there was a fun race. Many kids were eager to run. They wanted to win the race. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. It was a great day for a race. A mom had a camera. She wanted to record the race. She wanted to see her child run fast. The kids lined up, ready to go. The mom stood by, with her camera on. The race started, and the kids ran fast. They were eager to win. The mom recorded the race. She was so happy to see her child run. At the end, everyone was tired but happy. The race was fun for all.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big, loud river. The river had many pretty minerals in it. These minerals made the water sparkle and shine. Every day, the animals from the forest came to play and splash in the river. One day, a big bear named Bob and a small rabbit named Ray went to the river. They wanted to have fun and play together. Bob said, "Ray, let's splash in the river and look for pretty minerals!" Ray was happy and agreed. They splashed and played all day long. They found many beautiful minerals in the river. As the sun went down, Bob and Ray were tired but very happy. They went back to the forest to tell their friends about the fun day they had by the loud river.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He wanted a snack. He asked his mom for a snack. His mom said, "Okay, but only one snack." Tim went to the kitchen and saw many snacks. He saw cookies, chips, and fruit. Tim wanted all the snacks. He took all the snacks to his mom. His mom said, "No, Tim, you can only have one snack." Tim was sad. He insisted, "But I am very hungry!" His mom said, "You can have one snack now and another snack later." Tim thought about it and picked the fruit. He ate the fruit and felt better. Later, he had another snack. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tom went to a big church with his mom. The church was very old and needed some color. Tom's mom said, "Tom, let's paint the church to make it look good." Tom was happy and said, "Yes, mom! I want to help!" They got paint in many colors like red, blue, and green. They started to paint the church walls. Tom painted a big sun and his mom painted pretty flowers. When they finished painting, the church looked very good. People came and said, "Wow, the church is so pretty now!" Tom and his mom were happy that they could help make the church look good.
