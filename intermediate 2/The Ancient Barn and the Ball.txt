Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ancient Barn and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was an ancient barn. It was very old and big. In the barn, there lived a cat and a dog. They were good friends. One day, the cat and the dog found a big ball. They wanted to play with it. The cat tried to stretch and reach the ball, but it was too high. The dog tried to jump, but he could not reach it either. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to move on its own! It rolled down and stopped right in front of the cat and the dog. They were so surprised! It turned out that the ball was a magic ball! The cat and the dog played with the magic ball all day and had lots of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He had a new toy jeep. He wanted to play with it all day. Tim went to the park with his mom and his new jeep. At the park, Tim saw a big hill. He thought it would be fun to make his jeep roll down the hill. "Mom, can I roll my jeep down the hill?" Tim asked. "Yes, but be careful," his mom said. Tim went to the top of the hill and let his jeep roll down. As the jeep rolled down the hill, it went faster and faster. Suddenly, the jeep hit a bump and flew into the air! It landed in a big puddle. Tim was sad, but then he saw that his jeep was still rolling. The jeep rolled out of the puddle and back to Tim. He was so happy and excited to see that his jeep was okay. Tim and his mom laughed, and they played with the jeep for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a nice witch. She had a big hat and a long dress. She liked to help people and animals. She was friends with everyone in the village. One day, the nice witch found a little bird that could not fly. The bird was sad and needed help. The nice witch wanted to help the little bird. So, she used her magic to carry the bird to a safe place. She took the bird to her house and gave it food and water. The little bird was very thankful for the nice witch's help. Soon, the bird could fly again. The bird told all its friends about the nice witch. The village people learned that it is good to help others, even if they are different. The moral of the story is to be kind and help those in need.

Once upon a time, there was a fast bug named Speedy. He had a pretty shell on his back. Speedy liked to run and play with his friends in the park. They would race each other to see who was the fastest. Speedy always won because he was very fast. One day, Speedy saw a new bug in the park. The new bug had a big shell and he was not fast like Speedy. Speedy thought the new bug would not fit in with his friends. He did not want to play with the new bug. Speedy's friends wanted to give the new bug a chance, so they asked him to play with them. At first, the new bug was slow and had a hard time keeping up with Speedy and his friends. But as they played more, the new bug got better and better. Speedy saw that the new bug was trying hard and was a good friend. He learned that it was not nice to judge someone by how they look or how fast they are. In the end, Speedy and the new bug became good friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. The new bug even helped Speedy when he needed it. The moral of the story is to give everyone a chance and not judge them by how they look or what they can do.

One day, a little girl named Sally went for a walk. She walked down the street near her house. Sally saw a delicate butterfly on a flower. She thought it was very pretty. Sally wanted to show the butterfly to her friend, Billy. She called out, "Billy, come here! Look at this pretty butterfly!" Billy came running to see what Sally found. Billy said, "Wow, it is so pretty and delicate!" They both watched the butterfly fly away down the street. Sally and Billy laughed and played, happy to have seen the pretty butterfly together.
