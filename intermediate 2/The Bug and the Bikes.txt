Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bug and the Bikes

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The bug liked to crawl. One day, the bug saw a big garage. The bug wanted to crawl inside the garage and see what was there. The bug crawled into the garage. Inside, it saw many things. There were big cars and little bikes. The bug was very careful, because some things in the garage were dangerous. The bug crawled around and found a safe place to rest. It was happy it had explored the garage, but knew it was best to stay away from the dangerous things. The bug crawled back home and told all its friends about the big garage adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly mechanic. His name was Dave. Dave loved cars and he liked to make them feel better. One day Dave saw a car that was not looking very well. He asked the owner of the car, "What's wrong with your car?". The owner said, "Oh, it's not running very well. It doesn't feel happy". Dave said, "Don't worry. I'm going to help your car feel better". So Dave took the car to his workshop. He fixed the car with his tools and some tasty oil. When Dave was done, the car started purring like a kitty. Dave said to the car, "Now all you need to do is relax and you'll feel even better". The car was so happy, it went merrily down the road. Everyone was glad and the car was feeling much better.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to read. She could read big books and small books. One day, Sue found a yellow book. The yellow book was very pretty. Sue wanted to read it all day. Sue sat under a big tree to read her yellow book. As she read, she saw a small bug. The bug was only an inch long. It was a nice bug. Sue said, "Hello, little bug. Do you want to read with me?" The inch-long bug said, "Yes, I love to read too!" So, Sue and the little bug read the yellow book together. They had lots of fun. They read and read until the sun went down. Then, Sue and the little bug said goodbye. Sue went home to sleep, happy with her new friend.

Tommy was angry. He hated his new pillow. It was too big and too hard. He wanted his old pillow. His old pillow was soft and cozy. He missed his old pillow. "Mommy, I don't like this pillow!" Tommy shouted. He threw the pillow on the floor. "I want my old pillow!" "Tommy, don't be rude. Your old pillow was dirty and torn. You need a new pillow. This pillow is clean and nice. You will get used to it." Mommy said. She picked up the pillow and put it back on the bed. "No, I won't! I hate this pillow! It hurts my head!" Tommy cried. He kicked the pillow with his feet. "I want my old pillow!" "Tommy, stop it. You are making a mess. You are hurting the pillow. You should be grateful. Some children don't have any pillows. You should say sorry to the pillow." Mommy said. She was angry too. She grabbed Tommy's hand and made him sit on the bed. "Sorry, pillow." Tommy mumbled. He didn't mean it. He still hated the pillow. He wished he had his old pillow. He felt sad and angry. He didn't want to sleep. He wanted to throw the pillow away. He didn't like his new pillow at all.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big, heavy book in her room. It was a dictionary. Amy liked to look at the pictures in the book and learn new words. She would enjoy sitting on the floor and turning the pages of the dictionary. Amy's friend, Tim, came over to play. "What's that?" he asked. "It's my dictionary," said Amy. "It's heavy, but I like it." Tim sat down and they looked at the pictures together. They were having fun learning new words. Suddenly, they heard a noise. It was a little bird that flew into the room! The bird landed on the dictionary and started to sing. Amy and Tim were surprised. They never thought a bird would come inside to look at the dictionary too! The bird stayed with them and they all enjoyed learning new words together.
