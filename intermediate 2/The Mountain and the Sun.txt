Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mountain and the Sun

Once upon a time there was a mountain. It was tall, and full of beauty. But on one side of the mountain, the ground was always dry. One day, a little girl set off to the mountain with a big bottle of lotion. She wanted to help make the ground more moist and green. She climbed the mountain until she reached the dry side. She set the lotion near the ground and started pouring it out. Unfortunately, it was too late: the bottle was already empty! The ground was still dry. The little girl was sad and angry. She wanted to help the mountain, but she couldn't. But then, the sun began to shine and it started to rain! Suddenly, the ground was wet and full of life. The little girl smiled, feeling proud of her efforts. She had helped the mountain after all!

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. One sunny day, Bobo saw a bike with a pedal. He thought, "I want to try that!" Bobo flew down and tried to use the pedal. He pushed with his tiny feet, but it was hard. A little girl named Lily saw Bobo and said, "Hi, Bobo! Can I help you?" Bobo nodded his head. Lily got on the bike and started to pedal. Bobo sat on the handle and enjoyed the ride. After their ride, Lily and Bobo went to the park. They saw a tasty ice cream truck. Lily bought ice cream for both of them. They sat on a bench and enjoyed their tasty treat. They became best friends and had many more fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her toys. One day, she found an empty box in her room. She thought it was a magic box, so she put her toy bear inside. After that, Mia went to help her mom with work. They made yummy cookies together. When they were done, Mia went back to her room to play with her toy bear. But, when she opened the box, her toy bear was gone! Mia was sad and wondered where her bear could be. Mia's mom came into her room and saw the empty box. She told Mia that the bear did not vanish. It was just hiding. They looked under the bed and there it was! Mia was so happy to find her toy bear. They played together, and Mia knew that her bear would never really vanish.

One day, a friendly pilot named Tom went to fly his big plane. He put on his hat and said, "Time to go up, up, up!" He started the plane and it went zoom, zoom, zoom! In the sky, Tom saw a bird. The bird said, "Hi, I am Bob!" Tom blinked his eyes and said, "Wow! A talking bird!" They talked and laughed. They became good friends. But then, something unexpected happened. Bob blinked and turned into a big, pretty butterfly. Tom was so surprised! Bob said, "I am a magic friend!" They had more fun and flew together in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little goat. He had a big horn on his head. He lived in a small house with his family. They did not have much, but they were happy. One day, the little goat wanted to spend time with his friends. He went to the park and saw his friends playing. They were playing with a ball. The little goat wanted to play too, but he was scared that his big horn would pop the ball. His friends saw him and asked him to play. The little goat was happy but still scared. He told his friends about his big horn. His friends said, "Don't worry, we will be careful!" So, the little goat played with his friends, and they had a lot of fun. In the end, the little goat learned that his big horn was not a problem. He could still have fun and spend time with his friends. The little goat was not poor anymore, because he had something more important: love and friendship.
