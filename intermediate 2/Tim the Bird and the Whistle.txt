Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Whistle

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to whistle all day long. One day, Tim sat on a big tree branch and started to whistle his favorite song. A boy named Sam heard Tim's whistle and wanted to be friends. Sam reached out his arm to touch Tim. But Tim got scared and flew away. Sam felt sad because he wanted to play with Tim. The next day, Sam tried to whistle like Tim. He hoped that Tim would come back. But instead of Tim, a big mean cat heard Sam's whistle. The cat chased Sam and he ran away. Sam learned to be careful when making new friends.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to hang a pretty picture on her wall. She asked her mom, "Can I hang this picture, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful, Lily." Lily started to hang the picture, but she slipped and fell. "Ouch!" she cried. Her knee was hurt. Her mom came and hugged her. "It's okay, Lily. Let's make it better." That night, there was a dance at Lily's school. Lily was sad because her knee was hurt. She didn't think she could go to the dance. But her mom said, "Let's try, Lily. Maybe you can still have fun." At the dance, Lily saw a boy in a wheel chair. He was smiling and having fun. Lily thought, "If he can dance and have fun, so can I!" She started to dance with her hurt knee, and she had a great time. The moral of the story is: even when things are hard, you can still find a way to have fun and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tim. He had a big machine. It was a special machine that could talk. Tim liked to play with his machine every day. One sunny day, Tim and his machine went to the park. They saw a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, I like your machine." Tim said, "Thank you, it can talk." The machine said, "Hello, Sue." Sue was very surprised. Tim, Sue, and the machine played all day. Then, something unexpected happened. The machine did not recognize Tim. It said, "Who are you?" Tim and Sue were sad. They helped the machine remember Tim. The machine said, "Thank you, friends." The moral of the story is that friends help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play hide and seek with his friends. One day, he found a big triangle in the park. It was a new place to hide! Tim called his friend, Sally. "Come play hide and seek with me!" he said. Sally came to the park and started to count. Tim ran to the big triangle and hid behind it. He could hear Sally counting, "One, two, three..." Sally finished counting and started to look for Tim. She looked under a tree, but he was not there. She looked behind a bush, but he was not there either. Finally, she found Tim hiding behind the big triangle. "I found you!" she said. Tim laughed and they decided to eat lunch. Tim's mom made spicy food for lunch. Tim and Sally ate the spicy food and drank water to cool their mouths. After lunch, they played hide and seek again, laughing and having fun all day long.

Anna loved to dance. She danced every day in her room, with her dolls and her teddy bear. She liked to change her clothes and pretend she was a different dancer. Sometimes she was a fairy, sometimes a princess, sometimes a star. One day, her mom told her they were going to see a real dance show. Anna was very excited. She put on her best dress and her shiny shoes. She asked her mom if she could change her hair too. Her mom smiled and made a pretty braid for her. At the theater, Anna saw many people and lights and music. She clapped her hands and sat on her mom's lap. She watched the dancers on the stage. They were so beautiful and graceful. They changed their costumes and their moves. They danced with joy and skill. Anna was amazed. She whispered to her mom, "Mom, I want to be a dancer like them. Can I?" Her mom hugged her and said, "Of course you can, my sweet. You are already a wonderful dancer. You just need to practice and learn and have fun. I am very proud of you, Anna." Anna smiled and hugged her mom back. She felt happy and hopeful. She decided to work hard and follow her dream. She knew she could be a dancer one day.
