Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Young and the Boy

Once upon a time there was a young boy who wanted to see some fireworks. So he asked his mom for a firework and she said no. The young boy did not know what to do and started to scream. He kept screaming and screaming and his mom got mad. He ran outside and found a firework. He was so excited and didn't know what to do with it. He picked it up and started to play with it. Suddenly, it started to spark. The young boy screamed and ran away, but it was too late. The firework exploded and the young boy got hurt. He was screaming in pain. His mom came outside and rushed him to the hospital. The doctors said the young boy was very lucky, but the firework had done a lot of damage. The young boy was sad and scared. He had learned his lesson not to mess with fireworks and never to scream again.

Ben and Lily were playing in their room. They had many toys, but they liked to make new ones. They found a big pillow and a pair of scissors. They had an idea. "Let's cut the pillow and make something incredible!" Ben said. "OK, but don't cut too much. Mom will be angry," Lily said. They started to cut the pillow. They cut out two ears, a tail, and four legs. They used some string to tie them to the pillow. They made a big, soft animal. "Look, we made a lion!" Ben said. "Wow, it's incredible! Can we play with it?" Lily said. They hugged the lion and pretended it was their pet. They gave it a name and a voice. They had a lot of fun. But then, Mom came in. She saw the pillow and the scissors and the mess. She was not happy. "Ben, Lily, what did you do? You cut the pillow! You made a big hole! You wasted the stuffing! This is very bad!" Mom said. Ben and Lily felt sorry. They did not want to make Mom angry. They looked at the lion and then at Mom. "We're sorry, Mom. We wanted to make something incredible. We made a lion. Do you like it?" Ben said. Mom looked at the lion. She saw the ears, the tail, the legs, and the string. She saw the happy faces of Ben and Lily. She sighed. "Well, it is incredible. But you should not cut the pillow. It was not yours. It was for the bed. Next time, ask me before you use the scissors. And clean up the mess," Mom said. Ben and Lily nodded. They said sorry again. They hugged Mom and the lion. They cleaned up the mess. They learned a lesson. They still loved their incredible lion.

One day, a cheerful girl named Lily went to the kitchen. She saw a sign on the refrigerator. The sign had a picture of a big red apple. Lily asked her mom, "What is the sign for?" Her mom said, "The sign is to remind us to eat fruit. We will have a surprise later if we eat our fruit first." Lily was excited and took an apple from the refrigerator. She ate the apple with a big smile. Later that day, Lily's mom showed her the surprise. It was a big cake with colorful sprinkles. Lily was so happy she ate her fruit. She knew the sign on the refrigerator helped her get the yummy cake.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter named Tim. Tim liked to pack his bag and go into the woods to look for food. One day, Tim felt uncomfortable because his shoes were too tight. He decided to go back home and change his shoes. When Tim got home, he saw a little bird with a hurt wing. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he forgot about his tight shoes. He took care of the bird and soon, the bird was better. The bird flew away, but it left Tim a shiny gift. The next day, Tim went back to the woods. He saw that same little bird with other birds. They all had shiny gifts for Tim. Tim was so happy and he learned that being kind to others is more important than being uncomfortable.
