Category: Intermediate
Title: The Noisy Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a noisy boat. This boat loved to sing all day. It would sing so loud that all the fish would swim away. One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to prevent the boat from being so noisy. She thought of a plan. She decided to talk to the boat and ask it to be quiet. Lily said, "Please, Mr. Boat, can you be quiet today?" The noisy boat listened to Lily and stopped singing. The fish came back, and everyone was happy. The boat learned to sing softly, and they all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a big, clear sky, the sun shined so bright. There lived a little girl named Lily. She liked to scatter seeds in her garden for the birds. One day, she met a talking bird named Chirpy. "Hi, I am Chirpy," said the bird. "Why do you scatter seeds?" Lily smiled and said, "I like to help birds find food. Do you like to eat seeds too?" Chirpy nodded and said, "Yes, thank you for the seeds. Can I ask you a question?" Lily said, "Of course, what is your question?" Chirpy asked, "Can we be friends?" Lily was happy and said, "Yes, we can be friends!" From that day on, Lily and Chirpy were the best of friends. They played together in the garden, and Lily always made sure to scatter seeds for her new friend.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to work in her garden every day. She would plant flowers, water them, and watch them grow. The sun was very bright, but Lily liked to work in the shade under a big tree. One day, while working in the shade, Lily found a tiny seed. She thought it was special, so she dug a small hole and put the seed inside. She covered it with dirt and gave it some water. Then, she waited for it to grow. Days went by, and the tiny seed started to grow. It grew bigger and bigger, and soon, it was a tall plant with beautiful flowers. The flowers made the quiet town even prettier. Lily was very happy that her hard work made her garden and town beautiful.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big rocket in the sky. It is very loud and bright. "Wow, look at that!" Sara says. "Where is it going?" Tom says, "Maybe it is going to the earth. The earth is our home. It is a big ball of land and water and air." Sara says, "But the earth is here. We are on the earth. How can the rocket go to the earth?" Tom says, "Maybe the rocket is going to another part of the earth. The earth is very big. There are many places to go. Some places are hot, some are cold, some are wet, some are dry." Sara says, "I want to go to another part of the earth. I want to see new things and meet new people." Tom says, "Me too. But we are too small to go on a rocket. Maybe when we are big, we can go on a rocket." Sara says, "OK. But for now, we can play here. We have a lot of fun here. We have our toys, our pets, our family, our friends." Tom says, "You are right. We are happy here. We are calm here. The rocket is loud and fast. Maybe it is scary." Sara says, "Yes, maybe. But maybe it is also exciting. Maybe the people on the rocket are brave and curious. Maybe they want to learn more about the earth and the sky." Tom says, "Maybe. But we can also learn more about the earth and the sky. We can read books, watch TV, ask questions, use our imagination." Sara says, "Yes, we can. We are smart and creative. We can make our own stories and adventures." Tom says, "Yes, we can. We are friends and we have fun. We can go anywhere we want in our minds." Sara says, "Yes, we can. We are happy and calm. We love the earth and the sky." They smile and hug. They look at the rocket one more time. Then they go back to their play.
