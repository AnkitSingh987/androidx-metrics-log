Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Creative Girl and the Castle

Once upon a time, in a small nation, there lived a creative girl named Lily. She loved to fold paper into fun shapes like birds, dogs, and flowers. All the kids in her town liked to watch her fold and play with her paper creations. One day, Lily had an idea. She wanted to make a big surprise for her friends. She thought and thought about what she could fold that would make them all happy. Then, she decided to fold a huge paper castle for everyone to play in. Lily worked hard, folding many big pieces of paper. She made walls, a door, and even a tower. When the castle was done, she called her friends to come and see. They were all so happy and amazed at her creative work. They played in the castle all day, and they knew that their nation was a special place because of Lily's folding magic.

Once upon a time, there was a modest alligator named Al. Al lived in a small pond with his friends. He was very nice and always helped others. One day, Al and his friend, a little bird named Bebe, were playing near the pond. Bebe said, "Al, let's play hide and seek!" Al agreed and started to count. Bebe flew away to find a hiding spot. Al counted to ten and said, "Ready or not, here I come!" Al looked and looked for Bebe, but he could not find her. He was starting to feel sad. Suddenly, he remembered that he forgot to check behind the big tree. He went to the tree and found Bebe. "I found you!" Al said. Bebe laughed and they continued to play all day. They had lots of fun and never forgot that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lizzie who was playing with her toys. She was so excited that she took one of her toy cars and held it in the palm of her hand. Suddenly, her Mum came in and said, "Lizzie, I think it's their time for dinner now". But Lizzie disagreed. She wanted to keep playing and said, "No, I don't want to eat. I want to stay here and play". Her Mum smiled and gently placed her hand on her daughter's forehead. She said, "Lizzie, I know playing is fun, but dinner can also be useful. You could have some energy afterwards, and you'll be able to play even longer after that." Lizzie thought about it and was convinced by her Mum's words. She nodded and put down her toy and followed her Mum to the dinner table. The dinner that night was tasty, and the energy it gave Lizzie was useful! She was so happy and ran back to play some more with her toys. Moral of the story: Sometimes it is useful to disagree!

Once upon a time, there was a small cat. The cat had soft fur. The cat was nervous. It was scared of big dogs. One day, the cat went for a walk. It saw a big dog. The dog was nice. The dog wanted to play with the cat. The cat was scared at first. But then, it started to play with the dog. They played with a ball. They ran and jumped. The cat was not nervous anymore. The cat and the dog had fun. After playing, the cat and the dog were tired. They lay down on the soft grass. The sun was warm on their fur. The cat and the dog were happy. They became best friends.

Lily and Ben were twins. They liked to play together, but sometimes they fought. One day, their mom made pizza for lunch. She cut the pizza into four pieces and put them on a plate. "Share the pizza nicely, okay?" she said. "I have to go to the phone for a minute." Lily and Ben looked at the pizza. They both wanted the biggest piece. Lily grabbed it quickly and put it on her plate. "Hey, that's not fair!" Ben said. "You are selfish!" "No, I'm not!" Lily said. "You are slow!" Ben was angry. He reached for Lily's plate and tried to take her piece. Lily held on tight and pulled back. They both pulled and pulled, until the pizza split in half. "Oh no!" Lily and Ben said. "We broke the pizza!" They looked at the pizza. It was torn and messy. They felt sorry and sad. Their mom came back from the phone. She saw the pizza and the twins. "What happened here?" she asked. Lily and Ben told her what they did. They said they were sorry. Their mom was not happy. She said they had to learn to share and be kind. She said they had to eat the pizza as it was, and no dessert. Lily and Ben nodded. They ate the pizza, but it did not taste good. They wished they had shared it nicely. They learned their lesson. Next time, they would not be selfish.
