Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Puddle and the Sun

Once upon a time, there lived a brave little girl. She liked to organize things and was always tidying up her room. One day, while the sun was out, she decided to go out and play. She soon found a big puddle in her yard and decided to go have some fun. She jumped right in and started splashing around, not realizing the puddle was deep. She kept jumping, but soon she found the water reaching her neck. The brave little girl knew she needed to get help, but she was alone. She kept calling for help, but no one heard her. Soon the water got higher and higher and she sunk into the puddle. No one found her, and the brave little girl was never seen again.

Ben and Lily are twins. They like to play outside in the snow. They make a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They give him a carrot nose and two rocks for eyes. They think he looks funny and nice. But then a big dog comes. He is black and furry. He likes to run and jump. He sees the snowman and thinks he is a toy. He runs to the snowman and bites his nose. He pulls his scarf and hat. He makes the snowman fall down. Ben and Lily are sad and angry. They shout at the dog. They throw snowballs at him. They want him to go away. They want their snowman back. But then the dog stops. He looks at Ben and Lily. He wags his tail. He thinks they want to play with him. He runs to them and licks their faces. He makes them laugh. Ben and Lily are not sad and angry anymore. They think the dog is cute and friendly. They pet him and hug him. They give him a new name. They call him Snowy. They make a new snowman with him. They give him a big smile. They are not cold anymore. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a snowman named Sam. Sam lived on a cool mountain with many friends. They all liked to play together. Sam's favorite game was to pass a big ball made of snow. One sunny day, Sam and his friends were playing with the snowball. They passed it back and forth, laughing and having fun. But as the sun got hotter, Sam started to feel warm. He noticed that the snowball was getting smaller too. Sam was scared that he would melt if he stayed in the sun. He told his friends he had to go back to the cool shade. His friends understood and they all went to find a shady spot to play. They learned that it's important to protect themselves and their snowball from the hot sun, so they can keep having fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben lived in a cave near a forest. He liked to play outside in every season. In the summer, he would swim in the river. In the fall, he would jump in the leaves. In the winter, he would play in the snow. And in the spring, he would sniff the flowers. One day, Ben found a big box in the forest. He was very curious and wanted to see if he could fit inside. He tried to push his head into the box, but it was too small. He tried to push his arm into the box, but it was still too small. He tried to push his foot into the box, but it would not fit. Ben was very sad. He wanted to fit in the box so badly. He tried one more time, and this time, he pushed very hard. His whole body fit into the box! But then, the box closed and locked on its own. Ben was stuck inside the box, and he could not get out. He missed playing outside in every season.

Once upon a time, there was a good dog named Sam. Sam loved to play with his cube toy all day. He would run, jump, and spread his legs wide to catch the cube when it bounced. One sunny day, Sam went to the park with his cube. He played and played, spreading his legs wide to catch the cube. But then, something unexpected happened. The cube did not bounce back to Sam. It rolled far away, behind a big tree. Sam ran to the tree and found a new friend, a cat named Luna, playing with his cube. Luna and Sam looked at each other, and then they started to play together. They took turns spreading their legs wide to catch the cube, and they had so much fun. Sam was happy to have a new friend, and they played together every day after that.
