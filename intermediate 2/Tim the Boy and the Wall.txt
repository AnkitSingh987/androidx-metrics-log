Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Wall

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to design things. One day, he decided to design a big wall. He wanted to make the wall very pretty. So, he used many colors to draw on the wall. While Tim was drawing, his big sister, Sue, saw the wall. Sue was angry. She did not like the wall. She thought it was not pretty. Sue told Tim to stop drawing. Tim was sad, but he stopped. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the wall and smiled. She said, "What a pretty wall! I love your design, Tim." Tim was very happy. Sue was surprised. She thought her mom would be angry too. But, she was not. Sue learned that it was okay for Tim to design the wall. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lively girl named Anna. Anna wanted to find out more about the world, so she decided to open the door of her house and explore. She stopped in front of a lake and was fascinated by it. Anna felt that the lake carried a lot of wisdom, and she wanted to know more about it. She opened her eyes wide to take in all the beauty around her. Anna spotted a fish swimming near her. To her surprise, the fish started talking to her! It taught Anna different lessons about nature and life. Anna listened carefully and learned so many new things. Anna realized that wisdom can be found in the most unexpected places. She thanked the fish and happily went back inside her house with the new knowledge that she had acquired. Anna was very excited that she discovered such meaningful wisdom on her journey.

Tom and Sue like to play in the park. They like to swing, slide and climb. But their favorite thing is to spin on the round thing. They hold on tight and go very fast. They feel like they can fly. One day, they see a new boy in the park. He is big and mean. He pushes other kids and takes their toys. He comes to the round thing and says, "This is mine. Go away." Tom and Sue are scared, but they do not want to leave. They say, "No, this is for everyone. You have to share." The big boy gets angry. He grabs the round thing and spins it very hard. He tries to make Tom and Sue fall off. He laughs and says, "This is fun. You are too weak to spin. You are not flies, you are bugs." Tom and Sue hold on tighter. They feel dizzy and sick. They want to stop, but they cannot. The big boy spins the round thing faster and faster. He does not see the park ranger coming. The park ranger is nice and strong. He helps the kids and keeps the park safe. He sees the big boy and says, "Stop that. You are being dangerous. You could hurt someone." He grabs the round thing and slows it down. He helps Tom and Sue get off. He says, "Are you okay? That was not nice of him. He has to go home now." Tom and Sue are happy. They thank the park ranger. They feel better. They say, "You are our hero. You saved us from the big boy. He was bad. He did not know how to spin. He did not know how to fly." The park ranger smiles and says, "You are welcome. You are brave and good. You know how to spin. You know how to fly. But remember, spinning is fun, but not too much. It can be dangerous. Be careful and have fun." Tom and Sue nod and hug. They go back to play. They spin on the round thing, but not too fast. They feel like they can fly, but not too high. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big cave. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, liked to play in the cave. They would enjoy running and laughing in the dry cave. One day, they went inside to play. As they played, they found a big, red ball. Tim and Spot were very happy. They played with the ball, kicking and rolling it. Suddenly, the ball started to grow bigger and bigger. Tim and Spot were surprised. The big red ball was not a ball at all. It was a friendly dragon! The dragon said, "Hello, I am Red. I was hiding as a ball." Tim and Spot were not scared. They played with Red the dragon and became good friends. From that day on, they enjoyed playing in the cave with their new friend.
