Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Pilot and the Safe

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tom. Tom loved to fly high in the sky with his big plane. He was very good at it. Tom always made sure that everyone on his plane was safe and happy. One sunny day, Tom had to provide a ride for some nice people. They wanted to go to a far away place. Tom said, "Don't worry, I will take you there safe and sound!" The people smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" So, Tom and the people got on the big plane. They flew high in the sky, looking at the pretty clouds. Tom made sure everyone was safe and having fun. Soon, they got to the far away place. The people were so happy and thanked Tom for the safe trip. Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome! Have a great time!" And with that, Tom flew back home, ready to provide more safe rides for people with his big plane.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a normal boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, his mom took him to a big store. Tim was very happy because he loved to go shopping with his mom. At the store, they saw many things. Tim saw a uniform hanging on a rack. The uniform was red and blue, just like the one his friend wore to school. Tim wanted to wear it too, so his mom bought it for him. Tim wore his new uniform to school. All his friends thought it was cool. They played games and had a great day. When Tim got home, he put his uniform away and went to bed. He had a big smile on his face because he had a fun day.

Once upon a time there was a family who had an attic in their house. The attic was open, but the family had never gone up there. One day, the family decided to buy some things to put in the attic. The father said, "Let's go and purchase the things we need and bring them back here to the attic!" So off they went to buy the things. When they returned, the father opened the attic and said, "now let's put the things we bought in the attic". So the family unpacked their items and placed them inside. They were delighted with how the attic looked and excited to show their friends the new things they had bought. From then on, the family enjoyed going up to their attic to play.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a pretty shirt that she loved to wear. The shirt was pink with flowers on it. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, let's spend the day at the park." Lily was very happy and put on her pretty shirt. At the park, Lily played with her friends. While playing, Lily met a new friend named Mia. Mia said, "I like your pretty shirt!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Mia! Do you want to play with us?" Mia nodded, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a mouse. The mouse was looking for a place to build her home. She went to a distant house and knocked on the door. When the door opened, she saw a little girl. The girl said, “Who are you?” The mouse said, “I’m looking for a place to stay. Can I stay here?” The little girl said, “Yes, you can stay here. But you have to quit being so noisy.” The mouse nodded her head and said, “I promise. I’ll be quiet.” And so the mouse stayed with the little girl. She was very happy to have found a home in that distant house.

Lia and Tom are best friends. They like to play space games. They pretend they are astronauts and fly to different planets. They have a big cardboard box that they use as a rocket. One day, they decide to go to a distant planet. They put on their helmets and get into the box. They count down from ten and make loud noises. "Blast off!" they shout. They fly through the sky and see stars and moons. They land on a planet that is very cold and has a lot of ice. They get out of the box and explore. They see penguins and snowmen. They have fun sliding and throwing snowballs. They forget about their rocket. "Look, Tom, a big ice castle!" Lia says. She runs towards it. Tom follows her. They enter the castle and see shiny crystals and frozen statues. They are amazed. They hear a loud noise. They look outside and see their rocket. It is moving. It is flying away. They forgot to turn it off. "Oh no, Lia, our rocket!" Tom says. He is scared. "How will we go back home?" Lia is scared too. She hugs Tom. "I don't know, Tom. I don't know." She says. They cry. They hope someone will find them. They miss their home.
