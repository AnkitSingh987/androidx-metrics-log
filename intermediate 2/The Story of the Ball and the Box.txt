Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Box

Once there was a boy. He liked to kick around. He had a gray leather ball. He kicked it around the street. He ran and kicked it to his friends. They laughed and kicked it back. Everyone had fun. The ball had a soft leather feel when they kicked it. The boy took the ball home. He hugged it and put it in his special box. He went outside and kicked it one last time. He watched it fly into the sky until it was out of view. The boy was happy. He had found his special gray leather ball. He hoped he could kick it again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a big team of ants. They loved to march together. They marched up and down, all day long. The ants were very good at working as a team. One day, as the ants were marching, they saw a big cake. They were very happy and excited. They thought, "We can take this cake back to our home and share it with everyone!" So, the ants started to march with the cake. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky. The bird wanted the cake too! The bird tried to take the cake away from the ants. But the ants did not give up. They marched together and held on to the cake. The big bird saw how hard the ants were working as a team. The bird felt bad and decided to help the ants instead. The bird picked up the cake with its beak and flew it to the ants' home. In the end, the ants and the big bird became friends. They all shared the big cake and had a great time. The ants learned that even when something unexpected happens, working together as a team can help them succeed.

Once upon a time, in a clear pond, there lived a little green frog named Tim. Tim loved to slide down the smooth rocks near his pond. The rocks were wet and slippery, which made it easy for Tim to slide all day long. One sunny day, while Tim was sliding down the rocks, he met a new friend. The friend was a tiny yellow bird named Sue. Sue saw Tim sliding and said, "Hi, frog! Can I slide too?" Tim was happy to have a new friend and said, "Yes, bird! Let's slide together!" So, Tim and Sue took turns sliding down the rocks, laughing and having fun. They played together all day long. When the sun started to set, they said goodbye and promised to meet again at the pond to slide and play together. And from that day on, the little frog and the tiny bird were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, red balloon. One day, she went to the park with her mom. The sun was warm, and the grass was green. Lily saw a boy named Tim. Tim was sad. "What's wrong?" asked Lily. Tim said, "I have a bitter taste in my mouth from my snack." Lily wanted to help, so she gave Tim a piece of her sweet candy. Tim was happy. Lily and Tim played in the park. They watched the flowers grow. They laughed and played with the red balloon. At the end of the day, they said goodbye. Lily and Tim were now friends, and they couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, in a hot, sandy land, there was a beautiful oasis. It was a happy place with tasty fruit and cool water. Animals came from far away to eat, drink, and play. One day, a little bird was very dirty. She needed to bathe. So, she flew to the oasis to find some water. She saw her friends, a big elephant and a small rabbit, playing near the water. The little bird asked her friends for help. The big elephant used his long nose to spray water on the little bird. The small rabbit helped to splash water too. The little bird was so happy that she could bathe and be clean again. After the bath, they all ate the tasty fruit and drank the cool water. They played together until the sun went down. The oasis was a place where they could be happy and clean, and they knew they would always be friends.
