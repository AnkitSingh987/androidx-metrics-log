Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Treasure

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to feed the ducks at the icy pond near her house. One day, while she was feeding the ducks, she saw something shiny in the water. Mia thought it was a treasure! She was very excited and wanted to show her mom. Mia ran home to tell her mom about the treasure. "Mom, I found a treasure in the icy pond!" she said. Her mom smiled and went with Mia to the pond to see the treasure. When they reached the pond, they looked into the water and saw the shiny thing. But it was not a treasure! It was a spoon! Mia was surprised, but she was not sad. She picked up the spoon and said, "Even if it's not a treasure, it's still a special spoon. I will use it to feed the ducks!" And from that day on, Mia used the shiny spoon to feed the ducks at the icy pond, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a loyal dog named Max. They liked to play outside in the sun. One day, Tim found sunglasses on the ground. He put them on and thought he looked cool. One day, while playing, Tim saw another boy. The boy said, "I lost my sunglasses!" Tim did not want to give the sunglasses back. The boy was sad and began to cry. Tim started to feel bad. Tim wanted to keep the sunglasses, but he knew it was not right. He gave them back to the boy. But the boy was still sad. He said, "You should not have taken them!" Tim felt very bad and went home with Max.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Max. Max liked to play with his rag all day. He would run, jump, and wag his tail with the rag in his mouth. One day, Max saw his toys all mixed up. He wanted to sort them into piles. He put all the balls in one pile, and all the rags in another pile. Max was very proud of his work. After sorting his toys, Max played with his rag again. He ran and jumped with his friends in the park. They all had fun and played until the sun went down. Max was a happy and fierce dog with his rag.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a thin cat named Tom. Tom liked to organize his gear. He had many toys, like a ball, a car, and a doll. One day, Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, came to play. Max saw the gear Tom had. He said, "Wow, Tom! You have many toys! Can we play with them?" Tom was happy to share. He said, "Yes, Max! I like to organize my gear. Let's play with my ball first!" So, they played with the ball and had lots of fun. After they played, Tom and Max put the gear back in its place. They were tired but happy. They sat down and talked about their fun day. Tom and Max were great friends who loved to play and organize together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a loud dog named Max. Max liked to bark all day and all night. The people in the town were not happy because they could not sleep. One day, a kind man gave Max a big diamond. The diamond was very special. The kind man told Max, "If you cut this diamond, it will make you quiet and everyone will be happy." Max wanted to make the people happy, so he cut the diamond with his teeth. After Max cut the diamond, he became a quiet dog. The people in the town were very happy and could sleep well. Max learned that being quiet can make others happy too. And everyone lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to think about how our actions can affect others and to try to make people happy.

Sara was very happy. Her grandma was coming to visit. Grandma always brought Sara a big chocolate bar. Sara loved chocolate. She wanted to eat it all. But Sara had a little brother, Ben. Ben also liked chocolate. He wanted to share Sara's chocolate bar. Sara did not want to share. She said, "No, Ben. This is my chocolate. Go away." Ben was sad. He cried. He said, "That's not fair, Sara. You are spoiled. You have to share." Mom heard Ben crying. She came to see what was wrong. She saw Sara holding the chocolate bar. She saw Ben with tears in his eyes. She said, "Sara, what are you doing? Why are you making Ben cry?" Sara said, "Mom, this is my chocolate. Grandma gave it to me. I don't have to share." Mom said, "Sara, that's not nice. You have to be kind to your brother. Sharing is caring. If you don't share, your chocolate will melt." Sara looked at her chocolate bar. It was warm and soft. It was starting to melt. She did not want to lose her chocolate. She said, "OK, Mom. I will share. But only a little bit." Mom smiled. She said, "That's good, Sara. Here, I will cut the chocolate bar in half. You can have one half, and Ben can have the other half." Sara gave Ben half of her chocolate bar. Ben stopped crying. He said, "Thank you, Sara. You are nice." Sara said, "You're welcome, Ben. You are nice, too." They ate their chocolate together. They were happy. Grandma came to visit. She hugged them both. She said, "I'm proud of you, Sara and Ben. You are good children."
