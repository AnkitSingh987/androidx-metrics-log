Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim saw a brilliant new toy in the store. He wanted it so much. But he did not have money to buy it. Tim had an idea. He thought he could take the toy without anyone seeing him. He put the toy in his pocket and left the store. Tim was happy to play with his new toy at home. But then, Tim's mom found the toy. She knew that Tim did not have money to buy it. She was very sad and angry. Tim's mom took the toy back to the store and said sorry to the store man. Tim could not play with the toy anymore, and he felt bad for what he did.

One day, a soft bunny named Bob went for a walk. He hopped to the west, where the sun was going down. As he hopped, he saw his friend, a big bear named Ben. "Hi, Ben!" said the soft bunny. "Can you lend me your big red ball?" "Sure, Bob!" said the big bear. "Here you go!" Ben gave the big red ball to Bob. They played with the ball until it was dark. As the sun went down in the west, Bob and Ben went home. They were happy to play together and share the big red ball. The soft bunny and the big bear were good friends who liked to help each other.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a thoughtful girl named Anna. She loved to collect small rocks from her yard. She had a big box full of them. Each day, she would go outside and look for new rocks to add to her box. One sunny day, while Anna was collecting rocks, she saw a grill in her yard. She had never seen a grill before. She thought it looked like a fun toy to play with. Anna decided to put her rocks on the grill to see what would happen. Anna left the rocks on the grill and went inside to eat lunch. She forgot about the rocks on the grill. Her mom turned on the grill to cook food for the family. The rocks got very hot and started to make a lot of smoke. The smoke filled the yard and the house. Anna and her family had to leave their home because it was not safe to stay inside.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved popcorn. One day, he went to the store with his mom to buy a big bag of popcorn. Tim was very happy and excited. When they got home, Tim opened the bag of popcorn. He ate and ate until he was full. Tim was fit and liked to run, so he decided to go outside and play. He left the popcorn on the table. While Tim was outside, a magic wind came into the house. The wind saw the popcorn and decided to make it disappear. The popcorn was gone when Tim came back inside. Tim was sad and confused. Tim's mom saw that he was sad and told him not to worry. She said they could go to the store and buy more popcorn. Tim smiled and hugged his mom. They went to the store again and Tim got a new bag of popcorn. Tim was happy, and he made sure to keep an eye on his popcorn so it wouldn't disappear again.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Grace. Grace loved to print pretty pictures. She had a big machine that helped her do it. Every day, she would print new pictures and show them to her friends. One day, Grace wanted to make something special. She thought about fashion. She wanted to make pretty clothes for her friends. So, she used her machine to print pictures of dresses, shirts, and shoes. Grace's friends loved the fashion she made. They put on the clothes and danced around. They looked so graceful in their new outfits. They thanked Grace for her wonderful gift. And from that day on, they all wore the pretty clothes that Grace printed for them.
