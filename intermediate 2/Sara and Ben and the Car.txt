Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Car

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and watch TV. One day, Sara has a new toy. It is a network of trains and tracks. Sara is very happy. She wants to show Ben her network. She calls Ben on the phone. "Ben, come to my house. I have a new toy. It is very fun. You will like it." Ben is excited. He says, "OK, Sara. I will come soon. Bye." Ben runs to his house. He puts on his shoes and coat. He grabs his bike. He does not tell his mom where he is going. He is in a hurry. He rides his bike fast. He does not look at the road. He does not see the car. The car hits him. Ben falls down. He is hurt. He cries. The car stops. The driver gets out. He is sorry. He says, "Oh, no. I am sorry. Are you OK? I did not see you. I will call for help." Sara waits for Ben. She does not know what happened. She is bored. She plays with her network. She makes the trains go round and round. She relaxes. She thinks Ben will come soon. She does not know he will not.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat. The cat liked to perform tricks. One day, the cat went to get a haircut. While getting the haircut, the cat saw a big dog. The dog was watching the cat perform tricks. The cat wanted to be friends with the dog. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog started to perform tricks too! The cat and the dog became best friends. They both liked to perform tricks and get haircuts together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a chubby boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside, but he especially loved swimming in the lake. One day, Tom asked his mom if she could teach him how to make a raft. His mom agreed, so they went to the lake. Tom and his mom gathered wood and worked together to build the raft. Tom was so excited as he watched it come together. Once it was finished, Tom couldn't wait to try it out. He tried to get on the raft but it was too wobbly. He and his mom tried to fix the raft but the wood kept breaking. Tom was so frustrated and started to cry. His mom smiled and decided to teach him how to swim. She showed him how to doggy paddle and soon Tom was swimming across the lake with ease. Tom was so happy, he had learnt a new skill! His mom hugged him as they watched the chubby boy swim across the lake. From that day forward Tom never stopped swimming.

Once upon a time, there was a big tower in a small town. In the tower, there lived a happy, enthusiastic bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly high in the sky and sing pretty songs. One day, a mean cat came to the town. The cat wanted to catch Tim and eat him. Tim was very scared. He did not want to be eaten by the mean cat. So, Tim decided to pray for help. He closed his eyes and prayed, "Please help me, I do not want to be eaten by the mean cat." A friendly dog heard Tim's prayer. The dog did not like the mean cat either. The dog wanted to help Tim. So, the dog barked very loud at the mean cat. The cat got scared and ran away. Tim was very happy and thankful. He thanked the dog for saving him. Tim and the dog became best friends. They played together every day, and the mean cat never came back to the town again.

Mia liked to polish her rocks. She had many rocks of different colors and shapes. She found them in the park, in the yard, and sometimes in her pockets. She used a soft cloth and some water to make them shiny and smooth. One day, she found a very special rock. It was white and sparkly, and it had many tiny bits inside. She thought it was the prettiest rock she ever saw. She ran to show it to her mom. "Look, Mom, look what I found!" she said, holding the rock in her hand. "Oh, Mia, that's a beautiful rock!" her mom said. "Do you know what it is?" Mia shook her head. She did not know. "It's a mineral, Mia. A mineral is a kind of rock that has special things inside. Some minerals are very rare and valuable. Do you want to polish it?" Mia nodded. She wanted to polish her mineral and make it even more pretty. She followed her mom to the sink, where they got some water and a cloth. But as Mia started to polish her mineral, something strange happened. The white and sparkly part started to dissolve in the water, and the tiny bits inside fell out. Mia gasped. Her mineral was gone! "Mia, I'm sorry, but your mineral was not a real mineral. It was a fake one, made of sugar and glitter. Someone must have left it in the park as a joke. It looked pretty, but it was not strong or lasting." Mia felt sad and angry. She wanted her mineral back. She did not like jokes like that. "Mom, who did this? Why did they do this?" she asked. "I don't know, Mia. Maybe they wanted to have some fun, or maybe they wanted to trick someone. But they were not very nice or smart. They wasted their sugar and glitter, and they made you unhappy. You know what, Mia? You don't need fake minerals. You have many real rocks that are much more pretty and interesting. Why don't we polish them together, and learn more about them?" Mia smiled. She liked that idea. She still missed her mineral, but she knew her mom was right. She had many real rocks that she loved, and she wanted to polish them and make them shine. She hugged her mom, and they went to polish her rocks. Mia learned that real rocks were better than fake minerals, and that polish could not hide the truth.
