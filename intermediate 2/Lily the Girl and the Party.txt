Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Party

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was very glad because today was her birthday party. She was turning four years old. She wanted everything to be perfect for her friends who were coming. In the morning, Lily helped her mom polish the table. She wanted it to be shiny and clean for the party. They put a pretty cloth on the table and set out plates and cups for everyone. When the party started, Lily and her friends played fun games. They laughed and danced together. They ate yummy cake and sang "Happy Birthday" to Lily. At the end of the party, Lily felt so happy and knew she had the best birthday ever.

Ben and Mia like to play with clay. They make shapes and animals with their hands. One day, they find a big lump of clay in the yard. They want to share it, but it is too heavy. "I can lift it!" Ben says. He tries to pick up the clay, but it falls on his foot. He cries out in pain. "No, I can lift it!" Mia says. She tries to pull the clay, but it sticks to the ground. She gets angry and stomps her feet. They start to fight over the clay. They push and pull and shout at each other. They do not see the dog coming. The dog is big and black. He likes to play with clay too. He sees the lump of clay and thinks it is a toy. He runs towards it, barking loudly. Ben and Mia hear the dog. They stop fighting and look up. They are scared of the dog. He is bigger than them and has sharp teeth. "Help!" Ben and Mia yell. They run away from the clay and the dog. They hide behind a tree and hug each other. The dog reaches the clay. He sniffs it and bites it. He does not like the taste. He spits it out and shakes his head. He runs away, looking for something else to play with. Ben and Mia peek from behind the tree. They see the clay on the ground. It is dirty and broken. They do not want it anymore. They look at each other and say sorry. They are friends again. They decide to play with something else. They are alert and careful. They do not want to meet the dog again.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a powerful gift. He could observe things that others could not see. One day, he saw a big cat stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help the cat, but he was too small to reach it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help. Sam was tall and strong. Together, they tried to get the cat down, but the tree was too high. They could not reach the cat, and it was getting dark. They felt sad that they could not help. Just when they were about to give up, Tim's gift helped them. He observed a long stick on the ground. Tim and Sam used the stick to reach the cat. They saved the cat and became heroes. In the end, Tim's powerful gift helped them solve the problem.

Jill and her dad were walking through the jungle. Suddenly, Jill's dad said to her, "Look! A tiger!". Jill looked up and saw a big, orange tiger. She was scared. Suddenly, the tiger dived into the river. The river was clear and blue, and Jill could see the tiger swimming in it. She got excited and told her dad, "Can I dive in too?". Her dad said, "Yes, but be careful." Jill smiled and dived into the river. The water felt cool and nice. Suddenly, she saw the tiger swimming over to her. She was so excited! She and the tiger swam together in the clear river. When they were done, Jill and her dad went home. Jill had the best day ever.

One day, a cute little elephant and a tiny mouse met in the forest. The elephant had a big trunk, and the mouse had a small tail. They wanted to play together, so they decided to unite and be friends. The cute elephant and the tiny mouse played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. The big trunk and the small tail made a great team. They were very happy to be friends. But then, a big storm came. The wind blew strong and the rain fell hard. The cute elephant and the tiny mouse tried to find a safe place, but they could not. The storm was too big and scary. In the end, the cute elephant and the tiny mouse got lost and could not find each other again.
