Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Slide

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He was eager to play on the slide. When he got there, he saw his friend, Sue. Sue was playing with a twisty toy. Tim asked, "Can I play too?" Sue said, "Yes, but let's go on the slide first!" They both climbed up and slid down. They laughed and had fun. Then, they went back to the twisty toy. Tim tried to twist it, but it was stuck. Sue said, "I know! Let's go on the slide again. Maybe it will help." They went on the slide many times. When they came back, the twisty toy was not stuck anymore. They twisted it and played together all day. The slide had helped them have a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby kid named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and run around all day. He had many friends and they all liked to play together. One day, Tim and his friends were playing outside. They played tag, hide and seek, and other fun games. After a while, they all got very tired. Tim's mom said it was time for a nap. Tim and his friends went inside and laid down on soft beds. They closed their eyes and took a nice long nap. When they woke up, they felt happy and full of energy. They went back outside and played some more. The end.

One day, young Henry went to visit his grandparents' house. As soon as he entered the house, he saw a cane in the corner. He asked his grandmother what it was for. She said: "Why don't you pretend to be an old man, and use the cane like one?". Knowing that his grandparents loved him, Henry thought it would make them happy, so he pretended to be an old man. He even walked slowly, as though he was very old. Surprised, his grandparents smiled. Henry was so happy that they were enjoying his game. Then, his grandmother said: "I have a surprise for you". She gave him a pair of glasses and said: "you can pretend to be even older now!". His grandparents smiled even wider. Henry put on the glasses and kept pretending. He wobbled and gave a toothless grin across the room. His grandparents laughed and said "you look so cute when you pretend!". Henry was so delighted that he kept pretending until it was time to go. He was happy to see his grandparents smiling and enjoyed the moment.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tommy. Tommy loved playing outside, but he also loved decorating his room. He had many ornaments stored in a box by his bed. One day, Tommy asked his mum to help him put the ornaments on the walls. His mum said "of course!", and took out the box of decorations. But there were too many for Tommy to reach the high walls by himself. So, Tommy's mum lifted him up so he could place the ornaments all around the room. She said, "There, now your room looks beautiful." Tommy was so excited about all of the decorations in his room and smiled from ear to ear. He made sure to store all of the decorations in the box so he could put them up again next time. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a wise bird. This bird lived in a tree. The bird liked to help his friends. One day, his friend, a little cat, got stuck on a wire. The cat was scared. The wise bird wanted to help the cat. He thought hard about what to do. The bird told the cat to act brave. The cat listened to the wise bird. The cat acted brave and got off the wire. The cat was happy and said "thank you" to the wise bird. The wise bird told the cat, "When you act brave, you can do big things." The cat learned to be brave and they lived happily ever after.

Lily loved to pick daisies in the field. She liked their white and yellow colors and their soft petals. She made a crown of daisies for her head and a necklace of daisies for her neck. She felt like a princess. She saw a big dog in the field. He was brown and furry and had a long tail. He ran towards her and barked. Lily was scared. She thought the dog might bite her or take her daisies. She held her daisies tight and ran away. But the dog did not want to hurt her. He wanted to play with her and be her friend. He followed her and wagged his tail. He licked her hand and nuzzled her face. He was gentle and kind. He did not touch her daisies. Lily stopped running and looked at the dog. She saw his big eyes and his wet nose. She felt his warm tongue and his soft fur. She smiled and petted him. She gave him a daisy to chew. She said, "You are a good dog. I trust you. You can be my friend." The dog was happy. He wagged his tail and barked. He put his head on her lap and closed his eyes. He felt her fingers in his fur and her breath on his ear. He said, "You are a nice girl. I trust you. You are my friend."
