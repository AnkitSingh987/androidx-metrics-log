Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Old and the Man

One day, a poor little girl was walking down the street. She noticed that everyone around her was skipping and having fun. She wanted to join in the fun, but she was too shy. Suddenly, she stubbed her toe and started to cry. A friendly old man saw the little girl crying and asked her what was wrong. She told him that she wanted to join in with the other children, and that she was too shy. The old man held out his hand and said, "Why not try skipping instead?" The little girl thought about it for a moment, and then she smiled at the old man. She took his hand and together they started to skip down the street. Soon enough, she was having as much fun as everyone else! The old man smiled at her and said, "You see? You just need to be brave and have a go!" The little girl smiled and thanked the old man for his kindness. From that day on, she remembered the old man's words and was not afraid to try things out of her comfort zone. She learned a valuable lesson that day - that with bravery, anything is possible!

Once upon a time, there was a lonely, yellow duck. The duck lived in a small pond. The pond was in a big park. The duck had no friends. It was sad. One sunny day, the duck went to soak in the water. It loved to play and swim. It was fun. The duck saw a little girl by the pond. She was holding a yellow ball. The little girl threw the ball into the pond. The duck swam to it. The duck pushed the ball back to the girl. They played together. The duck was not lonely anymore. They were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like the park. He would say, "I hate the park!" One sunny day, Tim's mom took him to the park. She wanted him to have fun and play with other kids. At the park, Tim saw a big, comfortable chair. He sat down and watched the other kids play. They were playing with a ball, running, and laughing. Tim started to feel a little better. He thought, "Maybe the park is not so bad." Tim's mom came over and asked, "Do you want to play with the other kids?" Tim said, "Yes!" He played with the ball, ran, and laughed with the other kids. From that day on, Tim loved the park. He learned that the park can be a fun and comfortable place to be.

One day, a fat kitten named Fluffy went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Fluffy jumped and climbed up, up, up. He was very happy. But then, Fluffy saw a bird. The bird said, "You can't stay in my tree!" Fluffy was sad and scared. He didn't know how to get down. Fluffy said, "Please help me, bird." The bird saw that Fluffy was scared. The bird said, "I will help you if you promise not to come back." Fluffy said, "I promise." Then, Fluffy closed his eyes and prayed for help. The bird flew down and showed Fluffy how to climb down the tree. Fluffy was very happy and thanked the bird. They both said goodbye, and Fluffy walked back home. From that day on, Fluffy never climbed the big tree again.

Once there was a girl named Mandy. She was only 3 years old. One day, Mandy was walking outside in the dark. Suddenly, she saw something that made her stop. It was a big, glowing ball. It was beautiful. Mandy was so excited that she clapped her hands and ran towards it. As she got closer, she saw a sign next to the glowing ball. It said “Welcome”. Mandy didn't know what it meant, so she walked closer and closer. She saw something strange. The glowing ball was a bit bitter. She was scared and stepped back. Mandy didn't know what was happening. But then, in the darkness, Mandy heard a voice. It said “Welcome, Mandy. Have fun and it won't be bitter.” Mandy smiled and stepped closer again. She couldn't believe how wonderful the glowing ball was. From then on, Mandy visited the glowing ball every day. It was her special place, filled with warmth and welcome.
