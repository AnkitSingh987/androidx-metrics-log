Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Duck

One day, a cute little dog named Max went to the river with his family. Max saw a kayak near the water and wanted to play with it. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the kayak, please?" His mom said, "Sure, but be careful not to kick it too hard, or it might go away!" Max played with the kayak, and he was very happy. He kicked it gently and it moved a little bit in the water. Max felt like a big, strong dog. Later that day, Max saw a duck swimming in the river. He wanted to play with the duck too. Max remembered what his mom said about not kicking too hard. So, he kicked the kayak gently again, and it moved closer to the duck. The duck saw Max and swam closer to the kayak. Max and the duck played together, and they became good friends. Max was very happy that he listened to his mom and did not kick the kayak too hard.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a good cat named Lily. Lily liked to balance on logs and rocks. She loved her life in the forest. One day, Lily saw a big bird in the sky. The bird came down and took her to a new place. Lily was scared but the bird was nice. The new place was full of fun things to balance on. Lily made new friends in the new place. She played with them all day. Then, the big bird came back. The bird took Lily home to the forest. But now, Lily knew that the world was big and full of fun things. Her life was even better now.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in the park with his friends. One day, Tim saw a new boy at the park. The new boy looked sad and lonely. Tim went to the new boy and said, "Hi, I am Tim. Do you want to play with me?" The new boy smiled and said, "Yes, I am Sam. I thought the park was boring, but now I am happy to play with you." Tim and Sam played all day at the park. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim told Sam the truth, "I was scared to talk to you at first, but I am glad I did." Sam said, "I am happy too, Tim. Now we are friends." The moral of the story is to always be kind and make new friends, even if you are scared at first.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old frog. He lived near a pond in the woods. One day, a little girl came to the pond to explore. She happened to bring a microscope with her. When the frog saw this, he hopped over to her. “What is that?” he croaked. “This is a microscope,” the little girl explained. “You can look at very small things with it.” The frog was fascinated. “Can I try it?” he asked. So the little girl helped him look through the microscope at some tiny flowers. When spring came, the frog used the microscope to watch the flowers sprout from the ground. The little girl was amazed at how wise the frog was. She would visit him often to learn more about the wonders of nature.

Jimmy liked to play outside with his friends. One day, his friend Benny asked, “Hey Jimmy. Do you want to play hide and seek?” Jimmy was ready to play, so he said, “Yes!” So, they found a great spot to hide in. Benny ran away to hide, and Jimmy started to count. Once he got to 10, he started searching. He peered around the park and spotted Benny hiding behind a mint bush. Jimmy shouted, “Found you!” and went to get Benny. But then he noticed Benny didn't have anything in his hands. Jimmy asked, “Where did you hide my mint?” Benny smiled and said, “I gained it. I wanted a prize for winning at hide and seek.” Jimmy was a little sad, but he was still happy that Benny was ready for their game.
