Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Hill

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, Tim saw an icy hill. He wanted to roll his ball down the hill. Tim was very excited. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to borrow his spike. Sam said yes. Tim put the spike at the bottom of the icy hill. He thought it would be fun to see the ball hit the spike. Tim rolled his ball down the icy hill. The ball went fast and hit the spike. But, the ball popped! Tim was very sad. His ball was broken and he could not play with it anymore. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. Tim was happy to play, but Spot was a bit fearful. Tim saw a big hill and said, "Let's go down the hill, Spot!" Spot looked at the hill and frowned. They started to go down the hill. Suddenly, a little girl named Sue came up the hill. Sue was on her bike, and she went zoom! Spot was scared and jumped away. Tim and Sue stopped and looked at each other. "Hi, I'm Tim. This is Spot," said Tim. "Hi, I'm Sue. I didn't mean to scare Spot," said Sue. They all became friends and played together. Spot wasn't fearful anymore, and they all had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lisa. She was only three years old. One day Lisa was walking in the park when she saw something shiny in the grass. She bent down and picked it up. It was a magic wand! She was very happy. She waved the wand and said "I wish I had magic powers!" Suddenly, Lisa felt very weak. The wand had taken away all her strength! She knew she had to go to the Magic Land to get her strength back. So she started to walk. She soon arrived at the Magic Land. Lisa saw a beautiful castle and knocked on the door. A wizard opened the door. He said 'Why have you come?' Lisa told the wizard her story and asked him for help. The wizard said "I'm sorry Lisa, I cannot help you. You should have wished for something else." Lisa was so disappointed. She knew she had to go home, but without her strength, the journey seemed too hard. She walked slowly, feeling weak. The end.

Tom and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. Sometimes they play pretend games. Today they are pretending to be doctors. Tom puts on a white coat and a hat. He takes a ruler and a clipboard. He says, "I am Doctor Tom. I will measure you, Mia. Lie down on the couch." Mia puts on a pink dress and a necklace. She lies down on the couch and smiles. She says, "I am Princess Mia. I have a stomach ache. Can you help me, Doctor Tom?" Tom nods and says, "Yes, I can help you, Princess Mia. Let me measure your stomach. It will not hurt." He puts the ruler on Mia's stomach and writes something on the clipboard. He says, "Your stomach is 20 centimeters long. That is very good. But why does it hurt?" Mia thinks for a moment and says, "Maybe because I ate too much cake. It was so yummy. It was chocolate cake with pink frosting. It was my original cake. Mommy made it for me." Tom says, "Oh, I see. That is why your stomach hurts. You ate too much cake. You should eat less cake and more fruits and vegetables. They are good for you. They make you healthy and strong." Mia says, "You are right, Doctor Tom. I will eat less cake and more fruits and vegetables. Thank you for helping me. You are a very good doctor." Tom says, "You are welcome, Princess Mia. You are a very brave patient. Now let us play something else. Do you want to be a pirate or a fairy?"

Once upon a time, there was a little intelligent insect named Buzz. Buzz lived in a big green tree. He had many friends in the tree. One day, Buzz saw a fire near the tree. The fire began to burn the tree. Buzz was scared. He went to his friend, Ant. "Ant, the tree is burning! We need to help our friends!" said Buzz. Ant nodded and they went to tell the others. Together, Buzz, Ant, and their friends worked to stop the fire. They used water from a nearby pond. Finally, the fire was out. All the insects were safe. They thanked Buzz and Ant for their help. From that day on, the insects were more careful and lived happily in their big green tree.
