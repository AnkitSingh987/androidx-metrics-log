Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Eraser

One day, a girl named Mia found a wide eraser. The eraser was big and pink. Mia liked the eraser a lot. She held it and smiled. She wanted to show her friend Tom. Mia ran to Tom and said, "Look! I found a big eraser!" Tom looked at the eraser and smiled too. "Wow! That is a big eraser!" he said. They both laughed and played with the eraser. Then, something strange happened. The eraser started to move! Mia and Tom were surprised. The eraser jumped up and down. It could talk! "Hello, Mia and Tom!" said the eraser. They all played and had fun together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a lonely cat named Tom. Tom was sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, Tom decided to go on a walk to find a friend. As Tom walked, he saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it, but it was too close to a house. Tom did not want to disturb the people inside, so he looked around for someone to ask for help. He saw a police officer named Lily. Tom said, "Lily, can you help me get the ball?" Lily smiled and said, "Of course, Tom! Let's get the ball together." They got the ball and started playing. Tom was not lonely anymore, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a curious little boy named Tim. Tim liked to ask questions and learn new things. One day, he heard his mom and dad discuss something he never heard before. They were talking about a hurricane coming to the village. Tim asked his mom, "What is a hurricane?" His mom said, "It is a big, strong wind that can do a lot of damage." Tim felt scared, but his mom told him not to worry. She said they would work together to keep their home safe. Tim, his mom, and his dad worked hard to protect their home. They closed all the windows and doors tightly. They put away all the things that could fly away. When the hurricane came, their home stayed safe. Tim learned that when we work together and help each other, we can face big problems. And that was a very good lesson for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, red bow that she loved very much. She wore it every day in her hair. One sunny day, Sue went to play at the park. It was very dry, and the grass crunched under her feet. She played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. Sue was having so much fun that she did not notice her bow had fallen out of her hair. Sue's friend, Tom, saw the bow on the ground. He picked it up and yelled, "Sue, your bow! Your bow fell out!" Sue ran to Tom and thanked him with a big hug. She put her bow back in her hair and they played together until it was time to go home.

Ben and Mia are playing in their room. They have many toys and books, but they are not tidy. They throw them on the floor and on the bed. Their mom is not happy. She says, "You have to tidy your room before dinner. It is a mess!" Ben and Mia do not want to tidy their room. They want to keep playing. They have a big case with wheels. They like to put things in the case and roll it around. They think it is fun. They say, "Let's hide in the case and roll away. Then mom will not find us and we will not have to tidy our room." They put some pillows and blankets in the case and climb inside. They close the lid and roll the case under the bed. They giggle and whisper. They think they are very clever. They do not hear their mom coming. Their mom opens the door and looks around. She sees the toys and books on the floor and on the bed. She shakes her head and sighs. She says, "Where are Ben and Mia? They have to tidy their room before dinner. It is a mess!" She starts to search for them. She looks in the closet, in the drawers, in the bathroom, but she does not find them. She wonders where they are. She goes back to their room and looks under the bed. She sees the case with wheels. She thinks it is strange. She says, "What is this?" She pulls the case out and opens the lid. She sees Ben and Mia inside. They look surprised and scared. They say, "Hi, mom!" Their mom is not angry. She is amused. She says, "What are you doing in the case? Are you playing hide and seek?" Ben and Mia nod and smile. They say, "Yes, mom. We are playing hide and seek. We are sorry we are not tidy. We will tidy our room after dinner. We promise." Their mom hugs them and says, "Okay, I forgive you. But you have to tidy your room after dinner. It is a mess!" She helps them out of the case and puts it away. She says, "Now, come on. It is time for dinner. You must be hungry." Ben and Mia follow their mom to the kitchen. They are hungry and happy. They think their mom is the best.
