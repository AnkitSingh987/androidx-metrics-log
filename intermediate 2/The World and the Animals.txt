Category: Intermediate
Title: The World and the Animals

Once upon a time, there was a big world. In this world, there were many animals. They were all friends. They liked to play and have fun together. But sometimes, it was difficult for them to share. One day, the animals wanted to play a game. They had to include everyone in the game. The big animals and the small animals all had to play together. It was not easy, but they tried very hard. In the end, the animals learned that sharing and including everyone was important. It made the game more fun for all of them. The world was a happier place when they worked together. And that is how they learned that being kind and including others makes everything better.

One day, Tim and Sue went to play in the attic. They found a big box with many old toys. Tim picked up a fake bear and said, "Look, Sue! This bear is not real!" Sue looked at the bear and did not understand. "What do you mean, Tim? It looks like a real bear to me." Tim laughed and said, "No, Sue. This bear is fake. It is not a real bear. It is a toy bear." Just then, something unexpected happened. The fake bear started to move! It stood up and began to dance. Tim and Sue were very surprised. "Wow!" said Sue. "I did not know a fake bear could dance like that!" Tim and Sue laughed and played with the dancing bear all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little square. The square had four friends. They liked to play together. One day, they saw a big sign. The sign was near a big tree. The sign had a picture of a round thing. The square and friends wanted to try the round thing. They pushed it and it started to spin. They went on the round thing and it spun faster. The square got dizzy. It was fun but also made him feel funny. After the round thing stopped, the square and friends went back to the tree. They played under the tree until the sun went down. They were happy and tired. The square went home and told his mom about the dizzy round thing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a powerful friend named Max. Max was a big, strong lion. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, Lily had a pain in her leg. She couldn't play with Max. Lily was very sad. She sat under a tree and started to cry. Max didn't want Lily to be sad, so he had an idea. Max told Lily to close her eyes and pray. Lily did what Max said. She closed her eyes and prayed for the pain to go away. When she opened her eyes, the pain was gone! Lily and Max were so happy. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tim. Tim loved to look at the stars at night. One night, he saw a very bright star. It was so bright that Tim wanted to hold it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I hold the star?" His mom said, "Stars are too far away, but you can hold this shiny toy star instead." Tim was happy with the toy star, and he held it tight. Tim learned that even if he couldn't hold a real star, he could still enjoy the shiny toy star. The moral of the story is to be happy with what you have, even if it's not what you wanted at first.

One day, a little dog named Spot had a very smelly nose. He did not like it at all. Spot wanted to find a way to make his nose not smelly anymore. So, he went to his friend, a big cat named Fluffy. "Fluffy, my nose is smelly. What can I do?" Spot asked. Fluffy thought about it and said, "You can wash your nose in the pond. That will make it not smelly." Spot liked the idea, so he went to the pond. When Spot got to the pond, he saw a duck swimming. The duck said, "Hi Spot! Are you here to swim too?" Spot told the duck, "No, I am here to wash my smelly nose." The duck watched as Spot washed his nose in the pond. After Spot washed his nose, it was not smelly anymore. Spot was happy, and the duck was happy too. They played together in the pond all day.
