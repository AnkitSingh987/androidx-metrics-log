Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Ann and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Ann. Ann wanted to go outside and play with her friends. She picked up her hat, which was a sunny yellow colour. Then she stepped outside, and the sunny day made the air feel clear and refreshing. Ann saw all of her friends playing together. She ran to join them, and they started to play an interesting game. "Let's play hide and seek," one of her friends said. Everyone agreed and Ann was chosen to count first. Ann started to count and then quickly stepped behind a tree. The other children all ran away to hide. It didn't take Ann long to find one of her friends. He was hiding behind a bush and wearing a green hat. "I found you!" Ann shouted. The little boy laughed and said, "It was so easy for you to find me, my green hat was too clear against the bush!" Ann and her friend laughed and ran away to join the others in the game. They had so much fun in the sunny and clear day!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved summer. She liked to play outside with her friends. One day, Lily found a magic key. The key could unlock a special door. Lily was very happy. Lily and her friends went to find the special door. They looked and looked. They found the door in a big tree. Lily used the key to unlock the door. When the door opened, they saw a beautiful land with lots of yummy treats. They were so excited! But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and closed the door. Lily and her friends were upset. They thought they were stuck in the beautiful land forever. But then, Lily found another key under a flower. She used it to unlock the door again, and they could go back home. They learned that sometimes, things can be scary, but they can be okay in the end.

Anna and Ben were friends. They liked to play outside. One day, they went to the river. The river was big and fast. Anna and Ben saw a tall tree near the river. The tree had a long vine. "Look, a vine!" Anna said. "We can swing on it!" "OK, let's try!" Ben said. They ran to the tree. Anna grabbed the vine. She wrapped it around her hand. She jumped and swung over the river. "Wow, this is fun!" Anna said. "You try, Ben!" Ben grabbed the vine. He wrapped it around his hand. He jumped and swung over the river. But he swung too far. He let go of the vine. He fell into the river. "Help, help!" Ben shouted. "The water is cold and strong!" Anna was scared. She ran to the edge of the river. She tried to reach Ben. But he was too far. The river took him away. "Ben, Ben!" Anna cried. "Come back, come back!" But Ben did not come back. He was gone. Anna was alone. She was sad. She wished she never played with the vine.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the kitchen. She saw her mom cooking in a gray pan. Mia noticed that the pan was very hot. She wanted to help her mom. Mia asked, "Mom, can I help you cook?" Her mom said, "Yes, Mia. You can help me stir the food in the pan. But be careful, it is very hot." Mia was happy to help her mom. She took a big spoon and started to stir the food in the gray pan. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise outside. Mia and her mom looked out the window. They saw a big red ball in their yard. The ball had hit their window. Mia and her mom were surprised. Mia's friend, Tim, came running into the yard. He said, "I am sorry, Mia. My ball hit your window. I did not mean to do it." Mia said, "It is okay, Tim. Let's go play with the ball after I help my mom cook." Mia and her mom finished cooking. Then, Mia and Tim went outside to play with the big red ball. They had lots of fun and forgot about the surprise from the ball hitting the window.
