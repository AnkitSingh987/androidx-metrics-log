Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Rabbit and the Turtle

One day, a fast rabbit and a slow turtle had a race. The rabbit was very sure he would win. He ran very fast at first, but then he saw a big tree. The rabbit thought, "I can rest here and still win." So, he lay down under the tree. The turtle did not stop. He kept walking, even when it started to rain. The rain made the ground wet, but the turtle did not mind. He just kept going. The rabbit woke up and saw the rain. He said, "Oh no! I must run fast now!" But the turtle was already near the end. The rabbit ran as fast as he could, but the turtle won. The rabbit said, "You kept going and did not stop, even when it was wet. You are a great racer!" The turtle smiled and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to look at the sky. The sky was blue and pretty. One day, Lily saw a big, dangerous bird in the sky. The bird had sharp claws and a long beak. Lily was scared, but she wanted to make friends with the bird. Lily thought, "I will invite the bird to play with me." She called out to the bird, "Hello, big bird! Do you want to come down and play with me?" The bird looked at Lily and flew down to the ground. The dangerous bird said, "I am not used to playing with little girls. I am big and scary. Are you sure you want to play with me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend. We can play together and have fun." So, Lily and the dangerous bird played together. They ran around and laughed. Soon, the bird was not dangerous anymore. The bird became Lily's best friend. They played together every day under the beautiful sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because it was very cold outside, and he had to go to the grocery store with his mom. He put on his coat and hat, but he still felt a shiver as they walked to the store. At the grocery store, Tim saw a small, shivering dog outside. The dog was also sad and cold. Tim told his mom, "Mom, the dog is cold and sad like me. Can we help him?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's find something to make him warm and happy." Tim and his mom went inside the store and found a soft, warm blanket. They bought the blanket and some dog food. When they went back outside, they wrapped the shivering dog in the warm blanket and gave him some food. The dog wagged his tail and looked very happy. Tim was no longer sad, and neither was the little dog. They had both found warmth and happiness together. And from that day on, Tim and the dog became best friends, and they never felt cold or sad again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was a good boy, but he would often forget things. One day, he went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for the house. Tom's mom told him, "Tom, don't forget to pick up the apples." Tom saw many things in the store. He saw toys, candy, and books. He forgot about the apples. He picked up a toy car instead. When they got to the checkout, Tom's mom saw the toy car. "Tom, did you forget the apples?" she asked. Tom looked sad and said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I forgot." They went back to get the apples. Tom's mom told him, "We don't want to waste time, so let's hurry." They found the apples and saw that there were many available. Tom picked the best ones and put them in the cart. When they got home, Tom's mom said, "Thank you for getting the apples, Tom. Next time, try not to forget." Tom nodded and said, "I will remember, Mom." From that day on, Tom tried his best not to forget things. He knew that it was important to listen and not waste time. He also learned that there are always good things available if you look for them.
