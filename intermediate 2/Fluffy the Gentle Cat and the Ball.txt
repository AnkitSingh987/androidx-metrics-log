Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Gentle Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her ball. One day, she saw the ball on top of a hill. Fluffy climbed the hill to get her ball. As she reached the top, she saw her friend, Bunny. Bunny was also playing on the hill. "Hi, Fluffy!" said Bunny. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Bunny, let's play!" said Fluffy. They started to play, and Fluffy's ball began to roll down the hill. Fluffy and Bunny ran after the ball, but they were not fast enough. The ball started to fall faster and faster down the hill. Fluffy and Bunny were sad that they could not catch the ball. But then, they saw the ball stop near a tree. They were happy to find the ball again. They went back to the top of the hill and played together all day long. The gentle cat and her friend Bunny had a fun day of play.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play outside. One day, she saw a long pole. Kitty thought it would be fun to play with the pole. Kitty tried to get the pole, but it was too high. She needed help. She asked her friend, Doggy, to help her. Doggy was big and strong. He could reach the long pole. Doggy got the pole for Kitty. They played with the pole all day. They had so much fun. In the end, Kitty and Doggy were happy. They knew they needed each other to have fun and play with the long pole.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his friend, Sam, went to play outside. They found a big box with a zip on it. Tim said, "Let's open it!" Sam said, "We must be careful." They carefully opened the zip. Inside the box, they saw many toys. They started to play with the toys. But then, they heard a voice say, "Hey! Those are my toys!" It was their friend, Lily. Tim and Sam were surprised. They didn't know the toys belonged to Lily. Tim said, "We are sorry, Lily. We didn't know these were your toys." Lily was upset. She said, "You should have asked me first!" Tim and Sam felt bad. They wanted to make Lily happy again. So, they decided to play a guessing game with her. Tim said, "Lily, let's play a game. You can guess which toy is in my hand." Lily agreed to play the game. They took turns guessing which toy was in each other's hands. Soon, they were all laughing and having fun together. They learned to be more careful and always ask before using someone else's things.

Once there were two friends, Billy and Joe. They loved playing together in the park nearby. Their favorite game was to have a tank race. Both of them had small plastic tanks with colorful stripes. They would put their tanks in the middle of the park and then try to be the first to race around the tree and back to the center. Billy was very popular with the other kids in the park. Whenever they heard Billy and Joe were going to have a tank race, they all gathered around to cheer them on. Billy usually won the races, but Joe didn't mind since he loved racing with his friend. He especially loved the feeling of speeding around the tree with his tank. The two friends raced tanks in the park every day and never got tired of it. Billy and Joe kept racing tanks and having fun in the park for many years after that.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty blouse that she liked a lot. One day, she wanted to wear it to play with her friends. While Sue was playing, she saw a cat. The cat was very dirty and spoiled. Sue wanted to help the cat, so she went to get some water. She found a bottle and filled it with water. Sue went back to the cat and said, "I will spray you with water to make you clean." She sprayed the cat, and the cat became clean and happy. But some of the water sprayed on Sue's blouse too. Sue didn't mind because she was happy to help the cat. They played together all day, and Sue's blouse dried in the sun.
