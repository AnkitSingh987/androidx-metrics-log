Category: Intermediate
Title: The Reindeer and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a reindeer. The reindeer had many friends. They liked to play together. One day, the reindeer saw a little girl. The little girl was lost. She was sad and scared. The reindeer knelt down and said, "Don't be sad. I will help you find your way home." The little girl was grateful. She climbed on the reindeer's back. They went through the forest, looking for her home. At last, they found the little girl's house. Her mom and dad were so happy to see her. They thanked the reindeer for helping their girl. The reindeer and the little girl became good friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a doll that she loved very much. She took her doll with her everywhere she went. Sue liked to play with her doll all day. They had a lot of fun together. One day, Sue and her doll were very busy. They played outside in the sun. They ran and jumped and laughed. Sue's doll got very tired from all the playing. Sue saw that her doll needed to sleep, so she took her doll inside the house. Sue put her doll in a soft bed. She gave her doll a big hug and a kiss. Then, Sue sang a sweet song to help her doll sleep. The doll closed her eyes and went to sleep. Sue smiled and knew her doll would have sweet dreams.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play chess with his big sister, Sue. They would sit on the floor and play for hours. One day, Tim and Sue were playing chess when their dog, Spot, ran through the room. Spot knocked over the chess board and spoiled the game. Tim and Sue looked at each other and felt sad. Sue said, "Oh no, Spot spoiled our game!" Tim looked at Spot and said, "I am sorry, Sue. We can start a new game." Sue smiled and said, "That's okay, Tim. Let's play again." They set up the chess board again and started a new game. This time, they made sure to close the door so Spot couldn't spoil their game again. Tim and Sue had fun playing chess and they were not sorry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy box that she loved. Inside the box, there was a special lock. One day, Sue wanted to unlock the box to play with her toys. Sue asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me unlock the box?" Her mom said, "Yes, Sue. I will help you." Sue's mom was slow at unlocking the box. Sue was not happy. She wanted to play with her toys now. While they were trying to unlock the box, Sue's mom accidentally broke the lock. The box could not open anymore. Sue was very sad. She could not measure how sad she was. She could not play with her toys. The end.

Mommy was really angry. She put on her coat, picked up her cell phone and left. Dolly was all alone. She started to get very scared. She called out for her mommy to come back. "Come back Mommy!" Dolly cried out. But Mommy didn't come back. Suddenly, the door opened. It was Daddy! He was wearing his work clothes. Daddy gave Dolly a big hug and said, "Mommy was feeling very angry, so she went out for a little while. But don't worry, she'll be back soon." Dolly smiled, relieved. Just then, Mommy came back. She was smiling, too. Mommy gave Dolly a big hug and said, "I'm not angry anymore, my love." Dolly felt happy and safe. She knew everything was alright now.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with a big kitchen. She loved to lie in the warm sun that came through the kitchen window. One day, a little mouse named Mimi came into the kitchen. Mimi saw Kitty lying in the sun and said, "Hello, Kitty! Can we be friends?" Kitty looked at Mimi and thought for a moment. She knew that cats and mice were not usually friends, but she was a wise cat and decided to give it a try. Kitty and Mimi played in the kitchen every day. They shared their food, told stories, and laughed together. And when they were tired, they would lie down in the warm sun and take a nap. The wise old cat and the little mouse became the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.
