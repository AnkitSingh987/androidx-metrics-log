Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy book. A little girl named Lily loved to read this book every day. It had many fun stories about animals, and she liked to look at the pictures. One day, Lily found a small bottle near the book. The bottle had the word "poison" on it. She did not know what it meant, but she knew it was not good. She showed her mom the bottle, and her mom took it away. Lily was happy that her mom took the poison away. She went back to her room and read her heavy book. She read about a silly cat and a funny dog. She laughed and had a great day.

---

Anna wanted a puppy very much. She liked to imagine how she would play with it, feed it, and cuddle it. She asked her mom and dad many times, but they always said no. They said puppies were too much work and too messy. One day, Anna saw a sign that said "Free Puppies" outside a house. She ran to the door and knocked. A lady opened it and smiled. "Hello, do you want a puppy?" she asked. "Yes, please!" Anna said. She followed the lady to the backyard, where there were many puppies. They were all cute and fluffy, but one caught her eye. It was black and white, with a spot on its nose. Anna picked it up and hugged it. "This is the one I want," she said. The lady nodded. "Okay, but you have to ask your parents first. Puppies need a lot of care and love. Do you have their phone number?" Anna felt uncomfortable. She did not want to ask her parents. She knew they would say no. She wanted to keep the puppy now. She thought of a plan. She said, "I don't have their phone number, but they are at the park. I can take the puppy there and show them. They will love it, I'm sure." The lady looked doubtful. "Are you sure? The park is far away. How will you get there?" Anna said, "I can walk. It's not too far. Please, let me take the puppy. I promise I will bring it back if they don't want it." The lady sighed. She felt sorry for Anna. She said, "Okay, but be careful. And come back soon. The puppy needs water and food." Anna thanked the lady and ran away with the puppy. She was happy. She imagined how her parents would be surprised and happy too. She did not think of the trouble she was in.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big tree with colorful leaves. They wanted to climb the tree and see the sky. "Let's spring up the tree!" Lily said. "OK, but be careful!" Tom said. They jumped and grabbed the branches and climbed higher and higher. They felt happy and free. They saw birds and clouds and flowers. But then, they heard a loud voice. "Hey, you two! Get down from there!" It was the park keeper. He was angry. He said the tree was old and weak and could break. Lily and Tom were scared. They did not want to fall or hurt the tree. They climbed down quickly and said sorry to the park keeper. He gave them a note. It said: "Trees are living things. They need care and respect. Do not climb on them or pull their leaves. They give us shade and air and beauty." Lily and Tom felt bad. They learned their lesson. They thanked the park keeper and promised to be kind to the trees. They decided to play with a ball instead. They threw and caught the ball and had fun. They still liked the tree, but they did not climb it anymore. They looked at its colorful leaves and smiled.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals. Tim liked the zebras the most. They had black and white stripes. Tim saw a zebra eating something. He asked his mom, "What is the zebra eating?" His mom said, "The zebra is eating a sour apple." Tim wanted to see the zebra eat the sour apple. He pointed at the zebra and said, "Look, mom!" The zebra looked at Tim and his mom. It made a funny face because the apple was very sour. Tim laughed and said, "Silly zebra!" His mom laughed too. They had a great day at the zoo.
