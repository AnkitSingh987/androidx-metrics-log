Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bull and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a big bull named Bob. Bob lived on a farm with many other animals. He loved to play with his friends, but sometimes he felt like he did not belong. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the farm. She saw Bob and said, "Hi, big bull! Do you want to play with me?" Bob was happy that someone wanted to play with him. He nodded his head and they started to play. They played all day and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Lucy said, "Bye, Bob! You are my friend and you belong with me." Bob was very happy and felt like he was a part of something special. From that day on, Bob always had a friend available to play with.

Lily liked to wash her toys. She had many toys, but her favorite was a pink bear. She called him Fluffy. She washed Fluffy every day with soap and water. She made him very clean and soft. One day, Lily wanted to wash Fluffy, but she could not find him. She looked in her room, in the living room, in the kitchen, but Fluffy was not there. She felt sad and worried. Where was Fluffy? She asked her mom, "Mom, where is Fluffy? I want to wash him." Her mom smiled and said, "I know where Fluffy is. He is on the bookshelf. He wanted to read some books with me. Do you want to join us?" Lily was happy and ran to the bookshelf. She saw Fluffy sitting on a pile of books. He had a big smile on his face. He was holding a book about bears. Lily hugged Fluffy and said, "Fluffy, I missed you. I'm glad you like books. Can I read with you and mom?" Fluffy nodded and said, "Yes, Lily. I love books. And I love you. But can you wash me later? I don't want to get wet now." Lily laughed and said, "Okay, Fluffy. I will wash you later. But only if you promise to wash me too." Fluffy agreed and said, "Deal. You are my best friend, Lily." Lily, Fluffy, and mom sat on the bookshelf and read some books. They had a lot of fun and learned many things. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a happy girl named Lisa. She loved to play outside with her friends. One sunny day, Lisa went to the park to play with her ball. While playing, Lisa noticed something interesting. It was a big net hanging from a tree. She thought it would be fun to play with the net. So, she ran to the tree and started to play with the net. But, as Lisa played with the net, she got tangled in it. She tried to get out, but she couldn't. Her friends saw her and tried to help, but they couldn't get her out either. They were all very sad. In the end, Lisa stayed stuck in the net and couldn't play with her friends anymore.

Once there was a little girl named Mary who loved animals. One day Mary went outside and saw a snake near her house. Mary thought it was so cool and wanted to feed it. But her mom said, "No way! That snake is dangerous, stay away from it!" Mary was sad, but listened to her mom and stayed away. The next day Mary returned to the same spot. The snake was still there, and Mary wanted so badly to feed it. She had brought a few carrots from the kitchen and was eager to give them to the snake. Again, her mom said, "No way!" She scolded Mary and said that the snake was too dangerous for her to go near. Mary was very sad, but she understood her mom's warnings. She stayed far away from the snake and looked for other animals to feed instead.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful little dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his group of friends in the park. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One sunny day, while they were playing, Buddy's best friend, a little girl named Lily, fell down and hurt her knee. She started to cry. Buddy ran to her side and said, "Don't cry, Lily. I will help you." Buddy gently kissed Lily's knee, and the pain went away. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Buddy. You are so helpful!" They hugged and continued to play with their group of friends, happy and full of love.
