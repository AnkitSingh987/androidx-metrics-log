Category: Intermediate
Title: The Team of Cats and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a team of cats, they were all very cute but they could be quite messy. One day they were playing together in a garden when they all started to ignore each other. The cats were so surprised and started asking why they were being ignored. Suddenly, a little 3 year old child, who was visiting the garden, started to speak. "I know why you are being ignored", said the child. "You all have been playing together for too long and it's become quite a mess. It's time to tidy up and then you can all be friends again". The cats all said thank you very much, they started to pick up all their toys and clean the garden. After they were done, they were all happily playing together again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that he loved very much. It was a very interesting toy. It could walk, talk, and even play with Tim. Every day, Tim and his toy would play together and have lots of fun. One day, Tim set his toy on the table to eat his lunch. While he was eating, he heard a noise. It was his toy talking! The toy said, "Hello, Tim! I want to play with you!" Tim was very surprised. He didn't know his toy could talk without him playing with it. Tim and his toy played all day. They laughed and had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy grew very big! It was now as big as Tim! Tim was so happy. Now he had a big friend to play with. From that day on, Tim and his big toy had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time there was a lonely little could named Dom. He had only one pillow and he slept on it every night. Dom wasn't happy being so lonely. He wished he had a best friend to play and talk with. One day, Dom went down the street to the park to see if he could find a friend. He didn't want to pay to play on the swings, but there was no one else around who he could play with. Then he heard it. "Hello Dom! I'm your friend! Will you pay for us to play together?" said a voice from behind him. Dom turned around and saw a big smile on the face of an old man. He asked Dom if he wanted to pay for them to play together. Dom was so excited he forgot he didn't have any money. But it was too good to be true. The old man grabbed Dom's pillow and ran away. Dom ran and ran, but the old man was too fast. Dom was left with nothing but his lonely heart. He waited, but his friend never came back.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She wanted to discover something new. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. One day, Mia saw a big horse. The horse was sad. Mia knew she had to help the horse. She thought she could make the horse happy again. She remembered she had an apple in her bag. Mia gave the apple to the horse. The horse ate the apple and became happy. Mia felt good because she helped the horse. Now, the horse was not a bad horse anymore. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a cop named Bob was walking in the park. He saw a boy named Tim. Tim was playing a game with his friends. They were running and laughing. Bob wanted to play too, so he went to Tim and asked, "Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes! We are playing a race game. You have to run fast to win." Bob was very excited. He liked to run fast. He was competitive and wanted to win. They started the race, and Bob was in the lead. He was sure he would win. But then, something unexpected happened. A little dog ran into the race. The dog was very fast! The dog passed Bob and won the race. Everyone was surprised. Tim said, "Wow, that dog is fast! Let's play again!" Bob laughed and agreed. He knew he didn't win, but he had fun playing with Tim and his friends.
