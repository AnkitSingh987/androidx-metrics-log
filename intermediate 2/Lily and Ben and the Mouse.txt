Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Mouse

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and look for bugs. One day, they found a small mouse hiding under a leaf. The mouse was scared and ran away. "Let's catch the mouse!" Ben said. He wanted to keep it as a pet. "No, Ben, that's not nice. The mouse wants to be free," Lily said. She was honest and kind. Ben did not listen. He ran after the mouse with a net. Lily followed him. She tried to stop him. They chased the mouse across the garden, but they could not catch it. The mouse was fast and smart. It ran into a hole under a big tree. "Ha! I got you now, mouse!" Ben said. He put his net over the hole. He waited for the mouse to come out. But the mouse did not come out. Instead, a big snake came out. The snake was angry and hungry. It saw Ben and his net. "SSSSS! What are you doing here, boy?" the snake hissed. It opened its mouth wide. "Help! Help!" Ben screamed. He dropped his net and ran away. Lily ran to him. She hugged him and comforted him. "Are you okay, Ben?" she asked. "Yes, I'm okay. But I'm sorry, Lily. You were right. I should not have tried to catch the mouse. It was not nice. And it was dangerous," Ben said. Lily smiled. She was glad that Ben was safe and sorry. "It's okay, Ben. I forgive you. But next time, listen to me. I have good ideas. I suggest we play something else. How about we make a sandcastle?" she said. Ben nodded. He agreed with Lily. He liked her ideas. "Okay, Lily. Let's make a sandcastle. And let's leave the mouse and the snake alone. They are not our friends," he said. They went to the sandbox and played happily. They made a big sandcastle with shells and sticks. They forgot about the mouse and the snake. They were good friends. The end.

Once upon a time, a little girl called Mandy wanted to take a train ride. She was so excited! She took a bright red train with furry seats. When Mandy was on the train, something strange started to happen. Her train began to shrink until it was the size of a toy. Mandy looked around and saw that everybody else on the train had shrunk too. Mandy was so scared. She looked out the window and saw her house still far away. She started to cry. Just then, an old lady appeared. She said, "Don't worry, Mandy. I'll help you get home." Mandy smiled and the old lady used her magic wand to make the train grow back to its normal size. "Where did you learn this magic?" Mandy asked. "That's a secret," the old lady said with a twinkle in her eye. Mandy went home that day, happy and excited from her shrinky train ride.

Once upon a time, in a great big forest, there lived a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find acorns and play with his friends. One sunny day, Sam found a very big acorn. It was the biggest acorn he had ever seen. He knew he had to show it to his best friend, Lily the bunny. Sam ran to Lily's house and said, "Lily, look at this big acorn I found!" Lily looked at the acorn and said, "Wow, Sam! That is a great acorn. We should hide it so we can find it later." Sam and Lily found a secret spot to hide the acorn under a big tree. Later that day, Sam and Lily played with their friends, but they didn't tell anyone about the big acorn. They knew it was their special secret. The sun was setting, and Sam and Lily went back to the big tree to check on their acorn. They found it safe and sound. Sam was so happy that he gave Lily a big kiss on the cheek. "Thank you for helping me hide the acorn, Lily," said Sam. Lily blushed and said, "You're welcome, Sam. That's what friends are for." From that day on, Sam and Lily knew that they would always be the best of friends, sharing secrets and looking out for each other. And they lived happily ever after in their great big forest home.
