Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Cop and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a cop named Tim. Tim was a very fine cop. He liked to decorate his car with stickers. One day, he saw a big box. The box had a note on it. The note said, "Do not open until tomorrow." The next day, Tim went to the box. He was very excited. He opened the box and found a lot of stickers inside. The stickers were for his car. Tim was very happy. Tim started to decorate his car with the new stickers. His friend, Sam, saw him and said, "Wow, Tim! Your car looks great!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sam! I love to decorate my car with stickers." And they both laughed and played together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a big house. The house had a long hall. The hall was dark. Tim was scared, but he wanted to see what was at the end of the hall. "Hello?" Tim said, but no one answered. He walked down the hall. It seemed very long. He saw a door at the end. Tim wanted to open it, but he was still scared. Tim's friend, Lily, came to the house. She saw Tim in the hall. "Don't be scared," Lily said. "I will go with you." They opened the door together. Inside, they found a room full of toys. They played all day and were happy.

Once upon a time, there were two friends - Peggy and Tim. Peggy wanted to go to the park, and Tim said "Yes, let's go". They rushed to the park, but when they got there, they saw a long wait. Peggy was troubled and asked Tim "Why do we have to wait?". Tim smiled and said "It is because there are lots of people here. We just have to be patient and wait our turn". Peggy smiled and said "Okay, I understand". So, the two of them waited, but not for too long. Finally, it was their turn, and they rushed into the park, skipping and laughing. They had a wonderful day, and when the sun started to set, they rushed back home.

Once upon a time there was a famous family with two little children. Every morning, the mother would raise them with a lot of love and care, giving them breakfast and making sure they were ready for school. One day when they were all sitting around the table they asked their mother why they were so famous. Their mother told them that long ago their grandfather had a dream to raise the life of children all over the world. He worked very hard and he was successful. He became famous and now, everyone remembers him and what he worked so hard to do. The children were surprised, but they were also very happy. They thanked their mother for telling them the story and for raising them the way their grandfather had once dreamt.

One day, a little bird wanted to whistle a song. The bird was eager to make friends. The bird flew around and saw a big tree. The tree had many leaves and branches. The bird thought it was a good place to find friends. In the tree, the bird saw a small bug. The bug was round like a pill. The bird said, "Hello, little bug! I want to be friends. Can you whistle a song with me?" The bug smiled and said, "Yes, I can try!" The bird and the bug started to whistle a song together. They whistled loud and soft. They whistled high and low. Soon, other animals heard the song. A cat, a dog, and a rabbit came to the tree. They all wanted to be friends with the bird and the bug. The bird, the bug, the cat, the dog, and the rabbit played together all day. They laughed, ran, and jumped. When the sun went down, they were tired but happy. They all said, "We had so much fun today! Let's play again tomorrow!" And they all became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a driver. He drove a big, heavy truck. The truck had many toys inside. The driver loved to give toys to kids in the town. One day, the heavy truck got stuck in the mud. The driver tried to pull the truck out, but it was too heavy. He needed help. The driver saw a small boy with a toy car. The boy loved to play with toy cars and trucks. The driver asked the boy for help. The boy had an idea. He ran to his toy box and got a big rope. The boy tied the rope to the heavy truck and the driver's car. Together, they pulled and pulled. The heavy truck came out of the mud. The driver was so happy! He thanked the boy and gave him a new toy truck. The boy was happy too. He knew he could help, even if he was small. From that day on, the driver and the boy were best friends. They always helped each other when they needed it.
