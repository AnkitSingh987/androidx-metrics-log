Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bird. The little bird was helpless. It could not find food. The bird was very hungry. One day, a kind squirrel saw the bird. The squirrel said, "Why are you so sad?" The bird said, "I am helpless. I can't find food." The squirrel wanted to help the bird. The squirrel and the bird looked for food together. They found some seeds and nuts. They shared the food. The bird was happy. They both succeed in finding food. The bird and the squirrel became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little white cat. The cat lived in a small house with a nice porch. The cat loved to sit on the porch and watch the birds fly by. One day, the cat saw a big red ball on the porch. The cat wanted to play with the ball, but it had to delay its playtime because it was time to eat. The cat's owner called it inside for some yummy food. After eating, the cat ran back to the porch. The big red ball was still there. The cat had so much fun playing with the ball. It was a very happy day for the little white cat.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She lived in a peaceful little village with her family. Lily loved to play with her friends and laugh all day long. One sunny day, Lily and her friend Tom were playing with a ball. They both wanted to play with the ball at the same time, so they started to quarrel. They were both very sad and didn't want to share. Then, Lily's mom came and saw them quarrel. She told them, "You both can play with the ball if you take turns." Lily and Tom listened to her and decided to share the ball. They played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Lily and Tom always shared their toys and never quarreled again. The peaceful village stayed happy, and everyone played together. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the beach with her mom. Amy was very glad because she loved the beach. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. Amy and her mom put their things on the sand and ran to the water. Amy played in the water and splashed the waves. She liked how the waves tickled her feet. Amy's mom said, "Amy, be careful with the waves. Don't go too far!" Amy smiled and said, "Ok, mom!" After playing in the water, Amy and her mom sat on the sand. They watched the waves go up and down. The sun started to go down, and it was time to go home. Amy was very tired from playing all day. She told her mom, "I'm ready to sleep, mom." Her mom smiled and said, "Me too, Amy. Let's go home and sleep." They walked back to their car, holding hands, and went home to sleep after a fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant. The ant loved to complain. One day, the ant found a big board. The board was very impressive. The ant wanted to show the board to his friends. The ant called his friends to come and see the board. They all came and looked at the impressive board. They were very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The board started to move! The board was not a board at all. It was a big, friendly bug! The bug said, "Hello, little ants! I am not a board. I am a bug just like you!" The ants were surprised but happy. Now, they had a new friend and they did not complain anymore. They played with their new friend, the big, impressive bug, every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a poor boy who lived with his mom. They did not have much food, and Tom was always hungry. One day, Tom found a magic pot. The pot could make spaghetti when Tom would blink his eyes. He blinked, and the pot made lots of yummy spaghetti. Tom and his mom ate the spaghetti and were very happy. Tom thought, "I can help other poor people with my magic pot." So, he went to the town and said, "Look at my magic pot! I can make spaghetti for all of you." The people were excited and came to see. But when Tom blinked, the pot did not make spaghetti. The people were angry and said, "You lied to us!" They took the magic pot away from Tom. Tom and his mom were sad and hungry again. The moral of the story is: do not be greedy and try to show off, or you may lose what you have.
