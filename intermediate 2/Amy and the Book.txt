Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and the Book

Once upon a time, a little girl named Amy loved to read books. One day, she found a new book on her shelf. It was a book about a game. Amy was very excited to read it. In the book, there was a naughty dog named Spot. Spot liked to take things that were not his. One day, Spot took a ball from his friend, Tim. Tim was sad and said, "Spot, that is not nice. You should give the ball back." Spot felt bad and gave the ball back to Tim. They decided to play the game together. As they played the game, they had lots of fun. They learned that sharing is better than being naughty. In the end, Spot and Tim became best friends. When Amy finished the book, she felt happy. She learned that being nice and sharing is important. From that day on, Amy always shared her toys with her friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a jolly cat named Tom. Tom had a pink ball that he loved to play with. He would play with the ball all day long. One day, a naughty dog named Max saw Tom's pink ball. Max wanted to play with the ball too, so he decided to steal it. He took the ball and ran away. Tom was very sad and cried, "Give me back my ball!" Tom's friend, a kind bird named Lily, saw what happened. Lily wanted to help Tom get his ball back. So, she flew high up in the sky to find Max. She saw Max playing with the pink ball near a big tree. Lily went to Max and said, "Max, it is not nice to steal. Please give Tom's ball back to him." Max felt sorry for what he did. He gave the pink ball back to Lily, and she brought it back to Tom. Tom was so happy to have his pink ball back. He thanked Lily for her help. From that day on, Tom, Lily, and Max became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a hurt heel. He could not run and play with his friends. He felt sad. One day, a serious cat named Whiskers saw Spot. Whiskers asked, "Why are you sad?" Spot said, "My heel hurts. I can't play with my friends." Whiskers thought for a moment and had an idea. Whiskers told Spot, "I know how to end your hurt heel." Spot was happy to hear this. Whiskers found a soft bandage and wrapped it around Spot's heel. Soon, Spot's heel felt better. Spot and Whiskers played together. They ran and jumped in the sun. Spot's heel did not hurt anymore. They were both very happy and became best friends. And that is the end of the story.

Once upon a time, there were three friends. One day, their school teacher asked them a question. The friend in the middle asked, "What is a symbol?" The teacher replied, "A symbol is something that looks weird but has a special meaning." The friends started to think about what that could mean. The friend to the left asked, "What kind of symbols can we find?" The teacher replied, "Symbols can be found on signs, flags, or clothes. They can also mean different things to different people." The friend on the right said, "That's weird. I don't like symbols." The teacher replied, "That's ok, but symbols can sometimes help us solve problems or understand things better." The friends thought about this for a while. The friend in the middle said, "I think I understand now. Symbols can help us make sense of things." The teacher smiled and said, "That's right! They can be very helpful." The friends nodded and smiled. They were happy they understood the concept of symbols now!

One day, a little bird was sitting on a twig. The bird was bossy and liked to tell other animals what to do. The bird saw a cat and told it to go away. The cat did not like the bossy bird, but it left anyway. The bossy bird saw a big bell and wanted to ring it. The bird flew to the bell and used its beak to make the bell ring. The sound was very loud and all the animals heard it. The animals came to see what was going on. When the animals saw the bossy bird ringing the bell, they did not like it. They wanted the bird to stop. The animals worked together to make the bird stop ringing the bell. The bossy bird learned that being bossy is not nice, and it is better to be kind and work together with friends.
