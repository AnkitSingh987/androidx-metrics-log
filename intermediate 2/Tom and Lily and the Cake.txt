Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Cake

Tom and Lily are brother and sister. They like to play and joke with each other. One day, they are bored at home. They decide to play a joke on their mom. They go to the kitchen and open the refrigerator. They see a big cake inside. It is mom's birthday cake. They take the cake out and hide it under the table. They put a note on the refrigerator. The note says: "Sorry, we ate your cake. Love, Tom and Lily." They wait for mom to come home. They are very quiet. They do not want mom to hear them. They want to see her face when she sees the note. Mom comes home from work. She is tired and hungry. She goes to the kitchen and sees the note on the refrigerator. She is shocked and sad. She says: "Oh no, my cake! How could they do this?" Tom and Lily hear mom's voice. They jump out from under the table. They hold the cake and say: "Surprise! Happy birthday, mom! We did not eat your cake. It was a joke!" Mom sees the cake and smiles. She is happy and relieved. She hugs Tom and Lily and says: "Thank you, my sweeties. You are very funny. But don't ever joke like that again. You scared me!" Tom and Lily say: "Sorry, mom. We love you. Can we have some cake now?" Mom says: "Of course. Let's have some cake and celebrate. You are the best gift I ever had."

Once upon a time, there was a modest little cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his best friend, a big dog named Sam. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, while Tim and Sam were playing outside, they found a shield. It was lying on the ground under a tree. They didn't know what it was, but they thought it was fun to play with. They took turns holding the shield and pretending to be brave knights. As the sun began to set, Tim and Sam decided to lie down and rest. They used the shield as a pillow and looked at the sky. They felt happy and safe with their new shield. And from that day on, they always played with their shield, having many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a queen. She lived in a big castle. The queen had a fast horse. She loved to ride her horse every day. One day, the queen went for a ride on her horse. They went very fast. They saw a big hill. The queen wanted to go up the hill. The horse tried hard to gain the top of the hill. At last, they reached the top of the hill. The queen was happy. She saw a beautiful view. The queen and her fast horse went back to the castle. They had a fun day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the lake with his mom. They saw an orange fish in the water. Tim and his mom gazed at the fish as it swam around. They were very happy to see the fish. As they were watching the fish, something unexpected happened. A big frog jumped into the lake and splashed water on Tim and his mom. They were very surprised and laughed. The orange fish swam away, and the frog took its place. Tim and his mom gazed at the frog now. They thought the frog was funny. Then, the frog jumped out of the lake and turned into a kind man. Tim and his mom were so surprised! The man said he was a magical friend who liked to make people laugh. They all laughed together and had a fun day at the lake.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy loved to soar high in the sky. He felt a strong urge to fly higher and higher every day. One day, Billy saw a yummy-looking worm on the ground. He wanted to eat it, but it was near a big, scary cat. Billy's friends told him not to go near the cat, but he didn't listen. He wanted the yummy worm so much. Billy flew down to get the worm, but the big, scary cat saw him. The cat jumped and caught Billy. He could not soar anymore. Billy should have listened to his friends.
