Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Potato

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He wanted to help his mom cook dinner. His mom said, "Tim, can you fold the napkins for the table?" Tim said, "Yes, Mom!" and he started to fold them. While Tim was folding, he saw a potato. The potato had a funny face on it. Tim laughed and showed it to his mom. His mom said, "That is a funny potato! Let's cook it with the others." When they put the funny potato in the pot, something unexpected happened. The potato jumped out and said, "Please don't cook me!" Tim and his mom were very surprised. They had never seen a talking potato before. Tim said, "Okay, we won't cook you. You can be our friend instead." The potato was very happy. From that day on, Tim and the potato became best friends, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to act like a pirate. He had a big hat and a toy sword. Tim and his dog, Spot, played pirates every day. One day, Tim and Spot went on a deep adventure. They dug a big hole in the yard. They found a skull! Tim and Spot were very excited. They thought it was a real pirate treasure. Tim and Spot showed the skull to Tim's mom. She told them it was just a toy skull. But Tim and Spot didn't care. They still had fun acting like pirates with their new treasure.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. She had a favorite carpet in her room. It was soft and had many colors. Lily liked to sit on the carpet and play with her toys. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came over to play. They both sat on the carpet and began to play with their toys. Tim said, "This carpet is boring. It doesn't do anything fun." Lily looked sad but didn't say anything. They kept playing with their toys on the carpet. Later, Lily's mommy came into the room. She saw Lily and Tim gazing at the carpet. Lily's mommy asked, "What's wrong, Lily?" Lily said, "Tim thinks my carpet is boring." Her mommy smiled and said, "This carpet is special because it's where you and your friends can play and have fun." Lily and Tim smiled, and they continued to play on the carpet, happy to have a special place to share their fun.

One day, a small boy named Tom went to play with his ball. He loved to play and score goals. He played with his ball in the park every day. His best friend, Sam, would come and play with him too. They had a lot of fun together. One day, as they played, Tom kicked the ball too hard. The ball flew far away and got locked in a big box. Tom and Sam were sad. They did not know how to open the box to get the ball back. They tried and tried, but the box would not open. Then, a kind bird flew down and said, "I can help you open the box." Tom and Sam were surprised but happy. The bird used its beak to unlock the box. Tom and Sam got their ball back and thanked the bird. They played and scored more goals, and now they had a new friend to play with too.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball. One day, Tom heard a sound. It was a little bird. "Hello, bird," said Tom. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Tom," said the bird. "Let's play together." So, Tom and the bird played with the ball. They had lots of fun. Then, the ball disappeared. Tom and the bird looked for the ball, but they could not find it. Tom and the bird were sad. They wanted to play more, but the ball was gone. They looked and looked, but the ball did not come back. Tom and the bird went home without the ball. They were not jolly anymore.
