Category: Intermediate
Title: The Green Hedge and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a green hedge. It was very big and tall. Many small animals lived in the hedge. They liked to play and eat the green leaves. One day, a big truck came to remove the hedge. The animals were scared. They did not want to lose their home. But then, something unexpected happened. The truck driver saw the animals and stopped. He did not want to hurt them. He decided to take the hedge to a new place. The animals were happy. They could keep their home and make new friends in the new place. The green hedge was saved, and the animals lived happily ever after.

Lily and Max are best friends. They like to play together every day. They have many toys, but their favorite is the phone. They pretend to call different people and have fun conversations. One day, Lily and Max decide to call their parents. They want to tell them about their incredible job. They are making a big castle with blocks and pillows. They are very proud of their work. Lily picks up the phone and dials her mom's number. She waits for a beep and then says, "Hi, Mom! It's Lily. I have something to tell you. I have an incredible job!" "What is your job, Lily?" her mom asks. "I am a princess and Max is a king. We are building a castle. It is very big and beautiful. You should see it!" Lily says. "That sounds wonderful, Lily. I'm very happy for you and Max. Can I talk to him?" her mom says. "Sure, Mom. Here he is." Lily hands the phone to Max. "Hi, Mom!" Max says. "Hi, Max! Lily told me about your incredible job. You are a king and she is a princess. You are building a castle. It is very big and beautiful. I'm very proud of you and Lily. Can I see it?" his mom says. "Yes, Mom. You can see it. We will show you when you come home. We love you, Mom!" Max says. "I love you too, Max. And I love you, Lily. Have fun with your job. I'll see you soon. Bye!" his mom says. "Bye, Mom!" Lily and Max say together. They hang up the phone and smile. They hug each other and go back to their job. They are very happy. They have an incredible job and an incredible friendship.

One day, a little girl named Sally went for a walk with her mom. It was very cold outside, so they wore big coats. Sally's mom said, "Brrr, it's so cold, I think I might freeze!" Sally laughed and hugged her mom to keep her warm. On their walk, they saw a candy store. Sally's eyes grew big and she asked, "Mom, can we get a lollipop?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, let's go inside." They went in the store and Sally picked a big, red lollipop. She was very happy. As they walked home, Sally tried to open her lollipop. But the wrapper was stuck and she couldn't open it. She felt frustrated and said, "Mom, I can't open my lollipop!" Her mom helped her and soon Sally was enjoying her sweet treat. They walked home together, happy and warm.

One day, a red ball went to play near a stream. The red ball liked to bounce high and low. It was a sunny day, and the red ball was very happy. As the red ball was bouncing, it saw a little frog. The frog said, "Hello, red ball! Do you want to play with me?" The red ball said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" So, the red ball and the frog played together near the stream. They had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The red ball bounced too high and fell into the stream! The frog was scared, but he jumped into the water to save the red ball. The frog pushed the red ball out of the stream, and they were both safe. The red ball and the frog became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a large drum. It lived in a small house with a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with the drum. He would hit it and make loud sounds. The drum was very happy when Tim played with it. One day, a new kid named Ben came to play with Tim. Ben did not like the drum. He said, "I hate that drum. It is too loud." Tim felt sad because he loved his drum. He wanted Ben to like it too. So, Tim and the drum thought of a plan. They decided to play a quiet game with the drum. They rolled the drum around and laughed. Ben saw how much fun they were having and joined in. Soon, Ben started to like the drum too. They all played together and became good friends.
