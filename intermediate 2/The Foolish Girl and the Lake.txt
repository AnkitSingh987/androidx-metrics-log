Category: Intermediate
Title: The Foolish Girl and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a foolish girl who decided to go for a swim in the lake all by herself. She thought she was very brave and smart, but she didn't think about the danger. The foolish girl waded into the water and began to swim. She felt so happy and free that she didn't notice the rain clouds above her. Suddenly, the sky darkened, and a big storm began. The girl was caught in the storm and the wind was too strong. No matter how hard she tried, she couldn't get back to shore. The girl settled deeper and deeper into the lake and eventually, she disappeared, never to be seen again. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a smelly notebook. She loved to draw in it. One day, she drew a big, funny face. She laughed and laughed. The next day, Sue felt a sneeze coming. She knew it would be a big one. She thought about the funny face in her smelly notebook. Maybe the sneeze would go away if she looked at it. Sue opened her notebook and saw the funny face. She laughed again. But the sneeze was still there. She sneezed so big, the smelly notebook flew out of her hands. The pages went everywhere. Sue was sad. She picked up the pages and put them back in the smelly notebook. Sue learned that laughing at a funny face could not stop a sneeze. But it did make her happy. And that was good enough for her.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a unique gem. This gem was very pretty and everyone liked it. The gem was kept in a big store where people could look at it. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. Lily saw the gem and said, "Mom, look at that! It's so pretty!" Her mom looked at the gem and replied, "Yes, it is very pretty, but we can't buy it. It's not for sale." Lily was sad, but she had an idea. She asked the store man, "Can we order a gem like that?" The store man said, "I'm sorry, but this gem is unique. There is no other gem like it." Lily and her mom went home, but they never forgot about the special gem.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful park. In the park, there was a big tree. Under the tree, a girl named Lily liked to play. One day, Lily found a trumpet. She did not know how to make it work. Lily asked her friend Tom, "Do you know how to make this trumpet work?" Tom shook his head. "No, but let's ask Mr. Owl. He knows many things." So, they went to Mr. Owl's tree. Mr. Owl said, "I can teach you how to make the trumpet work. But first, you must be kind and help others." Lily and Tom helped their friends all day. They felt happy. The next day, they went back to Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl smiled and said, "You did a good job. Now, I will show you how to make the trumpet work." He showed them, and they played a happy song. The park was filled with music and joy.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Tom. Tom was a fearful cat. He was scared of many things. One day, Tom went for a walk. While walking, he saw a big dog on the other side of the road. Tom was scared but wanted to be brave. Tom thought of a plan. He would yell at the dog, so the dog would go away. Tom took a deep breath and got ready to yell. He thought of how he could be brave and make the dog go away. Finally, Tom yelled at the dog. The dog looked at Tom and then ran away. Tom was so happy that his plan worked. He was not so fearful anymore. Tom walked back home with a big smile on his face, feeling proud and brave.

Once upon a time, there was a round ball named Rollie. Rollie loved to roll all day long. It was easy and fun for him. He would roll up and down the hills, in the grass, and on the paths. One day, Rollie met a little girl named Lila. Lila had a big smile on her face, and her red lips looked happy. She saw Rollie rolling by and wanted to play too. So, Lila picked up Rollie and started to roll him back and forth in her hands. Together, they had so much fun. They rolled and played in the sun all day. After some time, they got tired and sat under a big tree. Lila hugged Rollie and said, "You are my new friend, and I love to roll with you." Rollie felt very happy and loved. From that day on, they played together every day, and their friendship grew stronger.
