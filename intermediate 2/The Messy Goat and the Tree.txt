Category: Intermediate
Title: The Messy Goat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a messy goat. The goat lived on a farm with many other animals. The goat liked to play in the mud and make a mess. One day, the goat saw a big tree with a yummy snack on top. The goat wanted to eat the snack, but he was scared to climb the tree. He did not dare to go up there. A kind bird saw the goat and wanted to help. The bird flew up and got the snack for the goat. They both shared the snack and became good friends. The goat was happy he had a friend who dared to help.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a brilliant toy named Mr. Bear. They liked to play together every day. One day, Tim and Mr. Bear were playing near a big wall. They heard a loud noise. Tim said, "What's that, Mr. Bear?" Mr. Bear didn't know. They were both scared. Then, a tiny mouse came out from behind the wall. The mouse said, "Hi, I'm Molly! I didn't mean to scare you." Tim and Mr. Bear were happy to meet Molly. They all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a land full of trees, there was a banana. This banana was very intelligent. It could think and talk like people. The banana had many friends in the land of trees. One day, the intelligent banana wanted to stand like its friends. So, it asked a tall tree for help. The tree said, "I will help you stand, little banana." The tree held the banana up high, and the banana was so happy to stand like its friends. The other fruits saw the intelligent banana standing and they wanted to stand too. So, they all asked the tall tree for help. The tree helped them all to stand, and they had a big party. All the fruits were happy and had lots of fun. And the intelligent banana was the happiest of all because it could stand with its friends.

Once upon a time, there was a humble town. In the town, there was a big factory. The factory made toys for kids. Many people worked in the factory. They liked their job. One day, the boss of the factory decided to make a new toy. The toy was a big robot. It was very cool. The boss said, "This robot will be the best toy ever!" The workers were very excited. But, something unexpected happened. When they turned on the robot, it started to dance! The robot was not a toy, it was a dancing robot! The boss and the workers were surprised, but they laughed and danced with the robot. The humble town became a happy dancing town.

Lily and Ben love to sail. They have a small boat that they can take to the lake. They put on their life jackets and hats and get in the boat. Lily holds the rope and Ben steers the boat. They feel the wind and the sun on their faces. They see a lot of things on the lake. They see ducks and fish and frogs. They see big boats and small boats. They see a bridge and an island. They wave to the people and the animals. They have fun sailing. One day, they see something new on the lake. They see a tiny boat that is only an inch long. It has a white sail and a red flag. It looks like a toy, but it moves by itself. Lily and Ben are curious. They sail closer to the tiny boat. They see a tiny person on the tiny boat. He is wearing a blue shirt and a yellow hat. He smiles and waves to Lily and Ben. He says hello in a high voice. Lily and Ben say hello back. They ask him who he is and where he comes from. He tells them his name is Tom and he comes from a land of tiny people. He says he likes to sail and explore new places. He says he is healthy and happy. He says he wants to be friends with Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben say they want to be friends with him too. They invite him to sail with them. He says yes. They help him get on their boat. They sail together and have more fun. They are happy.
