Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy shield that he loved very much. He took it with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim's mom told him, "Don't forget your shield, Tim! It will remind you to be good and kind to others." At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because a bad boy took her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue. He said, "Sue, let's go find your toy and get it back from the bad boy." Tim and Sue found the bad boy with Sue's toy. Tim showed the bad boy his shield and said, "This shield reminds me to be good and kind. You should be good too and give Sue her toy back." The bad boy felt sorry and gave Sue her toy back. Sue was happy and thanked Tim for his help. The moral of the story is to always be reminded to be good and kind to others. Just like Tim's shield, we can carry something with us to remind us of this important lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant. The ant was troubled. It was lost and wanted to find its way home. One day, the ant saw a big tree. It thought, "Maybe if I run to the tree, I will find my home." So, the ant started to run. It ran and ran until it reached the tree. When the ant got to the tree, it saw other ants. The troubled ant was very happy to find its family. They all hugged and danced. From that day on, the little ant was never lost again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that could snap. He loved to snap it all day. One day, Tim's mom and dad had a big fight. They were not happy in their marriage. Tim thought it was stupid. Tim wanted to help his mom and dad. He thought that if he showed them his toy, they would stop fighting. So, Tim went to his mom and dad and showed them his toy that could snap. They looked at it and smiled. Tim's mom and dad stopped fighting. They saw how much fun Tim had with his toy. They played with Tim and the toy that could snap. The marriage was happy again. Tim knew that sometimes, even a little toy could help fix a big problem.

Once upon a time there was a little soldier. He was brave, but very small. He had a big mission ahead of him and was feeling very scared. One day, he went to his best friend for help. "I'm so scared," he said. "Please help me." His friend smiled and said, "Why don't you pray?" The little soldier had never prayed before, but he nodded. He bowed his head and said a little prayer. He felt much better afterwards. He thanked his friend and said, "I'm ready to do my mission now." His friend smiled and said, "Go do it. I'll be praying for you." The little soldier nodded and set off on his mission. He knew he could do it, even though it seemed very hard. And, sure enough, he achieved success! He completed his mission and came back to thank his friend again. "Thank you for helping me and for praying for me. I couldn't have done it without you!" The little soldier was very happy. His friend was proud of him, and they celebrated his success with a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a nurse named Brian who had the most perfect folding skills. Every time he was asked to fold something, he was able to do it so perfectly. One day, a 3-year old boy named David came to the hospital and Brian was assigned to take care of him. David noticed Brian's folding skills and was so amazed. "Can you teach me how to fold too?" asked David. Brian laughed and said, "Sure, I can teach you! But first, I want you to practice counting to 3. When you can count to 3 perfectly, we will start folding." David practiced counting and finally mastered counting to 3 perfectly. With excitement, he ran back to Brian's office and said, "I did it! I can count to 3 perfectly! Please, can you teach me folding now?" Brian smiled and said, "Sure! Now that you have perfect counting skills, I'm sure you will have perfect folding skills too!" And from then on, David and Brian practiced folding together. David learned so quickly and before long, he was as perfect a folder as Brian!
