Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Lion

One day, a little girl named Mia found an envelope on the floor. She felt very excited to see what was inside. Mia opened the envelope and found a picture of a big, dangerous lion. She felt scared, but also curious. Mia showed the picture to her friend, Tim. Tim looked at the dangerous lion and felt brave. He wanted to find the lion and play with it. Mia and Tim went to the park to look for the lion, but they couldn't find it anywhere. Suddenly, they heard a loud roar. Mia and Tim felt scared, but they looked around and saw that it was just a little kitten. The kitten had been hiding behind the picture of the dangerous lion all along. Mia and Tim laughed and played with the kitten, happy that the lion was not real.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They saw a big pot with a lid. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's open it!" said Tom. "No, we can't. Mom said not to touch it. It is her pot," said Lily. "But I want to know. Maybe there is something fun or yummy in it," said Tom. He lifted the lid a little bit. He saw something shiny and round. He reached his hand to grab it. "Stop!" said Lily. "That is not fun or yummy. That is a ball of fire. It will hurt you. Mom uses it to cook. Put the lid back!" Tom did not listen. He was too curious. He pulled the ball of fire out of the pot. It was very hot. It burned his hand. He dropped it on the grass. It made a loud noise and a big smoke. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" cried Tom. He ran to the house. He wanted Mom to help him. Lily followed him. She was scared and sad. She saw the pot and the ball of fire. They were still smoking. She hoped they did not start a fire. She was clever. She remembered what Mom had taught her. She got a bucket of water. She poured it on the pot and the ball of fire. They stopped smoking. She saved the garden. Mom came out. She saw what had happened. She was angry and worried. She took Tom to the bathroom. She put his hand under cold water. She put a bandage on it. She said he was very naughty. He should not have touched the pot. He should have listened to Lily. He should have trusted her. She hugged Lily. She said she was very brave and smart. She did the right thing. She trusted Mom. She helped Tom. She thanked her. Lily was happy. She loved Mom. She loved Tom. She hoped he would be okay. She hoped he would trust her next time.

One day, a big car went speed down the street. The car was red and shiny. The man in the car was very happy. He liked to go fast. He did not see the police car behind him. The police car had blue and red lights. The police car went speed too. The man got a ticket for going too fast. He was sad. He did not want a ticket. He went home and told his mom. His mom said, "Do not be sad. I have a surprise for you." She gave him a big, delicious cake. The cake was red like his car. He was very happy again. But then, something unexpected happened. The cake started to shake and jump. The man and his mom were scared. The cake opened up, and out came the police officer who gave the man the ticket. He said, "Surprise! I am your long lost brother!" The man, his mom, and the police officer all laughed and hugged. They ate the delicious cake together and had a great day.

Once upon a time there was a family who loved each other very much. One night, they were all gathered around the kitchen table. Mum and Dad had some exciting news. “Everyone,” shouted Dad, “we have something to tell you!” The family was curious. “The new baby is ready to come home!” said Mum, “and it’s a boy!” The family was so happy they all started to applaud. Mum and Dad brought their new baby into the room. Everyone was so grateful and happy to meet him. “We love you!” said Mum, giving him a big hug. “Yeah, we love you too!” shouted the other family members. The baby smiled and laughed. It was a wonderful moment for everyone. The end.
