Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Fluffy

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had long hair. One day, it was very cold outside, and Spot started to shiver. Spot saw a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was alert and saw Spot shivering. Fluffy said, "Spot, why are you shivering?" Spot said, "I am cold because of my wet hair." Fluffy wanted to help Spot. They went inside a warm house. Spot stopped shivering and was happy. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a red vase. The vase was very pretty. It made other things envious. One day, the vase saw a toy. The toy was sad. The vase said to the toy, "Why are you sad?" The toy said, "I am sad because I want to be pretty like you. I am envious." The vase wanted to help the toy. So, the vase told the toy, "I will leave you some of my pretty. Then, you will not be envious." But the toy did not know how to take the pretty from the vase. The toy tried and tried, but it could not do it. The vase got very sad. It wanted to help the toy, but it could not. In the end, the vase and the toy were both sad. The vase could not help the toy. The toy was still envious. And the vase could not leave its pretty for the toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat spicy food. Her mom made her yummy, spicy soup. Lily was very happy when she ate it. One day, Lily lost her tooth while eating the spicy soup. She was so excited! She showed her tooth to her mom and dad. They were very proud of her. That night, Lily put her tooth under her pillow. The next morning, she found a gift from the tooth fairy. She was so happy that she started to clap. Her mom and dad joined her and they all applauded together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big grape on the ground. He was very happy and picked it up. Tim wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. When Tim found Sue, she was upset. She had lost her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue feel better, so he gave her the big grape. Sue smiled and they began to play with the grape. As they played, the grape started to bounce and grow bigger! Tim and Sue were so surprised. The grape became a big ball. They laughed and played with the big grape ball all day.

One day, a big, loud bull lived on a farm. The bull liked to run and play in the sun. The farm had a big, strong gate to keep the bull safe. The farmer had a key to unlock the gate. One morning, the bull saw a little girl near the gate. The girl said, "Hello, big bull! I want to play with you." The bull was happy and wanted to play too. But the gate was locked, so the girl tried to unlock it. The girl found the farmer's key and unlocked the gate. The bull was so excited! But when the gate opened, the bull saw many other animals outside. It was a big surprise! The bull made new friends and played with them all day. In the end, the bull thanked the girl for unlocking the gate and giving him a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a round ball who was very sad. He wanted to marry the yummy cake who lived in the same town. One day, the round ball said to the yummy cake, "Will you marry me?" The yummy cake thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I will marry you, but first you must find a pretty dress for me to wear." The round ball was very happy and went to look for a pretty dress for the yummy cake. While the round ball was away, a big wind came and blew the yummy cake far, far away. The round ball came back with the pretty dress, but the yummy cake was gone! He was very sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a nice bird flew by and said, "I saw the big wind take the yummy cake far away. I can help you find her." The round ball was happy again and went with the bird to find the yummy cake. The bird and the round ball found the yummy cake in a big tree. They helped her down, and the yummy cake put on the pretty dress. The round ball and the yummy cake got married, and they all lived happily ever after.
