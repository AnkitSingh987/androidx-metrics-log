Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rock and the Chess

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. On top of the rock, there was a chess set. The chess set was very old and miserable. The pieces were sad because no one played with them. One sunny day, a little boy found the rock and the chess set. He wanted to play, but he did not know how. He moved the pieces around, trying to make them happy. Suddenly, the pieces began to move by themselves! The little boy was surprised. The chess pieces were so happy that they could dance and play with the little boy. They were not miserable anymore. The little boy and the chess pieces became best friends. They played together every day on the big rock, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy and a girl wanted to have a picnic. They asked their mom to help them get ready. They took a big, blue blanket and put it on the grass outside. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. The boy and girl wanted different foods for their picnic. The boy wanted apples and the girl wanted grapes. They also had sandwiches and juice. They put everything in a basket and carried it to the blanket. They sat down on the blanket and ate their picnic. The boy and girl shared their different foods and laughed. They had a great time at their picnic. After they finished eating, they played with their toys on the grass. It was a fun day for the boy and girl.

Once upon a time, there was a little red car named Tommy. Tommy loved to drive fast on the road. He was a very independent car and did not need help from anyone. One day, while driving, Tommy met a grape named Gigi. "Hi, I am Gigi the grape!" said Gigi. "I see you like to drive fast, Tommy!" "Yes, I do!" said Tommy. "I am an independent car and like to go fast!" Gigi thought for a moment and said, "Can I go with you on a drive? I want to see the world too!" Tommy agreed and Gigi hopped on. Together, they drove fast and saw many fun things. They were very happy and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was an independent dog who liked to play all day. He had a favorite hat that he liked to wear when he went outside. The hat was red and had a big, shiny star on it. One sunny day, Max went to the park to play with his friends. He wore his hat and was very happy. While playing, he saw a big, green ball. Max loved balls and wanted to play with it. So, he ran to the ball and bit it. The ball popped and Max was surprised. He looked at his hat and it was still on his head. Max learned that he should not bite things that are not his toys. He went back to playing with his friends, still wearing his special hat.

Lily was a little girl who liked to dance. She wanted to be a dancer when she grew up. She practiced every day in her room, with her music and her mirror. She had a big show at school, and she was very excited. But the day before the show, she fell down the stairs and hurt her leg. She cried and cried, and her mom took her to the doctor. The doctor said she had to wear a bandage and use a cane for a while. He said she could not dance in the show. Lily was very sad and angry. She did not want to miss the show. She did not want to use a cane. She did not want to stay at home. She wanted to dance. The next day, her mom came to her room with a surprise. She had a new dress for Lily, with flowers and ribbons. She said it was a gift from her dad, who was away on a trip. She said he wanted Lily to feel better and to be happy. Lily smiled and hugged her mom. She tried on the dress and looked at herself in the mirror. She felt pretty and special. She said, "Thank you, mom. Thank you, dad. I love you." Her mom said, "You're welcome, sweetie. You're very attractive. And you're very brave. I'm proud of you." Lily said, "Can I still go to the show, mom? I want to see my friends. I want to cheer for them." Her mom said, "Of course, you can, honey. You can go with me. And you can use your cane as a prop. You can pretend you're a princess with a magic wand. You can wave it and make wishes." Lily liked that idea. She said, "That sounds fun, mom. Maybe I can wish for my leg to heal. And for dad to come back soon. And for everyone to have a good time." Her mom said, "That's a wonderful wish, Lily. Maybe it will come true. You never know. You can succeed anything you want, if you believe in yourself." Lily nodded and smiled. She took her cane and her dress and went with her mom to the show. She had a good time. She saw her friends. She cheered for them. She waved her cane and made wishes. She felt happy. She was a dancer.
