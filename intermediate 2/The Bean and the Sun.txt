Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bean and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little bean. This bean was very sad and miserable. It did not like being a small bean. It wanted to be big and strong like the other beans in the garden. So, the bean made a request to the sun and the rain. The sun and the rain listened to the bean's request. They wanted to help the sad bean. They worked together to give the bean what it needed. The sun shined bright and warm, and the rain fell gently on the ground. The bean started to grow bigger and stronger. But one day, something unexpected happened. The bean grew so big that it turned into a giant beanstalk! The bean was not miserable anymore. It was happy and proud of what it had become. The other beans in the garden looked up to the giant beanstalk in surprise. The little bean's wish had come true, but in a way, it never thought was possible.

Once upon a time, there was a clever boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the dirt and find roots. One day, Tim found a big root and decided to keep it as his friend. He named the root Mr. Root. One day, Tim's friend Sally came to play. Sally saw Mr. Root and said, "I want to marry Mr. Root!" Tim did not like this idea. He wanted Mr. Root all to himself. Tim and Sally did not know what to do. Tim thought hard and had an idea. He said, "Let's find another root for you, Sally!" They looked and looked, and finally, they found a pretty root for Sally. Sally was happy, and they all played together. From that day on, Tim, Sally, Mr. Root, and the new root were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely mixer. The mixer was sad because it had no friends to play with. It sat in the kitchen all day, wishing for someone to talk to. One day, a little girl named Sally came into the kitchen. She saw the lonely mixer and wanted to help it. Sally had an idea. She took the mixer and some bowls and spoons, and they all went outside to play. Sally and the mixer found some rocks. They used the mixer to rock the rocks back and forth, making fun sounds. The mixer was very happy to play with Sally. They played with the rocks all day, and the mixer was not lonely anymore.

One day, a pretty bird sat on a tree. The bird loved to sing songs. It sang a happy song every day. All the kids loved to hear the bird's song. One day, a big wind came and shut the door of the bird's nest. The bird could not get back in. The bird was very sad. It stopped singing its happy song. Then, a small ant came to help the bird. The ant was very strong. It pushed the door open. The bird was so happy! The bird sang a new song. The new song was about the strong ant. The kids loved the new song too.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a regular boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim's mom said, "I want to introduce you to our new neighbor, Sam." Tim was excited to make a new friend. Tim went to Sam's house and knocked on the door. Sam came out, and they started to play. They played with a ball and tried to pass it to each other. They had lots of fun and laughed a lot. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came running towards them. The dog picked up the ball in its mouth and ran away. Tim and Sam looked at each other and laughed. "I didn't know we would make two new friends today!" said Tim.

One day, a long fish and a small crab found an oyster on the sea floor. The fish and the crab were friends. They wanted to eat the oyster, but they did not know how to open it. The fish said, "I can slap it with my tail!" The crab said, "I can pinch it with my claw!" The fish slapped the oyster, but it did not open. The crab pinched it, but it did not open. They tried again and again, but the oyster stayed closed. They were sad and tired. Then, a wise old sea turtle came by. The turtle said, "You need to work together." The fish and the crab listened to the turtle. The fish slapped the oyster, and the crab pinched it at the same time. The oyster opened, and they shared the food inside. They were happy and full. They learned that working together is better than trying alone.
