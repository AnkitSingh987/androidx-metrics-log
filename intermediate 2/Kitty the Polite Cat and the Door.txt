Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Polite Cat and the Door

One day, a polite cat named Kitty went to the store. She wanted to buy some meat for her lunch. Kitty said, "Hello, I want some meat, please." The store man gave her the meat and she went home. At home, Kitty put the meat on the table. She went to get a plate and a fork. But when she came back, the meat was gone! "Oh no! My meat disappeared!" said Kitty. She looked everywhere but could not find it. Just then, her friend Doggy came to visit. "Hello, Kitty! I found this meat outside your door. Is it yours?" Doggy asked. Kitty was so happy and said, "Yes, it is! Thank you, Doggy!" They both laughed and enjoyed the meat together. The wind had blown the meat outside, but now it was back, and they had a great lunch.

One day, Paul was walking down the street with his mom. He saw a big, grey cat and wanted to pet it. His mom said, "Paul, don't bother the cat. Leave it alone." But Paul ignored his mom and ran to pet the cat. The cat was very quick. It ran away and Paul felt upset. He looked at his mom and said, "The cat ignored me and I wanted to pet it!" His mom said, "That's okay, Paul. Let's keep walking and don't ignore me because we have to get to the store." Paul and his mom kept walking and when they passed by some trees, Paul saw a squirrel. He asked his mom if he could pet it, and again she said, "No, Paul. Ignore the squirrel and keep walking." So Paul ignored the squirrel and kept walking. He felt a bit better knowing he had listened to his mom.

Tim was a dependable little boy. He loved explorin the outdoors with his Otter friends. One day, he went to the lake, and saw his Otter friends all around him. He said “hello” but they didn’t respond. Curious, he asked, “Why don’t you answer me?” One of them replied, “We don’t want to talk to you anymore. You said no to our requests too much.” Tim was sad, because he hadn’t meant to be mean. He said, “I’m sorry I refused. I won’t do it again. Please forgive me.” The Otters looked at each other, then agreed. “Okay,” they said, “we forgive you. Just remember to be dependable and don’t refuse anymore.” Tim was so happy that his Otter friends had forgiven him. After that day, he was always dependable, and he never refused them again.

Lila and Ben were friends who liked to play with toys. One day, they found a big box of toys under a tree. They were very happy and ran to the box. "Look, Lila, there are so many toys here!" Ben said. "We can play with them all day!" "Yes, Ben, they are so pretty and fun!" Lila said. "But we should share them with other kids too. Maybe someone left them here for us to share." But Ben did not want to share. He wanted to keep all the toys for himself. He started to grab the toys and put them in his bag. He grabbed a doll, a car, a ball, a book, and more. "Ben, stop!" Lila said. "You are grabbing too many toys! Leave some for me and for other kids!" "No, Lila, they are mine!" Ben said. "I found them first! You can't have any!" Lila was sad and angry. She tried to grab some toys back, but Ben pushed her away. He was very mean and greedy. Then, they heard a loud voice. It was the owner of the toys. He was a big boy who had left the box under the tree for a while. He saw Ben and Lila fighting over his toys. "Hey, you two, what are you doing with my toys?" he shouted. "Give them back to me right now!" He ran to the box and grabbed his toys from Ben's bag. He was very mad and strong. He also grabbed Ben and Lila by their arms and dragged them to their parents. He told their parents what they had done. Their parents were very disappointed and angry. They scolded Ben and Lila and made them apologize to the big boy. They also took away their own toys for a week. Ben and Lila felt very sorry and ashamed. They learned a hard lesson. They learned that they should not grab things that are not theirs. They learned that they should share and be humble. They learned that being greedy and mean can lead to trouble and pain.
