Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sauce

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He saw a jar of sauce on the table. He wanted to open it, but it was too tight. Tim tried and tried, but the jar would not open. He felt sad because he wanted to eat the sauce with his lunch. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "I can't open the sauce, and my lunch is dull without it." His mom smiled and said, "Let me help you." She took the jar and opened it for Tim. He was so happy! Now, Tim could eat his lunch with the yummy sauce. His lunch was not dull anymore. He shared the sauce with his mom, and they both enjoyed their meal. Tim learned that sometimes, it's okay to ask for help when things are hard to do.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big white dog. The dog was lost and sad. Lily wanted to help the dog find its home. Lily and the dog walked and walked. Suddenly, a cop appeared. The cop asked, "Are you lost, little girl?" Lily said, "No, but this dog is lost. Can you help us find its home?" The cop smiled and said, "Of course!" They all walked together until they found the dog's house. The dog was happy to be home. Lily said goodbye to the dog and the cop. She went back to her own home, feeling good about helping her new friends.

Sara was a lonely woman. She lived in a small house with no friends or family. She worked at a big store, but no one talked to her there. She felt sad and bored every day. One day, Sara saw a flyer on a wall. It said, "Guess the number of jelly beans in the jar and win a prize!" Sara liked jelly beans. She thought, maybe this will make me happy. She went to the store where the flyer was. She saw a big jar full of jelly beans. She paid a coin and wrote a number on a paper. She hoped she guessed right. The next day, Sara got a phone call. It was a man from the store. He said, "You guessed the number of jelly beans in the jar! You won the prize!" Sara was surprised and happy. She asked, "What is the prize?" The man said, "The prize is a ticket to a fun park. You can go on rides and play games and eat cotton candy. And you can bring a friend with you!" Sara was very excited. She thanked the man and hung up the phone. She thought, who can I bring with me? She looked around her house. She had no friends or family. She felt lonely again. She wished she had someone to share her prize with. Then she had an idea. She remembered a woman who worked at the store with her. She was nice and smiled at Sara sometimes. She thought, maybe she would like to go with me. She picked up the phone and called the woman. She said, "Hi, this is Sara. I work with you at the store. I won a ticket to a fun park. Do you want to go with me?" The woman said, "Yes, I would love to go with you! That sounds like fun!" Sara was happy. She said, "Great, let's meet tomorrow at the store and go together!" The woman said, "OK, see you tomorrow!" Sara hung up the phone and smiled. She was not lonely anymore. She had a friend and a prize. She guessed right.

One day, Jake got injured and he cried. His mommy came to him and gave him a hug. She said, "Don't worry, Jake. I can help you heal". Jake's mommy took out a jar of makeup. She said, "This is the magic stuff. It can help with your injury". Jake looked at the makeup. He liked the pretty colors. Jake's mommy put some of the makeup on the injury and then hugged him again. She said, "It's all better now". Jake smiled and was happy. Every day, Jake's mommy made sure they were doing their regular healing routine. She said, "It's very important to do this every day. That way, we can make sure that you stay healthy and happy". Jake smiled and hugged his mommy. She tucked him into bed and said, "Now rest so you can be all healed up tomorrow". And Jake was very happy.
