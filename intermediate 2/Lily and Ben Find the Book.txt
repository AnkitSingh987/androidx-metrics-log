Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Book

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks. They make big buildings with blocks. They put blocks on top of blocks. They use strings to tie blocks together. They want to make the biggest building ever. "Look at our building!" Lily says. "It is so big and strong!" "Yes, it is!" Ben says. "We are very good at making buildings!" They are proud of their building. They smile and clap. They show their building to Mom and Dad. Mom and Dad come to see their building. They are happy and proud too. "Wow, what a wonderful building!" Mom says. "You worked very hard!" "How did you tie the blocks together?" Dad asks. "That is very clever!" Lily and Ben tell them how they used strings. They say they learned it from a book. They say they want to be builders when they grow up. Mom and Dad hug them and praise them. They take a picture of their building. They say they will put it on the fridge. Lily and Ben are happy. They love their building. They love their Mom and Dad. They love each other. They are the best friends ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little red wagon. It lived in a big yard with a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with the wagon every day. He would fill it with toys and pull it around the yard. The wagon felt so happy when Tim played with it. One day, Tim saw a big dog in the yard. The dog was big and it looked a little stupid. Tim thought it would be fun to play with the dog. He tried to hug the dog, but the dog just barked and ran away. Tim felt sad and went back to his wagon. Tim decided to play with the wagon again, but he was still thinking about the dog. He pulled the wagon very fast and wasn't looking where he was going. Suddenly, the wagon hit a big rock and broke into pieces. Tim cried and cried because his wagon was broken. The dog came back and barked at Tim, making him even more sad. Tim's day was very bad and he couldn't play with his wagon anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little independent fish named Bubbles. Bubbles loved to look at the world around her. She swam all day, looking at the rocks, plants, and other fish. One day, Bubbles saw a big bubble floating up to the top of the water. She was curious and wanted to see where it went. Bubbles swam up, up, up, following the bubble. She met a wise old turtle who said, "Hello, little fish! What are you doing?" Bubbles replied, "I am following this big bubble. I want to see where it goes." The wise old turtle smiled and said, "That is a good thing to do. But remember, it is important to also look out for yourself and stay safe." Bubbles swam back down, thinking about what the wise old turtle said. She realized that it is good to be curious and independent, but also important to be careful and look out for herself. From that day on, Bubbles continued to explore her world, but always made sure she stayed safe. And she lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big jar. He wanted to put something special in it. He looked around and saw icy snow on the ground. Tim thought, "Icy snow is special!" So, he put the icy snow in the jar. Tim showed the jar to his friend, Sue. Sue said, "Wow! Icy snow in a jar! Can I touch it?" Tim said, "Yes, but be careful!" Sue touched the icy snow and smiled. They both liked the icy snow in the jar. Soon, the icy snow started to melt. Tim and Sue were sad. Tim's mom said, "Don't be sad. You can always find more icy snow outside. But remember, it's important to share and enjoy things together." Tim and Sue learned that sharing and enjoying things together makes them even more special.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a shiny crown in her toy box. She put it on her head and felt like a queen. She went to show her brother Tom, but Tom wanted the crown too. "Give me the crown!" Tom said. Lily did not want to share, so she said, "No, it's mine!" Tom was mad. Tom tried to take the crown from Lily's head, but Lily held on tight. "Let go!" Tom yelled. But Lily did not reply. She was too busy holding onto the crown. Tom pulled harder, but Lily would not let go. They both felt cold because they were fighting. Mom came into the room and saw them fighting. "What is going on?" she asked. Tom and Lily stopped pulling and looked at Mom. "We both want the crown," Tom said. Mom thought for a moment and then said, "Why don't you take turns wearing the crown?" Tom and Lily agreed. They stopped fighting and took turns being the queen. They were happy and not cold anymore.
