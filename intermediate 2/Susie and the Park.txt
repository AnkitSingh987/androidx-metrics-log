Category: Intermediate
Title: Susie and the Park

Susie and her mommy were shopping at the store. Susie was looking for something special to take home. She saw something on the shelf and pointed at it. It was an ice cream bar! "Can we get that one mommy?", Susie asked. Her mommy said yes, and Susie was so happy. On their way home they stopped by the park. Susie decided to eat her ice cream there. After she finished, she thanked her mommy. "Thank you so much, mommy!" she said, giving her a big hug. Susie saw something in the park that she wanted to do. She ran over to a big tree and saw something special. It was a tiny bird's nest with two baby birds inside. Susie was so amazed and asked her mommy if they could stay and watch them. Her mommy said yes, and they sat there quietly for a while. Suddenly, Susie's mommy saw something in the ground. It was two pieces of ice. She was so kind and gave one to Susie as a treat. Susie was so happy and thanked her mommy again. "Thank you mommy!" she said, and smiled. They stayed at the park watching the baby birds and playing with the ice until it was time to go home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the old port with his mom. He wanted to play with his toy boat. When they got there, they saw a girl named Sue playing with her toy boat too. Tim said, "Hi, can we play together?" Sue said, "Yes, let's play!" So, they played with their boats in the water. But then, a big wave came and took Sue's boat away. Sue was sad and cried, "My boat is gone!" Tim wanted to help Sue, so he said, "Don't worry, we can find it!" They looked and looked for Sue's boat. Finally, they found it near some rocks. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They played with their boats until it was time to go home. They became good friends and played at the old port every day.

Once upon a time there was a young girl. She wanted to explore the valley, so she asked her mom if she could go. Her mom said yes, but she had to be careful. The girl got ready to go and she began her journey across the valley. As she ventured farther and farther along, she began to feel more successful. Everything was going her way! But then she heard an odd noise. It sounded like someone calling her name. She looked around and saw a figure in the shadows. It was a scary creature. It said it wanted a request from the girl. The girl was too scared to answer the creature! She stayed still, hoping it would go away. But the creature came closer. It said it would let her go if she gave it what it wanted. The girl sadly gave in and gave the creature what it requested. She ran away with tears streaming down her face. Though she had been successful in her journey, it had ended with a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a tiny bird. The bird was very hungry. It wanted to find food. It saw a pebble on the ground. The bird thought the pebble was a seed. It wanted to test if the pebble was good to eat. The bird picked up the pebble in its beak. It tried to bite the pebble. But the pebble was very hard. The bird could not eat it. The bird was sad. It was still hungry. The bird knew it needed to find real food. The bird flew around the forest, looking for food. It saw a big tree with many seeds. The bird was happy. It ate the seeds and was not hungry anymore. The bird knew that it should not try to eat pebbles again. The bird learned a lesson and lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to play by the river. They saw a raft near the water. Tim said, "Spot, let's go on the raft and have fun!" So, they got on the raft and started to float away. As they went down the river, they saw a mighty tree. The tree was big and tall. Tim and Spot were happy and shouted, "We love this raft ride!" They laughed and had a good time. But then, the sky got dark and it started to rain. The river got wild and fast. Tim and Spot were scared. They shouted, "Help, help!" But no one heard them. The raft hit a big rock and broke. Tim and Spot fell into the water and got very wet. They were sad and cold. The fun day on the raft had a bad ending.
