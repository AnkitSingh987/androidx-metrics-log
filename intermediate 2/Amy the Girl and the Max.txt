Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Max

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was sad because her dog, Max, was lost. She looked everywhere for him. One day, she heard a sound coming from underground. She thought maybe Max was there. Amy asked her mom about the sound. Her mom did not answer. Amy felt that something was not right. She wanted to find Max, so she went to the underground place. When Amy got to the underground place, she found Max. But Max was not happy. He was very sick. Amy tried to help him, but it was too late. Max did not get better. Amy was very, very sad. The end.

Once, there was a big dog named Bob. Bob lived in a yard. He was fierce, but he had a kind heart. He liked playing with his ball and running around. One day, a little bird came to the yard. The bird did not understand where it was. It was lost. The bird was scared of the fierce dog, but Bob wanted to help. Bob and the bird became friends. They played in the yard every day. Soon, the bird understood that Bob was not so fierce. They were happy together, and the yard was their favorite place to be.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom liked to dream a lot. Every day, he would find a warm spot and take a long nap. One day, while Tom was dreaming, he saw a big red ball. It was as big as his waist! Tom wanted to play with the red ball. He tried to catch it, but the ball rolled away. Tom chased the red ball all around in his dream. He ran and ran until he was too tired to run anymore. When Tom woke up from his dream, he felt happy. He had so much fun playing with the big red ball in his dream.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who had a bed. She loved her bed so much, but one day it started to shrink! The girl was very sad because she could no longer fit in her bed. She asked her daddy why it was shrinking and he said he didn't know. The girl tried to think of a way to make her bed bigger, and finally she had an idea. She ran to the kitchen, grabbed a bowl, and filled it with water. She brought it back to her room and put it on the bed. The bed started to grow again and soon it was the same size it used to be! The little girl was very happy. Now she could sleep in her bed, just like before! Her daddy was so happy that he gave her a big hug, and the girl never forgot her bed-shrinking adventure.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his room. He saw a mirror on the wall. Tim looked in the mirror and saw his face. He smiled and said, "Hello, me!" Tim's dog, Spot, came into the room. Spot was smelly because he played in the mud. Tim said, "Spot, you are smelly! We need to start your bath." Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" They went to the bathroom to clean Spot. Tim filled the tub with water and put Spot in. He washed Spot with soap and water until he was clean. Then, Tim and Spot went back to the mirror. Tim said, "Now we are both clean and happy!" They laughed and played together for the rest of the day.

One day, a dog named Max found a big bone. He was very happy. Max saw his friend, a cat named Luna. Luna was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Luna?" Luna said, "I am hungry. I want food, but there is no food available." Max thought for a moment. He wondered if he should share his bone with Luna. Max loved his bone, but he loved his friend more. Max decided to break the bone in half. He gave one half to Luna. Luna was very happy. They both ate their bones and were not hungry anymore. They played together all day. Max and Luna were best friends.
