Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Magic

Lily and Tom were very excited. They were going to see a magic show at the park. They put on their hats and coats and followed Mom and Dad to the car. At the park, they saw a big tent with a sign that said "Magic Show". They went inside and found some seats. The tent was full of people and noise. Lily and Tom clapped their hands and waited for the show to start. Soon, a man in a funny hat and coat came on the stage. He had a big smile and a wand. He said, "Hello, everyone! Welcome to the magic show! I am the great Magico, and I will amaze you with my tricks. For my first trick, I need a volunteer. Who wants to help me?" Lily and Tom raised their hands. They wanted to help. The man looked around and pointed at Lily. He said, "You, little girl, come up here. Don't be shy." Lily got up and walked to the stage. She felt nervous and happy. The man said, "What is your name, little girl?" Lily said, "Lily." The man said, "Nice to meet you, Lily. Now, do you see this egg?" He showed Lily a white egg. Lily nodded. The man said, "This is a magic egg. Watch what I can do with it." He tapped the egg with his wand and said, "Abracadabra!" He cracked the egg and opened it. It was empty. There was no yolk or white inside. Lily gasped. The man said, "But wait, there's more. If I tap the egg again, something will appear. Do you want to see?" Lily nodded. The man tapped the egg again and said, "Abracadabra!" He opened the egg and out came a fluffy yellow chick. It peeped and hopped on the stage. Lily laughed. The man said, "Isn't it cute? Do you want to hold it?" Lily nodded. The man gave her the chick and said, "Be careful, it's very soft and fragile." Lily held the chick gently and stroked its feathers. It peeped and snuggled in her arms. The man said, "Let's give a big hand to Lily and the magic chick!" The people in the tent clapped and cheered. Lily smiled and waved. She felt proud and happy. She gave the chick back to the man and went back to her seat. Tom hugged her and said, "That was awesome, Lily. You did great." Lily said, "Thank you, Tom. It was fun. I like the magic show." Tom said, "Me too. I wonder what the next trick will be." They watched the rest of the show and enjoyed the magic. They applauded after every trick. They had a wonderful time.

Once upon a time, there was a band of animals. The band had a lion, a bear, and a monkey. They all loved to play music together. The lion was fierce, but he was also kind. One day, the band wanted to send a letter to another band of animals. They wanted to invite them to a big party. The lion said, "Let's send them a letter with our music!" The bear wrote the letter and the monkey drew a picture. They put it all in a big envelope. Then, they gave it to a bird to send to the other band. The other band got the letter and loved it. They came to the party and played music with the fierce lion, the bear, and the monkey. All the animals danced and had fun. The two bands became best friends.

One day, a dependable reindeer named Bob was walking in the forest. He liked to mind his own business and help his friends. Bob met a little bird who was sad. "Hi, little bird," said Bob. "Why are you sad?" "I lost my way home," said the little bird. "Can you help me?" Bob wanted to help, so they went to find the bird's home. They walked and walked, but they could not find it. Then, something unexpected happened. They saw a big, shiny rock. "What is this?" asked the little bird. The big, shiny rock started to move! It was not a rock, but a big turtle! The turtle said, "I know where your home is, little bird." The turtle showed them the way, and they found the little bird's home. The little bird was happy, and they all became good friends.
