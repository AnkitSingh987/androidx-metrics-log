Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Grumpy Cat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to cook, and her favorite thing to cook was in a big pan. One day, Tom wanted to help Sue cook. He tried to load the pan with his favorite food, fish. But the pan was too heavy for him. Tom got very upset and knocked the pan over, making a big mess. Sue saw the mess and got sad. Tom felt bad and wanted to fix it. He found a smaller pan and tried again. This time, he could load the pan with fish. Sue saw Tom's hard work and smiled. They cooked the fish together and had a yummy dinner. From that day on, Tom was not grumpy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy oyster named Ollie. Ollie lived in the sea with his fish friends. Ollie liked to tell his friends what to do. One day, a little fish named Fin said, "Ollie, please don't be so bossy. We want to play too!" Ollie thought about what Fin said. He knew that he should be nicer to his friends. So, Ollie started to play with his friends and not be bossy. They all had fun together. In the end, Ollie learned that it is better to be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, in a lively green yard, there was a small, happy hedge. The hedge was home to a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends, the ants and the bugs. One sunny day, Tim and his friends went to the park to enjoy their day. They played hide and seek, and they laughed a lot. They also shared yummy food like seeds and crumbs. The sun was warm, and the grass was soft. As the day ended, Tim and his friends went back to the hedge. They were tired but happy. They said goodnight and went to sleep, ready for another fun day together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. Sarah was fearful of rainbows. She didn't understand why they made her feel so scared. One day, a big storm blew into town. Sarah was so worried that the rain would wash away her rainbow. She wanted to restore it so she started to look for help. She asked her mom but her mom was too busy. Then she asked her dad but he didn't know what to do. Sarah was getting sad. Just then, Sarah noticed a big, shimmering rainbow in the sky. She knew it was a sign that her rainbow could be restored. She gathered all her courage, grabbed some paint, and started to paint a new rainbow. When she finished, it was the most beautiful rainbow she had ever seen. Sarah was so happy. She had restored her rainbow and was no longer fearful of rainbows. She smiled to herself and went off to find something else to do.

Tom and Anna were friends. They liked to play outside in the sun. One day, they saw a mosquito on a flower. Tom wanted to catch it. He ran to the flower and tried to grab the mosquito. But he missed. He hit the flower instead. The flower fell to the ground. Anna was sad. She liked the flower. It was pretty and smelled good. She looked at Tom. Tom looked guilty. He knew he did a bad thing. He said sorry to Anna. He said sorry to the flower. He picked up the flower and gave it to Anna. Anna smiled. She was not mad at Tom. She knew he did not mean to hurt the flower. She said thank you to Tom. She said it was okay. She put the flower in her hair. She gave Tom a hug. They were still friends. They went to play somewhere else. They did not see the mosquito again.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor named Tom. Tom had a big boat. He loved to sail on the water. One day, Tom saw a difficult big wave coming. He said, "Oh no! I need to be careful!" Tom tried to sail away from the big wave. But the wave was too fast. Tom's boat started to shake. He held on tight, but then he began to fall. As he fell, he said, "Help me!" But then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly fish jumped out of the water. The fish caught Tom before he hit the water. Tom was so happy! He said, "Thank you, big fish!" The fish smiled and swam away. Tom knew he had a new friend in the sea.
