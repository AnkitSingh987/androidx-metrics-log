Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to make things with her hands. She would make toys, pictures, and even little houses with blocks. One day, Amy's mommy told her about a fun contest. The contest was to make the best toy, and the winner would get a prize. Amy was very excited. She wanted to win the prize. So, she thought very hard about what toy she could make. She decided to make a clear toy car. She used clear plastic to make the car so you could see through it. It was a special car that no one had ever seen before. Amy finished her toy car and took it to the contest. There were many other kids with their toys too. The judges looked at all the toys, and they liked Amy's clear car very much. They gave her the prize for the best toy. Amy was very happy and proud of her car. She knew that making things was fun and special.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big toy box full of amazing toys. One day, she wanted to play with her toy police car. But, she could not find it. She looked and looked, but the toy police car was not in the box. Amy was sad. She asked her mom to help her find the toy police car. They looked all around the house, but they could not find it. Then, Amy's mom had an idea. She asked Amy, "Did you try to fit the toy police car in your pocket?" Amy's eyes grew wide. She checked her pocket and there it was! The toy police car was in her pocket the whole time! Amy was so happy to find her toy police car. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, Mom!" The moral of the story is always to remember where you put your things. And if you can't find something, ask for help. Sometimes, the thing you are looking for might be closer than you think.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his blue ball. Every day, he would go outside and shoot the ball up in the sky. He liked to see how high it could go. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with his blue ball. He threw it up in the sky and watched it fly high. But this time, the ball went so high that it got stuck in a tree! Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. A nice bird saw Tim and wanted to help. The bird flew up to the tree and pushed the blue ball with its beak. The ball fell down, and Tim caught it! Tim was so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a kind man. He had a job to deliver things to people. One day, he had to deliver a big box to a house with a gate. When he got to the house, he saw a rude boy by the gate. The boy said, "You can't come in!" The man felt sad but he did not want to be rude too. So, he waited and thought of what to do. Then, the man had an idea. He gave the boy a nice toy from his bag. The boy was happy and opened the gate. The man delivered the box and went on his way. The rude boy learned to be nice and they all lived happily.

Tom and Lily were playing with blocks in the living room. They liked to build towers and houses and cars with the blocks. They had many colors and shapes of blocks to choose from. But then, their big brother Ben came in. He was bored and wanted to have some fun. He saw the blocks and had a terrible idea. He decided to knock down all the blocks that Tom and Lily had built. He ran around the living room and pushed and kicked the blocks with his feet and hands. Tom and Lily were very sad and angry. They had worked hard to make their blocks look nice. They shouted at Ben and told him to stop. But Ben did not listen. He laughed and laughed as he made a big mess of the blocks. He thought it was funny to see Tom and Lily cry. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was going on. She saw the blocks all over the floor and Ben laughing. She saw Tom and Lily crying and holding some blocks in their hands. She was very upset and disappointed. She scolded Ben and told him to pick up all the blocks and put them back in the box. She said he was wasting the blocks and hurting Tom and Lily's feelings. She said he had to say sorry and share the blocks nicely. Ben felt ashamed and sorry. He realized he had been very mean and rude. He picked up all the blocks and put them back in the box. He said sorry to Tom and Lily and gave them a hug. He asked them if they wanted to play with him and the blocks. Tom and Lily nodded and smiled. They forgave Ben and decided to give him another chance. They played with the blocks together and made new towers and houses and cars. They laughed and laughed as they had fun with the blocks. Mom was happy to see them get along and play nicely. She said they were good children and gave them a kiss.
