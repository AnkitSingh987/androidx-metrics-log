Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cold Day and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a cold day. On this day, a little boy named Tim and his sister Sue went to see an opera. They wore big coats to stay warm. At the opera, they saw people singing and dancing. Tim liked the opera a lot, but Sue did not. Sue wanted to go home and play with her toys. Tim and Sue started to disagree about staying at the opera or going home. In the end, their mom said they could leave the opera early. Tim was sad, but Sue was happy. When they got home, they played together and had fun. They learned that it's okay to disagree sometimes, but they should always love each other.

---

Once upon a time, there was a sad little girl named Lily. She had a yellow bike that she loved very much. But one day, her bike broke, and she could not ride it anymore. Lily was so sad that she cried. Lily's dad saw her crying and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" She told him about her yellow bike and how it was broken. Her dad said, "Don't worry, I will fix it for you." Lily was happy to hear that. Lily's dad worked hard to fix the yellow bike. When he was done, he had a big smile on his face. He called Lily to come and see. But when Lily saw her bike, she gasped! Her dad had not only fixed her bike, but he had also painted it with pretty flowers and butterflies. Lily was so happy and could not wait to ride her new, beautiful bike.

---

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and run after it. Sometimes they missed the ball and it rolled away. Then they had to chase it before it went too far. One day, the ball rolled into the street. Tom and Lily ran after it, but they did not look for cars. A big car was coming fast and honked its horn. Tom and Lily were scared and stopped. The car stopped too, and a man got out. He was wearing a blue shirt and a hat. He was a cop. The cop was angry and said, "What are you doing? You could have been hurt! You have to be careful and look both ways before you cross the street. Where are your parents?" Tom and Lily did not know what to say. They were sorry and afraid. They looked for their mom, who was sitting on a bench nearby. She saw what happened and ran to them. She hugged them and said, "Are you okay? I'm so sorry, officer. I was watching them, but they ran away so fast. I should have been more attentive." The cop was still angry, but he saw that the mom was sorry too. He said, "It's okay, ma'am. I'm glad they are not hurt. But you have to teach them how to be safe. The street is not a place to play. You have to use the crosswalk and wait for the green light." The mom said, "You are right, officer. Thank you for stopping and helping us. I will talk to them and make sure they understand. Can we offer you something to drink? We have some juice and water in our bag." The cop was surprised by the offer. He said, "That's very kind of you, ma'am. But I have to go back to work. I have to catch bad guys and keep people safe. Maybe another time." The mom said, "Okay, officer. Thank you again. Have a good day." The cop said, "You're welcome, ma'am. Have a good day too. And remember, be careful." He got back in his car and drove away. Tom and Lily waved goodbye to him. They learned a lesson that day. They never ran into the street again. They always looked both ways and used the crosswalk. They also learned that cops are not always scary. Sometimes they are nice and helpful. And sometimes they have unusual offers.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a razor in the bathroom. He did not know what it was, but it looked interesting. He thought it might be a toy. Tim wanted to show his mom, so he took the razor to her. "Mom, look at this toy I found!" Tim said. His mom saw the razor and was worried. "Oh no, Tim! That is a bad thing to play with. It can hurt you. Put it back in the bathroom, please." Tim felt sad, but he listened to his mom. He put the razor back in the bathroom. That night, Tim saw something glow in his room. It was a little bug. The bug was glowing in the dark. Tim was happy to see the glowing bug and forgot about the bad razor. "Look, Mom! A glowing bug!" Tim said. His mom came to see the bug. "Wow, that's a firefly, Tim. They glow in the dark. It's a safe and fun thing to play with." Tim and his mom watched the glowing firefly together. They were both happy, and Tim learned that some things are fun and safe to play with, while others are bad and can hurt you.
