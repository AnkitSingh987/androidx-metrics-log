Category: Intermediate
Title: True the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named True. True was a gifted boy who loved to draw. He knew how to draw many things like animals, trees, and houses. True had a best friend named Sam. They played together all the time. One day, True and Sam were playing in the park. True asked Sam, "Do you know how to draw?" Sam shook his head and said, "No, I don't know how to draw. Can you teach me?" True smiled and said, "Of course! I will show you how to draw a cat." True took out his drawing book and a pencil. He started to draw a cat, making simple lines and shapes. Sam watched closely and tried to copy what True was doing. After a while, Sam finished drawing his cat. He was so happy and said, "Thank you, True! Now I know how to draw a cat!" From that day on, True and Sam drew many pictures together. They had lots of fun and learned new things. They knew that being gifted was not just about knowing how to do something well but also about sharing it with others.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a trumpet in his toy box. He was very happy and wanted to play it. But first, he needed to gather all his friends to show them his new toy. He asked his mom to help him find all his friends that were available to come and play. When all his friends came, Tim started to play the trumpet very loud. His friends did not like the loud noise. They covered their ears and asked Tim to stop playing. But Tim did not listen and kept playing the trumpet. This made his friends very sad and they decided to go home. Tim felt sad and alone. His mom told him that he should have listened to his friends and not played the trumpet so loud. Tim learned that it is important to think about how others feel. The next day, Tim invited his friends back and played the trumpet softly. They all had fun, and Tim was happy that he had learned the right way to play with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a bit fearful of the dark. One night, she heard a noise in the kitchen. She held her teddy bear tight and went to see what it was. When she got to the kitchen, she saw a fork and a spoon talking to each other. The fork said, "Hi, I am Forky! Do you want to play with us?" Lily was scared at first, but then she saw that they were smiling and friendly. Lily smiled back and said, "Yes, I want to play!" They all played together, and Lily was not fearful anymore. She became friends with Forky and Spoonie, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big red ball and a small blue circle were playing in the park. They were very good friends. The red ball bounced fast, and the blue circle rolled slow. They liked to play together. As they played, the blue circle got dirty. "Oh no!" said the blue circle. "I need to get clean." The red ball had an idea. "I can help you," said the red ball. "I can wipe the dirt off of you." The red ball wiped the blue circle with a soft cloth. The blue circle became clean again. "Thank you, red ball," said the blue circle. "You are a good friend." The red ball and the blue circle continued to play in the park. They laughed and had lots of fun. And if the blue circle got dirty again, the red ball was always there to help wipe it clean.

Once upon a time, in a big, wild forest, there lived a little bunny named Binky. Binky loved to hop and play with his friends. The forest was always full of fun things to do and see. One day, Binky found a shiny stamp on the ground. He picked it up and saw it had a picture of a big, happy sun. Binky got very excited and wanted to show his friends the stamp. He hopped and hopped, looking for them. Finally, Binky found his friends, and they all gathered around to see the stamp. They were excited too! They took turns stamping the shiny sun on leaves and rocks, giggling and laughing. They played with the stamp all day, and it was the best day ever for Binky and his friends.
