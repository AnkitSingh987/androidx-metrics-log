Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Ball

Sara and Tom were twins. They liked to play in the big hall with their toys. One day, they found a new toy in the hall. It was a shiny ball that made funny noises when you rolled it. "Look, Tom, a new toy!" Sara said. "Let's play with it!" "OK, Sara, but we have to be careful. Mom said not to touch anything that is not ours," Tom said. They rolled the ball back and forth in the hall. They laughed and had fun. They did not hear Mom calling them for lunch. Mom came to the hall and saw them playing with the ball. She was very angry. She took the ball away from them. "What are you doing with this ball? This is not your toy! This is Dad's gift for his boss. He was going to give it to him today. You have ruined it! Look, it has scratches and dents. How could you be so naughty?" Mom yelled. Sara and Tom were very scared. They did not know the ball was important. They only wanted to play. "Mom, we are sorry. We did not know. We thought it was a toy. Please don't be mad at us," Sara said. "Sorry is not enough. You have to learn to respect other people's things. You are grounded for a week. No TV, no games, no snacks. Go to your room now!" Mom said. Sara and Tom cried. They ran to their room. They felt very sad and worried. They wished they had not played with the ball. They wished they had listened to Mom. They wished they had a better day.

Once there was a little girl named Hannah. One day, she was walking home from school when she saw a strange man. He was tall and had a funny hat on. Hannah was curious about the stranger, so she walked over to him. "Hi," she said. The stranger smiled. "Hi," he said in a friendly voice. Hannah started to talk to him. She told the stranger all about school and the things she liked to do. At first the stranger seemed normal, but soon he started acting funny. He kept asking her all sorts of questions, which made her feel uncomfortable. Hannah was scared, so she quickly said goodbye and ran away. She ran all the way home and never saw the stranger again.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate butterfly. The butterfly had wings with beautiful ink on them. The butterfly liked to fly and play with her friends. She was very happy. One day, a big wind came. The wind was very strong. The butterfly was scared. She did not want the ink on her wings to go away. The butterfly needed to protect her wings. But then, something unexpected happened. A kind turtle came to help. The turtle said, "I will protect you and your ink." The butterfly was surprised but happy. She flew onto the turtle's back. The turtle walked slow and kept the butterfly safe from the wind. From that day on, the butterfly and the turtle were best friends.

Anna liked to help her mom in the kitchen. She liked to wash the vegetables and cut them with a plastic knife. She liked to make salads and soups and pies. She liked to smell the yummy food and taste it with a spoon. One day, her mom asked her to help make a vegetable stew. Anna was happy and ran to the fridge. She took out some carrots, potatoes, onions and peas. She washed them and put them on the counter. She took her plastic knife and started to cut the carrots. "Be careful, Anna," her mom said. "Don't cut your fingers." "I know, mom," Anna said. "I'm a big girl." She cut the carrots into small pieces and put them in a big pot. She did the same with the potatoes and the onions. She opened a can of peas and poured them in the pot. She added some water and salt and pepper. She stirred the stew with a wooden spoon. "Can I turn on the stove, mom?" Anna asked. "No, Anna," her mom said. "That's too dangerous. I'll do it for you." She turned on the stove and put the pot on it. She lowered the heat and put a lid on the pot. "Now we have to wait for the stew to cook," she said. "It will take some time." "Can we do something else while we wait, mom?" Anna asked. "Sure, Anna," her mom said. "What do you want to do?" Anna thought for a moment. She looked around the kitchen and saw a book on the table. It was a book of fairy tales. She loved fairy tales. She loved the stories of princesses and dragons and magic. "Can we read a fairy tale, mom?" Anna asked. "Of course, Anna," her mom said. "That's a charming idea. Let's go to the couch and read a fairy tale." They went to the couch and sat down. Anna picked up the book and opened it. She saw a picture of a beautiful princess with long hair and a handsome prince with a sword. She pointed to the picture and said, "I want to read this one, mom." "OK, Anna," her mom said. "This one is called Rapunzel. It's about a princess who was locked in a tower by a witch and how a prince came to rescue her." She started to read the story to Anna. Anna listened with wonder and excitement. She imagined she was Rapunzel and her mom was the prince. She smiled and hugged her mom. They read the story until they heard a whistle from the kitchen. It was the pot of stew. It was ready. "Oops, we have to pause the story, Anna," her mom said. "The stew is done. We have to turn off the stove and serve it." "OK, mom," Anna said. "But can we finish the story later?" "Sure, Anna," her mom said. "We can finish the story after we eat. Come on, let's go to the kitchen and enjoy our vegetable stew." They went to the kitchen and ladled the stew into bowls. They sat at the table and ate the stew. It was hot and delicious. They praised each other for their cooking skills. "This is the best vegetable stew ever, mom," Anna said. "Thank you, Anna," her mom said. "You are a great helper. And this is the best fairy tale ever, Anna." "Thank you, mom," Anna said. "You are a great reader. I love you, mom." "I love you too, Anna," her mom said. They smiled and hugged each other. They were happy.
