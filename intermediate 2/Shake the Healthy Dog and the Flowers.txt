Category: Intermediate
Title: Shake the Healthy Dog and the Flowers

Once upon a time, in a big pretty temple, there lived a healthy dog named Shake. Shake loved to run, jump, and play with all his friends. The temple was a fun place with lots of trees, flowers, and sunshine. One day, Shake and his friends decided to play a game of hide and seek. Shake was very good at this game. He would shake his tail and run to find the best hiding spot. His friends would laugh and try to find him, but Shake was always the last one to be found. After a long day of playing, Shake and his friends would sit under a big tree and eat their snacks. They would talk about their day and share stories. Shake was a happy and healthy dog, and he loved living in the beautiful temple with all his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a green fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day long. He lived in a big zoo with his friends. One day, the zoo had a big party. All the animals were happy. Fin wanted to swim fast to show everyone how good he was. So, he swam as fast as he could. Fin swam so fast that everyone clapped for him. They all said, "Wow, Fin is the best swimmer!" Fin felt very proud and happy. From that day on, everyone knew Fin was the best swimmer at the zoo.

Once upon a time, in a colorful forest, there lived a little squirrel named Nutty. Nutty loved to play with his friends, but he did not like to share his toys. He would say, "I hate sharing!" and keep all his toys to himself. One sunny day, Nutty and his friends went to play near a big tree. Nutty climbed high up the tree with his toy car. He was having so much fun, but his friends were sad because they had no toys to play with. They asked Nutty to share, but he still said, "I hate sharing!" Then, something bad happened. Nutty lost his balance and had a big fall from the tree. His toy car broke into many pieces, and Nutty was hurt. His friends came to help him, but they were not happy with Nutty. They said, "If you had shared, maybe you wouldn't have fallen." Now, Nutty was sad and hurt. He learned that not sharing can lead to bad things. Nutty wished he had shared his toys with his friends.

One day, a polite boy named Tim went to the airport with his mom. They wanted to go on a trip to see Tim's grandma. Tim was very excited because he loved to fly in big planes. At the airport, Tim saw a big plane. He asked his mom, "Can we go inside now?" His mom said, "Yes, but first we need to put our bags on the belt." They put their bags on the belt and went inside the plane. On the plane, Tim sat by the window. He saw many other planes outside. Tim said, "Thank you, Mom, for taking me to the airport. I love planes!" His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Have fun looking at the planes!"

Once upon a time, there was a little cat who loved to play at night. This cat was different because it had a big, long tail. One day, the cat was playing with a ball when something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the ball away. The cat tried to catch the ball but it was too fast. The cat was sad and didn't know what to do. It looked around and saw a new friend, a little dog with a short tail. The little dog wanted to help the cat find the ball. They looked and looked, but they couldn't find it. Then, the little dog had an idea. It used its tail to slap the ground and make a loud sound. The cat did the same with its long tail. Suddenly, the ball came rolling back to them. It was like magic! The cat and the dog were so happy. They played together all night and became best friends. From that day on, they knew that being different was a good thing.
