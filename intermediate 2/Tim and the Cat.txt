Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Cat

One day, a little boy named Tim was watching his favorite cartoon. The cartoon had a funny dog and a big cat. They liked to play and run around. Tim's mom came into the room and saw Tim laughing. "Tim, do you like this cartoon?" she asked. Tim shrugged and said, "Yes, mom! It's so funny!" Tim's mom smiled and said, "Okay, I have a surprise for you later." Later, Tim's mom took him to the park. There, Tim saw a big tree with a dead branch. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim. Don't go near the dead branch." Tim remembered the funny dog and big cat from the cartoon and decided to play far away from the dead branch. At the end of the day, Tim's mom gave him the surprise. It was a toy of the funny dog from the cartoon! Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, mom!" He played with the toy and remembered to stay away from the dead branch at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play with her ball. One day, Fluffy saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. She looked up and saw a bird on a branch. Fluffy thought that if she climbed the tree, she could play with the bird. Fluffy started to climb the tree. The bird flew away, but Fluffy kept going higher and higher. Fluffy was having fun, but then she looked down and got scared. She was stuck in the tree and didn't know how to get down. Fluffy started to cry. A nice man named Tom heard Fluffy crying. Tom was good at fixing things. He thought he could help Fluffy. Tom got a long ladder and put it against the tree. He climbed up the ladder and carefully picked up Fluffy. Tom brought Fluffy down and she was so happy. Fluffy gave Tom a big hug and they became good friends. Now, Fluffy knew that if she ever needed help, Tom would be there to fix things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to clean her room. One day, her mom asked her to go to the grocery store to buy food. Sue was excited and said, "Yes, Mom! I will go to the grocery store!" On her way to the grocery store, Sue met a crazy dog. The dog jumped and barked at Sue. Sue was scared and said, "Please, dog, don't hurt me!" But the crazy dog did not listen. It chased Sue all the way back to her house. Sue ran inside and told her mom about the crazy dog. Her mom was worried and said, "We will not go to the grocery store today." Sue was sad because she could not help her mom. The crazy dog made it so they could not get food from the grocery store. And that day, Sue and her mom had to eat old food for dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he was very proud of. One day, while playing with his toy, a screw fell out. Tim was sad because his car could not move anymore. Tim's mom saw him crying and came to comfort him. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can fix your car!" Tim's eyes lit up, and he felt happy again. They looked for the screw together, and they found it under the table. Tim's mom helped him put the screw back into the car. Now, the car could move again! But, as they played, something unexpected happened. The car started to talk! It said, "Thank you for fixing me!" Tim and his mom were surprised, but they laughed and played with the talking car all day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small noisy town, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to walk with her dog, Spot. One sunny day, Lily and Spot went for a walk to the post office. On their way, they met a friendly cat. The cat said, "Meow! Where are you going?" Lily replied, "We are going to the post office to send a letter to my grandma." The cat said, "That sounds fun! Can I come too?" Lily smiled and said, "Of course!" Together, they walked to the post office. Spot barked happily, and the cat purred. They reached the post office and sent the letter. After that, they walked back home, laughing and playing all the way.
