Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily had an amazing toy named Mr. Fork. Mr. Fork was not only a toy, but also her best friend. They played together every day. One day, a new boy named Tim moved next door. Lily wanted to welcome Tim, so she invited him to play with her and Mr. Fork. Tim said, "Thank you, Lily! I would love to play with you and Mr. Fork." While they were playing, Tim accidentally broke Mr. Fork. Lily was very sad and cried. Tim said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to break Mr. Fork." But it was too late, Mr. Fork was broken and could not be fixed. Lily and Tim did not play together again, and Lily missed her amazing friend, Mr. Fork.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was a foolish boy who liked to scare his friends. One day, he found a magazine with a big, scary face on it. Tim had a plan to scare his best friend, Sam. Tim took the magazine to Sam's house. He hid behind a tree and waited for Sam to come out. When Sam walked out of his house, Tim jumped out from behind the tree with the magazine. He shouted, "Boo!" to scare Sam. But Sam did not get scared. He laughed at Tim and said, "That is just a magazine, Tim. It's not real." Tim felt silly for trying to scare Sam with a magazine. From that day on, Tim learned not to be foolish and stopped trying to scare his friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a message in a bottle. He was playing by the water when he saw it. Tim picked up the bottle and opened it. Inside, he found a piece of paper with the word "start" on it. Tim ran to his mom and showed her the message. "Look, Mom! I found a message in a bottle!" he said. His mom looked at the paper and said, "That's interesting, Tim. I wonder what it means." Just then, a big, disgusting bug came out of the bottle. Tim and his mom screamed and jumped back. The bug looked at them and said, "Thank you for letting me out of the bottle! I was stuck in there for a long time. The word 'start' was a magic word to set me free." Tim and his mom were very surprised. They never thought they would meet a talking bug. The bug thanked them again and flew away. Tim and his mom smiled at each other, happy that they could help the bug, even though he was disgusting.

One day, a boy named Tom found a toy rocket. It was red and shiny. Tom loved his new rocket. He played with it all day long. He threw it up in the air and watched it come back down. He ran with it in his hands, making it fly fast. One hot day, Tom took his rocket outside to play. The sun was very bright and hot. Tom started to feel hot too. He began to complain. "I'm too hot," Tom said. But he did not want to stop playing with his rocket. Tom had an idea. He went inside his house and got a big fan. He put the fan outside and turned it on. The fan made a cool wind. Tom felt better and stopped complaining. He played with his rocket all day long, happy and cool in the hot sun.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big pile of snow in the park. They wanted to make a snowman, but they had different opinions. Tom wanted to use a carrot for the nose, and Lily wanted to use a button. Tom wanted to use a hat for the head, and Lily wanted to use a scarf. They started to argue and shout at each other. While they were fighting, they did not notice that it was getting colder and darker. The wind blew harder and harder, and they began to shiver. They were too angry to share their coats or gloves. They felt very cold and sad. Then, they saw a little boy walking by. He had a big smile on his face and a lovely snowman in his arms. He had used a carrot, a button, a hat, and a scarf to make his snowman. He had mixed and matched the things he had found. He saw Tom and Lily and said, "Hello, do you want to play with me and my snowman? He is very friendly and warm." Tom and Lily looked at each other and felt ashamed. They realized that they had been silly and selfish. They said sorry to each other and hugged. They thanked the little boy and joined him to play. They learned that it is better to be kind and share than to fight and shiver. They had fun and made a new friend. They also made a bigger and better snowman together. They used a carrot, a button, a hat, and a scarf, and they added some more things they had found. They gave their snowman a big smile and a hug. They felt very happy and warm.
