Category: Intermediate
Title: The Reindeer and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a reindeer who loved to swim. One day, he went to the pond to play in the water. He was so happy to splash and swim around. But then, a smelly frog came to the pond. The smelly frog did not like the reindeer playing in his pond. The frog said, "Go away, reindeer! This is my pond!" The reindeer felt sad, but he did not want to fight with the smelly frog. So, he went to find a new place to swim. He walked and walked until he found a big river. The reindeer jumped into the river and started to swim. He was so happy to swim again. The smelly frog stayed at the pond, and the reindeer found a new, fun place to play. And they both lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a chair in his room. He had never seen this chair before. It was blue and had a soft seat. Tim was ready to sit on it and read his favorite book. Before he sat down, Tim wanted to examine the chair. He looked at the legs, the back, and the seat. It was a very nice chair. Tim was very happy to have a new chair in his room. Tim sat down on the chair and opened his book. The chair was very comfortable. He read his book and had a great time. Tim loved his new chair and knew he would use it every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She was an honest girl who always told the truth. One day, Sue was playing outside when she saw a big drain. She was curious and wanted to see what was inside. Sue walked closer to the drain and saw a small, wet kitten. The kitten was shivering and looked very cold. Sue felt sad for the kitten and wanted to help. She ran back to her house and found a soft, warm blanket. Sue knew she had to wrap the kitten in the blanket to make it warm. Sue carefully picked up the kitten and said, "Don't worry, little kitten. I will wrap you in this blanket to keep you warm." She gently wrapped the kitten in the blanket and carried it back to her house. Sue's mom saw her and asked, "What do you have there, Sue?" Sue replied, "I found a cold kitten by the drain, Mom. I wrapped it in a blanket to keep it warm." Sue's mom was proud of her honest and kind daughter. They took care of the kitten and soon, it became a happy and healthy part of their family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy called a magnet. The magnet could stick to things made of metal. Tim loved to play with his magnet all day. One day, Tim saw two metal toys stuck together. He wanted to separate them. So, Tim used his magnet to pull them apart. It worked! The toys were not stuck anymore. Tim was very happy. Later, Tim felt hungry. He went to his mom and asked for a snack. His mom gave him a yummy sandwich. Tim ate the sandwich and felt full. He went back to play with his magnet and his toys, having a fun day.

One day, a silly cat named Max went for a walk in the woods. Max liked to nap on soft grass. Max saw a big tree with soft grass under it. Max thought, "This is a good place to nap." So Max lay down on the soft grass and went to sleep. While Max was napping, a big man came into the woods. The big man had an axe. The big man wanted to cut the big tree down. Max woke up and saw the big man with the axe. Max was scared. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and sat on the axe. The little bird sang a pretty song. The big man smiled and put the axe down. He did not cut the tree. Max was happy. The big man, the little bird, and Max all took a nap under the big tree.
