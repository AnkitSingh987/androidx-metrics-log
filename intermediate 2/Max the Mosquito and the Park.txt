Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Mosquito and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a friendly mosquito named Max. Max lived in a big green park with lots of trees and flowers. He had many friends, like bees, butterflies, and ladybugs. They all played together every day. One day, Max and his friends decided to play a game. They wanted to separate into two teams. Max said, "Let's make a team of big bugs and a team of small bugs!" The other bugs agreed, and they began to play. They had lots of fun chasing each other around the park. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew all the bugs away. Max was scared and cried, "Help! Help! I can't find my friends!" But then, something unexpected happened. The wind had blown Max and his friends to a new park. This park was even bigger and more beautiful than the one they lived in before. Max and his friends were so happy to be together again, and they played in their new home every day.

Once upon a time, there was a group of animals who lived in a big forest. They were all friends and helped each other. In this group, there was a strong and dependable bear named Ben. Everyone liked Ben because he was always there to help them when they needed it. One day, a little rabbit named Remy got stuck in a hole. He tried to get out but he struggled a lot. Remy called for help and soon Ben and the other animals came to see what was wrong. They saw Remy in the hole and wanted to help him. Ben reached down with his big paw and gently lifted Remy out of the hole. All the animals were happy that Remy was safe. They thanked Ben for being dependable and helping their friend. The animals learned that it is good to have friends who are always there for you when you need help. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Kelly. She was playing bow and arrow with her best friend, Todd. Todd had a great big arrow that went very far and Kelly was very impressed. She asked if she could try it. Todd handed the arrow to Kelly, but then he forgot to tell her how to use it. Kelly was embarrassed and put the arrow down on the ground. Todd saw Kelly feeling silly and said, "I'm sorry! I forgot to tell you how it works. Here, let me show you how to do it. Put the arrow on your bow like this, then pull back and let go." Kelly tried it and was amazed by how far the arrow flew. She smiled and said, "Thanks for teaching me how to use it Todd! Let's play again!"

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a bird with a broken wing. The bird could not fly. It was sad. One day, a kind rabbit saw the bird and said, "I will help you." The rabbit went to find help. It met an unknown bug who could perform magic. The bug said, "I can help the bird, but I need something in return." The rabbit agreed to the deal. The unknown bug performed magic on the bird's wing. The bird could fly again. But the rabbit had to give the bug all its food. The rabbit was hungry and sad. The bird flew away, and the rabbit was left with nothing.

Sara and Ben like to play in the park. They have a big box of toys. They have cars, dolls, balls, and books. But their favorite toy is a dragon. The dragon is green and soft. It has wings, horns, and a long tail. One day, a new boy comes to the park. His name is Max. He sees Sara and Ben playing with the dragon. He wants to play too. He runs to them and grabs the dragon. "Hey, that's mine!" Sara says. She tries to pull the dragon back. "No, it's mine now!" Max says. He holds the dragon tight. "Stop it, you're hurting him!" Ben says. He stares at Max with angry eyes. Max stares back. He does not care. He likes the dragon. He thinks it is cool. Sara and Ben are sad and mad. They want their dragon back. They do not like Max. They think he is mean. Then, they hear a voice. It is the dragon. He speaks in a low and gentle voice. "Please, let me go. I do not like to be squeezed. I am delicate. I want to be with Sara and Ben. They are my friends. They take good care of me." Max is surprised. He did not know the dragon can talk. He feels sorry. He realizes he was wrong. He lets go of the dragon. "I'm sorry, dragon. I'm sorry, Sara and Ben. I was rude. Can I play with you?" Max says. Sara and Ben look at the dragon. The dragon nods. He smiles. "Okay, you can play with us. But you have to be nice. And you have to share. The dragon is not only yours. He is ours." Sara says. Max agrees. He says he will be nice and share. He apologizes again. Sara and Ben forgive him. They all play together. They have fun. The dragon is happy. He has more friends.
