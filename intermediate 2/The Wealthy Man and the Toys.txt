Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wealthy Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who had a big house. He had a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toys all day long. One day, Tom wanted to play outside, but he did not know how to find the exit. He walked around the big house, looking for a way out. He saw a door and opened it, but it was not the exit. It was just a closet with more toys. Finally, Tom found the exit and went outside to play. He played with his ball and ran around in the sun. He was very happy. After playing all day, Tom went back inside the big house. He knew where the exit was now and could play outside whenever he wanted.

Once there was a rich lady who wanted to go on a train. She asked a 3 year old child to go with her. The child was very excited and said, "Yes, I'll come!" The rich lady then explained how a train moves. She said, "A train moves like a big car. It has lots of wheels and a loud engine!" The child thought for a moment and then asked, "What does the engine do?" The rich lady explained, "The engine is what makes the train go so fast. It makes a loud noise and then the train starts to move." The 3 year old was very impressed and said, "That's so cool!" The rich lady smiled and said, "Yes, let's go now and take a ride on the train!" So the 3 year old and the rich lady took a ride on the train together. They had lots of fun and made special memories that would last forever.

Ben and Lily liked to collect minerals. They had a big box full of shiny and colorful rocks that they found in the park, in the garden, or on the beach. They were very organized and sorted their minerals by size, shape, and color. One day, they decided to go on a picnic with their mom and dad. They packed some sandwiches, juice, and cookies in a basket. They also brought their box of minerals, because they wanted to show them to their friends. They walked to a nice spot under a big tree and spread a blanket on the grass. They ate their food and played with their toys. Then they saw some other kids nearby. They waved and said hello. "Hi, I'm Sam, and this is Mia," one of the kids said. "What are you playing with?" "We are playing with our minerals," Ben said. "We like to collect them. They are very pretty and interesting. Do you want to see?" "Sure, we love minerals too," Mia said. "Can we trade some with you?" Ben and Lily agreed. They opened their box and showed their minerals to Sam and Mia. They admired each other's rocks and exchanged some of them. They learned new names and facts about the minerals. They had a lot of fun. They became friends and decided to collect more minerals together. They thanked their mom and dad for the picnic and said goodbye to Sam and Mia. They put their new minerals in their box and carried it home. They were very happy.

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big tent in the park. It was a camp for older kids who learned new things. Lily and Tom wanted to see what was inside the tent. They ran to the tent and opened the flap. They saw many books, toys, and games on the floor. They picked up some of them and started to play. They did not hear the teacher coming back from the bathroom. "Hey, what are you doing here?" the teacher asked in a loud voice. "This is not your camp. This is for big kids only. You are not allowed to touch anything here. You are very naughty!" Lily and Tom felt scared and sorry. They dropped the things they were holding and ran out of the tent. They hoped the teacher would not tell their mom and dad. But the teacher was not so mean. She knew they were just curious and did not mean to make a mess. She followed them and called them back. "Wait, wait, don't run away. I'm not going to punish you. I just want to talk to you. Why did you come to the camp?" Lily and Tom stopped and turned around. They looked at the teacher with big eyes. "We wanted to see what was inside the tent. We like to learn new things too. We are sorry we touched your stuff. We did not break anything," Lily said. "Yes, we are sorry. We will not do it again. Please don't be mad at us," Tom said. The teacher smiled and knelt down to their level. "I'm not mad at you. I'm glad you like to learn new things. But you have to ask before you enter someone else's camp. And you have to be careful with the things you play with. They belong to other kids who worked hard to make them. Do you understand?" Lily and Tom nodded. "Yes, we understand. Thank you for not punishing us. You are very nice," Lily said. "Yes, thank you. You are very strong too. You can lift all those books and toys," Tom said. The teacher laughed and hugged them. "You are very sweet. And you are very smart too. You can learn a lot of things if you keep being curious and respectful. Maybe one day you can join the camp when you are older. Would you like that?" Lily and Tom nodded again. "Yes, we would like that very much. Thank you for letting us see the camp. Can we say goodbye to the other kids?" Lily asked. "Of course you can. Come on, let's go and meet them. They are very friendly and they will show you some of their projects. Maybe you can even play with them for a while. How does that sound?" Lily and Tom smiled and followed the teacher to the tent. They were happy they made a new friend and learned something new. They decided they would always ask before they enter someone else's camp. And they would always be careful with the things they play with. They knew that was the right thing to do.
