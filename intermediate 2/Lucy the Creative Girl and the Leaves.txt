Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Creative Girl and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a creative little girl named Lucy. She liked to make things out of sticks and leaves. One day, she found a big stick and had a fun idea. She wanted to make a sword! Lucy worked hard to make her stick look like a sword. She used leaves and some string to make it special. When it was done, she was very happy. She wanted to show her new sword to her friend, Sam. Lucy went to Sam's house and said, "Sam, I want to introduce you to my new sword!" Sam thought the sword was very cool. They played with the sword all day, having fun and being creative.

One day, a little shrimp was swimming in the sea. The little shrimp was very careful. He did not want to get lost. He had many friends in the sea. They liked to play together. The little shrimp was happy. One day, the little shrimp saw a big fish. The big fish had a game. The game was to race to a big rock. The little shrimp was excited. He wanted to play the game too. All his friends wanted to play the game too. The little shrimp was very careful. He swam very fast to the big rock. All his friends were excited too. They all swam very fast. The little shrimp won the race. He was very happy. All his friends were happy too. They all played together and had fun.

One day, a big machine was in a small town. The machine was very big and made a lot of noise. The people in the town did not like the noise. They wanted the machine to be soft and quiet. They tried to make the machine soft, but it was a struggle. A little girl named Lily lived in the town. She was very smart and loved to help people. Lily had an idea to make the machine soft. She went to her mom and dad and told them her plan. They thought it was a good idea, so they helped her. Lily and her mom and dad went to the big machine. They put soft things all around it. The machine became very quiet and soft. The people in the town were happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The machine started to make ice cream! The people in the town were very surprised. They loved the soft, quiet ice cream machine. Now, everyone in the town could enjoy ice cream and be happy.

One day, a little girl named True went to the park with her mom. They saw a big tree with pretty leaves. True saw a small, cute bird on a branch. It looked important. "Hi bird," True whispered. The bird whispered back, "Hi True, I have a secret to tell you." True was very surprised. She didn't know that birds could talk! The bird told True, "I am a magic bird. I can make one wish come true for you." True thought and thought. She wished for a new friend to play with. The bird flapped its wings, and suddenly, there was a little boy standing next to her. True and the boy became best friends and played together all day.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called James. He was three years old and he loved wearing his lovely blue caps. One day, his grandma asked him if he would like to go and get some ice-cream. He was so excited and said yes with a big smile on his face. So off they went to the ice-cream shop. James looked around and decided he wanted the one with the colourful sprinkles on top. Grandma said: "That looks lovely, let's get it!" So James got a big bowl of ice-cream with his favourite sprinkles on top. He was so happy and enjoyed eating it with Grandma. When they finished it, Grandma smiled and said: "That was just what we needed! Now let's go home and put on something warm." So, James and Grandma went home and put on some warm clothes, and then they cuddled up together on the sofa. James was so happy, he felt so loved.
