Category: Intermediate
Title: The Town and the Steel

Once upon a time, there was a big town. In the town, there was a steel factory. The factory made things from steel. One day, a boy named Tim found a big, dull steel bar. Tim wanted to play with it, but his mom did not allow him. Tim was sad. He wanted to show his friends the steel bar. He thought it would be fun to play with. But his mom said, "No, it is too heavy and not safe for you." Tim did not like this. He wanted to find a way to play with the steel bar. One day, Tim had an idea. He asked his dad to help him make the steel bar safe to play with. His dad said, "We can make it into a swing!" They worked together and made a strong swing from the steel bar. Tim's mom allowed him to play with the swing. Tim was happy and played with his friends on the swing every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy cars. One day, he found a magic wand under his bed. Tim was very excited and wanted to try it on his toys. He waved the wand at his toy car and said, "Shrink!" The car became very small. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He wanted to show his mom, so he ran upstairs. His mom was in the kitchen making a loud noise with the mixer. "Mommy, look!" Tim said. "I made my car small with the magic wand!" His mom smiled and said, "That's amazing, Tim! You are very clever." Tim was happy and played with his tiny car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to pretend he was a big, strong superhero. He wanted to save the earth from bad things. He wore a red cape and a blue mask every day. Tim was not a regular boy, he was a superhero! One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a little girl named Lucy crying. She lost her toy bunny. Tim wanted to help her. He put on his red cape and blue mask, and started to search for the toy bunny. Tim felt like a real superhero. Tim looked under the slide, behind the swings, and in the sandbox. Finally, he found the toy bunny near the big tree. Lucy was so happy to see her toy bunny again. She thanked Tim for his help. From that day on, Tim and Lucy became best friends. Tim learned that even regular boys can be superheroes when they help others.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was miserable because he could not finish his puzzle. He tried and tried, but the pieces just would not fit. Tim felt very sad. One day, a friendly dog named Max came to visit Tim. Max saw the puzzle and wanted to help. "Woof! Let's finish the puzzle together!" said Max. Tim smiled and nodded his head. They started to work on the puzzle together. With Max's help, Tim was able to finish the puzzle. As they put in the last piece, Tim and Max both cheered. Tim was not miserable anymore. He was very happy. From that day on, Tim and Max were best friends and they played together all the time.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cool woman. She had many pretty clothes. She loved to match her clothes every day. One day, the cool woman wanted to go for a walk. She put on her favorite dress and shoes that matched. She looked very pretty. She walked down the street, and everyone looked at her. They liked her matching clothes too. As she walked, she saw a big puddle. She did not want to get her pretty dress and shoes wet. But she did not see the big rock near the puddle. She tripped on the rock and fell into the puddle. Her pretty dress and matching shoes got all wet and dirty. The cool woman was very sad.
