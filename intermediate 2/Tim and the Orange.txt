Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Orange

Tim likes to play basketball with his dad. They have a big ball that is orange and black. They throw the ball to each other and try to make it go in the hoop. Tim thinks it is fun and he laughs a lot. One day, Tim and his dad go to the park to play basketball. They see a lot of other people playing too. Some of them are big and some of them are small. Some of them wear red shirts and some of them wear blue shirts. Tim and his dad find a free hoop and start to play. Tim sees a boy who is about his size. He has a green shirt and a yellow hat. He also has a basketball, but it is smaller than Tim's. The boy looks at Tim and smiles. He says, "Hi, I'm Sam. Do you want to play with me?" Tim is shy and does not know what to say. He looks at his dad, who nods and says, "Go ahead, Tim. You can make a new friend." Tim nods too and walks to Sam. He says, "Hi, I'm Tim. I like your ball." Sam says, "Thank you. I like your ball too. Do you want to share?" He gives his ball to Tim and Tim gives his ball to Sam. They start to throw the balls to each other and try to make them go in the hoop. They repeat the same words and actions over and over. They think it is fun and they laugh a lot. Tim and Sam play for a long time. They forget about the other people and the other hoops. They only see each other and their balls. They become friends and they are happy. They do not care that they are unknown to each other. They only care that they have fun.

Tim and Lily were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to run, jump and slide on the grass and the swings. They saw many birds, bugs and flowers. They were happy and curious. One day, they saw something new in the park. It was big and yellow, with black spots. It had a long tail and sharp teeth. It was a leopard! The leopard was hiding behind a tree, looking at them. Tim and Lily were scared and excited. They wanted to see the leopard better, but they also wanted to be safe. "Mom, look! A leopard!" Tim shouted, pointing at the tree. "Where?" Mom asked, confused. She did not see the leopard. She thought Tim was joking. "Behind the tree, Mom! There!" Lily said, pulling Mom's hand. Mom followed Lily's finger and saw the leopard. She was surprised and worried. She did not know how the leopard came to the park, or what it wanted. She grabbed Tim and Lily and moved away from the tree. "Come on, kids, we have to go. The leopard is not a toy. It is a wild animal. It can be dangerous," Mom said, in a serious voice. "But Mom, the leopard is so pretty. Can we pet it?" Tim asked, in a sad voice. "No, Tim, we can't. The leopard is not a pet. It belongs to the jungle, not the park. Maybe it is lost, or escaped from the zoo. We have to tell someone who can help it," Mom said, in a kind voice. "Okay, Mom. We hope the leopard finds its home," Lily said, in a sweet voice. They walked to the exit of the park, where they saw a man in a uniform. He had a big truck and a big cage. He was looking for the leopard. He was a zookeeper. "Hello, I'm looking for a leopard. Have you seen it?" the zookeeper asked, in a friendly voice. "Yes, we have. It is behind that tree. We can show you," Mom said, in a helpful voice. "Thank you, you are very brave and smart. The leopard is one of our animals. It escaped from the zoo this morning. We have been looking for it all day. We want to take it back to its friends and family," the zookeeper said, in a grateful voice. They followed the zookeeper to the tree, where the leopard was still hiding. The zookeeper opened the cage and put some meat inside. He called the leopard by its name. The leopard smelled the meat and came out of the tree. It walked slowly to the cage and ate the meat. The zookeeper closed the cage and locked it. He smiled and waved at Tim, Lily and Mom. "Thank you again for your help. You have done a good thing. The leopard will be happy to see its home again. Do you want to visit the zoo someday and see the leopard and its friends?" the zookeeper asked, in a friendly voice. "Yes, we do! We love animals. Can we, Mom?" Tim and Lily asked, in an excited voice. "Sure, we can. That sounds like a fun idea. Maybe the leopard will remember us and say hello," Mom said, in a happy voice. They said goodbye to the zookeeper and the leopard. They watched the truck drive away. They felt proud and happy. They had discovered a leopard in the park, and helped it find its way back home. They had a great adventure.
