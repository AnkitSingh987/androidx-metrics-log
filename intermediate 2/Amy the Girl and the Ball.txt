Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy lived in a small house with her mommy, her daddy, and her dog, Spot. Amy loved her daddy very much. Every day, she would play with her daddy in their yard. One sunny day, Amy and her daddy were playing with a ball. Amy's daddy was not feeling strong. He felt weak. "Amy," he said, "I need to sit down. I feel weak today." Amy looked at her daddy and wanted to help him feel better. Amy had an idea. She ran inside the house and got a big glass of water for her daddy. She gave the water to her daddy, and he drank it. After a little while, her daddy started to feel strong again. "Thank you, Amy," he said with a smile. "You helped me feel better!" Amy was happy that she could help her daddy. They played with the ball until the sun went down.

Once, there was a boy who had a case. Inside the case, he stored all sorts of special things. One day, his sister wanted to see his case. But, the boy felt ashamed, so he said no. His sister wouldn’t take no for an answer, and she started to poke him until he let her inside the case. Finally, the boy opened the case, and his sister saw all of the special things he been keeping inside. She gasped in amazement, and they both laughed. From that day on, they were even better friends and shared their secrets—without poking each other!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a black cat named Tom. Tom was a good cat who liked to follow the law. One day, Tom went for a walk and saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. "Hi Sue," said Tom. "Why are you sad?" "I lost my toy," said Sue. "Can you help me find it?" Tom and Sue looked for the toy together. They looked near the big tree and under the small bridge. They looked near the pond and in the park. At the end of the day, they found the toy under a bush. Sue was very happy. "Thank you, Tom!" she said. And they became good friends.

One day, a big, impressive dog named Max found a spade. Max wanted to move dirt with the spade. He took the spade in his mouth and went to the garden. In the garden, Max saw a little girl named Lily. "Hi, Max!" said Lily. "What are you doing with the spade?" Max wagged his tail and showed Lily that he wanted to move dirt. Lily said, "Let's move the dirt together!" Max and Lily moved the dirt and made a big pile. They had fun playing in the garden with the spade. Max was happy he found the spade and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big car. The car liked to be very organized. Every day, it would make sure all of its toys were in the right place. One day, the car went for a walk. It saw a little dog. The little dog was not organized. The dog's toys were all over the place. The car wanted to help the dog. The car showed the dog how to be organized. They put all the toys in a neat line. The dog was happy, and they both walked back home. From that day on, the dog and the car were best friends.

Lily and Ben are friends who live on a farm. They like to play with the animals in the barn. One day, they see a big truck bring a new animal to the barn. It is a horse with a long mane and a shiny coat. "Wow, look at the horse!" Lily says. "He is so pretty and big!" "Can we pet him?" Ben asks. The farmer smiles and says, "Sure, you can pet him. His name is Star. He is a fair horse. He likes to run and jump." Lily and Ben run to the barn and gently stroke Star's nose. He snorts and nuzzles them. They giggle and feed him some carrots. "Star, you are our new friend," Lily says. "We will play with you every day." The horse neighs and nods his head. He is happy to be in the barn with Lily and Ben. They are fair friends.
