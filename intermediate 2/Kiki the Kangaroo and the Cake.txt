Category: Intermediate
Title: Kiki the Kangaroo and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki was a very happy kangaroo. She loved to jump and play with her friends. One day, Kiki and her friends decided to celebrate her birthday. They had a big party with lots of yummy food. But, there was one food that Kiki thought was disgusting. It was a big, green cake. Kiki did not like the green cake at all. Her friends laughed and said, "It's just a cake, Kiki! Try it!" Kiki took a small bite of the green cake. To her surprise, it was not disgusting at all! It was sweet and tasty. Kiki and her friends laughed and danced. They had a great time at the party. And from that day on, Kiki learned that sometimes, things that look disgusting can be very yummy!

One day, a big dog and a little dog were playing in the park. The big dog was strong, and the little dog was weak. They saw a ball and both wanted to play with it. The big dog said, "I will get the ball, and you can't come." The little dog felt sad but still wanted to play. So, the little dog ran to the ball too. They both got to the ball at the same time. The big dog tried to take the ball from the little dog. But the little dog had a cut on his leg, and it hurt. The little dog said, "Please, can we play together?" The big dog saw the cut and felt bad. He said, "Yes, we can play together." And they played with the ball, happy and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She liked to spend her days playing with her toys and talking to her friends. She was a normal and happy girl. One day, Lucy had a worry. She lost her favorite toy, a small yellow duck. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. She asked her mom, her dad, and her brother, but no one knew where her duck was. Lucy's mom saw her worry and wanted to help. They looked for the duck together. Finally, they found it under Lucy's bed. Lucy was so happy to have her duck back. She gave her mom a big hug and went back to playing like a normal, happy girl.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing in the park. He felt itchy and saw a tiny flea on his fur. Max was confused and did not know what to do. He tried to shake the flea off, but it did not work. Max saw his friend, a big cat named Lily. Lily looked at Max and saw his frown. She asked, "Why are you sad, Max?" Max said, "I have a flea and I don't know how to make it go away." Lily knew just what to do. She told Max, "Let's take a bath. That will make the flea go away." Max and Lily went to the pond and splashed in the water. The flea jumped off Max and swam away. Max was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" They played in the park all day, with no more fleas to bother them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside in all the different seasons. In the summer, she would run in the sun. In the fall, she would jump in the leaves. In the winter, she would play in the snow. And in the spring, she would dance in the rain. One day, Lucy was playing outside with her cat, Mittens. They were having so much fun! But then, Lucy saw Mittens do something bad. Mittens broke a pretty flower. Lucy felt guilty because it was her job to watch Mittens. She didn't want the flower to be hurt. Lucy didn't know what to do. She looked at the broken flower and started to scream. Her mom heard her and came outside. Lucy told her mom what happened. Her mom hugged her and said it was okay. They could plant a new flower together. Lucy felt better, and from that day on, she made sure to always watch Mittens more closely.
