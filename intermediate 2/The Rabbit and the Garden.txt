Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rabbit and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a cute little rabbit. She was hopping around the garden looking for carrot. Suddenly she heard a loud noise. It was the police! She was so scared she wanted to hide. She quickly hopped away and found a nice warm spot beneath a bush. The police officer poked his head around the bush and saw the little rabbit. He said, "Hello there little one! What are you doing here?" The little rabbit was too scared to say anything. But then Spring came and the sun shone brighter. The little rabbit saw the kind police officer and felt a bit braver. She slowly stepped out from behind the bush and said, "I'm looking for carrots". The policeman smiled and said, "That sounds like a fun activity. Why don't I help you?" The little rabbit smiled. Spring had brought her a new friend! Together they looked around the garden and soon found some yummy carrots. They munched on them happily as the sun set. The policeman said, "It's getting dark, I should go now." The little rabbit thanked him and they said goodbye. She watched him go with a happy feeling in her heart. The little rabbit knew that the policeman had been kind and they will be friends forever.

One day, a modest cat went to the garden. The cat liked to play with herbs. The cat saw a big herb and wanted to squeeze it. The herb smelled nice. The cat met a dog. The dog said, "What are you doing?" The cat said, "I squeeze herbs. They smell nice." The dog wanted to try it too. The dog and the cat squeezed herbs together. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down to the garden. The bird said, "I like to squeeze herbs too!" The dog and the cat were surprised. They all played together, squeezing herbs and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big pond with his family. He loved to swim and play with his friends all day long. One day, Fin swam very fast and become tired. He needed to rest. As he rested, he felt his little lung work hard to help him breathe. Fin was very happy that he could breathe and play in the water. After resting, Fin felt hungry. He looked around for some food to eat. He found some small plants and bugs in the water. Fin ate them all up and felt better. He swam back to his family and friends, ready to play again.

Tim and Pam are friends. They like to play with toys and eat cheese. One day, they find a big box of cheese in the kitchen. They are very happy. "Look, Tim, so much cheese!" Pam says. "Let's eat some!" "Yes, Pam, let's eat some!" Tim says. "But we have to share. Sharing is good." They open the box and take some cheese. They eat and eat. The cheese is yummy. They want more and more. Soon, there is only one piece of cheese left in the box. Tim and Pam both reach for it. "Mine!" Tim says. "I saw it first!" "No, mine!" Pam says. "I want it more!" They pull and pull. The cheese breaks in half. They are both angry. "Fine!" Tim says. "You can have your half. But you are not my friend anymore!" "Fine!" Pam says. "You can have your half. But you are not my friend anymore!" They go to different corners of the kitchen. They eat their half of cheese. They are not happy. They are lonely. They are sad. They ate too much cheese. Their tummies hurt. They feel sick. They cry. They wish they had shared. They wish they had been kind. They wish they had been friends. But it is too late. The cheese is gone. And so is their friendship. The end.

Ruth was a little girl who lived with her mum. Today was a special day! Her mum was sorting all the things in the house. She opened cupboards, boxes and drawers. Ruth wanted to help. So her mum handed her a big stack of things. She said, "These are all special labels, Ruth. Please put them on each thing." Ruth took the labels and was excited. She put the labels on everything she could find. But then Ruth made a mistake. She put a label on her mum's favourite dress! Her mum was very cross. She said, "Ruth, that dress is special. You shouldn't have put a label on it." Ruth felt very sorry. Her mum smiled and said, "It's ok, Ruth. I know you were trying to help. Let's sort this out together." So Ruth helped her mum take the label off the dress and put it back in its special place. Ruth and her mum were very happy when the sorting was done. And Ruth learned an important lesson: sorting is fun, but you should be careful!
