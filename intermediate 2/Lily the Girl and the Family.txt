Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Family

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lily. She was three years old and very excited for the day. Her family was going on a trip and she could hardly wait. Mommy gathered all the luggage in the middle of the room. Lily wanted to help and she offered to sort them. She worked hard to make sure everything was organized. Each family member had their own stack of luggage. When it was time to go, everyone was ready. Lily happily grabbed her things and went out to the car. She was feeling very grateful for all the help she had given. The family drove away and Lily’s heart was filled with joy. She had done her part to get them all ready and it made her feel special. She smiled the whole way to their destination, content with how organized she had made the luggage.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved playing with her toys and running around in the park. One day, while she was playing, she saw a big, dangerous dog. Amy was scared of the dog, so she called out, "Daddy, come help me!" Daddy heard Amy and came running to her. He picked her up and hugged her tight. Daddy said, "Don't worry, Amy. I won't let the dangerous dog hurt you." He took her back home and they played together with her toys. Amy was very happy and felt safe with her daddy.

---

Ben and Sam are playing with toy guns in the park. They like to pretend they are cowboys and robbers. Ben has a red gun and Sam has a blue gun. They run and hide behind trees and rocks. "Bang, bang, bang!" Ben shouts, pointing his gun at Sam. "I got you, robber! You are in big trouble!" "No, you didn't!" Sam says, dodging behind a rock. "You missed me, cowboy! You are a bad shot!" Sam pops out from the rock and shoots back at Ben. "Bang, bang, bang!" Sam yells, aiming his gun at Ben. "I got you, cowboy! You are in big pain!" "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben cries, holding his stomach. "You hit me, robber! You hit me in my stomach! It hurts a lot!" Sam runs over to Ben and sees him lying on the ground. He feels sorry for him. He drops his gun and hugs him. "I'm sorry, Ben. I didn't mean to hurt you. It's just a game. Are you okay?" Ben stops crying and hugs Sam back. He feels better. He smiles and says, "I'm okay, Sam. It's just a game. You are a good friend." They get up and pick up their guns. They decide to play something else. They see a slide and a swing. They run to them and have fun. They forget about their rough game. They are happy.

One day, a big storm came to the town. The wind blew hard, and the rain fell down. The people in the town were scared. They needed to find a safe place to hide from the storm. A little boy named Tim saw a big tree. He thought it would be a good place to hide. "I need to tell my friends!" Tim said. He ran to his friends and told them, "Come to the big tree! We can hide from the storm there." They all went to the big tree and hid under it. The storm was loud, but they were not scared. They were lucky to have found a safe place. After the storm, the sun came out, and they all went back to play. They were happy they had each other during the scary storm.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to march around the yard and bark at the birds. One sunny day, Buddy found a big, shiny box under a tree. He thought it was a treasure! Buddy ran to his friend, the cat named Whiskers. "Whiskers, I found a treasure!" Buddy said. Whiskers came to see the treasure. They both looked at the box and wondered what was inside. Together, Buddy and Whiskers opened the box. Inside, they found fun toys and treats! They played with the toys and shared the treats. They were so happy they found the treasure. From that day on, Buddy and Whiskers loved to march around the yard, looking for more treasures to share.
