Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the House

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to build a house with bricks. He asked his mom if he could buy some bricks. His mom said yes, and they went to the store. At the store, Tim saw a helpless little bug on the ground. He picked it up and put it outside. Then, Tim and his mom bought the bricks they needed. They went home to start building the house. Tim and his mom worked together to build the house with the bricks. The helpless little bug came back to watch them. When the house was done, the bug had a new home too. Tim was happy that he could help the bug and build a house with his mom.

One day, a cat named Sam was very restless. He wanted to go outside and play. He saw a big tree and wanted to stretch his legs to climb it. Sam jumped and climbed the tree. He felt very happy up there. While Sam was in the tree, he saw a cooler on the ground. He was curious about what was inside the cooler. Sam climbed down the tree and went to the cooler. He opened it and found many yummy treats. Sam was very excited and started to eat the treats. Then, something unexpected happened. The cooler started to shake and move. It was not a cooler, but a big turtle! The turtle was just sleeping and Sam didn't know. The turtle woke up and said, "Hello, I am Tim the Turtle. You can have some of my treats, but let's share them!" Sam and Tim became good friends and they shared the treats together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was always very nice to everyone. One day, Tim saw a new girl named Lily. She looked sad because she had no friends. Tim wanted to help her. Tim went to Lily and said, "Hi! My name is Tim. Do you want to take my hand and play with me?" Lily looked at Tim and said, "Yes, thank you! I would like that." They took each other's hands and went to play with the other kids. Together, they played with the toys that were available. Tim showed Lily how to build a tower with the blocks. Lily was very happy. She said, "Tim, you are so kind. Thank you for the warm welcome." Tim smiled and said, "You are welcome, Lily. I am happy to be your friend." And from that day on, Tim and Lily were the best of friends.

One day, a chubby cat named Tom found a computer. Tom wanted to make something fun with the computer. He thought and thought, and then he had a big idea. He wanted to make a game. Tom worked hard to make the game on the computer. He made a game with a ball and a bat. The game was very fun to play. Tom played the game and laughed. Tom showed the game to his friends. They all played the game and had a fun time. The chubby cat was happy that he made a fun game on the computer for everyone to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big drain near his house. He wanted to see what was inside. Tim got close to the drain, but his mom said, "No, Tim! That drain is dangerous. You can't go near it." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. He didn't want to get hurt. Later, Tim saw a bird near the drain. The bird didn't know it was dangerous. Tim talked to the bird and said, "Bird, don't go near the drain. It's dangerous!" The bird listened to Tim and flew away. Tim was happy he could help the bird stay safe.

In the open field, a huge tree stood tall and strong. Just beyond its roots was a large pile of rocks. The rocks looked old and tired, but they caught the attention of a little boy. He wondered if the rocks had a secret to tell. But, before the boy could investigate further, his mom quickly took his hand and said, "Come on, it's getting late." With a nod, he followed her home. The next morning, the boy woke up excited. He went to the same open field and saw the same huge tree, but the large pile of rocks was gone! The boy was very curious and wondered what had happened to them. Then, he saw a big hole in the ground. His heart raced. He quickly ran back to his mom to tell her what he had found. With great enthusiasm, the boy shared his discovery with his mom. She nodded and led the boy back to the open field. Gently, she pointed to the huge hole in the ground. "Do you want to know what happened?" his mom asked. The little boy nodded. She smiled and said, "Those rocks were hiding a secret - a treasure! We can use the money to get whatever you want!" At that moment, the little boy realized the rocks were not so tired after all. They were actually hiding a huge surprise!
