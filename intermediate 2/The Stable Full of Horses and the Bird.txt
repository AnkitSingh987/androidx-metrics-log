Category: Intermediate
Title: The Stable Full of Horses and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a stable full of horses. The horses were all happy living together. But one day, a storm came and caused one of the stable walls to collapse. All the horses curiously looked at the broken wall and one of them, called Daisy, stuck her head out. Suddenly, Daisy saw a fragile bird hanging from a tree branch nearby. The little bird was chirping sadly and Daisy knew that she had to help it. Daisy reached out her neck and stretched it as far as she could. She tried to grab the bird but it was too far away. Daisy looked around and noticed a long rope hanging from a hook. She grabbed it in her teeth and managed to pull the bird closer. Daisy then grabbed the bird in her mouth, pushed it gently with her nose, and brought it back inside the stable. Everyone in the stable cheered when they saw Daisy bringing the bird back safe and sound! The bird thanked Daisy with a soft chirp and Daisy smiled. Daisy had saved the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a happy girl who loved to play with her toys. One day, Lily found a pretty bottle of perfume in her mommy's room. She thought it would be fun to play with it. Lily took the perfume to her room and started to spray it everywhere. It smelled nice, but she used too much. She felt foolish for making her room smell so strong. Her mommy came in and said, "Lily, you shouldn't play with perfume. It's not a toy." Lily felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, mommy. I won't do it again." Her mommy hugged her and said, "It's okay. Let's lock the perfume away so you won't be tempted to play with it again." They put the perfume in a special box and locked it. From that day on, Lily never played with her mommy's perfume again. She learned her lesson and played with her toys instead. And her room smelled nice and fresh, just like a little girl's room should.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in the big ocean with his family. They all liked to swim and play together. One day, Fin's mom said, "Fin, stay close and be safe." Fin and his family started to swim to a new place. As they swam, Fin saw a big, shiny thing. He wanted to see it closer. Fin swam away from his family to the shiny thing. He did not feel safe, but he was curious. The shiny thing was a big, friendly whale. The whale said, "Hello, little fish! Are you lost?" Fin said, "Yes, I am. Can you help me find my family?" The whale smiled and said, "Of course, let's go find them!" They swam together, and soon Fin was back with his family. They were all happy and safe again.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the farm. She saw a big purple cow. Sue had never seen a purple cow before. She knew something fun was going to happen. Sue walked up to the cow and said, "Hi, cow! Why are you purple?" The cow smiled and said, "I ate some magic grass. It made me purple and gave me a special gift. I can make yummy ice cream now!" Sue was very happy to hear this. The cow made some ice cream for Sue. Sue ate the ice cream and said, "Thank you, cow! This is the best ice cream I have ever had!" The cow was happy that Sue liked the ice cream. From that day on, Sue and the purple cow became good friends, and they shared many more ice cream treats together.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big box in his room. It was open. He was very happy. He looked inside the box and saw a new toy car. He loved the toy car and played with it all day. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the toy car and was jealous. He said, "I want a toy car too!" Tim did not like that. They both started to weep. Just then, Tim's mom came in and said, "Look, boys! I have another open box!" Inside the box, there was another toy car for Sam. Now, both boys were happy and stopped weeping. They played together with their new toy cars and were best friends again.
