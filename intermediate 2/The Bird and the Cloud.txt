Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. She was so excited because she wanted to open her wings and fly. But when she tried, she kept falling down. She tried and tried but she couldn't get into the air. One day, a wise old bird saw her struggling. He said, "You have to believe in yourself, little bird. As long as you think you can do it, you will be able to fly." The little bird was determined to do it. She opened her wings, took a deep breath, and jumped. She flapped her wings very hard and finally, she was flying. But suddenly, a stupid cloud came between her and the sun. The little bird couldn't find her way and she was confused. However, she didn't give up. She kept focusing on the sun and soon she was out of the cloud. The little bird flew high into the sky. She was so happy that she had been able to do it. She knew she wasn't stupid, she had just needed to believe in herself to make it happen.

The little boy was scared. He had to go to a meeting with his mom and dad. His mom and dad looked troubled, so the little boy was really scared. He was not sure why, but he knew it would be something bad. At the meeting, everyone talked, but the little boy could not understand them. His mom and dad were still troubled and they looked worried. There was an old man who spoke in a loud voice. He asked the little boy lots of questions, and that scared him even more. The little boy was very glad when the meeting was over. He hugged his mom and dad and told them he loved them. His mom and dad held him tight and said everything was going to be alright. That made the little boy feel better, but he was still scared.

Ben and Mia were at the beach with their mom and dad. They liked to play in the sand and look for shells and rocks. Sometimes they saw crabs and fish in the water. They had a lot of fun. One day, they found a big shell that was closed. Ben said, "What is this? Maybe there is a treasure inside." Mia said, "Let's open it and see." They tried to pull the shell apart, but it was too hard. They asked their mom and dad for help. Their dad said, "This is an oyster. It is a kind of animal that lives in the shell. Sometimes there is a pearl inside, which is a shiny ball. But not always. Do you want to try to open it?" Ben and Mia nodded. Their dad used a knife to pry the shell open. Inside, there was a soft and slimy thing. It was the oyster. Ben and Mia looked at it and made faces. They did not think it looked like a treasure. They did not want to touch it or eat it. Their mom said, "Some people like to eat oysters. They say they taste good. They are mild, which means they are not spicy or sour. Do you want to try a bite?" Ben and Mia shook their heads. They did not want to try the oyster. They gave the oyster to their dad, who ate it with a smile. He said, "Mmm, delicious. Thank you for finding it for me." Ben and Mia smiled too. They were happy that their dad liked the oyster. They went back to playing in the sand and looking for other things. They yawned, which means they were tired. They had a good day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a brown flower. The brown flower lived in a big garden with many other flowers. The brown flower wanted to be like the other flowers. The other flowers were red, blue, and yellow. They were very pretty. One day, the brown flower saw a little girl. The little girl was playing near the flowers. The brown flower wanted to do something nice for her. So, the brown flower tried very hard to change its color. The little girl saw the brown flower trying to change. She knew what to do. She went to get some water. She gave the water to the brown flower. The brown flower drank the water. The brown flower started to change. It turned red, blue, and yellow, just like the other flowers. The little girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you, brown flower, for being so nice. You are now very pretty too." The brown flower was very happy. It was now just like the other flowers. The brown flower and the little girl became good friends. They played in the garden every day.
