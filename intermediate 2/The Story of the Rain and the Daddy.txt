Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Rain and the Daddy

Once upon a time, there was a big, enormous house. In the house lived a family of four. There was a mommy, a daddy, a little girl, and a little boy. One day, it started to rain. The sky was filled with droplets of water and the little girl was not happy. She said to her parents, “I don’t want it to rain! I can't go play outside.” Her mommy said softly, “Don’t worry my dear. I'm sure the rain will stop soon”. Then her daddy said, “Why don't you put on your raincoat and collar. You can still go out and play.” The little girl thought about it for a moment and decided that her daddy was right. So she put on her yellow raincoat and her big red collar. Her parents said goodbye and watched her dance in the rain. She was so happy. The raindrops sprayed around her and she spun around in circles. She felt so free and enormous. When the rain stopped, she ran inside and told her parents it was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a wild cat named Tim. Tim lived near a big temple. One day, Tim met a small mouse named Max. Max was very sad. Tim asked Max, "Why are you sad?" Max said, "I lost my way. I can't find my home." Tim wanted to help Max, so they went to the temple to look for Max's home. They walked and walked. But they could not find Max's home. They saw a big door at the temple. Tim said, "Let's part the door and look inside." Max agreed. Inside the temple, they saw a big dog. The dog was very mean. The dog chased Tim and Max. They ran and ran, but the dog caught them. In the end, Tim and Max could not find Max's home. They were very sad. The big dog scared them, and they could not go back to the temple.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful planet called Earth. On this planet, there lived a very attractive girl called Lola. She enjoyed exploring the world around her. One day, Lola saw a shiny red rocket in a nearby field. She couldn't take her eyes off of it. She had to find out where it came from. So Lola knelt down to take a closer look. Suddenly, a voice from above said, "What do you think you're doing?" Lola looked up and saw an old man looking down at her. The old man said, "That rocket belongs to me. You can't just take it!" Lola was surprised and scared, but she told the old man that she just wanted to take a look. The old man relaxed and said, "That's alright. Just don't touch it. I'm sure if you ask nicely, I'll let you fly it someday." Lola was very happy. She thanked the old man and stood up, admiring the rocket.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was a very independent girl. She loved to do things all by herself. She had a soft, red velvet dress that she liked to wear. One sunny day, Amy went outside to gather flowers. She walked around her yard, picking pretty flowers of all colors. She filled her little basket with the flowers she found. Amy was very happy. Amy brought the flowers inside and showed her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Amy! You gathered these flowers all by yourself!" Amy felt proud and knew that she could do many things on her own.

One day, a little crab went for a walk by the sea. The crab saw a big box. The box was unknown to the crab. The crab wanted to unlock the box. It used its claws to open the box. Inside the box, the crab found a small fish. The fish said, "Hello, crab! Thank you for unlocking the box. I was stuck in there." The crab said, "You're welcome, fish! Let's be friends." The crab and the fish played by the sea. They had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The box started to shake. They went to look at the box. A big, friendly octopus came out of the box. The octopus said, "Thank you for unlocking the box! Now we can all be friends." The crab, the fish, and the octopus played together and had a great day.
