Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a fragile toy. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim's toy broke. He felt very sad. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can you help me make my toy better?" His mom looked at the broken toy and said, "Yes, Tim. We can make it better together." So, Tim and his mom sat down and fixed the toy. Tim was very happy. Later, Tim felt hungry. He told his mom, "Mom, my stomach is hungry. Can we make a snack?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. Let's make a yummy snack together." They went to the kitchen and made a delicious snack. Tim ate it and felt happy. From that day on, Tim learned that he could make things better and have fun with his mom. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, Mandy was polishing the windows in her house. She was so proud of her work but then all of a sudden, she heard loud noises outside. It was the police! Mandy ran out of her house to see what was going on. The police were being very playful. They were throwing a Frisbee around and chasing each other. Mandy laughed at how funny they were and hoped they would stay for a bit. But then one of the police officers noticed her and said, "Hey little girl, don't you have some windows to polish?" Mandy nodded and went back inside. She grabbed the polishing cloth and started cleaning the windows. After a while, she looked outside and saw the police were still being very playful. She smiled and waved goodbye to them. Mandy was very happy that she had been able to finish polishing her windows and that the police had been so playful. She knew that her work was done and she could enjoy the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a very smart cat named Bob. Bob loved to wear a big bow on his neck. One day, Bob went for a walk in the park. In the park, he saw a little dog named Tim. Bob wanted to make friends with Tim. So, he said, "Hello, I am Bob. Let me introduce myself. I am a smart cat with a big bow." Tim looked at the bow and said, "I like your bow, but I don't like cats." Bob was sad. He wanted to show Tim that cats and dogs can be friends. Bob had an idea. He found a ball and said, "Let's play with the ball together." Tim thought it was a good idea. They played with the ball and had a lot of fun. Tim said, "You are an intelligent cat, Bob. I am sorry I was not nice before." Bob was happy. Now, Bob and Tim are best friends, and they play together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy sack. It was full of many colors of thread. The sack lived in a small house with a girl named Lily and her mom. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, can you help me with the heavy sack?" Lily said, "Yes, Mom!" She tried to lift the sack, but it was too heavy for her. Lily felt sad. Then, a big wind came and blew the heavy sack away. The threads went everywhere! Lily and her mom looked for the sack, but they could not find it. They were very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The big red ball had a life full of fun. It loved to roll and bounce all day long. One day, the big red ball met a small blue ball. The big red ball said, "Hi, I want to introduce myself. I am Big Red." The small blue ball said, "Hello, I am Little Blue. Let's play together." So, Big Red and Little Blue played all day. They rolled and bounced around the park. They had so much fun together. But then, a big wind came. The wind blew Big Red and Little Blue far away. They tried to stay close, but the wind was too strong. Big Red and Little Blue got lost. They could not find each other anymore. They both felt very sad. The big red ball's life was not fun anymore without Little Blue.
