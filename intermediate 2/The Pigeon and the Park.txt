Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pigeon and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a pigeon. The pigeon lived in a park. The park had many trees and a big pond. The pigeon liked to play with his friends. One day, the pigeon saw a big, harsh storm coming. He started to panic. He was scared of the storm. He told his friends, "We need to find a safe place!" The pigeon and his friends flew to a big tree. They hid under the tree. The storm came and went. The pigeon and his friends were safe. They were happy they found a safe place.

---

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Buddy. Buddy was a pet who loved to play with his boy, Tim. They had lots of fun in the park, running and laughing. One day, Tim and Buddy met a cat. The cat was not nice. It hissed at Buddy and Tim. Tim's mom warned them, "Be careful, the cat might scratch you." Tim said to Buddy, "We should go play somewhere else." Buddy barked in agreement. They ran away from the cat to play on the swings. Buddy and Tim were happy and safe. They had a great day at the park.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily went into the garden. She saw a vine. The vine was long and green. Lily wanted to play with the vine. She asked the vine, "Can you become a fun toy for me?" The vine heard Lily and wanted to be obedient. So, the vine became a jump rope. Lily was so happy. She jumped and played with the vine all day. They had lots of fun together. But then, something unexpected happened. The vine turned into a talking cat! The cat said, "Thank you for playing with me, Lily. I was a vine, but your fun made me become a cat again." Lily and the cat became best friends and played together every day.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a walk. He wanted to discover new things. He walked and walked until he found a big box. The box was huge! Tim was very excited to see what was inside. He looked around and found some scissors. Tim used the scissors to open the big box. He was very careful not to hurt himself. Inside the box, he found many colorful toys. There were cars, dolls, and balls. Tim played with the toys all day. He had so much fun! He was happy that he discovered the huge box with the scissors. Later, he showed his friends the toys and they all played together.

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big truck. The truck has many lollipops. The lollipops are different colors and shapes. Tim and Mia want a lollipop. They run to the truck and ask the man. The man is nice. He says, "You can have one lollipop each. But you have to do something for me. You have to tell me a joke. If I laugh, you get a lollipop. If I don't laugh, you get nothing." Tim and Mia think hard. They want to make the man laugh. Tim says, "Why did the chicken cross the road?" The man says, "I don't know. Why?" Tim says, "To get to the other side." The man does not laugh. He says, "That is an old joke. I have heard it many times. No lollipop for you." Mia tries next. She says, "What do you call a fish with no eyes?" The man says, "I don't know. What?" Mia says, "Fsh." The man laughs. He says, "That is a funny joke. I have not heard it before. You get a lollipop." The man gives Mia a lollipop. It is pink and round. It looks like a flower. Mia is happy. She says, "Thank you, mister. This is a pretty lollipop." The man says, "You're welcome, little girl. Enjoy your lollipop." Tim is sad. He has no lollipop. He says, "Can I have another chance, mister? Please?" The man says, "OK, I will give you another chance. But you have to tell me a better joke. A joke that makes me laugh a lot." Tim thinks harder. He wants to make the man laugh a lot. He says, "What do you get when you cross a cow and a duck?" The man says, "I don't know. What?" Tim says, "Milk and quackers." The man laughs a lot. He says, "That is a very funny joke. I have never heard it before. You get a lollipop." The man gives Tim a lollipop. It is green and long. It looks like a snake. Tim is happy. He says, "Thank you, mister. This is a cool lollipop." The man says, "You're welcome, little boy. Enjoy your lollipop." Tim and Mia are happy. They have lollipops. They say, "Bye, mister. Thank you for the lollipops." The man says, "Bye, kids. Thank you for the jokes. Have a nice day." Tim and Mia go to the swings. They eat their lollipops. They share their jokes. They have fun. They are friends. They like lollipops. They are not ordinary. They are special.
