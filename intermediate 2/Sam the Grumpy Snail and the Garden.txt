Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Grumpy Snail and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy snail named Sam. Sam was always sad because he was slow. All the other bugs in the garden would win races, but not Sam. Sam wanted to win a race too, but he didn't know how. One day, Sam met a wise old ant who told him a secret. The ant said, "If you want to win a race, you must be kind and help others. Then, they will help you too." Sam thought about what the ant said and decided to try it. He started to help his friends by sharing his salt with them. Salt was very special to the bugs because it made their food taste better. Soon, all the bugs in the garden liked Sam and wanted to help him too. They had a big race, and all of Sam's friends helped him go faster. In the end, Sam won the race! He was very happy and learned that being kind and helping others is the best way to win.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big lake near his house. The man loved to go to the lake and look at the water. One day, the man went to the lake and saw a big fish. The fish was very special. It could talk! The fish said, "If you let me go, I will make something disappear for you." The man was very surprised, but he said, "Okay, fish. Make my big house disappear." The fish splashed its tail and the man's house disappeared! But then, something unexpected happened. The man's house was gone, but so was all his money! The man was not wealthy anymore. He was sad, but he learned that there are more important things than being wealthy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sophie. One day she set off to explore the woods near her home. As she walked, she kicked at the ash on the floor. She had never seen ash before! As she continued to walk she heard a noise. It sounded like a little voice. She peered around a tree and saw an old lady. The lady was very small and wrinkly. The old lady spoke to Sophie. "Why have you come to the woods?" she asked. Sophie told the old lady that she was exploring. The old lady then told Sophie a story. "Once upon a time, there was a foolish knight who set off to find a dragon. He was sure he could catch it and make it his pet. But what he didn't know was that dragons can't be caught." The old lady, who had once been the foolish knight, then smiled at Sophie. "Always remember to not be foolish like me," she said, "because you never know the consequences." Sophie saw the wisdom in the old lady's words and thanked her, before heading back home.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat. The cat lived in a small house with a nice porch. The cat liked to sit on the porch and watch the birds fly by. Every day, the cat would see a little bird with a sign. The cat did not know what the sign said. One day, the cat saw the little bird with the sign again. The cat was very curious about the sign. So, the cat walked up to the bird and asked, "What does your sign say?" The bird looked at the cat and said, "It says that there is a big party today!" The cat was very excited about the party. The cat and the bird went to the party together. They had lots of fun, ate yummy food, and made new friends. At the end of the party, the cat was very happy that it had asked about the sign. And the cat and the bird became best friends forever.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play with puzzles. Sara has a big puzzle with many pieces. Tom has a small puzzle with few pieces. Sara thinks her puzzle is better than Tom's puzzle. One day, Sara and Tom are playing with their puzzles in Sara's room. Sara says to Tom, "Look at my puzzle. It is big and pretty. Your puzzle is small and ugly. You are not good at puzzles." Tom feels sad. He says to Sara, "That is not nice. I like my puzzle. It is not ugly. It is fun. You are not a good friend." Sara does not care. She smiles and says, "I don't need you. I have my puzzle. Go away." Tom goes away. He cries. He does not want to play with Sara anymore. Sara finishes her puzzle. She is happy. She puts her puzzle on the shelf. She goes to get a snack. But then, something bad happens. The shelf is old and thin. It cannot hold the weight of the puzzle. The shelf breaks. The puzzle falls. The pieces scatter on the floor. Some pieces break. Some pieces get lost. Sara comes back. She sees her puzzle. She is shocked. She is angry. She is sad. She has no puzzle. She has no friend. She cries. She does not smile.
