Title: Tim the Boy and the Horn

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big horn that he loved to play. He would play his horn all day long, making loud sounds. One day, he saw a friendly man waving at him from across the street. The man was very polite and kind. Tim wanted to be polite too, so he stopped playing his horn and waved back at the man. The man smiled and walked over to Tim. He said, "Thank you for being polite and not playing your horn when I waved. It is nice to meet someone who knows when to be quiet." From that day on, Tim learned to play his horn only when it was the right time. He would play it at home, but not when he saw someone waving or when people were talking. He became known as the polite horn player in the town. Everyone in the town liked Tim because he was so polite. They would wave at him and he would wave back, always with a smile. Tim's horn playing made people happy, but his politeness made them love him even more. The moral of the story is: it is important to be polite and think about others' feelings.

Once upon a time, a little green frog named Fred lived in a small pond. Fred loved to travel and see more of life. One sunny day, he decided to hop away from his pond and go on a big trip. Fred hopped and hopped until he found a big green tree. He saw a squirrel high in the tree. The squirrel said, "Hi, Fred! What are you doing?" Fred said, "I am traveling to see more of life!" The squirrel smiled and wished him good luck. As Fred hopped away, he met a big green turtle. The turtle said, "Hello, Fred! Where are you going?" Fred said, "I am traveling to see more of life!" The turtle smiled and said, "I like to travel too. Let's be friends!" And so, Fred and the turtle traveled together, seeing more of life and making more friends along the way.

One day, a boy named Mark went to play at the park. There, he found a toy called a laser. The laser could make pretty lights on the ground. Mark was very happy to find the toy. Mark saw a dog at the park. The dog was deaf, so it could not hear well. Mark wanted to play with the dog. He pointed the laser at the ground and the dog saw the pretty light. "Come, dog! Let's play!" said Mark. The dog chased the light and they played together. But then, something unexpected happened. The laser light went up into a tree. The dog tried to get the light, but it could not climb the tree. Mark had an idea. He climbed the tree and got the light. The dog was happy and they played some more.

Once upon a time, there was a big round ball. The ball was very troubled. It was sad because it was locked in a box. The ball wanted to be free and play with the kids outside. One day, a little girl named Amy found the box. She saw the lock and wanted to help. Amy looked for a key to unlock the box. She looked high and low, but she could not find the key. Finally, Amy found the key under a rock. She was so happy! She used the key to unlock the box. The round ball was free! The ball was not troubled anymore. Amy and the ball played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a wealthy man. He had a pretty vase. He loved his vase very much. It was red and blue, and he put it on a table. One day, a little boy came to visit. The boy saw the vase and wanted to play with it. The wealthy man told the boy to remember not to touch the vase. The boy said, "Okay, I will remember." But the boy did not remember. He played with the vase and it fell. The vase broke into many pieces. The wealthy man was very sad. The boy was sad too. They could not fix the vase. The end.
