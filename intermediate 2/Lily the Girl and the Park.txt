Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a toy sword that she loved to play with. One day, she went to the park with her friend, Tom. At the park, they saw a big tree. Lily said, "Let's climb the tree!" Tom agreed, and they both started to climb. Lily used her toy sword to help her reach the high branches. She stretched her arm to grab the branches, and her toy sword was flexible, so it bent a little bit. As they climbed higher, they heard a voice from above. "Hello, little ones!" it said. They looked up and saw a big bird sitting on a branch. The bird had a shiny key in its beak. "I found this key," said the bird, "but I don't know what it opens. Can you help me?" Lily and Tom agreed to help the bird. They took the key and climbed back down the tree. They searched the park and found a small door hidden in the bushes. They used the key to open the door, and inside, they found a room full of toys! The bird had led them to a secret playroom. Lily and Tom were so happy, and they played there all day with their new friend, the bird.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a little girl named Lisa. She loved to go on walks with her dog, Spot. They liked to look at the pretty flowers and feel the warm sun on their faces. One day, while they were walking, they saw a big blanket spread on the grass. Lisa and Spot went to see who it belonged to. As they got closer, they saw a big cat named Whiskers. Whiskers was not happy to see them. He said, "This is my blanket! You can't be here!" Lisa and Spot were sad, but they didn't want to fight with Whiskers. So, they went to find another place to play. Just when they were about to leave, a big wind came and blew the blanket away! Whiskers was very sad and scared. Lisa and Spot were alert and knew they had to help. They all ran together to catch the blanket. With their help, Whiskers got his blanket back. Whiskers learned that it was more fun to share and play together. From that day on, Lisa, Spot, and Whiskers became the best of friends and enjoyed many walks and fun times at the park.

One day, a boy and his mom went to the museum. The boy felt proud because he was a big boy now. He could see big and fun things in the museum. The mom said, "We have to wait for dad. He is delayed at work." While they waited, the boy saw a big red button. He wanted to push it. His mom said, "No! Don't push the button." But the boy did not listen. He pushed the button. Boom! The museum lights went off. Everyone was sad and scared. The boy felt bad for not listening to his mom. The moral is to always listen to your mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lucy. Lucy loved to support her friends. One day, she went to a place called University. University was where big people went to learn new things. At University, Lucy met a little bird named Billy. Billy was sad because he could not fly. Lucy wanted to help him. She said, "Let's be friends, and I will support you, Billy!" Billy smiled and said, "Thank you, Lucy!" Together, they tried many things to help Billy fly. Then, something unexpected happened. Lucy found a big balloon. She tied it to Billy, and he started to float up in the air. Billy was so happy, he could finally fly! Lucy and Billy were the best of friends and had lots of fun at University.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside with her friends. They played a fun sport called "tag." Lucy liked to run fast and laugh with her friends. One day, Lucy found a big, rough ball. She wanted to play with it, but it was locked in a box. Lucy said to her friend Tom, "How can we unlock the box to get the ball?" Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's ask my mom for help!" They went to Tom's mom, and she helped them unlock the box. Now, they could play with the rough ball. Lucy and Tom played with the ball all day. They had so much fun with their new sport. They laughed and played until the sun went down. And they knew they would always remember the day they found the rough ball.
