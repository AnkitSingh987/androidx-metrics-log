Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Dog and the Bird

One day, a kind dog named Ben saw a little bird on the ground. The bird could not fly. Ben wanted to help the bird go home. Ben said, "I will try to help you." The bird was happy. Ben put the bird on his back and they walked to find the bird's home. They saw a big tree. The bird said, "That is my home!" Ben tried to reach the tree but he was too short. A tall giraffe saw them and helped the bird go home. Ben and the bird said, "Thank you!"

Once upon a time there was a humble shrimp. He wanted to perform a special show. So he practiced and practiced every day. He sang and danced and jumped around. One day, he felt ready and he was excited. He performed a wonderful show in front of a big crowd. Everyone cheered and clapped. He was so proud of himself! Afterwards, he went home and told all his friends about his show. They were so happy for him. He was glad that he had followed his dream and performed.

One day, a playful cat named Tom found a costume. The costume looked like a big dog. Tom put on the costume and walked around. He met his friend, a little bird named Sue. "Look at me, Sue!" said Tom. "I am a big dog! Woof, woof!" Sue laughed and played with Tom. They had lots of fun together. Then, something unexpected happened. The costume vanished! Tom was a cat again. Sue was surprised but happy. "You are still my friend, even if you are not a dog," said Sue. They laughed and played together all day.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a nervous gorilla named Gogo. Gogo was always scared to make new friends. One sunny day, while Gogo was playing with his favorite red ball, he saw a little bunny hopping around. Gogo wanted to talk to the bunny, but he was too nervous. He took a deep breath and said, "Hi, I am Gogo. Do you want to play with me?" The little bunny stopped and looked at Gogo. She nodded her head and said, "Yes, I am Binky. I want to play with you." Gogo was so happy that he wasn't nervous anymore. They played with the red ball all day long. They laughed and had so much fun. When it was time for Binky to go home, Gogo said, "Bye, Binky! Let's play again tomorrow." Binky nodded and said, "Yes, Gogo! I will come back tomorrow to play with you." And from that day on, Gogo and Binky were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in his backyard. One day, he saw a big ball in the backyard. He wanted to play with it, but he needed to be careful. Tim tried to be careful, but he kicked the ball too hard. The ball went flying and hit a big tree. The tree shook, and a big branch fell down. Tim was scared. Tim ran to his mom and told her what happened. His mom was not happy. She said, "You needed to be more careful, Tim." The backyard was not safe to play in anymore, and Tim was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim was a messy mouse. He liked to play in the mud and make a big mess. One day, Tim saw a big trap with some cheese in it. Tim wanted the cheese, but he knew the trap was not safe. He had an idea. He would become a brave mouse and find a way to get the cheese without getting caught. Tim thought hard and came up with a plan. Tim rolled in the mud until he was very messy. Then, he went to the trap and pushed the cheese with his muddy nose. The cheese slid out of the trap, and Tim was safe. He ate the cheese and was happy. From that day, Tim knew he could be brave and smart, even if he was a little messy.
