Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lucy was feeling adventurous. She decided that on Tuesday, she would go on a big journey to the park. She told her friend, Tom, about her plan. Tom was excited too. At the park, Lucy and Tom saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play, but Lucy was scared. "I don't want to play with the dog," said Lucy. Tom was brave and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." Then, something unexpected happened. The big dog started to sing and dance! Lucy and Tom were very surprised. They laughed and clapped their hands. The big dog was not scary after all. They all played together and had a fun Tuesday.

Sara liked to read books. She had many books with pictures and words. She read them every day. She read them to her toys and her cat. She read them to her mom and dad. She read them to herself. One day, Sara went to the farm with her class. She saw many animals. She saw cows and pigs and chickens. She saw horses and sheep and ducks. And she saw a goat. The goat was white and brown. He had horns and a beard. He looked charming. Sara wanted to read to the goat. She took her book from her backpack. She walked to the goat. She said, "Hello, goat. Do you want to hear a story?" The goat said, "Baa." Sara thought that meant yes. She opened her book and started to read. But the goat was not interested in the story. He was interested in the book. He sniffed the book. He licked the book. He bit the book. He tore the book. Sara was shocked. She said, "No, goat! Stop! That is my book! You are ruining it!" The goat said, "Baa." He did not stop. Sara cried. She ran to her teacher. She said, "Teacher, teacher, the goat ate my book! He was very bad!" The teacher said, "Oh, Sara, I'm sorry. The goat did not know. He thought it was food. He likes to eat paper. Come, I have another book for you. It is the same as yours. You can read it to me." Sara said, "Thank you, teacher. You are very kind. But I don't want to read to the goat anymore. He is not charming. He is naughty." The teacher said, "I understand. Maybe next time you can read to another animal. How about the duck? He is very charming." Sara said, "Maybe. But I think I like the cat better. He is very charming. And he does not eat books." The teacher said, "That is true. The cat is very charming. And he likes to listen to stories. Let's go and find him." Sara said, "Yes, let's go." And they went.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a very good dog. He loved his owner, Jane. Jane was not nice. She was terrible to Spot. Spot did not like Jane. One day, Jane said she would marry a man named Bob. Spot did not know Bob. Spot was sad. He wanted a good owner. Spot had an idea. He would meet Bob and see if he was nice. Spot met Bob and they played. Bob was very nice. Spot liked Bob. Then, something unexpected happened. Bob talked to Jane. He told her to be nice to Spot. Jane listened to Bob. She became a good owner. Spot was happy. Jane and Bob got married and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big mule named Max. Max lived on a farm with many animals. One day, Max saw a little bunny. Max said, "Hi, bunny! Let's play!" The bunny said, "Okay, Max! Let's spring and jump!" So, Max and the bunny played together. They jumped high and low. They had lots of fun. In the end, Max and the bunny became good friends. They played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Bill and Bob. Bill was a lazy egg. He preferred to take naps instead of playing with Bob. One day, Bob had an idea. He wanted to introduce Bill to something new! He brought out a special game and said, "Hey Bill, let's play this game together!" But Bill refused. So Bob found a way to make Bill want to play. He told Bill that if he played with him, he would get a special reward - a delicious egg! Bill was so excited, he agreed to play the game! The two were having such a great time, until Bill got lazy. Bob had to introduce a new rule to the game to keep Bill motivated. He told Bill that if he wanted to win the egg, he had to work harder. Finally, after an hour of playing, Bill worked hard enough to win the egg. He was so happy and thanked Bob for introducing him to the game. They both enjoyed the reward and became best friends forever.
