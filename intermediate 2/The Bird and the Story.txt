Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird wanted to fly high in the sky, but it was too scared. Every day, for weeks and months, the bird tried and tried to fly up high. But each time it failed. One day, as the little bird was trying to fly, another bird flew by. “You can do it!” said the other bird. The little bird said, “But I am too scared. I cannot do it.” The other bird said, “Just believe in yourself and have faith and there will be victory. Work hard, never give up and you will succeed.” From that day on, even though it was hard and scary, the little bird worked hard, never giving up. And when it tried to fly again, the little bird achieved its goal. The result was beautiful - the sky was no longer filthy, and the little bird rose to new heights! It was a victory indeed! Moral of the story: Believe in yourself, work hard and never give up. Victory is possible no matter how hard the obstacles.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange boat. The boat liked to relax in the harbor. The harbor was a fun place for the boat to rest and play with its boat friends. One day, the orange boat saw a little fish. The fish was sad because it was lost. The orange boat wanted to help the little fish find its family. So, the boat and the fish went on a fun trip around the harbor. In the end, they found the little fish's family. The orange boat was happy to help. The fish family thanked the boat, and they all played together. After a fun day, the orange boat went back to relax in the harbor.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a swing. Tim loved to swing high in the air. He felt like he had a lot of power when he was on the swing. As Tim was swinging, he saw a little bird in the tree. The bird looked troubled. It was trying to fly, but it couldn't. Tim stopped swinging and went to help the bird. He asked his mom to help him reach the bird. Just as Tim and his mom were about to help the bird, something unexpected happened. The bird jumped off the tree and landed on the ground. But it didn't get hurt! The bird started to hop around and play. It was not troubled anymore. Tim and his mom were happy to see the bird was okay. They had fun playing with the bird in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, while he was playing with his car, he saw a wire on the floor. The wire was split and looked dangerous. Tim knew he had to make it safe. Tim went to his dad and told him about the split wire. His dad said, "Good job, Tim! We need to make it safe so no one gets hurt." They went to the room with the wire and Tim's dad picked it up. He took some tape and fixed the split wire, making it safe to touch. Now that the wire was safe, Tim could play with his toy car again. He was very happy and proud that he had helped keep his family safe. Tim's dad gave him a big hug and told him he did a great job. From that day on, Tim always made sure things were safe in his house.

Once upon a time, in a big open field, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his friends. One day, while he was playing, Spot heard a sound. It was a soft sound, like a whisper. Spot was full of wonder, and he wanted to find where the sound came from. Spot looked around and saw a small, open box. He walked closer to the box and heard the sound again. Inside the box, there was a tiny bird with a hurt wing. Spot felt sad for the bird and wanted to help it. With his big open heart, Spot carefully picked up the bird in his mouth and carried it to his home. His human friend helped fix the bird's wing. The bird was very thankful, and they all played together in the open field. Spot was happy that he could help his new friend, and they all lived happily ever after.
