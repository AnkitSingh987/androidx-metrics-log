Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobby the Blue Bird and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there lived a blue bird named Bobby. Bobby loved to eat berries. One day, he found a big bush with lots of red berries. He ate and ate until his belly was full. Later, Bobby met his friend, Sally the squirrel. "Look, I found these yummy berries! Do you want some?" Bobby asked. Sally nodded her head and said, "Yes, please! I love berries too." So, Bobby decided to deliver some berries to Sally. He picked up the berries in his beak and flew to Sally's tree. He gave her the berries and they both enjoyed eating them together. They were very happy and played all day long in the forest. And from that day on, Bobby and Sally always shared their berries with each other.

Anna and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Anna had a shiny diamond that she got from her grandma. She loved to look at it and pretend it was magic. Ben had a truck that he could push and make noises with. He liked to drive it around and crash into things. "Can I see your diamond, Anna?" Ben asked. He wanted to touch the shiny thing and see if it was real. "No, Ben, it's mine!" Anna said. She held the diamond close to her chest and turned away from him. "You have your truck, go play with that!" "But I want to see it, please!" Ben begged. He reached out his hand and tried to grab the diamond from Anna. Anna pulled away and shouted. "Stop it, Ben, you're mean! Leave me alone!" She ran to the other side of the living room and hid behind the couch. Ben was frustrated. He didn't understand why Anna wouldn't share with him. He thought she was being selfish and rude. He decided to get back at her. He took his truck and drove it towards the couch. He made loud noises and pretended to crash into the couch. "Bang! Boom! Crash!" He yelled. He hoped to scare Anna and make her come out. Anna heard the noises and peeked from behind the couch. She saw Ben's truck coming closer and closer. She was scared and angry. She didn't like Ben's truck. She thought it was noisy and ugly. She threw her diamond at the truck and hoped to stop it. "Take that, Ben!" She screamed. She threw the diamond with all her might. The diamond flew through the air and hit the truck. It made a loud clink and bounced off the truck. It landed on the floor and rolled away. Anna and Ben watched in horror as the diamond rolled under the sofa. "Uh-oh," Ben said. He realized he had made a big mistake. He didn't want to lose Anna's diamond. He knew it was special to her and her grandma. He felt sorry and ashamed. "Uh-oh," Anna said. She realized she had made a big mistake. She didn't want to lose her diamond. She knew it was precious and beautiful. She felt sad and guilty. They both ran to the sofa and tried to reach for the diamond. They couldn't see it or touch it. It was too far and too dark. They looked at each other and started to cry. "Mama! Papa!" They called. They hoped their parents could help them find the diamond. They hoped they could say sorry and be friends again. They learned a hard lesson. They learned to share and be nice. They learned to not throw or crash their toys. They learned to not leave their diamond.

Lola was very excited. She saw a comet in the sky! It was the most beautiful thing she had ever seen. She couldn't wait to go outside and grab it. So, Lola asked her mom if she could go outside and get it. Her mom said no and warned her not to touch it. But Lola was spoiled, so she ignored her mom and ran outside. She was about to grab the comet when a giant wave suddenly came up from the sea and swept her away. Lola screamed for help, but no one heard her. The wave took her far away and no one ever saw her again. The wave had foreshadowed Lola's fate. No matter how excited she was, it was a bad idea to go outside and try to grab the comet.
