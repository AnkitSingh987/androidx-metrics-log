Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Boy and the Glass

Once upon a time there was a boy named Jack. He was three years old and loved to explore. One day, Jack was exploring outside and he saw a glass on the ground. It looked very clean, so Jack wanted to grab it. He reached down, grabbed it with two hands, and brought it to his mom. Mama was very surprised to see the clean glass! She said “Good job, Jack! We can use this to drink from.” So, Jack and his Mom put the clean glass in the cupboard. Jack loved to know that he had found something special! From that day on, Jack was always looking to grab things that he found outside. He made sure to bring them to Mama to see what she thought!

One day, a little cat named Kitty saw a big tree. Kitty wanted to climb the tree. So, she started to climb up. She climbed higher and higher. But then, Kitty got scared. She felt helpless and didn't know how to get down. A nice boy named Tim saw Kitty in the tree. He said, "Don't worry, Kitty! I will help you." Tim found a long stick. He used the stick to help Kitty climb down. Kitty was very happy. After Kitty was safe, Tim's mom gave him some cream as a treat. Kitty and Tim shared the cream. They became good friends. The moral of the story is: always help others when they feel helpless.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside in the cold. One day, it was so cold that the water would freeze. Mia put on her coat and went outside to play. Mia found a candle on the ground. She picked it up and tried to light it. But the wind was too strong and blew it out. Mia was sad, but she kept trying to light the candle. She did not hear her mom calling her inside because she was deaf. The wind got stronger and colder. Mia was so focused on the candle that she did not notice the cold. Soon, Mia started to freeze. She was too cold and could not move. When her mom found her, it was too late. Mia stayed frozen and could not play outside anymore.

Jack was a little boy. He was only 3 years old. One day, he was playing outside his house. Suddenly, it started to rain. So, Jack looked for shelter. He saw a tree nearby and decided to sit under it. But when he walked closer, he saw an old man sleeping there. He was rude to Jack and shouted. Jack was very scared. He ran away. He didn't know where to go. He started to walk and hope that he would find shelter soon. After a while, he saw a cave and went in. Inside, he saw a big blanket and he was so happy! He cuddled up in the blanket and soon went to sleep. Jack was safe at last. He hoped that he would never meet the rude old man again.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big sister named Sue. They liked to play outside together. One day, while they were playing, Tim fell down and hurt his knee. "Ouch!" cried Tim. "My knee hurts!" Sue saw Tim's hurt knee and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." Sue took a tissue and began to wipe Tim's knee gently. Tim felt better and smiled at Sue. "Thank you, Sue," he said. One day, Tim found a big, yummy cake in the kitchen. He wanted to eat it all by himself. Sue saw him and said, "Tim, don't be selfish. We should share the cake with our friends." Tim thought about it and agreed. They invited their friends over and shared the cake. Everyone was happy, and Tim learned that sharing is better than being selfish.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was an ugly bug. The bug was sad because nobody liked to play with him. Every day, he would crawl under the big tree and watch the other bugs play. One day, the wise old ant saw the ugly bug and decided to give a lecture to all the bugs. He told them that it is not nice to judge others by how they look. It is important to be kind and play with everyone, no matter how they appear. The other bugs listened and understood the wise old ant's words. They started to play with the ugly bug, and soon they all became good friends. The ugly bug was happy, and the other bugs learned that being kind and not judging others is what makes them beautiful inside.
