Category: Intermediate
Title: Miles and the Girl

Miles was on his way home from the playground when he noticed two girls fighting. He stopped and watched them, never having seen a fight before. Suddenly, a friendly woman came up to him and explained what was going on. "They are fighting because one girl said something mean about the other," she said. "The girl on the right is trying to be humble and not get angry, and the girl on the left doesn't like that." Miles pondered what the woman had told him and realized he needed to learn to be humble like the girl on the right. He thanked the woman and continued on his way home, feeling inspired by what he had seen.

Once upon a time, there was a clever penguin named Pip. Pip lived in a cold place with lots of snow. He had a small igloo for his home. One day, Pip saw that his igloo was broken. He felt sad and wanted to fix it. Pip had an idea. He could replace the broken part with new snow. He started to work, using his small wings to push the snow. He worked very hard, and soon, his igloo looked good again. But then, something unexpected happened! While Pip was inside his igloo, he saw that the snow on the floor was moving. The snow turned into a friendly snowman! The snowman said he was hiding in the snow when Pip fixed his igloo. Pip was surprised, but happy to have a new friend. They played together, and Pip knew that he had done a great job fixing his igloo.

Tom is a boy who likes to play with his toys. He has a blue car, a blue ball and a blue hat. He likes to make noises with his car and throw his ball in the air. He also likes to wear his hat and pretend he is a driver. One morning, Tom wakes up early and sees the sun shining. He jumps out of his bed and runs to his toys. He picks up his blue car and says, "Vroom, vroom, I am going fast!" He drives his car on the floor and makes loud sounds. His mom hears him and comes to his room. She smiles and says, "Good morning, Tom. You are very happy today. Do you want to have breakfast?" Tom nods and says, "Yes, mom. I want to eat." He puts down his car and follows his mom to the kitchen. In the kitchen, Tom sees his dad and his sister. His dad is making pancakes and his sister is setting the table. They say, "Hello, Tom. You are up early. Do you want some pancakes?" Tom says, "Yes, please. I like pancakes." He sits on his chair and waits for his food. His mom gives him a plate with a big pancake and some syrup. Tom says, "Thank you, mom. This looks yummy." He cuts his pancake with his fork and takes a bite. He says, "Mmm, this is good." He eats his pancake and drinks his milk. Tom is happy. He likes his blue toys, his sunny morning and his family. He says, "I love you, mom. I love you, dad. I love you, sister." They say, "We love you, too, Tom. You are a good boy." They hug and kiss him and smile.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a triangle. The triangle had three sides and lived with its friends, the circle and the square. They liked to play in the sun and have fun all day. One day, the sun was very harsh. It was too hot for the triangle and its friends to play outside. So, they decided to stay inside their little house. They played with their toys and had a good time. The triangle saw a yummy treat on a high shelf. It wanted to reach the treat to share with its friends. The circle and the square tried to help, but they could not reach it. Then, they had an idea. They all stood on each other, with the triangle on top. The triangle reached the treat, and they all enjoyed it together. They were happy and knew that they could do anything when they worked together.
