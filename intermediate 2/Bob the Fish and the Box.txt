Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Fish and the Box

One day, a happy fish named Bob liked to dive in the water. He had a friend, a bird named Tim. Tim liked to sit on the tip of a tree branch and watch Bob dive. One day, Bob saw something dirty in the water. He told Tim, "Look, Tim! There is something dirty in the water!" Tim looked and said, "Let's go see what it is!" They went to the dirty thing and saw it was a big box. They opened the box and found many toys inside! Bob and Tim were very happy. They played with the toys all day and forgot about the dirty water.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia and her mom. Mia was very bored because it was a dull day. Mia asked her mom, "What can we do today?" Her mom thought for a moment and then suggested, "Let's play a game on the board!" Mia was excited and ran to get the board. They set it up on the floor and started to play. As they played, Mia's mom said, "The winner gets a big hug!" Mia tried her best to win the game. In the end, Mia won the game and her mom gave her a big hug. They laughed and played more games. The dull day became a fun day for both of them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He was very happy and excited because it was almost his birthday. He wanted to finish something special before his birthday. He got an idea. He went to the park and found a green tree. He asked the nurse if it was okay to help him finish the tree. The nurse said yes and gave him some tools. She showed him how to use the tools. First, he cut the green branches of the tree. Then he used the drill to make holes in the trunk. Finally, he sawed the trunk with a saw. He worked very hard and finished the tree in no time. When he was done, the little boy was very happy. He thanked the nurse and hugged the tree. His birthday was the best ever because he finished the tree. The green tree was very special to him!

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny garden, there was a little plant. The plant was green and small. It lived with many other plants. They all liked the sun. One day, a girl came to the garden. She wanted to do something nice for the plant. She gave it water and sang a song. The plant was very happy. As the days went by, the girl came back to do more for the plant. She gave it more water and more songs. The plant grew big and strong. It had bright, pretty flowers. The girl and the plant were the best of friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was very nervous. She wanted to find her favorite toy, a small red ball. Amy looked in her room, under her bed, and in her toy box. But she could not find her red ball. Then, she thought, "Maybe it's in the drawer!" So she went to the drawer and tried to open it. But the drawer was stuck! Amy called her mom and said, "Mom, can you help me open the drawer?" Her mom came and helped her. With a big push, they split the drawer open. Inside, they found the small red ball. Amy was so happy to find her favorite toy. She thanked her mom and played with her red ball all day. From that day on, Amy was not nervous anymore. She knew if she ever needed help, her mom would be there.

Once upon a time, there was a lively bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop and play with his friends in the big green field. One day, a big dog came to the field. The dog wore a uniform with a shiny badge. The dog said he was the boss of the field. Bob and his friends were scared of the big dog. But Bob had an idea. He said, "We can be friends with the dog. Maybe he will be nice to us." So, Bob went to the dog and said, "Hi, I am Bob. Will you be my friend?" The dog smiled and said, "Yes, I will be your friend, Bob." Bob and the dog played together with all the other bunnies. They learned that it is good to surrender their fear and be friends with others who are different. The big dog in the uniform became their best friend, and they all lived happily together in the big green field.
