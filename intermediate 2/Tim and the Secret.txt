Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Secret

Once upon a time, in a little house, lived a boy named Tim. He had a secret. Tim could perform a special dance. This dance could make bitter fruit taste sweet. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over. She brought a bitter lemon. Sue did not like it. She made a face and said, "Yuck! I don't like this lemon. It's too bitter!" Tim smiled and said, "I can help! Watch me perform my dance." Tim danced around the lemon. Sue watched with big eyes. When he was done, she tried the lemon again. "Wow! The lemon is sweet now! Your dance is magic!" Tim was happy to share his secret with his friend. From that day on, they enjoyed sweet fruit together, and Tim's dance made everyone happy.

Sara and Tom were playing in the garden. They had a big box and some crayons. They wanted to make a lovely thing with the box. "What do you want to make, Sara?" Tom asked. "I want to make a castle, Tom. A castle with a princess and a dragon and a knight," Sara said. "That sounds fun, Sara. How do we make a castle?" Tom asked. "We can draw windows and doors and towers on the box. And we can cut out some flags and stick them on the top. And we can imagine that we are the princess and the knight, and the dragon is hiding in the bushes," Sara said. They worked hard on their castle. They drew and cut and stuck and colored. They made a lovely thing with the box. Then they put on some hats and capes and went inside the castle. "Hello, princess Sara. I am knight Tom. I have come to save you from the dragon," Tom said. "Hello, knight Tom. Thank you for coming. The dragon is very scary. He roars and breathes fire and tries to eat me," Sara said. "Don't worry, princess Sara. I have a sword and a shield and I am very brave. I will fight the dragon and protect you," Tom said. They pretended to fight the dragon. They made loud noises and moved the box. They had a lot of fun. They imagined that they were in a castle. They made a lovely thing with the box.

One day, a little girl named Sue put on her pink dress. She wanted to go outside and play. Her mom said, "Sue, don't forget your hat!" Sue looked all around but could not find her hat. She asked her brother, Tim, "Have you seen my hat?" Tim said, "No, but let's search for it together!" They looked high and low. They looked under the bed and behind the door. But they could not find the hat. Then, Sue had an idea. She put her hand in her pocket and felt something. She pulled it out and it was her hat! She had forgotten that she put it there. Sue and Tim laughed and laughed. They were happy that they found the hat. Now, Sue could go outside and play with her brother.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to match her clothes. Every day, she wore a dress with adorable socks. She was so happy when they matched. One day, Lily was eating her favorite snack, jam on bread. She was so excited that she spilled some jam on her dress. "Oh no!" cried Lily. "Now my dress and socks don't match." Her mom came into the room and saw the mess. "Don't worry, Lily," she said. "We can clean your dress." Lily smiled, and her mom helped her wash the dress. When it was clean, Lily put it back on, and her dress and socks matched again. Lily was happy, and they all enjoyed jam on bread together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in an ordinary house with her mom, dad, and cat named Thunder. Lily loved to play with Thunder, and they would keep each other company all day long. One day, Lily and Thunder went to the park to play. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. But then, the sky turned dark, and it started to rain. Lily and Thunder needed to find a place to keep dry, so they hid under a big tree. As they waited for the rain to stop, Lily and Thunder listened to the raindrops falling on the leaves. They felt safe and warm under the tree. Soon, the rain stopped, and the sun came out. Lily and Thunder went home to their ordinary house, happy to have spent a fun day together.
