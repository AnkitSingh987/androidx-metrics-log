Category: Intermediate
Title: The Strong Deer and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a strong deer. He was walking through the forest, looking for a place to rest and sleep. Then, he heard a voice. "Hey, deer! Come here," said the voice. The deer was curious, so he slowly walked towards the voice. He noticed a man with a net in his hand. "Come closer," said the man. The deer thought he could trust this man, so he kept walking. Unfortunately, the man had bad intentions. As soon as the deer was close enough, the man threw the net and caught the deer. The deer screamed for help but no one came to help him. He was stuck in the net and he knew it was the end. The deer lay in the net, feeling scared and alone. He slept there that night, wishing he had never met the man. But it was too late. The next morning, the man came back and took the deer away. The deer was never seen again. Moral of the story: Trust no one, especially in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to mix things in her toy kitchen. One day, she went to the park with her mom. There, she found a berry bush. Sue picked some berries to take home. At home, Sue wanted to mix the berries to make a pie. But her mom said, "Some of these berries are bad. Let's only use the good ones." Sue listened to her mom and they picked out the good berries together. Sue and her mom mixed the good berries and made a yummy pie. They shared it with their friends, and everyone loved it. Sue was happy that she could make something so tasty with the help of her mom.

Lily was a small girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna, who had blue eyes and long hair. Lily liked to dress Anna in different clothes and make her talk. One day, Lily wanted to play with Anna in the garden. She asked her mom, "Mom, can I take Anna outside?" Mom looked at the sky and saw some dark clouds. "No, Lily, you can't," she said. "It might rain soon. You can play with Anna inside." Lily was sad. She wanted to see the flowers and the birds with Anna. She said, "But mom, Anna likes the garden. She wants to see the colors and hear the sounds." Mom smiled and hugged Lily. "I know, Lily, but Anna doesn't like to get wet. If it rains, her hair and clothes will be wet and dirty. And her eyes might get hurt by the raindrops. You don't want that, do you?" Lily shook her head. She loved Anna and didn't want her to be unhappy. She said, "No, mom, I don't want that. I will play with Anna inside. Maybe we can read a book or draw a picture." Mom nodded and kissed Lily. "That's a good idea, Lily. You are a good girl and a good friend to Anna. Maybe later, when the sun comes out, you can play with Anna in the garden. But for now, let's go inside and find something fun to do." Lily smiled and took Anna by the hand. She said, "Come on, Anna, let's go inside. Mom will allow us to play in the garden later. But for now, we can have fun inside. I love you, Anna." Anna smiled back and said, "I love you, Lily. You are the best friend I ever had."

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. It was a sunny day, and he was having fun. He was running and jumping in the grass. After a while, Tim saw a dead bug on the ground. He felt sad for the bug and said, "Oh no! Poor bug!" Tim's mom called him inside to take a bath. He went inside and took off his dirty clothes. His mom filled the tub with warm water. Tim got in and splashed around, washing away the dirt from playing outside. After the bath, Tim's mom wrapped him in a big, soft bathrobe. She helped him dry off with a towel. Tim felt warm and cozy in his bathrobe. He looked at his mom and said, "I'm all clean now, Mommy!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you are, Tim. Now, let's go have dinner." Tim felt happy and ready for a yummy meal.
