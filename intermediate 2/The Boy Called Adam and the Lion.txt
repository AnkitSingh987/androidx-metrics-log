Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Adam and the Lion

Once upon a time, there was a tiny boy called Adam. Every day, he liked to explore his garden. Today was special. He found a huge maze! It was so cool. He went in and started to explore it. He stumbled around the corridor for a long time. Eventually, he got lost. He felt so scared in the dark maze. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. It was a big, bad lion! Adam started to run but the lion blocked his way. Adam stumbled and the lion roared. That was the last thing that Adam saw before he passed away. It was a bad end for the tiny boy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his old dog, Spot. Tim and Spot loved to play together. They would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Tim's mom told him to go upstairs and clean his room. But Tim did not want to clean his room. He wanted to play with Spot. So, Tim refused to go upstairs. He said, "No, I want to play with Spot." Tim's mom was not happy. She said, "Tim, you need to clean your room. Then you can play with Spot." Tim thought for a moment. He knew that if he cleaned his room, he could play with Spot all day. So, Tim went upstairs, cleaned his room, and then had lots of fun playing with his old dog, Spot.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a toy bunny made of soft velvet. Lily loved her bunny very much and took it everywhere she went. One day, Lily and her velvet bunny were playing hide and seek. "I will count to ten, and you hide," said Lily to her bunny. She closed her eyes and counted. "One, two, three..." When she reached ten, she opened her eyes and started to look for her bunny. She looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the closet. But she could not find her bunny anywhere. Suddenly, Lily saw her bunny's ears peek out from behind the big chair. She tried to be very quiet as she tiptoed closer. "I see you!" she shouted, as she picked up her velvet bunny. They both laughed and continued to play.

One day, a boy named Tim found a hard tray in his room. He did not know what to do with it. So, he went to his mom and asked, "Mom, what can I do with this hard tray?" His mom thought for a moment and said, "You can use it to carry your toys from one place to another." Tim liked the idea. He put his toys on the tray and carried them to the living room. But, he walked too fast and the toys fell off the tray. Tim felt sad. His mom saw what happened and said, "Don't worry, Tim. Just reverse and pick up the toys. Then, walk slowly with the tray." Tim did what his mom said. He reversed, picked up the toys, and walked slowly with the tray. This time, the toys did not fall off. Tim was happy and played with his toys in the living room.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lucy. She had a big heart and loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Lucy and her mom were making yummy soup for their family. Lucy's mom asked her to pour some salt into the soup. Lucy was very happy to help. She took the salt and poured it into the soup pot. But, oh no! Lucy poured too much salt! The soup was now too salty. Lucy's mom was not mad. She knew that Lucy was trying her best to help. Together, they poured more water into the soup to make it just right. Lucy learned that even when we make mistakes, it's important to keep trying. Her family enjoyed the soup, and Lucy felt proud of her work.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy staff that she loved to play with. She would dance and twirl with her staff in her hands. One day, Amy took her staff to the park to play. While at the park, Amy met a new friend named Tim. They played together and had lots of fun. Amy showed Tim her staff and they took turns twirling it around. They were very glad to have met each other. As they played, a big wind came and blew the staff away. Amy and Tim looked and looked, but they could not find it. They were very sad. Just then, a nice dog came running up to them. The dog had Amy's staff in its mouth! The dog had found it! Amy and Tim were so surprised and happy. They gave the dog a big hug and continued to play, now with their new dog friend. They never would have thought that something so unexpected could happen, but they were very glad it did.
