Category: Intermediate
Title: Nat and Julie and the Park

Nat and Julie were playing in the park when they saw smoke coming from a nearby temple. Nat said, "What's that smoke?" Julie said, "Looks like the temple is burning!" She could see flames coming out of the top of the temple. Suddenly, they heard a loud roar coming from the temple. Nat was scared, "What was that sound?" Julie said, "It sounds like a fierce fire! We should go closer and see what's happening." So the two kids walked closer to the temple, carefully. They saw the fire was burning and that it was getting bigger and bigger. Nat said, "That's so bright and loud! Do you think anyone is in there?" Julie said, "We should try to help, but we should stay far away from the flames. We can find someone to help." So Nat and Julie ran to find an adult to help put out the fire. Luckily, they were able to find someone in time and the temple was saved.

Once upon a time, there was a shy dog named Sam. Sam was not like other dogs. He did not like to play with balls or run in the park. Sam only liked to chew on his leather toy. One day, Sam met a cat named Tom. Tom asked Sam, "Why do you just play with your leather toy? Come and play with me!" Sam felt shy but wanted to try. Sam and Tom played together. They ran and jumped and had fun. After a while, Sam quit being shy. He was happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Sam and Tom played together, and Sam still loved his leather toy.

Once upon a time, there was a pale dog named Lulu. Lulu liked to run and play in the park. One day, Lulu saw a big ball in the park. She wanted to play with it. But she had a question: "Whose ball is this?" Lulu asked her friend, the cat. The cat did not know. Then she asked her friend, the bird. The bird did not know either. Lulu decided to keep the ball and play with it. She was very happy. As Lulu played with the ball, she felt a little tired. She took a deep breath with her lung. Suddenly, the ball popped! It was not a ball, but a big balloon! Lulu was surprised, but she laughed and continued to play in the park with her friends.

Once upon a time, in a small and organized house, there lived a nice family. They always ate their meals together. One day, they received a letter. It was an invitation to a big party. The family was very excited. They went to the party and saw many friends. They played games, danced, and had fun. Then, it was time to eat the meal. The family sat down at a big table with their friends. They were ready to eat. But, something unexpected happened! The meal was not normal food. It was a big cake that looked like a meal. The family and friends laughed and laughed. They ate the cake and had more fun. The party was the best they had ever been to.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max liked to play and run in the park. One day, Max saw a big pit in the park. He thought it would be fun to jump over the pit. Max took a big step back and started to run. As he got closer to the pit, he heard his friend, Tom the cat, shout, "Be careful, Max!" But Max was not scared. He knew he was a mighty dog. Max jumped over the pit and landed on the other side. He was very happy and proud. Tom the cat clapped and shouted, "You did it, Max! You are a mighty dog!" Max wagged his tail and they both played in the park all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his yard. He saw a snake in the deep grass. Tim was scared but he wanted to be brave. So, he walked near the snake to see what it would do. The snake looked at Tim and said, "Why are you scared? I am not a bad snake. I am just lost. Can you help me find my way home?" Tim thought about it and decided to help the snake. They walked together to look for the snake's home. While they were walking, they saw a big rock. The snake said, "My home is under that rock!" Tim tried to move the rock, but it was too heavy. He decided to squeeze the rock with all his might. To his surprise, the rock was soft like a pillow! It moved away easily, and the snake found its home. Tim and the snake became good friends and played together every day.
