Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Diamond

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom liked to freeze things. One day, he found a big, shiny diamond. He was very happy. He wanted to show his friends the diamond, so he put it in his bag. Tom went to his friend's house. He showed his friends the diamond. They were very excited. But then, Tom had an idea. He wanted to freeze the diamond to see what would happen. His friends said, "No, don't do it!" But Tom didn't listen. Tom put the diamond in the freezer. After a while, he opened the freezer. The diamond was gone! Tom was sad. His friends were sad too. Then, they saw something shiny on the floor. The diamond had turned into ice and melted! Everyone was surprised. Tom learned not to freeze everything he found.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big ball. She liked to roll the ball up and down the street. She played with the ball every day after school. Mia was very proud of her ball. One day, Mia met a boy named Tom. Tom had a bottle of glue. Tom said, "Look, I can stick things with my glue!" Mia was excited and said, "Let's play together!" Mia rolled her ball and Tom put glue on it. They stuck leaves, flowers, and small sticks to the ball. The ball became very colorful and pretty. Mia and Tom were proud of their work. They showed their new ball to everyone in the neighborhood. The kids loved the ball and played with it together.

One day, a boy named Tim found a hard mask. He wanted to play with it, but his mom said, "Be careful, it can break." Tim did not listen and ran outside to show his friends. Tim and his friends played with the mask all day. They took turns wearing it and making funny faces. But then, Tim tripped and fell. The mask broke into two pieces. Tim was sad and scared because his mom had warned him. Tim went home and showed his mom the broken mask. His mom was not mad. She smiled and said, "I knew this would happen, so I got another one." She gave Tim a new mask that was even better than the first one. Tim was happy and promised to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a charming dragon named Dee. Dee was very big and had a long tail. Dee lived in a big cave near a small town. One day, Dee heard a little girl crying. Dee went to see what was wrong. The little girl said, "I can't find my way home." Dee wanted to help, so they tried to solve the problem together. They looked around and saw a big tree. Dee said, "Do you know this tree?" The little girl said, "Yes! My house is near the big tree." They walked to the big tree, but they didn't see the little girl's house. Instead, they found a big party with lots of people. The people were not scared of Dee. They were happy to see the charming dragon. They said, "Dee, you solved our problem! We needed a big, fun friend for our party." Dee was happy to join the party, and the little girl found her family there. Everyone had a great time, and Dee made many new friends. From that day on, Dee was known as the charming dragon who could solve problems and make people happy.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom liked to drink milk every day. One day, Tom was very thirsty and went to find some milk to drink. Tom saw a big party. It was a marriage party for two dogs. The dogs were very happy. Tom saw a big bowl of milk at the party. He wanted to drink it, but he was scared of the dogs. Tom had an idea. He put on a dog costume and went to the party. Nobody knew he was a cat! Tom drank all the milk and felt very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Tom started to bark like a dog! The dogs at the marriage party were surprised. They did not know Tom was a cat. They thought Tom was a new dog friend. Tom was happy too. He made new friends and got to drink milk at the party. And they all lived happily ever after.
