Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and the Missile

Ben likes to play with his toy soldiers and rockets. He has a big missile that can make a loud noise when he pushes a button. He wants to teach his little sister, Lily, how to play with it. "Look, Lily, this is a missile. It can fly very fast and go boom!" Ben says, holding the missile in his hand. "Missile? Boom?" Lily repeats, looking curious. "Yes, missile. Boom. Watch this!" Ben says, and pushes the button. The missile makes a loud noise and shoots out a red light. Lily jumps and claps her hands. "Wow! That's cool! Can I try?" Lily asks. "Sure, Lily. But be careful. Don't point it at anyone. It's only for pretend." Ben says, and gives the missile to Lily. Lily holds the missile and pushes the button. The missile makes a loud noise and shoots out a red light. She laughs and says, "Missile! Boom!" Ben and Lily play with the missile for a long time. They pretend they are famous heroes who save the world from bad guys. They have a lot of fun. They don't know that their mom is watching them from the window, smiling. She is happy that Ben is teaching Lily how to play nicely. She thinks they are very cute and smart. She loves them very much.

One day, a hairy bear was looking for food in the forest. He was very hungry and wanted to find some honey or berries. He saw a big cave and thought there might be something good inside. He walked to the cave and sniffed the air. "Hello, is anyone there?" he asked in a loud voice. But he heard no answer. He decided to go inside and see for himself. He did not know that the cave belonged to a lion, who was sleeping in the dark. The bear entered the cave and saw a pile of bones and fur. He thought they were leftovers from the lion's meal. He picked up a bone and started to bite it. It was hard and dry, but he was too hungry to care. Suddenly, he heard a roar behind him. It was the lion, who had woken up and saw the bear eating his bone. He was very angry and wanted to chase the bear away. "Hey, that's mine! Get out of my cave!" he shouted. The bear was scared and dropped the bone. He turned around and saw the lion's big teeth and claws. He knew he had to run fast or he would be in trouble. "Sorry, I did not know. I was just hungry. Please don't bite me!" he said. He ran out of the cave and back to the forest. He learned his lesson and never went to the lion's cave again. He looked for food somewhere else, where it was safe and no one would bite him.

One day, a squirrel was playing in the park. He liked to balance on the thin branches of the trees. He would jump from branch to branch, having lots of fun. A little bird saw the squirrel and felt envious. She wanted to balance and play like the squirrel. So, she flew to the squirrel and said, "Can I play with you? I want to balance like you do." The squirrel smiled and said, "Of course! Let's play together!" The bird and the squirrel played all day, balancing and jumping from branch to branch. They became very good friends and had a fun time in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful hunter named Tom. Tom lived in a small house near the woods. He liked to read books about animals. One sunny day, Tom went to the woods to find a big bear. He walked and walked until he saw a little rabbit. The rabbit said, "Please, don't hurt me, Tom." Tom smiled and said, "Don't worry, little rabbit. I am looking for a big bear, not a rabbit." The rabbit was happy and hopped away. Tom walked some more and finally found the big bear. He used his powerful arms to catch the bear. Tom took the bear home and read a book to him. The bear liked the story and became Tom's friend. They lived happily ever after.
