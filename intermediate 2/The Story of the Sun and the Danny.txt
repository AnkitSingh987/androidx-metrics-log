Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Sun and the Danny

The sun was rising in the sky. It was a modern sunrise. Danny was looking at it. He called out, "Mommy, what is that?" Mommy was nearby. She smiled and said, "That's the sunrise, Danny. It's how the day starts." Danny looked in awe. "It's so big and pretty!" he said. Mommy smiled again and said, "Yes, Danny. You should be happy to see it every day." Danny nodded and ran off to play. He knew that the sunrise was special and he was happy to be alive to see it.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, green frog near the pond. Tim thought the frog was disgusting, but his mom had a different opinion. She told Tim, "I think the frog is very interesting and pretty." Tim and his mom walked around the pond, when they saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was crying. Tim's mom asked, "Why are you crying, Sue?" Sue said, "I forget where my toy is. I lost it in the park." Tim wanted to help Sue find her toy. Tim, Sue, and Tim's mom looked all around the park for the toy. They looked near the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. Finally, they found the toy near the pond. Sue was so happy and hugged her toy. Tim felt good that he helped Sue, and he didn't think the frog was disgusting anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a swamp. In the swamp, there lived a very generous frog named Fred. Fred liked to talk to all his friends. He had many friends, like the fish, the birds, and the bugs. One day, while Fred was talking to his friend, the fish, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and tried to eat the fish! But Fred was quick and he jumped on the bird's back. The bird was very surprised. The bird and Fred started to talk. The bird said he was sorry and didn't want to eat the fish anymore. The bird was very hungry. Fred, being generous, shared his bugs with the bird. They all became good friends and lived happily in the swamp.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big sign. The sign was near the road. It was a police sign. The sign told cars to stop. One day, a happy and enthusiastic dog saw the sign. The dog wanted to help the police. So, the dog sat near the sign and waited for cars to stop. A car came and stopped. The dog was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog saw a cat in the car. The dog forgot about the sign and started to chase the cat. The car drove away, and the dog followed it. The sign was left alone, but the dog had a fun day with the cat.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Tim. Tim loved to slide down the big hill near his home. One day, Tim saw a lot of cars on the road. This was traffic. Tim was nervous because he had never seen so many cars before. Tim wanted to slide down the hill, but he was scared of the traffic. He thought, "What if I slide into the other cars?" Tim's friend, Sam the truck, saw him looking sad. Sam asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told Sam about the traffic and how he was nervous to slide down the hill. Sam had an idea. He said, "I will go with you, and we will slide down the hill together." Tim felt better with Sam by his side. They both slid down the hill, and Sam made sure Tim was safe. Tim was so happy that he could slide down the hill and not be scared of the traffic. From that day on, Tim and Sam always played together and were the best of friends.

Sara and Tom were playing with their toy animals in the garden. Sara had a big elephant and Tom had a small mouse. They made noises and pretended to be in the jungle. "Look, my elephant is so strong and brave!" Sara said. "He can squeeze anything with his trunk!" "Can he squeeze my mouse?" Tom asked. "Sure, he can!" Sara said. "But he won't, because he is nice and harmless. He only squeezes bad things, like snakes and crocodiles." Tom was curious. He wanted to see if the elephant could really squeeze his mouse. He moved his mouse closer to the elephant's trunk. "Hey, what are you doing?" Sara asked. "Don't touch my elephant!" "I just want to see!" Tom said. "It's only a toy, it won't hurt!" But Sara was angry. She did not like Tom touching her elephant. She grabbed the elephant and pulled it away from Tom. "Give it back!" Tom shouted. "That's not fair!" They both tugged on the elephant, trying to get it to their side. The elephant's trunk stretched and twisted. "Stop it!" Sara cried. "You're breaking my elephant!" They heard a loud snap. The elephant's trunk broke off. They both gasped and let go of the elephant. They looked at each other, then at the broken toy. They felt sad and sorry. "I'm sorry, Sara," Tom said. "I didn't mean to break your elephant." "I'm sorry, Tom," Sara said. "I shouldn't have been so mean to you." They hugged and said they were still friends. They decided to share their toys and play nicely. They fixed the elephant's trunk with some tape and glue. They learned to be careful and gentle with their toys and each other.
