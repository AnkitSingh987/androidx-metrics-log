Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom had a big garden with many pretty flowers. One day, Tom found a problem. His flowers were not getting enough light. Tom needed to solve the problem. Tom had an idea. He went to the store and bought a big bulb. He put the bulb in a tall lamp and turned it on. The light from the bulb made the flowers very happy. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to visit. Sam saw the tall lamp and asked, "Why is there a big lamp in your garden?" Tom said, "The lamp has a bulb that helps my flowers grow. They need light to be happy and pretty." Sam thought it was a smart idea and decided to do the same thing in his garden. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a compassionate boy named Tim saw a big aeroplane in the sky. He wanted to fly like the aeroplane. So he asked his mom, "Mom, can I fly like the aeroplane?" Mom said, "No, Tim, you can't fly. But you can make a paper aeroplane and play with it." Tim made a paper aeroplane and played with it all day. He threw it up high and it flew far. But then, something unexpected happened. The paper aeroplane got stuck in a tree. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a kind bird saw the paper aeroplane and flew close to it. The bird picked up the aeroplane with its beak and brought it back to Tim. Tim was so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends. They played with the paper aeroplane together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a serious cat named Tom. Tom loved to cook in his favorite pan. One day, Tom wanted to make a big cake for his friend, Sam the dog. Tom was mixing the cake when he saw that the pan was too small. He said, "Oh no! How can I solve this?" Just then, Sam came in and heard Tom. Sam said, "I can help you solve this, Tom!" Sam and Tom looked around the kitchen and found a big pan. They put the cake mix in the big pan and baked it. The cake came out perfect! Tom and Sam were so happy that they solved the problem together. They ate the cake and had a great time.

Lily and Tom were playing with blocks in their room. They liked to build towers and bridges and houses with the blocks. They used different colors and shapes to make their buildings. "Look, Lily, I made a big tower!" Tom said, showing his sister his creation. "It is very tall and strong." Lily smiled and clapped her hands. "That is a good tower, Tom. But I made a better bridge. It is very long and thin. It can go over the water and the cars can drive on it." Tom looked at Lily's bridge and nodded. "That is a good bridge, Lily. But can it hold my tower? Let me try to put it on your bridge." Lily shook her head. "No, Tom, don't do that. Your tower is too heavy for my bridge. It will break it and make a mess." But Tom did not listen. He picked up his tower and tried to balance it on Lily's bridge. But the bridge was too thin and the tower was too heavy. The bridge snapped and the tower fell down. The blocks scattered all over the floor. "Tom, you broke my bridge!" Lily cried, feeling sad and angry. "That was not nice. You should say sorry." Tom felt sorry too. He saw that Lily was upset and that he had ruined their game. He did not mean to break her bridge. He wanted to make her happy. "I'm sorry, Lily. I did not mean to break your bridge. I was just curious. Can we build it again?" Tom said, looking at Lily with a sorry face. Lily looked at Tom and saw that he was sorry. She knew that he was her brother and that he loved her. She decided to forgive him. "Okay, Tom. I forgive you. But next time, please ask me before you touch my things. And let's clean up this mess before dinner. Mom will be mad if she sees it." Lily said, giving Tom a hug. Tom hugged Lily back. "Okay, Lily. I will ask you next time. And let's clean up together. Mom will be happy if she sees it. And maybe she will make us our favorite dinner. I hope it is pizza." Lily and Tom smiled and started to pick up the blocks. They put them back in the box and made their room tidy. They were happy to play together and to have each other. They hoped that mom would make them pizza for dinner. They loved pizza.
