Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sally. Sally was so excited to go to the park. She woke up that morning and put on her nicest dress. She grabbed her pink diary and they started walking. When they got to the park, Sally ran straight to the swings. She loved swinging so high, as if she could touch the clouds. She pumped her legs back and forth and felt the cool wind on her face. Then, she suddenly remembered that she had forgotten her snack. She got so hungry that she though she might faint. "Mommy, I forget my snack. Can I have something to eat, please?" Sally asked. "Don't worry, sweetie," said Mommy. "I brought you an extra snack. I always remember to bring you snacks when we go to the park." Sally was so happy that her mommy always remembered her snack. She hugged her tight and ran back to the swings. Little did she know that her mommy had something else up her sleeve. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved her pretty dress. One day, Lily wanted to wear her dress to play outside. Her mom said, "No, you can't wear your dress outside. It will get dirty." Lily was very frustrated. Lily tried to resist, but her mom was right. She put on her play clothes and went outside. While playing, she saw a big, shiny box. Lily was very curious. She opened the box and found a beautiful dress inside. It was even prettier than her pretty dress. Lily put on the new dress and ran to show her mom. Her mom was surprised and said, "Wow, that dress is so pretty! Where did you find it?" Lily told her mom about the shiny box. They both laughed and played together in the new dress.

Once upon a time, there was a red bird. The red bird liked to relax in the sun. It sat on a tree and sang happy songs. One day, the red bird saw a knob on a door. The knob was shiny and round. The bird wanted to see what was behind the door. The red bird flew to the knob and turned it. The door opened, and inside was a big room. In the room, there were many toys and treats. The red bird was very happy and played all day. At the end of the day, it went back to its tree to relax and sing.

Once upon a time, there was a normal goat named Billy. Billy lived on a big farm with many friends. Billy liked to play with his friends every day. One day, Billy was playing with his friend, the cow. The cow said, "Billy, let's play hide and seek!" Billy said, "Yes, I like that game!" So, they started to play. The cow closed her eyes and started to count. Billy ran to find a good place to hide. Billy found a big bush to hide behind. He waited for the cow to find him. But he heard a loud yell, "Help!" It was the cow. Billy came out from behind the bush and ran to the cow. The cow was stuck in a big hole. Billy said, "Don't worry, I will help you!" Billy found a long rope and pulled the cow out of the hole. The cow was so happy and said, "Thank you, Billy! You are a good friend." They both laughed and continued to play on the farm. From that day on, Billy and the cow became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a very generous girl who loved to share her toys with her friends. One sunny day, Mia and her friends went for a walk on a path in the park. As they walked on the path, they saw a small bird that looked worried. The bird had lost its way and could not find its nest. Mia and her friends wanted to help the bird, so they looked around for the nest. At last, they found the nest high up in a tree. They helped the bird get back to its nest, and the bird was very happy. Mia and her friends felt good that they could help the worried bird. From that day on, they always looked out for each other and helped anyone in need.
