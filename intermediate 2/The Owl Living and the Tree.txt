Category: Intermediate
Title: The Owl Living and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a brave little owl living in a tree. Everyday the owl wanted to climb to the top of the tree, but he could not manage it. One day he decided to ask for help. He looked around and saw a deaf mole. He decided to ask her for help. But the mole couldn't understand what he was saying as she was deaf. The brave owl was very sad and started to cry. But then, something amazing happened. The mole felt sorry for the little owl and pointed her paw to the top of the tree. She managed to help the little owl understand what she was saying. The little owl was so happy and flew up to the top of the tree. He was finally able to manage it! From the top of the tree, the little owl could see the whole world. The brave owl and the deaf mole became best friends forever.

Anna and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. But they also made a big mess. Toys were everywhere on the floor, on the couch, on the table. "Anna, Ben, it's time to clean up!" Mommy called from the kitchen. "You have to put your toys away before dinner." Anna and Ben did not want to clean up. They wanted to keep playing. They looked at each other and had an idea. "Let's hide!" Anna whispered to Ben. "Maybe Mommy won't find us." "OK, let's hide!" Ben agreed. They ran to the hallway and looked for a good hiding spot. They saw a little door at the end of the hall. It was the door to the attic. They had never been there before. "Let's go in there!" Anna said. She opened the door and climbed the stairs. Ben followed her. They closed the door behind them. The attic was dark and dusty. There were boxes and bags and old things. Anna and Ben felt scared. They heard Mommy calling their names. "Anna! Ben! Where are you? Come out, come out, wherever you are!" Anna and Ben did not answer. They hoped Mommy would not find them. They hugged each other and tried to be quiet. But then they heard a loud noise. It was the door. It slammed shut. They were trapped in the attic. "Help! Help!" Anna and Ben cried. They banged on the door. But it was locked. They could not open it. Mommy heard their cries. She ran to the hall and saw the little door. She tried to open it. But it was locked. She did not have the key. "Don't worry, Anna, Ben, I'm here!" Mommy said. "I'll get you out. Just stay calm and rest. I'll be right back." Mommy ran to the phone and called Daddy. Daddy had the key. He was at work. He said he would come home as fast as he could. Anna and Ben waited in the attic. They were scared and sorry. They wished they had cleaned up their toys. They wished they had not hidden in the little door. They learned their lesson. They never hid there again.

Tim and Ella were best friends. They loved to talk and play together. One day, Tim and Ella decided to discuss what to watch on their video. They were both flexible about the choice. Tim wanted to watch a funny movie, and Ella wanted to watch a dance show. After discussing, they decided to watch a movie about animals. They put the video in and grabbed some snacks. Then they settled down and watched. They had so much fun watching the movie together. they laughed and cheered when their favourite animals appeared. They made plans to watch another video the next day. They high-fived each other after the movie finished. Tim and Ella had a great time.

One day, a driver named Tom was driving his car. He was going to take his friend, Sam, to the park. Tom was a bit frustrated because the road was very busy. As Tom was driving, he saw a little girl trying to cross the street. She looked scared. Tom stopped his car and said, "Do you need help?" The little girl nodded her head. Tom got out of his car and took her hand. He helped her cross the street safely. After that, Tom felt happy. He was not frustrated anymore. He picked up Sam and they went to the park. They had a great time playing together. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel better, even when you are frustrated.
