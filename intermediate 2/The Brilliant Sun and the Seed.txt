Category: Intermediate
Title: The Brilliant Sun and the Seed

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant sun. It shone on the ground. It made the ground warm and happy. The sun would smile and say, "Grow, little plants, grow!" One day, a tiny seed was in the ground. The seed knew the sun could help it grow. The seed waited for the sun to shine on it. The sun came and said, "Grow, little seed, grow!" The seed was so happy. But then, some bad bugs came. They wanted to eat the seed. The seed was scared. The sun saw the bugs and said, "No, bad bugs! You will make the seed rot!" The sun shone very bright and the bugs went away. The seed was safe, and it grew into a big, strong plant. The sun and the plant were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little nosy mouse named Mimi. She loved to run and play all day long. One day, Mimi found a big maze in her yard. Mimi was excited to play in the maze. She ran inside and started to look around. The maze had many paths and turns. Mimi did not know which way to go, but she kept running and trying. After a while, Mimi found her way out of the maze. She was so happy that she did it! Mimi ran back home to tell her friends about her fun day in the maze. They all wanted to try it too, and they had a great time playing together.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a package near his house. He was very happy and wanted to see what was inside. He tried to remove the paper around the package, but it was hard. Tim's cat, Fluffy, saw him and wanted to help. Fluffy used her claws to remove the paper. The package was open, and inside was a toy car! Tim was so happy, and he played with the car all day. But then, Tim's sister, Lily, took the car. Tim was angry. He wanted his car back. Lily saw that Tim was angry and gave the car back to him. They played with the car together and were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little cloud named Puffy. Puffy loved to stay high up in the sky and watch over the animals below. Puffy was always alert, making sure the animals were safe and happy. One day, Puffy saw a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was sad because he could not find his way back to his nest. Puffy wanted to help Chirpy, so he floated down and said, "Chirpy, I will help you find your nest. Stay close to me." Chirpy was happy and stayed close to Puffy. They looked for the nest together. Puffy stayed alert, watching for the nest in the trees. Soon, they found Chirpy's nest, and Chirpy was very happy. "Thank you, Puffy!" said Chirpy. "You are a good friend." Puffy smiled and floated back up into the sky, knowing he had helped his new friend.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. Lily wanted to buy a toy for her friend. Her friend was having a birthday party soon. Lily was very excited to find the perfect toy. When they got to the store, Lily saw a big, hungry dog outside. The dog looked sad and lonely. Lily wanted to help the dog. She asked her mom if they could buy some food for the hungry dog. Lily's mom said yes, and they bought some dog food. They gave the food to the hungry dog, and he was very happy. Then, Lily found a nice toy for her friend's birthday. Lily felt good because she helped a hungry dog and found a gift for her friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree and he wondered what was behind it. He walked around the tree and found a small, confused dog. The dog was cold and hungry. Tim felt sad for the dog and wanted to help. Tim took the dog home and gave him a warm bed to sleep in. He also gave the dog some cocoa to drink. The dog was very happy and wagged his tail. Tim's mom saw the dog and smiled. She was proud of Tim for helping the dog. The next day, Tim and the dog played all day long. They became the best of friends. Tim learned that it is good to help others who are in need. And the dog learned that there are kind people like Tim in the world.
