Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gorilla and the Magic

Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, there lived a little gorilla. The gorilla had a dream. In his dream, he could jump high, high up in the sky. He wanted to touch the clouds and play with the birds. One sunny day, the little gorilla went to his friend, a wise, old owl. The owl knew how to help the gorilla. He gave the gorilla a magic drink. The gorilla drank it and felt dizzy. He closed his eyes and took a deep breath. When the gorilla opened his eyes, he was high up in the sky! He was flying! The gorilla was so happy. He played with the clouds and the birds, just like in his dream. But then, something unexpected happened. The gorilla turned into a beautiful bird! The magic drink had changed him. Now, the gorilla could fly and play in the sky forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to paint. One day, her mom asked her to paint their old bookshelf. Lily was very happy and said, "Yes, Mom! I will paint it!" Lily started to paint the bookshelf, but she was careless. She did not look where she was painting, and paint went everywhere. The floor, the wall, and even her clothes had paint on them. Her mom saw the mess and said, "Oh no, Lily! You need to be careful when you paint!" Lily felt sad, but her mom helped her clean up the mess. They worked together to paint the bookshelf the right way. When they finished, the bookshelf looked beautiful. Lily learned that she should be careful when she paints, and that it is always better to work together.

One day, a little boat named Bob was sailing in the sea. Bob had a big anchor. He always smiled when he saw his anchor. Bob liked his anchor very much. One day, Bob saw another boat named Tim. Tim looked sad. Bob asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my anchor. I feel guilty because I did not take care of it." Bob wanted to help Tim find his anchor. They looked for the anchor everywhere. But they could not find it. The sky turned dark and it started to rain. The wind blew very hard. Bob and Tim were scared. They tried to use their anchors to stay safe, but Tim did not have his anchor. The big waves took Tim away. Bob felt sad and guilty because he could not help his friend.

One day, a furry cat named Luna went for a walk. She saw a table in the park. Luna thought, "This is a good place to relax." Luna jumped on the table and lay down. She closed her eyes and started to relax. Soon, a little boy named Tim came to the park. He saw Luna on the table. "Hi, furry cat!" said Tim. "Can I sit with you?" Luna opened her eyes and looked at Tim. She said, "Yes, you can sit here too." Tim sat down next to Luna. They both relaxed on the table together. They were happy and had a nice day at the park.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sam, near the big tree. The sky was clear and blue. Tim was happy to see Sam. "Let's race to the swings!" said Tim. Sam nodded and smiled. They stood next to each other, ready to run. "One, two, three, go!" Tim shouted. They raced to the swings as fast as they could. Their legs moved quickly, and their arms swung back and forth. Tim and Sam laughed as they ran. They loved to race each other. When they got to the swings, they were both out of breath. Tim said, "That was fun! Let's race again later!" Sam agreed, and they played on the swings together, enjoying their day at the park.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a key. The key was small and shiny. Lucy was glad she found the key. She wanted to find what the key opened. Lucy looked and looked for a lock that the key would fit in. Lucy saw a little boy named Tim crying. He was sad because he lost his toy. Lucy wanted to help Tim. She tried to comfort him by giving him a hug. Tim felt a little better, but he still missed his toy. Lucy had an idea. She showed Tim the key she found. They looked for the lock together. They found a small box with a lock. Lucy used the key to open it. Inside the box was Tim's toy! Tim was very happy, and Lucy was glad she could help him. They played together and became good friends.
