Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy the Loyal Cow and the Wolf

Once upon a time, there was a loyal cow named Daisy. Daisy lived on a farm with her farmer, who she loved very much. They spent their days together, and Daisy always did what the farmer asked her to do. One day, while Daisy was eating grass, she saw a big, scary wolf. The wolf wanted Daisy to surrender and go with him. Daisy was scared, but she loved her farmer and didn't want to leave him. She thought of a plan to trick the wolf. Daisy told the wolf she would go with him, but first, she needed to eat more grass to get stronger. The wolf agreed and waited for Daisy to eat. While the wolf wasn't looking, Daisy ran as fast as she could back to her farmer. The wolf was surprised and couldn't catch her. Daisy's plan worked, and she stayed with her farmer, safe and sound.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a purple ball that she loved to play with. One day, she went to the park to play with her ball. She threw it up in the air and it went very high. As Lily ran to catch the ball, she saw a shiny key on the ground. She picked it up and looked around. She saw a small box under a big tree. Lily thought, "Maybe this key will open the box!" So, she put the key in the box and turned it. The box opened! Inside the box, Lily found a small, purple kitten. The kitten looked up at her and meowed. Lily was so happy to have a new friend. She picked up the kitten and her ball, and they ran home together to show her mom. From that day on, Lily and the purple kitten were always together, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a popular bee. The bee liked to play hide and seek with his friends. They would all laugh and have fun together. One day, while they were playing, the bee found a big tree to hide behind. He thought it was the best hiding spot ever. But then, something unexpected happened. The tree began to shake and make a loud noise. The bee and his friends were scared and ran away. But when they looked back, they saw it was just a friendly squirrel. The squirrel had been hiding in the tree too. They all laughed and the squirrel joined their game. Now, they had a new friend to play hide and seek with.

Once upon a time, there was a messy soldier named Tim. He lived in a small house with his dog, Max. Tim was not very good at cleaning, so his house was always a mess. Max liked to play in the mess and make it even messier. One day, Tim saw the sun shine very bright. He had an idea. "Max, let's clean the house today!" he said. Max wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" They started to clean the house together. Max picked up toys with his mouth, and Tim put them away. After they cleaned the house, Tim and Max went outside to play. The sun was still shining. They played catch and had lots of fun. When they went back inside, their house was clean and bright. Tim and Max were happy and tired, so they went to bed.

Once upon a time, there was a big smile on a little girl's face. She found a silver rock in the park. The girl was so happy. She wanted to show her friends the shiny rock. But, a big boy saw the girl with the silver rock. He wanted it too. The girl did not know the boy was not nice. She was ignorant of his mean ways. The boy walked up to the girl and asked for the rock. The girl said no, but the boy took it anyway. The girl was sad and her smile was gone. The big boy walked away with the silver rock. The girl cried and went home with a bad ending to her day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a human named Tom. Tom was a very miserable boy. He was always sad and did not like to play with others. One day, Tom found a magic wand. He waved the wand and said, "I wish I could shrink!" Suddenly, Tom became very small. He was as tiny as a bug. Tom was scared but he also felt excited. Tom met a little ant named Amy. Amy asked, "Why are you so sad, Tom?" Tom replied, "I am miserable because I am too small." Amy said, "Don't be sad. We can be friends and play together." They played all day long and had lots of fun. Tom was no longer miserable. He was happy to have a new friend.
