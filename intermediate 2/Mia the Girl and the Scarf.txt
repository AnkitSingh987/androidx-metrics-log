Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Scarf

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a pretty red scarf. But one day, the scarf became dirty. Mia was sad. Mia went to her mom and said, "Mom, my scarf is dirty. Can you help me?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia. Let's test something to clean it." They went to the kitchen and found a big bowl. They filled it with water and soap. Mia put her dirty scarf in the bowl. She moved it around and around. The scarf became clean! Mia was happy. She said, "Thank you, Mom! My scarf is clean now!" Her mom hugged her and said, "You're welcome, Mia. I'm glad we could make it clean again." They both smiled and Mia wore her pretty red scarf.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily saw a big tree with lots of apples. "Mom, can I pick some apples?" she asked. Her mom said, "Yes, but only if you are organized and put them in a bag." Lily picked apples and put them in a bag. She was very organized. When she had ten apples in the bag, her mom said, "Good job, Lily! Your reward is a big ice cream cone!" They went to the ice cream shop. But when they got there, they saw a big surprise. All the ice cream was gone! The shop owner said, "I am sorry, a dog came in and ate all the ice cream." Lily was sad, but her mom had an idea. They went home and made their own ice cream. Lily enjoyed her reward and shared it with her mom. They laughed and had a great time eating the ice cream they made together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a very intelligent dog named Buddy. Buddy was a happy dog who loved to help his owner, Tom. Tom had a big garden with many pretty flowers and trees. One sunny day, Tom was cleaning his garden with a rake. Buddy watched Tom and wanted to help. Buddy picked up a small rake with his mouth and started to clean too. They both worked together and had fun. Later, Tom made a small fire to burn the dry leaves. Buddy was careful around the fire. He knew it was hot and could hurt him. Tom and Buddy sat and watched the fire together. They felt good about their hard work in the garden.

One day, a little puppy named Spot was playing in the park. He saw a big dog named Max. Spot wanted to race Max to see who was faster. Spot said, "Max, let's race to the tree and back!" Max smiled and said, "Okay, Spot! Let's go!" They both got ready to race. They ran as fast as they could. Spot was fast, but Max was faster. Max won the race and they both had fun. After the race, Spot was tired but he felt fine. He said, "Max, you are very fast! Let's race again someday." Max agreed and they both went home. Spot and Max became good friends and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a young, small bird named Joe. Joe always wanted to explore the world, but his mum warned him not to go away too far. One day, he ignored his mum’s warning and flew off. Far away, he saw something interesting. He flew close to have a good look. Suddenly, he couldn’t see the way back home. Joe panicked and started to shout for help. A nice rabbit heard him. He asked: “What happened, young bird?”. Joe said: “I want to find my way back home, but I can't!” The rabbit said wisely: “Follow the sun to find your way home. That’s the only way to make sure you won’t disappear!” Joe thanked the rabbit and flew off looking for the sun. He finally came back home, with a lesson learned! Moral of the story: Never disappear from the view of family, because that’s the only way to make sure you’ll stay safe.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to help Mom in the kitchen. Mom is making soup for dinner. She asks Sara and Ben to set the table. Sara and Ben take the dishes from the cupboard. They put them on the table. They also take the spoons and the napkins. They are careful not to drop anything. Mom says, "You are very good helpers. Thank you, Sara and Ben." Sara and Ben smile. They like to make Mom happy. Mom brings the pot of soup to the table. She pours some soup into each dish. The soup is warm and smells good. Sara and Ben sit down and wait for Mom to join them. Mom says, "Let's say grace before we eat." Sara and Ben fold their hands and close their eyes. They say, "Thank you, God, for this food and this family. Amen." Mom says, "Enjoy your soup, Sara and Ben." Sara and Ben say, "Thank you, Mom." They pick up their spoons and start to eat. The soup is warm and tasty. They are happy and full.
