Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Yogurt

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They bought many things like apples, yogurt, and bread. Lily was happy because she loved yogurt. It was her favorite food. When they got home, Lily and her mom started to unpack the bags. Lily found the yogurt and said, "Mom, can I eat the yogurt now?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can have one now." Lily ate the yogurt and it tasted great. She was so happy that she shared her yogurt with her little brother, Tim. They both enjoyed the yogurt together and had a fun time.

Lily liked to play on the beach. She liked to make sand castles and look for shells. One day, she saw a crab on the sand. It was small and red and had big claws. Lily wanted to touch the crab. She was not afraid of it. She was independent and curious. She walked slowly to the crab and reached out her hand. The crab saw her and snapped its claws. "Ouch!" Lily said. She pulled back her hand. It hurt a lot. She started to cry. The crab ran away and hid under a rock. Mommy heard Lily cry and ran to her. She saw her hand was red and swollen. She picked up Lily and hugged her. "What happened, sweetie?" Mommy asked. "I touched a crab and it bit me," Lily said. "Oh, no. I'm sorry, honey. Crabs don't like to be touched. They are scared and defensive. You have to be careful with them," Mommy said. She took Lily to the water and washed her hand. Then she rubbed some cream on it. The cream made the pain go away. "Thank you, Mommy," Lily said. "You're welcome, Lily. I love you," Mommy said. They went back to their blanket and had a snack. Lily learned a lesson. She decided not to touch crabs anymore. She was still independent and curious, but she was also respectful and careful. She played with her sand castle and her shells and had a fun day on the beach.

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim was good at playing games. His most liked game was when he would shoot a ball into a hoop. He played this game with his friend, Sam. Sam was a reliable friend. They had many fun memories together. One day, Tim and Sam saw a big tree with a hole in it. They thought it looked like a hoop. They wanted to play their game with the tree. They smiled and knew this would be a fun memory. Tim and Sam found a ball and started to play. They took turns trying to shoot the ball into the hole in the tree. After many tries, Tim finally got the ball in the hole! Sam cheered and clapped for Tim. They both laughed and knew this was a great memory to keep.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy was very intelligent. He lived in a big tree with his family. Every day, he would rise with the sun and start his life in the tree. One day, Billy saw a big red ball on the ground. He wanted to play with it, but he was too small. So, he asked his big brother, Bobby, for help. Bobby was also very intelligent. Together, they pushed the big red ball with their beaks. They played with the big red ball all day long. They had so much fun! When the sun started to set, they knew it was time to rise up and fly back to their tree. They were happy and tired from their day of play. Billy and Bobby lived a good life in their tree, always being intelligent and helping each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that he loved very much. It was a big red cross. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sally with her toy. Her toy was a shiny gold star. Tim felt envious because he thought her toy was better. Tim wanted to have that shiny gold star. So, he took it from Sally when she was not looking. Sally looked for her toy, but could not find it. She was sad and began to weep. Tim saw her cry, but he did not want to give the shiny gold star back. In the end, Tim kept the shiny gold star and the big red cross. But he was not happy. He knew he did the wrong thing. Sally was still sad and cried. Tim wanted to say sorry, but he was too scared. And so, they both stayed sad and did not play together again.
