Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Tree

One day, Tom went for a walk. He saw a big ball in the sky. It was a mysterious planet. He wanted to go there. He put on his space hat and shoes. He was ready to step on the planet. Tom jumped very high. He was on the planet now. He saw a big tree. The tree had many yummy fruits. He took a fruit and ate it. It was so good. He was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The tree started to talk! It said, "Hello, Tom." Tom was very surprised. The tree was a friend. They played and had fun. The mysterious planet was not scary. It was a happy place.

---

One day, a cool cat went for a walk. He saw a little girl with a big smile on her face. The girl saw the cat and wanted to talk to him. She said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat liked the girl and said, "Yes, I want to play!" They played all day long, and they were very happy. The girl's face was full of joy, and the cat felt good too. They played with a ball, and they ran in the grass. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball they were playing with turned into a big, talking ball! The ball said, "I am a magic ball! I can make wishes come true!" The girl and the cat were very surprised. They looked at each other and smiled. They knew they would have many more fun days together with their new magic friend.

Once upon a time, in a far away place, there was a little planet. This planet was not big, not small, just ordinary. It had pretty trees, blue sky, and soft grass. The animals on the planet were happy and played all day. One day, a little bunny and a little bird met. They liked to play together. They ran, they jumped, and they sang. The bunny and the bird became best friends. They played from morning until the sun went down. But one day, the bird had to fly away to a new home. The bunny was sad. They hugged and said goodbye. The bird flew away, and the bunny watched. The bunny knew their playtime had to end. But they knew they would always be friends, even if they were on different planets.

One day, a little monkey named Momo was playing in the trees. Momo was very fragile, and he had to be careful not to fall. He loved swinging from branch to branch, laughing and having fun. As Momo was playing, he saw a big, round ball stuck in the tree. He wanted to play with the ball, so he reached out and tried to grab it. But the ball was too high, and Momo fell down. He hurt his arm and started to cry. A kind lady named Sue heard Momo's cries and came to help him. She looked at his arm and said, "Oh no, you have a boo-boo. Let me help you." Sue took a soft cloth and gently rubbed Momo's arm to make him feel better. "Thank you, Sue," said Momo, as his tears stopped. "You're very nice." Sue smiled and said, "You're welcome, Momo. Remember to be careful when you play, especially since you're so fragile." Momo nodded, and they both went to play with the ball together.

Anna liked to play with her cubes. She had many cubes of different colors and shapes. She could make towers, houses, and animals with her cubes. She was very happy with her cubes. But one day, her brother Tom came into her room. He saw her cubes and wanted to play with them too. He did not ask Anna. He just took some of her cubes and threw them around. He made a big mess and broke some of her towers. Anna was very sad and angry. She shouted at Tom and tried to get her cubes back. Tom did not listen to Anna. He laughed and threw more cubes. He said they were his cubes now. He was very mean and rude. Anna cried and ran to her mom. She told her mom what Tom did. She asked her mom to help her. Her mom hugged Anna and said she was sorry. She said she would talk to Tom and make him stop. She said she would help Anna clean up her room and fix her towers. She said she loved Anna very much. She said Anna was a charming girl who deserved to play with her cubes. Anna felt better and thanked her mom. She said she loved her mom too. She said she would pray for Tom to be nicer and share. She said she hoped Tom would learn to play with her cubes and not break them. She said she wanted to be friends with Tom and not fight. She said she was a good girl who liked to play with her cubes.
