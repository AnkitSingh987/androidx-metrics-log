Category: Intermediate
Title: The Incredible Prince and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was an incredible prince. He lived in a beautiful castle and everyday, he would take a walk in the forest. One day, he found something special- it was an icy rock. He touched it and the ice began to melt. The prince was amazed and he took the icy rock back to his castle. He showed it to all of his friends and they were amazed too! The prince thought the icy rock was so incredible that he decided to keep it forever. He put it in a safe place and it stayed there for many years. But one day, the prince heard a noise coming from the safe place. When he opened the door, he saw that the icy rock had melted! He smiled to himself and said, “What an incredible surprise!”

Once upon a time, there was a big maze. In the maze, there were many different paths. A little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue wanted to play in the maze. They hoped to find the way out together. As they walked, they saw a cat. The cat said, "I know the way out. Follow me." Tim and Sue followed the cat, but they got lost. The cat laughed and said, "I tricked you. I don't know the way out." Tim and Sue were sad. They tried to find the way out, but it was too hard. They hoped someone would help them, but no one came. In the end, Tim and Sue stayed lost in the maze.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play golf. One day, he saw a hungry squirrel in his yard. The squirrel was looking for food. Tim said to the squirrel, "Do you want to play golf with me?" The squirrel nodded and smiled. Tim lent the squirrel a small golf club. They played golf together and had lots of fun. After playing golf, Tim and the squirrel were very hungry. Tim's mom gave them yummy snacks. They ate and laughed together. From that day on, Tim and the squirrel were best friends.

Once upon a time, a tall girl named Lucy went for a walk. She wore her favorite red boots. Lucy loved her boots very much. They were shiny and pretty. As she walked, Lucy saw a small house. She heard a tiny voice inside. "Hello," said the voice. Lucy peeked through the window. She saw a little mouse in a hat. "Hi," Lucy said, "I like your hat." The mouse smiled and said, "Thank you, I like your boots." Lucy and the mouse became good friends. They played together every day. Lucy was happy to have a new friend. They laughed and had fun in the sun. And they both loved their hat and boots.

Once upon a time, there was a little child named Tim. Tim liked to be clean. Every day, Tim would prepare to go outside and play with his friends. He would wash his hands, put on his clean clothes, and brush his hair. One day, Tim went outside to play. He saw a big puddle of mud. Tim's friends were jumping in the puddle and having fun. Tim thought, "If I jump in the puddle, I won't be clean anymore." But he wanted to have fun with his friends, so he decided to jump in the puddle too. Tim got very dirty, but he had a lot of fun. When he got home, his mom helped him get clean again. Tim learned that sometimes it's okay to get dirty if it means having fun with your friends. The important thing is to always clean up after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy car that was very fast. Tim loved to play with his fast car all day. One day, he saw a hanger on the floor. He had an idea. Tim said to his car, "I hope we can make a fun game with this hanger." He put the hanger on the floor and used it like a ramp. The fast car went up the hanger and jumped high in the air. Tim's mom saw him playing and said, "That's a great idea, Tim! The hanger and your fast car make a fun game." Tim smiled and hoped he could find more fun things to play with.
