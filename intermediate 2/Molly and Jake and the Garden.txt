Title: Molly and Jake and the Garden

Molly and Jake were playing together in the garden. It was a sunny day and the grass was very soft and comfortable. They found a big pit filled with mud in the corner of the garden. Molly said to Jake, “Let’s pull the mud out of the pit!” So, they both grabbed a handful of mud and started pulling and pushing it out of the pit. They filled up their hands and kept going until the pit was empty. After they were done, they sat down in the soft grass and felt very comfortable. “That was lots of fun”, Jake said. “Yeah, it was! Let’s do it again tomorrow!” replied Molly.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run around all day. One day, Spot met a big dog named Max. Max was very friendly and wanted to play with Spot. "Hi, Spot," said Max. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Max," said Spot. "Let's play!" They played and played until they were very tired. Then, Spot's mom called for him. "Spot, it's time to go home!" she said. Spot didn't want to leave his new friend, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. "Max, I have to go home now," said Spot. "But can you stay at this spot and play with me tomorrow?" "Sure, Spot," said Max. "I will stay here and wait for you." The next day, Spot came back to the same spot, and Max was there waiting for him. They played and played again, and they became the best of friends. And that is how Spot and Max stayed friends forever.

One day, a little girl named Sue went outside to play. The sun was shining, making her feel warm and happy. She saw her friend, a small cat named Tom. Tom was wet because he had just played in a puddle. Sue said, "Tom, you are wet! Let me help you." She found a big, soft towel to wrap around Tom. The towel was pink and fluffy. Tom liked the towel and felt warm and dry. After Tom was dry, they played a fun game in the sun. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Then, Sue and Tom went inside to rest. They both had a great day.

Once there was a family that had to choose which pet to buy. They had to pick a reliable pet that would be fun to play with. After looking at lots of pets, they finally chose a puppy. The puppy was so small, fluffy and friendly. They knew he was reliable because he always shook hands with his new friends. The puppy was so excited to have a new home. He started to run around, exploring his new home and playing with his new family. The family took him for long walks and played lots of games with him. They were so happy to have him. He was always so reliable, always there to give them cuddles and kisses. The puppy was the best pick for the family and he was a reliable pal ever since.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn new things. One day, he went outside to play in his yard. Soon, he saw a big hole near the tree. He was very curious about the hole. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a hole in our yard. What is it for?" His mom smiled and said, "That hole is home for a little animal. It's not expensive, but it's important to them." Every day, Tim would go outside and look at the hole. He would learn about the animal living there. He found out it was a rabbit. Tim and the rabbit became good friends. They played and learned together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his red bike. One day, the sky was gloomy, but Tim still wanted to ride. Tim put on his yellow rain boots and went outside. He rode his bike up and down the street. Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue had a big cake. Sue asked Tim if he wanted some cake. Tim said "yes." Tim and Sue sat under a big tree. They ate the yummy cake together. The gloomy day became a happy day. Tim and Sue laughed and played. They rode their bikes until the sun went down.
