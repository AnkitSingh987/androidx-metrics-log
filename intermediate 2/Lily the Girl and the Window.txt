Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Window

One day, a little girl named Lily found a small cube in her room. It was a pretty cube with many colors. She liked the cube a lot and played with it every day. One day, while playing with her cube, Lily saw a scared kitten outside her window. The kitten was stuck in a tree and could not get down. Lily wanted to help the kitten, so she called her dad. "Daddy, the kitten is scared! Can we save it?" she asked. Her dad smiled and said, "Of course, Lily. Let's go save the kitten." They went outside and her dad climbed the tree. He carefully picked up the kitten and brought it down. The kitten was happy and not scared anymore. Lily held her cube close and felt happy that they saved the kitten.

---

Lily was a little girl who liked to do many things. She liked to draw, to sing, to play with her toys and to read books. But she did not like to sleep in her crib at night. She thought her crib was too small, too dark and too boring. One night, she heard a mysterious sound from her crib. It was like a soft whisper, but she could not understand what it said. She was curious and scared at the same time. She got out of her bed and tiptoed to her crib. She peeked inside and saw a tiny fairy sitting on her pillow. "Hello, Lily," the fairy said. "I am Faye, and I am here to be your friend. I can show you many wonderful things in your dreams, if you sleep in your crib." Lily was surprised and happy. She had never seen a fairy before. She wanted to see the wonderful things the fairy could show her. She asked, "What kind of things can you show me, Faye?" Faye smiled and said, "I can show you the stars, the moon, the flowers, the animals, the rainbows and more. I can also tell you stories and sing you songs. But you have to sleep in your crib, or I will disappear." Lily thought for a moment. She still did not like her crib very much, but she liked Faye a lot. She decided to give it a try. She said, "OK, Faye, I will sleep in my crib tonight. But you have to promise to stay with me and not leave me alone." Faye nodded and said, "I promise, Lily. I will stay with you and not leave you alone. Now, let's go to sleep and have some fun." Lily climbed into her crib and snuggled with Faye. She felt warm and safe. She closed her eyes and waited for Faye to show her the wonderful things in her dreams. She did not know that Faye was a magic fairy who could make her crib bigger, brighter and more comfortable. She did not know that Faye was sent by her mother, who wished for her daughter to sleep well and be happy. She only knew that she had a new friend who made her feel special. She smiled and fell asleep.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and climb on the equipment. But there was one equipment that they never tried. It was a big tower with a rope bridge and a slide. It looked scary. "Let's go to the tower," Tom said. He wanted to be brave. "No, it's too high and wobbly," Lily said. She was afraid. "Come on, it will be fun," Tom said. He took Lily's hand and pulled her to the tower. They climbed up the steps and reached the rope bridge. It was long and shaky. Tom went first and crossed the bridge. He waved at Lily. "Your turn," he said. Lily looked down and saw the ground far below. She felt dizzy and scared. She wanted to go back. "No, I can't," she said. She started to cry. Tom saw that Lily was sad and scared. He felt sorry for her. He crossed the bridge again and hugged her. "It's okay, Lily. You don't have to do it. Let's go back," he said. They climbed down the steps and ran to the slide. They slid down together and laughed. They played on the other equipment until it was time to go home. They had fun, but they never went to the tower again. The end.
