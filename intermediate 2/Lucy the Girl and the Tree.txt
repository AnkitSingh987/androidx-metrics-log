Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a soft kitten named Fluffy. They loved to play together. One day, Fluffy went outside to play, but he did not come back. Lucy was sad and wanted to find her kitten. She said to her mom, "I want to search for Fluffy." Her mom said, "Okay, let's go search for Fluffy together." They looked in the yard, under the big tree, and behind the bushes. They could not find Fluffy. Then, Lucy heard a soft "meow" from the little house. She looked inside and saw Fluffy! Lucy was so happy. She said, "I found you, Fluffy!" They went back home and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. He loved to play outside and find new things. One day, Tim went to the park to play. While at the park, Tim saw a big tree. He thought, "I want to climb that tree!" So, Tim climbed the tree and found a small box. He opened the box and saw a picture of a cartoon. The cartoon was funny and made Tim laugh. Tim showed the picture to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow! That's a cool cartoon!" Just then, the cartoon came to life! It jumped out of the picture and played with Tim and Sam. They all laughed and had a great time. The unexpected magic made their day even more adventurous.

Once upon a time, there was a soft cat named Kitty. Kitty had a job. Her job was to catch mice in the house. She liked her job very much. One day, Kitty saw a big mouse. She wanted to catch it, but it was too fast. The mouse ran away and hid. Kitty felt sad. She thought she spoiled her job. Kitty's friend, Doggy, saw her sad face. Doggy told Kitty not to worry. They would catch the mouse together. Kitty and Doggy worked as a team, and they caught the big mouse. Kitty was happy again. She did not spoil her job after all.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to hear stories from her mom. Every night, she would be eager to listen to a new story before going to sleep. One day, Lily found a letter in the mailbox. It was a letter from her friend, Sam. Sam lived far away, but they liked to send letters to each other. Lily was very happy to hear from Sam. Lily wrote a letter back to Sam. She told him about her day and how she loved to hear stories. She was eager to know if Sam liked stories too. Lily put the letter in the mailbox and waited for Sam's reply. They continued to send letters and share their love for stories with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to whistle all day long. He was very fast, and he liked to show off his speed to his friends. Tim made flying look easy. One sunny day, Tim wanted to play a game. He told his friends to race him to the big tree. Tim was sure he would win. He started to whistle and flew as fast as he could. Tim was so fast that he did not see the big spider web in his way. As Tim flew into the web, he got stuck. He tried to whistle, but he couldn't. His friends tried to help him, but the web was too strong. Tim was sad because he was not the winner. He learned that being fast and showing off was not always good.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small bunny named Ben. Ben loved to play in the open grass with his friends. One day, while playing, Ben saw a big tree with lots of yummy leaves. Ben wanted to eat the leaves, but the tree was very tall. Ben asked his friends for help, but they were too small to reach the leaves. Ben felt sad, but he did not give up. He thought, "I will find a way to get those leaves!" Ben looked around the forest and saw a big hill. He had an idea. Ben asked his friends to help him roll a big rock from the hill to the tall tree. They all pushed and pushed until the rock was under the tree. Ben climbed on the rock and reached the yummy leaves. He shared the leaves with his friends. Ben and his friends learned that if they care and work together, they can solve any problem.
