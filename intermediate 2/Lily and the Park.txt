Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Park

One sunny day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a boy who looked sad. Lily walked up to him and said, "Hi, my name is Lily. Why are you sad?" The boy looked at her and said, "Hi Lily, my name is Tom. I lost my toy car. I can't find it anywhere." Lily thought for a moment and then had an idea. "Let's look for your car together," she said with a smile. As they searched the park, they asked people if they had seen the toy car. No one mentioned seeing it. Tom started to feel sad again, but Lily stayed calm. She knew they would find the car if they kept looking. Finally, they found the toy car under a big tree. Tom was so happy that he hugged Lily. "Thank you for helping me find my car," he said with a big smile. From that day on, Lily and Tom were best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big open forest, there lived a kind bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop and play with his friends, the birds, and the squirrels. One sunny day, Bobo and his friends were playing when they heard a loud "strike!" They saw a big tree fall down. "Oh no!" said Bobo, "Who did this?" His friend, Squeaky the squirrel, said, "I saw a big man with a big stick. He hit the tree and it fell down." Bobo and his friends wanted to help the forest. They talked and said, "We need to make the forest safe again." They worked together to pick up the broken tree parts. They made a big pile of wood. After they finished, they were very tired but they were happy. The forest was safe and clean again. Bobo and his friends played and hopped around. They knew they had done a good job.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He found a big bottle with a thing to spray. He thought it would be fun to spray his bed. So, he sprayed the mattress all over. His mom heard the noise and came into the room. She saw the wet mattress and said, "Oh no, Tim! You made the bed all wet and miserable. Now you can't sleep on it tonight." Tim felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I didn't know it would make the bed wet." Just then, Tim's dad came into the room. He looked at the wet mattress and smiled. He said, "Wait a minute! That's not water you sprayed, that's a magic spray that makes things grow!" They all watched as the mattress grew bigger and bigger. Tim's bed was now the biggest bed in the house. Tim and his parents laughed and played on the big bed together.

One day, a little infant named Tim was playing in the park. He was very alert and liked to watch the birds and the trees. Tim saw a funny man who liked to joke. The funny man wore a big hat and had a big smile. The funny man said, "Hi Tim, do you want to hear a joke?" Tim nodded and smiled. The funny man told a joke about a cat that liked to eat cheese. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. The funny man said, "I have a surprise for you, but first, you need to close your eyes." Tim closed his eyes and waited. He heard the funny man walk away and then come back. When Tim opened his eyes, he saw a big, colorful ball. The funny man said, "I told you I had a surprise! Now we can play with the ball together." Tim was so happy and they played with the ball all day.

Once upon a time, there was a kind little girl named Lily. She had an incredible big tree in her garden. The tree had many branches and it gave a lot of shade. Lily loved to play under the tree with her toys and her dog, Max. One hot day, Lily saw a tired bird under the tree. The bird was thirsty and needed water. Lily promised the bird she would help it. She went inside her house and got a bowl of water for the bird. The bird drank the water and felt much better. Lily and the bird became good friends. They played under the tree every day in the shade. Lily learned that it was important to help others who were in need. She promised to always be kind and help others. And the bird promised to always be there for Lily. They both lived happily under the incredible tree, and their friendship grew stronger each day.
