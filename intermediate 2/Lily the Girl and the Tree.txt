Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a bright, happy town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to ask questions. She asked her mom, her dad, and her friends. But sometimes, they did not know the answer. Lily felt sad when she did not get an answer. One day, Lily heard about a wise old owl that lived in a big tree. The owl knew many things and could answer any question. Lily wanted to meet the wise owl. She thought, "If I ask the owl my questions, I will always get an answer." Lily walked to the big tree where the owl lived. She called out, "Hello, wise owl! Can you help me?" The owl looked down and said, "Welcome, little girl. What do you want to know?" Lily asked the owl many questions. The owl knew all the answers. Lily was so happy! From that day on, Lily visited the wise old owl every day. She always got the answers she wanted. Lily and the owl became good friends. And the bright, happy town became even happier because Lily shared her answers with everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a bright doll. She liked to zoom around the room. She zoomed so fast that she ran all the way to the other side of the room. She was so happy when she reached the other side that she laughed and laughed. But then she saw a ball in the corner and wanted to zoom over and get it. But she was tired from running so quickly, so she had to rest for a few minutes first. So she sat down and closed her eyes. After a few minutes, the bright doll felt better. She stood up and zoomed across the room. She zoomed so fast she felt like she was flying, and she even went higher up than the ceiling! Finally, she reached the ball, and she laughed and said, "I zoomed so fast, I even flew!" She was so proud and happy to have zoomed so fast, that she picked up the ball and zoomed all the way back to the other side of the room. She was so happy that she zoomed and zoomed until she went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Sam. Sam liked to take naps all day long. One day, while Sam was napping under a tree, a tiny bird came and sat on the tree. The bird saw Sam and wanted to be friends. The bird said, "Hi, cat! I am Birdy. Do you want to play with me?" But Sam was too sleepy to play. He said, "Please, do not disturb me, Birdy. I want to sleep now." Birdy wanted to help Sam sleep better. She saw a pin on the ground. Birdy thought that if she put the pin under Sam's head, it would be like a small pillow. So, Birdy picked up the pin with her beak and placed it under Sam's head. But the pin was not soft like a pillow. It poked Sam's head and woke him up! Sam said, "Ouch! That hurt!" Birdy felt bad and said sorry to Sam. She took the pin away and found a real pillow for Sam. Sam was happy with his new pillow. He said, "Thank you, Birdy. Now I can sleep well." And so, Sam and Birdy became good friends. They played together when Sam was not sleepy, and Birdy helped Sam sleep when he was tired.

One day, a zebra named Zara went to the lake. Zara saw her friend, a bird named Bob. Bob said, "Let's dive in the water!" Zara was scared, but she wanted to try. Zara and Bob went to the edge of the lake. Zara closed her eyes and jumped. She went under the water and then came back up. "That was fun!" Zara said. Bob was happy that Zara liked it. As they played, they saw a big log. It looked dead. But then, the log moved! It was not a log, it was a big snake! Zara and Bob were scared. They ran away fast. The snake did not follow them. They were safe and went back to play in the water.
