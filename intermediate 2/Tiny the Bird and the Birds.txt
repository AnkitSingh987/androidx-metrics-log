Category: Intermediate
Title: Tiny the Bird and the Birds

Once upon a time, there was a small bird named Tiny. Tiny lived in a wood, where he had many friends. He liked to play with them all day long. One day, Tiny saw a big bird ordering some small birds around. The big bird wanted them to bring him food. Tiny felt bad for the small birds and wanted to help them. So, Tiny went to the big bird and asked him to be nice to the small birds. The big bird listened to Tiny and stopped ordering them around. From that day on, all the birds in the wood lived happily and played together.

---

One day, a bald man named Tom went to the beach. He liked to dive in the water. Tom saw a big wreck in the water. The wreck was old and had many fish. Tom met a new friend named Jane. Jane liked to dive too. Tom said, "Let's dive in the water and see the wreck!" Jane said, "Yes, let's go!" They both dived into the water. They saw the wreck and many fish. They played with the fish and had fun. Tom and Jane were happy. They had a good day at the beach.

---

One day, a dog named Sam wanted to serve food to his friends. He had a black bowl to put the food in. Sam thought it was difficult to find food for everyone, but he tried his best. Sam met his friend, Cat. "Do you want to help me?" Sam asked. Cat said, "Yes, let's find food to serve our friends!" They looked for food together and found some in a big box. They put the food in the black bowl and went to serve their friends. But, surprise! The food was really toys! Sam and Cat laughed and played with the toys instead. Their friends joined them, and they all had a fun day together.

---

Once upon a time, there was a dinosaur named Dino. Dino loved to mix things in his big bowl. One day, he found some red, green, and yellow things to mix. He put them all in his bowl and mixed them together. It made a new, pretty color. Dino's friend, Birdy, came over to play. "What are you doing, Dino?" Birdy asked. "I am mixing colors," Dino said. "I made a new one. It is pretty." Birdy looked at the new color and liked it too. Dino and Birdy wanted to taste the new color. They put a little bit on their tongues. "Oh, it is spicy!" Dino said. Birdy agreed, "Yes, very spicy!" They laughed and played with the spicy mix all day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly monkey named Momo. Momo lived in a big tree with lots of vines. He loved to swing on the vines all day long. One day, Momo met a little bird named Bibi. Bibi was sad. "I don't know how to swing on the vines," she said. Momo wanted to help his new friend. Momo said, "I will teach you how to swing on the vines!" They tried and tried, but Bibi was still scared. Momo had an idea. He held Bibi's wing and together they swung on the vines. Bibi was happy and not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a charming pirate named Bob. He had a big boat and a funny hat. Bob liked to find treasure and play with his friends. One day, Bob got a letter. It said, "Please send help. We need you, Bob." So, Bob and his friends got on their boat to go help. When they got there, they saw a little girl. She said, "Thank you for coming, Bob. Can you help me find my lost toy?" Bob and his friends looked and looked. They found the toy and the little girl was happy. They all played together and had fun.

---

Once upon a time, there was a lovely girl named Lily. She had a pretty skirt that she loved to wear. It was her favorite because it was pink and had flowers on it. One sunny day, Lily wanted to help her mom. Her mom was busy polishing the table to make it shiny and clean. Lily asked if she could help polish, too. Her mom said yes and gave her a soft cloth to use. Lily was very happy to help. She polished the table carefully, making sure not to get her pretty skirt dirty. When they were done, the table looked shiny and new. Lily's mom was proud of her and they both had big smiles on their faces.
