Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He was very tired, but he wanted to play. Tim saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. While he was sitting, he heard a sound. It was a tiny bird singing a song. The bird had a note in its beak. "Hi, bird," said Tim. "What is that note for?" The bird gave the note to Tim. Tim looked at the note, but he could not read it. He asked the bird, "Can you help me read this note?" Just then, something unexpected happened. The bird opened its beak and said, "Freeze!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that birds could talk! Tim did what the bird said and froze. The bird read the note out loud. It said, "You have found a magic bird. Say the word 'unfreeze' and you can move again." Tim said, "Unfreeze!" and he could move again. He was so happy to meet a magic bird. Tim and the bird played in the park all day, and they became good friends.

John and Jill were getting married. On their special day, John smiled at Jill and waved. Jill waved back, smiling too. Suddenly a cool breeze blew. John and Jill both looked up. Above them, the clouds were white and fluffy. It looked like the clouds were waving too! John turned to Jill and said, "It's like the sky is waving to us!" Jill nodded and replied, "It's magical! This day is perfect!" John and Jill smiled at each other and hugged. Their family and friends cheered. Marriage was the coolest thing ever.

One day, in a big forest, there lived a panda named Pandy. Pandy was upset because he had a big boo-boo on his leg. He didn't know how to make it heal. He sat under a tree and started to cry. A little bird named Chirpy heard Pandy crying. Chirpy flew down and asked, "Why are you upset, Pandy?" Pandy showed Chirpy his boo-boo and said, "My leg hurts and I don't know how to heal it." Chirpy knew what to do. She told Pandy, "Let's find a big leaf and put it on your boo-boo. It will help you heal." They found a big leaf and put it on Pandy's leg. Soon, Pandy started to feel better. He was so happy that his new friend Chirpy helped him heal his boo-boo. From that day on, Pandy and Chirpy were best friends. They played together in the big forest all the time. Pandy was never upset again because he knew Chirpy would always be there to help him.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy bear. He always slept in his bed and never did anything. One day, some birds said to him, "Let's go and have some fun!" But the lazy bear said, "No, I don't want to do anything today." The birds did not give up and kept trying to convince him. Finally, the lazy bear agreed to go outside. As soon as they arrived, they saw some toast spread on a plate. The birds wanted to eat it, but the lazy bear said, "Let us spread it all around so that everyone can share it." So they did. They spread the toast everywhere until it was all gone. When they were done, all the animals were happy. They had a fun day, playing and eating the toast. The lazy bear, however, was even lazier than before. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Tom. Tom liked to take things from the kitchen. One day, Tom saw a big box with a label on it. The label had a picture of a fish. Tom loved fish. Tom wanted to take the box to his bed. He tried to take the box, but it was too big. Tom was sad. He wanted the fish inside the box. He looked at the label again and thought of a plan. Tom called his friend, Sam the dog. Sam was not shy. Sam helped Tom take the box to his bed. They opened the box and shared the fish inside. Tom was very happy. Tom and Sam ate the fish and played all day.
