Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wealthy Man and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big house and many toys. One day, he saw a ladder in his garden. A little bird lived on the ladder. The bird said, "Please, can I have some food?" The man shared his food with the bird. They became good friends. The bird taught the man to be kind. The man kissed the bird and said, "Thank you for being my friend." The bird was happy too. Now, the wealthy man shared his toys with other kids. Everyone loved him. They all played together and had fun. The moral of the story is that sharing and being kind makes us happier and loved by others.

One day, a small fish named Bob wanted to go for a swim. Bob loved to swim in the big blue sea. He liked to play with his friends, the other fish. They would swim and play all day long. One day, while Bob was swimming, he saw something disgusting. It was a big, dirty bag. The bag was floating in the water, making the sea look not nice. Bob did not like the disgusting bag. He wanted to make the sea clean and pretty again. Bob had an idea. He asked his friends to help him shut the bag. They all swam together and pushed the bag to the shore. They shut the bag tight so the dirty things would not come out. The sea was clean and pretty again. Bob and his friends were happy they could swim and play in the clean sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved the ocean very much. He decided to prepare to go sailing on the ocean. "I'm all ready," he said, as he hopped into the boat with his hat, a fishing rod and his smile. He sailed off towards the ocean and began to feel the sea air on his face. He was so excited, he couldn't wait to see all the sea creatures. But soon something went wrong. The waves were too big, and the boat tipped over in the water. The little boy was struggling to keep his head above water. Suddenly, he heard a voice. "Hold on, I'm coming!" it said. But it was too late. By the time the rescue team came, the little boy was already gone. The ocean had taken him. The end.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man named Tom. Tom had a wallet that he loved very much. One day, Tom went for a walk in the park with his wallet. While walking, Tom saw a big ice cream truck. He wanted to buy an ice cream, so he took out his wallet. But, oh no! The wallet was so cold, it started to freeze! Tom was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, a nice lady saw Tom and his cold wallet. She took Tom's hand and helped him warm up the wallet. The wallet stopped freezing and Tom was happy. He bought an ice cream and shared it with the nice lady. They became good friends and went for walks together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a big tree. Many animals lived near the tree. One hot day, a little lion was playing under the tree. He was very happy. While the little lion was playing, he heard a loud roar! He was scared. He thought it was a big lion. He ran away to hide behind another tree. The little lion was very quiet. But then, he saw that it was not a big lion. It was just a tiny frog! The frog had made the loud roar. The little lion was surprised. He laughed and played with the frog. They became good friends under the big tree.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a statue. The statue was a big man with a funny hat. People liked to look at the statue and take pictures with it. The statue was very proud and happy to make people smile. One day, a little bird came to the park. The bird was very hungry. It saw the statue and thought it was food. The bird tried to eat the statue, but it was too hard. The bird was confused and kept trying to eat it. The people in the park saw the bird and laughed. The statue felt embarrassed because it was not used to being laughed at. But soon, the bird realized it could not eat the statue and flew away. The people stopped laughing, and the statue was happy again. The statue knew it was not food, but a special thing for people to enjoy.
