Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Park

Max was walking in the park with his parents when he found a rail. He was so excited that he ran over to his parents with it, telling them to take a look. His dad looked closely at the rail and said it was very special. He told Max that it was an original railroad rail - the very first kind of rail made. Max was very happy. He had never seen anything like it before. But then his mom said that it was too dangerous to keep. She told him he had to leave it where he found it. Max started to cry. He wanted to keep this special rail so much. His dad reassured him, saying that he could come back to the park and visit it anytime he wanted. So, Max left the rail in the park. Although he was a bit sad, he felt happy that the original rail was back in its home in the park. Max smiled and said, “Let’s go home and find something else special!”

Lily and Anna were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and make them pretty. One day, Lily got a new doll from her grandma. It had long hair, a pink dress and shiny shoes. Lily was very happy. "Look at my new doll!" Lily said to Anna. "She is so pretty. I want to decorate her hair with ribbons and flowers." Anna looked at the new doll. She felt a bad feeling in her chest. She wanted a new doll too. Her doll was old and had a tear in her dress. She was jealous of Lily. "I don't like your new doll," Anna said. "She is not pretty. She is ugly and boring. My doll is better." Lily was hurt by Anna's words. She loved her new doll and her old doll. She did not want to make Anna sad. She wanted to share with her. "No, Anna, your doll is not better. She is different. She is pretty too. And she is your friend. We can decorate both dolls and make them happy. Do you want to play with me?" Anna thought for a moment. She knew she was wrong. She was sorry for being mean to Lily and her new doll. She wanted to be a good friend. "Yes, Lily, I want to play with you. I'm sorry for being mean. Can I see your new doll? She is pretty. And can I give my doll a new dress?" Lily smiled and hugged Anna. She was glad they were friends again. She gave Anna some ribbons and flowers. They decorated both dolls and made them happy. They played together and had fun.

Anna likes to play with her dolls. She has many dolls, but her favorite is Lily. Lily has long hair, a pink dress and a flexible lip. Anna can put Lily's lip up or down to make her smile or frown. One day, Anna and Lily are having a tea party in the garden. Anna puts some water in a cup and gives it to Lily. She says, "Here, Lily, drink some tea. It is very yummy." She puts Lily's lip up to make her smile. But then, a big wind comes and blows Lily's hat away. Lily's hat is also pink and has a flower on it. Anna and Lily love the hat. Anna says, "Oh no, Lily, your hat is gone! We have to find it!" She puts Lily's lip down to make her frown. Anna and Lily run after the hat. They see it flying over the fence, into the neighbor's yard. The neighbor has a big dog. The dog sees the hat and thinks it is a toy. He grabs the hat with his teeth and shakes it. Anna and Lily are very sad. They cry and hug each other. They say, "Bye bye, hat. We will miss you." They go back to the garden and sit on the grass. They put their lips down and make a sad face. But then, they hear a voice. It is the neighbor. He says, "Hello, Anna and Lily. I saw what happened to your hat. I am very sorry. The dog did not mean to hurt it. He just wanted to play. Here, I have something for you." He comes over the fence and gives them a new hat. The hat is also pink and has a flower on it. It is even prettier than the old hat. Anna and Lily are very happy. They say, "Thank you, neighbor. You are very nice. We love the new hat." They put the hat on Lily's head and smile. They put their lips up and make a happy face. They have a tea party again and enjoy the sun. They are very glad they have a flexible lip. They can show how they feel.
