Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Orange Bug and the Bird

One day, a small orange bug named Tim went to the park. He saw a big squash. Tim thought, "I want to eat it!" He went closer to the squash. Tim heard a voice say, "Don't eat it!" Tim looked around and saw a big bird. The bird said, "If you eat the squash, you will regret it." Tim did not listen. He took a big bite of the squash. Suddenly, the squash started to shake. Tim was scared. Out of the squash came a tiny fairy. The fairy said, "Thank you for letting me out! I was stuck in there!" Tim was happy he did not listen to the bird. The fairy gave Tim a big hug. They became good friends and played in the park all day.

One day, a big, reliable dog named Max sat on the porch. He liked to watch the birds and cars go by. Max was a very good dog who always listened to his mom and dad. One day, a little girl named Lily walked by Max's house. She saw Max on the porch and said, "Hello, big dog! Can you come play with me?" Max looked at Lily and then paused. He knew he had to stay on the porch, but he really wanted to play with Lily. Just then, a big wind came and blew Lily's hat into the air. It went up and up, and then fell into a tree. Lily was sad and said, "Oh no! My hat is stuck!" Max wanted to help, so he jumped off the porch and ran to the tree. He jumped high and got the hat. Lily was so happy and said, "Thank you, Max! You are a very good dog!" Max wagged his tail and went back to the porch, knowing he did a good thing.

One day, a big dog named Bob lived on a street. Bob was a tough dog who liked to play and run. He had many friends on the street who liked to play with him. One sunny day, Bob went out to play with his friends. They played catch and ran around. But after a while, Bob felt tired. He let out a big sigh and sat down under a tree to rest. As Bob sat under the tree, a little girl came by. She saw the tough dog and wanted to play with him. Bob was happy to have a new friend. They played together and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Bob and the little girl walked home, ready for a good night's sleep.

One day, a helpful king lived in a big castle. He had a special chamber where he liked to order food for his friends. He loved to share and make his friends happy. The king had a big party coming up, and he wanted to make it the best party ever. Before the party, the king talked to his cook. "I want to order a big cake for my friends," he said. The cook nodded and went to make the cake. The king was excited, but he knew he needed to wait for the party to see the cake. At the party, all of the king's friends came to the castle. They played games and had fun. Then, the king took them to the special chamber. There, they saw the big cake the king had ordered. Everyone was so happy and thanked the helpful king. The party was a big success, and everyone had a great time.

In the distance, there was a big house with a big garden. Every morning, the sun rose and shone. In the garden, something magical happened. A large, yellow flower began to bloom. One day a little girl called Clara saw the flower from the window in her house. She was so excited that she ran out the door and over to the garden. When Clara looked closely at the flower, she noticed something special about it. Every petal of the flower was shaped like a cushion! Clara smiled and said to the flower: "Dear flower, you look like a couch! I want to take a nap on you". The flower smiled and replied: "Yes, little girl, you may lie on me. I will keep you safe and sound while you have your nap". Clara jumped with joy and lay down on the flower. She had a wonderful sleep in the distant garden.
