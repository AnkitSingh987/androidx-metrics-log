Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the River

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia was a flexible girl who could bend and twist like a snake. One day, Mia wanted to go on a trip to see her friend, Tom. Mia needed to pass a big river to get to Tom's house. She looked for her passport, a special paper that would let her go across the river. Mia found her passport under her bed and was very happy. Mia went to the river and showed her passport to the boatman. The boatman let her pass and took her across the river. Mia was so flexible that she could sit in the boat in many fun ways. When they got to the other side, Mia thanked the boatman and went to see her friend Tom. They had a great day playing together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play outside. She saw a big pile of mud. Lucy was a naughty girl, and she loved to play in the mud. She jumped into the mud and got very dirty. Lucy's mom saw her playing in the mud. She was not happy. She told Lucy to pray and say sorry for being naughty. Lucy closed her eyes and prayed. She said sorry for playing in the mud. Then, something unexpected happened. When Lucy opened her eyes, the mud was gone! She was clean again. Lucy was surprised and happy. She learned that praying can help her when she is naughty. From that day on, Lucy tried to be a good girl and always said her prayers.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to be independent. He wanted to make food all by himself. So, he went to the kitchen and found a pan. He put the pan on the stove and turned it on. He wanted to cook an egg. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, be careful! The stove can burn you. Let me help you." But Tim said, "No, Mom! I want to do it by myself." As Tim tried to cook the egg, he saw a sink full of water. He thought it would be fun to put water in the pan. But when he did, the hot pan hissed and the water jumped out! The water went everywhere and made a big mess. Tim was surprised and a little scared. Tim looked at his mom and said, "Mom, I'm sorry. I guess I need your help after all." His mom smiled and said, "That's okay, Tim. We can learn to be independent together." And so, Tim and his mom cleaned up the mess and cooked the egg together. Tim learned that it's good to be independent, but sometimes it's okay to ask for help.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to clean his room. One day, while cleaning, he found a big, expensive box. The box was hidden under his bed. Tim was very happy to find the box. He wanted to know what was inside. Tim opened the box and found a big sausage. He was very surprised to see the sausage. He thought, "Why is there a sausage in an expensive box?" Tim wanted to eat the sausage, but he was not sure if it was good to eat. Tim took the sausage to his mom. His mom looked at the sausage and said, "This is a magic sausage!" Tim was very excited. He asked his mom what the magic sausage could do. His mom told him that the sausage could clean anything. Tim was so happy. He used the magic sausage to clean his room and make it sparkle. And from that day on, Tim and his magic sausage lived happily ever after.

One day, a big red ball went high up in the sky. It went so high that it could not come back down. A little girl named Sue saw the ball and wanted to get it back. She thought and thought about how to get the ball down. Sue had an idea. She went to her dad's car and saw a can of gas. She knew that gas made cars go fast. She thought maybe the gas could help her get the ball down too. So, she took the can of gas and rubbed it on her hands. Sue jumped and jumped, trying to reach the high red ball. But, the gas did not help her jump high like she thought it would. Instead, her hands felt very slippery. She slipped and fell on the ground. Suddenly, the ball started to come down. It bounced on the ground and rolled to Sue. She was happy to have her ball back and learned that gas does not help you jump high.
