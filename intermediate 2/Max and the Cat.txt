Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a brave dog named Max. Max loved to help people and animals. One day, Max saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was in trouble and needed help. Max said, "Don't worry, cat, I will help you!" He urged the cat to jump from the tree into his arms. The cat was scared, but Max told the cat, "Be brave, just like me!" The cat jumped, and Max caught the cat. The cat said, "Thank you, Max! You are a brave dog and a good friend!" They became best friends and had many fun days together. And whenever someone was in trouble, brave Max was there to help.

Once upon a time, there was a monkey. The monkey loved to eat tasty food. One day, the monkey found a big tree with tasty fruit. The monkey was very happy and climbed the tree to eat the fruit. While the monkey was eating, a big wind came. The wind was so strong that it made the tree shake. The monkey was scared and held the tree tight. Then, the wind did something unexpected. The wind made the tree strike a big rock. The rock broke and out came a lot of tasty food. The monkey was surprised and happy. The monkey ate the tasty food and shared it with all his friends. From that day on, the monkey and his friends had a lot of tasty food to eat. They were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big pond. One day, he saw something red in the water. It was a radish! Fin thought it was a good thing to eat. Fin wanted to eat the radish, but it was too big for him. So, he swam to his friend, the frog. The frog was good at eating big things. Fin told the frog about the radish and asked for help. The frog agreed to help Fin. They swam back to the radish together. The frog took a big bite and shared it with Fin. They both enjoyed the good radish. From that day on, Fin and the frog were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ann. Ann was an independent girl who loved to look at the landscape. She saw trees, flowers, and animals. Ann liked to walk and talk to her friends in the forest. One day, Ann met a frog. The frog said, "Hi, Ann! I am a prince. If you marry me, I will become a prince again." Ann thought about it and said, "Okay, I will marry you." They had a small party with their friends in the forest. But, something unexpected happened. When Ann married the frog, he did not turn into a prince. Instead, Ann turned into a frog too! Now, they were both frogs. They laughed and played in the forest. Ann and the frog prince were happy together, and they loved their new life as frogs.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer called Jack. He was very impatient and wanted to get the work done quickly. Every week, he had to load hay onto his tractor. One day, he was late loading the hay and his wife scolded him. Jack was very frustrated and couldn't stand it anymore. He grumbled to himself and said, “I have to take this pill to calm down.” His wife, who was nearby, heard him and came to him. She said, “What pill are you taking?” Jack replied, “It’s a pill to help me relax. I get too impatient sometimes.” His wife smiled and said, “You don’t need a pill, you just need to take your time and not rush.” Jack smiled and said, “You’re right. I should take my time and not be so impatient.” He finished loading the hay and went off to do the rest of his work - this time much calmer.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown pigeon. The pigeon lived in a small nest on a tree. He had many friends, but he felt sad. His nest was too small, and he did not fit in it. One day, the pigeon met a big bird. The big bird said, "Why are you sad, little pigeon?" The pigeon told the big bird about his small nest. The big bird said, "I can help you make a bigger nest." The pigeon and the big bird worked together to make a new nest. They used sticks and leaves to make it big and strong. The pigeon was very happy because he could fit in his new nest. But then, something unexpected happened. The big bird tried to fit in the new nest too. But the big bird was too big, and the nest broke! The pigeon and the big bird laughed. They knew they had to make two nests, one for each of them. So they did, and they both fit in their nests. They lived happily as good friends.
