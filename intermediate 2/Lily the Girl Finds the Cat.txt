Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a big box. Inside the box, there was a statue. It was an original statue. Lily wanted to unpack the box and see the statue. Lily opened the box. She saw the statue. It was a cat. The cat was very pretty. It was an original cat statue. Lily was very happy. Lily showed the cat statue to her friends. They liked it too. They played with the cat statue all day. They had a lot of fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play at the park every day. They loved to go on the slide, the swings, and the seesaw. They had so much fun together. One day, they saw a new slide at the park. It was very big and shiny. It looked amazing. Lily and Ben wanted to try it. They ran to the slide and climbed up the ladder. "Are you ready, Ben?" Lily asked. "Yes, I am, Lily," Ben said. They sat on the slide and held hands. They smiled at each other. They were very excited. They pushed off and slid down the slide. It was very fast and smooth. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They laughed and screamed. But then, something bad happened. The slide had a sharp turn at the end. Lily and Ben did not see it. They flew off the slide and landed on the ground. They hit their heads and their arms and their legs. They felt a lot of pain. They cried and cried. A lady who worked at the park heard them and came to help. She called their moms and dads. She said they had to go to the hospital. She said they had to be careful on the new slide. She said it was not safe for little kids. Lily and Ben looked at each other. They were scared and sad. They wished they had not gone on the new slide. They wished they had stayed on the old slide. They wished they had listened to their moms and dads. They shrugged and said sorry to each other. They hoped they would be okay. They hoped they would still be best friends. They hoped they would play at the park again. But they did not know. They did not know what would happen next.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the park. They played on the swings and slides. They saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tom thought they should climb the tree from the left side, but Sue wanted to climb from the right side. They were confused about what to do. Tom said, "I think the left side is better." Sue disagreed and said, "No, the right side is better." They could not agree on which side to climb. This made them sad. Then, a big bird flew down from the tree. It had a saw in its beak. The bird sawed a small branch from the tree and put it on the ground. Tom and Sue smiled and decided to use the branch to help them climb. They both climbed the tree together and had lots of fun.

Sara was a shy girl who liked to draw. She had many pictures in her folder. She wanted to show them to her teacher, but she was afraid of talking in front of the class. One day, her teacher said they would have a show and tell. She asked the children to prepare something to share with their friends. Sara thought she could bring her folder and show her pictures. She liked her pictures very much. She hoped her friends would like them too. She took her folder home and looked at her pictures. She saw a picture of a flower, a picture of a cat, a picture of a rainbow, and a picture of her family. She smiled and put them back in her folder. She was ready for the show and tell. The next day, she brought her folder to school. She waited for her turn. She saw her friends share their toys, their books, their clothes, and their hobbies. They talked and laughed and clapped. Sara felt nervous and scared. She wished she could hide. When it was her turn, she walked to the front of the class. She held her folder tightly. She looked at her teacher and whispered, "I have pictures to show." Her teacher smiled and said, "That's wonderful, Sara. Let's see your pictures." Sara opened her folder and showed her pictures one by one. She told the class what they were and why she drew them. She said, "This is a flower. I like flowers because they are pretty and smell good. This is a cat. I have a cat at home. His name is Fluffy. He is soft and cuddly. This is a rainbow. I saw a rainbow after the rain. It had many colors and made me happy. This is my family. I love my family. They are kind and fun." The class listened and looked at her pictures. They liked her pictures very much. They said, "Wow, Sara, you are a good drawer. Your pictures are beautiful and interesting. Thank you for sharing them with us." Sara felt happy and proud. She smiled and said, "Thank you for listening and liking my pictures. You are nice and friendly." She put her pictures back in her folder and went back to her seat. She felt less shy and more confident. She learned that sharing her pictures was not so scary after all. She learned that her friends were supportive and curious. She learned that she had something valuable and unique to offer. She learned that she could be brave and creative.
