Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat liked to sail on the water. One day, the boat sailed to a new place. It was spring, so the sun was out, and the flowers were blooming. In this new place, the boat met a big fish. The fish was very dangerous. The boat was scared. The fish tried to chase the boat, but the boat was fast. The little boat sailed away from the dangerous fish. It went back to its safe place. The boat was happy it was safe. From that day on, the little boat always remembered to be careful when it sailed to new places.

Once upon a time, a fast rabbit and a slow turtle had a race. They wanted to see who could arrive at the big tree first. The winner would get a shiny coin. The fast rabbit ran very fast. He was sure he would win the race. The slow turtle walked very slow. The fast rabbit thought he had time to take a nap. He slept under a tree. When the fast rabbit woke up, he saw the slow turtle near the big tree. The fast rabbit ran as fast as he could, but it was too late. The slow turtle arrived at the big tree first and got the shiny coin. The fast rabbit was sad because he did not win the shiny coin.

One day, a girl named Sue went on a trip. She was very happy. She went to a big farm. There, she saw a regular cat. The cat said, "Welcome to the farm!" Sue was surprised that the cat could talk. Sue and the cat walked around the farm. They saw cows, pigs, and sheep. Then, they saw a big red barn. Inside the barn, there was a party! All the animals were dancing and having fun. The cat said, "This is not a regular farm. It is a magic farm!" Sue danced with the animals. She had so much fun. Then, the cat said, "It is time to go home." Sue was sad, but she knew she would never forget her trip to the magic farm.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a wolf. This wolf loved to keep his house clean and nice. He would sweep the floor, wash the dishes, and make his bed every day. One day, the wolf heard a sound outside. He went to see what it was. There, he saw a little bird singing a song. The wolf liked the song and wanted to learn it. So, he asked the bird to repeat the song for him. The bird sang the song again and again. The wolf tried to sing along, but he couldn't get the words right. He asked the bird to repeat the song one more time. This time, the wolf sang the song perfectly. They both sang the song together and had lots of fun. From that day on, the wolf and the bird became best friends, and they sang songs together every day.

Once upon a time, in a land with lots of nature, there was a white bird. The bird loved to fly high in the sky and play with other birds. One day, the white bird met a little girl named Lily. Lily said, "Hi, bird! I like to write. Can you write too?" The white bird shook its head. It could not write like Lily. But Lily had an idea. She took a stick and started to write in the dirt. The white bird watched as Lily wrote her name. Then, the bird took the stick in its beak and tried to write too. It was hard, but the bird did not give up. Soon, the bird could write its name too. Lily and the white bird became good friends. They played together in the beautiful nature, and sometimes they would write together. They were very happy, and they learned that even birds can write with a little help from a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. He would run, jump, and catch it all day long. One day, Max saw an expensive ball in the store. It was very pretty and he wanted it very much. Max sighed because he knew he could not get the expensive ball. He decided to play a game to win it. He had to score ten points to get the ball. Max was very excited and started to play right away. Max played the game and scored one, two, three points. He played more and scored four, five, six points. Finally, he scored seven, eight, nine points! Max was so close to winning the expensive ball. With one last try, Max scored the tenth point and won the ball! Max was very happy and played with his new ball all day long.
