Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very fit boy who loved to run and play all day. One day, Tim found a big bow in his room. He was very happy and decided to play with the bow. Tim took the bow and went to the kitchen. He saw the fridge and thought it would be fun to put the bow on the fridge. Tim opened the fridge and saw lots of yummy food. He took an apple and closed the fridge. Tim sat down to eat the apple and looked at the fridge with the big bow. It made him smile. His mom came into the kitchen and saw the bow on the fridge too. She smiled and gave Tim a big hug. They both laughed and had a great day playing with the big bow.

One day, a little boy named Tim found an incredible box in his room. He did not know where it came from. It was a mystery. Tim was very excited to see what was inside the box. Tim tried to open the box, but it was hard. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me open this mystery box?" His mom said, "Not now, Tim. We have to delay it until after dinner." After dinner, Tim and his mom opened the incredible box. Inside, they found a big, red ball. Tim was so happy. He played with the ball all day long. The mystery box made Tim's day very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy dog named Max. One day, Lucy and Max went to play outside in the dirt. They got very dirty. Lucy said to Max, "I'm tired, let's go home." Max yawned and agreed. As they walked home, they saw something shiny in the dirt. Lucy picked it up and saw it was a diamond! She was very happy. Lucy and Max went home and showed the diamond to Mom. Mom said, "Good job finding the diamond! Let's clean it and you two." They all cleaned up and Lucy put the diamond in a special box. Now, Lucy and Max always remember their fun, dirty adventure.

Once upon a time, in a small, noisy town, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, warm coat for the cold winter days. Lily loved to play outside in the snow with her friends. One day, Lily saw a small, cold bird shivering in the snow. She felt sad for the bird and wanted to help it. Lily decided to own the bird and take care of it. Lily took the bird home and made a warm, cozy nest for it. She fed the bird every day and played with it. The bird was very happy and became Lily's best friend. They had lots of fun together during the winter, and the bird was never cold or hungry again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a huge tree. Tim wanted to touch the tree, so he walked up to it and put his hand on its skin. The tree was very big and very old. "Hello, little boy," said the tree. Tim was so surprised! He didn't know that trees could talk. "My name is Mr. Tree," the tree said. "I have been here for a very long time." Tim wanted to know more about Mr. Tree, so he started to examine the tree's skin. He saw many little marks and scratches. "What are these?" Tim asked. "Oh, those are from my many friends," said Mr. Tree. "Birds, squirrels, and even other kids like you have left their marks on me." Just then, Tim saw his friend Sue running towards him. "Tim, come quick!" she said. "I found something amazing!" Tim said goodbye to Mr. Tree and ran with Sue. They found a secret door hidden in the ground. They opened the door and found a magical world full of talking trees just like Mr. Tree!

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Max. Max loved to climb. One day, Max saw a big tree with a small chamber at the top. Max wanted to see what was inside the chamber. Max started to climb the tree. He slipped a little because he was clumsy, but he did not give up. Max reached the chamber and found a little bird inside. The bird was scared of Max. "Please don't hurt me," said the bird. Max replied, "I won't hurt you, I just wanted to see the chamber." Max and the bird became friends. They played together every day. Max learned to climb better and was not so clumsy anymore. And they lived happily ever after.
