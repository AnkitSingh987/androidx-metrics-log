Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a cat named Tom. Tom was a crazy cat who loved to stretch his long legs. One day, Tom was walking when he saw a big tree. He thought, "I want to climb that tree!" As Tom was climbing, he saw a bird named Billy. Billy said, "Hi Tom! I am going to a meeting with my bird friends. Do you want to come?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to go to the meeting too!" So, Tom and Billy went to the meeting together. At the meeting, Tom and Billy met many new friends. They played games, sang songs, and had a lot of fun. After the meeting was over, Tom and Billy went back to the big tree. Tom said, "I had so much fun at the meeting! Let's do it again soon!" Billy agreed, and they both went home to rest after their long, fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a big sun who was very jealous. He wanted to be the best at everything. One day, he saw a little girl playing with a spear. The sun thought, "I want to play with that spear too!" So, the sun asked the little girl, "Can I play with your spear?" The little girl said, "Yes, but you have to help me dry my clothes first." The sun agreed and helped the girl dry her clothes quickly. After the clothes were dry, the sun and the girl played with the spear. They had so much fun! The sun was not jealous anymore. He learned that sharing and helping others made him feel happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a heavy anchor. One day, the boat went out to the sea. The boat saw a big fish. The big fish was upset. The upset fish swam to the boat. The fish wanted to play. The boat wanted to play, too. The boat let the anchor down. The anchor made a splash in the water. The big fish smiled and swam around the anchor. The boat and the big fish played all day. They played a game called "squeeze". The big fish squeezed the anchor with its tail. The boat made funny sounds. They laughed and played until the sun went down. The boat and the big fish were happy.

One day, a cool cat named Sam went for a walk. He saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. Sam tried to push the ball, but it would not move. He tried to sit on the ball, but it was too big. Sam was sad. Then, Sam saw a little switch next to the ball. He was curious and pushed the switch. Suddenly, the big red ball started to get smaller and smaller. Sam was very surprised and happy. Now, the ball was small enough for Sam to play with. He could push it, sit on it, and have lots of fun. The cool cat Sam played with the ball all day long. He was very happy that he found the little switch.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very organized boy. He liked to keep his toys in the right place. One day, Tim saw a toy that did not belong to him. It was a small car. Tim went to his friend, Sam, and asked, "Is this your car? It does not belong in my toy box." Sam looked at the car and said, "Yes, that is my car! Thank you for finding it, Tim." Sam was very happy to have his car back. He learned that it is important to keep things in their right place, just like Tim. When they eat, they remember to put food in their mouth and not make a mess. They also learned to share and help each other. The moral of the story is to be organized, know where things belong, and help others when you can.

Once, there was an attractive lady who wanted to borrow something. She found a small cold lake far away from her house. She decided to borrow some of its water. She was very happy when she carried it home in a bucket. When she reached home, the water was still cold. She made a delicious cold drink out of it. It was so refreshing! Everyone in her house loved it. The attractive lady was very happy that she had borrowed the cold water. From then on, she borrowed cold water from the lake whenever she wanted a cold drink. Everyone was very thankful for her and it was a great success!
