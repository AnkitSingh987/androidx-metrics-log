Title: Lily and the Song

Lily liked to dance. She danced with her feet, her arms, and her hips. She danced to the rhythm of the music. The rhythm was the beat that made her want to move. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, do you want to dance with me?" Lily nodded and smiled. She loved to dance with her mom. They went to the living room, where the music was playing. Lily's mom said, "Let's dance to this song. It is a clean song. That means it has no bad words or sounds. It is a nice song." She held Lily's hand and spun her around. Lily giggled and followed her mom's steps. They danced to the clean song for a while. Then Lily's mom said, "Lily, do you want to include your teddy bear in our dance?" Lily nodded and ran to get her teddy bear. She brought him back and hugged him. She said, "This is Teddy. He likes to dance too." Lily's mom said, "Hello, Teddy. Welcome to our dance. You can dance with us. We will include you in our rhythm." She took Lily and Teddy's hands and swung them from side to side. Lily, Teddy, and mom danced to the rhythm of the clean song. They had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy who liked to help his family. One day, Tim's mom asked him to clean their yard. "Tim, can you clean the yard, please?" she asked. Tim said, "Yes, mom! I will do it." Tim started to clean the yard. It was a difficult job because there were many leaves and sticks. He worked very hard to make the yard look nice. While cleaning, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam was not following the law and was throwing trash in the park. Tim didn't like this and said, "Sam, we should follow the law and not throw trash in the park. It is not nice." Sam listened to Tim and helped him clean the yard. Together, they finished the difficult job. Tim's mom was very happy and proud of them. She said, "Good job, boys! You both did a great job and followed the law. I am proud of you." The moral of the story is that it is important to follow the law and help keep our world clean. When we work together, even difficult jobs can be done.

One day, a girl named Sue was in the kitchen with her mom. Her mom had a beautiful apron on. Sue wanted to help her mom cook. She saw a big bowl on the table. Sue thought it was for mixing. Sue went to the big bowl and started to mix. She was happy to help her mom. But then, something unexpected happened. Sue saw that the bowl was not for mixing. It was for the dog's food! Sue was very surprised. Sue's mom saw her and laughed. She gave Sue a small apron just like hers. She said, "Now you can help me with the real mixing." Sue and her mom had fun cooking together. They made a big, yummy cake. And Sue learned to always check what she was mixing.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play together. But sometimes they did not behave well. They would fight over toys, make a mess, and not listen to their mom and dad. One day, mom and dad had to go to a wedding. They told Anna and Ben to wear nice dresses and suits and to behave well at the party. Anna and Ben nodded, but they did not really want to go. They thought weddings were boring and they wanted to stay home and play. At the party, Anna and Ben were troubled. They did not like the food, the music, or the people. They ran around, knocked over glasses, and made loud noises. Mom and dad were very angry and embarrassed. They told Anna and Ben to stop and to say sorry to the hosts. But Anna and Ben did not listen. They kept being naughty and rude. Then, something bad happened. Anna tripped over a long dress and fell down. She hurt her knee and tore her dress. She started to cry. Ben saw what happened and ran to help her. He felt sorry for his sister and for being mean. He hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Anna. Are you okay?" Anna looked at her brother and stopped crying. She said, "I'm sorry, too, Ben. I was not nice to you or to anyone. We should behave better and be kind." They both realized that they had made a mistake and that they had to change. They went to mom and dad and apologized for their bad behavior. They also said sorry to the hosts and the guests. Mom and dad were glad that Anna and Ben learned their lesson. They hugged them and said, "We love you, but you have to behave well and respect others. That is the right thing to do." Anna and Ben nodded and smiled. They felt better and happier. They decided to enjoy the party and to have fun with their family and friends. They also promised to behave well and to be nice to each other from then on.
