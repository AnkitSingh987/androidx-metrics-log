Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Smart Cat and the Dog

One day, a smart cat named Tim went to a quiz. He was very excited to show everyone how smart he was. Many animals were there, like a dog, a frog, and a pig. They all wanted to win the quiz. When the quiz started, Tim listened very carefully. He knew all the answers! When the quiz master asked a question, Tim would nod his head to show he knew the answer. The other animals were not as smart as Tim. But then, something bad happened. A big, mean dog came to the quiz. He didn't like that Tim was so smart. The mean dog scared all the animals away. Tim was sad that he couldn't finish the quiz.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big balloon tied to a string. The balloon was high in the sky, and it had many colors. Ben and Lily wanted to touch the balloon. "Let's get the balloon!" Ben said. "We can pull the string and make it come down." "OK!" Lily said. "But be careful, don't poke the balloon. It might pop." Ben and Lily ran to the string. They pulled it hard, and the balloon came closer. They reached for the balloon, but it was still too high. They pulled harder, and the string broke. "Oh no!" Ben said. "The balloon is flying away!" Ben and Lily watched the balloon go higher and higher, until they could not see it anymore. They felt sad and angry. They had lost the balloon. They looked at the string in their hands. It was short and useless. They threw it on the ground. "That was a bad balloon!" Ben said. "It was mean and selfish. It did not want to play with us." "Yes, it was!" Lily said. "It was a bad, bad balloon!" They stomped their feet and cried. They did not have any fun in the park. They wished they had never seen the balloon.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was confused because he could not find his pants. He looked all over his room, but they were not there. Tim wanted to solve this problem, so he went to ask his mom for help. His mom said, "Let's look in the laundry room." They looked and looked, but still, they could not find Tim's pants. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. Then, his mom had an idea. "Maybe your sister knows where your pants are," she said. So, they went to ask Tim's sister, Lily. Lily was playing with her toys when Tim and his mom came in. They asked Lily if she knew where Tim's pants were. Lily giggled and said, "I put them on my teddy bear because he was cold!" Tim and his mom looked at the teddy bear, and there were Tim's pants! They all laughed and Tim put his pants back on. The moral of the story is that sometimes, when we are confused, we just need a little help from our family to solve our problems.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick door. It was the door to a closet. The closet was full of toys and fun things to play with. But, the door was always locked. No one could open it. One day, a little boy named Tim found a key. He thought the key could unlock the door. He was very excited. He wanted to see what was inside the closet. So he took the key and put it in the lock. He turned the key and the door opened! Inside the closet, Tim found many toys. There were balls, dolls, and cars. He played with them all day. He was very happy that he could unlock the door. From that day on, Tim and his friends played with the toys in the closet every day. They had lots of fun and were always happy.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tree. Its limbs were thick and its trunk was strong. Everyday a rabbit would stare up at it and admire it. One day a little girl saw the rabbit staring up at the tree. She thought it was lovely and wanted to come closer to it. But when she took a step closer, a sigh escaped the tree. The little girl was so scared that she quickly ran away. She thought the tree was trying to scare her. Everyday she would walk past it, but never got too close. Then one day she tried again. She slowly walked towards the tree, and suddenly it spoke. It said "I'm just tired. I was searching for peace. Will you help me find it?". The little girl smiled and nodded. She helped the ancient tree find peace by bringing it water, sunshine, and love. And soon enough, the old tree was happy again. The little girl and the rabbit became good friends and would often stop by to stare up at the ancient tree and admire its strength.
