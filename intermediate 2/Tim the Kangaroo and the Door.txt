Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Kangaroo and the Door

Once there was an old kangaroo named Tim who wanted to organize a surprise party. He wanted to invite all his friends and family. Tim thought of a great idea. He hopped to the kitchen and opened all the cupboards. He sorted the food and drinks and organized them on the table. Tim said to himself, "This looks perfect!" He hopped back to his room and invited all his friends. When they arrived, they were so surprised to see the party. Suddenly, there was a tap on the door. Tim opened the door and found an old kangaroo standing there. Tim asked, "Who are you?" The old kangaroo said, "I'm here to join the party! It looks like you organized a wonderful surprise party!" Everyone cheered, "Yes! Let's party!"

Once upon a time, there was a hairy bear who lived in the forest. He wanted some oil to make his fur smooth and shiny, but the trees wouldn't give him any. So the bear went to visit his friend, a fox, who lived in a nearby den. The fox had some oil, so the bear asked if he could have some. The fox said no and started to tease the bear by dancing around and telling jokes. The bear got mad and tried to grab the oil from the fox. But the fox was too quick and ran away. The bear was so angry that he roared and roared. He roared and roared until he stumbled and fell into a deep, dark well. The bear was never seen again. He never got his oil, and he was never able to smooth his hairly fur. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a book about planets. He felt very lucky to have it. He read the book every day and learned a lot. He loved to tell his friends about the planets. One day, Tim's friend, Sarah, came over to play. "I want to read the book too," she said. They sat down and read the book together. They talked about the planets and how big they were. The next day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big, round ball that looked like a planet. He was very excited and called his friends. "I found a planet!" he shouted. They all ran to see it, but when they got close, they saw it was just a big, round ball. They laughed and played with the ball, feeling lucky to have found it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat spicy food. One day, she found a magic lamp on her way to the park. She was very excited and picked it up. Lily rubbed the lamp and a big genie came out. He said, "I will give you one wish. What do you want?" Lily thought for a moment and then she knew what she wanted. "I wish for the spiciest food in the world!" she replied. The genie clapped his hands and a big plate of spicy food appeared in front of Lily. She took a bite and her face turned red. It was very spicy, but she loved it. She thanked the genie and went home to share the spicy food with her family. They all loved it too, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim had a tooth that was not sharp. It was dull. Tim did not like his dull tooth. He wanted to have a tooth like his friends. They all had sharp teeth. One day, Tim met a kind fairy. The fairy saw that Tim was sad. She asked him, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told the fairy about his dull tooth. The fairy wanted to help Tim. She said, "I will let you have a wish. What do you want?" Tim thought for a moment. He said, "I want a sharp tooth, not a dull one." The fairy smiled and waved her wand. She let Tim have his wish. Tim's tooth became sharp. He was very happy. Tim thanked the fairy and ran to show his friends his new sharp tooth. They all played together, and Tim was not sad anymore.
