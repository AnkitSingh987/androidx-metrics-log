Category: Intermediate
Title: Chicky the Colorful Chicken and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a colorful chicken named Chicky. Chicky lived on a small farm with many friends. Every day, Chicky liked to play with her friends in the big yard. One sunny day, Chicky saw a new friend. It was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky was shy and did not play with the other animals. Chicky wanted to help Ducky make new friends. Chicky went to Ducky and said, "Hi, Ducky! Do you want to join us and play?" Ducky looked at Chicky and smiled. "Yes, I would like that," Ducky replied. So, Chicky and Ducky played with the other animals in the yard. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. All the animals were happy to have a new friend to play with. From that day on, Chicky and Ducky were best friends. They played together every day, and the farm was a happier place because of their friendship.

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there lived a little lizard named Lizzy. Lizzy was a competitive lizard who loved to race with her friends. One sunny day, Lizzy and her friend, Tommy the turtle, decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. "Ready, set, go!" said Lizzy, and they both started to run. Lizzy was very fast, and Tommy was very slow. Lizzy was sure she would win the race. But then, something unexpected happened. Lizzy saw a big, pretty flower and stopped to look at it. She forgot all about the race and started to play with the flower. Meanwhile, Tommy kept going, slowly but surely. He did not stop or turn to look at anything. He just kept moving forward. When Lizzy remembered the race, she ran as fast as she could, but it was too late. Tommy had already won. "Good job, Tommy!" said Lizzy. "You won because you did not stop or turn. I learned that it is important to stay focused and not get distracted." And from that day on, Lizzy the lizard always remembered to stay focused on her goals, and she became a much better racer.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with kites. One day, they go to the park with their kites. The sky is blue and the wind is strong. Tom has a red kite and Sam has a green kite. They run and laugh and make their kites fly high. But then, Tom sees a big yellow kite. He thinks it is very pretty. He wants to have it. He runs to the boy who has the yellow kite. He says, "Give me your kite. It is mine now." He tries to take the yellow kite from the boy. The boy is sad and angry. He says, "No, it is not yours. It is mine. My mom gave it to me. Go away." He holds the yellow kite tight. Tom and the boy pull and tug and shout. They make a big mess. Sam sees what is happening. He feels bad for the boy and for Tom. He runs to them and says, "Stop, stop, stop. You are not being nice. You are not being good friends. You should share and care. You should not take things that are not yours. You should say sorry and be kind." Tom and the boy stop and listen to Sam. They feel sorry for what they did. They say sorry to each other and to the boy's mom. They decide to play together and share their kites. They have fun and make new friends. The moral of the story is: You should not take things that are not yours. You should share and care. You should say sorry and be kind.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But today, they have to study for a test. Their mom gives them a big book with many words and pictures. She says, "You have to study this book for your test tomorrow. It is very important." Sara and Ben do not want to study. They want to play. They look at the book and feel bored. They start to make noises and throw things. Their mom gets angry. She says, "Stop that! You have to be quiet and study. If you do not study, you will not do well on your test. And you will not get a prize." Sara and Ben do not like to make their mom angry. They want to get a prize. They look at each other and nod. They decide to try to study. They open the book and point at the words and pictures. They say them out loud. They try to remember them. But studying is hard. Sara and Ben get tired and hungry. They want to take a break. They close the book and go to the kitchen. They see a big cake on the table. It looks yummy. They want to eat some. They reach for the cake. But their mom sees them. She says, "No, no, no! You cannot eat the cake. That is for later. After you finish studying. And after you pass your test. Then you can have some cake. And a prize. Now go back to your room and study." Sara and Ben feel sad. They want to eat the cake. They want to have a prize. They do not want to study. They go back to their room. They see their shoes on the floor. They have an idea. They put on their shoes. They say, "Let's go outside and play. Maybe mom will not see us. Maybe she will forget about the test." They sneak out of their room and run to the door. But their mom sees them. She says, "Hey, hey, hey! Where are you going? You cannot go outside and play. You have to stay inside and study. You have to pass your test. You have to be grateful. You have a big book and a nice room and a yummy cake and a prize. Some kids do not have these things. You are lucky. Now go back to your room and study." Sara and Ben feel sorry. They do not want to be bad. They want to be good. They want to make their mom happy. They want to be grateful. They take off their shoes and go back to their room. They open the book and study. They say, "We are sorry, mom. We love you, mom. We will study, mom. We will pass the test, mom. We will be grateful, mom."
