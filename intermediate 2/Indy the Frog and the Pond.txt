Category: Intermediate
Title: Indy the Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Indy. Indy was an independent frog who liked to do things by himself. He lived in a small pond with his family and friends. One day, Indy saw a big hole near the pond. He thought it would be fun to jump over the hole. He told his friends, but they were scared. Indy said, "Don't worry, I can do it by myself!" Indy went to the hole and jumped as high as he could. He made it over the hole and landed on the other side. His friends were so happy and proud of him. From that day on, Indy the frog knew that he could do anything he set his mind to, all by himself.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to look at the stars in the sky. She dreamed of going to a far away planet. She knew that on this planet, there were many tasty treats to eat. One day, Mia found a big, round ball. She thought it was the planet she dreamed of. She wanted to find the tasty treats inside. So, Mia decided to break the ball open. She hit it with her hands, but it was too hard to break. Mia's mommy saw her trying to break the ball. She helped Mia break it open. Inside, they found tasty cookies shaped like stars. Mia and her mommy ate the cookies together, and they were very happy. Mia knew that even if she couldn't go to the far away planet, she could still have fun with her mommy and eat tasty treats.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a little rabbit. The rabbit had a soft, white fur and loved to hop around all day. The rabbit had many friends, like the birds and the squirrels, and they played together under the warm sun. One day, the rabbit went too far from home and got lost. The rabbit was sad and scared. But a big, strong dog saw the rabbit and wanted to help. The dog knew the rabbit needed to be rescued and taken back home. The dog was very kind and gentle. He picked up the rabbit in his mouth and carried him back to the big green field. The rabbit was so happy to be home and safe. All his friends came to see him and make sure he was fine. They all thanked the dog for rescuing their friend. From that day on, the rabbit and the dog became the best of friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Mark wanted to go on a trip. He had a passport that would let him go to new places. Mark was very excited because going on a trip was easy for him. Mark packed his bag and went to the big car that would take him far away. He showed his passport to the man at the car. The man looked at the passport and let Mark get on the big car. But, Mark forgot his toy at home. He was very sad. The trip was not fun without his toy. Mark wished he had his toy with him. The trip had a bad ending because Mark could not be happy without his toy.

Mia and Sam liked to play in the woods behind their house. They liked to climb trees, find bugs, and make forts. But there was one place they never went: the swamp. The swamp was dark and smelly, and their mom said it was dangerous. One day, they met a new boy named Leo. Leo said he was brave and strong, and he could do anything. He said he had been to the swamp many times, and it was fun. He said there were frogs and snakes and treasures there. He said Mia and Sam were scaredy-cats for not going with him. Mia and Sam felt frustrated. They were not scaredy-cats. They wanted to show Leo they were brave and strong too. So they said they would go to the swamp with him, but only if he dared them. Leo smiled and said, "I dare you to go to the swamp with me and touch the biggest frog you can find." Mia and Sam agreed, and they followed Leo to the swamp. They held their noses and walked through the mud and water. They saw frogs and snakes and bugs, but they did not see any treasures. Leo pointed to a big green frog sitting on a log. He said, "There it is. The biggest frog in the swamp. I dare you to touch it." Mia and Sam looked at each other. They were scared, but they did not want to back down. They walked slowly to the log, and reached out their hands. But before they could touch the frog, it jumped into the air and landed on Leo's head. Leo screamed and ran away, dropping his backpack. Mia and Sam laughed and ran after him. They did not see the backpack open, and the treasures fall out. They were shiny cans and bottles that Leo had taken from the trash. He had lied to them. He had never been to the swamp before. He was the scaredy-cat.
