Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was nervous because she wanted to help her mom make a cake. Amy asked her mom, "Can I please help you?" Her mom said, "Of course! Let's make the cake together." Amy's mom gave her a teaspoon to add some sugar to the cake mix. Amy was very careful and said, "I can do it, Mom!" She put the sugar in the mix and felt happy that she was helping. After the cake was done, Amy and her mom shared it together. Amy was not nervous anymore. She knew that she could help her mom in the kitchen. They both enjoyed the cake and had a fun day.

One day, a big rain came. It rained so much that a flood came to the town. All the people were scared. They needed to find a way to stop the water from coming into their homes. A wise old man said, "We need to shut the door and put something heavy in front of it. This is very important to keep the water out." The people listened to the wise old man and did what he said. They shut their doors and put heavy things in front of them. The rain stopped and the flood went away. The people were happy that their homes were safe. They learned that it is important to listen to wise people and work together to solve problems.

One day, a nice cat found a can that could spray. The cat wanted to make things look pretty. So, the cat went around and spray all the junk it saw. The junk became nice colors like red, blue, and green. The cat was very happy. But then, a big dog saw the cat. The dog did not like the cat. The dog wanted the can that could spray. The cat and the dog fought for the can. They both wanted to have it. They did not want to share. Then, something unexpected happened. The can that could spray broke. Now, the cat and the dog could not spray anything. They both were sad. The cat and the dog learned that they should have shared. But now, it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Spot. Spot was a regular dog, with a big heart. He loved to show his friends new places. One day, Spot wanted to show his friends a new place he found. Spot and his friends walked and walked until they saw the place. It was a big, fun park. But there was a big, mean cat who said, "You can't play here!" Spot and his friends were sad. They wanted to play in the park. Spot had an idea. He asked the big, mean cat, "Can we play if we share our toys?" The big, mean cat thought about it and said, "Okay, you can play here." Spot and his friends were happy. They played in the park and shared their toys with the big, mean cat. From that day, the big, mean cat was not mean anymore. They all became friends and had fun together.

One day, a dog named Ben was very stubborn. He did not want to wear his collar. His mom tried to comfort him. She said, "Ben, you need to wear your collar. It will help keep you safe." But Ben did not listen. He ran out of the house without his collar. He played in the park with his friends. They all had fun, but Ben still did not wear his collar. His mom was sad and worried. In the end, Ben got lost. He could not find his way home. People did not know he was lost because he did not have his collar. Ben was very scared and wished he had listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little girl named Lily. She loved to explore and learn new things. One day, Lily saw her mom display a big cup of coffee on the table. Lily had never seen coffee before and she wanted to know what it was. "Mommy, what is that?" Lily asked, pointing at the cup. Her mom smiled and said, "That is coffee, Lily. It is a drink for grown-ups, not for little girls like you." Lily was very curious about the coffee. She wanted to know what it tasted like, but she knew she had to listen to her mom. Instead, she decided to display her toys on the floor and pretend they were having a tea party. She poured pretend coffee into little cups for her toys and had a fun time playing. Later, her mom saw Lily's display of toys and smiled. She was proud of her curious little girl for finding a way to learn about coffee without trying it. Lily was happy too, because she got to have a fun tea party with her toys.
