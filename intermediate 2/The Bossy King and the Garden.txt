Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bossy King and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a far away planet, there lived a bossy king. The king had a big swing in his garden. Every day, he would swing high up in the sky and look at his kingdom. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the garden. She saw the swing and wanted to play on it too. She asked the king, "Can I play on the swing too, please?" The king laughed and said, "No, this is my swing. You cannot play on it." Lily was sad and angry. She wanted to play on the swing, but the bossy king did not let her. So, she thought of a plan. She went to the king's helper and said, "I have a big, yummy cake. I will give it to you if you let me play on the swing." The helper agreed and let Lily play on the swing. Lily swung so high that she could see the whole planet. The bossy king saw her and was very mad. But, all the people in the kingdom saw Lily and cheered for her. They were happy that someone else could play on the swing too. From that day on, the king learned to share and let others play on the swing. And everyone lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved the color pink. One day, her mom had a big surprise for her. She could not wait to see it. Lily's mom took her to a big room. She said, "Close your eyes, Lily." Lily closed her eyes. Her mom said, "Now, I will reveal the surprise." When Lily opened her eyes, she saw a big pink box. She was so happy. Inside the box, Lily found a pretty model. It was a model of a pink house. Lily loved it. She played with the pink house all day. And they lived happily ever after.

Mama was measuring the wall today. She was trying to hang a picture for a surprise. But she was having trouble. "Mama, why are you having trouble?" asked Uncle J. "I can't find a bulb to fit. They are all too small," Mama said. Uncle J laughed. "You know what? I have a bulb in my armoire that should fit. I'll go get it," he said, and set off to get it. But just then, the bunny hopped by, looking very lazy. "Wait! Come here," said Mama. The bunny hopped over, but it was too lazy to help. "I need you to go find me a bulb," said Mama. The bunny just groaned and shook its head. Mama thought for a moment. Then, she said, "If you help me find the bulb, I'll give you a carrot!" The bunny's ears perked up! It hopped off, and soon, it came back with the perfect bulb! Mama measured it, and it was just right. She hugged the bunny tight and thanked it for its help. The bunny happily munched its carrot, proud that it had helped Mama.

Once upon a time, there was a big kitchen. In the kitchen, there was a dishwasher. The dishwasher was very organized. It liked to make sure all the dishes were clean and neat. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the kitchen. Tim wanted to help his mom. He saw the dishwasher and wanted to hang his toy on it. But, something unexpected happened! When Tim put his toy on the dishwasher, the dishwasher started to talk! It said, "Hello, Tim! I can help you clean your toy too!" Tim was very surprised. From that day on, Tim and the dishwasher became best friends and cleaned the kitchen together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very restless. He could not sit still. One day, Tim's mom and dad said they were going on vacation. Tim was so excited! On the day of the vacation, Tim and his family got in the car. Tim had a special seat just for him. But Tim was still restless. He wiggled and squirmed in his seat. His mom told him to be still, but Tim could not. As they drove, Tim's dad said they were going to a big park. Tim's eyes grew big. He knew what that meant. When they got to the park, Tim jumped out of his seat. He ran and played all day. Finally, Tim was not restless anymore. He was tired and happy. And that night, Tim slept very well in his car seat.
