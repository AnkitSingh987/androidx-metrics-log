Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Strong Boy and the Treasure

One day, a strong boy named Tim went to class. He liked to find new things. In class, his teacher talked about a big treasure. Tim wanted to find the treasure. Tim and his friends went to look for the treasure. They walked and walked. Then, they saw a big box. Tim thought the treasure was inside. He was very excited. Tim opened the box. But there was no treasure. Instead, they found a small cat. The cat was lost. Tim and his friends were happy to find the cat. They took the cat home. Now, Tim had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there lived an ostrich who was always very hungry. Everyday, the ostrich would wander around looking for food. One day, he came across a big door, and he decided to go in. He found himself in a room filled with wonderful treats, but the door had shut behind him. The ostrich was so happy at being surrounded by delicious food and he quickly ate it all up. He was so full that he soon fell asleep. But when he woke up, he found that the door was still shut. He started to panic as he realised he was trapped. The ostrich tried and tried to open the door, but no matter how hard he tried, he could not get it open. Suddenly, he saw a little mouse scurrying around the floor. The mouse said to the ostrich, “If you really want something, you must be willing to work for it. You can't just eat your way out.” The ostrich understood what the mouse was saying. He stopped panicking and used all his strength to push open the door. To his relief, it opened and he walked out, feeling relieved. He never forgot the lesson the little mouse had taught him and the moral of the story is that hard work is more important than just having what you want.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a special box. The box was very important to her. She kept all her toys in it. She loved playing with her toys every day. One day, Lucy could not find her box. She started to worry. She looked all around her room. She looked under her bed. She looked in the closet. But she could not find her box. Lucy felt very sad. Then, Lucy's mom came to help. They looked all around the house. Finally, they found the box in the living room. Lucy was so happy! She was successful in finding her box. She played with her toys and did not worry anymore. Lucy and her mom had a fun day together.

One day, a little gray cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. So, the cat went up the tree and saw a bird. The bird said, "Hi, cat! Be careful up here!" The cat said, "Why?" The bird said, "There is poison in the tree. It can make you sick. Do not touch it!" The cat saw the poison and stayed away from it. The cat climbed down the tree and went home. The cat told its friend, a dog, about the poison. The dog said, "Thank you for telling me. We must be careful when we play." The cat and the dog were safe and happy because they knew about the poison. They played together and had fun.

One day, a furry dog named Max went to the river. Max saw a red kayak near the water. He wanted to go for a ride. Max jumped into the kayak and started to paddle. Max saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Max wanted to help Sue. "I will collect your toy," Max said. Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Max." Max paddled the kayak and looked for Sue's toy. He found it stuck in a bush. Max took the toy and paddled back to Sue. Sue was happy to see her toy again. They both played by the river and became good friends.

One day, a big truck was driving down the road. The truck was very old and bad. It needed to be fixed. The truck driver, Tom, was sad. He wanted to restore his truck and make it like new again. Tom saw a little boy named Tim on the side of the road. Tim said, "Hi, Mr. Truck Driver! Your truck looks bad. I can help you restore it!" Tom was surprised. He didn't think a little boy could fix his big truck. But he let Tim try. Tim touched the truck and, like magic, the truck was new again! Tom was so happy. He thanked Tim and said, "Wow, Tim! You are amazing! My truck is good now!" And they both laughed and played with the truck together. The end.
