Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Wheel

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a pale blue dress that she loved to wear. One day, Lily went for a visit to her friend Tom's house. They loved to play together. While playing, Lily and Tom found a big box. They were very curious. They opened the box, and inside was a big, colorful wheel. They decided to turn the wheel to see what would happen. As they turned the wheel, something unexpected happened. The room started to change colors! First, it was red, then green, and then yellow. Lily and Tom were so surprised and happy. They laughed and played in the colorful room all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his favorite toy, a triangle. One sunny day, Tom went outside to play with his triangle in the park. While playing, Tom saw a dog named Max. Max was a big, friendly dog who wanted to play too. Tom showed Max his triangle and said, "Look, Max! I have a triangle. Let's play together!" Max and Tom played with the triangle, taking turns to toss it in the air and catch it. They had a lot of fun. As they played, they saw a sign that said, "No pets on the grass." Tom and Max looked at each other and decided to play somewhere else. They found a nice spot under a tree and continued playing with the triangle. They played and played until they were tired. Tom and Max became best friends, and from that day on, they always played together with Tom's triangle. And they always remembered to look for signs before playing in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a light little bird. The bird lived in a big tree. One day, the bird saw a man with an axe. The man wanted to cut the tree down. The bird did not like it. The bird said to the man, "Please do not cut my tree. I live here." The man looked at the bird and felt sad. He did not want to make the bird sad. He put the axe down and did not start to cut the tree. The bird was very happy. The bird and the man became friends. They played and talked every day. The tree stayed up, and the bird had a home. The man learned to love the tree too.

Mommy was in the kitchen, preparing something special. She hummed a song as she stirred and chopped. "What are you making?" the little girl asked standing in the doorway. "Strawberry tart, for your birthday party tomorrow!" Mommy said with a smile. The little girl's eyes lit up. "Delicious!" she exclaimed. Mommy hugged the little girl before going back to cooking. She mixed in fresh strawberries and sprinkled sugar and cinnamon on top. The little girl watched in awe as her mom prepped the tart. Once it had finished baking, Mommy cut the tart into pieces and served it up. The little girl took a bite and smiled. It was deliciously sweet and full of strawberry goodness. She felt like the luckiest little girl in the world.

Sara and Ben went to the store with Mom. They saw many things they liked, but Mom said they could only buy one thing each. Sara and Ben looked around and saw a big display of candy. It was high on a shelf, with many colors and shapes. "Wow, look at all the candy!" Sara said. "I want some!" "Me too!" Ben said. "But how can we reach it?" They tried to jump and stretch, but they were too small. The candy was too high. They asked Mom for help, but Mom said no. "No, you can't have candy today," Mom said. "It's not good for you. You can choose something else." Sara and Ben were sad. They liked candy very much. They looked at the display and wished they could have some. Then they saw a man with a ladder. He worked at the store. He was putting more candy on the display. He saw Sara and Ben and smiled. "Hello, little ones," he said. "Do you like candy?" "Yes, we do!" Sara and Ben said. "Well, I have a surprise for you," he said. "You can have one piece each, if you promise to brush your teeth later." He reached up and took two pieces of candy from the display. He gave one to Sara and one to Ben. They were very happy. They said thank you to the man and to Mom. They ate their candy and enjoyed it. It was sweet and yummy. They forgot about the other things they wanted to buy. They had the best thing of all: candy from the high display.
