Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a chubby dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball in the sun. One day, it was very hot outside, but Max still wanted to play. Max played and played until he felt very tired. The sun was so hot that his ball started to melt! Max didn't like that. He wanted to keep playing with his ball. Max felt a little pain in his paw from playing so much. He knew it was time to go inside. Max took his melted ball and went to his house. He was happy to rest and drink some water. The next day, Max got a new ball and played again, but this time, he played in the shade.

Once upon a time, there was a lady who loved the color orange. She had an orange house, orange clothes, and even an orange cat. One day, she had a plan to make an orange cake for her friend's birthday party. But when she went to the store, there were no more orange fruits left. The lady was sad and didn't know what to do. She thought her plan was ruined and her friend would not have a special cake. Then, she saw a little girl with an orange in her hand. The lady asked the girl if she could have the orange for her cake. The girl said yes, and they went to the lady's house to make the cake together. The orange cake was a big hit at the party, and the lady and the little girl became good friends.

One day, a nice girl named Lily went to the park. She had a bag of oats to give to the ducks. The sun was shining, but the air was icy. Lily wore a warm coat. At the park, Lily saw the ducks. They were so happy to see her. She gave them the oats, and they ate them up. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the oats away. Lily was sad, but the ducks had a surprise. They gave her a big hug. The ducks were not sad about the oats. They were just happy to see their friend Lily. And that made Lily happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a modern mark. It floated in the sky and was so shiny. It was so beautiful. Two friends, Mary and Jody, were playing in the park one day. Suddenly, they both saw the mark. "Wow!" shouted Mary. "What is that?" Jody smiled. "My dad told me that it's a special kind of mark. He said it will permit us to go on very special adventures!" Mary's eyes lit up. "I want to go!" Jody nodded. "Then we must go find it. Come on!" So together, Mary and Jody searched for the modern mark. After an hour of searching, they saw it in the sky and ran towards it. When they got close enough, the modern mark opened up and let them inside. Inside was a magical world with lots of surprises. Mary and Jody were so excited! They had lots of new adventures, all thanks to the modern mark.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia liked to sort her toys. She had many toys, but her favorite was a harmless little bear named Ben. One sunny day, Mia and Ben went for a walk in the park. They saw a boy who was sad. Mia asked the boy, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy car. I can't find it anywhere." Mia wanted to help the boy. She said, "Let's sort the toys in the park. Maybe we can find your car." Mia, Ben, and the boy looked around the park. They sorted all the toys they found. They put the balls in one pile, the dolls in another, and the cars in the last pile. At last, they found the boy's toy car in the car pile. The boy was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Mia and Ben!" From that day on, Mia, Ben, and the boy became good friends. They played together in the city park and sorted toys all the time.
