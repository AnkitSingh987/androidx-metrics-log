Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Fearful Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a fearful little boy named Tim. Tim had a pen that he loved very much. He used it to draw and write all day. One day, he lost his pen and felt very sad. Tim went to his friend, Sam, and said, "I can't find my pen. I'm so sad." Sam told him not to worry, they would look for it together. As they looked, Tim started to feel a little better. At last, they found the pen under a big tree. Tim was so happy that he didn't suffer anymore. He thanked Sam and they both played together for the rest of the day. And Tim was never fearful again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was going to the station with his mom. The station was a place where big trains stopped. Tim loved trains and was very happy. At the station, Tim saw many different trains. Some were red, some were blue, and some were green. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, why are the trains different colors?" His mom just shrugged and said, "I don't know, Tim. Maybe they like to be pretty." As they waited for their train, Tim and his mom talked to a nice man who worked at the station. The man told Tim that the different colors helped people know which train to get on. Tim thought that was a good idea. Soon, their train came, and Tim and his mom got on. They had a fun trip, and Tim learned something new.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She liked to write about different things. One day, she wanted to write a story about food. In her story, there were different kinds of food. There were red apples, yellow bananas, and green peas. The food could talk and play with each other. They liked to have fun and be friends. One day, the food friends went on a big adventure. They climbed a big hill and saw a beautiful rainbow. They were so happy to be together. And Lily was happy too, because she wrote a fun story about her new food friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big church in a small town. The church was old and a little filthy. One day, a nice man named Tom came to the church. He saw that it needed to be cleaned. So, Tom went to his house and got a big broom. Tom started to clean the church. He swept the floor and spread the dirt out of the church. The people in the town saw Tom cleaning the church. They said, "Thank you, Tom, for making our church clean!" The people wanted to help Tom. They came to the church with their brooms and rags. Together, they cleaned the church until it was shiny and new. The people were so happy. They said, "Our church is beautiful now, all thanks to Tom and his big broom!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to climb things. She climbed trees, chairs, and even her bed. One day, Amy saw a big thing she wanted to climb. It was a huge hill near her house. Amy told her mom, "I want to climb that big thing!" Her mom said, "Be careful, Amy. That's a big climb for a little girl." Amy smiled and said, "I can do it, Mom!" She put on her shoes and went outside. Amy started to climb the big thing. It was hard, but she kept going. At the top, she looked around and saw her house. She felt successful! Amy ran back down and told her mom, "I did it, I climbed the big thing!" Her mom hugged her and said, "I knew you could do it, Amy. You are a great climber!"

John pulled his Mummy's blouse from the cupboard. She had worn it for a special occasion. He was excited to find something hidden in the pocket. He decided to keep it to himself. Mummy asked, "John, what are you doing?" John replied, "Nothing." Mummy was not happy. She asked again, "John, why did you take my blouse?" John was caught. He had to admit he took it to keep what he found. Mummy was very sad because it was her special blouse. She said, "John, you are never to do this again". John felt bad. He had made a mistake and it had a bad ending.
