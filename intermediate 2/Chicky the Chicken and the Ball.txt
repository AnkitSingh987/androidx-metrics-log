Category: Intermediate
Title: Chicky the Chicken and the Ball

One day, a little chicken named Chicky went for a walk. Chicky was a calm and happy chicken. She liked to take walks in the sun. She saw her friend, Bunny, and said, "Hi, Bunny! Let's take a walk together." Bunny said, "Yes, Chicky! I want to show you something. It's a big surprise!" Chicky was very excited. They walked and walked until they saw a big tree. Bunny said, "Look up, Chicky! The surprise is in the tree!" Chicky looked up and saw a big, red ball stuck in the tree. She remembered that she lost her ball a few days ago. Chicky was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Bunny! You found my ball!" Bunny smiled and said, "I knew you would like it!" Together, they found a long stick to take the ball out of the tree. Chicky and Bunny played with the ball all day. They were very happy to be best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who had a big house. He had many things that he liked to put in cases. One day, he found a new case that was very pretty. He wanted to see if his favorite toy would fit in the case. He tried to put the toy in, but it was too big. He tried another toy, but it was too small. The wealthy man was sad because he could not find a toy that fit just right. Then, he had an idea. He went to his friend's house and found a toy that was just the right size. He put the toy in the case, and it fit perfectly. The wealthy man was very happy, and he showed the case with the toy to all his friends. They all liked it and had a fun day playing with the toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play in her mommy's kitchen. One day, Sue's mommy had to go out. She said, "Sue, promise me you won't touch the stove. It's very hot!" Sue said, "I promise, mommy!" While mommy was gone, Sue played in the kitchen. She saw the stove and thought, "I want to make a cake for mommy!" Sue got out the cake mix, eggs, and milk. She made a big, messy mix on the table. Sue was so happy to make the cake, she forgot her promise to mommy. Sue put the messy mix in the oven and turned it on. But she did not know how to use the stove. Soon, the kitchen was full of smoke! Sue was scared and cried. Just then, mommy came home. She saw the smoke and ran to the kitchen. Mommy turned off the stove and hugged Sue. She said, "Sue, you must always keep your promises. That's how we stay safe and happy." Sue learned to always keep her promises and to ask for help when she needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside in the sun. He would run and jump and bark with joy. One day, Spot found a towel on the grass. The towel was wet and helpless on the ground. Spot wanted to help the towel. Spot picked up the towel with his mouth and took it to his friend, Cat. Cat was very good at cleaning things. "Cat, can you help me clean this towel?" asked Spot. Cat said, "Yes, I can help you, Spot." So, Cat and Spot worked together to clean the towel. As they were cleaning the towel, they saw a little bug. The bug would blink its eyes at them. Spot and Cat laughed and played with the bug. They were very happy. Soon, the towel was clean and dry. Spot and Cat were proud of their work. They had helped the helpless towel and made a new friend in the little blinking bug.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to fold paper. She would fold paper into many shapes like birds, boats, and stars. But one day, Lily found it difficult to fold a big paper into a small frog. Lily's mom saw her trying hard and said, "Lily, do you need help?" Lily said, "Yes, please. I want to make a frog, but it is difficult." Her mom smiled and showed her how to fold the big paper into a small frog. Lily was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Later, Lily went to play near a well with her friends. They all had paper shapes that they made. One friend dropped his paper boat into the well. He was sad. Lily remembered how her mom helped her, so she gave her friend her paper frog. The friend was happy, and they all learned that helping others can make everyone feel good.
