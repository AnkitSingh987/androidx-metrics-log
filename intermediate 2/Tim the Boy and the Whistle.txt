Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Whistle

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a pencil that he liked a lot. He would take it with him wherever he went. One day, Tim was walking in the park when he saw a bird. The bird could whistle very well. Tim wanted to whistle too, but he did not know how. Tim asked the bird, "How do you whistle?" The bird said, "Just blow air out of your mouth and make a sound." Tim tried to whistle, but he could not do it. He felt sad. Then, Tim had an idea. He took his pencil and started to draw. He drew a picture of the bird and the naughty cat that tried to catch the bird. The bird liked the picture and showed Tim how to whistle again. This time, Tim could whistle! They both laughed and played together in the park.

Once there was a little girl who liked to create things. She especially liked to make things with pipes. One day she found lots of pipes scattered on the floor. She had a great idea - she decided to build something new with them. She moved the pipes around carefully, thinking how she could make something nice. As she worked, a small voice said, "That's not tidy!" The little girl looked up, and saw an old man standing there. She said, "These pipes are fun to use. I'm creating something great!" The old man smiled and said, "Making something great is always more fun when you keep it tidy. That way you can really show it off. Now go create something amazing!" The little girl worked hard to make something new and special, and when it was finished her work was very tidy. People were so impressed when they saw her creation and she learned an important lesson - that when you create something special, tidiness really does matter.

Once upon a time there was a tire called Bob. Bob was ready to roll. One day, he rolled into a big field, full of tall trees and bright blue sky. Bob was so excited to explore. He rolled around the field and smelled all the flowers. He was having such a wonderful time. Suddenly, Bob saw another tire. "Hi!" said the tire. Bob was excited to make a new friend, so he said "Hello!" back. The two tires rolled around, having fun together. Bob said, "Let's get ready to roll together!" But the other tire said "No, I'm not ready yet. I'm still too scared." Bob was surprised and sad. Bob said, "Don't worry, we'll have a lot of fun!" But the other tire still wasn't happy. He said, "No, I can't do it. Goodbye." And then, he rolled away. Bob was very sad. He had wanted a friend to roll with, but it was not to be. He rolled home, feeling lonely. The End.

One day, a red cat named Tim went to a stage. He wanted to sing a song for all his friends. He was very excited. When Tim started to sing, a big dog named Max came to the stage. Max said, "Stop singing, Tim! I want to sing too!" Tim did not like this. He wanted to sing alone. Tim said, "No, Max! This is my stage!" Then, Tim slapped Max's face. Max was sad and ran away. Tim felt bad and knew he should not have slapped Max. Tim found Max and said, "I am sorry, Max. Let's sing together on the stage." Max smiled and they both went back to the stage. They sang a beautiful song together and all their friends clapped. Tim and Max were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a messy little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play in the mud and get her clothes dirty. One day, Sue saw her mom hang a pretty cloth on the line to dry. Sue wanted to join her mom and help. But Sue was very messy from playing in the mud. Her mom said, "Sue, you can't join me with your messy clothes! You'll get the pretty cloth dirty too!" Sue was sad, but she had an idea. She ran to her room and put on clean clothes. Sue came back and her mom was happy. "Now you can join me and help with the pretty cloth," said her mom. They hung the cloth on the line together. Sue learned that she could be messy when playing, but she needed to be clean when helping her mom.
