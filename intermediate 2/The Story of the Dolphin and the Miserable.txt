Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dolphin and the Miserable

Once there was a boy who was very sad. He felt very miserable. One day, he went out for a walk. He looked up in the sky and saw some birds flying. He looked down and saw a dolphin in the water. He was so excited to find the dolphin. He waded into the water and swam with the dolphin. He felt much better after that, and he was no longer so miserable. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, red towel in his room. He liked the towel very much. He put it on his back and pretended to be a superhero. He ran around the house, saying, "I am Super Tim! I can save the day!" Tim's mom saw him playing and said, "Tim, be careful! Running in the house can be dangerous. You might fall and get hurt." Tim did not listen. He kept running and playing with the towel. Soon, Tim saw a door that was open. He knew he should shut the door, but he wanted to keep playing. He ran past the door and, suddenly, he tripped on the towel. Tim fell down and started to cry. His mom came to help him. She said, "I told you it was dangerous to run in the house. Let's shut the door and put the towel away." Tim agreed and learned to be more careful when playing inside.

Sammy had a band. He liked it very much. But one day, it got all messy. He needed to make it clean again. So Sammy went to the sink and washed the band. He put it under the water and used the soap. When he was done, his band was shiny and clean. Sammy was very happy and he went to show it off to his friends. They all admired it because it was so beautiful.

One day, a bossy cat and a little dog were friends. The cat liked to tell the dog what to do. The dog did not like this, but he did not want to complain. The bossy cat said, "I know a magic spell. I will use it to make you big and strong." The little dog was excited to be big and strong, but the cat's spell did not work. Instead, the dog became very small. The little dog was sad and said, "I don't like this spell. I want to be big again." The bossy cat tried to fix the spell, but it did not work. The dog was still very small. The little dog decided to be brave and complain. He said, "I don't like your bossy ways. I want to be big again, and I want to be friends with someone who is nice." The bossy cat felt bad and said sorry. They worked together to find a way to make the dog big again. In the end, they both learned to be better friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny who loved to explore. He wanted to find the best camp with lots of things to see. On the way, he spotted a big, colorful camp with lots of different things to do. But, the bunny was scared because of a big, furry bear near the camp. The bunny said to the bear, "You won't bite me, will you?" The bear looked at the little bunny and smiled. The bear said, "No, I won't bite you. I'm just curious about the camp. I have been too scared to go inside." The bunny was very brave. He said to the bear, "Come on, let's go together and explore it. I don't think it's so scary." The brave bunny and bear went inside the camp and had the most amazing adventure. They realized the camp was full of wonderful things and nothing was scary. In the end, the moral of the story was that things can look scary and uncertain, but if we take a chance, great things can happen.

One day, a little girl named Jane found a small red ball. She thought it was the perfect ball to bounce. She decided to take it to the park with her. Jane also took her favorite blue bucket to play with in the sand. At the park, Jane met a boy named Tim. Tim saw Jane's ball and said, "I like your ball! Can we bounce it together?" Jane smiled and said, "Yes! We can bounce it and play with my bucket too!" They played together, bouncing the ball and filling the bucket with sand. As they played, the sky turned dark and it started to rain. Jane and Tim were sad, but then they had an idea. They used the bucket to catch the rain. When the bucket was full, they took turns pouring the water on the ground to make a big puddle. Then, they bounced the ball in the puddle and laughed as it splashed them. The rain made their playtime even more fun!
