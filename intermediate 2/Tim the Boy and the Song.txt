Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Song

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some apples. Tim saw a big machine. It was a scale to weigh the apples. He thought it looked silly. "Mom, what is this silly machine for?" asked Tim. His mom said, "This machine helps us weigh the apples. We need to know how heavy they are before we buy them." Tim put the apples on the machine. Suddenly, the machine started to make funny noises. Tim and his mom were surprised. The machine was not just for weighing apples, but it could also talk! The machine said, "Hello, Tim! I'm a talking scale! I can tell you how heavy the apples are and even sing a song!" Tim and his mom laughed. They never thought a silly machine could be so much fun.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a shy little girl named Lily. She had a big wish to make friends, but she was too scared to talk to other kids. One sunny day, Lily saw a cute dog in the park. She wanted to pet it, but she was too shy. The dog's owner, a kind boy named Tom, noticed Lily and said, "Don't worry, my dog is very nice. You can trust him." Lily slowly reached out her hand and pet the dog. The dog wagged its tail and licked her hand. Lily smiled and said, "I like your dog. My wish is to have a friend like him." Tom smiled back and said, "You can be friends with my dog, and you can be my friend too." Lily was very happy and they all played together in the park. From that day on, Lily, Tom, and the dog were best friends. Lily learned to trust her friends and her wish came true. She was not shy anymore, and she made many more friends in the village.

Once upon a time, there was a rose. The rose was red and pretty. It lived in a big garden with many friends. The sun was warm, and the rose was glad. One day, a big wind came. It was so strong that the rose was scared. It thought it would fall. But then, a little girl came to save the rose. She held the rose tight so it would not fall. The rose was so glad. But, the little girl was not a girl. She was a fairy! She used magic to make the rose even more pretty. The rose was so happy and glad. It thanked the fairy for saving it. From that day, the rose and the fairy were best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very hungry. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, I am hungry. Can I have a lemon?" His mom gave him a big, yellow lemon. Tim was happy. Tim wanted to count how many lemons he could eat. He ate one lemon. Then he ate two lemons. He ate three lemons! He was still hungry. He ate four lemons. Tim's mom was surprised. She said, "Wow, Tim! You ate four lemons!" But then, something funny happened. Tim's face turned all red and he started to laugh. He said, "Mom, these are not lemons! They are oranges!" They both laughed and laughed. Tim's mom said, "You are right, Tim. I gave you oranges, not lemons!" They had fun counting oranges, and Tim was not hungry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to measure some celery for a yummy snack. Sue was excited because this was an easy job for her. Sue took the celery and measured it with her hands. She counted one, two, three, four, five pieces of celery. She felt very happy that she did a good job. She put the celery on a plate and carried it to her mom. Her mom was so proud of Sue for measuring the celery. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue! You did a great job and made it look easy!" Sue felt happy and proud of herself. She knew that helping her mom was fun and she couldn't wait to help again.
