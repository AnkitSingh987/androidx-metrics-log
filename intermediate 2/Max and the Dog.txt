Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big, happy dog named Max. Max loved to play in the water and splash all day. He also loved to eat meat. Max was a very good dog, but sometimes he was a little ignorant. One day, Max went to the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They played near the pond and Max would splash in the water. Lily laughed and clapped her hands as Max splashed around. Then, they saw a man with a big bag of meat. Max wanted some, but he didn't know how to ask. So, Max just barked and jumped. The man didn't understand Max and got scared. Lily told Max that he should be nice and not ignorant. Max learned his lesson and promised to be a better dog.

Once upon a time, there was a kind lion named Leo. He lived near a big lake. One day, Leo saw a little bird who was thirsty. The bird wanted to drink water from the lake, but it was too high for her to reach. Leo said, "Don't worry, little bird, I will help you." He went to the lake and used his big paw to splash water up to the bird. The water went up, up, up, and the little bird drank it happily. "Thank you, kind lion," said the little bird. "You are a good friend." Leo smiled and felt happy that he could help. From that day on, Leo and the little bird became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, clear sky. In the sky, there was a bright moon. The moon liked to display its light for all to see. Every night, the moon would shine and make the sky pretty. One night, the moon was shining as always. But then, something unexpected happened. The moon saw a little star that was sad. The star said, "I want to display my light too, but I am too small." The moon thought for a moment. The moon had an idea. It said, "Let's share my light with you, little star." And the moon shared its light with the little star. The little star became bright and happy. Now, they both displayed their light in the clear sky, shining together. And from that night on, the moon and the little star were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Joe. He wanted to sing, but he had to wait. He asked his brother, "When will I get to sing? His reliable brother said, "When it's the right time, you will." Joe waited and waited. When days passed, the right time came. Joe's brother said, "It's time for you to sing now." Joe was so excited! He sang his best and his brother applauded him. Joe was so happy and he couldn't believe it all happened. Joe went to bed that night and he knew he could always count on his reliable older brother to help him when he needed it. He thanked his brother for his trustworthiness. The next day, Joe sang again. He was even better than before and everyone had a lot of fun. Joe knew he made the right decision to wait. His reliable brother helped him have a lot of fun and do something special.

Once upon a time, there was a fragile little bird named Bree. Bree loved to sing and dance in the nice weather. One day, Bree saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to dance on one of its branches. Bree asked a squirrel named Sam, "Can I dance on that branch?" Sam said, "Yes, but be careful. It looks weak." Bree climbed up the tree and started to dance. The other animals watched and started to applaud. But then, the branch broke, and Bree fell down. Bree was sad, and the animals stopped clapping. The weather turned bad, and it began to rain. Bree learned to be more careful when dancing on fragile branches.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play together every day. One day, they decided to make a cake for their moms. They found a big bowl, some flour, some eggs, some sugar, and some milk in the kitchen. "Let's stir everything together," Lily said. She cracked an egg and poured it into the bowl. Ben added some flour and sugar. They took turns stirring with a big spoon. The mixture was sticky and lumpy. "This is fun," Ben said. "But how do we make it look like a cake?" "We need to bake it in the oven," Lily said. "But we have to ask mom first. She knows how to use the oven." They ran to find Lily's mom, who was in the living room. They showed her their bowl of cake batter. "Wow, what a nice surprise," Lily's mom said. "You two are adorable. Do you want to bake your cake now?" "Yes, please," Lily and Ben said together. Lily's mom helped them pour the batter into a cake pan and put it in the oven. She set the timer and told them to wait for the beep. Lily and Ben waited patiently. They smelled the cake as it baked. It smelled sweet and yummy. When the timer beeped, Lily's mom took the cake out of the oven. She let it cool down a bit and then cut a slice for each of them. "Here you go, enjoy your cake," she said. "You did a great job." Lily and Ben tasted their cake. It was soft and moist and delicious. "Thank you, mom," Lily said. "Thank you, mom," Ben said. They hugged Lily's mom and then each other. They were happy and proud of their cake. They shared it with their moms and ate it all up. It was the best cake ever.
