Category: Intermediate
Title: The Vendor Was Selling Cups and the Frog

One day, a vendor was selling cups. He had many cups to sell. The vendor was nice and always smiling. He liked to help people fill their cups with water. A little girl came to the vendor. She said, "Please fill my cup with water." The vendor filled her cup. The little girl was happy and went away. Later, a big man came to the vendor. He looked at the cups and picked an empty one. He said, "Fill my cup with water too." But when the vendor tried to fill the cup, the water disappeared! The big man was not a man, but a magic frog! The frog laughed and jumped away. The vendor was surprised but smiled, and he kept helping people fill their cups.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big tree and a wet puddle under it. Spot was very happy because he loved to play in wet puddles. He jumped in the puddle and splashed water all around. Spot saw a stand where a lady was selling colorful balloons. The lady said, "Hi Spot! Would you like a balloon?" Spot barked and wagged his tail. The lady tied a red balloon to Spot's collar. Spot was very happy with his new balloon. As Spot walked, the wind started to increase. It blew his red balloon up high in the sky. Spot tried to jump and catch it, but he couldn't. He was sad that his balloon was gone. But then, he saw another wet puddle and jumped in it. Spot was happy again, playing in the wet puddle.

Once upon a time, there was a curious queen. She loved to cook. One day, she went to the market to buy some food. She wanted to make a big meal for her friends. She bought apples, carrots, and peas. While she was cooking, a little cat came into her kitchen. The cat said, "Can I help you cook?" The queen was surprised! She didn't know cats could talk. But she said, "Yes, you can help me." The cat helped the queen cook the food. They made soup, salad, and pie. When the friends came to eat, they were very happy. They said, "This is the best food ever!" The queen smiled and said, "Thank you, but it was the cat who helped me." The friends were very surprised, but they loved the cat too. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big, reliable bus named Buddy was driving down the road. He saw a little car named Carrie who was confused about traffic. Buddy wanted to help, so he stopped next to her. "Hi, I'm Buddy! I can teach you about traffic," he said to Carrie. Carrie was happy and said, "Yes, please! I want to learn." Buddy taught Carrie how to stop at red lights and go at green lights. He showed her how to look both ways before crossing the street. They practiced together until Carrie felt good about traffic. Now, Carrie is a smart car who knows all about traffic. She and Buddy are best friends, and they help other cars learn about traffic too. And everyone is safe and happy on the road.

Once upon a time, in a regular town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy sword. He would swing it around and pretend to be a hero. One day, Tim and his friends had a game. They wanted to see who could win by finding the biggest stick. They all ran around the park, looking for the perfect stick to be their sword. Tim found a big stick, but he still liked his toy sword more. In the end, Tim's friend Sam found the biggest stick and won the game. Even though Tim didn't win, he was happy because he had his toy sword. They all played together, laughing and having fun, and Tim knew that his toy sword made him feel like a winner.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved summer. He liked to play outside when the sun was bright. But one day, the sky was gray, and it made Tim sad. Tim had a dog named Max. Max was a good dog, but sometimes he did bad things. One day, Max went into the garden and made a big mess. Tim's mom saw the mess and was very angry. She told Tim he must punish Max for the bad thing he did. Tim did not want to punish Max, but he knew he had to. He told Max to stay in the dog house all day. Max was sad, and Tim was sad too. The gray sky stayed, and the summer day was not fun for Tim and Max. They both wished they could play together, but they could not.
