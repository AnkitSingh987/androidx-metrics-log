Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Ice

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to dive deep in the water. One day, while diving, he saw a big block of ice. The ice was very cold and shiny. Fin was curious and wanted to touch it. As Fin swam closer, he met a soft and friendly jellyfish named Jilly. Jilly said, "Hi, Fin! Do you want to play with me?" Fin smiled and said, "Yes! But first, can you tell me what that big block of ice is?" Jilly laughed and said, "That's ice! It comes from far away, where it's very cold. It's fun to play with, but it can be slippery!" Fin and Jilly decided to play together near the ice. They took turns diving and sliding on the ice. They had so much fun playing together. Fin and Jilly became best friends. From that day on, they always met near the ice to dive and play. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a reliable dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his friends. One day, while Spot was taking a nap under a big tree, he heard a cry for help. It was his friend, the little bird named Blue. Spot ran to Blue and saw that Blue was stuck in a big knot in a tree. Spot tried to help Blue get out of the knot. He pulled and pulled with all his might, but the knot was too strong. Spot did not give up. He thought of a plan to help Blue. Spot called his friend, the strong bear named Brown. Brown came and saw Blue in the knot. With a big swipe of his paw, Brown broke the knot, and Blue was free. Blue was so happy and thanked Spot and Brown. They all became the best of friends. The moral of the story is: always help your friends, and never give up. Sometimes, unexpected help can come from a reliable friend, just like Spot.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Anna's mom had to go out. She told Anna, "Do not touch the stove, it is hot!" Anna was alone and felt helpless. She wanted to make a surprise for her mom. She thought, "I will make a cake!" Anna found a box with a cake mix. She put it in a big bowl with water. She mixed it all up. Anna wanted to bake the cake. She looked at the stove. She remembered what her mom said. Anna did not touch the stove. When her mom came home, Anna showed her the bowl. She said, "I made a cake!" Her mom smiled and said, "Let's bake it together!" They put the cake in the stove and waited. Soon, they took the cake out and it was done. Anna's surprise was revealed and her mom was so proud of her for not touching the stove.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful pirate named Sally. She had a big ship with many friends. Sally and her friends loved to sail the sea and look for treasure. One day, they found a map that showed where a big treasure was hidden. Sally said, "Let's go find it!" They sailed to the island on the map. They had to measure how far they needed to go. They used a long rope to measure the distance. Sally's friend, Tim, said, "We are almost there!" When they got to the island, they started to dig. They dug and dug until they found a big chest. They opened it and found many shiny gold coins. Sally and her friends were very happy. But instead of sharing the treasure, they began to fight over it. They wanted more and more for themselves. In the end, nobody got any of the treasure because they were too busy fighting. The beautiful pirate Sally and her friends learned that being greedy is not good. They should have shared the treasure and been happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very smart. He could count very well. One day, Tim saw a brilliant red bird. The bird sat on his shoulder. Tim was happy. But then, a big cat came. The cat wanted the bird. Tim did not want the cat to get the bird. The cat tried to jump and get the bird. Tim was scared. Tim had an idea. He started to count loudly. "One, two, three!" The cat did not like the loud noise. The cat ran away. The bird was safe. Tim was happy.
