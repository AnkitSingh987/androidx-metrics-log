Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Tiger

Lily loved to play with her toys, especially her stuffed tiger. She named him Toto and took him everywhere. She liked to pretend that Toto was a real tiger and they had adventures in the jungle. One day, Lily's mom told her to clean her room. Lily did not want to clean her room. She wanted to play with Toto. She had an idea. She put Toto on her bed and said, "Toto, you are in charge of cleaning my room. I will be back soon." Then she ran outside to play. Toto did not move. He was a toy. He could not clean the room. He could only watch as the room got messier and messier. He felt guilty. He wished he could help Lily, but he did not know how. Lily played outside for a long time. She had fun with her friends. She forgot about her room and Toto. She did not hear her mom calling her name. When Lily came back inside, she saw her mom standing in front of her room. Her mom looked angry. She pointed at the door and said, "Lily, what did you do? Look at this mess!" Lily felt scared. She ran to her room and saw Toto on her bed. She grabbed him and hugged him. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I asked Toto to clean my room, but he did not do it. He is a bad tiger." Her mom sighed. She said, "Lily, Toto is a toy. He cannot clean your room. You have to do it yourself. You cannot use Toto as an excuse. You have to be responsible." Lily nodded. She felt sorry. She said, "I know, mom. I'm sorry. I will clean my room now. And I will be nice to Toto. He is a good tiger. He is my friend." Her mom smiled. She said, "That's good, Lily. I'm glad you understand. I love you and Toto. Now, let's get to work."

Once upon a time, there was a tiny little puppy that lived in a nice cozy home. One day, the puppy went on an adventure and explored the backyard of his home. Whenever his mom called, the puppy wouldn't listen and ran away. One day, while on his adventure, the puppy found something bad. It was a hole, and it was very deep. His mom warned him and said, "No, puppy! Don't go near the hole. It is bad and you could get hurt." The puppy listened to what his mom warned him. The next day, the puppy found a nice tree in the backyard and he wanted to climb to the top. His mom warned him again and said, "No, puppy! Don't climb the tree. It is bad and you could get hurt." The puppy listened to his mom and stayed away from the tree. The puppy's mom taught him a valuable lesson that day. He learned that it was important to listen to those who have more experience, and that it was important to follow their warnings. If the puppy had not listened to his mom, he may have gotten hurt. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to climb trees. One day, he saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tom started to climb the tree. He went up, up, up. He saw a bird with a red eye. The bird was sad because it could not find any food. Tom wanted to help the bird. Tom looked around and saw some bitter berries on the tree. He picked the berries and gave them to the bird. The bird ate the berries and was happy. Tom and the bird became friends. They played together in the tree all day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went with her mom to the store. Lily loved to watch all the things in the store. She saw a big, red ball. It looked incredible. Her mom said, "We can't buy it today, but maybe next time." The next day, Lily went to the park with her dad. She saw a boy playing with a big, red ball. It was the same incredible ball from the store. Lily watched the boy play and wished she had the ball too. A few days later, Lily's mom took her back to the store. Lily saw the big, red ball again. This time, her mom said, "Surprise! We can buy the ball today!" Lily was so happy. She played with her incredible ball at the park, just like the boy she had watched.
