Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Surf

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to surf on the calm sea. He would try every day to ride the waves. He would swim fast and jump on the water to feel like he was flying. One day, while Fin was trying to surf, he met a big fish named Bob. Bob saw Fin having fun and wanted to try surfing too. Fin was happy to teach Bob how to surf on the calm sea. Together, Fin and Bob tried to surf all day long. They swam fast and jumped on the water, feeling like they were flying. They had so much fun, they became best friends. From that day on, Fin and Bob would always surf together on the calm sea.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He loved to play outside with his friends. Today, Jack was feeling tired. He yawned and said to his mom, "I'm sleepy." His mom said, "Come on, let's go to bed." So Jack went to his bedroom, and he snuggled up under the covers. The bed felt hot and he dozed off into dreamland. In his dreams, Jack found himself in a magical garden. Everywhere he looked there were delicious fruits and vegetables. All the fruits were so sweet and juicy. Jack ate and ate until he was filled up. Then, he laid down in the grass and went to sleep. The sun was bright and warm, and Jack fell into a deep sleep. He was so happy and peaceful. When he woke up, Jack realized it was time to go home. He thanked the garden for the delicious treat. And he knew the next time he felt sleepy, he would think back to his magical garden.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird named Ollie. Ollie was an ostrich who lived in the woods. He was a careless bird and did not watch where he was going. One day, Ollie saw a big tree with yummy red fruits. Ollie said, "I want to eat the fruits!" But the fruits were high up in the tree. Ollie had a plan. He would turn and run fast to hit the tree. The fruits would fall down, and he could eat them. Ollie ran fast and hit the tree. But he was careless and did not see a big rock. He tripped on the rock and fell down. Ollie was sad and hurt. A wise old owl saw Ollie and said, "Ollie, you must be careful and look where you are going." From that day on, Ollie learned to be careful and watch where he was going. He never got hurt again. The moral of the story is to always be careful and pay attention to where you are going.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Amy's mom asked her to polish a big pot. Amy was happy to help. Amy took a cloth and started to rub the pot. The pot was very dirty, but Amy did not give up. As she polished the pot, she found a small, unknown mark on it. Amy was curious about the mark. Amy showed the mark to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "This pot was your grandma's. She put that mark on it." Amy felt happy to know about the mark. She finished polishing the pot, and it looked shiny and new.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box, but it was empty. He wanted to fill it with fun toys. One day, he found a long string in his room. Tim had an idea. He went to his sister, Sally, and said, "Let's make a toy with this string. We can play together!" Sally agreed, and they started to think about what to make. They decided to make a kite, so they could play outside. Tim and Sally worked together to make the kite. They tied the string to the kite and made it fly high in the sky. They were so happy that they could succeed in making a toy. Now, Tim's toy box was not empty anymore, and they had a fun time playing with their new kite.
