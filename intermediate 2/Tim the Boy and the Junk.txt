Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Junk

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box of junk in his room. He did not know what to do with it. He started to think. "What can I make with this junk?" he asked himself. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She looked at the box and said, "I think we can make a fun game with this junk!" They started to put the pieces together. They made a big tower with the junk. They were very happy. But then, the tower fell down and hurt Tim's foot. "Ouch!" he cried. Sue felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't think that would happen." Tim smiled and said, "It's okay. Let's think of a safer game to play with the junk." They laughed and played together all day, making new games with the junk.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a yellow house. In the yellow house lived a nice family with a big dog. The dog loved to play and run all day long. The family had a neighbor who also had a yellow house. The neighbor was nice too, and he had a big cat. The dog and the cat were friends, and they liked to play together. One day, the dog and the cat saw a big tree with a yellow bird. The bird sang a pretty song. The dog and the cat wanted to play with the bird. They tried to climb the tree, but they could not reach the bird. They were very sad. The neighbor saw them and had an idea. The neighbor brought a big, soft pillow and put it under the tree. He told the dog and the cat to rest on the pillow. The dog and the cat were confused, but they did what the neighbor said. They rested on the pillow and waited. After a while, the yellow bird saw the dog and the cat resting on the pillow. The bird liked the big, soft pillow too. So, the yellow bird flew down from the tree and rested on the pillow with the dog and the cat. The dog, the cat, and the yellow bird became best friends. They played together every day and always rested on the big, soft pillow under the tree. The neighbor was happy to see them having fun, and the two yellow houses were filled with joy and laughter.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben had a small spoon that he loved. It was a very expensive spoon, but Ben did not know that. One day, Ben wanted to invite his friends to his house for a fun party. He told his friends, "Come to my house! We will have a great time!" When the friends came to Ben's house, they saw the expensive spoon. They knew it was very special. They told Ben, "Wow, your spoon is very nice! Be careful with it." Ben smiled and said, "Thank you, I will be careful." But he did not know why they said that. At the party, they played games and ate yummy food. Ben used his expensive spoon to eat his food. When the party was over, Ben's friends went home. They said, "Thank you for inviting us, Ben! We had fun. And remember to keep your spoon safe!" Ben nodded and said, "I will!" The next day, Ben found out how special his spoon was. He saw a man on TV who said, "This spoon is very expensive and rare!" Ben was happy that he still had his spoon. He remembered his friends' words and knew they were right. From that day on, Ben always kept his expensive spoon safe and thanked his friends for their good advice.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to read books. Her favorite thing to read was a big book called a novel. She had a difficult time reading it, but she tried her best. One day, Amy found a pretty box. She opened it and saw a magic stone. The stone could make wishes come true. Amy thought hard about what she wanted. She decided to pray for help with her novel. After Amy prayed, she went to bed. The next day, she opened her novel and found that the words were easier to read. She was so happy! The magic stone had helped her. From that day on, Amy enjoyed reading her novel even more.
