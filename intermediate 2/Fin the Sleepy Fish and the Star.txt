Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Sleepy Fish and the Star

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy fish named Fin. Fin loved to surf on the waves in the big blue sea. Every day, he would play with his friends and surf all day long. One day, while Fin was surfing, he saw a sharp rock in the water. He was scared and didn't want to get hurt. So, Fin called his friend, Star the starfish, to help him. Star was very smart and knew just what to do. She told Fin to swim around the sharp rock and not to touch it. Fin listened to Star and swam around the rock. He was safe and happy. Fin and Star went back to surfing and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy shelf that he loved. The shelf was full of popular toys like cars, dolls, and balls. Tom played with his toys every day. One day, Tom saw a new toy on the top of the shelf. He wanted to reach it, but he was too small. He called his big sister, Lily, for help. "Lily, can you help me reach the toy on the top shelf?" Tom asked. Lily came and helped Tom reach the toy. They played with the new toy together and had a lot of fun. From that day on, Tom and Lily became the best of friends and played with the toys on the shelf every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a pink baseball. He was very happy. He wanted to play with the baseball and improve his throws. So, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Lily. Lily also had a baseball. Tim said, "Hi, Lily! I like your baseball. Do you want to play with me?" Lily said, "Yes! Let's play together and improve our throws." Tim and Lily threw the pink baseball back and forth. They were having a lot of fun. As they played, their throws got better and better. They both improved a lot. At the end of the day, they were very happy and tired. Tim and Lily became good friends, all thanks to the pink baseball.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very careless. He always made a mess. One day, Tom wanted to go to a party. He needed to wear a nice suit to look good. Tom went to the store to buy a suit. He saw a pretty blue one. He tried it on and it fit just right. Tom was very happy. The store man said, "Please be careful with your new suit, Tom." At the party, Tom was having fun. He danced and laughed with his friends. But then, Tom was careless again. He spilled juice on his suit. His friend said, "Oh no, Tom! Your suit is dirty." Tom was sad. He wanted to complete the party with a clean suit. His friend helped him clean it. They used a cloth and water. The suit was clean again. Tom learned to be more careful and had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a big, busy hotel. Many people came and went every day. One day, a little cat came to the hotel. The cat was very tired and wanted to rest. The cat found a quiet room and began to stretch. The cat stretched and stretched its legs, and then something unexpected happened. The cat became very, very big! The cat was now bigger than the hotel! Everyone was surprised and didn't know what to do. The big cat was kind and helped the people. The cat used its long legs to help build a new, bigger hotel. After that, the cat stretched again and became small once more. The people were happy, and the cat stayed in the new hotel, taking naps and stretching every day.

Once upon a time, there was an old lady who lived in a small house. She had a comb that she loved very much. One day, she lost her comb and was very sad. She asked her friend, the cat, "Did you see my comb?" The cat said, "No, but I will help you find it." They looked all around the house, but they could not find the comb. Then they met a bird who said, "I can make you a new comb!" The old lady was happy. The bird found some sticks and made a comb for the old lady. She said, "Thank you, bird!" The old lady, the cat, and the bird were all happy and became good friends.
