Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Loyal Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to march around the big loop in the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They would march together every day, laughing and having fun. One sunny day, while Max and Lily were marching around the loop, they saw a little boy crying. He had lost his toy. Max and Lily decided to help the boy find his toy. They looked high and low, under trees and behind rocks. Finally, Max found the toy and gave it back to the boy. The boy was very happy, and he thanked Max and Lily for their help. The moral of the story is that it is good to be loyal and help others. Max and Lily showed kindness by helping the little boy find his toy, and they made a new friend. When we are loyal and help others, we spread happiness and make the world a better place.

Once upon a time, there was a gray dog. The gray dog had a collar. The collar was red. The dog liked to smile and play. One day, the gray dog went for a walk. The dog saw a big tree. The dog wanted to play near the tree. The dog ran to the tree and smiled. The gray dog played by the tree for a long time. The dog got very tired. The dog wanted to go home. But the dog did not know the way. The gray dog was lost. The dog did not smile anymore.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk in the park. She saw a mysterious light near a big tree. She walked to the tree and found a small spark on the ground. The spark was shiny and pretty. Amy said, "Hello, spark! My name is Amy. Do you need help?" The spark jumped up and down. It was happy to see her. The spark said, "Thank you, Amy! I am lost. Can you help me find my way home?" Amy and the spark walked together. They saw a big rock. The spark jumped on the rock and started to shine very bright. The rock moved and showed a secret door. Amy and the spark went through the door. Inside, they found a room full of more sparks. They were all different colors and very pretty. The first spark said, "Thank you for helping me, Amy. You found my family!" Amy was happy to help the spark. The sparks gave Amy a big hug and a pretty stone as a thank you gift. Amy went back to the park and played with her new stone. She now had a secret place to visit her spark friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the snow. One day, he found a big icy hill. Tim thought it would be fun to slide down the hill. Tim found some stuff to sit on, like a big box. He put the box at the top of the hill and sat on it. Tim was so excited to slide down the icy hill. He gave a little push and off he went! The wind blew in his face as he slid down the hill. It was so much fun! When Tim got to the bottom, he saw his friend, Sue. Sue was sad because she didn't have anything to slide on. Tim thought for a moment and then decided to share his box with Sue. They both took turns sliding down the icy hill, laughing and having fun together. In the end, Tim learned that sharing his stuff with others made playing even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a lively girl called Amy. She was a happy three year old. One day, Amy went out to play. When she got outside, she saw a piece of gum on the ground. She was excited. She grabbed the gum and put it in her pocket. It felt so good to have something to play with! When she got home, Amy's mum told her that she mustn't eat the gum. Amy was sad, but she knew that it was important to listen to her mum. But then, something amazing happened. Amy's mum said she could stick the gum to the window and make a picture with it. Amy smiled and grabbed the gum. She stuck it to the window and made a big blob. Then, she used her finger to make a happy face in the middle. The lively little girl laughed with delight. She had made a lovely picture that made her smile every time she looked at it.
