Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Monkey and the Banana

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Tim. Tim loved to eat bananas. One day, he saw a big, yellow banana high up in a tree. Tim wanted to lift himself up to get the banana. Tim called out to his friend, the bird. "Bird, can you help me lift up to get the banana?" The bird said, "Yes, Tim, I can help you. Hold on to my legs, and I will fly up to the banana." So, Tim held on to the bird's legs, and the bird lifted Tim up to the banana. Tim was so happy that he could reach the banana. He said, "Thank you, bird, for helping me. That is very fair of you." They both enjoyed the banana together and played all day long.

Once upon a time there was a cow. She was very far away in a distant field. Every night she would glow with a very bright light. One day, a little boy was walking past the cow’s field. He saw the cow glowing and was very curious. He decided to go closer and take a look. The little boy slowly opened the gate and walked right into the cow's field. The cow did not like this and she made a loud noise. The little boy was start and ran away. The next night the cow was still glowing. But this time, the little boy stayed away. He was too scared of the cow and did not go close again. The end.

One day, a brave little girl named Jen went for a walk in the snow. She saw an igloo, and she knew that something special must be inside. Jen wanted to find out what was inside, but she was a bit scared. She closed her eyes and opened the door. When she opened her eyes, Jen saw the most beautiful thing she had ever seen. It was a bright flower blooming in the middle of the snow. She knew that this flower could stay alive even in the cold snow. Jen was so brave, she decided not to let the cold stop her. She put a coat on and went outside to search for more special things. Sure enough, she found a few more flowers blooming in the snow. Jen was amazed. She had found something so special, even though she thought there was nothing in the snow. Jen learned that if you stay brave, you can discover something beautiful.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was worried because she had to act in a play at school. She had to wear a funny hat and say her lines in front of everyone. One day, her mom said, "Sue, we have a meeting with your teacher today." Sue was scared. She thought her teacher would be mad about her being worried. But at the meeting, her teacher just smiled and said, "Don't worry, Sue. You will do great in the play!" On the day of the play, Sue put on her funny hat and went on stage. She remembered what her teacher said at the meeting and acted her best. Everyone clapped and cheered for Sue. She was so happy and not worried anymore.

Tom and Sam were friends. They liked to play with toys and draw in their notebooks. One day, they found a big bell in the park. It was shiny and loud. "Let's ring the bell!" Tom said. "OK!" Sam said. They took turns to pull the rope and make the bell ring. It was fun. They laughed and shouted. But then, a mean man came out of a house near the park. He was angry and scary. He had a big stick in his hand. "Stop that noise!" he yelled. "You are bothering me!" He ran towards Tom and Sam. They were afraid. They dropped their toys and notebooks and ran away. The mean man picked up their notebooks. He saw their drawings. He did not like them. He tore the pages and threw them in the mud. He made the notebooks filthy. Tom and Sam hid behind a tree. They saw what the mean man did. They felt sad and angry. They wanted their notebooks back. But they were too scared to go near the mean man. They cried and hugged each other. They wished they never rang the bell.
