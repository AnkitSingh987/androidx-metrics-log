Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Stairs

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big dollhouse. They liked to pretend they were a family and make up stories. One day, they had a problem. Sara wanted to play with the dollhouse in the living room, where it was warm and cozy. Ben wanted to play with it in the basement, where it was dark and spooky. They could not agree on where to go. They decided to discuss it. Sara said, "The living room is better because it has a nice rug and a big window. The basement is cold and scary. There might be spiders or rats." Ben said, "The basement is better because it has a lot of space and a secret door. The living room is boring and noisy. There might be a baby or a dog." They argued and argued, but they could not find a solution. They both wanted to play with the dollhouse, but they did not want to go to the other's place. They felt miserable and sad. Then, they had an idea. They could move the dollhouse to the stairs. The stairs were in the middle of the house, so they were not too warm or too cold, not too bright or too dark. They could use the steps as different rooms and pretend they had a tall house. They both liked this idea. They carried the dollhouse to the stairs and set it up. They had fun playing with it and making up new stories. They were happy and glad. They learned that sometimes they had to compromise and share. They hugged and said, "We love you, twin."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was an obedient boy and he always listened to his mom. His mom wanted to prepare a yummy treat for him, so she got a flame and lit it up. The flame made a nice cooking heat. Then, Mom got out the flour and milk and put them into a bowl. She mixed them together until it was a thick, yummy goo. "Now it's time for the flame," said Mom and she put the bowl on top of the flame. She stirred the goo round and round until it was warm and soft. Tom couldn't wait to try it. So once the goo was ready, Mom gave him a spoon and the yummy treat. He gobbled it up and said, "Yummy! Mommy, can we make this again?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tom. But only if you stay obedient." So Tom promised to keep listening and was happy that he would get to enjoy the yummy treat again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma loved to dance and sing. One day, she saw a big stage in the park. It was very attractive with bright colors and pretty lights. Emma wanted to go on the stage and dance for everyone. Emma asked her mom if she could go on the stage. Her mom said yes, but she had to be careful. Emma was so excited! She ran to the stage and climbed up the steps. She reached for the microphone, but it was too high. She tried to jump, but she could not reach it. A nice boy named Jack saw Emma trying to reach the microphone. He was taller than Emma, so he helped her. Jack reached up and got the microphone for Emma. She was so happy! Emma and Jack danced and sang on the attractive stage. Everyone clapped and cheered for them.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy cars in the garden. They liked to make noises like the motors and race them on the grass. Tom had a red car and Lily had a blue car. They were having fun. But then, Tom saw a bright light in the sky. He looked up and saw a big plane flying over them. He wanted to see it better, so he ran to the fence and climbed on it. He forgot about his car. Lily saw Tom leaving his car on the grass. She wanted to play with it, so she ran to get it. She did not see the bright light or the big plane. She only saw the red car. "Tom, can I play with your car?" she shouted. But Tom did not hear her. He was too busy watching the plane. He blinked his eyes to see it clearer. He did not see Lily taking his car. Lily picked up the red car and smiled. She liked it better than her blue car. She turned it on and made it go. She did not see Tom on the fence. Tom heard the noise of his car. He turned his head and saw Lily with it. He got angry. He wanted his car back. "Lily, give me my car!" he yelled. But Lily did not hear him. She was too busy playing with the car. She did not see Tom jumping off the fence. Tom ran to Lily and tried to grab his car. But Lily did not let go. They both pulled the car and fought over it. They did not see the big rock on the ground. The car slipped from their hands and hit the rock. It broke into pieces. The motor stopped and the light went out. The car was ruined. Tom and Lily looked at the car and cried. They had no more car to play with. They were sad and mad at each other. They did not say sorry. They did not hug. They did not play anymore. They had a bad ending.
