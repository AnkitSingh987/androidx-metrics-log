Category: Intermediate
Title: Pinky the Pig and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a little pig named Pinky. Pinky lived on a farm with his family. They were very happy and safe in their cozy home. One day, when Pinky was sleeping, he heard a loud noise. Pinky woke up and saw a big storm outside. It was very scary. Pinky knew he needed to help his family stay safe. Pinky ran to his mom and dad. He told them about the storm. They all worked together to make their home safe. They closed the doors and windows. The wind and rain stayed outside. The storm went away and the sun came out. Pinky and his family were safe and happy. They learned that working together keeps everyone safe. And Pinky knew that even a little pig can help in a big way.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a big, ugly toy frog. The frog was green and had big eyes. Lily loved her frog very much. One day, Lily was playing with her frog under the big tree in her yard. She was having so much fun. Then, it was time to go inside for dinner. Lily put her frog down and said, "I promise I will come back for you!" After dinner, Lily went back to the tree to get her frog. She looked under the tree, but her frog was gone! Lily was very sad. She looked everywhere for her ugly frog. Finally, Lily found her frog under her bed. She was so happy! She gave her frog a big hug and said, "I promise I will never leave you again!" From that day on, Lily and her ugly frog were always together.

Lily and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, it was very cold and windy. Lily and Ben wore hats and coats, but they still felt cold. "Let's go on the slide!" Ben said. "It will be fun!" "OK!" Lily said. They ran to the slide and climbed up the ladder. The slide was metal and shiny. It was also very slippery. Ben went first. He slid down fast and laughed. "Whee!" he said. "That was cool!" Lily smiled and followed him. She slid down too, but she did not laugh. She screamed. The slide was too cold and it hurt her skin. She landed on the ground and cried. "Ow, ow, ow!" she said. "My legs are red and sore!" Ben ran to her and looked at her legs. They had frostbite. He felt sorry for her. He tried to hug her and make her feel better. "I'm sorry, Lily," he said. "I did not know the slide was so cold. Are you OK?" Lily shivered and shook her head. She was not OK. She was in pain and scared. She wanted her mom. "Help!" she said. "I want my mom!" Ben did not know what to do. He was not clever. He did not have a phone or a car. He did not know where Lily's mom was. He looked around and saw no one. They were alone in the park. He felt cold and scared too. He wished they had not gone on the slide. He wished they had stayed on the stand and watched the birds. He wished they had listened to their moms and stayed home. But it was too late. They had made a bad choice. And now they had to pay for it.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly reindeer named Remy. Remy had a big red nose and loved to play with his friends in the snow. One day, Remy met a little girl named Lily. Lily said, "Remy, I have a request. Can you help me find my lost toy?" Remy said, "Yes, I will help you, Lily." Remy and Lily looked everywhere for the toy. They looked in the snow, under the trees, and behind the rocks. Finally, they found the toy near a big snowman. Lily was so happy and said, "Thank you, Remy! You are the best reindeer friend!" Remy and Lily played together in the snow all day. They were very happy and had lots of fun. And from that day on, Remy and Lily were the best of friends.
