Category: Intermediate
Title: Fred and the Frog

Fred was a young boy who loved playing outside. One day, he was running around when he noticed something unusual on the ground. When he got closer, he realized it was a head! He was very scared, but he asked it what it was doing there. The head told him it had been searching for its body. Fred wanted to help. He asked the head where it had last seen its body. The head told Fred it had been in the big field near the pond. Fred ran off to look for it, but all he found was a frog. He took the frog back to the head and asked if it belonged to it. The head told Fred no. But the frog was very excited. It asked the head if it wanted to join it on an adventure. The head was surprised, but agreed! Together, they traveled through the forest, until they spotted an unusual sight. A body, with the head of the frog! The body had been looking for its head, just like the head had been searching for its body. The head and body finally found each other and were very thankful for Fred's help. From then on, the head and body were always together!

Once upon a time, there was a healthy boy named Tom. Tom loved to whistle and play with his friends. One day, he found a big, round orange. It was the biggest orange he had ever seen. Tom was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tom ran to Sam's house and said, "Look, Sam! I found a big orange!" Sam looked at the orange and smiled. "Let's whistle a happy song and dance with the orange," said Sam. So, they whistled and danced, having lots of fun with the big orange. As they played, a big dog came running towards them. The dog wanted the orange too. Tom and Sam tried to run away, but the dog was too fast. The dog jumped and took the orange from them. Tom and Sam were very sad. They lost their big orange and couldn't play with it anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max liked to run and play all day. One day, Max saw a big, red car. The car could drive very quick. Max wanted to see the car drive. Max asked the car, "Can you drive quick for me?" The car said, "Yes, Max! I can drive quick for you." The car started to drive. It went very quick. Max was very happy. He ran next to the car as it drove. Max and the car became good friends. They played together every day. Max liked to watch the car drive quick. The car liked to see Max run and play. They were happy together.

Lily and Max were playing hide and seek in the park. Lily was the seeker and Max was the hider. Lily counted to ten and then opened her eyes. She looked around and saw a big tree, a slide, a swing, and a bench. Where could Max be? She ran to the tree and looked behind it. No Max. She ran to the slide and looked under it. No Max. She ran to the swing and looked on it. No Max. She ran to the bench and saw a big red bag on it. She thought it was Max's lunch bag. She opened it and saw a sandwich, a banana, and a cookie. She was hungry and wanted to eat the cookie, but she knew it was not nice to take Max's food. She heard a giggle behind the bench. She turned around and saw Max hiding there. He had a big smile on his face. He had revealed himself. He said, "You found me, Lily! Good job! Do you want to share my lunch?" Lily nodded and smiled. She said, "Yes, please! Your lunch looks very pretty and yummy!" They sat on the bench and ate the sandwich, the banana, and the cookie. They were happy and full. They decided to play hide and seek again, but this time Max was the seeker and Lily was the hider.
