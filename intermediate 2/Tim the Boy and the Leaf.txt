Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Leaf

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big line of ants. They were on a march. Tim thought it was fun to watch them walk in a line. Tim saw a sad ant sitting on a leaf. He asked, "Why are you sad, little ant?" The ant said, "I am bad at marching. I always guess which way to go, and I am wrong." Tim wanted to help the ant. He said, "I will show you how to march. Just follow me." They marched together. The ant was happy and not bad at marching anymore. They became good friends and marched together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. The little fish lived in a big, deep pond. The pond had many colors. The fish loved to swim and play with his friends. One day, the little fish saw a beautiful flower. The flower was a new color he had never seen before. The little fish wanted to become that color too. He wished and wished to change his color. The next day, the little fish woke up and saw that he had become the same color as the flower! He was so happy. He swam around the deep pond, showing all his friends his new color. They all loved it, and they played together all day long.

There was an elderly man who was getting ready to go for a drive in his jeep. His son asked him what he was doing and he replied that he was going to release the jeep. The young boy wondered why they had to release the jeep so his father explained that they had to be careful because the jeep was old. He said that if they released it very slowly then it would not get too tired. The boy thought about this for a moment and then asked his father why it was important for the jeep to not get tired. The old man smiled and said that if the jeep got too tired then it might not take them for a very nice drive. So the elderly man carefully opened the jeep and the two of them got in. They drove around the town, taking in the sights and enjoying the day. The boy thought the ride was the best ever! The elderly man had made sure to release the jeep carefully and it took them all around without getting too tired.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a mysterious shield in his yard. It was big and shiny. Tim was very happy and picked it up. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look, I found a shield!" Sam looked at the shield and wanted to play with it too. They decided to slide down the hill with the shield like a sled. But they both wanted to go first. "I want to go first!" said Tim. "No, I want to go first!" said Sam. They were both upset and did not know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. "Let's both slide down the hill together!" he said. Sam agreed and they both held the mysterious shield. They slid down the hill, laughing and having fun. From that day on, Tim and Sam shared the shield and played together. They learned that sharing was more fun than fighting.

Once, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys all day. One day, Tim found some gum on the table. He thought it would be fun to play with the gum. So, he took the gum and started to make a big, sticky ball. Tim's mom saw him playing with the gum and said, "Tim, please do not play with the gum. It will make a big mess." But Tim did not listen to his mom. He kept playing with the gum and it got stuck to his toys. Tim felt sorry because he did not listen to his mom. Tim's mom helped him clean up the mess. She told him that it is important to listen and not delay when someone tells you to do something. Tim learned his lesson and promised to listen to his mom next time. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and not delay doing what they say.
