Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boots

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the rain. He would put on his rain boots and splash in the puddles with a big smile on his face. One rainy day, Tim found something shiny in a puddle. He picked it up and saw it was money! Tim was so happy. He knew he could buy his favorite toy with the money he found. Tim went to the store with his mom and bought the toy. He played with it all day, even in the rain. Tim knew that sometimes, even on rainy days, good things can happen. And that made him very happy.

Once there was a dog who loved to bow. His name was Spot. He was brown and had a white spot on his back. Spot went to the park to show off his bowing skills. At the park, he saw a cat. Spot was excited and he bowed to the cat. However, the cat just got an itch and madly scratched itself instead of bowing back. Spot felt sad. Then, Spot noticed something interesting. It was a big red ball. "Wow, that looks interesting," Spot thought. He chased the ball and bowed in front of it. The ball did not move, so Spot bowed again and again. Suddenly, the ball spoke! "Stop bowing and play with me!" Spot was so surprised, he stopped bowing and started to play with the ball. Together, they had lots of fun.

One day, a red jeep went to the park. The jeep wanted to play with the other cars. But the red jeep could not find any cars to play with. The red jeep felt sad and started to search for friends. The red jeep searched high and low. It looked behind trees and under bushes. While searching, the red jeep got dirty and felt embarrassed. It wanted to look nice for its new friends. Finally, the red jeep found a group of cars playing. The cars saw the dirty red jeep and laughed. The red jeep felt bad, but then a blue car came over. The blue car said, "Let's all help clean our new friend." The cars worked together to clean the red jeep. Soon, the red jeep was shiny and clean. The red jeep felt happy and played with its new friends all day.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny froggy who loved to pay. He had to pay for all the things he wanted to do, like going to the park to play. One day, he was walking in the park when he saw a special rock. When he touched it, it made a very lively sound. Suddenly, sparkles of magic flew out of the rock and filled the air. Froggy was so excited, he didn't know what to do. Suddenly, he knew! He decided to pay the rock some money! Very carefully, he put a penny in the rock and the magic sparkles just kept growing. The park was very lively and everyone was so happy. Even Froggy was having a great time. All the sparkles gave the park a special kind of magic that no one could explain. Froggy had a feeling this park was the best place to pay! He knew he'd be back again soon!

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to read. They had many books on their bookshelf in their room. But they did not like to share their books with each other. They always said, "This is my book, not yours!" One day, their mom came to their room and saw their bookshelf. She was troubled. She saw that some books were torn, some books were dirty, and some books were missing. She asked Lily and Ben, "What happened to your books? Why are they in such a bad state?" Lily and Ben looked at each other and did not answer. They knew they had been fighting over their books and not taking care of them. They felt ashamed. Their mom said, "Books are precious. They are your friends. They teach you many things. You should respect them and love them. And you should share them with each other. Sharing is caring. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nodded. They said, "We are sorry, mom. We will share our books and take care of them. We will be nice to each other." Their mom smiled. She said, "That's good. I'm proud of you. Now, let's fix your bookshelf and make it neat and tidy. And then, you can choose a book to read together." Lily and Ben hugged their mom. They said, "Thank you, mom. We love you. And we love our books." They helped their mom to fix their bookshelf and then picked a book to read together. They learned to share and enjoy their books. They were happy.
