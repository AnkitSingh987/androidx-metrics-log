Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Coin

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim found a mysterious copper coin. He decided to spend it on candy. Tim went to the candy store to buy his favorite candy. At the store, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "I lost a copper coin today!" Tim felt sad for Sue. He thought maybe the coin he found was Sue's. Tim said, "I found a copper coin. Is it yours?" Sue looked at the coin and said, "Yes! Thank you, Tim!" Tim and Sue became friends. They decided to spend the coin together and share the candy. But when they gave the coin to the store man, something unexpected happened. The store man said, "This is a magic coin! It can buy all the candy in the store!" Tim and Sue were very happy and shared the candy with all their friends.

Once upon a time there was a mighty cat. She wanted to reach the highest tree in the forest, but she was scared to try. All her friends saw and told her that she could do it, but she just didn't believe them. One day she decided to give it a try and began to climb the tree. As she went higher and higher, she felt scared, but then something amazing happened. Every time she felt scared, her friends cheered her on and helped her to keep going. Finally, she reached the top of the tree. She looked around and felt proud and happy. She had achieved her goal. "You did it!" shouted her friends. The cat looked down and was amazed. She had never realized how mighty she was, until now. This taught the cat a valuable lesson. She learned that if you recognize how brave and strong you are, you can reach any goal.

Once upon a time, there was a crazy dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. He was very happy and always had fun. One day, Spot saw a faucet outside. He was very thirsty, so he went to drink some water. But, oh no! The sun was so hot that day, it made the faucet burn. Spot was sad and still thirsty. Then, Spot had an idea. He found a big bowl and put it under the faucet. He waited for the sun to go away. When the sun was gone, the faucet was not hot anymore. Spot could drink the water from the bowl. He was happy and not thirsty anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play and pretend he was a rich king. He had a big box of gold made of yellow paper. He put the gold in his little red wagon and pulled it around his yard. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue wanted to play with Tim and his gold. They both pretended to be rich kings and queens. They had fun playing together and sharing the gold. After a long day of playing, Tim and Sue were tired. They put the gold back in the box and said goodbye. They were happy to have a new friend and couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his cheap toy car. One day, Tim was playing outside near the drain. He was having so much fun! Suddenly, the wind blew and Tim's toy car rolled into the drain. Tim was very sad. He tried to reach for the toy car, but his arms were too short. He didn't know what to do. Then, Tim saw a big stick. He used the stick to reach into the drain. Tim was happy when he got his cheap toy car back. He played with his car all day and stayed away from the drain.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her pet cat, Whiskers. Mia liked to play with Whiskers and look at the sky. She was always full of wonder. One sunny day, Mia and Whiskers went for a walk. They saw a big tree with a small door at the bottom. Mia thought it was very strange. She opened the door and found a fit bird inside. The bird was weeping. Mia wanted to help the bird. Mia and Whiskers took the bird home. They gave it food and water. To their surprise, the bird started to grow bigger and bigger! It was a magic bird! The bird thanked Mia and Whiskers for their help. It took them on a fun ride in the sky. Mia and Whiskers had a great day and made a new friend.
