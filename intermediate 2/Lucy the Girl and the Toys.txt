Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Lucy felt anxious. She did not want to go to bed. Her mom said, "Lucy, it is time for bed." Lucy put on her bathrobe and walked to her room. In her room, she saw her toys were not in their place. She did not want her toys to disturb her sleep. So, she picked up her toys and put them away. Then, she went to bed. The next morning, Lucy felt happy because she had a good sleep. She told her mom, "I put my toys away and slept well." Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Lucy. When we keep our room clean, we sleep better." The moral of the story is to keep our room clean for a good sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet dinosaur named Dino. Dino lived in a big forest. He had many friends like birds, rabbits, and squirrels. They all liked to play together. One sunny day, Dino and his friends were playing hide and seek. Dino was very big, so it was hard for him to hide. He found a big tree and decided to lie down behind it. He tried to be very quiet so his friends could not find him. As Dino lay there, a little bird flew by and saw him. The bird said, "Dino, why are you lying down?" Dino whispered, "I am hiding. Please be quiet so my friends don't find me." The bird nodded and flew away. Soon, Dino's friends found him. They all laughed and said, "Dino, you are too big to hide, but we love playing with you anyway!" Dino smiled and they all played together until the sun went down. They were all very happy friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. It was cold, so he wore a big coat. He saw his friend, Sally, and they decided to play with their toy guns. "Ready, set, shoot!" said Tim. They both made a loud sound with their mouths as they pretended to shoot their toy guns. They laughed and ran around, having lots of fun. As they played, they saw a big tree. "Let's hide behind the tree," said Sally. They hid and pretended to shoot from there. They heard a real bird making a sound from the tree. They looked up and saw the bird. "Hi, bird!" said Tim. The bird looked at them and flew away. Tim and Sally laughed and continued to play until it was time to go home. They said goodbye and went inside to get warm from the cold.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous armchair. It was so big that many friends could sit on it. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily and her dog, Spot, found the armchair in a park. They liked it very much and played on it all day. The next day, Lily and Spot went back to the park. They hoped the big armchair was still there. But when they got there, the armchair was gone! They looked around and saw a little boy named Tom sitting on it. He had moved the armchair to another part of the park. Lily and Spot were sad at first, but then they saw Tom was all alone. They walked over to him and asked if they could all play together. Tom said yes, and they all became good friends. They played on the enormous armchair every day, and they were all very happy.

One day, a clever dog named Max found a flashlight. He did not know what it was, so he decided to learn. He went to his friend, a wise old cat named Whiskers. Max asked, "Whiskers, what is this thing?" Whiskers looked at the flashlight and said, "That is a flashlight, Max. It makes light when it is dark. You can learn to use it." Max was very happy. He learned how to use the flashlight. At night, he turned it on and saw a big, dark shadow on the wall. Max was scared. He thought it was a big monster. But then, Whiskers came and said, "Don't be scared, Max. That is just your shadow. The flashlight makes it big on the wall." Max felt silly and laughed. Now he was not scared of the dark anymore. Max and Whiskers played with the flashlight and had lots of fun.
