Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a nice cat. The cat had a job. His job was to deliver mail. He liked his job very much. Every day, he would take letters and give them to people. The cat had a name. His name was Tim. One day, Tim met a dog. The dog was big and had a big smile. The dog said, "Hi, my name is Max. What is your name?" Tim said, "My name is Tim. I deliver mail." Max said, "That is a nice job. Can I help you?" Tim said, "Yes, you can help me." Tim and Max became good friends. They delivered mail together. People liked to see them. They were very happy. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They saw a distant hill with many rocks. They wanted to sort the rocks by size. Tim said, "Spot, let's sort the rocks from small to big!" As they were sorting the rocks, dark clouds came. Soon, it started to rain. The rain made a flood near the hill. Tim and Spot were scared. They did not know what to do. Just then, a big friendly frog hopped over. The frog said, "Jump on my back! I can help you!" Tim and Spot jumped on the frog's back, and the frog hopped away from the flood. They were safe and happy. The frog took them back to their home, and they all had a fun day together.

One day, a happy boy named Tim found a big box. Inside the box was a computer. Tim wanted to complete a puzzle on the computer. He turned it on and started to play. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play too. "What are you doing?" she asked. "I am trying to complete this puzzle," Tim said. Sue helped Tim and they worked together. They were close to finishing the puzzle when something unexpected happened. The computer screen changed, and a big "Thank You" appeared. Tim and Sue were happy they completed the puzzle, but they were surprised too. They learned that working together is more fun and makes things easier. And that was the day Tim and Sue became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and eat spicy food. One day, his mommy made a very spicy soup for lunch. Tim was so happy and ate a lot of soup. After lunch, Tim started to feel a little sick. His mommy took him to the doctor. The doctor said, "We need to take an x-ray to see what is wrong." Tim was scared, but his mommy held his hand and told him it would be okay. The x-ray showed that Tim's tummy was full of spicy soup. The doctor told Tim to drink lots of water to help his tummy feel better. Tim learned that eating too much spicy food can make you sick. From that day on, he made sure to increase the amount of water he drank when he ate spicy food.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, rough dog named Max. Lucy and Max loved to play outside. They liked to run, jump, and pick flowers. One day, they went for a walk in the park. As they walked, Lucy saw a big tree. She wanted to pick the apples from the tree. She asked Max, "Can you help me pick apples?" Max barked and jumped up to get the apples. But Max was too rough, and the apples fell on the ground. Lucy started to complain. She said, "Max, you are too rough! Now the apples are dirty." Just then, a friendly squirrel came out of the tree. The squirrel picked up an apple with its little hands and gave it to Lucy. She was very surprised and happy. Lucy said, "Thank you, squirrel!" From that day on, Lucy, Max, and the squirrel became best friends. They played together every day and never complained again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very eager to see the world. He wanted to go on a big adventure. One day, he decided to go for a walk in the woods. In the woods, Tim met a wise owl. The owl said, "Hello, Tim! Where are you going?" Tim replied, "I want to see the world!" The owl thought for a moment and said, "The world is big, but it can be scary too. Always be kind and help others, and the world will be a better place." Tim walked deeper into the woods and saw a small bunny stuck in a hole. He remembered what the owl said and decided to help the bunny. The bunny was so happy and thanked Tim. Tim felt good and knew he made the world a better place. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others, and the world will be a better place.
