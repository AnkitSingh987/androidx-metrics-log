Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rock

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big eraser. The eraser was very rich and shiny. Tim was so happy to have it. He went to show it to his friend, Sam. Sam wanted the eraser too, but Tim did not want to share. They began to fight over the eraser. As they fought, they did not see a big rock in their way. Both boys stumble on the rock and the eraser flew out of their hands. It landed far away from them. A little girl named Lily found the rich eraser. She saw Tim and Sam still fighting. Lily went to them and said, "Stop fighting! You can both share the eraser." Tim and Sam looked at each other and felt bad. They agreed to share the eraser and all three became good friends.

Lily loved to play with her mom's makeup. She liked to put on lipstick, eyeshadow, and blush. She felt like a princess when she looked in the mirror. But her mom did not like it when Lily took her makeup. She said it was not for children and it could make Lily sick. One day, Lily saw her mom's makeup bag on the table. She wanted to play with it so badly. She thought her mom would not notice if she took it to her room. She grabbed the bag and ran to her room. She opened the bag and saw all the pretty colors. She started to put on some makeup on her face. But then she heard her mom's voice. "Lily, where are you? What are you doing?" Her mom sounded angry. Lily felt scared. She tried to hide the makeup bag under her pillow. But it was too late. Her mom opened the door and saw Lily's face. She gasped. "Lily, what have you done? You took my makeup without asking! That's very naughty! You know you are not allowed to play with my makeup! Look at your face! You look like a clown! And you made a mess of my makeup! How could you?" Her mom scolded Lily. Lily felt miserable. She did not mean to make her mom mad. She just wanted to have some fun. She started to cry. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to be pretty like you. Please don't be mad at me. I love you." Her mom sighed. She felt sorry for Lily too. She knew Lily did not understand why makeup was not for children. She hugged Lily and said, "I love you too, Lily. But you have to listen to me. Makeup is not for children. It can hurt your skin and your eyes. And it is not what makes you pretty. You are pretty just the way you are. You don't need makeup to be a princess. You are my princess. Now let's go and wash your face. And please don't take my makeup again. OK?" Lily nodded. She said, "OK, mom. I'm sorry. I won't take your makeup again. Can we still play dress up?" Her mom smiled. She said, "Yes, we can still play dress up. But with clothes and accessories, not with makeup. OK?" Lily smiled too. She said, "OK, mom. Thank you. I love you." They went to the bathroom and washed Lily's face. Then they went to the closet and picked some clothes and accessories to play dress up. They had fun and laughed together. Lily felt happy again. She learned her lesson. She did not need makeup to be a princess. She had her mom. And her mom loved her.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to joke. He thought silly jokes were so funny! He had a silly little nose that he wiggled every time he tried to joke. One day, something wacky happened. Suddenly the little boy's nose started making jokes! It was the craziest thing! He couldn't believe it. Every time he wiggled his nose, out came a funny joke. Everyone who heard his jokes were so amazed. They laughed and laughed, and soon everyone wanted to hear the little boy's jokes all the time. Pretty soon, he was known all over town as "the nose that jokes"! It was the craziest thing, but the little boy was so happy. Every day he would wiggle his nose and make everyone laugh. The little boy was so glad he had the nose that jokes!
