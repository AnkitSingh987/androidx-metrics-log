Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Hairy Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Bob. Bob liked to serve his friends. He would bring them food and toys. One day, Bob found a bottle of perfume. He thought it smelled nice. Bob wanted to share the perfume with his friends. He thought it would make them happy. So, he took the perfume to his friend's house. His friends were playing outside. Bob served the perfume to his friends. They all smelled it. But, they did not like it. The perfume made them feel sick. They all went home sad. In the end, Bob learned that some things are not good to share. He felt bad that he made his friends sick. Next time, he will be more careful.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to school. He was very excited because it was his first day. Tim walked into the big room and saw many other kids. They were all playing and having fun. Tim felt a little shy and embarrassed because he did not know anyone. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was playing with blocks. Tim walked up to Sue and said, "Hi, can I play too?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's build a big tower!" They started to build the tower with the blocks. It was fun! Suddenly, another boy named Max came over and pointed at the tower. He said, "That tower is not good. I can build a better one." Tim and Sue felt sad. They did not like what Max said. Tim looked at Sue and said, "Let's build an even bigger tower together!" Sue agreed and they both worked hard to make the best tower they could. In the end, they were proud of their tower and became good friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. Lily saw a pair of soft mittens. She liked the mittens so much. They were red and blue. Lily asked her mom if she could have the mittens. Her mom said yes. Lily wore her new mittens every day. She liked how warm and soft they were. One day, while playing outside, Lily lost one of her mittens. She looked everywhere but could not find it. Lily was very sad. The next day, Lily found her mitten in a very unexpected place. Her dog, Max, was wearing the mitten on his head like a hat! Lily laughed and took the mitten off Max's head. Now, Lily and Max both had fun with the soft mitten.

Once upon a time there was a brave little mouse. He lived all alone in a special, cozy house. Every day he would scatter around, looking for food and a place to play. One day, he ran straight into a cat! The cat was big and scary, but the brave little mouse wasn't afraid. He quickly scurried away and scrambled up a tree. The cat couldn't catch him! The brave mouse knew that cats were not his friends so he scattered away, running as fast as he could. He ran and hid in a very small hole in the ground. He stayed there a long time and the cat couldn't find him. The brave little mouse had a safe home again and he never scattered so close to the cat again! He stayed safe with his friends and was very happy.

Jack was excited. He was at the beach with his family. His parents said they were sending him on an adventure. They handed him a bright red oyster. Inside was a special surprise. Jack took the oyster to the edge of the water. He opened it up and out popped a shiny pearl. He was so lucky to find it! His mom said it was a sign of good luck. Jack couldn't wait to show his friends. He ran back to the sand and searched for shells. Every day he would go to the beach and find something special in the sand. On his way home, he would always stop by the oyster market and send bottled pearls to all of his friends. He was happy to spread the luck he had found.
