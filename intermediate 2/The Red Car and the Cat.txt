Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Car and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. The car had four seats. One day, a cat and a dog wanted to go for a ride. The cat sat in the front seat and the dog sat in the back seat. The big red car started to go. It went slow at first, then it went fast. The speed made the cat and dog very happy. They laughed and clapped as the car went faster. After a while, the big red car stopped. The cat and the dog got out. They said "thank you" to the car for the fun ride. Then, they went home to tell their friends about their big adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was three years old and loved to pick apples. She went to the apple tree in her backyard every day with her mom. One day, mom said, "Let's go pick some apples, Lucy!" Lucy was so excited. She hopped around and said, "I want some sweeter apples!" Mom smiled and said, "Oh, okay. I think I know which apples are the sweetest!" They picked a lot of sweet apples. Suddenly, Lucy realized she had forgotten her basket. She said, "Mom, I miss my basket!" Mom replied, "It's okay. I will help you carry all these apples. Let's go home now." When they got home, Lucy was so happy. She couldn't wait to eat her sweet apples. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little owl. The owl loved to fly high in the sky. One day, while flying, the owl saw a hurt bird on the ground. "Hi bird, why are you hurt?" asked the owl. The bird said, "I fell from my nest and now I can't fly." The owl wanted to help the hurt bird. So, the owl flew up to the tree and found the bird's nest. The owl then carried the hurt bird back to its nest. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, owl, for helping me!" The owl smiled and flew away. From that day on, the owl and the bird became best friends. They played together and flew high in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She had a bike with pedals. Every day, the little girl would step on the pedals to make her bike go. One morning, the girl stepped on the pedals and started to ride her bike. She was so happy that she started to sing. Suddenly, she noticed something was wrong. Her pedals were broken. The little girl was so sad. She started to cry and her parents came to help her. They fixed the pedals but the girl was still sad. They all hugged the girl and told her stories about when her parents had a broken bike. The girl smiled and felt better. She stepped on the pedals and started to ride her bike again. She was so happy and rode her bike around the park all day. From that day on, the little girl was always careful with her pedals. She wanted to make sure they never got broken again!

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a kind girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends in the park. One sunny day, Lily and her friends were playing with a ball. They would take turns throwing and catching it. As they played, Lily's friend, Tim, threw the ball too far. It went past the trees and into the woods. Lily said, "I will go get it!" So, she turned and went into the woods to find the ball. As Lily walked, she met a talking squirrel. The squirrel said, "I saw your ball! It is by the big tree." Lily thanked the squirrel and went to the big tree. She found the ball and ran back to her friends. They were happy to see her and the ball. They continued to play and had a great day at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim was very frightened because he saw a big dog. The dog walked up to Tim and said, "Do not be scared. I am a nice dog. Will you accept my gift?" The dog had a big piece of ice in his mouth. Tim was not frightened anymore. He said, "Yes, I will accept your ice. Thank you, dog." The dog was happy and gave the ice to Tim. Tim held the ice and it was very cold. Then, something unexpected happened. The ice started to melt and turned into a beautiful butterfly. The butterfly flew away and Tim was very happy. He learned that sometimes, when we are frightened, we can still accept help from others. And sometimes, something unexpected and beautiful can come from it.
