Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Door and the Library

Once there was a girl who loved to dream. One day, she decided to visit the library. When she got there, it was so cold! She shivered and rubbed her arms to keep warm. But when she opened the library door, the girl felt so excited! She loved to read and she was so eager to explore the books. She walked around and looked at all the different stories and characters. The girl loved it so much that she never wanted to leave. She stayed in the library and read her favorite books, dreaming of all that she could do and all the places she could go. The girl stayed at the library until it was time to go home. She was so tired from all the dreaming, but she knew it was all worth it! She said goodbye to the library and walked out the door with a smile on her face.

Sam and Lily were very excited to see the fireworks. They put on their warm coats and hats and went outside with Mom and Dad. They saw many people and cars on the street. They heard loud noises and saw bright colors in the sky. "Wow, look at that one!" Sam said, pointing at a big red firework that exploded like a flower. "It's so pretty!" Lily said, clapping her hands. They saw a man selling popcorn and candy. Mom and Dad bought some for them. Sam and Lily tasted the popcorn and candy. They were sweet and salty and crunchy. "Yum, this is good!" Sam said, munching on a caramel corn. "Can I have some?" Lily asked, reaching for his bag. "Sure, here you go," Sam said, sharing with his sister. They saw a bald man walking by. He had no hair on his head. He smiled at them and said hello. "Hello, sir," Sam and Lily said politely. "Why are you bald?" Lily asked, curiously. The bald man laughed and said, "I lost my hair when I was young. But I don't mind. I like being bald. It makes me feel cool and free." "Does it hurt?" Sam asked, worried. "No, it doesn't hurt. It's just different. Everyone is different in some way. And that's okay," the bald man said, kindly. He gave them each a sticker of a firework and said goodbye. Sam and Lily thanked him and stuck the stickers on their coats. They watched more fireworks and enjoyed the night. They learned something new and made a new friend. They were happy.

One day, a helpful dog named Buddy went for a walk. Buddy liked to encourage his friends to be happy. He saw a big box on the tip of a hill. Buddy was curious about the box. Buddy went to the box and pushed it with his nose. The box started to move! It rolled down the hill very fast. Buddy was surprised and chased the box to see where it would go. At the bottom of the hill, the box opened. Out came a big, bouncy ball! Buddy's friends saw the ball and came to play. They all had fun and thanked Buddy for his helpful surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Bob. Bob liked to play with his cart. He would push it up and down the street. All the kids loved to watch Bob play with his cart. One day, a new kid named Tim came to play. Tim saw Bob's cart and wanted to play with it too. Bob did not like to share. He did not offer the cart to Tim. Tim was sad and went home. Later, a big storm came and blew the cart away. Bob was very sad. No one wanted to play with him anymore. The moral of the story is to always share and be kind to others.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big cat sitting on a wall. Max wanted to play with the cat, but the cat was too quick for him. The cat jumped off the wall and ran away. Max felt sorry that he could not catch the cat. Max saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad because she lost her toy. Max wanted to help her find the toy. He looked around and saw the toy under a bush. Max got the toy and gave it to Lily. Lily was happy and said thank you to Max. Then, Max saw the big cat again. This time, the cat did not run away. Max asked the cat if he wanted to play. The cat gave a little shrug and said, "Okay!" Max and the cat played together and had lots of fun. Max was not sorry anymore, and he made a new friend.
