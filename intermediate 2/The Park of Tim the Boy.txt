Category: Intermediate
Title: The Park of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he went to the park to meet his friends. At the park, Tim saw a tree with a pick on the ground. He picked up the pick and started to dig a hole. His friends came and helped him dig the hole. They all had fun playing together. After they dug the hole, they found a little box. They opened the box and found a treasure! Tim and his friends were so happy. They shared the treasure and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a modern toy box full of fun toys. One day, Lucy found a needle in her toy box. She did not know what it was, so she played with it. Lucy played with the needle and her toys. She made the needle disappear in her toy box. Then, she found it again. She was happy to play with the needle. But then, Lucy's mom came into the room. She saw the needle and took it away. Lucy was sad, but her mom said, "Needles can hurt you. Let's play with your other toys." Lucy and her mom played with her modern toy box and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly bunny named Bob. Bob liked to hop and play all day. He loved to eat carrots. One day, he went to find a big carrot to eat. Bob found a big carrot in the ground. He tried to pull it out, but it was very big. He pulled and pulled, but it would not come out. Bob sat down and started to imagine how good it would be to eat the big carrot. Just then, a jolly bird named Sam came to help Bob. Sam had a plan. They pushed the big carrot together. Suddenly, the big carrot popped out of the ground! But it was not a carrot, it was a big orange key! Bob and Sam were very surprised. They used the big orange key to open a hidden door in the ground. Inside, they found a big room full of carrots! They were so happy and ate carrots together all day. And they became best friends forever.

One day, a little yellow bird named Sunny woke up early. She was very thirsty. She wanted to find a cup of water to drink. She flew out of her nest to look for a cup. Sunny saw a big cup of water on the ground. But a big cat was sitting next to the cup. Sunny was scared of the cat. She said, "Please, can I have some water from the cup?" The cat said, "No, this is my cup of water." Sunny was sad, but she did not give up. She flew up high and found a big stick. She dropped the stick on the cat's head. The cat ran away, scared. Sunny flew down and drank the water from the cup. She was happy and not thirsty anymore.

One day, a restless dog named Bobo was playing in the park. He saw a small box under a tree. The box had a note on it. The note said, "This box includes a magic pill." Bobo was very curious. He opened the box and found a small, round pill inside. He thought, "Maybe this magic pill will make me feel better and not so restless." So he ate the pill. Later, Bobo's friend, a cat named Mimi, came to the park. Mimi saw Bobo and said, "Wow, you look so calm and happy now! What happened?" Bobo told her about the magic pill he found. They both smiled and played together in the park, with Bobo feeling much better.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a regular boy who liked to play with his toys, eat ice cream, and run in the park. But Tim had a worry. He wanted to win a race at school, but he was not fast like the other kids. One day, Tim's mom saw him looking sad. She asked, "Why the worry, Tim?" Tim told her about the race and how he wanted to win. His mom smiled and said, "You can do it, Tim! Just practice and believe in yourself." So, Tim practiced every day. He ran in the park, in the yard, and even in his house. Soon, the day of the race came. Tim was nervous, but he remembered his mom's words. He ran as fast as he could, and guess what? Tim won the race! He was so happy, and his worry was gone. From that day on, Tim knew that he could do anything if he tried his best.
