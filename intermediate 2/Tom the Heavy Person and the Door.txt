Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Heavy Person and the Door

One day, a heavy person named Tom wanted to enter a small house. The small house was blue and had a pretty garden with flowers. Tom liked the small house and wanted to make friends with the person inside. Tom knocked on the door. A nice person named Jane opened the door. She said, "Hello! My name is Jane. Who are you?" Tom smiled and said, "Hi Jane! I am Tom. I like your small house. Can I enter and be your friend?" Jane was happy to hear this. She said, "Yes, Tom! You can enter my small house. Let's play and be friends." Tom and Jane played with toys and had fun. They became good friends and had many happy days together in the small house.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there was a boy named Tim. One sunny day, Tim went for a walk to the west. He wanted to find something special. As he walked, he saw a big tree with a hole in it. Tim thought, "I wonder what's inside?" He did not realize that this tree was magic. He put his hand in the hole and felt something soft. He took it out and saw it was a hat. Tim put the hat on his head and started to walk back home. Suddenly, he felt very different. He could now talk to animals! Tim was so happy. He knew the hat was magic, and it made his ordinary day very special.

Once upon a time there were two friends who were walking on the beach. It was a bright, sunny day. "I am getting dizzy," said one of the friends. The other friend looked around. "Let's sit on the sand and take a rest," she said. The first friend lay back on the soft beach sand. Suddenly, her friend heard a noise and felt something tickle her toes. "What was that?" she asked. "It was only a crab trying to disturb us," said the second friend. The crab stopped and they laughed. Then they sat back and looked out at the ocean until they felt better. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big hand that he loved to use to spread things. One day, he went to the kitchen to make a sandwich. Tim took some bread and used his hand to spread a lot of butter on it. Then, he added some jam too. The butter and jam were very soft, but the bread was a bit harsh. Tim didn't mind, though. He liked the mix of soft and harsh. When Tim finished his sandwich, he shared it with his best friend, Sam. They both liked the sandwich a lot. They laughed and clapped their hands. From that day on, they always used their hands to spread things on their sandwiches together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a wild lion named Leo. Leo lived in a big forest with many trees. One day, he found a long line of ants on the ground. He was very curious and decided to follow the line of ants. As he followed the line, he met a little bird named Bella. Bella was watching the ants too. She said, "Hi Leo! Do you want to improve the ants' line?" Leo was excited and said, "Yes, Bella! How can we improve it?" Bella and Leo worked together. They moved sticks and leaves out of the ants' way. They made the line straighter and smoother. The ants were happy and thanked Bella and Leo. From that day on, Bella and Leo became good friends. They always helped each other and their animal friends in the forest. The wild forest became a better place because of their teamwork.

One day, a girl named Sue was playing in her room. She saw her boring glove on the floor. Sue wanted to have fun, so she put on the glove and went outside to play. Sue saw her friend Tom. Tom said, "Hi Sue! I see your glove. Let's decide what to play." Sue thought for a moment and said, "Let's play catch!" Tom agreed and they started to throw the ball back and forth. As they played, something unexpected happened. When Sue caught the ball with her boring glove, it changed colors! It became a bright, pretty glove. Sue and Tom were so surprised. They laughed and played with the magic glove all day long.
