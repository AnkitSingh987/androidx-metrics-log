Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Gold

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy truck. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. While playing, Tim found something shiny in the sand. It was gold! He was very happy. But his friends saw the gold and felt jealous. They wanted the gold too. Tim's friends tried to take the gold from him. They began to fight. In the fight, Tim's toy truck got broken. No one got the gold and everyone was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a calm bird named Bobby. Bobby had many pretty feathers. He liked to fly in the sky and play with his friends. One day, Bobby had a job to do. He had to deliver a letter to his friend, Timmy the Turtle. Bobby put the letter in his beak and flew to Timmy's house. When Bobby gave Timmy the letter, Timmy was very happy. Timmy gave Bobby a big hug and a thank you. Bobby felt good for helping his friend. Then, they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bird named Blue. Blue loved to fly high in the sky. One day, Blue found a box of cereal on the ground. Blue picked up the cereal and flew to his friend, Cat. Cat was sitting under a tree. Blue said, "Look, I found cereal! Let's eat it together." Cat was happy and said, "Yes, let's eat!" They sat under the tree and shared the cereal. Blue and Cat were very happy. They played together all day, and when the sun went down, Blue flew back to his nest, and Cat went home. They knew they would always be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a bottle that she thought was boring. It was just a plain, clear bottle with a blue cap. One sunny day, Mia went to play outside with her bottle. As she played, she met a nice boy named Tim. Tim had a fun, colorful bottle with lots of stickers on it. Mia felt sad and said, "I wish my bottle was fun like yours." Tim smiled and said, "Don't worry, Mia. We can make your bottle fun too!" They found some stickers and put them all over Mia's bottle. Now her bottle was not boring anymore. It was fun and pretty! But then, Mia tried to drink from her bottle. She couldn't because there were stickers on the top. Mia felt regret. She said, "I wish I didn't put stickers on my bottle." Tim helped her take the stickers off the top so she could drink again. From that day on, Mia and Tim played together all the time. They learned that sometimes, a boring bottle can still be a good bottle. And they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a strong bunny named Bob. Bob loved to spring high into the sky. He had so much fun playing with his friends in the soft grass. One day, Bob found a little door with a knob. He was very curious. He turned the knob and the door opened. Inside, there was a room full of toys and yummy treats. Bob had a great time playing with the toys and eating the treats. He felt so happy and strong. He knew he could spring even higher now. And so, Bob went back outside, and he sprang higher than ever before.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion who liked to roar very loud. One day, the lion was walking in the forest when he saw a tire hanging from a tree. The lion was curious and wanted to play with the tire. The lion jumped up and grabbed the tire with his teeth. He swung back and forth, having lots of fun. But soon, the lion started to feel upset. His tummy hurt because he was swinging too much. Just then, a little mouse came out of the bushes. The mouse saw the upset lion and wanted to help. The mouse climbed up the tree and cut the tire free. The lion fell softly on the ground, and his tummy felt better. The lion was so happy that he roared very loud. The mouse and the lion became good friends, and they played together in the forest every day.
