Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. The park was big and fun. But today, it was very quiet. There was a lot of silence. Tim felt it was a bit boring. Tim saw a big ball on the ground. He had an idea. "Spot, let's play!" Tim said. Spot barked happily. Tim picked up the ball and slapped it with his hand. The ball flew through the air. Spot ran and caught the ball in his mouth. They played and played. The park was not boring anymore. Tim and Spot had a great time, and the silence was gone. They went home tired and happy.

Once upon a time, on a farm, there was a tall scarecrow. He was very good at his job. He would scare all the birds away from the farm. The farmer was very happy with the tall scarecrow. One day, a little bird came to the farm. The tall scarecrow tried to scare the bird away, but the bird was not scared. The bird sat on the scarecrow's arm and sang a pretty song. The tall scarecrow felt sad because he could not scare the bird away. The farmer saw the bird and got mad. He thought the tall scarecrow was not doing his job. The farmer took the tall scarecrow away from the farm. The birds came back and ate all the seeds. The farm did not grow any food and the farmer was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a big building with an elevator. A little girl named Lily and her mom went to the building. They needed to go up to the top floor. Lily was excited to use the elevator. Lily's mom said, "When we finish our work, we will have a successful day." They got in the elevator and pushed the button for the top floor. The elevator went up and up. Lily was happy and couldn't wait to finish their work. When they reached the top floor, Lily and her mom did their work. They finished it quickly. Then, they went back to the elevator to go down. Lily's mom said, "We did it! We are successful!" Lily smiled and they went home, happy about their day.

Once upon a time, there was a famous hippo named Bob. Bob was big and fat. He lived in a big lake. Bob had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, a little bird came to Bob and said, "Bob, I want to play with you. But if you spoil our game, I will go away." Bob did not want his friend to go away, so he said, "I promise I will not spoil our game." They played and played, but then Bob did a big splash in the water. The little bird got wet and was very sad. "You spoil our game, Bob!" the little bird cried. Bob was sorry, but the little bird flew away. Bob was sad and alone. He missed his friend. Because Bob did not keep his promise, he had a bad ending. From that day on, Bob learned to be more careful when playing with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play in her backyard. One sunny day, while she was playing, something appeared. It was a veil, but it looked broken. Lily picked up the veil and said, "Oh no! This veil is broken. I need to fix it." She went to her mom and showed her the veil. Her mom said, "Let's fix it together, Lily." They worked together to fix the veil. They sewed it and made it look new again. Lily went back outside with the fixed veil. She said, "Now you are not broken anymore, veil!" Lily played with the veil all day. She wore it and danced around the yard. She was so happy that she could fix the veil. Now, she had a new friend to play with. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a huge plastic ball. It was so big that he could not hold it with his hands. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him play with the ball. "Sam, can you help me play with this huge ball?" Tim said. Sam agreed and they started to play with the ball. They were having so much fun that they did not see the time pass. Suddenly, the huge ball started to vanish. Tim and Sam were very sad. They tried to find the ball, but it was gone. They could not play with the huge ball anymore. The end.
