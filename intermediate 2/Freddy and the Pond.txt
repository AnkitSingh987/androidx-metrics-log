Category: Intermediate
Title: Freddy and the Pond

Once upon a time, a funny frog named Freddy lived near a pond. He liked to jump and play all day. One day, he found a long pole near the water. He thought it would be fun to play with the pole. Freddy went to his friend, a duck named Ducky. He asked, "Ducky, can you permit me to play with this pole?" Ducky said, "Yes, Freddy, I permit you to play with the pole." Freddy was very happy. He started to play with the pole near the pond. Suddenly, the pole moved! It was not a pole, but a long snake! Freddy and Ducky were surprised, but the snake was nice. They all played together and had a great day.

One day, a hippo and a mouse were friends. They liked to walk by the river and look at the fish. The hippo was big and strong, and the mouse was small and fast. They helped each other when they were in trouble. One day, they saw a big tree with red fruits. The mouse said, "Look, hippo, those fruits look yummy. Let's try some." The hippo said, "OK, mouse, but be careful. They might be bitter." The mouse said, "Don't worry, hippo, I have a good nose. I can smell if they are sweet or bitter." The mouse climbed the tree and picked a fruit. He bit into it and made a face. It was very bitter. He spat it out and said, "Yuck, hippo, this fruit is bitter. Don't eat it." The hippo said, "Thank you, mouse, for warning me. You are a good friend." The mouse said, "You are welcome, hippo. You are a good friend too." They walked away from the tree and found a patch of grass. They ate the grass and drank some water. They were happy and full. They said, "We don't need bitter fruits. We have each other." They hugged and smiled. They were the best friends in the world.

One day, a group of ants went on a march. They wanted to find a big score of food. They walked and walked, looking for something yummy to take back to their home. As they marched, they found a comfortable spot with lots of food. The ants were so happy! They started to pick up the food and carry it back home. But then, something unexpected happened. A big gust of wind came and blew all the food away! The ants were sad, but they did not give up. They marched again, and this time, they found an even bigger score of food. They took it back to their home and shared it with their friends. The ants were happy and knew that even when things go wrong, they can still find a way to make it right.

Once there was an ostrich. He was very proud. One day he went for a walk and saw something sparkly in the grass! He was so excited and went over to look at it. But then he saw something he wasn't expecting. There was an evil fox trying to steal the sparkly thing. The ostrich didn't like it. He got angry and decided he had to do something. He ran over to the fox and shouted at him. This made the fox so embarrassed that he ran away! The ostriches was so happy to have saved the sparkly thing. He was very proud of himself. The bird picked up the sparkly thing, and he took it home with him. From then on he looked after it and kept it safe. He never let any fox try to steal it again!

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bird. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. One day, the bird saw the moon. The moon was big and bright. The bird wanted to fly to the moon. The bird tried to fly to the moon every night. It flew higher and higher. But the moon was very far away. The bird was very tired. It did not want to give up. It wanted to reach the moon. One night, the bird flew too high. It could not fly any more. The bird fell down from the sky. The bird was very sad. The moral of the story is to not try to do things that are too hard for you.
