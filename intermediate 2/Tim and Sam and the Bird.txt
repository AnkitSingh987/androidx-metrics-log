Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sam and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a calm little town, there lived a cat named Tim and a dog named Sam. They were good friends and liked to play together. They loved their life in the town. One day, while they were playing, a big bird came and started to bother them. The bird was loud and not nice. Tim said to Sam, "Let's ignore the bird and maybe it will go away." So they tried to ignore the bird and kept playing. But the bird did not go away. Tim and Sam didn't know what to do. Then, a kind lady saw them and shooed the bird away. Tim and Sam thanked the lady and went back to their calm life, playing together and ignoring any mean birds that came their way.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. The farm was very dry. On the farm, there was a big tree. The tree liked to think a lot. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird was sad. The bird said, "I am so thirsty. I cannot find any water to drink." The tree thought and thought. Then, the tree had an idea. The tree said, "Dig a hole under me. You will find water there." The bird dug a hole under the tree. Soon, the bird found water. The bird was happy. The bird said, "Thank you, tree. Now I am not thirsty." Many other animals came to the tree. They were all thirsty too. The tree told them to dig holes. They all found water. The animals were happy. They said, "Thank you, tree. You are so smart." The moral of the story is: when you think and help others, everyone can be happy.

Once upon a time, a pale boy named Tim had a small boat. He loved to sail his boat on the lake near his house. One hot day, Tim went to the lake with his boat. He was very excited to sail. As Tim began to sail, he saw a big fish in the water. The fish was very friendly and wanted to play. Tim and the fish played in the water all day. They had lots of fun together. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish got too close to Tim's boat, and it tipped over. Tim fell into the water and his boat sank. The fish tried to help, but it was too late. Tim was sad because his boat was gone, and he could not sail anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to think about all the things she could do. One day, she found a measure in her house. She thought it would be fun to play with it. Sue went outside and saw her friend, Tom. Tom was a naughty boy who liked to play tricks. Tom saw the measure and thought it would be fun to play a trick on Sue. He told her that he could jump higher than the measure could show. Sue didn't believe Tom, so they had a contest. Tom jumped and jumped, but he couldn't jump higher than the measure. Sue laughed and said, "I knew you couldn't do it!" Tom felt silly, but he knew that he had been naughty for trying to trick Sue. From that day on, Tom promised to be a good friend and not play tricks on Sue. They had lots of fun playing with the measure and thinking of new games to play together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to play outside in nature. She liked to look at trees, flowers, and animals. One day, Sally saw a little bird trying to build a nest. Sally asked the bird, "Why are you building a nest?" The bird replied, "I am building a nest to keep my eggs safe and warm." Sally thought the bird was very intelligent. She wanted to help the bird build the nest. So, Sally picked up some sticks and gave them to the bird. The bird was happy and thanked Sally for her help. Together, Sally and the bird built a strong nest. The bird put her eggs in the nest and kept them warm. Soon, the eggs hatched, and there were baby birds. Sally was happy that she could help her new friend, and they played together in nature every day.
