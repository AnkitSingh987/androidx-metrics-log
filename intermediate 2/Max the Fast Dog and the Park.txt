Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Fast Dog and the Park

One day, a fast dog named Max went to the park. He wanted to race with his friend, a slow cat named Sam. Max said, "Sam, let's race to the big tree!" Sam was not sure, but he said, "Okay, Max. I will try." They started the race. Max ran very fast, and Sam tried to run fast too. But Sam was not as fast as Max. Max got to the big tree first. Sam felt sad because he lost the race. Max saw that Sam was sad and said, "Sam, don't be sad. We can try a different game." Max and Sam played a new game together. They played hide and seek. Sam was very good at hiding, and Max had fun trying to find him. They had a great time at the park. In the end, they both were happy because they played together.

Tim and Jen are two rabbits who live in a big field. They like to hop and play with their friends every day. They also like to eat the green grass and the sweet carrots that grow in the field. One day, they see a big fence around the field. They wonder what it is for. They hop closer to the fence and see a sign that says "Stay out! This is a farm now!" They are scared and sad. They do not want to leave their home. They hear a voice behind them. It is Sam, a friendly dog who lives on the farm. He says, "Hello, rabbits! Do not be afraid. I am here to help you. The farmer is not mean. He just wants to grow some crops in the field. He will not hurt you. You can still hop and play in the field, but you have to stay away from the fence and the crops. Do you understand?" Tim and Jen nod their heads. They are happy to hear that they can still live in the field. They thank Sam for being friendly and helpful. They hop back to their friends and tell them the good news. They also tell them to listen to Sam and follow the rules. They learn that it is important to respect other people's property and to be friendly to new neighbors. They are glad that they can still enjoy their home and their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to design pretty pictures with her crayons. One day, Lily drew a big, colorful butterfly. When her mommy saw the butterfly, she said, "Wow, Lily! I love your butterfly design!" Lily felt very happy and proud. She wanted to show her butterfly to her friend, Tim. Lily went to Tim's house and said, "Look, Tim! I made a butterfly!" Tim looked at the butterfly and laughed. "That's not a butterfly, Lily! It looks funny!" Lily felt embarrassed and her voice became very quiet. "I thought it was pretty," she said. Tim saw that Lily was sad and felt bad for laughing. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Let's design a new butterfly together!" So, Lily and Tim drew a new butterfly, and it was the most beautiful butterfly ever. They both felt very happy, and Lily's voice was loud and proud again.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little fish named Fin. Fin wanted to improve his swimming, but he had a small hook stuck in his fin. It made swimming very hard for him. One day, a wise old fish told him about a magic fish who could help him. Fin swam and swam to find the magic fish. He knew the magic fish could help him with his hook. On the way, he found a big rock. He thought, "Maybe this rock can help me later." So, he pushed the rock with him as he swam. Finally, Fin found the magic fish. The magic fish saw the hook and said, "I can help you, but you must give me something in return." Fin remembered the big rock he found. He showed it to the magic fish. The magic fish loved the rock and agreed to help. With a wave of his fin, the hook was gone, and Fin could swim better. He was so happy and thanked the magic fish. From that day on, Fin improved his swimming and was never poor again.
