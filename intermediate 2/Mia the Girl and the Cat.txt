Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a soft, velvet toy cat. She loved her cat very much. They played together every day. One gloomy day, the sky was full of dark clouds. Mia and her velvet cat were inside the house. Mia said, "Come, cat. Let's play hide and seek." The cat nodded, and they started to play. Mia closed her eyes and counted. The velvet cat ran to hide. When Mia opened her eyes, she looked for her cat. She found the cat under the bed. They laughed and played until the gloomy sky turned bright again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a colorful dish. This dish was very special. It had many bright colors like red, blue, and yellow. The dish was always happy and liked to make friends. One sunny day, the dish met a chair. The chair was very nice and said, "I can seat you on me." The dish was happy and climbed onto the chair. They became good friends and played all day long. Every day, the colorful dish and the chair would play together. They would seat and talk about their day. They were very happy to be friends and they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben like to pretend. They have a big box of toys and clothes. They can be anything they want. Today, they pretend to be pirates. They wear hats and scarves and belts. They have swords and maps and coins. "Arrr, matey, where is the treasure?" Ben asks Lily. He holds his sword and his map. "Follow me, captain. I know where it is." Lily says. She leads him to the sofa. She lifts a pillow and shows him a pile of coins. "Look, we found it. It is very shiny and value." "Wow, you are a good pirate, Lily. This is a lot of value. We are rich." Ben says. He puts some coins in his pocket and his belt. "Shh, be quiet. There are other pirates. They want our value. We have to hide it." Lily says. She puts the pillow back and grabs her sword. "Who are they? Where are they?" Ben asks. He looks around. "They are very loud and mean. They are in the kitchen. They are Mom and Dad." Lily says. She giggles. "Mom and Dad are pirates? No, they are not. They are nice and love us." Ben says. He is confused. "Shh, they are coming. We have to pretend. We have to act like we are not pirates. We have to act like we are Lily and Ben." Lily says. She takes off her hat and scarf and belt. She puts them in the box. "Okay, okay. I will pretend too." Ben says. He does the same. He puts his sword and map and coins in the box. Mom and Dad come in. They smile and hug Lily and Ben. "Hello, my sweeties. What are you doing?" Mom asks. "We are playing. We are pretending." Lily says. "Pretending what?" Dad asks. "Pretending to be pirates. We found a lot of value. But we hid it. You can't have it." Ben says. Mom and Dad laugh. They tickle Lily and Ben. "You are very funny and clever, pirates. But we don't want your value. We have something more value. We have you." Mom says. "We love you, Lily and Ben. You are our treasure." Dad says. Lily and Ben smile. They hug Mom and Dad. "We love you too, Mom and Dad. You are our value." Lily and Ben say.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a wide closet in her room. Lily loved her closet, but she hated cleaning it. Inside the closet, there were many toys, clothes, and shoes. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, it's time to clean your closet." Lily did not want to clean it. She said, "Mom, I hate cleaning my closet." Her mom smiled and said, "If you clean your closet, I'll give you a big surprise." Lily thought about the surprise and decided to clean her closet. She put her toys, clothes, and shoes in their places. When she finished, her mom came in with a big, shiny, red ball. Lily was so happy! She thanked her mom and played with her new ball all day. From that day on, Lily did not hate cleaning her closet as much as before.
