Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Boy and the Fish

Once there was a little boy named Sam. He was only three years old, but he was very brave. He always wanted to try new things. One day, Sam's mom said that he should go down to the creek. She said he should take a fishing rod with him. Sam was a bit scared, but he was brave and he wanted to try it. When Sam got to the creek, he was so proud of himself. He stared at the water, and then he finally dared to try it. He put his fishing rod in the creek and waited. Eventually, something big and surprising happened. It was a big fish! Sam was so proud of himself for being brave enough to try fishing. He knew that if he was brave, he could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a restless boy who always liked to run and play. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and dad. At the park, Tim saw a big ball. He wanted to pass the ball with his new friend, Sam. They passed the ball back and forth, laughing and having fun. They played until they were tired. After playing, Tim and Sam sat down to rest. Tim's mom told them a story about always telling the truth. Tim and Sam listened carefully and promised to always tell the truth. They learned that telling the truth is important. From that day on, Tim and Sam were best friends who always told the truth.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play with his toy gear. One day, Tom found a magic wand in his room. He was very excited and wanted to play with it. Tom waved the wand around and said, "Shrink!" Suddenly, his toy gear started to get smaller and smaller. Tom was scared, but he also thought it was fun to see his gear shrink. He wanted to make it big again, so he waved the wand and said, "Grow!" The gear grew back to its normal size. Tom played with the magic wand all day, making his gear shrink and grow. He was no longer scared, and he had lots of fun with his new magic toy. At the end of the day, Tom put the wand away and went to bed, excited to play with it again the next day.

Lily and Tom were playing hide and seek in the park. Lily was very good at hiding. She found a big bush near the lake and hid behind it. Tom looked for her everywhere, but he could not find her. He called her name, but she did not answer. He felt sad and tired. Lily saw Tom walking near the lake. She felt sorry for him. She wanted to end the game and have fun with him. She had an idea. She picked up a small stone and threw it into the lake. It made a big splash. Tom heard the splash and turned around. He saw Lily waving at him from behind the bush. He smiled and ran to her. "Lily, you are so clever!" he said. "You hid very well. But how did you make the splash?" Lily giggled and showed him the stone. "I threw it into the lake. It was easy." Tom hugged her and said, "You are very clever, but you are also very kind. You surrendered to make me happy. Thank you, Lily. You are my best friend." They held hands and walked to the swings. They played and laughed together until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl named Amy. She was very good at helping others. One day, Amy saw a small boy named Tim who was sad. Tim had lost his toy and did not know where to find it. Amy wanted to help Tim feel better. Amy asked Tim, "Did you mention where you last saw your toy?" Tim said, "Yes, I was playing near the big tree." Amy and Tim went to the big tree to look for the toy. While they were looking, they found a nice lady who had found the toy. She gave it back to Tim. Tim was so happy to have his toy back. He gave Amy a big hug to say thank you. Amy felt good for helping her new friend. The moral of the story is to always help others when you can. It can make both you and the person you help feel better.
