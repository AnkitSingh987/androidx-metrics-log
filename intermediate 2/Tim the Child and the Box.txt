Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Child and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a shy child named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy cars and trucks. One day, Tim found a big red box. He was very curious about what was inside the box. Tim tried to open the box, but it was locked. He looked around for a key, but he could not find one. Tim felt sad. He wanted to see what was inside the box. He asked his mom for help, and she said, "Let's look for the key together." As they looked for the key, Tim found a small, shiny key under his bed. He was so excited! Tim and his mom went to the big red box and used the key to unlock it. When they opened the box, they found another small box inside. Tim was surprised! Tim opened the small box and found a note that said, "Surprise! This is a special gift for you, Tim." Inside the box was a brand new toy car! Tim was so happy and couldn't believe the big red box had a gift just for him. From that day on, Tim was not shy anymore and loved to share his new toy car with his friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He brought his yellow ball and a big lemon with him. His mom told him, "Tim, don't leave the lemon on the grass. It will get dirty." But Tim was a little foolish and did not listen to his mom. While Tim was playing with his ball, a funny dog came to the park. The dog saw the lemon on the grass and thought it was a ball. The dog picked up the lemon in its mouth and ran to Tim. "Hey, that's my lemon!" Tim shouted at the dog. The dog dropped the lemon and looked sad. Tim felt bad for the dog and said, "It's okay, dog. You can have my lemon." The dog wagged its tail and barked happily. Tim's mom saw what happened and smiled. She was proud of Tim for sharing his lemon with the dog. And from that day on, Tim and the dog became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small, clean town. In the town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to watch the sun rise in the sky. Every day, she would wake up early and go outside to see the sun come up. One morning, she saw a big, round thing in the sky. It was not the sun. She ran to her mom and said, "Mom, look! There is something big in the sky!" Her mom looked up and saw it too. They were both very surprised. They went to tell the other people in the town. The people were scared. They did not know what the big thing was. They all hid in their homes. The big thing came closer and closer. Then, it landed in the town. It was a giant, round rock. The rock crushed many of the clean houses. The people were sad, and the town was not clean anymore.

Once there was a dad who wanted to take his daughter for a walk. He took her to the park and he lead the way. The daughter had lots of fun and the dad was happy. Suddenly, a big tornado started. Everyone was scared and it was very difficult to see. The dad held his daughter's hand to lead her and they hurried away. But the tornado was very strong and it was getting closer. The dad tried very hard to get his daughter away from the tornado. But the tornado was just too strong and it lead them to a bad end. In the end, the dad and the daughter got very hurt and they were sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. Lucy loved to play with Max in the backyard. They would run, jump, and move all around. They had so much fun together. One day, Lucy's mom had to go to the hospital. Lucy was sad and worried. She wanted her mom to be perfect and happy. Dad told her that the doctors would help mom feel better. Lucy decided to make a special card for her mom. Lucy and Max went outside to find the perfect things to put on the card. They found pretty leaves, flowers, and even a small feather. Lucy and Max moved all around the yard to find the best things. When the card was done, they took it to the hospital. Mom loved the card, and it made her feel better. Soon, mom was perfect and happy again, and they all went home to play in the backyard together.
