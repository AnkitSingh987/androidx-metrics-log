Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bull and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big bull. The bull was wild and liked to run. One day, the bull saw a small cat. The cat was lost and sad. "Hi cat," said the bull. "Why are you sad?" The cat replied, "I can't find my home. Can you help me?" The bull wanted to help the cat, so they went to look for the cat's home together. As they walked, they saw a big tree. The tree had a door in it. The bull and the cat opened the door, and they found a big party inside! All the animals were there. They were all happy to see the cat and the bull. The cat's home was the tree, and the party was for the cat. The bull and the cat had fun at the party, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there lived a little bird. The bird had a dream to raise her nest high up in the trees. She wanted to be safe from the big animals that walked on the ground. But the little bird was sad because she did not know how to raise her nest up high. One day, as the little bird was looking for help, she met a kind monkey. The monkey wanted to help the little bird raise her nest. So, they worked together to find a high tree to put the nest in. They were very happy when they found the perfect tree. But, when they tried to raise the nest, they found out that the tree was not a tree at all! It was a tall, green animal with a long neck. They had found a big giraffe! The giraffe was friendly and wanted to help too. So, the giraffe lifted the nest high up on its neck and carried it to the top of a real tree. The little bird was so happy that her nest was now high and safe. The bird, the monkey, and the giraffe became the best of friends and lived happily ever after in the big jungle.

Mia was very careful. She had a special mop. One day, Mia was playing with the mop when she accidentally broke it. She was so sad. She started to cry. Mia's mom saw what happened and ran to give her a big hug. She told Mia it was ok, and that the mop could be fixed. Mia was very relieved, and her mom hugged her even tighter. Mom and Mia went to the store to buy a new mop. They were both so careful when choosing the new mop. They wanted to make sure it was just right. Finally Mia found the perfect mop. Mom bought it for her, and gave it a big hug! Mia was so happy. From then on, Mia was very careful with her mop. She made sure it did not break again.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to her mom's room. She saw pretty jewelry on the table. Sally asked her mom, "Can I wear your jewelry, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but only for a little bit." Sally put on the shiny jewelry and started to dance. She spun around and around until she felt dizzy. She laughed and said, "Look at me, Mom! I'm a dizzy dancer!" Just then, their cat jumped up and tried to grab the jewelry. The cat knocked the jewelry off Sally, and it broke into pieces. Sally felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Mom." Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay. We can fix it together." And they did, making the jewelry look like new again.

Once upon a time, there was a little curtain. It was red and very pretty. The curtain loved to be open during the day and closed at night. One day, the curtain was upset because it could not be open. It was stuck! In the house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue saw the upset curtain and wanted to help. She tried to pull the curtain open, but it was still stuck. Sue was sad, but she did not give up. She thought and thought about how to fix the curtain. Then, Sue had an idea! She asked her mom for help. Together, they found a little toy car that was stuck in the curtain. The toy car was the reason the curtain could not be open. Sue and her mom took the toy car out, and the curtain was happy again. The curtain could be open during the day and closed at night, just like it loved to be.
