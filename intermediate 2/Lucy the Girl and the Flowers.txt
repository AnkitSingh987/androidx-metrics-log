Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Flowers

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to choose her clothes. She looked outside to see the weather. It was sunny and warm. Lucy picked a comfortable dress to wear. It was red with little flowers on it. She put on her dress and went outside to play. Lucy played in the sun all day. She was happy because she chose the perfect clothes for the weather. At the end of the day, she went inside, tired but smiling.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a band in the park. He put the band on his wrist and felt very happy. Tim went to his friend, Sue, to show her the band. Sue looked at the band and said, "I like it too! Can I wear it?" Tim felt frustrated because he wanted to keep the band for himself. But he promised Sue, "You can wear it for one day, and then give it back to me." Sue was happy and they played together all day. The next day, Sue gave the band back to Tim. They both learned that sharing can make them happy and keep their friendship strong. The moral of the story is that when we share, everyone is happy.

Once upon a time there was a boy. His name was Tom. Tom loved to play. Every day, Tom would go outside and play with his friends. Tom was very playful. He would run around, jump, and do all kinds of fun activities. He also liked doing exercises. Tom would do stretches and run around in circles. One day, all of Tom's friends were playing a game. They were having a lot of fun. Tom wanted to join them, but he didn't know how. He stood there and watched them play. Suddenly, Tom had an idea. He ran over to his friends and showed them all the exercises he had been doing. His friends were impressed! They decided to join him and all of them started doing exercises together. They were so happy and having a great time! That day, Tom and his friends learned that exercise can be fun, and playful too!

Once there was a little black kitty called Shadow. Shadow was very anxious and liked to run around the house all day. One day Shadow heard two voices. He stopped dead in his tracks and listened closely. He heard a little boy and his mom. The little boy said: "Mom, can I have a kitty please?" His mom replied: "No, sweetheart. You know we can't have a kitty." That made Shadow very anxious. He was hoping to be the kitty for the little boy. He moved closer to hear what the little boy would say. The little boy said: "Please, mom? I would take care of it and love it." His mom said: "No, sweetheart. We can’t have a kitty, but how about I get you a stuffed kitty instead?" Shadow was relieved! He was so happy the little boy would get a stuffed kitty instead. Shadow purred and went back to running around the house. He was much less anxious now.

One day, a little bird felt helpless. He wanted to fly to the moon. He asked his friend, the big bird, "Can you help me fly to the moon?" The big bird said, "Yes, I will help you. Let's settle on my back and I will take you there." So, the little bird settled on the big bird's back. They flew up and up, but the moon was too far away. The little bird said, "Thank you for trying to help me." The big bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, my friend." They flew back home and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a star named Twinkle. Twinkle loved to bake yummy treats for all her friends. One day, she baked a big cake for her best friend, Moon. Moon said, "Wow, Twinkle! This cake is so good! You are the best baker I know!" Twinkle was happy that Moon liked her cake. But there was a jealous cloud named Puffy. Puffy wanted to bake a cake too. So, Puffy asked Twinkle, "Can you teach me how to bake a cake?" Twinkle said, "Of course, Puffy! Let's bake a cake together!" So, Twinkle and Puffy baked a cake. It was fun and the cake was yummy. Puffy was not jealous anymore. They all ate the cake and were happy friends.
