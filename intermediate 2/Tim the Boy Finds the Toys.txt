Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his football. He would play with it all day long. He would kick it high in the air and catch it. Tim was very happy. One day, Tim's mom said they needed to sell some of their toys. Tim did not want to sell his football, but he knew he had to help his mom. So, Tim put his football in a box with other toys to sell. At the sale, a little girl saw the football. She wanted to play with it too. She gave Tim some money and took the football home. Tim was sad, but he knew it was the right thing to do. Later, Tim's mom gave him a hug and said she was proud of him. Tim felt better and knew he could always make new friends to play with.

Once upon a time there was an otter. Every day he would go for a swim and then lay on the rocks in the sun. He was a regular visitor to the river. One day, as the otter lay on the rocks, a big bird swooped down and tried to grab him! The otter quickly jumped off the rock and swam away. But the bird came back again and again. Each time it tried to strike the otter but the otter was too quick and swam away. The otter stayed away for a few days, but then one day he decided it was time to return to the river. This time he was brave and didn't swim away when the bird flew down. Instead he waved his tail and the bird flew away. The otter was back to being a regular visitor to the river and he was happy. He learnt that he can be brave and that made him feel good.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the beach with her mom. The sand was smooth under her feet. She liked to play in the water and wave at the boats. Lily saw a man with a big bag. It was the mail man. He was giving mail to the people at the beach. Lily ran to her mom and said, "Mom, can we get mail too?" Her mom smiled and said, "Sure, let's ask the mail man." They walked to the mail man and Lily waved at him. He waved back and gave them a letter. Lily was happy to get mail at the beach. They opened the letter and it was a picture of a big, yellow sun. Lily and her mom laughed and put the picture on their beach towel. They played in the water and waved at the boats all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very quick dog, but he was deaf. He could not hear anything. Spot loved to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Spot was playing with his friends when a big ball appeared. The ball was very big and very red. All the little animals wanted to play with the big red ball. They were so happy and they all took turns playing with it. But because Spot was deaf, he did not hear his friends calling him to play with the big red ball. Spot felt sad because he thought his friends did not want to play with him. Then, one of his friends, a kind bunny, saw Spot looking sad. The kind bunny went over to Spot and tapped him on the shoulder. When Spot looked up, he saw the big red ball and his friends smiling at him. They all played with the big red ball together, and Spot was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick bear named Ben. Ben liked to organize things in his cave. One day, Ben found a pan in the woods. He thought, "I can use this pan to cook food!" Ben took the pan back to his cave. He organized his cave and made a special place for the pan. Later, his friend, a small rabbit named Remy, came to visit. Remy saw the pan and said, "Wow, what a nice pan! Can we cook something together?" Ben and Remy decided to cook a big meal. They went to the woods and found yummy things to cook. They cooked and cooked, and the smell was so good. Other animals came to see what they were cooking. At the end of the day, Ben, Remy, and their new friends enjoyed a big meal together. They were all very happy, and the pan helped them make new friends. From that day on, Ben and Remy always cooked together with the pan they found in the woods.
