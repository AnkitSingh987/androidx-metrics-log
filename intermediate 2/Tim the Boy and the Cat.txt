Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He loved to hear stories from his mom. One day, his mom told him a story about a big cat and a small mouse. In the story, the big cat would not let the small mouse play with him. The small mouse felt sad. One day, the big cat got stuck in a net. The small mouse saw the big cat and wanted to help. The small mouse used his sharp teeth to cut the net and set the big cat free. The big cat said sorry to the small mouse and they became friends. Tim learned that being nice and helping others can make good friends. He knew that he should let others play with him and be a good friend too.

Lily likes to play with tubes. She has many tubes of different colors and sizes. She puts them on her ears, her nose, her mouth, and her fingers. She likes to hear the sounds they make. One day, Lily finds a new tube in her toy box. It is shiny and silver, and has a button on it. She is curious and puts it on her ear. She presses the button and hears a loud beep. She is surprised and scared. She drops the tube and runs to her mom. Mom sees Lily crying and hugs her. She asks her what happened. Lily points to the tube and says, "Bad tube, bad tube, make noise, hurt ear." Mom picks up the tube and looks at it. She smiles and says, "This is not a bad tube, Lily. This is a normal tube. It is a whistle. It makes noise when you blow it, not when you press it. See?" She blows the tube and makes a beep. Lily stops crying and looks at the tube. She is curious again. She takes the tube from mom and blows it. She hears a beep and laughs. She likes the noise. She says, "Good tube, good tube, make noise, fun ear." Mom laughs too and says, "Yes, good tube, Lily. You can play with it, but be careful not to blow it too hard or too close to your ear. It can be loud and hurt your ear. OK?" Lily nods and says, "OK, mom. Thank you, mom." She hugs mom and goes back to play with her tubes. She is happy.

Lily liked to play with rocks. She had many rocks in her room. Some were big, some were small, some were shiny, some were dull. She liked to look at them and touch them and pretend they were her friends. One day, she went to the store with her mom. She saw a big basket of rocks near the door. They were different from her rocks. They had many colors and shapes and sparkles. They were minerals, the sign said. Lily wanted to have some minerals. She asked her mom if she could buy one. Her mom looked at the sign. It said the minerals were very expensive. She told Lily they could not buy any minerals today. They had to buy food and clothes and other things they needed. Maybe another day, she said. Lily was sad. She really liked the minerals. She thought they were beautiful and special. She looked at them for a long time. She wished she could have one. Then she saw a small mineral on the floor. It was round and green and shiny. It looked like a little jewel. It must have fallen from the basket. Lily picked it up and put it in her pocket. She thought no one would notice. She thought it was a gift for her. She was happy. She had a mineral. She wanted to show it to her mom and her rocks. She did not know she had done something wrong. She did not know she had stolen. She did not know stealing was a serious thing. She did not know she would get in trouble.

Once there was a mighty lion. He was so brave and had the strongest roar. Everywhere he went, other animals knew to stay out of his way. One day, the lion was walking around his kingdom when he heard a loud noise. He walked closer to the noise and saw an old man giving a lecture to some other animals. The lion was so mad that he wanted to roar, but he knew it wouldn't help the situation. So instead, he stuck out his claws and split the ground in two! The lion's mighty roar scared everyone away and the animals ran for their lives. The old man looked very scared and he quickly ran away too. From then on, animals knew never to argue with the mighty lion. He had shown that he was stronger than anyone else.
