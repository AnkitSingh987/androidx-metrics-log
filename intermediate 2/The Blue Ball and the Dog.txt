Category: Intermediate
Title: The Blue Ball and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big blue ball. It lived in a room with a small boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with the blue ball every day. They had lots of fun together. One day, a naughty dog named Max came into the room. Max saw the blue ball and wanted to play with it too. But Max did not play nice. He bit the blue ball and made it flat. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw what happened and she said, "Max, that was naughty!" She took Max out of the room. Then, she fixed the blue ball for Tim. Tim was happy again, and he played with the blue ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom liked to jog every day. He jogged in the park, near the big tree. One day, Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry said, "I dare you to race me to the big tree!" Tom smiled and said, "Okay, let's race!" They ran very fast. Tom was ahead, but Jerry was very quick too. They reached the big tree at the same time. They both laughed and said, "Good race!" From that day on, Tom and Jerry became best friends. They jogged and played together every day. And they always dared each other to have fun and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird named Bob. Bob liked to hide in the big tree. One day, Bob saw some ants. The ants were in a war with the bees. The ants and bees were very mad at each other. Bob wanted to help, but he didn't know how. So, he thought of a fun game. Bob got a big leaf and put it on the ground. He told the ants and bees to hide under the leaf. They all hid under the leaf together. When the ants and bees came out from under the leaf, they were not mad anymore. They saw that they could be friends. The war was over. Bob was happy that he could help the ants and bees. Now, they all played together in the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet turkey. Her name was Tina. She loved to skip and play with her friends in the big farm. One day, Tina saw a big red ball. She wanted to play with it, but it was on the other side of the fence. Tina's friends told her not to go, but she did not listen. She went under the fence to get the ball. Tina was happy to play with the ball, but then she got lost. She was sad and scared. Tina's friends went to find her. They found her and brought her back home. They all learned that it is important to listen to your friends and stay safe.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a lot of fun playing with a ball, and they laughed a lot. Tim felt very happy. After playing, Tim and his mom went to get a tasty ice-cream. Tim picked a big, pink ice-cream. His mom got a tasty, brown ice-cream. They both loved their ice-cream very much. Tim saw a small girl who was sad. She did not have an ice-cream. Tim cared for the girl, so he gave her some of his tasty, pink ice-cream. The girl smiled and said, "Thank you!" Tim felt happy that he could help and care for someone else.

Once upon a time there was a girl who was very curious. Everywhere she went, she wanted to explore and learn new things. One day she stumbled upon an old tap. She gently touched it and it started to shrink until it was the size of her tiny little hand. She was so amazed and excited; she had never seen something shrink like that before. She quickly wanted to explore more and touched other objects to see what would happen. But none of them shrank, nothing changed. She was a bit sad, but she quickly remembered when she touched the tap and it shrank. Maybe if she touched other objects, nothing would happen. She learned the moral lesson that she could only explore things in her own special way. That same day, she went home and told her parents what had happened. Her parents were very proud of her and agreed that touching things and making them shrink was not the best way to explore the world. From that day on, she decided to use her eyes and brain to explore things, instead of using her hands.
