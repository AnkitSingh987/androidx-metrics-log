Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Box

Once upon a time, a thin cat named Max lived in a small house. Max loved to play with his toy ball. One day, Max saw a big box. He wanted to enter the box and find out what was inside. Max was very excited. As Max entered the box, he saw many colors on the walls. Max saw a print of a big fish on one wall. He liked the fish print and wanted to touch it. Max jumped and touched the print, but something unexpected happened. When Max touched the fish print, the walls of the box started to close in. Max was scared and tried to run out, but it was too late. The box closed with Max inside. Max could not play with his toy ball anymore. The end.

One day, a little girl named Sally went on an adventurous walk with her mom. They went to the park to play. Sally saw her mom doing yoga on the grass. Sally wanted to try yoga too. She asked her mom, "Can I do yoga too?" Her mom said, "Of course! Just follow my order and do what I do." So Sally tried to do yoga like her mom. She bent and stretched her arms and legs. They both had fun doing yoga together. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came running up to them. The dog wanted to play with Sally and her mom. Sally's mom said, "Look, Sally! The dog wants to do yoga with us!" So, they all did yoga together, and had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a wise old owl. The owl had a symbol on his chest. The symbol was a star. One day, a small bird came to the wise owl. The bird said, "I hope I can be as wise as you one day." The wise owl smiled and said, "You can. Just listen and learn from others." The small bird listened to the wise owl's words. The bird did what the wise owl said. The bird became wise too. The end.

Once upon a time there was a loyal dog named Jake. Jake lived near a bridge and everyday he would walk along the bridge and look for someone to play with. One day, Jake saw a boy who looked a lot like him. The boy was sad and he was having trouble. He had lost his ball and he couldn't find it anywhere. Jake saw this and he knew he had to help. He jumped onto the bridge and started to work. He searched and searched until he eventually found the ball! The boy was so happy. He asked Jake if he wanted to come home with him and play. Jake was loyal and he knew this was the right thing to do so he happily followed the boy back to his house. From then on they were the best of friends and would play together every day. The end.

One day, a colorful bird named Wink went to play with his friend, Zoom the car. They loved to race each other. Zoom was very fast and Wink could fly high. "Ready to race, Zoom?" Wink asked. "Yes, let's go!" Zoom replied. They started to race. Zoom went fast on the ground and Wink flew high in the sky. They both laughed and had fun. At the end of the race, Wink and Zoom were both tired but happy. They sat under a big tree to rest. "You are very fast, Zoom," said Wink. "Thank you, Wink. You can fly so high!" Zoom replied. They smiled at each other and knew they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ellie. She was 3 years old and was very curious and brave. Every day, she would explore her neighborhood and look for new and exciting things. One day, while she was walking around, she saw a big pear tree across from her house. Ellie wanted to pick a pear from the tree, but she knew she wouldn't be able to reach the pears on her own. So, she asked her neighbor if she could borrow his ladder so that she could pick the pears. The neighbor said no at first, but then Ellie told him that she wanted to pick the pears because they looked really good. The neighbor said okay, and Ellie started to climb the ladder. When she reached the top, she picked the pear, but it was bad! It was brown and mushy and smelled funny too. Ellie was so sad, but then her neighbor said she could pick another. The neighbor told her that if the pear looked bad, she should just put it back and pick a different one. This time, Ellie was careful to pick a good pear. It was juicy and sweet. The neighbor let Ellie borrow the ladder every day so she could pick lots of pears. Now, every day, Ellie eats a yummy pear and is very happy!
