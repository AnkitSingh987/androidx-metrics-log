Category: Intermediate
Title: Jay the Monkey and the Banana

Once upon a time, there was a cute little monkey named Jay. Jay loved bananas! Everywhere he went, he would look for a banana to eat. On this particular day, Jay found a very big and yummy looking banana in a tree. Jay was so excited that he ran up to the tree and tried to take it. But, no matter how hard he tried, he couldn't remove the banana. Jay started to get really frustrated. Just then, Jay heard a voice calling out, "Wait! Don't remove the banana!" Jay looked around and saw a kind old man, who was pointing up at the tree. The old man said, "My friend, if you remove that banana now, you may be sorry later. See... surrounding the banana are a group of angry bees! Once you remove the banana, the bees will come after you. You must wait for them to go away first." Jay listened to the wise old man and waited for the bees to leave. When all the bees were gone, Jay quickly removed the banana and ate it right away. He was so happy that he had been able to get the yummy banana without getting stung by the bees.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray cat. The cat liked to play with a ball. The ball was her favorite thing. She played with it every day. One day, the cat saw a big gray cloud in the sky. The cloud became bigger and bigger. The cat was scared. She ran to her home with her ball. When the cat got home, she found her mom. Her mom told her not to be scared of the gray cloud. The cloud would become rain, and rain was a good thing. The cat felt better and played with her ball again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved pandas. She wanted to have a panda of her own, more than anything in the whole world. One day she asked her mom, "Can I have a panda?" Her mom smiled and said, "Wow! Pandas are very expensive. We can't afford one right now." The little girl thought and thought, and then she came up with a plan. She said, "Mommy, I'll sit very quietly and not move until you buy me a panda!" Her mom laughed and said, "Well, sitting is not going to get us a panda. But I promise that one day soon, if you keep being a good girl, we will get one." The little girl smiled and said, "Okay, Mommy! I'll keep sitting and being good until we get a panda!"

Once upon a time, there was a small car named Zoom. Zoom was very fast and loved to race around the town. He had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, Zoom's best friend, a little dog named Spot, wanted to play a new game. Spot said, "Zoom, let's play a game where you demand what we do next!" Zoom thought this was a fun idea, so they started to play. Zoom said, "First, let's go to the park!" So, they went to the park and played on the swings. Then, Zoom demanded, "Now, let's go to the ice cream shop!" They went to the ice cream shop and had a yummy treat. After that, Zoom demanded, "Let's go to the beach and build a sandcastle!" They went to the beach and built a big sandcastle together. Zoom and Spot had so much fun playing their new game all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a messy boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the mud and make a big mess. One day, he found a little bird in the mud. The bird was in shock because it could not fly. Tim said to the bird, "Don't worry, I will help you." He tried to clean the bird's wings, but he was too messy. The bird started to weep because it was scared. Tim's mom came outside and saw the bird. She said, "Oh no, the bird is hurt!" Tim felt sad because he could not help the bird. In the end, the bird was still in shock and could not fly. Tim learned that sometimes he needed to be clean to help others.
