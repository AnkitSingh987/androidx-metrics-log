Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Bird

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park in summer. They see many flowers, birds and bugs. They have fun. One day, they find a little bird on the ground. The bird is hurt. It cannot fly. Anna and Ben feel sad for the bird. "Can we help the bird?" Anna asks. "Maybe we can," Ben says. "Let's take it to my mom. She knows about birds." They carefully pick up the bird and put it in a box. They run to Ben's house. Ben's mom is charming. She smiles and hugs them. "What do you have there?" she asks. "A hurt bird, mom. Can you help it?" Ben says. "Let me see," she says. She opens the box and looks at the bird. She gently touches its wing. "It has a broken wing. But don't worry. I can help it. I have some bandages and medicine. It will get better soon." "Thank you, mom. You are the best," Ben says. "Thank you, Ben's mom. You are very kind," Anna says. "You are welcome, children. You are very brave and caring. You did a good thing by helping the bird," she says. She wraps the bird's wing and gives it some water and seeds. She puts it back in the box. She tells Anna and Ben to keep the bird in a quiet and warm place. She says they can visit the bird every day until it can fly again. Anna and Ben are happy. They hug the bird and each other. They are proud of themselves. They helped the bird. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an obedient boy who always helped his mom. One day, his mom asked him to bake a cake with her. Tim was very excited and said, "Yes, Mom! I want to help you bake!" They started to bake the cake. Tim's mom got a big box with all the things they needed. Tim helped his mom put the things from the box into a big bowl. They mixed everything together and put it in the oven. Tim and his mom were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. When they opened the oven, they found a big, colorful toy inside! Tim was very surprised and said, "Wow, Mom! How did the cake turn into a toy?" His mom smiled and said, "This was a magic box, Tim. Because you were so obedient and helpful, the magic box turned our cake into a special toy just for you!" Tim learned that being obedient and helpful can bring good surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a kind king. The king wanted a new crown. So, he asked his friend to design a new crown for him. His friend was very good at making pretty things. His friend made a beautiful crown. It was gold and shiny. It had many pretty colors on it. The king was very happy when he saw the new crown. He said, "Thank you, my friend, for making me this pretty crown." The king wore the new crown every day. He felt very safe with it. The people in his land were happy too. They knew their king was safe with his new crown. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They saw a big, green leaf on a plant. They wanted to see what was under the leaf. They lifted the leaf and saw a brown, hairy thing. It was a caterpillar. "Look, a caterpillar!" Tom said. "It is so fuzzy and cute. Can we keep it?" Lily shook her head. "No, we can't. It is not a pet. It is a bug. It is disgusting. It will eat our plants and make holes in them." Tom did not care. He liked the caterpillar. He wanted to touch it. He reached out his hand and gently twisted the caterpillar. The caterpillar wiggled and curled up. "Stop it, Tom!" Lily said. "You are hurting it. Put it back on the leaf. It needs to be free." Tom did not listen. He twisted the caterpillar again. He wanted to see what it would do. He did not see the butterfly that flew over his head. The butterfly was bright and beautiful. It had orange and black wings. It landed on Lily's hair. "Wow, a butterfly!" Lily said. "It is so pretty and graceful. Can we keep it?" Tom looked up and saw the butterfly. He dropped the caterpillar and ran to Lily. He wanted to catch the butterfly. He reached out his hand and tried to grab the butterfly. The butterfly flew away. "Stop it, Tom!" Lily said. "You are scaring it. Let it go. It needs to be free." Tom did not listen. He chased the butterfly around the garden. He wanted to see it up close. He did not see the caterpillar that crawled away from the leaf. The caterpillar was tired and hungry. It needed to make a cocoon. It found a safe and cozy spot under a flower. It wrapped itself in silk and fell asleep. The end.
