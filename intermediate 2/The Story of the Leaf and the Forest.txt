Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Leaf and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a big, interesting place called the forest. In this place, there were many animals who liked to play and have fun. One day, a little rabbit named Benny wanted to make a special gift for his best friend, Lucy the squirrel. Benny thought and thought, and he decided to cut out a heart from a leaf to show his love for his friend. He hopped around the forest, looking for the perfect leaf. He found a big, green leaf and used his sharp teeth to cut the shape of a heart. Benny was very happy with his gift and went to find Lucy. When he found her, he gave her the leaf heart. Lucy loved it! She thought it was the most interesting gift she had ever gotten. Benny and Lucy played and laughed all day, knowing that they had a special friendship in their interesting place, the forest.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. He saw a shiny scale on the ground. He picked it up and thought, "I wonder who this belongs to?" Fin swam around and asked his friends. He asked a crab, "Do you know who this scale belongs to?" The crab said, "No, I don't know. Maybe ask the turtle." Fin swam to the turtle and asked, "Do you know who this scale belongs to?" The turtle said, "I think it belongs to the big fish, Gill." Fin found Gill and asked, "Is this your scale?" Gill looked guilty and said, "Yes, it is. Thank you for finding it." Fin felt happy that he helped his friend. They all swam and played together in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little swan named Lily. She lived by a big lake with her family. Lily loved to play with her friends in the water. One day, Lily saw a new swan near the lake. She swam up to the new swan and said, "Hi! I am Lily. Do you want to play with me?" The new swan smiled and said, "Yes! I am Sam. I would love to play with you." Lily and Sam played all day long. They splashed in the water and chased each other. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Lily said, "Sam, let's add you to our group of friends." Sam was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lily! I am so glad I met you." From that day on, Lily and Sam were best friends. They played together every day and had many fun adventures. And the little swan group was even better with Sam added to it.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a mysterious wire in his room. He did not know where it came from. He picked it up and looked at it. It was long and thin. Tim went to his mom and asked, "Mom, what is this?" His mom looked at the mysterious wire and said, "I don't know, Tim. Maybe it is for a toy." Tim decided to wipe the dust off the mysterious wire. He used a soft cloth and wiped it clean. As he wiped, the wire started to shine. It was very pretty. Tim showed the shiny wire to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow, that is a cool wire, Tim!" They played with the wire all day, making shapes and having fun. They were happy to have found the mysterious wire.

One day, a tall girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw her friend, a wide tree named Mr. Tree, and said, "Hi, Mr. Tree!" "Hi, Lucy!" Mr. Tree said. "Can you bring me some water? I am very thirsty." Lucy wanted to help Mr. Tree. She went to the river and filled a big cup with water. She carried the water back to Mr. Tree. But on the way, something unexpected happened. A small bird flew down and drank all the water from the cup! "Oh no, the bird drank all the water!" Lucy said. "I will go back to the river and bring you more water, Mr. Tree." Lucy went back to the river and filled the cup again. This time, she covered the cup with her hand so the bird could not drink the water. She brought the water to Mr. Tree and he drank it all. Mr. Tree was very happy. "Thank you, Lucy!" Mr. Tree said. "You are a good friend." The moral of the story is to never give up, even when something unexpected happens.
