Category: Intermediate
Title: The Toy Train Was on Track and the Fast

One day, a toy train was on a track. The train was going round and round. It was a bit boring for the train. The train wanted to have fun and go fast. So, the train went faster and faster on the track. As the train went fast, it saw a break in the track. The train was scared. It did not want to fall off the track. But the train could not stop. It was going too fast. The train closed its eyes and hoped for the best. Then, something unexpected happened. Instead of falling, the train jumped over the break in the track. The train was so happy! It was not boring anymore. The train had fun going fast and jumping over the break. From that day, the train loved to go fast and jump on the track.

One day, a little bird named Tim went to the park to play. Tim liked to whistle. He whistled all day long. He was a very noisy bird. All the other birds liked to listen to Tim whistle. In the park, Tim saw a big cone. The cone was on the ground. Tim thought it was a fun toy. He pushed the cone with his beak. The cone rolled and rolled. Tim was happy and whistled a happy song. Soon, other birds came to play with Tim and the cone. They all had fun and made a lot of noise. They whistled and sang together. Tim and his new friends played with the cone all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. He had a red wagon that he loved very much. One day, he wanted to mail a letter to his friend, Sam. Tim put the letter in his wagon and went to the mail place. On his way, a big wind came and blew the letter out of the wagon. Tim was sad, but he kept looking for the letter. He found the letter in a big tree. Tim was happy, but he did not know how to get it. A nice bird saw Tim and helped him. The bird took the letter from the tree and put it back in the wagon. Tim said "thank you" to the bird and went to mail the letter. Tim and Sam were very happy, and they played together all day.

One day, a little girl named Ann went to the park with her mom. They played on the swings and slides. Ann saw a hidden door in a big tree. She told her mom, "Look, a door!" Her mom said, "Let's open it!" They opened the door and found a room with many numbers on the walls. Ann touched the number two, and something unexpected happened. The room started to shake! Suddenly, a big, friendly bear appeared. He said, "Hello! You found my secret room. When you touch a number, I will give you that many hugs!" Ann was happy and touched the number three. The bear gave her three big hugs, and they all laughed and played together.

Miss Brown lived in a cheerful yellow house and had a lovely garden with lots of flowers. Every morning she would sit on her porch and watch the birds in the trees. One morning, she saw a little bird tapping on the windowsill. "Hello there!" Miss Brown said to the bird. She asked, "What can I do for you?" The bird chirped and flew around Miss Brown. She thought for a moment and knew just what to do. She went into her kitchen and came out with a brown nut. She held it out to the bird, who hopped closer and took it gratefully. Miss Brown smiled, happy that she could help the little bird. "You're welcome," she said. The bird chirped again and flew off, carrying the nut in its beak. Miss Brown waved goodbye and sat back on the porch to watch the bird fly away.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing with her ball. She loved to kick it very high into the sky. Lucy also liked to wear great clothes. She thought it was fun to play in fashion. Lucy's mom said, "Be careful when you kick the ball near the window." Lucy nodded and kept playing. She knew she needed to be careful, but she was having so much fun. As she played, Lucy kicked the ball very high. It went up, up, up, and then it came down, down, down. The ball hit the window with a big crash! Lucy was sad, but she remembered what her mom said. Lucy said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I will be more careful next time." Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lucy. We all make mistakes. Just remember to be careful." From that day on, Lucy was more careful when she played with her ball. She still had fun and wore great fashion, but she made sure to keep her ball away from the window.
