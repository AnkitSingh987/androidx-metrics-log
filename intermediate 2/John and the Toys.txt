Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Toys

John was an independent boy. He loved to share his toys with his friends. One day, he saw a shiny pistol in his toy box. He was excited because he wanted to share it with his friends. He asked his dad if he could. His dad said: "John, you shouldn't share the pistol with your friends. It's not safe." John replied: "I promise to be careful. Please?" His dad thought for a moment and said: "Ok, you can share it, but please be careful and don't point it at anyone." John was very happy and hugged his dad. He ran outside to show his friends the shiny pistol. Everyone was so excited to see it. They all took turns showing each other how the gun worked. Everyone played safe and had a lot of fun with it. John was proud to share his pistol and be an independent boy.

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Whiskers. Whiskers had a best friend named Spot, who was a dog. They played together all day and had lots of fun. One day, Whiskers got very dirty while playing outside. Spot said, "Whiskers, you need to bathe." Whiskers did not like to bathe. He thought it was no fun. But Spot told him, "If you don't bathe, you will get sick." So, Whiskers decided to listen to his friend and bathe. He got into the water, and it was not so bad. After the bath, Whiskers felt clean and nice. From that day on, Whiskers and Spot took a bath every week. They learned that it is important to listen to friends and stay clean. And they lived happily ever after, playing and having fun together. The moral of the story is to always listen to your friends and take care of yourself.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird named Bob. Bob loved to eat bugs with sauce. He would find a bug, dip it in sauce, and eat it with a big smile. One day, Bob found a very big bug. He thought, "This bug will be so good with sauce!" But when he tried to pick it up, it was too heavy. Bob needed to weigh the bug to see how much it weighed. Bob found a stick and put the bug on one end and a rock on the other end. The stick moved up and down until both sides were the same. Now, Bob knew how heavy the bug was. He called his friends to help him carry the bug. They all dipped the bug in sauce and had a big feast. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Amy. One day she spotted a gray bird off in the distance. She was so excited and ran to it. When she got there, she saw it was a broken bird that was stuck in a tree. Amy said, "Oh no! What can I do?" Then she had an idea. She said, "I'm going to dive into the river and get the bird!" So she jumped in the river and started swimming. After a few minutes, she reached the bird and gently grabbed it with her hands. She swam to shore and handed the bird to her mom. Her mom checked it out and saw it wasn't hurt. Amy said, "Hey look, the bird isn't broken anymore!" Her mom smiled and they hugged. The end.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a fork that he liked very much. He used his fork to eat all of his food. One day, Tim's fork broke. He was very sad. Tim wanted to fix his fork. He asked his mom for help. His mom said, "Let's try to fix it together." They tried to fix the fork, but it was still broken. Tim felt embarrassed because he couldn't fix his fork. Then, Tim's dad came home. He saw Tim and his mom trying to fix the fork. Tim's dad said, "I can help you fix the fork." They all worked together, and they fixed the fork. Tim was very happy, and he said, "Thank you, Mom and Dad, for helping me fix my fork!"
