Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ranch and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big ranch. On the ranch, there was a wealthy family. They had many animals. They had cows, horses, and pigs. The family was very happy. One day, the family got a new dog. The dog was small and cute. The family loved the dog. They wanted to teach the dog new tricks. The dog was very smart. The family taught the dog to sit, jump, and fetch. The dog learned quickly. The family was very proud. The dog loved to play and learn. They all lived happily on the ranch.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk with her mom. They walked into a mist. The mist was wet and made Lily feel uncomfortable. She told her mom, "I don't like this mist, Mommy." Her mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Lily. The mist will go away soon." They kept walking, and Lily saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it, but her mom mentioned that it might be too slippery because of the mist. Lily listened to her mom and didn't climb the tree. Instead, they found a dry spot under the tree to sit and have a snack. As they ate, the mist went away, and the sun came out. Lily felt happy and warm again.

---

Ben and Lily were playing in the pool. They liked to splash and swim and have fun. Ben had a big ball that he threw in the air and caught. Lily wanted to play with the ball too. "Can I have the ball, Ben?" Lily asked. "No, it's mine!" Ben said. He threw the ball again, but this time it went too far. It hit a rock and bounced back. It hit Ben on the head and made him cry. "Ow, ow, ow! That hurt!" Ben said. He held his head and felt a bump. Lily felt sorry for Ben. She swam to him and gave him a hug. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. Are you okay?" Ben stopped crying and looked at Lily. He said, "I'm okay, Lily. Thank you. Do you want to play with the ball?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, please. But let's be careful. We don't want to get hurt again." Ben nodded and said, "Okay. Let's be careful. And let's share the ball." They set the ball in the water and played together. They were happy and had fun. They were good friends.

One day, a little girl named Jane went for a walk. She liked to look at things. The sun was bright, and the sky was blue. Jane felt happy. As Jane walked, she saw a quiet cat. The cat was looking at some mail on the ground. Jane looked at the mail too. It was a pretty picture of a dog. Jane picked up the mail and showed it to the cat. The cat looked at the picture and then at Jane. They both smiled. Jane and the cat walked home together, looking at the pretty picture of the dog.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy copper dragon. He was mean to everyone around him. One day, a young boy wanted to understand why the dragon was so grumpy. He had an idea. He gathered some of his friends and brought some of their favorite treats to the dragon. The dragon was surprised. "Why are you giving me food?" he asked. The boy smiled and said, "I want to make you happy!" The dragon, who wasn't used to people being nice to him, was shocked. He reluctantly accepted the treats, but he remained grumpy and didn't understand why. The boy and his friends were determined to make the dragon happy. They thought of a plan, and they gathered again the next day with even more treats. When the dragon saw what they had brought, he softened a little. He smiled and accepted the treats. After some time, the copper dragon became less grumpy and instead of being mean to everyone, he started to be friendly. The boy was happy to see this change, he understood that a little kindness can go a long way and can truly make people feel better.
