Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Boy and the Butterfly

Once upon a time there was a boy named Sam. He was very excited to go outside and play in the sun. As Sam opened the door, he saw a bright yellow shirt floating in the air. It was moving so quickly, he couldn't get a close look. Sam shouted "What is that? Come here!" The bright shirt moved towards him until it stopped close to his feet. Sam was surprised to see it was a butterfly. The butterfly fluttered its wings and asked in a tiny voice, "Do you like my shirt?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, it's very bright!" He then reached out his hand to touch the butterfly, but it moved away quickly and flew off into the sky. Sam waved goodbye and smiled as he watched the butterfly move away. He was very happy to meet the bright shirt wearing butterfly.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had an adorable pet cat named Fluffy. Sally and Fluffy loved to play together. One day, they went to the park to play. At the park, they met a boy named Tim. Tim had a cute dog named Spot. Sally and Tim decided to trade pets for a day. Sally took Spot, and Tim took Fluffy. They both said, "Let's play with our new pets!" As they played, a big wind came and blew the park gate closed. It was locked! Sally, Tim, Fluffy, and Spot were all stuck inside. They were scared. But then, Fluffy jumped very high and unlocked the gate with her paw. They were free! Sally and Tim were so happy. They decided to trade back their pets. They learned that even if something unexpected happens, friends can help each other. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot loved to look out the window every day. He liked to see the birds, trees, and people walking by. One day, Spot saw a big ball stuck in a tree outside the window. He wanted to get the ball down for the kids who were sad. Spot had an idea. He would attach a long rope to the ball and pull it down. Spot asked his friend, a helpful cat named Whiskers, to climb the tree and attach the rope to the ball. Whiskers was scared, but Spot said he would help. Whiskers climbed up the tree and attached the rope. Then, Spot pulled the rope with all his strength. The ball came down from the tree, and the kids were happy. Spot and Whiskers were proud that they could help. From that day on, Spot and Whiskers became best friends and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a tough boy who liked to play outside. One day, Tim found a magic stand in the park. The stand was very pretty, and Tim wanted to keep it. Tim took the stand home and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a magic stand!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "That's nice, Tim. What does it do?" Tim didn't know, so he decided to find out. Tim tried many things with the stand. Then he found out that when he put something on the stand and said "shrink," it became very small. Tim was so happy! He showed his mom, and she was amazed. They had lots of fun with the magic stand, and Tim learned to be careful with his new power.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was an owl. This owl was not a regular owl. He had a big seat on his back. One day, a little boy saw the owl and said, "Wow! Can I sit on your seat, Mr. Owl?" The owl nodded his head and let the boy sit on his seat. As they flew in the sky, the boy was very happy. He could see his house, his friends, and the park from up high. The owl flew around and showed the boy many things. But the owl started to feel tired. He was not used to flying with someone on his seat. The boy noticed that the owl was getting tired. He said, "Mr. Owl, are you okay?" The owl did not answer. He was too tired to speak. The boy started to get worried. He remembered that the owl was not a regular owl, and maybe he could not fly for a long time with someone on his seat. Suddenly, the owl could not fly anymore. They started to fall down, down, down from the sky. The boy was very scared. He closed his eyes and hoped for the best. But, sadly, they fell to the ground, and both the owl and the boy got hurt. The boy learned that he should not have sat on the owl's seat, because the owl was not a regular owl.
