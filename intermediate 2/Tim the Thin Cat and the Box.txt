Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Thin Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with a nice family. One day, Tim found a big box in the living room. He was very curious about what was inside. Tim asked his friend, a small mouse named Max, "What do you think is in the box?" Max thought for a minute and said, "I don't know, let's reveal what's inside!" They worked together to open the box. Inside the box, they found many colorful toys. Tim and Max were very happy and played with the toys all day. They had a lot of fun and became even better friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a fancy hat. He loved his hat very much. One day, he wanted to show his hat to his friend, Sam. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look at my fancy hat!" Sam liked the hat too. Sam said, "We should hang it on the wall so everyone can see it." Tim agreed, and they found a nail to hang the hat on. They hung the hat on the nail, but then the wind blew it down. Tim was sad. Sam said, "Don't worry, we can hang it up again." They tried again, and this time the hat stayed on the nail. They were happy. The moral of the story is to never give up. If something does not work the first time, you can try again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to go to the park with his mom. They would run and play all day. Tim liked to play on the swings and slide down the big slide. His mom would push him on the swings, and they would laugh and have fun. One day, Tim found a fork near the park. It was a small, shiny fork. He showed it to his mom and she said, "That's a nice fork, Tim! Let's take it home and use it for dinner." Tim was very happy and put the fork in his pocket. At dinner time, Tim's mom made a yummy meal. There were potatoes, chicken, and sour apples for dessert. Tim used his new fork to eat his food. He liked the sour apples the best because they made his face scrunch up. Tim's mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you found that fork, Tim. It made our dinner even more fun!" And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a friendly cat named Tom lived in a small house. He loved to play with his ball. One day, Tom saw a long line of ants. They were walking and carrying food. Tom thought it was fun to watch them. Tom said to the ants, "Can I borrow your food for a little while? I want to play with it." The ants stopped and said, "Okay, but please be careful. We need the food for our family." Tom played with the food, but then something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the food away. Tom was sad and scared. He didn't know what to do. He told the ants what happened and they were sad too. But then, a friendly bird named Sue saw what happened. She flew up high and found the food. Sue brought the food back to Tom and the ants. They were all very happy. Tom said, "Thank you, Sue. I will be more careful next time." From that day on, Tom, the ants, and Sue became good friends. They played together and always helped each other when they needed it.
