Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy dog named Max. Max was small and soft. Tom loved Max very much. One day, Tom and Max went to the park to play. They were very happy. At the park, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue had a toy cat named Kitty. Tom and Sue played together with Max and Kitty. They had lots of fun. Then, Tom said, "Let's call our toys and see who comes first!" Sue agreed. Tom called Max, and Sue called Kitty. Max came to Tom, but Kitty did not come to Sue. Sue was sad. Tom felt bad for Sue. He said, "Don't worry, we will find Kitty." They looked for Kitty, but could not find her. Then, a big wind came and took Max away. Tom was very sad. He remembered how grateful he was when he got Max as a gift. Now, both Max and Kitty were gone. Tom and Sue were very sad. The big wind came again and took Tom and Sue away too. They never found Max and Kitty. They were lost forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to visit new places. One day, she went on a visit to a forest she had never seen before. As she walked, she saw a little gray bird perched atop a branch. "Hello bird!" she said cheerfully. "Where are you going?" The bird chirped and said, "I'm going to find some useful things." The girl decided to join the bird on its quest. Together they walked through the forest, until they saw a gray rock. "Look, a useful rock!" the bird happily said. The little girl picked up the rock. She thought it would be the perfect present for her mom. With the rock tucked securely in her pocket, they continued their adventure. The bird and the little girl spent the rest of the day visiting different places in the forest. Every new place they visited had something useful to offer. The girl was so happy that she had gone on this visit with the little gray bird.

One day, a little spider felt very tired. He had a long day and wanted to rest. He saw a big tree and thought it would be a good place to sleep. So, the little spider climbed up the tree and found a nice, cozy spot. The next day, the little spider met a friendly bug. The bug told the spider about a fun place to play. The spider felt excited and wanted to go, but he was still tired. The bug urged the spider to come with him. The little spider thought about it and decided to rest a little more before going to play. After a short nap, the little spider felt better. He went to the fun place with the friendly bug. They played and laughed all day long. The little spider was happy he listened to the bug's urge to play, but also glad he took a nap first. The nap helped him have more energy to enjoy the fun day with his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dull stable. In the stable, there was a horse named Tom and a cow named Sue. They were friends, but they always wanted to play different games. One day, Tom said, "Let's play tag!" But Sue did not want to play tag. Sue wanted to play hide and seek. Tom did not like that game. They could not pick a game to play. This made them sad. As they sat in the stable, they started to notice a smell. It was a bad smell. They looked around and saw a big mess. They did not clean up the stable. The smell got worse and worse. In the end, they were still sad, and their home was very smelly.

Once upon a time there was a very smart jeep. Every day it would go on adventures around the neighborhood. One day while the jeep was out, it drove by a park. The jeep thought it looked like a fun place and wanted to explore, so it drove inside. Once the jeep was in the park, it was amazed by all the things it could do. There were swings and slides and lots of other things to play on. The jeep stayed for hours until it was time to go home. When the jeep was ready to leave, it parked in the very same spot it had come from. It couldn't wait to come back and play in the park again soon.
