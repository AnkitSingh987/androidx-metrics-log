Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Drawing

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to draw. He enjoyed drawing images of the sun, trees, and animals. One day, Tom drew a clear image of a big, happy dog. He was very proud of his drawing. Tom showed his drawing to his friend, Sara. "Look, Sara! I drew a big, happy dog," said Tom. Sara looked at the image and smiled. "Wow, Tom! That is a very clear and nice drawing of a dog! I like it a lot!" said Sara. Tom and Sara decided to draw more images together. They enjoyed drawing animals, flowers, and houses. They had a lot of fun and their pictures were very clear. The friends were happy to share their drawings with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Lucky. Lucky liked to play near the post by his house. One day, a big bird came to the post. Lucky wanted to protect his post, so he barked at the bird. The bird was scared and flew away. The next day, the bird came back with a friend. Lucky was not scared. He barked louder to protect his post. The two birds flew away, but they left a shiny stone behind. Lucky picked it up and took it to his house. Lucky showed his mom the shiny stone. She told him that it was a special stone that could make one wish come true. Lucky felt bad for scaring the birds away. He wished for the birds to be his friends. The birds came back, and they all played together near the post. Lucky learned that being kind is better than protecting things all by himself.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw a perfect red ball on the ground. Amy wanted to play with the ball, so she grabbed it with her small hands. She was very happy. Amy walked more and found a radio. The radio was playing fun songs. She grabbed the radio and danced to the music. Amy smiled and laughed as she danced. Amy took the perfect red ball and the radio to her home. She played and danced all day. She had the best day ever with her new toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside all day. One day, Tim saw a beautiful sunset. It was orange and pink. He wanted to show his mom. "Mom, come see the sunset!" Tim said. His mom came outside and saw the important sunset. She smiled and hugged Tim. "Tim, it's time to wash your hands for dinner," Mom said. Tim went inside and washed his hands. They had a yummy dinner together. They talked about the pretty sunset they saw. Tim felt happy and loved.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a small pocket on his shirt. One day, he saw a big open field. In the field, there were many kids playing a game. Tim wanted to play too. The game was to see who could run the fastest. Tim was very fast. He ran and ran. His legs moved like the wind. The other kids tried to catch up, but Tim was too quick. He crossed the finish line first. Tim won the race! After the race, Tim felt very happy. He put a small toy in his pocket to remember the fun day. He went back to the open field every day to play and win more games. Tim loved his pocket and the open field.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim was a happy baby who liked to play and laugh. One day, Tim's mommy took him to the park. The sun was shining, but the wind felt a little harsh. Tim's mommy made sure to keep him warm with a soft blanket. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. Tim's mommy said, "Let's play peek-a-boo with the tree!" She hid Tim behind the tree and then peeked out. "Peek-a-boo!" she said with a big smile. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. They played this fun game many times. After playing, Tim and his mommy sat on a soft, green grass. Tim's mommy gave him a yummy snack to eat. They talked and laughed together. Tim said, "I love you, mommy!" His mommy hugged him and said, "I love you too, Tim!" They had a great day at the park, even with the harsh wind.
