Category: Intermediate
Title: Buddy the Clever Dog and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a clever little dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to collect things. One day, he went on a walk to find new things to add to his collection. As Buddy walked, he saw some furniture outside a big house. He saw a small chair that was just his size. Buddy thought it would be perfect for his collection. He grabbed the chair with his mouth and started to take it home. But as Buddy walked, the chair began to talk! It said, "Hello, Buddy! I am a magic chair that can help you find more things for your collection!" Buddy was very surprised. The magic chair showed Buddy where to find the best things to collect. And they lived happily ever after, always looking for new things to add to their collection.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a long vine. The vine was very strong and it helped animals. The vine provided a way for animals to play and have fun. One day, a tired little monkey came to the vine. He wanted to rest and find food. The vine saw the tired monkey and wanted to help him. So, the vine let the monkey swing and play on it. After playing, the tired monkey found some food and was happy. The vine was happy too because it could help the monkey. From that day on, the monkey and the vine became best friends. They played together every day, and the vine always provided help to the tired monkey.

One day, a nice cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a row of pretty flowers. Tom wanted to grab a flower to give to his friend, Sam the dog. As Tom reached to grab a flower, a big bee came out from behind it. The bee was not happy. Tom was scared and ran away fast. He did not want the bee to be mad at him. Tom ran back to Sam and told him about the bee. Sam had an idea. They went back to the row of flowers and found the bee. Sam said, "We are sorry, Mr. Bee. We did not mean to make you mad." The bee forgave them and let Tom grab a flower. They all became friends and had a nice day together.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big house and a lot of toys. He wore a belt with his pants. One day, the man lost his belt. He was sad. A little boy found the belt in the park. He knew it was the wealthy man's belt. The boy wanted to keep it, but he knew it was not right. He decided to give it back to the man. The boy went to the man's house and gave him the belt. The man was very happy. He said, "Thank you for giving me my belt back." The boy said, "I am sorry I wanted to keep it." The man said, "I forgive you." They became good friends and played with the toys together.

Once upon a time, in a big sky, there was a helpful comet. This comet liked to send light to the night sky. It made the sky bright and pretty. One day, the comet met a little star. The star was sad. The comet asked, "Why are you sad, little star?" The little star said, "I am too small. I can't make the sky bright like you." The helpful comet wanted to make the little star happy. So, it had an idea. The comet said, "Let's play a game. I will send you some of my light, and you will shine bright like me." The little star agreed, and the comet sent some light to the star. The little star started to shine bright and big. Now, the little star was happy, and the night sky was even more pretty. The comet and the little star became best friends, and they made the sky bright together.

One day, a sad cat named Tim found a big sack. Inside the sack, there was a lot of dirty laundry. Tim wanted to help, so he took the sack to his friend, a happy dog named Max. Tim said to Max, "I found a big sack of dirty laundry. Can you help me clean it?" Max smiled and said, "Yes, let's clean it together!" So, they took the laundry to the water and started to wash it. As they washed the laundry, they found a small toy. It was a ball! They knew that once the laundry was clean, they would have a fun toy to play with. The sad cat and the happy dog finished washing the laundry, and then they played with the ball. Tim was not sad anymore because he had a new friend and a fun toy.
