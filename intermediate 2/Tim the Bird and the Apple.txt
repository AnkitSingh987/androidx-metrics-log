Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to whistle all day long. One sunny day, he found a pair of sunglasses on the ground. Tim thought the sunglasses were very funny, so he picked them up with his beak. Tim wore the sunglasses and flew around the forest, whistling a happy song. He saw a big tree with a delicious red apple. He wanted to eat the apple, but it was too high for him to reach. He tried to jump and flap his wings, but he could not get the apple. Just then, a friendly squirrel named Sam came by. Sam saw Tim wearing the sunglasses and thought it was funny too. Sam said, "I can help you get the apple if you give me the sunglasses." Tim agreed, and Sam climbed the tree to get the apple. But when Sam reached the top, he found out the apple was not real. It was a shiny red ball! They both laughed and played with the ball, and Tim gave Sam the sunglasses. They became best friends and had lots of fun together.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play games. Today they play a game with forks. They have two clean forks and two dirty forks. They put them on the table and mix them up. "Who can find the clean forks first?" Sara asks. "Me! Me!" Ben says. He tries to grab a fork. "Wait, Ben! We have to say 'go' first!" Sara says. She counts to three. "One, two, three, go!" Sara and Ben look at the forks. They try to pick the clean ones. Sara finds one clean fork and puts it in her hand. Ben finds another clean fork and puts it in his hand. "Look, I have a clean fork!" Sara says. "Me too!" Ben says. They look at the table. There are two dirty forks left. "Who can find the other clean fork?" Sara asks. "There is no other clean fork, silly!" Ben says. "We have them both!" "Oh, you are right!" Sara says. "Then we both win!" Sara and Ben smile. They hug each other. They are happy. They like to play games with forks.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, while playing, Mia saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it and find a good snack. Mia climbed the tree and found a yummy snack. But then, she heard a loud noise. It made her scare. She looked around and saw a small bird. The bird was sad and scared too. Mia felt ashamed for scaring the bird. She wanted to help the bird feel better. Mia shared her snack with the bird. They both ate and became friends. The bird was not scared anymore. It was a happy surprise for both of them. They played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Mia and the bird were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She was three years old and she loved to explore. She had a pet rabbit, Cotton, and they decided to go on an adventure and explore the forest. As Lucy and Cotton walked through the forest, it was very gloomy. But Lucy was excited to discover what surprises were around the corner! Suddenly, Lucy heard a strange noise. She stopped and looked around. Then she saw an old tree with an owl sitting on a branch. The owl looked at Lucy with eyes that twinkled in the gloom. Lucy asked the owl, “Who are you?” The owl answered, “I am the spirit of the forest. I live here and watch over everything. I am here to help you discover the wonderful things the forest has to offer.” Lucy was amazed! She and Cotton clapped their paws together in excitement. The owl smiled at them and waved her wings. “Come with me,” said the owl. “Let’s go on a journey of discovery!” And they did! Lucy and Cotton discovered all sorts of amazing things in the forest with the help of their friend, the spirit owl. They found bugs and flowers, sticks and stones, and it was an adventure they would never forget.
