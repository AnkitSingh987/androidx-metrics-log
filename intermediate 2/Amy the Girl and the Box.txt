Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Box

Once there was a little girl named Amy who loved to skip. She could skip from the flower bed to the mailbox, the sidewalk to the trees. Every time she skipped she looked so happy that everyone in the neighbourhood just couldn't help but smile. One day Amy's dad asked her to come into his workshop. He wanted to show her something special. He pointed to an old box with an original knob on the front. He said, “Amy, can you open this box? It might be filled with something magical.” Amy was so excited! She tried to turn the knob but it was so tight she just couldn't do it. She asked her Dad, “Can you help me open the box, Daddy?” Her Dad smiled and gave the knob a big twist. When he opened the box, Amy saw there were lots of ribbons inside. She let out a cheer and said, “I can use them to skip!” So, with her ribbons in hand, Amy skipped her way around the neighbourhood. Everywhere she went, she put a smile on everyone's faces. The original knob had opened a box of joy!

Ben likes apples. He likes to eat them when he is hungry. He likes to bite them and hear the crunch. He likes to feel the juice in his mouth. One day, Ben sees a big red apple on the table. It is Mom's apple. She is going to eat it later. But Ben wants it now. He thinks Mom will not know. He takes the apple and runs to his room. He bites the apple and hears the crunch. He feels the juice in his mouth. But he also feels something else. He feels guilty. He knows he did a bad thing. He knows Mom will be sad. He hears Mom coming. She is looking for her apple. She asks Ben if he saw it. Ben does not say anything. He hides the apple under his pillow. He hopes Mom will not find it. But Mom is smart. She sees the apple core on the floor. She sees the juice on Ben's face. She knows Ben ate her apple. She is not happy. She is angry. She tells Ben he was naughty. She tells him he has to say sorry. She tells him he has to share his toys with his sister. Ben feels more guilty. He wishes he did not eat the apple. He wishes he listened to Mom. He says sorry to Mom. He says he loves her. He hopes she will forgive him.

Once upon a time, there was a red ball. The red ball loved to play with its friends, the square and the triangle. They all had fun with their different shapes. They played games and laughed together. One sunny day, the red ball, the square, and the triangle went to the park. They played hide and seek. The red ball was good at hiding because it could roll away fast. The square and the triangle tried to find the red ball, but it was hard. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the red ball up high into the sky. The square and the triangle were sad. They missed their friend, the red ball. They looked up and saw the red ball floating away. But the wind also did something special. It changed the red ball's shape. The red ball became a red heart. The square and the triangle were so surprised! They loved the new shape of their friend. The red heart floated back down to the ground. The red heart, the square, and the triangle played together again. They were happy and loved each other's shapes. They knew that even if their shapes changed, they would always be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Chewy. Chewy loved to chew on everything he could find. He would chew on shoes, toys, and even his own tail sometimes. Chewy was a very happy dog and always had a big smile on his face. One day, Chewy found a big, red ball in the park. He was so excited to chew on it! He ran to the ball and started to chew and chew. The ball was very big and Chewy had to open his mouth wide to chew on it. But he didn't know that the ball was not a toy. As Chewy chewed on the ball, it popped! Chewy was very scared and started to cough. He had swallowed a piece of the ball by accident. The piece went into his lung and Chewy couldn't breathe. He tried to bark for help, but no sound came out. Chewy's owner saw that he was in trouble and ran to help him. They tried to get the piece out, but it was too late. Chewy's lung was hurt and he couldn't breathe anymore. The charming little dog named Chewy was gone. Everyone was very sad and they all missed Chewy very much.
