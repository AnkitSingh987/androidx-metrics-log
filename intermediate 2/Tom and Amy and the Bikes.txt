Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Amy and the Bikes

Tom and Amy are friends. They like to play at the ranch. The ranch has many animals. Tom and Amy like to feed the cows, the horses and the chickens. They also like to ride the bikes on the dirt road. One day, they see a big truck on the road. The truck is loud and fast. It scares the animals. The cows moo, the horses neigh and the chickens cluck. Tom and Amy are alert. They want to help the animals. They run to the fence and wave their arms. They shout, "Stop! Stop! You are scaring the animals!" The truck driver sees them and slows down. He stops the truck and rolls down the window. He says, "Sorry, kids. I didn't mean to scare the animals. I have to deliver some hay to the barn. Can you show me the way?" Tom and Amy nod. They say, "Yes, we can. Follow us. We know the way." They hop on their bikes and lead the truck to the barn. The truck driver thanks them and gives them some candy. He says, "You are very brave and kind. You helped the animals and me. I have to return to the city now. Goodbye, kids." Tom and Amy wave goodbye. They say, "Goodbye, mister. Come back soon." They share the candy and smile. They are happy. They love the ranch.

One day, a girl named Sally went to the market with her mom. They wanted to sell some things. Sally had a big palm tree in her yard. The palm tree was very crazy. It had lots of funny leaves that danced in the wind. Sally said to her mom, "Can we sell the crazy palm tree?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can try to sell it." So, they took the crazy palm tree to the market. They put a sign on it that said, "Crazy Palm Tree for Sale!" A man came to look at the crazy palm tree. He said, "I like this crazy palm tree. How much does it cost?" Sally's mom said, "It costs five coins." The man gave Sally's mom five coins, and they sold the crazy palm tree. Sally and her mom were very happy. They had sold the crazy palm tree and made some money. They used the money to buy some new toys and yummy ice cream. They had a fun day at the market, and they went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time there was an ugly monster. The monster was scared of everything and never wanted to try anything new. He was so scared he almost never left his house. One day, the monster decided he wanted to learn how to make popcorn. He tried and tried to make popcorn but he struggled. He could not seem to succeed. But finally, the monster put his mind to it, and the popcorn started to pop! Soon enough he was enjoying a delicious bowlful of popcorn - yummy! He tried again and again, and he started to get better and better. Before long, he was a popcorn expert! He was so excited that he finally succeeded in making popcorn that he gave some of his popcorn to all his friends. They all loved it and thanked him for his delicious popcorn. The monster was so happy that he had succeeded. He wasn't scared anymore and he tried lots of new things. He had learnt that if he even if something was a little bit tricky, he could do it if he kept trying.

Once upon a time, in a mild forest, there lived a furry cat named Tom. Tom had soft fur and was very kind. He loved to play with his friends in the forest. One day, Tom met a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird said, "Help me, please! My wing hurts and I can't fly." Tom wanted to act fast to help his new friend. He looked around and found a soft leaf. He used the leaf to wrap the bird's wing gently. After some time, the bird's wing got better. The bird said, "Thank you, Tom, for being so kind and helping me." Tom was happy that he could help his friend. From that day on, all the animals in the forest knew that Tom was a good friend who would always act kindly and help others. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help those in need.
