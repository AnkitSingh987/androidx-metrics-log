Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cactus

One day, a girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a cactus. The cactus was very big and had an open spot on it. Sue was very excite to see the cactus. Sue said to the cactus, "Hi, Mr. Cactus! You are very big and have an open spot. I am excite to see you." The cactus did not talk back, but Sue felt happy. Sue went home and told her mom about the cactus. Her mom said, "That is nice, Sue. I am happy you found a big cactus." Sue smiled and went to play. She hoped to see the cactus again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a busy owl. The owl liked to play all day. He had many friends in the forest. They all liked to play together. One day, the owl and his friends were playing hide and seek. The owl was very good at hiding. He found a big tree and hid behind it. His friends looked and looked, but they could not find him. The owl was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl came into the forest. She saw the owl and said, "Hello, Mr. Owl! Will you play with me?" The owl was surprised, but he said yes. The little girl and the owl played together and had fun. The owl learned that it is good to share and make new friends. The moral of the story is to be kind and share your fun with others.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to find something fun to do. As he walked, he saw a big open box. He felt excited and curious. What could be in the box? Tim went closer to the box and called out, "Is anyone here?" A small voice from inside the box said, "Yes, I am here. My name is Cat. I am guilty. I took a cookie from the jar." Tim was surprised to hear this. Tim said, "Don't be sad, Cat. We all make mistakes. Let's go say sorry and give the cookie back." Cat and Tim went to the house and said sorry for taking the cookie. They all became friends, and no one was guilty anymore. They played together and had a happy day.

One day, a polite cat named Zip went to the store. He wanted to buy some cream for his mom. When he got to the store, he saw a big cow. The cow was also polite. They both said, "Hi!" Zip asked the cow, "Do you know where I can find cream?" The cow said, "Yes, follow me!" They walked together to find the cream. Zip was happy to have a new friend. But then, something unexpected happened. The cow said, "I can make cream for you!" Zip was surprised. The cow made cream and gave it to Zip. Zip said, "Thank you!" Then, Zip and the cow went home and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a big valley. In the valley, there was an old tree. The old tree was very wise. It knew many things because it was so old. The old tree liked to teach the little animals in the valley. One day, a small bird came to the old tree. The bird wanted to learn how to fly high. The old tree was happy to teach the bird. The bird listened and tried very hard. Soon, the bird could fly high in the sky. The bird was very happy. The old tree was proud of the bird. It liked to help the animals in the valley. The old tree knew that teaching was a good thing to do. The animals in the valley loved the old tree. They knew the old tree was wise and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. She loved to draw with her chalk. One day, she found a big, useful box of chalk in many colors. She was very happy and wanted to share it with her friends. Amy and her friends went to the park to draw on the ground. They drew a big, pretty picture of a house, a tree, and a sun. Then, something unexpected happened. It started to rain, and the rain washed away their drawings. The kids were sad to see their art go away. Amy had an idea. She asked her friends to help her carry the big box of chalk to a place where they could draw and not worry about the rain. They found a big wall under a roof and started to draw again. They learned that working together and sharing can make things even more fun. The moral of the story is to always help your friends and share your things.
