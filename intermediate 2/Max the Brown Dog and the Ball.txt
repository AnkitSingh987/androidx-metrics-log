Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Brown Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big brown dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play together in the yard. One day, Lily's mom got an amazing new thing for Max. It was a leather ball! Max and Lily were very excited to play with the new leather ball. They went out to the yard to play. Lily would throw the ball and Max would run to catch it. They played for a long time and had lots of fun. Max loved his new ball because it was strong and did not break. After playing, they went back inside the house. Lily's mom had ordered pizza for dinner. Max and Lily sat at the table and ate their yummy pizza. They talked about their fun day and how much they loved the new leather ball. They knew they would have many more fun days playing together.

Once there was a little child. The child liked to play outdoors on cold, icy days. The child's mom didn't want the child to get too cold, so she gave the child a red scarf to wear. The child loved the scarf because it made him feel snuggly and warm. One day, the child was playing in the snow when he noticed that his scarf was full of ice. The icy scarf made the child sad, so the child decided to take a nap to make the sadness go away. When the child woke up, the icy scarf was gone! In its place was a nice, new scarf. The child was so excited, he jumped up and bowed to the sky. Then, he wrapped the new scarf around his neck and smiled. The child was so thankful for the new scarf, he decided to never take it off!

Once upon a time, in a small village, there were two friends named Tom and Tim. They were very cheerful boys who loved to play together. One day, they found a big ball near the park. They both wanted to play with the ball, but they could not agree on who would play first. So, they began to quarrel. They pushed and pulled at the ball, each trying to take it for themselves. In the middle of the quarrel, Tom fell down and hurt his knee. He cried because it was painful. Seeing Tom cry, Tim felt bad. He helped Tom stand up and said, "I am sorry, Tom. We should not quarrel over a ball. Let's play together and share." Tom agreed, and they played with the ball, taking turns. From that day on, they learned to share and never quarreled again. The moral of the story is that sharing is caring, and it is better to play together than to fight.

Once upon a time, there was a big lake. The lake was very clean. Many animals liked to play in the lake. One day, a dog named Max went to the lake to bathe. Max liked to be clean too. While Max was in the lake, he saw a big fish. The fish was sad. Max asked the fish, "Why are you sad?" The fish said, "I lost my friend. Can you help me find my friend?" Max said, "Yes, I will help you find your friend." Max and the fish looked for the friend. They found a big rock in the water. The rock moved! It was not a rock, it was the fish's friend! The friend was a big turtle. The fish and the turtle were happy. Max was happy too. They all played in the clean lake together.

Once upon a time, in a pretty valley, there lived a stubborn donkey named Dolly. Dolly liked to play and run all day. She never wanted to relax, even when her friends told her to take a break. One sunny day, Dolly's friends, a cat and a dog, decided to help her learn how to relax. They took her to a soft grassy spot in the valley. The cat lay down in the sun and the dog stretched out in the shade. They told Dolly to try it too. At first, Dolly was stubborn and didn't want to relax. But when she saw her friends enjoying their rest, she decided to give it a try. She lay down in the soft grass and felt the warm sun on her back. Soon, Dolly learned to relax and enjoy the quiet moments in the beautiful valley with her friends.
