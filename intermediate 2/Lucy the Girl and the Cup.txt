Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cup

One day, a kind girl named Lucy went to the cupboard. She wanted to get a cup. But when she opened the cupboard, she found it was very cold. Lucy was surprised. "Mom, the cupboard is cold!" said Lucy. Her mom came and looked. "Oh no, it is cold! It must be magic. We need to be careful, or everything will freeze." Lucy and her mom were kind to the cupboard. They talked to it and said nice things. The cupboard liked that. One day, the cupboard said, "Thank you for being kind to me. I will not freeze your things anymore." From that day on, the cupboard was not cold anymore. Lucy and her mom were happy. They learned that being kind can make good things happen.

One day, a big snowman stood in the park. He was very impressive. He had a big hat and a long scarf. Everyone liked the snowman. They said, "Wow, he is so big and nice!" Soon, the sun came out. It was very hot. The snowman started to melt. People were sad. They did not want the snowman to go away. A little boy said, "We can save him! Let's make a plan." The little boy and his friends put ice around the snowman. They hoped this would stop him from melting. They all worked hard. The snowman said, "Thank you, friends, for helping me!" The sun went away, and it was cold again. The snowman did not melt anymore. The little boy and his friends were happy. They felt like they had a big win. They played in the park with the snowman all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little word named Crawl. Crawl was a very sorry word because he was not like the other words. While the other words could walk and run, Crawl could only crawl on the ground. One day, Crawl saw a big ball. He wanted to play with the ball, but it was too far away. So, Crawl started to crawl very fast. He crawled and crawled until he reached the ball. When Crawl got to the ball, he was very happy. He played with the ball all day long. The other words saw Crawl and they wanted to play too. Even though Crawl was a sorry word, he learned that he could still have fun and make friends. And that made Crawl a very happy word.

Once upon a time, in a humble little house, there lived a girl named Jane. Jane loved to look at photos. She had a big box of photos from her family. She liked to show her friends the photos and tell them stories. One day, Jane's friend, Tom, came over to play. Jane wanted to let Tom see her photos. They sat on the floor and looked at the photos together. They laughed and talked about the pictures. They were having so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew through the open window. The wind picked up all the photos and took them away. Jane and Tom tried to catch the photos, but they were too slow. The wind blew the photos far, far away. Jane and Tom were very sad. The photos were gone forever.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. Tom saw a big hill and wanted to climb it. He thought the view from the top would be good. So he walked and walked to get to the top of the hill. When Tom got to the top, he saw a long tree branch. Tom thought, "I can stretch and reach that branch. Then I will have an even better view!" So Tom stretched and stretched his body to reach the branch. Just as Tom was about to touch the branch, a bird flew out from the tree. The bird said, "Hello, Tom! I can take you higher for a better view!" Tom was surprised, but he said yes. The bird took Tom high in the sky, and they saw the best view ever. Tom was happy he made a new friend and saw something he never thought he would see.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a favorite color, and that color was purple. She loved purple so much that she had a purple dress, purple shoes, and even a purple hat. One day, Lily went to visit her aunt. Her aunt had a big, purple chair. Lily wanted to sit on the purple chair, but her aunt said, "No, Lily, that chair is not for sitting." But Lily did not listen. She ignored her aunt and sat on the purple chair anyway. Lily's aunt was not happy, but she did not get mad. Instead, she said, "Lily, let's go outside and play." So, Lily and her aunt went outside and played in the sun. They had so much fun that Lily forgot all about the purple chair. And from that day on, Lily always listened to her aunt.
