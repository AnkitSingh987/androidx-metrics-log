Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cow

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy some butter. Lily loved butter on her toast. She thought it tasted so good. At the store, Lily saw a healthy cow named Daisy. Daisy could talk! Daisy said, "Hello, Lily! Do you want some butter?" Lily was very surprised. She said, "Yes, please!" Daisy told Lily to come closer. Lily came closer to Daisy. Daisy gave Lily some butter. Lily said, "Thank you, Daisy!" Lily and her mom went home with the butter. They had a yummy snack with the butter on their toast. And they were happy to have met Daisy, the healthy cow.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. Sally loved candy very much. Her room was very tidy, and she put all her candy in a big box. One day, Sally went to her room and saw her candy box was open. She had a question in her mind, "Who opened my candy box?" She looked around and saw her little brother, Timmy, eating her candy. Sally was sad and asked Timmy, "Why did you eat my candy?" Timmy said, "I did not eat your candy." Then, they heard a noise from the window. They looked and saw a big bird with candy in its beak. The bird was eating Sally's candy! Sally and Timmy laughed and said, "We found the candy eater!" They closed the window and made sure the bird could not eat more candy. In the end, Sally and Timmy shared the candy and became good friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big bag. The bag was cold and had many fun things inside. Mia was very happy to find the bag. She wanted to show her friends the bag. Mia took the bag to her friend Tom. Tom saw the cold bag and said, "Wow! What a big, cold bag!" Mia and Tom played with the things inside the bag. They had a lot of fun with the cold bag. Then, Mia and Tom went to show their friend Sue. Sue saw the cold bag and said, "I like your big, cold bag!" The three friends played with the cold bag all day long. They were very happy and had a fun day with the big, cold bag.

Once upon a time, there was a red tomato named Tom. Tom lived in a big garden with many other plants. They all liked to play together. One day, a selfish bird saw Tom and wanted to eat him. The bird flew down and took Tom away. "Help!" Tom cried. His friends heard him and wanted to help. A kind bug heard Tom's cry and said, "Don't worry, I will help you return to the garden!" The kind bug flew to the selfish bird and asked, "Please, give Tom back to us. He is our friend." The bird thought for a moment and decided to be nice. The bird let Tom go, and the kind bug helped Tom return to the garden. Tom was happy to be back with his friends, and they all played together again.

Lily loved to help her mom in the kitchen. She liked to watch her mom cut the fruits and vegetables with a big knife. The knife was shiny and sharp, and Lily wanted to touch it. One day, when her mom was busy on the phone, Lily saw the knife on the counter. She climbed on a chair and reached for it. She wanted to cut a purple grape that was in a bowl. She thought it would be fun. But when she grabbed the knife, it hurt her hand. She cried out loud and dropped the knife. It made a loud noise on the floor. Her mom heard her and ran to the kitchen. She saw the blood on Lily's hand and the knife on the floor. She was scared and angry. She hugged Lily and took her to the sink. She washed her hand and put a band-aid on it. She told Lily that the knife was not a toy, and that she should never touch it. She said that knives can hurt people very badly, and that Lily was lucky that she was not hurt worse. Lily felt sorry and sad. She said sorry to her mom and hugged her back. She said that she loved her mom and that she would not touch the knife again. Her mom said that she loved her too and that she was glad that she was okay. She gave her a kiss and a purple grape. Lily smiled and ate the grape. She learned her lesson.
