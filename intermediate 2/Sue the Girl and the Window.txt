Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a smart girl who liked to look out of her window every day. She loved to see the birds, trees, and clouds outside. One day, while she was looking out the window, she saw a little boy named Tim. "Hi, Tim!" Sue said through the window. "Do you want to play today?" "Yes, Sue! I want to play!" Tim said with a big smile. So Sue put on her shoes and went outside to play with Tim. They played catch, hide and seek, and drew pictures with chalk on the ground. They had so much fun, and they looked very happy. At the end of the day, they went back to their homes and looked out their windows, waving goodbye to each other.

Lily was a spoiled girl who always wanted to have her way. She did not like to share her toys, or to listen to her mom and dad. She always threw tantrums when she did not get what she wanted. One day, Lily wanted to go to the park and play with her friends. But her mom said no, because it was raining and cold outside. Lily got very angry and screamed at her mom. She said, "I hate you, mom! You are mean and boring! I want to go to the park now!" Lily ran away from her mom and opened the door. She ran outside without her coat or shoes. She did not care that it was wet and muddy. She just wanted to have fun. But soon, Lily felt very cold and tired. She started to cough and wheeze. Her lung hurt and she could not breathe well. She realized that she had made a big mistake. She wished she had listened to her mom and stayed inside. She saw her mom running after her with a worried look on her face. Her mom hugged her and said, "Lily, I'm so sorry. I love you, sweetie. I only want you to be safe and healthy. Let's go home and get you warm and dry." Lily felt sorry for being rude and selfish. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I love you too. You are not mean and boring. You are kind and smart. I should have obeyed you and stayed inside." Lily learned her lesson that day. She learned that being spoiled and stubborn does not make her happy. She learned that being respectful and grateful makes her happy. She learned that her mom knows what is best for her. She learned that running away is not a good idea.

Lila was a little girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite one was Daisy. Daisy had long yellow hair and a pink dress. Lila liked to pretend that Daisy was a princess who lived in a castle. One morning, Lila woke up and saw that the sun was shining. She decided to take Daisy outside and play in the garden. She put on her shoes and coat and carried Daisy in her arms. She said to Daisy, "Good morning, princess. Are you ready to have fun?" Daisy nodded and smiled. Lila took her to the garden and showed her the flowers, the birds, and the butterflies. They played hide and seek, tag, and hopscotch. They had a lot of fun. After a while, Lila felt thirsty. She said to Daisy, "Let's go inside and get some water. I'm sure you're thirsty too, princess." Daisy nodded and smiled. Lila took her to the kitchen and put her on the table. She went to the sink and turned on the tap. She filled a cup with water and drank it. She was about to fill another cup for Daisy, when she heard a strange noise. She looked at the table and saw that Daisy was moving. Daisy was rising from the table and walking towards her. Lila was very surprised and scared. She said, "Daisy, what are you doing? How can you move?" Daisy smiled and said, "Don't be afraid, Lila. I'm not an ordinary doll. I'm a magic doll. I can only move and talk when you're alone with me. I'm your special friend. I wanted to thank you for playing with me and taking care of me. You're the best, Lila." Lila couldn't believe her ears. She dropped the cup and ran to Daisy. She hugged her and said, "Daisy, you're amazing. I'm so happy you're my friend. I love you, Daisy." Daisy hugged her back and said, "I love you too, Lila. You make me happy too. Can we play some more?" Lila nodded and smiled. She said, "Yes, Daisy. Let's play some more. You're the best doll ever." They went back to the garden and played until it was time for lunch. Lila and Daisy were very happy. They had a secret that no one else knew. They were magic friends.
