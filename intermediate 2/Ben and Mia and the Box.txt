Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Box

Ben and Mia were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big box in the yard. It had a bright red bow on it. They ran to the box and tried to open it. "Who is it for?" Ben asked. "I don't know. Maybe it is for us!" Mia said. They pulled and tugged at the bow, but it was too tight. They could not unwrap the box. "Let's ask Mom," Ben said. They ran inside the house and found Mom in the kitchen. She was making cookies. "Mom, Mom, there is a big box outside. Can we open it?" Mia asked. Mom smiled and said, "That box is a surprise for your grandma. She lives far away, in the east. We are going to send it to her for her birthday." Ben and Mia looked sad. They wanted to see what was in the box. "Can we help you wrap it?" Ben asked. "Of course, you can. That would be very nice of you," Mom said. She gave them some paper and tape and scissors. They went back outside and wrapped the box with Mom. They made it look pretty and put a new bow on it. "Grandma will love it," Mia said. "Yes, she will. And she will love you, too," Mom said. She hugged them and said, "You are very good helpers. And you know what? I have another surprise for you. Come inside and see." They followed Mom inside and saw a plate of cookies on the table. "Yay, cookies!" Ben and Mia said. They ate some cookies and smiled. They were happy to help Mom and to make Grandma happy. They were also happy to have cookies. It was a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a big sneeze. This sneeze was so big that it made the whole universe. In the universe, there were many stars, and one of them was very special. This star was called Independent Star. Independent Star was a happy star. It liked to dance and sing with its friends, the other stars. One day, Independent Star saw a little star far away. The little star looked sad, so Independent Star went to say hello. When Independent Star got close, the little star started to sneeze. Independent Star said, "Oh no, you have a cold!" The little star nodded. Independent Star told the little star to rest and feel better soon. The little star smiled and thanked Independent Star. The two stars became good friends, and they all lived happily in the big universe.

Tim and Lily were best friends. They liked to play with toys and make noise. One day, they found a big machine in the garage. It had buttons and levers and wheels. They wanted to see what it did. "Let's try it!" said Tim. He pushed a button and the machine made a loud sound. "Wow!" said Lily. She pulled a lever and the machine moved a big arm. "Cool!" said Tim. He turned a wheel and the machine opened a big mouth. They saw a teddy bear on the floor. They wanted to put it in the machine's mouth. "Maybe it will make it bigger or softer or funnier!" said Lily. She picked up the teddy bear and threw it in the machine's mouth. Tim closed the mouth with the wheel. The machine made a louder sound and shook a lot. Tim and Lily were scared. They ran away from the machine. They heard a pop and a rip. They looked back and saw the teddy bear come out of the machine. It was not bigger or softer or funnier. It was smaller and harder and sadder. The machine had squeezed it too much and torn it apart. Tim and Lily felt bad. They loved the teddy bear. It was their favorite toy. They wished they had not put it in the machine. They hugged the teddy bear and said sorry. They learned a lesson. They should not play with things they do not understand. They should be careful and gentle with their toys. They should be happy with what they have. They should not try to change what is normal. Normal is good.
