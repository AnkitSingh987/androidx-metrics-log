Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Gift

One day, a little girl named Lily felt nervous. It was her friend Tom's birthday party. She had a gift for him and hoped he would like it. At the party, Lily saw Tom and said, "Happy birthday, Tom! I have a gift for you." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily!" He tried to resist opening the gift right away, but he was too excited. When Tom opened the gift, he saw a big, soft ball. He was very happy. He said, "I love it, Lily! Let's play with the ball." They played and had fun at the party. Lily was happy that Tom liked her gift.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy cars and color with his crayons. One day, he saw a candle on the table. It was pretty and shiny. Tim wanted to know what it was. Tim asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom replied, "That is a candle, Tim. We use it to make light when it is dark." Tim thought that was very cool. He wanted to see the candle make light. That night, Tim's mom lit the candle. The room was filled with a warm, soft light. Tim was very happy to see the candle work. He replied, "Thank you, Mom, for showing me the candle." They sat together and enjoyed the candle's light until it was time for bed.

Mommy and Daddy took little Timmy to the store to buy some modern supplies. Timmy was so excited because they never went to the store anymore. When they got to the store, Daddy said, "Let's get some drain supplies. We have to make sure the drain is working correctly." So, Mommy and Daddy walked around the store and found the modern supplies they needed. When they got to the register to pay, Timmy noticed the cashier smiling. Daddy asked her, "Why are you so happy today?" The cashier replied, "I'm smiling because I love helping people get the things they need. Without supplies, things can't work properly. I'm glad to help make things better." Mommy and Daddy took Timmy back home, and Timmy understood the cashier's message. He realized how important it is to make sure you have everything you need to work properly. The moral of the story is: always make sure you have the right supplies, so you can make sure everything works as it should.

Sara and Ben are playing in the yard. They see some dry leaves and sticks. They gather them in a pile. They want to make a fire. "Can we make a fire, Mom?" Sara asks. Mom comes out of the house. She sees the pile of leaves and sticks. She shakes her head. "No, fire is not safe for you. Fire can burn you and the house. Fire is only for grown-ups," she says. Sara and Ben are sad. They like fire. Fire is bright and warm. Fire makes sparks. Sparks are like little stars. "Can we make something else with the leaves and sticks?" Ben asks. Mom thinks for a moment. She smiles. "Yes, you can make something else. You can make a fancy hat. A fancy hat is fun and pretty. You can use the leaves and sticks to decorate it. You can also use some flowers and ribbons. Do you want to make a fancy hat?" she asks. Sara and Ben nod. They like fancy hats. Fancy hats are colorful and silly. They can wear them and pretend to be kings and queens. Mom helps them find some old hats and some glue. They use the leaves and sticks and flowers and ribbons to make their fancy hats. They put them on and look at each other. "You look fancy, Sara," Ben says. "You look fancy, too, Ben," Sara says. They laugh and hug. They thank Mom for the idea. They run around the yard with their fancy hats. They forget about the fire. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a big, green park, there was an adorable little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his red ball. He would run and jump and catch the ball all day long. One day, Max was playing with his red ball when he felt an itch on his back. He tried to scratch it, but he could not reach it. Max rolled on the grass to try to make the itch go away, but it did not work. Max saw a big tree and had an idea. He ran to the tree and rubbed his back against it. The tree helped Max scratch the itch, and Max was so happy. But while Max was scratching his itch, he did not see his red ball roll away. When Max looked for his ball, he could not find it. Max missed playing with his red ball. Just then, a kind little girl found Max's red ball and gave it back to him. Max wagged his tail, and they played together in the big, green park.
