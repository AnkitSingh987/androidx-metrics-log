Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside and touch everything she saw. One day, she found a big mystery box in her backyard. It was different from the other boxes she had seen before. Lucy was very excited to find out what was inside. She touched the mystery box, and it opened. Inside, there were many toys and games. Lucy was so happy to see all the different toys. She played with the toys all day long, touching each one to see how they felt. She found a soft teddy bear, a bouncy ball, and a puzzle with big pieces. At the end of the day, Lucy put all the toys back in the mystery box. She knew that tomorrow she could touch and play with them again. And every day after that, Lucy would find new and different toys in the mystery box. It was her favorite thing to do, and she loved the surprise of finding new toys to touch and play with.

One day, a big fish and a small fish were swimming near a reef. They saw a dirty boat arrive. The big fish said, "Look, a dirty boat is here. Let's go see what it is doing." The small fish was scared. He said, "No, I don't want to. I am scared of dirty boats. They make the water not nice." But the big fish wanted to see, so he went to the dirty boat. The big fish saw people on the boat. They were trying to clean the boat. The big fish was happy. He went back to the small fish and said, "Don't be scared. The people are cleaning the boat. They want to make the water nice too." The small fish was happy to hear this. They both swam near the boat and watched the people clean it. Soon, the boat was not dirty anymore. The big fish and the small fish were very happy, and they played near the reef all day.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they see a large teddy bear in the shop. They want the teddy bear very much. "How much is the teddy bear?" Tom asks the shop lady. "The teddy bear is ten coins," the shop lady says. "It is a good price for a large teddy bear." Tom and Lily look at their coins. They only have five coins each. They do not have enough coins for the teddy bear. "What can we do?" Lily asks Tom. "Maybe we can solve this problem," Tom says. "Maybe we can share the teddy bear. We can take turns to play with it. We can be fair and kind." Lily thinks for a moment. She likes Tom's idea. She nods and smiles. "OK, let's do that," she says. "Let's buy the teddy bear together. We can be happy and have fun." Tom and Lily give their coins to the shop lady. The shop lady gives them the teddy bear. Tom and Lily hug the teddy bear and each other. They are happy. They have solved their problem. They have a new toy and a good friend.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy pirate named Bob. He had a big hat and a loud laugh. Bob liked to play with his friends on their big ship. They would sail the sea, looking for fun and adventure. They hoped to find treasure one day. One day, while sailing, they saw a small island. They hoped to find treasure there. They got off the ship and walked on the island. They heard a strange sound. It was a big, noisy bird! The bird was stuck in a tree. Bob and his friends helped the bird get out of the tree. The bird was very happy. It said, "Thank you for helping me!" The pirate and his friends were very surprised. They did not know that birds could talk! The bird told them that it was a magic bird. As a thank you gift, the bird showed them where to find the treasure. The pirates were very happy and shared the treasure with the bird. They all became best friends and had many more adventures together.
