Category: Intermediate
Title: The Loud Raven and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a loud raven who lived in a big tree. The raven liked to talk a lot. He would ask many questions to his friends in the forest. His friends sometimes got tired of his questions, but they knew he was just curious. One day, the raven saw a small, shiny thing on the ground. He picked it up with his beak and showed it to his friends. "What is this?" he questioned. His friends did not know, but they thought it was pretty. They all wanted to keep it, but the raven said, "I found it, so I will keep it safe." The next day, a girl came to the forest looking very sad. She had lost her shiny necklace. The raven saw her and questioned, "Are you looking for this?" He showed her the shiny thing he had found. The girl was so happy and thanked the raven. She gave him a big hug and went home. The raven's friends were proud of him for helping the girl. They knew that his questions could be helpful sometimes. The loud raven learned that asking questions can be a good thing, and he continued to ask questions and learn new things every day.

Lily loves to play with her toy cars. She has many cars of different colors and sizes. She likes to make them zoom and beep on the floor. Sometimes she pretends they are racing or going on a trip. One day, Lily's mom has a surprise for her. She tells Lily to close her eyes and follow her outside. Lily is curious and excited. She wonders what the surprise is. She holds her mom's hand and walks to the driveway. When they get there, mom tells Lily to open her eyes. Lily gasps. She sees a big car with a bow on it. It is shiny and red. It is a new car for mom and dad. Mom says, "This is our new car, Lily. Do you like it?" Lily nods. She thinks the car is very pretty. She wants to touch it and look inside. Mom says, "You can, but be careful. It is very new and clean." Lily runs to the car and opens the door. She sees the seats and the buttons and the wheel. She smiles. She says, "Wow, mom, this car is amazing. Can I ride in it?" Mom laughs. She says, "Of course, Lily. You can ride in it with us. We are going to the park today. Do you want to go?" Lily claps. She says, "Yes, mom, I want to go. I love the park. And I love the new car. Thank you, mom. You are the best." Mom hugs Lily. She says, "You are welcome, Lily. You are the best, too. Now, let's go and have some fun." They get in the car and drive away. Lily is happy. She thinks this is the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little girl named Lily. She loved to draw and paint. One day, Lily saw a tiny trail on the ground. She wanted to see where it went. So, she decided to follow it. As Lily followed the trail, she saw a funny little bug. The bug had a big thumb for a head! Lily thought it was very strange. She had never seen a bug like this before. The thumb-bug looked at Lily and started to follow her too. Lily and the thumb-bug walked and walked, and then they found a big, colorful wall. The wall was full of drawings and paintings. Lily was surprised! She never knew this wall was here. The thumb-bug smiled and pointed at the wall with its tiny leg. Lily knew what to do. She took out her paint and started to paint with the thumb-bug. Together, they made the wall even more beautiful.

Mommy and Daddy took their little girl to the park. She was so excited to go and play. The little girl and her mommy and daddy went to the playground. She saw a big, round and colourful swing. It looked so cute. Mommy asked her: "Do you want to go on a swing?" The little girl smiled and said "Yes!" Daddy lifted her up and put her in the swing. "Swing, swing, swing," her mommy said as she pushed her daughter. The little girl felt like she was flying! She held tight and round and round she went. Finally, she said goodbye to the swing. "That was so much fun," she said. Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, it was. Let's go do something else now." The little girl was so happy!
