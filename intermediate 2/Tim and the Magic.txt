Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Magic

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a big factory with his dad. The factory made toys for kids. Tim was very happy to see all the toys. But when they got there, Tim saw a troubled man. The man looked sad. Tim asked the man, "Why are you sad?" The man said, "I lost my special toy. It was a magic toy that could appear and disappear." Tim wanted to help the man find his toy. Tim and the man looked all around the factory. They looked under tables and behind boxes. Then, Tim saw the magic toy appear on a shelf. Tim and the man were very happy. The man was not troubled anymore, and they both had a great day at the factory.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toys and wear her favorite dress with a big button on it. Every day, she would run and play in the sun with her friends. One day, Lucy was very tired from all the playing. She decided to rest under a big tree in her yard. As she lay down, she looked up at the sky and saw the clouds moving slowly. It was so peaceful and quiet that she started to feel sleepy. While Lucy was resting, her mom called her inside the house. She got up, feeling much better after her rest, and went inside. Her mom saw the big button on her dress and said, "What a pretty button, Lucy!" Lucy smiled and hugged her mom, happy to have a tidy home and a loving family.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sara. She had a toy box with a lock on it. One day, the lock was broken. Sara was sad. She wanted to fix the broken lock. Sara's mom saw her sad face and asked, "Sara, why are you sad?" Sara pointed at the broken lock and said, "My toy box is broken. I want to lock it." Her mom smiled and said, "Let's fix it together." Sara and her mom worked together to fix the broken lock. They used a new lock and put it on the toy box. Sara was so happy. She gave her mom a big hug and said, "Thank you, Mommy! Now my toys are safe." Sara's mom hugged her back and said, "You're welcome, my little love." From that day on, Sara always locked her toy box and kept her toys safe.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the zoo with her mom. Mia loved to see all the animals, but her favorite was the big tiger named Tim. Tim had soft fur and big eyes. Mia liked to watch Tim play and run in his cage. Mia's mom said, "Let's make a yummy treat for Tim the tiger." Mia was very happy and they went home to make the treat. They took a big bowl and mixed some meat, water, and cat treats. Mia's mom shaped it into a big ball and put it in the fridge to get cold. The next day, Mia and her mom went back to the zoo with the yummy treat for Tim the tiger. They gave it to the zookeeper, who said, "Thank you! Tim will love this." The zookeeper put the treat in Tim's cage. Tim sniffed it, took a big bite, and smiled. Mia was so happy to see Tim enjoy the yummy treat she helped make.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day. A boy named Tom wanted to play hockey. He put on his warm coat and went outside. Tom saw his friend Sue. "Let's play hockey!" Tom said. Sue smiled and said, "Yes! I love hockey!" They played hockey on the ice. Tom and Sue had to speed to hit the puck. They laughed and had fun. They were very fast. "You are good at hockey!" Tom told Sue. Sue said, "You are good too!" After a while, it was time to go home. Tom and Sue were tired, but they were happy. They said, "We will play hockey again soon!" They went to their homes to rest. They had a great day playing hockey in the cold.
