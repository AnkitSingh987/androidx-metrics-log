Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gloomy Room and the Tim

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gloomy room. In this room, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because there was a hole in his favorite toy. He wanted to fix it. One day, a tiny mosquito came into the room. The mosquito saw Tim and wanted to help. The mosquito had an idea to fix the toy. It flew around the room, looking for something to help with the hole. The mosquito found some thread and a needle. It brought them to Tim. Tim used the thread and needle to fix his toy. The toy was now as good as new! Tim was very happy, and the mosquito was happy too. They became good friends, and the room was not gloomy anymore.

One day, a bossy cat named Tom went to the lake. He saw a big boat. Tom had a plan. He wanted to zip on the boat and have fun. Tom met a nice dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, let's zip on the boat!" Max was scared, but Tom was bossy. So, Max said, "Okay, Tom. Let's go." They zipped on the boat. The boat went fast. Max was happy now. They saw a big fish jump. Tom and Max laughed. They had a fun day on the boat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, hard helmet. He loved to wear it when he rode his bike. But one day, he did not want to wear it. He said "no" to his mom. Tim refused to put on the helmet. Tim rode his bike fast down the street. He saw a big hill and wanted to go down it. As he went down the hill, something unexpected happened. A big, round ball came out of nowhere! It hit Tim's bike and made him fall. But guess what? Tim was not hurt! The hard helmet that he refused to wear was in his bike basket. When he fell, the helmet fell on his head and saved him. Tim was so happy and knew that he should always wear his helmet. From that day on, he never refused to wear it again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big festival. The sun was warm and everyone was happy. People were laughing, dancing, and eating yummy food. The children played games and won fun prizes. A little boy named Tim saw a big, red balloon. He wanted it so much. He asked his mom, "Can I have the red balloon, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but I must warn you, hold it tight or it will fly away." Tim held the balloon tight and played with his friends. They had so much fun at the festival. They ate ice cream and played on the swings. Tim was careful not to let go of his balloon. But then, Tim saw a cute dog. He wanted to pet the dog, so he let go of the balloon for just a second. The warm wind blew the balloon away, and it flew high up in the sky. Tim was sad, but he knew his mom had warned him. Next time, Tim promised to listen better and hold on tight to his balloon. And even though he lost his balloon, he still had a great time at the festival with his friends and family.

Once there was a girl called Sue. She had a bright brother. He was nice to her, even though they were different ages. One day Sue's brother went away. She missed him very much. She wished he would come back. Every day Sue saw other children playing with their brothers and sisters. She was sad because she didn't get to do that with her own brother. One day Sue decided enough was enough. She set off to find her brother. She searched high and low but could not find him. Sue never saw her brother again. She missed him every day, but she never found him. She was sad and lonely without her bright brother.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree with tasty oranges. Tim wanted to pick one, but he was too short. He tried to jump, but he could not reach the orange. Tim was sad. Tim's friend, Sam, came by and saw him. "Why are you sad, Tim?" Sam asked. Tim said, "I want an orange, but I can't reach it." Sam thought for a moment and said, "Let's find a way to get the orange!" They looked around and found a big stick. Tim tried to use the stick to reach the orange. He poked at the orange, but it did not fall. Suddenly, Tim stumbled on a rock and the stick hit the orange really hard. The orange fell down! Tim and Sam were so happy. They shared the tasty orange and laughed. They knew that by working together, they could do anything.
