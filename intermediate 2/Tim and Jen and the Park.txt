Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Jen and the Park

Tim and Jen were friends who liked to play with round things. They had balls, balloons, wheels and apples. One day, they found a big round thing in the park. It was shiny and hard and had a hole in the middle. "Wow, what is this?" Tim asked. "I don't know, but it looks fun. Let's play with it," Jen said. They rolled it, threw it, spun it and wore it like a hat. They had a lot of fun with the round thing. But then they started to argue. "I want to have it. It's mine," Tim said. "No, it's mine. I saw it first," Jen said. They pulled and tugged at the round thing. They were not humble. They were greedy and mean. They did not want to share. Then, something bad happened. The round thing broke. It split in two. The shiny pieces fell to the ground. Tim and Jen were shocked and sad. "Look what we did. We broke the round thing. Now we can't play with it anymore," Tim said. "I'm sorry, Tim. I was wrong. We should have shared," Jen said. "I'm sorry, too, Jen. I was wrong, too. We should have been humble," Tim said. They hugged and said sorry. They learned a lesson. They picked up the pieces and gave them to a man who was working in the park. He said he could fix the round thing. It was a bell. He hung it on a tree. It made a nice sound. Tim and Jen smiled. They were happy again. They played with other round things. They shared and were humble. They were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a healthy boy who loved to play outside. One day, he went to the park to observe the birds and the squirrels. He saw a squirrel eating a nut and a bird flying in the sky. While he was at the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was also a healthy girl who liked to play outside. They talked and played together. Tim said, "I like to watch the birds and squirrels!" Sue replied, "Me too! Let's be friends!" One day, Tim and Sue found a small pill on the ground. They knew it was not good to eat things they found on the ground. They showed the pill to their moms. Their moms were happy that they did not eat it. Tim and Sue learned to always be careful and to stay healthy. They continued to play outside, observe the animals, and be the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a good cat named Tim. Tim had a leather ball that he loved to play with. One day, his friend, a dog named Max, came over to play. "Can I play with your leather ball, Tim?" Max asked. Tim said, "Yes, but we must not fight over it. We can play together and have fun." So, Tim and Max played with the leather ball all day. They did not fight, and they were very happy. In the end, they learned that it was good to share and play together. And they became best friends forever.

Lucy was very impatient. She wanted her special envelope to be filled. Lucy asked her mom to help. "Mommy, can you fill the envelope for me please?" asked Lucy. "Sure thing," said her mom. She grabbed the envelope and started filling it. But Lucy was very impatient. She wanted the envelope to be filled now. She fidgeted and sighed. "Hurry up, mommy!" Lucy said. Her mom was working very hard, but Lucy thought she was taking too long. Suddenly, the envelope tore apart. All of her mom's hard work was ruined. "Oh, no!" said Lucy. She wanted to cry, but all she could do was stare in awe. The envelope was ruined, and the special thing that was going to be inside had been destroyed. Lucy was very sad.

Sally was 3-years-old and loved to make things. One day she was having fun in her room when she got a wonderful idea. She decided to make an object out of paper and glue. She was feeling so proud of herself that she wanted to mail it to her grandparents. She put it in an envelope, addressed it to Grandma and Grandpa, and put it in the mailbox. She was very excited to show them her creation. The week after she mailed it, Sally was excited for her grandparents to write back to her. But when the letter came, it was smelly. Grandma had opened the envelope and found the object inside was dripping wet and growing mold. Sally was so upset that she started to cry. Despite her best intentions, the object was ruined and it couldn't be mailed. Grandma had to tell Sally to try again the next time.
