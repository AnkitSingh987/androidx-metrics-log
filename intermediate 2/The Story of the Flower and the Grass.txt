Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Flower and the Grass

Once upon a time there were two young friends. One day, they had an idea. They wanted to explore the meadow, but their mom said no. The friends weren't happy about this, so they decided to give their mom a challenge. "If we can find something special in the meadow," they said, "will you let us go?" Their mom thought about this and said yes. "But," she said, "you need to promise to stay together and come right back." The friends agreed. So, they set off into the meadow. They searched around and talked to some of the animals they found. Then, suddenly, they spotted something shiny in the grass. It was a golden flower! They rushed back to their mom and showed her the flower. She was so happy! As a reward, she said that they could go exploring in the meadow once a week as long as they stayed together. The friends were very pleased! Throughout their adventures in the meadow, they often thought back to the special day where their demand had been rewarded.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a walk. She heard a song, but she did not know the song. It was an unknown song. Sue wanted to find out who was singing the song. Sue walked and walked until she found a big tree. A bird sat on a branch and sang the unknown song. Sue asked the bird, "Do you know this song?" The bird said, "Yes, I do. I made this song." Sue was happy to find the bird. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bird away. Sue was sad because she could not hear the unknown song anymore. The bird was gone, and the song was gone too.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim lived near a big lake. Tim loved to play by the lake when the sun was warm. He liked to throw stones into the water and watch the fish jump. One day, Tim saw a frog near the water. The frog looked sad, and Tim wanted to help him. "Why are you sad, frog?" Tim asked. The frog just gave a little shrug. "I can't wake my friend up. He is sleeping, and I need him to play with me," said the frog. Tim thought for a moment. "I can help you wake your friend up!" he said. So, Tim and the frog went to the friend's home. Tim clapped his hands and shouted, "Wake up, wake up!" The friend woke up and was happy to play with the frog and Tim. The frog was no longer sad, and they all played by the warm lake together.

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat. The cat had no name. It liked to play and run all day. One day, the black cat saw a big tree. The cat gazed at the tree and wanted to climb it. So, the cat jumped up and up, and it went very high. At the top of the tree, the cat met a bird. The bird said, "Hello, what is your name?" The cat said, "I have no name." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I will give you a name. Your name is now 'Blacky'." The cat liked the name and said, "Thank you, bird." From that day on, the black cat was happy and had a name.

Once there was a brilliant seal called Steve. Steve lived in an underwater cave near the beach. He liked to swim around and explore the ocean around him. One day Steve wanted to swim further than ever before. But as he swam further out, he got distracted and suddenly realized that he was lost. Steve was scared and glided through the deep dark waters. Suddenly, Steve saw a light up ahead. As he got closer, he noticed an old fisherman in a small wooden boat. "Help me please!" cried Steve. He swam up to the boat and explained how he got lost. The old fisherman understood Steve's plight and offered to help him. He said, "Don't worry. I have a clever plan. I'm going to lower a rope into the water. You will have to swim, grasp the rope and suffer me as I pull you in. Can you do that?" Steve nodded his head and leaped towards the rope. With a few powerful strokes, he grabbed the rope and the old fisherman pulled him back in the boat. Steve was relieved and thanked the old fisherman for rescuing him. The old man nodded and said, "You are a brilliant seal, Steve. I'm glad I was able to help you."
