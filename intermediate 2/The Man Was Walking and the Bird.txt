Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man Was Walking and the Bird

One day, a man was walking in the park. He saw a big tree with a small bird on it. The man was ready to change his life. He wanted to be like the bird, free and happy. The man said to the bird, "I want to change. I want to be like you, free and happy." The bird looked at the man and said, "If you want to change, you must be ready to do something new." The man was ready. He climbed the tree to be closer to the bird. But as he got higher, the tree started to shake. The man was scared, but he kept going. When he reached the top, the bird was gone. The man was sad and wanted to go back down. As he tried to climb down, the tree shook even more. The man fell and hit the ground. He was hurt and could not move. The bird came back and looked at the man. The bird said, "You were not ready to change. You should have stayed on the ground." The man learned that he should not try to change too fast. Sometimes, it is better to be happy with who you are.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his hose in the yard. Every day, he would run and jump, and have lots of fun. One day, Spot saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he had no one to play with. Spot wanted to help Tim, so he went over to him with the hose. Spot said, "Hi, I am Spot. Let's play with my hose!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I want to play!" They played with the hose all day. They laughed and got wet. They had so much fun together. From that day on, Spot and Tim were best friends. They played with the hose every day. They were always happy and never sad again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was standing on the beach with her mom. They were both looking out to the sea. The little girl wanted to paint the beach. So the mom said, "Let's paint the beach!" The little girl smiled. She grabbed her paints and a brush and started to paint the beach. She was making lots of different shapes and colors. Then a big wave came up the beach and splashed onto the painting the little girl had created. Her mom said, "That's ok. We can just paint over the wave". The little girl agreed and she started to paint again. She thought carefully about the shapes and colors she wanted to make. This time, she wanted to make something that was flexible. The little girl was happy with her painting. She and her mom looked at the flexible shapes and colors. Her mom said, "You did a great job painting the beach". The little girl smiled.

Once upon a time there was a silly girl named Max. Max was always getting into trouble and playing games. Today, Max wanted to go explore the nearby forest. She skipped through the forest and heard lots of noisy animals. But then, she stumbled and went sprawling across the ground. Max brushed the dirt off her clothes, but something else caught her eye. She saw a big hole in the ground. Max wowdered what was inside, so she decided to look. When Max got down in the hole she saw a big chest, with a golden key next to it. With some difficulty, Max managed to open the chest and saw so many treasures inside. Max felt so happy and hugged the chest, but then she heard someone coming. Someone was shouting her name, it was Max's dad. He was searching for her and was looking everywhere. Max tried to hide the chest and the key, but she was too late. Her dad was too strong and he was mad at her for getting in the way of all of the noisy animals. He scolded her and took all of the treasures away. Max was sad, but she knew it was all her fault. She had stumbled, and now she had to pay the price.
