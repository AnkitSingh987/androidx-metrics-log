Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Castle

Lily and Ben are playing in the sand. They have a big bucket and a small bucket. They fill the big bucket with sand and turn it over. They make a sand castle. "Look at our castle!" Lily says. "It is so big and pretty!" Ben nods. He sees a crab crawling near the castle. He wants to catch it. He runs after the crab with the small bucket. "Ben, be careful!" Lily shouts. "The crab might pinch you!" Ben does not listen. He puts the small bucket over the crab. He lifts it up and looks inside. He sees the crab moving its claws. "Wow, I caught a crab!" Ben says. "It is so red and funny!" He shows the crab to Lily. Lily is scared. She does not like crabs. "Ben, put it back!" Lily says. "The crab does not like being in the bucket. It wants to go home!" Ben thinks for a moment. He is hungry. He remembers that his mom sometimes cooks crabs for dinner. He thinks that the crab might be tasty. "Or maybe we can eat it!" Ben says. "It might be healthy!" Lily gasps. She does not want to eat the crab. She thinks that the crab is alive and has feelings. "Ben, no!" Lily says. "We cannot eat the crab. It is our friend. We have to let it go!" Ben looks at Lily. He sees that she is sad and serious. He feels sorry for the crab. He decides to listen to Lily. "Okay, okay," Ben says. "We will not eat the crab. We will let it go." He puts the small bucket back on the sand. He lifts it up and lets the crab crawl out. The crab scurries away to the water. "Bye, crab!" Lily and Ben say. "We are sorry for bothering you!" They hug each other and smile. They are happy that they did the right thing. They go back to their sand castle and play some more.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a chubby cat named Tom. He loved to walk and play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. One day, they found a big board in the backyard. "Let's walk on the board!" said Lily. Tom meowed happily and agreed. They took turns walking on the board, laughing and having fun. But suddenly, the board started to move! "Wow!" said Lily, surprised. "The board can walk too!" Tom and Lily looked at each other and giggled. They decided to keep the walking board as their new friend. From that day on, Tom, Lily, and the walking board had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to stare at things. One day, Fluffy saw a big bowl of cream on the table. Fluffy wanted the cream very much. But Fluffy was too small and weak to jump up on the table. Fluffy had a friend named Tiny, the mouse. Tiny was very smart. Tiny told Fluffy to wait and watch. Tiny knew Fluffy loved to stare. So, Fluffy stared and waited. Tiny ran off and came back with a big spoon. Tiny used the spoon to push the cream off the table. The cream fell down, and Fluffy was so happy. Fluffy licked the cream and shared it with Tiny. They were both happy and full. Fluffy learned that staring and waiting can sometimes lead to good things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a tight hole in the ground. He wondered what was inside the hole. Tim found a stick and started to poke the hole. He poked and poked, but nothing happened. Then, all of a sudden, a little mouse popped out of the hole! Tim was very surprised. The mouse looked at Tim and said, "Thank you for helping me get out of the tight hole!" Tim could not believe that the mouse could talk. They became friends and played together all day.

One day, a thoughtful cat named Missy went for a walk. She liked to listen with her big ears. Missy heard a small sound. She looked around and saw a little bird on the ground. The bird was sad because it could not fly. Missy wanted to help the bird. She thought of a plan. She found a big leaf and put the bird on it. Then, Missy pushed the leaf with her nose. The bird went fast on the leaf and started to smile. Just then, something unexpected happened. The bird jumped up and started to fly! It turns out, the bird was just scared to fly before. Missy was happy that she could help her new friend. They played together all day and became best friends.
