Category: Intermediate
Title: The Giant and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a giant. The giant was very slow. One day, he went for a walk. He saw a big rock. The giant wanted to lift the rock. The giant tried to lift the rock, but it was too heavy. A little boy saw the giant. The boy said, "Let me help you lift the rock." The giant and the boy tried to lift the rock together. But the rock was still too heavy. They tried and tried, but they could not lift it. The giant became very sad. The boy said, "I am sorry, we cannot lift the rock." The giant went home, still feeling sad.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful town. In this town, there lived a kind girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toy horn. She would blow her horn every day and make happy sounds. One sunny day, Lily went to the park with her horn. She saw her friends and they all played together. They blew their horns and laughed. The birds in the trees sang along with the happy horn sounds. As the sun went down, Lily and her friends went home. They waved goodbye and said, "I love playing with you!" Lily felt happy and peaceful. She knew she would play with her horn and her friends again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp. The shrimp lived in the deep, dark sea. The shrimp had many friends, like fish and crabs. One day, the shrimp heard about a big party. All the sea animals were going. The shrimp was excited and wanted to go too. But the party was far away, and the sea was very dark. The shrimp decided to be brave. He swam through the dark sea. He saw many new things. He met new friends on his way to the party. When the shrimp got to the party, everyone was happy to see him. They danced and played all night. The shrimp was so glad he decided to be brave and go to the party. And from that day on, the shrimp was never scared of the dark sea again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a soft cat named Ash. Ash loved to play and get his toys. One day, Ash saw something new in the house. It was a big, red ball. Ash was very happy to see the ball. Ash played with the big red ball all day. He would get it and push it around the house. He knew that something fun was going to happen with the ball. Ash's owner, a little girl named Lily, was watching him play. She had a big smile on her face. The next day, Lily said, "Ash, today is a special day!" She took Ash and the big red ball outside. There were many other cats and their owners there too. It was a cat ball game! Ash was so excited. He played with the other cats and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Ash and Lily went home, happy and tired. And Ash knew that the big red ball was the best toy ever.

One day, a smart hippo named Bob was playing in the water. He saw a little bird. The bird was sad. "Can I play with you?" the bird asked. Bob said, "No, I refuse. You are too small." The bird flew away. Bob felt bad. He wanted to say sorry. He looked for the bird. Bob found the bird in a tree. The bird was happy now. It was with a big group of friends. Bob asked, "Can I play with you now?" The bird said, "Yes, you can." They all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate lady who loved to cook. She had a big frame on her kitchen wall. The frame had many pictures of her family and friends. She liked to look at the frame while she cooked. One day, the lady decided to cook a big meal for her family. She cooked yummy soup, tasty chicken, and sweet cake. She worked very hard in the kitchen. She was happy because she knew her family would love the food. When the meal was ready, her family came to eat. They all sat around the table and enjoyed the food. They talked and laughed together. The compassionate lady looked at the frame on the wall and smiled. She was happy to cook for the people she loved.
