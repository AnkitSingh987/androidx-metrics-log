Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cart

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a big, colorful cart. Tim wanted to push the cart and help his mom. But his mom said, "No, Tim. You are too small to push the cart." Tim felt sad, but he did not give up. He thought, "I will show mom that I can push the cart." So, he waited and watched his mom. When she was not looking, Tim ran to the cart and started to push it. The cart was very big and heavy. Tim pushed and pushed, but the cart did not move. Then, he tried to pull the cart. It moved a little bit, but it was very hard. Tim's face turned red, and he started to cry. His mom heard him cry and came to help. She said, "Tim, you are a brave boy for trying to push the cart. But you are still too small. Let's find a small cart for you to push." Tim smiled and felt happy again. They found a small, colorful cart just for Tim. He pushed it all around the store and helped his mom.

One day, a big and honest dog named Max was walking in the park. He took big steps as he walked. Max saw his friend, a small cat named Lily. Lily was scared because she heard there might be an earthquake soon. "Max, I am scared of the earthquake," said Lily. Max wanted to help his friend. "Do not worry, Lily. I will be with you when it happens," Max said. They walked together, step by step, feeling safer with each other. As they walked, the ground started to shake. It was the earthquake! Max and Lily held each other tight. They were scared, but they were brave together. After the earthquake stopped, Max and Lily were happy that they had each other. They knew that they could face anything as long as they were friends.

The sun was shining one winter day. Little Bunny was hopping around the garden looking for something to chew. Suddenly, he saw something shiny and new. He hopped a little closer and saw it was a shiny new carrot. Little Bunny started to chew the carrot, but it was hard! The more he chewed, the harder it got. He tried harder and harder but it wouldn’t get any softer. Little Bunny was frustrated and started hopping around in a circle. Then he had an idea. He hopped over to the snow pile and started to dig. He made a big dent in the snow and at the bottom, he found a old and mushy carrot! He quickly grabbed it and started to chew. It was much softer and Little Bunny was happy. He nibbled it until it was all gone then happily hopped away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sam. They played together every day. One day, they found an ashtray in Tim's house. They didn't know what it was, but they thought it was a toy. Tim said, "Let's play catch with it!" Sam thought it was a great idea. So, they started to throw the ashtray to each other. But then, something terrible happened. The ashtray broke into pieces when it hit the ground. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the broken ashtray. She was very sad. Tim and Sam felt bad. Tim's mom said, "This ashtray was a special gift. You should not play with things that are not toys." Tim and Sam learned a lesson that day. They said sorry to Tim's mom and helped her clean up the mess. From that day on, they only played with their real toys. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to skip and play in the park. One day, Lily saw a small flame near the icy pond. She was scared, but she knew she had to do something. Lily called out to her friend, Tim, who was playing nearby. "Tim, come help me! There's a flame by the icy pond!" Tim ran to Lily and saw the flame too. They knew they had to put it out to keep everyone safe. Together, Lily and Tim used their water bottles to put out the flame. They were happy they could help. Their moms saw what they did and said, "Good job, Lily and Tim! You were brave and helped others." Lily and Tim learned that if they work together, they can solve any problem.
