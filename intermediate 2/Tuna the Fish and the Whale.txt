Category: Intermediate
Title: Tuna the Fish and the Whale

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little fish named Tuna. Tuna was a very comfortable fish. She liked to swim slowly and relax in the warm water. One day, Tuna met a new friend, a big fish named Whale. Whale was very kind and they played all day. They swam fast, they swam slow, and they even swam in circles. Tuna was very happy to have a new friend to play with. After a long day of playing, Tuna and Whale were very tired. They found a nice, soft place to lay down and relax. They talked and laughed until they fell asleep. Tuna was very comfortable and happy with her new friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a normal boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, he found a notebook in his room. It was a very special notebook. Tim used the notebook to draw pictures. He drew a chair and a seat. Then, he sat on the seat. It was very fun. Tim had an idea. He drew a big cake and shared it with his friends. His friends were happy. They all sat on seats and ate the cake. It was a good day. The next day, Tim drew a big toy. But the toy was not nice. It took all the seats and cakes. Tim was sad. He learned that not everything he drew was good. He drew a big eraser and erased the bad toy. Tim and his friends were happy again. They sat on the seats and ate more cake. Tim learned to be careful with his drawings and to share with his friends. The moral of the story is to be careful with what you create and always share with others.

Once upon a time there was a fat dish. It was very round and happy. One day, a little girl wanted to play with the dish. She got a piece of string and decided to tie it around the dish. The dish was so excited - it had never been tied up before! The little girl held the dish and twirled in circles, making the string fly and wrap around it. When the dish was all tied up, the little girl jumped for joy. She and the dish danced around holding hands and having so much fun. The little girl then decided it was time for a game. She put the dish on the ground and asked it to stand up. The fat dish wobbled and wobbled, but eventually it stood up! The little girl clapped in delight and gave the dish a big hug. The dish smiled as it wobbled and wobbled. The little girl was so proud of her dish for standing up, she gave it one last big hug and went off to play with her other toys. The fat dish stayed where it was, still standing and still smiling, forever tied up and happy.

One day, a sad dog found a trumpet in the park. He did not know how to play it. He tried to blow, but no sound came out. He was very sad and wanted to play the trumpet. A little girl saw the sad dog and walked up to him. She said, "Why are you sad, dog?" The dog showed her the trumpet. The little girl knew how to play the trumpet. She told the dog, "You need to rub it first, then blow." The dog rubbed the trumpet and then blew into it. To their surprise, instead of a sound, colorful bubbles came out! The sad dog was now a happy dog. He loved the bubbles and played with the little girl all day. They laughed and played with the magical trumpet, making more and more bubbles.

One week, Nick and his mum went shopping. Nick was very excited! He wanted to buy a new toy, but mum said he needed to wait. "I'm ready to buy it!" Nick said. Mum smiled and said, "Not yet, Nick. Patience is a good thing! Let's have another look and see what else is here that's interesting." Nick knew that waiting was important, and he wanted to help. He said to Mum, "It's okay. I can be patient. What could I look at that is fun?" Mum said cheerfully, "There are lots of puzzles and coloring books! Would you like to have a look?" Nick found a new puzzle that he liked, and it wasn't too hard for him. He smiled and said, "Yes, I can do this one!" Mum encouraged him, "That's great. Put it in the basket and wait until the end of the week. Then, if you've been good, we can come back and buy it!" Nick was so excited and he was ready! He said, "Ok, mum! I'm gonna do it!" He put the puzzle in the basket and they both looked around some more. Nick was so happy that he could wait and eventually buy what he wanted. He knew patience was the key.
