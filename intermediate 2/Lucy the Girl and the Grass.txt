Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Grass

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a big house with her mom and dad. Every day they would go outside and play together. One day, Lucy noticed a small shadow on the grass. She pointed and said to her mom, "Mom, what's that little shadow?" Her mom smiled and said, "That shadow is an imaginary friend. We can call it a Friend of Shadow." Lucy was excited and asked, "Can it answer my questions?" Her mom said, "Yes, it can answer your questions. Just imagine what you would like to ask it, and then focus on the shadow until an answer appears." So Lucy did just that. She thought of a question and asked the Friend of Shadow, "Will it rain tomorrow?" And suddenly, the shadow seemed to move. It almost looked like it was nodding its head. Lucy smiled and said, "Thanks, Friend of Shadow!" Then she ran back to her house, laughing with joy.

One day, a little dog went out for a walk. He saw a big tree with a big root. The dog was glad because he liked to play on roots. He jumped on the root and ran around the tree. Then, the dog saw a small box near the tree. He was very curious about the box. He tried to open it, but it was shut. The dog tried and tried, but the box would not open. Suddenly, the box opened by itself! The dog was surprised. Inside the box, there was a toy. The toy looked just like the little dog. The dog was so happy to have a new friend to play with. Now, the dog and his toy friend played on the big root and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tall tree. The tree was very dependable. All the little birds liked to make nests in the tree. One day, a small bird tried to stand on a branch of the tall tree. The bird was scared at first, but the dependable tree kept the bird safe. The bird was happy and sang a pretty song. As time went by, more birds came to the forest. They all wanted to stand on the dependable tree's branches. The forest was full of happy birds, singing and playing. The tall tree was very proud to help its friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Lily wore her clean clothes to play with her friend, Tom. "Look at my clean clothes!" Lily said to Tom. Tom smiled and said, "Wow, your clothes are so clean and pretty!" While they were playing, Lily saw a small kitten stuck in a tree. She wanted to help the kitten, so she told Tom, "Let's help the kitten!" They both went to the tree and Lily kneeled down to let Tom climb on her back. Tom reached up and carefully took the kitten out of the tree. Lily and Tom were very happy that they saved the kitten. They played with the kitten all day and took it home to show their parents. At the end of the day, Lily's clothes were not clean anymore, but she was happy that she helped the kitten. Her mom said, "Don't worry, we can clean your clothes again." Lily smiled and knew that helping the kitten was worth it.

Ben and Sam were friends. They liked to play with cars and balls. They lived in a big nation with many people. The people had different colors and languages. Ben and Sam did not care. They were happy. One day, some bad men came to their nation. They had guns and bombs. They wanted to change the nation. They wanted everyone to be the same as them. They did not like people who were different. They hurt and killed many people. They made big fires and smoke. They made the nation sad and scared. Ben and Sam were at school when the bad men came. They heard loud noises and saw bright flashes. They ran to the teacher. The teacher told them to hide under the desks. She said they had to be quiet and wait. She said she would protect them. Ben and Sam were afraid. They held each other's hands. The bad men came to the school. They kicked the doors and windows. They shouted and laughed. They looked for people to hurt. They found the teacher and the children. They pointed their guns at them. They asked them what color and language they were. The teacher said they were all children of the nation. She said they were all good and kind. She said they did not want to change. She said they wanted to live in peace. The bad men did not listen. They did not care. They were angry and mean. They shot the teacher and the children. They did not leave anyone alive. They threw a bomb in the school. They made it explode. They made the school fall down. They made dust and ash. They made the nation cry and mourn. Ben and Sam were gone. They did not play with cars and balls anymore. They did not see their families and friends anymore. They did not grow up and learn anymore. They did not smile and laugh anymore. They were dead. The bad men were not happy. They did not change the nation. They made it stronger and braver. They made it fight back and resist. They made it unite and love. They made it proud and free. The bad men were ashamed. They knew they were wrong and evil. They knew they had no place in the nation. They knew they had no friends and hope. They knew they had no future and joy. They wished they were dead.
