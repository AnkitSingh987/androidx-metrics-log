Category: Intermediate
Title: The Clock and the Car

Once upon a time, there was an old clock. It was big and had a long hand and a short hand. The clock lived in a small room with a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toy car. She would make it zoom all around the room. One day, Lily's toy car zoomed too close to the old clock. The clock said, "Please don't let your car zoom near me, Lily. I don't want to get hurt." But Lily didn't listen. She kept making her car zoom all around the room. The old clock got very sad. It didn't want to get hit by the toy car. So, the clock decided to move away from Lily. It moved to the other side of the room. But Lily didn't care. She just made her car zoom even faster. The car zoomed so fast that it hit the old clock. The clock fell down and broke into many pieces. Lily was sad because her old clock was broken. She wished she had listened to the clock and not let her car zoom near it. Now, the old clock was gone forever.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a big red ball and ran to it. Tim was very happy to play with the ball. A girl named Sue came by and wanted to play too. Tim was rude and yelled, "No, this is my ball!" Sue felt sad and walked away. Later, Tim saw an emergency. Sue fell and hurt her leg. Tim wanted to help but Sue said, "No, you were rude to me." Tim felt bad and learned to be nice to others.

A dad and a son were walking in the park. The dad wanted to show his son something interesting. He pointed to a big man with a gun. The dad asked the son, "Do you see the man with the gun? Can you tell me what you see?" The son was curious. He looked carefully at the man and said, "I see a big man with a gun. It looks scary!" The dad said, "That's right! The man is a soldier and he is kneeling to say his prayers. Kneeling is when you bend your knees and put your hands together like this." The dad then kneeled down and the son copied the dad. The son said, "Oh, now I understand. He is praying!" The dad smiled and said, "Yes, that's exactly what he is doing. Praying is talking to God." The dad and son then bowed their heads and said a small prayer together. They thanked God for the beautiful park and for the soldier.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a gentle cat named Fluffy and a little dog named Spot. They were best friends and played together every day. They loved to run and jump in the warm, green grass. One sunny day, Fluffy and Spot found a shiny pin on the ground. They both wanted to play with it. They began to quarrel. "I found it first!" said Fluffy. "No, I saw it first!" said Spot. As they quarreled, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird picked up the pin in its beak and flew away. Fluffy and Spot stopped quarreling and looked at each other. "We should not quarrel," said Fluffy. "You are right," said Spot. "We are best friends." Fluffy and Spot learned their lesson. They promised to always be kind to each other and never quarrel again. From that day on, they played happily together, and their friendship grew even stronger.

It was a warm day and two friends were playing outside. One was a fireman and the other was a little girl. The fireman wanted to show the girl how to dance. He spun around and around, his feet moving quickly across the grass. The girl clapped her hands and laughed. She wanted to dance too! So she jumped up and began to spin. The fireman watched and smiled. The girl was twirling and dancing like a fairy! Soon the sun began to set and it was time to go home. But before they left, the fireman showed the girl one last dance. He spun around and around, and the little girl followed. They were twirling and laughing and having so much fun! When the fireman stopped, the little girl was out of breath, but she smiled. The warm evening air was filled with joy and laughter. The fireman was so proud of the girl, and she was proud of herself too. The two friends waved goodbye to each other, and the fireman went back to the fire station. The little girl went home, still smiling from their special dance.
