Category: Intermediate
Title: Henny the Horse and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a little horse named Henny. Henny lived on a farm with lots of hay. Every day, Henny would eat hay and play with her friends. But one day, Henny felt restless. She wanted to do something fun and new. Henny had an idea. She thought, "I will imagine a fun game to play with my friends!" Henny closed her eyes and thought hard. She imagined a game where they would jump over the hay and race each other. Henny told her friends about the game. They all liked the idea and wanted to play. So, they lined up and got ready to race. Henny felt so happy and not restless anymore. They played the game all day long and had lots of fun. Henny learned that using her imagination could make her days more exciting.

Once upon a time, there was a nice mama who had a baby. She wanted to teach her baby how to love. Every day she would give her lots of hugs and kisses, so that her baby would learn how to show love. One morning, the mama was out in the cold winter sun. She saw a little boy standing all alone. The mama asked the little boy why he was all by himself. He said he had nobody to teach him how to show love. The mama said, "I can teach you. It's easy. All you have to do is give hugs and kisses to the people you care about. Then you'll know how to show love." The little boy smiled. The mama hugged the little boy and kissed him on the forehead. Then she said, "See? Now you know how to show love. Do this for other people, and you'll always stay warm, even in the coldest of winters."

Once upon a time, there was a charming cow named Daisy. Daisy lived on a farm with her friends. She liked to play in the grass and eat yummy food. One sunny day, Daisy saw a little boy named Timmy. Timmy was playing a guessing game with his toy. He looked at Daisy and said, "Do you want to play, too?" Daisy nodded her big cow head and said, "Yes, I want to play!" Timmy held up a toy and said, "Guess what this is!" Daisy looked at the toy and said, "It's a ball!" Timmy clapped his hands and said, "You're right! Good job, Daisy!" They played the guessing game all day and had lots of fun. Daisy and Timmy became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a nice bird was in a tree. The bird liked to sing songs. The bird sang a happy song. A little girl heard the bird's song. She liked the song too. The little girl said, "Hi, bird! I like your song. Can you go with me and sing more songs?" The bird said, "Yes, I will go with you and sing more songs." So, the bird and the girl went on a walk. As they walked, the bird sang more songs. The little girl was happy. But then, it started to rain. The rain was very heavy. The bird got wet and could not sing anymore. The girl was sad, and they both went home.

One day, a nice cat named Mark went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Mark started to climb the tree, but his foot began to slip. He was scared, but he kept going up. Mark met a bird on a branch. The bird said, "Hi, Mark! What are you doing up here?" Mark replied, "I want to see the top of the tree." The bird said, "Be careful, it's very high." Mark kept climbing, but then he slipped again. This time, he fell all the way down to the ground. Mark was sad that he could not get to the top of the tree. He had tried so hard, but did not make it.

The sun was shining and the birds were singing in the open sky. Clouds of mist rolled across the horizon. Jimmy the bear was feeling so sleepy that he decided to take a nap. He found a nice, sunny spot and curled up in a tight ball. Jimmy closed his eyes and listened to the birds singing in the open. The mist felt cool in the warm sunshine and before long he was fast asleep. Jimmy's nap felt so nice and soothing. As the sun set on the horizon, Jimmy yawned and opened his eyes. He had slept the whole day away! The mist had cleared and Jimmy was ready to start a new day. He thanked the birds for singing him to sleep and took off into the open sky.
