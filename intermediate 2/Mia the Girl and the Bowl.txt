Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bowl

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to bake a cake. She asked her mom, "Can we bake a cake together?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's bake an enormous cake!" They mixed flour, sugar, and eggs in a big bowl. Mia helped her mom pour the mix into a cake pan. They put the pan in the oven and waited for the cake to bake. While they waited, Mia's mom said, "I have a surprise for you!" She showed Mia a box of colorful decorations for the cake. Mia was so happy and couldn't wait to decorate the enormous cake. When the cake was done baking, they took it out of the oven. Mia and her mom decorated the cake with the colorful decorations. They made the cake look like a big, happy face. Mia's dad came home and saw the enormous cake. He said, "Wow, what a big surprise!" They all laughed and enjoyed eating the delicious cake together.

One day, a tall man was walking in the park. He saw a little boy playing with a leather ball. The tall man went near the boy and said, "Hello, little boy! Be careful with the ball." The boy looked at the tall man and said, "Why? I like to play with my ball." The tall man wanted to warn the boy. He said, "If you throw the ball too far, it may get lost or hit someone." The boy did not listen to the tall man. He threw the ball very far. The ball went up, up, up in the sky. The boy and the tall man looked up. They saw something unexpected. The ball did not come down. It stayed up in the sky and turned into a big, shiny star. The boy and the tall man were very surprised. They smiled at each other and said, "Wow! The ball is now a star!" From that day on, the boy and the tall man became good friends. They always remembered the day when the leather ball turned into a star.

One day, a group of animal friends wanted to watch a movie. They could not agree on which movie to watch. Some wanted a funny movie, some wanted a scary one. They all began to argue and fight. Then, a humble little mouse spoke up. He said, "We should unite and find a movie we all like." The animals stopped fighting and listened to the mouse. They decided to work together to find a movie they all would enjoy. The animals talked about what they liked in movies. They found out that they all liked movies with animals in them. They chose a movie about animal friends who unite and have fun together. The animals learned that working together and being humble was better than fighting. They all enjoyed the movie and had a great time.

Sara and Ben like to play in the garden. They have many toys and books. But sometimes they make a big mess. Mom says they have to organize their things. She gives them a big box and a small box. Sara and Ben start to put their things in the boxes. Sara puts her dolls and books in the big box. Ben puts his cars and balls in the small box. They are almost done. But they see one more thing. It is a long vine. It is green and curly. They do not know where to put it. They look at the vine. It is very pretty. They have an idea. They take the vine and wrap it around the boxes. They make a bow on top. They smile. They have made a gift for Mom. They are very lucky. They have a nice garden, many toys and books, and a beautiful vine. They run to Mom and show her their gift. Mom hugs them and says they are very clever and kind. She loves their gift. Sara and Ben are happy. They have organized their things and made Mom happy. They love to play in the garden.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a rope tied to it. Tim was tired, but he wanted to play. He saw a girl named Sue standing by the tree. Sue was holding a pair of scissors. "Hi Sue, what are you doing?" Tim asked. Sue said, "I want to cut the rope and make a swing, but I can't do it alone." Tim thought for a moment. He knew they should ask for help. So, they went to find a grown-up. They found Tim's dad and asked him to help. Tim's dad cut the rope and helped them make a swing. Tim and Sue played on the swing all day. They had so much fun that they forgot how tired they were. At the end of the day, the tree said, "Thank you for not cutting me down. Now, I have a surprise for you." The tree opened a secret door, and inside was a room full of wonder. There were toys, games, and treats for Tim and Sue to enjoy. The moral of the story is that it's important to ask for help and be kind to nature. You never know what surprises and wonders are waiting for you.
