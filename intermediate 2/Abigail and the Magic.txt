Category: Intermediate
Title: Abigail and the Magic

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Abigail. Abigail had a special glass. It was a magic glass - it could make people better if they touched it! One day Abigail was walking when she saw her friend Timmy crying. She asked him what was the matter. "I hurt my knee," said Timmy. Abigail smiled and said, "Don't worry Timmy, I have a special glass that can make it better." Timmy was excited. He touched the glass and it worked. He felt much better! Abigail was so happy to see her friend smile again. She said to him, "Remember Timmy, be careful with your knees and if you hurt yourself again you can touch the glass again and it will heal you!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a naughty girl who loved to play with her toys all day. One day, she found a shiny rock and decided to polish it. She rubbed and rubbed the rock until it sparkled. Next door, there was an old lady named Mrs. Smith. Mrs. Smith was a patient lady who loved to talk to Lily. One day, Mrs. Smith saw Lily playing with her shiny rock. She asked, "What are you doing, Lily?" "I'm polishing my rock, Mrs. Smith!" Lily said with a big smile. "Do you want to help me?" Mrs. Smith smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to help you, Lily!" They both sat down and took turns polishing the rock until it was very shiny. In the end, Lily and Mrs. Smith became good friends. Lily learned to be more patient like Mrs. Smith, and they had lots of fun polishing rocks together. And from that day on, Lily wasn't as naughty as before.

The sun was shining high in the sky. Dave and Bob were fighting over their toys. Dave said, "I have the my table, it is clear and I will carry it around." Bob said, "That's not fair. I want it too!" Dave growled, "No, I am carrying it around and you cannot have it!" Bob got so mad that he picked up a rock and threw it at the table. Dave was so angry and he yelled, "Now I can't carry it around anymore!" Bob giggled and said, "Yeah! Now you can't have it." Dave was so mad and he started to cry. He was sad that he could not carry the table around anymore. The end.

Tim and Kim like to sail. They have a small boat with a big sail. They take the boat to the lake and sail on the water. They have fun and see many things. One day, they see a weird thing in the water. It is long and thin and has scales. It moves fast and makes a hiss sound. Tim and Kim are scared. They think it is a snake. They sail away from the snake. They do not want to get close to it. They sail to the other side of the lake. They see a big duck. The duck is friendly and quacks. Tim and Kim are happy. They feed the duck some bread. They forget about the snake. They sail back to the shore. They are tired and hungry. They tell their mom and dad about the snake. Their mom and dad say it was not a snake. It was an eel. An eel is a fish that looks like a snake. Tim and Kim are surprised. They did not know that. They learn something new. They still like to sail. But they do not like eels. They think eels are weird.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They had a pretty porch in front of their house. Mia liked to play with her toys on the porch. One day, Mia found a key on the ground. She showed it to her mom and said, "Look, I found a key!" Her mom smiled and said, "Let's see what it can unlock!" They tried to unlock the door to the house, but it did not work. They tried to unlock the gate, but it did not work either. Then, Mia had an idea. She went to the porch and tried to unlock a small box that was there. The key worked! Inside the box, they found a sour lemon. Mia's mom said, "Let's make lemonade!" They squeezed the lemon, added sugar and water, and made delicious lemonade. They drank it together on the porch, happy that they had unlocked the secret of the sour lemon.
