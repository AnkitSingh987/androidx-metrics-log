Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Jolly Pilot and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a jolly pilot named Tom. He flew a big plane in the sky. Tom was a happy man who loved to laugh and make others laugh too. One day, Tom met a pretty lady named Sue. They liked to talk and play together. Tom and Sue became very good friends. Tom said to Sue, "I like you a lot. Will you marry me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, I will marry you." Tom and Sue had a big party to celebrate. All their friends and family came. They ate cake and danced. Everyone was happy for Tom and Sue. They lived happily ever after, flying in the big plane together.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play and explore. One day, Kitty saw a big cupboard in the kitchen. She had never seen it before. Kitty thought, "I dare to look inside the cupboard." Kitty opened the cupboard door and saw many things. She saw a big, dead bug on the floor of the cupboard. Kitty was scared, but she wanted to be brave. She said to herself, "I dare to touch the dead bug." So, she touched the bug with her paw. Just then, the bug started to move! Kitty was surprised. The bug was not dead after all. It was just sleeping. The bug looked at Kitty and said, "Thank you for waking me up. I was having a bad dream." Kitty and the bug became good friends, and they played together every day.

Lily did not like vegetables. She liked candy and cake and ice cream. But her mom said she had to eat vegetables every day. She said vegetables were good for her and made her strong and healthy. One day, Lily had a plan. She saw her mom give her a plate of vegetables for lunch. She saw her dog Spot under the table. She decided to give her vegetables to Spot. She thought Spot would like them and she would not have to eat them. She put some carrots and broccoli and peas on the floor for Spot. But Spot did not like vegetables either. He sniffed them and turned away. He barked and wagged his tail. He wanted something else to eat. Lily's mom heard Spot barking. She looked under the table and saw the vegetables on the floor. She saw Lily's empty plate. She was very angry. She said, "Lily, did you give your vegetables to Spot? That is very naughty. You have to eat your vegetables. They are good for you. And Spot needs his own food. He cannot eat your food." Lily felt bad. She did not want to make her mom angry. She did not want to make Spot unhappy. She did not want to lie. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I don't like vegetables. I thought Spot would like them. But he didn't. Can I have something else to eat?" Lily's mom was still angry, but she was also sad. She said, "Lily, I know you don't like vegetables. But you have to try them. They are good for you. And you have to be honest. You cannot give your food to Spot. He needs his own food. He cannot eat your food. You have to eat your food." Lily nodded. She said, "OK, mom. I will try to eat my vegetables. And I will not give them to Spot. And I will be honest. I'm sorry, mom. I love you." Lily's mom hugged her. She said, "I love you too, Lily. I'm glad you are sorry. And I'm glad you will try to eat your vegetables. And I'm glad you will not give them to Spot. And I'm glad you will be honest. You are a good girl, Lily." Lily smiled. She felt better. She said, "Thank you, mom. Can I have a hug from Spot too?" Lily's mom said, "Of course, Lily. Spot loves you too. He is a good dog. He just doesn't like vegetables." Lily and her mom and Spot hugged. They were happy. They ate their lunch. Lily tried to eat her vegetables. She did not like them, but she ate them. Spot ate his own food. He liked it. He did not eat vegetables. They were all good.
