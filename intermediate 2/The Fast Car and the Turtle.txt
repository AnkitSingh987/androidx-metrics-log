Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fast Car and the Turtle

One day, a fast car went zoom down the road. A little boy saw the car and wanted to race it. He put on his shoes and tied a knot in the laces. But the knot was stubborn and hard to untie. The boy tried and tried, but the knot would not come undone. While the boy was trying to untie the knot, a big, slow turtle walked up to him. The turtle saw the boy's stubborn knot and wanted to help. The turtle had a secret trick to untie knots. With a little wiggle of his nose, the turtle made the knot come undone. The boy was so happy and thanked the turtle. Now the boy could race the fast car. But when the car zoomed by again, the boy had a better idea. He asked the turtle if he wanted to race instead. The turtle smiled and said yes. They had a fun race together, and the boy learned that sometimes, slow and steady can be just as fun as going zoom.

Once upon a time, there was a small village filled with people who were compassionate with each other. One day, it began to rain. The rain was so heavy that the villagers had to run to get shelter. One villager named Tiya ran to her neighbor's house. When Tiya knocked on the door, her neighbor opened it and said, "Come inside, Tiya! It's too wet to stay outside." Tiya went inside and she saw that her neighbor had a big refrigerator. She asked, "What's in the refrigerator?" Her neighbor replied, "It's filled with lots of food that I use to take care of my family. Come and get something to eat." Tiya was very happy and thanked her neighbor for being so compassionate. Her neighbor said, "It's no problem, you're always welcome here." The two villagers stayed inside and ate until the rain stopped. Finally, the sun came out and Tiya went back home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to a big store. They needed to buy some new things for their home. Tim saw an adorable toy car. He really wanted it, but his mom said they could not buy it today. Later, Tim saw his friend Jack at the store. Jack had a toy car just like the one Tim wanted. Tim had an idea. He asked Jack, "Do you want to trade my toy truck for your toy car?" Jack smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that!" When they got home, Tim was excited to unpack his new toy car. He played with it all day and was very happy. His mom was happy too, because Tim learned how to trade and make a friend happy. And that made Tim feel good inside.

Once there was a small cat named Tim. Tim was sad because he had a test to take. He did not want to take the test because he did not know if he would pass. Tim asked his friend, a big dog named Sam, for help. "Sam, I have a test and I am scared. Can you help me?" Sam said, "Yes, Tim. I will help you with your test." They tried to study, but they could not focus. They played instead. The day of the test came, and Tim was not ready. He tried his best, but the test was very hard. In the end, Tim did not pass the test. Tim and Sam were sad because they did not study enough.

Once upon a time, there was a cute sailor. He lived near the sea. He had a big boat. He loved to sail on the water. One day, the cute sailor saw a little bird. The bird was sad. It could not fly. The sailor wanted to help the bird. He called out to the bird, "Come, I will help you." The sailor and the bird became friends. They played and had fun. They sailed on the boat together. The bird was happy. The sailor was happy too. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a tiny home, there lived a little mouse. The mouse loved to chew on things. One day, he found a fragile egg. It was so pretty and looked yummy. The mouse thought about what to do. He knew that an egg had life inside. But he was hungry and wanted to chew it. He thought and thought. Then, he decided to chew the egg. As the mouse chewed the egg, it broke. The life inside was gone. The mouse was sad. He learned that some things are too fragile to chew. The moral of the story is to think before you act, or you may hurt something you didn't mean to.
