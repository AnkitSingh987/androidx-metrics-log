Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Letter

One day, a little boy named Tim got a letter. He was nervous to open it. He did not know who sent the letter. Tim ran to his mom and showed her the letter. "Mom, I got a letter. I am nervous. Can you help me?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Let's open the letter together." Tim and his mom opened the letter. It was from a mean kid at school. The letter said, "I don't like you, Tim." Tim felt sad. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. We will talk to your teacher about it." The next day, Tim's mom talked to the teacher. But the mean kid did not say sorry. Tim was still sad. He did not want to go to school anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Lily saw a pretty flame in the fireplace. She wanted to touch it, but her mom said, "No, Lily! It's hot and can hurt you." One day, Lily was playing outside when she saw a big, empty box. She thought it would be fun to play with. So, Lily went inside the box and started to pretend it was her house. She was having so much fun! Suddenly, Lily heard a loud noise. She looked up and saw a big tree branch fall near her box house. She was scared and ran to her mom. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. I'm here to keep you safe." From that day on, Lily knew she should always be careful and listen to her mom.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there was a dirty snake. The snake liked to play in the mud all day long. He loved to get dirty and make marks in the mud with his long body. One day, a small bird saw the dirty snake making marks in the mud. The bird thought it looked fun, so she flew down to play with the snake. The snake and the bird became good friends, and they played in the mud together every day. One day, it rained very hard, and the mud was washed away. The snake and the bird were sad because they could not make marks in the mud anymore. But then, the sun came out, and the mud came back. The dirty snake and the small bird were very happy, and they played in the mud, making marks all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a toy named Silver. Silver was incredible and shiny. Lily loved Silver very much. One day, Lily and Silver went outside to play. They played in the mud and had a lot of fun. But when they went back into the house, they were very dirty. Lily's mom saw them and said, "Oh no, you both need to clean up!" Lily took Silver to the sink and turned on the water. She used soap and a soft cloth to clean Silver. Lily said, "We have to make you shiny again, Silver." After they were both clean, Lily and Silver went back to play. They were very happy and had a great day.

Once, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a toy cat he loved a lot. Tom took the cat everywhere he went. One day, Tom's mom told him to be patient. She said there was a big surprise coming soon. Tom was excited and waited. A few days later, Tom saw a big box in the living room. He knew it was the surprise his mom talked about. Tom opened the box and found a real cat inside! The cat was soft and cute. Tom was so happy. He knew he had to take care of the cat with his own body. Tom and the cat became best friends. They played together all day. Tom was patient with the cat, even when the cat was slow or didn't want to play. He remembered how he waited for the surprise, and now he had a new friend. Tom learned that being patient is a good thing.
