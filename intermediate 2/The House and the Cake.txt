Category: Intermediate
Title: The House and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was an attic. The attic was a fun place to play. One day, a little girl named Sally went to the attic to play with her toys. She found a box with a fake cake inside. The cake was very pretty and had many colors. Sally wanted to play with the fake cake. She got a toy knife to cut the cake. She cut the cake into small pieces. Sally pretended to eat the cake and shared it with her dolls. They had a fun tea party with the cake. After the tea party, Sally put the fake cake back in the box. She left the attic and went downstairs. Sally told her mommy about the fun she had in the attic with the fake cake. Her mommy smiled and hugged her. They had a real snack together, and Sally was happy.

Billy was walking through the park with his mom. He saw lots of bright flowers and sunshine. Suddenly, he started to hear a funny noise. It was coming from a person standing nearby. Billy asked his mom, "What is that noise?" His mom smiled and answered, "It's called singing. That person is supplying the park with some music". As they were walking, Billy noticed a funny looking stick in the man's hand. He asked his mom, "What is that thing?". His mom said, "That's called a measure. It helps the man sound good". Billy thought this was intriguing, but the man stopped playing his music. He said he was done for the day and walked away. The next day, Billy and his mom returned to the park. When they arrived, they saw the man back with his measure. Billy was excited to hear the man play and sing again. The man was playing even better than the day before. The music was bright and cheerful. The man had supplied the park with beautiful music and Billy smiled.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient igloo. It was very old and big. Inside the igloo lived a kind old man and his small, fluffy dog. One day, the old man and his dog went for a walk. They gazed at the pretty sky and the green trees. They saw many birds and bugs along the way. As they walked, they found a new friend. The friend was a little girl. She was lost and scared. The old man, his dog, and the little girl went back to the ancient igloo. They all became happy friends and lived together in the big, old igloo.

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and read books. One day, Tim saw his dad reading a newspaper. Tim was curious and wanted to read the newspaper too. "Can I read the newspaper, Dad?" Tim asked. His dad looked at him and said, "This newspaper has big words, Tim. You might not understand them yet. Let's read a book together instead." But Tim refused. He wanted to try reading the newspaper. So, his dad gave him the newspaper to look at. Tim tried his best to read the words, but they were too big and hard for him. He felt sad and wished he could read like his dad. Just then, his little sister, Amy, came into the room with a picture book. "Tim, let's read this book together," she said. Tim smiled and agreed. They sat down and read the book, looking at the pictures and sounding out the words. Tim was happy to read with his sister, and he knew that one day, he would be able to read the newspaper too.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and make mud pies. They had a big box of toys to play with. But they were bored of the same toys. They wanted to find something new. They saw a small door in the wall. It was locked with a rusty key. Tom said, "Let's unlock the door and see what is inside. Maybe there are more toys or a treasure." Lily said, "But what if there is a monster inside? I am scared of monsters. They have big teeth and claws and they roar." Tom said, "Don't be silly. There are no monsters in the garden. Come on, I have the key. It was in the shed." Tom put the key in the lock and turned it. The door opened with a creak. Tom and Lily peeked inside. It was dark and dusty. They saw some old boxes and bags. They smelled something bad. They heard a growl. They screamed and ran away. They slammed the door behind them. They dropped the key on the ground. They hugged each other and cried. A big dog came out of the door. It was the monster. It had brown fur and a black nose. It had a dull collar with a tag. It said, "Woof. I am Max. I am not a monster. I am a friend. I was locked in the door by mistake. I was hungry and lonely. Can you play with me?" Tom and Lily looked at the dog. They saw that it was not a monster. It was a friendly dog. They felt sorry for it. They said, "We are sorry, Max. We thought you were a monster. We were scared. We will play with you. Do you want a mud pie?" Max wagged his tail. He said, "Yes, please. I like mud pies. Thank you for unlocking the door. You are nice." Tom and Lily smiled. They gave Max a mud pie. They played with him in the garden. They had fun. They made a new friend.
