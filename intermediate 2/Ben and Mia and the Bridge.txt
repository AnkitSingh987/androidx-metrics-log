Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Bridge

Ben and Mia like to play with their toys. They have a lot of toys, but their favorite is the camera. The camera can record what they do and say. They can watch the record later and laugh. One day, they decide to make a record of their adventure in the backyard. They take the camera and go outside. They see a big pile of lumber. The lumber is for Daddy's new shed. Ben and Mia think the lumber is a mountain. They climb on the lumber and pretend they are explorers. They find a long piece of lumber that is fit for a bridge. They put it across two other pieces of lumber and walk on it. They record how they cross the bridge and wave to the camera. They are very brave and happy. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is Daddy. He is coming to work on the shed. He sees Ben and Mia on the lumber and he is angry. He tells them to get down and go inside. He says the lumber is not a toy and it is not safe. He takes the camera and the bridge away. Ben and Mia are sad and scared. They go inside and sit on the couch. They are sorry they made Daddy mad. They hope he will not break the camera and the record. They want to watch their adventure again and remember how they had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a red penguin named Poppy. Poppy was not like other penguins. She had red feathers and lived in a big, cold land with ice and snow. One day, Poppy saw a line of other penguins walking far away. She wanted to be friends with them. So, Poppy went to the line and started to lead them. They walked and walked, and Poppy showed them the way. After a while, the penguins found a big pool of water. They all jumped in and played together. Poppy was happy to lead her new friends. And they were happy to have a red penguin friend like Poppy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a king. The king had a big, pretty crown. He loved his crown very much. One day, the king lost his crown. He was very sad. He looked for it, but he could not find it. In the town, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue wanted to help the king. She thought, "If I find the crown, I will be useful." So, Sue started to look for the crown too. She looked up, and she looked down. She looked all around the town. Sue saw a tall tree with a shiny thing on top. She thought it was the crown. Sue tried to raise her hand high to reach the crown, but she could not. She got a big stick and tried again. This time, she got the crown! The king was so happy. Sue was very useful, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little dog. He was hurt and needed to heal. One day, he saw a big lamp. The lamp was bright and shiny. The dog went near the lamp and looked at it. A kind cat saw the dog and came to him. The cat said, "Hello, little dog. You look hurt. Do you need help to heal?" The dog looked at the cat and said, "Yes, I am hurt. I need help to heal. I saw this big lamp and thought it might help me." The cat looked at the lamp and said, "This lamp is very bright. But it cannot help you heal. We need to find something else." The dog and the cat walked together to find something to help the dog heal. They looked and looked, but they could not find anything to help the dog heal. The dog was very sad. The cat tried to help, but it was not enough. In the end, the dog did not heal, and he was still hurt. The cat and the dog were both very sad.

One day, a big group of ants went on a march. They were all in a long line, walking together. They wanted to find some ice-cream to eat. The ants were very glad because it was a hot day and ice-cream would be very cool and yummy. The ants marched and marched until they found a big ice-cream cone on the ground. They were so happy! The ants climbed up the ice-cream and started to eat it. They loved the sweet taste and how it made them feel cool. After they ate the ice-cream, the ants were very full and glad. They marched back to their home, feeling happy and ready to take a long nap. The ants knew that they would always remember the day they found the big ice-cream cone.
