Category: Intermediate
Title: Mr and the Frog

Once upon a time there were two young friends, Mr. Rabbit and Mr. Frog. They were both very youthful and always had lots of fun together. They would always play in the forest and go swimming in the lake. One day, they wanted to do something special so they decided to split two tall trees in the forest. Mr. Frog got busy sawing the bark and soon he had cut through the trees. Then both of them put their hands together and pushed against the logs. With one loud crack, the logs split in two! They both cheered in excitement but then heard a disappointed voice. It was their old friend, Mr. Turtle. He had been too slow to join them and said "You two have so much youth. Why didn't you split something more boring, like a stale loaf of bread?" Mr. Frog and Mr. Rabbit felt embarrassed and realized that their friend was right. They should have thought about others and chosen something fun for everyone to split. They thanked Mr. Turtle for his advice and from then on, whenever they wanted to have fun with their youth, they made sure to think of something exciting for everyone to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty snake named Sam. Sam liked to be silly and play tricks on his friends in the forest. One day, Sam had a big idea to do something funny with his friends. Sam hid in a big box near the trees. When his friends walked by, he would jump out and say "boo!" His friends would be scared and then laugh. Sam thought it would be a fun game. But something unexpected happened. When Sam jumped out of the box, there was a big mirror in front of him. Sam saw himself and thought it was another snake! He was scared and said "boo!" to the mirror. Then, Sam realized it was just him and laughed. His friends saw what happened and laughed too. From that day on, Sam was not so naughty and enjoyed playing nice games with his friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a fun day playing with a ball and running around. After a while, Tim got hungry, so his mom made him a sandwich. She put it on a plate and went to get some water. While she was gone, a squirrel came and took the sandwich. It ran up a tree and hid it in a hidden spot. Tim saw this and told his mom. They both observed the squirrel eating the sandwich in the tree. Tim's mom made another sandwich and they ate together. Tim was happy and learned to watch his food so the squirrel wouldn't take it again.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max was a little nervous because he did not know who his owner was. He wanted to find his owner and be a good dog. Max thought he should mind his manners to make his owner happy. One day, Max went for a walk in the park. He saw a big tree and a little girl. The little girl was very sad. Max went to her and licked her hand. The girl smiled and hugged Max. She said, "I lost my dog, but you are very nice. Will you be my dog?" Max was surprised! He did not know that the little girl was his owner. He was so happy to find her. Max barked and wagged his tail. The little girl laughed and said, "I am your owner, and you are my dog. We will be best friends forever." And they were.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys in the living room. One day, they find a big piano in the corner. It is black and shiny and has many keys. "Look, Lily, a piano!" Tom says. "Can we play with it?" "OK, Tom, but be careful. It is very big and heavy," Lily says. They run to the piano and start to press the keys. Some keys make high sounds and some keys make low sounds. They try to match the sounds with their voices. "La, la, la!" Tom sings, pressing a high key. "Ba, ba, ba!" Lily sings, pressing a low key. They giggle and have fun. But then they smell something bad. It is coming from the piano. "Yuck, what is that?" Tom asks, holding his nose. "Let's see," Lily says, opening the lid of the piano. They see a smelly sock inside the piano. It is old and dirty and has holes. "Ew, a sock!" Tom says. "Who put it there?" "Maybe it is Dad's sock. He plays the piano sometimes," Lily says. They take out the sock and throw it in the trash. Then they close the lid and keep playing. "Next time, we will check the piano before we play," Tom says. "Yes, and we will tell Dad to wash his socks," Lily says. They laugh and match the sounds again. They like the piano, but not the sock.
