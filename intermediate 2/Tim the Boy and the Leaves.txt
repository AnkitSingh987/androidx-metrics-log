Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to collect rocks. One day, while he was playing outside, he saw a big hole in the ground. Tim went to the hole and shouted, "Hello! Is anyone there?" A small voice came from the hole, "Hi! I am a little bug, and I live here. My home is spoiled because it rained and now it is wet." Tim wanted to help the little bug. He said, "I will collect some dry leaves and sticks for you. They will make your home warm and dry again." The little bug was happy and thanked Tim for his help. Tim collected the leaves and sticks and put them in the hole. The little bug's home was not spoiled anymore. The little bug and Tim became good friends, and they played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, heavy head. One day, Tim went for a walk in the park. He saw a big tree with a bird standing on a branch. "Hi, bird!" said Tim. "Why do you stand on the branch?" "Because I have a light head and I can stand here," said the bird. "But you have a heavy head, so you can't stand on the branch like me." Tim felt sad because he could not stand on the branch like the bird. He walked away from the tree and saw a big rock. He thought he could stand on the rock instead. But as he climbed on the rock, something unexpected happened. The rock started to move! "Surprise!" said the rock. "I am not a rock. I am a big turtle with a heavy head like you. We can be friends and stand together." Tim was so happy to find a friend with a heavy head like him. They stood together and played in the park all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he liked to play with. He would make the car go left and right, up and down, all day long. Tim loved his toy car so much that he took it everywhere he went. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue playing with a toy plane. Sue made the plane fly high in the sky. Tim felt jealous because he wanted a toy plane too. He thought the plane was more fun than his car. Tim asked his mom if he could have a toy plane too. His mom said, "You can have a toy plane if you are good and help me with chores." Tim worked hard and helped his mom. Soon, he got a toy plane as a gift. Now, Tim could make his car go left and right, and his plane fly high in the sky. Tim was happy, and he played with Sue every day. They both had fun with their toys, and they were not jealous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little bee named Buzz. Buzz lived in a hive with all his bee friends. They all worked hard to make sweet honey. But one day, Buzz felt bitter and sad. Buzz said to his friend, "I want to quit. I don't want to make honey anymore." His friend tried to help him feel better. "Let's play a game," his friend said. They played together, and Buzz started to feel happy again. The next day, Buzz went back to the hive. He didn't want to quit anymore. He worked with his friends to make the sweetest honey they ever made. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy pistol. He would run around the house and pretend to be a cowboy. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. "Hi Sam," said Tim. "Do you want to play with my toy pistol?" Sam thought for a moment and said, "I recommend we play with something else. My mom said pistols are not good to play with." Tim agreed and decided to make a delicious snack instead. They went to the kitchen and made peanut butter and jelly sandwiches. They ate their sandwiches and talked about their favorite things. In the end, Tim and Sam had a great day playing together. They learned that it's important to listen to the recommendations of others, and that there are many fun things to do without playing with toy pistols. And they both agreed that peanut butter and jelly sandwiches are delicious!
