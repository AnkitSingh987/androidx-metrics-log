Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a fancy dress that she loved to wear. One day, she saw a pretty stamp on the table. It was a picture of a beautiful flower. Lily wanted to put the stamp on a letter for her friend, Sam. Lily said, "Mom, can I use this stamp for Sam's letter?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can. But don't miss the mailbox when you put the letter inside." Lily smiled and put the stamp on the letter. She was so excited to send it to her friend. Lily walked to the mailbox with her mom. She held the letter tightly in her hand. When they got to the mailbox, Lily opened the door and put the letter inside. She made sure she didn't miss the mailbox. Lily was happy that her friend would get the pretty flower stamp and her letter soon.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to run fast. He would run all day, every day. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam liked to make pretty prints with his paint. Sam was good at it. Tim was jealous of Sam's prints. One day, Tim and Sam went to the park. Tim saw a big hill and wanted to run up it. Sam wanted to make prints on the hill. Tim got mad. He did not want Sam to make prints on the hill. Tim wanted to be the best at something. Tim ran up the hill very fast. He did not look where he was going. Sam called out to Tim, but Tim did not listen. Tim tripped on a rock and fell down. Tim got hurt very bad. Sam was sad. Now, Tim could not run anymore, and the hill had no pretty prints.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a small cat with incredible soft skin. The cat was stuck in a tree and could not get down. Lily wanted to help the cat, so she tried to think of a way to manage getting the cat down from the tree. Lily asked her friend, Tom, "Can you help me get the cat down?" Tom said, "Yes, let's use a long stick to reach the cat." They found a long stick and managed to get the cat down from the tree. The cat was very happy and thanked Lily and Tom. They both learned that working together can help solve problems. The moral of the story is that teamwork makes everything easier.

One day, a little rabbit named Remy was playing with his friends in the forest. They were running and jumping, and having a lot of fun. Remy saw a soft spot on the ground and lay down to rest. He was tired after playing so much. Remy's friend, a small bird named Beaky, flew down next to him. Beaky said, "Remy, what is that white stuff on the ground?" Remy looked and saw white powder on the ground. "I don't know," said Remy, "but it's difficult to see where it came from." Beaky had an idea. "Let's follow the powder and see where it leads!" So Remy and Beaky followed the white powder. They walked and walked until they found a big bag of powder. A kind squirrel was using it to make cookies for all the forest animals. The squirrel saw Remy and Beaky and said, "Hello, friends! Would you like a cookie?" Remy and Beaky smiled and said, "Yes, please!" They ate the yummy cookies and thanked the squirrel. Then they went back to play with their friends, their tummies full and happy.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Sam. He was three years old. Sam liked to play hide and seek. One day, his mommy took him to a red hotel. Sam was so excited and his mommy said, "Let's go hide and seek inside the hotel!" Sam looked around and saw lots of fun places to hide. He ran and hid behind a big red couch. His mommy said, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looked everywhere, but she couldn't find Sam. Finally, she saw Sam behind the red couch with a big smile on his face. His mommy said, "There you are! I can't believe I didn't find you right away! You're very good at hide and seek!" Then they both laughed and hugged each other. Sam was so happy and he couldn't wait to go back to the red hotel and play hide and seek again.
