Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Mouse and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a small mouse. They lived in a house. The cat liked to chase the mouse, but the mouse was very fast. They played all day, but the mouse always felt safe. One day, the mouse found a tip of cheese. The mouse was happy and wanted to eat it. But the big cat saw the cheese too. The cat said, "Surrender the cheese, or I will catch you." The mouse did not want to give up the cheese, but he did not want to be caught. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came into the house. The dog wanted the cheese too. The cat and the mouse were scared. They decided to work together to keep the cheese safe. The cat and the mouse hid the cheese from the dog. The dog could not find it and left the house. The cat and the mouse were happy and shared the cheese. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toys and laugh all day. One day, Lucy found a big clock in her house. She did not know what it was, so she decided to examine it. As Lucy looked at the clock, she saw its hands going round and round. She thought it was a game. So, she called her friend, Timmy. Timmy came over and said, "What is it, Lucy?" Lucy said, "I found a big clock. Let's play with it!" Timmy and Lucy played with the clock, moving its hands and watching them spin. They laughed and had lots of fun. They learned that the clock helps them know what time it is. From that day on, Lucy and Timmy always knew when it was time to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little caterpillar named Catty. Catty had a big friend named Doggy. They liked to spend time together, playing and laughing. One day, Catty and Doggy were playing in the garden. Catty said, "Let's go to the big tree!" Doggy nodded, and they went to the tree. But when they got there, Catty saw a big, scary bird. Catty was scared and hid behind Doggy. Doggy said to the bird, "Please don't hurt my friend Catty." The bird looked sorry and said, "I didn't mean to scare you. I thought you were a bug I could eat. I'm sorry." Catty came out from behind Doggy and said, "It's okay. We can all be friends." From that day on, Catty, Doggy, and the big bird spent their days playing and having fun together in the garden. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run fast. He liked to play with his red ball. One day, Tim's mom took him to the store. Tim was happy to go to the store with his mom. At the store, Tim saw a big clock. The clock had a long arm and a short arm. Tim liked to watch the arms move. He wanted to play with the clock. Tim's mom said, "No, Tim. We need to buy food." Tim did not listen. He ran fast to the clock. He tried to strike the clock with his red ball. The clock fell down and broke. Tim's mom was sad. They had to pay for the broken clock. They could not buy food. Tim felt bad for not listening to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike. He knew that he had to wear a helmet to be safe. One day, Tim's mom told him to choose a new helmet from the store. At the store, Tim saw many helmets. He saw a red one, a blue one, and a yellow one. Then, he saw a great big green helmet. It was the best one! Tim chose the great green helmet and put it on his head. Tim rode his bike with his new helmet every day. One day, while riding, he fell off his bike. But he did not get hurt because he was wearing his great green helmet. Tim was happy he chose the right helmet. The moral of the story is to always wear a helmet to be safe.
