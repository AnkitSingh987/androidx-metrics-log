Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and the Cushion

Once upon a time, a little girl named Amy had a big, soft cushion. She loved her cushion very much. One day, she saw her mom write on a paper. Amy wanted to write too. So, she took a pen and wrote on her cushion. Amy's mom saw her write on the cushion and said, "Amy, we don't write on cushions. We write on paper." Amy felt sad. She didn't know that. Her mom gave her a paper to write on, but Amy wanted her cushion to be clean again. Just then, Amy's dad came home with a big surprise. He had a new cushion! It was the only one available in the store. He said, "I saw this cushion and thought you would like it." Amy was so happy! She hugged her dad and promised to only write on paper from now on.

Once upon a time, there was a monkey who lived in a big tree. One day, the monkey saw a butterfly and wanted to catch it. He jumped from the tree and tried to catch the butterfly with his hands. The monkey was not able to catch it and fell down. A boy saw the monkey fall and ran to help him. The boy said, "Are you okay, Mr. Monkey? I saw you fall." The monkey looked at the boy and said, "I am sorry I fell. I just wanted to catch the butterfly." The boy smiled and said, "It's okay, Mr. Monkey. I will help you catch the butterfly." The monkey and the boy tried to catch the butterfly together. They had lots of fun playing and running around. At the end of the day, they caught the butterfly and let it go. The monkey and the boy became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lucy. She had a magic pocket in her dress. She loved to help her friends. One day, Lucy's friend Tim lost his toy. Tim cried and said, "Lucy, please help me find my toy." Lucy smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." She put her hand in her magic pocket and said the magic word "vanish." Suddenly, the toy appeared in her hand. Tim was so happy and hugged Lucy. He said, "Thank you, Lucy. You are the best friend ever." Lucy smiled and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a silly owl. The owl lived in a big tree. The tree was in a pretty park. Kids played there every day. One day, a little girl lost her red ball. The ball rolled under the tree. The girl looked up and saw the silly owl. She said, "Please, Mr. Owl, can you help me find my ball?" The owl nodded and looked around. The silly owl found the red ball. He gave it to the little girl. She said, "Thank you, Mr. Owl!" The girl and the owl became good friends. They played together every day in the pretty park.

Once upon a time, there was a little fly. This fly was not like other flies. It was a disgusting fly. It liked to play in the dirt and make a mess. One day, the fly found a big pile of dirt. It was so excited! The fly decided to bury itself in the dirt. It dug and dug, going deeper and deeper into the pile. After a while, the fly got tired. It decided to take a nap in its new dirt bed. When it woke up, the fly was happy to see that it was still in the pile of dirt. The fly flew away, ready to find more dirt to play in. And the disgusting little fly lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben are playing in the garden. They see a big hole in the ground. They wonder what is inside. "Maybe there are bugs in the hole," Lily says. "Or maybe there are treasures," Ben says. They decide to look in the hole. They bend down and put their faces close to the hole. They smell something bad. "Yuck, what is that?" Lily says. They see a brown and green thing in the hole. It looks like a rotten apple. "Maybe it is a treasure," Ben says. He reaches his hand in the hole and grabs the thing. He pulls it out and shows it to Lily. "Look, it is a treasure!" he says. He bites the thing. He makes a face. He spits it out. "Blech, it tastes bad!" he says. Lily laughs. She says, "That is not a treasure, that is a bad apple. You should not eat things from the hole. They are dirty and bad." Ben says, "You are right, Lily. That was a bad idea. Let's go and find something else to play with." They throw the bad apple back in the hole. They run away from the hole. They look for something fun and good to play with. They are happy.
