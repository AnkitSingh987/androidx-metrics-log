Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Monkey

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there were many different animals. One day, a little monkey named Max wanted to find a new friend. He asked his mom, "Where can I find a new friend?" His mom said, "I recommend you go to the river. You will find new friends there." So, Max went to the river. On his way, he saw a big shadow. He was scared, but he wanted to be brave. He thought, "Maybe this is my new friend." He went closer to the shadow, but it was a big, mean crocodile! The crocodile said, "I want to eat you, little monkey." Max was very scared now. He remembered his mom's words and knew he should have listened to her. The big, mean crocodile ate Max. From that day on, all the animals in the jungle learned to listen to their parents' recommendations.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a patient reindeer named Remy. Remy had many friends who played with him. They liked to run, jump, and laugh together. Remy was always nice and waited for his friends to catch up when they played. One day, Remy and his friends saw a big, tall tree with a pretty, shiny star at the top. They all wanted the star to play with. Remy said, "Let's ask for help to get the star." So, they all closed their eyes and prayed, "Please help us get the pretty star." A little bird heard them pray and flew down to help. The bird said, "I can help you get the star." Remy and his friends were so happy. The bird flew up, took the star, and gave it to Remy. They all said, "Thank you, little bird!" Remy and his friends played with the star all day. They were very happy and thankful for the help they got. And from that day on, they always remembered to be patient and pray when they needed help.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to point at things and say their names. One day, Mia found a big book with many spells inside. She wanted to try one of the spells, but she didn't know how to read yet. Mia went to her friend, Tom, and asked, "Can you help me read this spell?" Tom was a selfish boy and didn't want to help Mia. He said, "No, I will not help you. I want to keep all the spells for myself." Mia was sad, but she didn't give up. She asked her mom to teach her how to read. After some days, Mia learned how to read the spell. She pointed at the book and read the spell out loud. Suddenly, a big pile of toys appeared in front of her. Mia was very happy and decided to share the toys with all her friends, including Tom. When Tom saw how kind Mia was, he felt bad for being selfish. He said, "I'm sorry, Mia. I will help you next time." From that day on, Tom was no longer selfish, and he and Mia became best friends. They both learned that sharing and helping each other is much better than keeping things for themselves.

One day, a big bird was in the sky. The bird saw a tree with ash. The tree was hurt from a fire. The bird felt sad for the tree. The bird wanted to help the tree. The bird said, "Tree, I will help you. I will struggle to find water for you." The tree said, "Thank you, bird." The bird flew away to find water. The bird looked and looked, but it was hard. In the end, the bird could not find water. The tree stayed hurt and sad. The bird felt bad too. The moral of the story is that we should try to help, but sometimes we cannot fix everything.

Once upon a time there was a chicken who wanted to work. She found a job at a soft farm. She was so excited that she ran around the farm, looking for something to do. One day she found a farmer and asked him, "What can I do?" The farmer smiled and said, "I need someone to scratch the ground. You can do it if you like." The chicken agreed and started to work. Every day the chicken went to the farmer and scratched the ground for him. The farmer was so pleased with the chicken's work that he gave her some extra food. The chicken was so happy that she could work and eat more food. She thanked the farmer for the soft ground and delicious food. The farmer was delighted and said, "You are a wonderful worker. I'm glad to have you here." The chicken smiled and kept working.
