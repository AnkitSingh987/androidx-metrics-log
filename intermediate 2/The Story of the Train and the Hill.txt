Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Train and the Hill

One day, a red train and a blue train were on the tracks. The red train wanted to go fast, but the blue train wanted to go slow. They could not agree on what to do. The red train said, "I want to go fast!" The blue train said, "No, I want to go slow." As they talked, they saw steam coming from a distant hill. They looked at each other and decided to go see what was making the steam. They went closer and closer until they saw a big pot of soup on a fire. A kind man was cooking the soup. He saw the trains and said, "Hello! I am making soup. Would you like some?" The red train and the blue train both agreed to try the soup. They forgot about their disagreement and enjoyed the soup together. From that day on, they learned to work together and became good friends.

Sara and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to ride on the big cart that had wheels and a handle. They took turns to push and pull each other on the cart. It was fun and fast. But it was also cold. The wind was blowing and the sky was gray. Sara and Ben shivered in their coats. They wished they had gloves and hats. "Let's go home soon," Sara said. "I'm hungry and cold." "Me too," Ben said. "But let's have one more ride on the cart. I'll push you this time." Sara agreed. She climbed on the cart and held the handle. Ben pushed her hard. The cart went faster and faster. Sara laughed and screamed. But then, something bad happened. The cart hit a rock on the path. The cart tipped over and Sara fell off. She landed on the ground and scraped her knee. It hurt a lot. She cried and cried. Ben ran to her and helped her up. He saw the blood on her knee. He felt sorry and scared. "I'm sorry, Sara," he said. "Are you okay?" Sara shook her head. She was not okay. She was hurt and cold and sad. "Let's go home now," she said. "I need a band-aid and a hug." Ben nodded. He took her hand and they walked home. They left the cart behind. They didn't want to play with it anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a big house with her mommy and daddy. Lily was very popular in her school because she was kind and always shared her toys. Today, Lily was going to open a new toy her mommy bought for her. She was very excited. She ran to her mommy and said, "Mommy, can I open my new toy today?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, Lily, you can open your new toy today. But remember to share it with your friends." Lily opened her new toy and it was a pretty doll. She loved it so much. She played with her doll all day and shared it with her friends. Everyone was happy and had a great time. And Lily learned that sharing made her even more popular with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a funny cow named Molly. Molly lived in a big red barn on a farm. She had many friends who also lived in the barn. They all liked to play together. One day, Molly saw a new animal outside the barn. It was a little duck. The duck said, "Hi, I am Ducky. Can I play with you?" Molly said, "Yes, you can play with us. You belong with us in the barn." Molly and Ducky had a lot of fun playing in the barn with their friends. They laughed and played all day long. And from that day on, Ducky knew that he belonged in the big red barn with his funny friend Molly the cow.

Once upon a time, there was a little bee named Buzzy. Buzzy was very busy. Every day, Buzzy would fly around and visit many plants. Buzzy loved to help the plants. One day, Buzzy found a new plant. This plant was sad. Buzzy wanted the plant to be happy. Buzzy had an idea. "I will accept this plant as my friend," thought Buzzy. Buzzy knew that being a friend would make the plant happy. Buzzy visited the plant every day. Buzzy helped the plant grow big and strong. The plant was now happy. Buzzy and the plant were best friends. And they lived happily ever after.
