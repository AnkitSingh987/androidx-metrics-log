Category: Intermediate
Title: The Stable and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a stable. In the stable, there were many animals. They liked to play and have fun. One day, a little bird came to the stable. The bird liked to sing. The animals liked the bird's song. The animals thought the bird was important. They wanted the bird to sing every day. The bird was happy to sing for them. The stable was a happy place with the bird's song. One day, the bird had to leave the stable. The animals were sad. They knew the bird was important. They hoped the bird would come back. And one day, the bird came back and sang for the animals again. The stable was happy once more.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a long bench. The bench was always available for people to sit on. One sunny day, a little girl named Lucy and her mom went to the park. They wanted to sit on the bench, but it was not there. They were very surprised. Lucy and her mom looked around the park to find the bench. They saw a big, strong ant carrying the bench on its back. The ant was taking the bench to its ant friends. Lucy and her mom could not believe their eyes. They thought ants could not carry big things like a bench. Lucy and her mom followed the ant to see where it was going. The ant took the bench to a group of ants who were having a party. The ants were happy to have a bench to sit on. Lucy and her mom smiled and decided to let the ants keep the bench. They sat on the grass and enjoyed the day, happy that they saw something unexpected.

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a witch. She had a big hat and a long nose. The witch loved to play with a ball. She would kick it high into the sky and watch it come back down. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim saw the witch kicking the ball. He wanted to play too. So, he went up to the witch and asked, "Can I play with you?" The witch smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" Tim and the witch kicked the ball back and forth. They laughed and had so much fun. All the kids in the town saw them play and joined in. The witch was happy to have new friends. From that day on, the witch and the kids played together every day, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big, mighty dog named Max went to the grocery store. He wanted to buy food to make a mix for his friend's birthday. Max was very excited. He walked down the store aisles and picked up all the things he needed. While Max was shopping, he met a little cat named Lily. Lily was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Max. "I can't find my toy," said Lily. "I lost it in the grocery store." Max wanted to help Lily, so they looked for the toy together. They looked in every aisle, but they couldn't find the toy. Then, they heard a noise near the mix aisle. They went there and found Lily's toy stuck in a big mix box. Max used his mighty strength to get the toy out. Lily was very happy. They both finished shopping and went home. Max and Lily became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a weird temple with his mom. The temple was big and had many colors. Tim was happy to see the temple. Inside the temple, Tim saw a talking bird. The bird said, "Hello, Tim!" Tim was surprised and asked, "Can you talk?" The bird said, "Yes, I can talk. I live in this temple." Tim and the bird talked and became friends. They played together in the temple. The weird temple was a fun place for Tim and his new friend.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her white toy bunny. She was very happy. She saw her friend Tom playing with his toy truck. Lily wanted to play with Tom, so she took her bunny and went to him. "Can I play with you, Tom?" asked Lily. Tom looked upset. "No, my truck is broken, and I can't fix it," he said. Lily felt sad for Tom and wanted to help him. Lily had an idea. She thought if they put the bunny in front of the truck, it would prevent the truck from moving and they could play together. So, they put the white bunny in front of the broken truck. The truck didn't move, and they started playing together. Tom was not upset anymore. They had fun playing together with the white bunny and the truck. They learned that helping each other can solve problems and make them happy.
