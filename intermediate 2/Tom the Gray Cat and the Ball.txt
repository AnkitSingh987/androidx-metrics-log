Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Gray Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Tom. Tom loved to play in the kitchen with his friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to open the refrigerator and look at all the food inside. One day, Sue found a big, green ball in the refrigerator. She took it out and showed it to Tom. "Let's play with it!" Sue said. Tom was very excited. They took turns rolling the ball on the floor and trying to squeeze it with their hands and paws. But the ball was too big and hard to squeeze. After playing for a while, Sue's mom came into the kitchen. She laughed when she saw the big, green ball. "That's not a ball, Sue. That's a watermelon!" she said. Sue and Tom looked at each other and laughed too. They put the watermelon back in the refrigerator and found a soft, small ball to play with instead. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a lively dog named Max went for a walk. Max liked to play and run. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But Max was a dog and dogs can't climb trees. Max was sad and started to complain. Max's friend, a bird named Tom, heard Max complain. Tom wanted to help Max. Tom had an idea. He told Max about a test. If Max could pass the test, he could climb the tree. Max was excited and wanted to try. Max tried the test. He jumped and jumped, but he couldn't climb the tree. Max was sad again. But then, Tom showed Max a secret. Tom showed Max that he could fly! Max was surprised. He didn't know dogs could fly. Max and Tom flew up to the tree and played together. Max was happy he passed the test and could fly with Tom.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tree. This tree was very old and big. Many things happened near this tree. One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the tree. Tim said to Spot, "This tree is so old! I wonder what things happened here." Spot barked and wagged his tail. They sat under the tree and looked at the sky. They saw birds flying and clouds moving. Suddenly, they heard a voice. It was a little bird. The bird said, "I can tell you what happened here. Many years ago, this tree was a home for many animals. They played and ate here. It was a happy place." Tim and Spot smiled and thanked the bird. They knew that this ancient tree had seen many happy things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She was three years old and very alert. One day, Jane went to the park with her mom. She was so excited that she wanted to explore. When they got to the park, Jane wanted to jump on the swing. But her mom said, "No, you need to remove your vest before you can swing." But Jane didn't want to. She did not want to take off her vest. So, Jane got frustrated. She started to cry and said, "No! I want to keep it on!" But her mom said, "No, Jane. You need to remove your vest in order to swing. It's not safe otherwise!" Finally, after a few minutes of crying, Jane agreed to take off her vest. She removed her vest and went on to have a lot of fun in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sam. She was a beautiful girl and had a very special clock. Every day, Sam would wind the clock up and it would reverse. It was the most beautiful clock that Sam had ever seen. One day, Sam winded the clock up, but this time instead of reversing, it kept spinning faster and faster. She watched in amazement, until it stopped. Sam was so excited that the clock had not reversed this time. The moral of the story is that sometimes it's better not to repeat the same things over and over again. Changing things once in a while can bring about something beautiful.
