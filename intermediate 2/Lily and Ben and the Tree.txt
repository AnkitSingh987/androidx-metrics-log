Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Tree

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big tree with many branches. They want to climb the tree. "Look, Ben, I can stretch my arm and reach that branch!" Lily says. She grabs the branch and pulls herself up. "Wow, Lily, you are good at climbing!" Ben says. He tries to stretch his arm too, but he is too short. He cannot reach the branch. "I can help you, Ben. Give me your hand!" Lily says. She leans down and holds Ben's hand. She helps him climb up to the branch. "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend!" Ben says. He smiles and hugs Lily. They sit on the branch and look around. They see many things. They see birds and flowers and cars and people. They see the sun and the clouds and the sky. "Everything looks different from up here!" Lily says. She points at the things she sees. "Yes, it does. It is like a test. Can you name all the things you see?" Ben says. He likes to learn new words. Lily and Ben take turns to name the things they see. They have fun and learn a lot. They are happy on the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to tell everyone what to do. One day, Kitty saw a little bird named Birdy. Birdy was playing with a towel. Kitty said, "Birdy, mind me! Give me the towel now!" Birdy looked sad but gave the towel to Kitty. Kitty took the towel and walked away. Later, Kitty saw Birdy crying. Kitty felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Birdy. Let's play with the towel together." Birdy smiled, and they played and had fun. Kitty learned not to be bossy, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. Many people would go there to watch games and have fun. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the stadium with his family. They were very excited to be there. As they sat in their seats, Tim noticed a little bird flying around the stadium. The bird was very persistent. It kept flying and flying, trying to find a place to land. The bird was very tired, but it did not give up. Finally, the bird found a spot to rest on a tree near the stadium. Tim was happy to see that the bird was okay. He clapped his hands and smiled. The bird looked at Tim and chirped, as if to say thank you. From that day on, Tim always looked for the bird whenever he went to the stadium.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were spies and shoot bad guys with their fingers. They hid behind the slide and the swings and made noises like guns. "Bang! Bang! You're dead!" Lila shouted at a squirrel. "No, I'm not! You missed!" Ben said, pretending to be the squirrel. They laughed and ran to find another target. But then they saw a big white bird on the grass. It had long legs and a long neck and a long beak. It looked very graceful and calm. "Wow, look at that bird!" Lila said. "It's so pretty!" "Let's shoot it!" Ben said, raising his finger. "No, don't!" Lila said, grabbing his arm. "That's a swan. Swans are nice. They don't hurt anyone. They like peace." "Peace?" Ben asked, confused. "Yes, peace. Peace means no fighting, no shooting, no hurting. Peace means being kind and happy and quiet. Swans like peace. And we should too." Lila smiled at Ben and he smiled back. They lowered their fingers and watched the swan. It flapped its wings and made a soft sound. Then it swam away on the pond. "Bye, swan!" Lila and Ben said. "Thank you for showing us peace!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved the colour purple. She had purple ribbons in her hair, and a purple dress from her Grandma. Her favourite thing of all, though, was her purple passport! Every morning she would gaze out the window and dream of the wonderful places she could go with it one day. One day she decided to take a journey. She took her trusty purple passport and set out to explore a new country. As she was walking, she stopped to admire the view. Suddenly, she noticed a little puppy sitting alone in the corner. The puppy looked sad, so the little girl softly said hello. The puppy looked up with hope in its eyes. The girl smiled, gave the puppy a hug, and soon they were best friends. She knew that the puppy could not go with her on her journey, so the little girl promised to bring it a special souvenir. After that day, the little girl never forgot the importance of being kind. There will always be people in the world who need your help, and taking a few moments to pause, smile, and lend a helping hand can make a huge difference.
