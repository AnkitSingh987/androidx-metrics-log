Category: Intermediate
Title: The Porch and the Song

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there was a small porch. On the porch lived a happy frog named Fred. Fred loved to jump and play all day long. One sunny day, Fred met a shy bird named Betty. Betty was scared to sing. Fred wanted to help his new friend. He said, "Betty, you can do it! I encourage you to sing a pretty song." Betty listened to Fred and started to sing. Her song was beautiful! They played and sang on the tidy porch all day long. They became the best of friends, and they encouraged each other to be happy and brave.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ancient dragon. He lived in a cave on a tall hill. Every day, he would fly around and play with his friends, the birds. One day, the dragon saw a girl crying near the hill. He flew down to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I lost my toy. I can't find it." The dragon wanted to help her, so he said, "I will help you solve this problem. Let's look for your toy together." As they looked for the toy, they found a big, shiny egg. The girl was happy and said, "This is not my toy, but it's so pretty!" The dragon said, "Let's take it home and see what's inside." They took the egg home and waited for it to open. The next day, the egg cracked open, and out came a baby dragon! The girl was so happy to have a new friend. The ancient dragon said, "Now you have a new toy and a new friend!" They all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a nice team of friends. They loved to play together. They were happy when they were with each other. One day, they decided to play a fun game of catch. "Can you throw the ball to me?" asked Tom, with a big smile on his face. "Yes, I can!" said Sue, as she threw the ball to him. They all laughed and smiled as they played. As they played, more friends came to join the team. They all took turns throwing and catching the ball. They were all smiling and having a great time. "Playing with friends is so much fun!" said Tom. "Yes, it is!" agreed Sue. They all smiled and played until it was time to go home. At the end of the day, the nice team of friends said goodbye to each other. They were all very happy and couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a thoughtful little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to eat mushrooms. One day, while he was looking for mushrooms, he met a small rabbit named Rosie. "Hi Rosie!" said Sam. "Do you want to help me find mushrooms?" "Yes, Sam!" said Rosie. They looked and looked, but they could not find any mushrooms. They started to miss the yummy taste of mushrooms. Then, they saw an old turtle named Tim. Tim was wise and knew where to find the best mushrooms. "Tim, can you help us find mushrooms?" asked Sam and Rosie. "Of course," said Tim. "But first, you must promise to share the mushrooms with others in the forest." Sam and Rosie agreed. Tim showed them a secret place with lots of mushrooms. Sam and Rosie shared the mushrooms with all their friends. They learned that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally was an adventurous girl who loved to play outside. One sunny day, Sally found a big box in her room. She opened the box and saw a magic shoe. "Wow!" said Sally, "I want to print my name on the magic shoe!" Sally found a marker and printed her name on the shoe. She put the shoe on her foot and something amazing happened. The shoe started to talk! "Hello, Sally!" said the shoe, "I am a magic shoe and I can take you on an adventure!" Sally was so excited. She held the shoe's hand and they went on a fun adventure together. They ran, jumped, and laughed all day long. At the end of the day, Sally and the magic shoe became the best of friends.
