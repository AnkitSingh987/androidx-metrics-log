Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin and the Fish

One sunny day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the big sea. Fin wanted to be famous. He saw a net and thought, "If I jump in the net, I will be famous!" A wise old fish named Mr. Whiskers saw Fin. He swam over and said, "Fin, don't go in the net! It's not safe!" Fin felt sad, but Mr. Whiskers encouraged him. "You can be famous in other ways. Help others and be kind." Fin listened to Mr. Whiskers. He swam around the sea, helping his friends. He helped a crab find his home, and he cheered up a sad jellyfish. Soon, everyone knew Fin as the kindest fish in the sea. The other sea animals loved Fin. They told stories about how he helped them. Fin became famous for being kind and helpful, not for jumping in the net. The moral of the story is: You can become famous by being kind and helpful to others.

One day, a girl named Sue went with her mom to a big store. Sue's mom wanted to buy a pretty bracelet for her friend's birthday. Sue was very excited to go to the store. While they were at the store, Sue saw a toy car. She asked her mom, "Can I have the toy car, please?" Her mom said, "No, Sue. We are here to buy a bracelet, not a toy car." Sue was sad but she knew her mom was right. Sue was a little careless and she dropped the bracelet. It broke into pieces! Sue and her mom were surprised. A nice store man came and said, "Don't worry. We can fix the bracelet for you." Sue and her mom were happy. They thanked the store man and went home.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to the park. They both had a big tube of candy. Tim was very happy to share his candy with Sue. But Sue was impatient and did not want to wait. Sue tried to take all the candy from the tube. Tim got sad and tried to get the tube back. They both pulled on the tube very hard. The tube was not strong and it broke. All the candy fell on the ground. Tim and Sue were very sad. They could not eat the candy anymore. They learned that being impatient can make things bad.

Once upon a time, a little child named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to meet new friends. Tim walked and walked until he saw a big tree. Under the tree, Tim saw a small cat. The cat said, "Meow! I am lost. Can you help me find my home?" Tim was happy to help. He held the cat and they walked together. After a while, they found the cat's home. The cat's mom was grateful. She said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome!" Now Tim had a new friend, and they played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mommy. The sun was shining, and the sunshine was warm on his face. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to play under it. Under the tree, Tim found a yummy apple. He picked it up and showed it to his mommy. "Look, mommy! Yummy apple!" he said. His mommy smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, that's a very yummy apple. You can have it." As they sat under the tree, eating the yummy apple, Tim saw a little bird. The bird flew close to him and said, "Hello, Tim! Can I have some of your yummy apple?" Tim broke off a piece and gave it to the bird. They all enjoyed the sunshine and the yummy apple together.

Once upon a time, a clean little cat named Kitty went for a walk. She liked to blink her big eyes at the pretty flowers. The sun was warm and the air was sweet. As Kitty walked, she saw an ice-cream truck. The ice-cream man smiled at her. He gave her a big ice-cream cone with her favorite flavor, vanilla. Kitty was very happy. Kitty licked her ice-cream as she walked back home. She blinked at the birds in the trees and the butterflies in the grass. When she got home, she was a very happy and clean little cat.
