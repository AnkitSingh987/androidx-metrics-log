Category: Intermediate
Title: The Car and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an old car. This car had lots of gear inside. The gear helped the car go fast. Everyone in the town loved the old car. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the car. He wanted to play with the gear. But his mom said, "No, Tim. You can't play with the gear. You are too small." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. Later, when no one was looking, Tim went to the old car. He started to play with the gear. Suddenly, the car began to move. It went faster and faster. Tim was scared. The car hit a big tree. The old car was broken, and the gear was gone. Tim was punished by his mom for not listening. The town people were sad because their old car was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. She was 3 years old and loved to eat apples. Every day, Emma would go to school to learn and play with her friends. One day, Emma noticed that all her friends seemed worried. She asked her teacher what was wrong. The teacher told her that the school was running out of apples! Emma was very worried. She knew that all the children loved to eat apples. She thought for a moment and then had an idea. She ran home and collected all the apples she could find and brought them back to school! When the other children saw all of the apples, they were so happy! Everyone had plenty of apples to eat. Emma smiled and knew she had done something good. She was very proud.

Once upon a time, there was a red bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to eat lemons. One day, he saw a big lemon tree with many lemons. He was very happy. Bobo flew to the tree and said, "Hello, tree! Can I have a lemon, please?" The tree did not answer. Bobo did not remember that trees cannot talk. He felt sad and did not know how to get a lemon. A little girl named Lily saw Bobo looking sad. She asked, "Why are you sad, Bobo?" Bobo told her about the lemons. Lily said, "I can help you get a lemon!" Lily climbed the tree and picked a big lemon for Bobo. Bobo was very happy and thanked Lily. They both laughed and played under the lemon tree. Bobo and Lily became best friends, and they always shared lemons together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that could zoom very fast. He loved to play with his car all day. But sometimes, Tim felt troubled because he didn't have any friends to play with. One sunny day, Tim took his toy car outside to play. He made the car zoom very fast on the sidewalk. Suddenly, the car zoomed too fast and went into the street. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. Just then, a girl named Sue came running to help. She picked up the toy car and gave it back to Tim. Sue told Tim that he should be careful and not let the car zoom into the street. Tim knew he made a mistake, but Sue didn't punish him. Instead, she asked if they could play together. Tim was so happy and surprised! From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends and played with the zooming car together.

Once upon a time, there was a nice cat. The cat lived in a small house made of lumber. The cat liked to play with a big ball of yarn. One day, the cat found a piece of paper. The cat wanted to fold the paper. So, the cat folded the paper into a small boat. The cat was happy and proud of the boat. The cat put the small boat on a puddle near the lumber house. The boat floated on the water. The cat played with the boat all day long. The cat had a fun day with the boat it made.

Jenny and Tom are friends. They like to play with dolls and cars. They share their toys and have fun. They love each other. One day, a new girl comes to their class. Her name is Lisa. She has long hair and a pretty dress. She brings a big teddy bear with her. Jenny and Tom want to see the teddy bear. They go to Lisa and say hello. "Hi, Lisa. We are Jenny and Tom. Can we play with your teddy bear?" Jenny asks. "Sure, you can play with my teddy bear. He is very soft and cuddly. His name is Mr. Fluffy. Do you want to hug him?" Lisa says. Jenny and Tom hug Mr. Fluffy. He is very soft and cuddly. They like him a lot. They smile at Lisa. They want to be friends with her. But Lisa does not smile back. She looks at Jenny and Tom with angry eyes. She does not want to share her teddy bear. She does not want to be friends with them. She is jealous of their love. She snatches Mr. Fluffy from their arms. She hugs him tight and says, "No, you can't play with my teddy bear. He is mine. Only mine. Go away. I don't like you." Jenny and Tom are sad and confused. They don't understand why Lisa is so mean. They look at each other and hold hands. They walk away from Lisa and her teddy bear. They still love each other. The end.
