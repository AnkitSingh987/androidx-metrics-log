Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lucy and her dog, Max. They were best friends and loved to play together. One sunny day, they went for a slow walk in the park, holding hands and wagging tails. In the park, they met a friendly squirrel. The squirrel was hungry and asked Lucy, "Please, can I have a peanut?" Lucy looked at Max and they both nodded. They wanted to help the squirrel. Lucy gave the squirrel a big peanut from her pocket. The squirrel was very happy and said, "Thank you!" Lucy and Max smiled and continued their slow walk in the park. They had a fun day, and the squirrel had a full belly.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to help his mom in the kitchen. His mom was making dough for bread. Tim saw the big bowl and wanted to carry it for her. "Mom, can I carry the dough?" he asked. Mom said, "Yes, Tim. But be careful, it's heavy!" Tim held the bowl with both hands and started to walk slowly. He felt proud that he could help his mom. But then, he tripped on a toy and the dough fell on the floor. Tim was very upset. He started to cry. His mom came over and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can make more dough." But Tim was still upset. He didn't want to help anymore. So, they didn't make any more bread that day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big parade. People were happy and excited. They were clapping and dancing. In the parade, there was a big, serious elephant. The elephant was strong and wise. One day, a little girl was playing near the parade. She was laughing and having fun. But then, she got too close to the parade and fell down. The people were scared. They did not know what to do. The serious elephant saw the little girl. He knew he had to save her. So, he stopped the parade and carefully picked her up with his long nose. The little girl was safe, and everyone was happy. The people learned that it is important to be careful and help each other.

One day, a girl named Jane wanted to test her new color pens. She had many colors, but there was one unknown color. Jane was very excited to see what the unknown color would look like. She started to draw a picture of a big tree with her color pens. She used green for the leaves, brown for the trunk, and yellow for the sun. Then, she used the unknown color to draw a big flower. When she was done, she saw that the flower was a very pretty color. Jane showed her picture to her mom. Her mom was surprised and said, "Wow, that flower is a beautiful color!" But then, something unexpected happened. The flower on the paper started to grow and became a real flower! Jane and her mom were so happy and amazed. They put the flower in a pot and it became their favorite flower in the house.

Tom and Sam are young boys who like to fish. They have a rod and a bucket. They go to the pond with their dad. They see many fish in the water. "Look, dad, a big fish!" Tom says. He throws his rod into the pond. He waits for the fish to bite. "Me too, me too!" Sam says. He throws his rod too. But he throws it too hard. He slips and falls into the pond. Splash! "Sam, are you okay?" Tom and dad ask. They run to help him. "I'm okay, I'm okay!" Sam says. He is wet and cold. He laughs. He holds his rod. He has a fish on it. "Wow, Sam, you caught a fish!" Tom says. He is happy for his brother. "Yay, I caught a fish!" Sam says. He is proud of himself. Dad hugs them both. He is proud of them too. "Let's go home and cook the fish," dad says. "You are both good fishermen." Tom and Sam nod. They are hungry and tired. They are happy. They had a fun day.
