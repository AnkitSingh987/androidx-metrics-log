Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Owner

Once upon a time there was a dog named Spot. Spot was the most playful dog in his neighbourhood. Spot often went out to play in the park, but one day something strange happened. Spot was running around when he found something strange in the park- a bag of sour candy. Spot didn't know where it had come from, but it sure smelled delicious! He picked up the bag and started to chew on the candy. Suddenly, Spot heard a loud voice coming from behind him. "Spot! What are you doing?!" Spot turned around to see his owner. Spot had never seen his owner so angry before. The owner asked Spot why he was eating the bag of sour candy. Spot could not explain why, but he knew he was in trouble. His owner told him that he had to leave the park right away. Spot sadly walked back home. From that day on, Spot was extra careful when running around in the park. He had learned his lesson that it was important to always ask his owner before eating something in the park!

Lily liked to wear her pink dress. It was her favorite dress. She wore it every day. She did not like to wash it. She said it was too hard. One day, Mommy said, "Lily, you have to wash your dress. It is very dirty. Look at the stains. You need soap and water to make it clean." Lily did not want to wash her dress. She was naughty. She ran away from Mommy. She hid under the bed. She said, "No, no, no. I do not want to wash my dress. I like it dirty." Mommy looked for Lily. She found her under the bed. She said, "Lily, come out. You have to wash your dress. If you do not wash it, you cannot wear it. You have to wear something else." Lily did not like that. She loved her dress. She did not want to wear something else. She came out from under the bed. She said, "Sorry, Mommy. I will wash my dress. Please help me." Mommy smiled. She said, "That's a good girl. Come on, let's go to the bathroom. I will show you how to use soap and water to make your dress clean and pretty." Lily and Mommy went to the bathroom. They washed the dress together. Lily learned how to use soap and water. She saw the stains go away. She was happy. She said, "Thank you, Mommy. I like my dress clean and pretty. I will wash it more often." Mommy hugged Lily. She said, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm proud of you. You are not naughty anymore. You are a good girl. Now you can wear your dress again."

One day, a wise old owl sat on a tree. He liked to gaze at the sky. He saw a small leak in a cloud. The cloud was sad because it could not hold its water. A little bird saw the sad cloud too. The bird wanted to help. The wise owl had an idea. He told the bird to find a big leaf. The bird flew and found the leaf. The bird took the leaf to the cloud. The cloud used the leaf to cover the leak. The cloud was happy and the sky was bright again. The wise owl and the little bird were happy to help.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Jack. Jack was three years old, and he liked to explore his neighborhood. One day, Jack was walking near his house when he heard a strange noise. He was worried, so he looked around to see what it was. Suddenly, he saw a small puppy! Jack didn't recognize the puppy, and he was very surprised. The puppy was also scared and worried. Jack felt bad for the puppy, so he whispered, "Hi! I'm Jack. Who are you?" The puppy didn't answer. It just looked at Jack with its big, brown eyes. Then, Jack noticed that the puppy had a collar. On the collar, it said 'Chloe'. Jack smiled. "Nice to meet you, Chloe!" he said to the puppy. Chloe seemed to recognize her name and cocked her head to the side. She was no longer scared or worried. Jack and Chloe became friends. From then on, Jack and Chloe were often seen taking walks and playing together in the neighborhood.
