Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Top

Once upon a time, there was a happy boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy top. Every day, he would spin the top and watch it go round and round. Tim would laugh and clap his hands as the top spun faster. One day, Tim wanted his friend, Sally, to play with the top too. But Sally didn't want to play. She said, "No, I refuse to play with the top. I want to play with my doll." Tim felt sad, but he kept playing with his top. Later, Sally saw how much fun Tim was having with the top. She changed her mind and said, "Tim, can I play with the top too?" Tim was happy to share his top with Sally. They played together, spinning the top and having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to sing. She had a microphone that she used every day. One day, Amy's mom made a spicy food for dinner. Amy ate it and felt her mouth get hot. She ran to her room, sad because she couldn't sing. Amy's mom saw her run to her room and knew she had to help. She went to the store and bought a special treat for Amy. When Amy's mom came back, she gave Amy the treat. It was a cold ice cream! Amy ate the ice cream, and her mouth felt better. She was so happy. Amy went back to her room and picked up her microphone. She started to sing again, but this time, she sang a new song. It was a song about the spicy food and the cold ice cream. As she sang, her mom listened and smiled. She knew that Amy would never quit singing, no matter what happened. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a horn. The horn was hidden in a big forest. Many animals lived in the forest. They were all friends. One day, a girl named Lily came to the forest. She found the hidden horn. Lily showed the horn to her new animal friends. The animals were happy to see the horn. They wanted to play with it. A rabbit named Hoppy said, "Let's have a party with the horn!" All the animals agreed. They invited everyone in the forest. At the party, Lily met a boy named Jack. They liked each other a lot. Lily said, "Jack, will you marry me?" Jack said, "Yes, I will marry you." The animals played music with the horn at the party. Lily and Jack danced and laughed. They were very happy. The animals were happy too. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big chair in the yard. The chair was brown, just like him! Spot wanted to sit on the chair. But, it was too high for him. He called his friend, Cat. "Cat, can you help me get on the chair?" he asked. Cat was very smart and had an idea. "Let's rush and find a box," said Cat. They ran fast and found a box. They put the box near the chair. Spot jumped on the box, and then he jumped on the brown chair. He was so happy! Now, Spot and Cat could sit together and enjoy the sun. They had a great day!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a humble cat named Tom. Tom had a big bowl. He loved to eat from his bowl every day. Tom seemed happy with his life. One day, Tom saw something new by his bowl. It was a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was eating from Tom's bowl too! Tom was very surprised. He did not know what to do. Tom thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. Instead of being mad, Tom shared his bowl with Jerry. They became best friends. They ate from the same bowl every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big temple in a small town. Many people went to the temple to feel happy. One day, a boy named Tim went to the temple with his mom and dad. While Tim was at the temple, he saw a dog with a funny hat. Tim felt foolish because he laughed at the dog. But the dog did not feel bad. The dog just wagged its tail and played with Tim. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog took off its hat, and it was not a dog at all! It was a little girl dressed like a dog. Tim was so surprised. The little girl laughed and said, "I fooled you!" Tim and the girl became good friends, and they played together at the temple every day.
