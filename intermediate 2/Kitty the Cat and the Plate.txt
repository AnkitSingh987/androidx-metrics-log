Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Plate

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to eat from her plate. Every day, she would wait for her friend, a little boy named Tim, to fill her plate with yummy food. One day, Kitty had a dream. In her dream, she saw a big plate filled with her favorite food. She was so happy! When Kitty woke up, she told Tim about her dream. "Tim, I had a dream about a big plate with lots of food!" Tim smiled and said, "Let's make your dream come true, Kitty!" Tim filled Kitty's plate with all her favorite foods. Kitty was so happy and ate everything on her plate. From that day on, Kitty and Tim always had fun eating together. And sometimes, Kitty still dreamed about her big plate of food.

One day, a little bunny named Ben went to the park. He saw his friend, a squirrel named Sam. Sam was eating a tasty nut. "Yum!" said Sam, "This nut is very tasty!" Ben wanted to do something fun. He had an idea! "Let's put on a show for our friends!" said Ben. Sam liked the idea and they started to plan their show. They were going to sing and dance for everyone. All their friends came to watch. Ben and Sam did their best singing and dancing. When they finished, everyone was so happy. They clapped their hands and paws. "Applaud, applaud!" said the little mouse, clapping her tiny hands. Ben and Sam were proud of their show. "Thank you for watching!" said Ben. "We hope you liked it!" Sam added. All their friends smiled and nodded. It was a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to play with her pretty veil. One day, she went to the park with her mom. Lily saw her friend, Sam, and she waved to him. Sam waved back and ran to play with Lily. Lily and Sam played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. They spun around and around on the merry-go-round. Lily started to feel dizzy from all the spinning. She held on tight to her veil, but it flew off her head. The veil flew high in the sky and landed on a tall tree. Lily and Sam tried to get it down, but they were too small. Lily's mom also tried, but she was not tall enough either. Lily felt very sad and cried. A big wind came and blew the veil even higher. It got stuck in the clouds, and no one could reach it. Lily went home with her mom, feeling very sad. She missed her pretty veil and wished she could have it back. But the veil was gone forever, and Lily learned to be more careful with her things.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved Sundays because he could play with his friends in the park. One Sunday, he saw his friend, Sam, with a big, red ball. Tim felt envious because he wanted a big, red ball too. Tim asked Sam, "Can I play with your ball?" Sam said, "Yes, but we need to delay our game. My mom wants me to eat lunch first." Tim agreed and waited for Sam to finish his lunch. While waiting, Tim saw a dog near the park. He wanted to play with the dog too. But the dog grabbed Sam's ball and ran away. Tim and Sam couldn't play with the ball anymore. They felt sad and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy computer. He would play all day, even when it was dark outside. One day, Tim's mom said it was time to bathe. Tim didn't want to stop playing, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. So, he took his toy computer with him to the bath. He thought it would be fun to play with it in the water. But something unexpected happened! When Tim put the toy computer in the water, it changed into a real computer! Tim was so surprised. He didn't know that his toy was magic. Now, Tim could play with his computer even more, but not in the bath. He learned that some things are better when they are not wet.
