Category: Intermediate
Title: Timmy and the Dog

Timmy loved to visit the park with his mom. He liked to swing, slide and run on the grass. But his favorite thing was to pet the dogs that came with their owners. He always asked his mom if he could say hello to the dogs, and she always said yes, but to be careful and polite. One day, he saw a big, fluffy dog with an elderly owner. The dog had a red collar and a long tail. Timmy thought the dog looked very friendly and soft. He ran to his mom and requested to pet the dog. His mom nodded and followed him. The elderly owner smiled when he saw Timmy. He said his dog's name was Max and that he was very gentle. He said Timmy could pet him if he wanted. Timmy reached out his hand and touched Max's fur. It felt warm and cozy. He stroked Max's head and ears, and Max licked his face. Timmy giggled and hugged Max. But then, something bad happened. Another dog, a small, black one, came running towards them. It barked loudly and snapped at Max. Max growled and barked back. The two dogs started to fight. Timmy was scared and let go of Max. He cried and ran to his mom. The elderly owner tried to pull Max away, but he was too weak. The other dog's owner came and shouted at him. He said Max was a bad dog and that he should not be in the park. He said he would call the police. Timmy felt sorry for Max and his owner. He knew Max was not a bad dog. He knew Max was just protecting him. He wanted to help them. He had an idea. He ran to the slide and climbed up. He saw a big stick on the ground. He grabbed it and slid down. He ran to the dogs and waved the stick. He shouted, "Stop! Go away! Leave Max alone!" The other dog saw the stick and got scared. It ran away, whimpering. Max stopped barking and wagged his tail. Timmy threw the stick and hugged Max again. Max licked his face. The elderly owner thanked Timmy for saving Max. He said Timmy was a brave and kind boy. He said he and Max were very grateful. He gave Timmy a cookie and a pat on the head. Timmy's mom was proud of him. She said he did a good thing. She said he and Max were friends. Timmy smiled and agreed. He said he loved Max. He said he wanted to visit him again. The elderly owner said he would love that. He said they would come to the park every day. Timmy was happy. He said goodbye to Max and his owner. He said he would see them tomorrow. He and his mom went home. He told his mom he had a great day. He said he learned a lot. He said he learned how to request, how to pet, and how to be a hero.

Once upon a time, there was a big, loud red truck. Whenever the truck drove by, the kids would stand near the window and shout, "Look! Look at the truck!" The truck kept going, until one day it stopped. Mason, a 3 year old, hopped out of the truck and ran to a nearby house. He knocked on the door and said, "Can I stay here?" A nice old woman opened the door and smiled. "Come on in," she said warmly. Mason's eyes lit up, and he followed the old woman inside. She showed him around her house and they had a nice chat. Mason stayed with the old woman for a few days, and they soon became friends. The old woman introduced Mason to her dog and taught him how to play games. She also taught him how to be careful and to use his mind. "Always think before you act," she said. The old woman was happy to have such a curious, smart little boy in her home. But soon, Mason had to go. He said goodbye to the old woman and hopped back in the big, loud red truck. The kids shouted again when it drove by, and Mason was happy to show them his new friend, the old woman.
