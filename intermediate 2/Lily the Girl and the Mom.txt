Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Mom

One day, a girl named Lily went to a fun event with her mom. There were lots of games, food, and nice people. Lily was very happy. At the event, Lily saw a harmless cat. She wanted to pet it. She asked her mom, "Can I pet the cat, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can. Be gentle." Lily went to pet the cat, but it was not a real cat! It was a big toy cat. Lily was surprised. She went to tell her mom, "Mom, the cat is not real! It's a toy!" Her mom laughed and said, "That's a funny twist, isn't it?"

Once upon a time, there was an adorable bunny. The bunny loved to hop all day long. He hopped in the grass, under the sun, and near the big tree. One day, while hopping, the bunny found a little cell. The cell was very small and round. The bunny was curious and decided to hop closer to the cell. As the bunny hopped closer, something unexpected happened! The little cell started to hop too! The bunny and the cell became friends and hopped together all day long. And that's how the adorable bunny found a new friend in the hopping cell.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a mysterious box. Inside the box, there was a lime. The lime was very cold. Lily did not know why the lime was so cold. She picked it up and said, "Why are you so cold, lime?" The lime did not talk back. Lily was sad, but she still liked the lime. She took it with her to play. They played hide and seek, and tag. Lily and the lime had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The lime started to glow. It turned into a magic lime! The lime said, "I can freeze things! Watch!" The lime froze a little puddle on the ground. Lily was very happy. Now she had a new friend and they could play freeze tag together.

One day, a tall man named Tom went for a walk. He wore a coat with a zip. Tom saw a big sign that said, "No walking on the grass." This was a law. Tom saw a dog on the grass. He said, "Dog, you can't be on the grass. It's a law." The dog looked at Tom and said, "I can't read. I didn't know." Just then, a cat came by. The cat said, "I can help. I know how to read." This was a big surprise! The cat read the sign to the dog. The dog said, "Thank you, cat." Now, the dog knew the law. He did not go on the grass anymore. Tom, the dog, and the cat became good friends. They all followed the law and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a big old house. In the house, there was an ancient book. This book had many stories in it. A little girl named Lily lived in the house. She loved to read the stories in the book. One day, Lily read a story about a magic frog. The frog lived in a pond near the house. Lily wanted to meet the frog. So, she went outside to find the pond. Lily found the pond and saw the magic frog. The frog told her a secret. If she read the stories out loud, they would come true. Lily was very happy. She went back to the house and read the stories every day. And they all came true.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run around with his friends. One day, Spot found a big box with a bow. He knew it was a surprise for someone. Spot was very excited and wanted to see what was inside. Spot called his friend, a happy cat named Fluffy. "Fluffy, come see the surprise!" he said. Fluffy came running and saw the big box. They both wanted to see what was inside, but they knew it was not for them. They tried to be good and not open the box. But they could not wait any longer. Spot and Fluffy opened the box and found a big, shiny ball. They played with the ball all day long. When it was time to go home, they knew they had done something wrong. They were sad and scared that no one would forgive them. Spot and Fluffy went to their friend, a wise old owl named Hoot. "Hoot, we did something bad. We opened the surprise and played with the ball. Will anyone forgive us?" they asked. Hoot thought for a moment and said, "If you say sorry and promise not to do it again, I am sure everyone will forgive you." Spot and Fluffy said sorry to their friends and promised not to open surprises again. Everyone forgave them and they all played with the big, shiny ball together. From that day on, Spot and Fluffy learned the importance of forgiveness and always tried to be good friends.
