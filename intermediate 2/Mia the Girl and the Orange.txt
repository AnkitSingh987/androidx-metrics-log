Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Orange

One day, a little girl named Mia found an orange stamp. She picked it up and saw a twisty picture on it. Mia was very happy and wanted to show her friend, Max. Mia ran to Max and said, "Look, Max! I found an orange stamp with a twisty picture!" Max looked at the stamp and smiled. "Let's use it to make pretty pictures!" he said. They took a big paper and started to make twisty pictures with the orange stamp. As they stamped, something unexpected happened. The twisty picture on the stamp changed into a twisty rainbow! "Wow!" said Mia. "Our stamp is magic!" Max laughed and said, "Let's make more twisty rainbows!" They played with the magic stamp all day, making twisty rainbows and having lots of fun.

One day, a big boat went to a port. The port was near the water. The boat had many people on it. One of the people was a kind and compassionate man named Tom. Tom saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Tom wanted to help Lily find her toy. They looked around the port together. Tom saw the toy near the water. He yelled, "Lily, I found your toy!" Lily was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom!" They both smiled and played with the toy at the port.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a red mill. The mill was near a big river. People went to the mill to make flour from wheat. The mill was very important to the town. One day, a girl named Sally went to the mill with her dad. They wanted to make flour for bread. Sally saw a big, red button on the wall. "Dad, can I push the button?" she asked. Her dad said, "No, Sally. That button is to prevent the mill from working if something goes wrong. We don't want to stop the mill right now." Sally listened to her dad and didn't push the button. They made flour and went home. The town was happy, and the red mill kept working. Sally learned how to prevent problems by listening to her dad.

Once upon a time, in a large palace, there lived a kind king. The king had a big garden with many flowers. He loved his garden and took care of it every day. One day, the king found a small seed in the garden. He thought, "I will raise this seed and see what grows." The king planted the seed in the ground and gave it water. He waited for days and days, but the seed did not grow. The king was sad, but he did not give up. He kept giving the seed water and waited some more. He knew that something special would grow from the seed. One day, the seed began to grow. It grew bigger and bigger, until it became a large, beautiful tree. The tree had many branches and pretty flowers. The king was very happy. He knew that raising the seed was worth the wait. From that day on, the large tree stood tall in the palace garden, and the king loved it very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a black dog named Sam. They loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Tim and Sam saw a poor bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Tim said to Sam, "We must help the bird." Sam wagged his tail and agreed. Tim and Sam took the poor bird home. They gave it food and water. Soon, the bird felt better. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim and Sam!" And they all played together, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a basketball. He loved to play with it every day. Tom felt lucky to have the basketball. One sunny day, Tom saw a girl named Sue. She was sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my toy." Tom wanted to help Sue. He said, "Let's look for your toy together." Tom and Sue looked and looked. Then, Tom remembered where he saw the toy. He said, "I saw your toy near the big tree!" They went to the big tree and found Sue's toy. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" They played with the basketball and Sue's toy together. They became good friends. The moral of the story is to always help others and share your toys.
