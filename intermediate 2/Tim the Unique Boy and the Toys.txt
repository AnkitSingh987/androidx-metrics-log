Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Unique Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a unique little boy named Tim. He had a big brain and loved to learn. Every day, he would play with his toys and find new ways to have fun. One sunny day, Tim found a twisty toy. He loved to twist and turn it with his hands. The twisty toy was very special because it could change into many shapes. Tim would twist the toy into a circle, a square, and even a star. Tim showed his twisty toy to his friends. They all thought it was amazing. They took turns twisting the toy into different shapes. Tim was happy to share his unique toy with his friends, and they all had a great time playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to design. One day, she found a big sheet. Lily wanted to make a dress with it. Lily asked her mom, "Can I design a dress with this sheet?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Lily was so happy. She started to work on the dress. As Lily was working, a big wind came. It blew the sheet away. Lily was frightened. She ran after the sheet but could not catch it. The sheet flew far away, and Lily could not find it. She was very sad. The dress she wanted to design was gone. Lily learned to be more careful next time.

Tom and Sue were friends. They liked to play in the woods. One day, they saw a big cave. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's go in the cave," Tom said. "Maybe we can find some treasure." "No, Tom, that is foolish," Sue said. "The cave is dark and scary. There might be bears or snakes or spiders. We should go back." But Tom did not listen. He ran into the cave. Sue followed him. She did not want him to get lost or hurt. They walked deeper and deeper into the cave. It was cold and wet and quiet. They could not see anything. They only had a small flashlight. "Tom, I don't like this," Sue said. "Let's go back. Please." But Tom did not listen. He saw something shiny in the corner. He thought it was gold. He ran to get it. But it was not gold. It was a trap. A big net fell on Tom and Sue. They were stuck. They could not move. "Help! Help!" they shouted. "Someone help us!" But no one heard them. They were too far away. They heard a loud roar. It was a bear. The bear lived in the cave. He was hungry. He smelled Tom and Sue. He came to eat them. The bear saw the net. He thought it was a gift. He thought someone had served him a tasty snack. He licked his lips. He opened his mouth. Tom and Sue were very scared. They wished they had never gone into the cave. They wished they had listened to Sue. They were very foolish. They said sorry to each other. But it was too late. The bear was ready to bite. He was very happy. He thanked whoever had served him. He said: "Yum, yum, yum!"

Sarah was a very curious little girl. She looked out of her bedroom window and saw an old gray tent in the backyard. She ran downstairs excited to explore it. Sarah got to the tent and stood there staring at it. She touched it and felt its texture. Then Sarah heard a voice, "It's a nice tent, do you like it?" Sarah turned to see who was talking and saw a big friendly looking bear! It continued, "You can go inside and explore it if you want." Sarah couldn't believe her eyes! She opened the tent and went in. Inside she could see the tent was all gray and had some old things inside. She had the best time ever exploring the tent. At the end of the day, Sarah thanked the bear and ran back inside her home. She looked out of her bedroom window one more time, at the old gray tent, thinking of all the fun they'd had together.
