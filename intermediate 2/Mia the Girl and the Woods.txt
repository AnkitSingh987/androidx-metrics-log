Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Woods

Once upon a time, in a small hidden house near the woods, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside with her dog, Spot. One sunny day, they went for a walk in the woods to look for new things. As they walked, Mia saw something on the ground. She pointed at it and said, "Look, Spot!" It was a pile of ash from a campfire. Mia and Spot looked around and found more ash piles. They followed the trail of ash, curious to see where it led. Finally, they found a big tree with a small door hidden in it. Mia and Spot opened the door and found a little room inside. The room was filled with toys and games. They played and laughed all day long. When it was time to go home, they promised to come back and visit their new secret playroom in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a playful girl named Sue. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, she found some clay in her yard. The clay was soft and fun to touch. Sue had a great idea to make something with the clay. Sue wanted to make a little dog with the clay. She tried to make the dog, but it did not look good. Sue asked her mom how to improve the dog. Her mom said, "Keep trying and you will get better." So Sue tried again and again. Each time, the dog looked a little better. Finally, Sue made a dog that she was happy with. She showed her mom and her friends. They all said the dog was very nice. Sue felt happy that she could improve and make a good clay dog. Now, she wanted to make more things with the clay and share with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. Fred lived in a small pond with his friends. They liked to play together, but sometimes Fred would complain when he didn't win. One day, Fred's friend, a fish named Sam, told him a story. The story was about a happy memory they all shared. It was a sunny day when they all played a fun game. Fred liked the memory and stopped complaining. From then on, Fred and his friends had a lot of fun together. They made more happy memories and played many games. And even when Fred didn't win, he didn't complain anymore. He learned that having fun with his friends was more important.

Tom and Mia were playing in the living room with their toys. They heard a loud noise outside. They looked out the window and saw big drops of water falling from the sky. It was raining hard. "Wow, look at the rain!" Tom said. "It makes a lot of noise." "I don't like the rain," Mia said. "It makes me wet and cold." "Let's go to the fireplace," Tom said. "It will make us warm and cozy." They ran to the fireplace, where their mom was sitting on a big chair. She had a book in her hand and a smile on her face. "Hi, kids," she said. "Do you like the rain?" "No, mom," Mia said. "It's scary and wet." "I like the rain," Tom said. "It's fun and loud." Mom laughed. "The rain is not scary, Mia. It's just water. It helps the plants and the animals grow. And it's not wet inside the house. We have a roof and walls to keep us dry." She opened her arms and hugged Tom and Mia. "And we have a fireplace to keep us warm. And we have each other to keep us happy. We are safe and loved, no matter what the weather is." Tom and Mia smiled and snuggled with mom. They felt the heat of the fireplace and the love of mom. They watched the rain and listened to the noise. They were not afraid or cold anymore. They were warm and cozy. They liked the fireplace.

Once upon a time there was a generous bear named Jim. Jim was always ready to help those in need. One day he heard a sad story from his friend, Alice the rabbit. Alice wanted to go to the theater, but she didn't have enough money. Jim was so generous that he decided to buy Alice's ticket. He was so excited to see her happy face and they went to the theater together. After the show, Jim asked Alice how she liked it. She had a big smile on her face and said “I loved it! Thank you so much for accepting me to come with you!” Jim was so happy that he had made his friend so happy. From that day, everyone in the forest knew that Jim was a generous bear.
