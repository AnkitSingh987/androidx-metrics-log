Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pot

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the mud. One day, he found a big wet pot in his yard. He was so happy and wanted to show his mom. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a big wet pot!" His mom looked at the pot and started to worry. She said, "Oh no, Tim! That pot is very wet. We need to clean it." Tim and his mom took the pot inside and washed it. Tim was sad because he liked the wet pot. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can play with the pot after we clean it." When the pot was clean, Tim and his mom went outside. They filled the pot with water and made mud. Tim was happy again. He played with the wet pot all day long.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big tent in the park. It says "Museum". They want to go inside. "Can we go, please?" Sara asks her mom. "Sure, but stay close to me," her mom says. They go inside the tent. They see many things. They see rocks, and shells, and bones. They see a big skeleton. It looks like a big lizard. "What is that?" Ben asks. "That is a dinosaur," Sara's mom says. "It is very old. It is ancient." "Wow," Sara and Ben say. They go closer to the skeleton. They see a sign. It says "Do not touch". "Can I touch it?" Ben asks. "No, you can't," Sara's mom says. "It is not ours. It is lent to us by another museum. We have to be careful with it." "Why?" Sara asks. "Because it is very rare and fragile," Sara's mom says. "It could break easily. And then we would be very sad." "Oh," Sara and Ben say. They look at the skeleton. They think it is amazing. They want to learn more about it. They ask Sara's mom many questions. She tells them stories about the dinosaur and how it lived. They have fun in the museum. They thank Sara's mom for taking them. They say goodbye to the skeleton. They hope to see it again.

Once upon a time, there was a dog. He was a very lonely dog. His collar was a dull grey. One sunny day, the dog was wandering in the park when he heard a voice from a nearby bush. He stopped to look and he saw a tiny puppy. The puppy was so small and its collar was a bright yellow. The dog stepped forward and wagged his tail. He said to the tiny puppy, "Hi, I'm feeling lonely! Would you like to be my friend?" The puppy looked up at the dog and smiled. It barked happily. After that, the two dogs were always playing together. They went for walks in the park and chased each other around the trees. Now they are the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad and miserable because he could not find his favorite picture. The picture was of Tim and his dog, Max, playing in the park. Tim looked everywhere for the picture, but he could not find it. One day, Tim went to the park with Max. Tim thought he might find the picture there. They played together, and Tim decided to release Max from his leash. Max ran around the park, wagging his tail and barking happily. Tim laughed and chased after Max, feeling less miserable. As they played, Max found a big tree and started digging. Tim went to see what Max found. To his surprise, Max dug up the missing picture! Tim was so happy and hugged Max. They went back home, and Tim put the picture on the fridge. Now, every time Tim felt miserable, he looked at the picture and remembered the fun day at the park with Max.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like the heat. It made him feel bad. He wanted to solve this problem. He thought and thought about what to do. One day, Tim found a big fan. He thought it would help with the heat. He turned it on and felt the cool air. But, there was a problem. The fan made a disgusting smell. Tim did not like the smell at all. Tim had an idea. He cleaned the fan very well. The disgusting smell went away. Now, Tim was happy. He had solved his problem with the heat and the bad smell. Tim and the fan lived happily ever after.
