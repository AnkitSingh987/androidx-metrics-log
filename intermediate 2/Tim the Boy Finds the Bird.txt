Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to work on his journal every day. He was very serious about his journal. He drew pretty pictures and wrote fun stories in it. One day, Tim went to the park with his journal. He sat under a big tree and started to work on his journal. Suddenly, a big wind came and took his journal away. Tim was very sad and started to cry. A nice bird saw Tim and wanted to help. The bird flew high and found Tim's journal stuck in a tree. The bird brought the journal back to Tim. But when Tim opened his journal, he saw that the wind had made all the pages blank. Tim was surprised, but he smiled. Now he could make new fun stories and pretty pictures in his journal.

Once upon a time, there was a big flood in a small town. The rain poured down, and the water went up and up. All the animals in the town were scared. They did not know how to make the water go away. One day, a creative little bird named Blue had an idea. Blue said to the other animals, "We can work together to dry the town! Let's all flap our wings and blow the water away!" The animals thought it was a good idea. They all started to flap their wings and blow the water away. Slowly, the town began to dry. The water went down and down. The animals were happy and proud of their hard work. They learned that by working together, they could solve big problems. The moral of the story is that teamwork is important, and we can achieve great things when we help each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was an icy day, but he was excited to play in the snow. He put on his warm coat, hat, and gloves. Tim saw his friend, Sue, making a big snowman. He ran to her and said, "Hi, Sue! Can I help you make the snowman?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's make the best snowman ever!" They worked together, rolling big snowballs and stacking them up. But then, Tim accidentally knocked down the snowman. Sue started to weep. Tim felt bad. Tim said, "I'm sorry, Sue. Let's build the snowman again, and I'll be more careful." Sue wiped her tears and smiled. They built the snowman again, and it was even better than before. They learned that when you work together and forgive each other, you can make great things happen.

Tom and Lily are playing with their toys in the living room. Tom has a big truck and Lily has a doll. They are having fun, but they also want to try each other's toys. "Can I play with your truck, Tom?" Lily asks. "OK, but only for a little bit. Then you have to give it back," Tom says. Lily takes the truck and makes it go vroom vroom on the carpet. She likes how it has big wheels and a loud horn. Tom watches her and then looks at the doll. He wonders what it can do. "Can I play with your doll, Lily?" Tom asks. "OK, but only for a little bit. Then you have to give it back," Lily says. Tom takes the doll and tries to make it talk. He squeezes its belly, but nothing happens. He looks at its face, but it does not blink. He feels its hair, but it is not soft. It is rubber. "This doll is boring," Tom says. "It does not do anything." Lily hears him and gets angry. She likes her doll very much. She thinks it is pretty and nice. "That is not true," Lily says. "My doll can do many things. She can dance, and sing, and hug, and smile. You just do not know how to play with her." Tom does not believe her. He thinks she is making it up. "Show me then," Tom says. Lily takes the doll back and puts it on the floor. She holds its hands and makes it move side to side. She sings a song and makes it open and close its mouth. She wraps its arms around her and makes it say "I love you". She makes it smile with her finger. "See?" Lily says. "My doll is impressive. She can do many things." Tom watches her and feels sorry. He realizes he was wrong. He sees that Lily loves her doll and knows how to play with her. He also sees that she can manage to make the doll do many things with her imagination. "You are right, Lily," Tom says. "Your doll is impressive. You can do many things with her. I am sorry I said she was boring." Lily smiles and forgives him. She is happy that Tom understands. "Thank you, Tom," Lily says. "Do you want to play with her again?" Tom nods and takes the doll. He tries to make it do the things that Lily did. He dances, and sings, and hugs, and smiles with the doll. He has fun, and so does Lily. They share their toys and play together. They are good friends.
