Category: Intermediate
Title: The Magic Cat and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a magic cat. This cat could vanish and come back. It was easy for the cat to do this. One day, the cat met a little boy. The boy liked the cat. They played together all day. The cat showed the boy how it could vanish and come back. The boy was happy and laughed. But one day, the cat vanished and did not come back. The boy was sad. He waited and waited, but the magic cat never came back. The boy missed his friend and cried.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the woods. She saw a tiny flame dancing on a leaf. The flame was very bright and warm. Lily thought it was pretty and wanted to take it home. Lily put the flame in a small jar and carried it back to her house. She showed her mom the flame and told her about finding it in the woods. Her mom smiled and said, "We can use this flame to make something useful." Together, they used the flame to cook a big pot of soup for dinner. As they ate, Lily realized that the flame was not just pretty, but also very helpful. She was happy that she found the flame and could use it to do something good for her family.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big, strong friend named Tom. Tom was very tall and could lift heavy things. Mia liked to play with Tom because he made her feel safe. One day, Mia wanted to introduce Tom to her mom. She said, "Mom, this is my friend Tom. He is very strong." Tom smiled and shook Mia's mom's hand. Mia's mom was happy to meet Tom and said, "Hello, Tom! Nice to meet you!" Mia's mom went to the kitchen and brought a mug with juice for Mia and Tom. Mia and Tom said "thank you" and drank the juice. They laughed and played together all day long. Mia was happy to have a strong friend like Tom, and her mom was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he got a new car. It was a model car. It was red and shiny. Tim was so happy! Tim wanted to show his new car to his friend, Sarah. He put the car in a box. He wanted to deliver it to her house. He walked and walked. But he could not find her house. Tim felt frustrated. Then, Tim saw a big dog. The dog was friendly. It wagged its tail. Tim asked the dog to help him find Sarah's house. The dog sniffed the box with the car. It barked and ran. Tim followed the dog. They found Sarah's house. Tim delivered the model car to Sarah. They played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty box with a stubborn lock that was hard to open. Inside the box, there were many shiny jewels. Lily loved her jewels and liked to attach them to her clothes. One day, Lily's friend Sam came to play. Lily showed Sam her pretty box and said, "Look, I have jewels!" Sam looked at the stubborn lock and asked, "Can I help you open it?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Together, they opened the stubborn lock and saw the shiny jewels. Lily and Sam took turns attaching the jewels to their clothes. They had so much fun playing with the jewels all day. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little ghost. The ghost lived in a big, fancy house. The house was very old and had many rooms. The ghost liked to play in the big house all day. One day, the ghost found a big room with a lot of dust. The dust made the ghost sneeze. The sneeze was so big and loud that it made the fancy house shake. The ghost was scared and did not know what to do. The big sneeze made the fancy house fall down. The little ghost was sad because the house was gone. The ghost learned that sneezing in a dusty room can make bad things happen. The moral of the story is to be careful when you play in old, dusty rooms.
