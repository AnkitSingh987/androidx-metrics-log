Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Fish

One day, a boy named Tom went to the lake. He saw a big fish in the water. Tom was very proud. He wanted to catch the fish. He shouted, "I will catch you, big fish!" Tom's friend, Sam, came to the lake too. Sam saw the big fish and wanted to catch it as well. Sam said, "I will catch the fish first!" They both tried to catch the fish. They shouted and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky. The bird caught the big fish in its mouth. Tom and Sam were very sad. They did not catch the fish. The bird flew away with the fish, and the boys went home.

One day, a big cat named Tom was very tired. He wanted to sleep in his warm bed. But his bed was wet because it rained last night. So, Tom had to find a new place to sleep. Tom saw a big box near a tree. He thought it was a good place to sleep. But, the box had an order inside. The order was for a big cake. Tom did not know about the cake. He just wanted to sleep. So, he went inside the box and slept. After a while, a man came to take the box with the order. He did not know Tom was inside. He put the box in a big truck and drove away. When Tom woke up, he was far away from home. He was lost and could not find his way back. Tom was very sad because he just wanted to sleep, but now he was lost.

Once upon a time, there was a little purple fox. The fox lived in a big forest. The forest had many trees and flowers. The purple fox liked to play with his friends, the birds and the rabbits. One day, the purple fox was feeling sad. He wanted to imagine a new game to play with his friends. He sat under a tree and thought very hard. Then, he had a great idea! He called his friends and said, "Let's play pretend! We can be anything we want!" The birds and the rabbits liked the idea. They all took turns saying what they wanted to be. The purple fox said, "I want to be a big dragon!" The birds said, "We want to be fast airplanes!" The rabbits said, "We want to be strong superheroes!" So, they all played pretend together. The purple fox was a big dragon, the birds were fast airplanes, and the rabbits were strong superheroes. They had so much fun using their imaginations. They played until the sun went down and it was time to go home. The purple fox was happy that he thought of a new game for his friends to play. They all said goodnight and went to their homes in the forest.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to pretend she was a brave explorer. She would put on her hat and go on big adventures in her backyard. One day, she decided to go to the unknown west part of her yard. As Mia walked west, she saw a big, scary dog. The dog barked at her, and she was scared. Mia knew she had to be brave like an explorer. She thought of a plan to get past the dog. Mia picked up a stick and threw it far away. The dog ran after the stick, and Mia quickly went past the dog. She had won! Mia reached the unknown west part of her yard and felt very proud. She went back home and told her mom all about her adventure.

One day, a girl named Sue went into her backyard. It was a normal day with the sun shining bright. Sue saw a flower that was about to bloom. She was very happy and excited. Sue called her friend, Tim, to come and see the flower. "Tim, come look at the flower in my backyard! It's going to bloom!" Tim ran to Sue's backyard to see the flower. They both waited and watched. As they looked at the flower, something unexpected happened. The flower started to grow bigger and bigger! It was not a normal flower anymore. Sue and Tim were very surprised. They had never seen a flower grow so big before. They laughed and played in the shadow of the giant flower. It was a fun and special day for both of them.
