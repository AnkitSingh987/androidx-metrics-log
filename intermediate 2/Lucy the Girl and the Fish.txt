Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a fish friend named Bob. Lucy and Bob liked to play and relax near the water. Lucy was happy, but Bob was troubled. He needed more oxygen in the water to breathe. One day, Lucy saw a big green plant. She thought it could help Bob. Lucy put the plant in the water with Bob. The plant made more oxygen for him. Bob was happy, and he could breathe better. Then, something unexpected happened. The plant grew bigger and bigger. It started to talk! The plant said, "Thank you, Lucy!" Lucy was surprised. The plant helped Bob, and they all became best friends. They played and relaxed together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She had a big job to do on Tuesday. She had to prepare a cake for her mom's birthday. Jane was excited but a little bit scared. She had never made a cake all by herself before. On Tuesday, Jane put on her apron and got all the things she needed. She mixed the cake stuff in a big bowl. But, oh no! She made a big mess on the floor. Jane felt ashamed. She wanted the cake to be perfect for her mom. Jane's big sister, Lucy, saw her looking sad. "What's wrong?" Lucy asked. Jane told her about the mess. "Don't worry," said Lucy, "We can clean it up together." Jane smiled and they cleaned the mess. Then, they finished making the cake. Jane's mom loved it and gave them both a big hug.

Lily and Tom went to the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, but their favorite was the monkey. The monkey was brown and hairy, and it jumped and swung on the trees. It made funny noises and faces at the children. "Look, mom, the monkey is so funny!" Lily said, pointing at the monkey. "Yes, it is, dear. Do you know what the monkey is doing?" mom asked. "No, what is it doing?" Tom said. "It is counting. The monkey is very smart, and it can number the bananas it has. See, it has one, two, three, four bananas. Can you number them, too?" mom said. Lily and Tom nodded, and they counted with the monkey. "One, two, three, four!" they said together. The monkey heard them, and it smiled. It peeled a banana and threw the peel at them. Lily and Tom laughed and dodged the peel. "Don't worry, the monkey is harmless. It is just playing with you. It likes you," mom said. Lily and Tom smiled back at the monkey. They liked the monkey, too. They waved at the monkey and said goodbye. They thanked mom for taking them to the zoo. They had a lot of fun.

Once there was an old dog named Max. Max was going on a trip with his family. They went to the airport. Max saw big planes and many people. He was very excited. Max's family talked to a lady at the airport. "We are going on a big plane," said Max's mom. Max wagged his tail and looked at the planes. He wanted to go on one too. Max and his family went on the plane. It was cold inside. Max started to shiver. "Brrr, it's cold," said Max. His mom gave him a warm blanket. Max felt warm and happy. Soon, the plane took off and they went on their fun trip.

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tim. Tim was a slow worker, but he was good at fixing things. He had a small shop where he fixed bikes, cars, and toys. People liked Tim because he was kind and always helped them. One day, a little boy named Ben came to Tim's shop with his broken toy car. Ben was sad and asked Tim, "Can you fix my toy car, please?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I can fix it. But you need to stay here and watch me work." Ben agreed and stayed in the shop. Tim worked slowly and carefully to fix the toy car. Ben watched and learned how to fix things too. In the end, Tim fixed the car and gave it back to Ben. Ben was happy and thanked Tim. The moral of the story is: good things come to those who stay patient and learn from others.
