Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Mia found a mysterious letter on her doorstep. She picked it up and saw that it had her name on it. Mia was very excited to see what was inside the letter. Mia ran to her mom and said, "Mom, look! I found a letter with my name on it." Her mom helped her open the letter. Inside, there was a map and a note. The note said, "Follow the map to find a special surprise!" Mia and her mom decided to follow the map. They walked down the street, turned a corner, and found a big truck. The truck was loading big boxes. Mia's mom said, "Let's ask the truck driver if he knows where the surprise is." The truck driver looked at the map and said, "I know where this is! Follow me." Mia and her mom followed the truck driver to a big park. There, they found a big box with Mia's name on it. Mia opened the box and found a beautiful toy inside. She was so happy and thanked the truck driver for helping her find her surprise. Mia and her mom played with the new toy all day long in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big task to do. Lily had to bow to the Queen. She was very happy to do it. But her friend, Tom, was jealous. He wanted to bow to the Queen too. One day, Lily and Tom went to the Queen's house. Lily was ready to bow. Tom was sad because he could not bow. But then, something unexpected happened. The Queen came out of her house and saw Tom. She smiled at him. The Queen asked Tom to bow too. Lily and Tom both bowed to the Queen. They were both very happy. Tom was not jealous anymore. They went home and played together. And they were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable dog named Max. He was very kind and cared for his friends. Max had a little friend named Timmy. Timmy was a small, happy bird who lived in a tree. They liked to play together every day. One day, Max had an idea. "Let's play hide and seek!" he said. Timmy liked the idea and said, "Okay! I will hide first, and you find me!" So, Timmy flew away to find a hiding spot while Max closed his eyes and counted. Max counted to ten and then started to look for Timmy. He looked under the bushes and behind the trees. Finally, he found Timmy hiding in a small hole in the tree. "I found you!" Max said with a big smile. Timmy laughed and said, "You are good at this game, Max!" They played hide and seek all day long. Max and Timmy were very happy because they cared for each other and had fun together. They knew they could always depend on each other to have a good time.

Tom was a tough boy who liked to play with his toy pistol. He liked to pretend he was a cowboy and shoot at cans and bottles. He had a big hat and a belt with a holster for his pistol. One day, it was his birthday and he got a big box from his grandma. He was very happy and wanted to open it right away. He ripped off the paper and saw a shiny new pistol. It was bigger and better than his old one. It had a red light and made a loud noise when he pulled the trigger. He thanked his grandma and ran outside to play with his new pistol. He saw his friend Sam, who also had a toy pistol. They decided to have a duel, like in the movies. They stood back to back and took ten steps. Then they turned around and aimed at each other. But Tom's new pistol was too big and heavy for him. He could not hold it steady and it slipped from his hand. It fell to the ground and broke into pieces. Tom was very sad and angry. He started to cry and shout. Sam felt sorry for Tom and wanted to help him. He picked up the pieces of Tom's pistol and tried to put them back together. He found some tape and wrapped it around the pistol. He gave it back to Tom and said, "Here, I fixed it for you. It's still a good pistol. Don't cry, Tom. We can still play together." Tom looked at his pistol and saw that it was still broken, but Sam had tried to make it better. He stopped crying and smiled. He said, "Thank you, Sam. You are a good friend. I'm sorry I was mad at you. Let's play something else. How about cowboys and Indians?" Sam nodded and smiled. He said, "OK, Tom. That sounds fun. You can be the cowboy and I'll be the Indian. Let's go find some horses and feathers." They ran off to play, holding their pistols. They forgot about the broken pistol and had a lot of fun. They were tough boys, but they were also kind boys.
