Category: Intermediate
Title: The Book and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a pretty book. The book lived in a small house with a girl named Lily. Lily loved to read the pretty book every day. The book was filled with fun stories about animals and their adventures. One day, while Lily was reading the pretty book, a magic thing happened. The book started to talk to her! "Hello, Lily!" said the book. "I want to become your friend and play with you." Lily was very happy and said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend too!" Lily and the pretty book played together all day. They had so much fun, and the book told her many more stories. When it was time for bed, Lily gave the book a big hug and said, "Thank you for becoming my friend. I love you, pretty book!" The book smiled and said, "I love you too, Lily. Good night!" From that day on, Lily and the pretty book were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big sack. He liked to fill it with toys and things he found. One day, Tim was playing with his sack near his friend, Sally. Sally was having a nap under a tree. Tim didn't want to disturb her, so he tried to be quiet. But, he tripped on a rock and all the toys in his sack fell out. The noise woke Sally up. Sally was not happy. She said, "Tim, you disturb my nap! I am not happy with you!" Tim felt very ashamed. He said, "I'm sorry, Sally. I didn't mean to." Tim tried to put all the toys back in the sack, but they didn't fit anymore. The sack was broken. Tim was sad and so was Sally. They couldn't play with the toys anymore. The day ended with both of them feeling sad and upset.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys all day. Tim had a favorite toy, a ball player. The ball player was very good at playing games. He was serious about winning. One day, Tim's mom told him they needed to sell some of his toys because they had too many. Tim did not want to sell his ball player. He was sad but knew he had to help his mom. Tim and his mom went to the market to sell the toys. At the market, Tim saw a girl who looked sad. She did not have any toys to play with. Tim thought about it and decided to give his ball player to the girl. The girl was so happy, and they played together all day. Tim learned that sharing can make others happy too.

One day, Tim and Sue were playing with blocks. They wanted to build a big tower. Tim had some blocks, and Sue had some too. But they were worried if they didn't have enough blocks to make the tower. Tim said, "Sue, let's share our blocks so we can build a big tower together." Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Sharing is fun." They started to build the tower with their shared blocks. The tower grew taller and taller. Tim and Sue were so happy to see their big tower. Suddenly, their dog, Max, came running and bumped into the tower. The tower fell down. Tim and Sue looked at each other and laughed. They said, "Let's build it again! We can always share and make it even better." And so, they built the tower again, and it was the best tower ever.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play with water. One day, he found a spray bottle. Tim thought it was fun to spray water on things. He sprayed water on a page from his book. The page got wet and the colors ran. Tim knew it was dangerous to play with water near his books. He felt sad for the wet page. Tim learned his lesson. He promised to be careful with the spray bottle. He only sprayed water outside, away from his books. Now, Tim plays safe and has fun with his spray bottle.
