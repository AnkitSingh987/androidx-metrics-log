Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Window and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a club where kids played together. They had fun every day. One day, a new boy named Tim joined the club. Tim was very happy to make new friends. One cold day, the kids were playing with a big ball. Tim threw the ball very hard. It hit a window and the window broke. Everyone was scared. Tim said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to break it." The other kids said, "It's okay, Tim. We can help fix it." They all worked together to fix the window. After that, they played more carefully and had lots of fun.

---

One day, a boy named Tim saw a dull boat by the water. He wanted to play with it. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the boat?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim played with the boat, but he saw a big rock in the water. He thought, "I should move the boat away from the rock." But he did not move the boat. Later, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play too. They both played with the boat, but they forgot about the big rock. The boat went closer and closer to the rock. Then, the boat hit the rock! Tim and Sam were sad. They wished they had moved the boat away from the rock. Next time, they will remember to be careful.

---

Once upon a time, there was a modest mouse named Max. Max had a best friend, a tiny peanut named Pete. They lived in a small house near a big tree. One sunny day, Max and Pete wanted to have a race to see who was the fastest. They asked their friend, a wise old owl, to watch their race. Max said, "Ready, set, go!" and they both started to run. Max was very fast, but Pete was slow because he was just a little peanut. Max won the race, but he saw that Pete was sad. Max went to Pete and said, "It's okay, Pete. We can race again, and I will help you this time." The wise old owl smiled and said, "That's very kind of you, Max. Remember, being a good friend is more important than winning a race." Max and Pete hugged each other, and they knew that their friendship was the real prize.

---

One day, a girl named Amy had a plan. She wanted to play with her friends in the park. She put on her shoes and went to the park. Amy was very happy. In the park, Amy saw a big, modern toy. It was new and shiny. She was excited to play on it. She called her friends to come and play with her. They all came running and laughing. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came and sat on the modern toy. The dog was very big and funny. Amy and her friends could not play on the toy. They laughed and played with the big dog instead.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He noticed a big crack in the ground. Tim thought it was important to tell his mom about the crack. Tim ran back home to tell his mom. His mom was busy making food. Tim said, "Mom, there is a big crack outside!" His mom stopped and listened. She knew it was important to check the crack. Mom and Tim went outside to see the crack. They saw that it was very big. Tim's mom said, "We need to be careful and not fall in." They went back inside and made a plan to fix the crack. They were happy they found the crack and could make it safe again.

Once upon a time, there was a small hut in a big forest. In the hut, there lived a kind rabbit and a shy squirrel. They were friends and liked to play together. The rabbit liked to hop and the squirrel liked to climb trees. One sunny day, the rabbit and the squirrel went for a walk in the forest. They saw a big tree and decided to climb it. The squirrel went up first, but the rabbit was scared. The squirrel said, "You can do it, just be careful not to slip!" The rabbit tried to climb, but it was very uncomfortable for him. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came out of the tree and said, "I can help you!" The bird grabbed the rabbit and flew him up to the top of the tree. The rabbit was so happy and thanked the bird. The bird said, "You're welcome! I'm always here to help my friends in the forest." From that day on, the rabbit, the squirrel, and the bird were best friends and played together every day.
