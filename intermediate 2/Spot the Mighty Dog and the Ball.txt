Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Mighty Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a land with lots of stuff, there was a mighty dog named Spot. Spot liked to poke things with his nose. He had a lot of fun poking stuff all day long. One day, Spot saw a big ball. He wanted to poke it, but a mean cat named Whiskers was there. Whiskers didn't want Spot to poke the ball. This made Spot very sad. Spot thought and thought about how to poke the ball. He had an idea! He ran fast and jumped high over Whiskers. Spot poked the ball with his nose and it rolled away. Whiskers ran after the ball, and Spot was happy. From that day on, Spot and Whiskers shared the ball and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big mouth that could make very loud sounds. Lily loved to sing and dance. One day, she saw her friend, Tom, playing with a toy she wanted. Lily felt envious. Lily said to Tom, "I can sing a song with my big mouth. If you like it, can I play with your toy?" Tom agreed and started to watch Lily sing and dance. Lily sang a fun song, and her big mouth made the loudest sounds. Everyone around started to clap their hands and applaud. They loved Lily's song! Tom said, "That was great! You can play with my toy now." Lily was happy, and she learned that sharing and using her talents can make friends and solve problems.

Once upon a time there was a boy who wanted to sail. So one day he put on his hat and gloves and went to the beach. The beach was very big and the sea was very blue. He decided to get his boat out and go sailing. He put the boat in the water and started to sail far away. But then he saw a police boat in the distance. It came closer and closer and soon it was right next to him. The police asked what he was doing and the boy told them he was sailing. The police smiled and said he could continue sailing as long as he was careful. The boy thanked them and sailed away. He sailed until the police boat was very distant. Then he sailed back to the beach. He had had a wonderful sail and couldn't wait to go sailing again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a judge who loved eating spicy food. Every day, she would buy the spiciest food she could find. One day, a wise old man saw the judge and warned her that spicy food was bad for her body. But the judge would not listen to the wise old man. The next day, the judge ate the spiciest food she could find. But she started to feel very sick. Her stomach hurt and her head felt awful. The wise old man saw the judge once again and shook his head sadly. He warned her again that spicy food is bad for the body, but this time the judge listened. From that day onwards, the judge tried to eat healthier food. She realized that the wise old man was right and that it's better to listen to people who know what's good for us. The moral of the story? Don't ignore warnings from people who know what's best for you!

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a gray cat sitting on a warm rock. Lucy decided to pet the cat. The cat looked at her and started to purr. Lucy was very happy to make a new friend. As they played, the gray cat found a ball. Lucy and the cat played with the ball, and they had so much fun. They decided to play hide and seek. Lucy closed her eyes and counted, while the cat went to hide. When Lucy opened her eyes, she saw the gray cat sitting on the warm rock again. But, she also saw another gray cat! The two cats looked the same. Lucy was surprised and didn't know which cat was her friend. She decided to pet both cats, and they both purred. Now, Lucy had two gray cat friends, and they all played together.
