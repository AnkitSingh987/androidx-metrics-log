Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Strong Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max loved to eat dough. One day, Max saw a big ball of dough in the kitchen. He was very happy and wanted to eat it all. But Max's mommy said, "No, Max! That dough is for making bread." Max was sad, but he waited. He knew that bread would come soon. He could smell it. His mommy put the dough in the oven. Max watched the oven and waited. He could not wait to eat the bread. But then, something unexpected happened. The oven made a loud noise! Max began to panic. He barked and barked. His mommy came running and saw that the oven was broken. She quickly turned it off and saved the dough. Max was a hero! Now, they could make the bread again and Max could finally eat it.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown dog. He loved to play outside. One day, he found a big hook on the ground. The brown dog wanted to pick up the hook. He used his mouth to lift it. It was heavy, but he was strong. He carried the hook to his favorite tree. Under the tree, the brown dog played with the hook. He swung it around and dug in the dirt. He was very happy. And that is how the little brown dog found a new toy to play with.

Once upon a time there were two best friends called Jack and Jill. Jack and Jill loved to play together. One day Jack had a very special present he wanted to give to Jill. It was time to unpack it! Jack unwrapped a long box. He opened it and the lid made a loud noise. Inside the box was something magical! It was a will. Jill was so excited! She played with the will for hours. They drew faces on it, made it talk and laughed. The will was so funny it made them both giggle lots. Then it was time for Jack and Jill to say goodbye. Jack gave Jill the will so she could keep playing with it at home. She was so happy! She even gave Jack a big hug. Jack and Jill were sure they would have lots of fun again soon!

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a jolly ostrich named Ollie. Ollie loved to play with his friends, the monkeys, and the elephants. They would run and jump all day long. One day, a new animal came to the zoo. It was a big lion named Leo. Leo was very loud and scary. Ollie wanted to play with Leo too, but all his friends said, "No, don't play with Leo. He is not nice." Ollie was sad, but he said, "Okay, I won't play with Leo." The next day, Leo asked Ollie, "Do you want to play with me?" Ollie thought about his friends' words and said, "No, I can't play with you. My friends say you are not nice." Leo was sad, but he didn't refuse. He said, "I will show you that I can be nice too." Leo tried very hard to be nice. He played gently with the monkeys and the elephants. Soon, all the animals saw that Leo was nice too. Ollie and his friends were happy to play with Leo, and they all became good friends.

Jimmy was feeling envious. He watched as his older brother Tim went outside and made a big bag full of goodies. Jimmy wanted a bag too, but he was too little. "I dare you Jimmy," said Tim, "I dare you to fill a bag all by yourself and come back outside!" Jimmy's eyes lit up and he was very excited. He quickly started gathering items from around the house to fill his bag. When Jimmy returned outside, he proudly showed Tim what he had done - a full bag of his own! Tim's eyes widened in excitement and he praised Jimmy for a job well done. The two brothers went on to share the items in their bags and have a big party in the backyard. Jimmy showed everyone who said he was too little that he could do what Tim dared him to do.
