Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Charming Cat and the Dog

One day, a charming cat named Tom saw a big billboard. The billboard had a picture of a dog on it. Tom did not like dogs. He said, "I hate dogs!" Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "I like dogs. They are nice." Tom was sad because he did not like dogs. He felt alone. Then, something unexpected happened. A dog named Max came to play with Tom and Sue. Tom was scared at first, but Max was very nice. They played together and had fun. Tom said, "I do not hate dogs anymore. Max is my friend!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He wore a uniform every day to school. One day, Tim felt frightened because he saw a big dog near the school gate. Tim whispered to his friend, "I am scared of the big dog. What should I do?" His friend said, "Let's ask our teacher for help." When they went to their teacher, she smiled and said, "Do not be scared. The big dog is friendly." The teacher walked with Tim and his friend to the dog. They saw the dog wag its tail and play with a ball. Tim and his friend felt happy and not scared anymore. The moral of the story is to always ask for help when you are scared. You might find out that there is nothing to be scared of after all.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia went to the market with her mom. Mia loved melons, and she wanted to buy the biggest and sweetest one. She saw a big green melon and asked her mom to get it for her. One day, Mia's friend Tom came to play with her. They played hide and seek, and Mia hid the melon so that Tom could not find it. But Mia forgot where she hid it. She felt terrible because she really wanted to eat the melon. Mia and Tom looked everywhere in the house for the melon. Mia was sad because she missed her melon. Finally, they found it under the bed. Mia and Tom were so happy. They shared the melon and it was the sweetest one they ever had.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there were many staff who worked. The staff helped to keep the house clean and nice. They also served food to the people who lived there. One of the staff was deaf, but he was very good at his job. One day, a little girl named Sue came to visit the house. Sue saw the deaf staff serving food. She wanted to say "thank you" to him, but she did not know how. Sue asked her mom, "How can I say thank you to him?" Her mom said, "You can wave and smile." Sue waved and smiled at the deaf staff. He saw her and smiled back. Just then, a big wind came and blew the food away. Everyone was surprised! The deaf staff quickly picked up more food and served it again. Sue and her mom clapped and cheered for him. The deaf staff felt happy and proud.

Once upon a time, there was a green house. In the house, there was a big family. They were very happy. They had a little dog. The dog was very nice. One day, the dog saw a big ball. The ball was green too. The dog wanted to touch the ball. But the family said, "No, do not touch the ball!" The dog was sad, but he listened to the family. The next day, the family went out. The dog was alone. The dog saw the green ball again. The dog thought, "I will touch the ball just one time." The dog touched the ball. The ball opened! Inside the ball, there were many toys. The dog was very happy. When the family came back, they saw the toys. They were happy too. The family learned that sometimes, it is good to try new things. The dog learned to listen, but also to be brave. They all played with the toys and had lots of fun. And they all lived happily ever after.
