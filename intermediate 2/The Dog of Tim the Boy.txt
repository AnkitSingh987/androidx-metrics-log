Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a best friend named Sam. Tim and Sam liked to stay at each other's houses. They always felt comfortable when they were together. One day, Tim and Sam went to play in the park. They saw a big box. They did not know what was inside. Tim and Sam were very curious. They opened the box, and something unexpected happened. Out of the box jumped a little dog! The dog was so happy to see Tim and Sam. The boys were surprised, but they liked the dog. They decided to keep the dog and named it Max. Now, Tim, Sam, and Max stay together all the time, and they are all comfortable and happy friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a cool well. A little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue liked to play near the well. They loved to throw small stones into the well and listen to the splash. One sunny day, Tim and Sue were playing near the well when they saw something shiny inside. They wanted to see what it was, so they took turns to peek into the well. But they couldn't see it clearly. They both wanted the shiny thing, but they didn't know how to get it. Tim had an idea. He found a long stick and tried to reach the shiny thing. But the stick was not long enough. Sue then thought of tying a rope to the stick to make it longer. They tried again, and this time, they got the shiny thing out of the well. It was a beautiful, shiny coin. Tim and Sue were so happy that they had worked together to get it. They decided to share the coin and buy some ice cream to celebrate their teamwork. From that day on, they always played together and shared everything they found. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to make an igloo. He knew he would need a lot of snow. So, he went outside and started to make snowballs. He put them together to make the igloo. It was hard work, but he was happy. A big dog named Max saw Tim's igloo. Max felt guilty because he wanted to play with the snowballs too. So, he went to Tim and said, "Can I help you make the igloo?" Tim said, "Yes, let's make it together!" But Max didn't know how to make an igloo. He tried to help, but he was too big and clumsy. He broke the snowballs and the igloo fell down. Tim was sad because his igloo was gone. Max felt very guilty and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to break your igloo." Tim was upset, and he didn't want to make another igloo with Max. He went back inside his house, leaving Max outside feeling guilty and sad. The igloo was gone, and Tim and Max didn't get to play together that day.

One day, a big red ambulance was driving down the road. It went fast with its lights on and a loud sound. The ambulance was always ready to help people who were hurt or sick. A little car named Tim was on the same road. Tim saw the ambulance and felt jealous. He wished he could be big and fast like the ambulance. As they both drove, Tim tried to step on his gas to go faster. But no matter how hard he tried, he couldn't catch up to the ambulance. Tim said, "I wish I could be as fast and important as the ambulance. Then, I could help people too." Just then, Tim saw a little girl who was lost. She was crying and didn't know where her mom was. Tim stopped and asked her, "What's wrong?" The little girl said, "I can't find my mom. Can you help me?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I can help you." Tim let the little girl sit in his car and they looked for her mom. They found her mom at the park. The little girl was so happy to see her mom. She hugged Tim and said, "Thank you for helping me." In the end, Tim learned that he didn't have to be big and fast like the ambulance to help people. He was just as important and could do great things too.
