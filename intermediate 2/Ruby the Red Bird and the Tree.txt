Category: Intermediate
Title: Ruby the Red Bird and the Tree

One day, a red bird named Ruby wanted to soar high in the sky. She loved to fly and see all the pretty colors below her. Ruby saw a big tree with many leaves. She thought it would be fun to play in the tree. As Ruby flew to the big tree, she saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with paint. He had red, blue, and yellow paint. He was making a beautiful picture of the sky, the sun, and the trees. "Hi, Tim!" said Ruby. "I like your paint. Can I play too?" "Hi, Ruby!" said Tim. "Yes, you can play with me. Let's make a picture together!" So Ruby and Tim played with the paint. Ruby used her wings to spread the red paint on the paper. They made a beautiful picture of Ruby soaring high in the sky with the big tree below her. They were so happy to make art together and be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved ice cream, and she loved the sun. One hot day, she went out to play with her ice cream. But she did not know that the sun can be harsh sometimes. While she played, her ice cream began to melt. She was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, a boy named Tom came along. He was sad too, because his ice cream had also melted. They decided to be friends and find a way to fix their ice cream. Just then, a funny thing happened. Their melted ice creams mixed together and made a beautiful rainbow. They were so happy that they wanted to have a marriage to celebrate their new rainbow ice cream. They invited all their friends, and everyone had a great time at the party. And from that day on, Lily and Tom were the best of friends, and they always remembered the magic of the rainbow ice cream.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went on a safari with his mom and dad. They saw many animals like big elephants, tall giraffes, and fast lions. Tim was very excited and wanted to touch all the animals. One day, Tim's dad found a useful stick. He gave it to Tim and said, "You can use this stick to touch the animals from far away. It will keep you safe." Tim was happy and started to touch the animals with the stick. But Tim did not listen to his dad's warning. He went too close to a big lion to touch it with his stick. The lion got angry and chased Tim. Tim was scared and ran away, but he fell and got hurt. His mom and dad took him home, and Tim learned that he should always listen to his parents.

Lily liked to spring on her bed. She liked to jump up and down and make funny noises. She felt happy and free. One day, she heard a knock on her door. She stopped springing and ran to open it. It was her uncle Tom, who was a musician. He had a big guitar with him. "Hello, Lily!" he said. "Do you want to hear some music?" "Yes, please!" Lily said. She loved music too. She invited her uncle to come in. Uncle Tom sat on the bed and played his guitar. He sang a gentle song about the stars and the moon. Lily listened and clapped her hands. She liked the sound of his voice and the strings. "Can I try?" she asked. "Sure, Lily," Uncle Tom said. He gave her the guitar and helped her hold it. He showed her how to strum and pluck the strings. Lily tried to play the guitar. She made some loud and funny noises. She laughed and tried again. Uncle Tom smiled and encouraged her. "You are a natural musician, Lily," he said. "You have a lot of talent and joy." Lily felt proud and happy. She hugged her uncle and thanked him. She wanted to learn more music from him. She also wanted to spring on her bed again. She liked to do both.

One day, a big bell wanted to ring. It liked to ring loud and make everyone happy. But a little bell was envious. It wanted to ring too, but its sound was not as loud as the big bell. They both lived in a tall tower where they could see kids play. The little bell felt sad and thought of a plan. It wanted to ring when the big bell was not looking. So, the little bell waited and waited. When the big bell took a nap, the little bell started to ring. It rang as loud as it could, but it was still not as loud as the big bell. The big bell woke up and saw the little bell trying to ring. Instead of being mad, the big bell felt sorry for the little bell. The big bell had an idea. They could both ring together and make a beautiful sound. The little bell was happy and they rang together. Now, everyone loved the sound of both bells and the little bell was not envious anymore. They became best friends and rang together every day while the kids played.
