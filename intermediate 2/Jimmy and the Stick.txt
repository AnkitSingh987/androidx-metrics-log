Category: Intermediate
Title: Jimmy and the Stick

Jimmy and his mom were in the backyard playing with their dog, Spot. They were throwing a stick and Spot was fetching it over and over. It was a lively game! “Look, Spot!” Jimmy’s mom said, pointing at the stick. She threw it again and Spot was off after it. Jimmy noticed something glimmering in the grass. He went to take a closer look and saw it was a shiny penny. “Cool!” Jimmy said, picking it up and pointing it out to his mom. “Why don’t you give the penny to Spot for a special treat?” She said. Jimmy agreed and he took out his scissor and cut out a small hole in the penny. Then, he tied the penny to Spot’s collar. Spot was so excited he barked loudly. The game continued, but this time with a lot more enthusiasm and it was even more lively!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to drink juice. Every day, he would stare at the fridge and ask his mom for more juice. His mom would say, "Tim, too much juice is not good for you." One day, Tim saw a fierce dog outside his house. The dog was very big and scary. Tim wanted to make friends with the dog. He thought, "Maybe the dog will like juice too." So, he took a big glass of juice and went outside to share it with the dog. Tim walked up to the fierce dog and showed it the juice. The dog did not like the juice. Instead, the dog barked loudly and took Tim's juice away. Tim was very sad and ran back into the house. He learned that not everyone likes the same things, and you should be careful when trying to make new friends.

Tom is a little pig who lives in a big barn with his mom and dad and many brothers and sisters. He likes to play in the mud, eat corn and apples, and snuggle with his family. But today, he is not feeling well. He has a sore throat, a runny nose, and a fever. He does not want to play or eat. He just wants to rest. "Mom, I don't feel good," he says to his mom, who is busy making breakfast for the other pigs. "I know, my sweetie. You are sick. You need to rest and drink some water. I will bring you some soup later," she says, giving him a kiss on his forehead. "Can I stay in the barn today? I don't want to go outside. It is cold and windy," he says, shivering. "Of course, you can. You can stay in your cozy bed and read some books or listen to some music. I will check on you often. Don't worry, you will get better soon," she says, tucking him in. Tom feels sad. He misses his friends and his fun. He wishes he could join them in the yard, where they are running, jumping, and laughing. He hears them through the window, and he feels lonely. He tries to read a book, but he cannot focus. He tries to listen to some music, but he cannot enjoy it. He closes his eyes and tries to sleep, but he cannot relax. "Mom, I'm bored," he says, when his mom comes back with a bowl of soup. "I know, my sweetie. Being sick is no fun. But you have to rest and eat some soup. It will make you feel better. And soon, you will be able to play again. I promise," she says, spooning some soup into his mouth. Tom eats some soup, but he does not like it. He wants some corn and apples, but his mom says they are too hard for his sore throat. He wants some cuddles, but his mom says he has to stay in his bed, so he does not spread his germs to the other pigs. He wants some company, but his mom says she has to take care of the other pigs, who need her too. He feels sad and alone. "Mom, I love you," he says, when his mom leaves. "I love you too, my sweetie. You are my brave and strong pig. You will get well soon. Just rest and dream of happy things," she says, blowing him a kiss. Tom tries to rest and dream of happy things. He dreams of playing in the mud, eating corn and apples, and snuggling with his family. He dreams of being healthy and happy again. He dreams of the big barn, where he feels safe and loved. He smiles in his sleep, and he feels a little better.
