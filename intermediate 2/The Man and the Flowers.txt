Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a funny little man who liked to pick flowers. He lived in a tiny house near a big volcano. Every day, the funny little man would go out and pick flowers near his house. One sunny day, he went to pick flowers near the volcano. He saw a big, red flower that he wanted to pick. As he picked the flower, he heard a loud noise. The volcano started to shake and smoke came out of it. The funny little man was scared and ran back to his house. But the volcano kept shaking and making noise. Then, the volcano erupted and hot lava came out. The little man's house was covered in lava and he could not live there anymore. The funny little man was sad because he could not pick flowers near his house again.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little house, there lived a cat named Tim and a dog named Sam. They were good friends and liked to play together. One day, they saw a big, red ball in the yard. They were very excited to play with it. Tim and Sam took turns pushing the ball with their noses. They ran and laughed, having lots of fun. Then, they heard a noise. They stopped and listened. There was silence. Tim and Sam stared at the big tree near the ball. A little bird flew down from the tree and landed on the ball. Tim and Sam stared at the bird, and the bird stared back. They all became friends and played together. The bird would fly and push the ball, and Tim and Sam would chase it. They all had a very fun and jolly day.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. They had a big jar where they kept the bugs they found. They were very curious about the bugs. "Look, Tom, I found a big worm!" Lily said, holding up a wriggly thing in her finger. "Wow, let me see!" Tom said, running over to her. He touched the worm with his finger. It felt slimy and cold. "Can we put it in the jar?" Lily asked. "Sure, but we have to be gentle. Worms are good for the dirt. They make it soft and rich," Tom said. They carefully put the worm in the jar with some dirt and leaves. They saw the worm wiggle and burrow in the dirt. "Hello, worm, welcome to our jar!" Lily said. Just then, they heard a loud honk. They looked up and saw a big car arrive in front of their house. It was their grandma. She had come to visit them. "Grandma!" Tom and Lily shouted, dropping the jar and running to the car. They hugged their grandma and kissed her cheek. She smiled and gave them a big bag. "I brought you some cookies and books. Do you want to see?" she asked. "Yes, please!" Tom and Lily said. They followed their grandma into the house, leaving the jar in the garden. They did not notice that the lid had come off and the worm had escaped. The worm was happy to be free. It crawled back into the dirt and met some other worms. They had a lot to talk about.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful cat named Tom. Tom loved to display videos on his big TV. He would watch videos of other cats playing and having fun. One day, Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, came to visit. "Tom, can I watch a video with you?" Max asked. Tom said, "Yes, Max! Let's watch a fun video together." As they watched the video, something unexpected happened. The cats on the screen jumped out of the TV! Tom and Max were very surprised. The cats from the video wanted to play with Tom and Max. They all played together and had a great time. In the end, the cats went back into the TV, and Tom and Max were happy they made new friends.

One day, a chubby cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big purple ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball. So, the cat pushed the ball with its nose. The ball rolled away and hid behind a tree. The cat said, "Where did the ball go? I want to find it!" The cat looked behind the tree, but the ball was not there. The cat looked under a bush, but the ball was not there either. The cat felt sad and wanted to find the ball. Then, a little bird flew by and said, "I saw the ball! It is hiding in the tall grass!" The cat was happy and went to the tall grass. But when the cat found the ball, it was not purple anymore! The ball was now green! The cat was surprised and said, "How did you change color?" The ball just smiled and said, "I like to play tricks!" The cat and the ball played together all day long.
