Category: Intermediate
Title: Bounce the Gifted Ball and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a gifted ball named Bounce. Bounce had a big spot on it. Bounce loved to play with kids all day long. One day, Bounce was playing with a little girl named Lily. They were having so much fun. They would bounce and laugh in the sun. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew Bounce away from Lily. Bounce was sad and scared. But then, a big twist happened. Bounce met a new friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim and Bounce played together, and they had a lot of fun too. Bounce missed Lily, but was happy to have a new friend. And one day, Lily and Tim met, and they all played together. Bounce was so happy to have two friends to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow dog. The dog liked to play in the yard. The yard had many pretty flowers and green grass. The dog loved to run and jump all day long. One sunny day, the dog saw a little bird in the yard. The bird was singing a happy song. The dog wanted to play with the bird. The dog tried to guess where the bird would fly next. The bird flew to the top of a tree. The dog tried to jump and reach the bird, but the tree was too tall. The bird laughed and sang a new song. The dog was happy to have a new friend in the yard. They played together every day, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little ghost named Boo. Boo lived in a big old house. Boo loved to play tricks on people who came to the house. He would hide their things and make loud noises at night. One day, Boo found a big pumpkin in the kitchen. He thought it would be fun to hide it and scare people. So, he put the pumpkin in the dark, damp basement. Days went by and Boo forgot all about the pumpkin he hid. One day, Boo went to the basement to find something to play with. He saw the pumpkin he hid, but it looked different. It was all soft and smelly. The pumpkin had started to rot! Boo was sad that his trick didn't work. He learned that sometimes, being stubborn can lead to things not going as planned.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat that loved to sail. The boat had a big zipper on its sail. The boat liked to go fast, and the zipper helped it do that. The boat would sail on the water every day, and it was very happy. One day, the weather was harsh. The wind blew very hard, and the water was rough. The little boat was scared, but it wanted to sail anyway. So, the boat went out on the water and started to sail. The boat went very fast with the help of the big zipper. But then, something unexpected happened. The big zipper got stuck! The little boat could not slow down. The boat was scared, but then it saw a big whale. The whale saw the little boat and helped it. The whale pushed the boat back to the shore. The little boat was safe, and it was very thankful to the whale. From that day on, the boat and the whale became best friends, and they would sail together on the water.

Once upon a time, there was a rich lady named Jane. She lived in a big, pretty house. Jane had lots of nice things. She also had a big heart and liked to help people. One day, Jane met a girl named Lily. Lily was cold and needed a sheet to keep warm. Jane said, "I can supply you with a sheet to keep you warm." Lily was very happy and thanked Jane for her help. Jane gave Lily a soft, warm sheet. They became good friends. They played games and shared their things. Jane and Lily were very happy and always helped each other. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max were playing in the park. They saw a little frog who looked sad. Tom asked the frog, "Why are you sad, little frog?" The little frog said, "I worry because I cannot do a spell like the other frogs. They can make things change colors, but I cannot." Tom and Max wanted to help the frog. Max said, "We can try to find a way to help you do a spell." They all looked around the park for something that could help the frog. They found a big leaf and gave it to the frog. The frog held the leaf and tried to do a spell. The leaf changed color! The frog was so happy. The other frogs saw this and were envious. They wanted to be friends with Tom, Max, and the little frog. From that day on, they all played together in the park, and no one was sad or worried anymore.
