Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Wagon

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his red wagon. He would pull it all around his yard. One day, he found a shiny metal toy in the park. Tim liked the metal toy, so he put it in his wagon and pulled it home. When Tim got home, his friend Sue saw the metal toy and said, "That's my toy! I lost it in the park." Tim didn't want to give it back. They both wanted the toy and started to pull it. The metal toy was about to break! Tim's mom saw them and said, "You need to share and be kind to each other." Tim and Sue stopped pulling the toy. They decided to share the metal toy and play together. From that day on, Tim and Sue learned to share and be good friends. And the quiet little town was happy again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a holiday with his family. They went to a place with a big, blue sea and a thin, long beach. Tim loved playing in the sand and splashing in the water with his sister, Sue. While playing, Tim's hands and face got all dirty. Sue said, "Tim, you need to wipe your face and hands!" Tim found a soft, wet towel and wiped his face and hands clean. He felt much better after that. Tim and Sue made a big sandcastle together. They used a small bucket and their hands to build it. They were very happy and proud of their sandcastle. Their mom and dad smiled and took a picture of them with their sandcastle. Tim and Sue had a fun holiday at the beach.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He was going to play outside with his dog, Max. They loved to run and jump in the big green yard. As they played, Tim saw a loop on the ground. It was a hoop! Tim picked up the hoop and started to spin it around. Max jumped through the hoop and Tim laughed. Tim tried to jump through the hoop too, but he stumbled and fell. Max came over and licked Tim's face. Tim laughed again and they kept playing. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a shy mole. The mole lived in a hole in the ground. He liked to play in the dirt all day. One day, he heard a sound outside his hole. The mole was scared, but he wanted to know what the sound was. The shy mole stood by the hole and looked out. He saw a big tree with many leaves. He saw a little bird on the tree. The bird was singing a happy song. The mole felt happy too, but he was still scared to go out. He thought, "Maybe one day, I will be brave and go out to play with the bird." The next day, the shy mole heard the bird singing again. This time, he was not so scared. The mole stood by the hole and looked out. He saw the bird and waved. The bird waved back and flew down to the mole. They played together all day, and the mole was not shy anymore. The mole learned that it's okay to be scared, but sometimes, you have to stand up and face your fears.

Once there was a farmer. He wanted to grow everything he needed for his family. He planted potatoes, carrots, and broccoli. But what he needed he couldn't grow, he needed to find it somewhere else. He needed salt. So he decided to take a trip. The sun was hot and the air was warm. He traveled far and wide and then eventually he found it; a large rock of salt. He happily picked it up and carried it home with him. When he arrived, he was very happy. He had brought home all he needed to supply his kitchen with salt. He brought the large rock of salt into his kitchen and split it into small pieces. He carefully sprinkled it onto his meals. Everything tasted so yummy! The farmer was so thankful to have found his salt. He was happy that he could now supply his family with a warm and delicious meal.
