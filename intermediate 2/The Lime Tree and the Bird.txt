Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lime Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a lime tree. The lime tree had many green limes. One day, an unusual bird came to sit on the tree. The bird said, "Hi, tree! I like your limes. Can I eat one?" The tree said, "Yes, you can eat one. But be careful, they might fall!" The bird picked a lime and started to eat it. It was very yummy. Just then, something unexpected happened! The bird did not know that eating limes made it very sleepy. The bird started to feel tired and it fell asleep on the tree. It was a very funny day for the bird and the lime tree.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She was very excited because she wanted to find a new thing to admire. She put on her shoes, opened the door, and went outside. As Lily walked, she saw a big tree. She looked up and saw a bird in the tree. "Hello, bird!" she said. "I admire your pretty feathers." The bird sang a happy song for Lily. Lily kept walking and found a shiny rock. "Wow, what a nice thing!" she said. She picked up the rock and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! I found a shiny rock!" Tom was excited too. "I admire your rock, Lily!" he said. They both smiled and played with the shiny rock all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his old dog, Max. Max was a good dog, but he liked to run and play outside. One day, Max saw a squirrel and wanted to chase it. Tim's mom had left the door open, and Max saw his chance to escape. He ran out the door and into the yard. Tim saw Max running and knew he had to help get him back. He ran after Max, calling his name, but Max was too fast. Tim had an idea. He found a big stick and threw it. Max loved sticks! Max saw the stick and stopped chasing the squirrel. He picked up the stick and ran back to Tim. Tim grabbed Max's collar and led him back to the house. Tim's dad saw them coming and turned the knob to open the door. Tim and Max went inside, happy to be safe and sound.

Once there lived a little girl named Anne. She was only three years old, but very playful and imaginative. One day her parents decided to send her to the nearby park. She was very excited. As she reached the park, she saw a huge squash plant. It was unique - she had never seen one before! Anne went closer to the plant and started talking to it. "Hello there! My name is Anne. What is yours?" Suddenly the squash plant spoke. "I am a special squash. I come from a far away land, and my name is Jack. Please take me home with you!" Anne was amazed. She couldn't believe her ears. She happily agreed and send Jack home with her. That was a very special day for Anne, because she made a unique friend!

One day, a little bug named Bob wanted to find some cocoa to eat. He loved cocoa more than anything in the world. So, he started to crawl on the ground, looking for some cocoa. The sun was shining, and the grass was green. As Bob crawled, he saw an enormous tree. He thought, "Maybe there is cocoa up there!" He crawled up the tree and saw a big bird. The bird said, "Hi, little bug! What are you doing here?" Bob said, "I am looking for cocoa. Do you know where I can find some?" The bird smiled and said, "Yes, I know where to find cocoa. But first, you must help me. My nest fell down, and I need help to put it back up." Bob wanted to help, so they worked together to fix the bird's nest. When they were done, the bird said, "Thank you, little bug! Now, I will show you where the cocoa is." But instead of finding cocoa, they found a big pile of cookies! Bob was surprised but happy. He now had new friends and delicious cookies to eat. And from that day on, Bob and the bird shared cookies and played together every day.
