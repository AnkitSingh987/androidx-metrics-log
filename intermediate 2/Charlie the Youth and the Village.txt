Category: Intermediate
Title: Charlie the Youth and the Village

Once upon a time, there was a little youth named Charlie. Charlie had a big dream. He wanted more than anything else to be the king of his village. One day, Charlie went to meet the village leader to make his dream a reality. He was so impatient when he arrived that he couldn't even wait to say hello. “I want to be the king!” he shouted. The village leader answered, “Dear Charlie, you can do anything if you pray.” “I will pray, I will pray!” Charlie responded. Charlie knelt down and closed his eyes. He prayed and prayed and wished for his dream to come true. The next day, a miracle occurred. Charlie was made the new king of the village. He thanked God for helping him and proving that even the most impossible dreams can come true if you pray and believe.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a new boy on the swing. He was chubby and had glasses. Lily and Tom wanted to swing too, but they had to wait. "Can we have a turn?" Lily asked the boy. "No, go away. This is my swing. You are too small and silly to swing," the boy said. He was mean and rude. Lily and Tom felt sad and angry. They wanted to say something mean back, but they did not. They knew that was not nice. They decided to play with the slide instead. The boy kept swinging, but he did not have fun. He was lonely and bored. He saw Lily and Tom laughing and smiling on the slide. They looked happy and friendly. He wished he had friends like them. He got off the swing and walked to Lily and Tom. He felt sorry for what he said. "Can I play with you?" he asked. Lily and Tom looked at him. They remembered how he was mean and rude. They did not want to play with him. But then they saw his face. He looked sad and sorry. He wanted to be nice and have fun. Lily and Tom thought for a moment. They knew that everyone makes mistakes. They knew that everyone deserves a second chance. They knew that being kind and forgiving is better than being mean and angry. They smiled at him and said, "Yes, you can play with us. Come on, let's go to the slide." The boy smiled too. He was happy and grateful. He joined Lily and Tom on the slide. They had fun and became friends. The moral of the story is: Be kind and forgiving to others, even if they are mean and rude. You might make a new friend and have more fun.

One day, a little goat named Billy was very excited. He loved to sing. He sang all day and all night. His favorite song was "Old Macdonald Had a Farm." Billy sang and danced in the field with his goat friends. One day, a big goat named Gruff came to the field. Gruff did not like Billy's singing. "Stop singing!" Gruff said to Billy. "I don't like your song." Billy felt sad. He loved to sing, but he did not want to make Gruff mad. Billy had an idea. He asked Gruff, "Can we sing a new song together?" Gruff thought for a moment and said, "Okay, let's try." They sang a new song and danced together. Gruff and Billy became friends. They sang and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. He had a club, a big stick, that he would carry with him to feel safe. One day, he went for a walk in the woods. In the woods, he saw a big bear. The bear saw Tim and his club. The bear did not want to fight, so he warned Tim. The bear said, "Stay away, little boy. I do not want to fight you." Tim was scared, but he listened to the bear. He walked away from the bear and went back home. Tim told his mom about the bear, and she was proud of him for listening. Tim learned that it was important to listen to warnings, even when he was scared.
