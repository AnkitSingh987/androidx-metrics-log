Category: Intermediate
Title: The High Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a high tree. A bird lived in the tree. The bird had many friends. One day, the bird wanted to have a party. The bird said, "I will invite all my friends to play on the lawn." The bird invited all his friends. The friends were happy. They came to the lawn under the high tree. They played games and sang songs. The sun was shining and the grass was soft. The bird and his friends had so much fun. At the end of the day, the bird said, "Thank you for coming to my party on the lawn." All the friends said, "Thank you for inviting us. We had a great time." They all went home, and the bird went back to the high tree. The bird was happy to have such good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl called Mandy. Mandy had a brilliant green dress that she used to wear every day. She loved the dress, it was her favorite color. One day, Mandy was wearing her green dress was playing near a lake. Suddenly, something bad happened. Mandy started to slip on a rock and she started to fall into the lake. Mandy was very scared but luckily, her mom saw what was happening and ran over to help. She quickly grabbed Mandy and stopped her from falling in. When Mandy was safe, her mom said, "Mandy, you must be more careful. You need to be careful near water, it can be very dangerous." Mandy nodded and said, "Yes, mommy." Mandy quickly learnt her lesson and from then on, she was more careful. She loved her green dress but she knew that safety was more important. The moral of the story is that it is always important to be careful and listen to grown ups when you are playing outside.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Spot. Spot was playing outside with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They liked to run and play in the sun. One day, while they were playing, Spot fell and hurt his leg. He cried, "Ouch! That hurts!" Sue saw that Spot was hurt and wanted to help him. She went inside the house and came back with a bandage. Sue said, "Don't worry, Spot. I will help you." She put the bandage on Spot's leg. Spot felt better and was happy to receive help from Sue. They both smiled and continued to play together, being more careful this time. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, Mark wanted to go on an exciting adventure. He decided to pack his things and go explore the world! He put his favorite things in a bag and sealed it tightly. Mark set out on his adventure and soon found himself in a strange and dark forest. The moon was pale, the trees were tall and the air was cold. He was feeling scared but carried on walking. Suddenly, a wild bear appeared out of the bushes! Mark was so frightened that he dropped his bag and ran away as fast as he could. The bear roared and grabbed the bag with its sharp claws. Mark was upset as he had lost all of his things. He watched from a safe distance as the bear opened the seal on the bag. Mark could see all of his things, but he was too scared to try to get them back. The adventure had ended on a bad note for Mark. He was sad and disappointed that his things were now gone. He never wanted to go on an adventure again.

Lila and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. They see a big squash on a vine. They want to pick it and eat it. "Look, Ben, a squash!" Lila says. "It is big and orange. Let's get it!" "OK, Lila, but how?" Ben asks. "It is too high. We are too small." They think for a moment. They see a stool near the fence. They run to get it. They are fast. They put the stool under the squash. Lila climbs on the stool. She tries to reach the squash. She stretches her arms. She can almost touch it. "Come on, Lila, you can do it!" Ben cheers. Lila reaches a little more. She grabs the squash. She pulls it hard. The squash comes off the vine. Lila falls off the stool. She lands on the grass. She holds the squash in her hands. "Yay, Lila, you did it!" Ben says. He runs to help her. He hugs her and the squash. They are happy. They have a big squash. They can eat it for dinner. They smile.
