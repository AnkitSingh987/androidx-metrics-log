Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Rocks

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived with her family in a small house. One day, her mom and dad said they were going on vacation. Lily was very excited. She wanted to join them. They went to a place with ancient, big rocks. Lily saw the rocks and said, "Wow, these rocks are very old!" Her mom replied, "Yes, Lily, they are ancient." They walked around the rocks and took pictures. Lily had a lot of fun on her vacation. She played games with her mom and dad. They also ate yummy food. When it was time to go home, Lily said, "I want to join you on vacation again!" Her mom and dad smiled and said, "Of course, Lily. We would love that."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a quiet town where everyone was very friendly. Tim was always ready to go on adventures and see new things. One day, Tim went to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was shy and didn't have any friends. Tim wanted to be her friend. He walked up to Sue and said, "Hi! I'm Tim. Do you want to play with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes! I'm Sue. Let's play together!" Tim and Sue played all day. They went on the swings, down the slide, and even played in the sandbox. They were very happy to have each other as friends. From that day on, Tim and Sue were always ready to see each other and play in their quiet town.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to work or play. He only liked to sleep all day long. The other animals in the village did not like this, but Tom did not care. One day, there was a big storm with loud thunder. The animals were scared and needed help. They all tried to find a safe place to hide. Tom was still sleeping, but the thunder woke him up. He saw the other animals trying to hide, and he felt bad for them. Tom decided to try and help the animals. He found a big cave where they could all hide from the thunder. The animals were happy and thanked Tom for his help. From that day on, Tom was no longer lazy. He learned that when you try to help others, you can make a big difference. And that was the moral of the story: helping others is a good thing to do.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to climb the big trees and swing on the ropes. They saw a new tree that was very tall and had many branches. Ben wanted to climb it, but Lily was scared. "Ben, don't go up there!" Lily said. "It is too high and dangerous. You might fall and hurt yourself." Ben did not listen to Lily. He wanted to be brave and have fun. He said, "Lily, you are a baby. I am not afraid of anything. Watch me climb to the top!" Ben started to climb the tree. He went higher and higher, until he reached a big hole in the trunk. He looked inside and saw something shiny. He reached his hand to grab it, but then he screamed. "Help! Help! There is a skull in here!" Ben shouted. He saw a white bone with two holes for eyes and a mouth. It looked scary and old. He was so scared that he let go of the branch and fell to the ground. Lily ran to Ben and hugged him. She was glad that he was not hurt, but he was very pale and shaking. She said, "Ben, I told you not to climb that tree. You should have listened to me. I was trying to warn you." Ben nodded and said, "You were right, Lily. I was silly and foolish. I will never climb that tree again. I am sorry for calling you a baby. You are a good friend." They walked away from the tree, holding hands. They decided to play somewhere else, where there were no skulls or scary things. They learned to be careful and listen to each other.
