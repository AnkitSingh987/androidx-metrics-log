Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Mom

Lily was miserable. She had a cold and she could not go out and play with her friends. She stayed in bed and read her books, but she was bored and lonely. She sneezed a lot and her nose was red and sore. Her mom came in with a tray of soup and crackers. She smiled and kissed Lily's forehead. "How are you feeling, my sweetie?" she asked. Lily sniffled and coughed. "Not good, mom. I want to go out and play. I miss my friends. And I hate sneezing. It hurts." Mom hugged Lily and stroked her hair. "I know, honey. I'm sorry you're sick. But you have to rest and drink lots of water. The sneezing will go away soon. And your friends will be here to visit you tomorrow. They are worried about you too, you know." Lily looked up at her mom with hopeful eyes. "Really? They are coming to see me?" Mom nodded and smiled. "Yes, they are. They made you some cards and drawings. They want you to get well soon. They love you, Lily. And so do I." Lily felt a little better. She smiled and hugged her mom back. "I love you too, mom. Thank you for taking care of me. And thank you for the soup. It smells good." Mom kissed Lily again and gave her the tray. "You're welcome, my darling. Enjoy your soup. And don't worry. You'll be better in no time."

Once upon a time, there was a little worm named Wiggly. Wiggly was an impressive worm because he was very good at wiggling. One day, Wiggly was wiggling in the dirt when he met a big ant named Anthony. "Hi, Wiggly!" said Anthony. "Do you want to part the dirt with me?" Wiggly was happy to help. They started to part the dirt together, making a big hole. They found some yummy food to eat and had a lot of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big raindrop fell into the hole and made it fill up with water. Wiggly and Anthony had to swim very fast to get out. After they got out, they laughed and said, "That was a big surprise! But we had fun and we are still friends." And they wiggled and crawled away, looking for more fun things to do.

Once upon a time, there was a weak little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy trumpet that he loved to play. He would play it all day long, making happy sounds. One day, Tim and his friend, Sam, were playing with the trumpet. Tim wanted to play it, but Sam wanted to play with it too. They both pulled on the trumpet, and then, snap! The trumpet broke in half. Tim and Sam were very sad. They knew they had to fix the trumpet. They found some tape and put the broken pieces together. The trumpet was not as good as before, but it still made sounds. Tim and Sam learned to share and played the trumpet together, making happy sounds again.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. The bear liked to sing. One day, the bear met a little bird. The bird liked to sing too. They became friends. The bear said, "Let's sing together!" The bird said, "Yes! Let's be organized and sing a nice song!" So they sang a song about the sun and the moon. Their song was very pretty. The bear and the bird sang every day. They were happy and organized. All the animals in the forest liked to hear them sing. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to dress up in fun clothes. One day, Spot found a tight hat and put it on his head. He thought it was so funny and ran around to show all his friends. As Spot was running around, he saw a big box that looked like a jail. Spot was very curious and wanted to see what was inside. He went closer and saw that the door was open. Spot went inside the jail, but then the door closed behind him. He was stuck! Spot started to bark loudly so his friends could hear him. His friends heard the barks and came to help. They all worked together to open the jail door. Finally, the door opened and Spot was free! Spot was so happy to be out of the tight jail and thanked his friends for saving him. They all played and laughed together, and Spot never went near that jail again.
