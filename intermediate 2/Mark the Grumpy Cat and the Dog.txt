Category: Intermediate
Title: Mark the Grumpy Cat and the Dog

One day, a grumpy cat named Mark had a task. He had to find his toy. Mark looked under the bed, but it was not there. He looked in the box, but it was not there either. Mark felt sad. Mark saw his friend, Dog. "Did you see my toy?" Mark asked. Dog shook his head. "No, I did not see it," Dog said. Mark sighed and kept looking. Finally, Mark found his toy under a big tree. He was so happy. He ran to Dog and showed him the toy. "I found it!" Mark said. Dog wagged his tail and they played together. Mark was not grumpy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to take trips with her mom and dad. They would go to fun places like the park, the beach, and the zoo. One day, Sue and her mom went to the store. Sue saw a toy she wanted, but her mom said no. When her mom was not looking, Sue took the toy and hid it in her pocket. They went home, and Sue felt bad about taking the toy. Sue knew she needed to be honest, so she told her mom what she did. Her mom was sad but said they would go back to the store and give the toy back. Before they went, Sue's mom told her to wash her hands for the trip. Sue washed her hands and felt better. When they got to the store, Sue gave the toy back and said sorry. The store lady was happy that Sue was honest. She gave Sue a hug and a small treat. From that day on, Sue learned that being honest was the best way to be.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some food for their home. Tim saw a big box of chocolate on the shelf. He asked his mom if they could buy it. His mom said yes, because it was the only chocolate available. When they got home, Tim was very excited to unpack the chocolate. He opened the box and saw many small pieces of chocolate inside. Tim wanted to eat them all, but his mom said he could only have one piece. Tim picked the biggest piece of chocolate and ate it. It was very yummy. He put the rest of the chocolate back in the box and shared it with his friends later. Tim was happy that he could enjoy the chocolate with everyone.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to find the sunrise. He walked and walked until he saw a crazy bird. The bird was jumping and dancing. Tim thought it was very funny. "Hello, bird!" Tim said. "Can you help me find the sunrise?" The bird stopped dancing and looked at Tim. "Yes, I can help you!" the bird said. They walked together, looking for the sunrise. But then, something unexpected happened. The crazy bird started to grow bigger and bigger! Tim was surprised. The bird turned into a big, friendly giant. The giant picked up Tim and put him on his shoulder. They walked up a big hill, and there they found the beautiful sunrise. Tim was very happy and thanked the giant for his help.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a regular day. She liked to count her toys and play with her friends. One day, she found a new toy. It was a funny toy with a big lip. Lucy laughed and showed her friends. Her friends liked the toy too. They started to count its big lip. They counted one, two, three times. Suddenly, the toy started to talk! Lucy and her friends were very surprised. They never saw a toy talk before. The toy said, "I am a magic toy. If you count my lip three times, I can make a wish come true." Lucy and her friends were so happy. They took turns counting the lip and making wishes. They played with the magic toy all day, having the most fun they ever had.

Lily and Max were restless. They did not want to stay at the ranch with their grandma. They wanted to go to the lake and sail their toy boats. They asked their grandma if they could go. "Not today, my dears," grandma said. "It is too windy and cold. You might catch a cold. Why don't you play with the horses and the cows instead?" Lily and Max sighed. They liked the horses and the cows, but they liked the lake more. They went to the barn and fed the animals some hay and carrots. They petted their fur and their noses. They said hello to them. But they still felt restless. They looked at the sky and saw the clouds moving fast. They felt the wind blowing their hair. They heard the lake water splashing. They wanted to sail their toy boats. They had an idea. They took their toy boats and put them in a big bucket. They filled the bucket with water from the tap. They carried the bucket outside and put it on the grass. They pretended the bucket was the lake and the wind was the sail. They made their toy boats go fast and slow, left and right, in the bucket. They had fun. They laughed and cheered. They forgot they were restless. They played until grandma called them for lunch. "Come on, my dears," grandma said. "It is time to eat. You must be hungry after sailing your toy boats." Lily and Max smiled. They picked up their toy boats and their bucket. They ran to the house. They hugged their grandma and thanked her for letting them play. They were not restless anymore. They were happy.
