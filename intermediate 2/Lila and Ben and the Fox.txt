Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and Ben and the Fox

Lila and Ben were friends who lived in a hut near the forest. They liked to play and explore together, but sometimes they argued over toys and food. One day, they found a big basket of apples in the forest. They were very happy and wanted to take them home. "Let's share the apples," Lila said. "You can have half and I can have half." "No, I want more apples," Ben said. "I found them first. You can have only one." "That's not fair," Lila said. "You are not a good friend. I don't want to play with you anymore." Lila ran away, crying. Ben took the basket and went back to the hut. He ate some apples, but he felt lonely and sad. He missed Lila and wished he had been nicer to her. Meanwhile, Lila met a fox in the forest. The fox was hungry and saw the apple in Lila's hand. He smiled and said, "Hello, little girl. What a nice apple you have. Can I have a bite?" Lila was scared of the fox, but she remembered what her mother had taught her. She said, "No, thank you. This is my apple. But I can give you something else. I have some bread and cheese in my bag. You can have them if you are hungry." The fox was surprised and touched by Lila's kindness. He said, "Thank you, little girl. You are very generous and good. You have a big heart. I will not hurt you. I will be your friend." Lila and the fox shared the bread and cheese. They talked and laughed and had fun. Lila felt happy and forgot about Ben and the apples. Later, Lila and the fox went back to the hut. They saw Ben sitting outside, looking sad and bored. He had eaten all the apples, but he still felt hungry and sick. He saw Lila and the fox and felt ashamed and sorry. He said, "Lila, I'm sorry. I was mean and greedy. I should have shared the apples with you. Can you forgive me? Can we be friends again?" Lila smiled and said, "Of course, Ben. I forgive you. We can be friends again. But you have to learn to care for others and not only for yourself. Sharing is good. It makes you and others happy. Look, I have a new friend. This is the fox. He was hungry and I gave him some food. He is very nice and friendly. He can be our friend too." Ben was happy and grateful. He said, "Thank you, Lila. You are a good friend. And hello, fox. You are welcome to our hut. Can we play together?" Lila, Ben, and the fox hugged and played together. They learned to care for each other and share what they had. They lived happily in the hut near the forest. The end.

Once upon a time there was a small ant and it was looking for something to eat. Suddenly it saw something shining in the distance. It was a shiny, red apple! The ant was so excited and scurried towards the red apple. But before it could reach the apple, it noticed a big sign ahead. It read "DO NOT TOUCH - STAY AWAY." The ants knew it had to obey the sign, so it stayed away. Just then another ant came along and was about to take the apple and ignore the sign. The first ant quickly stopped it and said, “We must obey the sign and not take the apple!” The other ant listened and for the first time it thought about why the sign was there. It thought that if it ignored the sign, it might be in trouble. So it heeded the advice of the first ant and stayed away from the apple. The moral of the story is when we see a sign, it's important to listen and obey. The ants showed us if we ignore signs, we can be in trouble.

Lily and Ben went to the mall with Mom. They liked to see the big shops and the bright lights. Mom said they could pick one toy each, but they had to be good. They walked around the mall and looked at many toys. Lily saw a doll that had long hair and a pretty dress. Ben saw a car that had wheels and a horn. They both wanted the toys, but they were too expensive. Mom said they had to find something cheaper. Then they saw a shop that had many things that glow. There were stars, moons, animals, and letters that glow in the dark. Lily and Ben thought they were impressive. They wanted to have something that glow for their room. "Can we have one, Mom?" they asked. Mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can. You can each choose one thing that glow. But you have to share and take turns." Lily and Ben were happy. They thanked Mom and hugged her. They looked at the things that glow and tried to decide. Lily picked a star that glow pink. Ben picked a moon that glow blue. They paid for the things that glow and left the shop. They went home and put the things that glow in their room. They turned off the light and watched the star and the moon glow. They felt cozy and sleepy. They said good night to Mom and to each other. They closed their eyes and dreamed of the mall and the toys and the things that glow.
