Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty and Max and the Bird

One day, a lovely cat named Kitty and a small dog named Max were in the kitchen. They wanted to make a cake. Kitty said, "Max, let's mix the cake stuff!" Max agreed, "Yes, let's do it!" They mixed flour, sugar, and butter in a big bowl. Max stirred with a big spoon, and Kitty poured in the milk. They were having so much fun! Suddenly, a bird flew in through the open window. The bird saw the butter and wanted some too. The bird said, "Can I have some butter, please?" Max and Kitty looked at each other and then said, "Okay, you can have some." The bird took a little butter and flew away. Max and Kitty laughed and kept mixing. When the cake was done, they shared it with their new bird friend.

John was a very happy boy. He loved his bus. Each day the bus would pick him up from home and take him to school. He loved the colours of the bus. Every morning his mom would come out to the bus stop with him to say goodbye. One day, as the bus arrived, John noticed a crack on the window. It was thin and it went all the way up the window. His mom asked the bus driver: "What is that crack?" The bus driver smiled and replied: "Oh don't worry, it's just age. The bus still loves to take us to school and back home again. There's no need to worry." John was so happy. Even though the bus was a bit old, it still loved taking them to school. From then on, John loved his bus more than ever.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom had a big, soft body. He liked to sleep all day long. One day, Tom was sleeping under a tree when he heard a voice. "Hey, Tom!" said a little bird. "Can you help me complete my nest?" Tom opened one eye and looked at the bird. He was very sleepy, but he wanted to help his friend. So, Tom got up and helped the bird find sticks and leaves for the nest. They worked together until the nest was complete. Just as they finished, a big wind came and blew the nest away! Tom and the bird were surprised. But they did not give up. They worked together again to build a new nest. This time, they made it stronger so the wind could not blow it away. When the new nest was complete, Tom and the bird were very happy. Tom went back to sleep under the tree, and the bird thanked him for his help.

Lily and Ben were twins who lived on a farm with their mom and dad. They liked to help with the animals and the crops, but they also liked to play and explore. One day, they found a big shiny car parked near the barn. They had never seen such a car before. It was red and shiny and had four doors. "Wow, look at this car!" Ben said. "It must belong to someone very wealthy." "What does wealthy mean?" Lily asked. "It means they have a lot of money and nice things," Ben explained. "Can we go inside the car?" Lily asked. "No, we can't. It's not ours. Mom and dad would be angry if we touched it," Ben said. But Lily was curious and wanted to see the car. She insisted that they should go inside. She said it would be fun and they would not break anything. "Please, Ben, please. Just for a little while," she begged. Ben was not sure, but he did not want to upset his sister. He agreed to go inside the car with her. They opened the door and climbed in. The car was very soft and smelled nice. There were buttons and screens and lights everywhere. "Wow, this is amazing!" Lily said. "Look, there is a radio and a map and a phone!" She started to press the buttons and turn the knobs. Ben tried to stop her, but she did not listen. She was having too much fun. Suddenly, they heard a loud honk. They looked outside and saw a man standing by the car. He looked angry and had a suit and a hat. He was the owner of the car. "Hey, you kids! What are you doing in my car?" he shouted. "Get out of there right now!" Lily and Ben were scared. They quickly got out of the car and ran to their mom and dad. They told them what happened and said they were sorry. Their mom and dad were not happy. They apologized to the man and explained that they were their children and they were just curious. The man was still angry, but he accepted their apology. He said he was a businessman who came to talk to their dad about buying some of their crops. He drove away in his car. Lily and Ben learned their lesson. They realized that they should not touch things that did not belong to them. They also learned what wealthy meant. They decided that they liked their farm better than the car. They hugged their mom and dad and said they loved them. They were happy to live on the farm.
