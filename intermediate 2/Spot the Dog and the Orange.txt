Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Orange

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had an orange collar. He loved to wear it every day. Spot's job was to work on a farm with his best friend, a boy named Tim. They worked together to help the farm grow. One day, while they were working, Spot's orange collar fell off. He looked everywhere for it, but it was gone. Spot was very sad. He told Tim, "I lost my collar. I don't know where it is." Tim said, "Don't worry, Spot. We will find it." They looked all over the farm, but they could not find Spot's orange collar. Then, something unexpected happened. A bird flew down from the sky and dropped the collar right in front of them. Spot and Tim were so surprised! Spot said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird smiled and flew away. Spot put on his orange collar and was happy again. Tim and Spot went back to work on the farm. They knew that they could always count on each other, and sometimes even a little help from a new friend. And from that day on, Spot and Tim always made sure to keep an eye on the orange collar.

Once there was a silly box. It was pink and had a bright yellow bow on top. One day, two kids played with the silly box. They opened it and found a yummy treat inside. It was so yummy that they ate it all up. The next day they opened the box again, but the treat was all gone! Instead, all that was inside was a smelly, rotted mess. They were so sad that the treat had rotted. They decided to go play something else and leave the silly box behind.

Once upon a time, there was a big, rough cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat tuna. One day, Tom saw a small, shiny can of tuna on the table. He wanted it so much! Tom jumped up to get the tuna, but a little girl named Sally saw him. Sally said, "No, Tom! That's my tuna!" Tom and Sally both wanted the tuna. They did not want to share. Tom thought about it and decided to surrender. He said, "Okay, Sally. You can have the tuna." Sally was happy and gave Tom a big hug. They both learned that sharing is important.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a long tunnel. The tunnel was very dark. A small, cute bunny lived near the tunnel. The bunny had many friends, but one day, a big, bad wolf came to the forest. The bunny was scared of the bad wolf. The bad wolf wanted to catch the bunny. So, the bunny ran into the tunnel to hide. Inside the tunnel, the bunny met a friendly frog. The frog said, "Don't be scared. I will help you." The friendly frog had an idea. The frog told the bunny to kiss him. The bunny did not know why, but the bunny kissed the frog. Suddenly, the frog turned into a big, strong bear. The bear said, "I will protect you from the bad wolf." The bear and the bunny went out of the tunnel. They saw the bad wolf waiting for them. The strong bear tried to fight the bad wolf. But the bad wolf was too strong and fast. The bad wolf caught the bear and the bunny. The bad wolf took the bear and the bunny away. The bad wolf was happy, but the bear and the bunny were very sad. The forest was quiet again, and the long, dark tunnel was empty. The bad wolf had won, and the story had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little fish. The little fish liked to store food in a cave. He stored berries, nuts, and other yummy things. One day, the tide came in very fast. The water got high and the little fish's cave got full. The little fish was sad. He did not want his food to get wet. Then, the little fish saw a big, dead tree. He swam to the tree and put his food inside. The tree kept his food safe and dry. The little fish was happy. And from that day on, he stored his food in the big, dead tree.
