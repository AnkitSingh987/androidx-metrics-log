Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Car

Ben and Mia were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big dollhouse. They pretended to be a family and made up stories. One day, they decided to play lawyers. They put on their dad's ties and glasses and used books as papers. Ben was the lawyer for the dollhouse family, and Mia was the lawyer for the toy car family. They had a problem: both families wanted to use the same parking spot. Ben and Mia sat on the floor and talked to each other. "I think the dollhouse family should have the parking spot, because they have more dolls and need more space," Ben said. "No, I think the toy car family should have the parking spot, because they have faster cars and need to go to work," Mia said. They could not agree. They started to raise their voices and argue. They got angry and frustrated. Then, Mia did something naughty. She reached over and pinched Ben's arm hard. Ben felt a sharp pain and cried out. "Ow! That hurts! Why did you pinch me?" Ben asked. "Because you are a bad lawyer and you don't listen to me," Mia said. "That's not fair! You are a mean lawyer and you don't share," Ben said. They both felt sad and sorry. They did not want to fight. They wanted to play and have fun. They looked at each other and said, "I'm sorry." They hugged and made up. They decided to be nice lawyers and find a solution. They agreed to take turns using the parking spot and to play with both the dollhouse and the toy car families. They were excited and happy. They continued to play and laugh. They learned to be good lawyers and good friends.

Sara and Tom were very happy. They were going to the lake with Mom and Dad. They had a big car with many bags and toys. They liked to swim and play in the water. When they got to the lake, they saw a little house. It was their cabin. Mom and Dad said they could unpack their bags and toys. Sara and Tom ran to the cabin. They wanted to see their room. They opened the door and saw two beds. One was pink and one was blue. Sara liked the pink bed. Tom liked the blue bed. They put their bags on their beds. Then they unpacked their toys. Sara had a doll and a book. Tom had a truck and a ball. "Look, Sara, I have a truck. I can drive it on the sand," Tom said. "Look, Tom, I have a doll. She can swim in the water," Sara said. They smiled at each other. They were happy. They wanted to go to the lake and have fun. They put their toys in a basket. Then they ran to Mom and Dad. "Mom, Dad, we unpacked our bags and toys. Can we go to the lake now?" they asked. Mom and Dad nodded. They took the basket and followed Sara and Tom. They went to the lake and had a wonderful day.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. They loved to play together all day. The sun was shining and they wanted to go outside to play. Before they went outside, they needed to discuss who would take the first turn on the swing. The cat said, "Dog, I think I should go first on the swing." The dog agreed and said, "Okay, Cat, you can have the first turn." So, the cat took the first turn on the swing and the dog waited. After a while, it was the dog's turn. They played on the swing and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they went back to their tidy little house and slept, dreaming of more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a kid who wanted to become better at being polite. He asked his mom for some advice. "Mom, how can I improve being polite?" the kid asked. His mom smiled. She said, "The best way to improve is to be kind to everyone you meet. That means saying 'please' and 'thank you', and maybe even holding a door open if you can." The kid thought hard about how to be kind to others. He decided to start by smiling to people he saw when he was walking around. As days passed, the kid noticed that he was being rewarded for his kind behaviour. People smiled back, thanked him for his help, and said 'well done!' The kid was very happy. He was improving all the time. He kept on being polite, kind, and smiling to everyone he met, and the result was that he had lots of friends who enjoyed being around him. The End.
