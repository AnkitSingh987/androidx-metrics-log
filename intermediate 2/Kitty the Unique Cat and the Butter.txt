Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Unique Cat and the Butter

Once upon a time, there was a unique cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to eat butter. One day, Kitty found a big jar of butter in the kitchen. Kitty said, "Yummy! I want to eat all the butter!" So, Kitty put her paw in the jar and ate some butter. But, when she tried to take her paw out, it was stuck! Kitty's friend, Doggy, heard her cry. Doggy came running and saw Kitty's paw stuck in the jar. Doggy said, "Don't worry, Kitty! I will help you." Doggy pulled and pulled, and suddenly, the jar broke! Butter was all over the floor, and Kitty's paw was free. Kitty and Doggy laughed and played in the butter. They had so much fun, and they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to visit his grandma's house. At grandma's house, they would read a story together. One day, Tom went to visit his grandma on an icy day. While they were reading a story, they heard a loud noise outside. They went out to see what it was. To their surprise, they found a big, icy snowball. Tom and his grandma decided to make a snowman. They worked together and made the best snowman ever. The snowman came alive and started to dance! They were so happy. But soon, the sun came out and the snowman began to melt. Tom felt sad, but his grandma told him a lesson. She said, "Tom, we must enjoy what we have now, because things can change fast." Tom learned to be happy with what he had and to enjoy every moment.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to wake up early every day. He also loved to read his favorite novel. It was a big, colorful book with lots of pictures. One day, Tim woke up and heard a noisy sound outside his window. He looked out and saw a big, noisy truck. Tim was curious and wanted to know what the truck was doing. He put on his shoes and went outside. He saw a man standing by the truck. "Hi, what are you doing with that noisy truck?" Tim asked the man. The man said, "I am here to take away all the old things people don't want anymore." Tim went back inside his house to get his favorite novel. He wanted to show the man his book. But when he came back outside, his book was gone! The man had put it in the noisy truck with all the other old things. Tim was very sad. He cried and cried. He had lost his favorite novel and could not read it anymore. And that was the bad ending of the story.

Tom and Mia are playing in the snow. They make a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They also find some tape in the garage and use it to make a smile on the snowman's face. "Look, our snowman is happy!" Mia says. "Yes, he is. But he is also cold. Let's give him a hug to warm him up," Tom says. They run to the snowman and hug him. But as they do, they hear a loud noise. It is a snow plow coming down the street. It is clearing the snow from the road. "Oh no, the snow plow is coming! It will knock down our snowman!" Mia cries. "Quick, let's hide behind the tree and wait for it to pass," Tom says. They run to the tree and watch the snow plow. It is big and noisy and has a shovel in front. It pushes the snow to the side of the road. But it does not see the snowman. It goes right over him. "Look, the snow plow smashed our snowman!" Tom says. They see the snowman's hat and scarf flying in the air. They also see the tape on the snowman's face. It is still smiling. "That's funny. The snowman is still happy, even though he is broken," Mia says. They are curious. They want to see the snowman's face. They run to the snow and find the tape. They peel it off the snow and look at it. "Wow, the tape is frozen. It is hard and cold," Tom says. He touches the tape with his tongue. It sticks to his tongue. He tries to pull it off, but it hurts. "Ow, ow, ow! The tape is stuck to my tongue!" Tom says. Mia laughs. She thinks it is funny. She helps Tom to get the tape off his tongue. They throw the tape away. "Let's go inside and have some hot chocolate. It will make your tongue feel better," Mia says. "OK, but next time, let's not hug the snowman. He is too cold," Tom says. They go inside and have some hot chocolate. They are happy. They still love the snowman, even though he is broken. They think he is the best snowman ever.
