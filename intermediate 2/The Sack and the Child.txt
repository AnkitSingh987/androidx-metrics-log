Category: Intermediate
Title: The Sack and the Child

Once upon a time, there was a sack. It belonged to a three-year-old child and was filled with special things. Every day, the child would take out the things from the sack

---

clear balls, soft toys and colors. One day, the child had a big fall and the sack was left behind. She was taken to the hospital in an ambulance. A doctor there checked her over and said that everything would be clear. The child was feeling better soon, but she was still missing her sack. She asked the doctor about it and he smiled and said, "Don't worry! I put it in the hospital safe. You can have it back once you are feeling better." The child smiled. She knew that she was going to be fine and that she would soon get the sack back. And she did! When she was feeling better, the doctor opened the safe and gave her the sack. She was so happy. From then on, the child took the sack with her wherever she went.

Once upon a time, there was a famous ball named Rolly. Rolly loved to roll all day long. One day, Rolly saw a ladder in the park. Rolly wanted to roll up the ladder, but it was very hard. A big wind came and pushed Rolly up the ladder. Rolly was so happy! But then, Rolly saw a small bug at the top of the ladder. The bug was scared and couldn't get down. Rolly felt sad for the bug. Rolly had an idea! Rolly rolled down the ladder very fast and got help. The bug was saved and happy. Rolly learned that helping others is good. And from that day, Rolly became even more famous for being kind and helpful.

One day, a big lion was walking in the woods. He saw a rich house with many toys. The lion wanted to play with the toys, but he was too big. He looked through the window and saw a little boy. The boy was reading a book. The lion said, "Hello, little boy. Can you read to me?" The boy was not scared. He said, "Yes, I can read to you, Mr. Lion. Come sit down." The lion sat down, and the boy started to read the book. As the boy read, something funny happened. The lion began to get smaller and smaller. Soon, the lion was the same size as the boy. Now, the lion could play with the toys too. They played and read together all day long, and they became best friends.

Once, a boy named Jack went to the park to pick some flowers. He saw a colourful nose on the ground. He felt excited and wanted to pick it up. But then he heard a voice. "Don't pick it up," the voice said. Jack looked around and saw an old man. Jack asked, "Why not?" "It looks so pretty but it's no good," said the old man. The old man explained to Jack, "That nose is hurt. If you pick it up and play with it, it will get hurt even more." Jack listened to the old man and put the nose back to the ground. Then, he went to pick some flowers with joy. Moral of the story: It is better to show kindness to hurt things, instead of making them hurt more.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to jog. He liked to jog every day because it made him feel good. The park was a regular place for him to go. While jogging, Tim saw a big stack of colorful blocks. "Wow!" said Tim. "I want to play with those blocks!" Tim ran to the stack and started to build a tall tower. He put one block on top of the other, making the tower grow higher and higher. Just then, a little girl named Sara came over. "Hi!" she said. "Can I help you build the tower?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's build it together!" So Tim and Sara worked together, laughing and having fun. They built the tallest tower in the park, all thanks to their teamwork.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. The sky was gray and full of rain. Tim took his fragile toy boat to play in the water. He liked to watch it float and make waves. Tim's friend, Sue, came over and saw the toy boat. She wanted to play too. "Give me the boat now!" Sue demanded. Tim did not want to share, but he gave the boat to Sue because she asked so strongly. As Sue played with the boat, the rain stopped and the sun came out. The water began to go away. Sue and Tim were sad that they could not play with the boat anymore. But then, they saw a big puddle. They put the boat in the puddle and played together, happy that they could both enjoy the toy.
