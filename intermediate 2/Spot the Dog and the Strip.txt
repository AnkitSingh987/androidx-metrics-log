Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Strip

One day, a little dog named Spot saw a big dog with a pretty strip on his back. Spot was envious of the big dog. He wanted a strip too. Spot asked the big dog, "How do you do that? I want a strip too!" The big dog said, "I roll in mud to make my strip. You can try it too!" So Spot went to the mud and rolled in it. He got a strip on his back like the big dog. But the strip was not pretty. It was dirty and smelly. Spot's mom saw him and was not happy. She said, "You are dirty now. You need a bath." Spot did not like baths. He had to take a bath and lost his strip. Spot was sad and learned that being envious was not good.

Once there was a curious little girl named Daisy. She loved going outside to explore and play. On a sunny day, Daisy found a small hole in the ground. She looked inside and saw a tiny caterpillar! She borrowed her mom's magnifying glass to take a closer look. When Daisy put the magnifying glass down, the caterpillar was gone. Where did it go? Daisy was curious so she asked her dad to help her. Dad lent her a small shovel and said, "Let's try to dig it up!" Daisy jumped right in and soon she saw the caterpillar again. It had been hiding in the soil. She said "Hi!" and it crawled away quickly. Daisy was happy she got to see the caterpillar and she thanked her dad for lending her the shovel. She then skipped off to go tell all of her friends about the curious caterpillar she found in the ground.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun. One day, Spot went with his family to the store. He was very happy. At the store, Spot saw many things he liked. He saw toys, food, and other dogs. Spot enjoyed playing with the toys and sniffing the food. He also made new friends with the other dogs. In the end, Spot and his family left the store with a new toy for him. Spot enjoyed his day at the store and couldn't wait to go back again. He played with his new toy all day and was a very happy dog.

One day, a big elephant with a long trunk walked in the forest. He heard a small voice. It was a tiny mouse. The mouse was sad. The elephant asked, "Why are you sad?" "I lost my way home," said the mouse. The elephant wanted to help the mouse. They walked together to find the mouse's home. The elephant used his big ears to hear if any other animals knew where the mouse's home was. They met a crazy, funny bird who liked to sing and dance. The bird said, "I know where your home is, little mouse!" The bird led them to a small hole under a big tree. The mouse was happy to see his home. The elephant and the crazy bird were happy too. They all became good friends and played together in the forest.

Sam and Lily were playing with their toys in the yard. Sam had a big crane that could lift and move things. Lily had a small doll that she liked to dress and comb. Sam wanted to show Lily how his crane worked, but Lily was not interested. "Look, Lily, my crane is so cool! It can pick up this rock and put it over there!" Sam said, making his crane grab a rock with its sharp claw. "I don't care, Sam. I want to play with my doll. She has a pretty dress and long hair," Lily said, holding her doll close to her. Sam felt angry. He wanted Lily to play with him and his crane. He thought his crane was better than her doll. He decided to take her doll away from her. "Give me your doll, Lily. My crane wants to play with it," Sam said, reaching for her doll. "No, Sam, don't! That's my doll! Leave her alone!" Lily said, pulling her doll back. Sam and Lily started to tug and pull on the doll. They both shouted and cried. They did not hear their mom coming out of the house. "Sam! Lily! What are you doing? Why are you fighting?" their mom asked, looking at them with a stern face. Sam and Lily stopped and looked at their mom. They felt ashamed. They knew they had been bad. "We were playing with our toys, mom. But Sam wanted to take my doll and I didn't want to give it to him," Lily said, sniffling. "I wanted to show Lily my crane, mom. But she didn't want to see it and she only cared about her doll," Sam said, lowering his head. Their mom sighed. She knew they both loved their toys, but they also needed to share and respect each other. "Sam, Lily, I understand that you have different toys and different interests. But you can't take or break each other's toys. That's not nice. You have to ask and wait for permission. And you have to be gentle and careful with them. Do you understand?" their mom said, kneeling down to their level. Sam and Lily nodded. They understood. They felt sorry for what they had done. "I'm sorry, Lily. I shouldn't have tried to take your doll. Can I see it, please?" Sam said, looking at Lily with a soft voice. "I'm sorry, Sam. I shouldn't have ignored your crane. Can I see it, please?" Lily said, looking at Sam with a sweet voice. Sam and Lily smiled. They gave each other their toys. They looked at them with curiosity and admiration. They decided to play together and learn from each other. Their mom smiled too. She was glad they had made up. She hugged them and kissed them. "That's better, Sam and Lily. I'm proud of you. Now, why don't you play quietly and nicely in the yard? I'll make you some cookies for later," their mom said, standing up and going back to the house. Sam and Lily nodded. They thanked their mom. They played quietly and nicely in the yard. They enjoyed their toys and each other. They were happy.
