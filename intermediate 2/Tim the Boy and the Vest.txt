Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Vest

One day, a little boy named Tim found an old vest in his house. He put it on and went outside to play. He saw his friend, Sue, and ran up to her. "Look at my vest!" said Tim. Sue looked at the vest and said, "I don't like it. It's old." Tim and Sue started to disagree about the vest. Tim thought it was cool, but Sue did not. While they were talking, a big wind came and blew the vest off of Tim. The wind took the vest up, up, up into the sky. Tim and Sue were very surprised. They stopped talking and just watched the vest fly away. As they looked up, they saw the vest change. It became new and shiny, with pretty colors. The wind brought the vest back down, and Tim caught it. "Wow!" said Sue. "Now I like your vest. It's not old anymore." Tim smiled and said, "I like it too!" They both laughed and went off to play together, happy that the old vest had become new again.

One day, a gentle cat and a dog were playing in the yard. They found a big pile of coal. The cat and the dog wanted to share the coal, so they took turns picking up pieces. As they were sharing the coal, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird wanted some coal too. The cat and the dog were surprised, but they let the bird join in and share the coal. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird took a piece of coal and drew a picture on the ground. The cat and the dog looked at the picture, and they saw that it was a map to a hidden toy box. The three friends followed the map, and they found the toy box together. They were all happy to share the toys and play together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a stable. The stable was home to many animals, like a cow, a pig, and a horse. The animals were happy in their stable, but they were also very bored. They wanted to do something fun. One day, a little bird flew into the stable. The bird was very brave and liked to dare other animals to do fun things. The bird dared the horse to jump over a big log. The horse was scared, but he tried it and had fun. Then, the bird dared the cow to run fast around the stable. The cow was scared too, but she also tried it and had fun. The pig watched the other animals play and have fun. The bird dared the pig to climb a small hill. The pig was scared, but he wanted to try. He climbed the hill and when he reached the top, he saw the whole village. The pig was so happy and proud. The animals in the stable were not bored anymore. They all played together, and they thanked the little bird for daring them to try new things.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He liked to poke things. One day, Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big, dry hill. Tim wanted to poke the hill. Tim's mom said, "We can't poke the hill. There is a law. The law says we can't poke things that are not ours." Tim was sad, but he knew his mom was right. On their way home, they found a small, dry stick. Tim's mom said, "You can poke this stick. It is not against the law." Tim was happy. He poked the stick all the way home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called June. She was very small and shy. June always felt kind of lonely. One day, June decided to go out and explore. She followed her little shadow, walking and walking until it was nearly dark. Suddenly, June's shadow started to blink. "What's happening?", asked June. "I'm finding a way home," said the shadow. So June and her shadow continued on until they reached home. June was no longer lonely, because she had her shadow by her side. They were together every day after that, even when it was dark and June felt scared. June's shadow would always blink and bring her home.
