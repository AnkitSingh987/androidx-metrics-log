Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Cake

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the kitchen. They see a big cake on the table. It is Mom's birthday cake. It has pink frosting and sprinkles. "Let's taste the cake," Lily says. "It looks yummy." "No, we can't," Ben says. "Mom will be mad. She made the cake for her birthday." "But I want to taste it," Lily says. "Just a little bit. She won't notice." Lily takes a knife from the drawer. She tries to cut a small piece of the cake. But the knife is sharp and she cuts her finger. She cries out loud. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily says. "It hurts!" Ben hears Lily cry. He runs to her. He sees blood on her finger and on the cake. "Oh no, Lily!" Ben says. "You cut yourself and the cake! Mom will be very mad!" He wraps a napkin around Lily's finger. He tries to fix the cake. But it is too late. Mom comes into the kitchen. She sees the mess. "What happened here?" Mom says. "Who cut the cake and why?" Lily and Ben look at Mom. They feel sorry and scared. They don't know what to say. Mom sees their faces. She sees Lily's finger. She feels sorry and worried. She hugs them both. "It's okay, my cuties," Mom says. "I'm not mad. I'm glad you're not hurt badly. But you shouldn't play with knives or touch the cake. They are dangerous and not for you. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nod. They say sorry to Mom. They say happy birthday to Mom. They promise to be good. Mom smiles. She kisses them. She says she loves them. She says they can have some cake later. She says they are her cute twins.

Mum and Dad were getting ready to go on holidays. "We must hurry!" said Mum. She grabbed her passport and Dad's passport. They were going to the airport. On the way, they saw a beautiful park. Mum and Dad looked at each other and smiled. "We should stop!" said Mum. Dad was feeling tired, but he agreed. So they stopped at the park and played on the swings. They were having so much fun! But before they knew it, the sun was setting. "We have to go," said Mum. "We need to get to the airport soon!" Mum and Dad rushed to the car and drove to the airport. But unfortunately, it was too late. They missed their flight and had to wait for the next one. They were so tired and very sad.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Lily. She had a cat named Tom. Lily and Tom loved to play. One day, Lily heard about a holiday. This holiday was unusual. It was a day when everyone would clap and applaud for their pets. Lily was very excited. Lily wanted to teach Tom a trick for the holiday. She thought and thought. Then, she had an idea. Lily would teach Tom to jump through a hoop. Every day, Lily and Tom practiced. Tom would jump, and Lily would applaud. They were getting ready for the big day. Finally, the unusual holiday came. Lily and Tom went to the park. All the people and pets were there. It was time for Tom to show his trick. Lily held the hoop, and Tom jumped through it. Everyone clapped and applauded. Lily and Tom were so happy. They knew their hard work had paid off.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird called Charlie. Charlie was a happy bird who loved to soar in the sky. One day he decided to go on an adventure and explore the countryside. As he flew along, he noticed something. In the distance he saw a wreck. Charlie was curious so he flew closer to see what it was. When he got there, he saw a broken-down caravan. It was empty, but it looked miserable. Charlie felt sad. He perched on a nearby tree and looked around. Suddenly he heard a voice come from the wreck. It was a little mouse! "Help me", she said. Charlie flew down and peered inside the caravan. In the corner he saw the mouse, who was stuck in the wreck. Charlie smiled at her. He grabbed a twig and used it to help the mouse get out. The little mouse was so relieved to be free. Charlie and the mouse became best friends. All around the country together they'd soar, exploring new places and having so much fun.
