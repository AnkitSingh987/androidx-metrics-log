Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bunny and the Garden

Once there was a little bunny who lived in a home. He was always very alert and kept watch over the garden. He was always very careful and always trusted his instincts. One day he heard a noise coming from the other side of the garden. He was a bit scared but he trusted that he was safe. He hopped closer to have a look. That's when he saw a big, scary fox! The fox didn't notice the bunny and just walked away. But the little bunny was still alert and hopped back to his home. Inside his home he felt safe again. He knew that he could trust that his home was the safest place to be. He put his head down and fell asleep, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a young cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a big house with a nice family. Fluffy liked to lie in the sun and play with her toy ball. One day, Fluffy met a new friend named Whiskers. Whiskers was a small mouse. Fluffy and Whiskers liked to play together. They would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Whiskers said to Fluffy, "My back hurts." Fluffy wanted to help her friend. Fluffy gave Whiskers a massage with her soft paws. Whiskers felt better and said, "Thank you, Fluffy." Fluffy and Whiskers played together every day. They were very happy and had lots of fun. And whenever Whiskers felt sore, Fluffy would give her a massage to help her feel better.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He wanted to find a big, pretty rock to give to his mom. The sun was shining and the sky was pale blue. Tim walked and walked, looking at the ground to find a rock. As he walked, Tim saw a track on the ground. He thought, "Maybe a big animal made this track!" He followed the track, hoping to find the animal. The track went on and on, and Tim kept walking. Finally, Tim found a big, pretty rock near the track. He picked it up and said, "This is perfect for Mom!" He also saw a big, friendly dog near the rock. Tim asked the dog, "Did you make the track?" The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, I did!" Tim and the dog walked back home together, happy to have found each other and the perfect rock for Tim's mom.

One day, a little girl named Poppy went for a walk. She saw a big dog and a small cat. The dog and the cat were not happy. They looked confused. Poppy wanted to help them. Poppy saw what happened. The dog's ball was stuck in a tree. The cat's toy was in the dog's house. Poppy had an idea. She got the ball from the tree and gave it to the dog. Then, she got the toy from the dog's house and gave it to the cat. The dog and the cat were happy now. They were not confused anymore. Poppy felt good. She helped the dog and the cat. They all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little lamp. The lamp had a tight hat that it wore all the time. The lamp liked to go on walks with its hat. One day, the lamp decided to go to the park. It saw kids playing and birds singing. The lamp felt very happy to be outside with its tight hat. As the sun went down, the lamp went back home. It was time to shine bright and help people see in the dark. The lamp felt proud to be a good helper. And every day, the lamp would go on new adventures with its tight hat.

Once upon a time, there was a young fox. The fox was very happy. He liked to play in the sun. One day, he went to the park. He saw a big tree and a pond with ducks. The young fox met a dog. The dog said, "Hello, fox! Do you want to play with me?" The fox said, "Yes! I enjoy playing with new friends." So, the fox and the dog played together. They ran around the tree and chased the ducks. After playing, the young fox and the dog were very tired. They lay down under the tree. The fox said, "I had so much fun today." The dog said, "Me too! Let's play again tomorrow." The young fox and the dog became best friends and enjoyed playing together every day.
