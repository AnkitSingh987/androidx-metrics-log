Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a stick on the ground. Tim picked up the stick and started to play with it. He felt happy because he liked the stick. Tim saw a sad bird sitting on a tree. The bird looked miserable. Tim asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I am cold. I need something to wrap around me to keep me warm." Tim wanted to help the bird. He took the stick and found a soft leaf. He wrapped the leaf around the bird using the stick. The bird felt warm and happy now. "Thank you, Tim!" said the bird. They both smiled and played together with the stick. They were not miserable anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog named Max. Max loved to sleep and kick his legs while he dreamed. One day, Max found a note under his bed. The note had big letters that said, "Come to the park." Max went to the park and saw his friend, a cat named Luna. Luna said, "Max, I found a note too! Let's play here." They played and kicked a ball. They had lots of fun. Suddenly, a big bird came down and took the ball. Max and Luna were sad. But then, the bird came back with a bigger ball. The bird said, "I saw your notes and wanted to play too!" Max, Luna, and the bird played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tom. He loved to count and learn numbers. Every week, he would play a numbers game with his friends. One day, Tom and his friends decided to play the game near a big road. They were so excited to find out who could count the highest number. They started to count, "One, two, three..." As they were playing, a big car came down the road. The car was going very fast. Tom and his friends did not see the car because they were too busy counting their numbers. Sadly, the car hit Tom and his friends. They were hurt and could not play the numbers game anymore. The thoughtful boy and his friends learned that they should never play near a big road again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was always ready to play outside. One day, Tim went to the park to fly his kite. The wind was blowing just right. Tim was very happy. As Tim flew his kite, he saw a big tree. The tree was very tall and had many leaves. Tim thought it would be fun to climb the tree. He laughed and started to climb. The wind blew harder, making the tree shake. Tim climbed higher and higher. But the wind was too strong. Suddenly, Tim lost his grip and fell down. He landed on the ground and hurt his leg. Tim was sad and cried. His kite flew away, and he couldn't play anymore.

Once there was a little girl and she was three years old. She was playing in her room when she heard a loud noise. She got scared and called for her daddy. Daddy quickly came into the room and saw what had made the loud noise. There was a big box on the floor. Daddy said it was nothing to be scared of and asked the little girl if she wanted to push it. The little girl was scared and didn't want to push the box but daddy told her it was alright. He said he would help her push the box so it would be less scary. The little girl grabbed one side of the box and daddy grabbed the other side. Together they pushed it across the room until it was in the right spot. The little girl was very excited and happy. She was no longer scared of the big box. She thanked daddy for his help and hugged him tight. Daddy smiled and said he was glad she wasn't scared anymore. Then they went back to playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a fragile computer in a big house. The computer was very special to a little girl named Lucy. She liked to play games on it with her friends. One day, a naughty boy named Tom came to the house. He wanted to steal the computer. Tom sneaked into the house and saw the fragile computer. He said, "I will take this computer and play games at my house." But then Lucy saw Tom and shouted, "No! That is my computer! You cannot take it!" Tom felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Lucy. I will not steal your computer." Lucy said, "Thank you, Tom. You can play games with me and my friends instead." So Tom and Lucy played games on the fragile computer together, and they all had fun.
