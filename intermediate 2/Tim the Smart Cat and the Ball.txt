Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Smart Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, while playing, his ball vanished. Tim looked everywhere but could not find his ball. He felt sad and missed his ball. Tim asked his friend, a dog named Sam, "Have you seen my ball?" Sam shook his head and said, "No, but I will help you look for it." They looked under the trees and behind the bushes, but the ball was still not there. Finally, they asked a wise old owl named Mr. Wise. He listened to their question and said, "I saw your ball roll into the big hole by the tree." Tim and Sam went to the big hole and found the ball. They were so happy! From that day on, they always played together and never let the ball vanish again.

One day, a cat and a dog wanted to sit on a big sack. The sack was in the sun and it was nice and warm. The cat got to the sack first and sat down. The dog came and saw the cat on the sack. The dog was not happy. He wanted to sit on the sack too. The dog said to the cat, "I want to sit on the sack!" The cat said, "No! I got here first!" The dog was very sad and felt bitter. He wanted to find a way to sit on the sack too. Then, the dog had an idea. He said to the cat, "Let's share the sack! You can sit on one side, and I can sit on the other side." The cat thought about it and said, "Okay, we can share the sack." So, the cat and the dog both sat on the sack, and they were both happy in the warm sun.

Once upon a time, there was a rare sunflower. It was in a big field with other flowers. The sunflower was sad because it was different. One day, a little girl came to the field. She saw the rare sunflower and wanted to help. She tried to solve the sunflower's problem. The girl thought if she moved the sunflower, it would be happy. The girl pulled the sunflower out of the ground. But the sunflower needed the ground to live. The sunflower died, and the little girl was sad. She learned that sometimes, it's okay to be different.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Bob. Bob loved to swim all day long. He was a very happy fish. One day, Bob went on a trip to a big museum. He was very excited. When Bob got to the museum, he felt a little anxious. There were so many big things to see. He saw a big dinosaur, a tall rocket, and lots of colorful paintings. But Bob could not swim in the museum, so he was a little sad. Bob's friend, a little girl named Sue, saw that he was sad. She had an idea! She took Bob to a big pond outside the museum. Bob was so happy to swim again. They played and laughed all day. And from that day on, Bob and Sue were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. It was a mild evening, and the sun was going down. Lily loved to skip in her yard, so she put on her shoes and went outside. As Lily skipped around, she saw a cute bunny. The bunny hopped and Lily skipped. They played together in the yard, having lots of fun. The sky turned pretty colors as the sun went down. Soon, it was time for Lily to go inside. She said goodbye to the bunny and went into her house. After a fun evening of skipping and playing, Lily was ready for bed. She fell asleep with a big smile on her face, dreaming of her new bunny friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, dark forest. In the forest, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with her friends, but one day, she got lost in the dark forest. While walking, Lily found a shiny shield on the ground. She picked it up and said, "Wow, this is a pretty shield!" Just then, a big monster came out from behind a tree. The monster wanted to take the shield from Lily. Lily was scared, but she held the shield tight. The monster tried to break the shield, but it was too strong. "You can't break my shield!" said Lily. The monster felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, I just wanted to play. Can I be your friend?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, let's be friends!" And they played together in the dark forest, safe and happy with the strong shield.
