Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Toys

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. But they do not like to clean up their toys. Their room is very dirty. There are toys everywhere. One day, their mom says, "Sara and Ben, you have to clean up your room. It is too dirty. If you do not clean up, you will not get any cookies after dinner." Sara and Ben do not listen to their mom. They say, "We do not want to clean up. We want to play more." They keep playing with their toys. They make more mess. They throw their toys in the air. They build towers and knock them down. They laugh and have fun. But then, something bad happens. Ben throws a toy car in the air. It hits the lamp on the table. The lamp falls down and breaks. The light goes out. The room is dark. Sara and Ben are scared. They cry and call for their mom. Their mom comes and sees the broken lamp and the dirty room. She is angry and sad. She says, "Sara and Ben, you did not listen to me. You did not clean up your room. You broke the lamp. Now you have to go to bed without any cookies. And tomorrow, you have to clean up your room very well." Sara and Ben say, "We are sorry, mom. We will clean up our room. We will not play with our toys so much. We will listen to you next time." Their mom hugs them and says, "I love you, Sara and Ben. But you have to learn to be responsible. Cleaning up your room is important. It makes your room nice and safe. And it makes me happy." She takes them to bed and kisses them good night. Sara and Ben feel sorry and sad. They wish they had cleaned up their room. They learn their lesson. The end.

Tommy was going on a trip. He was so excited! He ran to his room to pack his things. He put his toys, clothes and a few books into his suitcase. Then he hugged his mom and dad goodbye and they drove to the airport. On the way they saw some amazing sights. There were tall buildings, big parks and lots of cars. When they arrived at the airport, Tommy was so excited he almost forgot his suitcase! At the airport they said goodbye and Tommy got on the plane. It was a little bit scary but the flight attendants were friendly and soon the plane took off. Tommy was so happy to see the clouds and the ground below. Finally the plane arrived at Tommy's destination. It was a new city and Tommy couldn't wait to explore. But it was getting dark and Tommy's mom said it was time to go to the hotel. At the hotel, Tommy was surprised at how terrible it looked. The walls were crumbling and the furniture was old. But it was still the most exciting place Tommy had ever seen! He couldn't wait to explore the city in the morning. So Tommy unpacked his things and settled into the night. He dreamed of all the wonderful sights he'd see the next day.

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny land, there lived a big ostrich named Ollie. Ollie liked to run and play with his friends. One day, Ollie and his friends decided to play a game with a ball. Ollie said, "Let's throw the ball to each other and see who can catch it!" His friends agreed and they started to play. Ollie was very good at catching the ball because he was tall and fast. As they played, a little girl named Lily came to watch them. She saw how much fun they were having and asked, "Can I play too?" Ollie and his friends welcomed her and gave her a turn to throw the ball. Lily threw the ball high into the air, and Ollie jumped up to catch it. Everyone clapped and cheered for them. They all played together until the sun went down, and they were all very happy. From that day on, Ollie, Lily, and their friends played together every day. They had lots of fun throwing the ball and running in the bright sun. And they all became the best of friends.
