Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bug and the Fly

Once, there was a bug who loved to crawl. He had a friend, Fly. Fly liked to fly high in the sky. Bug wished he could fly too, but he had no wings. One day, Bug found a pair of fake wings. He put them on and asked Fly, "Can I fly with you now?" Fly looked at the wings and said, "Let's try!" Bug tried to fly, but the fake wings did not work. He felt sad. Fly saw his friend and said, "Don't be sad, Bug. We can still play together on the ground." Bug and Fly played and crawled together. They had lots of fun. Bug learned that he did not need to fly to be happy with his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a fish who lived in a reef. The fish had a friend named Crab. They liked to play together in the water. One day, the fish and the crab found a dress. The dress was pretty and blue. They wanted to wear it, but they were scared. They heard a scary noise near the reef. The fish and the crab decided to be brave. They put on the dress together. The scary noise was just a big, friendly whale. The fish, the crab, and the whale had a fun day playing dress-up in the reef.

Once upon a time, there was a little hut in the woods. In the hut lived a kind bunny named Bob. Bob's hut was very filthy, but he didn't mind. He liked to play in the dirt all day. One day, a small bird named Sue came to visit Bob. Sue saw the filthy hut and said, "Oh no, your hut is so dirty!" Bob looked around and said, "Yes, but I like it this way." Sue wanted to help Bob clean his hut, so they worked together. They picked up the dirt and put it outside. After they finished, Bob felt happy with his clean hut. He gave Sue a big hug and said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue hugged him back and said, "You're welcome, Bob!" From that day on, Bob and Sue were the best of friends. They played together in the woods and always made sure to keep the hut clean. And whenever they were happy, they would hug each other and laugh.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucy who was three. She loved many things, but the thing she loved most of all was her bicycle. Every day she would go out on her bicycle and have the most wonderful rides. One day, while she was riding down the street, she saw some older kids bullying a small boy. Lucy was so angry that she shouted at the kids to stop. The kids laughed at Lucy and started to throw stones. Lucy tried to run away with her bicycle but the big kids caught up with her and her bike was broken. Lucy began to cry because she knew her parents would be angry. She was so ashamed of herself. The next day, when Lucy's parents asked her what happened she just said that she did not like her bicycle anymore and she wanted a new one. But Lucy knew deep down that she didn't actually hate her bike, she was just ashamed of what she had done. She knew she had been wrong to stand up to the kids, but she was also angry with them for breaking her bicycle.

Once upon a time, there was a 3 year old called Peaches. Peaches loved to play in the garden, and one day she decided to dig a big hole. After some digging, she found something strange - it was a box full of shrimp! Peaches was so excited and she quickly opened the box. But when she did, she realised the shrimp were spoiled. Peaches started to cry and shouted, "I don't want this! Everything is so yucky!" A kind fairy appeared and said, "It's ok! We can still use the shrimp. I'll show you how." The fairy used her magic wand to make a delicious shrimp dish. Peaches tasted it and said, "Wow, this is yummy! I'm glad I have this." The fairy smiled. Peaches had learned an important lesson - things can still be useful, even if they're spoiled!
