Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Book

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big box under a tree. It looks like a chest. "Wow, what is this?" Lily asks. "Maybe it has toys inside," Ben says. They open the chest. It is full of books, puzzles, balls, and dolls. "Look, Lily, a book about animals!" Ben says. He picks up the book and shows it to Lily. "Look, Ben, a puzzle of a rainbow!" Lily says. She picks up the puzzle and shows it to Ben. They are very happy. They take out more things from the chest and play with them. "Who gave us this chest?" Lily wonders. "I don't know. Maybe someone nice," Ben says. They see a note on the chest. It says: "To Lily and Ben, from Grandma and Grandpa. We hope you enjoy these gifts. We love you very much." Lily and Ben smile. They know Grandma and Grandpa are great. They love them too. They run to the phone and call Grandma and Grandpa. "Thank you, thank you, thank you!" they say. "You gave us a great chest. We like it a lot. We love you too." Grandma and Grandpa are happy to hear their voices. They tell them they are welcome and they are proud of them. Lily and Ben hug the phone. They are very lucky to have Grandma and Grandpa. They are very lucky to have each other. They are very lucky to have a great chest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play with her toy shapes. One day, she was playing with her shapes when she saw a dull, gray shape on the floor. She picked it up and said, "What shape is this?" A tiny voice came from the shape, "I am a star, but I am not shiny like the other stars. I am sad because I am dull." Sue wanted to help the star. She said, "Do not quit, little star. I will make you shiny and happy again!" Sue took the star to her mom, who helped her paint the star a bright, shiny yellow. The star was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sue! Now I am shiny like the other stars!" Sue smiled and played with the star and her other shapes all day long. From that day on, Sue and the shiny star were the best of friends. They played together every day, and the star was never dull or sad again.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big, clear book in her room. She was very happy. She opened the book and saw many pretty pictures. Lily wanted to show the book to her best friend, Tom. Lily ran to Tom's house with the book. She knocked on the door and Tom came out. "Look, Tom!" Lily said. "I found a big, clear book with pretty pictures!" Tom looked at the book and smiled. They both sat down and looked at the pictures together. Suddenly, they saw a picture of a big, scary monster. Lily and Tom screamed, "Ahh!" Lily's mom heard them scream and came to see what was wrong. "Don't worry," she said, "it's just a picture in the book." Lily and Tom felt better. They laughed and kept looking at the book, enjoying the rest of the pretty pictures.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boring man who loved pizza. He ate pizza every day for lunch and dinner. One day, he saw a small seed on the ground. He picked it up and thought, "I will plant this seed and see what grows." The next day, he planted the seed in his yard. He watered it and waited for it to grow. After a few days, a small plant started to grow. The man was very happy. He took care of the plant every day, and it got bigger and bigger. He thought it was a normal plant. One sunny day, the plant started to bloom. But it was not a normal flower. It was a pizza flower! The boring man was so surprised and excited. He picked the pizza flowers and shared them with his friends. Everyone in the town loved the pizza flowers, and the boring man became very popular. From that day on, the town was never boring again.
