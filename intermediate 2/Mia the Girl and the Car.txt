Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a toy car that she loved to play with. One day, she wanted to push her toy car up a big hill, but it was very difficult. Mia tried and tried, but the car would not go up the hill. Then, Mia had an idea. She found a sparkly stick and put it behind the car. She pushed the stick and the car together. With the help of the sparkly stick, Mia was able to push her toy car up the big hill. She was so happy! At the top of the hill, Mia played with her toy car and the sparkly stick. She had so much fun! When the sun went down, Mia knew it was time to go home. She pushed her toy car back down the hill and took her sparkly stick with her. From that day on, Mia knew that even difficult things can be done with a little help.

One day, a thoughtful boy named Tim went to the sea with his mom. He saw a small fish in the water. The fish looked sad. Tim wanted to help the fish. Tim found a big rock. He thought, "I will throw the rock in the sea to make a big splash. The fish will be happy and not sad." He picked up the rock and threw it into the sea. The fish jumped and smiled. Tim's mom saw what he did. She said, "Tim, you did a good thing. You helped the fish and made it happy." Tim learned that it is good to help others. He felt happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Bella. Bella loved to play with her plastic ball. One day, she saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Bella went up the tree and found a nice branch to sit on. She saw a bird and tried to catch it, but she slipped and fell. Bella was scared, but she landed on something soft. It was her plastic ball! Bella was so happy that her plastic ball saved her. She rubbed her head on it to say thank you. From that day on, Bella and her plastic ball were always together, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely girl named Lily. She had a pretty box with a big jewel on it. Lily loved her jewel box very much. She kept her special things inside the box. One day, Lily's friend Tom came to play. Tom saw the jewel box and wanted to take it home. Lily was sad and didn't want to lose her lovely box. So, she had an idea. She would tie the box to her bed, so Tom could not take it. Lily found a big, strong rope and tied her jewel box to her bed. When Tom saw this, he knew he could not take the box. Tom was sad, but Lily told him they could share the lovely box when they played together. Tom was happy and they played with the jewel box all day.

One day, a sad old dog was walking in the park. He saw a little girl playing with a ball. The dog wanted to play too, but he was too old and slow. He sat down and watched the girl play. The girl saw the sad old dog and walked up to him. She said, "Hi dog, do you want to play with me?" The dog wagged his tail and barked. The girl took the ball and threw it. The dog tried to run, but he was too slow. The girl saw the dog was too old to play, so she sat down next to him. She said, "It's okay, dog. We can just sit and watch together." The dog wagged his tail and licked her hand. They sat together in the park, happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer who had a very useful mule. He lived in a small village and the mule helped him with his everyday chores. One day, the farmer had a great idea and he decided to build a cart for the mule. He and the mule started gathering wood and supplies. "This cart will be so useful," said the farmer. "We will be able to carry heavy things easily!" The farmer and the mule worked very hard to build the cart for a few days. Finally, it was ready! They put the cart in front of the mule, and the farmer tied it to the mule's harness. The mule pulled the cart around the village with ease. The villagers were so impressed with the farmer and his mule. The farmer had built something very useful. The villagers congratulated him. They all agreed that the farmer and his mule were an even better team now. They were proud of the useful cart they had built.
