Category: Intermediate
Title: Mark the Famous Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Mark. Mark loved to play in the kitchen. One day, he found a big freezer. Mark was very curious about the freezer. Mark asked his friend, a little bird, "What is this big cold box?" The little bird said, "It is a freezer. It keeps food cold." Mark wanted to see what was inside the freezer. He asked the little bird to help him open it. Together, they opened the freezer. Inside, they found ice cream and cold treats. Mark and the little bird laughed and shared the treats. They had a fun day playing with the freezer and eating ice cream.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat, but he was very impatient. He always wanted his food right away. One day, Tim's mom was making a yummy sauce for dinner. Tim could not wait and wanted to eat it now. His mom said, "Wait, Tim! The sauce is not done yet." But Tim did not want to wait. Tim tried to quit being impatient, but he could not. He took the sauce from the pot and put it on his plate. It was too hot! Tim cried and his mom came to help. She told him to be patient next time. Tim learned his lesson and never took the sauce too soon again.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly horse. The horse loved to run and play in the park. Every day, the horse would go to the park and have fun with his friends. The sun was always shining, and the birds were always singing. One day, a big, mean dog came to the park. The dog did not like the horse. The dog said, "This is my park now, and you can't play here." The horse was sad, but he did not want to fight with the dog. So, the horse left the park and went home. The next day, the horse went back to the park, hoping the mean dog was gone. But the dog was still there. The dog saw the horse and chased him away again. The horse was very sad and knew he could not go back to the park anymore. The sun stopped shining, and the birds stopped singing. The horse missed his friends and the fun times they had in the park.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful land, there was a cliff near the sea. On the cliff, there was a store. The store had many toys and yummy treats for kids. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a big, red ball in the store. He asked his mom if he could have it. His mom said yes, and they bought the ball. Tim and his mom went to the beach near the cliff. They played with the big, red ball. They had lots of fun. The sun was shining, and the sea was calm. It was a happy day for Tim and his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to pretend he was a race car driver. He would run around the house with great speed, making car noises. One day, Tom was pretending to race when he saw his friend, a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry wanted to play too. "Let's pretend to race together!" said Tom. Jerry was happy and they started their race. As they raced, something unexpected happened. Tom and Jerry found a hidden door in the house. They opened it and saw a room full of toys and treats! They forgot about the race and played with the toys and ate the treats all day long. They became the best of friends and shared their secret room with each other.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Jack. He wanted to see what was on the big screen on the wall. He walked over, waved his hands and said to it: "Hello, please show me something!" But the screen remained blank. Jack was very disappointed. He tried again and again and waved his hands harder and harder, but nothing happened. He was starting to get cross and was getting less and less patient. Suddenly, he heard his mom's voice behind him. "Jack, you have to be patient. I can show you what you want, but you have to be patient." Jack nodded and took a few deep breaths. Mom turned to the screen and waved her hands, and suddenly the screen came alive and showed Jack a movie about a brave knight going on an adventure. Jack smiled and clapped. Mom had showed him what he wanted, and he was very happy. In the end Jack learned that being patient was the best way to get what he wanted.
