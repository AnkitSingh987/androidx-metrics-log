Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and jump all day. One sunny day, Spot went to the park to play. At the park, Spot saw a vendor selling toys. Spot wanted to join and see the toys too. He wagged his tail and ran to the vendor. The vendor smiled and showed Spot a big, red ball. Just when Spot was about to play with the ball, a little bird flew down. The bird picked up the ball in its beak and flew away! Spot was very surprised. He thought the bird was joining to play too. Spot chased the bird and the ball. The bird dropped the ball into a pond. Spot jumped into the water and got the ball. Now, Spot, the bird, and the vendor all played together. They had lots of fun in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to do exercise every day. He liked to run, jump, and play with his ball. Max was very strong and happy. One day, Max found an empty box in his yard. He thought it would be fun to play with the empty box. Max decided to pull the box around the yard. He put the box on the grass and used his teeth to pull it. As Max pulled the box, he saw his friends, the little birds. They wanted to play too. So, Max pulled the empty box faster and faster. The birds laughed and flew around Max. They all had a great time playing with the empty box.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gloomy day. The sky was full of dark clouds. Tom, a little boy, went out to play. He saw a bird lay an egg in a nest up in a tree. Tom wanted to see the egg up close, but he was too short to reach the nest. Tom had an idea. He went to his dad's shed and found a tool. It was a long stick. He thought he could use the stick to get the egg from the nest. He showed the stick to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "That's not a good idea, Tom. You might break the egg." Tom thought about what Sam said, and he realized that his friend was right. He put the stick back in the shed and decided to just watch the nest from far away. He learned that it's better to be careful and think about the consequences of his actions. The clouds went away, and the sun came out. Tom and Sam played outside all day, happy that they had made the right choice. The bird and her egg were safe, and Tom learned to be more careful with his actions. And that was the end of the gloomy day.

Once upon a time there was a family of birds who wanted to find a place to build their crib. One day, they found a perfect spot: a tall tree with lots of leaves. So they decided to build the crib there. The birds worked hard building their crib. They flew from tree to tree, gathering twigs and leaves, then they carefully put them all together. Finally, after a lot of hard work, the crib was finished. When it was done, the birds were so glad. They had a safe, comfortable home for all of them and their little baby birds, who were still too small to fly. Now, instead of having to look for food all the time they could stay in the crib and relax. Moral: When you work together to serve a common purpose, you can accomplish great things. So always be glad to share in the work and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a curious boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect things. One day, he went on a walk to find something new to collect. As Tim walked, he saw a big castle. He was very excited! He went inside the castle and saw a friendly king. The king said, "Hello, Tim! What are you looking for?" Tim said, "I want to collect something new. Can you help me?" The king thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, I have an idea!" The king took Tim to a room full of beautiful rocks. Tim was so happy! He picked up a shiny red rock and said, "Thank you, king! I will add this to my collection!" From that day on, Tim and the king were great friends. They would often meet at the castle and collect new things together. And they lived happily ever after.
