Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Compassionate Girl and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lily. She loved to help others and make them happy. One day, she found a big piece of metal in her yard. She thought of a way to use it to spread joy. Lily asked her dad to help her make the metal into a big, shiny slide. They worked hard together and soon, the slide was ready. Lily invited all her friends to come and play on the new slide. Everyone had fun going up and down the slide. They laughed and smiled as they played. Lily was happy to see her friends enjoying the slide. She knew that her compassionate heart had helped spread happiness to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red hotel. It was a brilliant hotel that many people liked. One day, a nice man came to the hotel. He wanted to find a good place to stay. The hotel boss said, "I recommend our best room for you!" The nice man went to the room and saw a big, soft bed. He was very happy. But at night, something bad happened. The man heard a loud noise. It was a big storm outside. The storm was very strong and scary. The storm broke the hotel. The nice man was very sad. The brilliant hotel was not safe anymore. The hotel boss said, "I am sorry, we cannot help you now." The nice man left the broken hotel and looked for a new place to stay.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the park. She had an amazing red ball. Sue saw her friend Tim there. Tim had a big blue mug. They played together and had fun. Then, a big wind came. It blew Sue's amazing red ball far away. Sue and Tim were sad. They looked for the ball but could not find it. Suddenly, the wind struck again. It blew Tim's big blue mug high in the sky. The mug flew and hit the ball. The amazing red ball came back to Sue and Tim. They were very happy and played together again. The wind helped them find the ball in a surprising way.

Tom and Lily were playing in the yard. They liked to run and jump and roll on the grass. They also liked to look at the flowers and the bugs. Sometimes they picked some flowers and gave them to their mom. One day, they saw a big, shiny book on the porch. It had many pictures and words inside. Tom and Lily wanted to see what the book was about. They ran to the porch and grabbed the book. "Let me see, let me see!" Tom said, pulling the book from Lily. "No, I want to see, I want to see!" Lily said, pulling the book from Tom. They pulled and pulled and pulled. The book was very strong, but they were stronger. They pulled so hard that the book tore in half. The pages flew everywhere. Some of the pages landed on the grass, some on the flowers, some on the bugs. Tom and Lily stopped pulling. They looked at the book. They looked at each other. They looked at the yard. They felt very bad. They had broken the book. They had made a mess. They had hurt the flowers and the bugs. They heard their mom coming. She had heard the noise. She saw the book. She saw the yard. She saw Tom and Lily. She was very angry. She was very sad. "What have you done?" she asked, in a loud voice. "This was my favorite book. This was a gift from grandma. This was very special. How could you tear it?" Tom and Lily did not know what to say. They did not know what a gift was. They did not know what special meant. They were very confused. They were very scared. They started to cry. Mom took the book and the pages. She went inside. She did not talk to Tom and Lily. She did not hug them. She did not smile at them. Tom and Lily stayed in the yard. They felt very bad. They felt very alone. They wished they had not torn the book. They wished they had not played with it. They wished they had listened to mom. They wished they could say sorry. But they did not know how.
