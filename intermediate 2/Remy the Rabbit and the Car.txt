Category: Intermediate
Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Car

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a small rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to play with his stuff like his ball and his toy car. One day, Remy saw a big, dark hole in the ground. He was curious and wanted to see what was inside. But his mommy told him to stay calm and not to go near the hole, because something bad might happen. Remy didn't listen to his mommy. One day, when his mommy was not looking, Remy went to the hole and fell inside. It was dark and scary. Remy wanted to escape, but he didn't know how. He started to cry and wished he had listened to his mommy. Then, Remy remembered his toy car. He had an idea! He put the toy car on the ground and pushed it really hard. The toy car went up and out of the hole. Remy's mommy saw the toy car and knew that Remy was in the hole. She quickly came and helped Remy escape. Remy was so happy to be out of the hole and back with his mommy. He promised to always listen to her and stay calm when she told him something important.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn dog named Max. He lived in a small house with a big yard. Max loved to play with his ball all day. One sunny day, Max's friend, a bird named Tim, came to visit. Tim urged Max to play a new game. Max did not want to play the new game yet. He still wanted to play with his ball. But Tim was excited and took Max's ball away. Max was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Tim and the ball up into the sky! Max and Tim had to work together to get the ball back. They became best friends and played the new game together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a cold day, so he wore a big coat. He saw a long rope on the ground. Tim had an idea. He wanted to fold the rope and make a fun game. Tim called his friend, Sue. "Sue, come play with me!" he said. Sue ran to Tim, excited to play. She saw the rope and smiled. "Let's fold the rope and jump over it!" Sue said. They folded the rope and took turns jumping. As they played, more friends came. They all wanted to play with the rope. Tim and Sue showed them how to fold the rope and jump. They laughed and played together. The cold day became warm with fun and friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a dream that she found a magic thread. This thread could make her toys come to life. In her dream, Lily played with her toys all day long. They laughed and danced together, and it was so much fun. One day, Lily was playing with her toys when she saw a thread on the floor. It was the same magic thread from her dream! She was very excited and quickly tied the thread to her toys. Just like in her dream, her toys came to life. They all played together and had a great time. But then, something unexpected happened. The toys started to get tired and wanted to rest. Lily was still alert and wanted to play more, but the toys were too sleepy. They asked Lily to take off the magic thread so they could sleep. Lily felt sad but understood. She took off the thread and the toys went back to being normal. From that day on, Lily learned to enjoy her playtime with her toys, even if they couldn't come to life.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to provide her with a pot. Sue was very happy to help. Sue looked and looked for the pot. She found it under the sink. But, oh no! The pot was disgusting! It had dirt and yucky stuff all over it. Sue knew she had to clean the pot for her mom. Sue washed and scrubbed the pot until it was shiny and clean. She gave the pot to her mom with a big smile. Her mom was so proud of Sue for helping and making the pot clean. They cooked a yummy meal together and had a great day.
