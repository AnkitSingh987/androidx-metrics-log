Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Nest

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a comfortable nest on a big tree. One day, Tim saw a new twig on the ground. Tim thought it would be good for his nest. Tim said to his friend, the ant, "I want to load the twig to my nest. Can you help me?" The ant said, "Yes, let's do it together!" So, they both picked up the twig and carried it to the nest. After they put the twig in the nest, Tim and the ant sat down. They were very happy with their work. Tim said, "Thank you, friend. My nest is even more comfortable now!" The ant said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad I could help!" And they both smiled and enjoyed the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a large boat. A daddy and his little girl liked to sail on the boat. They went to the big blue sea to have fun. The sun was shining, and the water was calm. One day, the daddy saw a small boat in trouble. The daddy and the little girl decided to help. They sailed their large boat to the small boat. They pulled the small boat to safety. The people in the small boat were happy. The little girl learned that it is good to help others. She was proud of her daddy. They sailed back home with smiles on their faces. The little girl knew that helping others made the world a better place.

Once there was a bear. This bear had something very special. It was a colorful way to pay. The bear was always happy to pay with this way. It was very fun and special. But one day, the bear noticed something strange. It saw another bear with the same colorful way to pay. The bear was so angry. He couldn't understand why the other bear had a way to pay too. It was his special way. The bear was so angry, he ran to find the other bear. He wanted to take its way to pay. But luckily, the other bear was too smart for the bear! The other bear showed the bear a whole new way to pay. It was even more colorful and magical than the first way. The bear was amazed and thanked the other bear for showing him this magical way. From that day on, the bear used the new way to pay and was always very happy with it.

Once upon a time, there was a famous muffin. Everyone in the town loved the muffin. One day, the muffin was on the table. A little girl named Lily saw it. She wanted to eat the muffin. Lily's mom said, "Wait, we will eat it later." Lily went to play with her toys. She forgot about the muffin. Then, something unexpected happened. The muffin disappeared! Lily looked everywhere for the muffin. She asked her mom, "Where is the muffin?" Her mom did not know. Lily was sad. She thought the muffin was gone forever. But then, her dog, Max, came into the room. Max had crumbs on his face. The muffin did not disappear! Max ate the famous muffin. Lily laughed and hugged Max. They played together all day.

One day, a little dog named Max was walking in the park. It was a hot day, and Max was very thirsty. He saw a big red boot by a tree. Max thought it was a toy and wanted to play with it. Max went to the boot and tried to pick it up. But the boot was too heavy for him. Max started to tremble because he was tired and hot. Then, he heard a voice say, "Hey, little dog, are you okay?" Max looked up and saw a kind girl standing there. She said, "I see you are hot and tired. Let me help you." The girl took off her hat and filled it with water from a nearby fountain. Max drank the water, and he felt much better. The girl picked up the big red boot and said, "This is not a toy, Max. It is my dad's boot. He is a firefighter, and he uses it to help people." Max wagged his tail and thanked the girl for helping him. From that day on, Max and the girl became best friends, and Max learned that not everything is a toy.
