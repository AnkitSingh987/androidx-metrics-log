Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Fish and the Crab

One day, a little crab was walking on the beach. He found a pretty, expensive shell. He wanted to take it home. But a big fish saw the shell too. The big fish wanted the expensive shell just like the little crab. The little crab and the big fish did not know how to share. They both wanted the shell very much. The little crab was sad, and the big fish was angry. They were not happy at all. Then, a kind girl came to the beach. She saw the little crab and the big fish fighting. She wanted to help them. The kind girl took the expensive shell and broke it into two parts. She gave one part to the little crab and the other part to the big fish. The little crab and the big fish were happy now. They both thanked the kind girl for helping them. Now, they could both have a piece of the pretty, expensive shell.

Once upon a time, there was a nation where everyone was very loud. In this nation, the people loved to grow big, beautiful flowers. One day, a little girl named Lily was planting a seed to grow a flower. Lily watered her seed every day and waited for it to grow. One morning, she saw a tiny, green sprout coming out of the ground. Lily was so happy! She kept watering it, and it grew bigger and bigger. The flower was red and smelled nice. One day, something unexpected happened. The flower started to talk! It was loud, just like the people in the nation. The flower said, "Hello, Lily! Thank you for helping me grow!" Lily was surprised but happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Lily and the flower talked and laughed together, making the nation even louder and happier.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy human named Tim. Tim loved to sleep all day. One sunny day, Tim was lying under a big tree. A small bird saw Tim and said, "Why are you so lazy, human? I have lots of things to do. I have to find food and sing songs." Tim looked at the bird and said, "I like to rest. I have no work today." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! Let's play together. It will be fun and not lazy." Tim smiled and said, "Okay!" So, Tim and the bird played all day. They had so much fun, and Tim was not lazy anymore.

Lily liked to make music with anything she could find. She had a drum that her dad gave her, but she wanted more sounds. She looked around her room and saw some things that she could attach to her drum. She saw a spoon, a bell, a feather and a ribbon. She took the spoon and tied it to the drum with the ribbon. Then she took the bell and tied it to the drum with the feather. She was very happy with her new drum. She tapped it with her hands and heard the spoon and the bell make different noises. She ran to the living room, where her mom was reading a book. She wanted to show her mom her drum. She said, "Mom, mom, look at my drum! I attached a spoon and a bell to it. It makes music!" Her mom smiled and put down her book. She said, "Wow, Lily, that's a very creative drum. You are very enthusiastic about music. Can I hear you play?" Lily nodded and started to play her drum. She tapped it fast and slow, loud and soft. She made up a song with her words. She sang, "I love my drum, it's so much fun, I attached a spoon and a bell, they make music very well." Her mom clapped and cheered. She said, "That's a wonderful song, Lily. You are very talented. I'm proud of you." Lily felt happy and hugged her mom. She said, "Thank you, mom. I love you. Do you want to play with me?" Her mom said, "Sure, Lily. Let's play together. Maybe we can find some more things to attach to your drum." They went back to Lily's room and looked for more things to make music with. They had a lot of fun playing with Lily's drum.
