Category: Intermediate
Title: The Factory and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a big factory. In the factory, there were many people who made toys. They all loved their work. They made toys for kids to play with and be happy. One day, a man named Tom came to the factory. He was very proud of the toys he made. He made a teddy bear and showed it to his friend, Sam. Tom said, "Look at my bear, Sam! I love making toys for kids." Sam smiled and said, "I love making toys too, Tom. We are all proud of our work here." Every day, the people in the factory made more toys. They loved their work and were proud of the toys they made. The kids who got the toys were happy, and that made the people in the factory happy too.

Lila was a small bird who liked to run and play with her friends. She had a blue wing and a yellow wing, and she was very fast. One day, she saw a big cat hiding in the grass. She wanted to run away, but she was curious. "Hello, cat," she said. "Why are you hiding?" The cat smiled and said, "I am not hiding. I am waiting for a friend. Do you want to be my friend?" Lila thought the cat was nice, and she said, "Yes, I want to be your friend. What do you like to do?" The cat said, "I like to run and chase things. Do you want to run with me?" Lila nodded and said, "Yes, I like to run too. Let's run together." The cat said, "OK, follow me." He ran out of the grass and into the open field. Lila followed him, flapping her wings. But the cat was not a friend. He was a hunter. He ran faster and faster, until he caught up with Lila. He jumped on her and bit her neck. Lila cried out, but it was too late. The cat had killed her. The cat licked his lips and said, "You were a tasty snack. Thank you for being my friend." He dragged Lila's body to his den and ate her. He did not care that she had a blue wing and a yellow wing, or that she was very fast. He only cared that she was small and easy to catch. He was a bad cat.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big parade. Everyone was happy and excited. They all wanted to see the parade. There were many animals, like elephants, horses, and dogs. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. In the parade, there was a little mouse. The mouse was very miserable. He felt sad because he was small and nobody noticed him. The mouse wanted to be part of the fun and make people smile too. So, he had an idea. The mouse decided to squeeze into a small hole in a big drum. When the mouse squeezed into the hole, the drum made a loud sound. Everyone in the town heard the sound and looked at the drum. They saw the little mouse and started to laugh and smile. The mouse felt happy because he made people happy too. The moral of the story is that no matter how small you are, you can still make a big difference and bring happiness to others.

Once upon a time, there was a king. He was a very hungry king. He lived in a big castle with his queen. They had a dog named Mark. Mark was a good dog. One day, the king said to the queen, "I am so hungry. Can you make me a big cake?" The queen said, "Yes, I will make you a big cake. But you must share it with Mark." The queen made the big cake. The king and Mark sat down to eat it. The king gave Mark a big piece of cake. Mark was very happy. After they ate the cake, the king was not hungry anymore. He said to Mark, "Thank you for sharing the cake with me." Mark wagged his tail and barked. They all lived happily ever after.
