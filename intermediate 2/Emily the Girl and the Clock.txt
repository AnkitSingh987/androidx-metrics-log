Category: Intermediate
Title: Emily the Girl and the Clock

Once upon a time there was a girl named Emily. She was three years old and wanted to relax. But no matter how hard she tried, she couldn't do it. Everywhere she looked there was a clock ticking. It made her frustrated. She asked her mum, “Mummy, why won’t the clock be still?” Her mum looked at her kindly and replied, “I know it’s difficult to relax when the clock is ticking. Just take some deep breaths and count to five. That will help you feel better.” Emily took a deep breath and counted to five twice. She felt a bit calmer. When she looked at the clock, it was still ticking but it didn't bother her as much. She smiled gratefully at her mum and gave her a big hug. From then on, Emily always remembered to take some deep breaths when she was feeling frustrated. She even taught her friends the same trick, so they could all relax.

Jill and Tom were twins who liked to play together. They also liked to surprise each other with gifts. One day, Jill found a big box in her closet. She opened it and saw a beautiful sweater inside. It was pink and soft and had a picture of a unicorn on it. Jill was very happy and thought that Tom had bought it for her. She ran to Tom's room and knocked on the door. "Tom, Tom, can I show you something?" she asked. Tom opened the door and saw Jill holding the sweater. He was very angry and shouted, "That's my sweater! I bought it for Mom's birthday! How did you find it? You are so nosy!" Jill was very sad and confused. She didn't know that Tom had bought the sweater for Mom. She thought he had bought it for her. She started to cry and said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I didn't know. I thought you wanted to surprise me. I wanted to surprise you too. I made you a card with stickers and glitter." Tom didn't care about the card. He snatched the sweater from Jill and slammed the door in her face. He didn't want to talk to her or play with her anymore. He was too mad at her for ruining his surprise. Jill ran back to her room and cried on her bed. She felt very bad and lonely. She wished she had never opened the box. She wished Tom would forgive her and be her friend again. She wished they could surprise each other with nice things and not with mean words.

Once upon a time, in a brilliant green forest, there lived a tiny caterpillar. The caterpillar was very hungry. He wanted to eat all day long. One day, the caterpillar found a big, green leaf. The leaf was on a tall tree. The caterpillar was very happy. He started to eat the leaf. It was very yummy. As the caterpillar ate more and more, he grew bigger and bigger. He ate the whole leaf. The caterpillar was not hungry anymore. He was very full and happy. And that is the end of the story of the caterpillar and the leaf.

Ben was bored. He had no toys to play with. His mom was busy in the kitchen. He wanted to find something fun. He looked around the living room. He saw a big cupboard. He walked to the cupboard and opened it. Inside, he saw many books and papers. They were dull. He did not like them. He pushed them aside and saw a small box. He pulled the box out of the cupboard. He opened it and saw a shiny car. It was red and had four wheels. He liked it. He took the car and ran to his mom. He showed her the car and said, "Look, mom, I found a car!" His mom smiled and said, "That's a nice car, Ben. Where did you find it?" Ben said, "In the cupboard. It was behind the dull books." His mom said, "That's your dad's car. He used to play with it when he was a boy. He will be happy to see you playing with it." Ben said, "Can I keep it, mom?" His mom said, "Yes, you can keep it, Ben. But you have to be careful with it. It's very old and fragile." Ben said, "Okay, mom. I will be careful. I love this car. It's not dull. It's fun."
