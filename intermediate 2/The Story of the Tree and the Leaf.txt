Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Leaf

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree. The tree was friends with a small worm. The worm lived in the earth under the tree. They liked to talk and play together. One day, the worm wanted to see the world. He asked the tree if he could borrow a leaf. The tree said yes, and the worm crawled onto the leaf. The wind blew, and the leaf with the worm flew away. The worm saw many things on his trip. He saw a big hill, a small dog, and a pretty flower. When he was done, the wind blew him back to his friend, the tree. The worm said thank you for the leaf. They were happy to be together again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He saw a big freezer. Tim was very curious. He wanted to see what was inside the freezer. He opened the door and saw many cold things. Tim saw a silly toy frog in the freezer. He took the frog out and said, "Why are you in here, silly frog?" The frog did not say anything. Tim put the frog on the floor. The frog began to spring and jump. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. Tim played with the silly frog for a long time. He forgot that the freezer door was still open. The cold air made the kitchen very cold. Tim's mommy came in and said, "Oh no! The freezer is open!" All the food in the freezer was bad now. Tim's mommy was very sad. Tim felt bad for leaving the freezer open.

One day, a big line of ants march near a house. They are very careful not to step on any big rocks. The ants want to find food for their family. A little girl sees the ants and wants to help them. She has a big ear and can hear them talk. They say they want a sweet treat. The girl goes inside her house and gets a cookie. She breaks the cookie into tiny pieces and puts them near the ants. The ants are happy and take the cookie pieces back to their family. The girl smiles and watches them march away.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go to school. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I go to school today?" His mom said, "Yes, I permit you to go to school today." Tim was very happy. He went to school and saw many interesting things. He saw his friends, and they played together. They played with blocks, and they painted pretty pictures. Tim also learned new words from his teacher. At the end of the day, Tim went back home. He told his mom, "I had a fun day at school. It was very interesting." His mom smiled and said, "I am happy you had a good day at school. You can go again tomorrow." Tim went to bed excited for another interesting day at school.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play in Lily's room. Lily has a big carpet with many colors and shapes. Ben likes to look at the carpet and find different things. One day, Lily says to Ben, "I want to show you something. Come here and sit on the carpet with me." Ben follows Lily and sits next to her. Lily points to a part of the carpet that has a yellow circle and a red heart. "Look, Ben, this is a sweet. It is like the ones we eat sometimes. Do you like sweets?" Lily asks. Ben nods and smiles. He likes sweets very much. He says, "Yes, I like sweets. They are yummy and make me happy. Can we pretend to eat this sweet on the carpet?" Lily nods and smiles too. She says, "Yes, we can. Let's pretend to eat this sweet and then find more sweets on the carpet. Maybe we can find a chocolate or a candy or a cookie." Ben and Lily pretend to eat the sweet on the carpet. They make sounds like "mmmm" and "yum". Then they look for more sweets on the carpet. They have fun and laugh together. They are happy friends.
