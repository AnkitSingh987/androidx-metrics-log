Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Stubborn Cat and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn cat named Fluffy. Fluffy did not like to share her toys. One day, Fluffy met a dog named Spot. Spot was very nice and wanted to play with Fluffy. Spot said, "Fluffy, let's trade toys and play together." Fluffy thought about it and decided to give it a try. They traded toys, and Fluffy gave Spot her ball, while Spot provided Fluffy with his bone. At first, Fluffy was not sure about the trade. But soon, she saw how much fun they were having. Fluffy and Spot played all day and became the best of friends. From that day on, Fluffy was not stubborn anymore and loved to share her toys with her new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small village, lived a mighty elephant named Ellie. Ellie was very big and strong. She had many friends, but there was one little mouse, named Mikey, who always wanted to play with her. Ellie would ignore Mikey because she thought he was too small to play with. One day, Mikey found a sharp pin on the ground. He had an idea. He went to Ellie and said, "Ellie, I found something that can help you if you ever get in trouble." Ellie just laughed and said, "I am mighty. I don't need help from a little mouse like you." Mikey felt sad but kept the pin with him. Later, Ellie got stuck in a big net. She tried to break free, but she couldn't. Mikey saw Ellie in trouble and wanted to help. He took out the pin and used it to cut the net. Ellie was free! She thanked Mikey and said, "I am sorry for ignoring you before. You are a true friend." From that day on, Ellie and Mikey played together and were the best of friends. The moral of the story is: Never ignore someone just because they are small. Everyone has something special to offer.

One day, a small shrimp was swimming in the sea. He wanted to be very famous. He thought, "I will cut the big rope near the boats to show how strong I am." He told his friend, the fish, about his plan. The fish said, "No, don't do that! It is not nice to cut things that are not yours." But the shrimp did not listen. He swam to the big rope and started to cut it with his sharp claws. As he cut the rope, he saw a big wave coming. The wave pushed the boats away from the shore. The shrimp was scared and swam back to his friend, the fish. The fish said, "You should have listened to me. Now the boats are gone, and you are not famous." The shrimp was sad, but he learned a lesson. It is better to be kind and listen to your friends than to do something bad to be famous. From that day on, the shrimp always tried to be nice and helpful to everyone in the sea.

Jill and Jack were the best of friends. Every day they played together until the sun went down. One day Jill had a very creative idea. She wanted to make a barrel slide. Jack was so excited, he said "That is such a great idea! How can I help?". Jill frowned, "Let's go and find some barrels. We can use them to make the slide!" The two of them went off in search of barrels. They looked around the town, but everywhere they searched had no barrels. Then, Jack had an idea. He said "I saw some barrels in the big Park! Let's go and try our luck there". The two ran to the Park and found the barrels. They smiled and felt relieved. With the barrels, they could make the slide. That day, the two friends became very creative and managed to make a very big and fun barrel slide. They both were very happy and they played around the slide all day. What a great day they had!

Jack and Jill were best friends. One day, Jack ran out to play in the park with his chalk. But what Jack didn't know was that Jill was coming too. She had a large, pink chalk in her pocket. Jack saw Jill coming and smiled. He shouted, "Hi, Jill! I'm drawing with chalk. Would you like to join me?" Jill smiled back. She nodded and took out her chalk. "Yes," said Jill. "I'd love to join you." Jack and Jill drew together for a while. But suddenly, Jack had a brilliant idea. He said, "Let's play a game. I'll kick the chalk and you try to catch it." Jill laughed and agreed. She was surprisingly good at catching the chalk with her pink hands. Jack cheered each time she caught it. They played the game until they were both tired. Jack and Jill hugged each other goodbye, and promised to play the game again soon.
