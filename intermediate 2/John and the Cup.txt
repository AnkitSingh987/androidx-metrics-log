Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Cup

John was a tall boy who liked to play with his cup. He liked to fill it with water and make bubbles in the water. But his little sister, Lisa, wanted to play with the cup too. She didn't understand why he wanted to keep the cup all to himself. “John, can I play with the cup too?” she asked. But John ignored her and kept playing with the cup by himself. Lisa tried to take the cup away but John wouldn't let her. He held it tightly in his hands and wouldn't let her take it away. Lisa got angry and started pulling on the cup. Finally, the cup came out of John's hands and Lisa ran away with it. She celebrated and shouted, “I have the cup!” John was very angry that Lisa had taken the cup away from him. He ran after her but she was too fast. In the end, Lisa ignored John’s cries and kept the cup for herself.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. Tim did not know about hockey. He was ignorant. One day, Tim saw kids playing hockey in the park. They were having fun. Tim wanted to play too. Tim went to the kids and asked, "Can I play?" One kid said, "Do you know how to play hockey?" Tim said, "No, but I want to learn." The kid said, "Okay, we will teach you." Tim tried to play, but it was hard. The kids got mad. They did not want to play with Tim. Tim was sad. He went home and asked his dad to teach him hockey. Tim's dad taught him how to play. Tim practiced every day. He got better and better. One day, Tim went back to the park. He asked the kids, "Can I play now?" They said yes. Tim played hockey with the kids. They saw that Tim was good. They were happy to play with him now. Tim was happy too. He had new friends and learned how to play hockey.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big white box in his room. He did not know where it came from. Tim was very excited to see what was inside the box. He tried to open it, but it was too hard for him. He called his mom and said, "Mom, can you help me open this big white box?" His mom came into the room and helped Tim open the box. Inside the box, they found a very pretty white dress. Tim was confused because he did not wear dresses. His mom smiled and said, "This must be a surprise for your sister, not for you!" Later, Tim's sister came home and saw the white dress. She was so happy and put it on right away. She wanted to show her friends her new dress. She ran outside but did not see the water on the floor. She slipped and fell down. Her white dress got dirty. Tim's sister was sad, but Tim gave her a big hug. In the end, Tim and his sister learned to be careful when they were excited. They also learned that sometimes surprises can be for other people, and that is okay.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Abigail. Abigail was 3 years old and she loved to play outside. One day, Abigail was outside, playing in the garden. Suddenly, she felt something cold in the air. Abigail started to tremble and she got scared. Just then, Abigail's dad appeared. He was holding a pale, fluffy towel. He wrapped the towel around Abigail, and said, “Don't worry, my sweet girl. I’ll always be here to take care of you.” Abigail smiled and clutched the towel. She felt much better and the trembling stopped. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little mouse. They were friends and lived in a small house. One day, they wanted to bake a cake. The big cat put on an apron and the little mouse did too. "Be careful not to break the eggs," said the big cat. The little mouse was foolish and dropped an egg on the floor. It broke and made a mess. The big cat was sad, but the little mouse said, "I am sorry, let's try again." They tried again and this time, the little mouse did not break the egg. The cake was ready and they ate it together. They were very happy and learned that being careful is important.
