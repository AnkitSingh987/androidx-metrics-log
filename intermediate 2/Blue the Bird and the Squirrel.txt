Category: Intermediate
Title: Blue the Bird and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, in a big, high tree, there was a little bird named Blue. Blue loved to fly and play with his friends all day. One day, when the sun went down, it was time for Blue to go back to his nest. But Blue could not find his way home. He had forget where his nest was. It was getting dark and Blue was scared. Blue flew here and there, trying to find his nest in the dark. He met a kind squirrel who wanted to help him. The squirrel said, "Don't worry, Blue. I will help you find your nest." They looked high and low, but they could not find Blue's nest. Blue was very sad and cried. Then, a wise old owl saw Blue and the squirrel. The owl told them, "I know where your nest is, Blue. Follow me." The owl led them to the big, high tree. There, Blue saw his nest. He was so happy! Blue hugged the squirrel and the owl and said, "Thank you for helping me." From that day on, Blue never forget where his nest was, and he always went home before it got dark.

Once upon a time, there was a zoo. The zoo had many animals. The sun was shining and the sky was clear. The animals were happy. One day, a new animal came to the zoo. It was a big bear. The bear was not happy. The bear did not like the zoo. The bear wanted to go home. The other animals tried to help the bear. They wanted to make the bear feel better. But the bear did not feel better. The bear started to suffer. The bear was very sad. The other animals tried to help, but they could not. In the end, the bear stayed sad and the other animals were sad too. The zoo was not a happy place anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat spaghetti. He would eat it for lunch and dinner. His mom would make the best spaghetti for him. Tim would always enjoy his spaghetti meal. One day, it was very wet outside. It rained and rained all day. Tim could not go out to play. He was sad. But then, his mom had an idea. She said, "Let's make spaghetti together!" Tim was so happy. He helped his mom make the spaghetti. They cooked the spaghetti and made the sauce. Tim stirred the sauce with a big spoon. When it was ready, they sat down to eat. Tim enjoyed the spaghetti even more because he helped make it. And even though it was wet outside, Tim had a great day inside with his mom.

Once upon a time there was an unusual insect. He lived in the garden and was always very busy. He liked to explore and go on adventures. One day, he decided to leave the garden to find out what was beyond it. He kept walking and walking until he found a very big pond. He was so excited but then he saw that the pond was full of scary, noisy frogs. He tried to leave, but the frogs started to hop after him. The insect was scared, so he hid under a leaf. He stayed quiet and still until the frogs stopped looking for him. When they went away, the insect left quietly and went back to the garden. He was safe again and was very glad to be back in his special place. He had learned a valuable lesson: sometimes it is better to stay where you know and feel safe.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to mail a letter. She put the letter in a big blue box. The sun was hot and harsh, but Mia was happy. On her way back home, Mia saw her friend Tom. Tom was eating his lunch. "Hi, Mia!" said Tom. "Do you want to share my lunch?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, thank you!" They sat under a big tree and ate lunch together. They talked and laughed a lot. After lunch, they played hide and seek. Mia and Tom had a fun day.
