Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Smelly Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a smelly dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toys. He would sort them into piles: big toys and small toys. Max was very good at sorting his toys. One day, Max saw a new toy near the exit of his house. He was very excited and wanted to play with it. He picked it up and ran outside. Max played with the toy all day and had lots of fun. As the sun began to set, Max felt tired. He carried his new toy back to the exit of his house. Max sorted his new toy into the pile with the big toys. He was very happy and went to sleep with a big smile on his face.

One day, a boy named Tom and his friend, Sue, went to play in the park. They saw a big box that said "include." They were very curious about what was inside the box. Tom said, "Let's open the box and see what's inside!" Sue agreed and they opened the box. Inside, they found many colorful blocks with different patterns on them. They were very excited to play with the blocks. Tom and Sue started to build a tower with the blocks. They tried to make a successful tower by putting the blocks with the same pattern together. They took turns putting blocks onto the tower. "Wow, look at our tower!" Sue said, smiling. Tom nodded and said, "It's very tall and strong!" They were very happy with their successful tower and played with the blocks all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named John. He was very brave. He loved to explore the woods. He had already found many things in the forest, like rabbits and butterflies. One day, he went deeper into the woods than he ever had before. He came upon an old pine tree. The pine tree was very tall and there was something strange about it. He felt a bit frightened, but he was still curious. John decided to climb the pine tree. He had to go very high up in the tree, but he kept going. When he reached the top, he saw something shining in the branches. It was a little girl, who was very scared. She was stuck! John knew he had to save the little girl. He called out to her and asked her to hold onto a branch. He then carefully climbed back down the tree and helped her down. When they both safely made it to the ground, the little girl thanked John and ran away. John was so brave and saved the little girl! He knew he'd made the right choice and was very proud. He had a happy and triumphant smile on his face as he left the forest and continued exploring.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play and have fun. But sometimes they are naughty and do not listen to Mom and Dad. One day, they sneak out of the house and go to the subway station. They want to ride the trains and see new places. "Look, Ben, there is a train!" Lily says. "Let's go inside and find a seat." "OK, Lily, but we have to be quick. Mom and Dad will be angry if they find us," Ben says. They run to the train and hop on. They find a soft seat and sit down. They are happy and excited. They do not see the sign that says "Express Train. No Stops." The train starts to move. It goes very fast. It does not stop at any station. Lily and Ben are scared. They do not know where they are going. They do not know how to get back home. They start to cry. "Help, help! We want to go home!" they shout. But no one hears them. The train is too loud. The other people are too busy. The train keeps going and going. Mom and Dad are worried. They do not see Lily and Ben anywhere. They call their names. They look in every room. They look outside. They do not find them. They call the police. They tell them what happened. The police try to help. They look for Lily and Ben. They check the cameras. They see them on the train. They tell Mom and Dad. Mom and Dad are very sad and angry. They do not know if they will see Lily and Ben again. They do not know what to do. Lily and Ben are lost. They are on the wrong train. They are far away from home. They are in trouble. They will be punished. They wish they had listened to Mom and Dad. They wish they had not been naughty. They wish they had stayed home and played. But it is too late. The end.
