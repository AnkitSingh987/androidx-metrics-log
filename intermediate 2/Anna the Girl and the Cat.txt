Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She had a toy cat named Mr. Whiskers. They liked to play together all day long. One day, Anna wanted to understand how big Mr. Whiskers was. She thought a measure would be useful. She found a small ruler and measured her toy cat. Mr. Whiskers was ten inches long! Anna was happy to understand how big Mr. Whiskers was. She knew the measure was useful for more things too. Now, Anna and Mr. Whiskers could play even more fun games together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Buddy, went to the park to play. They were having lots of fun running and jumping. Tim was happy, but he didn't know much about the park rules. He was ignorant about them. In the park, they met a kind man named Mr. Smith. Mr. Smith saw that Tim and Buddy were having fun, but he wanted to help them be safe. Mr. Smith said, "Tim, did anyone mention the park rules to you?" Tim shook his head no. Mr. Smith told Tim, "It's important to know the rules so you can have fun and stay safe. If you don't follow the rules, it can result in problems." Tim listened carefully and learned the rules. Now, Tim and Buddy can have fun and be safe in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw with his chalk. He had many colors of chalk like red, blue, and yellow. Tim would draw on the sidewalk outside his house every day. One day, Tim found a piece of fake chalk in his chalk box. It was a pretty purple color. He was so excited to draw with it. Tim drew a big sun, but something strange happened. The sun he drew started to vanish! It was like magic. Tim was amazed by the fake chalk. He drew a cat, and it vanished too! Tim's friends saw what happened and they all wanted to try the magic chalk. They had so much fun watching their drawings vanish. From that day on, Tim and his friends loved to play with the magic chalk together.

Lily loved to go to the park with her mom. She liked to see the birds, the flowers, and the big trees. But her favorite thing was to feed the deer. The deer lived in a special place in the park, where they had grass and water and a fence to keep them safe. Lily always brought some carrots or apples for them. One day, Lily saw a new deer in the park. It was smaller than the others, and had white spots on its brown fur. It looked shy and scared, and stayed away from the other deer. Lily wanted to be its friend. She took a carrot from her bag and walked slowly to the fence. She held out the carrot and said, "Hello, little deer. I have a treat for you. Don't be afraid. I won't hurt you." The little deer looked at Lily with big, dark eyes. It sniffed the air and smelled the carrot. It was hungry, but it was also nervous. It had never seen a human before. It wondered if Lily was nice or mean. It took a step closer, then another, then another. It reached the fence and stretched its neck. It touched the carrot with its nose and licked it. It tasted sweet and crunchy. It liked it. It took a bite and chewed it. It looked at Lily and made a soft sound. Lily smiled and said, "Good deer. You are very brave. Do you want another carrot?" She took another one from her bag and offered it to the deer. The deer nodded and ate it. It felt happy and warm. It decided that Lily was nice and kind. It accepted her as a friend. It moved closer to the fence and rubbed its head against Lily's hand. Lily giggled and petted it. She said, "You are very sweet, little deer. What is your name?" The deer didn't have a name, but it liked the sound of Lily's voice. It made another sound and nodded. It felt like it had a name now. It felt like it had a home. It felt like it had a family. It stayed with Lily until her mom came to get her. Lily said goodbye to the deer and promised to come back soon. The deer watched her go and waited for her to return. It knew that she would. It knew that she was its friend. It knew that it was happy.
