Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sue

Once there was a boy named Tim. He had lots of money. He liked to buy many toys. But he felt uncomfortable. He did not have many friends. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue. She was sad. She had no toys. Tim thought about the value of sharing. He gave Sue one of his toys. Sue was happy. Tim felt happy too. From that day, Tim shared his toys with others. He made many friends. Tim learned that sharing has more value than money. He was no longer uncomfortable. Tim was very happy.

Anna liked to create things with her crayons and paper. She made pictures of her family, her dog, and her favorite toys. She was very proud of her creations and showed them to everyone. One day, she went to the park with her mom and saw a boy sitting on a bench. He had a big book and a pencil. He was drawing something on the book. Anna was curious and wanted to see what he was drawing. She walked over to the boy and said, "Hi, I'm Anna. What are you drawing?" The boy looked up and saw Anna. He smiled and said, "Hi, I'm Ben. I'm drawing a map of the park. See, this is the slide, and this is the swing, and this is the pond." Anna looked at the map and was amazed. It was very detailed and colorful. She said, "Wow, that's a great map. You're very good at drawing." Ben said, "Thank you. Do you like to draw too?" Anna nodded and said, "Yes, I do. I like to create pictures with my crayons and paper. Do you want to see them?" Ben said, "Sure, I'd love to see them." Anna ran back to her mom and asked her to give her the crayons and paper. She took them and ran back to Ben. She showed him her pictures and explained what they were. Ben looked at the pictures and said, "These are very nice pictures. I like your family, your dog, and your toys. You're very good at creating." Anna smiled and said, "Thank you. Do you want to create something with me?" Ben said, "Yes, I do. What do you want to create?" Anna thought for a moment and said, "How about we create a story with our pictures? We can use the map as the setting and the people and animals as the characters." Ben said, "That sounds like fun. How do we start?" Anna said, "We can start by choosing a name for our story. How about 'The Adventure of Anna and Ben in the Park'?" Ben said, "I like that name. Let's begin." They began to create their story with their pictures. They used their imagination and made up a plot. They had a lot of fun and became friends. Meanwhile, their mom watched them from a distance. She was happy to see them play and create together. She thought they were very clever and creative. She decided to let them have some more time and went to buy them some ice cream. She came back with two cones and said, "Anna, Ben, I have a surprise for you. Do you want some ice cream?" Anna and Ben looked up and saw the ice cream. They were very excited and said, "Yes, please!" They took the cones and thanked their mom. They ate the ice cream and continued their story. They had a wonderful day in the park. They did not feel uncomfortable at all. They felt happy and friendly. They decided to share their crayons and paper and their book and pencil. They also decided to share their stories and their friendship. They created a pass to the park that said, "Anna and Ben, best friends forever." They put it in their pocket and smiled.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, hard rock that he liked to play with. One day, he found a twisty stick called a candle. Tim thought the candle was fun to twist and play with, too. Tim took his hard rock and twisty candle to show his friend, Sue. Sue thought they were fun to play with, too. They twisted the candle together and laughed. At night, Tim's mom lit the twisty candle for him. It made a pretty, warm light. Tim and Sue played with the hard rock and twisty candle until it was time for bed. They were happy and had a good day.
