Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy and Max and the Car

One day, a soft cat named Lucy was playing outside with her friend, a dog named Max. They were running and having fun when they saw a big lightning in the sky. They were scared and ran to their little house. Inside the house, they found that the lightning had broken their toy car. Max said, "Oh no! Our toy car is broken. What can we do?" Lucy thought for a moment and said, "I know! We can fix it!" So, Lucy and Max worked together to fix the toy car. They used glue and tape to put the pieces back together. When they were done, they were very proud of their work. Just then, the toy car started to talk! It said, "Thank you for fixing me, Lucy and Max! I am a magic toy car, and now I can take you on a fun ride!" Lucy and Max were very surprised but happy. They got into the magic toy car and went on a big adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy called Tom. He liked to study a lot, and he was very interested in learning how to use a spear. One day, a modern spear appeared in his room. It was shiny and sleek, and it looked like it must be special. Tom was so excited, he couldn't wait to try it out. Every day, he studied the spear. He learned how to hold it, the best way to throw it and the different types of wood for mekking it. After months of hard work, he was finally ready to try his spear out for real. He went to the woodlands, and threw the spear with all his strength. He hit a target! He had finally become a master spear thrower. Tom was so proud of himself. He wanted to keep practising and perfect his technique even more. He had truly become a modern spear master.

Once upon a time, there was a kind mole. The mole was very generous. He loved to share his food with his friends. One day, the mole found a big apple. He wanted to share it with his friends. But the apple was too big for him to carry. The mole started to struggle. He pushed and pulled, but the apple would not move. Then, a bird saw the mole. The bird asked, "Do you need help?" The mole said, "Yes, please." So, the bird helped the mole. Together, they moved the apple. The mole and the bird shared the big apple with their friends. All the animals were happy. They thanked the kind and generous mole. From that day on, they all became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with a shiny ornament that hung on a tree. One day, Tom saw the ornament and wanted to play with it. He jumped and tried to catch it. As Tom jumped, he did not see the ball that was close to him. The ball rolled and hit Tom's leg. This was unexpected! Tom was surprised and fell down. The ornament did not fall, and Tom felt a little sad. But then, Tom had a new idea. He found a stick and used it to strike the ornament. The ornament started to swing, and Tom was happy. He played with the ornament all day long, using the stick. Tom learned that even when something unexpected happens, he could still find a way to have fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a wide spoon in the kitchen. He liked the spoon very much. Tim took the spoon to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tim showed his friends the wide spoon. They all thought it was fun. Tim's friend, Sue, asked if she could play with the spoon. "Can I play with the spoon, please?" Sue asked. Tim said, "Yes, you can play with the spoon." While they were playing, the wide spoon fell on the ground and broke. Tim started to weep. He was very sad. Sue said, "I am sorry, Tim. I did not mean to break the spoon." Tim's friends all tried to make him feel better. They gave him hugs and said nice things. Tim stopped weeping and smiled. He knew that his friends cared about him. Even without the wide spoon, they still had fun playing at the park.
