Title: Spot the Sweet Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little dog named Spot. He loved to play with his toys and run around the house. One day, Spot saw his family sitting on the big couch. They were laughing and having fun. Spot wanted to join them and be a part of the fun too. Spot jumped up onto the couch and sat next to his family. They were all very happy to have Spot join them. They petted him and gave him lots of love. Spot felt so warm and cozy on the couch with his family. As they sat on the couch, Spot's family shared a big bowl of yummy treats. Spot got to taste some too! He loved the sweet taste of the treats and wagged his tail with joy. Spot knew that being with his family on the couch was the best place to be. And from that day on, Spot always loved to join his family on the couch for more fun and sweet treats.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big pond with ducks and fish. Anna wants to feed the ducks. Ben wants to catch a fish. "Can we go to the pond, Mommy?" Anna asks. "OK, but you have to promise me something," Mommy says. "You have to stay away from the water. It is deep and wet. You can get hurt." Anna and Ben nod and promise. They run to the pond with some bread and a net. They see the ducks and the fish. They are happy. Anna throws some bread to the ducks. The ducks come closer and quack. Anna laughs and throws more bread. Ben sees a big fish in the water. He wants to catch it. He forgets his promise. He leans over the pond with his net. "Ben, no!" Anna shouts. "You promised Mommy!" But Ben does not listen. He reaches for the fish. He slips and falls into the pond. He is wet and scared. He cries for help. Anna screams and runs to Mommy. Mommy hears her and runs to the pond. She sees Ben in the water. She jumps in and grabs him. She pulls him out. He is wet and cold. He is sorry. Mommy is angry and sad. She hugs Ben and scolds him. She says he broke his promise. She says he could have drowned. She says he is in big trouble. Anna is sad too. She says she is sorry. She says she tried to stop him. She says she loves him. Mommy says she loves them too. But she says they have to go home now. She says they can't play in the park anymore. She says they have to learn their lesson. Anna and Ben cry. They don't want to go home. They don't want to lose their fun. They don't want to make Mommy mad. They wish they kept their promise.
