Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and the Mouse

One day, Jack and his mum were playing on the computer. His mum wanted to show him something special. She asked, "Can you see the mouse?" Jack looked and saw a small grey mouse. His mum said, "Let's use the mouse to play a game on the screen." Jack moved the mouse and it made the arrow move on the screen! He was so excited and started moving the mouse around in all directions. It made the arrow move in lots of directions! His mum smiled and said, "Very good! Let's do it again!" They kept playing and Jack improved his skills. Now he could move the mouse quickly and make the arrow fly around the screen! His mum clapped her hands and said, "You are so good! Now you can use the mouse regularly!" Jack was very proud of himself. He loved using the mouse and improved his skills with it every day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bug named Bob. Bob was a patient bug who liked to play with leaves. One day, Bob found a big leaf. It was the best leaf he had ever seen. He wanted to show it to his friend, Tim the ant. But when Bob went to get his leaf, it was gone! "Oh no!" said Bob. "Someone did steal my leaf!" Bob was sad, but he knew he had to find out who took his leaf. So, he asked his friends in the forest if they saw who took it. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim the ant came running to Bob. "Bob, I found your leaf!" Tim said. "I took it to keep it safe for you." Bob was so happy! The plot twist was that his friend Tim had the leaf all along. Bob and Tim played with the leaf together and had lots of fun.

One day, a little boat named Bobby wanted to spend time at the big port. The port was a fun place where all the boats would meet and play. Bobby was very excited to make new friends. When Bobby got to the port, he saw an unusual boat. This boat had big, round wheels and a funny hat. Bobby went up to the unusual boat and said, "Hi, I am Bobby. Do you want to play?" The unusual boat smiled and said, "Yes, I am Wally. Let's play!" Bobby and Wally spent the whole day playing at the port. They raced, splashed water, and laughed together. All the other boats at the port liked playing with Bobby and Wally too. At the end of the day, Bobby was happy he made a new, unusual friend.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful ghost. He flew all around, looking for something he could miss. Everywhere he went, people would try to run away as fast as they could. But one day, he saw a happy 3 year old child. She was jumping around, feeling the wind on her face. She smiled and said 'Hello, Ghost.' The ghost was amazed. He'd never met anyone this brave. He told the child that he had been missing something but he wasn't sure what it was. The child took the ghost's hands and said, 'It's okay. You don't have to miss anything. You can just stay here with me.' The ghost was so happy. She was right. He didn't need to miss anything anymore. He found someone to share his powerful spirit with. From then on, the happy ghost and the brave 3 year old child were never afraid to be together. They were the best of friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, proud cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy mouse. One day, Tom saw a big black thing in the room. It was a piano! Tom had never seen a piano before. He wanted to play with it. Tom jumped on the piano and started to walk on it. He noticed that it made fun sounds. "This is so fun!" he said. Just then, a little girl named Sue came into the room. Sue saw Tom on the piano and laughed. "Silly Tom, that's not a toy!" she said. Sue started to play the piano. Tom listened and liked the music. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom began to sing along! Sue was so surprised. "Wow, Tom, you can sing!" she said. Tom felt so happy and proud. From that day on, Tom and Sue would play and sing together every day.
