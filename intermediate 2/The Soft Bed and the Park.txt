Category: Intermediate
Title: The Soft Bed and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a soft bed. On the bed, there was a toy skeleton. The toy skeleton was a friend to a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim brought his toy skeleton to the park. He played with the toy on the swings and the slide. Soon, a girl named Amy came up to Tim and asked, "What's your toy's name?" Tim smiled and said, "His name is Skelly. He's my best friend. Do you want to play with us?" Amy nodded and they all played together. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Amy, you can come to my house and play with Skelly again." Amy was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim. I will come to your house and we can all play together." Tim, Amy, and Skelly had lots of fun. They all became best friends. Tim was happy to welcome Amy and Skelly into his life.

Once there was a mommy and her little boy. Mommy was paying for some food at the store. The little boy wanted some candy, but mommy said no. The little boy was hurt. Back at home, mommy told the little boy to clean his room. He was unhappy but he did what mommy said. He saw a lot of dust and he used a cloth to wipe it away. Mommy noticed the little boy was paying attention to the dust. She praised him for working so hard. The little boy was happy. He felt like his efforts were being paid back.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little mushroom. The little mushroom was very sorry because it was too small. It wanted to grow big and strong like the trees around it. One day, a kind rabbit came to the little mushroom and asked, "Why are you so sorry, little mushroom?" The mushroom replied, "I am sorry because I am too small. I want to grow big and strong like the trees." The kind rabbit thought for a moment and said, "I can help you manage to grow bigger, but you must be patient." The little mushroom agreed, and the kind rabbit helped by bringing water and sunshine to the mushroom every day. Slowly, the little mushroom started to grow bigger and stronger. One day, a lost squirrel came to the mushroom and said, "Thank you for being so big and strong. You helped me find my way home." The mushroom felt very happy and proud. The moral of the story is: Be patient, and with the help of good friends, you can manage to grow and become strong.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with it, but it was hard to reach. Tim tried and tried, but he could not get the ball. Then, Tim saw a small cat. The cat was trying to escape from a big dog. The dog was running fast, but the cat was faster. The cat ran up a tree and the dog could not catch it. The cat was safe. Tim thought of an idea. He asked the cat to help him get the big, red ball. The cat climbed down the tree and jumped up to the ball. The cat pushed the ball and it fell down. Tim was happy and played with the ball all day.

One day, a tidy cat named Tim went for a walk. Tim loved to keep his fur clean and his things in order. He walked down the street and saw a big tree. Tim thought it would be fun to climb the tree and look around. As Tim climbed the tree, he saw a veil stuck in the branches. The veil was pretty, but it was making a mess. Tim wanted to help, so he tried to take the veil down. But it was not easy. The veil was stuck very tight. Just then, Tim met a kind bird named Bella. Bella wanted to help Tim with the veil. They worked together and got the veil out of the tree. Tim and Bella felt happy. They learned that when you work together, you can solve problems. And that is how Tim and Bella became good friends.
