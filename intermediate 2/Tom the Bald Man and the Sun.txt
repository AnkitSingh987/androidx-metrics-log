Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Sun

One day, a bald man named Tom went to the store. He wanted to buy a jar. He had saved up his money to spend on a pretty jar. Tom looked at many jars, but he could not find one he liked. Then, he saw a small girl looking at a jar. The jar was blue and had a big yellow sun on it. The small girl said, "This jar is so pretty. I wish I could buy it." Tom thought the jar was pretty too. Tom asked the girl, "Do you want this jar?" The girl said, "Yes, but I don't have money to spend." Tom smiled and said, "I will buy the jar for you!" The girl was very happy. They went to the front of the store and Tom spent his money on the jar. The girl thanked Tom and they both left the store with big smiles.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with a ball, jump, and run around the house. One day, Kitty saw a screen. The screen was big and bright. Kitty was very curious and wanted to play with it. Kitty jumped and touched the screen with her paw. The screen showed a funny picture of a dog. The dog looked like it was going to sneeze. Kitty thought it was so funny that she started to laugh. As she laughed, she felt a sneeze coming too. Kitty sneezed so hard that she fell on the floor. She looked up at the screen and saw the dog had sneezed too. Kitty laughed even more. She was happy to have found a new friend on the screen to play with. From that day on, Kitty loved to watch the screen and play with her sneezy dog friend.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tree. The tree had a strong root. A little monkey liked to hang from the tree. He felt very comfortable there. One day, the little monkey saw a big box under the tree. He was very curious. He climbed down to look at the box. Inside the box, he found a big, soft bed. It was so comfortable! The little monkey decided to sleep on the bed instead of hanging from the tree. He was very happy with his new bed. Now, the little monkey sleeps on the comfortable bed every night. He still loves the tree and its strong root, but he also loves his new, soft bed.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her friends in the park. One day, Fluffy saw a pretty blouse hanging on a line. It was red and blue, and it looked fun to play with. Fluffy wanted to play with the blouse, but she knew it was wrong to steal. She thought about it for a moment, and then she had an idea. Fluffy went to her friend, the bird who lived in a tree. She asked the bird if they could play with the blouse together. The bird said yes, and they both played with the blouse. They had so much fun that they forgot all about stealing. In the end, Fluffy and the bird put the blouse back where they found it. They learned that it's always better to share and play together than to steal.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big box. Inside the box was a clean computer. Tim was very happy. He wanted to mark the computer with his name. He took a pen and wrote "Tim's computer" on it. Now everyone would know it was his. Tim's mom saw the computer and said, "Wow, Tim! That's a very clean computer you found. Let's turn it on and see if it works." They pushed the button, but nothing happened. Tim was sad. His mom thought for a moment and said, "Maybe it needs to be charged. Let's plug it in and try again later." After some time, the computer started to make funny noises. Tim and his mom were surprised. They didn't know that the computer could talk! The computer said, "Hello, Tim! I am your new friend. Thank you for marking me with your name." Tim and his mom laughed. They never thought they would have a talking computer as a friend.
