Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very good at finding useful things. One day, she found a big, open box. She wanted to invite her friends to see the box. Sue and her friends were very excited. They played in the open box all day long. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The box started to move! The box was not just a box. It was a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, I am Tim the Turtle." Sue and her friends were surprised but happy. They made a new friend, and they played with Tim the Turtle every day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was feeling very scared. She was standing alone in a pine forest. The tall trees and shadows made her feel even more fearful. Suddenly, she heard a sound! It was a soft hooting, like an owl. The sound made her heart beat faster. She looked around and saw a small bird in a nearby tree. She nodded at it, hoping it would go away. The bird flew down and perched on a branch of the pine tree, right in front of her. She was surprised; birds usually flew away when they saw her. This bird didn't seem scared of her! The little girl was happy and nodded at it again. The bird flew into the air and then back again to the branch. It looked like it wanted to dance with her! She smiled and started to tap her feet and sway her arms. She felt calm and happy. The little girl spent lots of time with her new feathered friend in the pine forest. She was no longer fearful, now she was filled with joy!

One day, a colorful bird named Billy was flying in the sky. It was Wednesday and he saw a little girl named Lucy playing in the park. Lucy looked up and saw Billy. She said, "Hi, bird! You are so colorful!" Billy flew down to Lucy and said, "Hi, Lucy! I wonder if you want to play with me?" Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played and laughed all day long. The sun was going down, and they were both tired. As they sat down, a big wind blew and took away Billy's colors. Lucy was sad and said, "Oh no, Billy! Your colors are gone!" Billy smiled and said, "That's okay, Lucy. I learned that being colorful is not important. What is important is being a good friend and having fun together." Lucy smiled and hugged Billy. They knew they would be friends forever.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big hill with green grass and yellow flowers. They want to climb the hill and see the sky. "Let's go up the hill!" Tom says. "It will be fun!" "OK!" Mia says. "But be careful. The hill is steep and slippery." They hold hands and start to climb. They are happy and excited. They see birds and bugs and clouds. They are almost at the top. "I am the king of the hill!" Tom says. He feels proud. He stands up and raises his arms. But then he slips. He falls down the hill. He rolls and rolls. He hits rocks and sticks. He gets dirty and hurt. He cries and cries. Mia sees Tom fall. She is scared and sad. She runs down the hill. She tries to help Tom. But she slips too. She falls down the hill. She rolls and rolls. She hits rocks and sticks. She gets dirty and hurt. She cries and cries. They lie on the soil. They are bleeding and bruised. They are not happy and excited. They are not proud. They are sorry and ashamed. They wish they had listened to Mia. They wish they had been careful. They wish they had not climbed the hill. They wish they had stayed on the ground.

Once upon a time, there was a princess who lived in a palace. The palace was big and fancy, but the princess was very bored. Every day she looked out of the palace window to find something fun to do. One day, the princess set out to find some adventure. She followed a path through a meadow, and found a dark, deep forest. She kept walking and soon she heard some animals. The princess saw a deer, some foxes, and a rabbit. She said hello and the animals began to play with her! They ran and jumped and laughed in the forest. The princess was so happy and forgot all about the boring palace. The princess had lots of fun and didn't go back home until the sun began to set. She thanked the animals for showing her a great time and said goodbye. Then, she happily went back to the palace.
