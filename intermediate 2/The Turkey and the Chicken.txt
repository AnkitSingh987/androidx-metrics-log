Category: Intermediate
Title: The Turkey and the Chicken

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there lived a pretty turkey. The pretty turkey had many friends. They played all day and had fun. One day, a little chicken dared the pretty turkey. The little chicken said, "I dare you to race me to the big tree!" The pretty turkey agreed, and they got ready to race. The pretty turkey and the little chicken ran as fast as they could. All their friends cheered for them. The pretty turkey won the race, and they all laughed and played together. They were very happy, and they all had a fun day at the farm.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a group of birds playing on the grass. Spot wanted to play too, but the birds didn't see him. He felt sad and walked away. Spot found a hidden spot under a big tree. He sat there and said, "I want to play with the birds, but they don't see me." A little ant heard Spot and said, "I can help you! I will talk to the birds for you." The ant went to the birds and said, "Spot wants to play with you. He seems sad because you didn't see him." The birds said, "We didn't see Spot! We want to play with him too!" So, the ant took the birds to the hidden spot under the tree. Spot was happy to see the birds, and they all played together. From that day on, Spot, the birds, and the ant were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable boat named Buddy. Buddy loved to sail around the world. He had many friends who lived in the sea, like fish and whales. One day, Buddy met a little fish named Fin. Fin was sad because he lost his way home. Buddy said, "Don't worry, Fin! I will help you find your way back home." Fin smiled and said, "Thank you, Buddy!" They sailed together and soon found Fin's home. All the fish were so happy to see Fin again. Buddy felt very proud to have helped his new friend. From that day on, Buddy and Fin were best friends. The moral of the story is: always be kind and helpful to others.

One day, Mom and Dad took their daughter to the beach. When they arrived, their daughter was so excited. She saw so many seagulls flying in the sky, the sand felt so soft, and the ocean seemed to sparkle like diamonds. She shouted, "Welcome to the beach!" Mom and Dad were so proud and began to laugh. Dad joked, "What a fat seagull!" The daughter couldn't believe it. She started to laugh too. They spent the rest of the day playing in the sand and building sandcastles. Every once in awhile, Dad would tell another joke and the daughter would giggle with excitement. It was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a house with her mom, dad, and baby brother. Lily loved to play with her toys upstairs in her room. One day, she wanted to play with her brother's toy car. But her mom said, "No, Lily. That is not your toy." Lily felt sad, but she started to think. She thought about how her brother would feel if she took his car. She knew it would make him sad too. So, Lily decided to ask her mom if they could play together with the toy car. Her mom said, "Yes, that is a good idea!" Lily, her baby brother, and her mom played together with the toy car upstairs. They had so much fun! Lily learned that it is better to share and play together, instead of taking things that are not hers. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Max. Max lived in a big lab with his friend, a kind scientist named Sam. They liked to have fun and play together every day. One day, Max saw a big, colorful ball in the lab. Max loved balls! He wanted to play with it. He could imagine how much fun it would be to chase the ball and catch it in his mouth. Max barked and wagged his tail. Sam saw Max and smiled. He knew Max wanted to play. So, Sam picked up the ball and threw it for Max. Max was so happy! He ran, jumped, and caught the ball. They played all day and had a great time.
