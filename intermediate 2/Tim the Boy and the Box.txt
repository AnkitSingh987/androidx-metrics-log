Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a lucky boy. He had a big toy box with many toys. One day, Tim wanted to support his friend, Sam. Sam was sick and had a bad lung. Tim thought he could make Sam feel better with a toy. Tim picked a toy from his toy box and went to Sam's house. On the way, something unexpected happened. A big dog came and took Tim's toy. Tim was sad and scared. He didn't know what to do. Tim went back home without the toy. He felt bad that he couldn't support Sam. The moral of the story is, sometimes, even if we try our best, things don't go as planned.

Once upon a time, in a big, dirty pond, there lived a friendly alligator named Ally. Ally loved to work hard every day, helping her friends in the pond. She would move logs, find food, and play games with her friends. One sunny day, Ally met a small, lost duckling named Ducky. Ducky was scared and sad. "I can't find my mommy," cried Ducky. Ally wanted to help her new friend. "Don't worry, Ducky. I will help you find your mommy," said Ally. So, they both started to look for Ducky's mommy around the pond. They asked Froggy, the green frog, if he had seen Ducky's mommy. "Yes, I saw her near the big tree," said Froggy. Ally and Ducky swam to the big tree, and there they found Ducky's mommy. Ducky was so happy, and she hugged Ally. "Thank you, Ally, for helping me," said Ducky. From that day on, Ally, Ducky, and all their friends played together in the dirty pond, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods. She saw a black arrow on the ground. Mia picked up the arrow and looked around. She saw a big tree with a target on it. She thought it would be fun to try and hit the target with the arrow. Mia pulled back the arrow and let it go. The arrow flew through the air and hit the target. Suddenly, a squirrel jumped out of the tree. "Hey!" said the squirrel. "That was my arrow! Can I have it back, please?" Mia was surprised to hear the squirrel talk, but she said, "Yes, you can have it back." The squirrel took the arrow and climbed back up the tree. Then, he threw a big acorn at the target. The acorn hit the target and made a loud noise. Mia laughed and said, "You are good at this game!" The squirrel smiled and said, "Thank you! I like to play this game with my friends. Do you want to play with us?" Mia nodded, and they all played together, having lots of fun in the woods.

One day, a group of five friends went to play in the park. They saw a wide slide and wanted to go down it. They were a little scared, but they wanted to try it anyway. The first friend said, "I am scared, but I will manage!" He went down the slide and had a big smile on his face. The other friends saw him and thought, "If he can do it, we can do it too!" Then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came running to the slide. The dog went down the slide too! The friends all laughed and said, "Wow, we didn't know dogs could slide too!" They all had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom was not just any cat, he was a very fast cat. He loved to speed around the yard, chasing birds and playing with his friends. One day, Tom met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a little mouse who lived in a hole in the ground. Tom said to Sam, "Let's play a game! I will run fast and you try to catch me." Sam thought it was an easy game, so he agreed. They started to play, and Tom began to speed away. Sam tried his best to catch up with Tom, but he was just too fast for him. Sam said, "You are very fast, Tom! I cannot catch you." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Sam. I will slow down so we can play together." From that day on, Tom and Sam became the best of friends. They played together every day, and Tom always made sure it was easy for Sam to catch him. And they lived happily ever after.
