Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Book

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Lily. She loved yoga. Every day, she would push and bend her body into different shapes. She had a lot of fun doing yoga. One day, Lily saw a new yoga pose in a book. It was unknown to her. She wanted to try it. Lily was very excited. She thought she could do it. Lily tried to do the new yoga pose. She pushed her body very hard. But, she fell down and hurt her arm. It was a bad ending for Lily. She learned to be careful when trying new things.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient store. In the store, there was a hose. The hose was very old and special. The store was in a big town where many kids lived. One day, a boy named Tim went to the store. He saw the hose and said, "Wow! Look at that old hose!" The store man said, "Yes, it is very old. It has been here for a long, long time." Tim wanted to play with the hose. He asked the store man, "Can I play with the hose, please?" The store man said, "Yes, you can play with it. But be careful, it is very old." So, Tim took the hose outside and played with it. He had lots of fun. The hose was very long and strong. Tim played with the hose all day long. When it was time to go home, he said goodbye to the hose and the store man. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and was very glad. He wanted to slide down it fast. Tim ran to the slide and started to climb up. At the top, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was scared to slide down. She said, "It seems too high!" Tim told her, "Don't worry, it's fun! I will slide down with you." So, Tim and Sue sat at the top of the slide. They held hands and slid down together. They laughed as they went fast. At the bottom, they were both very happy. They played on the slide all day and became good friends.

Tom and Anna are playing with blocks. They build a big tower with many colors. They are happy and proud of their tower. "Look, Mommy, look!" Tom says. "We made a big tower!" Mommy comes and smiles. "Wow, you did a great job, Tom and Anna! Your tower is very tall and pretty!" But then, a cat jumps on the table. The cat knocks down the tower with its paw. The blocks fall and make a loud noise. Tom and Anna are sad and angry. "Bad cat, bad cat!" Anna cries. "You broke our tower!" Mommy picks up the cat and puts it on the floor. She hugs Tom and Anna and says, "It's okay, my loves. The cat didn't mean to break your tower. It was just curious and playful. You can build another tower, or something else. You have many blocks to use." Tom and Anna wipe their tears and nod. They still love the cat, even if it was naughty. They decide to build a house for the cat, with a door and a window and a roof. They use the tip of a block to make a handle for the door. They are happy again and have fun. "Look, Mommy, look!" Anna says. "We made a house for the cat!" Mommy comes and smiles. "Wow, you did a great job, Anna and Tom! Your house is very cozy and cute!" The cat comes and sniffs the house. It likes the house and goes inside. It purrs and curls up on a soft block. Tom and Anna are happy and proud of their house. "Good cat, good cat!" Tom says. "You like our house!" Mommy claps and says, "You are very kind and creative, Tom and Anna! You made the cat happy, and you made me happy too!"

Once upon a time, there was a little beetle named Ben. Ben was a compassionate beetle, always ready to help his friends. One day, Ben decided to travel to a new place to make more friends. As Ben traveled, he met a sad bird named Billy. Billy couldn't fly because he hurt his wing. "Don't worry, I'll help you," said Ben. Ben and Billy became good friends, and they traveled together. One day, they found a big tree with other birds and beetles. They all played and had fun together. Ben and Billy were happy because they found new friends. In the end, Ben, the compassionate beetle, and Billy, the bird, were the best of friends, and they all lived happily together.
