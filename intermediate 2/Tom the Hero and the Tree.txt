Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Hero and the Tree

One day, a hero named Tom was very hungry. He walked in the park and saw a big apple tree. He wanted to eat the apples, but they were too high up. Tom saw a small girl named Lily near the tree. She was sad because she couldn't reach the apples too. Tom said, "I am a hero. I can help you get the apples." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, hero Tom!" Tom climbed the tree and gave some apples to Lily. They were both very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Tom and Lily into the air! They were scared, but Tom held Lily's hand. They landed on a cloud and found a big cake! They ate the cake and were not hungry anymore. They laughed and said, "This is the best day ever!" The wind blew them back to the ground, and they went home with a big story to tell.

One day, two children were playing in the park. They both stood in the sand pit, making a castle together. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise coming from the drain. They were curious, so they stood up and went to the drain to find out what was causing the noise. When they reached the drain, they found a little red bird. The bird had fallen in and was stuck. The first child said: "We must help the bird get out! We need to tidy the drain, so that it can fly away." The other child said: "Ok, let's start tidying the drain. We can move the stones and leaves away from the edge of the drain, so that the bird can fly out." The two children worked together to make the drain tidy. They moved the stones and leaves away and scraped away some of the mud. Finally, they made enough space for the bird to fly out of the drain. The bird flew away and chirped back at the two children to thank them. Then it flew off into the sky. The two children smiled and said goodbye to the bird, then they went back to their castle in the sand pit. They had been very brave and helpful.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily was very excited for her birthday. She knew her mom and dad were planning a big party for her. Lily wanted to help, so she decided to fold some paper to make hats for her friends. On the day of her birthday, Lily was a bit tired. She had stayed up late folding the paper hats. When her friends came to the party, they all wore the hats that Lily made. They played games, ate cake, and had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big box appeared in the middle of the room. Lily and her friends were very surprised. They opened the box and found a big, soft, and fluffy pillow. It was a gift from Lily's mom and dad, so she could rest when she was tired. Lily and her friends laughed and played with the pillow, and they all took turns resting on it. In the end, Lily's birthday was the best one ever!

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a stand with many toys. He wanted to pick a toy to play with. He picked a big, red ball. Tom was very happy. Tom played with the ball near a tree. Suddenly, a big wind came and took the ball away. Tom was upset. He looked for the ball but could not find it. He went back to the stand to pick another toy. When Tom got to the stand, he saw a girl holding his ball. She was upset too. The girl said, "I lost my ball, but I found this one." Tom smiled and said, "This is my ball, but we can play together." The girl was happy, and they played with the ball all day. The unexpected thing that made them both happy was finding a new friend to play with.

One day, a pale dog named Spot went to play with his friends. They played ball in the park. Spot was very happy. But then, Spot began to suffer. His leg hurt a lot. Spot's friend, a cat named Fluffy, saw him suffer. Fluffy said, "Spot, are you okay?" Spot said, "No, my leg hurts." Fluffy wanted to help Spot feel better. Fluffy and Spot went to find their friend, a bird named Chirpy. Chirpy knew how to help. Chirpy said, "Spot, you need to rest your leg." Spot listened to Chirpy and rested his leg. Soon, Spot felt better and could play with his friends again.
