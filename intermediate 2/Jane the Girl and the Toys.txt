Category: Intermediate
Title: Jane the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She had her own room with a big bed and lots of toys. Jane loved to draw and write notes to her friends. One day, she decided to write a note to her best friend, Tim. Jane wrote her note and put it in her pocket. She went outside to play in the rain with Tim. They jumped in puddles and got very wet. Jane forgot about the note in her pocket. When they went back inside, Jane remembered the note and took it out of her pocket. The note was all wet too! Jane was sad about her wet note. But Tim said, "Don't be sad, we can write a new note together!" So, they wrote a new note and had fun together. Jane learned that sometimes things get wet, but friends can always help make it better.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy bear. He lived in a forest. The bear had many friends in the forest. They liked to play together. One day, the bear and his friends were playing a game. They had to find the best tree. The bear found a very big tree. His friends found small trees. They did not agree on which tree was the best. They all disagreed. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down from the sky. The bird said, "All the trees are good. They are all the best!" The bear and his friends were happy. They learned that every tree in the forest was special. They played together and had a lot of fun.

Once, there was a stubborn little donkey named Jack. Jack didn't like to take turns with the other donkeys, so he always stayed far away. One day, the other donkeys had an idea. They thought that if they could get Jack to take a turn, then maybe he would change. So they all decided to go to Jack's house and ask him to take a turn. Jack was so stubborn he refused to move. He said he'd never ever take a turn. But then, the other donkeys put a carrot in front of Jack and asked him just one time. At first, Jack was still stubborn, but then something strange happened - he changed his mind. He suddenly started taking turns with the other donkeys. The other donkeys were so happy that Jack had changed! They celebrated with a special feast and Jack became the best turn taker of them all!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play at the park with her friends. One sunny day, Lily saw a big slide that she wanted to try. She was very excited. As Lily started to climb up the slide, she saw her friend Tim. Tim was upset and said, "I can't go on the slide. I need help." Lily wanted to help her friend, so she had an idea. She said, "Let's add a game to our slide fun!" Lily and Tim held hands and climbed up the slide together. They laughed and smiled as they slid down together. They played the game again and again until they were both happy. From that day on, Lily and Tim always played together at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy gun. He liked to play with the gun. One day, Tom saw a cat. The cat was in a tree. Tom wanted to help the cat. He had an idea. Tom said, "Please, cat, don't be scared. I will help you." He used his toy gun to poke the tree. The cat jumped out of the tree. Tom was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat was not a real cat! It was a toy cat. Tom felt stupid. He laughed and said, "Silly me! I thought you were real!" Then, Tom picked up the toy cat and went home. He played with his toy gun and the toy cat all day.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Sally. She had a toy car that she loved to play with. One day, her friend Tom came over to play. Tom saw the toy car and wanted to play with it too. Sally decided to lend her toy car to Tom so they could play together. They had lots of fun racing the car around the room. Suddenly, the car hit a wall and broke. Both Sally and Tom were in shock. They didn't know what to do. Sally's mom saw what happened and came to help. She fixed the toy car and gave it back to Sally and Tom. They were so happy to play with the car again. From that day on, Sally and Tom always shared their toys and played nicely together.
