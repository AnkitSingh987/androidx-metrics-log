Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and Dog Meet and the Park

One day, a cat and a dog meet in the park. They sit and discuss a toy they find. The toy looks like a gun. The cat says, "Is this toy safe for us to play with?" The dog says, "I am not sure. Let's ask a big person." They see a big person near and show him the toy gun. The big person looks at the toy and says, "This toy is not safe for little ones like you. I will take it away." The cat and the dog are sad, but they know the big person is right. Later, the big person comes back with a surprise. He gives the cat and the dog a new toy. The new toy is safe and fun for them to play with. The cat and the dog are happy and play together all day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a huge tree. The tree was so big that all the kids liked to play under it. Winter came and it was very cold. The tree's branches had snow on them. One day, a little boy named Tim and his friend, Lily, went to play near the huge tree. They saw a squirrel on a branch. The squirrel said, "Brrr, I'm so cold! Can you help me?" Tim and Lily wanted to help the squirrel. They ran back to their homes and got warm blankets. They spread the blankets on the branches of the tree. The squirrel was so happy and thanked them, "Thank you, Tim and Lily! Now I am warm and cozy." From that day on, Tim, Lily, and the squirrel became the best of friends. They played under the huge tree every day, even in the cold winter. And they always made sure their friend, the squirrel, stayed warm and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big gorilla named Gogo. Gogo loved to joke and play with his friends in the jungle. All the animals liked him because he made them laugh. But there was one small monkey named Momo who was jealous of Gogo. Momo wanted to be the funniest animal in the jungle. One day, Gogo and Momo were playing together. Gogo said, "Why don't you try to joke, Momo?" Momo thought for a moment and then told a joke. To his surprise, all the animals laughed! Momo was very happy that he could make his friends laugh too. From that day on, Gogo and Momo played and joked together. They made all the animals in the jungle laugh and have fun. The unexpected thing was that Momo and Gogo became best friends. They learned that sharing jokes and laughter was better than being jealous. And all the animals in the jungle lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim lived on a farm with his mom, dad, and a big red stable. The stable was old and needed to be fixed. Tim wanted to help restore the stable so the animals would be happy. One day, while Tim was working on the stable, he heard a voice. "Hello, young boy!" said the voice. Tim looked around and saw a talking horse. Tim was surprised but happy to have a new friend. The horse said, "I can help you fix the stable if you want." Together, Tim and the talking horse worked hard to restore the stable. They made it strong and beautiful. All the animals were now happy in their new home. Tim's mom and dad were so proud of him and the talking horse. They all lived happily ever after.

It was Tom's birthday and he was very happy. He had a big cake with four candles and many presents. His friends came to his house to celebrate with him. They played games, sang songs and ate cake. "Happy birthday, Tom!" his friends said. "You are a big boy now!" "Thank you, thank you!" Tom said. "I love you all!" But then, Tom saw something bad. There was a leak in the ceiling. Water was dripping on the floor. Tom was scared. He did not want his cake and presents to get wet. "Mom, Dad, look!" he cried. "There is a leak!" His mom and dad came to see. They saw the leak and frowned. They tried to stop the water with a bucket and a towel. But the leak was persistent. It did not stop. "Sorry, Tom," his dad said. "We have to call someone to fix the leak. We have to end the party now." Tom was sad. He did not want his party to end. He wanted to celebrate more with his friends. "But, Dad, it's my birthday!" he said. "I know, Tom, I know," his dad said. "But we have to be safe. The leak could make the ceiling fall. We don't want that to happen." Tom understood. He knew his dad was right. He hugged his friends and said goodbye. "Thank you for coming to my party," he said. "I had a lot of fun." "Sorry about the leak, Tom," his friends said. "We hope you have a better birthday next year." Tom smiled. He was still happy. He had a good party and good friends. He had a lot of cake and presents. He decided to celebrate again when the leak was fixed. He knew his mom and dad loved him and would make him happy. The end.
