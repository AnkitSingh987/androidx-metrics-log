Category: Intermediate
Title: Albert and the Door

Once there was a little boy called Albert who was three years old. Albert was very troubled and was feeling very sad one day. He lay in his bathtub and shivered from the cold. Suddenly he heard someone calling his name. He looked around and said, "Who's there?" "It's me," said a voice from outside the bathroom door. Albert got out of the bathtub and opened the door. There was his mom with a big hug for him. She asked him what was wrong and why he was feeling so sad. Albert said he was shivering from the cold and was feeling troubled. His mom said, "It's important to talk about anything that upsets you. Let's go make some hot cocoa together." Albert smiled and his mom dried his eyes. Together they went to the kitchen and made some hot cocoa. Albert soon felt much better.

Once there was a girl called Lucy. She loved watching cartoons on TV. However one day, when she was watching her favourite cartoon, her mum came in and switched off the TV. Lucy started to weep. She said to her mum, "Why do you do this? It's so weird". Mum replied, "Because I want you to be more creative and use your imagination. You can't get everything from watching cartoons". Lucy felt very sad but soon she understood what mum meant. She stopped weeping and started to draw her own cartoon characters. At the end of the day, Lucy realised that although she had learnt something by watching cartoons, she could be much more creative if she used her own ideas. The moral of the story is that you can be more creative when you use your own ideas and imagination.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree in a forest. The tree had many blue birds living in it. The birds loved to sing and play all day long. They were happy and always had fun. One day, the blue birds decided to have a race. They wanted to see who could finish flying around the thick tree first. They lined up, ready to start. "Ready, set, go!" they all shouted, and the race began. The birds flew around the thick tree as fast as they could. They were all trying their best to finish first. In the end, one little blue bird won the race. All the other birds clapped and cheered for the winner. They were all happy and continued to sing and play in their big, thick tree home.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with blocks. They make a big stack of blocks in the living room. They are very proud of their stack. "Look, Mom, look!" Tim says. "We made a stack!" Mom comes to see. She smiles and says, "Wow, that's a very high stack! You did a good job, boys!" But then, the cat comes. The cat is hungry. She sees a mouse on the floor. She runs after the mouse. She does not see the stack of blocks. "Oh no, watch out, cat!" Sam says. But it is too late. The cat bumps into the stack of blocks. The stack falls down. The blocks make a loud noise. The cat and the mouse run away. Tim and Sam are sad. They worked hard to make the stack. Now it is gone. "Sorry, boys," Mom says. "The cat did not mean to do that. She was hungry and wanted to catch the mouse. Maybe you can make another stack later." Tim and Sam nod. They are still sad, but they understand. They hug Mom and say, "We love you, Mom." Mom hugs them back and says, "I love you too, boys. How about we have some snacks? I'm sure you are hungry too." Tim and Sam smile. They like snacks. They follow Mom to the kitchen. They forget about the stack of blocks for now. They will play with them again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and dog, Spot. Lily loved to play outside in her big yard. She liked to run, jump, and laugh with Spot. They had so much fun together. One day, Lily and Spot found a vine. The vine was long and green. It grew on a tall tree. Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's play with the vine, Spot!" They decided to include the vine in their playtime. Lily and Spot took turns swinging on the vine. They felt like they could fly! Lily and Spot played with the vine all day. They were very happy. When the sun went down, Lily and Spot went back inside the house. They ate dinner with mom and dad. Lily told them all about the vine and how much fun they had. Mom and dad smiled and said, "We are happy you had a great day, Lily."
