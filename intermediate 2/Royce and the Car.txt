Category: Intermediate
Title: Royce and the Car

Royce was very excited today because he and his mom were going to the mall. He ran around the house, telling everyone how excited he was, until his mom finally said, "Let's go!" His mom opened the car door and he hopped in. When they arrived at the mall, Royce was so amazed. Everything was so strange- there were colorful lights, weird decorations, and lots of happy music. He couldn't believe how many people were walking around! Royce said to his mom, "This is so weird! Can I please explore?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, but only if you stay close by. Okay?" Royce nodded and jumped out of the car. He was so excited, as he skipped through the entrance of the mall. He had the best time exploring the strange stores and watching people walk by. There were so many interesting things to see and experience in the mall that he didn't want to leave. At the end of the day, Royce told his mom, "This was the best day ever! Can we come back soon?" His mom nodded and replied, "Of course, but for now, let's open the car door and go home."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to draw and make prints with her hands. One day, she made a big print on a paper with her hand. She was very patient and waited for it to dry. But then, her little brother Tom came and accidentally stepped on her print. Lucy was so sad that she started to weep. Her eyes filled with tears, and her brother saw how sad she was. Tom felt bad for making his sister weep. He wanted to help her feel better. So, he got some paint and made a new print next to hers. They both smiled and their prints looked very pretty together. Lucy stopped weeping, and they played together happily.

Anna liked to climb. She climbed on chairs, on tables, on beds, on trees. She climbed everywhere she could. But her mom did not like it. Her mom had a job. She worked on a big computer. She had to write and talk and think a lot. She did not have time to watch Anna climb. One day, Anna saw a big ladder. It was in the yard. It was next to the house. Anna wanted to climb it. She thought it would be fun. She did not see the sign. The sign said "Danger. Wet paint." Anna did not know what that meant. She ran to the ladder. She started to climb. She climbed higher and higher. She felt happy. She saw the roof. She saw the sky. She saw a bird. She said "Hi, bird!" But then she heard a voice. It was her mom. Her mom was angry. Her mom was grumpy. Her mom said "Anna, what are you doing? Get down from there right now!" Anna looked down. She saw her mom. She saw her mom's face. It was red. It was not happy. Anna felt scared. She said "Sorry, mom. I just wanted to climb." But then she saw something else. She saw her hands. They were not clean. They were blue. They were sticky. They were wet. She had touched the paint. The paint was on the ladder. The paint was on the house. The paint was on Anna. Anna said "Uh-oh."

One day, a girl named Lucy found a pen. It was a big and impressive pen. She wanted to draw pretty pictures with it. So she took the pen and started to draw on the wall. Her mom saw the wall and said, "Lucy, you should not draw on the wall. It is not good." Lucy felt sad. She wanted to make the wall clean again. She took a cloth and started to wipe the wall. As she wiped, something unexpected happened. The pen was magical! Every time Lucy wiped the wall, the pictures turned into real things. The flower she drew became a real flower, and the sun she drew shined brightly. Lucy and her mom were amazed. They decided to use the magical pen to make their home even more beautiful.
