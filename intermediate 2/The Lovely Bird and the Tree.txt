Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lovely Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little bird. The bird lived in a small cell. The cell was in a tree. The bird was not happy in the cell. The bird wanted to fly and see the world. One day, a kind girl came to the tree. She saw the bird in the cell. The girl said, "Oh, you poor bird! You suffer in that cell. I will help you." The girl opened the cell. The bird was free. The bird was so happy. The bird and the girl became friends. They played together every day. The bird sang lovely songs for the girl. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and her dog, Max. Max was a big dog with a fierce bark, but he was very kind and loved to play with Lily. One day, Lily's mom said, "Max is a big dog now. He needs to eat healthy food to stay strong. What do you recommend, Lily?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "I think Max should eat vegetables like the ones we have in our garden." So, Lily and her mom went to their garden and picked some vegetables for Max. They gave him carrots, peas, and a big, green cabbage. Max sniffed the vegetables, took a bite, and wagged his tail. He liked the vegetables very much, and Lily was happy that she could help her friend. From that day on, Max ate vegetables every day and grew to be a strong, healthy dog.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. One day, dark clouds came and a big storm started. The rain made everyone feel miserable. The wind blew hard, and the trees danced. Lily wanted to go outside and play, but her mom said, "No, it's not safe." So Lily had to choose something else to do. She looked around her room and saw her toys. She picked up her favorite doll and started to play with it. As the storm got louder, Lily's doll made her feel less scared. Lily and her doll played all day long until the storm went away. When the rain stopped, Lily's mom said, "Now you can go outside." Lily smiled and went outside to play, happy that she had her doll to help her through the miserable storm.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the beach with her mom and dad. She liked to play in the sand and feel the cool water on her feet. She saw many fun things at the beach, like shells and fish. Sue found an oyster in the sand. She picked it up and showed it to her mom and dad. They told her to put it back, but she wanted to keep it. So, she hid the oyster in her bag. Later, Sue wanted to go swimming in the water. She ran to the water, but she forgot about the oyster in her bag. As she ran, she felt something slip in her bag. Then, she fell down and started to cry. Her mom and dad came to help her. They saw the oyster in her bag and knew what happened. They were sad that Sue did not listen to them. Sue had to go home early, and she could not play at the beach anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play in his backyard. One day, Max saw a big cat in the backyard. The cat was very alert and watched Max closely. Max wanted to chase the cat away, but he needed help. He went to his friend, a bird named Bella. Max said, "Bella, please remind me to be brave." Bella agreed to help Max, and they both went to the backyard. With Bella's help, Max felt brave and barked at the cat. The cat ran away, and Max and Bella were happy. They played in the backyard all day, and Max never forgot how Bella helped him be brave.

One day, a cop named Bob was walking down the street. He was a dependable cop, always helping people. He saw a cat up in a tree. The cat was scared and did not know how to get down. Bob looked up and said, "Don't worry, cat. I will help you." The cat stared at Bob, hoping he could help. Bob tried to climb the tree, but he could not reach the cat. Just then, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird said, "I can help you, Bob." The bird picked up the cat with its beak and put it on the ground. The cat was happy and ran away. Bob said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird said, "You're welcome!" and flew away.
