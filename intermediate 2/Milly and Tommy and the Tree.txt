Category: Intermediate
Title: Milly and Tommy and the Tree

Once upon a time there were two friends, Milly and Tommy. They were the best of friends and were always together. One day, Milly had an idea. She said to Tommy, "Tommy, let's mark a hug tree!" Tommy was excited and thought it was very clever! So Milly and Tommy got some paint and started to mark a tree. Once they had finished they hugged the tree tight. Then they noticed something special, they could see their hug on the tree! Milly said, "That was clever of us! Now we can always remember the special hug we shared with this tree". From that day forward, Milly and Tommy always hugged the hug tree. This taught them the important moral value that special memories are treasures that can last forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a small, white envelope in her hand. Lucy was very hungry. Lucy walked to her mom and said, "Mom, I am hungry. Can I eat now?" Her mom looked at her and blinked her eyes. She said, "Lucy, first give me the envelope. Then, we can eat." Lucy gave the envelope to her mom. They opened it together and found a big, shiny gold coin inside! They were so happy. Now, they could buy lots of food to eat. So, they went to the store and bought yummy food. Lucy was not hungry anymore. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Sally. Sally had a big pile of blocks. She loved to make tall stacks of blocks and count them. One day, Sally was playing with her blocks, when suddenly, her block tower began spinning faster and faster! Her head felt dizzy and she had to sit down. Sally's mom came into the room. "What happened, Sally? Are you okay?" she asked. Sally told her mom what happened and explained that her tower of blocks made her feel dizzy. "Oh, Sally," said her mom, "you have to be careful when you build stacks of blocks. They can become too tall and start to spin. We don't want you to get dizzy again. Next time, build a smaller stack." Sally nodded. She was careful and always listened to her mom. From then on, she made sure to only build short stacks of blocks.

Once upon a time, there was a group of toys that lived in a big box. They were very happy and played together all day long. One day, a toy called Tim broke his arm. He was very sad. His friend, Sam, wanted to help him. Sam said, "I will fix your arm, Tim." Sam was very patient. He tried to fix Tim's arm with tape, glue, and string. But nothing worked. Tim's arm was still broken. The other toys in the group were sad too. They wanted to help, but they didn't know how. Finally, Sam had an idea. He asked a big toy named Max for help. Max tried to fix Tim's arm, but it was too hard. Tim's arm could not be fixed. All the toys in the group were very sad. They knew that Tim would never be the same again.

Anna and Ben love to play on the beach. They make sand castles, dig holes and look for shells. One day, they see a big boat on the sea. It is white and shiny and has a flag on it. "Wow, look at that boat!" Ben says. "It is so big and pretty!" "Can we go on it?" Anna asks. "I don't know," Ben says. "Maybe we can ask Mom and Dad." They run to their parents, who are sitting on a blanket. They point to the boat and ask if they can go on it. "Sorry, kids, but that boat is not for us," Mom says. "It belongs to someone else. And it is too far away." "But it looks so fun!" Anna says. "I know, sweetie, but we have our own fun here," Dad says. "Why don't you play with this adorable puppy we found?" He shows them a small, brown puppy that is wagging its tail. It has a red collar with a tag that says "Spot". "Aw, he is so cute!" Anna says. She picks up the puppy and hugs it. "Can we keep him?" Ben asks. "Well, we have to find out if he has an owner first," Mom says. "But maybe we can play with him for a while." The puppy licks Anna's face and makes her giggle. Ben pets the puppy and makes him roll over. They take the puppy to the water and splash him gently. The puppy barks and wags his tail. He likes the water. But then, a big wave comes and makes a loud noise. The puppy gets scared and trembles. He runs back to the blanket and hides under it. "Oh, poor puppy!" Anna says. She follows him and tries to comfort him. "It's okay, puppy, it's just water," she says. "It won't hurt you." She cuddles the puppy and sings to him softly. The puppy stops trembling and looks at her with his big, brown eyes. He trusts her. Anna smiles and kisses the puppy. She loves him. The end.
