Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Lively Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a lively little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy dinosaur. His dinosaur was big and green. They had lots of fun together. One day, Tim and his dinosaur went to the park. They saw a big tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree with his dinosaur. So, they started to climb up, up, up. Tim held his dinosaur tight so he wouldn't fall. When they got to the top of the tree, they looked around. They could see the whole park. Tim and his dinosaur felt so happy. They climbed down the tree and went home. They had a great day at the park.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a small, ordinary cabinet in her room. She did not know where it came from. She opened the cabinet and saw a tiny frog inside. The frog was green and could jump very high. "Hi, little frog!" Sue said. "Do you want to play with me?" The frog jumped out of the cabinet and onto Sue's hand. "Yes, I want to play!" said the frog. Sue and the frog played all day. They jumped on the bed, and they jumped over the toys. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, Sue put the frog back in the cabinet. "Good night, little frog," she said. "See you tomorrow!"

One day, a nice seal was at the beach. She wanted to find a seat to rest on. She saw a big rock and sat on it. The sun was warm and the seal was happy. A little boy came to the beach. He saw the nice seal on the rock. He walked up to the seal and said, "Hi! My name is Tim. Do you want to play with me?" The seal nodded and they played together in the sand. Then, something unexpected happened. The rock that the seal was sitting on started to move! It was not a rock, but a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, I am Tina the turtle. I was sleeping, but now I am awake. Can I play with you too?" The nice seal, the little boy, and the big turtle all played together on the beach, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to laugh and play with her friends. One sunny day, she saw a big wall in the park. It was very tall and clear. Lily was curious and wanted to know what was behind the wall. Lily asked her friends to help her climb the wall. They tried and tried, but they could not reach the top. Lily felt sad, but then she had an idea. She asked her friends to stand on each other's shoulders. With their help, Lily was able to climb the wall. When Lily looked over the wall, she saw a beautiful garden filled with flowers and butterflies. She laughed with joy and told her friends about the garden. They were all very happy. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can do great things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy bear, Teddy. One day, Lily and Teddy went on a walk in the quiet woods. The woods were very quiet, and Lily liked it. While they were walking, Lily saw a weird-looking bug. She thought it was funny and wanted to touch it. Teddy said, "No, Lily, don't touch the bug. It might be bad." But Lily didn't listen. She touched the bug, and it bit her finger. "Ouch!" she cried. Lily felt sad and said, "I regret not listening to you, Teddy." Teddy hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. We all make mistakes. Let's go home and make your finger feel better." They walked back home, and Lily learned to listen to her friend Teddy.

One day, a little girl named Lily was eager to hang her new star on her bedroom wall. She wanted her room to be pretty at night. "Mom, can you help me?" she asked. "Sure, Lily," said her mom. "Let's hang the star right here." They hung the star on the wall together. Lily was so happy. She could not wait for the night to see her star shine. That night, Lily went to bed and looked at her star. But something unexpected happened. The star began to move! It danced around the room, making pretty lights. Lily was surprised and happy. Her star made her room so beautiful at night.
