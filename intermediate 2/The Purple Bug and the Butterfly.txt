Category: Intermediate
Title: The Purple Bug and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a big purple bug. This bug lived in a small garden with many pretty flowers. The bug loved to play with his friends, the birds and butterflies. One day, it started to rain very hard. All the animals ran to find a place to hide. The purple bug said to his friend, the butterfly, "Let's go hide under the big green leaf!" The butterfly agreed, and they both went to hide. The rain did not stop for a long time. The garden started to soak with water. The purple bug and the butterfly were scared. They tried to stay dry under the leaf, but the water was too much. In the end, the purple bug and the butterfly got very wet and cold. They wished they had found a better place to hide from the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a little toy car with a motor. The car was very sad. It wanted to play with the other toys, but it did not know how to move. The car would watch the other toys play and have fun, but it could not join them. One day, a wise old toy came up to the sad car. The wise toy said, "I guess I know why you are sad. You don't know how to move, do you?" The sad car nodded. The wise toy smiled and said, "I will help you. Let's find the key to your motor." The sad car and the wise toy looked all over the playroom. They looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the toy box. Finally, they found the key under a big stuffed bear. The wise toy put the key in the car's motor and turned it. The car started to move! It was so happy! Now, the car could play with the other toys and have fun too. And the sad car was sad no more.

One day, a bossy bear named Ben went for a walk. He wanted to find some honey to eat. He walked and walked until he saw a big tree with a bee hive. Ben was very happy to find the honey. As Ben tried to get the honey, a small bird named Lily saw him. She flew down and said, "Ben, don't take the honey! The bees will be angry!" But Ben did not listen. He was too bossy to listen to a small bird. He called to Lily, "I want the honey, and I will get it!" Just as Ben was about to take the honey, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bee hive away! Ben and Lily looked at each other, surprised. Then, they saw the bees flying after their hive. Ben felt bad for not listening to Lily. Lily called to Ben, "Let's help the bees find a new home!" Ben agreed, and they worked together to find a safe place for the bees and their honey. In the end, Ben learned that it was better to be kind and helpful, rather than bossy. And he even made a new friend, Lily the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug who loved to crawl. One day, he found a new thing on the ground. It was a passport. The bug did not know what it was, but it was very pretty. The little bug wanted to show his friends the new passport. He crawled very fast to find them. He found his friend, the butterfly, and showed her the passport. The butterfly thought it was pretty too. Together, the bug and the butterfly crawled and flew around with the new passport. They had a lot of fun playing with it. And they lived happily ever after.

Lila and Ben are best friends. They like to play in the park and look at the stars. One night, they see a big, bright star in the sky. "Look, Lila, that is a planet!" Ben says. He knows a lot about space. He is very smart. "What is a planet?" Lila asks. She leans closer to Ben and listens. "A planet is a big ball of rock or gas that goes around the sun. There are many planets in space. Some are very hot and some are very cold. Some have rings and some have moons. The planet we live on is called Earth. It is blue and green and has water and air and plants and animals." Ben explains. "Wow, that is amazing!" Lila says. She wants to learn more about space. She thinks Ben is very smart. "Maybe one day we can go to space and see the planets up close. That would be fun!" Ben says. "Yes, that would be fun!" Lila agrees. They smile and hold hands. They lean back and watch the stars. They dream of space. They are happy.
