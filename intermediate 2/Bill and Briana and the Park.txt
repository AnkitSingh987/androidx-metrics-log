Category: Intermediate
Title: Bill and Briana and the Park

Bill and Briana were two best friends. They were always together, playing and having fun. One day, they took a walk in the park and passed by a charming purse. Briana said to Bill,"I wish I had that purse!". Bill said, "I'm sure we can find it in the store next time." The next day, they went to the store, but it was all sold out. Briana was very sad, and so was Bill. They both started to regret not buying the purse the day before. Briana looked at Bill and said," I'm sorry, Bill. I didn't buy the purse in the park." Bill felt badly for his friend and said,"It's okay, Briana. We can just look for a different purse that is just as charming." Briana smiled, "I guess we can do that!" With that, the two best friends continued on their walk with lots of laughs and smiles. Bill and Briana still regret not buying the purse, but they are more determined to find something even better!

Anna and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Anna has a red car and Ben has a blue truck. They make noises and pretend to drive around the carpet. "Vroom, vroom, my car is fast!" Anna says. "Beep, beep, my truck is strong!" Ben says. They have fun and laugh together. But then, Anna sees something outside the window. She stops playing and looks closer. She sees a big red fire truck parked on the street. It has lights and a ladder and a hose. She realizes that it is a real fire truck, not a toy. "Wow, look, Ben!" Anna says. "A fire truck!" Ben hears Anna and looks out the window too. He sees the fire truck and his eyes get big. He realizes that it is a real fire truck, not a toy. "Wow, cool!" Ben says. "A fire truck!" They drop their toys and run to the window. They press their noses against the glass and watch the fire truck. They see the firefighters get out and put on their helmets and coats. They see them roll out the hose and climb the ladder. They wonder what they are doing and where they are going. "Maybe there is a fire somewhere," Anna says. "Maybe they are saving someone," Ben says. They hope that everyone is safe and that the fire truck will come back soon. They think that the fire truck is the best thing they have ever seen. They forget about their toys and only look at the window. They are amazed by the red fire truck.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a doll that she loved very much. The doll had a pretty dress and a big smile. Lily took her doll everywhere she went. She was so grateful to have a doll to play with. One day, Lily and her doll went to the park. They saw a big slide that made Lily very excited. She wanted to slide down with her doll. So, Lily held her doll tight and climbed up the slide. Then, they slid down together. They went so fast, and it was so much fun! After playing on the slide, Lily and her doll went back home. They were tired but happy. Lily gave her doll a big hug and said, "Thank you for being my friend." That night, Lily and her doll slept very well, dreaming of more fun days to come.

Once there was a smart cat and a big dog. They lived in a house with a nice family. The cat and dog did not like each other. They would quarrel all the time. One day, the cat found a skull in the yard. The cat said to the dog, "This is mine!" The dog said, "No, it is mine!" They started to quarrel again. The little girl in the family heard them. She said, "Stop fighting! You can both play with the skull." The cat and dog listened to the girl. They played with the skull together and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to watch movies. His favorite movie was about a big, friendly bear. One day, Tom wanted to show his friend Sam the movie. Tom said, "Sam, come watch the movie with me!" Sam came over and they started to watch the movie. They were very careful not to make a mess while eating their snacks. While they were watching, a funny part came on. In the movie, the big bear tried to catch a butterfly. The bear moved too fast and accidentally slapped a tree with his paw. Tom and Sam laughed and laughed. They loved the movie and had a great time together.
