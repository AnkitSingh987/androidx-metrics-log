Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Story

One day, a little fish named Fin wanted to swim fast. He wanted to win a swim race with his friends. He asked his mom, "How can I swim fast and win the race?" His mom said, "You need to eat good food and be healthy. Then you can swim fast and win." Fin ate good food and felt healthy. He went to the race, ready to swim fast. His friends were there too. They all wanted to win. The race started and Fin swam very fast. He was ahead of his friends. He saw a shiny thing in the water. He wanted to see it. Fin swam to the shiny thing, but it was a trap! He got stuck in a net. His friends swam past him and finished the race. Fin did not win. He was sad. He learned that he should not get distracted by shiny things when he is racing. The moral of the story is to stay focused on your goal and not get distracted by things that may not be important.

Once upon a time, there was a little red house. In the house, there was a big red bowl. Inside the bowl, there was a lot of red jelly. The red jelly was very yummy. One day, a small boy named Tim saw the red jelly. He wanted to eat it. He tried to get the red jelly, but it was too high. Tim shouted, "Help, I want the red jelly!" A kind cat heard Tim's shout. The cat jumped up and got the red jelly for Tim. Tim was very happy. He said, "Thank you, cat!" Tim and the cat shared the red jelly and had a fun day.

Once upon a time there was a little brown boat. It sailed across the lake, zipping through the water. A little duckling who was swimming by stopped and said, “Hi boat! Where are you going?” The boat replied, “I’m going to have an adventure! Wanna come?” The duckling asked, “What kind of adventure?” The boat answered, “Well, I’m going to zip across the lake and try to find new friends. Who knows what I’ll find?” So the little duckling said goodbye and watched the little brown boat zip away. The end.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a big slide there. Sue was very happy and wanted to play on the slide all day. She ran to the slide and climbed up to the top. As she was about to slide down, she met a boy named Tim. Tim was wearing rich, shiny clothes. They both said "Hi!" and decided to slide down together. They laughed and played on the slide for a long time. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's rich, shiny clothes changed into normal clothes. He was not a rich boy after all. He was just like Sue. They both laughed and kept playing. They did not care about being rich or not. They were just happy to have a new friend to play on the slide with.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a tiny fairy. The fairy had impressive wings that sparkled in the sun. She loved to fly and play with her friends, the birds and the butterflies. One day, the fairy was playing hide and seek with her friends. She hid behind a big tree and waited for her friends to find her. While she was hiding, she heard a voice. "Hello," said the voice. The fairy peeked out and saw a little girl. "Hi," said the fairy. The little girl was very happy to meet the fairy. They played together all day. But then, the fairy did something silly. She flew too close to a flower and got her wings stuck. She felt embarrass. The little girl helped her get free. The fairy thanked the girl and they both laughed. After that day, the fairy and the little girl became best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. And the fairy learned to be more careful with her impressive wings.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to swim in the big pond. Every day, Ducky would push through the water with his strong feet. One sunny day, Ducky saw a little fish in the pond. The little fish was sad because he could not swim very well. Ducky wanted to help the little fish, so he had an idea. Ducky told the little fish to follow him. They swam together, with Ducky showing the little fish how to push through the water. The little fish learned how to swim better, and they both had fun. From that day on, Ducky and the little fish were the best of friends.
