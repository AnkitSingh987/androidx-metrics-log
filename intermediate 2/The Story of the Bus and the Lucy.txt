Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bus and the Lucy

One day, a gray bus drove down the street. It had big wheels and a loud horn. The bus stopped to pick up people. The people got on and sat down. Inside the bus, a little girl named Lucy could not manage to find a seat. She looked around and saw a kind old lady. The old lady said, "Come sit next to me, dear." Lucy smiled and sat down. As the bus drove on, Lucy and the old lady talked. They laughed and had fun. When it was time for Lucy to get off the bus, she said, "Thank you for helping me." The old lady smiled and waved goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie was different from the other cows. She had a big red spot on her side. One day, Bessie and her friend, a little bird named Chirpy, were talking. Bessie said, "I wish I didn't have this red spot. I want to be like the other cows." Chirpy said, "Don't be sad, Bessie. One day, your red spot will help you." Bessie didn't understand, but she trusted Chirpy. The next day, the farmer was looking for his favorite cow to give a special treat. He saw Bessie's red spot from far away and knew she was the one. The farmer gave Bessie a big, yummy treat. Bessie was happy and realized that being different can be good. She thanked Chirpy for his wise words.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went on a walk. She wanted to discover new things. She walked and walked until she found a big, hairy monster. The monster was sad and hungry. Lucy asked the monster, "Why are you sad?" The monster replied, "I am hungry and I can't find food." Lucy had an idea. She said, "Come with me. I know where there is food." Lucy took the monster to her home. She showed him the fridge. The monster was happy to see so much food. But when they opened the fridge, they found a tiny, talking mouse inside! The mouse said, "Please don't eat me! I can help you find more food." Lucy, the monster, and the mouse became friends and they all ate together.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He lived in a big house. He had many things, like a big car and a big TV. One day, he found a dirty ashtray on his table. He did not like it. He asked his friend, "Who put the ashtray here?" His friend did not know. The man dared his friend to find out. His friend said, "I will find out for you." His friend asked many people. No one knew. Then, he found a little boy. The boy said, "I put the ashtray there. I am sorry." The wealthy man was happy to know. He said to the boy, "It is okay. Just don't do it again." The boy said, "I promise." And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a long road. On this road, a girl named Lily walked every day. One day, she saw a little spark on the ground. It was a pretty stone. Lily took the stone and went home. At home, Lily showed the stone to her mom. Her mom said, "This stone is special. It will make your dreams come true. But you must return it to where you found it after one day." Lily was very happy. She wished to have a big, fun party with her friends. The next day, Lily had the best party ever. All her friends came, and they had lots of fun. Lily remembered that she had to return the stone. She went back to the long road and put the stone where she found it. The spark was gone, but Lily was happy. She knew that she had a great day because of the special stone.

Sara and Tom are twins. They like to eat cereal for breakfast. They have different cereals in the kitchen. Sara likes the cereal with the red box. Tom likes the cereal with the blue box. One morning, they go to the kitchen and see only one box of cereal. It is the red box. Sara is happy. Tom is sad. "Can I have some cereal, please?" Tom asks Sara. "No, this is my cereal. You can have something else," Sara says. "But I want cereal too. Why do you get all the cereal?" Tom says. Sara and Tom start to discuss. They raise their voices. They do not share. Mom hears them from the bedroom. She comes to the kitchen. She sees the red box of cereal and the twins. "What is going on here?" Mom asks. "Sara won't let me have any cereal. She says it is hers," Tom says. "But Tom wants my cereal. He has his own cereal," Sara says. Mom looks at the box. She sees a picture of a beautiful rainbow. She has an idea. "Look, kids, this is not just Sara's cereal or Tom's cereal. This is rainbow cereal. It has many colors. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. Just like the rainbow in the sky. Do you like rainbows?" Mom says. "Yes, we like rainbows. They are beautiful," Sara and Tom say. "Then why don't you share the rainbow cereal? You can each have a bowl and see the colors. Maybe you can even make a pattern with them. How does that sound?" Mom says. Sara and Tom think about it. They nod. They smile. "Okay, Mom. We can share the rainbow cereal. Thank you," Sara and Tom say. Mom pours the cereal into two bowls. She adds some milk. She gives them to the twins. They sit at the table and eat the cereal. They see the colors. They make patterns. They enjoy the cereal. They also enjoy each other. They stop discussing. They start talking. They say nice things. They laugh. They have fun. Mom watches them. She is happy. She thinks they are beautiful. Just like the rainbow.
