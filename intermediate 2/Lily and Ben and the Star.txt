Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Star

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks. They make towers and houses and bridges. Sometimes they add more blocks to make them bigger and stronger. One day, Lily finds a shiny star in her toy box. She shows it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I have a magic star!" she says. "What does it do?" Ben asks. "I don't know. Let's try it with our blocks!" Lily says. They put the star on top of their tower. They wait and watch. Nothing happens. "Maybe it is a slow magic star," Lily says. "Maybe we need to add something else," Ben says. He looks around and sees a red scarf. He wraps it around the star and the tower. Suddenly, the tower starts to glow. The blocks move and change. They turn into different shapes and colors. The tower grows taller and wider. It looks like a castle. Lily and Ben clap and cheer. "Wow, it is a magic star!" Ben says. "And a magic scarf!" Lily says. They run to the castle and knock on the door. A voice says, "Who is there?" Lily and Ben are surprised. They look at each other. "Who are you?" they ask. The door opens and they see a friendly dragon. He smiles and says, "I am the king of the castle. And you are my guests. Come in and play with me!" Lily and Ben are happy. They go inside and play with the dragon and his magic star. They have a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Emily. Emily was three years old and loved to explore. Every day, she liked to wander around in the park and wonder at all the beautiful things she could find. One day, Emily got lost in the park. She was very scared and tried to find her way back, but she couldn't. She had forgotten how she had gotten there in the first place. Just then, a big yellow monster appeared. Emily was very scared of him at first, but the monster was kind and offered to help her find her way back home. Together, they travelled around the park. Finally, the monster showed Emily the way out, and thanked her for being so brave. Emily went home with a smile on her face, happy to have found her way back. She had forgotten the fear and remembered the wonderful things she had seen.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. Today is a sunny day with a lot of wind. Anna and Ben have a kite. The kite is red and blue and has a long tail. "Let's fly the kite!" Anna says. "Yes, let's go to the park!" Ben says. They run to the park with the kite. They find a big grassy field. They hold the kite and run. The kite goes up in the sky. It looks like a bird. "Wow, look at the kite!" Anna says. "It is so high and fast!" Ben says. They laugh and clap. They enjoy flying the kite. They take turns holding the string. The kite dances in the wind. It makes shapes and loops. The sun is warm and the sky is blue. It is a perfect day for flying a kite. Anna and Ben are happy. They are having fun. They love their kite. They fly the kite until it is time to go home. They say goodbye to the kite and to each other. "See you tomorrow, Anna!" Ben says. "See you tomorrow, Ben!" Anna says. They hug and smile. They go home with their kite. They can't wait to fly it again.

One day, a big, pretty rainbow was in the sky. A little boy named Tim saw the rainbow and smiled. He wanted to play with the rainbow, so he tried to tease it. He made funny faces and danced around. The rainbow did not move. Tim thought the rainbow was deaf. He tried to yell and make loud sounds, but the rainbow still did not move. Tim felt sad that the rainbow could not hear him. Then, Tim's mom came outside. She saw the rainbow and smiled too. She told Tim that the rainbow was just a pretty thing to look at, not to play with. Tim understood and was happy just to see the rainbow.
