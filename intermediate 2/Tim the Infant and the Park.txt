Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Infant and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim loved to play at the park. His favorite thing to do was to swing on the swings. He would swing high up in the sky and feel like he could fly. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue at the park. Sue was swinging on the swings too, but she was swinging in a crazy way. She was going too fast and too high. Tim was scared for her. He said, "Sue, please don't swing so crazy. You might fall and get hurt." Sue listened to Tim and slowed down her swing. They both played together and had lots of fun. Tim and Sue learned that it is important to play safe, so no one gets hurt. And they became good friends who always looked out for each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lady. The lady had a big garden with many wild flowers. She loved her flowers very much. Every day, she would provide water to the flowers to help them grow big and strong. One day, the lady found a small seed in her garden. She thought it was a special seed. She planted the seed in the ground and gave it lots of water and love. She knew that one day, the seed would grow into something amazing. Days went by, and the seed began to grow. The lady was very excited. One day, the seed turned into a big, beautiful, wild flower. The flower was the most wonderful thing the lady had ever seen. It made her very happy. The lady knew that her love and care had helped the flower grow, and she was very proud.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. The rock was near a tunnel. Many kids liked to play near the rock and the tunnel. They would run, jump, and have fun. One day, two friends, Tim and Sam, wanted to see who was faster. They were very competitive. They decided to race from the rock to the tunnel. They asked their friend, Lily, to watch and say who won the race. Tim and Sam got ready to race. Lily counted, "One, two, three, go!" Tim and Sam ran as fast as they could. They both wanted to win. They reached the tunnel at the same time. It was a tie! Tim and Sam laughed and decided to race again. They had lots of fun being competitive together.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day. A boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to play outside. They took their favorite toy, a football. While playing, the football went into a big tree. Tim looked up and said, "Oh no! Our football is stuck!" Max barked and tried to rescue it by jumping high. But Max could not reach the football. A big bird in the tree saw the football. The bird took the football in its beak and flew away. Tim and Max looked at each other and felt very sad. They could not play with their football anymore. The end.

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to travel. They had a big map on the wall of their room and they would point to different places and pretend they were there. Sometimes they would pack their bags and go on a trip around the house, looking for new things to see and do. One day, they decided to travel to the moon. They put on their helmets and suits and got into their rocket, which was really a cardboard box. They counted down from ten and then shouted, "Blast off!" They made loud noises and shook the box as if they were flying through the air. They looked out of the window and saw the stars and the planets. They were very excited and wondered what the moon would look like. They hoped they would find some aliens or some cheese. They talked to each other on their radios, which were really tin cans with strings. "Ben, do you see the moon?" Sara asked. "Yes, Sara, I see it. It's big and round and shiny. We're almost there!" Ben answered. They got ready to land on the moon. They pushed a button, which was really a sticker, and the rocket slowed down. They felt a bump and then they were on the moon. They opened the door and stepped out of the rocket. They looked around and saw that the moon was very different from their home. It was dark and quiet and cold. There was no grass or trees or flowers. There was only dust and rocks and craters. They felt very light and could jump very high. They explored the moon and had fun. They collected some rocks and made some footprints. They waved to the earth, which was a small blue ball in the sky. They pretended they met some aliens, who were really their stuffed animals. They were having so much fun that they didn't notice the time. They didn't hear their mom calling them for dinner. They didn't see the sun setting and the moon getting darker. They didn't realize they were in trouble. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. They looked up and saw a big shadow on the ceiling. It was their dad, who had come to look for them. He was not happy. He lifted the box and turned on the light. "Sara and Ben, what are you doing?" he asked. "We're on the moon, dad!" they said. "You're not on the moon, you're on the floor. And you're making a mess. And you're late for dinner. And you're in trouble. Come on, let's go." he said. He took them out of the box and carried them to the kitchen. They were sad and sorry. They had to clean up their room and eat their dinner and go to bed early. They learned their lesson. They decided that traveling to the moon was not so easy after all.
