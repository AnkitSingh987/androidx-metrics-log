Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The big tree had many leaves. A little bird lived under the big tree. The little bird liked to measure things. One day, the little bird saw a big rock. The little bird wanted to measure the big rock. So, the little bird flew to the big rock. The little bird measured the big rock with its wings. But while the little bird was measuring the big rock, a big wind came. The big wind was very strong. The little bird tried to fly back to its home under the big tree, but the big wind was too strong. The little bird could not fly back home. The big wind took the little bird far away from the big tree. The little bird was lost and could not find its home under the big tree again. The little bird was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and make new games. One day, she saw her toys fight with each other. They were jumping on the furniture and making a big mess. Lily thought it was fun to watch her toys fight, but soon, the furniture got hurt. The couch got a big rip, and the chair lost a leg. Lily felt sad because her furniture was broken. Lily knew she had to fix the mess. She used her creative mind to make the toys stop fighting. She told them a story about how friends should be kind and help each other. The toys listened and learned the lesson. They stopped fighting and started to help fix the furniture. Lily was happy because her toys were friends again, and her furniture was fixed. The moral of the story is to be kind and help each other instead of fighting.

Tom and Sue were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big hill near their town. They wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. They asked their moms if they could go, but their moms said no. It was too cold and too far and too dangerous. But Tom and Sue did not listen. They waited until their moms were busy and then they ran away. They took their coats and hats and gloves and a backpack with some snacks and water. They thought they were very brave and smart. They started to climb the hill, but it was harder than they thought. The wind was strong and the snow was deep. They got tired and thirsty and hungry. They also got scared. They heard strange noises and saw dark shadows. They did not know where they were or how to go back. They found a cave and decided to rest there. They hoped their moms would find them soon. They ate some snacks and drank some water and tried to warm up. But they were still very cold and very scared. They shivered and hugged each other. "Tom, I'm sorry we ran away," Sue said. "I wish we listened to our moms." "Me too, Sue," Tom said. "I wish we were home." They did not know that the cave was not empty. It was the home of a big, hungry bear. The bear smelled them and woke up. It roared and ran towards them. Tom and Sue screamed and tried to run, but it was too late. The bear caught them and ate them. They never saw their moms again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy bear named Ted. Sue and Ted loved to play together every day. One Wednesday, they played in the mud outside. They got very dirty and needed to wash. Sue said, "Ted, we are so dirty. Let's wash our hands and face." Ted was glad to wash with Sue. They went inside the house and washed up together. The water was warm and made them feel clean and happy. After they were clean, Sue and Ted went back to play. They learned that it's important to wash after getting dirty. From that day on, they always washed up after playing. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to always keep clean and wash up after playing.
