Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cake

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a yellow radio in her room. She was very happy. She turned it on and heard fun music. Lucy started to dance and sing along with the songs. She felt so happy! Lucy's mom came into the room and saw the yellow radio. "Wow, Lucy! That's a nice radio!" said her mom. Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, mom! I love it! Let's dance together!" So, Lucy and her mom danced and had lots of fun. Later, Lucy and her mom went to the kitchen to make a cake. Lucy's mom let her stir the cake mix. She stirred and stirred, making the cake mix smooth. After they baked the cake, they ate it while listening to the yellow radio. It was a great day!

Once upon a time, there was a bear cub named Bobo. He lived in a cozy den in the forest. One day, Bobo got frustrated because he didn't know how to get out of his den. He cried out for help but no-one heard him. While Bobo was crying, a tiny cell appeared in the air. The cell spoke to Bobo and said, "Why are you so frustrated, little one? Maybe I can help you." Bobo replied, "I want to rise up and get out, but I can't do it by myself!" The cell said, "All you have to do is imagine you can rise up and you will start to float." Bobo did as the cell said and sure enough, he began to rise up until he was out of his den. He was so happy and thanked the cell for its help. The cell smiled and said, "It was my pleasure to help, little one. Now you can go and explore your world." Bobo waved goodbye to the cell and ran off to explore the world outside his den.

Lily was bored at home. She wanted to play with her toys, but they were all in her room. Her mom was working in the office. She said she had to do important things on the computer. Lily did not like the office. It was quiet and dull. She decided to go to the office and see what her mom was doing. She opened the door and saw her mom typing on the keyboard. She had papers and books on the desk. Lily thought they looked interesting. She wanted to touch them and see what they said. She walked over to the desk and reached for a book. But her mom saw her and yelled, "Lily, no! Don't touch that! You'll make a mess!" Lily was scared and sad. She did not mean to make her mom angry. She just wanted to see the book. She started to cry and ran out of the office. Her mom felt bad for yelling at her. She knew Lily was curious and bored. She finished her work and went to find Lily. She hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to yell at you. I love you very much. Do you want to read the book with me?" Lily nodded and smiled. She wiped her tears and said, "Yes, please. I want to read the book. It looks interesting." Her mom took her to the couch and opened the book. It was a story about a princess and a dragon. Lily listened and looked at the pictures. She forgot about the office and the yelling. She was happy and interested.

Sara and Tom were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big, long snake in the grass. Sara was scared and ran away, but Tom was curious and wanted to touch it. "Tom, don't go near the snake!" Sara shouted. "It can bite you and hurt you!" But Tom did not listen. He walked slowly to the snake and gazed at its shiny skin. He reached out his hand and felt the texture of the snake. It was smooth and cold. The snake did not like Tom's touch. It hissed and coiled around Tom's arm. Tom screamed and tried to shake it off, but the snake was strong and tight. Sara heard Tom's scream and ran back to help him. She found a big stick and hit the snake on the head. The snake let go of Tom and slithered away. Sara hugged Tom and checked his arm. It was red and swollen, but not bleeding. She helped him walk to their house and told their mom what happened. Mom was angry and worried. She scolded Tom for being foolish and careless. She put some ice and medicine on his arm and told him to rest. Tom felt sorry and ashamed. He thanked Sara for saving him and apologized for not listening to her. He learned his lesson and never touched a snake again. Sara was happy that Tom was okay. She forgave him and told him she loved him. She learned that she was brave and caring. She always looked after her brother and protected him from danger.
