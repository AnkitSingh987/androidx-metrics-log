Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Mia and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Mia. She was three years old and loved to wear colourful skirts. One sunny day, Mia asked her mom if she could go outside and play. Her mom said yes, but asked her to trust that if she ever needed help, she would come running. Mia was so excited to go and play that she ran out to the garden and began to explore the world. She tried to climb up the tree, but it was difficult. Mia became scared that she wouldn't be able to make it up the tree and she started to cry. That's when she thought about what her mom said about trusting her if she ever needed help. So Mia called out for her mom. Her mom came running and said, "You can do it, sweetheart. Just keep trying and trust yourself." Together, Mia and her mom climbed up the tree and the view was amazing! Mia learnt an important lesson that day. Her mom had taught her that it's important to trust yourself and that sometimes it's okay to ask for help when things get too difficult!

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot could do many tricks. One day, Spot heard a noise. It was a little bird that fell from its nest. Spot knew he had to help. Spot tried to explain to the bird how to get back to its nest. The bird listened very carefully. With Spot's help, the bird tried to fly again. Spot knew that the bird would need more help to reach its nest. So, Spot got his friends, the ants, to make a long line. The bird climbed on the ants, and they carried it back to its nest. The bird was very happy and thanked Spot and the ants. The moral of the story is: when we help each other, we can do great things.

One day, a colorful bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a big, red ambulance on the road. The ambulance was going fast with its loud sound. The bird was curious and wanted to see what was happening. The colorful bird flew down and saw a little girl crying. She had fallen off her bike and hurt her knee. The bird wanted to help, so it said, "Do not cry, little girl. The ambulance is here to help you feel better." The little girl looked at the bird and said, "Thank you, colorful bird. I accept your help." The ambulance took the little girl to the doctor, and the bird flew with them. The doctor fixed her knee, and she felt better. After that, the little girl and the colorful bird became best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. The bird always made sure that the little girl was safe and happy.

Anna and Ben were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They also made a snow fort with a flag. They pretended to be soldiers and threw snowballs at each other. "Give up, Ben! You can't win!" Anna shouted from behind her fort. "Never! I will never surrender!" Ben shouted back. He threw a snowball at Anna, but missed. Suddenly, they saw something black and white waddle towards them. It was a penguin! The penguin looked at them with curious eyes. It tilted its head and made a funny noise. "Wow! A penguin! Where did it come from?" Anna asked. "I don't know. Maybe it escaped from the zoo. Let's go say hello!" Ben said. They put down their snowballs and ran towards the penguin. The penguin was not afraid. It let them touch its feathers and beak. It followed them to their snowman and their fort. It even tried to wave the flag with its flipper. "Hello, penguin! What is your name?" Anna asked. "Quack, quack!" the penguin answered. "We can call you Quack, then. Do you want to play with us?" Ben asked. "Quack, quack!" the penguin nodded. Anna and Ben smiled. They forgot about their snow war. They had a new friend. They played with Quack until it was time to go home. They gave Quack a hug and a kiss. They hoped to see him again.
