Category: Intermediate
Title: Melody the Girl and the Mommy

Once there was a girl named Melody. She loved to play outside, but one day her mommy said she had to work. She didn't know what work meant, but she decided to do what her mommy said. Outside, the ground was all bumpy. Melody liked the texture of the dirt, so she started to dig and explore. As she dug, she found a small, harmless creature. It was a little critter with a soft, furry back. She was so happy to find it. But her mommy told her she needed to get to work. Melody knew she had to keep digging, but she also wanted the be sure the critter was safe. So instead of digging she decided to put the critter away from the hole and make sure it was safe. Even though her mommy said she had to work, Melody still thought it was important to keep the critter safe. She was sure this harmless little creature would find its way.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom to buy a crab. Tim was very happy. He loved crabs and wanted one as a pet. His mom said, "Okay, we can buy a crab today." At the store, they saw many crabs. One crab looked troubled. It was sad and did not play with the other crabs. Tim felt bad for the troubled crab. He said, "Mom, I want to buy this crab and make it happy." Tim and his mom bought the troubled crab and took it home. They gave it a nice home with water, sand, and food. The crab became happy and played a lot. Tim loved his new pet crab and they were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a regular family. They had a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lily. They all lived in a small house with their dog, Max. Every Sunday, they would leave their house and go to the park. One Sunday, Lily said to Max, "Let's go to the park and play!" Max wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's go!" So, they told mom and dad, and they all left the house together. At the park, the sun was shining, and the grass was green. Lily and Max played catch with a ball. Mom and dad sat on a bench and watched. They all had a fun time. After a while, they were tired and hungry. So, they went back to their small house and had a big meal. They were all happy and had a good Sunday.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had big eyes and a fluffy tail. One day, Max saw a naughty cat named Tom. Tom liked to play tricks on Max. Max wanted to be friends with Tom, so he decided to invite Tom to his house to play. Max went to Tom's house and said, "Tom, I invite you to my house to play." Tom looked at Max with his green eyes and said, "Okay, Max. I will come to your house to play." Max was very happy that Tom said yes. When Tom came to Max's house, they played with Max's toys. Max and Tom had a lot of fun together. They played all day long. Max looked into Tom's eyes and saw that Tom was not naughty anymore. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim lived in a peaceful town with his mom and dad. He liked to play with his friends and have fun. One day, Tim saw a big cake on the table. His mom said, "Tim, do not eat the cake. It is for later." Tim tried to resist, but the cake looked so yummy. When his mom was not looking, Tim took a big bite of the cake. Suddenly, a little fairy jumped out of the cake! The fairy said, "Hello, Tim! I am here to grant you a wish because you are a good youth." Tim was very surprised. He thought for a moment and said, "I wish for more cake!" The fairy laughed and waved her wand. Then, more cakes appeared on the table. Tim and his mom were both happy, and they shared the cakes with all their friends.
