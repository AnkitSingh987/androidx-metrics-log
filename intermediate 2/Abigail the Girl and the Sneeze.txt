Category: Intermediate
Title: Abigail the Girl and the Sneeze

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Abigail. Abigail loved to play in her room, but one day when she was playing, she started to feel a tickle in her nose. Suddenly, she let out a big sneeze. But this wasn't an ordinary sneeze. Something really strange happened. The sneeze was so powerful that it made the floor shake! Abigail was very frightened. She held onto the wall and started to cry. Then, she heard a voice. “It’s okay Abby,” the voice said. Abigail looked up and saw her mom standing there. She was smiling. “It’s alright, sweetie," her mom said. "That was just a big sneeze! I used to do the same thing when I was little. You just have to practice a few times and you’ll be able to do it too!” Abigail was relieved. She smiled at her mom and decided to practice her sneezes. She knew she would get better with practice and she wasn’t frightened anymore.

One day, a big cat named Tom saw a flame. The flame was bright and hot. Tom blinked his eyes and looked at the flame. Tom met a small dog named Sam. Sam said, "Tom, don't go near the flame. It can hurt you." Tom didn't listen to Sam. He wanted to play with the flame. Tom touched the flame, and it hurt him. He cried and ran away. Sam was sad too. The big cat didn't listen, and now he was hurt.

One day, a big black dog named Buddy went to the park. He saw a boy with a ball in his hand. The boy threw the ball and said, "Buddy, go get it!" Buddy ran fast and caught the ball. He was happy to serve the boy. Buddy brought the ball back to the boy. The boy said, "Good job, Buddy!" He patted Buddy's head with his hand. Buddy wagged his tail and felt proud. The boy threw the ball again, and Buddy ran to catch it. They played for a long time until the sun went down. The boy said, "Bye, Buddy! See you soon!" Buddy waved his tail and went home. He had a fun day serving the boy and playing with the ball.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. She was very eager to see what was lurking in the garden. Jane peered through the window and saw a small worm. Jane was so excited that she ran outside to get a closer look. Her mum watched as Jane moved closer to the worm's hole, peeking and listening. Jane tapped on the hole and said, "Hello, is anyone there?". Suddenly, the worm peeked its head out of the hole and looked at Jane. She smiled and said, "Would you like to come and play with me?". The worm seemed excited and wiggled its way out of the hole. The two friends then ran around the garden together, playing hide and seek. Jane was so happy to have made a new friend.

One day, a cute little dog went to play outside. He saw a big rake in the yard. The dog wanted to play with the rake, but it was too big for him. He tried to push it, but it did not move. The dog felt sad. Then, the dog had an idea. He ran to the house and called his friend, a small cat. The cat came to help the dog. Together, they tried to push the rake. It was still hard, but they did not give up. After trying and trying, the dog and the cat finally moved the rake. They were so happy and proud of themselves. They played in the yard all day with the rake. The dog and the cat were the best of friends.

Mummy, Daddy and baby Jack were walking to the restaurant. Jack was very excited. He couldn't wait to eat all the yummy food. When they got to the restaurant, it was very hot inside. Jack started to feel very sleepy. He yawned and stumbled into his chair. Mummy looked at Jack and said, "We shouldn't have stayed outside for so long." Daddy agreed and said, "Yes, let's order quickly so we can get some delicious food and then get home for a nap." Jack was happy. He wanted to stay at the restaurant and eat, but he was too tired. So off they went, to have a nice rest at home.
