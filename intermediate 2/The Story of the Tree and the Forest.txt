Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree in a forest. The tree was very tall and strong. Many animals lived in the tree and they were all happy. One day, a bright lightning hit the tree. The tree started to burn. The animals that lived in the tree did not understand what was happening. They were all scared and ran away to find a new home. The animals found a new tree, but it was not as thick as the old one. They learned that they should not live in just one tree. They started to live in many trees so that if something bad happened again, they would not lose their homes. The animals were now safe and happy. The moral of the story is to not put all your trust in one place.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there were many different fruits. There were red apples, yellow bananas, and green pears. The pears were very yummy. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the tree. He saw the pears and wanted to take some home. So, he took a sack and filled it with the green pears. Tim was very happy. As Tim was walking home, he met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the sack of pears and said, "Wow, those pears look good! Can I have one?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, you can have one." They shared the pears and became good friends. From that day on, they always played together and shared different fruits from the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with his best friend, a big dog named Max. They were very happy playing together all day. One sunny day, Tom and Max found a big box in their yard. They didn't know where it came from. They tried to open it, but it was too hard. Tom had an idea. He would use his power to break it open. He ran and jumped on the box. Crash! The box broke open and many colorful balls came out. Tom and Max were so happy and started to play with the balls. But then, something unexpected happened. The balls started to move on their own! Tom and Max were surprised. The balls were magic! They could talk and play with Tom and Max. Now, Tom and Max had many new friends to play with. The poor little cat and the big dog were not lonely anymore. They all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their ball. They saw a big house with a fence and a gate. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's go there," Ben said. "Maybe they have toys and candy." "No, Ben, that's not our house. That's a rich house. We can't go there," Lily said. But Ben did not listen. He ran to the gate and opened it. He saw a pool with blue water and a fountain. He was thirsty and wanted to drink some water. He ran to the pool and leaned over the edge. But he slipped and fell into the water. He could not swim and he was scared. He shouted for help. Lily heard him and ran to the pool. She saw Ben in the water and tried to reach him. But she was too small and the pool was too deep. She cried and called for help too. A man came out of the house and saw the children. He was angry and scared. He ran to the pool and pulled Ben out of the water. He was wet and cold and he coughed. The man called an ambulance on his phone. He told the children to stay on the grass and wait. He said they were naughty and they should not have come to his house. Soon, the ambulance came with a siren and a light. Two people came out of the ambulance and looked at Ben. They put a blanket on him and a mask on his face. They said he needed to go to the hospital. They put Ben on a stretcher and took him to the ambulance. Lily followed them and wanted to go with Ben. She was sorry and scared. "Can I go with him?" she asked. "Yes, you can. But you have to be quiet and sit still," one of the people said. They let Lily sit next to Ben in the ambulance. They closed the door and drove away. Lily held Ben's hand and said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I hope you get better soon."
