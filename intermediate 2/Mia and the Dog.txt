Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and the Dog

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia and her dog, Buddy, went to spend the day outside. They wanted to see the big landscape with all the trees, flowers, and animals. They knew it was the best place available to play and have fun. As they walked, Mia said to Buddy, "Look at all the pretty colors! We can play here all day!" Buddy wagged his tail and barked, showing he was happy too. They played with the available sticks and chased each other around the trees. At the end of the day, Mia and Buddy were tired, but they had a lot of fun in the beautiful landscape. They went home and told Mia's mom about their adventure. Mia's mom was happy to see them so joyful and gave them both a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very sleepy. Tim had an aunt who loved him a lot. Her name was Aunt May. One day, Tim and Aunt May went for a walk. They saw a big tree with a swing. Tim wanted to play on the swing. Aunt May said, "Let me tie the swing for you, Tim." Aunt May tied the swing to the tree. Tim played on the swing and had lots of fun. After playing, they both went back home. Tim was so sleepy that he went to bed right away.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig in the dirt. One day, Spot was in the yard and saw a big pit. He knew he had to part the dirt to see what was inside. Spot started to dig and dig. He was very happy. He knew he would find something good. The sky started to get dark, and Spot knew it was time to go inside. But he was not done yet. He wanted to be successful in finding what was in the pit. The next day, Spot went back to the pit. He dug and dug some more. At last, he found a big, shiny bone! Spot was so happy and proud. He had been successful in his digging. He took the bone home and showed it to his family. They all knew that Spot had done a great job because he did not give up.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. All the animals wanted to win. They wanted to be the most powerful. The race was very important, and everyone wanted victory. A little mouse named Timmy wanted to race too. His friend, the elephant, said, "Timmy, you are too small. You can't win." But Timmy wanted to try. He said, "I will watch and learn. I will be fast like the wind." The day of the race came. All the animals ran very fast. Timmy watched them and learned. He saw a secret way to go. The other animals did not see it. Timmy ran very fast through the secret way. He passed all the other animals. At the end of the race, Timmy was first. He won and had victory. All the animals were surprised. They said, "Wow, Timmy is powerful!" Timmy was happy. He showed that even a little mouse can be strong and fast.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a smelly tray in his room. He did not like the smell. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, my tray is smelly. What should I do?" His mom thought for a moment and then suggested, "Why don't you clean the tray, Tim? That will make it smell better." Tim took his mom's advice and cleaned the tray. He scrubbed and scrubbed until the smell was gone. He felt very happy and proud that he had fixed the problem. Later, Tim's mom said, "Good job, Tim! You solved the problem by yourself." Tim learned that when there is a problem, it is important to ask for help and try to fix it. And so, the moral of the story is: If you have a problem, don't be afraid to ask for help and work hard to solve it.
