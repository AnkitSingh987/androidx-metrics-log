Category: Intermediate
Title: The Helpful Child and the Adult

Once upon a time, there was a helpful child. The child wanted to do something fun and asked an adult to let them do it. The adult said yes, and the child was so happy. Next, the helpful child went outside and made a big sandcastle in the yard. Then they used some pebbles and shells to decorate it. But the child saw that they needed something else: a flag! The helpful child asked the adult if they could let them go to the store and buy a flag. The adult said yes, and the child went quickly. At the store, they found a big colorful flag and bought it. When the child got home, the adult put the flag on the top of the sandcastle. The helpful child was so proud of their sandcastle and the flag - it was perfect! The end.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there lived a monkey. The monkey was very sad and miserable. He had no friends to play with, and he was all alone. One day, a little boy came to the tree. He saw the sad monkey and wanted to help. The boy had a big, red ball. He decided to lend his ball to the monkey so they could play together. The monkey was so happy to have a friend and a ball to play with. They played all day long, and the monkey was not miserable anymore. The boy and the monkey became best friends, and they always shared their toys with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fierce dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with a little girl named Lily. Lily loved Max very much and wanted to teach him new things. One sunny day, Lily found a big map in her room. She wanted to show Max the map, so she called him. "Max, come here! Look at this big map!" Max came and looked at the map with Lily. They saw many fun places on the map. Lily decided to teach Max how to find places on the map. She pointed to a park and said, "Max, this is the park we play at!" Max looked at the park on the map and barked happily. They spent the whole day looking at the map and learning about new places.

Lily loved to play sport with her friends. She liked to run, jump, kick and throw. She was good at sport and always had fun. But one day, a new boy came to her school. His name was Max and he was very mean. He did not like to share or play nice. He wanted to have all the sport things for himself. When Lily and her friends went to play sport, Max took their ball and ran away. He laughed and said, "This is my ball now. You can't have it." Lily and her friends were very sad and angry. They wanted their ball back. They tried to chase Max, but he was too fast. He hid behind a big tree and threw the ball at them. It hurt and made them cry. Lily and her friends did not know what to do. They felt miserable. They did not want to play sport anymore. They went to their teacher and told her what Max did. The teacher was very upset and went to find Max. She saw him behind the tree, holding the ball. She said, "Max, that is not your ball. That is Lily and her friends' ball. You need to return it to them and say sorry. That is not how we play sport. We play sport to have fun and be kind, not to hurt and be mean." Max felt ashamed and scared. He knew he was wrong. He gave the ball back to Lily and her friends and said sorry. He asked them if they would let him play with them. Lily and her friends were surprised, but they were also generous. They said yes, but only if he promised to share and play nice. Max agreed and said he would try to be a better friend. Lily and her friends taught Max how to play sport the right way. They ran, jumped, kicked and threw together. They had fun and laughed. Max learned to be more respectful and friendly. He was happy and grateful. He realized that playing sport was more fun when you share and play nice. He thanked Lily and her friends for giving him a chance. They all became good friends and played sport every day. The moral of the story is: Be kind and share when you play sport, and you will have more fun and make more friends.
