Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Fair Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a fair cat named Tom. Tom was a nice cat who liked to play with his friends. One day, Tom saw a ball in a drain. He wanted to get the ball out to play with it. Tom set a plan to get the ball out of the drain. He got a long stick and tried to reach the ball. He pushed and pushed, but the ball did not move. Tom was sad, but he did not give up. Then, Tom asked his friend, a big dog named Max, to help. Max used his strong paws to lift the drain cover. Tom got the ball out and was very happy. Tom and Max played with the ball all day, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. They decided to play together in the east. But when they were done, one of the friends had disappeared. The other friend was very confused. She looked and looked, but she couldn't find her friend anywhere. She was sad and sorry. Then a little bird flew over and told her that her friend had gone east again. She was so happy to hear this. She ran east as fast as she could. Finally she found her friend, and they hugged each other tight. They never disappeared again after that.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found an unusual balloon. It was big and shiny. Lucy was very happy. She wanted to show her friend, Tim. She ran to Tim's house with the balloon. "Look, Tim!" said Lucy. "I found a big balloon. It is not like other balloons. It is unusual." Tim looked at the balloon and smiled. "Wow, Lucy! It is so pretty!" he said. Lucy and Tim played with the unusual balloon all day. They did not want to lose it. So, they wrap the balloon with a string. They learned that when you find something special, you should take care of it. And when you share it with a friend, it becomes even more special.

Once there was a small boy named Tim. Tim had a cute dog named Spot. They loved to play together. One day, Spot got sick. Tim didn't know what to do. He felt ignorant. Tim's mom said, "Let's take Spot to the veterinarian." The veterinarian is a doctor for animals. Tim and his mom took Spot to the veterinarian. They wanted Spot to get better. The veterinarian looked at Spot. She said, "Spot will be okay. He just needs some rest." Tim was happy. He knew that the veterinarian would help Spot. Now, Tim felt like he could own his problem and take care of his dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big box. She wanted to mail the box to her friend, Tim. But, the way to the mail place was scary. She had to go through a network of dark, twisty paths. One day, Amy was brave. She took the big box and started to walk. She went into the dark, twisty paths. She was scared, but she kept going. She knew that Tim would be happy to get the box. As Amy walked, she saw a big, scary shadow. She was very scared. Then, the shadow came out. It was just a big, friendly dog! The dog helped Amy find her way to the mail place. They mailed the big box together. Tim was very happy when he got the box, and Amy was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his red ball. One day, while playing with his ball, it took a big turn and rolled far away. Tim was very sorry that he lost his ball. Tim went to look for his ball. He saw many balls, but he could not recognize which one was his. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. He asked his friends to help him find his red ball. Together, they looked and looked. At last, they found a red ball. Tim recognized it was his ball! He was so happy and thanked his friends for their help. They all played together with the red ball and had lots of fun. Tim was careful not to let his ball turn far away again.
