Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bunny

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a new toy, a cute bunny. One day, Amy went to bed and the bunny went to work. The bunny's job was to make sure Amy would wake up with a big smile. In the morning, the bunny woke up before Amy. He thought of a fun way to wake her up. He jumped on her bed and said, "Wake up, Amy! It's a new day to play!" Amy opened her eyes and saw her bunny. She smiled and said, "Good morning, bunny! Let's play!" Amy and her bunny played all day. They had so much fun. When it was time for bed, Amy said, "Thank you, bunny, for making me wake up happy." The bunny smiled and said, "You're welcome, Amy. I love to work and make you happy." And they both went to sleep, ready for a new day of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a shiny metal toy. The toy could walk and talk all by itself. Tim thought the toy was very special because it was independent. Tim and the toy played all day, and they had a lot of fun. Tim had a question for the toy. He asked, "Where did you come from, toy?" The toy did not know the answer. So, they went to ask Tim's mom. She looked at the toy, and she was very surprised. "Tim," she said, "this is not a toy. This is a magic robot!" Tim was so excited. The magic robot could do many things. It could fly, it could make things appear, and it could even make Tim's room clean. Tim and the magic robot became best friends, and they had many adventures together.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful forest, there lived a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo had a best friend, a tiny bird named Titi. They loved to play together all day long. One day, while they were playing, Bobo noticed that Titi had a hurt lip. "Oh no, Titi! Your lip is hurt!" said Bobo. Titi looked sad and said, "Yes, I fell from my nest and hurt my lip." Bobo wanted to help Titi feel better. They went to find some leaves to put on Titi's lip. After they put the leaves on, Titi smiled and said, "Thank you, Bobo. My lip feels better now." They continued to play and have fun in the peaceful forest.

Once upon a time, there was a kangaroo named Kip. Kip was very popular in his town. All the little animals wanted to be friends with him. Kip loved to hop and play with his friends. One day, Kip found a big, red apple. He wanted to taste it, but his friend, a small bird named Blue, also wanted the apple. Kip and Blue did not know what to do. They both wanted to taste the sweet apple, but there was only one. Kip and Blue thought for a moment. They decided to share the apple. Kip took a big bite and then gave it to Blue. Blue took a small bite too. They both tasted the sweet apple and were happy. They learned that sharing is good and it made their friendship even stronger.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a healthy boy who loved to play outside. One day, Tim saw a big chair in his room. He thought it would be fun to play with the chair. Tim's mom said, "Tim, be careful with the chair. You need to tie your shoes first." Tim listened to his mom and tied his shoes. Then, he started to play with the chair. He pushed it, climbed on it, and even tried to spin it around. Suddenly, the chair started to talk! "Thank you for playing with me, Tim," said the chair. Tim was very surprised. He didn't know that chairs could talk. The chair told Tim that it was a magic chair and because Tim had tied his shoes, the chair could now talk and play with him. From that day on, Tim and the magic chair had lots of fun together.
