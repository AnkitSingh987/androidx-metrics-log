Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Hill

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big hill. It was green and had flowers. They wanted to go up the hill and see what was on the other side. They asked their moms if they could go. Their moms said yes, but they had to be careful and come back soon. Lily and Tom were happy. They took their bikes and rode to the hill. They left their bikes at the bottom and started to climb. The hill was steep and hard. Lily and Tom had to use their hands and feet. They got tired and dirty. But they did not give up. They wanted to see the other side. They thought it would be perfect. They reached the top of the hill. They looked around. They saw a big road with many cars. They saw a lot of smoke and noise. They saw no flowers or trees. They saw no animals or birds. They saw nothing they liked. They were sad and angry. They had climbed the hill for nothing. They had wasted their time and energy. They had missed their snack and nap. They had no fun. They sighed. They wished they had stayed at home. They turned around and went down the hill. They got their bikes and rode back. They did not talk to each other. They did not look at each other. They were not friends anymore.

Lily was lonely. She had no friends to play with on the holiday. Her mom and dad were busy with work. She wished she had a pet to keep her company. One day, she saw a little bird on the window. It was hurt and could not fly. Lily felt sorry for the bird. She opened the window and took the bird inside. She gave it some water and seeds. She made a nest for it with a box and some cotton. "Hello, bird," Lily said. "I will take care of you. I will raise you until you can fly again. Do you want to be my friend?" The bird looked at Lily with its bright eyes. It chirped softly. It seemed to say yes. Lily was happy. She had a friend to play with on the holiday. She named the bird Blue. She sang to it and read it stories. She taught it some words. Blue learned to say Lily and hello. Lily and Blue had fun together. They were not lonely anymore. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big soft bear named Ben. Ben loved to rock in his chair all day. One day, Ben found a suit. The suit was very pretty and had many colors. Ben put on the suit and said, "Look at me! I am so fancy!" His friend, Tom the cat, came to see Ben. Tom saw Ben in the suit and said, "Wow, Ben! You look great in your new suit!" Ben and Tom played all day. They rocked in the chair together and laughed. They were very happy. At the end of the day, Ben took off his suit and put it away. He knew he would wear it again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a poor girl. She was always kind and caring to everyone around her, but no one seemed to care much in return. One day, she got into a fight with a friend over a magazine. She tried to stay calm and explain her side, but her friend kept shouting. The poor girl began to feel sad. Her mother saw that she was feeling low, so she took her daughter aside and said, "My sweet child, why did you get into a fight? Fighting won't help solve a problem but talking kindly can." The poor girl thought about her mother's wise words. She decided that next time, she wouldn't fight but rather talk kindly and explain herself. The poor girl went back to her friend and apologized for her part in the fight and explained her feelings. Her friend apologized too and the two of resolved the argument without a fight. The moral of the story is that it is important to talk kindly and not fight when we have disagreements.
