Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Lucky Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a lucky little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big pond with his family. They swam and played all day long. Fin was a happy fish, but sometimes he wanted to do things all by himself. One day, Fin saw a big, shiny thing in the water. He wanted to see it up close. Fin swam and swam, but he could not manage to get to the shiny thing. His mom saw him trying and swam over to help. Fin felt sad because he could not do it alone. Fin's mom told him not to be sad. "You are still a little fish, Fin. It's okay to ask for help," she said. Fin opened his mouth and smiled. He knew his mom was right. From that day on, Fin learned that it was okay to ask for help when he could not manage something alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to draw and design pretty pictures. One day, she decided to make a special picture for her friend, Tom. Lucy worked hard on her picture. She drew a big, heavy sun with a big smile. She drew trees with lots of leaves. She also drew a message that said, "You are my best friend." When Lucy finished her picture, she showed it to Tom. Tom was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lucy! I love your heavy sun and your message!" They both smiled and continued to play together, knowing they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little pig named Pippin. Pippin was a quiet pig who lived on a farm with his friends. He liked to play and run in the big green field. One sunny day, Pippin met a new friend named Daisy. Daisy was a cute, small dog. Pippin said, "Hi, I am Pippin. Do you want to play with me?" Daisy wagged her tail and said, "Yes, I want to play!" Pippin and Daisy played all day. They ran fast and slow, played hide and seek, and rolled in the grass. They were very happy. When the sun went down, Pippin and Daisy said goodbye and went home. They knew they would play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play with his friends. They were always competitive, trying to see who could run the fastest. One day, Tim fell and felt a lot of pain in his leg. He cried and his mom came to help him. "Mom, it hurts so bad!" Tim said. His mom looked at his leg and saw a big red spot. She knew what to do. She took Tim inside the house and put some ice on his leg. She told him, "This will make the pain go away." The next day, Tim's friends came over to play. They wanted to record their race to see who was the fastest. Tim wanted to join, but his leg still hurt. His mom told him to rest and watch his friends race. Tim was sad, but he knew it was best for his leg to heal. He cheered for his friends and recorded their race on his toy camera. Soon, Tim's leg felt better, and he could run and play with his friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to write. He wrote about a big rocket. The rocket was red and blue. Tim's rocket went up, up, up in the sky. The rocket was very fast. Tim was happy. One day, the rocket was angry. The rocket did not want to go up, up, up. It wanted to stay on the ground. Tim was sad. He did not know why the rocket was angry. Tim wanted to help the rocket. Then, something unexpected happened. The rocket started to talk! The rocket said, "I am not angry, Tim. I am just tired." Tim was surprised. He did not know rockets could talk. Tim said, "It is okay, rocket. We can rest." So, Tim and the rocket sat down and took a nap. When they woke up, the rocket was happy again. They went up, up, up in the sky together.
