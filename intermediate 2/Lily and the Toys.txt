Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Toys

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily lived in a beautiful house. She had a big room with lots of toys. One day, she realized her favorite teddy bear was missing. She looked all around her room, but she could not find it. Lily felt sad. She asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my teddy bear?" Her mom said, "No, but let's look for it together." They looked in every room, but they still could not find the teddy bear. Finally, Lily's dad came home. He said, "I found this outside. Is it yours?" He showed Lily her favorite teddy bear. Lily was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Dad!" From that day on, Lily always made sure to keep her teddy bear safe in her beautiful room.

Once upon a time, in a land far to the east, there was a lucky cat named Whiskers. Whiskers was a happy cat who loved to help others. One day, Whiskers met a little boy named Timmy. Timmy was sad because he lost his toy. "Please help me find my toy," Timmy requested. Whiskers wanted to help, so they started to look for the toy together. They looked in the park, under the big tree, and even in Timmy's house. But they could not find the toy anywhere. Just when they were about to give up, a bird flew by with Timmy's toy in its beak! Whiskers jumped high in the air and caught the toy. "Thank you, Whiskers!" Timmy said, happy to have his toy back. Whiskers felt lucky to have made a new friend, and they played together all day long.

Lily and her mom went to the grocery store to buy some food. Lily liked to help her mom put the food in the cart. She saw some apples, bananas, carrots, and cheese. She also saw some cookies, but her mom said they were not good for her. When they were done, they went to the cashier to pay. The cashier was an elderly lady with gray hair and glasses. She smiled at Lily and said, "Hello, little one. What is your name?" "Lily," Lily said shyly. "That's a pretty name. Do you like to help your mom with the groceries?" the cashier asked. "Yes, I do. I like to fix things too," Lily said proudly. "Really? What do you like to fix?" the cashier asked. Lily thought for a moment. She remembered how she helped her dad fix his bike last week. She also remembered how she tried to fix her doll's dress with some tape, but it didn't work very well. "I like to fix bikes and dolls and puzzles and books," Lily said. "Wow, you are very smart and creative. You can be anything you want when you grow up," the cashier said. "Thank you. I want to be a fixer like you," Lily said. "A fixer like me? What do you mean?" the cashier asked. Lily pointed at the cashier's name tag. It said, "Rita, Customer Service." "You fix people's problems when they buy things. You are a good fixer," Lily said. The cashier laughed and hugged Lily. She said, "Thank you, Lily. You made my day. You are a good helper and a good friend." Lily smiled and hugged her back. She said, "You're welcome, Rita. You are a good fixer and a good friend too."

Mummy and Daddy were playing with baby Jack. They were throwing a yellow ball. Jack was so clever, he could catch it every time. Mummy said "Let's play a sport" and Daddy said "Yes, let's do it". Jack asked "What sport?". Mummy said "Let's play hide and seek". Daddy said "Yes, let's hide together". Jack was very excited and asked "Who will find me?". Daddy said "I will find you, Jack, I'm very clever". Jack was very happy and started counting. When Jack finished counting, he shouted "Ready or not, here I come!" and ran to look for his parents. Daddy and Mummy were laughing and Jack was very happy.

Tom and Lily liked to play with their toy ship. They put it in the tub and made it sail. They made noises like the wind and the waves. They had fun. One day, Mom said they had to go to the store. She said they could not play with the ship. She said they had to be good and quiet. Tom and Lily did not want to go. They wanted to play with the ship. They sighed. They got in the car and went to the store. It was big and loud. There were many people and things. Tom and Lily did not like it. They wanted to go back home. They sighed. Mom bought some food and clothes. She said they were almost done. She said they could have a treat if they were good. Tom and Lily tried to be good. They wanted a treat. They sighed. They went to the candy aisle. There were many colors and shapes. Tom and Lily looked at them. They saw something new. It was a candy ship. It was blue and red and yellow. It looked like their toy ship. Tom and Lily smiled. Mom saw them. She said they could have the candy ship. She said it was a special treat. She said they were good and quiet. Tom and Lily were happy. They thanked Mom. They did not sigh. They went back home. They opened the candy ship. They ate some of it. It was sweet and crunchy. They liked it. They saved some of it. They put it next to their toy ship. They played with both ships. They had fun. They thought it was a normal day. But it was a special day. They had a candy ship. They did not sigh. They smiled.
