Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Bear

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a lot of toys, but her favorite was a white teddy bear. She always hugged him and took him everywhere. One day, she went to the park with her mom and her teddy bear. She saw a big slide and wanted to try it. She climbed up the stairs and sat on the slide. She held her teddy bear tight and slid down. It was fun and fast. She laughed and wanted to do it again. But when she looked at her teddy bear, she saw that he had a big stain on his fur. It was brown and sticky. She was sad and angry. She had dropped her teddy bear on some ice cream that someone had left on the ground. She ran to her mom and showed her the stain. She cried and said that her teddy bear was ruined. Her mom hugged her and said that it was okay. She said that they could go home and wash the teddy bear. She said that the stain would come off and the teddy bear would be white again. She said that Lily should not be so upset and that she should relax. Lily listened to her mom and calmed down. She realized that her mom was right. She said that she was sorry for crying and that she loved her mom. Her mom smiled and said that she loved her too. They went home and washed the teddy bear. The stain came off and the teddy bear was white again. Lily was happy and thanked her mom. She learned that things can be fixed and that she should not worry too much. She learned to relax and enjoy her toys.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a watch that he loved. He wore it every day. One day, Tim saw a big frog near a pond. The frog had big eyes that would blink. Tim went up to the frog and said, "Hi, frog! I am Tim. I like your big eyes that blink." The frog blinked and said, "Hi, Tim! I like your watch. It is nice." They became friends and played by the pond. Every day, Tim would come to the pond to play with the frog. They would watch the fish swim and the birds fly. And the frog would always blink its big eyes at Tim. They were happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a white flower named Lily. She lived in a big garden with many other flowers. Lily loved to bloom and show her pretty petals to everyone. One day, a little girl named Amy came to the garden. She saw Lily and said, "Wow, you are so pretty! I love your white petals." Lily felt very happy and proud. She bloomed even more for Amy. But soon, the sun became very hot. The flowers needed a shield to protect them from the sun. So, Amy found a big leaf and put it over Lily and her friends. The leaf was like a shield, and it kept them safe and cool. Now, Lily and her friends could bloom happily without getting hurt by the hot sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cars. One day, Tim saw a big, sour lemon in the kitchen. He had an idea for a fun game. Tim asked his mom if he could borrow the lemon for his game. His mom said yes, but only if he was careful. Tim was so excited. He took the lemon and went to his room. He called his game "Lemon Operation." In the game of Lemon Operation, Tim and his toy cars had to work together to move the sour lemon from one side of the room to the other. The cars pushed and pulled the lemon, and they all had a lot of fun. Tim was so happy that he borrowed the lemon for his game. And when they were done playing, he put the lemon back in the kitchen, just like his mom asked.
