Category: Intermediate
Title: Poppy the Purple Poppy and the Garden

One day, a purple poppy named Poppy was in the garden. She liked to joke with her friends. Poppy was happy and fun. A little girl named Lily came to the garden. She saw Poppy and said, "Hi, Poppy! You are so purple and pretty!" Poppy said, "Thank you, Lily! Do you want to hear a joke?" Lily said, "Yes, please!" Poppy told a funny joke, and they both laughed. Then, a big wind came and blew Poppy away. Lily was sad. But, she saw another purple poppy in the garden. She said, "Hi, new Poppy! Do you like jokes too?" The new Poppy said, "Yes, I do!" And they laughed together again. The moral of the story is that even when we lose something, we can still find happiness in new things.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. The tall tree had many friends. One day, a little bird came to the tree and said, "I want to give a surprise to my friend. Can you help me?" The tall tree said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's start by finding a good spot to hide your surprise." They looked around and found a small hole in the tree. The little bird put the surprise inside the hole. As the sun went down, the little bird's friend came to the park. The little bird said, "I have a surprise for you! Look at the tall tree!" The friend looked at the tree and saw the small hole. When the friend found the surprise, they both laughed and played together. The tall tree was happy to help and smiled as the friends enjoyed their surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a big house with a tall chimney. In the house lived a boy named Tim and his mom. Tim liked to play with his toy cars and look at the chimney outside his window. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, we need to test the chimney. It is getting cold, and we need to see if it works well." Tim was excited and wanted to help his mom. They went outside and looked at the chimney. Tim's mom said, "Look, Tim! We have a rare bird living in our chimney!" Tim saw the bird and smiled. The bird was small and had pretty colors. They decided to let the bird stay in the chimney and keep it warm. Tim's mom said, "We can find another way to stay warm, but the bird needs the chimney more than we do." Tim agreed, and they went inside to find warm blankets.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to play outside. The sky was gray, and it looked like it might rain. Lily did not care; she wanted to play with her ball. Lily kicked the ball, but it went into a big mud puddle. "Oh no!" she said. She tried to get the ball out, but it was difficult. The mud was deep, and Lily started to struggle. She called for her mom, "Mom, help! I can't get my ball!" Her mom came outside and saw Lily in the mud. "I told you it might rain, and now it's hard to get your ball," her mom said. She helped Lily pull the ball out of the mud. Just then, the rain started to fall. They both laughed and went inside to clean up. The next day, the sun was out, and the mud was gone. Lily remembered how difficult it was to get her ball when it was muddy, so she played in the grass instead. She kicked her ball and had lots of fun, without any struggle.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go on adventures. One day, Tim found an empty box in his room. He was very curious about what could be inside the box. He opened it and found a map! Tim decided to follow the map to find treasure. He walked and walked until he found a big tree. The map said that the treasure was under the tree. Tim kneeled down and started to dig with his hands. He dug and dug until he found a small box. Tim was so excited! He opened the small box and found shiny gold coins inside. He was very happy to have found the treasure. Tim took the coins home and showed them to his mom and dad. They were very proud of him. And from that day on, Tim always remembered his big adventure.
