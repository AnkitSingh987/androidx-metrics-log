Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She saw a pretty angel sitting on her bed. The angel had big wings and a kind face. Lily was happy to see the angel and wanted to be friends. "Hi, angel," Lily whispered. "Do you want to play with me?" The angel smiled and nodded. They played with toys and had fun. But then, Lily's brother Tom came in. Tom was ignorant and did not see the angel. He started to take Lily's toys. "No, Tom! Those are mine!" Lily said. The angel whispered to Lily, "Share with your brother, and he will be kind too." So, Lily shared her toys with Tom, and they all played together. The angel was happy, and Lily and Tom learned to share and be nice to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to play the guitar. She was very good at it and the music made her very happy. One day, she asked her mom to buy her a new guitar, please. So her mom took her to the shop and the little girl picked out a beautiful guitar. The next day, the little girl decided to play her new guitar in her backyard. But suddenly, her dog came running by and was very excited. The little girl was so startled that she dropped the guitar and started to cry. When she picked up the guitar, she noticed something was wrong. She couldn't hear the music because the guitar was deaf! The little girl was so sad, she never got to play her guitar again.

Once upon a time, there was a happy robot. The robot could move and play with kids. The robot had many friends. They all liked to play ball and run. One day, the robot was playing with his friends when something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took the robot's ball. The robot and his friends were sad. They wanted their ball back. The robot had an idea. He asked the bird, "Can we play together?" The big bird was happy and said yes. So the robot, the big bird, and all the friends played together. They had lots of fun, and the robot was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a wise mail bird named Blue. Blue loved to speed through the sky to deliver mail to all his friends. Every day, he would fly very fast with a big bag of mail on his back. One day, as Blue was speeding through the sky, he met a new friend named Red. Red was a little bird who wanted to learn how to be fast and wise like Blue. Blue said, "I can help you be fast and wise, Red. Just follow me and I will show you how." Red followed Blue as they sped through the sky. They delivered mail to all their friends who were very happy to see them. Blue showed Red how to be fast and wise, just like him. They became the best of friends, speeding through the sky and delivering mail together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with rough bark. Tim liked to touch the bark because it felt bumpy. While Tim was playing, a stranger came to the park. The stranger had a big, fluffy dog. "Hi, I'm Sam," said the stranger. "Do you want to play with my dog?" Tim loved dogs, but he knew he should not talk to strangers. So, Tim ran to his mom and told her about Sam and the dog. "Good job, Tim," said his mom. "We should not talk to strangers. But I will go with you to meet Sam and his dog." Tim's mom met Sam and they all played together. Tim was happy and did not quit playing until it was time to go home.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the park. She saw a big orange ball by the tree. Mia picked up the ball and started to play with it. Suddenly, Mia heard a noise behind her. It was a small bird. The bird said, "Hello, can you help me find my nest?" Mia looked at the bird and said, "Yes, I will help you." So, they started to examine the park to find the bird's nest. They looked high and low, under the bushes and in the trees. At last, they found the bird's nest in a tall tree. The bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mia!" Mia smiled and said, "You're welcome!" And they played with the orange ball together until it was time for Mia to go home.
