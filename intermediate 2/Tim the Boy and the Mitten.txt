Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mitten

Once upon a time, in a cute little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the snow. One day, while playing, he found a mitten on the ground. "Whose mitten is this?" Tim asked his friend, Sam. Sam looked at the mitten and said, "I don't know, but it's very cute!" Tim and Sam decided to play a game to find the owner of the mitten. They asked all their friends if the mitten belonged to them. But no one knew who the mitten belonged to. Finally, they met a little girl named Lily who was looking for her lost mitten. Tim showed her the mitten and asked, "Is this your mitten, Lily?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes! Thank you for finding it!" Tim, Sam, and Lily played together in the snow all day, happy to have made a new friend. And Lily was very happy to have her cute mitten back.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her dolls. One day, Lily found a box in her room. The box was from an unknown place. She opened the box and found a long thread inside. Lily remembered her mom told her not to play with unknown things. But Lily was curious about the thread. She took the thread and started to play with it. She tied the thread around her dolls and made them dance. But the thread was not a good thread. It started to move on its own and tied Lily's hands together. She could not move her hands and she was scared. Lily cried for help, but no one could hear her. The thread kept tying her more and more. In the end, Lily was all tied up and could not play with her dolls anymore.

One day, a big dog named Sam was ready to play. He went to the park with his friend, a small cat named Lily. They liked to play with a ball, but today, they found a new toy. It was a massage tool. They did not know what it was, but they wanted to play with it. Sam and Lily started to fight over the new toy. They both wanted it so much. They yelled at each other, "It's mine!" and "No, it's mine!" They pulled and pulled, but the toy did not break. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the toy away. It flew high in the sky. Sam and Lily were very sad. They lost their new toy and their ball was gone too. They learned that fighting over things is not good. It is better to share and be kind to each other. Now, they had no toys to play with and their day was bad. They wished they had shared the toy instead of yelling and fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a small fish named Splash. He lived in a pond with his friends. One day, a big bird came to the pond. Splash and his friends were scared. They did not like the big bird. The big bird wanted to eat the fish. Splash had an idea. He swam fast and jumped out of the water. He jumped one inch high. The big bird was surprised. It did not see a fish jump before. Splash jumped again, and the big bird moved back. The bird was not hungry anymore. It flew away. Splash and his friends were happy. The pond was mild and calm again. They played and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big pink rug in a little girl's room. The little girl loved to play on her rug. Her name was Lily. One day, Lily's friend, Tom, came over to play. They decided to act like animals on the rug. Lily acted like a cat, and Tom acted like a dog. They had so much fun. "Meow!" said Lily. "Woof!" said Tom. They laughed and played on the pink rug all day long. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat who loved to have a shiny coat. He spent all of his time polishing it with a special brush. The other animals in the woods started to ignore the silly cat, because they thought he was too vain and focused on his appearance. One day, the bear said to the cat, "Why don't you play outside with us? Polishing all the time is not a good style!" The silly cat was confused and just kept on polishing his fur. The other animals decided to show the cat how to play and have fun. Suddenly, the cat stopped polishing and started to enjoy the outdoors. The moral of the story is that you should never forget to have fun and be silly! Even though looking nice is important, it's ok to take a break from time to time.
