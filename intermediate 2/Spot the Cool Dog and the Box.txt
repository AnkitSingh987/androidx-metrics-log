Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Cool Dog and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a cool dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with his friend, Sam. They liked to play and have fun together. Spot was a very happy dog. One day, Spot found a big box. He wanted to fit inside the box, but it was too small. Spot tried and tried, but he just couldn't fit. Sam saw Spot and wanted to help. Sam found a bigger box for Spot. Spot tried to fit in the new box, and it was just right! Spot was so happy, and he wagged his tail. The moral of the story is that sometimes, things don't fit right away, but with a little help from friends, we can find the perfect fit. And when we find the right fit, we can be happy and play together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a deaf boy named Tim. Tim had a best friend named Sam. They loved to play together. Tim could not hear, but he used symbols to talk to Sam. One day, Tim requested a toy car from Sam. He showed Sam the symbol for "car" and pointed at the toy. Sam understood and said, "Oh, you want the car? Here you go!" Sam gave the toy car to Tim, and they played all day. As they played, they laughed and had fun. They did not need many words to be friends. They understood each other with their hearts. And that made them the best friends ever.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with cars and trucks. One day, they find a big box in the garage. Inside the box, there are many shiny things. Lily sees a shiny thing that looks like a star. She says, "Look, Tom, a diamond!" Tom says, "Wow, it is so pretty! Can I hold it?" Lily says, "Yes, but be careful. It is cold and hard." Tom holds the diamond and feels how cold and hard it is. He says, "It is like ice. I wonder where it came from." Lily says, "Maybe it came from a faraway place. Maybe someone drove a long way to bring it here. Maybe they drove through snow and ice." Tom says, "That sounds fun. I wish we could drive through snow and ice." Lily says, "Me too. Let's pretend we are driving through snow and ice." They put the diamond in a small car and push it around the garage. They make noises like a car engine and a horn. They pretend they see snowflakes and icicles. They have a lot of fun driving with the diamond. They forget about the other shiny things in the box. They only care about the diamond and their car. They are happy.

Once upon a time there was a lazy couch. It sat in the corner of the room all day and never moved. All the other furniture in the room danced and spun around, but not the lazy couch. One day, the other furniture decided to have some fun. They asked the lazy couch to join them and dance. The couch said, "No way, I'm too lazy for that!" But the other furniture wouldn't take no for an answer. They pulled and prodded until the lazy couch finally agreed to join. The couch said, "OK, but only if you promise to go slow and let me do the reverse!" All the furniture agreed and started to move slowly in a circle, with the lazy couch leading! Around and around they went, until finally they finished and every furniture was moving in the opposite direction! The lazy couch was very proud of itself that it had led the others. The End.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. They saw a big structure. It was a play area with slides and swings. Tim was very excited to play there. The sun was shining, and the ground was dry. Tim looked at the structure and said, "Daddy, I want to choose where to play first!" His dad smiled and said, "Okay, Tim. You choose what you want to play on." Tim thought for a moment and then said, "I want to play on the slide first!" Tim and his dad went to the slide. Tim climbed up the stairs and then slid down the slide. He laughed and said, "Daddy, that was fun! Now I want to choose the swings!" His dad pushed him on the swing, and they had a great time. After playing on the swings, Tim and his dad took a break. They sat on a bench and had a snack. Tim looked at the structure and said, "Daddy, I had so much fun today. Thank you for letting me choose what to play on." His dad hugged him and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad you had fun." They stayed at the park for a little while longer, and then it was time to go home. Tim waved goodbye to the structure and said, "Bye, big play area! I'll come back soon!" As they walked home, Tim couldn't stop talking about all the fun he had at the park.
