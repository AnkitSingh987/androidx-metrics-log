Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Room

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a very important girl because she loved to help her mommy and daddy. One day, Sue saw that her room was not clean. She decided to clean it all by herself. As Sue was cleaning her room, she found a sock under her bed. "Oh no!" she said. "My toe is cold!" So, Sue put the sock on her toe to keep it warm. She felt so happy with her warm toe. After Sue cleaned her room, her mommy and daddy were so proud of her. They gave her a big hug and said, "Thank you for being such an important helper, Sue!" Sue smiled and knew that she did a good job.

Once upon a time, there was an object. It was an old, broken object. It was in the corner of the room and nobody used it anymore. One day, a little girl came into the room. Her name was Chloe. She had a big smile on her face as she saw the object. She looked around and noticed that there were also a lot of fluffly toys in the room. Chloe wanted to mix them all together. She grabbed the old object, picked up a toy and started mixing them. The parents came in to see Chloe mixing them. "Chloe, what are you doing?" asked Mom. "I am mixing the object and the toys," said Chloe. "That looks harsh, sweetheart," said Mom. Chloe stopped and droppped the object. She hugged Mom's leg and said, "I'm sorry. I won't do it again." Mom hugged her daughter and said, "Let's mix the toys in a softer way! It's much more fun that way!" Chloe smiled and started mixing the toys in a gentle way. Soon, the room was filled with giggles and laughter as the two mixed their toys together. The End.

One day, a bunny named Bob went out to play. Bob loved to hop all day long. He hopped to the park and saw a big tent. He thought, "What a fun place to hop!" Inside the tent, there was a party with loud music. Bob hopped and danced with his new friends. They laughed and played games. But then, something unexpected happened. The loud music made the tent shake! Suddenly, the tent lifted into the sky! Bob and his friends were scared. But they kept hopping and dancing. As they danced, the tent went higher and higher. Then, with one big hop, they all landed safely back in the park. Everyone cheered and clapped. The hopping and dancing had saved them! They had a big hug, and Bob went home to tell his family the story of his amazing adventure.

One day, a little dove felt different. He saw his friends fly high and fast. The little dove wanted to fly like them, but he was scared. His friend, a big bird, saw him and said, "Why are you sad?" The little dove said, "I feel different. I want to fly high and fast like my friends, but I'm scared." The big bird smiled and said, "I will help you. I will encourage you to fly." The next day, the big bird and the little dove went to a tall tree. The big bird said, "You can do it! I believe in you!" The little dove felt brave. He jumped from the tree and started to fly. He flew higher and faster than ever before. The little dove was so happy and thanked the big bird for encouraging him. Now, the little dove knew that being different was okay.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Emma. Every night, she went to bed to get a good night's sleep in order to wake up feeling refreshed in the morning. But this night was different - Emma woke up to a bright light shining in her room. She didn't know what it was, so she stepped out of bed to go and look. When she got to the source of the light, she found a heavy laser! It was so shiny and exciting that Emma wanted to take it with her. But just as she was about to pick it up, she heard a voice. The voice said "No Emma, if you take this laser it will bring bad luck. So put it back and get some rest." So Emma put the laser back and went back to bed. She learned that night that some things are better left alone - even if they look very exciting.
