Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Cake

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog. He jogged to the park and saw his friend, Sam. Sam was holding a plate with cake on it. Tim felt happy because he liked cake. Tim asked Sam, "Can I have some cake?" Sam said, "Yes, but don't be rude. Share with our friends too." Tim took a small piece and said thank you. They knew more friends would come to the park soon. Later, more friends came to play. They all saw the cake on the plate. Tim told them, "Sam said we can share the cake, but don't be rude." They all took a small piece and said thank you. Tim and Sam smiled, happy that everyone could enjoy the cake together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a sweet, soft cat named Fluffy. Mia and Fluffy loved to play together. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Mia and Fluffy went to the park. They saw a big slide with a seat at the top. Mia wanted to go down the slide. She climbed up and sat on the seat. But the seat was wet! Mia did not want to get her dress wet. Mia had an idea. She took a towel from her bag and used it to wipe the seat. The seat was now dry and clean. Mia and Fluffy went down the slide together, laughing and having fun. They played at the park until the sun went down. Then, they went home, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to climb trees. One day, he saw a tall tree and thought it would be fun to climb. But this tree was very dangerous. As Tim started to climb, he heard a voice. It was a squirrel who lived in the tree. The squirrel said, "Tim, don't climb this tree. It's dangerous, and you might fall!" Tim didn't listen and kept climbing. Suddenly, Tim slipped and started to fall. The squirrel quickly ran down the tree to help. The squirrel told Tim, "I will fix your hurt leg, but you must promise not to climb dangerous trees again." Tim agreed and thanked the squirrel. The moral of the story is to listen to others when they warn you about danger.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the sea and play with his friends. One day, while swimming, Fin found a big oyster on the sea floor. Fin was curious about the oyster, so he called his friend, Star, the starfish. "Star, come look at this oyster," said Fin. Star swam over and looked at the oyster with Fin. They both wondered what was inside the oyster. Fin and Star decided to open the oyster together. They both took a deep breath and started to pull. They pulled and pulled, and finally, the oyster opened. Inside, they found a beautiful pearl. Fin and Star were so happy and proud of their teamwork. From that day on, they knew that they could do anything if they worked together and were brave.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy little cat named Luna. Luna loved to study the big, bright moon at night. She would sit by the window and look up at the sky. One night, the moon was very big and very bright. Luna felt so happy. She wanted to play with the moon. Luna tried to jump up and touch the moon, but it was too high. Luna was sad, but she did not give up. She studied the moon every night. She learned that the moon changed shape and size. Luna was happy to watch the moon and learn about it. And she was not sleepy anymore.

One day, a jolly cat named Tom went for a walk. Tom was very good at balance. He liked to walk on walls and fences. He saw a big, yummy steak on a table in a house. Tom wanted the steak, but the house had a big dog named Max. Tom had an idea. He would balance on the fence and get the steak. He walked very slow and careful. Max saw Tom and barked very loud. Tom was scared, but he kept going. He was close to the steak. Then, something unexpected happened. Max jumped on the table and ate the steak very fast! Tom was sad, but he didn't give up. He saw another house with a big cake on the table. Tom smiled and went to the next house, ready to balance again.
