Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Monster and the Doctor

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly monster. The monster had a big nose. One day, the monster had to sneeze. "Achoo!" he sneezed. The sneeze was very loud. The monster felt sick. He went to see the doctor. The doctor was a nice man. He looked at the monster's nose. The doctor said, "You have a cold." The doctor gave the monster some medicine. The monster took the medicine. Soon, the monster felt better. He did not sneeze anymore. The monster was happy and said, "Thank you, doctor!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box that was empty. He wanted to fill it with toys, so he asked his mom for help. One day, Tim's mom said, "I will mail a letter to Grandma. Maybe she can send you some toys." Tim was very happy. He helped his mom write the letter and put it in the mailbox. A few days later, a big box came in the mail. Tim was so excited! He opened the box and found many toys inside. But there were two toy trucks that looked the same. Tim's brother, Sam, saw the trucks and wanted one too. They started to fight over the trucks. Tim's mom heard the fight and came to help. She said, "You both can play with the trucks. There is no need to fight." Tim and Sam listened to their mom and stopped fighting. They played with the trucks and filled the empty toy box with all the new toys. They were happy and never fought again.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a charming little girl named Lily. She had a big smile and she loved to make new friends. One day, she saw a new girl in her village. She wanted to say hello and introduce herself. Lily walked up to the new girl and said, "Hi, I'm Lily. What's your name?" The new girl smiled and said, "I'm Rose. Nice to meet you, Lily!" Lily saw a pretty bracelet on Rose's arm. It had many colors and it made a soft sound when Rose moved her arm. Lily said, "I like your bracelet, it's very pretty." Rose smiled and said, "Thank you, my mom gave it to me. Do you want to be friends?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, I would love to be friends!" From that day on, Lily and Rose became best friends and they played together every day.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big and impressive rainbow in the sky. It had many colors and it was very bright. "Wow, look at the rainbow!" Tom said. "It is so pretty!" "I want to touch it!" Lily said. "Maybe it has magic!" They ran towards the rainbow, hoping to reach it. But the rainbow was very far away. They had to step over rocks and puddles and grass. They got tired and wet and dirty. "Are we there yet?" Tom asked. "I can't see the rainbow anymore!" "Me neither!" Lily said. "Maybe it is hiding!" They stopped and looked around. They were lost. They did not know where their home was. They felt scared and sad. "Mommy! Daddy!" they cried. "Where are you?" They heard a voice. It was their dog, Spot. He had followed them from their home. He barked and wagged his tail. He was happy to see them. "Spot!" Tom and Lily said. "You found us!" They hugged their dog and followed him back to their home. Their parents were waiting for them. They were worried and angry. "Where have you been?" Mommy asked. "We were looking for you!" "We were chasing the rainbow!" Tom said. "We wanted to touch it!" Lily said. Mommy and Daddy shook their heads. They explained to Tom and Lily that rainbows are not things you can touch. They are made by light and water in the sky. They are beautiful, but they are not magic. They also told them that they should never run away from home without telling them. They said that it was dangerous and foolish. They said that they loved them and wanted them to be safe. Tom and Lily felt sorry. They said they were sorry. They hugged their parents and thanked Spot for saving them. They learned their lesson. They never chased rainbows again. They still liked to look at them, but they knew they were better off at home.
