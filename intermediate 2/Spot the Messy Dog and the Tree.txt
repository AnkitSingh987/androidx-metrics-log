Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Messy Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a messy dog named Spot. He loved to play in the mud and dig in the dirt. Spot lived in a big country with lots of trees and grass. He had many friends like birds, cats, and other dogs. One day, Spot saw a big tree shaking. It made him tremble with fear. He did not know why the tree was shaking. Spot asked his friends if they knew what was happening. The birds flew up high to see, but they could not find the answer. Then, a kind squirrel told Spot that it was just the wind making the tree shake. Spot felt happy and safe again. He thanked the squirrel and went back to playing with his friends in the messy country. They all had a fun day together.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. Fin was a dependable fish who always helped his friends. He loved to dive deep into the water and play with his friends. One day, while Fin was diving, he saw a piece of paper on the sea floor. He picked it up and swam to his friend, Sally the seahorse. "Look, Sally! I found paper!" said Fin. Sally looked at the paper and said, "Let's draw a picture for our friend, Tim the turtle!" Fin and Sally drew a beautiful picture of the sea on the paper. They were so excited to give it to Tim. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wave came and took the paper away! Fin and Sally were sad, but they didn't give up. They decided to dive deeper and look for the paper. They looked everywhere and finally found the paper near a big rock. They were so happy! They took the paper and swam to Tim's house. When they gave the picture to Tim, he was so surprised and happy. "Thank you, Fin and Sally! You are the best friends ever!" said Tim. From that day on, they all became even better friends and had many more adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to go on trips with her family. One day, her mom said they were going on a trip to the big park. Amy was so happy and couldn't wait! Amy put on her tight shoes and got ready to go. When they got to the big park, Amy saw a big slide. She wanted to go down the slide, but she was scared. Amy looked at the slide and thought, "I dare to try it." Amy climbed up the slide and sat at the top. She was scared, but she thought of her tight shoes. They made her feel brave. Amy took a deep breath and went down the slide. Whee! It was so much fun! Amy went down the slide again and again. Her tight shoes helped her dare to try something new. She was so happy she went on the trip to the big park. And from that day on, Amy was never scared to try new things.

One day, a smelly cat named Tom saw a big chimney. He wanted to see what was inside. Tom tried to follow the smell to find out where it came from. Tom met a bird named Sally. Sally said, "Don't go near the chimney, it's not safe!" But Tom didn't listen. He wanted to know what was inside. So, Tom and Sally had a fight about going to the chimney. Tom went to the chimney anyway. But, when he got there, he saw something he didn't expect. A big, happy dog was living inside the chimney! The dog said, "Hi, I'm Sam! I live here because it's warm and cozy." Tom and Sally were surprised. They became friends with Sam and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to walk in the park with his mom. One day, Tim saw a big bush with red berries on it. He wanted to eat the berries, but he didn't know if they were good or bad. Tim asked his mom, "Can I eat the berries?" His mom said, "No, Tim. Those berries are bad. They can make you sick." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. The next day, Tim and his mom walked to the park again. This time, they saw a bush with blueberries. Tim's mom said, "These berries are good. You can eat them." Tim picked the berries and shared them with his mom. They were very happy and had a fun day at the park.
