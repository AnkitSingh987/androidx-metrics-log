Category: Intermediate
Title: Molly and the Toys

Molly was playing with her toys in her room. She had a big pile of objects in the corner of her room. She wanted to be organized and sort the objects. Mommy entered the room and asked, "Molly, what are you doing?" Molly replied excitedly, "I'm sorting the objects!" Mommy smiled, "That's great! So what are you sorting them into?" Molly said, "I'm sorting them by color. I want one pile with all the pink objects!" Mommy said, "That sounds fun, but be careful not to make too much of a mess!" Molly nodded and started sorting the objects. She picked up the objects one by one and placed the pink objects in one pile. When she was done all the objects were sorted into different piles. She was so proud of herself for organizing her room.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird had new, shiny feathers. The bird lived in a big tree. The bird loved to fly and play with friends. One day, the bird saw a car. The car was driving very fast. The bird wanted to drive too. So, the bird flew down and sat on the car. The bird was happy to be on the car. The car drove and drove. The bird saw many new things. The bird saw a big dog, a cute cat, and a tall tree. The bird was very happy. The bird flew back home and told friends about the fun drive.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max had an incredible talent. He could sing very well! Max loved to sing with his microphone. He would sing happy songs all day long. One day, a sneaky cat named Tom saw Max's microphone. Tom wanted to steal Max's microphone so he could sing too. So, when Max was not looking, Tom took the microphone and ran away. Max was very sad when he found out his microphone was gone. Max went to look for Tom and found him singing with the microphone. Max said, "Tom, it's not nice to steal. We can share the microphone and sing together!" Tom felt sorry and gave Max his microphone back. They sang together and became good friends. The moral of the story is that sharing is better than stealing.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to help his mom and dad. He was very good at being organized. He always put his toys away when he was done playing with them. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her deliver some special rocks called minerals. They were going to take them to Tim's grandma's house. Tim put the minerals in a big bag and carried it to the car. He was excited to help his mom. When they got to grandma's house, Tim and his mom gave her the minerals. Grandma was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim, for being so organized and helping your mom deliver these special rocks to me." Tim smiled and knew he had done a good job.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a gray cat. The cat had no name. One day, the cat saw a little boy playing with a ball. The boy was very happy. The gray cat wanted a name too. So, it decided to steal a name. It walked up to the boy and said, "Hi, I am a cat. Can I have your name, please?" The boy looked at the cat and said, "No, you can't have my name. But I can give you a new name." The cat was very excited. The boy thought for a moment and said, "Your new name is Fluffy!" The gray cat was very happy with its new name. The boy and Fluffy played with the ball all day long. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a slide and ran to it. Tim felt sleepy, but he wanted to play. As he climbed up, he saw mist in the air. It was cold and wet. Tim met a girl named Sue at the top of the slide. Sue said, "I am scared to slide down." Tim told her, "Don't be scared. I will slide with you." They sat down and slid together. The slide was fast and fun. But the mist made it slippery. When they reached the bottom, they slipped and fell. Tim and Sue cried. Their knees were hurt. They went home with tears in their eyes. The fun day at the park had a bad ending.
