Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mountain and the Flower

Once upon a time, in a tidy little town, there was a big mountain. People in the town were happy and kind. They liked to keep their town clean and nice. Every day, they would pick up trash and put things away. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the mountain. Tim saw a flower and wanted to smell it. When he smelled the flower, he started to sneeze. "Achoo! Achoo!" Tim sneezed so loud that it made the rocks on the mountain shake. The people in the town heard the sneeze and saw the rocks shake. They were scared. They knew they had to be careful and help each other. They worked together to keep the town tidy and safe. They learned that by being kind and helping each other, they could make their town a better place. The moral of the story is to always help each other and keep things clean and tidy. When we work together, we can make our home a happy and safe place.

Once upon a time, in a small lab, there lived a big, heavy dog named Max. Max liked to plan fun games with his friends. One day, he wanted to play a new game with his best friend, a little cat named Luna. Max said to Luna, "Let's plan a fun game today!" Luna was happy and agreed. They both thought hard and came up with a plan to play a game of hide-and-seek. They played the game all day long. Max, being heavy, found it hard to hide, but he tried his best. Luna, being small, was good at hiding. They laughed and had a great time together. At the end of the day, they were both tired but happy. They promised to plan more fun games in the future.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was not an original cat. He looked like all the other cats. One day, Tom found a pill on the ground. He thought it might make him special. Tom went to his friend, a small dog named Tim. "Look what I found!" Tom said. "I will eat this pill and maybe I will be original." Tim was scared. "No, Tom! We don't know what it is!" But Tom did not listen. Tom ate the pill. Suddenly, he felt funny. He opened his mouth and began to spray water everywhere! Tom didn't feel good. He wished he had listened to Tim. Now he was wet and sad. The pill did not make him original, it only made him sick.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl. She was three years old and her name was Lucy. One day, Lucy was playing in the garden. She was looking for something to do. That's when she saw her dad's old, broken pen. She picked up the pen and ran to her dad. "Daddy, I want to write!" she shouted. Her dad smiled and gave Lucy a piece of paper and a new wet pen. Lucy carefully began to write her name on the paper. "Look daddy!" she shouted and showed her dad her work. Her dad smiled and said, "One day Lucy, you'll write amazing things". Lucy didn't quite understand what her dad meant, but she enjoyed writing with the new wet pen. She knew that writing was something special.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods. She saw many trees and birds. Mia was very happy. She saw a squirrel playing with a nut. The squirrel was impatient because it wanted to eat the nut. Mia kneeled down to look at the squirrel. She said, "Hi, squirrel! Do you want to play with me?" The squirrel looked at Mia and then at the nut. It decided to play with Mia for a little while. They played in the woods, jumping over sticks and running around trees. After a while, the squirrel was tired. It said, "Thank you, Mia. I had fun playing with you. Now I am hungry and want to eat my nut." Mia smiled and said, "Bye, squirrel! Have a yummy snack!" The squirrel went back to the nut and Mia went home. She had a fun day in the woods.
