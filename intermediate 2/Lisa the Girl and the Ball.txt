Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She had a big red ball that she loved to play with. One day, while she was playing outside, a thief took her ball away. Lisa was very sad and miserable without her ball. She told her mom about the thief and her mom said, "I promise we will find your ball." Lisa and her mom went to look for the thief. They found him hiding behind a tree, and he looked very sorry. Lisa's mom asked him to give the ball back. The thief gave the ball back to Lisa and said, "I am sorry for taking your ball. I promise I will not do it again." Lisa was happy to have her ball back and the thief learned that taking things from others is wrong. From that day on, he never stole again and everyone lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is that it is important to always be honest and not take things that do not belong to you.

One day, a brave little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw her friend, Tim, playing with a ball. Tim looked sad, so Amy asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to win the game, but I can't kick the ball far." Amy wanted to help Tim, so she said, "I can show you how to kick the ball far. Be brave and try again." Tim tried to kick the ball, but it didn't go far. He looked at Amy and shrugged. Amy said, "It's okay, Tim. Let's try again." This time, Amy showed Tim how to kick the ball with more power. Tim tried again and kicked the ball very far. He was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Amy! Now I can win the game!" They both played together and had lots of fun. Tim was brave, and Amy helped him win.

Once upon a time, there was a big, lazy cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to sleep all day and play all night. She had a best friend named Buddy, who was a small, happy dog. One day, Buddy decided to teach Kitty a new game. He wanted to play "Find the Price." They would look for things with a price and then show each other. Kitty liked this game because it was easy and fun. First, they found a big toy with a price. Then, they found a small hat with a price. Kitty and Buddy played the game all day long. They were very happy and not lazy anymore. From that day on, they loved to play "Find the Price" together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a pet cat named Max. Max liked to play in the house and sleep in the warm sun. One day, Max saw a mouse run across the floor. Max wanted to catch the mouse, so he made a trap. Max said to Tim, "I will catch the mouse with my trap. It will be easy!" Tim watched Max put a box on the floor with a yummy treat inside. Max hid behind a chair and waited for the mouse to come. The mouse saw the box and smelled the treat. It went inside the box to eat it. Max jumped up and closed the trap. The mouse was caught! Tim said, "Good job, Max! You caught the mouse!" Max felt proud of his trap. But then, Max thought about the mouse. It was scared and wanted to go home. Max felt sad for the mouse. He opened the trap and let the mouse go. The mouse said, "Thank you, Max!" and ran away. The moral of the story is to be kind to others, even if they are different from you.

Once upon a time, in a small, organized room, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and make a big mess. One day, Tim found a paper with a poem on it. The poem was about a cat and a dog who were friends. Tim wanted to show the poem to his mom, but first, he decided to play with his toys. While playing, Tim accidentally stepped on the paper and destroyed it. The poem was torn and had footprints on it now. Tim was sad that the poem was destroyed, but his mom saw him and asked what was wrong. Tim showed her the destroyed poem. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can write a new poem together." Tim smiled, and they wrote a new poem about a cat and a dog who were best friends.
