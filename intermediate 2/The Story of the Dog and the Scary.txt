Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Scary

Once upon a time, there was a big, scary dog. He lived in a small house with a nice boy. The boy loved the scary dog, even when he seemed angry. They played together every day. One day, the scary dog got sick. The boy was sad. He took the scary dog to a patient doctor. The doctor looked at the dog and said, "Don't worry, he will be okay." The boy and the patient doctor helped the scary dog get better. They gave him medicine and lots of love. Soon, the scary dog was not sick anymore. The boy and the scary dog played together again, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat. The cat had a small cell. The cat liked to play in the cell. One day, the cat saw a big orange ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball. The cat went to the ball and started to play. The cat was happy. But then, the ball started to scream. The cat was scared. The cat did not know balls could scream. The ball stopped screaming. The cat was still scared. The ball started to grow bigger and bigger. The ball was not a ball. It was a big orange monster. The monster ate the cat. The cat was gone.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to run. He put on his shoes and went outside. Tim liked to run with style. He ran fast, like a race car. His shoes were dull, but he did not care. As Tim ran, he saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play. Tim thought the dog would chase him, but the dog did not. The dog just sat and looked at Tim. This was unexpected. Tim felt sad. He wanted to play with the dog. Then, he had an idea. He took off his dull shoes and put them on the dog's feet. The dog loved it! Now, Tim and the dog could run with style together.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Timmy had a big orange. He loved his orange very much. It was comfortable to hold and it smelled nice. Timmy wanted to eat the orange, but Mommy said, "Not now, Timmy." One sunny day, Timmy went to the park with his orange. He played with his friends and showed them his big orange. His friend, Billy, said, "Can I hold it?" Timmy said, "No, Mommy didn't permit me to share it." Timmy played all day with his orange, but he started to feel hungry. He looked at his orange and thought about what Mommy said. He wanted to eat it, but he remembered Mommy didn't permit him to eat it yet. Later, Timmy went home and put the orange on the table. He went to his room to play with his toys. When he came back, he saw that his dog, Max, had eaten the orange. Timmy was very sad. Mommy came in and saw Timmy crying. She said, "I'm sorry, Timmy. I should have let you eat the orange." Timmy learned that sometimes it's better to listen to his own feelings, even if Mommy didn't permit something.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. He loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he went to the park to play with his friend, a dog named Max. Tom and Max were playing ball when Tom felt too hot. He said, "Max, I need to find some shade. I am too hot." Max agreed, and they walked to a big tree. The tree had a lot of shade, and they both felt better. As they were resting, a big bird came and took their ball. Tom and Max tried to get it back, but the bird flew away. They felt embarrass because they could not catch the bird. Tom and Max were sad and went home without their ball.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a youth who loved to play with his friends. One sunny day, Tim and his friends were playing with a ball in the park. Suddenly, the ball rolled far away and Tim went to get it. He saw a new girl, named Sue, standing alone and upset. Tim asked Sue, "Do you want to join us and play with the ball?" Sue looked at Tim and nodded her head. As Sue joined Tim and his friends, she began to smile and have fun. They all played together, laughing and sharing the ball. In the end, Sue was not upset anymore, and Tim was happy to have a new friend. They all went home tired but happy after a day of fun at the park.
