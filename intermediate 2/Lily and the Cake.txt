Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Cake

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to make a cake. She was very excited because it was her mom's birthday. Lily put on her new apron and started to mix the cake ingredients. But, oh no! The cake did not look good. It was broken and messy. Lily was sad. She wanted to repair the cake, but she did not know how. She thought, "I need help." Lily asked her dad to help her repair the cake. They worked together and fixed the cake. It looked very nice and yummy. Lily was happy. Her mom loved the cake, and they all had a great birthday party.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her room. She heard a noise outside. It was a sad, miserable sound. Sue decided to go see what was making the noise. When Sue went outside, she saw a little bird. The bird was crying. Sue asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I lost my mommy. I can't find her." Sue decided to help the bird find his mommy. Sue and the bird looked everywhere. They were about to give up when they heard a noise. It was the bird's mommy! She was not lost after all. She was hiding to surprise the bird with a birthday party. The bird was so happy, and Sue was happy she could help.

Tim and Jen are friends. They like to play with their wagon. They put their toys in the wagon and pull it around the yard. They have fun. One day, Tim sees a big dog. The dog is brown and has a long tail. Tim likes dogs. He calls the dog. "Come here, dog! Come and play with us!" he says. Jen is scared. She does not like dogs. She thinks the dog is mean. She says to Tim, "No, Tim! Don't call the dog! The dog might bite us! Let's go away!" But Tim does not listen. He calls the dog again. The dog hears Tim and runs to him. The dog is not sweet. The dog is angry. The dog barks and jumps on Tim. The dog bites Tim's arm. Tim cries. He drops the wagon. The toys fall out. Jen runs to Tim. She tries to help him. She yells for help. "Help! Help! The dog bit Tim! Help us!" she says. But no one hears Jen. No one comes to help. Tim and Jen are alone. They are hurt and scared. The dog is still there. The dog is not their friend. The end.

Once upon a time there lived a deer named Sam in a big, blue forest. Sam was excited to explore the forest and all of its animals. He was even more excited to find new friends. One day he was walking through the trees when he heard a voice. It was a frog! Little Sam was so excited and asked the frog to be his friend. The frog was a bit hesitant, but eventually he said yes. He asked Sam if he wanted to play a game. Sam excitedly nodded his head. The frog said that he was going to twist and if Sam could twist too, then they could be friends. Sam tried, but his body just wouldn't twist. He was sad and felt embarrassed. He knew he wasn't good enough for the frog, so he ran away. The frog called after him, and explained that friendships were not based on how well one could twist. The moral of the story is that friendships should not be based on perceptions or abilities. No one should feel inferior due to physical characteristics. Instead, friendship should be based on understanding and trust.

Once upon a time, there was a lively girl named Olive. She loved to dance and play all day. Olive had a big heart and always took care to be nice to everyone. One sunny day, Olive found a small box in her yard. It was a pretty box, with many colors. Olive was excited and wanted to see what was inside. She opened the box, and a tiny cat jumped out! Olive was very surprised. Olive wanted to care for the cat, so she gave it some food and played with it. They had lots of fun together. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to grow bigger and bigger! Soon, the cat was as big as Olive's house! Olive was scared, but the big cat was still her friend. They played together and had even more fun than before. And Olive learned that sometimes, surprises can be good.
