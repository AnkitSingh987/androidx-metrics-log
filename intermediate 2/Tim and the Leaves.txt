Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he saw something big and impressive in his yard. He wanted to know what it was. He went closer to the big thing. Tim moved some leaves and dirt to reveal what was hiding. It was a big box! Tim was very happy. He wanted to see what was inside the box. Tim opened the box and found lots of oats. He knew that oats were good for eating. Tim shared the oats with his friends. They all had a fun day eating and playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a big room. In the room, there was a hot sun. The sun was so hot, it made everyone in the room feel warm. One day, a little girl named Lucy came into the room. She saw the sun and said, "Hello, Sun! You are very hot today." The sun did not like being called hot. It felt sad and said, "I don't want to be hot. I want to be cool, like the moon." The sun wanted to leave the room, but it was stuck there. Lucy thought about how to help the sun. Lucy had an idea. She said, "Sun, if you bow to the moon, maybe you will become cool like the moon." The sun listened to Lucy and bowed to the moon. Suddenly, the room became cool and the sun was happy. Lucy and the sun became friends and played together in the cool room.

Once there was a young boy named Tim. He lived on a farm with his mom and dad. They grew wheat in their big field. Tim liked to watch the wheat grow tall and strong. One day, a kind man from the next farm came to visit. The man asked Tim's dad if he could lend him some wheat. Tim's dad said yes and gave the man a big bag of wheat. The man was very happy and thanked Tim's dad. Tim felt good that his dad helped the man. The wheat they lent would help the man and his family too. From that day on, Tim knew that lending and helping others was a very good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a very special pillow. This pillow was not like other pillows. It was a creative pillow. When Lily put her head on the pillow, she could feel the magic inside. One day, Lily felt sad. She went to her room and lay down on her creative pillow. She started to feel better. The pillow helped her think of fun things to do. Lily felt happy again. Lily loved her creative pillow very much. She took it with her everywhere she went. It always made her feel good. And everyone who saw it wanted a creative pillow too.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a dependable dog who loved to play outside. One day, Spot saw a hedge near his house. The hedge had many pretty flowers and green leaves. As Spot moved closer to the hedge, he heard a small voice. "Hello, Spot!" said the voice. Spot looked around and saw a tiny bug on a leaf. "Hi," said Spot, "What is your name?" The bug smiled and said, "My name is Buzz. I live in this hedge." Spot and Buzz became good friends. They played near the hedge every day. Spot would move around the hedge and Buzz would fly next to him. They were happy and had lots of fun together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to collect blue rocks. One day, Tim went on a trip to find more blue rocks. He put on his hat and went outside. On his trip, Tim walked and walked. He looked under trees and near the river. Then, he saw a big blue rock! But, when he tried to pick it up, it moved! It was not a rock, it was a blue turtle! Tim was happy to meet the blue turtle. They became friends and played all day. Tim did not collect blue rocks anymore. Instead, he and the blue turtle went on trips together, having fun and finding new friends.
