Category: Intermediate
Title: Ann the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ann. Ann was very good at delivering things. One day, she had to deliver a thick book about marriage to a friend named Tom. On her way, Ann met a talking dog. The dog said, "Hello, Ann! What do you have there?" Ann said, "I have a thick book about marriage for my friend Tom." The dog wanted to help Ann. So, they walked together to Tom's house. When they got there, Tom was happy to see them. He said, "Thank you, Ann and dog, for delivering my thick book about marriage!" Ann and the talking dog felt good that they could help Tom. They said goodbye and went back to their homes. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Amy had a thermometer. She liked to play with it and pretend she was a doctor. One day, her friend, Tim, came over to play. Tim was very persistent about trying to use the thermometer, but Amy did not want to share it with him. Amy's mom saw that they were not getting along, so she told Amy to accept that Tim wanted to play with the thermometer too. Amy agreed and let Tim use the thermometer. They took turns pretending to be doctors, and they were having a lot of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The thermometer started to talk! It said, "Thank you for sharing me, Amy and Tim!" Both kids were so surprised. They had never heard a talking thermometer before. From that day on, Amy and Tim always shared their toys and had even more fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play on his porch. He had a big, red ball and a small, blue bucket. Tim loved playing on his porch because it was a reliable place to have fun. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim saw his friend, Sue, walking by. "Hi Sue!" Tim shouted. "Do you want to help me fill my blue bucket with water?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, I want to help!" They went to the water tap and filled the blue bucket with water. They carried it back to the porch, being careful not to spill any water. Tim and Sue had so much fun playing with the water on the porch. They laughed and splashed each other. They took turns filling the bucket and pouring it out. At the end of the day, they were wet and happy. The porch was their favorite place to play.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. Tim had a big sack. He liked to put toys in the sack and take them to his friends. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, let's go to the park." Tim took his sack with him. At the park, he met a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she had no toys to play with. Tim wanted to make Sue happy. He opened his sack and let Sue pick a toy. Sue was so happy, and they played together all day. Tim was glad to be kind and share his toys with his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was eager to go outside and play. He put on his shoes and ran out the door. Tim saw a big red ball in the yard. He ran to the ball and said, "Hi, ball! Let's play!" Tim kicked the ball high into the air. The ball went up and up. Tim looked up at the sky to see where the ball would go. The ball came down and landed near a hot fire pit. Tim didn't know it was hot. He went to get the ball. "Ouch!" Tim shouted. He touched the hot fire pit and it hurt his hand. Tim's mom heard him and came running. "Tim, you should look before you touch something hot!" she said. Tim cried and his hand hurt a lot. The day was not fun anymore.

One day, a lovely bus went to the store. The bus was big and yellow. It had many friends. The bus liked to help people go places. At the store, the bus saw a little cat. The cat was sad. It could not find its toy. The bus wanted to help the cat. They looked for the toy together. Then, something unexpected happened. The bus found the toy, but it was not a toy for a cat. It was a toy for a dog! The cat was not a cat, it was a dog all along! The dog was so happy to have its toy back. The bus and the dog became best friends.
