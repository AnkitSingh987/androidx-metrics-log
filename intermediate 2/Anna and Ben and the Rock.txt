Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Rock

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play in the garden. They had a dog named Spot who was very obedient and followed them everywhere. One day, they found a shiny rock in the dirt. They wondered what it was and decided to show it to their mom. "Mom, look what we found!" they said, running into the house with the rock. Mom smiled and took the rock from them. She looked at it closely and said, "This is a mineral, children. It is a part of the earth that has different colors and shapes. Some minerals are very rare and valuable, and some are very common and useful. This one is called quartz, and it is very pretty." "Wow, a mineral!" Anna and Ben said, amazed. "Can we keep it, mom?" "Of course, you can keep it, but you have to share it and take good care of it. It is a gift from nature, and we should respect and appreciate it," Mom said. Anna and Ben nodded and thanked their mom. They took the mineral back to the garden and put it in a special place. They played with it and showed it to Spot, who wagged his tail and licked their faces. They had so much fun that they forgot about the time. "Children, it is getting late. Time to go inside and get ready for bed," Mom called from the window. Anna and Ben yawned and realized they were very tired. They picked up the mineral and ran to the house. They gave it to their mom and asked her to keep it safe for them. They hugged and kissed their mom and Spot and went to their room. They brushed their teeth, put on their pajamas, and climbed into their beds. "Good night, Anna. Good night, Ben. Good night, Spot. I love you all," Mom said, tucking them in. "Good night, Mom. We love you too. And we love our mineral," Anna and Ben said, smiling. They closed their eyes and fell asleep, dreaming of the shiny rock and the wonderful world of minerals. The moral of the story is: We should be curious and learn about the things around us, but we should also be grateful and respectful of the gifts of nature. We should also listen to our parents and be obedient when they tell us what to do. And we should always share and care for the things and the people we love.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big box in the park. It has many amazing things inside. There are balls, cars, dolls, books and more. "Wow, look at this!" Tom says. He holds a red car and makes it go vroom vroom. "This is my car now." "No, it's mine!" Lily says. She holds a pink doll and hugs it. "This is my doll now." They both want the same things. They start to fight. They pull and push and shout. The car and the doll fall on the ground. They are sad and angry. A lady sees them. She is nice. She comes to them and says, "Stop fighting, children. You can share the toys. There are enough for everyone. Why don't you play together and have fun?" Tom and Lily look at each other. They feel sorry. They say, "Sorry, lady. Sorry, friend." They pick up the car and the doll. They give them to each other. They smile and say, "Thank you, friend. You can have this. I can have that." They play with the toys. They are happy. They make new friends. They have an amazing day.

Lily and Ben liked to jog in the park with their dog Spot. They ran fast and had fun. One day, they saw a big car with a flag on it. It stopped near the pond. A lady came out of the car. She wore a shiny crown and a long dress. She was the queen! Lily and Ben wanted to see the queen. They ran to the car with Spot. But Spot saw a duck in the pond. He barked and chased the duck. The duck was scared and flew away. Spot jumped into the pond and splashed water everywhere. The water wet the queen's dress and crown. The queen was angry and shouted. "Who let this dog near me? He ruined my clothes and my hair! Go away, you naughty dog!" Lily and Ben were ashamed. They said sorry to the queen. They grabbed Spot and ran away. They did not want to jog in the park anymore. They went home and hugged their dog. They still loved him, even if he was naughty. They hoped the queen would forgive them.
