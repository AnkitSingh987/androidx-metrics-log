Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She loved to eat grapes. One day, she saw a big grape in her yard. She was very happy and wanted to eat it. But her mom told her, "Sally, don't eat that grape. It's not good for you." Sally felt sad but she decided to ignore her mom. Sally picked up the grape and took a big bite. Suddenly, she felt very ashamed. Her mom was right. The grape did not taste good. It was not a grape at all! It was a round, purple ball. Sally felt so silly. She did not know what to do. Her mom saw Sally and said, "It's okay, Sally. We all make mistakes. Next time, listen to me when I tell you something." Sally hugged her mom and promised to listen. From that day on, Sally always listened to her mom and never ignored her again. And she still loved grapes, but only the real ones.

Anna liked to play with her goat, Lily. Lily was a modern goat, with a pink collar and a bell. Anna and Lily would run and jump in the field, and have fun. One day, Anna's mom said, "We have to part with Lily. We are moving to the city, and there is no place for a goat there." Anna was sad. She hugged Lily and said, "I don't want to part with you. You are my best friend." Lily bleated and licked Anna's face. She seemed to understand. The next day, a man came to take Lily away. He had a big truck and a smile. He said, "Don't worry, Anna. Lily will be happy in her new home. She will have other goats to play with, and a lot of grass to eat." Anna tried to be brave. She gave Lily one last hug and said, "Goodbye, Lily. I will miss you. Maybe we can see each other again someday." Lily bleated and nodded. She looked at Anna with her big eyes and said, "Goodbye, Anna. I will miss you too. You are my best friend." The man put Lily in the truck and drove away. Anna waved and cried. She hoped Lily would be happy. She hoped she would be happy too.

One sunny day, a little dog named Max was feeling gloomy. He had no friends to play with. So, he thought of a plan to invite other animals to his house to play. Max went to the park and saw a big cat with a soft ear. "Hi, cat! Do you want to come to my house to play?" Max asked. The cat said, "Yes! I would like that." Then they saw a bird with a long ear. Max asked the bird, "Do you want to come to my house too?" The bird said, "Yes, I will come." When they got to Max's house, they played with toys and ate yummy food. The gloomy day turned into a fun day. Max, the cat, and the bird became good friends. They played together every day after that.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a fierce boy who loved to play and run. One day, Tim saw a big piece of paper on the table. He had an idea. Tim took the paper and started to fold it. He folded it again and again. He made a little paper boat. Tim was very happy with his boat. He wanted to show it to his friends. The next day, it rained a lot. Tim took his paper boat outside. He put it in a puddle and watched it float away. The rain had made a big river for his boat. Tim's friends saw the boat and clapped. Tim smiled because his boat was the best.

Once upon a time, there was a humble little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a small house with his best friend, a girl named Lily. They played together every day and were very happy. One night, Fluffy had a bad dream, a nightmare. In the dream, he was trapped in a big, dark room and couldn't escape. He was very scared. But then, he heard Lily's voice calling his name, and he knew she would help him. Fluffy woke up and saw that he was safe in his little house with Lily. He was so happy to escape the bad dream. Lily hugged Fluffy and told him, "Don't worry, I will always be here to help you." And they lived happily ever after.
