Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Soup

Once upon a time, in a big city, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Mia's mom asked her to stir the big pot of soup. Mia felt like a big girl, and she was very happy to help. While Mia was stirring the soup, her cat, Tom, jumped onto the table. Mia tried to stop Tom, but she was not fast enough. The soup spilled all over the floor. Mia felt very sorry and sad. She knew she had to clean up the mess. Mia's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She knew Mia was sorry, and she hugged her. Together, they cleaned up the mess and made a new pot of soup. Mia was more careful this time, and the soup was perfect. Mia learned that it's okay to make mistakes, as long as you try to fix them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to draw with her pencil. One day, she went outside to draw a big, clear sky. She sat under a tree and started to draw. As she was drawing, Mia saw a big, red ball. She thought it would taste good, so she took a bite. But it did not taste good. It tasted bad! Mia was sad and did not want to draw anymore. Then, something unexpected happened. A funny dog came and took the ball. The dog played with the ball and was so happy. Mia laughed and felt happy too. She went back to drawing the clear sky and the funny dog playing with the big, red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jane. She loved to paint. One day, she saw a big wall that was fit for her art. She was excited to paint on it. Jane asked her friend Tom, "Will you help me paint the wall?" Tom said, "Yes, I will help you, Jane." They started to paint the wall with bright colors. They painted a sun, a tree, and a house. They were having fun. While they were painting, a rain came and washed away all the colors on the wall. Jane and Tom were sad. But then, they saw a rainbow in the sky. The rain had made something beautiful, too. They learned that sometimes things don't go as planned, but you can still find something good in it.

Lila was a little girl who liked to help her mom. She helped her mom cook, clean, and take care of her baby brother. Lila's mom was very sick and had to take a lot of pills every day. Lila wanted to make her mom feel better, so she decided to give her a special gift. Lila went to her room and looked for something incredible. She found a shiny necklace that her dad gave her for her birthday. It had a big red heart and sparkled in the light. Lila thought her mom would love it. She put the necklace in a small box and wrapped it with a pretty ribbon. She ran to the kitchen, where her mom was sitting on a chair. She gave her the box and said, "Mom, this is for you. I love you so much. You are the best mom in the world." Her mom smiled and hugged her. She opened the box and saw the necklace. She gasped and said, "Lila, this is so beautiful. Thank you, my sweet girl. This is the most incredible gift I ever got. You are so kind and thoughtful. I love you too." Lila was happy to see her mom happy. She helped her mom put on the necklace and said, "Mom, you look so pretty. The necklace matches your eyes. You are incredible too." Her mom kissed her and said, "Lila, you are my treasure. You are more valuable than any pill or necklace. You make me feel better every day. You are my sunshine."

One day, a little cat named Tim went for a walk in the park. Tim saw a big fountain with water. He liked the water and wanted to play near it. Tim was a bit fearful because he had never seen a fountain before. But he was brave and walked closer to the fountain. As Tim got closer, he saw a small bird near the fountain. The bird was thirsty and wanted to drink water. But the bird was also fearful of the fountain. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he had an idea. Tim told the bird, "Don't be scared. I will help you." Tim and the bird started to walk to the fountain together. They both felt brave now. They rushed to the fountain and drank the water. They were not fearful anymore. Tim and the bird became good friends and played near the fountain all day.
