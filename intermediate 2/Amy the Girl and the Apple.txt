Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Apple

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She had her own umbrella because it was raining. Amy liked the rain. It made her happy. As she walked, she saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad. He did not have an umbrella. Amy said, "Do you want to share my umbrella?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Now, they both were happy under the umbrella. After the rain stopped, they found a big, red apple on the ground. It looked delicious. Amy and Tim shared the apple. It was very yummy. They became good friends and played together every day.

Anna loves to play the piano. She likes to make loud and soft sounds with her fingers. Sometimes she sings along with the sounds. She has a big piano in her living room. It is black and shiny. One day, Anna hears a strange noise from the piano. It sounds like a squeak. She wonders what it is. She walks to the piano and looks around. She does not see anything. She opens the lid of the piano and peeks inside. She sees a small mouse. The mouse is orange and furry. It has big ears and a long tail. The mouse sees Anna and gets scared. It runs away and hides under the strings. Anna is not scared. She thinks the mouse is cute. She says hello to the mouse and smiles. She tries to make friends with the mouse. She gives the mouse a piece of cheese. The mouse smells the cheese and comes out. It takes the cheese and nibbles on it. It looks at Anna and squeaks. Anna and the mouse become friends. They play the piano together. Anna makes sounds and the mouse dances. They have fun and laugh. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball that loved to bounce. The ball lived in a big room called a museum. In the museum, there were many things to see and touch. The ball was very happy there. One day, the ball wanted to bounce really high. It tried so hard to go up, up, up in the air. But the ball could not bounce as high as it wanted. The ball felt sad. Then, a kind little boy came to the museum. He saw the ball and gave it a big push. The ball bounced so high, it touched the sky! The ball was so happy and thanked the little boy. They played together all day in the museum, and the ball never forgot how fun it was to bounce high with a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big pocket on his shirt. He loved to put things in it. Tim had a friend named Sam. They both liked to play games. They were very competitive. One day, Tim and Sam played a game of who could find the most rocks. Tim put all the rocks he found in his pocket. But Sam was finding more rocks. Tim wanted to win, so he asked his mom for help. His mom said, "Maybe you can find something else to put in your pocket that is better than rocks." Tim thought about what his mom said. He looked around and saw a shiny coin on the ground. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Then, he showed it to Sam. Sam was surprised and asked, "Where did you find that?" Tim smiled and said, "I found it when I was looking for rocks." Sam was happy for Tim and they both agreed that the shiny coin was better than all the rocks. In the end, they both felt like winners. They learned that sometimes it is not about who wins or loses, but about having fun and helping each other.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a wide tree. Under the tree, she found a lollipop. Sue was very happy. She wanted to eat the lollipop. But first, she wanted to show her friend Tom. Sue went to Tom's house. She said, "Look, I found a lollipop!" Tom looked at the lollipop. He saw a number on it. The number was 5. Tom said, "Wow! That is a big number!" Just then, a big wind came. It blew the lollipop out of Sue's hand. The lollipop flew up into the sky. Sue and Tom were sad. They did not know what to do. But then, the wind changed. The lollipop came back down. It landed in a big pile of leaves. Sue and Tom laughed. They found the lollipop again. They were very happy. They shared the lollipop and played in the park all day.
