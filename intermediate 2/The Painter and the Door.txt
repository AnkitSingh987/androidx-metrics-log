Category: Intermediate
Title: The Painter and the Door

One day, a painter had a long brush. He wanted to reach high up on the wall. He tried to reach, but he was too short. He was sad. Then, a tall friend came to help. The tall friend could reach the high wall. The painter was happy. They painted the wall together. But, the wall was not a wall. It was a big door! The door opened, and there was a fun park behind it! The painter and tall friend were surprised. They played in the park all day.

Once upon a time, in a big yard, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his water spray toy. He would spray water on the plants and on the ground. One day, Tim found a small hole in the yard. He thought it would be fun to spray water into the hole. As Tim sprayed water into the hole, a tiny bug came out. The bug was wet and looked dead. Tim felt sad because he didn't want to hurt the bug. He picked up the bug and put it in the sun to dry. After a while, the bug started to move again. Tim was happy that the bug was not dead. Tim learned that he should be careful when playing with his water spray toy. He didn't want to hurt any more bugs or make them wet. From that day on, Tim only sprayed water on the plants and not on the ground. The moral of the story is to always be careful and kind to all living things, even the tiny bugs.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals. Tim liked the big cat called a leopard. The leopard had pretty spots. It was a warm day, so the leopard was lying in the sun. Tim said, "Mom, I want to turn into a leopard!" His mom laughed and said, "You can pretend to be a leopard, but you can't really turn into one." Tim started to act like a leopard. He crawled on the ground and made a soft growl. He felt warm in the sun, just like the leopard. Tim and his mom had a fun day at the zoo.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a rare toy boat. He loved to sail it in the pond near his house. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue who was playing with her pedal bike. "Hi Sue!" said Tim. "Do you want to see my boat sail?" "Yes, Tim!" said Sue. "I want to see it sail!" Tim put his boat in the pond. The boat sailed on the water. Sue and Tim watched the boat sail together. They had so much fun. After the boat sailed, Sue showed Tim her pedal bike. Tim tried to ride the bike, but it was too big for him. They laughed and played together all day. Tim and Sue became good friends, and they loved to sail the boat and ride the pedal bike together.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She loved to paint pretty pictures with bright colors. One sunny day, Lily went to the park to paint the flowers and the trees. In the park, Lily met a small bird who was sad. The bird said, "I want to paint too, but my wings are too small to hold a brush." Lily did not want the bird to complain or be sad, so she had an idea. Lily dipped her brush in the paint and let the bird pick the colors. Then, she painted a beautiful picture of the bird and the park. The bird was so happy and thanked Lily. They became good friends and painted together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a human named Tom. Tom had a big box. Inside the box were many things. Tom liked to attach things from the box to his clothes. One day, he found a clear button in the box. He thought it was very pretty. Tom wanted to show his friend, Sam, the clear button. He attached it to his shirt and went outside. But when Tom walked to Sam's house, something unexpected happened. The clear button on his shirt started to glow. Tom was surprised. He touched the glowing button, and suddenly, he became invisible! Now, no one could see Tom. He went to Sam's house and knocked on the door. Sam opened the door, but he could not see Tom. Tom laughed and said, "It's me, Tom! The clear button made me invisible!" Sam was amazed. Together, they played invisible games all day. In the end, Tom took off the clear button and became visible again. He and Sam put the button in the box and decided to keep it as their secret. They knew they would have many more fun days with the magic clear button.
