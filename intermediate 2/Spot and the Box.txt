Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run in the park. One day, Spot found an empty box made of steel. It was shiny and new. Spot liked the box and wanted to save it. Spot took the steel box to his best friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue was happy to see the empty box. She said, "We can save this box and use it to keep our toys safe!" Spot wagged his tail and barked with joy. Together, Sue and Spot put their toys in the empty steel box. They closed the lid and knew their toys were safe. Spot had saved the day with his new steel box. They played and laughed, happy that their toys had a safe place to stay.

Once upon a time there was a man who ran a factory. His factory was big and had lots of machines that he used to make things. The man was careless and didn't take care of his factory. He also never checked the machines and left them running, even when he was away. One day, the man had gone away, and the machines kept running. But there was a problem - one of the machines was leaking oil. The oil dripped onto the factory floor and made a big mess. Luckily, the man's neighbour saw the oil and knew what to do. She rushed into the factory and shut off the machines so that the oil couldn't leak anymore. The man was very grateful and he thanked the neighbour for being so brave and careful. From then on, he made sure to check his factory every day and be careful with his machines. The moral of this story is that being careless can cause big problems, but a little bit of carefulness can help to keep us safe.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But sometimes they get tired and need a nap. Mom and Dad tell them to nap after lunch. One day, Tom and Lily do not want to nap. They want to play more. They sneak out of their room and go to the living room. They see a big box on the sofa. It is a gift for their birthday. Tom and Lily are curious. They open the box and find a big puzzle. It has many pieces and colors. They think it is fun. They start to make the puzzle on the floor. But the puzzle is not easy. Some pieces do not fit. Tom and Lily get frustrated. They try to force the pieces together. They break some pieces. They make a mess. Mom and Dad hear the noise. They come to the living room and see the puzzle and the twins. They are not happy. They scold Tom and Lily for opening the gift and ruining the puzzle. They tell them to pay for the puzzle with their allowance. They also tell them to nap right now. Tom and Lily are sorry. They cry and say sorry. They pick up the pieces and put them back in the box. They go to their room and nap. They learn their lesson. They do not open gifts without permission. They nap when they are told. They are good twins.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. One day, Tim found a mysterious black rock. The rock was called coal. Tim took the coal home. He wanted to show his mom. But on the way, he realized the coal made his hands dirty. Tim did not like dirty hands. He tried to clean them, but the black did not go away. Tim started to cry. His mom came to see what was wrong. She saw the coal and the dirty hands. She was not happy. Tim felt sad. The mysterious coal was not fun after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to test things with her finger. One day, Sue found a small, weak bug in her yard. Sue touched the bug with her finger to test if it was okay. The weak bug moved a little bit, but not much. Sue felt sad for the bug, so she decided to help it. Sue picked up the weak bug with her finger and took it to a safe place. She gave it some food and water. The bug got stronger and started to move more. Sue was happy that she could help the bug. They became good friends and played together every day.
