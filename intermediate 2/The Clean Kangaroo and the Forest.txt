Category: Intermediate
Title: The Clean Kangaroo and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a clean kangaroo. The kangaroo lived in a big forest. One day, a little boy came to play in the forest. He saw the clean kangaroo and wanted to be friends. The little boy said, "Hi, kangaroo! Do you want to play with me?" The clean kangaroo was happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" So, they played together all day. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. But then, the little boy did something bad. He kicked the clean kangaroo. The kangaroo was very sad and ran away. The little boy was left all alone in the forest. He cried and cried, but the clean kangaroo did not come back.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They were buying food for dinner. Tim saw a man take something from the store without paying. Tim said, "Mom, I think that man is a thief!" His mom looked serious and told Tim to stay close to her. They told the store man about the thief. The store man called a big man to catch the thief. The big man asked Tim, "Can you guess where the thief went?" Tim pointed to the door. The big man ran after the thief. But, there was a twist! The thief was really a dad playing a game with his little girl. The dad said, "I was just playing with my daughter. I did not want to steal." The store man and the big man laughed. Tim's mom said, "We are sorry for the mix-up." They all went back to shopping, and Tim learned that sometimes things are not what they seem.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog in the open park. He liked to run and play in the big open space. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim felt happy. As Tim jogged, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a big bag of toys. Tim stopped and said, "Hi! What are you doing?" Sue smiled and said, "I am going to fill my toy box with these toys. Want to help me?" Tim nodded and helped Sue pick up the toys. Together, Tim and Sue filled the toy box with all the toys. They laughed and had fun. After the toy box was full, they played with the toys in the open park. They became good friends and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They were very happy. They had a lovely garden with many pretty flowers. The man and the wife liked to take care of their garden together. They always had fun when they worked in their garden. One day, the man found a new tool to help them. It was a spray bottle. He showed it to his wife, and she was happy too. They filled the spray bottle with water and went to their garden. They took turns spraying the flowers with water. The flowers were very happy and grew bigger and bigger. The man and his wife were very proud of their lovely garden. They sprayed the flowers every day. They loved to watch the flowers grow and become more beautiful. And they lived happily ever after, taking care of their lovely garden together.

Once upon a time, there was a red bird. The red bird lived in a big oak tree. The oak tree was in a pretty park. One day, the red bird wanted to soar high in the sky. The bird flapped its wings and soared up, up, up. The red bird was happy to fly so high. As the red bird soared, it saw many things. It saw kids playing and dogs running. The red bird loved to soar in the sky above the big oak tree. And at the end of the day, the red bird went back to its home in the big oak tree, ready to soar again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman. He was big, strong, and reliable. He helped people when they needed him. He wore a red hat and drove a big red truck. Everyone liked him because he was a good helper. One day, a little cat was stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and started to tremble. The fireman saw the cat and knew he had to help. He got his big ladder and climbed up to save the cat. The little cat was happy to see the fireman. The fireman picked up the cat and climbed down the ladder. He gave the cat back to its owner. The owner was happy and thanked the fireman. The fireman smiled and drove away in his big red truck. The cat was safe, and everyone knew they could count on the fireman to help.
