Category: Intermediate
Title: The Judge and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind judge. The judge helped people with their problems. One day, a big boy named Tom came to the judge. He was sad because he felt guilty. Tom had taken a toy from a little girl named Sue. The judge told Tom that he needed to complete a task to make things right. Tom must find Sue, say sorry, and give her toy back. This would make both Tom and Sue happy again. Tom went to find Sue and said sorry. He gave her toy back, and Sue smiled. Tom learned that it is important to say sorry when you do something wrong. The moral of the story is that saying sorry can help make things right when we make mistakes.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play ball and run around in his yard. One day, while Spot was playing, he saw a big ball up on the roof. He wanted to get the ball down, but he didn't know how. Spot had an idea. He found a long stick and tried to strike the ball with it. He jumped and jumped, but he couldn't reach the ball. Spot was sad because he couldn't get the ball down. Then, something unexpected happened! A big bird flew down and picked up the ball with its beak. The bird dropped the ball right in front of Spot. Spot was so happy and surprised! He thanked the bird and they played ball together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a soft cat named Penny. Penny loved to play with her ball and run around the house. One day, Penny saw a big sign in the yard. The sign had a picture of a cat on it. Penny's friend, a little boy named Tim, saw the sign too. He asked his mom, "What does the sign say?" His mom said, "It says, 'Lost cat. If you find her, please call this number.'" Tim looked at Penny and said, "Penny, that cat looks like you!" Penny knew that she was not lost, but she wanted to help find the lost cat. She went around the neighborhood with Tim, looking for the cat in the picture. They asked people if they had seen the cat, but no one had. Finally, they found the cat hiding under a bush. The cat was scared, but Penny was soft and gentle, so the cat came out. Tim called the number on the sign, and the cat's owner came to pick her up. The owner was so happy to see her cat again. She gave Penny and Tim a big hug and said, "Thank you for finding my cat!" Penny and Tim were happy they could help, and they went back home to play together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a favorite chair that was red and soft. Mia loved to sit on the chair and read her books. One day, Mia's mommy asked her to shut the door. Mia was an obedient girl, so she said, "Okay, mommy!" Mia went to the door and shut it gently. Later, Mia's friend, Sam, came to visit. They played with toys and talked. Sam saw the red chair and asked, "Can I sit on the chair?" Mia said, "Yes, you can!" They took turns sitting on the chair and had a fun day together.

One day, a colorful deer went to the forest. The deer saw a big tree with many leaves. The tree was very tall and had a hole in it. The colorful deer looked in the hole and saw a small cup. The cup was empty. The deer said, "I will fill the cup with water for my friends." So, the deer went to the river and filled the cup with water. The deer brought the cup back to the tree. The other animals saw the water and said, "Thank you, colorful deer, for filling the cup!" They all drank the water and played together in the forest. The colorful deer was happy to help its friends.
