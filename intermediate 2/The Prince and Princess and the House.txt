Category: Intermediate
Title: The Prince and Princess and the House

Once upon a time there was a prince and a princess. The prince wanted to marry the princess, but she said she wouldn't marry him until he cleaned up his house. The prince thought this was very strange but he nevertheless tidied his house as best he could and when he was done, he invited the princess over to see his work in progress. The princess frowned as she looked around the room. There was a skeleton hidden in the corner. "Why haven't you tidied this room?," she asked. The prince replied, "I didn't know the skeleton was there". The princess nodded and said, "Take care of what you haven't noticed and you will have a tidy house. This is true for both houses and minds. If you take care of your mind, your thoughts will be tidy". The prince thanked the princess for her wisdom. He was so lucky to find such a wise princess to marry. From them on he took care of both his house and mind and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to gather toys and play with them. One day, Fluffy found a memory box with a picture of a big dog. Fluffy was scared of the big dog in the memory. Fluffy did not want the big dog to take the toys. So, Fluffy hid the toys under the bed. But the toys wanted to play with Fluffy. The toys talked to Fluffy and said, "We want to play with you. The big dog is not here." Fluffy listened to the toys and felt better. Fluffy and the toys played together and had lots of fun. The big dog stayed in the memory, and Fluffy was not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a sleepy cat named Max. One day, Tim and Max went for a walk. Max wore a collar with a shiny buckle. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As they walked, Max saw a bird in the tree. Max looked at the bird and began to wave his tail. Tim saw Max wave his tail and knew Max wanted to catch the bird. Tim thought, "I will help Max get the bird when I climb the tree." Tim started to climb the tree. Max sat and watched Tim go up. When Tim got to the top, he saw the bird fly away. Tim felt sad for Max. He climbed back down and hugged Max. As they walked back home, Max saw another bird on the ground. Max ran and caught the bird. Tim was happy for Max. They both went home, and Max showed off his bird to everyone. In the end, Tim's climb up the tree helped Max catch the bird after all.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree with many shapes hanging from it. The shapes looked very pretty. One shape was a star, another was a heart, and there was even a moon shape. Mia was very happy to see them. A squirrel came and said, "Hi, Mia! I hang these shapes on the tree. But I was careless and dropped one shape on the ground. Can you help me find it?" Mia nodded her head and started to look for the missing shape. Together, Mia and the squirrel searched the park. They found the shape near the swings. It was a beautiful sun shape. Mia picked it up and gave it to the squirrel. The squirrel was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mia!" Then, the squirrel hung the sun shape back on the tree. The tree looked even prettier now. Mia smiled and waved goodbye to her new friend, the squirrel, and went back home.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park with their bikes. They liked to ride fast and pretend they were cars. They had small cards with their names on them that they called licenses. They put them on their bikes to show they were good drivers. One day, a big dog came running towards them. It was black and hairy and had sharp teeth. It barked loudly and chased them. Ben and Lily were scared. They tried to pedal faster, but the dog was faster. They trembled and cried for help. "Go away, bad dog!" Ben shouted. "We have licenses, you can't catch us!" But the dog did not listen. It jumped and bit Ben's license. It tore it apart and spat it out. Then it looked at Lily's license. It wanted to bite that too. "Help, help!" Lily screamed. "Mommy, Daddy, where are you?" Just then, their parents came running. They saw the dog and the children. They shouted at the dog and scared it away. They hugged Ben and Lily and checked if they were hurt. "Are you okay, sweeties?" Mommy asked. "Yes, Mommy, we're okay," Ben said. "But the dog ate my license. It was my favorite." "I'm sorry, Ben," Daddy said. "But don't worry, we can make you a new one. And we can give you a tasty treat to make you feel better. How about some ice cream?" "Yay, ice cream!" Lily said. "Can I have chocolate?" "Of course, you can," Mommy said. "And you, Ben, what flavor do you want?" "I want strawberry," Ben said. "And I want a new license with a star on it." "Okay, then," Daddy said. "Let's go get some ice cream and a new license. And let's stay away from that bad dog." Ben and Lily smiled and held their parents' hands. They were happy and safe. They forgot about the dog and thought about the ice cream and the new license. They hoped they would be tasty and shiny.
