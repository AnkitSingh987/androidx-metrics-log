Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Dog

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She wore her favorite blouse. It was red with white dots. Sue liked to listen to the birds sing in the trees. One day, while Sue was playing, she saw a big, dangerous dog. The dog growled at her. Sue was scared. "Go away, dog!" Sue said. But the dog did not go away. It came closer. Just then, a bird flew down from the trees. It started to sing loudly. The dog stopped and listened to the bird. The bird sang a pretty song, and the dog forgot about Sue. The dog became nice and went away. Sue was happy and thanked the bird. She went back to play, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a sister named Lily. Mia and Lily liked to play together. One day, they decided to bathe their toy ducks in a big tub of water. They were very excited. While they were playing, Mia did a stupid thing. She splashed water all over the floor. Lily laughed and splashed water too. The floor was very wet. They didn't know that something unexpected was about to happen. Suddenly, the wet floor made a big rainbow appear in their room. Mia and Lily were very surprised. They had never seen a rainbow inside before. The rainbow made them happy, and they forgot about the wet floor. From that day on, Mia and Lily always remembered the magic rainbow when they played together.

One day, a flexible cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a taxi on the road. Tom wanted to go for a ride, so he jumped into the taxi. The driver was a big dog named Max. Tom was scared of dogs, but he wanted to be brave. As they drove, Tom started to struggle. He was still scared of Max. Suddenly, the taxi stopped. Tom thought Max would be mad, but Max just smiled. He gave Tom a big hug. They became best friends. From that day on, Tom and Max went on many rides together in their taxi. They were happy and had lots of fun. They learned that even though they were different, they could still be friends.

Molly was very excited. She was going to have steak for dinner! She could smell it cooking, so she tip-toed to the kitchen and had a peek. But when she opened the door, she saw all her mom's dinner plates were empty! Molly was so sorry. Where was her dinner? She looked in the fridge and in the cupboards. But still, no steak! She decided to look in the garden. Molly tiptoed outside and peeked around. And there, under the apple tree, she saw something special. It was a big, juicy steak! Molly clutched the steak tightly with her little hands and ran into the kitchen. Her mom was so surprised! Molly was so happy she got to eat the steak all by herself.

One summer day, Tom and Sam went to play. The sun was hot, and the sky was gray. They saw a big tree with a swing. "Let's play on the swing!" said Tom. Sam went on the swing first. He went high and fast. Tom was happy to see Sam have fun. Then, it was Tom's turn. But when Tom got on the swing, he felt a slap on his leg. "Ouch!" he cried. A little bug had slapped Tom. Sam got off the swing to help. "Don't be mad at the bug," said Sam. "It didn't know better." Tom felt better and they played nicely with the bug. The bug was happy too. The unexpected slap made Tom and Sam learn to be kind to all, even the little bugs. The moral of the story is to be kind and understand others, no matter how small they are.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was an obedient dog. He always did what his owner, Tim, said. Tim and Spot liked to play at the park. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park. They saw a bench. Tim sat on the bench and told Spot to watch. Spot watched as Tim threw a ball. Spot ran and got the ball. He brought it back to Tim. They played and played until it was time to go home. Spot was a good dog and went back to the bench when Tim called him. Tim and Spot walked home, happy after a fun day at the park.
