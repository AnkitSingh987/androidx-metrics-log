Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Gate

Sara and Tom are going to the zoo with Mom and Dad. They are very happy to see the animals. They have tickets in their hands. Mom says, "Don't lose your tickets, or you can't go in." They walk to the gate. There is a man who checks the tickets. He has a big scissors. He cuts a small piece from each ticket. Sara and Tom watch him. They wonder why he does that. "Excuse me, why do you cut the tickets?" Sara asks. The man smiles. He says, "I cut the tickets to show that you have paid. This way, you can't use the same ticket again. It's a rule." Sara and Tom nod. They understand. They say, "Thank you." They go inside the zoo. They see lions, monkeys, elephants, and more. They have a lot of fun. They are glad they have their tickets. They don't want to miss anything.

Once upon a time, there was a small car named Red. Red was very honest and always tried to help others. One day, Red was driving down the road when he saw a big truck stuck in the mud. Red wanted to help the truck, so he tried to pull the truck out of the mud. He pulled and pulled, but the truck did not move. Red was sad because he could not help the truck. The truck was also sad because it could not release itself from the mud. Then, a strong tow truck came down the road. The tow truck saw Red and the stuck truck and wanted to help. The tow truck pulled the big truck out of the mud. Red was so happy that the truck was free. The truck thanked Red and the tow truck for being honest and helpful friends. And they all drove away, happy and smiling.

Once upon a time, there was a huge cat. The cat had no name. He lived in a big house with a little girl named Lily. Lily loved the cat very much. She played with him every day. They had lots of fun together. One day, Lily said to the cat, "You need a name. I will call you Mr. Whiskers." The cat liked his new name. He was very happy. Mr. Whiskers and Lily played all day. They ran and jumped on the floor. They laughed and had so much fun. As the sun went down, Lily and Mr. Whiskers were very tired. They lay down on the soft floor together. Lily gave Mr. Whiskers a big hug. "Good night, Mr. Whiskers," she said. "I am glad you have a name now." And they both fell asleep, dreaming of more fun days together.

One day, a little girl named Lily found her mom's makeup. She saw the pretty colors and wanted to play with it. Lily thought she could make her face look impressive like her mom. She put on red lips, blue eyes, and pink cheeks. Lily was very happy with her new look. Lily wanted to show her mom her impressive face. She ran to find her mom in the kitchen. But, as she was running, she did not see the water on the floor. Lily's foot went on the water and she started to slip. She fell down on her bottom and it hurt a little. Lily's mom heard the noise and came to help her. She saw Lily's face with the makeup and smiled. Mom hugged Lily and told her to be careful when walking. She said that Lily looked impressive, but it was important to be safe too. They cleaned the makeup and water together, and Lily learned to be more careful.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little girl named Lily. She loved to travel with her mom and dad. One day, they went to the beach. The beach had lots of sand. Lily liked to play in the sand. She made a big sand castle with her mom and dad. A little boy came up to Lily. He said, "Hi, my name is Tom. Can I play with you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, you can help us make our sand castle bigger." So, Lily, Tom, and Lily's mom and dad all worked together. They made the biggest sand castle on the beach. After they finished the sand castle, Lily and Tom played in the water. They splashed and had fun. The sun began to set, and it was time for Lily and her family to go home. Lily said goodbye to Tom and they promised to play together again soon. Lily and her family traveled back home, and she couldn't wait for their next adventure.
