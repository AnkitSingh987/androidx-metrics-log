Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sand

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in the sand at the park. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He was very happy and could not wait to build a big sandcastle. As Tim played in the sand, he saw a little girl. She looked anxious and sad. Tim asked her what was wrong. The little girl said she lost her toy in the sand. Tim wanted to help her find it. Tim and the little girl started to dig in the sand together. They looked and looked until they found the toy. The little girl was so happy, she wanted to pay Tim with a big hug. Tim felt very proud and happy that he could help his new friend. They played in the sand together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, a bossy cat lived in a little house. The cat had a chain around its neck. One sunny day, the cat went outside to play. The bossy cat met a dog. The dog said, "Why do you have a chain?" The cat said, "I must remain near my house. The chain helps me remember." The dog and the cat played together. They had fun. The cat learned to be less bossy. They became good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim found an icy tube in his yard. He did not know what it was, so he picked it up to look at it. It was cold and slippery. Tim was very curious about the tube. Tim showed the icy tube to his friend, Sam. Sam wanted to keep the tube, but Tim said no. They started to get mad at each other. They did not want to share the icy tube. This made them both sad. Then, Tim had an idea. They could take turns with the icy tube. Tim would have it one day, and Sam would have it the next day. They both liked the idea and started to play with the icy tube together. They were happy friends again.

Tommy and Sally were playing in their room. Tommy had a toy truck and Sally had a toy doll. Suddenly, Tommy's truck stopped working. He was so sad. He didn't know what to do. Sally said "Don't worry, Tommy. I can help you replace it!" Tommy was very surprised. He said, "How can you replace it?" Sally smiled and said, "I know a man who has lots of toys. He is very friendly and he has lots of spare parts. We can go to his store and get a new part for your truck". Tommy replied, "Will it be the same size as my truck? I need the part to fit perfectly!" Sally laughed and said, "Yes it will! The man measures things in inches, so he can make sure the part is the same size". So Sally and Tommy went to the store. The store was very big and lively. They had so much fun looking for the perfect part for Tommy's truck. In the end, they found the perfect one. The friendly man replaced the old part and soon, the truck was back to life! Tommy was very happy, and he thanked Sally for helping him.

One day, a girl named Sue found a big bottle of glue. She liked to use glue to stick things together. Sue had an idea. She wanted to make a pretty picture with the glue and some colorful paper. Sue started to put glue on the paper. But, she smelled something spicy. She did not recognize the smell. It was not the glue. Where was the smell coming from? Sue looked around and saw a big red pepper on the table. The spicy smell was coming from the pepper! Sue had a fun idea. She used the glue to stick the red pepper on her picture. Now, her picture had a spicy smell too! Sue showed her picture to her mom. Her mom was surprised to see the red pepper on the picture. They both laughed and enjoyed the spicy twist in Sue's art.
