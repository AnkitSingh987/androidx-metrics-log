Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yummy Cake and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a yummy cake. It had a pretty pattern on it. The cake was in a big kitchen. A little girl named Lily loved the cake. She wanted to eat it all by herself. One day, the cake vanished. Lily was sad and looked everywhere for the cake. She asked her mom, dad, and brother if they knew where the cake was. No one knew where the yummy cake had gone. Lily was about to give up when she saw her dog, Spot, hiding under the table. Spot had cake crumbs all around him. Lily knew that Spot had eaten the cake. She was upset at first, but then she laughed. Spot had a funny pattern on his face from the cake. Lily and her family made a new cake together, and they all shared it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a cold place with lots of snow. One day, he wanted to make an igloo. So, he asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam was very smart. He said, "I recommend we make the igloo big, so we can play inside." Tim was grateful for Sam's idea. They started to build the igloo together. They used snow and ice to make it strong. When they finished, they went inside the igloo to play. Suddenly, they heard a noise. They looked around and saw a big, friendly bear! The bear wanted to play with them too. Tim and Sam were scared at first, but then they saw the bear was nice. They all played together in the igloo and became good friends. The unexpected visitor made their day even more fun!

One day, a little girl named Mia went out to play. The sky was blue, and the sun was bright. Mia liked to jump and run in her big, green yard. She saw her friend, the small, red bird. "Hello, bird!" said Mia. But then, it started to rain. Mia felt the cold water on her knee. "Oh no, rain!" she said. The bird flew to a tree to stay dry. Mia ran to her house. She was wet and her clothes did not fit well. They were too tight on her. Mia's mom saw her and said, "Let's get you dry and find clothes that fit." Mia put on her warm, dry clothes. The rain stopped, and the sun came out again. Mia went back outside to play. The bird flew back to her and said, "Now we can play again!" And they played until it was time for dinner.

Once there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to do lots of things and was very helpful. One day she went to the shop to buy an ice-cream. She was very excited. When Lily arrived at the shop, she lifted the ice-cream from the shelf and tried to carry it to the cashier. But it was too heavy for her! She struggled and tried to lift it but it was no use. She started to cry. Just then, a kind boy named George came over to help. He was very helpful. He lifted the heavy ice-cream for Lily and walked with her to the cashier. When they got to the cashier, Lily was happy. She thanked George and they both enjoyed their ice-creams. Lily was very grateful for George’s help and they became good friends.

Once there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with her toys. One day, Amy's aunt came to visit. She was very nice and always alert. She liked to play with Amy too. Amy and her aunt went to the park. They saw a big box. "Let's open it!" said Amy. Her aunt said, "Okay, let's open it together!" They were very excited to see what was inside. They opened the box and saw a big, angry cat. The cat jumped out and scared them. They ran away, but the cat chased them. They tried to hide, but the cat found them. In the end, the cat took Amy's toy and ran away. Amy and her aunt were sad, but they learned to be careful when opening boxes they didn't know.
