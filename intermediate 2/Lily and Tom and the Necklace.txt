Title: Lily and Tom and the Necklace

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play with toys and draw pictures. One day, Lily had a big surprise for Tom. She made a card for him with a symbol on it. A symbol is a shape that means something. "Look, Tom, this is for you!" Lily said, giving him the card. "It is a heart. A heart means love. I love you, Tom. You are my best friend." Tom smiled and hugged Lily. He liked the card very much. He saw the heart symbol and knew what it meant. He loved Lily too. He wanted to give her something back. "Thank you, Lily. This is a very nice card. You are very kind. I have something for you too." Tom said, looking in his backpack. He found a small box and opened it. Inside was a necklace with a symbol on it. A symbol is a shape that means something. "Look, Lily, this is for you!" Tom said, giving her the necklace. "It is a star. A star means wish. I wish you happiness, Lily. You are my best friend." Lily gasped and put on the necklace. She liked the necklace very much. She saw the star symbol and knew what it meant. She wished Tom happiness too. He gave her something very unusual. Unusual means not like other things. "Thank you, Tom. This is a very nice necklace. You are very clever. I have never seen anything like this." Lily said, admiring the necklace. "It is a star. A star means wish. I wish you happiness, Tom. You are my best friend." They hugged and smiled. They were very happy. They had received symbols of love and wish from each other. They were best friends.

Once upon a time there lived a crazy girl called May. She loved to wash her post every day. One day, May was washing her post when she heard a voice from behind her. She jumped and turned around to find her friend Ben. "What are you doing, May?" Ben asked. May smiled and said, "I'm washing my post. I like to keep it clean!" Ben thought it was very funny that May was washing her post. He laughed and said, "That's crazy!" May laughed too. She knew it was crazy but she just couldn't help it. She loved washing her post and she would do it every day. The end.

One day, a gifted boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to joke and play with his friends. Today, he brought a bottle of bubbles to play with. At the park, Tim blew big bubbles into the air. His friends laughed and tried to catch the bubbles. They were having so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bubble did not pop when Tim's friend touched it. Instead, it grew bigger and bigger! The big bubble picked up Tim and his friends, and they went on a fun ride in the sky. They laughed and joked as they floated around. The big bubble was a fun surprise!

One day, a charming cat named Kitty went out to play. Kitty loved to have fun with her friends. Today, she met a big dog named Max. Max was nice, but he liked to shrug a lot. Kitty thought it was funny. Kitty and Max played all day. They ran and jumped in the sun. They were very happy. But, the sky began to get dark. Kitty and Max didn't notice because they were having too much fun. The dark sky meant a big storm was coming. Kitty and Max didn't know that. They kept playing and laughing. Then, the rain came down very fast. Kitty and Max were wet and cold. They tried to find a place to hide, but it was too late. The storm was very bad, and Kitty and Max were sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pretty bucket that she loved very much. The bucket was attractive with many colors, like red, blue, and yellow. One sunny day, Sue wanted to gather some pretty rocks from the park. Sue went to the park with her mom. She saw many rocks on the ground. Sue said, "Mom, can I gather these rocks in my bucket?" Her mom said, "Yes, Sue, you can. But only the pretty ones." Sue was very happy. She gathered many pretty rocks in her attractive bucket. She showed her mom all the pretty rocks she found. Her mom was proud of her. They went home and put the rocks in a special place. Sue loved her pretty bucket and the pretty rocks she found.
