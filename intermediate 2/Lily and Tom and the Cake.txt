Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Cake

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play at the park. They liked to swing, run and slide on the big slide. One day, they saw a cake on a table near the slide. It was a big cake with pink frosting and sprinkles. It looked yummy. "Who do you think the cake is for?" Lily asked Tom. "I don't know. Maybe it is for a birthday party," Tom said. "Can we have some?" Lily wondered. Tom looked around. He did not see anyone near the cake. He felt hungry. "Maybe we can have a little bit. No one will notice," he said. He walked to the table and took a knife. He cut a small piece of cake and gave it to Lily. He cut another piece for himself. They ate the cake. It was sweet and soft. They heard a voice behind them. It was a woman. She looked angry. "Hey, what are you doing? That is my cake! I made it for my daughter's birthday! How dare you steal it!" she shouted. Lily and Tom felt scared. They dropped the cake and ran away. They ran to the slide and slid down. They hoped the woman would not catch them. They ran to their moms, who were sitting on a bench. They told them what happened. Their moms looked worried. "That was very wrong of you. You should not take things that are not yours. You should say sorry to the woman and her daughter. You hurt their feelings and ruined their cake," their moms said. Lily and Tom felt sorry. They knew they did a bad thing. They walked back to the woman and her daughter. They said sorry and gave them a hug. The woman and her daughter forgave them. They invited them to join the party and share the cake. Lily and Tom learned a lesson. They learned to respect other people's things and to be honest. They also learned to be kind and make new friends. They had fun at the party and enjoyed the cake. They thanked the woman and her daughter for being nice to them. They promised to never steal again.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. It lived in a group with other rocks. They all liked to play in the sun. One day, a little boy came. He saw the smooth rock and said, "Hi, rock! Can you read?" The smooth rock did not say anything, but the little boy took it home. At home, the little boy read a book to the smooth rock. The smooth rock liked the story. It felt happy to listen to the little boy. The next day, the little boy took the smooth rock back to its group. The smooth rock told the other rocks about the story. They all wanted to hear it too. So, the little boy read the story to the group of rocks. They all had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little horse named Max. Max lived on a farm with many animals. Max loved to play and run in the fields. He was a very calm horse and liked to make friends with everyone. One day, Max saw a big rock in the field. He wanted to jump over it. Max took a few steps back and then sprang high into the air. He jumped over the rock and landed safely on the other side. All the animals clapped and cheered for Max. Max felt so happy that he could help his friends. He learned that being calm and trying new things can make everyone happy. And from that day on, Max and his friends played together all the time, and they all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to always be calm and try new things, because it can bring happiness to you and others around you.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to play and swing on the flexible vines all day. He had many friends, like the birds, the bugs, and the other animals in the forest. One sunny day, Momo saw a big bird send a little bird away. The little bird was sad and could not play with the others. Momo felt bad and wanted to help the little bird. He thought of a plan to make the little bird happy again. Momo climbed up the tree and found a long, flexible vine. He held the vine and swung to the little bird. Momo said, "Come, little bird! Swing with me on this vine, and we will have fun together!" The little bird was so happy and joined Momo. They played and laughed all day long. The big bird saw Momo and the little bird having fun. The big bird felt sorry for sending the little bird away. The big bird flew down and said, "I am sorry, little bird. I want to play with you too." The little bird forgave the big bird, and they all played together. The moral of the story is to be kind and include everyone in our play. When we are nice to others, we can have more fun and make more friends.
