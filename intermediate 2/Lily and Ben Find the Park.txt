Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Park

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to dig holes in the sand and bury their toys. They had a lot of fun making pretend treasure maps and finding their hidden things. One day, they saw a shiny chain on the ground. It was very pretty and had many colors. Lily wanted to keep it, but Ben said they should bury it and make a new map. Lily agreed, but she was a little sad. She liked the chain very much. They dug a deep hole and put the chain inside. Then they covered it with sand and drew a map on a piece of paper. They ran to the slide and slid down. They were very happy. But then, a tall man came to the park. He had a big bag and a shovel. He looked around and saw the chain sticking out of the sand. He smiled and walked towards it. He did not see Lily and Ben. "Hey, that's our chain!" Lily shouted. She and Ben ran to the hole and tried to stop the man. But the man was too big and strong. He pushed them away and grabbed the chain. He put it in his bag and ran away. Lily and Ben were very sad and angry. They cried and hugged each other. They wished they had kept the chain or given it to someone nice. A lady who was sitting on a bench saw what happened. She felt sorry for Lily and Ben. She walked to them and gave them a hug. She said, "I'm sorry, children. That was a very bad man. He stole your chain. He should not do that. It is wrong to take things that are not yours." She took out a box from her purse and opened it. Inside, there were two bracelets made of beads. They were very pretty and had many colors. She said, "These are for you. I made them myself. They are not as shiny as the chain, but they are more special. They are made with love. You can keep them or share them with your friends. They will always remind you of how kind and brave you are." Lily and Ben smiled and thanked the lady. They put on the bracelets and felt happy. They learned that some things are more valuable than others. And they learned to be careful with their things and not to bury them in the sand.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He was a very patient boy. Tom had a friend named Sally. One day, Tom and Sally were playing in the park. It was a sunny Wednesday. Tom had an idea. He said, "Suggest a game, Sally!" Sally thought for a moment and said, "Let's play hide and seek!" So, they started to play. Tom closed his eyes and began to count, while Sally ran to hide. Tom looked and looked for Sally. He was very patient but could not find her. Suddenly, a big dog came running up to Tom. The dog was wagging its tail and had a note in its mouth. The note was from Sally! She had decided to play a trick on Tom. The note said, "I am hiding at home! Come find me there!" Tom laughed and ran to Sally's house to find her. The game was even more fun with the unexpected twist.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Big. He lived in a big house and had many things. One day, Mr. Big wanted to order a toy for his friend's child. But the toy seller only spoke a different language. Mr. Big felt sad because he could not talk to the toy seller. Then, a little girl named Amy came to help. She knew the language of the toy seller. Amy told the toy seller what Mr. Big wanted. The toy seller smiled and gave the toy to Mr. Big. Mr. Big was very happy and thanked Amy for her help. The moral of the story is that we should learn different languages to help others. It does not matter if you are wealthy or not, knowing more languages can bring people together and make them happy.
