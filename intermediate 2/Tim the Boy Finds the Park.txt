Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, interesting watch. The watch was red and blue. Tim loved his watch very much. One day, Tim was playing in the park. He saw a girl named Lily. Lily was sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad?" Lily said, "I lost my toy." Tim wanted to help Lily find her toy. Tim looked at his interesting watch. He called out, "Watch, help us find the toy!" The watch made a beep sound. Tim and Lily followed the sound. They found the toy under a big tree. Lily was happy. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a purple bird named Polly. Polly loved to sing and clap her wings. She lived in a big tree with her family. They were all very happy. One day, Polly saw a little bug trying to climb up the tree. The bug was very small and kept having falls. Polly wanted to help, so she flew down and said, "Hi, little bug! I see you are trying to climb up the tree. Can I help you?" The little bug looked up at Polly and said, "Yes, please! I want to see the top of the tree, but I keep falling." Polly gently picked up the bug with her beak and carried him to the top of the tree. The little bug was so happy and thanked Polly for her help. Together, Polly and the little bug enjoyed the view from the top of the tree. They clapped and sang songs together. The other birds and bugs saw them and joined in the fun. Everyone was so happy, and they all learned a valuable lesson: when you help others, you make the world a happier place.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty cat named Luna. She loved to play with her toy tube. One day, Luna rolled her tube outside to play. While playing, something unexpected happened. The tube rolled away into the woods. Luna was sad, but she went into the woods to find her tube. She looked everywhere, but could not find it. Then, she saw a big dog holding her tube in his mouth. Luna was scared, but she asked the dog if she could have her tube back. The big dog said he was sorry for taking her tube. He did not know it was hers. Luna decided to forgive the dog. To her surprise, the dog wanted to play with her and the tube. They became friends and played with the tube together. From that day on, Luna and the dog shared the toy and had lots of fun in the woods.

One day, a clever cat named Tom went for a walk. He blinked his big eyes as he saw a big chest under a tree. Tom was very curious and wanted to see what was inside the chest. Tom jumped on the chest and tried to open it. Suddenly, a small bird flew down and landed on the chest too. The bird said, "Hi, I am Sam. What are you doing?" Tom replied, "I want to see what's inside this big chest." Together, Tom and Sam opened the chest. They were very happy to find lots of toys and treats inside. They played and shared the treats with each other. From that day on, Tom and Sam became the best of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a park with his family. They wanted to have a fun day together. Tim's dad brought a grill to cook food. Tim's mom brought a ball to play with. Tim was very excited. Tim's dad put the grill on the grass. He started to cook hot dogs and hamburgers. Tim and his mom played with the ball. They kicked it and threw it. Tim and his mom admired how good his dad was at cooking on the grill. They were all very happy. After they ate, Tim felt proud of his dad. He thought his dad was successful at cooking. Tim wanted to be like his dad when he grew up. They all had a great day at the park, and Tim loved his family very much.
