Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cool Boy and the Towel

Once upon a time, there was a cool boy. He was out walking one day and he found a surprise! It was a big, soft, fluffy towel. He was so excited and he quickly took it home. When he arrived home he showed the towel to his mom. His mom smiled and said, "That's a very cool towel, we can use it for the beach tomorrow!" The cool boy was filled with joy. He couldn't wait to use the towel. The next day, he took the towel to the beach and everything was great. But when it was time to go home, he noticed the towel was gone! He searched around but he couldn't find it anywhere. He started to cry and his mom said, "Oh no! That was a big surprise! Someone must have taken it away! You'll just have to find a new towel now." The cool boy was very sad and disappointed. He hadn't expected this bad ending!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had an unusual couch in her home. The couch had big, round eyes and a smile. Sue loved to sit on the couch and talk to it. One day, Sue asked the couch, "Do you want to play with me?" The couch nodded its head and said, "Yes, Sue, I would love to play with you!" So, they went outside to play in the sun. Sue and the couch played a game of tag. Sue would run and the couch would chase her. They laughed and had so much fun. After playing, they went back inside and sat down. Sue hugged the couch and said, "You're the best friend ever!"

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little bird named Blue. Blue lived in a big tree with his family. They were all very happy together. One day, a big wind blew a hammer into their tree. The hammer was stuck! Blue and his family tried to get it out, but they couldn't. They were very sad. Then, Blue had an idea. He pushed and pushed the hammer with his beak. With one big push, the hammer escaped from the tree! Blue and his family were so happy. They sang and danced together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a polite little bee named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to visit flowers and help them bloom. One day, Buzzy found a special flower that smelled like mint. "Hello, mint flower!" said Buzzy. "May I help you bloom?" "Yes, please!" the mint flower replied with a smile. Buzzy buzzed around the mint flower, helping it to bloom. The mint flower grew big and bright. Buzzy and the mint flower became good friends. They spent their days making the world a more beautiful place.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. She had a doll that she loved very much. The doll had a hard head, but Amy didn't mind. She took the doll with her everywhere she went. One day, Amy's friend, Tim, came to play with her. They played with the doll and had lots of fun. Tim said, "Let's joke with the doll!" They made the doll say funny things and laughed a lot. After playing, they were both very tired. Amy said, "I'm happy we had fun, but it's time to put the doll away." Tim didn't listen and hid the doll instead. Amy looked everywhere, but she couldn't find her doll. She was very sad and started to cry. Tim saw Amy cry and felt bad. He said, "I'm sorry, Amy. I hid the doll as a joke, but I see it made you sad." Tim gave the doll back to Amy. They both learned that jokes can be fun, but we should always think about how our jokes might make others feel.

Once upon a time, in a gray world, there lived a cat and a dog. They were friends and played together every day. The cat was small and gray. The dog was big and brown. They liked to run and jump and hide in the world. One day, the cat said, "Let's play a new game!" The dog agreed and they thought of a game. The cat would hide in the world and the dog would look for the cat. The dog looked and looked, but he could not find the cat anywhere. The dog was sad and said, "I give up. Where are you, cat?" The cat jumped out from behind a tree and said, "Here I am!" The dog was happy to see the cat. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat said, "I don't want to play anymore." The dog was surprised and asked, "Why not?" The cat said, "I refuse to play because I want to be the one who looks for you." The dog thought for a moment and said, "Okay, let's switch roles. You look for me and I will hide." The cat agreed, and they continued to play their new game. In the end, both the cat and the dog were happy and had lots of fun in their gray world.
