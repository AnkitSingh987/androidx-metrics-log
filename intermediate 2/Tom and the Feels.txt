Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Feels

Tom likes math. He likes to count and add and take away. He likes to draw shapes and colors. He likes to play with blocks and puzzles. Math is fun for Tom. But today, Tom has to do a math test. He has to write numbers and answers on a paper. He has to do it fast and right. He has to do it by himself. Tom does not like math tests. Math tests are hard and scary for Tom. Tom feels frightened. He feels his heart beat fast and his hands sweat. He feels his head hurt and his eyes water. He feels like he wants to run away and hide. He feels like he wants to cry and scream. He feels like he does not know anything. Tom looks at the paper. He sees numbers and signs and lines. He does not understand what they mean. He does not know what to do. He feels lost and alone. He feels like he is the only one who is frightened. But he is not. He looks around. He sees other kids. He sees some kids write and smile. He sees some kids think and frown. He sees some kids look and sigh. He sees some kids who are also frightened. He sees some kids who are like him. Tom feels a little better. He feels like he is not the only one who is frightened. He feels like he is not the only one who has to do a math test. He feels like he is not the only one who can try. He feels like he can do math. He feels like he can do the math test. Tom picks up his pencil. He looks at the paper. He tries to remember what he learned. He tries to count and add and take away. He tries to draw shapes and colors. He tries to play with blocks and puzzles. He tries to do math. He tries to do the math test. Tom feels proud. He feels like he did his best. He feels like he learned something. He feels like he had fun. He feels like he likes math. He feels like he likes math tests. Math tests are not hard and scary for Tom. Math tests are fun and easy for Tom.

Billy was walking down the street with his mom. He saw something that made him stop and ask what it was. His mom said it was a telephone and asked him if he wanted to drink from it. Billy found the idea strange, but he was curious and said yes. They walked closer to the telephone and he saw it was white. As they walked closer, he saw something coming out of it. He asked his mom what it was and she said it was water. Jumping up and down with excitement, he asked if he could drink it. His mom nodded and told him to go ahead. He eagerly took a sip of the cold water. It was refreshing! From then on, Billy would always stop by the white telephone whenever he was with his mom. He was sure that the telephone would provide him with a refreshing drink when he needed it. Little did he know that the telephone was really a drinking fountain!

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Mo and Zee. They lived close to each other and loved to play. One day, Mo and Zee decided to have a picnic by the lake. They gathered some food and blankets and took off for the lake. Once they arrived to the lake, they both jumped for joy and spread out the blankets. Then Mo said to Zee, “Let’s gather some flowers for our picnic.” So, they grabbed a basket and picked some beautiful flowers in the meadow. When their basket was full, Mo and Zee admired their handiwork. They smiled brightly and gathered up their things to go back home. On the way, Mo noticed a bumblebee buzzing around a flower. He pointed to it with his finger and said, “Look Zee! A bee gathering nectar from the flower. It's so happy!” Zee’s eyes were wide with wonder. She reached out with her finger to touch the bee's lip and said, “It tickles!” Mo and Zee laughed and enjoyed the rest of the way home. They were happy to have shared such an amazing adventure together.
