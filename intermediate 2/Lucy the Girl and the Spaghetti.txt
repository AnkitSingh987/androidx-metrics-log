Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Spaghetti

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lucy. Lucy loved spaghetti. She ate spaghetti every day. One day, Lucy had a big problem. She ran out of spaghetti! Lucy was very sad. She wanted to eat spaghetti again. She asked her friend Tom for help. Tom was a nice boy who liked to help. Tom said, "Let's go to the store and buy some spaghetti!" At the store, they found a big box of spaghetti. Lucy was very happy. She said, "I want to marry spaghetti because I love it so much!" Tom laughed and said, "You can't marry spaghetti, but you can eat it every day." Lucy and Tom went home with the spaghetti. They cooked it and ate it together. They were both very happy. Lucy learned that it was okay not to marry spaghetti, as long as she could eat it and share it with her nice friend Tom.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing in the park. Max saw a big fog coming. He was scared and started to tremble. Max did not like the fog because it made it hard to see. Max heard a voice in the fog. It said, "Don't be scared, Max!" Max looked around and saw a crazy-looking cat. The cat had a big hat and a funny face. Max thought the cat was nice, even though he looked crazy. The cat said, "I am a magic cat! I can make the fog go away." Max was very happy. The cat waved his tail, and the fog was gone! Max and the cat played together in the park all day. They had lots of fun and became good friends.

It was a dark night. Ollie was walking down the street, looking at all the fun things around him. He stopped in front of a big mailbox. He admired it - it was bigger and shinier than his! He put his hands on it and looked up and around at it, up and down. He wished he could have one like it! He took his hands off of it and stepped back, looking at it one last time. Then he noticed something strange - it was talking! He was scared, but listened. It said, "Come closer, I have a secret for you." Ollie took a few steps toward it, and it talked again. "When you peek inside," it said in a whisper, "you will find something special." Ollie was so excited, he moved right up to the mailbox and peeked inside. There was a box of candy, just for him! He couldn't believe it. He opened the box, and his face lit up with a big smile. He thanked the mailbox and ran home with his candy. He'd never forget the secret that the dark, shiny mailbox had told him.

There was a yellow bird that wanted to know what peace felt like. So, it hopped onto a bus and rode far away. When the bird finally arrived, it noticed that everything was very quiet. There was no yelling or any noise. It felt like all the birds, cats and dogs were happy. But then, the bird noticed something odd. All the creatures were wearing yellow masks! The bird realized that nobody could know if anyone else was smiling or not, because nobody could see each other's faces. The bird felt sad that it could not enjoy the peace it so wanted to know. It was hard to tell if the creatures were scared or if they were having fun. The bird hopped away, feeling very lonely.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big red flower. It looks pretty. "Can we pick it?" Sue asks. "I don't know," Tom says. "Maybe it is poison." "What is poison?" Sue says. "Poison is something bad. It can make you sick or hurt," Tom says. "Oh. I don't want to be sick or hurt," Sue says. "Me neither. Let's leave it alone," Tom says. But Sue is curious. She wants to learn more about the flower. She thinks it smells nice. She reaches out her hand to touch it. "Stop! Don't do that!" Tom shouts. But it is too late. Sue touches the flower. It is hot. Very hot. It burns her hand. She screams. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Sue cries. Tom runs to her. He sees her hand. It is red and swollen. He is scared. "Help! Help! Sue is hurt!" Tom yells. But no one hears them. They are alone in the park. Sue keeps crying. Tom doesn't know what to do. He wishes he had listened to his mom. She told him not to go to the park without her. He is sorry. He hugs Sue. He hopes she will be okay. But he doesn't know. He is afraid. He thinks they are in trouble. He thinks the flower is poison. He thinks they made a big mistake.
