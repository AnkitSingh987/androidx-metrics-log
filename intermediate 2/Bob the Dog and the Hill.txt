Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Dog and the Hill

One day, a little dog named Bob was out for a walk. He was a lucky dog because he had a big yard to play in. But today, he wanted to go to a new place. So, he went down the street, looking for a fun spot. As he walked, he saw a big hill. He thought, "Wow, this looks like a fun place to run and play!" So, he ran up the hill as fast as he could. But when he got to the top, he saw a big puddle of water. He didn't see it in time and he started to slip. Bob slipped down the hill and landed in a soft pile of leaves. He was scared at first, but then he saw something shiny. It was a big, shiny bone! Bob was so happy and lucky to find it. He took the bone home and showed it to his family. They all laughed and played together. And from that day on, Bob loved going to new places and finding new surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was always ready to play and have fun. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom to play with his friends. At the park, Tim saw a big ball. He wanted to hold it and play with it. So, he picked up the ball and started to play with his friends. They kicked the ball with their foot and laughed a lot. After playing for a while, Tim's mom called him. It was time to go home. Tim was happy he had so much fun at the park. He held his mom's hand and they walked home together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Milly. She was three years old and loved to play in the garden. One day, Milly's mum said to her, "Let's go on an adventure, Milly!" Milly was so excited. She put on some dry clothes and ran outside with her mum. They had a wonderful time until suddenly it started to rain. Milly's mum said, "Oh no! Quickly, let's find somewhere to hide". They ran into a nearby shop but Milly felt embarrassed because she was wet. The shopkeeper smiled and said, "It's okay, darling. I have something to help you feel better". He gave Milly a towel so she could dry her clothes. Milly was very grateful. She thanked the shopkeeper and said goodbye. She put on her dry clothes and then went outside with her mum and continued with their adventure. They never forgot the kindness of the shopkeeper. The end.

One day, a messy dog named Spot was playing in his yard. Spot loved to whistle. He would whistle all day long. His friend, Tim, came over to play with him. Tim was a little boy who lived next door. They liked to watch TV together. "Spot, can you whistle for me?" Tim asked. Spot whistled a happy tune. They laughed and played, and then went inside to watch TV. They saw a show about a bird who could also whistle. They thought it was funny. While they were watching the show, something unexpected happened. The bird on the TV started to whistle Spot's favorite tune! Spot and Tim looked at each other in surprise. "Wow, Spot! The bird knows your song!" Tim said. Spot whistled back to the bird on the TV, and the bird whistled the same tune again. They all laughed and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot lived with his best friend, Tim. They loved to play in the big field near their house. Every day, they would run, jump, and hug each other in the field. One sunny day, while they were playing in the field, they saw a big box. It was a surprise! They didn't know what was inside. Spot and Tim looked at each other and decided to open the box together. They were very excited. When they opened the box, they found a big, soft teddy bear. Spot and Tim thought it was a new friend for them. They hugged the teddy bear and played with it in the field. But then, the teddy bear started to move! It was a real bear, just a little one. The bear became their new friend, and they all hugged and played together in the field every day.
