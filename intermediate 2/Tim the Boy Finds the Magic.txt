Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Magic

One day, a boy named Tim found a rod. He thought it was a magic rod. He showed it to his friend, Sara. Sara said, "I don't think it's magic, Tim." But Tim insisted, "It is a magic rod, Sara. I know it!" Tim wanted to show Sara that the rod was magic. He said, "Rod, please do something nice for Sara." Suddenly, the rod made a pretty flower appear. Sara was happy and surprised. She said, "Wow, Tim! You were right. It is a magic rod." Later, they met a sad dog. Tim wanted to help the dog. He said, "Rod, please make the dog happy." The rod made a big bone appear. The dog was very happy. Tim and Sara were compassionate friends. They learned that the rod could make good things happen for others, too.

Mama put the poem on the wall. It was a beautiful poem with lots of colours. Mama looked happy and proud when she put it on the wall. The poem was about the importance of being obedient. It reminded us to always listen to our mamas and daddies. We should never be naughty or do bad things. We all smiled when we saw the poem. We felt so proud and happy that mama had put it up. She said it was there to remind us to always be obedient and do the right thing. Every morning when we wake up, we look at the poem and it reminds us to be good and listen to our mama and daddy. We don't want to be naughty. We want to be good and obey our parents. That's what the poem tells us to do.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He took his red spade with him. He wanted to dig in the sand. At the park, he saw a big, hairy dog. The dog was playing with a ball. Tim thought the dog was fun. "Can I play with your dog?" Tim asked the dog's owner, a nice lady. "Yes, you can," she said with a smile. So, Tim threw the ball and the dog ran to get it. They had a lot of fun. Tim then went to the sand with his spade. He started to dig a hole. The hairy dog came over and helped him. The dog dug with his paws. They made a big hole together. Tim and the dog enjoyed their day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a man who loved his motorcycle very much. He would go fast on it and make loud noises. One day, he saw a big box with the word "mix" on it. He did not know what it was, but he thought it would be fun to use with his motorcycle. The man opened the box and found a lot of colorful things inside. He put them on his motorcycle, making it look very pretty. He did not know that the colorful things were for fixing dead things, like old cars and bikes. But he thought his motorcycle looked better now, so he was very happy. The next day, the man went for a ride on his pretty motorcycle. As he was going fast, he saw a big hill. He wanted to go up the hill, but his motorcycle started to make funny noises. It was not happy with the colorful things from the "mix" box. The motorcycle stopped, and it was dead. The man was sad, but he learned that he should not put things on his motorcycle without knowing what they are for.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a small bird with a mild hurt wing. Lily wanted to help the bird. She took off her hat and put the bird in it. Lily walked with the bird and saw a tree. In the tree, there was a note. The note said, "Help me find my home." Lily realized the bird's home was in the tree. She put the bird back in the tree. The bird was happy to be home. It sang a pretty song for Lily. Lily smiled and waved goodbye to the bird. She knew she did a good thing by helping the bird.
