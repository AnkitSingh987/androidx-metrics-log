Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a shy little boy called Joe. He loved going for sails on the lake near his house, but he was so shy that he stayed in the corner of the boat and refused to talk to anyone. One day, his dad took him out on the lake and told him: " Joe, you need to be brave and talk to the other sailors. That way you can all have fun together". Joe was scared, but he agreed to try. So they sailed around the lake and Joe said hello to all the other boats. He was so shy at first, but soon he was talking, laughing, and having a great time. At the end of the day, Joe's dad looked at him and said: "See, when you try to improve, amazing things can happen". Joe smiled and nodded. The moral of the story is: when you put in the effort to improve, you can enjoy so much more.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw his shadow. It was deep and dark. He was scared. He tried to run away from it, but it followed him. "Why are you following me?" Tim asked his shadow. The shadow did not say anything. Tim went to his friend, Sue. He told her about the shadow. "I don't like my shadow. It is deep and dark. How can I make it go away?" Sue thought for a moment. "Maybe you can own it. If you own it, it will listen to you." Tim went back to his shadow. He said, "I own you. You have to do what I say." The shadow still did not talk, but it stopped being scary. Tim and his shadow played together all day. Now, Tim was not scared of his shadow anymore. They were friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his mom went to the park. They saw a big red ball near a tree. Tim ran to the ball and kicked it high in the sky. While they played, a small dog came to the park. The dog saw the red ball and wanted to play too. The dog said, "Can I play with you and the red ball?" Tim and his mom said, "Yes, come play with us!" They all played with the red ball until it was time to go home. But when they got home, they saw the dog had followed them! The dog said, "I have no home. Can I live with you?" Tim and his mom were surprised, but they said, "Yes, you can be our new friend and live with us!" And they all lived happily together.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to see a show on a big stage. She sat down and waited for the show to start. She was very excited to see the people on stage act and dance. Soon, a foolish boy named Tim came on the stage. He was trying to understand how to dance. He asked Sue, "Can you help me understand how to dance?" Sue said, "Yes, I can help you!" So, Sue got up on the stage and showed Tim how to dance. They danced and danced, and then something unexpected happened. A big, colorful bird flew down onto the stage! The bird started to dance too! Everyone laughed and clapped. Sue, Tim, and the bird danced together, and it was the best show ever.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily and a boy named Tom. They were best friends. They loved to play together at the park. One sunny day, they went to the park to play. The sky was blue, and the day was perfect. Lily and Tom were hungry after playing for a long time. They sat down on a bench to eat their lunch. Lily had a sandwich, and Tom had toast. Tom's toast was big, so he decided to share it with Lily. Tom said, "Lily, let's share my toast!" Lily was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" They both ate the toast and felt happy. After their meal, they continued to play and had a great time. Sharing made their perfect day even better.
