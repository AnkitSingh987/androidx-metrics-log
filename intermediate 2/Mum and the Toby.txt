Category: Intermediate
Title: Mum and the Toby

Mum was teaching Toby how to make sounds. Toby looked at Mum, with a curious face. Mum said, “Let’s start with the ‘A’ sound.” Toby listened and tried to make the same sound. His sound came out a bit strange but Mum said, “That was great!” Toby beamed with pride. Mum said, “Let’s try the ‘B’ sound.” Toby closed his eyes and tried to make the right sound. He opened his eyes to find Mum clapping and saying, “That’s it!” Toby felt really happy. They kept going through the sounds of the alphabet. Toby listened to Mum carefully and made the same sound. Every time he got one right, he felt so happy. At the end, Toby said, “I like teaching sounds with you, Mum!” Mum smiled and hugged Toby tight. Toby felt so loved.

Once upon a time, there was a king. The king liked to speak a lot. He talked to everyone in his big home. The people in the big home listened to the king. One day, the king met a bird. The bird was very smart. The bird told the king, "You talk too much. You should listen more." The king felt a little stupid, but he knew the bird was right. From that day on, the king talked less and listened more. He became a better king. The people in the big home were happy. The king and the bird became good friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom wanted to walk, but it was hard. He always fell over and his legs were tired. He got frustrated. One day, he asked his mum for help. She said 'lean on me, Tom'. He did and felt better. Tom and his mum walked together. Every time Tom felt scared, his mum helped him by leaning on her. They walked until they got to the park. It was fun. Tom kept leaning on his mum, but eventually he wanted to try on his own. He tried really hard, but he just couldn't do it. He started to feel frustrated. Then something amazing happened. Tom felt better when his mum put her hand on his back and said 'It's okay, I'm here'. With her help, Tom made it to the park. He was so proud. Tom and his mum had a great time in the park. Tom was so happy that his mum helped him lean. After a few weeks, Tom could finally walk on his own - and no longer felt frustrated.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious box in a small town. The box had been there for a very long time. No one knew what was inside the box. People were scared to open it. One day, a brave boy named Tim saw the box. He was not scared. He went to the box and asked, "What's inside you?" The box did not say anything. Tim tried to open the box, but it was locked. He needed a key. Tim went to the wise old man in the town. He asked, "Do you have the key to the box?" The old man said, "Yes, but you must promise to be careful." Tim promised to be careful. The old man gave Tim the key. Tim went back to the mysterious box and opened it. Inside the box was a tiny jail. In the jail, there was a small, friendly bird. The bird said, "Thank you for letting me out!" Tim and the bird became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Tom. Tom was very good at finding things. One day, Tom went to the store with his friend, a little girl named Sally. Sally wanted to buy a toy, and Tom wanted to help her. At the store, they saw many toys. Tom looked at the toys and saw one with a label. The label said "best toy". Tom knew that this toy would make Sally very happy. So, Sally and Tom went to the man at the store to buy the toy. Sally was so happy with her new toy, and she thanked Tom for helping her. They went home and played with the toy all day. Tom was proud that he found the toy with the label. He knew he was a famous cat for a reason.
