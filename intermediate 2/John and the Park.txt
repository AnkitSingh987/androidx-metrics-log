Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Park

John and his Dad were walking in the park. John looked around and noticed a shiny pebble on the ground. He bent down and picked it up. John asked his Dad what it was and Dad said, "It's a pebble. It's very pretty. That's for you!" John was so excited. He asked what the pebble did. Dad smiled and said, "It can be a secret. Keep it safe and never forget it." John nodded and put the pebble in his pocket. His Dad said, "Let's pause and look around. See if there's something else you can find." John looked around and spotted something unknown. He reached into some bushes and found an old toy car. He showed it to his Dad. His Dad smiled and said, "It looks like you found a special treat!" John smiled and ran around the park, happy with his new secret pebble and unknown toy car.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a bit cold outside, so his mom said, "I recommend you wear your orange jacket, Tim." Tim put on his orange jacket and they walked to the park. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Lily. Lily was wearing a blue jacket. Tim said, "Hi Lily, I like your blue jacket. My mom said I should wear my orange jacket today." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. I like your orange jacket too!" They played together all day. They went on the swings, the slide, and even played in the sand. Tim was happy he wore his orange jacket because it kept him warm. When it was time to go home, Tim and Lily said goodbye. Tim couldn't wait to come back to the park and play with Lily again.

Once, there was a boy. He was only 3 years old. Every day, he would take long walks around his little town. One day, he was at the park and he saw a rare spot he hadn't seen before. It was really tricky but he thought he could get to it. He started to walk towards it. As he got closer, he could see it was pretty far away, so he sighed. He kept walking and eventually he made it to the spot. It was so special! There was a big tree with a tire swing hanging from it. The boy gasped in excitement. He hopped right in and started swinging. As he was playing, the boy saw something else rare, a rainbow! He couldn't believe it. He was so excited he had to take a deep sigh. He jumped off the swing and ran to the rainbow. It was so beautiful and magical. He put his hands out and could feel the warmth of the sun. He knew it was a very rare moment and he was so thankful. When it was finally over, the boy looked back at the spot he found and smiled. He was so happy he followed his instincts and went on his adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his basketball. One day, Tom went to the park to play. At the park, Tom saw another boy named Sam. Sam was playing with a toy car. Tom asked Sam, "Do you want to play basketball with me?" Sam said, "No, I only like to play with my toy car." Tom was sad. Tom tried to play basketball by himself, but it was not as fun. He wanted to play with a friend. Tom asked Sam again, "Please play basketball with me." Sam thought for a moment and said, "Okay, I will try." They played together and had lots of fun. Tom and Sam became good friends.

One day, a curious little boy named Tim went to play outside. He had a big red bucket. He liked to fill it with sand, water, and leaves. Tim had a lot of fun with his bucket. As Tim played, he saw a big green frog. He wanted to touch it, so he poked it with his finger. The frog jumped high in the air. Tim was very surprised. He thought the frog would be his friend. But when the frog landed, it was not a frog anymore. It turned into a big yellow bird! The bird looked at Tim and his red bucket. The bird wanted to play too. So Tim, the bird, and the red bucket played all day long. They had so much fun with the big twist in their day.
