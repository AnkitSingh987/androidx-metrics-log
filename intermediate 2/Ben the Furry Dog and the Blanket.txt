Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Furry Dog and the Blanket

One day, a furry dog named Ben went for a walk near the river. Ben saw a big crocodile lying on the ground. Ben was not scared. He wanted to play with the crocodile. Ben said, "Hello, Mr. Crocodile! Do you want to play with me?" The crocodile was cold and shivering. He said, "I am too cold to play. Can you help me get warm?" Ben thought for a moment. He had an idea! He ran back to his house and got a big, warm blanket. He put the blanket on the crocodile. The crocodile stopped shivering and felt warm. "Thank you, Ben!" said the crocodile. "Now I can play with you!" Ben and the crocodile played all day long. They became best friends and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop around the forest and talk to all his friends. One day, Bob met a pretty bunny named Bella. They both liked to eat carrots, and they became good friends. One sunny day, Bob said to Bella, "Bella, will you marry me?" Bella was so happy and said, "Yes, Bob! I will marry you!" They told all their friends, and everyone was excited. They planned a big party for the wedding. On the day of the wedding, all the animals in the forest came. There were lots of yummy carrots for everyone to eat. Bob and Bella danced and laughed with their friends. They were so happy to be married and to share their love of carrots with all their friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big window. He looked out the window and saw a harsh wind blowing. Leaves flew in the air and trees shook. Tim felt scared, so he ran to his mom. His mom tried to explain to him that wind can be strong, but it won't hurt him. Tim felt better and went back to the window. He watched the wind blow and thought it was fun. Suddenly, the wind stopped. Tim was confused. He looked around and saw a big, friendly giant outside the window. The giant was the one making the wind! Tim smiled and waved at the giant, and the giant waved back. They became friends and played in the wind together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucy. She was three years old and very enthusiastic about trying new things. One day, Lucy went to the park with her Mum. It was a very sunny day and they decided to spread a blanket and have a picnic. Lucy was so excited! Mum brought out some delicious sandwiches and they ate them together. Lucy was so happy - she loved eating sandwiches! They had so much fun in the sunshine that they stayed at the park for the whole day. Moving around the grass and playing games. Lucy felt so excited - she had the best day! At the end of the day, Mum and Lucy packed up their things and headed home. Lucy was a bit tired but she was still feeling very enthusiastic. The day in the park had been so much fun.

Once upon a time, there lived a brilliant fox. She lived near a big creek. Each day, she walked around the creek looking for adventures! One day, she noticed something strange on the opposite side of the creek. She couldn't figure it out. She thought really hard, but she couldn't solve this problem. Then, she asked her best friend, a little bird, for help. "What is it?" asked her friend. The fox stepped back and said, "I don't know, I can't solve it! Can you help me?" The little bird looked closely and then he said, "Ah! I see, it's a bridge that needs fixing. We can solve this by working together!" The fox smiled and said, "That's brilliant! Let's get started, shall we?" They collected some sticks and twigs, and then carefully built a bridge across the creek. When they were done, the fox and the little bird crossed the bridge and they were very proud of what they achieved. The end.

Mia liked to play in her room. She had many toys and books and dolls. She was very independent. She did not need anyone to help her. She could dress herself, brush her teeth, and make her bed. One day, she wanted to play a new game. She looked around her room and saw the curtain. The curtain was long and soft and blue. Mia had an idea. She grabbed the curtain and twisted it. She made a rope with the curtain. She tied one end to her bed and the other end to her chair. She had a swing! She climbed on her bed and held the curtain. She pushed herself off the bed and swung in the air. She felt happy and free. She laughed and shouted, "Whee!" But then, she heard a loud rip. The curtain tore and Mia fell to the floor. She was not hurt, but she was scared. She looked at the curtain and saw a big hole. She knew she was in trouble. She heard her mom coming to her room. "Mia, what did you do?" her mom asked. She saw the curtain and the mess. She was angry and sad. "I'm sorry, mom," Mia said. "I wanted to play a new game. I twisted the curtain and made a swing. But it broke. I didn't mean to ruin it." Her mom sighed. She hugged Mia and said, "I know you didn't mean to, but you have to be careful. The curtain is not a toy. It is for the window. You could have hurt yourself or the furniture. Next time, ask me before you play with something that is not yours." Mia nodded. She felt sorry and ashamed. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I love you. Can we fix the curtain?" Her mom smiled. She said, "I love you too, Mia. We can try to fix the curtain. But first, you have to help me clean up this mess. And then, we can find a better game for you to play. How about we read a book together?" Mia smiled. She said, "OK, mom. That sounds good. Thank you for being nice to me." They hugged and kissed. They cleaned up the room and fixed the curtain. They read a book and cuddled. Mia learned a lesson. She still liked to be independent, but she also liked to listen to her mom. She knew her mom loved her and wanted her to be safe and happy.
