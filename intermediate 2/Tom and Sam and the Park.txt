Title: Tom and Sam and the Park

One day, Tom and Sam went to the park to play football. They were very careful not to hurt each other while playing. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. They had a lot of fun kicking the ball and running around. Tom said to Sam, "I want to be good at football. Can you support me?" Sam smiled and said, "Of course, Tom! We can practice together and help each other get better." So, Tom and Sam played football every day, supporting each other and being careful not to get hurt. They became very good at football and had lots of fun together. And they always remembered to be careful and support their friends.

One day, a little cat named Fluffy went out to play. Fluffy was grumpy because she could not find her favorite toy. She looked under the bed, behind the chair, and even in the yard. But she could not find it. Fluffy was very sad. Fluffy saw her friend, Bunny, hopping in the yard. Bunny asked, "Why are you grumpy, Fluffy?" Fluffy said, "I can't find my toy. I looked everywhere." Bunny wanted to help, so they looked together. They looked high and low, but they still could not find the toy. Then, Fluffy's mom came out with a bowl of cream. She said, "Fluffy, come have some cream. It will make you feel better." Fluffy and Bunny shared the cream. They felt better and stopped being grumpy. They learned that sometimes, when you can't find what you want, it's good to share and be happy with what you have.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her dad. She was very proud of her new shoes. They were pink and shiny. At the park, Mia saw a swing. She loved to swing high in the sky. So, Mia and her dad went to the swing. Mia sat on the swing and her dad pushed her. She was swinging high and laughing. While she was swinging, a big bird flew by. The bird saw Mia's shiny shoes and wanted them. The bird flew down and took one shoe in its mouth. Mia was sad and her dad was surprised. They did not know what to do. Then, the bird dropped the shoe in a tree. Mia's dad climbed the tree and got the shoe back. Mia was happy to have her shoe back and the bird flew away. In the end, Mia and her dad went home with a funny story to tell.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named May. It was a beautiful spring day and she was very excited. May went outside and saw blue flowers in the garden. She was so happy to see the pretty flowers that she did a special dance. Suddenly, the sky turned gray and May heard the sound of rain. She stepped into the shower and smiled. The cool water felt so nice and refreshing on her skin. May then looked up at the sky and noticed big blue clouds. She started singing and jumped up and down. Then, a raindrop hit her face and May said to herself, "It's raining! Yay!" She opened her arms and spun around, laughing with joy. The rain and the blue sky made May very happy. She knew that the warm weather of spring was back and the days of play had just begun.

Lily liked pasta very much. She liked to eat it with cheese and sauce. She liked to make shapes with it on her plate. She liked to share it with her cat, Mimi. One day, Lily's mom made pasta for lunch. But Lily was not hungry. She wanted to play with her toys. She said no to pasta. She said no to cheese and sauce. She said no to her mom. Lily's mom was grumpy. She said Lily had to eat some pasta. She said pasta was good for her. She said pasta would make her grow big and strong. She said Lily could not play with her toys until she ate some pasta. Lily was grumpy too. She did not want to eat pasta. She did not want to grow big and strong. She wanted to play with her toys. She said no to her mom. She said no to pasta. She said no to Mimi. Lily's mom was very grumpy. She said Lily had to leave the table. She said Lily had to go to her room. She said Lily had to stay there until she was sorry. She said Lily could not have any toys or Mimi. Lily was very sad. She did not like to leave the table. She did not like to go to her room. She did not like to stay there alone. She did not like to be sorry. She missed her toys and Mimi. Lily thought about pasta. She thought maybe pasta was not so bad. She thought maybe pasta was yummy. She thought maybe pasta was fun. She thought maybe pasta was nice. Lily said sorry to her mom. She said she would eat some pasta. She said she would eat it with cheese and sauce. She said she would share it with Mimi. Lily's mom was happy. She said Lily was a good girl. She said she could come back to the table. She said she could have some pasta. She said she could play with her toys and Mimi. Lily was happy too. She ate some pasta. She ate it with cheese and sauce. She shared it with Mimi. She played with her toys. She hugged her mom. Lily liked pasta very much.
