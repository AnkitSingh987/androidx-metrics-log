Title: Tom and Mia and the Elephant

Tom and Mia were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were animals. Tom was a lion and Mia was a monkey. They ran and jumped and made noises. "Roar!" Tom said. "I am the king of the park. No one can stop me." "Ooh-ooh-ah-ah!" Mia said. "I am the smartest of the park. I can climb and swing and trick you." They chased each other around the trees and the slides. They had a lot of fun. Then they saw a big gray thing. It had a long nose and big ears. It was an elephant. The elephant was in a truck. It was going to the zoo. "Wow!" Tom said. "Look at that elephant. It is so big and cool. I want to be an elephant." "Me too!" Mia said. "Let's go and see it." They ran to the truck. The elephant saw them and made a noise. "Truuu!" the elephant said. It was happy to see the children. "Hi, elephant!" Tom said. "You are so cool. Can you roar like a lion?" "Truuu!" the elephant said. It tried to roar, but it sounded funny. Tom and Mia laughed. They liked the elephant. "Can you swing like a monkey?" Mia said. "You have a long nose. You can use it like a tail." "Truuu!" the elephant said. It lifted its nose and waved it around. Tom and Mia clapped. They liked the elephant's nose. The elephant liked the children. It sprayed some water from its nose. It made them wet. "Hey!" Tom said. "That's not fair. You are too cool." "Ooh-ooh-ah-ah!" Mia said. "That's funny. You are too cool, too." They all laughed. They became friends. They played until it was time to go. The elephant went to the zoo. Tom and Mia went home. They said goodbye. "Truuu!" the elephant said. "Roar!" Tom said. "Ooh-ooh-ah-ah!" Mia said. They smiled. They had a cool day.

It was a clear day and Sam was playing in the garden. He enjoyed playing with his toy toys, running around and feeling the wind in his face. Suddenly, something caught his eye under the tree. He walked over to it and as he got closer he saw a little present! He carefully reached down and opened it. Inside was a beautiful bright yellow kite! Sam was so excited he just had to try it out. He ran out onto the meadow with the kite and asked his Mum "Will it yield?" His Mum laughed and said of course it will, just hold onto the string with both hands and run! Sam followed her instructions; he ran, the wind lifting the kite higher and higher. Soon, he was lost in it, feeling the breeze and watching the kite dance in the clear blue sky. Sam smiled and laughed, finally feeling the kite yield to his command.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to jog every day. She jogged to the park and back home. Lily had a big smile when she jogged. One day, while jogging, Lily met a bossy cat. The cat said, "I want dessert!" Lily thought, "I like dessert too. I will help the cat." So, Lily and the bossy cat went to find dessert. They found a yummy dessert shop. Lily and the bossy cat ate ice cream. They were happy. After eating dessert, they jogged back to the park. Lily and the bossy cat became good friends. And they jogged and ate dessert together every day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl, who was feeling very helpless. No matter what she did, she couldn't seem to make things better. One day she went to the beach and she saw some crabs. At first she didn't care much, but then she noticed they weren't like most crabs. They were very, very tiny! The little girl thought this was so cute, so she decided to help them by bringing them home. She found some tiny containers and placed a single crab in each one. Carefully, she brought them all home. When the little girl returned home, she told her mom all about the crabs. Her mom said that the crabs could not stay in the tiny containers, and that she should find something better for them. So, the little girl started to look around her house. When she looked in the kitchen, she spotted a small bowl that she could use to replace the tiny containers. She knew that this would make the crabs much more comfortable, so she put them all in the new bowl. The little girl was happy that she could help the helpless crabs, and she thought about how good it felt to do something nice for them. From then on, she was glad to be kind to all the little creatures around her!
