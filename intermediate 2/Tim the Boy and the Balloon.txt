Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Balloon

One day, a little boy named Tim found a red balloon. It was warm and big. He was very happy. Tim wanted to show his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a balloon!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! But don't let go, or it will fly away." The next day, Tim took his balloon to the park. He saw his friend Sue. Sue had a blue balloon. They played together, running and laughing. But then, Tim's balloon reminded him of what his mom said. He held his balloon tight, so it would not fly away. Suddenly, a strong wind came. Sue's balloon flew away. Sue was sad. Tim felt bad for Sue. He thought about what his mom said. Tim had an idea. He gave his red balloon to Sue. Now, both Tim and Sue were happy. They learned to share and be good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big hill. Tim was hungry and wanted to find food. His mom said, "Look, Tim, there is a volcano!" She pointed to the big hill. Tim looked at the volcano and saw smoke coming out. "Mom, can we go see the volcano?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, let's go!" They walked to the volcano and saw a big hole at the top. Tim was scared, but his mom held his hand. They walked closer and closer to the hole. When they got to the top, they saw something very strange. There was a big pot of soup cooking in the hole! Tim said, "Mom, I am hungry. Can we eat the soup?" His mom laughed and said, "Yes, Tim, we can eat the soup." They ate the soup and were not hungry anymore. The volcano was not a real volcano, but a big stove!

Once upon a time, there was a big forest. In the forest, there was a small house. A girl named Lily lived in the house. She loved to play with her friends, the animals. One day, Lily and her friends found a shiny crystal. They all admire the crystal. They were eager to show it to everyone in the forest. So, they took the crystal and went to tell their friends about it. They showed the crystal to the birds, the rabbits, and the squirrels. Everyone was happy to see the crystal. They all played together and had a fun day. At the end of the day, Lily and her friends went back to her house. They put the crystal in a special place to remember their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She wanted to do things all by her own self. She wanted to show daddy that she could be independent. So the little girl tried her hardest. She wanted to show everyone that she could succeed. She worked hard every day and didn't give up. Every morning, she would wake up early and try her hardest to do new things. One day, she finally did it. She had succeeded! Daddy was so proud of her. He gave her a big hug and told her that she was amazing. She smiled so big because she had done it. Now, the little girl was happy. She had showed daddy that she could do it all by herself. She was independent and proud of it. The End.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the market with her mom. They wanted to buy some yummy food for dinner. Lucy saw a big pile of soft balls at the market. She wanted to play with them. Lucy asked her mom, "Can I throw the ball, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to hit anyone." Lucy picked up a ball and threw it high in the air. She felt happy. But the ball hit a man who was carrying a big bag of apples. The man felt uncomfortable. He said, "Ouch! That hurt!" Lucy said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to hit you." The man smiled and said, "It's okay, just be more careful next time." Lucy and her mom went back to shopping, and they had a fun day at the market.
