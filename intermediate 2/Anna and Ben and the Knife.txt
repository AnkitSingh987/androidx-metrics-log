Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Knife

Anna and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They saw a big knife on the table. Ben wanted to use the knife to cut some bread. He said to Anna, "Look, I can use the knife. I am big and strong." Anna was scared. She said to Ben, "No, Ben, don't use the knife. The knife is sharp and dangerous. You can hurt yourself or me. Mommy and Daddy said we should not touch the knife. We should wait for them to come back." But Ben did not listen to Anna. He took the knife and tried to cut the bread. He did not know how to hold the knife. He slipped and cut his finger. He cried out loud, "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts, it hurts!" Anna ran to Ben and saw the blood. She was worried. She said to Ben, "Oh, Ben, I told you not to use the knife. Let me see your finger. We need to put a band-aid on it. And we need to tell Mommy and Daddy what happened. They will help you." Ben was in pain. He was also embarrassed. He said to Anna, "I'm sorry, Anna. I was silly and naughty. I should have listened to you. I don't want to use the knife anymore. I hope Mommy and Daddy are not angry with me." Anna hugged Ben and said, "It's okay, Ben. I forgive you. You made a mistake, but you learned from it. Mommy and Daddy will not be angry with you. They will love you and take care of you. But next time, please don't use the knife. It is not a toy. It is for grown-ups only."

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful pig. The pig wanted to explore and have fun, so he decided to go to the maze. He had never been before and was excited to see what was inside! When the pig arrived at the maze, he saw lots of winding paths and a big door. He tried the door and it was locked! He wondered what he should do. Suddenly, he heard a voice behind him. It was a big bee. The bee said he could help the pig by replacing the door with his buzzing sound. The pig was happy! He nodded and the bee began to buzz. As the bee buzzed, the sound seemed to unlock the door. Now the pig could explore the maze! He thanked the bee and went inside. The pig was having so much fun as he made his way around the maze. When he reached the centre, he was very happy! The pig left the maze feeling cheerful. Thanks to the bee, he had had a wonderful adventure.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big rainbow in the sky. It has many colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. "Wow, look at the rainbow!" Tom says. "It is so brilliant!" Mia nods. She has a question. "How does the rainbow happen?" she asks. Tom thinks hard. He does not know the answer. He says, "Maybe the sun and the rain make the rainbow. They are friends, too." Mia likes this answer. She says, "That is a nice idea. Can we run to the rainbow?" Tom smiles. He says, "Yes, let's run to the rainbow. Maybe we can find a pot of gold." They hold hands and run to the rainbow. They laugh and have fun. They do not reach the rainbow, but they do not mind. They are happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man who could whistle very well. He lived on a small planet far, far away. The planet was blue and green, and it was very pretty. The elderly man loved to sit under a big tree and whistle happy songs. One day, a little bird came to the elderly man. The bird said, "I heard you whistle. I like your songs. Can you teach me how to whistle?" The elderly man smiled and said, "Of course, little bird! I will help you learn to whistle." The elderly man and the little bird practiced whistling together under the big tree. They had a lot of fun. After some time, the little bird learned to whistle just like the elderly man. They both were very happy and whistled songs together all day long.
