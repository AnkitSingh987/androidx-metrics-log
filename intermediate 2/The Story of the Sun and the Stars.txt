Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Sun and the Stars

Once there was a little girl. Every day she would watch the sun slowly increase in the sky. But today was different. Today, the girl felt something unknown. She looked around and saw that the day seemed to be getting longer. She watched as the sun kept increasing until it was almost too big in the sky. The little girl was unsure what was happening. She decided to go on a walk and see what would happen. As she walked, the sun kept increasing in the sky. She reached a field and looked up - the sun was now so big that no one could see the stars. The little girl was so excited to experience something so unknown. She started running in circles, spinning around, and singing with joy. The sun stayed up in the sky until it was the end of the day. The little girl had never experienced anything like it before. She was so curious and full of joy that she decided to come back the next day, to see if it would happen again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw an incredible cobweb on a big tree. She looked at it and said, "Wow! That is a big cobweb!" A small spider heard her and said, "Hi, I made that cobweb. My name is Sam." Lily smiled and said, "Hi Sam, I am Lily. I did not know spiders could talk." Sam said, "We can talk, but only to very special friends. Do you want to be my friend?" Lily said, "Yes, I want to be your friend!" Together, Lily and Sam played under the tree. They laughed and had fun. They knew they would be friends forever.

Anna and Ben like to play outside. They have a big yard with a stream. The stream has rocks and fish and frogs. Anna and Ben like to catch frogs and put them in a bucket. One day, Anna and Ben find a big frog. It is green and brown and has spots. Anna wants to catch it, but Ben says no. He says the frog is too big and stubborn. He says the frog will jump out of the bucket and run away. Anna does not listen to Ben. She is stubborn too. She runs after the frog and tries to grab it. But the frog is fast and smart. It jumps over rocks and under plants. It splashes water on Anna and makes her wet. Ben laughs at Anna. He says she is silly and wet. He says she should give up and play with the small frogs. But Anna does not give up. She wants the big frog. She chases it to the end of the stream. There, the frog finds a hole. It jumps into the hole and disappears. Anna is sad and angry. She can't catch the big frog. She stomps her feet and cries. Ben feels sorry for Anna. He goes to her and hugs her. He says it's okay. He says they can play with the small frogs. He says they are cute and friendly. He says maybe the big frog will come back another day. Anna stops crying. She smiles at Ben. She says he is a good brother. She says she is sorry for being stubborn. She says they can share the bucket and the frogs. Anna and Ben go back to the stream. They play with the small frogs. They have fun. They forget about the big frog. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a charming dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to run and play all day long. His owner, a kind little girl named Lily, liked to race with him in the big park. One sunny day, Buddy and Lily went to the park to race. They both ran as fast as they could. Buddy was very fast, and he won the race. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. They were both so happy. After the race, Buddy and Lily sat under a big tree. They shared a yummy treat and watched the birds fly in the sky. They had a great day at the park, and they knew they would have many more races together.
