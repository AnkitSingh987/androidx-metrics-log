Category: Intermediate
Title: Wednesday the Blue Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big blue cat named Wednesday. Wednesday liked to play with a red ball. One day, Wednesday could not find the red ball. This made Wednesday very sad. Wednesday looked and looked for the red ball. Then, Wednesday saw a little girl with the red ball. The little girl did not notice Wednesday. Wednesday wanted the red ball back, but did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The little girl saw Wednesday and smiled. She gave the red ball back to Wednesday. They both played with the red ball and became good friends. Now, Wednesday was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a triangle toy in her toy box. She was very happy and played with it all day long. Lily liked to rock back and forth on her rocking horse while holding her triangle toy. She would laugh and giggle as she rocked faster and faster. But one day, her triangle toy got stuck in the rocking horse. Lily felt frustrated because she could not get her toy out. Lily's mommy saw her sad face and came to help. With a little push and pull, she got the triangle toy out of the rocking horse. Lily was so happy and hugged her mommy. She played with her triangle toy and rocked on her rocking horse, laughing and having fun again.

One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big rock and said, "Mom, what is that?" His mom said, "That is a mineral, Tim. It comes from the ground." Tim thought it was very interesting. Tim picked up the mineral and took it home. He showed it to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "I think it looks terrible." Tim felt sad because he liked the mineral. The next day, Tim and Sam played with the mineral. They found out it was fun to draw with. They made pretty pictures on the ground. Sam said, "I was wrong, this mineral is not terrible. It's fun!" Tim smiled and they played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tom. He flew a big plane in the sky. One day, the sky was icy and cold. Tom was careful when he flew the plane. As Tom was flying, he saw a big bird. The bird was blue and happy. Tom wanted to say hi to the bird, but he had to ignore it because he had to fly the plane. Then, something unexpected happened. The big bird started to talk! The bird said, "Hi Tom! I can help you fly the plane!" Tom was surprised, but he let the bird help. Now, Tom and the big bird are friends and they fly the plane together.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. He wanted to sell his toy car. He had the car for one year, but he wanted to buy a new toy. One day, Tim went to his friend, Sam. Tim said, "Sam, do you want to buy my toy car?" Sam looked at the car and said, "Yes, I want to buy it!" Tim was happy, and Sam was happy too. They played with the toy car together. And they were best friends for many years.

One day, a cat and a dog were in a room. They saw a curtain. The cat said, "Let's play a game. Who can rush to the curtain first?". The dog said, "Yes, I want to play!" The cat and dog were very competitive. They got ready to run. The cat said, "One, two, three, go!". They rushed to the curtain as fast as they could. The dog was faster, but the cat was very close. When they reached the curtain, the dog said, "I won!". The cat said, "You're fast, but I'll be faster next time!". They both laughed and played more games together. They had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his blocks. He had many blocks in all colors: red, blue, green, and yellow. One day, Tim was troubled. He wanted to build a big tower with his blocks, but it kept falling down. He tried and tried, but it just wouldn't stay up. Tim's friend, Sally, came over and saw him looking sad. She asked, "Why are you troubled, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to build a big tower with my blocks, but it won't remain up. Can you help me, Sally?" Sally nodded and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's build it together!" So, Tim and Sally worked together to build the big tower. They made sure the blocks were strong and steady. And guess what? The tower remained up! Tim was so happy, and he thanked Sally for her help. From that day on, Tim and Sally always played and built things together, and they remained the best of friends.
