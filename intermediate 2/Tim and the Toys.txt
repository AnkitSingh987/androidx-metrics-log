Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a perfect backyard. He loved playing in it every day. His backyard had a big tree and lots of flowers. It was a fun place to be. One day, Tim's mom wanted to sell some toys. She asked Tim to help her. They put the toys in the backyard. Tim's friends came to see the toys. They wanted to buy some toys too. Tim and his mom sold many toys. They had a fun day together. The backyard was the perfect place to sell toys. In the end, Tim and his mom were happy. They played in the backyard and enjoyed the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim who loved to put things in a zigzag. One day, he was very eager to play with his new toy car. Tim said to his friend, "Let's put the car on a zigzag road!" His friend agreed, and they started to build a zigzag road with wooden blocks. They were very happy and excited. But Tim was too eager and did not think about safety. He put the zigzag road near the stairs. When they raced the car, it went down the stairs and broke into pieces. Tim cried and said, "My car is broken!" His friend felt sad too. They learned that they should always think about safety before playing. The moral of the story is: Always think about safety first when playing.

Once upon a time, there was an old orange cat who was always hungry. One day, the cat decided he wanted a snack. He heard about a delicious cream cake from one of his friends, so he decided to go and find one. The old cat walked and walked until he arrived at a bakery. He saw many wonderful treats in the window, including an orange cream cake! The cat was very excited. He walked up to the baker and said, "Can I have an orange cream cake?" The baker smiled and said, "Of course, my friend. That will be one dollar." The old cat paid the baker and took his cake. He couldn't wait to take a bite. He devoured the cake in no time! He was so happy that he had decided to get the cake. The old cat thanked the baker and said goodbye. He licked his lips and headed home, satisfied with his orange cream cake.

Lily and Tom are playing in the park. They like to swing, slide and run. They have a lot of fun. But they also make a lot of mess. They drop their juice boxes, wrappers and toys on the grass. A man comes to the park. He has a big bin and a stick. He picks up the trash and puts it in the bin. He makes the park tidy. Lily and Tom stare at him. They wonder what he is doing. The man sees Lily and Tom. He smiles and waves. He says, "Hello, kids. Do you like the park?" Lily and Tom nod. They like the park. The man says, "Then you should help me keep it tidy. Can you put your trash in the bin, please?" Lily and Tom look at their trash. They feel sorry. They say, "Yes, sir. We are sorry. We will put our trash in the bin." They pick up their trash and put it in the bin. The man says, "Thank you, kids. You are very good. Now you can play some more." Lily and Tom say, "Thank you, sir. You are very nice. We will be tidy next time." They smile and run to the swing. The man smiles and walks away. He is happy. The park is tidy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to play with her friends. She was very competitive and loved to compete in different games. One day, she and her friends were playing outside, when they noticed a giant statue in the park. The statue was so tall and imposing! The little girl couldn't stop looking at it. Suddenly, the statue started to move and the little girl's friends ran away! The little girl was so scared but she stayed. The statue moved closer to her and opened its mouth. Then, it started to bite! The little girl screamed and ran away from the statue. The little girl was so scared but she was brave too. She went back to the park the next day and the statue was gone. It seemed like it had moved away! The brave little girl and her friends were so happy that they didn't have to worry about the scary statue anymore. They cheered and cheered, and then went back to their competitive games.
