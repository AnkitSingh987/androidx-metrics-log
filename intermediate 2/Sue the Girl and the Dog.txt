Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her friend Tom's house. They wanted to play a fun game. They decided to play on the stairs. Sue said, "Let's clap every time we jump on a stair!" Tom agreed and said, "Yes, that sounds fun!" So, they started to play. As they jumped and clapped, they laughed and had a great time. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, guilty-looking dog came running up the stairs. The dog was chasing a small, scared cat. Sue and Tom stopped playing and looked at the animals. Sue said, "Oh no! The dog wants to catch the cat!" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Let's clap really loud to scare the dog away!" They clapped as loud as they could. The dog stopped chasing the cat and ran away. The cat was safe, and Sue and Tom felt like heroes. They laughed and continued to play their stair game, happy that they had saved the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to jog and play all day. One day, Spot saw a big couch outside a house. The couch looked fun to jump on, so Spot decided to play on it. As Spot jumped on the couch, he heard a funny noise. He stopped and looked around, but he did not see anything. Spot thought it was just the wind, so he kept jumping and having fun on the couch. Suddenly, the couch started to move! Spot was scared and tried to jump off, but the couch moved too fast. The couch was not a couch at all, but a big, hungry monster! The monster opened its mouth and ate poor little Spot. And that was the end of Spot's fun day.

Once upon a time there was a young root who wanted to mix up some extra nutrients for his garden. He thought that if he could mix together the right combination of ingredients he would make his garden thrive. So he started mixing different ingredients together, but sadly he made a big mistake. The mixture he made was too strong and very unhealthy. The young root quickly realized his mistake, but it was too late. The mixture had already spread throughout his garden and all the plants and vegetables that grew there had died. The young root was sad because his garden was gone and he had learned a valuable lesson. The moral of the story? Taking shortcuts can bring disastrous results.

Once upon a time there was a mom, dad and two kids. The two kids were named Lily and Alex. Today was Lily's birthday and they were all celebrating together. Alex had a block he was smashing together while they got the party started. "What are we doing to celebrate, Mommy?" Lily asked. "We're going to have a special cake," Mom said. "And you can eat one piece of yummy cheese too." Lily smiled. "That sounds like fun. Is the cake pretty?" "It sure is," Dad said. "It's nice and pink with pretty designs on it and lots of sprinkles to make it extra special." So they all gathered around the table and blew out the candles. Mom cut the cake and everyone had a piece. They gathered around the cheese and all said, "Cheese!" Then they all clapped for Lily's birthday. It was a fun day of celebrating for Lily's birthday. Everyone was happy — and full of yummy cake and cheese!

Lily and Ben are playing with their dog, Spot. Spot is a big, brown dog who likes to run and jump. Lily and Ben love Spot very much. "Spot, sit!" Lily says. She holds a ball in her hand. Spot sits and looks at the ball. He wags his tail. He wants to play. "Good boy, Spot!" Lily says. She throws the ball far away. "Go get it, Spot!" Spot runs after the ball. He is fast and strong. He catches the ball in his mouth. He is happy. He runs back to Lily and Ben. He drops the ball at their feet. "Spot, shake!" Ben says. He holds out his hand. Spot lifts his paw and puts it in Ben's hand. Ben shakes Spot's paw. He smiles. "Good boy, Spot!" Ben says. He gives Spot a treat. Spot eats the treat. He licks his lips. "Spot, heel!" Lily says. She picks up the ball and the leash. She clips the leash to Spot's collar. "Let's go for a walk, Spot!" She starts to walk. Spot follows her. He stays close to her heel. He is a good dog. He is patient. He knows Lily and Ben will play with him again. He loves Lily and Ben very much.
