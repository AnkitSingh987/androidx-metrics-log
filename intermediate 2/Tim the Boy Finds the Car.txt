Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a rare toy car. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim's toy car broke. He was very sad. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will fix it with tape." But Tim refused. He said, "No, Mom. I want a new toy car." Tim's mom was not happy. She said, "You must learn to fix things, Tim. We cannot always buy new things." But Tim still refused. He only wanted a new toy car. Days went by, and Tim's toy car was still broken. His mom never bought him a new one. Tim was very sad. He wished he had let his mom fix his rare toy car with tape. Now, he had no toy car to play with.

One day, a big red car went to the park. In the car was a mom and her little boy, Tim. Tim was very impatient. He wanted to play on the swings and slides right away. At the park, they saw a big factory. It made a lot of noise. Tim asked, "Mom, what is that?" Mom said, "It's a factory. It makes things we use every day." Tim thought the factory was very big and loud. While Tim played, a girl named Sue came up to him. Sue wanted to play on the swing, but Tim was on it. Sue said, "I want to swing now." Tim didn't want to get off. They both felt sad. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's take turns. I'll swing, then you swing." Sue agreed. They played together and had a great day at the park.

Once there was a little boy named Joe. Joe was happy and wanted to have some fun. He saw a large tree with some numbers on it. He was curious and wanted to pick one. Joe's mom saw him and said, "Joe, why don't you pick the largest number? That is the most special one". Joe thought that was a good idea and picked the largest number. When Joe touched the number it started to glow. It opened up and a magical creature popped out! It was a fairy. The fairy said, "Thank you for picking me, Joe. You have done something very nice and you get one wish". Joe thought for a moment and then he said, "I wish for everyone in the world to be happy". The fairy smiled and said, "Your wish will come true, Joe". Then the fairy disappeared in a bright light. Joe was quite happy. He happily ran to his mom and told her all about his magical adventure. His mom was very proud of him. The moral of the story is that when you do something kind and helpful, good things happen.

Once upon a time, there was a small rocket. The rocket was red, blue, and yellow. It was broken and could not fly. The rocket was sad because it wanted to go up high in the sky. One day, a magic bird appeared. The bird saw the broken rocket and wanted to help. The bird had magic dust that could fix things. The bird sprinkled the magic dust on the rocket and something amazing happened. The rocket was not broken anymore! It could fly now! The rocket was so happy. It flew high up in the sky with the magic bird. They played together all day long, and the rocket was never sad again.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Sue. Sue loved to swing on her big tree in her yard. Every day, she would swing high in the sky, laughing and playing. One sunny day, while Sue was swinging, she saw the mail truck come by. The mailman stopped at her house and put some mail in the box. Sue stopped swinging and ran to the mailbox. She opened it and found a letter just for her! "Mom, look! I got mail!" Sue said, excitedly. Her mom came outside and smiled. "Let's open it together!" Mom said. They opened the letter and found out that Sue had won a big prize! Sue and her mom were so happy and surprised. They never thought that swinging and mail could bring such a wonderful surprise.
