Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Sally found a mop in her house. She did not know where it came from. It was unknown to her. Sally wanted to play with the mop, so she took it outside. Sally saw her friend Tom. Tom looked at the mop and said, "What is that?" Sally frowned because Tom did not know what a mop was. She said, "This is a mop. I found it in my house." Sally and Tom played with the mop. They pretended it was a big tree. They had lots of fun. Then, they put the mop back in Sally's house. They smiled and went to play more games together.

---

One day, a bald man named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big house and decided to enter. Inside the house, he found a guitar. Tom loved to play the guitar, so he picked it up and started to play. Tom played a happy song on the guitar. A little cat heard the music and came to listen. The cat liked the music too. The cat said, "I like your song, Tom! Can we be friends?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, we can be friends!" Tom and the cat played together every day. They had a lot of fun with the guitar. They made many happy songs. And they were always very good friends.

---

Once there was a bald man named Bob. Bob had a blue shirt. He loved his blue shirt very much. Every day, he would wear his blue shirt and go for a walk. One day, while Bob was walking, his blue shirt got stuck on a tree. The tree tore his blue shirt. Bob was very sad. He started to cry. Bob's friend, Sue, saw him cry. She wanted to help Bob. Sue had a new blue shirt. She gave the new blue shirt to Bob. Bob stopped crying and smiled. Now, Bob and Sue were happy friends.

---

One day, a little girl named Amy went to play outside. She liked to pretend she was a delicate fairy. She put on her fairy wings and her shiny crown. Amy felt so happy. Amy saw her friend, Tom, in the park. Tom asked, "What are you doing?" Amy said, "I am a delicate fairy. Do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to be a fairy too." They both laughed and played together. Amy and Tom pretended to fly around the park. They helped flowers grow and made the sun shine. They had a lot of fun being delicate fairies. At the end of the day, they went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her favorite toy, a folder. She would run, jump, and roll around with it all day long. One sunny day, Fluffy was playing with her folder in the park. She saw a big tree and decided to climb it. Up, up, up she went! When she got to the top, she saw a nice, big branch. Fluffy thought it was the perfect spot to sit and rest. As Fluffy sat on the branch with her folder, she saw her friends, a dog and a bird, playing below. She called out to them, "Come and sit with me!" Her friends happily ran and flew up the tree to join her. They all sat together on the branch, looking at the pretty pictures in the folder and having a fun time. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Max saw a big tree and wanted to borrow a newspaper from his friend, Tom the cat. Max went to Tom's house and asked, "Can I borrow your newspaper?" Tom said, "Yes, you can!" Max was very happy. He took the newspaper and went back to the tree. Max was persistent and tried to read the newspaper, but the wind blew it away. Max chased the newspaper and caught it. He tried to read it again, but the wind blew it away once more. Max was persistent and did not give up. He chased the newspaper and caught it again. This time, Max sat under the tree and held the newspaper tight. He finally read the newspaper and was very proud.
