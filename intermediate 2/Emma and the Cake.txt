Category: Intermediate
Title: Emma and the Cake

Once upon a time, in a small house, a girl named Emma wanted to bake a cake. She was very excited. She asked her deaf dog, Max, to help her. Max could not hear, but he could understand what Emma wanted by watching her. Emma said, "Max, let's bake a cake with a special mineral." They went to the kitchen and started to mix the ingredients. Emma showed Max how to stir the bowl. Max wagged his tail and barked, even though he could not hear his own bark. When the cake was ready, Emma and Max shared it together. They were so happy. Emma said, "Thank you, Max, for helping me bake this special cake." Max licked his lips and wagged his tail, showing Emma he was happy too. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a girl named Sue. She liked to scatter corn for the birds to eat. Every day, she went to the yard with a big bag of corn. She threw the corn on the ground and watched the birds come to eat. The birds were happy and delicate. They flew from the trees to eat the corn. Sue liked to see them eat and play. She laughed and clapped her hands when they danced in the air. One day, a big wind came and blew the corn away. Sue was sad, but she knew what to do. She got more corn and scattered it on the ground. The birds came back, and Sue was happy again.

One day, a naughty boy named Tim found a toy gun. He thought it would be fun to play with it. He went outside to find his friend, Sam. They started to play together. Tim said, "I will pinch you with my toy gun!" Sam laughed and said, "You can't pinch me, silly! That's a toy gun, not a pincher!" They ran around, laughing and playing. Suddenly, the toy gun broke into two pieces. Tim and Sam were surprised. They didn't know what to do. Then, they saw a little bug crawl out from the broken toy gun. They realized the toy gun was the bug's home! They felt bad for breaking its home, so they decided to help the bug find a new one.

Once upon a time, in a land far to the west, there was a smelly old dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max and his friend, a little boy named Tim, went to the park to play. As they played, Max would pass the ball to Tim, and Tim would pass the ball back to Max. They were having so much fun! Then, Max saw another dog with a very big bone. Max wanted the bone, so he stopped playing with Tim and ran to the other dog. Max said, "Give me your bone!" The other dog was not happy. He growled and snapped at Max. Max was scared and ran back to Tim. Tim looked sad because Max had left him to play alone. Max felt bad for leaving Tim. Then, Max had an idea. He went back to the other dog and said, "I'm sorry for trying to take your bone. Can we all play together?" The other dog agreed, and they all played happily. Max learned that it was better to share and play with friends than to be greedy and want something that was not his.
