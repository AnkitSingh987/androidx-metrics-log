Category: Intermediate
Title: The Colorful Plane Flew and the Bird

One day, a colorful plane flew in the sky. The plane was very big and pretty. It had many colors like red, blue, and yellow. People looked up and saw the plane. They were very happy to see it. A little girl named Amy was playing with her friends. They saw the colorful plane too. Amy said, "Wow! Look at the pretty plane!" Her friends said, "Yes, it is so nice!" They all clapped their hands. They liked the plane a lot. But then, something unexpected happened. The colorful plane started to change. It became a big, colorful bird! The bird was very friendly. It flew down and said, "Hello, friends!" Amy and her friends were very surprised. They laughed and clapped their hands again. They had a fun day with their new bird friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to study big books with her mom. One day, Amy saw a gray bird outside her window. The bird said, "Hello, Amy! I am lost. Can you help me find my way back to the west?" Amy wanted to help the bird, so she asked her mom, "Mom, can we help this gray bird go back to the west?" Her mom said, "Yes, Amy. Let's look at our big map and find the way." They studied the map, and Amy's mom said, "We need to go left to find the west." So, Amy and her mom walked with the gray bird. They went left and found the bird's home. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Amy and Mom! I am home now." Amy and her mom went back to their house. They were happy they could help the gray bird. From that day on, Amy loved to study maps and help her new bird friends find their way home.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play football. He was very good at it. Tim had a big dream. He wanted to perform in a big football game. One day, Tim's team had a big game. They were very excited. Tim's mom and dad came to watch him play. Tim played very good. He kicked the football high and far. His team was winning. But then, something bad happened. Tim fell down and hurt his leg. He could not play anymore. His team lost the game. Tim was very sad. He had to go to the doctor. Tim's dream to perform did not come true.

One day, a little girl named Sue was excited to read her big book. She loved to skip and read at the same time. Sue found a nice spot under a tree to sit and read. Sue opened her book and started to read. She saw a pretty picture of a cat. She wanted to see more pictures, so she turned the page. But, the page was stuck! Sue tried very hard to turn the page, but it would not move. Sue felt sad and did not know what to do. She asked her friend, Tom, for help. Tom came over and they both tried to turn the page together. With a little push, the page finally turned. Sue was happy again and thanked Tom. They both sat under the tree and enjoyed reading the big book together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emily who was three years old. One day, Emily was playing in the garden when she found a dusty old box. She blew on the box to try and clean it up and when she opened it, she found an old counting frame inside. “Look what I've found!” she said to her mom. Her mom smiled. “That’s a counting frame, it can help you count up to the whole universe.” Emily was very excited and she started to count. She counted the stars in the night sky and the leaves on the trees. Everywhere she looked she found something new to count. But there were some places that were too dirty to count. Emily's mom had to help her get rid of the dirt so she could count again. Emily was so excited to count more of the universe that she couldn't stop. And they both kept counting until the whole universe was counted. The end!
