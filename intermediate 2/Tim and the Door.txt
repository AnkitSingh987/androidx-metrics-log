Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, empty closet in his room. One day, he had an idea. He wanted to mix all his toys and put them in the closet. Tim took all his toys out of their boxes. He had cars, dolls, and balls. He mixed them all up on the floor. Then, he put them into the empty closet. It was fun to mix and match his toys. After playing, Tim was tired. He closed the closet door and went to bed. When he woke up the next day, he was excited to play with his mixed toys again. Tim loved his new, mixed-up toy closet.

Once upon a time there was a treasure. It was a box filled with lots of good things inside. But it was really hard to get the treasure because it was wrapped up very tight. It had lots of strings around it that wouldn't come off. One day a silly girl tried to take the treasure. She tried to open it with her tiny hands but she couldn't. She was so stupid! Suddenly, a big strong man came and he picked up the box. He was very strong so he was able to wrap the strings around the box easily. The girl was happy because the man had given her the treasure. She opened the box and found lots of lovely things inside. She thanked the man and they both shared the treasure.

One beautiful day, a little girl named Amy woke up. It was Wednesday, her favorite day. She jumped out of bed, excited to play outside in the sun. She put on her shoes and ran out the door. Amy saw her friend, Tom, in the park. They played with a ball, laughed, and had fun. Then, they found a tree with yummy fruit. Tom said, "Let's see if the tree will yield fruit for us!" They reached up high, and the tree gave them fruit to eat. Amy and Tom sat under the tree, eating the fruit. They talked and laughed. "I love Wednesdays!" said Amy. "Me too!" said Tom. They played until the sun went down, happy to have spent a beautiful Wednesday together.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck. The duck liked to play by the water. One day, the duck saw a dry log next to the water. The duck thought it would be fun to balance on the log. So, the duck jumped on the log and started to balance. The duck wobbled a little, but soon it was balancing very well. The duck was very happy that it could balance on the dry log. It played on the log all day long. And that is how the little duck learned to balance and had a fun day by the water.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a gray bench near his house. The bench was broken. Tim wanted to fix it so he could sit on it with his friends. He thought, "I will need help to fix the gray bench." Tim went to his friend, Sue. He said, "Sue, can you help me fix the gray bench?" Sue said, "Yes, I can help you!" They went to the bench and started to fix it. They worked hard to make it strong again. While they were fixing the bench, a big dog came. The dog wanted to play and jumped on the bench. The bench broke again! Tim and Sue were sad. But they did not give up. They fixed the bench again, making it stronger than before. After they fixed the bench, Tim and Sue sat on it with their friends. They were happy that they worked together to fix the gray bench. Now, they could sit and play on it every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play in the open garden. In the garden, there were many herbs. Kitty liked to smell the herbs. One day, Kitty found a big box in the garden. She did not know what was inside. Kitty was scared, but she wanted to look. She slowly opened the box. Inside, she saw a small dog. The dog was not scary. The dog said, "I surrender! I was hiding in the box." Kitty and the dog became friends. They played together in the open garden. They smelled the herbs and had fun. And they lived happily ever after.
