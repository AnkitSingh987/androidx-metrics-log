Category: Intermediate
Title: The Colorful Hose and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a colorful hose. The hose lived in a big garden. In the garden, there were many pretty flowers and trees. One day, the hose said to the flowers, "Hi, friends! Do you want to play? I can give you a splash with water." The flowers said, "Yes, please! We are thirsty." The hose started to splash water on the flowers. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The water turned into yummy candy! The hose and the flowers were so surprised and happy. They all shared the candy and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to polish her red shoes. She would polish and polish them every day. Sue was very persistent. She wanted her shoes to look nice and shiny. One day, Sue went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree with a touch of gold on it. She thought it was very pretty. She wanted to touch the gold. She reached out her hand and touched the gold. Suddenly, her shoes started to shine very bright. Sue looked down at her shoes and saw that they were now gold. She was very surprised. She thought the touch of gold on the tree had made her shoes change color. She was so happy to have shiny gold shoes. She knew that being persistent in polishing her shoes had led her to this magical tree. And from that day on, Sue always had the shiniest shoes in town.

Once upon a time, in a big new castle, there lived a kind king and a sweet queen. They had a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to run and play all day. One sunny day, Tom was playing with his toy horse in the castle garden. His friend, the talking bird, flew down and said, "Hi Tom! You look tired. You should rest for a bit." Tom smiled and said, "You're right, bird. Let's sit under this big tree and rest." So, they sat under the tree, and the bird sang a soft song. Tom closed his eyes and took a short nap. When he woke up, Tom felt happy and full of energy. He said, "Thank you, bird, for helping me rest. Now I can play more!" They played together until the sun went down, then went back to the big new castle for dinner. The king and queen were happy to see Tom so full of joy.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big bird with many feathers. The bird was blue and yellow and green. It was very pretty. "Look, Ben, a parrot!" Lila said. She wanted to touch the bird. "Wait, Lila, it might bite!" Ben said. He was anxious. He did not like birds. Lila did not listen. She ran to the bird and tried to part its feathers. The bird did not like that. It squawked and flapped its wings. It pecked Lila's hand. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lila cried. She ran back to Ben. Her hand was red and sore. "I told you, Lila, it might bite!" Ben said. He hugged Lila and helped her wash her hand. He gave her a band-aid. "I'm sorry, Ben, I was silly," Lila said. She was sad. She liked the bird, but the bird did not like her. "It's okay, Lila, you are brave," Ben said. He smiled. He liked Lila, even if she was silly. They decided to play with something else. They found a ball and a slide. They had fun. They did not bother the bird anymore. The bird was happy. It flew away.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet bird named Bella. Bella loved to fly and soar high in the sky. She had many friends who liked to play with her. One day, Bella saw some clothes hanging on a line. They were colorful and pretty. Bella thought it would be fun to play with the clothes, so she flew down and danced around them. As Bella played, her friends came to join her. They all laughed and had a great time playing with the clothes. After a while, they flew back up into the sky to continue their fun day of soaring and playing together.
