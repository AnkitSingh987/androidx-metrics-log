Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Boat and the Fish

One day, a little boat was on the shore. The sun started to rise, and the boat wanted to be useful. It asked a little fish, "Can I help you today?" The little fish said, "Yes, please take me to the other side of the water." So, the boat took the little fish across the water. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! A big wave came and pushed the boat and little fish back to the shore. The boat was sad, but the little fish said, "Don't worry, we can try again." They tried again and made it to the other side. The boat was useful and they both had a great day.

One day, a poor cat was sitting in the sunshine. She was very sad because she had no friends. The cat saw a big red ball and started to play with it. She was so happy to have a new toy. As she played, she heard a ring. It was a little bell. The cat looked around and saw a small dog with a bell on his collar. The dog said, "Hi! I heard you were sad. Can I play with you?" The cat smiled and said, "Yes! Let's play together!" They played with the big red ball all day. The cat and the dog became the best of friends. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball popped! They were sad, but then they saw a butterfly. They started to chase the butterfly and had even more fun. The cat and the dog were happy to have each other and forgot all about the big red ball.

Anna and Ben were very happy. They were going on a safari with Mom and Dad. They wanted to see lions and zebras and giraffes. They packed their bags and got in the car. "Are you ready for an adventure?" Dad asked. "Yes!" Anna and Ben shouted. They drove for a long time. They saw many trees and hills and flowers. They sang songs and played games. They were having fun. They arrived at the safari park. They got out of the car and followed a guide. He showed them a big map. He pointed to different places where they could see animals. "First, we will go to the lion zone. You have to be very quiet and sit in the car. The lions are wild and can be dangerous. But they are also very beautiful and strong. Do you want to see them?" he asked. "Yes!" Anna and Ben said. They got back in the car and drove to the lion zone. They saw a big sign that said "Lions ahead. Stay in your car. Do not feed the animals." They looked out of the windows and saw some lions lying on the grass. They were sleeping and snoring. They had yellow fur and big teeth and claws. "Wow, look at them!" Anna said. "They are so big and scary!" Ben said. "They are also very fair," Mom said. "They share their food and take care of their cubs. They are the kings and queens of the safari." Anna and Ben nodded. They liked the lions. They took some pictures and smiled. They were having a great time. They did not see the lion that was hiding behind a bush. It was watching them. It was hungry and curious. It waited for the right moment. It jumped out and ran towards the car. It roared and bared its teeth. "Look out!" Dad shouted. He started the car and drove away. The lion chased them for a while, but then gave up. It went back to the bush and licked its paws. "That was close!" Dad said. "Are you okay?" Mom asked. Anna and Ben were scared, but also excited. They had seen a lion up close. They had had a real adventure. "That was awesome!" Anna said. "That was cool!" Ben said. They hugged each other and laughed. They were very happy. They still had more animals to see. They wondered what would happen next. They loved the safari.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and her old cat, Mr. Whiskers. Lily loved to bake cookies with her mom. One day, she found a diary under her bed. It was her mom's diary from when she was little! Lily opened the diary and saw a recipe for special cookies. She wanted to bake these cookies for her mom. But there was a problem. The recipe was missing some words. Lily didn't know what to do. She asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me bake these special cookies?" Her mom looked at the diary and smiled. "Of course, Lily. Let's bake them together!" They worked hard to figure out the missing words and baked the best cookies ever. In the end, Lily, her mom, and even Mr. Whiskers enjoyed the special cookies from the old diary.
