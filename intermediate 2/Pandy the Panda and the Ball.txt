Category: Intermediate
Title: Pandy the Panda and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little panda named Pandy. Pandy loved to play and was always enthusiastic. One day, Pandy wanted to carry a big ball to his friend, Kitty. As Pandy walked, he saw a big dog. The dog said, "Give me the ball, or I will take it from you!" Pandy was scared but didn't want to lose the ball. So he said, "No, this ball is for my friend, Kitty." Pandy ran fast and the dog chased him. Pandy saw a tree and climbed up. The dog couldn't reach him. Pandy held the ball tight and thought of a plan. He remembered Kitty's house was close. Pandy threw the ball towards Kitty's house. The dog ran after it. Pandy climbed down and ran to Kitty's house. He found the ball and gave it to Kitty. They were happy and played together all day. The big dog never bothered them again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a filthy basketball in his yard. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. He saw his friend, Sam, walking by and yelled, "Hey, Sam! Come play with me!" Sam came over and they started to play with the filthy basketball. They had a lot of fun, but they got very dirty. Their clothes were covered in dirt and mud. They didn't care, they just wanted to play. After a while, Tim's mom came outside and saw them playing. She was very angry because they were so dirty. She yelled, "Tim! Sam! Stop playing and come inside right now!" They were sad, but they listened to Tim's mom. They went inside and had to take a bath. They couldn't play with the filthy basketball anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a funny clown. The clown liked to make people laugh. He had a big red nose, big shoes, and a hat with a flower on it. The clown lived in a small house with his pet dog, named Spot. One day, the clown was going to a party. He wanted to bring a spicy snack for everyone to eat. So, he made a big bowl of spicy popcorn. He put the popcorn in a bag and went to the party with Spot. At the party, the clown and Spot played games with the kids. They laughed and had fun. But, the clown missed his turn to give out the spicy popcorn. When he remembered, he opened the bag and shared the spicy popcorn with everyone. The kids loved it and laughed even more. The clown was happy he didn't miss the chance to share his snack.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She had yellow hair and was wearing a yellow dress. She loved playing outside when the sun shone, but today it was cloudy. Suddenly, the sky darkened and a storm was coming. The girl got scared and ran to her mom. "Mommy, there is a storm coming! What should I do?" she asked. "Don't worry, my love. Come here and sit with me," her mom said. The little girl hugged her mom, and they watched the storm together. Lightning lit up the sky and thunder roared. "See, it's not so scary," her mom said. The little girl snuggled close to her mom as the storm passed over. When it was gone, the sun came back out. The little girl felt safe and happy.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her friends in the park. One day, a new boy arrived in town. His name was Tom. Sue and Tom became friends. They played together every day. One day, while playing, Tom got a small cut on his hand. Sue saw this and said, "Oh no, Tom! You have a cut on your hand!" Tom looked at his hand and said, "It's okay, Sue. It's just a little cut." Sue helped Tom clean the cut and put a bandage on it. They continued to play and had a lot of fun. From that day on, Sue and Tom were the best of friends. They always took care of each other and played together in their ordinary little town. And they lived happily ever after.
