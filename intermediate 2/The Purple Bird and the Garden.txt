Category: Intermediate
Title: The Purple Bird and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a purple bird. He flew around in the sky, singing happily all day. But one day, the purple bird forgot to fly high enough. He flew too low and smacked into a tree. The purple bird was dead. The little girl who owned the purple bird was very sad. She'd had the bird since she was a baby. It was her best friend in the world. Every day, she'd take him for walks in the park. The little girl sadly placed the purple bird in a box and buried him in the garden. She planted a seed over his grave, so that he'd always be remembered. From that day on, the little girl never forgot her purple bird. Even though she couldn't see him, she knew he was there in her garden, watching over her.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily who loved to play with her toys. One day, she decided to give her toys a bath. She filled a big cup with water and put her fragile doll, Daisy, into the cup. Lily said, "Daisy, it's time for your bath!" She started to bathe the doll gently in the water. Suddenly, the cup tipped over and the water spilled all over the floor. "Oh no!" cried Lily, as she saw her doll floating in the water. She picked up Daisy and dried her off. Lily learned that she needed to be more careful when giving her toys a bath. From that day on, she always made sure to hold the cup steady and not let it tip over.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a poor boy named Tom. Tom did not have many toys, but he loved to play and have fun. One day, while he was playing outside, he saw a big box. The box had a note on it that said, "For Tom, with fun inside." Tom was very excited and said, "Wow! Fun for me!" He opened the box and found a big red ball. He played with the ball all day, kicking it, throwing it, and rolling it. Tom was very happy and had lots of fun with his new toy. The next day, Tom saw another poor child named Sue who did not have any toys to play with. Tom thought about how happy the big red ball made him, and he wanted to share the fun with Sue. He went to Sue and said, "Look, I have a big red ball. Let's play together!" Sue smiled, and they played with the ball all day long. Tom was glad he could share his fun with Sue, and they became good friends. They promised to always share their toys and send fun to other kids in the village who needed it.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She wanted to relax and play with her toys. She had a lot of stuff in her bag. She had a ball, a doll, and a toy car. While Amy was playing, a big wind came. It blew her stuff away! Amy felt helpless. She did not know what to do. She started to cry. Then, a nice boy named Tom came to help. He saw the ball in a tree. The wind blew again, and the ball fell down. It was a twist! Now, Amy and Tom played with the ball together. They were not helpless anymore. They were happy and relaxed.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. She loved to eat tasty food. One day, she saw a big cake on the table. It smelled so good. She wanted to eat it. Kitty asked her friend, Doggy, "Can I eat the cake?" Doggy said, "No, Kitty. Mom said we cannot eat the cake. It is not ours." Kitty felt sad but she tried to remind herself not to eat the cake. Later, Kitty forgot what Doggy said. She jumped on the table and ate the cake. It was very tasty. But when Mom came home, she was mad. Kitty got in trouble and had to go to her room. She did not get to play with her friends. Kitty learned that she should listen and not eat things that are not hers.
