Category: Intermediate
Title: Tommy and the Bird

Tommy was playing in the park. Suddenly, a strange voice called to him. It said, "Tommy! Come here!" He narrowed his eyes and looked around. The voice seemed to be coming from a very tall tree. Tommy started to climb the tree but it was very tough. He was slipping and sliding. He got higher and higher, but the voice faded away. Finally, he reached the top. He couldn't see anyone. The voice had vanished. Tommy frowned. He was about to get down when the voice came again. It seemed to be coming from a bird! The bird said, "Follow me, Tommy!" Tommy followed the bird until they reached an old house. The bird flew in through an open window. Tommy followed. Inside, there were lots of toys scattered on the floor. Suddenly, the voice spoke again. It said, "Welcome, Tommy! I'm your new friend!" A big, friendly teddy bear smiled at him. Tommy smiled too. He had found a new friend!

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing near a door. They both had toys, but they did not want to share. They both thought that sharing was silly. Tim said to Sue, "I don't want to share my toy car with you. It's mine!" Sue said, "I don't want to share my doll with you, too. It's mine!" They both played alone, but they soon felt sad. A dog came by and saw them. The dog said, "Why are you sad? You should share your toys and play together!" Tim and Sue looked at each other and thought about what the dog said. They decided to share their toys and play together. Tim said, "Sue, you can play with my toy car." Sue said, "Tim, you can play with my doll." They both played together and had lots of fun. They learned that sharing was not silly, but it made them happy. From that day on, Tim and Sue always shared their toys and played together. They became best friends and always had fun near the door. They knew that sharing was a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky lived near a big river. One day, Ducky saw a small fish stuck in the mud near the river. The fish was ashamed because it could not swim back to the river. Ducky wanted to help the fish. So, Ducky said, "Don't be sad, little fish. I will help you get back to the river." The fish smiled and said, "Thank you, Ducky." Ducky pushed the fish with its beak, and the fish slid back into the river. The fish was very happy and said, "You are a good friend, Ducky." Ducky was proud to help and they both played in the river all day.

Once upon a time, there lived Mia and her pet cat, Max. Mia loved Max very much and spent her days playing with him. One day, Mia noticed that Max had gone missing. She searched the entire house but he was not to be found. She searched outside but still, no Max. Mia was sad and started to cry. Suddenly, she heard a meow. She followed the sound and found Max in the alley. When Mia found Max, he was carrying a big, delicious sausage in his mouth. "Where did you find this?" she asked. Max meowed a few more times and took off running. Mia chased after Max and when she caught up to him, he was standing beside an adorable little girl. The little girl was giving Max a friendly pat and Mia noticed that Max and the little girl were friends. "I brought this sausage for you," the girl said and handed the sausage to Mia. Mia thanked the little girl and gave her a hug. "Thank you for my friend, Max," Mia said. The girl smiled and said, "I saw him outside my house and he looked so cute, so I decided to take him for a walk." Mia said goodbye to the little girl and she and Max returned home. Max was thrilled to have a new friend and Mia was grateful for the delicious sausage. The two of them were very happy.
