Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Garden

Sara and Ben like to play with water. They have a big hose in the garden. They turn on the tap and water comes out. They spray each other and laugh. "Spray me, spray me!" Ben says. "OK, here I come!" Sara says. She sprays Ben on his head and his shirt. Ben sprays Sara on her legs and her dress. They run and chase each other. They hear a sound. It is their mom. She is playing music on her phone. She is dancing and clapping. She has a healthy smile. "Come and dance with me!" Mom says. Sara and Ben drop the hose and run to mom. They join her dance. They move their arms and feet. They follow the rhythm of the music. They feel happy and warm. They dance and spray and laugh. They have fun in the garden. They love water and music and mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat called George. He was very lonely and wanted a friend. He asked all the animals in the field but no-one wanted to be his friend. One day, George heard a voice calling out, "Get over here, little goat!" He hopped over to the fence and saw a little girl smiling at him. She said, "Hello, little goat! My name is Lucy. Want to be my friend?" George was so happy. He nodded his head and said, "Yes, please!" So Lucy and George became the best of friends. They played all day in the field and George was no longer lonely. They even talked to each other and told stories. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there lived a healthy, happy bunny named Bob. Bob loved to run and play with his friends in the valley. The valley was full of green grass, tall trees, and pretty flowers. One day, Bob met a new friend named Tim the Turtle. Tim was slow and didn't know how to play with the other animals. Bob wanted to help Tim, so he decided to explain the games they played. He told Tim how to play tag, hide and seek, and race. Tim listened carefully and tried his best to play with Bob and the other animals. Soon, Tim was having fun and feeling healthy too. Bob was happy that he could help his new friend. From then on, they all played together in the valley every day.

One day, a little girl named Mia was in her room. She wanted to change her bed sheet. She thought it would be easy. Mia asked her mom, "Can you help me change my sheet?" Her mom said, "Sure, let's do it together." They took off the old sheet and put on a new one. It was a pretty sheet with many colors. Just as they finished, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew in through the window and landed on the bed. The bird said, "Thank you for my new home!" Mia and her mom were surprised but happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little red car. The car was very popular in the town. All the people loved to see the car drive by. One day, the car went on a long journey with its friend, the blue car. They were both very excited to see new things. As they drove, the red car said to the blue car, "I love this journey! We are having so much fun!" The blue car agreed, "Yes, this is the best day ever!" They drove through the town, the forest, and the hills, having a great time together. But then, something unexpected happened. The little red car and the blue car saw a big green car driving very fast. The green car was going the wrong way! The red car and the blue car stopped and looked at each other. They knew they had to help the green car. So, they drove fast and stopped the green car just in time. The green car said, "Thank you for saving me!" The little red car, the blue car, and the green car became best friends and they all drove back to the town together, happy and safe.
