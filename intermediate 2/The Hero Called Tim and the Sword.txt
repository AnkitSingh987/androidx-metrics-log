Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hero Called Tim and the Sword

Once there was a hero called Tim. He wanted to go on an adventure, and people said he could do it if he was brave and persistent. So he set out on his adventure. Tim was determined, and he seemed to always find what he was looking for. One day, he stumbled upon a big sword in the stones. It glowed in the sunlight and seemed to be calling to him. He reached out and grabbed the sword. He felt like he was meant to have it. With his new sword in hand, Tim set out again. He faced all sorts of dangers, but nothing seemed to stop him. He was strong and persistent, and he eventually made it to the end of his journey. Tim thought back to his sword. He realized how important it was to have it with him on his adventure. He was thankful for being so persistent and brave, and he smiled. He knew he had the courage to go on many more adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toys and splash in the water. One day, she found a big towel in her room. She put the towel on the floor and pretended it was a magic carpet. Lucy's mommy came into the room and saw the towel on the floor. "Lucy, did you put the towel on the floor?" she asked. Lucy thought about it and decided to be honest. "Yes, mommy, I did," she said. "I was playing with it like a magic carpet." Mommy smiled and said, "Thank you for being honest, Lucy. But we don't put towels on the floor. They are for drying our hands and body." Lucy felt sad, but she knew mommy was right. Mommy did not punish her because she was honest. Instead, she showed Lucy how to fold the towel and put it away. From that day on, Lucy always put her towel away and only used it for drying.

Mia and Sam are twins. They like to play outside in the morning. They have a big rock in their yard. They like to climb on it and pretend it is a mountain. One day, they wake up very early. They see the sky is pink and orange. They hear the birds sing. They know it is sunrise. They want to see it better. They put on their shoes and jackets. They run to the rock. They help each other climb up. They sit on top of the rock. They see the sun peek over the trees. They see the sun make the sky bright and yellow. They smile and clap. "Look, Mia, look!" Sam says. "The sun is so big and round!" "Yes, Sam, yes!" Mia says. "The sun is so pretty and warm!" They hug each other. They are happy. They are ready for a new day. They rock back and forth on the rock. They sing a song. They wait for Mom and Dad to join them.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very enthusiastic and loved to play with his hoop. He would run and jump through the hoop all day long. One day, Max had a plan. He wanted to show all his friends how he could jump through the hoop. So, Max went to the park where his friends were playing. He brought his hoop with him. When Max got to the park, he put the hoop on the ground and jumped through it. All his friends clapped and cheered. Max was so happy that his plan worked, and he played with the hoop all day long with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball and a small blue ball. They were best friends. They always played together. One day, they had to separate. The big red ball went to the left, and the small blue ball went to the right. They were very sad. As they rolled away, the big red ball saw a spark. It was a little fire. The fire was hungry. The big red ball gave the fire some wood. The fire said, "Yum, that is delicious!" The fire grew bigger and bigger. The small blue ball saw the big fire and ran back to the big red ball. The fire was so big, it made the balls come back together. The balls were happy to be friends again. They learned that even when they separate, they can always find a way back to each other. The unexpected spark helped them come back together, and they played happily ever after.
