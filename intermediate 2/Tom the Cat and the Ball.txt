Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball and run around in the park. One sunny day, Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was trying to count the flowers in the park, but she found it hard. Tom wanted to help Sue, so he thought of an easy way to teach her. He offered to show her how to count using his ball. Tom would throw the ball and Sue would say the next number. They started with one, and soon, Sue could count up to ten. Sue was very happy that Tom helped her learn the numbers. They became the best of friends and played together every day in the park. And from that day on, counting flowers became easy for Sue.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the ocean with his mom and dad. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. Tim loved the ocean. He liked to play in the sand and watch the big waves. Tim and his dad built a big sand castle near the water. They used a red bucket and a yellow shovel. Tim was very happy. Then, Tim saw an icy spot in the ocean. He wanted to touch it, but his mom said, "No, it's too cold!" Tim's dad saw a big wave coming. He shouted, "Watch out, Tim!" Tim and his dad ran away from the wave. The wave hit the sand castle and washed it away. Tim was sad, but his dad said they could build another one. Tim smiled and they started to build a new sand castle.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted figure who could climb very well. He lived in a big tree. His name was Tom. Tom loved to climb the tree every day. He was very happy. One day, Tom saw a new tree. He wanted to climb it too. Tom went to the new tree and started to climb. He went up and up. When he reached the top, something unexpected happened. Tom saw another gifted figure who could climb. Her name was Jane. Jane was also very happy to climb trees. Tom and Jane became friends. They climbed trees together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, big tree. The tree had an enormous knot on its trunk. This knot was not like any other knot. It was very special. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird saw the knot and thought, "I want to play with it!" So, the bird tried to slap the knot with its wing. But, the knot did not move. The bird tried again and again, but still, the knot did not move. The bird became very sad. Then, a wise old owl came and said, "Little bird, don't be sad. You don't need to slap the knot to be happy. Be kind to the tree and the knot, and you will find joy." The little bird listened and stopped slapping the knot. The bird and the tree became friends and lived happily ever after. And the moral of the story is: Be kind to others and you will find happiness.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Mia. Mia loved to sort her toys. She had a big toy box with many toys inside. One day, Mia found a new patch in her yard. The patch was amazing! It was full of colorful flowers. Mia's mom said, "Mia, I have a surprise for you. Go to the amazing patch and you will find it." Mia was very excited. She ran to the patch and started to look for the surprise. Mia looked and looked. Then, she found a small box. Inside the box were many toy animals. Mia was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Mom! I love my new toys!" Mia took the toy animals to her toy box. Mia had an idea. She decided to sort her toys into two groups: animals and other toys. Mia worked hard and soon, her toys were sorted. She was very proud of her work. From that day on, Mia loved to play with her toys in the amazing patch. She loved to sort her toys and make new stories with them. Mia and her mom had many fun days playing together in the amazing patch.
