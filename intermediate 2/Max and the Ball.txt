Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Ball

One day, there was a big, enthusiastic dog named Max. Max loved to play with his red ball. He would run, jump, and catch the ball all day long. Max's house had a little leak in the roof, but he didn't care. He just wanted to play with his ball. One sunny day, Max was playing with his ball when something unexpected happened. The ball started to shrink! It got smaller and smaller. Max was very sad. He didn't know what to do. He tried to play with the tiny ball, but it wasn't the same. Then, Max had an idea. He took the tiny ball to the leak in his house. He put the ball under the leak, and the water made the ball grow big again! Max was so happy. He played with his big ball all day long, and the leak in his house helped him have fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a cart that he liked very much. His cart was rare and very pretty. He loved to play with his cart every day. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue who did not have a cart. Sue was sad because she wanted to play too. Tim thought about his own cart and how much fun he had with it. He wanted Sue to be happy and have fun too. So, Tim decided to share his rare cart with Sue. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim learned that sharing his own things with others made him happy too. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful street. On this street, there were many pretty houses. In one of the houses, lived a little girl named Lily and her mom. One day, Lily wanted to go play with her friend, Tom. She asked her mom, "Can I go play with Tom?" Her mom said, "Yes, but remember to stay on our colorful street." Lily went to Tom's house, but Tom was not there. She saw a new street and thought Tom might be there. She remembered her mom's words, but she still went to the new street. Lily could not find Tom and she got lost. She was sad and scared. Then, she saw her mom coming to find her. Her mom said, "Lily, you did not remember to stay on our colorful street." Lily hugged her mom and said, "I am sorry, mom. I will remember next time." They went back to their colorful street and found Tom waiting for them. Lily and Tom played happily, and Lily always remembered to stay on her colorful street.

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They played together every day and had lots of fun. One day, Tom and Sue were playing under a big tree. They heard a tiny whisper coming from the tree. "Help me, please," said the whisper. Tom and Sue looked up and saw a small bird stuck in the tree. They wanted to help the bird, so they tried to think of a plan. Tom climbed up the tree to save the bird. When he reached the bird, something unexpected happened. The bird whispered, "Thank you, but I don't need help. I am a magic bird!" The bird flew down and landed on Sue's shoulder. From that day on, the ordinary cat, the little girl, and the magic bird were the best of friends, and they had many more adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a big bald man. He lived in a small house near a big rock. Every day, he would go out and lean on the big rock. He liked to watch the sun go up and down. One day, a small bird came to the big rock. The bird was sad because it could not find its mom. The big bald man wanted to help the small bird. He leaned down and let the bird sit on his hand. Together, the big bald man and the small bird looked for the bird's mom. They walked and walked until they found her. The bird's mom was so happy to see her baby. The big bald man, the small bird, and the bird's mom all leaned on the big rock and watched the sun go down. They were all happy and became good friends.
