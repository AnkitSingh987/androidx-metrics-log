Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pine Tree and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a dark, dark forest, there was a little pine tree. The pine tree was all alone, and he did not know any other trees nearby. He felt very sad. One day, a small squirrel came and said, "Hi, pine tree! I know you are alone. Do you want to be my friend?" The pine tree was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend!" From that day on, the squirrel and the pine tree were the best of friends. They played together and had lots of fun. The dark forest was not so dark anymore, because now the pine tree had a friend.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big purple fish. This fish loved to swim and play with his friends. One day, while playing, the fish found a long stick called a spear. The purple fish showed the spear to his friends. They all thought it was very cool. But two of his friends, a red fish and a blue fish, did not agree on what to do with the spear. The red fish wanted to play with the spear, but the blue fish did not think it was safe. The purple fish, the red fish, and the blue fish talked about it. They did not want to disagree, so they decided to ask a wise old turtle for help. The turtle told them that they should put the spear back where they found it. The three friends agreed and swam happily together, knowing they had made the right choice.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of colorful toys, like cars, dolls, blocks and books. They liked to share and have fun together. But then, Tom saw a new toy that Lily had. It was a mop with a shiny handle and a fluffy head. Tom wanted to play with it, but Lily said no. She said it was her toy and she got it from her grandma. Tom got angry and tried to grab the mop from Lily. Lily pulled it back and shouted at Tom. They started to fight over the mop. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Tom and Lily fighting and crying. She was not happy. She took the mop away from them and said they had to stop. She said fighting was not nice and they had to say sorry to each other. She said they had to share and take turns with the toys. Tom and Lily felt bad. They did not want to make Mom sad. They said sorry to each other and hugged. Mom smiled and gave them a kiss. She said she loved them and they were good kids. She gave them the mop and said they could play with it together. She said it was a special mop that could make bubbles and rainbows. Tom and Lily were happy. They thanked Mom and took the mop. They went to the bathroom and filled the tub with water. They dipped the mop in the water and swished it in the air. They saw bubbles and rainbows coming out of the mop. They laughed and clapped. They played with the mop and had fun. They forgot about their fight and were friends again. They comforted each other and said they were sorry. They learned to share and be kind.

Once upon a time, there was a patient cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play in the sun with her best friend, a little boy named Tim. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, while they were playing, they found a shiny diamond on the ground. Kitty and Tim wanted to know how much the diamond weighed. They asked their friend, a wise old owl, how to weigh it. The owl said, "You need a scale. Put the diamond on one side and something that you think weighs the same on the other side." Kitty and Tim looked for something to use as a scale and found a small seesaw. Kitty and Tim put the diamond on one side of the seesaw and tried to find something that weighed the same. They tried a toy car, a small rock, and even a banana. Finally, they found a toy that made the seesaw balance. They were very happy and learned that being patient helps you find the answer. The moral of the story is to always be patient and keep trying, and you will find what you are looking for.
