Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Stack

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with her blocks. One day, she made a big stack with her blocks. She was very happy. Lily's mommy came into the room and saw the big stack. "Wow, Lily!" she said. "That's a big stack!" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, mommy. I did it all by myself!" But then, Lily's little brother, Timmy, came into the room. Timmy was just a baby and he couldn't walk yet. He crawled over to the stack and knocked it down. Lily felt helpless as she watched her stack fall. Lily was sad, but her mommy said, "Don't worry, Lily. We can wrap the blocks with a string to make a new stack." So, Lily and her mommy wrapped the blocks with a string and made a new stack. This time, it was even bigger and better than before. Lily was happy again, and she learned that with a little help, she could fix anything. She also learned to be patient with her little brother, because he was just a baby and didn't know any better. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom likes motorcycles. He likes to look at them and hear them go vroom. He wants to have a motorcycle of his own, but his mom says they are too expensive and dangerous. Tom is sad. One day, Tom sees a motorcycle in the street. It is red and shiny and has a big sticker that says cheap. Tom thinks it is a good deal. He runs to the motorcycle and tries to get on it. He does not see the man who owns the motorcycle. "Hey, kid, what are you doing?" the man yells. He is angry and scary. He grabs Tom by the arm and pulls him off the motorcycle. "This is my motorcycle, not yours. You can't just take it. That's stealing." Tom is scared and sorry. He did not mean to steal. He just wanted to try the motorcycle. He cries and says, "I'm sorry, mister. I like motorcycles. I thought it was cheap." The man does not care. He is still angry and scary. He calls the police and tells them that Tom tried to steal his motorcycle. The police come and take Tom away. Tom's mom does not know where he is. She is worried and sad. Tom is in trouble. He wishes he never saw the motorcycle. He wishes he listened to his mom. He learns a hard lesson. He does not like motorcycles anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. His mom gave him a big pear to eat. Tim was very happy. He sat under a tree to eat the pear. The tree was very big and tall. A little bird was in the tree. The bird was angry because it was hungry. The bird saw Tim's pear and wanted it. The bird said, "Please give me some of your pear. I am very hungry." Tim was kind and broke off a piece of the pear for the bird. The bird was happy and flew away. Later, Tim was playing in the park when he saw a big dog. The dog was angry and barked at Tim. Tim was scared. Suddenly, the little bird came back with its friends. The birds flew around the dog and scared it away. Tim was safe, and he thanked the birds. The birds were happy to help Tim because he had shared his pear with them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to balance on one foot. One sunny day, she went to the park to play. In the park, she saw a small, frightened bird. The bird was scared of a big dog. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she said, "Don't worry, bird. I will help you." Lily picked up the bird and took it to a safe place. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind." Lily felt love for the bird and they became best friends. They played together in the park every day, and the bird was never frightened again.
