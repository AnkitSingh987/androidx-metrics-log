Category: Intermediate
Title: The Blue Car and the Bluey

Once upon a time, there was a little blue car. The car was very tired. It had been driving all day long. The car's name was Bluey. Bluey liked to drive fast, but today, Bluey was too tired to drive fast. Bluey had to delay going to the big race. Bluey's friends were sad. They wanted Bluey to race with them. Bluey wanted to race too, but Bluey knew it was better to rest. So, Bluey stayed home and took a nap. When Bluey woke up, Bluey was not tired anymore. Bluey went to the race and saw all the friends. Bluey was happy to see them. They all raced together and had lots of fun. Bluey was glad to be with friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Spot. Tim liked to play with Spot in the yard. One day, it rained and everything got wet. Tim and Spot were sad because they could not play outside. Tim's mom said, "Why don't you play upstairs?" Tim and Spot were happy with the idea. They ran upstairs to Tim's room. In the room, Tim found his toy cars and his big ball. Tim and Spot played with the toys and had lots of fun. After a while, Tim's mom called from downstairs, "Tim, mind your toys and keep them neat." Tim listened to his mom and put his toys away. Then, Tim and Spot went back downstairs. The rain had stopped, and they could play outside again. Tim and Spot were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty vase. It was very big and colorful. Many people would admire the vase because it was so nice to look at. The vase felt very lucky to be so loved. One day, a small cat came to the room where the vase was. The cat liked to play and jump around. The cat was having so much fun that it did not see the vase. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat jumped and hit the vase. The vase fell down and broke into many pieces. Everyone was sad about the broken vase, but then they saw something amazing. Inside the vase, there was a beautiful flower. The flower was more pretty than the vase. People started to admire the flower instead. The flower felt very lucky to be found and loved by everyone.

Tom and Mia like to play hide and seek in the big house. They run and hide in different rooms and try to find each other. One day, they decide to hide in the basement, where there are many boxes and old things. Tom finds a good spot behind a big box and waits for Mia to find him. He hears her footsteps and giggles. He thinks he is very clever. But then he hears a loud bang and a scream. He jumps out of his hiding spot and runs to see what happened. He sees Mia on the floor, holding her knee. She has stumbled over a metal thing and hurt herself. She is crying and scared. Tom feels sorry for her and hugs her. He says, "It's okay, Mia. I'm here. You are brave. Let's go upstairs and find Mommy. She will make it better." He helps her stand up and they walk to the exit of the basement. They see a light and hear Mommy's voice. She is looking for them and calling their names. They run to her and tell her what happened. Mommy is worried and hugs them. She says, "Oh, my poor Mia. Let me see your knee. You have a scratch. I will put a band-aid on it and give you a kiss. And Tom, you are a good brother. Thank you for helping Mia. You are both very brave." Tom and Mia feel better and smile. They are glad they have Mommy and each other. They say, "We love you, Mommy." Mommy says, "I love you too, my sweeties. Now, let's go have some cookies and milk. And no more hiding in the basement, okay?" They nod and follow her to the kitchen. They are happy and hungry. They have learned a lesson. The basement is not a good place to play hide and seek.
