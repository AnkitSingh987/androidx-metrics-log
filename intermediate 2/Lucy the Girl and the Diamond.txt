Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Diamond

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She loved to collect shiny things. One day, Lucy found a big, shiny diamond in her yard. She was very happy and wanted to show her friends. Lucy went to her friend Tim's house. She said, "Look, Tim! I found a diamond!" Tim looked at the diamond and felt embarrassed. He said, "Lucy, that's not a diamond. That's a big piece of glass." Lucy felt sad and surprised. She thought it was a real diamond. But she still liked her shiny glass. She decided to keep it and collect more shiny things. And even though it wasn't a diamond, Lucy and Tim had fun playing with it together.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She wanted to find something fun to play with. She looked under the slide, around the swings, and by the trees. Then, Amy found a small box. Inside the box, there was some tape. The tape was not real, it was fake. It was very colorful and had pretty pictures on it. Amy was happy to find the fake tape. Amy took the fake tape and used it to make her toys look nice. She put the tape on her teddy bear, her doll, and her toy car. All her friends liked the toys with the fake tape. They all had fun playing together at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big factory. It was very loud. In the factory, there were many people who worked. They made toys for kids to play with. The noise from the machines was too much for some of the people. One day, a man named Bob was tired of the loud noise. He wanted to find a quiet place to sit and rest. He looked around the factory, but all the places were noisy. Bob felt sad and upset. Finally, Bob had an idea. He found a big box and put it in a corner. He sat inside the box, and the noise was not so loud anymore. Bob was happy to have a quiet place to sit. The other people in the factory saw Bob and liked his idea. They all made their own quiet places to sit too. Now, everyone could work and rest without the loud noise bothering them. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing in the park with his friends. They were running, jumping, and laughing. Tim's friends were very fast, but Tim was faster. He could run like the wind. One day, Tim fell down and hurt his knee. It was red and sore, but Tim was brave and didn't cry. Tim's mom saw his hurt knee and said, "Oh no, Tim! Your knee is hurt. We need to go home and make it feel better." Tim didn't want to leave the park, but he knew his mom was right. He said goodbye to his friends and went home with his mom. At home, Tim's mom put a bandage on his knee. She told Tim, "You need to be careful when you play, or you might get hurt again. If you don't listen, I will have to punish you." Tim knew his mom was right. He promised to be more careful when he played. The next day, Tim's knee felt better, and he played with his friends again. But this time, he was careful not to run too fast or jump too high. And his knee stayed happy and not dead.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in his garden. One day, he found a pepper plant. The pepper was red and round. Tim thought it was silly because it looked like a ball. Tim wanted to pick the pepper, so he kneeled down. He held the pepper and pulled it from the plant. Tim was happy because he had a new toy. He played with the pepper like a ball, and it made him laugh. Tim's mom saw him playing with the pepper. She thought it was a funny idea. She kneeled down and played with Tim and the pepper. They had a fun time being silly together. Tim learned that sometimes, even a pepper can be a toy.
