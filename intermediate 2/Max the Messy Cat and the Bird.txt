Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Messy Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a messy cat named Max. Max loved to play in nature. One day, Max saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He stretched his legs and jumped up to the first branch. "Hi, Max!" said a little bird sitting on the tree. "What are you doing?" "I want to climb this big tree!" Max said with a big smile. The little bird laughed and flew away. As Max climbed higher, he saw a big, shiny thing on top of the tree. He wanted to see it, so he stretched his legs even more and jumped to the top. But when Max got there, he found out that the shiny thing was not a toy. It was a big, wet, sticky spider web! "Oh no!" Max cried. "I am stuck in this messy web!" Just then, the little bird came back and saw Max in trouble. The bird flew around the web, and with a big pull, Max was free. "Thank you, little bird!" Max said. "You saved me!" From that day on, Max and the little bird became best friends, and they played together in nature every day.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tom. Tom had a big, ugly toy. He did not like to play with it. One day, Tom found a key on the floor. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Mom, what does this key do?" Tom asked. His mom just gave a little shrug. "I don't know, Tom. Maybe it opens something," she said. Tom went around the house trying to open things with the key. He tried the door, but it did not work. He tried his toy box, but it did not work. Then, he tried the ugly toy. To his surprise, the key fit! When he turned the key, the ugly toy opened up. Inside, there were many pretty, colorful toys to play with. Tom was so happy and played with his new toys all day.

One day, a pretty jellyfish was swimming in the sea. It was very happy. The jellyfish liked to play with the fish and the sea animals. The jellyfish had many friends. One day, the jellyfish saw a big fish with a camera. The big fish wanted to record the pretty jellyfish. The jellyfish was scared. It did not want to be on the camera. The jellyfish swam away fast. The jellyfish told its friends about the big fish with the camera. They all decided to hide from the big fish. They swam far away. The big fish could not find them. The jellyfish and its friends were safe. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a frog who lived in a pond. The frog was not very smart, and sometimes, the other animals would call him "stupid". This made the frog very sad. One day, a beautiful fish swam into the pond. The frog saw the fish and fell in love. He wanted to marry the fish, but he was scared that she would think he was stupid too. So, the frog decided to be brave and ask the fish to marry him. The fish said yes, and they had a big party at the pond. All the animals came to the party, and they all danced and played. The frog was so happy to marry the fish, and he didn't feel stupid anymore. The other animals learned that it's not nice to call someone "stupid" and they all became friends.

Lily and Max were playing in the bathtub. They had many toys to make bubbles and shapes. They liked to splash the water and laugh. Sometimes they splashed too much and made a mess. "Look, Lily, I can make a big wave!" Max said. He moved his arms fast and hard. The water went up and down and up and down. "Wow, Max, that's fun!" Lily said. She joined him and made another wave. The water went over the edge of the tub and onto the floor. "Oh no, we are wetting the floor!" Max said. He stopped splashing and looked around. He saw a bookshelf near the door. The bookshelf had many books and pictures and toys. Some of the water had reached the bookshelf and made it wet too. "Uh oh, we are wetting the bookshelf!" Lily said. She stopped splashing and looked too. She saw a book that she liked. It was a book about animals and colors. The book was wet and the pages were sticking together. "Mommy, mommy, we are sorry!" Lily and Max said. They got out of the tub and wrapped themselves in towels. They ran to the door and called for their mommy. Mommy came and saw the mess. She was not happy. She looked at the wet floor and the wet bookshelf and the wet book. She looked at Lily and Max and their towels. "What did you do?" Mommy asked. She was not loud, but she was not quiet either. "We were playing and splashing and we made a big wave and we wetted the floor and the bookshelf and the book and we are sorry!" Lily and Max said. They talked fast and loud. Mommy sighed. She was not angry, but she was not happy either. She picked up the wet book and the wet toys and put them in a basket. She got a mop and a cloth and cleaned the floor and the bookshelf. She told Lily and Max to help her. "Next time, you have to be careful when you splash. You can have fun, but you can't make a mess. You have to respect the books and the pictures and the toys. They are not for wetting, they are for reading and looking and playing." Mommy said. She was quiet, but she was clear. Lily and Max nodded. They were sorry, but they were not sad. They helped Mommy and said sorry again. They hugged Mommy and said they loved her. Mommy hugged them back and said she loved them too. She was quiet, but she was warm.
