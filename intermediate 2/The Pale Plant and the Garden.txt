Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pale Plant and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a pale plant. It lived in a big garden with many other plants. The pale plant was not happy because it was not as green as the other plants. One day, a kind girl came to the garden. She saw the pale plant and felt sad for it. The girl had an idea. She knew that if she gave the plant some love, it might become green and happy like the other plants. So, the girl started to rub the pale plant gently every day. She would talk to it and sing songs to it. Slowly, the pale plant started to become green. It was so happy to have a friend who cared for it. The girl and the plant became best friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun with his friends. One day, Spot found a shiny watch on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his friends. Spot's friends thought the watch was cool. They wanted to learn how to tell time, so Spot decided to teach them. They all sat in a circle and listened as Spot taught them about the big hand and the little hand on the watch. After they learned about the watch, they played a game. They ran around and around, laughing and having fun. But then, the watch fell off Spot's paw and broke into pieces. Spot and his friends were sad because they could not play with the watch anymore. The bad ending made them learn to be more careful with their things.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a bald man named Tom. Tom was very friendly and liked to meet new people. One day, Tom went for a walk to the park to make new friends. At the park, Tom saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was playing with her toy bear. Tom walked over and said, "Hello, I am Tom. Can I introduce myself to your toy bear?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, this is my bear, Teddy." Tom took a step closer to Teddy and said, "Hello Teddy, nice to meet you." Lily giggled and made Teddy wave at Tom. They all played together in the park, and Tom was happy to have made new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dance. All the animals went to the dance. They were very happy. They danced and played all day. One day, a little bird was angry. He could not dance. He sat and watched all the other animals dance. The little bird felt sad. A kind frog saw the angry bird. The frog went to the bird and said, "Let's dance together!" The bird and the frog danced together. They were not angry anymore. They had fun at the big dance.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pink dress that she loved to wear. One day, she went outside to play with her friends in the park. They played all day, and soon it was night. At night, Lily looked at the sky and saw the stars. They were so pretty and shiny. She saw that some stars matched the color of her pink dress. She felt happy and smiled. Lily went home and told her mom about the stars that matched her dress. Her mom hugged her and said, "Yes, the stars are beautiful, just like you." Lily went to sleep, dreaming of the pink stars in the night sky.

Sara and Ben like to create things with clay. They have many colors of clay, but their favorite is purple. They make purple animals, purple flowers, purple cars and purple houses. One day, they decide to create a big purple cake. They roll the clay into a ball and then flatten it with their hands. They use a knife to cut out a circle and put it on a plate. They make another circle and put it on top of the first one. They use a spoon to make some cream and spread it on the cake. They add some purple berries and candles. "Look, we made a purple cake!" Sara says. "It looks yummy!" "Can we eat it?" Ben asks. "No, silly, it's not real. It's clay. We can pretend to eat it," Sara says. They cut some slices of the cake and pretend to eat them. They make yummy noises and smile. They have fun with their purple cake. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is the water in the sink. It is running and running. They forgot to turn it off when they washed their hands. The water is filling the sink and spilling over. It is going to the drain on the floor. "Oh no, the water!" Sara says. "We have to stop it!" They run to the sink and try to turn the knob. But it is too hard. They can't turn it. The water keeps running and running. "Help, help!" Ben says. "We need Mommy!" They shout for Mommy. Mommy hears them and comes to the kitchen. She sees the water and the clay and the purple cake. "What happened here?" Mommy asks. "We were creating a purple cake, but we forgot the water, and now it's going to the drain," Sara says. Mommy turns off the water and cleans up the mess. She is not happy, but she is not angry. She knows they did not mean to make a mess. "Next time, you have to be careful with the water and the clay," Mommy says. "And you have to ask me before you use the knife and the spoon." "We're sorry, Mommy," Sara and Ben say. Mommy hugs them and smiles. She sees their purple cake and says, "It's a very nice cake. You are very creative. But maybe next time, you can use paper and crayons instead of clay."
