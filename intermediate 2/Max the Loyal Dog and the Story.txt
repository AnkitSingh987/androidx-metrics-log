Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Loyal Dog and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max loved to play games with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim found a new game called chess. He wanted to play it with Max. Tim set up the chess pieces and said, "Max, do you want to play chess with me?" Max wagged his tail and barked, showing that he was excited to play. They began to play, but Max didn't know how to move the pieces. Tim taught Max how to play chess by showing him what each piece could do. As they played, Max started to question which moves were best. He would bark once for "yes" and twice for "no" when Tim asked if he wanted to make a certain move. They had lots of fun playing chess together. Max was a quick learner and soon became a good chess player. One day, another boy named Sam came over and wanted to play chess with Max. Max looked at Tim to see if it was okay. Tim nodded and said, "Yes, you can play chess with Max. Just remember to be kind and have fun." Sam and Max played chess, and Max won the game. Sam was sad, but Max licked his face to cheer him up. Tim said, "It's okay to lose, Sam. The important thing is that we all had fun and learned something new." The moral of the story is that it's important to be kind, have fun, and always try to learn new things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a special book called a diary. In the diary, she wrote all her secrets and thoughts. She loved her diary very much. One day, Lucy found a little bird. The bird was stuck in a deep hole. Lucy wanted to help the bird. She tried to reach down, but her arm was not long enough. Then, Lucy had an idea. She used her diary to reach the bird. The bird grabbed the diary with its beak, and Lucy pulled it up. She was happy to release the bird and see it fly away. Lucy learned that helping others is important, even if it means using something special to us. She felt proud of herself for saving the bird. And Lucy knew that she could always write more secrets in a new diary. The moral of the story is that it's good to help others, even when it's not easy for us.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big sister named Lily. Lily liked to tease Mia. She would say, "Mia, there is a witch in the woods. She will come and get you!" One day, Mia and Lily went to play in the woods. Mia was scared, but Lily said, "Don't worry, it's easy to hide from the witch." They played hide and seek, and Mia felt better. As they played, they heard a noise. Mia asked, "Is that the witch?" Lily looked and saw a big, friendly dog. "No, Mia, it's just a dog. There is no witch." Mia felt happy, and they played with the dog until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little green frog. The frog lived near a cold pond. He liked to play with his friends, the fish and the ducks. One day, while playing, the frog saw a big rock. He wanted to jump on it. So, he took a pause and got ready to jump. The frog took a deep breath and jumped high in the air. The frog landed on the cold rock and smiled. He was very happy. Now, he could see his friends in the pond better. The frog, the fish, and the ducks played all day long. They had so much fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a busy bee named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to fly around and find pink flowers to get food. Buzzy had lots of friends who liked to clap their little bee wings when they were happy. One day, Buzzy was flying around, looking for pink flowers. He saw a big pink flower and went to get food from it. But when he got close, the pink flower started to clap! Buzzy was very surprised. The pink flower was not a flower at all! It was a butterfly named Flutter who was also looking for food. Buzzy and Flutter became friends and clapped their wings together. From that day on, they always looked for pink flowers and played together.
