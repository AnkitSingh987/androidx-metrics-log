Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to touch things. One day, she went to her grandma's house. Grandma had many delicate things. Mia was very excited to see them. Grandma said, "Mia, I have a fun game for you. I will hide something, and you will guess what it is." Mia was very happy to play the game. Grandma hid a delicate bird behind her back. Mia tried to guess, "Is it a ball?" Grandma shook her head, "No, try again." Mia thought very hard. Then, she remembered the delicate things in the house. She guessed, "Is it a bird?" Grandma smiled and said, "Yes, you are right!" Mia was very happy that she guessed the right thing. From that day on, Mia learned to be careful with delicate things. She always remembered to think before she touched anything. And she loved playing the guessing game with her grandma.

Lily wanted to draw a picture for her mom. She found some paper and crayons in the drawer. She was very happy. She started to draw a big sun, a blue sky, and a green grass. Her brother Max came into the room. He saw Lily's picture. He did not like it. He said, "That is a terrible picture. You can't draw at all. You should stop." He grabbed the crayons from Lily's hand. He threw them on the floor. Lily felt very sad. She started to cry. She said, "No, Max, that is not nice. I want to draw for mom. She will like it. Please give me back the crayons." Max did not listen. He said, "No, Lily, you are a baby. You can't draw. I can draw better than you. Watch me." He took the paper from Lily. He drew a big monster on it. He said, "This is a good picture. Mom will love it. You should learn from me." Lily cried louder. She said, "No, Max, that is a scary picture. Mom will not love it. You ruined my picture. You are mean. Go away." Max did not care. He said, "No, Lily, you are silly. You don't know anything. I am the best. You are the worst. I will show mom my picture. She will be proud of me. She will not like you." He ran out of the room with the paper. Lily was left alone. She had no paper, no crayons, and no picture. She felt very bad. She wished someone would encourage her. She wished Max would be nice to her. She wished mom would come home soon. She did not know that Max was wrong. She did not know that mom would not like the monster picture. She did not know that mom would be angry with Max. She did not know that mom would hug her and tell her that she loved her picture. She did not know that mom would help her draw a new picture. She did not know that mom would put her picture on the fridge. She did not know that mom would be happy. She only knew that she was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Jack. He wasn't very big or strong, but he was very competitive. Every day, Jack would play all kinds of games with his friends at the park. One day, Jack was playing a game with his friends when he lost his special shoe. He was so sad and started to cry, but then he remembered something. That day, he had been playing a game of hide and seek. He thought his shoe must be hidden somewhere and he needed to rescue it. So, Jack looked all around the park, calling out for his special shoe. He looked in the trees and behind rocks, but no luck. Then he heard a giggle coming from the playground. He carefully went over to the playground and saw a little girl hiding behind the slide. She had his shoe in her hand and she was giggling. Jack was so happy to have his shoe back, but he needed to get from the girl. He knew that he had to think of a game that would be tempting enough for the girl to part with his shoe. So, Jack suggested a game of tag. The girl agreed and Jack was lucky to rescue his shoe when the girl forgot about it in the heat of the chase. Jack was very happy to have his special shoe back, and he was proud of himself for being so competitive. He won the game and rescued his shoe!
