Category: Intermediate
Title: Will the Boy and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Will. Will was a very nice boy, but he was also very dull. He did not like to play with toys or run around with his friends. Will liked to sit and watch the trees and the sky. One day, Will's mom asked him to bring some apples from the tree. Will was happy to help. He went to the tree and picked the apples. As he was picking the apples, he saw a little bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly. Will wanted to help the bird. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea! Will brought the bird some sticks and leaves. The bird used the sticks and leaves to make a little nest. The bird was happy and said "thank you" to Will. From that day on, Will was not dull anymore. He learned that helping others made him happy. Will and the bird became very good friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once there was a lonely tuna who wanted a friend. One day he saw a small fishing boat heading his way. He was so happy that he decided to push his way up and surprise the fishermen inside. But when he reached the boat, he was pushed back away by the big motor. He was so sad to be alone again. The lonely tuna swam back home, and never had a friend again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Susie who loved to dance. She would make up new dances and practice them every day. Susie even asked her mom to make her a special dress, so she could practice all the time. One day, Susie's mom said, "Susie, it's time to clean your room." But Susie didn't want to stop dancing. She asked her mom, "Why do I have to clean my room? Can't I just practice my dances?" But her mom said, "No, Susie, you have to clean. First, put all your toys in the toy box and then dust the shelves with a cloth." Susie was persistent, so she did as her mom said. She put away all the toys and then dusted the shelves. Then she went back to her dancing. Her mom smiled and said, "See? That wasn't so bad. Now you can practice your dances and have fun!" And Susie danced and danced and danced until it was time for bed.

Once upon a time, there were two friends who wanted to have a picnic outside. They packed some tasty food and loaded up their basket. They were ready to have fun! But they ran into a problem. It was getting dark outside and they were both feeling really sleepy. They tried to resist the urge to sleep, but it was hard. So they decided to go inside, find a cozy spot, and have a picnic indoors. That way, they could still eat the food and not get too sleepy! They spread out a blanket and enjoyed their tasty food together. What a great way to spend the night! Even though they were sleepy, they were glad they didn't give up and resisted the urge to go to bed.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a younger brother named Sam. Tim was good at playing with toys, but Sam was a clumsy little boy. He often broke things when he played. One day, Tim wanted to lend his toy car to Sam. Tim said, "Sam, do you want to play with my toy car? But please be careful, okay?" Sam was very happy and said, "Yes, Tim! I will be careful, I promise!" Sam played with the toy car, but he was still clumsy. He accidentally broke the toy car. Sam felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I broke your toy car." Tim hugged his brother and said, "It's okay, Sam. We can fix it together. I love you, brother." They fixed the toy car and played together, having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a dog named Tim. Tim was a tough dog with a big hat. He loved to wear his hat everywhere he went. One day, Tim went to the park. He saw a little cat who was lost and scared. The cat was trembling because it was cold and alone. Tim wanted to help the cat. Tim took off his big hat and put it on the cat to keep it warm. The cat stopped trembling and smiled at Tim. They became best friends and played together in the park every day. And they both wore big hats to keep warm and look tough.
