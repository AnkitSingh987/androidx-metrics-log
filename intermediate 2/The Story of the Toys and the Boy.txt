Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Toys and the Boy

Once upon a time, there lived a little boy who loved playing with his toys in his bedroom. He always layed down his toys on his bedroom floor. One day he heard a noise from his kitchen, the noise sounded very big. The little boy was curious, so he ran over to the kitchen to see what was going on. He saw a huge monster laying on the floor! The little boy was scared and wanted to run away, but he then remembered that he had to be brave. He slowly walked over to the monster and thought it was perhaps just sleeping. The little boy was brave and touched the monster's arm, but the monster moved and the little boy realised it was not sleeping. The little boy smiled because he knew what to do. He ran back to his bedroom and grabbed all his toys. He went back to the kitchen, layed out all the toys in a circle around the monster, and watched as the monster started playing with them. The little boy had learned that with a brave heart, even the biggest things, can be conquered. He smiled, happy with his discovery and ran back to his bedroom.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. Tim's mom said, "Look, Tim! That hole goes underground. It could be interesting to see what's inside." Tim looked inside the hole and saw a little bug. The bug had a leaf attached to its back. "Hello, bug!" Tim said. "Why do you have a leaf on your back?" The bug replied, "I'm taking it home to my family. We use leaves to make our beds." All of a sudden, a strong wind blew the leaf off the bug's back. The bug was sad. Tim had an idea. He found a small stick and attached the leaf to the bug's back using the stick. The bug was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are very kind." Then, the bug went underground to its home with the leaf. Tim and his mom smiled and continued their day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to design pretty things. One day, she found a bell in her toy box. Sue wanted to make the bell look nice. She took the bell to a distant hill far away from her house. Sue put shiny things on the bell. It looked very pretty. But she was far from home now. As the sun went down, Sue got lost. She rang the pretty bell, but no one could hear her. She was too far away. Sue was sad and scared. She missed her home and her family.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was very foolish. He liked to play tricks on his friends. One day, Tim had an idea to become the best trickster in the forest. Tim thought of a funny message to tell his friends. He flew to his friend, the big bear. "The sky is falling!" Tim said. The big bear was scared and ran away. Tim laughed and flew to his next friend, the quick rabbit. "The sky is falling!" Tim said again. The quick rabbit was scared too and ran away. Tim was very happy with his tricks. But soon, Tim's friends found out that the sky was not falling. They were not happy with Tim's foolish message. They told Tim that his tricks were not funny. Tim felt sad and said sorry to his friends. From that day, Tim stopped playing tricks and became a helpful friend to everyone in the forest.

Once there was a little boy who enjoyed going for rides in his dad's jeep. His dad had a special place they liked to go together, so the little boy was always excited when his dad took him. He would complain and wiggle in the jeep until they'd arrive and his dad would say, "Ok, do you want to see it?" The little boy was always amazed by what he saw. This time it was a big old tree, its branches so tall they almost took up the whole sky. He loved running around it and talking to the birds. But one day, when they arrived at the place, the little boy noticed that the tree was gone and his dad was standing there with a shovel in his hands. The little boy was embarrassed and he knew he had something to do with it. His dad explained that the tree was rotten and had to be cut down. The little boy felt ashamed, but his dad told him it was for the best. They planted a flower there instead. The little boy smiled, happy to see something new in its place.
