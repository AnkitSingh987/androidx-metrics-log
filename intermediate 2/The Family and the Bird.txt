Category: Intermediate
Title: The Family and the Bird

Once upon a time there was a family who had a secret. Every day the mom, dad and their little girl would get together and guess what was in the basket. The mom and dad had so much fun guessing what was in the basket. They would smile and laugh and have a great time. Finally one day they were ready to find out what was in the basket. They were all very excited to see what was inside. When they opened the basket, they found a baby bird. The little girl was so happy. She put the baby bird in a safe nest and made sure it was nice and warm. The baby bird stayed with the family for a long time. Each day the little girl would check on the bird and make sure it was safe. The family was so happy to have the baby bird as part of their family. They could not guess how happy they were to have a new addition.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max loved to drive a little red car. He would drive his car to his favorite place, the lab. The lab was a fun place for Max to play and learn new things. One day, Max wanted to show his friends the lab. He thought it would be easy to drive them there. So, Max and his friends got in the little red car and started to drive. They laughed and sang songs as they drove down the road. Finally, they reached the lab. Max and his friends had so much fun playing and learning together. After a long day, they drove back home in the little red car, happy and tired. Max knew that he could always drive to the lab to have a great time with his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to run fast. One day, there was a big race in the village. Tim wanted to win the race, so he practiced running every day. On the day of the race, the sun was shining bright. Tim lined up with the other kids. His friend Sam said, "You are stupid if you think you can win." Tim felt sad but did not say anything. The race started, and Tim ran as fast as he could. He did not listen to Sam's mean words. Tim won the race! Everyone cheered for him. Sam said, "I am sorry for calling you stupid." Tim forgave Sam, and they became good friends. The moral of the story is to believe in yourself and not let others bring you down.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sit by himself and watch the birds. One day, he saw a big knot in a tree. Tom was very curious about the knot. Tom's friend, a happy dog named Max, came over. Max mentioned the knot in the tree too. Tom and Max wanted to see what was inside the knot. They climbed the tree together. When they reached the knot, they found a little bird inside. They thought the bird would be scared, but the bird was happy to see them. The bird said, "Thank you for visiting me!" Tom and Max were surprised. They became friends with the bird and played together all day. Tom was not grumpy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat fruit. His favorite fruit was the twist fruit. The twist fruit was very yummy. Tim ate twist fruit every day. One day, Tim saw a big twist fruit on a tree. He wanted to get it. Tim climbed up the tree. He was not scared. But when he got to the top, he felt ashamed. He knew he should not climb the tree. But he wanted the twist fruit so much. Tim tried to grab the twist fruit. But he lost his balance. Tim fell from the tree. He was hurt. His mom and dad were sad. Tim was sad too. He knew he should not have climbed the tree. Tim learned his lesson. But it was a bad ending for Tim.
