Category: Intermediate
Title: Ella the Girl and the Wolf

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ella. Ella loved to fill her days with stories. One day, she found a big book with many stories inside. She was very happy and started to read the stories to her friends. One of the stories was about a bad wolf. The bad wolf was always mean to others. He would not share and would take things from others. Ella and her friends did not like the bad wolf. They talked about how they should never be like the bad wolf. In the end, the bad wolf learned that being mean was not good. He started to share and be nice to others. Ella and her friends were happy that the bad wolf changed. They learned that being kind and sharing is important. And from that day on, they always tried to be good and kind to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to create things. One day, she found a big box. Sue had an idea. She wanted to make something adorable with the box. Sue worked very hard. She painted the box with pretty colors. She put cute pictures on it. Sue made the box look like a little house. It was so adorable! Sue was very happy with her creation. Then, Sue saw a small cat outside. The cat was cold and hungry. Sue thought about the adorable box she made. She decided to give the cat the box to live in. The cat was warm and happy in the box. Sue felt good about helping the cat. The moral of the story is that it's important to share and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Emma. Emma liked to complete puzzles. Today, she was working on a big puzzle with many pieces. As Emma put the pieces together, she saw that the puzzle was a picture of a cat. The cat was big and had a long tail. But, as she put in the last piece, something unexpected happened. The cat in the puzzle came to life and jumped out of the picture! Emma was surprised but happy to have a new friend. The cat was very nice, and they played together all day. In the end, Emma learned that sometimes, the best things in life can come from the most unexpected places.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there was a princess. She had a big smile and loved to play with her friends. One day, she found a tight box in her room. She did not know what was inside, but she knew it was very special. The princess showed the tight box to her friends. They all wanted to know what was inside. They tried to open it, but it was too tight. The princess had an idea. She said, "Let's ask the wise old owl for help!" They went to the wise old owl, and he told them a secret. The owl said, "The box will open if you all smile together." The princess and her friends were surprised. They all smiled at the same time, and the tight box opened! Inside the box, there was a magic wand. The princess picked it up, and it made her smile even bigger. She waved the wand, and it made her friends smile too. From that day on, the princess and her friends had the biggest smiles in the whole kingdom.

Sara loved to dance. She danced in the morning, she danced in the afternoon, she danced at night. She danced with her doll, she danced with her dog, she danced with her mom and dad. She danced when she was happy, she danced when she was sad, she danced when she was bored. One day, Sara found a new place to dance. It was a big bookcase in the living room. It had many books and pictures and toys on it. Sara thought it looked like a stage. She climbed on the bookcase and started to dance. She twirled and jumped and waved her arms. She felt like a star. But the bookcase was not strong enough for Sara's dance. It wobbled and shook and made a loud noise. Some books and pictures and toys fell down. Sara was scared. She tried to get down, but she slipped and fell too. She hurt her knee and her elbow. She cried. Her dog heard her cry. He was a loyal dog. He ran to Sara and licked her face. He barked and wagged his tail. He wanted to make Sara feel better. He also wanted to warn her mom and dad. Sara's mom and dad heard the noise and the bark. They came to the living room and saw the mess. They saw Sara and her dog on the floor. They were worried. They picked up Sara and hugged her. They checked her knee and her elbow. They said it was okay. They said they were not mad. They said they loved her. Sara felt better. She hugged her mom and dad. She hugged her dog. She said she was sorry. She said she loved them too. She said she would not dance on the bookcase again. She said she would dance on the floor. She said she would dance with her dog. He was a loyal dog. He liked to dance with Sara. They danced together. They were happy.
