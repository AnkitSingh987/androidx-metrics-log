Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Tom's owner went to the grocery store to buy food for Tom and herself. Tom thought it would be fun to go with her. He slipped out the door and followed her to the store. When they got there, Tom saw so many things he wanted to play with. He saw balls, toys, and food. Tom tried to play with the toys, but he slipped on the floor and made a big mess. His owner saw him and said, "Tom, you are a lazy cat, but I love you. Let's go home and clean up." Tom and his owner went home, and Tom learned not to slip into the grocery store again.

Once upon a time, there was a white trumpet. It made a noise people liked to hear. One day, the trumpet blew in the wind and got stuck in a tree. It couldn't come down. Suddenly, a brave guy came to rescue the trumpet. He climbed the tree and was able to get the trumpet down. The trumpet was so happy! It stopped blowing in the wind and started to make music for everyone to enjoy. The brave guy was a hero! He rescued the white trumpet and made everyone smile.

Once upon a time, there was a big, big rocket. It was so big, it was called a missile. The missile was enormous, bigger than a house! It was hiding in a tall, tall box. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim found the tall, tall box. Tim was very curious. He wanted to see what was inside. So, he opened the box to reveal the enormous missile. Tim was so surprised! Tim knew he had to tell someone about the enormous missile. He ran to his mom and dad. They were amazed too! They called some friends to help them move the missile to a safe place. Everyone was happy, and they had a big party to celebrate.

One day, a big box arrives at Tim's house. Tim is very excited. He opens the box and finds a soft, warm bathrobe inside. He puts on the bathrobe and loves how it feels. Tim's friend, Sam, comes over to play. Tim shows Sam his new bathrobe. "Look, Sam! I got a new bathrobe. It is so soft and warm!" Sam says, "Wow, Tim! I like your bathrobe. Can I try it on?" Tim says, "Sure!" Sam tries on the bathrobe, but it is difficult for him to put it on. The bathrobe is too big for Sam. They both laugh and decide to play a game. They have a fun day playing together in Tim's new bathrobe.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a rare cloth in his room. The cloth was red and soft. Tim liked the rare cloth a lot. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. So, he took the rare cloth and went to Sam's house. When Tim showed the rare cloth to Sam, they both decided to play with it. They played catch and hide and seek with the cloth. They had so much fun! But then, a small dog came running towards them. The dog saw the rare cloth and wanted to play too. The dog said, "Can I play with you and the rare cloth?" Tim and Sam looked at each other and said, "Okay, you can play with us." The dog was very happy. They all played together, but then the dog tried to bite the rare cloth. Tim and Sam said, "No, don't bite the cloth!" The dog stopped and they all continued to play happily.

Once upon a time, there was a goose named Gertie. Gertie lived on a farm with her friends. She loved to play with her friends, but sometimes she would do something terrible. Gertie liked to throw things. One day, she found a big red ball. Gertie thought it would be fun to throw the big red ball at her friends. She threw the ball at her friend, the cow. The cow was very sad. Gertie felt bad for making her friend sad. But Gertie could not stop thinking about throwing the ball. The next day, Gertie saw a big tree with a hole in it. She thought it would be fun to throw the ball into the hole. Gertie threw the ball as hard as she could. The ball went into the hole and out came a big, beautiful butterfly. The butterfly said, "Thank you for helping me get out of the tree. I was stuck in there for so long!" Gertie was very happy that her terrible throwing had helped someone. From that day on, Gertie only threw things to help her friends.
