Category: Intermediate
Title: The Church and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a big church in a small town. The church was very pretty and everyone was proud of it. Every day, people went to the church to sing and pray. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the church with his mom. Tim saw that the church was dirty. He said, "Mom, look at the dirt. We should wipe it." His mom agreed and they started to wipe the dirt off the church walls. As they wiped the dirt, they found a hidden door. Tim opened the door and saw a room full of toys. All the kids in the town came to play with the toys. The church became a fun place for everyone, and they were all very proud of it.

Tom and Jane were walking to the park on a Sunday. Tom saw a kite in the sky and started to wave at it. Jane felt guilty for not joining him, so she waved too. When the kite began to fall, Tom and Jane ran to catch it. They ran as fast as they could, but the kite was too far away. They watched sadly as the kite flew away. That evening, Tom and Jane's mum was very cross. She told them that they had been running too close to the road. She said they were not allowed to go to the park on Sundays anymore. Tom and Jane felt guilty and sorry. They knew that it was their fault that they couldn't go again. They wished they had never waved at the kite.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly fish named Fin. He loved to swim near the shore where the water was shallow. Fin had many friends who lived in the sea with him. They would play all day long. One day, Fin's friend, a starfish named Stella, was about to swim too close to the shore. "Stop, Stella!" said Fin. "We must prevent you from going too close. It's not safe!" Stella listened to Fin and stayed away from the shore. Fin and Stella swam back to their friends and continued to play. They were all very happy and had a great day. From then on, they always looked out for each other to keep everyone safe near the shore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to open things, like boxes and doors. One day, she found a big, round box with lots of colors on it. Lily was so excited to see what was inside! Lily opened the colorful box and found many small, round things. They were all different colors: red, green, blue, and yellow. She thought they looked like fun, so she picked up a red one and put it in her mouth. It tasted very sour! Lily made a funny face and laughed. Later, Lily tried the other colors too. The green one was sour, just like the red one. The blue one was sweet, and the yellow one was even sweeter. Lily liked the sweet ones best. She shared the sour ones with her friends, and they all made funny faces together. And that's how Lily learned that sometimes, colors can tell you how things taste.

Once there was a little boy. He was very hungry but he had no food. He looked up at the big roof that he could see. He wondered how he could get up there. He wanted to see if he could find some food. "I'm so hungry", he said. He thought and thought and he had an idea. He tried to climb up the roof. After a few tries, he was able to grab onto something and pull himself up. When he got up to the roof, he saw a lot of food. He was so happy, he wanted to eat all of it. Just as he reached out to grab some food, a big bird swooped down and started pecking at him. The boy was so scared, he ran away. He tried to get back down from the roof, but he could not. He cried and shouted for help, but no one came. In the end, the boy was stuck on the roof and was very hungry.
