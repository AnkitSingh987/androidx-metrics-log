Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fish Swam and the Interesting

One day, a little fish swam in the sea. This fish liked to complain a lot. He would say, "I am not interesting. I wish I was a big, fun fish." One morning, the little fish saw a big, interesting fish. The big fish had many colors and could do fun tricks. The little fish wanted to be friends with the big fish. He thought, "If I am friends with the big fish, I will be interesting too." The little fish followed the big fish. They swam together and did fun tricks. The little fish was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish got caught in a net. The little fish was sad and scared. But he was also brave. He swam fast and helped the big fish get out of the net. The big fish said, "Thank you, little fish. You are very interesting and a great friend." The little fish was happy and didn't complain anymore.

One day, a clumsy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a mitten on the ground. Tom wanted to touch the mitten. He put out his paw and touched it. The mitten was soft and warm. Tom heard a voice say, "Hey, that's my mitten!" It was a little girl named Sue. She was looking for her mitten. Tom felt bad for touching Sue's mitten. Tom picked up the mitten with his mouth and gave it to Sue. She said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom was happy he could help Sue. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to listen to the birds sing and the wind blow. She loved to play by the pool in her backyard. Lily had a friend named Max. Max was deaf, so he could not hear the birds or the wind, but he could still have fun with Lily. One sunny day, Lily and Max were playing by the pool. They laughed and splashed water at each other. Then, something unexpected happened. A big frog jumped into the pool! Lily and Max were so surprised. Lily tried to tell Max about the frog, but he could not hear her. Then, Lily had an idea. She pointed at the frog and Max saw it too. Max jumped into the pool and caught the frog. They both laughed and played with the frog. Even though Max could not hear, he and Lily had a great time by the pool.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite shirt. It was red and soft. He wore it all the time. One day, Tim went to the park to play. At the park, he saw a big tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but it was difficult. He tried and tried, but he couldn't do it. Then, a friendly girl named Sue came to help. She said, "Let's try together!" Tim and Sue tried to climb the tree. They held hands and helped each other. Finally, they did it! They were so happy. But suddenly, Tim started to fall. Sue caught him just in time. "Thank you, Sue!" Tim said with a big smile. They both laughed and played together for the rest of the day. And Tim's favorite shirt stayed nice and clean.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box with many toys. One day, his favorite toy, a small car, broke. Tim felt sad. He wanted to fix his car, but he needed glue. Tim asked his mom for glue. His mom said, "Thank you for asking nicely, Tim." She gave him the glue. Tim was happy. He used the glue to fix his car. The car was not spoiled anymore. After the car was fixed, Tim played with it all day. He was very happy. Before going to bed, Tim said, "Mom, thank you for the glue." His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad you could fix your car."

Once upon a time, there was an ancient pot. It was a very old pot, a magical pot filled with impossible dreams and surprises. One day, a boy named Jimmy decided to do something special. He wanted to open the ancient pot and play with whatever he found inside it. Jimmy was a brave little boy, so he walked towards the pot, placed his hands on the lid and said, “I’m going to open you!” But, the ancient pot spoke to him, “Should you do that? Who knows what’s inside me? Maybe something dangerous!” Jimmy was surprised, but he smiled and said, “Don’t worry! I’ll be careful!” Then he opened the ancient pot and found a beautiful surprise inside.
