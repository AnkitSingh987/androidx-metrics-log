Category: Intermediate
Title: The Party and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a big party. A nice boy named Tim wanted to invite all his friends to come. He was very happy. He made cheap paper hats and fun games for everyone to play. On the day of the party, Tim's friend Sue got sick. She had to go to the hospital. Tim was sad. He wanted Sue to come to the party too. He thought the party would not be fun without her. The party started, but Tim was still sad. He missed Sue a lot. Then, it started to rain. The rain ruined the cheap paper hats and the games. The party was over. Tim and his friends went home feeling sad.

Jen and Sam were twins who liked to play in the forest. One day, they found a big gray rock that sparkled in the sun. They saw that it had many crystals inside it, like little stars. "Wow, look at this!" Jen said. "It is so pretty!" "Let's take it home!" Sam said. "We can show it to Mom and Dad!" They tried to lift the rock, but it was too heavy. They pushed and pulled, but it did not move. "Maybe we can break it," Jen said. "Then we can take some crystals." She picked up a stick and hit the rock hard. But the stick broke and the rock did not. Jen hurt her hand and cried. "Stop, Jen!" Sam said. "You are hurting yourself and the rock. It is not nice to break things that are not yours." "But I want a crystal!" Jen said. "They are so shiny!" Sam hugged Jen and said, "I know, Jen. But sometimes we have to be happy with what we have. The rock is beautiful as it is. And we have each other. That is more important than any crystal." Jen nodded and said, "You are right, Sam. I am sorry. I love you." They kissed and said sorry to the rock. Then they decided to rest under a tree and watch the rock sparkle. They felt happy and peaceful. The moral of the story is: We should not be greedy and hurtful, but be grateful and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One hot day, they decided to make cookies together. They got all the things they needed and started to mix everything in a big bowl. Tim was so happy to help his mom. After a few minutes, the cookie dough was ready. Tim's mom put the cookies in the hot oven. They had to wait for a little while. Tim was very excited and could not wait to eat the cookies. He danced around the kitchen and counted the minutes. When the cookies were done, Tim helped his mom take them out of the oven. They were very hot, so they had to wait for a minute to cool down. Tim and his mom shared the cookies and they were so yummy. Tim felt proud because he helped his mom make the best cookies ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Carol. She was three years old and liked to go on adventures. One day, Carol went outside to play. Suddenly, she found a big needle in the grass! She was very scared and started to panic. She wanted to get away from the needle. Carol ran back to her house to tell her mom. Her mom told her that needles are important objects and she should always be careful when around them. Carol was very happy to go back outside and play. She made sure to be careful and never pick up a needle again!

Once upon a time, there was a little, messy puppy. He loved to play in the mud. One day, he saw a big cat. The cat looked at the puppy and said, "You are very messy!" The puppy just shrugged and said, "I like being messy!" The cat and the puppy started to play together. They ran and jumped in the mud. The cat got messy too. After playing, the cat and the puppy were both very tired. They sat down and looked at each other. The cat said, "Being messy is fun!" The puppy smiled and they both went home to take a bath.
