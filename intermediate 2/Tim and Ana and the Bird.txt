Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Ana and the Bird

Tim and Ana are friends who like to play in the jungle. They have a big tree house where they keep their toys and books. One day, they decide to explore a new part of the jungle, where they see many colorful birds and flowers. "Look, Tim, a blue bird!" Ana says, pointing to a branch. "It is so pretty!" "Yes, it is!" Tim agrees. "Do you think it can sing?" They listen carefully, but they do not hear any sound from the bird. They wonder why it is so quiet. "Maybe it is shy," Ana says. "Or maybe it is sad." "Maybe it needs a friend," Tim says. "Let's try to encourage it to sing." They climb up the tree and sit near the bird. They smile and talk to it softly. "Hello, blue bird," Ana says. "We are Tim and Ana. We are your friends. You have a beautiful voice. Can you sing for us?" "Yes, please, blue bird," Tim says. "We want to hear your song. It will make us happy. And you too." The bird looks at them with curious eyes. It sees that they are friendly and kind. It feels a little less shy and sad. It opens its beak and sings a sweet melody. Tim and Ana clap and cheer. They are so happy to hear the bird sing. The bird is happy too. It sings louder and louder, and soon other birds join in. "Wow, Tim, you were right!" Ana says. "The bird just needed a friend to encourage it to sing. Now it has many friends!" "Yes, Ana, you were right too!" Tim says. "The bird is not shy or sad anymore. It is healthy and happy!" They hug each other and laugh. They are glad they helped the bird. They decide to visit it again soon. They love the jungle and all its creatures.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to go on trips with his friends. One day, he went on a trip to the park with his best friend, Sam. At the park, Sam had a big, red ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball, but he did not have one. Sam saw that Tim was sad, so Sam let Tim borrow his ball. They played together and had lots of fun. But then, a selfish girl named Sue came to the park. Sue wanted to play with the ball too, but she did not want to share. Tim and Sam did not like that. They told Sue that it was not nice to be selfish. Sue learned that sharing was better, and they all played with the ball together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. Sally loved to paint. She had a long paintbrush and bright colors. One day, she decided to paint a picture of her friend, Tom. Tom came over to Sally's house to pose for the painting. He sat on a chair in front of a big white canvas. Sally dipped her long paintbrush into the colors and began to paint Tom's face. She painted his eyes, nose, and mouth. "Can I see my face in the painting?" Tom asked. Sally said, "Yes, come look!" Tom looked at the painting and saw his eyes in it. They were big and bright. He was happy with the painting and thanked Sally for making it. They both smiled and continued to paint together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play outside in the sun. One day, the sun was very hot, and the heat made Kitty feel tired. But Kitty wanted to try to catch a butterfly, so she went outside to play. As Kitty chased the butterfly, she met a little bird named Birdy. Birdy was also feeling the heat. "Kitty, it's so hot today! Can we play together?" asked Birdy. Kitty said, "Yes, let's try to catch the butterfly together!" Kitty and Birdy tried and tried, but the butterfly was too fast. They were having so much fun, they forgot about the heat. After a while, they got tired and decided to rest under a big tree. The tree gave them shade from the heat. Kitty and Birdy became best friends. They played together every day, even on hot days. They always tried new games and had lots of fun. And when the heat was too much, they knew they could always find shade under the big tree.
