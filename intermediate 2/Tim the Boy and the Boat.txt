Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boat

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some chocolate. Tim loved chocolate a lot. It was his favorite treat. Inside the store, there were many types of chocolate. Tim looked around and picked the one he liked best. At home, Tim's mom showed him how to fold a piece of paper to make a boat. Tim was very excited. He wanted to put the chocolate inside the boat. They folded the paper together, and the boat was ready. Tim put the chocolate inside the boat and said, "Look, mom! My chocolate is going on a trip!" Just then, they heard a loud noise outside. Tim and his mom went to see what it was. They saw a big truck driving by. Tim looked at his mom and said, "Mom, the truck is very loud!" His mom agreed and said, "Yes, it is. Let's go back inside and play with your chocolate boat." They went back inside and had fun playing with the paper boat and eating the chocolate.

Anna likes to help her mom at work. Her mom works with many files. Files are papers with words and numbers on them. Her mom puts the files in big boxes and gives them names. Anna thinks her mom is very smart. One day, Anna goes to work with her mom. Her mom has to do many things. She has to talk on the phone, write on the computer, and meet with other people. Anna wants to help her mom, but she does not know how. She sees a box of files on the floor. The box is open and the files are messy. Anna thinks she can make the files neat. She picks up a file and looks at it. It has a name on it. She tries to find another file with the same name. She does this for all the files. She puts the files with the same name together. She thinks she is managing the files. She is very proud of herself. She calls her mom and says, "Mom, look! I managed the files for you. They are all neat and tidy. Aren't I smart?" Her mom looks at the box of files. She sees that Anna mixed up the files. The files are not in the right order. They are not by date or by topic. They are by name. Her mom is not happy. She says, "Anna, thank you for trying to help me, but you did not manage the files correctly. You made a big mistake. You have to put the files back the way they were. This is very important for my work. Please be careful next time." Anna feels sad. She did not mean to make a mistake. She thought she was helping her mom. She says, "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to be smart like you. I wanted to manage the files for you. I did not know I had to put them by date or by topic. I only saw the names. How can I learn to manage the files like you?" Her mom hugs her and says, "It's okay, Anna. I know you meant well. You are smart, but you have to learn more. Managing files is not easy. It takes time and practice. I will teach you how to do it, but not today. Today, you can help me by doing something else. How about you draw me a picture of us working together? I would love that." Anna smiles and says, "Okay, mom. I will draw you a picture. I love you, mom." Her mom says, "I love you too, Anna."

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a small bird named Tilly. Tilly was a reliable friend, always there to help. One day, Tilly saw a tiny twig on the ground. She wanted to use the twig to fix her nest. As Tilly picked up the twig, she met a big bear named Ben. Ben was hungry and cold. He said, "Tilly, can you help me find food? I'm so hungry and I don't want to freeze." Tilly wanted to help her new friend, so she put down the twig and went with Ben. They looked for food, but it was hard to find. The sun went down and it got very cold. Tilly and Ben started to freeze. They found a cave to hide in, but it was dark and scary. Suddenly, they heard a loud sound. It was a big monster! The monster chased Tilly and Ben out of the cave. They ran away as fast as they could, but the monster caught up to them. The monster was not nice, and the story had a bad ending for Tilly and Ben.
