Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cool Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a tall chimney. He loved to play and jump all day long. One day, Tom saw a bird on the chimney. He wanted to play with the bird. So, Tom climbed up the chimney to say hello. The bird was happy to see Tom, and they became friends. Tom and the bird liked to dance and sing together. When they danced, their friends would clap their hands and applaud. Tom and the bird were so happy, and they played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat. One day, Tom found a menu. The menu had many yummy things like fish, cake, and milk. Tom was very happy. Tom wanted to eat everything on the menu. But he knew he had to manage. So, he made a plan. First, he would eat fish. Then, he would eat cake. Last, he would drink milk. Tom followed his plan. He ate the fish. It was good. He ate the cake. It was sweet. He drank the milk. It was cold. Tom was very successful. He ate everything on the menu and was full. Tom was a happy cat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his big toy car. One day, he found a cable under the bed. It was a long and thin cable. Tim had an idea. He wanted to use the cable to pull his toy car. Tim was very careful when he tied the cable to the car. He did not want to break his toy. He started to pull the car with the cable. Tim was having so much fun. He pulled the car around the house. Then, something unexpected happened. When Tim went to squeeze the cable tighter, a big, friendly dog jumped out from behind the couch! The dog had been hiding there all along. The dog started to play with Tim and the toy car too. They all had a great time playing together. And that is how Tim found his new best friend, the big, friendly dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a troubled boy. He did not like to share his toys with others. One day, Tim found a big book with many pages. He was very happy. Tim wanted to gain more pages for his book. He thought that more pages would make him the happiest boy ever. So, he went to his friends and took their pages. They were sad, but Tim did not care. In the end, Tim had many pages, but no friends. He was very sad and all alone. Tim learned that having many pages did not make him happy. He wished he had been nice to his friends instead.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a triangle. The triangle was very serious. One day, the triangle met a circle. The circle liked to play and have fun. The circle said, "Hi, triangle! Let's play!" The triangle thought for a moment and said, "Okay, but only if we finish our work first." The circle agreed, and they both started to work. They had to move big blocks from one side of the village to the other. They worked very hard, and soon they were almost finished. But then, something unexpected happened. A big square came rolling down the hill and knocked all the blocks they had moved! The triangle and the circle were surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They knew they had to start all over again, but now they were not so serious. They had fun while they worked, and they finished even faster than before.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. The rock was very dry because the sun was hot. The rock had a friend, a little bird. They liked to play together. One day, the bird had an idea. "Let's play a game," said the bird. "We can race to see who is faster." The rock liked the idea. They started the race. The bird flew fast, and the rock rolled fast too. The bird and the rock raced all day. At the end of the day, the bird was tired, but the rock was not. The rock won the race. The bird was happy for the rock's win. They both laughed and played more games together.
