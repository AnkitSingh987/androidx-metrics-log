Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in his big, rough garden. One sunny day, he saw a little vegetable poking out of the ground. It was a tiny carrot! Tim said, "Hi, little carrot! Do you want to join me and play?" The little carrot wiggled and said, "Yes, Tim! I would love to join you and play in your rough garden." So, Tim and the little carrot played all day long. They laughed and had so much fun together. When the sun went down, Tim put the little carrot back in the ground, so it could sleep. The next day, Tim went back to the garden to play with the little carrot again. And they lived happily ever after.

Jill was a very independent girl. She liked to do things by herself, without help from her mom or dad. She liked to look at books, play with her toys, and explore the garden. One day, she saw some red cherries on a tree in the garden. She wanted to pick them and eat them. She thought they looked yummy and juicy. She did not ask her mom or dad if she could go to the tree. She just ran to it and climbed on a branch. "Jill, where are you?" her mom called from the house. She did not hear her mom. She was busy reaching for the cherries. She grabbed one and put it in her mouth. It was sweet and sour. She liked it. She wanted more. She stretched her arm to get another cherry. But she lost her balance and fell from the tree. She hit the ground hard and hurt her head. She cried out loud. She felt pain and fear. Her mom heard her cry and ran to the garden. She saw Jill lying on the grass, bleeding from her head. She was scared and sad. She picked up Jill and hugged her. She called her dad and told him to come quickly. They took Jill to the hospital. Jill had to stay there for many days. She had a big bandage on her head. She could not play or look at books. She wished she had listened to her mom and dad. She learned a hard lesson. She was not so independent anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was very sleepy. She let out a big yawn. It was time for bed. Fluffy had a flashlight that she liked to take to bed. She turned it on and saw many fun shadows on the wall. She played with the shadows and smiled. After a while, Fluffy got very sleepy again. She yawned one more time and turned off her flashlight. She closed her eyes and went to sleep. Fluffy had happy dreams all night.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a cute dog named Spot. Spot was an adorable dog with big, round eyes and a wagging tail. Lily and Spot loved to play in the park every day. One day, Lily found a cane on the ground. She picked it up and said, "Look, Spot! I found a cane!" Spot barked happily and wagged his tail. Lily and Spot played with the cane, throwing it and fetching it, having lots of fun. After a few days, the cane started to rot. Lily said, "Oh no, Spot! The cane is bad now." Spot looked sad, but Lily had an idea. She said, "Don't worry, Spot. We can find a new toy to play with!" Spot barked and wagged his tail, excited to find a new toy. And so, Lily and Spot continued to have fun and play in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a secret. Sue found an unusual big box. She wanted to start to open it, but she was scared. Sue asked her friend Tom to help her open the box. When they opened it, they saw a big red ball. They played with the ball, but something unexpected happened. The ball started to grow bigger and bigger! Sue and Tom were very surprised. The big red ball was not just a ball, it was a big balloon! The balloon took them up high into the sky. They had a fun adventure and then the balloon took them back home. Sue and Tom learned that even unusual secrets can be fun.
