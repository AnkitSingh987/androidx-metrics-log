Title: Anna and the Says

Anna likes fashion. She likes to wear pretty dresses and shoes. She likes to look at magazines and see what other girls wear. She wants to be like them. But Anna's mom does not have a lot of money. She works hard, but she can only buy cheap clothes for Anna. Anna does not like her clothes. They are old and boring. They do not look like the clothes in the magazines. One day, Anna sees a dress in a store window. It is pink and sparkly. It has flowers and ribbons. It is the most beautiful dress Anna has ever seen. She wants it so much. She asks her mom to buy it for her. But her mom says no. She says the dress is too expensive. She says they have to save money for food and rent. She says Anna has enough clothes at home. She says they have to go. Anna is sad and angry. She does not want to go. She wants the dress. She cries and screams. She kicks and hits. She makes a big fuss. Her mom is tired and frustrated. She does not know what to do. She tries to calm Anna down. She tries to explain. She tries to bargain. But Anna does not listen. She only wants the dress. Then, a lady comes by. She is wearing a nice suit and a hat. She has a big smile and a kind voice. She sees Anna and her mom. She stops and talks to them. She says she is a fashion designer. She says she makes clothes for magazines and shows. She says she likes Anna's style and spirit. She says she has a gift for her. She reaches into her bag and pulls out a dress. It is not pink and sparkly. It is blue and soft. It has stars and buttons. It is a different kind of beautiful. She gives the dress to Anna. She says it is a special dress. She says she made it herself. She says it is one of a kind. She says it is perfect for Anna. Anna is surprised and curious. She takes the dress and looks at it. She feels the fabric and the details. She likes the dress. It is different, but it is nice. She says thank you to the lady. She says she likes the dress. She says she wants to try it on. The lady says yes. She says she will help her. She says they can go to the fitting room. She says she will lift Anna up and put her on the stool. She says she will zip her up and fix her hair. She says she will make her look like a star. Anna smiles and nods. She says yes. She says she wants to go. She says she wants to see. She holds the dress and the lady's hand. She follows her to the fitting room. She leaves her mom and the store window behind. She goes to try on her new dress. She goes to see a new fashion.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was going on a trip with her family. Lily had a big luggage full of her favorite toys and clothes. She was very excited to go on this trip. On the way to their car, Lily saw a small kitten. The kitten looked helpless and sad. Lily wanted to help the kitten, but she knew she had to go on the trip with her family. She tried to resist her feelings, but she could not. Lily decided to tell her mom and dad about the kitten. Her mom and dad saw how much Lily cared for the kitten. They decided to help the kitten too. They took the kitten home and gave it food and a warm place to sleep. The kitten was very happy, and so was Lily. The family went on their trip, and when they came back, the kitten was still there waiting for them. Lily learned that it is important to help others, even when it is hard to resist doing something fun.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful town, there was a big festival. Everyone was happy and excited. They ate yummy food and played fun games. A little girl named Lucy went to the festival with her mom. Lucy's mom said, "Stay close to me and don't leave." Lucy said, "Ok, mom." But while they were walking, Lucy saw a big, colorful balloon. She let go of her mom's hand and ran to get it. As Lucy picked up the balloon, she turned around and saw that her mom was not there. She was scared and started to cry. Then, a nice lady saw her and said, "Don't cry, little girl. I will help you find your mom." They looked for Lucy's mom together. Then, something unexpected happened. The nice lady took off her hat and it was Lucy's mom! She had dressed up to surprise Lucy at the festival. Lucy was so happy to see her mom, and they hugged each other. From that day on, Lucy never left her mom's side at the festival.
