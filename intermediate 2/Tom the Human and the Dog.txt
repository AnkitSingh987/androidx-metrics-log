Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Human and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a human named Tom. Tom was a kind man who had a dog named Max. They were best friends and played together every day. One day, Tom had to send a letter to his friend who lived far away, but he was frustrated because he didn't know how to send it. Tom asked Max for help. Max was a smart dog and knew what to do. Max took the letter in his mouth and ran to the post office. The postman saw Max and took the letter from him. The postman smiled and said, "Thank you, Max! You are a very helpful dog." Tom was so happy when he found out that Max had sent the letter. He hugged Max and said, "You are the best friend a human could have!" From that day on, Tom and Max learned that they could always help each other when they were frustrated. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is: always help your friends when they need you, and never give up when you are frustrated.

Once upon a time, in an ancient forest, there was a big tree. The tree had a lot of fruits. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the forest. He saw the tree and wanted to taste the fruits. He thought they would be yummy. Tim set a plan to get the fruits. He found a long stick and used it to reach the fruits on the tree. He pulled the fruits down with the stick. The fruits fell on the ground. Tim was happy. Tim picked up the fruits and took a taste. They were very sweet and juicy. He shared the fruits with his friends. They all loved the taste too. They played in the forest and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom had a green ball that he loved to play with. Every day, he would play with his green ball in the yard. One day, Tom left his green ball in the yard. He went inside the house to eat his food. When he came back outside, he saw that his green ball was gone. Tom was very sad and wanted to find his green ball. Tom looked everywhere for his green ball. He asked his friends if they had seen it. His friend, the dog, found the green ball under a big tree. Tom was so happy to have his green ball back. From that day on, Tom never left his green ball outside again.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her friends, Doggy and Bunny. One sunny day, they were playing in the yard when they saw a pan. Kitty said, "Look, a pan! Let's play with it." Doggy and Bunny agreed, and they took turns kicking the pan. The pan made a loud sound every time they kicked it. As they played, a bird flew down and asked, "Can I play too?" Kitty, Doggy, and Bunny said, "Yes! Let's all play together." So, they kicked the pan and had fun all day long.

Once upon a time there was a scary monster living in the forest. He had two big feet and two big hands. His head was big and round and he had sharp teeth. One day a brave knight arrived at the forest to deliver the monster. The knight was very strong and brave. He walked slowly towards the scariest part of the forest where the monster was hiding. When the monster saw the knight, he said, "Who are you and why did you come here?" The knight replied, "I'm here to deliver you. So come with me now!" The monster was scared but he had no choice but to follow the knight. He slowly walked behind the knight all the way to a castle where the knight was staying. When they arrived at the castle, the knight said, "I have delivered you and now you must stay here." The monster was scared but he did as he was told. He stayed in the castle and never left the knight's side. From then on, the knight protected the monster and they became best friends.
