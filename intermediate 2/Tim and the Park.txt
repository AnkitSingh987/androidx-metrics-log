Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Park

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with many pears on it. Tim loved pears, so he asked his mom if he could pick one. His mom said, "Yes, you can pick one pear, but be careful." Tim tried to reach the pear, but it was too high. He saw a deaf man driving a small truck nearby. The deaf man saw Tim and stopped. He pointed at the pear and smiled. Tim nodded and said, "Yes, I want the pear, but it's too high for me." The deaf man understood Tim's words. He got out of his truck and picked the pear for Tim. Tim said, "Thank you!" The deaf man smiled and waved goodbye. Tim and his mom went home, and Tim enjoyed eating the sweet pear.

Once upon a time, there was a doctor who liked to help people. The doctor was very independent and did everything by himself. One day, the doctor was walking in the park when he saw a little girl crying. The doctor went to the girl and asked, "Why are you crying?" The little girl did not answer at first. Then she said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" The doctor and the little girl looked for the toy together. They looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and near the pond. They couldn't find the toy anywhere. Just when they were about to give up, a big bird came flying down from the sky. The bird had the little girl's toy in its beak! The bird dropped the toy in front of the little girl. She was so happy to have her toy back. The doctor was surprised by the bird's help but happy for the girl. The little girl smiled and said, "Thank you, doctor!" From that day on, the doctor learned that sometimes it's good to have help from others.

Once there was a boy who loved to ride. He was always outside, riding his bike. One day, he was riding near the beach and he saw a big shell. He was so excited, he picked it up and put it in his pocket. The next day he was very upset because he couldn't find his shell. He looked everywhere but it was nowhere. He was so sad. But then a surprise happened! He was riding his bike and he saw the shell lying on the road! He quickly rode over to it and was so happy to have it back. He rode all the way home with a big smile on his face.

Once there was a little boy called Sam. He was three years old and loved to learn. One day, Sam’s dad took him to the jungle. Sam had never been to the jungle before and was very excited. He learnt all about the animals that lived there, the trees, and the plants. Sam’s dad was very helpful. He showed him lots of things and taught him about how to stay safe in the jungle. Sam had lots of fun and learnt lots of new things. He made some new animal friends and even helped his dad collect some fruits from the jungle. At the end of the day, Sam and his dad went home. Sam had had a wonderful day in the jungle and was very happy. He couldn’t wait to go back again soon!

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, were playing in the park. They were having a lot of fun. Tim had a ball and Max would run to get it. Tim's mom called out, "I made a delicious snack for you!" Tim and Max ran to her. She had a plate of yummy cookies. They ate the cookies and were very happy. Tim's mom said, "I have a surprise for you, Tim." She showed him a video of a big, fun party. In the video, there were lots of kids and balloons. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, green dinosaur came into the party! The kids were scared at first, but the dinosaur said, "Don't be scared. I am here to serve you more delicious treats!" The kids laughed and clapped. The dinosaur gave them more cookies and everyone was happy. Tim and Max loved the video and couldn't wait to meet the dinosaur one day.
