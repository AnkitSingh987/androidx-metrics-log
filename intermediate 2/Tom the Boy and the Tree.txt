Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He was a sleepy boy who loved to nap under a big cherry tree. One sunny day, Tom went to his favorite cherry tree to take a nap. As Tom lay down, he saw a squirrel. The squirrel looked at Tom and said, "Why do you sleep here all the time?" Tom frowned and said, "I like to sleep under the cherry tree because it's cozy and quiet." One day, the sky turned dark and grey. It started to rain very hard. Tom was still under the cherry tree, but he did not want to leave. The rain made Tom sad and he frowned. The squirrel came back and said, "You should go home, Tom. It's not safe here." Tom didn't listen to the squirrel. He stayed under the cherry tree, even though he was cold and wet. The rain got stronger and stronger. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the cherry tree down. Tom was hurt and cried. He should have listened to the squirrel and gone home.

Once upon a time, there was a big cabinet in a room. It was full of toys. In the room, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a selfish boy. He did not like to share his toys with anyone. One day, Tim's friend, Max, came to play with him. Max wanted to play with the toys in the cabinet, but Tim did not let him. Max was sad and sat in a corner. Tim continued to play alone. Suddenly, a magic fairy appeared in the room. She saw Max sitting sad and Tim being selfish. The fairy waved her wand, and the cabinet disappeared! Tim looked around and gazed at the empty space where the cabinet was. He realized that he had been selfish and should have shared his toys with Max. Tim said sorry to Max and promised to be a better friend. The fairy saw this and brought back the cabinet. From that day on, Tim shared his toys and played happily with Max.

The sun was setting and the sky was getting gloomy. Carlos was walking home when he heard a rustling noise in a nearby bush. He went closer to take a look and saw a note next to a small box. He opened the box and found a small bird inside. The bird was scared and Carlos tried to calm it down. "Don't be scared, little bird. I won't hurt you," he whispered. The bird calmed down a bit and Carlos read the note. It said that the bird's owner was going away for a long time and couldn't take him along. Carlos felt sad for the bird and decided to keep him. He took the bird home and made a small nest for him. He closed the window so the bird wouldn't fly away and fed him his favorite snacks. He also wrote a note of his own explaining that he had taken the bird in. The little bird was very grateful and they became the best of friends. Living with him made Carlos forget all his worries and sadness, even on the gloomiest of days.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was very cold, so he put on his big coat. Tim saw a rich man walk by. The rich man had a big hat and a big coat too. Tim wanted to say hi, so he ran up to the man. "Hi!" said Tim. "Why do you have a big hat and coat?" The rich man smiled and said, "I need them to keep me warm. It's very cold today!" Suddenly, a big dog came running by. It was in a rush! The dog bumped into the rich man and knocked off his hat. The hat flew through the air and landed on Tim's head. Tim laughed and the rich man laughed too. The rich man said, "You look good in that hat. You can keep it!" Tim was so happy. He thanked the rich man and ran home to show his mom. His mom was surprised but happy too. From that day on, Tim wore his new hat every time it was cold outside.
