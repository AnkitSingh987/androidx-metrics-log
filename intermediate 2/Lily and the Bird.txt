Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a friend named Ben. They liked to play together in the park. One day, they saw a graceful bird. The bird had big wings and it could fly high in the sky. Lily and Ben watched the bird and they wondered where it was going. Lily said, "I wonder if the bird has a friend too." Ben thought for a moment and said, "Maybe the bird is looking for a friend." They decided to follow the bird to see if it found a friend. The bird flew to a big tree. In the tree, there was another bird. The two birds sang a happy song together. Lily and Ben smiled. They were glad the bird found a friend. They played in the park until the sun went down, and then they went home to their families.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She wanted to learn lots of things and so, everyday she asked her mummy to teach her something new. One day, Lucy wanted to learn how to make butter. Mummy taught Lucy all about how to get the butter. Lucy loved making her own butter and often shared with her friends. But one of Lucy's friends, Emily, was so envious of Lucy's ability to make butter that she refused to share with her. Lucy felt really sad and asked her Mummy why Emily had refused to share. Mummy explained to Lucy that sometimes people feel jealous when you are good at something and that it was important to remember to be kind to your friends and share with them. From then on, Lucy always shared her butter with everyone, even with Emily. Moral of the story: Sharing is caring.

Tim and Sam were playing in the yard. They saw a big box near the fence. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's open it!" Tim said. "OK!" Sam said. They tried to lift the box, but it was too heavy. They could not move it. "Maybe we can use this!" Tim said. He found a saw near the shed. He picked it up and ran to the box. "Be careful!" Sam said. "That saw is sharp!" Tim did not listen. He tried to cut the box with the saw, but he did not know how. He pushed and pulled the saw, but it did not work. "Let me try!" Sam said. He took the saw from Tim and lead him to the other side of the box. He showed him how to use the saw. He moved it back and forth, slowly and steadily. He made a hole in the box. "Look!" Sam said. He put his hand in the hole and pulled out something. It was a toy car. It was shiny and red. "Wow!" Tim said. He put his hand in the hole and pulled out something else. It was a toy truck. It was big and blue. They smiled and hugged each other. They had found a treasure. They played with the toys until it was time to go inside. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a little tree. The tree was not very high, but it was happy. The little tree had a dream to grow big and strong. One day, the little tree saw a pretty flower bloom next to it. The little tree asked the flower, "How did you grow so pretty?" The flower said, "I got help from my friend, the sun. The sun gives me light, and I grow pretty and strong." The little tree wanted to be like the flower, so it tried to grow high to reach the sun. One day, a man came to the forest with an axe. The man wanted to cut down the little tree. But the pretty flower said, "Please, do not cut the little tree. It wants to grow high and strong like me." The man felt sorry for the little tree and did not cut it down. The little tree was very happy and thanked the flower. They both grew high and strong together, with the help of the sun. The moral of the story is to be kind and help others, just like the flower helped the little tree.
