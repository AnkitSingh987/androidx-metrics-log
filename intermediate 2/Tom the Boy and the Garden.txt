Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Garden

Once there was a boy named Tom. Tom wanted to get a chocolate from his mum, but his mum said no. Tom was very disappointed, but he tried to ignore it. Tom's mum said, "Tom, you don't need chocolate. It's not good for you. Eat something healthy." Tom was sad but he decided to try something else. He climbed a high tree in the garden. But when he got to the top, he realised it was too high and he was scared. So he decided to ignore his fear and slowly climbed down. When Tom reached the bottom, his mum hugged him and said "I'm very proud of you for not ignoring your fear and climbing down. That takes a lot of courage." Tom learned a valuable lesson that day - Whenever you face difficulty, don't ignore it. But don't be afraid to try something new. With courage and determination, every challenge can be conquered.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. She loved to work with yarn. Mia would make delicate things like scarves and hats for her friends. One day, Mia found a big ball of yarn in her room. It was so big and colorful! Mia went to her friend Tom and said, "Look at this big yarn! Let's make something together." Tom was excited and said, "Yes, let's do it!" So, they started to work with the yarn. They made a long, long scarf. It was so long that it could wrap around the whole town. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the scarf away! Mia and Tom were sad. They thought their hard work was gone forever. But the next day, they saw the scarf hanging in a tree. The wind had wrapped the scarf around the tree to keep it warm. Mia and Tom learned that even when things don't go as planned, good things can still happen. They were happy to see their scarf help the tree. And from that day on, Mia and Tom always worked together to make more beautiful things for their town.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a big tree with a small hole in it. Lucy thought it was the perfect spot to sit and have her snack. She sat down and took out her yummy apple. A little squirrel came up to Lucy and said, "Hello! That apple looks very good. Can I have some?" Lucy smiled and broke off a piece of her apple for the squirrel. They shared the snack and talked about their day. The squirrel told Lucy that the hole in the tree was his home. He said, "This is where I belong. I keep my food and my toys here." Lucy was happy to make a new friend and share her snack. They played together until it was time for Lucy to go home.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the pool with his mom. They wanted to swim and have fun. The pool was big and blue. Tim saw a broken toy by the pool. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. While they were swimming, Tim saw a fish in the pool. The fish was not like other fish. It could talk! The fish said, "Hi, I am a talking fish. Can you help me?" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that fish could talk. Tim and his mom helped the talking fish. They put the fish in a big jar with water. They took the fish to the sea. The fish was happy to be back in the sea. The fish said, "Thank you for helping me." Tim and his mom were happy too. They went back to the pool and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Tom. Tom liked to run very fast, so he was always running here, there and everywhere. One day, Tom noticed a dark juice over by the wall. He had never seen such juice before, so he wanted to try it. Tom ran over to the juice and quickly picked it up. He took a big gulp, but it was too spicy for his taste. He started to run away, but he was going much too fast and slipped over. He banged his head and started to cry. Suddenly, an old man appeared from behind the wall. He said: "Don't worry, Tom. That juice is called chili juice. It is much too spicy for little boys like you to drink. Give it to me and I'll keep it safe." Tom smiled and handed the juice to the old man. From then on, Tom was careful to not speed too quickly or he might slip over again!
