Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Park

Lily and Ben are best friends. They like to play in the park every day. They swing, slide, run and laugh. They are very happy. But one day, Lily's mom says they have to move to a new house. Lily is sad. She does not want to leave Ben. She hugs him tight and says, "I will miss you, Ben. You are my best friend." Ben is sad too. He says, "I will miss you, Lily. You are my best friend too. Can we still play together?" Lily's mom says, "Yes, you can. We are not moving very far. We will come back to the park every week. You can see Ben then." Lily and Ben feel a little better. They say, "Okay, we will wait for you. We will remain friends." They wave goodbye and smile. They know they will see each other again soon. They are still best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. He lived in a small hole with his mom and dad. One day, Bob was playing outside and saw a dull, grey box. He was curious, so he hopped closer to the box. Bob heard a tiny voice from inside the box say, "Help me escape!" He pushed the box and the lid opened. A little firefly flew out. The firefly said, "Thank you, Bob! I was stuck in that box for a long time." As the firefly flew, it made a bright light. Bob and the firefly played together all day. When it was time to go home, Bob said, "Bye, new friend!" The firefly said, "Bye, Bob! See you soon!" And they both went back to their homes, happy to have made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big black cat. The cat liked to play with a twisty toy. One day, the cat found a book with many pages. The cat wanted to play with the book too. The cat started to twist the pages of the book. The pages made a fun sound. The cat was very happy. But soon, the cat saw that the pages were not happy. The pages were getting hurt. The cat did not stop twisting the pages. The pages became very sad. In the end, the pages were all twisted and broken. The cat was not happy anymore, and the book was ruined.

Once upon a time, there was a very special pilot. He was popular with everyone. Everywhere he flew, the people below loved to watch him. One day, he flew over a small village and noticed a little girl looking up at him with admiration. He knew right away that she belonged with him. He shouted down, "Do you want to come up and fly with me?" The little girl was so excited, but she wasn't sure what to say. "Yes, please!" she said at last. The pilot smiled and waved his hand, and the girl flew up to join him in the sky. They flew around the clouds, laughing and pointing out different things. They flew so high that when they looked down, the village seemed so small! Finally, it was time to go. The pilot hugged the little girl and said goodbye. He flew away, and the little girl watched him go until he was out of sight. She had found a place where she belonged, and the pilot was now her friend. From then on, she became the most popular girl in the village.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very enthusiastic. He loved to play and run around in the park. One day, Spot's mom set a big plate on the floor. On the plate was a juicy steak. Spot was so happy to see the steak. He wagged his tail and jumped around. He could not wait to eat the yummy steak. Spot's mom said, "Sit, Spot!" Spot sat down and looked at the steak with big, excited eyes. Spot ate the steak very fast. He thought it was the best thing he had ever tasted. After he ate the steak, Spot felt very full and happy. He went to the park to play with his friends, and he told them all about the delicious steak.
