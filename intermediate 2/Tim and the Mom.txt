Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Mom

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a big, black toy car that he really wanted. He asked his mom, "Can I have the black car, please?" His mom looked at the price and said, "We don't have enough money right now, but maybe next time." The next day, Tim found a shiny coin on the street. He was so happy and ran to show his mom. "Look, mom! I found money! Can I buy the black car now?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "We need more money for the car, but you can save this coin and we will add more later." Tim started to save money in a jar. One day, his mom said, "You have enough money for the black car now!" They went to the store, but the black car was gone. Tim was very sad. A store helper saw Tim and said, "Wait here, I have a surprise for you." The helper came back with an even bigger, black toy car! Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you!" He used his money to buy the big, black car and played with it all day.

Once upon a time, on a quiet planet, there lived a happy family. There was a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and have fun with her friends. The quiet planet was a peaceful place where everyone was nice to each other. One day, Sue decided to wash her toys. She filled a big bowl with water and soap, and she dipped her toys in it. She scrubbed them gently until they were all clean and shiny. Sue liked to keep her toys clean because she knew it was important to take care of the things she loved. After washing her toys, Sue went outside to play. She saw her friends and they all played together on the quiet planet. They had so much fun running, jumping, and laughing. When the sun went down, Sue went back home, hugged her mom and dad, and went to bed. She dreamt of more fun days on the quiet planet.

One day, a cool cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and decided to climb it. As he climbed higher and higher, he could see the whole park. Suddenly, dark clouds covered the sky. Tom heard a loud noise and saw a bright lightning. The lightning split the sky into two parts. Tom was scared and wanted to go down the tree. A friendly bird saw Tom and said, "Don't be scared. I'll help you get down." Tom and the bird slowly went down the tree together. When they reached the ground, the sun came out again. Tom thanked the bird and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big folder with many interesting things inside. He liked to show his folder to all his friends. They would look at the pictures and laugh together. One day, Tim could not find his folder. He started to worry. He looked under his bed, in his toy box, and behind the couch. But the folder was not there. His mom saw him worry and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim told his mom about his missing folder. His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I know where it is." She took Tim to the kitchen and there it was, on the table. Tim was so happy to see his folder. He hugged his mom and said, "Thank you!" From that day on, Tim always put his folder in a safe place, so he would never worry again.

Once upon a time, there was a little jellyfish. The jellyfish lived in the big sea. The jellyfish had many friends. They liked to play all day. One day, the jellyfish felt a tickle in its nose. It tried to stop it, but it could not. The jellyfish sneezed a big sneeze! All its friends laughed and laughed. They thought it was funny. The jellyfish and its friends were hungry. They found some delicious food to eat. They ate and ate until they were full. Then, they played some more. They had a fun day in the big sea.
