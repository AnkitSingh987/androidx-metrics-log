Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a best friend named Max. Max was a very obedient dog. Lily and Max liked to play together every day. One day, Lily found a big box. She had an idea. She called Max and said, "Let's play with the box!" They played hide and seek with the box. Max hid in the box and Lily looked for him. When she found Max, she gave him a big hug. Lily and Max had a lot of fun with the box. They played all day. When it was time to go to bed, Lily gave Max another big hug. They were happy and tired. They went to sleep and dreamed about their fun day with the box.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy trumpet. He loved to play it all day. It was a fine trumpet. It was red and shiny. One day, Tim's trumpet was lost. He looked everywhere. He looked under his bed. He looked in the toy box. But he could not find it. Tim was sad. He needed his trumpet to play and be happy. Tim's mom saw him looking for the trumpet. She knew where it was. She had put it on a high shelf. She got the trumpet and gave it to Tim. Tim was so happy. He played his fine trumpet all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big fire truck. It was red and shiny. Tim loved fire trucks, so he asked his mom if they could go near it. "Mom, can we see the fire truck?" Tim said. His mom said yes, and they walked closer. Tim saw a wet hose on the ground. It was very long. The fire truck had just put out a fire, and the hose was wet from the water. Tim saw a man with a big printer. The man was printing pictures of the fire truck. Tim asked the man if he could have a picture. The man said, "Of course! Here you go." Tim took the picture and said thank you. Just then, a little dog ran up to Tim. The dog was wet from playing in the water. The dog jumped on Tim, and the picture flew into the air. The wind blew the picture away. Tim was sad, but he still had a fun day at the park with the fire truck, the wet dog, and the man with the printer.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy car. This car had a flat tire. The car was not happy at all. A nice truck came by and saw the grumpy car. The truck wanted to help. The truck said, "I recommend you get a new tire." The car listened to the truck. They went to the tire shop together. The car got a new tire. The car was not grumpy anymore. Now the car and the truck are friends. They play together every day. The end.

Once upon a time, a thin cat named Tom and a small dog named Max were best friends. They lived in a big house with a nice lady. One day, the nice lady brought a pepper home. Tom and Max had never seen a pepper before. Tom said, "Max, what is that?" Max replied, "I don't know, but it looks fun!" They played with the pepper, rolling it around the floor. Suddenly, the pepper started to talk! "Please don't hurt me," said the pepper. Tom and Max were very surprised. The talking pepper told them that it wanted to marry another pepper. Tom and Max decided to help their new friend. They searched the house and found another pepper in the kitchen. The two peppers got married, and everyone was happy. The nice lady even made a little pepper house for them to live in. Tom, Max, and the peppers all became best friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who liked to go fishing with her dad. They had lots of fun together, spending all day in the dark waves of the ocean. One day, the girl caught a big and delicious tuna. She was so proud of her catch, and quickly showed her dad. He smiled and said, "Well, that's a big one! I'd say it's dinner tonight." The girl sat down with the fish and noticed something strange. It wasn't moving around like it should! She looked over at her dad, who had a mischievous grin on his face. "What's going on here?" She asked him. Her dad replied, "Well, I may have told a little lie - this tuna isn't alive anymore! We'll have to find another one to eat tonight." The little girl was disappointed, but she had a lot of fun spending the day with her dad. She was determined to find another tuna the next day!
