Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wise Owl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The wise owl lived in a big tree. He had a friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to visit the wise owl every day after playing. One day, Lily found a comb on the ground. She picked it up and put it in her pocket. She went to see the wise owl and showed him the comb. The wise owl smiled and said, "That is a nice comb, Lily." Lily and the wise owl played a game. They took turns using the comb to make their hair look nice. They laughed and had fun. After the game, Lily put the comb back in her pocket. She said goodbye to the wise owl and went home.

One day, an intelligent fox decided to wander around the forest. He saw a mattress in the middle of the forest and sat down on it. He started to drift off to sleep but he was soon woken up by a loud noise. He saw a pesky raccoon stealing all the food from his bag! The fox quickly sprang into action and started to chase the raccoon. The fox quickly outran the raccoon and snatched the food back from him. The fox took the food and returned to the mattress. He was so tired from all the running, he quickly fell asleep for the rest of the day. From now on, he was careful not to let anyone sneak up on him while he rested on his mattress.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his best friend, Tim. Tim was a small boy with a big heart. They lived in a little house with a big yard. One day, Tim's mom said they had to move to a new house far away. "Spot, we have to unpack our things in the new house," Tim told Spot. Spot was sad but wanted to help Tim. They went on their long way to the new house. Spot carried a small box with his toys. Tim carried a big box with his clothes. When they got to the new house, they saw a big cat. The cat did not want them to unpack. The cat said, "This is my house. You can't stay here." Tim was scared, but Spot was brave. Spot barked at the cat, and the cat ran away. Tim and Spot could finally unpack and play in their new home. They were happy and knew they could face anything together.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. On the rock, there was a small soldier. The soldier was a toy. He liked to play and have fun with other toys. One day, it started to rain. The rain was pouring down. The soldier was scared. He did not want to get wet. So, he tried to find a place to hide from the rain. The soldier saw a big tree. He ran to the tree and hid under it. The tree kept him dry. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. The soldier was happy. He went back to play with his friends. They all had fun in the sun.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a big piece of copper. It was shiny and pretty. Tim wanted to show his mom. He ran to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found copper!" His mom smiled and said, "Wow, Tim! That's a nice find! What do you want to do with it?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I want to make a comfortable chair for Dad. He works hard and needs a nice place to sit." Tim's mom helped him find a man who could supply wood and tools. Together, they made a beautiful chair with the copper on the back. It was very comfortable. When Tim's dad came home, he saw the chair and said, "This is the best gift ever! Thank you, Tim!" Tim was so happy that he could make something nice for his dad. They all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a girl named Daisy. Daisy loved to swim. Every day, she would get into the pool, take a deep breath and swim, and swim, and swim. One day, she decided to swim for zero minutes. That meant she wasn't allowed to take a break, not even for a second. She was very determined and reliable. So Daisy dove into the water and started swimming, counting the seconds in her head. When she was done, she looked up at the clock, and it said zero! She had made it! She was so proud of herself. She had swum for zero minutes! Daisy was so happy that she told everyone she knew. Her schoolmates were impressed and her teachers gave her a pat on the back for being so reliable. From that day on, Daisy was known for being a reliable and brave swimmer.
