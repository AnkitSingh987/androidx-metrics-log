Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Knee

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing in his room with his toys when he fell down. He hurt his knee and it was difficult to walk. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw him cry and knew she needed to help heal his knee. She took Tim to the couch and sat him down. She got a band-aid and put it on his knee. Tim felt better and smiled. Now, Tim could play again. He was careful not to fall this time. He played with his toys on the couch and had lots of fun. Tim's knee healed and he was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She liked to play outside in the dirt. One day, Amy found a rough nail on the ground. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. "Look, Mom!" Amy said. "I found a nail!" Her mom looked at the nail and said, "Be careful, Amy. It is very rough. Let's examine it together." They sat down and looked at the nail closely. It was brown and had a pointy end. Amy's mom told her, "This nail is old and rusty. We should throw it away so no one gets hurt." Amy agreed and threw the rough nail in the trash. She felt proud that she had helped keep her family safe. From that day on, Amy always examined things she found outside to make sure they were safe to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He was three years old and he loved presents. One day, Jack's mom said to him, "Jack, on your birthday you'll receive a special present." Jack was so excited and could not wait to see what his present would be. On the morning of his birthday, Jack woke up and ran to the living room. He was so surprised when he saw a tall, brightly wrapped present standing in the corner. He jumped up and down and cried, "My present! My present!" Jack peeled away the wrapping paper and opened the box. He couldn't believe his eyes. Inside the present, was a tall toy fire truck that lit up and made loud noises. Jack hugged his mom and said, "Thank you, this is the best present I ever received!"

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He liked to play outside in the garden. One day, he found some unknown seeds. Tom was very curious and wanted to see what would grow from the seeds. Tom decided to build a small garden for the seeds. He dug little holes and put the seeds in them. Every day, Tom watered the seeds and waited for them to grow. After a while, the seeds grew into big, beautiful flowers. The flowers were so pretty that everyone in the village loved them. The people in the village learned that even unknown things can be good if you care for them and give them a chance to grow. Tom was very happy that he had built a garden for the unknown seeds. The moral of the story is to not be afraid of the unknown, and to give love and care to help it grow.

Once upon a time, there was a humble bird. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. Every day, the bird would soar and play with its friends. One day, while the bird was flying, it saw a big bug. The bug wanted to fight the bird. The bird was scared, but it did not want its friends to get hurt. So, the bird got ready to fight the big bug. But then, something unexpected happened. Instead of fighting, the big bug started to sing! The bird was surprised. The big bug did not want to fight after all. It just wanted to have fun and sing with the bird. So, the bird and the big bug became friends and played together in the sky.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a bench and sat down. Tim had a yummy sandwich in his bag. He took it out and started to eat it. A dog walked up to Tim and said, "Can I have some of your yummy sandwich?" Tim was surprised, but he shared his sandwich with the dog. The dog was very happy. After they ate, the dog showed Tim a spot in the park where he liked to bury his toys. Tim and the dog dug a hole and buried a toy together. They had fun and became good friends.
