Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Frog

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily loved to feed the ducks at the pond. She had a big bag of bread with her. "Quack, quack," said the ducks when they saw Lily. "Here you go, ducks," she said, as she threw the bread into the water. While she was feeding the ducks, she saw a big green frog. The frog had tired eyes and dry skin. Lily wanted to help the frog. "Mom, can we help the frog?" she asked. Her mom said yes, and they took the frog home. At home, they put the frog in a big bowl of water. The frog jumped and splashed, and then something unexpected happened. The frog turned into a beautiful princess! The princess said, "Thank you, Lily! I was under a spell, and your kindness broke it. Now I can go back to my kingdom." Lily and her mom were very surprised, but happy they could help.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found an unknown muffin on the ground. She picked it up and looked at it. It was big and round. Lucy wanted to eat the muffin, but it was too big to fit in her mouth. Lucy had an idea. She ran to her friend Tom's house. Tom had a big mouth, so maybe the muffin would fit in his mouth. Lucy showed Tom the muffin, and he tried to eat it. But the muffin was too big for Tom's mouth, too. They went to ask their friend, the big dog Max, for help. Max had a very big mouth. Lucy and Tom were sure the muffin would fit in Max's mouth. Max tried to eat the muffin, but something unexpected happened. The muffin was not a real muffin, it was a toy! Max's teeth broke the toy muffin into pieces. Lucy and Tom were sad. They could not eat the muffin, and now Max had a broken toy.

One day, a small, noisy bug named Buzz was flying around. He saw a big cobweb in a tree. Buzz wanted to sit on the cobweb. He flew to the cobweb and asked, "Can I sit here?" The cobweb's owner, a spider named Spidey, said, "Yes, you can sit on my cobweb. But, you must be quiet. My baby spiders are sleeping." Buzz sat on the cobweb and tried to be quiet. But, he was a very noisy bug. He made loud sounds that woke up the baby spiders. The baby spiders started to cry. Spidey was not happy. He said, "Buzz, you are too noisy. You cannot sit on my cobweb anymore. Please go away." Buzz felt sad, but he understood. He said sorry to Spidey and flew away. As he was flying, he saw a big drum. He flew to the drum and started to play. Buzz was very happy. He found a place where he could be as noisy as he wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had very long hair. One day, she was playing with her friend, Tom. They were having fun in the park. Sue said, "My hair is hard to take care of." Tom looked at her hair and said, "I can help you with your hair." They went to Tom's house to find something to help Sue's hair. In the house, they found a big hat. Tom said, "This hat can help you. It will keep your hair safe." But when Sue put the hat on, her hair was gone! They were both very surprised. The hat did not belong to Tom, it belonged to a magic man who lived next door. They went to the magic man and asked for help. He said, "I can give you back your hair, but you must promise to always take care of it." Sue and Tom agreed, and Sue's hair came back. From that day on, Sue and Tom always took care of her hair together. They were happy and played in the park every day.

One day, a little girl named Amy wanted to build something big. She had an idea to build a wonder castle for her loyal dog, Spot. Amy got her toy blocks and started to build the castle. As she built, she talked to Spot, "This castle will be the best, and you will love it!" Spot wagged his tail and barked happily. He was excited to see the wonder castle. When the castle was done, Amy showed it to Spot. "Look, Spot! Your wonder castle is ready!" Spot ran to the castle and played inside. He loved his new home. Amy and Spot played together all day, and they were very happy.
