Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Ring

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play in the pool. They had a lot of fun splashing each other and making bubbles. But sometimes they also liked to have secrets from each other. One day, Lily found a shiny ring at the bottom of the pool. She thought it was very pretty and wanted to keep it for herself. She did not tell Ben about it. She hid it in her pocket and waited for him to go inside. When Ben went inside to get a snack, Lily took out the ring and looked at it. She felt very happy and proud. She put it on her finger and pretended to be a princess. But then she heard Ben coming back. She quickly put the ring back in her pocket and pretended to be bored. She hoped he would not notice. "Hey, Lily, what are you doing?" Ben asked. "Do you want to play some more?" Lily nodded and smiled. She splashed him with water and said, "Sure, let's play!" But Ben saw something shiny in her pocket. He was curious and asked, "What's that in your pocket, Lily?" Lily felt scared and guilty. She did not want to share her ring with Ben. She thought he would take it away from her. She was selfish. She lied and said, "Nothing, it's just a rock. It's not important." But Ben did not believe her. He reached for her pocket and tried to grab the ring. Lily screamed and pushed him away. They started to fight and pull each other's hair. Their mom heard the noise and came outside. She saw them fighting and was angry. She asked, "What's going on here? Why are you fighting?" Lily and Ben stopped fighting and looked at their mom. They both felt ashamed and sorry. They told her the truth about the ring and how they hid it and lied. Their mom was disappointed and sad. She said, "That was not nice of you, Lily and Ben. You should not be selfish and lie to each other. You should share and be honest. The ring is not yours anyway. It belongs to someone else. You should give it back." Lily and Ben realized their mom was right. They felt bad for being selfish and lying. They said, "We're sorry, mom. We were wrong. We will give the ring back." Their mom hugged them and said, "I'm glad you understand. I love you both. Now, let's go and find the owner of the ring. Maybe they are looking for it." Lily and Ben nodded and smiled. They took the ring and followed their mom. They learned their lesson and decided to be more kind and truthful. They also decided to have more fun and less secrets. They still liked to splash each other and make bubbles. But they also liked to share and be honest. They were happy twins.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was very happy because yesterday, his friend, a little girl named Lucy, said she would bring him a surprise today. Max could not wait to see what the surprise was. In the morning, Max went to the park to meet Lucy. Lucy had an enormous smile on her face. She said, "Max, I have a big surprise for you!" Max wagged his tail and jumped up and down. He was so excited to see what Lucy had brought for him. Lucy showed Max the surprise. It was an enormous bone! Max's eyes grew wide and he barked with joy. He knew that this bone would be the best thing ever. Max and Lucy played in the park all day, and Max chewed on his enormous bone. They had so much fun, and Max was very thankful for his surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Tim. Tim loved to go fast and make loud noises. One day, Tim went to the gas station to get some gas. He was very excited because he wanted to go fast again. At the gas station, Tim met a big truck named Tom. Tom was very spoiled and liked to print his name on everything. Tom said, "I can go faster than you, Tim!" Tim didn't like that Tom was being mean. Tim and Tom had a race. They both went very fast, but Tim was faster. Tom was sad and said, "You are a good car, Tim." Tim smiled and said, "Let's be friends, Tom." They both went to the gas station and printed their names on a big paper. From that day on, they were best friends.
