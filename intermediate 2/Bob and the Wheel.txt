Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Wheel

Bob went to the park with a big wheel. He was so excited to take it for a spin! But when Bob tried to ride the wheel, it suddenly started going too fast. Soon the wheel was wobbling in a weird way and Bob got scared. Bob said, "Help me! Please stop this wheel!" But no one came to help him. The wheel kept spinning and spinning until Bob fell off. When Bob finally woke up, he was in the hospital. His parents were beside him and they looked very sad. Bob's mom said, "The wheel was so weird, honey. It was an old, broken one and it made you go too fast. We are just glad you are OK!" But Bob was sad. He had wanted to have fun with his wheel, but instead he got hurt. He never wanted to go near a wheel ever again.

One day, a curious little boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and elephants. Tim was very happy and excited to see all the animals. As they walked, Tim saw a big bird with a long neck. He asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom said, "That is an ostrich, Tim." Tim and his mom stood by the ostrich and looked at it. Just then, the ostrich started to talk! "Hello, Tim!" said the ostrich. Tim was very surprised. He looked at his mom and she was surprised too. Tim asked the ostrich, "Can all ostriches talk?" The ostrich said, "No, just me. I am a special ostrich." Tim and the ostrich became friends and they discussed many things like the zoo and other animals. In the end, it was time for Tim and his mom to go home. Tim said goodbye to the talking ostrich and promised to visit again. The ostrich said, "I will miss you, Tim. Please come back soon." Tim and his mom left the zoo, and they talked about the talking ostrich all the way home.

One day, a sweet cat named Kitty found a soft bathrobe on the floor. Kitty thought it was a nice place to take a nap. She jumped onto the bathrobe and curled up into a ball. Kitty slept for a long time. When she woke up, she thought, "I want to play now." She saw a toy mouse on the floor. Kitty played with the toy mouse and had lots of fun. After playing, Kitty felt tired again. She went back to the soft bathrobe and took another nap. Kitty loved her new nap spot. She thought the bathrobe was the best thing ever.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear who lived in a green forest. The bear was very healthy, but he did not like to hug. He would say, "I hate hugs!" All the animals in the forest knew this, so they stayed away from the bear. One sunny day, a little rabbit was playing near the bear's home. The rabbit did not know about the bear's hate for hugs. The rabbit saw the bear and thought he looked sad. So, the rabbit went to the bear and gave him a big hug. The bear was surprised by the hug. It was something he did not expect. But instead of getting mad, the bear felt happy. The rabbit's hug made the bear feel warm and loved. From that day on, the bear did not hate hugs anymore. He even started to give hugs to all his friends in the forest. And they all lived happily together.

Once there was a mommy and her little girl. The little girl was three years old. Mommy said, "Let's go outside and play!". The little girl stood up from her chair to go. When they got outside, the little girl saw her brother. He was playing in the grass. She got jealous because she wanted to play too. Mommy said, "Let's put some lotion on your skin. That way it won't get too hot." The little girl stood still while Mommy put lotion all over her skin. Mommy said, "Now you can go play outside with your brother!" The little girl smiled and ran off to play with her brother. She wasn't jealous anymore.
