Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Car

One day, a boy named Tim went to play with his toy network of cars and trains. He was very glad because he loved his toys. As he played, he made the cars go fast and the trains go slow. Suddenly, Tim saw his toy car rise into the air! He was so surprised. "Car, why are you up in the air?" Tim asked. The car said, "I want to be a plane today!" Tim laughed and said, "Okay, car, you can be a plane." The toy car flew around the room, and Tim was so happy. But then, the car got stuck on a high shelf. Tim tried to reach it, but he was too small. Just then, his toy train rose into the air too! The train said, "Don't worry, Tim, I will help you!" The train flew up and pushed the car off the shelf. Tim was so glad that his toys could help each other. From that day on, Tim knew that his toy network of cars and trains was very special.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a beautiful flower. This flower was very intelligent. It could talk and play with the other flowers. They all had fun together. One day, the intelligent flower saw a little girl with a bike. The girl had a big smile on her face. She was riding her bike very fast. The flower saw that the bike had a pedal. The flower wanted to try the pedal and ride the bike too. The intelligent flower asked the little girl if it could ride the bike. The girl said yes. The flower started to bloom bigger and bigger, so it could reach the pedal. It pushed the pedal with all its strength. But, the flower did not know how to ride the bike well. The flower fell off the bike and broke its stem. The other flowers were sad. The intelligent flower could not play with them anymore. The moral of the story is that we should not try to do things that we are not meant to do.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play outside. But today the weather is bad. It is raining and windy. Sara and Tom are sad. "Can we go outside?" Tom asks his mom. "No, you have to stay inside. The weather is too bad. You can get wet and cold," mom says. "But we want to play!" Sara says. Mom thinks for a moment. She has an idea. "Why don't you play pretend? You can be mighty heroes who save the world from the bad weather. You can use your toys and blankets and pillows to make a fort and a rocket," mom says. Sara and Tom like mom's idea. They run to Tom's room and start to play pretend. They make a fort with blankets and pillows. They make a rocket with a big box and some crayons. They put on their capes and helmets. "We are the mighty heroes! We can stop the rain and the wind!" Sara says. "Yes, we can! Let's go to the rocket and blast off to the sky!" Tom says. They get into the rocket and pretend to fly. They make noises and gestures. They have fun. Sara and Tom forget about the bad weather. They are happy. They stay inside and play pretend. They are the mighty heroes.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable little girl. She loved to relax by the sea. One day, she asked her mum, "Can I go to the sea?" Mum said, "Yes, my love. Just make sure to be careful". So the little girl put on her sunhat and ran to the beach. She was so excited! When she got there, she saw the blue sea and its sparkling waves. She took a deep breath and felt the salty air fill her lungs. The little girl lay down on the sand and closed her eyes. She felt so relaxed. The sound of the waves soothed her and she soon fell asleep. When she woke up, she was refreshed. She thanked her mum for being so dependable and letting her have the day by the sea.
