Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bossy Frog Was and the Bird

One day, a bossy frog was near a pond. He liked to tell the other animals what to do. The frog saw a little bird and said, "Yield! You must give me your food!" The bird was sad but gave the frog some food. Then, a big bear came to the pond. He saw the bossy frog and said, "You must yield to me! Give me your food, too!" The frog was scared of the big bear, so he gave the food back to the bird. The bird was happy and thanked the bear. The bear said, "You're welcome. We should all be nice to each other." The bossy frog learned that it is better to be kind than bossy. From that day on, he was nice to all the animals at the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old dishwasher. It lived in a big kitchen with a nice family. The dishwasher loved to help the family clean their plates and cups. One day, the dishwasher saw a shiny coin. It knew that the coin was something people value. The dishwasher wanted to give the coin back to the family, but it didn't know how. The wise dishwasher thought and thought. It decided to wash the coin with the plates and cups. When the family saw the shiny coin, they were happy. They knew the dishwasher was very helpful and smart. The family and the dishwasher lived happily ever after.

One day, Tom and Tim went to a cheap restaurant to eat. They wanted to have a fun time together. Tom saw a big cake and said, "I want to eat that!" Tim wanted to eat the cake too. They started to fight over the cake. Tom said, "I saw it first!" Tim said, "But I want it too!" Their fight got louder and louder. A nice lady saw them fighting. She came over and said, "Why don't you both share the cake?" Tom and Tim thought about it. They decided to share the cake and put it in the middle of the table. Tom and Tim learned that it is better to share than to fight. They had a great time at the restaurant and became better friends.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat had a cut on its leg. The dog felt guilty because he had been too rough when they were playing. "Can I join you?" asked a big bird, flying down from a tree. The cat and the dog looked at each other and said, "Yes, you can join us!" They played together all day, having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird picked up the cat and the dog with its strong wings and flew high up into the sky. They were scared at first, but then they saw how beautiful everything looked from up there. They all became best friends and played together every day, and the cat's cut got better.

Once upon a time, a white hen had an egg. The hen was very happy. She wanted to test if the egg was good. One day, a big wind came. The wind blew the egg away. The hen was sad. She looked for the egg, but could not find it. The egg landed near a big tree. A kind bird found the egg. The bird took care of the egg. Soon, a baby chick came out of the egg. The bird and the chick became good friends. The hen found the chick and the bird. She was happy to see her baby. The bird was sad to say goodbye, but the hen said they could all be friends. The moral of the story is that sometimes, when we lose something, we find new friends who help us.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to soar high in the sky. The bird was very happy when it could fly up and up. One day, while flying, the bird saw a cup on the ground. The bird was curious and wanted to see the cup up close. The bird flew down to the cup and looked inside. The cup was empty, but the bird thought it could be a nice place to rest. The bird sat in the cup and felt very cozy. It was a warm and sunny day, so the bird fell asleep in the cup. While the bird was sleeping, a big wind came and blew the cup away. The bird woke up and was very upset. It tried to fly out of the cup, but it was too late. The cup fell into a deep hole, and the bird was trapped inside. The little bird could not soar in the sky anymore, and it was very sad.
