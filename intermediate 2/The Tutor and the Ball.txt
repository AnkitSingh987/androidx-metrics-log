Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tutor and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was an elderly tutor. She loved to read books to little kids. The tutor was very kind and always smiled. One day, the tutor read a story about a big, red ball. The ball went on an adventure to find new friends. The kids listened and laughed as the ball met a funny dog and a happy cat. In the end, the ball, the dog, and the cat became best friends. They played together every day. The kids loved the story and clapped their hands. The elderly tutor was happy to read more stories to them.

---

One day, a little black cat named Mimi went to a festival. At the festival, there were many fun games and yummy food. Mimi was so happy to be there. While walking around, Mimi saw a big frog. The frog said, "Hi Mimi, do you want to play a game with me? Let's see who can jump the highest!" Mimi and the frog started to jump. Mimi jumped high, but the frog jumped even higher. Mimi felt sad that she could not jump as high as the frog. A nice dog saw Mimi and said, "Don't be sad, Mimi. We all have things we are good at. You are good at climbing trees, and the frog is good at jumping. We should be happy for our friends when they do well." Mimi felt better and learned that it is important to be happy for others and not just ourselves.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a new dress for Lucy to wear at her friend's birthday party. At the store, Lucy saw a beautiful, soft dress. It was pink and had pretty flowers on it. She asked her mom, "Can I have this dress, please?" Her mom said yes, and they took the dress home. The next day, Lucy put on her new dress and went to the party. She was so happy and excited. But when she got there, she saw another girl wearing the same dress! Lucy was sad and thought her day was ruined. Then, the two girls started talking and found out they both loved the same things. They played together all day and became best friends. The moral of the story is that even when something unexpected happens, it can lead to something good.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Snow. Snow was a naughty cat. She liked to play tricks on her friends. One day, Snow had a plan to do a trick on her friend, Dog. Snow went to Dog's house and said, "Dog, do you want to play with me?" Dog was happy and said, "Yes, Snow! I want to play with you." Snow and Dog went to the park to play. Snow was thinking about the trick she wanted to do. At the park, Snow found a big box. She told Dog, "I will hide in this box, and you try to find me." Dog said, "Okay, Snow. I will find you." Snow hid in the box, and Dog started to look for her. But then, something unexpected happened. The box started to move! It was not a box, but a big toy car! The toy car took Snow far away from Dog. Dog was sad and worried. He didn't know where Snow went. But then, Dog saw the toy car with Snow inside. He ran to the toy car and stopped it. Snow was sorry for being naughty. She said, "Thank you, Dog. I am sorry for the trick. I will not be naughty again." Dog forgave Snow, and they played happily together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mum. They saw an aeroplane in the sky. Tim loved aeroplanes, so he pointed at it and said, "Look, mum! An aeroplane!" His mum smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Aeroplanes can fly high in the sky. They take people to far away places." On their walk, they found an ice cream shop. Tim's mum bought him a delicious ice cream cone. Tim was very happy. He said, "Thank you, mum! This ice cream is so delicious!" As they walked back home, Tim saw a girl who was sad. She didn't have an ice cream. Tim thought for a moment and then gave her his ice cream. The girl was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Tim! You are very kind." Tim's mum was proud of him. She said, "Tim, you did a good thing. Sharing makes others happy, and it makes you happy too." Tim smiled and knew that sharing was a good thing to do.
