Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable dog named Spot. He was very friendly and loved to play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a little bird who was stuck in a cage. The bird wanted to escape and fly with the other birds in the sky. Spot knew he had to help the little bird. He used his strong teeth to open the cage. The bird was so happy to be free. It flapped its wings and flew up high into the sky. Spot felt very proud that he could help his new friend. To celebrate, all the animals in the forest came together. They danced, played games, and shared yummy treats. Spot and the little bird became best friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a curious dragon. He liked to roll in the grass and play all day. One day, he saw a big hill and wanted to roll down it. As the dragon rolled down the hill, he met a little girl. The girl said, "Hi, dragon! Will you play with me?" The dragon was happy and nodded his head. They played together all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The dragon rolled too fast and bumped into a tree. The tree shook, and lots of apples fell down. The dragon and the girl laughed, and they ate the apples together. They became best friends and had lots of fun every day.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl named Lily. She loved to dance and sing. One day, Lily wanted to put on a show for her friends. So, she asked her mom if she could borrow a stage. Her mom said yes and helped her build a small stage in their backyard. Lily invited all her friends to watch her show. She put on a pretty dress and got on the stage. Her friends sat on the grass and clapped for her. Lily danced and sang her favorite songs. She was so happy to share her gift with her friends. After the show, Lily's friends wanted to try dancing and singing on the stage too. Lily let them borrow the stage, and they all took turns. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, they all thanked Lily for sharing her stage and her gift with them.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. It was very cold outside, and there was icy snow on the ground. Tim saw a big switch on the wall. He wanted to touch it, but his mom said, "No, Tim! Don't touch the switch!" Tim was sad and asked his mom, "Why can't I touch the switch?" His mom replied, "Because it's not safe for you to play with it. It can make the lights go on and off." Tim thought about it and decided to listen to his mom. He found a big leaf and used it to cover the switch. This way, he couldn't see it and wouldn't want to touch it anymore. His mom was happy to see that Tim listened to her, and they continued their walk together.

Once upon a time, in a soft sand land, there lived a big cat and a small dog. They were friends and played together every day. One day, they found a big, soft sand hill. They wanted to play on it, but they didn't know how to share. The big cat said, "I want to play on the sand hill first!" The small dog said, "No, I want to play on the sand hill first!" They started to quarrel. They pushed and pulled, trying to get to the sand hill first. A wise old bird saw them quarreling and flew down to help. The bird said, "Why don't you both play on the sand hill together?" The big cat and the small dog stopped quarreling. They looked at each other and smiled. They said, "Yes, let's play together!" So, the big cat and the small dog climbed the soft sand hill together. They played and laughed all day long. They learned that it is better to share and play together, rather than quarrel. And from that day on, they always shared everything and had lots of fun.
