Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She was an independent girl who loved to explore. One day, she found a pretty jewel in the park. It was shiny and bright. Sue was very happy and ran to show her mom. "Look, Mom! I found a pretty jewel!" Sue said. Her mom looked at the jewel and smiled. "That's very pretty, Sue. But remember, we should always remove things we find and give them back to the owner." The next day, Sue went to the park again. She saw a sad boy sitting on a bench. The boy was crying. Sue asked, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my pretty jewel. It was very special to me." Sue remembered what her mom told her. She took out the jewel and gave it to the boy. The boy was very happy and thanked Sue. Sue felt good about doing the right thing. The moral of the story is to always return things that don't belong to us.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. Tim had a soft, red ball in his hand. He liked his ball very much. Tim had an opinion that his ball was the best in the world. One day, while walking, Tim saw a big, soft pillow on the road. He thought it would be fun to play with it too. So, he put his ball down and started to play with the pillow. He was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A dog came and took Tim's ball away! Tim was sad and wanted his ball back. He ran after the dog and asked for his ball. The dog gave the ball back to Tim, and they both played together. At the end, Tim's opinion changed. He thought that making new friends was even better than his soft, red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat. The cat had a tie. The tie was pretty. The cat liked to play with the tie. One day, the cat went outside to play. The cat found a box. The box was open. The cat put the tie in the box. Then, the cat closed the box. The cat went home. The cat was happy. The cat had a fun day with the tie and the box. The cat liked the orange tie and the closed box.

Once upon a time, in a gloomy nation, there lived a kind lady named Jane. Jane did not like the gloomy nation, so she tried to make it a happy place. She would sing and dance to make people smile. One day, Jane met a sad boy named Tim. Tim was crying because he lost his toy. Jane asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my toy, and I don't know where it is." Jane wanted to help Tim, so she said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you find your toy." Jane and Tim looked everywhere for the toy. They asked people if they had seen it, but no one knew where it was. They did not mind, because they were having fun looking for it together. Finally, they found the toy under a big tree. Tim was so happy, and he said, "Thank you, Jane, for helping me find my toy!" From that day on, Tim and Jane became best friends. They played together every day, and they helped make the gloomy nation a happier place. They showed everyone that being kind and helping others can make a big difference. And so, the gloomy nation became a happy nation, all because of Jane and Tim. They taught everyone to be kind, to help others, and to always mind their friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue was enthusiastic about helping her mom. She wanted to help tie a knot in the rope for their new swing. Sue's mom showed her how to twist the rope and make a loop. They pulled the rope tight together. Sue was proud of the knot she helped make. She ran to tell her friend, Tom, about the swing. Tom was excited too. They both went to the swing and took turns pushing each other. They laughed and had lots of fun. After playing, Sue and Tom went back to Sue's house. Sue's mom gave them a big hug for being good helpers. They were happy they learned how to tie a knot and help with the swing.
