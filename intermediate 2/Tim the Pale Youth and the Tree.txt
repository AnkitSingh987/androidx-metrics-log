Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Pale Youth and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a pale youth named Tim. Tim had a big heart and loved to help others. One day, he saw a little girl named Sue sitting on the ground, and she was crying. Tim went to Sue and asked, "Why do you cry, Sue?" Sue looked up and said, "I lost my toy and now I am sad." Tim wanted to help Sue feel better, so he said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will help you find your toy." They looked everywhere and finally found the toy under a big tree. Sue was so happy and stopped crying. From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends. They learned that helping others can make both people happy. The village people saw how kind Tim was, and they all wanted to be like him. The moral of the story is that helping others brings happiness to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She had a big fan that she used to cool herself when it was hot. One day, she saw a new boy named Tom at the park. Lily wanted to say hi, but she felt too shy. Lily went home and thought about it. She did not want to feel regret for not talking to Tom. So, she took her fan and went back to the park. When she saw Tom, she walked up to him and said, "Hi, I'm Lily. Do you want to play with me?" Tom smiled and said, "Sure, Lily! I'm happy you said hi. I was shy too." They played together all day and became good friends. From that day on, Lily was never too shy to make new friends, and she always had her fan to help her feel brave.

Once upon a time, in a lovely little house, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to mix things. She would mix her toys, her food, and even her clothes. One day, Sue found a big, soft blanket in her room. Sue thought, "I can mix this blanket with my toys!" So, she put all her toys on the blanket. She mixed them all up, and it was so much fun! The toys and the blanket made a big, happy mess. After playing, Sue was very tired. She lay down on the lovely, soft blanket with all her toys. Sue felt warm and cozy. She closed her eyes and went to sleep, dreaming of all the fun things she could mix the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a bit nervous because he lost his toy car. He looked all around his room, but he could not find it. He felt sad and did not know what to do. One day, Tim saw the sun shine through his window. The light shined on a small box in the left corner of his room. Tim had never seen that box before. He thought maybe his toy car was inside the box. Tim walked to the left corner and opened the box. Inside, he found his toy car! He was so happy that he jumped up and down. Now, Tim was not nervous anymore. He played with his toy car all day long, and the sun kept shining brightly.

Once upon a time, there was a unique cat named Tom. Tom had a special belt that he loved to wear. He wore it every day because it made him feel happy. One sunny day, Tom was playing outside with his friends. They were running, jumping, and having lots of fun. Tom was so happy that he didn't notice his belt was loose. Suddenly, Tom's belt started to fall. He tried to catch it, but it was too late. His belt fell on the ground. Tom was sad, but his friends helped him find it. They put the belt back on Tom, and he was happy again. They all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl who wanted to play outside. She asked her mom to play, but her mom said no. The girl was sad. The girl thought, "I'm going to go outside anyway!" So she opened the door and stepped out. The light was so bright and the birds were so noisy. She played and played until it was late. Finally, the sun was going down and the girl's mom said it was bedtime. But the girl was mad and refused to go to bed. Her mom said "If you don't go to bed, I will spoil your fun!" But the girl still refused and stayed outside. Eventually, the girl got very tired and had to go to bed. Her mom was angry and took away all her fun toys. The girl was so sad, she cried all night long. The girl learned her lesson and never disobeyed her mom again. From then on, she always went to bed on time.
