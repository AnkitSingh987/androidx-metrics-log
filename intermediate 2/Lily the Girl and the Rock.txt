Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Rock

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a new road. The road was long and gloomy. Lily was scared, but she wanted to see what was at the end of the road. As she walked, she saw a boy. The boy was sad. Lily said, "Hi, I am Lily. What is your name?" The boy said, "I am Tim." Lily wanted to make Tim happy, so she said, "I will introduce you to my friends. They will make you happy." Lily and Tim walked together. They found a big, shiny rock. Tim said, "Wow, what is that?" Lily said, "I don't know, but it is pretty!" They touched the rock, and suddenly, it started to talk! The rock said, "Hello, I am Rocky. I am a magic rock. I can make wishes come true." Lily and Tim were surprised. They wished for a fun day with their new friend, Rocky. The gloomy road became bright and happy. They played and laughed all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a rich uncle. He had a big house and many toys. He loved to play with his niece and nephew. One day, the uncle planned a fun day for them. They played with balls, dolls, and cars. They had a big lunch with yummy food. The uncle was happy to see them laugh and have fun. But the uncle forgot something important. He forgot to give them a special gift. When he remembered, he ran to his room and got it. It was a big box with a red bow. The niece and nephew were very excited. They opened the box and found new toys inside. They hugged their uncle and said, "Thank you!" They all played together and had a great day.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a gloomy sweater in her room. She did not like the gloomy sweater. It was too big and dark. Mia wanted to wear her pretty pink sweater instead. Mia went to her mommy and said, "Mommy, I don't want to wear the gloomy sweater. Can I wear my pink one?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Of course, Mia. You can wear your pink sweater today." Mia was happy. She took the gloomy sweater and decided to hang it in her closet. She put on her pink sweater and went outside to play. The sun was shining and Mia felt warm and happy in her pretty pink sweater.

Once upon a time, there was a modern house. In this house, there lived a boy named Tom and his cat, Mimi. One day, Tom found a shiny rock outside. He brought the rock inside to examine it. He thought it was a special mineral. Tom showed the rock to Mimi and said, "Look, Mimi! I found a shiny rock. I think it's a special mineral." Mimi looked at the rock and meowed. Tom didn't know that Mimi was trying to tell him to be careful with the rock. The next day, Tom and Mimi played with the shiny rock. They didn't know that the rock was not a good rock to play with. Tom threw the rock, and Mimi chased it. They had fun, but they didn't know something bad was going to happen. Suddenly, the shiny rock broke a window in the modern house. Tom and Mimi were scared. They knew they were in trouble. Tom's mom came into the room and saw the broken window. She was not happy. Tom's mom said, "Tom, you can't play with rocks inside the house! You broke the window." Tom felt sad and said sorry. Mimi meowed sadly too. They learned their lesson and knew they should be more careful with things they find outside.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a long string. He thought it was fun to play with. Tim pulled the string all around the house. It was very noisy. The string made a big mess. Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the mess and said, "Tim, please mind your mess. It is too noisy and not safe." Tim felt sad. He did not want to make his mom upset. Tim said, "I am sorry, Mom. I will clean up the string." So, Tim picked up the noisy string and put it away. His mom was happy. They hugged and played a quiet game together.
