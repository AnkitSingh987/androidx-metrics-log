Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Fan

One day, a little girl named Mia found a different toy in her room. It was a fan with pretty colors. Mia wanted to play with the fan, but she did not know how to use it. Mia started to struggle with the fan. She tried to make it spin and blow air, but she could not do it. Mia felt sad and asked her mom for help. Mom showed Mia how to use the fan. Mia was very happy when the fan started to spin and blow air. Now, Mia could play with her different toy and have fun with the fan.

Once upon a time there was an old man. He was walking in a dry forest and he heard a strange noise. The old man looked around and saw a mosquito. It was demanding food. The old man was not sure what to do. He took out a piece of bread from his pocket and gave it to the mosquito. The mosquito sucked the juice out of the bread. The old man was happy that he had made the mosquito happy. The mosquito flew away and the old man continued walking through the dry forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy bricks. He would build big towers and houses with them. One day, he built a very big tower with his bricks. Tim was very proud of his tower. A careless dog named Spot came running into Tim's room. Spot did not see Tim's big tower. Spot said, "Woof! Woof! Let's play, Tim!" Then, Spot ran into the tower and destroyed it. The bricks fell down and the tower was gone. Tim was sad that his tower was gone. But he knew he could build it again. He looked at Spot and said, "Spot, be careful next time. Let's build a new tower together." Spot wagged his tail and helped Tim build a new tower. They were both happy and played together all day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the market with her mom. They wanted to buy some fruits and a spicy sauce for dinner. Lily saw a big red apple and asked her mom, "Can we get this apple, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but we have to wash it first when we get home." At home, Lily helped her mom wash the apple. She was excited to eat it. But when she took a bite, it was very spicy! She didn't know the spicy sauce had spilled on the apple at the market. Lily said, "Mom, the apple is spicy!" Her mom laughed and said, "I guess the spicy sauce spilled on it. Let's wash it again." After they washed the apple again, Lily took another bite. This time, it was not spicy. She was happy and learned a new word, "spicy". Lily told her mom, "I like the word 'spicy' but not on my apple!" They both laughed and enjoyed their dinner together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little deer. The deer was very sorry because it had lost its way. It wanted to find its family, but didn't know how. One day, the deer met a wise old owl. The deer said, "I am sorry, I lost my way. Can you help me find my family?" The owl thought for a moment and said, "I will help you, but first, you must pass a test." The deer was scared but wanted to find its family, so it agreed to take the test. The owl told the deer, "You must find three things in the forest: a red flower, a big rock, and a small stick." The deer went on a long journey to find these things. It found the red flower near a pond, the big rock under a tree, and the small stick in a bush. The deer brought them back to the owl. The owl was happy and said, "You passed the test! Now, I will show you the way to your family." The deer was very happy and thanked the owl. The owl led the deer back to its family, and they all lived happily ever after.
