Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Gifted Boy and the Drawing

Once upon a time in a small town, there lived a gifted boy named Tim. Tim was very good at drawing. One day, he decided to draw a big picture on a wall. As Tim was drawing, his friend Sam came by and said, "Wow, Tim, you are very good at drawing! Will you teach me?" Tim smiled and said, "Of course, Sam! We can draw together." But as they were drawing, it started to rain. The rain washed away the big picture on the wall. Tim felt sad and wanted to quit. Sam said, "Don't quit, Tim! We can have success if we try again." So they went inside and found a big piece of paper. They drew a new picture together. When they were done, they showed their picture to everyone in the town. The people were so happy and clapped for them. Tim and Sam learned that even when something unexpected happens, they should never quit.

One day, a big horse and a little horse lived in a stable. They liked to play and run in the field. The big horse was serious and the little horse was funny. The big horse said, "We should clean the stable." The little horse disagreed, "No, let's play more!" The big horse knew that something would happen if they didn't clean the stable. Later, while they were playing, they saw a big cloud of dust coming from the stable. The big horse and the little horse ran to the stable to see what was happening. When they got to the stable, they saw that the dust had made a big mess. The big horse said, "We should have cleaned the stable." The little horse agreed, "Yes, you were right. Let's clean it now." So, the big horse and the little horse cleaned the stable together. They learned that it's important to listen to each other and take care of their home.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He had an incredible toy car. It was red and very fast. Tom loved to play with his car every day. One day, the car broke. Tom was sad. Tom's dad saw him crying. He asked, "What's wrong, Tom?" Tom said, "My car is broken. Can you help me?" Tom's dad smiled and said, "Let's repair it together." So, Tom and his dad worked hard to fix the car. They made sure not to touch the poison glue. They knew it was not good. After some time, they fixed the car. Tom was happy again. He played with his car and thanked his dad for helping him.

One day, a boy named Tim found a penny on the ground. He was very happy. Tim wanted to store the penny in his piggy bank. He ran to his room, but the piggy bank was gone. Tim felt bad. His mom saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "My piggy bank is gone, and I want to store my penny." His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, I have a surprise for you!" She took Tim to the living room. In the living room, there was a big, new piggy bank. Tim was so happy! He put his penny in the new piggy bank. The old piggy bank was not bad, but the new one was bigger and better. Now Tim could store even more pennies!

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to ride her bike. She was learning to be independent. Sue put on her helmet and went outside. Her bike was red and shiny. Sue saw her friend Tom. Tom said, "I had an operation. I can't ride my bike now." Sue felt sad for Tom. She wanted to help him feel better. Sue had an idea. She told Tom, "You can watch me ride. I will ride slow and you can cheer for me." Tom smiled and said, "Okay!" So, Sue rode her bike and Tom cheered. They were happy and had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to print pictures on her computer. One day, she printed a very big picture of her favorite animal, a cat. It was a noisy printer, but Lily didn't mind. She liked the sound it made. Yesterday, Lily was playing in the park with her friends. She saw a real cat that looked just like her picture. She wanted to take the cat home, but her mom said no. Lily was sad, but she knew her mom was right. They didn't know if the cat had a home or not. The next day, Lily went to the park again. To her surprise, the cat was still there. This time, the cat had a collar with a phone number on it. Lily's mom called the number, and they found out the cat had been lost. They returned the cat to its owner, and Lily felt happy. The owner was so thankful that they let Lily play with the cat whenever she wanted. The moral of the story is that it's important to do the right thing, even when you want something very much.
