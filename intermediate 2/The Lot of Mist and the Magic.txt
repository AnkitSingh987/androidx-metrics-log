Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lot of Mist and the Magic

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lot of mist. In this town, there was a girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her town was always misty and boring. She wanted to find a way to make her town fun and bright. One day, Lily heard a story about a magic man who could make the mist go away. The magic man lived far away, but Lily knew she had to find him. She asked her mom and dad if she could go, and they said she could if she saved her money to pay for the trip. Lily did many jobs to save money. She helped her mom and dad, and her neighbors too. After some time, she had enough money to pay for the trip. Lily was excited and went to find the magic man. When she found the magic man, he was happy to help her. He gave Lily a magic stone and told her to take it back to her town. The magic stone would make the mist go away and make her town fun and bright. Lily went back to her town and used the magic stone. The mist went away and her town became fun and bright, just like she wanted. Everyone was happy, and they all thanked Lily for her hard work. From that day on, the town was never boring again.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the garden. One day, they found a big bean on the ground. It was green and shiny and looked yummy. "Wow, look at this bean!" Lily said. "I want to eat it!" "No, I want to eat it!" Tom said. "I saw it first!" They both grabbed the bean and tried to pull it away from each other. They began to fight over the bean. They shouted and pushed and tugged. "Give me the bean!" Lily said. "No, it's mine!" Tom said. They fought so hard that they did not see the big dog that came into the garden. The dog was hungry and smelled the bean. It ran towards Lily and Tom and barked loudly. "Roof! Roof!" the dog said. Lily and Tom were scared of the dog. They let go of the bean and ran away. The dog picked up the bean and ate it in one bite. It wagged its tail and licked its lips. Lily and Tom hid behind a tree and looked at the dog. They felt sorry for fighting over the bean. They realized that the bean was not worth losing their friendship. "I'm sorry, Tom," Lily said. "The bean was not that important. You can have it next time." "I'm sorry, too, Lily," Tom said. "The bean was not that yummy. You are my friend and I don't want to fight with you." They hugged and made up. They decided to play something else in the garden. They learned that fighting over a bean was a terrible idea.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, his toy car got filthy from playing in the mud. Tim knew he had to change his car and make it clean again. Tim had an idea. He saw a post in his yard, near the water hose. He took his toy car to the post and used the water to wash his car. He scrubbed and scrubbed until his car was shiny and clean. Tim was very happy with his clean car. He thanked the post for helping him change his filthy car into a clean one. From that day on, Tim made sure to keep his toy car clean and always remembered the helpful post in his yard.

Once upon a time, there was a big crocodile named Coco. Coco was an amazing crocodile with many friends. One day, Coco's toy ball broke. Coco was very sad. Coco went to his friend, the wise old owl. "Can you help me fix my ball?" Coco asked. The owl said, "Yes, let's try to fix it together." So, they worked together to fix the broken ball. After a while, Coco and the owl fixed the ball. Coco was very happy and said, "Thank you, wise owl, for helping me fix my ball!" They played with the ball all day and had lots of fun. And from that day on, Coco and the wise old owl were the best of friends.
