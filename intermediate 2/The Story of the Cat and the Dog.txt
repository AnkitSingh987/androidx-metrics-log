Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Dog

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat was very frustrated because the dog kept taking her toy. They started to fight over the toy. The cat tried to slap the dog to get her toy back. The dog did not like being slapped. He barked at the cat and tried to slap her back. They were both very mad and did not want to share the toy. They kept fighting and slapping each other. A big bird saw the cat and dog fighting. The bird flew down and took the toy away from them. The cat and dog stopped fighting and looked at the bird. They realized that fighting was not the answer. They both said sorry and decided to play together without the toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy made of rubber. It was a big red ball. Amy loved to play with her big red ball every day. One day, Amy found her big red ball had become small. It had shrunk! She was sad and didn't know what to do. She asked her mom, "Mom, why did my ball shrink?" Her mom said, "Sometimes, things made of rubber can shrink when they get cold." Amy wanted her big red ball back. Her mom had an idea. She told Amy, "Let's put the little ball in warm water. It might grow big again." So, they put the little ball in warm water. After some time, the ball grew big again! Amy was so happy to have her big red ball back. From that day on, Amy and her mom played with the big red ball, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a bald painter named Bob. He liked to work with many colors. Bob was very happy when he painted. One day, Bob painted a big tree. The tree had green leaves and a brown trunk. He also painted pretty flowers under the tree. The flowers were red, yellow, and blue. Bob showed his painting to his friends. They all liked it very much. They said, "Good job, Bob!" Bob felt proud and happy. From that day on, he painted more and more beautiful pictures.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red balloon. It was very loud when you hit it. A little girl named Lily loved to play with her balloon. She would throw it up in the sky and catch it when it came down. One day, Lily saw her balloon start to shrink. It was getting smaller and smaller. She was very sad because she loved her balloon so much. She wanted to make it big again. Lily asked her mom for help. Her mom blew air into the balloon and it became big again. Lily was so happy! She played with her loud, red balloon all day long, and every time it started to shrink, her mom would help make it big again.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Bob. Bob liked to eat at a big restaurant. One day, Bob went to the restaurant and asked for a menu. The waiter gave him the menu and Bob looked at it. Bob saw a yummy dish on the menu. He said, "I want to eat that!" The waiter said, "Okay, but it is very, very hot." Bob did not listen. He wanted the yummy dish. The waiter went to the kitchen to get the food. When the food came, Bob ate it very fast. But, oh no! The food was too hot! Bob's mouth was on fire! He said, "Help me! Help me!" But it was too late. Bob learned that he should listen to the waiter.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly frog. The frog lived near a small pond. The frog loved to splash in the water all day long. One day, the frog saw a tiny fish. The fish was trying to find a way to measure how deep the pond was. The frog thought it would be fun to help the fish. They worked together to find a stick. The frog would splash the stick into the water, and the fish would swim down to see if it touched the bottom. They tried many times, but the stick was always too short. Finally, they found a long stick that touched the bottom of the pond. The fish was happy, and the frog was proud. From that day on, the ugly frog and the tiny fish were best friends.
