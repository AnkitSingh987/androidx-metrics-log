Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Furry Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the park with his friends. One day, while playing, he saw a shiny coin on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to show his friends. As Spot ran, he saw a big ice cream truck. The ice cream man was giving out cold treats to the kids. Spot wanted to get a treat too, but he had to freeze and wait for his turn. He sat down, still holding the coin in his mouth. Finally, it was Spot's turn. He gave the coin to the ice cream man and got a yummy ice cream cone. He shared it with his friends, and they all enjoyed the cold treat together. Spot was happy that he found the coin and could share a fun moment with his friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a hole in his yard. He thought it was amazing. He wanted to know what was inside the hole. So, Tim asked his friend, Sam, to come and see the hole with him. Sam came over and looked at the hole. He also thought it was amazing. Sam had a good idea. He suggested that they put a stick in the hole to see how deep it was. They found a long stick and put it in the hole. The stick went very deep. Tim and Sam were very excited. They thought that maybe a treasure was hidden in the hole. They decided to dig and dig until they found something. After a while, they found a small box. Inside the box, there were shiny rocks. Tim and Sam were very happy with their amazing find.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very hungry. He did not know where to find food. He looked around and saw a big tree with a lot of apples. Max wanted to eat the apples, but he was too small to reach them. In the evening, Max met a tall giraffe named Lily. Lily saw that Max was hungry and wanted to help him. She used her long neck to reach the apples in the tree. Max was very happy and thanked Lily for her help. Max and Lily became best friends. They played together every day. Max was never hungry again because he always had Lily to help him. They laughed and played until the sun went down, and they knew they would be friends forever.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw dark clouds in the sky. He heard a loud noise. "What is that?" he asked his mom. "That is thunder," she said. Tim felt a little scared. "Mom, I am sleepy," Tim said. His mom took him inside their house. She gave him a warm hug. Tim felt safe and loved. He was not scared of the thunder anymore. That night, Tim went to bed early. He listened to the thunder outside. He recognized the sound now. It was not so scary. Tim closed his eyes and fell asleep. The thunder sang him a lullaby.

One day, a happy mechanic named Bob was working in his shop. He liked to fix cars and make them go fast. Bob had a big toy car that could shoot water. He loved to play with it when he was not fixing cars. One morning, a boy named Tim came to the shop with his broken toy car. Tim was sad because his car could not go. Bob wanted to help Tim and make him happy too. "I will fix your car," said Bob, and he began to work. As Bob was fixing Tim's car, he thought about his water-shooting toy car. He had an idea! He worked harder and harder to fix the car and add something special. When he was done, he gave the car back to Tim. "Try it now," said Bob, with a big smile. Tim pushed the car, and it went very fast! Then, Tim saw a button on the car. He pushed it, and the car began to shoot water! Tim was so happy, and he thanked Bob. From that day on, Bob and Tim became good friends. They played with their water-shooting cars and had lots of fun. And whenever a toy car needed fixing, they knew just what to do.
