Category: Intermediate
Title: The Unique Princess and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a unique princess who lived in a big castle. She had a pretty dress and a shiny crown. She loved to play with her toys and sing happy songs. One sunny day, the princess met a little frog near a pond. The frog said, "Hello, princess. I dare you to play with me!" The princess was surprised but said, "Okay, frog. What game should we play?" The frog thought for a moment and said, "Let's play hide and seek!" The princess agreed, and they played all day long. They laughed and had so much fun together. After that day, the princess and the frog became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a shy boy named Tom. Tom had a fun uncle named Sam. Uncle Sam liked to play with Tom, but Tom was too shy to play. One day, Uncle Sam had an idea. He put a big red ball in the yard. Uncle Sam said, "Tom, let's play with the ball." Tom looked at the ball and then at Uncle Sam. Tom said, "I am shy, Uncle Sam." Uncle Sam smiled and said, "That's okay, Tom. We can play together." Tom and Uncle Sam played with the ball all day. Tom was not shy anymore. They had lots of fun and laughed a lot.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he had no friends. He liked to play in the park, but he always played alone. One day, Tim found a small, lost dog. The dog was very sad too. Tim decided to save the dog and take it home. He named the dog Max. Max was very happy with Tim, and they became best friends. Tim and Max played together every day. They had so much fun, and they were not lonely anymore. They saved each other's world by being friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bird named Bob. Bob liked to stretch his wings and fly high in the sky. He was very loud and would sing all day long. One sunny day, Bob got a message from his friend, Cat. Cat asked Bob to come to her house for a playdate. Bob was so excited and he stretched his wings to fly to Cat's house. When Bob got to Cat's house, they played with toys and shared yummy snacks. They laughed and had a great time. At the end of the day, Bob stretched his wings again and flew back home. He was so happy he had a fun day with his friend Cat.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Tom. Tom had a funny mustache. He liked to play with his ball. One day, Tom saw a big door open. He thought it would be fun to escape and play outside. Tom went outside and saw a big dog. The dog said, "Hello, I am Sam. I like your mustache!" Tom said, "Thank you, Sam. Let's play together!" They played with Tom's ball and had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. Sam's mustache started to grow! It grew bigger and bigger. Tom and Sam were surprised. They laughed and played with their big and small mustaches. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. Every night, he would hide under his bed covers and shake. His body would feel cold and his heart would beat fast. One day, Tim's mom had an idea. She wanted to help Tim not be scared anymore. She decided to organize a fun game for Tim. She called the game "Brave Tim's Adventure." In the game, Tim had to find all of his toys in the dark. His mom hid the toys in easy places. Tim had to be brave and find them all. With each toy he found, Tim felt less scared. His body felt warm and his heart slowed down. By the end of the game, Tim was not scared of the dark anymore. He felt brave and strong. Tim's mom was happy too. She knew that her little boy would not be scared at night anymore. And they all lived happily ever after.
