Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Park

Once upon a time there was a girl who was very intelligent. Her name was Sue. She was always running and playing. One day she decided to take a jog. She put on her shoes and went outside. Sue started her jog in the park. She jogged and jogged around the park until she came to a turn. Sue decided to take the turn. She ran around and around until she noticed something. On the other side of the turn was her friend, Ryan. Ryan was also very intelligent. When he saw Sue he smiled and waved. Then, they both kept jogging together. They ran around and around the park. It was so much fun. When the sun started to set, they both decided to go back home. Sue and Ryan said bye and continued on their jogs. Sue felt very happy and intelligent after spending time with her friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play in her room. One day, she saw her mom pour juice into a cup. Sue wanted to try pouring too. In her room, Sue found a cabinet. The cabinet was open. Inside the cabinet, there were many cups and a big jug. Sue thought, "I can pour like mom!" She took the jug and tried to pour into a cup. But, the jug was too big and heavy for her. The juice spilled all over the floor. Sue's mom heard the noise and came to help. She saw the mess and said, "Sue, you should ask for help when trying new things." Sue learned that it's okay to ask for help. From that day, she always asked her mom for help when she needed it. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a polite cat named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toy frame. One day, she wanted to show her friends how she could shake the frame. Lily went to her friends, a dog named Max and a bird named Polly. She said, "Look what I can do!" and started to shake the frame. Max and Polly were very happy to see Lily's trick. They clapped and cheered for her. But then, something unexpected happened. The frame broke into two pieces! Lily felt sad, but Max and Polly helped her fix it. Together, they learned that it's important to be careful with our things. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a factory where people sort big and small things. In this factory, there was a thin man named Tom. Tom was very good at sorting things fast. He liked to help his friends at the factory. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, said, "Tom, can you help me sort these things? I am very slow." Tom said, "Yes, Sam. I will help you." So Tom and Sam worked together to sort all the big and small things. But Tom did not listen to the factory rules. He did not wear his safety hat. A big thing fell on Tom's head. Tom was hurt and could not work anymore. The other people in the factory were very sad. The moral of the story is to always follow the rules to stay safe.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. She had a long, pretty dress made of soft wool. Mia loved her dress very much. One day, she went outside to play with her friends. While playing, Mia's dress got stuck on a tree branch. "Oh no!" she cried. Her friend Tom came to help her. "Don't worry, Mia. I'll help you," said Tom. He carefully tried to free her dress from the branch. But, the branch was strong and the dress was stuck. Tom pulled harder and accidentally, the dress began to break. Mia's eyes filled with tears. "My dress!" she said, feeling very sad. Tom felt bad too. He said, "I'm sorry, Mia. I didn't mean to break your dress." Mia looked at Tom and knew he was sorry. She said, "It's okay, Tom. We can fix it together." So, Mia and Tom went to Mia's mom, who helped them sew the dress back together. Mia's dress was as good as new, and she was happy again. She learned that even when things break, they can be fixed with a little help from friends and family.
