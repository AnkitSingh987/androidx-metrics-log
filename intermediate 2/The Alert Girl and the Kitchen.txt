Category: Intermediate
Title: The Alert Girl and the Kitchen

Once there was an alert little girl who was exploring her home. She opened the kitchen drawer and was excited to find a prize inside. She carefully removed it and squealed with delight when she saw a shiny toy! It was heavy, so she took it to show her Mommy. Her Mommy was so proud of her for being alert and exploring her house. After showing her Mommy the prize, she went back to the kitchen and opened the drawer again. This time there was nothing inside, so she decided to keep it closed. As she skipped away, she made sure to stay alert and watch for something new to find. Maybe tomorrow she would go back to the kitchen drawer and see what else it had to offer. She was sure there were lots of fun surprises to be found!

---

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. They had a big bag of candy with them. Sue was very happy because she loved candy. At the park, they saw a big tree. Sue's mom said, "Sue, let's sit under the tree and eat some candy." Sue took a candy from the bag and put it in her mouth. But it was very sour! She made a funny face and said, "Mom, this candy is sour!" Her mom laughed and said, "Sue, you need to listen to me. I told you to wait before eating the candy. You need to wait for the candy to become sweet." But Sue didn't want to wait. She wanted to eat the candy now. She tried to eat another candy, but it was still sour. Sue was sad and didn't know what to do. Her mom said, "Sue, I will help you. Let's wait together." So Sue and her mom waited under the tree. They talked and laughed. After a while, Sue tried the candy again. This time, it was sweet! Sue was so happy. She learned that sometimes, it's good to wait and listen to her mom.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, he went outside to play. The sun was shining, and the ground was very dry. Tim saw his friend, Sam, and they started to play together. They took turns to kick the ball to each other. Tim was happy, but he did not see the big rock on the ground. He was about to kick the ball again. When Tim kicked the ball, his foot hit the big rock instead. "Ouch!" he cried. Sam ran to help him. Tim's mom heard him and came out too. She gave Tim a big hug and took him inside to make him feel better. Tim learned to watch out for rocks when he played, and he and Sam had lots of fun playing together after that.

John was playing in the park with his wagon. He pushed his wagon around the park exploring all the cool things. He saw the slide and ran over to it. He climbed the steps, but then he saw the swings and ran over to them. John loved pushing his wagon around the park. He would go over hills, around corners and visit all the interesting places. He kept pushing his wagon until he saw the pond. He stopped and looked out over the water. He saw some ducks and smiled. John sighed as he watched the ducks. He took a deep breath of the cool air and felt happy. He waved to the ducks, then shrugged his shoulders and pushed his wagon away from the pond. John started pushing his wagon around the park again, exploring and having fun.

Tom is a loyal dog. He loves his family and his home. He likes to play with his ball and his bone. He also likes to wear his vest. His vest is red and warm. It has his name on it. One day, Tom sees a cat in his yard. The cat is black and sneaky. It has green eyes and sharp claws. Tom does not like the cat. He barks at the cat and chases it. The cat runs away, but not before it scratches Tom's vest. Tom's vest has a big hole in it. Tom is sad and angry. Tom goes back to his family. He shows them his vest. His family is sorry for Tom. They hug him and pet him. They say, "Don't worry, Tom. We will provide you with a new vest. You are a good and loyal dog. We love you very much." Tom feels better. He wags his tail and licks his family. He is happy and grateful. He knows his family will always provide for him and care for him. He is a loyal dog. He loves his family and his home. He likes to play with his ball and his bone. He also likes to wear his vest. His vest is red and warm. It has his name on it.
