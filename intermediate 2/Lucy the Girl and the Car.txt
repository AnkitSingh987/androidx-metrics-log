Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a pretty room with lots of toys. Lucy loved her room so much. One day, her friend Tom came over to play. Lucy said, "Tom, do you like my room? I think it is very pretty." Tom looked around and said, "Yes, Lucy, your room is very pretty. But I have a toy car that I think is more fun." Lucy was sad because she did not have a toy car like Tom. She wanted to have one too. So, Lucy and Tom went to Lucy's mom and asked her if Lucy could have a toy car. Lucy's mom said, "Lucy, you have so many toys already. You do not need a toy car right now. Maybe you can have one later." Lucy was still sad, but she understood. She played with her other toys with Tom and they had lots of fun. Even though she did not have a toy car, Lucy still loved her pretty room and her many toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Suzy. Suzy had a big wardrobe in her room. In the wardrobe, she had many pretty dresses and shirts. She loved to look at them and admire how nice they were. One day, Suzy's mom told her they were going to a party. Suzy was very excited. She wanted to wear her most pretty dress. She looked in her wardrobe and found a pink dress. It was her favorite. At the party, Suzy saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with a ball. The ball was very tough. Suzy and Tim played together. They had a lot of fun. They became good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a house with her mom, dad, and her pet dog, Spot. Sue loved to play with Spot and look at the world around her. She thought everything was full of wonder. One day, Sue and Spot were playing in the yard. They were having so much fun. But then, Spot saw a butterfly and ran away. Sue was sad and scared. She needed to find her best friend. So, Sue started to search for Spot. Sue looked behind the trees, under the bushes, and in the flowers. She even asked her mom and dad if they had seen Spot. But no one knew where Spot was. Sue felt very sad and worried. Finally, Sue heard a bark. It was Spot! He was stuck in a hole near the fence. Sue ran to him and helped him get out. They were both so happy to be together again. Sue gave Spot a big hug, and they went back to playing in the yard. And from that day on, Sue made sure to keep a close eye on her little friend.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near the big trees. One day, the man went to work and the wife stayed at home. She was very happy to stay in the house and play with her cat. While the man was away, a big storm came. The wind blew very hard and the rain fell down fast. The wife was scared, but she stayed inside with her cat. They hid under the bed together. When the storm stopped, the man came back. He saw that their house was broken. The wife was sad, but the man said, "Don't worry, we will fix it." They worked together to fix the broken house. Soon, the house was good again and they were happy. They stayed together in their fixed house, with their cat, and lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play baseball with his friends. The sun was shining, but the wind was a bit harsh. They played on the grass and laughed a lot. Suddenly, the sky turned dark, and it started to rain. Tim and his friends ran to a big tree to stay dry. They looked at the rain and felt sad because they could not play baseball anymore. Tim said to his friends, "Let's sing a song to make the rain go away." They sang and clapped their hands. Soon, the rain stopped, and the sun came out again. Tim and his friends went back to playing baseball, happy that their song worked.
