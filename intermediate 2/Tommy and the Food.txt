Category: Intermediate
Title: Tommy and the Food

Once upon a time, a little boy called Tommy was getting ready to have dinner. His tummy was growling - he was very hungry! He looked at the table, and saw an onion on his plate. Tommy didn't like onions, so he closed his eyes and prayed. He asked if he could have his favourite food instead. Tommy's mom was very patient, but Tommy was very impatient. He was so cross about the onion that he stomped his feet and shouted. His mom waited for a few minutes, until Tommy had calmed down. She asked Tommy to come and sit at the table. Tommy still prayed that his favorite food would appear. But instead of his favorite food, his mom gave him some nice vegetables. She said that although she can't always give him what he wants, she still loves him very much. Tommy took a bite of the vegetables, and to his surprise - he loved them! It turned out that he actually really liked the onions. He finished his dinner, and thanked God for his mum's love.

Once upon a time, there was a warm sun. It loved to shine on the earth. It made the flowers grow and the animals happy. One day, the warm sun saw a little girl playing outside. She was making a poem. The poem was about how much she loved the sun. The warm sun wanted to help the little girl. It saw a big puddle of water nearby. The sun thought, "I can soak up the water and make the ground dry for her." So, the sun worked very hard to soak up the water. The little girl was happy because she could play without getting wet. The little girl finished her poem and read it out loud. The warm sun was so happy to hear the poem. The sun kept shining, and the little girl kept playing. They were both very happy, and they loved each other very much.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there lived a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. He would drive them all around his room, making roads and bridges with his building blocks. Tim's favorite car was a red one with a big weight on the back. One sunny day, Tim decided to drive his red car outside in the garden. He made a long path with the weight, making lines in the soft dirt. As he was driving, he saw a little bird. The bird looked at Tim and said, "Can I play too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with me!" So the little bird sat on the weight of the red car, and Tim drove it all around the garden. They played together all day long, making new paths and having lots of fun. In the end, Tim and the little bird became best friends, and they always played together with the red car in the ordinary town.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin named Pippin. Pippin was a tough penguin. He lived in a cold place with lots of ice and snow. One day, Pippin saw a little bird with a broken wing. The bird was very sad. Pippin wanted to help the bird. He thought he could lend a hand. Pippin took the bird to his warm home. He gave the bird food and let the bird stay with him. The bird got better and they became good friends. The bird was happy that Pippin lent a hand. And Pippin was happy to have a new friend.

Lily and her mom moved to a new house. They had many boxes to unpack. Lily wanted to help, but she did not know where to put her toys and books. She asked her mom to teach her. Mom smiled and gave Lily a hug. She said, "I will teach you how to unpack. First, we need to find the box with your name on it. Then, we need to open it and take out your things. Next, we need to choose a place for them in your room. Last, we need to put them away neatly and throw away the paper and tape." Lily nodded and followed her mom to her room. She saw a big box with her name on it. She opened it and saw her teddy bear, her doll, her puzzle, and her coloring book. She was happy to see her things. She picked up her teddy bear and hugged it. She said, "I want to put him on my bed. He will keep me company at night." Mom said, "That's a good idea. Let's put him on your bed. Where do you want to put your doll?" Lily thought for a moment. She saw a shelf on the wall. She said, "I want to put her on the shelf. She will look pretty there." Mom said, "That's a good place for her. Let's put her on the shelf. What about your puzzle and your coloring book?" Lily said, "I want to put them in the drawer. I can play with them later." Mom said, "That's a smart choice. Let's put them in the drawer. You are doing a great job, Lily. You are learning how to unpack." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom. You are a good teach. I like our new house. It is cozy and bright." Mom smiled and said, "You are welcome, Lily. I am proud of you. I like our new house too. It is much better than our old house. It was cold and dark." Lily remembered their old house. It was pale and sad. She did not like it. She was glad they moved. She said, "Me too, Mom. Me too."
