Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Joe and the Story

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Joe. One day Joe was feeling quite sad. He didn't understand why things didn't feel like they fitted like they used to. When asked why he was feeling sad Joe replied, "My life doesn't feel like it fits". His mom gave him a big hug and said, "It's alright darling, sometimes things don't fit the way they did before. That's OK. It means you can try something new." Joe thought about this and decided to try something new. He visited his neighbor and made her a card. It was very thoughtful and she was very surprised. The moral of the story is that trying new things can help you find what fits in your life. Being thoughtful of others can also be rewarding.

Once upon a time, in a big red house, there lived a small dog named Bobo. Bobo had a red ball that he loved to play with. One day, Bobo saw a little strip of light coming through the door. He wanted to go outside and play with his red ball in the sun. Bobo pushed the door open and escaped into the yard. He saw a big red bird named Lulu sitting on a branch. Lulu said, "Hi, Bobo! What are you doing outside?" Bobo said, "I escaped to play with my red ball in the sun!" Lulu and Bobo played together all day long. They laughed and had so much fun. But when the sun started to go down, Bobo's mom called him to come back inside. Bobo didn't want to go. Lulu said, "Bobo, you should listen to your mom. It's getting dark and cold." Bobo listened to Lulu and went back inside. His mom hugged him and said, "I'm glad you came back, Bobo. Remember, it's important to listen to your mom and dad." Bobo learned that it's good to have fun, but it's also important to listen to your parents.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max had a job. His job was to protect a little girl named Lily. Lily and Max were great friends. One day, Max and Lily went to the park. They saw a big bird. The bird wanted to take Lily's toy. Max said, "No, bird! I protect Lily!" The bird was sad but went away. Max and Lily played and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The big bird came back with a toy for Max. The bird said, "I am sorry, Max. I want to be friends too." Max, Lily, and the bird all played together. They learned that sharing and being kind is what makes friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a big piece of cardboard in the garage. She had a fun idea. Sue wanted to raise the cardboard up high and make a slide for her toys. She put one end on the couch and the other end on the floor. Her toys slid down the cardboard, laughing and having fun. After playing, Sue was hungry. She went to the kitchen and found a sour apple. She took a bite and made a funny face. The sour apple was not her favorite, but she ate it anyway. Sue's day was full of fun and new things.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat sauce. One day, Tom saw a big bowl of sauce on the table. He wanted to eat it all by himself. But his friend, a little dog named Max, also liked sauce. Tom did not want to share the sauce with Max. Tom had an idea. He would make the sauce wet so Max would not want to eat it. He poured water into the bowl of sauce. The sauce became very wet. Tom thought Max would not like the wet sauce. But Max did not care. He still wanted to eat the sauce. Tom started to regret what he did. He wished he did not make the sauce wet. Tom and Max both tried the wet sauce. It did not taste good anymore. They were both sad. In the end, Tom and Max learned that they should share and not be selfish.
