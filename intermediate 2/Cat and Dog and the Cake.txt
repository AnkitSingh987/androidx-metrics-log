Category: Intermediate
Title: Cat and Dog and the Cake

One day, two friends, Cat and Dog, were playing together in the park. They found a big, delicious cake on a table. Both Cat and Dog wanted to eat the cake, but they didn't want to share it. This made them very sad and upset. Cat said, "I want the cake!" Dog said, "No, I want the cake!" They were not happy with each other. They needed to use their brains to find a way to solve their problem. Then, a wise owl saw them and said, "Why don't you unite and share the cake?" Cat and Dog thought about it and agreed to share the delicious cake. They used their brains to cut the cake in two parts. Cat and Dog were happy to share the cake, and they continued to play together in the park. They learned that when they unite and work together, they can solve their problems and have more fun.

One day, a seal went to play by the sea. The seal was very happy. He liked to swim and jump in the waves. The sun was shining, and the water was not bitter. It was a good day. A big fish saw the seal and said, "Why are you so happy?" The seal answered, "I like to play in the sea. It is fun!" The big fish did not like it. He wanted to destroy the seal's fun. So, he thought of a plan. The big fish told the seal, "I know a place where the water is warmer and more fun. Follow me!" The seal was excited and followed the big fish. But the big fish led the seal to a place with big rocks and strong waves. The seal got scared and tried to swim away, but the waves were too strong. The seal was stuck. The big fish laughed and said, "Now you cannot play and be happy!" The seal was very sad. The big fish had destroyed the seal's fun day. And the day ended bad for the seal.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny. The bunny had a big horn on his head. He loved to play with his friends. One day, he wanted to push his friends on the swing. He was very happy. But, a spicy smell came from the bushes. It was a big, angry fox. The fox wanted to take the horn from the bunny. The bunny was scared. He did not want to give his horn to the fox. So, the bunny and his friends made a plan. They pushed a big rock on the fox's tail. The fox was trapped! The bunny and his friends were safe. They played and laughed together, and the angry fox went away.

Once upon a time, there was a pink cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play with her steel ball. She would push it with her nose and chase it around the house. One day, Kitty lost her steel ball. She looked and looked but could not find it anywhere. Kitty asked her friend, Doggy, "Please help me find my steel ball." Doggy agreed and they started to search together. They looked in the garden and under the bed, but the ball was not there. Kitty was very sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down from the sky with the steel ball in its beak. The bird had taken the ball to play with it in the sky! The bird said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know it was yours. Please take it back." Kitty was so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Kitty, Doggy, and the big bird became best friends and played together all the time.

Once there was an octopus named Annie. She loved to explore the ocean and collect interesting things. One day she was swimming when she saw something amazing. It was a beautiful colorful stone with sparkles. Excitedly, Annie wanted to take the stone home. She tried to collect it, but it was stuck to a rock. Annie was persistent. She tried and tried, but the stone wouldn't come off. Suddenly, a friendly fish swam up and asked what she was doing. Annie told her about the stone and the fish said, "Maybe I can help!" The friendly fish used her fin to bump the rock the stone was stuck to. Annie was so happy - the stone was loose and she was able to collect it. She thanked the friendly fish and swam off with her new treasure. Annie was so proud of her collection!
