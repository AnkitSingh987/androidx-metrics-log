Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tree and the Strong

Once upon a time, there was an old tree. The tree was very strong. It had big branches and lots of green leaves. Many birds lived in the tree. They were happy and sang songs every day. One day, a part of the tree started to rot. The birds were sad and worried. They did not want to lose their home. They asked their friend, the wind, for help. The wind blew strong and cleaned the rot away. The old tree felt better and strong again. The birds were happy and sang louder than before. They thanked the wind for helping them. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run in the park. One day, Max saw a big cat. The cat gave Max a wink. Max was not sure if the cat wanted to play or not. Max decided to be nice and feed the cat some of his food. The cat was very happy and ate the food. Max felt a little guilty because he knew the food was his, but he wanted to be a good friend. After the cat ate, they both played together in the park. They ran and chased each other. Max and the cat became best friends. From that day on, Max and the cat shared their food and played together all the time.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived an elderly lady. She had a little house with a big garden. The garden was full of life with many flowers, trees, and animals. The elderly lady loved to sit in her garden and watch the animals play. One day, a little bird came to the elderly lady's garden. It was lost and needed help. The elderly lady saw the bird and said, "Come here, little bird. I will help you." The bird came to her, and she gave it some water and food. After a while, the little bird felt better. It was happy and started to sing. The elderly lady smiled and knew that she had made a new friend. From that day on, the little bird would come and visit her every day, bringing more life and joy to her garden.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely cat named Tom. Tom did not have any friends. One day, he went on a tour to find new friends. He walked and walked, looking for someone to play with. On his tour, Tom met a dog named Max. Max was big and strong. Tom was scared of Max. But Max said, "Don't be scared, Tom. I want to be your friend!" Tom was very surprised. He did not think a dog could be friends with a cat. Tom and Max played together all day. They ran, jumped, and had a lot of fun. The other animals saw them and wanted to play too. Soon, Tom was not lonely anymore. He had many friends and was very happy.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were playing in the park. They were fine friends. They liked to run, jump, and play together. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. They were very happy. Suddenly, there was a loud noise. It was an earthquake! The ground shook and the trees danced. The big cat and the little dog were scared. They held each other tight. Then, the earthquake stopped. The big cat and the little dog looked around. The park was a mess. But, they were safe. The big cat gave the little dog a soft slap on the back. They laughed and knew they would be fine. They were still friends, even after the scary earthquake.

One day, a big bear and a little rabbit sat down to have tea. The big bear said, "I want strong tea." The little rabbit said, "I want tea too." They ordered tea from a bird who brought them tea in tiny cups. They drank the tea and talked about fun things. They laughed and played with their tea cups. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew their tea cups away. The big bear and little rabbit were sad. But then, the bird came back with new tea cups. They were so happy and continued to drink their tea and have fun.
