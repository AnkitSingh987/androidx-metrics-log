Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Sam and the Bowl

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Sam. Sam loved to go exploring in the woods. One day, he came across an herb. He had never seen an herb before and he thought it looked normal. So he touched it. As soon as he did, the herb started to rot. At first, Sam was worried and he felt sad, but then he noticed something else. As the herb was rotting, it was turning into a really yummy smelling sauce. Sam was so excited and he rushed off to get a bowl. He came back and put the bowl right underneath the herb and let the sauce drip into the bowl. Once the bowl was full, Sam was very happy. He served himself a big bowl of the yummy smelling sauce, and it was the best dinner he ever had. From then on, Sam started to go exploring in the woods every day, and he always looked for normal herbs.

One day, a blue bird wanted to organize her nest. She flew around and found a magazine. The bird thought the magazine would be perfect to make her nest nice and cozy. The blue bird took the magazine back to her nest. She used her beak to tear the pages and put them in the right place. Her nest started to look very pretty and neat. The blue bird was happy with her organized nest. She felt proud and comfy in her new home. Her friends saw her nest and loved it too. And they all lived happily in their cozy homes.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play in her garden. One day, she found a big jug. The jug was very heavy. Sue wanted to take the jug home. But it was not easy to carry the jug. Sue started to struggle with the jug. She tried to pull it. She tried to push it. But the jug was too heavy for her. Sue did not want to give up. She tried again and again to move the jug. At last, Sue was very tired. She sat down and cried. The jug was still in the garden. Sue could not take it home. She was sad. The jug was too heavy, and Sue did not have a happy day.

Once there was a boy. He loved to go outside and play. One summer day, he went to the park. He paused and looked around. He saw a dove sitting on a branch. The dove was white and shiny. The boy was so glad to see it. The boy asked the dove, "Hello there! What are you doing?". The dove flapped his wings and said, "I'm having a picnic. Would you like to join me?". The boy was so happy! He said, "Yes, I would love to join you!" So he grabbed a blanket and set up his picnic. The boy and the dove spent the afternoon eating and playing together. When it was time to go home, the boy said goodbye to the dove and went home. He was so glad he had spent the day with his new feathered friend.

One day, a girl named Sally wanted to make a special dish for her mom. She went to the kitchen and found a big bowl. Sally was very generous and put lots of yummy things in the bowl. She put in apples, bananas, and even some cookies. Sally started to mix everything in the bowl. She mixed and mixed until it was all mixed up. Suddenly, her dog, Spot, came into the kitchen. He saw the dish and thought it was for him. Spot jumped up and ate the whole dish very fast. Sally was sad that her dish was gone. But then, she had a great idea. She would make another dish, but this time, she would share it with Spot. Sally and Spot made a new dish together and had a fun time eating it. In the end, they both were happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat. The fat cat loved to store many things in his big house. One day, the fat cat found a green ball in the park. The fat cat liked the green ball very much. The fat cat played with the green ball every day. He would store the green ball in a special box at night. One day, the fat cat heard a small voice coming from the box. The voice said, "Please let me out." The fat cat was scared but curious. He opened the box and saw a tiny green bug. The bug said, "Thank you for letting me out. I will give you three wishes." The fat cat wished for more food, a big toy, and a new friend. The bug said, "I will give you these things, but first you must promise to never store me in the box again." The fat cat agreed and got his wishes. But one day, the fat cat forgot his promise. He stored the green bug in the box again. The bug was very sad and angry. The bug took away all the wishes and left the fat cat all alone. The fat cat was sad and learned that he should always keep his promises.
