Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Quiet Girl and the Bird

One day, a quiet little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a pretty bird in a tree. Lily was so happy to see the bird. She clapped her hands and said, "Look, Mom! Pretty bird!" Then, the bird flew away and disappeared. Lily was in shock. She asked her mom, "Where did the bird go?" Her mom said, "The bird went to find food, dear." Lily and her mom played at the park for a while. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, they saw the pretty bird again. This time, it had a worm in its beak. Lily smiled and waved goodbye to the bird. The bird flew away, and Lily knew it would come back again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to help make a dish for dinner. Mia was very happy and excited to help. Mia's mom said, "We will make a spicy dish today." Mia didn't know what "spicy" meant, but she wanted to help. Her mom showed her how to add special things to the dish to make it spicy. Mia was a good helper and her mom encouraged her to try her best. When the spicy dish was done, Mia and her mom sat down to eat together. Mia tasted the dish and it was very yummy! She was proud that she helped her mom make the dish. From that day on, Mia loved to help in the kitchen and try new spicy dishes with her mom.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big festival. Many people came to have fun and eat sweet food. They played games and danced all day long. Everyone was very happy. One day, a sneaky fox came to the festival. He wanted to steal the sweet food. The fox was very quiet and took the food when no one was looking. The people at the festival were sad because their sweet food was gone. But then, a little girl saw the fox with the sweet food. She was not scared of the fox. Instead, she walked up to him and asked, "Why did you steal our food?" The fox looked surprised and said, "I was hungry and wanted to try the sweet food." The little girl smiled and said, "You don't have to steal. You can join us at the festival and share the food." The fox felt bad for stealing and decided to join the festival. Everyone was happy again, and they all shared the sweet food with the fox.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful dog named Max. Max was very big and strong. He liked to scare cats and play with his friends. One day, Max found a big book on the ground. It was a math book. Max did not know what math was, but he thought it was fun to look at the pictures. Max showed his friends the math book. They all thought it was a magic book. They believed it would make them more powerful. Max and his friends decided to try the magic. They stood in a circle and started to bark at the book. They hoped the book would give them powers. But, instead of getting powers, they made a big noise. The noise was so loud that it scared all the cats away. Max and his friends were sad. They thought the math book would make them powerful, but it only scared away their friends. From that day on, Max and his friends learned to be more careful with things they did not understand.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to gather buttons of all colors. She had a big box full of them. One day, she found a big, red button outside her door. She picked it up and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! I found a big, red button!" Lucy said. Tom looked at the button and laughed. "That's a foolish button! It's too big for your box," he said. Lucy felt sad, but she didn't want to throw the button away. So, Lucy and Tom decided to find a new box for the big, red button. They went to the store and bought a new, bigger box. They put the button in the box and carried it back to Lucy's house. Lucy was happy now. She had a new box for her big, red button. From that day on, Lucy and Tom would gather more buttons together. They found buttons of all sizes and colors. And they never thought any button was foolish again. They were the best of friends, and they loved their button collection.
