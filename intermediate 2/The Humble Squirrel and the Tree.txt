Category: Intermediate
Title: The Humble Squirrel and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a humble squirrel. The squirrel liked to play with his friends in the park. They would run, jump, and climb trees all day long. The squirrel was very fast and could run like the wind. One day, the squirrel and his friends were playing a game of tag. They were having so much fun that they didn't see the little girl walking near them. The squirrel almost bumped into her, but he stopped just in time. The little girl was scared and started to cry. The humble squirrel felt bad for making the little girl cry. He stopped playing and went to her. He gave her a big, sweet nut to make her feel better. The little girl smiled and thanked the squirrel. From that day on, the squirrel and his friends learned to always be careful and watch out for others while playing. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big whale swam in the sea. The whale was very smelly. He saw a fish and said, "Hello, fish! Will you be my friend?" The fish said, "No, you are too smelly. I don't want to be your friend." The whale was sad. He swam and swam until he found a box. The box was locked. The whale wanted to unlock the box. He tried and tried, but he could not unlock it. He asked a crab to help him. The crab said, "I will help you if you help me find my home." So, the whale and the crab went to find the crab's home. They found it under a big rock. The crab was happy. He said, "Thank you, whale! Now I will help you unlock the box." The crab used his strong claw to unlock the box. Inside the box, there was a big sponge. The whale used the sponge to clean himself. He was not smelly anymore. The fish saw the whale and said, "You are not smelly now. I want to be your friend." The whale, the fish, and the crab all played together and were happy.

One day, two friends, Jake and Jenny, were playing in the park. They were having lots of fun but then some rude boys came and started making fun of them. Jake got angry. He ran over to the pond and started pouring water into it. Then, a magical jet of water shot up from the pond! Jenny and Jake were so excited - they had never seen anything like it before. They had lots of fun playing in the pond and the jet of water. They cheered and cheered. After a while, the rude boys left and they went back to playing. They had so much fun that day!

Once upon a time, there was a big cauliflower. It lived in a garden with many other plants. The big cauliflower was very happy because it was growing strong and big. One day, the big cauliflower began to suffer. It felt sad and weak. A little boy named Tim saw the big cauliflower and wanted to help it. Tim thought of a plan to make the big cauliflower feel better. Tim brought some water and food for the big cauliflower. He took care of it every day. Then, something unexpected happened. The big cauliflower started to grow bigger and bigger. It became so big that it turned into a big, friendly giant! The giant cauliflower could talk and play with Tim. They became best friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a strong owl. The owl lived in a big tree. The owl liked to fly high in the sky and look for food. One day, the strong owl saw a small cat. The cat was lost and sad. The owl wanted to help the cat find its way home. The cat asked the owl, "Can you help me?" The owl just gave a shrug. The owl did not know where the cat's home was. The strong owl and the small cat looked for the cat's home all day. They could not find it. The sky became dark and the stars came out. The cat was still lost and sad. The owl felt bad. They could not find the cat's home and the cat could not be happy again.
