Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Gifted Boy and the Bikes

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tim. Tim could ride his bike very fast. One day, Tim wanted to play a game with his friend, Sam. Tim said, "Sam, let's ride our bikes and see who can get the best score!" Sam agreed, and they started to ride their bikes around the park. They were both very happy and laughed as they raced each other. In the end, Tim got a higher score than Sam. Tim was very proud of himself, but he also knew that playing with his friend was more important than winning. So, Tim gave Sam a big hug and said, "Good job, Sam! Let's play again tomorrow!"

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the garden. She saw a big squash. Lucy was troubled. How could she take the squash to her mom? She tried to pick it up, but it was too heavy. Lucy asked her friend, Tom, for help. "Tom, can you help me with this big squash?" Tom said, "Yes, let's take it to your mom together." They tried to lift the squash, but it was still too heavy. Then, Lucy had an idea. They found a big blanket and put the squash on it. They each held one side of the blanket and walked slowly. The squash settled on the blanket. Lucy and Tom carried the squash to Lucy's mom. She was happy and made a yummy meal with it.

Once, there was a reindeer. He lived in a cold land where the wind was bitter. The reindeer had a big family. They played and ate together. One day, the reindeer saw a big hill. He wanted to go up the hill. He thought it would be fun. The reindeer went up the hill, step by step. At the top of the hill, the reindeer looked around. He saw his family far away. He felt happy. The reindeer went down the hill and told his family about the big hill. They all went up the hill together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom lived in a big house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue and Tom liked to play, but Sue had to go to work with her mom every day. Tom was sad when Sue was not home. One day, Tom had an idea. He wanted to surprise Sue when she came back from work. Tom thought, "I will make a big tower with her toys!" So, Tom started to build the tower. He worked very hard and was very careful. Finally, the tower was ready. When Sue came home, she saw the tower and was so surprised! She laughed and hugged Tom. They played with the tower together. The moral of the story is that hard work can bring happiness to others.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little bird. The bird would wake up every morning and speak to all his friends. He would say "Hello!" and they would say "Hello!" back. They loved to chat with each other. One day, the cute bird met a new friend. This friend was a small, green frog. The frog could not speak like the bird. But they still liked to play together. They would jump and splash in the water. As the days went by, the cute bird and the green frog became best friends. They would wake up every morning and play together. Even though they could not speak the same way, they still understood each other. And they were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a chubby witch. She had a big hat and a long broom. She liked to fly in the sky at night. One day, she saw a little boy crying. He was sad because he lost his toy. The chubby witch wanted to help the little boy. She used her broom to strike the ground. Suddenly, the toy appeared! The little boy was very happy. He thanked the witch and they became friends. From that day on, the little boy and the witch played together. The other kids in the village saw this and learned that being kind to others is good. They also became friends with the chubby witch. Everyone in the village was happy and kind to each other. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when they need it.
