Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Shoes

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was an obedient boy who always listened to his mommy and daddy. He loved to play with his toys and have fun with his friends. One day, Tim's mommy asked him to please go to the store to buy some milk. Tim put on his shoes, took the money, and went to the store. He walked slowly and carefully, just like his mommy told him to. When he got to the store, he found the milk and gave the money to the man at the store. The man smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. You are such an obedient boy." Tim walked back home with the milk. When he got home, his mommy was so happy to see him. She said, "Thank you for being an obedient boy and getting the milk, Tim. I am so proud of you." Tim felt very happy that he could help his mommy. He learned that when you listen and do what you are asked, it makes everyone happy. And that was the moral value of the story: being obedient and helpful makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a kind fireman. He had a big red truck. He liked to help people. One day, he saw a little girl. The girl was scared. She lost her way home. The fireman said, "Do not be scared. I will help you." He took her hand and they walked together. The girl began to smile. She was not scared anymore. Soon, they found her house. The girl's mom and dad were so happy! They said, "Thank you, fireman!" The fireman smiled too. He was glad he could help. The girl was not scared anymore, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a cat named Tom went to the kitchen. He wanted to find some food. He saw a big bowl on the table. Tom was very happy. He jumped on the table to see what was inside. Tom looked into the bowl and found some butter. The butter was very big and impressive. Tom wanted to eat the butter, but he needed to reveal it first. He pulled off the cover and saw the butter. Tom ate the butter and it was very yummy. He was happy and full. Now, Tom could go play with his friends. He had a great day!

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Tom. Tom had a special hat. The hat could shine very bright. Tom loved his hat so much. He wore it every day. One day, Tom met a little dog named Tim. Tim saw Tom's hat and said, "Wow, your hat is so bright! Can I try it?" Tom didn't want to share his hat, so he said, "No, it's mine!" Tim felt sad and walked away. Later, Tom saw Tim playing with a shiny ball. Tom wanted to play too. He asked Tim, "Can I play with you?" Tim remembered how Tom didn't share his hat. But Tim was kind and said, "Yes, you can play with me." Tom was happy, and they played together. Tom learned that sharing is good. He let Tim wear his shiny hat, and they became best friends. From that day on, Tom always shared his things with others. The moral value of the story is to always share with others, and you will make many friends.

One day, a boy named Tom was playing with his blocks. He loved to build big towers with them. He had red blocks, blue blocks, and yellow blocks. Tom's friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw Tom's big tower of blocks and wanted to help. "Can I play too?" she asked. Tom said, "Yes, you can help me build the tower." So, Sue picked up some blocks and started to build with Tom. As they were building, Tom's little dog, Zip, ran into the room. Zip was very fast and very excited. He ran right into the tower of blocks, and it fell down. Tom and Sue were sad. Zip looked sorry for what he had done. Tom said to Zip, "It's okay, we can build it again." Sue agreed, and they all started to build the tower together. This time, they made it even bigger and better than before. Zip was careful not to knock it down again. In the end, Tom, Sue, and Zip had a great time playing with the blocks. They learned that it's okay to make mistakes, as long as you say sorry and help fix them.
