Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big, bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to chase and catch things. One day, Tom saw a little red ball with the word "zoom" on it. He thought it would be fun to play with the ball. He watched the ball roll away and knew he would catch it soon. The next day, Tom saw the little red zoom ball again. It was rolling fast down the street. Tom ran after the ball, but it was very hard to catch. He tried and tried, but the ball was too fast for him. Tom felt sad, but he didn't give up. He knew he would find a way to catch the zoom ball. Finally, Tom had a great idea. He waited behind a big tree until the little red zoom ball came rolling by. As the ball rolled past the tree, Tom jumped out and caught it with his paws. He was so happy that he finally caught the zoom ball! From that day on, Tom and the little red zoom ball were best friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a thin cat went for a walk. On the way, the cat saw a grill. The cat was very hungry, and the grill smelled good. The cat wanted to find some food to cook on the grill. As the cat walked, it met a friendly dog. The dog said, "Hi, cat! What are you doing?" The cat said, "I am hungry. I want to find some food to cook on the grill." The dog said, "I can help you find food." Together, the cat and the dog looked for food. They found a big, juicy hot dog. They went back to the grill and cooked the hot dog. They shared the hot dog and ate it all up. The cat and the dog were very happy and full. They had a great day, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little red bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to sing loud songs all day long. One day, he met a big lion named Lulu. Lulu was very sad because she had zero friends. Bobo wanted to help Lulu. He thought that if he could scare away the bad animals, then Lulu could make friends. So, Bobo sang a very loud song to scare away the bad animals. But something unexpected happened. Instead of scaring them away, the loud song made the bad animals laugh and become happy. Now, all the animals in the forest wanted to be friends with Bobo and Lulu. They learned that being loud and funny could make everyone happy. The moral of the story is that sometimes, being loud can bring people together and make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very enthusiastic. He loved to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a magnet. Tim liked to use the magnet to pick up things like paper clips and coins. One day, Tim went to the park with his magnet. He saw a little girl named Sue playing with her toy car. Tim thought it would be fun to tease Sue with his magnet. He used the magnet to make the toy car move away from Sue. Sue got sad and started to cry. Tim's mom saw what was happening and told Tim that teasing is not nice. Tim felt bad and said sorry to Sue. He showed Sue how to use the magnet to pick up things. They became friends and played together with the magnet and the toy car. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and not tease them.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to paint. She would paint all day long. She painted trees, sun, and sky. She used red, blue, and yellow colors. Lily was very happy when she painted. One day, Lily saw a high wall. She wanted to paint the wall with love. So, she took her paint and brush. She painted hearts and smiles on the wall. She painted them big and bright. The wall looked very pretty. People saw the wall and smiled too. They felt the love from Lily's painting. They said, "Thank you, Lily!" Lily was happy. She knew her painting made people feel good. And that made her love painting even more.
