Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Picture

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very curious and loved to explore. One day, while she was exploring, she saw a big picture on the wall. She looked closely and saw lots of colourful animals. It was so funny that she started to giggle. Suddenly, something tickled her nose and she started to sneeze. The animals on the picture started to move, hopping around the wall. Lucy was so surprised that she started to laugh. "Wow!" she said in amazement. "That was the funniest picture ever!" She ran to tell her mom and dad about the moving picture. They were so surprised that they came to see it for themselves. The family all laughed together, delighted at the funny picture. That was the funniest surprise they had seen in a very long time.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Whistle. Whistle loved to fly and sing all day. One day, Whistle saw something new on the ground. It was small, white, and looked like popcorn. Whistle went down to eat the popcorn. But when Whistle tried to eat it, it was too hard. Whistle felt sad because he could not eat the popcorn. He wanted to find a way to make the popcorn soft. Whistle had an idea. He went to find his friend, Rainy Cloud. Rainy Cloud could make water fall from the sky. Whistle asked Rainy Cloud to help make the popcorn soft. Rainy Cloud agreed and made it rain on the popcorn. The popcorn became soft and Whistle was happy. He ate the popcorn and sang a happy song.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Peter. Peter was a very curious child. He wanted to taste everything he saw. One day, while on a walk, he saw something red in a bush. He reached his hand inside and pulled out a cookie. "Yummy," he said. He took a bite of the delicious cookie. Its sweet taste made him smile. Suddenly, a voice boomed out, "Who are you and what are you doing?" Peter was startled and he quickly showed the cookie to the big man. "Ah, it's just a cookie," said the man. "You can have it, if you like." Peter thanked the man and he continued munching his red cookie. He couldn't believe his luck. It was the best tasting cookie ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Pinchy. Pinchy loved to pinch things with his tiny legs. He pinched leaves, sticks, and even the soft dirt. One day, Pinchy found a fragile root. He wanted to pinch it, but he was scared it might break. As he got closer, the root started to wiggle. Pinchy was surprised! He didn't know that roots could move. The wiggly root was not a root at all! It was a tiny snake named Twisty. Twisty and Pinchy became friends. They played together every day, and Pinchy learned not to pinch too hard.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the kitchen. She wanted to make cocoa. She was very quiet so her mom would not hear her. She knew she should ask for help, but she wanted to do it alone. Mia tried to make the cocoa, but she did not know how. She spilled the cocoa all over the floor. She felt sad and scared. She realized she needed help. So, she called her mom. Her mom came to the kitchen and saw the mess. She was not mad. She helped Mia clean up and showed her how to make cocoa the right way. Mia learned that it is okay to ask for help when you do not know how to do something.

Tom and Anna were playing in the park. It was cold, so they wore hats and scarves. Tom had a red scarf and Anna had a blue scarf. They saw a big slide and ran to it. Tom went first and slid down fast. He was happy. Anna followed him and slid down too. She was happy too. But when Anna reached the bottom, she saw that her scarf was gone. She looked up and saw that it was stuck on the slide. She was sad and scared. "Tom, help me! My scarf is up there!" she cried. Tom looked at the scarf and said, "No, I won't help you. That's your scarf, not mine. You should have held it tight. I want to slide again." He ran to the slide and climbed up. He was selfish and mean. Anna felt angry and hurt. She wanted to get her scarf back, but she was too small to reach it. She started to cry. A nice lady saw Anna crying and asked her what was wrong. Anna told her about her scarf and Tom. The lady felt sorry for Anna and said, "Don't worry, dear. I'll help you. Let me get your scarf for you." She walked to the slide and reached for the scarf. But as she did, she saw Tom sliding down. He was going too fast and did not see her. He bumped into her and made her drop the scarf. The scarf flew away in the wind. Tom and Anna both watched the scarf disappear. They were both sad and sorry. The lady said, "Oh, dear. I'm sorry, Anna. I tried to help you, but your friend was careless and rude. He should have listened to you and helped you. He should have shared the slide and waited his turn. He should have been kind and polite. He should have said sorry to me and to you." Tom felt ashamed and guilty. He realized that he was wrong and that he hurt Anna and the lady. He wished he could get the scarf back, but it was too late. He said, "I'm sorry, Anna. I'm sorry, lady. I was selfish and mean. I did not help you. I did not share. I did not wait. I did not say sorry. I was a bad friend. Please forgive me." Anna looked at Tom and said, "I'm sorry too, Tom. I'm sorry that I lost my scarf. I'm sorry that I was angry at you. I was a sad friend. Please forgive me too." The lady smiled and said, "It's okay, children. I forgive you both. You made a mistake, but you learned from it. You said sorry and you meant it. That's good. You can still be friends. But next time, remember to be kind and polite. Remember to help and share. Remember to wait and say sorry. That's how you show respect and love." Tom and Anna nodded and hugged each other. They thanked the lady and said goodbye. They went to play again, but this time, they were careful and nice. They learned their lesson. They learned the moral value.
