Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat. Her name was Ash. She lived in a small house with her best friend, a boy named Sam. They played all day and had lots of fun together. One day, Sam and Ash went for a walk in the park. They saw a big tree and decided to climb it. While they were up in the tree, they saw a small bird. The bird said, "Hi, my name is Blue. Do you want to play with me?" Sam and Ash said, "Yes, we would love to play with you!" As they played, something unexpected happened. Blue started to change colors! First, he turned red, then yellow, and then green. Sam, Ash, and Blue were all very surprised. They didn't know that Blue was a magic bird. From that day on, they had even more fun playing with their new magic friend.

Once there was a little girl called Anna. She liked to wear her bright yellow pants and poke everything she saw. She poked the trees, the rocks, and even the clouds! One day, while she was standing in the garden, she saw a little mouse poking its nose out of a hole. "Hi there!" said Anna, "What are you doing?". The mouse was so quiet that Anna could barely hear what it said. "I'm looking for cheese," it said after a few moments. Anna smiled and said, "Let me help you!". She poked around the garden until she found a little piece of cheese near a bush. She smiled and handed it to the mouse. The mouse was so happy it squeaked in delight. "Thank you, Anna" it said before scurrying off. Anna beamed and said, "Anytime!" and continued to poke around the garden. She had so much fun!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big event. Everyone was excited. A funny clown was there to make the kids laugh. The clown had a fake red nose. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the event. She saw the clown and was scared. "Mommy, I don't like the clown," she said. Her mommy told her, "Don't worry, it's just a fake nose. The clown is here to make us happy." Lily thought about it and decided to watch the clown. Soon, she was laughing too. Later, she told her mommy, "I don't regret coming to the event. The clown is funny!" And they both enjoyed the rest of the day.

One day, a little seed was in the ground. It wanted to grow big and strong. The sun was shining and the rain fell. The seed started to grow. It grew into a small plant. The plant was very happy. One day, a bird came to visit the plant. The bird said, "Do you want to go on a journey?" The plant was excited. "Yes, I want to go on a journey!" The bird told the plant about a distant place with big trees and pretty flowers. The plant wanted to go, but it could not move. The bird had an idea. It picked up the plant and carried it on its back. They went on a long journey to the distant place. The plant saw many new things. When they reached the distant place, the plant was amazed. It saw the big trees and pretty flowers. The plant was very happy. The bird put the plant down in the soft ground. The plant grew bigger and stronger in its new home. It made many new friends. The plant thanked the bird for taking it on the journey. They were friends forever.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. But they also liked to make up stories. They pretended to be pirates, astronauts and superheroes. One day, they saw a big tree with a hole in it. They wondered what was inside. Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's pretend we are explorers and find a treasure in the tree!" Lily agreed. They ran to the tree and looked inside. It was dark and spooky. They heard a weird noise. It sounded like a growl. They got scared. "Maybe there is a monster in the tree!" Lily said. "Or maybe it is a friendly animal that needs our help!" Tom said. They decided to be brave and find out. They took a stick and poked the hole. The noise got louder and angrier. Suddenly, something jumped out of the hole and shouted at them. It was a squirrel. It was not happy that they disturbed its home. It had a big bushy tail and sharp teeth. It chased them away from the tree. Tom and Lily ran as fast as they could. They screamed and laughed. They learned that some things are better left alone. They also learned that squirrels can shout.
