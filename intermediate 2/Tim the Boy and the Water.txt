Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Water

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big water spray. Tim was scared. He did not want to get wet. The water spray looked like fun, but he did not want to go near it. A little girl named Sue saw Tim. She said, "Hi, I am Sue. Do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, but I am scared of the water spray." Sue said, "Do not worry, I will help you." Sue took Tim's hand and they went near the water spray. Tim felt a little wet on his lip. He laughed and said, "That is not so bad!" They played near the water spray all day and had lots of fun. Tim was not scared anymore.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who had a silver toy that she loved, more than anything else. One day she went out to play, and she lost her silver toy. She was so sad, it was terrible. She looked all around, but she couldn't find the silver toy anywhere. She asked her friends and her family and searched high and low, but the silver toy was nowhere to be seen. Suddenly, the little girl saw a big dog carrying something in its mouth. When the dog came closer, she saw that it was her silver toy! The little girl was so excited. She clapped her hands and thanked the dog for finding her toy. The little girl was so happy to have her silver toy back. She never wanted to lose it again!

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a rare bird. He wanted to take an image of it. But the bird flew away. Tim was sad. He asked his friend, Sue, "Did you see the rare bird? I want an image of it." Sue said, "Yes, I saw it. Let's find it together." They walked and walked. They saw the bird in a tree. Tim and Sue both wanted to take an image of the bird. But they only had one camera. They did not know what to do. Tim said, "Let's share the camera. You take an image first." Sue took an image. Then Tim took an image. They were both happy. In the end, Tim and Sue both got an image of the rare bird. They learned that sharing is good. They walked home, happy and smiling.

One day, a young girl named Jenny was walking in the park. She stopped by a special bench and started to imagine. She wondered what it would be like if she could travel around the world. Suddenly, an old man wearing a bluetooth headset appeared to be talking to himself. He sat down at the bench next to her and opened a pen. He looked at Jenny and smiled. "I can imagine you can fly around the world," the old man said. Jenny stood there, with her mouth wide open. She imagined flying around the world with the old man. "It'd be so much fun," the old man said. Jenny nodded. She felt excited. "Yes, let's do it!" she said. The old man chuckled and nodded. "Go now, young one," he said. Jenny turned around and started to run. She imagined flying high above the clouds and feeling the wind in her hair. With the old man's advice, she knew she could make her dream come true.

Once upon a time, there was a cool theater in a small town. Many people went there to watch movies and have fun. In this theater, there were two friends named Tom and Sam. They loved going to the theater together. One day, Tom and Sam went to watch a movie. They were very excited. But during the movie, Tom started to yell. He was making a lot of noise. Sam told Tom to be quiet, but Tom did not listen. This made the other people in the theater very sad. After the movie, Sam told Tom that he should not yell in the theater. Tom felt bad for making the other people sad. He said sorry to Sam and promised to be quiet next time. The moral of the story is to be kind and think about how our actions can affect others.
