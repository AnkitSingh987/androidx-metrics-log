Category: Intermediate
Title: The Car of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because he was going to have a visit from his friend, Sam. Sam was coming to play with Tim and his toys. Tim's mom said, "You and Sam will have lots of fun!" When Sam came, they played with Tim's fancy toy car. They laughed and had a great time. Then, something unexpected happened. The toy car started to talk! It said, "Hello, Tim and Sam! I am a magic car. I can do tricks. Do you want to see?" Tim and Sam looked at each other, surprised. They said, "Yes, please show us!" The fancy toy car did flips and spins in the air. Tim and Sam clapped and cheered. They never knew that Tim's toy car was magic. From that day on, they had even more fun playing with the toy car. And they always remembered the day when the toy car surprised them with its magic tricks.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who lived near a dark forest. One day, she wanted to explore and stepped in. She was so scared that she stopped when she saw the shadows of tall trees. Suddenly, she saw something colorful in the distance and started walking towards it. As she went closer, she realized that it was a light! She was so excited. She reached out to try and save the light, but she couldn't reach it. She didn't want to give up, so she thought about what else she could do. After looking around, she saw some sticks and colorful rocks. The little girl built a bridge with the sticks and rocks and managed to save the light. The light started burning and it was even more colorful than before. She was very happy and started dancing around the light. It was like a magical moment! The little girl knew one thing for sure—she had saved something very special for the forest. From that day on, the little girl always remembered the light and promised to take care of it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a bit nervous because he had a big job to do. He had to bring coal to his mom. She needed the coal to make the house warm. Tom put the coal in a big bag and started to walk. On his way, he saw a big hill. He was scared, but he knew he had to go over the hill to get home. As he climbed the hill, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the bag with the coal away! Tom was so sad, but he didn't give up. He ran after the wind and found his bag in a big tree. He got the bag and went back to the hill. This time, he held the bag very tight. Tom made it over the hill and brought the coal to his mom. She was so happy, and the house was warm again. Tom was not nervous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball and run around the big park. One day, Spot found a shiny rock in the park. He picked it up in his mouth and took it home. Spot showed the shiny rock to his friend, Tim. Tim said, "Wow, Spot! That's a pretty rock! Let's polish it to make it even more shiny!" So, they got a soft cloth and rubbed the rock until it was very shiny. They were so happy with their shiny rock. Later, they went to the park again and met a new friend. The new friend asked, "What's your name?" Spot barked, "My name is Spot, and this is my friend Tim. We found a shiny rock and polished it!" The new friend smiled and said, "That's so cool! Can I play with you?" And they all played happily together in the park.

Sue and Jack were best friends. Every day, they liked to go to the store. At the store, Jack liked to buy things for his pet turtle. Sue always gave him a hug before going in. On this special day, Sue and Jack went to the store. This store was very flexible. They could get all sorts of things there. Sue had saved some coins to buy something special. When they got there, Sue saw a pretty toy. She wanted to give it to Jack to say thank you for always being so nice. Sue gave Jack the toy with a big smile. Jack was so happy and he gave her a big hug. They had so much fun in the store. Even though they didn't buy much, they had a great time. Sue and Jack were always there for each other.
