Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pair of Cats and the Friend

Once upon a time, there was a pair of cats. They were very close friends and loved to race each other. Every day they would compete to see who was the fastest. One day, one of the cats had to go to the vet for an operation. The other cat was very worried, but he waited patiently to see his friend again. Eventually the operation was over, but full of worry, the second cat raced to the vet to check on his friend. His heart sank when he arrived - his friend was dead. The second cat was very sad and he cried and cried. Nothing he did could bring his friend back. His racing days were over.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a kind and generous cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to share her toys and food with her friends. One day, Fluffy decided to have a big party at her house. She made invitations for all her friends and gave them out. On the day of the party, Fluffy felt a little sick. She had been sneezing all morning. She thought, "Maybe I should not have the party today." But she knew her friends were excited, so she decided to go on with the party. She cleaned her house and set up the food and toys for her friends. When her friends came to the party, they saw Fluffy sneezing. They knew she was not feeling well. They said, "Fluffy, you should rest. We can help you with the party." So, Fluffy's friends helped her set up the party and took care of her. They all had a great time at the party. In the end, Fluffy was happy she had such caring friends. She learned that when you are generous and kind to others, they will be there to help you when you need it. And that is the story of the generous cat named Fluffy and her big party.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a blue kite. The kite loved to play with the wind. The wind would blow and the kite would fly high in the sky. One day, the wind stopped blowing. The kite was sad and could not fly. The kite said to the wind, "Wind, please blow so I can fly high again." The wind did not want to blow. But the kite did not give up. The kite said, "Wind, I demand you to blow. I want to fly high and play with you." The wind listened to the kite and started to blow again. The blue kite flew high in the sky, happy and free. The kite and the wind played together all day long. And they were the best of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They liked to play and have fun. Tim was a good boy, but Spot was sometimes naughty. They walked to the park and saw a big tree. Under the tree, they found a bowl. Tim said, "Look, Spot! We received a bowl!" Spot barked and wagged his tail. They were very happy. Tim and Spot decided to take the bowl home and show it to their mom. When they got home, Tim showed the bowl to his mom. But the bowl started to shake and jump! Out of the bowl came a little frog. The frog said, "Thank you for finding my bowl!" Tim, Spot, and their mom were very surprised. The frog hopped away, and they all laughed.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little dog. They lived in the same house. They were very different. The cat was quiet and liked to sleep. The dog was loud and liked to play. One day, the cat and the dog had a quarrel. The cat wanted to sleep, but the dog wanted to play. They were both very upset. They did not want to be friends anymore. Then, their owner came and saw them fighting. She told them to be nice to each other and make a promise. The cat and the dog promised to be friends and not fight anymore. They learned that even if they were different, they could still be friends.
