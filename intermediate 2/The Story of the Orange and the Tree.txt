Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Orange and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She wanted to know more about the world around her. One day, she knelt down by a tree and held out her palm. The leaves of the tree tickled her hands. The little girl felt something drop onto her hand. She saw a delicious orange! She had never seen such a thing before. The little girl was very excited and tried to take a bite of the orange. Soon, she learned that she needed to peel it first. She carefully peeled the orange and ate it a piece at a time. The orange tasted so sweet and delicious. The little girl learned a valuable lesson that day. No matter how delicious something may be, it is important to always look closer before you act. And remember to kneel down to discover something new.

Lila liked to polish her star. It was a big star that hung on her wall. She got it from her grandma, who said it was very special. Lila used a soft cloth and made the star shine bright. One night, Lila heard a noise outside her window. She looked and saw a big bird. It had black feathers and a yellow beak. It looked angry and scary. It saw Lila's star and wanted to take it. It flew towards her window and tried to break it. Lila was scared and hugged her star. She wished the bird would go away. She felt the star get warm in her arms. It started to glow and make a sound. It was a powerful star. It sent a beam of light at the bird and made it fly away. The bird was scared and did not come back. Lila was happy and thanked her star. She said it was her friend and protector. She kissed it and put it back on her wall. She went to sleep and dreamed of the star. She felt safe and loved.

One day, a girl named Sue saw a vase on a high shelf. Sue wanted the vase to put pretty flowers in it. She tried to reach it, but she was too small. Sue felt sad and sat down. A nice cat came to Sue and said, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I want the vase, but I can't reach it." The cat said, "I can help you." The cat jumped up and pushed the vase down. But, the vase did not break. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, cat!" But then, Sue felt dizzy and fell down. The cat was worried and ran to get help. A big dog came and carried Sue to her bed. Sue woke up and saw the vase with flowers in it. She was happy and thanked the cat and the dog. They all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a daughter who loved to play with her toys. One day, Lily was feeling grumpy because her toys were dirty. She did not like dirty toys, so she decided to clean them. Lily took a soft cloth and started to wipe her toys. She wiped the toy car, the toy bear, and the toy blocks. As she wiped each toy, it became clean and shiny. Lily was happy that her toys were clean again. After wiping all her toys, Lily was not grumpy anymore. She played with her clean toys and had lots of fun. Her mommy saw her and smiled. Lily learned that if she wiped her toys, she could always have fun with them. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Sue's favorite part of the house was the fireplace. She liked to sit near the warm fire on cold days. One day, Sue felt uncomfortable. She was cold and wanted the fire to be bigger. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, I demand more fire!" Her mom smiled and said, "Okay, let's make the fire bigger." Sue and her mom put more wood on the fire. The fire got bigger and Sue felt warm and happy. She sat near the fireplace all day, feeling warm and cozy. Sue learned that when she asked for help, her mom was always there to make things better.
