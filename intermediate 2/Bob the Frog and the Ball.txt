Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Frog and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a jolly land, there lived a happy frog named Bob. Bob loved to play in the water all day. He would splash and soak in the pond with his friends. One day, a big, round ball fell into the pond. Bob swam to the ball and tried to lift it with his mouth. He wanted to give it back to the kids who were playing near the pond. But the ball was too big for his small mouth. Bob's friends saw him trying to lift the ball. They swam to Bob and helped him. Together, they pushed the ball out of the pond. The kids were so happy to have their ball back. They cheered for Bob and his friends. From that day on, Bob and his friends played with the kids, and they all had lots of fun.

One day, a white cat went to the barber. The barber was a nice man who cut hair. The cat sat on the chair and yawned. "I am sleepy," said the cat. The barber smiled and said, "Don't worry, cat. I will make you look nice and pretty." He started to cut the cat's hair. But then, something unexpected happened! The cat's hair turned into soft, white feathers! The cat was now a beautiful bird. The bird looked at the barber and said, "Thank you for making me so pretty!" The barber was surprised but happy. They both laughed, and the bird flew away, feeling happy and free.

---

One day, a fast cat named Speed saw an unusual cupboard. It was big and blue. Speed was very curious about the cupboard. Speed went to the cupboard and opened the door. Suddenly, he heard a noise coming from inside. He was a little scared, but he wanted to find out what was making the noise. Speed looked inside the cupboard and saw a small, round ball. The ball started to bounce and make funny noises. Speed was surprised and jumped back. Then, the ball turned into a friendly dog! They became best friends and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a rocket. It was a big and reliable rocket. It could fly very high in the sky. The rocket had a friend called Tim. Tim was a little boy who loved to play with the rocket. One day, Tim and the rocket went to the park to play. They saw a dog. The dog was running and playing with a ball. Tim said, "Hello, dog! Do you want to play with us?" The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, I want to play!" As they played, the dog saw the rocket and thought it was a toy. The dog tried to bite the rocket. Tim said, "No, dog! Don't bite the rocket. It is our friend." The dog stopped and said, "I'm sorry. I didn't know." They all played together happily and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends: Heather and David. They loved playing and laughing together. But one day, Heather wanted to watch a cartoon. She asked David if he wanted to join her, but he said no. Heather was so angry that she yelled, "You keep playing, but I'm watching the cartoon! You don't understand how warm it makes me feel!". David turned around and told her, "But cartoons can't keep you warm, Heather. I want to keep playing with you!" Heather hesitated and thought about what David said. After a moment, she replied, "Okay, let's keep playing - and maybe we can watch a cartoon later." So, with a smile, Heather and David kept playing until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly baby. The baby liked to laugh and play all day. The baby had a big toy that would remind him to be happy. One day, the baby went outside to play. He saw a big dog. The dog was not nice. The baby wanted to play with the dog, but the dog did not want to play. The baby got sad and tried to find his toy. But the toy was not there. The baby cried and cried. The bad dog ran away. The baby was not jolly anymore.
