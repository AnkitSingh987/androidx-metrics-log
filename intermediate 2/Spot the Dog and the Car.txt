Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with a ball all day. He would run and jump and catch the ball. One day, Spot met a boy named Tim. "Hi, my name is Tim. Let's play!" said Tim. Spot was happy and wagged his tail. Tim threw the ball and Spot would run and catch it. They played for hours. Tim would repeat, "Good dog, Spot!" But one day, while they were playing, the ball went into the street. Spot did not look for cars and ran to get the ball. A big car came fast and hit Spot. Tim was very sad and cried. Spot was not happy anymore and could not play with Tim again.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toys, but he also loved to help his family. One day, his family went on a trip, and they took Spot with them. They put all their bags in the car and buckled up. On the way, Spot looked at the bags and knew he would have to unpack them later. He was excited to help his family, so he wagged his tail and barked happily. His family smiled and knew that Spot would be a big help when they got to their new place. When they arrived, Spot jumped out of the car and ran to the bags. He used his clever nose to sniff out the toys and clothes. He helped his family unpack everything, and they were so happy. They knew that bringing Spot on the trip was a great idea. The family had a fun time on their trip, and Spot was the best helper ever.

One day, a bee named Buzz went out to find some food. Buzz was a flexible bee, he could fly high and low, fast and slow. He lived in a big hive with many other bees. The hive was their home, and they all worked together to make honey. As Buzz flew around, he met a butterfly named Flutter. Flutter was very pretty, with big wings that had many colors. Buzz said, "Hello, I am Buzz. I live in a hive with my bee friends. We make honey." Flutter smiled and said, "Hello, I am Flutter. I like to fly around and visit flowers." Buzz and Flutter decided to fly together and look for flowers. They found a big field with many flowers. They both got food from the flowers, and Buzz took some back to his hive. The other bees were happy to see Buzz and the food he brought. Buzz and Flutter became good friends, and they met every day to fly and find flowers. They had lots of fun and were always happy when they were together. And Buzz's hive had more honey than ever before because of his new friend, Flutter.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to help his mom cook. One day, his mom asked him to load the pan with some rich, tasty food. Tim was very happy to help. Tim put the rich food in the pan. He was very careful. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, funny dog ran into the kitchen. The dog jumped and knocked the pan out of Tim's hands. The rich food fell on the floor. Tim was sad, and the dog looked sorry. But then, Tim had a big idea. He picked up the pan and loaded it with more rich food. This time, he put the pan up high, where the dog could not reach it. Tim's mom was so proud of him. They all laughed and had a great dinner together. The dog was happy too, because Tim gave him a little treat.

Once upon a time, there was a little baby. The baby lived in a small house with his mom and dad. In the house, the baby had a crib. The crib was where the baby slept. One day, the baby smelled something bad. He looked around and saw a little fire. The fire was close to his crib. The baby started to cry. He was scared of the fire. His mom heard the baby cry and came running into the room. She saw the fire and said, "Oh no! The fire will burn the crib!" The mom quickly put out the fire with some water. The baby stopped crying and smiled at his mom. The mom hugged the baby and said, "Don't worry, my little one. The bad fire is gone now."
