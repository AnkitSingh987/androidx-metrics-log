Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was very independent. She liked to do things all by herself. One day, Amy wanted to cook something yummy. She put on her apron and went to the kitchen. Amy found a recipe for a cake. She took a teaspoon and started to mix the ingredients. She was having so much fun. She put the cake in the oven and waited. She was very excited to eat the cake. But something unexpected happened. When Amy opened the oven, there was no cake! Instead, there was a big, friendly bear. The bear had eaten the cake. Amy was surprised, but she was not scared. She laughed and said, "Silly bear, let's cook another cake together!" So, Amy and the bear cooked a new cake and became best friends.

One day, a little dog named Max was very thirsty. He wanted to drink some cool water. He walked to his bowl on the floor and saw that it was empty. Max was sad and didn't know what to do. Max saw his friend, a little bird named Sam. Max said, "Sam, I want to drink cool water, but my bowl is empty." Sam thought for a moment and said, "I know where we can find cool water. Follow me, Max!" They went to a big tree and saw a hole in the tree. Sam said, "Look, Max. There is cool water in the tree hole." Max was happy and started to drink the water. But, surprise! The water started to talk! It said, "Hello, Max and Sam. I am a magic water. If you drink me, you can talk to all the animals in the world." Max and Sam were very happy. They drank the magic water and could talk to all their animal friends. They had lots of fun and made many new friends.

One day, a cat named Tom wanted to do something fun. He saw a big shelf in the room. Tom thought it would be fun to climb the shelf. So, he started to climb very slow and careful. As Tom went up, he saw many things on the shelf. He saw a ball, a toy car, and a book. Tom liked the ball and played with it for a little bit. Then, he went higher on the shelf. When Tom got to the top of the shelf, he saw a big, soft bed. He was very tired from climbing slow and playing with the ball. So, Tom lay down on the bed and took a long nap. He was very happy he climbed the shelf.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a long cord in her room. She did not know what it was for, but she liked it. Lucy started to imagine all the fun things she could do with the cord. She thought about making it into a jump rope or a leash for her toy dog. Lucy took her cord outside to play. She saw her friend Tom from next door. Tom was sad because he lost his kite. The kite was stuck high up in a tree. Lucy wanted to help Tom feel better. Lucy had an idea! She tied the cord to a strong stick. Then, she threw the stick up into the tree. The stick hit the kite and it fell down. Tom was happy to have his kite back. Lucy was glad that her cord helped her friend. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big firework show in the town. All the kids were very excited to see the bright colors in the sky. Tim, a little boy with a generous heart, wanted to share his fireworks with his best friend, Ben. But Ben's mom said, "No, it's too late. Ben needs sleep." Tim was sad because he wanted to share the fun with his friend. Tim's mom saw her son's sad face and wanted to help. She said, "Why don't we suggest to Ben's mom that we can have a small firework show tomorrow during the day?" Tim liked the idea and went to talk to Ben's mom. She agreed, and the next day, they all had a great time watching the fireworks together.
