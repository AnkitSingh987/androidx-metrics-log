Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Turtle

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big brick on the ground. The brick was red and heavy. Tim felt an urge to pick up the brick. He wanted to see if there was something hidden under it. Tim picked up the brick and saw a little bug. The bug was trying to hide. Tim thought the bug was cute. He wanted to help the bug. So, Tim put the brick back down very gently. The bug was safe and hidden again. But then, something unexpected happened. The brick started to move! It was not a brick at all. It was a big turtle! The turtle said "thank you" to Tim for helping him. Tim was very surprised but happy. Now, Tim had a new friend, the turtle.

Once upon a time, a restless little girl named Lily lived with her mom and dad. They had a small garden where they grew yummy things to eat. One day, Lily's mom asked her to pick an onion from the garden. Lily went to the garden and saw a big onion. She tried to pull it out, but it was very hard. She pulled and pulled, but the onion didn't move. Lily sighed and said, "Oh, onion, please come out!" Just then, a tiny bug came by and heard Lily. The bug said, "Don't be sad. I can help you pull the onion out!" So, Lily and the tiny bug pulled and pulled together. This time, the onion came out! Lily thanked the tiny bug and took the onion to her mom. Her mom was very happy and they cooked a tasty dinner with it. From that day on, Lily and the tiny bug became best friends and always helped each other.

Jazz is a regular cat. He likes to sleep, eat, and play with his toys. He does not like to be poked. But Sam, the little boy who lives with him, likes to poke him a lot. He thinks it is fun to see Jazz jump and hiss. One day, Jazz is sleeping on the sofa. He is dreaming of fish and cream. He feels a poke on his tail. He opens one eye and sees Sam. Sam is smiling and poking him again. Jazz is not happy. He growls and swats at Sam with his paw. "Ow!" Sam says. "Jazz, why are you mean? I just want to play with you." Jazz does not want to play. He wants to sleep. He jumps off the sofa and runs to his bed. He hopes Sam will leave him alone. But Sam follows him. He finds Jazz under the blanket. He pokes him again. Jazz is very angry. He comes out of the blanket and scratches Sam on the nose. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Sam cries. He runs to his mom. He has a red mark on his nose. He tells his mom what happened. "Sam, you should not poke Jazz. He does not like it. It hurts him and makes him scared. You should be gentle and kind to him. Then he will be your friend." Mom says. Sam feels sorry. He says sorry to Jazz. He gives him a hug and a treat. Jazz purrs and licks his hand. He decides to forgive Sam. He thinks maybe they can play later. But not with poking.

Jack wanted a new cane. So he went to the shop. It was full of canes of all different shapes and sizes. But the one he really wanted was yellow. So he looked and looked, until finally he found it! It was exactly what he wanted - a yellow cane. Jack was so excited he couldn't contain himself. He grabbed it and hurried back home. All the way, Jack held his cane tightly. When he got home, Jack put his new cane away carefully and played with it all day. No matter what he did, his yellow cane followed him around. Everywhere Jack went, the cane went too. Jack and his yellow cane were the best of friends. Together they did everything!

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to spend time in the yard playing with her ball. One day, she saw a big box in the yard. Kitty was very curious about what was inside. Kitty's friend, Doggy, came over and said, "I heard there is a surprise in the box!" They both looked at the box and wondered what could be inside. They decided to wait until the next day to open it. The next day, Kitty and Doggy went to the yard to open the big box. They were so excited to see what was inside. When they opened it, they found a big bouncy house! They laughed and played in the bouncy house all day long. The surprise was even better than they had imagined.
