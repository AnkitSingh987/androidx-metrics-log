Title: Lila and the Rock

Once upon a time there was a rich baby girl called Lila who had a favourite toy. It was a big, yellow square that she liked to rock and zigzag around all day. One day, when she was having a nice time playing, a little boy with a grumpy face crept up on her. He wanted to take her favourite toy! She shouted out to him, "No, that's mine! Please don't take it!" But the little boy didn't listen. He grabbed the toy and tried to run off with it. Lila cried and ran after him. He was too fast, and Lila couldn't catch up. The little boy was gone and so was her toy. Poor Lila! Everyone tried to comfort her, but nothing made her feel better. Her heart felt heavy and she was very sad. She realised she had to let go of her favourite toy. But she would never forget the naughty little boy and his zigzag run.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Sam. Sam was only 3 years old and he loved to ride his scooter. One day, after playing on his scooter for a long time, Sam needed to take a bath. But Sam didn't want to take a bath. He wanted to keep playing on his scooter. Sam's mommy told him he had to take a bath, but Sam got very angry. He stamped his foot and shouted, "NO! I will not take a bath!" But his mommy said that he needed to take a bath and that he couldn't keep playing on his scooter until he did. Sam was so angry that he started to cry. He asked his mommy to let him keep playing, but she said he had to take a bath first. Finally, Sam had to do what his mommy said. He took his bath, but he was still very angry. The moral of the story is that it's important to listen to your parents. They are usually right and it's important to respect them, even though it might feel hard sometimes.

One day, a team of kids played a game. They wanted to play fair. They had fun and laughed a lot. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. They played with a big, round ball. A girl named Lily wanted to record the game. She had a small box that could make pictures move. She said, "I will record the game so we can watch it later." The other kids were happy and said, "Yes, please do it!" But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and took the ball. The kids were sad and said, "Oh no, our ball is gone!" But the bird heard them and brought the ball back. The kids were happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" They all played together and had more fun than before.

One sunny day, a chubby cat named Max went for a walk. Max loved to play outside. He saw a big tree and decided to climb it. Max looked around and saw a big pool. He wanted to dive into the pool to cool off. Before Max could dive, a little girl named Lily came by. She was holding an ice-cream. Lily said, "Hi Max! Do you want some ice-cream?" Max looked at the ice-cream and nodded his head. Lily gave Max some ice-cream and they both enjoyed the cold treat. After eating the ice-cream, Max and Lily played by the pool. Max looked at the water and said, "I want to dive in!" Lily smiled and said, "Let's dive together!" They both took a big jump and dove into the pool. Max and Lily splashed and laughed, having a fun day in the sun.

One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. It was cold, so she wore a big coat. Lily saw her friend, a small cat named Mimi. Mimi had a cold nose. Lily asked Mimi, "Do you want a hug to feel warm?" Mimi said, "Yes, please!" So, Lily gave Mimi a big hug. Mimi's nose felt warm after the hug. They played all day in the cold. They were happy and had fun. At the end of the day, Lily and Mimi hugged each other again. They went inside to get warm and eat yummy food.
