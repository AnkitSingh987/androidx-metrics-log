Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Park and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to wrap things up. She would wrap her dolls up in blankets and also wrap things up in colorful papers and ribbons. One day, the little girl was walking in the park when she saw an icy pear lying on the ground. She was very curious and wanted to wrap it up. So, she knelt down and carefully picked up the icy pear. She found some bright cloth in her bag and carefully wrapped it up. With a satisfied smile, she looked around and ran back home to show it to her family. When she arrived home, her mom and dad were very surprised to see what she had. Her dad said, smiling, "What a nice icy pear you have there! Would you like to share it with us?" The little girl giggled and smiled back at her dad. She wrapped the icy pear in some more cloth and handed it over to her dad. Everyone was so happy and enjoyed the icy pear together. The little girl was very happy to share her special gift with her family. From then on, she continued to wrap up all sorts of things and never stopped being curious.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toy car. She would push the car around her room and make car sounds. One day, Lily saw a big cat playing outside. She had an idea. "I will give my car to the cat," she said. Lily went outside and showed her car to the cat. The cat liked the car and started to play with it. Lily was happy to see the cat having fun. But then, the cat took the car in its mouth and ran away. Lily was sad and didn't know what to do. Lily's mom saw her crying and asked, "What's wrong, Lily?" Lily told her mom about the cat and the car. Her mom said, "Don't worry, we will find the cat and get your car back." They searched for the cat and found it in a tree. Lily's mom told the cat, "Please give back the car." The cat seemed to understand and surrendered the car. Lily was happy to have her car back and thanked her mom. From that day on, Lily learned to be careful when sharing her toys with others.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a big toy trunk in his room. One day, Tom was playing with his toys when he heard a tiny voice. It came from a little toy bear in the trunk. "Please, don't leave me in the trunk," said the toy bear. "I feel hurt when I remain in the dark." Tom was surprised but he wanted to help his toy bear. "Okay, Mr. Bear, I will take you out of the trunk," said Tom. He picked up the bear and put it on his bed. The bear felt happy and thanked Tom. From that day on, Tom made sure that all his toys were out of the trunk when he was done playing. He did not want any of them to feel hurt or sad. They all remained on his bed, and they had a lot of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little bean who loved to swim. Bean lived near a big pond. Every day, Bean would go to the pond and swim with the fish and ducks. One day, the sky got dark and it rained a lot. The pond got very full and heavy. Bean was scared, but still wanted to swim. Bean put on a brave face and jumped into the heavy water. Bean swam and swam in the heavy water. The fish and ducks were happy to see Bean. They all played and splashed together. Bean felt brave and strong. And from that day on, Bean was never scared to swim in the heavy water again.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends. One sunny day, Spot and his friends went to the park to play. As they played, they found a big tap. Spot's friend, Kitty, said, "Let's play with the water!" So, they turned the tap and water came out. They laughed and splashed in the water. They had so much fun, they didn't see the water scatter all over the ground. A wise old owl saw the mess they made. He flew down and said, "Oh no, look at the water everywhere! We must clean it up." Spot and his friends felt bad. They all helped to clean the water. The wise old owl said, "Thank you for helping. Remember, it's good to have fun, but always clean up after yourself." From that day on, Spot and his friends always made sure to clean up after they played. They learned that being responsible was important. And they all lived happily ever after.
