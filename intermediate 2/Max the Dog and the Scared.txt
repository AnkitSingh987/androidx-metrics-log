Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Scared

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a fearful dog, always scared of big things. One day, Max was playing in the yard when he saw a big, scary shadow. He was very scared, but he wanted to be brave and help his family. Max followed the shadow to the kitchen. The shadow was coming from a big steak on the table. Max was still scared, but he knew he had to help. He jumped up and grabbed the steak, pulling it down from the table. Max ran outside with the steak, thinking he was helping his family. When his family saw what he did, they laughed and clapped. They were not scared of the shadow or the steak. Max felt happy that he helped his family, and he was not fearful anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Joe. He wanted to have a yummy snack, but he didn't know what to make. Joe asked his mom, "What can I bake?" His mom smiled and said, "How about a nice surprise from the forest?" Joe was so excited! He raced out the door and went to the forest. He found some yummy berries, nuts and mushrooms. He picked them all up and brought them home. Joe was so excited, he decided to bake a tasty snack right away. He mixed all the ingredients together, popped them in the oven and waited. The kitchen smelled yummy! When the snack was ready, Joe was so excited! He enjoyed his yummy snack and was very happy.

One day, a pale bunny named Bob went to the store. He wanted to buy some salad to eat. He hopped happily to the store, excited to get his favorite food. At the store, Bob found a big, green salad. He put the salad in his cart. Then, he went to pay for it. The nice lady at the store helped him pay. Bob was so happy to have his salad. Bob went home and wanted to eat his salad. He had to load it on a plate first. He put the salad on a big, round plate. Then, he sat down and ate the salad. It was so yummy! Bob was very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tom and a girl named Sue. They liked to play together every day. One day, they were playing near a delicate flower in the park. Tom and Sue heard a shout from far away. They were scared, but they wanted to see what was going on. They walked to the sound and saw a big party. It was a marriage party! They saw a man and a woman holding hands, and many people were clapping and smiling. Tom and Sue thought the man and the woman were just friends. But then, they saw them kiss! They were very surprised! They did not know that friends could get married. Tom and Sue laughed and clapped with the other people at the marriage party. They were happy to learn something new and to be part of the fun.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a kite. They run and laugh and have fun. One day, a fat boy comes to the park. He has no toys. He looks at Lily and Ben. He wants to join them. He says, "Can I play with you?" Lily and Ben are nice. They say, "Yes, you can play with us. We have a ball and a kite. You can share with us." The fat boy is happy. He takes the ball and throws it. He takes the kite and flies it. He does not share with Lily and Ben. He does not let them play. He says, "This is my ball. This is my kite. Go away!" Lily and Ben are sad. They want their toys back. They say, "Please give us our toys. They are ours. You are not nice. You are mean." The fat boy is angry. He does not listen to Lily and Ben. He keeps the toys. He says, "No, they are mine. You go away!" Lily and Ben are scared. They run away from the fat boy. They cry and look for help. They do not play in the park anymore. The fat boy is alone. He has the ball and the kite. But he has no friends. He is bored and lonely. He does not have fun. The end.
