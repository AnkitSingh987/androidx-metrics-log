Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pink Cushion and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a pink cushion. It was soft and pretty. The pink cushion lived in a big house with a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came to play. They played with toys and had fun. Then, Tim saw the pink cushion. He loved it and wanted to take it home. Tim said, "Can I have this pink cushion? It's so nice!" Lily thought for a moment. She said, "No, Tim. This pink cushion belongs to me. My mommy gave it to me. But we can share it while you're here!" Tim agreed, and they played with the pink cushion together. After a fun day, Tim went home. Lily hugged her pink cushion. She was happy to have her cushion and a good friend like Tim. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little worm named Wiggly. Wiggly lived underground in a small hole. He liked to keep his home clean and cozy. One day, Wiggly heard a sound from outside his hole. He peeked out to see what it was. "Hello!" said a tiny ant named Andy. "Do you want to play with me?" Wiggly was shy, so he thought about it for a moment. Then, he said, "Okay, I will play with you!" They started to play a game of tag. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. A big raindrop fell from the sky and made a hole in the ground. Wiggly and Andy were surprised! The hole was big enough for them to go inside. "Wow, look at this!" said Andy. "It's a new underground place for us to play!" Wiggly was happy too. Now, they had a new place to keep playing and have fun together. From that day on, Wiggly wasn't shy anymore, and he and Andy became the best of friends.

One day, Tom and his dog, Spot, went out to play in the backyard. They liked to jog and run around the big tree. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a happy day for Tom and Spot. But then, Spot saw a little bird on the ground. The bird looked worried and scared. Spot stopped jogging and went to see the bird. Tom came too. "What's wrong, little bird?" Tom asked. "I can't find my mom," said the little bird with a sad voice. Tom and Spot wanted to help. They looked all around the backyard. Finally, they found the bird's mom near the fence. The little bird was so happy to see her mom again. Tom and Spot felt good for helping the little bird. They learned that it is important to help others when they are worried or scared. From that day on, they always tried to help their friends in need.

Once upon a time there was a boy. The boy was nice and fine. He loved to run and play. The boy ran around the park, playing tag with his friends. He ran so fast that the wind blew through his hair. The boy ran past some trees, and saw a squirrel. He stopped and watched it for a few moments before continuing his running fun. The boy ran faster and faster, until he was out of breath. He stopped for a few minutes to catch his breath, then he continued to run around the park again. The boy ran until it was dark outside, then he stopped and went home. He was so tired from all his running, but he was still feeling fine. And that is the story of the boy who liked to run!

Tom and Lily were playing in the bathroom. They liked to splash water and make bubbles with the soap. But they had to be careful not to make a mess or their mom would be angry. "Look, Lily, I can display the soap on the shelf like this," Tom said, holding a pink bar of soap. He put it next to a green one and a blue one. "They look like pretty flowers." "That's nice, Tom, but I want to play with the soap too," Lily said, reaching for the pink soap. "Give it to me, please." "No, Lily, it's mine. I found it first," Tom said, pulling the soap away from Lily. "You can have the green one or the blue one." "But I like the pink one. It smells like strawberries," Lily said, trying to grab the soap from Tom. "It's not fair. You always get what you want." Tom and Lily started to tug and pull on the soap. They did not notice that the soap was slippery and wet. They did not notice that the shelf was shaky and loose. They did not notice that their mom was coming to check on them. Suddenly, the soap slipped out of their hands and fell on the floor. The shelf wobbled and tipped over. The other soaps and bottles and towels crashed down with a loud noise. Water and bubbles spilled everywhere. Tom and Lily gasped and looked at the mess. They heard their mom's footsteps and voice. "What's going on in here? What have you done?" their mom said, opening the door. She saw the soap and the shelf and the water and the bubbles. She saw Tom and Lily's guilty faces. She was very angry. "Tom and Lily, you are in big trouble. You know you are not supposed to play with the soap and make a mess in the bathroom. You could have hurt yourselves or broken something. How many times have I told you to be careful and listen to me?" their mom said, scolding them. Tom and Lily felt sorry and scared. They knew they had done something bad. They wished they had shared the soap and played nicely. They wished they had not made their mom angry. They said in a small voice, "We're sorry, mom. We won't do it again."
