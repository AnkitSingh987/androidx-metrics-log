Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Bossy Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to tell all the animals what to do. One day, Fluffy saw a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was eating a grape. Fluffy wanted the grape, so she said, "Give me that grape, Chirpy!" Chirpy was a kind bird, so he gave the grape to Fluffy. But Chirpy had an idea. "Let's play a game," he said. "If I can make you clap, you have to be nice and not bossy." Fluffy agreed to play the game. Chirpy started to sing a fun song. Fluffy tried not to clap, but the song was so good that she clapped her paws. Chirpy smiled and said, "You clapped! Now you have to be nice." Fluffy agreed and from that day on, she was a nice and friendly cat. All the animals were happy and they all played together.

Anna and Ben were very happy. Today was a special day. They were going to celebrate with a parade. They put on their red and blue clothes and their hats. They took their flags and their horns. They walked with their mom and dad to the big street. There were many people and cars. They saw balloons and flowers and banners. They heard music and drums and cheers. They waved their flags and blew their horns. They smiled and shouted. "Look, Anna, look!" Ben said. "There are the horses and the clowns and the fire trucks. They are in the parade. They are so cool!" Anna nodded. She liked the parade too. She saw a big elephant and a lion and a monkey. They were in a cage on a truck. They looked funny and scary. She saw a man with a thick beard and a long coat. He was a magician. He made a rabbit appear from his hat. "Wow, Ben, wow!" Anna said. "There are the animals and the magician and the candy. They are in the parade. They are so amazing!" They clapped and laughed. They enjoyed the parade. They had a lot of fun. They were very happy. Today was a special day. They celebrated with a parade.

One day, a lively cat named Tim saw a big parade. He liked the colors and the music. He wanted to join in and have fun too. Tim's friend, a dog named Sam, saw him watching the parade. "Want to join the parade, Tim?" Sam asked. "Yes, I want to zoom with them!" Tim said. Sam had an idea. He told Tim to wait and ran away. Tim did not know what Sam was doing. Soon, Sam came back with a little red car. He gave it to Tim. "Now you can zoom in the parade too!" Sam said. Tim was so happy! He got in the car and started to zoom with the parade. All the people clapped and cheered for Tim and Sam. They had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved the color pink. She had a pink dress, pink shoes, and a pink hat. She was always happy and kind to her friends. One day, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he could not find a match for his blue sock. Lily wanted to help him. She said, "Let's look for your sock's match together!" Tim smiled and they started to search. They looked high and low, and finally, they found the match for Tim's blue sock. Tim was so happy, and he said, "Thank you, Lily! You are a good friend." Lily felt happy too because she helped Tim. The moral of the story is that helping others can make us feel good and happy.

Mother was famous for being an amazing cook. Every morning she would wake up early and cook delicious things. One morning, her little three-year-old boy watched as she was cooking. He asked his mother, "What are you making?" "Something special," she said. The boy's eyes lit up. He loved when his mother cooked special things. Mother took out a bowl and started to mix things together. She added lots of spices and stirred quickly. The smell was so yummy. The boy couldn't wait to try the special dish his mother had made. When it was ready, he took a big bite and said, "Yum, this is the best thing you've ever cooked!" Mother smiled and said, "I'm so glad you like it. That's why I'm famous for my cooking!"
