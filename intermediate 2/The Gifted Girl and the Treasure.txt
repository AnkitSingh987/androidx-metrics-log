Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gifted Girl and the Treasure

Once upon a time there lived a gifted little girl who was very brave and clever. She was always looking for new adventures and one day she stumbled upon a beautiful cliff. The cliff stood tall and proud, with a clear view of the sea. She wanted to stay longer and explore, but she couldn't find a way down. The little girl talked to her friend, a bird, and asked him to help her down. The bird agreed to help and flew down the cliff, telling her that he had found a secret pathway. The little girl was so excited, she couldn't believe her luck. She carefully made her way down, and to her surprise and delight she found a treasure chest at the bottom. She opened the chest and found some shiny gems inside. Suddenly, she heard a loud voice behind her. "Those gems are mine! Give them back!" a man shouted. The little girl knew he was trying to steal her treasure and she quickly closed the chest. She ran away as fast as she could, leaving the thief behind. The little girl was very brave and gifted. She managed to outwit the thief, and kept her treasure safe. She shared her gems with her friends and they all lived happily ever after.

Tommy liked yellow. He liked yellow cars, yellow bananas, yellow suns. He had a yellow shirt that he wanted to wear every day. But his mom said no. She said he had to wear different shirts on different days. She said yellow was for Tuesday. "Why, mom?" Tommy asked. "Why only Tuesday?" "Because, Tommy, that's the rule. You have to share your shirts with the other kids. They like yellow too. And you can wear other colors. They are nice too. Red, blue, green, purple. You can try them all." Tommy did not like other colors. He only liked yellow. He did not want to share his shirt. He did not want to try other colors. He was sad and mad. He did not want to listen to his mom. On Monday, he hid his yellow shirt under his bed. He put on a red shirt, but he did not like it. He hoped his mom would not see his yellow shirt. He went to school, but he did not have fun. He did not play with the other kids. He did not smile. He did not learn. He only thought about his yellow shirt. On Tuesday, he was happy. He took out his yellow shirt and put it on. He smiled. He felt good. He went to school, and he had fun. He played with the other kids. He smiled. He learned. He liked Tuesday. But his mom saw his yellow shirt. She was not happy. She knew he hid it under his bed. She was sad and mad. She did not like that he lied to her. She did not like that he did not share. She did not like that he did not try other colors. "Tommy, why did you hide your yellow shirt?" she asked. "Why did you lie to me? Why did you not share? Why did you not try other colors?" Tommy did not know what to say. He was sorry. He did not want to make his mom sad and mad. He did not want to lie. He did not want to be selfish. He did not want to be boring. He just liked yellow. "Mom, I'm sorry. I like yellow. It makes me happy. Can I wear it every day?" "No, Tommy, you can't. You have to wear different shirts on different days. That's the rule. You have to share your shirts with the other kids. They like yellow too. And you have to try other colors. They are nice too. Red, blue, green, purple. You can learn to like them too." "Please, mom, please. Can I wear yellow on Tuesday? Just on Tuesday?" Mom thought for a moment. She loved Tommy. She wanted him to be happy. She wanted him to learn. She wanted him to grow. She knew he liked yellow. She knew it was hard to change. She decided to allow him to wear yellow on Tuesday. But only on Tuesday. "Okay, Tommy, okay. You can wear yellow on Tuesday. But only on Tuesday. And you have to wear different shirts on other days. And you have to share your shirts with the other kids. And you have to try other colors. Do you understand?" "Yes, mom, yes. Thank you, mom, thank you. I understand. I will wear different shirts on other days. I will share my shirts with the other kids. I will try other colors. But I will wear yellow on Tuesday. I love yellow. And I love you." "I love you too, Tommy. And I love yellow too. But I also love red, blue, green, purple. And I love you in any color."
