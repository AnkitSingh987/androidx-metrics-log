Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drum

One day, a boy named Tim found an amazing drum in his room. He was very happy. He set the drum on the floor and started to play. Tim loved the sound it made. He played all day and all night. The next day, Tim took the drum to the park. He set the drum on the grass and played for his friends. His friends liked the drum too. They clapped and danced to the beat. Then, a talking cat came to the park. The cat said, "I like your drum, can I play too?" Tim was surprised but he said, "Yes, you can play my drum." The cat played the drum and made an even more amazing sound. All the kids at the park were amazed. They never knew a cat could play a drum so well. From that day on, Tim, his friends, and the cat played the drum together, and they all had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big swamp. In the swamp, there was a frog named Tim. Tim liked to sit on a comfortable rock and watch the sun go down. One day, Tim saw a little bug. The bug was cold. Tim wanted to help the bug. He found a soft leaf and used it to wrap the bug. The bug felt warm and happy. From that day on, Tim and the bug were best friends. They played in the swamp and had fun. Tim's comfortable rock became their special place to watch the sun go down together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a fat cat and a little dog were in the park. The fat cat saw a big cake with a lot of cream. The cat wanted to eat the cake. The little dog wanted to eat the cake too. They both said, "I want the cake!" The fat cat had an idea. She told the little dog, "Let's send a bug to get the cake. The bug can put the cake in his mouth and bring it to us." The little dog liked the idea. They found a bug and told him their plan. The bug said, "Okay, I will help you." The bug went to the cake and put it in his mouth. But then, something unexpected happened. The bug became very big and fat! He ate the whole cake by himself. The fat cat and the little dog were sad. The bug said, "I am sorry, the cake was so good, I could not stop eating it." The fat cat and the little dog forgave the bug, and they all played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Buddy. Buddy was very competitive. He loved to race with his friends. One day, Buddy went to the park to play with his friends. Buddy saw his friend, Timmy the Truck. "Let's race!" said Buddy. Timmy agreed, and they started to race. Buddy was fast, but Timmy was faster. Buddy wanted to keep going, but he felt tired. Buddy's dad told him, "You need oil to keep going." So, Buddy went to the car doctor. The car doctor gave him oil. Buddy felt better and went back to the park. Buddy found Timmy and said, "Let's race again!" They raced, and this time, Buddy won. Buddy was happy that he could keep playing with his friends. From that day on, Buddy always remembered to get oil to stay strong and competitive.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a pretty veil that she loved to wear. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Amy met a boy named Tim. Tim was rude and not nice. He said, "I want your veil!" Amy said, "No, it is mine!" She wanted to protect her veil from Tim. They both got mad at each other. In the end, Tim took Amy's veil and ran away. Amy was very sad and cried. She could not protect her veil and lost it to the rude boy. The day ended badly for Amy.

Once upon a time there was a ugly zipper. The zipper was so ugly that no one ever wanted to use it. Then one day, it started to glow. The glow was so pretty that everyone wanted to come see it. The glow was so bright that it lit up the whole town. People kept coming and looking at the zipper in amazement. Everyone was so amazed that they started giving the zipper names, like "magical zipper" or "golden zipper". The zipper glowed brighter and brighter each day until one day it stopped glowing. But the people will always remember the beautiful zipper that made them happy. And the town kept its special name, "The Town of Glowing Zipper".
