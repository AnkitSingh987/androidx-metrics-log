Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Puddle

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. Today it is sunny, but yesterday it rained. They see many puddles on the grass. "Look, Ben, a big puddle!" Anna says. She runs to the puddle and jumps in. Splash! Water goes everywhere. Anna laughs. "Me too, me too!" Ben says. He runs after Anna and jumps in the puddle. Splash! More water goes everywhere. Ben laughs. They jump and splash and laugh in the puddle. They make the water go high and low. They get wet and dirty, but they do not care. They are having fun. "Anna, Ben, time to go home!" Anna's mom calls. She sees them in the puddle. She shakes her head, but she smiles. She knows they are happy. "OK, mom!" Anna says. She holds Ben's hand. They walk to Anna's mom. They are dripping and muddy, but they smile. They had a good day.

Once there was a bin. It was very attractive. Everyone wanted to get close to it. One day a little girl decided to attach something to it. She found the perfect item. It looked like a star and was very bright. Then the little girl's friends noticed and gathered around. All of them wanted to attach things too. They each found something special and went around the bin adding it. They chose things like colorful feathers, small rocks and lots of smiley faces. When they were finished, the bin was beautiful and sparkly. Everyone was so proud of what they had created. The bin was now much more attractive and everyone was happy.

Once there was a little girl called Susan. She was three years old and loved to explore. One day she decided to explore the desert. But it was too hot and she was very thirsty. Susan began to cry because she didn't know what to do. Then, a little bird called Bip came and said, “Don’t cry! I can help you. Follow me!” So Susan followed Bip through the desert until they came to an oasis. It was a magical place with lots of refreshing water. Susan was so happy and drank lots of water. She thanked Bip for helping her! But not all the creatures in the desert were as friendly as Bip. When the sun went down, the desert became scary and dangerous. Luckily, with Bip’s help, Susan found a safe place to stay the night. In the morning, Susan was so relieved that she was safe and sound. She hugged Bip and thanked him again. With Bip's help, she had survived a terrible night in the desert.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play at the park. One day, they see a big truck at the park. The truck has many toys and prizes. Tom and Mia want to get a prize. They run to the truck and see a man. The man says, "Hello, kids. Do you want to play a game? If you can grab a ball from this box, you can get a prize." Tom and Mia nod. They want to play. The man gives them each a long stick. He says, "You can use the stick to grab a ball. But be careful, the balls are slippery and bouncy. You have to be quick and smart." Tom and Mia try to grab a ball with their sticks. They poke and push and swing the sticks. But the balls are too hard to grab. They keep falling and rolling away. Tom and Mia get frustrated. They want a prize. Then, Mia has an idea. She says to Tom, "Let's work together. You hold one end of the stick, and I hold the other end. We can make a long hook with the stick. Then, we can grab a ball together." Tom likes Mia's idea. They join their sticks and make a long hook. They look for a ball that is close to the edge of the box. They slide the hook under the ball and lift it up. They grab the ball! The man claps and smiles. He says, "Well done, kids. You are very clever and cooperative. You can choose a prize from the truck." Tom and Mia are happy. They thank the man and choose a prize. Tom picks a red car and Mia picks a yellow doll. They hug their prizes and run to the slide. They have fun at the park.
