Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day under the warm sun. He had a friend, a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to play with Tom, but sometimes Tom was too lazy to play. One day, Sally found a shiny gold rock near the river. She was very happy and wanted to show it to Tom. Sally ran to Tom and waved the gold rock in front of him. Tom opened his eyes and saw the gold rock. He liked it very much and wanted to play with it too. Sally and Tom played with the gold rock all day. They waved it in the air and watched it shine in the sun. They even put it in the river and watched it sparkle in the water. At the end of the day, Sally and Tom were very tired but happy. They had a lot of fun playing with the gold rock, and Tom wasn't lazy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play on the beach. One day, he found a big shell. He was very happy. Tim wanted to put the shell in his room. He took it home and put it on his table. He thought it was very pretty. Tim also found some delicious fruit on the beach. He took the fruit home to share with his family. The next day, Tim went back to the beach. He saw the tide coming in. The water went up and down, up and down. Tim played in the water and had lots of fun. He couldn't wait to find more treasures on the beach.

Sara and Tom like to go to the library with Mom. They can find many books with pictures and stories. Mom says they can borrow some books and take them home. But they have to be quiet and careful in the library. Sara and Tom try to be quiet and careful, but they are restless. They want to run and play and talk. They look at the books, but they get bored. They whisper to each other, but they are too loud. The librarian says, "Shh, this is a quiet place. Please be respectful of others." Sara and Tom say, "Sorry, librarian." But they are still restless. They see a big shelf with many books. They want to climb it and see what is on top. They start to climb, but the books fall down. They make a big noise and a big mess. The librarian says, "Oh no, what are you doing? Stop, this is dangerous. You could get hurt or break the books." Sara and Tom say, "Sorry, librarian." But they are scared and sad. They don't want to get hurt or break the books. They don't want to make the librarian angry. They want to go home. Mom comes and sees the mess. She says, "Sara and Tom, what happened? You have to be careful and respectful in the library. You have to keep the books safe and clean. You have to keep the library quiet and peaceful. You have to keep the librarian happy and friendly." Sara and Tom say, "Sorry, Mom. Sorry, librarian. We were restless. We wanted to have fun. We didn't mean to make trouble." Mom says, "I understand, but you have to follow the rules in the library. The library is a special place where you can learn and enjoy, but you have to be responsible and polite. Next time, we will choose some books that you like and read them together. Then you can have fun and learn at the same time. OK?" Sara and Tom say, "OK, Mom. OK, librarian. Thank you for letting us borrow the books. We will keep them safe and clean. We will keep the library quiet and peaceful. We will keep the librarian happy and friendly." Mom says, "Good. Now let's go home and read the books. Maybe you will find some stories that will make you less restless and more curious." Sara and Tom say, "Yes, Mom. We love the library. We love the books. We love you." Mom says, "I love you too. Let's go." They hug and leave the library. The librarian smiles and waves. She says, "Bye, Sara and Tom. Come back soon. I hope you enjoy the books." Sara and Tom say, "Bye, librarian. We will. Thank you." They smile and wave. They are happy and calm. They are ready to read and learn.
