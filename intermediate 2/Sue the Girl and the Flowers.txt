Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Flowers

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk with her dog, Max. Sue put a leash on Max to keep him close. They saw many interesting things on their walk, like big trees and pretty flowers. Sue saw her friend, Tom, and they talked. Sue said, "Look at my dog, Max. He has a leash!" Tom said, "That is interesting! I want a leash for my dog too." Sue and Tom walked together, but they didn't know something unexpected was about to happen. As they walked, a big wind came and blew the leash out of Sue's hand! Max ran after a butterfly, but Sue and Tom were scared. They said, "Thank you, wind, for showing us that we need to hold the leash tight!" They caught Max and held the leash tight for the rest of their walk.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl. Her name was Maggie and she was three. One day, Maggie went to the park to play. At the park, she saw an incredible thing. There were many birds in the sky, singing words she could never imagine. Maggie was amazed. She wanted to tell everyone about the incredible thing she had seen. Maggie began to complain. She wanted everyone to know about the birds that sang words! But no one listened. Maggie cried and said, "Will nobody listen to me?" Suddenly, one little bird flew down. He said, "Don't worry, Maggie. I am here to listen." Maggie smiled and told the bird about the incredible thing she had seen. The bird nodded, understanding every word. After that, Maggie and the bird played together. Maggie was happy because she had found a friend who was willing to listen. And they enjoyed the incredible singing of the birds in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. Tom had a red kayak. He loved to go on the river with his kayak. The sun was bright, and the water was cool. Tom was very happy. One day, Tom saw a big rock in the river. He stopped his kayak and went to peek behind the rock. He saw a little duck. The duck was scared and alone. Tom wanted to help the duck. Tom picked up the duck and put it in his kayak. They went down the river together. The duck was happy and safe with Tom. They became best friends. Tom and the duck had lots of fun in the strong, red kayak.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play all day long. One hot day, Spot went to the park with his friend, a little girl named Jane. At the park, Jane said, "Spot, it's too hot to run today. Let's find some shade." So, they went under a big tree. Jane had a ball and said, "Spot, I will drop the ball, and you can catch it." Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" Jane dropped the ball, and Spot ran to catch it. They played for a while, but the heat made them tired. They decided to rest under the tree and drink some water. As they rested, they talked about their fun day at the park.

One day, a cat named Tuna was looking for a seat. Tuna was a healthy cat with a big smile. She liked to sit in the sun and play with her friends. Tuna walked around and found a big tree. Under the tree, there was a nice seat made of soft grass. Tuna sat on the seat and said, "This is a good seat. I like it here." Her friend, a small bird, heard her and flew down to sit with her. The bird said, "Hi Tuna! Can I sit with you?" "Yes, you can sit with me," Tuna replied. They both sat on the seat and talked about their day. They laughed and played together under the tree. Soon, it was time for Tuna and the bird to go home. They said goodbye and promised to meet again at the seat under the tree. Tuna went home feeling happy and healthy from her fun day with her friend.
