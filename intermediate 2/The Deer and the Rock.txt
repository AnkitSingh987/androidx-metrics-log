Category: Intermediate
Title: The Deer and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little deer. The little deer liked to jump and run all day. One day, while the little deer was playing, it saw a big, heavy rock. The heavy rock was in the way, and the little deer could not go past it. The little deer tried to push the rock, but it was too heavy. The little deer felt sad and did not know what to do. Then, a wise old owl told the little deer to rest and think. The little deer sat down and rested. After some time, the little deer had an idea. It went to find its friends, the ants, to help. The ants were very strong and, together, they moved the heavy rock. The little deer was happy and thankful. Now, it could run and play in the forest again.

One day, a big cat named Tom and a small dog named Tim went to play in the park. They liked to run and jump on the soft green grass. The sun was shining, and the sky was pale blue. As they played, Tom heard a strange sound. "What is that sound?" he asked Tim. Tim stopped and listened too. "I don't know," Tim said, "let's go see!" So, they ran towards the sound, excited to find out what it was. They found a little bird sitting on a tree branch, making the sound. Tom and Tim decided to sit and watch the bird sing. They sat under the tree, listening to the beautiful sound. After a while, they got up and played some more, happy to have found a new friend in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a nosy squirrel with a big fluffy tail. The squirrel was looking at Tim's bag of nuts. Tim thought the squirrel was funny and wanted to be friends. "Can I give you some nuts?" Tim asked the squirrel. The squirrel's face was happy and it nodded its head. Tim gave the squirrel a nut, and they played together in the park. Suddenly, the nosy squirrel ran up a tree with the whole bag of nuts. Tim was sad and surprised. He asked the squirrel to give the nuts back, but the squirrel did not. Then, a big bird came and took the bag from the squirrel. The bird flew to Tim and gave him the bag of nuts back. Tim was happy again, and the squirrel learned not to be so nosy.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a little star. This star loved to perform for all the people in the land. Every night, the star would shine so bright in the sky, making everyone happy. One day, the star met a little boy. The boy said, "Hi, star! I love your show! Can you teach me how to perform like you?" The star was so happy and said, "Of course, little boy! Let's practice together!" The little boy and the star practiced every day. But one day, while they were practicing, something unexpected happened. The little boy started to shine just like the star! They were both so surprised and happy. Now, the little boy and the star could perform together in the sky, making everyone in the distant land even happier.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a purple dog. This dog loved to play with his friends. They would run and jump all day long. One day, while they were playing, the purple dog had to sneeze. When the purple dog sneezed, something unexpected happened. His sneeze was so strong that it blew all the leaves off a nearby tree! The purple dog and his friends were very surprised. They had never seen a sneeze so big. But then, they noticed that the tree was full of yummy fruit. The purple dog's big sneeze had made it easy for them to reach the fruit. They all ate the fruit and had a great time. The purple dog's sneeze was a big success! From that day on, the purple dog and his friends loved the big sneeze and played happily ever after.
