Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Dad

One day, a little dog named Max went to an office with his dad. Max's dad had to do some work, so Max had to stay with him. Max was a good dog, and he liked to help his dad. At lunch time, Max's dad said, "Max, I am going to eat some spicy food. Do you want to try it?" Max wanted to try the spicy food, but he was a little scared. He had never eaten spicy food before. Max took a small bite of the spicy food. His mouth felt hot! Max drank some water to cool down. He decided he did not like spicy food. Max and his dad laughed, and Max stayed in the office, helping his dad until it was time to go home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. He was very excited to play in the sand and swim in the water. He put on his swim clothes and ran to the water. Tim noticed something in the sand while he was playing. It was a little crab! The crab was red and had big eyes. Tim was not scared of the crab, but he thought it looked a little terrible. He decided to be friends with the crab anyway. Tim and the crab played all day on the beach. They built a big sand castle together. When it was time to go home, Tim said goodbye to his new crab friend. He couldn't wait to come back to the beach and play with the crab again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy wanted to grow big and strong. He had small wings, but he dreamed of having big wings to fly high in the sky. One day, Billy saw a big tree with delicious red fruit. Billy asked his friend, "Can I eat that fruit to grow big?" His friend said, "Yes, eat the fruit and you will grow big and strong!" Billy ate the delicious fruit every day. Soon, his wings grew bigger and stronger. He was so happy! Now, Billy could fly high in the sky with his big wings. And he lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved the color orange. He had an orange shirt, orange shoes, and even an orange bed. One day, Tim saw a big orange cross in the park. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. Tim ran to the park and saw a girl named Sue. Sue was playing with the orange cross. Tim asked, "Can I join you?" Sue said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played with the orange cross all day long. They took turns holding the cross and running around the park. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the orange cross up into the sky. Tim and Sue were sad. But then, the wind brought the cross back down. It landed in a tree. Tim and Sue climbed the tree to get the cross. They were happy and continued to play with the orange cross. And from that day on, Tim and Sue were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a famous swan. The swan was very kind and loved to give things to others. One day, the swan met a little duck who had no food. The swan said, "Hello, little duck! I see you have no food. I will give you some of mine." The little duck was very happy and said, "Thank you, famous swan! You are so kind." The little duck learned from the famous swan to be kind and give to others. The duck grew up to be a very kind duck, just like the swan. All the animals in the pond were happy because they learned to share and give. The moral of the story is to be kind and always give to others.

One day, a large cat named Tom found a phone. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was fun to play with. He pushed the phone with his paw and it made a loud noise. Tom was very happy. Tom showed the phone to his friend, a small dog named Max. Max looked at the phone and said, "What is that?" Tom replied, "I don't know, but it's fun!" They both played with the phone, pushing it around and listening to the noise it made. As they played, they accidentally pushed the phone into a puddle. Water began to pour onto the phone. Suddenly, the phone stopped making noise. Tom and Max were sad, but then they saw a big, colorful ball. They played with the ball and forgot all about the phone.
