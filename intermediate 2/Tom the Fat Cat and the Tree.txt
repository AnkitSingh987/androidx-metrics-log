Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Tree

One day, a fat cat named Tom was walking down the street. He saw a telephone on the ground. Tom did not know what it was, but he thought it was a toy. He picked it up with his paw and started to play with it. Suddenly, the telephone made a loud noise. It scared Tom, and he dropped it. The telephone started talking. Tom was surprised, and he said, "Hello? Who is there?" The voice on the telephone said, "Hi, I am a bird named Polly. I am lost. Can you help me?" Tom wanted to help Polly. He looked around and saw a tree. In the tree, there was a nest. Tom thought maybe Polly was in the nest. He climbed the tree and found Polly in the nest. Tom said, "I found you!" Polly was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! Let's be friends." They both settle in the tree and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Lily. She loved to play in her garden. One day, she found a vine growing near her house. The vine was green and pretty. Lily asked her friend Tom, "Do you know how to print a picture of this vine?" Tom said, "Yes, I can help you print it." So, they went inside Lily's house to print the picture of the vine. They were excited to see the pretty vine on paper. But when they came back outside, they saw that the vine had grown too big. It was now all over Lily's garden. It covered her flowers and trees. Lily and Tom were sad. They tried to pull the vine away, but it was too strong. The garden was not pretty anymore.

Once there was a little girl named Lola. She loved playing in her garden. One day she saw a bottle of spray and she grabbed it. She was very curious to see what it did so she asked her mum. "Mummy, what is this?" Lola asked. "It's a test, darling," her mum said. "It's quite easy. I just want you to spray it in the garden and see what happens." Lola was so excited and she was sure that something wonderful would happen. She sprayed the garden and waited. But nothing happened at all. She went inside and when she came back out the garden was a mess. The grass was brown and dead, the flowers had wilted and the trees whisper dying. She had killed the garden. Lola was in shock and she ran inside to tell her mum what she had done. She was desperate for her mum to fix the garden but there was nothing she could do. The garden was ruined forever.

Once upon a time, there was a small house. In the house lived a kind girl named Emma. Emma liked to clean her house every day. One day, as she was cleaning, she found a small box full of ash. "Oh, what is this?" she asked herself. Emma decided to clean the ash from the box. As she cleaned, a magic fairy came out of the box. The fairy was very happy and said, "Thank you, Emma! I was stuck in that box with all the ash. I am very grateful you cleaned it!" The fairy wanted to give Emma a gift. She asked, "What do you want, Emma?" Emma thought for a moment and said, "I want to make everyone happy." The fairy smiled and said, "Your wish is granted!" From that day on, Emma could make everyone around her happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to display his toys. He would put them all in a row and show them to his friends. One day, Tim saw a cheap toy at the store. He wanted it so much, but he only had a little bit of money. Tim went home and thought of a plan. He wrote a note to his mom. The note said, "Can I do jobs for you? I want to buy a toy." His mom read the note and said yes. Tim was very happy. Tim did jobs for his mom. He cleaned his room, washed the dishes, and helped with the laundry. Soon, Tim had enough money to buy the cheap toy. He went to the store and bought it. Now, Tim could display his new toy with his other toys. Tim was so proud and happy.
