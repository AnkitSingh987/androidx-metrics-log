Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ball and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little ball that could bounce very high. The ball had a friend named Tom. Tom was not very strong, he was weak. Tom liked to carry the ball on his shoulder. They would go to the park and play together every day. One day, Tom saw a big tree at the park. He wanted to see if the ball could bounce high enough to reach the top of the tree. He put the ball on his shoulder and started to walk towards the tree. He felt excited and couldn't wait to try. Tom threw the ball with all his strength, but he was weak. The ball did not bounce high enough to reach the top of the tree. The ball felt sad for Tom. But then, a strong wind blew and pushed the ball higher. The ball finally reached the top of the tree and both Tom and the ball were very happy. They learned that even when you are weak, with a little help, you can still do big things.

Sara loved to ride her bike. She had a red bike with a bell and a basket. She liked to go fast and feel the wind on her face. She rode her bike every day around her house and the park. One day, Sara's mom said they were going to visit her grandma. Her grandma lived in the country, far away from the city. Sara was excited to see her grandma, but she was also sad to leave her bike. She asked her mom if she could take her bike with them. "I'm sorry, Sara, but there is no room for your bike in the car. You can play with your grandma's toys when we get there. Maybe she has a bike you can ride," her mom said. Sara nodded, but she felt lonely without her bike. She hugged her bike and said goodbye. She hoped her bike would not miss her too much. When they got to the country, Sara saw a lot of green fields and trees. She also saw some cows and horses. She thought they were pretty, but she still missed her bike. She went inside her grandma's house and gave her a big hug. "Hello, Sara, I'm so happy to see you. Do you want to see what I have for you?" her grandma said. Sara smiled and followed her grandma to the backyard. There, she saw a surprise. It was a bike, just like hers, but blue. It had a bell and a basket, too. "Wow, grandma, is this for me?" Sara asked. "Yes, my dear, it is. I knew you loved to ride your bike, so I got you one for your visit. You can ride it as much as you want, and explore the country with me. Do you like it?" her grandma said. Sara was so happy, she could not believe it. She hugged her grandma and thanked her. She ran to the bike and got on. She rang the bell and smiled. "I love it, grandma, thank you so much. Can we go for a ride now?" Sara said. "Of course, my dear, let's go. I'll show you some beautiful places. And don't worry, your bike at home will be waiting for you when you come back," her grandma said. Sara and her grandma rode their bikes together, and Sara felt the wind on her face. She saw the cows and the horses, and the flowers and the birds. She felt happy and not lonely anymore. She loved the country, and she loved her grandma. And she loved her bike, both the red and the blue.

One day, a big cat named Tom saw a cool bug near a hedge. The bug was very small and could not lift things. Tom wanted to help the bug. Tom said, "Little bug, I can lift you up and put you on top of the hedge." The bug said, "Thank you, Tom. That is very nice of you." Tom used his paw to lift the bug and put it on the hedge. Then, something unexpected happened. The little bug changed into a big, beautiful butterfly. The butterfly said, "Thank you, Tom. You helped me and now I can fly!" Tom and the butterfly became best friends and played together every day.
