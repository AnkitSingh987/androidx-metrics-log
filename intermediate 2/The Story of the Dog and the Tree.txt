Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Tree

One day, a little soldier was walking. He was upset because he could not find his toy. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. He saw his friend, the dog, and asked, "Dog, did you see my toy?" The dog said, "Yes, I saw it. It is up in the tree." The little soldier was sad. He was too small to get the toy from the tree. But he did not give up. He tried to think of a way to get his toy back. The little soldier managed to find a big stick. He used the stick to hit the toy in the tree. The toy fell down, and the little soldier was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy turned into a big, beautiful butterfly! The butterfly flew away, and the little soldier was not upset anymore. He was happy to see his toy become a beautiful butterfly.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a mighty witch. She had a big hat and a long dress. She liked to do magic tricks and make things appear. One day, the witch walked into the woods. She saw a little bird who could not fly. The bird was sad. The mighty witch wanted to help the bird. She waved her magic wand and made a big, soft pillow appear. The witch put the pillow under the bird. The bird jumped on the pillow and tried to fly again. This time, the bird flew high into the sky. The bird was so happy and thanked the mighty witch. From that day on, the bird and the witch became best friends. They played together in the woods every day. The mighty witch always used her magic to make fun things appear for them to play with. And they lived happily ever after.

One sunny day, a little girl named Amy went to her tidy room. She saw her favorite toy, a small mirror. Amy loved to play with her mirror. She could see her face and make funny faces in it. Amy's mom came into the room and said, "Let's play a game, Amy!" Amy was very happy and asked, "What game, Mom?" Her mom said, "We will play the peek game with your mirror!" Amy and her mom started to play. They took turns hiding the mirror and then tried to find it. When they found the mirror, they would peek at it and make a funny face. They laughed and laughed. After playing for a while, Amy's mom said, "Time for a break, Amy. Let's have a snack." Amy agreed and they went to the kitchen. They had a fun day playing the peek game with the mirror and eating snacks.

Once upon a time, there was a famous castle. In the castle, there lived a king, a queen, and their little boy. They were very happy. The little boy liked to play with his toys and run around the castle. One day, while the little boy was playing, he saw a big bird fly into the castle. The bird looked lost and scared. The little boy said, "Don't be scared, bird. I will help you." The bird was happy and thanked the little boy. The little boy and the bird became good friends. They played together every day. But one day, the bird saw something bad. A big, mean giant was coming to take the famous castle. The bird told the little boy, "We have to save the castle!" The little boy had an idea. He asked the bird to fly high and drop water on the giant. The bird did it, and the giant went away. The little boy and the bird saved the famous castle. Everyone was happy, and they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Max were playing in the park. They saw a big slide and ran to it. Lily went first and slid down fast. She laughed and said, "That was fun!" Max wanted to slide too, but he saw a boy with a lollipop. He liked lollipops. He ran to the boy and said, "Give me your lollipop!" The boy said, "No, it's mine! Go away!" Max was angry. He grabbed the lollipop and tried to pull it. The boy held on tight and said, "Stop it! That's not nice!" Lily saw what Max was doing. She ran to him and said, "Max, don't do that! That's bad! You have to share!" Max did not listen. He pulled harder and harder. The boy pulled harder and harder. The lollipop broke in half and fell to the ground. Max and the boy looked at the lollipop. It was dirty and sticky. They both felt sad. Lily picked up the lollipop and said, "Look what you did! You ruined the lollipop! You should say sorry to the boy!" Max did not want to say sorry. He was still angry. He slapped Lily on the face and said, "No, you say sorry! You are a mean sister!" Lily felt a sting on her face. She felt tears in her eyes. She said, "Ow, that hurt! Why did you slap me? I was trying to help you!" Max did not answer. He ran away from Lily and the boy. He felt bad, but he did not know how to say it. Lily and the boy looked at each other. They did not know what to do. They decided to throw away the lollipop and play on the slide. They slid down together and smiled. They said, "We are friends!" The end.
