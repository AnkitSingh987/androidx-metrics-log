Category: Intermediate
Title: The Modest Moon and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a modest moon in the sky. Every night it shone brightly and created beautiful shadows on the ground. One night, a young girl called Emma was out playing in the garden. She saw the beautiful moon and said, "I want to become the moon." Suddenly, a magical fairy appeared. She said to Emma, "If you wish to become the moon, you must be very brave." So Emma set off on an adventure. She trotted up a hill and climbed to the top. There, she saw the beautiful moon and became part of it. Once she completed her journey, Emma said, "I have become the moon!" The magical fairy smiled and said, "Your wish has come true. I am so proud of you for being so brave!"

Anna was a young girl who liked to play piano. She had a big piano in her room that her mom gave her. She wanted to learn how to play songs like her mom, who was a good piano player. One day, Anna saw a book on the piano. It had pictures of notes and keys and words. She thought it was a book to help her learn piano. She opened the book and tried to read it. But the words were too hard and the notes were too many. She got angry and threw the book on the floor. "Anna, what are you doing?" her mom asked, coming into the room. She saw the book on the floor and the piano open. She was very mad. "This is not a book for you, Anna. This is a book for grown-ups. You can't play piano like this. You have to start with easy songs and practice a lot. You can't just throw things around when you don't understand them." "But I want to learn piano, mom. I want to play like you. Why can't I?" Anna said, crying. "Because you are too young, Anna. You have to be patient and listen to me. I can teach you piano, but you have to follow the rules. You can't touch the piano without my permission. You can't use the books without my help. You can't be rude and naughty. Do you understand?" her mom said, picking up the book and closing the piano. Anna nodded, but she was still sad. She wanted to learn piano, but she didn't like the rules. She didn't like the book. She didn't like her mom. She wished she had a different piano, a different book, a different mom. The next day, Anna saw a small piano in the toy store. It was red and shiny and had buttons that made sounds. She liked it a lot. She thought it was a piano for her. She wanted to have it. She asked her mom to buy it for her, but her mom said no. She said it was too expensive and too noisy and not a real piano. She said Anna already had a piano at home and she should be happy with it. Anna was not happy. She was angry. She wanted the small piano. She wanted to play with it. She wanted to learn piano her way. She decided to take it. She waited until her mom was looking at something else and she grabbed the small piano and ran out of the store. "Hey, stop! That's not yours!" the store man shouted, chasing after her. Anna ran faster, but she was not fast enough. The store man caught her and took the small piano from her. He was very mad. He called her mom and the police. He said Anna was a thief and a liar and a bad girl. Anna was scared. She cried and said she was sorry. She said she just wanted to learn piano. She said she didn't like her big piano and her hard book and her mean mom. She said she liked the small piano and the fun buttons and the nice sounds. But no one listened to her. No one cared. They took her to the police station and called her dad. They said Anna was in big trouble. They said she might go to jail. They said she might never play piano again. Anna wished she never saw the small piano. She wished she never ran out of the store. She wished she never threw the book on the floor. She wished she listened to her mom and followed the rules. She wished she learned piano the right way. But it was too late. She had made a big mistake. And she had to pay for it.
