Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Gifted Boy and the Park

One day, a gifted boy named Tim had a favorite shirt. He loved his shirt because it was red and soft. Tim wore his shirt all the time. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. They played on the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. Tim was having so much fun. While playing, Tim got very hot. He said to his friend, "I am hot. I will take off my shirt." So, Tim took off his shirt and put it on the bench. They continued to play and have fun. Later, it was time to go home. Tim forgot his shirt on the bench and left the park. When he got home, his mom asked, "Where is your shirt, Tim?" Tim remembered he left it at the park. They quickly went back to the park, and Tim found his shirt on the bench. He was happy to have his favorite shirt back.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to talk to his friends. He had a big dream. He wanted to ride a bicycle. One day, Tom saw a bicycle in the park. He was very happy. He tried to ride the bicycle, but it was hard. His friends, the birds, saw Tom and wanted to help him. The birds picked up the bicycle and flew with it. Tom was surprised! He jumped on the bicycle and started to ride. Now, Tom can ride a bicycle in the sky with his bird friends.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy had a broken wing and could not fly. All the other birds would soar high in the sky, but Timmy could only watch them from the ground. Timmy was sad and wished he could fly too. One day, Timmy met a kind lady named Mary. Mary saw Timmy's broken wing and said, "Oh no, your wing is broken! Let me help you." Mary took Timmy to her home and gave him a soft bed to sleep in. Timmy lived in luxury, with yummy food and a warm place to stay. After some time, Timmy's broken wing started to heal. One sunny day, Mary took Timmy outside and said, "Try to fly now, Timmy!" To Timmy's surprise, he could fly again! He soared high in the sky, just like the other birds. Timmy was so happy and thankful to Mary for helping him. From that day on, Timmy and Mary were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a smart cat named Tom found a big box. The box had tape on it. Tom wanted to open the box. He used his sharp claws to cut the tape. Inside the box, he found a lot of toys and a big jug of water. Tom was thirsty, so he tried to pour water into a small cup. But he was not very good at it. Water splashed on the floor. Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, came in and saw the mess. Max said, "Tom, you need help. Let me pour the water for you." Tom agreed and let Max pour the water. Max was very good at pouring. He did not spill any water on the floor. Tom and Max played with the toys and drank water from the cup. They had a lot of fun. They were happy to be friends and help each other.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a restless little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run all day. He was always very happy. One sunny day, Max met a kind lady who wanted to teach him something new. The lady showed Max how to sit and stay. Max was very good at learning, and he liked learning new things from the kind lady. After they played and learned, the kind lady gave Max a treat. It was a big bowl of cream. Max loved the cream. It was sweet and cold. Max was very happy and not restless anymore. He liked the kind lady and his new treat. And from that day on, Max and the kind lady were best friends.
