Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Bird and the Hill

One day, a pretty bird named Lily wanted to rise high in the sky. She asked her friend, Tim the cat, "Can you show me the way to rise high?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I can't fly, but I can help you find a way." As they looked for a way, they saw a big hill. "Maybe if you jump from the top, you can rise high," said Tim. Lily was scared, but she wanted to try. They climbed the hill together, and Lily got ready to jump. When Lily jumped, she didn't rise high like she wanted. Instead, she fell and hurt her wing. Tim felt bad for his friend. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't know that wouldn't work." But Lily didn't give up. She knew there was a way to rise high, and she would find it. And one day, when her wing felt better, she did.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dove. The dove liked to play with other animals in the park. They all had fun together. One day, a new animal came to the park. It was a big dog. The other animals were scared of the big dog. But the friendly dove was not scared. The dove went to the big dog and asked, "Will you play with us?" The big dog was happy to play. But then, something unexpected happened. The big dog changed into a little bird! The friendly dove was surprised. The little bird said, "I am a magic bird. I changed into a big dog because I was scared too. But now, I accept that I am a little bird." Now, the friendly dove and the magic bird played together with the other animals. They all learned that it is okay to be who they are. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy and a girl walking in the woods. They were exploring and looking for adventure. Suddenly, the girl stopped and pointed. "Look!" she said. The boy saw a bright, colourful trail on the ground. "What is it?" asked the boy. "I don't know," said the girl, "let's go and see!" The boy and the girl followed the trail until it led them to a beautiful pond. The girl smiled and laughed. "Let's go for a swim!" she said. The boy agreed, so he and the girl jumped in the water. They played and splashed until the boy saw something in the water. It was a stupid fish! He laughed and tried to catch the fish, but it swam away. The girl laughed too and gave him a kiss. The boy and girl played in the pond until they were too tired. When they got out of the pond, the trail was still there. They held hands and followed the trail back home.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play with a red ball of thread. She would chase it and roll it around the house. One day, Kitty's friend, Doggy, came over to play. "Kitty, let's play with the thread!" Doggy said. Kitty and Doggy played with the thread all day, running and jumping together. After playing for a long time, Kitty and Doggy were tired. They decided to relax under a big tree. They laid down and looked at the sky. "This was a fun day," Kitty said. Doggy agreed, and they both fell asleep, dreaming of more days playing with the red ball of thread.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a lot of hay. A horse named Sam and a cow named Lucy lived on the farm. They were good friends. One day, Sam and Lucy saw a big pile of hay. Sam said, "I want to jump in the hay!" Lucy was a careful cow. She said, "No, Sam! We must be careful. We don't know what is in the hay." Sam disagreed, but he did not jump in the hay. Later, the farmer came and moved the hay. Under the hay, there was a big hole! Sam was happy he listened to Lucy. They both knew that being careful was a good thing. They played together and stayed safe on the farm.
