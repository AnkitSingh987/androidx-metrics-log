Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Zoo

Once upon a time there was a girl named Lucy. She loved animals so much that she wanted to go to the zoo every day. One day, her mommy said, "Let's go to the zoo!" Lucy couldn't believe it and was so excited to go. On their way to the zoo, Lucy noticed a small toy store. She asked her mommy if she could go inside and look at the toys. Her mom was very thoughtful and said, yes I'll go in with you. So they went in and Lucy saw many interesting toys. She noticed a broken toy and asked her mommy, "Can I fix it?" Her mommy said, "Yes, of course, you can." Lucy was so excited to try to fix it and she worked hard on it. She plugged the pieces back together and fixed the toy. Finally, they went to the zoo. Lucy was so happy to see all the animals. She had a great time and she was so proud of herself for fixing the toy.

One day, Jimmy went outside to play. He saw some bricks and started to pick them up. Suddenly, a big dog came running towards Jimmy. Jimmy felt scared. He shouted, "Help!" The dog ran around Jimmy in circles. It barked loudly. Jimmy was very fearful. Suddenly, an old man came out from his house. The old man said, "Come on now, don't be scared. Let's all jog together." Jimmy and the dog jogged together around the block. They ran, they stopped, they jumped, and they laughed. At the end of the day, Jimmy and the dog said goodbye. Jimmy went home feeling happy. He had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted girl named Amy. Amy loved to dance. One day, she found a big shield in her yard. Amy picked up the shield and said, "Wow! This shield is so big and strong! I want to dance with it!" So, Amy danced and danced with the shield. She twirled and jumped, and the shield made her feel safe. Amy's friend, Tim, saw her dancing and asked, "Can I dance with you, Amy?" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's dance together!" So, Amy and Tim danced with the shield. They had lots of fun and felt very happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the woods with his dad. They wanted to make a tree house. Tim had a toy saw and his dad had a big saw. They found a big tree and started to work. As they were working, a rare bird came by. The bird said, "Hello, what are you doing?" Tim and his dad were very surprised. They did not know birds could talk. Tim said, "We are making a tree house." The bird said, "I have an idea. I can help you make it faster." The bird called his bird friends. They all came and helped Tim and his dad. They finished the tree house very fast. Tim, his dad, and the birds were very happy. They all played in the tree house together.

Once there was a girl named Lily. Lily had a big, comfortable chair. One day, while she sat on her chair, she saw a small needle on the floor. She picked it up and thought about what to do with it. Lily had a friend named Tim. Tim was good at sewing. She decided to give the needle to Tim. She put on her shoes and hurried to Tim's house. When Lily got to Tim's house, she said, "Tim, I found a needle. I thought you might like it." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. I will use it to sew a pretty dress for my sister." They both felt happy and played together for the rest of the day.

One day, a green bird named Bob found a pretty vase. The vase was big and shiny. Bob wanted to settle and make a home in the vase. He flew inside and said, "This is my new home!" A little girl named Sue saw the vase with Bob inside. She said, "Hello, green bird! Why are you in the vase?" Bob said, "This is my new home. I want to settle here." Sue was happy and wanted to be friends with Bob. But then, something unexpected happened! The vase started to shake and wiggle. A tiny mouse was hiding inside the vase too! The mouse said, "I am Tim, and this is my home too!" Bob, Sue, and Tim all became friends and shared the pretty vase as their home.
