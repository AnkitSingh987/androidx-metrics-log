Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Clever Fish and the Water

Once upon a time, there was a clever little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big blue sea. The water was salty, but Fin liked it. He had lots of friends in the sea. One day, Fin met a big fish named Bubbles. Bubbles was sad. "I can't spray water like the other fish," Bubbles said. Fin wanted to help. "I have an idea!" said Fin. "Let's find a way to help you spray water." They swam around and found a big shell. Fin had a plan. "Put this shell in your mouth, and you can spray water too!" Bubbles put the shell in his mouth and tried to spray water. But it didn't work. Bubbles was very sad. "I will never be able to spray water like the other fish," he cried. Fin felt bad that his plan didn't work. They swam away, still looking for a way to help Bubbles spray water. But they never found one, and Bubbles stayed sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very creative. She liked to draw and paint all day. One day, she saw a pretty lamp on a high shelf. The lamp was shiny and colorful. Mia wanted to see the lamp up close. Mia tried to reach the lamp. She stood on her toes and stretched her arms high. But she was too small to reach it. Mia was sad, but she did not give up. She had a smart idea. Mia got her big toy box and pushed it near the shelf. Mia stood on the toy box and tried to reach the lamp again. This time, she could touch it! Mia was so happy. She held the lamp and looked at it. It was even more beautiful up close. Mia put the lamp in her room and turned it on. The lamp made her room bright and colorful. Mia smiled and knew that being creative helped her reach the pretty lamp.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl called Sally. She used to play outside in the garden with her best friend, Jack. One day Sally spotted something shiny in the garden. It was a little pile of gold! Sally was so excited and quickly picked it up. "This is so pretty! I'm taking it home," she said. Jack was not so keen. He said, "No! That's not good. We should put it back." But Sally just gave him a kiss and said, "Don't worry, Jack. I promise I'll look after it." So Sally took the gold home, where she polished it and kept it safe forever. She was very happy and knew she had done the right thing. Her love and care made everything good. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tom. Every Sunday, Tom and his mom would go to church. Tom did not like to wake up early to go to church. One day, his mom said, "Tom, if you wake up early and go to church, I have a surprise for you." The next Sunday, Tom woke up early and went to church with his mom. He was still a bit sleepy, but he was excited for the surprise. At church, Tom listened and learned about being kind and helpful. After church, his mom gave him a big, yummy ice cream cone. From that day on, Tom would always wake up early for church. He learned that being stubborn and not listening to his mom was not good. The surprise his mom gave him made him happy, but learning to be kind and helpful made him even happier. And that was the best surprise of all.

Once upon a time, there lived a loyal prince. He had lots of friends and they were all always together. One day, the prince's friends asked him to go on an adventure and save a bottle of special perfume. Everyone said it was a secret, so the prince agreed and set off. On his way, the prince discovered many things and had to use his courage to pass through a dangerous forest. But he was loyal to his friends and kept going. Finally, when he arrived at the special location, he saw the most wonderful bottle of perfume. He managed to get hold of it and carry it safely home. When the prince's friends saw the bottle, they were so delighted. They shouted "Thank you, prince! You have saved us!" The loyal prince felt proud and his friends were thankful. It was the best adventure ever!
