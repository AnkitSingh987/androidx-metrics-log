Category: Intermediate
Title: Jazz the Bossy Cat and the Bird

One day, a bossy cat named Jazz went for a walk. Jazz liked to zip around and tell other animals what to do. In the park, Jazz saw a big tree and decided to climb it. Jazz met a bird in the tree. "Hi, bird!" said Jazz. "You should zip around with me and be my friend!" The bird looked at Jazz and said, "Okay, we can be friends, but I don't like bossy friends." Jazz and the bird zipped around the park together. They played and had fun. Jazz learned that being bossy was not nice. They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a colorful rocket was in the sky. It was big and bright. The rocket wanted to fly high, but a big bird did not let it. The bird was not nice to the rocket. This made the rocket sad. The rocket tried to fly away from the bird. It went left, right, up, and down. But the bird followed it. The rocket did not know what to do. It wanted to be free and fly high. Then, a good thing happened. A strong wind came and blew the bird away. The colorful rocket was now free. It flew high in the sky, happy and safe. The rocket knew it could do anything it wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a hurt leg. He was frightened because he did not want to walk on his leg. He wanted his leg to feel better. Spot's mom tried to help him walk, but Spot would refuse. He would say, "No, no, my leg hurts too much!" His mom would feel sad because she wanted to help him. One day, a kind friend came to visit Spot. The friend was gentle and helped Spot walk on his leg. Spot was still frightened, but he tried very hard. Soon, Spot's leg felt better and he was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little town. In this town, there was a shop that sells many things. One day, a nice lady went to the shop to buy something special. The lady saw a big potato. She wanted to buy the potato for her dinner. She picked up the potato and went to the shopkeeper. The shopkeeper was a kind man. He said, "This potato is very good. You will like it." The nice lady took the potato home. She cooked a yummy dinner with the potato. The quiet town was very happy. The nice lady shared her dinner with her friends. They all loved the potato. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tom was very excited. He got a big, yummy lollipop from his mom. He loved the lollipop so much. It was his favorite treat. While he was eating the lollipop, it broke. Tom was sad. He did not want to lose his yummy treat. He thought, "I need to fix my lollipop." Tom went to his dad and asked for help. His dad helped him fix the lollipop with some tape. Tom was happy and excited again. He thanked his dad and went back to enjoying his yummy treat.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big boat with a sail. Mia was adventurous and wanted to play on the boat. She climbed on the boat and pretended to be a captain. She shouted, "Ahoy! Let's go on an adventure!" While Mia was on the boat, she heard a loud roar. She looked around and saw a big lion. The lion said, "Hello, Mia! Can I play on the boat with you?" Mia was scared at first, but the lion looked friendly. She said, "Yes, you can play with me, Mr. Lion." Mia and the lion played on the boat together. They pretended to sail across the sea and roar like lions. They had a great time being adventurous friends. At the end of the day, Mia and the lion said goodbye and went back to their homes. They promised to play together again soon.

One day, a little bird named Tim was in the forest. Tim was very good at whistling. He liked to whistle all day long. All his friends loved to hear him whistle. Tim's friend, Sam the squirrel, was in a trap. Sam was scared and called for help. Tim heard Sam and got ready to help. He said, "Don't worry, Sam! I will help you." Tim used his beak to open the trap. He whistled a happy song while he worked. Soon, the trap was open and Sam was free. Sam said, "Thank you, Tim! You saved me." Tim and Sam were very happy. They played together in the forest all day. And Tim whistled his happy song for everyone to hear.
