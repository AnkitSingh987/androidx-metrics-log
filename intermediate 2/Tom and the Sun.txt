Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big, sleepy cat named Tom. Tom loved to take long naps in his tent. One day, while Tom was sleeping, a little bird named Sue came to visit him. "Tom, Tom!" Sue chirped, "Wake up! Let's play outside!" Tom opened one eye and yawned. He was still very sleepy, but he loved playing with Sue. "Okay, Sue," Tom said, "Let me shut my tent and then we can play." Tom slowly got up and shut his tent. They both went outside and played in the sun. They ran, jumped, and laughed all day. As the sun went down, Tom and Sue were very tired. They said goodnight to each other and went to their homes. Tom went back to his tent, opened it, and went inside. He was so sleepy that he fell asleep right away, dreaming of more fun days with Sue.

Tom likes to collect stamps. He has many stamps from different places. He keeps them in a big book. He likes to examine them with a magnifier. He feels happy when he sees the colors and pictures on the stamps. One day, Tom finds a new stamp in his book. He does not know where it is from. It is unknown to him. It has a strange shape and a scary face on it. He wants to examine it more. He takes out his magnifier and looks at it closely. But when he does, something bad happens. The stamp starts to glow and make a loud noise. It hurts Tom's eyes and ears. He drops the magnifier and the stamp. He screams and runs away. He is scared and sad. The stamp is not a stamp. It is a trap. It is a device that someone put in Tom's book to hurt him. The person who did it is mean and evil. He wants to steal Tom's stamps and make him cry. He is hiding and watching Tom. He laughs and says, "Gotcha!"

Once upon a time, there was a big temple in a small town. Many people went to the temple every day. The temple was incredible. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the temple with his mom. Tim saw a big stone. He said, "Look, mom! The big stone can talk!" His mom said, "No, Tim. Stones can't talk." But then, the big stone said, "Hello, Tim!" Tim and his mom were very surprised. They did not know that the stone could really talk. The big stone told them it was a magic stone. Now, everyone in the town knew about the talking stone. They all thought it was incredible.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He had a boat. He liked to sail on the calm sea. One day, he saw a big, shiny rock on the beach. He thought it was special. He picked it up and took it with him on his boat. The sailor showed the shiny rock to his friend. His friend said, "Wow! That is a nice rock. I think if you rub it, something good will happen." The sailor was excited. He decided to rub the rock when he needed help. One day, the sailor was lost at sea. He did not know where to go. He remembered the shiny rock and what his friend said. He took out the rock and rubbed it. Suddenly, a big fish appeared. The fish said, "Hello, sailor. I will help you find your way home." The sailor followed the big fish. Soon, they reached the beach. The sailor was happy. He thanked the big fish and the shiny rock. From that day on, the sailor and the big fish were best friends. They had many fun adventures together on the calm sea.

One day, a little cat named Tiny was very hungry. She went to the kitchen to find some food. She saw a big jar of butter on the table. Tiny wanted to get the butter, but she was scared. She was scared of the big chair she had to climb. Tiny started to tremble because she was so scared. Tiny's friend, a brave dog named Max, saw that Tiny was scared. Max wanted to help Tiny. He had a plan. Max pushed the chair close to the table for Tiny. Tiny saw what Max did and felt brave too. She climbed the chair and got the butter. Tiny and Max were very happy. They ate the butter together. But then, something unexpected happened. The butter made them feel funny. They did not know that this was magic butter! Tiny and Max started to dance and sing. They were so surprised but they had so much fun.
