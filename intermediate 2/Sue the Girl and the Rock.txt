Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, red pot. She liked to play with her pot. One day, she found a small, ordinary rock. She put the rock in the pot. Sue showed her friend, Tim. Tim said, "What is in the pot?" Sue said, "A rock." Tim did not think the rock was fun. He shrugged and said, "Okay." Sue took her pot and rock to the park. She met a dog. The dog liked the rock in the pot. Sue and the dog played with the pot and rock all day. They had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a tiny cell, there lived an ancient bug named Bob. Bob was very old and had many friends. He liked to play with his friends in the big green grass. One day, Bob wanted to try something new. He saw a big, tall tree and thought it would be fun to climb it. Bob asked his friends to come with him, but they were scared. So, Bob decided to try by himself. Bob started to climb the tree. He tried very hard and went up, up, up. When he reached the top, he saw the whole world! He felt happy and proud. Then, he went down and told his friends about his adventure. They all wanted to try climbing the tree too. And so, they all tried together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lily. She was three years old, and she loved to dream. One day, Lily was thinking about what she wanted to do when she grows up. She wanted to look cool and have her own unique style. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, when I'm a big kid, what kind of style should I have?" Her mom smiled. She said, "Well, Lily, it's up to you! You can pick a cool style that is all your own." Lily was so excited. She thought about all her ideas for her style. She knew it was going to be special and awesome. Lily kept dreaming and imagining all the cool styles she could have. She was so happy and looking forward to the day when she could be a big kid and pick her style.

One day, a fireman named Bob saw a big fire. Bob was a strong man who helped people. He put on his red hat and jumped in his big, red truck. The truck had a loud horn that went "beep beep". Bob drove fast to the fire to save the day. Bob got to the fire and saw a dirty cat in a tree. The cat was scared and cried, "meow meow". Bob said, "Don't worry, little cat. I will save you." He used a long ladder to climb up the tree. The cat watched Bob come closer. Bob took the cat in his arms and went down the ladder. The cat was so happy and said, "Thank you, Bob." Bob felt good to help the cat. He then used water to put out the fire. Everyone clapped and cheered for Bob, the brave fireman.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly queen. She lived in a big castle. The queen had many friends. They liked to play and talk together. One day, the queen and her friends were eating cake. The queen said, "Yummy cake! Let's finish it all!" Her friends agreed and they all ate the cake. They were very happy. After they finished the cake, the queen and her friends played outside. They ran, jumped, and laughed. They had a lot of fun. The friendly queen and her friends loved spending time together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big, red motorcycle was very nervous. It was going to ring its bell for the first time. The motorcycle wanted to make friends and play with the other motorcycles. The red motorcycle went to the park. It saw other motorcycles playing and having fun. The red motorcycle wanted to join them, but it was too nervous to ring its bell. It just watched from far away. A small, blue motorcycle saw the red motorcycle and came over. The blue motorcycle said, "Hi! Do you want to play with us?" The red motorcycle said, "Yes, but I am nervous to ring my bell." The blue motorcycle said, "Don't be scared. We all had to learn how to ring our bells too." The red motorcycle took a deep breath and rang its bell. All the motorcycles heard the bell and came to play with the red motorcycle. The red motorcycle was so happy and not nervous anymore. The moral of the story is to not be scared to try new things and make friends.
