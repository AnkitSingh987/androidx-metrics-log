Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Scarf

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red scarf that he loved very much. He wore it every day, even when it was not cold outside. One day, Tim was playing with his friends. They were playing a game called "sack race". In this game, they had to jump in a big sack and hop to the finish line. Tim thought it would be fun to wear his red scarf while playing the game. But his friends told him it was foolish because it was hot outside. Tim did not listen and wore the scarf anyway. When the sack race started, Tim jumped into his sack and began to hop. But soon, he felt very hot because of his red scarf. Tim stopped hopping, took off his scarf, and put it in the sack. He learned that it was not a good idea to wear a scarf when it was hot outside. From that day on, Tim only wore his red scarf when it was cold.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy car. The car was broken. Tim wanted to fix the car. He went to his dad and asked, "Can you help me fix the car, dad?" His dad said, "Of course, Tim. Let's fix it together." They worked hard to fix the car. When it was fixed, Tim played with it. But then, the car started to smell bad. It smelled like spoiled milk. Tim didn't know why the car smelled so bad. He asked his dad, "Why does the car smell like spoiled milk?" His dad looked at the car and found a note. The note said, "This car has poison inside. Be careful." Tim and his dad were scared. They didn't want to play with the car anymore. They put the car in a box and took it far away from their home. In the end, Tim and his dad were safe. They learned to be careful with things they find. They were happy they could fix the car, but sad it had poison inside. They went home and played with other toys that didn't smell like spoiled milk.

Once upon a time, there was an adventurous boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with cardboard. He would make big forts and pretend he was a king. One day, he found a big piece of cardboard in his yard. Tim said, "I will make a seat with this cardboard!" So, he folded the cardboard and made a seat. He was very happy and sat on his new seat. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and said, "Thank you for making a seat for me!" Tim was surprised but smiled. He said, "You're welcome, little bird!" They became friends and played together every day. And they both loved the cardboard seat.

One day, a playful cat named Tom saw a big jar in the kitchen. He wanted to enter the jar to play. He jumped inside and had a lot of fun. He rolled and spun around in the jar. Tom's friend, a little girl named Amy, walked into the kitchen. She saw Tom in the jar and laughed. "Tom, what are you doing in there?" she asked. Tom just looked at her and meowed. Amy reached into the jar and helped Tom out. They played together all day long. Tom was happy that he had found the jar and had a fun day with Amy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong man named Tom. Tom had a special power. He could make things grow very fast. He was very generous and liked to help his friends. One day, Tom went to visit his friend, Sue. Sue had a little garden with flowers and vegetables. But her garden was not growing well. Tom wanted to help Sue with her garden. Tom went to Sue's garden and knelt down. He touched the ground with his hands. His power made the flowers and vegetables grow big and tall. Sue was so happy and thanked Tom for his generous help. They had a big feast with the vegetables from the garden and shared them with all their friends.
