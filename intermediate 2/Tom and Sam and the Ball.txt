Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sam and the Ball

Tom and Sam were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big ball with many spikes. It was weird. They wanted to touch it. "Let's pick it up!" Tom said. He ran to the ball and grabbed it with his hands. "Ow!" he cried. The spikes hurt his hands. They were sharp and pointy. He dropped the ball and looked at his hands. They were red and bleeding. "Tom, are you okay?" Sam asked. He was scared. He did not want to touch the ball. He wanted to help Tom. He ran to Tom and hugged him. "I'm sorry, Tom. That was a bad ball. Let's go home." But it was too late. The ball was not just weird. It was also bad. It had a bad thing inside. It was a bomb. It made a loud noise and a big fire. It hurt Tom and Sam and the park. It was a very bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a big green palace, there lived a kind king and queen. They had a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys and run around in the palace garden. One day, Lily heard a loud roar while she was playing. She looked around and saw a big, friendly lion. The lion was also green, just like the palace! Lily was not scared, and she went to say hi to the lion. Lily and the green lion became best friends. They played together every day in the palace garden. They would run, jump, and roar together. Lily and the green lion were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a nosy whale. The nosy whale liked to set out on adventures. He swam far and wide to see new things. One day, the nosy whale set out to find a new friend. He swam and swam until he met a little fish. The little fish was shy, but the nosy whale was kind. They played and laughed all day. As the sun went down, the nosy whale and the little fish set off for home. They swam side by side, happy to have found each other. From that day on, the nosy whale and the little fish were the best of friends.

Lily and Tom were twins who lived in a big house with their mom and dad. They were wealthy, which meant they had a lot of things and money. They had many toys, books, clothes, and snacks. They also had a big shower in their bathroom, where they could wash and play with water. One day, mom and dad told them they had a surprise for them. They took them to a big store, where they saw many people and things. Mom and dad said they wanted to add something to their shower. They showed them a big box with a picture of a rainbow on it. "What is that?" Lily asked. "It's a shower head that makes colorful water," dad said. "You can choose different colors and patterns. Do you like it?" "Yes, yes, yes!" Lily and Tom said. They loved rainbows and colors. They wanted to try it right away. Mom and dad bought the shower head and took it home. They asked a man to help them install it in their shower. Then they told Lily and Tom they could try it. Lily and Tom ran to their bathroom and turned on the shower. They saw red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple water coming out of the shower head. They laughed and clapped and jumped in the shower. They felt like they were in a magic land. They played with the water for a long time, making shapes and words and pictures. They thanked mom and dad for the wonderful surprise. They said it was the best thing they ever added to their shower. They felt very happy and lucky.

Once upon a time, there was a creative woman. She liked to make things with her hands. She made toys, clothes, and many other fun things for the kids in her town. One day, the woman found a big box. She wanted to make something special with it. She thought and thought, and then she got an idea. She decided to make a playhouse for the kids to have fun in. The woman worked hard on the playhouse. She cut windows and a door, and she painted it with pretty colors. When it was done, she told the kids to come and play. The kids loved the playhouse and played in it all day long. The woman was happy to see the kids having fun, and the playhouse remained a favorite place for them to play.
