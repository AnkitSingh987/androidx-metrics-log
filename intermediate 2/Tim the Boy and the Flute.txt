Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Flute

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a flute that he loved to play. One day, he took his flute to the park to play with his friends. While Tim was playing his flute, a big dog came up to him. The dog was scared of the noise. It barked at Tim and tried to take the flute away. Tim was scared too and said, "Please don't hurt my flute!" Tim's friend, Sarah, came to help. She told the dog, "Don't destroy the flute!" The dog listened to Sarah and stopped trying to take the flute. Tim was happy and thanked Sarah for helping him. They played together in the park and had a great day.

One day, a girl named Mia went outside to play. She saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was white and fluffy. Mia liked to look at clouds and think about what they looked like. This cloud looked like a big spicy pepper. Mia's friend Tom came over and they started to play. They played a game where they had to stand on one foot. Mia was good at standing on one foot, but Tom was not. He fell down and got mad. "I don't like this game!" Tom said. Just then, the spicy cloud started to rain. But instead of water, it rained spicy peppers! Mia and Tom were surprised. They picked up the peppers and laughed. "Let's try a new game with these peppers," Mia said. They played a new game and had lots of fun. The spicy cloud made them forget about their fight and they played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. He was very big and liked to play with his friends. One day, Tom went to his class to learn new things with his friends. In the class, Tom's friends began to complain. They said that Tom was too big and took up too much space. Tom became sad and did not want to play anymore. But then, Tom's teacher came up with a fun idea. She asked Tom to be the leader in a game. Tom felt happy and his friends stopped complaining. They all played together and had a great time in class.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty phone. The phone was big and strong. It could talk and be very loud. The phone lived in a small house with a nice family. One day, the family was sad. They could not find their cat. The cat was lost. The mighty phone wanted to help. It knew it could be a hero. So, the phone began to call for the cat. The phone called and called. It was so loud that the cat heard it from far away. The cat followed the sound of the mighty phone. Soon, the cat was back with the family. The family was so happy. They thanked the mighty phone for being a hero. The phone was proud to help. From that day on, the phone and the family lived happily ever after.

Mum was getting ready for Sunday. She was feeling very anxious, as she wanted to make sure everything was perfect. Mum said to her little daughter, “I’m preparing for Sunday, so I need to make sure everything is ready. Can you help me?” The little girl was so excited to help her Mum. She jumped up and down, clapping her hands, and said “Yes, Mummy! I can help you!” Her little eyes shone with joy. Mum smiled and they started to prepare together. First, they tidied up the living room and made the bed. Then Mum taught her daughter how to make breakfast and set the table for Sunday. She was so proud of her little helper. When everything was ready, Mum looked around and said “Well done darling! Now we are ready for Sunday!” and hugged her daughter tight. The couple were so happy and relieved.

Once upon a time, in a big hotel, there was a little girl named Lily. She was sad because her toy was lost. The hotel was very big and fancy. It had many rooms and people. Lily's family came to stay there for a fun trip. Lily looked in her room for her toy, but it was not there. She started to weep. Her mom and dad heard her and came to help. They asked the hotel people to help find the toy too. Everyone wanted to make Lily happy again. After some time, one of the hotel people found Lily's toy. They gave it back to her. Lily was so happy and stopped weeping. She hugged her toy tight. Her family was happy too. They enjoyed the rest of their stay in the big hotel.
