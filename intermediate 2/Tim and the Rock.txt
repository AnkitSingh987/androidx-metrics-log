Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a quiet town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch big jets fly in the sky. He would always request his mom to take him to the park to see the jets up close. One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. They saw a big jet on the ground. Tim was very excited. He requested his mom to let him go near the jet. His mom said "Okay, but be careful." Tim went close to the jet. He wanted to touch it, but he was too small. He looked around and saw a big rock. Tim requested the rock to help him reach the jet. The rock did not answer, but Tim thought it said yes. Tim climbed on the rock and reached the jet. He was so happy. But suddenly, the jet started to make a loud noise. Tim got scared and fell off the rock. He hurt his leg and cried. His mom came running to help him. The jet flew away, and Tim was sad. The end.

One day, a fat cat and a thin dog met in a big park. The cat had many toys, while the dog had just one ball. The cat saw the dog's ball and wanted to play with it. The dog saw the cat's toys and wanted to play with them too. They thought of a plan. They decided to trade their toys and share them with each other. As they played, they both started to laugh. They were so happy that they had traded their toys. The cat liked the dog's ball, and the dog liked the cat's toys. They played all day and became good friends. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. The fat cat and the thin dog learned that sharing and trading can make everyone happy. The moral of the story is to share what you have with others, and you will find happiness and make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to prepare food for her friends. She liked to cut fruits and make sandwiches. She had a very happy spirit and always wanted to help. One day, Amy wanted to prepare a big meal for her friends. She went to the store with her mom to buy food. They got bread, cheese, and fruits. Amy was very excited to make the meal. Amy was persistent in her work. She cut the fruits and made the sandwiches. When her friends came, they were all happy. They ate the food and played together. Amy's spirit made everyone feel good. And they all had a great day.

Little John waited patiently at the kitchen table. His mom was busy preparing dinner. She had two big pots on the stove, and several vegetables on the countertop. Little John watched her for a few minutes, and then asked, “Mom, what are you preparing?” His mom smiled and said, “I’m preparing a delicious dinner for us to eat. How about you, what are you preparing?” Little John smiled and said, “I’m preparing a picture.” His mom asked, “What do you need?” Little John said, “I’ll need a little pencil to draw it.” His mom nodded and gave Little John a fat pencil. Little John thanked her, and then started to draw a picture of his mom. As he drew, he was very careful with the little pencil and made sure to be precise. When he was done, he showed his mom the picture and she beamed with pride.

Once upon a time, in a normal town, there was a little shop. It was a fun shop with many toys. One day, a boy named Tim went to the shop with his mom. Tim saw a big box with a pretty bow. He wanted to know what was inside. "Mom, can we open the box? I want to see!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "Okay, let's reveal what's inside." They opened the box and found a cute, soft teddy bear. Tim was so happy and hugged the teddy bear tight. From that day on, Tim and the teddy bear were best friends. They played together every day. The normal town became a happier place, and the little shop was always full of kids looking for new friends in boxes.
