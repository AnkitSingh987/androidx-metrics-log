Category: Intermediate
Title: Tammy the Fish and the Hook

Once there was a tiny fish named Tammy. Tammy had a big problem; she couldn't decide what to do. She was very loyal and wanted to be able to make her own choices. So she asked her friends if they could help. One of her friends, a crab named Carlos, offered her a hook. "With this hook," he said, "you can make your own choices!" Tammy was happy. She thanked Carlos for his help and took the hook. Then, Tammy swam around deciding what to choose. She looked at the coral reef, the deep blue ocean, and the seagrass. Finally, she chose the seagrass and was so excited. She hadn't ever chose anything before and felt like she was in control. Tammy was very loyal to her new options and thanked her friends for helping her make her own choices. She swam around and was grateful for the hook she was given. From then on, she was able to choose whatever she wanted and be happy.

One day, a smart cat named Tom saw a big bookcase. Tom wanted to climb it. He thought it would be fun to balance on the edge of the bookcase. So, Tom jumped up and started to climb. As Tom climbed higher, he saw his friend, a bird named Sue. Sue asked, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom replied, "I am climbing this bookcase to balance on the edge. It looks fun!" Sue said, "Be careful, Tom. I will watch you." Tom reached the top of the bookcase and started to balance. He felt proud and happy. Sue clapped her wings and said, "You did it, Tom! You are so smart and brave!" Tom smiled and carefully climbed back down. They both played together and had a great day.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to act like a bird. He jumped up and tried to fly. But Tom could not fly. He fell down and felt sad. Tom met a frog who said he knew a magic spell. The frog told Tom to say the spell and he would become a bird. Tom was happy and said the spell. He felt something change. Tom looked down and saw he did not turn into a bird. He was now a graceful fish! Tom was surprised but happy. He jumped into a pond and swam with his new fish friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue had a pretty sweater. She loved her sweater very much. It was soft and warm. Sue felt calm when she wore her sweater. Sue went to the park to play. She played with her friends and had fun. But she got hot, so she took off her sweater. Sue put her sweater on a bench and kept playing. After playing, Sue went to find her sweater. But it was gone! Sue looked everywhere but could not find it. She felt very sad. Sue lost her sweater and never found it again. The end.

One day, a little girl named Jane felt adventurous. She wanted to go to the park. She put on her shoes and went outside. At the park, she saw a bench. She sat down and watched the birds play. A bird flew down and said, "Hi, I'm Bob. Do you want to play with me?" Jane was surprised but happy. She urged, "Yes, let's play!" So, Jane and Bob played together. They ran and jumped and had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The bench started to move! It was a magic bench. The bench said, "I can take you on a ride." Jane and Bob were excited. They sat on the bench, and it flew them all around the park. They had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big pond with his family. He liked to wave his tail and swim fast. One day, Fin saw a small hole in the pond's wall. He wanted to see what was on the other side. Fin was persistent and kept trying to escape through the hole. After many tries, Fin finally got through the hole. But, instead of finding a new place to explore, he was back in the same pond! It turns out that the hole was a secret passage that led right back home. Fin was surprised and happy to be with his family again.
