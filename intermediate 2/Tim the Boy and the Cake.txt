Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat delicious cake. One day, he saw a big cake on the table. He wanted to eat it all by himself. But, he knew he should not eat the whole cake. Tim had an idea. He would take a piece of cake and replace it with an apple. He thought no one would notice. But, when Tim took a piece of cake, he felt an itch on his arm. He did not care about the itch and ate the cake. The itch got worse and worse. Tim cried because of the itch. He knew he should not have taken the cake. The moral of the story is to not be greedy and always share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to race. He would race with his friends, his dog, and even his shadow. Tom would run as fast as he could, always trying to win. One day, Tom found a box of crayons. There were many colors in the box. There was a red crayon, a blue crayon, a green crayon, and an ugly brown crayon. Tom liked the pretty colors, but he did not like the ugly brown crayon. Tom had an idea! He decided to have a race with the crayons. He lined up the red, blue, green, and ugly brown crayons. Then, Tom said "Ready, set, go!" and the crayons started to race. The red crayon was fast, the blue crayon was faster, and the green crayon was the fastest. But the ugly brown crayon was slow and lost the race. In the end, Tom learned that even if the brown crayon was ugly and slow, it was still special. All the crayons were unique and had their own place in the box. Tom used the ugly brown crayon to draw a big tree in his yard. And from that day on, Tom loved all the crayons, even the ugly brown one.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a favorite magazine. It was full of fun pictures and stories. He loved to look at it every day. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Tim wanted to show Sam his magazine, but he couldn't find it. He felt very sad. Tim and Sam looked all over the house. They looked under the bed, behind the sofa, and even in the kitchen. But they couldn't find the magazine. Tim's mom saw them looking and said, "Don't be foolish, boys. Keep looking and you will find it." Tim and Sam didn't know what she meant, but they kept looking. Finally, they went outside to play. They saw Tim's big brother, Ben, sitting under a tree. Ben was reading a magazine. Tim and Sam ran over to him. They saw that Ben had Tim's magazine! Ben said, "I found it on the floor and I wanted to pass it back to you, but I forgot." Tim was happy to have his magazine back and shared it with Sam. They all sat under the tree and looked at the fun pictures and stories together. From that day on, Tim made sure to keep his magazine in a safe place.

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there was a big mountain. On top of the mountain lived a kind bird named Blue. Blue had a very important job. He would deliver things to the people in the town. One day, Blue saw a shiny ball on the mountain. He knew that the shiny ball was special. He thought, "I must deliver this shiny ball to the town. Maybe it will make someone very happy." Blue picked up the shiny ball with his beak and flew down the mountain. He saw a little girl named Lily who looked sad. Blue gave the shiny ball to Lily. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Blue!" The shiny ball was magic, and it made Lily very happy. Blue knew that delivering the shiny ball was a good thing to do. And from that day on, Blue and Lily were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an urge to eat a big, red apple. He saw one up high in a tree. He tried to reach it, but he was too small. So, he asked his big sister, Sue, to help him. "Please, help me get the apple!" Tim said. Sue was nice and tall. She reached up and got the apple for Tim. But when she gave it to him, they saw that it was spoiled. It had a big, brown spot on it. The apple was starting to rot. Tim was sad. He really wanted to eat the apple. Sue tried to tell him, "It's not good to eat a spoiled apple. Let's find a new one." But Tim didn't listen. He took a big bite of the apple. It tasted yucky! Later, Tim got a tummy ache. He felt very sick. Sue told him, "I told you not to eat the spoiled apple." Tim learned his lesson. He should have listened to Sue. Next time, he would not eat a spoiled apple. But for now, Tim had a bad ending with a big tummy ache.
