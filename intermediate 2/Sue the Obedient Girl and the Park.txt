Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Obedient Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was an obedient girl named Sue. She loved to go outside and play. One day, her mom gave her a pretty veil to wear on her head. Sue went to the park with her veil. She met her friend, Tom. Tom said, "I like your veil, Sue. Be careful not to lose it." Sue nodded and they played together. As they played, the wind blew Sue's veil away. She was sad but remembered what Tom said. They looked and looked, and finally found the veil stuck in a tree. Sue was happy and thanked Tom for his help.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty white dove. The dove lived in a big tree near a small pond. The dove had a friend, a kind frog. They liked to play together every day. One day, the dove was flying high and saw a dirty ball in the pond. The dove wanted to help the frog clean the pond. So, the dove flew down and picked up the dirty ball with its beak. The dove then flew up and dropped the dirty ball far, far away. The frog was very happy. The frog said, "Thank you, dove!" The dove felt good for helping the frog. From that day on, they played in the clean pond and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a very important job to do. She had to take a delicate package to her grandma's house. She knew she had to be very careful with the package. It was a surprise for her grandma's birthday. As Lily walked to her grandma's house, she met her friend, Tim. Tim asked, "What's in the package, Lily?" Lily said, "It's a surprise for my grandma's birthday. I can't drop it because it's very delicate." Tim wanted to help Lily, so they walked to grandma's house together. When they got to grandma's house, they gave her the delicate package. Grandma opened it very carefully. Inside was a beautiful glass bird. Grandma was so happy and said, "Thank you, Lily and Tim! This is the best birthday surprise ever!" Lily and Tim smiled, happy they didn't drop the package and that grandma loved her gift.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and run after it. Sometimes they missed the ball and it rolled away. Then they had to chase it before it went too far. One time, the ball rolled past a big tree and into a distant corner of the park. Tom and Lily ran after it, but they did not see anyone else there. They saw the ball near a fence, but there was also a stranger there. The stranger was a big man with a beard and a hat. He was holding a bag and looking around. Tom and Lily stopped and looked at the stranger. They felt scared. They did not know who he was or what he wanted. They wanted to get their ball, but they did not want to go near him. They wished their mom or dad were with them. The stranger saw Tom and Lily and smiled. He said, "Hello, kids. Do you want your ball? Come here and I'll give it to you." He waved his hand and pointed to the ball. Tom and Lily did not move. They did not like his voice or his smile. They felt a cold wind and started to tremble. They wanted to run away, but they did not want to leave their ball. Then they heard a familiar voice. It was their mom. She had followed them and saw the stranger. She said, "Tom, Lily, come here right now. Leave the ball. It's not safe." She sounded angry and worried. Tom and Lily turned and ran to their mom. They hugged her and felt warm and safe. They told her about the stranger and the ball. She said, "It's OK, my loves. You did the right thing. The stranger was a bad man. He wanted to take you and your ball. He had no right to do that. You are very brave and smart. I'm proud of you." She took them by the hand and walked away from the distant corner. She said, "Let's go home and have some cookies. We can get a new ball later. The most important thing is that you are safe and happy." Tom and Lily nodded and smiled. They loved their mom and they were glad she was with them. They forgot about the stranger and the ball. They only thought about the cookies and the hugs.
