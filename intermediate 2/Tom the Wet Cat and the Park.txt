Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Wet Cat and the Park

One day, a wet cat named Tom went out to play. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. Tom wanted to help Sue feel happy. Tom had an idea. He found a message in a bottle. He said, "Sue, look! A message!" Sue smiled a little. She said, "What does it say?" Tom said, "It says there is a fun game at the park." Tom and Sue went to the park. But the grass was wet. Tom slipped on the wet grass. Sue laughed. They played the fun game together. Tom and Sue were happy.

Once upon a time, in a fair land, there lived a big cat and a small dog. They were good friends. One day, they had a meeting under a big tree. The big cat said, "I am tired. I want to lay down and sleep." The small dog said, "Let's lay on the soft grass and rest." So, the big cat and the small dog lay on the soft grass. They talked and laughed. They had a fun time at their meeting. Soon, the sun went down, and it was time for the big cat and the small dog to go home. They said goodbye and went to their homes. They were happy they had their meeting and looked forward to the next one.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful princess. Every day, she liked to sing sweet songs. One day, a thin cat stumbled across the princess singing in the palace garden. The king liked the singing so much that he invited the cat over for tea. The cat was so excited and thanked the king. The cat asked the princess, “Can you teach me how to sing like you?” The princess smiled and said, “Yes, of course. Just do what I do and try to copy my voice.” The cat tried and tried, but it couldn't sing as nicely as the princess. But the cat kept singing and the princess kept teaching. Finally, the cat could sing as nicely as the princess. They both sang and delighted the palace with their beautiful voices. The king praised them both, giving them treats and hugs!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to work in her mom's big kitchen. One day, Sally's mom said, "Sally, let's make a cake!" Sally was very happy and excited. She knew it would taste delicious. Sally and her mom worked hard to make the cake. They put in eggs, sugar, and flour. Then, they put the cake in the oven to bake. Sally could smell the cake baking. It smelled so good! She knew it would taste very good too. When the cake was ready, Sally and her mom took it out of the oven. They let it cool down. Then, they put sweet frosting on top. Sally took a big bite of the cake. It tasted just as delicious as she knew it would. She was so proud of the work she and her mom did to make the cake.

Once there was a naughty little girl called Anna. Anna liked playing in her garden and she liked playing tricks on her friends. One day Anna and her friends were playing in the garden when Anna came across a bottle of perfume. She sprayed the perfume on her friends, but it made them angry and sad. Anna's mum saw what she did, and told her not to do it again. Anna felt bad, but she wanted to make it right. So Anna and her mum went to the shop to pay for the perfume. They paid for it and Anna apologized to her friends. Everyone was happy again. From then on, Anna remembered to be careful with the things she played with. She never forgot how nice it felt to make things right by paying for the perfume.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big stand with lots of toys. Max wanted to get a toy, but he had no money. He looked at the toys and felt sad. A nice girl named Lily saw Max looking at the toys. She asked, "Do you want a toy?" Max nodded his head. Lily said, "I will get one for you." She used her money to buy a toy for Max. Max was very grateful. Max and Lily played with the toy together. They had a lot of fun. Max said, "Thank you for the toy." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome." They became good friends and played together every day.
