Category: Intermediate
Title: The Event and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big event in the park. All the kids were happy and excited. They played games, ate yummy food, and had a lot of fun. In the park, there was a little girl named Lily. She saw an old lady who needed help. The old lady had a big bag and it was very heavy. Lily wanted to make the old lady happy, so she went to help her. Lily took the big bag and carried it for the old lady. The old lady was very happy and thanked Lily. They walked together and enjoyed the event. And from that day on, Lily and the old lady became good friends.

---

One day, a smart cat found a pretty bracelet on the ground. The cat loved the bracelet and put it on its paw. The cat walked around, showing off the shiny bracelet to all its friends. The other animals liked the bracelet too and wanted one just like it. A naughty dog saw the cat with the bracelet and wanted it for himself. The dog tried to take the bracelet from the cat, but the cat did not want to give it up. The cat and the dog began to fight over the pretty bracelet, making a lot of noise. A big elephant saw the cat and the dog fighting and wanted to help. The elephant had an idea! It used its long nose to spray water on the cat and the dog. They stopped fighting and looked at the elephant. The elephant told them to share the bracelet and be friends. The cat and the dog agreed, and they played together with the pretty bracelet, happy to be friends again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a shy cat named Lily. Lily was very quiet and always tried to ignore the loud noises around her. She liked to hide under the big tree in the park. One sunny day, Lily met a friendly dog named Max. Max said, "Hi, Lily! Do you want to play with me?" Lily was scared at first, but she wanted to make a new friend. So, she said, "Yes, I want to play." Max and Lily played together all day. They ran, jumped, and chased each other. They had so much fun, they forgot all about the order to be home before dark. When the sun went down, they said goodbye and went back to their homes. From that day on, Lily was not so shy anymore, and she and Max became best friends.

---

Once upon a time there was a boy called Jonah. He was very gifted and could do lots of amazing things. He loved the landscape in his town, which changed every season. He liked to fly kites in the wind in the summer time, and he liked to sled in the snow in winter time. Jonah had a new friend, Sophie. She was very kind and gentle and they had lots of fun together in the landscape. One day, Sophie and Jonah had a really big surprise. They got married! They stood together in the lovely landscape and said "I do". Everyone was so happy for them. They had a big party and lots of friends came to see them. It was the most special day of their lives. They knew they would always have each other, no matter where they lived or what they did. They were best friends forever.

---

One day, a brave boy named Tim found a hammer. He wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look, Sam! I found a hammer." Sam looked at the hammer and had an idea. "Let's play a game. We can shoot rocks with the hammer!" So, they went outside and started to play. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came running up to them. The dog picked up the hammer in its mouth and ran away. Tim and Sam chased after the dog, laughing and shouting, "Come back, dog! That's our hammer!"

---

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They like to run and jump and slide. Lily sees a big green hill. She wants to climb it. "Look, Ben, a green hill! Let's go up!" she says. Ben follows her. He is not sure. The hill is very high and steep. They start to climb. Lily is fast and happy. Ben is slow and scared. "Come on, Ben, don't be a baby!" Lily says. Ben tries to catch up. He slips on the grass. He falls down the hill. He screams. Lily hears him. She turns around. She sees Ben rolling and crying. "Oh no, Ben!" she says. She runs down the hill. She helps Ben get up. He has a scratch on his knee. He is dirty and sad. "I'm sorry, Ben. Are you okay?" she says. Ben nods. He is still in pain. He is angry at Lily. "You are mean, Lily. You made me fall. I don't want to play with you anymore!" he says. Lily feels bad. She did not mean to hurt Ben. She wants to be his friend. "Please, Ben, don't be mad. I was just having fun. I didn't know you would fall. I'm sorry. Can we still play?" she says. Ben looks at her. He sees that she is sorry. He remembers that she is his friend. He sighs. He forgives her. "Okay, Lily. I'm not mad. But can we play something else? I don't like the green hill. It is too high and steep." he says. Lily smiles. She agrees. "Okay, Ben. Let's play something else. How about the swings? They are fun and safe." she says. They go to the swings. They push each other. They laugh and forget the green hill. They are happy again.
