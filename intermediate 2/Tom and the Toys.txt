Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Toys

Tom is a boy who likes to play with his toys. He has a lot of toys, but his favorite is a brown bear. He calls him Ted. Tom and Ted do everything together. They eat, sleep, read and play. One day, Tom sees a new toy in the store. It is a big red truck. It has wheels, lights and sounds. Tom wants the truck very much. He asks his mom if he can have it. His mom says no. She says he has enough toys and he should be happy with what he has. Tom is sad and angry. He does not want to be happy with what he has. He wants the truck. He thinks his mom is mean. He goes home and throws Ted on the floor. He does not want to play with Ted anymore. He only wants the truck. Ted is sad and scared. He does not know why Tom is mad at him. He loves Tom very much. He wants to play with Tom and make him happy. He looks at Tom with his big eyes and hopes he will pick him up. Tom stares at Ted on the floor. He sees his big eyes and his soft fur. He remembers how much fun they have together. He remembers how Ted hugs him when he is sad or scared. He feels sorry for Ted and for himself. He realizes he was wrong to be angry at his mom and to throw Ted away. He runs to Ted and picks him up. He hugs him and says he is sorry. He tells him he loves him and he is his best friend. Ted is happy and relieved. He hugs Tom back and says he loves him too. He is glad Tom is not mad anymore. He does not care about the truck. He only cares about Tom. Tom and Ted go to Tom's mom and say they are sorry. They tell her they love her and they are happy with what they have. Tom's mom smiles and hugs them. She says she loves them too and she is proud of them. She says they can play with the truck for a minute if they want. Tom and Ted say thank you, but they do not need the truck. They have each other and that is enough. They go to Tom's room and play with their toys. They are happy.

Once there was a family and they lived near a library. Every week they would visit the library and take out books. The library was always very calm, and the family would stay there for a long time. One day when they were there, the mother asked the children, "What should we do today?". Her son, Jack, shouted, "I want to look at the books!" The mother smiled and said, "Okay, let's explore and find some books. There are a lot of things we can do here in the library. It includes looking at books, reading stories, and making new friends." The family soon found some interesting books and set out to read them. They stayed in the library for the entire day, reading their books and exploring the different things it had to offer. When they were done, they were very satisfied and thanked the librarian before heading back home.

Once upon a time, there was a pigeon named Pete. Pete lived in a big tree with his friends. One day, Pete felt uncomfortable in his nest. He wanted to make it better. Pete asked his friend, Sam the squirrel, "Can you help me prepare a new nest?" Sam said, "Yes, Pete! Let's find soft things to make it cozy." They looked for leaves, grass, and feathers. They worked together to make the new nest. When it was done, Pete felt very happy and comfortable. Pete and Sam played and laughed in the tree. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Amy wanted to help her mom cook. So, her mom let her put some food on the stove. Amy was very happy to help. Amy's mom said, "Amy, can you put the pot on the stove, please?" Amy said, "Yes, mom!" and she put the pot on the stove. Then, her mom asked her to put some water in the pot. Amy did it and felt proud. When the food was ready, Amy's mom said, "Thank you for helping me, Amy. You did a great job!" Amy smiled and said, "You're welcome, mom. I love to help!" They sat down and ate the yummy food that they cooked together. Amy was happy that she could help her mom with the available things at home.
