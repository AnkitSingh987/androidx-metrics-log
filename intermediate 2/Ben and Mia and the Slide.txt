Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Slide

Ben and Mia like to play in the park. They run, jump, swing and climb. But their favorite thing is to slide. They slide down the big slide, the small slide, the twisty slide and the bumpy slide. They laugh and shout as they go fast and feel the wind. One day, the sun is very hot. Ben and Mia are thirsty and tired. They want to slide, but the slides are hot too. They touch the slides and say "ouch". They look for a cool slide, but they cannot find one. They are sad and bored. Then, they see a new slide. It is not metal, but plastic. It is not hot, but wet. It is a water slide. They see other kids sliding down and splashing in a pool. They think it looks fun and harmless. They ask their mom if they can try it. She says yes and gives them towels. Ben and Mia run to the water slide. They wait for their turn and then sit on the top. They hold hands and smile. They slide down the water slide and feel the water on their skin. They scream and giggle as they go fast and feel the splash. They get out of the pool and want to slide again. They are happy and cool. They love the water slide.

One day, a little boy named Tim found some gum. He did not know what it was, so he asked his mom, "What is this?" His mom said, "That is gum, Tim. You can chew it, but do not swallow it." Tim was very happy to try the gum. He thought it was fun to chew. He saw his friend, Sam, and wanted to share the fun. Tim said to Sam, "Look, I have gum! I can shoot it out of my mouth!" Tim tried to shoot the gum, but it did not go far. Sam laughed and said, "Let me try." Sam did not know how to shoot gum either. They both tried again and again, but the gum just stuck to their hands. They were both ignorant about how to shoot gum. They laughed and had a lot of fun trying. In the end, Tim and Sam decided to ask their moms for help. Their moms showed them how to blow bubbles with the gum instead of shooting it. They loved blowing bubbles and had a great day playing together.

Mama needed to supply some food for dinner. She went to the store and looked around. She wanted to buy something special. Finally, she saw a big red tomato! Mama was careful when she picked it up. She put it in her basket and bought it. When Mama got home, she cut up the tomato for dinner. It was so tasty! Everyone loved it. Mama was glad she was careful when she chose the tomato. Now they had a delicious dinner. The next day, Mama went back to the store. She bought lots of tomatoes. Now they had a supply of tomatoes! They could have them for dinner all week. Mama was happy she was careful when shopping. Now they had enough tomatoes for the whole week.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick hedge. Many animals lived in the hedge. One day, a small bird got lost in the hedge. The bird was sad and scared. She wanted to find her way out. A kind rabbit saw the bird and wanted to help. The rabbit knew the hedge very well. He led the bird through the hedge. They saw many animals on their way. The bird started to feel happy again. At the end, they found the way out of the hedge. The bird was very happy and thanked the rabbit. The rabbit felt good helping the bird. The animals in the hedge learned that being kind and helping others is important. And they all lived happily together.

One day, a small girl named Lily went for a walk in the park. She loved to observe the leaves and the birds. Today, she saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. The leaves were turning yellow and red. It was fall. Lily saw a little bird on the tree. The bird looked nervous. Lily asked the bird, "Why are you nervous, little bird?" The bird said, "I am scared to fly. I see the other birds fly, but I think I will fall." Lily wanted to help the bird. She said, "You can do it! Just try and you will fly." The bird looked at Lily and took a deep breath. Then, it tried to fly. To their surprise, the bird flew up high and did not fall. The bird was so happy and thanked Lily. The other birds in the park saw this and clapped their wings. They all learned that sometimes, you just need a friend to believe in you. And that day, Lily and the bird became the best of friends. The moral of the story is that with a little help and encouragement, we can face our fears and do anything.
