Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a harmless dog who loved to play and run around. He lived in a big house with his best friend, a girl named Jane. Jane loved Spot very much and they were always together. One day, Jane had to take an exam at school. She was very sad because she could not take Spot with her. Spot did not like to be alone, so he tried to escape from the house to find Jane. He pushed the door with his little paws and it opened. Spot was so happy that he could go outside and look for Jane. Spot ran and ran, looking for Jane. He asked other animals if they had seen her, but no one knew where she was. Finally, a kind bird told Spot that Jane was at school. Spot was so excited and ran as fast as he could to find the school. When he got there, he saw Jane through a window. She was doing her exam and looked very focused. Spot knew that he should not disturb her, so he waited patiently outside. When Jane finished her exam, she came out and saw her best friend Spot. They were both so happy to see each other and went home together, ready for more adventures.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a funny boy named Tim. He loved to joke and make people laugh. Tim had a pair of pants that he thought were very funny. They were big, red, and had yellow dots. He liked to wear them when he told his jokes. One sunny day, Tim went to a party. He was polite and said "hi" to everyone. He wanted to tell a joke to make his friends laugh. He put on his funny pants and stood in the middle of the room. Tim started to tell his joke, but something unexpected happened. As Tim was talking, a little dog ran into the room. The dog saw Tim's funny pants and thought they were a toy. The dog jumped up and grabbed the pants with his teeth. Tim's friends laughed, but not because of his joke. They laughed because the dog was playing with Tim's pants. Tim didn't mind. He was happy that he made his friends laugh, even if it was not the way he planned.

One day, a little dog named Max was walking in the park. Max saw a big tree with a lot of numbers on it. The numbers were crazy! They went up, down, and all around the tree. Max wanted to know what the numbers meant. Max asked his friend, a cat named Luna, if she knew what the numbers were for. Luna did not know. She said, "Let's ask our friend, the wise owl." So they went to find the owl, who lived in a big tree house. When they found the owl, Max and Luna asked about the crazy numbers on the tree. The owl sighed and said, "Those numbers are for a game. You have to jump from one number to the next. If you make it to the top, you win a prize!" Max and Luna were excited to play the game and try to win the prize.

Once there was a big dog named Bill. He loved to stretch whenever he woke up. He'd stretch to the sky, and he'd stretch to the ground. One day, as Bill was stretching, he found a switch. It was a big, open switch, so he wondered what it did. He asked his friend, a cat named Tim. "What does this switch do?" Bill asked. "Why don't you press it and find out?" Tim said. So, Bill pressed the switch and the tree behind them opened. There was a big tree house inside, with plenty of room for Bill and Tim to play. They ran inside, and they had so much fun! Bill and Tim spent the day in the tree house, and when it got dark, they pressed the switch to close the tree. The switch was open, and the tree was shut tight again. From then on, Bill and Tim had the best adventures!
