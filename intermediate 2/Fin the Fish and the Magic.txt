Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day long. One day, while swimming, he saw a shiny thing. He wanted to collect it, so he swam closer. "Hi, I am Fin," said the fish. "I want to collect you." The shiny thing did not talk. It was a toy fish. Fin felt sad and miserable. He wanted a friend who could talk and swim with him. Suddenly, the toy fish began to move and talk. "Hi, I am Tim, the magic toy fish," it said. Fin was so happy, and they swam together. They collected more shiny things and had many fun days.

Tom and Mia like to play outside. They like to run and jump and laugh. They see many things that are fun. They see flowers and birds and bugs. One day, they see a big black cloud in the sky. The cloud is very dark and scary. It covers the sunshine and makes everything cold. Tom and Mia do not like the cloud. They want the sunshine back. They have an idea. They find a big stick and a basket. They run to the field where the flowers grow. They pick many flowers that are yellow and orange and red. They put the flowers in the basket. They make a big pile of flowers. They take the stick and the basket and go to the hill. They climb to the top of the hill. They see the black cloud. They wave the stick and shout, "Go away, cloud! Go away! We want the sunshine!" They throw the flowers in the air. The flowers fly like little suns. They shine and sparkle and dance. The black cloud sees the flowers. It is surprised and scared. It moves away and lets the sunshine through. Tom and Mia are happy. They see the sunshine again. They hug and smile and cheer. They play with the flowers and the stick. They have fun in the sun. They say, "We love the sunshine! We love the flowers! We love each other!"

Lily was a little girl who loved to draw. She had many crayons and papers and she made pictures of everything she saw. She drew her mommy, her brother, her dog, and her toys. She also drew things she imagined, like a big castle, a flying dragon, and a rainbow. One day, she wanted to draw her daddy, who was at work. She remembered how he looked, with his brown hair, his blue eyes, and his smile. She took a yellow crayon and drew a circle for his face. Then she took a brown crayon and drew his hair. Then she took a blue crayon and drew his eyes. Then she took a red crayon and drew his mouth. She was very proud of her picture and she showed it to her mommy. Mommy looked at the picture and smiled. She said, "That's a very nice picture, Lily. You are a very good artist. Who is this?" Lily said, "It's daddy, of course. Don't you recognize him?" Mommy said, "Yes, I do, but he looks a little different. Do you know why?" Lily said, "No, why?" Mommy said, "Because you used yellow for his face. Daddy's face is not yellow, it's peach. Like this." She took a peach crayon and showed it to Lily. Lily looked at the peach crayon and then at her picture. She said, "Oh, I see. I used the wrong color. I'm sorry, mommy. I didn't mean to make daddy look funny." Mommy said, "It's okay, Lily. You don't have to be sorry. Daddy will still love your picture, because he loves you. And he knows that you love him, too. And he will think that your picture is incredible, because you made it with your heart." Lily smiled and hugged her mommy. She said, "Thank you, mommy. You are right. Daddy will like my picture, even if it's yellow. And I will tell him that I love him very much. And I love you, too, mommy. You are the best mommy in the world." Mommy said, "I love you, too, Lily. You are the best daughter in the world. And you make me very happy with your drawings." The moral of the story is: Love is more important than perfection.
