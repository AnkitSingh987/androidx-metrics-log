Category: Intermediate
Title: Alex and the Park

Alex and his mom were out for a walk. It was a sunny day, and Alex was enjoying the fresh air and the smells of the outdoors. "Let's stop at the park, Mommy," said Alex. "Okay," said his mom. "Let's take a little rest." They stopped at the park, and Alex ran to the swings. He soon got bored and looked around. He saw a bin that had been left by the playground. "What's in there?" asked Alex. "I think it's full of tasty snacks," said his mom. She went to the bin, opened it, and took out a couple of treats. "Yum," said Alex. "Delay them for me, please." His mom laughed. "Delay, Alex?" she said. "That's a funny word to use." But she did as he asked and they shared the delicious treats. After a short while, Alex was ready to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Kitty. She liked to watch everything that was happening around her. One day, she saw a rail near her house. She was curious about it and decided to check it out. When Kitty got to the rail, she saw a big hose. She wanted to play with it. She put her paw on the hose and it started to spray water. The water went up, down, and all around. Kitty was having so much fun! Kitty played with the hose for a long time. She sprayed water on the grass, on the trees, and even on herself. She was wet and happy. After a while, she got tired and went back home. Kitty had a great day playing with the hose and the rail.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She was excited because she had a big, red apple to take with her. She wanted to share it with her friend, Max. When Lily got to the park, she saw Max playing with a ball. She ran over to him and said, "Max, I have an apple! Let's share it!" Max was excited too, and he said, "Yes, I love apples!" Lily and Max sat down under a big tree. Lily took the apple and broke it into two pieces. They both ate their half of the apple, and it was very yummy. After they finished, they played with the ball together, happy and full from their tasty snack.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a generous little girl named Mindy. She loved to help her friends and share her toys. One day, Mindy found a beautiful red flower called a poppy. Mindy wanted to show the poppy to her friends. She picked up the poppy and ran to her friends. But, something unexpected happened. The poppy began to talk! "Please, put me back. I am a magic poppy and I can make a wish come true," said the poppy. Mindy was very surprised, but she put the poppy back in the ground. She wished for more toys to share with her friends. The magic poppy made her wish come true. Mindy and her friends played happily ever after, and the poppy stayed in the garden, waiting for the next kind child to find it.

Max wanted to put his wheel on his toy truck. So he dragged it to the front door and tried to reach it. He couldn't, it was too high! Max hit the ground in frustration. "Why can't I do it?!" he shouted. At that moment his Dad came in the room and saw the situation. He knelt down, picked up the wheel and gently said to Max, "It's ok, I'll put it on the truck for you." Max smiled and nodded. His Dad put the wheel on the truck and asked Max, "Now can you make the truck go?" Max thought for a moment and said, "Yes! I can do it!" He grabbed the truck and pulled it as hard as he could. It moved a little but then stopped. Max's Dad smiled and said, "You have to push one of the wheels to make it move. Here, let me show you." Dad pushed the wheel, and the truck started to move! Max was very happy. The end.
