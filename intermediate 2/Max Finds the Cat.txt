Category: Intermediate
Title: Max Finds the Cat

One day, a big, loud dog named Max went for a walk. He liked to run and play in the park. Max had a lot of fun and made many friends. After playing, he felt tired and hungry. So, Max thought it was time to come home. On his way home, he saw a small cat. The cat was sad and lost. Max wanted to help the cat. He asked the cat to come with him. They walked and walked, looking for the cat's home. Max was a good friend to the cat. Finally, they found the cat's home. The cat was very happy. But when they got there, Max saw something funny. The cat's home was his home too! The cat was his new sister! Max and the cat were now family. They went inside their home and had a big, loud party to celebrate.

Ben and Lily were adventurous kids. They liked to explore new places and have fun. One day, they found a big bike in the garage. It was shiny and red, and had two seats. "Let's ride the bike to town!" Ben said. "We can see the shops and the park and the people." "OK!" Lily agreed. "But we have to be careful. The bike is very fast and the road is very busy." They put on their helmets and got on the bike. Ben pedaled hard and Lily held on tight. The bike started to speed along the road. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. "Wow, this is so cool!" Lily shouted. "Look, there is the town!" Ben pointed. "We can see the big clock and the fountain and the library." They reached the town and slowed down. They parked the bike near a bench and got off. They walked around and looked at the different things. They saw a bakery with yummy cakes, a toy store with colorful toys, a flower shop with pretty flowers, and a bookstore with many books. "Can we buy something?" Lily asked. "Sure, we have some money in our pockets." Ben said. "What do you want?" "I want a cake!" Lily said. "I want a book!" Ben said. They went to the bakery and the bookstore and bought what they wanted. They sat on the bench and ate the cake and read the book. They were happy and full. "That was a great adventure!" Ben said. "Yes, it was!" Lily said. "But we should go back home soon. Mom and Dad will be worried." They got on the bike and speed back to their house. They put the bike in the garage and ran inside. They hugged their parents and told them about their adventure. "We're sorry we went without telling you." Ben said. "We're glad you're safe and had fun." Mom said. "But next time, please ask us first." Dad said. "OK, we will." Lily said. They smiled and kissed their parents. They were adventurous kids, but they were also good kids.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a tough and strong boy. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim found a big helmet in his room. He did not know where it came from. He was very surprised. Tim put on the helmet and went outside to show his friends. He saw his friend Sue playing with a ball. "Look, Sue!" said Tim. "I found a helmet in my room!" Sue looked at the helmet and was surprised too. "Wow, Tim! That's a big helmet!" she said. Tim and Sue played with the helmet all day. They pretended to be superheroes and had lots of fun. When it was time for Tim to go home, he took off the helmet and put it in his room. He was happy to have a new toy to play with and share with his friends.

The sun was high in the sky and two children stood outside their house. They looked around and gazed at their town. They saw a playground, houses and some fields. Everything was peaceful. The children went to explore the town and see what they could find. They strolled a long the sidewalk and looked around every corner. They saw people walking, animals grazing and butterflies flitting between the flowers. They giggled at the sights and kept going until they reached the park. They sat on a bench near the pond to take in the peacefulness of their town. They watched the birds floating across the water and enjoyed the warm sunshine. Finally, they looked at each other and smiled before continuing their adventure.
