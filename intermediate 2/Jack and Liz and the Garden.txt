Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Liz and the Garden

One day, two children - Jack and Liz - were playing in the garden. They were feeling very jolly and having a lot of fun. Suddenly, it started to rain. The two children ran for the house. But when Jack turned the door handle, it wouldn't open. Jack asked Liz, "What can we do?" Liz replied, "Let's try again!" So they both turned the handle once more but unfortunately it still didn't open. The children looked out of the window and saw that the rain had turned the garden into a big puddle! They were very sad. Eventually, the day was over, and the jolly children had to go home without playing in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. He lived with his mom, dad, and little sister, Sue. They all loved each other very much. One day, Tim and Sue were playing with their toys. Sue broke her favorite toy and started to cry. Tim felt sad for his sister and wanted to help. He told Sue not to worry and that he would fix it. But Sue was still very sad and told their mom that Tim broke her toy. Their mom came and asked Tim if he broke the toy. Tim knew the truth that it was Sue who broke it, but he didn't want her to be punished. So, he said he did it. Mom told Tim that he should be more careful with toys and not to break them again. Tim felt sad but knew he did the right thing by being kind to his sister. Later, Sue found out the truth and felt sorry for blaming Tim. She gave him a big hug and said sorry. From that day on, they both promised to always tell the truth and be kind to each other.

One day, a tidy little girl named Lily went to the garden. She saw a big squash. She wanted to pick it up, but it was too heavy. Lily was sad and started to cry. A kind bird heard Lily cry and flew down. The bird said, "Why do you cry, little girl?" Lily said, "I want the big squash, but it is too heavy for me." The kind bird wanted to help Lily. The bird called all his friends. Many birds came and they all picked up the squash together. They carried it to Lily's home. Lily was so happy and said, "Thank you, kind birds!" Then, the squash opened and many fun toys came out. It was a big surprise! Lily and the birds played with the toys all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was three years old and she loved to learn new things. One day, Jane wanted to eat a meal, so she went to the kitchen. She found some humble ingredients and she wanted to learn how to prepare a meal. Jane asked her father: "Daddy, can you teach me how to cook a meal?" Her father smiled and said: "Yes, of course, honey. Let's go to the kitchen and I'll show you how to prepare a humble meal". So the father and the daughter went to the kitchen and he taught her how to prepare a delicious and humble meal. When the meal was ready, Jane and her father both ate it and it was delicious. Jane felt so proud that she had learned something new. From that day on, Jane always tried to learn new things, and she was always happy to eat her humble meals.

One day, a little bird was worried. He did not know how to fly. His mom and dad seemed to fly so easy. He wanted to fly like them. The little bird saw a big tree and thought it had wisdom. He went to the tree and asked, "How can I fly?" The big tree said, "Just jump and flap your wings." The little bird tried, but he fell down. He was sad. He thought the tree did not have wisdom. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny bug came to the little bird and said, "You can fly, but you need to believe in yourself." The little bird was surprised. He did not think a tiny bug could help him. But he tried again. This time, he believed he could fly. And he did! The little bird was happy. He learned that wisdom can come from anyone, even a tiny bug.
