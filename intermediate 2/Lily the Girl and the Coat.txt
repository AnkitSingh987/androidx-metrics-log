Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily liked to play outside, but today was a gloomy day. The sky was gray, and it looked like it would rain soon. Lily's mom said it was time to dress up. Lily put on her pretty dress, her warm coat, and her rain boots. She was ready to go outside and play, even on the gloomy day. Before going out, Lily's mom gave her a small bottle of oil to take with her. Lily went outside and saw her friend Tom. Tom's bike was making a loud noise. Lily knew that the oil could help make the noise go away. She put some oil on Tom's bike, and the noise stopped. They were both very happy and played together all day, even on the gloomy day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Lily. She loved to eat spicy food. She would eat spicy food for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. One day, Lily went to the park to play. She saw a group of children talking in a different language. They were laughing and having fun. Lily wanted to join them, but she didn't understand their language. Lily went home and asked her mom to teach her the new language. Her mom said, "If you eat a spicy pepper, you will learn the language." Lily ate the spicy pepper and felt a warm feeling in her mouth. She went back to the park and joined the children. Now, she could understand and speak their language. They all played together and had a great time.

Lily and Ben like to play in the park. They run and jump and slide on the grass. One day, they see a big hill with a lot of flowers. They want to climb the hill and smell the flowers. They hold hands and start to go up the hill. But the hill is very steep and the grass is very wet. Lily and Ben slip and fall on their bottoms. They laugh and try again. But they slip and fall again and again. They are about to give up when they hear a loud voice. "Hello, little ones. Do you need some help?" They look up and see a giant. He is very tall and strong, but he has a kind smile and a lovely beard. He wears a big hat and a long coat. Lily and Ben are scared at first, but the giant is very friendly. He picks them up and puts them on his shoulders. He walks up the hill with them and shows them the flowers. They are very pretty and smell very sweet. Lily and Ben thank the giant and give him a hug. The giant says, "You are very welcome. I like to help people who love flowers. They make me happy. Do you want to see more?" Lily and Ben nod and smile. The giant takes them to his home, where he has a big garden with many kinds of flowers. They have a lot of fun with the giant and his flowers. They are very glad they met him.

Once upon a time, there was a great king. He lived in a big castle with his people. The king wanted to provide good things for his people, like food and toys. One day, a little boy came to see the king. The boy said, "King, we need help. There is a war in our land. We need you to make it stop." The king was sad and wanted to help the boy. The king went to the land with his big army. They stopped the war and made the people happy. The king provided food and toys for everyone. The little boy thanked the king and said, "You are a great king. Thank you for helping us."

Once upon a time, there was a big, red car. It liked to eat. It ate leaves, grass, and small sticks. The car was very nosy. It liked to know what everyone was doing. One day, the car saw a little bird. The bird was eating a worm. The car wanted to eat the worm too. So, it went to the bird and tried to eat the worm. But the worm was too small for the car to eat. Then, something unexpected happened. The worm turned into a big, juicy apple. The car was so happy! It could eat the apple now. The car and the bird became friends and shared the apple. They had a great time together.
