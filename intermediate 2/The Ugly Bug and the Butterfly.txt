Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ugly Bug and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was an ugly bug. The bug lived underground. The bug wanted to improve and become a beautiful bug. One day, the bug met a pretty butterfly. The butterfly said, "You can be beautiful too! Just try to improve yourself and believe in yourself." The ugly bug wanted to follow the butterfly's advice. The bug worked hard every day to improve. The bug ate good food, cleaned its underground home, and tried to be kind to others. One day, the bug became a beautiful butterfly too! The conflict was over, and the ugly bug was now a beautiful butterfly. They both lived happily ever after.

Lily liked to write in her orange diary every day. She wrote about her toys, her friends, her dreams and her secrets. She kept her diary under her pillow and never let anyone see it. One day, her big brother Tom came into her room. He was bored and wanted to play with Lily. He saw the orange diary on her bed and picked it up. He opened it and started to read. "Hey, what are you doing?" Lily shouted. She ran to Tom and tried to grab her diary. "That's mine! Give it back!" Tom laughed and held the diary high. He read some of Lily's words out loud. "I love my pink teddy bear. I wish I had a pony. I hate broccoli. I have a crush on Ben from school." He made fun of Lily and teased her. Lily felt angry and sad. She cried and begged Tom to stop. She wanted her diary back. She wanted her secrets to be safe. But Tom did not listen. He kept reading and laughing. He did not care about Lily's feelings. He did not close the diary. He did not give it back. He was a mean brother. He hurt Lily's heart. He ruined her diary. He made her day bad.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big blue sea. One day, while swimming, he found a cheap toy boat. He wanted to play with it, but he didn't know how. Fin swam to his friend, Crab. "Crab, look what I found!" said Fin. "It's a cheap toy boat. Do you know how to play with it?" Crab looked at the boat and said, "I don't know, but let's try together." Fin and Crab played with the cheap toy boat all day. They pushed it with their lips and watched it float on the water. They had so much fun swimming and playing with their new toy. And from that day on, Fin and Crab were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pink cat named Kitty. Kitty went to the grocery store with her mom. They wanted to buy some yummy food for dinner. Kitty was very excited to help her mom pick out the food. At the store, Kitty saw a locked door. She was curious about what was behind it. She asked her mom, "Mom, what is behind the door?" Mom said, "I don't know, Kitty. Let's go find the key and unlock it together." They found the key and unlocked the door. When they opened the door, they saw a room full of toys! It was a surprise toy room in the grocery store. Kitty and her mom laughed and played with the toys. They had so much fun that they almost forgot to buy their dinner food. They picked up the food and went home, happy about their unexpected adventure.

One day, a bird named Bob was in his tree. Bob loved to sing. He sang all day. The sun was hot, and the sky was blue. Bob was happy. A big wind called Hurricane came to the town. The wind was very strong. The trees moved a lot. Bob was scared. He stopped singing. The town was very quiet. Bob's friend, Cat, came to visit. Cat said, "Why is it so quiet?" Bob said, "Hurricane is here. I am scared." Cat had an idea. "Let's sing a song to make the wind go away." They sang a happy song together. The wind heard the song and liked it. The wind stopped being strong and went away. The town was happy again.
