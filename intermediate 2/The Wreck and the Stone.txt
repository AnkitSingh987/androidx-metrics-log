Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wreck and the Stone

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big wreck. It was unknown how it happened. The wreck was a mess of broken things. The people in the town were sad. One day, a little girl named Lucy walked by the wreck. She saw a shiny thing inside it. She pulled it out and saw it was a pretty stone. She showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look what I found in the wreck!" she said. Tom looked at the stone and said, "Wow, this is a very special stone. It has great value!" They decided to clean up the wreck and find more valuable things. The town's people saw Lucy and Tom working hard and joined them. They all worked together and made the town beautiful again.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted goose. She was the most talented goose in the world. One day, she decided to go on an adventure. She wanted to explore the world around her. So she spread her wings and took off. She flew in circles, over meadows and rivers. She flew higher and higher, until she reached the clouds. She enjoyed flying in the sky for hours and hours. Eventually, she noticed a rainbow below her. "Wow!" she thought. "I should follow the rainbow!" She followed the rainbow for what felt like forever. Finally, she saw something ahead of her. It was a big, green field with a pond in the middle. She flew down and landed next to the pond. Just then, she saw another goose in the pond. She introduced herself, and the other goose said, "Hi! I'm Jack. Would you like to explore the field with me?" "Yes!" the gifted goose said. So the two of them explored the field. They chased each other, splashed at the pond, and rolled in the grass. The two geese had the best time together. And they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to skip and play with her friends. One day, Lily was skipping around the table in her house. Her mom was watching her and smiling. Lily's friend, Sam, came over to play. They wanted to play a game with the table. But they couldn't agree on what game to play. Lily wanted to play tea party, but Sam wanted to play cars. They both felt frustrated and didn't know what to do. Lily's mom said, "Why don't you both take turns? Lily can play tea party first, and then Sam can play cars." Lily and Sam liked that idea. They played tea party and then played cars. They both had fun and learned that taking turns makes everyone happy.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the attic. The attic was dull and dark. In the attic, Lucy found a small, sad bird. The bird could not fly. Lucy said, "I will save you, bird!" She took the bird to her room. She gave the bird food and water. The bird started to feel better. But Lucy's mom did not like birds in the house. She said, "Lucy, the bird must go!" Lucy was sad. She took the bird outside. The bird could fly now. It flew away, but it came back to visit Lucy every day.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play outside at night. They looked at the stars and the moon and asked many questions. "Why is the moon so big and bright?" Anna asked. "How far is it from us?" Ben wondered. Their dad heard them and came to join them. He smiled and said, "The moon is a big ball of rock that goes around the earth. It is very far from us, but we can see it because it reflects the light of the sun. Sometimes it is full and round, and sometimes it is only a slice or a curve." Anna and Ben nodded and asked more questions. "Can we go to the moon?" Anna asked. "What is on the moon?" Ben asked. Their dad said, "Some people have gone to the moon, but it is very hard and dangerous. They need a special rocket and a suit to protect them from the cold and the air. There is not much on the moon, only dust and rocks and craters. But it is very beautiful and quiet." Anna and Ben thought about it and said, "We want to go to the moon someday. We want to see it for ourselves. We are brave and curious." Their dad hugged them and said, "I am proud of you, my little explorers. You are brave and curious, and that is good. But you also need to be careful and smart, and listen to your parents and teachers. They can help you learn more about the moon and the world. And remember, the moon is always there, even when you can't see it. It is like a friend who watches over you at night." Anna and Ben smiled and said, "Thank you, dad. We love you and the moon. Good night, moon. Good night, dad." They went to bed and dreamed of the moon.
