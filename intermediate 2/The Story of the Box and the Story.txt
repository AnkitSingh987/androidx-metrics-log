Title: The Story of the Box and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a young boy who loved sending mail. He loved to send letters to his friends, his family and even strangers when he had the chance. One day, the boy saw a disgusting man walking down the street. He thought about sending mail to him, just for fun. The boy smiled as he imagined the surprise when the man opened the mysterious letter. So the boy put a letter in the mail box, addressed to the man. The boy was so excited that he ran to the man's house, to see his reaction when he read the letter. But when the boy got there, he noticed the man was very angry. He looked even more disgusting than before. "Why did you send this letter to me?" the man asked. The boy was scared and he did not answer. The man then said,"You should never send things through the mail without knowing what the consequences will be. This letter contained some terrible information that I would rather keep private." The boy was filled with regret and sadness. He realized he had made a big mistake. This story reminds us to think twice before we send something through the mail. We should always stay to our morals and be respectful of other people's privacy.

One day, a fat cat saw a wire. The wire was on the floor. The cat wanted to play with it. So, the cat moved near the wire. The wire was long and thin. A little boy saw the cat. He said, "Cat, do not play with the wire. It is not a toy." But the cat did not listen. The cat moved the wire with its paw. The wire moved back and forth. The little boy went away. The cat kept playing with the wire. Then, the wire got stuck on the cat's tail. The cat tried to move, but it could not. The cat was sad and scared. The boy did not come back to help. The cat stayed stuck with the wire.

Once upon a time, there was a curious youth named Tim. He liked to explore and learn new things. One day, he found a big tree with many branches. Tim thought the tree was very pretty. Tim saw a bird on one of the branches. The bird said, "Hello, I am Bob. I need help to cut a small branch for my nest. Can you help me, please?" Tim wanted to help the bird, so he found a stick to cut the branch. As Tim cut the branch, Bob the bird was very happy. They worked together to make the nest. After they finished, Bob said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim smiled and felt happy too. From that day on, Tim and Bob were best friends and had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy rod that he loved to play with. Tim would enjoy playing with his rod all day long. He would pretend to catch fish and have so much fun. One day, Tim felt miserable because he lost his rod. He looked everywhere but could not find it. He was very sad and did not know what to do. His mom saw him and tried to help, but they still could not find the rod. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's little sister, Sue, came into the room holding the rod! She had found it under the bed. Tim was so happy and gave Sue a big hug. They both played with the rod and enjoyed the rest of the day.

One day, a fish named Bob wanted to dive deep in the sea. He saw a big piece of food at the bottom. Bob was hungry, so he wanted to get the food. But, his mom said, "No, it's not safe down there." Bob was sad, but he still wanted the food. When his mom was not looking, he went to dive down. As he got close to the food, a big fish came out of a cave. Bob was scared. But, the big fish said, "Hi, I'm Sam! I will help you get the food." Sam and Bob became friends. They got the food and went back up. Bob's mom saw them and was happy that Bob was safe. From that day, Bob and Sam were best friends and always had fun diving together.
