Category: Intermediate
Title: Mary and Jake and the Picnic

Once upon a time there were two friends, Mary and Jake. Jake asked Mary, "Where are we going today?" Mary replied, "I want to go on a picnic!" Jake was so excited! The two friends went to the picnic spot and enjoyed some tasty snacks. As they were having their picnic, something mysterious happened. A magical fairy flew by and said, "It's time for your special day!" The friends had no idea what the fairy meant by that, so they asked her what she meant. The fairy said, "Do something that reminds you of a special memory!" Jake smiled and said, "I have an idea! Let's take some pictures of us having a picnic together." Mary agreed and the two friends ran off to take some photos of their picnic. They were so happy with the pictures they had taken and the fun they had on the picnic. The mysterious fairy had reminded them of something special that they would always remember.

Once upon a time, there was a reindeer. He lived in a big forest. He had many friends. One day, it started to rain. All the animals went to find a place to hide. The reindeer saw a big tree. He thought it was a good place to stay dry. Under the tree, there was a small bird. The bird was envious of the reindeer. The bird wanted to be big like the reindeer. The bird asked the reindeer to share the tree. The reindeer said no. The reindeer wanted the tree all for himself. The rain got harder. The bird was very sad. The bird flew away to find another place to hide. Then, a big wind came. The big tree fell down. The reindeer was hurt. The bird was safe in a small bush. The reindeer learned that it is good to share with friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there was a lot of hay. The hay was soft and warm. Many animals lived in the barn. They liked to sleep on the hay. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the barn. She saw a pretty red dress. She wanted to wear the dress. Lucy put on the dress and felt very happy. Lucy played with the animals in the barn. They all had fun together. They jumped on the hay and laughed. Lucy and the animals loved the big red barn. They played there every day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in his yard. He had a rake and a zip bag. One day, it was very dry outside. Tim wanted to play with his rake and zip bag. Tim went to the yard and used the rake to make a big pile of dry leaves. He was so happy. Then, he put the dry leaves in the zip bag. He closed the bag and ran around with it. But, Tim did not see a big hole in the ground. He tripped and fell. The zip bag opened and the dry leaves flew away. Tim was sad. He learned to be careful when playing with his rake and zip bag.

One day, a little boy named Sam went to the park. He saw a dog and wanted to play with it. The dog wagged its tail, and Sam knew it was happy. Sam threw a ball, and the dog ran after it. The dog's motion was fast, and it caught the ball. Sam clapped his hands and said, "Good dog!" The dog's owner came over and said, "Thank you for playing with my dog. He is grateful for new friends." Sam smiled and said, "I like playing with dogs. They are fun!" Sam and the dog played for a long time. When it was time to go home, Sam waved goodbye to the dog and its owner. He was happy to have made new friends at the park.

Once upon a time there was a new puppy named Daisy. She liked to explore her new house and play outside in the big garden. One day, when Daisy was playing outside she noticed something strange — a giant shadow. It was dark, and Daisy was scared! She ran back inside the house and shut the door. Daisy's mom came to see what was going on and saw the shadow too. She said, "Don't worry Daisy, it's just a new friend!" Daisy was still scared, but she opened the door to take a closer look. It was a big, friendly cat! Daisy was so happy. She said, "Hi, I'm Daisy, what's your name?" The cat replied, "Hi Daisy, I'm Shadow! Would you like to be friends?" Daisy smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that very much!". They both ran and played together all day, under the new shadow. The end.
