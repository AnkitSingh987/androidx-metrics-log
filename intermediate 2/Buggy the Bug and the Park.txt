Category: Intermediate
Title: Buggy the Bug and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Buggy. Buggy loved to move and play all day. One day, Buggy saw a big stage in the park. He wanted to go on the stage and dance, but he was scared. Buggy saw his friend, Birdy, and said, "Birdy, I want to dance on the stage, but I am scared." Birdy smiled and said, "Don't worry, Buggy. I will help you. We can dance together." So, Buggy and Birdy went on the stage and started to dance. They moved and jumped, and everyone clapped for them. Buggy was very happy. After they finished dancing, Buggy thanked Birdy for helping him. The moral of the story is that friends help each other when they are scared. With a good friend by your side, you can do anything, even dance on a big stage.

Once upon a time, there was a big, friendly dog named Buddy. Buddy lived in a house with a family. The family had a baby girl named Lily. Buddy and Lily were best friends. They played together all day long. One day, Buddy heard a strange noise coming from the attic. He was scared, but he wanted to protect Lily. So, he went to the attic to see what was making the noise. When he opened the door, he saw a tiny mouse. The mouse was scared too. "Hi, mouse," said Buddy. "I'm Buddy. I won't hurt you." "Hi, Buddy," said the mouse. "I'm just looking for some food. I didn't mean to scare you." Buddy and the mouse became friends. They played together in the attic. Buddy wasn't scared of the attic anymore. And Lily was happy because her friend Buddy was brave and friendly.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys in the yard. One day, they find a big trunk under a tree. The trunk is old and brown. It has a lock on it. "Wow, what is in the trunk?" Tom asks. "I don't know. Maybe treasure or candy or books," Sam says. They try to open the trunk, but it is too hard. The lock is tight and the trunk is heavy. "We need a key to unlock it," Tom says. "Or we need to be strong and break it," Sam says. They look around for a key or a tool, but they find nothing. They push and pull and kick the trunk, but it does not move. They are sad and tired. They sit on the grass and sigh. "Maybe we can ask Dad for help," Tom says. "Or Mom. She is strong too," Sam says. They run to the house and find their parents. They tell them about the trunk and ask them to come. Dad and Mom follow them to the yard. They see the trunk and smile. "Where did you find this?" Dad asks. "Under the tree. Can you open it?" Tom asks. "Let me see. I think I have a key for this," Dad says. He takes out a key from his pocket and puts it in the lock. He turns it and the lock clicks. He lifts the lid and the trunk opens. Tom and Sam gasp. Inside the trunk, there are many things. There are clothes and hats and shoes and bags. There are toys and games and books and puzzles. There are pictures and letters and cards and stamps. "These are some of our old things. We used to play with them when we were kids," Mom says. "Really? Can we play with them too?" Sam asks. "Sure, why not? But be careful and gentle. They are old and fragile," Dad says. Tom and Sam are happy. They thank their parents and start to explore the trunk. They try on the clothes and hats and shoes and bags. They play with the toys and games and books and puzzles. They look at the pictures and letters and cards and stamps. They have a lot of fun with the trunk. They unlock a treasure of memories.

Once upon a time, there was a big wardrobe in a room. It was impressive because it was very tall and had many colors. Inside the wardrobe, there were many clothes and a little sack. One day, a small cat found the sack inside the wardrobe. The cat played with the sack, pushing it around the room. The cat was very happy and had a lot of fun with the sack. After some time, the cat got tired and went to sleep. The cat dreamed of the impressive wardrobe and the fun sack. When the cat woke up, it went back to the wardrobe and played with the sack again. The cat and the sack became best friends, and they played together every day.
