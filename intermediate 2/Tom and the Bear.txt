Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Bear

Tom liked to play chess with his dad. He had a big chess board with black and white pieces. He knew how to move the king, the queen, the horse, the castle, the bishop and the pawn. He liked to say "checkmate" when he won. One day, his dad had to go away for work. He said he would be back soon, but Tom missed him a lot. He wished he could play chess with him. His dad said he would mail him a letter and a surprise. Tom waited for the mail every day. One morning, he heard a knock on the door. It was the mailman. He had a big box for Tom. Tom was very excited. He opened the box and saw a letter from his dad. He also saw something furry and brown. It was a teddy bear. But not just any teddy bear. It was a chess teddy bear. It had a chess board on its tummy and chess pieces in its paws. Tom smiled. He hugged the teddy bear and read the letter. "Dear Tom, I miss you very much. I hope you like your chess teddy bear. He is your new chess friend. You can play chess with him anytime you want. He knows all the rules and moves. He is very smart and kind. He will keep you company until I come back. I love you, Dad." Tom was very happy. He thanked the mailman and ran to his room. He put the chess teddy bear on his bed and set up the chess board. He said hello to the teddy bear and asked him if he wanted to play. The teddy bear nodded and moved a pawn. Tom moved a pawn too. They started to play chess. Tom felt like his dad was with him. He said "checkmate" and the teddy bear smiled. They hugged and played again. Tom loved his chess teddy bear. He was the best surprise ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She loved to spin around, so one day she went to the market. When she arrived the market was peaceful and she spun around and around until she was too dizzy to walk. Suddenly, a big man walked up to her and said: "Hey, what do you think you're doing?" Lucy was scared and she said: "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to make a mess." But the big man wasn't happy and he grabbed her arm and spun her around and around. Lucy was so scared that she screamed out for her mom but the big man just kept spinning her until she was too dizzy to move. Finally the big man let her go and she ran away from the market as fast as she could. But even though she was safe, Lucy was so scared that she never went back to the market again.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Bounce. Bounce loved to jump up and down all day. One day, Bounce saw a bottle on the ground. Bounce wanted to play with the bottle. Bounce tried to jump on the bottle, but it was hard. Bounce got frustrated. Bounce tried again and again, but the bottle would not let Bounce play with it. Bounce was very sad. Then, a big wind came and blew the bottle away. Bounce was happy again. Bounce could jump up and down without the bottle. And Bounce played all day with other balls and toys.

One day, a wealthy man named Bob was walking in a park. He saw a beautiful view of the sun, trees, and flowers. He thought, "I want to write a story about this view." Bob met a girl named Sue. He told her, "I want to write a story about the pretty view." Sue said, "I can help you write the story!" They sat under a tree and started to write. The sun was very bright, and they felt happy. As they wrote, the sky became dark. They knew something was going to happen. Suddenly, it started to rain. Bob and Sue ran to a big tree to stay dry. They laughed and said, "We will finish our story another day." They knew they would come back to the park to write about the beautiful view again.
