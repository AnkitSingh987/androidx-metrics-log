Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bird

One day, a girl named Amy found a trumpet. She wanted to play it very loud. She went to her friend Tom, who was deaf. Amy said, "Tom, look at my trumpet!" Tom could not hear, but he could see the trumpet. He was happy for Amy. They went to a big hill to play the trumpet. Tom wanted to help Amy, so he held the trumpet for her. Amy started to play the trumpet, but no sound came out. They were confused. Then, a big bird flew down and settled on the trumpet. The bird said, "I can help you make it loud!" The bird blew into the trumpet, and it made a very loud sound. Amy and Tom were so happy and they played the trumpet with the bird all day.

Once upon a time, there was a polite man named Tom. Tom wanted to build something nice for his home. He thought a bookshelf would be perfect. Tom got some wood and started to build the bookshelf. He used a hammer and nails to make it strong. Tom worked very hard and soon, the bookshelf was done. Tom put his books on the new bookshelf. It looked very nice in his home. His friends came to visit and they all loved the bookshelf too. Tom was very happy and proud of his work.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a troubled cat that was lost. The cat was walking in a zigzag. Tim wanted to help the cat find its way home. Tim asked the cat, "Why are you walking like that?" The cat said, "I am lost and I don't know where my home is." Tim felt sad for the cat and wanted to help. They walked together to find the cat's home. As they walked, the cat started to complain. "I am so tired and hungry," said the cat. Tim gave the cat some food from his pocket. They kept walking and soon they found the cat's home. The cat was very happy and thanked Tim for his help. Tim felt good that he could help his new friend.

One day, a little dog got an invitation. It was a card that said, "Come to my party!" The dog was very happy. He wanted to rush to the party because he was impatient. The dog put on his hat and ran to the party. On the way, he saw his friend, the cat. The dog said, "I am going to a party. Do you want to come?" The cat said, "Yes, I want to come too!" They both rushed to the party. But they were too impatient. They did not look where they were going. They fell into a big hole. The party went on without them. They were sad because they missed the fun.

One day, a little bird got an invitation to a big party. All his friends were going. He was very happy. At the party, they had lots of yummy food. But one bird, named Billy, was very spoiled. He wanted all the food for himself. He said, "This is all mine!" The little bird felt sad, but then he had an idea. He said to Billy, "Let's play a game. We can soar high in the sky and see who can go the highest!" Billy agreed, thinking he could win. They both soared high into the sky. The little bird went very high and won the game. Billy was not happy, but he learned that being spoiled is not fun. They all shared the food and had a great time at the party.

One day, a little boy went to play outside. He saw a big tree with an adorable raven sitting on a branch. The boy wanted to be friends with the raven, but the raven was scared and flew away. The boy was sad and wanted to find the raven. The boy looked everywhere for the raven. He looked under rocks, behind bushes, and up in the sky. He did not find the raven. The boy was very sad. Then, he saw something shiny on the ground. He picked it up and discovered it was a pretty stone. The boy held the pretty stone and wished for the raven to come back. Suddenly, the adorable raven flew down and landed on the boy's shoulder. The raven liked the shiny stone too! The boy and the raven became best friends and played together all day.
