Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Ball

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to throw the ball to each other and catch it. They were cheerful and laughed a lot. "Tom, catch!" Lily said, as she released the ball from her hand. The ball flew in the air towards Tom. But Tom did not catch the ball. The ball went past him and rolled into the street. Tom ran after the ball, without looking for cars. "Tom, stop!" Lily shouted. "There are cars! It is not safe!" But Tom did not hear Lily. He wanted to get the ball. He did not see the big truck that was coming fast. The truck did not see Tom either. The truck hit Tom with a loud noise. Tom fell to the ground and did not move. The ball bounced away. Lily screamed and ran to Tom. She cried and hugged him. She hoped he would wake up. But he did not. The truck driver got out of his truck. He was scared and sorry. He called for help. But it was too late. Lily and Tom's mom and dad came to the park. They saw Tom on the ground. They were very sad and angry. They wished they had not let Tom and Lily go to the park alone. They wished they had given them lunch before they left. They wished they had their son back. But they did not.

One day, a small cat named Kitty was very angry. She did not want to play with her friends. Kitty's friends were sad. They did not know what to do. Kitty's friend, a big dog named Doggy, had an idea. He thought Kitty needed a massage. Doggy tried to give Kitty a massage, but he struggled. His paws were too big and it was hard for him. Then, a small mouse named Tiny came to help. Tiny gave Kitty a massage with his little paws. Kitty felt better and was not angry anymore. Kitty, Doggy, and Tiny played together and had fun. The moral value of the story is that friends can help each other feel better when they are sad or angry.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to draw lines on paper. One day, he drew a long line that was broken in the middle. He showed it to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I drew a line, but it's broken." Mom looked at the broken line and said, "That's okay, Tom. Sometimes lines can be broken, and that's okay too. Let's see if we can fix it." Just then, something unexpected happened. The broken line on the paper started to wiggle and move! Tom and his mom watched as the line fixed itself and became a whole line again. They were both very surprised. Tom looked at his mom and said, "Wow, Mom! The line fixed itself!" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tom. Sometimes, when things are broken, they can fix themselves if we give them time." From that day on, Tom learned that it's okay if things are broken sometimes, and he kept drawing lines and making new friends with his magical paper.

Once upon a time there was a lonely cow. She wanted to touch something soft and smooth. But, she did not know what to do. She looked around and saw a big tub of cream. She thought it might be nice and soft. So, she decided to touch it. She put her hoof in the cream and it felt so nice! She moved her hoof around in the cream and it felt so smooth. She felt much less lonely. Every day she came back to the tub of cream and touched it. The cow was so happy when she touched the cream. She even made some new friends who also loved to touch the cream. They touched it together and were so happy!

Once upon a time, a little dog named Buddy liked to wander. He loved to go outside and play. He liked to see the sunrise every morning. It made him very happy. One day, Buddy found a heavy ball in the grass. He tried to pick it up with his mouth, but it was too big. Buddy pushed the ball with his nose. He wanted to show his friends. As Buddy pushed the ball, it started to roll faster and faster. Suddenly, the heavy ball broke open! A lot of fun, colorful balls came out. Buddy was so surprised and happy. Now, he had many balls to play with and share with his friends.
