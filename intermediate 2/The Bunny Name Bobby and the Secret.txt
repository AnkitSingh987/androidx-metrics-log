Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bunny Name Bobby and the Secret

Once there was a bunny name Bobby. He liked to crawl around the garden. One day, Bobby heard a noise coming from under the bushes. He was very scared but he was also curious so he started to crawl closer. When he got there, he saw a little mouse. The mouse said "Hello! I'm Charlotte. I'm lost and I'm looking for a secret." Bobby said "I don't know any secrets. I'm scared". Charlotte said "don't worry Bobby, I won't hurt you. I just want to know where my secret is". Bobby thought for a while. He knew a secret spot in the garden. He said to Charlotte "There is a secret spot under the slide. Maybe your secret is there". Charlotte said "Thank you Bobby! I will go and look". And so, Bobby watched as Charlotte crawled away to the secret spot. He knew she would find her secret. Maybe, one day, he would find his own secret too!

Once upon a time, in a sunny beach town, there lived a wise old fish named Fin. Fin loved to surf on the big waves. He was very fast and could speed through the water. One day, a little fish named Bubbles came to Fin. Bubbles said, "Fin, I want to surf like you. Can you teach me?" Fin smiled and said, "Of course, Bubbles. Let's go to the water and I will show you how to surf." Fin and Bubbles went to the water. Fin showed Bubbles how to speed up and ride the waves. Bubbles tried and tried, and soon she could surf too. They both had fun surfing together. From that day on, Bubbles and Fin were the best of friends, and they would surf together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small, humble house, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside with her cat, Fluffy. One day, Lily and Fluffy went on an adventure in their backyard. As they walked, they saw a big tree with many cobwebs hanging from its branches. Lily and Fluffy thought the cobwebs looked pretty, so they got closer to look at them. Fluffy tried to reach for the cobwebs, but they were too high. Lily found a big stick and tried to help Fluffy. She used the stick to touch the cobweb. But as she did, her foot began to slip on the wet grass. Lily fell down and laughed, and Fluffy laughed too. They both had fun on their adventure, even if they didn't get the cobweb. And they learned that sometimes, it's okay to slip and fall, as long as you get back up and keep having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a happy boy who loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tom's mom called him in for a meal. She had made his favorite food, and he was very excited. As Tom sat down to eat, he saw his friend, Tim, walking by. Tom opened the window and shouted, "Hey Tim, come join me for a meal!" Tim was hungry too, so he came inside and they shared the food together. They both enjoyed the meal and had a great time. After they finished eating, Tom's mom said, "It's nice to share your meal with others." Tom and Tim learned that sharing makes everyone happy. From that day on, they always shared their food and toys with each other and their friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Anna liked to play with her toys in her room. She had dolls, cars, blocks and books. She had a lot of fun making stories with them. But sometimes she got bored and wanted to see what her mom and dad did in their rooms. One day, when her mom was cooking and her dad was working on his computer, Anna decided to sneak into their rooms and discover what they had. She opened the door to her mom's room and saw a big box on the bed. She climbed on the bed and opened the box. Inside, she found a lot of shiny things that looked like necklaces and rings. She thought they were pretty and wanted to try them on. She put some of the shiny things around her neck and on her fingers. She felt like a princess. She wanted to see how she looked in the mirror. She got off the bed and walked to the mirror. But she did not see that one of the shiny things was stuck on the bed sheet. As she walked, she pulled the bed sheet with her. The box fell off the bed and all the shiny things spilled on the floor. Some of them broke and some of them rolled under the bed. Anna heard a loud noise and got scared. She ran out of the room and closed the door. She hoped her mom would not notice what she did. She went to her dad's room and opened the door. She saw a big desk with a lot of papers and a machine that had buttons and a screen. She wondered what her dad did with them. She thought it was his job. She walked to the desk and looked at the papers. They had a lot of words and numbers that she did not understand. She thought they were boring. She wanted to play with the machine. She touched the buttons and saw the screen change. She thought it was fun. She pressed more buttons and saw different colors and shapes on the screen. She did not know that she was deleting and changing her dad's work. She heard her dad's voice from the kitchen. He said he was coming to his room. Anna got scared again. She ran out of the room and closed the door. She hoped her dad would not notice what she did. She went back to her room and hid under her bed. She felt sorry for what she did. She wished she did not play with her mom's and dad's things. Her mom and dad came to their rooms and saw the mess. They were very angry and sad. They knew it was Anna who did it. They went to her room and found her under the bed. They asked her why she did it. Anna said she was sorry and that she just wanted to discover what they had. Her mom and dad said they loved her, but they were very disappointed. They said she had to learn to respect their things and not touch them without permission. They said she had to help them clean up and fix what she broke. They said she had to stay in her room for the rest of the day and not play with her toys. Anna felt very bad. She cried and said she was sorry again. She did not like the bad ending of her story. She wished she had played with her toys instead.
