Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bird

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They were very bored because they always walked on the same path. Tim saw a big cliff and thought it would be fun to go there. So, they walked to the cliff. At the cliff, Tim and Spot met a bird. The bird said, "This cliff is not safe. You should keep away." But Tim and Spot did not listen. They thought the bird was just being boring. So, they went closer to the edge of the cliff to see what was there. Tim and Spot got too close to the edge, and they both fell down the cliff. They did not have a good ending. If only they had listened to the bird and kept away from the cliff, they would have been safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big, open farm. On the farm, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a pet dog. The dog was named Bob. Tom and Bob loved to play together. One day, Tom saw a big, shiny car. A man got out of the car. The man was rich. He had a lot of luxury things. The man said, "Hello, I am Mr. Rich. I like your farm. Can I look around?" Tom said, "Yes, you can look." Tom and Mr. Rich walked around the farm. They saw many animals. They saw cows, pigs, and chickens. Tom called Bob to come with them. Bob came running. Mr. Rich liked Bob. They all had a fun day on the farm.

Sara loves to paint with her pink brush. She makes big flowers and stars on the paper. She is very happy and proud of her art. But then, she spills some water on the table. The water makes her paper wet and messy. Sara is sad and angry. She cries and says, "No, no, no! My art is ruined!" Mom hears Sara and comes to see what is wrong. She hugs Sara and says, "Don't worry, sweetie. It's okay. We can fix it." She takes a cloth and wipes the water from the table and the paper. She says, "Look, your art is still beautiful. The water made some new colors and shapes. You are very lucky and creative." Sara looks at her paper and sees that Mom is right. The water did not ruin her art, but made it different and interesting. She smiles and says, "Thank you, Mom. You are right. I am lucky and creative. I like my art." She gives Mom a big kiss and a hug. Mom says, "You're welcome, Sara. I love you and your art. You are a wonderful painter." She hangs Sara's paper on the fridge and says, "Let's go and have some cookies and milk. You deserve a treat." Sara nods and says, "Yes, please. I love cookies and milk." She holds Mom's hand and they go to the kitchen. Sara is happy and proud again.

One day a ship went out to test the sea. The waves were harsh and the ship rocked from side to side. But the ship kept going, no matter how hard the sea tried to stop it. Suddenly, a giant wave came up and pushed the ship up higher and higher. The ship crashed back down, but the captain and the crew stayed safe. Then the ship finished testing the sea and sailed back home. Everyone was happy the sea was so harsh.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It loved to encourage the flowers to grow and the birds to sing. The sun was friends with a loud bird named Chirpy. Chirpy liked to sing all day long. One day, Chirpy was singing very loud near a big tree. The sun was shining and encouraging Chirpy to sing even louder. Suddenly, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and blew Chirpy high up into the sky. As Chirpy was flying high, he saw a big cloud. The cloud was sad because it could not make any sound. Chirpy had an idea. He asked the sun to encourage the cloud to try making a sound. The sun agreed and encouraged the cloud. To everyone's surprise, the cloud made a loud thunder sound! The cloud was so happy that it started to rain. Chirpy and the cloud became friends, and they all lived happily ever after.
