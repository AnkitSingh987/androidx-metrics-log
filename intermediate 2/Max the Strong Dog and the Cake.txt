Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Strong Dog and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends. One day, Max heard about a big party. He was very happy and wanted to go to the party with his friends. At the party, Max saw many other animals. There was a cat, a bird, and even a fish! Max and his friends played and ate yummy food. They were having a lot of fun. But then, Max saw a big cake on a table. He wanted to eat the cake. Max tried to get the cake, but he was not tall enough. He jumped and jumped, but he could not reach it. Then, Max had an idea. He would slap the table with his strong paw to make the cake fall. Max slapped the table, but the cake did not fall. Instead, the table broke and all the food fell on the ground. The party was ruined, and Max felt very sad.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a nice leopard. The nice leopard had many friends. One day, the nice leopard met a small bird that was sad. "Hello, small bird. Why are you sad?" asked the nice leopard. "I want to fly high, but I am too small," said the small bird. The nice leopard had an idea. He told the small bird to sit on his back. Then, the nice leopard started to run very fast. He could not fly, but he could drive the small bird through the forest. The small bird was happy and said, "Thank you, nice leopard!" The other animals in the forest saw the nice leopard and the small bird. They learned that helping each other can make everyone happy. And they all became better friends.

One day, a duck named Bob wanted to decorate his home by the river. He was worried that it was not pretty. Bob had a friend, a fish named Tim. Tim lived in the river and wanted to help Bob. They started to decorate Bob's home with leaves and flowers. They were having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard and took away all the leaves and flowers. Bob and Tim were sad, but they learned a lesson. They learned that it is not important to have a pretty home. It is more important to have good friends and be happy. And they played together by the river, happy with what they had.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy tube. He liked to play with the tube all day. He would roll it, throw it, and even slip it on his arm. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He took his tube with him. He was very patient, waiting for his turn to play on the slide. When it was his turn, he put the tube at the top of the slide and let it slip down. Tim laughed and clapped as the tube slipped down the slide. He ran to the bottom, picked up the tube, and climbed back up to do it again. Tim and his tube had so much fun at the park. They played until the sun went down, and then it was time to go home.

One day, a cat named Tom wanted to balance on a big wall. He was a proud cat and loved to show his friends what he could do. He climbed up the wall and started to walk very carefully. Tom was happy that he could balance so well. On the other side of the wall, there was a lot of traffic. Many cars were going fast and making a lot of noise. Tom was not scared, he kept walking and balancing on the wall. His friends watched him and clapped their hands. When Tom got to the end of the wall, he jumped down. His friends ran to him and hugged him. They were very proud of Tom and his balancing act. Tom smiled and knew he did a great job. They all went home, happy and excited about their day.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball named Bounce. Bounce loved to play with his friends. One sunny day, Bounce met a friend named March. March was a little toy soldier who liked to walk in a straight line. Bounce and March decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could go the farthest. Bounce said, "Let's have a race!" March replied, "Okay, I will try my best." They started the race. Bounce went boing, boing, boing, while March walked step by step. Bounce and March were very competitive, but they were also having fun. In the end, Bounce won the race. Bounce said, "Good job, March! You did great!" March smiled and said, "Thank you, Bounce! I had fun!" From that day on, Bounce and March became best friends. They played together every day and always had a great time.
