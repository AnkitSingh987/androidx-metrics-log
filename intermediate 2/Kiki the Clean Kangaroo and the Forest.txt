Category: Intermediate
Title: Kiki the Clean Kangaroo and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a clean kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki loved to jump and play all day. She had many friends in the forest. They liked to play with her too. One sunny day, Kiki's friend, a little bird, urged her to join a race. The race was for all the animals in the forest. Kiki was excited and wanted to win. All her friends cheered for her. The race began, and Kiki hopped as fast as she could. She passed the other animals and crossed the finish line first. Kiki was so happy, and her friends were proud of her. They all celebrated her big win together.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat with soft fur. The cat lived in a house with a kind girl. The girl was generous and shared her toys with the cat. They played together every day. One day, the cat saw a small mouse. The mouse had a frown on its face. The cat wanted to help the mouse. The cat shared the girl's toys with the mouse. The mouse started to smile. The girl, the cat, and the mouse became good friends. They played together all the time. The girl's generous heart made everyone happy. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. The sky was gray, but Tim did not mind. He knew that it was going to be a fine day. He saw his friend Sue and went to her. Sue had an umbrella with her. Tim did not know what it was. He asked Sue to explain. Sue said, "This is an umbrella. It helps us stay dry when it rains." Tim liked the umbrella. It was big and colorful. Soon, it started to rain. Tim and Sue opened the umbrella and stood under it. They were happy and dry. The rain stopped and they closed the umbrella. They played all day and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Bob. Bob lived near a big pond. He loved to jump and play with his friends. One day, Bob heard a sound. It was a happy sound. He followed the sound and found his friend, the duck. The duck was having a party. He saw his friends playing and having fun. Bob wanted to join the fun. The duck saw Bob and said, "Bob, I invite you to my party." Bob was very happy. He joined the party and played with his friends. They all had a great time. And they lived happily ever after.

Charlie was a lonely little boy. He was walking in the park and saw some other kids talking about a race. He wanted to join in but he didn't know what to say. He was feeling a little shy. The kids asked Charlie if he wanted to join their race. He was so excited. Charlie nodded yes and the other kids cheered. They discussed the rules of the race and then they lined up. Charlie felt very brave and excited. He ran as fast as he could, and he beat all the other kids. He was the winner of the race. The other kids were surprised and very happy for Charlie. Charlie was very proud of himself. He smiled and thanked the other kids for letting him join the race. Now Charlie had some friends and he wasn't so lonely anymore.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. He had a pupil, a 3 year old child, that came over for a visit. The old man said to the child, "Let's take a walk and listen to the birds." The child smiled and said, "Yes, I'd like to listen to the birds!" The old man and the child walked outside. It was hot. The child said, "Oh, it's hot out here." The old man said, "Yes, it's hot, but I promise you'll have fun." They walked around and listened. They heard the birds singing. The child said, "I love to listen to the birds." The old man smiled and said, "Me too!" They kept walking around, listening to the birds, until it was time for the child to go home. It had been a fun day listening.
