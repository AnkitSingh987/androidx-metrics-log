Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat apples. One day, his mom told him to behave and play nicely with his friends. Tim and his friends went outside to play. It was a hot day, and they were very thirsty. Tim saw a big apple tree with many red apples on it. He had an idea. Tim and his friends picked the apples from the tree. They shared the apples with each other. Everyone was happy and behaved well. They had a great time playing together on the hot day, and they all enjoyed the yummy apples.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Lucky. Lucky loved to play in the park with his friends. One day, while they were playing, Lucky saw a cat stuck in a tree. Lucky asked his friends, "How can we rescue the cat?" They all thought about the question. One friend said, "We can use a ladder." So, they found a ladder and helped the cat get down from the tree. The cat was very happy and thanked Lucky and his friends. "You are very lucky to have such good friends," the cat said. They all played together in the park and had a great day. They were all very lucky to have each other.

---

One day, a dog and a cat went for a walk. The dog had a thin tail and the cat had a thin tail too. They were friends. As they walked, the dog said, "Stay by my heel!" The cat did not like this. The cat said, "No, I will not stay by your heel. I want to walk by myself." The dog was sad. He did not want to fight with the cat. So, he said, "Okay, we can walk together, but not too close." The cat was happy and they walked together without being too close. In the end, the dog and the cat walked and played together. They had fun and were still friends. They both learned that they could be friends even if they did not walk close to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a tall boy named Tom. He loved to spend his days playing with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Tom found a big, round ball. He wanted to play with it, but it was stuck around his waist. Tom tried to pull, push, and twist, but the ball would not come off. Tom's friends came to help him. They all tried to pull the ball off, but it was still stuck. Finally, they had an idea. They pushed Tom's waist through the ball, and it came off. Tom was so happy, and they all played with the ball together.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl in a gloomy forest. She was very brave and every day, she would go looking for food. One day, she found an onion in the bushes. She reached out and touched it; it was cold and slimy. Suddenly, she heard a noise. The little girl was scared. She grabbed the onion and ran away as fast as she could. She hid behind a big tree and peeked out. She saw a big, furry fox! She held the onion close and waited. The fox sniffed around and soon, it left the forest. She was relieved! The little girl smiled. She was brave enough to touch the onion and it saved her! She decided to keep the onion and use it to make soup. She took it home and cooked a delicious dish! The end.

One day, Johnny was walking through the park. It was a very long walk. As he walked, he looked around at the different sights. He especially liked the meadow. He could see the long grass and smell the sweet flowers. It was a very beautiful picture. He kept walking until he came to a huge lake. He had never seen anything like it. He stood there looking at the lake and hoping that maybe he could play in it. The next day, Johnny's mum took him back to the park. Johnny was excited. He remembered the lake and what he had hoped for. When they got to the lake, he was surprised to see that it was now open for swimming. Johnny couldn't believe his luck. He grabbed a towel and some swimming clothes and he was off! He spent the whole day swimming and playing in the water. Johnny had been so hopeful the day before, and now his wish had come true. He knew to never give up hope.
