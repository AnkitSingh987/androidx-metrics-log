Category: Intermediate
Title: Pete the Clumsy Pigeon and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy pigeon named Pete. He liked to sack around the park and pick up sticks, stones and leaves. One day, Pete was having a lot of fun sacking when he saw a shiny gold button lying on the ground. "Wow!" he squawked. He quickly flew over to have a closer look. Just then, a little boy walked out of the bushes. He smiled at Pete and said, “Hello birdie! Can I have my button back, please?” Pete shook his head and tucked the button safely away in his feathers. “No!” he said. “This button is mine!” The little boy sighed. “That’s ok,” he said. “You can keep the button, but I really wish you hadn’t been so clumsy.” Pete was sad that he had made the little boy unhappy. So, he gave the button back and said, “Here you go. It’s all yours now.” The little boy was delighted and the two of them became friends. Every day, Pete and his new friend would sack around the park, pick up sticks and have lots of fun.

One day, a pretty bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a small office. The office was very little and had many colors. The bird liked the office and wanted to live there. Inside the office, a man was working. The man saw the pretty bird and said, "Hello, bird! Do you want to live in my office?" The bird was happy and said, "Yes, I would like that!" The man and the bird became friends. They played together every day. One day, they found a box. The man said, "Let's bury the box in the ground." They dug a hole and put the box inside. Then, they covered it with dirt. The man and the bird were very happy and continued to play together in the pretty office.

Once there was a little orange race car. It was so special because it had never seen an opera before. The little car was so excited that it wanted to race over to see one. So it set off, driving down the road as fast as it could go. Suddenly it heard a voice, “Don’t go too fast, little car!” It was an old wise car, who stopped beside it on the road. The little orange car asked, “Why not?” The old car said, “You must go slowly, so you can enjoy the opera when you get there. It’s no fun to race through it!” The little orange car thought about this and decided the old car was right. So it drove slowly and enjoyed the opera when it arrived. What a fun day it had!

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy. He was very happy. But then, he saw a big tear in his toy. Tim felt sad and hurt. Tim went to his mom with the toy. He said, "Mom, my toy has a tear. It hurts me." Mom looked at the toy and saw the tear on its shoulder. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will fix it for you." Mom took a needle and thread. She fixed the tear on the toy's shoulder. Tim was happy again. He hugged his mom and said, "Thank you, Mom. My toy is not hurt anymore." Now, Tim and his toy could play together again. They had lots of fun, and there was no more hurt or tears.

One day, Mandy was in her room playing with a ball. She had a wonderful time and was having lots of fun. All of a sudden, she heard a loud crash. It came from outside. She went to the window and looked out. She saw her dad tying something to the wall of their house. It looked strange. Mandy was curious and wanted to find out what he was up to. She ran outside as fast as she could and asked her dad what he was doing. He said, "I'm tying some twigs with string. You'll see why soon!" But Mandy couldn't work it out. The next day, she heard a terrible noise coming from the wall. She ran outside and finally saw the reason why her dad had been tying twigs to the wall. There were hundreds of crows in the twigs, making a lot of noise. Mandy was horrified. Her dad smiled and said, "I tied the twigs here to keep the crows away. I knew they would be a terrible nuisance. Now they will stay away and we won't have to worry." Mandy smiled and thanked her dad for thinking ahead. She felt so proud of him!
