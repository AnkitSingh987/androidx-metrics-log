Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Leaves

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big tree and gazed at the pretty leaves. Max was happy to be outside. He wagged his tail and ran around the tree. Then, Max saw a bottle on the ground. He sniffed it and pushed it with his nose. The bottle rolled and made a fun sound. Max barked and played with the bottle. But Max was a naughty dog. He forgot to go home. His family looked for him and called his name. Max heard them and ran back. They were happy to see Max and hugged him. Max knew he should not be naughty again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a pretty red flower called Poppy. Poppy was not an ordinary flower. She could talk! Lily and Poppy started to discuss the weather. They talked about the sun and the rain. As they talked, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Poppy away. Lily was very sad. She looked for Poppy everywhere, but could not find her. She missed her new friend. Then, Lily had an idea. She went home and drew a picture of Poppy. She put it on her wall. Now, Lily could see Poppy every day and remember their fun talk. And one day, she found a new Poppy flower outside her house. Lily was very happy and knew that Poppy was still with her.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his big, red ball. One day, Tim was in the house, and he kicked the ball very hard. It made a noisy sound as it hit the chair. Tim's mom came into the room and said, "Tim, please do not kick the ball inside the house. It is too noisy, and you might break something." Tim looked at his mom and said, "Sorry, Mom. I will play outside." So, Tim went outside to play with his ball. He kicked it high into the sky and watched it fly through the air. He was very happy to play outside where he could be as noisy as he wanted. As the sun began to set, Tim's mom called him back inside. They sat in the cozy living room, and Tim told his mom all about his fun day playing with his ball. They both smiled and laughed as they shared their day together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, open field. In the field, there was a small, metal box. The box had a part that moved. This part was a door. The door could open and close. One day, a boy named Tim saw the box. He went to the box and said, "Hello, box! Can I open your door?" The box did not talk, but the door opened. Tim was happy. Inside the box, Tim found a toy car. The car had a metal part that was broken. Tim took the car home and fixed it. He played with the car all day. The car and the box became Tim's best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big puddle on the ground. He wanted to jump in it. So, Tim jumped in the puddle and water went everywhere. He was very happy. But then, his friend Sue came to him. She was wet and sad. Tim had struck her with the water from the puddle. "You made me wet!" said Sue. Tim looked at her and felt sorry. He didn't want to make his friend sad. "I'm sorry, Sue," said Tim. "I didn't mean to make you wet." Sue looked at Tim and smiled. "It's okay," she said. "Let's jump in the puddle together!" So, Tim and Sue jumped in the puddle and had a lot of fun. They were happy and friends again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the farm. She saw a small pony. The pony was very pretty. Lily wanted to play with the pony. She ran to the pony and gave it a big hug. Lily wanted to ride the pony, but the pony was dizzy. It walked around in circles. Lily thought it was funny. She laughed and clapped her hands. The pony stopped and looked at her. It was not dizzy anymore. Lily tried to ride the pony, but she slipped. She did not fall, because the pony caught her. The pony was not a real pony. It was a magic pony! The magic pony took Lily on a fun ride around the farm. They had a great day together.
