Category: Intermediate
Title: The Skeleton and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was an elderly skeleton that lived in a dark cave. One day, the skeleton heard a noise outside the cave. The noise was a little voice asking questions. The skeleton was curious, so it decided to answer. When the skeleton peeked out of the cave, it saw a small 3 year old girl. She asked the skeleton what it was doing in the cave. The skeleton answered that it was living there. Then the girl asked why the skeleton was living in a dark cave. The skeleton answered that it was a nice and quiet place to live. The little girl smiled and gave the skeleton a hug before she ran off. The skeleton watched the girl run away and smiling.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a favorite toy, a small mirror. She loved to carry it everywhere she went. The mirror was very special to her because it was a gift from her grandma. One day, Amy took her mirror to the park. She felt comfortable sitting on the soft grass, looking at her reflection. She made funny faces and laughed. Amy liked the way the sun made her hair shine in the mirror. As the sun began to set, Amy's mom called her to go home. Amy picked up her mirror and carried it back to their cozy house. When she got home, she put the mirror on her bed and gave it a gentle pat. Amy knew that her mirror would always be there for her, making her feel happy and comfortable.

Sally was a little girl. She wanted to sell something. She found a pan in her house. She wanted to see if she could sell it. She went outside with the pan. Everyone said it was very nice. She smiled. Sally saw a man. He asked her what she wanted to sell. Sally showed him the pan. The man said it was very pretty. He said he would buy it from Sally. She was very happy. The man gave Sally some money. He said it was very mild money that was special. Sally thanked the man and put the money in her pocket. Sally was very proud of herself. She had sold the pan and made some mild money. She was so proud of herself.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a creative boy named Tim. Tim liked to draw and make things with his toys. One day, he went to the park to play. At the park, Tim met a new friend named Sam. They played with toy cars and had a lot of fun. Then, Sam said, "Look, I have a toy gun!" Tim was excited and wanted to play with the gun too. But when Tim took the gun, it was not a toy. It was a real gun. Tim did not know what to do. He tried to put it down, but it was too late. The gun went off and made a loud noise. Tim and Sam got scared, and they both cried. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. Tim was a very obedient boy and always listened to his mom. So, he asked his mom if he could go on the slide. His mom said, "Yes, Tim, you can go on the slide." As Tim climbed up the slide, he saw a small crocodile at the bottom. The crocodile looked up at Tim and said, "Hi, I am Carl. Can I play with you?" Tim was surprised but happy to have a friend. He said, "Yes, Carl, let's slide together!" Tim and Carl took turns sliding down the slide. They laughed and had so much fun. The other kids at the park saw them and wanted to play too. Tim and Carl taught them how to slide with a crocodile. Everyone had a great day at the park, and Tim and Carl became best friends.

Lily was a kind goat who lived on a farm. She liked to play with her friends, the chickens, the pigs and the cows. She also liked to eat grass and hay, and drink water from the pond. One day, a new goat came to the farm. His name was Max, and he had big horns on his head. He looked very strong and proud. He said to Lily, "Hello, I am Max. I am the best goat on this farm. I can run faster, jump higher and weigh more than you. Do you want to see?" Lily was curious, but also a little scared. She said, "Okay, I will see. How can we know who weighs more?" Max said, "We can use the scale. It is a big thing that tells you how heavy you are. You stand on it, and it shows a number. The bigger the number, the heavier you are. Come, I will show you." They went to the barn, where the farmer kept the scale. Max went first, and stood on the scale. It showed 80. He said, "See, I weigh 80. That is a lot. Now, you try." Lily went next, and stood on the scale. It showed 60. She said, "I weigh 60. That is less than you." Max said, "Ha, I told you. I am the best goat. You are too small and weak. You should not play with me. Go away." Lily felt sad and hurt. She did not understand why Max was so mean. She ran away, and looked for her friends. She found them near the pond, and told them what happened. They said, "Don't worry, Lily. You are a kind goat. We like you. Max is not nice. He does not know how to be a friend. You can play with us." Lily smiled, and felt better. She said, "Thank you, friends. You are kind too. I am happy to play with you." They played together, and had fun. They did not care about the scale, or the horns, or the numbers. They cared about each other.
