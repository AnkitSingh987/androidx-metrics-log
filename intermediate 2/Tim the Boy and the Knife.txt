Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Knife

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He saw a big knife on the table. Tim knew he should not touch it, but he wanted to feel it. He pinched the knife and said "Ouch!" It hurt his finger. Tim's mom heard him and came into the kitchen. She saw Tim's finger and the knife. "Tim, did you touch the knife?" she asked. Tim felt guilty and said, "Yes, Mommy, I pinched it and it hurt." Mom hugged Tim and said, "I'm glad you told me the truth. Remember, knives are not for playing. They can hurt you." Tim nodded and promised not to touch knives again. From that day on, Tim always listened to his mom and stayed away from the things that could hurt him.

Once upon a time, there was a bee named Buzzy. Buzzy lived in a hive with his family. The hive was in a tree near a big flower field. Buzzy loved to go to the flower field every day. One day, Buzzy saw a tasty treat on the ground. It was a big, red apple. Buzzy wanted to eat the apple, but he was scared to go down. He thought about the apple all day and all night. The next day, Buzzy decided to go down and get the apple. He flew down from the hive and landed on the apple. But when Buzzy touched the apple, it was not tasty. It was bad and made Buzzy sick. Buzzy was sad and wished he had stayed in the hive with his family.

Lily was a little girl who loved to play with her cat, Mimi. Mimi had a long, fluffy tail that Lily liked to touch and hold. But Mimi did not like it when Lily picked her tail. She would hiss and scratch and run away. One day, Lily was playing with Mimi in the garden. She saw a butterfly and wanted to catch it. She ran after the butterfly, but it flew away. Then she saw Mimi's tail under a bush. She thought it was the butterfly and picked it. "Ow!" Mimi cried. "Let go of my tail, Lily!" Lily was surprised and scared. She let go of Mimi's tail and said, "Sorry, Mimi. I thought you were a butterfly." Mimi came out of the bush and looked at Lily. She was angry and hurt. She said, "No, I am not a butterfly. I am a cat. And you are a bad girl. You always pick my tail and make me sad." Lily felt sorry and worried. She did not want to make Mimi sad. She said, "I am not a bad girl, Mimi. I love you. I just want to play with you. Can we be friends again?" Mimi looked at Lily and saw that she was sincere. She said, "Okay, Lily. We can be friends again. But you have to promise me something. Never pick my tail again. It is not a toy. It is part of me." Lily nodded and said, "I promise, Mimi. I will never pick your tail again. I will be gentle and kind to you. Can I give you a hug?" Mimi smiled and said, "Yes, you can give me a hug. But be careful. I have claws." Lily hugged Mimi and said, "I love you, Mimi. You are my best friend." Mimi purred and said, "I love you too, Lily. You are my best friend too."

Once upon a time, there was a sunflower named Sunny. Sunny was very high and lived in a big garden. Sunny had many friends, like bees and butterflies. They all loved to play together. One day, a little bee named Buzz was flying around Sunny. Buzz said, "Sunny, I want to quit flying. I'm so tired." Sunny replied, "Buzz, don't quit! You can do it. Just rest for a while and then fly again." So Buzz took a break and sat on Sunny's leaf. After a while, Buzz felt better and said, "Thank you, Sunny. I won't quit now." Sunny smiled and said, "You're welcome, Buzz. Remember, never quit. Just take a break when you need it." From that day on, Buzz never quit flying. He and Sunny stayed good friends, and they played together every day in the big garden. And they all lived happily ever after.
