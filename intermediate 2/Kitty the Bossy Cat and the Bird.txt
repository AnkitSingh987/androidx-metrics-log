Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Bossy Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to watch videos on the TV. Her favorite video was about a big dog who could dance. Kitty would watch this video every day. One day, Kitty saw a little bird outside her window. The bird was cold and trembling. Kitty felt bad for the bird, so she opened the window and let the bird come inside to get warm. Kitty and the little bird became friends. They would watch the dancing dog video together. Kitty was not bossy with the bird, and they were both very happy. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was an old boat. The boat had a big anchor. The boat liked to create big waves in the water. One day, the old boat met a small fish. The fish said, "Hi, old boat! Can you help me? I need to find my family." The old boat said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's go find your family." They looked and looked for the fish's family. The old boat created big waves to help them go fast. The fish swam next to the boat. They found the fish's family near the big anchor. The fish was happy, and the old boat was happy too. They were all friends forever.

David was an impatient little boy. He was walking along the street with his mother looking for something fun to do. Suddenly, his eye caught something shiny on the ground. It was a penny! He was so excited, he squealed with joy. He ran to pick it up before his mom could stop him. David held the penny tightly in his hand and showed his mom. He asked her if he could keep the penny. His mom told him he could. David was so happy he couldn't believe his luck. They decided to go to the park. David was feeling very impatient because he wanted to play with his penny. As soon as they arrived, he rushed over to the slide and placed the penny at the bottom. He waited patiently as his mom watched. Then he dashed up the stairs and slide down the slide with the penny. He was very excited as the penny flew through the air. He picked it up, ran to the swings and played games with his penny all day. He couldn't have been happier!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was always alert and loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a magic wand under his bed. He was so happy and excited to play with it. Tim took the magic wand and waved it around. When he did, his toys came to life! They danced and played with Tim all day long. Tim smiled and laughed as they played together. As the sun went down, Tim knew it was time for bed. He took the magic wand and waved it one more time. His toys went back to being just toys. Tim put the magic wand under his bed, gave his toys a hug, and went to sleep. Tim knew that he would always have the magic wand to play with his toys again.

Once, there was a bald man. He had no hair on his head. His name was Bob. Bob was a very nice man. He liked to help people. One day, Bob found a big toy car. The toy car had a button that said "reverse." Bob pushed the button. The toy car went backward. It was fun to watch. Bob wanted to show his friend, Tim. Tim was a little boy. Tim liked the toy car too. They played together. They laughed and had a great time. Bob was happy to share his toy car with Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Sue. Sue loved to play games with her friends. One day, Sue and her friends played a game called "Find the Key." They had to find a key to lock a box. The one who locked the box would win the game. Sue searched high and low for the key. She looked under her bed and behind the door. Then, she heard her friend Tom say, "I found the key!" Sue ran to Tom and asked, "Can I help you lock the box?" Tom said, "Yes, let's lock the box together!" Sue and Tom went to the box and used the key to lock it. They were both happy and shared the win. They learned that when they work together, they can do anything. From that day on, Sue and Tom were always kind and helpful to each other.
