Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Wagon

John was a happy little boy. He liked to play in his red wagon. He liked to spread the toys around and make his wagon bigger. One day, John was being careless. He spread his toys all over the backyard. He was so excited, he ran around and pretended to be a racecar. His mom saw him and laughed. She said he was making a big mess. She told him to pick up the toys, but he just kept running around. John's mom smiled. She took the red wagon and put all the toys inside. John ran around the wagon, excited, and spread the toys around again. His mom laughed, loving John's creativity once again.

There once was an elderly man named Tom. He had a plan to make a big, strong house with steel. He wanted his house to be the best in town. One day, Tom met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, I am a talking bird. Can I help you with your plan?" Tom was surprised and said, "Yes, you can help me find steel for my house." They looked and looked for steel. But they found something different. It was a magic rock! The rock could make anything. Tom said, "I have a new plan. Let's use this magic rock to make my house." So, Tom and the bird used the magic rock to make the house. The house was not made of steel, but it was still the best house in town. Everyone was happy and surprised.

Once upon a time, there was a bear named Ben. Ben loved honey more than anything. Every day, he would look for honey in the big forest. One day, Ben found an incredible tree. The tree was very tall and had a lot of honey. Ben wanted to succeed in getting the honey. He tried to climb the tree, but it was not easy. Ben did not give up. He tried and tried until he finally reached the top. There, he found lots of honey. Ben was very happy. He succeeded in getting the honey and shared it with his friends. They all enjoyed the sweet honey and had a great day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a small box under a tree. Lily was very curious. She wanted to reveal what was inside the box. Lily opened the box and found a cute teddy bear. The teddy bear had a mild smile on its face. Lily loved the teddy bear and wanted to take it home. On her way home, Lily tripped and fell. She hurt her knee. A kind boy named Tim saw her and asked, "Are you okay?" Lily said, "Yes, but my knee hurts." Tim helped Lily stand up and they both walked home together. Lily was happy to have a new friend and a new teddy bear.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a regular bench near the big tree. Tim wanted to sit on the bench, but a big dog was there. The dog was very loud and scary. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim's mom saw him and came to help. She wanted to prevent the dog from scaring Tim. So, she clapped her hands and said, "Go away, dog!" The dog ran away, and Tim was happy. He could now sit on the bench and play with his toys. The moral of the story is that we should always ask for help when we need it. Tim's mom helped him, and he could enjoy his day at the park. We should not be afraid to ask for help from our family and friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a black cat named Max. They loved to play together in the yard. One sunny day, Max saw a big bird in the sky. He wanted to catch it. "Hurry, Max, hurry!" said Lily. Max ran fast, but the bird flew away. Max was sad. Lily wanted to make Max happy again. She found a piece of string and said, "Look, Max! Let's play!" Max saw the string and started to play. They played and laughed all day. Max was happy again.
