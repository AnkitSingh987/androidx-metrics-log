Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a large, friendly bear named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big forest with his best friend, a tiny bird named Titi. They loved to play together all day long. One sunny day, Titi said to Bobo, "I believe I can fly high up to the sky!" Bobo smiled and said, "I promise to help you, Titi!" So, they made a plan. Bobo would throw Titi up in the air, and Titi would flap her wings to fly high. Bobo picked up Titi gently and threw her up. Titi flapped her wings very hard, and she flew high up in the sky. She was so happy! Bobo was happy too, because he kept his promise to help his friend. From that day on, Titi believed she could fly high, and Bobo was always there to help her. They played together in the forest, and were the best of friends forever.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the woods. She was a bit fearful, but she wanted to be brave. As she walked, she saw a big chest under a tree. Lily was curious and wanted to see what was inside. Lily opened the chest and found a small, sad bird. The bird sighed and said, "I am lost and can't find my way home." Lily felt sorry for the bird and wanted to help. "Don't be sad, little bird. I will help you find your way home," she said. Together, they walked through the woods, looking for the bird's home. At last, they found it! The bird was so happy and thanked Lily. They hugged and said goodbye. Lily learned that even when you are fearful, you can still be brave and help others.

One day, a little girl named Mia got an invitation. It was for a party at her friend Tom's house. Mia was very happy and excited. She put on her favorite dress and went to the party. At the party, Mia saw a big cake. It looked yummy. But then, a naughty boy named Jack put his hands on the cake and rubbed it. The cake became messy and disgusting. Mia was sad and said, "Jack, that's not nice!" Tom heard Mia and had an idea. He ran to his room and came back with another cake. It was even bigger and more beautiful than the first one. Mia and Tom smiled at each other. The party was fun again, and everyone enjoyed the new cake.

One day, a little elephant named Ella was playing near the big trees. She saw a small, helpless bird on the ground. The bird was crying because it could not fly. Ella wanted to help the bird. Ella used her long trunk to pick up the bird. She was very careful not to hurt it. She set the bird on a soft pile of leaves. The bird was happy and stopped crying. Ella felt good that she could help. Ella and the bird became good friends. They played together every day. Ella used her trunk to help the bird fly, and the bird showed Ella how to sing. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy who felt very scared. He looked out of the window and saw his cat in his yard. He became very worried and shouted for help. His mom heard him. She rushed to the door and asked what was wrong. The boy said, “My cat is stuck in the yard!” His mom smiled and said, “Don’t worry. I will rescue your cat.” She ran out to the yard and was able to help the cat. The cat hopped out of the yard and was safe. The boy was very happy and no longer felt scared. He hugged his mom and thanked her for her help. He now knows his mom will always rescue him when he needs help. The end.

Anna liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Lily. Lily had a green dress and long hair. Anna liked to brush her hair and make her talk. One day, Anna's friend Ben came to visit. Ben liked to play with cars and trucks. He saw Lily on the floor and picked her up. "Can I play with this doll?" he asked Anna. Anna felt sad. She did not want to share Lily with Ben. She wanted to play with her alone. "No, that is my doll. You can play with the other dolls," she said. Ben frowned. He did not like the other dolls. They were too small and boring. He wanted to play with Lily. She had a green dress and long hair. "Please, Anna, let me play with Lily. I will be kind to her. I will not break her or make her dirty," he said. Anna thought for a moment. She liked Ben. He was her friend. He was kind and funny. He shared his cars and trucks with her. Maybe she could share Lily with him. "Okay, Ben, you can play with Lily. But you have to be careful. She is my favorite doll," she said. Ben smiled. He was happy. He thanked Anna and hugged her. He took Lily and made her talk. Anna and Ben played together. They made Lily and the cars and trucks have adventures. They had fun. They were good friends.
