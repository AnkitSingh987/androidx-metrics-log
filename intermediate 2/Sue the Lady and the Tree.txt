Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Lady and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a lady named Sue. Sue loved to lead her dog, Spot, on walks. Spot was a good dog who liked to play and run. One day, Sue and Spot went to the park. They saw a big tree. Sue said, "Spot, let's go sit under the tree." Spot wagged his tail and followed Sue. They sat under the tree and enjoyed the nice day. Sue had a tasty snack in her bag. She took out a big apple and said, "Yum, this apple is so tasty!" Spot looked at Sue, and she gave him a tasty treat too. They both ate their snacks and played together all day.

Mary was feeling sad. She wanted to go to a museum to cheer up, but everyone else was busy. She felt all alone. One day, her grandmother asked her, "Do you want to go to the museum?" Mary smiled and said, "Yes! Can I go?" "Of course," said her grandmother. So, Mary and her grandmother went to the museum. As soon as she stepped inside, Mary felt happy again. There were so many interesting things to see! She explored all the different galleries, looking at paintings and sculptures. When it was time to go home, Mary felt much better. She hugged her grandmother and said, "Thanks for taking me to the museum. I feel much happier." Her grandmother smiled and said, "I am so glad that you enjoyed it."

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys, but sometimes they fight. One day, Tim takes Sam's rare car and hides it under his bed. Sam is very sad and angry. He wants his car back. "Tim, where is my car?" Sam asks. "Give it to me now!" "I don't know. I don't have it," Tim lies. He smiles and thinks he is clever. Sam does not believe him. He starts to search for his car. He looks in the closet, in the drawers, in the boxes. He cannot find it. He is very upset. "Mom, Tim took my car and he won't tell me where it is!" Sam cries. He runs to their mom and tells her what happened. Mom is not happy. She goes to Tim's room and sees the car under his bed. She takes it and gives it to Sam. She scolds Tim and says he is very naughty. "Tim, you are not allowed to take Sam's car without asking. That is not nice. You have to say sorry to Sam and share your toys. And you have to punish for lying. You cannot watch TV or play games for one hour. Do you understand?" Mom says. Tim feels sorry and ashamed. He nods and says sorry to Sam and Mom. He hugs Sam and gives him his car. Sam forgives him and hugs him back. They decide to play together and share their toys. Mom smiles and hugs them both. She is proud of them for making up.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny flea named Fifi. Fifi lived on a big dog named Spot. Spot and Fifi were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Fifi felt embarrassed because she was so small. She wanted to increase her size to be as big as Spot. Fifi asked her friend, the wise old ant, "How can I increase my size? I want to be big like Spot." The wise old ant told her, "You don't need to be big to be important. Being small has its own special power." Fifi didn't understand, so the wise old ant explained, "You can jump very high and help Spot when he has an itch. You can also hide easily and play fun games with him." Fifi thought about it and realized that the wise old ant was right. She didn't need to increase her size to be a good friend to Spot. Fifi learned to be happy with who she was, and she and Spot continued to play and have fun together. The moral of the story is that you don't need to change who you are to be important or to be a good friend. Everyone has their own special qualities that make them unique and valuable.
