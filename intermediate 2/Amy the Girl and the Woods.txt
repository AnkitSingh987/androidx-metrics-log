Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Woods

Once upon a time there was a young girl named Amy who was walking through the woods. Suddenly she heard a noise and stopped in her tracks. She shivered as she looked around, unsure of what to do. Just then, she saw a small creature peeking out from behind a tree. Amy smiled, relieved to see it was only a little bunny. She said, “Hello!” The bunny replied, “What are you doing here? You’re too young to be walking around in the woods.” Amy held up a tiny watch that she had been given as a present. “I’m looking for my friend,” she said. “He’s been missing since yesterday.” The bunny was silent for a moment and then said, “Be careful! Don’t stay out too late, or you might shiver from the cold.” With that, it hopped away. Amy wandered around for a little bit longer, eventually finding her friend and happily running home in the fading light.

One day, a team of kids went to play outside. They were very excite to play together. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. They all wore colorful clothes, but one kid wore a pale shirt. The kids wanted to play a game. They had a big ball to kick. But the kid with the pale shirt was too shy to join. He sat on the grass and watched the others play. The team saw him and felt sad. They walked to the shy kid and asked him to play. The kid with the pale shirt was happy. He got up and joined the team. They all played and had fun. The shy kid was not shy anymore. The team was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his ball of yarn. One day, Tom went outside to see the world. As Tom walked, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was trying to tie her shoes. She looked sad. Tom wanted to help Sue. So, Tom went up to Sue and said, "Meow, let me help you tie your shoes." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, furry cat!" Tom used his paws to tie Sue's shoes. They became friends and played together in the world. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a graceful cat named Luna went to visit her friend, a bird named Loop. Luna walked up to Loop's tree and called out, "Loop, can I come up to see you?" Loop looked down and said, "Yes, Luna, come up! I can't wait to play with you." Luna climbed up the tree and they played together. They played hide and seek, and tag. They had so much fun. Luna was very happy to visit her friend Loop. After they played, Luna and Loop sat on a branch and watched the sun go down. They talked about their day and how much they liked to play with each other. Luna said, "Loop, I had a great time today. Let's do this again soon." Loop smiled and said, "Yes, Luna, let's visit each other more often." And they promised to be best friends forever.

One day, a little boy named Tom found a weird toy car. It was not like other toy cars. This car had a button that said "reverse." Tom was very happy to play with his new toy car. He showed it to his mom and dad. They all thought the car had a lot of value because it was so special. Tom pushed the car forward and it went fast. Then, he pushed the "reverse" button. The car went backward just as fast. Tom laughed and clapped his hands. He played with the car all day, making it go forward and backward. At bedtime, Tom told his mom and dad about the weird car. They were happy that the car brought Tom so much joy. They knew it had a lot of value to Tom. The little boy slept with the car next to him, dreaming of more fun days playing with his special toy.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a noisy cave. A little bird named Tim lived near the cave. One day, Tim went inside the cave to see what was making all the noise. As Tim flew deeper into the cave, he saw a big bear. The bear was snoring very loud. Tim blinked his eyes and said, "Hello, Mr. Bear. Why are you so noisy?" The big bear woke up and blinked back at Tim. He said, "I'm sorry, little bird. I didn't know I was noisy. I was just taking a nap." Tim and the bear became friends. They played and had fun outside the cave. And from that day on, the bear tried to be quiet when he slept, so he would not be a noisy bear anymore.
