Category: Intermediate
Title: The Stream and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a little stream. It was very popular with all the animals because it had the best water to drink. One day, a big frog named Freddy and a small fish named Finny met at the stream. Freddy said to Finny, "I am the biggest and most popular animal here. I can pinch you with my strong legs." Finny was scared but replied, "We should be friends and not hurt each other." Freddy thought about it and agreed. They became best friends and played together every day. All the animals saw how much fun they had and wanted to be friends too. Soon, the stream was filled with happy animals playing together. The moral of the story is that being kind and friendly is better than being mean and scary.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their dolls. They liked to pretend that their dolls were going on adventures in the woods, the sea, or the moon. They made up stories and acted them out with their dolls. One day, a big boy came to the park. He saw Lily and Ben and their dolls. He wanted to play with them, but he did not ask nicely. He said, "Give me your dolls. They are mine now. They are too babyish for you." Lily and Ben were scared of the big boy. He was much taller and stronger than them. He looked mean and angry. They did not want to give him their dolls. They loved their dolls very much. The big boy grabbed Lily's doll. It was a soft pink bunny with a leather jacket. Lily had made the jacket herself with her mom's help. She cried, "No, please, don't take my bunny. She is my best friend. She is not babyish. She is cool and brave." The big boy laughed. He said, "A bunny with a leather jacket? That is silly. Bunnies are not cool or brave. They are weak and scared. This is a dangerous jacket. It is only for tough guys like me. You can have this doll instead." He threw a dirty rag doll at Lily. Lily did not accept the rag doll. She threw it back at the big boy. She said, "No, thank you. I don't want your doll. She is not my friend. She is dirty and ugly. You can keep her. Give me back my bunny. She is not dangerous. She is kind and sweet." Ben saw that Lily was brave. He decided to be brave too. He said, "Yeah, give us back our dolls. They are ours. You are not nice. You are mean and rude. Go away and leave us alone." The big boy was surprised. He did not expect Lily and Ben to stand up to him. He felt ashamed and scared. He said, "Fine, fine, take your dolls. I don't want them anyway. They are stupid and boring. I have better toys at home." He ran away from the park. Lily and Ben hugged their dolls. They were happy and proud. They said, "We did it. We saved our dolls. We are cool and brave. We are best friends." They continued to play with their dolls and have fun. They did not let the big boy bother them again.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They saw a lemon tree. Lucy liked lemons, so she asked her mom, "Can I pick a lemon, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Lucy picked a lemon, but it was a poor lemon. It was small and not very yellow. Lucy was sad. She wanted a big, yellow lemon. But then, the lemon started to smile! Lucy was surprised. She never saw a lemon smile before. The smiling lemon said, "I may be small and not very yellow, but I can be your friend." Lucy smiled too. She was happy to have a new friend. They played together in the park all day. And from that day on, Lucy and the lemon were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park, there was a lot of mud. A boy named Tim and a girl named Sue liked to play in the mud. They had so much fun making mud pies and getting dirty. One day, they saw a little dog stuck in the mud. Tim and Sue wanted to help the dog. They urged the dog to move, but it was too scared. So, Tim and Sue went into the mud to get the dog out. They got very dirty, but they didn't care. After they saved the dog, they all went to a fair nearby. They played games, ate yummy food, and went on rides. The dog was happy and clean now. Tim and Sue were happy too, even though they were still dirty from the mud. And they all became the best of friends.
