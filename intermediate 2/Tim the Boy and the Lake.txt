Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Lake

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the wild. He saw many big trees and pretty flowers. Tim liked to march like a toy soldier when he walked. He marched up and down the hills, and he was very happy. In the wild, Tim saw a big, quiet lake. The water was still and looked like a mirror. Tim stopped marching and sat by the lake. He saw some fish swimming in the water and tried to count them. After a while, Tim started to march again. He marched all the way back to his house. When he got home, he told his mom and dad about the wild and the quiet lake. They were very proud of Tim and his big adventure.

One day, a big bird with huge wings woke up early. It wanted to find wisdom. The big bird looked everywhere for wisdom. It looked in the sky, in the trees, and even in the water. But it could not find wisdom anywhere. The big bird saw a small bird with tiny wings. The small bird was sad because it could not fly high like the big bird. The big bird wanted to help the small bird. It thought that if it could help the small bird, it might find wisdom. The big bird tried to teach the small bird how to fly high. But the small bird was too scared to try. So, the big bird had an idea. It put the small bird on its back and flew high into the sky. The small bird was very happy and not scared anymore. The big bird and the small bird became good friends. They played and flew together every day. One day, while they were playing, the big bird found wisdom. It was not in the sky, the trees, or the water. It was in helping the small bird and being a good friend. The big bird was very happy to find wisdom. It knew that being kind and helpful was the best way to be wise. The big bird and the small bird stayed friends forever, and they shared their wisdom with all the other birds.

Once there was a little girl named Grace. She was very excited to go to the park with her mom to play. The sun was shining brightly when they arrived and there were lots of things to do. Grace saw some dogs playing in the park and she wanted to go and pet them. She asked her mom, "Mom, can I pet the dogs?" Her mom said yes, but told her to be very gentle. Grace walked towards the dogs and was just about to pet them when suddenly one of the dogs kicked her! It was scary, but luckily she wasn't hurt. The dog's owner said, "I'm so sorry, he's usually very friendly. Are you ok?" Grace said, "Yes, I'm okay but I'm not sure I want to pet him anymore!" She still liked dogs but decided it was best to keep her distance.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy weapon in his room. He was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tim ran outside and found Sam playing with his ball. "Look, Sam! I found a weapon!" Tim said. Sam looked at the toy weapon and felt angry because he didn't have one. "I want a weapon too!" Sam said. Tim didn't want Sam to be angry, so he said, "Let's play together! We can share the weapon and take turns." They played together and had lots of fun. Sometimes, they would miss when they tried to hit the ball with the weapon. They would laugh and try again. After playing, Sam was not angry anymore. He learned that sharing can make everyone happy.

One day, a little bunny named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big track near his home. Tim was a bit frightened, but he wanted to see what was on the other side. So, he hopped along the track. As he hopped, he met a squirrel named Sam. Sam was looking for nuts to eat. Tim asked, "Can I borrow some nuts?" Sam said, "Yes, but first we need to find them." So, they looked for nuts together. They found a big tree with many nuts. But, when they tried to take the nuts, a big bird shouted, "Stop! These are my nuts!" Tim and Sam were very frightened. The bird then said, "I will share my nuts, but you must help me find more." So, they all worked together and had a big feast with lots of nuts.
