Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Slide

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a boy sitting alone on a comfortable bench. The boy looked sad. Lily walked over to the boy and asked, "Why is your face so sad?" The boy looked up and said, "I lost my toy. I can't find it anywhere." Lily wanted to help the sad boy. They walked around the park, looking for the toy. They looked under the slides and behind the trees. Suddenly, Lily saw the toy on top of a tall slide. She shouted, "There it is! Your toy is on the slide!" The boy's face lit up with a big smile. Together, they climbed the slide and got the toy back. The boy was so happy, he hugged Lily and said, "Thank you for helping me find my toy." They became good friends and played together all day. From that day on, the park became their favorite place to play and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. They loved to play outside, but they always made sure to lock the door to keep their house safe. One sunny day, while playing outside, they saw a big, scary cat. The cat looked like a threat to their dog, Spot. Amy's mom and dad were worried and told Amy to go inside the house. They didn't want the cat to hurt their little dog. Amy went inside the house, but she was curious about the cat. She peeked through the window and saw something unexpected. The big, scary cat was not a threat at all! It was just a lost kitten who wanted to play. Amy's mom and dad brought the kitten inside, and they all played together. Spot, the dog, and the kitten became friends. The house was safe and happy with their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to zip his coat very fast. One day, Tim went to his classroom. It was a fun place with many toys and friends. In the classroom, Tim saw a big box. It was an original box with many colors. He wanted to know what was inside. So, he asked his teacher, "Can I open the box?" His teacher said, "Yes, Tim. You can open the box." Tim was so happy. He zipped his coat and opened the box. Inside, he found many fun things to play with. He and his friends played all day. At the end of the day, Tim zipped his coat again and went home. He told his mom about the original box in the classroom. Tim had a great day and could not wait to go back to school again.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a brave boy named Tim. He loved to run and play with his friends. One sunny day, Tim and his friends went to the park to have fun. In the park, they saw a row of big trees. The trees were very tall and green. Tim and his friends decided to run from one tree to another. They laughed and had a great time running and playing. As they ran, Tim saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and cried for help. Tim, being a brave boy, climbed the tree and helped the cat come down. The cat was very happy, and Tim's friends cheered for him. They all went home with big smiles, knowing that they had a fun and exciting day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in his yard. One day, he found a small, harmless bug. The bug was hungry, so Tim decided to feed it. Tim looked around and found a hole in the ground. He thought, "Maybe the bug will like to eat what's inside the hole." So, he picked up the bug and put it near the hole. The bug went inside and found some yummy food. The bug was very happy that Tim helped it find food. It came out of the hole and played with Tim all day long. They became the best of friends and had lots of fun together. And from that day on, Tim always made sure to feed his little harmless bug friend.
