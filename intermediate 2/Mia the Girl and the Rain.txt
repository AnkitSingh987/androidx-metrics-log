Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Rain

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside. One day, it started to rain. Mia did not like the rain because she could not play outside. She felt sad. Then, Mia saw a small bird who was wet from the rain. She wanted to help the bird. Mia gave the bird a warm welcome and brought it inside her house. The bird was grateful for Mia's help. Mia and the bird became good friends. They played inside the house while it rained outside. But one day, the bird flew away and never came back. Mia was very sad and missed her friend. The rain stopped, but Mia's heart was still full of sadness.

One day, a cat was walking through the hot summer day. As it walked along, the cat stopped to take a rest under a tree. Suddenly, the cat heard a funny noise coming from a bush nearby. The cat was curious and crept towards it. In the bush, the cat saw a little rabbit. The rabbit was shivering and looked very scared. "Why are you shaking?" asked the cat. The rabbit looked up. "I'm scared of the loud thunder," it said. The cat said, "Just close your eyes and listen. The thunder won't hurt you." The rabbit listened and the thunder slowly faded away. The rabbit was so relieved that it hopped out of the bush and thanked the cat. The cat smiled, then said, "You must now be careful when it is hot outside. Thunder may come again from far away." The rabbit nodded and hopped away. The cat watched it go and smiled knowingly.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient statue in a small town. The statue was big and old. People in the town liked the statue. They would say "Welcome" to the statue every day. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the statue. He looked up at the statue and said, "Hello, Mr. Statue! Welcome to our town!" The statue did not say anything back. Tim felt sad. Suddenly, a loud noise came from the sky. A big storm was coming! The wind blew very hard. The ancient statue fell down and broke into many pieces. Tim and the people in the town were very sad. They could not say "Welcome" to the statue anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Match. Match was a small car. Tim and Match liked to play together. They had lots of fun. One day, Tim saw a big rock. He thought it would be fun to make Match speak. So, Tim put Match on the rock and said, "Speak, Match!" But Match did not speak. Tim tried again and again, but Match was still quiet. Tim felt sad and thought playing with Match was boring now. He went to his mom and told her about Match. His mom said, "Match is just a toy, it can't speak." Tim understood and decided to find new ways to play with Match. Soon, they were having fun again.

One day, a big orange cat named Sam saw an envelope on the floor. He wanted to see what was inside. Sam tried to stretch his paw to reach it, but it was too far away. He called his friend, a little mouse named Tim, to help him. "Tim, can you help me get the envelope?" Sam asked. Tim nodded and ran to the envelope. He pushed it closer to Sam. Together, they opened the envelope and found a note inside. The note said, "Surprise! You both get a big cake!" Sam and Tim were so happy. They did not expect to get a cake. They shared the cake and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a big cow. The cow was very careless. She would walk and run all over the farm without looking where she was going. One day, the cow saw a big pile of hay. The cow wanted to carry the hay to her friends, the horses. The cow tried to pick up the hay with her mouth, but she was still being careless. She dropped the hay all over the ground. The cow felt sad because she could not carry the hay to her friends. A little boy saw the cow and wanted to help. The boy was not careless like the cow. He picked up the hay and put it in a big bag. Then, the boy and the cow carried the hay to the horses together. The horses were happy, and the cow learned to be more careful.
