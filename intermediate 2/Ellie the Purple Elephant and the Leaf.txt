Category: Intermediate
Title: Ellie the Purple Elephant and the Leaf

Once upon a time, there was a big purple elephant named Ellie. Ellie lived in a nice forest with many friends. She was a very happy elephant. One day, Ellie saw a tiny ant trying to lift a big leaf. The ant was very small and the leaf was very big. The ant said, "Help me, Ellie! I can't lift this leaf all by myself." Ellie wanted to help her friend. Ellie used her long nose to lift the big leaf. The ant was very happy and said, "Thank you, Ellie! You are a good friend." Ellie felt happy that she could help her friend. They played together all day and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy who liked to help his mom. One day, his mom asked him to wipe the table. Tim was happy to help. As Tim wiped the table, he saw a small, heavy box. He opened the box and found a tiny cell inside. The cell was very pretty and shiny. Tim wanted to play with it, but he knew he should ask his mom first. But, he couldn't wait and started to play with the cell. Suddenly, the cell grew bigger and bigger! It became so big that it filled the whole room. Tim was scared and called for his mom. But it was too late. The big cell broke the house and everything was gone. Tim was very sad. The moral of the story is to always ask before you play with something new.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big red house. In the house, a dad was working. He had to print many papers for his job. He was very busy. One day, his little girl, Amy, wanted to help her dad. She saw the big machine that made the prints. Amy thought, "I can do that!" So, she pushed the buttons on the machine. Oh no! The machine started to print too many papers. They went all over the floor. Amy felt embarrassed. She did not want to make a mess. Dad saw the mess and smiled. He was not mad. He said, "Thank you for trying to help, Amy." They cleaned up the mess together. From that day, Amy learned to ask for help when she wanted to work on something new.

Once upon a time, a little cat named Kitty lived in a small house. Kitty had a happy life. She liked to play with her ball and eat her food. One day, Kitty saw a big dog outside her house. The dog was very big and loud. Kitty felt scared. Kitty realized she needed help. She wanted her friend, the mouse, to help her. Kitty looked for the mouse, but she could not find him. She felt frustrated. Kitty did not know what to do. Kitty decided to hide from the big dog. She hid under a tree. The big dog found her and chased her. Kitty ran away as fast as she could. But the big dog was too fast. The dog caught Kitty and she could not escape. And so, Kitty's happy life came to a sad end.

One day, a big cloud was floating in the sky. The cloud felt very empty. It wanted to have rain inside it. The cloud saw a kind man named Tom. Tom had a big truck. The cloud asked Tom, "Can you help me feel full?" Tom wanted to help the cloud. He used his truck to load water from the river. He drove the truck up a tall hill. Tom poured the water into the cloud. The cloud felt very happy. The next day, the cloud rained on the dry land. The plants and animals were happy too. The cloud thanked Tom for his help. Tom learned that helping others can make you feel good. And the cloud learned that friends can help you when you feel empty.

Once upon a time, there was a hero. The hero was big and strong, but also delicate. He liked to help people and animals who needed help. One day, the hero saw a little bird. The bird could not fly. It was sad. The hero wanted to help the bird. So, he decided to take the bird to its nest. The hero was very careful. He did not want to hurt the little bird. He took the bird in his hands and walked to the tree. The hero put the bird back in its nest. The bird was happy. The hero was happy too. They were all happy because the hero was kind and delicate.
