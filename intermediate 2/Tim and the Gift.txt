Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy boat with a big sail. He liked to play with it in the water. One day, he saw a man deliver a big box to his house. Tim was curious and asked the man, "What is in the box?" The man smiled and said, "I don't know, Tim. I just deliver the boxes. But maybe it's a new toy for you!" Tim was excited and ran inside to ask his mom about the box. His mom told him that the box was a gift for his little sister, but Tim didn't know what was inside. He was ignorant about the gift. Tim and his sister opened the box together, and they found a new toy sailboat! They played with their sailboats in the water all day long, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a toy wreck. The toys were sad because they were not played with. They wanted to be played with and provide fun to a child. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the room. He saw the wreck and said, "Oh no! The toys are in a mess!" He started to pick up the toys and put them in their right place. The toys were happy because Tim was making the room tidy again. After Tim finished, he looked at the toys and said, "Now you are all tidy, let's play together!" The toys were very happy. They played with Tim all day long, providing him with lots of fun. The toys and Tim became best friends, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a stage. He saw his friend, Jane, on the stage. Jane was a tiny bug who loved to dance. Tim said, "Hi, Jane! Can I dance with you?" Jane said, "Yes, Tim! Let's dance together!" They danced and danced on the stage. They were very happy. But Tim was getting tired. He said, "Jane, I am tired. Can we stop dancing?" Jane said, "Okay, Tim. Let's take a break." As they sat down, Jane felt a pinch on her leg. She saw another bug pinching her. Jane said, "Ouch! That hurts! Please stop!" The other bug said, "I am sorry, Jane. I just wanted to play too." Jane, Tim, and the other bug all played together on the stage. They had lots of fun, and they were not tired anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy who loved to ignore her mom when she asked her to do something. One day, Lucy's mom asked her to take a clean spoon and put it in the kitchen drawer. Lucy ignored her mom. She was feeling very lazy and she decided to play with her dolls instead. But later that night, when Lucy was getting ready for bed, she realized that all of her spoon were missing! She realized that if she had put the clean spoon away in the kitchen drawer as her mom had asked, she would have had all her spoon. But now, Lucy had to go to bed without any spoon. She knew she should have listened to her mom earlier, and decided to give it more thought tomorrow.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a pretty, original thread in her room. It was red, blue, and yellow. She wanted to show it to her friend Tom. So, she went to his house. "Tom, look at my thread!" Sue said. Tom looked at it and liked it too. "Can I have it?" Tom asked. Sue thought for a moment and said, "Yes, but you must always listen to your mom and dad." Tom agreed and took the thread. The next day, Tom didn't listen to his mom. He played in the mud when she said not to. The thread saw this and jumped out of his pocket. It ran back to Sue's house. When Sue saw the thread, she was surprised. She asked the thread, "Why are you back?" The thread told her that Tom didn't listen to his mom. Sue learned that it is important to listen to our parents. She decided to keep the thread and always listen to her mom and dad.
