Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Amy and the Butterfly

Once there was a little girl called Amy. She loved to go fast. She had a blue bike with blue streamers on the handlebars and a shiny blue bell. One day, Amy decided to speed around her garden. She jumped onto her bike and went as fast as she could, feeling the wind in her hair. After a while, Amy heard a noise. She looked down and saw a fragile blue butterfly on the ground. Amy stopped her bike. She knelt down to pick up the butterfly, so she could help it fly again. She carefully cupped it in her hands to keep it safe. Amy then blew on the butterfly, and it flew up into the sky. She was so happy to watch it go! As she watched it fly away, Amy felt like she had done something really special.

One day, a boy and his dog went to play outside. They liked to do fun things together. They played with a ball, ran around, and had a good time. The boy threw the ball very hard and the dog ran to get it. They were both very happy. After some time, the boy and the dog felt very tired. The boy said to the dog, "I am tired. Are you tired too?" The dog barked, which meant "Yes, I am tired too." They decided to sit under a tree and rest. The tree had big branches and gave them shade. While they were resting, a big wind came. The wind was so strong that it broke a branch from the tree. The branch fell on the boy and the dog. They were hurt and cried for help. But no one was around to help them. The boy and the dog had a bad day because they were hurt and could not play anymore.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Tim was excited to help his mom pick out the food. In the store, they saw a man with an unusual hat. The hat was big and had many colors. Tim said, "Mom, look at that hat! What do you think?" His mom said, "Well, Tim, everyone has their own opinion. Some people might like it, and some people might not." Tim thought the hat was funny and wanted one too. As they were shopping, the man with the unusual hat came up to Tim and his mom. He said, "I heard you talking about my hat. I have a surprise for you!" The man took off his hat and pulled out another hat just like it. He gave it to Tim and said, "Now you can have your own unusual hat!" Tim was so happy and couldn't wait to wear it everywhere.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Timmy. Timmy loved cheese more than anything in the world. He lived in a small hole in a big house. The house had a big kitchen with lots of cheese. One day, Timmy's mommy told him it was time to learn how to find cheese. She wanted to teach him so he could get his own cheese. Timmy was very excited, but he was also a little impatient. He wanted to find the cheese right away. Timmy's mommy showed him how to look for cheese in the kitchen. They went to the big fridge and found a big piece of cheese. Timmy was so happy. He learned how to find cheese, and now he could get his own cheese whenever he wanted.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw an enormous bush. The bush was so big, it was in the way. Tim wanted to go past the bush, but he could not. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was good at work. Tim said, "Sue, can you help me? The big bush is in the way." Sue said, "Yes, I can help. Let us work together." Tim and Sue worked hard. They pushed and pulled the big bush. The bush moved a little bit. They worked more and more. At last, the bush was not in the way. Tim and Sue were happy. They walked past the bush and played together all day.
