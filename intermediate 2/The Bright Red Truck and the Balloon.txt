Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bright Red Truck and the Balloon

Once upon a time, there was a bright red truck. The truck had big wheels and went very fast. Every day, the truck would drive around the town. One day, the truck saw a little boy. The boy was sad. The truck wanted to make the boy happy. The truck had an idea. The truck would come back the next day with a surprise for the boy. The next day, the truck came back with a big, bright balloon for the boy. The boy saw the truck and the balloon. The boy recognized the truck from the day before. The boy was very happy. The bright red truck had made the boy's day better.

One day, Tom, a little boy, wanted to invite his friends to a fun party. He thought it would be nice to have a band play music at the party. So, he asked his mom if they could have a band come and play. His mom said yes and helped him invite all his friends. On the day of the party, the weather was mild and warm. Tom and his friends were very excited to hear the band play. They all danced and played games while they waited for the band to come. But then, something unexpected happened. Instead of a band with people, a group of animals came to play music! There was a cat playing the drums, a dog playing the guitar, and a bird singing. Tom and his friends were surprised but they loved the animal band. They danced and laughed all day long with their new animal friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little planet. It was a dependable planet, always spinning around and around. On this planet, there was a big, kind bow. The bow loved to help others. One day, a small boy named Tim came to the bow. He said, "Bow, can you help me? I need to tie my shoe." The bow smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! I am here to help." So, the bow helped Tim tie his shoe. After that, Tim and the bow became good friends. They played together on the dependable planet every day. They laughed and had fun. And from that day on, the bow always helped Tim whenever he needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a barber. The barber was scary. He had a big shop. In the shop, there was a big chair. People came to the barber to cut their hair. One day, the barber found a big fruit. The fruit was in the shop. The barber did not know where the fruit came from. The fruit was very big and very red. The barber was happy. He put the fruit in the shop. The fruit began to rot. It smelled bad. The barber did not like the smell. People did not want to come to the shop. The barber was sad. He wanted to make the shop nice again. The barber had an idea. He took the big, rot fruit out of the shop. He put it far away. The smell was gone. The barber was happy. People came to the shop again. The barber cut their hair. Everyone was happy.

One day there was a bull in a meadow. He was an ordinary bull and he wasn't particularly special. Suddenly the bull felt like he wanted to soar in the sky. So he stood up on his back legs and started running. "What are you doing?" asked one of the cows. "I'm going to soar in the sky!" replied the bull. The cow laughed and shook her head. Suddenly the bull stopped running and jumped into the air. To everyone's surprise he started flying. He was soaring in the sky! All the other animals cheered as the bull flew higher and higher. Eventually the bull came back down to the ground. He was so proud of himself and the other animals congratulated him on his feat. "You did it! You can really soar!" they said. The bull smiled and thanked them for their encouragement. From that day forward he was no longer an ordinary bull - he was a flying bull!

One day, a playful cat named Tim was walking in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim was very good at climbing trees, so he went up and up. When he reached the top, he could see the whole park. He felt happy. But then, Tim saw an emergency. A small dog was stuck in a hole and could not get out. Tim knew he had to help the dog. He climbed down the tree very fast and ran to the dog. The dog was scared, but Tim told him not to worry. Tim thought hard about how to help the dog. He remembered that he could perform a trick to get the dog out of the hole. He jumped on a big rock and then jumped over the hole. The dog saw Tim and tried to do the same thing. With Tim's help, the dog was able to jump out of the hole. They were both very happy and became good friends.
