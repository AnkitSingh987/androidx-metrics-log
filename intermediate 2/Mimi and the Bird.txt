Category: Intermediate
Title: Mimi and the Bird

Once upon a time, a little cat named Mimi lived in a small house. Mimi was very flexible and could jump high. One day, Mimi saw a big tree with a bird on its shoulder. Mimi wanted to play with the bird. Mimi jumped and jumped to reach the bird, but the bird did not want to play. The bird was scared of Mimi. Mimi did not know that the bird was scared. Mimi thought the bird was just playing a game. Then, something unexpected happened. Mimi jumped too high and fell down. Mimi got hurt and was very sad. The bird flew away and Mimi learned that not everyone wants to play.

Once upon a time, there was a clever girl named Lily. She loved to spend time with her best friend, a talking cat named Whiskers. They played together all day long. One day, Lily and Whiskers saw a wedding. "What is that?" asked Lily. Whiskers said, "It is a marriage. When people love each other, they have a big party to show it." Lily thought it was very nice. Lily and Whiskers decided to have a pretend marriage for fun. They put on pretty clothes and invited all their friends. Everyone danced and ate yummy cake. It was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. He liked to play with string. One day, he saw a red string hanging from a tree. He wanted to play with it, but it was too high for him to reach. A little girl named Sally saw Tom and asked, "Why are you grumpy, Tom?" Tom said, "I want to play with the red string, but it's too high." Sally wanted to help Tom, so she found a long stick. Sally used the stick to rescue the red string from the tree. Tom was so happy to play with the string. He was not grumpy anymore. Tom and Sally played with the string all day long, and they became good friends.

One day, Billy and his family decided to go to school. Billy was so excited! He put on his blue T-shirt and shoes and took his lunch box. When they got to school, everything was blue: the walls, the floor, even the desks! Billy couldn’t believe it! He thought to himself this was the best school ever. But then something strange happened; the teacher asked all the kids to put away their lunch boxes and take out their pencils and paper. Billy got so nervous because he didn’t know what was coming. Soon, the teacher said the word "test", which made everyone so scared. Everyone got so quiet. But then the teacher handed out a blue paper with puzzles and riddles. Everyone started cheering and laughing. Everyone was so happy! They got to use their brains and have fun. Billy was so excited! He was no longer scared because it was nothing like he expected. Going to school was one of the best days he had ever had. Even the blue walls and floors were a part of the fun! School wasn’t so scary after all.

One day, a pink bird with a long neck was walking in the park. She saw a little boy with a toy. The boy was sad because his toy was broken. The pink bird wanted to help the boy. The bird said, "I can help you fix your toy. Just say 'please' and I will try my best." The little boy looked at the bird and said, "Please." The bird tried to fix the toy, but it was hard with her long neck. The bird tried again and again. Each time, she would say, "I will try again," and the boy would repeat, "Please." They did this many times. Then, something unexpected happened! The bird's neck got shorter and shorter with each try. Finally, the bird fixed the toy and the boy was happy. The bird was also happy because her long neck was now shorter and it was easier for her to help others. The boy thanked the pink bird and they became good friends.

Tom liked to mark on the wall with his crayons. He made big lines and circles and stars. He thought his marks were pretty and fun. But Mom did not like Tom's marks. She said they were messy and bad. She made Tom wash the wall with a wet cloth. One day, Tom found a new thing to mark with. It was a stick that had ash on the end. Tom saw Dad use it to make fire in the fireplace. Tom thought the ash was black and cool. He wanted to mark with it. He took the stick and went to the wall. Tom made ash marks on the wall. He made big lines and circles and stars. He thought his ash marks were pretty and fun. But Mom did not like Tom's ash marks. She said they were dirty and worse. She made Tom wash the wall with a wet cloth and soap. Mom was angry and sad. She said Tom was naughty and careless. She said he could not mark on the wall anymore. She took away his crayons and his stick. Tom was sorry and sad. He said he was sorry and he loved Mom. Mom was thoughtful and kind. She said she loved Tom too. She said he could mark on paper or on a board. She gave him some paper and some chalk. Tom was happy and glad. He said thank you and he hugged Mom. He marked on the paper with the chalk. He made big lines and circles and stars. He thought his chalk marks were pretty and fun. And Mom thought so too.
