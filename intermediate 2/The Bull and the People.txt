Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bull and the People

Once upon a time there was a bull. He had an important job. He helped the farmers and the people know what time it was! He would go to a field every day and make a loud noise for everyone to hear. The people in the village would listen for the bull's sound. When it was time for lunch, he would make a big sound and everyone knew it was time to eat. In the evening, he would make his loud noise again, so everyone would know that it was time to go to bed. The bull was very important because all the people in the village depended on him. They would howl and clap to show their appreciation. Everyone was very happy to have the bull on their side. They knew that with him around, they always have a good sense of time!

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys. One day, they find a big feather in the park. It is soft and colorful. They take it home and put it in their room. The next day, Tom wants to buy a new car. He sees a man who sells toys on the street. He thinks the man will give him money for the feather. He takes the feather from the room and runs to the man. Lily looks for the feather. She wants to make a hat with it. She cannot find it. She asks Mom where it is. Mom does not know. She asks Tom. Tom says he sold it to the man. Lily is angry and sad. She cries. Tom comes back with the new car. He is happy. He shows it to Lily. Lily does not look at him. She says he is bad. He took her feather and sold it. He did not ask her. He did not share. He is guilty. Tom feels sorry. He sees Lily's tears. He remembers how they found the feather together. He thinks he was wrong. He wants to make Lily happy again. He takes the car and runs to the man. He asks for the feather back. He gives the car to the man. The man gives him the feather. Tom comes back with the feather. He gives it to Lily. He says he is sorry. He says he loves her. He says they can share the feather and the toys. Lily smiles. She hugs him. She says she loves him too. She says they can make a hat with the feather. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jake. He was only three years old and loved his toys. Every day, he would play with them and have so much fun. One day, Jake had a special toy - a long, colourful train. He loved it more than anything else! But then, his little brother wanted to play with it. "Can I play with your train?" he asked. "No, it's mine!" said Jake. But then, his brother took the train away and wouldn't give it back. So Jake got angry and replaced it with a big piece of wood. When his brother saw it, he was very upset. He started crying and ran away. Later, Jake realized how wrong it was to replace something he loved with something so bad. The moral value of the story is that it's wrong to replace something precious with something bad. Even when a person is angry and doesn’t want to share, there are better ways to solve a problem than making someone else unhappy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a favorite spoon. The spoon was very big and shiny. She used it to eat her cereal every morning. The spoon made her happy. One cold day, Amy could not find her spoon. She looked everywhere. She asked her mom, "Where is my spoon?" Her mom did not know the answer. Amy felt sad. She wanted her spoon back. Then, Amy's dog, Max, came into the room. Max had her spoon in his mouth! He gave it to her. Amy was so happy to have her spoon back. She hugged Max and said "Thank you!" Now, Amy could eat her cereal with her favorite spoon, even on a cold day.
