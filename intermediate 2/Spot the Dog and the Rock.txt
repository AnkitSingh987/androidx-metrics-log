Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Rock

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the yard. He saw a big chain on the ground. Spot wanted to play with the chain, but it was stuck to a heavy rock. He pulled and pulled, but the chain would not move. Spot felt frustrated. "Spot, come rest with me," said his friend, a little cat named Fluffy. Spot went to sit with Fluffy under a big tree. They talked and laughed together. Spot forgot about the chain for a while. After they rested, Spot and Fluffy decided to try to move the chain together. They both pulled and pulled. With their teamwork, the chain finally moved! Spot and Fluffy were so happy they could play with the chain. They knew that sometimes, all you need is a little help from a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends decided to have a race. Tim wanted to win and feel the victory. He put on his fit shoes and got ready. The race started, and Tim ran as fast as he could. His friends were running fast too. As they got closer to the finish line, Tim saw his mom and dad waiting for him. They had a surprise for him. Tim's eyes got big and he ran even faster. Tim crossed the finish line first and won the race. He felt so happy. His mom and dad said, "Surprise, Tim! We are so proud of you!" They gave him a big hug and a special treat. Tim felt the victory and loved the surprise from his mom and dad.

One day, a big dog named Max and a little cat named Lucy went on a search. They wanted to find a fun toy to play with. Max was very impatient, but Lucy told him, "We will find it soon, don't worry!" As they looked, Max made a joke. He said, "Why did the cat sit on the toy? Because it wanted to play!" Lucy laughed and said, "That's a funny joke, Max!" Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the toy into the air! Max and Lucy watched as the toy flew high up into the sky. They were both very surprised. Max said, "Oh no, our toy is gone!" But Lucy had an idea. She said, "Let's make a new toy out of leaves and sticks!" They worked together to make a new toy and had lots of fun playing with it. And they never forgot the day their toy flew away.

Once, there was a girl called Lucy. Every day at lunchtime, Lucy liked to eat her sandwich. She would take big bites, and chew really loudly! One day, her dad heard her chewing and said, "Lucy, why don't you chew a bit more quietly?" Lucy looked at him and said, "Sorry, Daddy!" She put down her sandwich and slowed down her chewing. After lunch, Lucy had to get dressed. She put on a pair of nice pants and looked out at the garden. She felt a bit sleepy, so she decided to take a nap outside on the grass. She lay down and closed her eyes. Pretty soon, Lucy was full of energy and ready to play. She had a great day in the garden, running and laughing—all in her special pants!

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to imagine he was in a band. He would play with his toy drum all day long. One day, Spot met a little girl named Sue. Sue asked, "Do you want to be in a real band with me?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, I do!" They played music together and had lots of fun. But one day, while they played, a big wind came and blew their instruments away! Spot and Sue were sad, but then they had an idea. They started to sing and clap their hands. They didn't need instruments to have fun and make music. They just needed each other.

Once upon a time, a little wolf lived in the woods. The little wolf loved to play and jump in the spring. The sun was warm and the flowers were pretty. One day, while the little wolf was playing, he met a big wolf. The big wolf was not nice. He was ignorant and did not like to share. "Hey, little wolf, this is my part of the woods," said the big wolf. "You can't play here." The little wolf was sad, but he did not want to fight. So, he went to another part of the woods to play. But then, something unexpected happened. A big rain came and made the woods wet and muddy. The big wolf's part of the woods was now all wet and not fun to play in. The big wolf went to the little wolf and said, "I'm sorry I was mean. Can I play with you in your part of the woods?" The little wolf was kind and said, "Yes, we can play together." And so, the big wolf and the little wolf played and jumped in the spring, and they became good friends.
