Category: Intermediate
Title: Buzzy the Busy Bee and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a busy little bee named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to fly around and find pretty flowers. One day, Buzzy saw a big, red flower. Buzzy thought, "I will go to that flower and get some paint." Buzzy went to the flower and found a new friend, a tiny bug named Tiny. Tiny liked to clap all day long. Tiny said, "Buzzy, I will clap for you while you get paint!" Buzzy smiled and started to get paint from the flower. Buzzy and Tiny played together all day. Buzzy got lots of paint, and Tiny clapped and clapped. When the sun started to go down, Buzzy and Tiny knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye and promised to meet again at the big, red flower. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were birds and build nests with twigs and leaves. They found a big tree with a lot of twigs on the ground. They picked up the twigs and carried them to a branch. "Look, Anna, we have a lot of twigs. We can make a big nest," Ben said. "Yes, Ben, we can. Let's put them here and here and here," Anna said. They worked hard and made a nice nest. They were very happy with their nest. They climbed inside and pretended to be birds. They sang and flapped their wings. A lady was walking by with her dog. She saw the children in the tree. She smiled and stopped to watch them. "Hello, little birds. What a lovely nest you have. Did you make it by yourselves?" she asked. "Yes, we did. We are birds. We like twigs and leaves," Anna said. "You are very clever and creative. I applaud you for your work. Can I clap for you?" the lady asked. "Yes, please. We like clapping," Ben said. The lady clapped her hands and cheered for the children. The dog barked and wagged his tail. The children felt proud and happy. They thanked the lady and waved to her. The lady walked away with her dog. She thought the children were very sweet and funny. She wished she had a nest like theirs. She had a big house and a lot of money, but she was not very happy. She thought the children were richer than her.

Lily and Ben are brother and sister. They like to play hide and seek on Sunday. They take turns to hide and seek in the house. Lily is good at hiding. She can hide under the bed, behind the curtain, or in the closet. Ben is good at seeking. He can find Lily by listening, looking, or smelling. One Sunday, they play hide and seek again. Lily says, "I will hide first. You count to ten and then seek me." Ben says, "OK. One, two, three..." Lily runs to find a good hiding place. She sees the basement door. She thinks, "Maybe I can hide in the basement. Ben will never find me there." She opens the door and goes down the stairs. The basement is dark and cold. Lily feels fearful. She hears strange noises. She sees shadows and spiders. She wants to go back. But she thinks, "I have to hide. Ben is coming soon." She finds a box and crawls inside. She closes the lid and waits. Ben finishes counting. He says, "Ready or not, here I come!" He seeks Lily in the house. He checks under the bed, behind the curtain, and in the closet. He does not find Lily. He thinks, "Where is Lily? She is very good at hiding. Maybe she is outside." He goes to the door and looks outside. He does not see Lily. He thinks, "Maybe she is in the basement." He goes to the basement door and opens it. He hears Lily's voice. She says, "Help! Help! I am scared!" Ben runs down the stairs. He sees the box and opens the lid. He sees Lily. She is crying. He says, "Lily, I found you! Why are you crying?" Lily says, "I am sorry, Ben. I was fearful. The basement is dark and cold. There are strange noises and shadows and spiders. I want to go back." Ben says, "It's OK, Lily. I am here. I will protect you. Let's go back." He hugs Lily and helps her up the stairs. They go back to the house. They do not play hide and seek in the basement again. They play with their toys and have fun. They love each other.
