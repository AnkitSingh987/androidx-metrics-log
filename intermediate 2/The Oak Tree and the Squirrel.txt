Category: Intermediate
Title: The Oak Tree and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a big oak tree. The oak tree was in a beautiful park. Many children loved to play near the oak tree because it was so big and strong. One day, an incredible thing happened. A little squirrel came to the oak tree. The squirrel found a small piece of food. But the food was old and it started to rot. The squirrel did not want to eat the rotting food, so it decided to dig a hole and bury it under the oak tree. After some time, the rotting food turned into rich soil. The oak tree grew even bigger and stronger. The children continued to play near the incredible oak tree, and the squirrel made a home in its branches. They all lived happily together in the beautiful park.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, it was very cold and icy. The snow was white and shiny. Tom and Lily put on their hats, coats, boots and gloves. They wanted to make a snowman and slide on the ice. "Come on, Lily, let's go!" Tom said. He ran out of the door and into the yard. "Wait for me, Tom!" Lily said. She followed him, but she was not as fast. She saw Tom run to the pond. The pond was frozen and looked like a big mirror. "Tom, be careful!" Lily shouted. "The ice might break!" Tom did not listen. He jumped on the ice and slid around. He laughed and had fun. He did not see the crack in the ice. Lily ran to the pond and saw the crack. She was scared. She called for their parent. "Mom, dad, help! Tom is on the ice and it might break!" Their parent heard Lily and came outside. They saw Tom on the ice and the crack. They were worried. "Tom, stay where you are! Don't move!" their parent said. "We will get you!" Their parent got a rope and threw it to Tom. Tom grabbed the rope and held on. Their parent pulled him to the shore. Tom was safe. "Tom, you were very silly and dangerous!" their parent said. "You could have fallen into the water and got hurt!" "I'm sorry, mom, dad," Tom said. "I won't do it again." Lily hugged Tom and said, "I'm glad you are okay, Tom. I love you." "I love you too, Lily," Tom said. "Thank you for calling for help." Their parent hugged them both and said, "We love you, Tom and Lily. You are our brave and good twins. But next time, please stay away from the icy pond."

Tom and Lily liked to play with their water balloons. They had many colors and shapes of balloons. They filled them with water from the hose and tied them. Then they threw them at each other and laughed. One day, they found a big yellow balloon in the garage. It was bigger than their heads. They wanted to fill it with water and make a huge splash. They put the balloon on the hose and turned it on. But the balloon had a leak. Water came out of a small hole and sprayed everywhere. Tom and Lily were sad. They wanted to play with the big balloon. They tried to fix the leak with tape, but it did not work. The water still came out. They asked their mom for help, but she said the balloon was too old and had to go in the trash. Tom and Lily did not want to throw away the balloon. They liked the balloon. They valued the balloon. They thought it was special and popular. They decided to keep the balloon and use it for something else. They cut off the leaky part and made a hat out of it. They put it on their heads and pretended to be astronauts. They had fun with their new hats and forgot about the water. They learned that they could value things even if they were not perfect.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to decorate her room with pretty things. One day, Sue found a magic bubble wand. Sue waved the magic bubble wand and big, round bubbles came out. The bubbles were mild and soft. They did not pop when they touched her hands. Sue was so happy! She played with the magic bubbles all day. She used the bubbles to decorate her room. The bubbles made her room look so pretty. Sue loved her magic bubble wand and the beautiful, mild bubbles it made.
