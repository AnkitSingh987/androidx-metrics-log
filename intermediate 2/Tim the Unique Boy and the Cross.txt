Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Unique Boy and the Cross

Once upon a time, there was a unique boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cross. Every day, he would take it with him and show it to his friends. They all liked Tim's cross, and they played together. One sunny day, when Tim was playing with his cross, a big wind came. The wind took the cross away from Tim. Tim was very sad. He looked for the cross, but he could not find it. He went home and told his mom. Tim's mom had a surprise for him. She said, "I found your cross, Tim!" But when Tim looked at it, he saw that it was not his cross. It was a new, bigger cross. Tim was happy again. He loved his new cross and played with it all day.

Once there was a young rabbit who wanted to join in on a game. He asked a frog who was playing with a perfect blue ball. The frog said no. The young rabbit felt sad but thought of a plan. He hopped over to a young rabbit who was making a big wonderful castle out of sand. He asked if he could join in and the young rabbit said yes. But as soon as the young rabbit started packing up sand he realized the frog had taken his perfect blue ball. The young rabbit hopped over to the frog and asked for his ball back. But the frog wouldn’t give it. He said it was a perfect ball and he was going to keep it. The young rabbit thought for a moment until he had a bright idea. He said, “I’ll join your game if you give me the perfect blue ball back”. The frog agreed and the two rabbits started a perfect game together. They both laughed, hopped and had so much fun playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a patient cactus. The cactus wanted to explore the world. One day, it opened its eyes and looked around. The cactus saw clouds in the sky and rocks on the ground. It opened its arms and felt the warm sun. Then, the cactus opened its feet and walked away. The cactus explored many places, like a big hill and a deep lake. Everywhere it went, the patient cactus opened its ears and listened carefully. Soon, the cactus saw a beautiful field of flowers. So, it opened its arms and hugged them. The cactus decided to stay in the field for a long time. It opened its eyes and watched the sun setting. Then, when it was time to go, the cactus reopened its feet and went home.

One day, a big red bus arrives at the park. Many kids are there to play. They are all glad to see the bus. The bus has a big sign that says, "Story Time!" Inside the bus, there is a lady with a big book. She starts to read a story to the kids. The kids sit and listen. They like the story a lot. They look at the pages with big, colorful pictures. When the lady finishes the story, she turns to the last page. The kids clap and say, "More, more!" The lady smiles and starts a new story. The kids are very glad and listen to her read again. They love the story time at the park.

Tim and Anna are friends who like to play at the park. They both love the slide, but there is only one slide and many children want to use it. Sometimes they have to wait for a long time to get their turn. One day, Tim and Anna are waiting in line for the slide, when a big boy cuts in front of them. He says, "I want to go first, I am bigger than you." Tim and Anna are angry and sad. They say, "That is not fair, you have to wait like everyone else." The big boy does not listen. He raises his voice and says, "No, I don't. I can do what I want. You are too small and weak to stop me." He pushes Tim and Anna aside and climbs up the ladder to the slide. Tim and Anna feel like crying. They do not know what to do. They look around and see a girl who is watching them. She has a kind smile and a big bow in her hair. She says, "Hi, I am Lily. I saw what happened. That was very mean of him. Do you want to play with me?" Tim and Anna nod and smile. They say, "Yes, please. We are Tim and Anna. Thank you for being nice to us." Lily says, "You are welcome. I have an idea. Why don't we share the slide with each other? We can take turns and have fun together." Tim and Anna like the idea. They agree to share the slide with Lily. They wait patiently for their turn and help each other up the ladder. They slide down with joy and laughter. They have a lot of fun. The big boy sees them and feels lonely. He realizes that he was wrong to be rude and selfish. He wishes he had friends like them. He says, "I am sorry for being mean to you. Can I play with you too?" Tim, Anna and Lily are generous and forgiving. They say, "Yes, you can. But you have to be nice and share with us." The big boy nods and says, "I will. Thank you for giving me another chance." He joins them and learns to play nicely. The moral of the story is: It is better to be kind and share than to be rude and selfish. You will have more friends and more fun that way.
