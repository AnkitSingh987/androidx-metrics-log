Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hot and wanted to find a cooler place to play. He walked around with his toy car, looking for a nice spot to sit. Tim saw a big, open field with lots of green grass. He thought it would be cooler there. So, he walked to the field with his toy car and started to play. He felt much better in the open field. After playing for a while, Tim got thirsty. He said, "Please, can I have some water?" His mom heard him and came with a bottle of water. Tim drank the water and felt cooler. He was very happy to play in the open field with his toy car.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a soft, blue sweater that she loved to wear. She liked to wear it when she played outside with her friends. One day, Mia saw a small cat stuck in a tree. Mia wanted to help the cat, so she kneeled down and thought of a plan. She decided to climb the tree and save the cat. Mia put on her soft sweater and climbed the tree. She reached the cat and it was very happy. Mia and the cat went back down the tree safely. They played together all day and became best friends.

One day, a colorful bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a cup on the ground. The bird wanted to fill the cup with water. So, the bird flew down to a pond and filled the cup. A small boy saw the bird and said, "Hi bird! I like your colors. Can you fill my cup too?" The bird nodded and filled the boy's cup with water. They both smiled and laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to fly very fast. The boy was amazed by the bird's speed. The bird flew around and around, making a big rainbow in the sky. The boy and the bird became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had many friends in her neighborhood. One day, Lucy wanted to have a party with her friends. She thought it would be a great idea. Lucy wrote a note to invite her friends to the party. The note said, "Come to my party. It will be great fun!" She gave the note to her friends. All her friends were happy to get the note. At the party, Lucy and her friends played games, ate snacks, and had a lot of fun. One of her friends said, "Thank you for inviting us, Lucy. This is a great party!" Lucy smiled and said, "I am happy that you all came. Sharing fun with friends is the best!" Moral: Sharing fun times with friends makes everyone happy.

Maya was in the gym with her grandmother. Urging her to try the jungle gym, her grandmother said, "Come on, Maya! Lean on the railings. You can do it!" Maya was scared and didn't want to do it. She was too young and it looked too high. But her grandmother was persistent. "Just have a go," she said. Maya bagan to climb. Slowly, she leaned on the railings and just when she got to the top she felt a hand on her shoulder. It was her grandmother, who had been watching all along. Maya was so happy and turned around to smile at her. But her grandmother wasn't smiling. Instead, she had a look of disappointment. "I can't believe how selfish you were," she scolded. "You didn't think of anyone else. You were too busy thinking of yourself." Maya was stunned. She had been so scared, and all she wanted was to make her grandmother proud. But then her grandmother smiled and said, "Good job, Maya. I'm very proud of you!"

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big book. She opened the book and saw a perfect page. On the page, there was a picture of a magic cat. Mia was very happy to see the cat. She wanted the cat to come out of the book and play with her. Mia said, "Please, magic cat, come out and play with me." She closed her eyes and began to pray. When she opened her eyes, the magic cat was standing in front of her! Mia was so surprised and happy. But the magic cat was not a nice cat. It said, "I am not here to play. I am here to take your toys away!" Mia was sad and scared. She tried to stop the cat, but it was too strong. The cat took all of Mia's toys and went back into the book. Mia was left with nothing, and she learned to be careful what she wished for.
