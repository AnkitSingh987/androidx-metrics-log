Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Tree and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a little tree named Tim. Tim was a fine tree who lived in a big forest with his family. He loved to grow tall and strong to protect all his little animal friends who lived with him. One day, Tim learned that he could make oxygen for his friends to breathe. He was so happy to help them. He would wave his green leaves in the sun and make the air fresh and clean for everyone. As Tim grew bigger, he became the best tree in the forest. He protected his friends from the hot sun and the cold rain. And with all the oxygen he made, everyone lived happily and healthy. Tim was proud to be a fine tree who could protect and help his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a judge. The judge was very soft and nice. He helped people when they needed help. He had big glasses to see everything better. One day, the judge went for a walk. He saw a little bird who was sad. The judge asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I lost my mommy." The judge wanted to help the bird, so they went to look for the mommy. They walked and walked, and then they saw the mommy bird. The mommy bird was so happy to see her baby. The judge felt happy too, because he helped the little bird. The mommy bird thanked the judge, and they all went home. The judge knew that being soft and kind was very important.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They saw a fragile bird on the ground. The bird had a hurt wing. Lucy wanted to help the bird. She asked her mom, "Can we help the bird?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can take it home and make it better." They put the bird in a soft box. They took it home and gave it food and water. The bird started to feel better. After a few days, the bird's wing was not hurt anymore. Lucy and her mom took the bird back to the park. They let the bird go. The bird flew away and Lucy was happy. She said, "I'm glad we could help the bird." Lucy and her mom went home, and they finished their day with a big hug.

One day, a little boy named Tim heard his dad mention a comet. Tim did not know what a comet was, but it sounded fun. He asked his dad to tell him more about it. His dad said, "A comet is a big rock that flies in the sky. It has a long tail and is very bright. Tonight, we can go outside and look at the comet together." Tim was very excited to see the comet with his dad. That night, the sky was a bit gloomy, but Tim and his dad went outside. They looked up at the sky and saw the comet. It was big and bright, just like his dad said. Tim was so happy to see the comet and share that special moment with his dad.

Once upon a time, in a quiet town, there lived a boy named Tom. He was a youth who loved to play with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day. Tom liked to give his toys to his friends so they could play together. One day, Tom found a big, red ball in his yard. He was very happy and wanted to give the ball to his friends. They all played with the ball, kicking it and throwing it high in the sky. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. It was a perfect day. But then, the ball went too high and far away. Tom and his friends tried to find the ball, but it was lost. They were all very sad. The quiet town was not happy anymore. Tom and his friends went home with heavy hearts, missing their big, red ball.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a comfortable sweater in his room. He put it on and felt very warm and happy. He loved his sweater and wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. So, Tim went outside to find Sam. When Tim found Sam, he said, "Look at my new sweater! It's comfortable and warm!" Sam looked at the sweater and felt a little jealous. He wanted a sweater like that too. Sam asked, "Can I try it on?" Tim trusted Sam, so he said, "Okay, but be careful." Sam put on the sweater and started to run around. He was having so much fun that he didn't see the big mud puddle in front of him. Sam fell into the mud puddle and the comfortable sweater got all dirty. Tim was sad and angry. His sweater was ruined. Tim said, "You ruined my sweater! I trusted you to be careful!" Sam felt very bad and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to." But the sweater was still dirty and Tim was not happy. He went home feeling sad, and Sam felt bad too. From that day on, Tim did not trust Sam with his things anymore. The comfortable sweater was never the same again, and their friendship was hurt.
