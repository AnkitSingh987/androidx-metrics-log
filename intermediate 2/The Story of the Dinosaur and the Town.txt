Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dinosaur and the Town

One day, a big dinosaur came to a small town. This dinosaur was very smelly. All the people in the town did not like the smelly dinosaur. They wanted the dinosaur to go away. The dinosaur wanted to make the people happy. So, the dinosaur tried to reverse. He walked backward, step by step. The people in the town laughed and clapped. They liked the funny dinosaur now. The smelly dinosaur made a lot of friends in the town. They all played together and had fun. The dinosaur was happy too. And the town people learned to love the smelly dinosaur.

One day, a big bear and a little rabbit were in the woods. They wanted to build a house together. The big bear was very strong and dependable. The little rabbit was fast and smart. They thought they would make a great team. As they started to build, the big bear said to the little rabbit, "You pass me the wood, and I will put it up." The little rabbit agreed and passed the wood to the big bear. They worked very hard all day and were very happy with their new house. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew their house down! The big bear and the little rabbit were sad. They talked and decided to build their house again, but this time they would make it stronger. The big bear and the little rabbit worked together again. They built a new house that was strong and safe from the wind. They learned that working together and never giving up made their house better than before. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is that when friends work together and are dependable, they can overcome any problem, even when something unexpected happens.

Lisa and Tom are friends. They go to the same class. They like to play and learn together. One day, their teacher says they can pick a book to read. Lisa and Tom are happy. They run to the bookshelf. "Look, Tom, this book has a big red car on the cover. I like cars. Can we read this one?" Lisa asks. "OK, Lisa, but be careful. The book is high up. You need to reach for it," Tom says. Lisa nods. She stands on her toes and stretches her arm. She tries to grab the book. But the book is too far. Lisa slips and falls. She bumps her head on the floor. She starts to cry. "Ow, ow, ow! That hurts!" Lisa says. Tom is scared. He runs to Lisa and hugs her. He says sorry. He calls the teacher. The teacher comes and helps Lisa. She puts a band-aid on her head. She gives her a hug and a kiss. "Lisa, are you OK? You were brave. But next time, please ask for help when you want to pick a book that is high up. You could get hurt," the teacher says. Lisa nods. She says thank you. She feels better. She smiles at Tom. Tom smiles back. They pick another book that is low down. They sit on the carpet and read together. They are still friends. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very eager to find his toy car. He looked all over for it. He looked under his bed, on the floor, and in the toy box. But he could not find it. One day, Tim asked his mom, "Mom, have you seen my toy car?" His mom said, "No, Tim. I have not seen it." Tim was sad. He really wanted to play with his toy car. Then, Tim's dad came home from work. He saw Tim was sad. Dad said, "Tim, I have a surprise for you!" He took Tim to the living room. There, on the floor, was a big new toy car! Tim was so happy. He said, "Thank you, Dad!" They played with the toy car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to become very smart. He asked his mom for a tutor to help him learn. The tutor came to Tim's house every day. They read books and played learning games. Tim was happy and the tutor was nice. They had fun together. One day, the tutor didn't come. Tim waited and waited, but the tutor never came back. Tim was sad and cried. He didn't become smart like he wanted to.
