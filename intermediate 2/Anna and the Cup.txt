Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Cup

Anna likes to play hide and seek with her daddy. He is very good at finding her, but sometimes she can trick him. One day, she had a new idea. She saw her daddy's coffee on the table. It was hot and brown and smelled nice. She thought, maybe she could hide in the coffee. She took a big spoon and scooped some coffee into a cup. Then she ran to the bathroom and poured the coffee over her head. It was very hot and wet and sticky, but she did not cry. She put the cup on the floor and hid behind the shower curtain. She waited for her daddy to come and look for her. Her daddy finished his work and went to the table. He saw his coffee was gone. He was confused and sad. He liked his coffee very much. He looked around the room and saw a trail of brown drops on the floor. He followed the trail to the bathroom. He opened the door and saw the cup and the spoon. He smiled and said, "I think I know where Anna is." He pulled back the shower curtain and saw Anna. She was covered in coffee and had a big grin on her face. She said, "Surprise! You found me!" Her daddy laughed and hugged her. He said, "You are very clever and funny, Anna. But you are also very messy and sticky. Let's take a bath and clean you up." He turned on the water and put some soap and bubbles in the tub. He lifted Anna and put her in the water. She splashed and giggled and said, "I love you, daddy. You are very dependable and kind." He said, "I love you too, Anna. You are very sweet and brave." They had a lot of fun in the bath and then drank some juice and ate some cookies. They were very happy and cozy. They played hide and seek again, but this time they did not use coffee.

One day, a sad kitten was lost in the big park. The kitten was very little and scared. It was cold and hungry. The kitten cried for help, but no one heard it. The kitten was all alone. A nice boy named Tim heard the kitten's cry. He wanted to help the sad kitten. Tim looked for the kitten and found it under a big tree. The kitten was so happy to see Tim. Tim picked up the kitten and wanted to take it home. But Tim's mom said no. She did not want a kitten in their house. Tim was very sad. He had to leave the kitten in the park. The kitten was sad too. It cried and cried, but no one came to help. The kitten stayed alone and sad in the big park.

One day, a wise cat named Tom and a little dog named Tim were part of a big work. They had to fix a broken toy. Tom and Tim were very good friends. They liked to play and have fun together. Tom said, "Tim, let's work together to fix the toy." Tim agreed and they started to work. They were very happy to help each other. They worked hard and were almost done. But then, something unexpected happened! A big bird came and took the toy away. Tom and Tim were very surprised. They looked at each other and said, "We must get the toy back!" Tom and Tim worked together to make a plan. They were very wise and knew just what to do. They found the big bird and got the toy back. They finished fixing the toy and were very proud of their work. In the end, Tom and Tim learned that working together is the best way to get things done. They were happy to be part of the work and even happier to be friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia walked in her garden. She saw a big rose. Mia thought the rose was boring. She wanted to see something new and fun. Mia yawned and closed her eyes. When she opened them, a big butterfly came out of the rose! Mia was so happy. The butterfly was not boring. It was fun and pretty. The butterfly flew around Mia. It made her laugh and dance. Then, the butterfly went back into the rose. Mia knew the rose was not boring anymore. It was full of magic and fun.
