Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a small room with his friend, a little girl named Lucy. They loved to play together every day. One day, Lucy's mom said, "Fluffy, you have been such a good cat. Today, I will allow you to pick a new toy from the store." Fluffy was very happy and excited. He meowed and danced around the room. At the store, Fluffy saw a big, red ball. He wanted it so much. "Can I have this ball, please?" Fluffy asked Lucy. Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, Fluffy! You can have the ball." They went home and played with the new toy all day long. Fluffy and Lucy were very happy and had lots of fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to collect dough to make cookies. He went to his friend, Sue, to ask for help. "Sue, can you help me collect dough?" Tim asked. Sue said, "Yes, Tim! Let's collect dough together!" They went to the store to buy dough. On the way, Tim tripped and fell. His knee hurt a lot. Sue helped him up and said, "Let's be more careful when we walk, Tim." Tim said, "Thank you, Sue. I will be more careful." After they got the dough, they made cookies together. Tim and Sue shared the cookies with their friends. Everyone was happy and enjoyed the cookies. The moral of the story is to be careful and to always help your friends when they are hurt.

One day, a gentle cat and a small dog found a big case. They did not know what was inside. The cat and dog wanted to share the case, but they could not open it. They tried and tried, but it was too hard. Then, a tiny mouse came by and saw the cat and dog with the case. The mouse wanted to help. The cat and dog were not sure if the mouse could do it, but they let the mouse try. The mouse opened the case with a small key it had. Inside the case, there were many toys and treats. The cat, dog, and mouse were very happy. They all shared the toys and treats from the case. The cat and dog learned that even a tiny mouse can do big things. They all became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a warm, sunny garden, there lived a little boy named Tim and his best friend, a ball. Tim loved to play with his ball every day. They would run and kick in the garden, laughing and having fun. One day, while they were playing, a girl named Sue came to the garden. She saw Tim and the ball and wanted to play too. Tim was happy to share his fun with Sue. So, they played together, and they all had a great time. As they played, the ball suddenly kicked too high and got stuck in a tree. Tim and Sue were sad and didn't know what to do. A friendly bird saw them and said, "Don't worry, I will help you get your ball back." The bird flew up and pushed the ball out of the tree. Tim, Sue, and the ball were so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Tim, Sue, and the ball played together in the warm garden every day. They learned that sharing is good and that friends can help each other when they need it. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy with a motor that he loved to play with every day. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her cut some paper for a project. "Tim, can you help me cut this paper?" asked Mom. Tim didn't want to stop playing with his toy, so he said, "No, Mom, I want to play with my motor toy." Mom was sad, but she knew Tim was stubborn. She thought of a fun idea. "What if we use your motor toy to help cut the paper?" she asked. Tim's eyes lit up, and he agreed to help. Together, Tim and his mom found a way to use his motor toy to cut the paper. They had fun working together and finished the project quickly. Tim learned that helping others can be fun, even when he was stubborn.
