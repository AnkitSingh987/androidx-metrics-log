Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Forest and the Messy

Once upon a time, there was a big, messy dinosaur. He lived in a big forest. He liked to play with his friends, the little animals. They played all day long. One day, the messy dinosaur had to leave his forest to find some food. He said goodbye to his friends and went on a long walk. He saw many new things and met new friends along the way. When the messy dinosaur came back, he had a big party with all of his friends. They ate lots of food and played fun games. Everyone was happy the messy dinosaur was back. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a big, powerful van was driving down the road. It was very fast and loud. The van was going to the park to play with the other cars. On the way, the van saw a small car. The small car was suffering because it was stuck in the mud. "Help me, please," said the small car to the powerful van. The van stopped and thought for a while. It knew that it was strong and could help the small car. So, the van decided to help the small car. The powerful van pulled the small car out of the mud. The small car was very happy and thanked the van. They both went to the park and played together. They had lots of fun and became good friends. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it, because it is a good thing to do.

Anna and Ben love to go to the park with their mom. They like to run, slide, swing and play with their ball. Sometimes they see a vendor who sells ice cream and treats. They always ask their mom if they can have some, but she says they have to eat lunch first. One day, they see a playful puppy near the vendor. The puppy is brown and fluffy and has a red collar. Anna and Ben want to pet the puppy, but the vendor says no. He says the puppy is his and he is busy. He does not like children to bother him. Anna and Ben are sad and angry. They think the vendor is mean and the puppy is lonely. They wait for the vendor to pause and look away. Then they run to the puppy and try to hug him. But the puppy does not like that. He barks and bites and scratches. He makes Anna and Ben cry. The vendor hears the noise and turns around. He sees Anna and Ben with the puppy. He is very mad. He yells at them and tells them to go away. He says they are naughty and rude. He says they hurt his puppy. Mom hears the yelling and comes to see what is wrong. She sees Anna and Ben with tears and scratches. She sees the vendor with the puppy. She is sorry and angry. She says sorry to the vendor and scolds Anna and Ben. She says they should not touch things that are not theirs. She says they should listen to grown-ups. She says they have to go home and no ice cream for them. Anna and Ben are very sorry and ashamed. They say sorry to the vendor and the puppy. They say sorry to mom. They hold mom's hand and walk home. They learn their lesson and promise to be good. They hope the puppy is okay and the vendor is not too mad. They hope they can go to the park again and play nicely.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was frustrated because he could not fly high in the sky like other birds. He wanted to escape his home and see the big world. One day, Tim saw a big tree. He thought, "If I can climb the tree, I can jump and fly in the sky." So, Tim started to climb the tree. It was hard, but he did not give up. He wanted to fly so much. Finally, Tim reached the top of the tree. He looked down and saw his home far away. He was scared but wanted to fly. Tim jumped from the tree to fly in the sky. But, he could not fly. Tim fell down and got hurt. He was sad and cried. Tim learned that he should not try to escape without knowing how to fly.
