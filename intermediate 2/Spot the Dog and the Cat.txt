Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a chubby dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig in the yard. One day, Spot found a big bone. He was very happy. Spot wanted to hide the bone so no one could take it. He dug a big hole and put the bone in it. Then, Spot used his paws to cover the hole with dirt. He wagged his tail and went to take a nap. When Spot woke up, he went to the yard to get his bone. But the bone was not in the hole! Spot was sad. Then, he saw his friend, a chubby cat named Whiskers, playing with the bone. Whiskers had found the bone and wanted to share it with Spot. Now, both friends were happy and played with the bone together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a large dog named Spot. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park to play. At the park, they saw a little bird. The bird was hungry. Tim said, "Spot, we should feed the bird." Spot wagged his tail and agreed. Tim found some bread on the ground and gave it to the bird. The bird ate the bread and was very happy. After that, Tim and Spot played with the bird. They ran, jumped, and laughed. The bird flew around them, and they all had a great time. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Bye bird, we will come back to play again." The bird flew away, and Tim and Spot went home, excited for their next adventure.

One day, a thin cat and a big dog lived in a house. They did not like each other. The cat would tell the dog, "You are too loud!" The dog would tell the cat, "You are too small!" One day, they saw a ball on a high shelf. They both wanted the ball. The cat tried to jump, but she was too thin and weak. The dog tried to reach, but he was too big and clumsy. They both could not get the ball. They were sad. Then, the cat had an idea. She told the dog, "Let's help each other!" The dog agreed. The thin cat climbed on the big dog's back. She jumped and got the ball. They both were happy and felt success. From that day, they became friends and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Max. Max was very good at protecting his family. One day, Max and his family went for a walk. They came to an open field. In the field, Max saw a big cross. Max said to his family, "Look at the big cross!" His family looked and said, "Wow, Max! You found a big cross!" Max wanted to protect the cross. He barked at the birds that flew near it. Max's family was proud of him for protecting the cross. When it was time to go home, Max said, "Bye, big cross!" Max and his family walked back to their house, happy and safe.

One day, a boy named Tom was very excited. He got a new bicycle for his birthday. Tom wanted to ride it all day. He put on his helmet and went outside. Tom saw his friend, Sue, in the park. "Hi, Sue!" he shouted. "Look at my new bicycle!" Sue was happy for Tom. She asked, "Can I try it?" Tom said, "Yes, but be careful!" Sue rode the bicycle and Tom watched. They both had fun. After a while, they got tired. They sat under a tree and talked. Sue said, "Thank you for letting me try your bicycle, Tom!" Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sue!" They played together until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was an amazing automobile. It was red and shiny. The automobile could talk and play with kids. It had many friends. One day, the amazing automobile had a big problem. It could not sleep at night. It was very tired. The automobile tried to close its eyes, but it could not sleep. It felt sad. The amazing automobile asked its friends for help. One of the friends had an idea. They sang a soft song to the automobile. The song made the automobile feel sleepy. Finally, the amazing automobile could sleep. It was so happy and said thank you to its friends. They all played together the next day.
