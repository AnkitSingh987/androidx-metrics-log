Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gloomy Day and the Stone

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gloomy day. The sky was gray, and the rain was coming down. All the people in the town were sad because they could not play outside. But there was one little boy named Tim who wanted to find a way to make the day better. Tim thought about what he could do. He remembered his mom telling him about a special law that said if you rub a magic stone, the sun will come out. Tim wanted to find this magic stone and make the day sunny again. He put on his rain boots and went outside to look for the stone. Tim looked under trees, by the flowers, and in the grass. He finally found the magic stone near a big puddle. Tim picked up the stone and rubbed it with his hands. Suddenly, the rain stopped, and the sun came out. The town was happy again, and everyone went outside to play. Tim was very proud that he could help his friends and make the gloomy day sunny again.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On this farm, there was a lot of hay. The hay was in a big pile. The animals on the farm liked to play in the hay. There was a little cow named Momo, a small pig named Pipo, and a tiny bird named Titi. One day, Momo, Pipo, and Titi were playing near the hay. Momo said, "I want to poke the hay!" Pipo and Titi thought it was a fun idea. So, they all took turns poking the hay. Poke, poke, poke! They laughed and had a great time. But then, they saw a big, terrible bug in the hay. The bug was very scary. Momo, Pipo, and Titi did not want to play with the hay anymore. They ran away from the hay and the bug. Momo said, "Let's not poke the hay again." Pipo and Titi agreed. From that day on, they found new games to play on the farm, far away from the hay and the terrible bug.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He liked to imagine big things. One day, he found a chest in his room. It was not there before. Tom was very excited. Tom called his friend, Sue. "Sue, I found a chest!" he said. "Let's open it!" Sue came over, and they opened the chest. Inside, there were many toys. They were the best toys they had ever seen. They played with the toys all day long, but they did not share with others. Soon, Tom and Sue found out that the toys were not as fun anymore. They wished they had shared with their friends. They learned that sharing is important. From that day on, they made sure to share all their toys with their friends, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, old tree. The tree had many leaves. A small boy named Tim liked to lie under the tree. He loved to look at the sky and see the clouds. One sunny day, Tim found a bubble wand. He blew on it and made many bubbles. The bubbles were round and shiny. They flew up into the air. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. Tim played with the bubbles under the old tree. The bubbles danced in the sky. They made Tim very happy. He lay under the tree and watched the bubbles until the sun went down.

One day, a big, fit cat named Tom went out to play. It started to rain, but Tom did not mind. He saw a big melon on the ground. Tom thought, "What a fun toy!" A little dog named Max saw Tom and the melon. Max said, "Tom, that is my melon! I want to eat it." Tom said, "No, Max. I found it. It is my toy now." Just then, the melon started to roll away. Tom and Max ran after it. They did not know that the melon was alive! The melon said, "I am not a toy or food. I am a melon friend." Tom and Max were surprised. They said, "We are sorry, melon friend." The melon said, "It is okay. Let us all play together." So, Tom, Max, and the melon played in the rain. They were happy and had a fun day.
