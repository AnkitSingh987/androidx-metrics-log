Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy cars, made of steel. One day, he felt uncomfortable because he thought his favorite car was lost. Tom looked everywhere for his car. He asked his mom, "Mom, do you believe my car is lost?" His mom said, "Let's look for it together, Tom." So they started to look for the car. They looked in his room, in the kitchen, and even in the garden. Finally, they found the car under his bed. Tom was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Tom felt comfortable again and played with his steel car all day long.

One day, a rich man went to the store. He wanted to buy beef for his big dinner. He put the beef in his bag and walked home. On the way, he met a nice lady. She said, "Hello, how are you?" The rich man said, "I am good. I have beef for my dinner." They talked and laughed for a while. But when the rich man looked in his bag, the beef was gone! He had lost it. The nice lady said, "Oh no! Let's look for it together." They looked and looked, but they could not find the beef. Then, they saw a big dog. The dog had the beef in its mouth! The dog ran to them and put the beef down. The rich man and the nice lady laughed. They were happy to find the beef. The rich man said, "Thank you for helping me. Let's share the beef and have dinner together." They all had a great dinner and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a thin cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his guitar. Every day, he would sit in the sun and play his guitar. All the animals would come to listen and admire his music. One day, a small bird named Sue came to hear Tom's music. She sat on a branch and listened to the guitar. Sue liked the music very much. She started to sing along with Tom's guitar. The other animals admired Sue's singing too. Tom and Sue became good friends. They played music together every day. The animals loved to listen to them. Tom and Sue were very happy. They knew that they made the other animals happy with their music. And they continued to play their guitar and sing for all their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful cat named Mark. Mark loved to play with his friends. One day, Mark saw a big, pretty toy. He wanted it very much. But the toy belonged to another cat. Mark did a bad thing. He tried to steal the toy. Mark's friend saw him and said, "Mark, you should not steal. It is not nice." Mark felt sad. He knew his friend was right. He did not want to be a bad cat. So, Mark gave the toy back to the other cat. Then, something unexpected happened. The other cat said, "Thank you, Mark. You are a good cat now. I will share my toy with you." Mark and the other cat played together with the toy. Mark learned that sharing is better than stealing. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky king who lived in a big castle. One day, the king was taking a nap when a bee flew into his room. The bee went, "Buzz, buzz!" and woke up the king. The king was not happy to be disturbed. The king said, "Who has disturbed my nap?" He saw the bee and decided to talk to it. The king asked, "Why did you disturb me?" The bee replied, "I am sorry, king. I did not mean to disturb you. I need your help." The king listened to the bee's story. The bee told the king that all the flowers in the kingdom were gone, and the bees had no food. The king was surprised and said, "I will help you, little bee." He called all his people and told them to plant more flowers for the bees. Soon, the kingdom was full of flowers, and the bees had food again. The king and the bee became good friends. The lucky king learned that sometimes, being disturbed can lead to good things. And they all lived happily ever after.
