Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a very important paper to mail. It was an exam. She worked very hard on it. She wanted to make sure it got to her teacher. Lucy put on her comfortable shoes and went to the mailbox. She put the exam inside and closed the mailbox door. She felt happy and proud. Later, Lucy's teacher got the exam. The teacher was very happy with Lucy's work. Lucy got a big smiley face on her paper. Lucy felt great and danced in her comfortable shoes.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big room with a small light. The light was not bright, but Tim liked it. He liked to hang his toys near the light. One day, Tim saw a big, filthy bug near the light. "Go away, bug!" Tim said. But the bug did not go away. It liked the light too. Tim did not want the bug near his light. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a big, filthy bug near my light. Can you help me?" Mom went to Tim's room and saw the bug. She took a long stick and moved the bug away from the light. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" Now, Tim could hang his toys near the light without the big, filthy bug.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue had a pet cat named Lily. Sue and Lily loved to play together in the house. One day, Sue saw a big tree with lots of pretty leaves. She thought it would be fun to pick the leaves and bring them home. Sue said to Lily, "I will pick some leaves, and we can play with them at home." Lily meowed and seemed excited. Sue went outside and picked many leaves. She brought them back to the tidy little house and put them on the floor. Sue and Lily played with the leaves all day. They laughed and had so much fun. But then, they looked around and saw that the house was not tidy anymore. The leaves were everywhere! Sue knew they had to clean up the mess they made. Sue said, "Lily, we need to pick up the leaves and make our house tidy again." Lily meowed and helped Sue pick up the leaves. They worked together, and soon the house was tidy once more. Sue and Lily were happy that they had fun and learned to clean up after themselves.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the farm with his mom. They saw many animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. Tom liked to run and play with the animals. He also liked to zip around the farm. Tom and his mom walked to the corn field. The corn was tall and green. They picked some corn to take home. When they put the corn in the bag, they saw something cold and wet inside. It was a big block of ice! Tom and his mom were very surprised. They didn't know how the ice got in the corn field. They showed the ice to the farmer. The farmer laughed and said, "I put the ice there to keep the corn cold and fresh. Now it will taste very good!" Tom and his mom laughed too. They took the cold corn home and had a yummy dinner.

Lila was a little girl who liked to act. She had many costumes and toys that she used to pretend to be different people and animals. One day, she found an ancient collar in her grandma's closet. It was shiny and had many colors and shapes on it. She put the collar around her neck and said, "Now I am a queen! I rule over this land and everyone must do what I say!" She walked around the house with a crown and a wand, and ordered her stuffed animals to bow and serve her. But then she saw her dog, Max, sleeping on the couch. He did not bow or serve her. He just snored and wagged his tail. Lila was angry. She said, "Max, you are a bad dog! You must obey me, the queen! Get up and fetch me a bone!" Max opened one eye and looked at Lila. He did not like the collar on her neck. He thought it was his toy. He jumped off the couch and ran towards Lila. He barked and tried to bite the collar. Lila was scared. She said, "No, Max, stop! This is not your toy! This is my collar! It is very old and special! Grandma gave it to me!" Max did not listen. He pulled the collar with his teeth and tore it off Lila's neck. He ran away with it and hid under the bed. Lila cried. She said, "Max, you are a very bad dog! You ruined my collar and my game! I am not a queen anymore!" Grandma heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Lila crying and Max hiding. She said, "What happened, Lila? Why are you so sad?" Lila told Grandma what Max did. She said, "He took my collar and broke it! It was so pretty and ancient! You gave it to me, Grandma!" Grandma hugged Lila and said, "I'm sorry, Lila. I did not give you the collar. I forgot to put it away. It was not a collar, it was a necklace. It belonged to my grandma, who was a queen in a faraway land. She gave it to me when I was a little girl, and I loved it very much. But it is not a toy, it is very fragile and precious. You should not have played with it, Lila." Lila felt sorry. She said, "I'm sorry, Grandma. I did not know. I just wanted to act like a queen. I did not mean to hurt your necklace. Can you fix it, Grandma?" Grandma smiled and said, "Maybe I can, Lila. But you have to promise me something. You have to promise me that you will not play with things that are not yours, and that you will be kind to Max. He is a good dog, he just thought you were playing with him. He did not know the necklace was important. Can you promise me that, Lila?" Lila nodded and said, "I promise, Grandma. I will not play with things that are not mine, and I will be kind to Max. I love you, Grandma. And I love Max, too." Grandma kissed Lila and said, "I love you, too, Lila. And I love Max, too. Now let's go and find him and give him a treat. And then we can play a different game. How about we act like pirates, Lila?" Lila smiled and said, "Yes, Grandma. Let's act like pirates. That sounds fun!"
