Title: A disservice to dignity of labour Part Three

A disservice to dignity of labour

The political establishment must understand that no Bill can quell that aspiration. It is appropriate to remind the ruling party that the MGNREGA was passed in 2005 amidst chants of "rozgar guarantee zindabad" with a historic consensus between all parties. The Bharatiya Janata Party's attempt to stealthily repeal the law is not just a disservice to the dignity of labour, but also undermines the basic tenets of democratic consensus-building.

By Nikhil Dey & Aruna Roy The writers are social activists and founder members of the Mazdoor Kisan Shakti Sangathan
