Title: Going to the Museum
Category: Beginner

Saturday morning was cool and cloudy. Emma and her mother stood on the sidewalk outside their apartment building, waiting. A few minutes later, a silver car pulled up to the curb. It was Sarah and her mother. 

"Get in!" Sarah shouted happily from the back seat. 
Emma and her mother climbed into the car. The two girls sat together in the back, holding their notebooks and pens tightly. They were ready for their big research trip. 

The City Museum was located in the center of the city. As they drove closer, the buildings became older and much grander. Finally, Sarah's mother parked the car on a quiet street. 
They walked around the corner and stopped. 

"Wow," Emma said, looking up. 
The museum was not a normal building. It was huge. It looked like an ancient Greek temple. It had wide stone stairs leading up to the entrance. Across the front of the building, there were eight massive stone columns holding up a heavy roof. The building was made of pale grey stone, and it looked very old and very important. 

"It looks like a palace," Sarah whispered. 
"It is a palace for history and science," Emma's mother said. "Let's go inside."

They walked up the wide stone stairs. Emma felt very small standing next to the massive columns. They pushed open the heavy wooden doors and stepped into the main hall. 

The inside of the museum was just as impressive as the outside. The ceiling was incredibly high, made of glass and steel. The floor was covered in shiny white and black marble tiles. When they walked, their shoes made a sharp 'click-clack' sound that echoed through the huge room. It was a very quiet place. People spoke in soft whispers, respecting the history around them. 

"First, we need tickets," Sarah's mother said. 
They walked to a long wooden desk. Behind the desk, a friendly man in a grey suit gave them four paper tickets and a glossy museum map. 
"Enjoy your visit," the man smiled. "If you need any help, just ask one of the guides in the blue jackets."

Emma and Sarah opened the map. It was very complicated. The museum had four different floors and dozens of different rooms. 
"Okay, where do we start?" Emma asked, looking at the colorful squares on the paper. 
"Look," Sarah pointed to a large green square on the first floor. "This room is called 'Giants of the Past'. It has a picture of a dinosaur bone."
"And look here," Emma pointed to a dark blue square on the top floor. "This room is called 'Journey to the Stars'. That must be the space room."

The two girls looked at each other. 
"Dinosaurs first?" Sarah suggested. 
"Definitely dinosaurs first," Emma agreed. 

They walked across the shiny marble floor, following the signs for 'Giants of the Past'. At the entrance to the room, there was a tall man wearing a blue museum jacket. He was a guide. 
"Hello, girls," the guide said kindly. "Are you here to see the dinosaurs?"
"Yes, sir," Emma said. "We are doing a school project."
"Well, you came to the right place," the guide smiled, stepping aside to let them in. "Prepare to be amazed. They are very big."

Emma and Sarah held their notebooks tightly and walked through the wide doorway. The light in the room was a little darker, and the air felt cool. They turned the corner and stopped completely still. The guide was right. They were about to see something truly amazing. The research for their school project had officially begun.
