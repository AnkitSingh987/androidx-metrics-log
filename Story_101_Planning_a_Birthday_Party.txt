Title: Planning a Birthday Party
Category: Beginner

It was a quiet Tuesday evening, and fourteen-year-old Mia was sitting at the kitchen table with her parents. Next week was a very important week. It was Mia's fifteenth birthday. She was holding a pen and looking at a blank piece of paper. 

"So, Mia," her father said, taking a sip of his tea. "What are your plans for your birthday? It is a big milestone. We should celebrate properly."
"I don't know yet," Mia sighed. "I was thinking about having a party, but I can't decide what kind of party to have. What do you guys think?"

Her mother sat down next to her. "Well, last year we had a barbecue in the back garden," she remembered. "That was very nice, but the weather is a bit unpredictable this year. It might rain."
"I agree," Mia said, nodding. "I don't want a barbecue this year. I want to do something different. Maybe something a little more exciting."

"We could rent a lane at the bowling alley," her father suggested enthusiastically. "You love bowling. You could invite ten friends, and we could order pizzas. Bowling is always fun, and it doesn't matter if it rains."

Mia thought about the idea for a moment. She liked bowling, but she had been to three bowling parties this year already. It felt a little repetitive. 
"Bowling is okay," she said slowly, expressing her honest opinion. "But to be honest, Dad, I think it is a bit boring. Everyone does bowling for their birthday. I want something more unique."

"That is fair enough," her father replied, not offended at all. "It is your birthday, so it is your choice. What about the cinema? We could pay for you and your friends to see the new action movie, and then we could go to a nice restaurant for burgers afterwards."

Mia tapped her pen on the table. The cinema was a good idea, but it was very dark, and you couldn't really talk to your friends while the movie was playing. A party was supposed to be social. 

"I have an idea," her mother said suddenly. Her eyes were bright. "Have you ever heard of an escape room?"
"An escape room?" Mia repeated, curious. "I have heard of them, but I have never been to one. How does it work?"

"It is a very popular new activity," her mother explained. "You and your friends are locked in a themed room for one hour. You have to work together as a team to find hidden clues, solve difficult puzzles, and unlock the door before the time runs out. It requires a lot of brainpower and teamwork."

Mia's face lit up with a huge smile. "That sounds amazing!" she said. "I love solving puzzles, and it sounds really exciting. I think my friends would love it too. We can work together to escape."
"I think it is a brilliant idea," her father agreed. "It is definitely unique. I will look online tonight and see if there is an escape room near our house. We can book it for next Saturday afternoon."

"Thank you, Mom! Thank you, Dad!" Mia said, feeling a rush of excitement. 
She finally knew what she wanted. The blank piece of paper didn't seem scary anymore. She wrote 'ESCAPE ROOM PARTY' in large capital letters at the top of the page. 

"Now," her mother said practically. "An escape room usually has a limit on how many people can play. Usually, it is a maximum of six people. So, you need to think carefully about who you are going to invite. You can only choose five friends."
Mia nodded seriously. "I know. That will be the hardest part of the planning. I will make a list of my closest friends right now."

The evening was no longer quiet. The kitchen was filled with energy as they discussed the future plans. They talked about what time the party should start, how they would travel to the escape room, and what kind of food they would eat afterwards. Planning a birthday party was almost as much fun as the party itself, especially when the plan was a secret escape room adventure.
