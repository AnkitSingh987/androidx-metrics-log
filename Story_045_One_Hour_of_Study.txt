Title: One Hour of Study
Category: Beginner

It is Saturday morning. Saturday is usually a day for playing. It is a day for riding a bicycle. It is a day for watching television. 
But today, David has a plan. He is twelve years old. Next week, he has a big science exam. He must study. 

David is in his bedroom. His bedroom is tidy. He cleaned his desk yesterday. There are no toys on his desk. There are no comic books on his desk. There is only his science book, his notebook, and a blue pen. 
He also has a small digital clock on his desk. The numbers on the clock are red. 
It is exactly ten o'clock. 10:00. 

"I will study for one hour," David says to himself. "I will study from ten o'clock to eleven o'clock. One hour of hard work. Then I can play."
One hour is sixty minutes. It is a good amount of time to learn something new. 

David sits on his chair. He opens his science book. The chapter is about the human body. He needs to learn about the heart and the lungs. 
He starts to read. He reads the first page slowly. He reads every word carefully. 
He learns that the heart is a muscle. He learns that it pumps blood around the body. 

He takes his blue pen. He opens his notebook. He writes a heading: 'The Heart'. 
Under the heading, he writes a short sentence. 'The heart is a strong muscle. It pumps blood.'
Writing helps him remember. 

He looks at the clock. It is ten-fifteen. 10:15. Fifteen minutes have passed. 
He is doing well. He is not tired. 
He turns the page in his science book. Now he reads about the lungs. He learns that we have two lungs. He learns that they take air into the body. They take oxygen from the air. 

He takes his pen again. He writes a new heading in his notebook: 'The Lungs'. 
He writes a sentence. 'We have two lungs. They take oxygen from the air.'
He looks at a picture of the lungs in his book. He tries to draw the picture in his notebook. His drawing is not perfect, but it helps him understand. 

He looks at the clock again. It is ten-thirty. 10:30. 
Half an hour is gone. Half an hour is left. 
David starts to feel a little restless. He wants to look out the window. He wants to see if his friends are playing outside. He can hear a dog barking far away. 
He moves in his chair. He puts his pen down. 

"No," David says to himself. "I must focus. Only thirty more minutes."
He takes a deep breath. He picks up his pen again. 

He reads the next page. It is about how the heart and lungs work together. This is the difficult part. He reads the paragraph twice. He reads it a third time. 
Now he understands. The blood goes to the lungs to get oxygen. Then the blood goes back to the heart. Then the heart pumps the oxygen to the body. 
It is like a circle. 

He writes a long sentence in his notebook explaining this circle. He draws arrows to show how the blood moves. 
He is working very hard. His brain is busy. He forgets about the window. He forgets about his friends outside. He is only thinking about the human body. 

He looks at the clock. It is ten-fifty. 10:50. 
Ten more minutes. 
He uses the last ten minutes to review his work. He closes his science book. He looks only at his notebook. 
He reads his notes from the beginning. 
'The heart is a strong muscle...' 
He covers the notebook with his hand. He tries to say the words from his memory. He remembers almost everything. 

The numbers on the digital clock change. 
10:57. 
10:58. 
10:59. 
And then... 11:00. 
It is eleven o'clock. 

David drops his pen on the desk. He closes his notebook with a slap. 
"Finished!" he says loudly. 
He stretches his arms high above his head. He stretches his legs. He feels very good. He feels proud of himself. 

He studied hard for exactly one hour. He did not stop. He did not look at his phone. He learned a lot about the heart and the lungs. He is ready for his science exam. 
Now, the hard work is over. The rest of the Saturday is his free time. 
He stands up. He runs out of his bedroom. He runs down the stairs. 
"Mother!" he calls. "I finished my study! I am going outside to ride my bicycle!"
"Have fun, David!" his mother answers. 
David opens the front door and runs into the sunshine. The play time feels much better after one hour of good study.
