Title: The Early Morning
Category: Beginner

It is morning. The sun is in the sky. The sun is yellow and bright. It is a beautiful morning. 
Tom is in his bed. He is sleeping. His eyes are closed. He is very quiet. The room is quiet. 
Then, a bird sings outside. The bird is on a tree. The tree is near the window. Tom hears the bird. He opens his eyes. He looks around the room. 

The room is small, but it is nice. The walls are white. The floor is brown. There is a small desk. There is a chair. There are some books on the desk. Tom likes his room.
Tom looks at the clock. The clock is on a small table near his bed. It is six o'clock in the morning. It is early. Tom likes the early morning. 
He sits up in his bed. He stretches his arms. He is not tired. He is ready for a new day.

Tom gets out of bed. He stands on the floor. The floor is a little cold. He puts on his slippers. The slippers are blue. They are soft and warm. Now his feet are warm.
He walks to the window. He opens the window. The air is fresh. The air is cool. He breathes in the fresh air. He feels good. He feels happy. 
He looks outside. He sees the tree. He sees the bird. The bird is small and brown. 
"Good morning, bird," Tom says. 
The bird sings again. 
"You have a beautiful song," Tom says.

Tom leaves his bedroom. He walks down the hall. The house is quiet. His family is still sleeping. His mother is sleeping. His father is sleeping. His sister is sleeping. Her name is Anna. Anna is a little girl. 
Tom walks slowly. He does not want to wake them up. He is very quiet.

Tom goes to the bathroom. He turns on the light. The light is bright. He washes his face with cold water. The water feels good. It wakes him up completely. 
He takes his toothbrush. It is a green toothbrush. He puts toothpaste on it. He brushes his teeth. He brushes them well. He washes his mouth with water. He wipes his face with a towel. The towel is white and clean. 

Now, Tom is clean and fresh. He goes to the kitchen. The kitchen is big. It has a big window. The morning light comes through the window. 
Tom is thirsty. He wants to drink something. He takes a glass from the cupboard. It is a clear glass. He goes to the sink. He turns on the tap. Cold water comes out. He fills the glass with water. He drinks the water. It is very good. 

He puts the glass on the table. The table is made of wood. It is round. There are four chairs around the table. 
Tom looks at the fridge. He is a little hungry. He opens the fridge. What is inside? He sees milk. He sees eggs. He sees butter. He sees some fruit. There are apples and bananas. 
Tom takes an apple. It is a red apple. It is big and round. He washes the apple under the water. He dries it. He bites the apple. It is sweet and crunchy. He eats the apple slowly.

While he eats, he looks out the kitchen window. He sees the street. The street is quiet. There are no cars. There are no people. It is still very early. 
Tom likes this quiet time. He likes to be alone in the morning. It is peaceful. 

Suddenly, he hears a noise. It comes from the hall. He listens. 
Footsteps. Someone is walking. 
The kitchen door opens. It is his mother. She is wearing a pink robe. Her hair is a little messy. She is rubbing her eyes. 
"Good morning, Tom," she says. Her voice is soft.
"Good morning, Mother," Tom says. 
"You are up early today," she says.
"Yes," Tom says. "I woke up at six o'clock."
"Did you sleep well?" she asks.
"Yes, I slept very well, thank you," Tom says. "Did you sleep well?"
"Yes, I did," she says. 

She walks to the kitchen counter. "I am going to make some tea," she says. "Do you want some tea, Tom?"
"Yes, please," Tom says. "I would like some tea."
His mother takes a small pot. She puts water in the pot. She puts the pot on the stove. She turns on the stove. The water will get hot soon.
"What are you doing today?" his mother asks.
"I am going to school today," Tom says. "It is Monday."
"Do you have all your books?" she asks.
"Yes, my books are in my bag," he answers.
"That is good," she says. 

The water is hot. The water is boiling. His mother puts tea leaves in the water. The water turns brown. It smells good. 
She takes two cups. She pours the tea into the cups. 
"Do you want milk in your tea?" she asks.
"No, thank you," Tom says. "But I want some sugar, please."
She gives him a spoon of sugar. He stirs his tea. He takes a sip. It is hot, but it is good. 

"Where is Father?" Tom asks.
"He is still sleeping," his mother says. "He will wake up soon."
"And Anna?" Tom asks.
"Anna is sleeping too," she says. "You know Anna. She loves to sleep."
Tom smiles. "Yes, she does."

They sit at the table. They drink their tea. They do not talk much. It is a quiet morning. The sun gets higher in the sky. The kitchen gets brighter. 
More birds are singing outside. The street is not so quiet now. Tom hears a car. He hears a dog barking. The day is starting. 

Tom finishes his tea. He puts his cup in the sink. 
"Thank you for the tea, Mother," he says.
"You are welcome, Tom," she says. 
"I am going to my room now," Tom says. "I need to get dressed for school."
"Okay, Tom," she says. "Have a good day."
"You too, Mother," he says.

Tom walks back to his room. His room is full of light now. The early morning is over. A busy day is starting. But Tom is happy. He had a good start. He is ready for school. He is ready for the day. He takes his school uniform from the chair. It is time to get ready.
