Title: The Book I Wanted
Category: Beginner

It is Thursday afternoon. School is finished. Tom is at the public library. He is looking for a very special book. 
Tom loves dinosaurs. He knows many things about dinosaurs. He knows about the big T-Rex. He knows about the dinosaurs with long necks. He likes to draw them in his notebook. 

His friend, Peter, told him about a new book. 
"It is a very good book," Peter said. "It has big pictures. The name of the book is 'The Big Book of Dinosaurs'."
Tom really wants to read this book. 

Tom walks to the children's section in the library. He knows where the dinosaur books are. They are on a low wooden shelf near the window. 
He stands in front of the shelf. He looks at the books. He reads the titles on the sides of the books. 
'All About Dinosaurs'. No, that is not it. 
'Dinosaurs and Other Animals'. No, that is not it. 
'The World of the T-Rex'. No, that is not it. 

Tom looks at every book on the shelf. He looks from the left to the right. He looks from the top shelf to the bottom shelf. 
'The Big Book of Dinosaurs' is not there. 

Tom feels sad. Maybe the library does not have the book. Maybe another child borrowed it. 
He turns around. He sees the librarian. The librarian is a tall man with a beard. His name is Mr. Evans. Mr. Evans is putting books on a cart. 

Tom walks over to Mr. Evans. 
"Excuse me," Tom says quietly. 
Mr. Evans stops. He looks down at Tom and smiles. 
"Hello," Mr. Evans says. "How can I help you today?"
"I am looking for a book," Tom says. "But I cannot find it on the shelf."
"What is the name of the book?" Mr. Evans asks. 
"It is called 'The Big Book of Dinosaurs'," Tom says. 

Mr. Evans nods his head. "Ah, yes. That is a very popular book. Many children want to read it. Let me check the computer for you."
Tom follows Mr. Evans to his desk. The desk has a computer on it. 
Mr. Evans types the name of the book on the keyboard. Click, clack, click. He looks at the screen. 

"Well, Tom," Mr. Evans says. "The computer says we have one copy of this book. And the computer says it is here in the library. It is not borrowed."
"But I looked on the dinosaur shelf," Tom says. "It is not there."
"Sometimes, people put books back in the wrong place," Mr. Evans explains. "Or maybe someone left it on a table. Let's go and look together."

Mr. Evans and Tom walk back to the children's section. 
First, they look on the tables. There are some books on the tables, but not the dinosaur book. 
Next, they look at the shelves near the dinosaur books. Sometimes a book is just one shelf away. 
Mr. Evans looks at the books about space. No. 
He looks at the books about cars. No. 

Then, Mr. Evans looks at a small cart near the door. The cart is full of books that people returned today. The librarian must put these books back on the shelves later. 
Mr. Evans looks through the pile of books on the cart. 
Suddenly, he pulls out a large, heavy book with a bright green cover. There is a huge picture of a T-Rex on the front. 

"Is this the book you wanted?" Mr. Evans asks. He holds up the book. 
Tom's eyes open wide. "Yes! That is it! 'The Big Book of Dinosaurs'!"
"Someone returned it just an hour ago," Mr. Evans says. "It did not go back to the shelf yet. You are very lucky, Tom."

Tom takes the heavy book with two hands. "Thank you very much, Mr. Evans. Thank you for helping me find it."
"You are very welcome," Mr. Evans says. "Librarians love to help people find good books. That is our job."

Tom carries the heavy green book to the checkout desk. He is very happy. He gives the book and his library card to the lady at the desk. She scans the book. Beep! 
"Enjoy your reading," she says. 

Tom puts the big book into his school bag. The bag is heavier now, but Tom does not mind. He walks out of the library into the sunshine. 
He is going straight home. He is going to sit on his bed. He is going to open 'The Big Book of Dinosaurs'. He is going to look at all the big pictures. He is glad he asked for help. The librarian is a good person to know.
