Title: Feeling Better
Category: Beginner

It is Thursday morning. Sam opens his eyes. The first thing he notices is that he feels different. His head does not hurt at all. His throat is not sore. He does not feel hot, and he does not feel weak. 
He sits up in his bed. He stretches his arms high above his head. He takes a deep breath. 
He feels good. He feels like himself again. 

He jumps out of bed. His legs feel strong. He is not tired. The sickness is gone. The rest and the pink medicine worked. 
He walks to the window and opens the curtains. The sun is bright, and the sky is blue. It looks like a beautiful day. 

Sam walks out of his bedroom and goes down the stairs. He walks quickly. He is very hungry. For two days, he only ate soup and toast. Now, his stomach is making a loud noise. He wants real food. 

He walks into the kitchen. His mother is making tea. 
"Good morning!" Sam says loudly. 
His mother turns around. She looks surprised. 
"Sam! You are out of bed," she says. She walks over to him and puts her hand on his forehead. "Your skin is cool. You do not have a fever anymore."

"I know," Sam smiles. "I feel perfectly fine. My headache is gone. And I am starving! Can I have a big breakfast?"
His mother laughs happily. "Yes, of course you can. A hungry boy is a healthy boy. What do you want to eat?"
"I want eggs, toast, and a big glass of milk, please," Sam says. 

He sits at the kitchen table. He waits while his mother cooks the eggs. The smell of the food makes him even more hungry. 
When she puts the plate in front of him, he eats everything very fast. The food tastes wonderful. When you are sick, food does not taste good. But when you are healthy, everything is delicious. 

"Take it easy," his mother says. "Do not eat too fast."
"I cannot help it," Sam says. "I was so hungry."
He drinks his milk and wipes his mouth. 

"Now," his mother says. "You must still take your medicine. You have to finish the bottle."
She pours the pink liquid into the small cup. Sam drinks it. He still does not like the fake strawberry taste, but he does not complain. The medicine helped him get better, so he is grateful for it. 

"Can I go to school today?" Sam asks. 
His mother thinks for a moment. "You feel better, but you were very sick yesterday. I think you should stay home one more day to be completely strong. You can go back to school tomorrow on Friday."
Sam is a little disappointed, but he understands. "Okay," he says. "But I do not want to stay in bed today."
"You don't have to stay in bed," his mother says. "You can play in the living room. You can watch a movie or play with your toys. Just do not run around outside yet."

Sam goes to the living room. He brings his building blocks. He sits on the rug and builds a tall tower. It feels great to play again. Yesterday, he was too tired to even lift a block. Today, he builds a tower, a bridge, and a small city. 

In the afternoon, he feels bored, but it is a good kind of bored. He is bored because he has energy. He wants to do something active. 
He helps his mother wash the dishes. He helps her fold the laundry. 
"You are very helpful today," she says. 
"I have too much energy," Sam laughs. 

That evening, he eats a big dinner with his parents. He talks a lot. He tells them about the mystery book he read in bed. He tells them about the block city he built. 
"It is good to have you back, Sam," his father says. "The house was too quiet when you were sick in bed."

Sam goes to sleep easily that night. He is not tired from being sick; he is tired from a good day of playing and eating. He is excited for tomorrow. Tomorrow, he will put on his uniform, take his school bag, and go back to his normal life. He is very happy to be healthy again.
