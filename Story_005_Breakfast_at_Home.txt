Title: Breakfast at Home
Category: Beginner

It is Sunday morning. It is a slow and happy morning. There is no school today. There is no work today. The family can sleep late. The family can eat breakfast together. 

Mr. Davis is awake first. He is the father. He goes to the kitchen. He likes Sunday mornings. He likes to cook breakfast for his family. He washes his hands. He puts on an apron. The apron is white. It keeps his clothes clean. 
He opens the fridge. He looks inside. He takes out eggs, butter, and milk. He takes out some bacon. He takes out orange juice. He puts everything on the kitchen counter. 

Then, Mrs. Davis comes into the kitchen. She is the mother. She is wearing her comfortable clothes. She smiles.
"Good morning, dear," she says.
"Good morning," he says. "Are you hungry?"
"Yes, I am very hungry," she answers. "What are you cooking today?"
"I am making a big breakfast," he says. "I am making eggs, bacon, and toast. And I have orange juice."
"That sounds wonderful," she says. "Can I help you?"
"Yes, please," he says. "Can you make the coffee?"
"Of course," she says. 

Mrs. Davis takes the coffee pot. She puts water in it. She puts coffee powder in it. She turns it on. Soon, the kitchen smells like fresh coffee. It is a very good smell. 
Mr. Davis takes a big pan. He puts it on the stove. He turns on the heat. He puts the bacon in the pan. The bacon makes a loud noise. Sizzle, sizzle! It smells very good too. 
He takes a bowl. He breaks six eggs into the bowl. He adds a little milk. He adds a little salt and pepper. He mixes the eggs with a fork. Beat, beat, beat. 

Now, the children come into the kitchen. There are two children. The boy is Jack. He is twelve. The girl is Lily. She is nine. They are still in their pajamas. 
"Good morning, Mother. Good morning, Father," they say. 
"Good morning, kids," their parents say.
"Wow, it smells great in here," Jack says. 
"I love bacon!" Lily says. She is very happy. 
"Sit at the table, please," their father says. "Breakfast is almost ready."

Jack and Lily sit at the table. The table is clean. Mrs. Davis brings the plates. She brings the forks and knives. She brings glasses for the juice. She brings cups for the coffee. 
She pours orange juice into the glasses. The juice is cold and sweet. Jack and Lily drink some juice. 
"This juice is very good," Lily says. 

The bacon is ready. It is brown and crispy. Mr. Davis puts the bacon on a plate. 
Now he cooks the eggs. He puts some butter in another pan. The butter melts. He pours the mixed eggs into the pan. He stirs the eggs softly. They cook very fast. They are yellow and soft. 
He also makes toast. He puts four pieces of bread in the toaster. The toaster goes 'pop'! The bread is brown and warm. He puts butter on the toast. 

"Breakfast is ready!" Mr. Davis says. He carries the food to the table. He puts the eggs, the bacon, and the toast on the table. 
The family looks at the food. It looks delicious. 
"Thank you for cooking, Father," Jack says.
"You are welcome," his father says. 
They all start to eat. The food is very hot. It is very tasty. The eggs are soft. The bacon is crunchy. The toast is warm. 

"How is the food?" Mr. Davis asks.
"It is excellent," Mrs. Davis says. She drinks her hot coffee. 
"It is the best breakfast," Jack says. He is eating very fast.
"I like the bacon the most," Lily says. She has a little egg on her face. Her mother wipes it with a napkin. 

They talk while they eat. They do not hurry. They have a lot of time. 
"What are we going to do today?" Jack asks. 
"It is a sunny day," his mother says. "We can go to the park."
"Can we play football at the park?" Jack asks. 
"Yes, we can take the football," his father says.
"Can we feed the ducks at the park?" Lily asks. 
"Yes, we can take some old bread for the ducks," her mother says. 

They are all happy. They like to go to the park. 
They finish their food. The plates are empty. They drank all the juice. They drank all the coffee. Their stomachs are full. 
"That was a great breakfast," Jack says. 
"Yes, it was," Lily agrees.
"Now, who is going to wash the dishes?" Mr. Davis asks with a smile. 
"Jack and I will wash the dishes," Lily says. 
"That is a good idea," Mrs. Davis says. "Thank you, children."

Jack and Lily take the dirty plates to the sink. They turn on the water. They use soap. They wash the plates. They talk and laugh while they wash. 
Mr. and Mrs. Davis sit at the table. They drink one more cup of coffee. They watch their children. It is a perfect Sunday morning. They have a full stomach and a happy family. Now they are ready for a fun day at the park.
