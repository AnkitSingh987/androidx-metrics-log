Title: Making a Simple Breakfast
Category: Beginner

It is a quiet Sunday morning. The house is peaceful. 
Ben is an eight-year-old boy. He wakes up early. He is hungry. His stomach is empty. 
He listens. He does not hear his mother. He does not hear his father. They are still sleeping. It is Sunday, so they sleep late. 

Ben does not want to wake them up. He is a big boy. He can make his own breakfast. He wants a simple breakfast. 
He walks to the kitchen. He wears his warm pajamas. He does not wear shoes. 
The kitchen is clean and bright. The morning sun comes through the window. 

Ben thinks about what he wants to eat. He likes eggs, but he cannot use the stove. The stove is hot and dangerous for a young boy. He likes toast, but the toaster is sometimes tricky. 
He decides to have cereal. Cereal is easy. Cereal is safe. Cereal is delicious. 

First, he needs a bowl. He goes to the low cupboard. He opens the door. He sees many bowls. There are white bowls, blue bowls, and yellow bowls. 
Ben chooses a yellow bowl. It is his favorite bowl. He puts the yellow bowl on the kitchen table. 

Next, he needs a spoon. He goes to the cutlery drawer. The drawer is next to the sink. He pulls the drawer open. He sees knives, forks, and spoons. 
He takes a silver spoon. He places the spoon next to the yellow bowl on the table. 

Now, he needs the cereal. The cereal is in a tall cupboard. Ben cannot reach the tall cupboard. It is too high. 
He looks around the kitchen. He sees a small wooden stool. The stool is in the corner. 
He carries the stool to the tall cupboard. He steps onto the stool. Now he is taller. He can reach the cupboard. 
He opens the door. He sees many boxes. He sees a box of cornflakes. He sees a box of chocolate cereal. He sees a box of fruit cereal. 
He chooses the chocolate cereal. He likes chocolate. 

He carefully takes the big box down. He steps off the stool. He carries the box to the table. 
He opens the top of the box. He tilts the box over his yellow bowl. 
'Crunch, crunch, crunch.' 
The brown chocolate cereal falls into the bowl. He fills the bowl halfway. He does not want too much. He puts the box on the table. 

Now, the most important part. He needs milk. Cereal is dry without milk. 
Ben goes to the fridge. The fridge is big and white. He pulls the heavy door open. A cold light comes on. 
He looks inside. He sees the milk on the middle shelf. It is a big plastic bottle with a blue cap. 
He takes the bottle with two hands. It is heavy. He closes the fridge door with his foot. 

He carries the heavy milk to the table. He puts it down. 
He twists the blue cap. It is tight. He twists harder. 'Click.' The cap opens. He puts the cap on the table. 
He lifts the heavy bottle again. He tilts it over his yellow bowl. 
The white milk pours out. 'Splash, splash.' It hits the brown cereal. The milk turns a little brown. 
He stops pouring when the milk is near the top of the cereal. He does not want to spill it. 

He puts the bottle down. He picks up the blue cap. He twists it back on the bottle tightly. 
He is almost ready. He just needs one more thing. 
He goes to the fruit bowl on the counter. He takes a yellow banana. He brings the banana to the table. 
He peels the banana. He breaks the banana into small pieces with his hands. He drops the pieces into his cereal bowl. 
Now his breakfast is perfect. Chocolate cereal, cold milk, and a sweet banana. 

Ben sits down on a chair. He takes his silver spoon. He scoops up some cereal, some milk, and a piece of banana. He puts the spoon in his mouth. 
He chews. It is sweet, crunchy, and cold. It is very good. 
He eats his simple breakfast. The kitchen is very quiet. He can hear the birds singing outside. 

He eats everything in the bowl. He drinks the sweet brown milk at the bottom. His stomach is full. He feels strong and happy. 
He takes his yellow bowl and his silver spoon. He carries them to the sink. He puts them in the sink. 
He takes the milk bottle. He carries it to the fridge. He puts it back on the middle shelf. 
He takes the cereal box. He steps on the stool. He puts the box back in the tall cupboard. 
He puts the stool back in the corner. 

The kitchen is clean again. Ben made his own breakfast. He did not wake his parents. He is a big boy. Now he is ready to play. He goes to his room to find his toys. It is a good start to a Sunday.
