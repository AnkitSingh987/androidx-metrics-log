Title: International law optional for powerful Part Three

International law, 'optional' for powerful states

The challenge in reaffirming the value of international law is that it requires strengthening multilateral institutions, enhancing accountability mechanisms, and cultivating a global political culture that prizes restraint over adventurism. It also requires recognising that international law is not a panacea but a framework  -  a set of shared expectations that guide behaviour even when perfect compliance is elusive. Without it, the world risks returning to a state where power alone determines outcomes.

And in such a world, it is not only the weak who suffer; ultimately, everyone does. Shashi Tharoor is the fourth-term Member of Parliament (Lok Sabha) for Thiruvananthapuram, the Chairman of the Parliamentary Standing Committee on External Affairs and the Sahitya Akademi Award-winning author of 29 books, including Pax Indica (2012) and The New World Disorder (2020)
