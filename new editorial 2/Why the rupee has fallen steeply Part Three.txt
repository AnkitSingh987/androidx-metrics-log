Title: Why the rupee has fallen steeply Part Three

Why the rupee has fallen steeply

A spell of rupee stability lets trade and finance plan ahead, and restores a measure of calm to an economy. Hopefully, peace will endure in West Asia, risk perceptions of oil-importing developing countries will subside, and India's economy can look forward to a more stable external environment in the near future. In some sense, the recent rupee swings in an otherwise strong economy with the highest growth among the G-20 economies, reminds one of Luigi Pirandello, the Italian playright, who in his celebrated play, Six Characters in Search of an Author, said, "Life is full of strange absurdities, which, strangely enough, do not even need to appear plausible, since they are true." By Parthapratim Pal & Partha Ray Pal is with IIM Calcutta, Kolkata and Ray is with NIBM, Pune.
