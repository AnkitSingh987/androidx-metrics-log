Title: A Rainy Morning
Category: Beginner

It is Thursday morning. The alarm clock rings at seven o'clock. 
Emma is sleeping. She hears the alarm. She opens her eyes. 
She listens. She hears another sound. It is not a clock. It is a continuous sound against the window. 
Pitter, patter, pitter, patter. 
It is rain. 

Emma sits up. She looks out the window. The sky is not blue today. The sky is dark grey. The sun is not shining. It is raining heavily. The water is falling from the sky. 
The trees are wet. The street is wet. Everything is wet and cold. 
Emma sighs. She does not like the rain very much. She likes sunny days. 

But she must go to work. She cannot stay in bed. 
She gets out of bed. The room feels a little cold. She puts on her warm slippers. 
She goes to the bathroom. She washes her face. She brushes her teeth. 
Then she goes to her closet to choose her clothes. 

"What should I wear today?" she asks herself. 
She cannot wear her new white shoes. The rain will make them dirty. She cannot wear a thin shirt. She will be cold. 
She chooses a thick green sweater. She chooses black trousers. She chooses her old black boots. The boots are made of rubber. They keep her feet dry in the water. 

She goes to the kitchen. She makes a hot cup of tea. The hot tea warms her hands. She eats a bowl of oatmeal. Oatmeal is good on a cold day. 
She looks at the clock. It is seven-forty. It is time to go. 
She must prepare for the rain outside. 

She goes to the hall. She takes her raincoat from the hook. Her raincoat is long and yellow. It has a hood for her head. She puts on the raincoat. She buttons it up to her neck. 
Next, she needs her umbrella. Her umbrella is red. She finds it in the corner. 
She has her boots. She has her raincoat. She has her umbrella. She is ready for the rain. 

She takes her work bag. She opens the front door. 
The rain is very loud now. The wind is blowing a little. 
Emma steps outside. She opens her red umbrella. It goes 'Pop!' 
The umbrella covers her head. The rain hits the umbrella. Tap, tap, tap. It is a nice sound when you are dry underneath. 

She starts to walk to the bus stop. 
The sidewalk is full of water. There are many puddles. A puddle is a small pool of water on the ground. 
Emma must look at the ground. She does not want to step in a deep puddle. 
Splash! 
She steps in a small puddle. But her feet do not get wet. Her rubber boots protect her. The yellow raincoat keeps her body dry. 

She sees other people on the street. 
They also have umbrellas. There are black umbrellas, blue umbrellas, and green umbrellas. It looks like a garden of colorful mushrooms walking down the street. 
Some people are walking very fast. They want to get out of the rain. Some people are running. 

A car drives past Emma. It drives through a big puddle. 
Whoosh! 
The car splashes dirty water onto the sidewalk. Emma jumps back quickly. The water misses her. 
"That was close," she says. 
She must be careful of cars on a rainy day. 

Emma reaches the bus stop. There is a small shelter at the bus stop. It has a roof. 
She stands under the roof. She closes her red umbrella. She shakes the water off it. 
There are three other people waiting. They are all wearing coats and boots. They look a little cold. 
Nobody is talking. They are all watching the rain and waiting for the bus. 

After five minutes, the big yellow bus arrives. Its lights are on because the morning is so dark. Its windshield wipers are moving fast left and right, clearing the rain from the glass. 
The doors open. Emma gets on the bus. It is warm inside. 
She finds a seat near the window. She sits down. She puts her wet umbrella by her feet. 
She looks out the window. The rain is still falling. The city looks grey and wet. 
It is a rainy morning, but Emma is safe, dry, and warm on the bus. She is ready for her day at work.
