Title: The Train and the Rail

Once upon a time, there was a little train. This train had a broken rail. The train was very sad. It could not go on the rail. One day, a kind man came to see the train. He said, "Don't worry, little train. I will care for you and fix the broken rail." The train was happy. The kind man worked hard to fix the rail. Soon, the rail was not broken anymore. The little train said, "Thank you for caring and fixing the rail!" The kind man smiled, and the little train went on its way. They both were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy was a dependable friend to everyone. She liked to play with her ball and run in the grass. One day, Fluffy saw a small boy sitting on a bench. The boy was weeping. He had lost his toy. Fluffy wanted to help the boy. She walked up to him and said, "Why do you weep? I can help you find your toy." The boy looked at Fluffy and smiled. "Thank you, Fluffy," he said. They looked for the toy together. They looked under the bench, behind the trees, and in the grass. At last, they found the toy near a big rock. The boy was happy, and he hugged Fluffy. From that day on, Fluffy and the boy were best friends. They played together every day, and Fluffy was always dependable. The boy never wept again, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sharon. She was only three years old. She had a very heavy heart because she wanted to go the harbor, but it was so far away. One day, her mommy took her to the harbor. Sharon was so excited, she couldn't believe it. She had never been to the harbor before. At the harbor, they got on a boat. The boat was very high, and Sharon was a bit scared. However, her mommy said, “It’s okay Sharon, everything will be alright.” The boat rocked back and forth in the water, and Sharon said, “Mommy, I'm scared.” Her mommy replied, “Let’s look around, it’s beautiful here. Don’t worry, I’m right here with you.” Sharon looked around and saw the seagulls soaring through the sky. She took a deep breath and felt a bit better. After a while, the captain told them it was time to weigh anchor and head back home. Sharon was sad to go, but she had a smile on her face. She knew that one day she would go back to the beautiful harbor.

Once, there was a rat. It was a rare rat with beautiful, soft fur. Its eyes were bright and it was very brave. The rat went out into the woods and is soon spotted by a little boy. The boy followed the rat, trying to catch it and have some fun. But the rat was too fast and quickly disappeared into the trees. The boy was sad and he said, "Where did you go, rat? I want to play with you!" But there was no answer. The rat had disappeared. The boy took a deep breath and continued on his way. He kept looking for the rat, but it was nowhere to be found. Suddenly, out of the corner of his eye, he saw a big, black cat. The cat had caught the rat! He wanted to save the rare rat, but it was too late. The cat ran away with the rat in his mouth and quickly disappeared. The boy was very sad. He knew he'd never see the beautiful rat again.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs and worms. They had a big bucket to put their finds in. "Look, Lily, I found a shiny stone!" Tom said, holding up a smooth pebble. "Wow, Tom, that's lovely!" Lily said, admiring the stone. "Can I see?" Tom nodded and handed her the stone. Lily turned it over in her hand and saw something sparkly on the other side. "Tom, Tom, there's something on the stone!" Lily said, excited. "It's like a little star!" Tom took the stone back and looked at it closely. He rubbed the dirt off and saw a tiny patch of gold. "Wow, Lily, you're right!" Tom said, amazed. "It's gold! We found gold!" Lily and Tom jumped up and down, hugging the stone. They ran to show their mom, who was sitting on the porch. "Mom, mom, look what we found!" Lily said, holding out the stone. Mom smiled and took the stone. She looked at the patch of gold and then at Lily and Tom. "That's very nice, my dears," she said. "You found a very special stone. Where did you find it?" Lily and Tom pointed to the garden. They told mom how they dug and dug and how Tom found the stone and how Lily revealed the gold. Mom listened and nodded. She was proud of her children. She hugged them and said, "You two are very lucky. You found a lovely treasure. But you know what? You are my treasure. And I love you very much."
