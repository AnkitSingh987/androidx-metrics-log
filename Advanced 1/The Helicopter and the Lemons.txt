Title: The Helicopter and the Lemons

Once upon a time, there was a big helicopter. It was red and blue. It could fly high in the sky. One day, the helicopter saw a big, sour lemon on the ground. The lemon was sad because it was too big to be with the other lemons. The helicopter wanted to help the lemon. It flew down and said, "I can help you. I can split you into two smaller lemons. Then you can play with the other lemons." The lemon was happy and said, "Yes, please help me!" The helicopter used its strong blades to carefully split the big, sour lemon into two smaller lemons. Now, the lemons were not too big. They were happy and could play with the other lemons. The helicopter flew away, happy that it could help the lemons.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to play with his friends near the tall trees. One day, Ben and his friends were playing a game. They had to pass a small ball to each other. Ben was very good at this game. But then, Ben saw a little bird who looked sad and helpless. The bird had a hurt wing and could not fly. Ben wanted to help the bird, so he stopped playing the game. His friends did not know why he stopped and they got mad at him. Ben asked his friends to forgive him for stopping the game. He told them about the helpless bird. His friends forgave him and they all helped the bird together. They gave the bird some food and a warm place to rest. Soon, the bird felt better and could fly again. Ben and his friends were very happy and they all went back to play their game with the ball.

One day, a tall boy named Tom was playing outside. He saw a big, red ambulance with lights and a loud sound. Tom was scared and did not know what it was. Tom ran to his mom and asked, "Mom, what is that big, red thing?" His mom said, "Tom, that is an ambulance. It helps people who are hurt." She explained that the ambulance takes them to the doctor fast, so they can feel better. Later, Tom saw a girl fall off her bike and cry. He wanted to help her. He remembered the ambulance and said, "Don't worry, the ambulance will come and take you to the doctor." The girl stopped crying, and they waited for the ambulance to come.

Once upon a time, there was a fan. This fan was very old, like an ancient toy. It had many colors on it. The fan liked to spin around and make people happy. One day, the fan wanted to change. It wanted to be something new and fun. So, the fan spun around very fast. It spun so fast that it started to change its colors. The colors mixed together and made new colors. Now, the fan was even more fun than before. It had new colors and made people even happier. The fan was proud of its change and loved to spin around for everyone to see. And so, the ancient fan with new colors kept spinning, making people happy every day.

Once, a cat and a dog lived in a small house. They were good friends. One day, they saw a big box with a sign on it. The sign had a picture of a big bone and a fish. The cat and the dog wanted to see what was inside the box. The cat tried to fit into the box, but it was too tight. The dog tried too, but it was also too tight for him. They both wanted to get inside the box and find the bone and the fish. They tried to push and pull, but the box did not open. The cat and the dog thought of a plan. They went to their friend, the mouse. They asked the mouse to help them. The mouse was small and could fit into the box. The mouse went inside the box and got the bone and the fish for the cat and the dog. They were all very happy and shared the treats. The cat, the dog, and the mouse became even better friends.
