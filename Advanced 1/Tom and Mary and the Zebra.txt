Title: Tom and Mary and the Zebra

Once upon a time, two friends called Tom and Mary were walking through a forest. Suddenly, they noticed a zebra speeding across the grass. Tom and Mary had never seen an animal running so fast! “Wow, how beautiful is it!” exclaimed Tom in amazement. “That zebra is so fast!” Mary added. Tom had an idea. “Let’s try to catch it!” But Mary shook her head. “I don’t think that’s a good idea. We should just let the zebra go free.” Tom sighed. He was disappointed, but he understood. He smiled at Mary and said, “You’re right. We shouldn’t try to catch the zebra.” In the end, Tom and Mary decided to show their agreement by signing with their hands. They made a sign that showed they would protect the zebra and let it be free. The moral of this story is that it is important to respect the freedom of animals. We should not try to control or capture them. It is better to understand and appreciate them, and give them their freedom.

Once upon a time, there was a big frog named Fred. Fred lived near a small pond. Every day, he would jump and play with his friends in the pond. They all loved to play in the water. One day, the sun came out, and the water in the pond started to dry up. The pond became smaller and smaller. Fred and his friends felt sad. They began to complain, "Where will we play now?" Then, one of the little frogs had an idea. "Let's go to the spring!" he said. So, Fred and his friends hopped and jumped all the way to the big spring. There, they found lots of water to play in. They were so happy and never complained again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with every day. One day, Tim's toy car was broken. He was very sad and asked his mom to fix it. His mom tried to fix the toy car. She used a wrap to hold the broken pieces together. Tim was happy to see his mom fixing his toy car. He took the car and played with it again. But the car was still not working well. Tim asked his mom to fix it again. His mom tried to fix it one more time, but it was still broken. Tim was very sad that his toy car could not be fixed. He played with it again and again, but it was not the same. The wrap came off and the broken pieces fell apart. Tim's mom said they could not fix the broken car anymore. Tim cried and had to say goodbye to his favorite toy car.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to sell things. She wanted to sell weather to her friends. Sue thought it would be fun to sell sunny days, rainy days, and snowy days. One day, Sue tried to sell the weather. She found it was very tough. She could not put the weather in a box to give to her friends. Sue felt sad. Sue's mom saw her feeling sad. She told Sue that she could not sell the weather, but she could sell other things like toys or lemonade. Sue felt happy again. She decided to sell lemonade and share the weather with her friends for free.

One day, a little boy named Tim had an adventurous idea. He wanted to play in his bathtub like it was a big ocean. He asked his mom, "Can I play in the bathtub?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have fun in the bathtub, but be careful!" Tim filled the bathtub with water and put his toy boats and rubber ducks in it. He pretended to be a brave captain sailing on the big ocean. The water splashed as he moved his boats around. The rubber ducks were his friends, and they talked to each other. "Quack, quack," said one duck, "This is fun!" "Quack, quack," said the other duck, "I like this adventure!" Tim played in the bathtub for a long time. He had so much fun on his adventurous ocean trip. When it was time to get out, he told his mom all about his great adventure. She smiled and said, "I'm glad you had fun, Tim. Now let's get you dry and ready for bed."
