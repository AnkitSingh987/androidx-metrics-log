Title: The Girl Called Tess and the Stars

Once upon a time there was a happy girl called Tess. Tess loved cheese! She ate cheese every day and it made her very healthy. One day, Tess went to her neighbour's house and they gave her something very special. It was some cheese that glowed in the dark! She was so excited that they shared it with her. Tess went home and showed her parents the glowing cheese. "Look!" she said, pointing to the cheese. They were amazed and just had to try it. "Let's get some crackers and make glowing cheese sandwiches", Tess said with a smile. Her mum and dad said yes and the family had a delicious and healthy snack. They ate their sandwiches outside under the stars. They watched the cheese glow in the night sky and smiled. It was a magical moment that Tess would never forget.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hockey. He had a cheap hockey stick, but it was his favorite. One day, Tim saw a big rock near the park. He thought it would be fun to balance on it. Tim tried to balance on the rock with his cheap hockey stick. He was happy when he could do it. He played hockey on the rock every day. Tim knew that one day, he would need to use his balance for something important. One day, Tim's hockey ball went into a small river. Tim knew he could get it if he used his balance. He took his cheap hockey stick and walked on the rocks in the river. Tim was careful not to fall in. He used his balance to get the ball back. Tim was so happy that he could play hockey again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play dress up with her clothes. One day, Lily found a big box of white clothes in her room. She was so happy! Lily put on the white clothes and went to play outside. But her friend Tom came by and saw her. Tom didn't like the white clothes. He wanted to play in the mud. Tom said, "Lily, come play in the mud with me!" Lily didn't want her white clothes to get dirty. Lily and Tom tried to guess what to do. They thought and thought. Then, Lily had an idea. She ran inside and changed her clothes. She came back out with her old clothes. Now, they could play in the mud and have fun. Lily and Tom were happy again.

Once upon a time, in a small, calm house, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys and have fun. One day, while playing in her room, she saw a cobweb in the corner. "Mommy, look! A cobweb!" Lily said. Her mommy came to see what she was talking about. "Oh, we should clean that up," said her mommy. Lily didn't want her mommy to clean the cobweb. She thought it was pretty. "No, Mommy, please don't!" she insisted. "Let's leave it there." Her mommy smiled and said, "Okay, Lily. We can leave it there." So, they let the cobweb stay in the corner of Lily's room. Every day, Lily would say hello to the cobweb and the little spider who lived there. They became good friends, and Lily was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was playing down at the shop. All of a sudden, she heard a strange noise coming from the back of the shop. She went to look and saw a little puppy in a box. It was crying and struggling to get out. Amy was so envious. She wanted to rescue the puppy, but it was too big to pick up. She ran to the shopkeeper and said, "Please! Help me rescue the puppy!" The shopkeeper was worried because the puppy was very naughty and might make a mess. He said, "We can't keep the puppy here, it's too dangerous." Amy shouted "Please! Can't you see, the puppy needs help!" The shopkeeper agreed to help Amy rescue the puppy. Together they worked hard to get the puppy out of the box safely. Amy was so proud she had rescued the puppy. She gave it a big hug and it licked her face!
