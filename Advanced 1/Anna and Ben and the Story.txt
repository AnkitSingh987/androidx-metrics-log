Title: Anna and Ben and the Story

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play together, but sometimes they fight. One day, they are playing with their blocks in the living room. Anna wants to build a tower, but Ben wants to knock it down. "Stop it, Ben!" Anna says. "I want to make a tower. It is fun!" "No, it is not!" Ben says. "It is boring. Knocking it down is fun!" They start to push and pull the blocks. They make a lot of noise. Mom hears them and comes to see what is wrong. "What are you doing, Anna and Ben?" Mom asks. "Why are you fighting?" "Ben is bad!" Anna says. "He breaks my tower!" "Anna is mean!" Ben says. "She won't let me play!" Mom sighs. She is tired. She needs to go to the toilet. She has an idea. "Anna and Ben, I have to go to the toilet. I want you to stay here and play nicely. Can you do that?" Mom asks. "Yes, Mom!" Anna and Ben say. They want to make Mom happy. Mom goes to the toilet. She hopes Anna and Ben will behave. She is gone for a few minutes. Anna and Ben look at each other. They are quiet. They don't want to fight anymore. They want to play together. "Ben, I'm sorry I was mean to you," Anna says. "Do you want to help me build a tower?" "Anna, I'm sorry I was bad to you," Ben says. "Do you want to knock it down together?" They smile. They hug. They start to play with the blocks. They make a big tower. Then they knock it down. They laugh. They have fun. Mom comes back from the toilet. She sees Anna and Ben playing nicely. She is happy. She is proud. "Anna and Ben, you are good children," Mom says. "You stayed here and played nicely. You learned to share and cooperate. You made me happy." Anna and Ben look at Mom. They are happy. They are proud. "Mom, we love you," Anna and Ben say. "We learned to be kind and fair. We made you happy." Mom hugs Anna and Ben. She kisses them. She tells them a story. They listen. They cuddle. They are a happy family. The end. Moral value: The story teaches that fighting is not fun, and that sharing and cooperating are better. It also shows that staying where Mom tells you to stay is safe and good. It also shows that Mom loves them and is happy when they are good.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog living in a pond. Every day, the frog would take a pleasant bath in the water. One day, a teeny tiny jar of yogurt fell from the sky. The frog hopped around the jar, wondering what to do. Suddenly, a small bird flew by and shouted, "I can help! Put some of the yogurt in the pond and you can have a super special yogurt bath!" The frog clapped his hands and thanked the bird for the idea. He carefully spooned the yogurt into the pond and hopped in. Instantly, he felt the cool and creamy yogurt all around him. The frog smiled and enjoyed the special yogurt bath.

Once upon a time, there was a gray crystal in the center of a big meadow. The crystal was as big as a house, and it shone like a star. One day, a young boy and his father came across the crystal. "What is this crystal?" the boy asked. His father smiled and said, "This is a magical crystal. If you look into it, it will help you mind what is important." The boy wanted to see if it was true, so he put his face close to the crystal and his father told him to think of something important, like being kind to others. The boy did as his father said and after a few moments, he heard a beautiful melody coming from the crystal. He looked in the crystal and saw a beautiful rainbow of colors. "Daddy, this is amazing!" the boy said. The boy's father smiled and said, "That rainbow of colors is the crystal showing you what is important. Remember this - it is important to be kind to others, no matter what." The boy thanked his father and went home with a smile. From that day on, he tried to mind what was important and make sure he was kind to others.
