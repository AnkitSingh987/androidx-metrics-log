Title: Tim the Boy and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the dirt with his toy truck. One sunny day, Tim found a big root in the ground. He tried to add it to his truck, but it was too big. A cheap bird saw Tim and said, "What are you doing?" Tim said, "I want to add this big root to my truck, but it's too big!" The bird had an idea. "Let me help you," said the bird. So, the bird started to peck at the root, making it smaller and smaller. Suddenly, the root popped out of the ground! But, it was not a root at all. It was a treasure chest! Tim and the bird were very surprised. They opened the chest and found lots of shiny toys inside. Tim and the bird became best friends and played with the new toys every day.

Once upon a time there was a copper coin in the dirt. It looked so shiny and new! The coin was very glad and wanted someone to play with it. That's when it saw a little girl. She was enjoying a sunny day outside in the garden. She was humming and filling her bucket with flowers. The glad coin raced over to her and said "Hi!" The girl was so surprised! The coin told her all about how it wanted to be friends. The girl smiled and picked it up. The two enjoyed playing games together in the garden. The girl used the coin to dig in the dirt and the coin laughed when she made funny faces. They spent the whole day together and the coin was very happy. In the end, the girl put the coin back in the dirt. She smiled and said goodbye to her new friend. The glad coin smiled too, so happy to have made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a best friend named Tom. They loved to play together all day long. One day, it was very hot outside, so they decided to play under a big tree. Tom had some news to share with Mia. He said, "Mia, I have news! My family is going away for a few days. I will miss you!" Mia felt sad and said, "I will miss you too, Tom." Mia wanted to comfort Tom, so she gave him a big hug. She said, "It's okay, Tom. We can play when you come back. Have fun with your family!" Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Mia. I will see you soon!" Mia and Tom played together until it was time for Tom to leave. They hugged each other one more time and said goodbye. Mia knew she would miss Tom, but she also knew they would play together again soon.

Tommy was sitting in his garden, wondering what he could do on this bright, sunny day. He looked across the country and noticed a foolish bird, squawking loudly and flapping its wings. Tommy picked up his binoculars and looked more closely. The bird was perched on top of a tree, and he wondered why it looked so foolish. He walked up to the tree and said, “Hi silly bird, why are you being so foolish?” The bird looked back at him and replied in its language, “I just wanted to see the whole country! It’s so beautiful!” Tommy smiled and said, “Well, I love it too.” The two were now friends and continued to talk about the beauty of their country. They were so pleased to be able to share their love of the country with one another.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a peaceful subway. The subway would go up and down, taking people to work and back home. Everyone liked the subway because it was so quiet and nice. One day, the subway met a new friend. The friend was a little bird who flew into the subway. The bird was lost and did not know how to go home. The bird asked the subway, "Can you help me find my way home?" The subway said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's work together and find your home." As the subway and the bird worked together, they found out something unexpected. The bird's home was on top of the subway! The bird was so happy to be home, and the subway was happy to have a new friend. From that day on, the bird would ride the subway every day, and they would have fun together.
