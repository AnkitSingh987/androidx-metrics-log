Title: Tim the Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a strong boy with big muscles. He liked to play with his toys and run around the park. One day, he found a big, tight ball on the ground. It was so tight that it was hard to hold. Tim wanted to know what was inside the ball, so he tried to separate it into two parts. He used his muscles to pull and pull, but the ball was too tight. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam was also a strong boy with big muscles. Together, they pulled and pulled, but the ball was still too tight. Finally, Tim and Sam asked their dog, Max, to help them. Max was a big, strong dog with tight muscles too. Max grabbed the ball with his teeth and pulled as hard as he could. With the help of Tim, Sam, and Max, the tight ball finally separated into two parts. Inside the ball, they found a treasure map. Tim, Sam, and Max decided to follow the map and look for the treasure. They had a great adventure and became the best of friends.

Mama was busy in her room. She had just unpacked a box full of things for her special project. Inside the box was a wise mask. Mama's 3 year old daughter, Sofia, came into the room and saw the mask. She was fascinated and asked her mama "What is it?" Mama replied "This is a wise mask. It helps me when I do my special project". Sofia was curious, "Can I try it?" Mama smiled and said "Yes, but first we need to unpack it carefully. Then you can try it". So Mama and Sofia carefully unpacked the mask and Sofia put it on. Suddenly Sofia felt like she was being filled with special knowledge. Mama smiled again, "Now you are very wise". Sofia smiled and said "I love this mask!"

Once upon a time, there was a jolly thief named Tom. Tom liked to write funny notes when he took things. One day, Tom went to a big house to take some toys. He wrote a funny note and left it on the table. In the house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue found the note and read it. She thought it was a game. Sue wanted to play with Tom, so she wrote a note too. She put it on the table and hid with her toys. Tom came back to the house to take more toys. He saw Sue's note and read it. He laughed and thought it was funny. Tom decided to play with Sue instead of taking her toys. They played all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there lived a little mouse. She was always very anxious, because of all the cats around. One day, she found a hole. She decided to go in and explore. She wanted to make it her home, but the hole wasn't big enough. The mouse kept insisting that the hole was big enough. All the other cats around were laughing at her and saying that it wasn't. She became very angry and told them to leave her alone. At last, the mouse found another hole, much bigger than the other one. She rushed inside and proudly declared that this one was perfect for her home. All the other cats around had to agree, and she found her home at last. She was no longer anxious about cats bothering her.

Once upon a time, there was a tall ladder in a big tree. A small bird lived in the tree. One day, the small bird heard a loud noise. The bird looked down and saw a big cat. The cat wanted to climb the ladder to catch the bird. The bird said, "Please, cat, don't climb the ladder. I have a secret to tell you." The cat was ignorant and liked secrets, so it stopped. The bird said, "If you don't climb the ladder, I will give you a big surprise." The cat thought about it and said, "Okay, I will wait." The cat waited and waited. Then, the bird's friends came. They all had food for the cat. The cat was happy and forgot about the bird. The cat learned to be nice to the bird and its friends. They all lived happily together. The moral of the story is that being kind to others can bring good things to you.
