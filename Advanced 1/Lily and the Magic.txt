Title: Lily and the Magic

Lily liked to play in the garden when it was sunny. She had a new doll that she called Rose. Rose had a pink dress and a fairy hat. Lily pretended that Rose was a fairy who could do magic. One day, it started to rain. Lily ran inside with Rose. She was sad because she could not play in the garden anymore. She looked out of the window and saw the raindrops falling on the flowers and the grass. "Rose, do you like the rain?" Lily asked her doll. "I do not like the rain," Rose said in a small voice. "The rain makes me wet and cold. I want to see the sun and the rainbow." Lily hugged Rose and said, "Do not be sad, Rose. The rain will stop soon. Then we can go back to the garden and have fun. Maybe we will see a real fairy there. Would you like that?" Rose nodded and smiled. "I would like that very much, Lily. You are my best friend. Thank you for taking care of me." Lily and Rose waited for the rain to stop. They played with some books and toys in Lily's room. They sang some songs and told some stories. They had a good time together. After a while, the rain stopped. Lily and Rose looked out of the window again. They saw a bright sun and a colorful rainbow in the sky. They also saw something shiny and sparkly in the garden. It was a fairy! Lily and Rose ran outside. They said hello to the fairy. The fairy said hello back. She had a blue dress and a fairy wand. She said she came to visit Lily and Rose because they were so kind and happy. The fairy played with Lily and Rose in the garden. She showed them some magic tricks. She made some flowers grow and some butterflies fly. She gave them some fairy dust and some fairy cake. She said they could make a wish. Lily and Rose made a wish. They wished to be friends forever. The fairy said their wish would come true. She said she had to go back to her home in the clouds. She said goodbye to Lily and Rose. She said she would come back again someday. Lily and Rose said goodbye to the fairy. They thanked her for the fun and the gifts. They waved to her as she flew away. They looked at each other and smiled. They were very happy. They had a new friend. A fairy friend.

One day, a little boat went to sail on the water. The boat liked to sail when it was quiet. It liked to feel the wind and the waves. The boat was very happy when it sailed. In the quiet water, the boat met a friend. The friend was a spicy pepper. The pepper liked to swim and play with the boat. They had a lot of fun together. The boat and the pepper were very good friends. One day, when the boat and the pepper were playing, something unexpected happened. The spicy pepper turned into a big, soft pillow! The boat was surprised. Now, the boat and the pillow could sail and sleep in the quiet water together. They were still very good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a scary monster. The monster lived in the woods. The people in the town were scared of the monster. They did not go to the woods. They had a question: "Why is the monster so scary?" One day, a brave boy named Tim went to the woods. He wanted to find the monster. He walked for a week, looking for the monster. He was not scared. He wanted to ask the monster the big question. At last, Tim found the monster. But, the monster was not scary. The monster was sad. The monster told Tim, "I am not scary, I am just lonely." Tim was surprised. He did not expect the monster to be sad. Tim and the monster became friends. They went back to the town together. The people were not scared anymore. The monster was happy, and the town was happy too.
