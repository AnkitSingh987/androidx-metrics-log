Title: Josh and Molly and the Wisdom

Once upon a time there were two friends: Josh and Molly. They were very special because they had lots of wisdom. One day they played together and decided to polish their wisdom. Josh had a big idea to polish his wisdom, but Molly thought it was a bit crazy. She said, "No Josh! We shouldn't do that. It's too risky!" Josh was determined to go ahead with his plan. "Come on Molly," he said. "It'll be fun! Trust me!" Molly still wasn't sure, but she eventually agreed. So they both decided to polish their wisdom. But something strange started to happen! Josh's wisdom suddenly became very crazy; it completely changed! Molly was scared and tried to get Josh to stop. But it was too late! Josh's crazy plan had worked, but it had taken him too far. He soon realised he needed Molly's wisdom to help him find his way back. And with Molly's help, the two friends polished their wisdom into something even better than before!

Once upon a time, there was a hero. This hero was very honest. He always told the truth. He lived in a small town with many people. The people loved the hero because he was kind and helpful. One day, the sun did not shine. It was dark and everyone was sad. They needed the sun to shine so they could play and be happy. The honest hero wanted to help the people in the town. The hero went on a journey to find the sun. He walked and walked. He met a wise old man who told him to climb a big hill. The hero climbed the hill and found the sun hiding behind a cloud. The hero asked the sun to please shine again. The sun listened to the honest hero and began to shine. The people in the town were happy and thanked the hero. And from that day on, the sun always shined, and the honest hero was loved by all.

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tom. Tom was going to marry a nice girl named Sue. Tom wanted to look his best, so he got a new suit. On the big day, Tom was very careless. He spilled juice on his suit. Tom was sad and didn't know what to do. Sue was waiting for him and everyone was looking. Tom tried to clean his suit, but it didn't work. He had to wear the dirty suit to marry Sue. When Sue saw Tom, she was very sad too. She didn't want to marry Tom with his dirty suit. So, Sue didn't marry Tom and they didn't live happily ever after.

One day, a red seal named Sam lived by the sea. Sam loved to play with his friends, but sometimes he would have too much fun and forget to share. His friends would get sad when Sam did not share. One sunny day, Sam found a big red ball. He was very happy and wanted to play with it all day. His friends saw the ball and wanted to play too. But Sam did not want to share the ball. His friends were sad and walked away. Sam felt bad and thought about what he did. He knew that sharing was good and would make his friends happy. So, Sam went to find his friends and shared the red ball with them. They all played together and had a great time. Sam learned that sharing is important and it makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It was a happy day. A little girl named Lily went to the park. She loved to play with her friends. Lily saw her friend Tom. He looked sad. Lily wanted to remind him to be happy. She gave Tom a big hug. They played together all day. But then, the sky turned dark. The bright sun was gone. Lily and Tom were sad. They wanted to go home. They did not get to play anymore. The day had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big lemon tree in her yard. She loved to pick lemons and make lemonade. One day, Lily saw a troubled bird sitting on a branch of the lemon tree. The bird looked very sad. Lily asked the bird, "Why are you so troubled, bird?" The bird said, "I can't find my way home. I guess I am lost." Lily wanted to help the bird, so she thought of a plan. Lily said, "I will give you a lemon from my tree. You can give it to your family when you find them. They will be happy to see you and the lemon." The bird liked the idea and took the lemon from Lily. The bird flew high in the sky and looked around. Soon, it saw its family in a tree far away. The bird gave the lemon to its family, and they were all very happy. The bird thanked Lily for her help. The moral of the story is that helping others can make them happy and make you feel good too.
