Title: Sue and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a basket. She was very polite. One day, she went to the park with her basket. At the park, Sue saw a cat. The cat was hungry. Sue wanted to give the cat some food from her basket. But the cat did not want it. The cat refused the food. Sue felt sad. Then, a big dog came to the park. The dog was nice. Sue gave the dog some food from her basket. The dog was happy. The cat saw this and felt sad. The cat came back to Sue and was polite. The cat said sorry and ate the food. Sue, the cat, and the dog all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a generous girl named Lily. She loved to share her toys with her friends. One day, Lily went to the park to play. At the park, she saw a big bow on a tree. The bow was red and pretty. Lily wanted to show the bow to her friends. She called her friend Tom to come see the bow. "Tom, come see the bow on the tree!" said Lily. Tom ran to Lily and saw the bow. "Wow, it's so pretty!" said Tom. They both smiled and played near the tree with the big red bow.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and run all day. One day, he saw a door. He wanted to see what was behind the door. Spot tried to resist, but he was too curious. Spot pushed the door open with his strong legs. Behind the door, he found a big room with many toys. Spot was so happy to play with all the toys. He played and played for a long time. But then, something unexpected happened. The door closed on its own! Spot tried to push the door open again, but it was too heavy. He was stuck in the room with all the toys. Spot was sad and missed his home.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly dog named Spot. Spot loved to wander around the big yard near his home. One sunny day, Spot went out to play and have fun. While wandering, Spot found a long rope on the ground. He picked up the rope with his mouth and started to play with it. Spot was so happy and wagged his tail. Spot played with the rope all day long. He even showed it to his friend, a small cat named Whiskers. Whiskers liked the rope too, and they played together. At the end of the day, Spot and Whiskers were tired but happy. They went home to eat and sleep, ready for another fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a grand house by the sea. Inside this house lived a father and a son, who loved each other very much. One day, the father said to his son, "Let's go for a walk by the sea!" The son happily agreed and the two of them followed the beach until they found a strange shape made out of sand. The shape was very lively, even though the wind was blowing. The father smiled and said to his son, "The shape was made with love, it is healing for us to look at." The son thought about this for a moment and said, "What does it mean to heal?" The father thought for a moment then said, "To heal can mean many things. It could mean to have a lovely time, to really feel your happiness, or it could mean to recover from an illness. It can also mean to understand somebody else." The son thought about this for a moment and said, "Will this shape heal us?" The father said, "Yes, if we look at it with happy eyes, it will help us heal our hearts." This story teaches us that to heal can mean different things and that even a simple things like looking at a lively sand shape can help our hearts.

Once upon a time there was a little rabbit who wanted to be brave. He wanted to get up on the stage and make a speech but he was very fearful. The little rabbit felt his heart pounding in fear and he wanted to run away. He tried to calm himself down and he thought about what his mom always said, "You can do anything if you just try". The little rabbit hopped up on the stage and everyone clapped. He nervously chewed on his little paws as he began to speak but he soon forgot his fear and spoke confidently. Everyone cheered and the little rabbit was so proud of himself! He hopped off the stage, feeling brave and brave. He was so proud of himself for overcoming his fears. The little rabbit was now the bravest rabbit in the world!
