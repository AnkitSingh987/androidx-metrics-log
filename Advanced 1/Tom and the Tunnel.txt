Title: Tom and the Tunnel

One day, a small, fit cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tunnel and thought it would be fun to go inside. Tom was very brave and not scared at all. As Tom walked into the tunnel, he met a friendly dog named Max. Max asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Tom nodded his head and said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" Tom and Max played in the tunnel, running and jumping. They had a lot of fun together. After a while, they were tired and decided to go home. They said goodbye and went back to their homes. Tom and Max were happy they had a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a big bottle and a small bottle. They lived in a little house with a girl named Lucy. The big bottle was always full of water, and the small bottle was always empty. The small bottle was very jealous of the big bottle because it could fit so much water. One day, Lucy came to the bottles and said, "I need a bottle to take with me outside." She looked at the big bottle and the small bottle. The big bottle smiled and said, "Pick me! I can fit a lot of water." The small bottle felt sad but said nothing. Lucy thought for a moment and then picked up the small bottle. She said, "I will take you, small bottle, because you are easy to carry and you fit in my bag." The small bottle felt happy and proud. From that day on, the small bottle was never jealous of the big bottle again, because it knew that it was special too.

Once upon a time, in a big friendly forest, there lived a little bunny named Patch. Patch loved to hop around and play with his friends. One day, Patch saw a big tree with a hole in it. He thought it would be fun to see what was inside. "Hello?" Patch called into the hole. "Is anyone in there?" A small voice answered, "Yes, I am here. My name is Tiny. Who are you?" Patch replied, "I am Patch, the bunny. Do you want to come out and play with me?" Tiny said, "I would love to, but I am stuck. Can you help me?" Patch tried to help Tiny out of the hole. He pulled and pulled, but then something unexpected happened. Tiny came out of the hole, and Patch saw that Tiny was not a bunny, but a friendly little bird! Patch was surprised, but happy to have a new friend. They played together all day, and from that day on, Patch and Tiny were the best of friends.

Anna likes to do yoga with her mom. Yoga is when you make shapes with your body and breathe slowly. Anna thinks yoga is fun and good for her. One day, Anna and her mom are doing yoga in the living room. They are making a shape like a bridge. Anna lifts her hips and puts her feet and hands on the floor. She feels strong and happy. But then, something strange happens. A big, furry, brown thing appears in the window. It has a long nose, big ears, and sharp teeth. It looks at Anna and her mom and makes a loud noise. "Roar!" Anna and her mom are very scared. They stop doing yoga and run to the couch. They hide behind the pillows and hug each other. What is that thing? Where did it come from? They are very confused. The thing in the window is a bear. It was looking for food in the trash cans outside. It smelled something yummy in the house and wanted to see. It saw Anna and her mom doing yoga and was curious. It did not want to hurt them. It just wanted to say hello. But the bear did not know that people are afraid of bears. It did not know that people do not like bears in their windows. It did not know that people do not do yoga with bears. It was also confused. The bear decided to leave. It walked away from the window and went back to the woods. It hoped to find some berries or honey instead. Anna and her mom peeked from behind the pillows. They saw that the bear was gone. They were relieved and happy. They hugged each other and said, "That was a big surprise!" They decided to finish their yoga later. They also decided to lock the windows and doors. They did not want any more bears to appear. They wanted to be safe and calm.
