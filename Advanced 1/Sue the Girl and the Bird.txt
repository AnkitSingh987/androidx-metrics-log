Title: Sue the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a kind girl named Sue. She had a big cooler. Sue loved to rock in her chair by the cooler. She would rock and sing all day. One day, Sue saw a hurt bird by her cooler. The bird was very sad. Sue wanted to help the bird. She took the bird inside her house and put it by the cooler. The cooler made the bird feel better. Sue was happy to help the bird. As Sue rocked in her chair, the bird started to sing. The bird sang a beautiful song. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird turned into a fairy! The fairy thanked Sue for helping her. She gave Sue a magic gift. Sue learned that being kind and helpful brings happiness and magic into our lives.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball in the park. One day, he saw a big cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was not very nice, but Spot wanted to be friends. Spot went up to Fluffy and said, "Do you want to play with me?" Fluffy looked at Spot and nodded. They started to play with the ball, but Fluffy played too rough. Spot felt pain in his leg. Spot tried to tell Fluffy to stop, but Fluffy didn't listen. Instead, Fluffy took the ball and ran away. Spot was sad and hurt. He limped back home, wishing he had never tried to play with Fluffy.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. Today, they have a picnic with their moms. They sit on a big blanket and eat sandwiches and fruit. But Lily and Ben are not happy. They think the picnic is boring. They want to run and jump and slide and swing. They do not want to sit and eat. "Mom, can we go play?" Lily asks. "Not yet, Lily. You have to finish your food first," her mom says. "But we are not hungry. We are bored," Ben says. "Me too," Lily says. Their moms look at each other and smile. They know how Lily and Ben feel. They remember when they were little and wanted to play all the time. "Okay, how about this?" Lily's mom says. "You can go play for a little while, but you have to come back when we call you. And you have to be careful and stay close. Can you do that?" Lily and Ben nod and smile. They say thank you and run to the playground. They are happy. They do not think the picnic is boring anymore. They think it is fun.

Once there was a little boy who wanted to go on an adventure. He put on his warm coat and headed outside. He saw a large tree and ran towards it. He marked the tree with his foot and then waited. He waited and waited, but nothing happened. After a while, he noticed the air was cold, but he stayed and waited some more. After a while, he started to shiver from the cold and he eventually gave up. He reluctantly turned around and started walking home. He kept looking behind him, hoping the tree would do something magical, but it never did. The little boy continued walking and eventually he made it home. He was sad that his big adventure didn't happen, but he realized he was safe and warm, even though it was cold outside.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a black cat. This cat was not pretty like other cats. Many people thought the cat was ugly. But the cat had a big heart and was very kind. One day, the cat found a little bird who could not fly. The bird had fallen from its nest and was alone. The cat wanted to help the bird, so it did not leave the bird alone. The cat stayed with the bird and played with it. As the days went by, the bird grew strong and learned to fly. The bird and the cat became best friends. The people in the town saw how loving and caring the ugly black cat was. They learned that being kind is more important than being pretty. The cat and the bird stayed friends forever.
