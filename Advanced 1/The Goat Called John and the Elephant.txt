Title: The Goat Called John and the Elephant

Once upon a time, there was a goat called John. He lived on a farm with a lot of other animals. One day, John felt very excited. He decided that it was time to celebrate something. John asked his best friend, the wise elephant, "What should I celebrate?". The elephant thought carefully and then said, "Why don't you celebrate a foolish thing. Make up something silly and let's do it!" John liked the idea and decided to celebrate the silliest thing he could think of - he decided to celebrate sitting on a small chair! He laughed and hopped on to the chair. The other animals saw him and started to laugh too. They all thought it was a very foolish idea to celebrate sitting on a chair. But John was having fun and he wanted them to join in. He waved at them and shouted, "Come on! Let's celebrate sitting on a chair!" His friends laughed and came to join him. They all laughed, had fun and celebrated together.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there were many animals. The animals liked to play and have fun. One day, a strong horse named Sam came to the farm. Sam was very big and the other animals liked him. One day, the animals heard a loud noise. They were scared. The noise came from the big tree near the farm. Sam, the strong horse, wanted to help his friends. He walked to the tree to see what made the noise. When Sam got to the tree, he saw a little bird. The bird was not scary at all! The bird was just learning to sing. The animals laughed and were not scared anymore. They all became friends with the little bird and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big red heart. He liked to throw his heart up in the air and catch it. One day, he felt troubled because he could not find his heart. He was very sad and did not know what to do. Tim looked everywhere for his heart. He looked under his bed, in the toy box, and even in the garden. But he could not find it. Tim was about to give up when he saw a little girl holding his heart. She had found it under a tree. Tim was so happy to see his heart again. But there was a twist! The little girl had a big blue heart. She liked to throw her heart too, just like Tim. They decided to play together and throw their hearts in the air. They had so much fun that they became best friends. From that day on, Tim and the little girl played together every day, and they were never troubled again.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to study. He put on his gray hat and went to the park. In the park, he saw a big tree. Tim wanted to sit under the tree and study. As Tim sat under the tree, a little girl named Sue came. Sue said, "I want to sit here too!" Tim did not want to share the spot. They both wanted to sit under the tree alone. Tim and Sue looked at each other. They thought about it. Then, they decided to sit together. They both sat under the tree and studied. They became good friends and liked to study together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mark. He loved to bake with his mom. They would make yummy cakes and cookies. One day, Mark and his mom were baking a big cake for a party. It was a fun and noisy day in the kitchen. Mark wanted to help his mom mix the cake batter. He used a big spoon to mix, but it was hard. The spoon slipped from his hand and made a loud noise. Mark's mom said, "It's okay, we can try again." Mark was happy his mom was not mad. They baked the cake and took it to the party. Everyone said the cake was very good. Mark was proud that he helped his mom. The moral of the story is: it's okay to make mistakes, as long as you learn from them and keep trying.
