Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Acorn

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam was an adventurous squirrel who loved to play outside. One sunny day, Sam went to the big tree to find some acorns to eat. As Sam was looking, he saw a big, shiny acorn on the ground. "Wow!" said Sam, "This is the biggest acorn I have ever seen!" He picked it up and started to carry it home. On his way, Sam met his friend, a small bird named Bella. "Hi, Sam!" said Bella. "What do you have there?" Sam showed her the big acorn. "I found this big acorn, and I am taking it home to eat," he said. Bella looked at the acorn and said, "That is a very big acorn! I hope you enjoy it!" Sam and Bella played together for a little while, and then Sam took his big acorn home. He was very happy to have found such a special acorn on his adventurous day. And from that day on, Sam and Bella played together every day, looking for more big acorns to share.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly skeleton named Skelly. Skelly liked to wander in the woods near his home. One day, he met a little girl named Lucy. "Hi, I am Skelly. I am friendly. Do you want to play?" asked Skelly. Lucy looked at him and smiled. "Yes, let's play," she said. So, Skelly and Lucy played together all day. They wandered in the woods, picked pretty flowers, and laughed a lot. They became best friends, and from that day on, they always played together.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly tiger. Every day, the tiger roars happy songs. One day, he saw a big, shiny puzzle. He wanted to try it. The tiger asked some birds where he could find it. They told him it was at the big hill. He was excited - he wanted to find it! So the tiger ran and ran until he reached the big hill. But when he got there, it wasn't what he expected. He saw a big bear by the puzzle. The bear was very angry. He roared loud and not so friendly. The tiger was scared, but remembered all the friendly things he did. He used his best roar and scared away the bear. The puzzle was now his! The tiger was so happy - he solved the puzzle and roars his happy songs again!

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. Lily had a big, shiny jewel. She loved her jewel very much. One day, she lost her jewel while playing in the park. Lily was very sad. Lily looked for her jewel everywhere. She asked her friend Tom, "Did you see my jewel?" Tom said, "No, but I will help you find it." They looked and looked, but they could not find the jewel. At last, they found a big, strong man. The man said, "I found a jewel. Is it yours?" Lily was happy to see her jewel. She said, "Yes, it is mine! Thank you!" The man gave the jewel back to Lily, and she was happy. She said to Tom, "We finished our search. Let's go home." They went home, and Lily was kind to everyone she met.

Anna was sad. It was her birthday, but it was a gloomy day. The sky was gray and it rained a lot. She could not go outside and play with her friends. She only had her old toys and books to play with. She heard a knock on the door. She ran to see who it was. It was her grandma. She had a big smile and a big bag. She hugged Anna and said, "Happy birthday, my dear! I have a surprise for you!" Anna was curious. She opened the bag and saw a lot of things. She saw a new dress, a new doll, a new book, and a new puzzle. She was very happy. She thanked her grandma and gave her a kiss. But there was one more thing in the bag. It was a small box. Anna opened it and saw a red pepper. She was confused. She did not like peppers. They were spicy and made her mouth hurt. She asked her grandma, "Why did you give me a pepper?" Her grandma laughed and said, "It is not a real pepper, my dear. It is a toy pepper. You can squeeze it and it will make a funny sound. Like this." She squeezed the pepper and it made a loud honk. Anna laughed too. She thought it was funny. She squeezed the pepper and it honked again. She played with the pepper and her other gifts. She forgot about the rain and the gloom. She was happy. She received a lot of love and fun from her grandma. She said, "This is the best birthday ever!"
