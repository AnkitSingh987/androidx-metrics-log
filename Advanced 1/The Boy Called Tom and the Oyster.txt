Title: The Boy Called Tom and the Oyster

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tom. Tom was really excited one morning because he was going to visit the beach. As Tom built sandcastles on the beach, he noticed a huge pile of rocks close by. He began to explore the pile and soon discovered something unexpected. It was an enormous oyster! It was so big that Tom almost couldn't carry it. Tom quickly ran back to tell everyone what he had found. He wanted to show them the enormous oyster. But when Tom got back to the beach, the oyster was gone. Someone had taken it! Tom was so sad and began to cry. He had lost the huge oyster. Suddenly, he saw a big, sparkly shell in the sand. It was much prettier than the enormous oyster he had found before and Tom was so happy. He smiled and soon forgot all about the oyster he had lost.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dark crane. The crane lived by a river. It liked to catch fish for food. One day, it saw a small, pretty bird. The bird was playing near the water. The dark crane wanted to be friends with the pretty bird. It went close to the bird and said, "Hello, bird! Let's be friends and play together." The pretty bird was scared of the big, dark crane. It said, "No, thank you. You are too big and dark. I want to play with my small friends." This made the dark crane very sad and angry. It wanted to show the pretty bird that it could be a good friend. The dark crane had an idea. It tried to strike the water to catch a fish for the pretty bird. But, it struck too hard and fast. The water splashed all over the pretty bird. The pretty bird was very upset. It flew away and never came back. The dark crane was sad and all alone. The moral of the story is to be careful with your actions and not to scare others away when trying to make friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red truck that would deliver things to people's homes. One sunny day, the truck had to deliver a big box to a house with a blue roof. The truck driver, named Tom, was excited to do his job. As Tom drove the truck, he saw a small spoiled cat sitting on the blue roof. The cat was meowing loudly and looked sad. Tom thought the cat might need help, so he stopped the truck and got out. Tom climbed up to the roof to save the cat. But, as he got closer, he saw that the cat was not sad at all. The cat was playing with a toy mouse! The cat had tricked Tom! Tom laughed and climbed back down. He delivered the big box to the house and drove away, happy that he had met the funny cat on the blue roof.

Jimmy looked out of the window and saw something strange in the distance. He grabbed his sweater and opened the door. He stepped into the garden. He saw a big bird slowly flying over the trees. He watched it as it flew nearer. Jimmy felt excited as the bird landed in the garden. He didn't want to scare it, so he stayed very still and watched. The bird looked around and then flew away again. Jimmy watched until it was far away and couldn't be seen anymore. Jimmy was sad to see the bird go. He wanted to get a closer look. He put his sweater back on and went inside. He knew he would never see the bird again, but he remembered how it looked in the distant sky.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom lived in a small house near a big hedge. The hedge was very tall and green. Tom liked to look at the hedge every day. One day, Tom saw a little bird on the hedge. The little bird was sad. It did not have any food. Tom wanted to help the little bird. He went to the store and got some bird food. Tom put the bird food near the hedge. The little bird was very happy. It ate the food and sang a pretty song. The next day, the little bird received a gift from another bird. It was a shiny stone. The little bird shared the shiny stone with Tom. Tom and the little bird became good friends. They lived happily near the big hedge.
