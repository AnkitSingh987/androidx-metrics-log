Title: Spot and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot had a big spirit and always tried to help his friends. He loved to play and make everyone happy. One day, Spot saw a ball stuck high up in a tree. His friend, a small bird, was sad because she could not reach it. Spot wanted to help, so he tried to jump and get the ball. He jumped and jumped, but he could not get the ball down. Spot did not give up. He kept trying to succeed. He found a long stick and used it to poke the ball. Finally, the ball fell down, and the bird was happy again. Spot's spirit and hard work made his friend happy, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat cream. One day, he found a big jar of cream in the kitchen. He was very happy and started to eat it. But he did not know that the cream was old and had started to rot. As Tim ate the cream, he began to feel angry. His tummy hurt and he did not know why. He thought maybe the cream was making him feel this way. So, he stopped eating the cream and told his mom about it. His mom looked at the jar and saw that the cream was bad. Tim's mom threw away the bad cream and gave Tim some good cream instead. Tim's tummy felt better and he was not angry anymore. From that day on, Tim always asked his mom to check the cream before he ate it. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and eat yummy food. One day, Tim found some weird food in the kitchen. It was green and slimy. Tim's mom told him to cover the weird food and not to eat it. But Tim didn't listen to his mom. Tim wanted to try the weird food, so he took a big bite. It tasted very bad, and Tim felt sick. He wished he had listened to his mom and covered the food like she said. His tummy hurt, and he started to cry. The moral of the story is to always listen to your mom and dad. They know what is best for you. If Tim had listened to his mom, he would not have felt sick and sad.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friend, a little cat named Luna. One day, Max and Luna found a shiny thing on the ground. It was a license. Max said, "I want the license!" Luna said, "No, I want the license!" They began to quarrel. They pulled and pulled on the license, but neither would let go. As they quarreled, a big bird saw them. The bird swooped down and grabbed the license from Max and Luna. The bird flew away, and Max and Luna were sad. They had lost the license because they could not share.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful city. The city was full of people, but they were all very selfish. They only thought about themselves and didn't care about anyone else. One day, it was springtime. The weather was warm and sunny and all the flowers were blooming. Suddenly, a special thing happened. A tiny seed bumped into the ground and started to grow. It got bigger and bigger and eventually became a beautiful flower. Everyone in the city smiled when they saw the flower. Suddenly the people stopped being selfish and started to think of others. They shared their food, their toys and their love. Everyone was happy and they decided to make it a special spring tradition. They held hands and danced in the city square every year when the first flower blossomed.

Once upon a time, in a cold land, there was a little igloo. In the igloo lived a small boy named Tim and his big dog, Sam. Tim and Sam liked to play in the snow all day. The snow was so smooth and fun to slide on. One day, Tim saw a big sled near his igloo. He thought it would be fun to borrow the sled and ride it with Sam. "Sam, let's borrow this sled and go down the hill!" Tim said. Sam barked happily, and they both got on the sled. As they went down the hill, the sled went faster and faster. Tim and Sam were scared, but they could not stop the sled. At the bottom of the hill, the sled hit a big rock. Tim and Sam fell off the sled and got hurt. They should not have borrowed the sled without asking.
