Title: Tim the Boy and the Magic

One day, a little boy named Tim found a sock under his bed. It was a funny sock with many colors. Tim felt dizzy when he looked at it. He didn't know what to do with the sock, so he just shrugged. Tim put the sock on his hand like a puppet. He played with the sock puppet all day. They had a lot of fun together. But then, something unexpected happened. The sock puppet started to talk! The sock puppet said, "Hello, Tim! I am a magic sock!" Tim was very surprised. He didn't know that his sock was magic. The magic sock helped Tim clean his room and find his lost toys. Tim and the magic sock became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big pie. It was in the kitchen on a table. The pie was very yummy. It was made by a mom for her kids. The pie was waiting for the kids to eat it. In the room next to the kitchen, there was a little girl. She was playing with her toys. She did not know about the pie. The little girl was having fun with her toys. She did not want to disturb her mom. Then, a boy came into the room. He was the little girl's brother. He told the girl about the pie. The girl was very happy. She wanted to try the pie. The brother and sister went to the kitchen. They saw the pie on the table. It was a new pie. They had never seen this pie before. It was an unknown pie. The brother and sister looked at the pie. They wanted to eat it. But they did not want to disturb the pie. They asked their mom if they could eat the pie. The mom said yes. The brother and sister ate the pie. It was very yummy. They were very happy. The end.

Lily liked to read. She read books, signs, labels, and anything with words. One day, she saw a big envelope on the table. It had her name on it. She was curious. She opened the envelope and found a letter inside. It was from her grandma. She read the letter out loud. "Dear Lily, I miss you very much. I hope you are having fun at home. I sent you a surprise. It is in the envelope. Can you find it? Love, Grandma." Lily looked inside the envelope again. She saw something shiny and round. It was a necklace. It had a heart pendant with her name on it. She was happy. She ran to her mom and showed her the letter and the necklace. "Look, mom, look! Grandma sent me a letter and a necklace. She loves me!" Mom smiled and hugged her. "That's very nice, Lily. Grandma is very thoughtful. Do you want to write her a letter back?" Lily nodded. "Yes, mom, yes! I want to say thank you and I love her too. Can you help me?" Mom nodded. "Of course, Lily. I can help you. You are very helpful too. You can read and write very well. I'm proud of you." They went to the desk and got some paper and a pen. They wrote a letter to grandma together. Lily drew a picture of her and grandma holding hands. She put the letter and the picture in a new envelope. She wrote grandma's name and address on it. "Can we mail it now, mom?" Lily asked. "Sure, Lily. Let's go to the mailbox. Maybe we'll see the mailman. He is very helpful too. He delivers letters and packages to people." They put on their coats and shoes and went outside. They walked to the mailbox and put the envelope inside. They waved to the mailman who was driving by. He waved back and smiled. Lily felt happy. She had read a letter, written a letter, and worn a necklace. She had a loving grandma, a helpful mom, and a friendly mailman. She had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful carpet in the middle of a room. A little boy named Jack was sitting on the carpet. He looked around, and then clapped his hands together. “Who wants to play?” he shouted. His mom came into the room. She saw the beautiful carpet and smiled. “So, what are you doing?” she asked. “I’m clapping,” Jack said. “Do you want to join me?” Mom laughed, and clapped her hands too. Jack laughed and clapped even louder. They both clapped and laughed and sang. Then, all of a sudden, Mom stopped. “Why did you stop?” asked Jack. “We have to applaud the carpet,” said Mom. “What’s that?” “It’s when we show our thanks to someone or something because something beautiful happened,” said Mom. So together, Jack and Mom clapped their hands and said loud and proud, “Thank you, beautiful carpet!”Then they hugged each other and smiled.
