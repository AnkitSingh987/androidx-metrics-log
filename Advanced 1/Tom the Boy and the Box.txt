Title: Tom the Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toys. He had a big toy box. His toy box was on the left side of his room. The top of the box was clear, so he could see all his toys. One day, Tom went to play with his toys. He saw a new toy in his toy box. It was a big red ball. Tom was so happy. He did not know where it came from, but he wanted to play with it. Tom took the big red ball outside to play. He threw it up in the air and it went very high. But when the ball came down, it hit a big glass window. The window broke and made a loud noise. Tom was very sad and scared. He knew he did something bad.

Once upon a time, there was a big piano in a small room. A curious little girl named Lily lived in the house with the piano. She loved to play and listen to the music it made. But one day, the piano would not make any sounds. Lily was sad and did not know what to do. She tried to turn the piano on and off, but it still would not make any sounds. She was very upset and wanted to fix the piano so she could play her favorite songs again. Lily asked her mom for help. Her mom was very smart and knew how to fix things. They worked together, and soon the piano started to make music again. Lily was so happy, and she played her favorite songs for her mom. They danced and laughed together, and the piano was fixed.

Once upon a time there was a very sad little girl. Everywhere she went, she complained. She said things like "that isn't fair" and "I don't like that". One day, she saw a man on the side of the street. He was very old and he was deaf. He had no friends and he felt lonely. The little girl went over to him and said hello. He didn't answer, so the little girl tapped on his shoulder. The man turned around and saw the little girl. He smiled at her and she smiled back. The little girl stopped complaining and played games with the old man. They laughed and smiled for hours. The man and the little girl were now good friends. They were never apart and were always happy together. The little girl no longer complained, she just felt happy and content.

Once upon a time, two friends went for a walk in a very big, wide park. They looked around and saw the nation was very sad and miserable. The first friend said to the other, "People in this nation are so unhappy, we must do something." The other friend smiled and said, "Let's promise to make this nation happy." So the friends smiled and shook hands, and made a promise to make the nation happy. The friends then went on their way, finding ways to make the nation happy. Every day they worked together, and soon enough the nation was happy and cheerful once again. The friends held each other's hands and smiled, knowing that they had kept their promise.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play together and share their toys. But they do not like the same cereal. Lily likes the cereal with stars and moons. Ben likes the cereal with flakes and nuts. Every morning, they fight over the cereal box. One day, their mom has an idea. She says, "Why don't you try to unite your cereals? Maybe you will like the new taste. You are both intelligent, you can find a way to mix them." Lily and Ben look at each other. They think mom's idea is silly. But they are curious. They decide to try it. They pour some of each cereal in a big bowl. They stir it with a spoon. They add some milk. They take a bite. "Mmm, this is good!" Lily says. "I like it too!" Ben says. They smile at each other. They realize they can like different things and still get along. They thank mom for her idea. They finish their bowl and ask for more. They unite their cereals every morning and enjoy their breakfast. They are happy twins.
