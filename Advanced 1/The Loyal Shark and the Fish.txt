Title: The Loyal Shark and the Fish

Once there was a loyal shark. Her name was Liz. She was a very good shark. Every day she swam in the sea, looking for food. She had lots of friends, too. She loved and was loved very much. One day Liz went on a big adventure. She saw different fish, wonderful coral and lots of beautiful things. But she missed her friends. She missed their kindness and their love. Liz was very loyal, so she decided to go home. When Liz arrived back home all her friends came to meet her and hugged her. They were so happy to see her and they all loved her so much. Liz was touched, and she realized that there is no greater thing than love shared with wonderful friends.

One day, a little dog named Max found a big stick in the park. He was very happy. Max wanted to play with the stick, but he did not know what to do. So, Max made a plan. Max's plan was to find a friend to play with the stick. He saw a big cat named Lucy. Max went to Lucy and showed her the stick. Lucy was angry because she did not like sticks. She did not want to play with Max. Max was sad, but he did not give up on his plan. He looked around and saw a little bird named Sam. Max went to Sam and showed him the stick. Sam was happy and wanted to play with Max. They played with the stick all day and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the surf at the beach. One sunny day, he went to the beach with his family to have fun and splash in the water. While Spot was playing in the surf, he saw a big fish. The big fish looked angry. Spot thought the big fish might punish him for playing in the water. So, Spot ran away from the big fish as fast as he could. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish jumped out of the surf and started to dance! It was not an angry fish after all. It was a happy fish that just wanted to play with Spot. They danced and played together in the surf, and Spot was not punished. They became best friends and had a great day at the beach.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to wake up early and watch the sunrise. Every morning, she would sit by her window and see the sky change colors. It was so pretty! One day, Lily decided to complain to her mom. She was very serious. "Mom, I don't want to wake up early for the sunrise anymore," she said. Her mom was sad, but she let Lily sleep in. The next day, Lily missed the most beautiful sunrise ever. All her friends talked about it at school. They said it was the best one they had ever seen. Lily felt very sad that she missed it. She learned that sometimes, it's better to do things we don't want to do, because we might miss out on something amazing.

Once upon a time there was a turtle called Tom who lived in a park. He loved going out and playing with the other animals. One day, he met a friendly bird called Lucy. Tom asked, "What are you up to, Lucy?" Lucy smiled. "I am looking for a shell for my nest." Tom opened his shell. Inside, he had collected some feathers and sticks. He said to Lucy, "You can have these!" Lucy was very serious. "Oh no, I need a shell to keep me safe." Then Tom pointed to a big shell in the distance. "I see one over there!" Lucy ran to the shell, but it was too heavy for her to open. She said to Tom "Can you help me?" Tom opened the heavy shell and found lots of feathers and sticks inside. Lucy smiled and thanked Tom. "Now I have everything I need to make my nest." Tom and Lucy were very happy. They made a cozy nest together and Lucy stayed with Tom in the park.
