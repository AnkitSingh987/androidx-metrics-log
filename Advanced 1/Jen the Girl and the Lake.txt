Title: Jen the Girl and the Lake

Once there was a little girl named Jen. She was only three years old but she was already very brave. One day, she decided to explore the deep forest near her home. As she walked through the trees, she felt a little afraid but she kept going. Suddenly she noticed a beautiful butterfly and she promised herself she would try to capture it. She slowly walked towards her but the butterfly flew away. She felt sad, but she kept going. As she kept walking, she stopped at the edge of a deep lake. She was amazed by what she saw. The lake was so still and blue, just like in her dreams. She wanted to jump in but it looked a little too deep. Then the little girl heard a voice. She realized the voice was coming from the lake! She asked, “Who’s there?” The voice answered, “I am the guardian of this lake. I can make all your dreams come true if you jump into the lake with me.” The little girl was scared but also excited. After a long moment of thinking, she finally decided to take the plunge. She jumped into the lake and realized that she was safe. She had found a new magical friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy loved to slide down the rainbow after it rained. All the other birds liked to watch him slide and have fun. Billy was a popular bird. One day, a new bird named Timmy came to the sky. Timmy also wanted to slide down the rainbow like Billy. But Timmy was scared. Billy saw Timmy and wanted to help him. So, Billy taught Timmy how to slide down the rainbow safely. Soon, Timmy was sliding down the rainbow with Billy. All the other birds clapped and cheered. Timmy was happy and thanked Billy for helping him. Billy was happy too because he made a new friend. The moral of the story is to help others when they need it, and you can make new friends.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little ant. This ant was troubled. He needed help to carry a big leaf. He asked his friends for support, but they were all busy. The ant felt sad. One day, the ant saw a big, strong bug in the garden. The ant thought the bug could help him. He asked the bug for support with his leaf. The bug said yes, but only if the ant could help him with a secret task. The next day, the ant and the bug started to work. They moved the leaf together. But then, the secret task began. The bug wanted the ant to help him scare the other bugs in the garden. The ant did not like this idea. He wanted to be kind and help, not scare his friends. The ant decided to talk to the bug. He told him that scaring friends was not nice. The bug listened and felt bad. He said he was sorry and promised not to scare anyone. The ant and the bug became good friends. They helped and supported each other in the garden. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fast jeep. The jeep had four big wheels and it was red. It could go very, very fast. The jeep liked to race with other cars and it would always win. One day, the jeep met a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to see how fast the jeep could go. So, the jeep said, "I will show you how fast I can go!" Tim was very happy and excited. The jeep started to go fast. It went faster and faster. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. The jeep showed Tim how fast it could go and they both had a lot of fun. They raced all day and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a dog named Max. Max was a very obedient dog. He always listened to Lily when she told him what to do. They loved to play together outside. One day, Lily and Max went to the park. They saw a small pebble on the ground. Lily picked it up and showed it to Max. Max looked at the pebble and wagged his tail. Lily threw the pebble and Max ran to catch it. They played with the pebble for a long time. Lily's mom called them to go home. Lily asked her mom if she could keep the pebble. Her mom allowed her to keep it. Lily put the pebble in her pocket and Max followed her home. They had a lot of fun that day and they were very happy.
