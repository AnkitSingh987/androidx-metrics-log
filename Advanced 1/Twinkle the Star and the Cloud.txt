Title: Twinkle the Star and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a little star named Twinkle. Twinkle was a filthy star, covered in dirt and dust. She wanted to be clean and bright like the other stars in the sky. One day, Twinkle saw a big star named Sparkle. Twinkle asked, "How can I be clean and bright like you?" Sparkle said, "You need to act like a clean star. Take a bath in the sky water to wash away the dirt." Twinkle went to the sky water and washed herself. The dirt and dust went away, and she became clean and bright. But then, a mean cloud came and pushed Twinkle, making her dirty again. Twinkle was sad, but she remembered what Sparkle said. She acted like a clean star and took another bath in the sky water. This time, she stayed clean and bright. The mean cloud saw how happy Twinkle was and decided to be nice. From that day on, Twinkle and the cloud became friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Lucy. She loved to play in her garden. One day, she found a pretty rose in her garden. It was red and smelled very nice. Lucy wanted to show her mom the rose. She took a ribbon and attach the rose to the ribbon. Then she put the ribbon in her hair. Lucy looked so pretty with the rose. Lucy went to her mom and showed her the rose. Her mom was so happy to see it. They both smiled and hugged. From that day on, the rose in her hair became Lucy's favorite thing to wear.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. Jack loved to go on adventures. Every day he would explore and find exciting things. But today, he wanted to stay at home. So, Jack went to his kitchen. He was so happy to see the kitchen was quiet. Jack softly said a prayer and he closed his eyes. He wished for a new adventure and that he could stay safe while he explores. Suddenly, Jack heard a voice. He opened his eyes to see who it was. To Jack’s surprise, it was a fairy. She said “Hi Jack! I heard your prayers and I have come to take you on your new adventure.” Jack smiled and said thank you. He was so excited. He ran around the kitchen and he quickly packed a bag of clothes and snacks. Then, Jack and the fairy flew off on the adventure of a lifetime.

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship had a captain named Tom. Tom was deaf, but he could still sail the ship very well. Tom had a friend named Sam. Sam was not deaf, but he liked to help Tom. One day, the ship needed to sail to a far place. Tom and Sam worked together to make the ship go. Sam told Tom what he heard. Tom told Sam what he saw. They were a good team. On the way, they met a little fish. The fish said, "Hello! Can you help me find my family?" Tom and Sam wanted to help. They looked for the fish's family. Finally, they succeed! The fish was happy, and Tom and Sam were happy too. They said goodbye to the fish and sailed back home.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to school. She was very excited because today she would receive a new book. The book was about math. Sally loved to learn and wanted to be very smart. In her class, there was a boy named Tim. Tim thought math was stupid. He did not like to learn new things. He would always make fun of Sally for liking math. Sally felt sad, but she knew that learning was important. The teacher gave Sally her new math book. She was so happy! She studied and practiced every day. One day, the teacher asked a hard question. Sally knew the answer because she had learned it from her math book. Tim did not know the answer. He felt silly for not knowing. He saw how happy Sally was and decided to try learning too. From that day on, Tim and Sally both loved math and became very smart.
