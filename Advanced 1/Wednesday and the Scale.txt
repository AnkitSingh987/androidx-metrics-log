Title: Wednesday and the Scale

Once upon a Wednesday it was time for the weigh-in. Everyone lined up and marched towards the scale. The scale was very fierce. One by one they stepped on the scale to find out how heavy they were. Sammy was the first one. He slowly stepped up onto the scale. The scale slowly moved up and down. Sammy was very light - it only took a few seconds for the scale to weigh his weight. Next it was Minnie's turn. She marched up the scale with confidence. The scale could feel the weight of Minnie and started to descend. It took longer for the scale to weigh Minnie than it did for Sammy. Finally it was Becky's turn. She stepped onto the scale with a fierce look in her eye. The scale groaned and then moved down slowly as it weighed her weight. After a few moments, the scale finished and they had weighed everyone's weight. They all marched back to the start laughing and happy to have weighed.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was sad because her room was messy. She wanted to clean it up but didn't know where to start. Mia felt miserable. One day, a talking cat appeared in her room. The cat said, "Hello, Mia! I can help you clean your room. First, let's put the flowers in the vase." Mia was happy and put the flowers in the vase with the cat's help. The cat told her, "Now, remember to water the flowers every day, and something fun will happen!" Mia cleaned her room with the cat. She watered the flowers in the vase every day. One day, when Mia watered the flowers, something amazing happened. The flowers grew bigger and turned into colorful balloons! Mia's room was full of balloons, and she wasn't miserable anymore. She played with the balloons and thanked the talking cat for helping her.

Once upon a time, there were two kids. One of them was called Jack and the other called Jill. They were best friends. One day, Jack and Jill went to the park to play. When they got there, they saw a man strike a match. The match made a big flame and a lot of smoke came out of it. Jack and Jill were curious and asked the man why he struck the match. The man said, "I need to light a fire. It is an expensive thing to do but it will help me to cook my dinner." Jack and Jill were amazed. They had never seen an expensive thing before. The man showed them how to be careful when they lit their own match and they thanked him. When they got home, Jack and Jill decided to show their parents what they had learned. They asked if they could strike a match and make a fire. Their parents said okay, but they needed to be careful. Jack and Jill smiled and went outside to light their fire. It was the best night ever.

Bob was an intelligent man. He had a shiny yacht that he loved. One day, he went for a drive on his yacht. Suddenly he heard someone calling for help. Bob looked around and saw a little girl. She said to Bob, “Please help! I want to go home!” Bob smiled and said, “Ok, I can help you. Jump aboard my yacht, and I will take you home.” The little girl happily jumped onto the yacht. Then Bob said, “Where do you live?” The little girl said, “I live in the big house with the green roof, near the beach.” Bob smiled again, and said, “No problem. I can take you there.” He carefully drove the yacht through the water until they reached the little girl's house. She said, “Thank you so much! You are very kind.” Bob smiled and said, “You’re welcome!” He waved goodbye as the little girl ran off to the green roofed house. Bob was happy to have helped the little girl, and proud of his intelligent thinking.

One day, a friendly bird named Bob went to the airport. He wanted to fly with his friends. Bob saw a big plane and was very happy. He believed he could fly as high as the plane. Bob met a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she could not find her mom. Bob said, "I will help you find your mom. I believe we can do it!" They looked and looked for Sue's mom. Then, something unexpected happened. Bob saw that the big plane was really Sue's mom! Sue's mom was a pilot. Sue was very happy to find her mom. They all learned that if you believe in yourself and help others, good things will happen.
