Title: Lizzie and the Door

Lizzie was a little girl. She liked to hop everywhere she went. She hopped down the hallway, through the living room, and into the kitchen. That was where she found a delicious treat waiting for her. It was a cupcake that her mom had made. Lizzie smiled and hopped around, enjoying the treat. She finished it quickly, and then hopped back towards the hallway. She wanted to take a rest in the bathroom. On her way there, however, something strange happened. Lizzie found herself hopping higher and higher with each hop! She thought it was funny and laughed. What she didn't know was that her mom had filled the bathroom with balloons! When Lizzie finally made it to the bathroom, she opened the door and was overwhelmed by the sight of all the colorful balloons. But the best part was the delicious smell in the room. Someone, her mom, had made a surprise for her - chocolate chip cookies! Lizzie hopped around the room enjoying the surprise. She had never had anything so delicious before!

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide there. It was very interesting. Tim wanted to play on the slide. He was so excited. Tim met a girl named Sue at the park. Sue wanted to play on the slide too. Tim said, "Sue, do you want to play with me?" Sue said, "Yes, Tim, I want to play with you. Let's go on the slide together." They both climbed up the slide. But when they got to the top, they saw a big hole in the slide. They didn't know what to do. Tim and Sue were sad. They couldn't play on the slide anymore. The slide was broken. They had to go home.

One day, a boy named Tim had a crazy week. His mom would tell him fun stories every night. This week, she told him a story about a big, green frog. The big, green frog lived in a small pond. One day, the frog saw a little, red ball. The frog thought it was a tasty bug. He jumped and tried to eat it. But, it was not a bug! It was a ball that a girl named Amy lost. The next day, Amy went to the pond to find her ball. She saw the big, green frog with her ball. She said, "Oh, Mr. Frog, please give me my ball back." The frog did not want to give it back. He liked his new toy. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to grow bigger and bigger. The frog got scared and jumped away. The ball turned into a big, red balloon! It was a magic balloon. Amy was so happy to have her magic balloon back. The frog was happy too because he made a new friend. And from that day on, they all played together at the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a big land. In this land, there were many animals. They all liked to play and share their toys with each other. They were very happy. One day, a smelly skunk came to the land. The other animals did not want to play with the smelly skunk. They did not want to share their toys with him. The smelly skunk was sad. Then, a kind rabbit saw the sad skunk. The rabbit wanted to help. The rabbit shared her toys with the smelly skunk. They played together and had fun. Soon, all the other animals saw this and wanted to play too. They all shared their toys and played together. The smelly skunk was not sad anymore. The animals in the big land were happy and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pink vase. The pink vase lived on a table in a big room. The vase was very happy because it had pretty flowers inside it. The flowers made the vase feel special. One night, the vase saw a little bug. The bug had a light that could glow. The bug said, "Hello, pink vase! I like your flowers." The vase said, "Thank you, little bug! I like your glow." The little bug and the pink vase became friends. They played and talked every night. The bug's glow made the vase feel even more special. And they lived happily ever after.
