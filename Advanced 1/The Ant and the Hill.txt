Title: The Ant and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little ant. The ant was not pretty. The ant was ugly. The ugly ant had many friends. They all lived in a big ant hill. One day, the ugly ant wanted to teach his friends a new game. They played and laughed. The sun was shining, and it was a happy day. But then, something unexpected happened. A big rain started to fall. The ants were scared. They ran back to their ant hill. But the ugly ant was not scared. He had an idea. The ugly ant taught his friends to make a big umbrella from a leaf. They all worked together. The rain stopped, and the ants were dry and safe. The other ants learned that the ugly ant was very smart. They loved him even more. From that day on, they played many more games and had lots of fun. And the ugly ant was the happiest ant of all.

Once upon a time there was a duck who wanted to stretch. He was a very honest duck, so he waddled over to the stretchy field. He spread out his wings and stretched his neck and legs. He felt so tall and wide! Then he heard a noise. It was a singing noise! He looked up and saw a tiny bird in a tree. The duck was so curious, he stretched up and leaned in closer. The bird kept singing and singing. The duck listened and listened, and he laughed and shook his head. It was such a funny song! The sun started to set and the duck knew it was time for him to go home. He said goodbye to the singing bird and stretched one last time. He was so happy that he had been honest and stretched his body. The duck smiled as he waddled away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a thing that he liked a lot. It was a small, soft toy. He took the toy everywhere he went. Tim loved his toy so much. One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. They played and had fun. But then, Tim looked around and saw that his toy was gone. He started to worry. "Mom, where is my thing?" he asked. His mom looked around and saw the toy under a tree. "There it is, Tim!" she said. Tim ran to get his toy and hugged it tight. He was so happy to have his little thing back. From that day on, Tim made sure to always keep his toy close to him.

Once upon a time there was a chubby little girl, Katie, who loved playing in the sand. Every day after school, she’d head straight to the park and start digging in the sand with her yellow spade. One day, she was digging in the sand when something special happened. A fairy appeared. She said, “I have a surprise for you. But you have to promise to keep it a secret until tomorrow.” Katie was excited and couldn’t wait to find out what the surprise was. She smiled and said, “Okay, I promise.” The next day, Katie eagerly headed to the park. When she arrived, she saw her friends, her mom and dad, and lots of decorations. Everyone was gathered around a big sandcastle that Katie had built the day before. The fairy appeared again. “It’s time for your surprise,” she said. “We’re celebrating your amazing sandcastle!” Katie was so happy. She laughed and celebrated with her friends and family. They ate cake and played games. At the end of the day, Katie thanked the fairy and said goodbye, hoping the surprise would happen again soon.

One day, a thief went to a house. The thief was miserable. He wanted to take things from the house. He went inside and saw a little girl. The little girl said, "Why are you sad?" The thief said, "I am miserable because I have no food or toys." The little girl wanted to help. She said, "I can provide you with food and toys." The thief was surprised. He did not expect the little girl to be kind. He thought about what he was doing. He did not want to be a thief anymore. The little girl and the thief became friends. They played with toys and ate food together. The thief was not miserable anymore. He was happy.
