Title: Molly the Cat and the Bowl

One day, a kind cat named Molly found a big bowl. The bowl was empty and dry. Molly wanted to fill the bowl with water for her friends. Molly went to the river to get water. She met a duck named Ducky. Ducky said, "Hi Molly! What are you doing?" Molly said, "I am getting water for my friends." Molly filled the big bowl with water. She shared the water with all her friends. They were happy and said, "Thank you, Molly!" Molly felt good because she was kind and helped her friends.

One day, a little girl named Emily went out for a walk with her dad. It was a warm summer evening and Emily noticed the sky was glowing in orange and pink colors. She asked her dad, "What does it mean when the sky glows?" Her dad paused and said, "I'm not sure yet, but it looks like something special is about to happen". The next day Emily and her dad went back to the same spot and this time there was a bright, sparkly bracelet lying in the grass. “It must be a magical bracelet!” Emily exclaimed. She quickly picked it up and put it on her wrist. As soon as Emily got the bracelet she felt a warm tingle throughout her body. She was filled with joy and excitement. She caught a glimpse of herself in the mirror and saw the bracelet was glowing brightly. Emily's dad looked at Emily and said, "I'm glad that you are here my daughter and I'm grateful to have you in my life". Emily looked at her dad and smiled. She knew that this special moment was meant for the two of them. For Emily the bracelet was the reminder of their special relationship. From that moment on, whenever Emily felt happy, she looked at her glowing bracelet and was grateful for the love that surrounded her.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big pile of leaves in the garden. They wanted to jump in the leaves and make a lot of noise. "Come on, Lily, let's go!" Tom said. "OK, Tom, let's go!" Lily said. They ran to the pile of leaves and jumped in. They laughed and threw leaves in the air. They felt happy and free. But then, they heard their mom call them from the house. "Tom! Lily! Come inside now! It's time for lunch!" Mom said. Tom and Lily did not want to go inside. They wanted to stay in the leaves and have more fun. "Mom, can we play a little more?" Tom asked. "No, you can't. You have to be obedient and come inside. The leaves will be here later. Now hurry up!" Mom said. Tom and Lily knew they had to listen to Mom. They did not want to make her angry or sad. They got out of the pile of leaves and walked to the house. But before they went inside, they had an idea. They each took a big leaf and put it on their foot. Then they stepped on the porch and made a loud stamp sound. "Stamp, stamp, stamp!" Tom and Lily said. They giggled and looked at each other. They thought they were very clever and funny. Mom heard the stamp sound and opened the door. She saw Tom and Lily with leaves on their feet. She smiled and shook her head. "You two are very silly, but I love you. Now come inside and wash your feet. Lunch is ready." Mom said. Tom and Lily took off the leaves and went inside. They hugged Mom and said they were sorry. They were obedient and ate their lunch. But they still remembered the fun they had in the leaves. They hoped they could play again soon.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to get to the other side of the river. He saw a bridge and walked across it. Tim was very happy to find the bridge. On the other side of the river, Tim found a big apple tree. The apples were red and looked very yummy. Tim picked one and took a big bite. It was the best apple he had ever tasted. Tim wanted to share the yummy apples with his friends. So, he filled his bag with apples and walked back across the bridge. When he got home, he gave the apples to his friends. They all loved the yummy apples from the other side of the river.
