Title: The Mummy and Her Girl and the Park

One day, a mummy and her little girl went to the park. The girl looked around at the trees and the grass and saw that it was all green! Mummy and the little girl talked together as they walked. They talked about the flowers and the birds, and how hot the sun was. The mummy turned to her little girl and told her that it was almost the end of the month. She said that she could look at the calendar and see what day it was. The little girl looked at the calendar, and she saw that it was green! Every day was coloured green, so she knew it was the end of the month. Mummy smiled and said that now it was time to go home. She told the little girl that they could come back to the park another day and talk about the different colours of the days on the calendar.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to watch a cartoon. He asked his mom, "Can I watch a cartoon, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can watch one available on TV." Tim was happy and started to watch a cartoon that had funny animals. The animals were playing and having fun. The cartoon also included a big, friendly bear. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, friendly bear turned into a tiny mouse! The other animals were very surprised. They all laughed and played together with the tiny mouse. Tim laughed too and enjoyed the funny twist in the cartoon.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman. He was very intelligent. He had a big red truck. Every day, he would watch for fires to put out. One day, the fireman saw a big fire. He drove his red truck to the fire. He put on his hat and used water to put out the fire. The fire went away and everyone was happy. The people said, "Thank you, fireman!" The fireman smiled and waved. He was proud to help. He went back to his red truck and watched for more fires to put out.

Lila and Ben were young and liked each other very much. They played together every day and shared their toys and snacks. They liked to pretend they were married and had a big house and a dog. They said they would always be together and never leave each other. One day, Lila's mom told her they had to move to another town. Lila was very sad and scared. She did not want to leave Ben and her friends. She did not want to go to a new place where she did not know anyone. She cried and hugged Ben and told him she loved him. Ben was sad and angry. He did not want to lose Lila. He had an idea. He asked Lila to marry him for real. He said they could run away and live in the woods. He said he would take care of her and protect her. He said they would be happy and free. Lila looked at Ben and smiled. She liked his idea. She wanted to be with him always. She said yes and kissed him. They packed their bags and took some food and water. They sneaked out of their houses and ran to the woods. But the woods were not like they imagined. They were dark and cold and scary. There were no houses or dogs or toys. There were only bugs and thorns and noises. They got hungry and thirsty and tired. They got lost and scared and hurt. They missed their moms and dads and friends. They cried and wished they could go back. They saw a man walking in the woods. He had a big hat and a big gun. He saw them and asked them who they were and what they were doing. They told him their names and their story. He shook his head and said they were silly and foolish. He said they were too young to get married and run away. He said they should go home and listen to their parents. He said he would take them back and help them. Lila and Ben looked at each other and nodded. They realized they had made a big mistake. They were not ready to get married and run away. They were just kids who liked to play. They thanked the man and followed him. They hoped their parents would forgive them and love them. They hoped they would see each other again and be friends. They learned a hard lesson and grew up a little.
