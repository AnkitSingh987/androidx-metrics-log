Title: The Orange Ball and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big orange ball. It was a football. The football had a friend, the sun. The sun would shine very bright every day. One day, the football saw a little boy. The little boy wanted to play. The football was happy. The sun shined brighter, as if it knew something fun was going to happen. The little boy kicked the orange football high in the sky. The football flew up to the sun. The sun shined even more bright. The football came back down, and the little boy laughed. They played all day, under the shining sun.

Once upon a time, there was a fat dolphin. The dolphin liked to play in the water. The fat dolphin had many friends. They all lived in the big, blue sea. One day, the fat dolphin saw a boat. The boat was making a big spray of water. The fat dolphin wanted to play with the boat. So, the fat dolphin swam very fast to catch the boat. The fat dolphin jumped high out of the water. It made a big spray, just like the boat. The people on the boat laughed and clapped. They loved the fat dolphin's big spray. The fat dolphin was very happy and played with the boat all day.

One morning, Maddy and her dad drove to the lake. She had heard about its beauty and the popular music that always plays around it. When Maddy and her dad arrived, the lake was surrounded by mist. It was so thick, she couldn't even see the sun. Maddy jumped out of the car and started running towards the lake. Her dad yelled, "Wait! You have to put your shoes on and prepare your swimming suit!" Maddy stopped running. She quickly put on her shoes and grabbed her swimming suit. She was ready to explore the lake with her dad. Maddy and her dad laughed as they walked through the mist. They could hear the popular music playing in the distance. Maddy knew it was going to be a great day.

Sara was a spoiled girl who always wanted to have her way. She did not like to share her toys with her brother Tom or her friends. She would lock her toys in a big box and hide the key under her bed. One day, Sara had a birthday party and invited many children to her house. She got many presents, but she did not say thank you or open them. She just put them in her box and locked it. She told the other children to play with her old toys or go home. Tom was angry with Sara for being so rude and selfish. He wanted to teach her a lesson. He sneaked into her room and found the key under her bed. He took the key and ran outside. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. He had an idea. He threw the key into the hole and ran away. Sara soon noticed that her key was gone. She searched everywhere, but she could not find it. She asked Tom if he had seen it, but he said no. He pretended to be innocent, but he had a point in his eye. Sara started to cry. She wanted to open her box and see her new toys. She begged Tom to help her, but he said he did not know where the key was. He said maybe a bird or a squirrel took it. Sara was very sad and sorry. She realized that she had been a bad girl and that she had hurt her brother and her friends. She wished she had been nicer and more generous. She learned a hard lesson that day. She never locked her toys again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a blue balloon. She loved her blue balloon very much. She took it with her everywhere she went. One day, Lily and her blue balloon went to the park. They played with other kids and had a lot of fun. Then, they saw a man with a big box. The man opened the box and released a lot of delicious cookies. All the kids were happy and excited. Lily got a cookie and shared it with her blue balloon. They both enjoyed the delicious cookie. Then, they played more games and had a great day at the park. At the end of the day, Lily and her blue balloon went home, excited to come back to the park again soon.
