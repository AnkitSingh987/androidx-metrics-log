Title: The Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. The fish lived in a pond with many other fish. The fish liked to swim and play all day. One day, the fish found a smelly, green plant. The fish thought it was fun to hide under the plant. So, the fish would cover itself with the smelly plant and wait for its friends to find it. The other fish would swim by and say, "Ew! What is that smelly thing?" Then, the little fish would jump out from under the plant and shout, "Surprise!" All the fish would laugh and play together, having lots of fun in their pond.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom loved to play outside and chase birds. One day, Tom saw a big stair in the park. He wanted to climb the stair to see what was on the other side. As Tom climbed the stair, he saw many pretty flowers. He saw red flowers, blue flowers, and yellow flowers. Tom liked the flowers very much. He saw that the flowers grow bigger and bigger every day. One day, Tom found a tiny seed near the flowers. He planted the seed in the ground and watered it every day. Soon, a small green plant started to grow. Tom was very happy to see the plant grow. He knew that one day, the plant would be as big and pretty as the other flowers.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys and help her mom. One day, her mom asked her to supply some coins for the store. Lily went to her room and looked for her coin box. She found it and took out some coins. On her way to the store, she saw a big, colorful bird. The bird took the coins from her hand and flew away. Lily was sad and didn't know what to do. She ran home to tell her mom about the bird. Her mom smiled and said, "That bird is magical! It will bring us something special in return for the coins." The next day, the bird came back with a big, shiny toy for Lily to play with. Lily was so happy and thanked the lovely bird. From that day on, Lily and the bird became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a large ball. The ball had a friend named Hoop. They liked to play together. One sunny day, they went to the park to play. They were very happy. While playing, they saw a big tree. The tree asked, "Do you want to play with me?" The ball and Hoop looked at each other and said, "Yes!" They started to play a fun game. The tree would ask questions, and the ball and Hoop would answer. After playing for a while, the ball got stuck in the tree. Hoop tried to help, but it was too high. They thought hard and remembered the tree could talk. They asked the tree for help. The tree shook its branches, and the ball fell down. The ball and Hoop thanked the tree and went home, happy they could play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. This tree had many avocados. Two friends, Tom and Tim, loved to eat avocados. One day, Tom and Tim saw the tall tree with the avocados. They both wanted the biggest avocado on the tree. Tom said, "I want the big avocado. It is mine." Tim said, "No, I want the big avocado. I saw it first." They did not know how to settle this. They both wanted the big avocado. A wise bird saw them argue. The bird said, "You can both have the big avocado. Just share it!" Tom and Tim thought about it. They agreed to share the big avocado. They were both happy. The conflict was settled and they stayed friends.

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play in the garden. They had a big tree with a swing and a slide. One day, they saw a bird on a branch. The bird was blue and yellow and sang a pretty song. "Hello, bird," said Lily. "What is your name?" "My name is Sunny," said the bird. "I live in this tree. Do you want to be my friends?" "Yes, we do," said Max. "You are very nice and colorful. Can you teach us something?" Sunny nodded. "I can teach you some wisdom. Wisdom is when you know good things and do them. Like being kind and obedient and sharing." Lily and Max wanted to learn wisdom. They liked being kind and obedient and sharing. They listened to Sunny as he told them stories and gave them advice. "Thank you, Sunny," said Lily and Max. "You are very smart and helpful. We will remember your wisdom." "You are welcome, Lily and Max," said Sunny. "You are very sweet and curious. I am happy to have you as my friends." They hugged Sunny and continued to play in the garden. They often discussed wisdom with Sunny and learned new things every day. They became the best of friends.
