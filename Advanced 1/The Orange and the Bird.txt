Title: The Orange and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there was a little orange insect. The insect had many friends to play with every day. They liked to run, jump, and laugh together. One sunny day, the orange insect and his friends were playing near a big tree. They were having so much fun! Suddenly, they saw a big bird flying down from the sky. The bird looked hungry and wanted to eat the little insects. The orange insect was scared and screamed, "Help! Help!" All of his friends heard his scream and came to help. They all screamed together, "Go away, big bird!" The big bird got scared and flew away. The little orange insect and his friends were safe and happy again. They played together and had fun until the sun went down.

Tom liked dinosaurs a lot. He had many dinosaur toys and books. He knew all their names and sounds. He wanted to be a dinosaur expert when he grew up. One day, he went to the park with his mom and dad. He saw a big slide that looked like a dinosaur. He ran to it and climbed up. He was very happy and excited. He shouted, "Look, mom and dad, I'm a dinosaur too! Roar!" But then he saw some other kids playing near the slide. They were older and bigger than him. They laughed at him and said, "You're not a dinosaur, you're a baby! Dinosaurs are for babies. You're silly and stupid. Go away!" Tom felt very sad and angry. He wanted to cry. He felt embarrassed. He wished he had never climbed the slide. He wished he had never liked dinosaurs. He wished he had never come to the park. He slid down and ran to his mom and dad. He hugged them and said, "I want to go home. I don't like dinosaurs anymore. I don't like the park anymore. I don't like anything anymore." His mom and dad tried to comfort him and tell him that the other kids were wrong and mean. They told him that he was smart and brave and that they loved him. They told him that dinosaurs were cool and fun and that he could like whatever he wanted. But Tom didn't listen. He was too hurt and ashamed. He didn't want to talk or play anymore. He just wanted to go home and hide. He didn't want to be a dinosaur expert anymore. He didn't want to be popular anymore. He just wanted to be alone.

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship had many tasty treats on it. The ship was going on a long trip. One day, the ship had to complete a big job. The job was to bring the tasty treats to a far away land. The people in the far away land were very hungry and needed the treats. The ship worked very hard to complete the job. It sailed through the water and finally reached the far away land. The people were so happy to see the ship and the tasty treats. They said thank you to the ship for completing the job. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little bug named Bob wanted to help his mom. His mom had a lot of laundry to do. Bob thought he could be a reliable helper. So, he decided to crawl to the laundry room. When Bob got to the laundry room, he saw a big cat named Tom. Tom was not nice. He said, "You can't help with the laundry, little bug. Go away!" Bob felt sad but he really wanted to help his mom. Bob crawled back to his mom and told her what happened. His mom said, "Don't worry, Bob. You can help me fold the clothes." Bob felt happy and crawled to help his mom. In the end, Bob was a reliable helper and his mom was proud of him.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who lived in a tiny town. She liked to dance all the time – it was her favorite thing to do. One day she heard the birds singing outside and she just wanted to join them. She looked out into the garden and saw that the flowers were all bright purple. The little girl smiled and thought how fun it would be to dance with the purple petals. So, she ran outside and started twirling and spinning in circles. She did a grand finale and was delighted to see the purple petals falling all around her. Suddenly, a man came walking by and stopped to watch the little girl. He said to her "That was a very special dance. It looks like you love dancing!" The little girl smiled and said "Yes. I love to dance in my little town."
