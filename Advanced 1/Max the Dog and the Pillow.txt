Title: Max the Dog and the Pillow

One day, a little dog named Max found a big, soft pillow in his house. Max was a stubborn dog and he loved to bite things. He looked at the pillow and thought, "This will be fun to bite!" Max started to bite the pillow, but then he heard his mom say, "Max, don't bite that pillow! It's not a toy." Max didn't listen because he was stubborn. He thought, "I will bite it when mom is not looking." Later that day, Max saw his chance. His mom was in the kitchen, so he went to the pillow and started to bite it again. Suddenly, he heard a strange noise. Max stopped biting and looked around. He saw a little bird outside the window. The bird said, "Max, you should listen to your mom. She knows what's best for you." Max thought about what the bird said. He decided to stop being stubborn and listen to his mom. Max stopped biting the pillow and went to play with his toys instead. And from that day on, Max never bit the pillow again.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On this farm, there was a mule. The mule was not like other mules. He was very cheap. He did not want to share his food or toys with other animals. One day, the farmer told the cheap mule that a big storm was coming. The mule did not want to help the other animals. He thought, "I will save my food and toys. I will not let the storm take them." The mule did not know that by not helping, he could not prevent bad things from happening. The storm came and it was very big. The cheap mule tried to hide his food and toys, but the wind was too strong. The wind took all of his things away. The mule was very sad. He wished he had helped the other animals. Then, maybe they could have worked together to prevent the storm from taking their things.

Lily and Ben are friends who live on a farm. They like to play in the wheat field and look for bugs and birds. Sometimes they tease each other and make funny noises. One day, they see a big orange cat in the wheat. It is hiding and watching them. Lily and Ben are curious and want to pet the cat. They walk slowly and quietly towards it. But the cat does not want to be petted. It is scared and angry. It hisses and scratches at them. Lily and Ben run away and cry. They have red marks on their arms and legs. The farmer hears them and comes to see what is wrong. He sees the cat and chases it away. He tells Lily and Ben that the cat is wild and does not like people. He gives them a hug and a band-aid. He says they should not tease the cat or go near it. Lily and Ben say they are sorry and they learned their lesson. They decide to play somewhere else and be nice to animals. They are still friends and they are happy.

Once upon a time there lived a little man called Jack. Every day, he would get up and set off on an adventure. One day, he came across a large, tough veil. Jack tried to pull the veil away but it wouldn't budge. Suddenly Jack heard a voice behind him. It was an old man. "Hey little man, why are you trying to pull away the veil?" asked the old man. To which Jack replied, "I thought that if I get rid of the veil, I could find something important." The old man shook his head, "Son, something important is not found on the other side of the veil. It is found within us. We are all born with a wisdom, a courage and a strength that keeps us going in difficult times. That is why the veil is so tough, so that we don't forget the power we already have." Jack nodded, understanding the old man's words and feeling proud of the strength that he already has. He thanked the old man and went on his way, feeling brave and empowered. The moral of the story is that we can find strength and courage within ourselves and that is something that nothing and no one can ever take away.
