Title: Anna and the Dog

Anna liked to play with her doll, Lucy. Lucy had a velvet dress and long hair. Anna thought Lucy was very attractive. She liked to brush Lucy's hair and make her dance. One day, Anna and Lucy went to the park with Mommy. They saw a big dog with brown fur and a wagging tail. The dog ran towards them and barked. Anna was scared. She hugged Lucy and cried. "Hello, don't be afraid," said a boy who came after the dog. "This is Max. He is friendly. He just wants to say hi." The boy held Max's leash and made him sit. He smiled at Anna and said, "I'm Ben. What's your name?" "I'm Anna. This is Lucy," Anna said, holding up her doll. "Lucy is pretty. Can I see her?" Ben asked. Anna nodded and gave Lucy to Ben. Ben looked at Lucy's velvet dress and touched her hair. He said, "She is soft and shiny. Can I make her shake?" Anna said, "Yes, but be gentle. She is fragile." Ben nodded and moved Lucy's arms and legs. He made her shake like she was dancing. He said, "Look, Lucy is happy. She likes to shake." Anna laughed and said, "Yes, she does. She likes to play with you and Max." Ben smiled and said, "Do you want to play with us too? We can go on the swings and the slide." Anna said, "Yes, please. That sounds fun." Mommy said, "Okay, but stay close and be careful." Anna, Ben, Lucy, and Max went to play together. They had a good time. They made new friends. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a soft, comfy pillow that she loved very much. Lily would hug her pillow every night as she went to sleep. One day, she started to wonder if her pillow could talk. One evening, as Lily was getting ready for bed, she heard a small voice. "Hello, Lily," said the voice. Lily looked around and saw that her pillow was talking to her! She was very surprised. "Hi, Pillow," said Lily. "I didn't know you could talk!" The pillow smiled and said, "I am a magic pillow. I can make you feel rich with love and happiness." Lily hugged her pillow tight and felt very happy. From that day on, Lily and her pillow were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play on the slide at the park. One day, she saw an amazing new slide. It was very big and had a label on it. The label said, "For big kids only." Lily wanted to slide down the amazing slide. But she was scared because the label said it was for big kids only. She asked her friend Tom, "Can I slide down the big slide?" Tom said, "I don't know, the label says it's for big kids only." Lily was sad but she wanted to try. She climbed up the ladder and sat at the top of the slide. She was scared, but she knew she could do it. She slid down the amazing slide and laughed. When she got to the bottom, she was so happy. She said, "I did it! I can slide on the big kids slide!"

John and his mom were having a lovely day in the valley. The sun was shining, the trees were swaying in the breeze and the birds were singing. John and his mom had a picnic, playing together in the grass and sharing stories. Suddenly John began to feel very tired. "Mom, I'm so sleepy," he said. His mom smiled and said, "That's okay, let's find a nice spot to take a nap in the shade. We can have a little rest." So they put down a blanket in the valley, snuggled up and soon both of them fell fast asleep. As they slept, a storm began to brew overhead. Soon it had darkened so much that John started to feel scared. He woke up and tried to shake his mom's arm, but she was fast asleep. John started to cry, but no amount of shouting was enough to wake his mom. John was all alone, in the dark and the storm, and he had no idea which way he should go. Eventually, he wandered alone until morning. He was lost and wet, but he eventually found his way back to his mom. She was so grateful that John was safe, but John couldn't help but feel sad. His mom had forgotten him.
