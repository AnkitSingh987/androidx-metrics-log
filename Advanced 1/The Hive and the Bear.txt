Title: The Hive and the Bear

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a hive. Bees lived in the hive. They made sweet honey. One day, a weak bear came to the tree. He saw the hive and wanted the honey. The weak bear tried to resist eating the honey, but he was very hungry. He climbed the tree to get to the hive. The bees were not happy. They wanted to protect their home. The bees buzzed around the weak bear. They made him scared. The weak bear decided to resist the honey and climbed down the tree. The bees were happy that their hive was safe. The weak bear learned to resist things that were not his.

One day, a little fish named Finny loved to swim. He swam in the big blue sea with his fish friends. Finny was a careless fish, always laughing and playing. "Let's take a photo!" said Finny to his friends. They all smiled and swam close together. Snap! The photo was taken. The fish friends looked at the photo and laughed. One day, Finny and his fish friends were swimming and playing. They saw a big shark. The shark was big and scary. Finny and his friends swam away fast. They were safe and happy. They learned to be more careful when they swam and played.

Once there was a girl and her mom. They went to a shop to buy candy. The girl saw something she wanted, and asked her mom if she could have it. Her mom said no, that she should stay calm. The girl was very rude and didn't listen to her mom. She kept asking for the candy and making a fuss. She made a scene and stomped her feet. The shopkeeper was very mad. He told the girl to stop and gave her mom a strip of paper. He said "If you don't get her to stay calm, I'll have to call the police". The girl's mom was very worried. She quickly took the girl and left. On the way home, she explained to the girl why it is important to stay calm and to always be nice. The story ends with the girl's mom reminding her to be careful and to stay calm, no matter what. The moral of the story is that it is important to be respectful and to not be rude.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to rise early in the morning when the sun was just coming up. She felt comfortable in her warm bed, but she liked to see the pretty colors in the sky. One day, Sue woke up and saw a big box in her room. It was a gift from her mom and dad. Sue was very happy and excited. She ran to the gift and opened it up. Inside, she found a soft, fluffy pillow. Sue loved her new pillow. She put it on her bed and lay down on it. It was so comfortable! Now, Sue could rise early and watch the sun while feeling cozy on her new pillow. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play a game called "guess". She would think of something and her friends had to guess what it was. One day, Amy thought of a very hard thing to guess. Her friends tried to guess what it was. They guessed a cat, a dog, and a ball. But Amy shook her head and said, "No, it's not any of those things." Her friends kept guessing, but they could not find the right answer. Finally, Amy told her friends the answer. "It was a year!" she said. Her friends were surprised. They never thought to guess a year. They all laughed and played the game again. This time, they picked an easier thing to guess.

Once upon a time, there was a big party to celebrate Tom's birthday. All his friends came to have fun and play games. They were very happy and excited to be there. At the party, they had a big pizza with cheese and tomato. Everyone loved the pizza and ate it all up. After eating, they played a game called "dead bug". In this game, when the music stopped, they had to lie down like a dead bug. Tom and his friends had a great time at the party. They laughed, played, and enjoyed the pizza. Everyone went home with a big smile on their face, remembering the fun they had celebrating Tom's birthday.
