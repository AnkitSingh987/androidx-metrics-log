Title: Sue and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her room. She wanted to pick her favorite toy. She saw her toys were organized in a box. Sue was happy. Sue picked a toy with a big button on it. The button was red. She pushed the button and the toy said, "Hi, I am Tim!" Sue was surprised and said, "Hi, Tim!" Tim the toy started to talk more. He said, "I can help you pick up your toys!" Sue was very excited. She did not know her toy could talk and help her. Sue and Tim had fun picking up toys all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he felt impatient because his best friend, Sam, was late to come over and play. When Sam finally arrived, Tim said, "Sam, I was waiting a long time. Let's play now!" Sam smiled and said, "Sorry, Tim. Let's play with your toy cars." As they played, Tim encouraged Sam to make a big race track for their cars. They had so much fun racing their cars on the track they made. Tim was happy that Sam came to play, and they both enjoyed the day together.

One day, John had an important job to do. He had to take all of his family's waste to the dump. He was nervous because it was a big responsibility. So, before he started his job, John prayed. He asked the Lord to help him with his work. John took the family's waste very carefully. He drove very slowly and tried not to make any mistakes. Finally, John was done. He felt very proud that he had taken care of everything. Again, he thanked the Lord for helping him. Now John knew why it was so important to take waste to the dump. He also knew that prayer was a powerful thing.

Once upon a time, in a humble little village, there lived a kind farmer. The farmer worked hard every day to grow his crops. One day, he went to his field to see if his plants were ready to yield food. As the farmer walked through the misty morning, he saw the plants were big and strong. He knew it was time to pick them. He was very happy that his hard work had helped the plants grow well. The farmer shared his food with the other humble people in the village. They were all grateful and thanked him. The farmer felt proud of his work, and the village was full of joy. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary cat named Tom. Tom loved to speed around the house, playing with his toys. One day, Tom saw a menu on the table. He did not know what it was for, but he liked the pictures of food on it. Tom's friend, a little girl named Lily, saw him looking at the menu. She had an idea for a fun game. She took the menu and hid it under a big pillow. Tom did not see where she put it, so he started to speed around the house, looking for the menu. Tom looked everywhere for the menu, but he could not find it. Then, something unexpected happened. As Tom ran past the big pillow, he heard a noise. He stopped and looked back at the pillow. He pushed it with his paw and saw the menu! Tom was so happy that he found the menu, and he and Lily laughed and played together for the rest of the day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a small bird on it. The bird was sad because it was hungry. Mia gazed at the bird and wanted to help it feel good. She went back to her home and found a piece of bread. Mia took the bread and went back to the tree. She gave the piece of bread to the bird. The bird ate the bread and felt good. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird grew bigger and bigger! It turned into a big, friendly bird. The big bird thanked Mia for her help and took her on a fun ride in the sky. Mia learned that being kind can bring good things to you.
