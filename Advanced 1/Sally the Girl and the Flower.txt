Title: Sally the Girl and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. Sally liked to play in her garden with her dog, Max. One day, Sally saw a pretty flower that was red and smooth. She wanted to show Max the flower. Sally called Max and said, "Come, Max! Look at this smooth flower!" Max came running and kneeled next to Sally. They both looked at the pretty flower together. Max wagged his tail because he liked the flower too. Later, Sally and Max played near the smooth flower. They were very happy. They kneeled and looked at more flowers in the garden. Sally and Max had a fun day playing with the pretty, smooth flowers.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play and have fun. One day, he went to the park with his best friend, a little girl named Anna. At the park, they saw a big, cold puddle. Buddy jumped in and splashed around. "Buddy, you are so silly!" Anna laughed. They played in the cold puddle until they were very wet and dirty. Anna's mom said, "Buddy, you need to bathe before you go inside the house." Buddy did not like baths, but he knew that he had to be clean. So, he let Anna help him bathe. They laughed and splashed water until Buddy was clean. As they were bathing, a little bird flew down and said, "Thank you, Buddy and Anna, for playing in the cold puddle. You helped me find my lost toy!" The bird showed them a shiny toy that was hidden in the puddle. The moral of the story is that sometimes unexpected good things can happen when you are being dependable and doing what you are supposed to do, like taking a bath.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Bob. Bob loved the sea and had a big boat. One day, Bob saw a dolphin in the water. The dolphin was very friendly and smart. Bob wanted to play with the dolphin, so he jumped into the water. Bob said to the dolphin, "Let's play a game. I will test how fast you can swim." The dolphin agreed and was happy to play. They started the game, and the dolphin swam very fast. Bob tried to swim too, but he was not as fast as the dolphin. As they played, the sky got dark and the water got rough. Bob was scared and wanted to go back to his boat. But the boat was far away, and he could not swim fast enough. The dolphin tried to help, but the waves were too big. In the end, Bob could not make it back to his boat, and he was lost at sea.

Once upon a time, there was a poor tomato. This tomato was very sad. It wanted to improve and become a big, strong tomato. One day, a little boy found the poor tomato. The boy said, "Don't be sad, little tomato. I will help you improve and become strong." The boy took the tomato home and put it in a pot with good soil. Day by day, the boy took care of the tomato. He gave it water and lots of love. The tomato started to improve and grow bigger. The boy was very happy. One morning, the boy went to check on the tomato. He saw that the tomato was now a big, strong tomato. But something unexpected happened. The tomato could talk! The tomato said, "Thank you for helping me improve, little boy. I am now a magic tomato and can grant you one wish." The boy thought for a moment and said, "I wish for more tomatoes, so I can help them improve too!" The magic tomato smiled and made lots of tomatoes appear. The boy and the magic tomato worked together to help all the tomatoes improve and become big, strong tomatoes.

Ben was a boy who liked to cook with his mom. He liked to chop carrots, stir soup, and sprinkle cheese. But he did not like onions. He thought onions were disgusting. They made his eyes water and his nose run. One day, mom asked Ben to help her make a salad. She gave him a knife and a cutting board and said, "Ben, can you chop this onion for me?" Ben did not want to chop the onion. He wanted to say no. But he did not want to make mom sad. So he nodded and took the onion. He tried to chop the onion as fast as he could. But the onion was strong and smelly. It made Ben cry and sneeze. He hated the onion. He wished he could throw it away. But he did not want to waste food. So he kept chopping. Mom saw Ben's face and felt sorry for him. She said, "Ben, you are doing a great job. Thank you for helping me. But you don't have to chop the whole onion. You can stop now and wash your hands." Ben was happy to hear that. He said, "Thank you, mom. I don't like onions. They are disgusting." Mom smiled and said, "I know, Ben. But they make the salad tasty. Maybe you will like them when you are older. But for now, you can have some extra cheese on your salad. How does that sound?" Ben said, "That sounds good, mom. I love cheese. And I love you." Mom said, "I love you too, Ben. You are a good helper. And a good son." They hugged and finished the salad. Ben was glad he helped mom. But he was gladder he did not have to eat the onion.
