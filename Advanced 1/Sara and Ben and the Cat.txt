Title: Sara and Ben and the Cat

Sara and Ben like to visit their grandma every week. She lives in a big house with a garden and a cat. They always have fun with her and the cat. One day, grandma said she had a surprise for them. She took them to a new place where they could eat. It was called a restaurant. There were many people and tables and chairs. A nice man gave them a menu. It had pictures of different foods. Sara and Ben looked at the menu. They wanted to try everything. They were very hungry and competitive. They said, "I want this, and this, and this!" They pointed at the pictures. Grandma smiled and said, "You can't have everything. You have to choose one thing each. And you have to share with me and the cat." Sara and Ben thought hard. They wanted to pick the best thing. They looked at each other and said, "I want the same thing as you!" They both pointed at the same picture. It was a big pizza with cheese and tomatoes and mushrooms. Grandma laughed and said, "Okay, okay. We can have the pizza. But you have to leave some for me and the cat." She told the nice man what they wanted. He nodded and went away. Soon, the pizza came. It was hot and smelled good. Sara and Ben each took a big slice. They ate and ate. They said, "Mmm, this is yummy!" They were very happy. They gave some to grandma and the cat. They were happy too. They finished the pizza. They were full and satisfied. They thanked grandma for the surprise. She hugged them and said, "You're welcome, my darlings. I love you very much." They said, "We love you too, grandma." They went back to her house. They played with the cat and told her stories. They had a wonderful day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sadie. One day, Sadie asked her mom if she could visit her friend, Jane. So Sadie and her mom packed a rag and visited Jane. When they were there, Jane had something to show Sadie. It was a very special and original toy! Jane said, "Sadie, I have something for you. It's very special, and I think you will like it." Sadie was so excited that she could barely breathe. She asked Jane, "What is it?" Jane smiled and said, "It's a surprise. close your eyes and I'll show it to you." Sadie closed her eyes, and when she opened them back up she saw the most beautiful and original toy she had ever seen. She was excited, and she thanked Jane for her surprise. From that moment on, Sadie and Jane visited each other often, and they shared lots of other original and special toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play outside with his ball. One sunny day, Max went outside to play but his ball was gone. Max was sad. Max started to look around for his ball. He peeked behind a tree and under a bush. Then he saw his ball across the street. Max knew he had to be careful with the traffic. He waited and looked both ways. Suddenly, a big bird swooped down and picked up Max's ball. Max was surprised! The bird flew up high and dropped the ball into Max's yard. Max was grateful and wagged his tail. He could play with his ball again, thanks to the bird. Max learned that sometimes, unexpected friends can help you out.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was going on a trip with his family. They packed their luggage and got in the car. Spot was very excited. On the way, they saw a big puddle. Spot wanted to play, but his family said, "No, Spot! You will get dirty and we don't want a terrible mess in the car!" Spot was sad, but he knew they were right. Finally, they got to the beach. Spot played all day in the sand and water. At the end of the day, he was very dirty. His family knew it was time to bathe him. Spot didn't like baths, but he knew it was important to be clean. After his bath, he was happy, and his family was happy too.
