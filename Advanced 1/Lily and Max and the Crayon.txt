Title: Lily and Max and the Crayon

Lily and Max were playing with crayons. They liked to draw many things with different colors. Lily had a red crayon and Max had a blue one. They were having fun. But then, Lily saw a yellow crayon on the table. She wanted to use it to draw a sun. She reached for the yellow crayon, but Max grabbed it first. "Hey, that's mine!" Lily said. "No, it's mine!" Max said. They both pulled the yellow crayon, but it broke in half. The pieces fell on the floor. Lily and Max looked at the broken crayon. They felt sorry. "I'm sorry, Lily," Max said. "I'm sorry, Max," Lily said. They hugged each other. They picked up the pieces of the crayon and put them in the trash. They decided to share the other crayons. They drew a sun with red and blue. They made a new color. They smiled. They remained friends.

Once upon a time, there was a great big bear named Bob. Bob lived in a big open forest with many trees and flowers. One day, Bob met a small rabbit named Lily. They became good friends. Bob and Lily played in the open forest every day. One day, they found a big box. Bob said, "Let's open it!" Lily said, "No, we must resist! It might be bad." Bob thought about it and agreed with Lily. They decided to resist opening the box and went back to playing. They had so much fun in the open forest. Bob and Lily were very happy and stayed great friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a kind and generous girl named Lily. She loved to share her toys with her friends. One day, she saw a big shower in the sky. It was raining cats and dogs! Lily had an idea. She said, "Imagine if we could play in the shower!" So, Lily and her friends put on their boots and went outside. They jumped in the puddles and laughed as the rain fell on their faces. After they played in the shower, they went back inside. Lily shared her warm towels with her friends. They were all happy and dry. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a thick forest. In the forest, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lamp that he loved very much. Every night, he would turn on the lamp to help him see in the dark. One day, Tim met a big bear. The bear said, "I need help to load these big boxes. Can you help me?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you!" So, they loaded the big boxes together. The bear was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's lamp started to talk! The lamp said, "I can help you both see better in the dark!" Tim and the bear were very surprised. They had never seen a talking lamp before. From that day on, Tim, the bear, and the talking lamp became the best of friends. They had many fun adventures together in the thick forest.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a deaf dog, so he could not hear anything. One day, he went for a walk in the park. As he walked, he saw a big, red ball. Spot loved to play with balls, so he ran to get it. As he got closer, another dog named Fluffy entered the park. Fluffy saw the ball too and wanted to play with it. Both dogs ran fast to get the ball. They did not know who would get it first. Spot and Fluffy reached the ball at the same time. They both wanted to play with it, but they did not want to share. They started to bark at each other. A little girl saw them and came over. She picked up the ball and threw it far away. Spot and Fluffy ran after it together. They learned that playing together was more fun than fighting.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an ambulance. The ambulance was always alert and ready to help people. It had a loud sound that went "wee-woo-wee-woo" when it was going fast. One sunny day, a cat named Fluffy was playing near the road. Fluffy saw the ambulance coming and wanted to welcome it. Fluffy ran to the road and waved her paw. The ambulance driver saw Fluffy and tried to stop. But the ambulance could not stop in time. Fluffy was not fast enough to get out of the way. The ambulance hit Fluffy, and she got hurt. The driver was very sad. From that day on, Fluffy did not play near the road anymore.
