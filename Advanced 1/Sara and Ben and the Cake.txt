Title: Sara and Ben and the Cake

Sara and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. Sara had a big spoon and Ben had a wooden bowl. They put some flour, water, salt and sugar in the bowl and mixed them together. "Look, we made dough!" Sara said. "Now we need to bake it in the oven." "But the oven is too hot and scary," Ben said. "I don't want to go near it." Sara looked at the oven. It was big and black and made a loud noise. She felt scared too. But she wanted to finish their game. "Maybe we can find something else to bake it in," she said. "Let's look in the cupboard." She opened the cupboard door and saw many things inside. There were plates, cups, pots, pans and boxes. She reached for a small metal tin that looked like a cake. "Here, this will do," she said. "We can put the dough in here and pretend it's a cake." She gave the tin to Ben and he put the dough inside. He smiled and said, "Good idea, Sara. You are very brave." They put the tin on the table and pretended to turn on the oven. They waited for a few minutes and then pretended to take out the cake. "Mmm, it smells good," Sara said. "Let's share it with Mommy and Daddy." They cut the cake into four pieces and put them on plates. They carried the plates to the living room where their parents were reading. "Look what we made," Ben said. "It's a cake for you." Their parents looked surprised and happy. They praised Sara and Ben for their creativity and kindness. They took a bite of the cake and pretended it was delicious. Sara and Ben felt proud and happy. They hugged their parents and each other. They had fun playing in the kitchen and learned to be brave.

One day, a girl named Sue found a purple bottle. It was very pretty. Sue wanted to see what was inside. She tried to pull the top off, but it was hard. Sue asked her friend, Tom, for help. Tom said, "Let me try!" He pulled and pulled, and finally, the top came off! Inside the bottle, they found a thick sauce. Sue and Tom were very hungry, so they decided to eat the sauce. But as soon as they took a bite, something unexpected happened. They both turned purple, just like the bottle! Sue and Tom laughed and laughed, and they stayed purple for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little otter named Ollie. Ollie liked to play and pretend all day. He would pretend to be a big fish or a fast boat. But one day, Ollie felt miserable. He did not want to play or pretend. He just wanted to sit and be sad. Ollie's friend, a little duck named Dolly, saw him sitting all sad. She wanted to help Ollie feel better. So, Dolly thought of a game to play. "Let's pretend to be big, strong trees," said Dolly. Ollie did not want to play, but he tried anyway. As they were pretending to be trees, something unexpected happened. A strong wind came and blew them into a new part of the river. There, they saw a big, fun slide! Ollie and Dolly went down the slide and laughed. Ollie was not miserable anymore. He was happy and ready to play and pretend again.

Once upon a time, there was a kind bird named Blue. Blue lived in a big tree with many green leaves. Blue had many friends who lived in the tree too. One day, Blue saw a little bug on a palm tree near the big tree. The little bug was sad because it wanted to fly like Blue and the other birds. Blue wanted to help the little bug. Blue had an idea. Blue found a big leaf and told the little bug to sit on it. Then, Blue held the leaf with its beak and started to zoom through the sky. The little bug was happy and laughed as they flew together. Blue and the little bug became good friends and played together every day.
