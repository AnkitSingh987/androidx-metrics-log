Title: Tim the Boy and the Bunny

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Bunny. He loved Mr. Bunny very much. One day, Mr. Bunny's ear had a tear. Tim was sad. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, I will use scissors to fix Mr. Bunny's ear." Tim watched as his mom cut a little cloth. She was very careful. Tim was happy to see Mr. Bunny getting fixed. But then, the scissors disappeared! Tim and his mom looked everywhere. They found the scissors under the table. Tim's mom finished fixing Mr. Bunny's ear. Tim was very happy and hugged Mr. Bunny tight.

One day, a big, strong dog named Max went to play outside. He had brown skin with a long tail. Max loved to run and jump in the park with his friends. He saw his friend, a small cat named Lily, and they started to play together. "Let's play catch," said Max. Lily liked to play catch too, so they played for a long time. They ran and jumped, having lots of fun. Max was very strong, and he could run very fast. Lily was not as strong, but she was very smart. As they played, they saw a big tree with a high branch. Max had an idea. "I can jump and catch the branch," he said to Lily. Lily was worried, but she knew Max was strong. Max jumped and caught the branch, but it broke! Max fell to the ground, but he was okay. Lily ran to Max and said, "Are you okay, Max?" Max was a little hurt, but he was still strong. "Yes, I am okay," he said. They learned that even strong friends need to be careful when they play. From that day on, Max and Lily played safely and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a great person named Tom. Tom was very tall and had a big smile. He liked to help people and make them happy. One day, Tom saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and couldn't get down. Tom wanted to help the cat, so he climbed the tree. He held the cat and climbed down. The cat was so happy, it started to purr. Tom saw a door that was open. He knew it was not safe to leave doors open. So, he went to the door and shut it. Everyone was happy that Tom helped them. They all said, "Thank you, Tom! You are a great person!" Tom smiled and felt good that he helped his friends.

Once upon a time there was a dependable, little girl named Sarah. She liked playing in the garden every day and she was always given a task to do. Today, Sarah was given a special job. She was told to fill a bucket with water from the well. She was deeply excited to help her mom. She gave a big smile and ran to the well. Sarah started to fill the bucket with water. But everytime she poured, the water would just slip through her fingers. She tried again and again, but nothing changed, Sarah began to cry. All of a sudden, a kind nearby voice spoke. It said: "Perhaps if you tried again, Sarah, you will be successful". Sarah looked up and saw an old lady smiling at her. She nodded and tried the task again. This time, Sarah placed her hands around the bucket and said "repeat" very slowly and firmly. The bucket then began to fill with water. Sarah was surprised! And her mom was so happy. Later, Sarah thanked the old lady for her help. From that day on, Sarah earned the name of the most dependable girl in town.

Once upon a time, there was a kind little girl called Ivy. She always thought of things to do to help others. One day, Ivy looked out the window and saw a pit in the garden. She knew the giant pit would need lots of filling up. When she told her mum, her mum thought it was such a thoughtful idea. So she gave Ivy a shovel and a bucket, and Ivy requested her mum help her. So they both began to fill up the pit. Ivy got lots of dirt and put it in the bucket. Then she asked her mum to give the bucket to her so she could pour it into the pit. Soon, Ivy and her mum filled up the pit. Then, Ivy thanked her mum for being so helpful. The two of them were very happy to have the pit filled up, and Ivy thought about ways she could help others too.
