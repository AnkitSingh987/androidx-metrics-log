Title: Tim the Boy and the Wagon

One day, a little boy named Tim found a noisy wagon in his yard. He was so happy and wanted to show his best friend, Sue. Tim ran to Sue's house with the noisy wagon making loud sounds as he pulled it. When Tim got to Sue's house, he said, "Sue, look at my noisy wagon! Let's play with it!" Sue looked at the wagon and said, "But Tim, the wagon is too noisy. My baby sister is sleeping, and we can't wake her up." Tim was sad, but he had an idea. "Let's surprise Sue's sister with a quiet wagon!" Tim and Sue looked around for things to make the noisy wagon quiet. They found some soft cloth and put it on the wheels. Now, the wagon was not noisy anymore! Tim and Sue played with the quiet wagon all day. They were happy they could play without waking up Sue's sister. In the end, they had lots of fun and even gave Sue's sister a ride in the wagon when she woke up.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mark. Mark was sad and miserable. He had no friends to play with. One day, he went to the park to be alone. He sat on the grass and gazed at the sky. While Mark was gazing at the sky, he saw something strange. It was a big, shiny thing in the sky. It came closer and closer to him. Mark was scared but also curious. He had never seen anything like it before. As the shiny thing came closer, Mark saw that it was a big, friendly bird. The bird landed next to Mark and smiled at him. Mark was not miserable anymore. He had found a new friend in the most unexpected way. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a small mouse. They lived in a fair house with many toys. Every day at noon, they would play together and have fun. One day, while they were playing, the small mouse saw a big door. The small mouse wanted to see what was behind the door. So, he opened the door and went in. The big cat did not see him go. Inside the room, the small mouse found a big cake. He was very happy and started to eat the cake. But then, something unexpected happened. The cake was a trap, and the small mouse could not escape. The big cat heard the small mouse cry for help and came running. The big cat saw the trap and knew what to do. He used his strong paws to break the trap and save the small mouse. The small mouse was very thankful, and they both learned to be careful when exploring new places. From that day on, they continued to play together every day at noon but always stayed away from the big door.

One day, a girl named Sue found some chalk. Sue loved to draw with chalk. It was a hot day, so Sue went outside to play. She wanted to draw on the sidewalk. Sue saw her friend Tom and asked, "Do you want to draw with me?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to draw too!" They started to draw big pictures on the sidewalk. They drew a sun, a house, and a cat. They were having fun. But then, a big wind came and blew their chalk away. Sue and Tom were sad. They tried to prevent the chalk from blowing away by holding it tight, but it was too late. They learned that they should put their things away when they are not using them. This way, they can keep their things safe and have more fun next time.

Once upon a time, there was a big gorilla. His name was Bobo. Bobo lived in a big open space with trees and grass. He liked to play all day with his friends. One day, Bobo met a new friend. The new friend said, "Hi, my name is Toto. I am a bird!" Bobo was happy to meet Toto. They played together and had lots of fun. Bobo and Toto were playing when something unexpected happened. Toto said, "Bobo, look! I can fly!" Bobo was amazed. He never saw a friend fly before. Toto flew high and low, and Bobo clapped his big hands. They laughed and played even more. From that day on, Bobo and Toto were best friends.
