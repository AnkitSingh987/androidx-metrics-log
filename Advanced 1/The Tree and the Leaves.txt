Title: The Tree and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had many leaves. One day, a strong wind came. It was difficult for the tree to stand tall. The wind wanted to remove the leaves from the tree. The tree asked the wind, "Why do you want to remove my leaves?" The wind said, "I want to show you wisdom." The tree was sad, but it let the wind remove its leaves. After the wind was gone, the tree saw that it was still strong. It learned the wisdom that it could be strong without its leaves. The tree was happy and thanked the wind. And the tree grew more leaves and became big and strong again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was only three years old. She had a dream to become a successful dancer. Every day, she practiced with her nose in the air and big smile on her face. Her mom saw how hard she was working, so she decided to sign her daughter up for a dancing class. The girl was excited and she practiced even more. When the time for the dancing class finally arrived, the little girl was ready. With a nose pointed at the ceiling, she completed all the exercises with a shining smile. The girl was so successful that her teacher gave her a special reward. She was very proud of her accomplishment and she couldn't believe how far she had come. She had danced her way to success!

Once upon a time, there was a big red tube. The tube was very long and fun to play with. Kids liked to roll it, sit on it, and even hide inside it. The tube was the most popular toy in the park. One day, a little boy named Mark came to the park. Mark saw the big red tube and wanted to play with it too. But a jealous girl named Jane didn't want to share the tube. She wanted the tube all to herself. Jane thought of a plan to keep the tube away from Mark. Jane rolled the tube to the top of a hill. She knew that Mark would try to get the tube, but she didn't think about what would happen next. When Mark tried to grab the tube, it started to roll down the hill very fast. Mark and Jane both ran after it, but they couldn't catch it. The big red tube rolled faster and faster until it hit a tree. The tree shook and many apples fell down. The apples hit Mark and Jane on their heads. They both fell down and started to cry. The big red tube was now broken and nobody could play with it anymore. And that was the sad end of the big red tube.

Once upon a time there was a nosy doctor. He liked to pass by people's homes. One day, he passed by an old lady's house and peeked in the window. Inside he saw a little girl playing with her toys. She looked very happy. The doctor waved to the little girl and she came to the window. The nosy doctor asked her name and she told him "Mary". He then asked her if she wanted to go for a walk. She smiled and said yes! The nosy doctor and Mary went for a walk in the park. They saw many beautiful flowers and played a game of tag. They laughed and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, the nosy doctor thanked Mary for her company. He said goodbye and passed by the old lady's house one more time. Mary waved goodbye to the doctor as he walked away. The nosy doctor smiled and waved back. He knew that Mary would be okay.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tap in the middle of the park. Tim was very happy to see the tap. He loved playing with water. His mom let him play near the tap. Tim was a little frightened to complete turning the tap at first. He was scared the water might splash on him. But he was brave and turned the tap slowly. When the water came out, he was so excited! He played and played, splashing water all around. Then, something unexpected happened. A big frog jumped out of the tap! Tim was very surprised. He thought the frog was a toy. But the frog was real and friendly. Tim and the frog became friends and played together in the water. Tim was not frightened anymore, and he had a great day at the park.
