Title: Molly and the Radio

Molly wanted a radio. She asked her mom, but her mom said no. Molly was sad. She wanted to try and find a radio all on her own. Molly searched the house, but she couldn't find a radio. She even looked outside, but still no radio. Molly decided to go to the store. She walked until she got to the store, and found a large radio. She didn't have enough money to buy it. Molly's mom was walking by and saw Molly in the store. She asked Molly what she was doing, and Molly told her all about the radio. Molly's mom said they could try and find a cheaper radio somewhere else. Molly smiled and they went looking for a cheaper radio. They found one and it was just right! Even better, Molly's mom bought it for her. Molly hugged her mom, so happy that she had her very own radio.

One day, a little girl named Mia saw a different stair. This stair was not like the others. It was very colorful. Mia wanted to go up the stair, but she was scared. She stood at the bottom and looked up. Her friend, Tom, came over and saw Mia looking at the stair. "Mia, why are you not going up the stair?" he asked. Mia looked at Tom and said, "I want to, but it is different and I am scared." Tom held Mia's hand and said, "Don't cry, Mia. I will go with you." They went up the colorful stair together. At the top, they found a big, fun slide. They laughed and played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot liked to relax in the sun. One day, he saw a napkin fly by. Spot thought it was fun to chase the napkin. So, he ran after it. Spot chased the napkin all day. He was very tired. He wanted to relax, but he knew he had to catch the napkin first. Spot saw the napkin go into a big tree. He knew he had to be helpful and get it down. Spot jumped and jumped. Finally, he caught the napkin in his mouth. He was very happy. Spot went back to his spot in the sun and laid down. He was so tired from chasing the napkin, he fell asleep. Spot had a nice, long nap, and he dreamed of more helpful adventures.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a hedge. The hedge was home to many animals. They all played and shared the hedge together. One day, a new animal came to the garden. His name was Max, and he was a big, selfish cat. Max wanted to fit in the hedge, but he was too big. He tried to push the others out of the way. The animals were not happy. They did not like Max being selfish. They wanted to be friends, but Max only thought about himself. One day, a little bird named Tim spoke to Max. Tim said, "Max, if you want to fit in the hedge and be our friend, you must not be selfish. We all share the hedge." Max listened to Tim and decided to try to be a better friend. He stopped being selfish and started to share the hedge with the other animals. Soon, Max found a way to fit in the hedge without pushing the others. The animals were happy to have Max as their friend. They all played and shared the hedge together, and Max learned that it was more fun to be kind and share with his new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bird named Bella. Bella loved to hop and sing. She lived in a nest high up in a big tree. One day, Bella saw a little bunny hopping on the ground. Bella wanted to play, so she hopped down from her nest. The bunny was happy to see Bella and they hopped together. After a fun day of hopping and playing, Bella hopped back up to her pretty nest. She was tired and happy. As she sat in her nest, she sang a sweet song for her new friend, the bunny.
