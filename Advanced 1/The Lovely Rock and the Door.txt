Title: The Lovely Rock and the Door

Once upon a time there was a lovely rock. It was very special, so special that it had to be delivered all the way to the big house at the end of the street. Mum said, "This rock is so lovely that I'm sure the people in the big house will love it! I'll ask my friend Tony to deliver it." But Tony was scared of the big house. He said, "No way, I'm not delivering that rock!" Mum said, "Please Tony, it's very important. I'll buy you a lolly if you do it." Tony thought for a moment and then said, "Ok, I'll do it." So Tony carried the rock to the big house. He knocked on the door and a friendly face answered. Tony said, "Mum sent this lovely rock for you!" The face smiled and said, "Oh, thank you! I've been waiting for this rock for ages. I'm so glad you were brave enough to deliver it." Tony smiled and ran off with his lolly. He was glad he had found the courage to deliver the rock. He was rewarded with a big bunch of lovely flowers too!

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and find rocks. Lily found a big rock that was shiny and smooth. She was very happy and showed it to Ben. "Wow, that is a nice rock!" Ben said. "Can I see it?" Lily nodded and gave him the rock. But Ben was not careful. He dropped the rock on the ground and it broke into two pieces. Lily gasped and felt sad. Her rock was delicate and now it was ruined. "Sorry, Lily, I did not mean to break your rock!" Ben said. "Please forgive me!" Lily looked at Ben and saw that he was sorry. She knew that he was her friend and he did not want to hurt her. She decided to forgive him. "It's okay, Ben, I forgive you," Lily said. "But next time, be more gentle with my rocks." Ben smiled and hugged Lily. "Thank you, Lily, you are a good friend. Maybe we can find another rock together." They put the broken pieces of the rock in a box and went back to digging. They had fun and forgot about the accident. They learned to be more careful and kind with each other's things.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot liked to chew on things. He found a big, hard stick and started to chew on it. His friend, Sam the cat, saw him and said, "Be careful, Spot! That stick looks very hard." Spot did not listen to Sam. He kept chewing on the hard stick. Suddenly, his tooth broke! Spot cried and Sam said, "I told you to be careful. Now we need to go to the hospital." At the hospital, the doctor looked at Spot's tooth. The doctor said, "You are lucky, Spot. Your tooth will grow back." Spot and Sam were happy. They went home and Spot promised to be more careful when he chews on things. The next day, Spot found a soft toy and chewed on it instead of a hard stick.

Once upon a time there was a brother. He was walking in the forest and feeling very nervous. Suddenly he heard a loud noise. He stopped and listened. His heart was beating faster and faster. Then he saw something. It was a big bear! The bear looked very angry. The bear opened his mouth and roared. The brother was so scared that he froze. The bear stepped closer and closer. He looked very angry. Then the brother heard a voice behind him. It was his younger brother. His brother said "run, run!" But it was too late. The bear caught the brother and froze him with his icy breath. The younger brother ran away as fast as he could. He was so scared and sad. He wanted to help his brother, but he couldn't. The bad ending was that the big bear kept the brother frozen in the forest. The younger brother never saw his older brother again...

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo. The hippo lived in a beautiful lake with many fish. The sun was always shining, and the hippo was very happy. One day, the sky turned dark, and it started to get cold. The hippo felt a shiver go through his body. He had never felt cold before, and he didn't like it. The hippo decided to find a warm place to stay. He swam and swam until he found a sunny spot in the lake. The sun warmed him up, and he stopped shivering. The hippo was happy again, and he played with his fish friends all day long.
