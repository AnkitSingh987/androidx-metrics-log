Title: Lily the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily was impatient to play with her friends. She saw her friend Tom playing with a big ball. Lily ran to Tom and said, "Can I play too?" Tom said, "Yes, you can play with me. But first, we need to remove the dirt from the ball. The ball has a funny texture now." So they took the ball to the water and washed it. The dirt went away and the ball was clean again. Now, Lily and Tom played with the ball. They kicked it and threw it. They laughed and had fun. The sun was shining, and they played until it was time to go home. They said goodbye and went home with their moms.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bird. The bird had many colors. It liked to sing and play. One day, it saw a cane on the ground. The cane was long and brown. The bird wanted to find out who the cane belonged to. The bird began to search. It looked in the trees and on the ground. It asked its friends, "Do you know who lost this cane?" But no one knew. The bird kept searching, hoping to find the owner. At last, the bird found an old man. The old man was sad. He had lost his cane. The bird gave the cane back to the old man. The old man was happy. He thanked the beautiful bird. The bird felt good for helping the old man.

Once upon a time, there was a big mill near a small town. The mill was very old and dangerous. No one was allowed to go there. People said, "Stay away from the mill!" One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw the mill and wanted to look inside. He did not remember that it was dangerous. He walked closer and closer to the mill. Just then, his friend Sally came running. She yelled, "Tim, remember! The mill is dangerous!" Tim stopped and looked at her. He said, "Thank you, Sally. I forgot." They both walked back to the town, safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the color purple. She had a purple dress, purple shoes, and even a purple hat. One day, Sue went to the park to play. At the park, Sue saw a big purple box. She was very happy and wanted to add her name to the box. Sue found a pen and wrote her name on the purple box. Then, something unexpected happened. When Sue added her name to the box, it started to shake and wiggle. Suddenly, the box opened, and out came a friendly purple dog! The dog could talk, and it said, "Thank you, Sue, for adding your name to my box. Now, we can be best friends forever!" Sue was so surprised but very happy to have a new purple friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. A small bird lived in the tree. The bird was very obedient. Every day, the bird would sing with its sweet voice. One day, the bird saw a man leaning on the tree. The bird thought the man was a friend. The bird sang a song to the man. The man smiled and listened. But then, the man did something unexpected. He started to climb the tree! The bird was scared. The man reached for the bird's nest. The bird sang louder, and a big dog came running. The dog barked at the man, and the man ran away. The bird and the dog became best friends.

Once, there was a fancy church in a small village. The church was so fancy that all the villagers admired it. One day, a little girl called Clara ran past the church. She was a small 3 year old and she always liked to run around, exploring the village. On this day, she was running unusually fast and she crashed into the gate of the church! She was startled by the loud sound, but she didn't know that she had done something wrong and kept running. Soon, the Pastor of the church stepped out to find out what the noise was! When he noticed Clara running, he called to her, "Clara! You mustn't run around the church like that! It's disrespectful and you could hurt yourself!" Clara stopped running, ashamed that she had made a mistake. She apologized to the Pastor and promised to be careful around the church. The Pastor smiled at her, knowing that this was a lesson she would never forget. He told her, "Now you know, it's always best to be respectful wherever you go. And if you're ever running somewhere, always pay attention to where you are going!" Clara thanked him and ran off, sure to remember the lesson she had learned. From that day forward, she made sure to be cautious and respectful around churches and all other places she visited.
