Title: John and the Parents

John was a 3 year old boy. He was very anxious about when his parents would buy him a new toy. Every Saturday he begged them to take him to the store. His parents always said no and explained that they didn't have any money. On a cloudy Saturday morning, John's parents woke up with a surprise. They told John that he could go to the store with them and pick out a toy. He was so excited! He hopped out of bed and raced to the store. When they got to the store, John was overwhelmed. He looked around but couldn't decide which toy he wanted most. He became so anxious that he started to cry. His parents explained that they didn't have enough money to buy all of the toys he wanted. John was crushed and all the joy he had earlier that morning, quickly faded away. He had been looking forward to the toy, but now he was filled with disappointment and despair. Although John was sad that day, he learned an important lesson. Money doesn't always buy happiness.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named True. True had a toy that she loved very much. It was a soft, unknown animal that she carried with her everywhere she went. She loved to play with it and hug it when she was sad. One day, True's friend, Sam, came to play at her house. Sam saw the unknown toy and wanted to play with it too. True thought about it and decided to borrow the toy to Sam for a little while. Sam was very happy and played with the toy all day long. When it was time for Sam to go home, he gave the toy back to True. True was glad that she had shared her toy with her friend. She learned that it was good to share and borrow things with others. The moral of the story is that sharing can make people happy and bring friends closer together.

Once upon a time, there was a little car. The car loved to drive fast. One day, it saw a big missile. The missile was not nice. The car was scared. The little car had a friend. The friend was a tasty cake. The cake told the car, "Don't worry, I will help you." The car felt better. They thought of a plan to stop the missile. The cake went to the missile. The missile saw the tasty cake and got hungry. It ate the cake and was happy. It did not want to be mean anymore. The car was not scared, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a birdcage with a bird named Bob. Tim loved his bird and they played together every day. One day, Tim went to a fun game with his friends. They played a game with a ball. Tim and his friends were competitive. They all wanted to win. While Tim was away, something unexpected happened at home. When Tim came back, he saw the birdcage was open. Bob was gone! Tim was sad and looked everywhere for Bob. Then, he saw that the birdcage had a secret door. Behind the door, there was another bird! It was Bob's friend, Sue. Now, Tim had two birds and they all played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a small pond. In the pond lived a happy frog named Fred. Fred was an honest frog who always told the truth. One day, Fred met a little bird named Betty. Betty asked Fred, "Do you like to play?" Fred said, "Yes, I like to play. I like to jump and swim in the pond." Betty said, "I like to fly and sing. Let's play together!" Fred and Betty played together all day. They were so happy. They would repeat their games over and over again. At the end of the day, they sat by the pond and talked. They promised to always be honest and kind to each other. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little kid named Tim. Tim was very good at making plans. One day, Tim saw a small bird. The bird was helpless. It could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird. Tim made a plan to help the bird. He asked his mom for a box. He put the bird in the box. Tim gave the bird some food and water. The bird was happy. After a few days, the bird felt better. Tim took the bird outside. The bird was able to fly again. Tim was happy that his plan worked. The bird was not helpless anymore. And the bird and Tim became good friends.
