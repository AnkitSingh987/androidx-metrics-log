Title: The Restaurant and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big restaurant. Many people came to eat there. The food was very good, and the people who ate there became rich. One day, a man named Tom went to the restaurant. He wanted to be rich too. He ate and ate, but he did not become rich. Tom was sad. He thought, "Why am I not rich like the others?" Then, Tom saw a little bird outside the restaurant. The bird was hungry. Tom decided to send some food to the bird. When the bird ate the food, something unexpected happened. The bird became big and shiny! The bird was now rich! Tom was surprised. He learned that sharing with others can make them rich too. From that day on, Tom shared his food with everyone, and they all became rich together.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little girl named Amy. Amy loved the color pink. She had a pink dress, pink shoes, and a pink hat. She loved to explore and learn new things. One day, Amy found a big pink bag with a zip. She was very excited. She opened the zip and found many fun toys inside. There were dolls, cars, and balls. Amy played with the toys all day long. At the end of the day, Amy put all the toys back into the big pink bag. She zipped it up and went to show her mom. Her mom was happy to see Amy's new toys. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Mia. Mia loved to keep her toys in a big box. She had many toys, but her favorite toy was a ball with the number zero on it. Mia played with the zero ball every day. One day, Mia's zero ball rolled away and went under her bed. She looked and looked, but she could not find it. Mia was very sad. She asked her mom for help. Her mom was also very clever and knew just what to do. Together, Mia and her mom moved the bed and found the zero ball. Mia was so happy! She gave her mom a big hug and promised to keep her toys in the big box. From that day on, Mia always kept her toys in the big box and never lost her zero ball again.

Once there was a little girl who dreamed of being a dancer when she grew up. Every day she would imagine what it would feel like to dance on the stage. One day, the little girl was playing in her garden when she heard some music from the windows of a nearby house. She stopped and listened. It was so beautiful that she had to dance! The little girl tapped her way to the house and when she reached the window, she saw that a busy dancer was inside practicing. She watched in awe and wished she could also be a dancer like them. Suddenly, a woman came out of the house and shouted at the little girl. She said that she must never come back. The little girl was so scared that she ran away. From then on, the little girl stopped dreaming of being a dancer. She stopped imagining she could dance on the stage like the busy dancer in the window.

One day, a lazy dog named Spot was lying in the sun. He saw a big gate next to his house. Spot had never seen the gate open before. He wondered what was on the other side. A little bird flew by and saw Spot. The bird said, "Hey, Spot! I saw the gate open this morning. There's a big park on the other side!" Spot got excited and wanted to see the park. He asked the bird to show him the way. The bird flew up and pointed with its wing. "Follow me, Spot!" it said. They went to the gate, and it was open! Spot ran through the gate and saw the big park. He played all day and had lots of fun. In the end, Spot was happy he wasn't lazy anymore.
