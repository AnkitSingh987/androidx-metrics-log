Title: Mommy and Baby and the Toys

One day, Mommy and Baby went to the store to supply toys. Baby saw all the colorful, fun toys and asked for one. Mommy said, “First, you have to be obedient. Then you can have one.” Baby was so excited! He started to behave very well, making sure to listen to Mommy. Then they went to the toy aisle and he chose a shiny yellow car. Before they left the store, Mommy promised Baby, “If you’re a good boy and obey me, you can have a surprise when we get home.” Baby was very obedient, as they went all the way home. When they arrived, Mommy said, “You were so obedient! Here’s your surprise!” With a big smile, she held out another toy for Baby - an even bigger, green car. Baby was so happy to have two toys! He hugged Mommy, thanked her, and ran off to play.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play in the park with his friends. One day, Spot saw a sandwich on the ground. It looked yummy, so he ate it very fast. After eating the sandwich, Spot's tummy hurt. He didn't feel good at all. Spot's friend, a kind bird, saw him feeling sad. The bird asked, "Why are you sad, Spot?" Spot said, "I ate a sandwich and now my tummy hurts." The bird knew that Spot was feeling regret for eating the sandwich. The bird told Spot, "Next time, don't eat food you find on the ground." Spot said, "You're right, I won't do it again." The next day, Spot felt better and played with his friends in the park. He learned his lesson and never ate a sandwich from the ground again.

Once upon a time, there was a big machine. The machine was very stubborn. It did not like to do what it was told. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted the machine to dance. He asked the machine nicely, but the machine said no. Tim was sad but he did not give up. He tried to make the machine dance by singing a fun song. The machine started to move a little, but it still did not dance. Tim felt bad, but he still wanted the machine to dance. Finally, Tim thought of a plan. He would push the machine to make it dance. But the machine was too big and too stubborn. It did not move when Tim pushed it. Instead, the machine fell on top of Tim's toys and broke them. Tim was very sad because his toys were gone. The stubborn machine never danced and Tim learned to be careful with big things.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Bob. Bob was not like other fish. He was a very fancy fish. He had a big, pretty tail that was blue and green. All the other fish liked to look at Bob's fancy tail. One day, Bob wanted to dive deep in the water. He wanted to see what was at the bottom. As he swam down, he saw a network of caves. The caves were dark and scary, but Bob was brave. He wanted to explore the caves and see what was inside. Bob swam into the network of caves. He found many new friends there. There were little fish, big fish, and even a crab. They all played together and had fun. Bob was happy he had dived deep into the water and found the network of caves. Now, he had so many new friends and a fun place to play.

One day, a cat and a dog went to a food place. The cat was very loud. The dog said, "Please be quiet, cat!" The cat said, "I am sorry, dog." They looked at the menu to pick food. The cat wanted fish and the dog wanted a bone. They were happy with their food. The cat said, "Yum, I love fish!" The dog said, "Yum, I love bones!" After they ate, they played a game. The game was to squeeze a toy. The cat and the dog took turns to squeeze the toy. They had a lot of fun. The cat was not loud anymore. They were good friends.
