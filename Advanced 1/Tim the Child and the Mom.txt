Title: Tim the Child and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a child named Tim. Tim loved to swim. One day, Tim went to the big blue sea with his mom. "Mom, can I swim now?" asked Tim. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. But stay close to me." Tim jumped into the water. It was soft and cool. He swam and swam, laughing and splashing. Tim's mom swam with him, and they had so much fun. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim and his mom left the sea, happy and tired. They knew they would come back to swim again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl called Clara. She had shiny brown hair and was three years old. One morning, Clara wanted to go and play outside. She said to her mummy, "Can I run?" Mummy smiled and said, "Yes, of course!" Clara ran and ran in the garden. She ran around the flowers and chased the birds. Then she ran to the big tree at the end of the garden. Under the tree was a big, fluffy bunny. Clara said, "Hello bunny!" The bunny looked up and said, "Hello!" Clara was so happy. She gave the bunny a big hug. Then she ran back to her mum and said, "Mummy, look! I found a bunny!" Mummy said, "That's great, Clara! He looks very fine." Clara smiled. She hugged the bunny again, then ran off to play with her new friend.

One day, a boy named Tim went to get a haircut. He was very excited because he wanted a creative haircut. Tim told the hair cutter, "I want a fun haircut, please." The hair cutter smiled and said, "I have a great idea for you!" While the hair cutter was giving Tim his haircut, he started to rub Tim's head. Tim felt happy and relaxed. The hair cutter was having fun too. They both talked and laughed as the hair cutter worked on Tim's hair. When the haircut was done, the hair cutter showed Tim his new haircut in the mirror. Tim was very surprised! Instead of a creative haircut, he had a big smiley face shaved into his hair. The hair cutter said, "Now you have the happiest haircut in town!" Tim laughed and said, "Thank you! I love my new haircut!"

Once upon a time, there was a lazy gorilla. He was tired of life in the jungle, so he decided he wanted to find a helper. One day he saw a 3-year-old child walking by and he asked for help. The little girl said yes to the gorilla and she came along to support him. The gorilla was very happy to have a helper and he showed the little girl what he needed help with. He asked her to bring food to him and he gave her banana treats as a thank you. She also helped him wash his fur and the gorilla was so thankful for her support. The 3-year-old girl and the lazy gorilla were best friends and the girl helped him with all of his needs. She was always determined to do her best to support her away friend and the gorilla appreciated it. The lazy gorilla and the 3-year-old girl were a true example of friendship and they always had each other's backs. They were always there to offer support to one another and they will remain friends forever.

One day, a little bird wanted to fly. The bird was blue and loved to fly high in the sky. It was an icy cold day, but the bird did not mind. It put on a warm hat and started to fly up, up, up. As the bird flew, it saw a house. Inside the house, there was a girl eating toast. The girl saw the bird and opened the window. "Hi, bird! Do you want some toast?" she asked. The bird was hungry and wanted to eat. The bird flew into the house and ate the toast. The girl and the bird talked and laughed. They became good friends. After eating, the bird said, "Thank you for the toast! I must fly now." The girl waved goodbye as the bird flew back into the icy sky.
