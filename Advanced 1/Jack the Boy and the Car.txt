Title: Jack the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack who loved to play. One day, Jack saw a big, shiny car driving by and he thought it looked so nice! He wished he had a car like that one. Jack's mom saw him staring at the car, so she said, "If you ever want a car, you have to pay for it." Jack smiled and said, "Yes ma'am!" A few days later, Jack was playing in the neighborhood and he decided to take his toy car for a spin. Suddenly, two police officers drove up and stopped him. They told Jack he was driving too fast and that he had to pay a fine. Jack was very sad, but he remembered what his mom said. He had to pay for a car if he wanted one, so he nodded and said, "Yes sir!" The police officers were very kind and they let him go. Jack was so happy to be free! When he got home, Jack asked his mom if he could have some money. She smiled and said, "Sure! Now you know what it means to pay for something, so you can start saving for that car you wanted." Once upon a time, there was a small, heavy rock. The rock lived in a big park. The rock loved to stay in the park and watch kids play. The rock hoped to be a part of their games one day. One day, a big storm came. The wind blew hard, and the heavy rock started to roll. The rock rolled and rolled, far away from the park. The rock was sad and hoped to go back to the park. Then, something unexpected happened. A kind boy found the heavy rock and picked it up. The boy took the rock back to the park. The rock was so happy to be back! The boy used the rock in his games, and the rock's hope came true. From that day on, the rock and the boy were the best of friends.

One day, a little duck named Dizzy went to a restaurant. Dizzy was very hungry. He wanted to eat some yummy food. At the restaurant, he saw a big bowl of soup. He thought it looked very good. Dizzy jumped into the soup to bathe. He splashed and played in the soup. It was so much fun! But soon, Dizzy started to feel dizzy. He did not know why. Dizzy got out of the soup and went home. His mom told him that he should not play in the soup at the restaurant. She gave him a nice bath in clean water. Dizzy felt much better. He learned not to play in the soup again.

Once upon a time, there lived a little dog named Boomer. Boomer was a very happy pup, but one day he noticed that the sky had gone gray and gloomy. Everywhere he looked he could see the sun hid behind clouds. Suddenly, Boomer noticed a man walking towards him. The man had a big mustache and he wore a hat. He leaned in close, looking down at Boomer and said, "What's the matter? You look lonely." Boomer whimpered and said, "The sky is so gloomy and it's making me sad." The man smiled and said, "I know how to make you feel better. We're going to have a picnic and I can show you some of my favorite games. Then we'll take a nap in the grass and I'll tell you stories." Boomer's eyes lit up and he jumped up, excited to play. Soon, they had a wonderful picnic and Boomer was feeling much better. He had lots of fun and the man even let him play with his mustache. They said goodbye to each other and Boomer wagged his tail, feeling much brighter than he had before. The sky still looked gloomy but Boomer was happy. He had made a new friend who was full of fun and they had shared a lovely day together.
