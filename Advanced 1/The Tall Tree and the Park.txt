Title: The Tall Tree and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. The tree had a swing on it. Many kids liked to play on the swing. They would laugh and have fun. One day, a little boy named Tom came to the park. Tom wanted to play on the swing, but he was too short. He tried and tried, but he could not reach the swing. Tom was sad. Then, a kind girl named Sue saw Tom. Sue wanted to help Tom have fun on the swing. She lifted Tom up, and he could reach the swing. Tom and Sue played on the swing together. They laughed and had a great time.

Once upon a time there was a purple dragon. It lay in the forest, sleeping. Its scaly skin was so impressive that birds avoided flying over it. Every day, a curious mouse would come and watch it. He loved seeing the dragon's style when it slept. Months went by until one day, the mouse's patience paid off. The dragon woke and stretched its wings. The mouse was amazed by the impressive sight- the dragon was so huge! It flew off into the sky and the mouse smiled, happy that his friendship with the dragon had finally started.

Once upon a time there was a hairy dog named Rover. Rover was always searching for something exciting and one day he heard about a cartoon show that was starting up. He was so excited! He searched everywhere for the cartoon show but it was nowhere to be found. One day he decided to search in the forest. He brought a friend with him and they set off together on an adventure. As they were walking through the forest, Rover found a cave and inside the cave, he saw a TV playing the cartoon show! He was so happy! He watched the show with his friend until it was finished. From then on, Rover and his friend would go searching for the cartoon show every weekend. They never found it, but it was still fun to go searching and explore.

Once upon a time, there was a big sun and a small snowman. The sun was very competitive. He wanted to be the best at everything. The snowman was made of snow and had a cut on his face. The cut was made by a little boy who was playing with him. One day, the sun said to the snowman, "I bet I can make you melt!" The snowman replied, "Please don't! I want to stay with my friend, the little boy." The sun felt bad for the snowman and decided not to melt him. Instead, the sun and the snowman became friends. They played games together and had lots of fun. The little boy was happy that his snowman friend did not melt, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim loved to paint. He painted all day long. One day, he wanted to paint the street. He thought it would be fun. So, he took his paint and brush and went outside. Tim started to paint the street with big, bright colors. He painted red, blue, and yellow. It was a very pretty street now. But, Tim did not see the car coming. The car driver was very sorry. He could not stop in time. The car went through the paint on the street. The pretty colors were all mixed up. Tim was sad. The street was not pretty anymore. The car driver said sorry, but the street stayed messy. And that is the bad ending of the story.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy he loved very much. It was a yellow car. Tom would play with his car every day. One day, Tom saw his dad using a razor to cut his beard. Tom wanted to be like his dad. He thought, "I will use the razor to cut my yellow car's beard too!" But Tom's mom said, "No, Tom! The razor is not for you. It can hurt you." Tom was sad. He wanted to be like his dad and admire the razor. Tom's mom saw him sad and said, "I have an idea! Let's make a pretend razor for you." Tom and his mom made a pretend razor out of paper. Tom was happy. He could now be like his dad and play with his yellow car without getting hurt.
