Title: The Story of the Car and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a very sorry police officer. He drove his police car every day and kept everyone safe. One day, he drove his car too fast and got in an accident. His car was broken, and he was so sad. He called a nice man to come and help. The man looked at the car very carefully. He fixed the wheels and the door, but he could not fix the broken windows. He said, "I'm sorry, but I cannot restore your car this time." The police officer was very sad, but he thanked the man for trying. He called for another nice man to come and help. When the second man came, he was sure he could fix the car. He fixed the wheels and the door again, and he also restored the windows. The police officer was very happy! He thanked both men for their hard work and invited them to come back and help him again if needed. He was so relieved that he could keep driving his car again!

Once upon a time, there was a strong duck. He lived near a big pond. All the other ducks liked to play with him. One day, the strong duck saw something glow in the pond. "Look!" said the strong duck to his friends. "I see something glow. Let's go see!" They all swam to the glow. It was a magic stone! The stone made the duck who held it glow too. The strong duck picked it up and he started to glow. The strong duck and his friends played with the magic stone. They took turns making each other glow. But then, a big wind came and blew the stone away. The ducks were sad, but the strong duck had an idea. "Let's go find the magic stone!" said the strong duck. They all looked for the stone, but they could not find it. Then, they saw a little fish glowing in the water. The fish had found the stone and was now glowing too! The little fish was happy to share the magic stone with the ducks. Now, they all played together and took turns glowing. The strong duck, his friends, and the little fish had lots of fun with their new glowing game.

One day, a powerful king lived in a big castle. He had a special room called a chamber. The king loved his chamber and spent a lot of time there. He had a little boy who liked to play and laugh. One day, the little boy asked the king, "Daddy, can I go into the chamber?" The king said, "No, my child. The chamber is not for play. Stay away from it." The little boy was sad but listened to his daddy. The next day, the little boy saw the door to the chamber open. He thought, "Maybe I can take a quick look." He went inside the chamber. Suddenly, the door closed and locked. The little boy was scared and started to weep. The king heard the cries and ran to the chamber. He tried to open the door, but it was locked. The king wept too. He said, "I told you not to go in there, my child." The little boy cried louder. The king could not open the door. The little boy stayed in the chamber and cried for a long time. The king was very sad. He wished he had listened to his daddy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw the sunrise and it was very pretty. Tim felt proud because he woke up early. He saw his friend, Sally, and asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Sally said, "Yes, let's choose a fun game!" They thought and thought, and finally chose to play hide and seek. They played and laughed until the sun went down. Tim and Sally were very happy and proud of their fun day together. They said "bye" and went home to rest for another day of play.

One day, a little girl saw something very curious. On the ground, she saw a little red stamp. She had never seen this type of stamp before and wanted to find out what it was. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. Her mom was very happy and told her that the stamp was clear. She said the stamp meant that she had chosen to ignore it. This made the little girl laugh because she had no idea how to ignore something. Her mom explained that sometimes it is better to ignore things rather than to take notice of them. The little girl found this very interesting. She went around all day trying to ignore things she came across - friends calling her name, her brother trying to get her attention. She even put the little stamp in her pocket as a reminder to ignore when she needed to. This made the little girl so happy. She had learned something new, and it was something that she could do to make her day more peaceful!
