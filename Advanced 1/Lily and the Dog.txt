Title: Lily and the Dog

Lily liked to follow her big brother Sam wherever he went. She wanted to play with him and his friends, but Sam did not like that. He said she was too little and slow. He told her to go away and leave him alone. One day, Sam had a plan. He wrote a note and gave it to Lily. He said it was a treasure map and she had to follow it. Lily was happy and excited. She took the note and ran to the backyard. She looked at the note. It had a picture of a tree, a fence, a dog and a big X. Lily followed the note. She found the tree and the fence. She saw the dog. It was not a nice dog. It was a big, angry dog. It barked and growled at Lily. Lily was scared and troubled. She wanted to go back to Sam, but the dog was in the way. She did not know what to do. She started to cry. She called for Sam. She hoped he would hear her and help her. But Sam did not hear her. He was busy playing with his friends. He had forgotten about Lily and the note. He had tricked her and left her alone. Lily cried louder and louder. She wished she had not followed the note. She wished she had stayed with Mom. She wished Sam was a nicer brother. She wished the dog would go away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He wanted to learn how to make a sandwich. Tim asked his mom, "Can you teach me how to make a sandwich?" Tim's mom said, "Yes, I can help you." She showed him how to open the bread bag and take out two pieces of bread. Then, she taught Tim how to put jam and peanut butter on the bread. Tim was so happy to learn this. One day, Tim's friend came over to play. His friend was hungry too. Tim said, "I can make us sandwiches!" He made two sandwiches, one for him and one for his friend. They both ate and were not hungry anymore. Tim's friend said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim felt very happy and proud that he could help his friend. The moral of the story is that learning new things can help you and others.

Once upon a time, there was a busy little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his red motorcycle toy. He would ride it up and down the hills in his backyard, making "vroom vroom" sounds as he went. Tim's motorcycle was his favorite toy, and he took it with him everywhere he went. One sunny day, Tim's mom told him they were going to the park. Tim was so excited! He put on his shoes, grabbed his red motorcycle, and ran to the car. When they got to the park, Tim saw a big, open field. He knew it was the perfect place to play with his motorcycle. Tim played with his motorcycle all day, racing it through the grass and pretending it could fly. He was so busy having fun that he didn't notice the sun was starting to set. When Tim's mom called him to go home, he picked up his motorcycle and waved goodbye to the park. Tim couldn't wait to come back and play with his motorcycle again.

Once upon a time, in a quiet land, there was a big volcano. It was very tall and red. Many animals lived near the volcano. They were happy and played all day. One day, a wise old owl said, "We must include everyone in our games. The volcano might get mad if we don't." The other animals listened and made sure to include everyone. One day, the volcano started to shake. The animals were scared. The wise owl said, "Let's all be quiet and see what happens." They all became quiet, and the volcano stopped shaking. The animals were happy and continued to play, always remembering to include everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to perform tricks for his friends. He would jump, roll, and dance around. All the other animals liked to watch Spot. Spot had a special style when he did his tricks. He would wag his tail and bark happily. This style made him very popular with his friends. They would clap and cheer for Spot when he did his tricks. One day, Spot performed his tricks at a big party. All the animals were there to see him. They clapped and cheered as Spot did his tricks in his popular style. Spot was very happy, and so were his friends. They all had a great time at the party.
