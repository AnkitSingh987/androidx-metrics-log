Title: Joe and the Sarah

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, a boy and a girl. Their names were Joe and Sarah. They both liked to play together and always made each other laugh. One day, Sarah asked Joe to marry her. Joe was so happy, he said yes right away. They were both so excited, they decided to throw a big party. Everyone was having a great time until a mysterious cage appeared. Nobody knew where it came from. Joe and Sarah were scared, but they were brave. They decided to open the cage and see what was inside. Inside, they found two birds. Joe and Sarah explained to the birds that they wanted to get married someday, and said that the birds could come with them if they wanted. And so, the birds flew around Joe and Sarah as they said ‘I do’. Everyone cheered and clapped. Joe and Sarah were now married. From that day on, the birds always stayed close by Joe and Sarah, remembering the special day when their new friends got married.

Once upon a time there was a brave bear. Every day he went to the opera to listen to beautiful music. One day he sat in the audience and watched the show. The singer was singing a beautiful song about the blooming season. Everyone in the audience clapped as the song ended. The brave bear was so impressed that he got up and said, "That was so amazing! I want to sing too! Can I take the stage?" The audience was surprised and the singer smiled. She said to the brave bear, "Yes, of course! I'd love to hear you sing." So the brave bear made his way to the stage and began to sing. But his singing was so full of joy and happiness that his singing made the entire audience bloom. Everyone cheered and clapped as the brave bear took his final bow. The opera has never seen such a brave bear before!

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Bella. Every night, she would match the flame of a candle and light up the room with its orangey glow. One night, she was sat by the candle when her mum came up to her. “What are you doing darling?” asked her mum. “I’m matching the flame,” Bella said. “Oh, that looks very cosy,” Mum said with a smile. In the corner of the room was a bowl of sour apples. “Would you like to try one?” asked Mum. “Yes, please” Bella said. Bella took one of the sour apples and ate it. “Mmm, it’s yummy!” she exclaimed. Mum smiled and said, “Glad you like it. Now, let’s make some more matches for the flame.” And so, the pair made extra matches and lit up the room with a cosy orangey glow.

Lily and Ben were playing in the yard. They liked to jump in the big pile of leaves that Dad had made with the rake. The rake was important. Dad said they had to put it away when they were done. "Look, Lily, I can make a leaf hat!" Ben said. He grabbed some leaves and put them on his head. He laughed and ran around. "That's silly, Ben!" Lily said. She grabbed some leaves too and made a leaf dress. She twirled and smiled. They had fun with the leaves until they heard Mom call them. "Kids, time for lunch!" Mom said. "Come inside and wash your hands!" "Okay, Mom!" Lily and Ben said. They ran to the door, but they forgot something. "Wait, Ben, we have to put the rake away!" Lily said. She remembered what Dad had said. "Oh, right!" Ben said. He ran back to the pile of leaves and grabbed the rake. He carried it to the shed and put it next to the shovel. "Good job, Ben!" Lily said. She gave him a hug. "The rake is important. We don't want to lose it or hurt someone." They went inside and had lunch. They told Mom and Dad about their leaf hats and dresses. Mom and Dad smiled and said they were very creative. Lily and Ben felt happy. They liked to play in the yard.
