Title: The Worm and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little worm. The worm was very nervous. It was his first day at a new school. He had a big bag to unpack. Inside the bag were his books, pencils, and lunch. The worm did not know if the other kids would like him. When he got to school, he went to his new classroom. The teacher said, "Hello, little worm! Please unpack your bag and find a seat." The worm was still nervous, but he did as the teacher said. He unpacked his bag and found a seat near the window. As the day went on, the worm made new friends. They played games, drew pictures, and ate lunch together. The worm was not nervous anymore. He was happy to be at his new school. And when he went home, he told his mom and dad all about his great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and have fun. One day, she found a big, soft pillow in her room. She was so happy and decided to play with the pillow. Sue played with the pillow all day long. She jumped on it, hugged it, and even used it as a chair. But one day, she found something disgusting on the pillow. Sue didn't know what it was, but it was yucky and made her feel sad. She didn't want to play with the pillow anymore. Sue told her mommy about the disgusting thing on the pillow. Her mommy cleaned the pillow and made it look nice again. Then, her mommy surprised Sue with a new pillowcase. It had her favorite colors and made the pillow look pretty. Sue was so happy and started to play with the pillow again. They all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play in the garden. One day, they saw a big melon growing on a vine. They both wanted to eat it, but they did not want to share. "I saw it first!" Tom said. "It is mine!" "No, it is mine!" Lily said. "I watered it every day!" They started to fight over the melon. They pulled and pushed and shouted. They did not see the cat sneaking behind them. The cat was hungry and liked melons too. He saw the twins fighting and had a plan. He waited for the right moment, then jumped on the melon and bit it. The melon was ripe and juicy, and it burst open with a loud pop. Tom and Lily stopped fighting and looked at the cat. They saw the melon was gone and only the skin was left. They felt sad and angry. "Bad cat!" Tom said. "You stole our melon!" "Go away!" Lily said. "You are not our friend!" The cat did not care. He licked his paws and smiled. He had eaten the whole melon and was full and happy. He walked away, leaving the twins with nothing. Tom and Lily looked at each other. They realized they had been silly and selfish. They wished they had shared the melon and played nicely. They felt sorry and hugged each other. "Next time, we will not fight," Tom said. "Next time, we will share," Lily said. They went back to the garden and looked for another melon. They hoped they would find one soon. They were still hungry and thirsty. They were also worried. They hoped the cat would not come back.

Once upon a time, there was a fish who loved to swim. He lived in a big lake with his fish friends. They were very organized and always played together during the beautiful sunset. One day, the fish saw a new place to swim. He wanted to explore it, but his friends said no. They were scared and wanted to stay in their safe lake. But the fish was curious and swam away. As the fish swam in the new place, the sun started to set. He got lost and couldn't find his way back to his friends. The fish was sad and scared. He missed his organized friends and their sunset swims. The fish was alone, and the ending was bad.
