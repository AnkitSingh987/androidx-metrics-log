Title: Jane the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there lived a girl named Jane. She was only three years old and had beautiful eyes and long, golden hair. One day, Jane was playing in the garden when she heard a beautiful singing voice. She looked up to the sky and saw a beautiful angel. She was in awe and said, "Hello angel!" The angel replied, "Hello Jane, it's so nice to meet you. Would you like to go for a walk with me?" Jane smiled and eagerly nodded. The angel flew down and took Jane's hand. Together they walked through the dry grass and past the trees in the garden. Suddenly, Jane stopped and said, "I cannot go any further, I must go back home!" The angel said,"I understand. Thank you for taking this special walk with me today. Take care of yourself and I will see you again soon." Jane waved goodbye and skipped back home, dreaming of angel visits.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Joe. He found a peanut on the ground and he believed it was extra special. He was very delicate with the peanut, holding it between his tiny fingers. He showed it to his mom and said, "Look mommy, I found a special peanut!" His mother smiled and replied, "That's wonderful, Joe! What do you think you could do with this special peanut?" Joe's eyes lit up as he said, "I think it will make a nice snack for me and my friends! So let's go share it." His mom smiled, said "Ok" and took Joe to the park to share the peanut with his friends. At the park, Joe said, "Let's all believe this peanut is magical! That way, no matter how much we divide it, there will always be enough for everyone." Everyone agreed and shared the delicate peanut. And when they were all through, Joe ran off to show his mom he was right. His mom smiled and said, "I believe it too, Joe. I believe it too!"

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big crane. The crane was dull and not very pretty. But the crane was helpful. It would lift heavy things for people in the town. One day, a little boy named Tom saw the crane. He did not understand why the crane was so dull. He thought it should be bright and happy. So, Tom had an idea. He wanted to make the crane pretty. Tom got paint in many colors. He painted the crane with red, blue, yellow, and green. The crane was not dull anymore. It was now bright and pretty. The people in the town were happy, and the crane felt special. The crane kept helping people, and everyone understood how important the crane was.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He liked to play in the woods. One day, he saw a big tree. He wanted to make it into a smooth toy. Tom went to his dad and said, "Dad, can I have an axe, please?" His dad gave him a small axe. Tom was happy to receive the axe. He went back to the woods to cut the tree. Tom worked hard with his axe. He cut the tree into a smooth toy. He showed it to his dad. His dad said, "Good job, Tom!" They both smiled and played with the new toy.

One day, a boy and a girl went for a jog in the park. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. They liked to run and play together. They thought it was fair to race each other. As they ran, they saw a big tree. They wanted to touch it and then run back. The boy was fast, but the girl was faster. They both laughed and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A little dog came out of the tree! The dog wanted to play too. The boy and the girl were surprised. They played with the dog and they all became friends. They did not end their jog, but they had more fun with their new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a race with his dad. They were very excited because they loved to watch the fast cars. The cars were very loud, and Tim liked to cover his ears when they went by. During the race, something strange happened. One of the cars disappeared! Everyone was surprised and didn't know where it went. Tim and his dad looked around, but they couldn't see the car anywhere. Then, they heard a loud noise behind them. The missing car was there! It had taken a wrong turn and was now back on the track. Tim and his dad laughed and cheered as the car joined the race again. They were happy that the car was found and could keep racing.
