Title: Tom the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with a big yard. Tom loved to play in the yard, but he was too shy to play with other animals. One day, Tom found a magic ball in the yard. When he touched the ball, he started to shrink! Tom became very small, like a tiny bug. Now, he could play in the yard without being seen by other animals. Tom had so much fun playing in the yard as a small cat. He made new friends with the bugs and birds. They all played together and had a great time. Tom was not shy anymore. He was happy to have friends and play in the yard every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. Lisa was very independent. She loved to do things by herself. One day, she decided to wear her favorite dress all by herself. Lisa went to her bedroom to find her dress. She looked around and saw it hanging on the wall. She reached up high and took it down. Then she put on her dress all by herself. She felt so proud and happy. Lisa went to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I wear my dress all by myself!" Mom smiled and said, "Good job, Lisa! You are so independent!" Lisa grinned and danced around the room, showing off her dress. From that day on, Lisa knew she could do anything she set her mind to.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who lived by the sea. He had a small yacht. The yacht was his best friend. He took care of it every day. One day, the old man heard a story. The story said that there was a magic island far away. People believe that the island had yummy food and fun toys. The old man wanted to go there with his yacht. The old man and his yacht went on a long trip. They saw fish, birds, and big waves. After many days, they found the magic island. They played with toys and ate yummy food. The old man and his yacht were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bird. The little bird liked to fly high in the sky. One day, the little bird saw a big, tall tree. The tree had a long neck. The little bird wanted to sit on the neck of the tree. As the little bird flew up to the tree, it did not see a big hole in the tree's neck. The bird was ignorant of the hole. When the little bird tried to sit on the tree's neck, it started to fall into the hole. The bird was scared. But, something unexpected happened. As the bird fell into the hole, it found a new home inside the tree. The little bird was happy and safe in its new home. The bird learned that sometimes, even when you fall, good things can happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little olive. The little olive lived on a big tree with many other olives. The tree was in a pretty garden with flowers and bees. One day, a small bird came to the tree. The bird said, "Hello, little olive! Can I be your friend?" The little olive was happy and said, "Yes, you can be my friend, little bird!" The little olive and the little bird played every day. One day, while they were playing, the little olive fell off the tree. The little bird was scared, but then, something unexpected happened. The little olive grew wings and started to fly! The little bird and the little olive were very happy and they flew and played together in the sky.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very nervous. It was his first night alone in his room. He was scared because it was dark and quiet. Tim called out to his mom, but she did not answer. Tim tried to sleep but he could not. He heard a strange sound outside his window. He was too scared to look, so he hid under his blanket. He called out for his mom again, but she still did not answer. Then, the strange sound came closer and closer. Tim started to cry because he was very scared. The sound was very loud now and it was inside his room. He wished his mom would come, but she never did. Tim was all alone with the scary sound.
