Title: Tim and Jen and the Bench

Tim and Jen like to play in the park. They like to slide, swing, and run. One day, they see something shiny under a bench. They run to the bench and kneel down. They find a lot of money in a bag. They do not know whose money it is. They look around and see no one. They think maybe someone forgot the money. They think maybe they can keep the money. They think of all the things they can buy with the money. They can buy candy, toys, and books. They are very happy. But then they see a man running to the bench. He looks sad and scared. He is looking for something. He sees the bag of money in Tim and Jen's hands. He says, "That's my money! I lost it! Please give it back to me!" Tim and Jen are surprised. They do not know the man. He is unknown to them. They do not want to give the money back. They like the money. But they also feel sorry for the man. He looks like he needs the money. They do not know what to do. They decide to be kind and honest. They give the money back to the man. The man is very happy and thankful. He says, "You are very good kids. You did the right thing. Here, take this as a reward." He gives them some candy and a toy. Tim and Jen are happy too. They learned a lesson. They learned that money is not everything. They learned that being kind and honest is better. They go back to play in the park. They share the candy and the toy. They have fun. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day long. One day, while playing in the park, Spot saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to dig a hole under the tree. As Spot started digging, he met a little worm named Wiggly. Wiggly said, "Hello, Spot! What are you doing?" Spot replied, "I am digging a hole to find water." Wiggly was excited and wanted to help Spot, so they dug together. After some time, they found water under the tree. They were both very happy. Spot received a drink from the water and shared it with Wiggly. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a small soldier. He lived in a tiny house near the big woods. The soldier loved to go for walks and look at all the trees and flowers. One sunny day, the soldier went for a walk. He noticed a big red apple on the ground. "Yummy!" he said, and picked it up to eat. He took a big bite of the apple and it was very sweet. As he walked, the soldier saw a little squirrel. The squirrel looked hungry. The soldier shared his yummy apple with the squirrel. They sat under a tree and ate the apple together. The soldier and the squirrel became good friends and played in the woods every day.

John and Susan were playing hockey in the park. John said, "Let's move around the field and see who can score more goals!" Susan was very fierce and went really fast on the skates. She was excited. John was moving quickly too, but he couldn't catch up to Susan. Then, suddenly, Susan tripped and fell. John said, "Are you okay?" Susan nodded her head and said, "Yes, I am okay now." John and Susan continued to play hockey and moved around the field. John was getting better and better at the game. He moved the hockey stick and suddenly hit the ball hard. The ball flew into the goal. Susan clapped and said, "Well done John! You were very fierce in that move!" John smiled, happy that Susan noticed his improved skills. They continued to play until they had to go home. They said goodbye and moved away, planning to meet up again soon to continue playing hockey.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food. Tim saw a big display with many boxes of cereal. It was very impressive. He wanted to pick one. Tim asked his mom, "Can I get a box of cereal, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can pick one." Tim was very happy. He looked at all the colorful boxes and picked the one with a fun picture on it. They went home, and the next morning, Tim ate his new cereal. It was very yummy. Tim and his mom were both happy with the impressive cereal display that they found at the store.
