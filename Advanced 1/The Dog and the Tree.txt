Title: The Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. His name was Max. Max was a perfect dog. He liked to play and run all day. One day, Max met a big cat. The cat said, "Hi, my name is Kitty. Do you want to play?" Max and Kitty played all day. They had lots of fun. But then, Max had to sneeze. "Achoo!" he sneezed. The sneeze was so big that it made Kitty fly up into a tree! Max was sad. He did not want to hurt his new friend. Kitty said, "Don't be sad, Max. I can jump down." And Kitty jumped down from the tree. They were both happy again. Max and Kitty played together every day after that.

Juan was walking in the city when he noticed something strange. He saw a man wearing a mask. Juan couldn't understand why the man was wearing this strange thing on his face. Suddenly, he was very scared. He began to feel helpless. Juan wanted to run away, but the man kept getting closer. He observed the man carefully, but he couldn't escape. The man grabbed him and took him away. He was too weak to resist. Juan felt so helpless. He wanted to get away, but he couldn't do it. He didn't know why the man grabbed him and put him in a dark room. The room was filled with sadness and pain. The man never took off his mask. Juan was never seen again. He was helpless and he could not escape.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Elly. Elly was very heavy. Every day, Elly would try to weigh herself on a big scale. But the scale was very expensive, and Elly was sad because she could not buy it. One sunny day, Elly went to the store with her friend, a little mouse named Minnie. Minnie was very small and light. They saw the expensive scale in the store. Minnie had an idea. She said, "Elly, let's wait for a minute and see if the price goes down." They waited for a minute, but the price did not go down. Elly was very sad. She wanted to know how much she weighed. Minnie tried to help Elly, but she was too small and could not lift the expensive scale. Elly decided to try one more time. She went to the store and tried to weigh herself on the expensive scale. But the scale broke because Elly was too heavy. Elly was very sad, and she had to pay for the broken scale. In the end, Elly still did not know how much she weighed, and she had no money left.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the shop with his mom. They wanted to buy some food for dinner. Tim was very excited because he loved going to the shop. While they were shopping, Tim saw an unknown toy that he had never seen before. It was big and colorful. He wanted to play with it, but his mom said, "We need to complete our shopping first, then you can play." Tim helped his mom complete the shopping quickly, and they went back to the toy. But when they got there, the toy was gone! Tim was sad because he wanted to play with it. But then, he saw his dad holding the toy with a big smile! Dad had bought the toy for Tim as a surprise. Tim was so happy and couldn't wait to play with his new toy at home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a small puddle on the ground. Tim was curious, so he decided to examine the puddle. He saw that water was coming out of a pipe. Tim thought, "Oh no, there is a leak!" Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a leak outside!" His mom was impatient, but she went to look at the leak with Tim. They both saw the water coming out of the pipe. Tim's mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will fix the leak." Tim's mom got a tool and fixed the pipe. The leak stopped, and the puddle went away. Tim was happy that the leak was fixed. He thanked his mom and went back to play outside.
