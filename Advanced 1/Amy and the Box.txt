Title: Amy and the Box

Once upon a time, a little girl named Amy had a tidy room. In her room, there was a big box. Amy loved to play with her toys and keep them in the box. One day, Amy's friend, Tom, came to visit. He saw the box and asked, "What's in the box, Amy?" Amy smiled and said, "I believe it is full of toys. Let's open it and see!" Tom and Amy opened the box and found many toys inside. They played with the toys all day. When it was time for Tom to go home, they put the toys back in the box. Amy's room was tidy again. Tom said, "Thank you for letting me play with your toys, Amy." Amy replied, "You're welcome, Tom. Come back soon!"

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to search for a magic power. She had heard about it from her friend, Tom. Tom said, "I heard this power can make you jump very high!" Lucy walked a long way to find the power. She looked under rocks, in trees, and behind bushes. She searched all day, but she could not find the magic power. As she was walking home, Lucy saw a big dog. The dog was angry and barked at her. Lucy was scared and did not know what to do. She wished she had the magic power to jump very high and escape the dog. Just then, Tom came running with a long stick. He waved the stick at the dog, and the dog ran away. Lucy thanked Tom for saving her. She said, "I did not find the magic power, but I am glad I have you as my friend." Tom smiled and said, "Maybe the real power is having good friends to help you when you need them." Lucy agreed, and they walked home together, happy to have each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to mix things up. One day, he found a big bowl and a big spoon. He thought it would be fun to mix all his toys together. Max's friend, a little cat named Sam, saw what he was doing. Sam wanted to teach Max how to mix things the right way. So, Sam showed Max how to mix colors with paint. They had a lot of fun mixing red, blue, and yellow to make new colors. Max was happy that Sam taught him how to mix colors. They painted a big picture together. Max and Sam were very proud of their big, colorful picture. They hung it up in their playroom for everyone to see. And from that day on, Max and Sam loved to mix and paint together.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big green forest with his friends. They liked to play games and have fun all day. One day, Bobo and his friends were playing hide and seek. Bobo hid behind a big tree. He saw something shiny appear near the tip of the tree. It was a pretty star! Bobo wanted to show his friends the star. Bobo called his friends to come and see the star. They all looked up at the shiny star at the tip of the tree. They were so happy to see it. They played under the star until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl called Tilly. Tilly was very special because she admired the spirit of the world around her. One day Tilly was out for a walk with her mum when she saw a beautiful lake. The lake was so big and wide that she couldn't help but admire it. "Mummy, look," Tilly said. "It's so wide and so beautiful, I just want to stare at it forever!" Mummy smiled and agreed. She was so proud of Tilly for having such admiration for the world around her. "That's true, the lake is wonderful, but look the other way, Tilly. Can you see that mountain?" Tilly looked and admired the mountain. It was so tall, it stretched up into the sky! It was even more beautiful than the lake. With admiration in her voice Tilly said, "Wow! It's so tall, I wish I could climb it one day!" Mummy smiled again. She was so pleased to see her little girl admiring the wonders of the world. With admiration of her own, she said, "What a great wish, Tilly. Maybe one day we will!" They continued to admire the wide world around them.
