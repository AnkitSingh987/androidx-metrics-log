Title: Rufus and the Car

Rufus and his mommy had been on a long journey. The traffic was heavy, but Rufus had been very enthusiastic as they passed through the cities and countryside. Eventually they arrived at their destination - grandma and grandpa's house. "Yay!" shouted Rufus. He hopped out of the car and gave grandpa a big hug. "You must be hungry," said grandma. She opened her baking basket and showed Rufus her freshly-made muffins. Rufus gasped. "Yummy! I love muffins." But then he noticed that the muffins were starting to rot. Grandma had been on the road too long and forgotten to eat them in time. "Don't worry," said grandpa. "We can make some more. I can show you how to do it." A few minutes later, Rufus and grandpa were in the kitchen. With enthusiasm, Rufus learnt all about how to make muffins. Soon the kitchen was full of delicious smells. Rufus smiled. "These muffins taste even better than grandma's!"

One day, a boy named Tim had a clear rod. He liked to play with it. He would run, jump, and do many fun things with the rod. Tim loved his clear rod so much. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue also had a clear rod. They both wanted to play with each other's rods. But, they did not want to share. They both wanted to keep their own clear rods. Tim and Sue did not know what to do. They both liked their clear rods so much. Then, Tim had an idea. They could play together with both rods. They both agreed to share, and they had lots of fun playing with their clear rods.

John and Lucy were playing in the park. Suddenly, they came across a large nut on the ground. John was very excited and began to pick it up. Lucy however, shrugged, and said there was probably nothing inside. John wouldn't take no for an answer, and began to tap the nut with a stick. After a few taps the nut split open, revealing something inside! John and Lucy gasped in amazement. Inside the nut was a large, shiny coin. They both jumped up and down with excitement. They split the coin equally and thought about what they would buy with their newfound treasure. But whatever they bought, they would never forget their adventure in the park.

One day, a cool cat named Sam was walking down the street. He saw a big bin with a lid. Sam was curious about what was inside the bin. He walked up to the bin and tried to open the lid. It was very heavy, but Sam was a strong cat. As Sam opened the bin, he heard a scream. "Help me!" said a little mouse named Tim. Tim was stuck inside the bin and couldn't get out. Sam looked at Tim and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." Sam reached in and pulled Tim out of the bin. After Sam saved Tim, they became good friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. Sam and Tim were very happy, and they never forgot the day they met by the big bin.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked to a big hill near their house. Tim saw the sun getting low in the sky. He knew it was time for the sunset. "Look, Mommy, the sun is going down!" Tim said. His mom looked at the sky and smiled. "Yes, Tim, it is time for the sunset. It looks very pretty and good tonight." They sat on the hill and watched the sun go down. The sky turned orange and pink. Tim felt happy and warm inside. "I like the sunset, Mommy," he said. "Me too, Tim," his mom said. "It's a good time to be with you." As the sun went down, the stars came out. Tim and his mom looked at the stars and the moon. They felt good and happy to be together. They walked back home, hand in hand, after a nice time watching the sunset.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat. The cat had an owner. The owner was a nice girl. The girl and the cat played all day. One hot day, they went outside. They saw ice cream on the ground. It was melting in the sun. The cat and the girl wanted to eat it. But they did not know whose ice cream it was. Then, a big dog came. The dog was the owner of the ice cream. The dog was sad because the ice cream was melting. The girl had an idea. She put the ice cream in the shade. The ice cream stopped melting. The dog was happy, and they all played together.
