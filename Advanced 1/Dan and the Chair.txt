Title: Dan and the Chair

Dan was in classroom and he was very angry. He felt upset, so he wanted to strike something. Dan looked around the room, and his eyes spotted a big black chair. He ran to the chair and he struck it with his hand. The chair made a loud thump and it felt good to Dan. He kept striking the chair until he felt better. Dan was surprised when he heard his teacher's voice. She was standing in the doorway, looking at him with a stern expression. She said "Dan, why are you so angry? We don't strike things in the classroom." Dan apologized and he put his hands in his pockets. He knew it was wrong to strike the chair, but he was just so angry. From now on, he would try to think before he acted.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went on an adventurous walk with her mom. They saw pretty flowers, big trees, and little bugs. Lucy wore her favorite sweater. It was pink and soft. Lucy's mom said, "Lucy, it's hot. Take off your sweater." But Lucy loved her sweater. She insisted, "No, I want to wear it!" They walked to the park. Lucy played with her friends. She went on the slide and the swings. She still wore her sweater. Her friends asked, "Aren't you hot?" Lucy said, "No, I like my sweater." After playing, Lucy and her mom went home. Lucy finally took off her sweater. She was hot and tired, but happy. She had a fun and adventurous day with her sweater.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a big, attractive box in her room. Lily was very happy and excited. Inside the box, she found many plastic toys. There were animals, cars, and dolls. Lily wanted her friends to join her and play with the new toys. She ran outside and called her friends Tom and Sue. Tom and Sue came to Lily's house and saw the attractive box with plastic toys. They all played together and had a lot of fun. They made the animals join the cars and dolls in a big parade. Lily, Tom, and Sue laughed and played until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a cute snake named Sam. Sam lived in a big forest with many friends. Sam liked to play with his friends, but sometimes he was sad because he was too big to play with them. One day, Sam met a wise old owl. The owl saw Sam's sad face and asked, "Why are you sad, Sam?" Sam told the owl that he was too big to play with his friends. The wise old owl had an idea. He said, "I can help you shrink, but you must promise to be kind to all your friends." Sam agreed. The wise old owl cast a magic spell, and Sam began to shrink. Soon, Sam was small enough to play with his friends. Sam was so happy, and he kept his promise to be kind to all his friends. They all played together every day. The moral of the story is that being kind to others can bring happiness and make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a robot named Rob. Rob was not like the other robots. He was sometimes rude. He did not like to share his toys with his robot friends. This made them very sad. One day, all the robots wanted to celebrate a robot friend's birthday. They planned a big party with cake and balloons. They did not invite Rob because he was rude. Rob saw the party from far away and started to cry. Rob decided to change and be a good robot. He went to the party and said sorry to his friends. They forgave him and let him join the celebration. From that day on, Rob was never rude again. He learned to share and be kind. All the robots were happy and became best friends.

One day, a boy went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy a pet. The boy saw a normal lizard and liked it very much. The mom said they could buy the lizard. The boy and his mom took the lizard home. They gave it a nice home with food and water. The boy named the lizard Max. Max was a happy lizard. The boy played with Max every day. They became best friends. The boy and Max had lots of fun together. The boy was happy he got a normal lizard.
