Title: Mommy and Sam and the Water

Mommy and Sam were in the kitchen. Mommy turned on the faucet and the water came out warm and gentle. Sam was so excited and said "Mommy, look!" Mommy smiled at him and said, "Yes, the water is warm!" Sam reached out and put his hand under the warm water. He made it dance and splash, giggling loudly. "Snap!" he said, as he grabbed his hands together and some of the water jumped in the air. Mommy smiled and said, "Let's play a game. I will snap and you try to catch the water with your hands." Sam was very excited. He watched carefully and then put his hands up as Mommy snapped. He was very happy as some of the water splashed into his hands. They kept playing until Sam was too tired. Mommy turned off the faucet and said, "That was so much fun. Now, let's go and rest." Sam agreed and went with Mommy to the living room to relax.

One day, a tidy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little nail on the ground. Tom was curious about the nail and wanted to learn more. Tom saw a bird and asked, "Can you speak? Do you know about this nail?" The bird said, "Yes, I can speak. The nail helps people build things." Tom wanted to help build something too. He picked up the nail in his mouth and carried it to a man building a house. The man was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! This nail will help me finish the house." Tom felt proud and went back to his tidy home. He told his friends about the nail and how he helped the man. Everyone was happy, and they all learned something new.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to create a tent. She had a pale blue sheet that she thought would be perfect. Sue asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you help me make a tent?" she said. Her mom smiled and said, "Of course! Let's find some things to hold up the sheet." They found two chairs and a big stick. They put the chairs far apart and the stick across the top. Then, they put the pale blue sheet over the stick and chairs. Sue was excited to play in her new tent. As Sue played in her tent, she heard a noise outside. She peeked out and saw her friend Tom. "Hey, Tom! Come see my tent!" she called. Tom came over and they played in the tent for hours. When it was time for dinner, Sue's mom said, "I knew that tent would be fun for you and your friends." Sue and Tom smiled, happy that they had such a great day in the pale blue tent.

Once upon a time, there was a bear who was very ashamed. It was because he was very small, and he wasn’t as big as the other bears. He was very sad, but he had an idea. His finger was very tiny, but he wanted to weigh himself. The bear went to a pond and picked up a stone with his finger. He weighed himself with the stone, and he was surprised. He weighed the same as the other bears! He was so happy and he celebrated. Suddenly, another bear appeared. He looked amazed and asked, “How did you do that?” The bear smiled and said, “I used my finger and a stone to weigh myself.” The other bear was so impressed that he taught him new ways to weigh himself, using different things like leaves and twigs. The bear was no longer ashamed – he was so proud of himself! From then on, he weighed himself every day, and he was always the same size as the other bears.

Once upon a time, there was a happy cow. The cow lived on a big farm. The farm had many animals like pigs, chickens, and horses. The happy cow liked to play with all the animals. One day, the happy cow saw a big bowl. The cow wanted to mix something in the bowl. The cow found some mud, grass, and water. The cow mixed them all together in the bowl. The cow was very happy to mix. But, the happy cow did not know that the bowl was not for mixing. The farmer saw the mess in the bowl. The farmer was very mad. The happy cow was sad. The cow could not play with the other animals anymore. The happy cow was not happy anymore.
