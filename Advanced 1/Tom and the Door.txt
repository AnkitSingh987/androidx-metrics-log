Title: Tom and the Door

One day, Tom went to see a parade. He was very happy. The parade had big balloons and people playing music. Tom saw his friend, Jane, and waved at her. Jane waved back and shrugged, because she did not know what was going to happen next. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, open door appeared in the middle of the parade. People stopped and looked at it. They did not know where the door came from. Tom and Jane were very curious, so they walked closer to the door. When they got to the door, they saw a big room full of toys behind it. Tom and Jane were so surprised! They went inside and played with the toys. After a while, they came back outside and the door was gone. The parade started again and Tom and Jane had a secret they would never forget.

Once upon a time, there was a kind doctor named Dr. Tom. He had a big box that could take x-ray pictures. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to see Dr. Tom. She had a tummy ache after eating some spicy food. Dr. Tom said, "Let's take an x-ray of your tummy to see what is wrong." Lucy was scared, but Dr. Tom was very nice. He offered her a soft teddy bear to hold while he took the x-ray. Lucy hugged the teddy bear tight and felt less scared. The x-ray showed that Lucy just needed to rest and drink water. Dr. Tom told Lucy, "Next time, don't eat too much spicy food." Lucy thanked Dr. Tom and went home. She learned that it's important to be careful with what we eat. And Lucy was happy that nice people like Dr. Tom are there to help when we don't feel well.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. Lily had a pretty bracelet. She loved her bracelet very much. It was her favorite thing to wear. One day, Lily lost her pretty bracelet. She looked all around her house, but she could not find it. She was very sad. Lily had a question for her mom. She asked, "Mom, have you seen my pretty bracelet?" Her mom said, "No, but let's look for it together." Lily and her mom looked all around the house. They looked under the bed, behind the couch, and in the toy box. Finally, they found the pretty bracelet in the garden. Lily was so happy! She put the bracelet back on her wrist and said, "Thank you, Mom, for helping me find my pretty bracelet!"

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big statue. The statue was very serious. It stood tall in the park. Everyone in the town loved the statue. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw the serious statue and said, "Mom, can I give the statue a hug?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can give the statue a hug, Tim." Tim hugged the statue and felt happy. He played in the park all day with his mom. When it was time to go home, Tim waved goodbye to the serious statue. He knew he would come back to give the statue another hug soon.

One day, Tom and his friend, Sam, went to get a haircut. They wanted to look nice for a big party. Tom had an idea. "Let's be creative and get fun haircuts!" he said. Sam agreed, and they both marched to the hair place. At the hair place, Tom told the hair person, "I want a haircut that looks like a big tree!" Sam said, "I want a haircut that looks like a funny hat!" The hair person smiled and began to cut their hair. When the haircuts were done, Tom and Sam looked in the mirror. But, something unexpected happened. Tom's haircut looked like a funny hat, and Sam's haircut looked like a big tree! They laughed and said, "We got each other's haircut!" They went to the party, and everyone loved their creative haircuts.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent cat named Max. Max was very smart and liked to play with his friends. One day, Max found a long line on the ground. He didn't know what it was, but he wanted to play with it. Max tried to pick up the line, but he started to struggle. It was hard for him to grab it. He tried and tried, but he just couldn't do it. Max's friends saw him and wanted to help him too. They all tried to pick up the line, but they also struggled. Finally, after trying very hard, Max and his friends were able to pick up the line. They were so happy! They played with the line all day and had lots of fun. Max was proud that he never gave up, and his friends thought he was very intelligent.
