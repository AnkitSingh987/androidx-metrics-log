Title: The Raven and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big raven. He was very dependable. He helped his friends all the time. He lived in a tree near a small house. One day, a little boy came out of the house. He saw the raven and said, "Hi, raven! Can you help me? My toy is stuck in the tree." The raven said, "Sure! I can help you." The raven flew up and got the toy for the little boy. The little boy was happy. But then, he started to complain. "Raven, I am hungry," he said. The dependable raven flew away and brought back some food for the little boy. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once there was a mole living in his hole. One day, he saw a stupid bird scatter his food on the ground. The mole ran over to gobble it up! He was so happy with his surprise meal that he jumped up and down in his hole. But then the stupid bird realised what had happened. He started to flap his wings and screech at the mole. The mole was frightened and ran away, scattering the food all around. The bird had one more chance to get his food back. He started to fly around and pecked at the ground until he had collected all his food. At last, the stupid bird flew away with his meal. The mole was so relieved that he snuggled back into his hole never to be disturbed again.

Once there was a little girl called Sally. Sally loved her Mamma and Pap. One day, Mamma gave Sally a job. "Sally," said Mamma, "I need you to go outside and keep the heat in the greenhouse." Sally put on her coat and boots and ran out. She opened the greenhouse and sat inside. She looked around and saw the hot sun shining inside. But then she heard something. Was it a noise? No, it wasn't a noise, it was a monster! The monster had big eyes, huge teeth and a mean face. The monster wanted to steal the heat from the greenhouse. Sally was very scared and she wanted to run away. But then she heard her Mamma's voice in her head. "Be brave and be wise," said Mamma. "Don't show the monster that you're scared. Just keep the heat in the greenhouse safe." Sally took a deep breath and faced the monster. She glared at it, and it began to shrink down in size. It was so scared that it ran away. Sally smiled and hugged the warmth inside the greenhouse. She had been very brave and wise, and the heat was safe.

One day, a big rubber ball went to play outside. It was a sunny day, and the ball was very happy. The ball loved to bounce high in the sky. It wanted to fly like the birds. While the ball was playing, a strong wind came. The wind picked up the ball and made it fly very high. The ball was scared, but it was also excited. It was finally flying like the birds! The ball flew over the trees and the houses. It saw many new things. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball met a bird who was sad. The bird could not fly because its wing was hurt. The ball wanted to help. It let the bird sit on top of it, and they flew together. The bird was very grateful to the ball. They became the best of friends and had many adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a white dog named Snow. Lily and Snow loved to play together. One day, they found a drum in the park. Lily hit the drum and it made a loud noise. Snow barked and danced around. Lily took the drum home and started to play it every day. One day, while Lily was playing the drum, her mom said, "Lily, please stop. The noise is too loud." Lily was sad because she loved to play the drum. The next day, Lily and Snow went to the park to play. They saw a man spreading a big blanket on the grass. He had many drums with him. Lily asked, "Can I play the drum here?" The man smiled and said, "Yes, you can." Lily and Snow played the drums with the man in the park. More people came and joined them. They all played the drums together, and it was a lot of fun. The man told Lily, "You can come here and play the drums whenever you want." From that day on, Lily and Snow went to the park to play the drums with their new friends. They were happy and had lots of fun. And Lily's mom was happy too, because the house was quiet again.
