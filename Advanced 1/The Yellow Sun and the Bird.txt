Title: The Yellow Sun and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow sun. The sun was very independent and liked to pass by the sky every day. The sun would say "hello" to the clouds, the birds, and the trees as it passed by. One day, the sun met a little blue bird. The bird was sad because it could not find its way home. The yellow sun wanted to help the little blue bird. The sun said, "Don't worry, little blue bird. I will help you find your home." The sun and the little blue bird passed by many trees, flowers, and rivers. They asked other animals if they knew where the little blue bird's home was. Finally, they found the little blue bird's home. The little blue bird was so happy and said "Thank you, yellow sun!" From that day on, the yellow sun and the little blue bird became best friends. They would pass by the sky together, saying "hello" to the clouds, the birds, and the trees. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had very long hair. Tim wanted to be adventurous. He thought, "I will get a haircut." Tim went to the haircut place with his mom. The haircut man said, "Don't worry, I will manage your hair." Tim sat still in the big chair. The haircut man cut and cut Tim's hair. When the haircut was done, Tim looked in the mirror. He felt very adventurous with his new haircut. He said, "Thank you!" to the haircut man. Tim and his mom went home, happy with his new haircut.

One day, a big blue van went to the park. In the van, there were many kids. They were happy and excited. The sun was shining, and the sky was bright. The kids wanted to play and have fun. At the park, there was a big pool. The kids wanted to dive into the pool. They put on their swim clothes and ran to the pool. One by one, they jumped in. They laughed and splashed in the water. But then, something unexpected happened. A big ice cream truck came to the park. The kids heard the music and ran to the truck. They all got a delicious ice cream cone. The kids forgot about the pool and played with their ice cream instead. They all had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a boy. His name was Sam. He had a lot of gear, but his favourite was a thick coat. He wanted to take his coat everywhere, but sometimes it was too heavy to carry. He asked his Mom for help. Mom said "You can carry your coat, Sam. It'll just take a little practice." So Sam tried. He worked hard and slowly he started to get stronger. Soon Sam could carry his coat with no problem. Now he could take it everywhere, even to the park. He was so happy that he could carry his gear on his own. The End.

One day, a pretty cat named Luna went for a walk. She saw a big fence and wanted to see what was on the other side. Luna was a smart cat, so she found a hole and went through it. On the other side of the fence, Luna saw a big dog. The dog was very nice and friendly. They played together and had lots of fun. But then, Luna heard her mom calling her. Luna started to panic because she knew she should not have gone through the fence. Luna ran back to the hole in the fence, but it was gone! She looked around and saw a new hole. Luna went through the new hole and found herself back at home. She was so happy to be home and safe. The twist was that the fence was magic and could move its holes to help animals in need.

Merry was so proud of her new plant. It was growing so nicely in the sun. After school one day, she ran to the garden to check on it. She excitedly bent down and touched the soft leaves. Merry asked her mum, "Can I stay with my plant and relax?" Mum smiled and said, "Yes, you can relax with your plant as long as you like." So Merry put down her school bag and slowly sat beside the plant, letting the warm sun spread over her. She felt so peaceful, with the gentle breeze and the smell of the flowers. Merry took in a deep breath, and smiled contentedly. She was so proud of this calming garden.
