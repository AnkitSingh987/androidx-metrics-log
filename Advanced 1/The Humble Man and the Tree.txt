Title: The Humble Man and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a humble man. He lived in a small house. The man loved to stretch every morning. One day, the man went for a walk. He saw a big tree. The tree had many pretty leaves. The man wanted to touch the leaves. He stretched his arms up high. The man felt happy. He walked back to his small house. From that day on, the humble man stretched every day under the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Billy. Billy was a very disgusting bug, but he had many friends. They all lived in a big, green garden. One day, Billy and his friends wanted to play a game. They decided to move a big weight from one side of the garden to the other. They all worked together to push and pull the weight. At first, it was very hard to move the weight. But they did not give up. They kept trying and trying. Finally, they moved the weight to the other side of the garden. They were all very happy and proud of themselves. The end.

One sunny day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a mailbox near her house. Lucy wanted to release her letter to her friend. She was very persistent and tried many times to open the mailbox. Finally, Lucy asked her mom for help. "Mommy, can you help me open the mailbox?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, sweetie!" Together, they opened the mailbox and Lucy released her letter inside. The next day, Lucy and her mom went to check the mailbox again. To Lucy's surprise, there was a letter for her! It was from her friend. Lucy was so happy that her persistent efforts had worked. She learned that sometimes, asking for help can make things easier.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wanted to travel to a new place. He asked his mom, "Can we go somewhere new today?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim! Let's go on a trip!" So, Tim and his mom went to a new park. They saw many kids and animals there. They played on the swings and slides. Tim was very happy. Then, Tim heard a new word. A little girl said, "Look at that big red ball!" Tim never heard the word "ball" before. Tim asked his mom, "What is a ball?" His mom said, "A ball is a round toy you can play with." Tim wanted to play with the ball too. But when he went to the big red ball, it was not a ball! It was a big red balloon! The little girl was just playing a fun joke. Tim and his mom laughed and had a great day at the new park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was three years old and loved to play with her toys. Today, Sarah was feeling organized and had neatly placed all of her toys in their rightful places. Sarah's big brother walked into the room, surprising Sarah. “Hi Sarah,” he said. “What are you doing?” Sarah smiled. “Guessing games!” she replied. Sarah’s brother was curious and wanted to know what kind of guessing game she was playing. “It’s a toy guessing game,” explained Sarah. “I turn around and you hide one of my toys. Then, using my other toys, I guess which one you hid.” Sarah’s brother thought this game sounded like fun and so he agreed to play. He hid one of her toys and then she quickly guessed which one it was. “You’re so organized!” said her brother. Sarah beamed with pride. She loved being organized and showing that she was a big girl.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a huge bear. The bear had a friend, a tiny mouse. They liked to play and eat together. The bear loved the taste of honey, and the mouse loved the taste of cheese. One day, the bear saw a big tree with a beehive. The bear wanted to taste the honey, but he could not reach it. He asked the mouse, "Can you lend me a hand?" The mouse said, "Yes, I can help you!" The mouse climbed the tree and pushed the beehive down. The bear was very happy to taste the honey. After that, the mouse found a hole with cheese inside. The hole was too small for the mouse to get the cheese. The mouse asked the bear, "Can you lend me a hand?" The bear said, "Yes, I can help you!" The bear used his big paw to get the cheese out of the hole. The mouse was very happy to taste the cheese. The bear and the mouse were very good friends, and they always helped each other.
