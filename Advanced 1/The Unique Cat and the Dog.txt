Title: The Unique Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a unique cat. This cat liked to hide under the big tree. One day, a dog came to the tree. The dog wanted to play with the cat. The cat did not want to play. The cat said, "No, let me be." The dog was sad, but he went away to find another friend. The cat stayed under the tree, happy to be alone. Later, the cat saw the dog playing with a bird. The cat felt lonely and wanted to join them. The cat learned that it is good to let others play with you. Sharing and playing together makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time there was a happy little girl called Penny. Penny had a big, colourful hat that she loved to wear. One day, Penny went to the park with her family. She ran around and played games with her cousins. Everyone laughed and had lots of fun. When they were done playing, Penny's family brought out something special. It was a special, healthy treat! Penny and her cousins laughed with joy and all eagerly tried the treat. They finished the treat and everyone was filled with happiness. They thanked each other for sharing the treat and laughed even louder. Penny put on her favourite big hat and ran off to play more games. She was feeling very healthy and happy. The End.

Once there was a boy called Fabian who was very excited to try kayaking. His mum had taken him to a nearby lake so he could have a go. Fabian had never kayaked before and he was a bit impatient. He wanted to get in the kayak right away and try paddling the lake. His mum said "OK, Fabian, now first of all you need to sit in the middle of the kayak so it doesn't rock. Then you need to start paddling." Fabian was so excited, he hopped into the kayak and started paddling as fast as he could with his little arms. But as he got further out into the lake the kayak started rocking more and he started sinking lower into the water. His mum shouted "Stop!" She explained to Fabian that he needed to paddle more gently and slowly, so the motion of the kayak would increase the more he paddled. Fabian listened and started paddling slowly. This time the kayak stayed up higher in the water and he felt a lot more stable. He waved to his mum proudly and shouted "I'm kayaking now!"

Once upon a time, there was a big bull. He lived on a farm with his new friend, a little bird. They liked to play together all day long. One day, they saw a big rock near the farm. The bull and the bird had an idea. They decided to play a new game with the rock. The bull would rock the big rock with his strong head, and the little bird would fly and sit on top of it. They played this game all day long and had lots of fun. The bull and the bird were very happy to have each other as friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Glenda. Glenda was three years old. One day, Glenda was feeling troubled. She went for a walk in the park. While she was walking, she found a big rock. Glenda wanted to know what the rock was for. She started to discuss it with her friend Thomas, who she had met at the park. Thomas told Glenda that the rock was very old. He said that the rock had been there since before they were born. Glenda was excited to hear that the rock was so old. She imagined all the things a rock of that age must have seen. So Glenda and Thomas talked and talked about the rock, and all its possible stories. Glenda liked hearing them, and it made her feel much less troubled. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom liked to make things. One day, Tom found a big iron. He wanted to make a toy with it. Tom tried to make the toy, but it was hard. The iron was too heavy. Tom's friend, a little dog named Sam, saw Tom was sad. Sam wanted to help Tom make the toy. Sam and Tom worked together to make the toy. They both pushed the heavy iron. It was still hard, but they did not give up. At last, they made a fun toy to play with. Tom and Sam were happy and played with their new toy all day.
