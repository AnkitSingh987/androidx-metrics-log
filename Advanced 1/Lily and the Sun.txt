Title: Lily and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a big, yellow ball in the sky. The ball was the sun. Lily asked her mom, "Why is the sun so big and yellow?" Her mom said, "The sun is yellow because it is very hot and it can burn things." Lily and her mom played in the park. They ran, jumped, and laughed. After a while, Lily's stomach started to hurt. She told her mom, "My stomach hurts, Mom." Her mom asked, "Did you eat something bad?" Lily thought about it and said, "No, I don't think so." Suddenly, a big cloud covered the yellow sun. It started to rain. The rain cooled down the hot day. Lily's stomach stopped hurting. She looked at her mom and said, "The rain made my stomach feel better!" Her mom laughed and said, "That's great, Lily! Now we can play some more!" And they played in the rain, happy and surprised by the unexpected change in the weather.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big structure. It was a treehouse. A boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were proud of the treehouse they built. They loved to play in it every day. One day, Tim and Sue met a new friend, Sam. Sam saw the treehouse and thought it was very nice. He asked, "Can I play in the treehouse too?" Tim and Sue looked at each other and smiled. They said, "Yes, we can all play together!" Tim, Sue, and Sam climbed up the treehouse and played. They shared their toys and had lots of fun. They were very happy to have a new friend to share their treehouse with. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to look at the sky. One night, Lily saw a big, round thing in the sky. She did not recognize it. She asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "That is the moon, Lily. It comes out at night to say hello." Lily liked the moon. She wanted to say hello too. But she felt uncomfortable. She did not know how to say hello to the moon. Lily's mom saw that she was not happy. She said, "Lily, you can wave to the moon. The moon will see you." Lily smiled. She waved to the moon. She felt happy. Now, Lily knew how to say hello to the moon.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a little girl named Sue had a mitten. She loved her mitten very much. It was her favorite thing to wear when it was cold outside. One day, her friend Tom came over to play. "Look at my mitten!" Sue said to Tom. "It's so pretty!" Tom looked at the mitten and smiled. "It is nice, but be careful not to break it," he said. They played outside in the snow. They made a big snowman together. Sue was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Sue's mitten got caught on a tree branch and it broke! She was very sad. Tom saw that Sue was sad and had an idea. "Don't worry, Sue. I have a plan," he said. Tom was very intelligent for his age. He took the broken mitten and used some string to fix it. He tied the string around the mitten, and it was as good as new! Sue was so happy to see her mitten fixed. She hugged Tom and said, "Thank you, Tom! You are a very good friend." They played in the snow all day, and Sue's mitten stayed fixed. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pink clock. The clock lived in a small room with a girl named Lily. Lily loved her pink clock very much. One day, Lily said to the clock, "I wish you could become my friend and play with me." The next day, when Lily woke up, she saw that her pink clock was gone. She looked everywhere in her room but could not find it. She felt very sad and missed her pink clock. Lily did not know that something unexpected was about to happen. As Lily sat on her bed, she heard a small voice say, "Hello, Lily!" She looked around and saw a pink cat with a clock on its belly. The cat said, "I am your pink clock! I became a cat so we can play together." Lily was so happy and surprised! From that day on, Lily and her pink clock-cat played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.
