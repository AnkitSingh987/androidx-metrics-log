Title: The Bear and Rabbit and the Orange

Once upon a time, there was a bear and a rabbit who were very good friends. One day, the bear asked the rabbit, "What shall we do today?" The rabbit exclaimed, "Let's make a sheet!" The bear agreed and added, "Let's make it orange!" So the bear and the rabbit went to the store and bought an orange sheet. When they got home, they included all sorts of things in their sheet, like ribbons and pompoms. They made it into a colourful game! The bear and the rabbit had so much fun playing with the orange sheet that they forgot the time. When they finally finished, they realized how late it was and it was almost dark outside. The two friends said goodbye as they went to their own homes, filled with memories of their happy day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy hammer that he liked to play with. One day, he was outside with his hammer, when he saw a group of kids playing together. Tim wanted to join them. Tim walked up to the kids and said, "Can I join you?" The kids looked at Tim and his hammer, and one of they said, "Why do you have a hammer? That's so silly!" Tim felt a little sad, but he didn't want to give up. He thought of a fun game they could all play with his hammer. "We can use my hammer to play a game," Tim said. "We can take turns hitting a ball with it!" The kids thought it was a great idea. They all played the game together and had lots of fun. In the end, Tim's silly hammer helped him make new friends.

John and his mum were at the zoo. John was three and very excited. John saw a furry, long-legged animal. His mum said the animal was an ostrich. John wanted to hold it but his mum said it was too big. He was sad. Suddenly, a very ugly ostrich came up to John. He looked angry. The ostrich let John hold its neck and then he smiled. John was so happy! He held the ostrich for a long time and when it was time to go he said good-bye to the ostrich. He gave it a big hug and waved goodbye as his mum took him away.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw his friend, Sue, who was playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play too, so he asked, "Can I play with you?" Sue said, "Yes, let's throw the ball!" They had lots of fun throwing the ball to each other. But then, Tim threw the ball too hard and it went into a big bush. They both felt helpless because they could not find the ball. Just when they were about to give up, a little dog came out of the bush with the ball in its mouth. The dog had found the ball for them! Tim and Sue were so happy and thanked the dog. They learned that sometimes, trouble can lead to a happy surprise.

Once there was a girl who had a pin. She was very competitive and wanted to sell the pin for lots of money. One day, she decided to take it to the market to try to make some money. When she arrived, she saw that there were lots of other people with pins they were trying to sell. Everyone was competing to try to get the most money. The competition was stiff. The girl was determined to get the most money. She offered the pin for sale and tried to get people to buy it. But no one wanted to buy her pin - they all wanted the other pins instead. The girl was very upset. She wanted to be the most competitive and make the most money. She didn't know what to do. Then, suddenly, an idea came to her. She decided to take her pin and offer it at a different market. The girl was so excited - this time, she was sure she'd be the most competitive and make the most money from her pin. And she was right - when she arrived at the market, people were queuing up to buy her pin! The girl was so proud and happy that she had succeeded. She had won the competition and sold her pin!
