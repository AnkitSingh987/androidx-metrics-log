Title: Speedy the Fast Car and the Friends

Once upon a time, there was a fast car named Speedy. Speedy loved to speed down the street. He was very competitive and always wanted to win races. One day, Speedy met a tap named Drippy. Drippy was sad because he could not move fast like Speedy. Speedy said, "Don't be sad, Drippy. We can be friends and race together." Drippy was happy to be friends with Speedy. They had a race, and even though Speedy was much faster, he waited for Drippy at the end. They both had fun, and Drippy learned that being competitive was not the most important thing. The most important thing was having fun with friends.

Lilly was walking outside in the rain. She noticed a window with a small leak. She got close to see what was going on. Suddenly, a bug came out of the window. It was bright green and had wings. "What are you doing?" asked Lilly. The bug answered, "I'm looking for something to bite!" Lilly thought for a moment, then said, "Please don't bite me!" The bug flew away and Lilly continued walking in the wet rain. Suddenly, she saw another window with a bigger leak. She got close to it and realized it was a beehive. She heard buzzing and dozens of bees flew out. She was scared and ran away! The bees followed her until she was safely back inside her house. Lilly sighed with relief and decided to never go near wet windows again!

One day, a little bird felt very bored. He lived in a small nest high up in a tree. He looked around and saw his friends playing and flying. The little bird wanted to have fun too. He flew down and saw a big dog. The little bird said, "Hi dog, let's play!" The dog looked at the little bird and said, "Okay, let's play. But first, I need to lie down and rest." The little bird waited for the dog to lie down, but the dog did not move. The little bird got bored again and flew away. He saw a cat near a tree. The little bird said, "Hi cat, let's play!" The cat looked at the little bird and said, "Okay, let's play. But first, I need to find my toy." The little bird waited for the cat, but the cat did not find the toy. The little bird got bored again and flew back to his nest. He decided that sometimes, it is fun to just watch his friends play from his nest.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly tree. It was very old and did not have many leaves. The ugly tree would stand all alone in the forest. All the other trees did not want to be near the ugly tree. One day, a little bird came to the ugly tree. The bird did not care that the tree was ugly. The bird wanted to learn how to fly high, and the ugly tree was tall enough to help. So, the ugly tree said to the little bird, "I will teach you how to fly high." Day by day, the little bird would fly higher and higher. The ugly tree was happy to help and teach the bird. Soon, the little bird could fly very high. The other trees saw how kind the ugly tree was and wanted to be friends too. The ugly tree was not alone anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane had a rare toy, a big red ball. She loved to play with it every day. One day, Jane's mom said, "Jane, I promise I will play with you after I finish my work. But, you must not open the door to strangers." Jane said, "Okay, Mommy, I promise!" Jane played outside with her ball. She saw a man at the door. The man said, "Hello, can you open the door?" Jane remembered her promise to her mom. She said, "No, I cannot open the door. My mom said not to." The man left, and Jane's mom came out to play. They had fun with the big red ball. Jane was happy she kept her promise, and her mom was proud of her.
