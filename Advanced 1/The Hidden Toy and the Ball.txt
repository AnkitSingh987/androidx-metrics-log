Title: The Hidden Toy and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hidden toy. The toy was a ball. The ball was very good at playing catch. One day, a boy found the ball. The boy loved to throw the ball very high in the sky. He would laugh and clap his hands when the ball came back down. One day, the town had a big earthquake. The ground shook and many things fell down. The boy was scared, but he held the ball tight. The ball made him feel safe. The earthquake stopped and everyone was okay. After the earthquake, the boy and his friends played with the ball. They would throw the ball and laugh. The ball made them happy. The hidden ball was not hidden anymore. It was now a special ball that helped the town feel better after the earthquake.

Once upon a time, a little train was going down the track. The train leaned to the left and leaned to the right as it went around the bends. The train was very good at leaning, and it made the ride fun for all the people inside. One day, the train saw a big, dead tree on the track. The train didn't know what to do. It was scared that it would hit the tree and hurt the people inside. But then, something unexpected happened. A strong wind came and blew the dead tree away from the track. The train was so happy! The little train leaned to the left and leaned to the right as it went around the bends. The people inside cheered and clapped their hands. The train was so proud that it could keep going and keep the people safe. And they all had a fun ride that day.

One day, a funny dog named Spot went for a walk. He loved to play in the mud. Spot saw a big mud puddle and ran to it. He was very happy and jumped in the mud. Spot got all dirty and laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. Spot saw a little bird stuck in the mud. The little bird was sad and scared. Spot wanted to help the bird, so he used his paws to gently pull the bird out of the mud. Spot and the little bird became best friends. They played in the mud together and had lots of fun. The little bird was not sad anymore, and Spot was very proud. From that day on, they always played together, and the mud was even more fun with a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big log in the woods. It was broken and old. One day, a little girl named Amy saw the log. She wanted to play on it but it looked too weak to hold her. A wise bird saw Amy and said, "Don't go on the broken log, Amy. It may not yield your weight." Amy listened and did not play on the log. She played with the bird instead. They had lots of fun. One day, when Amy and the bird were playing, they heard a loud noise. The broken log had fallen and broke into pieces. Amy was happy she listened to the wise bird. The moral of the story is to listen to good advice, even if it comes from a little bird.

Jim was a very healthy little boy. He was getting ready to go to a costume party and he was almost ready. Suddenly his mom shouted, "Jim! Hurry up! You're almost out of time!" Jim rushed to make sure he didn't miss the party. Once Jim arrived at the party, he saw many children wearing their costumes. There was a princess, a knight, and a tiger. Jim was disappointed when he realized he had forgotten to wear his costume. Jim went over to his mom and sadly said, "I forgot to wear my costume." His mom smiled and gave him a hug. She said, "It's ok, I can fix this. Do you mind waiting a few minutes while I go and buy a costume?" Jim happily agreed and waited while his mom hurried to the store to buy a costume. A few minutes later, Jim's mom returned with a costume. Jim was so excited to wear it, and everyone at the party said he looked so handsome. He felt proud and happy to be able to join in the fun. The end.
