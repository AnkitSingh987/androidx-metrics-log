Title: Tim the Boy and the Hat

One day, a boy named Tim found a dull desk. It was big and brown. He thought it was fun to play near the desk. Tim liked to draw and write on the desk. He also liked to hide his toys in the desk. One day, Tim was playing near the desk. He heard a voice say, "Hi! Let's start a game!" Tim was surprised. He did not see anyone. He looked around and saw a little man sitting on the desk. The little man was very small and had a big hat. Tim asked, "Who are you?" The little man said, "I am a magic friend. I can make your toys come to life!" Tim was very happy. He and the little man played with the toys all day. They had so much fun. Tim was glad he found the dull desk.

Once upon a time, there was a big, gloomy closet in a little boy's room. The closet was dark and scary. The little boy, named Tim, was afraid to go near it. He thought monsters lived inside the closet. One day, Tim found a toy car on the floor. He played with it and had lots of fun. But then, he accidentally pushed the toy car too hard. It went very fast and hit the closet door. The toy car broke into pieces. Tim was sad. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the broken toy car. She hugged Tim and said, "Don't worry, we can fix it." They picked up the pieces and fixed the toy car together. Tim was happy again. He even opened the gloomy closet and found out there were no monsters inside, just his clothes and toys. Tim was not scared of the closet anymore.

Once there was a mom and a dad. They were very happy and had two children, a girl and a boy. One day, the dad wanted to do something special for his family. He set off to find something special. When he arrived at the beach, he saw a big tree. The tree was very tall and it was full of lightning bugs. He gathered some of the lightning bugs and set them in the tree. Suddenly, big balls of lightning lit up in the tree. The family was amazed. The lightning bugs lit up the night sky and everyone was happy. The dad had found something special to show his family--the lightning bugs! The parents and their children watched as the lightning bugs flew high above them. As they sparkled in the night sky, the family knew that the dad had set something very special for them. They were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a nice nurse named Nelly. Nelly loved to help people and make them feel healthy. She worked at a big hospital where she took care of many people. One day, Nelly needed to borrow a big red ball for her friend Timmy. Timmy was a little boy who was not feeling well. Nelly thought that playing with the big red ball would make Timmy happy and help him feel better. Nelly went to her friend Sally and asked, "Can I borrow your big red ball for Timmy?" Sally said, "Yes, of course!" So Nelly took the ball to Timmy and they played together. After playing with the ball, Timmy started to feel better. He smiled and thanked Nelly for helping him feel healthy again.

Once upon a time, there was a race in a small town. Many kids came to run. They were all different. Some were big, and some were small. They all wanted to win the race and get a medal. Tom and Tim were friends. They wanted to organize the race. They talked to each other. Tom said, "We need to make sure the race is fair for everyone." Tim said, "Yes, and we need to give the medal to the winner." The race started. All the kids ran as fast as they could. But then, something bad happened. Two kids started to fight. They pushed each other and fell down. The other kids stopped running to watch the fight. Tom and Tim tried to stop the fight, but they could not. The race was ruined, and no one got the medal. All the kids were sad, and they went home. It was a bad ending to the race.
