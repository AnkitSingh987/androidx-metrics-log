Title: The Goose and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a chubby goose. The goose liked to eat a lot. One day, the goose went for a walk. As the goose walked, he saw a big dog. The dog said, "Stop, goose! You cannot walk here!" The goose was scared, but he did not stop. He kept walking. The dog got angry at the chubby goose. He chased the goose. The goose tried to run away, but he was too slow. The dog caught the goose. The goose was sad, and the dog was not nice. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo. He had an unusual job. He liked to complete puzzles. One day, the hippo found a new puzzle. It was very big and had many pieces. He was very happy and started to put the pieces together. The hippo worked hard on the puzzle. He put many pieces in their right place. But then, something unexpected happened. The puzzle started to move! The hippo was very surprised. He had never seen a puzzle do this before. As the hippo watched, the puzzle became a real place. There were trees, a river, and other animals. The hippo was now inside the puzzle! He made new friends and had fun playing with them. This was the best puzzle he had ever completed.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a foolish dog named Max. Max liked to play with a screw he found in the yard. One day, Max met a cat named Lily. Max said to Lily, "Do you want to play with my screw?" Lily looked at Max and said, "No, I don't want to play with your screw. That is foolish!" Max felt sad, but he did not quit. He said, "Okay, we can play a different game." So, Max and Lily played a fun game of catch. They became good friends and played together every day. And Max learned that it was not foolish to try new things.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl who lived in a big tree. One day, the owl saw a small pink bird in the tree. The pink bird looked dirty and sad. The wise owl asked the pink bird, "Why are you so sad?" The pink bird said, "I can't find water to bathe. I want to be clean and pretty." The wise owl knew where to find water. He told the pink bird, "Follow me, I will show you where to bathe." The wise owl and the pink bird flew to a small pond. The pink bird was very happy and jumped into the water. She splashed and played, and soon she was clean and pretty. The pink bird thanked the wise owl and they became good friends. From that day on, they played together in the big tree and had lots of fun.

One day, a girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a big, unknown horse. The horse was in a field. Mia had never seen this horse before. She was very excited. Mia said, "Hi, horse! I have not seen you before. What is your name?" The horse looked at Mia and smiled. The horse said, "Hello, Mia! My name is Star. I am new here." Mia and Star became good friends. They played in the field every day. They were very happy. Mia would always remember the day she saw the unknown horse named Star.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, it was very cold and snow was everywhere. Tim had a fun idea. He wanted to make an igloo. He put on his warm coat and boots, and went outside. Tim started to build the igloo. He made many snow blocks and stacked them up. It was hard work, but he was having so much fun. Tim's friend, Sam, saw him and came to help. They laughed and made crazy shapes with the snow. After a while, the igloo was finished. Tim and Sam went inside. They had a surprise. Inside the igloo, it was warm and cozy. They sat and talked for a long time. They were so happy they built the igloo together.
