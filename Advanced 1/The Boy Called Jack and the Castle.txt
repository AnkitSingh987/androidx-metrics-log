Title: The Boy Called Jack and the Castle

Once there was a little boy called Jack. He really liked to play with his friends in the park but they were all away today. Jack decided to build a castle out of blocks instead. He was doing well until he accidentally knocked the castle down. He was so sad. Suddenly he had an idea! He ran inside and got his toy x-ray. He wanted to use it to replace the blocks so he could rebuild the castle. He went back outside and used the x-ray to pick up the red blocks. When he put them back in a pile, the castle looked like it did before. Jack was so happy he shouted, "Look, I replaced the blocks with my x-ray!"

Once upon a time, in a small warm house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a big smile and loved to play with her toys. One day, Lily felt something strange in her mouth. She looked in the mirror and saw her tooth was wiggling. It was her first loose tooth! The next day, Lily's tooth fell out while she was eating an apple. She was so surprised and didn't know what to do. She started to weep because she thought she did something wrong. Her mom came over and gave her a big hug. "Don't worry, Lily. It's normal for your tooth to fall out. It means you're growing up!" Lily's mom told her about the Tooth Fairy, who would come at night and leave a gift for her lost tooth. That night, Lily put her tooth under her pillow and went to sleep. In the morning, she found a shiny coin where her tooth had been. She was so happy and couldn't wait to tell her friends about the Tooth Fairy. From that day on, Lily wasn't scared of losing her teeth anymore.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a big building with his mom. They needed to go up to the top floor, so they got into an elevator. The elevator had a button that was glowing. Tim liked the glow and pressed the button. As the elevator went up, it stopped on a floor with a room full of fragile things. There were glass animals, thin paper, and pretty lights. Tim and his mom went inside to look around. They were very careful not to break anything. Then, something unexpected happened. A cat jumped out from behind the fragile things and pressed the glowing button in the elevator! The elevator doors closed and it started to go up without Tim and his mom. They had to use the stairs to catch up to the elevator. When they got to the top floor, they found the cat waiting for them by the elevator, and they all laughed.

Anna and Ben were very excited to see the parade. They put on their hats and coats and followed Mom and Dad to the big street. There were many people and cars and noises. Anna and Ben held Mom and Dad's hands tightly. The parade started. Anna and Ben saw big balloons and colorful floats and funny clowns and loud bands. They clapped and cheered and waved. They liked the parade very much. But then, a big wind came. It blew one of the big balloons away from the rope. The balloon was a dragon. It was green and red and scary. It flew over the people and the cars and the noises. It came near Anna and Ben. "Look, Mom, look, Dad, the dragon!" Anna and Ben shouted. "Uh-oh, that's not good," Mom and Dad said. "The dragon needs to go back to the parade." But the dragon did not want to go back. It wanted to escape. It saw a park with trees and grass and flowers. It liked the park. It flew to the park. "Come on, kids, let's follow the dragon," Mom and Dad said. "Maybe we can help it go back to the parade." Anna and Ben nodded. They liked the dragon too. They ran after the dragon, with Mom and Dad behind them. They crossed the big street and entered the park. The dragon landed on a big tree. It was happy. It liked the tree. It wrapped its tail around the branch and rested its head on the leaves. It smiled. "Hello, dragon," Anna and Ben said. "We are Anna and Ben. We saw you in the parade. You are very big and pretty." The dragon looked at Anna and Ben. It was surprised. It did not expect to see people in the park. It was a little scared. It did not know if they were friendly or not. "Hello, Anna and Ben," the dragon said. "I am the dragon. I was in the parade. But I did not like the parade. It was too noisy and crowded and heavy. I wanted to escape. I wanted to find a quiet and green and light place. I found this tree. I like this tree." Anna and Ben smiled. They understood the dragon. They liked quiet and green and light places too. They liked this tree too. "We understand, dragon," Anna and Ben said. "But the parade needs you. The parade is not the same without you. The people and the cars and the noises miss you. They want to see you again. They want to cheer for you again. They want to be happy with you again. Can you please go back to the parade?" The dragon thought for a moment. It liked the tree, but it also liked the people and the cars and the noises. It liked to make them happy. It liked to be part of the parade. It decided to go back. "Okay, Anna and Ben," the dragon said. "You are very nice and kind. I will go back to the parade. But can I come back to this tree later? I want to visit it again. I want to be friends with it." "Of course, dragon," Anna and Ben said. "You can come back to this tree anytime. We will come with you. We want to be friends with you too." The dragon smiled. It thanked Anna and Ben and Mom and Dad. It unwrapped its tail from the branch and lifted its head from the leaves. It flew back to the parade, with Anna and Ben and Mom and Dad following it. The people and the cars and the noises saw the dragon. They clapped and cheered and waved. They were happy to see the dragon again. The dragon was happy to see them too. It was part of the parade again. But it also remembered the tree. It looked forward to seeing it again. It was part of the park too.
