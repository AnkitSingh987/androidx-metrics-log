Title: Mary the Girl and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mary. Mary was very brave, and was always ready to help others. One day, Mary went to the meadow to pick some flowers. Suddenly, a dark cloud appeared in the sky. It started to rain and lightning began to strike. Mary got scared and quickly ran home. When she arrived at home, Mary felt something strange. She could feel a powerful spell coming from her house. She slowly opened the door to find a light coming from her bedroom. She went inside and saw a man holding a big stick. The man told Mary he was a wizard and he had cast a powerful spell. The wizard said that he would only release the spell if Mary gave him all her jewels and money. Mary refused, so the wizard shouted a few words and pointed his stick towards her. Suddenly, she fell down to the ground and felt very weak. The wizard laughed and he disappeared in a light. When Mary woke up, she realised the wizard had taken all her stuff. Mary was so sad. She sadly looked outside and noticed the dark clouds were still there. She learned her lesson; next time, she will be more careful in the meadow.

Tom and Lily are having lunch with Mom and Dad. They like to eat soup and bread and cheese. Mom gives them each a napkin to put on their laps. She says napkins help to keep their clothes clean. Tom wants to be a big boy like Dad. He sees Dad tie his napkin around his neck like a scarf. Tom tries to do the same, but he does not know how to tie. He makes a big knot and pulls hard. The napkin is too tight and hurts his neck. He cries and drops his spoon. Lily sees Tom crying and feels sorry for him. She knows how to tie. She learned from Mom when they played with ribbons. She goes to Tom and helps him untie the knot. She shows him how to make a loop and a bow. She says tying is fun and easy. Tom stops crying and smiles. He thanks Lily for helping him. He tries to tie his napkin like Lily. He makes a loop and a bow. He is proud of himself. He says tying is fun and easy. He shows Mom and Dad his napkin. They clap and praise him. Mom and Dad finish their lunch and say it is time for a nap. They take Tom and Lily to their beds. They tuck them in with their blankets. The blankets are soft and smooth. Tom and Lily are happy and sleepy. They hug their napkins and close their eyes. They dream of tying and soup and cheese.

Once upon a time, there was a three-year-old boy named Trevor. He always wanted to try the magic that he saw the adults doing. One day, he asked his mom if he could try some magic. His mum said no, and Trevor was embarrassed because he wanted to show off his magical skills to his friends. To try and convince his Mum, Trevor said, "Please mind, mum, I would love to try some magic!" But his mum said no again. Suddenly, Trevor had an idea! He ran to his room and came back holding a magic wand. "Look mum, look!" he said. He started to wave his wand around and made the toys around him move. His mum was very surprised to see her son do magic. She said, "That's wonderful, Trevor, you are a natural at this! But, in the future, ask me before trying magic, ok?" Trevor nodded and promised not to do magic without his mother's permission.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tim. Tim loved to sleep all day in his little tent. The tent was Tim's favorite place to rest. One day, Tim saw his friend, a big bird named Joe, spread his wings and fly high in the sky. Tim thought, "I want to fly like Joe." So, Tim asked Joe, "Can you help me fly?" Joe said, "Yes, but you must not be lazy. You must try hard." Tim agreed and they made a plan to meet the next day. The next day, Tim went to his tent and found a surprise. Joe had spread big wings for Tim to wear! Tim put on the wings and tried very hard to fly. With Joe's help and Tim's hard work, they both flew high in the sky, and Tim was not lazy anymore.
