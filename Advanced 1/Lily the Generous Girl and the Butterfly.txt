Title: Lily the Generous Girl and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a generous girl named Lily. She loved to buy gifts for her friends. One day, she saw a pretty butterfly in the store. She knew her friend Tim would love it. Lily bought the butterfly and went to Tim's house. She gave him the butterfly and he was very happy. They played with the butterfly all day long. But something unexpected happened! The butterfly started to grow bigger and bigger! Suddenly, it turned into a beautiful fairy. The fairy thanked Lily and Tim for being kind and gave them three wishes. They were so surprised and happy. They wished for more fun days together and the fairy made their wishes come true.

Once upon a time, in a pretty little house, there was a wise old cat named Wisdom. She loved to sit by the window and watch the birds fly by. One day, a small bird came to the window and said, "Hi, Wisdom! Can I sit with you?" Wisdom smiled and said, "Sure, little bird! Come sit with me." They sat together, watching the birds and talking about their day. Wisdom told the little bird about a big tree that she knew, where many birds liked to play. The next day, the little bird went to find the big tree that Wisdom had told him about. He found it and played all day with the other birds. When he went back to the pretty house, he told Wisdom all about his fun day at the big tree. From that day on, Wisdom and the little bird would sit by the window every day, talking and watching the birds. They became the best of friends, and the little bird learned a lot from Wisdom. The little bird was very happy he had met Wisdom, the wise old cat.

One day, a funny bird named Bobo was playing near the big tree. He saw a little girl named Lily wearing a pretty apron. She was picking up red apples that fell on the ground. Bobo flew down and said, "Hi Lily! Can I help you pick up apples?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Bobo! Let's do it together!" They picked up apples and put them in a big basket. After they picked up all the apples, they saw a pond. Bobo said, "Let's dive into the pond and play!" Lily said, "Okay!" They both dived into the pond and had a lot of fun splashing water. They played until the sun went down, and then they went home happy.

Poppy was a wise old dog who lived on a farm. He liked to do many things, like chase rabbits, dig holes and bark at cows. He also liked to teach the young dogs how to do these things. He was very proud of his skills and his wisdom. One day, a new dog came to the farm. His name was Spot and he was very playful and curious. He wanted to do everything that Poppy did, but he did not listen to Poppy's advice. He chased the rabbits too fast and scared them away. He dug holes too deep and got stuck. He barked at the cows too loud and made them angry. Poppy tried to tell Spot to be more careful and respectful, but Spot did not care. He said, "You are old and slow, Poppy. You do not know how to have fun. I can do anything better than you." Poppy was sad and hurt by Spot's words. He decided to leave Spot alone and let him learn by himself. He hoped that Spot would realize his mistakes and apologize. One day, Spot saw a big red ball in the barn. He thought it was a toy and he wanted to play with it. He ran towards it and bit it hard. But the ball was not a toy. It was a balloon. And it popped with a loud bang. Spot was scared and hurt by the pop. He cried and ran away. He looked for Poppy, but he could not find him. He felt lonely and sorry. He finally found Poppy under a tree. He said, "Poppy, I am sorry. I was wrong. You are wise and kind. You tried to help me, but I did not listen. I did many bad things and I hurt you and myself. Can you forgive me?" Poppy looked at Spot and saw that he was sincere. He said, "Spot, I forgive you. You are young and brave, but you need to be more careful and respectful. You can do many things, but you also need to learn from others. I can teach you, if you want." Spot nodded and smiled. He said, "Yes, please, Poppy. I want to learn from you. You are my friend." Poppy licked Spot's nose and said, "You are my friend, too. Come, let us go and play. But not with balloons. They are not toys. They are pops." The end.
