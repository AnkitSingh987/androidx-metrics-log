Title: Bob and the Flowers

The sun was shining as Bob and his dad walked in the park. Bob was so excited that he was about to pick up some pretty flowers for his mom. Dad said to Bob, "You must make her a promise that you will never pick these pretty flowers again." Bob looked down, feeling a bit ashamed because his dad was scolding him. He nodded and said, "Okay, Dad. I promise." Bob and his dad laughed and hugged each other. Then Bob picked the prettiest flower he could find and gave it to his dad. Dad smiled and said, "This is the prettiest flower I ever saw. Thank you, Bob." Bob grinned and said, "You're welcome, Dad." They both walked back home, Bob feeling proud and happy. He had kept his promise and his dad was happy.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous rhinoceros named Remy. Remy lived in a big forest with many friends. One day, Remy met a small bird named Benny. Benny was sad because he could not find his way home. Remy wanted to help Benny, so he said, "I can urge you to find your home, Benny!" Benny was happy to have a friend like Remy. They walked and talked, looking for Benny's home. As they walked, Remy saw a big tree that he remembered. "Look, Benny! I know this tree. Your home is near!" Remy had seen this tree before and knew Benny's home was close. They walked a little more and found Benny's home. Benny was so happy and thanked Remy. Remy and Benny became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Tom who loves palaces. One day Tom was walking through a wood and he saw a big, black palace. He was very happy and decided to explore it. He opened the palace door and saw the King and Queen inside. Tom was so surprised to see them and he bowed to them. The King said, "Tom, we saw you looking at our palace and we would like to ask you something. Would you marry our daughter?" Tom was overwhelmed. He shyly said yes and the King smiled. The King then said, "My daughter and you will be married right here in this palace." Tom was very happy and he thanked the King. He then went to prepare for the wedding. On the day of the wedding, Tom and the princess had a beautiful ceremony in the black palace. Everyone was very happy and Tom smiled from ear to ear. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a wallet on the ground. He was very excited. He wanted to celebrate because he thought there might be treasure inside. Tim ran home to show his mom the wallet he found. When he got home, Tim opened the wallet with his mom. They found a picture of a man and his dog. Tim was frightened because the dog looked big and scary. But his mom told him not to worry, the dog was just a picture. Tim and his mom decided to find the man in the picture and give him back his wallet. When they found him, the man was so happy that he gave Tim a big hug. The man's dog was there too, but it was not scary at all. The dog was very friendly and licked Tim's face. They all celebrated together, and Tim made a new friend.

Anna and Ben were restless. They wanted to go outside and play, but it was raining. They looked at the window and saw the gray sky and the wet street. "Mom, can we go out?" Anna asked. "Not now, sweetie. It's too cold and wet. Why don't you play with your toys?" Mom said. Anna and Ben sighed. They did not want to play with their toys. They wanted to do something fun and exciting. "I have an idea!" Ben said. "Let's plan a parade!" "A parade?" Anna said. "What's that?" "It's when people walk and dance and sing and wear funny clothes and hats. They make noise and have fun. Like on TV," Ben said. "That sounds fun! How do we plan a parade?" Anna said. Ben thought for a moment. He looked around the room and saw some things they could use. "We need some music. We can use the radio. We need some clothes and hats. We can use Mom's scarves and Dad's hats. We need some noise. We can use the pots and pans and spoons. We need some flags. We can use the paper and crayons. Come on, let's go!" Ben said. Anna and Ben ran to the kitchen and grabbed the pots and pans and spoons. They ran to the bedroom and grabbed the scarves and hats. They ran to the living room and grabbed the paper and crayons. They turned on the radio and found a happy song. They put on the scarves and hats and made them look funny. They colored the paper and made them look like flags. They banged the pots and pans and spoons and made them sound loud. They were ready for the parade. They marched and danced and sang around the house. They waved the flags and smiled. They had fun. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was going on. She saw Anna and Ben in their parade. She smiled and clapped. "What a wonderful parade! You two are very creative and clever. I'm proud of you," Mom said. Anna and Ben hugged Mom and thanked her. They were happy. They had planned a parade. They were not restless anymore.
