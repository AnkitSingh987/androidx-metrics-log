Title: The Story of the Rabbit and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a nice big forest, there were many animals. They loved nature and played together every day. One day, they found a big scale to weigh themselves. First, the little rabbit jumped on the scale. The animals saw that the rabbit did not weigh much. Next, the big bear tried. He weighed a lot more than the rabbit. All the animals laughed and had fun. In the end, all the animals weighed themselves and enjoyed their day in the nice nature. They played together and were happy. They knew that no matter how much they weighed, they were all friends in the big forest.

---

One day, a boy named Tom found a tight helmet. He put it on his head and said, "I am a rescue hero!" He ran around, looking for someone to rescue. Tom saw his friend, Sue, stuck in a tree. "Help, Tom! I can't get down!" Sue cried. Tom climbed the tree to rescue Sue. They both held on tight to the branch. But, the branch broke! Tom and Sue fell down. They were both hurt and sad. Tom's helmet did not help them. The rescue hero could not save the day.

---

One day, a big dog named Max and a little cat named Lily were playing in the park. They were very good friends. Max was a serious dog, and Lily was a happy cat. They liked to play games and sing songs together. Max said to Lily, "I suggest we sing a song." Lily was excited and started to sing a loud song. They were having a lot of fun. Suddenly, they heard a strange sound coming from a bush. They went to the bush and saw a tiny bird. The bird was sad because it could not sing. Max and Lily wanted to help the bird. They had an idea. "Let's teach the bird our song," Lily said. They started to teach the bird their song, and the bird tried to sing. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird sang the song so loudly that it scared Max and Lily! They were surprised, but they laughed and clapped for the bird. They all became friends and sang songs together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom loved to clean his small house. One day, Tom found a big red tomato in his garden. He was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, dog Tim. Tom went to Tim's house and said, "Look, Tim! I have a big red tomato from my garden. Let's eat it together!" Tim was excited and said, "Yes, let's eat the tomato. But first, we need to clean it." Tom and Tim went to the kitchen to clean the tomato. They washed it with water and made it shiny. They cut the tomato into small pieces and put it on two plates. They sat down to eat their yummy tomato snack. As they ate, Tom and Tim talked and laughed. They had a great time eating the big red tomato. After they finished, Tom said, "Thank you for helping me clean the tomato, Tim. You are a good friend." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom. I had fun too." From that day on, Tom and Tim became the best of friends. They always helped each other and shared their food. And they always remembered to clean everything before they ate it.

Once there was a big storm. The sky was dark, and there was lightning. A little girl named Lily was scared. She hid under her bed. The room felt empty because her family was not there. Lily's big brother, Tom, was not scared. He liked the storm. He saw the lightning and started to clap his hands. He said, "I applaud the lightning! It's like a big show in the sky!" Tom was not scared at all. But then, the lightning hit their house. The house broke and fell down. Lily and Tom were very sad. They lost their home and had nowhere to go. The storm was not fun anymore, and they wished it would stop.

One day, Bob the Cat was walking around his neighborhood and saw a big blue toy car parked in a driveway. Bob was very impressed with the car. He stepped closer and saw that the car had been marked with a bright yellow "Left" sign. Bob hopped in the car and pushed the pedals. He zoomed around the neighborhood, having so much fun! He came upon Johnny Rabbit who asked, "What are you doing? How did you get that car?" Bob proudly said, "I marked it with the yellow Left sign and now I'm driving it around. It's been so impressive!" Johnny stopped to admire Bob's car, and they both enjoyed the sunny day together.
