Title: Bob the Fish and the Boat

One day, a big fish named Bob lived in the sea. Bob was a shark. He liked to swim fast and rush through the water. He had many friends, like little fish and big fish. They all played together in the sea. One day, Bob saw a big thing in the water. It looked like a fish, but it did not move. Bob was scared. He thought the big thing was a dead fish. He told his friends about the big thing. But then, something unexpected happened. The big thing started to move! It was not a dead fish. It was a big boat! The big boat was nice and did not hurt the fish. Bob and his friends were happy. They played around the big boat and had fun.

Sara liked to play with her dad's machine. It was big and shiny and made funny noises. She wanted to press the buttons and see what they did. But her dad always said no. He said the machine was not for playing. He said it could be dangerous and hurt Sara. One day, Sara saw the machine in the garage. Her dad was not there. He was in the house. Sara thought maybe she could play with the machine for a little bit. She was very curious. She went to the garage and opened the door. She saw the machine on the table. It looked harmless. It was not moving or making noises. Sara climbed on a chair and reached for the machine. She touched a button. The machine started to beep and flash. Sara was scared. She tried to turn it off, but she did not know how. She heard her dad running to the garage. He saw Sara and the machine. He was angry and worried. He grabbed Sara and took her away from the machine. He turned it off and hugged Sara. He said he was glad she was not hurt. He said he was sorry he yelled at her. He said he loved her. He said he did not permit her to play with the machine because it was not a toy. He said it was a tool for his work. He said it could be harmful if she did not know how to use it. Sara said she was sorry too. She said she did not mean to make the machine beep and flash. She said she was curious and wanted to see what it did. She said she loved her dad. She said she would not touch the machine again. She said she would play with her toys instead. Her dad smiled and kissed her. He said he was proud of her. He said he would show her the machine when she was older and teach her how to use it safely. He said they could play with it together. Sara nodded and smiled. She was happy. She and her dad went to the house and played with her toys.

One day, a funny rabbit named Remy loved to joke. He had a friend, Ava the avocado. Ava lived in a deep hole in the ground. Remy and Ava liked to play and laugh together. One day, Remy could not find Ava. He looked and looked but could not find her. Remy was very sad. He asked his other friends, "Have you seen Ava the avocado?" They all said, "No, we have not seen her." Finally, Remy found a big bird named Benny. Benny said, "I saw Ava. She fell into a deeper hole." Remy was scared but he knew he had to save his friend. Remy and Benny went to the deeper hole. They saw Ava and helped her out. Ava said, "Thank you, Remy and Benny!" Remy joked, "You are a deep avocado now!" They all laughed and played together, happy to be with their friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Tara. She loved her family watch, which she kept safe in her bedroom. She never wanted to take it off! One day, when Tara was playing in the garden, she dropped the watch and it broke. She was so sad! All of the pieces of the watch spread everywhere in the garden. Tara looked for the pieces, but it was too hard. She searched and searched, but it was too easy to lose the pieces in the grass. Eventually, Tara had to admit that she had lost her favourite watch forever. She was so sad that she never wanted to go outside again. The end.
