Title: The Bug and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big bug. It seemed sad and dull. One day, a little girl saw the bug and wanted to cheer it up. She said to the bug, "Hello! What's wrong?" The bug answered, "I'm lonely. Nobody wants to talk to me." The little girl said, "I can be your friend. Would you like that?" The bug smiled. It seemed so happy. They talked and laughed all day. They even played games together. Finally, the sun started to set, so the little girl said goodbye and went home. The bug waved goodbye and seemed so much happier.

Once upon a time, there was a child. This child was troubled. He felt sad because he wanted to help his family. He had an idea. He would sell something to make them happy. The child looked around his home. He found some toys. He thought, "I will sell these toys." He took the toys outside and put them on a table. He waited for people to come and buy them. Soon, a nice lady came. She saw the toys and smiled. She gave the child some money and took a toy. The child was happy. He had sold a toy! More people came and bought the toys. The child was not troubled anymore. He had helped his family and they were all happy.

Tommy was a little bunny who liked to hop. He hopped in the grass, he hopped in the mud, he hopped in the snow. He hopped every day, except for Wednesday. On Wednesday, he had to go to school. School was not fun for Tommy. He had to sit still, listen to the teacher, and learn new words. He did not like new words. He liked to hop. He wished he could hop in school, but the teacher said no. One cold Wednesday, Tommy was very bored in school. He looked out the window and saw the snowflakes falling. He wanted to hop in the snow and make snowballs. He raised his paw and asked the teacher if he could go outside. "No, Tommy, you have to stay inside and finish your work," the teacher said. "It's too cold to go outside. You might catch a cold." Tommy was sad. He did not want to catch a cold, but he also did not want to do his work. He looked at his friend Lily, who was a little mouse. Lily was also bored. She winked at Tommy and whispered, "Let's hop away." Tommy nodded. He and Lily waited until the teacher was not looking. Then they hopped out of their seats, hopped to the door, and hopped outside. They hopped in the snow and had fun. They made snowballs and threw them at each other. They made snow angels and snowmen. They laughed and hopped. The teacher did not notice that Tommy and Lily were gone. She was busy with the other students. She did not see them hop back inside, wet and cold, but happy. She did not hear them sneeze and sniffle. She did not know that they had hopped away. She only knew that they had finished their work. "Good job, Tommy and Lily," she said. "You are very smart. You learned new words today." Tommy and Lily smiled. They did learn new words today. They learned "snowflake", "snowball", "snow angel", and "snowman". They also learned "hop away". They liked those words. They liked to hop. They decided that Wednesday was not so bad after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to play outside. One day, she was walking through the field and saw something poking out of the ground. She went over to take a closer look. It looked like a long handle sticking out of the ground. She tried to dig it out, but she couldn't move it. She wondered what it was and decided to ask her mom. When they got home, her mom explained that it was a special handle. With this handle, she would be able to get something special. The little girl couldn't wait to use it! She ran outside with her mom and pointed the handle at the ground. The little girl then started to dig, and sure enough, she found a treasure! It was a big, beautiful box with many different items inside. The little girl was so excited. Her mom said that she had found a special handle and now she had found a special treasure! The little girl learned that day that if you keep looking and never give up, amazing things can happen. She also learned that it pays to pay attention to the little things because they can lead to big things!
