Title: Daisy the Once Girl and the Flower

There was once a little girl named Daisy. She was so excited to see her very first flower blooming in her garden. Daisy had planted it weeks ago, but now she could finally see the blossoming petals in the warm sunshine. Daisy went up to the flower and smiled at its beautiful face. She touched its petals and admired it's soft colors. Just then, a strong wind came and blew the flower away. Daisy was upset and started to cry. Her mom came and hugged her tight. "Don't worry Daisy," her mom said. "The flower might have gone away for now, but more will bloom soon. I'm sure you'll be able to see many more flowers in your garden soon." Daisy wiped away her tears and smiled. She was excited to see more of her beautiful flowers bloom. Sure enough, a few days later, a new flower had bloomed in her garden. Daisy was so happy to see it and she went to admire it.

Once upon a time, there was a poor princess. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They did not have much, but they were happy. One day, the princess found a magic rock. The rock could glow in the dark. It was very pretty. The princess showed the glowing rock to her mom and dad. They were amazed. Every night, the princess would put the glowing rock by her bed. The rock made her feel safe and happy. The poor princess and her family loved the magic rock very much. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to play in the kitchen. One day, she saw a big jar on the table. The jar was closed. Sue wanted to see what was inside the jar. Sue was not careful. She tried to open the jar. She pulled and pulled, but the jar did not open. Sue got mad. She wanted the jar to open now. Sue was very careless. She hit the jar with her hand. The jar fell on the floor. It broke into many pieces. Sue cried. She was sad because the jar was broken. The ending was bad for Sue and the jar.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to clean the stove. Sue was happy to help. Sue took a wet cloth and started to clean the stove. She wiped it until it was shiny and dry. Then, she showed her mom the clean stove. Her mom was very proud of her. "Good job, Sue!" her mom said. "The stove is very clean and dry now. Thank you for helping me!" Sue smiled and felt happy that she could help her mom. They both went on to cook a yummy meal together.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to wear a bow on top of her head. One day, Kitty met a big dog named Max. "Max," Kitty said, "please don't take my bow. It's my favorite." Max laughed and said, "I want your bow, Kitty. It's so pretty!" Kitty was sad but she did not give up. She insisted, "No, Max, you can't have my bow." The next day, Max saw Kitty playing with a ball. Max wanted to play too, but he remembered how sad Kitty was when he wanted her bow. Max thought about it and said, "Kitty, I'm sorry for wanting your bow. Can we play together now?" Kitty was happy and said, "Yes, Max, we can play together." So, Max and Kitty played together and became good friends. They learned that it was better to share and be kind to each other. And from that day on, they always played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Elly. Elly loved to play with her friends in the jungle. One day, she met a small mouse named Mimi. Mimi was very sad because she felt too light to play with the other animals. "Elly, can you help me?" asked Mimi. "I am too light and the wind blows me away. I want to play with my friends like you do." Elly thought for a moment and said, "Mimi, listen to me. We can find a way to help you play with your friends. Let's think together." So, Elly and Mimi sat down and talked. They did not know what the word "weight" meant, but they knew Mimi needed to be heavier. They were not smart, but they tried their best. Elly had an idea. She said, "Mimi, why don't you hold onto my tail when we play? That way, you won't be blown away by the wind." Mimi was so happy with Elly's idea. From that day on, Mimi held onto Elly's tail when they played. They had so much fun together, and Mimi felt like she was a part of the group. Elly and Mimi learned that even if they were not smart, they could still find ways to help each other.
