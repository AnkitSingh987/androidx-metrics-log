Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he found a big rock. It was a special rock. He loved his rock very much. Tim took his rock everywhere. They went to the park and played together. They played hide and seek, and Tim ran very fast. His rock was always with him. One day, Tim and his rock raced with a friend. Tim was very fast, but his friend was faster. Tim and his rock did not win, but they still had fun. They loved playing together, and they were happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sue. Sue lived in a small house near a big tree. Tim liked to play with Sue because they always had fun together. When Tim got to Sue's house, he saw her playing with a filthy ball. "Sue, why is your ball so dirty?" Tim asked. Sue said, "I found it near the tree. It was in the mud." Tim and Sue laughed and decided to clean the ball. They went to the water point near Sue's house. Tim held the ball while Sue poured water on it. The ball was no longer filthy. Now, Tim and Sue could play with the clean ball. They had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Dave and Kayla, who were both three years old. One day, Dave had an idea. He said to Kayla, “let’s tie together our toy cars and make them go in motion.” Kayla excitedly agreed. So they tied the cars together with a big rope. They all jumped on the cars and made them go faster and faster. Just then, the rope snapped and the cars went in different directions. They were both very scared and started to cry. A big man came running over and said, “What do we have here? Kids should not be playing with big toys like these. You should not be using big ropes either!” The big man was very angry and told Dave and Kayla to never play with big toys again. The two friends were very sad and never played with their toys again. And they never tied anything together ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a compassionate child who loved to play with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends decided to pretend they were painters. They took out their paint and brushes and started to paint on big pieces of paper. They painted the sun, the sky, and the trees. They pretended to be famous painters, making beautiful art for everyone to see. As they painted, a little girl named Lily walked by. She was very shy and didn't have any friends. Tim saw her and invited her to join them. Lily was so happy and started to paint with them. Together, they all pretended to be the best painters in the world. And that day, Lily made new friends who were kind and compassionate, just like Tim.

Once upon a time, in a big, big park, there was a fat, bouncy ball. This ball was not like other balls, it had a brain! It could think and talk to the kids who played there. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw the fat ball and wanted to play with it. Tim kicked the ball and it started to bounce. The ball said, "Hello, my name is Bouncy! Let's play together!" Tim was so happy to have a new friend. They played all day, bouncing and laughing together. Tim and Bouncy had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Goodbye, Bouncy! I'll come back tomorrow to play with you again!" And they did, every day, for a very long time.

One day, a big group of ants decided to march. They marched to find food for their ant friends. They walked in a long line, one ant after the other. The sun was bright and warm on their backs as they marched. As they marched, they came across a scary drain. The ants did not know what to do. They were scared, but they knew they had to find food. One brave ant decided to march across the drain first. The other ants watched as the brave ant made it to the other side. The rest of the ants saw the brave ant and decided they could march across the drain too. They all marched together, one by one, until they were all on the other side. They found food and were very happy. They marched back home, not scared of the drain anymore.
