Title: Anna and the Song

Anna loves music. She likes to sing and dance and play with her toy piano. She wants to be a music teacher when she grows up. She is very enthusiastic about music. Every day, Anna works hard to learn new songs and sounds. She listens to her mom and dad and her music teacher. She practices with her piano and her voice. She is very happy when she makes music. One day, Anna has a big surprise. Her music teacher invites her to perform in a concert. She is very excited and nervous. She wants to do her best. She works harder than ever to prepare for the concert. On the day of the concert, Anna wears a beautiful dress and a big smile. She goes on the stage with her music teacher and her friends. She sees her mom and dad in the audience. They are very proud of her. She plays her piano and sings her song. She hears the applause and the cheers. She feels very happy. She loves music.

Once upon a time, there was a big earthquake in a small town. The ground shook and made everyone scared. In this town, there was a boy named Tim who had an impressive toy truck. Tim loved his truck very much. One day, after the earthquake, Tim saw that his truck didn't fit in his toy box anymore. He was sad and didn't know what to do. He asked his mom, "Mom, why doesn't my truck fit in the box?" His mom replied, "The earthquake made the box smaller, dear." Tim thought hard and came up with a plan. He asked his friends to help him make the box bigger. They all worked together and made the box bigger. Now, Tim's truck fit in the box again. Everyone was happy, and they learned that working together can fix problems.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big dream. She wanted to meet a fairy. Every day, she would look for fairies in her garden, but she could not find any. Lily thought this was very boring. One day, Lily was sitting in the garden when she saw a tiny light. She went closer and saw a fairy! The fairy was very pretty and had wings that sparkled. Lily was very happy to see the fairy. "Hello, fairy!" said Lily. "I have been looking for you! My days have been boring without you." "Hello, Lily!" said the fairy. "I am here to make your days fun! Let's play together." From that day on, Lily and the fairy played in the garden every day. Lily's days were not boring anymore. She was very happy because her dream came true.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a big tree. One day, Tom saw a prune stuck in the tree. Tom wanted to eat the prune, but he could not reach it. Tom's friend, a bird named Polly, saw Tom trying to get the prune. Polly asked, "Tom, do you need help?" Tom said, "Yes, I want the prune, but I can't get it." Polly wanted to help Tom, so she tried to rescue the prune from the tree. But the prune was too high for Polly too. So, they both asked a tall giraffe named Gerry for help. Gerry reached the prune with his long neck and gave it to Tom. Tom was very happy and not grumpy anymore. They all shared the prune and became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent. One day, he saw a big circle on the wall. Tim did not like the circle. He wanted to remove it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "How can I remove the circle?" Sam said, "We can use water and a cloth." They tried to remove the circle, but it was very hard. They needed help. Tim and Sam asked their teacher for help. The teacher helped them remove the circle. They were all happy. The wall was clean again. Tim and Sam thanked their teacher for helping them.

Lily and Max were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a doll and Max had a car. They were having fun, but they were also making a lot of noise. "Shh, shh, be quiet, please," their mom said. "Your dad is working in his office. He needs to finish a note for his boss. Do not disturb him." Lily and Max nodded, but they soon forgot. They started to shout and laugh and bang their toys. Their mom came back and looked angry. "I told you to be quiet. Why are you so noisy?" she asked. "Sorry, mom. We were just playing," Lily said. "Playing is good, but you have to respect others. Your dad is working hard. He needs silence. How would you feel if he came and messed up your toys?" mom said. Lily and Max felt bad. They did not want to upset their dad or their mom. They wanted to be good. "Can we say sorry to dad?" Max asked. "Of course. But first, let's make him a card. That will make him happy," mom said. She gave them some paper and crayons. Lily and Max drew a picture of their dad and wrote "We love you" on it. They also added some orange hearts and stars. They thought orange was his favorite color. They went to his office and knocked softly. Their dad opened the door and smiled. "Hi, kids. What do you have there?" he asked. "We have a card for you. We are sorry for being noisy. We know you are working on a note. We love you," Lily and Max said. They gave him the card and hugged him. Their dad was touched. He thanked them and said he loved them too. He also said he was done with his note and he could play with them for a while. Lily and Max were happy. They learned that being quiet and respectful was important. They also learned that saying sorry and making a card was a nice way to show love. They played with their dad and their mom and had a wonderful time.
