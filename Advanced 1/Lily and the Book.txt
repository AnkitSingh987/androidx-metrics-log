Title: Lily and the Book

Lily liked to learn new things, but she did not like her tutor. Her tutor was mean and always made her do hard work. She wanted to play with her toys and have fun. One day, her tutor came to her house and told her to sit at the table. He gave her a book and a pencil and said, "You have to write all the letters and numbers in this book. If you make a mistake, I will be angry." Lily felt sad and scared. She did not want to write in the book. She wanted to disappear. She looked around and saw her toy box. She had an idea. She said to her tutor, "I need to go to the bathroom. Can I go?" Her tutor said, "Fine, but hurry up. And don't touch anything else." Lily nodded and ran to the bathroom. She closed the door and opened the window. She threw her book and pencil out of the window. Then she climbed out of the window and jumped into her toy box. She hid under her toys and hoped her tutor would not find her. Her tutor waited for a long time. He wondered where Lily was. He knocked on the bathroom door and said, "Lily, are you done? Come out now." He did not hear any answer. He opened the door and saw that the bathroom was empty. He saw the window was open and the book and pencil were gone. He was very angry. He shouted, "Lily, where are you? You are a bad girl. You have to come out and do your work." He searched the whole house, but he could not find Lily. He did not think to look in the toy box. He thought Lily had run away. He called Lily's mom and told her what happened. Lily's mom came home and saw her tutor. She was very worried. She asked, "Where is Lily? What did you do to her?" Her tutor said, "She disappeared. She threw away the book and pencil and ran away. She did not listen to me. She did not want to learn." Lily's mom did not believe him. She knew Lily was a good girl who liked to learn. She thought her tutor was lying. She said, "You are a bad tutor. You are mean and scary. You made Lily unhappy. You have to leave now." She told her tutor to go away and never come back. She looked for Lily and found her in the toy box. She hugged her and said, "Lily, I'm sorry. I did not know your tutor was so mean. You don't have to see him again. I will find you a nice tutor who will make you happy and help you learn." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, mom. I love you. Can I play with my toys now?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can. But first, let's clean the window and pick up the book and pencil. We don't want to waste them." Lily agreed and helped her mom. She was glad her tutor was gone. She hoped her new tutor would be kind and fun. She learned a new word today: disappear. She thought it was a good word to use when she wanted to hide. But she also learned that she could not disappear forever. She had to face her problems and find a solution. She was a smart girl who could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Abbie who was feeling very troubled. She was thinking about how she wished her life would be different. Then one day, something special happened. Abbie saw a magical rainbow in the sky. She was filled with joy and ran towards it. As she got closer, a beautiful fairy appeared. The fairy told Abbie that if she wished hard enough, then her wish would come true. Abbie was filled with hope and she closed her eyes and wished with all her might. Suddenly, she felt a strange feeling wash over her. When Abbie opened her eyes, the fairy was gone, but a beautiful white dove floated in the sky. The dove flew through the sky and then it stopped and hovered over Abbie's head. Abbie looked around and saw a glimmering rainbow waterfall nearby. Abbie knew that if she surrendered to the event that was happening, then her wish would come true. So Abbie surrendered to the event and let the rainbow waterfall wash over her. Suddenly, Abbie felt a sense of peace wash over her and all her troubles disappeared. Abbie smiled and knew that her wish had come true. TheEnd.
