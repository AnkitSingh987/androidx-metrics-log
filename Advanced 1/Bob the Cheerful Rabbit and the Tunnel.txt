Title: Bob the Cheerful Rabbit and the Tunnel

One day, a cheerful rabbit named Bob hopped near a tunnel. He saw a squirrel named Sam. Sam was busy storing nuts for the winter. Bob wanted to help Sam. "Can I help you store nuts?" asked Bob. Sam said, "Yes, thank you!" So, Bob and Sam worked together. They put the nuts in the tunnel. They were very happy. But, one day, it rained a lot. The tunnel filled with water. The nuts got wet and yucky. Bob and Sam were sad. They had no nuts for the winter. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to spring and jump all day long. One day, while Remy was playing, he met a new friend, a bird named Benny. Benny was a bit bitter because he could not jump like Remy. "Hi Benny!" said Remy, "Why are you sad?" "I can't jump like you, Remy," said Benny, "I wish I could spring and play like you do." Remy thought for a moment and had an idea. "Let's play a new game, Benny! We can both have fun!" As they started to play, something unexpected happened. Benny found out that he could fly! Remy and Benny were so happy. Now, Benny could fly high in the sky while Remy jumped on the ground. They played together all day long and became the best of friends. And Benny wasn't bitter anymore because he found his own way to have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful pink bird. The bird loved to write with her feet in the sand. One day, she met a little boy named Tim. "Hi bird, what are you doing?" asked Tim. The bird looked at him and said, "I am writing my name in the sand. My name is Pinky." Tim thought that was so cool. He asked, "Can you teach me how to write in the sand too?" Pinky nodded and showed Tim how to write his name with his finger. Together, they wrote many words in the sand. They were very happy and became good friends. Tim and Pinky played and wrote in the sand every day.

Lily and Jack were best friends. They were always playing together, but today was different. Jack wanted to play ball, but Lily wanted to hopscotch. They argued for a while, and neither wanted to give in. Suddenly, Jack slipped and fell down. His face went red with embarrassment. Lily laughed and pointed. Jack was very angry and shouted at Lily. He said she was ignorant for not understanding his style of play. Lily was so mad that she pushed Jack away. He stumbled backwards and hit the ground again. Lily glared at him, but felt guilty because he was her friend. Jack got up and brushed himself off. He apologized to Lily and said he didn't mean to slip. Lily was glad and they hugged each other. They played ball and hopscotch together, and realized that it was more fun to do both.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer. He had many animals on his farm. One day, the farmer did a foolish thing. He left the gate open. All the animals ran out. The farmer was very sad. He did not know what to do. He walked and walked to look for his animals. He was not excited because he was worried. Then, something unexpected happened. The animals came back! They were excited to see the farmer. They had a big party on the farm. The farmer learned to always close the gate.

One day, a curious little boy named Tim found a long stick in the yard. He did not understand what it was. He picked it up and pretended it was a spear. He ran around, playing and laughing. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and called her, "Hey, Sue! Look at my spear!" Sue came over and looked at it. She was curious too. "What do you do with a spear?" she asked. Tim said, "I don't know, but it's fun to play with!" As they played, something unexpected happened. A big dog came into the yard. The dog saw the spear and wanted to play too. The dog took the spear in its mouth and ran away. Tim and Sue laughed and chased the dog. They all played together, happy and having fun with the new toy.
