Title: The Nosy Chicken and the Turtle

Once there was a nosy chicken. It wanted to know everything. So one day it went to the pond to ask the turtle a question. "Hello, what's your name?" clucked the nosy chicken. The turtle was tired and just wanted to rest. He didn't want to answer any questions. "Please, just tell me your name," begged the nosy chicken. The turtle waited a long time and finally said, "My name is Turtle." The nosy chicken couldn't believe it. "Turtle? That's a funny name! Can I ask you another question?" The turtle sighed. He knew the nosy chicken was not going to stop. "OK, what is it?" "What are you waiting for?" asked the nosy chicken. "I'm waiting for my friends to come and join me for a swim," answered the turtle. The nosy chicken was disappointed. It wanted to know more, but the turtle had already answered the question. The nosy chicken silently waddled away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play in the wood near his house. One day, he found a long, flexible stick. He was very happy and played with the stick all day. But then, something unexpected happened. Tom was swinging his flexible stick and it broke! He felt sad and regret. He didn't want his new toy to break. But, surprise! The broken stick began to grow into a new tree. Tom was so happy that he didn't regret breaking the stick anymore. And from that day on, he had a new tree friend in the wood to play with.

Sara and Ben are kids who like to play with blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. They can build towers, houses, cars and animals with their blocks. One day, they are playing in Sara's room. Sara has a big box of blocks and Ben has a small bag of blocks. Sara says, "Look, Ben, I can make a tall tower with my blocks. How high can you make yours?" Ben tries to make a tower with his blocks, but he does not have enough. He says, "I only have a few blocks, Sara. I cannot make a high tower like you." Sara sees that Ben is sad. She is a generous kid. She says, "You can use some of my blocks, Ben. I have many blocks. We can share and make towers together." Ben smiles and says, "Thank you, Sara. You are very kind. Can I have some red blocks? I like red." Sara nods and gives Ben some red blocks. They start to build towers with their blocks. They are happy and have fun. But then, Sara's cat jumps on the bed. The cat sees the towers and thinks they are toys. The cat paws at the towers and makes them fall. The blocks drop on the floor and make a loud noise. Sara and Ben are surprised and say, "Oh no! The cat dropped our towers!" They look at the cat and see that it is playing with the blocks. The cat does not know that it did something bad. The cat looks cute and happy. Sara and Ben are not angry. They are good kids. They say, "It's okay, cat. We can make new towers. But please be careful next time." They pick up the blocks and start to build again. They share their blocks and help each other. They are generous and friendly kids. They love to play with blocks and with the cat.

One day, a cheerful dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a bakery and smelled something good. It was a pastry. Max wanted to eat it, so he went inside. Max said, "Hello, can I have a pastry, please?" The nice baker smiled and gave Max a pastry. Max was so happy. He wagged his tail and did a little shake. Max took the pastry outside and sat down. He took a big bite and it was yummy. His friend, a cat named Lucy, came by. "What do you have, Max?" she asked. Max shared his pastry with Lucy. They both enjoyed it and played together all day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a fragile diary that she loved very much. Every day, she wrote about her day in the diary. One day, Lily's friend, Sam, came to play. They had a lot of fun with their toys. Sam saw Lily's diary and said, "What is this, Lily?" Lily said, "This is my diary. I write about my day in it." Sam liked the idea and wanted a diary too. Lily helped Sam organize a new diary. They wrote and drew pictures in their diaries together. They learned that it is good to share ideas and help each other. The moral of the story is that sharing ideas and helping others can bring happiness and create special memories.
