Title: The Story of the Garden and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a big, pretty garden. The garden had a small gate. An elderly lady lived in a little house next to the garden. She loved to watch the flowers bloom. Every day, the elderly lady would open the gate and walk in the garden. She would look at the flowers and smile. Her favorite flowers were the big, red ones that bloomed in the sun. One day, a little girl came to visit the elderly lady. They walked through the gate and into the garden. The little girl saw the flowers bloom and laughed. They had a fun day in the garden, and the elderly lady was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived with his mom in a small house. One day, Tim's mom told him, "Remember, never touch the stove." Tim was ignorant and didn't know why. One day, Tim saw a cat near the stove. The cat jumped on the stove and got scared. "Meow!" said the cat. Tim thought the cat was having fun, so he wanted to try it too. Tim put his hand on the stove and it was very hot! "Ouch!" he cried. His mom came running and said, "I told you not to touch the stove!" Tim learned his lesson and never touched the stove again.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little oyster. The oyster had a shell and inside, it felt safe and good. The oyster liked to play with the little fish that swam by. One day, a small fish came to the oyster and said, "Hi, oyster! Do you want to play with me?" The oyster felt happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with you, fish!" So, they played together near the rocks and had a lot of fun. After playing for a long time, the fish said, "I have to go now, oyster. It was fun playing with you." The oyster felt a little sad, but said, "Okay, fish. Come back soon!" The fish swam away, and the oyster went back to its shell. The oyster felt good knowing it had a new friend to play with in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. The bear was very healthy. He loved to spread his arms wide and give big hugs to his friends in the forest. One day, he met a little bird. "Hi, bird!" said the bear. "Do you want a hug?" The bird looked at the bear and said, "No, thank you. I am scared." The bear was sad, but he kept walking. He saw a big tree and thought, "I will give a big hug to the tree." But when the bear hugged the tree, it fell down! The tree was not strong. Now, the bear was very sad. He went to his friend, the fox. "Why can't I hug my friends?" he asked. The fox said, "You are too strong, bear. Your hugs are too big." The bear was so sad that he ran away from the forest. He did not know that the tree he hugged was home to many animals. Now, the animals had no home. The bear's big hugs made everyone sad.

Mommy was in the kitchen, preparing lunch. She had a big tube of macaroni, which was very cheap. She was going to cook it, so that it was warm and ready to eat. While she was cooking, a three-year-old boy came in the kitchen. He saw Mommy stirring the macaroni and he asked, "What are you doing?" Mommy smiled and said, "I'm making lunch. I'm preparing this tube of macaroni so we can all eat it when it is ready." The boy nodded his head and asked, "Can I help you prepare it?" Mommy said, "Sure! You can put the macaroni in cups, and then I'll add hot water. When that's done, it will be time for lunch." The boy smiled and started getting cups from the cupboards. Then, he carefully put some cold macaroni into each cup. After that, Mommy added hot water to each cup and stirred the macaroni. Finally, the macaroni was ready! A delicious, cheap meal was prepared by Mommy and the boy.
