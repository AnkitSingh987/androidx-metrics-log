Title: The Crayon Person and the Rainbow

Once upon a time there was a crayon person who lived in the woods. His name was Jack and he loved to explore the woods. One day Jack was going on a walk and he found a hidden path with tall trees. As he walked down the path he found a crayon box. He was so excited he picked it up and opened it. Inside he found lots of different crayons and a note. "Help me make a rainbow" it said. Jack was confused but wanted to help so he found lots of purple, green, orange and red crayons and arranged them into the shape of a rainbow. Suddenly the rainbow began to sparkle and vanish. Jack was so amazed that he smiled and said "Thank you for helping me create a rainbow." The rainbow had vanished and in it's place was a toy robot. The robot said, "You are a kind and courageous boy. I will do you a favor and keep your crayon box hidden so no one can take it away from you." Jack thanked the robot and gave it a big hug. From that day on he always kept his crayon box hidden so no one could take it away.

One day, a friendly dog named Spot went for a walk. He walked and walked until he found a big tree. Under the tree, he saw a little bird. The bird looked sad. Spot asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I am lost. I need to find the oasis. Can you help me?" Spot thought for a moment and said, "I will help you find the oasis, little bird." They walked and walked, looking for the oasis. Spot saw something shiny. He thought, "Maybe the shiny thing can help us find the oasis." They went closer and saw a big, shiny rock. The rock showed them the way to the oasis. When they got to the oasis, the bird was very happy. "Thank you for rescuing me, Spot!" said the bird. Spot was happy too. They played together at the oasis for the rest of the day.

Mommy and Johnny were walking home from the park. It had been a sunny day and the birds were singing. Everything was looking great. Suddenly, Johnny stepped on a drain and the cover flew off. All the water had come out of the drain. Johnny was feeling miserable. He started to cry. Mommy was worried and wanted to solve the problem. She picked up the cover and tried to put it back on the drain. But it just wouldn’t close. Mommy was getting very frustrated. She tried again and again but it still wouldn’t close. Then, Daddy drove up and saw what was happening. He got out of the car and helped Mommy close the drain. Mommy and Daddy smiled at each other. They had fixed the drain! Johnny stopped crying and felt happy again. He was no longer miserable!

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big forest. The tree had many friends, like birds and squirrels. One day, the tree heard a small voice. The voice was sad and needed help. The tall tree wanted to help the small voice. The tree asked all its friends to find the voice. The birds and squirrels looked everywhere. They knew they would soon receive a surprise. After a long time, the birds found the small voice. It was a little lost kitten. The tall tree and its friends helped the kitten find its way home. They were all very happy. The kitten received love and care from the tall tree and its friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a lucky dress in her closet. She put on the dress and went outside to play. Her friend, Tom, saw her and said, "Wow, Mia! You look nice in that dress!" Mia and Tom played in the park all day. They had a lot of fun. Then, Mia's mom called them for a snack. She gave them yogurt to eat. Mia and Tom loved yogurt and they were very happy. After eating the yogurt, Mia and Tom went back to the park. They felt lucky to have such a fun day together. They played until the sun went down and then went home, excited to play again tomorrow.
