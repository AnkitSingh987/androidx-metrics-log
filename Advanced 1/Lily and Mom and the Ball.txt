Title: Lily and Mom and the Ball

Lily and Mom went to the market. Lily saw a big red ball. She wanted the ball very much. She asked Mom to buy the ball for her. Mom said, "No, Lily. We have no money to pay for the ball. We need to pay for food and milk and eggs. The ball is too much." Lily was sad. She cried and cried. She did not listen to Mom. She ran away from Mom and took the ball. She hid behind a big box. Mom looked for Lily. She was scared. She called Lily's name. She did not see Lily. She saw a man with a big bag. The man saw the ball. He liked the ball. He took the ball and put it in his bag. He ran away. Lily came out from behind the box. She saw the man with the bag. She saw the ball in the bag. She screamed and screamed. She wanted the ball back. She ran after the man. She did not see the car. The car was fast. The car hit Lily. Lily fell down. She did not get up. She was hurt. She was bad. Mom heard Lily scream. She saw Lily on the road. She saw the car. She saw the blood. She ran to Lily. She cried and cried. She hugged Lily. She said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I love you, Lily." She was too late. Lily was gone. Lily was bad.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Max. Max loved to play all day with his friends. Today, Max was very excited to go to the park and have fun. At the park, Max met a big dog named Sam. Sam was not happy today. Sam said, "I don't want to play today." Max asked, "Why not, Sam?" Sam said, "I don't like the sun. It is too hot." Max knew Sam was complaining, but he wanted to help. Max thought of a plan to make Sam happy. He said, "Let's play under the big tree. It will be cool there." Sam liked the idea and they played together all day. Sam stopped complaining and they had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big festival. The festival had fun games, yummy food, and pretty lights. Everyone in the town was happy and excited. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the festival with her mom. They saw a cheap toy store. Lily saw a toy she liked and asked her mom, "Can I have this toy, please?" Her mom said, "No, Lily. We need to save money for food and other things." Lily was sad and angry. She wanted the toy so much. She tried to remember what her mom said about saving money, but she still wanted the toy. Later, Lily saw a boy who was sad too. He had lost his toy. Lily felt bad for him and wanted to help. She asked her mom if they could help the boy find his toy. Her mom said yes, and they all looked for the toy together. In the end, they found the boy's toy, and everyone was happy. Lily forgot about the cheap toy she wanted and learned that helping others was more important. The festival was a fun day for everyone, and Lily would always remember the joy of helping a friend.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She had a big, red lollipop in her hand. She was very happy. She saw a pond with water and ducks. Lily wanted to play with the ducks. Lily went close to the pond and saw a big duck. She wanted to give the duck her lollipop. But the duck did not want the lollipop. The duck went splash in the water. Lily was sad and embarrassed. Lily tried to give her lollipop to another duck. But something unexpected happened. The lollipop fell into the pond. The ducks went splash, splash, splash! The lollipop was gone. Lily went home sad and without her lollipop.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. Her name was Ruby. She was three years old, and she loved to explore. One day, Ruby was standing outside when she spotted something big in the distance. She couldn't quite make out what it was, but it was moving fast! Ruby: "Mommy, what is that?" Mommy: "That's a very large motorcycle, Ruby. It's rushing by us very quickly." Ruby smiled and watched with curiosity as the large motorcycle zoomed by. Suddenly, she felt an urge to hop on the bike and rush around like the driver! She jumped up and down with excitement. Mommy: "Oh Ruby, that's not a good idea. You're too young to drive a motorcycle!" Ruby pouted, but she knew her mother was right. With a heavy heart, Ruby said goodbye to the large motorcycle and went back inside the house. The days that followed were filled with adventures close to home, but Ruby never forgot the thrill of watching the large motorcycle rush by.
