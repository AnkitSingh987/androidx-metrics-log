Title: Molly and the Ice

Molly was having a great day. She was eating her favourite ice-cream. It was delicious! While she was enjoying her snack, she saw a little bug on the floor. She thought it was cute so she decided to poke it. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise. It was Molly's mom and she was very angry. She said in a terrible voice: "Molly, you must never poke bugs!" Molly was very sad and she started to cry. Her mom felt bad and decided to buy Molly a new ice-cream. Molly was so happy and she said: "Thank you, mommy!" The bug was also very happy and it ran away.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She loved going to the theater. One day, she decided she wanted to escape. She opened the door and stepped outside. She saw the long theater with a purple roof. She was very excited. The girl ran towards the theater, but the door was closed. She knocked on the door, but no one answered. She was very sad and started to cry. Suddenly, a bird flew above and had an idea. He showed her a secret way. It was a long, dark tunnel underneath the theater. The girl was scared, but she followed the bird. After a long journey, the girl finally made it out of the tunnel. She was amazed to be outside in the sunshine. She had escaped the theater!

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. He saw a shelf deep in the water. He wanted to dive down and see what was on the shelf. He asked his friend, a big fish named Bubba, "Can you help me dive down to the shelf?" Bubba said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's go together." They swam down to the shelf. When they got there, they saw a bitter lemon. The lemon was not happy. It was making a sad face. Fin asked the lemon, "Why are you so bitter and sad?" The lemon said, "I fell off a boat and now I am lost. I want to go home." Fin and Bubba felt bad for the lemon. They decided to help the lemon find its way back home. They all swam up to the top of the water and found the boat. The lemon was happy to be back home and thanked Fin and Bubba for their help.

Once upon a time, there was a brave rabbit. He lived in a small hole in the woods. He had many friends like birds, squirrels, and bugs. One day, the brave rabbit found a big bush with yummy leaves. But the bush was too thick to get through. So, the rabbit had an idea. He would cut the bush to make a path. The brave rabbit cut and cut until there was a way through the bush. His friends were so happy! They could all eat the yummy leaves now. And they all had fun playing in the woods together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a famous piano. It was big and shiny. The piano lived in a big house. The house was in a pretty town. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the house. She saw the famous piano and wanted to play it. "Can I play the piano?" she asked her mom. "Yes, but be careful," her mom said. Lucy started to play. She played very well. But then, she started to worry. "What if I make a mistake?" she thought. She stopped playing and looked at her mom. "Don't worry, just keep playing," her mom said with a smile. So, Lucy played and played. The famous piano made beautiful music. Everyone in the town came to listen. They all clapped and cheered for Lucy. She was very happy and didn't worry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom had a mask. He liked to wear the mask and play with his friends. One day, Tom met a little dog named Tim. Tim was sad because he had no mask to play with. Tom said, "Why don't we share my mask? We can take turns and play together." Tim was very happy and said, "Yes, let's share the mask and be friends." As they played together, something unexpected happened. The mask fell into a big puddle of mud. Tom and Tim were both sad, but then they saw a big, shiny mask in the sky. It was a gift from the stars. Now they had a new mask to share and play with, and they were best friends forever.
