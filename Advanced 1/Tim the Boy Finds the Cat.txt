Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Cat

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a big rake next to a furry cat. Tim wondered what the cat was doing there. He asked the cat, "Why are you here?" The cat looked at Tim and said, "I am here to help you find a surprise." Tim was very excited. He picked up the rake and started to look around. The cat followed him as they looked for the surprise. As Tim and the cat looked around, they found a big box. Tim used the rake to open it. Inside, they found a lot of toys and a note. The note said, "These toys are for you and your new friend." Tim was very happy and thanked the furry cat. They played with the toys all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Molly and Max. Whenever they went to the store, Molly liked to buy jam. One day, when the two of them went to the store, there was no jam available. "No jam!" yelled Molly in disappointment. "No jam?" asked Max. "That's too bad. What can we do?" Molly thought and then said, "Let's skip around the store to cheer ourselves up!" Max was delighted and the two of them started to skip together. As they were skipping they made new friends who were shopping at the store. Everyone was having great fun. Suddenly, a shopkeeper hurried up to them and told them to stop jumping. "Why?" asked Max. "Because," said the shopkeeper, "you are disturbing the other customers and making it difficult for them to shop!" Molly and Max felt sorry for causing a problem. Molly had an idea and she smiled. She said, "Let's make things better by asking the store to get more jam for us." The shopkeeper agreed and promised take their request seriously. Molly and Max cheered and went off to continue skipping. Soon the store had more jam available and Molly was able to get her favourite jam!

Once upon a time, there was a big tree with a vine. On the vine, there were tasty red fruits. A little girl named Lily loved to pick the fruits and eat them. She would share them with her friends, too. One day, Lily found a shiny stone under the tree. She wanted to make it even shinier, so she decided to polish it. She rubbed it with a soft cloth. The stone became so shiny that it sparkled like the sun. Lily showed the shiny stone to her friends. They all loved it and wanted to find one too. They went to the tree with the vine and picked more tasty fruits. They also looked for shiny stones to polish. They had a fun day playing under the tree and eating the tasty fruits.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a broken toy. It was a mystery how it got broken. A little girl named Lily lived in the house. She wanted to fix the toy. Lily found a big ruler to measure the toy. She wanted to find out how big the toy was. She saw that the toy was very small. She needed help to fix the broken toy. Lily asked her friend Tom to help her. They tried to fix the toy together. They worked hard and soon, the toy was not broken anymore. They were very happy and played with the toy all day. The mystery was solved, and they had lots of fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play at the park. He saw a wild cat with pretty fur. The cat was very fast and liked to rush around. Tim wanted to be friends with the cat, so he tried to catch it. Tim and the cat played in the park. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. The cat liked Tim, and Tim liked the cat. They were both very happy to have a new friend. After playing, Tim and the cat were dirty. They wanted to be clean again. So, they went to Tim's house and took a bath. The cat liked the warm water and the bubbles. Now, they were clean and ready to play some more.
