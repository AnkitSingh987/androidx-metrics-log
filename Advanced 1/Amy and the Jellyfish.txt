Title: Amy and the Jellyfish

Amy was playing on the beach. She saw something small rise out of the water. She ran close to see what it was. "Look what I found," she said to her Mom. "It's a jellyfish," her Mom replied. It was very small and had many legs. Amy picked it up and tried to hold it in her hand. She was careful not to squeeze it too hard. The jellyfish waved its arms as it moved through the air. Amy giggled. She walked over to the water and put the jellyfish back into the sea. Amy watched as the small jellyfish rose to the surface and disappeared. She smiled and waved goodbye.

One day, a girl named Sue went to play with her friend, Tom. They played ball in the park. They were happy and had fun. Sue saw a dead bug on the ground. She told Tom, "Look, a dead bug!" Tom said, "Yuck! Let's go play ball again." Sue and Tom played more ball. Sue ran and fell down. She hurt her knee. It hurt a lot. She cried and Tom came to help her. He said, "Don't cry, Sue. I will serve you some ice cream to make you feel better." Tom went to get ice cream for Sue. When he came back, Sue was not crying anymore. She was smiling. She said, "Tom, look! The dead bug is not dead! It was just sleeping!" The bug flew away, and Sue and Tom laughed. They ate ice cream and played more ball. Sue's knee felt better, and they had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a popular olive tree. Many kids loved to play near the tree. They would laugh and have fun under the tree. One day, a little boy named Tim did not understand why the olive tree was popular. He thought it was just like any other tree. He wanted to find out what made the tree special. Tim decided to climb the tree. As he climbed higher and higher, he saw something shiny. He reached out to touch it and found a hidden treasure box! Now Tim understood why the olive tree was so popular. It had a magical treasure that made everyone happy!

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He was wearing his favorite jacket. He saw a curious cat and wanted to play with it. The cat was fluffy and cute. Tim tried to pet the cat, but the cat was scared and ran away. Tim felt sad, so he went to his mom. "Mom, the cat ran away from me," he said. His mom thought for a moment and said, "Maybe you can try to reverse your jacket. The cat might like the other side better." Tim was confused, but he tried it anyway. He took off his jacket and put it on the other way. He went back outside to find the cat. When he found the cat, it looked at him and didn't run away. Tim was happy and pet the cat gently. The cat liked Tim's jacket now and followed him home. Tim's mom saw them and said, "See, the cat likes your jacket reversed!" Tim smiled and played with the cat all day. They became best friends, and the cat stayed with Tim and his family.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jonny. He was three years old and very adventurous. One day he wanted to go explore the park but his mom said no. He was very disappointed and sad. He decided to explore his backyard instead. He was about to leave his house when he heard a faint zip. He looked down and saw a little grey creature. It was very cute and had big eyes. The little creature was scared. Jonny gently pet him then he took the little creature inside and showed it to his mom. She was so surprised! She said it was an ash - a very special creature. Jonny loved the ash and brought it everywhere with him. But one day Jonny's friend came to visit him. He thought the ash was disgusting and said mean things about it. Jonny was very upset and decided to sneak out and find a special place just for the ash. They explored the park until they found the perfect spot. Jonny and the ash played together all day and they even had a picnic! Jonny smiling and they said bye with a big zip. From then on, they spent every day together.
