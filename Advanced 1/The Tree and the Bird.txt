Title: The Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in a nice park. The tree had a little friend, a bird named Blue. Blue loved to dream about flying high in the sky. One day, Blue was sitting on a branch and talking to the tree. "Tree, I want to find a special thing," said Blue. "Something very important." "Look in your heart, Blue," said the tree. "You will find the important thing there." Blue thought about it and decided to fly away to find the important thing. He flew far away and saw many things. But he could not find the important thing he was looking for. Then, something unexpected happened. Blue met a very wise bird named Wise. Wise told Blue, "The important thing is not far away. It is inside you. It is your love for your friends and family." Blue was surprised. He flew back to the tree and told the tree what Wise had said. The tree smiled and said, "I knew it all along, Blue. The important thing is the love we have for each other."

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue lived in a small house near the woods. One morning, Sue saw a thick fog outside her window. She wanted to go outside and gather pretty stones from the woods. As Sue walked into the foggy woods, she met a big, honest bear. The bear said, "Hello, Sue. What are you doing in the woods?" Sue told the bear she was looking for pretty stones. The bear said, "I can help you gather them." Sue and the bear found many pretty stones. But then, something unexpected happened. The fog cleared, and they saw a big, shiny treasure chest! Sue and the bear opened the chest and found more pretty stones inside. They were so happy and shared the stones with their friends.

Once upon a time, there lived a selfish little girl. Every day she wanted to keep all her toys to herself and never wanted to share. One day, she was playing in her bedroom when she noticed something sticking out from underneath her bed. She thought to herself, "What could that be?" She slowly and slowly crept over to the bed and slowly peeked underneath. To her surprise, she saw a little puppy. The puppy was shivering and scared, so the selfish little girl felt bad for not sharing her toys. She gently picked up the puppy and hugged it. “Who are you?” She said softly. “My name is Spot,” the puppy replied, “I was looking for a place to sleep, but it’s too dark and cold outside. Can I stay here?” The selfish little girl didn't want to share her room but she put on a brave face and smiled. “Yes, you can stay here. You can sleep here every night.” The puppy curled up on the bed beside her. From that day onward, Spot and the little girl became best friends and the selfish little girl learned how to share her toys with Spot.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the warm sun. One day, Tim found a seed. He thought, "I want to grow a big tree!" So, he put the seed in the ground and gave it water. As the days went by, Tim watched the seed grow. He saw a small green thing come out of the ground. Tim was very happy. He said, "Grow, grow, grow!" But then, he saw something he didn't like. There was waste near the small green thing. Tim was sad. He thought, "Who put this waste here?" He looked around and saw a cat. The cat said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know the waste would hurt your plant." Tim forgave the cat, and they both cleaned up the waste together. The small green thing grew bigger and bigger. One day, something unexpected happened. The tree had grown big and strong, but instead of leaves, it grew candy! Tim and the cat were very surprised and happy. They shared the candy with all their friends and had a big party under the candy tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Beep. Beep loved to drive fast. One day, Beep saw a big cube in the road. Beep did not stop and hit the cube. Beep felt very sorry. Beep's friend, Toot, saw what happened. Toot said, "Beep, you should drive slow and look at the road. Then you will not hit things." Beep knew Toot was right. Beep said, "I am sorry, Toot. I will drive slow and look at the road." From that day on, Beep drove slow and looked at the road. Beep did not hit any more cubes or other things. Beep was happy and safe. Beep learned that it is good to say sorry and learn from mistakes.
