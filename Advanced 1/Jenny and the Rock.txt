Title: Jenny and the Rock

One morning, Jenny got up and went outside to the garden. She found a pretty rock and picked it up. She said to herself, "I'm not going to forget this pretty rock." She brought it with her back inside and put it on a shelf. Later in the day, Jenny was busy playing with her toys. Then suddenly she remembered the rock. She asked her mom, "Mommy, did I forget something today?" Mommy looked at her carefully and said, "No, I don't think so. What did you think you forgot?" Jenny said, "I found a pretty rock this morning but I don't remember where I put it!" Mommy smiled and said, "Oh, the rock. I put it on the shelf for you. You can go get it anytime you want." Jenny smiled and ran off to get the rock. She looked at it and said, "Thanks, Mommy! I won't forget this pretty rock again!"

Jill was very excited. She was about to solve a great mystery! Jill put on her hat and grabbed her magnifying glass. She wanted to find out what was inside the pale box. She knocked on the box, but there was no answer. She waited and waited. Nothing happened. Jill kept on looking and suddenly saw something glimmering! It was a bright, shiny key. She put the key in the lock and turned it. The box opened! Jill was so happy she had found the answer to the mystery. Inside the box was a pale doll. Jill smiled and wondered what other mysteries she could solve.

One day, a big owl sat on a tree. It was very hot outside. The owl was sad because he was hot and thirsty. He looked for water, but he could not find any. He needed water to feel better. The owl saw a little girl named Mia playing near the tree. He said, "Mia, I need water. Can you help me?" Mia looked at the owl and said, "Yes, I can help you. I have a cup of water in my bag." Mia took out her cup of water and gave it to the owl. The owl was very happy and drank the water. But then, something unexpected happened. The owl turned into a beautiful bird with many colors. Mia was surprised and asked, "Who are you?" The bird said, "I was an owl, but I needed your help to change into a beautiful bird. Thank you, Mia, for your kindness." Mia and the bird became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a baby called David. David was three years old. David enjoyed playing with his toys, including his favourite car. One day, the car stopped working and David got very sad. He asked his mummy to help him but she said there was nothing she could do. David thought he would never be able to play with his car again. But then his big sister came to the rescue! She said she could repair the car. But first, she had to make a network. Then she would be ready to repair the car. She worked hard and soon the network was done. Then she was ready to fix David's car. Her repair was a success! David was thrilled and they all celebrated. He finally had his beloved car back to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go for walks in the park with his dog, Spot. One sunny day, they went on a new trail they had never been on before. As they walked, Tim saw a big, disgusting mess on the trail. "Yuck!" Tim said, "What is that, Spot?" Spot barked and wagged his tail, but he didn't know what it was either. They decided to keep walking and find out what made the mess. Soon, they found a little girl named Sue. She was crying. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, Sue?" Sue said, "I miss my cat, Fluffy. She ran away." Tim and Spot wanted to help Sue find her cat. They all looked around and saw Fluffy near the disgusting mess. They were so happy to find her! Sue thanked Tim and Spot for their help. They all walked back on the trail together, and Sue took Fluffy home. Tim and Spot went back to their walk, happy that they had made a new friend and helped her find her cat.
