Title: Daisy the Cheerful Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little girl named Daisy. She loved to play in the park with her friends. One sunny day, Daisy and her friends went to the park to play. They were all very happy and excited. While they were playing, Daisy saw a big tree with a lot of marks on it. She went to the tree to look at the marks. As she got closer, Daisy found out that the marks were drawings of animals. She was very surprised and wanted to know who made these marks. Daisy asked her friends if they knew who made the marks. They all said they didn't know. Just then, a squirrel came down from the tree. The squirrel had a small stick in its hand. Daisy and her friends watched as the squirrel made a new mark on the tree. It was a drawing of a flower. Daisy and her friends were so surprised! They never thought a squirrel could make such beautiful marks. From that day on, Daisy and her friends played with the squirrel and learned how to make pretty marks on the tree too.

Once upon a time, there was a small, noisy dog named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy tool. One day, Tim saw a big, yellow ball in the park. He wanted to play with it, so he ran to the ball. When Tim got to the ball, he tried to pick it up with his mouth. But the ball was too big! So, he used his toy tool to squeeze the ball. The ball became smaller and smaller, and Tim was very happy. Tim played with the small ball all day. He ran, jumped, and squeezed the ball with his toy tool. At the end of the day, Tim was tired but happy. He went home with his toy tool and the small ball, ready to play again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable cat named Lily. Lily lived in a big house with her best friend, a little girl named Emma. They played together every day and had lots of fun. One day, Emma's mommy and daddy had a big party called a marriage. People came to the house wearing pretty clothes. Emma and Lily wore matching pink dresses. During the party, Lily saw a yummy cake on the table. She jumped up and took a big bite, but she made a mess on her face. Emma saw Lily's messy face and laughed. "Lily, you need to wipe your face!" she said. Emma took a soft cloth and gently wiped Lily's face clean. Then, they both went back to the party and danced together. Everyone said they were the cutest friends ever.

Alice, who was three years old, was walking through the park and saw a delicious apple that was hanging from a tree branch. She wanted it, so she yelled for it. Alice said, "Please give me that delicious apple!" But a man who was sitting nearby shouted back, "I'm sorry, you can't have it." Alice yelled, "Please give me the delicious apple! I really want it!" The man said, "No, you can't have it. It's not safe, it's not part of your life." Alice yelled even louder, "Please, please give me the delicious apple!" The man shook his head and said, "No, this apple is not safe for your life. You should go home and forget about it." Alice was very sad and angry. She yelled with all her strength, "Give me the delicious apple!" But instead of giving her the apple, the man yelled at her and said, "Go home now or I'll call the police!" Alice sadly walked away and went home, never getting the delicious apple. She was unhappy and upset and never wanted to talk about that day again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big lawn with green grass. He liked to run and play on the lawn. Tim saw a little girl named Sue sitting on the lawn. Sue was crying. Tim went to Sue and asked, "Why do you cry?" Sue said, "I lost my toy. I am sad." Tim wanted to help Sue. He was a reliable friend. He started to look for Sue's toy. He looked under the trees and behind the bushes. Finally, Tim found the toy. It was a small, yellow car. Tim gave the toy back to Sue. Sue was very happy. She stopped crying and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim and Sue played with the toy car on the lawn. They laughed and had fun together. Now, they were both happy.
