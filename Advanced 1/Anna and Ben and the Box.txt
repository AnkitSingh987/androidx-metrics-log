Title: Anna and Ben and the Box

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play pretend. Today they pretend to be chefs in a restaurant. They have a big box, some pots and pans, and some toy food. Anna says, "I am the boss chef. You are the helper chef. We have to make food for the customers. They are very hungry and serious. They want good food fast." Ben says, "OK, boss chef. What do we make?" Anna says, "We make soup, salad, and pizza. You make the soup. I make the salad and pizza. Here, take this pot and spoon. Put some water and toy food in the pot. Stir it well. Then raise the pot and pour the soup into the bowls." Ben does what Anna says. He puts some water and toy food in the pot. He stirs it well. Then he raises the pot and pours the soup into the bowls. But he pours too fast and too much. The soup spills over the bowls and onto the floor. Anna says, "Oh no! You made a big mess! The customers will not like this. They will be angry and sad. You are a bad helper chef. You have to clean up the mess." Ben says, "I'm sorry, boss chef. I did not mean to spill the soup. I was trying to be fast and good. Please don't be mad at me. I will clean up the mess." Anna says, "OK, helper chef. I forgive you. But you have to be more careful next time. The restaurant is very important. We have to make the customers happy and full. Now, help me make the salad and pizza. Then we can eat some too." Ben says, "OK, boss chef. Thank you for forgiving me. I will help you make the salad and pizza. They look yummy. I love playing pretend with you." Anna says, "I love playing pretend with you too. You are my best twin. We are a good team. Now, let's finish the food and serve the customers. They are waiting for us." They smile and hug. Then they finish the food and serve the customers. The customers are their dolls and stuffed animals. They pretend that the customers are happy and full. They say thank you and give them tips. Anna and Ben are proud and happy. They eat some of the food too. They have fun playing pretend in their restaurant.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there lived a nice little bug. The bug was very good at spraying water. He loved to spray water on flowers to help them grow big and strong. The bug had a friend, a big, rude bird. The bird always took the bug's water and sprayed it in the sky. One day, while the bug was spraying water on the flowers, the rude bird took the water and sprayed it in the sky again. The bug was very sad. He wanted to help the flowers grow, but the bird kept taking his water. But then, something unexpected happened. As the bird sprayed the water in the sky, it turned into beautiful, colorful rain. The rain fell on the flowers, and they grew bigger and more beautiful than ever before. The bug and the bird were both happy, and they learned to work together to make the park even more beautiful.

Maria and her best friend were playing in the garden. "Let’s paint a picture!" Maria said. Her friend jumped up in delight. The two children collected some paints and a large white sheet. Maria and her friend took turns painting the sheet with bright colourful swirls. They laughed and giggled as they played. Suddenly it started to rain. Maria and her friend ran indoors to enjoy their picture. But when they looked at their painting, they noticed that the colours had run and blended together. "Oh no! It looks hurt" Maria said sadly. Her friend frowned and looked sadly at the painting. "We can make it better," said her friend. "Let’s put some bright yellow flowers around it. They can make it happy again." So the two children worked together to add some yellow flowers around the edge of the painting. When they finished, they both stepped back and smiled. "It looks much better!" Maria exclaimed. They both hugged and then enjoyed the beautiful painting. Maria thanked her friend for helping her turn their painting from hurt to happy.
