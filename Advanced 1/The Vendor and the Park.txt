Title: The Vendor and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a vendor. The vendor sold many long things. He sold long sticks, long ropes, and long socks. Every day, people came to buy his long things. One day, a little girl came to the vendor. She wanted a long rope to play with her friends. The vendor gave her a long rope that could stretch. The little girl was very happy. The little girl took the long rope to the park. She and her friends played a game with the rope. They stretched the rope and jumped over it. They had so much fun with the long rope that could stretch. And the vendor was happy to see them play.

---

One day, a little girl and her mother went to the park. They saw a big, dry tree. The mother said, "Let's sit under the tree and have a snack." The little girl was very happy. As they sat under the tree, they heard a loud noise. The little girl looked up and saw a big bird in the tree. The bird dropped something from the tree. The little girl and her mother were surprised. The thing that the bird dropped was a small, shiny box. The little girl opened the box and found a pretty necklace inside. The mother said, "This must be a gift from the bird!" They both laughed and went back home, happy with their new treasure.

Once upon a time, there was a black cat. She was very curious. One day, she found an incredible spirit. The spirit was so interesting that she wanted to keep it for herself. So, she put it in a small box and carried it everywhere she went. But, one day, the spirit disappeared. The cat was very sad. She looked everywhere trying to find her special spirit but she did not find the spirit anywhere. The moral of this story is that it is not good to keep something that belongs to someone else. If you do, it may disappear and you will not be able to get it back.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a walk. He saw a wild rabbit. The rabbit was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Tim. The rabbit said, "I lost my home. It was a cave. Can you help me find it?" Tim wanted to help the rabbit. They looked for the cave together. They walked and walked, but they could not find the cave. Then, they saw a big bear. The bear was angry. "This is my cave!" said the bear. "You can't have it!" Tim had an idea. "Can you lend the cave to the rabbit for a little while?" he asked the bear. The bear thought about it. Then, he said, "Okay, I will lend the cave to the rabbit. But only for a little while." The rabbit was very happy. He thanked Tim and the bear. The bear learned that it was good to share. And Tim learned that helping others can make you feel happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Lucy. Lucy liked to play with her toy blocks. She had many blocks in different shapes, but her favorite was the square block. One day, Lucy was playing with her square block when her friend Tom came over. "Can I play with your square block?" Tom asked. "Yes, but please be careful," Lucy said. Tom took the square block and started to play with it. Suddenly, the square block began to speak! "Please repeat after me," the square block said. Lucy and Tom were very surprised. They looked at each other and decided to do what the square block asked. "Please repeat after me," they both said. The square block then turned into a magical talking toy. It could sing songs and tell stories. Lucy and Tom were very happy to have the magical square block as their new friend. They played together all day long and had lots of fun.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to paint. She was very enthusiastic. She took her colors and her brush. Lily started to paint a big sun in the sky. She felt happy. Then, her friend Tom came to see her. "What are you doing?" he asked. "I am painting," said Lily. Tom wanted to paint too. He took a brush and began to paint a tree. They painted together, moving to the rhythm of a happy song. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and took Lily's brush. The bird started to paint too! It painted beautiful flowers all around the sun and tree. Lily and Tom were surprised and laughed. They made a new friend, the painting bird.
