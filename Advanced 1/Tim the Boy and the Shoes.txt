Title: Tim the Boy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited to go to his friend's house. He put on his shoes, said bye to his mom, and ran out the door. As Tim walked, he saw a big stair. He wanted to climb it, so he did. When he got to the top, he saw a small bird. The bird looked sad. Tim asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I broke my friend's toy. I don't know if he will forgive me." Tim told the bird, "It's okay. Just say sorry to your friend. I'm sure he will forgive you." The bird thanked Tim and flew away to find his friend. Tim went down the stair and continued to his friend's house. He was happy he could help the bird. Tim knew that friends forgive each other.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy fish. This fish lived in a big tank with other fish. The bossy fish liked to tell the other fish what to do. All the fish in the tank were not happy with the bossy fish. One day, a new fish came to the tank. The bossy fish tried to tell the new fish what to do. But the new fish did not like it. The new fish said, "I do not want you to be bossy." The bossy fish did not like what the new fish said. So, the bossy fish tried to bite the new fish. But the new fish was fast and swam away. The other fish in the tank saw this and they were happy. They all swam together and played. The bossy fish learned not to be bossy and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to wake up early to play outside. One day, he saw something weird on the earth. It was a big, shiny rock. Tom touched the rock, and it started to talk! The rock said, "Hi, I am a magic rock. I can make your dreams come true." Tom was very happy. He asked the rock to give him a pet dog. But, the rock did something unexpected. Instead of a dog, it gave Tom a pet frog. Tom was sad at first, but then he saw the frog was special. It could jump very high and play with Tom. In the end, Tom loved his frog and they played together every day. The magic rock had made Tom's dream come true, but in a different way. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Penny. Penny was only three years old, but she was very helpful. One day, Penny was walking in the park with her mom, when she saw something sparkly in the grass. "Mama, what's that?" she asked. Her mom bent down and picked up a white powder that was in the grass. "It looks like a kind of magic powder," said her mom. "Let's take it home, it might be helpful." Penny got very excited and she took the powder home with her. She and her mom put the powder in a special jar, and put it on a shelf in the kitchen. The following morning, Penny's mom found something strange in the kitchen. A rainbow appeared right above the jar of powder when she opened the door! "Oh my goodness!" said Penny's mom. "Penny, I think your special powder has magical powers!" Penny smiled and said, "That's so cool! I'm glad it's helpful." The End.
