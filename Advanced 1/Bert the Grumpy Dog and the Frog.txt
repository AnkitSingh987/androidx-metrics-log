Title: Bert the Grumpy Dog and the Frog

Once upon a time there was a grumpy old dog named Bert. He was always grumpy and never liked to play. One day he was walking around the park, when he saw a big puddle of water. It was the perfect size for a swim! Suddenly, something came out of the water - it was a frog! "Ribbit ribbit," said the frog. Bert was scared, so he barked and barked. The frog got very scared of Bert and started to scatter away as fast as he could. Even though Bert was grumpy, he thought it looked like fun. He jumped into the puddle and started scattering around too. For the first time, Bert was happy and having fun! Finally, he knew what it felt like to play.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, wanted to play a game. They decided to play hide and seek. Tim would hide and Max would try to find him. Tim thought it would be a hard game for Max, but Max was a smart dog. Tim said to Max, "Max, you wait here and count to ten. I will go hide." Max barked and started to count. Tim ran and hid behind a big tree. Max finished counting and started to look for Tim. Max looked near the house, under the car, and behind the bushes. He could not find Tim. Max barked and said, "Tim, this game is hard!" Suddenly, Max saw Tim's foot behind the big tree. Max ran to the tree and found Tim. Tim laughed and said, "Good job, Max! You found me!" They both had fun playing the game and decided to play again.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends called Lucy and Zoey. Zoey was an animal-lover, and often wanted to introduce different animals to Lucy's world. One day, Zoey took Lucy to the park to introduce her to a cricket. It was a very pretty cricket with red wings that sparkled in the sun. Zoey asked Lucy, “Do you like the cricket?” “Yes!” Lucy replied excitedly. She loved the cricket and kept asking questions about it. She watched the cricket fly around and nearly clapped her hands with joy. Zoey asked, “Would you like to have a cricket at home?” “Yes, please!” Lucy said. Zoey smiled. She was so happy that Lucy liked the cricket and wanted to have one for herself. From then on, Lucy and the cricket were best friends and would always play together in the garden. Whenever Lucy needed a friend, the pretty cricket was there.

Lily was a nosy girl. She liked to look at everything and ask many questions. One day, she saw her mom make juice in the kitchen. She wanted to try it too. She waited until her mom went to the living room. Then she climbed on a chair and reached for the juice maker. She put some fruits inside and pushed the button. The juice maker made a loud noise and juice came out. Lily was happy. But she did not see the juice spill on the floor. She tried to grab a cup and pour some juice. She slipped on the wet floor and fell down. The cup and the juice maker crashed on the floor too. Lily was sad and hurt. Her mom heard the noise and ran to the kitchen. She saw the mess and Lily crying. She hugged Lily and said, "Are you okay, my sweetie? What were you doing?" Lily said, "I wanted to make juice like you, mom. But I did not succeed. I made a big mess. I'm sorry, mom." Her mom said, "It's okay, Lily. I know you were curious and wanted to try something new. But you should always ask me first before you use the kitchen things. They can be dangerous and you can get hurt. Next time, I will show you how to make juice safely, okay?" Lily nodded and said, "Okay, mom. I love you, mom." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lily. Now let's clean up this mess and get you some juice."

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike. He always wore a helmet to stay safe. Tim was very popular with his friends because he was nice and shared his toys. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue trying to zip her jacket. Sue was having a hard time. Tim went up to her and said, "Can I help you?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Tim zipped up Sue's jacket and they became friends. Tim, Sue, and their friends played together every day. They always wore helmets when they rode their bikes. They learned that helping others and being safe made them happy. The moral of the story is to be kind and always wear a helmet when you ride your bike.
