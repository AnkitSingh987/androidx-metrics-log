Title: The Mommy and Baby and the Present

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a baby. The mommy wanted to store something in the baby's bedroom. Every night, the mommy came in and said a very special word to the baby - "important". The baby wasn't sure what it meant, but the mommy always smiled and then went about her business. One day the baby gathered up its courage and asked the mommy what it meant. The mommy looked at the baby and said, "Well, soon you will find out. But first, let's store something in your bedroom." The mommy opened the door and revealed a huge, special box. Inside was a birthday present for the baby. The baby was so excited! The mommy had been trying to tell the baby all this time that the present was important! The baby was so thankful that the mommy had finally been able to tell it the important news. They shared a huge hug, and the baby knew the surprise was now safe and sound, stored away in its bedroom.

Tommy loves to play soccer with his friends. He wants to improve his kicks and goals. Yesterday, he practiced a lot with his dad in the park. He kicked the ball hard and fast. He scored many goals. He was very happy and proud. Today, he has a soccer game with his team. He is excited to show his skills. He puts on his soccer shirt and shoes. He runs to the field with his dad. He sees his friends and coach. They smile and cheer for him. The game starts. Tommy runs after the ball. He passes it to his friend. His friend passes it back to him. He sees the goal. He kicks the ball with all his might. The ball flies into the net. He scores! He jumps and shouts. His team and dad clap and hug him. He improved a lot. He is the best. He is very happy and proud.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved gum. He had gum every day. One day, Tim found a big gum under his bed. He was very happy. Tim took the big gum and started to chew it. But then, something unexpected happened! The gum started to cover Tim's face! Tim got angry. He tried to pull the gum off, but it was too sticky. Just when Tim thought he would be stuck with the gum forever, his dog came and licked the gum off his face. Tim was free! He was not angry anymore. Tim learned that he should be careful with gum. From that day on, he only had a little gum at a time.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom liked to take naps all day. His favorite place to sleep was on the warm floor near the big window. The sun would shine on him, making him feel cozy and happy. One day, Tom was having a nice nap on the floor. But, a little mouse named Jerry came to play. Jerry did not want to disturb the sleepy cat, but he wanted to have fun too. So, Jerry tiptoed around Tom, trying not to wake him up. But, Jerry's tail touched Tom's nose, and it made Tom wake up. Tom was not mad, but he was not sleepy anymore. So, Tom and Jerry played together on the floor. They had lots of fun and became good friends. From that day on, they played together every day, and Tom was not sleepy all the time.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, rough rock in his yard. He picked it up to examine it. The rock was not smooth like other rocks. It was very different. Tim wanted to show his mom the rock. He ran inside the house with the rock in his hands. But, he tripped and the rock hit his head. Tim cried because it hurt a little. Mom came and saw Tim crying. She hugged him and looked at his head. It was okay, just a small bump. Tim showed her the rough rock and they both smiled. They put the rock in their special box to remember the day.
