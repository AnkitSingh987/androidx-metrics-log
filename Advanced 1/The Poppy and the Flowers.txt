Title: The Poppy and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a poppy. It was big and red and it had wide petals that waved in the wind. The poppy blinked in the sunshine and it felt so happy! All around the poppy were other flowers. A yellow daisy was next to it, and there was a tall bluebell too. But none of them were as beautiful as the poppy. Suddenly, a bee flew by. It was buzzing loudly and the poppy blinked in surprise. The bee went from flower to flower, collecting sweet, sticky nectar from all of them. The poppy watched and it felt so happy. All of the flowers made the garden look so alive and the poppy enjoyed being a part of it. The poppy smiled and blinked in the sunshine.

Once there was a big farm with a broken fence. The farmer, named Tom, wanted to fix the fence. He asked his friend, Jim, to help him. Tom said, "Jim, can you help me fix the fence?" Jim said, "Yes, Tom, I will help you." Tom and Jim were very organized. They put all the tools and wood in a neat line. They worked together to fix the fence. The fence looked very nice and strong after they fixed it. The animals on the farm were very happy. They liked the new fence. The animals played near the fence and thanked Tom and Jim. Tom and Jim were proud of their work and the farm looked good again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to nap on her soft bed. She also liked to play with her toys. Her toys were always organized on her shelf. Sue was a very neat girl. One day, Sue found a long thread on her bed. She did not know where it came from. The thread made her bed look messy. Sue was sad because she liked her room to be organized. She wanted to find out where the thread came from. Sue looked all around her room. She found that the thread came from her favorite blanket. The blanket had a hole in it. Sue was worried. She did not want her blanket to get ruined. Sue asked her mom for help. Her mom fixed the hole with a needle and thread. Sue was happy again. She took a nap with her fixed blanket and felt cozy.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day long. He liked to run, jump, and bark. He was very happy. One day, Spot found a big clock. The clock went tick-tock, tick-tock. Spot did not understand what the clock was for. He tried to play with it, but it did not play back. Spot was sad. Then, Spot's friend, a little girl named Lucy, came to visit. Lucy saw the clock and said, "Spot, that is a clock. It tells us the time." Spot looked at the clock and wagged his tail. Now he understood. Spot and Lucy played together, and they had a great time.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She wore a thin scarf because it was a bit cold. Lily liked to enjoy playing with her ball in the park. She saw her friend, Tom, and said, "Hi Tom, do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to play too!" They played with the ball and had lots of fun. They threw the ball back and forth, laughing and running around. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball went too far and it fell into a big hole. Lily and Tom looked into the hole, but they could not see the ball. They tried to get the ball, but the hole was too deep. They were sad because they could not play with the ball anymore.

Once upon a time there was a generous elephant. The elephant loved to lend things to others. One day, a rabbit and a bee came to him asking for help. The bee wanted a faucet to wash his face and the rabbit wanted to get some water. The generous elephant lent them the faucet. The bee and the rabbit thanked the elephant and the bee washed his face. The rabbit filled up his bucket with water. Both the bee and the rabbit were very happy. The end.
