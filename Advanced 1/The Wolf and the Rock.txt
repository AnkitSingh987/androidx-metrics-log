Title: The Wolf and the Rock

Once there was a big wolf. The wolf was very dependable. He had many friends in the forest. They all liked him because he was always there to help them. One day, the wolf and his friends were playing. They were having so much fun. But then, the wolf saw a big rock in front of him. He did not see it in time. He stumbled on the rock and fell. His friends saw him fall and were worried. The next day, the wolf's friends wanted to help him. They remembered how dependable he was. They made a plan. They all worked together to move the big rock. The wolf was happy and said thank you. They played again, and this time, no one stumbled.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved her home very much. It was warm and cozy, and she had her own soft bed to sleep in. Every morning, Kitty would wake up and stretch her legs before starting her day. One day, while Kitty was playing outside, she saw a little bird on a tree. The bird was singing a happy song. Kitty wanted to be friends with the bird, so she stretched her neck to try and talk to the bird. The bird saw Kitty and flew down to the ground to meet her. Kitty and the bird became good friends. They played together all day long. When it was time to go home, Kitty said goodbye to her new friend. She went back to her warm and cozy home, feeling very happy. That night, she stretched out in her soft bed, thinking about her fun day with the little bird.

Once upon a time, in a small cabin, there was a broken chair. A little boy named Tim lived in the cabin with his mom and dad. Tim wanted to fix the broken chair, but he didn't know how. One sunny day, Tim's dad found a big piece of wood. He said, "Tim, let's cover the broken chair with this wood and make it new again!" Tim was very happy and helped his dad. They worked together and fixed the broken chair. Now, the cabin had a new chair for everyone to sit on. Tim, his mom, and his dad were very happy. They had fun in their cabin with the chair they fixed together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a bee named Bob. He was very bad and he had an injury. He could not fly and he needed help. Luckily, a little girl saw Bob and was very kind. She wanted to help Bob so she took him home. She talked very kindly to Bob and tried to heal his injury. The little girl sung a sweet song to Bob and fed him honey. Then she put some special medicine on his wound. She worked to heal Bob for days. Finally, Bob felt better and the girl was very excited. She held Bob in her hand, took him outside and let him go. Bob flew away and thanked the girl for helping him. He was so happy to be healed.

Tina tried on a blouse one day. It was soft and gentle against her skin and she thought it was very pretty. "Mommy, can I keep this blouse?" Tina asked. Her mom nodded and said, "Yes, but you must be gentle with it. Take good care of your blouse." Every day, Tina would try to be gentle with her new blouse. She was careful when she reached into her closet and never pulled too hard when she put it on and off. But one day, something different happened. She pulled too hard her blouse and heard a tear. She was sad and scared as she looked down and noticed that her blouse was torn. But then, Mommy came and hugged Tina. She said, "It's alright, my love. Let's try and fix your blouse." Mommy worked on the blouse and Tina watched. Soon enough, her blouse was fixed and ready to wear once again. Tina smiled and hugged her mom. From that day onward, she was extra careful and gentle with her blouse, just like her mom had said.
