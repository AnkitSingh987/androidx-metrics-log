Title: The Mummy and the Ball

Once upon a time there was a mummy, a daddy and a little girl. Every morning the mummy took the little girl to the park for a walk. One day, when they were walking in the park, the little girl found a leather ball in the grass. She picked it up and showed it to mummy. "Daddy will like this," said the little girl. Mummy smiled. "Let's take it home," she said. The little girl was so happy she ran all the way home. When she arrived, Daddy was waiting outside the house. "Daddy! I got you a ball!" said the little girl. Daddy smiled. "Thank you," he said. They took the ball inside and played for hours. Soon it was time for dinner, and the little girl had to say goodbye to her daddy. She hugged him tightly and said, "I miss you, Daddy." Daddy smiled. "I miss you too. Have a peaceful night," he said.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys. Her favorite toy was a long, flexible snake. She named him Mr. Slithers. One day, Lily wanted to play a new game with Mr. Slithers. She thought and thought, and then she had an idea. She would add a soft wrap to Mr. Slithers to make him look like a present. Lily found a pretty wrap and put it around Mr. Slithers. Now, Mr. Slithers looked like a special gift. Lily played with him all day long. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, Lily was happy she added the wrap to Mr. Slithers. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, big cat. The cat was so enormous, it was bigger than a house. The big cat liked to scatter things all over the place. It would scatter toys, food, and even leaves in the yard. One day, the enormous cat saw a group of kids having a dance. The kids were laughing and having fun. The cat wanted to join the dance, but it was afraid it would scatter the kids with its big size. The big cat sat and watched the dance from far away. It saw how much fun the kids were having. The cat decided to be brave and join the dance. It walked up to the kids and started to dance too. The kids were happy to see the enormous cat dance and did not mind the scattering. They all danced and laughed together, and they had the best time ever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cat and a dog. The cat and the dog were friends. They played together and liked to eat meat. One day, the cat found a tight box in the kitchen. The cat wanted to open the box and see what was inside. The cat tried to open the box, but it was too tight. The dog saw the cat and wanted to help. Together, they pushed the box and it opened. They were very happy to reveal what was inside the box. Inside the box, they found some meat. The cat and the dog were very happy. They shared the meat and ate it together. They played and had a fun day. From that day on, they always helped each other and shared everything they had.

Once upon a time, there was a cute dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his toy firework. He would run around and try to fit the firework into small places. One day, he found a tiny hole in the ground. Spot said, "I want to fit my firework in this hole!" He pushed and pushed, but the firework was too big for the hole. Spot's friend, Sam the cat, came by and saw Spot trying to fit the firework in the hole. Sam said, "That won't fit, Spot. Let's find a bigger hole." But Spot didn't listen. He kept trying to fit the firework in the tiny hole. He pushed so hard that the firework broke into pieces. Spot was sad. His cute firework was broken, and he couldn't play with it anymore. Sam tried to cheer him up, but Spot couldn't stop thinking about his broken firework. From that day on, Spot learned to listen to his friends and not force things into places they don't fit.
