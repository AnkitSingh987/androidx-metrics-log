Title: Lily and Ben and the Stick

Lily and Ben were hot and thirsty. They wanted to drink some juice. They saw a big cooler in the park. It had a sign that said "Free juice for everyone". They ran to the cooler and opened it. Inside the cooler, there were many bottles of juice. They looked yummy. Lily and Ben each took a bottle and opened it. They smelled the juice. It smelled bad. They tasted the juice. It tasted worse. They spit out the juice and made a face. They looked at the bottles again. They saw that the juice was old and rotten. There were bugs and mold in the juice. Someone had put the bad juice in the cooler to trick people. Lily and Ben felt sick and sad. They threw away the bottles and closed the cooler. They saw a man walking by. He had a big smile and a bag. He was the one who had put the bad juice in the cooler. He wanted to see people drink the bad juice and laugh at them. He was a mean and nasty man. He did not care about people. He was a poor man, not in money, but in heart. Lily and Ben had an idea. They found a stick and some mud. They stirred the mud with the stick until it was thick and gooey. They waited for the man to come back to the cooler. When he came back, he opened the cooler and reached for a bottle. Lily and Ben threw the mud at him. The mud hit him in the face and covered his clothes. He screamed and dropped the bottle. He ran away, looking like a dirty pig. Lily and Ben laughed and cheered. They had taught the man a lesson. They hoped he would never do such a mean thing again. They felt happy and proud. They went to find some good juice and some good friends. They had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Tim's mom made some delicious cookies for him and his friends. She put them in a safe place so they could eat them later. She told Tim, "I permit you to eat the cookies after you play outside." Tim and his friends played outside until they were tired. They remembered the delicious cookies and went inside to eat them. They found the safe place where the cookies were and ate them all up. The cookies made them happy and they had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a hairy dog named Toby. Toby loved to play outside. One day, during spring, Toby found a big puddle. He jumped in and got very wet and muddy. Toby's friend, a little boy named Tim, saw the muddy dog. Tim wanted to help Toby get clean. He got a cloth and started to wipe the mud off Toby. It took a long time, but Tim did not give up. After Toby was clean, they played together in the sun. They were happy and had fun. The moral of the story is to always help your friends and never give up.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends called Mark and Tony. They liked to do lots of fun things together. One day, Tony asked Mark to watch a video with him. Mark thought it looked a bit crazy, but he said yes anyway. When they watched the video, they found out it was much too scary. It gave them both a fright. Mark and Tony knew that it was important to be careful about what they watched. The moral of the story is that one should only watch nice and appropriate videos that are not too scary.

One day, a little girl named Lisa went for a walk. She saw a big, pretty rainbow in the sky. She wanted to hug the rainbow, but it was too high up. Lisa felt sad because she could not hug the rainbow. As Lisa walked, she heard a loud noise. It was a big bird with many colors. The bird looked like the rainbow! Lisa was so happy to see the bird. She asked the bird if she could hug it. The bird said yes, and Lisa hugged the bird. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to change! It turned into a real rainbow, just for Lisa to hug. Lisa hugged the rainbow, and it was the best hug ever. The rainbow then went back into the sky, but Lisa knew she would always remember her special hug.
