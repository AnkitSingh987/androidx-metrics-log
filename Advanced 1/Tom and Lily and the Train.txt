Title: Tom and Lily and the Train

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big train set with tracks, cars, and a station. They always hoped to get more train pieces for their birthdays or holidays. One day, their mom said they had to clean their room before they could play with the train. Tom and Lily did not like to clean. They had many objects on the floor, like books, clothes, and blocks. They did not know where to put them. They were not organized. "Let's just hide them under the bed," Tom said. "Then mom won't see them and we can play with the train." "No, that's not a good idea," Lily said. "Mom will find out and be mad. And we won't be able to find our things later. Let's put them in the right places, like the bookshelf, the closet, and the toy box." Tom did not listen to Lily. He pushed all the objects under the bed and ran to the train set. Lily sighed and started to put the objects in the right places. She hoped Tom would help her soon. But Tom did not help Lily. He played with the train by himself. He made the train go fast and loud. He pretended to be the conductor and the passengers. He had fun. Then he heard a loud crash. He looked under the bed and saw that the objects had fallen out. They were everywhere. Some of them had broken. He saw his mom coming to the door. He was scared. He wished he had listened to Lily. He wished he had been organized. He hoped mom would not be too angry. He learned a lesson.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends. They were both 3 years old. One day, they decided to go explore an old, hidden cave that they had seen earlier. So, they went to the cave. When they reached the entrance, it was too dark for them to go inside. So, one of they said: "We must manage to find a way to get some light in here". Suddenly, the other one noticed something in his pocket. It was a flashlight! He smiled, and said: "Look! We can use this!". They then entered the cave with the flashlight on. Everything looked magical and mysterious. But then, when they went forward, one of them stepped on something. It was a spoiled message with a secret code. They were surprised! They tried to manage to decipher the code, but they couldn't. So, they decided to come back another day, and kept the message with them. The end.

Lily and Max were twins who loved to play outside. They had a big yard with a swing, a slide and a sandbox. But their favorite thing was to ride their bikes on the road. The road was not busy, and their mom watched them from the porch. One day, they saw a new friend on the road. It was a small, brown dog with floppy ears and a wagging tail. It looked adorable and friendly. Lily and Max stopped their bikes and got off. "Hi, doggy! What is your name?" Lily asked, bending down to pet the dog. The dog licked her hand and barked happily. It seemed to like Lily and Max. "Can we play with you, doggy?" Max asked, throwing a stick for the dog to fetch. The dog ran after the stick and brought it back to Max. It jumped and sprang around, wanting more. Lily and Max laughed and played with the dog for a long time. "Look, mom! We made a new friend!" Lily called to their mom, who smiled and waved. "That's wonderful, sweeties! But remember to be careful and gentle with the dog. And don't go too far on the road, okay?" their mom said. "Okay, mom!" Lily and Max said. They hugged the dog and gave it some water and a biscuit. They hoped the dog would come back and play with them again. They felt happy and lucky to have such an adorable friend.

Once upon a time, in the deep of the swamp, there lived a little creature called Pete. Pete was very creative, and liked to shoot pebbles at things. One day, he thought of a new way to shoot his pebbles. He put his pebbles in a circle and shot them out one by one. As they spun round and round, they made a whirly, whizzing noise that filled the swamp. When Pete was finished, he heard a voice from the trees. It was a mean voice, and it shouted, "I said stop shooting those pebbles!" It was the Swamp Creature! Pete didn't know what to do. He looked around for a place to hide, but it was too late. The Swamp Creature was already here. Pete was scared, but he remembered his creative plan. He quickly gathered his pebbles and shot them out again, making a giant, whirly circle. The Swamp Creature was so confused, it couldn't find Pete! The Swamp Creature surrendered and said, "You're very creative Pete. I was wrong, keep shooting your pebbles." From that day on, Pete and the Swamp Creature were friends. They shared the swamp together and enjoyed watching Pete's creative pebble shooting.
