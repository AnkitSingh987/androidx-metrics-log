Title: Tim the Bird and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a small cage. The cage was his home, but he did not like it. He wanted to fly and see the world. Tim wanted to escape. One day, Tim saw that his cage door was open just a little bit. He thought, "This is my chance to escape!" So, he pushed the door with his beak. But, the door did not open more. Tim did not give up. He pushed and pushed until he heard a "crack". Tim had break the door! Now, Tim was free to fly and see the world. But, he saw a big cat outside his cage. The cat wanted to catch Tim. Tim was scared, but he was also brave. He flew high, and the cat could not catch him. Tim had his escape, and he was happy to fly in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion who liked to roar. The lion lived in a big forest with many friends. They played all day, and the lion was very happy. One day, the lion found a card on the ground. It was a special card with a picture of a bird. The lion thought it was very pretty. He wanted to make more cards for his friends, but he did not know how. The lion asked his friend, the monkey, for help. The monkey was very creative. Together, they made many cards with pictures of animals and trees. The lion roared with happiness. His friends loved the cards and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little otter. The otter was restless. He did not like to sit still. He always wanted to play and swim. One day, the otter went too far from his family. He swam and swam, but he could not find them. The otter started to cry. He was very sad and scared. A big fish saw the crying otter. The fish wanted to help, but he did not know how. The otter could not find his family and cried all day. The otter was alone and it was a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a wise old cat named Whiskers. Whiskers loved to sit in the sun and watch the birds fly by. One day, Whiskers saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad because she lost her toy. Whiskers went up to Lily and said, "Do not be sad. I promise to help you find your toy." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Whiskers." They looked everywhere, under the trees and behind the bushes. At last, they found the toy under a big rock. Lily was so happy and jumped with joy. She said, "Whiskers, you are the best cat ever!" Whiskers had a surprise for Lily. He gave her a pretty flower to make her even happier. They both laughed and played together in the sun, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk and explore. One day, while walking, he found a big box. The box was original, not like other boxes he had seen before. Tim called his friend, Sue. "Sue, come see the box I found!" he said. Sue came running to see the box. "Wow, it's an original box!" she said. They decided to open the box together. Inside the box, they found many colorful toys. Tim and Sue played with the toys all day, having lots of fun. They were happy they found the original box during their walk. And they promised to walk and explore together every day.

One day, a big dog went for a walk in the cold. He saw a big structure with thick walls. He wanted to go inside to get warm. The dog went into the structure and saw a small cat. The cat was shivering because it was so cold. The dog felt bad for the cat. The dog and the cat became friends. They played together in the structure with the thick walls. They were both warm and happy. They did not shiver anymore.
