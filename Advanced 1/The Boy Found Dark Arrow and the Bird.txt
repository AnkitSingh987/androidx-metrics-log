Title: The Boy Found Dark Arrow and the Bird

One day, a boy found a dark arrow in the woods. He did not know what it was. He showed it to his friend, the girl. "Look what I found!" he said. The girl looked at the arrow and thought it was cool. They played with the arrow, but then they began to fight. The boy wanted the arrow, and the girl wanted it too. They did not want to share. The boy said, "I found it, so it is mine!" The girl said, "But I want it too!" As they fought, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird took the dark arrow from them. The boy and the girl were surprised. They looked at each other and said, "We should have shared the arrow." Now, the dark arrow was gone. The boy and the girl were sad, but they learned to share. They promised to remain friends and share their toys. They played together and were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a driver who drove a car. The car was bright and the driver felt happy when he drove it. One day, something strange happened. When the driver stopped the car and stepped out, the car started to glow. The driver was scared, but he was also curious. He stepped back and looked at the glowing car. Suddenly, the driver felt guilty. He knew he had done something wrong, but he couldn't remember what it was. He stepped back even further and kept watching the car. The driver was so scared that he didn't want to touch the car. He stayed back, watching it, until he was sure the car had stopped glowing. After it was done, the driver was relieved. He had no idea what had happened, but he was very happy that the car had stopped glowing.

Once upon a time there were two little friends, one was a bear and one was a fox. They were going on a fun adventure and they decided to take a nap first. They decided to take a nap on separate sides of a tree. When the two friends woke up, they noticed something different. Instead of being side by side like when they went to sleep, the two friends had made a turn around the tree and were now on the opposite side of the tree from when they had gone to sleep. The friends were surprised and wondered what had happened. Then one of the little friends said: “This is a good lesson for us, that even when we take a nap we need to pay attention to where we are and take turns around the tree if we need to move somewhere else.” The friends agreed and ended up having a great adventure, never forgetting their lesson of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to dance. She would dance in her room, in the kitchen, and even in the park. She was a very fit girl because she danced all the time. One day, Mia went to school. She was a new pupil in her class. The teacher said, "Hello, Mia! We are happy to have you here. Today, we will learn to dance!" Mia was so excited. She couldn't wait to dance with her new friends. Mia and her friends stood in a circle. They held hands and started to dance. They danced and laughed. Mia said, "I love to dance with all of you!" Her new friends smiled and said, "We love to dance with you too, Mia!" And they all danced happily together.

One day, a chubby cat named Tom went for a march. He liked to walk and see the world. The sun was hot, and the sky was blue. Tom was happy to be outside. As Tom walked, he saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. Tom went up the tree and looked around. From up high, he saw something shiny on the ground. He wanted to know what it was, so he went back down. Tom went to the shiny thing and saw that it was a pair of scissors. He knew that scissors could cut things. Tom had an idea. He found a long leaf and cut it with the scissors. Suddenly, the leaf turned into a beautiful butterfly! Tom was very surprised, but happy. From that day on, Tom and the butterfly were the best of friends.
