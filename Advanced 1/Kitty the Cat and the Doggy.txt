Title: Kitty the Cat and the Doggy

One day, a small cat named Kitty went to a big hotel with her family. Kitty was scared because she had never been to a hotel before. She did not know what to expect. In the hotel, Kitty met a nice dog named Doggy. Doggy said, "Don't be scared, Kitty. I come to this hotel all the time. Let's play together!" Kitty felt better and started to play with Doggy. They played hide and seek, and Kitty was very good at hiding. But then, Kitty saw a big, dark room. She was scared again, but she wanted to hide there. Doggy said, "Don't go in there, Kitty! It is dark and scary." Kitty did not listen and went into the dark room. She lay down and hid under a big chair. Doggy was worried about Kitty. He went into the dark room to find her. He said, "Kitty, where are you?" Kitty heard Doggy's voice and came out from under the chair. She was not scared anymore because Doggy was with her. Together, Kitty and Doggy left the dark room and went back to their families. They had a great time at the hotel and were not scared anymore. They became best friends and promised to play together again soon.

Lila and Sam were best friends. They liked to play in the park and look for shiny things. One day, they found a big gem in the grass. It was red and sparkly. "Wow, look at this!" Lila said. "It is so pretty!" "Can I see it?" Sam asked. "Sure, but you have to promise to give it back," Lila said. "I promise," Sam said. He took the gem and held it in his hand. He liked how it felt and how it glowed in the sun. They played with the gem for a while, then it was time to go home. Lila reached out her hand and said, "Can I have the gem back, please?" Sam hesitated. He wanted to keep the gem for himself. He thought of a lie. "I lost it," he said. "It fell out of my hand and rolled away. I'm sorry." Lila was sad and angry. She knew Sam was lying. She saw the gem in his pocket. She felt betrayed by her friend. She said, "You are not sorry. You are not a good friend. You broke your promise. I don't want to play with you anymore." She ran away, crying. Sam felt guilty and ashamed. He realized he had hurt Lila and lost her trust. He wished he had kept his promise and given the gem back. He learned that being greedy and dishonest was not worth losing a friend. He decided to say sorry to Lila and give her the gem back. He hoped she would forgive him and be his friend again. He learned that a promise is a big word that means you have to do what you say.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a gifted bunny named Bob. Bob could jump very high. One day, Bob and his friends decided to go to a camp. They were very happy. At the camp, they played many fun games. But then, a big, mean fox came. The fox wanted to eat them. Bob's friend, Sam the squirrel, said, "We need to run away!" But Bob had a better idea. Bob said, "I will jump high and scare the fox away." So, he jumped very high, and the fox got scared. The fox ran away, and all the friends were safe. They were very happy and played more games at the camp.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had a long tail that she liked to swish around. She lived in a big, pretty house with her best friend, a small mouse named Squeaky. One day, Fluffy and Squeaky found a big stick. They wanted to use the stick to make a bridge over a small puddle. Fluffy used her strong tail to support one end of the stick, while Squeaky held up the other end with his tiny paws. Together, they made a great bridge for all their animal friends to cross the puddle. Fluffy and Squeaky were very happy to help their friends, and everyone had a fun day playing in the sun.
