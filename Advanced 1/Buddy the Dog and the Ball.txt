Title: Buddy the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play and run around with his friends. One day, Buddy found a big steel ball in the park. He was very excited to play with it. Buddy went to his friend, the cat named Whiskers, and said, "I found a big steel ball! Let's play with it!" Whiskers was happy to play with Buddy and the ball. They rolled the ball around and had lots of fun together. That night, Buddy had a dream. In his dream, he and Whiskers were playing with the big steel ball in a land full of toys and treats. Buddy was so happy in his dream that he wagged his tail while he slept. When he woke up, he told Whiskers about his dream, and they both hoped that they could have more fun adventures together.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. They can make towers, houses, cars and animals with their blocks. One day, they decide to make a big castle with their blocks. They work hard and have fun. They use red blocks for the walls, blue blocks for the windows, yellow blocks for the doors and green blocks for the roof. They also make a flag with a white block and a stick. "Look at our castle!" Lily says. "It is so pretty and strong!" "Yes, it is!" Ben agrees. "We are very good at building!" They enjoy their castle for a while. Then they hear a noise. It is Max, the big boy from next door. He is mean and likes to break things. He sees their castle and smiles. "Hello, little ones," he says. "What a nice castle you have. Can I play with you?" Lily and Ben do not like Max. They know he wants to ruin their castle. They shake their heads. "No, you can't," Lily says. "This is our castle. Go away!" Max gets angry. He pushes Lily and Ben aside. He kicks their castle and knocks it down. He laughs and runs away. Lily and Ben are sad and mad. They look at their broken castle. They feel a sour taste in their mouths. "That was not nice!" Ben says. "He is a bad boy!" "He is!" Lily says. "He is a bully!" They hug each other and cry. They do not want to play with blocks anymore. They want to tell their moms what happened. They hope Max will get in trouble. They hope they can make a new castle someday.

Tommy liked to play doctor with his toys. He had a white coat, a stethoscope, and a thermometer. He would introduce himself to his patients and check their ears, their noses, and their mouths. He would say, "I am Doctor Tommy. I will make you feel better." One day, he found a new toy in his room. It was a big, green dinosaur with sharp teeth and a long tail. Tommy was curious and excited. He wanted to play doctor with the dinosaur. He put on his white coat and grabbed his thermometer. He said, "Hello, dinosaur. I am Doctor Tommy. I will make you feel better." He walked towards the dinosaur and tried to put the thermometer in its mouth. But the dinosaur was not a toy. It was a powerful and hungry dinosaur. It roared and snapped at Tommy. Tommy was scared and dropped the thermometer. He ran away and hid under his bed. He heard the dinosaur stomping and growling in his room. Tommy wished he had listened to his mom. She had told him not to touch the dinosaur. She had said it was a special gift for his dad, who loved dinosaurs. She had said it was a remote-controlled dinosaur that could move and make noises. She had said it was not a toy for little boys. Tommy learned his lesson. He never played doctor with the dinosaur again. He only played doctor with his toys that were nice and friendly. He was glad that his mom and dad came to his room and turned off the dinosaur. He hugged them and said, "I am sorry, mom and dad. I will not touch the dinosaur again. I love you."
