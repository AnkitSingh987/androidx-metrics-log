Title: Suzy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a very special panda called Suzy. She lived in the jungle with lots of other animals. Suzy was always very well-behaved. She liked to be helpful - helping her friends and looking after the jungle. One day, Suzy decided to do something different. She was feeling very brave, so she decided to climb to the top of the tallest tree in the jungle. Everyone was watching her to see if she could do it. Suzy was brilliant. She managed to make it all the way to the top with no problems. At the top, Suzy saw the most beautiful view. The whole jungle looked like a giant sea of green. Everybody cheered for her when she came back down. From then on, Suzy was even more popular in the jungle. Everyone thought she was so brave and so brilliant. And Suzy was proud that she had been so well-behaved.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who lived in a house full of blueberries. Every day he would go out and pick some blueberries, then come back and separate them into small piles. One day, when the old man was away picking blueberries, a little three-year-old girl came to the house. She saw all the blueberries and as she was very hungry she started to eat them. She ate and ate until there were no blueberries left. When the old man came back he found that all the blueberries were gone. The old man was very sad so he called for the little girl. When he saw her he said, “Why did you eat all the blueberries?” The little girl said, “I was hungry and I wanted to eat the blueberries.” The old man said, “But now I can’t separate them into small piles. What am I going to do?” The little girl thought for a minute. Then she said, “Maybe we can pick some more blueberries and you can separate them into small piles again.” The old man smiled and said, “That’s a great idea! Let’s go!” So they both went out and picked some more blueberries. When they got back they separated the blueberries into small piles and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a rough toy. He liked to play with it all day. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, we need to store your toy in the case." Tim didn't want to put his toy in the case. He said, "No, Mom! I want to play with my toy!" But his mom said, "Tim, we need to go to the store. You can play with your toy later." So, Tim put his rough toy in the case. They went to the store. When they came back, Tim wanted to play with his toy again. But when he opened the case, his toy was gone. Tim was very sad, and he cried. His mom hugged him, but they never found his toy again.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet forest. In the forest lived a big, selfish bear. He did not like to share anything with his friends. One day, a little bird came to the forest. The bird wanted to play with the bear. The bird tried to urge the bear to play. The bear said, "No, I do not want to play." The bird felt sad but did not give up. The bird knew something was going to happen that would make the bear want to play. One sunny day, the bear found a big, red ball. The bear wanted to play with the ball, but he could not play alone. The bear thought about the little bird. He went to find the bird and asked, "Will you play with me?" The bird was very happy and said, "Yes, I will play with you." They played together and had lots of fun. The bear learned to share and was not selfish anymore.

One day, a cow named Bessie went for a walk. Bessie saw her friend, a fierce dog named Max. Max liked to race with Bessie. "Let's race!" said Max. Bessie said, "Yes, let's race to the big tree!" They started to race. Bessie ran fast, but Max ran faster. They were almost at the big tree when something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and picked up Bessie! Bessie was scared. Max stopped running and barked at the bird. The bird heard Max and put Bessie down. Bessie was happy to be back on the ground. Max said, "I am glad you are safe, Bessie." Bessie said, "Thank you, Max. You are a good friend." They did not finish the race, but they were happy to be together.
