Title: Lucy the Girl and the Leaves

One day, a little girl named Lucy went outside to play. She saw a rake lying on the ground. Lucy picked up the rake and started to gather leaves into a big pile. She had a lot of fun making the pile bigger and bigger. Suddenly, a squirrel popped out of the leaves. "Hi, I'm Sam!" said the squirrel. Lucy was surprised but happy to meet a new friend. "Hi, I'm Lucy. Do you want to play with me?" she asked Sam. The squirrel nodded, and they played together with the leaves and the rake. After playing, Lucy and Sam went inside the house. Lucy's mom was baking tasty cookies. The smell was so good! Lucy asked her mom if they could have some cookies. Her mom said yes, and gave them each a warm, tasty cookie. They both loved the cookies and had a great day together.

Lily likes to draw with her crayons. She draws flowers and stars and cats. She is very happy when she draws. She wants to show her mom what she draws. She takes her paper and runs to the kitchen. But on the way, she trips on a toy. She falls and hurts her knee. She cries and drops her paper. The paper lands on the floor. The dog sees the paper and thinks it is a toy. He runs to the paper and bites it. He tears it with his teeth. Lily sees the dog and screams. She is very sad and angry. She loves her paper. She wants her mom. Mom hears Lily and comes to see what is wrong. She sees Lily on the floor, the dog with the paper, and the crayons everywhere. She hugs Lily and says sorry. She takes the paper from the dog and looks at it. It is very ugly. It has holes and dirt and dog spit. It does not look like flowers and stars and cats. It looks like a mess. Mom says it is okay. She says they can make a new paper. She says they can print a picture from the computer. She says they can draw on it with crayons. Lily stops crying and smiles. She likes the idea. She says thank you to mom. She says sorry to the dog. She says she still loves him. She says she will be more careful with her toys. She says she will make a beautiful paper. She says she will show it to mom. Mom says she loves Lily. She says she is proud of her. She says they will have fun. They go to the computer and print a picture. They take the crayons and draw on it. They make a beautiful paper. They are very happy.

Once upon a time, there were two old friends, Jack and Jacky. One day, Jack was walking under a mighty tree and said to Jacky, "Let's play today". The sky suddenly darkened, and it started to rain. Jack and Jacky had to find shelter quickly. Jack looked around and said "Look! There's an old hut over here." Jacky nodded and they ran towards the hut. When they reached the hut, they were relieved - it was dry inside. They could hear the sound of the rain outside. Jack said, "We are lucky that we got here in time!" "Yes," agreed Jacky, "the old hut is mighty strong". And they had a great time playing their games until the rain stopped.

Once upon a time, a playful dog named Max lived in a small house. Max loved to run, jump, and play all day long. One day, Max saw a red wagon in the yard. He was very curious and wanted to play with it. Max decided to enter the wagon and started to push it. The wagon began to move and Max was having a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The wagon started to move very fast and Max couldn't stop it. The wagon finally stopped by a big tree. Max was scared, but then he saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad because her wagon was lost. Max realized that the wagon belonged to Lily. He decided to give the wagon back to her. Lily was so happy and thanked Max. They became best friends and played together every day. The moral of the story is that when we do good things for others, we make new friends and everyone is happy.
