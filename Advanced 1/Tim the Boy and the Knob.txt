Title: Tim the Boy and the Knob

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy named Mr. Knob. Mr. Knob was a filthy toy because Tim played with him all the time. Tim loved Mr. Knob very much. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw Mr. Knob and started to tease Tim. Sam said, "Your toy is so filthy! It's yucky!" Tim felt sad and angry. He didn't want Sam to tease him or Mr. Knob. Tim got very mad and threw Mr. Knob at Sam. The toy hit Sam in the face, and he started to cry. Tim's mom heard the crying and came into the room. She saw what happened and was very upset. Tim got in big trouble, and Sam had to go home. Tim felt bad and wished he hadn't thrown Mr. Knob.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy dog named Spot. Spot loved to jog in the park. One day, he saw a big box near a tree. Spot went to the box and sniffed it. Inside the box, he found a guitar. Spot did not know what it was, but he knew it was special. He took the guitar and ran back home. When he got home, Spot showed the guitar to his owner, Tim. Tim was so happy to see the guitar! He played a fun song, and Spot danced. From that day on, they played music and jogged in the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a poor girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lucy loved to play and laugh with her friends. One day, Lucy's mom asked her to go to the store. "Lucy, can you bring some sugar home for me?" she asked. Lucy said, "Yes, mom!" and went to the store. At the store, Lucy saw a small cat. The cat was sad and hungry. Lucy decided to help the cat. She kneeled down and gave the cat some sugar. The cat ate the sugar and smiled. "Thank you, Lucy!" said the cat. They became friends and went home together. Lucy's mom was happy to see the cat and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a sleepy dog named Bobo was walking in the park. He saw a big, black rock on the ground. Bobo did not know it was coal. He thought it was a toy to play with. So, he picked it up with his mouth and took it home. At home, Bobo showed the coal to his friend, a little bird named Lulu. Lulu was very smart. She knew it was not a toy, but coal. She said, "Bobo, please put the coal in the yard. We can use it later to make a fire and keep warm at night." Bobo did not understand, but he listened to Lulu. He put the coal in the yard and forgot about it. A few days later, it was very cold and windy. Bobo and Lulu were shivering in their home. Lulu remembered the coal and said, "Bobo, please bring the coal inside. We can use it to make a fire and keep warm." Bobo brought the coal inside, and Lulu showed him how to make a fire. The fire made their home warm and cozy. Bobo and Lulu were happy and thankful for the coal. They learned that sometimes things that seem unimportant can be very helpful later.

Timmy was a curious 3 year old who had lots of ideas. He loved looking at the world and thinking up all kinds of plans. One day he was looking around his house and noticed a spoon on the kitchen counter. Timmy had a great idea that he wanted to share with his mommy. He ran over to her, practically bursting with excitement. "Mommy! I've got an original plan," he said. "What is it, sweetheart?" she asked, smiling widely. Timmy held up the spoon. "I'm gonna make a boat with this spoon. I can use it to float around the kitchen floor!" he exclaimed. His mommy smiled and said, "That's a great plan, Timmy! Let's go find some other things to make our boat with." Together, Timmy and his mommy went around the house to find pieces for their boat. Pretty soon they had everything that was needed to make the boat. Timmy and his mommy worked together and made the boat out of the spoon and other pieces. Together, they sailed the boat through the kitchen and had so much fun. Timmy was proud of his original plan and just knew he was gonna have a lot of fun and come up with new plans in the future.
