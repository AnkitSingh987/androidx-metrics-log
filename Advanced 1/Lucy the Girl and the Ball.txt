Title: Lucy the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She loved to play with clay. One day, her mom bought her an expensive clay set. Lucy was very happy. Lucy's friend, Tom, came over to play. They both sat down and started to play with the clay. Lucy said, "Let's squeeze the clay and make a big ball!" Tom agreed and they both squeezed the clay together. As they squeezed the clay, something unexpected happened. The clay started to grow bigger and bigger! Lucy and Tom were surprised. They kept squeezing the clay and it grew so big that it filled the whole room. They laughed and had lots of fun with the giant clay ball.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit who loved to design things. He would spend his days with his materials making fun and beautiful creations. Then one day, something changed. He became very sad. The rabbit thought he couldn't design anything ever again when his best friend, a bee, came to check on him. The bee asked, "Why are you so sad?" The rabbit replied, "I don't know if I can design anything anymore." The bee smiled and said, "I can help you with that. Let's design something together. You can use your materials and I will cheer you on." The rabbit's face lit up as he got to work. Soon enough, the rabbit had designed the perfect thing. The rabbit was happy again and thanked the bee for helping him. They celebrated by designing something even more beautiful and enjoyed the rest of their day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very careless. He would forget things all the time. One day, the weather was very nice. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim's mom said, "Tim, remember to take your hat when you go out to play." Tim said, "Okay, Mom!" But when he went out, he did not take his hat. He was too excited to play with his friends. They played and played, but Tim started to feel hot. The sun was very strong, and he had forgotten his hat. Tim's face turned red, and he felt very sick. His friends took him home, and his mom was very sad. Tim learned that he should not be careless and always remember what his mom says.

One day, a zebra was very sad. It started to rain, and the zebra did not like rain. He wanted to find a warm place to hide from the rain. He looked around and saw a big tree. The zebra went under the tree to stay dry. But, there was a problem. A big lion was under the tree too. The lion was not nice. He did not want to share the warm, dry place with the zebra. The zebra was scared, but he did not want to be wet and cold. The zebra had an idea. He asked the lion to play a game. The lion liked games. They played a fun game together. The lion was happy and let the zebra stay under the tree. They both stayed warm and dry, and they became friends.

Tom and Mia like to play with paper and scissors. They cut out shapes and make pictures. One day, they want to make a big city with many buildings. They cut out squares and rectangles and glue them together. They make some buildings high and some buildings low. They are very proud of their city. But then, their baby brother Sam comes into the room. He sees the paper city and thinks it is fun. He grabs the scissors and starts to cut the buildings. He cuts them into small pieces and throws them in the air. He laughs and claps his hands. He does not know he is making a mess. Tom and Mia are very angry. They see their city ruined by Sam. They shout at him and try to take the scissors away. But Sam does not want to stop. He cries and kicks and holds the scissors tight. He cuts his finger and it hurts. He drops the scissors and starts to wail. Mom hears the noise and comes into the room. She sees the paper city destroyed and Sam bleeding. She is very worried and sad. She picks up Sam and puts a band-aid on his finger. She hugs him and tells him it is okay. She tells Tom and Mia that Sam did not mean to hurt their city. He is too little to understand. She asks them to forgive him and share their toys. Tom and Mia feel sorry for Sam. They see his finger and his tears. They hug him and tell him they love him. They say they will make a new city with him. They help Mom clean up the paper and find the scissors. They decide to put the scissors away until Sam is older. They learn to be careful and kind.
