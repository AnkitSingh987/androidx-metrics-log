Title: The Garden and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, there were many plants. Some plants were spicy. People did not want to eat the spicy plants. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the garden. He wanted to play. Tim saw the spicy plants. He thought they were pretty. Tim did not know they were spicy. He touched the plants. Then, he touched his mouth. "Oh no!" said Tim. "My mouth is hot!" Tim ran to his mom. She knew what to do. She gave him a drink. The drink was magic. It could change things. Tim drank the magic drink. His mouth was not hot anymore. The spicy plants changed too. They were not spicy now. They were sweet. The garden was now a sweet garden. Everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a small hotel in a big town. People loved the hotel because it was dependable. They knew they could always come and stay there. One day, a little dog came to the hotel. He was very cold and his body trembled. The hotel owner saw the dog and said, "Oh, poor dog! You can stay in my hotel to get warm." The little dog wagged his tail and went inside. The next day, a big storm came to the town. The wind blew hard and the rain fell down. The people in the town were scared. But the little dog was not scared. He went outside and barked at the storm. The storm went away and the sun came out. The people were so happy and thanked the little dog. The moral of the story is that even the smallest can be brave and make a big difference.

Once upon a time there was a big family picnic. All the family were together playing and having a good time. There were two children; Joe and Amanda. Joe was three years old and Amanda was five. Joe was excited for his turn on the swings, but Amanda wanted to go first. Joe could not wait any longer so he decided to get on the swing. "Wait, Joe," said Amanda, "You'll have to delay until I'm finished." Joe was very quiet and didn't say anything. When it was Joe's turn to go on the swings he was very excited. He jumped in and held on tight. He had so much fun that he wanted to stay on the swings a bit longer than the others. But the adults were getting a bit sour. "Joe, wait!" said his father. Joe was sad because he had to get down. The moral of the story is that good things come to those who wait. Joe learned that patience is important. If he had just waited his turn, he would have been able to enjoy the fun for a bit longer.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Amy loved to play dress-up. She had a big box full of costumes. Her favorite costume was a superhero cape. Amy liked to put on her cape and pretend to fly. One sunny day, Amy went to the park with her mom. She wore her superhero cape. At the park, Amy saw a little boy who looked frightened. He was watching a big dog running around. The dog barked loudly, and the boy was scared. Amy wanted to help the frightened boy. She put on her superhero cape and walked up to him. "Don't be scared," she said. "I am a superhero, and I will watch the dog for you." The boy smiled, and they played together. The boy was not frightened anymore, thanks to Amy and her costume.

One day, a pink bug went for a walk. It saw a big tree and wanted to play. The bug saw a cobweb on the tree. The bug said, "Hello, cobweb! Can I play with you?" The cobweb said, "Yes, you can play with me, but be careful not to break me." The pink bug played on the cobweb, but it was too heavy. The cobweb split in two! The pink bug was sad and said, "I'm sorry, cobweb. I didn't mean to break you." Just then, a small spider came down from the tree. The spider said, "Don't worry, I can fix the cobweb." The spider fixed the cobweb and made it even bigger and stronger. The pink bug and the spider became friends and played on the cobweb together. From that day on, they had lots of fun and took care of each other.
