Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Ball

One day, a girl named Lily went to play outside. It was very cold and icy. She saw a cane on the ground. Lily picked up the cane and started to draw in the snow. She wanted to design a big snowman. Lily worked hard to design her snowman. She used the cane to make a big round ball. Then she made a smaller ball for the head. She found two sticks for arms and a hat for its head. Lily was very happy with her snowman. But then, something unexpected happened! The snowman started to move! It picked up the cane and waved it at Lily. The snowman was alive! Lily was very surprised but also happy. Now, she had a new friend to play with in the snow.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a flexible branch. Tim saw a pretty cone under the tree. He wanted to get the cone, but it was too high for him to reach. Tim asked his mom, "Can you help me get the cone?" His mom tried to reach for the cone, but she was not tall enough. She had an idea. She told Tim to jump and try to touch the flexible branch. Tim jumped and touched the branch. As he touched it, he started to fall. But something unexpected happened! A big, friendly bird caught Tim and helped him get the cone. Tim said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird smiled and flew away. Tim and his mom went home, happy with their new cone.

Once upon a time, there was a big city. In this city, there lived a nice dog. This dog was very obedient. He always listened to his family. One day, the dog found a pretty thing. He knew it was special. He wanted to show his family the value of the thing he found. So, he took the pretty thing to his family. The family was very happy. They thanked the obedient dog for showing them the value of the pretty thing. The dog was happy too. They all lived happily in the big city.

One day, a charming dog named Spot saw a photo on the wall. It was a photo of a big, tasty bone. Spot wanted the bone in the photo very much. He tried to get it, but he could not. Spot was sad. Spot's friend, a cat named Whiskers, saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Spot?" Spot said, "I want the bone in the photo, but I can't have it." Whiskers thought for a moment and had an idea. Whiskers told Spot, "Let's make a sign that says 'We want a bone!'" They made a big sign with crayons and paper. They showed the sign to their owner, who saw how much they wanted a bone. Their owner went to the store and bought a big, tasty bone for Spot. Spot and Whiskers were very happy and played together all day.

One day, Anna and Ben were playing together. Ben had a special card. It was different from all other cards. Anna wanted to share it too. “Can I have a turn?” asked Anna. Ben shook his head. “No. It’s mine.” Anna was sad. She looked at the card. Then, she reached out to take it. Ben pulled the card away. “No. I said it’s mine!” Anna started to cry. They fought and Anna grabbed the card. Ben was mad. He said, “That’s not fair! Give it back!” Anna was scared. She ran away with the card. Even though she was afraid, she kept running. Ben never saw the card again.

Once upon a time a police was protecting a high place. It was very cold outside and the police was shivering in the wind. One day, a bad person came along. The bad person was looking for trouble and the police noticed him. The police said, "You must go away now." But the bad person said, "No, I won't go away." The police was starting to get scared. They said in a loud voice, "Go away now or I'll have to protect this place!" But the bad person didn't listen. Then the police grabbed their gun and the bad person ran away. Finally, the police thought they were safe. But the bad person came back with more bad people. They were all armed and were bigger than the police. The police tried to protect the high place but it was too late. The bad people broke in and destroyed everything. The police was very sad. They always wanted to protect the high place but they had failed.
