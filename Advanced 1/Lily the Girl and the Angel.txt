Title: Lily the Girl and the Angel

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She had a toy angel that her mom gave her. The angel was mighty and had big wings. Lily loved her angel very much. One day, her friend Tim came over to play. They played with blocks and cars. Then, Lily showed Tim her mighty angel. "Look, this is my angel. It can fly!" she said. Tim looked at the angel and said, "Wow! Can it remind us to be good?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, it can!" Later, they went outside to play. They forgot about the angel. They played and played until it was time for Tim to go home. When Lily went back to her room, she saw the mighty angel on her bed. It reminded her to be a good friend and share her toys. From that day on, Lily always kept her mighty angel close. It reminded her to be good and kind to her friends. And every time she played with Tim, they both remembered to share and have fun together.

One day, a girl named Lily had an idea. She wanted to visit her friend Tom who lived in the house next to hers. Tom had a big dog named Max. Lily was a little scared of Max, but she wanted to be brave. Before she went to Tom's house, Lily's mom told her to be careful. She said, "Stay away from Max if he is too big for you." Lily said, "Okay, Mom. I will be careful." When Lily got to Tom's house, Max was in the yard. Max saw Lily and started to run to her. Lily was scared and didn't know what to do. Tom saw what was happening and ran to help Lily. He told Max to stop and Max listened. Lily was happy that Tom helped her, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a baby. The baby was very small and was only 3 years old. One day they were having lunch together. The mommy had a big bowl of soup and a teaspoon. The baby wanted to help her eat the soup, so she asked her mommy to share with her. But the mommy said no, the teaspoon was too big for the baby. The baby did not like her mommy's answer and was very sad. She began to cry, so the mommy said, "Ok, I will share the soup with you, but you must be careful not to spill it." The baby was very happy and carefully scooped a little bit of soup with the teaspoon. But then, all of a sudden, the baby dropped the teaspoon in the bowl and it made a big mess. The mommy sighed, "Oh no, now you have made a careless mess." The baby was sad, but then the mommy said, "It's ok, accidents happen, let's clean it up together." The baby smiled and said, "Yes, mommy!" Then they both cleaned up the spill and had a happy lunch together.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little farm. On this farm, there was a goat named Billy. Billy liked to play with his friends, the cow and the pig. They loved to run and jump in the green grass. One day, Billy found a big, red ball. He was so happy! He wanted to share the ball with his friends. He said, "Cow, Pig, let's play with this ball together!" Cow and Pig were excited to play too. They took turns kicking the ball and had lots of fun. Billy, Cow, and Pig learned that sharing made their playtime even better. They promised to always share their toys and be good friends.

Once upon a time there was a big bear. He lived in a big forest with lots of trees and a big pond. One day, the bear was walking around in the forest when he saw a belt lying on the ground. He wanted to try it on and look handsome, so he put it on. The bear was so excited with his new belt. He said to himself, "I'm going to get my friends to ride with me in the forest. They will love my new belt!" The bear quickly ran off to find his friends in the woods. As he ran, he noticed the belt was getting very tight around his waist and he started to feel funny. Before he could reach his friends, he tripped on a root and rolled down a hill. All of his friends came to help him and saw the big belt around his waist. The bear was embarrassed and said, "I wanted to look stylish and I put it on, but it's too big!" His friends started to laugh. Everyone had a great time playing in the forest and riding their bikes. The bear was so relieved because he was not embarrassed anymore. From that day on, the bear always chose belts that fit him better.
