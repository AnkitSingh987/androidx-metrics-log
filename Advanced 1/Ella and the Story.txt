Title: Ella and the Story

Once upon a time there was a clever little girl called Ella. Ella loved to hear stories and she was always asking for them. One day, she asked her dad to tell her a new story. "I don't have a story," said Dad. But he had an idea. "Let me send a story to you!" Ella was so excited that she could hardly wait. Dad sent the story, and it was even better than she expected. It was a magical adventure story about a brave prince who saved the day. Ella loved the story! She thanked her dad and saved it in her special collection of stories. She couldn't wait to tell her friends about the clever story that her dad had sent her.

One day, a little girl named Lucy saw a trophy in a store. The trophy was big and shiny. Lucy wanted the trophy very much. She asked her mom, "Can I have the trophy, please?" Her mom said, "No, we don't need a terrible trophy." At night, Lucy went to sleep and had a dream. In her dream, she found the trophy in her room. The trophy started to glow! It was so bright that it lit up the whole room. Lucy was so happy. Suddenly, the glowing trophy turned into a big, friendly monster. The monster said, "Hi, Lucy! I am not a terrible trophy, I am a magic friend!" Lucy and the monster played and had fun all night. When Lucy woke up, she knew it was just a dream, but she was happy to have a new magic friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sara. They wanted to play together. Tim walked to Sara's house and knocked on the door. "Sara, can you come out and play?" he asked. Sara came out of her house and they went to the park. At the park, they saw a big, empty box. They were very excited. "Wow, look at that big empty box!" said Sara. "Let's play with it!" Tim and Sara played with the empty box all day. They pretended it was a car, a boat, and even a house. They had so much fun together. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye and promised to play again soon.

One day, a small girl decided to go for a walk in the woods. As she meandered through the trees, she stumbled upon a big twig lying on the ground. The twig looked very tight, and she was tempted to pick it up. She picked up the twig and inspected it in her small hands. However, it was not as tight as she thought - the twig started to rot in her hands. The girl felt scared and regretted picking up the twig. She was about to throw it away when she noticed a big ant hill not far from the twig. She realised that a tight twig would make the perfect bridge for the ants to move around and explore. So she pulled the twig tight and used it to create a bridge over the ant hill. The ants were so excited to have a bridge that they started to move and cross over to the other side of the hill. The girl was proud of her work and happy that is was able to help the ants. She smiled and left happily, feeling a great sense of satisfaction.

One day, a little girl named Sue saw a small kitten in her yard. The kitten was very cute and was playing with a ball. Sue wanted to play with the kitten too. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, can I play with the kitten outside?" Her mom looked serious and said, "You can play with the kitten, but you must be careful. Kittens are very small and can get hurt easily." Sue went outside and started to play with the kitten. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. Sue's friend, Tim, came over and saw them playing. Tim wanted to play with the kitten too, but he was playing too rough. Sue told Tim, "You have to be gentle with the kitten. My mom says we must be careful." But Tim did not listen. Sue had to insist again, "Tim, please be gentle. The kitten is small and can get hurt." Tim listened to Sue and started to play gently with the kitten. They all played together and had a great time. The kitten was happy and safe, and Sue and Tim learned how to take care of their new little friend.
