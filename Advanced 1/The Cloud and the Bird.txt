Title: The Cloud and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a big cloud. This cloud was very sad because it could not carry rain. All the other clouds could carry rain, but not this one. One day, a small bird saw the sad cloud and asked, "Why are you sad, cloud?" The cloud replied, "I am sad because I cannot carry rain like the other clouds." The small bird thought for a moment and said, "Maybe I can help you. Let's find a way to make you carry rain." The small bird and the sad cloud went on a long journey to find a way for the cloud to carry rain. They met many other clouds and asked them for help. Finally, they found a wise old cloud who knew the secret. The wise old cloud said, "To carry rain, you must first carry love in your heart." The sad cloud thought about the small bird and how much it cared for it. Suddenly, the cloud felt love in its heart and started to carry rain. The small bird and the cloud were so happy that they danced together in the sky. From that day on, the cloud was never sad again, because it could finally carry rain like all the other clouds.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a thoughtful rose. The rose was very pretty and loved to talk to the other flowers. One day, a little bird came to the rose and asked, "Can you help me find my friend?" The rose was happy to help and said, "I will ask the other flowers if they have seen your friend." The rose asked all the flowers in the garden, but no one had an answer. The little bird was sad but the rose said, "Don't worry, we will find your friend." The next day, the little bird and the rose saw a big tree with lots of leaves. The rose had an idea and said, "Maybe your friend is hiding in the tree." The little bird flew up to the tree and found his friend. They were so happy and thanked the thoughtful rose for helping them. From that day, the little birds and the rose became best friends.

Little Joe was very excited. Today was the day he was going to the temple. He hopped up and down, preparing for his adventure. At the temple, Joe was very polite. He said hello to everyone, and listened very carefully. He was eager to learn something new. The priest showed Joe around the temple. He showed him a statue of the gods, and told Joe a story. Joe was fascinated. He also saw many colorful paintings. The priest explained what each one meant, and Joe was happy to learn. Finally, before he left, the priest gave Joe a big hug. Joe smiled and waved goodbye. He couldn't wait to come back and learn more.

Once upon a time, there was a grape named Gigi. Gigi was a little ignorant. She did not know many things. But she was very happy living on a big green vine. One day, a bird came and said, "Hi, Gigi! Do you want to play?" Gigi said, "Yes, I want to play!" The bird and Gigi played all day. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, the bird said, "Gigi, you are not ignorant. You are very smart and nice." Gigi smiled and said, "Thank you, bird. I am happy to be your friend." From that day on, Gigi and the bird were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a unique cat named Mark. Mark had a big bow on his head. He liked to show his bow to his friends. His friends liked his bow too. One day, Mark went to the park to play with his friends. They played catch and hide-and-seek. Mark was very good at these games. His friends were happy to play with him. But then, a big dog saw Mark's bow. The dog wanted the bow. He ran up to Mark and took his bow. Mark was very sad. His friends tried to help, but the dog was too big. Mark lost his unique bow and never found it again.
