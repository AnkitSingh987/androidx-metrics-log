Title: Tim the Fragile Animal and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a fragile little animal named Tim. Tim was not like the other animals. He was small and had a hard time doing big things. But Tim had a special power. He could record sounds with his tiny ears. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. He heard the birds singing, the dogs barking, and the wind blowing. Tim felt happy and started to record all the sounds he heard. When Tim went home, he shared the sounds with his family. They all listened and felt happy too. The fragile little animal had made everyone smile with his special power. And from that day on, Tim knew he was special in his own way.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the living room. They have many toys and books there. But they also have to be careful. There is a big vase on the table. It is blue and shiny. Mom says it is very old and special. She says they must not touch it or break it. One day, Sara and Ben are bored. They have played with all their toys and books. They want to do something new. Sara has an idea. She suggests to Ben that they pretend to be spies. They can sneak around the living room and look for clues. Ben thinks that sounds fun. He agrees to play with Sara. They put on their hats and glasses. They take their flashlights and notebooks. They start to explore the living room. They look under the sofa, behind the curtains, and on the shelves. They find some coins, a button, and a feather. They write them down in their notebooks. They are having a good time. But then, Sara sees the vase. She wonders what is inside it. She thinks it might have a secret message or a treasure. She decides to take a peek. She climbs on the table and reaches for the vase. She does not see Ben behind her. He is looking at a picture on the wall. He does not see Sara on the table. He bumps into the table by accident. The table shakes. The vase wobbles. Sara gasps. She tries to catch the vase, but it is too late. The vase falls to the floor. It breaks into many pieces. It makes a loud noise. Mom hears the noise. She comes to the living room. She sees the mess. She sees Sara and Ben. She is very angry. She scolds them for being careless and naughty. She tells them to clean up the mess and go to their room. She says they are in big trouble. Sara and Ben are very sorry. They did not mean to break the vase. They did not know it was so important. They wish they had listened to Mom. They wish they had played something else. They clean up the mess and go to their room. They hug each other and cry. They learn their lesson. They promise to be more careful and respectful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp. This shrimp was different from the other shrimps. He had a big, red nose. The other shrimps did not have a big, red nose. They had small, pink noses. One day, the little shrimp with the big, red nose wanted to tell a story. He swam to his shrimp friends and said, "I want to tell you a story." His friends looked at him and smiled. They liked stories. The little shrimp told a story about a big fish and a small fish. The big fish wanted to play with the small fish, but the small fish was scared. In the end, the big fish and the small fish became friends. The shrimp friends liked the story. They clapped their little shrimp hands and said, "Good story! Tell us more!" The little shrimp with the big, red nose was happy. He knew that being different was okay.

One day, a girl named Lily and a boy named Tom went to a big building. They wanted to go up to see the sky. The building had an elevator. They got in the elevator and pushed the button to go up. As they went up, they saw the sky through the clear window. Lily said, "Look at the sky! It's so blue!" Tom said, "No, it's not blue. It's green!" They started to disagree about the color of the sky. Suddenly, the elevator stopped. The door opened, and they saw a big room filled with colorful balloons. They forgot about their disagreement and played with the balloons. They had so much fun that they became friends again.
