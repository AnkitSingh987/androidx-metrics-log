Title: The Planet and the Clean

Once upon a time, there was a big planet. It was very dirty. Many people lived on this planet. They wanted to make it clean. One day, a little girl named Lily said, "Let's clean our big planet!" Her friend, Tom, agreed. "Yes, let's clean it together!" They put on gloves and picked up trash. Lily and Tom worked hard. They cleaned and cleaned. The big planet started to look better. The people were happy. They said, "Thank you, Lily and Tom, for cleaning our big planet!" Now, the big planet was clean and beautiful. Everyone took care of it. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a light goat. The goat lived on a farm with many animals. The goat had a job. The goat's job was to sort the hay for the other animals. Every day, the goat would sort the hay into piles. One pile was for the big animals, like the cows and horses. The other pile was for the small animals, like the chickens and rabbits. The goat was very good at his job. One day, the goat was sorting the hay when he saw a lost baby bird. The baby bird was sad and hungry. The goat wanted to help the baby bird. So, the goat shared some hay with the baby bird. The baby bird was happy and not hungry anymore. The goat and the baby bird became best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

One day, a little sunflower felt helpless. It was too small to see the sun. The sunflower wanted to grow big and tall like its friends. It tried to peek at the sun, but it was too short. A kind bird saw the sunflower and asked, "Why are you sad?" The sunflower said, "I want to see the sun, but I am too small." The bird thought of a way to help the sunflower. The bird flew up high and found a long stick. It brought the stick back to the sunflower. The bird put the stick next to the sunflower and said, "Hold on to this stick, and it will help you grow tall." The sunflower held on to the stick, and soon it grew taller and taller. Now, the sunflower could peek at the sun and was not helpless anymore. The sunflower was very happy and thanked the kind bird for its help.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was only three years old. One day, Mia was walking through the woods with her mom and dad when she saw a fat fairy. The fairy said to Mia, “I am going to bring you something special. What would you like?” Mia thought for a moment and then said, “I want a new dress!” The fairy smiled and said, “What color dress do you want?” Mia thought for a moment before saying, “I want a pink dress!” The fairy smiled again and said, “I will bring you a beautiful pink dress!” Mia was so excited and thanked the fairy. She gave the fairy a big hug before running off to tell her mom and dad about the special gift she was going to get.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big, loud dog named Max. She loved Max very much. They played together every day. Max would bark and run, and Amy would laugh and clap. One day, they went for a walk in the park. Max was very happy and barked very loud. Amy felt safe when Max was with her. They played catch with a red ball. Max would jump high to catch it, and Amy would cheer. As the sun went down, they went back home. Amy gave Max a big hug and said, "I love you, Max. You make me feel safe and happy." Max wagged his tail and licked her face. They were best friends forever.

One day, a boy named Tim did not know where his pants were. He looked all around his room. He looked under his bed, in his toy box, and even in the closet. Tim was worried because he had to go to a party soon. Tim asked his sister, Sue, if she knew where his pants were. She said no, but she saw a big dog outside with something blue. Tim's pants were blue! He ran outside to find the dog. Tim saw the big dog with his blue pants in its mouth. He tried to get his pants back, but the dog ran away. Tim was very sad and worried. He could not go to the party without his pants. The day had a bad ending for Tim because he never found his pants.
