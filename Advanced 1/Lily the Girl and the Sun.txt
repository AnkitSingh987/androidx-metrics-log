Title: Lily the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside where the sun would shine bright. One day, she saw a big, disgusting bug in her yard. Lily was scared of the bug, but she knew she had to be brave. Lily thought of a plan to make the bug go away. She ran inside her house and got a big, shiny toy. She hoped the sun would shine on the toy and scare the bug away. Lily felt like a big, brave youth as she went back outside. Lily held the shiny toy up to the sun. The sun made the toy shine so bright that the disgusting bug got scared and flew away. Lily was so happy that her plan worked. She knew that she could be brave and solve problems all by herself.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. Today, they want to go on the slide. But there is a problem. A big boy is on the slide. He does not let Tom and Lily go on the slide. He says, "This is my slide. Go away!" Tom and Lily are sad. They ask the big boy nicely, "Please, can we go on the slide? We will wait our turn." But the big boy refuses. He says, "No, you can't. You are too small and stupid. This slide is only for big and smart kids like me." Tom and Lily are angry. They think the big boy is mean and rude. They do not want to play with him. They look for another game. They see a swing, a seesaw, and a sandbox. They decide to go to the sandbox. They find some toys and buckets. They start to make sand castles and animals. The big boy sees Tom and Lily having fun. He feels bored and lonely. He wants to join them. He gets off the slide. He walks to the sandbox. He says, "Hey, can I play with you? Your sand toys look cool." But Tom and Lily refuse. They say, "No, you can't. You are too big and mean. This sandbox is only for small and nice kids like us." They turn their backs on the big boy. They continue to play with their sand toys. The big boy feels sad and sorry. He realizes he was wrong. He wishes he had shared the slide. He says, "I'm sorry. I was stupid. Can we be friends?" But Tom and Lily do not hear him. They are too busy and happy. The big boy walks away. He looks for another slide. He hopes to find someone who will play with him.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby brown bear called Grumbly. He had a neck that was very long and flappy and he often felt sad about it. One day, Grumbly was feeling extra grumbly and he knew it was time to find someone who could comfort him. So Grumbly left his home and went to find a friend who could make him feel better. He soon spotted a baby rabbit, who was snuggled up in a cosy hole in the ground. Grumbly approached the hole and said hello. The baby rabbit looked up and smiled, it was exactly what Grumbly had been looking for. The rabbit was so kind and understanding, he asked Grumbly why he was feeling so sad. Grumbly explained that he was sad because his neck was so long and flappy and he had no one to hug him. The baby rabbit then did something amazing - he reached up and hugged Grumbly's neck, which made him feel much happier. Grumbly was so grateful for the rabbit's kindness and was comforted by his hugs. From then on, Grumbly and the baby rabbit were the best of friends and went everywhere together. Whenever Grumbly felt sad, he knew the rabbit was there to comfort him.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom liked to give signs to people. One day, he went to the park and saw a big tree. Tom thought it would be fun to put a sign on the tree. The sign said, "I am a tree." But then, something unexpected happened. A bird came and took the sign away. Tom was sad, but then the bird came back. The bird had a new sign. The sign said, "Thank you for the sign. Love, the birds." Tom was very happy and smiled big. The birds had given him a nice surprise.
