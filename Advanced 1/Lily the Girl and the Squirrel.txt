Title: Lily the Girl and the Squirrel

Once, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to bounce her ball outside. One day, she saw a squirrel with an acorn. The squirrel was very patient, waiting for Lily to play with him. Lily said, "Hi, squirrel! Do you want to bounce my ball with me?" The squirrel looked at her and nodded. They began to bounce the ball together. They had so much fun playing and laughing. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball hit the acorn, and it flew far away. The squirrel ran to find it. Lily and the squirrel looked and looked, but they could not find the acorn. They were sad. Suddenly, they saw a big tree with many acorns. The squirrel said, "Look, Lily! We found a new acorn!" They both smiled and continued to play with the new acorn and the ball. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

One day, a little ball named Rolly went to an office. In the office, there were big people who did work. Rolly wanted to help, but he did not know how. Rolly saw a big man drop his papers on the floor. Rolly said, "I can help!" He rolled over the papers to pick them up. The big man smiled and said, "Thank you, Rolly!" Rolly felt happy to help. Later, Rolly saw a big lady who looked sad. She had dropped her pen under a table. Rolly remembered how he helped the big man. He rolled under the table and got the pen. The big lady said, "Thank you, Rolly!" Rolly felt proud and not ashamed anymore. He knew he could help in the office, even if he was just a little ball.

Once, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a good dog. Spot had a collar. The collar was red and flexible. Spot liked his collar. One day, Spot met a cat. The cat did not understand Spot. The cat did not like Spot's collar. Spot was sad. Spot wanted the cat to like his collar. Spot had an idea. Spot showed the cat how flexible his collar was. The cat liked it. The cat and Spot became friends. They played together. Spot was happy.

One day, a lovely cat named Fluffy went out to play. Fluffy liked to jump and run in the sun. She was very happy. But then, Fluffy saw a big gas truck. The truck was there to supply gas to the houses. Fluffy was scared of the big truck. She did not want to play anymore. Fluffy ran back to her house. She told her mom about the big gas truck. Her mom said, "Do not worry, Fluffy. The truck is there to help us. It supplies gas so we can cook and be warm." Fluffy felt better and went back to play.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. In the field, there were many animals. One day, a little mouse and a big elephant met in the field. "Hello," said the little mouse. "Do you want to play with me?" "Sure," said the big elephant. They played and had fun. But then, the big elephant stepped on the little mouse's tail by mistake. The little mouse was hurt and the big elephant felt ashamed. The big elephant tried to say sorry, but the little mouse did not want to be friends anymore. They could not part as friends. The big elephant walked away, sad and alone. The little mouse stayed in the field, hurt and angry. And they never played together again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a slow turtle named Tim. Tim was always scared of many things. One day, Tim met a big bear named Ben. Ben was strong and brave. "Tim, why do you tremble so much?" asked Ben. Tim said, "I'm scared of the truth that I am too slow, and I can't do things like others." Ben smiled and said, "Being slow is not bad. You just need to be brave and try." So, Tim and Ben became friends. They played together every day. Tim learned to be brave and not tremble as much. And the truth was, Tim could do many things, even if he was slow.
