Title: Joe and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a naughty raccoon called Joe. Joe loved to steal things but he was also very obedient. Every night, Joe would sneak around his town looking for things to take. One night, Joe came across an old apple tree. He saw how many apples had been left behind and he decided to take them. Joe felt guilty but his naughty nature soon took over. Just then, he heard a loud voice yelling, "Hey Joe, what are you doing?!". Joe looked up and saw an old man standing in the shadows of the tree. The old man said, "These apples belong to me, don't you understand? I left them here to feed the birds, because if I don't they won't have food to eat. Why don't you do something kind and put the apples back, instead of taking them?" Joe felt embarrassed but he was also very obedient. He put the apples back and never tried to steal again. The old man thanked Joe for being so obedient and told him, "Sometimes it's better to help than to steal." From that day onwards, Joe always found ways to help others instead of stealing things. His obedience had taught him a valuable lesson. From then on, Joe was much kinder and more responsive to others.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful nation, there lived a happy cat and a friendly dog. They were best friends and loved to play together. Every day, they would run and jump in the bright sun. One day, the cat said to the dog, "Can I borrow your ball? I want to play with it too." The dog said, "Of course, you can borrow my ball. We can share and have fun together!" As they played with the ball, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky and took the ball away! The cat and the dog were very sad. They did not know what to do. The cat and the dog decided to work together to get the ball back. They climbed up a tall tree and jumped on the bird. The bird was surprised and let go of the ball. The cat and the dog were very happy to get the ball back. They learned that by working together, they could do anything. They played with the ball all day long and continued to be best friends in their cheerful nation.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite jacket. The jacket was red and made him feel fierce like a lion. He wore his jacket everywhere he went. One day, Tim and his friend, Sue, went to play outside. They saw a big ball. Tim said, "Let's play with the ball!" Sue agreed, "Yes, let's pass the ball to each other!" They passed the ball back and forth, laughing and having fun. Tim felt warm, so he took off his jacket and put it on the ground. After a while, they got tired and went home. Tim forgot about his jacket. When he went to bed, he remembered his jacket and felt sad. The next day, Tim and Sue went back to the place where they played. They found the jacket on the ground. Tim put on his jacket and felt fierce again. They played and had fun all day, and Tim never forgot his jacket again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a bottle of lotion. He liked to put the lotion on his hands to make them soft. One day, Tim saw a big, tall tree. He wanted to climb it and be successful. Tim started to climb the tree. He went up and up. But his hands were slippery from the lotion. Tim's friend, Sue, saw him and said, "Tim, be careful! The lotion makes it hard to climb!" Tim didn't listen to Sue. He wanted to keep going. He climbed higher and higher. But then, Tim's hands slipped. He screamed as he fell down from the tree. Tim's mom and dad came running. They saw Tim on the ground, hurt and sad. They told him, "Next time, listen to your friend and be more careful." Tim learned that being successful means knowing when to stop and listen to others.
