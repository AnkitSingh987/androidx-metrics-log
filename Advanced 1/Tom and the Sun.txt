Title: Tom and the Sun

One day, Tom was very sleepy. He wanted to take a nap, but he saw something on the ceiling. It was a dark spot. Tom was scared. He called his mom. "Mom, look!" he said, and he used his finger to point at the spot. Mom looked at the ceiling and said, "Don't worry, Tom. It's just a shadow." Tom felt better, but he was still sleepy. Mom said, "Go to sleep, Tom. The shadow will go away when the sun goes down." Tom closed his eyes, but he couldn't sleep. He kept looking at the ceiling. The dark spot was still there. The sun went down, but the spot did not go away. Tom felt more scared now. He called his mom again, but she did not come. The dark spot on the ceiling grew bigger and bigger. Tom was very scared. He wanted his mom, but she did not come. The dark spot got so big that it covered the whole ceiling. Tom closed his eyes tight, but he could not sleep. The dark spot stayed on the ceiling, and Tom was scared all night.

One day, a gifted crab named Bob met a big oyster. The oyster was very heavy. Bob wanted to know how much the oyster weighed. He asked his friend, a little fish named Tim, to help him. "Tim, can you help me find out how much the oyster weighs?" asked Bob. Tim agreed to help. They tried to lift the oyster together but it was too heavy for them. They needed a better idea. Just then, a friendly whale named Willy swam by. Willy was very strong. He helped Bob and Tim lift the oyster. As they lifted it, something unexpected happened. The oyster opened up and inside was a beautiful pearl! Bob, Tim, and Willy were so happy. They had found a treasure while trying to weigh the oyster.

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big box. He wanted to mail the box to his friend, Sam. But the box was not open yet. Tim was sad. Tim asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me open the box?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Let's open the box together." They opened the box and found a toy inside. Tim was happy. He put the toy back in the box and went to mail it. But on the way, a dog took the box. Tim was sad again. He asked the dog, "Please give me the box back." The dog gave the box back to Tim. Tim mailed the box to Sam. Tim was happy again.
