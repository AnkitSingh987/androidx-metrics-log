Title: The Green Car and the Candy

Once upon a time, there was a green car. The green car loved to drive fast. It would drive up and down the street, making all the kids happy. The green car had a special job. Its job was to bring candy to all the kids. One day, the green car went to the candy store. It filled up with lots of candy. There were red candies, blue candies, and even green candies, just like the car! The green car was so happy to have all the candy inside. The green car started to drive around the town. It stopped at each house to give the kids some candy. The kids were so happy to see the green car and get their candy. The green car felt very proud and smiled as it drove away.

Once upon a time, there was a big cave in a forest. Many animals lived in the forest. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the forest to play. He saw the cave and went inside. In the cave, Tim met a bear. The bear was very big and had a deep voice. Tim said, "Hello, bear!" The bear replied, "Hello, little boy." But the bear was rude and did not want to play with Tim. Tim felt sad and left the cave. Tim met a rabbit outside the cave. The rabbit was nice and wanted to play with Tim. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim was happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Tim and the rabbit played together in the forest and never went back to the rude bear in the cave.

Once upon a time there was a brave girl who lived in a big city. She looked around the tall buildings and watched people go by. Every day she would go to the park and stare up at the sky. She felt so small and imagined what it would be like to be big and go to the city! One day the brave girl decided she wanted to go to the city, she wanted to explore what was there. She was a bit scared, but she was brave and went anyway. When she arrived the city was so big and loud! She was overwhelmed. Everywhere she looked there were people and cars and so many things to look at. She was so busy looking around that she didn't realise how much time had passed. At night when she got back home, she would go to bed feeling brave and go to sleep looking forward to the next day when she could go back and stare at the city.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and run. One day, he saw a big tower made of blocks. It was so tall and pretty. Spot wanted to be independent and build a tower too. Spot went to his friend, Cat. "I want to build a tower," Spot said. Cat replied, "I can help you, Spot." So, they started to build a tower together. They put one block on top of another. Their tower was getting taller and taller. As they were building, Spot saw a ball. He wanted to play with it. Spot tried to grab the ball, but he accidentally bit the tower. The tower fell down and the blocks went everywhere. Spot and Cat looked at each other and laughed. They knew they could always build another tower together.

Once upon a time, there was a filthy horse named Max. Max lived in a big field with his friends. One day, Max hurt his leg and it was very sore. He did not know what to do. A nice girl named Lily saw Max and said, "Oh no, Max! Your leg is hurt. I will help you heal." Lily put a soft band on Max's leg and gave him a hug. Max felt better and said, "Thank you, Lily!" The next day, Max found a magic stone in the field. He touched it with his nose and suddenly, he could talk like a person! Max was so happy and ran to tell Lily. They talked and laughed all day. Now Max and Lily were the best of friends.
