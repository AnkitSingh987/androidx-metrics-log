Title: Lucy the Girl and the Castle

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a bright sweater in her room. She put it on and started to imagine. She imagined she was a big princess in a pretty castle. Lucy smiled and twirled around in her sweater. As Lucy played, she saw a little cat outside her window. The cat was cold and shivering. Lucy thought the cat needed her bright sweater more than she did. So, she took off her sweater and put it on the cat. The cat was warm and happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The cat turned into a fairy with a big smile. The fairy said, "Thank you, Lucy, for being so kind. I was a cat to see if you would help me. Now, I will make your imagine come true!" And with a wave of her wand, Lucy became a real princess in a pretty castle, just like she imagined.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a skip in the park. She liked to skip because it made her happy. In her hand, she held a napkin with her snack inside. While skipping, Sue saw a small boy named Tim. He looked upset. Sue wanted to help, so she walked up to him and asked, "Why are you upset?" Tim said, "I lost my napkin with my snack. Now I am hungry and sad." Sue thought for a moment, and then she had an idea. "Let's share my snack!" Sue said, opening her napkin. Tim smiled, and they both sat down to eat. After they finished, they played and skipped together. They were both happy, and they became good friends.

One day, Tom and Sue found a big thing called a microscope. They did not know what it was. They made a plan to find out. Tom said, "Let's look through it!" Sue put her eye near the microscope and saw a clear picture of a tiny bug. "Wow, I see a bug!" she said. Tom and Sue took turns looking at the bug. They laughed and played with the microscope all day. They were happy they made a plan to learn about the big thing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, her mom said they would go out to eat. Lily was very excited! They went to a nice place where people order meals. Lily looked at the menu and picked her favorite: a big plate of spaghetti. Her mom and dad ordered their meals too. They all sat and waited for the food to come. When the meals came, Lily saw that her spaghetti looked spoiled. She told her mom and dad, and they asked for a new plate. The new spaghetti came and it was perfect! Lily ate and ate until her tummy was full. They all left the place with big smiles on their faces.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very curious and wanted to solve puzzles. One day, Lucy found a line on the ground. It was long and cold. She was very excited and wanted to solve it. So Lucy tried to follow the line with her fingers. She traced and poked until she reached the end. Suddenly, she had a big smile on her face. She had solved the line! Lucy was so happy and proud of herself. She jumped around and clapped her hands. It was so much fun to solve the line. Then, Lucy decided to make her own line. She used some chalk to draw a new line. Then, she started to trace and solve it. She had so much fun that day!

Mum was carrying a pot as she walked to the spot in the garden. She put it down and smiled at her daughter who was standing near by. “I have something special for you,” said Mum. “Look, there is a spot in this pot. It’s pink and there are lots of different colours too. What do you think it is?” The little girl looked all around the pot carefully. She pointed to a bright yellow spot and said, “Is that it?” Mum laughed and said, “Yes, it is! That’s a special flower. Each spot is different - can you see?” The little girl smiled and said, “Yes! I can see all the different colours.” She looked at the pot for a few moments and then she bent down and carefully carried the pot back to their house.
