Title: Mum and Dad and the Car

One day, Mum and Dad took their little boy for a drive. They were looking for something green. After a while, they found a big green field. “Oh look!” said the little boy. “It looks so nice! Can we stop here?” Mum and Dad said yes, so the little boy got out of the car. He ran around, exploring the field. He spotted something dead lying on the ground. Mum and Dad gasped when they saw it. “That’s a dead animal,” said Dad. He warned the little boy not to touch it. Suddenly, the little boy felt something crawling on his arm. He screamed, and saw that it was a big, green spider. Dad ran over and tried to get it off, but it bit the little boy. The little boy went dead. Mum and Dad were so sad. They drove home, but the little boy was never seen again.

One day, a cat named Kitty and a dog named Doggy were playing in the house. They were running and jumping around. They saw a big mattress in the room. Kitty and Doggy decided to jump on the mattress. They were having so much fun. Kitty said, "Let's pause and have a snack." Doggy nodded and they went to the kitchen. They found some tasty treats on the table. Kitty had a fish treat and Doggy had a bone treat. They both liked their snacks a lot. After eating their tasty treats, Kitty and Doggy went back to the mattress. They jumped and played until they were tired. Then, they lay down on the mattress and took a nap. They had a happy day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. One day, Tim saw a scooter. He wanted to ride it. Tim said, "Mom, can I ride the scooter?" Mom said, "Yes, Tim. Be careful and have fun." Tim was so happy. He got on the scooter and started to ride. He went fast and slow, round and round. Tim was having so much fun. As Tim was riding, he saw a big door. Tim was curious. He wanted to see what was behind the door. Tim got off the scooter and entered the door. Inside, he saw a room full of more scooters! Tim was so surprised. Tim said, "Wow! Look at all the scooters!" He rode all the scooters and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Thank you, scooters. I had a great time." And from that day on, Tim and his friends would go to the scooter room to have fun and be adventurous together.

Once there was a girl who loved to play. Every day she would run and play outside in her yard. One day while she was playing, she heard a noise. It sounded like a whisper coming from the tree. The girl went to the tree and whispered "Hello?". Suddenly, a word floated down from the tree. It said "Come". The girl did not know what the word meant, but she knew that she should return home. So the girl ran as fast as she could. When she arrived home, the word was still in her head. She asked her mom what it meant and her mom said it was an invitation to explore. The girl smiled and ran back out the door. As she explored the world around her, she wondered if the word would ever return. But it never did. But she did not mind because she had discovered a world of wonders. She was exploring and learning new things which made her happy!

Once upon a time there was a big island with a huge beach. On the beach there were many friends who loved to play together. One day they all ran and laughed, sliding down the pale sand dunes. They built castles in the sand and ran down to the ocean to enjoy the waves. They all had fun splashing around. The island was so beautiful and full of happy friends. They all enjoyed running around, playing in the hot sun. Then they found some boxes of colourful paints and painted pictures in the sand. They made lots of circles and stars and beach balls. At the end of the day they all hugged each other, waving goodbye until the next day. And each day the beach became more and more beautiful and the friends were able to enjoy it even more. They allleared lots of fun things about the island, and made more and more memories.
