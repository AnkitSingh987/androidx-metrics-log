Title: Pippin the Penguin and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a penguin named Pippin. Pippin loved to play with his friends. One day, Pippin saw a big, dangerous rock. He knew he should not go near it, but he was curious. Pippin's friend, Sammy, said, "Pippin, don't go near the dangerous rock! It's not safe!" Pippin listened to Sammy and stayed away from the rock. Pippin and Sammy played a fun game of tag instead. At the end of the day, Pippin's mom came to take him home. She was happy that Pippin stayed safe and listened to his friend. She said, "I love you, Pippin. I'm glad you had fun and stayed away from the dangerous rock." Pippin hugged his mom and went home, excited to play with Sammy again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Jimmy. He lived in a big street and loved to explore the world. One day, Jimmy was walking in his street and he heard a strange sound. He listened carefully and realized that the sound was coming from a fake lion. It was roaring! Jimmy was scared and ran back home. In his house, Jimmy asked his mummy about the roaring. His mummy said "Oh Jimmy, that wasn't a real lion. It was a fake lion. It's made of plastic and it's just pretending to roar". Jimmy wasn't scared anymore and he ran back to the street. He was ready to look at the fake lion! When Jimmy got back to the street, he saw the lion. It was so big and colourful that Jimmy was very excited. He smiled and said: "Hello, fake lion". Jimmy and fake lion became friends. Every day they would meet in the street and they would roar together. It made Jimmy really happy!

Once upon a time, there was a big blue whale. The blue whale lived in the deep sea. It liked to swim and play with its friends, the fish. One day, the blue whale found a nice hat floating in the water. It wanted to wear the hat, but it was too small. The blue whale asked a fish friend, "Can you help me put the hat on my head?" The fish said, "Yes, I will help you." The fish swam to the hat and picked it up. The fish and the whale worked together to put the hat on the whale's head. The hat fit just right. The blue whale was very happy and said, "Thank you, fish friend!" They swam and played together all day, with the blue whale wearing its new hat.

One day, a gentle cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with lots of shade. Tom thought it was a good place to rest. So, he went under the tree and lay down. He felt cool and happy in the shade. Soon, a little girl came to the tree. She saw Tom and said, "Hello, cat! What is your name?" Tom looked at the girl and said, "My name is Tom. What is your name?" The girl smiled and said, "My name is Lily." Just then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the leaves away from the tree. Now, there was no shade for Tom and Lily. They looked at each other and laughed. Tom said, "Let's go find another tree with shade!" So, they walked together, looking for a new tree to rest under.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to wear jewelry. One day, she found a pretty necklace in her mom's room. It was very shiny and she wanted to wear it. Lily put on the necklace and went to play outside. She met her friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow, Lily! That necklace is so pretty!" Lily felt happy and proud. But as they played, the necklace started to feel tight and tough on her neck. She felt sad but did not want to take it off. After a while, Lily's mom came outside and saw the necklace. She said, "Lily, you should not take things without asking. It is not nice." Lily felt sorry and gave the necklace back to her mom. She learned that it is important to ask before taking things. And even without the jewelry, Lily and Sam had fun playing together.
