Title: Lan and the Skeleton

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. Her name was Lan. Lan was very curious. One day, she asked her mum, "What's a skeleton?" Mum replied, "A skeleton is like a picture of your body made with bones. It is very impressive." Lan was very excited to learn this new thing. She asked, "Can I make a skeleton too?" Mum nodded and said, "Yes, you can. We can add the bones, one by one." Lan and mum started to search for the bones. They looked in the kitchen and the bedroom. In the end, they had all the bones needed to make the skeleton. They carefully put the bones together. When the skeleton was finished, everyone was very impressed. Mum said, "That's a great job, Lan. You have made an impressive skeleton!" Lan was very proud of her work. The end.

One day, a large bird built a nest in a big tree. The bird had many eggs in the nest. The bird was very happy. One morning, the bird woke up and saw that one egg was gone. It had disappeared! The bird was very sad and looked for the egg everywhere. The bird found the egg near the tree. A nice squirrel had moved it to keep it safe. The bird was very happy and thanked the squirrel. They became good friends and lived happily in the tree.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to her bed. She was very tired. Lucy had a thick blanket on her bed. She loved her blanket because it kept her warm at night. Lucy was ready to finish her bedtime story and go to sleep. As Lucy started reading her story, she heard a noise under her bed. She was scared, but she looked under the bed. She saw a small cat with thick fur. The cat was lost and cold. Lucy felt happy to see the cat because she loved animals. Lucy took the cat out from under the bed. She let the cat sleep with her. The cat was warm and happy in the thick blanket. Lucy finished her bedtime story and went to sleep with her new friend. They both had sweet dreams.

One day, little Johnny was playing in his room. He picked up some paint and a wooden board and took them to his parents. He asked them, "Can I paint?" His parents smiled and nodded. They said, "Yes. Paint the board!" Little Johnny was so excited. He grabbed the paintbrush and started painting the board. He was so focused on the painting that he forgot to talk. After a while, his parents watched as Johnny finished the painting. It was a beautiful picture of a bird with a yellow beak and orange feathers. His parents were very proud of him and said, "You did a great job, Johnny! We love your painting!" Little Johnny beamed with joy. He was so happy that he had painted something that his parents liked so much.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a soft blanket. She loved her blanket very much. It was her favorite thing to have when she went to sleep. One day, Mia's blanket started to smell sour. Her mom said it was time to change the blanket. Mia was sad because she did not want a new blanket. She wanted her old blanket. Mia's mom washed the blanket and it did not smell sour anymore. Mia was happy to have her blanket back. She hugged her mom and said thank you. Now, Mia could sleep with her soft blanket again. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big bull was in a field. The bull was very creative. He liked to draw in the mud with his big feet. He made fun shapes and patterns. A little girl named Sue saw the bull. She said, "Wow! You are so creative, Mr. Bull!" The bull looked up and smiled at Sue. Sue had an idea. She called her friend Tom to come and see the bull's art. Tom came running to the field. He looked at the bull's art and said, "This is great! Let's show everyone!" They called all their friends to come and see. But when they got there, the mud was dry and the art was gone! The bull looked sad, but then he had an idea. He used his feet to make new art in the soft dirt. Everyone clapped and cheered for the creative bull.
