Title: The Computer and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a computer. The computer was very patient. It sat in a room all day and waited for someone to play with it. The computer liked to play games with kids. One day, a little boy came to play with the computer. The boy was not patient. He pushed the computer's buttons very hard. The computer started to tremble. The boy did not stop. He kept pushing the buttons harder and harder. The computer trembled more and more. It did not like the boy pushing its buttons so hard. Then, the computer broke. It could not play games anymore. The boy was sad, and the computer was sad too.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. They saw a big tree with many leaves. They wanted to see what was behind the tree. They ran to the tree and looked. They saw a small door in the tree. The door had a sign that said "Do not open". They also saw a bell next to the door. The bell had a cross on it. Tom was curious. He wanted to open the door and see what was inside. He said to Lily, "Let's open the door and ring the bell. Maybe there is something fun inside." Lily was scared. She did not want to open the door and ring the bell. She said to Tom, "No, we should not open the door and ring the bell. The sign says do not open. The bell has a cross on it. Maybe there is something bad inside." Tom did not listen to Lily. He opened the door and rang the bell. The bell made a loud noise. The door opened wider. A mysterious voice came from inside the tree. The voice said, "Who are you? What do you want?" Tom and Lily were very afraid. They ran away from the tree. They did not stop until they reached their mom. They told her what they saw and heard. Their mom hugged them and said, "You should not open doors and ring bells that you do not know. You should listen to your sister. She is smart and brave. The tree might have a secret that is not for you."

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was a very incredible boy. He loved to help his friends and family all the time. He knew that sharing and lending things made everyone happy. One sunny day, Tom's friend, Lily, lost her toy car. She was very sad. Tom saw that Lily was sad and wanted to help. He thought for a moment and decided to lend her his toy car. Lily's face lit up with a big smile. She thanked Tom and they played together for a long time. Tom felt very happy that he could help his friend. He knew that lending his toy car was the right thing to do. From that day on, Tom and Lily became the best of friends. They always helped each other and shared their toys. Everyone in the town knew that Tom was an incredible boy who loved to lend and share all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a family of three. They were a mommy, a daddy, and their little boy. One day they went to the park. The little boy excitedly ran around and laughed with glee. Suddenly, he saw a huge and shiny velvet cushion lying on the grass. He thought it looked so soft and lovely. He wanted to have it! He said, "Mommy! Can I have that cushion?" His parents told him that velvet was very special and delicate. The little boy said, "But I really really really want it!" His dad said, "Son, sometimes we can't always get what we want. It's not fair to be selfish." The little boy nodded and smiled. He thought it was a funny thing to happen. He decided that he didn't need that velvet cushion and went to the playground to have some more fun with his parents. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck. The duck was very restless. He did not like to sit still. He always wanted to play and have fun. One day, the little duck went to the pond. He saw his friends, the fish, and the frogs. They were all playing together. The little duck wanted to join them, but he was not careful. He tripped and fell into the water. The little duck was sad because he suffered a small hurt. He went back to his mom and dad. They hugged him and made him feel better. The little duck learned to be careful when playing. And from that day on, he was not so restless anymore.
