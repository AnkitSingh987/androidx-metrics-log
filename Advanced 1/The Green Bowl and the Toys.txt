Title: The Green Bowl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a green bowl. It was always filled with the most delicious fruits, like pears and apples. One day, a 3 year old girl saw the bowl on the kitchen table and she was so excited to taste the fruits inside. She asked her mom, “Can I fill my tummy with the fruits in the green bowl?” Her mom smiled and said, “Yes! You can have some if you first complete one task.” “What task?” the little girl asked. Her mom replied, “I need you to pick up all the toys from the living room.” The girl quickly scurried away to pick up the toys, and when she was finished, she went back to the kitchen. Her mom said, “Good job! Now you can eat your reward.” The girl happily grabbed a few pieces of fruit from the green bowl and gobbled them up. Then she smiled, content that her tummy was now full.

Jack was a naughty boy. He was always getting into trouble. His whole family were not very pleased with him. One day Jack's mom asked him to perform a task for her. She asked him to go to the store and buy her a new blouse. She thought it would be a good way to teach Jack a lesson. Jack decided to disobey his mother. He decided to just walk around the neighborhood instead. When he returned home later, his mom asked him if he had completed the task. Jack lied and said he was finished. His mom was very angry with him. Jack was in big trouble. His mom asked him to leave the house and asked him to never come back. She said he was not a good boy and she would not forgive him for what he had done. Jack had to leave, feeling sad and unfortunate. The end.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big, noisy truck. The truck had lots of gears on it. Lily was curious and wanted to know more about the gears. She asked the truck driver, "What do the gears do?" The driver was kind and answered, "The gears help the truck go fast or slow. They also help the truck go up and down hills." Lily liked the noisy truck and the gears. She said, "Thank you for telling me about the gears." The driver smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. Have a nice day!" Lily waved goodbye and continued her walk. She was happy to learn something new.

One day, a girl named Lily went to her club. She had a big bag with her. She wanted to unpack her bag and show her friends what was inside. Lily's friends, Tim and Sue, were very excited to see what was in the bag. Lily opened her bag and took out a big, silly hat. She put it on her head and said, "Look at my silly hat!" Tim and Sue laughed and clapped their hands. They thought the hat was very funny. Then, Lily took out a big red ball. She said, "Let's play with the ball!" But when they started to play, something unexpected happened. A little dog ran into the club and took the ball! Tim, Sue, and Lily were very surprised. They looked at each other and said, "Where did the dog come from?" The little dog ran around the club, playing with the ball. Lily, Tim, and Sue laughed and chased the dog. They had a lot of fun playing with the dog and the ball. When it was time to go home, the dog's owner came to the club. He said, "I'm sorry, my dog got away from me. I hope he wasn't too much trouble." Lily, Tim, and Sue smiled and said, "No, he was a lot of fun! We had a great time playing with him." And from that day on, the little dog would sometimes come to the club to play with Lily, Tim, and Sue. They all became good friends and had many more silly and fun days together.

Once there was a squirrel named Peter. He was feeling quite sad one day, as he had not been able to find any nuts to eat. He wanted to find a nut, but he was too afraid to go look for one. Suddenly, he heard a voice. It was a gentle voice, that spoke gently and kindly. The voice said, "Peter, why don't you go look for a nut?" Peter was so excited at the suggestion he followed the voice's instructions. The voice encouraged him to keep looking. Finally, Peter spotted a large gray nut in the grass nearby. He was so happy he danced around the nut, until he tired himself out so much that he lay down to take a nap. He lifted the nut up carefully, and took it home. Whenever he felt down or scared, he would think of the voice that had encouraged him to find the nut.
