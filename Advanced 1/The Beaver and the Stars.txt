Title: The Beaver and the Stars

Once upon a time there was a beaver who was feeling very sleepy. He yawned and yawned, but he just couldn't get to sleep. He tried counting stars, but it didn't help. He tried drinking warm milk, but still, nothing worked. The beaver started to feel something strange and decide to go to the doctor. The doctor examined the beaver and said, "I'm sorry, there's nothing I can do. You have a bad pain in your tummy, and there is no way to make it go away." The beaver was very sad. He tried to eat healthy food, but the pain was still there. Nothing could stop it. The beaver started to cry and gave up hope. He could no longer go for his daily swim and no longer do all of the things he loved. He eventually had to give up and lay in his bed feeling sick and sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside. One day, she found a small stone in her yard. The stone was special because it was very spicy. Lily wanted to show her friends the spicy stone. Lily put the spicy stone in her bag and zipped it up. She took the bag to the park where her friends were playing. When she got there, she opened her bag and showed them the spicy stone. Her friends were very surprised. Lily and her friends played with the spicy stone all day. They made up fun games and had a great time. When it was time to go home, Lily zipped the spicy stone back in her bag. She knew she would have many more fun days with her special stone.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the park. He saw a big tree with many green leaves. Tim felt happy and wanted to play near the tree. As he played, he saw a little bird on the ground. It looked sad and guilty. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I took a leaf from the tree, and now the tree is mad at me." Just then, the tree started to shake! The leaves fell down, and the tree became a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "I was just pretending to be a tree. I am not mad at you, little bird. You can have the leaf." The little bird was happy again, and Tim was very surprised. They all played together and had fun. The bear promised to remain their friend forever.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a rough ball that he loved to play with. Yesterday, his friend, a cat named Whiskers, came over to play. Spot wanted to lend his rough ball to Whiskers, so they could play together. They rolled the ball back and forth, and had lots of fun. Whiskers was very happy that Spot lent the ball to him. After playing with the ball, Spot and Whiskers were tired. They lay down in the soft grass and took a nap. When they woke up, they played some more. They were very good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy lived in a small cage in a big house. He was not happy in the cage. He wanted to fly and play with his friends outside. One day, a rude cat came into the house. The cat saw Timmy in the cage and wanted to eat him. Timmy was scared and asked the cat to go away. But the cat did not listen. Timmy had a plan to delay the cat. He told the cat, "First, let's play a game. If you win, you can eat me." The cat agreed. They played the game, and Timmy won. The cat was sad but went away. Timmy was safe and happy in his cage.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her friends in the park. They would run, jump, and have lots of fun. One day, Kitty found a note on the ground. It said, "Let's all unite and play together!" Kitty was very excited. She showed the note to her friends, and they all agreed to unite and play. So, Kitty and her friends played together all day. They were happy to be united, and they had the best time ever. And from that day on, they always made sure to unite and play together as much as they could.
