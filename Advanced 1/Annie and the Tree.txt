Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to pick blueberries. One day, she saw a big blueberry bush. Lily was so happy! But Lily was foolish. She climbed too high on the bush and fell down. "Ouch!" she cried. Her knee was hurt. Lily's mom came running and hugged her. "Don't worry, it will heal," said her mom. Lily's mom put a band-aid on her knee. They waited and soon, Lily's knee healed. Lily learned to be careful when picking blueberries. She was happy and not foolish anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little stamp. The stamp was very dry. It lived in a small box with other stamps. The little stamp wanted to be wet, so it could make pretty pictures. One day, the little stamp saw a big cup of water. It wanted to jump in, but it was too small. The little stamp said, "Help me, please!" A friendly bug heard the stamp and came to help. The bug pushed the stamp into the water. The little stamp was so happy. It was not dry anymore! The stamp said, "Thank you, bug!" The bug smiled and flew away. Now the little stamp could make pretty pictures with its friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there were two twins named Sam and Pam. They were very nice and loved to play together. One day, they found a magic toy that could shrink things. Sam said, "Let's use the magic toy to shrink our toys and have more fun!" Pam agreed and they started to play with the magic toy. They shrunk their toys and made a tiny town to play in. As they played in their tiny town, they had so much fun. Their toys looked so small and cute. But then, they wanted their toys to be big again. So, they used the magic toy to make their toys grow back to normal size. Sam and Pam learned that it's fun to try new things and play together. They were happy twins and continued to have many nice adventures with their toys.
