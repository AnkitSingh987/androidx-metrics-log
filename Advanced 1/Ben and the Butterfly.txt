Title: Ben and the Butterfly

Ben was playing in his garden, when he saw a butterfly resting on a flower. He felt scared and wanted to touch it. He moved very slowly but the butterfly flew away. Ben started to cry. Just then, his mother came out and saw Ben crying. She knelt down and said, “Come here, Ben. Let me give you a hug.” She gave Ben a big and warm hug. She said softly, “Sometimes we cannot do the things we want to do. But that’s ok. We can just surrender and try something else.” Ben nodded, feeling a bit better. He and his mum then went on to play a game. She said in a loud voice, “Let’s play hide and seek!” They ran around and laughed, playing the game for a long time. Finally, Ben decided to surrender and he gave up quickly. He hugged his mother and said, “Thank you for the fun!” The story ended with a happy ending, with Ben feeling content and secure with his mother’s touch.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to dress up. She put on a pretty dress, a hat, and shoes. Lily felt like a princess. She went to show her mom and dad. They smiled and said she looked nice. Lily wanted a snack. She went to the kitchen and saw a big jar of cookies. She tried to open the jar, but it was too tight. Lily got upset. She tried again and again, but she could not open the jar. She started to cry. Her big brother Tom saw her crying. He asked her what was wrong. Lily told him about the cookie jar. Tom helped her open it. They both got a cookie and shared a snack together. Lily was not upset anymore, and they played dress up together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a clever cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy mouse. One day, Tom found a big box in the kitchen. On the box, there was a label with a picture of a big, red ball. Tom was very happy and wanted to play with the ball. Tom tried to open the box, but it was hard to do. He used his paws to squeeze the box, but it did not open. Tom thought and thought, and then he had a great idea. He would ask his friend, the dog named Max, for help. Max came to the kitchen and saw the box. He used his big teeth to open the box. Inside the box, there was the big, red ball. Tom and Max were so happy. They played with the ball all day long. They knew that they were clever friends who could do anything together.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary house, there lived a cat named Whiskers. Whiskers liked to play with his ball and take naps in the sun. One day, Whiskers saw a bird outside the window. He wanted to play with the bird, but he knew he could not go outside. Whiskers asked his friend, the dog named Spot, "How can I play with the bird outside?" Spot said, "You can't, Whiskers. It is not safe for you or the bird. We must stay inside and play with our toys." Whiskers was sad, but he listened to Spot. They played with their toys and had lots of fun. Soon, Whiskers forgot about the bird. He learned that it is important to listen to friends and stay safe. And that was the day Whiskers and Spot became best friends.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, were playing outside. They were very enthusiastic and happy. They saw a hose lying on the ground. Tim said, "Let's play with the hose, Max!" Tim picked up the hose and turned on the water. Max was excited and jumped around. They played and laughed together. The water made a big puddle on the ground. Max loved to splash in the puddle. Suddenly, the hose started to wiggle and move! Tim and Max were surprised. They didn't know what was happening. A friendly frog jumped out from the hose and said, "Hello, I was sleeping inside the hose!" Tim and Max were not scared anymore, and they all played together in the water.
