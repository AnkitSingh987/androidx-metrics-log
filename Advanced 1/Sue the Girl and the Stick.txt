Title: Sue the Girl and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very independent. She loved to do things all by herself. One day, she saw a well in the woods. Sue wanted to find out what was inside the well. Sue walked up to the well and saw a frog. The frog said, "Hi, I am Fred the Frog. Can you help me get out of the well?" Sue was happy to help. She found a big stick and put it in the well. Fred the Frog climbed out of the well using the stick. "Thank you," said Fred the Frog. He was so happy that he wanted to give Sue a gift. Fred the Frog made a sign that said, "Sue is my friend." Sue was very happy and proud to have a new friend. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big, pink house, there lived a happy family. There was a mommy, a daddy, and a little girl named Lucy. They all loved their big, pink house very much. One day, while Lucy was playing with her toys, she heard a noise. It was a loud noise that disturbed her playtime. She looked around and saw a big, pink bird outside her window. The bird was making the noise. Lucy went outside to see the big, pink bird. She asked the bird, "Why are you making so much noise?" The bird said, "I am lost and need help to find my family." Lucy decided to help the big, pink bird. They looked and looked until they found the bird's family. The bird was happy and stopped making noise. In the end, Lucy and the big, pink bird became friends, and they played together every day. The noise did not disturb Lucy anymore because she knew it was her friend. And they all lived happily ever after in the big, pink house.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly frog. He lived in a pond with many pretty fish. The frog felt sad because he was not pretty like the fish. One day, the frog found a jar of jelly near the pond. The jelly was red and shiny. The frog had an idea. He put the jelly on his body to make himself shiny like the fish. The fish saw the shiny frog and thought he was pretty too. They played together in the pond, and the ugly frog was happy. The frog learned that being shiny made him feel good, but his new friends liked him for who he was, not just because he was shiny.

One day, a big train was going down the tracks. The train had a big engine. The engine was very strong and could pull many cars. The train was very happy to be so strong. Then, one day, the engine started to feel sick. It was not as strong as before. The train was sad and said to the little car, "I am broken. I cannot show you how strong I am anymore." The little car was sad too. They both wanted to find a way to fix the broken engine. They went to see a wise old train who lived in the train yard. The old train said, "I know how to fix your engine. You need a special part." The big train and the little car went on a journey to find the special part. They knew that when they found it, the engine would be strong again, and the train could show everyone how strong it was.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mommy. Amy saw a long line of black ants. She was very excited. "Mommy, look at the ants!" she said. Her mommy looked at the ants and smiled. Amy wanted to follow the ants to see where they were going. So, she walked behind the ants with her mommy. They walked and walked until they found the ants' home. It was a tiny hole in the ground. Amy looked at her mommy and said, "Can we come back tomorrow to see the ants again?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, we can come back tomorrow." Amy was so excited to see her new ant friends again.
