Title: The Lion and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big lion who lived in the forest. The lion had a very big head and a very loud roar. One day, the lion met a little bird who was singing a pretty song. The lion said, "You have a nice song, but I can roar louder." The bird said, "Okay, let's see who is louder." The lion roared as loud as he could, but the bird's song was still louder. The lion felt stupid because he thought his roar was the loudest. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird told the lion, "You can roar very loud, but I can teach you how to sing too." The lion was happy and learned to sing with the bird. They became best friends and sang together every day in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a nice bear. The bear lived in a cave. The cave was big and warm. The bear liked his cave a lot. One day, the bear heard a sound. He stopped to listen. The sound was coming from outside the cave. The bear went to see what it was. Outside, the bear found a little bird. The bird was singing a pretty song. The bear listened to the song and smiled. They became friends and played together every day. The bear was happy to have a nice friend to listen to.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to go to the library with Mom. The library has many books with pictures and stories. Lily and Ben can borrow some books and take them home. One day, it is very cold outside. The wind blows and the snow falls. Lily and Ben wear warm coats and hats and gloves. They walk to the library with Mom. They see the trees and the cars and the houses covered with white snow. They see some kids making a snowman and throwing snowballs. "Can we play in the snow, Mom?" Lily asks. "Not now, sweetie. We have to go to the library first. Maybe later, if it is not too icy," Mom says. Lily and Ben nod. They like the library, too. They go inside and say hello to the librarian. She smiles and gives them a sticker. They look for some books they want to read. Lily picks a book about a princess and a dragon. Ben picks a book about a pirate and a treasure. They sit on a big couch and look at the pictures and the words. Mom helps them read some of the words they do not know. They are so busy reading that they do not notice how dark it gets outside. The librarian comes to them and says, "It is time to go home, kids. The library is closing soon. Do you want to borrow these books?" "Yes, please," Lily and Ben say. They give the books to the librarian. She scans them and puts them in a bag. She gives the bag to Mom and says, "Be careful on the way home. It is very icy outside. Have a good night." "Thank you, bye," Mom says. She takes Lily and Ben by the hand and leads them outside. They see the snow sparkling in the street lights. They see the snowman wearing a hat and a scarf. They see the snowballs piled up on the ground. "Can we play in the snow now, Mom?" Ben asks. "Sorry, honey. It is too late and too icy. We have to go home and sleep. Maybe tomorrow, if it is not too cold," Mom says. Lily and Ben sigh. They want to play in the snow, but they are also tired and hungry. They follow Mom to the car. Mom buckles them in and starts the engine. She turns on the heater and the radio. She drives slowly and carefully on the icy road. Lily and Ben lean on each other and close their eyes. They listen to the music and the sound of the car. They think about the books they borrowed and the stories they read. They think about the snow and the fun they will have tomorrow. They fall asleep and dream of princesses and dragons and pirates and treasures.
