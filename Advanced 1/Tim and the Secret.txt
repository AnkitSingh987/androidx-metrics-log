Title: Tim and the Secret

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room with a chess set. A little boy named Tim and his sister, Sue, loved to play in this room. They would shout and laugh all day long while playing with the chess set. One day, Tim and Sue found a note under the chess board. The note said, "Move the black king and find a surprise." Tim and Sue moved the black king on the chess board. Suddenly, the room started to shake! The wall opened up, and they saw a secret room. The secret room was full of toys and games. Tim and Sue were so happy to find this new room. They played in the secret room all day long, and their shouts and laughter could be heard throughout the house.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in a forest. A small bird lived in a hidden nest on the tree. The bird liked shiny things. One day, she found a silver coin on the ground. The bird picked up the coin and flew back to the tree. She showed it to her friend, the squirrel. "Look what I found!" said the bird. "It's so shiny and pretty!" The squirrel agreed, "Yes, it's very nice." As the sun began to set, the bird and the squirrel decided to settle down for the night. They put the silver coin in a safe place near their homes. They both went to sleep, dreaming of more shiny things they might find in the forest.

Once upon a time there was a cute little bunny. His name was Blackie and he always wore a black bow on his head. One day, Blackie was invited to Daisy's birthday party. He couldn't wait to show off his favourite black bow and see all of his friends. But when he arrived at the party he was very sad. Everyone else was wearing bright colours, but he was still in his all black outfit. He stood in the corner, feeling sad and alone. The Birthday girl, Daisy, noticed him and came over to talk. She smiled and said, "Don't feel left out, Blackie. You can promise to wear bright colours next time with us." Blackie smiled. He was glad that Daisy still wanted him to come to the party even though he wasn't wearing the right colour. He said, "I promise, Daisy. I will wear a bright colour for the next invitation." The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Mimi. Mimi was not happy in her home. She wanted to find a perfect place to live. One day, she had an idea. "I will think of a way to escape!" she said to herself. Mimi saw an open door and decided to run away. She ran and ran until she found a big tree. "This is perfect!" she thought. Mimi climbed the tree and met a bird named Chirpy. "Hello, Mimi!" said Chirpy. "Why are you here?" Mimi told Chirpy about her escape and her search for a perfect place to live. Chirpy and Mimi became good friends. They played and had fun in the tree. But one day, it started to rain. The tree was not a perfect place anymore. Mimi was cold and wet, and she missed her warm home. She tried to go back, but she could not find the way. Mimi was lost and sad, and she never found her perfect place.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Speedy. Speedy loved to go fast. He would speed down the street, making all his car friends happy. One day, Speedy saw his friends watching a video. It was a video of a big, dark forest. Speedy and his friends wanted to see the dark forest for real, so they went on a trip together. They drove and drove until they reached the dark forest. Speedy was very brave and led the way. As they sped through the forest, they saw many pretty trees and birds. At the end of the day, they went back home and told everyone about their fun adventure in the dark forest.

One day, a sad boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a pigeon. The pigeon was very pretty. Tim wanted to own the pigeon. He went near the pigeon and said, "Can I own you, pigeon?" The pigeon looked at Tim and said, "Yes, but you must find my friend first." Tim was happy and asked, "Where is your friend?" The pigeon told Tim, "My friend is hiding in the park." Tim looked for the pigeon's friend all over the park. He looked behind trees and under benches. Suddenly, he found a big, green frog. The frog said, "I am the pigeon's friend! Thank you for finding me." Tim was surprised that the pigeon's friend was a frog. But he was happy to help. The pigeon was also happy. The pigeon said, "Now you can own me, Tim." Tim, the pigeon, and the frog played in the park all day. They were all very happy.
