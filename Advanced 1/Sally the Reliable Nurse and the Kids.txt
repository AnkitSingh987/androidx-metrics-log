Title: Sally the Reliable Nurse and the Kids

Once upon a time, there was a reliable nurse named Sally. She worked in a big house where she took care of many kids. Sally was always kind and made sure the kids were happy and healthy. One day, Sally set a big table for the kids to eat. She put plates, cups, and yummy food on the table. The kids were very excited to eat. One little boy named Timmy said, "Sally, you set the best table ever! I love the food you made!" Sally smiled and said, "Thank you, Timmy. I am happy you like it." After they ate, Sally and the kids played games and had lots of fun. The kids loved Sally because she was a reliable nurse who always took good care of them. They knew they could always count on Sally to make their days happy and bright.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He was very glad to play with his toy car. He found a big hill to race his car down. But at the start, his car would not go. Tim tried and tried, but his car just remained at the top of the hill. Tim was sad and asked his mom for help. His mom looked at the car and saw that a small rock was stuck under it. She moved the rock and told Tim to try again. This time, when Tim pushed his car, it raced down the hill very fast. Tim was so happy! Now, every time Tim went to the park, he made sure to check for rocks before racing his car down the hill. He was always glad to play with his car and never had a problem again. Tim and his mom had lots of fun racing cars together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her ball. One day, the ball got stuck in a tight place. Lily was sad. Lily asked her friend, Max, "Can you help me?" Max said, "Yes, I will help you!" Max got a measure stick. He tried to get the ball out with the stick. Then, Max had an idea. He got a glow stick. He put the glow stick in the tight place. The ball started to glow too! They could see the ball better. Max used the measure stick to get the ball out. Lily was so happy. They played with the ball and the glow stick all day.

Once upon a time there was a generous bell. Every day, a young girl named Mary would come by and give it a ring. She would always say a kind word to the bell, no matter how tired or sad she was. One day, Mary had a special surprise for the bell. She presented it with a sweet, shiny gift! The bell was so happy and thanked Mary for her generous gift. The next day, Mary decided she wanted to make something special for the bell. She ran to the shops and bought the ingredients for a delicious cake. When she got home, she baked the cake and then carefully placed it on top of the bell. As she rang the bell to let the bell know she was there, it let out a sound of joy and surprise. Mary excitedly opened the cake and found a beautiful golden key inside. Mary smiled happily - she knew the bell had given her a present, and now, it was her turn to say thank you.

Anna was a princess who lived in a big palace with her mom and dad. She had many toys and dresses and books, but she was not happy. She wanted to have a friend to play with and talk to. One day, she saw a little girl outside the palace gate. The girl had a dirty dress and no shoes. She was crying because she was hungry and cold. Anna felt sorry for her and wanted to help her. She ran to her room and got a blanket and a sandwich. She sneaked out of the palace and gave them to the girl. The girl smiled and thanked Anna. She said her name was Lily and she had no mom and dad. She lived on the street with other poor children. Anna asked her if she wanted to be her friend. Lily nodded and hugged Anna. They shared the sandwich and the blanket and talked for a while. Anna felt happy and warm. She had found a friend who liked her for who she was, not for what she had. She decided to visit Lily every day and bring her more food and clothes. She also asked her mom and dad if they could help the other poor children. They agreed and said they were proud of Anna. Anna and Lily became best friends. They played and learned and laughed together. They comforted each other when they were sad or scared. They made each other calm and happy. They learned that friendship is more precious than anything in the world.
