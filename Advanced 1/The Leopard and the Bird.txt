Title: The Leopard and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big gray forest, there lived a nice leopard. The leopard had many friends, but his best friend was a little bird. They played together every day and had lots of fun. One day, the little bird found a shiny stone. "Look, leopard!" said the little bird. "I found a pretty stone!" The leopard looked at the stone and said, "Wow, it is very nice. But we must value our friendship more than things." The next day, they found a big tree with many fruits. The little bird and the leopard shared the fruits with all their friends. They were very happy. The moral of the story is that friendship is more important than things. We should value our friends and be kind to them.

One day, a creative little girl named Mia wanted to wake her family. She had a fun idea to make them smile. Mia found paint and paper. She made a big, pretty picture with lots of colors. Mia ran to her mom and dad's room. She said, "Wake up, wake up! Look at my picture!" Mom and Dad opened their eyes. They saw Mia's picture and smiled. But Dad said, "It's too early, Mia. We are still sleepy. Please let us sleep more." Mia was sad. She wanted to show her picture now. She thought and thought. Then, Mia had a better idea. She put the picture on the table where her family eats. When they wake up, they will see her picture and be happy. Mia felt good and went back to sleep.

Once, there was a boy named Tom. He had a fine scooter. He loved to ride it all day. One day, Tom saw a big hill. He wanted to ride his scooter down the hill very fast. As Tom went down the hill, he saw a cat. The cat was near a tree. The tree had a big branch. The branch was about to fall. Tom was scared. He did not want the cat to get hurt. Tom had an idea. He cut the branch with his strong hand. The cat was safe. The cat said, "Thank you, Tom." The cat was his new friend. The moral of the story is to always help others when they are in need.

A young girl named Emilia was walking down the street with her mother. As they passed by a grocery store, they noticed a basket of olives. The olives were a beautiful dark shade of green and looked delicious. Emilia stopped and looked at the olives with hunger in her eyes. Her mother smiled and said, "Would you like an olive, Emilia?" Emilia nodded and her mother went in and bought the olives. She then popped one into Emilia's mouth. Emilia was so excited to try the olives. She took a bite and smiled. She said, "Delicious!" Her mother smiled and gave her another. After eating four olives, Emilia was too full. She gazed back at the basket and sighed happily. She had enjoyed her delicious olives and was looking forward to having more soon.

Lila likes to fold paper. She can make many shapes with paper. She can make a bird, a boat, a hat, and more. She likes to show her paper shapes to her mom and dad. They say she is very good at folding paper. One day, Lila wants to play with her paper shapes outside. She takes her paper and goes to the backyard. She sees a big tree with many leaves. She thinks the tree is very pretty. She wants to make a paper shape like the tree. She takes a green paper and folds it many times. She makes a paper leaf. She is happy with her paper leaf. She makes more paper leaves with different green papers. She has many paper leaves now. She thinks they are unique. She runs to the tree and puts her paper leaves on the branches. She wants to make the tree more colorful. She thinks the tree will like her paper leaves. She smiles and says, "Look, tree, I made you some friends. They are paper leaves. They are unique like you."

One day, Tom went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy a melon for a snack. Tom saw a big, round melon. It looked perfect. He said, "Mom, can we get this one?" His mom looked at the melon and smiled. "Yes, Tom, we can get that melon. It looks perfect for us to eat," she said. They put the melon in the cart and went to pay. When they got home, Tom's mom cut the melon into pieces. She gave Tom a piece and he took a big bite. "Yum!" Tom said. "This melon is so good!" His mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you like it, Tom. You picked the perfect melon!" They shared the melon and enjoyed their snack together.
