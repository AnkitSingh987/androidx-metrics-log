Title: Lucy the Girl and the Rug

One day, a little girl named Lucy saw an amazing rug in a shop. It had many colors and shapes. She told her mom, "I want that rug for my room, please!" Her mom said, "If you help me clean the house, we can buy the rug." The next day, Lucy and her mom cleaned the house. Lucy was very good at it. Her mom was happy and said, "We can go buy the rug now!" They went to the shop, but the amazing rug was not there. Lucy was sad. Her mom asked the shop man, "Where is the amazing rug?" The shop man said, "We will display it tomorrow." The next day, Lucy and her mom went back to the shop. They saw the amazing rug on display. Lucy was so happy! They bought the rug and put it in Lucy's room. She loved the rug and played on it every day.

Lucy's mom said, "Let's go outside and play with your car." Lucy was happy and said, "Yes, please!" They went outside and Lucy put the car on the ground. She turned the car left and right, and it went fast! Lucy and her mom laughed and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The car went into a big open box. Lucy and her mom looked inside and saw a cute puppy! The puppy was playing with the car. Lucy said, "Hi, puppy!" The puppy wagged its tail and barked. Lucy's mom said, "What a nice surprise!" They took the puppy home and they all played together.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow ball. The ball was very happy and liked to play with two little friends, Tim and Sue. They loved to roll, bounce, and throw the ball to each other. One day, Tim and Sue started to fight over the ball. They both wanted to play with it, but they didn't want to share. "Give me the ball!" said Tim. "No, it's my turn!" said Sue. The yellow ball felt very sad. It didn't like when its friends fought over it. The ball wanted them to be happy and play together. So, the ball rolled away from Tim and Sue, hoping they would stop fighting and learn to share. Tim and Sue saw the ball rolling away and felt sorry. They knew they should not fight over the ball. They decided to say sorry to each other and to the ball. "I'm sorry, Sue," said Tim. "I'm sorry too, Tim," said Sue. They both promised to share the yellow ball and play nicely together. The ball was very happy and rolled back to them. Tim and Sue played together, sharing the ball, and they all lived happily ever after.
