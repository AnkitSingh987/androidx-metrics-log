Title: Tom the Cat and the Bird

One day, a chubby cat named Tom was walking down the road. He saw a little bird on the ground. The bird looked sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I hope to find my family, but I am lost." Tom wanted to help the little bird. They walked down the road together, looking for the bird's family. As they walked, they found a big box in the middle of the road. They did not know what was inside. Tom and the bird opened the box, and they found the bird's family! They were all happy and hugged each other. Tom was happy too, because he made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to eat meat. One day, Kitty's friend, Doggy, gave her some meat to eat. Kitty was very happy. While Kitty was eating the meat, she made a big mess. She felt embarrass because all her friends were watching. Kitty looked at the mess and felt sorry. Kitty said to her friends, "I am sorry for the mess." Her friends smiled and said, "It's okay, Kitty. We all make messes sometimes." They all helped Kitty clean up the mess, and then they played together. Kitty was happy to have such good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a competitive boy who liked to win. One day, Tim and his friend, Sam, decided to have a race. They wanted to see who could eat toast the fastest. "Ready, set, go!" said Tim. They both started to eat their toast. Tim ate very fast, but Sam ate even faster. Tim wanted to win, so he ate faster and faster. But then, he bit his toast too hard and heard a "crunch!" Tim had break his toast. "Oh no, my toast!" said Tim. Sam stopped eating and looked at Tim. He saw that Tim was sad. Sam said, "It's okay, Tim. We can share my toast." Tim smiled, and they both ate the rest of the toast together. From that day on, Tim learned that it is more important to have fun with friends than to always try to win.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there was a very hairy bear. The bear had a friend, a little bird. The bird and the bear liked to play all day. They were very happy friends. One day, the bird told the bear a secret. The bird said, "Trust me, I know where to find a big, big treasure!" The next day, the bird and the bear went to look for the treasure. They walked and walked until they found a big, big box. The box had a picture of an x-ray on it. The bird said, "This is it! This is the treasure!" The bear was very excited. The bear and the bird opened the box. Inside, they found a lot of yummy food. There were apples, and nuts, and fish. The bear was so happy that he trusted the bird. They ate and ate, and they were very full. The bird and the bear played all day, and they were happy friends forever.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She wanted to choose a pretty landscape to draw. The sun was light and warm on her face. Lucy liked to draw pictures of trees, flowers, and animals. As Lucy walked, she saw a big tree. The tree had many leaves and was very tall. Lucy thought it was perfect for her drawing. So, she sat down and started to draw. She drew the tree, the grass, and the sky. Lucy was very happy with her picture. But then, something unexpected happened! A tiny door opened in the tree. A small, friendly squirrel came out. The squirrel said, "Hello, Lucy! I saw you drawing and I like your picture." Lucy was very surprised. She didn't know squirrels could talk! The squirrel invited Lucy to come inside the tree and meet his family. Lucy made new friends that day and had a great adventure. She learned that sometimes, the best things happen when you least expect them.
