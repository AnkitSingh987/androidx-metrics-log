Title: Tiny and the Bird

Tiny was out in the backyard picking the spiciest peppers he could find. He wanted to make a sauce to pour over his lunch. He took some of the peppers, chopped them up and placed them on the patch he had by the garden. Suddenly, he heard a noise from the sky. He looked up and saw a large, white bird circling above him. It came lower and lower, until it landed on the patch of peppers. Tiny was so scared he started to cry. It was then that he heard a voice. It was coming from the bird. "Do not cry, little one," said the bird. "I am here to help. I will raise these peppers so that you can turn them into the yummiest sauce." Tiny wiped away his tears and smiled. He watched as the bird flapped its wings and the peppers rose up into the air. When they were high enough, the bird turned back to Tiny and said "There, they will turn into a spice sauce that will make your lunch extra delicious!" Tiny thanked the bird again and watched as it flew away. He carefully collected the peppers and ran inside to his kitchen. After a few minutes, he was able to make the most delicious, spicy sauce for his lunch. He was very pleased.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very successful dog. He could run very fast and jump very high. One day, Spot saw a big, wide lake. He had never seen a lake so big before. Spot thought, "I want to go play in the lake!" The next day, Spot went to the wide lake with his friend, a little cat named Fluffy. They were both very excited to play in the water. Spot jumped in the lake and started to bathe. Fluffy was a little scared, but she saw how much fun Spot was having, so she jumped in too. As they played in the water, they saw a big fish. The fish said, "Hello, Spot and Fluffy! I saw you coming to the lake and I knew you would have fun!" Spot and Fluffy laughed and splashed in the water with their new friend, the fish. They had a great day at the wide lake, and they knew they would come back again soon.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy car with four tires. He loved to play with his car all day long. One day, while playing outside, Tim saw another boy with a toy car. The boy said, "Hi, I'm Tom. I like your car!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you! I like your car too. Do you want to play together?" Tom and Tim played with their cars all day. They raced them and had lots of fun. But when it was time to go home, they saw that they had mixed up their cars. Tom said, "Oh no! Which car belongs to me?" Tim looked at the cars and said, "I think this one with the clean tires belongs to you." Tom smiled and said, "Yes, you're right! Thank you, Tim." They both took their cars and went home. From that day on, Tim and Tom were best friends and always played with their cars together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a poor cat. The cat was very hungry. One day, the cat found a big orange on the ground. The cat was very happy. The cat tried to eat the orange, but it was too big for its mouth. The cat rolled the orange to a tree. The cat pushed the orange up the tree, but it was very hard. The cat was tired and needed to rest. After the cat rested, it tried again. This time, the cat pushed the orange to the top of the tree. The orange fell down and broke open. The cat ate the sweet orange and was not hungry anymore. The cat was very happy and rested under the tree.

Tom and Lily were playing in the living room. They saw a shiny telephone on the table. It was Mom's telephone. It was very expensive. "Let's pick it up and talk to someone," Tom said. "Okay," Lily said. They picked up the telephone and pressed some buttons. They heard a beep. "Hello, hello, who is this?" a voice said. Tom and Lily giggled. They did not know who it was. "Hi, we are Tom and Lily. We are playing with the telephone," Tom said. "You are playing with the telephone? That is not good. That is Mom's telephone. It is very expensive. You should put it down and play with something else," the voice said. Tom and Lily felt scared. They did not want to make Mom angry. "Sorry, sorry, we did not know. We will put it down now. Bye bye," Lily said. They put down the telephone and ran away. They decided to play with their toys instead. They hoped Mom would not find out.
