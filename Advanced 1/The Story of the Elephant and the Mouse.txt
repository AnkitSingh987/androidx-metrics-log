Title: The Story of the Elephant and the Mouse

Once upon a time there were two best friends, an elephant and a mouse. One day they were walking by the river and they saw an enormous oat. The elephant picked it up and said to the mouse, “Let’s mark this oat to remember it, so we can find it later.” The mouse agreed and the elephant took a marker out of his pocket. He drew a big star on the oat, so they could find it easily. The two friends then went their separate ways, but the mouse kept thinking about the oat. When he went back to the river to look for it, it was nowhere to be seen. He asked the other animals if they had seen it, but nobody had. The mouse was disappointed and a little sad, so he decided to go back home. On his way home, he saw the elephant again. “Did you take the oat?” he asked the elephant. The elephant shook his head and said, “No, I didn't. It's not nice to take things that don't belong to us. We should always respect the things that other people have.” The mouse thought about the elephant's words and he realised that they were true. From that day forward, the mouse always thought twice before taking something that didn't belong to him.

Lily was a curious girl who liked to explore new things. One day, she found a shiny band in the grass. It was round and had many colors. Lily thought it was very pretty and put it on her finger. She showed the band to her friend Ben, who also liked it. He asked Lily if he could have it, but Lily said no. She wanted to keep the band for herself. Ben was sad and walked away. Lily felt happy with her band, but soon she saw that Ben was playing with other children. They had fun with a ball, a kite, and a bike. Lily wanted to join them, but they said no. They were angry with Lily for being mean to Ben. Lily realized that she had made a mistake. She had valued the band more than her friend. She ran to Ben and said she was sorry. She gave him the band and asked if they could be friends again. Ben smiled and said yes. He thanked Lily and put the band on his finger. They hugged and went to play with the other children. Lily learned that friends are more important than things. She was glad that she had been curious, but also kind. She and Ben shared the band and enjoyed their day.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the beach with his mom. The sky was gloomy, but they wanted to have fun. They saw a big sand hill and decided to play on it. Tom and his mom started to build a sand castle. They worked hard, and it looked great. But then, a big wave came and crashed into their castle. Tom felt sad and began to shout, "No! Our castle is gone!" His mom looked at him and said, "Tom, sometimes things don't go as planned. But we can always try again." They started to build a new castle, and this time, it was even better than before. Moral: Don't give up when things go wrong; try again and you might succeed.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim was very excited. He loved to play on the swings, slide, and in the sand. The sun was shining and it was a beautiful day. Tim's mom gave him some fruit to eat while he played. The fruit was dry, but Tim liked it a lot. He shared his fruit with his new friends at the park. They all laughed and had fun eating the fruit together. After they ate the fruit, Tim and his friends played more games. They ran and jumped and had a great time. When it was time to go home, Tim was tired but happy. He couldn't wait to come back to the park again.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful forest, there lived a little bear. The bear loved to wear a big hat made of pine. He wore it every day when he played with his friends. One day, the bear was playing with his friends, a squirrel and a rabbit. They were all very happy. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. The squirrel and the rabbit also wanted to wear a pine hat like the bear. So, the bear, the squirrel, and the rabbit went to look for more pine to make hats for everyone. They found a big pine tree and picked up the pine from the ground. They made two more pine hats, one for the squirrel and one for the rabbit. Now, the bear, the squirrel, and the rabbit all wore pine hats. They played together in the cheerful forest every day. They were very happy, and all their friends loved their pine hats too. And they all lived happily ever after.
