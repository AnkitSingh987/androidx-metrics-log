Title: Jack and Lola and the Toys

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Jack and Lola. They played together all the time and had lots of fun. One day, Jack and Lola were playing with their toys when Jack excitedly said, "Look at this switch I just found!" Lola was very impressed. She said, "It looks very impressive, Jack! What does it do?" Jack grinned and said, "I don't know, let's find out." He carefully ordered the switch and suddenly music began playing all through the house. Jack and Lola were so excited they began to dance around the room. They could not stop laughing. This was one of the best days they had ever had.

Lila and Sam were very thirsty. They had been walking in the hot sand for a long time. They saw some green trees and a blue pond. They ran towards it. They thought it was an oasis. "Look, Sam, water!" Lila said. "We can drink and rest here." They reached the pond and dipped their hands in it. But the water was not water. It was sticky and slimy. It smelled bad. It was a trap. Suddenly, a big furry monster jumped out of the pond. It had sharp teeth and claws. It roared and grabbed Sam. Sam screamed and tried to get away. But the monster was too strong. It dragged Sam into the pond and disappeared. Lila was scared and sad. She cried and called for Sam. But Sam did not answer. He was gone. Lila was alone in the sand. She did not know what to do. She wished she had never seen the oasis.

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his dad to get some coal. Coal is a black rock that people use to make things hot. It was a mild day, not too hot and not too cold. Tim was happy to help his dad. As they were picking up the coal, Tim started to complain. "Dad, this is hard work," he said. His dad smiled and said, "You're doing a great job, Tim. Just a little more and we can go home." When they were almost done, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and started to help Tim pick up the coal. The bird was very fast and picked up lots of coal. Tim was surprised and happy. The bird helped them finish the work quickly, and they all went home together.

One day, a polite cat named Max wanted to mail a letter. He wrote the letter on a big leaf. Max walked to the mail box, but there was a long vine in the way. Max tried to climb over the vine, but it was too high. He tried to go under, but it was too low. Max was sad. He didn't know how to mail his letter now. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the vine away. Max was happy! Now he could mail his letter. Max put the letter in the mailbox and went home with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, the sky was dark and the ground was wet. Tim's mom did not permit him to play outside because it was not safe. Tim was sad and looked out the window. He saw a beautiful rainbow in the sky. Tim knew that rainbows come after the rain. He thought that maybe the ground would not be wet soon. The next day, the sun was shining bright. Tim's mom saw that the ground was not wet anymore. She permitted Tim to play outside. Tim was so happy! He played all day and remembered the beautiful rainbow.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Sarah. She was three years old. One day, Sarah was feeling very excited. She asked her Mom, "Where are we going?" Her Mom said they were going to the city. Sarah got very nervous, but her Mom told her there was nothing to worry about. When they arrived in the city, there were lots of people and lights and cars. Sarah was very scared and stayed close to her Mom. They started to walk and soon they came to a big park. Sarah's Mom said that she was going to gather some nice flowers in the park while Sarah waited. Sarah was still feeling nervous, so she stayed near the entrance while her Mom went to pick some pretty flowers. When her Mom came back, she was carrying a big bunch of bright flowers. Sarah was so happy to see them. She said, "Mommy, these are beautiful!" They started their walk back home. Sarah was still feeling a little bit nervous, but she was also feeling excited about the beautiful flowers from the city.
