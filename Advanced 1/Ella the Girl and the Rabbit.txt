Title: Ella the Girl and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, in a big palace, there was a little girl named Ella. Ella was an independent girl who liked to play and have fun. She had many friends in the palace, including a kind rabbit named Hoppy. One day, Ella and Hoppy were playing near the palace garden. Hoppy saw a big bee coming close to Ella. Hoppy wanted to warn Ella, so he said, "Ella, be careful! There is a big bee near you!" Ella heard Hoppy and moved away from the bee. She said, "Thank you, Hoppy, for helping me!" After that, Ella and Hoppy played more and more. They had a lot of fun together in the palace. They knew they would always help each other and be good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a gift for Tom's friend, Sam. Sam's birthday party was tomorrow. Tom and his mom walked around the store, looking at all the toys. They saw a big, expensive toy car. "Mom, can we buy this for Sam?" Tom asked. "It's too expensive, Tom," said his mom. "Let's find something else." Tom's mom saw a nice toy truck with a cut on its side. "How about this truck, Tom? It's not expensive and it still looks fun!" she offered. Tom looked at the truck and thought Sam would like it. At the birthday party, Tom gave Sam the toy truck. Sam loved it! He said, "Thank you, Tom! I love my new truck!" They played with the truck all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a plastic ball that he loved to play with. One day, the ball rolled away and got stuck under a big tree. Tim went to the tree and saw a squirrel. He asked the squirrel, "Can you help me get my ball?" The squirrel nodded and climbed up the tree. It kicked the ball, and it fell down. Tim was grateful and said, "Thank you, squirrel!" They played together, kicking the plastic ball. They had lots of fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very strong boy with big muscles. One day, he saw an incredible big rock in the park. He wanted to show his friends how strong he was by moving the rock. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Can you help me move the rock?" Sam questioned, "Why do you want to move it?" Tim said, "I want to show everyone my big muscles!" Sam agreed to help, but they couldn't move the rock. A wise old man saw them trying. He said, "Boys, you don't need to show off your muscles. It's better to be kind and help others." Tim and Sam listened to the old man. They learned that being kind is more important than showing off. And from that day on, they used their strength to help others and be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big market. In the market, there were many things to buy. There were fruits, toys, and clothes. People went to the market to get things they need. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the market with her mom. They wanted to buy some fruits. When they got there, they saw a man selling fruits. Lily asked, "Mom, can we buy some apples?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can. But we have to see if there are any apples left." They looked and looked, but there were no apples. The man said, "I'm sorry, we don't have any apples. But we have many other fruits available." Lily was sad, but she saw some oranges. She asked her mom, "Can we buy oranges instead?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can buy oranges." So, Lily and her mom bought some oranges and went home. They were happy that they could still find some fruits at the market. They learned that sometimes, when things are not there, it is good to remain happy and find something else that is available.
