Title: The Pigeon and the Duck

Once upon a time, there was a little pigeon. The pigeon had a big problem. His friends didn't want to play with him. They said he was too serious. The pigeon was very sad. One day, a kind duck saw the sad pigeon. The duck said, "Why are you so sad?" The pigeon told the duck about his problem. The duck had an idea. "Promise me that you will try to be less serious and have more fun," said the duck. The pigeon promised to try. The next day, the pigeon played with his friends. He was less serious and had more fun. His friends liked the new pigeon. They played together every day. The pigeon was happy, and he never forgot his promise to the duck.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant girl named Amy. She had a favorite toy, a doll with a thread dress. Amy loved to play with her doll all day long. One day, Amy went to the park with her mom and dad. She brought her doll to play with. But, while playing on the swings, she lost her doll. Amy was very sad. Amy and her parents looked for the doll everywhere. They found the doll under a tree, with its thread dress still bright and pretty. Amy was happy again, and they all went home with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a cheerful boy named Tom went to the park to play. He saw a big tent and wanted to fill it with his toys. He ran home, got his toys, and went back to the tent. Tom started to fill the tent with his toys, when a small dog came in. The dog looked at the toys and started to play with them. Tom was happy to have a new friend to play with. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog started to talk! He said, "Thank you for the toys, Tom!" Tom was surprised, but he did not mind. They played together all day in the tent, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a very good dog, but he could not hear. People would say Max was deaf. Max had a special job. He would carry things for people who could not carry them by themselves. One day, Max met a little girl named Lily. Lily wanted to learn how to carry things like Max. So, Max decided to teach her. They would carry toys, food, and even sticks together. Lily was very happy to learn from Max. As they played and carried things together, Max and Lily became good friends. Even though Max could not hear, he was a great teacher. And Lily learned how to carry things just like Max. They were the best of friends and helped many people together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to watch the sky. She would sit outside and look up at the sky. She saw big, white clouds and a bright, yellow sun. She liked the sky a lot. One day, Amy saw a distant star in the sky. It was very far away, but it was very pretty. She began to wonder about the star. She thought, "What is it like to be up there?" Amy asked her mom about the star. Her mom told her that the star was very far away, but it was always watching over her. Amy felt happy knowing that the star was there. She knew that she had a friend in the sky.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big rock on the ground. "Look, Tim!" said his mom. "That is an interesting rock. It has a mineral inside." Tim looked at the rock and saw something shiny. Tim wanted to see more, so he tried to peek inside the rock. He put his eye close to the rock and saw a tiny hole. Inside the hole, there was a shiny thing. "Mom, I see something!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "That's the mineral, Tim. It's very special." Tim picked up the rock and took it home. He put it on his shelf and looked at it every day. He loved his interesting rock with the shiny mineral inside. And every time he peeked at it, he felt happy and excited.
