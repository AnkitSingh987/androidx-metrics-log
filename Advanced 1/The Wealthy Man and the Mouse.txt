Title: The Wealthy Man and the Mouse

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a wealthy man. He had a lot of money and many things. One night, he went to sleep with a big smile on his face. In the morning, the man woke up and found a small mouse in his pocket. The mouse looked up at the man and said, "Hello! I am your new friend." The man was very surprised but happy to have a new friend. They played together all day long. They laughed, ran, and had so much fun. The man learned that having a friend was better than having a lot of money. And they lived happily ever after.

Once, there was a poor little girl. She was walking in the forest and suddenly she saw an old jar. She was curious and wanted to take a closer look. So, she decided to climb up a big tree nearby. The little girl was excited and she reached her hand out to grab the jar, but it was too high! She tried to climb higher, but it was too hard for her. Suddenly, she heard a voice in the distance. It came from a friendly old tree spirit. He said, "Do not worry, little one. If you want to reach the jar, I can help you." The spirit then made a special rope out of magic twigs and leaves. He showed her how to put the rope around her waist and then the spirit helped her climb up the tree. The little girl was so happy that she finally reached the jar! She opened up the lid and what a surprise – it was filled with shiny coins! She thanked the kind tree spirit and ran home to her family. With the coins, she was able to buy things they needed and they all lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, a little bug named Billy lived in a cold, old house. Billy loved to crawl all over the house. He crawled on the walls, the floor, and even the ceiling. One day, while he was crawling, he found a shiny nail on the floor. Billy wanted to show the nail to his friend, Sally the spider. So he said, "Sally, come see what I found!" Sally came down from her web and saw the nail. "Wow, that's a big, shiny nail!" she said. Together, they decided to crawl and find more nails to make a collection. They found many nails, big and small, and had a lot of fun. Billy and Sally became best friends, and they loved to crawl and explore the cold, old house together.

One day, a jolly dog named Max went to the garage to relax. The garage was his favorite spot because it was cozy and full of fun things to play with. Max liked to take naps there and dream about chasing cats and playing with his friends. While Max was in the garage, his friend, a little girl named Lily, came to visit. She saw Max and said, "Hi Max, what are you doing in the garage?" Max wagged his tail and barked, "I am just relaxing here. It's so nice and quiet." Lily smiled and said, "I want to relax too. Can I join you?" Max barked happily and moved over to make room for Lily. Lily and Max spent the day in the garage, relaxing and playing with toys. They had so much fun together. When it was time for Lily to go home, she gave Max a big hug and said, "Thank you for letting me relax with you in the garage, Max. You are a jolly good friend." Max wagged his tail and barked, "You're welcome, Lily. Come back anytime!"

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a patient dog. He liked to help his friends. One day, Max's friend, a big cat named Sam, asked Max to do a job for him. Sam wanted Max to bring him some food. Max went to find food for Sam. He saw a bird with a big piece of bread. Max asked the bird, "Can I have some bread for my friend Sam?" But the bird refused to give Max the bread. Max was sad, but he did not give up. Max kept looking for food. He saw a squirrel with a big nut. Max asked the squirrel, "Can I have the nut for my friend Sam?" The squirrel was kind and gave Max the nut. Max was happy and brought the nut to Sam. Sam was very happy too. They both ate the nut and played together all day.
