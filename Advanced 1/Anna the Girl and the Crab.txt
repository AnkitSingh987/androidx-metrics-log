Title: Anna the Girl and the Crab

One day, a little girl named Anna went to the beach. She saw a lovely crab walking on the sand. Anna wanted to examine the crab, so she went closer. The crab was red, with big eyes and small legs. As Anna looked at the crab, something unexpected happened. The crab started to grow bigger and bigger! Anna was very surprised. She had never seen a crab grow so big before. It was now as big as a car! Then, the big crab did something even more surprising. It began to talk! The crab said, "Hello, Anna! I am a magic crab. Because you were nice to me, I will give you a wish. What do you want?" Anna thought for a moment and said, "I wish for a new toy." The crab snapped its claws, and a new toy appeared. Anna thanked the crab and went home to play with her new toy.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily lived in a small house. One day, her mom gave her an expensive toy. It was a pretty toy car. Lily loved her new car very much. She played with it every day. One day, Lily took her toy car outside. She made it turn left and then turn right. She made it go fast and slow. She was having so much fun! But then, she saw a big cross on the ground. It was made of sticks. Lily was curious and wanted to see it. Lily walked to the cross with her toy car. She made her car turn around the cross. She played with her car near the cross for a long time. Then, it was time to go home. Lily picked up her expensive toy car and walked back to her house. She had a great day playing with her car and finding the big cross.

Once upon a time, there was a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was not happy. It was a bad cloud. It wanted to make rain on the happy town below. One day, the cloud met a little bird. The bird said, "Why are you so bad, cloud? You make everyone sad with your rain." The cloud said, "I don't want to be bad, but I can't help it. I think I need a friend to help me." The little bird thought for a moment, and then said, "I will be your friend, cloud. Let's try to make the town happy together." The cloud felt happy and started to change. With the help of the little bird, the bad cloud became a good cloud. It did not rain on the town anymore. Instead, it made pretty shapes in the sky for the people to look at. The people in the town were happy, and the cloud and the bird were happy too. They were glad they had each other to help them be good friends to the town.

Once there was a girl named Alex. She was three years old and loved playing with her toys. She had a doll, a stuffed bear, and a bracelet. One day, Alex was playing with her bracelet and she noticed something strange. All the colors had faded and the bracelet looked dull. She asked her mom what could be done. Her mom said “Let’s see if we can match the colors in the bracelet.” So, her mom took her to the craft store and they found all sorts of colors that matched her bracelet. They got crayons and paint and markers in all different colors. Alex and her mom worked together to match all the colors and make the bracelet look beautiful again. When they were finished, they both admired their work. Alex smiled and said “I love my bracelet!”

Once upon a time, there was a little pet cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to dance all day long. She would dance when she was happy and dance when she was sad. Fluffy was always grateful for her dance moves. One sunny day, Fluffy was dancing in the garden. She saw a big box near the tree. Fluffy was curious and went to look at the box. When she got close, the box opened and a big, friendly dog jumped out! The dog said, "Hi, I'm Buddy!" Fluffy was scared at first, but then she saw Buddy start to dance. She was so happy to see another pet who loved to dance just like her. Fluffy and Buddy danced together all day long. They became best friends and were grateful to have each other. And from that day on, they always had fun dancing together.
