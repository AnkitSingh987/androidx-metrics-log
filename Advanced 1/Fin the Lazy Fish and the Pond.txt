Title: Fin the Lazy Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a lazy fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big pond. He liked to swim slow and take long naps. One day, while swimming, Fin saw a pretty frame in the water. The frame had a picture of a fast fish. "Wow!" said Fin. "I want to be like that fish!" Fin met a new friend, a little frog named Lily. Lily liked to swim fast. "Come on, Fin! Let's swim fast together!" said Lily. Fin tried to swim fast, but he was still lazy. Lily had an idea. She put the pretty frame with the fast fish picture near the pond. Every day, Fin looked at the picture and swam a little faster. Soon, Fin was swimming fast like the fish in the frame. He was happy and thanked Lily for helping him. Now, Fin and Lily swim fast together and have lots of fun in the big pond.

Once upon a time there was a mom and a dad and their daughter. The daughter was so pale. One day, the dad had to go away. The mom was so sad. The daughter wanted her dad to stay, so she asked her mom, "Where did dad go?" Mom said, "He had to be brave and surrender." Daughter asked, "What is surrender?" Mom said, "Surrender means to give up." The daughter started crying. She wanted her dad to stay with them. The mom hugged her daughter and said, "Don't cry, everything will be ok." The daughter was a bit sad, but now she knew what surrender meant.

Once upon a time, there was a proud dog named Spot. Spot was a good dog who loved his owner, Tim. They lived in a small house and played in the yard every day. One sunny day, Tim told Spot to remain in the yard while he went to the store. Spot sat and waited for Tim to come back. Suddenly, a big ball came flying over the fence. Spot thought it was a game, so he jumped over the fence to get the ball. When Spot got the ball, he saw a girl named Lily. Lily was very happy to see Spot and thought he was her dog, Buddy. Spot was confused but liked playing with Lily. They played together until Tim came back from the store. Tim saw Spot was gone and began to worry. He looked everywhere for Spot. Finally, he heard laughter from the other side of the fence. Tim looked over and saw Spot playing with Lily. He called Spot, and Spot ran back to him. In the end, Spot and Tim were happy to be back together. They became friends with Lily and Buddy, and they all played together every day. Spot remained a proud dog and loved his owner, Tim, very much.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a humble boy named Tim. He lived in a little house with his mom and dad. Tim had a small patch in the backyard where he grew pretty flowers and yummy vegetables. One sunny day, Tim went outside to pick some flowers and vegetables from his patch. He saw his friend, Sue, playing in her yard next door. Tim thought it would be nice to share his flowers and vegetables with Sue. So, he walked over to her with a big smile. But when Tim gave Sue the flowers and vegetables, she said, "No, thank you. I don't want them." Tim felt very sad and walked back to his house with his head down. That night, he didn't want to eat dinner, and he cried himself to sleep.

One day, a little girl named Lucy got an invitation to play at her friend Tom's house. She was very happy and excited to go. She put on her favorite dress and shoes, then ran to Tom's house. When Lucy got there, she saw Tom and his friends playing with a big red ball. Lucy wanted to play too, but she tripped and fell on the ground. She felt embarrassed and her face turned red. Tom and his friends stopped playing and ran to help her. Lucy thought they would laugh at her, but they didn't. Instead, they helped her up and asked if she was okay. Lucy was surprised and happy that her friends were so kind. They all played together with the big red ball and had a great time.
