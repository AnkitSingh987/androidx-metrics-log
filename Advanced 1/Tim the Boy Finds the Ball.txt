Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play hockey. He had a hockey stick and a ball. Tim thought it would be fun to invite his best friend, Sam, to play with him. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Sam, do you want to play hockey with me?" Sam was happy and said, "Yes, I want to play hockey with you, Tim!" They played hockey together for a long time. They laughed and had a lot of fun. But then, Tim hit the ball into a big pile of mud. The ball became very dirty and disgusting. Tim and Sam looked at the disgusting ball and said, "Yuck! We don't want to play with that ball anymore." So, they went to Tim's house to wash the ball and make it clean again. After they cleaned the ball, they played more hockey and had a great day together. Tim was happy he invited Sam to play.

Once upon a time, in a quiet park, there was a swing. The swing was very happy. It loved to play with the kids. Every day, the sun would shine and kids would come to play on the swing. One day, a big dog came to the park. The dog saw the swing and wanted to play too. The dog jumped on the swing and started to swing back and forth. The swing was scared but tried to be brave. Then, something unexpected happened. The swing broke! The big dog fell off and ran away. The kids were sad and the swing was sad too. The park was quiet again and the sun did not shine as bright anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Mia was a happy girl, but she did not like tea. She would say, "I hate tea!" every time her mommy made it for her. One day, Mia's mommy was very sad. She wanted to make Mia happy, so she tried to make a new kind of tea. She put in sweet things like honey and yummy fruits. Mia saw the new tea and thought it looked different. Mia took a sip of the new tea. She still did not like it. "I hate tea!" she said again. Her mommy was very sad. Mia felt bad for making her mommy sad, but she could not like tea no matter how hard she tried. In the end, Mia's mommy was still sad and Mia still hated tea.

Once there was a brilliant girl called Mia. Every day Mia would follow a post that was near her home. Everyday Mia would watch the post and imagine magical things it might be telling her. One day, Mia followed the post further than usual and discovered a magical surprise. It was a shiny red balloon glistening in the sun. Mia followed the balloon higher and higher until suddenly it came free and she was left with a wishful feeling in her heart. Mia wished for a day filled with surprises like this, so she decided to follow the post every day. Each day Mia would find something different, like little gems and magical feathers. Mia's adventures were the most brilliant adventures of all. Whenever people asked how Mia could have such a brilliant day, Mia smiled and said, "Just follow the post."

Once upon a time, in an ancient house, there lived a young boy. His name was Tim. Tim was a good boy. He liked to play with his toys and his dog, Sam. One day, Tim found a big, old box in the house. He wanted to see what was inside. Tim tried to open the box, but it was hard. Tim asked his mom for help. She said, "No, Tim. You should not open that box. It is not for young kids like you." Tim was sad, but he still wanted to open the box. He thought about it all day and all night. When everyone was asleep, Tim went to the box. He tried and tried, and then he opened it. But, oh no! Out came a big, scary monster. The monster was very angry. It chased Tim and his family out of the ancient house. They could never go back home again.
