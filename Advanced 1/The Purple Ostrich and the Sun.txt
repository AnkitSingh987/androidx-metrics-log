Title: The Purple Ostrich and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big purple ostrich. The ostrich had a very long neck. It liked to play in the sun all day. One day, the ostrich saw a tree. The tree had a swing. The ostrich wanted to hang on the swing. It tried to reach the swing with its long neck. The ostrich hung on the swing and went up and down. It had so much fun. The sun was shining, and the purple ostrich was happy. The end.

Once upon a time there was a kid. He was a bald kid, because he didn't have any hair. He enjoyed pretending. He would pretend to be a cowboy, or a pirate, or a super hero. One day, he decided to pretend to be flying in the sky. He held his arms out like wings and he pretended to soar high above the ground. He pretended to be a bird, flying through the clouds. He imagined that he could see mountains and rivers and trees below him. He pretended to look down and see all kinds of animals running around. He loved pretending, because it was so much fun. When he was pretending, he could be whomever he wanted to be. He could go anywhere he wanted to go. After he finished pretending, the kid smiled and ran off to find his next adventure. He couldn't wait to start pretending again.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived an honest tiger named Tim. Tim was friends with all the animals in the forest. He liked to play with them and wave his big tail. They all knew that Tim was a good friend and would never hurt them. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his friend, a small bird named Bella. They were running and laughing when they saw a big box in the middle of the forest. They had never seen a box like this before. Tim and Bella looked at each other, and then they looked at the box. They decided to open it. When they opened the box, they found a magic wand inside. Tim waved the wand, and something unexpected happened. All the animals in the forest turned into tigers! Tim and Bella were shocked. They did not know what to do. Tim waved the wand again, and all the animals turned back to normal. They all laughed and played together, happy to be themselves again. And they knew that Tim, the honest tiger, would always be their friend.

One day, a little angel was flying in the sky. She saw a boy with a dirty face. The boy was sad because he lost his toy. The angel wanted to help him. The angel said, "Do not cry, I will find your toy. But you must hurry, it will be dark soon." The boy nodded and they both started to look for the toy. They looked under the trees and near the flowers, but they could not find it. Then, they saw a big dog with the toy in its mouth. The boy was scared, but the angel said, "Do not worry, I will get your toy back." The angel flew to the dog and asked, "Please give the toy back to the boy." The dog listened and gave the toy back. The boy was very happy and thanked the angel. They both went home with smiles on their faces.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. They saw many fun things to do. Mia played on the swings, jumped on the slide, and ran around with her friends. It was a happy day. Mia saw a boy named Tom sitting on a bench. He had an interesting object in his hand. It was a big, colorful book. Mia wanted to know what it was. She went to Tom and asked him about the object. Tom said, "This is a book! I like to read it. It has fun stories inside." Mia thought that was very cool. She asked her mom if she could read the book too. They sat down with Tom and read the interesting book together. They all had a great time at the park.
