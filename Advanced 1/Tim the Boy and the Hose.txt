Title: Tim the Boy and the Hose

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a hose. He liked to water the plants in his yard. Tim was very generous. He shared his hose with his friends. One hot day, Tim and his friends played with the hose. They sprayed water on each other. They laughed and had fun. But Tim sprayed too much water on his friend, Sam. Sam got very wet and cried. Tim felt bad. He knew he made a mistake. He said sorry to Sam. Tim learned to be more careful with the hose. He did not want to make his friends sad again. Tim did not regret saying sorry. He knew it was the right thing to do.

One day, a rich man named Tom had a big house. He had a dishwasher, too. Tom wanted to mail a letter to his friend, Sam. He put the letter in the mailbox and went back inside. Tom's cat, Fluffy, saw the letter. Fluffy thought the letter was a toy. She took it out of the mailbox and played with it. The letter got all messy. Tom saw Fluffy and said, "Oh no, Fluffy! That is not a toy. That is my letter to Sam." Tom was sad. He did not know what to do. Then, he had an idea. He put the letter in the dishwasher to clean it. The letter got all clean and dry. Tom was happy. Just then, Sam came to visit Tom. Tom said, "Sam, I was just mailing you a letter!" Sam laughed and said, "That's funny, I was mailing you a letter too!" They both laughed and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sarah. She was three years old. One day Sarah went with her Mama to the park. There, Sarah saw the prettiest rose flower. It was red and beautiful. 'Mama, what is this?' Sarah said and pointed at the rose. 'It's a rose', Mama said. Sarah smiled. She was very excited. 'Can I pick it?' she asked. 'Yes, go ahead,' Mama said. So Sarah carefully picked the rose. She admired its beauty and smelled its sweet scent. Then Sarah said, 'I'm going to keep it forever.' Mama smiled. Then she discovered a patch of strawberries. 'Look, Sarah. Do you see those tasty strawberries?' 'Yummy!' Sarah said. She picked some strawberries and ate them. They were so sweet and tasty. Sarah was so happy. She had discovered the prettiest rose, and tasted yummy strawberries.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, boring folder. He did not like it because it was just plain and had nothing fun on it. Tim wanted to make it look nice. One day, Tim found some colorful stickers. He thought they were very pretty. He put the stickers on his boring folder. Now, the folder was not boring anymore. It was very nice to look at. Tim went to his friend's house. They all saw his folder and liked it a lot. They all enjoyed looking at the stickers. Tim was very happy. He had turned his boring folder into something fun for everyone to enjoy.

One day, a wise rabbit hopped through the forest. As she hopped, a curious object caught her eye. She hopped closer and saw something she had never seen before. She hopped around it in circles as she tried to understand what it was. Suddenly, a soft voice asked, "What are you looking at, little rabbit?". She looked up, and there was a bird perched on a branch above her. The rabbit smiled and said, "I'm looking at this strange object. What is it?". The bird laughed and said, "That's a stick! A wise rabbit like you should already know that". The rabbit smiled and thanked the bird. Then with a hop, she was off again.

Mira and Sami are best friends. They like to play in the park with their toys. One day, they see a big truck with a trunk. The trunk is open and full of boxes. "Wow, look at that!" Sami says. "I wonder what is inside the boxes." "Maybe we can find out," Mira says. She has an idea. She urges Sami to follow her. "Come on, let's go and peek." "But what if someone sees us?" Sami asks. He is a little scared. He knows they should not touch other people's things. "Don't worry, it will be quick," Mira says. She is very curious. She thinks it will be fun. She is not a reliable friend. They run to the truck and climb on the trunk. They open one of the boxes. Inside, they see a lot of shiny things. They are necklaces, bracelets, rings and earrings. They are very pretty. "Wow, look at these!" Mira says. She takes a necklace and puts it around her neck. She feels like a princess. "Hey, these are not for us!" Sami says. He sees a sign on the box. It says "Jewelry Store". He knows they are doing something wrong. He wants to put the necklace back. But Mira does not listen. She takes another necklace and gives it to Sami. "Here, you can have this one. It matches your eyes." "No, thank you," Sami says. He does not want to take it. He hears a noise. He looks around. He sees a man coming to the truck. He is the driver. He looks angry. "Uh-oh, we have to go!" Sami says. He jumps off the trunk and runs away. But Mira does not hear him. She is too busy looking at the shiny things. She does not see the man until he is very close. He shouts at her. "Hey, you little thief! What are you doing with my jewelry? Give it back!" Mira is very scared. She drops the necklace and tries to run away. But the man is faster. He grabs her by the arm. He is very strong. "Help, help!" Mira cries. She wishes she had listened to Sami. He is a reliable friend. He would not leave her alone. She hopes he will come back and save her.
