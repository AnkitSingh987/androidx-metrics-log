Title: Lily the Girl and the Mirror

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys in her room. One day, she found a big mirror in her room. She had never seen it before. Lily felt a little uncomfortable because she did not know where it came from. Lily decided to peek at the mirror. She saw her face and her toys in the reflection. She thought it was fun to see herself and her toys in the mirror. She started to dance and play in front of the mirror. She felt happy and not uncomfortable anymore. Lily's mom came into the room and saw her playing with the mirror. She told Lily that the mirror was a gift from her grandma. Lily was happy to know where the mirror came from. She thanked her mom and continued to play and dance in front of the mirror.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat said, "Let's have a race! We will see who can zoom the fastest." The dog agreed, and they got ready to race. The cat and dog ran and ran. They were both very fast. The cat was tired and wanted to relax for a bit. The dog saw the cat relax and thought it was not fair. He said, "You can't relax! We need to finish the race!" The cat did not want to listen to the dog. She wanted to relax and not race anymore. The dog got very mad and they started to fight. They fought and fought until they both got hurt. In the end, no one won the race and they were both sad.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to paint. He had many friends because he was popular. One day, he wanted to make a new color. So, he decided to mix his paints. He asked his friend, Sally, "Do you want to help me mix paint?" Sally said, "Yes, I want to help!" They mixed red and blue paint. They made a beautiful purple color. Tim and Sally showed their friends the new color they made. All their friends loved it. They all wanted to paint with the new color too. They learned that when they work together, they can make beautiful things. The moral of the story is that working together can help make something great.

One day, a girl named Lucy found a lucky celery. She was so happy! She showed it to her friend Tom. Tom wanted the lucky celery too. They both wanted to keep it. Lucy said, "I found it first, so it is mine." But Tom said, "I saw it first, so it should be mine." They both did not want to share the lucky celery. They started to fight. They both held the lucky celery and tried to stretch it. They pulled and pulled. The lucky celery broke into two pieces. Lucy and Tom looked at each other and said, "Now we both have a lucky celery!" They stopped fighting and shared the lucky celery. They were both happy and played together. They learned that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named True. True had a big friend, the elephant. They liked to play together in the forest. One day, the elephant wanted to send True on a fun ride. The elephant used its long nose to send True up in the air. True felt like he was flying. It was so much fun. When True came back down, he and the elephant laughed and played some more. They were very happy together. And they stayed best friends forever.

Anna liked to draw with her pencil. She drew many things: flowers, stars, animals, and people. She had a big book of paper where she kept all her drawings. She was very proud of them. One day, a new boy came to her class. His name was Ben. He was shy and did not talk much. He sat next to Anna and watched her draw. Anna saw him and smiled. She wanted to be his friend. "Do you like to draw?" she asked him. Ben nodded. He looked at her pencil. It was yellow and had a pink eraser. He reached out his hand to touch it. Anna felt scared. She did not want Ben to take her pencil. It was her favorite one. She pulled it away from him and hugged it to her chest. "No, this is mine!" she said. "You can't have it!" Ben looked sad. He lowered his hand and turned away. He did not want to make Anna angry. He just wanted to see her pencil. Anna saw his face and felt sorry. She realized that Ben was not trying to steal her pencil. He was just curious. He was harmless. She decided to trust him. "Sorry, I was mean," she said. "Do you want to draw with me?" Ben looked up and smiled. He nodded again. Anna gave him another pencil from her bag. It was blue and had a green eraser. She opened her book of paper and tore out a page. She gave it to Ben and showed him how to draw a flower. Ben followed her and drew a flower too. It was not very good, but Anna did not mind. She liked his flower. She liked Ben. They drew more things together: stars, animals, and people. They talked and laughed. They became friends. Anna was happy. She was glad she shared her pencil.
