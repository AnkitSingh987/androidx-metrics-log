Title: The Story of the Story and the Friends

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there lived a big, red hen. She loved to lay eggs for all her friends. The hen's friends would come from the east, west, north, and south to get her eggs. They loved her eggs because they were big and tasty. One day, the hen laid a very special egg. It was shiny and golden. All her friends wanted the golden egg. They began to fight over who should have it. The hen was sad to see her friends fight. Then, something unexpected happened. The shiny, golden egg began to crack. Out came a little, yellow chick. The chick looked at the fighting friends and said, "Please don't fight. Share and be kind to each other." The friends stopped fighting and felt sorry. They learned that being kind and sharing is more important than having the most special things. From that day on, the friends never fought again. They shared everything and lived happily ever after. The hen continued to lay eggs, and the little, yellow chick grew up to be a wise and kind leader. The moral of the story is to always share and be kind to your friends.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play together. One day, Lily has a cake. It is her birthday cake. It is big and pink and has candles on it. Ben sees the cake. He wants some cake. He asks Lily, "Can I borrow some cake?" Lily says, "No, you cannot borrow cake. Cake is not for borrowing. Cake is for eating. And this is my birthday cake. You can have some later, after we sing and blow the candles." Ben is sad and angry. He thinks Lily is mean. He waits until Lily is not looking. Then he tries to take some cake. But he is not careful. He knocks the cake off the table. The cake falls on the floor. It is broken. The candles go out. Lily hears the noise. She turns around. She sees the broken cake. She sees Ben. She is very upset. She cries, "Ben, what did you do? You broke my birthday cake! That was not nice. That was very bad. You are not my friend anymore." Ben feels sorry. He did not mean to break the cake. He says, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was hungry. I wanted some cake. I did not mean to break it. Please forgive me. Please be my friend again." Lily stops crying. She looks at Ben. She sees that he is sorry. She says, "OK, Ben. I forgive you. But you have to promise me something. You have to promise me that you will not borrow cake again. Cake is not for borrowing. Cake is for sharing. Do you understand?" Ben nods. He says, "I understand, Lily. I promise. I will not borrow cake again. I will share cake with you. And I will help you clean up the mess." Lily smiles. She says, "Thank you, Ben. You are my friend again. Come on, let's clean up. Maybe we can still have some cake. And maybe we can make another wish." They hug. They clean up. They have some cake. They make another wish. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man named Grandpa Joe. He lived in a small house with a big yard. Grandpa Joe loved to pour water on his plants to help them grow big and strong. One day, Grandpa Joe heard a loud noise outside. He went to see what it was. In the sky, he saw big, bright fireworks! They were red, blue, and green. Grandpa Joe liked to watch the fireworks with his friends. Grandpa Joe invited his friends to come see the fireworks. They all sat together in the yard, watching the beautiful colors light up the sky. They laughed and clapped as the fireworks went boom. Grandpa Joe was happy to share this special night with his friends.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went on a trip with his mom and dad. They went to the big zoo. Tim was very happy. He saw a lot of animals like lions, monkeys, and elephants. At lunch, Tim's mom said, "Tim, do you want a spicy sandwich?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" He took a big bite and his face turned red. It was very spicy! Tim drank a lot of water to cool down. After lunch, they saw a man giving away balloons. Tim received a big, blue balloon. He was so excited! But, as they walked, the balloon popped! Tim was sad. Then, a nice zoo worker saw Tim and gave him a new, even bigger balloon. Tim's trip to the zoo was the best day ever!
