Title: Spot the Dog and the University

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and jump all day. He was very happy. One day, Spot went to a big place called University. At the University, there were many kids to play with. Spot was very excited to make new friends. Spot found a tasty treat on the ground. He tasted it and liked it a lot. He shared the treat with his new friends. They all laughed and played together. Spot had a great day at the University.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big piece of chalk. He loved to rub the chalk on the ground to make pretty pictures. One day, Tim drew a big sun with a smiley face. He was very happy with his picture. Tim's friend, Sue, saw the picture and said, "Wow! Your sun is so pretty, Tim!" Sue was jealous because she did not have any chalk to draw with. She asked, "Can I use your chalk to draw a picture too, please?" Tim smiled and said, "Of course, Sue! Let's draw together." They took turns rubbing the chalk on the ground. Sue drew a beautiful flower next to Tim's sun. They were both very happy with their pictures. In the end, they were not jealous anymore because they shared the chalk and had fun together. And from that day on, Tim and Sue always drew pretty pictures together with their chalk.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lily. Lily loved to sit in the big hall with her friends. They would play and have fun all day long. One day, Lily's friend, a small dog named Max, said, "Let's sit in the hall and play a new game!" Lily and her friends were excited to try something new. They all sat in the hall and played the new game. They laughed and had a great time. In the end, Lily and her friends were very happy that they tried the new game together.

Ben and Lily were playing with their yellow ball in the park. They liked to throw the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they missed and the ball rolled away, but they always ran after it and brought it back. One time, the ball went too far and landed near a big dog. The dog was black and brown and had sharp teeth. It saw the ball and picked it up with its mouth. Ben and Lily were scared. They wanted their ball back, but they did not want to go near the dog. "Hey, that's our ball!" Ben shouted. "Give it back!" The dog growled and shook its head. It did not want to give the ball back. It liked to chew on it and make it squeak. "Please, mister dog, can we have our ball?" Lily asked. "We will give you a treat if you do." The dog looked at Lily and tilted its head. It was curious. It wondered what kind of treat she had. It dropped the ball and walked towards her. Ben saw his chance. He ran to the ball and grabbed it. He felt happy and relieved. He had his ball back. But the dog felt angry and cheated. It had lost its toy and its treat. It barked and chased after Ben. Ben and Lily ran away as fast as they could. They did not stop until they reached their mom, who was sitting on a bench. They hugged her and told her what happened. "Wow, that was a close one," mom said. "You were very brave, but you should be careful around strange dogs. They might not be friendly." Ben and Lily nodded. They learned their lesson. They decided to play with their yellow ball somewhere else, away from the dog. They felt safe and happy with their mom.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Daisy who was very excited. She was so excited because she had a special message. She was full of joy and ran to her mom to share the message. She said to her mom, “Mom, Mom I'm so excited, I got a special message! What do you think it is?” Her mom smiled and said, “I'm not sure sweetie, why don't you open it and tell me?” Daisy opened the message and was even more excited. She shouted, “Mom, Mom! It's an invitation to my best friend's birthday party!” Her mom smiled and said, “That's wonderful sweetie, now you have something to look forward to.” Daisy ran off with a big smile on her face. She couldn't wait for her friend's birthday party. Now she had something to be excited about!
