Title: Tom the Boy and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a restless boy who loved to play all day. He liked to dig in the dirt with his toy trucks. One day, Tom found a big hole in his yard. He thought it was a mine. Tom wanted to work in the mine like a big, strong man. He put on his hat and grabbed his toy shovel. He dug and dug all day. He found rocks, dirt, and bugs. Tom was very happy with his mine. At the end of the day, Tom was very tired. He had worked hard in his mine. His mom called him inside for dinner. Tom washed his hands and sat down to eat. He told his mom and dad all about his mine. They were so proud of their little worker.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a faucet. The faucet was very honest. It always gave water when people needed it. One day, the faucet wanted to become friends with the other things in the house. The faucet said to the cup, "Hi, cup! Can we be friends?" The cup said, "Yes, faucet! We can be friends. You are very honest and always give water when I am empty." The next day, the faucet saw the plate. The faucet asked, "Hello, plate! Can we be friends?" The plate said, "Of course, faucet! You are honest and help me when I need to be washed." Soon, the faucet became friends with all the things in the house. They all liked the faucet because it was honest and helpful. And they all lived happily in the small house.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was an adventurous boy. He loved to play outside and try new things. One day, Tim saw a big box of cereal on the kitchen table. He thought it would be fun to play with the cereal. Tim called his friend, Sam. "Come play with me and my cereal!" he said. Sam came over and they started to play. They threw the cereal in the air and laughed as it fell down. They made shapes with the cereal on the floor. It was so much fun! But then, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the mess and was very sad. "Why did you make such a big mess with the cereal?" she asked. Tim and Sam looked at each other and knew they were in trouble. They tried to clean up, but it was too late. Tim's mom did not applaud their adventurous play. Instead, she sent Sam home and Tim had to go to bed early. It was a bad ending to their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl who had a pretty frame. It was her favorite possession and she loved it very much. One day, the girl thought it would be fun to play with the frame by throwing it around. She threw it up high in the sky and it flew away. The girl was sad. She tried to look for her pretty frame, but try as she might, she could not find it. The girl was very unhappy. Without her favorite frame, she was miserable. She was sad that she had made a bad choice and had thrown away her pretty frame.

One day, a kind cat named Kitty met a little dog named Spot. Spot had a hurt lip. Kitty asked, "What happened to your lip, Spot?" Spot said, "I was playing and fell. It hurts a lot." Kitty felt sad for Spot and wanted to help. Kitty insisted, "Let me help you. I know what to do." Kitty went to find a big leaf. She put the leaf on Spot's lip to make it feel better. They played together all day. After playing, they found out that the leaf was magic! The magic leaf made Spot's lip all better. Spot and Kitty were so happy. They thanked the magic leaf and played together every day. They became the best of friends and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to travel. He would fly high in the sky and visit many places. Tim was a very popular bird because he was friendly and always happy. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a small bug who also liked to travel. Tim asked Sam, "Do you want to travel with me? We can fly together and see many fun places!" Sam was excited and said, "Yes, I would love to travel with you, Tim!" So, Tim and Sam started to travel together. They flew over big trees, pretty flowers, and sparkling water. They played, laughed, and had a great time. All the other animals loved to see Tim and Sam fly together. They were the most popular friends in the whole forest. And they lived happily ever after.
