Title: Max and Lily and the Ball

One day, a big dog named Max went to the park with his friend, a small cat named Lily. They loved to play together. Max was very clumsy, and Lily was very fast. They played in the sun and had lots of fun. Max said, "I am tired. Let's take a break." They both sat under a tree. Max let out a big yawn. Lily said, "Wait, I see a ball! Let's play with it." Max agreed, but he was still very tired. When Max tried to catch the ball, he was too clumsy and missed it. The ball rolled away very fast. Max and Lily ran after it, but they could not catch it. They were sad. But then, a nice boy found the ball and gave it back to them. Max and Lily were happy again and played until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a humble lily in a pond near a river. One day, a frog was swimming by, but the river was too swift and swept the frog away. Lily saw this and with her tiny legs, she jumped into the river to help rescue the frog. The frog said to Lily, “Thank you! I thought I was doomed!” Lily smiled and said, “It was no problem. I am happy to help!" Lily then put the frog safely back onto the shore and the frog hopped away. The lily felt proud that she could help the frog, and from that day onward, she was known as the rescue lily.

Once upon a time, there was a wide comet in the sky. It hung above the sky like a shiny star. All the children loved it and wanted to touch it. One night the comet was even bigger, and brighter. Everyone was so excited to see it. They ran outside and danced around, trying to reach it. But the comet was much too high. So they decided to make a plan. Everyone stopped, then grabbed each others hands to make a big circle. Then, they all jumped and grabbed onto the comet. They hung there for a while, bouncing and laughing. The children could see the stars and even some houses. Then, the comet slowly started to move. It flew away, taking the children in the sky. The children waved goodbye to everyone on the ground. They kept flying until the comet was back in the sky. The children were so happy, they never wanted to leave!

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play with blocks. They had many blocks of different colors and shapes. They could build houses, towers, cars, and animals with their blocks. They had fun sharing and helping each other. One day, Lily and Ben found a big box of new blocks in the playroom. They were very happy and excited. They opened the box and saw many shiny and sparkly blocks. They wanted to play with them right away. "Let's build a castle with these blocks!" Lily said. "OK, but I want to use the red blocks. They are my favorite color," Ben said. "No, I want the red blocks. They are prettier than the other blocks," Lily said. They started to argue and fight over the red blocks. They pulled and pushed and shouted. They did not share or help each other. They did not have fun. Soon, the red blocks were all broken and scratched. The other blocks were scattered and mixed up. The castle was not built. The playroom was a mess. Lily and Ben looked at each other and felt sad and sorry. They realized they had been terrible friends. They had wasted their time and ruined their blocks. They said sorry to each other and hugged. They decided to share and help each other again. They picked up the blocks and put them back in the box. They agreed to play with the old blocks first and save the new blocks for later. They learned that playing with blocks was more fun when they were kind and generous. They also learned that new things were not always better than old things. They were happy and grateful for what they had.
