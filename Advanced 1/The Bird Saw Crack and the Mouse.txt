Title: The Bird Saw Crack and the Mouse

One day, a big bird saw a crack in a tree. The bird thought, "I guess I can make an impressive nest here." So, the bird started to build a nest in the crack. A little mouse saw the bird building the nest. The mouse said, "Wow, that is an impressive nest!" The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, little mouse. I guess it is a good place for a nest." But then, something unexpected happened. The tree started to shake! The crack opened wide and out came a squirrel. The squirrel said, "This is my home, too!" The bird and the mouse were surprised. They all became friends and shared the impressive home in the tree.

There was a boy. He was very sick so he went to the doctor for help. The doctor said he needed to go to a meeting. The boy went to the meeting and it was calm. But even though the meeting was calm, the boy was still sick. The boy made lots of friends at the meeting and everyone was happy, but the boy still stayed sick. The boy was worried. He tried to find help, but nothing seemed to work. His friends at the meeting were sad and the boy was worried too. He wanted to be healed, but he didn't know how. So the boy kept trying and eventually his friends convinced him to try one more time. But when the boy tried to heal himself, he got much worse. His friends were sad and the boy was too. The boy never got to be healed. He remained sick until the end.

Once upon a time, there was a big crane who lived by a lake. The crane had a long neck and liked to bow to say hello to his friends. One day, a little duck came to the lake. The duck was silly and liked to splash in the water. The crane bowed to the duck and said, "Hi, do you want to be my friend?" The duck looked at the crane and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend! Let's play in the water together." So, the crane and the duck played and splashed in the water all day. As the sun went down, the crane and the duck said goodbye to each other. The crane bowed and the silly duck quacked. They promised to play together again the next day. And from that day on, the crane and the duck were best friends who loved to play in the water together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wise man. He had a car. One day, he wanted to wash his car. So, he got soap and water. As he was washing his car, a little boy came by. The boy asked, "Can I help you wash your car?" The wise man said, "Yes, you can help me." They washed the car together. Then, something unexpected happened. The boy found a gold coin in the car. The wise man said, "You can keep it because you helped me." The boy learned that it is good to help others. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy family. One day, they decided to go to the beach. As they walked, they could hear the sound of the waves crashing in the background. When they arrived, they saw a fisherman sitting on the beach near them. He was holding a tube full of fish. The family asked him what he had caught. The fisherman replied, "I've caught some really nice fish for dinner tonight. Would you like to buy some?" The family said that they would and began to walk away with the tube full of fish. As they went, the fisherman said, "Good luck with your dinner! And be sure to enjoy the wealth of seafood this tube has provided you!" The family was very thankful for the fisherman's kind words. They waved goodbye and enjoyed the seafood dinner that night.
