Title: Lily the Girl and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a box full of words. Lily loved her words very much. She thought they were very useful. One day, Lily found a word that she did not know. The word was "rot". She asked her mom what it meant. Her mom said, "Rot is when something gets old and not good anymore, like an old apple." Lily did not want her words to rot. She wanted them to stay useful. So, she took very good care of her words. She read them every day and shared them with her friends. And her words stayed useful and happy, just like Lily.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little pine tree. The pine tree felt helpless because it was so small. All the other trees were big and strong, but the pine tree was not. One day, a kind squirrel came to the pine tree. The squirrel said, "Don't worry, little pine tree. I will help you." The squirrel went to find some nuts. The squirrel put the nuts on the ground near the pine tree. The squirrel told the pine tree, "Eat these nuts and you will grow big and strong." The pine tree ate the nuts and started to grow. Soon, the pine tree was not helpless anymore. It was big and strong, just like the other trees. The pine tree said, "Thank you, kind squirrel, for helping me." The squirrel and the pine tree became good friends, and they lived happily in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a yellow ball that she loved to play with. One day, while playing, the ball hit a tree and got a hole in it. Mia looked at her ball and let out a big sigh. Mia's mom saw her sad face and had an idea. She found some tape and showed Mia how to fix her yellow ball. Mia put the tape on the hole and her ball was as good as new. She smiled and hugged her mom. Now, Mia could play with her yellow ball again. She bounced it high and low, and ran around with it all day. She was so happy that her ball was fixed. And every time she played with it, she remembered how her mom helped her with the tape.

Little Emma had a very special bike. She was always so excited to go outside and ride it. It was blue and red, and very comfortable to ride. Every day, Emma would go outside before dinner and take her bike for a ride. One day, Emma was riding her bike when she saw her friend, Sarah. "Hello Sarah!" yelled Emma as she rode past. Sarah smiled. "Would you like to play an exercise game?" She asked. Emma was so excited. She quickly hopped off her bike and said, "Yes, let's play!" The girls decided to ride around the block three times. When they finished, both of the girls felt tired but also very happy. "That was so fun!" Emma said. Sarah nodded in agreement. "Riding is such a fun exercise! I feel so comfortable when I am riding my bike!" Emma smiled and gave Sarah a big hug. It was definitely a day to remember!

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Bob. Bob loved to play games with his friends. One day, Bob and his friends decided to play a game called "Catch the Ball." They wanted to see who could score the most points. Bob's friend, a little dog named Tim, called out, "I want to play too!" Bob said, "Yes, come join us!" They all played together, laughing and having fun. Tim was very good at the game and scored many points. Bob was happy to see his friend do so well. At the end of the game, Tim had the highest score. Bob called out, "Great job, Tim! You won!" All the friends cheered for Tim. They were all very happy and had a great day playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a barber who lived in a small town. The barber had a deep voice that everyone liked. He was very good at cutting hair and making people look nice. One day, a little boy came to the barber shop for a haircut. The barber asked the boy, "Do you permit me to cut your hair?" The boy said, "Yes, please." The barber was happy and started to cut the boy's hair. After the haircut, the boy looked in the mirror and saw his new haircut. He was very happy with how he looked. He thanked the barber and went home with a big smile. The boy's mom also liked the haircut and they both thanked the barber again. The barber felt very happy that he could make people look nice and feel good.
