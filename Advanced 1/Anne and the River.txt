Title: Anne and the River

Anne went to the river with her mom. The river was dark and purple. "Look at the water," Mom said. "It's so pretty." But then it started to rain. Slowly, the river started to fill up and flood. Anne's mom said, "It's time to go home, Anne." But Anne didn't want to leave. She was having so much fun. She begged her mom to stay. "Please Mom, I don't want to go home!" she said. But her mom wouldn't listen. She grabbed Anne's hand and said it was time to go. Anne was so upset that she slapped her mom's hand away. "NO!" she shouted. Her mom was very angry. She shook her head and said, "That was very bad, Anne." Anne knew it was bad, but she didn't care. All she cared about was staying by the river. But the rain had gotten too heavy. The flood waters quickly rose and soon the river was too dangerous. "We have to go NOW, Anne," her mom shouted. It was too late. The flood waters were too strong and they had to rush home to safety. Anne had to learn the hard way that sometimes it is best to listen to her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big bottle of glue. Tim loved to make things with the glue. He made houses, cars, and trees. But sometimes, the glue was difficult to use. It would stick to his fingers and make a mess. One day, Tim wanted to make a big tower. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam said, "I can help, but we need to be careful with the glue. It can be difficult to use." Tim allowed Sam to help him, and they started to build the tower. As they built the tower, Tim and Sam learned to be patient with the glue. They took turns and helped each other when it was difficult. Finally, the tower was finished, and it was beautiful. Tim and Sam were proud of their work. Tim said, "Thank you, Sam, for helping me. We did a great job together!" Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. We learned that when we work together and are patient, we can do difficult things." The moral of the story is that working together and being patient can help us accomplish difficult tasks.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. She was three years old and loved to act. On one afternon, Lucy went to the park with her mom. She ran around, giggled, and played some games. She had so much fun. Later, she went on the swing and acted like she was a bird soaring through the sky. As the sun set, Lucy got very tired. Her mom said it was time to go home. She grabbed Lucy and Lucy yawned with a big smile. Lucy and her mom hugged and walked home together. Lucy’s mom tucked her in bed and said goodnight. As she drifted off to sleep, Lucy was full of happy thoughts from all the fun she had at the park.

Sam and Lucy went to the harbor. The harbor was big, and they could see lots of boats with flags. They were excited to explore. Sam grabbed Lucy's hand and they began to walk. As they approached the harbor, they saw a beautiful boat. "Look, Lucy!" Sam said, pointing at the boat. "Let's go explore it!" Lucy was a bit embarrassed, because she had never been on a boat before. But she bravely nodded her head in agreement. Sam grabbed Lucy's hand and pulled her onto the boat. They explored the deck and looked around for a while. Soon it was time to leave, but just as they were about to go, a big wave rocked the boat and Lucy lost her balance. Sam grabbed her quickly and held her until the rocking stopped. "Thank you!", Lucy said, smiling. Sam smiled back. He was happy he could help her. They waved goodbye to the harbor and went home.

Once upon a time there was a group of friends. They all liked to play together in the park. But one day one of them felt selfish. He wanted to play on the swings but the others wanted to play in the sand pit. He wouldn't listen and so he went to the swings. He felt happy that he was swinging by himself. The others felt sad. They went to find him and tapped him on the neck. They asked him why he was being so selfish. He felt embarrassed. He apologized to his friends and said he would come and play in the sand pit. The friends were happy again and they all played together. They laughed together and made sand castles. It was even more fun than playing on the swings. The friends learnt that it was not nice to be selfish. They promised to always have fun together and never be selfish again. And they lived happily ever after!
