Title: Tim and Spot and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim and his dog, named Spot. They were best friends. They loved to play outside in the sun. One day, they found a big, rough rock. Tim said, "Let's play with this rock!" Tim and Spot played with the rock all day. They rolled it, they kicked it, and they made big towers with it. Then, it was time for dinner. Tim said to Spot, "We must go inside now. I promise we will play with the rock again tomorrow." The next day, Tim and Spot went outside to play with the rock. But the rock was gone! It had vanished! Tim and Spot looked everywhere for the rock. They looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and in the tall grass. But they could not find the rock. Tim said to Spot, "I am sad. I made a promise to play with the rock today, but it is gone." Spot barked and wagged his tail. He wanted to help Tim feel better. So, Tim and Spot played a new game called "Find the Rock". They looked and looked, and they had lots of fun. In the end, they found a new rock, even bigger and rougher than the first one. Tim and Spot were very happy, and they played together all day long.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked to the big open field near their house. Tim loved to run and play in the open field. As they walked, Tim gazed at the pretty blue sky with white clouds. In the field, Tim saw a big red engine. It was a shiny fire truck. Tim liked fire trucks a lot. He ran to the engine and looked at it. A nice fireman saw Tim and came over to say hello. "Hi there," said the fireman. "Do you like our fire engine?" "Yes, I love it!" Tim said with a big smile. The fireman let Tim sit in the big red engine. Tim was so happy. He gazed at all the buttons and levers. After a while, it was time to go home. Tim said goodbye to the fireman and the big red engine. He walked back with his mom, feeling very happy. They went home to tell dad all about their fun day in the open field.

Once upon a time there was a lion called Larry. Larry was a scary lion, but he was gentle with everyone. He lived in the jungle and he loved to eat. One day, Larry went very hungry and could not find food anywhere. As he was walking, he smelled some nice food. He felt his stomach rumble and his mouth began to water. He followed the smell until he came to a clearing. There he saw a little boy. The boy was crying and seemed to be in pain. "Please help me," the boy said. "I was trying to feed the birds with some bread, but one of them bit me." Larry was surprised and he wanted to help. He slowly approached the boy and said, "Do not be scared. I'll stay here and watch you while you feed them. That way, you won't get hurt again". The boy wiped his eyes and smiled at Larry. He thanked him for being so kind and said, "I thought you were scary, but you are really a nice lion". Larry watched over the boy and helped him feed the birds. Afterwards, Larry and the boy parted ways. The next time Larry visited the clearing, he could see that the birds were still eating the food he had helped the boy feed them. He was proud that he had been able to help the boy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. Her mother always asked Sarah to help number the dishes before putting them away. This was something Sarah enjoyed doing—she liked to count out the plates, the silverware, and the cups. But one day, when Sarah was counting the dishes, she noticed something strange. One of the cups was missing! Sarah asked her mother what happened to the cup but her mother didn't seem to know. She simply replied, "It's an unknown, I guess." Sarah thought about this unknown for a long time. She even dreamed about the missing cup. Suddenly she had an idea. She asked her mother if she could help look for the missing cup. They looked all over the house, but it was nowhere in sight. Finally, Sarah remembered what her mother said—the cup was an unknown. Could this be why they couldn't find the cup? Sarah thought maybe if they could give the cup a number, then they would be able to find it. Sarah and her mother decided to go to the store and buy a new cup. The new cup was marked with the number zero. When they got home, Sarah put the new cup with the rest of the dishes. That night, while they were counting the dishes, they noticed something strange. Sarah pointed to the cup with the number zero and said, “Mom, I think we found our unknown!"
