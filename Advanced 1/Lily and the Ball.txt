Title: Lily and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved the summer. She liked to play outside in the sun with her pink ball. One day, Lily saw a new girl in the park. The new girl had a pretty pink dress. Lily walked up to the new girl and said, "Hi! I'm Lily. Do you want to play with my pink ball?" The new girl smiled and said, "Yes, I would like to play. My name is Mia." Lily and Mia played all day in the summer sun. They laughed and had fun. At the end of the day, Mia said, "Thank you for letting me play with your pink ball, Lily." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome! I'm happy that you accepted to play with me." From that day on, Lily and Mia were best friends. They played together every summer day, and their favorite toy was always the pink ball.

Lily likes to create things with paper and crayons. She makes pictures of her family, her dog, and her toys. She also makes notes for her friends. She writes their names and draws hearts and stars. One day, she wants to make a note for her mom. She looks for a paper, but she cannot find any. All the papers are gone. She feels sad. She asks her dad, "Where are the papers?" Her dad says, "You used all the papers, Lily. You are very creative, but you need to save some papers for later." Lily says, "But I want to make a note for mom. She is at work and I miss her." Her dad smiles and hugs her. He says, "I have an idea. Come with me." He takes her to the kitchen and shows her an empty cereal box. He says, "We can use this to make a note. It is like a big paper. We can cut it and color it." Lily likes the idea. She says, "Yes, let's do that!" They cut the cereal box and make a big note. Lily writes "Mom" and draws a flower and a smiley face. She also writes "I love you" with her dad's help. She colors the note with her crayons. They put the note on the fridge with a magnet. Lily says, "Mom will see it when she comes home. She will be happy." Her dad says, "Yes, she will. You are very sweet, Lily. And very creative."

Once, there was a dark velvet rose. The dark velvet rose loved to do adventurous things. One day, it was determined to rise, up and away. The rose described its plan to its friend, a sunflower. The sunflower asked, "But why would you want to do something dangerous like that?" The dark velvet rose replied, "Because I am brave and I will never give up. I must rise up and away to get the adventure that I seek." The sunflower warned the rose of the dangers that awaited it. But the dark velvet rose was not fazed; it knew that the risks were worth it. The rose made its way up, carefully and slowly. Halfway, it encountered an obstacle: a giant nasty monster appeared in it's path. It had long, prickly spines and big sharp teeth. But the brave dark velvet rose was determined not to give up. It faced off with the monster, and defeated it with its bravery. The rose continued on and eventually it reached the top. It had accomplished its goal and saw the world in a different light. The dark velvet rose had achieved its adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a big stretchy dog named Spot. Spot loved to stretch his long legs every morning. He would stretch and stretch until he was very tall. Spot had a best friend named Tim. Tim was a small boy who loved to read books. They were always together. One sunny day, Tim and Spot went to the park. They saw other kids and dogs playing. Tim had an idea. He wanted to make a fun game with his book. Tim and Spot were both very competitive. They loved to win. Tim put the book on the ground and said, "Let's see who can stretch the farthest and touch the book first!" Spot wagged his tail and got ready. Tim and Spot stretched and stretched. Spot's long legs helped him win the game. They both laughed and had a great time. And from that day on, Tim and Spot played the stretch and touch the book game, always having fun together.
