Title: Lily the Girl and the Duck

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a curious girl who liked to explore. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, she saw a big pond. Lily asked her mom, "Can I go near the pond?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to fall in." Lily went near the pond and saw a small, shiny gem in the water. She wanted to get it, but she didn't want to get wet. She saw a duck near the water and asked, "Duck, can you help me get the gem?" The duck said, "Sure, I can help you." The duck went into the water and used its beak to pick up the gem. Then, the duck gave the gem to Lily. Lily was so happy to have the gem. She thanked the duck and went back to her mom. When they got home, Lily's mom helped her bathe and clean the gem. Lily loved her new gem and kept it as a special treasure.

Sally was feeling very guilty. She knew she had done something wrong, but she couldn't quite figure out what it was. She looked around the kitchen and saw the sauce spilled on the floor. She realized what she had done - she had spilled the sauce! Sally quickly grabbed a cloth and tried to sort out the mess. She was so relieved when the sauce was all cleaned up! She was just putting away the cloth when her mom came into the kitchen. She admitted to her mom that she had spilled the sauce and apologized. Her mom said it was all right, and gave Sally a big hug. She told Sally to be more careful next time, and the two of them laughed. Then, they went out to get ice cream - the best way to sort out a guilty conscience!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a monkey on the tree. The monkey was very naughty. It liked to hang on the tree and make funny faces at Tim. Tim's mom said, "Be nice to the monkey, and maybe it will be nice to you." Tim promised his mom that he would be nice to the monkey. The next day, Tim went back to the park. He saw the naughty monkey hanging on the tree again. Tim remembered his promise to his mom, so he gave the monkey a big smile. The monkey saw Tim's smile and stopped making funny faces. It seemed like the monkey was starting to be nice. But then, the monkey took Tim's hat and hung it high up in the tree! Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a big bird flew by and saw Tim's hat. The bird took the hat from the tree and gave it back to Tim. The naughty monkey was surprised and finally learned to be nice. From that day on, Tim and the monkey became good friends, and they all played happily at the park.

Once upon a time there was a smart kid. He was walking outside in the park one day when he heard a noise. He was surprised! It sounded like a strange creature. He decided to investigate. When he got closer, the kid saw a big, furry monster! The kid said, "What are you doing here?" But the monster didn't answer. Instead, it chased the kid! The kid ran faster and faster until he couldn't run anymore. He was so scared! But the monster was too smart. It suddenly jumped out from behind a bush and caught the kid! The poor kid screamed and screamed, but he couldn't get away. The monster said, "I'm sorry, but you have to stay here forever!" The monster disappeared, leaving the poor kid all alone. The kid never got out of the park. He was stuck there forever. It was a bad ending for the poor, surprised kid.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to help his mom. His mom was doing work in the kitchen. She was using the dishwasher to clean the plates. Tim saw the regular dishwasher and had an idea. Tim urged his mom to let him help. He said, "Mom, I want to help you!" His mom smiled and said, "Okay, Tim. You can help me with the dishwasher." Tim was very happy. He helped his mom put the plates in the dishwasher. Then, they turned it on. The dishwasher cleaned the plates. Tim felt proud that he helped his mom with the regular dishwasher.
