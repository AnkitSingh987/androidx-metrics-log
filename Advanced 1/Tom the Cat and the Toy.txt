Title: Tom the Cat and the Toy

One day, a cat named Tom was in a messy room. He wanted to find a good place to stay and sleep. He looked around and saw a big, soft bed. Tom was very happy and wanted to jump on the bed. As Tom tried to jump on the bed, he did not see a toy on the floor. He put his foot on the toy and slip. Tom fell down and made a loud noise. He was sad and did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The toy that made Tom slip started to talk! The toy said, "I'm sorry I made you slip, Tom. I will help you find a better place to stay." The toy and Tom became friends and found a nice, clean spot for Tom to sleep.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a kind doctor. The doctor liked to heal people when they were sick. One day, a little girl came to the doctor. She was not feeling well. The doctor gave the little girl some mild medicine. He told her to drink lots of water. The little girl did what the doctor said. She drank water and took the mild medicine. Soon, the little girl started to feel better. She was happy that the doctor helped her heal. The doctor was happy too. They both knew that water and mild medicine can make you feel better when you are sick. And they lived happily ever after.

One day there was a brave mouse named Max. He wanted to find some olives so he could eat them. Max rolled across the floor and looked everywhere. He was so brave he even checked under the big sofa. "Oh look!" he exclaimed. "I found some olives!" He was so excited. Max gathered up the olives and rolled back across the room towards the door. Then he heard a noise. Max stopped and asked, "Who's there?" But no one answered. Max was a bit scared but he was also brave. He decided to keep rolling so he could get home. Max ran across the room and out the door. He was so happy to be safe. He rolled back to his home where he could eat his olives in peace. He was very brave.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Mary and Jay. Mary mentioned that she wanted a green lime with a hairy coating to make a yummy snack. Jay said, "I know the perfect place to get a hairy lime!" The two friends went over to the store and found the biggest and greenest lime they had ever seen. It was also very hairy! They both took a bite, and it was the most delicious lime either of them had ever tasted. They hugged each other and finished the lime in no time. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Ella. Ella had a long trunk that was very smooth. She lived in a forest with her animal friends. One day, Ella saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Ella wanted to help. She used her smooth trunk to lift the bird gently. Then, she took the bird to a safe place to rest. All the animals saw what Ella did. They started to clap their paws and wings. They all applauded Ella for helping the little bird. The bird got better and could fly again. The animals learned that it is good to help others when they are in need.

One day, a big and strong animal, named Bobo, went for a walk. He liked to watch and observe things. As he walked, he saw a small human girl. The girl was sad and lost. Bobo wanted to help her. He was a dependable friend. Bobo said, "Hi, I am Bobo. Why are you sad?" The girl looked at him and said, "I am lost. I can't find my home." Bobo wanted to help the girl find her home. He knew he could do it. They walked and walked. Bobo observed the way back to the girl's home. He was a good helper. The girl was happy when they found her home. She said, "Thank you, Bobo. You are a dependable friend." They hugged and said goodbye.
