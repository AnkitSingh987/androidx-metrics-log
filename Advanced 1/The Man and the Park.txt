Title: The Man and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a man who wanted to sell a computer. He did not know much about computers. He was ignorant. He thought it would be easy to sell the computer. One day, he took the computer to the park. He called out, "Who wants to buy my computer?" Many people came to see it. They asked him questions about the computer. But he did not know the answers. The people were not happy. No one bought the computer. The man was sad. He went home with the computer. He never sold it. That was the bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tom. He loved to play with his aeroplane toy. Every day, he would fly his aeroplane high in the sky and make it do fun tricks. One day, Tom's aeroplane broke. He was very sad. His mommy saw him crying and wanted to help. She said, "Tom, let's restore your aeroplane and make it like new again." Tom and his mommy worked together to fix the aeroplane. They put the pieces back and soon, the aeroplane was all better. Tom was so happy to have his aeroplane back. He knew he was gifted because he could fix things with his mommy's help. And they played with the aeroplane all day long.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom saw a big grape. The grape was on a table. Tom wanted to eat the grape. He jumped on the table and tried to get the grape, but it rolled away. Tom chased the grape all around the house. Tom's friend, a bird named Sam, saw Tom running after the grape. Sam asked, "Tom, what are you doing?" Tom said, "I want to eat the grape, but it keeps running away from me." Sam wanted to help Tom, so he flew down and tried to catch the grape with his beak. As Sam tried to catch the grape, it suddenly folded open like a flower. Inside the grape, there was a tiny fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for helping me. I was stuck inside the grape. As a thank you, I will give you both a wish." Tom and Sam were very surprised, but they were happy to meet the fairy and get a wish. Tom wished for a big, soft bed to sleep on. Sam wished for a beautiful tree to live in. The fairy granted their wishes, and Tom and Sam were very happy. They thanked the fairy and went to enjoy their new gifts. From that day on, Tom and Sam were always ready to help others, even if it was a little grape.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Max. He was very clever and could name all the animals. One day, he heard someone calling for him. He looked up in the sky to see who it was. It was a big bird with soft feathers. Max thought he had never seen such a soft texture before. The bird asked Max to come up and fly with him. Max was so excited and wanted to go. The bird said he could fly if he was clever enough. Max thought he could do it. He started to sway back and forth and twirl around. Soon he was flying in the air with the bird. They flew so high and had so much fun! They laughed and played until the sun went down. The bird said goodbye to Max and called out in the sky. Max waved goodbye and thanked the bird for the adventure. Max had so much fun and learned he was clever enough to fly with the bird!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a market. In this market, there were many animals. One day, a little dog named Spot went to the market. He was very confused because there were so many things to see and smell. Spot saw a big cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was not happy. She said, "I suffer because I can't find my toy." Spot wanted to help. He said, "Let's look for your toy together." They looked and looked, but they could not find the toy. Then, a nice bird named Chirpy flew by. Chirpy said, "I saw your toy near the big tree." Spot and Fluffy went to the big tree and found the toy. Fluffy was so happy! They all became good friends and went to the market together every day.
