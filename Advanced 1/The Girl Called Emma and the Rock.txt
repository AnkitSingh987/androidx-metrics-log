Title: The Girl Called Emma and the Rock

Once there was a little girl called Emma. She loved playing games by the sea. One day, Emma sat on a rock, watching the waves come in. She picked up something from the sand and it was a pretty shell. She was so excited that she gave it to her mum. She pointed and said, “Mummy, look!” Her mum smiled. “Oh, this shell is so pretty. What a creative find!” Emma and her mum went to the beach many more times after that, and each time, Emma pointed and found a different kind of shell. Eventually she collected many different types of shells which she sorted and put into a big jar. Everyday Emma brought her creative ideas for things to make out of shells. Sometimes she used them as decoration for a toy house, or even to make pretty jewels. Emma finally had a box full of treasures from the beach and was really proud of her creativity and the shells she had collected.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy mule named Max. Max liked to know what was happening around him. One day, he saw a big red ball near the fence. He wanted to play with it. Max went to his friend, the cow, and asked, "What is happening with the big red ball?" The cow said, "I don't know, let's go see." They walked to the fence and saw a little girl playing with the ball. The little girl saw Max and the cow watching her. She smiled and said, "Hi, I'm Lily. Do you want to play too?" Max and the cow nodded their heads. They all played with the big red ball together and had lots of fun. After a while, it was time for Lily to go home. She said goodbye to Max and the cow. They were sad to see her go, but they knew they would see her again soon. From that day on, Max and the cow always played with the big red ball, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very proud because he had a prize toy car. He loved to play with his car every day. It was shiny and fast, and all his friends liked it too. One day, Tim was playing with his car when something unexpected happened. A big dog came and took his car in its mouth. Tim was scared and sad. He thought the dog would destroy his prize toy car. But then, the dog did something very surprising. Instead of destroying the car, the dog gently put it down on the ground and wagged its tail. Tim was so happy! He patted the dog and said "thank you!" The dog and Tim became best friends, and they played with the car together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big red button in a small room. No one knew what the button did. One day, a girl named Lily found the room with the button. She wanted to push the button, but she was scared. She thought, "What if something bad happens?" Lily went to find her friend Tom. She told him about the big red button. Tom was brave and fast. He ran to the room with Lily. When they got there, Tom said, "I will push the button!" Lily was scared, but she trusted Tom. Tom pushed the button and they both closed their eyes. They heard a loud noise and opened their eyes. To their surprise, a big pile of candy and toys appeared! They were so happy and played all day. The big red button was a surprise for everyone who found it. And from that day on, Lily and Tom were never scared of the big red button again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her chalk. She drew on the ground with many colors: red, blue, and yellow. Sue's friends liked her chalk drawings, too. One day, Sue found a dark piece of paper. She wanted to draw on it with her chalk. But first, she needed to fold the paper. Sue asked her mom to help her fold it. Her mom showed her how to fold the paper in half. Sue was happy. Sue took her folded dark paper and her chalk outside. She drew a big sun, a tree, and a house on the paper. Her friends came to see her drawing. They all liked it very much. Sue was proud of her dark paper chalk drawing.
