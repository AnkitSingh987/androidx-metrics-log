Title: Amy the Girl and the Machine

One day, a girl named Amy found a big machine in her room. She did not know what it was. It looked scary. Amy asked her mom, "What is this scary machine?" Her mom said, "It is a reading machine. It can read books to you." Amy was not sure if she wanted to use the scary machine. But her mom said, "Don't worry, it will be fun. Let's try it together." The next day, Amy and her mom picked a book. They put the book in the machine. The machine started to read the book out loud. It was not scary anymore. Amy liked the machine now. It read the book in a nice voice. From that day on, Amy and her mom used the reading machine every day. Amy was happy. She loved to hear the machine read her favorite books. And the scary machine became her best friend.

One cold winter day, Jack was playing in the snow. He started to build a big snowman. His dad came to join the fun and soon the two of them had built the biggest and the best snowy friend. "We did it dad! It looks great," said Jack with a big smile. "It certainly does! That's a job well done," said his dad. Suddenly, Jack felt a bit nervous. It had started to snow harder and he was worried that the snowman might melt away. His dad saw Jack's worried face. "Don't be nervous, Jack. Let's go inside and get you a hot chocolate," he said reassuringly. The two of them went inside and Jack soon forgot about his worries. The snowman stood tall and strong, and the snow kept it safe and sound.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a chubby little bunny. The bunny liked to hop and play all day. One day, the bunny saw a pretty flower. The flower was very pretty, but the bunny did not know that the flower had poison in it. The bunny thought, "I want to take this pretty flower home." So, the bunny took the flower and went home. The bunny's mommy saw the flower and said, "No, bunny! That flower has poison. You must not touch it. It is not good for you." The bunny was sad but knew mommy was right. The flower with poison had to remain outside. The next day, the bunny went out to play again. The bunny saw another pretty flower. The bunny thought, "This flower must be good. It is not like the other flower." But the bunny was wrong. This flower also had poison in it. The bunny took the flower and went home. The bunny's mommy was not home to help. The bunny touched the flower and got very sick. The bunny could not hop and play anymore. The bunny did not know that the pretty flower was not good. And that is the sad end of the chubby little bunny's story.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to explore. One day, they found a big cave in the woods. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's go in, Lily!" Ben said. "Maybe we can find treasure or animals." "I don't know, Ben," Lily said. "It looks dark and scary. What if we get lost or hurt?" "Don't be afraid, Lily," Ben said. "We have a flashlight and a map. We can pray to God to keep us safe. Come on, it will be fun!" Lily was not sure, but she loved her brother. She followed him into the cave. They saw many rocks and shapes on the walls. They heard water and wind. They felt cold and wet. They walked deeper and deeper into the cave. They did not notice that their flashlight was getting dimmer and dimmer. They did not notice that their map was getting wetter and wetter. Suddenly, their flashlight went out. They were in the dark. They could not see anything. They could not find their way back. They were scared. They hugged each other. They prayed to God to help them. They heard a noise. It was loud and growly. It was coming closer and closer. It was a bear! The bear smelled them. It wanted to eat them. It roared and showed its teeth. They screamed. They ran. They tripped and fell. They dropped their map. The bear was about to catch them. But then, it saw something shiny on the ground. It was their map. It was made of delicate paper. It was covered with glitter. The bear liked shiny things. It forgot about the twins. It picked up the map. It tore it and chewed it. It made a mess. The twins saw a light. It was the entrance of the cave. They ran towards it. They escaped. They were happy. They thanked God for saving them. They learned a lesson. They never went into the cave again.
