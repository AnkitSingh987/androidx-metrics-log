Title: The Lady and the Broccoli

Once there was an old lady. She was making dinner and wanted to add something special. She asked her three year old grandson, "What should I add to dinner?". The little boy smiled and excitedly said, "Broccoli! You should add broccoli!" The old lady thought that was a great idea and asked, "Is there any available?" The little boy ran to the fridge and said, "Yes, there is. Come and see!" Together they checked the fridge and found a big bunch of broccoli. The old lady smiled, "This is perfect! We can add the broccoli to dinner." The little boy was so happy and ran off to tell the news to his mom and dad. The old lady then got to work. She washed the broccoli and added it to the dinner. Everyone was very pleased when they were served the dinner with the special addition of broccoli. It was a delicious meal.

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In this town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom was a serious boy who liked to help everyone. He was always busy doing something good. One day, Tom saw a big hole in the ground. It was very dry and animals could fall in and get hurt. Tom thought, "I must help the animals." So, he ran to his house and got a big bucket of water. He poured the water into the hole to make it not dry. As Tom poured the water, the hole became smaller and smaller. Soon, the hole was gone! The animals were now safe and happy. They thanked Tom for his help. The people in the town were proud of him too. The moral of the story is: be kind and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a lucky girl because she had a big, fun family. Lucy loved to play with her toy boat in the bath. She would rock the boat back and forth, making big waves. One day, while Lucy was playing in the bath, her mom gave her a new shampoo. "This shampoo will make your hair very clean and shiny," her mom said. Lucy was excited to try the new shampoo. She put some on her hair and scrubbed it in. Then, she rinsed it off with water. After her bath, Lucy looked in the mirror and saw her shiny, clean hair. She felt very lucky to have such a nice mom who gave her the new shampoo. She ran to her mom and gave her a big hug. "Thank you, Mommy," Lucy said. "I love my new shampoo!"

There was a girl called Lucy. She wanted to go to the mall. Every day she worked hard to save up enough money. Finally, one day Lucy saved up enough to go to the mall. When she arrived, Lucy felt very excited. At the mall, Lucy saw lots of delicate items. She wanted to buy something special. She saw a beautiful necklace in one of the shops and asked her mom "Can I buy it?". Her mom responded, "Yes, you worked really hard for the money. You deserve a reward". So, Lucy bought the delicate necklace. She felt so happy and proud. She thanked her mum for helping her save up the money she needed. Now, every time she wears the necklace, it serves as a reminder of all her hard work.

Lily and Tom were playing in the sandbox with their toys. They had a crane, a truck, a shovel and a bucket. They liked to make hills and holes and move the sand around. "Look, I can bury the truck with the crane!" Tom said. He used the crane to scoop up some sand and drop it on the truck. The truck was almost covered. "No, don't do that!" Lily said. She liked the truck. She used the shovel to dig out the truck. "The truck is not for burying. It is for driving." "But I want to bury it. It is fun!" Tom said. He used the crane again to put more sand on the truck. The truck was hidden. "That is bad, Tom. You are not nice. The truck is mine. Give it back!" Lily said. She was angry. She tried to pull the crane away from Tom. "Hey, stop it! The crane is mine. Let go!" Tom said. He was angry too. He held on to the crane. They both pulled and pulled. The crane broke in half. They both cried. Mom came and saw what happened. She was not happy. She said, "What did you do? You broke the crane. You were not careful. You were not kind. You need to say sorry and share your toys." Lily and Tom felt sorry. They said sorry to each other and to Mom. They hugged and made up. They decided to play together and share their toys. They fixed the crane with some tape. They drove the truck and lifted the sand. They had fun.
