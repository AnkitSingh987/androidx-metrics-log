Title: The Flower and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a pretty flower. The flower wanted to bloom. It lived in a garden near a big brick wall. The flower was very tired. It needed sun and water to grow and bloom. One day, a kind boy came to the garden. He saw the tired flower. The boy wanted to help it bloom. He gave the flower water and took it out to the sun. The flower was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The tired flower started to grow and bloom. But it didn't bloom like a normal flower. It bloomed into a big, beautiful butterfly! The butterfly flew away, over the brick wall, and into the sky. The boy was surprised but happy to see the flower turn into a butterfly. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a rich family who liked to go places. They decided to go on a trip to the hotel. When they got to the hotel, it was so big and so pretty. They were so excited to come inside. When they stepped inside, there were so many things to do. They could swim in the pool, play in the playground and eat lots of yummy food. But the most exciting thing of all was that they could come back and stay at the hotel again one day. They were so rich and they could travel everywhere. And that's why the rich family kept coming back to the hotel over and over again. They loved it so much!

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive tree. Many kids loved to play under the tree. One day, a little boy named Tim and his friend, Sue, were playing near the tree. Tim saw an old cane on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Look, Sue! I found a cane!" Sue looked at the cane and said, "I don't like it. I hate it. It's old and dirty." Tim felt sad but then he had an idea. He said, "Let's clean it and make it pretty!" Sue agreed, and they both worked together to clean the cane. They used water and soap to make it shine. After they cleaned the cane, it looked new and pretty. Sue said, "Wow, Tim! The cane is impressive now! I don't hate it anymore." They both smiled and continued to play under the big, impressive tree with their new, pretty cane. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She wore a pretty blouse with flowers on it. Lucy saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. She was a brave girl. As Lucy climbed the tree, she saw a small bird stuck in a hole. The bird said, "Help me, please! I am stuck!" Lucy wanted to help the bird. She reached out her hand to the bird. Lucy had to squeeze her hand in the hole to get the bird out. It was hard, but she did it. The bird was so happy. It said, "Thank you, Lucy! You are very brave!" Lucy felt good that she helped the bird. They became good friends and played in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On the hill, there were many stones. One day, a boy named Tim went to the hill. He wanted to play with the stones. Tim saw a big stone and wanted to take it home. Tim said, "I will load the stone on my toy truck." He tried to pick up the stone, but it was very heavy. Tim was careless and dropped the stone on his foot. "Ouch!" he cried. A girl named Sue saw Tim and came to help. "Be careful, Tim," Sue said. "Let's load the stone together." They picked up the stone and put it on the toy truck. Tim and Sue were happy and played with the stone all day.

Once upon a time, a big helicopter lived in the sky. It liked to wear a hat. One day, the hat fell off and landed on the ground. The helicopter was sad and wanted to find the hat. The helicopter asked a bird, "Did you see my hat?" The bird said, "No, but I will help you look." They were patient and looked everywhere. They looked in the trees and on the ground, but they could not find the hat. Finally, they found a squirrel wearing the hat! The squirrel said, "I found this hat and I like it." The helicopter said, "That is my hat. Can I have it back, please?" The squirrel thought for a moment, then said, "Okay, we can share the hat." The helicopter and squirrel became friends and took turns wearing the hat. They were all happy.
