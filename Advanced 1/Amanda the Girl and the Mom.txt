Title: Amanda the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amanda. Amanda was three-years-old, and she loved to explore. One day, Amanda went to the store with her mom. When they arrived, Amanda saw a huge dinner. "Wow!" she exclaimed, her eyes wide with excitement. Her mom smiled. "Do you want to try it, Amanda? It looks really yummy." Amanda nodded eagerly and thanked her mom. She picked up the huge dinner and began to eat. She smiled when she tasted it—it was delicious! After they finished their shopping, they went home. Amanda couldn't wait to tell her dad what she had discovered at the store!

Once upon a time, in a gloomy town, there was a man who loved to work with bricks. Every day, he would build big walls with his bricks. One day, a little girl walked by and saw the man working. "Hi!" the little girl said. "What are you doing with those bricks?" The man looked up and smiled. "I am building a big wall to make our town look nice," he replied. The little girl thought for a moment. "Can I help you?" she asked. The man nodded and handed her a brick. Together, they worked all day, and by the end, the wall was complete. The gloomy town now looked bright and happy, all thanks to the man and the little girl who worked with bricks.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She met a nosy bird who liked to talk a lot. The bird was very friendly and wanted to play with Amy. They played hide and seek and had a lot of fun. Amy had a special friend, her finger named Pinky. Pinky was always with her. Pinky and the bird became friends too. They talked and laughed all day. The nosy bird liked to know what Pinky and Amy were doing. At the end of the day, Amy, Pinky, and the nosy bird said goodbye. They were all happy to have a new friend. The bird flew away, but promised to come back and talk with Amy and Pinky again.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to decorate their room with stickers, drawings and toys. They had a lot of fun making their room look colorful and cozy. But they also made a lot of mess and noise, and did not listen to their mom when she asked them to clean up and be quiet. One day, their mom had to go to the store and asked their grandma to stay with them. She told them to be good and not to bother their grandma. Lily and Ben said yes, but as soon as their mom left, they started to decorate their room again. They threw their clothes on the floor, ripped their books, and shouted and laughed loudly. Their grandma heard the noise and came to their room. She was very angry and sad. She said, "Lily and Ben, what are you doing? You are making a big mess and a lot of noise. You are not being good. You are being rude and naughty. You are hurting your mom's feelings and mine. Don't you love us?" Lily and Ben felt ashamed and sorry. They realized they had been wrong and selfish. They said, "Grandma, we are sorry. We love you and mom. We were just having fun, but we did not think about you and mom. We will clean up and be quiet. Please forgive us." Their grandma hugged them and said, "I forgive you, Lily and Ben. I love you too. But you have to learn to respect your mom and me, and your room and your things. You have to be responsible and polite. You can have fun, but not at the cost of others. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nodded and said, "We understand, grandma. We will do better. Thank you for teaching us." They helped their grandma to clean up their room and make it nice again. They also made a card for their mom to say sorry and that they love her. When their mom came back, she was surprised and happy to see their room and their card. She hugged them and said, "I'm proud of you, Lily and Ben. You have learned a valuable lesson today. I love you very much." The end.
