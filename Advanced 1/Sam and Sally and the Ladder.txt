Title: Sam and Sally and the Ladder

Once upon a time there were two friends, Sam and Sally. Sam wanted to show Sally a joke. But every time he tried to show it Sally would giggle and tell everyone the joke. One day Sam decided to find an incredible joke that Sally wouldn't be able to share. He searched everywhere but couldn't find one. Just when he was about to give up, Sam noticed a long ladder. He climbed up the ladder and then he saw the most incredible joke. He couldn't believe his eyes! Sam knew that the only way to prevent Sally from sharing the joke was to keep it a secret. So he took the joke with him and never showed it to anyone. And that's why Sam's joke remained a secret.

Anna likes to help her mom clean the house. She has a small broom and a dustpan. She sweeps the floor and puts the dirt in the trash. She feels happy when she sees the floor shiny and neat. One day, Anna sees a long cable on the floor. It is black and has a plug at the end. She thinks it is dirty and does not belong there. She picks up the cable and tries to put it in the trash. But the cable is heavy and tangled. She pulls and pulls, but it does not move. Suddenly, she hears a loud noise. It is the TV in the living room. It is making a lot of sounds and colors. Anna is scared. She drops the cable and runs to her mom. She hugs her mom and says, "Mommy, the TV is noisy!" Her mom looks at the TV and sees that it is not working properly. She sees the cable on the floor and realizes what happened. She smiles and says, "Anna, you were trying to help me clean, but you unplugged the TV. That's why it is noisy. The cable is not dirty, it is important. It makes the TV work. Come, I will show you how to plug it back in." Anna follows her mom and learns how to plug the cable back in. The TV stops being noisy and shows her favorite show. Anna is happy. She says, "Mommy, I'm sorry. I did not know. I just wanted to help you clean." Her mom hugs her and says, "It's okay, Anna. I love you. You are a good helper. But next time, ask me before you touch the cable, okay?" Anna nods and says, "Okay, Mommy. I love you too." They watch the TV together and enjoy the show.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She found a small pebble on the ground. Lily thought the pebble was very pretty. She picked it up and put it in her pocket. She felt happy and wanted to show her mom the pretty pebble. As Lily walked home, something silly happened. She saw a cat and a dog playing together. They were jumping and running around. Lily laughed and thought it was funny. She took out her pebble to show the cat and the dog. But then, the pebble started to glow! It became bigger and bigger. Suddenly, the pebble turned into a big, shiny ball. Lily, the cat, and the dog were all very surprised. They played with the shiny ball together and had lots of fun. In the end, they all became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a mama bear who loved her cub very much. Mama bear wanted her cub to learn new things. So every day, mama bear would take her cub to a wide field. There, mama bear would teach her cub how to replace old and worn out things. She would show her cub how to take out an old tool and how to put in brand new ones. Mama bear's cub was very quick to learn. Every day the cub got better at replacing things. One day, when they were walking back home, mama bear said to her cub, "You have learned so much! What did you replace?" The cub replied, "I replaced the old tools with new ones!" Mama bear smiled and said, "That's right, cub. I'm so proud of you!"
