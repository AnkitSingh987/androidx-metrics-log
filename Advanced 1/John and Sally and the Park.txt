Title: John and Sally and the Park

John and Sally were playing in the park. It was a hot day and John saw a flame that seemed tidy. He said to Sally, "You can touch it. It won't hurt you." Sally smiled and moved closer to the flame. Suddenly, the flame grew bigger and brighter. The kids seemed scared and they started running away. But it was too late. The flame burned the grass and spread to the trees. John and Sally were very sad. It seemed like a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Henry. Henry had a broken toy. It was his favorite toy, and he was very sad. He wanted to fix it so he could play with it again. One day, Henry went to talk to the judge. The judge was a big and wise person who helped people when they had problems. Henry explained his problem to the judge and asked for help. The judge knew what to do! He said, "We must take the toy apart, fix the broken parts, and put it back together." Henry was so happy he could hardly contain his excitement. He started to take the toy apart and put it back together. When it was done, the judge took a look at the toy. He had a big smile on his face. "It looks great! You did a wonderful job, Henry!" Henry smiled and hugged the judge. And they both lived happily ever after. The end.

One day, a big red truck was driving on an icy road. The truck was going to the store to bring some toys. The road was very slippery because it was cold outside. The truck saw a small blue car on the side of the road. The car looked sad. The truck stopped and asked, "Why are you sad, little car?" The blue car said, "I can't drive on this icy road. I'm scared I will slip and fall." The big red truck wanted to help. The truck said, "I can drive on the icy road. I'm big and strong. I will go slow and you can follow me." The blue car was not sure, but the truck insisted, "I will help you. Just stay close to me." So, the big red truck and the small blue car drove slowly on the icy road together. They made it to the store safely. The blue car was very happy and thanked the big red truck. The truck felt good because it helped a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a street where all the animals lived. In this street, the animals loved to play games. They were very competitive with each other. One day, a little mouse named Mimi wanted to play with the other animals. She went to her friend, a big dog named Dodo. Mimi said, "Dodo, can I request to play with you and the others?" Dodo was happy to hear this and said, "Yes, Mimi! We will play a fun game together." All the animals in the street played the game. They laughed and had a great time. Even though they were competitive, they were all good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a lucky red basket that she loved. One day, she went to the park with her basket. At the park, Lucy met a little boy named Tom. He saw the basket and said, "Wow! That's a lucky basket! Can I play with you?" Lucy smiled and said, "Yes! Let's play together." They played and played until the sun started to set. Lucy and Tom were tired, so they decided to settle down under a big tree. They put their toys in the lucky red basket and said goodbye. Lucy went home, happy to have made a new friend.

One day, a big red bus arrives from the east. It is very unusual because it has big eyes and a big mouth. The bus is very happy and wants to make friends with everyone in the town. The bus goes to the park and sees kids playing. The kids are scared at first, but then they see that the bus is nice. They play with the bus and have lots of fun. The bus is so happy to have new friends. But then, something unexpected happens. The bus starts to change. It becomes a big, red, friendly dog! Everyone is surprised, but they still love their new friend. The dog and the kids play all day, and they are all very happy.
