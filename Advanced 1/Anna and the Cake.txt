Title: Anna and the Cake

Anna liked to play with her dolls and pretend they were her staff. She had a big table where she would serve them tea and cake. She would say, "Hello, staff. How are you today? Do you like your tea and cake?" The dolls would nod and smile. Anna was a good boss. One day, Anna's mom came to her room and saw the mess. There were toys and clothes everywhere. She said, "Anna, your room is not tidy. You need to clean up before dinner. Put your toys in the box and your clothes in the closet." Anna did not want to clean up. She said, "But mom, I am busy. I have to serve my staff. They are hungry and thirsty." Mom said, "You can serve them later. First, you need to tidy your room. It is part of your job as a boss. A good boss keeps things tidy and organized." Anna thought about it. She wanted to be a good boss. She said, "Okay, mom. I will tidy my room. But can I have some tea and cake too?" Mom said, "Yes, you can. But only after you finish cleaning up." Anna said, "Thank you, mom. You are a good mom." Mom said, "You are welcome, Anna. You are a good daughter." Anna smiled and started to tidy her room. She hoped her staff would not mind waiting for a little while.

Once upon a time, there was a dark night. The night was so dark that you couldn't see anything. Suddenly, a big storm came. There came loud thunder and lightning, and it started to rain. The rain made a big puddle on the ground. The puddle started to get bigger and bigger until it was so big it split into two. One part was full of water and the other part was empty. The two parts of the puddle started to move further away from each other. The part with the water made a river, and the empty part made a dry path. The storm was over and the night was calm and dark again. The River and the Path stayed there forever, so everyone could see them in the dark night.

Once upon a time, there was a man. He had a little boy who was very spoiled. The boy always wanted more toys and candy. The man tried to make the boy happy, but it was hard. One day, they went to the park. The boy saw a big ice cream. He wanted it all for himself. The man said, "No, you have to share." The boy didn't like that. He started to scream. The man felt sad. He wanted the boy to be nice. He told the boy, "If you share, you will make friends." The boy thought about it. He stopped screaming and shared the ice cream. The boy made new friends and they all played together. The man was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Max. Max loved to spend his days running and playing outside. He had many friends, like the birds and the squirrels. One day, Max found a big red ball. He played with the ball all day long. But then, something strange happened. Max started to feel tired and needed more air. He didn't know that the ball was taking away his oxygen. The next day, Max saw the big red ball again. He was scared, but he wanted to play. So, Max found a way to get his oxygen back. He poked a hole in the ball with a stick. The ball went flat, and Max could breathe again. He learned that sometimes things can be fun, but also a little dangerous.

Little Tommy was very excited. He had just been given a brand new box of crayons. They were so colorful and made all of his drawings so much more fun! He decided to use his new crayons to make a special picture for his mom. He was so focused on all the fun he was having, that he never noticed the smoke that was starting to fill the room. When the flames suddenly blazed up, Little Tommy let out a shriek and started to panic. He quickly looked around the room and tried to find an escape route. But the fire had grown too powerful and the smoke now blanketed the entire room. He was trapped! Little Tommy tried to hold back his fear, desperately grasping his box of crayons. But sorrow soon replaced the happiness he had felt earlier, as he realized he had no way out. He was just so grateful that he had been able to hold onto his new crayons for a little bit. Finally, Little Tommy collapsed from the smoke, letting go of his crayons for the last time. Although he was unable to escape, at least he had his beloved crayons to keep him company until the end.
