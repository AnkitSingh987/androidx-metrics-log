Title: Annie the Girl and the Copper

Once, there was a girl named Annie. Annie was very persistent with her chores. Every morning, she would raise the chickens in the hen house, fill up the water containers and collect eggs. One day, Annie found a piece of something shiny in the dirt. She picked it up and it was very heavy. It was a piece of copper! Annie asked her parents what it was, and they said it was something very special. They told her to be persistent and keep looking for more pieces of copper. So, that is what Annie did. Whenever she had free time, she went outside to look for more pieces of copper. She was very persistent and eventually, Annie was able to raise a big pile of copper coins!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a dream that she was a doctor. In her dream, she helped people by doing an operation. She loved her dream and wanted to be a doctor when she grew up. One day, Amy's friend Tom came to her house. Tom was angry because his toy was broken. Amy said, "Don't be angry, Tom. I can fix your toy." Tom looked at her and asked, "How can you fix it?" Amy smiled and said, "I will do an operation on your toy, just like in my dream." Amy got her toy doctor tools and started the operation. She fixed Tom's toy, and he was so happy. They played together all day, and Amy knew that she would be a great doctor someday.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She had a big box of toys, but her favorite thing was a flexible pencil. She liked to bend it and draw funny shapes with it. As Mia played, she heard a knock on her door. It was her friend, Tom. "Hi Mia! Can I play with you?" he asked. Mia smiled and said, "Yes, come in!" Tom saw the flexible pencil and said, "Wow, that's a cool pencil!" They played together, drawing and bending the pencil. But then, Mia stumbled and dropped the pencil. It rolled under her bed. They both looked for it, and Mia found it. "Here it is!" she said, happy to have her special pencil back. Mia and Tom spent the rest of the day playing with the flexible pencil and drawing funny pictures. They had lots of fun and promised to play together again soon.

Lily was a little girl who liked to dream. Every night, she would close her eyes and imagine wonderful things. She would dream of flying with birds, swimming with fish, or playing with fairies. She felt happy and free in her dreams. One night, she had a different dream. She dreamed that she was in a big forest, and she saw a big bear. The bear was not friendly. It growled and showed its big teeth. Lily was scared and wanted to run away, but she could not move. She felt a pain in her mouth. She touched her tooth and it was loose. She wondered if the bear wanted her tooth. She heard a voice in her dream. It was her mom. She said, "Lily, wake up. It's okay. You're having a bad dream." Lily opened her eyes and saw her mom's face. She hugged her mom and felt safe. Her mom said, "What did you dream about, sweetie?" Lily told her mom about the bear and the tooth. Her mom said, "Don't worry, honey. The bear was not real. And your tooth is just ready to fall out. That's normal. You will get a new tooth soon. And maybe the tooth fairy will visit you and leave you a surprise." Lily smiled and felt better. She said, "Thank you, mom. I love you." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lily. Now go back to sleep. Have a nice dream." She kissed Lily and tucked her in. Lily felt comfortable and cozy in her bed. She closed her eyes and dreamed of something else. She dreamed of the tooth fairy and what she would bring her. She hoped it would be something shiny and pretty. She felt happy and curious in her dream.
