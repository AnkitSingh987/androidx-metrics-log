Title: Sammy and Rose and the Toys

There was once two friends, one named Sammy and one named Rose. They both wanted to explore the world, but didn't know how. So Sammy had an idea. He suggested that they should go for a walk around the bedroom. Rose thought this was a great idea and agreed. The bedroom was so big and colourful with different types of toys lying around. At first Sammy and Rose were a little scared but quickly they started to laugh and they felt super playful. They even took some time to try out some of the toys and play games. Eventually they got to the end of the bedroom and they both said goodbye before they headed back to the living room. Sammy was so exhausted after the walk but it was the most fun he had ever had!

Sam and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys in Sam's room. Sam has a big fan that makes wind. Mia likes the fan. She wants to touch it. "Can I play with the fan?" Mia asks Sam. "No, it is mine. You can play with something else." Sam says. He is not nice. Mia is sad. She waits for Sam to pass the fan to her. But Sam does not pass the fan. He keeps it for himself. He makes the fan go faster and faster. The fan makes a loud noise. It shakes and wobbles. Sam is scared. He tries to turn it off. But he can't. The fan falls off the table. It breaks into pieces. Sam and Mia are shocked. They hear a knock on the door. It is Sam's mom. She is angry. She sees the broken fan. She does not know what happened. "Who did this?" She asks. She looks at Sam and Mia. Sam and Mia do not say anything. They are afraid. They do not know what to do. They wish they had shared the fan.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious big box. It was silver and shiny. A little girl named Lucy found the box in her yard. She was very excited to see what was inside. Lucy opened the box and found a toy. It was a small toy car. She played with the toy car all day. But then, Lucy did something bad. She took her brother's toy without asking. Her mom saw this and was not happy. Mom said, "Lucy, you must learn to share. I will punish you by taking away your new toy car for one day." Lucy was sad, but she knew she did something wrong. The next day, she got her toy car back and promised to always share.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a cauliflower. The cauliflower was very happy. It had many friends like carrots, peas, and potatoes. They all played together every day. One day, a big wind came and hurt the cauliflower. It was very sad. The cauliflower's friends wanted to help it feel better. They thought and thought about what to do. Then, the carrot had an idea. They would send the cauliflower a big hug. They all hugged the cauliflower and it started to feel better. The cauliflower was happy again, and they all played together in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hungry dog named Max. Max liked to dig in the dirt. One day, Max found a shiny jewel in the ground. He thought it was a toy, so he picked it up with his mouth and ran away. Max met a small, hungry cat named Lily. Lily saw the jewel in Max's mouth and said, "Can I have the shiny toy, Max? I'm so hungry, and I think it will make me happy." Max did not want to share, but he felt bad for Lily. So, Max gave the jewel to Lily. Lily was very happy to have the jewel. But, she did not know that the jewel was magic. When Lily touched the jewel, it spoiled all the food around her. Lily and Max became even hungrier. They could not eat the spoiled food. They were sad and hungry, and they wished they had never found the shiny jewel.
