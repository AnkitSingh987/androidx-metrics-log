Title: Bobo the Unique Bird and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird named Bobo. Bobo was very colorful and liked to sing. One day, Bobo saw a cup on the ground. He was curious, so he went to look at it. As Bobo got closer, he heard a little voice say, "Please, do not disturb me." Bobo looked around and saw a tiny bug under the cup. The bug was scared and did not want to be bothered. Bobo wanted to help the bug, so he thought of a plan. He flew up high and found a big leaf. He brought the leaf to the bug and said, "Here, you can hide under this leaf instead of the cup." The bug was happy and thanked Bobo. They became friends and played together every day. And that is how Bobo learned that sometimes, the most unexpected things can lead to the best friendships.

One day, a girl named Lily was in her room. She wore a pretty blouse. She heard a noisy sound outside. Lily wanted to see what made the sound. She walked to the door and opened it. Outside, she saw a big truck. The truck was very noisy. The truck driver saw Lily and stopped. He asked her to move so he could drive on the road. Lily did not want to move. She wanted to keep watching the truck. The truck driver talked to Lily. He said, "Please yield and let me go by." Lily thought about it. She decided to yield and let the truck go by. She waved goodbye to the truck driver. Then she went back inside her house, happy with her day.

Lila and Tom were playing with their new pens. They liked to draw on paper and make different shapes and colors. Lila had a red pen and Tom had a blue pen. They were having fun. "Look, I made a flower!" Lila said, showing her paper to Tom. "That's nice, but I made a car!" Tom said, showing his paper to Lila. They wanted to see who could make the best drawing. They tried to draw more things with their pens. But then, something unusual happened. Tom's pen ran out of ink. He shook it and pressed it, but nothing came out. He was sad and angry. "Hey, give me your pen!" he said to Lila. "No, it's mine!" Lila said, holding her pen tight. "Give it to me, or I'll tell mom!" Tom said, trying to grab it. "No, you can't have it!" Lila said, pushing him away. They started to fight over the pen. They pulled and pushed and yelled. They did not notice that the pen was leaking ink. The ink spilled on the paper, on the table, on the floor, and on their clothes. It made a big mess. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was going on. She saw the ink everywhere and the children fighting. She was very angry and disappointed. "What are you doing?" she said, in a loud voice. "Look at this mess! You ruined your drawings, the table, the floor, and your clothes! How could you be so careless and selfish?" She took the pen from Lila and threw it in the trash. She made them clean up the mess and put them in time out. She did not mention the new pens again. Lila and Tom felt sorry and ashamed. They wished they had shared and played nicely. They learned their lesson. But it was too late. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big envelope. He wanted to know how heavy it was. So, he went to his mom to ask her to help him weigh it. His mom was busy, so she could not help Tim. Tim felt a little ignorant because he did not know how to weigh the envelope by himself. He asked his friend, the cat, to help him. But the cat did not know how to weigh things either. Finally, Tim's big sister saw him and the cat looking sad. She asked what was wrong. Tim told her about the envelope and how he wanted to know how heavy it was. She smiled and showed Tim how to weigh the envelope. Tim was happy, and he thanked his sister for helping him. From that day on, Tim was not ignorant about weighing things anymore.
