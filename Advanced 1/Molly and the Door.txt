Title: Molly and the Door

Molly was very excited because she was going to a bounce house party. She got out of bed and quickly got ready. Her favorite task was to get dressed, and she was very successful. Molly's mom took her to the party where she was greeted by her friends. They all went inside the big bounce house and started jumping up and down. They laughed and sang songs and had a great time. After a while, the kids started to get tired and Molly's mom told everyone it was time to go. But when Molly got to the door she said, "Mommy, can I please stay? I haven't finished my bouncing yet!" Molly's mom smiled, "OK Molly, you can stay for a few more minutes," she said. Molly was so happy and excited, and she bounced around the bounce house for one more hour. When she was finally done, she was very successful with her task. She said goodbye to her friends and went home with her mom, feeling very happy and fulfilled.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong whale named Willy. Willy lived in the deep blue sea with his family. He loved to swim and play with his friends all day long. One day, Willy's mommy asked him to help her work. They needed to move some big rocks to make a new home for their family. Willy was very strong, so he knew he could help his mommy. Willy and his mommy worked together, pushing the big rocks with their strong tails. They moved all the rocks and made a nice new home. After they finished working, Willy felt happy and proud. His mommy gave him a big hug and said, "Thank you, Willy, for being such a strong helper!"

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big stack of cheap toys near a pool. "Can I have one, Mom?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, you can pick one toy." Tim chose a small boat to play with. He put the boat in the pool and watched it float. Suddenly, a big dog ran over and jumped into the pool. The dog made a big splash and the water went everywhere! The splash was so big that it knocked over the stack of cheap toys. Tim's mom was surprised, but she laughed. "What a silly dog!" she said. Tim laughed too and they played with the dog and the boat in the water.

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play together. One day, they were playing with their blocks and cars in the living room. Sara wanted to build a big tower with the blocks, but Ben wanted to drive his cars around the tower and make noises. "Ben, stop!" Sara said. "You are making my tower fall down!" "But I want to play with my cars!" Ben said. "They are fun and fast!" Sara and Ben started to argue and shout. They did not hear their mom calling them for lunch. Mom came into the living room and saw the mess and the noise. She was not happy. "Sara and Ben, what are you doing?" she asked. "It is time for lunch. Why are you fighting?" Sara and Ben stopped and looked at their mom. They felt bad. "We are sorry, mom," they said. "We wanted to play different things and we did not agree." Mom nodded and said, "I understand. Sometimes it is hard to share and cooperate. But you are twins and you love each other, right?" Sara and Ben nodded and hugged each other. "Right, mom," they said. "We love each other." Mom smiled and said, "That's good. Now, let's go to the kitchen and have some lunch. I made your favorite sandwiches and fruit salad." Sara and Ben followed their mom to the kitchen. They were hungry and happy. "Mom, you are so thoughtful," Sara said. "Thank you for making us lunch." "Yes, mom, you are the best," Ben said. "Can we request something?" Mom looked at them and said, "Sure, what is it?" Sara and Ben said, "Can we help you clean up the living room after lunch? We want to show you that we are sorry and that we can work together." Mom hugged them and said, "Of course, you can. That is very kind and responsible of you. I am proud of you, Sara and Ben." Sara and Ben smiled and ate their lunch. They learned that it is better to share and cooperate than to fight and argue. They also learned to appreciate their mom and her thoughtful gestures. They had a good day.
