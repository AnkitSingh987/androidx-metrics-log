Title: Pete and the Sun

The sun was out, so Pete decided to go on a walk. He stumbled along the path, peering around every corner. Soon, he stumbled upon a small house, tucked away in the shade. He clapped his hands and laughed. He opened the door, and stepped inside. The house was empty and very quiet. As Pete tiptoed around the rooms, searching for something interesting, he heard a voice. "Pete? Is that you?" it said. Pete looked up in surprise. An old man was sitting in the corner. He motioned for Pete to come closer. "I have a message for you," the old man said, in a stubborn voice. Pete inched closer, but he couldn't understand why the old man seemed to be talking to him. "This message, is for you," the man said, looking into Pete's eyes. "When you’re ready, you’ll know what it means." Pete nodded and thanked the man. He stepped outside and stumbled back along the path. As he walked, he thought about the message. What could it mean? He was determined to find out.

Once upon a time, there was a rocket. The rocket was big and red. It was bossy, too. It would tell the other toys what to do. The toys did not like the bossy rocket. One day, a box came to the playroom. The rocket said, "I will unpack the box." The rocket opened the box. Inside, there was a new toy. It was a small, green car. The green car was nice. It played with the other toys. The bossy rocket saw this and felt sad. The rocket said sorry to the toys. They all played together and had fun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a humble old man who lived in the deep woods. He loved to spend his days in peace and quiet, taking care of nature. "One day," he thought, "I will attach something special to the trunk of this old tree." Everywhere he went, he could see that the trees needed some love and care. The old man decided to go on a visit, to a distant place. He gathered some special materials and happily took them home. One day, he came to the tree and attached the very special item. He smiled and said, "Now, this old tree can finally have some special decoration!" When the old man finished, he proudly stepped back. He gasped when he saw that, suddenly, the tree was glowing with a magical light. The old man had created a magical surprise for the tree! He was so happy that he decided to go on more visits and attach more special items, so that the old tree would stay beautiful forever.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, rare book. It was in a box under his bed. He did not know where it came from. Tim was very happy. He wanted to read the story in the book. Tim pulled the book out of the box. He opened it and saw a lot of fun pictures. Tim's mom came into the room. She said, "Oh, that is a special story. I will read it to you tonight." That night, Tim's mom read the story to him. It was about a magic cat who could fly. The cat helped people by finding lost things. Tim loved the story. He knew the cat would find more things to help people.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. The fish lived in a dark, deep part of the sea. The fish liked to play with his friends. They would swim and splash all day. One day, the fish saw a big book. He wanted to learn new things. So, the fish started to study the book. He read about the sun, the moon, and the stars. He also learned about other animals that lived in the sea. The fish became very smart. He shared his knowledge with his friends. They all studied the book together. Soon, they knew a lot about the world. Even though it was dark in the deep sea, the fish and his friends felt happy and bright.

Once upon a time, there was a normal driver. He drove a big bus. The driver had a friend named Mr. Cat. Mr. Cat had a soft, fluffy tail. One day, the driver took Mr. Cat on the bus with him. As the bus went down the road, Mr. Cat's fluffy tail began to move. It moved back and forth, like a fan. The driver and Mr. Cat were happy. They liked the cool air from the tail. Then, the fluffy tail went "slap!" It hit the driver's face. The driver laughed. Mr. Cat laughed too. They had so much fun on the bus. And they lived happily ever after.
