Title: Lily and Sam and the Bench

Lily and Sam were playing in the park. They liked to run, slide and swing. They saw a big tent with many people inside. They heard music and laughter. "What is that?" Lily asked. "Let's go and see," Sam said. They ran to the tent and peeked inside. They saw a man and a woman in pretty clothes. The woman had a veil on her head. The man had a flower on his chest. They were holding hands and smiling. "They are getting married," a lady said. She was sitting near the tent. She had a plate of food in her lap. "Do you want to join them?" Lily and Sam nodded. They liked weddings. They went inside the tent and sat on a bench. They clapped and cheered when the man and the woman kissed. They got a piece of cake and a glass of juice. The cake was sweet and the juice was spicy. Lily and Sam liked the spicy juice. They drank it all. Then they felt hot and thirsty. "Can we have some water, please?" they asked the lady. "Of course, dear," the lady said. She gave them a bottle of water. "But be careful, this juice is very spicy. It can make you cough and sneeze." Lily and Sam drank the water and felt better. They thanked the lady and went back to the bench. They watched the man and the woman dance. They liked the music and the laughter. They were happy to join the wedding.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a cane in his grandpa's room. The cane was very useful for grandpa when he walked. Tim liked the cane because it was long and he could reach high things with it. One sunny day, Tim took the cane outside to play. As he played, he saw the sun shine brightly in the sky. He thought it was fun to use the cane to point at the sun and make shadows on the ground. Tim showed his friends how the cane could make shadows. They all laughed and played with the cane. They thought the cane was very useful, too. Tim was happy that he could share the fun with his friends.

One day, a compassionate cat named Tom saw a tie on the ground. He picked it up and decided to find its owner. Tom walked around asking his friends, "Is this your tie?" But no one knew whose tie it was. Tom met a big dog named Max. Max saw the tie and said, "I want that tie!" Tom asked, "Is this your tie?" Max said, "No, but I demand you give it to me!" Tom did not want to give the tie to Max because it was not his. Just then, a little bird flew down and said, "That's my tie!" The bird put on the tie and it turned into a beautiful scarf. Max was surprised and said, "I'm sorry for demanding the tie." Tom and Max learned that it's important to be kind and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a great snake. He liked to mix things up and he was always getting into trouble. One day, he was slithering around in the park when he met a little girl. She was playing with her toys and she was so curious to see the snake. The snake said, "Hi, I'm Mixy! What's your name, little one?" The little girl was in awe. She said, "My name is Eva and I'm three years old!" Mixy got an idea. He said, "Let's mix our toys together and make something new!" Eva wasn't sure. She said, "But won't we get in trouble?" Mixy said, "Don't worry! We'll be careful and have great fun!" Eventually, Eva got excited and she agreed. They mixed all the toys together and created the most wonderful thing ever. Mixy and Eva were so proud of their creation. They had overcome a conflict and made something great together.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little lizard. This lizard was very successful at catching bugs. He had many friends, but sometimes they would quarrel over who could catch the most bugs. One day, the lizard and his friends decided to have a bug-catching contest. They all went out into the forest to see who could catch the most bugs. The lizard was very good at catching bugs, so he thought he would win. But while they were playing, something unexpected happened. A big bird saw the lizard and his friends and thought they would make a good meal. The bird swooped down and grabbed the lizard in its beak. The lizard's friends were very sad. They stopped their quarrel and worked together to try to save the lizard. But they were too late. The bird had already flown away with the lizard, and they never saw their friend again.
