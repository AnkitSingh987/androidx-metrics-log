Title: The Man and the Coat

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a man who would deliver beef to the people. He was a very nice man, but he was also a little gloomy. He had an old, slow truck that he used to carry the beef from one house to another. One day, the man had a big job to do. He had to deliver beef to many houses. The sky was gray and it was a gloomy day. The man put on his coat and hat and started to drive his truck. He went from house to house, giving beef to the people. As he was driving, the man saw a little girl standing in the rain. She was sad because she was all wet. The man stopped his truck and gave the girl some beef to make her happy. The little girl smiled and thanked him. The man felt happy too, and the gloomy day didn't seem so bad anymore.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But they do not like to share. Sometimes they fight over who gets what. One day, their mom brings home a newspaper. She says it has important news and pictures. She puts it on the table and tells the twins not to touch it. She goes to the kitchen to make lunch. Anna and Ben are curious. They want to see the newspaper. They sneak to the table and grab it. They pull it from both ends. They hear a loud rip. The newspaper is torn in half. "Oh no!" Anna says. "Mom will be mad!" "Let's hide it!" Ben says. They run to their room and stuff the newspaper under the bed. But mom is reliable. She knows everything. She comes to their room and sees the newspaper sticking out. She is not happy. She makes the twins suffer. She takes away their toys and books for a week. She tells them to say sorry and learn to share. Anna and Ben are sad. They wish they had listened to mom. They hug each other and promise to be good. They hope mom will forgive them and give them back their toys and books. They learn their lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat pasta. One day, Tom was very hungry and needed to find some pasta to eat. Tom went to his friend, a jolly dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, I need pasta. Can you help me find some?" Max nodded and they went to look for pasta together. They looked high and low, but they could not find any pasta. Finally, they saw a big bowl of pasta on a table. Tom and Max were so happy! But the pasta was too high for them to reach. They tried to jump, but they could not get the pasta. Tom and Max were very sad because they could not eat the pasta.

Ben and Lily like to play with their toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big cage with animals. The animals are not real, they are made of plastic. But Ben and Lily pretend they are real and make noises for them. One day, Ben and Lily decide to make a zoo with their cage and animals. They put the cage on the floor and put some animals inside. They put a lion, a monkey, a zebra, and a giraffe. They leave some animals outside, like a bear, a snake, an elephant, and a crocodile. "Look, we have a zoo!" Ben says. "We are the zookeepers. We have to feed the animals and keep them happy." "Yes, and we have to be careful of the animals outside. They are wild and dangerous." Lily says. "They might try to eat the animals inside the cage." Ben and Lily pretend to give food and water to the animals inside the cage. They also make sounds for them, like roar, ooh-ooh, neigh, and maa. They have fun playing with their zoo. But then, something happens. The bear outside the cage sees the lion inside. The bear is hungry and wants to eat the lion. The bear is smart and knows how to open the cage. He pushes the cage door with his paw and makes it open. "Oh no, the bear is opening the cage!" Ben shouts. "He wants to eat the lion!" "Quick, we have to stop him!" Lily says. "We have to close the cage!" Ben and Lily run to the cage and try to close the door. But the bear is faster and stronger. He pushes them away and goes inside the cage. He roars at the lion and tries to bite him. The lion is scared and roars back. He tries to fight the bear. The monkey, the zebra, and the giraffe are also scared and try to run away. The cage is noisy and messy. Ben and Lily are scared too. They cry and call for help. They hope their mom or dad will come and save them and their animals. They wish they did not make a zoo with their cage and animals. They wish they played with something else.
