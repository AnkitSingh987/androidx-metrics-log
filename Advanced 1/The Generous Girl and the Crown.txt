Title: The Generous Girl and the Crown

Once upon a time there was a generous girl. She wanted to play with her friends today but she had nothing to do. So she started to imagine. She imagined that she was a princess. She had a beautiful dress, jewellery and crown. She thought she looked so special. As she was imagining, her friends appeared. They had a wonderful surprise for her, it was a new doll! They were so generous! The girl was delighted. She thanked them, hugged them and continued to imagine. She was a queen and the new doll was her princess. As they were all playing, the girl and her friends had so much fun. Today was the best day ever!

The two friends, Jose and Maria, were sitting in a meadow, looking up at the blue sky and chatting. Jose thought of something to say and said, "Let's play war!" Maria replied, "No, I don't want to play war. Let's just play tag, or do something else." Jose pressed on. "Why don't you want to play war? You know it's a dependable game!" Maria was not convinced. She said firmly, "No, I don't like it. It's not fun, and it's not dependable. It's too dangerous." Jose refused to back down, and he said, "It's dependable, and we can keep it safe by following some rules. Please, can't we just try? For a little bit?" Maria was worried, but she knew Jose was dependable. She finally agreed. The two of them set up the rules for their war game and cautiously began to play. They had a lot of fun and made sure to play by the rules. Maria was happy she trusted her friend and agreed to play war.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a friend named Sam. They liked to play together at the park. One day, they saw a big dog. Tim was nervous because the dog was very big. Sam was not scared, but he wanted to help his friend. Sam said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will remind you of something. Remember when we saw a big cat and we were not scared? This is just like that." Tim thought about it and felt a little better. The big dog came closer to them. Tim and Sam held hands and stood up tall. They said, "Go away, big dog!" The dog stopped and looked at them. Then it wagged its tail and walked away. Tim and Sam were happy they faced their fear together, and they went back to playing at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom clean the house. One day, Tim's mom asked him to polish the floor. She told him that if he did a good job, they would have a successful clean house. Tim was excited to polish the floor. He got a big cloth and started to rub the floor. He rubbed and rubbed until the floor was shiny. Tim's mom was happy to see him work so hard. She knew that he would be successful in making the floor look nice. After Tim finished polishing the floor, his mom came to see his work. She saw the shiny floor and was very happy. Tim felt proud of his successful job. They both knew that their house was now clean and pretty. And from that day on, Tim always helped his mom polish the floor to make their house look nice.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw his dad reading a newspaper. Tim wanted to help his dad finish the newspaper, so he could play with him. Tim's dad said, "Thank you, Tim! Let's finish reading this newspaper together." As they were reading, Tim saw a picture of a fit man running. Tim said, "Dad, I want to be fit like that man!" His dad smiled and said, "You can be, Tim! Let's start running together after we finish the newspaper." But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and took the newspaper from Tim's hands. The bird flew high up into a tree. Tim and his dad looked at each other and laughed. "Well, I guess we finished the newspaper!" said Tim's dad. "Now, let's go run and be fit like the man in the picture!"
