Title: Lily the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was scared of the dark. One day, her mom asked her to remove the laundry from the washing machine. Lily was scared because the laundry room was dark. Lily took a deep breath and went into the dark laundry room. She found the washing machine and started to remove the laundry. She felt better because she was doing something to help her mom. As she removed the laundry, she found her favorite teddy bear. She hugged the teddy bear and felt brave. Lily was not scared anymore. She finished removing the laundry and brought it to her mom. Her mom was proud of her, and Lily was proud of herself for not being scared anymore.

Once there was a young boy named Tom. Tom lived in a very hot place. Every day he would go outside and try to resist the heat. But it was always too hot. Even his favorite things such as long walks and playing with his friends were too hard in the heat. One day Tom decided he didn't want to resist any more. He wanted to stay inside. He curled up in his bed, where it was cool and comfortable. As he was lying there, he realized that it was actually nice not to resist. The next day, Tom went outside with a new mindset. He didn't fight against the heat. Instead, he enjoyed it. He played games in the hot sun and really started to appreciate the heat. Tom learned an important lesson that day. Although young, he figured out that if he resisted everything, he was missing out. Life is too short to resist the good things!

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick bear. The bear had a key. The key was for his cave. The bear wanted to go on a trip. So, he started to pack his bag. He put his favorite toy, a red ball, in the bag. He also put in some yummy food. The bear was very happy. He was ready to go on his trip. He locked his cave with the key and left. The bear walked and walked. He played with his red ball. But then, the ball went into a deep hole. The bear was sad. He tried to get the ball out, but he could not. The bear started to cry. He wanted to go home. The bear went back to his cave. But he lost the key. The bear was very sad. He could not go into his cave. The bear had a bad day.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big, red ambulance. He liked how it looked, so he decided he wanted to be a helper like the people in the ambulance. Tim told his mom, "I want to be a helper like them!" His mom smiled and said, "You can be anything you want, Tim." Tim knew he had to be flexible to be a good helper. He practiced by helping his mom pick up toys and clean the house. He also helped his friends when they needed it. Everyone saw that Tim was good at being flexible and helping others. One day, Tim's friend fell and hurt his leg. Tim remembered how the ambulance helpers made people feel better. He ran to get a grown-up, and they called the ambulance. When the ambulance came, Tim told the helpers what happened. They were happy that Tim was there to help. Tim felt proud that he could be a helper like the people in the ambulance.

Anna and Ben were friends. They liked to play together and share their toys. One day, they decided to pretend they were married. They put on their parents' clothes and rings and said, "I do". They wanted to go on a vacation, like their parents did. They packed a bag with some snacks and a blanket and went outside. They saw a big bus parked on the street. They thought it was going to a fun place. They climbed on the bus and hid under a seat. The bus started to move. Anna and Ben were excited. They thought they would see the beach or the zoo or the park. But the bus did not stop. It went on and on and on. Anna and Ben started to feel scared. They did not know where they were. They did not see their home or their school or their friends. They wanted to go back. They tried to get off the bus, but the door was locked. They cried for help, but no one heard them. They were alone and lost. They wished they had not pretended to be married. They wished they had not gone on the vacation. They were ashamed of their silly game. They missed their parents and their toys and their bed. They did not know what to do. They hugged each other and hoped someone would find them. But no one did. The end.
