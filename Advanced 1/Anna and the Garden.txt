Title: Anna and the Garden

Anna liked to play in the garden. She saw many things there. She saw flowers, bees, birds and ants. She liked to watch the ants. They were very busy. They carried food and dirt to their home. One day, Anna saw a big ant. It was bigger than the other ants. It had a shiny back. It looked strong and smart. Anna wanted to be its friend. She said hello to the big ant. The big ant said hello back. Anna asked the big ant what it was doing. The big ant said it was looking for food. It said it was hungry and tired. It said it wanted to rest. Anna said she had some food. She had a cookie in her pocket. She broke a piece and gave it to the big ant. The big ant said thank you. It ate the cookie. It said it was very good. Anna asked the big ant if it wanted to play. The big ant said it was sorry, but it had to go. It said it had to help its friends. It said it had to work hard. It said it was honest and loyal. It said it loved its family. Anna said she understood. She said she was honest and loyal too. She said she loved her family. She said she hoped to see the big ant again. The big ant said it hoped so too. It said goodbye to Anna. It went back to its home. Anna smiled. She felt happy. She had made a new friend. She had learned something new. She had shared something good. She decided to rest too. She lay down on the grass. She closed her eyes. She dreamed of the big ant and the garden.

One day, a little bird was in a birdcage. The bird was very sleepy. It wanted to fly and soar high in the sky. The bird looked out of the birdcage and saw the big blue sky. A kind boy came to the birdcage. He saw the sleepy bird and said, "Little bird, do you want to go out and play?" The bird nodded its head. The boy opened the birdcage door and let the bird out. The bird was so happy. It started to soar high in the sky. The bird sang, "Thank you, kind boy!" The boy smiled and waved to the bird. The sleepy bird was now wide awake and very happy to be free.

Once upon a time, there was a prince and a princess. They were both very happy and excited because they were going to marry each other. The day of the wedding was very special. Everyone was wearing their best clothes and the prince and princess were so happy and excited. But then, something terrible happened and the prince and princess could not marry each other. Everyone was very sad and upset. But then, something amazing happened! The prince and princess decided to join forces and help others in need. They worked very hard and eventually they reached a big victory. They were really proud that they could help others and they learned a valuable lesson: it is better to help others than focus on achieving victories by yourself.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ball and jump around the house. One day, she saw a big bowl of dessert on the table. It was her favorite, ice cream! Kitty wanted to eat the dessert, but she knew she had to wait for Mommy. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse came out from behind the fridge. The mouse saw the dessert too and wanted to eat it! Kitty did not want the mouse to eat her dessert, so she started to scream. "No, mouse! That's my dessert!" But the mouse did not listen and kept going towards the ice cream. Just when Kitty thought the mouse was going to eat her dessert, a twist happened. The mouse stopped and looked at her. "I'm sorry, Kitty," said the mouse. "I didn't know it was your dessert. Let's share it!" Kitty was surprised but happy. They both enjoyed the ice cream together and became friends. And from that day on, Kitty and the mouse shared many more desserts and fun times.
