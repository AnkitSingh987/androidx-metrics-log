Title: Tom the Strong Man and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a strong man named Tom. Tom loved to raise heavy things. One day, Tom saw a big piano. The piano was very heavy, but Tom wanted to raise it. Tom tried to raise the piano. He pushed and pulled with all his strength. The piano moved a little bit. Tom was happy, but he wanted to raise it more. Tom asked his friends to help him. They all pushed and pulled together. The strong friends raised the piano high in the air. They were very happy and proud. They played a fun song on the piano and danced all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship had a loyal dog named Spot. Spot was always happy to be on the ship. He would run and play all day with his friends. One day, the ship went to a new place. Spot saw many new things. He saw big trees, pretty flowers, and lots of birds. Spot wanted to play with the birds, but they were too high in the sky. As the sun went down, Spot began to feel very tired. He let out a big yawn. His friends on the ship saw him yawn and knew it was time to go to sleep. They all went back to the ship and snuggled up together. Spot was happy to be with his friends on the big ship. They all slept well, ready for a new day of fun and adventure.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to perform for her friends and family. She would dance and sing for them all the time. One day, Mia's mom told her, "Mia, you are so good at performing, you should share your talent with everyone in town!" Mia thought this was a great idea. So, she and her mom made a plan. They would invite everyone in town to a big show in the park. Mia's mom would make chocolate treats for everyone to eat while they watched Mia perform. Mia was so excited! The day of the show came, and it was a little bit icy outside. But that didn't stop Mia! She put on her warmest clothes and went to the park with her mom. When they got there, they saw that many people had come to watch Mia perform. Mia's mom passed out the chocolate treats to everyone, and they all sat down to watch. Mia started to dance and sing, and everyone clapped and cheered. Even though it was icy, everyone was having a great time. Mia felt so happy that she could share her talent with everyone she knew. After the show, Mia's friend said, "Mia, you are the best performer ever! Thank you for sharing your talent with us!" From that day on, Mia knew that she could bring happiness to others by performing. And she never let a little icy weather stop her from sharing her love of dance and song with her friends, family, and the whole town.

Once upon a time, there was a toy soldier named Tim. Tim liked to march around the playroom with his friends. They would march up and down, and side to side. Tim had a toy missile that he was very proud of. One day, a new toy came to the playroom. Her name was Sally, and she was a pretty doll. All the other toys liked Sally, and they wanted to play with her. Tim felt jealous because he wanted to play with his friends too. Tim marched up to Sally and said, "Do you want to see my missile?" Sally smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Tim showed her his missile, and Sally clapped her hands. "That's so cool!" she said. Tim felt happy that Sally liked his missile. He wasn't jealous anymore, and they all played together. From that day on, Tim, Sally, and their friends would march around the playroom, having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a restless boy who liked to run and play all day. One day, while playing outside, Tim found a small magic rock. He picked it up with his hand and took it home. That night, Tim had a dream. In the dream, the magic rock told Tim that it could make his restless legs run faster. Tim was very happy and excited. He wanted to be the fastest boy in his town. The next day, Tim held the magic rock in his hand and ran as fast as he could. He was faster than all his friends and even the big kids. Tim was very happy and thanked the magic rock. From that day on, Tim and the magic rock were best friends, and they played together every day.
