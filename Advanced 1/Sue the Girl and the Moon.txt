Title: Sue the Girl and the Moon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a yummy cake for her birthday. She was very happy because all her friends came to her party. In the party room, there was a long cord hanging from the ceiling. Sue's mom tied colorful balloons to the cord. The balloons made the room look very pretty. At night, the moon and stars came out to play. They shined brightly in the sky. Sue looked out the window and saw them shine. She smiled and went to bed, dreaming of her fun birthday party.

One day, a weak little bird was sitting on a rock. The bird saw a big tornado coming. The bird was scared and did not know what to do. The bird said, "Help! I am too weak to fly away from the tornado." A big, strong dog heard the bird. The dog said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you." The dog picked up the bird in his mouth and ran away from the tornado. They hid behind a big tree. Then, something unexpected happened. The tornado stopped and turned into a big pile of toys! The bird and the dog were happy and played with the toys together. They became best friends and had fun all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was in a class with many other kids. Sue liked to learn and improve, but sometimes she was a bit rude. One day, Sue was talking with her friend, Tom. "I want to improve in class, but I don't like being rude," said Sue. Tom thought for a moment and said, "Let's try to be nice to everyone and help each other improve." The next day, something unexpected happened. A new girl named Lily joined the class. Lily was very shy and quiet. Sue and Tom decided to be kind and help her improve too. They all became good friends and learned that being kind was much better than being rude. And they all improved in their class together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very impatient. He wanted to play catch with his big brother, Sam. But Sam was not home yet. Tim sat by the door and waited. Finally, Sam came home. Tim jumped up and said, "Let's play catch!" Sam smiled and said, "Okay, let's go to the park." At the park, they found a good spot to start their game. Tim was excited to play with his big brother. But then, a big dog came running up to them. The dog wanted to play too! Tim got scared and did not want to play catch anymore. Sam saw that Tim was upset, so he found a way to play catch with the dog and Tim at the same time. They all had fun, and Tim was not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Mia. She loved to dance and go outside to play. Every day, when Mia walked in the park she would dance around in circles with the birds. One day, when Mia was in the park, she saw something shiny and sparkly in the trees. It was a box that said 'blue chocolate' on it. Mia was so excited to open the box. She asked her mom, "Can I open the box please?" Her mother smiled and said, "Yes, you can open it, but only after you dance for 10 minutes!" Mia was so happy that she started to jerk and twirl around like a monkey. When Mia finished her dance, her mom opened the box to reveal a big plate of blue chocolates. Mia ate all of the chocolates and smiled a very big smile. She learned that if you dance and have patience, you can get what you wish!

Ben and Lily were friends. They liked to play in the forest. One day, they saw a big bush with many blueberries. They were hungry and wanted to eat some. "Look, Ben, blueberries!" Lily said. "Let's pick some and share." "OK, Lily, but we have to hurry. Mom said we have to be home soon." Ben said. They ran to the bush and started to pick the blueberries. They were sweet and juicy. They filled their hands and mouths with them. But they did not see the big bear behind the bush. The bear was also hungry and wanted to eat some blueberries. He saw the two children and growled. Ben and Lily heard the growl and turned around. They saw the bear and were very scared. They dropped the blueberries and screamed. "Run, Lily, run!" Ben said. They tried to run away, but the bear was faster. He caught them and ate them. He was not calm. He was angry and hungry. The end.
