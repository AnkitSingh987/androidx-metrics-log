Title: Phillip and the Car

Phillip was playing in his bedroom. He had a Lego set and was so excited, he started to build. But then he heard a loud noise coming from outside. It sounded like a horn. Phillip ran outside and saw his neighbor Mr. Jenkins. He was holding a horn and looking angry. "Why did you blow that horn?" asked Phillip. "I brought a new car and I wanted to test it," said Mr. Jenkins. Phillip was sad. "But I'm sorry. I didn't mean to ruin your fun." Mr. Jenkins smiled. "It's OK. I'm sorry too. But, why don't you help me build a garage for my car? Then we can both have fun." Phillip's face brightened up. Together they started to build a garage. The garage was so big and so strong that it could fit three cars! Phillip was excited. He learned a great lesson that day - building with a friend is more fun than playing alone.

Once upon a time, there was a red house. In the house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to paint. She would paint all day. One day, Lily found a razor. She did not know what it was. She thought it was a new paint tool. Lily wanted to try it with her paint. Lily started to paint with the razor. But, oh no! The razor was sharp! It cut the paper and made a big hole. Lily was sad. Her painting was ruined. And that is the end of the story.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Bessy. One day, Bessy was walking in the park with her Mom. Something made her stop and look up into the sky. Bessy said, "Mom, what's that?" Mom looked up and saw a beautiful kite in the sky. It was big and brown and seemed to be flying very high. Mom said, "That's a kite, Bessy. It belongs to someone and they are flying it in the sky." Bessy looked sad and said, "Mom, can we have a kite too?" Mom said, "Of course we can get a kite, but first we need to take care of our own things. We have to make sure everything is in order and nobody else is missing anything." Bessy nodded and smiled, understanding the lesson that Mom was teaching her. From then on, Bessy knew that she needed to be responsible and take care of her own things before asking for something new.

Once upon a time, there was a brave squirrel. He lived in a big tree in the park. Every day, he would play with his friends, the birds and the bugs. One day, the brave squirrel saw a little girl crying. She had lost her toy in the tree. The squirrel wanted to help her. He leaned on a branch and reached out to get the toy. The brave squirrel gave the toy back to the little girl. She was so happy and hugged the squirrel. They became good friends and played together in the park every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a yellow house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and run around in the big land behind his house. Every day, he would go outside and have fun with his dog, Spot. One day, while Tim and Spot were playing, they heard a loud scream. They looked around and saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird couldn't fly and was very sad. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he picked it up gently and took it to his mom. Tim's mom helped him take care of the bird. They gave it food and water, and soon the bird's wing was all better. The bird was so happy that it sang a beautiful song for Tim and his mom. Then, it flew away into the sky, back to its home in the trees. Tim and Spot waved goodbye to their new friend and went back to playing in the big land behind their yellow house.

Once there was a girl named Lily. Lily had a mom who loved smelly perfume. One day, Lily wanted to give her mom a gift. She thought, "I will wrap her perfume in pretty paper." Lily found the perfume and pretty paper. She started to wrap the perfume. But then, something unexpected happened. A cat jumped on the table and knocked the perfume over. The perfume broke and made a big mess. Lily was sad, but she had an idea. She took the pretty paper and put it in the smelly perfume. The paper soaked up the perfume. Lily wrapped the paper around a new gift, a book. When her mom opened the gift, she was happy. The book smelled just like her favorite perfume.
