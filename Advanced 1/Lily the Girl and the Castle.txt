Title: Lily the Girl and the Castle

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big yellow castle. She wanted to see what was inside. She walked up to the big door and knocked. "Hello, can I come in?" she asked. Inside the castle, there lived a lonely king. He heard Lily's voice and opened the door. "Hello, little girl! I am the king of this castle. You can come in, but you must be quiet. There is a big, mean dragon that lives here too." Lily and the king walked around the castle. They saw pretty flowers and big rooms. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was the dragon! The dragon was angry and wanted to scare Lily away. Lily was scared, but the king had an idea. They both picked up yellow flowers and threw them at the dragon. The dragon saw the pretty yellow flowers and stopped being angry. It liked the flowers and did not want to scare Lily anymore. The dragon, Lily, and the king all became friends. They played in the yellow castle and had a fun day together.

Lily likes to walk with her mom every morning. They walk to the park and see the birds, the flowers and the swings. Lily likes to swing high and feel the wind on her face. One day, Lily sees some people on the grass. They are doing yoga. They stretch their arms and legs and make funny shapes with their bodies. Lily is curious and wants to try. "Mom, what are they doing?" Lily asks. "They are doing yoga, Lily. It is a way to exercise and be healthy. Do you want to join them?" Mom says. "Yes, please!" Lily says. Mom and Lily walk to the people and say hello. They are very friendly and let them join. Lily follows the teacher and tries to copy the poses. She bends like a cat, stands like a tree and balances like a flamingo. She feels happy and strong. Lily likes yoga. She thinks it is fun and good for her. She thanks the teacher and the people and walks back with her mom. "Mom, can we do yoga again?" Lily asks. "Of course, Lily. We can do yoga anytime you want. It is good for us to walk and do yoga. We are healthy and happy." Mom says. Lily smiles and hugs her mom. She likes to walk and do yoga with her mom. They are healthy and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Joe. Joe was only three, but he was very brave. He decided he wanted to go for a little walk all by himself. Joe was walking, then he saw something. It was a big dog and it was barking. Joe was scared and he lay down in the grass. He stayed very still and looked at the enormous dog. The dog could sense that Joe was scared and it started to come closer. Joe lay there and stayed quiet. Then, all of a sudden, he heard a voice. "It's ok," the voice said. "The dog is weak and won't hurt you." Joe opened his eyes and saw a man, waving his hand to the dog. He called it away and Joe found himself in silence. Joe thanked the man for his help. The man smiled and said, "It's ok, no need to worry". Then he patted Joe on the head. Joe felt so happy and smiled. The man waved goodbye and Joe went home. He was relieved that he was safe and secure. He was proud that he was still so brave.

Once upon a time, there was a big machine in a gloomy room. A boy named Tim lived in a house close to the room. Tim was scared of the big machine. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was not scared of the big machine. She said, "Let's go see the machine." Tim and Sue went to the gloomy room. Sue looked at the machine and said, "I will start it." She pushed a button and the machine started. The machine made a loud noise. Tim and Sue were scared. They thought the machine was bad. But then, something unexpected happened. The machine stopped making loud noises. The machine started to sing a happy song. Tim and Sue looked at each other and laughed. They were not scared anymore. The machine was not bad. It was a fun, singing machine. Tim and Sue became good friends and they played with the singing machine every day.
