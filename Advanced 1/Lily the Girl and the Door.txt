Title: Lily the Girl and the Door

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to find her toy. She looked in her room and under her bed, but she could not find it. Then, she remembered her mom had an expensive dress in the closet. Maybe the toy was there. Lily went to the closet and opened the door. Inside, she saw the expensive dress and her toy. She was so happy! "I found you!" she said to her toy. She grabbed the toy, but the dress fell down too. Her mom came in and saw the expensive dress on the floor. She was not happy. "Lily, you made a mess," her mom said. Lily felt bad. She did not mean to make a mess. But now, her mom was sad, and the day had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to watch her bright screen. She watched it every day. One day, she saw a big, fluffy cat on the screen. The cat was so cute! Sue wanted to find a cat just like it. Sue went outside to look for a cat. She looked under a tree and behind a bush. Sue started to worry. She could not find a cat anywhere. She felt sad and went back inside. The next day, Sue went outside again. She saw something moving in the grass. It was a big, fluffy cat just like the one on the screen! Sue was so happy. She took the cat home and they played together all day. Sue never had to worry about finding a cat again.

One day, a jolly bunny went to play. The bunny loved to hop all day. The sun was bright and the grass was green. The bunny was happy to be outside. As the bunny hopped, it saw a figure. The figure was big and tall. The bunny was curious and wanted to see who it was. So, the bunny hopped closer and closer to the figure. When the bunny got close, it saw that the figure was not a person. It was a big, tall tree! The tree had a face and could talk. The tree said, "Hello, little bunny! I saw you hop and I wanted to be your friend!" The bunny was surprised, but happy to have a new friend. From that day on, the bunny and the tree were the best of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was cold and there was ice on the ground. Tim saw his friend, Sam, who was feeling weak because he was sick. They wanted to play together, but Sam was scared he might fall on the ice. "Please forgive me, Tim," said Sam. "I don't want to play on the ice today. I am weak and might fall." Tim was sad, but he understood. He wanted to help Sam feel better. So, he had an idea. "Let's play inside my house," said Tim. "We can be warm and safe." Sam agreed, and they went inside Tim's house to play. But when they opened the door, they saw something unexpected. Tim's mom had made a big ice castle in the living room! She wanted to surprise them with a fun game. Tim and Sam were so happy, and they played together all day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the kitchen. She saw her mom put a cake in the oven. Lily was very happy because she loved cake. She whispered to her cat, "We will eat cake soon!" Lily and her cat waited and waited. The oven made a sound. "Ding!" It was time to take the cake out. But when Lily's mom opened the oven, she saw something unexpected. The cake was not sweet and yummy. It was bitter and yucky! Lily's mom looked at the oven and saw a note. The note said, "I am the oven fairy. I made your cake bitter because you forgot to say 'please' when you put it in the oven." Lily and her mom said "sorry" to the oven fairy. They promised to be more polite next time.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a museum with his mom. The museum was big and had many things to see. Tim liked the museum a lot. In the museum, there was a mysterious room that no one knew about. Tim wanted to find the mysterious room. Tim and his mom walked around the museum. They saw many things like big animals and pretty pictures. Tim was happy but he still wanted to find the mysterious room. Then, he saw a small door. Tim thought the mysterious room was behind the door. Tim opened the door and went inside. It was dark, so he squeezed his mom's hand. They walked together and found the mysterious room. In the room, there were many soft toys and fun games. Tim played with the toys and had a great time. His mom was happy too, and they had a fun day at the museum.
