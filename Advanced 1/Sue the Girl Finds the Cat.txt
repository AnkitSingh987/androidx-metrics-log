Title: Sue the Girl Finds the Cat

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to write a note for her mom. Sue liked the sound of her pen on the paper. She wrote "I love you, Mom" on the paper. She put the note on the table for her mom to see. Sue felt happy. Later, Sue heard a sound outside. She saw a little cat by the door. The cat looked sad and hungry. Sue had an idea. She went back inside and got her note. Sue took the note to the cat. The cat looked at the note and then up at Sue. Something unexpected happened. The cat started to talk! "Thank you for the note, Sue. I am a magic cat. I can help you." Sue was surprised but happy. The magic cat helped Sue and her mom. They all became good friends. Sue learned that even a little note can make a big change.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a tall palm tree with yummy fruit on it. Tim wanted to pick the fruit, but he was too small. "Mom, can you help me pick the fruit?" Tim asked, looking worried. His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! I'll help you." So, Tim's mom picked the fruit from the palm tree and gave it to Tim. He was very happy and not worried anymore. They sat under the tree and ate the yummy fruit together. And they had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable turkey. Every day, she would jog around the farm. Her little legs worked hard and before long, she was running like the wind! When the other animals saw her, they cheered her on. They clapped and cheered her as she ran faster and faster. Then one morning, the turkey noticed something in the distance. It was a huge apple pie! She wanted to get closer, so she started to jog even faster. Finally, she reached the pie and what a delicious sight it was! The dependable turkey gobbled it up in no time. She had so much energy afterwards she decided to keep jogging around the farm. The other animals cheered and clapped as she raced around. The dependable turkey was so happy that she kept running until the sun went down. And that is why the turkey loves to jog!

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. Jane wanted something very special, but she wasn't sure what it was. She was very frustrated. One day, Jane and her mother went to the park. Jane couldn't wait to explore. She ran up to the swings, ready to swing high. But as she swung, she heard something. It sounded like singing! Jane concentrated and listened. Was this what she was looking for? Just then her mother called out. "Jane! Mind your lungs!" Jane remembered her mother's words of caution, and she stopped swinging. "I mind it," she said. Jane wasn't sure why her mother said that, but she kept it in her mind. The next day, Jane went back to the park. It was a special day because Jane's grandma was joining them. Jane ran up to the swings. She started to swing higher and higher, and soon, the singing she heard before began again. Grandma smiled. "Jane, you minding that singing with your lungs is very special," she said. Jane was so excited! She was sure this was the special thing she was looking for. Jane sang with her lungs, feeling no longer frustrated. She was sure that singing was the best gift for her.

One day, a goose went to the park. The goose liked to enjoy the sun and play with the kids. The goose had a hard time making friends, but he was happy. In the park, the goose met a boy. The boy said, "Hi, goose! Do you want to play?" The goose was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to play!" They played all day and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The boy took off his hat and showed the goose that he was a goose too! The first goose was so surprised, but now they were both happy. They played together every day and became best friends.
