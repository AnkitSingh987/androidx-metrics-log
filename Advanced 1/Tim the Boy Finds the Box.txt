Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved going to his classroom every day. His classroom was very organized. Tim's teacher, Miss Lily, liked to keep everything in its place. Tim and his friends knew where everything belonged. One day, Tim saw a big box in the classroom. He was very curious. Miss Lily said, "This box is a surprise for later. Remember where it belongs." Tim and his friends were excited and couldn't wait to see what was inside the box. After they finished playing and learning, Miss Lily said it was time for the surprise. She opened the big box and took out a big, colorful ball. Tim and his friends were so happy! They played with the ball all afternoon. At the end of the day, Tim remembered where the ball belonged and put it back in the box. Miss Lily was proud of Tim for remembering and keeping the classroom organized.

Once upon a time, in a big country, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very bored. Every day, he would walk around and look for fun things to do. One day, he saw a big, red ball. Tim thought, "I want that ball. I will steal it." So, Tim took the ball and ran away. He played with the ball all day long. But then, a little girl named Sue came to Tim. She said, "That is my ball. You took it from me. Give it back, please." Tim felt sad. He did not want to make Sue sad too. He said, "I am sorry. I did not know it was your ball." He gave the ball back to Sue. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue smiled and said, "It's okay. We can share the ball and play together." Tim and Sue played with the ball together. They had so much fun. From that day on, they were the best of friends. And Tim was never bored again.

Avery went to the park with her mom one sunny day. She saw some strange things there. Avery's mom said, "Avery, look. Do you know what that is?" Avery looked and said, "It's a weird baseball." "Yes, it's a weird baseball," said her mom. "Do you know what this means?" Avery smiled and said, "It means I can play!" Avery and her mom both had so much fun playing baseball. They played for a long time, and Avery even hit the ball at one point. When Avery was getting tired, her mom said, "Avery, thank you for playing. I'm so glad that you care about playing baseball." Avery smiled and said, "Me too."

Once upon a time, there was a little bridge. This bridge was open for everyone to use. It helped people go from one side to the other side of the river. The bridge liked to serve and make people happy. One day, a big, strong man came to the bridge. He looked at the bridge and said, "You are too little. I don't think you can help me." The bridge was sad but still wanted to serve the big, strong man. So the bridge said, "Please try me. I can help you." The big, strong man walked on the bridge. As he walked, something unexpected happened. The little bridge became a big bridge! The big, strong man was very happy and said, "Thank you, bridge. You are a good helper." The bridge was happy to serve and help people, no matter how big or small they were.

One day, a boy named Tom found a weird toy in his room. It was round and had many colors. Tom did not know what the toy could do. He decided to play with it and see what would happen. Tom moved the toy in a motion like a circle. Suddenly, the toy started to spin on its own. Tom was surprised and a little scared. He did not know how the toy could spin without him touching it. He thought it was magic. Tom wanted the toy to stop spinning. He said "please stop" and the toy stopped. The toy seemed to yield to Tom's words. Tom was happy that he could control the toy with his words. But then, his mom came into the room and told him that the toy was just a normal top and that it was not magic. Tom felt a little silly but still enjoyed playing with his new toy.
