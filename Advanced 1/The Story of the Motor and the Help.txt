Title: The Story of the Motor and the Help

Once upon a time, there was a big, gray motor. It worked all day, never taking a break. It was always very busy. One day, the motor got very tired. It stopped working. It said "Help me!" A little boy heard the motor and ran to help it. He fixed the motor with some tools and a few screws. The motor said "Thank you! Now I can keep working." The little boy was happy that he could help the motor. He said "You're welcome!" and the motor said "Thank you again!" The little boy smiled and went on his way. He was so glad he could help the big, gray motor.

Once there was a little girl called Daisy. She loved playing outside in the sunshine. One day, Daisy heard a loud noise in the sky. It was a deep rumbling sound. She realised it was thunder. The sound scared her so she ran back inside her house. She peeped through the window and noticed the sky was dark and grey. She started to feel afraid. Then Daisy's mum came in. Daisy said to her mum, "I'm scared of the thunder". Her mum thought for a moment and said, "You don't have to be scared of thunder Daisy. When it thunders the sky is just having a conversation with us". Daisy looked at her mum and smiled. She knew that she didn't need expensive things to be happy. Daisy knew that being outside in nature and having conversations were much more important than buying expensive things. Moral of the story: You don't need expensive things to be happy.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play at the park. There is a big slide at the park. Lily and Max want to slide on it. But there is a problem. The slide is too high for them. They are anxious. They are afraid to fall. They look at the slide and say, "Oh no!" Then they see Ben. Ben is older. He is good at sliding. He slides fast and smooth. He smiles and says, "Hi, Lily and Max. Do you want to slide with me?" Lily and Max nod. They want to try. Ben says, "Don't worry. I will help you. Sliding is fun. It is my job to teach you." Ben holds their hands. He takes them to the top of the slide. He says, "Ready, set, go!" He slides with them. They slide down together. They feel the wind and the sun. They laugh and shout, "Yay!" Lily and Max are happy. They are not anxious anymore. They like sliding. They say, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good teacher. Can we slide again?" Ben says, "Sure. Sliding is my job. And my job is fun."

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She was a very attractive girl with big blue eyes and long brown hair. One day, Lily went to the market with her mom to buy some vegetables. At the market, Lily saw a man loading a big truck with vegetables. She saw the man drop a carrot and said, "Mister, you dropped a carrot!" The man smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily! You are so helpful." When they got home, Lily's mom started to cook the vegetables. Suddenly, they heard a knock on the door. It was the man from the market! He had a big surprise for Lily. The man said, "Lily, because you helped me at the market, I want to give you a gift." He gave her a big, beautiful toy horse. Lily was so happy and couldn't believe the surprise. From that day on, she always helped others and had many more fun adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy who was walking around in the forest with his mum. While they were walking, they noticed something very unusual. It was an old, rusty axe. The little boy asked his mum: "What is that?" To which she replied: "It's an axe, it's very old." But the little boy was not sure, so he said: "Can I peek at it?". His mum said yes, so the little boy went closer to peek. The minute the little boy touched the axe, the axe suddenly grew in size. And before they knew it, the little boy and his mum were surrounded by giants an trees. It was so scary that the little boy held onto his mum's hand tightly. The giants said: "You are in Big Tree Forest! This is our home, no one is allowed to take any of the trees. If you do, you will be punished!" The little boy was very scared and wanted to run away, but his mum told him it was better to stay and talk to the giants. And so, his mum explained that they were only curious and had no intention to take any trees from the forest. The giants agreed and allowed the little boy and his mum to go.
