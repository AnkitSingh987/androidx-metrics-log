Title: Lily and Tom and the Cake

Lily and Tom like to play with their toys. They have dolls, cars, blocks and books. They play in their room or in the garden. Sometimes they play with their dog, Spot. Spot is a big, brown dog. He likes to run and jump and bark. One day, Lily and Tom want to do something new. They ask their mom if they can bake a cake. Mom says yes, but they have to help her. She gives them aprons and bowls and spoons. They mix flour, sugar, eggs and milk. They add some chocolate chips. They pour the batter into a pan. Mom puts the pan in the oven. Lily and Tom have to wait for the cake to bake. They are bored. They look for Spot. They find him on the couch. He is sleeping. He is lazy. They try to wake him up. They tickle his ears. They pull his tail. They say his name. Spot does not move. He snores. Lily and Tom give up. They go back to the kitchen. They smell something good. The cake is ready. Mom takes it out of the oven. She lets it cool. She cuts a slice for Lily and Tom. They taste it. It is yummy. They say thank you to mom. They share some cake with Spot. Spot wakes up. He licks his lips. He is happy. Lily and Tom are happy too. They like to bake. They like to play. They like to eat cake.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy little bunny named Bop. Bop lived in a small house with his mommy and daddy. Bop loved to eat vegetables like carrots and peas. One day, Bop went to the garden to pick some vegetables for dinner. He saw a big, green vegetable that he had never seen before. "Mommy, what is this?" he asked. "That is a broccoli," his mommy said. Bop was very excited to try the new vegetable. At dinner time, Bop took a big bite of the broccoli. Suddenly, he screamed! "Yuck!" he said. "I don't like this vegetable!" His mommy smiled and said, "That's okay, Bop. You don't have to like every vegetable. Just try a little bit of everything, and eat what you like." Bop was happy to eat his carrots and peas, and he never tried broccoli again.

One day, Rocky the pony was feeling bored. He wanted to find something to do, but he had no ideas. Just then, he heard a noise above him. It sounded like something zipping around in the sky! Rocky looked up and saw an amazing airplane zipping around in the sky. He watched it fly around in circles and zig zags with awe. Rocky really wanted to fly, just like the airplane. He was feeling adventurous and wanted to try something new. So, with a big jump, Rocky jumped and started zipping around in the sky! He flew high, low and in circles and zig zags, like the airplane. It was amazing! After a while, Rocky felt tired and decided to come back down to the ground. He was so happy with the experience and felt super proud when his best friends asked him what he did. Rocky told them all about his amazing flying adventure and showed them his new moves! They all had a good time and Rocky couldn't wait to do it again.

Once upon a time, there was a big castle. In the castle, there lived a king and a queen. They had a little boy named Tim. Tim was not very smart. People in the castle would say, "Tim is stupid." One day, a wise old man came to the castle. He wanted to talk to the king and queen. He said, "I can help Tim be smart." The king and queen were very happy. They said, "Thank you, wise old man." The wise old man gave Tim a magic book. He said, "Read this book every day, and you will be smart." Tim read the book every day. He became very smart. Tim said, "Thank you, wise old man, for the magic book." One day, a big dragon came to the castle. The dragon wanted to take the castle. Tim used his smart brain to make a plan. He gave the dragon a big cake. The dragon ate the cake and became very sleepy. The dragon fell asleep, and Tim saved the castle. The moral of the story is to always say "thank you" and never call someone "stupid." You never know what they can do when they try hard and have help.
