Title: Tim the Boy and the Mattress

One day, a boy named Tim went to a store with his mom. They wanted to buy a new mattress. Tim's mom said, "Let's buy the popular mattress, everyone likes it." As they were talking, a big dog came into the store. The dog jumped on the popular mattress and started to sleep. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They did not know what to do. Tim said, "Let's buy the popular mattress with the dog on it. He looks happy and we can be friends." His mom agreed and they bought the mattress with the dog. Tim and the dog became best friends and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown bunny. He was hopping along, looking for food. Then he came across a cherry tree. He was so excited, he started to bounce around! He quickly began to bite into the cherries. They were so juicy and delicious! When he finished eating all the cherries in sight, he had a great idea. He decided to bury some cherries for later. The bunny dug a hole underneath the cherry tree and carefully placed several cherries inside, one by one. He filled up the hole with dirt and said, "Now I can come back when I'm hungry and get some yummy cherries!" He hopped off happily, leaving the cherry tree behind. He knew that soon he would have a yummy treat whenever he wanted. The End.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a charming little house. In front of the house, there was a mailbox. A girl named Lucy lived in the house with her mom and dad. One day, Lucy's mom said, "Lucy, I hope you get a nice gift for your birthday." Lucy was very happy and hoped for a gift too. She went to the mailbox every day to see if her gift was there. One morning, Lucy saw a big box in the mailbox. She was very excited and took the box inside. Lucy opened the box and found a beautiful doll. She was so happy and played with her new doll all day long. And so, Lucy's hope for a birthday gift came true.

Once upon a time, there was a slow cat named Mimi. Mimi liked to play with her mitten. One day, she was playing with her mitten when it got lost. Mimi was sad and looked for the mitten everywhere. Mimi asked her friend, a fast dog named Max, to help her find the mitten. Max said, "Yes!" They looked and looked, but they could not find the mitten. They were very tired, so they sat down to rest. While they were sitting, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird had Mimi's mitten in its beak! The bird gave the mitten back to Mimi. Mimi and Max were very happy. They learned that sometimes, when you yield and take a break, good things can happen.

One day, a big bird with thick feathers was in the woods. She liked to sing all day. The bird had a friend, a little mouse. The mouse had a small house in the woods. The bird liked to visit the mouse. One day, the bird went to the mouse's house. The mouse said, "I can't find my food. I left it near the big tree." The bird said, "I will help you find it." So, they went to look for the food together. They looked left and right, but they could not find the food. Then, the bird started to sing. While she was singing, she saw the food under some leaves. The bird and the mouse were happy. They took the food back to the mouse's house and ate it together.

One day, a little fly was flying in the sky. The fly saw a light. The light was very bright and pretty. The fly wanted to go near the light. So, the fly flew close to the light. The light was in a house. The fly went into the house to see the light better. In the house, the fly met a big, friendly dog. The dog was happy to see the fly. The dog asked the fly a question, "Why are you here, little fly?" The fly told the dog about the light. The dog laughed and said, "That light is not special, little fly. It is just a light in the house." The fly was sad. But then, the dog had an idea. The dog took the fly outside and showed the fly the real special light. It was the sun! The fly was very happy and they both played in the sun all day.
