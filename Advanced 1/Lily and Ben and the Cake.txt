Title: Lily and Ben and the Cake

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play with clay. They had many colors of clay and they made different shapes with them. Sometimes they made animals, sometimes they made cars, sometimes they made flowers. One day, they decided to make something new. They wanted to make a big cake with clay. They used brown clay for the cake, white clay for the cream, and yellow clay for the butter. They put the clay in a big pan and pressed it hard. Then they used a knife to cut the cake into pieces. They were very creative and they decorated the cake with more clay. They made red cherries, green leaves, blue candles, and pink hearts. They put them on top of the cake and made it look very pretty. They were very happy with their cake. They wanted to share their cake with their friends. They took the pan and went outside. They saw their friends playing in the yard. They called them and showed them their cake. Their friends were amazed and said it looked very yummy. They asked Lily and Ben if they could have some. Lily and Ben nodded and smiled. They gave each friend a piece of cake and a fork. They all sat together and pretended to eat the cake. They said it was very delicious and thanked Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben were glad that their cake could unite their friends. They felt very proud and happy. They all played together and had a lot of fun. They agreed that clay was a wonderful thing to play with.

One day, a boy named Tim went to help his mom unpack the big box. Inside the box, there was a bag of sugar. Tim's mom said, "Be careful with the sugar, Tim!" Tim wanted to be successful and make his mom happy, so he tried to carry the sugar to the kitchen. But on the way, he saw his cat, Mimi. Mimi was playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play too, so he put the sugar down and played with Mimi. After playing, Tim forgot about the sugar. He didn't take it to the kitchen. Later, his mom found the sugar on the floor. She was sad and said, "Oh no! The sugar is all over the floor!" Tim felt bad because he didn't finish his job. The day didn't have a happy ending.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Ella. They were always looking for adventures. One day, Jack and Ella decided to find some special powder. They searched throughout the village and eventually found an old lady who said she had a powder that the two could gather. The old lady told them the powder was soft and sparkly. Jack and Ella were very excited. They said “Thank you!” to the old lady and then headed off to gather the powder. Jack and Ella searched for hours, until they eventually found a special, hidden spot that was full of the soft, sparkly powder. Jack smiled at Ella and said, “Let’s take some.” The two friends gathered the powder and filled their bags until they were full. Then they raced home to show their families the powder. Jack and Ella were so proud that they had gone on an adventure and found the soft, sparkly powder. They both laughed and said, “We should gather the powder again!” And so they did.

One day, a cool cat named Tom went to the park. Tom saw a hot dog stand and wanted to buy a hot dog. But he saw that the hot dog stand was broken. Tom wanted to restore it, so he asked his friend, Sam the dog, to help him. Sam said, "I can help you restore the hot dog stand, but we need to find some wood and nails." They looked around and found what they needed. They started to fix the hot dog stand. As they were fixing the hot dog stand, a big, mean bird came and started to fight with Tom and Sam. The bird said, "This is my park! Go away!" Tom and Sam tried to make the bird go away, but the bird was too strong. In the end, the bird broke the hot dog stand again. Tom and Sam were very sad. They couldn't restore the hot dog stand and they couldn't have a hot dog. The bird had won, and Tom and Sam left the park with a bad ending to their day.
