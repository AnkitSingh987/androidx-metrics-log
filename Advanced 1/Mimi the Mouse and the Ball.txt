Title: Mimi the Mouse and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi was strong and liked to run and play. One day, Mimi found a big maze. She wanted to learn how to get through it. Mimi's friend, Tom the cat, said, "I can help you learn the maze, but first you have to find a big red ball." Mimi went to look for the big red ball. She knew it was important for the maze. After a long search, Mimi found the big red ball. She brought it back to Tom. Together, they went into the maze. They used the ball to help them find their way. When they reached the end, they were very happy. Mimi had learned the maze, and she knew she could do it again.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw an ordinary butterfly. She felt excited because she loved butterflies. She wanted to follow the butterfly and see where it went. Amy thought that maybe, the butterfly would lead her to something fun. The butterfly flew around and Amy followed it. They went through the park and the butterfly stopped at a big tree. Amy looked up and saw something shiny in the tree. She thought it must be something special since the butterfly led her there. Amy climbed the tree and found a pretty, shiny box. She opened the box and inside was a beautiful dress. It was the perfect size for her! She put it on and felt like a princess. The ordinary butterfly had led her to something magical. From that day on, Amy knew that even ordinary things can lead to exciting surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a fast cat named Tom. Tom liked to run and jump all day. He lived in a small house with his friend, Sam. One day, a thief came to their house. The thief wanted to take their toys. Tom and Sam did not like this. They wanted to stop the thief. Tom had an idea. He told Sam to lift a big box. They put the box near the door. When the thief tried to run away, he fell into the box. Tom and Sam caught the thief. They got their toys back and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big alligator. He lived in a pond. The pond was empty. The alligator was sad. One day, a boy came to the pond. He saw the alligator. The boy had a big bag. The bag was full of water. The boy wanted to help the alligator. He started to carry the water to the pond. The alligator was happy. He started to play with the boy. They played all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The alligator could talk! He said, "Thank you for the water, my friend!" The boy was surprised but happy. They became best friends and played together every day.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. There is a big oak tree in the park. The oak tree has many leaves and acorns. One day, Lila and Ben find a bird's nest in the oak tree. The nest has three eggs. Lila and Ben are curious. They want to see the eggs. But they are polite. They do not touch the nest. They only look. They hear a sound. It is the bird. The bird is the mother of the eggs. She is flying back to the nest. She sees Lila and Ben. She is scared. She thinks they want to hurt her eggs. She calls loudly. She says, "Go away! Go away!" Lila and Ben are scared too. They do not want to make the bird angry. They say, "Sorry, bird. We do not want to hurt your eggs. We only want to look. We are polite." The bird hears them. She sees that they are not bad. She is not angry anymore. She is calm. She calls softly. She says, "Thank you, Lila and Ben. You are polite. You can look at my eggs. But please be careful. They are very precious." Lila and Ben are happy. They say, "Thank you, bird. Your eggs are very pretty. We will be careful. We are your friends." The bird is happy too. She says, "You are welcome, Lila and Ben. You are very kind. You are my friends too."
