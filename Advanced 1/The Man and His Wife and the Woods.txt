Title: The Man and His Wife and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near the woods. The man was big and strong, and the wife was kind and pretty. They were very happy together. One cold day, the man went outside to get some food. He saw a tree with sour apples. The man picked some apples for his wife. As he picked the apples, he started to shiver. It was so cold! The man said, "Brrr, I need to go back home." The man went back to the house with the sour apples. He gave them to his wife. She said, "Thank you!" They ate the sour apples and laughed. The man stopped shivering because he was warm and happy with his wife. They lived happily ever after.

Once there was a bunny named Patch. He loved to jump around the garden and explore. One day, he noticed a patch of carrots in the garden. They looked very tasty, so he decided to sneak up and take one. Just as he reached out to get the carrot, he was stopped in his tracks by a fierce growl! He turned around to see a big, scary dog staring at him. Patch was so scared he didn't want to move. He just stood there, staring back at the dog. The dog growled and barked again, it seemed very mad! Patch didn't know what to do, so he just kept staring. Then, he noticed something useful lying nearby. It was a big stick! Patch was so relieved! He quickly grabbed the stick and held it up in the air. He waved the stick at the dog, who backed away and stopped growling. Patch was so happy to have found such a useful stick. And now he was safe from the dog. He quickly ran off with the carrot back to his burrow.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a clever rabbit. He had many friends, but his best friend was a big bear. They liked to play games and have fun together. One day, they decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. They ran and ran, and the rabbit was very fast. But the bear was strong and did not give up. Near the end of the race, the rabbit saw a tree with a big branch. He had a clever idea. He jumped and grabbed the branch. He began to shake it with all his might. The bear was surprised by the shaking tree and stopped to look. The clever rabbit used this chance to run as fast as he could to the finish line. He won the race and felt very happy. His friends cheered for his victory. The bear laughed and said, "You are very clever, my friend!" They all played and had fun together, and the clever rabbit and the big bear stayed best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to study the world around him. He swam in the water every day, looking and learning. One day, Fin saw a hook with food on it. He was very hungry, but he remembered his mom saying, "Don't eat from hooks." Fin was tired, but he swam away from the hook. He told his friend, Sam the Seahorse, about the hook. Sam said, "That was smart, Fin. Hooks are bad." The next day, Fin and Sam saw a big fish named Max. Max was eating from the hook. Fin and Sam tried to stop him, but it was too late. The hook took Max away. Fin and Sam were sad, but they learned a lesson. They knew to always listen to their mom and stay away from hooks.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Amy. She loved to go to the market with her mama. They would buy pieces of fruit and colourful vegetables. One day, when they were walking back from the market, Amy tripped and fell. She shouted, "Mama, I remain hurt!" Mama said, "Amy, it's ok, you can get up. It wasn't a very big fall." Amy replied, "Yes, but it still hurt. Why are the steps so stupid?!" Mama said, "It's hard to say, Amy. But why don't you remain careful and watch where you are walking from now on." Amy nodded and the pair continued on their way back home. From then on, Amy knew to always remain very careful while walking at the market.
