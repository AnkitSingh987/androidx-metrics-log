Title: The Story of the Fish and the Shrimp

One day, a little shrimp was swimming in the sea. He was a serious shrimp who did not like to play. He only liked to work and clean his home. All the other sea animals liked to play, but not the serious shrimp. A silly fish came to the serious shrimp and said, "Come play with us, shrimp! We are having so much fun!" The serious shrimp looked at the silly fish and said, "No, I refuse to play. I have work to do." The silly fish and the other sea animals tried to make the serious shrimp play, but he refused. They played games, sang songs, and danced in the water, but the serious shrimp did not join them. He kept working and cleaning his home. In the end, the sea animals learned that the serious shrimp was happy in his own way. They played together, and the serious shrimp worked. They all lived happily in the sea.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Josh and Steve. They were looking forward to a special day out. Josh said to Steve: "let's go and get some waffles!" Steve happily replied "yes!" So they set off on their high adventure to find the waffles. It was a long walk, but soon enough they arrived at the waffle shop. Josh and Steve looked up at the window and saw piles of delicious waffles. They went inside and started to choose their favourite waffle. Josh picked a round one and said, "let's share it". Steve agreed. So the boys got a plate, split their waffle in half and started to part it between them. When the waffle was all gone, Josh and Steve grinned happily. They had enjoyed eating their waffle together. Best friends forever!

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Max. Max liked to sniff and dig in the yard. One sunny day, Max found a big bowl in the grass. The bowl was very pretty and shiny. Max wanted to play with the bowl, but he saw something sharp inside. It was a pointy stick! Max did not want to get hurt, so he barked for help. His friend, a fluffy cat named Luna, came running to see what was wrong. Luna saw the sharp stick in the bowl and used her paw to carefully take it out. Now, Max and Luna could play with the bowl safely. They took turns rolling the bowl and chasing after it. They played all day and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time in a lovely little town, there was a big billboard. On the billboard was a picture of a funny dog. Everyone who saw the billboard would smile and laugh. One day, a small bird named Tim was flying near the billboard. Tim saw the funny dog and wanted to talk to it. He landed on the billboard and said, "Hello, funny dog! My name is Tim. What's your name?" The funny dog on the billboard did not say anything because it was just a picture. Tim did not know this, so he started to panic. "Why won't the funny dog talk to me?" he thought. Tim's friend, a kind squirrel named Sally, saw Tim on the billboard. She climbed up and said, "Don't panic, Tim! That's not a real dog. It's just a picture on the billboard to make people smile." Tim felt better and said, "Thank you, Sally. I'm glad you're my friend." Tim and Sally left the billboard together and played in the lovely town. They laughed and had fun, knowing that the funny dog on the billboard was there to make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a red sweater. She loved her red sweater very much. Sue's best friend, Tim, did not have a sweater like Sue's. Tim was jealous. He wanted a red sweater too. One day, Sue and Tim saw a store. The store had sweaters that could match Sue's red sweater. Tim was happy. Sue and Tim went into the store to find a red sweater for Tim. They looked and looked, but they could not find a red sweater in Tim's size. Tim was sad. Then, Sue had an idea. She asked the store lady if there was a red sweater for Tim in the back. The store lady went to look. She came back with a red sweater just like Sue's, but in Tim's size. Tim was very happy. Now, Sue and Tim had matching red sweaters, and they were the best of friends.
