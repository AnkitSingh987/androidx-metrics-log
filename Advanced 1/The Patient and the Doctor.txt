Title: The Patient and the Doctor

Once upon a time, there was a patient who was very scared. A friendly doctor saw this and wanted to give the patient some help. The doctor asked, "Are you scared?" The patient replied, "Yes, I'm very scared." The doctor smiled and said, "Don't be scared, I'm here to give you some help." He gave the patient a hug and said, "Don't worry, everything will be alright." The patient felt a lot better and was very happy. The doctor then gave the patient some medicine to help make her feel better. She smiled and thanked the doctor for being so kind. She knew she was in good hands and was no longer scared. The doctor smiled again and said, "I'm always here to help. Don't forget that." The patient was so grateful and thanked the doctor again, before going on her way.

Lila and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and water the plants. Lila saw a red tomato on a vine. She wanted to pick it and eat it. "Look, Ben, a tomato!" she said. "Can I have it?" Ben shook his head. "No, Lila, that is not a tomato. That is a mighty tomato. It can become anything it wants. It can become a ball, or a hat, or a rocket." Lila did not believe him. She reached for the tomato. But as soon as she touched it, it became a ball. It bounced out of her hand and rolled away. "Wow!" Lila said. "How did it do that?" Ben smiled. "I told you, it is a mighty tomato. It can change its shape. Let's chase it and see what else it can become." Lila and Ben ran after the ball. They had fun playing with the mighty tomato. It became a hat, a rocket, a flower, and a star. They laughed and clapped their hands. They did not want to stop. They did not know that the mighty tomato was really a magic tomato. And that it had a secret.

Lila was a gifted girl who liked to draw and sing. She had long, curly hair that she combed every day. She wanted to look nice for her friends and family. One day, she was getting ready for school and she could not find her comb. She looked under her bed, in her closet, and in her drawers. She was very sad and worried. She did not want to go to school with messy hair. She asked her mom, "Mom, where is my comb? I need it to fix my hair." Her mom smiled and said, "Lila, I have a surprise for you. Come with me to the living room." Lila followed her mom and saw a big, shiny box on the table. Her mom said, "This is a present for you, my gifted girl. You have been working hard and being kind. I am very proud of you." Lila opened the box and saw a new comb. It was pink and sparkly, with flowers and stars on it. It was the most beautiful comb she had ever seen. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, mom. This is the best gift ever. I love you and I love my new comb." She ran to the mirror and combed her hair with her new comb. She felt happy and confident. She was ready to go to school and share her gift with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a popular dog named Spot. Spot had a bone that he loved very much. He would carry his bone with him everywhere he went. All the other dogs wanted to have a bone like Spot's, but he would not share it. One day, Spot met another dog named Fluffy. Fluffy was nice and wanted to be friends with Spot. Fluffy said, "I trust you, Spot. Can we play together?" Spot thought about it and said, "Yes, we can be friends and play together, but I will not share my bone." As they played, something unexpected happened. Spot's bone fell into a hole and he could not get it out. Spot was sad and did not know what to do. Fluffy saw how sad Spot was and said, "Don't worry, Spot. I will help you get your bone back." Fluffy dug and dug until he got the bone out of the hole. Spot was so happy and thanked Fluffy. From that day on, Spot learned to trust and share with his friends. He even shared his bone with Fluffy. Now, Spot and Fluffy were the best of friends and played together every day.
