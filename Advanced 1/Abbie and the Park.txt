Title: Abbie and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Little Abbie who was 3 years old. Little Abbie loved to play outside and often went to the park by herself. One day, when Little Abbie was walking in the park, it suddenly started to rain very heavily. Little Abbie was cold and started to shiver. She was scared and wanted to find somewhere safe. Little Abbie saw a big tree with lots of branches and leaves. It looked safe and cozy, so she ran towards it to hide. Little Abbie held onto one of the branches tightly with her tiny muscles and stopped shivering. Little Abbie felt safe under the big tree and she closed her eyes. After a while, the rain stopped and the sun came out. Little Abbie opened her eyes and was happy to feel warm and safe again. She smiled and then ran off to play in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, Kitty was playing and got very filthy. Her fur was full of dirt and knots. Kitty didn't like the knots in her fur, but she loved playing in the dirt. Kitty saw her friend, Doggy, and wanted to play with him. Doggy liked to stretch his long legs and run very fast. Kitty tried to run with Doggy, but the knots in her fur made it hard for her to move. Doggy saw that Kitty was not happy and wanted to help her. Doggy had an idea. He took Kitty to the water and helped her wash the dirt out of her fur. The knots became loose, and Kitty could stretch and play with Doggy again. They had lots of fun running and playing together. Kitty was happy that her friend helped her, and she learned that it's important to stay clean.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very nervous. He wanted to play outside, but the big gate in front of his house made him scared. One day, Max decided to be brave and go outside. As Max walked up to the gate, he saw a big, friendly cat. The cat said, "Hi, Max! My name is Kitty. Let's play together!" Max blinked and thought, "A cat wants to play with me? That's unexpected!" Max and Kitty played together all day. At the end of the day, Max was not nervous anymore. He learned that the big gate was not scary. The gate was just a way to find new friends like Kitty. And from that day on, Max and Kitty played together every day, and they were the best of friends.

Once there were two kids, Sam and Joe. Sam was 3 years old and Joe was 5. One day Sam and Joe went out to the park to play. As they played, they saw a huge cop walking down the street. Sam was very excited to see the cop and asked Joe, "What does that man do?" Joe explained, "He's a cop. He helps people be safe by making sure everyone follows the number rules." Sam had never heard of number rules before so he asked what they were. Joe told him all about them. Number one was to listen to mom and dad. Number two was to always tell the truth. Number three was to be kind to others. Finally Joe asked Sam if he understood the number rules. Sam nodded his head and said, "I understand." Joe smiled and said, "Good job, Sam!" Then they went back to playing and Sam felt happy and safe knowing the huge cop was out there looking out for him and his number rules.

One day, Tom found a toy pistol. He thought it was fun to play with. He ran around the yard, making silly noises and pretending to be a cowboy. He was having a lot of fun. Tom's sister, Sue, was in the yard too. She was folding her clothes. She saw Tom with the toy pistol and wanted to play. But Tom did not want to share. He kept running away from Sue. This made Sue sad. Sue had an idea. She took a shirt and folded it like a toy pistol. Now, Sue had her own toy to play with. Tom saw Sue's toy and thought it was cool. He asked if he could play with it. Sue said yes, but only if they shared both toys. Tom agreed, and they played together, having a silly and fun time.
