Title: The Monster and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big monster. He lived in a cold cave. The monster was always worried. He did not want to freeze like ice. One day, the sun was shining. The monster was happy. He went outside to play. He saw a little girl. The girl was not scared of the monster. The girl and the monster played all day. They ran and jumped. They laughed and had fun. The monster was not worried anymore. He did not freeze because the sun was warm. The monster and the girl were friends forever.

One day, a boy named Tim went to mail a letter. He was pale because he was a little scared. It was his first time mailing a letter all by himself. He put the letter in the big blue box and went back home. The next day, Tim got a big box in the mail. It was a prize! He was very happy and excited. He opened the box and saw a big, soft teddy bear. He played with the teddy bear all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's little sister, Sue, thought the teddy bear was for her. She took it and ran away. Tim was sad, but then he saw another box. He opened it and found a new prize: a toy car! Now, both Tim and Sue had fun toys to play with, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a busy rabbit called John. John was very happy living in the forest, with all of his forest friends. One day, John went to the river to take a drink of water. Suddenly, a big net fell from the sky and captured John. He was so scared! The net took him away to a strange place, with many cages. In one of the cages, John saw a fox. The fox told him that he was captured by the hunters and was going to be released into the woods and hunted for sport. John was terrified! He wanted to get back home to his friends in the forest. But, all of his attempts to escape failed and the hunters took away his name tag. He thought he would never be able to return home. The hunters opened the cages to release the animals into the woods, but John wasn't so lucky. The hunters decided to take him home with them, as a trophy. John was so sad. He never got to go home. He had to spend the rest of his life in the hunters' home, far away from his friends in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she saw a big, scary bug in her room. She was so scared and ran to her mom. "Mommy, there is a big, scary bug in my room!" Amy cried. Her mom went to her room, and saw the big, scary bug. She used a cup to catch the bug, and then took it outside. The bug was gone, and Amy felt safe again. The next day, Amy went to play with her friend, Tim. Tim had a toy with a buckle on it. Amy liked the toy and asked, "Can I borrow your toy with the buckle, please?" Tim said, "Yes, you can borrow it, but please be careful." Amy was very happy and played with the toy all day. She was not scared of the big bug anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a red bicycle that she loved to ride. One sunny day, she went to the park to ride her bicycle. At the park, Mia saw her friend Tim. Tim had a soft, blue ball. "Hi, Mia!" said Tim. "Want to join me and play with my ball?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! Let me park my bicycle first." Mia and Tim played with the soft, blue ball all day. They laughed and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Mia rode her bicycle, and Tim walked beside her. They promised to play together again soon.
