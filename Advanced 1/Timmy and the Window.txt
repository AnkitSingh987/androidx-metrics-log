Title: Timmy and the Window

Timmy was sitting in his bedroom, playing with his toys. He was having lots of fun. Suddenly, he heard something outside. He stopped to listen. It was a distant sound and it seemed to be getting louder. He got off his bed and looked out of his window. To his surprise, he saw a man carrying a big sack walking down the street. Timmy quickly ran to get his Mom. "Mom! Mom!" he said. "There's a man carrying a sack!" Mom looked out the window and recognized the man. "Oh, that's just the mailman. He's come to deliver some letters." The mailman had a special bag he carried with all the mail, and it was called a sack. It was a big one, too! Timmy waved and smiled at the mailman as he went by. The mailman smiled back and waved, but he still seemed kind of distant. Timmy was so excited that he ran into his bedroom and got all the letters he had written to his family and friends. He ran back out to the street and waited until the mailman came by, and then he put all the letters in the sack. The mailman thanked Timmy and said goodbye. Timmy smiled and waved until the mailman was long gone. He was so proud of himself for recognizing the mailman and helping him deliver all the letters.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant who lived on a big farm. The ant liked to play in the hay. One day, the ant went to the hay and saw a tiny bug. The tiny bug was sad and sorry. The ant asked the bug, "Why are you sorry?" The bug said, "I was playing and I pinch my friend. I did not want to hurt my friend." The ant wanted to help the bug feel better. So, the ant and the bug went to find the bug's friend. When they found the friend, the sorry bug said, "I am sorry for the pinch." The friend said, "It is okay. We can all play in the hay together." And they all played in the hay, happy and friends.

One day, a kind boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. When he got to the top, he found a message in a bottle. Tim was very surprised. Tim opened the bottle and read the message. It said, "Yell three times, and something fun will happen." So, Tim yelled one, two, three times. Suddenly, a big slide came out of the tree. Tim was so happy. He slid down the slide and landed in a big pile of soft pillows. Tim thought the message was magic. He put the message back in the bottle and hid it under his bed. Now, whenever Tim wants to have fun, he yells three times and the slide appears.

Once upon a time, there was a big birdcage. Inside the birdcage, there was a tiny bird. The tiny bird was sad because it wanted to fly and play outside. Every day, the bird would pray to be free and happy. One day, a huge, friendly giant came to visit. The giant saw the tiny bird in the birdcage and felt sad too. The giant wanted to help the bird. So, he opened the door of the birdcage and let the tiny bird out. The tiny bird was so happy to be free! But then, something unexpected happened. The tiny bird started to grow and grow! It became a huge bird, even bigger than the giant! The giant and the bird became best friends, and they played together every day. The bird was happy and free, all because of the giant's help.

Once there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big toy box. In her toy box, she had a lot of toys. Mia loved her toys a lot. She knew that all the toys belonged to her. One day, Mia's mommy and daddy took her to a camp. The camp was big and had a lot of fun things to do. Mia played with other kids at the camp. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. The sky was clear and blue, and the sun was shining brightly. At the camp, Mia saw a pretty toy. She wanted to take it home, but her mommy said, "No, Mia. That toy does not belong to you." Mia was sad but she understood. She played with her new friends and had a great time at the camp.
