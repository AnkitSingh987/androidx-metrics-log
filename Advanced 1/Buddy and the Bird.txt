Title: Buddy and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to help his friends. One sunny day, Buddy saw a little bird who was very sad. The little bird told Buddy that she lost her seat in her nest. Buddy wanted to help, so he said, "Let me find your seat for you." The little bird was happy and thanked Buddy. Buddy looked high and low. Soon, he found the seat on the ground. He picked it up and gave it to the little bird. The little bird put her seat back in her nest and was happy again. Buddy felt good because he was able to help his friend.

Lily was a girl who liked to learn new things. She had a tutor who came to her house every week to teach her letters, numbers, and colors. Lily liked her tutor, who was kind and patient with her. One day, Lily saw a big book on the tutor's bag. She asked, "What is that?" The tutor said, "This is a book of stories. It has many words and pictures. Do you want to see?" Lily nodded. She loved stories. The tutor opened the book and showed her a page. It had a picture of a king with a crown and a sword. He looked powerful and angry. The tutor said, "This is a story about a king who wanted to own everything. He was greedy and mean. He took away the lands and the things of other people. He made them work hard and pay him taxes. He did not care about anyone but himself." Lily frowned. She did not like the king. She said, "That is not nice. He should share and be kind. Why did he do that?" The tutor said, "He did that because he thought he was better than everyone else. He thought he could do whatever he wanted. He did not listen to anyone who tried to tell him he was wrong. He was foolish and proud." Lily asked, "What happened to him?" The tutor said, "One day, he made a big mistake. He tried to take away the land of a powerful wizard. The wizard was angry and used his magic to turn the king into a frog. He said, 'Now you will learn how it feels to be small and weak. You will not own anything anymore. You will have to live in a pond and eat flies. Maybe then you will be humble and grateful.'" Lily gasped. She felt sorry for the king. She said, "That is sad. Did he ever change back?" The tutor said, "I don't know. The story does not say. Maybe he did, maybe he did not. Maybe he learned his lesson, maybe he did not. What do you think?" Lily thought for a moment. She said, "I think he should change back. But only if he says sorry and promises to be good. And he should give back the things he took and help the people he hurt. And he should be nice to the wizard and the frog." The tutor smiled. He said, "That is a good answer, Lily. You are very wise and kind. You know that owning things is not as important as being good. And you know that being powerful is not as good as being humble. And you know that being mean is not as good as being nice. You have learned a lot today." Lily smiled too. She said, "Thank you, tutor. You are a good teacher. Can we read more stories next time?" The tutor said, "Of course, Lily. I have many more stories to share with you. Stories can teach us many things. They can make us laugh and cry and wonder and dream. They can make us better people. I am glad you like stories." Lily hugged the tutor. She said, "I love stories. And I love you, tutor." The tutor hugged her back. He said, "I love you too, Lily."

Once there was a girl, who was very persistent. She wanted to try an onion, even though her mom said it was too spicy for her. One day the girl saw an onion in the grocery store. She asked her mom if she could have it. Her mom refused but the girl refused to take no for an answer. "Please mom, can I try the onion?" Her mom hesitated, but could see the girl's persistence. She said "Alright, but if you don’t like it you don’t have to eat it". The girl took the onion home. She asked her mom for help peeling and cutting it. "It will be easier for you to eat if I cut it into small pieces", said her mom. The girl was very excited and started eating the pieces of onion, one by one. At first, the girl found it very spicy, but she kept eating and eventually, the spicy became a taste she liked. She ate the whole onion, piece by piece! Her mom was very proud- she was so persistent!
