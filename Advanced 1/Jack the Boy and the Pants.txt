Title: Jack the Boy and the Pants

Once upon a time there was a big boy named Jack. Jack liked to carry things. He had a bag that was blue. One day, he was feeling extra brave. He brought his bag outside to the backyard. He saw some big pants lying on the ground. He wanted to carry the pants, so he put the bag down and picked up the pants. He was surprised at how heavy it was! He held the pants in both of his hands and began to carry them back inside. When Jack walked into the house his mom was very surprised. She asked why he was carrying the big pants. Jack said he wanted to put them on. So his mom helped him try them on. They were a bit too big, but Jack was happy to wear them. That day, Jack showed everyone that he was brave and could carry big things. He was very proud of himself!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play all day. He did not like to take a nap. His mom would always encourage him to take a nap, but he did not want to. One day, Tim was playing near a big, dangerous hill. His mom told him to be careful. Tim did not listen and he kept playing. He was very tired but did not want to take a nap. Suddenly, Tim fell down the dangerous hill. His mom was very scared. She ran to help him. Tim was okay, but he learned his lesson. From that day on, he always took a nap when his mom encouraged him to.

Once, there was a mom and dad who wanted to have a picnic. They asked their three year old daughter if she would like to join them. She smiled and said, "Yes, please!" The mom and dad began to add all the things they needed to their picnic. They placed down a soft blanket and put some yummy food into a basket. The three year old daughter watched her mom and dad adding delicious things to their picnic. It made her very excited. The mom and dad noticed this and asked the little girl if she had something to add. She excitedly said, "Yes! I'll add some silly games to make it even more fun!" The mom and dad smiled and said, "That's a great idea! We'll make it an easy picnic". The child giggled and the family began their picnic. They laughed and enjoyed it very much and added even more fun things as they went.

Joey was very excited. He was getting a new tent. He had asked his parents for one for months and now it was finally his. He could not wait to play with it. He set it up in the backyard and looked around for the perfect spot for it. When he saw the perfect spot, he quickly finished setting up the tent. He was so excited that he started playing in it right away. He dreamed of camping trips he would take with his family. But then, a strong wind started blowing and Joey's new tent started shaking. Before Joey knew it, the tent blew away, and he could not find it again. Joey was sad. He was so disappointed that his new tent was gone. He had been so excited to play with it, but now it was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little lizard named Liz. Liz was a fine lizard with a long tail and many friends. She lived in a big tree with her family. One day, Liz saw a butterfly and wanted to play with it. Liz said to the butterfly, "Can I play with you?" The butterfly said, "Yes, but you must change like me." Liz did not know how to change, but she tried very hard. She closed her eyes and thought about being a butterfly. When Liz opened her eyes, she saw that she had changed! She had big, pretty wings just like the butterfly. Liz was very happy. She played with the butterfly all day long. When it was time to go home, Liz changed back into a fine lizard and went back to her tree.
