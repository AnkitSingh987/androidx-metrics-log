Title: Lily and the Coat

Lily was a curious girl who liked to explore new things. One day, she found a big zipper on her mom's coat. She wanted to see what was inside the coat, so she pulled the zipper down. But the zipper got stuck halfway and she could not move it anymore. "Mom, mom, help me!" Lily cried. "The zipper is broken!" Mom heard Lily's voice and came to see what was wrong. She saw Lily holding the zipper and trying to pull it up and down. "Oh, Lily, you should not play with the zipper. It is not a toy. It is for keeping the coat closed," Mom said. "But I was curious. I wanted to see what was inside the coat," Lily said. Mom smiled and hugged Lily. "I know you are curious, but sometimes you have to be careful. The zipper can hurt you or the coat. Let me help you fix it." Mom took the zipper and gently pushed it up and down until it was smooth again. She zipped up the coat and showed it to Lily. "See? The zipper is working now. It is a good zipper. It supports the coat and keeps it warm and cozy," Mom said. Lily nodded and touched the zipper. She felt sorry for breaking it. "I am sorry, Mom. I will not play with the zipper again. I will find other things to be curious about," Lily said. Mom kissed Lily and said, "That's okay, Lily. I love you and I support you. You can be curious about many things, but you have to ask me first. Then I can show you and teach you." Lily smiled and said, "Okay, Mom. Thank you for helping me and teaching me. I love you too." They hugged and went to find something else to be curious about together.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Fuzzy. Fuzzy lived in a small house with his friend, a girl named Lily. They liked to play in the yard and run around. One day, Fuzzy saw a big row of flowers in the yard. Fuzzy thought the flowers looked fun to play with, so he ran through the row. He knocked down all the pretty flowers. Lily saw what Fuzzy did and was sad. She loved the flowers and wanted them to be pretty again. Fuzzy felt bad for making Lily sad. Fuzzy wanted to restore the flowers and make Lily happy. He dug a hole and put the flowers back in the ground. He gave them water and made sure they had sun. Soon, the flowers were pretty again, and Lily was happy. Fuzzy learned to be careful and not hurt the things his friend loved.

Linda was playing in the park with her friends. They were having a great time playing tag. Suddenly, Linda's mum called for her. She was upset. Linda went to her mum. Linda's mum said, "You need to go to the store and buy some apples." Linda was upset, but she knew her mum was serious. Linda asked, "Do I need to bring money too?" Her mum answered, "Yes, you need to bring one penny." Linda demanded to know why she had to bring money. She didn't understand why she needed a penny. "The shopkeeper won't give you the apples for free," her mum explained. "You need to give him one penny." Linda was still upset, but she nodded her head yes and went off to the store. She found the store easily and was surprised to see that the shopkeeper gave her two apples! She was so happy. Linda proudly brought the apples back to her mum and thanked her for the penny. Her mum smiled and said "You're welcome."

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little light named True. True loved to shine so bright. All the animals in the forest liked True. True made their nights happy and fun. One day, a big cloud came to the forest. The cloud was jealous of True. The cloud did not like how much the animals liked True. The cloud wanted to be liked too. The cloud made a plan to stop True from shining. The cloud hid the sun so it was dark all day. The animals were sad and missed True's shine. True tried to shine, but the cloud was too big and dark. The animals could not see True's light. The cloud was happy it stopped True from shining. But the animals did not like the cloud. They were still sad and missed True. The cloud did not get what it wanted. The forest stayed dark and the animals stayed sad.
