Title: Will the Boy and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Will who lived in a big city. He didn't know what to do in all the tall buildings and busy streets. One day, he asked his mother, "Can I visit my friends?" His mother replied, "Yes, Will. But please walk there. It's too far to drive." Will was so excited - he had never been to his friends' houses by himself before. Will got ready to go. He put on his yellow coat, his shoes, and his socks. He was ready to go, but then he remembered - he had forgotten something important. He asked his mom, "Do I know the way?" His mom replied, "No, Will. You don't know the way. I'm sorry, but you can't go by yourself. I'll take you there." Will was a little disappointed, but he felt better when his mom answered. He said, "OK, mom. Thank you for always being there for me." Will hugged his mom goodbye, and they went off on the visit together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a bike with a broken pedal. Tim was sad because he wanted to ride his bike, but he couldn't. His dad saw him and said, "Don't worry, we will restore your bike." Tim and his dad went to the store to buy a new pedal. They found a cheap pedal that was perfect for Tim's bike. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, dad!" They went back home and fixed the bike. Tim's dad put the new pedal on the bike. Now, Tim could ride his bike again. He rode around the yard, laughing and having fun. Tim and his dad spent the whole day riding bikes together.

Ben and Lily were playing with a puzzle. It was a big puzzle with many pieces. They wanted to make a picture of a farm with animals and a tractor. "Look, I found the cow!" Lily said. She put the piece in the right place. "Good job, Lily!" Ben said. He looked for another piece. He saw a piece with a scale on it. He picked it up. "What is this?" he asked. "That is a scale. It can weigh things. You can see how heavy or light they are," Lily said. Ben was curious. He wanted to weigh the puzzle pieces. He put one piece on the scale. Then he put another piece on the other side. "See, this piece is heavier than this piece," he said. Lily was not happy. She wanted to finish the puzzle, not play with the scale. "Ben, stop that! You are messing up the puzzle. We need those pieces," she said. Ben was stubborn. He did not listen to Lily. He kept weighing the pieces. He thought it was fun. "No, I want to weigh them. It is my turn. You can do the puzzle later," he said. Lily was angry. She tried to take the pieces from Ben. Ben did not let go. They started to pull and tug. The pieces fell on the floor. The scale broke. The puzzle was ruined. They both started to cry. They were sad and sorry. They wished they had shared and played nicely.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play in the house. His favorite place to play was a small chamber under the stairs. One day, Fluffy found a shiny ball in the chamber. The shiny ball was very special. Fluffy played with the shiny ball all day. He rolled it, he chased it, and he even tried to eat it. But Fluffy didn't know that the shiny ball had magic. The magic ball could make things vanish. Fluffy's mom saw him playing with the ball and knew it was magic. She worried that Fluffy might make something vanish by accident. The next day, Fluffy was playing with the shiny ball in the chamber again. Suddenly, the ball made a bright flash of light. When Fluffy opened his eyes, the shiny ball was gone. It had vanished! Fluffy was sad, but his mom was happy. She knew the magic ball could be dangerous. Now, Fluffy could play safely in the chamber without making anything vanish. And Fluffy learned to have fun with his other toys, even if they didn't have magic.
