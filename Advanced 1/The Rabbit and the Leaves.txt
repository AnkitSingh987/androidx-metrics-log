Title: The Rabbit and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a happy rabbit. He lived in a big green field. The rabbit loved to wander and play all day. One day, the happy rabbit wandered far from his home. He saw a big tree with tasty leaves. The rabbit ate the leaves and felt very full. Soon, the happy rabbit found his way back home. He played with his friends in the big green field. They all laughed and had fun. The happy rabbit loved to wander and play with his friends every day.

Once upon a time, in a fine little town, there was a street. On this street, there were many cute houses with pretty flowers. The people living there were very nice. One day, a kind girl named Lucy wanted to show her new friend, Tim, her favorite street. They held hands and walked together. They saw the beautiful flowers and waved to the happy people. Tim liked the fine street too. He said, "Thank you for showing me, Lucy!" They played and laughed all day long. From that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a tall tree. The tall tree had many leaves. Every day, the sun would shine on the tall tree and make it feel happy. One day, a big wind came and blew many leaves away. The tall tree was sad. It wanted to find its leaves. It saw a small needle on the ground. The tall tree thought the needle could help. The tall tree asked the needle to help find the leaves. The needle said yes. They looked and looked, but they could not find the leaves. The sun went down and it was dark. The tall tree and the needle were very sad. They never found the leaves.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lily. She had a pretty coat that she always wore. One day, Lily went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree and thought it looked like fun to climb. As she climbed, she began to wonder what she might see from the top. When she got to the top, she looked around and saw a small bird. The bird said, "Hi, I'm Birdy. I like your coat!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Birdy! I like your feathers!" They became friends and talked for a while. Suddenly, the tree started to shake! Lily and Birdy were scared. Then, they saw a kind squirrel named Sam. Sam said, "Sorry for the shake! I was just playing a game." Lily and Birdy laughed, and they all played together. The unexpected shaking made Lily and Birdy find a new friend, and they all had a great day in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a powerful bird. This bird loved to sing a special song every day. All the other animals liked to listen to the bird's song. The bird felt happy when it sang. One day, a small cat came to the house. The cat wanted to play with the bird. The bird got scared and decided to hide from the cat. The bird hid in a big tree. The cat looked for the bird, but could not find it. The cat felt sad and left the house. The bird stayed hiding in the tree. The other animals missed the bird's song. They waited and waited, but the bird never sang again. The animals felt sad because they lost their friend and the beautiful song.

One day, a small girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big, red rose. Lily was very excite. She wanted to pick the rose and take it home. But, she was also a little fearful. She thought the rose might hurt her. Lily saw a boy named Tim near the rose. Tim was not fearful. He wanted to help Lily. Tim said, "I can pick the rose for you, Lily." Lily was happy and said, "Yes, please." Tim picked the rose and gave it to Lily. She was very excite and not fearful anymore. They both went home, holding the rose together. They had a fun day at the park.
