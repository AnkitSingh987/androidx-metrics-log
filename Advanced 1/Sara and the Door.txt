Title: Sara and the Door

Sara likes to help her mom in the kitchen. She likes to stir the soup and smell the bread. But she has to be careful, because the stove is very hot. Mom tells her to stay away from the fire and the pots. One day, Sara is helping mom make cookies. She wants to see the cookies in the oven. She climbs on a chair and opens the oven door. But she does not see the cookies. She sees a big flame. The flame is orange and red and scary. It jumps out of the oven and touches Sara's arm. Sara screams and drops the oven door. She feels a lot of pain in her arm. It hurts so much. She runs to mom and shows her the arm. Mom sees a red mark on Sara's arm. She says, "Oh no, you burned your arm. That is not good. We have to put some cold water on it." Mom takes Sara to the sink and runs cold water on her arm. She says, "This will make it feel better. It will stop the burn from getting worse. You are very brave, Sara. But you have to be more careful next time. The oven is not a toy. It is very dangerous. You could get hurt very badly." Sara nods and says, "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to see the cookies. I did not know the oven had fire. I will not do it again. I will listen to you and stay away from the stove. It is not easy to be a helper. It is hard." Mom hugs Sara and says, "I know, sweetie. I love you very much. And I love your help. But you have to be safe and smart. You have to wait for me to show you what to do. Then you can be a great helper. And you can have some cookies when they are ready. OK?" Sara smiles and says, "OK, mom. Thank you for making my arm feel better. And thank you for the cookies. They smell yummy. Can I have some milk too?" Mom says, "Of course, you can. Come on, let's go to the table. And be careful with the chair. It is not easy to climb either." Sara says, "OK, mom. I will be careful. I love you." Mom says, "I love you too, Sara."

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there lived a queen. The queen had a pretty garden with many flowers. She loved her flowers so much. One day, a boy came to the garden. He saw the flowers and thought they were very pretty. The boy wanted to take the flowers home, so he took a knife and cut them. He did not know that cutting the flowers was a stupid thing to do. The queen saw the boy cutting her flowers and became very sad. She asked the boy to leave her garden and never come back. The boy felt bad for what he did, but it was too late. The flowers were gone, and the queen's garden was not pretty anymore. The bad ending of this story teaches us that we should not do stupid things, like cutting flowers that do not belong to us. We should always think before we act, so we do not make others sad.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful garden. In the garden, there were many flowers. One day, a little girl named Mia and a little boy named Tom found the garden. Mia saw a pretty flower. She said, "Look, Tom! I found a flower." Tom looked at the flower and smiled. He said, "It's so colorful! Let's share it." So, Mia and Tom picked the flower together. They took turns holding the flower and smelling it. They were very happy to share the colorful flower. After they shared the flower, they played in the garden. They found more flowers and shared them, too. Mia and Tom had a fun day in the colorful garden, sharing flowers and playing together.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a bright toy dinosaur. He loved to play with it every day. One day, while playing with his dinosaur, he saw a big box in his room. Tim was very curious about what could be inside the box. Tim tried to open the box to reveal what was inside. But the box was too heavy for him to lift. He felt sad and wanted to know what was inside. He asked his mom for help, but she said it was a surprise for later. This made Tim even more curious. The next day, Tim tried again to open the box. This time, with the help of his bright toy dinosaur, he was able to lift the box. To his surprise, there was another big dinosaur toy inside! Tim was very happy and played with both his dinosaurs all day long. The surprise was worth the wait.
