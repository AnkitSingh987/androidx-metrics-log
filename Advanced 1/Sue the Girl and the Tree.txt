Title: Sue the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Sue found a hurt bird by a big tree. The bird had a hurt wing and could not fly. Sue wanted to heal the bird and make it feel better. She put the bird in a box and took it home. At home, Sue put the bird in a big trunk to keep it safe. She gave it food and water, and every day she checked if the bird was honest about its hurt wing. Sue loved the bird very much and hoped it would get better soon. One day, Sue opened the trunk to check on the bird. To her surprise, the bird was gone! Sue was sad, but then she saw something unexpected. The bird was flying around the room! It had been honest about its hurt wing, but now it was healed. Sue was so happy that her bird friend could fly again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy sack, a toy knife, and a brilliant red ball. He loved to play with his toys all day long. One day, Tim took his sack and put his toy knife and brilliant red ball inside. He went to the park to play with his friends. They played catch with the red ball and had so much fun. Tim felt happy. As the sun went down, Tim knew it was time to go home. He put his toy knife and brilliant red ball back into the sack. He said goodbye to his friends and went home with a big smile on his face.

One day, a little girl named Amy found an interesting toy in her room. It was a small, funny-looking doll. Amy picked it up and said, "Wow! What a fun toy!" Amy played with the toy all day. She showed it to her mom and dad. They both liked the toy too. Amy's mom said, "That's a very interesting toy, Amy!" But then, something strange happened. The toy started to shrink! It got smaller and smaller until it was as tiny as a bug. Amy was sad and worried. She asked her mom, "What do I do?" Her mom thought for a moment and said, "Let's put the toy in a safe place. Maybe it will grow big again." So they put the tiny toy in a small box and waited. The next day, Amy opened the box and saw the interesting toy was big again! She was so happy and played with it all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom loved to make shapes with clay. One day, he made a big round shape. He showed it to his friend, Sam. "Look at my shape, Sam!" said Tom. Sam looked at the shape and said, "Wow, Tom! That's a great shape!" Tom wanted to make an even bigger shape. He tried and tried, but he could not manage to do it. He felt sad. Sam saw Tom and asked, "What's wrong, Tom?" "I can't make a bigger shape," said Tom. Sam smiled and said, "It's okay, Tom. You can try again tomorrow." The next day, Tom tried again and managed to make a bigger shape. He was happy. Tom learned that it's okay to not succeed at first, but to keep trying.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to imagine and play all day. One sunny day, Lucy was in her room and thought about making a big, yummy salad for her family. She went to the kitchen and found a heavy bowl. Lucy put lettuce, tomatoes, and cucumbers in the bowl. She mixed the salad with her little hands. Lucy was so happy to make something special for her family. Lucy called her mom, dad, and little brother to come eat the salad. They all sat down and enjoyed the yummy salad together. Lucy felt proud and her family was very happy. They all had a great day, full of love and yummy food.

Harry was very curious. He loved exploring his home. Today he was looking for something new. He found a drawer in the kitchen and he opened it. Inside the drawer were cookies. They were big, round, and looked very delicious. Harry was very excited. He was not sure if he should eat them. He brought the cookies to his mummy. She asked him "Did you find these in the drawer?" Harry nodded. His mummy said "These cookies are for special occasions. They are not for you. Put them back in the drawer". Harry wanted the cookies so badly. He didn't want to give them back. He pinched one and popped it in his mouth. Mummy was angry. She said "Harry, no! Those are for everyone to share. Give me back the cookie." Harry put the cookie back in the drawer. He knew that he had made a mistake. He looked up at his mummy and she smiled. "I'm sorry Harry, but those delicious cookies are not for you. We will have them at a special time." Harry was glad his mummy wasn't angry anymore. He knew that he should listen to her and not pinch from the drawer.
