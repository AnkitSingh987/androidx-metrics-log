Title: Tim the Boy and the Elephant

One day, a little boy named Tim found an interesting magazine on the floor. He picked it up and looked at the pictures. He saw a big elephant and a small mouse. Tim thought the pictures were very nice. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, look at this magazine I found! It has an elephant and a mouse. Can you read it to me?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. Let's sit down and read it together." As they read the magazine, Tim's mom would step on the floor to make the sound of the big elephant. Tim would laugh and clap his hands. When they read about the small mouse, Tim's mom would make a tiny squeak. Tim had a lot of fun reading the interesting magazine with his mom.

Once upon a time, in a hot land, there lived a little girl named Lily. She liked to ask questions all day long. One day, she asked her mom, "Why is it so hot here?" Her mom said, "Because the sun is very close to us." Lily thought about the hot sun and the silence around her. She wanted to learn more. So, she asked her dad, "Why is it so quiet here?" Her dad said, "Because the animals are hiding from the hot sun." Lily felt happy to learn new things. She kept asking questions to everyone she met. And as she grew up, she became the smartest girl in the hot land. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big brown dog named Buddy. Buddy lived in a small house with his family. They all loved him very much. Every day, Buddy would go for a walk with his family. They would play, run, and jump in the park. One day, Buddy saw a hot cup of coffee on the table. He was curious about the smell. He tried to take a sip, but it was too hot and it hurt his tongue. "Ouch!" he said. Buddy's family told him, "Buddy, don't drink the coffee. It's hot and can hurt you." Buddy listened to his family and did not try to drink the coffee again. The next day, Buddy went for a walk with his family. They played, ran, and jumped in the park just like before. When they got back home, Buddy saw another cup of coffee on the table. He remembered what his family said and did not try to drink it. He didn't want to get hurt again. Buddy repeated the words in his head, "Don't drink the coffee." And from that day on, Buddy never tried to drink coffee again.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous cat named Big Kitty. Big Kitty loved to play with socks. One day, Big Kitty found a red sock under the bed. He played with it all day long, tossing it up in the air and catching it. Big Kitty's friend, Little Dog, came over to play. Little Dog saw the red sock and said, "Big Kitty, can I play with the sock too?" Big Kitty didn't want to quit playing with the sock, but he knew it was nice to share. So, Big Kitty said, "Okay, Little Dog, we can play with the sock together." Big Kitty and Little Dog played with the red sock all day. They took turns tossing it and catching it. They were both very happy and tired after playing. At the end of the day, Big Kitty and Little Dog decided to share the sock every day, so they could have fun together. And they never wanted to quit playing with their favorite red sock.

Once upon a time, there was a loud bird named Bob. Bob liked to sing all day. His favorite color was pink. One day, Bob found a pink flower. He was very happy. He wanted to show his friends the pink flower. But it was very hot that day. Bob felt the sun burn on his wings. He flew to his friends quickly. He showed them the pink flower. They all sang a loud song together. And they played under the tree, away from the hot sun.

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big melon on a table. It was green and round and looked yummy. "Let's take it and eat it," Sara said. "No, that's not ours. It belongs to someone else," Ben said. But Sara did not listen. She ran to the table and grabbed the melon. She tried to run away, but the melon was too heavy. She dropped it on the ground and it broke into pieces. "Oh no!" Sara cried. Just then, a lady came out of a nearby house. She saw Sara and the broken melon. She was angry. "Hey, you! What did you do to my melon? That was for my picnic!" she shouted. Sara felt very embarrassed. She wanted to hide. She looked at Ben for help, but he was shaking his head. "I'm sorry, lady. I was hungry and I wanted to try it," Sara said. The lady sighed. She saw that Sara was just a little girl who made a mistake. She decided to be kind. "Well, you should have asked first. But I guess you learned your lesson. Here, have a slice of the melon. It's very sweet and juicy," she said. She gave Sara and Ben each a piece of the melon. They thanked her and ate it. It was delicious. The lady smiled. She thought Sara and Ben were adorable, even if they were naughty. She invited them to join her picnic. They agreed and had a fun time. They made a new friend and learned to be polite. They never took things that were not theirs again.
