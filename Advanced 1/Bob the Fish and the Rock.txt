Title: Bob the Fish and the Rock

One day, a happy fish named Bob liked to dive deep in the water. He loved to play with his friends. They would swim and splash all day long. Bob also liked to take showers in the waterfalls. The showers made him feel very clean and comfortable. One day, Bob and his friends found a new waterfall. They were excited to take a shower there. As they swam closer, they saw a big, shiny rock under the waterfall. The rock looked very special. It was different from the other rocks they had seen before. Bob and his friends started to dive and play around the shiny rock. Suddenly, the rock moved! The rock was not a rock at all. It was a big, friendly turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, I am Tina the Turtle. I was taking a comfortable shower too." Bob and his friends were very surprised, but they were happy to make a new friend. From that day on, they all played and took showers together in their special waterfall.

Once upon a time, there was a little box. The box was very lonely because it had no friends to play with. It sat in a big room all by itself. One day, the lonely box saw a ball rolling into the room. The box was very happy to see the ball. The box said, "Hi, ball! Do you want to play with me?" The ball smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, box!" So, the box and the ball played together all day. They had so much fun, and the box was not lonely anymore. As the day ended, the box asked the ball, "Will you stay with me, so we can play together every day?" The ball said, "Yes, I will stay with you, box. We will be best friends forever." And from that day on, the box and the ball stayed together and were never lonely again.

Sara is a bossy girl. She likes to tell everyone what to do. Today is her birthday. She wants to have a big party with cake and balloons and games. She tells her mom and dad what to do. "Mom, make a pink cake with lots of frosting. Dad, blow up the balloons and hang them on the wall. And don't forget the candles and the presents!" She tells her friends what to do. "Lily, you wear a purple dress. Ben, you wear a blue shirt. And you all have to sing happy birthday to me when I blow out the candles!" She tells her dog what to do. "Spot, sit and stay. Don't bark or jump or lick. And don't touch the cake or the balloons or the presents!" But Sara is not happy. She sees that her mom and dad are tired and sad. She sees that her friends are bored and scared. She sees that her dog is lonely and hungry. She thinks, maybe I am too bossy. Maybe I should be nice and say thank you and share and play. Maybe then everyone will have fun and smile and hug. She says sorry to her mom and dad. "Mom, dad, I love you. Thank you for making my birthday so special. You can have some cake and balloons and presents too." She says sorry to her friends. "Lily, Ben, you are my best friends. Thank you for coming to my party. You can wear whatever you want and play whatever you like." She says sorry to her dog. "Spot, you are a good dog. Thank you for being with me. You can bark and jump and lick and have some cake and balloons and presents too." Sara is happy. She sees that her mom and dad are proud and happy. She sees that her friends are excited and happy. She sees that her dog is wagging and happy. She thinks, maybe being bossy is not good. Maybe being nice is better. Maybe this is the best birthday ever.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She was 3 years old and she loved going to the park to play with her friends. One day when she was at the park, she saw a tutor. The tutor looked a bit strange. He was very tall and had a long beard. He was wearing a dirty, filthy coat. Lucy wanted to learn from the tutor, so she asked him if she could borrow his coat. The tutor said yes and asked her to take good care of it. She happily agreed and put on the dirty and filthycoat. Lucy was so excited to learn all the new things the tutor was teaching her. He taught her how to tie her shoelaces, do maths and even read! She was so happy that she had borrowed the tutor's coat. Every day the tutor and Lucy went to the park to play and learn. They became very good friends. Lucy was so glad that she had borrowed that filthy coat from the tutor!
