Title: Lily and the Box

Lily liked to sort her toys by color. She had a big box with many small boxes inside. She put the red toys in one box, the blue toys in another, and so on. She was very careful with her toys, because some of them were fragile and could break easily. One day, she found a new toy in her box. It was a long, thin, white thing. She did not know what it was, but it looked like a toy. She tried to bend it, but it did not bend. She tried to squeeze it, but it did not squeeze. She wondered what it could do. She decided to put it in the white box, with the other white toys. But the white box was full, and there was no room for the new toy. She pushed and pushed, but the new toy did not fit. She pushed harder, and then she heard a loud snap. The new toy broke in half. Lily was shocked and sad. She looked at the broken pieces, and saw that they had a dusty powder on them. She touched the powder, and it felt soft and smooth. She rubbed it on her hand, and it made a white mark. She rubbed it on the floor, and it made another mark. She realized that the new toy was not a toy, but a chalk. She had seen her teacher use chalk to write on the board at school. She felt silly for breaking the chalk, but also curious. She wondered what else she could write with the chalk. She picked up the pieces, and started to draw on the floor. She drew a big heart, and wrote her name inside. She smiled, and forgot about the broken chalk. She had found a new way to play.

Once upon a time, there was a man with an impressive job. He was a gear cutter! Every day he had to cut very special gears. It was an important job and he always did an impressive job. One day, he was cutting an extra special gear and wanted to make it extra special. He used his most powerful tools and worked hard. When he was finished, the gear was so impressive that everyone wanted one! The man was very proud of his work. He sold the gears to everyone. It was so fun to see everyone enjoy his work. Everyone had an impressive gear now! He gained a lot of respect for his impressive gear cutting skills. He was always known for his special gears after that.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. He liked to go outside and look at the landscape. One day, while Spot was outside, he saw a big ball. He wanted to get it, so he ran fast to catch it. Spot found a little girl named Sue, who was playing with the ball. "Can I play too?" asked Spot. Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played with the ball and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball changed into a butterfly! Spot and Sue were very surprised. They watched the butterfly fly away and smiled. They had a new friend and a fun story to tell.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw many birds and wanted to feed them. So, she took some bread from her bag and began to scatter it on the ground. The birds came to eat the bread. An old man saw Sue and said, "You are a good girl for feeding the birds. I have a reward for you." He gave Sue a shiny coin. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, sir!" But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew the old man's hat away. Sue ran to get the hat and gave it back to the old man. The old man was happy and said, "You are an honest girl. Here is another reward for you." He gave Sue another shiny coin. Sue was very happy and went home to tell her mom about her day at the park.

One day, a little bird named Tim went on a trip. He was very sleepy but wanted to see new things. Tim flew high in the sky and saw a big lake. He thought it looked fun to play in the water. Tim decided to dive into the lake. He went down fast, but then something unexpected happened. Instead of splashing into the water, he landed on something soft and bouncy. It was a big, green lily pad! Tim was so surprised! He looked around and saw a friendly frog named Fred. Fred said, "Hi! I saw you were sleepy and wanted to help. I moved the lily pad so you didn't fall in the water." Tim was happy and thanked Fred. They became best friends and played together all day.
