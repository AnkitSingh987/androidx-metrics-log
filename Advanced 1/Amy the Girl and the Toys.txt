Title: Amy the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a room that was very tidy. Amy liked to play with her toys in her room. One day, her friend Tom came over to play with her. "Let's play with my toys," Amy said to Tom. They played with dolls, cars, and blocks. They had a lot of fun. But soon, the room was not tidy anymore. There were toys all over the floor. Amy's mom called her name, "Amy, time to clean up!" Amy and Tom picked up all the toys and put them away. The room was tidy again. They were happy and proud of their work.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved her soft teddy bear, which she hugged every night before bed. One day, Lily saw an angel in her room. The angel was very pretty, with big wings and a nice smile. The angel asked Lily if she could borrow her soft teddy bear. Lily was a kind girl, so she said yes. The angel took the teddy bear and flew up into the sky. Lily was sad, but she knew the angel would bring her teddy bear back. After a day, the angel came back to Lily's room with the soft teddy bear. The angel said thank you to Lily for letting her borrow the teddy bear. Lily was very happy to have her teddy bear back. She hugged the angel and her teddy bear, and then they all went to sleep.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her room. She looked up at the sky and saw a big, shiny thing. "What is that?" she asked her mom. Her mom said, "That is the universe, Lucy. It is very big and has many weird things in it." Lucy wanted to know more about the universe. She thought, "If I can taste it, maybe I can learn more." So, Lucy went outside with a spoon and tried to taste the universe. But it was too far away! "Oh no," said Lucy, "I cannot taste the universe." Just then, a friendly space cat came down from the sky. The cat said, "Hi Lucy! I heard you want to taste the universe. I can help you!" Lucy was very happy. The space cat took Lucy up into the universe. There, Lucy tasted a star. It tasted like candy! The space cat said, "Now you know the universe is sweet, just like you!" Lucy smiled and thanked the space cat. They went back home and Lucy told her mom all about her adventure.

Anna liked to collect things. She had a box full of shiny stones, feathers, buttons and shells. She liked to look at them and sort them by color and shape. Sometimes she gave some to her friends as gifts. One day, Anna went to the park with her mom. She saw a big tree with purple fruits. Mom said they were prunes. Anna liked prunes. They were sweet and juicy. Mom said they could pick some if they asked the gardener. The gardener was a kind man. He said they could have some prunes, but not too many. He said he had to prune the tree to make it healthy and strong. He gave Anna a basket and a pair of scissors. He showed her how to cut the prunes carefully. Anna was happy. She collected many prunes. She filled her basket with the purple fruits. She thanked the gardener and gave him a shiny stone. He smiled and said it was a modest gift, but very pretty. Anna and mom went home. They washed the prunes and ate some. They were delicious. Anna saved some for later. She put them in her box with her other things. She was proud of her collection. She thought it was the best in the world.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were in a restaurant. Tom was the cook and Lily was the waiter. They used leaves and flowers and mud to make food and drinks. "Here is your soup, sir," Lily said, serving a bowl of muddy water to a teddy bear. "It is very hot and tasty." "Thank you, miss," the teddy bear said. "You are very generous." Tom watched from the kitchen. He was not happy. He wanted to be the waiter too. He thought Lily was having more fun. He decided to take the teddy bear's place. He sneaked out of the kitchen and sat down at the table. He grabbed the bowl of soup and drank it all. He made a loud slurping noise. "Hey, that's not fair!" Lily said. "That was for the teddy bear. You are very greedy." "I don't care," Tom said. "I want to be the waiter. You can be the cook." "No, I don't want to be the cook. I like being the waiter. It's my turn," Lily said. They started to argue. They pushed and pulled and shouted. They knocked over the table and the chairs and the plates. They made a big mess. Suddenly, they felt something on their skin. It was a red rash. It was very itchy. They scratched and scratched, but it did not help. They had used poison ivy to make the soup. They had not known it was bad for them. They cried and cried. They wished they had not fought. They wished they had shared. They wished they had been more generous. But it was too late. They had to go to the doctor and get medicine. They had to stay in bed for a week. They had no more fun.
