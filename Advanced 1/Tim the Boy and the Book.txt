Title: Tim the Boy and the Book

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big bookshelf in his house. It was full of books. He loved to read and look at the pictures. His favorite thing to do was to joke with his mom. "Mom, I found a big bookshelf!" said Tim. "Can I read the books?" "Of course, Tim," said his mom. "But be careful, some books are very old." Tim was very persistent. He wanted to read all the books on the bookshelf. He started with the first book. As he opened it, something unexpected happened. A funny bird flew out of the book! "Wow!" said Tim. "A bird came out of the book!" The bird liked to joke too. It told Tim funny jokes, and they laughed together. Tim and the bird became best friends. They read all the books on the bookshelf and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to sing all day. He lived in a big tree near a small house. One day, Bobo saw a girl named Lily in the house. Lily was trying to cook. She wanted to make her food taste good. She needed to add a pinch of salt to her food. But Lily was too small to reach the salt on the high shelf. Bobo wanted to help Lily. He flew into the house and picked up the salt with his beak. He flew back to Lily and dropped the salt into her hand. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Bobo!" From that day on, Lily and Bobo became best friends. They played together and made lots of good food using a pinch of salt. And Bobo's noisy songs made everyone in the house very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. It had many branches. A careless bird lived in the tree. One day, the bird saw a shiny thing on the ground. It was a bottle of oil. The bird wanted to play with the oil. So, it picked up the bottle in its beak. Then, the bird tried to fly back to the tree. But, it dropped the bottle. The bottle fell and split open. The oil went all over the ground. A cat saw the oil on the ground. The cat thought it was water. It jumped into the oil to play. But, the oil was sticky. The cat could not get out. The bird felt bad for the cat. It tried to help, but it was too late. The cat was stuck in the oil forever.

Once upon a time, there was a well in a small town. The well was old and needed repair. Everyone in the town used the well for water. One day, a rude man came to the well. He was not nice to the people in the town. The rude man wanted to fix the well. He said he could make it better. The people in the town were not sure if they should let him. They did not like how rude he was. But they wanted the well to work better. So, they let the rude man try to repair the well. The rude man started to fix the well. But then, something unexpected happened. The rude man fell into the well! The people in the town were surprised. They helped the rude man out of the well. After that, the rude man was not rude anymore. He was nice to everyone. The people in the town were happy, and they all worked together to repair the well.

Once upon a time there was a mommy who was trying to sort out dinner for her family. She was very busy and also a little bit loud because there was so much to do! Mommy got out two plates and started putting lots of different foods on them. There were potatoes, fruits and vegetables - it was a colorful meal! All of Mommy's children were watching her very closely, excited to finally get dinner. They all cheered when the dinner plates were done and it made a very loud noise! Mommy put the plates on the table and everyone was happy to be eating dinner. It was a delicious meal and everyone ate until they were full. The family was very happy and Mommy was very proud of sorting out dinner for her family.
