Title: Anna and Ben and the Taxi

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with cars and dolls. One day, they see a big yellow taxi outside their house. The taxi driver is inside the taxi. He is talking on his phone. "Wow, look at that taxi!" Ben says. "It is so shiny and fast. I want to play with it." "Me too," Anna says. "But we can't. It is not ours. It belongs to the taxi driver." "Maybe we can borrow it," Ben says. "Just for a little while. He won't mind. He is busy on his phone." Ben runs to the taxi. He opens the door and gets in. He pretends to drive the taxi. He makes honking and zooming noises. Anna follows him. She sits next to him. She pretends to be his passenger. "Where do you want to go?" Ben asks Anna. "I want to go to the park," Anna says. "And see the ducks and the swings." "Okay, let's go," Ben says. He turns the key in the ignition. The taxi starts to move. The taxi driver hears the noise. He looks up from his phone. He sees Ben and Anna in his taxi. He is very angry. He jumps out of his taxi. He runs to the door. He opens it and pulls Ben and Anna out. "What are you doing?" he shouts. "This is my taxi. You can't take it. You are very naughty. You could have caused an accident." He gives them a harsh look. He scolds them. He tells them to go home and never touch his taxi again. Ben and Anna are scared. They are sorry. They did not mean to upset the taxi driver. They did not know it was wrong to borrow his taxi. They run back to their house. They cry and hug their mom. Mom is kind. She listens to their story. She explains to them that they should not borrow things without asking. She says that the taxi driver works hard to earn money. She says that the taxi is his tool and his pride. She says that they should respect other people's things. Ben and Anna understand. They feel bad. They want to say sorry to the taxi driver. They draw a picture of a taxi and write "We are sorry" on it. They give it to mom. Mom says she will give it to the taxi driver the next time she sees him. Ben and Anna hope the taxi driver will forgive them. They promise to never borrow things without asking again. They learn a lesson. They hug mom and each other. They play with their own cars and dolls. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sam. They liked to play with toys and run in the park. One day, Tim and Sam found a small fire burning in the park. They knew it was not safe to play near the fire. Tim and Sam were very smart. They went to tell a grown-up about the fire. The grown-up was not happy. He said, "You should never play with fire. It can be very bitter and hurt you." Tim and Sam listened carefully to the grown-up. Later, Tim and Sam saw other kids playing near the fire. They remembered what the grown-up had said, so they told the other kids to stay away from the fire. The other kids listened to Tim and Sam, and they all stayed safe. The moral of the story is to always listen to grown-ups and help keep your friends safe.

Once upon a time there was a young girl named Julia who loved to organize things. She was always tidying up her room and organizing her toys. One day, her mom decided to give her a special treat. “How would you like a massage?” asked Mom. “A massage? What’s that?” asked Julia. Mom laughed and said, “A massage is when someone uses their hands to help relax your body. It can be really relaxing and a lot of fun.” Julia was excited to try and said, “Yes please!” So Mom set up a comfortable bed in the living room and brought out a bottle of hot massage oil. She showed Julia how to rub the oil on her arms and legs and then made gentle circular motions with her hands on Julia’s back. The massage felt so hot and relaxing that Julia soon fell asleep on the bed. When she woke up, Julia felt so much better and happy. She thanked her Mom for the relaxing massage and then went back to organizing her room.
