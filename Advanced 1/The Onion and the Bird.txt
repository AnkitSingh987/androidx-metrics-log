Title: The Onion and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was an onion. This onion was very angry. It did not like living in the kitchen with all the other food. It wanted to escape and see the world outside. One day, the angry onion saw an open window. It rolled and rolled, trying to escape. It finally got to the window and jumped out. The onion was so happy to be outside, but it had no idea what was waiting for it. As the onion rolled down the street, a big bird saw it. The bird was very hungry. It swooped down and grabbed the onion in its beak. The angry onion could not escape. The bird flew away with the onion and ate it for dinner. The end.

One day, a little mouse named Tim went to the store. He wanted to buy some cheese. Tim saw a big sign that said "cheese available". He was very happy. Tim went inside the store and saw a nice lady. He asked her, "Can I have some cheese, please?" The lady said, "Yes, of course! We have a lot of cheese here." Tim was so excited. Tim picked a big piece of cheese and took it home. He shared it with his family. They all said, "Yummy! This is the best cheese ever!" And they lived happily with their cheese.

Once, there was a boy named Tom. He was three years old and had a huge dream. He wanted to become a pilot! One day, Tom found a license in an old box. He asked his mom what it was. She said, "It's called a pilot's license. You can fly a plane only with one of these." Tom was so amazed! He held the license in his hand and said, "I want to fly a plane." His mother smiled and said, "You need to work hard to reach your dreams. But if you do, you can do amazing things." Tom was determined to have his dream come true. Every day, he studied and worked hard to learn all the things he needed to get his license. And one day, Tom became a pilot! He was so happy. He learned that when you put in a lot of effort, you can make your dreams come true.

Tom and Lily are curious children. They like to learn new words and ask many questions. One day, they see a big bird in the garden. The bird has a long beak and colorful feathers. "Wow, look at that bird!" Tom says. "What is its name?" "I don't know," Lily says. "Let's ask Mom." They run inside and find Mom in the kitchen. They show her the bird through the window. "Mom, what is that bird?" Tom asks. "That is a toucan," Mom says. "It lives in the rainforest and eats fruits and nuts." "Can we feed it?" Lily asks. "Sure, you can try," Mom says. "But be careful, it might be shy or scared." She gives them some grapes and nuts and tells them to go outside. Tom and Lily go to the garden and approach the bird slowly. They hold out their hands with the food. "Hello, toucan," Tom says. "We have some grapes and nuts for you. Do you want some?" The toucan looks at them with its big eyes. It hops closer and sniffs the food. Then it picks a grape with its beak and eats it. It makes a happy sound. "Yay, it likes it!" Lily says. "Here, have some more." The toucan eats more grapes and nuts from their hands. It flaps its wings and bobs its head. Tom and Lily giggle and smile. They feel happy to feed the toucan and learn a new word. They are curious children.

Once there was a little girl named Molly who lived with her family near a woods. Every day Molly's parents would take her for a long walk through the woods. One day, while walking, Molly's parents heard a strange noise. Her father said, "Wait, do you hear that? What is it?". They all stopped to listen. Molly said, "It's coming from the other way." Molly's father said, "It sounds terrible, whatever it is. Let's go see what it is." So they all started walking in the direction of the noise. As they got closer, the sound got louder and louder. When they finally arrived to the source of the noise, they were very surprised. The noise was coming from an old, abandoned well. Molly's father said, "That was terrible. We should never come this way again." So they quickly turned around and went back home. Since then, Molly and her family always make sure to stay away from that way.
