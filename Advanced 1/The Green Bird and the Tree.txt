Title: The Green Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a green bird who lived in a big tree. The green bird loved to talk with his friends. One day, the green bird saw a fragile egg on a branch. The egg was small and looked like it could break very easy. The green bird asked his friend, the red bird, "Did you see the fragile egg? What should we do?" The red bird said, "I don't know, but we must be careful. Let's ask the wise old owl." The green bird and the red bird went to talk to the wise old owl. The owl thought for a moment and said, "We must keep the egg safe until it hatches." So, the birds made a soft nest for the egg and waited. One day, the egg started to shake and crack. The birds were very excited to see what was inside. But when the egg broke open, a big snake came out! The snake was hungry and ate the green bird and the red bird. The wise old owl flew away fast. The snake lived in the big tree and the birds were gone.

John was walking home from school one sunny day. He was carrying a fragile box in his hand, balancing it carefully. Suddenly, he heard a voice calling his name. It was his best friend, Mike. "Hi John," said Mike, with a big grin on his face. John smiled and said, "Hi Mike. Look what I am carrying - it's so fragile. I need to balance it very carefully." Mike nodded as he watched John walk carefully and slowly, trying to balance the box in his hand. The two boys laughed as they went their separate ways. John carried the fragile box all the way home and was very proud of himself!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. Her name was Lucy. One day, when Lucy was playing in her garden, she saw something in the grass. It was a letter. Lucy picked it up and looked at it. It was very tidy. She wanted to get it open so she ran to her mum and said, “Mummy, look! I got a letter!” Mum smiled and said, “Let's see what it is. It looks very tidy” Lucy got a scissors and carefully opened the envelope. Inside was a special story book for Lucy. She was so excited and she said, “Mummy, thank you!” Mummy smiled and said, “You're very welcome! Now, let's have a nice, tidy story time with your special book”. So they snuggled up on the sofa and spent some quality time reading Lucy's special story book. It was a wonderful moment!

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He had many toys, but he wanted to find his favorite toy car. He looked under the bed and in his toy box, but he could not find it. Tim felt sad. Tim's mom, who was very nice and loved to help, saw him looking for his toy. She asked, "What are you looking for, Tim?" Tim told her about his toy car. His mom smiled and said, "Maybe you should look near the couch." Tim went to the living room and looked near the couch. He did not see the toy car at first. But then, he moved a pillow and realized his toy car was hiding behind it! Tim was so happy to find his toy car. "Thank you, Mom!" Tim said, hugging his mom. She hugged him back and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm always here to help you." Tim knew his mom was very compassionate and loved her very much. They played with the toy car together, and Tim was happy again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a mighty cat named Tom. Tom was big and strong. He loved to play and eat. His favorite food was salad. Tom would eat salad every day. He liked to listen to the birds sing while he ate. One sunny day, Tom went outside to eat his salad. He sat under a big tree and started to eat. He listened to the birds singing. They sang a happy song. Tom felt happy too. After eating his salad, Tom saw a little mouse. The mouse was lost and scared. Tom wanted to help the mouse. He listened to the mouse's story. Then, Tom took the mouse back to its home. The mouse was happy and thanked Tom. They became good friends. From that day, Tom and the mouse played together and ate salad under the big tree.
