Title: Lily and the Necklace

Lily was a naughty girl. She liked to break things and make a mess. She did not listen to her mom or dad. One day, she found a shiny necklace in her mom's drawer. She wanted to wear it and play with it. She did not know it was very expensive and special. She put the necklace around her neck and ran to the living room. She saw a big mirror on the wall. She wanted to see how pretty she looked. She jumped on the couch and tried to reach the mirror. But she was too small and clumsy. She slipped and fell. The necklace broke and the beads flew everywhere. Mom heard the noise and came to see what happened. She saw Lily on the floor, crying and holding the broken necklace. She saw the beads all over the couch and the carpet. She was very angry and sad. She said to Lily, "What have you done? This was my grandma's necklace. It was very old and precious. You broke it. You are a very bad girl. You need to be punished." She took Lily by the hand and dragged her to the closet. She opened the door and pushed Lily inside. She said, "You will stay here until you learn to be good. You will not get any toys or snacks or hugs. You will be alone in the dark." She closed the door and locked it. She left Lily in the closet, sobbing and scared. Lily did not know how long she was in the closet. She felt cold and hungry and thirsty. She wanted her mom to come and say sorry and hug her. She wanted to say sorry and hug her mom. She wanted to play with her toys and eat her snacks. She wanted to see the sun and the sky and the birds. But no one came. She was alone in the dark. She wished she had never touched the necklace. She wished she had been a good girl. But it was too late. She was punished.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was very anxious because he lost his way home. He flew around trying to find his nest. One day, Tim saw a big tree. He thought he found his home. But the tree had sharp branches and dark ash around it. Tim was scared, but he wanted to go home. Tim went to the tree with the sharp branches. He tried to land on a branch, but it hurt him. Tim fell down near the ash. He was so sad and still far from home. Tim never found his way back, and he stayed near the scary tree forever.

One day, a big horse lived in a stable. The horse was very impressive. He could run very fast. He had a friend, a small car. The car could drive fast too. They liked to race each other. One sunny day, the horse and the car decided to race. They were ready to see who was faster. The race started, and they both ran and drove as fast as they could. The horse was in front, but the car was close behind. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and lifted the car into the air! The car was flying! The horse was very surprised. The car flew to the finish line and won the race. The horse was happy for his friend. They both had fun and laughed together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Annie. Annie was usually very happy and content, until one day she became very restless. She wanted to do something, something fun. So she decided to play a game. She went around the house looking for something to use. She found a big sack and a seat. She put the sack in the seat and hopped on it. Annie was very excited and began playing with the sack. She tried to swing it around and around. Then, she rode on it like a horse. She was so happy and joyful. Unfortunately, the relentless suspense of the game wore off and Annie got bored after a while. But she still had a big smile on her face from all the fun she had. She was off to find another game to play!
