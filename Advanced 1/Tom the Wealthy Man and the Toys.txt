Title: Tom the Wealthy Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, lived a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and many toys. He loved to play with his toys all day long. One day, Tom met a little boy named Tim. Tim had no toys and was sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "I have no toys to play with." Tom felt bad for Tim and wanted to help him. Tom had an idea. He gave Tim some of his toys. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you!" They played together and became good friends. But then, something unexpected happened. Tom's toys stopped working! They were puzzled. Tom and Tim asked a wise old man for help. The old man said, "The toys stopped working because you have learned the true meaning of sharing and friendship. You don't need toys to be happy." Tom and Tim smiled and continued to play together, without the toys, and they were the happiest they had ever been.

Once upon a time, there was a funny book called "The Laughing Novel". This book loved to laugh and make others laugh too. One day, a little boy named Tim found the book in his room. He picked it up and started to read it. As he read, the book made him laugh a lot. He had never read such a funny book before. Tim went to his friend, Sarah, and said, "Look at this book! It's so funny! Let's read it together." Sarah looked at the book and said, "Okay, let's read it!" They sat down and started to read the book. They both laughed and laughed as they read the funny words. But then, a bad wind came and blew the book away. Tim and Sarah were sad. They wanted to keep laughing with the funny book. They looked everywhere for it, but they could not find it. They were very sad, but they knew that they would always remember the funny book that made them laugh. And every time they thought of the funny book, they would laugh and smile.

One day, a cat and a dog were in a kitchen. The cat said, "I want to make a cake." The dog thought it was a stupid idea, but he wanted to help his friend. They found a pan and put all the things they needed in it. They tried to manage the big pan, but it was hard. They had to work together to mix everything. The cat and the dog were very excited to see their cake. When the cake was done, they took it out of the oven. But, surprise! The cake was not a cake at all. It was a big pizza! The cat and the dog laughed and said, "We made a pizza, not a cake! That's so funny!" They ate the pizza and had a great time.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jenny. She liked to swing when the sun came up in the morning. Every day Jenny would put on her shoes, grab her favourite pink doll and head outside to the park. At the park Jenny loved to swing as high as she could until she could almost touch the clouds. After a few minutes in the swing she would jump off and run to the exercise area. Here, she liked to crawl and climb over all the fun things like the big log and the big rock. On the way home Jenny enjoyed a yummy snack like a banana. She always ate it while swinging. After she'd had her snack, Jenny would say goodbye to the swings and head home for a nap. When Jenny's mum came home that afternoon she asked Jenny, “What did you do today?”. “I swung, exercised and ate yummy things!” Jenny said with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy loved to study. Every day, she would sit at her small desk and look at her books. Her books were very organized. She had a book about cats, a book about dogs, and a book about birds. Daisy liked to be neat and tidy. One day, Daisy saw something outside her window. It was a small bird with a yellow beak. The bird was looking for something. Daisy thought, "Maybe the bird is looking for a friend." She wanted to help the bird. So, Daisy started to study her book about birds very carefully. Daisy learned that birds like to eat seeds. She went outside and put some seeds on the ground. Then, she hid behind a tree and waited. Soon, the bird came and ate the seeds. Daisy was so happy. She had made a new friend by studying her book about birds. From that day on, Daisy and the bird became best friends. They played together every day. Daisy learned that studying can help make new friends. And the bird learned that having a friend like Daisy was the best thing ever.
