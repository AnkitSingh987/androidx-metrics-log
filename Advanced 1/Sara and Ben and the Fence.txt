Title: Sara and Ben and the Fence

Sara and Ben like to play in the garden. They like to look for bugs and flowers. One day, they see a cricket on a leaf. It is green and has long legs. It makes a loud noise with its wings. "Look, a cricket!" Sara says. "Can we catch it?" Ben nods. He has a jar with holes in the lid. He tries to grab the cricket, but it jumps away. Sara and Ben run after it. They are not calm. They are excited. The cricket leads them to the fence. It jumps over to the other side. Sara and Ben want to follow it, but they hear their mom calling. "Time for lunch, kids!" Mom says. "Come inside!" Sara and Ben look at each other. They want to see the cricket again. They ask Mom if they can delay lunch. "Please, Mom, can we delay lunch?" Sara says. "We want to catch the cricket!" Mom smiles. She knows they like to play. She says they can delay lunch for a little bit, but not too long. "Okay, Mom, thank you!" Ben says. "We will be back soon!" Sara and Ben hurry to the fence. They climb over to the other side. They look for the cricket. They hope it is still there. They want to be its friends.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter. He had a big dog. The dog liked to play with the hunter. They lived near a big tree. One day, the hunter found a bag of sour fruit under the tree. He wanted to eat the fruit. But the dog wanted to play. So, the hunter threw the fruit. The dog ran to catch the fruit. The fruit scatter all around. The hunter and the dog played with the sour fruit all day. They were very happy. They went home and slept under the big tree. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful land, there lived a little girl named Sue. Sue loved summer. She liked to play in the sun with her friends. They would run, jump, and pass a big ball to each other. One day, while Sue and her friends were playing, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird said, "Hello, Sue. I have a gift for you." Sue was very surprised. She did not know birds could talk. The bird gave Sue a magic hat. The hat could make summer last all year. Sue was very happy. She said, "Thank you, bird!" Now, Sue and her friends could play and pass the big ball every day, and the beautiful land stayed sunny and warm forever.

One day, a little girl called Mary was watching television in her room. She had been watching a wild cartoon when all of a sudden, the power went out. Mary sighed in a very disappointed way and started to cry, as she had wanted to keep watching her favourite cartoon. Mummy came into her bedroom and said that Mary's wild behaviour had caused the power outage. She explained to Mary that if she wanted to watch television, she should be quieter, as it was not good to make so much noise in the house. Mary nodded her head and promised to be quieter next time. She then sighed one more time, but this time, it was a sigh of relief. She knew that she had to be more careful and knew that she had learned an important lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a big rhinoceros. He lived in a nice forest. He was a perfect friend to all the animals. One day, the rhinoceros was very hungry. He walked to his friend, the monkey. He said, "Can you please request some food for me?" The monkey said, "Yes, I will help you." The monkey went to the other animals in the forest. He said, "Our friend, the rhinoceros, is hungry. Let's give him some food." All the animals gave food. The rhinoceros ate the food and was happy. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time there was an old man. He was bald, with not a hair to be seen on his head. He had a lovely voice and always sang when he entered a room. One day the old man went for a walk in the woods. While he was there he heard a tiny voice calling out for help. The old man couldn't see anyone so he followed the voice until he reached a small cave. The old man went inside the cave and saw a little girl with curly blonde hair. She told the old man that she had gotten lost and was very scared. The old man smiled and said, "Don't worry, I'm here to help you. We'll find your way back home together." The little girl smiled and took his hand. The old man and the little girl went off together, hand in hand. The old man sang along the way and made the little girl laugh. They continued walking until they came across a house, which the little girl knew was her own. She said a big thank you to her bald friend, and the old man entered the house with her. Eventually the little girl was safely back home. The old man had saved the day and the two were now friends. When the old man entered a room they would always sing together and it reminded everyone of the sweet voice of the bald old man.
