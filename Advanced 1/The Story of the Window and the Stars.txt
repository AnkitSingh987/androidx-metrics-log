Title: The Story of the Window and the Stars

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She had a very favorite green dress. But it was very expensive and her parents couldn't afford it. The little girl loved the dress so much that she sighed whenever she saw it in the store window. One day, she wished upon the stars for the dress. On the way home, her parents stopped at a store. Inside, the girl saw that same beautiful green dress! She was so happy she started to jump. Her parents purchased the dress for her as a surprise gift. The girl hugged her parents and put on the dress. She sighed with joy and twirled around in it. She wore it everywhere, twirling and feeling like a princess. She was so happy to finally have her favorite dress!

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Jenna. Jenna had a special magic which made her clothes shrink whenever she wished it. One day, Jenna was walking in her garden when she heard her mother calling her from the house. “Jenna!” she cried. “Come inside and get ready for dinner!” As Jenna entered the house, she felt her clothes shrinking around her. Jenna knew that her special magic had worked yet again! Once inside, Jenna's mother smiled and said, “Oh, how clever you are! I love seeing how you can make your clothes shrink and grow.” Jenna couldn't help but smile back. She was very pleased with her special magic. They both hugged and went to the kitchen to eat dinner. Jenna had worked her magic yet again!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wise old man. He had a big sack full of toys. One day, he saw a sad little girl named Lily. She was crying because she lost her toy. The wise old man walked up to Lily and said, "Don't cry, little girl. Look in my sack and pick a toy." Lily looked inside the sack and saw many toys. She picked a pretty doll and smiled. "Thank you, wise man," she said. From that day on, the wise old man went around the town with his sack of toys. He made all the sad children smile. Everyone in the town loved the wise old man and his big sack of toys. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a clumsy bird named Bobo was in a tree. Bobo had a big egg. He was very happy. But he was also sleepy. Bobo tried to stay awake, but he fell asleep. While Bobo was asleep, the egg started to crack. A little bird came out. The little bird was hungry. It said, "Mom, I want food!" Bobo did not wake up. A kind squirrel saw the little bird. The squirrel said, "Oh no! Your mom is asleep. I will help you find food." The little bird was happy. They went to find food together. Soon, Bobo woke up and saw his baby bird was gone. He looked everywhere and found the squirrel and his baby bird. They all ate food together and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big slide in the park. All the kids loved to play on it. One day, a new kid named Tim came to the park. He saw the slide and wanted to play on it too. But he was scared. He had never been on such a big slide before. Tim saw a girl named Sue playing near the slide. He asked her, "Can I borrow your toy car?" Sue said, "Yes, but don't be rude. Say please." Tim said, "Please, can I borrow your toy car?" Sue smiled and gave it to him. Tim took the toy car and put it on the slide. He watched it go down fast and fun. He thought, "If the car can do it, so can I!" So, he climbed up and slid down the slide. He was so happy and proud. From that day on, Tim and Sue became good friends and played on the slide together.

Once upon a time, there was a nice little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house near the shore with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play on the shore. She liked to build sand castles and watch the birds fly in the sky. One day, while Lily was playing on the shore, she found a pretty shell. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. Her mom loved the shell too. They decided to take it home and put it in their special box of treasures. Every day, Lily and her mom would go to the shore and look for more shells. They found shells in many colors and shapes. They loved their time together on the shore. It was their favorite place to be. And they lived happily ever after.
