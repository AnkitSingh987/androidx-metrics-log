Title: Max the Brown Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to eat muffins. One day, he found a big muffin on the table. It was a yummy blueberry muffin. Max was very happy. Max wanted to share the muffin with his friend, a little cat named Lily. So, Max split the muffin into two pieces. One piece was for him, and the other piece was for Lily. Max took the muffin pieces to Lily's house. When Lily saw the muffin, she was very happy too. Max and Lily ate the muffin together. They both said, "Yum, yum!" After they finished eating, they went outside to play. Max and Lily were very good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a job. He would deliver things to people. One day, he had to deliver a cable to his friend, the cat named Luna. As Max walked to Luna's house, he saw a big dog named Rex. Rex was envious of Max's job. Rex said, "I wish I could deliver things too." Max smiled and said, "You can help me deliver the cable to Luna!" Together, Max and Rex went to Luna's house. They gave Luna the cable. Luna was happy to see them. She said, "Thank you, Max and Rex!" They all played together and had a fun day. From that day on, Max and Rex worked together to deliver things. They were the best of friends, and everyone in the town loved them. And they were never envious of each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat. The cat liked to play and run. One day, the cat went out to discover new things. The cat saw a row of big trees. The cat thought it would be fun to climb the trees. So, the cat jumped from one tree to another. It was having a lot of fun. But then, the cat got very tired. It tried to jump to the last tree, but it could not make it. The cat fell down and got hurt. The cat was not happy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a clever boy named Tom. Tom liked to ride his bike very fast. He had a small figure toy that he took with him everywhere he went. Tom and the toy were best friends. One day, Tom and his toy went to the park. They saw a big hill and wanted to ride down it. Tom put the toy in his pocket and started to ride down the hill. He went faster and faster. At the bottom of the hill, there was a big rock. Tom did not see the rock. He hit the rock and fell off his bike. The toy fell out of his pocket and broke. Tom was very sad.

One day, a dog named Zip went for a jog. Zip was a pale dog with big, friendly eyes. He loved to run and play outside. While jogging, Zip saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. She sat on a bench and looked at the ground. Zip wanted to help Sue feel better. Zip went to Sue and said, "Let's jog together!" Sue looked at Zip and smiled. She said, "Okay, Zip! Let's jog and have fun!" So, Zip and Sue jogged and played in the park. They became good friends and were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little girl called Jane. Every day, Jane would run around the garden with her friends, counting the flowers. She would learn to number each flower and it made her very happy. One day, Jane felt a bit sick. Her mum and dad took her to see the doctor. The doctor checked Jane and said she was healthy. The doctor gave Jane some medicine and she quickly felt better. Jane was feeling very lively the next day and she ran around the garden with her friends again. They counted the flowers together, choosing the prettiest numbers for their favourite flowers. Jane was so happy to be feeling better and to be able to count with her friends. It was one of the best days ever.
