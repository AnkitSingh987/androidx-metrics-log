Title: Tommy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little mouse called Tommy. Tommy was walking down a street when he heard a scuttling sound. He wondered what made the noise, so he stopped and looked around the corner. Suddenly he saw a big tabby cat! Tommy was very scared and ran away as fast as he could. The cat started chasing Tommy, and they were running round and round in circles. "Please don't catch me!" cried Tommy. But the cat was too fast and soon it caught up with him. It swiped its paw at him and knocked him over. Tommy knew it was all over. He looked up at the cat and said, "Please don't hurt me." The cat just looked at him and then a wicked idea came into its head. It decided to play a game with Tommy. The cat said, "I'm going to let you go, but you have to close your eyes and count to ten. If you can do that, then you can go free. If you don't, I will catch you, again." Tommy thought about what to do. Should he close his eyes or should he keep running? In the end, he decided to take a chance and closed his eyes. He started to count, but before he could finish the cat caught up with him. The cat had tricked him, and Tommy was never seen again.

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to soar high in the sky. He had a memory of his mom telling him about the perfect day to fly. Tim was so excited to try it. He told his friend, Sam the squirrel, "I want to soar high today!" Sam said, "That sounds fun! I want to see you fly!" So, Tim and Sam went to the tallest tree in the forest. Tim got ready to fly. He flapped his wings and jumped off the tree. Tim started to soar high in the sky. It was a perfect day to fly, just like his mom said. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a big, shiny thing in the sky. It was a balloon! Tim got closer and closer to the balloon. He touched the balloon with his beak, and POP! The balloon burst, and Tim started to fall. Sam the squirrel saw this and quickly climbed down the tree. He found a big pile of leaves and pushed it under Tim. Tim landed softly on the leaves. Tim was happy that his friend Sam saved him. They both laughed and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pink bag with her favorite things inside. One day, she went to her grandma's house to play. When Lily got to grandma's house, she saw a big box in the living room. Lily was very excited and wanted to open the box. Grandma said, "Let's unpack the box together, but first, can you get your notebook from your pink bag?" Lily went to her pink bag and took out her notebook. She and grandma started to unpack the big box. Inside the box, there were many toys and games. Lily was so happy to see all the fun things. At the bottom of the box, Lily found a special surprise. It was a beautiful pink doll that looked just like the one she had drawn in her notebook! Grandma smiled and said, "I saw your drawing in your notebook and wanted to surprise you with the same doll." Lily hugged her grandma and thanked her for the wonderful surprise. They played with the new toys all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a very important job. He had to deliver a shiny coin to his grandma. She lived in a small house down the street. Tim walked and walked. On the way, he saw a big, filthy puddle. He didn't want to get the coin dirty. So, he jumped over the puddle. He was very careful not to drop the coin. Finally, Tim reached grandma's house. He gave her the shiny coin. Grandma was so happy! She gave Tim a big hug. They had a fun day together, and Tim felt proud that he had delivered the coin safely.
