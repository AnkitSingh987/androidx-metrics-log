Title: Tim the Boy and the Sue

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He was very tired but wanted to find a big rock to play with. As he walked, he saw his friend, Sue, who was also looking for something fun. "Hi Sue, I am looking for a big rock," Tim said. "Do you want to help me?" "Yes, Tim! Let's find it together," Sue replied. They walked and walked until they found a big rock. Tim and Sue were so happy. They used their hands to reveal a funny face on the rock. They laughed and played with the rock until they were too tired to play anymore. Then, they went home to rest.

Once upon a time, there was a family on a dull yacht. They wanted to explore the sea, but the yacht was too slow. They did not know what to do. Suddenly, one day the father said: "Let's surrender the yacht and find another way." The family agreed and soon found another, faster yacht. The new yacht was fun and fast, and the family spent many hours sailing. They explored islands and found hidden treasures. Soon it was time to sail back home. As they sailed along, the father said: "We can't forget the dull old yacht. We should go back and say goodbye as it was loyal and faithful." The family said their farewells to the old yacht and sailed away, full of joyful memories. The family was glad they had surrendered the old yacht and found something new and exciting.

One day, a boy named Tim found a trumpet in his toy box. He was very happy. He wanted to play the trumpet all day. Tim was not fearful at all. He loved the trumpet so much. Tim went outside to play with his friends. He showed them the trumpet. They all wanted to try it. They took turns and made loud sounds. Everyone was having fun. Suddenly, a big dog came running to them. The dog was not happy. He did not like the loud sounds. The kids were fearful. The dog took the trumpet and ran away. The kids were sad. They thought they lost the trumpet forever. But then, the dog came back. He had a ball in his mouth. The dog wanted to play with the kids. He gave them the trumpet and the ball. The kids were not fearful anymore. They played with the dog and the trumpet all day. The dog was their new friend.

Once there was a little girl. She was so excited because she found a penny on the ground. She wanted to keep it so she put it in her pocket. Then she went for a walk with her mom. On the way, she stumbled over a rock. But she was okay. Then she saw a dog. She wanted to pet it but her mom said no. She was a bit sad, but then she remembered the penny. She pulled it from her pocket and held it up. It was so shiny in the sun. Then she smiled and continued walking. She was so excited to keep her penny for a long time.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. The ball had a big nose. The ball liked to play with his friends. They played all day long. They laughed and had fun. One day, the ball wanted to surprise his friends. He hid behind a tree. He waited for his friends to come. He was very quiet. He did not make a sound. His friends came near the tree. The ball jumped out. "Surprise!" he said. His friends laughed. They were happy. They played a loud game of catch. They had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a shiny crystal. It was very pretty. The crystal lived in a big tree house with its friend, the dizzy bird. The dizzy bird would fly around the tree house all day, and sometimes it would bump into the crystal. One day, the dizzy bird bumped into the crystal so hard that it fell out of the tree house. The crystal was very sad and scared. It did not know how to get back home. The dizzy bird felt bad and wanted to help the crystal. But the bird knew that it would be punished if it did not find the crystal soon. The dizzy bird looked everywhere for the crystal. It searched high and low, near and far. At last, the dizzy bird found the crystal near a big rock. The bird was so happy to find its friend. It picked up the crystal and flew back to the tree house. When they got back, the other animals saw that the dizzy bird had found the crystal. They were all very happy. The other animals did not punish the dizzy bird. Instead, they all had a big party to celebrate the crystal being back home. From that day on, the dizzy bird was more careful when it flew around the tree house. It did not want to lose the crystal again. And the crystal and the dizzy bird lived happily ever after.
