Title: Tim the Boy and the Dog

One day, a boy named Tim felt happy. It was Wednesday, and he was playing with his toy car in the park. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. Tim said, "I feel fine today!" Then, a girl named Sue came to the park. She had a big red ball. Sue said, "Hi Tim, do you want to play with my ball?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" So, they played together, and they were having lots of fun. But, something unexpected happened! A dog came running and took Sue's ball in his mouth. Tim and Sue were sad. But, the dog came back, and he wanted to play too! So, they all played together, and they had a great time. Now, Tim, Sue, and the dog were all feeling fine on that Wednesday.

When Sam woke up in the morning, he was feeling happy and wanted to have some fun. He got out of bed and went outside. In the distance, he saw something that made him really excited. It was a surfboard! Sam ran to it and started to shake the board. He felt the board vibrate and he knew that meant the surf was coming. The waves started to build and Sam was ready to surf. He paddled out to the waves and jumped up on the board. The water was calm and the waves were smooth, but Sam was a bit ignorant. He hadn't had much experience surfing and so he kept falling off the surfboard. But no matter how many times he fell off, Sam kept on trying. Eventually he learned how to balance on the board and started catching some waves. Sam had a lot of fun and eventually made it back to shore. He was so proud of himself for learning how to surf and he knew he'd never be ignorant of surfing ever again!

Once upon a time, there was a skeleton. He liked to play games. He was very competitive. He wanted to win all the time. One day, the skeleton went to the park. He saw a little boy. The boy was playing with a ball. The skeleton wanted to play too. He asked the boy, "Can you give me the ball?" The boy was scared, but he gave the ball to the skeleton. They played with the ball. The skeleton tried very hard to win. He threw the ball too far. The boy ran to get the ball. But the ball went into the street. The boy didn't look both ways. The boy got hit by a car. The skeleton was sad because he just wanted to play.

One day, a fast car named Speedy loved to go very fast. He was very independent and did not like to slow down. He saw a lot of smoke in the air and wondered where it was coming from. He wanted to find out, so he went on a journey. On his way, he met a slow car named Slowpoke. Slowpoke said, "Speedy, you should not go so fast. It can be dangerous." Speedy did not listen and said, "I am independent and I can go fast if I want to." He went faster and faster, leaving Slowpoke behind. As Speedy got closer to the smoke, he saw a big fire. He was scared and didn't know what to do. Then, Slowpoke came to help. Slowpoke had a big hose to put out the fire. Together, they put out the fire and saved the day. Speedy learned that sometimes it's good to slow down and listen to others.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the kitchen. She wanted to make a yummy snack. She saw some bread and butter. Amy thought, "I can make a butter sandwich!" Amy took a piece of bread and put butter on it. She saw something weird in the butter. It was a tiny toy car! She was very surprised. "How did the car get in the butter?" she asked her mom. Her mom laughed and said, "I put it there to surprise you! I knew you would find it when you made your sandwich." Amy smiled and took the toy car. She covered the butter and put it away. Then, she ate her sandwich and played with her new toy car.
