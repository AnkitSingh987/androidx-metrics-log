Title: Sue the Girl and the Lily

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to swim in the big blue pool. She had a doll that she took with her everywhere. Her doll's name was Lily. One day, Sue and Lily went to the pool. Sue put Lily on a chair so she could watch her swim. Sue swam and splashed in the water. She was having so much fun! But then, Sue saw that Lily had fallen off the chair. Sue ran to pick her up. Lily looked hurt. Sue felt sad. She hugged Lily and said sorry. Sue promised to be more careful with her doll. From that day on, Sue and Lily had lots of fun together, but Sue made sure Lily was always safe.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. She had a pretty blouse. One day, she went to play outside. Mia saw a big, unknown box. She was very curious. Mia decided to use a stick to open the box. When she opened it, out came a small, friendly cat. The cat was hungry, so Mia shared her lunch with the cat. They became good friends. The next day, Mia and the cat played outside again. But this time, the cat put on Mia's blouse! Mia was very surprised. She did not know that the cat could wear a blouse. They both laughed and played together all day long.

One day, there was a very tidy caterpillar named Tom. Tom wanted to feel relaxed so he crawled over to his friend, the massage therapist. He asked her to give him a massage! The massage therapist put some nice oil on Tom's back, and then she massaged him with her hands. Tom felt very cozy and relaxed. He thanked her for the massage and then he crawled away. Tom was feeling so relaxed that he fell asleep under a bush. When he woke up, he felt refreshed and super-tidy. He was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, enormous elephant named Elly. Elly had a long trunk that she used to pick up things. She lived in a big forest with her friends. One day, Elly was taking a walk with her friend, a tiny mouse named Mimi. They liked to step on the soft grass and look at the pretty flowers. As they walked, Elly saw a big tree with a big trunk. She wanted to show Mimi how strong she was. Elly said, "Mimi, watch me push this tree with my trunk!" Mimi watched as Elly used her long trunk to push the tree. The tree shook, and many leaves fell down. Mimi clapped and said, "Wow, Elly, you are so strong!" Elly was very happy that she could show her friend how strong she was. They continued to step through the forest, looking at all the beautiful things around them. And from that day on, Elly and Mimi were the best of friends, always helping each other and having fun in the big, enormous forest.

Once there was a young dragon named Tink. Tink lived in a cave with her mom. Every day, Tink's mom would give her yummy food to eat. One day, Tink was feeling extra hungry, so her mom said, "Let's go see if we can find something else for you to eat today." Tink and her mom flew around the forest looking for something for Tink to taste. Finally, they found a big apple tree. Tink was so excited! She flew up to an apple and took a bite. But something wasn't right. It was too sour. Tink said, "I don't like it! It tastes too sour!" Then, a bossy little bird flew up to them. He said, "You should try my apples! They are much sweeter!" Tink tried one of the bird's apples and it was delicious! She smiled and said, "You were right, your apples are much better!" Tink and her mom thanked the little bird and went back to the cave full and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He lived with his mom and dad in a small house. One day, Tom's mom asked him to stay in the house while she went to the store. Tom said, "Okay, mom." While Tom stayed in the house, he saw a light in the kitchen. It was a small, pretty light. Tom went closer to see what it was. The light was coming from a jar. The jar was open and available for Tom to look inside. Tom looked into the jar and saw a tiny, glowing bug. The bug was the one making the light. Tom was happy to see the bug. He decided to keep the bug as a friend. So, he closed the jar and stayed with his new friend until his mom came back.
