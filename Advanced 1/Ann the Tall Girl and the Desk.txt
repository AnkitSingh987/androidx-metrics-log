Title: Ann the Tall Girl and the Desk

One day, a tall girl named Ann went to school. She had a big desk where she liked to draw and write. Ann loved her desk very much. Every day, she would give her desk a little kiss to say thank you for helping her learn. One day, something strange happened. When Ann kissed her desk, it started to move! The desk grew legs and arms and stood up. Ann was very surprised. She did not know what to do. The desk gave Ann a big hug and said, "Thank you for being so nice to me every day." Ann smiled and hugged her desk back. They became best friends and had lots of fun together. From that day on, Ann's desk would always help her with her work and play with her at school.

Once, a girl named Lily liked to visit her grandma. Her grandma lived in a small house near a big tree. They loved to sit and talk. One day, Lily heard an unknown word from her grandma. She did not know what it meant. Lily asked her grandma, "What is that word?" Her grandma smiled and said, "It is a big word for a little girl like you." Lily wanted to learn the unknown word, so she asked her grandma to teach her. Grandma was happy to help. She told Lily the word and what it meant. Lily practiced saying the word over and over. Soon, Lily knew the unknown word very well. She was proud and happy. Now, Lily and her grandma could talk even more when they visited each other.

Once upon a time, there was a turkey. The turkey was very sad because he was dirty. He wanted to be clean. So, he went to the river to wash himself. At the river, the turkey met a big fish. The fish said, "Hello, turkey! Why are you so sad?" The turkey replied, "I am dirty, and I want to be clean. Can you help me wash?" The fish agreed to help the turkey. They started to wash the turkey in the river. But the river was very fast, and the turkey could not swim well. The big fish tried to save the turkey, but it was too late. The turkey was dead. The fish was very sad, and he swam away.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily loved to play hide and seek with her friends. One day, Lily and her friends were playing near the big tree. The sun was going down, and the sky looked like a pretty painting with a sunset. Lily's friend, Tom, said, "I will count, and you all hide!" Lily ran and hid behind the big tree. She could smell the sweet flowers that grew near the tree. After Tom found all the other friends, he still could not find Lily. He called out, "Lily, where are you?" Lily giggled and came out from behind the tree. "Here I am!" she said with a big smile. All of Lily's friends clapped and said, "You are so good at hiding!" They all laughed and played until the sunset was gone, and the sky was dark. It was time to go home, but they knew they would play hide and seek again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ball. One day, while she was playing, she saw a big red button. Kitty was curious and pressed the button. When Kitty pressed the button, it made a loud sound. Then, there was silence. Kitty looked around and saw that all her friends were gone. She was sad and wanted to find them. Kitty searched everywhere for her friends. She looked under the bed, behind the chair, and even in the closet. Then, she found another button. This time, it was a green button. Kitty thought maybe this button could help her find her friends. She pressed the green button and it released her friends. They all came back and they were happy again. The twist was that the red button made her friends disappear, but the green button brought them back. Kitty learned to be careful with buttons and they all played happily together.
