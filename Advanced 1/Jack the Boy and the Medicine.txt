Title: Jack the Boy and the Medicine

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. Jack was not feeling very well, so his mom took him to a doctor. The doctor told Jack that he needed to take some powder medicine in order to heal. Jack was scared because he had never taken powder medicine before. He didn't want to take it and he started to cry. His mom tried to comfort him and promised that the medicine would help him heal. Finally, Jack agreed to take the medicine. He took one teaspoon of powder medicine every day for one week. After one week, Jack started to feel much better. He smiled and was happy that he healed. Jack has never been so scared of powder medicine ever since.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw an enormous tree with big green leaves. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but his mom said, "No, it's too high." While Tim was playing, he saw a funny bug on the ground. The bug had many legs and would blink its tiny eyes. Tim giggled and said, "Hi, little bug!" The bug blinked back at him. Tim's mom called him over for a snack. She had a cup of yogurt for him. Tim sat down and ate the yummy yogurt. He looked back at the bug and said, "Bye, little bug!" The bug blinked and went on its way.

Tim and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to throw the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they missed and the ball rolled away. Then they ran after it and laughed. One time, the ball went too far and landed near a big tree. Tim and Lily ran to get it, but they saw something move behind the tree. It was a big dog with black fur and sharp teeth. The dog growled and showed its teeth. Tim and Lily were scared and stopped. "Go away, dog!" Tim shouted. He raised his arm and waved it. He hoped the dog would leave them alone. But the dog did not go away. It barked louder and louder. It came closer and closer. Tim and Lily backed away. They looked for help, but no one was near. They felt alone and afraid. Then they heard a whistle. It was a man with a blue hat and a badge. He was a park ranger. He saw the dog and the children. He ran to them and blew his whistle. The dog heard the whistle and stopped barking. It looked at the man and wagged its tail. The man called the dog by its name and put a leash on it. "Sorry, kids. This is Rex. He is a good dog, but he likes to chase balls. He escaped from his pen. He did not want to hurt you. He just wanted to play." The man said. Tim and Lily felt relieved. They smiled and said thank you to the man. The man said sorry again and took Rex away. Tim and Lily picked up their ball and hugged each other. They were happy and safe. They decided to play somewhere else. They left the tree and the dog behind. The park was quiet again. There was silence. But Tim and Lily were alert. They looked around and listened. They did not want to meet another dog. They wanted to have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Timmy. Timmy loved to play and exercise. Every day, he would run and jump in the park with his friends. But one day, he felt very miserable. He did not want to play or run anymore. Timmy's friend, a big cat named Kitty, saw him and asked, "Why are you so sad, Timmy?" Timmy replied, "I don't want to play anymore. I had a quarrel with my best friend, and now he won't play with me." Kitty said, "Don't be sad, Timmy. Let's go talk to your friend and fix the quarrel." So, Timmy and Kitty went to find Timmy's friend, a little bird named Bobby. They found Bobby sitting on a tree branch, looking sad too. Timmy said, "Bobby, I am sorry for our quarrel. Can we be friends again?" Bobby replied, "I am sorry too, Timmy. I missed playing with you." They all smiled and hugged each other. From that day on, Timmy, Kitty, and Bobby played and exercised together every day. They promised never to quarrel again and to always be there for each other. And they all lived happily ever after.
