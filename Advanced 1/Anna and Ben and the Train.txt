Title: Anna and Ben and the Train

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big train set. The train set is modern. It has lights, sounds and buttons. Anna and Ben can make the train go fast or slow, stop or start, and change tracks. One day, their mom calls them for dinner. "Anna, Ben, come to the table. We have soup, bread and salad. And a special dessert for you!" Mom says. Anna and Ben are hungry. They run to the table and sit on their chairs. They eat their soup, bread and salad. They are good eaters. "Mom, what is the dessert?" Anna asks. "It is a surprise. You will see soon." Mom says. She goes to the kitchen and comes back with a big cake. The cake has chocolate, cream and strawberries. It looks yummy. Anna and Ben are happy. They clap their hands and say "Wow!" Mom cuts the cake and gives them each a slice. Anna and Ben take a bite and smile. The cake is delicious. They eat their cake and lick their fingers. They thank mom for the dessert. "Can we play with the train set now?" Ben asks. "Yes, you can. But first, help me clean the table and wash the dishes." Mom says. Anna and Ben nod. They help mom with the dishes. They are good helpers. Then they go back to their room and play with the train set. They lean over the table and watch the train go around. They press the buttons and make the train do different things. They have fun with their modern toy. They are happy twins.

Tim likes to create things with his blocks. He makes houses, cars, and animals. One day, he wants to create something new. He thinks of a jet. A jet is a big and fast thing that flies in the sky. He has seen jets in books and on TV. He gets his blocks and starts to create his jet. He uses long blocks for the wings and the tail. He uses round blocks for the wheels and the nose. He uses red blocks for the fire that comes out of the back. He is very proud of his jet. He shows it to his mom and dad. They say it is a great jet. Tim wants to fly his jet. He puts it on the floor and runs with it. He makes a loud noise like a jet. He pretends he is going to far and deep places. He goes to the jungle, the ocean, and the moon. He sees many things and has fun. He loves his jet. He thinks of what he will create next.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Steven who was always curious. One day, Steven decided he wanted to explore the big cave he saw nearby. So, he grabbed a flashlight and headed out into the wilderness. As he entered the big cave, he could see some strange shapes in the walls. "What could they be?" wondered Steven. He continued exploring and eventually he saw something he never expected: a big stage inside the cave! Steven was so excited that he wanted to perform on it. But then he saw a big bear sleeping on the stage. Steven knew that the bear could hurt him, so he was a bit scared. Nevertheless, Steven knew he couldn't let this opportunity pass by, so he gathered up his courage and whispered "Be quiet, bear!" He carefully walked up to the stage and started to perform a song. Suddenly, the bear opened its eyes and looked rather surprised. For a brief moment, Steven was scared. But then the bear showed its appreciation by nodding its head and joining in Steven's song. The two of them performed together for a while, and then the bear left. Steven smiled, happy that he had a wonderful experience in the big cave!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Joe. Joe was three years old, and he liked to explore the world. One day, Joe decided to go out and explore the earth. He ventured out onto the grassy fields, through the woodlands, and he even hiked up to the nearby hills. Joe stopped for a rest, and he looked out at the horizon. As he gazed, he started to wonder what else was out there. Suddenly, he realized there was more beyond the earth - he could see stars twinkling in the night sky. Joe was excited, and he started running back home. He was suddenly so eager to tell his family about his discovery. As he ran, he met his friend, who stopped Joe to ask where he was going in such a hurry. Joe told his friend about the stars he had seen, and his friend became very sad. Joe's friend told him that the stars were only visible because of the regular darkness of night. Joe was a bit confused, but he soon realized that if it wasn't for the night, the stars wouldn't shine so brightly. Joe thanked his friend and promised to keep exploring. Then, they both ran off to find more things to discover.
