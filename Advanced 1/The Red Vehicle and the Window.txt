Title: The Red Vehicle and the Window

Once, there was a small red vehicle. It was a very happy vehicle. It liked to go fast and play with other vehicles. One day, the sky was wet, and the ground was slippery. The red vehicle went too fast and it slipped. It fell and got hurt. The red vehicle was sad. It could not play with the other vehicles. It stayed in its house and looked out the window. The other vehicles came to see the sad red vehicle. They brought it a big, soft bandage. They helped the red vehicle feel better. Soon, the red vehicle was happy again, and they all played together.

Once upon a time, there was a modern town. In this town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim wanted to buy a toy car. He needed money to buy the toy car. His mom said, "If you help me, you will receive money." Tim helped his mom all day. He was very happy because he would receive money. He thought about the toy car he would buy. His mom gave him the money and said, "Now you can buy your toy car!" Tim went to the toy store. He saw the toy car he wanted. But then, he saw a girl crying. She wanted a toy doll, but she had no money. Tim thought about his toy car. He looked at the girl and said, "Here, take my money. You can buy your toy doll." The girl was very happy, and they both played together with their new toys.

One day, a little helicopter wanted to go high in the sky. It was a sunny day, and the little helicopter was very happy. It went up and up, higher than the trees and the birds. The little helicopter felt so free and loved going high. As the little helicopter went higher, it saw a big bird. The big bird said, "Hello, little helicopter! Why are you going so high?" The little helicopter replied, "I want to see the world from up here. It's so fun!" The big bird smiled and said, "Be careful not to go too high, or you might get lost." The little helicopter didn't listen to the big bird and kept going higher. Soon, it was very high and could not see the ground. The little helicopter felt scared and alone. It wished it had listened to the big bird. Finally, the little helicopter remembered what the big bird said and started to go down. It went lower and lower until it could see the ground again. The little helicopter was so happy to be safe and back with its friends. The moral of the story is to always listen to the advice of others and not to go too high or too far from what is safe.

Tom and Sue like to go to the park with Mom. They play on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They have fun with their friends and eat snacks. One day, they see a big pile of junk near the park. It has old cars, bikes, toys and clothes. Mom says it is ancient junk. She says people throw away things they do not want anymore. Tom and Sue are curious. They want to see the junk. They ask Mom if they can go. Mom says no. She says the junk is dirty and dangerous. She says they might get hurt or sick. But Tom and Sue do not listen. They wait until Mom is busy talking to a friend. Then they sneak away and go to the junk. They climb on the cars, ride the bikes, play with the toys and try on the clothes. They think they find treasures. But they are wrong. The junk is rusty, broken, sharp and smelly. They cut their hands, scratch their legs, tear their clothes and get mud on their faces. They feel bad and scared. Mom sees them and gets angry. She runs to them and takes them away from the junk. She scolds them and cleans them. She says they are naughty and foolish. She says they should never go to the junk again. Tom and Sue are sorry. They say they are sorry to Mom. They hug her and promise to obey her. They learn their lesson. They do not go to the junk anymore. They stay in the park and play safely. They are happy.
