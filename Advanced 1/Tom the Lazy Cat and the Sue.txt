Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He liked to sleep all day and did not want to play. One day, his friend, a little girl named Sue, came to visit. She brought her notebook with her. Sue said, "Tom, let's draw in my notebook!" But Tom did not want to draw. He just wanted to sleep. Sue felt sad and went to play alone. Later, Tom woke up and saw Sue having fun. He felt regret for not playing with her. He went to Sue and said, "I'm sorry. Can I draw with you now?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tom! Let's draw together!" So, they both drew in the notebook and had a great time. From that day on, Tom was not lazy anymore, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim was an ordinary boy who liked to play with his toys and eat his favorite snack, apples. One day, Tim was playing with his toys when he noticed a big bag of waste near the door. He called his mom and asked, "Mom, what is this waste doing here?" His mom replied, "That waste is for the trash truck. I will take it out soon." Tim nodded and went back to playing with his toys. Later, Tim heard a loud noise outside. He ran to the window and saw the trash truck close by. But something unexpected happened! The big bag of waste started to move and shake. Tim couldn't believe his eyes when a big, friendly dog jumped out of the bag! The dog wagged its tail and licked Tim's face. Tim's mom and dad were surprised too. They decided to keep the dog and name him Lucky. From that day on, Tim and Lucky became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a great big bean plant. The bean plant grew tall and strong, reaching high up into the sky. All the kids loved to play near the bean plant, and they would look up and wonder what was at the very top. One day, a sneaky fox came into the village. The fox saw the great big bean plant and decided to steal some beans. He climbed up the plant, taking beans as he went higher and higher. The kids saw the fox and started to shout, "Stop! Don't steal our beans!" The fox heard the kids and felt bad. He climbed back down the bean plant and said, "I'm sorry, kids. I didn't know these beans were so important to you. I won't steal them anymore." The kids forgave the fox, and they all became friends. From that day on, the fox promised never to steal again. The great big bean plant continued to grow, and the kids, along with their new friend, the fox, played happily under its big, green leaves. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful baker. He made dough every day. The dough would rise and become big. The baker was very happy. One day, a little boy came to the bakery. He saw the baker making dough. The boy was very curious. He asked the baker, "What are you doing?" The baker smiled and said, "I am making dough. It will rise and become big. Then, I can make yummy bread for everyone." The little boy was excited. He wanted to help the powerful baker. The baker let the boy help him. They made the dough together. The dough rose and became big. In the end, they made lots of yummy bread. Everyone in the town was happy. The powerful baker and the little boy became good friends. They made dough together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy vehicles all day long. He had a big red car, a shiny blue truck, and a fast green motorcycle. One day, Tim's mommy said, "Let's go to the park!" Tim was very excited. He wanted to take his vehicles with him. So, he put his red car, blue truck, and green motorcycle in a bag. Tim and his mommy walked to the park, holding hands. At the park, Tim played with his lively vehicles. He made the red car go up and down the slide, the blue truck go round and round the merry-go-round, and the green motorcycle zoom all over the playground. Tim had so much fun playing with his vehicles at the park.
