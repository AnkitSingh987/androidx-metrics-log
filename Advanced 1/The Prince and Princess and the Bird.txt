Title: The Prince and Princess and the Bird

Once upon a time there was a prince and a princess who lived in a green castle. The prince and princess were very sad and did not know why. Then, one day, the prince and princess heard a voice. The voice came from a tiny, green bird who sat on the castle window sill. The bird said, "You two should hope, and your sadness will go away!" At first, the prince and princess did not understand the bird's message. They asked the bird what they should do and how they could hope. The green bird said that they should follow it and they would find out the answer. The prince and princess followed the bird and soon they reached a lake. The lake was so large and still that they could see their reflection in it. The bird said "Hope is always within you! Just take a look at your reflection in the lake and you will understand!" The prince and princess looked at the lake and for the first time, they felt happy. They had found the answer within themselves. They thanked the green bird and said goodbye, before walking back to their castle with hope in their hearts.

Once upon a time, there was a tall man. The tall man had a big sack. In the sack, there were many onions. He liked onions very much. He carried the sack with him everywhere he went. One day, the tall man went for a walk. He saw a big tree. He put his sack of onions under the tree and sat down. He was very tired. He closed his eyes and went to sleep. While the tall man was sleeping, a small dog came by. The dog smelled the onions in the sack. The dog liked onions too. So, the dog took the sack and ran away. When the tall man woke up, his sack was gone. He was very sad. But then, he saw the dog with his sack of onions. The dog came back and gave the sack to the tall man. They both smiled and became friends. From that day on, they shared the onions and had fun together.

One gloomy day, Sally wanted to make something special. She saw a strawberry and thought of an idea. "I can print a picture of this strawberry," she said. Sally grabbed a piece of paper and a pen. She drew a picture of the strawberry and then printed it. When she was finished, she had a very nice picture. Sally was very excited about her creation. She hung it up on the wall for everyone to see. Everyone was very impressed by the picture. Sally smiled and felt proud of her work. She looked at the picture and thought of the strawberry. It was a happy and gloomy day.

Once upon a time, there was a big reef in the sea. On the reef, there lived a creative fish named Fin. Fin loved to make new things with the shells he found. One day, Fin saw another fish named Gilly. Gilly was playing with a pretty shell. Fin wanted the pretty shell too. Fin and Gilly began to quarrel. "This shell is mine!" said Fin. "No, it's mine!" said Gilly. A wise old turtle saw them quarrel. He said, "Why don't you both be friends and share the pretty shell?" Fin and Gilly thought about it. They stopped the quarrel and became friends. They played together with the pretty shell and had lots of fun.

Jim and Tim both wanted the new jeep. Jim had his eye set on it for a long time. So when the jeep arrived at the car lot, he was so excited. He told Tim that it was his and that he should get another car. Tim, feeling jealous, lied to Jim and said that he wanted the jeep too. Jim was so surprised but decided to give it to Tim. He told him they could share it. The boys enjoyed the jeep for a while. But soon, Jim noticed that Tim seemed to use the jeep more and more. When he asked him why, Tim lied again. So Jim decided not to share the jeep with him anymore. In the end, the boys had a big fight because of Tim's lying. They stopped being friends and the jeep was put away. The moral of the story is that lying can ruin relationships. It's always better to be honest and tell the truth.
