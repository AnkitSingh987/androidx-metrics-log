Title: The Brown Crocodile and the River

Once there was a brown crocodile, he liked to show off to all his friends. All his other reptile friends were so very impressed with him. He would show them how low he could go in the river and then how high he could jump out. Everyone was amazed. His best friends were two turtles and they loved to watch him show his tricks. He would dive underwater and back up again so quickly that they would be squealing with delight. He even showed them his huge mouth full of sharp crocodile teeth! The turtles were always a bit scared of those teeth, but they loved to watch him show them off. One day, the brown crocodile wanted to really show his friends something special. He wanted to show them how he could swim all the way across the lake! So he took a deep breath and started swimming. He swam and swam for what felt like forever, but finally he made it to the other side! His friends were so proud of him and clapped and cheered for him. It was the best day ever!

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big aeroplane in the sky. He was very happy and wanted to fly like the aeroplane. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I want to be an aeroplane and fly high in the sky!" Mom smiled and said, "You can pretend to be an aeroplane, Tim. Just flap your arms and run around. You will feel like you are flying." Tim was excited and started to flap his arms. He ran around the yard, pretending to be an aeroplane. As he played, he saw a bug on a leaf. Tim wanted to say hello to the bug, so he stopped running and tried to give the bug a high-five. But instead, he accidentally gave the bug a little slap. The bug was okay, but it flew away. Tim felt sad that the bug flew away. He told his mom what happened. Mom said, "It's okay, Tim. Bugs are very small and sometimes we don't see them. Just be more careful next time." Tim played more and was very careful. He felt fit and happy, running around like an aeroplane. He knew that he could play and have fun, as long as he was careful with his new bug friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fish who loved to spin. Every day, the fish would spin in the water and have lots of fun. All the other fish would watch and clap their fins. One day, the fish was very tired. He did not want to spin. But all the other fish asked him to spin. The tired fish tried to spin, but he could not. The other fish were sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The tired fish started to grow legs! The fish was not a fish anymore. He was a frog! Now, the frog could jump and play with the other fish. The other fish were happy and clapped their fins. The frog was happy too, even if he could not spin anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a new toy car. Tom loved to play with his car every day. One day, his mom told him, "Tom, I have to warn you. Do not play with your car near the big tree on Wednesday." Wednesday came, and Tom forgot his mom's warning. He took his new car outside to play. The big tree looked fun to play near, so Tom went close to it. He did not see the big hole by the tree. Suddenly, Tom's car fell into the big hole. Tom was very sad. He remembered his mom's warning about the tree. Tom learned to listen to his mom's warnings from that day on.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his toy truck. One day, while playing, he found a big hole in the ground. Tim thought, "I wonder if my truck will fit in the hole." So, he tried to take his truck and put it in the hole. To his surprise, it fit just right! Just then, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. Sue saw the hole and asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim replied, "I found this hole and my truck fits in it!" Sue laughed and said, "That's so cool!" They played together with the hole and the truck for the rest of the day, having lots of fun.
