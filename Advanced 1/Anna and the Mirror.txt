Title: Anna and the Mirror

Anna likes fashion. She likes to wear different clothes and colors. She likes to make hats and scarves with her mom. She likes to look at pictures of fashion in books and magazines. One day, Anna and her mom visit her aunt. Her aunt lives in a big city. She works in fashion. She makes clothes for people who are on TV and in movies. She has a lot of unusual clothes in her house. Some are very shiny, some are very fluffy, some are very long, and some are very short. Anna is very curious. She wants to try on some of the unusual clothes. She asks her aunt if she can. Her aunt says yes, but she has to be careful. She says some of the clothes are very expensive and delicate. She says Anna can only wear them for a little while. Anna is very happy. She picks a dress that is very shiny and has a lot of sequins. She puts it on and looks at herself in the mirror. She feels like a princess. She picks a hat that is very fluffy and has a feather. She puts it on and feels like a bird. She picks a pair of shoes that are very long and have heels. She puts them on and feels very tall. Anna walks around the house in her unusual clothes. She pretends she is on TV and in a movie. She smiles and waves at her mom and her aunt. They smile and clap for her. They tell her she looks very beautiful and very fashionable. Anna feels very proud and very happy. She loves fashion. She loves to visit her aunt.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to improve his toy car. He thought it would be fun to make it go faster. He asked his mom for help, "Mom, can you help me make my car better?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's see what we can do." They looked around the house for things to use. They found an empty box and some big rubber bands. Tim's mom showed him how to use the rubber bands to make the car go faster. They put the car in the empty box and pulled the rubber bands back. When they let go, the car zoomed out of the box! Tim was so happy with his improved car. He played with it all day long. He even let his little sister, Amy, play with it too. They had a great day, and Tim learned that he could improve his toys and have even more fun. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Thank you, Mom, for helping me improve my car. I had the best day!" His mom hugged him and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad you had fun. Now, let's stay inside and get ready for bed."

Once upon a time, there was a calm girl named Lily. She loved to collect pretty things. One day, Lily found a beautiful veil in the park. It was soft and shiny. Lily wanted to keep the veil. But there was a boy named Tim who also found the veil. Tim wanted the veil too. Lily and Tim did not know what to do. They both wanted the veil, but they did not want to fight. So, Lily and Tim decided to share the veil. They took turns wearing it and playing with it. They became good friends and played together every day. And they both were happy with their pretty veil.

Johnny and his mom were walking in the park one day. They saw a soccer ball; it was blue and round. Johnny wanted to kick it. "No, Johnny," his mom said. "We don't kick soccer balls without permission." Johnny was angry. He didn't understand why he couldn't kick the ball. He kicked anyway and it flew across the park. An old man saw what happened. He was very mad. "That was hard," he said. He went over to Johnny and said, "I want a reward if you don't kick anymore." Johnny didn't understand what "reward" meant. His mom asked him if he wanted a reward. Johnny agreed and the old man gave him a chocolate bar. Johnny was happy but his mom was sad. She knew that the old man was still mad and he might want more rewards. Johnny didn't understand why his mother was worried. In the end, Johnny never got another reward. Whenever he wanted to kick something, his mom said no. Johnny felt bad and his mom felt sad.
