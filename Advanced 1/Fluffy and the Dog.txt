Title: Fluffy and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play outside with his ball. One day, Fluffy saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Fluffy wanted to help Lily. He went to look for the toy. Fluffy found a big green bottle. The bottle had a word on it, "oxygen." Fluffy didn't know what it was, but he thought it might make Lily happy. Fluffy offered the bottle to Lily. Lily saw the bottle and started to laugh. She said, "Thank you, Fluffy, but that's not my toy! That's for people who need help to breathe." Just then, they saw the toy behind the bottle. Lily was so happy! Fluffy was glad he could help, even if it was not what he thought.

Jimmy was out playing in the garden. He loved to rock in the chair, feeling the sunshine and the peace of being outdoors. Suddenly, he saw something spicy in the tree. It was a bright, round fruit. Jimmy asked his Mum, "What is it?" His Mum replied, "It's a chili pepper. Don't touch it, it's too spicy for you." But Jimmy didn't listen. He grabbed the pepper and ate it. It was hot, like fire! Jimmy screamed. His Mum quickly ran to him. But it was too late. The pepper was too spicy and Jimmy's throat burned. Jimmy's Mum was very sad. Nothing could help. Jimmy was very quiet and soon he didn't rock in the chair anymore. The peace he felt earlier was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter who lived in a small village. He loved to go hunting and provide food for the village. One day, the hunter decided to go hunting and he prepared for his journey. When he was about to leave, the hunter heard a little voice calling after him, "Wait, Hunter!" He stopped and turned around to see who was there. It was only a little girl. She asked him "Where are you going?" The hunter replied, "I'm going hunting. It's the only way to provide food for our village." The little girl smiled happily and said, "I know what you should take with you! You should take healthy things, like fruits and vegetables. That will give you energy during your journey." The hunter listened and took her advice. He loaded his bag with a variety of fruits and vegetables. On his journey, he felt full of energy and the animals he hunted found quickly. The hunter ended his journey with a full bag of food. He learned a lesson that day, that it is not just enough to prepare your journey physically but also mentally and healthily.

Ben and Mia were playing in the backyard. They saw a big pile of lumber that Dad had brought for building a shed. Ben wanted to touch the lumber. He thought it looked fun and hard. "Can we touch the lumber, Mia?" he asked his sister. "No, Ben. Dad said no. It is not safe. The lumber can hurt us. It has sharp edges and splinters. We can play with something else," Mia said. But Ben did not listen. He was careless. He ran to the lumber and touched it with his hand. He felt a pain in his finger. He had a splinter. He cried out loud. "Ow! Ow! Ow! It hurts! It hurts!" he shouted. Mia heard him and ran to him. She saw the splinter in his finger. She felt sorry for him. She hugged him and said, "I told you not to touch the lumber, Ben. You were careless. Let me help you. We have to go to Mom. She can take out the splinter and make it better." She took his hand and led him to the house. Mom saw them and asked what happened. Mia told her everything. Mom took out the splinter and put a band-aid on Ben's finger. She kissed him and said, "You have to be careful, Ben. You have to listen to Mia. She is a good sister. She knows what is safe and what is not. Next time, do not touch the lumber, okay?" Ben nodded and said, "Okay, Mom. I am sorry. I will not touch the lumber again. Thank you, Mia. You are a good sister. I love you." Mia smiled and said, "I love you too, Ben. Come on, let's go play with something else."
