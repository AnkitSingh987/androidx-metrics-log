Title: Max the Loyal Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a loyal dog named Max. Max loved his family very much. He liked to play with the little girl, Jane. They would run and play all day. One day, Jane's mom mentioned that they were going to the park. Max was very excited. He wanted to run and play with Jane in the park. When they got to the park, Jane let Max off his leash. Max was so happy. He ran and ran. Max saw a squirrel and wanted to play with it. He ran after the squirrel and did not see Jane. Jane called for Max, but he did not come back. Jane was very sad. Max could not escape from the squirrel. He was lost and could not find his way back home. "Max, where are you?" Jane cried. But Max was too far away to hear her. He was scared and missed his family. Jane and her mom looked for Max, but they could not find him. The sky got dark, and it was time to go home. Jane and her mom had to leave the park without Max. They were very sad. Max was still lost and could not find his way back home.

Once upon a time, there were two children called John and Susie. They were very helpful and always shared their favourite things. One day, John found a box of delicious chocolate. He was so happy that he decided to share it with Susie. But he was feeling greedy, so he only gave Susie a small piece. Susie was so angry that she started to cry. John saw the sunshine outside and said that if he came out with him it would help her feel better. Susie agreed, and they stepped out into the sunshine. Susie forgot about the chocolate and smiled. Suddenly, John grabbed the rest of the chocolate and ran away from Susie! She chased him but she wasn't fast enough. Susie felt so sad that the sunshine didn't shine anymore. She realised that John wasn't as helpful as she thought. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a man and his wife. They loved each other very much. The man liked to jump and spring around. His wife thought it was funny and would laugh. One day, the man said, "Wife, let's go outside and play." His wife replied, "Yes, that sounds fine!" So, they went outside to have fun. The man would spring high into the air, and his wife would clap and cheer. As they played, the sun began to set. The man and his wife felt happy and tired. They went back to their small house, hand in hand. The man said, "Wife, today was a fine day." His wife smiled and said, "Yes, it was. I love you." They hugged and went to sleep, ready for another day of fun and love.

Once upon a time, in a small home, there was a lonely book. The book was a novel. It sat on a shelf all by itself. The other books did not want to be near it. The lonely novel was sad. One day, a happy blanket saw the lonely novel. The blanket wanted to help. So, the blanket went to the novel and said, "Why are you sad, friend?" The novel replied, "I am lonely. No one wants to be near me." The kind blanket wanted to help the lonely novel. It decided to cover the novel and give it a warm hug. The novel felt happy and loved. Soon, the other books saw how happy the novel was with the blanket. They wanted to be friends with the novel too. From that day on, the lonely novel was not lonely anymore. Everyone was friends and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with clay. He would put the clay on the table and make all kinds of shapes. He made a dog, a cat, and a big round ball. Tim was very happy when he played with his clay. One day, Tim could not find his clay. He looked under his bed, in his toy box, and behind the door. Tim was upset because he wanted to play with his clay. He asked his mom for help. Mom looked everywhere too, but they still could not find the clay. Finally, Tim's little sister, Sue, came into the room. She saw Tim and Mom looking for the clay. Sue said, "I know where the clay is!" She took Tim's hand and led him to the kitchen. There, on the table, was the clay! Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" Tim, Sue, and Mom played with the clay together and made even more fun shapes.
