Title: Sara and Ben and the Bird

Sara and Ben were playing in the backyard after dinner. They had a flashlight and a bag of crackers. They wanted to feed the birds and see them in the dark. "Look, there is a big bird on the fence!" Sara said. She pointed the flashlight at the bird and threw a cracker. The bird flew away. "Hey, don't scare the birds!" Ben said. He was angry at Sara. He wanted to feed the birds too. "Sorry, I just wanted to see it better," Sara said. She felt bad. She liked the birds too. They walked around the yard, looking for more birds. They saw a small bird on a branch. Ben threw a cracker and the bird ate it. "Good job, Ben!" Sara said. She smiled at Ben. She was happy for him. "Thank you, Sara!" Ben said. He smiled back at Sara. He was happy too. They saw another bird on the ground. It was slow and did not fly away. Sara and Ben went closer to it. They saw that it had a hurt wing. "Oh no, poor bird!" Sara said. She felt sad for the bird. "Maybe we can help it," Ben said. He had an idea. He took the flashlight and the bag of crackers. He said to Sara, "You stay here and watch the bird. I will go get Mommy. She will know what to do." "Okay, Ben. Hurry back!" Sara said. She stayed with the bird and talked to it softly. She hoped Mommy could help it. She hoped the bird would be okay.

Once upon a time, there was a kind king. The king had a magic touch. When he touched things, they became beautiful. The people in the kingdom loved the king. One day, a man became envious of the king. He wanted the magic touch too. The man thought of a plan to take the magic touch from the king. The man tried to trick the king, but the king was smart. The king touched the man's heart, and the man became kind. The man was not envious anymore. They became friends, and the kingdom was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a nice dress with big pockets. One day, she found some colorful seeds. She thought they were attractive, so she put them in her pocket. Lily went to her friend Tom and said, "Look at the pretty seeds I found!" Tom looked at the seeds and said, "Wow! Let's spread them in the garden!" So, they went to the garden and spread the seeds all around. After a few days, beautiful flowers grew in the garden. All the kids in the neighborhood came to see the flowers. They were so happy and played together every day. Lily and Tom's garden became the most attractive place to be.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tom wanted to study. He had a big book with lots of pictures. Tom liked to look at the pictures and learn new things. One day, Tom's friend, Sue, came over to play. "Tom, let's play!" said Sue. Tom said, "I want to study my big book first. Will you help me?" Sue agreed, and they sat down to look at the book together. They saw a picture of a tree with a tip. The tree was very tall, and the tip was high up in the sky. As they turned the pages, they found a picture of a dark night. The sky was full of stars, and the moon was shining bright. Tom and Sue liked looking at the stars together. They talked about how the stars were far away and how they twinkled in the dark sky. After they finished studying the big book, Tom and Sue went outside to play. They climbed the tall tree with the high tip and looked up at the dark sky. They pretended they were up in the stars, exploring the sky together. And they lived happily ever after.

Ben and Lily liked to go to the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, like monkeys, lions, and giraffes. But their favorite animal was the rhinoceros. The rhinoceros was big and gray, with a horn on its nose. It looked strong and funny. One day, they went to the zoo and saw something different. The rhinoceros had a baby! The baby was small and brown, with no horn yet. It stayed close to its mom and made cute noises. Ben and Lily were very happy to see the baby rhinoceros. They wanted to touch it and play with it. But their mom said no. She said the baby rhinoceros was new and shy, and needed time to grow and learn. She said they had to be quiet and gentle, and not scare the baby or its mom. She said they could come back another day and see how the baby was doing. Ben and Lily nodded. They understood. They said goodbye to the rhinoceros and the baby, and went to see other animals. They had fun, but they also thought about the baby rhinoceros. They hoped it would be happy and healthy. They decided to return to the zoo soon, and see the baby rhinoceros again. They wanted to be its friends. They thought the baby rhinoceros was different, but also very special.
