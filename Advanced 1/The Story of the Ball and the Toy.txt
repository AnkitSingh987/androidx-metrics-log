Title: The Story of the Ball and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a big, round toy with a horn. It liked to spin all day. The toy had a friend, a small, crazy bug. They played together every day. One day, the toy saw the crazy bug was sad. The bug lost its favorite ball. The toy wanted to help. It thought, "Maybe if I spin fast, I will find the ball." So, the toy spun and spun, but still, no ball. Then, the toy had an idea. It used its horn to make a loud sound. The sound went far, far away. Soon, the ball heard the sound and came bouncing back. The crazy bug was happy again. They played together and spun around all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had many leaves. One day, a dry leaf fell from the tree. The leaf wanted to be the best at everything. It was very competitive. The dry leaf saw a race. Many leaves were racing to see who was the fastest. The dry leaf joined the race too. The leaves raced and raced. The dry leaf was winning! But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the leaves away. The dry leaf was not the best anymore. The wind took the dry leaf back to the big tree. The dry leaf learned that it's okay not to win all the time.

Once there was a little girl named Chloe. She had a wild imagination and used it to design all kinds of fun things every day. One day Chloe used her imagination to design a purple door. She was so excited to have her own door and began running around showing everyone. Chloe's little brother, Justin, saw Chloe with her door and became jealous. He started to complain and grumble. "It's not fair!" Justin said. "Why do you get a door and I don't?" Chloe smiled at her brother and said "Don't worry Justin, I'll design you a door too.""Will you do it soon?" Justin asked. "Yes, I can do it this afternoon," Chloe said. Justin cheered with happiness and he and Chloe smiled at each other. Justin was very relieved that he would get a door too.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sally. Sally was always very naughty and didn't obey the rules. One day, Sally ran into a big, green field and started playing there. Sally's mum shouted loudly and told her to come back. But Sally was too busy playing and wasn't listening. After a while, Sally's mum found her in the field. She was very angry with Sally and said "You have been naughty and have disobeyed the rules. So now you must be punished!" Sally felt helpless and didn't know what to do. She started to cry as her mum scolded her. In the end, mum decided that Sally would have to stay in her room for the rest of the day, without her toys. Sally was very sad and knew that she had done wrong. She promised to always be good from then on.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She loved to print pictures with her mom. One day, Lisa found a weird box in her room. She opened it and saw a lot of pretty colors. It was powder! Lisa wanted to use the powder to make her pictures even more pretty. She took the powder and put it on her printed pictures. The powder made the pictures look very weird, but Lisa thought they were beautiful. Lisa showed her mom the weird pictures with the powder on them. Her mom smiled and said, "Wow, Lisa! You made your pictures so special!" Lisa was very happy and knew that her weird powder made her pictures the best.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a lonely boy named Tim. Tim had no friends to play with. One day, Tim found a pan in the kitchen. He thought it would be fun to play with the pan. Tim took the pan outside and saw a cat. The cat looked at the pan and said, "What is that? Can I play with it too?" Tim was happy to have a friend to play with. They played a game of catch with the pan. As they played, the pan flew high into the sky. When it came down, it hit a big tree. The tree started to tear. Tim and the cat were scared. They did not know what to do. But then, a friendly bird came out of the tree. The bird said, "Don't be scared. I can fix the tree." The bird fixed the tree and became friends with Tim and the cat. They all played together and were not lonely anymore.
