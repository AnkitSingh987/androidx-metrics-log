Title: The Ant and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little ant. The little ant was always impatient. He wanted to do everything fast. One day, he decided to build a network of tunnels under the ground. As the little ant started to dig, it began to rain. The rain made the ground wet and muddy. The little ant did not like the rain, but he kept digging. He wanted to finish his network of tunnels very quickly. After some time, the little ant was very tired. He realized that being impatient did not help him build the network. He decided to slow down and take his time. The little ant learned that it is better to be patient and do things well. And that is the moral of the story: being patient helps us do better work.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy horse named Bob. He lived in a stable on a big farm. Bob loved to travel and see new things. One day, Bob decided to travel far away from his stable. He walked and walked until he found a beautiful field with lots of flowers. Bob was very happy to be in this new place. After playing in the field, Bob started to miss his noisy stable. So, he traveled back home. When he got there, all his friends were happy to see him. Bob told them about his adventure and they all wanted to travel too. From that day on, Bob and his friends would travel together and have lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a small garden where she grew many things. One day, she found a bean. Sally was very happy. Sally showed the bean to her friend, Tom. Tom was jealous. He wanted a bean too. So, Sally and Tom went to examine the garden to find more beans. They found many beans in the garden. Sally and Tom were both happy. They took the beans and went home. Now, they could grow more beans together. And they were not jealous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a heavy anchor. The boat was in the sea. The sea had many fish. The fish were happy and played all day. One day, a new fish came to the sea. The new fish did not know much. The new fish was ignorant. The new fish saw the anchor and thought it was a toy. The new fish tried to play with the anchor, but it was too heavy. Near the sea, there was a garden. In the garden, there were many flowers. The flowers were pretty. One day, a new flower started to grow. The new flower was small and weak. The sun was warm, and the rain was soft. The new flower began to bloom. The other flowers were happy to see the new flower bloom. The fish saw the new flower and were happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bob. Bob lived in a pond with other fish. The pond was hot because the sun was shining on it all day long. Bob liked to dive deep in the water to find cool spots to play. One day, Bob saw a shiny chain at the bottom of the pond. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. So, Bob took a deep breath and dove down to get the chain. He picked it up with his mouth and swam back up to show Sam. "Look, Sam!" said Bob, "I found a shiny chain!" Sam looked at the chain and smiled. "Wow, Bob! That's so pretty!" said Sam. They both played with the chain all day, diving and swimming in the hot pond. And they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little alligator named Al. Al was very excited to play with his friends. They wanted to play a fun game called "steal the rock." In this game, Al and his friends took turns trying to steal a rock from each other. They laughed and ran around the pond. Al was very good at the game. He could grab the rock and run away fast. One day, while they were playing, Al saw a big alligator watching them. The big alligator wanted to play too. Al and his friends let the big alligator play, and they all had fun together. They learned that playing with new friends is a lot of fun.
