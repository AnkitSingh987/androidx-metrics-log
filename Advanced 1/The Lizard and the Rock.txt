Title: The Lizard and the Rock

One day, a little lizard went for a walk. The lizard was deaf, so he could not hear anything. He liked to think a lot and play in the sun. As the lizard walked, he saw a big rock. He thought it was a good place to play. But when he got closer, the rock began to move! The lizard was very surprised. The rock was not a rock at all. It was a big turtle who was sleeping! The turtle woke up and smiled at the lizard. They became friends and played together in the sun. The deaf lizard was happy to have a new friend who did not mind that he could not hear.

One day, Tom went to a big house with his mom. In the big house, there was an elevator. Tom had never seen an elevator before. His mom said, "Tom, let me introduce you to the elevator. It will take us up and down." Tom was a little scared. He looked at the elevator and saw a rough button. The button had a big arrow on it. His mom said, "When we push the rough button, the elevator will come. Let's push it together." They pushed the button, and the elevator came. They went inside the elevator. Tom's mom said, "Now we go up!" The elevator went up, and Tom felt like he was flying. They reached the top, and the elevator stopped. Tom smiled and said, "Mom, I like the elevator. Can we go again?" His mom smiled too and said, "Yes, we can go again." And they went up and down in the elevator, having a fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was restless. He wanted to help his mom in the kitchen. His mom was making food for the whole family. Tim asked, "Mom, can I help you?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. You can serve the food to everyone when it's ready." Tim was very happy. He waited for the food to be done. When the food was ready, he took the plates and started to serve the food. As Tim was serving the food, he saw a little bird fly into the house. The bird was hungry too. Tim thought, "I can share my food with the bird." So, he gave some food to the bird. The bird was very happy and surprised. The bird sang a beautiful song for Tim and his family. Everyone was happy and enjoyed the food together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big bin near the slide. It was broken. Tim wanted to fix it, so he tried to twist the broken part back into place. As he twisted the broken bin, something unexpected happened. A little dog jumped out of the bin! The dog was hiding inside because it was scared. Tim was very surprised. Tim and the little dog became friends. They played in the park all day. The dog was not scared anymore. Tim was happy he could help the dog and fix the broken bin.

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big pond with many ducks. They wanted to feed the ducks some bread, so they ran to the pond with a bag. But when they got to the pond, they saw a sign that said "No bread for ducks". They did not know what the sign meant, so they ignored it and threw some bread to the ducks. The ducks were happy and ate the bread. But soon, the ducks started to feel sick. They could not swim or fly well. They made sad noises and shivered in the cold water. Anna and Ben felt sorry for the ducks. They did not know they had hurt them. Then, they saw a lady who worked at the pond. She was wearing a hat and a coat. She had a bucket and a net. She was trying to help the ducks. She told Anna and Ben that bread was bad for ducks. It made them sick and fat. She said they should only give them seeds or lettuce. Anna and Ben felt ashamed. They said they were sorry and asked how they could help. The lady smiled and gave them some seeds and lettuce. She said they could feed the ducks with those instead. She said they were good friends for caring about the ducks. Anna and Ben fed the ducks with the seeds and lettuce. The ducks liked them and felt better. They swam and flew in the pond. They were graceful and happy. Anna and Ben watched them and smiled. They learned that they should always read and follow the signs. They also learned that they should be kind to animals and not give them things that hurt them. They hugged the lady and thanked her for teaching them. They promised to be good friends to the ducks.
