Title: Sue the Girl and the Ladder

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her yard. She saw a big tree with yummy pears on it. Sue wanted to eat a pear, but she was too small to reach them. Sue asked her friend Tim, "Can I borrow your ladder?" Tim said, "Yes, you can borrow it!" Sue was very happy. She got the ladder from Tim and put it by the tree. Sue climbed the ladder and picked a big, yummy pear. She shared the pear with Tim. They both ate the pear and smiled. Sue and Tim were very happy. They played together all day.

One day, a kind girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big gate. Lily wanted to see what was behind the gate. She pushed it open and went in. Inside, she found a small dog. The dog was sad. Lily wanted to help the dog. She offered the dog some of her snack. The dog ate it and was happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog started to grow bigger and bigger! It was not a dog, but a big, friendly bear! Lily was surprised but still liked her new friend. They played together and had lots of fun.

Tom and Lily went to the zoo with their mom. They saw many animals, but their favorite was the panda. The panda was black and white and very big. He was eating bamboo and playing with a ball. "Look, mom, the panda is so cute!" Lily said. "Yes, he is, but he is also very dirty. See how his fur is muddy and sticky?" Mom said. "Why is he dirty, mom?" Tom asked. "Because he lives in a place where it rains a lot and there is a lot of mud. He needs to clean himself sometimes, but he is too lazy or too weak to do it." Mom said. "Can we help him, mom? Can we give him a bath?" Lily asked. Mom smiled and said, "No, sweetie, we can't. The panda is not our pet, he is a wild animal. He belongs to the zoo, and the zookeepers take care of him. They know how to clean him and make him healthy and happy." "Oh, I see. But I wish he was cleaner. He would look even cuter." Lily said. "Maybe he will clean himself later, when he is not so hungry or tired. Or maybe he likes being dirty. He is still a beautiful animal, and we should respect him and his home." Mom said. Tom and Lily nodded and waved goodbye to the panda. They thanked mom for taking them to the zoo and learned a lot about animals. They also learned to be clean and not lazy or weak. They went home and took a bath and brushed their teeth. They felt good and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a yummy cookie. It was hanging on a tree. A big bird and a little mouse both wanted the cookie. The big bird tried to get the cookie, but it made a crack in the tree. The little mouse was scared. The big bird and the little mouse did not want to share the cookie. The big bird and the little mouse both pulled on the cookie. The crack in the tree got bigger. The tree started to fall down. The big bird and the little mouse stopped pulling. The big bird and the little mouse decided to share the yummy cookie. They broke the cookie into two pieces. They ate the cookie together and were happy. The tree did not fall down.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl called Maria. She lived in a modern, big city, surrounded by noise all day long. One day Maria decided to go on an adventure outside, so she put on her coat and went on her way. When Maria got out, the noise was so loud that it made her ears ache. But she kept walking, until she stumbled upon a park. It was beautiful, but the noise was still coming from the city! Maria wanted to stay but she was getting scared. She wanted to go back home but she couldn't, it felt like she was stuck. She looked around, desperate for help, and noticed an old man. He was sitting on a bench. Maria walked towards him and bravely asked him for help. He welcomed her with a warm smile but just as she was about to thank him, a group of strangers came in and began to yell at the old man. Maria was so scared that she began to cry. The strangers yelled even louder and the old man told her to run away. She did, but the struggle was over: they had stolen all of the old man's possessions. Maria was sad and angry. She returned home, feeling miserable, and never returned to that park again.
