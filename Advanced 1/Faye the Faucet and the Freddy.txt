Title: Faye the Faucet and the Freddy

Once upon a time, there was a pretty faucet named Faye. Faye was very proud of her shiny silver color. She lived in a big house with a nice family. One day, a new faucet named Freddy came to live in the house. Freddy was also proud of his shiny silver color. Faye and Freddy liked each other very much. They would talk and laugh all day long. One day, Faye said to Freddy, "I like you a lot. Let's marry and be happy together!" Freddy smiled and said, "Yes, I want to marry you too, Faye!" So Faye and Freddy married and became a happy faucet couple. They were very proud to be together and the family loved them very much.

One day, a brave boy named Tim went to a show. He was very happy. At the show, there was a band. They played music and everyone danced. While they danced, a big wind came. It blew the band's hats away. Everyone was sad. They stopped dancing. Tim wanted to help. Tim ran fast and caught all the hats. He gave them back to the band. The band was happy and played music again. But, the wind was not bad. It was a magic wind! When the band played with their hats, the music made everyone fly. Tim and his friends flew and danced in the sky.

One day, a strong man named Tom was walking in the park. He saw a big truck. The truck had oil. Tom said, "Hi, truck! You are very big and strong." The truck said, "Thank you, Tom! I am here to give oil to the park." But then, something unexpected happened. The truck's oil started to spill out. The truck began to panic. He said, "Oh no! My oil is spilling! What do I do?" Tom saw the truck was in trouble. He thought fast. He found a big rock and put it on the hole where the oil was spilling. The oil stopped spilling. The truck was happy. He said, "Thank you, Tom! You are very strong and smart. You saved the day!" And they both smiled, happy that everything was okay again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Susie. She liked to play in the garden and have fun. The sun was shining very brightly in the sky and the flowers were light and colourful. One day, Susie decided to play a game of pretend. She pretended that she was a grown up and made herself some tea. She put a teaspoon of sugar in it and stirred it with a spoon. But something bad happened – her pretend friends began to ignore her. They all sat around the table and talked to each other but nobody talked to her. Susie was sad, so she put away her pretend tea and went back out into the garden. She found a little patch of sun and curled up in it. After a while, the warm sun began to make Susie feel better. She forgot all about being ignored and smiled in the light.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Spot found a small house. Inside the house, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was sad because she had no friends to play with. Spot wanted to make Fluffy glad. So, Spot asked Fluffy to come outside and play. Fluffy liked the idea and they played together in the sun. They played catch and had fun. Spot and Fluffy soon became best friends. After a long day of play, Spot and Fluffy were tired. They both went back to the small house for a stay. They were glad to have each other as friends. Spot and Fluffy learned to value their friendship and knew that they would always be there for each other.

There were two friends called Tommy and Sarah. Tommy had some gum. He said to Sarah, "Would you like an add of my gum?" Sarah was excited and said, "Yes please!" So Tommy gave her the gum and they both started chewing. Suddenly, Tommy saw something very busy. It was a nasty dog! The dog grabbed the gum from both of them and ran away with it. Tommy and Sarah were very sad. They didn't have any gum left. They looked at each other and started to cry. They knew the gum was gone for good. Their plan for a fun day didn't come true.
