Title: Jimmy and the Ships

Jimmy was playing with his toy navy ships in the bathtub. He loved pretending the ships were sailing around the tub and crashing into each other. Suddenly his sister Susie came into the bathroom. She saw the navy ships and wanted to play with them. Jimmy panicked and grabbed them away. He was jealous and didn't want to share his fun with Susie. Jimmy was so scared Susie was going to take them away. He ran out of the bathroom with the navy ships and hid them in his closet.

Once upon a time, there was a purple cat. This cat loved to follow a little boy. The little boy's name was Tim. Tim and the purple cat were best friends. They played games and had fun every day. One sunny day, Tim found a shiny coin on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to the purple cat. They were very happy. They wanted to buy a big ice cream with the coin. So, they went to the ice cream shop. At the ice cream shop, something unexpected happened. The coin started to talk! The coin said, "I am a magic coin. I can make one wish come true." Tim and the purple cat were very surprised. They wished for a big ice cream mountain to share. The magic coin made their wish come true. Tim and the purple cat ate ice cream and played all day long. They were very happy and had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a green avocado. Tim loved his avocado so much. He played with it every day. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. Sue saw the avocado and wanted to have it too. "Can I have the avocado?" she asked. Tim was upset. He did not want to share his avocado. Tim and Sue talked about it. They decided to cut the avocado in half. They both had a piece of avocado now. Tim was not upset anymore. They played together and had fun with their avocado halves. And they became best friends forever.

Once there was a little animal. This animal had a broken leg. It hurt a lot. The animal wanted to get better. One day, the animal tried to test its leg. It tried to walk, but it was still hard. The animal was sad. It wanted to play with its friends. The animal tried again and again to walk. But each time, it fell down. The animal's leg did not get better. It was still broken. The animal could not play with its friends. It was a sad ending for the little animal.

Once upon a time, there was a creative prince who lived in a big castle. He loved to take walks in the forest and look at the pretty flowers and tall trees. He liked to make new things with sticks and leaves he found on his walks. One day, the prince found a small bird that could not fly. He wanted to help the bird, so he took it home to the castle. The prince used his creative mind to make a soft bed for the bird with leaves and sticks. As the days went by, the bird got better and could fly again. The prince took the bird back to the forest and let it go. The bird flew high in the sky and sang a happy song. The creative prince smiled and knew he had done a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named Tim. He had a big, shiny helmet that he loved to wear. He would put it on every day and pretend to be a hero. Tim wanted to be brave like the heroes he admired. One sunny day, Tim saw a little girl named Sue who was sad. She had lost her toy in a tall tree. Tim wanted to help her. He put on his helmet and climbed the tree. It was hard work, but he was brave and did not give up. Tim got the toy and gave it back to Sue. She was so happy and thanked him. Tim felt proud and learned that being brave and helping others is what makes a true hero. Everyone admired Tim for his kind heart and bravery.
