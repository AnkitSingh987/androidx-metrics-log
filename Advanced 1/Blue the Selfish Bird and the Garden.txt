Title: Blue the Selfish Bird and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a selfish bird named Blue. Blue did not like to share his food with anyone. One sunny day, Blue found a tasty insect to eat. He was very happy and did not want to share it with his friends. As Blue was about to eat the insect, he saw that it was stuck in a spider's web. The insect was very scared and needed help. Blue thought about being selfish but then decided to rescue the insect. He used his strong beak to break the web and set the insect free. The insect was very thankful and became friends with Blue. They played together in the garden every day. Blue learned that sharing and helping others was more fun than being selfish. He became a kind and happy bird, loved by all his friends in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a big fair event in a small town. Many kids and their moms and dads went to the fair to have fun. They saw a big wheel, a puppet show, and many games to play. Everyone was happy and excited. At the fair, there was a kind man who sold balloons. The man had red, blue, yellow, and green balloons. The kids loved the balloons and wanted to take one home. The man said, "You need to pay me one coin for a balloon." A little boy named Tim wanted a blue balloon but did not have a coin to pay. Tim was sad. His friend, Sara, saw him and wanted to help. Sara had two coins, so she decided to pay one coin for her balloon and one coin for Tim's balloon. Tim was very happy and thankful to Sara. The kids at the fair learned that it is good to help others when they need it. They also learned that it is important to pay for things at the event. Everyone had a great time at the fair and went home with big smiles on their faces.

Molly and her mommy were visiting the park. While walking, Molly spotted something green, prickly and small. Molly asked, "What is dat?" Her mommy replied, "That is a cactus. They have long spikes and small flowers." Molly was very curious and wanted to get closer. She asked, "Can I have dat one, please?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, you can have it. You can take it home and take care of it. Make sure you give it lots of sunlight and water." Molly was so excited! She carefully picked up the cactus, and smelled it. It had a sweet smell. She said, "I will love it and take care of it always!" Her mommy smiled and said, "I'm sure you will take good care of it."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to run and play in the park. One day, Tim saw a small, slow bug. The bug was crawling on his finger. Tim thought the bug was funny and wanted to show it to his mom. Tim ran to his mom, but he fell. His finger suffered a little hurt. Tim cried and his mom came to help him. She looked at his finger and gave him a hug. She said, "Don't worry, you will be okay." After a while, Tim felt better. He saw the slow bug again and smiled. He was more careful this time. He showed the bug to his mom and they both laughed. Tim learned to be careful when playing with bugs.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She was a great girl, and she always liked to play. One day, Sue went to the park with her mom. Sue wanted to start playing, but first her mom told her to stretch. Sue didn’t want to stop playing, so she said to her mom, “Why should I do that? I just want to play!” Her mom smiled and told Sue, “Stretching is important. It will help you play better. Plus it’s a great way to get started.” So Sue listened to her mom and stretched for a few minutes. When she was done stretching, she felt ready to play. She ran around the park, chasing her friends and having a great time. At the end of the day, Sue realized that stretching before she started playing really did help. From then on, she always stretched before she started playing, and she had lots of fun! The moral of this story is that taking the time to stretch before you start playing is a great way to get ready. Stretching helps you do your best, and it makes playtime more fun!
