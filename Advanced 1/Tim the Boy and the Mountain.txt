Title: Tim the Boy and the Mountain

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to go to the mountain. He asked his mom, "Can I go to the mountain?" His mom said, "Yes, but be back by dinner." Tim started his walk to the mountain. It was a regular day with the sun shining bright. As he walked, he saw a small rabbit. The rabbit said, "I want to go to the mountain too!" So, Tim and the rabbit walked together. When they got to the mountain, they saw a big door. Tim and the rabbit went inside. They found a big room full of toys! It was not a regular mountain after all. They played with the toys all day. When it was time for dinner, Tim and the rabbit went back home, happy with their new secret place.

John was walking down the street when he saw a fancy shop. He wanted to look inside but the door was closed, so he decided to come back later. A few days passed and finally, one day he decided to go back to the shop. When he arrived, a sign on the door said, "Here you can write something special". John had never done that before and he was very excited to try! He walked into the shop and saw lots of colourful pens, fancy notebooks and special paper. John chose a special notebook and he started to write his special story. It took him a few hours but when he finished he was so proud that he had written something all on his own. John shared his story with all his friends and they thought it was very special. John realised that it was worth the wait to go back to the shop and write something special!

One day, a boy named Tim saw an unusual big truck. The truck was going to space! Tim wanted to go too. He asked the truck driver, "Can I go to space with you?" The truck driver said, "Yes, but first we need to load the truck with things for space." So, Tim and the driver loaded the truck with food, toys, and clothes. They were ready to go up, up, up to space. But when they started the truck, something unexpected happened. The truck turned into a big, friendly dragon! The dragon said, "Climb on my back, Tim and driver! I will take you to space!" So, Tim and the driver went to space on the dragon, and they had the best time ever.

Lila and Tom were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but they always wanted more. They were spoiled children who did not share or say thank you. One day, their mom and dad took them to a big park. The park had a lot of trees, flowers, and animals. It also had a big hill that looked like a volcano. Lila and Tom wanted to go to the volcano, but their mom and dad said no. They said it was too far and too hot. Lila and Tom did not listen. They ran away from their mom and dad and went to the volcano. They thought they would find more toys there. They climbed the hill and looked inside the hole. They saw red and orange and yellow. They thought it was candy. They reached out to grab some, but they burned their hands. They screamed and cried. The volcano was not candy. It was fire and rocks. It was angry and loud. It did not provide toys. It provided danger. Their mom and dad heard them and ran to help them. They hugged them and took them to a safe place. They put cold water on their hands and gave them medicine. They also gave them a big scold. They told them they were naughty and foolish. They told them they should be happy with what they have and not be greedy. Lila and Tom felt sorry and ashamed. They said sorry to their mom and dad. They said they learned their lesson. They said they would not go to the volcano again. They said they would be good and share their toys. They said they loved their mom and dad. Their mom and dad smiled and kissed them. They said they loved them too. They said they were glad they were safe. They said they would still have fun at the park. They said they would show them the trees, the flowers, and the animals. They said they would provide love and fun. They said they were a happy family.
