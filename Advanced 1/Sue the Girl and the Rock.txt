Title: Sue the Girl and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to collect things. She collected rocks, leaves, and sticks. One sunny day, Sue went for a walk in the park with her mom. While walking, Sue found a brush on the ground. The brush was very old and dirty. Sue picked it up and said, "Mom, can I keep this brush? I want to add it to my collection!" Her mom looked at the brush and said, "Sue, that brush is dead. It won't work anymore. Let's leave it here and find something else for your collection." Sue put the dead brush back on the ground. She kept walking with her mom and found a pretty, shiny rock. Sue was very happy with her new rock and added it to her collection. She forgot all about the dead brush, and she and her mom had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there were two pets called Max and Ruby. They were best friends and did everything together. One day they decided to go exploring in the woods near their house. Max said, "I wonder what we'll discover". As they walked, they saw something amazing. It was an enormous camera lying on the ground. Ruby said, "Wow! Let's pick it up and take a look". They carefully picked up the camera and found that it was in perfect condition. Max said, "Let's keep it, it's ours now". Ruby disagreed and said, "No, this belongs to someone else. We can't take it as it's not ours." Max thought for a moment and then agreed. They hung the camera back up on a nearby tree, so the owner could find it. Max and Ruby were very pleased with themselves for doing the right thing. It showed them the importance of respecting other peoples property.

Once upon a time there was a monkey called Frank. Frank was a very happy monkey and loved painting. Every day he would take his paints and papers and paint pictures of every kind: birds, trees and people. One day, Frank painted a picture of a bird with a very long tail. Whilst he was painting, an evil bird flew by and snatched the tail off the picture. Poor Frank was so sad and helpless that he couldn't do anything. Just then, a little friendly bird called Bob came to help Frank. Bob said, "I can help you get your tail back." Frank smiled, he was so happy. So Bob flew off to find the evil bird, and after a long search he finally spotted the tail. He flew as fast as he could, grabbed the tail and brought it back to Frank. Frank had his tail back and was so excited! He thanked Bob with a big hug and went on to paint even more pictures with his beautiful tail.

One day, a fireman named Sam saw a naughty dog. The dog was running and barking near a big red sign. The sign said, "Do not play here." Sam wanted to help the dog. Sam went to the dog and said, "Hey, little dog, you should not be here. It is not safe." The dog looked at Sam and barked. The dog did not want to listen to the fireman. Sam tried to catch the naughty dog, but the dog was too fast. The dog ran into a small house. The house had a fire! Sam could not save the dog. The naughty dog should have listened to the fireman.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big, black piece of coal. All his friends were envious of his coal. They wanted it too. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, asked him, "Can I hold your coal, please?" Tim said, "No, it's mine!" Sam was sad and walked away. Tim saw that his friend was sad, and he felt bad. Later, Tim went to Sam and said, "I'm sorry. You can hold my coal." Sam was happy and held the coal. They shared and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy named Bunny. Tim and Bunny were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Tim found a pair of scissors on the table. He thought it would be fun to play with them. Tim started to tease Bunny with the scissors. He pretended to cut Bunny's ears. Bunny did not like this game. Bunny felt scared. He tried to tell Tim that he was being naughty. But Tim did not listen. He kept teasing Bunny with the scissors. Bunny decided to hide from Tim. He jumped under the bed where Tim could not find him. Tim felt sad and lonely. He missed his best friend. Tim's mom saw him feeling sad and asked what was wrong. Tim told her about teasing Bunny with the scissors. His mom told him that it was naughty to do that. Tim said he was sorry and promised not to tease Bunny again. He put the scissors back on the table. Then, he found Bunny under the bed and gave him a big hug. Bunny forgave Tim, and they played happily together again.
