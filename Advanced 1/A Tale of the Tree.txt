Title: A Tale of the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree. The tree had a job to make everyone smile. Every day, kids would come to play near the tree. The tree loved to see them happy. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird was sad. The tree wanted to make the bird smile too. So, the tree danced and shook its branches. The bird started to smile and sing. The kids saw the bird and the tree. They clapped and laughed. Now, the tree and the bird had a new job: to make everyone smile together. And they were very good at it.

Mommy was walking down a dark street. She heard a sound that made her heart glow. It was a spirit. Mommy knew she must follow it. She followed the spirit until she reached an old house. The walls looked so bitter and cold. Mommy knew the spirit was inside and knocked on the door. Suddenly, the door opened and a voice said "Come in". Mommy stepped inside and the door shut behind her. The voice said, "You must stay here". Mommy felt so scared. Then, the spirit giggled and flew away. Mommy was trapped in the old house. She cried and shouted, but no one could help her. She never returned home and was trapped in the old house forever.

One day, a big, ugly frog named Bobo lived near a pond. He had many friends, but one day, he saw a pretty tea cup near the pond. He wanted it, but it was not his. He felt an urge to take it. Bobo's friend, a small bird named Lulu, saw him looking at the tea cup. Lulu knew it was wrong to take things that were not yours. She told Bobo, "We should find who owns the tea cup and give it back." But Bobo did not want to give the tea cup back. He wanted it for himself. This made Lulu sad, and they began to fight. Lulu did not want to be friends with someone who took things. Bobo thought about what Lulu said. He did not want to lose his friend. So, he agreed to find the owner of the tea cup. They asked all their friends near the pond, and finally found the owner, a kind turtle named Toto. Toto was happy to have his tea cup back, and Bobo learned that it was better to do the right thing. Lulu and Bobo stayed friends, and they all had a tea party by the pond to celebrate.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. Anna was very independent for her age. She was only three years old, but she loved to do things all by herself. One day, Anna's mum had to go to the market to sell some of her things. She asked Anna to come along, so Anna put on her shoes and followed her mum. When they got to the market, Anna ran around and looked at all the things that were being sold. She saw someone was selling a big bear toy and asked her mum if she could buy it. They decided to buy it together. Once they got home, Anna was so excited to play with her new toy. But when she opened it up, she noticed something wasn’t right. Inside the bear was a little doll! It was fighting with Anna's new toy bear. Anna was very sad. Her mum noticed Anna's sad face and gave her a hug. She explained to Anna that even though it can be hard, sometimes things don't go as planned. But Anna had been so independent, so her mum knew she could get through it. Anna smiled and hugged her mum back. She was so independent, she knew she could get through anything!

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily was happy. She liked to run fast. One day, there was a big race. Lily wanted to win a medal. The race started. Lily ran very fast. But, oh no! Lily saw a cat in the way. Lily did not want to hurt the cat. She stopped and picked up the cat. Lily started to panic. She thought she could not win now. But something unexpected happened. Everyone else in the race stopped to help Lily and the cat. They all crossed the finish line together. They all got medals. Lily was happy. The cat was happy too.
