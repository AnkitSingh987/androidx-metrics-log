Title: Wally and the Pastry

Wally and his friends wanted to go out and play but it was raining outside. They were disappointed. Suddenly, Wally remembered something. He remembered that his mom had bought him a pastry. Wally was excited and he ran to his mom. His mom said it was OK and she gave Wally the pastry. His friends were all very excited. They quickly found a safe place to eat the pastry. But the pastry was hard to eat because it was dry. Wally's friends started to get mad. Wally tried to think of something to make it more fun. Then, he had an idea. Wally ran to the kitchen and found some honey. He put it on the pastry and his friends were thrilled. They all ate the sweet honey pastry in a safe and dry place.

Once upon a time, there was a big sun. Every day, the sun would rise in the sky. It would make a zigzag path as it moved. The sun was very patient as it went up and down. One day, a little girl named Jane saw the sun. She said, "Hi, Sun! Why do you rise and make a zigzag path?" The sun smiled and said, "I do this to make the day fun and bright for everyone." But one day, something unexpected happened. The sun did not rise. The sky stayed dark. Jane was sad and asked, "Sun, where are you?" The sun answered, "I am here, but I am hiding. I wanted to surprise you and make today different." Jane was happy to hear the sun, and she laughed. The sun came out, and it was a bright and fun day again.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a fancy card on the ground. She picked it up and stared at it. The card had a picture of a big, shiny star. Mia liked the star very much. She wanted to show it to her friend, Tim. Mia went to Tim's house and said, "Look, Tim! I found a fancy card with a big star on it!" Tim stared at the card and said, "Wow, that's pretty! Can I have it?" Mia thought for a moment and said, "Okay, you can have it." Just then, the wind blew the card out of Tim's hand. Mia and Tim stared as the card flew up, up, up into the sky. All of a sudden, the card turned into a real, big, shiny star! Mia and Tim were very surprised. They stared at the star and smiled. Now, they could both enjoy the fancy star together.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a cricket. The cricket was not like the other crickets. He was original. He loved to play a game called kick. One day, the cricket found a big, round rock. He thought, "This rock would be fun to kick." So, he told his friends about the rock. They all wanted to play kick with the rock too. The cricket and his friends were very excited for the game. The next day, the cricket and his friends went to the garden to play kick with the rock. They took turns kicking the rock. The cricket was the best at kicking the rock. He kicked it so far away that it landed in the tall grass. His friends cheered for him. But, when they went to find the rock, they saw something shiny in the tall grass. It was a beautiful, shiny ball! The cricket and his friends were so happy. They played kick with the shiny ball instead of the rock. The original cricket had found something even better to play with. And they all played kick happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a light, happy dog named Max. Max loved to run and play. One day, Max saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was shivering in the cold wind. Max went up to Sue and said, "Why are you shivering? Do you want to play with me? We can do exercise and get warm." Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Max, I would like that." So Max and Sue ran and jumped together. They did exercise and soon, Sue was not shivering anymore. They both laughed and had a lot of fun. From that day on, Max and Sue were the best of friends.
