Title: Tim the Boy and the Cloud

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to look at the sky. He saw a big, round thing in the sky. It was a planet! But this planet was hidden behind a cloud. Tim was very excited. Tim ran to his friend, Sally, and said, "Sally, come look! I found a hidden planet in the sky!" Sally came and looked up. She saw the planet too. They both jumped up and down with joy. But then, something unexpected happened. The hidden planet started to move! It was not a planet at all, it was a big balloon! Tim and Sally laughed and watched the big balloon float away in the sky.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a silly monkey named Momo. Momo loved to joke and play with his friends. He liked to make everyone laugh and smile. One sunny day, Momo found a big red apple under a tree. He had an idea for a funny joke. He showed the apple to his friend, a little bird named Lulu. Momo put the apple on his head and made a silly face. Lulu laughed and clapped her wings. Momo and Lulu played with the apple all day. They rolled it, threw it, and even pretended it was a ball. They laughed and joked until the sun went down. Then, they shared the apple and ate it together. It was a fun and happy day for Momo and Lulu.

Jane was in the backyard playing with her kite. She ran around in circles, well, more like zig-zag circles, and turned round and round. It was so funny. She felt so free like a bird flying in the sky. Suddenly, Jane heard a loud noise. It was loud and made her pause. Jane looked around but couldn't see anything. Then from the bushes she heard a voice saying, "Lock the kite." Jane was really confused and a little scared. She looked around to see who was speaking. A big bird came out of the bushes. He said, "It's time to lock your kite away, otherwise the wind will take it away." Jane didn't understand what the bird meant. But, she did as she was told and reached up to her kite. She grabbed the handle and pulled. This time the kite moved and flew up, then flew back down and closed. Jane heard a click and knew the kite was locked away. She was so relieved and couldn't believe it. She had used her kite to lock itself away. It was so funny and Jane couldn't help but laugh.

Once upon a time, there was a happy clown named Bobo. Bobo loved to make kids laugh and smile. He had a big red nose, funny clothes, and big shoes. One day, Bobo went to a fun party with lots of kids. The kids were so happy to see him. They asked, "Bobo, will you play with us?" Bobo said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played fun games like tag and hide-and-seek. Bobo made funny faces and told silly jokes. The kids laughed and laughed. They had so much fun with the happy clown. When it was time to go, the kids said, "Thank you, Bobo!" Bobo smiled and said, "You're welcome! Let's play again soon!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Lucy and Ben. Lucy and Ben played together in the park every day. They had a lot of fun! One day, Lucy and Ben were playing tag when Ben tripped over and knocked the ball out of Lucy's hands. Lucy got angry and shouted, "It was your fault Ben! You should have been more careful". Ben was so angry and they started to fight. "No! I'm not going to fight with you," said Ben. He picked up the ball and gave it back to Lucy. Then he said to her, "Friends should not fight. Let's do some work together instead. That will make us feel better". So Lucy and Ben worked together and made something special. When they were finished they smiled and hugged each other. Lucy and Ben learned an important lesson. When things get heavy, it's better to work together than fight. That's how friendship can last forever!
