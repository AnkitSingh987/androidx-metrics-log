Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to lie down all day in the sun. One day, Tom saw a fan in the window of a house. He thought the fan would help him stay cool. Tom went to the house and knocked on the door. A little girl named Sue opened the door. Tom said, "Hi, I am Tom. Can I use your fan to stay cool?" Sue smiled and said, "Of course, Tom! You can lie down here and enjoy the fan." Tom was very happy. He lied down near the fan and felt the cool air on his fur. Sue sat next to Tom and they both enjoyed the fan together. They became good friends and Tom never felt too hot again.

One day, a little cat named Lucky woke up early in the morning. Lucky was a very lucky cat. She liked to wake up when the sun was coming up. She would stretch and yawn, and then go outside to play. Lucky saw a post near her house. She wanted to play with it. She jumped on the post and started to climb. A bird saw Lucky and said, "Be careful, Lucky! That post is not strong." But Lucky did not listen. She thought she was very lucky and nothing bad would happen. Lucky climbed higher and higher on the post. Suddenly, the post broke and Lucky fell down. She was not lucky this time. Lucky was hurt and sad. She wished she had listened to the bird. From that day on, Lucky learned to be more careful and listen to her friends.

Once upon a time there was a little cricket named Bob. Bob was a very happy cricket and loved singing and jumping all day. One day Bob was jumping around his garden when he heard someone call his name. He looked around and saw a big, tall bird. The bird said, “Hello Bob, my name is John. Do you recognize me?” But Bob didn’t remember the bird at all. John was very sad. He thought that Bob should have remembered him. So, John flew away without saying goodbye. Now Bob felt ashamed. He wished he could have recognized John. He decided to ask his friends if they knew who he was. He asked all the bugs, ants and worms in the garden. But none of them knew who John was. But then one day Bob heard the same voice from before. It was John! He had come back to see Bob. “Can you recognize me now?” he asked. This time Bob did recognize John and he was very happy. They had a long chat and then John said goodbye, but this time he promised to come back soon. Bob knew that this time he wouldn’t forget John!

Once upon a time, a happy little girl named Lucy loved to help her mom. One day, Mom said, "Lucy, we need to pack for our trip to Grandma's house!" Lucy was excited to help. Lucy went to her room and packed her toys and clothes. She put them in a big bag. Mom went to the kitchen and packed some food. She also packed something special: coffee. Mom loved coffee, and she knew Grandma loved it too. "This will make Grandma very happy," Mom said with a smile. When they got to Grandma's house, Lucy gave Grandma the big bag of toys and clothes. Mom gave Grandma the coffee. Grandma was so happy to see them and the coffee. She said, "Thank you for the coffee! Let's make some and have a fun day together!" Lucy, Mom, and Grandma had a great day, and they all knew that the coffee made it even better.

Once upon a time there was a lively little girl named Sarah. She was so full of energy and was always excited to explore. One day Sarah found a gate near her house. She wanted to try and open it, but it was too hard for her. She put her hands on the gate and thought, “I wish I could open it!” Suddenly, Sarah heard a noise behind her! She quickly turned around and saw a big brown bear! Sarah ran towards the gate and started to shake it with her tiny hands. The bear quickly ran towards her and tried to grab her with his huge paws. But Sarah was too fast! She kept shaking and shaking the gate until it opened and she ran away in the other direction. The bear was so surprised it just stopped still watching her. Sarah felt so proud of herself and ran back home. Although Sarah was a little scared, she decided to go back to the gate one day. When she got there, the bear was still there but this time it was not trying to get her. The bear just wanted to play. They started to play a game; Sarah would slap the gate and the bear would do the same. They laughed and had a lot of fun!
