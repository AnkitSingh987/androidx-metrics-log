Title: Amy the Girl and the Mom

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. They had a long list of things to buy. Amy saw a big cauliflower and said, "Mom, can we please get the cauliflower?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can get it for dinner tonight." At home, Amy's mom cut the cauliflower into small pieces. She put them in a big pot on the stove. Amy watched and waited. She was excited to eat the cauliflower. She did not know that her mom had a fun surprise for her. When dinner was ready, Amy sat down at the table. She looked at her plate and saw something funny. The cauliflower was now purple! "Wow!" said Amy. "How did you do that, Mom?" Her mom smiled and said, "I put some food color in the pot. I knew you would love it!" Amy was so happy. She ate all the purple cauliflower on her plate. She said, "Thank you, Mom! This is the best dinner ever!" Her mom laughed and said, "You're welcome, Amy. I'm glad you like it." And from that day on, Amy always asked for purple cauliflower for dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange helicopter. It lived in a small house near the park. The helicopter had many friends, like the blue car and the green bike. They all liked to play together. One sunny day, the orange helicopter wanted to rise up high in the sky. It said "bye" to its friends and started to go up, up, up. The blue car and the green bike watched the helicopter and clapped their hands. They were so happy for their friend. The orange helicopter rose so high that it could see the whole park. It saw kids playing, dogs running, and birds singing. The helicopter felt very happy up in the sky. After a while, it came back down to play with its friends. They all had a fun day at the park.

One day, a little crab was walking on the beach. The beach was very quiet. The crab liked the quiet beach because it could hear the sea. The crab saw a big shell and went to look at it. Inside the shell, the crab found a small fish. The fish said, "Hello, I am lost. Can you help me find my way back to the sea?" The crab said, "Yes, I will help you. But first, can you guess what I am?" The fish thought and thought, and then said, "Are you a crab?" The crab said, "Yes! I am a crab!" The crab and the fish became good friends. They played on the beach and had a lot of fun. But they did not go back to the sea yet. The crab wanted to show the fish something special. At the end of the day, the crab took the fish to a secret place. The fish could not believe its eyes! There was a big party with all the sea animals. The fish was so happy and surprised. The crab had planned this party just for the fish. They danced and played all night, and then the crab helped the fish find its way back to the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play outside in all kinds of weather. One sunny day, Fluffy found a big ring on the ground. Fluffy did not know what the ring did, but he thought it was fun to play with. The next day, the weather changed. It was now raining outside. Fluffy wanted to play with the ring again, so he went outside to find it. Fluffy searched and searched, but he could not find the ring. He felt sad and went back inside his home. The day after, the weather was sunny again. Fluffy went outside to play and saw the ring on the ground. He was so happy! He played with the ring all day long. And from that day on, Fluffy knew that no matter the weather, the ring would always come back to him.

One day, a famous dog named Max went to the beach. He loved to play in the sand and taste the salty water. Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was building a big sand castle. "Can I help you?" Max asked Lily. "Yes, please!" said Lily with a big smile. They worked together to make the sand castle even bigger. Suddenly, a big wave came and washed away the sand castle. Max and Lily were very sad. But then, they saw a big, shiny shell in the sand where the castle was. They picked up the shell and showed it to everyone. Max and Lily became famous for finding the prettiest shell on the beach.
