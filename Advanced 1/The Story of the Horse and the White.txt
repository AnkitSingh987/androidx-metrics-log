Title: The Story of the Horse and the White

Once upon a time there were two horses who were best friends. They lived together and did everything together. One day, the horses decided to have a race around the field. The horses were excited and the race began! The brown horse took off quickly and the white horse ran behind, trying to catch up. After a while, the white horse started to feel uncomfortable. He was not used to running so fast! The brown horse noticed that the white horse was having trouble, so he slowed down and asked, "Are you ok?" The white horse replied, "I don't know. I'm not used to running this fast and it's making me feel uncomfortable." The brown horse smiled and said, "Then let's have a different kind of race! We can race all the way around the field and then back again, but if you feel uncomfortable, then we'll slow down. Does that sound better?" The white horse grinned and said, "Yes! That sounds like a great idea!" The two horses raced around the field, taking it slow and having a lot of fun.

One day, a wise frog named Jump lived near a big billboard. The billboard had a picture of a tasty bug on it. Jump loved bugs! He wanted to eat the bug on the billboard. So, he jumped and jumped, but he could not reach it. He felt sad. Then, Jump met a tall bird named Help. Help saw Jump was sad and asked, "Why are you sad?" Jump told Help about the bug on the billboard. Help wanted to help Jump, so they made a plan. Help stood by the billboard and let Jump jump on his back. Together, they jumped high and touched the billboard. Jump found out the bug was not real, but he was happy because he had a new friend. Jump and Help played and had fun all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big house with a wealthy family. One day, Bobo saw a bone in the yard. He was very happy and ran to get it. As Bobo got close, a small dog named Toto arrived. Toto said, "That's my bone!" Bobo did not want to share, so they started to bark at each other. They were both very loud. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird swooped down and took the bone away. Bobo and Toto looked at each other, surprised. They decided to be friends and share the next bone they find.

Once upon a time, in a big space with lots of trees, there was a little weak ant. The ant was sad because it could not carry food like the other ants. It wanted to gain strength and be like the others. One day, the weak ant saw a big leaf with yummy food on it. The ant wanted to take the food to its friends. It tried very hard to lift the leaf. The ant was weak, but it did not give up. As the days went by, the weak ant kept trying to lift the leaf. Each day, it could lift it a little bit more. Soon, the ant began to gain strength. One day, the ant was strong enough to carry the leaf with food to its friends. The other ants were happy and proud of their friend. The weak ant had gained strength and became a strong ant.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a tiny flea friend named Fifi. Max and Fifi liked to play and tease each other. They had lots of fun together. One day, Max and Fifi were playing near a big tree. Max smelled something delicious. It was a big, yummy cake! Max wanted to eat the cake, but he wanted to share it with Fifi too. Max called Fifi to come and see the cake. But when Fifi jumped onto the cake, something unexpected happened. The cake was not a real cake! It was a big, soft pillow that looked like a cake. Max and Fifi laughed and laughed. They had so much fun playing on the soft cake pillow. And even though they didn't get to eat a real cake, they still had a great day together.
