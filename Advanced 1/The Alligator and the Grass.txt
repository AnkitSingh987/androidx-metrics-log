Title: The Alligator and the Grass

Once upon a time there was an alligator who was feeling very bored. He decided to take a walk and find something interesting to do. He started walking. Soon he found something hidden in the grass. He bent down and saw it was a tiny dress. He picked it up and said, "This looks like fun." He put it on and started twirling and dancing around. Suddenly a voice came from behind him. "What are you doing in my dress?" He spun around and saw a little girl. "I'm sorry," he said. "I was just playing around." The girl smiled. "Would you like to dress up some more? I have some other things hiding in the grass. We can put them on too!" The alligator nodded happily and went off to find more hidden clothes with the little girl. They had quite a fun day dress up together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sam. She loved to decorate her bedroom with her favourite things. One day, she found a new sock and was excited to do something special with it. She decided to decorate the sock with lots of fancy marks! She took out a crayon and coloured the sock purple. She added some glittery stars and some sparkly ribbons. When Sam was finished, she was very proud of her work! She couldn't wait to show it to her mum and dad. But when she looked at the clock, she was nervous - it was already getting late! So she quickly put the sock away and made it to bed on time. In the morning, she showed the sock to her mum and dad. They thought it was fantastic - and Sam was very proud of her decorations!

One day, Ally and her mum had a lovely plan. They were going to have a picnic in the park. Ally watched her mum as she started to pack the picnic. She had lots of pink food to pack. There was pink popcorn and pink cake! Ally was very excited. Mum said to Ally, “Do you want to help me pack the picnic?” Ally nodded her head and together they packed it. After they were done, Ally’s mum asked her, “Are you ready for the picnic?” Ally smiled and said, “Yes! Let’s go!” So, off they went to the park to enjoy their pink picnic.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a happy girl who loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a small, soft ball with a stitch on it. She liked the ball very much and played with it all day. One sunny day, Sue went to the park to play with her ball. She threw the ball high in the sky and watched it fall down. Suddenly, a big wind came and took the ball away. Sue was very sad and started to cry. She wanted her ball back. Just then, a kind dog saw the ball and started to rush towards it. The dog caught the ball in its mouth and brought it back to Sue. Sue was so happy and surprised! She did not expect the dog to help her. From that day on, Sue and the dog became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to pretend to be a fireman. Tim had a red hat and a thick coat to wear when he played. One day, Tim heard his friend, Sara, call for help. "Help, help!" she cried. Tim ran to her as fast as he could. Sara was pretending her dollhouse was on fire. Tim put on his red hat and thick coat. He said, "Don't worry, Sara! I am a fireman, and I will save your dollhouse!" He pretended to put out the fire with a big hose. Sara was so happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are the best fireman ever!" They both laughed and played together, pretending to be heroes all day long.

One day, a girl named Ann put on her apron. She wanted to help her mom in the garden. They went outside to look at the flowers. Ann saw a flower that did not bloom. It was hard for the flower to grow. Ann was sad. Ann asked her mom, "Why is the flower not blooming?" Her mom said, "It needs more sun and water." Ann wanted to help the flower. She took a small cup and filled it with water. She gave the flower a drink. The next day, Ann went to see the flower. It started to bloom! She was very happy. Ann learned that if you help and care for things, they can grow and be happy too. And that was the day Ann learned to always be kind and helpful to others.
