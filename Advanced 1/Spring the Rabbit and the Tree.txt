Title: Spring the Rabbit and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a small rabbit named Spring. Spring had many friends. They would play all day long. One day, Spring found a big tree with many rich, red apples. Spring told all his friends about the tree. Spring and his friends made a plan to have a welcome party under the big tree. They invited all the animals in the forest. Everyone was very excited. They knew the big tree would give them a fun day. The day of the welcome party came. All the animals were there. Spring hopped on the tree and shook it. Many rich, red apples fell down. All the animals ate the apples and played games. They were very happy. The big tree had brought them all together for a great day.

Jack was getting ready for a trip. He packed his bag with lots of clothes and shoes. He put his money inside his bag and closed it with a lock. He wanted to make sure nothing was lost. Then Jack took a flexible toy. He put it in his pocket and followed his mom. They walked to the train station. Jack was happy to get on the train because he knew he was going on a nice trip. At the station, they said goodbye to all the other passengers. Jack then found his seat on the train. He looked through his window and saw the countryside. He waved to all the people outside as the train began to move. Jack smiled as he looked out of his window. His trip was going to be a lot of fun!

Once upon a time, there was a big open field. In the middle of the field, there was a pond. Each day, various animals came to the pond to observe and admire it. One day, a small bunny hopped into the field. He was very worried because he did not know where he was. He looked around to observe what was happening. Suddenly, he saw a small duck. The duck approached the bunny and said,"Hi little bunny, don't be scared. Where have you come from?" The bunny was relieved and told the duck his story. The duck flew away and returned with his friends. They all said,"Bunny, don't be worried. You can stay here with us!" The bunny was so happy. Every day the animals played together in the open field. They were all so fond of the bunny, and he was fond of them in return.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a big, colorful lollipop and asked, "Mom, can I have the lollipop?" His mom said, "Yes, but you have to do some work at home first. Then, you can have the lollipop and it will be safe to eat." Tim was happy and worked hard at home. He picked up his toys and helped his mom with the dishes. When he was done, his mom gave him the lollipop. Tim was excited and started to eat it. But, suddenly, a little bird flew in through the window and took the lollipop from Tim's hand! Tim was sad and said, "Oh no, my lollipop is gone!" His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, we can get another one. You did good work and now you know to hold the lollipop safe in your hand."

One day, a blue pony named Blue was walking in the woods. Blue liked to march and sing. He was happy and having fun. As he marched, he met a big brown bear. The bear looked at the blue pony and said, "Why are you so blue?" Blue said, "I am blue because I am a blue pony. I like to march and sing in the woods." The bear liked the pony and wanted to march and sing with him. So, they marched and sang together, making loud and happy sounds. As they marched, they saw a big hill. The bear said, "Let's march up the hill!" But when they got to the top, they saw a big, deep hole. The blue pony did not see the hole and fell in. The bear tried to help, but he could not get the pony out. The blue pony was stuck in the hole, and the bear was very sad. They could not march and sing together anymore.
