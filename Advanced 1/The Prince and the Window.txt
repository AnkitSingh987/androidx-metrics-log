Title: The Prince and the Window

Once there was a prince who lived in a high tower. He had three year old friends, Sam, Rick and Mimmy, who loved to visit. One day, when Sam and Rick were visiting, the prince had a surprise for them. He whispered, “Follow me and I'll show you something!” The three friends followed the prince with curiosity. He led them to an open window in the tower, where they could see over the town. The prince said, “Look!” The three friends saw many houses and streets below. Rick gasped, “It’s so high!” Sam asked the prince, “What’s down there?" The prince whispered, “Let’s find out!” and the three friends followed him downstairs. The prince took them on an adventure through the town, showing them new things and exciting places. At the end of their journey, the prince said, “I want you to remember this day, for it will never be forgotten.” The three friends hugged the prince and whispered “Thank you!”. From that day on, Sam, Rick and Mimmy always looked up at the high tower and remembered their special day with the prince.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a kind judge. The judge liked to eat tasty food. One day, he went to the market to buy some tasty fruit. On his way, he saw a little girl. The girl was very worry. The judge asked her, "Why are you worry?" The girl said, "I lost my toy. I don't know where it is." The judge wanted to help the girl. So, they both looked for the toy together. After a while, they found the toy under a big tree. The girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you, judge!" The judge smiled and said, "You're welcome. Remember, when you are worry, it is good to ask for help." The girl learned that it is good to ask for help when you need it. And they both went home, happy and full of tasty fruit.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy had long, pretty hair. She liked to play with her friends and her dog, Buddy. One day, while playing, Amy saw a big sign. The sign had a picture of a boy and a girl. They were going to marry. Amy thought the boy and the girl looked happy. She wanted to marry too, but she was too young. She asked her mommy, "When can I marry, mommy?" Her mommy smiled and said, "When you grow up, you can marry someone you love." Amy was happy to know that she could marry one day. She told her friends and Buddy about the sign. They all laughed and played a game of pretend marry. They had a fun day, and Amy knew that she would have a long and happy life with her friends and family.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a big, thin tail that wagged all day long. He loved playing with his friends and going on fun walks. One day, Spot's owner, a kind girl named Sue, decided to have a party. She wanted to include all of her friends and their pets. She made a list of everyone to invite and got ready for a fun day. At the party, there were lots of games and yummy treats for everyone to enjoy. Spot and his friends played fetch and chase, while Sue and her friends laughed and talked. They all had a great time together. When it was time to go home, Sue gave everyone a special treat to take home. Spot and his friends were so happy and couldn't wait for the next fun party.

John and his family were getting ready for an exciting day. They were preparing to paint their house. John asked his mom, “Why do we need to prepare for painting?” His mom replied, “So we can make sure the wall looks nice and bright.” John was excited. He asked, “What color paint should I use?” His mom smiled and said, “You can pick any color you like.” John decided to use blue paint. He got a brush and a pot of paint, and started to paint the wall. He was busy painting, when his dad walked through the door. His dad was deaf, and couldn’t hear John talking. His mom waved and said, “Hi dad! Look at John, he is painting the wall!” John’s dad smiled and nodded. He was happy to see his son preparing for such a big project.
