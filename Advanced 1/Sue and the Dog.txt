Title: Sue and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Sue. Sue had a big heart and was always nice to others. One day, she met a new friend named Tom. Sue wanted to introduce Tom to her other friends. She thought they would all be happy together. Sue took Tom to the park to play with her friends. They played games and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running towards them. Sue and her friends were scared. But Tom was not scared. He was a compassionate boy and he had a memory of playing with dogs. Tom gently pet the dog and it became calm. Sue and her friends were amazed. They were happy that Tom was brave and kind to the dog. From that day, Sue, Tom, and their friends had even more fun together. They learned that being compassionate and brave can make the world a better place.

One day, a big machine was feeling very frustrated. It could not do its job well. It needed support to help it work better. A small machine saw the big machine and said, "I can help you! Let's work together and support each other." The big machine was happy and agreed. The small machine supported the big machine, and they worked well together. They were not frustrated anymore. They became good friends and helped each other every day.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Ben. Ben was three years old and he loved to dress up. One day he put on a terrible pair of clothes that his mum had given him to wear. He didn't like the way the clothes looked, so he tried to take them off. But his mum saw and came to help him. She said, "No Ben! You must raise your arms so I can help you get the clothes off!" Ben did as he was told. He put his hands up and his mum carefully took the clothes off. She told Ben he didn't have to wear them again if he didn't like them and replaced them with a nice set of clothes. Ben was happy and thanked his mum. He decided to never again wear the terrible clothes, and instead always choose something he liked.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful sea, there lived a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim and play with his friends. One day, while swimming, Fin saw something big and heavy. It was an anchor! "Look, friends!" said Fin, "I find an anchor!" His friends swam over to see what Fin found. They were amazed by the big anchor. They had never seen one before. Together, they decided to make the anchor their special meeting place. They played games, told stories, and had lots of fun around the anchor. It was their favorite spot in the beautiful sea. And they were very happy.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Jack and Jill. They were always together, playing and having fun. Jack and Jill both loved taking baths, but Jack's bath was very different from Jill's. Jack's bath was modern. He had a looping chain connected to the roof of the bathroom. He could slide down the chain and then slide back up for a fun bath time! Jill was very jealous and wished she had a modern bath like Jack. One day Jill finally decided to ask Jack if she could bathe with him. Jack hesitated because he wanted to keep the modern bath time all to himself. He didn't want to share it with Jill, so he said no. Jill was very sad and a little mad. She didn't know what else to do, so she crossed her arms and stomped her foot. Jack was surprised at her reaction, so he changed his mind. He told her that she could bathe with him, if they both took turns sliding down the chain. Jill was so happy she couldn't stop smiling. Jack and Jill shared many more modern baths together. They had so much fun and felt so happy! From then on, they both never hesitated to share with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the deep mud. He would jump and splash all day long. One day, Tim found a piece of paper in the mud. It was a poem! Tim wanted to read the poem, but he didn't know how. So, he asked his mom for help. His mom read the poem to him. It was a fun poem about a frog who liked to jump and play, just like Tim. After hearing the poem, Tim was so happy. He decided to leave the poem under a big rock so other kids could find it and enjoy it too. The next day, Tim went back to the deep mud to play and saw another kid had found the poem. They both smiled and played together all day long.
