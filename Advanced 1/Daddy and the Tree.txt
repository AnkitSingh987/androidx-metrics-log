Title: Daddy and the Tree

One day, Daddy and his little girl went to the park. They saw a dull, gray tree. The tree was big and tall. Daddy said, "Let's stand under the tree and look up." As they stood under the tree, they saw a big, red ball stuck in the branches. The little girl said, "Daddy, can you get the ball for me?" Daddy tried to reach the ball, but it was too high. Just then, a friendly bird flew down from the tree. The bird picked up the ball with its beak and dropped it to Daddy and the little girl. They were so surprised and happy! They played with the red ball all day and thanked the bird for its help.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lily. Lily loved to learn new things. One day, she saw a big clock on the wall. She wanted to learn how to tell the time. Lily went to her mom and said, "Mom, can you teach me about the clock?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily! Let's learn together." They sat down and started to learn about the clock. Lily was very happy. As they were learning, something unexpected happened. The big clock on the wall started to talk! It said, "Hello, Lily and Mom! I can help you learn about the time." Lily and her mom were very surprised, but they listened to the talking clock. They all laughed and learned together, and Lily became very good at telling the time.

Once there was a little girl named Mary who loved to sing. Every day before going to school, she sang loudly in the morning. She got up early to read the newspaper and sang while she ate breakfast. One day Mary was walking to school and she heard a noise coming from the playground. When she got there, she saw her friends singing and dancing with lots of energy. Mary was so excited that she joined them straight away and sang with them. But one of her friends knew a different song that he wanted all of them to sing. When Mary heard it she was not enthusiastic and refused to sing it. Everyone else was disappointed and stopped singing. Mary tried to cheer her friends up by offering to teach them her favourite song. Then everyone was enthusiastic again and started singing her song together. Mary was so excited to be singing with her friends that she sang louder than everyone else.

Once upon a time, there was a doctor who loved to make tasty treats. Every day, he would bake cookies, cakes, and pies for all the kids in the town. The kids loved the doctor very much because he was kind and his treats were so tasty. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the doctor. She had a big frown on her face. The doctor asked her, "What is wrong, Lily? Why are you sad?" Lily said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." The doctor wanted to help Lily, so he said, "Don't worry, I will mind your toy for you." The doctor looked all around the town for Lily's toy. He asked everyone he saw, "Have you seen a lost toy?" After a long time, the doctor found the toy under a big tree. He was very happy and ran back to Lily. He gave her the toy and said, "I minded your toy for you, Lily." Lily was so happy to have her toy back. She smiled and said, "Thank you, doctor! You are the best!" The doctor was happy to help Lily. He gave her a tasty cookie to celebrate. From that day on, Lily and the doctor were the best of friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Sam liked to zoom around the park on his bike. He was an adventurous boy who wanted to see everything. He wore his favorite pants with stars and rockets on them. He felt like a space explorer. One day, he saw a big hill in the park. He wanted to zoom down the hill very fast. He thought it would be fun and exciting. He pedaled hard and reached the top of the hill. He looked down and saw a pond at the bottom. He smiled and said to himself, "Here I go!" But he did not see the duck that was crossing the path. As he zoomed down the hill, he hit the duck and fell off his bike. He landed in the pond with a splash. He was wet and muddy. The duck was angry and quacked loudly. Sam was scared and sorry. He said to the duck, "I'm sorry, duck. I did not mean to hurt you. Are you okay?" The duck looked at Sam and saw his pants with stars and rockets on them. The duck liked stars and rockets. He thought they were pretty and shiny. He decided to forgive Sam. He said to Sam, "It's okay, boy. I'm okay. You have nice pants. Do you like stars and rockets?" Sam nodded and said, "Yes, duck. I like stars and rockets. They make me feel adventurous. Do you want to be my friend?" The duck nodded and said, "Yes, boy. I want to be your friend. You are adventurous too. Do you want to zoom with me?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, duck. I want to zoom with you. But first, I need to dry my pants. They are wet and muddy." The duck said, "That's okay, boy. I'll wait for you. Then we can zoom together." Sam and the duck became friends. They zoomed around the park on Sam's bike. They had fun and exciting adventures. They learned to be careful and kind. They wore their favorite pants with stars and rockets on them. They felt like space explorers.
