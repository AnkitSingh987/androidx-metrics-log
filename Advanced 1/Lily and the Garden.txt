Title: Lily and the Garden

Lily liked to play in the garden. She liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs and worms. One day, she found a big hole under a bush. She wanted to see what was inside. She took her shovel and dug some more. She saw something shiny in the hole. It was a metal thing with a needle and a circle. She did not know what it was, but it looked pretty. She picked it up and held it in her hand. She felt a tickle on her finger. She looked and saw a red mark. It hurt a little. She ran to her mom and showed her the metal thing and the mark. Her mom looked scared and angry. She took the metal thing and threw it in the trash. She said it was a bad thing that could make Lily very sick. She said it was a needle that some people used to put bad stuff in their body. She said Lily had to go to the doctor right away. Lily did not understand. She did not mean to find the bad thing. She just wanted to discover something new. She started to cry. She was scared of the doctor and the mark. She wished she had never dug in the hole. She wished she had a compassionate friend to hug her and make her feel better.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and watch the sunset. One day, she saw a sad flower in her garden. The flower had no water, so it could not grow big and strong. Lily thought, "I can help the flower!" She grabbed her spray bottle and filled it with water. She went to the flower and said, "Don't worry, flower! I will help you!" She sprayed the water on the flower, giving it a nice drink. The flower was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lily! You made me feel better." Lily smiled and felt proud. She learned that helping others can make you feel happy too. From that day on, Lily always took care of her garden and helped the flowers grow strong and beautiful.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a dog named Max. They loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, they found a dirty, shiny thing in the dirt. It was a jewel! Sue and Max wanted to show the jewel to their friends. So, Sue put a leash on Max, and she lead him to their friends' houses. They showed the jewel to all their friends, and everyone thought it was very pretty. After showing the jewel to their friends, Sue and Max went home. Sue's mom helped her clean the dirty jewel. It became shiny and pretty again. Sue and Max were very happy with their new treasure. They put the clean jewel in a special box and kept it safe.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the gym with her mom. Sue liked to watch her mom work out. Sue saw her mom snap her fingers to the beat of the music. Sue wanted to snap her fingers too, but she couldn't do it yet. Sue's mom gave her a spicy snack to eat while she watched. Sue loved spicy food. She took a bite and her mouth felt hot. Sue smiled and ate more of the spicy snack. She felt happy and strong, like her mom. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue tried to snap her fingers again, and this time, she did it! She was so excited that she snapped her fingers over and over. Sue's mom stopped working out and clapped for her. They both laughed and danced, snapping their fingers together at the gym.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to spring on the soft cushion in the living room. One day, Kitty saw a little mouse named Mousy run by. Kitty said, "I want to play with you, Mousy!" But Mousy was scared of Kitty. Mousy said, "No, you are big and I am small. You might hurt me!" Kitty felt sad because she just wanted to be friends. Kitty had an idea. She found a toy and showed it to Mousy. They played together and had fun. In the end, Kitty and Mousy became good friends. The moral of the story is to always find a way to be kind and make friends.
