Title: Spot the Dog and the Bird

One day, a cute little dog named Spot wanted to go play outside. It was cold, so his mom said, "You must wear your sweater." Spot did not like his sweater. He said, "No, I don't want to wear it!" But his mom insisted, "You must wear it to stay warm." Spot put on his sweater and went outside. He saw his friend, a small bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was cold and shivering. Spot felt bad for Chirpy and thought about his sweater. He decided to do something nice. He took off his sweater and gave it to Chirpy. Chirpy was very happy and warm. Soon, the sun came out and it was not cold anymore. Spot and Chirpy played all day. Spot learned that sharing can make others happy, and that made him happy too. The sweater that he did not want to wear helped his friend, and that was a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and imagine fun things. One day, she was playing in the park when the sky got dark. Lily looked up and saw big clouds. She heard a noisy sound. It was thunder! She was not scared, but she knew it was time to go home. Lily ran inside her house and told her mom about the thunder. Her mom said it was okay, and they could play inside. So, Lily and her mom played and imagined together until the noisy thunder went away.

One day, Tim and Sue were playing outside. They liked to pretend they were in a big tornado. The tornado was tall and went round and round. Tim and Sue would spin and spin until they fell down, laughing. Tim said, "Let's be tall like the tornado!" They both stood on their toes, trying to reach the sky. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. They stopped and looked around. A real, tall tornado was coming their way! Sue yelled, "Run, Tim, run!" They ran as fast as they could to their house. They hid under the table until the tornado passed. When it was safe, they went outside and saw that everything was okay. Tim and Sue knew they were safe, and they never pretended to be in a tornado again. Instead, they found new games to play, like being tall trees or fast cars.

One day, a little fish was swimming in the sea. He was nervous because he heard there was a big shark nearby. The little fish did not want to meet the shark, so he swam close to the rocks. The shark was hungry and saw the little fish. The shark swam fast to catch the little fish. The little fish was scared and swam faster. He saw a small hole in the rocks and swam inside to hide. A friendly crab saw the little fish in trouble. The crab wanted to save the little fish from the shark. The crab pinched the shark's tail to make him go away. The shark was scared and swam away. The little fish was safe and thanked the crab for saving him. They became good friends and played together in the sea.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big temple. The temple was very old and had many rocks. One day, a strong man named Tom came to the temple. Tom wanted to lift the rocks. He thought it would be fun and a little crazy. Tom started to lift the rocks one by one. It was hard work, but he was happy. As he lifted the rocks, he found a small, pretty box under them. Tom was very excited and wanted to see what was inside the box. He opened the box and found a small, shiny stone. The stone was so pretty and bright, it made Tom's eyes light up. He showed the stone to all his friends, and they all thought it was amazing. From that day on, Tom and his friends would go to the temple to play and have crazy, fun times together.

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. Many animals lived on the hill. They all liked to play together. But one day, a rude bird came to the hill. The bird did not care about the other animals. It would not play nice. The animals on the hill were sad. They did not like the rude bird. They wanted to play, but the bird would not let them. They tried to talk to the bird, but it did not listen. One day, a kind squirrel came to the hill. The squirrel cared for the other animals. It talked to the rude bird. The bird listened to the squirrel. The bird learned to play nice. All the animals on the hill were happy again.
