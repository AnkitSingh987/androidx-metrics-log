Title: Lily and the Bell

Lily liked to ride her bike. She had a pink bike with a bell and a basket. She rode her bike every day in the park with her mom and dad. She liked to see the flowers, the birds and the other kids. One day, she saw a boy with an unusual bike. His bike was green and had three wheels. It also had a horn and a flag. Lily wanted to see his bike. She rode over to him and said, "Hi, I'm Lily. What is your name?" The boy smiled and said, "Hi, I'm Max. This is my bike. It is a trike. Do you want to try it?" Lily nodded. She liked to try new things. She got off her bike and gave it to Max. She climbed on his trike and pushed the pedals. It was fun and easy to ride. She honked the horn and waved the flag. She felt happy. Max tried Lily's bike. He liked the bell and the basket. He rang the bell and put a flower in the basket. He felt happy too. They rode around the park and laughed. They became friends. They shared their bikes and their names. They had an unusual and fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a big goose named Gilly. Gilly was a reliable friend to all the animals on the farm. She always helped them when they needed it. One day, Gilly was standing by the pond, talking to her friend, Ducky. "Hi, Ducky!" Gilly said. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Gilly!" Ducky replied. "Let's play by the pond." As they were playing, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew and pushed Ducky into the pond. Ducky couldn't swim very well. "Oh no!" cried Gilly. "I will help you, Ducky!" Gilly stood on the edge of the pond and reached out with her wing. She pulled Ducky out of the water and saved him. All the animals cheered for Gilly the reliable goose. From that day on, Gilly and Ducky were best friends, and they always played safely by the pond.

Once upon a time, in a cold sea, there was a little octopus named Ollie. Ollie loved to play with his friends in the water. One day, Ollie saw something shiny on the sand. He wanted to show it to his friends, but he had to be careful not to slip. Ollie said to his friend, "I found something shiny! Let's go see it, but we must not slip on the sand." His friend nodded and they swam to the shiny thing. As they got closer, they saw it was a big, round, and shiny stone. When they reached the stone, Ollie tried to pick it up. But the stone was too slippery, and it made Ollie slip! His friend laughed and said, "Be careful, Ollie! You told me not to slip, but you slipped too!" Ollie laughed too and they both tried to pick up the stone together. With the help of his friend, Ollie picked up the stone and showed it to all their friends. Everyone loved the shiny stone, and they all played with it. The stone made their cold sea a little more fun and bright. From that day on, Ollie and his friends were always careful not to slip when they played with the shiny stone. They had lots of fun together, and the cold sea became a happy place for all the little sea creatures.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved to eat cheese. One day, Mimi's mom told her that she should eat healthy food too, like fruits and vegetables. Mimi went outside to play with her friend, a little bird named Bobby. Bobby saw Mimi looking sad and asked her what was wrong. Mimi told him that she wanted to eat cheese, but her mom said she should eat healthy food. Bobby had an idea and said, "Let's go find healthy food together!" Bobby flew up high and started to point at things he saw. He pointed at a big apple tree with lots of red apples. Mimi and Bobby picked some apples and ate them. They were sweet and juicy. Then, Bobby pointed at a garden with lots of vegetables. They picked some carrots and ate them. They were crunchy and yummy. After eating all the healthy food, Mimi and Bobby went back to Mimi's house. Mimi's mom was so happy that Mimi had eaten healthy food. She gave Mimi a big piece of cheese as a treat. Mimi and Bobby shared the cheese and they were both very happy. From that day on, Mimi learned that eating healthy food can be fun and tasty too.
