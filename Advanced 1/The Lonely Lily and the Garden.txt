Title: The Lonely Lily and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a lonely lily in a garden. She wished she could have friends, but no one had come to visit her. Then, one day, a kind little girl saw her and decided to pick her up and take her home. She wanted to help the lonely lily find friends. The kind little girl went to her friends and asked if they wanted to take the lily with them. They all said yes and lifted her gently, taking her to a new place. At the new place, the lily found lots of friends. She had all the company she wanted, and she was very happy. She learned that when you are kind and helpful, you can make a difference and help someone who needs it. Moral of the story: Kindness is always the best way to help others.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous oyster. It lived in the sea. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach. He saw the big oyster. Tim wanted to lift it. "Hello, big oyster," said Tim. "Can I lift you?" The oyster opened its shell and said, "Yes, Tim, you can try to lift me." Tim tried to lift the enormous oyster. But it was too heavy! Then, something unexpected happened. The oyster lifted Tim! Tim was so surprised. "Wow!" said Tim. "I didn't know you could lift me, big oyster!" The big oyster and Tim became good friends. They had lots of fun together at the beach. And that's the end of the story.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in the garden. They have a big box of toys with cars, dolls, balls and books. One day, they decide to play hide and seek with their toys. They take turns hiding a toy and then finding it. Anna hides a red car under a bush. Ben counts to ten and then starts to look for it. He looks behind the fence, under the slide, in the sandbox, but he cannot find the car. He asks Anna for a hint, but she forgets where she hid it. They both search for the car, but they cannot see it. They are sad and want to play with the car. They ask their mom for help. Mom comes to the garden and sees the car under the bush. She picks it up and gives it to Ben. She says, "Here is your car. You forgot to look under the bush. Next time, try to remember where you hide your toys." Ben and Anna are happy and hug their mom. They say, "Thank you, mom. You are the best. We will remember next time." They put the car back in the box and choose another toy to hide. They play hide and seek again, but this time they do not forget. They have a lively and fun time in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a poor plane. This plane was not like other planes. It could not fly very high or very fast. It was sad because it wanted to be like the other planes. One day, the poor plane tried to fly high. It tried and tried, but it could not. Then, a big bird came to help. The bird said, "You must yield and let me help you." The poor plane was scared, but it said, "Okay, I will yield." The big bird took the poor plane high in the sky. They flew together, and the poor plane was happy. It learned that sometimes, it is good to yield and let others help. From that day on, the poor plane was not sad anymore. It knew that it could fly high with the help of its friend, the big bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a hidden notebook under her bed. She liked to draw and write in her notebook every day. One day, Amy could not find her notebook. She looked everywhere but could not find it. She started to feel sad. She asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my notebook?" Her mom said, "No, but let's look for it together." They looked all around the house. They looked in the kitchen, the living room, and even outside. But they still could not find the notebook. Finally, they went back to Amy's room. Her mom looked under the bed and found the hidden notebook. She gave it to Amy and said, "Here it is!" Amy felt very happy and gave her mom a big hug. From that day on, Amy and her mom drew and wrote in the notebook together, making it even more special.
