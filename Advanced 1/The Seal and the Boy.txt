Title: The Seal and the Boy

Once upon a time there was a seal. He was very dirty and filthy. He wanted to get clean, but didn't know how. He thought and he thought, but he couldn't solve the problem by himself. One day he noticed a boy nearby. He asked the boy to help him get clean. The boy thought it was a good idea and agreed. He found an old bucket and filled it with water. He put many bubbles in the bucket to make it soapy. The seal had so much fun playing in the soap! But then the boy suddenly grabbed him out of the bucket and started scrubbing him with a hard brush. It was too hard and hurt so much! The seal cried and screamed. Eventually the seal was clean, but he was very sad. He learnt that trying to find a solution can be too much for him alone. Sometimes he needed help from someone else.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty dog named Max. Max loved to dig holes in the park. One day, Max dug a big hole and hid his favorite toy bone inside it. He thought it would be fun to play a game. Later, his friend, a little girl named Lily, came to the park. She saw the hole and said, "Oh no! There's a hole in the park. We need to solve this problem." Max wagged his tail and barked, "I can help!" Together, Max and Lily filled the hole with dirt. After they finished, Max realized that his toy bone was still buried in the hole. He felt sad but learned that being naughty can cause problems. From that day on, Max stopped digging holes in the park and played nicely with Lily. They both learned the importance of solving problems and being good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big race in the park. All the animals wanted to win. They were very competitive. The race was on the soft green grass. The day of the race came. All the animals lined up at the start. The rabbit, the turtle, the cat, and the dog were there. They all wanted to win. The race began, and the animals ran as fast as they could. The rabbit was very fast, but he saw a big hole in the grass. He began to panic. He did not want to fall. The other animals passed the rabbit. The turtle was slow, but he did not panic. He kept going and won the race. All the animals learned that it is okay to be slow and not panic.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big job to do. She had to take the mail to her grandma. Sue put the mail in her bag and went on her way. The sun was shining and it was a fine day. As Sue walked, she saw a big hill. She had to go up the hill to get to her grandma's house. Sue started to walk up the hill, but it was hard. She began to struggle. She pushed and pushed, but the hill was so steep. She was very tired, but she did not give up. Then, something unexpected happened! A friendly dog came running up to Sue. The dog wanted to help. It took the mail from Sue's bag and ran up the hill with it. Sue was so happy and followed the dog. When they got to the top of the hill, the dog gave the mail to Sue. She was so thankful for the dog's help. Together, they went to grandma's house and gave her the mail. Grandma was surprised and happy too.

Once there was a little girl called Lucy. She was three years old and loved playing outside with her friends. One day, she saw a broken cage lying in the grass. She asked her friend, "What is that?" Her friend replied, "It's a birdcage. Someone must have thrown it away." The next day, Lucy was playing in her garden when suddenly she saw a bird in the broken cage. "Oh, what a pretty bird," she exclaimed. She tried to open the cage but it was locked shut. Suddenly, a neighbour came around the corner. He stretched out his arm, took the cage and threw it over the fence into the lake. Lucy was very sad. But then something wonderful happened. The cage sprung open when it hit the water. The bird flew up into the sky and Lucy cheered with joy. From that day onwards, Lucy never threw away anything, just in case there was a beautiful bird inside.
