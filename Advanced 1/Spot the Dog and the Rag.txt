Title: Spot the Dog and the Rag

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a rag that he loved to play with. He would run and jump with the rag in his mouth all day long. Spot was very happy. One day, a big dog named Max saw Spot playing with his rag. Max wanted the rag too. He went up to Spot and tried to speak to him. "Give me the rag," said Max. Spot did not want to give up his rag. He held on to it tight. Max and Spot pulled on the rag, each trying to get it for themselves. They did not want to share. Then, a wise old dog named Buddy came by. He saw the two dogs fighting over the rag. Buddy spoke to them, "You can both have fun with the rag if you share it." Max and Spot listened to Buddy. They started to share the rag and play together. They both were happy and became good friends.

Once there was a mummy and a daddy. The mummy was tired so the daddy said he would do some laundry. He gathered the dirty clothes and put them in the machine. After a while, the machine stopped and the daddy took the clothes out. He put them in a basket. The mummy said that now he had to hang them outside. The daddy went outside with the basket, but he was feeling ashamed. He came back inside, put the basket down and said he could not do laundry. The mummy smiled and said, "Yes, you can." She took the basket and went outside. The mummy hung the laundry up and smiled. The mummy and daddy were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a tiny seed that she wanted to grow. She planted the seed in her yard and watered it every day. One day, a talking bird came to her yard. The bird said, "Hello, Lily! What are you doing?" Lily told the bird, "I am growing a seed. It will take a year to grow big." The bird was excited and said, "I will help you. We can watch it grow together." As the days went by, the seed started to grow. Lily and the bird became good friends. They played together and took care of the growing plant. The plant grew bigger and bigger. After a year, the plant had turned into a big, beautiful tree. The tree had many branches with pretty leaves. Lily and the bird were very happy. They found out that the tree was an expensive tree because it was so rare and special. They were proud of their hard work and loved their tree very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in the kitchen with her mom. They would make fancy cakes and cookies together. Lily always tried to behave and listen to her mom. One day, Lily's mom had to go to the store. She told Lily to behave while she was gone. Lily wanted to surprise her mom by making a fancy cake all by herself. She put on her apron and started to make the cake. Lily was almost done with the cake when something unexpected happened. She heard a noise coming from the kitchen. To her surprise, she found a little mouse trying to eat the cake! Lily didn't know what to do. She thought about it and decided to give the mouse a tiny piece of cake. The mouse was so happy that it started to dance. When Lily's mom came home, she saw the fancy cake and the little mouse dancing. She was so surprised and happy that Lily had made a new friend. From that day on, Lily, her mom, and the little mouse made fancy cakes together in the kitchen. And they all behaved very well.

One day, a man and his wife lived in a little house. The man did not like to clean, so the house was very messy. The wife said, "If you don't help me clean, I will punish you!" The man did not want to be punished, so he said, "Fine, I will help you clean." The next day, the man and his wife started to clean the house. They found a big box under the bed. "What is in the box?" asked the wife. The man did not know, so they opened the box. Inside the box, there were many toys and games. The wife thought the toys and games were for her, so she was very happy. But then, the man said, "No, these toys and games are not for you. They are for me!" The wife was sad, but the man was happy. He played with the toys and games all day, and did not help his wife clean. In the end, the wife punished the man by making him clean the house all by himself. The man learned that he should always help his wife and not be selfish.
