Title: The Statue and the Book

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big statue. The statue was of a kind man who always helped others. Everyone in the town loved the statue. One day, a little girl named Mia was walking with her mom. She saw the statue and asked, "Mom, who is that man?" Her mom said, "That is a man who helped many people. We like him a lot." Mia felt happy to learn about the kind man. Later, Mia saw a boy who was upset. He was trying to read a book but could not understand the words. Mia wanted to be like the kind man, so she went to the boy and said, "I can help you read." They read the book together, and the boy was not upset anymore. Mia felt happy that she could help, just like the man in the statue.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a thoughtful boy who loved to play in the field near his home. One sunny day, Tim saw a small bird hopping around the field. The bird looked lost and sad. Tim walked up to the bird and asked, "What is your name?" The bird looked up at Tim and said, "My name is Blue. I can't find my family. Can you help me?" Tim nodded and said, "Yes, I will help you find your family, Blue." Together, Tim and Blue looked all around the field. They asked other animals if they had seen Blue's family. Finally, they found Blue's family near a big tree. Blue was so happy and thanked Tim for his help. From that day on, Tim and Blue became the best of friends and played together in the field every day.

Lily was a spoiled girl who always wanted more toys and candy. She did not like to share or say thank you. One day, she saw a parrot at the pet shop and begged her mom to buy it for her. Her mom said no, but Lily threw a tantrum and cried very loud. Her mom was tired and gave in, and bought the parrot for Lily. Lily was happy for a while, but soon she got bored of the parrot. She did not feed it or play with it or talk to it. She only yelled at it when it made noise or tried to bite her. The parrot was sad and lonely, and wished for a better home. One day, Lily left the cage open and the parrot flew away. Lily did not notice until she heard her mom scream. She ran to the living room and saw the parrot sitting on the couch, tearing up her favorite doll. Lily was angry and tried to grab the parrot, but the parrot bit her finger and flew out the window. Lily cried and cried, but her mom did not comfort her. She said, "This is your punishment for being a spoiled and mean girl. You did not take care of the parrot, and now you lost it and your doll. Maybe this will teach you to be more grateful and kind." Lily felt sorry and ashamed, and realized that her mom was right. She wished she could have the parrot back and be nice to it, but it was too late. She learned her lesson, and decided to be a better girl from then on. She also hoped that the parrot would find a good home where it would be happy and loved.

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tom. Tom was very adventurous. He loved to paint outside in the sun. One day, he went to the park with his paint and brushes. Tom saw a big tree and wanted to paint it. He put colors on the tree with his brush. The colors were red, blue, and yellow. Tom was happy with his painting. The sun was hot, and it made the paint dry fast. Tom looked at his dry painting and smiled. He took his painting home to show his mom. His mom loved it too. They put the painting on the wall, and Tom felt proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big room with lots of furniture. Lily loved to hug her teddy bear, Mr. Snuggles, every day. One day, Lily saw a weak chair in her room. She said, "Oh no, Mr. Snuggles! The chair is weak. Let's fix it." So, Lily and Mr. Snuggles tried to fix the chair. As they were fixing the chair, something unexpected happened. The chair started to talk! The chair said, "Thank you, Lily and Mr. Snuggles, for fixing me. I am a magic chair!" Lily and Mr. Snuggles were very surprised but happy to have a new friend. They all hugged and played together in Lily's room.
