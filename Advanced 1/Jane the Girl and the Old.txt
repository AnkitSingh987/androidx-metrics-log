Title: Jane the Girl and the Old

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. She was so excited to travel. She packed up her things into a big bag and said goodbye to mom and dad. Jane was so impatient she couldn't wait to go. Finally, after a long time she arrived at her destination. Jane started to explore all around. She went to the kitchen and saw a lot of yummy treats. She wanted to try all of them! But then she heard a voice. "Oh no! You're eating all my food!" The voice belonged to a kind old lady who was standing in the kitchen. Jane was not sure what to say. She looked at the kind old lady and asked, "Can I please try one?" The kind old lady smiled and said, "Yes, of course you can! Help yourself!" Jane was so happy and excited. She tried a little bit of every treat and was feeling a lot better. Jane thanked the kind old lady for her hospitality and looked forward to her next travels.

Tom and Lily went to the beach with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and look for shells. They saw many different shells, some big, some small, some shiny, some dull. "Look, Lily, I found an oyster!" Tom said, holding a round shell with a crack in it. "Maybe there is a pearl inside!" "Wow, can I see?" Lily asked, running over to Tom. She tried to open the shell, but it was too hard. "How do we open it?" "Maybe we can ask Dad. He knows a lot of things," Tom said. They ran to their dad, who was sitting on a towel and reading a book. "Dad, Dad, look what we found!" Tom said, showing him the oyster. "Can you open it for us?" Dad smiled and took the oyster. He used a knife to pry it open. Inside, there was a shiny white pearl. "Wow, you are very lucky, Tom and Lily. You found a new pearl in an old oyster. That is very rare and precious," Dad said, giving them the pearl. "Thank you, Dad. It is so pretty. Can we keep it?" Lily asked. "Of course, you can. It is your treasure. You can make a necklace or a bracelet with it," Dad said. Tom and Lily were very happy. They hugged their dad and thanked him. They put the pearl in a small box and kept it safe. They had a lot of fun at the beach, but the pearl was their favorite thing. They could not wait to show it to their friends when they arrived home.

One day, a girl named Lily was in her room. It was a gloomy day outside. Lily was sad. She wanted to play with her friends, but they were not there. Lily sat by the window and looked out. Suddenly, she saw a package on the ground outside. "What is that?" she asked her mom. Her mom said, "I don't know, let's go see." They went outside to look at the package. It was big and wrapped in pretty paper. Lily tried to reach for the package, but it was too high. Her mom helped her reach it. They opened the package together. Inside was a big, colorful ball. Lily was so happy. She played with the ball all day and forgot about the gloomy day.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to study the weather. He went outside and looked up at the sky. It was a sunny day, and Tim was grateful for the nice weather. He saw birds flying and felt the warm sun on his face. As Tim was studying the weather, he saw a big, dark cloud coming. He ran home to tell his mom. "Mom, I was studying the weather and a big cloud is coming!" he said. His mom looked outside and saw the cloud too. Suddenly, the cloud started to rain, but it was not just water. It was raining candy! Tim and his mom were very surprised. They went outside and started to pick up the yummy treats. They were grateful for the unexpected candy rain and had a sweet day together.
