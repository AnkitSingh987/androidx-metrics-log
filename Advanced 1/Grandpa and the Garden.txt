Title: Grandpa and the Garden

Grandpa was in the garden, wearing a big apron. He was planting new vegetables. Grandma came to the garden, carrying a basket. "Hello Grandpa!" said Grandma. Grandpa smiled. "Hello Grandma! What have you got there?" "I've got some seeds to store in the garden. We need to plant them to make our vegetable patch look nicer." Grandpa took the basket from Grandma. "We'll make sure we store them in the right place," he said. The sky was getting gloomy. Grandma started to get up. "Come inside now," she said. "It's getting late." Grandpa and Grandma went inside and stored the basket in the kitchen. Then they made some tea and chatted about the garden. It was a cosy evening!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big statue. The statue was of a strong man who helped the people. Everyone loved the statue because it made them feel safe. One day, a little boy named Tim found a toy that could shoot. He thought it was fun to shoot at things. Tim saw the statue and wanted to play with it. He pointed his toy and shot at the statue. But the statue was too big and strong, so it did not move. Tim felt helpless because he could not play with the statue. He was sad and sat down. His mom saw him and said, "The statue is here to help us. Let's be kind to it." Tim learned that it was better to be kind than to shoot at things. He went home and played with his toy in a nice way.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely park. It had green grass and many pretty flowers. People liked to walk there with their dogs and kids. The kids would play and laugh. The dogs would run and bark. Everyone was happy in the lovely park. One day, a big rain came. The lovely park turned into a big mud puddle. The kids could not play. The dogs could not run. The flowers were not pretty anymore. The people could not walk in the lovely park. They were sad. The rain did not stop. The lovely park stayed a big mud puddle. The people and dogs were very sad. They could not walk or play in the park anymore. The flowers never came back. The lovely park was gone forever.

One day, in a small classroom, there was a little girl named Lily. She felt gloomy because it was raining outside. She wanted to play with her friends, but they could not go out in the rain. Lily's teacher saw her sad face and said, "Lily, why don't we play inside the classroom?" Lily's eyes lit up, and she smiled. "Yes, let's play!" she said. So, Lily and her friends played games in the classroom. They played with blocks, colored pictures, and even pretended to be animals. The gloomy day turned into a fun day, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a pink room. In the pink room, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy skeleton. She loved her toy skeleton very much. One day, Amy could not find her toy skeleton. She looked under her bed, but it was not there. She looked in her toy box, but it was not there either. Amy was very sad and began to weep. Amy's mom heard her weep and came into the pink room. She asked, "Amy, why are you sad?" Amy said, "I can't find my toy skeleton." Amy's mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, we will find it." Amy and her mom looked all around the house. They found the toy skeleton hiding behind a chair in the living room. Amy was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They both smiled and went back to the pink room to play with the toy skeleton.

Once there was a little boy who loved to play all day long. One day, he decided to try to climb a tree. He got up high into the branches. But then he suddenly felt a sharp pain in his hand. He had gotten a splinter! He tried to pull it out but it hurt too much and he started crying. His mother came running to him and said "What's wrong, my sweet little boy?" The boy sniffled and said "It hurts. I got a splinter." His mother smiled and said "Let me try to help you." She carefully pulled out the splinter and put a piece of gum on it. The little boy asked "Will it help?" His mother replied "Yes, the gum will help your hand not hurt anymore." The little boy was so happy it worked and he couldn't thank his mother enough!
