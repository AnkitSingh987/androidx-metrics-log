Title: Anna and the Book

Anna likes to go to the library with her mom. She likes to look at books with many pictures and colors. She likes to hear stories from the nice lady who works there. She likes to play with the toys and puzzles in the corner. One day, Anna finds a new book on the shelf. It has a big picture of a lion on the cover. Anna loves lions. She wants to see more pictures of lions. She takes the book and opens it. But the book is not nice. It has sharp edges that hurt her fingers. Anna feels a drop of red on her hand. She cries. Mom hears Anna cry and runs to her. She sees the book and the blood. She is worried. She takes the book and puts it away. She hugs Anna and says sorry. She takes a band-aid from her bag and puts it on Anna's finger. She kisses it and says it will be better soon. Anna stops crying and smiles. She says thank you to mom. She does not want the book with the sharp edges anymore. She wants a different book. Mom helps her find a nice book with soft pages and many pictures of lions. Anna is happy. She sits on mom's lap and listens to the story. She forgets about the sharp book and the hurt finger. She loves the library.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was playing in the garden when she found a potato. She was so excited that she decided to kick it. The girl's mom saw her and said, "No, sweetheart. That potato isn't yours. You shouldn't kick it." But the girl was so curious that she couldn't help but give the potato a little kick. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise. Startled, she looked around. She noticed that a big rock had rolled away from where she had kicked the potato. There were two beautiful little yellow flowers underneath it! The girl was so happy and she ran to her mom. She said, "Mom, look what I found!" Her mom smiled and said, "See, sweetheart. If you hadn't kicked that potato, you wouldn't have noticed the flowers. That's why it's important to not take things that don't belong to you." The little girl learned an important lesson that day. She thanked the potato for showing her the beautiful flowers.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a delicate bird named Blue. Blue loved to sing songs all day long. She had many friends who liked to listen to her songs. One sunny day, while Blue was singing her favorite song, a little girl named Lily walked by. Lily stopped and listened to Blue's song. "I want to sing too," said Lily. "Can you teach me your song?" "Of course!" said Blue, happy to help. So, Blue and Lily spent the day singing together. They sang the same song over and over until Lily knew it by heart. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, Lily hugged Blue and said, "Thank you for teaching me your song. Now, I can sing it whenever I want." Blue was happy to share her song with Lily. From that day on, Blue and Lily became the best of friends, singing songs together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy box. It was in a room with a shower. A girl named Amy wanted to wrap the box for her friend's birthday. She tried to lift the box, but it was too heavy. Amy was sad. Amy asked her mom for help. Her mom came to the room with the shower. Together, they tried to lift the heavy box, but it was still too hard. They both felt sad. Then, they had an idea. They would try to wrap the heavy box in the room with the shower. They worked very hard, but the shower turned on by accident. The water made the paper wet and the box was not wrapped. The gift was ruined and Amy's friend did not get a nice gift. Everyone was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play on the slide in his backyard. He would slide down it every day with a big smile on his face. One day, Tim saw a zigzag on his slide. He thought it was an original idea to slide down the zigzag. So, he climbed up the ladder and slid down the zigzag slide. It was so much fun! Tim told all his friends about his original zigzag slide. They all came to play and slide down the zigzag too. They had a great time playing together on the fun slide. And they all lived happily ever after.
