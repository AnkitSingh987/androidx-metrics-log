Title: Alex and the Puzzle

One day, Alex and his mom were heading to a birthday party together. Alex was very excited for the party. He couldn't wait to wear his cute costume. "Mom, what is my costume?" he asked. "You'll have to wait and see!" giggled his mom. When they arrived at the party, Alex's mom showed him his costume. It was a red cape with a bright golden star! "Wow! I look so creative!" Alex was so happy he started bouncing up and down. His mom smiled and said, "I thought you'd like it! Now it's time to solve the biggest game of the afternoon." She pointed to a big, colorful puzzle in the corner of the room. Alex immediately hopped over to the puzzle, eager to begin. He soon found it wasn't very difficult to solve. In no time, Alex had solved the puzzle and was beaming from ear to ear. "Good job, Alex! I knew you could do it," his mom praised him. Alex was so proud of himself. He couldn't stop showing off his costume, and his parents couldn't stop bragging about how creative and clever their son was.

Jimmy was three. He was ready to go outside. He wanted to go on an adventure! He looked up and saw a big blue sky. He was so excited. Jimmy dreamed of flying up through the sky, high above the clouds. He waved his arms, pretending to fly in the air. He wanted to get even closer to the big blue sky. Jimmy looked around and saw a big tree. He ran and climbed up into its branches. He pretended he was flying around like a bird. He flew up through the blue sky until he could touch the clouds. Jimmy was so happy. He had dreamed of flying and he was ready to make his dream come true.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy lake. It was surrounded by a lush forest. One day, a bird was trying to guess where the lake was. He flew in every direction looking for it. At last, he heard a gurgling noise from the north. He guessed that it must have been the lake. When he flew there, he saw that he was right! The bird was so happy and sang a happy song. To show his appreciation, he tidied up around the lake. He picked up all the sticks, leaves and rubbish. When he had finished, the lake was even tidier than before! Everyone who visited the lake was impressed with how tidy it was. The bird was proud of his work.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was an ancient tree. It was very old and very big. All the kids liked to play near the tree. One day, the kids wanted to have a race. They were very excited. They decided to start the race near the ancient tree. They asked the tree if it wanted to speak to them. The tree said, "Yes, I will speak to you." The tree told the kids that it had seen many races before. It was happy to see the kids having fun. So, the kids had their race, and they all had a great time. The ancient tree was happy to speak with them and watch them play.

Lily and Ben are twins. They are three years old. They like to play with blocks and cars. Today is their birthday. They are very happy. Mom and Dad give them a big present. They open it together. Inside, there are many new blocks and cars. Lily and Ben clap their hands and say, "Thank you, Mom and Dad!" They start to build a tower with the blocks. They add more and more blocks. They make the tower very tall. They put a car on top of the tower. They say, "Look, Mom and Dad, we made a tower!" Mom and Dad smile and say, "Wow, that's a great tower! You are very clever!" Lily and Ben are cheerful. They hug Mom and Dad. They say, "We love you, Mom and Dad!" They play with their new blocks and cars all day. They have a lot of fun. They are very happy. It is a good birthday.
