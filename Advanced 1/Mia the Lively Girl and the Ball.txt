Title: Mia the Lively Girl and the Ball

One day, a lively little girl named Mia was playing in her yard. She saw a ball and thought it would be fun to play with it. Mia picked up the ball and said, "I am going to shoot the ball!" Mia's friend, Ben, came over to play. He saw the ball and said, "I want to shoot the ball too!" Mia and Ben took turns shooting the ball at a point on the fence. They were both very good at it. After a while, they got tired of shooting the ball. Mia said, "Let's do something else." Ben agreed and they went to play with their toy cars. They had a fun day playing together with their simple toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spike. Spike loved to play and run all day. One day, Spike found a big, red ball. He played with the ball and had lots of fun. Spike got tired after playing for a long time. He wanted to relax under a big tree. The tree had lots of shade to keep Spike cool. Spike lay down and closed his eyes. He felt very happy and relaxed. After resting, Spike was hungry. He went home to find some yummy food. His mom had made a yummy bowl of dog food just for him. Spike ate the food and felt very happy. Now, he was ready to play again.

When Sammy woke up, the sun was shining brightly. He was so excited that he dashed out of his bed and scampered around his room. He grabbed his favorite toy and headed out the door. As he walked outside, the wind was blowing strongly. He could see the leaves scurrying around and scattering in all directions. When he got to the kitchen, his mom was standing over the stove. She said, " Sammy, I'm making you something special today! I'm making you a big plate of spaghetti." Sammy grinned from ear to ear and smiled so hard his cheeks began to hurt. His mom put the spaghetti on his plate and poured some sauce on it. It smelled so delicious that Sammy started to salivate. When he tasted it, it was even better than he thought it would be. His mom laughed and said, "Sammy, I'm glad you liked it! Now you can be a more independent eater. He was so proud of himself. He had eaten all of his spaghetti and hadn't had to be spoon fed -he felt so independent! His mom smiled and said, "You're growing up too fast Sammy! You think you're all grown up, but don't forget you'll always be my little boy." Sammy smiled, hugged his mom and said, "I will never forget, mom."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to sing with her microphone. She would sing all day and all night. Her voice was mighty and strong. Everyone loved to hear her sing. One day, Sue found a magic wand. She waved it around and said, "I want to be mighty!" Suddenly, she started to shrink. She got smaller and smaller. Sue was scared. Her microphone was now too big for her to hold. Sue knew she needed to fix this. She held the magic wand and said, "Please make me big again!" Just like that, she grew back to her normal size. Sue was happy. She picked up her microphone and sang a mighty song. Everyone clapped and cheered for Sue.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a jolly doll that she loved very much. They played together every day. One day, Lily's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the jolly doll and wanted to play with it too. Lily said, "You can play with my doll, but you must promise to be nice to her." Sam promised with a big smile. But Sam was not nice to the jolly doll. He pulled her hair and threw her in the air. Lily was very sad and said, "You did not keep your promise!" Sam felt bad and said sorry. From that day on, Sam was always nice to the jolly doll, and they all played happily together.
