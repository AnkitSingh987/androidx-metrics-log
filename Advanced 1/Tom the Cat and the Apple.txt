Title: Tom the Cat and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. On a Tuesday, Tom found a sour apple under a tree. He did not like sour things, so he decided to give it to his friend, a small mouse named Jerry. Tom went to Jerry's house and said, "Jerry, I have a sour apple for you. Do you want it?" Jerry was happy and said, "Yes, Tom, thank you! I love sour apples!" Tom gave the apple to Jerry and Jerry started to rub it on his face, because it felt good. But then, something bad happened. The sour apple was too sour for Jerry's face. It made his face hurt and turn red. Jerry cried and said, "Tom, this apple is too sour for me!" Tom felt sad that he gave Jerry the sour apple. From that day on, Tom and Jerry decided to never play with sour apples again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in his garden. One day, he saw a red tomato growing on a plant. Tim wanted to pick the tomato. He tried to lean closer to the plant. But he was too small. So, he asked his friend, a big dog named Max, to help him. Max leaned close to the tomato plant. He gently picked the tomato with his mouth. Then, he gave the tomato to Tim. Tim was very happy. He said "thank you" to Max. They ate the little tomato together and it was very yummy.

Once upon a time, there was a careless boy who loved cherries. He was always eating them and never shared them. One day, he decided to split one of the cherries in half. He pulled it apart and discovered a little surprise inside: a tiny note! The note was written in a magical language that only he could understand. He read it and it said to go on an adventure! So he packed his bag, left the house and walked down the street. The boy soon came across a cherry tree. He climbed it and picked a bunch of the bright red cherries. Then he ate them all, one by one, and laughed in delight. Finally, the sun began to set and the boy slowly walked home. He knew that he would never split a cherry again… not without being careful! He thanked the cherry tree for his adventure and vowed to always be careful when splitting cherries from now on.

Once upon a time, there was a long, tall lamp. It lived in a small room with a little boy named Tim. Every night, Tim would turn on the lamp before he went to bed. The lamp made the room bright and happy. But one day, the lamp did not work. Tim was sad that the lamp did not work. He told his mom about the lamp. She said, "Let's go to the store and buy a new light for the lamp." Tim was happy to go to the store. They had to delay their trip because it was raining outside. Tim waited and looked out the window. After the rain stopped, Tim and his mom went to the store. They found a new light for the lamp. They went back home and put the new light in the lamp. The lamp was bright and happy again. Tim was so happy that he could sleep with the light on. The lamp was happy too, because it could make Tim feel safe at night.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to paint. One day, she decided to paint a big thing on her bedroom wall. She was excited to show her friends her new painting. Lily's friend, Tom, came over to play. "Look at my wall, Tom! I paint this big thing!" said Lily. Tom looked at the painting and said, "But my mom said we can't paint on walls. You might get in trouble." Lily was sad. She didn't want to get in trouble, but she wanted her painting to be popular with her friends. She thought of a plan to fix the problem. She asked her mom if she could paint on paper instead of the wall. Her mom said yes, and Lily painted a new thing on paper. She showed it to Tom and her other friends, and they all loved it. Lily's painting became popular, and she didn't get in trouble. Everyone was happy.
