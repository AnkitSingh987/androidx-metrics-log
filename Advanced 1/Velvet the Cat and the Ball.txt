Title: Velvet the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Velvet. Velvet loved to play with her ball all day. She would keep chasing it around the house and make everyone laugh. One day, Velvet lost her ball. She looked in every room, under the bed, and behind the door. Velvet was sad because she could not find her ball. She wanted to keep playing and make everyone laugh again. Then, Velvet's friend, a little bird, found the ball outside. The bird brought the ball back to Velvet. She was so happy! Velvet and the bird played with the ball together. They had a great time and kept making everyone laugh.

Sally hopped to the playground. She saw a giant maze. "Wow, it's so crazy!" she said to her mum. Her mum replied, "Yes, it is. Do you think you can find your way out?" Sally thought for a moment and said, "I hope so!" Sally started to hop through the maze, she went round and round, up and down. It looked like she would never find her way out! But suddenly, something clicked and Sally knew the way out! She hopped and hopped, until finally, she reached the end of the maze! "Yay! I did it!" Sally cheered. Her mum smiled and said, "I knew you could. You never stopped hoping!"

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old man named Mr. Brown. He lived on a farm with many animals. One day, he found a bag of oats and had an idea. Mr. Brown met a nice lady named Mrs. Green. They liked each other very much. One day, Mr. Brown asked Mrs. Green to marry him. Mrs. Green said yes, and they were very happy. The day of the wedding, something unexpected happened. The animals on the farm found the bag of oats and ate them all. Everyone was surprised, but they laughed and had a fun time. Mr. Brown was not grumpy anymore, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went on a cool safari with his mom and dad. They saw big animals like lions, elephants, and giraffes. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to pick a flower for his mom. He saw a pretty red one near a big tree. When he picked it, a small monkey jumped down from the tree! Tim was surprised. The monkey took the flower from Tim and gave him a banana instead. Tim's mom and dad laughed. They didn't know the monkey would trade with Tim. It was a fun and unexpected day on their cool safari.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a rose. The rose was red and pretty. It lived with many other flowers. They were all happy and calm in the garden. One day, a big bird came. The bird wanted to take the rose away. The rose did not want to go. It wanted to stay in the garden with its friends. The rose had a plan. It would escape from the bird. The rose waited for the wind. The wind came and the rose flew away. The bird could not catch the rose. The rose was back with its friends in the garden. They were all happy and calm again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and her daddy. Lily loved to play with her toys and make her mommy and daddy guess what she was doing. One sunny day, Lily went to the park with her mommy. She saw a big, open field with lots of flowers. She ran to the flowers and started to pick them. Lily's mommy called out, "Lily, come back! Don't go too far!" Lily shouted back, "I promise, mommy! I won't go far!" As Lily picked the flowers, she saw a little boy near a tree. He was playing with a red ball. Lily walked up to him and said, "Hi, my name is Lily. What's your name?" The little boy smiled and said, "I'm Tim. Nice to meet you, Lily." They played together, guessing which flower was the prettiest and making promises to be friends forever. And that's how Lily and Tim became the best of friends.
