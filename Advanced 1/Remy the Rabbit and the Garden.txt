Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to eat radishes. One day, Remy was very hungry. He hopped and hopped to find some food. Remy found a big radish in a garden. It was red and looked yummy. Remy was so happy! He wanted to bite the radish and eat it all up. But Remy was a good rabbit and asked the garden's owner, a kind old man, if he could eat the radish. The kind old man said, "Yes, Remy, you can eat the radish." Remy took a big bite of the radish. It was so tasty! Remy was not hungry anymore. He thanked the old man and hopped back home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and shoot toy balls at each other all day long. One day, dark clouds came, and rain started to fall. Tim and his friends were sad because they could not play outside. They watched the rain from the window and wished they could be outside. Then, Tim had an idea. He asked his friends to grab their toy balls and they began to shoot them inside the house. They laughed and played together, forgetting about the rain outside. They had so much fun that they didn't even notice when the rain stopped. And that day, Tim and his friends learned that they could have fun no matter what the weather was like.

Once upon a time, there was a little chair that lived in a small house. The chair was very sad because it felt helpless. It wanted to be a big and strong chair like the others in the house. It didn't like when the other chairs would tease it for being small. One day, a big storm came and shook the house. The wind blew and the rain poured. The big chairs were scared and didn't know what to do. They felt helpless too. The little chair knew it had to do something to help the big chairs and the house. The little chair was very brave and went outside to face the storm. It pushed and pushed against the wind and rain. The little chair was strong and helped to keep the house safe. The big chairs were so thankful and never teased the little chair again. They all lived happily together in the house.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy who wanted to show everyone her drawing. She was so proud of her drawing and wanted to share it with her friends. So one day, she went around asking everyone for their opinions about her art. Everyone gave her a big smile and said she did a great job. They congratulated her and clapped their hands. Lucy was so happy to receive the applause, so she asked her best friend to help her make an even better drawing. But her friend had a different opinion – she thought it was too hard to draw something better. Lucy felt uncomfortable when she heard this and started to cry. Her friend didn't want to make Lucy sad, so she hugged her and tried to cheer her up. She pointed out that everyone had complimented her drawing, and that was the most important opinion. She reminded Lucy of all the people who had applauded her art. Hearing this made Lucy feel much better. She knew that if lots of people were applauding her art, she must be doing something right.

Mia and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. Mia has a lot of toys. She is gifted. She can make many things with her toys. Ben has few toys. He likes to watch Mia play. One day, Mia and Ben are playing in Mia's room. Mia is making a castle with her blocks. Ben is sitting on the floor. He is holding a teddy bear. "Can I play with your blocks?" Ben asks. "Sorry, no. These are my blocks. I need them for my castle," Mia says. "But I have nothing to play with," Ben says. He feels sad. Mia looks at Ben. She sees his sad face. She thinks for a moment. She remembers that sharing is good. She decides to be kind. "Okay, you can play with some of my blocks. Here, take these ones. They are blue and green. You can make a garden for my castle," Mia says. "Really? Thank you, Mia. You are a good friend," Ben says. He smiles. He takes the blocks. He starts to make a garden. Mia and Ben play together. They have fun. They share their toys. They are happy.
