Title: Sarah the Girl and the Wagon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was very excited that day because she was going to the fair. She had heard there were lots of games and tasty treats to enjoy. Sarah asked her mom. “Can I go to the fair, please?” Her mom smiled and said, “Yes, but you need to meet me there. I’m going to introduce you to your new wagon.” When Sarah arrived at the fair she looked around in amazement. There were so many things to see and do! Then, Sarah heard her mom call out her name. Sarah ran over and hugged her mom. Her mom introduced her to her new wagon. It was bright red with yellow stars on it and had big wheels. Sarah was so excited to take her wagon for a ride around the fair. She raced around in it, playing games and trying all the fun activities. Sarah had a fair day!

One day, a lovely little deer was in the woods. It was sunny and warm. The deer was happy and liked to jump and play. In the woods, there was a big tree. The deer wanted to touch the tree. A small boy named Tim came to the woods. He saw the lovely deer and wanted to be friends. Tim said, "Hi deer, can I touch you?" The deer was shy and did not know if it was safe. But the deer wanted to be friends too. Suddenly, the tree started to move! The deer and Tim were surprised. The tree was not a tree, it was a big green monster! The monster was nice and just wanted to play. Tim, the deer, and the big green monster became best friends and played in the woods every day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a selfish boy. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim went for a walk with his big sack. He wanted to find more toys to put in his sack. While on his walk, Tim found many toys. He put them all in his big sack. He saw other kids playing with their toys, but he did not want to share his toys with them. He kept walking and looking for more toys. Then, Tim met an old man. The old man told Tim that sharing is good. Tim thought about it and decided to try sharing. He went back to the kids he saw earlier and shared his toys from his sack. They all played together and had fun. Tim learned that sharing is better than being selfish.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to put on a show for her family. She built a stage in her room using chairs and a big sheet. Lily was so happy and couldn't wait to be successful. Lily asked her mom, "Can I show you my play on the stage?" Her mom said, "Yes, I would love to see it!" Lily put on a funny hat and started her play. She danced, sang, and told jokes. At the end of the play, Lily's mom clapped and cheered. She said, "You did a great job, Lily!" Then, she gave Lily a big kiss on the cheek. Lily felt so successful and happy. She couldn't wait to put on another show.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl called Anna who had a birthday. Her room was very tidy and she was very excited for her special day. When Anna woke up, she saw that her room had been decorated with colourful lights and balloons. Anna's mum and dad were there too, smiling at her. Anna said, "What a nice surprise!" Then she looked around the room and gazed at all of the decorations. She felt very happy. Anna's mum gave her a big hug and said, "Happy birthday, sweetheart!" Then they enjoyed a delicious cake with lots of sweets on top. Anna was so excited and couldn't stop smiling. It was the best birthday ever!

Once upon a time, there was an alligator named Ally. Ally was an honest alligator who lived in a big pond. She had many friends who liked to play with her. One day, Ally's friend, a little bird named Benny, was worried. Benny said, "Ally, I lost my toy ball. I am sad." Ally wanted to help her friend, so she said, "Don't worry, Benny. I will help you find your ball." They looked all around the pond, and finally, they found the ball near a tree. Benny was very happy and said, "Thank you, Ally, for being an honest and good friend." They played with the ball together and had lots of fun.
