Title: The Witch and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a peaceful forest, there lived a nice witch. She had a big book with many pretty pictures. She liked to look at the pictures every day. One day, a naughty boy came to the forest. He saw the witch's book and wanted to see the pictures too. But the boy was not careful. He tried to turn the pages too fast and he tore a page. The witch was very sad. The witch thought about being angry, but she had an idea. She used her magic to fix the torn page. Then, she showed the boy how to be gentle with the book. The boy said sorry and they looked at the pictures together. They became good friends and the forest stayed peaceful.

Once there was a boy. He liked to be creative. He always had ideas in his head. One day, he was out walking and he saw a pole. He thought it looked interesting. He wanted to know what it could do. He started to move around it. He moved his arms up and down and he even spun around it. He repeated the movements over and over again. He felt so happy and creative. He couldn't believe he was having so much fun! Then he stopped and a voice came from behind him. It was his mom. She said, "What are you doing?" The boy smiled and said, "I'm being creative, mom. I'm just having fun with the pole." His mom smiled and said, "That sounds like fun. Keep doing it." So the boy smiled and went back to repeating his creative movements around the pole. He had so much fun and he never tired of it. He realized the pole was magical. He continued to use it to make new and exciting things.

Once upon a time there was a monkey called Tim. Tim was very adventurous, so he decided to go explore the jungle. He ran and ran until he spotted a big bulb, shining brightly at the top of a big tree. “I’ve never seen something like this before!” gasped Tim. He climbed up the tree and carefully matched the bulb in his pocket. Tim was so excited and couldn’t wait to show it off. Just then, Tim heard a sound. He turned around and saw two elephants standing beside him. “What are you doing?” one of the elephants asked. “I’m matching this bulb,” Tim replied. The elephants laughed, and one said, “You are so adventurous!”. Tim smiled, put the bulb back in his pocket, and ran off to show his friends. He was so proud of himself and his new bulb!

One day, a girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy some cream. It was a warm day, so Lily wore her favorite dress. On the way to the store, Lily saw her friend Tom. "Hi Tom!" said Lily. "We are going to buy cream. Do you want to come with us?" "Yes, I want to come!" Tom said. So, they all went to the store together. At the store, Lily's mom let her pick the cream. But when they went to pay, the man at the store said, "I forgot to mention, the cream is free today!" Lily, Tom, and Lily's mom were very happy. They took the cream and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She lived in a happy and safe home with her mom and dad. One day when the girl was playing outside, she noticed a strange little animal in the garden. "What is that?", she asked her dad. He replied "It's a little bunny". The girl was very excited and asked if she can keep it. Her dad looked sad and told her "No sweetheart, this bunny would not be safe here. He needs to go back to his family in the woods." The girl was so sad. She had never had a pet before. She asked her dad why she can't keep the bunny. Her dad tried to explain, but the little girl didn't understand. Tears started rolling down her face. Suddenly, her dad had an idea. He said "You can keep the bunny for one weekend. We'll teach him about safety and how to live in the wild. Then he can go back to his family". The girl was so happy and hugged her dad. She took good care of the bunny and made sure he was safe. At the end of the weekend, the bunny hopped back to his family in the woods. The little girl waved goodbye and was glad to know he was safe.
