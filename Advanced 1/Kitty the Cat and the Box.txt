Title: Kitty the Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a big house with her best friend, a little girl named Amy. They loved to play together all day long. One day, while they were playing, they found a dusty box in the basement. Kitty and Amy were very curious, so they opened the box. Inside, they found a toy that was very important to Amy's mommy. The toy was very old and covered in dust. Amy's mommy had lost the toy when she was a little girl, and she had been looking for it for a long time. Kitty and Amy decided to rescue the toy from the dust. They carefully cleaned it and made it look like new. When they gave the toy to Amy's mommy, she was so happy! She hugged Kitty and Amy, and they all played with the important toy together. The end.

Once three friends, Suzie, Sam and Mark, wanted to make a big surprise for their mom. They had a great idea: they planned to make a special cake. The friends started to bake the cake and they added lots of love. When they put the cake down in front of their mom, they saw that she was very happy. The children were excited, but then something else happened. Just then, their big brother Thomas came in and he saw the cake. He was so spoiled that he spoiled their surprise. He said he wanted to have the cake all to himself! The children were so sad. But then, Suzie had a bright idea. She asked Thomas to help them decorate the cake. He liked the idea and agreed. They shined the cake with sprinkles and it looked amazing. Their mom was so proud of how they worked togethr to make the cake. The friends were very happy and laughed with each other. They all enjoyed the cake - and their surprise - a lot!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the sun. Tim had a small garden where he grew pretty flowers. He liked to dig in the soil and plant seeds. One day, Tim decided to become a gardener. He put on his hat and went outside. He dug and dug in the soil. His hands got dirty, but he didn't care. He was having fun making holes for the seeds. Tim planted the seeds and watered them. He watched as the seeds grew into beautiful flowers. The flowers made Tim very happy. Even though he got dirty, he knew it was worth it. And that's how Tim became a great gardener.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to gather pretty rocks in her backyard. One day, her aunt came to visit her. Lily wanted to show her aunt all the pretty rocks she had found. Her aunt looked at the rocks and saw one that was very shiny. She said, "Lily, this one looks fake." Lily looked at the shiny rock and said, "No, it is real. I found it by the big tree." Lily and her aunt played with the rocks all day. They made a big pile of rocks and then put them back where they found them. Lily was happy that her aunt liked her pretty rocks, even the fake one.

Once upon a time there were two friends called Ryan and Dave. They were both chubby and liked to play with their toys. One day, Ryan had a great idea. He wanted to mix all the toys together. Dave was excited but didn't want to do it. He said, "No, we can't do that! They won't work if we mix them up". Ryan didn't want to listen. He kept saying "Mix! Mix!" He even started to mix the toys with his hands. Dave saw him and ran over. He shouted "No Ryan! That's not a good tip!" Ryan stopped and thought for a moment. Then he said, "Okay, maybe we don't need to mix them together. We can just play with them like normal". Dave was relieved and thanked Ryan for his chubby little brain. They had learnt a valuable lesson that day - that mixing things up is not always the right thing to do.
