Title: Momo the Monkey and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo lived in a big tree with his family. He loved to play with his friends all day long. One day, Momo saw a tough vine hanging from a tall tree. He decided to climb it. As he climbed higher and higher, he saw a big bird trapped in a net. The bird said, "Help me, please! I need to escape!" Momo wanted to help the bird. So, he used the vine to swing over to the net. He pulled and pulled until the net broke. The bird was free! "Thank you, Momo!" said the bird. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy was sad because it was a gloomy day outside. She wanted to play with her friends, but they couldn't come over. Amy's mom saw her looking sad and said, "Amy, let's make a yummy dish together. We can share it with our friends later when the sun comes out." Amy smiled and said, "Yes, Mommy! I want to make a big dish to share with everyone." They went to the kitchen and made a big, yummy dish. Amy and her mom had fun making it together. When they finished, the sun came out, and Amy's friends came over to play. Amy said, "Friends, I made a dish to share with you!" Everyone was happy and enjoyed the yummy dish. They played together all day long. And from that day on, gloomy days were never sad for Amy because she knew she could always make a dish to share with her friends.

Yesterday, Anna and Ben went to the park with Mom. They saw a big slide, a swing, and a merry-go-round. Anna wanted to ride the merry-go-round. She liked the horses with the shiny hair and the bells. Ben wanted to ride the slide. He liked to go fast and feel the wind. Mom gave them some coins and they ran to the rides. Anna picked a white horse and Ben climbed to the top of the slide. The merry-go-round started to move and Anna held on to the horse. She felt happy and pretended she was a princess. The slide was clear and Ben could see the whole park. He slid down and laughed. He felt brave and wanted to go again. They rode the merry-go-round and the slide many times. They had a lot of fun. Mom watched them and smiled. She was proud of them. The sun was bright and the sky was clear. It was a good day.

One day, a cheerful boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big yellow ball in the park. Tim loved to play with balls, so he picked it up and started to play. He kicked the yellow ball high into the sky and it came back down. Tim was very happy. As Tim played, a little girl named Sue walked up to him. She looked sad. "That's my yellow ball," she said. "I lost it when I was playing here yesterday. Can I have it back, please?" Tim remembered that it was important to share, so he gave the yellow ball back to Sue. "Thank you," she said, and they both started to play together. As they played, something unexpected happened. The yellow ball flew up into a tree and got stuck! Tim and Sue looked at each other, not knowing what to do. Just then, a friendly dog came running up to them. It jumped high into the air and knocked the yellow ball out of the tree. Tim and Sue were so surprised and happy. They thanked the dog and kept playing together with their new friend.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived an ancient tree. The tree had a big circle on its trunk. All the animals loved the tree and played near it every day. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily came to the forest. She saw the circle on the tree and wanted to touch it. Lily didn't know that when she touched the circle, something unexpected would happen. As Lily touched the circle, the ancient tree started to talk! It said, "Hello, Lily! I am a magic tree. I can give you a wish!" Lily was so surprised. She thought and thought, and finally wished for all the animals in the forest to talk, too. The tree made her wish come true. From that day on, Lily and the animals had so much fun talking and playing together in the big forest.
