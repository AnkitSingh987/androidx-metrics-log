Title: True the Girl and the Apple

One day, a girl named True went for a walk. She wanted to discover new things. She walked and walked until she found a big tree. Under the tree, she saw a healthy, green apple. True was so happy and hungry, so she picked up the apple. As she was about to take a bite, the apple began to talk! True was so surprised. The apple said, "Please don't eat me! I am a magic apple. If you help me, I will give you a gift." True thought about it and said, "Okay, I will help you. What do you need?" The magic apple wanted to go back to its family in the apple tree. True put the apple back in the tree with the other apples. Then, the magic apple said, "Thank you, True! Now, I will give you a gift." Suddenly, all the apples in the tree turned into yummy, sweet candy. True was so happy and shared the candy with all her friends. They all had a fun and tasty day!

Once upon a time, there was a bee who needed some help. He asked all the animals, but none of them could give him what he needed. Finally, he asked a pretty butterfly. "Oh, Butterfly. I need your help," said the bee. She smiled and said, "Yes, Bee. I can help you. What is it?" The bee said, "I need to find some flowers so I can get some nectar. I am so hungry!" The butterfly looked around, and saw a pretty meadow in the distance. She pointed to the meadow and said, "The flowers you need are there. Go quickly and you will find what you need." The bee flew off and soon found the meadow. The flowers were so beautiful and there was so much nectar! He was so happy he did not know what to do. He thanked the butterfly for all her help and flew back to thank her. The moral of the story is that when we are in need of help, it is best to ask and we may find an answer.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim saw a big storm coming. He saw lightning in the sky. Tim was scared, but his mom said, "Don't worry, it's just a storm." Tim and his mom went inside the house. Tim's mom said, "Let's hang your coat." They hung the coat on a hook. Tim felt better. The storm was loud, but Tim's mom made it easy for him to be brave. She said, "Let's count the time between the lightning and the thunder." They counted together and Tim was not scared anymore.

One day, a big tornado came to a dry town. The people in the town were scared. They did not know what to do. So, they all went inside their homes and shut the doors. A little girl named Mia saw the tornado from her window. She was very brave. Mia said to her mom, "Mom, we need to help the people in the town!" Her mom was scared but agreed with Mia. Mia and her mom went outside with big fans. They turned on the fans and pointed them at the tornado. The tornado got smaller and smaller. Then, it went away! The people in the town were happy and thanked Mia and her mom for saving them.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a delicate flower. It was waiting to bloom. Nearby, an infant was playing with his toy. One day, a bee came to the flower and said, "Hello, little flower. When will you bloom?" The flower replied, "I want to bloom, but I am scared. What if the infant hurts me while playing?" The bee told the flower, "Don't worry, I will talk to the infant and ask him to be careful." The bee flew to the infant and said, "Hello, little one. Please be careful when you play near the delicate flower. She wants to bloom, but she is scared you might hurt her." The infant smiled and said, "I will be careful, Mr. Bee. I want to see the flower bloom too." The next day, the delicate flower bloomed, and the infant and the bee were both happy to see the beautiful flower. They all played together in the garden, and the flower was no longer scared.
