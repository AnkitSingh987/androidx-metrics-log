Title: The Story of the Door and the Boy

Once there was a little boy who was looking for something special. He rushed to the kitchen in search of it. When he opened the cupboard door, he found something unusual. It gleamed and sparkled in the light! It was a delicious dessert! The little boy quickly grabbed hold of it and rushed out of the kitchen. He was so happy to have found this special little treat. He knew it would make his day! He took his unusual dessert and enjoyed it with a smile on his face.

Once upon a time there was a naughty little bunny. His name was Little Pops. Little Pops had a favorite thing to do: run around the flower garden pretending to escape! He would hop away with a poppy in his mouth. One day, Little Pops's mommy caught him playing in the garden. "Little Pops, why are you being so naughty?" she asked with a stern voice. Little Pops stopped running and said, "I was pretending to escape with my poppy!" Mommy laughed and said, "Well, you can't escape forever. Come on now, it's time to go inside." So Little Pops hung his head a bit and followed his mommy back inside the house. But the next day, he was back in the garden pretending to escape with his poppy again!

One day, a large bird named Bob wanted to win a race. Bob liked to soar high in the sky. He saw other birds who wanted to race too. They all got ready to start the race. The race began and all the birds flew fast. Bob tried his best to keep up. He soared higher and higher. The other birds were fast, but Bob was faster. In the end, Bob won the race. All the birds clapped for him. Bob was happy that he could soar high and win the race. He knew that being large did not stop him from being fast.

Johnny liked gum. Each day he would beg his dad to get him a pack. His dad promised if he was good, he would get him a pack. But, Johnny was being very spoiled. He wouldn't listen to his dad and he didn't do what he was told. Dad warned him that if he didn't start listening, he wouldn't get any gum. One day, Johnny didn't listen and dad kept his promise. He said no gum for Johnny. Johnny was sad he didn't get his gum. He decided to try to be better so he could get it. He started being good and listened to his dad. One day, dad surprised him and brought him a pack of gum. Johnny was so happy he had been good and earned the gum. From then on, Johnny promised to be good and not be spoiled so he could keep getting his gum.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a toy bear that she loved very much. One day, Sally saw her friend, Tim, playing with a new toy car. Sally felt jealous because she wanted a toy car too. She asked her mom if she could have one, but her mom said the price was too high. Sally thought of a plan to get the toy car. She went to Tim's house and took the toy car when he was not looking. Sally played with the toy car all day, but she felt bad for taking it from Tim without asking. The next day, Sally went to Tim's house and said, "I'm sorry I took your toy car. I was jealous and I know it was wrong. Can you forgive me?" Tim said, "Yes, I forgive you, but please ask next time." Sally felt happy that Tim forgave her, and they played together with the toy car and the bear. From that day on, Sally learned to always ask before taking something and to not be jealous of others.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He loved to sleep all day and eat snacks. One day, Tom's owner bought a big freezer. It was very cold inside, and it could keep food frozen. Tom was very curious about the freezer. He wanted to see what was inside. So, when his owner was not looking, he jumped up and opened the door. He saw lots of yummy treats inside. Tom wanted to eat them, but they were attached to the freezer with a string. Tom was very smart. He thought he could take the treats without anyone knowing. He tried to pull the treats out, but the string was very strong. Tom pulled and pulled, but the treats didn't come out. Then, something unexpected happened. The freezer door closed, and Tom was stuck inside! Tom tried to meow for help, but no one could hear him. He was very cold and scared. He wished he hadn't been so lazy and curious. In the end, Tom's owner found him, but it was too late. Tom was now a very cold, frozen cat. He learned that being too lazy and curious can lead to a bad ending.
