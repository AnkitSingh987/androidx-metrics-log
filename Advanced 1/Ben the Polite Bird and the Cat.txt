Title: Ben the Polite Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a polite bird named Ben. Ben loved to whistle happy songs all day long. One day, Ben found a notebook on the ground. He picked it up with his beak and took it to his tree. In the tree, Ben met his friend, Cat. Cat was very curious about the notebook. "What's that?" asked Cat. "It's a notebook," said Ben. "I found it on the ground." Cat wanted to see what was inside the notebook, so they opened it together. Inside the notebook, there were many pictures of animals and trees. Ben and Cat looked at the pictures and talked about them. They had so much fun! Ben started to whistle a happy song, and Cat joined in. They spent the whole day looking at the notebook and whistling together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big pocket on his shirt. He liked to put things in his pocket. One day, Tim found a big red fruit. It looked good to eat, so he put it in his pocket. Later, Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue had a small green fruit. She said it was very good to taste. Tim wanted to taste it too. So, they traded fruits. Tim gave Sue the big red fruit from his pocket, and Sue gave Tim the small green fruit. Tim took a big bite of the green fruit. But it did not taste good. It tasted very bad! Tim felt guilty because he gave Sue the big red fruit. Sue took a big bite of the red fruit, and it tasted very good. Tim was sad because he wanted the good fruit back. But Sue did not want to trade back. Tim learned that not all things that look good taste good, and sometimes you can't go back to fix things.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat. She liked to watch what was happening in her neighbourhood all the time. One day, she was looking out the window when she saw some motion. A man was climbing a tree with a big saw! The cat got excited and wanted to see what would happen next. The man started to cut the tree, things were falling from it. The cat watched with curiosity, she had never seen anything like this before. After a while the man stopped cutting, he was tired from all his work. He took a break, but the cat was still curious. She wanted to know what the man had done. The man started to carry the pieces of the tree away. Finally, the cat realised what he had done - he had cut the tree down! She was so surprised to see how much hard work the man had put into it. The nosy cat was excited. She had seen something new and interesting today. She watched until the man finished, then ran off to tell all her cat friends what she had seen.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy boat in the tub. One day, his boat had a leak. The boat got wet and started to sink. Tim was sad. Tim asked his big sister, Sue, for help. Sue said, "I will help you fix the boat, but first you must clean your room." Tim did not want to clean his room. He said, "No! I refuse to clean my room!" Sue got upset and left. Tim decided to try to fix the leak himself. He found some tape and put it on the boat. The tape worked! The boat did not sink anymore. Tim was very happy. When Sue saw that Tim fixed the boat by himself, she was proud of him. She said, "Well done, Tim! You fixed the boat all by yourself!"

Once upon a time, there lived a happy family on the east side of town. They decided to decorate their house for a special occasion. To get the task done, they worked together to make sure everything was organized. “Let’s hang streamers from the ceiling,” said Mom. “We should also put up balloons!” said Dad. But when the little one heard about how fun it was going to be, she couldn’t resist getting involved as well. “I wanna help too!” she said. The family said yes and let her help them to decorate. But the little one was too excited and started taking all the decorations out of the box and throwing them around the house. It was a mess! The family got mad and told the little one that it was not okay to be so disorganized. The little one started to cry and ran to her room. The moral of the story is that it’s important to be organized when you’re decorating. If you’re too excited, it can lead to a bad outcome.
