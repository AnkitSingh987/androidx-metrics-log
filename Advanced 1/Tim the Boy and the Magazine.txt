Title: Tim the Boy and the Magazine

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to read his favorite magazine. It had many pictures of animals and fun stories inside. Tim would read his magazine every day. One day, Tim took his magazine to the park. While playing, his magazine got dirty from the mud. Tim was sad and worried about his magazine. He wanted to protect it and keep it clean. Tim's mom saw him and knew what to do. She wrapped the magazine in a big plastic bag to protect it from dirt. Tim was happy and could read his magazine again. From that day on, Tim always took care of his things and kept them clean.

Once upon a time, a soft bunny named Bob lived near a big river. One day, he saw a raft by the river. He wanted to play with the raft, so he jumped on it. The raft started to float away with Bob on it. As the raft moved, Bob saw a big duck named Dina. Dina was not happy. She demanded, "Stop! That is my raft!" But Bob did not know how to stop the raft. It kept going down the river. Dina flew to the raft and helped Bob stop it. They became friends and played together on the raft. They had so much fun, and they shared the raft from that day on.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a man who liked to buy things. He had a big room where he kept all his things. He was very organized. He had a special place for everything. One day, he went to the store to buy a pipe. He wanted to use the pipe to make a fun game. When he got home, he put the pipe in his big room. He was happy to see it there. The man played with the pipe every day. His friends would come over and they would all play together. They had so much fun with the pipe. The man was glad he bought it. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim and his mom. Tim loved to eat celery. He would eat it every day. One day, his mom brought home a big bag of celery from the store. Tim was very happy and ready to eat. Tim's mom said, "Tim, let's break the celery into small pieces first." So, they broke the celery together. Snap! Snap! Tim liked the sound it made. They put the small pieces of celery in a bowl. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. They shared the celery and talked. "I like celery too," said Sam. They laughed and ate all the celery until the bowl was empty. They had a fun day playing and eating their favorite snack.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a small, old house. The house looked sad. Tim wanted to see inside. He tried to open the door, but it was locked. He looked around and found a key. The key was in the ash from a burnt tree. Tim felt happy. He used the key to unlock the door. Inside, he saw a big, bitter fruit. The fruit was very big and very bitter. Tim wanted to taste it. He took a small bite. Suddenly, the house started to shake. Tim was scared. The house stopped shaking. Tim looked around and saw that the house was now clean and pretty. The old house was now a new house. The bitter fruit was gone. Tim learned that the fruit was magic. It made the old house new again. Tim felt happy and went back home.

One day, a little fish named Tuna was swimming in the sea. Tuna had a special gift. When the sun went down, Tuna would glow. Tuna's glow was very pretty and all the other fish loved to see it. Tuna had a friend named Fair. Fair was a very nice crab. They liked to play together. They would swim and dance in the water. When Tuna glowed, they would play even more. One night, Tuna and Fair had a big party. All the fish and crabs came to see Tuna glow. They danced and played all night. Everyone was happy and had lots of fun. And Tuna's glow made the sea look so pretty.
