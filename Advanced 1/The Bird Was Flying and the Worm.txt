Title: The Bird Was Flying and the Worm

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The little bird saw a helpless worm on the ground. The worm was lost and could not find its way home. The little bird felt sad for the worm and wanted to help. The bird said to the worm, "Hello, little worm. You look lost. I can help you find your way home." The worm was happy to receive help from the bird. The bird picked up the worm in its beak and flew up into the sky. Together, they looked for the worm's home. Soon, they found it under a big tree. The bird put the worm down, and the worm said, "Thank you, bird, for helping me find my home." The bird was happy to help the worm. From that day on, the bird and the worm became good friends. The moral of the story is: Always be kind and help others when they are in need.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Ally. She had long, curly hair, big bright eyes and a very big smile. One day Ally found a beautiful jewel in her garden. When she saw it, her eyes sparkled and she smiled even more. Ally went to her mum and said, “Mommy, look! I found a jewel!” Her mum smiled happily, “That’s lovely dear.” Ally wanted to keep the jewel, but her mum said, “No, that’s not yours. You must give it back to who it belongs to.” Ally was not happy. She wanted to keep it. Ally argued with her mum and after a while her mum said, “If you give back the jewel, I will buy you a new toy and you can stay in your room in a comfortable bed.” Ally smiled and agreed. She found the owner of the jewel and then her mum bought her a brand new toy and a comfortable bed. Ally was now happy and no longer wanted the jewel.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little dog named Spot. Spot liked to read books. He had many books in his small house. One day, Spot wanted to go outside and play. He put on his red hat and went out. While Spot was walking, he saw a big puddle. He thought it would be fun to jump in it. So, Spot jumped into the puddle with a big splash! His red hat fell into the puddle too. Spot was wet and happy. Just then, a friendly cat named Luna came by. Luna saw Spot and said, "Hi Spot! What are you doing?" Spot replied, "I am playing in the puddle. It is fun!" Luna smiled and said, "Can I play with you?" Spot was happy and said, "Yes, let's play together!" So, Spot and Luna played in the puddle and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a bicycle that he loved very much. One day, he rode his bicycle to the park to play with his friends. But when he got there, he saw that his friends were not happy. They looked miserable. Tim asked his friends, "Why are you sad?" His friend Sarah said, "We don't have bicycles like you, so we can't play together." Tim thought for a moment. He wanted to find a way to help his friends feel happy again. Then, Tim had a great idea. He said, "Let's take turns riding my bicycle!" All his friends liked the idea. They took turns on the bicycle and played together at the park. Everyone was happy again, and they had a fun day.

Jimmy was on his dream vacation with his family. They were visiting a beautiful place and having a lot of fun. Jimmy wanted to explore more, but his parents wanted to stay in the same spot. Jimmy complained and said, "It's my vacation too, why can't we do something else?" His parents said, "No, we have to stay here. It's too dangerous to go out alone." Jimmy was frustrated and said, "Why are you so selfish? I just want to escape and explore the world!" Jimmy's parents were worried and said, "It's not safe to go out alone. We can do something fun here instead." Jimmy thought for awhile and said, "Okay, I'll stay here today. But I'm still going to escape when I can!" Jimmy's parents didn't want him to go, but they understood how important exploring was to him. They hugged him and agreed that he could go, but he had to be careful. Jimmy was relieved to escape and explore outside. He learned a lot and had the best vacation of his life.
