Title: Sue the Girl and the Cake

One day, a little girl named Sue was in the kitchen with her mom. Sue wanted to help her mom make a tasty cake. Her mom said, "Yes, you can help me stir the mix." Sue took a big spoon and stirred the mix. As she stirred, she saw an image in the mix. It was a funny face! Sue laughed and showed her mom the funny face. When the cake was done, Sue and her mom ate it. It was the most tasty cake they ever had. They were happy they made the cake together.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Sue. Sue liked to test things. One day, she saw a big pot in the kitchen. Sue thought, "I want to test this pot!" She put the pot on the floor and tried to sit in it. But Sue was too big for the pot, and it broke. Sue was sad. She knew she made a mess. Her mom was mad at her. Sue learned not to test things without asking first.

Once upon a time, a pilot named Tom lived in a small town. Tom loved to fly his big plane in the sky. He was very good at it. Tom was also competitive, which means he liked to win. One day, Tom met a new friend named Sue. Sue also liked to fly planes. They decided to have a race in the sky. Tom said, "I will win!" Sue replied, "No, I will win!" They flew their planes up, up, up into the sky. They raced around the clouds. Tom was fast, but Sue was faster. In the end, Sue won the race. Tom was sad, but he knew that it was just a fun game. From that day on, Tom and Sue became best friends. They flew their planes together and had many more races. Sometimes Tom won, and sometimes Sue won. They learned that being competitive can be fun, but it is more important to be good friends and enjoy the time together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom heard a leak coming from his bedroom. He was very curious and explored his bedroom, looking for the leak. He eventually found a small hole in the wall where the leak was coming from. Tom was so enthusiastic that he took a hammer and tried to patch up the hole. Unfortunately, the patch didn't fit and the leak stayed open. His mom came to help him and showed him the right materials to fix it. Tom learned an important lesson that day: It's always better to ask for help when you don't know how to do something. Don't be too hasty and be willing to learn from others. With the right advice and help from others, you can achieve almost anything.

Tommy was playing in the park when he suddenly heard a loud bang! He looked around, but he couldn't see anything. Then he heard a loud roar - it was a flood coming! Tommy was scared and started to run. He raced through the park, trying to find somewhere safe. Suddenly he bumped into Sarah. She smiled at Tommy and said: "Don't worry, I know a place we can stay safe." She grabbed hold of Tommy's hand, and they raced off together. Sarah led them both to her family's house. The flood was coming close, and they just managed to get in time. Everyone was so grateful that Sarah was so thoughtful. Once they were inside, Sarah looked out at the flood with Tommy. They both said: "Let's never race the flood again!" Everyone laughed, and Tommy agreed.

Once upon a time, there was a big, lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day and play all night. He lived in a small house with a girl named Sue. Sue loved the color purple and had many purple things. One day, Sue decided to display her purple toys. She put them on a table in her room. Tom saw the pretty purple toys and wanted to play with them. But he knew he had to wait until Sue was not looking. Sue went to the kitchen to get a snack. Tom saw his chance and jumped onto the table. He played with the purple toys, but he was too lazy to put them back. When Sue came back, she saw the mess and knew it was Tom. Sue told Tom that he should not play with her purple toys. Tom felt sad and said sorry to Sue. From that day on, Tom learned to play with his own toys and not touch Sue's purple display. Sue and Tom were happy and played together every day.
