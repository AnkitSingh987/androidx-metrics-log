Title: Max the Dog and the Lily

One day, a playful dog named Max found a mixer in the park. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was a toy. He picked it up with his mouth and started to run around with it. Max saw his friend, a little girl named Lily. "Look what I found, Lily!" said Max. "Do you know what it is?" Lily looked at the mixer and said, "That does not belong to you, Max. It is a mixer for making cakes." Max felt sad. He did not want to keep something that did not belong to him. So, Max and Lily went around the park to find the owner of the mixer. They found a lady who was looking for it. She was happy to see her mixer and thanked Max and Lily for helping her. Max and Lily felt good for doing the right thing. They played together in the park until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big park. The tree had many leaves and branches. It was very pretty. One day, a little girl found a notebook under the tall tree. The notebook had many colors on it. The girl was happy and wanted to draw in the notebook. She sat under the tree and drew pictures of the tree, the sun, and a big smiley face. A nice squirrel saw the girl drawing and wanted to be friends. The squirrel came close to the girl and she saw it. She smiled and gave the squirrel a big hug. They became the best of friends and played together under the tall tree.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a hairy dog named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They had lots of fun together, but sometimes they would quarrel. One day, Tim's mom gave them a task. They had to clean up their toys in the yard. Fluffy and Tim did not want to do this task. They started to quarrel about who should do it. They could not agree, and they got very mad at each other. As they quarreled, a big wind came and blew their toys away. Fluffy and Tim stopped quarreling and tried to catch their toys, but it was too late. The wind took all their toys far, far away. Fluffy and Tim were sad because they lost their toys. They learned that quarreling was not good and they should work together next time.

Once upon a time, in a big classroom, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was worried because he could not find his toy car. He looked under the chairs and behind the doors, but he could not find it anywhere. He was sad because his toy car was his favorite thing to play with. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sara, playing with a toy car that looked just like his. He thought that maybe Sara had his car. But when he looked closer, he saw that Sara's car had a sticker on it. Tim had an idea. He would attach a sticker to his car, so he could tell it apart from Sara's car. Tim went home and found a sticker. He attached it to his car, and the next day, he showed it to Sara. They were both happy that they had their own toy cars. But then, something unexpected happened. Their teacher, Miss Lily, came to the classroom with a big box. She opened it, and inside were toy cars for everyone! It turned out that Tim's car was at home all along, and Sara's car was from the box. They all laughed and played with their new toy cars, and Tim was not worried anymore.

Once upon a time, a little cat named Fluffy lived in a small house. Fluffy loved to move and play all day long. One day, Fluffy felt hot and anxious. She wanted to find a cool place to rest. Fluffy went outside and saw a big tree. She thought, "I can climb the tree and find a cool spot." So, she moved fast and climbed up the tree. At the top, she found a nice, cool spot to lay down. As Fluffy lay there, she heard a sound. She looked around and saw a big bird. The big bird was not happy that Fluffy was in the tree. They both wanted the cool spot. Then, the big bird had an idea. They could share the cool spot and be friends. Fluffy liked the idea and they both lived happily in the tree, sharing the cool spot.
