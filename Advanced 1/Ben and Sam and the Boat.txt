Title: Ben and Sam and the Boat

Ben and Sam were brothers who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big boat that could float on water. They took their boat to the pond and put it on the water. The boat was very fast and fun. One day, Ben and Sam found a big rock near the pond. They thought it would be fun to put the rock on their boat and see what would happen. They lifted the rock and put it on the boat. The boat was very heavy and started to sink. Ben and Sam were sad. They tried to save their boat, but it was too late. The boat and the rock went under the water and disappeared. Ben and Sam cried and ran to their mom. They told her what happened and said they were sorry. Mom hugged them and said, "It's okay, boys. I know you didn't mean to lose your boat. But you learned a lesson today. You should be careful with your things and not put too much weight on them. Too much weight can make things sink and break. Next time, be more gentle and wise with your toys." Ben and Sam nodded and said they understood. They said they would be more careful and wise. Mom smiled and said, "That's good, boys. I love you. Now, let's go and find another toy to play with. Maybe we can make a new boat with some paper and sticks." Ben and Sam smiled and followed mom. They still missed their boat, but they were happy to have mom and each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big automobile. It was very big and heavy. One day, the big automobile wanted to know how much it weighed. So, it went to a place where it could be weighed. At the place, the big automobile met a small car. The small car said, "Hello, big automobile! I am here to be weighed too." The big automobile said, "Hello, small car! Let's find out how much we weigh together." The big automobile and the small car went on the big machine to be weighed. But the big automobile was too big and heavy for the machine. The machine broke and made a loud noise. The big automobile and the small car were very sad. The people who worked at the place were not happy. They said, "You broke our machine, big automobile! Now we can't weigh any more cars." The big automobile felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to break the machine." The big automobile had to leave the place without knowing how much it weighed. The small car couldn't be weighed either. They both went home feeling sad and sorry for what happened. And that was the end of their not-so-good day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys all day. One day, while playing, he accidentally broke his sister's toy. He felt embarrassed but did not tell her. The next day, Tim saw his sister looking for her toy. He knew he had to tell her the truth, but he was scared. He hid behind the door and watched his sister cry. He felt sad and knew he had to do something. Tim went to his room and found his favorite toy. He thought that if he gave his toy to his sister, it would make her happy again. He gave her the toy and told her he was sorry for breaking her toy. Tim's sister was happy, and they both learned that it is important to say sorry when we make a mistake or destroy something. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big snowman named Sam. Sam had a rope for a belt and a big smile on his face. He lived in a park where kids came to play every day. One sunny day, Sam saw a little girl named Lily. Lily looked at Sam and said, "Wow, you are amazing!" Sam felt happy. They played together all day. Lily liked to swing on Sam's rope belt. But, the sun was very warm. Sam started to melt. Lily saw this and said, "Oh no, Sam! You are melting!" Sam felt sad, but he knew this was what happened to snowmen. Lily had an idea. She ran to get a big umbrella. She put it over Sam to keep him cool. Sam stopped melting and felt happy again. They played together until the sun went down.
