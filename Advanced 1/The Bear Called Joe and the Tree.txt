Title: The Bear Called Joe and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a little bear called Joe. Joe was always very troubled because he was missing something important. He was missing a button! Everywhere Joe looked, all the other bears had buttons - but Joe had none. Joe was very sad. One day, while Joe was out walking, he saw a voice coming from a tree. The voice said "Joe, I can help you. You need a button to be happy". Joe was so excited and he rushed over to the tree and asked "How can you help me?". The voice replied "It's easy Joe. You need to help out other people with their problems, and then you will see your luck will change. You will get your button". Joe was so relieved and thanked the voice for his advice. From that day on, Joe helped out everyone he met with their troubles. Soon enough, Joe was given a button for his kind deeds. Joe was so happy, he had finally got the button he needed. The moral of the story is that when you put others first, you will be rewarded in the end.

One day, a strong boy named Tim found a folder. The folder was blue and pretty. Tim liked the folder a lot. He wanted to show it to his friends, so he took it to school. At school, Tim showed the folder to his friends. They all thought it was nice. But then, a big girl named Sally saw the folder. Sally seemed mean. She wanted the folder for herself. She took it from Tim and ran away. Tim felt sad. He wanted his folder back. He asked his friends to help. They all worked together to make a plan. They were not as strong as Sally, but they were smart. They found a way to get the folder back from Sally. In the end, Tim and his friends were happy, and they learned that working together is important.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty dress that she loved very much. One day, Lily was going to a party with her friends. She wanted to wear her pretty dress, but she could not find it. Lily began to panic. Lily looked everywhere for her dress. She was so tired from looking, but she did not give up. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Lily, did you check the laundry room?" Lily went to the laundry room and found her pretty dress. She was very happy. Lily learned that when she felt panic, she should ask for help. She knew that her mom was always there to help her. And Lily also learned that it is okay to be tired, but never give up. So, Lily went to the party with her friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and a dog named Max. Mia loved to play outside with Max and listen to the birds sing. One day, Mia found a toy arrow in the yard. She showed it to Max and said, "Look, Max! I found an arrow!" Max wagged his tail and barked. Mia and Max played with the arrow, throwing it and chasing after it. Mia trusted Max to bring the arrow back to her every time she threw it. They played until the sun went down and it was time for dinner. Mia and Max were happy to have each other and the fun they had with the arrow.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had long, soft fur. Every day, Fluffy would go out and play with her friends, and they would have lots of fun. But at the end of the day, Fluffy would always come home to her warm bed. One day, Fluffy found a new friend. It was a small, lost dog named Spot. Spot was sad because he had no place to stay. Fluffy wanted to help her new friend. She thought of a good idea and took Spot to her home. When they got home, Fluffy's mom saw Spot and said he could stay with them. Fluffy was so happy! She rubbed her head against Spot to show she cared. From that day on, Fluffy and Spot played together every day and had lots of fun. They were best friends and stayed together for a long, long time.
