Title: Bob and the Present

Bob was a three year old boy. He never had any decisions to make. But today he had to decide what to do. He closed his eyes, hoping for some answers. When he opened them, he saw a bright pin on the ground. He instantly knew it was for him. He picked it up and inspected it. It had a very pretty color. He decided to take it with him on his walk. As he continued walking, he thought of what to do with the pin. He decided to give it as a present. He looked around, trying to find someone special. Suddenly he saw his best friend, Rose. Bob ran towards her, smiling. He put the pin in her hands, and said, "This is for you. I decided to give it to you because it's so bright and pretty." Rose was so excited. She thanked him, and gave him a big hug. From then on, they were great friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a small car named Zoom. Zoom loved to drive fast on the earth. One day, Zoom met a big truck named Biggie. Biggie said, "Zoom, let's race to the big tree and back." Zoom was scared but said, "Okay, let's race!" They lined up to start. Biggie looked so big and Zoom felt very small. But Zoom knew something Biggie did not know. When they started to race, Zoom drove very fast. Biggie was slow. Zoom got to the big tree first and drove back. Zoom won the race. Biggie was surprised. Zoom said, "I may be small, but I am fast!" And they both laughed and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy guitar. It was red and shiny. It lived in a big, pretty house. The guitar had a best friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with the guitar every day. She liked its pretty sound. One sunny day, Sue and the guitar went to the park. They played and sang songs together. The birds and the bees danced to their music. Everybody was happy and having fun. The guitar felt very special. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the guitar away. Sue was sad and started to scream. She looked everywhere for her fancy guitar. She saw it in a tree! Sue was scared, but she climbed the tree and saved her guitar. The guitar was so happy to be back with Sue. They went home and played music together all day long. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with his friend, a little boy named Tim. They liked to play in the yard and have fun. One day, Tim and Spot found a big pit in their yard. Tim said, "Spot, let's supply the pit with toys and make it our secret play area." Spot barked happily, and they began to put toys in the pit. As they played in the pit, they heard a little bird. The bird said, "Thank you, brave friends, for making this pit a fun place for all of us to play!" Tim and Spot smiled, happy to share their new play area with their new friend. And they all played together in the pit every day.

Sara and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They had a lot of fun on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They also liked to look at the flowers, the birds and the clouds. They thought the park was very attractive. One day, they decided to play a new game. They pretended they were explorers and they had to find a treasure. They took a map, a compass and a shovel. They followed the clues on the map and dug in different places. They were very excited. They came to a big hill. The map said the treasure was under the hill. They started to dig with their shovel. They dug and dug and dug. They hoped to find something shiny and sparkly. Suddenly, they felt the ground shake. They heard a loud noise. They saw the hill crack and split. They were scared. They dropped their shovel and ran away. They did not know what was happening. They did not know it was an earthquake. They ran to their parents, who were sitting on a bench. They hugged them and told them what they saw. Their parents were surprised and worried. They explained to them that an earthquake was when the earth moved and broke. They said it was very rare and dangerous. They said they were lucky to be safe. Sara and Tom felt better. They were glad they had their parents. They did not care about the treasure anymore. They learned that the most attractive thing in the park was their friendship.
