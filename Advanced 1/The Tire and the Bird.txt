Title: The Tire and the Bird

Once upon a time there was a tire. It was a big tire and it liked to roll around. One day it came to a very unknown place. It had never been here before and it was feeling a bit scared. It wanted to go back to its home but it didn't know where home was. Suddenly, a friendly bird came down from the sky. It said, "Hi Tire! I can help you. We can find your home if you accept." Tire was excited and said, "Yes please!" So the bird started to fly and led Tire to its home. When Tire arrived home, it was so happy. Everyone was really glad to see it, especially Tire’s family. Everyone hugged it and Tire accepted their love. The end!

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was three years old and loved to play with her toys. One day, her mom told her to be careful when playing near the pond in the backyard. Jane was supposed to stay away from the pond, but she didn't listen. She got too close and fell in the water! Jane's mom was very worried and knew she needed help. She called her neighbor, Mrs. Jones, who quickly came over to help. Mrs. Jones recommended they call an ambulance and they did. Soon the ambulance came and they took Jane to the hospital. The doctors and nurses said Jane got a bad shock from the water and her body was hurt. They gave her medicine, applied bandages and said she had to rest in bed for a few days. Jane was very brave and after a few days and with help from the doctors, she soon felt better and was able to play with her toys again.

Once upon a time, there was a big tower in a small town. The tower was very tall and light. One day, a little boy named Tim wandered near the tower. He looked up and saw a friendly bird sitting on the tower. The bird had bright colors and a happy face. Tim waved at the bird and said, "Hi, bird!" The bird flew down and said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play with me?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" So, Tim and the bird played near the tower. They ran around and laughed a lot. They were very happy. After playing, Tim and the bird were tired. They sat near the tower and looked at the sky. The sun was setting, and the sky was full of pretty colors. Tim said, "I like playing with you, bird." The bird smiled and said, "I like playing with you too, Tim." They became good friends and played near the tower every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a big hall with his mom. The hall was full of toys and games. Tim was very happy because he loved toys. His mom told him to be good and not take any toys that were not his. Tim saw a big, shiny toy car. He wanted it so much, but he knew it was not his. He tried to resist taking the toy car, but it was very hard. He looked around to see if anyone was watching him. When he thought no one was looking, he took the toy car and put it in his bag. Later, Tim's mom found the toy car in his bag. She was very sad and told Tim that he was not good. She took the toy car away and made Tim say sorry to the person who owned the car. Tim felt bad and cried because he knew he did something wrong.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a pencil. The pencil could heal anything it touched. One day, a little boy found the pencil. He did not know it was a magic pencil. The boy had a broken toy. He was very sad. He used the pencil to try and fix his toy. The pencil touched the toy and healed it. The toy was not broken anymore. The boy was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The pencil started to heal everything in the room. The room became even more tidy. The boy was surprised. He did not know the pencil could do that. The boy decided to keep the magic pencil and use it to help others.
