Title: Lily and the Bowl

Lily wanted to bake a cake with Mommy. She put on her apron and climbed on a step to reach the counter. Mommy gave her a big bowl and a spoon. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Mommy!" Mommy showed Lily how to measure the flour, the sugar, the eggs and the milk. Lily tried to copy Mommy, but it was hard. She spilled some flour on the floor. She dropped an egg on the counter. She poured too much milk in the bowl. Lily felt frustrated. She said, "I can't do it, Mommy!" Mommy hugged Lily and said, "It's okay, Lily. Baking is fun, but it can also be messy. You are doing a great job. Let's clean up a little and then we can mix the batter and put it in the pan." Lily nodded and helped Mommy wipe the floor and the counter. She stirred the batter with the spoon and tasted it. It was sweet and yummy. She said, "Mmm, this is good, Mommy!" Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, it is. Now let's put it in the oven and wait for it to bake. Then we can decorate it with frosting and sprinkles. What do you think, Lily?" Lily clapped her hands and said, "I think that's a great idea, Mommy! I love baking with you!"

Once there was a little boy called Luke. He loved playing in the garden with his sister. But one day, they saw something very exciting on the ground. It was a bright-red radish! The siblings were overjoyed and Luke's sister said "Let's dive for it!". So Luke and his sister dove for the radish and both of them grabbed a corner of it. Suddenly, Luke's mom appeared from behind a bush and said "Oh no, you can't split the radish! It's too small". She looked very miserable and so did the kids. But suddenly, a brilliant idea came to Luke. He said to his mom "Let's dive together and share it!" His mom smiled and said, "That's a great idea!". So the three of them dove for the radish, and after a lot of laughter, they grabbed hold of it. They shared the radish and were all very happy. The siblings were so excited with the adventure that they found a new radish almost every day from then on, and they dove for them each time. It was the best game ever!

Jill was jogging down the street when she saw a car. She thought it looked interesting so she got closer to take a look. The car was bad. It had a lot of scratches and dents. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise and saw smoke coming out of the car. Jill started to run away, but it was too late. The car exploded! Jill was very scared and ran all the way home. She was very sad because the bad car had exploded.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little cat named Missy. Missy lived in a small house with her best friend, a little boy named Tim. They loved to play and explore together. One day, Missy and Tim found a big black rock in their yard. "What is this?" Tim asked Missy. "I don't know," Missy replied, "Let's ask mom." So, they went to Tim's mom and showed her the rock. "This is coal," she said. "It's used to make a fire." Missy and Tim were excited about their new find. They decided to keep the coal and play with it. They had a lot of fun rolling it, throwing it, and hiding it. But soon, it was time for dinner. "I'll miss playing with our coal," said Missy. "Me too," said Tim. "But we can play again tomorrow!" And they happily went inside to eat, looking forward to more adventures with their new friend, the coal.

Once upon a time there was a prince who wore a shiny crown on his head. But one day, he suddenly felt so tired. He was so tired that he wished he could just close his eyes and fall asleep. But his crown kept getting in the way. No matter how he moved his head, he couldn't get comfortable with it on. He thought for a moment, and finally had an idea. He quickly opened the clasp at the back of his crown. He carefully took it off his head and placed it on a table. Immediately, the prince sighed in relief; he was much more comfortable now! But before he could drift off to sleep, his crown suddenly began to glow. Out of the crown came a beautiful fairy! She said that the prince must not take off his crown, for it was where his power came from. Without it, he could not be the ruler of his kingdom. The prince was so sad. He asked the fairy if there was any way he could be comfortable with his crown, and she said yes! She taught the prince how to close the clasp of his crown so he could still wear it, but keep it comfortable on his head. The prince thanked the fairy and quickly closed the clasp. He was now able to wear his crown without feeling uncomfortable. And the kingdom was happy, because their prince was able to rule with his crown on. The end.
