Title: Fred and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, green frog named Fred. Fred liked to organize things. He organized his toys, his food, and even his friends. One day, he decided to give a lecture to his friends about organizing. Fred said, "Friends, I want to teach you how to organize. It is fun and easy!" His friends listened carefully. They wanted to learn from Fred, even though some of them thought organizing was disgusting. As Fred was teaching, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird saw all of Fred's friends in a neat row. The bird thought they looked like tasty snacks. The bird swooped down and grabbed Fred's friends one by one. Fred cried, "Oh no, my friends! I should not have made them sit in a row!" Now, Fred had no friends to play with. He was very sad and learned that sometimes, organizing can have a bad ending.

One day, a boy named Tim found a rare coat in his closet. He did not know where it came from. He put on the coat and went outside to play. He saw his friend, Sue. She said, "That is a nice coat, Tim!" Tim shrugged and said, "I do not know where it came from. I found it in my closet." Sue said, "Let's share the coat. We can take turns wearing it." Tim agreed, and they played all day, taking turns with the coat. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "You can have the coat, Sue." The next day, Tim found another rare coat in his closet. He smiled, knowing that sharing the first coat brought him good luck. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can bring happiness and good things.

Once upon a time, there was a big sack. It was full of cereal. The sack was very old. It was ancient. A little boy liked to eat the cereal every day. He was very happy. One day, the little boy was hungry. He went to the sack to get some cereal. But the sack was empty. The little boy was sad. He wanted to eat the cereal. He did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took the sack away. The little boy was very surprised. He tried to get the sack back, but the bird flew away. The little boy was very sad. He never got his cereal back.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She lived in a big castle. The princess was powerful and kind. She had a rocking horse that she loved to play with every day. One day, the princess met a little boy. The boy was sad. He said, "I have no friends to play with." The princess thought for a moment and said, "Come play with me! We can rock on my rocking horse together!" The boy smiled and they played together all day. They rocked on the rocking horse, and the boy was happy. The powerful princess learned that sharing her toys and playing with others made her feel even more powerful. Moral: Sharing and being kind to others makes you feel powerful and happy.

Once, there was a big dog named Tom. He loved to dress up in funny clothes. One day, he put on a hat, a shirt, and pants with a buckle. Tom looked in the mirror and felt happy. He wanted to show his friends. Tom went to the park to play with his friends. They all saw his clothes and got jealous. They wanted to dress up too. So, they all ran home to find clothes to wear. Tom was excited to see his friends dress up. But then, something unexpected happened. When Tom's friends came back, they were not wearing clothes. They had found big leaves and put them on instead. Tom was surprised but he laughed. Now, they all looked funny and had fun playing together in the park.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jane. They were walking in the park when suddenly they heard some crazy noises. They looked around and saw a jogger running at a very fast speed. Jack and Jane were very impressed. Jack said to Jane, "She's running so fast, it's crazy!" Jane replied, "I know! Let's do the same!" So Jack and Jane started running as fast as they could. Soon they were speeding all over the park! It was so funny, they couldn't stop laughing. It seemed like they were running forever. Finally, they had to stop because they were too tired. They both took a deep breath and smiled. Jack said to Jane, "That was a lot of fun! Let's go jogging together again soon, OK?" Jane smiled and said, "Yes, let's do that!" And so they made a plan to go jogging together again soon.
