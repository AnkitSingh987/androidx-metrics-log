Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play baseball. One day, Tim found a brilliant, shiny baseball in his yard. He was very happy and wanted to test how far he could throw it. Tim threw the baseball high into the sky. But something unexpected happened. The baseball did not come back down! Tim looked up and saw the baseball stuck in a big tree. He was sad and did not know how to get it back. Then, a friendly bird flew down from the tree with the brilliant baseball in its beak. The bird gave the baseball back to Tim. He was so surprised and happy! From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends and played baseball together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She loved playing with her rocket. Jane had been playing with it all day. At dinner time, she wanted to display her rocket to the grown ups. So, Jane brought it in to the kitchen and showed it to them. Everyone oohed and ahhed and asked her lots of questions. As they were talking, Jane's daddy had an idea. He said, "Let's see it fly!" Jane was very excited and ran outside with her rocket. She showed everyone how it worked, and it soared up into the night sky. The grown ups clapped, but then Jane was embarrassed. She had forgotten that the rocket was her brother's! She had borrowed it without asking and now everyone knew. But the grown ups didn't mind. They said Jane had been very brave and had done something amazing - and she should be proud of herself!

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Max, go for a walk. They see a big, smelly trash can near the park. Max runs to the trash can and starts to sniff it. Tim says, "No, Max! Don't go near the smelly trash can!" Just then, a little girl named Sue comes by with her cat, Luna. Luna sees Max by the trash can and starts to hiss. Max barks back at Luna. Sue says, "Luna, be nice to Max!" Tim and Sue want their pets to be friends, so they take them away from the smelly trash can. They go to a pretty spot with a big tree and soft grass. They sit down and talk while Max and Luna play in the light of the sun. Soon, Max and Luna start to get along. They chase each other and roll in the grass. Tim and Sue laugh and clap their hands. They are happy that their pets are now friends. From that day on, Tim and Sue go to the park together with Max and Luna. They play in the light, far away from the smelly trash can. And they all become the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, big cat. This cat was enormous. The cat loved to play with a ball. One day, the cat saw a small, small mouse. The mouse was very scared. The cat said, "Mouse, do not be scared. I will not eat you. Let's play with my ball." The mouse was happy. They played and had fun. Then, the cat saw a big, big dog. The dog wanted to eat the mouse. The cat had an idea. The cat knelt down and said, "Dog, I will give you my ball. You love to play, too." The dog was happy and took the ball. The dog did not eat the mouse. The cat and the mouse were safe. In the end, the cat and the mouse learned that sharing has value. They were happy to have each other as friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a boy who loved to sail. Every day he would sail around on his boat, with his best friend. One day, they looked up and saw an island on the horizon. They wanted to explore it, so they took the boat there. Just as they were about to reach the shore, the boy grabbed his sunglasses. His friend asked why he needed them. He said, "Oh, I always wear them when I sail - they help me see better." His friend trusted him, because he was so reliable. They got to the island and the boy was surprised. He saw a giant pirate ship! His friend was scared, but the boy stayed calm. He asked his friend to help him sail the boat closer. When they got near the pirate ship, they heard a loud noise. A pirate popped his head out of the ship and shouted, "Ahoy!" His friend screamed and dropped an oar! The pirate and his crew explained that they were nearby exploring the island and looking for new crew members. The reliable boy raised his hand and volunteered. His best friend was impressed - and jealous, too. The boy got his sunglasses ready and prepared to sail off on the pirate ship. He and his best friend shouted goodbye as the ship disappeared into the sunset.
