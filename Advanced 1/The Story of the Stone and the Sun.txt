Title: The Story of the Stone and the Sun

Once upon a time there was a girl. She was looking for something special. She began to gather her fingers together. She studied them very closely to see if something special was hidden within. She moved her fingers all around, up and down. But they were all just ordinary. The girl kept looking and suddenly, she found something very special, hiding between her fingers. It was a tiny stone. It shone in the sun like a rainbow. The girl smiled and held the stone tight. She knew that this was something just for her, something that made her special.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big jar. The jar was empty. Tim wanted to complete filling the jar with pretty rocks. One day, Tim went to the park. He found many pretty rocks. He put the rocks in his jar. The jar was almost complete. Tim was happy. But he needed one more rock. Tim saw a big rock near a deep hole. He wanted the rock to complete his jar. Tim went close to the hole. He tried to get the rock. But Tim fell in the hole. Tim was sad. The jar was not complete. The end.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and make jokes. One day, they find a big machine in the park. It has buttons and lights and sounds. "What is this?" Lily asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is a joke machine. Let's try it!" Ben says. They push some buttons and the machine makes funny noises. They laugh and clap. Then they push another button and the machine says "Hello, I am a joke machine. Do you want to hear a joke?" "Yes, yes!" Lily and Ben say. "OK, here is a joke. Why did the chicken cross the road?" the machine asks. Lily and Ben think hard. They have heard this joke before. "To get to the other side!" they say together. The machine makes a buzzer sound and says "Wrong! The chicken crossed the road because it was fit and wanted to exercise. Ha ha ha!" Lily and Ben look at each other. They don't think that is a funny joke. They think that is a silly joke. "That is not a joke. That is a lie. Chickens don't exercise. They eat and lay eggs." Lily says. "Yeah, that is a bad joke. You are a bad joke machine. We don't like you." Ben says. They turn away from the machine and go to play with other toys. The machine is sad and lonely. It wishes it had better jokes. It wishes it had friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a cat and a dog. They did not like to share. One day, they found a big bottle. It was wine. They both wanted it. The cat and dog started to fight. They did not know what to do. Then, a wise bird saw them. The bird said, "You should not fight. You must surrender and share the wine. There is enough available for both." The cat and dog listened to the wise bird. They stopped fighting and shared the wine. They were happy and became friends. The moral of the story is that sharing is good and makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time there lived a little boy called John. John was only three and had an adventurous spirit. He loved to explore and always looked for new opportunities. One day, John asked his mum for permission to explore. She said he could, but he mustn't go too far. John excitedly set off on his adventure and soon he came across a big park. In the park he saw a big tree and he decided to climb it. He did this cautiously, as not to hurt himself. He enjoyed the view from the tops of the branches. He was so high up he felt like he could touch the clouds. Suddenly, John heard a loud voice. An old man had spotted him in the tree and he shouted, "What are you doing up there? I offer you a flexible space to get down, so come down now." The old man's words frightened John and he was so scared that he couldn't move. Sadly, John fell from the tree and broke his arm. He was taken to the hospital and his mum was very upset. The end.
