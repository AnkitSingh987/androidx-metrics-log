Title: Ella the Elephant and the Secret

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Ella. Ella liked to display her tricks to her friends. She could stand on her back legs and lift a heavy weight with her trunk. All her friends clapped and cheered when she did her tricks. One day, Ella felt uncomfortable. Her tummy was not happy. She told her friend, "My tummy hurts. I can't do my tricks today." Her friend, a kind monkey named Max, wanted to help. Max said, "I will find something to make your tummy feel better." Max went to the wise old owl. The owl told Max a secret: "Give Ella a big hug. Hugs make everyone feel better." Max went back to Ella and hugged her tight. Suddenly, Ella's tummy felt better! Ella was so happy, she did her tricks again. Ella and Max learned that hugs can be magic. They hugged each other and laughed, knowing they would always be there to help each other.

Once there was a strong frog who was very brave. Everyone was scared of him, even the shadows. One day he decided to part from his friends and go on a journey. As he walked through the forest the shadow seemed to follow him everywhere he went. He looked back, but he could not see it. He stopped to rest and the shadow appeared quietly behind him. He was not scared, instead he was very brave. He said, "Are you coming with me?" The shadow was silent, but strange growls could be heard deep in the forest. The frog continued to walk, but the shadow still followed him. He thought he could make it home before dark, but it was getting darker quicker than he thought. The growls were getting closer and the frog started to get scared. He turned to the shadow and said, "Come on, let's get out of here!" But the shadow wouldn't move. The frog was very scared, but he tried to be brave and he kept walking, but the growls were closer and closer. Suddenly he heard something big, he turned around and saw a big bear. He shouted, "Run!" But it was too late, the bear caught him and he was never seen again.

Once upon a time, in a tiny town, there was a popular cat named Peek. Peek was very good at playing hide and seek. All the kids loved to play with Peek. One day, Peek and his friend, a little girl named Lily, were playing hide and seek. Peek said, "Lily, I will go hide first. You count to ten, then come find me!" Lily started counting, "One, two, three..." Peek ran to the east, where he found a big tree. He climbed up and hid in the branches. Lily finished counting and went to look for Peek. She looked everywhere but could not find him. Finally, she looked up and saw a little bird in the tree. The bird said, "Lily, look up here! Peek is hiding in the branches!" Lily found Peek, and they laughed and played all day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia was very creative. She loved to cut and make things with her hands. One day, she found some colorful cloth and a needle with thread. Mia had an idea. She cut the cloth into small pieces. Then, she used the needle and thread to make a stitch. She stitched the pieces together to make a beautiful toy for her little brother, Ben. Mia was very happy with her work. Mia showed her toy to Ben and said, "Look, I made this for you!" Ben smiled and hugged the toy. "Thank you, Mia!" he said. They played with the toy all day, and Mia felt proud of her creative work.

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a big station. It was full of people. The bird was happy and began to sing. A man at the station heard the bird sing. He said, "Wow, what a nice song!" The bird felt proud and sang even louder. Everyone at the station heard the bird's song and smiled. But then, a hurt cat heard the bird sing too. The cat was very hungry. It saw the bird and wanted to eat it. The cat jumped high into the air and caught the bird. The people at the station yelled, "No, stop!" But it was too late. The cat ate the bird, and the station was quiet again.
