Title: The Lady and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a nice lady. The lady had no hair. She was bald. She had a big, soft bed. The lady liked to nap on her bed. One day, the lady was very tired. She wanted to nap. She went to her bed and lay down. The sun was warm and bright. It made the lady feel good. While the lady napped, a small cat came in. The cat was soft and furry. It jumped on the bed. The cat napped with the lady. They both had a good nap. The lady and the cat were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Tim's friends told him about a big race. The winner would get a shiny trophy. Tim wanted to win the trophy, so he started to practice running every day. As Tim practiced, his speed began to increase. He ran faster and faster. His friends noticed and said, "Wow, Tim! You are getting so fast! The race will be easy for you!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you! I want to win the trophy!" On the day of the race, Tim and his friends lined up to start. When the race began, Tim ran as fast as he could. He was in the lead and could see the trophy waiting at the finish line. But then, he saw his friend, Sarah, fall down. Tim stopped running and helped Sarah up. They finished the race together, holding hands. Tim didn't win the trophy, but he was happy because he had helped his friend. His friends cheered and said, "You are a true winner, Tim!" The moral of the story is that being kind and helping others is more important than winning a trophy.

Once upon a time, there was a swan. The swan was very attractive. It lived in a big pond with many friends. The swan liked to try new things. One day, the swan saw a frog. The frog said, "Hello, swan! I can jump high. Can you try to jump too?" The swan tried to jump, but it could not. The frog laughed. The swan felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The frog turned into a prince! The prince said, "Swan, you are so attractive. Thank you for trying to jump. You are a good friend." The swan was happy and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy bear. Every day, he would sit on a hill and paint with pastels. One day, a little bunny hopped up to him. "Hello!" she said in a cheery voice. The bear looked at her grumpily. "What do you want?" "I was wondering," the bunny said, "Would you like help with your painting?" The bear sighed. "Fine," he said with a roll of his eyes. The bunny hopped up to him. She grabbed a pastel, and started to paint. Soon, the bear and the bunny were painting a beautiful picture together. They were so busy that they forgot about being grumpy. When the painting was finished, the bear said, "That was very nice. Thank you." The bunny smiled. "You're welcome!" They both admired their beautiful picture, and hopped away.

Once upon a time there was a lovely little girl who wanted to go to the park. She put on her bow, then ran to the door. But when she opened it, she found a surprise. There was a big bow blocking the exit! She hopped and jumped, trying to see above the bow to find out what was beyond. But it was too tall. No matter what she did, she couldn't get past the big bow. Just then, a voice came from behind the bow. It said "Step inside the bow and you will find a lovely surprise". The little girl was so excited! She stepped through the bow. On the other side was a beautiful park filled with trees and flowers, and a bubbling stream. There was a playground with swings and slides, and the air smelled of flowers. The little girl clapped her hands and laughed. She knew it was a lovely surprise! The little girl played in the park all day, then when it was time to go home, she said goodbye to the lovely park and stepped back through the bow.
