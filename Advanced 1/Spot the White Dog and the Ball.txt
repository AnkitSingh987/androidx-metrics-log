Title: Spot the White Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a white dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his blue ball. He would run and jump to catch it. Spot was a happy dog. One day, Spot saw a big number on the ground. The number was two. Spot did not know what it meant, but he wanted to find out. He decided to follow the number and see where it went. Spot followed the number two until he came to a big, blue lake. He saw his blue ball floating in the water. Spot jumped in to get his ball, but the water was too deep. Spot could not swim and he was scared. In the end, Spot could not get his ball and he was very sad.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. But soon they felt tired and hungry. "Mom, can we have a snack?" Ben asked. "Sure, sweeties. I have some potato chips and apple juice for you. Come to the kitchen," Mom said. Ben and Lily ran to the kitchen and sat on their chairs. They ate the potato chips and drank the apple juice. They liked the crunchy and salty chips and the sweet and cold juice. "Mom, can we have more chips?" Lily asked. "No, honey. That's enough for now. You need to eat some healthy food later. And you also need to take a nap. You look sleepy," Mom said. "But we don't want to nap. We want to play more," Ben said. "I know, but napping is good for you. It helps you grow and learn and feel happy. And you can have more fun after you wake up. How about this? I'll read you a story before you nap. And you can choose the story," Mom said. Ben and Lily thought about it. They liked stories. They had many books with pictures and words. They looked at their books and picked one. It was a story about a useful potato who helped his friends. "Okay, Mom. We'll nap if you read us the story," they said. Mom smiled and hugged them. She took them to their bedroom and tucked them in their beds. She read them the story about the useful potato. Ben and Lily listened and laughed and asked questions. They learned how the potato helped a carrot, a tomato, a onion, and a cheese. They thought the potato was very smart and kind. When the story was over, Mom kissed them and said, "Good night, my little potatoes. I love you. Have a nice nap." Ben and Lily closed their eyes and said, "We love you too, Mom. Thank you for the story. Good night." They fell asleep and dreamed of useful potatoes and happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to whistle. He would whistle all day long. One day, Tim found a bottle in the park. The bottle was very big and pretty. Tim picked up the bottle and said, "Wow! This is a nice bottle!" He wanted to show his mom. So, he took the bottle home. When he got home, he showed his mom the bottle. His mom looked serious. "Tim," she said, "this is a special bottle. It can do magic!" Tim was so happy. He whistled a happy song and danced with the magic bottle. From that day on, Tim and the magic bottle had lots of fun together.

Once there was a bear called Bob. He loved to shake furniture. He would shake chairs, tables and couches. He loved making them wobble. One day, Bob had a great idea. He decided to ask his friend, Sammy, to help him make the furniture more reliable. Sammy was a very clever rabbit. Bob asked Sammy: "Can you help me make the furniture more reliable?". Sammy thought for a while and then said: "Yes, I can try!" So, Bob and Sammy worked together. They put all the furniture pieces in different positions and then they tightened up all the screws. Finally, Bob and Sammy stepped back and admired their work. It looked much more reliable. They were both very happy with their achievement. From then on, Bob’s furniture was always reliable, no matter how much he shook it.
