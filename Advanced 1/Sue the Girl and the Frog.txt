Title: Sue the Girl and the Frog

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the beach. She saw lots of sand and water. Sue was very happy. She wanted to play and make a big sand castle. She started to dig and dig in the sand. Sue found a small box under the sand. She was very surprised. Sue was a little troubled, but she wanted to know what was inside. She tried to open the box. It was hard, but she did it. When Sue opened the box, she found a small toy frog. The frog jumped out of the box and started to dance! Sue laughed and clapped her hands. She played with the dancing frog all day at the beach. The frog became her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful park. In the park, there was a big swing. Many kids loved to play on the swing. The owner of the park liked to watch the kids play and laugh. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the park. Tim saw the swing and wanted to play on it. He asked the owner, "Can I play on the swing, please?" The owner smiled and said, "Yes, you can." Tim got on the swing and started to swing back and forth. He swung higher and higher. He felt so happy. The owner watched Tim play and felt happy too. The park was filled with joy and laughter.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little animal. This animal had a big, pretty home made of paper. The animal loved his home very much. But one day, the sky was dark and the wind was strong. The animal was scared. The wind blew and blew, and the animal's home started to tear. The animal felt very sad. He knew he needed help to fix his home. So, he went to find his friend, the wise old owl, who lived in a big tree. The wise old owl listened to the animal's problem. The owl knew that the wind was bitter and strong. He told the animal, "We must make your home stronger so it won't tear again." They worked together to fix the animal's home. They used strong sticks and leaves to make it better. The animal was very happy and thanked the wise old owl. Now, when the wind blew, the animal's home did not tear. The animal felt safe and warm in his new, strong home. And the wise old owl was always there to help his friend, the little animal.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bird named Lily. She liked to sing songs all day long. One day, she found a microphone in the park. Lily took the microphone and said, "Hello, friends! I have a new toy to sing with!" Her friends gathered around to listen to her sing. They all liked her voice and clapped their hands. Then, something unexpected happened. A small bug came out of the microphone and started to dance. Lily was surprised, but she kept singing. The bug said, "Thank you, Lily, for helping me. I was stuck inside the microphone. I value your singing." And they all sang and danced together, happy in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a blue cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to perform tricks for her friends. She would jump and dance, and everyone would clap and laugh. One day, Kitty was playing with her best friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily said, "Kitty, can you perform a new trick for me?" Kitty thought for a moment and then nodded her head. She went to find something to help her with the trick. Kitty found a bottle of lotion in the bathroom. She thought it would be fun to slide on the lotion. So, she poured some on the floor and started to slide. But, oh no! The lotion made her slip and slide all over the place! She couldn't stop! Lily heard Kitty's meows and came running. She saw Kitty sliding and laughed. "Kitty, you're so silly!" she said. Then, Lily helped Kitty clean up the lotion. They both laughed and decided that maybe lotion wasn't the best thing for tricks. From that day on, Kitty and Lily tried new tricks together, but they never used lotion again. And they lived happily ever after, laughing and playing in their small house.
