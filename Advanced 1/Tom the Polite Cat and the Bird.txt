Title: Tom the Polite Cat and the Bird

One day, a polite cat named Tom found a belt. Tom wanted to attach the belt to his toy. He thought it would be fun to pull his toy with the belt. So, he tried to attach the belt to the toy, but it was not easy. As Tom was trying to attach the belt, a big dog named Sam came by. Sam saw Tom and wanted to play too. Sam tried to help Tom attach the belt to the toy. They both tried very hard, but they could not do it. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and picked up the belt. The bird was able to attach the belt to the toy! Tom and Sam were very happy. They thanked the bird and played with the toy all day. The bird had helped them, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucy. She loved to polish things. Every day she would take an old rag and polish all of her toys to make them look extra shiny! One day, her mom came to her and said, "Lucy, why don't you come and help me in the garden?" Lucy happily said, "Yes, mom!" Together, they went to the garden and Lucy took out her old rag. She started to polish the leaves of the plants. She worked very hard and soon the leaves were shimmering! Mom said, "Wow, Lucy! You are so patient! I'm glad you helped me today." Lucy felt very proud and smiled from ear to ear. She was so glad she had been able to help her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a diary where she wrote all her secrets. One day, her diary became broken. Amy was very sad. She did not know how to fix her broken diary. Amy went to her friend Tom. Tom was very good at fixing things. "Tom, can you help me?" Amy asked. "My diary is broken, and I don't know what to do!" Tom looked at the broken diary and said, "Don't worry, Amy. I will try to save your diary." Tom worked very hard to fix the broken diary. He used glue and tape to put it back together. After a while, Tom gave the diary back to Amy. "Here you go, Amy. Your diary is not broken anymore. I saved it for you!" Amy was so happy to have her diary back. She thanked Tom and gave him a big hug. From that day on, Amy and Tom became the best of friends. They played together every day, and Amy always remembered how Tom had saved her broken diary.

Once upon a time there was a clever girl named Alice. She was walking through a valley one day and saw a bird sitting on a tree. She called out to the bird and asked it “What are you doing?”. The bird replied “I am waiting for the sun to rise so I can sing a song”. Alice was pleased to hear this and asked the bird “Could I hear you sing?” The bird nodded and replied “Yes, I will sing as soon as the sun is up”. Alice waited patiently until the sun rose. The bird opened its beak and sang a sweet, beautiful melody. Alice was so impressed she clapped her hands and said “Wow, you are so clever!” The bird thanked Alice for the compliment and flew away into the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his toys in the living room. He was having so much fun, but he started to feel like he needed to go to the toilet. Tim felt frustrated because he didn't want to stop playing. Tim tried to keep playing, but he couldn't stop thinking about the toilet. He decided to go look for it. He walked down the hall and saw a door. He opened the door and saw the toilet inside. Tim was happy to see it. Tim used the toilet and felt much better. He was not frustrated anymore. He went back to the living room and continued to play with his toys. Tim learned that it's important to go to the toilet when you need to, even if you're having fun.
