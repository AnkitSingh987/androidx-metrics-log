Title: The Cat and Dog and the Bird

One day, a cat and a dog went on a walk. They saw a big mill. The mill was very old. The cat said, "Let's go inside." The dog said, "Okay, let's include our friend, the bird." They all went into the mill together. Inside the mill, they saw a silly man. The man was dancing and laughing. The cat, dog, and bird laughed too. They thought the man was funny. They wanted to dance and laugh with him. But then, something unexpected happened. The silly man was not a man at all! He was a big puppet! The cat, dog, and bird were very surprised. They looked around and saw a little girl. The little girl was holding the puppet strings. She laughed and said, "I made you all laugh!" The cat, dog, and bird laughed again. They had fun with the little girl and her puppet.

Once upon a time, in a lovely little town, there was a big event. All the people in the town were very excited. They wanted to complete a big puzzle together. The puzzle had many colors and shapes. It was fun to play with. A little boy named Tim wanted to help too. He was a good boy, but sometimes he did not like to share. Tim took a piece of the puzzle and hid it in his pocket. He thought it would be fun to keep it for himself. The people in the town worked hard to complete the puzzle. They put all the pieces together, but they could not find the last one. Everyone was sad because they could not finish the puzzle. They looked everywhere, but they could not find it. Tim saw that everyone was sad. He felt bad for taking the piece. He took it out of his pocket and gave it back. The people in the town were happy to complete the puzzle, but they were also upset with Tim for taking the piece. The moral of the story is to always share and be kind to others. When we do not share, it can make others sad and create a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to eat broccoli. Her mom told her that eating broccoli made her flexible. Lucy wanted to be flexible so she could play with her friends. One day, Lucy found a big problem. All the broccoli in her town was gone! Lucy was sad. She wanted to solve the problem. She asked her friends to help her find more broccoli. Lucy and her friends looked everywhere. They found a big box behind a tree. They thought the box had broccoli inside. But when they opened it, they found lots of toys instead! They were so surprised. Lucy and her friends played with the toys all day long. They forgot about the broccoli. In the end, they learned that being flexible means having fun with friends, even when things don't go as planned.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play in the kitchen. One day, they saw a big piece of cheese on the table. It looked yummy and smelled good. "Let's eat some cheese!" Ben said to Lily. He climbed on a chair and reached for the cheese. "No, Ben, we can't!" Lily said. "That cheese is for Mommy and Daddy. They will be mad if we take it." But Ben did not listen to Lily. He felt a strong urge to taste the cheese. He broke off a small piece and put it in his mouth. "Yuck!" Ben spat out the cheese. It was bad! It was old and moldy and bitter. He felt sick and his tongue hurt. Lily saw Ben's face and felt sorry for him. She gave him some water and a hug. "I'm sorry, Lily," Ben said. "You were right. I should have listened to you. I was greedy and naughty." "It's okay, Ben," Lily said. "We all make mistakes. But we have to learn from them. Next time, we should ask Mommy and Daddy before we eat something." Ben nodded and smiled. He learned his lesson. He and Lily went to play with their toys and waited for Mommy and Daddy to come home. They hoped they would not notice the missing cheese.
