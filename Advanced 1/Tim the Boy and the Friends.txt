Title: Tim the Boy and the Friends

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a troubled kid. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim went to a big party. At the party, there was a fun game. The winner of the game would receive a prize. Tim played the game with all his might. He wanted the prize very badly. He pushed his friends away so he could win. At last, Tim won the game. He was so happy to receive the prize. But then, Tim looked around. He saw that his friends were sad. They did not want to play with him anymore. Tim felt very lonely. He had the prize, but he lost his friends. This was a bad ending for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive cloud. It loved to work all day making rain for the flowers and trees. The cloud was very happy when it could help the plants grow. One sunny day, the cloud met a little bird. The bird said, "Hello, cloud! Can you help me? My nest is very dry and my baby birds are thirsty." The cloud wanted to help the little bird and its babies. So, the cloud worked very hard to make rain. Soon, it started to rain and the bird's nest got wet. The baby birds drank the rainwater and were not thirsty anymore. The little bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, impressive cloud! You did a great job!" The cloud felt proud and continued to work every day, helping all the animals and plants in the forest.

Lila loved to dive in the pool. She liked to feel the water on her face and see the bubbles. She wanted to dive like a fairy, with wings and magic. She asked her mom to buy her a fairy costume for her birthday. But when her birthday came, there was no fairy costume. There was only a big box with a bike in it. Lila was sad and angry. She did not want a bike. She wanted to be a fairy. She threw the bike on the floor and ran to her room. Mom followed her and knocked on the door. She said, "Lila, I know you wanted a fairy costume, but they were all sold out. I got you a bike instead, because I thought you would like it. It is a great bike, with a bell and a basket. You can ride it to the park and have fun." Lila did not answer. She hid under her bed and cried. She did not want to ride a bike. She wanted to dive like a fairy. Mom opened the door and came in. She saw Lila under the bed and smiled. She said, "Lila, I have a surprise for you. Look what I found in the closet." She pulled out a fairy costume, with wings and a wand. She said, "I bought this for you a long time ago, but I forgot to wrap it. I wanted to give it to you as a special gift. Do you like it?" Lila came out from under the bed and saw the fairy costume. She was happy and surprised. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, mom. I love it. It is a great fairy costume. Can I try it on?" Mom said, "Of course, you can. And you can also keep the bike. You can use both of them to have fun. You can ride the bike to the pool and then dive like a fairy. How does that sound?" Lila said, "That sounds awesome, mom. You are the best. Can we go now?" Mom said, "Yes, we can. Let's go, my little fairy." She helped Lila put on the fairy costume and then they went to the bike. Lila rang the bell and smiled. She was ready to dive like a fairy.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful princess who lived in a big castle. Every day, she looked after the kingdom and made sure everyone was happy. One day, the princess had a problem. Her castle didn't have enough power, so the lights were starting to dim. She needed to find a way to get more power. The princess set out on a journey to find something that could help her. After a long time walking, she found a cord. The cord was long and sparkly, and it looked like it could supply power. The princess brought the cord back to the castle, and plugged it in. Immediately, the lights came back on! Everyone was overjoyed and thanked the princess for being so helpful. The princess was glad she could save the day and make everyone happy. She knew that the cord would supply enough power for a long time, so everyone could be safe and happy.
