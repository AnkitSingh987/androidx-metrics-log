Title: Daisy and the Sun

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Daisy. She was three years old and she was very curious. Today, she heard a noise that disturbed her. She looked up and saw a strange man playing jazz music. She thought it sounded funny and danced a little. Daisy jumped up and down, clapping her hands and smiling. The man smiled back but kept playing his jazz. She twirled around and spun in circles. She was having so much fun! Suddenly it started to rain and Daisy got very wet. The man stopped playing and ran away. Daisy was very sad. She wanted to keep dancing. But then the sun came out and Daisy felt a bit better. She saw a dry patch of grass and sat down for a rest. While she rested she imagined the man playing his jazz. Daisy smiled and closed her eyes.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely girl named Sarah. She loved to play outside and feel the wind shake her hair. One day, Sarah decided to explore a bit further than usual. She stumbled upon an old, dusty safe. She was intrigued and decided to find a way to open it. Sarah shook the safe, but nothing happened. She then thought of a clever plan. She wrapped her arms around the safe and gave it a big hug. As she did this, something clicked and the door opened! Inside the safe were all kinds of lovely surprises. Sarah was so happy that she danced and sang! Even though the safe had been scary and dusty on the outside, it held the most beautiful things Sarah had ever seen. From then on, she considered the safe as a special place; something to be hugged and loved.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He lived next to a nice neighbor named Sue. Sue loved to bake tasty cookies. One day, Tim saw Sue leave her house with a big plate of cookies. Tim thought Sue was going to give the cookies to him. But, Sue walked past Tim's house. Tim was sad. He thought Sue did not want to share her cookies with him. He went inside his house and sat by the window. He watched Sue walk down the street with the cookies. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue turned around and came back to Tim's house. She knocked on the door and Tim opened it. Sue said, "I wanted to surprise you, Tim! Here are some tasty cookies for you and your family!" Tim was so happy and thanked Sue for the cookies. From that day on, Tim learned that sometimes good things come when you least expect them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big orange ball that he loved to play with. One day, Tim went to the park with his orange ball. He saw other kids playing and wanted to join them. Tim walked up to the kids and said, "Can I play with you?" The kids looked at his orange ball and said, "Yes, but you must mark your ball, so we know it's yours." Tim took a marker and wrote "Tim's ball" on it. Now everyone knew the orange ball was his. They played together all day long. At the end of the day, Tim's ball rolled away and got lost. Tim was sad, but he remembered that he marked his ball. He looked around and found his orange ball with "Tim's ball" written on it. Tim was so happy he found his ball and could play again tomorrow.

Once there was a little boy called Tim. He was playing in the garden. He was shooting with his toy gun. Suddenly, he heard a noise coming from the house next door. He stopped to listen and heard a voice. He recognised the voice, it was his neighbour Mrs O'Connor. She was talking loudly. "I need to finish my novel," she said. Tim had heard Mrs O'Connor say before that she was writing a novel. Tim decided to go to her house. He opened the door and he saw Mrs O'Connor was busy typing at her computer. He said hello but she was too focused to answer. Tim asked her if he could help. Mrs O'Connor smiled and said she was almost finished with her novel. She said the only thing left to do was to shoot some pictures to go with the words. Tim said he could do that. He was so excited. So, he got his toy gun and took some pictures out in the garden. Mrs O'Connor was so pleased with the pictures. She said Tim had saved the day! The next day, Tim went back to Mrs O'Connor's house. She had a copy of her finished novel. She gave it to Tim and thanked him. Tim felt proud. He showed his finished pictures to his mum and dad. They were so proud of him.
