Title: Pip the Penguin and the Scared

Once upon a time, there was a penguin named Pip. Pip lived in a very cold place with lots of ice and snow. Pip had many friends, but he was a frightened penguin. He was scared of the big ocean. One day, Pip's friend, Sam the seal, said, "Pip, let's go play in the water." Pip was scared, but he didn't want Sam to know. So, he said, "Okay, let's go." They went to the ocean and started to play. Pip was starting to have fun. But then, a big wave came and soaked them both. Pip got very scared and tried to run away. As Pip ran, he slipped on the ice and fell into the water. He tried to swim, but he was too scared. Sam tried to help, but he couldn't reach Pip in time. In the end, Pip was lost in the big ocean, and his friends were very sad. They all wished Pip had not been so frightened and had learned to swim.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who lived near the sea. Every day, he would look out of his window and admire the beautiful blue water. The sea was so big and fair, it made him very happy. One day, the boy decided he wanted to help the sea. He gathered up some big bags and ran to the shore. He filled the bags with sand, shells and pebbles and placed them near the water. The next day he came back and saw that the sea had been supplied with sand, shells and pebbles. It looked so nice! The boy smiled and felt very proud of himself for helping the sea. From then on, every day the little boy would help supply the sea with a few more shells and pebbles. The sea became even more fair and beautiful. The boy was so happy that he was helping make the sea a little nicer.

Once upon a time, there was a little daisy. She was not like the other flowers. She was ugly. The ugly daisy felt sad because no one liked her. One day, the ugly daisy thought, "I will hide. Then no one will see me and feel bad." She found a big rock and hid behind it. The ugly daisy felt a little better. But then, something happened. A big wind came and blew the rock away. The ugly daisy was not hidden anymore. Everyone saw her. They still did not like her. The ugly daisy felt worse than before.

One summer day, two friends decided to have a competitive game of tag. They ran around the park, with one trying to catch the other. The friends laughed and had a lot of fun. Suddenly, one of the friends noticed a big, metallic grill just off the path. His eyes lit up and he yelled to his friend, "Let's have a race to the grill!" Full of excitement, the two began running towards the grill. They both got there at the same time, but the one friend made it first. He claimed victory with a triumphant yell. Unwilling to surrender, the other friend argued that he had made it there first. Both were very competitive and wouldn't give in. They argued until they were both out of breath. Finally, the friend who had been running to the grill said, "Okay, let's surrender and call it a draw, alright?" The other friend agreed and they both returned to their game of tag laughing and exhausted. They made a pact to always surrender if they ever encountered a competitive tie.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little fairy. She had a friend, a scary big bear. The bear was not mean, but he looked scary. The fairy was nice and liked to help her friends. One day, the fairy saw a little bird who could not fly. She wanted to encourage the bird to try. The scary bear came to help too. They both said nice things to the bird. The bird felt brave and tried to fly. But the bird was too small and weak. It tried very hard to fly, but it could not. The bird fell and got hurt. The fairy and the scary bear felt sad. They could not help the bird. The bird did not get better, and they all felt bad.
