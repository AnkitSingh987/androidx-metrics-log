Title: Max the Dog Finds the Ball

One day, a little dog named Max was feeling restless. He wanted to play with his favorite ball, but he could not find it. He looked all around his house, but the ball was not there. Max started to panic. "Where is my ball?" he asked his mom. His mom said, "I think I put it in the drawer. Let's look there." They went to the drawer and opened it. Inside was a big, shiny bone! Max was so happy. He forgot all about his ball and started to chew the bone. Later that day, Max's friend, a cat named Luna, came over to play. She saw the bone and said, "Max, that's not a bone, that's a magic wand!" Max and Luna waved the wand, and suddenly, all of their toys came to life. They had the best playtime ever!

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Bonnie. She loved to write, and every day she would draw pictures and make up stories with her crayons and paints. One day, Bonnie decided she wanted to write about a landscape. She imagined a place with big trees, green grass, and flowers. It looked so yummy! She painted the landscape and wrote a story about it with her crayons. When she finished, she showed it to her mom who said, "That looks so beautiful and yummy, Bonnie!" Bonnie was so happy and proud of her work. She hung it on the wall so she can always remember her beautiful landscape. And from then on, Bonnie kept writing stories and painting pictures.

Anna likes to paint. She has many colors to paint with. She likes purple the most. Purple is a pretty color. She paints purple flowers and purple stars. One day, Anna finds a new color in her paint box. It is not purple. It is not red or blue or yellow or green. It is a color she does not know. It is unknown. She wonders what it is. She dips her brush in the unknown color. She paints a big circle on her paper. The circle is not purple. It is not red or blue or yellow or green. It is a color she has never seen before. It is shiny and bright. It makes her eyes happy. Anna likes the unknown color. She paints more circles and shapes with it. She makes a picture with the unknown color and purple. She thinks it is beautiful. She shows it to her mom. Mom smiles and hugs Anna. She says, "You made a wonderful picture, Anna. You are very creative. Do you know what color this is?" She points to the unknown color. Anna shakes her head. She says, "No, I do not know. It is unknown. But I like it. It is pretty." Mom says, "It is pretty. It is silver. Silver is a special color. It is like the moon and the stars. You painted a silver and purple picture. I love it." Anna smiles and says, "I love it too. Silver and purple are my favorite colors." She hugs her mom and her picture. She is happy. She likes to paint.

One day, a polite cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big rock and wanted to play with it. Tom tried to push the rock, but it did not move. He thought very hard and decided to try a spell. Tom said the magic words, "Split, rock, split!" To his surprise, the rock split into two. Inside the rock, there was a small, shiny key. Tom was happy and picked up the key with his paw. Tom walked to his friend, a dog named Max. Max saw the key and said, "That key is magic too!" Max told Tom to say, "Unlock, door, unlock!" Tom did as Max said, and a secret door opened in a tree. They both went inside and found a room full of toys. They played all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a small fluffy bear. His name was Kevin and he was furry, cuddly and loved to play. Every day, Kevin would play with his friends in the forest. One day, Kevin decided to go for an extra-long walk. As he was walking, he started to get tired and stopped in the middle of the forest. Suddenly, he heard a noise. He looked around and saw a small bunny hopping towards him. "Hi! My name is Bunny," the bunny said. "It's very nice to meet you, Kevin. What are you doing here?" "I'm…missing my friends back home," Kevin said sadly. Bunny looked at him with sympathy. "I know how you feel. I miss my family, too. I have a mom and two brothers, and I miss them a lot," the bunny said. "But, I know how to cheer myself up. Maybe if you follow me, I can show you how it's done." So, Kevin followed Bunny as they hopped through the forest. The bunny showed him how to catch bugs, take naps under the trees, and hear the birds singing. As they walked, Kevin noticed a beautiful butterfly resting on a leaf. "See that butterfly? It's so furry! You can almost see its little lungs breathing in and out," the bunny said. Kevin smiled.
