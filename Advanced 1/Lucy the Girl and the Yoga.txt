Title: Lucy the Girl and the Yoga

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to do yoga. Every day, she would spread out her mat and stretch her arms and legs. One day, her friend Tom came over to play. He saw Lucy doing yoga and wanted to try too. Lucy said, "Tom, I must warn you, be careful! Yoga can be hard." Tom said, "I am lucky, I can do it!" He tried to do a yoga pose but fell down. Lucy helped him up and they both laughed. From then on, Lucy and Tom did yoga together every day. They were very lucky to have each other as friends. They always warned each other to be careful and had lots of fun.

One day, a curious little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods. She saw a big tree with a shield hanging on it. Mia thought, "I want to know more about this shield." Mia saw a wise old owl sitting on a branch. She asked the owl, "Can you tell me about this shield?" The owl said, "This shield is very special. It can help you when you are in trouble. But be careful, you must only use it when you really need it." One day, Mia was playing near the river when she saw a big, mean dog. The dog was barking and growling at her. Mia was scared, but she remembered the shield. She ran back to the tree and grabbed the shield. When she held the shield in front of her, the dog stopped barking and went away. Mia felt happy and safe with the shield. She knew that the wise old owl was right. The shield was special and it would help her when she needed it the most. From that day on, Mia always kept the shield close to her, knowing that it would protect her from any trouble that might come her way.

Sam and Tom were friends who liked to play outside. They liked to see the big aeroplanes in the sky and wave at them. They hoped the people inside would wave back. One day, they saw a very big and loud aeroplane. It was shiny and had a long nose. Sam and Tom ran to the fence and waved with both hands. They wanted the aeroplane to see them and say hello. But the aeroplane did not see them or say hello. It flew very low and fast over their heads. It made a scary noise and a lot of smoke. It dropped something that looked like a big ball. The ball fell on the ground and made a big boom. The boom was so loud that it hurt their ears and made them fall down. Sam and Tom were very scared and sad. They did not like the aeroplane anymore. They did not want to wave at it or any other aeroplane. They wanted to go home and hug their mums and dads. But they could not go home. The big boom had made a big hole in the ground and a big fire. The fire was very hot and bright and it burned everything around it. It burned the fence and the grass and the trees and the houses. It burned Sam and Tom and their mums and dads. They could not hug or talk or play anymore. They were gone.

Once upon a time, there was a big jet. The jet could fly high in the sky. It was a very important jet. It had a job to do. The jet needed to add more things to help people. One day, the jet got a call. It had to add food to its belly. The jet flew to a place where people needed the food. The jet was happy to help. The people were happy too. The jet knew it was important to add more things to help more people. The jet flew to many places. It added toys for kids, clothes for cold people, and love for everyone. The jet was the best helper in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty potato. This potato lived in a big garden. The mighty potato had lots of friends like carrot, tomato, and pea. One day, the mighty potato heard a whisper. It was a little bug. The little bug said, "Hello, mighty potato! Can I be your friend?" The mighty potato smiled and said, "Yes, little bug. We can be friends." From that day on, the mighty potato and the little bug played together in the big garden. They whispered to each other and laughed a lot. They were very happy to be friends.
