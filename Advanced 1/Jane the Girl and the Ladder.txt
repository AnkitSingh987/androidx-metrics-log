Title: Jane the Girl and the Ladder

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. She was very brave and liked going to new places. One day, Jane was in the park with her mom. She saw a ladder leaning against the tall slide. Jane wanted to climb up the ladder and have a view from the top of the slide! Her mom left Jane for a few minutes, but when she came back she saw Jane at the bottom of the ladder. Jane wanted to go up, but her mom said, "No, Jane. I don't think it's safe. We can't allow you to climb the ladder." Jane was a bit nervous, but still determined to go up the ladder. She said, "Mommy please. I just want to give it a try!" After Jane pleaded for a few minutes, her mom allowed her to climb the ladder, but made her promise to be extra careful. Jane was so excited! She climbed up the ladder, and safely got to the top of the slide. She was so proud of herself. At the top, she took in the scenery and waved to her mom. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she found a cold rock on the ground. Mia picked up the rock and decided to take it home. At home, Mia wanted to prepare a surprise for her mom. She took the rock and painted it with bright colors. She made it look like a beautiful flower. Mia was very happy with her work. Mia showed the rock to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I made this for you!" Her mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Mia! It is so pretty!" Mia felt happy that her mom liked the surprise. They put the rock in the garden, and it made their home even more beautiful.

One day, Tomy and Timmy decided to celebrate. They went with their mommy to the factory. While they waited, they saw a fat cow. "Moo!" said the cow. "Moo!" Timmy replied. "It's so fat!" Tomy exclaimed. Their mommy smiled. "Yes, it looks happy," she said. Finally, it was time to celebrate. Tomy and Timmy jumped for joy. They ran into the factory, filled with balloons and treats. "Let's party!" shouted Tomy and Timmy. They celebrated until it was time to go home. The fat cow also watched them leave.

John was a young boy. He was looking for something to do on a dry, summer day. His mom recommended playing baseball. John thought it sounded like fun. John gathered his friends and told them his mom's recommendation. "Let's play baseball!" he said. They all agreed, so they got their gloves and bats from the porch and headed to the park. After a few hours of play, everyone agreed it was a fun way to spend time on a dry summer day. One of John's friends asked John why his mom had recommended baseball. John smiled, "My mom said baseball is a great way for youth to be active and exercise." And they continued to play.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a big, slow turtle named Tim. Tim loved to watch the children play in the village. One day, a fast rabbit named Rob came to the village. Rob was very mean to the children and took their toys. Tim did not like this. He wanted the children to be happy. So, Tim went to talk to Rob. Tim walked slow, but he did not give up. When Tim found Rob, he said, "Please give the toys back to the children." Rob laughed at Tim and said, "You are too slow to make me give the toys back!" But Tim was brave. He challenged Rob to a race. If Tim won, Rob had to give the toys back. The race began, and Rob ran very fast. But Rob got tired and stopped to rest. Tim did not stop. He walked slow, but he did not give up. In the end, Tim won the race! Rob had to give the toys back to the children. The children were happy again, and they loved to watch slow Tim walk around the village.
