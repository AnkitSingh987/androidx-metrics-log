Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy automobile. His room was very tidy, and he always put his toys away when he was done playing. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with his own toy automobile. Sam dared Tim to race their toy cars. Tim thought it was a fun idea, so they both got ready to race. They raced their toy automobiles and had a lot of fun. Tim's car went faster, and he won the race. Sam was happy for Tim and they both laughed. After playing, they put their toy automobiles away in their tidy rooms. Tim and Sam were very good friends who loved to play and dare each other.

Little Jack was feeling nosy one day. He looked around his house to find something to do and noticed a bright red leaf lying on the floor. Jack was excited and he picked it up, eager to show it to his mum. He tried to take the leaf to his mum, but then he heard a voice. It was a big, scary dragon. He was trembling with fear. The dragon was looking straight at Jack. Jack quickly jumped behind his mum. The dragon said "I am here for that leaf". Jack was very scared and he silently handed the leaf over to the dragon. The dragon thanked Jack and flew away. Jack was still trembling when his mum asked him what had happened. After telling her the story, Jack ran off and hugged his teddy. He was very happy that he had been brave and returned the leaf safely to its rightful owner.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a wander in the big park. She saw many trees and flowers. Amy loved to play in the park. She saw a mysterious big box under a tree. She was very curious about the box. Amy opened the box and found a lot of salt inside. She did not know why the salt was there. She wanted to take some salt home, but it was too heavy to carry. Amy was sad because she could not take the salt with her. A nice boy named Tom saw Amy looking sad. He asked her what was wrong. Amy told him about the salt in the box. Tom had an idea. He went to his house and came back with a small bag. They put some salt in the bag and took it home together. Amy and Tom became good friends and they solved the mystery of the salt in the park.

One day, a big van came to the park. A man got out of the van. He had a lot of dry clothes in his arms. He walked to a bench and put the clothes down. A little girl named Sue saw the man. She walked up to him and said, "Hi! What are you doing?" The man smiled and said, "I am folding my clothes. Do you want to help me?" Sue nodded and started to help him fold the clothes. As they were folding, a big wind came. It blew all of the dry clothes up into the air! Sue and the man looked at each other and laughed. They ran around the park to catch the clothes. And after that, they became good friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a lot of birds. They were hungry. So, Lily had an idea. She went home and asked her mom for a muffin. Her mom said yes and gave her a big muffin. Lily went back to the park with the muffin. She broke the muffin into small pieces. Then she scatter the pieces on the ground. The birds came and ate the muffin pieces. They were very happy. A big bird with a serious face came to Lily. The bird said thank you to her. Lily felt happy. She knew she had helped the birds. From that day on, Lily always brought muffins to the park for her bird friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside, no matter the season. In the hot sun, she would test her new toys, and in the cold snow, she would build snowmen with her friends. One day, Lily and her friends saw a big, dangerous hole in the ground. They knew it was not safe, but they wanted to test how deep it was. So, they found a long stick and put it in the hole. It was very deep! They knew they should stay away from the hole and tell a grown-up about it. Lily and her friends told their parents about the dangerous hole. The grown-ups thanked them for being smart and safe. They put a big fence around the hole so no one would fall in. The children learned that it was important to tell grown-ups about dangerous things, and they played happily and safely for the rest of the season.
