Title: Johnnie and the Park

Johnnie was running through the park one day. He was racing against himself for fun; some days he raced against a friend, but today he was all by himself. As he ran he saw something gleaming in the grass. He picked it up; it was a jug! A very big jug. Curious, Johnnie started to play with it. He looked at it and thought how thoughtful it was. Suddenly he remembered that he was supposed to be racing. He picked up the jug and carried on running. Johnnie went as quickly as he could, and before he knew it he had circled the park. Suddenly he heard a voice from behind him. "Hey! You are a fast runner! Can I race against you?" Johnnie turned around to see a boy about his age smiling at him. He smiled back and said, "Sure!". So the two boys raced around the park, each one taking turns carrying the jug. In the end, Johnnie won the race. Both boys were breathless, but the competition had excited them. "Let's race again!" said Johnnie, holding the jug in both hands. The other boy agreed and they set off running, excited to race again.

One day a very wealthy man slipped on some oil in the street. The man was surprised, he had never slipped before. He noticed that the oil was from a can that had been lying there for a while. He thought to himself, "Why would someone leave oil on the street where anyone could slip?". The wealthy man soon forgot about the oil and decided to block it out of his mind. A few days later, the same man was coming back from a busy day when he noticed the same can of oil in the street. The man was angry; he had slipped on this oil already, and didn't understand why the can was still there! Suddenly, he got an idea – he would buy the oil. He bought it and put it in his bag. When the wealthy man got home, he smiled. He remembered why he bought the oil – he wanted to use it for his car. He was thankful that he bought the oil because without it, he would have kept slipping in the street. The man was happy to have been prepared and to have thought ahead, and he was no longer worried about slipping on the oil. Instead, he was glad that he had saved himself some money, and he was now more cautious than ever when walking down the street.

Anna liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Lily. Lily had long hair and a green dress. Anna liked to make knots in Lily's hair and pretend she was a princess. One day, Anna's mom said they had to go to the store. Anna wanted to bring Lily with her, but mom said no. Mom said Lily was too dirty and had too many knots in her hair. Mom said she would not permit Anna to take Lily to the store. Anna was sad and angry. She did not want to leave Lily alone. She did not want to go to the store. She did not want to listen to mom. She hid Lily under her bed and ran away. Mom looked for Anna. She called her name and checked every room. She did not find Anna. She was worried and scared. She did not know where Anna went. Anna ran to the park. She saw some kids playing on the swings and slides. She wanted to join them, but she felt lonely without Lily. She missed her doll. She wished she had listened to mom. She wished she had not made knots in Lily's hair. She wished she had not run away. She saw a bench and sat down. She cried and cried. She did not know how to go back home. She did not know how to say sorry to mom. A lady saw Anna crying. She asked her what was wrong. She asked her where her mom was. Anna told her everything. The lady was kind and gentle. She said she would help Anna find her mom. She said she would help Anna untie the knots in Lily's hair. She said she liked Lily's green dress. Anna smiled and hugged the lady. She felt happy and relieved. She thanked the lady and said she was sorry. The lady took Anna's hand and walked with her to the store. She found mom and gave her a big hug. Mom was happy and relieved. She hugged Anna and said she was sorry. She said she loved Anna and Lily. She said she liked Lily's green dress too. She said she would permit Anna to take Lily to the store. She said they would buy some shampoo and a comb for Lily's hair. Anna, mom, and the lady went to the store. They bought some shampoo and a comb. They also bought some ice cream and cookies. They went back home and washed and combed Lily's hair. They made her hair shiny and smooth. They ate ice cream and cookies and played with Lily. They had a lot of fun. They were a happy family.
