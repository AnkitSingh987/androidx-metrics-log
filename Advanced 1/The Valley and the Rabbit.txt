Title: The Valley and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a big valley. In the valley, there were many animals. One day, a small rabbit was very tired. He wanted to rest. He saw a big tree and lay down under it. But, the valley was scary. A big bear came to the tree. The rabbit was scared. The bear said, "Why are you scared?" The rabbit said, "The valley is scary, and I am small." The bear said, "Don't worry, I will help you." The bear and the rabbit became friends. They played in the valley and had fun. The rabbit was not scared anymore. They both lay down under the tree and took a rest. The scary valley became a happy place for them.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly aeroplane. It was sad because no one wanted to come and play with it. All the other aeroplanes were pretty and had many friends. One day, a little boy saw the ugly aeroplane. He did not care that it was ugly. He thought it was special. So, he came to play with it. They had a lot of fun together. Soon, the other kids saw the little boy and the ugly aeroplane playing. They all wanted to come and play too. The ugly aeroplane was happy because it now had many friends.

Once there was a small cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his ball. He would push the ball with his paw and watch it go in motion. One day, Tom did not know where his ball was. He looked all around his house. He looked under his bed, behind the door, and in his toy box. But he could not find his ball. Tom felt sad. Then, his friend, a little dog named Max, came to visit. Max saw Tom was sad and wanted to help. Max looked around and found the ball under a chair. Tom was so happy, and they played with the ball together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to balance on one foot like a flamingo. One day, she saw a pretty cloth on the ground. It was red, blue, and yellow. Lily picked it up and took it home. In her house, Lily's mom was making a delicious cake. It smelled so good! Lily showed her mom the pretty cloth she found. Her mom said, "What a nice cloth! Let's use it to cover the cake when it's done." When the cake was ready, Lily helped her mom put the pretty cloth on the cake. They took it to a fun party with all their friends. Everyone loved the cake and the pretty cloth. Lily felt happy that she found the cloth and could share it with her friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a chubby cat named Mimi. She loved to play and eat all day. Mimi had a special book that she liked to read with her best friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, Mimi and Tim were reading the book when Mimi got a boo-boo on her paw. Tim said, "Oh no, Mimi! Let me help you heal your boo-boo." Mimi meowed and let Tim see her paw. As Tim touched Mimi's paw, something unexpected happened. The book started to glow! Mimi and Tim looked at the book and saw a magic word in it. Tim read the word out loud, and suddenly, Mimi's boo-boo was all better. They both laughed and played, happy that the magic book had helped them heal Mimi's paw.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot liked to repeat everything his owner, Tim, said. Tim thought it was funny, so he would say "bitter" and Spot would bark "bitter" back. They played this game every day. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park. They saw a big tree with red fruit. Tim said, "I think these are bitter fruits, Spot." Spot barked "bitter" back, like always. They laughed and continued to play in the park. Later, Tim got hungry, so he picked a fruit from the tree. He took a big bite and made a funny face. It was a bitter fruit, just like Spot had said! They both laughed again, and from that day on, they only played the "bitter" game with things they knew were really bitter.
