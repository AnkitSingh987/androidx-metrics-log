Title: The Cool Cat Called Lucy and the Fox

Once upon a time there was a cool cat called Lucy. She really liked to stay in her special house. Every day, she had special things to eat and play with. But one day, Lucy's mommy told her that they ran out of things to supply her house. Lucy felt sad and didn't want to stay. She wanted to go and find more supplies to make her house cool again. So Lucy set off on an adventure. She visited many places and asked all the animals she met if they could help. She finally met a friendly fox who offered Lucy a lot of supplies to take home. She happily thanked the fox and hurried back home with all of her new supplies. When Lucy arrived home, she and her mommy worked together to make her house cool again. Lucy was so happy that she could stay and play in her house as before. The end.

In a warm jungle, there lived a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to play with his friends. They liked to jump, run, and catch things. One day, Momo and his friends were playing catch with a big, round ball. They threw the ball high into the air and tried to catch it. Momo was very good at catching the ball. His friends clapped and cheered for him. As they played, Momo saw a big, shiny butterfly. He wanted to catch it, too. Momo chased the butterfly through the warm jungle. He jumped and reached for it, but the butterfly flew away. Momo's friends came to help him catch the butterfly. They all laughed and played together in the warm jungle sun.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big bug in his room. He did not like bugs at all. He said, "I hate bugs!" The bug heard Tim and felt sad. The bug wanted to be friends with Tim. The bug went to Tim and said, "I am sorry, I do not want you to hate me. Can we be friends?" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that bugs could talk. Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, we can be friends." Tim and the bug played together in the open yard. They had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The bug turned into a beautiful butterfly. Tim could not believe his eyes. The butterfly said, "Thank you for being my friend. Now I can fly and be happy." Tim was happy too because he made a new friend and saw something amazing.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom liked to play in the park. One day, he saw a big fountain in the middle of the park. Tom walked up to the fountain and leaned on it. He looked at the water and saw fish swimming inside. Tom wanted to catch the fish, but he was too chubby to jump in. Tom tried to lean closer to the water, but he slipped and fell into the fountain. The fish swam away, and Tom got all wet. He climbed out and shook off the water. From that day on, Tom decided to be more careful when he played near the fountain.

Lily loved to sing. She sang all day, every day. She sang in the morning, she sang at lunch, she sang at night. She sang loud and high and low and soft. She sang songs she knew and songs she made up. She sang like an opera star. But Lily's mom and dad did not like her singing. They said it was too noisy and annoying. They said it gave them a headache and made the neighbors angry. They said she had to stop singing or they would take away her toys and her books and her crayons. Lily was stubborn. She did not want to stop singing. She loved singing too much. She thought her mom and dad were mean and silly. She said she would never stop singing. She said she would sing even louder and longer. One day, Lily's mom and dad had a surprise for her. They said they had a special gift for her. They said they would deliver it to her after dinner. Lily was curious and excited. She wondered what it could be. She hoped it was something fun and shiny and pretty. After dinner, Lily's mom and dad brought a big box to her room. They said it was her gift. They said they hoped she would like it. They said they wanted her to be happy and quiet. They opened the box and showed her what was inside. It was a microphone. A big, shiny, red microphone. It had a long cord and a switch and a stand. It looked like a real microphone that opera stars used. Lily's eyes widened and sparkled. She gasped and smiled and clapped. She thanked her mom and dad and hugged them. She said it was the best gift ever. She grabbed the microphone and turned it on. She put it close to her mouth and started to sing. She sang loud and high and low and soft. She sang songs she knew and songs she made up. She sang like an opera star. But the microphone was not a normal microphone. It was a special microphone. It had a secret button that Lily's mom and dad had pressed. It had a sound filter that changed Lily's voice. It made her voice sound funny and squeaky and silly. It made her voice sound like a mouse or a duck or a frog. Lily did not notice the change. She was too busy singing and enjoying her gift. She did not hear how she sounded. She did not see how her mom and dad laughed and winked at each other. She did not see how the neighbors smiled and clapped at their windows. She just sang and sang and sang. She sang like an opera star. A funny, squeaky, silly opera star. And she was happy. And so were her mom and dad. And so were the neighbors. And so was the microphone.
