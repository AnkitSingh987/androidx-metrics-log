Title: The Safari and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a safari. In the safari, there were many animals. There was a big lion, a tall giraffe, and a fast zebra. The animals were friends and liked to play together. One day, a little bird came to the safari. The bird said, "Hello, friends! I want to play with you." The animals were happy and played with the bird. They ran, jumped, and laughed. The bird told the animals about a delicious fruit tree far away from the safari. The animals wanted to taste the delicious fruit. So, they planned to escape the safari and find the fruit tree. They walked and walked until they found the tree. The fruit was red and sweet. The animals were excited and started to eat the fruit. But then, something unexpected happened! The fruit made the animals change colors. The lion turned blue, the giraffe turned pink, and the zebra turned green. They were surprised and didn't know what to do. The little bird said, "Don't worry, friends. This fruit is magic! The colors will go away soon." The animals laughed and played together with their new colors. They knew the colors would go away, but they were happy to have a fun adventure. The animals and the bird became best friends, and they all went back to the safari together.

Once upon a time there was a young pipe. The pipe was bored and wanted to improve. So the young pipe decided one day to go and explore. The young pipe kept walking and walking. He had been walking for so long that he felt tired. But he kept going. Soon the young pipe came across a pond. He wanted to explore the pond, so he jumped in and swam around. The water felt so cool and nice on a hot day. The pipe swam around for a bit and then he got an idea. He started to do some flips in the water, and then a backstroke. As he did each move, he felt himself improving. The young pipe was so proud of himself when he finished. He realised he had improved and was so glad he had decided to explore the pond. He swam back home feeling excited.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny ant who was only one inch long. He lived in a big garden with lots of flowers. The ant had a friend, a big green frog. The frog was envious of the ant because the ant could go into tiny places that the frog could not. One day, the ant found a big leaf. He put the leaf on the ground and called his friend, the frog, to come and see. The frog hopped over and looked at the leaf. The ant said, "Let's play on the leaf! We can slide down it like a slide!" The frog was happy to play with the ant. They took turns sliding down the leaf. The frog was not envious anymore because he could have fun with his friend, the tiny ant. They played together all day long in the big garden, sliding down the leaf and laughing.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a red box. The red box was full of oats. A little boy named Mark lived in the house. Mark loved to eat oats every day. He was a happy boy. One day, Mark saw a big bird outside. The bird was very hungry. Mark wanted to help the bird. He took some oats from the red box and gave them to the bird. The bird ate the oats and flew away. Mark felt good. But, the next day, the red box was empty. There were no more oats for Mark to eat. He was very sad. The bird came back with more birds. They all wanted more oats, but there were none left. Mark learned that he should not give away all his oats.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane had a mitten that she loved very much. It was a very cold day, and Jane wore her mitten to go outside and play. The sun was shining in the sky. Jane was playing with her friends when she saw a terrible big dog. The dog was very scary. Jane said to her friends, "Look at that big dog! I don't like it." Her friends were scared too, but Jane had an idea. She took off her mitten and waved it at the dog. The dog saw the mitten and started to play with it. Jane and her friends laughed and played with the dog too. The terrible big dog wasn't so scary after all. They all played together until the sun went down, and Jane went home with her mitten and new friend.
