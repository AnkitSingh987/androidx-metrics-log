Title: Tim and the Blocks

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to watch his toy cars go fast. One day, Tim saw a big stack of blocks. Tim was frightened, but he wanted to play with the blocks. Tim took a deep breath and walked up to the stack. He touched the blocks and they did not move. Tim felt happy and started to play. He built a tall tower with the blocks and put his toy cars around it. As Tim played, he forgot to be frightened. He laughed and watched his toy cars race around the block tower. Tim had so much fun playing with the stack of blocks that he was no longer scared. And from that day on, Tim loved to play with stacks of blocks and watch his toy cars race around them.

One day, a little fish named Finny swam near the beach. The water moved up and down. This was called the tide. Finny saw a big crab named Crusty. Crusty was not healthy. He looked sad and weak. Finny asked, "Crusty, why are you not healthy?" Crusty said, "I don't eat good food. I eat junk from the sea." Finny had an idea. He said, "I will help you. I will request good food for you." Finny swam to his other sea friends. He said, "Please give healthy food for Crusty." The sea friends agreed. They gave good food to Crusty. Crusty ate the food and became healthy and strong. In the end, Crusty learned to eat good food from his friends. He was happy and healthy. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends when they want to help you.

Once upon a time, on a lovely spring day, a little girl named Mia and her best friend, a dog named Max, went for a walk. They walked through the soft green grass and saw pretty flowers. They saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. Mia looked at Max and said, "Do you remember the fun we had last spring?" Max wagged his tail and barked, showing he remembered. They had played in the park, chased butterflies, and picked flowers. As they sat under the tree, a little bird flew down and sang a happy song. Mia and Max clapped and wagged their tails. They knew spring was a special time to have fun with friends. And they promised to always remember their lovely spring days together.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Tom. Tom was very nice to everyone he met. He lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. One day, Tom and Sue went outside to play. They looked up at the sky and saw the big universe above them. It was full of stars and planets. They thought it was so beautiful. Tom and Sue wanted to fill the universe with more beautiful things. They decided to blow bubbles into the sky. The bubbles went up, up, up into the sky. The universe was full of pretty bubbles, and Tom and Sue were very happy.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. One was a tiger and the other was a bear. They were walking in the forest together. All of a sudden, the bear saw something shiny in a hollow tree, so he decided to get it. The tiger saw this and said to the bear, "Let me help you, you can't reach it". But the bear didn't listen, he kept trying to get it himself. He kept stretching and stretching, but he couldn't reach it. Suddenly, the tiger said, "I have an idea! I can use my tail to lift you up so you can get it". The bear was very excited and said, "Yes, let's try it!" So the tiger put his tail below the bear and lifted him up and eventually the bear got the shiny thing. The bear and the tiger were very happy and hugged each other. They looked around and noticed that the forest was now empty. They smiled and knew that yield could be the solution. They high-fived each other and left the hollow tree with the shiny thing.
