Title: Tim and Mia and the Park

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play with their stuff in the park. They have cars, balls, dolls and books. They share their stuff and have fun. One day, they see a new boy in the park. He has a big bike. He rides very fast. He looks cool. Tim and Mia want to talk to him, but they are shy. "Hi, I am Sam," the boy says. He stops his bike near them. "Do you want to race with me?" Tim and Mia look at each other. They want to race, but they are scared. They do not have bikes. They only have cars. "Can we use our cars?" Tim asks. "Sure, why not?" Sam says. He smiles. "Let's go to the path. It is long and smooth. We can race there." Tim and Mia grab their cars. They follow Sam to the path. They line up their cars and their bike. Sam counts to three. "One, two, three, go!" Sam shouts. They push their cars and pedal their bike. They race along the path. They go fast. They feel the wind. They laugh. Sam wins the race. He is very good. He waits for Tim and Mia. They come second and third. They are happy. They did well. "That was fun!" Sam says. "You have nice cars. Can I play with them?" "Yes, you can," Tim and Mia say. "You have a cool bike. Can we try it?" "Sure, you can," Sam says. "We can take turns. We can be friends." They go back to the park. They play with their stuff and Sam's bike. They share and have fun. They are not shy anymore. They are happy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. Her name was Sarah and she had a favorite vegetable: cauliflower. Every morning, Sarah would help her mom in the kitchen and pick out the best cauliflower in the store. The cauliflower heads were always big and white and they smelled delicious! One day, Sarah's mom decided to buy a special cauliflower head. It had a green top and was very unique. Sarah's mom told her a special secret: "If you twist this green stem and it snaps, it will be even tastier!" Sarah was excited and carefully twisted the green stem. With excitement and anticipation, she waited… and soon a delicious smell wafted from the pan. Sarah tried the cauliflower head, and it tasted even better than before! Sarah and her mom laughed, full of joy from the delicious cauliflower. Sarah learnt a lesson that day: if we take care, we can make something special. With patience and love, even the simplest of things can be made delicious.

Once there was an orange bear named Price. Price loved going on adventures with his friends. One day Price decided he wanted to go on a special adventure. He asked his friends if they wanted to come along, but they said no. Price was a little sad that his friends couldn't come, but he decided to go on the adventure anyway. Price started his adventure and soon he came to an orange tree. Price was happy to see the tree and he jumped up, trying to grab one of the orange fruits. Suddenly, one of the fruits fell right into Price's paws. He was so excited he took a bite and it was delicious. Price thanked the orange tree and continued on his adventure. He soon came to a stream and he started to cross it. But Price slipped and fell into the water. He was very scared, but he knew he had to be brave. Suddenly, Price heard a voice calling his name. He looked up and saw a beautiful orange fish. The fish said that it had been watching Price's adventure and wanted to help. The fish told Price to put his paw on its back and it would help him across the stream. Price was so happy and quickly put his paw on the fish's back. The fish swam across the stream and Price made it safely to the other side. He said one final thank you to the orange fish, and continued on his way. Price's adventure was a success!
