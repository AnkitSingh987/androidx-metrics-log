Title: Lucy the Girl and the Mom

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her room. She saw her mom's shampoo bottle on the floor. Lucy thought it was a toy, so she picked it up and started to play with it. She was very happy and proud of her new toy. Lucy took the shampoo bottle with her everywhere she went. She played with it in the park, at her friend's house, and even at bedtime. Her mom and dad did not know that she had the shampoo bottle. One day, Lucy's mom was looking for the shampoo bottle. She could not find it anywhere. She asked Lucy if she had seen it. Lucy showed her mom the shampoo bottle. Her mom told her that it was not a toy. Lucy realized that she should not have taken it. Lucy felt sad, but she knew she had to give the shampoo bottle back to her mom. Her mom was very proud of Lucy for being honest and giving it back. From that day on, Lucy learned to ask before taking something that was not hers.

One day, a big polar bear named Bob and a little penguin named Tim were friends. They lived in a cold place with a lot of snow. They had a small igloo that they shared together. The igloo was their home and they liked to play inside. One day, a graceful swan named Sally came to visit them. She was very pretty and could fly. Sally said, "Hello, I am lost. Can I stay with you in your igloo?" Bob and Tim said, "Yes, you can stay with us. We like to share." They played together all day. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Sally told them, "I am not just a swan, I am a magic swan!" She waved her wings and their igloo got bigger. Now, they had even more space to play and share together. Bob, Tim, and Sally were very happy. They played in their big igloo every day. They learned that sharing is good and that friends can come from anywhere. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim had a shiny red jacket. He loved to wear the jacket every day. It was his favorite thing. One sunny day, Tim went to the park. He saw a big dog. The dog was fierce. It had big teeth and a loud bark. Tim was scared, but he wanted to be brave. Tim said to the dog, "You are fierce, but I am not scared. My shiny red jacket makes me brave." The dog looked at Tim and then at his shiny jacket. The dog stopped barking and wagged its tail. Tim and the dog became friends and played in the park all day.

One day, a wise cat named Tom found a pen on the ground. He wanted to use the pen to draw a picture. Tom asked his friend, a dog named Max, "Can you tie this pen to my paw? I want to draw." Max tied the pen to Tom's paw as he asked. Tom started to draw a picture of a big tree. Suddenly, the pen began to move by itself! It was an unexpected surprise. Tom and Max watched as the pen drew a beautiful flower next to the tree. Tom and Max were happy to see the lovely picture. They showed it to their friends and everyone liked it. The pen was magic and it made their day full of fun and joy.

Once upon a time, there was a big lake. In the lake, there was a little fish. The little fish was very hungry. One day, the little fish saw a leaf near the lake. The little fish said, "Yum! I want to eat the leaf!" So, the little fish swam to the leaf. The little fish tried to tear the leaf with its mouth. But the leaf was too big. Just then, a big frog saw the little fish. The frog was hungry too. The frog said, "Let me help you, little fish." The big frog and the little fish worked together. They tore the leaf into small pieces. The little fish and the big frog ate the leaf. They were not hungry anymore. They became good friends and played together in the lake every day. And they lived happily ever after.
