Title: Lily the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a cold month called March, there was a kind girl named Lily. Lily loved to scatter seeds in her garden for the birds to eat. She knew the birds were cold and hungry, so she wanted to help them. One cold day, a little bird saw Lily scatter the seeds. The bird was very happy and told all his friends. Soon, many birds came to eat the seeds in Lily's garden. They sang beautiful songs to thank her. As the days went by, Lily's garden became full of happy birds. The other children saw this and started to scatter seeds in their own gardens too. The whole town became a happy place filled with singing birds, and everyone learned the joy of helping others.

One day, it started to rain. A little girl named Sue had a case. The case was dirty. Sue wanted to clean her case, so she took it outside. She thought the rain would help wash the dirt away. Sue left her case in the rain. She went inside her house to wait. When she came back, the case was gone! Sue was sad and surprised. She looked around and saw a dog. The dog was playing with her case. Sue asked the dog, "Can I have my case back?" The dog wagged its tail and gave the case back to Sue. The rain had cleaned the case, and now Sue had a new friend, too. From that day on, Sue and the dog played together, even when it rained.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to read books. He had many books on his bookshelf. Every day, he would pick a new book to read. One day, Tim's mom made him a yummy snack. She poured some milk into a cup and gave him cookies. Tim was very happy. He took his snack and his book to a big chair. Tim sat in the chair and ate his yummy snack. He read his book and had a great time. When he finished, he put his book back on the bookshelf. Tim had a fun day reading and eating his yummy snack.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry bear. The bear lived in a cave. The cave was big and dark. The bear liked his cave. One day, the bear went outside. He saw a bunny. The bunny was scared. The bear did not want to hurt the bunny. The bear said, "I surrender. I will not eat you." The bunny was happy. The bear and the bunny became friends. They played together every day. The hungry bear found other food to eat. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little lizard named Lizzy. Lizzy lived in a big green garden with many trees and flowers. She had many friends, like birds, bugs, and frogs. One day, Lizzy saw her friend, the butterfly, crying. "Why are you crying, butterfly?" asked Lizzy. The butterfly said, "I have a tear in my wing, and I can't fly. I am sad." Lizzy wanted to help her friend. She thought for a moment and had an incredible idea. Lizzy went to find her friend, the spider. She asked the spider, "Can you help fix the butterfly's wing?" The spider agreed and spun a soft, strong thread. Together, they fixed the butterfly's wing with the spider's thread. The butterfly was so happy and said, "Thank you, Lizzy and spider!" Now the butterfly could fly again. All the friends in the garden learned that helping each other is a wonderful thing to do. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a dog named Max was playing outside. He had a red collar that he loved very much. His collar had his name on it. Max was always happy when he wore his collar. Max saw a squirrel and started to run after it. The squirrel ran up a tree and Max stopped. He looked around and saw that his collar was gone. Max was worried. He did not want to lose his collar. He looked everywhere for it. Max's friend, a cat named Luna, saw Max looking worried. "What's wrong, Max?" she asked. "I miss my collar," Max said. "I can't find it anywhere." Luna wanted to help Max, so they looked for the collar together. Then, they heard a sound in the bushes. They went to look and found a little bird. The bird had Max's collar! The bird was using the collar to make a nest. Max was happy to find his collar, but he did not want to take it from the bird. Max and Luna had an idea. They found some soft leaves and twigs and helped the bird make a new nest. The bird was happy with the new nest, and Max got his collar back. Max and Luna were happy they could help the bird and Max didn't have to miss his collar anymore.
