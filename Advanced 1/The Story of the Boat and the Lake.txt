Title: The Story of the Boat and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a big, calm lake. The lake was so calm that the sky could see its face in the water. The sky loved to look at its face in the lake. The sky was very blue and pretty. One day, the sky saw a small boat on the lake. The boat was trying to part the water to move. The boat was having a hard time. The sky wanted to help the boat. The sky asked the wind to blow gently. The wind blew, and the boat moved easily on the calm lake. The boat was happy, and the sky was happy too. They both learned that helping each other is good. The boat and the sky became friends. The moral of the story is that we should help each other when we can.

Once upon a summer, there lived a foolish mole. The mole was always getting into trouble. One day, it decided to go on a very foolish sort. It dug through the garden, eating all the flowers and fruits. Suddenly, it heard a loud noise coming from above. It had startled a grouchy old man who was watering his plants. The man yelled, "What are you doing?! You are being very foolish!" The mole was scared and ran away as fast as it could. The man, still mad, chased after the mole. The mole dug deeper and deeper until it found an underground bunker. Once inside, the mole felt safe. But, it was very dark and the mole couldn't find it's way out. The grouchy old man started to yell from outside, "You must sort your way out of the bunker! Or else you'll never leave!" The mole started to feel very scared. It desperately started to sort through the darkness in all directions until it found a way out. Finally, the foolish mole made it out of the bunker and ran back home, relieved to have escaped. From then on, the mole was much more careful and always thought twice before making a foolish sort.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Max. Max loved to play with his red ball. One day, he saw a big mug on the table. Max jumped up to get the mug. He heard his friend, Sam the cat, say, "Max, don't touch the mug!" But Max did not listen. He pushed the mug with his nose, and it fell on the floor. The mug broke into pieces. Sam came to help Max. "Let's fold a paper cup," Sam said. They took a paper and folded it into a cup. Max and Sam played with the paper cup, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was eager to learn new words. He had a big book with many words in it. Every day, he would read and try to own the words in his mind. One sunny day, Tim found a word he did not know. He was very excited. He ran to his mom and asked, "Mom, what is this word?" His mom looked at the word and smiled. "This word is a secret," she said. "You must find out what it means on your own." Tim was confused but he wanted to find out the secret word. He looked around the house for clues. In the kitchen, he found a big box with the secret word on it. Tim opened the box and saw a small, fluffy dog inside! The secret word was the dog's name. Tim was so happy and surprised. He now had a new friend and a new word to own in his mind.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lily. She loved to clap and dance all day. One day, Lily saw a lot of waste on the ground. She felt sad. Lily wanted to clean the waste and make her town pretty. Lily started to pick up the waste. She put the waste in a big bag. While she was cleaning, she found a box under the waste. The box was small and pretty. Lily was very curious about what was inside the box. She opened the box and found a little bird. The bird was hurt. Lily was sad for the bird. But then, the bird started to sing and dance. Lily clapped and laughed. The bird was not hurt, it was just playing! They became best friends and played together every day.
