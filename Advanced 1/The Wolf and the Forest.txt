Title: The Wolf and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a wolf. The wolf was not a bad wolf, but he was very hungry. He wanted to find some food that was easy to get. He looked around the forest and saw a small village nearby. In the village, there was a kind old man who liked to supply food to the animals. The wolf saw the old man and thought, "Maybe he will give me some food." So the wolf walked up to the old man and said, "Hello, can you please give me some food? I am very hungry." The kind old man smiled and said, "Of course, I will give you some food. But you must promise to be nice to the other animals and people in the village." The wolf agreed and the old man gave him some food. The wolf was happy and thanked the old man. From that day on, the wolf was nice to everyone in the village. He learned that being kind and asking for help was much better than being mean and trying to take things. The people in the village also learned that not all wolves are bad, and they should not judge someone before getting to know them. The moral of the story is: don't judge someone before you know them, and always be kind to others.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a brave girl named Lily. One day, a big hurricane came to the town. Everyone was scared, but Lily wanted to help. Lily said to her mom, "I want to find a way to stop the hurricane." Her mom was surprised, but she knew Lily was brave. She said, "Be careful, Lily." Lily went outside and saw a big tree. The tree was sad because the hurricane was hurting it. Lily hugged the tree and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." Suddenly, the hurricane stopped. It wasn't a real hurricane, but a sad wind that needed a friend. Lily and the wind became friends, and the town was safe again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and her dog. Lily loved to play outside with her dog. One day, they found a rough rock while playing in the yard. The rock was not pretty like the other rocks, but Lily liked it. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. Her mom nodded and said, "This rock may be rough, but it can teach us a lesson. It shows us that not everything has to be perfect to be special." Lily thought about what her mom said. She decided to keep the rough rock and put it in her room. Every day, when she looked at the rock, Lily remembered that it is okay to be different. And that was the most important lesson she learned from the rough rock.

Anna likes to design things with her blocks. She has many blocks of different colors and shapes. She can make houses, cars, animals and more. She likes to show her designs to her mom and dad. One day, Anna decides to design a big tower with her blocks. She uses many blocks to make a strong base. Then she puts more blocks on top, one by one. She tries to make the tower as high as she can. She uses some triangle blocks to make the top of the tower. "Look, mom, look!" Anna says. "I made a big tower with my blocks. Do you like it?" Mom comes to see Anna's tower. She smiles and says, "Wow, Anna, your tower is very impressive. You are very good at designing things. I like your tower very much." "Thank you, mom," Anna says. She is very happy and proud. But then, a loud noise makes Anna and mom turn around. It is Anna's brother, Ben. He is running towards them with his toy truck. He does not see Anna's tower. He bumps into it with his truck. The tower falls down. The blocks make a big mess. Some of the blocks are heavy and hurt Ben's truck. "Hey, Ben, what are you doing?" Anna shouts. "You broke my tower. You are very mean. You made a big mess. You hurt your truck." "Sorry, Anna, sorry," Ben says. He is sad and scared. He did not mean to break Anna's tower. He did not see it. He likes his truck. He does not want it to be hurt. Mom comes to hug Anna and Ben. She says, "It's okay, Anna, it's okay, Ben. It was an accident. Ben did not see your tower. He did not want to break it. He is sorry. You can build another tower. And we can fix Ben's truck. Let's clean up the mess together. And then we can play something else. How about that?" Anna and Ben nod. They say, "Okay, mom, okay." They are still sad and angry, but they also love each other. They help mom to clean up the blocks. They say sorry to each other. They hug and make up. Then they play something else. They are happy again.
