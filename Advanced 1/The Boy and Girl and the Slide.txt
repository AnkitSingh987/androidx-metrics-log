Title: The Boy and Girl and the Slide

One day, a boy and a girl went to the park. They saw a big slide. The boy said, "I want to go on the slide. It looks fun." The girl said, "Me too. Let's go together." They ran to the slide and climbed up the steps. But when they reached the top, they saw a dragon. The dragon was red and green. It had sharp teeth and claws. It breathed fire. The dragon said, "This is my slide. You cannot go on it. Go away or I will eat you." The boy and the girl were scared. They wanted to go back down. But the dragon blocked their way. They did not know what to do. They cried for help. A man heard their cries. He came to the park. He saw the dragon on the slide. He was not afraid. He had a hose. He turned on the water. He sprayed the dragon with the water. The dragon did not like the water. It made him wet and cold. He stopped breathing fire. He said, "Stop it. Stop it. That hurts." He ran away from the slide. He hid behind a tree. The man said, "Don't worry, kids. The dragon is gone. He was foolish. He thought he could scare you. But he was wrong. You are brave. You can go on the slide now." The boy and the girl thanked the man. They hugged him. They went on the slide. They had fun. They laughed and smiled. They forgot about the dragon. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big rhinoceros in his yard. The rhinoceros was lost and needed help. Tim wanted to help the rhinoceros find its way back home. He had a plan. Tim would grab a long stick and show the rhinoceros where to go. The rhinoceros saw the stick and started to follow Tim. They walked for a long time. The rhinoceros was happy to have a brave friend like Tim. At last, they found the rhinoceros's home. The rhinoceros was so glad to be back. Tim and the rhinoceros hugged and said goodbye. Tim went back home, feeling proud and brave.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and build towers with his blocks. One day, Tim saw his big sister, Sue, playing with a board game. Sue was very competitive and wanted to win all the time. Tim asked Sue, "Can I help you play?" Sue thought for a moment and said, "Yes, you can help me. But only if you promise to try your best to win." Tim agreed and they started to play the board game together. As they played, Tim learned how to be competitive too. They had lots of fun and laughed a lot. In the end, Tim helped Sue win the game. They were both very happy and decided to play the board game together every day.

Once upon a time there was a careless bunny. He was in the garden picking tomatoes. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. He turned around and saw his best friend, the fox. The bunny hopped over and said, "Hi Fox! What's wrong?" The fox said, "I finished all of the tomatoes. I'm so hungry!" "Oh no!" said the bunny. The bunny felt very sorry for his friend so he said, "Wait here. I'll find you some more tomatoes!" He hopped away and started searching the garden. He searched and searched, but he couldn't find any tomatoes. The bunny looked everywhere, but it was too late. The fox got very mad. He shouted, "You careless bunny! I'm still hungry!" Then, he trotted away, leaving the bunny all alone. The bunny felt sad. He had been trying to help but his carelessness had caused his friend to be angry. The bunny learned a valuable lesson about being careful. He knew that mistake would never happen again.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl. Her name was Sarah and she was three years old. She wanted to do something special, but she was scared. She was not sure if she could do it yet. Sarah's mom told her to trust herself. She said that if Sarah was brave, she could do anything. Sarah slowly nodded her head and agreed. Sarah put on her brave face and picked up her toys. She then looked around to make sure no one was watching before she carefully stepped out of the house. Sarah was scared and took slow, careful steps. As she kept walking, Sarah realized that she was doing it. She trusted herself, and she was so brave! She could do it! The end.
