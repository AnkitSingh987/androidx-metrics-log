Title: The Brown Puppy and the Box

Once there was a brown puppy. He loved to mark things with his paw. One day he was in the kitchen and he wanted to explore. He saw a big, white box called a freezer. He wanted to mark it so he used his paw and left a big brown mark on it. The puppy's owner saw the brown mark on the freezer. She was not happy and picked up the puppy. She said, "No more marking things without permission". But the puppy was only trying to explore and have fun. The owner saw this and hugged the puppy. She said, "It's okay, you can explore all you want, but please don't mark things without my okay". The pup was so happy. He promised he wouldn't mark anything else without his owner's permission. The brown pup and his owner had a lot of fun together from that day. They went exploring in the kitchen and elsewhere too. But the pup remembered to never to mark things without his owner's ok.

Sam and Mia like to play with their toy cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They pretend that the cars can go fast and make loud noises. Sometimes they race their cars on the floor or on the rug. One day, Sam and Mia find a big can in the garage. The can has a word on it: fuel. They do not know what fuel is, but they think it is something for their cars. They open the can and smell it. It smells bad and makes them cough. They pour some fuel on their cars and hope that they will go faster. But the fuel does not make the cars go faster. It makes them sticky and dirty. The fuel also spills on the floor and makes it slippery. Sam and Mia try to run with their cars, but they slip and fall. They are clumsy and hurt themselves. They cry and call for their mom. Mom comes and sees the mess. She is angry and scared. She tells Sam and Mia that fuel is not for their cars, but for the big car that dad drives. She says that fuel is dangerous and can catch fire. She takes the can and the cars away. She cleans the floor and the children. She gives them a hug and a band-aid. Sam and Mia are sorry and sad. They say they are sorry to mom and to each other. They promise not to play with fuel again. They ask mom if they can have their cars back. Mom says yes, but only if they play with them safely and nicely. Sam and Mia agree and thank mom. They go to their room and play with their cars. They do not kick or spill or slip. They are careful and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play, but sometimes he would trip and fall. One day, Spot found a big coat with a zipper on it. He wanted to wear the coat, so he tried to use the zipper. Spot asked his friend, a little bird named Chirpy, "How do I use this zipper?" Chirpy said, "You just need to believe you can do it, Spot!" Spot tried very hard to believe in himself, and he pulled the zipper up and down. The coat was now on Spot! Spot wore the coat and went to play with Chirpy. They ran and jumped, and Spot didn't feel clumsy anymore. He was so happy that he believed in himself and learned how to use the zipper. From that day on, Spot and Chirpy played together, and Spot always wore his coat with the zipper.

One day, a weird taxi was driving down the street. The taxi was blue and had a big, funny face on it. The taxi was happy to help people go to different places. One morning, the taxi saw an apple on the side of the road. The apple was not happy like the taxi. The apple was sad because it was starting to rot. The apple wanted to go to a better place where it could be happy again. The taxi wanted to help the apple. So, the taxi picked up the apple and they went on a fun ride. They saw many things like a big tree, a dog, and a happy sun. At the end of the ride, the apple felt better and was not rotting anymore. The apple and the taxi became good friends and they were both very happy.
