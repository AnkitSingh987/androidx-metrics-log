Title: Lily the Girl and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to look for pretty things in the woods. One day, Lily found a delicate crystal. It was shiny and sparkly. Lily wanted to show the crystal to her friend, Tom. She walked to his house and saw a sign on his door. The sign said, "Come in." So, Lily went inside to find Tom. Tom saw the delicate crystal and loved it too. They played with the crystal all day, being very careful not to break it. Lily and Tom were very happy to have found such a special thing. And they stayed best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a thin tube lying in a park. A three-year-old boy saw the tube and wanted to lift it. He walked over to the tube and asked his mother, "Can I lift it?" His mother said, "Yes, it looks too thin for you to carry." So the boy carefully picked up the tube. When he lifted it up, the boy was so surprised. There was a small teddy bear inside the tube! The boy excitedly said to his mother, "Look what I found!" He was so happy. His mother smiled and said, "Now you can take Teddy home and keep him safe." The boy proudly held the tube and began walking back home. The boy was sure the teddy bear would be the best friend he ever had.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy had a fragile nest in a big tree. He loved his nest and was very happy there. One day, Timmy found a big, round thing. It had a lot of weight. He thought it would be fun to play with it. So, he pushed it with his beak and it rolled down the hill. The big, round thing rolled faster and faster. It went into the woods and disturb a family of ants. The ants were not happy, but Timmy did not know. He just kept playing with the big, round thing. At last, the big, round thing stopped rolling. Timmy was tired and went back to his fragile nest. He learned that it was not good to disturb others. From that day on, he played with things that did not have a lot of weight and did not disturb anyone.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to race each other. They race on their bikes, on their feet, and in the pool. They are very competitive. They always want to win. One day, they find a razor in the bathroom. It is shiny and sharp. They wonder what it does. They decide to race with it. They say, "Who can shave the most hair from their legs?" They go to their room and close the door. They take off their pants and socks. They sit on the floor with the razor. They start to shave their legs. They are careful not to cut themselves. They say, "Look, I have more hair than you!" They laugh and tease each other. They shave faster and faster. They want to finish first. But then, they hear a knock on the door. It is their mom. She says, "What are you doing in there? Open the door right now!" They stop shaving. They look at each other. They look at their legs. They are bald and red. They are scared. They say, "Uh-oh."

Once upon a time, a thoughtful girl named Mia had a sunflower. She loved her sunflower very much. Every day, she would display it in the bright sun. The sunflower made Mia very happy. One sunny day, Mia went to see her sunflower. But, it was not there! Mia was sad. She looked and looked for her sunflower. She did not know where it went. Then, Mia saw a big sunflower on the ground. It was her sunflower! But now, it was not just one sunflower. It was many sunflowers! Mia's sunflower had made baby sunflowers. Mia was very happy and surprised. Now, she had a lot of sunflowers to display and share with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little girl. She loved watching cartoons on TV and wanted to catch them when they were on. Every morning she would get excited and run to the TV. One day, she was watching her favorite cartoon when an even cuter cartoon came on. She wanted to catch it so she jumped up from her chair and tried to grab it. But she was too small and couldn't catch it. But then, she had an idea. She ran to the kitchen and got some string. She tied one end to the TV and the other to her finger. When her favorite cartoon came on, she pulled the string and tried to catch it! It worked! She caught her cartoon and felt very happy. She watched it until it was finished and then went to show her mum what she had done!
