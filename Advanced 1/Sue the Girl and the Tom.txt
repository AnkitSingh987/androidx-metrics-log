Title: Sue the Girl and the Tom

One day, a little girl named Sue was walking outside. It started to rain. Sue was wearing her favorite vest but she did not want it to get wet. She saw a big tree and ran under it. Sue was happy because she stayed dry. Sue saw her friend Tom. Tom was wet because he did not have a vest. Sue said, "Tom, you need a vest like me!" Tom was sad because he did not have a vest. Sue wanted to help Tom. She had an idea. She said, "Let's go to my house and get you a vest." Sue and Tom went to Sue's house. Sue gave Tom a vest. Now, they both had vests and could play outside in the rain. They were independent and could take care of themselves. They learned that it is good to help friends when they need it.

One day, some ants were marching in a line. They were going to find food for their home. They were all the same, but one ant was different. This ant was bigger and stronger than the others. The ants found a big piece of food, but there was a fight. Some other bugs wanted the food too. The different ant wanted to help his friends. He thought of a new way to win the fight. The different ant started to march around the food. The other ants and bugs watched him. They all started to march too. They marched and marched until they were all tired. The fight stopped, and the bugs all decided to share the food. The different ant had saved the day by making everyone march instead of fight.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Pup. Pup had a big pole. He liked to play with the pole every day. Pup was very happy. One day, Pup met a cat named Kitty. Kitty liked spicy food. Kitty asked Pup if he had any spicy food. Pup did not own any spicy food. But Pup wanted to help Kitty. So, Pup went to the store and got some spicy food for Kitty. Kitty was very happy. Pup and Kitty played with the pole and ate spicy food. They became good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

George was feeling sad. He was sitting on the gray blanket in his bedroom, all alone. Mommy came in and asked George why he was so sad. "I don't have anyone to play with," George said while crying. Mommy comforted him with a hug and said "How about we set up a game with some of your stuffed animals? Then you can play together!" George's face lit up and he quickly ran to his toy sack. He tossed his toys out on the blanket and right away a game was made. He was so happy and laughed as he played with his new friends. He looked up at Mommy with a huge smile and said "Thank You!"

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He was only three years old and loved to explore. One day he decided to go for a walk in the woods. He saw a lot of trees and eventually stumbled across an old gumball machine. Jack was so excited that he ran up to it and started pressing buttons. Suddenly, his two older friends, John and Mary, showed up. Mary said, "What do you think you're doing? That's a stupid idea." But John said, "Let him try. We can let him manage this machine by himself." So Jack started pressing buttons again and was able to manage the machine. Suddenly a gumball rolled out! Jack was so excited and quickly grabbed it up and ate it. John and Mary were both amazed and laughed at Jack's success. Jack smiled back and said "See, I managed it!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside with her friends. One sunny day, she saw a pretty flower. The flower was red and yellow. An elderly lady was sitting near the flower. She said, "Hello, Lily. Do you like my flower?" Lily said, "Yes, I do. It is very pretty." The elderly lady smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. You can have it." Just then, a big bee came near the flower. Lily was scared of bees. She screamed, "Aaah! Bee!" The elderly lady said, "Don't worry, Lily. The bee won't hurt you. It just likes the flower too." Lily was brave and took the flower. She said, "Thank you, elderly lady." The lady smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. Enjoy the pretty flower." And they both enjoyed the sunny day together.
