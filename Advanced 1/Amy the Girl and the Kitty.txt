Title: Amy the Girl and the Kitty

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a soft toy cat named Kitty. Amy loved to play with Kitty all day. They would talk and laugh together. One day, Amy and Kitty had a tea party. Amy put a plate on the table for Kitty. "Here is your plate, Kitty," Amy said. Kitty just sat there, looking at the plate. Amy pretended that Kitty said, "Thank you, Amy!" Amy and Kitty had a fun tea party. They talked about their day and their favorite games. "I like to play hide and seek," said Amy. "What about you, Kitty?" Amy made Kitty's soft paw move and said in a funny voice, "I like to play with you, Amy!" After their tea party, Amy and Kitty played more games. They were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. One day, as he was hopping, he saw a big black mill. Bob had never seen a mill before, so he hopped closer to take a look. Inside the mill, Bob met a nice mouse named Max. Max said, "Hi, Bob! Want to see how the mill works?" Bob nodded and followed Max around the mill. They saw big stones that could crush things. Max told Bob that the mill helped make food for everyone. As they walked, Bob saw a big black lever. Max warned, "Don't touch that lever, Bob. It will make the mill stop working." Bob remembered this and continued to explore the mill with Max. They had so much fun. Later, as Bob was hopping around, he accidentally bumped into the big black lever. The mill stopped working! Bob was scared, but he remembered what Max had told him. He quickly hopped back to the lever and pushed it back up. The mill started working again. Max smiled at Bob and said, "Good job, Bob! You fixed the mill!" Bob felt happy and proud. He had learned something new and helped his new friend. From that day on, Bob and Max became best friends and played together at the mill every day.

Anna and Ben are playing in the mud. They like to make mud pies and mud castles. They are very happy and very messy. Mom comes out and sees them. She is not happy. She says, "Look at your body! You are all dirty and wet. You need to take a bath right now!" Anna and Ben do not want to take a bath. They want to play more. They say, "No, Mom, no! We are not dirty. We are having fun!" Mom says, "Yes, you are dirty. And you are making a mess. Look at the grass and the flowers. They are all muddy too. You need to clean up and go inside." Anna and Ben are sad. They do not like to clean up. They do not like to go inside. They say, "Sorry, Mom, sorry. We will clean up and go inside. But can we play in the mud again tomorrow?" Mom says, "Maybe, if you are good and listen to me. But first, you need to take a bath and put on clean clothes. Come on, let's go." Anna and Ben follow Mom. They are still messy, but they are also tired and hungry. They hope they can play in the mud again tomorrow. They love to play in the mud.

Once upon a time, there was a fine crane who liked to dress up. She wore hats, scarves, and even shoes. All the other birds thought she looked funny, but she did not care. She loved to play dress up. One day, the fine crane was walking by the pond. She saw something in the water. It was a big fish! The crane thought the fish would be her friend. She put on her best hat and went to say hello. The fish looked at her, but then swam away. The crane was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish came back with a hat on its head! The fish wanted to play dress up too! The fine crane and the big fish became best friends. They dressed up and played together every day. The other birds were surprised, but they all joined in the fun.
