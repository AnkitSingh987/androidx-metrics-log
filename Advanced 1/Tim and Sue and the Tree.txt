Title: Tim and Sue and the Tree

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to play golf. They both liked to play golf a lot. They took their golf sticks and balls and went to the big green field. When they got there, they saw a big scary tree. Tim and Sue were a little scared, but they wanted to play golf. So, they took turns and shared their golf sticks and balls. They hit the balls and tried to get them into the holes. As they played, the big scary tree started to look less scary. They saw that it had a big smile on its face. Tim and Sue were not scared anymore. They played golf and had fun all day. They learned that sharing and being brave made things less scary.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She loved to play in her backyard with her dog, Spot. One day, Sally and Spot found a small, filthy bird with a hurt wing. Sally wanted to help the bird, so she took it inside her house. Sally showed the bird to her mom and said, "Mommy, can we heal the bird?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Sally, we can try to help the bird get better." They cleaned the bird and put a small bandage on its wing. Every day, Sally and her mom took care of the bird, giving it food and water. After a few days, the bird started to feel better. Its wing was healed, and it could fly again. Sally and her mom took the bird to the backyard and let it go. As the bird flew away, Sally's mom said, "You did a good thing, Sally. You showed kindness to a creature in need." Sally learned that it was important to be kind and help others when they are hurt.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a helpful frog named Fred. Fred loved to hop and jump with speed. He was very fast and liked to help his friends. One day, Fred met a sad bee named Betty. Betty could not fly fast. "Why are you sad?" asked Fred. "I can't fly fast to find my friends," said Betty. Fred wanted to help Betty, so he said, "I can show you how to fly with speed. Just follow me." Fred hopped and jumped with speed, and Betty tried to fly fast too. They found Betty's friends near a big flower. Betty was so happy that she gave Fred a kiss on his cheek. "Thank you, Fred, for being so helpful," said Betty. All the friends played together and had a fun day in the forest.

One day an angry monkey had a idea to settle a problem. He found a whip lying on the ground and decided to use it. He quickly went around, swinging the whip hard, until everyone was settled. All the animals in the zoo were happy again, and the monkey felt good. But soon the angry monkey had to go home. He said goodbye to his friends and left the zoo happy. He went home and settled right in. The whip went back on the ground and the monkey was content.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Joey. Joey was a very adventurous boy. He loved to explore and try new things. One day, Joey went out in the garden. He was looking for something fun to do. He spotted a large bush and decided to hide in it. He snuggled in between the leaves and held onto an arm of the bush. Suddenly, he heard someone calling. It was his best friend, Lisa. She wanted to play with him. She asked, "Where are you, Joey?" Joey laughed and shouted "I'm over here!" He jumped out from the bush and the two of them ran around, laughing and chasing each other. They had the best time and Joey was glad he was so adventurous.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lucy. She had a big coat that she loved to wear. One day, Lucy felt hungry, so she went to the kitchen to find some food. While looking for food, Lucy saw her coat hanging by the door. She thought she saw something strange in the pocket. Lucy went closer to discover what it was. She put her hand in the pocket and found a small, shiny coin. Lucy was so happy with her discovery. She took the coin and ran to the store. She bought a big, yummy apple with the coin. Lucy went back home, put on her coat, and ate the apple. She was not hungry anymore, and she loved her coat even more.
