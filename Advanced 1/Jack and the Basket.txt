Title: Jack and the Basket

Once upon a time, there were two brothers named Jack and Ben. They were very excited, because their mom was carrying a big basket of food. "What's in the basket, mommy?" asked Jack. "It's yummy food," said their mom. "Can we have some?" asked Ben. "Yes, but you have to wait," said their mom. So Jack and Ben waited and waited, until it was time to eat. Their mom gave them each a teaspoon and she said, "Be careful not to spill!" Jack and Ben each used their teaspoon to carry yummy food from the basket to their plates. They ate the food with big smiles on their faces. When they were finished, their mom said, "You did a great job! You both used your teaspoon to carry the yummy food!"

Once upon a time there was a musician. He played beautiful music, but nobody came to listen. He felt sad that no one appreciated his music. He was feeling frustrated. One day, he had a big surprise. A very special person came to see him play. It was a 3 year old child! The child watched the musician and smiled. He waved and danced to the music. The musician was so happy that someone appreciated his music. He became very excited and gave the child a present. The present was a music book with lots of fun songs. The child was so excited about the present. He hugged the musician and thanked him for the gift. From that day onward, the musician and the child were best friends. They continued to play music together and the musician was no longer frustrated. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in his garden. One day, he found a small plant. The plant was very mild and green. Tim wanted to help the plant grow big and strong. He asked his mom, "What can I do to help the plant?" His mom said, "You can shoot water on it with your water can." Tim was very happy and went to get his water can. Every day, Tim would shoot water on the mild plant. He watched as the plant grew bigger and bigger. Soon, the plant had big, beautiful flowers. Tim and his mom were very proud of their pretty garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a shiny gem that she found in her backyard. Mia loved her gem and took it everywhere with her. She felt proud to own it. One day, while playing at the park, Mia met a boy named Tim. Tim saw the gem and said, "Wow, that's a pretty gem! Can I see it?" Mia, being alert, thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, but please be careful." Tim held the gem and admired it. He gave it back to Mia and said, "Thank you for letting me see your gem. It's so shiny!" Mia smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad you like it too." They played together all day, and Mia and Tim became good friends. From that day on, they always played together at the park, and Mia always had her shiny gem with her. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a loud bird. The bird liked to sing all day. One day, the bird found an apple on the ground. The apple was not good. It was a rot apple. The bird was sad because it could not eat the rot apple. It wanted a good apple to eat. The bird flew up to the tree to find a good apple. It saw many good apples in the tree. The bird picked a good apple from the tree. It was very happy. The bird sang a loud song to say thank you to the tree. The bird ate the good apple and was full. From that day, the bird and the tree were best friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jen. She was three years old. One night, Jen and her mom went for a walk in a dark, wild part of the forest. Jen could see all sorts of things in the shadows that she had never seen before! Jen was curious, so she asked her mom: "Mom, what are all these things? What are we doing?" Her mom explained, "These are animals, Jen. We are looking for wild animals in their natural habitat." Jen was amazed. She saw big and small animals, running, jumping and making strange and wonderful noises. She looked at her mom in wonder and whispered, "Wow!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's why I wanted to show you this wild and dark place, Jen. Nature is beautiful and mysterious, don't you think?" Jen smiled and said,”Yes, Mom! I love it!" Jen will never forget the wonderful experience in the dark, wild part of the forest.
