Title: Sarah the Lovely Girl and the Mum

Once, there was a lovely little girl named Sarah. She was very adorable, and liked singing and playing with her dolls. Everyone just adored her! One day, Sarah's mum took her to the big bathroom. She wanted to protect Sarah and make sure she was safe. When Sarah arrived in the bathroom, she was amazed! It was so big, and full of interesting things. Sarah ran around, discovering all sorts of surprises. Suddenly, Sarah's mum saw a big lizard. She quickly rushed to protect Sarah and grabbed her away. But Sarah wasn't scared, she thought the lizard was cute! Sarah and her mum happily left the bathroom. Looking back, Sarah waved at the lizard, saying goodbye with a smile.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big slide. Tom wanted to go on the slide, but Lily was scared. "Come on, Lily, it's fun!" Tom said. "Don't be scared. I dare you to go on the slide with me." Lily looked at the slide. It was very high and steep. She felt a funny feeling in her stomach. She shook her head. "No, Tom, I don't want to. It's too high. I might fall and hurt myself." Tom frowned. He thought Lily was being silly. He wanted to show her that he was brave. "Fine, then I'll go by myself. Watch me, Lily. I'm not scared of anything." Tom ran to the slide and climbed up the ladder. He reached the top and sat on the edge. He looked down and smiled. He was ready to slide. But then he heard a loud roar. He turned his head and saw a big dog. The dog was barking and running towards the slide. It looked angry and hungry. Tom's smile turned into a scream. He was scared. He wished he had stayed with Lily. He wished he had not dared her to go on the slide. He slid down as fast as he could. He hoped the dog would not catch him. Lily saw Tom and the dog. She was scared too. She ran to Tom and hugged him. "Tom, are you okay? That was a scary dog. I'm sorry I didn't go on the slide with you." Tom hugged Lily back. He was glad she was there. He was sorry he had been mean to her. "It's okay, Lily. I'm sorry too. I was wrong. The slide was too high. And the dog was too scary. Let's go and play something else. Something safe and fun." Lily nodded. She agreed with Tom. They held hands and walked away from the slide. They decided to play on the swings instead. They were happy and not scared anymore.

One day, a nice dog named Spot wanted to play with his ball. He asked his friend, a little bird named Chirpy, to bring the ball to the big tree. "Chirpy, can you bring the ball to the big tree?" Spot said. Chirpy said, "Yes, Spot, I can do that!" Chirpy picked up the ball with his beak and flew to the big tree. Spot and Chirpy wanted to play a game. The goal was to kick the ball into a box. Spot would kick the ball and Chirpy would try to stop it. They played and played, having lots of fun. Spot kicked the ball and Chirpy tried to stop it, but the ball went into the box. "Yay, I got a goal!" Spot said, wagging his tail. Chirpy was happy for Spot, but he wanted to try too. So, they switched places. Chirpy kicked the ball with his little feet and Spot tried to stop it. The ball went into the box again. "Yay, I got a goal too!" Chirpy said, flapping his wings. Spot and Chirpy were both happy and tired. They sat under the big tree, smiling and laughing. They were happy they played the game together.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray automobile. It was a very happy automobile. It liked to drive around the town with its family. One day, the gray automobile saw a big ball. The ball was very soft. The gray automobile wanted to play with the ball. So, it went close to the ball and gave it a little squeeze. The ball made a funny noise, and the gray automobile laughed. The gray automobile and the soft ball became good friends. They played together every day. The family in the gray automobile was very happy too. They loved to watch the gray automobile and the soft ball play and squeeze each other. And they all lived happily ever after.
