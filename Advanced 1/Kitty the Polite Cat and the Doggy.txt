Title: Kitty the Polite Cat and the Doggy

Once upon a time, in a small quiet town, there lived a polite cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her friends, but sometimes, she would suffer when they were not nice to her. One sunny day, Kitty was playing with her friend, Doggy. Doggy was not being polite and took Kitty's toy away. Kitty felt sad and suffered because of Doggy's actions. She didn't know what to do. Kitty decided to talk to her mom. Her mom told her that it is important to be polite and kind to others, even when they are not nice to us. So, Kitty went back to Doggy and shared her toy with him. Doggy learned from Kitty's kindness and started being polite too. From that day on, Kitty and Doggy played together happily and were always polite to each other. They learned that being kind and polite makes everyone happy and no one has to suffer. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little bunny who hopped outside to play. The bunny was lucky enough to find some sunshine and enjoy the scenery around them. As the bunny hopped around, they stumbled upon a patch of clover. The bunny was so happy, they couldn’t believe their luck. “Oh, I’m so lucky!” cheered the bunny. Just then, a big rabbit came by hopping. “I see you found a patch of clover. What will you do with that?” asked the big rabbit. The little bunny thought and then said “I think I’ll settle down and enjoy the clover in the sunshine.” The big rabbit smiled and said “That is a great idea! Make sure to find ways to enjoy what you already have.” The little bunny then hopped off, happy and content. The moral of this story is that it is important to take the time to enjoy the good things in life. You don’t need lots, just find a few of the things that make you happy, and settle down and enjoy them.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy rice for dinner. At the store, they found a big bag of rice. Tim's mom said, "Tim, help me move the rice to the cart." Tim helped his mom and they moved the rice to the cart. On their way home, Tim and his mom saw a dog. The dog was very hungry. Tim's mom had an idea. She opened the bag of rice and gave some to the dog. But the dog did not eat it. The dog said, "I don't like this rice. It is spoiled." Tim and his mom were very surprised. They did not know that dogs could talk. They took the spoiled rice back to the store and got a new bag. The dog was happy with the new rice and thanked Tim and his mom. From that day on, Tim and his mom became good friends with the talking dog.

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day. One day, Spot's family had a big meeting. They all sat together and talked. Spot's mom said, "Spot needs a bath before the meeting." Spot did not like baths, but he wanted to be with his family. So, he let his mom bathe him in warm water. After the bath, Spot went to the meeting. But, it was not a normal meeting. It was a surprise party for Spot! There were balloons, treats, and lots of friends to play with. Spot was so happy and had the best time ever!

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a jog. He liked to run in the big park near his house. As he ran, he saw a big monster. Tim was scared, but the monster said, "Hi, I am a polite monster. My name is Max." Tim stopped and said, "Hi, Max. I am Tim." They talked and played together. Max was very nice and not scary at all. They had a lot of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. Max turned into a little boy, just like Tim! Max said, "I was a monster because I was not nice. But when I met you, I learned how to be polite and kind. Now, I am a boy again." Tim and Max were very happy to be friends. They learned that being polite and kind is very important.
