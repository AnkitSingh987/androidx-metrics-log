Title: Lily the Girl and the Veil

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a pretty veil that she loved very much. One day, Lily's mom told her to remain inside the house, because it was going to rain outside. But Lily wanted to play outside with her veil. She did not listen to her mom, and she went outside. The sky was dark, and Lily felt a little scared. But she still played with her veil, running and laughing. Soon, the rain started to fall. The rain made the ground wet and muddy. Lily's veil got dirty and spoiled. She cried and ran back inside the house. Her mom was sad too, because Lily did not listen. Now, Lily's pretty veil was spoiled and could not be fixed.

Once upon a time, there was a big pond. In the pond, there lived a fish named Fin. Fin had a friend named Duck. They liked to play together every day. One day, Duck told Fin, "I am going away for a trip. Please take care of my baby ducks." Fin said, "Yes, I will." But, Fin forgets things. He was ashamed of this. The next day, Fin saw a big, scary bird. The bird wanted to eat the baby ducks. Fin tried to remember what Duck asked him to do. He forgot! The big bird took the baby ducks away. Fin was sad and scared. He did not know what to do. Duck came back and saw what happened. Duck was sad too.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy bean. The hairy bean lived in a big garden with many other beans. One day, a small boy named Tim came to the garden. Tim saw the hairy bean and said, "Hello, hairy bean! You are different from the other beans." The hairy bean felt sad because it was not like the other beans. It decided to leave the garden and find a new home. While walking, the hairy bean met a nice bird. The bird said, "Why are you sad, hairy bean?" The hairy bean told the bird, "I am not like the other beans. I am hairy, and they are not. I want to find a new home where I can be happy." The bird thought for a moment and said, "Being different is not bad. It makes you special. You should go back to the garden and be with your friends." The hairy bean listened to the bird and went back to the garden. The other beans welcomed the hairy bean and said, "We are happy you are back. We like that you are different. It makes our garden more fun." The hairy bean learned that being different is good and that true friends will always like you for who you are.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her mommy's makeup. One day, when her mommy was not looking, she took the makeup and stored it in her room. Later, Lily put on the makeup. She put it all over her face. It was a big mess. When her mommy saw her, she was not happy. Lily felt ashamed. She knew she should not have taken the makeup without asking. Mommy told Lily that it is always important to ask before taking something that is not yours. Lily learned her lesson and promised to always ask first. From that day on, Lily never took anything without asking, and she became a very good girl.

One day, Tim and Sam were playing outside. They saw a big, expensive toy car in the shop. They both wanted the toy car, but they did not have enough money. They decided to find a job to get money for the toy car. Tim and Sam went to Mr. Brown's house. Mr. Brown said, "I need help to split the wood. If you do the job, I will give you money." Tim and Sam were happy and said, "We will do the job!" They worked hard all day to split the wood. At the end of the day, they got the money from Mr. Brown. They went to the shop to buy the expensive toy car. But when they got there, the toy car was gone! They were sad. Then they saw a girl with the toy car. The girl said, "I saw you work hard. I wanted to say thank you and share my toy car with you." Tim and Sam learned that sharing is better than having things all to yourself.
