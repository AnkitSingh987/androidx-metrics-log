Title: The Lady and the Box

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a nice lady. She loved to cook. One day, she was cooking a big meal for her family. She was very happy and comfortable in her kitchen. While cooking, she saw a shiny razor on the table. The lady did not know how it got there. She thought it was a toy and put it in the toy box, so her little boy could play with it later. When the family sat down to eat, the little boy found the razor in the toy box. He knew it was not a toy and showed it to his mom. The mom was very surprised and thanked her son. She put the razor away in a safe place. The family learned that it is important to be careful with things that can hurt and to always tell an adult when they find something dangerous.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw an enormous corn field near his house. Tim thought it would be fun to play in the corn field. While playing, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Let's play hide and seek in the corn!" Tim agreed, and they started to play. But soon, they got lost in the enormous corn field. They could not find their way out. Tim and Sue started to cry. Then, they heard a voice. It was Tim's mom. She said, "I found you! Let's go home." Tim and Sue were so happy. They held hands and followed Tim's mom out of the corn field. They learned to never play in the corn field again.

Once upon a time, there was a big cave in the woods. Many animals lived there. One day, a selfish fox came to the cave. He wanted the cave all to himself. He told the other animals to leave. A small bird lived in the cave too. She did not want to leave her home. The bird had an idea. She went to find her friend, the big bear. The bird told the bear about the selfish fox. The big bear decided to rescue the cave. He went to the cave and saw the selfish fox. The bear roared loudly. The fox got scared and ran away. The bird and the other animals were happy. They all lived in the cave together again.

One day, a chubby cat named Tom wanted to organize a fun play day for his friends. He thought it would be nice to have treats for everyone too. So, Tom made a list of his friends and the treats they liked. Tom went to the store and got treats for all his friends. He got tasty fish for the cats, yummy bones for the dogs, and sweet fruits for the birds. He put all the treats in a big bag and took it to the park. At the park, all of Tom's friends came to play. They played fun games like hide and seek, tag, and catch. After playing, they all enjoyed their treats together. They were so happy and thanked Tom for organizing the fun play day.

Once upon a time, there was a silly bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to wake up early and watch the sunrise. Every morning, he would fly up high and sing a happy song as the sun came up. One day, Bobo met a little girl named Lulu. Lulu asked, "Why do you get up so early, Bobo?" Bobo replied, "I love to see the sunrise. It makes me happy." Lulu thought that was a great idea, so she decided to wake up early too. The next morning, Lulu and Bobo woke up early and watched the sunrise together. They laughed and sang silly songs as the sun came up. From that day on, they became the best of friends and enjoyed many more sunrises together.

Once there was a little boy. His name was Joe. He felt so miserable because he was so small. He wanted to do the things that bigger boys do. One day Joe saw his father lifting a big potato. Joe wanted to try too, so he asked his father for help. "Dad, can you help me lift a potato?" asked Joe. "Of course!" said his dad. He put the big potato down and kneeled beside Joe. With his father's help, Joe was able to lift the potato up. "I did it!" shouted Joe. He was so proud of himself that he tried to lift even bigger potatoes. Joe felt much better now. He wasn't so miserable anymore. He could finally do the same things as the bigger boys!
