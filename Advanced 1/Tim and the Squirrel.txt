Title: Tim and the Squirrel

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They liked to walk in the big park near their house. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim said, "Spot, let's hurry! I want to see the ducks at the pond." As they walked, they met a persistent little squirrel. The squirrel followed Tim and Spot, jumping from tree to tree. Tim laughed and said, "Silly squirrel, you can't come with us to see the ducks!" When they got to the pond, they saw the ducks swimming. But something unexpected happened. The ducks started to walk out of the pond and onto the grass. Tim and Spot were surprised. The ducks said, "We want to go for a walk too!" Tim, Spot, and the ducks all walked together in the park. The squirrel still followed them, jumping from tree to tree. They all had a fun day at the park.

One day, a polite cat named Kitty went for a walk. She liked to make friends with everyone. While walking, she heard a sound in a big tree. She looked up and saw a little bird. "Hi, little bird! Do you want to be my friend?" Kitty asked. The little bird nodded and they played together. They had so much fun that they forgot about the time. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came and scared the little bird. Kitty knew she had to help her friend. She made a loud sound to scare the big dog away. The dog was surprised and ran away. Kitty and the little bird were safe. They thanked each other for being brave. From that day on, they were best friends and always played together. And they were always polite to one another.

Sara and Ben liked to play in the swamp near their house. They liked to look for frogs and bugs and splash in the water. One day, they found a big log that looked like a boat. They decided to pretend they were pirates and sail the swamp. "Ahoy, matey!" Ben said to Sara. "Are you ready to serve on my ship?" "Yes, captain!" Sara said. "But where are we going?" "We are going to find treasure, of course!" Ben said. "But we have to be careful. There are many dangers in the swamp. Like crocodiles and snakes and monsters!" Sara and Ben sailed the log across the swamp, making pirate noises and waving sticks as swords. They had a lot of fun. But they did not see the big shadow under the water that followed them. Suddenly, the log shook and tilted. Sara and Ben screamed as they fell into the water. They saw a huge crocodile with sharp teeth and a long tail. It had bumped their log and wanted to eat them. "Help!" Sara and Ben cried. "Help us!" Luckily, their dad heard them. He was busy working in the garden, but he ran to the swamp when he heard their voices. He grabbed a rake and scared the crocodile away. He pulled Sara and Ben out of the water and hugged them. "Are you okay?" he asked. "What were you doing in the swamp?" "We were playing pirates, dad," Sara said. "But we met a crocodile. It was scary!" "I'm sorry, kids," dad said. "But you should not play in the swamp. It is not safe. There are many dangers in the swamp. Like crocodiles and snakes and monsters!" Sara and Ben nodded. They learned their lesson. They thanked their dad and went back to the house. They decided to play something else. Something safer. Like tea party or dress up. They never played pirates in the swamp again.

The storekeeper replied, "A mean, selfish man came in before you and he took the toy. He didn't care how disappointed you'd be!" Jill was so dismayed that she couldn't speak.
