Title: Mojo the Lovely Gorilla and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a lovely gorilla named Mojo. He lived in a big jungle and loved to swing through the trees. Every day Mojo would swing, play and explore. One day Mojo was resting in a tree when he heard something strange. He went down the tree and saw another gorilla, a smaller one. The smaller gorilla whispered something to Mojo. "Hello Mojo, I've been waiting for you". Mojo couldn't believe his eyes! He said, "Hello little one, why were you waiting for me?" The smaller gorilla smiled and said, "I wanted to be your friend! I think you're lovely!" Mojo was so happy that he gave the smaller gorilla a big hug! From that day on, the two gorillas were best friends, and they had lots of fun in the jungle. They played and laughed and whispered secrets to each other. Mojo knew that he had a lovely, new friend and he was very happy.

One day, a boy and a girl went to a farm. They saw many animals and plants. They saw some corn in the field. The boy said, "I love corn! It is so yummy!" The girl agreed, "Yes, it is very easy to eat and tasty too!" They started to discuss how to pick the corn. The girl said, "We should ask the farmer if we can take some." The boy said, "That is a good idea." So, they went to find the farmer. When they found the farmer, they asked if they could have some corn. The farmer said, "Of course! But first, you must find my lost chicken. It is hiding somewhere on the farm." The boy and girl were surprised, but they agreed to look for the chicken. They looked all over the farm for the chicken. They found it hiding behind a big tree. The boy said, "We found you, chicken!" The chicken said, "You found me! Now, I will give you a surprise." The chicken gave them a big basket of corn! The boy and girl were so happy. They thanked the chicken and the farmer. They went home with their corn and had a yummy dinner.

One day, a fast car and a big truck were on a road. They were very enthusiastic. They wanted to race each other to see who was the fastest. The big truck said, "Let's race to the big tree!" The fast car said, "Yes, I want to race too!" They started the race. The fast car went very fast. The big truck went fast too, but not as fast as the car. They raced and raced until they got to the big tree. The fast car won the race. The big truck was happy for the fast car, even though it lost. After the race, the fast car and the big truck saw two kids playing with toy soldiers. They were having a war. The car and the truck wanted to help the kids. So, they let the kids put the toy soldiers on them and they raced again. This time, they raced to see who could get the toy soldiers to the other side of the yard first. The big truck won this race because it could carry more toy soldiers. The fast car was happy for the big truck. They all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat oats for breakfast. One day, he found a big bag of oats in the kitchen. He wanted to make his oats brilliant, so he added all the colors he could find. The oats became red, blue, green, and yellow. But then, Tim's big sister, Jane, came into the kitchen. She saw the colored oats and became very mad. She did not like the mess Tim made. "You can't eat those oats!" she said. Tim was sad, but he did not want to fight with his sister. Tim went to his room and thought about how to fix the problem. He had an idea! He went back to the kitchen and cleaned up the mess. Then, he made a new bowl of oats and added only one color, his favorite, blue. Jane saw the blue oats and thought they were brilliant too. Tim and Jane ate the blue oats together and became happy again.
