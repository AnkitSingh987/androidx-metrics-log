Once there was a 3 year old girl named Daisy. She had a big red apple with her. She was skipping through the park, enjoying the beautiful day. All of a sudden she heard a loud voice behind her. She turned around and saw her mom, who was very frustrated. "Daisy, why did you lie to me?" said her mom. Daisy was very confused and didn't know what to say. She just stared at the apple she was holding, not sure what to do. "Daisy, did you tell your teacher that I am not your mom?" asked her mom again. Daisy was too afraid to say anything, so she just shook her head. "I'm so disappointed, Daisy," said her mom, "but I still love you. From now on, remember to be honest and never lie again." Daisy nodded and gave her mom a big hug. She was relieved that her mom still loved her. She knew that honesty was the best policy and made a promise to never lie again.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a clumsy bird named Daisy. Daisy loved to whistle and play with her friends. One sunny day, Daisy was flying and saw a big, pretty flower. Daisy landed near the flower and said, "Hello, pretty flower! My name is Daisy. Do you want to be my friend?" The flower nodded and smiled. Daisy was so happy to have a new friend. Daisy started to whistle a happy song. She danced around the flower, but she was clumsy and fell. Daisy's friends came to help her. They all laughed and played together in the garden.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful field, there was a cow. The cow was big and had spots. The cow loved to eat grass all day. One day, a little girl came to the field. She saw the cow and wanted to be friends. The little girl walked up to the cow and said, "Hi, cow! My name is Sue. Will you be my friend?" The cow was happy to have a new friend. The cow bowed its head to say yes. Sue and the cow played all day. They ran in the field and had fun. As the sun went down, Sue said, "I have to go home now, cow. But I will come back to play with you again." The cow bowed its head again and watched Sue walk away. The cow knew they would be friends forever.

Once there was a little boy called Joe. Joe loved the sea and wished he could go there all the time. His best friend was a tiny anchor he found washed up on the beach one day. They were inseparable, and went everywhere together. One day, Joe asked his mom if they could take a trip to the ocean. His mom said that would be a nice surprise and he was so happy. The day they left arrived quickly. Joe was so excited he couldn't stand still! He hugged his little anchor tightly in his arms, and off they went. When they finally arrived, Joe couldn't believe his eyes. The ocean was so vast and so beautiful. He wanted to stay there forever. But then an unexpected thing happened. Joe dropped his anchor overboard. He was so upset and cried very loudly. He wouldn't give up however; he was determined to find his anchor again and so he kept looking. Joe searched and searched, and eventually he found it down very deep in the ocean. He was surprised and very relieved. Joe's mom was so proud of his persistence. She was happy that he didn't give up. He had been so brave and wouldn't let anything surprise him. From that day on, Joe and his anchor stayed together no matter where they went.
