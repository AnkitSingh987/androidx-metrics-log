Title: The Man and the Garden

Once there was a friendly man. He found a nice tomato in the garden. He picked up the tomato and put it in a warm room. He wanted to dry it fast. He left it in the warm room and waited. After a while, he came back to check the tomato. But it was too dry! He was very sad. The tomato was no longer red and juicy. It was brown and dried up. He never found a tomato like that again. The friendly man was very sad. The dry tomato ruined his day.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy. He wanted to take a trip and have some fun, so he asked his mom if he could go. His mom said he could try, but she warned him to be careful. The boy was so excited! He got ready for his trip and off he went. He walked and walked and soon, he had gone too far away. When darkness came, the boy felt scared. He tried to find his way home but he didn't know how. Everywhere he looked, it all looked the same. He tried and tried until he was too tired and had to stop. The boy never made it home. He never had the chance to tell his mom where he had gone. He got lost and never came back. Moral of the story: Be careful when you try something new—you never know what might happen!

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Bob. Bob loved to play with dust bunnies under the bed. One day, he saw a big dust bunny and wanted to catch it. Bob jumped to catch the dust bunny, but he missed it. He felt sad, but he did not give up. He tried again and again to catch the dust bunny. Finally, Bob caught the dust bunny and felt very happy. He played with it all day long. From that day on, Bob always looked for dust bunnies to play with, and he never missed them again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a big party with his mom. There were many kids and they all played fun games. Tim was very happy. His mom gave him a napkin and said, "Please use this when you eat, Tim." At the party, there was a big cake. Tim wanted some cake, so he went to get a piece. But just as he was about to eat it, a little girl named Sue came up to him. "Please, can I have your cake?" she asked. Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, you can have it." Sue was happy and thanked him. Later, Tim's mom saw that he had given his cake away. She was so proud of him for being kind. She said, "Tim, you are a successful boy for sharing your cake." Then, something unexpected happened. The party clown came over and gave Tim a big, special cake just for him. Tim was so happy, and he remembered to use his napkin when he ate it.

Mother was always nosy. She wanted to know everything that was going on in the house. One day, she saw that the window was open and the toy box was empty. She knew her son must have been up to something. "What have you been doing?" Mother asked. Her son smiled and said, "I'm working!" Mother was confused. "Working? What do you mean?" "I wanted to build a special surprise. So I put the toys to work!" Mother was surprised. She had never seen her son work like that before. She was very impressed. "Can I see it?" She asked eagerly. "Not yet!" He said. "It's still a secret. You'll have to wait and see!" Mother was curious, but she was patient. She was sure that whatever her son was building was going to be something special.

One day, a small ball wanted to bounce. It liked to bounce up and down, and all around. The ball was very careful not to bounce too high or too far. It wanted to stay safe. But one day, the ball saw a poem on the ground. The poem was sad because it could not bounce like the ball. The poem wanted to have fun too. The ball felt bad for the poem and wanted to help. The ball had an idea. It told the poem to hold on tight. The ball started to bounce very carefully with the poem. They both had fun and were happy. They became best friends and played together every day.
