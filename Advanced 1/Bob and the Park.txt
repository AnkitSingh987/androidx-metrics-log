Title: Bob and the Park

One day, Bob and his mommy went to the park. Bob noticed the swing and ran over to it. He saw that it was covered in velvet and noticed how soft it felt. "Mommy," said Bob, "I want to go on the swing." "Okay," his mommy said. "But be careful!" Bob got on the swing and started to swing back and forth. He felt like he was flying and had a big smile on his face. Suddenly, Bob noticed something bad. He had reached too high and the rope was about to break! Bob shouted, "Mommy! The rope is bad!" His mommy ran over quickly and she was able to catch the rope before it snapped. "Thank you, Mommy," said Bob. "You're welcome," she said. "Let's go and find another swing without a bad rope."

One day, a pale boy named Tim wanted to drive his toy car. He loved to play with it in his room. Tim's room was small but very fun. He had many toys and games to play with. But Tim's little sister, Sue, did not want him to drive the toy car. Sue wanted to play with her dolls in the room too. They both wanted to play, but there was not enough space for both toys. Tim and Sue thought hard about how they could both play in the room. They decided to take turns. Tim would drive his toy car for a little while, and then Sue would play with her dolls. This way, they could both have fun in the room.

One summer day, a gray bird flew outside. He wanted to soar and explore the world. He chirped to the other birds, "Let's go! We can soar together!" The other birds agreed, so they all took flight. They flapped their wings, flew up high, and soared through the bright sky. The bird was amazed. He felt so free and sang: "This is the best summer ever!" The other birds replied, "It sure is! Let's keep on soaring until the sun sets!" So they did - they flew and flew, until the sky turned from blue to orange. When the sun was about to go to sleep, the birds returned home. The bird went to bed feeling happy. That summer day of soaring is one he'll never forget.

Once there were two best friends named Elizabeth and Mark. Mark had been saving his pocket money for a long time to go on a safari. Elizabeth wanted to go with him on the safari to explore the world and she said she would ask her parents if she could join. Mark and Elizabeth were very excited, and Elizabeth's parents said yes! They all packed their bags and headed to the airport. On their way they could see lots of birds flying in the sky. When they got to the airport they read the signs and checked in. They were about to board the plane, when they noticed a nosy woman. She seemed to be asking lots of questions. Then Elizabeth and Mark saw the lady start to board the plane. The nosy lady had got on the same plane! Elizabeth and Mark were worried as the plane took to the skies. Then suddenly, the nosy lady spotted them, and reported them to the airline staff. Elizabeth and Mark were taken off the plane and their safari dreams were destroyed! Elizabeth and Mark never got to explore the world. They both felt sad and soon made their way home.

"Hey, what do you see?" asked dad. Lilly looked around. "I see birds," she said, with a shy smile. Dad smiled. "Do you like them? They seem curious." Lilly nodded. She liked the birds. She liked how they seemed to fly with their hearts, up high in the sky. "Let's keep walking," said dad. They walked down the street, taking in the sights. Lilly began to feel like something was strange. She couldn't seem to figure out why. "Dad, why does it feel like something is different?" she asked. Dad stopped walking and looked at her. "It's because your heart is curious," he said, smiling. Lilly smiled back. She knew Dad was right. She was so curious to see what the world had to offer.
