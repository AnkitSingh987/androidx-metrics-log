Title: The Ant Was Walking and the Leaf

One day, an elderly ant was walking in the grass. He was tired and wanted to rest. He saw a big leaf and thought it would be a nice place to lay down. So, the elderly ant lay on the leaf and closed his eyes. While the elderly ant was laying on the leaf, a little boy came to play in the grass. The little boy did not see the ant. He saw the big leaf and wanted to pick it up. The little boy picked up the leaf with the elderly ant on it. The elderly ant woke up and was scared. He wanted to go back to the ground. But the little boy did not know the elderly ant was on the leaf. The little boy ran with the leaf, and the elderly ant fell off. The elderly ant could not find his way back home and was lost forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house lived a wealthy family. They had a big fireplace. The fireplace made the house warm and cozy. One day, the mom and dad disagreed about what to put above the fireplace. Mom wanted a pretty picture. Dad wanted a big mirror. They did not know what to do. So, they asked their little girl, Lily. Lily thought and thought. Then, she had a good idea. She said, "Let's put up both!" And so they did. The pretty picture and the big mirror looked nice above the fireplace. The family was happy and they did not disagree anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a soft velvet cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was an obedient cat who liked to play outside. She loved to feel the sun shine on her fur. One day, Fluffy went out to play when the sun was shining bright. While playing, she saw a big ball in the sky. The ball was shining too, just like the sun. Fluffy was very surprised and wanted to find out what it was. As Fluffy got closer, she saw that the shining ball was not a ball at all. It was a big, shiny balloon! The balloon was stuck in a tree. Fluffy was so happy to find the shiny balloon. She jumped up and down and decided to take it home. From that day on, Fluffy and the shiny balloon were best friends.

One day, Danny went outside to play. He felt so excited as he began to zoom around the garden. Suddenly, two big men appeared. They had paint buckets and brushes in their hands. Danny was so scared that he felt helpless. "What are you doing?" Danny asked. The men couldn't answer. Instead, the just started pouring the paint all around the garden. Danny felt like crying. The men finished painting quickly. They zoomed away in a car and Danny was left feeling very helpless. Danny looked around at the mess the men left behind. All of his garden had been painted green. He was so sad. Danny tried to clean the mess, but it was impossible. His garden would never look the same again.

Mommy and Jimmy were playing in the park. They found a car that was lying on the ground. Jimmy wanted to take the car home. Mommy said, "No, Jimmy. That car is fragile. We can't take it home." Jimmy started to cry and said, "Please, Mommy, I want this car. I promise I'll take care of it." Mommy thought for a moment and said, "OK, Jimmy, but we have to tie it together so it won't break. You have to be extra careful." So Mommy and Jimmy tied the car together and they took it home. When they got home, they put the car in a safe place so it wouldn't break. Jimmy was so excited to play with his new car. But he kept his promise and he was really careful with it. He had a lot of fun playing with the car!

One day, a sweet frog saw a big tree. The frog wanted to climb the tree. He heard a bird whistle a song. The frog said, "Can you teach me to whistle like you?" The bird said, "Yes, but you must be careful. Whistling can be loud and bring trouble." The frog was happy and learned to whistle. The frog whistled all day long. He did not listen to the bird's warning. One day, while he was whistling, a big cat heard him. The cat wanted to eat the frog. The cat found the frog and said, "I heard your whistle. Now, I will eat you." The frog was scared and could not whistle anymore. The cat ate the sweet frog. The frog should have listened to the bird's warning.
