Title: Jim and Sally and the Garden

Once upon a time, there were two children; Jim and Sally. One day, Jim accidentally broke Sally's favourite toy. Jim was so sad and felt terrible. He did not know what to do. Sally said, "It's ok Jim. We can solve this problem together". Jim thought and thought, until he had an idea. He smiled and said, "Let's go outside at sunrise and I'll show you". At sunrise the next morning, Jim built a strong fort out of sticks and stones that he had found in the garden. He brought Sally's toy inside and said, "Now your toy is safe and sound". Sally was so happy and gave Jim a big hug. They both learned that working together you can solve problems, no matter how big or small. That made them both feel very strong!

Once upon a time in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, unusual hat that she loved to wear. One day, she found a small peanut under her hat. She was very happy and decided to plant it in her garden. Lily went to her garden and dug a small hole. She put the peanut in the hole and covered it with dirt. Every day, Lily would go to the garden and pray, "Please grow big and tall, little peanut." Her mom would watch her and smile. One sunny day, Lily went to the garden and saw a big, tall plant had grown from the peanut. She was very excited and said, "Wow, my peanut grew so big!" Her mom came over and said, "Yes, Lily, your prayers helped the peanut grow." They both laughed and enjoyed their unusual peanut plant together.

One day, Tim found a big toy called a microscope. It was a tool to see tiny things. Tim wanted to play with it, but he had to struggle. It was hard to use. Tim's friend, Sam, came over. Sam was flexible and could bend his body in many ways. Tim said, "Sam, can you help me use the microscope?" Sam said, "Yes, I can help you." Together, they played with the microscope. They looked at tiny things and had fun. Sam was a good friend to help Tim. They laughed and played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. One day, while he was playing, he saw a small bird. The bird looked confused. It did not know which way to go. Tim wanted to help the bird. He offered the bird some food. The bird ate the food and started to follow Tim. They walked a long way together. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to grow bigger and bigger. It was not a bird anymore. It was a big, friendly dog! Tim was very happy. Now, he had a new friend to play with every day.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to make a cake. She asked her mom, "Can I mix the powder to make a cake?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can. But you need to be patient." Mia started to mix the powder in a big bowl. She was very patient and mixed it slowly. Her mom helped her add some more things like milk and eggs. They mixed everything together until it was smooth. Next, they put the mix into a pan and put it in the oven. Mia asked her mom, "Is the cake ready now?" Her mom said, "Not yet, we need to be patient and wait." They waited and waited. When the cake was ready, they took it out of the oven. Mia and her mom were very happy with their cake. They shared it with their family and everyone loved it.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Tom. They liked to play and have fun. One day, Tim and Tom went on a trip to the park. They were very happy. At the park, they saw a girl named Sue. Sue was good at counting numbers. She counted one, two, three, four, and five. Tim and Tom watched her and felt a little jealous. They wanted to count numbers too. So, Tim and Tom asked Sue to teach them how to count numbers. Sue was happy to help them. They all sat down and Sue taught them to count one, two, three, four, and five. Soon, Tim and Tom could count numbers too. They were not jealous anymore. They all became good friends and had fun counting numbers together.
