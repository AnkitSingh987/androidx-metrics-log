Title: Tommy and the Apple

Tommy looked at the apple in his hand. He had picked it up off the ground. It was still shiny and bright. He held it up in the air and smiled. He said, "It looks harmless!" Then he dropped it. The apple fell slowly, slowly, until it was on the ground again. But it didn't stop there. It rolled down the hill until it disappeared. Tommy watched it go. He was sad the apple was gone. He wanted to pick it up and keep it. But he smiled, too. That apple was harmless, and it was nice to watch it go. He liked to think of the apple rolling away, forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna who lived in a magical kingdom. She was curious about life, so every day she asked her parents for a special request. One day, she asked for something incredible - a wishing star. Her parents smiled and said that it was impossible to find a wishing star in the kingdom. But Anna was determined and she kept asking. Finally, her parents agreed and said they would help her find one. Anna was so excited that she could hardly wait! The next day, the family went on a magical journey to search for the wishing star. After a long time, they arrived at a beautiful hilltop where they saw a shining star in the night sky. Anna's parents said that this was the wishing star. She had found it! Anna smiled and made a wish. Her wish was for a life full of incredible moments. Suddenly, the star shone even brighter and Anna knew she had made a wish that would come true.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play in the snow. They make snowballs and snowmen. They have fun. But one day, a big dog comes. The dog is wild. It barks and runs at them. Lila and Tom are scared. They do not know what to do. Lila has an idea. She says to Tom, "Freeze! Do not move!" She hopes the dog will think they are snowmen and go away. Tom listens to Lila. He freezes. He does not move. He does not blink. He does not breathe. The dog comes closer. It sniffs them. It licks them. It does not bite them. It thinks they are snowmen. It gets bored. It runs away. Lila and Tom are happy. They hug each other. They are brave. They are smart. They are safe. They go back to their land. They tell their mom and dad. They have hot chocolate. They have fun.

One day, a little crab was walking on the sand. He saw a big ball near the water. The crab thought, "I want to play with the ball." So, he used his legs to kick the ball. The ball went very far. The crab was happy! Then, a little girl came to the beach. She saw the crab and said, "Hello, crab! I want to play with you." The crab was scared at first, but then he thought the girl was nice. They played with the ball together. They kicked the ball back and forth. They had a lot of fun. After they played, the girl said, "I am hungry. I want to eat something delicious." The crab thought the girl would eat him! But, the girl took out a sandwich from her bag. She shared the sandwich with the crab. They ate together and laughed. The crab was happy that the girl was his new friend.

Once upon a time there was a playful puppy called Fido. Fido wanted to find some lumber to chew, so he chased after a truck to see where it was going. After a while of running, Fido saw a big pile of lumber and lay down. Fido was so happy, he rolled around in the lumber and even made a playful growl. Suddenly, Fido heard a voice and froze. It was a mean old dog, who was growling and barking at him. Fido wanted to run away, but lay still. The old dog soon realized that Fido was just playing, so it lay down too, and they shared the lumber. Fido was so happy and ran around, playing with their new found friend. They both lay in the lumber, enjoying the sunshine, until Fido had to go home.
