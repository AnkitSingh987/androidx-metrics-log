Title: Lily and the Flower

Lily liked to design things. She used paper, crayons, scissors and glue to make pictures, cards and toys. She liked to show her designs to her mom and dad, who always said they were very pretty and clever. One day, Lily wanted to design something new. She looked around the kitchen and saw a bowl of broccoli on the table. She thought broccoli was green and funny-looking, and she wondered what she could make with it. She took some broccoli and put it on a plate. She used a knife to cut it into small pieces. She arranged the pieces into a shape of a flower. She was very proud of her broccoli design. She ran to the living room, where her mom was reading a book. She said, "Mom, look what I made! It's a broccoli flower! Do you like it?" Her mom looked at the plate and smiled. She said, "Wow, Lily, that's a very creative design! You are very good at making things. But you know, broccoli is not only for designing. It's also for eating. It's very healthy and yummy. Do you want to try some?" Lily made a face. She did not like to eat broccoli. She thought it was bitter and yucky. She said, "No, thank you, mom. I don't want to eat my design. I want to keep it." Her mom said, "I understand, Lily. But you can't keep broccoli for too long. It will get bad and smelly. And we don't want to waste food, right? There are many children who don't have enough food to eat, and they would be happy to have some broccoli. How about this? You can take a picture of your design, and then you can eat some of it. And then you can share the rest with me and dad. How does that sound?" Lily thought for a moment. She did not want to lose her design, but she also did not want to be wasteful or selfish. She said, "OK, mom. I will take a picture of my broccoli flower. And then I will eat some of it. But only a little bit. And then you and dad can have the rest. But I might not like it." Her mom said, "That's very brave and kind of you, Lily. I'm sure you will like it more than you think. And I'm very proud of you for trying something new. Come on, let's go to the kitchen and take a picture of your design. And then we can have a broccoli snack together." Lily nodded. She took her mom's hand and walked to the kitchen. She was still a little sad to say goodbye to her design, but she was also curious to taste it. She hoped it would not be too bad. She smiled at her mom and said, "Thank you, mom. You are the best."

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a tiny bug. It was a mosquito. Tim wanted to introduce the mosquito to his friends. He put the mosquito in a jar and ran to find his friends. When he found his friends, he said, "Look, I have a new friend. This is a mosquito." His friends were scared. They did not like the mosquito. They said, "No, Tim! Mosquitoes can bite and make us itchy." Tim felt sad. He did not want his friends to be scared. He thought about what to do. He decided to let the mosquito go. He opened the jar and the mosquito flew away. His friends were happy again. They played in the park all day. Tim learned that some bugs are better to watch than to keep as friends.

Once upon a time, there were two children named Amy and Ben. They were good friends, and liked to play together. One day, Amy showed Ben her cap. It was shiny and new. Ben was jealous and wanted it for himself. So he grabbed the cap away from Amy. Amy was very angry and shouted at Ben. Ben's mother heard the shouting, so she came out to see what was happening. When she saw the cap on Ben's head, she knew he had taken it without asking. She punished Ben. She told him he should have asked Amy if he could borrow it. But Ben didn't listen. He knew his family was wealthy, so he thought he could have whatever he wanted. He refused to apologize to Amy and kept the cap. Amy was very sad. Even though she was poor, she had been very generous to Ben. But he had broken her trust and never said sorry. The end.
