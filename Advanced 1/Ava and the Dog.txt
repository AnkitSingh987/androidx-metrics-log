Title: Ava and the Dog

Once upon a time, a girl named Ava loved to listen. She listened to her mom, dad, and her little dog too. Ava also loved avocados. She ate them every day. They were yummy and made her feel good. One day, Ava found a clean avocado on the ground. It was shiny and green. She picked it up and took it home. Ava showed her mom the avocado. Her mom smiled and said they could eat it for dinner. At dinner time, Ava and her mom cut the avocado. But, surprise! Inside was a tiny note. The note said, "Thank you for being so kind. You get a wish!" Ava wished for a big, fun party. The avocado made her wish come true. Ava, her mom, dad, and little dog all had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved the color blue. She had a blue dress, blue shoes, and a blue room. One day, Sally got a new blue toy. She was very happy. Sally played with her new blue toy all day. She loved it so much, she did not want to share it. Her friends wanted to play with the blue toy too, but Sally said no. Sally's friends were sad. They did not believe that Sally was being nice. Sally's mom saw that Sally was not sharing. She told Sally, "You are being spoiled. You need to share your blue toy with your friends." Sally thought about it and knew her mom was right. Sally went to her friends and said, "I am sorry. I will share my blue toy with you." They all played together with the blue toy and had fun. Sally learned that sharing is good and that being spoiled is not nice.

Once upon a time, there was a kind and compassionate boy named Tim. Tim had a little sister named Sue. Tim loved his sister and always wanted to protect her. One day, Tim and Sue were playing in the park. They found a small needle on the ground. Tim knew that needles can hurt. He told Sue, "Don't touch the needle, Sue. It can hurt you." Sue listened to her big brother and did not touch the needle. Tim picked it up very carefully and threw it in the trash. Sue said, "Thank you, Tim, for protecting me." From that day on, Tim and Sue always looked out for each other. They knew that being compassionate and protecting each other was very important. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken. The chicken lived on a farm with many friends. Every day, the chicken played with its friends and had fun. One day, the chicken heard a loud roar from behind the big tree. The chicken felt scared and ran to its friends. The friends were also scared, but they wanted to know what made the roar. They were all very tired, but they decided to be brave and look behind the tree. When they looked behind the tree, they saw a big lion. But the lion was not scary or mean. The lion was just very tired and needed help. The chicken and its friends helped the lion find a nice place to sleep. The lion was so happy and thanked the chicken and its friends. From that day on, the lion and the chicken became best friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy man. He liked to sell things. He sold many things, like toys and food. He was very good at selling. The hairy man was a part of the town's history. One day, the hairy man found a big toy. It was a very nice toy. He wanted to sell it for a lot of money. So, he took the toy to the town square. Many people came to see the toy. The people did not have enough money to buy the toy. The hairy man was sad. He wanted to sell the toy so much. He took the toy and went home. The hairy man never sold the toy, and he was sad for the rest of his life.
