Title: Tom the Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He was a very obedient boy who always listened to his mom and dad. Tom loved to play outside with his friends, and one of his favorite games was to kick a ball. One sunny day, Tom was playing with his friends when he saw a big cobweb in the corner of the yard. "Look!" Tom said to his friends, "That's a big cobweb!" His friends gathered around to see the cobweb. It was shiny and beautiful, and they all thought it was very interesting. Tom wanted to show the cobweb to his mom, so he called her to come outside. "Mom, come see the cobweb!" he said. His mom came out and looked at the cobweb with Tom and his friends. "That's a lovely cobweb," she said. "Let's leave it alone so the spider can catch bugs." Tom and his friends agreed, and they went back to playing and kicking the ball.

Once upon a time, in a cute little town, there was a zoo. In this zoo, there were many animals. There were big animals, small animals, and even tiny animals. Everyone loved to visit the zoo and see all the animals. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They walked around and saw all the animals. Tim's favorite animal was the baby elephant. The baby elephant was very cute. Tim said, "Mom, look at the cute baby elephant!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, it's very cute!" While they were looking at the animals, Tim saw a sign that said, "Please do not disturb the animals." Tim asked his mom, "What does 'disturb' mean?" His mom explained, "It means to bother or make noise that can upset the animals." Tim nodded and said, "Okay, I won't disturb the animals." They continued walking around the zoo, being careful not to disturb any of the animals. Tim had a great day at the zoo and learned a new word. He couldn't wait to come back to the zoo again and see all the cute animals.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a small, cheap chair. He wanted to seat on it in his chamber. But his big sister, Sue, wanted the chair too. They both wanted the chair very much. Tim said, "I saw it first, so I should have it." Sue did not agree. She said, "I am bigger, so I need it more." They both were not happy. They did not know what to do. Then, their mom came in the room. She saw the cheap chair and the unhappy kids. She said, "Why don't we take turns? Tim can have it today and Sue can have it tomorrow." Tim and Sue liked the idea. They both smiled and took turns with the chair. Now, they were happy in their chamber.

Lily liked to play with her mom's violin. It was big and brown and had strings that made sounds. Lily wanted to make sounds like her mom, who played the violin very well. But Lily did not know how to play the violin. She only knew how to shake it. One day, when her mom was not home, Lily found the violin in the closet. She took it out and held it in her arms. She felt very fancy, like a princess. She started to shake the violin, hoping to hear music. But instead of music, she heard a loud crack. She looked at the violin and saw that it was broken. The strings were loose and the wood was split. Lily felt very scared and sad. She knew her mom would be angry and sad too. She tried to hide the violin under the bed, but it was too big. She heard her mom coming home and ran to the door. She wanted to say sorry, but she did not know how. She hoped her mom would not see the violin. But her mom saw it right away. She saw the broken violin and the closet door open. She knew what Lily had done. She was very angry and sad. She yelled at Lily and told her to go to her room. She did not hug her or kiss her. She did not say she loved her. Lily felt very bad. She wished she had never shaken the violin. She wished she had listened to her mom. She wished she could make her mom happy again. But she did not know how. She cried in her room and felt very alone. The end.
