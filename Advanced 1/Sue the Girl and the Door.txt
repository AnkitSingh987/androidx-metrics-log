Title: Sue the Girl and the Door

One day, a little girl named Sue found some clay. She was very happy. She started to think about what she could make with the clay. Sue wanted to make something fun and safe for her little brother, Tim. Sue made a little house with the clay. It had a small door and two windows. She showed the clay house to Tim. He was very excited. He thought it was a safe place for his toy animals to live. Sue and Tim played with the clay house all day. They were happy because they made something fun and safe together. They put all the toy animals in the clay house and had a great time. At the end of the day, Sue and Tim put the clay house in a safe place so they could play with it again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear and a little bear. They lived in a nice, warm cave. The big bear loved to work. He worked hard every day to find food for him and the little bear. The little bear was very patient. He waited for the big bear to come home. One day, the big bear worked very hard. He was very tired when he came home. The little bear saw that the big bear was tired. He wanted to help the big bear feel better. The little bear gave the big bear a big hug. The big bear felt happy and warm inside. From that day on, the big bear and the little bear worked together. They learned that hugs and working together made everything better. The moral of the story is to always help each other and give hugs to make others feel better.

Once upon a time, there was a town that wanted to build a big airport. The town asked Tom, an honest man, to help them. Tom was very good at building things. Tom and his friends worked hard to build the airport. They used big trucks and strong tools. The people in the town were happy to see the airport being built. They knew it would help them travel to far away places. When the airport was finished, the town had a big party. They thanked Tom for being honest and for helping them build the airport. Now, they could fly in big planes and go on adventures. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cute cat and a little dog. They were best friends. They played together, ate together, and slept together. But one day, they had a quarrel. The cat said, "I want to drink some wine." The dog said, "No, wine is not for us. It's for people." The cat did not like this. "I want it!" yelled the cat. The dog yelled back, "No!" They were very sad. They did not talk to each other. The next day, the sun was shining. The cat and the dog saw each other. The cat said, "I am sorry." The dog said, "I am sorry too." They hugged and became best friends again. They played in the sun and forgot about the wine. They knew that being friends was more important than a silly quarrel. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a hairy caterpillar called Bill. Every day Bill would crawl along looking for shelter. But no matter where he looked, he couldn't find anywhere to rest. One day, Bill was walking along and he heard a patch of grass whispering to him. "Come here, Bill!", it said. So Bill went up to the grass, and saw a big, hairy leaf. The leaf said, "You can stay here and keep warm. I'll protect you from the wind and rain". Bill was so thankful. Every night, the leaf provided shelter. He was so happy that he forgot he had been looking for shelter in the first place. But one day, Bill noticed the leaf was starting to rot. He was so sad, he didn't know what to do. But then he thought of a plan. He found some other hairy leaves and made a shelter out of them! So now Bill has plenty of shelter, and he's very happy. He never forgets how the old leaf saved him from the wind and rain.
