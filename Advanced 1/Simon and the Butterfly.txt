Title: Simon and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a naughty boy called Simon. One day, Simon was playing in the garden when he suddenly saw something. It was a big box with a screw on top. He was curious and wanted to see what was inside, but the screw was stuck. Simon ran to his dad and asked for help. His dad had a look at the box and said, "If I have a tool kit, I can open this." So Simon's dad went to the garage and brought a tool kit with a screwdriver. He started to unscrew the screw, and soon the top opened. The moment the box opened, a butterfly flew out. It was so beautiful! Simon wanted to touch it, but his dad said, "No, Simon. Don't be ignorant. We should release the butterfly but not touch it." So Simon released the butterfly and watched it fly away. It was a happy ending for the butterfly and a lesson for Simon.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to read. Every day, he would read a new book. One day, he read a book about a dizzy dog. The dizzy dog would spin around and around until he got very dizzy. Tim liked the story and wanted to see a dizzy dog in real life. The next day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They took their lunch with them. Tim had a sandwich, an apple, and a juice box. While they ate, Tim told his mom about the dizzy dog story. His mom smiled and said, "Maybe you'll see a dizzy dog one day." After lunch, Tim and his mom went to play. Tim saw a dog running around in the park. The dog was chasing its tail, spinning around and around. Tim remembered the dizzy dog from his book. He ran to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! It's a dizzy dog like in my book!" His mom laughed and said, "Yes, it is! Just like the story you read." Tim was so happy to see a dizzy dog in real life. He knew that reading books could make his dreams come true. From that day on, Tim read even more books, hoping to find more amazing things in the world around him.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to skip and play outside. One day, she saw a blue butterfly. The butterfly was so pretty that Lucy wanted to catch it. Lucy tried to catch the butterfly, but it was too fast. She asked her friend, Tom, for help. "Tom, can you please help me catch the blue butterfly?" Tom said, "Yes, let's try together!" They both skipped after the blue butterfly. It led them to a big tree. Under the tree, they found a box. Inside the box, there was an image of the blue butterfly. Lucy and Tom were so happy to find the image. They took it home to show their families. From that day on, they always played and skipped together.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside and gaze at the sky. One day, she saw a big, round balloon floating high in the sky. Lucy wanted to get the balloon, so she asked her mom for help. Her mom told her that the balloon was filled with a special air called oxygen. This air made the balloon float up high. Lucy was very brave and climbed a tall tree to reach the balloon. She grabbed it and brought it down. She was so happy to have the balloon and showed it to all her friends. They all played with the balloon and had a fun day together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big chest near a tree. Tim's mom said, "Tim, please pick up the sticks around the chest." Tim was an obedient boy, so he picked up the sticks. Tim's mom opened the chest, and inside were many toys. Tim was so happy! He said, "Mom, can I play with the toys?" His mom said, "Yes, you can play with one toy today." Tim picked a big, red ball from the chest. He played with the ball all day at the park. Tim had a great day with his mom and the red ball.
