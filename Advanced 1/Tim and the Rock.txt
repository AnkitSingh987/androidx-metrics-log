Title: Tim and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to collect pretty rocks. One day, he went to the park with his mom and his toy camera. He wanted to take pictures of his pretty rocks and show them to his friends. At the park, Tim saw a big rock that was shiny and colorful. He was so excited that he yelled, "Mom, look at this big rock! Can I take it home?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim, but first, take a picture with your camera." As Tim was about to take a picture, a little bird flew down and picked up the rock. Tim was frustrated and said, "Oh no, the bird took my rock!" The little bird flew up to a tree and dropped the rock. Then, something unexpected happened. The rock broke into many small rocks, and they were even prettier than before. Tim was so happy, and he said, "Thank you, little bird!" He took pictures of the small rocks with his camera and collected them all. Tim and his mom went home, and he showed his friends the beautiful rocks and the pictures he took. Everyone loved them, and they all had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a powerful dog named Max. Max had a big chest full of toys. He loved to play with his toys all day long. One day, Max's family had to leave the house to go to the store. They told Max to stay and guard the house. While Max was alone, a sneaky cat came into the house. The cat saw Max's big chest of toys and wanted to take them. Max tried to stop the cat, but the cat was too fast. It grabbed the toys and ran away. Max was very sad that he could not stop the cat. When his family came back from the store, they saw the empty chest and the sad dog. They were upset that the toys were gone. Max felt bad that he could not protect his toys. The family and Max were sad, and they missed the toys that were taken away.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to play a game. She asked her friend Tom to play with her. They both liked to play a crazy game called "Blink". In this game, they would look at each other and try not to blink. Sue and Tom started to play the game. They looked at each other, trying not to blink. Their eyes were wide open. They tried very hard not to blink. But then something unexpected happened. A little cat jumped up and made them both blink at the same time! Sue and Tom looked at the cat and laughed. The cat blinked too, and it looked like it was playing the game with them. They decided to let the cat join in their game. Now, Sue, Tom, and the cat played the crazy "Blink" game together, and they all had lots of fun.

One day, a dog named Max went to a big place called a stadium. In the stadium, there were many people and animals. Max was very happy to be there. He saw a cat named Tom who was not nice. Tom liked to call others "stupid". Max wanted to play with a ball, but Tom said, "You are too stupid to play with the ball." Max felt sad and asked his friend, a bird named Sam, "Am I stupid?" Sam said, "No, you are not. Tom just likes to say mean things." Max decided to lie down and think. He thought, "I am not stupid. I can play with the ball." So, Max stood up and played with the ball. Everyone in the stadium was happy to see Max play. Tom saw this and felt bad. In the end, Tom said sorry to Max and they played together. They all learned that calling others "stupid" is not nice. It is better to be kind and play together.

Mummy and Thomas went to the park. When they got there, Mummy pointed to the swing and said, "Let's go swing, Thomas!" Thomas ran over to the swing and got in. Mummy gave him a push, and soon Thomas was laughing and smiling as he flew higher and higher. Mummy smiled and watched as Thomas's laughter filled the air. After a few minutes, Mummy said, "Thomas, it's time to go home." Thomas nodded. He got out of the swing and began to walk away. But, then he saw the slide. "Mummy, can I go on the slide?" Mummy smiled and pointed her finger at the slide. "That's very clear, Thomas. Go ahead and have some fun!" Thomas ran over to the slide and got in. He went down the slide, and soon his laughter filled the air again. Mummy smiled and nodded her head in approval. When Thomas was finished, they went home. Thomas had a big smile on his face, knowing that he had a day of wonderful fun.
