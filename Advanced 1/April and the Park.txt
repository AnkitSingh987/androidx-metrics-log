Title: April and the Park

Once upon a time there was a little girl called April. She was 3 years old and very adventurous. One day, April saw a tour bus outside her house. She ran to her parents and said, "Look! Look at the tour, can I go?" Her parents said, "Yes! But only if you are very careful." So April grabbed her hat and ran to the tour bus. When she got in, the driver smiled and said, "Where to?" April said, "Let's go anywhere that looks good!" The driver smiled again and said, "The park looks good today". So the tourbus drove to the park. April was so excited and grabbed a map so she could find the best places to go. At the park, the driver pointed April towards the best spots. She played games, saw the animals and ate yummy ice cream. April had a great day, and before she knew it, it was time to go home. She grabbed her hat and thanked the driver for the good tour.

Ben and Mia like to play with their toys. They have many toys: cars, dolls, blocks, books, and more. But they do not like to organize their toys. They leave them on the floor, on the bed, on the table, everywhere. One day, Mom says, "Ben and Mia, you have to organize your toys. It is a mess in here. You can not find anything. And you can break something. Look, there is a glass on the floor. It is not a toy. It is tough and sharp. If you step on it, it will hurt you. Come on, let's organize your toys together." Ben and Mia do not want to organize their toys. They want to keep playing. They say, "No, Mom, we do not want to organize. We want to play. We do not care about the glass. We are tough. We will not step on it." Mom is not happy. She says, "Ben and Mia, you are not tough. You are silly. The glass can cut you. And you can not play with all your toys if you do not organize them. You will lose them or break them. Organizing is fun. You can sort them by color, by shape, by size, by type. You can make a game out of it. And then you can play with them better. Please, help me organize your toys." Ben and Mia think about it. They do not want to get hurt by the glass. They do not want to lose or break their toys. They want to play with them better. They decide to help Mom organize their toys. They say, "OK, Mom, we will help you organize. We will make a game out of it. We will sort them by color, by shape, by size, by type. And then we will play with them better." Mom is happy. She says, "Thank you, Ben and Mia. You are good kids. You are smart and helpful. I am proud of you. Now, let's organize your toys together. And then we can have some fun."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to solve problems. One day, she went for a walk in the woods. She saw a stream with water. The water looked very clean and delicious. Mia saw a frog who was sad. The frog told Mia that he was thirsty, but he could not drink the water from the stream. Mia wanted to help the frog. She thought about how to solve the problem. Mia found a big leaf and put it in the stream. The water filled the leaf, and the frog drank from it. The frog was happy, and the water was delicious. They both learned that helping others can make you feel good too.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lola who loved to nap. Every afternoon, when she felt tired, she found a spot in her garden to nap in the sunshine. Today, Lola decided to have a crazy nap. She ran around her garden, dancing and jumping around until she got so tired she could barely stand up! Then, she flopped down on the grass and fell asleep. When Lola woke up, she noticed that a big yellow butterfly had sat on her nose! Lola smiled and said hello to the butterfly, who had been watching her nap. For the rest of the afternoon, Lola and the butterfly chased each other around the garden while Lola took lots of crazy naps. They even shared a nap in the same spot together! Finally, when the sun went down, Lola said goodbye to the butterfly and went inside to eat her dinner. She had the most wonderful, crazy nap in her garden that day!
