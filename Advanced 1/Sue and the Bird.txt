Title: Sue and the Bird

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue had a big, red bow. She loved her bow very much. One day, she went to the park with her bow. At the park, she met a boy named Tom. Tom had a fake bow. He wanted Sue's real bow. Tom was not nice. He took Sue's bow and ran away. Sue was very sad and cried. Then, a kind bird saw Sue crying. The bird flew to Tom and spread its wings wide. Tom got scared and dropped the bow. The bird took the bow back to Sue. Sue was very happy and thanked the bird. Tom learned not to take things from others.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and run around in the sun. One day, her mom said it was time to prepare a bath for Lily. Lily was very excited because she loved bath time. Lily's mom filled the tub with warm water and lots of bubbles. She got Lily's bathrobe ready for her to wear after the bath. Lily jumped into the tub and started to play with her rubber duck and splash in the water. After a while, it was time for Lily to get out of the bath. Her mom wrapped her in the soft bathrobe and gave her a big hug. Lily felt warm and cozy in her bathrobe. She was so happy and ready for bed.

Once upon a time there was a baby, who was called Jake. Jake liked to explore and discover things around the garden. One day, Jake was walking around the garden, listening to the birds and looking at the flowers, when suddenly he saw something small and shiny. He took a closer look and was so excited to find a bug! "Wow," he shouted, bending down to take a closer look. The bug was gentle and stayed still as he looked. At that moment, Jake's dad came to find him. "What have you discovered?" his dad asked. "A bug!" replied Jake. His dad smiled and said, "That's great! Why not put it outside so it can fly away again?" Jake smiled and nodded, gently placing the bug in a safe spot in the garden. He looked at the bug for a few moments before turning back to his dad, ready for his next discovery.

Once upon a time, in a tidy room, there was a little pen. The pen had many friends who lived with it on a big table. One day, the pen went on a walk and met a cat. The cat said, "Hello, pen! Can I play with you?" The pen was happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played all day and had a lot of fun. But, the pen started to miss its friends on the table. The pen told the cat, "I need to return to my friends on the table. They might be sad without me." The cat said, "Okay, let's go back together!" But when they got back to the tidy room, they saw that all the pen's friends were gone! The pen was very sad, but then, the cat said, "Don't worry, pen! I will help you find your friends." They looked everywhere until they found the pen's friends hiding under the bed. They were playing a fun game of hide and seek! The pen was so happy to see its friends again, and they all played together with the cat.

Once upon a time there was a boy who wanted to learn about the world. He asked his mom for help, and she gave him a special book with many pages. The pages were filled with colors, animals and fun things. He could learn about different kinds of plants and all the countries. He had fun turning the pages and reading what was available. His mom told him that if he ever ran out of pages, she would give him more! He was so happy to have this book and thanked his mom for the wonderful gift. The boy now spends time every day learning from the pages and discovering new things. He is so excited to explore the world with his book and he never runs out of things to read.
