Title: Tom the Wise Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a wise cat named Tom. Tom loved to sit on the shelf and look out the window. He had many toys, but his favorite was a red ball. One day, Tom saw a big dog named Max outside. Max wanted to play with Tom's red ball. Tom did not want to share. Max was sad and sat down. Tom thought about it and wanted to finish the fight. He had an idea. Tom rolled the red ball to Max. Max was happy and played with the ball. Tom and Max became friends. They played with the ball every day. Tom learned that sharing was nice, and Max thought Tom was a wise cat.

Once upon a time two kids named Mandy and Jack were on the subway. They were heading to the park to play. But Mandy was behaving in a crazy way on the train. She was running around and yelling all over the place. Jack tried to stop her but Mandy was out of control. Finally he said, "Mandy, you have to behave or else we won't be able to go to the park." Mandy stopped and looked at Jack. She said, "But I don't want to behave, I want to keep running and yelling!" Jack smiled and said, "You can run and yell at the park, but you need to be nice and calm on the subway." Mandy agreed and promised to behave. They both got off the subway and went to the park. They had a great time playing and giggling but Mandy remembered to be good and behave.

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary girl named Lily. She liked to play in the park. One day, Lily saw a pretty angel in the park. "Hi, angel!" said Lily. "Do you want to play with me?" The angel smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Lily." Lily and the angel played catch. They took turns to throw the ball. They laughed and had so much fun. Lily loved her new friend, the angel. And they played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a family who had a big van. The family decided to take a long road trip in their van. One day, they stopped to take a break. When they looked outside, they saw a dog. The dog looked sad and was walking around in circles. The family wanted to help the dog so they opened their van and asked the dog, "Do you want to come with us?" The dog barked and hopped in the van. Everyone was happy but the family wasn't sure where it could take the dog. They decided to take it to a nearby animal shelter. When they arrived, the family was sad to see the dog end its journey with them. They hugged the dog good bye and waved as it went inside. The family was glad that they could help the dog. And they finished the long trip with happy hearts.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He was very enthusiastic because it was his favorite season, summer. He loved to play with his friends and run around in the warm sun. While Tim was playing, he saw a sneaky fox trying to steal his friend's ball. Tim shouted, "Hey! That's not yours! Give it back!" The fox looked scared and dropped the ball. Tim's friend was happy to get his ball back. After that, Tim and his friends continued playing and having fun. They thanked Tim for being brave and saving the ball. The sneaky fox learned not to steal, and even joined them to play. They all became friends and enjoyed the summer day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside, but one day it was very wet outside. Spot stayed inside and looked at the ceiling. He saw a big spider on the ceiling. Spot wanted to be friends with the spider. Spot invited the spider to play with him. The spider said yes, and they played all day. They played with a ball, and they played hide and seek. Spot was very happy to have a new friend. The spider was happy too. The next day, the sun came out, and it was not wet anymore. Spot and the spider went outside to play. They had lots of fun in the sun. Spot learned that even on wet days when he had to stay inside, he could still make new friends and have fun. And Spot and the spider were friends forever.
