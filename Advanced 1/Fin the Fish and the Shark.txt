Title: Fin the Fish and the Shark

One day, a little fish named Fin saw a big shark in the sea. The shark was sad. Fin swam to the shark and asked, "Why are you sad, Mr. Shark?" The shark said, "I am sad because I am dry. I need water on me to feel good." Fin had an idea. "I can use my tail to splash water on you, Mr. Shark!" So, Fin used his tail to splash water on the shark. The shark felt wet and happy. "Thank you, Fin!" said the shark. "Now I feel much better!" Fin and the shark became good friends. They played together in the water every day, and the shark was never dry again.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. Tom liked to help his mom and dad at home. One day, Tom's mom asked him to help her lift a big blackboard. The blackboard was very heavy, but Tom was strong, so he could lift it. Tom and his mom took the blackboard to the playroom. Tom's little sister, Lucy, was there. Lucy saw the blackboard and said, "Wow, Tom! You are so strong! Can you teach me how to be strong too?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, Lucy. I will show you how to be strong." Tom and Lucy played and exercised together. They lifted toys, jumped, and ran around the playroom. Soon, Lucy became strong too. Now, Tom and Lucy could help their mom and dad with many things. They were very happy to be strong and helpful.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big tree with a long vine hanging down. Tim wanted to swing on the vine like a monkey. As he swung on the vine, he heard a loud "CRACK!" The vine was about to break. Tim's friend, Amy, who was deaf, saw this and ran to help him. She signed, "Tim, be careful! The vine will break!" Tim jumped off the vine just in time. He hugged Amy and said, "Thank you for saving me!" They walked back home together, happy that they were safe. And from that day on, Tim learned to be more careful when playing in the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy lady. She had a big house and many lemons. She liked to share her lemons with her friends. One day, she had a great idea. She wanted to mail a lemon to her friend, Tom. The wealthy lady put the lemon in a box. She went to the mail place to send the box to Tom. The mailman said, "Hello, what do you have in the box?" The wealthy lady said, "I am sending a lemon to my friend, Tom. He loves lemons too!" The box traveled far away. It went on a truck, a plane, and another truck. Finally, the box arrived at Tom's house. Tom was so happy to see the box. He opened it and found the lemon inside. He said, "Wow! My friend sent me a lemon! I will make lemonade and think of her when I drink it." The wealthy lady and Tom were very happy. They knew that sharing lemons made their friendship strong. And they lived happily ever after, always sharing and caring for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to play with his bone. One day, Bobo met a creative cat named Lulu. Lulu liked to paint and draw. They became good friends. Bobo said, "Lulu, can you help me finish my bone? I want it to look pretty." Lulu agreed and started to paint the bone with many colors. They were both very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The bone started to wiggle and jump. "Oh no!" said Bobo. "What is happening?" Lulu was surprised too. The bone turned into a magic bone! It could talk and dance. Bobo and Lulu laughed and played with the magic bone all day long.

The little bird loved to soar above the trees. One day, he spotted a lime in the grass and flew down to get it. As he pecked away at the lime, he noticed it tasted different than the normal ones he had eaten before. He started to feel a bit strange, and suddenly flew off! The little bird was soaring higher and higher. He looked down, and below him he could see all the trees. He felt a burst of energy inside him, and he flew even higher. He could see the sun, and the clouds. All he wanted to do was to keep flying. But the little bird became too tired, so he decided to head back to the ground. Suddenly, he heard someone call his name. He looked down and saw a little girl waving at him. She said, “Come down, little bird. You can’t keep flying forever.” The little bird followed the girl’s advice, and slowly circled back towards the ground. When he landed, the little girl smiled and gave him a big hug. She said, “You are so brave, little bird. Now you know why it is important to be normal.”
