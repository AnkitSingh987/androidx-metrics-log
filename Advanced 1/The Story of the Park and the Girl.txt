Title: The Story of the Park and the Girl

Once there was a little girl who was very honest. She loved to pack her things and get ready to go. She loved to pack her crayons and nothing else. One day she decided to pack her crayons in a special way. She put each color in its own bag and made sure everything was neat. When she was done she said "Now I'm ready for anything!" The girl went out and found lots of things to do. She went to the park, played with her friends and saw lots of new things. But no matter what she did she always remembered to be honest. When it was time to go home the girl packed her crayons again and said goodbye to her friends. She happily walked home, feeling good about being honest. The girl quickly fell asleep with a smile on her face, dreaming about exploring more with her crayons.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a very important job. He had to print a picture for his mom. Tim's mom loved onions. So, Tim wanted to print a big onion for her. One day, Tim found a pretty onion in the kitchen. He took the onion and put it on the table. He looked at the onion and tried to remember how it looked. He wanted to make the best onion picture for his mom. Tim went to his room and started to print the onion picture. He worked very hard. When he was done, he showed the picture to his mom. She was very happy and hugged Tim. Tim felt proud that he did the important job for his mom. The onion picture was now on the fridge, and everyone could see it.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a little seed. The little seed wanted to grow big and tall. One day, a sweet bird came by and dropped a shiny jewel right next to the seed. The little seed was so happy to have a new friend. The jewel sparkled in the sun and made the seed feel warm. The seed started to grow. It grew a little bit each day, reaching up towards the sky. As the seed grew, the sweet bird would visit and sing to it. The seed and the jewel became the best of friends. They were happy together in the small garden, and the seed grew into a beautiful, tall tree.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. They did lots of things together, like playing with toy cars and running around the park. One day, they found a secret while they were playing. The secret was a box with a strange lock. They both wondered what was inside the box. The friends started to encourage each other to figure out the secret. Every day, they would try to crack the strange lock. Time passed but the box remained a secret. Then, one day, the friends figured out a way to open the box. They couldn't believe their eyes when they opened the box. Inside was a very normal surprise for them both. They had lots of fun with the surprise! The friends found out that with encouragement, you can turn a secret into normal fun.

One day, a girl named Mia put on her pretty skirt. She thought it looked very nice on her. Mia wanted to show her friends her attractive skirt. So, she went outside to play with them. While playing, a big wind came. It blew Mia's skirt up high into the air. Mia and her friends looked up and saw something unexpected. A bird was holding the skirt in its beak! The bird thought the skirt was a pretty thing to make its nest with. Mia and her friends came up with a plan. They found some shiny things to trade with the bird. They gave the bird the shiny things, and the bird gave Mia her skirt back. Mia was happy to have her skirt again, and the bird was happy with its new shiny things for its nest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily and a little boy named Tom. They were best friends. They liked to play games together. One day, they found a new game in the park. They were very happy. Lily said, "Let's share this game, Tom." But Tom was feeling rude. He said, "No, I want to play alone." Lily was sad. She did not like it when Tom was rude. But then, Tom saw that Lily was sad. He did not want to make his friend feel bad. So he said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Let's share the game and play together." They played the game and had lots of fun. They learned that sharing is better than being rude.
