Title: Mr the Judge and the Bear

One day, a big judge named Mr. Bear went for a walk. He liked to march in the park. He marched up and down the path. His big feet went stomp, stomp, stomp. As he marched, he saw a little girl named Lily. She had a big box. Lily said, "Hi, Mr. Bear! I have something delicious for you!" Mr. Bear was happy and smiled big. Mr. Bear opened the box. Inside were yummy cookies. He took a bite and said, "Thank you, Lily! These are very delicious!" They both laughed and marched together, eating cookies in the park.

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a little bunny. The bunny loved to hop and play all day. The forest was big and open, with lots of room for the bunny to explore. One day, while the bunny was hopping, it found a big tree with a hole. Inside the hole, there was a small bird. The bird was trying to sleep, but the bunny's hopping disturbed it. The bird flew out of the hole and said, "Please, do not disturb me. I am trying to sleep." The bunny felt bad and said, "I am sorry, bird. I will not disturb you again." The bird went back into the hole to sleep, and the bunny continued to play in the open forest. From that day on, the bunny and the bird became good friends, and the bunny always made sure not to disturb the bird when it was sleeping.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. They all liked to play in the sand. Tim liked to stand in the sand and feel it between his toes. He also liked to build big sand castles with his mom and dad. One day, while Tim was playing in the sand, he saw something shiny. He dug and dug until he found a pretty box. Tim was scared to open it, but his mom and dad said it was okay. They all opened the box together and found a big red ball inside. Tim and his mom and dad played with the red ball all day. They were so happy they found the pretty box in the sand. But then, the ball started to talk! It said, "Thank you for finding me. I was lost and scared too!" Tim, his mom, and his dad were surprised, but they all laughed and played with their new talking friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Sam. Sam loved to serve food to his animal friends. One day, he put on his silly hat and went to the park. At the park, Sam saw a little cat named Lily. She was hungry. Sam wanted to serve her some food. He gave her a big plate of fish. Lily was happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew Sam's silly hat away! Sam and Lily ran after the hat. They saw a big bird wearing the hat! The bird looked funny. Sam, Lily, and the bird all laughed. The bird gave Sam his hat back, and they all became friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw an elderly man with a net. The man was trying to open the net to catch some butterflies. Tim thought the man looked sad, so he wanted to help him. The elderly man told Tim that he had a hard time opening the net. Tim said, "Don't worry, I can help you!" Together, they tried to open the net. They pulled and pulled, but the net was still closed. Tim knew they had to find another way to open it. Then, Tim had an idea. He remembered that his mom had a key at home that could open anything. He ran home and brought the key back to the park. They used the key to open the net, and the elderly man was so happy. Tim and the man caught many butterflies together, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very organized. She had a plan for everything she did and it made her very happy. One day the little girl was out for a walk and she came across a train station. As she walked in to the station, she noticed a little boy who was very sad. He had been crying for some time and she could tell that he had been there for awhile. The little girl decided to help the little boy and she gave him a big hug and kissed him on the cheek. When he saw that she was so kind and organized, he became very happy. He told her that he had been at the station for a long time, but he had no idea what to do next or how to get where he wanted to go. The little girl gave him some tips, which she had learned through being organized, and soon the little boy was feeling much better. He thanked her for her help and gave her a big kiss on the cheek. The moral of this story is that if you are organized and kind to others, you can make a difference in their lives. Being organized gives you the tools and knowledge to help those around you and make their lives better.
