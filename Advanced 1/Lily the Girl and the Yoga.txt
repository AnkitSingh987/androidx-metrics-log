Title: Lily the Girl and the Yoga

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to do yoga every day. One day, she saw a little, scared dog near her home. The dog had a hurt leg, and it looked sad. Lily wanted to help the dog feel better. She thought, "Maybe yoga can heal the dog's leg." So, she took the dog to her room and started to do yoga with it. At first, the dog was scared, but soon it started to copy Lily's moves. After a few days, the dog's leg began to heal. It was not scared anymore. The dog was happy and so was Lily. They became best friends and did yoga together every day. And that is how Lily and the dog found happiness in their small town.

Sara loved to play princess. She had a beautiful dress, a shiny crown and a big mirror. She liked to look at herself and say, "I am the most beautiful princess in the world!" One day, her mom told her, "Sara, you have to behave. You have to share your toys with your brother and help me with the dishes. You are not a real princess, you know." Sara did not like that. She said, "No, mom, I am a real princess and I can do what I want. I don't want to share or help. I want to play princess all day!" Her mom was angry. She said, "Sara, if you don't behave, I will take away your dress, your crown and your mirror. You will have nothing to play with. Is that what you want?" Sara did not listen. She said, "You can't do that, mom. You are not the boss of me. I am the boss of you. I am the princess and you are the servant. You have to do what I say!" Her mom was very angry. She said, "That's it, Sara. You have crossed the line. You are a very naughty girl and you need a time out. Come with me, now!" She grabbed Sara by the arm and took her to her room. She took away her dress, her crown and her mirror. She said, "You will stay here until you learn to behave. You will have no toys, no books, no TV, no snacks. You will have nothing but your bed and your pillow. Maybe then you will realize that you are not a princess, but a spoiled brat." She closed the door and left Sara alone. Sara cried and cried. She wished she had behaved. She wished she had shared and helped. She wished she had been a good girl. But it was too late. She had lost everything. She was not a princess. She was a very sad and lonely girl.

Once upon a time, there was a little skeleton who lived in a big, dark house. The skeleton was lonely and wanted a friend. He looked outside and saw a helpless little bird that was lost. The skeleton wanted to help the bird, so he opened the door and let the bird in. The bird was scared at first, but the skeleton was kind and gave the bird food and a warm place to sleep. The bird began to trust the skeleton and they became good friends. The skeleton was happy to have a friend and the bird was no longer helpless. One day, the skeleton decided to change his big, dark house into a bright and colorful home for him and his bird friend. They painted the walls with happy colors and filled the rooms with fun things to play with. The bird and the skeleton lived happily together in their new, bright home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was three years old, and she loved to play outside with her friends. One day, something special happened. The little girl noticed a witch in the sky. The little girl was so excited. She ran to her mom and said, “Mommy, did you see that? A witch in the sky!” Her mom smiled and said, “Yes, I saw it.” The little girl asked her mom, “What does it mean?” Her mom said, “It means the witch is coming to visit us. She's a spoiled witch, so we need to be good and polite.” The little girl nodded. Then her mom said, “Let's get ready for the witch's visit. Decorate the house, make snacks, and wear our nicest clothes!” The little girl and her mom quickly made the preparations and just in time, the witch arrived. Everyone was so excited when they saw her. The witch was greeted with a warm welcome, and the visit was a successful one. The little girl was so happy the witch had come to visit. After the witch had left, she told her mom, “I can't wait until the witch visits us again!” Her mom smiled and said, “Me too!”
