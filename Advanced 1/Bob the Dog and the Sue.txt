Title: Bob the Dog and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bob. Bob was good at solving problems. One day, Bob went to a big place called the university. He was a little frightened, but he wanted to see what was inside. When Bob got to the university, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was very sad. She said, "Bob, I can't find my toy. Can you help me solve this problem?" Bob wanted to help, so he started looking for Sue's toy. They looked high and low, but they could not find the toy. Bob and Sue were both frightened. The sky became dark, and it started to rain. They were wet and cold. In the end, they did not find Sue's toy, and they went home feeling sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big itch on his arm. He did not like the itch. It made him feel frustrated. One day, Tim went to his friend, Sally. Sally saw that Tim was sad. "Why are you sad, Tim?" asked Sally. Tim said, "I have a big itch on my arm, and it makes me feel frustrated." Sally wanted to help Tim. She had an idea. "Let's destroy the itch!" Sally said. Tim and Sally thought of ways to destroy the itch. They tried to wash it off with water. They tried to cover it with a bandage. But the itch did not go away. Finally, Tim's mom saw them trying to destroy the itch. She gave Tim some cream to put on the itch. The cream made the itch go away. Tim was happy, and he thanked his mom and Sally. Now, Tim and Sally could play without the itch bothering Tim anymore.

Jim was a competitive boy. He loved to try and beat his friends in everything he did. All his friends had come over to play a game. Everyone was ready to start, when suddenly Jim asked for a whistle. Everyone looked around, and wondered why Jim wanted a whistle. One of the friends brought out a folder and gave it to Jim. Jim opened the folder and was surprised to find a whistle inside. He quickly blew the whistle and everyone looked at him, wondering what the noise was for. Jim proudly announced that the whistle was his secret weapon to help him win the game. Everyone laughed and agreed to let Jim use the whistle. Jim ended up winning the game, whistling every time he wanted to boast about his moves and it helped everyone keep track. In the end, everyone had a great time and Jim showed them just how competitive as he could be. The next time they came together to play, everyone remembered the wonderful game that Jim had won with his trusty whistle.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She liked to sort her toys by color. One day, her mom told her that they had a new neighbor. Lucy was very excited to meet her new friend. The new neighbor was a boy named Tim. He had a big box of toys. He told Lucy that he had a lot of fake toys in the box. Lucy wanted to play with the fake toys, but Tim said he had to go home soon. Lucy asked if she could borrow the toys and sort them. Tim said yes, but only if she promised to bring them back the next day. Lucy took the fake toys home and sorted them by color all day. She was so happy. The next day, she went to Tim's house to give the toys back. But when she got there, Tim's house was empty. Tim and his family had moved away. Lucy was very sad because she didn't get to say goodbye to her new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked in the peaceful park, where the sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim liked to look at the pretty flowers and the soft, green grass. As they walked, Tim saw a big, red truck. He pointed at it and said, "Look, Mom! An ambulance!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. That is an ambulance. It helps people who are hurt." Tim clapped his hands and jumped up and down. He liked the ambulance. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim waved goodbye to the ambulance and the peaceful park. He held his mom's hand as they walked back to their house. Tim was happy he got to see the ambulance and spend time with his mom.
