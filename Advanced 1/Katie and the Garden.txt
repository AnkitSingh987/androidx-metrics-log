Title: Katie and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Katie. She was only three years old and lived in a lovely house with her family. One day, Katie was in the garden with her dad. She saw a big, beautiful tent and asked her dad what it was. Her dad smiled and explained that it was a tent for sleepovers and fun. Katie was so excited! She kept asking her dad lots of questions about the tent. Her dad explained that it was made from special, waterproof fabric that kept the rain out. Her dad said, "Let's have a sleepover in the tent one night, Katie. That will be so much fun!" Katie jumped up and down in excitement. "Yes, please!" she shouted. She couldn't wait to camp in the lovely, big tent.

Lila and Tom were very happy. Today was the festival day. They put on their best clothes and went with Mom and Dad to the park. There were many people, colors, and sounds. Lila and Tom saw balloons, clowns, games, and candy. "Look, Tom, a big cake!" Lila said, pointing to a table. The cake was white and pink, with candles and flowers. "Can we have some?" "Maybe later, Lila. First, let's see the other things," Tom said. He wanted to play with the balls and the cars. They walked around the park, holding Mom and Dad's hands. They saw a puppet show, a magic trick, and a band. They laughed and clapped. They had fun. But then, the sky became gray. The wind blew hard. The people looked up and saw dark clouds. It was going to rain. "Oh no, the festival will be ruined!" Lila said, feeling sad. "Don't worry, Lila. We can still enjoy the cake," Tom said, trying to cheer her up. They ran to the table, where a nice lady was cutting the cake. She smiled and gave them each a big slice. "Thank you, lady!" Lila and Tom said. They ate the cake, which was sweet and soft. They licked their fingers and smiled. They liked the cake. The rain started to fall. It was cold and wet. The people ran to find shelter. The balloons, clowns, games, and candy were gone. But Lila and Tom did not mind. They had each other, and they had the cake. They spread their arms and hugged. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to play outside. One day, he was playing in the backyard when he heard his parents mention something about "skin". He was curious, so he asked what it meant. His mom said, "Skin is the soft covering that is on you and everyone else. It comes in different colors and textures." The little boy was so excited and started feeling all around his arms and saying, "My skin is so soft and smooth!" His parents smiled and then his dad said, "I think we should get you a new shirt. The one you are wearing looks a bit cheap." The little boy excitedly ran to his room to find the perfect shirt to show off his new understanding of skin. And he lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat grapes. Every day, he would ask his mom to buy him grapes from the market. One day, Tim's mom was very busy, so she asked Tim to order the grapes from the store. She gave him a phone to call the store. Tim was very happy to order his grapes. He called the store and said, "I want grapes, please!" When the grapes came, Tim was so excited. But when he opened the bag, he saw something disgusting. There were bugs in the grapes! Tim didn't want to eat the grapes with bugs. He felt sad. Tim's mom saw him and knew something was wrong. She looked at the grapes and saw the bugs, too. She called the store and told them about the problem. The store said they were sorry and sent new grapes to Tim. The new grapes were clean and bug-free. Tim was happy again. He ate his grapes and thanked his mom for helping him. And they lived happily ever after.
