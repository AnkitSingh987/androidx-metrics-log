Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bear

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a shiny crystal. She picked it up and put it in her pocket. Lucy went for a walk in the forest. The trees were very high. She saw a big, sleepy bear. The bear let out a big yawn. Lucy had an idea. She took the shiny crystal out of her pocket and showed it to the bear. The bear looked at the crystal and started to dance! Lucy and the bear danced together in the forest, having lots of fun. After a while, the bear yawned again and stopped dancing. Lucy put the crystal back in her pocket. She said goodbye to her new friend and went back home. When she got home, she looked at the crystal and smiled. She had a fun day with the bear and her special crystal.

Once upon a time, there was a little player named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. He would throw it, kick it, and bounce it all day long. One day, while playing with his ball, Tim saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. He thought it would be fun to drop the ball from the tree and see how high it would bounce. Tim climbed the tree, holding his ball tightly. When he reached the top, he dropped the ball. But something unexpected happened! Instead of bouncing, the ball got stuck in a hole in the tree. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. A wise old owl, who lived in the tree, saw Tim's problem. The owl told Tim to think before he acted and to be careful when playing. Tim listened to the owl, and with the owl's help, they got the ball out of the hole. Tim learned his lesson and never dropped his ball from high places again. From that day on, Tim played more carefully and always remembered the wise words of the owl.

The sun was shining and it was a beautiful day. Daisy and her best friend, Jack, were playing together in the park. Daisy and Jack laughed and played games all day, as if they had all the time in the world. Whenever they tired of one game they would find something new and silly to do. Daisy had the most wonderful ideas and Jack liked to resist at first, but eventually gave in when he had too much fun and could not help himself. Little did Daisy and Jack know, that this day would be the last day ever that they would get to play together. Even though Daisy and Jack wanted the day to never end, of course it did and soon enough it was time for them to go home and go to sleep. That night, Daisy and Jack had the same dream. In their dream, a voice told them that a great storm was coming, and that storm would cause their friendship to come to an end. The next morning, Daisy and Jack woke up to find out that the storm was true! Daisy and Jack's parents had decided that Daisy had to move away and they could no longer be best friends. Daisy and Jack hugged each other goodbye, knowing that they had definitely made the most of the last silly day they got to spend together.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk with her mom. She wore her favorite hat. It was a big, red hat that made her feel happy. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Sue and her mom walked to the park to have a fun day. At the park, Sue saw a big ice cream truck. The ice cream looked delicious. Sue's mom bought her a big ice cream cone with three scoops. Sue was so happy, she could scream! She ate her ice cream and it was very yummy. While Sue was eating her ice cream, a big gust of wind came. It blew Sue's hat off her head and into a tree. Sue was sad, but her mom said not to worry. They found a long stick and got the hat back. Sue put her hat on and they continued their fun day at the park.
