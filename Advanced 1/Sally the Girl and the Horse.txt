Title: Sally the Girl and the Horse

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally had a white toy horse. She loved her horse very much. One day, while playing with her horse, she saw her friend Timmy. Timmy was sad. He lost his toy whip. Sally wanted to help Timmy. She said, "I can lend you my toy whip." Timmy smiled and said, "Thank you, Sally!" They played together all day. Sally's white horse and Timmy's whip made them very happy. At the end of the day, Timmy gave Sally her whip back. From that day on, Sally and Timmy were best friends. They learned that sharing and helping each other made them both happy.

Timmy was a little boy who liked to play with his toys. He had a big truck, a soft bear, and a yellow ball. He also had a special pillow that he slept with every night. His pillow was blue and had stars on it. He called it his star pillow. One day, Timmy went to visit his grandma. She was elderly and lived in a small house. She was very kind and gave Timmy cookies and milk. She also had a cat named Fluffy, who liked to nap on the couch. Timmy wanted to play with Fluffy, but Fluffy did not want to play. He hissed and scratched at Timmy. "Fluffy, be nice!" Grandma said. "He is just a little boy. He wants to be your friend." But Fluffy did not listen. He ran away and hid under the bed. Timmy felt sad. He wanted to have fun with Fluffy. He decided to look for him. He went to the bedroom and saw Fluffy's tail sticking out from under the bed. He crawled under the bed and tried to grab Fluffy's tail. "Meow!" Fluffy said. He was angry and scared. He bit Timmy's hand and ran out of the room. "Ow!" Timmy said. He cried and ran to Grandma. He showed her his hand. It had a red mark on it. "Timmy, what happened?" Grandma asked. She saw Fluffy run past her. She knew he was the culprit. She hugged Timmy and kissed his hand. "I'm sorry, Fluffy is not a nice cat. He does not like strangers. He needs to learn to share and be gentle." She took Timmy to the bathroom and washed his hand. She put a band-aid on it and gave him a lollipop. She also gave him his star pillow. She said he could keep it with him until he felt better. "Thank you, Grandma," Timmy said. He smiled and hugged her. He felt happy again. He liked his star pillow. It made him feel safe and cozy. He decided to forget about Fluffy and play with his toys instead. He hoped that one day, Fluffy would turn into a friendly cat. But for now, he was happy with his Grandma and his star pillow.

One day, a restless cat named Tom wanted to organize a play day for his friends. He chose Tuesday to have the play day because it was a sunny day. Tom was excited and went to tell his best friend, a dog named Max. "Max, let's have a play day on Tuesday!" said Tom. Max wagged his tail and agreed, "Yes, Tom! I love play days!" On Tuesday, Tom and Max met at the park. They played catch, ran around, and had fun with their friends. All the animals in the park were happy and enjoyed the sunny day. At the end of the day, Tom and Max were tired but very happy. They knew they would have more play days in the future.

Once upon a time, there was a very reliable pilot named Tom. He loved to fly his big airplane up in the sky. He was very good at it and everyone liked him. One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to see the big airplane. She asked her mom, "Can I see the airplane?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can go see the airplane and meet the pilot." Lucy was very happy. When they got to the airplane, Lucy saw Tom, the pilot. She said, "Hi, I am Lucy!" Tom said, "Hi Lucy! Do you want to see the airplane?" Lucy insisted, "Yes, please!" Tom showed Lucy the big airplane and she was very excited. Lucy and her mom went home and told everyone about the reliable pilot and his big airplane.
