Title: Big the Mighty Hero and the Stick

Once upon a time, in a land full of fun, there was a mighty hero named Big Ted. Big Ted was a big, happy bear who loved to help his friends. He lived in a cozy little house in the woods. One day, Big Ted found a magic weapon, a big stick. This stick could increase the size of any toy he touched. Big Ted thought this was so cool, and he wanted to share it with his friends. Big Ted went to his friend, Little Bunny. He touched Little Bunny's tiny ball with the magic stick, and it grew bigger and bigger. Little Bunny was so happy to play with the big ball. All the animals in the woods came to play with the big toys, and they all had a great time. And so, Big Ted became an even mightier hero in the land of fun, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion had a birthday. He wanted a brilliant party with all his friends. The lion was very happy. On the day of the party, the lion heard a loud noise. It was not a happy noise. The lion went to see what it was. He saw a little mouse. The mouse was stuck under a big rock. The lion wanted to help. The lion used his big roar to move the rock. The mouse was free! The mouse said, "Thank you!" The lion and the mouse went to the birthday party. Everyone was happy. The lion and the mouse became best friends. The lion's birthday was brilliant.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. The park has many trees and flowers. The trees and flowers are green. Green is Sara's favorite color. One day, Sara and Tom find a big scissors in the park. The scissors can cut things. Sara wants to cut a flower. She thinks the flower is pretty. She wants to take it home. "Look, Tom, a flower!" Sara says. She picks up the scissors and runs to the flower. "Wait, Sara, don't cut the flower!" Tom says. He runs after Sara. He knows cutting the flower is bad. The flower is part of nature. Nature is alive and needs care. But Sara does not listen. She cuts the flower with the scissors. She smiles and holds the flower. She thinks she is happy. But then, she sees the flower is sad. The flower is not green anymore. The flower is brown and dry. The flower is not alive anymore. The flower is dead. Sara feels sorry. She does not want to hurt the flower. She does not want to hurt nature. She cries and says sorry to the flower. Tom hugs Sara and says, "It's okay, Sara. You did not know. But now you know. Cutting the flower is not good. We should love nature and not hurt it. Nature is our friend." Sara nods and says, "You are right, Tom. I am sorry, flower. I am sorry, nature. I will not cut you again. I will love you and care for you. You are green and beautiful." Sara and Tom put the flower back in the ground. They hope the flower will be happy again. They throw away the scissors. They do not want to cut anything again. They play in the park and enjoy nature. They are happy and green.

One day, a little duck named Ducky saw a big puddle. The puddle was different from the other puddles he knew. It was very big and shiny. Ducky wanted to play in it, but he was scared. He asked his friend, Bunny, to come with him. "Will you follow me to the big puddle, Bunny?" asked Ducky. Bunny said, "Yes, I will follow you, Ducky. Let's go!" So, Ducky and Bunny went to the big puddle together. When they got to the puddle, they saw a big frog. The frog said, "This is my puddle. You can't play here." Ducky and Bunny were sad. They wanted to play in the puddle too. Ducky said, "Can we all play together?" The frog thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, we can all play together!" Ducky, Bunny, and the frog played in the big, different puddle all day. They had so much fun splashing and jumping. They all became good friends and shared the puddle happily.
