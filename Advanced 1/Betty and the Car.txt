Title: Betty and the Car

Betty was a poor, but gentle girl. Her parents had to work all day, so she often played alone. One day, she noticed a pile of junk in the street corner. She wanted to look closer, to see if there were any interesting things. Betty walked over to the pile of junk and shortly she was playing with an old toy car she had found. However, her delight, was interrupted when she heard a voice. "Why are you playing with this junk?" the voice asked. Betty was scared and she turned around, to see an old man walking toward her. "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to. I was just curious," Betty exclaimed, embarrassed. The old man ignored her explanation and he said "Sometimes people have to play with junk, when they have nothing else." Betty understood his meaning and she felt ashamed. She walked away. The moral of this story is that it is important to remember that not everyone is as privileged as you and you should not ignore or undermine their circumstances. Even something as simple as playing with junk can be seen as an offence if you don't understand.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. They were happy and tired. They saw the sun go down in the sky. It was orange and red and yellow. It was pretty. "Look, Ben, the sunset!" Lily said. "It is so attractive!" Ben nodded. He liked the sunset too. But he saw something else. He saw a fire in the trash can. It was big and bright and hot. It was burning. "Look, Lily, the fire!" Ben said. "It is so scary!" Lily turned and saw the fire. She was afraid. She did not like the fire. It was dangerous. "Let's go, Ben!" Lily said. "We need to tell someone!" They ran to the bench where their mom was sitting. They told her about the fire. She was worried. She called the firemen on her phone. She told them to come fast. The firemen came with their truck. They had hoses and helmets and coats. They sprayed water on the fire. They put it out. They were brave and strong. Lily and Ben thanked the firemen. They were glad they were safe. They hugged their mom. She was proud of them. They went home. They saw the stars in the sky. They were shiny and calm. They were happy.

One day, a big cactus named Carl lived in a hot place. He had many friends who were also cacti. They all liked to play together. Carl liked to remember the fun times they had. One day, a little bird named Betty flew by. She saw the cacti and wanted to play too. But when she tried to land on Carl, she got hurt by his sharp parts. "Ouch!" cried Betty. "Why did you hurt me?" Carl felt sad. He didn't want to hurt Betty. "I'm sorry," he said. "I didn't mean to hurt you. My sharp parts keep me safe." Betty was sad too. She wanted to play, but she didn't want to get hurt again. Carl and his cactus friends thought hard. They wanted to find a way to play with Betty without hurting her. Then, Carl had an idea. He told Betty to fly high and they could play catch with a soft ball. Betty liked the idea. She flew high and threw the ball to the cacti. They all played together and had fun. Carl was happy that he could play with his new friend, and he would always remember this special day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, it was very cold and everything started to freeze. Tim went outside to see the frozen world. He put on his warm coat and hat. As he walked, he saw a big pile of waste. It was disgusting! Tim didn't like the waste in his pretty frozen world. He decided to do something about it. He ran back home to get his mom and dad. Tim showed his mom and dad the disgusting waste. They agreed it was not good. So, they all worked together to clean it up. They put the waste in bags and took it away. Now, the pretty frozen world was clean and beautiful again. Tim, his mom, and dad were happy. They went back home, had hot cocoa, and enjoyed the rest of the cold day.
