Title: The Prince and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a nice prince. He lived in a big castle with his mom and dad. The prince had many friends, and they liked to play all day. One day, the nice prince and his friends went to play outside. They ran, jumped, and laughed. The sun was shining, and they were very happy. But then, the sky became dark, and it started to rain. The nice prince told his friends to come inside the castle. They all went inside and closed the door. They played inside until the rain stopped. When the sun came out again, they opened the door and went back outside to play. The nice prince and his friends had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly man called Bob. Bob had some gold and he was very thoughtful. He wanted to change something with it. So he decided to give it away and help others. But when Bob tried to give the gold away, some bad people came and took it away from him. They said they didn't want to change anything. Bob was feeling very sad. The bad people took the gold and ran away. Bob couldn't do anything. He didn't have any gold anymore. He had tried to be thoughtful, but instead he was very unhappy. Bob had wanted to change something with his gold, but he couldn't. He had a bad ending.

One day, a boy named Tim had a big exam at school. He was a good boy who always did his work. Tim was very excited for the exam. He knew he had to do very well to make his mom and dad happy. On the day of the exam, Tim went to school and found his seat. He sat down and waited for the teacher to give him the exam paper. Tim looked around and saw all his friends. They were all ready to take the exam too. The exam started, and Tim was doing a good job. He knew all the answers. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the exam papers away! The teacher tried to catch them, but she could not. The wind was too strong. The exam was gone, and Tim and his friends could not finish it. They were all very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She was 3 years old and she loved to play outside. One day, Lisa and her friends decided to have fun in the river near her house. Lisa saw a raft near the shore and told her friends, "Let’s steal this raft and go for a ride!" Her friends agreed, so they took the raft and hopped on. Suddenly, someone yelled, "Hey, that’s my raft!" It was an old man who was very upset that they’d taken his raft. Lisa felt guilty and said, "We're sorry. We didn’t know it belonged to you. Let’s give it back." The old man smiled and said, "It’s important to remember that everyone's stuff belongs to them. When you take something without asking, you’re stealing it. That’s wrong and it makes people very upset." Lisa thanked the old man and she and her friends paddled the raft back to where they found it. From that day on, Lisa and her friends never took anything without asking.

One day, a big cat saw a little mouse. The cat wanted to steal the mouse's food. The mouse had a big order of sweet and sour treats. The cat said, "I will take your food!" The mouse was sad and said, "Please, do not steal my food. It is not nice to take things that are not yours." The cat did not listen and took the food away. But when the cat ate the treats, they were very sour! The cat did not like the taste. The mouse came back and said, "I will share my treats with you if you promise not to steal again." The cat agreed and they became friends. The moral of the story is that it is better to share than to steal.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside and always was full of smiles. One day, she was playing with her ball in the park when she saw a novel rolling down the hill. She ran to get it, but she was too late. She heard a man's voice, "Oh no! I'm so sorry!" Lucy looked up to see the man with a sad look on his face. The man said to Lucy, "My novel was in that bag and I was very careless. I'm really sorry". Lucy said nothing, but she was feeling sorry for the man. She decided to try to help him find the novel. Lucy and the man rolled down the hill to look for the novel, but they didn't find it. When they got back to the top of the hill, the man was still upset. Lucy said, "It’s okay to make mistakes. I can help you make a new novel, if you want". Then, the man smiled and thanked her. Lucy and the man went home and made a new novel together. They also made a lesson for both of them: being careless can cause others to feel sorrow, but being kind and mindful can make everything better.
