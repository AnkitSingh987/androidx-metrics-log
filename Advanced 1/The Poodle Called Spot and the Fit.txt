Title: The Poodle Called Spot and the Fit

Once upon a time there was a poodle called Spot. Spot was always fit and had lots of fun playing with his doggie friends. But one day Spot was feeling very sad. His veterinarian said he had to go for a swim in the pool and stay there for a few hours to soak. So Spot had to do what the veterinarian said. It wasn't much fun at first, but soon he started to enjoy it. He liked the cool water and he splashed around happily. The hours passed and soon it was time for Spot to leave. He shook off the water and was ready to go home. But then Spot saw something horrible. His fur was falling out and he wasn't fit anymore. He had a bad reaction to the chlorine and his fur was ruined. Spot was sad and the veterinarian said he could not do anything to help. Spot had to stay the way he was and he was never fit again.

Once upon a time, there was a kind truck named Tommy. Tommy had a big job to do. He had to load lots of things into his back. He loved to help his friends. One day, there was an emergency. A little cat was stuck in a tree. Tommy wanted to help the cat. So, he loaded a long ladder into his back. His friends helped him carry the ladder to the tree. Tommy's friends climbed the ladder and saved the cat. The cat was so happy to be safe. Everyone cheered for Tommy and his friends. They learned that being kind and helping others is very important. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl, who was very excited. Today was her birthday and all her family and friends were coming to celebrate with her. She jumped from bed and quickly changed into her pretty dress. For breakfast, her parents made her a special breakfast with lots of yummy food. Just then, the doorbell rang, it was her friends. The little girl answered the door and let them in. They all sat around the table and began eating. They laughed and smiled, it was so much fun. Soon, it was time for presents. The little girl was so excited she began to jump and clap. Her friends and parents gave her lots of wonderful presents and they all had a big smile on their faces. Not far away, there was another little girl who was watching. She was very jealous. She wished it was her birthday too and wanted to be able to celebrate with her own parents. The jealous little girl watched as the other little girl opened her presents and ate yummy cake. She wished that one day she could have a celebration of her own.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing outside. They saw a tree with a rare pick on it. They both wanted the pick. Tim said, "I want it!" Sue said, "No, I want it!" They decided to have a race to see who could win the pick. They ran as fast as they could. Tim was fast, but Sue was faster. Sue said, "I win! The pick is mine!" But when Sue tried to get the pick, it fell and broke. They both felt sad. They did not get the rare pick. They learned that sometimes, wanting something too much can lead to a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He liked to play outside in the wind. One day, the wind was very strong. Tim had a big red ball that he loved to play with. He went outside to play with his ball, but the wind blew it away. Tim was sad and wanted to get his ball back. He saw a big tree and thought the ball might be there. As he got closer, he saw a big bug on the tree. The bug had pinched the ball with its legs. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to get his ball back. Tim thought of a plan. He found a long stick and tried to push the bug away. To his surprise, the bug let go of the ball and flew away. The wind blew the ball back to Tim. He was so happy! From that day on, Tim and the wind became friends. They played together every day, and Tim was never scared of bugs again.
