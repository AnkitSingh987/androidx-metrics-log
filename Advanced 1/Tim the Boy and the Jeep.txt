Title: Tim the Boy and the Jeep

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy jeep. It was an original jeep, not like the ones his friends had. He loved to play with his jeep every day. One day, Tim's friend, Lily, came over to play. "Look at my jeep!" Tim said. "Wow! Can I play with it too?" Lily asked. Tim and Lily played with the jeep, and they had lots of fun. They both used their hands to make the jeep go fast. They liked to imagine that the jeep was going on big adventures. Later, Tim's mom called them for a snack. They put the jeep down and went to eat. When they came back, the jeep was gone! Tim and Lily searched everywhere but could not find the jeep. They were sad. Then, Tim's dog, Max, came into the room. Max had the jeep in his mouth! Tim and Lily were so happy. They knew that Max loved to play with toys, and they should have put the jeep away before they went to eat. From that day on, they always remembered to put their toys away when they were done playing. And they continued to imagine great adventures with the original jeep.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to visit her friend Tom. Tom had a new lamp in his room. It was a pretty lamp with a picture of a cat on it. Lucy liked the lamp very much. "Tom, your lamp is so sweet!" Lucy said. "Can I touch it?" Tom said yes, and Lucy touched the lamp. But when she touched it, something unexpected happened. The lamp started to talk! "Hello, Lucy and Tom!" said the lamp. "I am a magic lamp. I can help you do one thing you want." Lucy and Tom were surprised. They thought about what they wanted. Lucy wanted a big ice cream, and Tom wanted a new toy car. Finally, they decided to share the wish. The magic lamp made a big ice cream and a new toy car appear. Lucy and Tom were happy. They thanked the magic lamp and played together all day.

Once upon a time there was a tiny light fairy named Krizzle. Krizzle was a small fairy who loved to make things and design. She especially loved to design cookies. Krizzle worked hard to make the best cookies so she could share them with all the other fairies. One day she had a great idea. Krizzle decided to make a special cookie that was light and airy. She sprinkled some magic fairy dust on it and began to design. Krizzle used sparkles and sprinkles to decorate the cookie so it was extra special. In no time at all, the cookie was done. Krizzle was so proud of the cookie she had designed. She held it up and said, “Look at my cookie! Isn't it so light and pretty?” All of the other fairies were so excited. They wanted to try the light cookie Krizzle had designed. Everyone loved it! They ran around with their light cookie and had so much fun. Krizzle was so happy that everyone enjoyed the cookie she had designed. She smiled and flew off into the night with a feeling of joy in her heart.

Once upon a time, in a big house, there was a rich mouse. The rich mouse had a lot of cheese. He was very happy. One day, the rich mouse went outside to see the sun. He saw a cat. The cat saw the mouse. The cat wanted to catch the mouse. The rich mouse ran fast back to his house. The cat could not catch the mouse. The mouse was safe. He ate his cheese and was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lily. She loved to collect pretty things. One day, she went to a garden to find more pretty things to add to her collection. The garden was big and had many nice flowers. In the garden, Lily found a big, shiny rock. She thought it was very pretty, so she picked it up and put it in her pocket. She knew it would be a great thing to add to her collection. Lily felt happy and excited to show her new rock to her friends. The next day, Lily went to play with her friends. She showed them the big, shiny rock from the garden. Her friends thought it was very pretty too. They all went back to the garden to collect more pretty rocks. They had a fun day in the garden, and Lily's collection grew bigger and nicer.
