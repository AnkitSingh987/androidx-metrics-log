Title: Lucy and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk in the park. She saw an old tree that was very big. Lucy walked up to the tree and touched it. She felt safe near the old tree. As Lucy played by the tree, she heard a sound. She looked around and saw a small cat. The cat was lost and scared. Lucy wanted to help the cat, so she said, "Don't be scared, I will keep you safe." Lucy and the cat became friends. They played together near the old tree. The cat was happy to meet Lucy, and Lucy was happy to have a new friend. They both felt safe and had a fun day in the park.

One day, a boy named Tom went for a walk. He saw a track in the woods. He wanted to see where it went. So, he walked on the track. The trees were big and scary. Tom saw a cat. The cat stared at Tom. "Why are you staring at me?" Tom asked. The cat said, "I am scared of the big trees." Tom said, "I am scared too, but we can walk together." They walked on the track. They saw a big house. They went inside. They found a big party! All the animals were there. The scary trees were just decorations. Tom and the cat laughed and played with the animals. They were not scared anymore.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Sally and Bill. They were playing outside in the park when they saw a big cone. "Let's set the cone on its side!" said Sally with a giggle. "Maybe we shouldn't," said Bill in a serious voice. "It looks like it belongs there." But Sally was very excited, and she didn't listen to Bill. She knocked the cone over and laughed. Suddenly, a park ranger came around the corner and saw Sally and the cone. "What do you think you're doing!?" he said in a stern voice. Sally was so scared that she started to cry. The Ranger said, "I can see that you didn't mean to do it, but it's important to respect other people's things." Bill gave Sally a hug and they knew that they must always be careful and respectful. They left the park with a lesson learned.
