Title: Pippin the Pig and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little pig named Pippin. Pippin lived on a farm with many other animals. Pippin had a lot of friends, but his best friend was a big cow named Daisy. One cold day, Pippin and Daisy were playing outside. They liked to enjoy the fresh air and the soft grass under their feet. Pippin said, "Daisy, I'm cold. Let's go inside the warm barn." Daisy replied, "Okay, Pippin. We can play inside." When they went inside the barn, they saw a small bird shivering from the cold. Pippin and Daisy wanted to help the bird, so they shared their warm hay with it. The bird was happy and thanked them. Pippin, Daisy, and the bird played together all day. The moral of the story is to be kind and help others when they need it.

Tom and Jen are birds. They are wife and husband. They like to soar in the sky and sing songs. They have a nest in a big tree. They are happy. One day, they see a big cake on a table. It smells good. They are hungry. They want to eat some cake. "Let's go and get some cake," Tom says. "But the table is near the house. The house has people. People are not polite. They can hurt us," Jen says. "Don't worry. We are fast and smart. We can get some cake and fly away. Come on, it will be fun," Tom says. Tom and Jen fly to the table. They see the cake. It is big and sweet. They land on the table. They peck at the cake. It is yummy. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is a dog. The dog sees them. The dog barks. The dog runs to the table. The dog jumps on the table. Tom and Jen are scared. They try to fly away. But the dog is faster. The dog catches Tom. The dog bites Tom. Tom is hurt. Tom is dead. Jen is sad. She cries. She flies away. She is alone. She misses Tom. She hates the cake. The cake is bad. The end.

Once upon a time there was a bossy bear named Bruce. Bruce was walking through the woods one day when he found an old, rusty box. He was so curious that he had to open it. Bruce opened the box, and a brilliant light shined in the air around him. He was filled with amazement! Inside the box was a bottle of fresh oxygen. Bruce held the bottle of oxygen in both hands and said to himself, "I must breathe it all in!" And so, Bruce took a big, deep breath and filled his lungs with the delicious oxygen. He began feeling so light and bouncy. With a big smile on his face he began to tap dance around and around the box, singing. His friends were watching and began to laugh and join in the dance. Suddenly, a deep, bossy voice yelled, "Stop, everyone! Who opened this box?" Bruce was a bit scared but told the truth: "It was me!" The voice said, "Okay then, you can keep the oxygen!" And, with that, the voice disappeared. Bruce was sure happy he opened the box!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play pretend. One day, he found a big shoe in his room. Tim thought it was a mighty shoe. Tim put on the mighty shoe and started to pretend he was a strong hero. He ran around the town, helping people with their work. He helped a lady carry her bags, and he helped a man fix his bike. At the end of the day, Tim felt very happy. He learned that even if you are just pretending, you can still do good things and help others. And that is what makes you mighty.

One morning, Jimmy woke up. He was so excited; today he was going to the theater with his parents! He quickly got dressed and ran downstairs to wait for his parents. Jimmy waited and waited, until finally his parents were ready and they began their journey. He wanted it to never end, but all too soon they arrived at the theater. Jimmy was amazed - the theater was so big and magnificent. Jimmy, along with his parents, took a seat. The room was very dry but Jimmy paid it no mind. He was too busy enjoying the show - it was one of the best ones he had ever seen! When the show finished, Jimmy and his parents had to leave the theater. But all the way home, Jimmy was still smiling, because he was so glad he had been able to see the show. He knew he would never forget it!
