Title: The Gorilla and the Best

Once upon a time, there lived a little gorilla. It lived all by itself in a big empty jungle. The gorilla was so lonely! Every day, it would imagine what it would be like to have a best friend. One day, the gorilla came across a different kind of animal. It was so excited at the sight of this new creature! The two quickly became best friends. They played together in the jungle every day. The gorilla was so happy that it had a friend. But suddenly, one day the other animal started to act differently. Instead of playing and cuddling, it began to roar and throw things at the little gorilla. The poor gorilla was so scared! As it tried to run away, the other animal caught it in its claws and bared its fangs. There was nothing the little gorilla could do. There was no one to help it and it was so empty without its best friend.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She had a big, soft pillow that she loved to hug. Lily liked to jump on her bed and scream, "I love my pillow!" One day, Lily saw her brother, Tom, playing with her pillow. Tom was angry because he lost his toy. He threw the pillow on the floor. Lily was sad and said, "Don't be angry, Tom. We can find your toy together." Lily and Tom looked for the toy under the bed and found it. Tom was happy now. He said, "Thank you, Lily." They both played with the pillow and the toy. They laughed and screamed, "We love our toys and pillow!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. He played with it every day, even on Wednesday. But one day, the toy car broke. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw him and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. We will replace the toy car." Tim felt a little better. They went to the store to find a new toy car. But there was a problem. They could not find the same toy car. Tim was sad again. But then, he saw a big, red toy truck. He liked it very much. His mom said, "We can replace your toy car with this toy truck." Tim was happy again. He played with his new toy truck every day, even on Wednesday. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a cat named Jazz went out to play. Jazz was a brilliant cat with a big smile. He liked to play with his ball and squeeze it with his paws. Jazz saw his friend, Dog, in the park. Dog was also playing with a ball. Jazz went up to Dog and said, "Hi, Dog! Can I play with you?" Dog wagged his tail and said, "Yes, Jazz! Let's play together!" They played together all day, taking turns to squeeze their balls and chase after them. The sun shone brightly, and they were both very happy. At the end of the day, Jazz and Dog went home, tired but still smiling. They couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He loved to play in the park with his friends. One day, while playing, Tim found a shiny diamond on the ground. He was very happy and showed it to his friends. "Wow, it's so pretty!" said his friend, Lily. "What will you do with it, Tim?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I don't know, but I will keep it safe." Tim took the diamond home and hid it under his bed. He didn't want to share it with anyone, not even his family. He thought it was his and only his. One day, Tim's mom asked him, "Tim, have you seen the shiny diamond I lost? It's very special to me." Tim didn't answer. He felt bad, but he wanted to keep the diamond. Later, his mom found the diamond under his bed and was very sad. Tim learned that being selfish is not good. Sharing with others is important, and it makes everyone happy.
