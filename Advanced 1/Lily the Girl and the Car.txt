Title: Lily the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a nice dress with a pretty bow around her waist. One day, Lily went to the park to play with her friend Tom. At the park, Lily and Tom played on the swings and slides. They were having lots of fun. Then, they saw a big toy car. Lily wanted to drive the car, but Tom wanted to drive too. They both disagreed on who should drive first. Lily said, "I want to drive first!" Tom looked confused and said, "No, I want to drive first!" They both looked at each other, not knowing what to do. Then, Lily had an idea. "Let's take turns," she said. Tom agreed, and they both had lots of fun driving the big toy car.

Once upon a time there was a small girl who wanted to go for a walk. She stepped out of the door and saw a big stand in the park. She was so enthusiastic that she ran towards it and stepped onto the platform. The stand had a long rope that led up to the top. She grabbed it and stepped up every rung and before she knew it, she was at the top! She could see all the way across the park and it made her so excited. She stepped down from the stand and ran off to explore some more. Everywhere she went the girl saw new things and was full of enthusiasm! She hopped, skipped and jumped around the park until it was time to go home. When it was time to leave, the girl stepped back into the house and went to bed, dreaming of the adventures she would have tomorrow.

The sun was setting and it was almost time for bed. Jack wanted to stay up and play longer, but he knew that he had to rest. Suddenly he heard a soft noise coming from outside. It sounded like someone was weeping. He asked his mother what was happening and she told him not to worry. But Jack's curiosity wouldn't let him leave it at that. He wanted to know who was crying and why. His mother said she'd tell him a story if he promised to go to bed without any further questions. Reluctantly, Jack agreed and his mother began her story. She told him about an old man who was very persistent. He never quit, no matter what happened. Even when he wrote a poem and nobody liked it, he kept writing. He refused to give up and he wept every day. Jack found the story a bit sad, but at least he knew the answer to his question. Someone was weeping because they were so determined to be successful. Jack found it inspiring and he vowed to be just as persistent in whatever he chose to do. With that in mind, he went to bed and the weeping stopped.

Once upon a time, there was a young cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ball in the yard. One day, she decided to find a new friend to play with her. Kitty had a great idea! She went to the park to find a friend. At the park, she saw a young dog named Buddy. Buddy was playing with a stick. Kitty said, "Hi Buddy! Do you want to play with me and my ball?" Buddy was happy and said, "Yes, Kitty! I would love to play with you!" So, Kitty and Buddy played with the ball together. They had so much fun, and they became the best of friends. From that day on, they always played together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a poor dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with a big rug. The rug was old and dirty, but it was Spot's favorite place to sleep. Spot wanted to clean the rug, so he made a plan. Spot's friend, Cat, came over to help. Cat said, "What is your plan, Spot?" Spot told Cat, "We can shake the rug outside to make it clean." Cat liked the plan and they both picked up the rug. They went outside and started to shake it. But as they were shaking the rug, a big wind came. The wind took the rug away from Spot and Cat. They tried to catch it, but they could not. The rug flew far, far away. Spot and Cat were very sad. They went back to the small house without the rug. Now Spot had no place to sleep, and they both felt bad.
