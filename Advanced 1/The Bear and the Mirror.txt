Title: The Bear and the Mirror

One day, a little bear went for a walk in the big, wide world. The bear liked to skip and play in the sun. He skipped near the tall trees and the pretty flowers. The bear was very happy. As the bear skipped, he found a big, wide hole. He wanted to see what was in it. The bear skipped closer and closer to the hole. Suddenly, he fell in! The bear was scared, but he was not hurt. He looked around and saw something shiny. The shiny thing was a big, wide mirror. The bear looked in the mirror and saw another bear! The little bear was surprised and happy to see a new friend. They both skipped and played together in the big, wide world. The bear was not alone anymore.

Once there was a cow. She was cold and lonely. She missed her friends. She wanted to find something to do. One day, she found a stick. It was sharp, so she carefully picked it up. She started to draw in the dirt with it. She made shapes, lines and pictures. The cow was having lots of fun with her stick. She was drawing very well. When she was done, she stepped back and saw a big drawing of a cool house. The cow was very happy with her drawing. She ran back to her friends and showed them the drawing. All of the cows oohed and ahhed. The cow was happy. With her sharp stick, she made something very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very excited because her family was going on a holiday. They were going to visit the beach and play in the sand. Sue could not wait to build sand castles and swim in the water. One day, as they were getting ready to leave, Sue's mom asked her to promise to be good on the trip. Sue promised she would be good and not make her mom or dad angry. She knew that being good would make the holiday more fun for everyone. On the holiday, Sue kept her promise. She played nicely with her brother and listened to her parents. They all had a great time at the beach. Sue's mom and dad were very happy with her, and they had the best holiday ever.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy sailor. He lived near the sea in a small house. The sailor had a boat that he loved very much. Every day, he would go to the sea and sail his boat. One day, the sleepy sailor felt very tired. He wanted to remove his heavy coat, but he was too sleepy. He thought it would be nice to take a nap in his boat. So, he went to his boat and lay down. As the sleepy sailor slept, the wind began to blow. The boat started to move away from the shore. The sailor was too sleepy to wake up. The boat went far, far away, and the sleepy sailor was lost at sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He wanted to eat something yummy. He saw a big tree with red apples. He picked one and took a big bite. It was so good! One day, Tim was playing with his ball. He saw a big jar of powder. His mom said, "Pass the powder, please." Tim passed the powder and his mom made a big cake. The cake smelled so good. Tim was very happy. The next day, Tim and his friends played in the park. They were all hungry. Tim's mom came with the big cake. They all sat down and ate the cake. It was the best day ever. They all said, "Thank you, Tim's mom!"

Once upon a time, Jake was walking in the forest. He was gathering sticks to take home. As he walked, he heard a noise. Jake looked around and saw a small sack filled with something. He bent down and touched it. It felt soft and warm. Suddenly, Jake heard a loud noise. It was a bear! Jake was scared, so he picked up the sack and ran away as fast as he could. But the bear was slow, so Jake managed to outrun him. Soon, Jake was back home with the sack. He opened it and saw a little puppy inside. The puppy was hurt. Its leg was in pain. Jake was sad, so he made sure to give the puppy a warm bed and some food and he took it to the vet. With some slow care, the puppy recovered and Jake was very happy.
