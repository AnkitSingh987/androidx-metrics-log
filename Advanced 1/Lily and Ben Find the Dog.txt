Title: Lily and Ben Find the Dog

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They had a plan to make a big row of rocks and sticks. They wanted to see how long they could make it. They found many rocks and sticks on the ground. They started to put them in a row. They worked hard and had fun. They made the row longer and longer. But then, a nosy dog came by. The dog saw the row of rocks and sticks. He thought it was a toy. He ran to the row and started to dig and bite. He made a big mess. Lily and Ben were sad and angry. They shouted at the dog. "Go away, dog! You are nosy and bad! You ruined our plan!" The dog barked and wagged his tail. He did not understand. He thought they wanted to play. He jumped on them and licked their faces. Lily and Ben did not like the dog. They ran away from him. They looked for another place to make their row. They hoped the dog would not follow them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She loved to draw and write in her journal. Every day, she would write about her day and draw pictures of her friends and family. Jane's journal was very important to her, and she valued it a lot. One day, Jane went to the park with her journal. She found a big, disgusting bug. The bug was green and slimy. Jane thought it was icky, but she wanted to draw it in her journal. So, she sat down and drew the bug with her crayons. When Jane finished drawing, she showed her picture to her mom. Her mom said, "Wow, that bug looks disgusting, but your drawing is very good!" Jane was happy that her mom liked her drawing. She knew that her journal was special and she would keep drawing and writing in it every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to collect things. She had many toys, but her favorite thing to collect was equipment. She had a red ball, a blue shovel, and a yellow bucket. One day, Mia found a new thing to collect. It was a sour green apple. She loved the sour taste so much that she wanted to find more. She took her equipment and went to the big tree where she found the sour apple. Mia used her blue shovel to dig a hole under the tree. She hoped to find more sour apples. But, as she dug deeper, the ground shook and the big tree started to fall. Mia was scared and ran away, leaving her equipment behind. The big tree fell down and broke Mia's red ball, blue shovel, and yellow bucket. Mia was very sad. She learned that sometimes when you want more, you might lose what you already have.

There were two friends, Tim and Ann. Tim loved to write and Ann loved to read. One day, Tim had an idea, so he started to write. He wrote about a windy day and how the leaves flew in the air. Tim wrote about how the birds sang in the trees and how the sun shone brightly in the sky. Ann was so excited to read about Tim's memory. As she read, a smile grew on her face. Tim smiled too and the two friends enjoyed their time together. The memory was a harmless one, so the two friends decided to keep it in their hearts forever. They both knew that they could always look back on this memory, no matter how much time had passed. So Tim and Ann continued to write and read, and their memories would stay safe and harmless forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to spend time with his toy police car. His toy police car was very old and broken, but Tim still liked to play with it. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his broken police car in the park. Suddenly, a real police car came zooming by. Tim was so excited that he started to follow the real police car. They went around and around the park until the car stopped. When the car stopped, the police officer got out and saw Tim. He smiled and said, "I saw you following me with your broken toy police car. I have a surprise for you!" The police officer went to his car and took out a brand new toy police car for Tim. Tim couldn't believe it! He was so happy and thanked the police officer. From that day on, Tim played with his new police car, but he still kept his old broken one as a reminder of the special day.
