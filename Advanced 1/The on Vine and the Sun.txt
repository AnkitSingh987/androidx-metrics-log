Title: The on Vine and the Sun

Once upon a time, in an organized line, many grapes lived on a vine. They were all very happy. The sun would shine on them every day, making them grow big and strong. One day, a little grape named Gracie wanted to talk to the sun. "Hello, Sun!" said Gracie. "Thank you for shining on us every day!" "You're welcome, Gracie!" replied the sun. "I love making all of you grapes happy and healthy." One day, a big wind came and blew some grapes off the vine. Gracie and her friends were very sad. They knew they had to help their friends find their way back to the vine. Gracie and her friends worked together to make a plan. They made a line to show the lost grapes the way back. The lost grapes saw the line and followed it back to the vine. Everyone was happy again! The moral of the story is that when we work together and stay organized, we can help our friends and make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy giraffe with a long neck. One day, the giraffe's neck broke. Lily was very sad and did not know what to do. Lily's mom saw her sad face and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily showed her mom the broken giraffe. "Oh, I see," said her mom. "Let's go to the store and replace it with a new one." At the store, Lily found a new giraffe with a long neck. She was happy and not ashamed anymore. She hugged her new giraffe and said, "Thank you, Mommy!" From that day on, Lily took good care of her new giraffe and they were the best of friends.

One day, a furry cat named Tom went for a walk. Tom saw a hole in the ground. He was very curious about what was inside the hole. So, he went closer to look. Tom heard a voice from the hole. "Hello!" said a tiny voice. Tom was surprised and said, "Hello! Who are you?" The tiny voice answered, "I am Sam, the mouse. I live in this hole. Do you want to be my friend?" Tom and Sam became good friends. They played together every day. One day, while they were playing, Tom had to turn around very fast. When he did, he saw a big dog! The dog wanted to play too, but Tom and Sam were scared. The dog said, "Don't be scared! I just want to play with you." Tom, Sam, and the big dog became best friends. They all played together and had lots of fun. The big dog even helped Tom and Sam find more friends to play with. In the end, they all lived happily and played together every day.

Tommy was a little boy who loved to jog. One day, he decided to take a jog to the top of the nearby cliff. His mom was worried about him and said, "Tommy, that is too expensive for you to do alone. I want you to wait for your dad, and then the two of you can go together.” Tommy nodded and waited for his dad. When his dad arrived, they started their jog up the cliff. It was a long, tiring journey with lots of steeper places. As they reached the top, they stopped to catch their breath and enjoy the view. They could see the whole city below them, it was beautiful! Then Tommy and his dad began to jog back down the cliff. Tommy laughed and felt so happy, it was his favorite place to run. They quickly made it back to the bottom of the cliff and Tommy said, “That was a fun jog!”

Once upon a time, there was a little dove. The dove lived in a calm tree near a big park. Every day, the dove would fly around and play with its friends. One day, while the dove was flying, it saw a girl crying. The dove flew down and asked, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" The dove wanted to help the girl, so they looked for the toy together. After a while, they found the toy near a pond. The girl was happy and said, "Thank you, dove!" The dove was glad to help. They played together until it was time for the girl to go home. The dove flew back to its calm tree, and they both said, "Goodbye!" They knew they would see each other again soon.
