Title: The House and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a small house. In the house lived a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Mia. Mia was a big sister now because she had a new infant brother named Tom. Mia was very happy but also a little fearful. She wanted to be a good big sister. One day, Mia heard a noise coming from Tom's room. She went to the door and heard Tom crying. Mia felt scared. She slowly opened the door and peeked inside. She saw her infant brother in his crib, crying. Mia felt braver and went closer to Tom. "Shh, it's okay, Tom," Mia said softly. "I'm here now." Mia gently patted Tom's back, and he stopped crying. Mia felt happy and not fearful anymore. She knew she was a good big sister. From that day on, Mia always peeked in on Tom to make sure he was okay. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a pretty fireman. Every day he put on his red fireman hat and shiny coat. He loved to help people. One day he was walking in the park. He saw a little girl eating an ice cream. She looked sad. The fireman said, “Hello! Why are you sad?” The little girl said, “I'm hungry. I wish I had something to eat.” The fireman said, “Don't worry. I'll get you some food.” He went to the store and came back with a sandwich. The little girl smiled. She said, “Thank you. You're so nice and pretty.” The fireman laughed and said, “You're welcome!” The little girl ate the sandwich and was happy. The fireman was happy too.

One day, a dog named Max and a cat named Luna went to play outside. They liked to play with a ball and have fun together. Max was big and strong, and Luna was small and fast. They were very good friends. While they were playing, Max saw some fire in the woods. He said to Luna, "Look, there is fire! We need to be useful and help put it out." They ran to the fire and started to think of a plan. Max thought he could use his big paws to put dirt on the fire, and Luna could use her fast legs to get water. As they were getting ready to help, a big bird named Toby flew down from the sky. Toby said, "Don't worry, friends! I can help with the fire!" Toby flapped his big wings very fast, and the wind from his wings put out the fire! Max and Luna were surprised and happy. They thanked Toby for his help and went back to play together. From that day on, Max, Luna, and Toby became best friends. They learned that even though they were all different, they could work together to be useful and help others. And they had lots of fun playing together every day.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to a big event with her mom. At the event, there were many fun games and yummy food. Lucy was very excited to play and eat. While they were there, Lucy's mom mentioned a special game. "Lucy, do you want to play the big ball game?" she asked. Lucy nodded her head, and they went to play the game. But when they got there, they saw that the line was very long. Lucy felt sad because she really wanted to play the big ball game. Then, something unexpected happened. A kind man who worked at the event saw Lucy's sad face. He came over to Lucy and her mom and said, "I have a surprise for you. Follow me." The kind man led them to a secret room with the same big ball game, but there was no line! Lucy was so happy that she could play the game without waiting. She had a great day at the big event with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a normal boy who liked to play with his toys and go to the park. One day, he asked his mom if he could go to the gym to play. His mom said yes, and they went to the gym together. At the gym, Tim saw many kids playing and having fun. He wanted to join them, so he requested that his mom let him play with the other kids. His mom said yes, and Tim ran off to play with his new friends. Tim played at the gym for a long time, and he had so much fun. He made many new friends and played lots of games. When it was time to go home, Tim was very tired but happy. He couldn't wait to come back to the gym and play again.
