Title: Poppy the Girl and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Poppy. Poppy loved to play outside in the dirt. She would get very filthy from playing all day. Her hands and clothes would be covered in dirt. Her mom would always tell her to clean up before eating, but Poppy would forget. One day, Poppy found a big, red apple on the ground. It looked so yummy that she wanted to taste it right away. She forgot what her mom told her about cleaning up before eating. Poppy took a big bite of the apple with her filthy hands. The apple did not taste good at all! Poppy's face turned yucky and she spit it out. She realized that the dirt from her hands had ruined her yummy apple. Poppy felt sad and knew she should have listened to her mom. From that day on, Poppy always remembered to wash her hands and clean up before eating. She learned that being clean is important to enjoy her food and stay healthy. And that was the moral value Poppy learned: always be clean before you eat.

Once upon a time, there was a loud dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to bark all day and all night. He was so loud that he bothered the other animals in the forest. The other animals did not like Buddy's loud barking. One day, a wise owl saw Buddy barking loudly. The owl thought of a way to help Buddy. He recommended that Buddy should join a class. In this class, Buddy could learn how to be quiet and play nice with other animals. Buddy went to the class and learned how to be quiet. He also made new friends in the class. The other animals in the forest were happy that Buddy was not loud anymore. Now, Buddy and his friends played together every day. The forest was a happy place. The moral of the story is that sometimes we need to learn new things to be better friends to others.

Sara and Ben were excited to go to the park with Mom. They put on their coats and hats and ran outside. But when they got to the street, they saw a lot of fog. They could not see the park or the cars or the trees. "What is that?" Sara asked, pointing at the fog. "It is fog," Mom said. "It is like a big cloud that comes down to the ground. Sometimes it goes away when the sun comes out." "Can we still go to the park?" Ben asked. Mom shrugged. She did not know if the fog would clear or not. She said, "Let's try. Maybe we can have some fun in the fog." Sara and Ben nodded. They held Mom's hand and walked slowly in the fog. They could hear the birds and the dogs, but they could not see them. They pretended they were explorers in a new land. They made up names for the things they saw in the fog, like fuzzy rocks and ghost trees and misty flowers. When they got to the park, they were happy to see the swings and the slide and the sandbox. They played with their toys and made friends with other kids. They did not mind the fog anymore. They had a lot of fun in the park.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there lived a little mouse named Tim. Tim loved to eat toast every day. It was his favorite food. He would put butter and jam on his toast and eat it with a big smile. One day, Tim went to his kitchen to make some toast, but he had no bread left. He was very sad. He knew he had to go to the store to buy more bread. Tim put on his little coat and hat and went outside. On his way to the store, Tim saw a big cat. He was scared, but he knew he had to remain brave to get his bread. So, he walked past the cat very quietly. The cat didn't see him, and Tim made it to the store safely. He bought his bread and went home to make his toast. Tim was so happy, and he enjoyed his toast even more that day.
