Title: Mia the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a small bird on the ground. The bird had a gentle look in its eyes. Mia wanted to help the bird, so she tried to pick it up. But the bird was scared and flew away. Mia felt sad and walked around the park. She found a small twig on the ground. She thought the bird might like the twig for its nest. Mia picked up the twig and went to find the bird again. When she found the bird, Mia said, "I have a twig for your nest." The bird looked at Mia and the twig. It was still scared but wanted the twig. Mia gently put the twig on the ground and took a step back. The bird slowly came closer, picked up the twig, and flew away to its nest. Mia felt happy that she could help the bird.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Anna who wanted to design something. She thought and thought but couldn't decide what to design. Suddenly, she got an idea. She would make a loop! She quickly got out the paper, straws and scissors to start making the loop. Anna's hands got a bit nervous as she worked and her little fingers trembled. Suddenly, she heard her mum say from the kitchen: "Anna dear, what are you doing?" With a smile she shouted "Mum, I'm designing a loop!" Mum smiled and said "That sounds great! Let me know when you are finished". Anna smiled, feeling excited to show her mum her design. She worked very hard to make her loop just right. Finally, Anna was finished. She proudly walked into the kitchen and said "Look, Mum! I designed a loop!" Her mum was so proud and said "It looks perfect, Anna!" Anna felt so happy and the feeling of accomplishment was clear on her face.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Jo. Jo was three years old and she loved to explore. One day, she wanted to explore a big hill near her house. As she walked up the hill, she felt strange heat coming from the ground. This made Jo very scared. When Jo got to the top of the hill, there was a big crack in the ground. Jo couldn't believe what she saw! She wanted to climb in but it was too big for her. She walked around the crack and tried to find a way to improve it. Suddenly, her Mom appeared. Jo was so excited to see her. Her Mom smiled and said, "What are you doing, Jo?" Jo told her Mom about the crack in the ground and asked if they could do something to make it better. Her Mom smiled and nodded. She said, "We need to make the crack smaller, so that you can get inside. Let's use some sticks and stones to help us do that." Jo and her Mom worked hard to make the crack smaller. Finally, they were successful. Jo's Mom said, "Now, Jo, you can get inside of the crack and explore." Jo was very excited now. She climbed inside the crack and was amazed at what she saw. She felt hot air coming from inside and she knew there was something interesting in there. Jo found crystals and gems and she was filled with joy.

Once upon a time, there were two small monkeys. They were very alert and loved to race each other. Every day, they challenged each other to see who was the fastest and it always ended in a lot of fun. One day, the two monkeys raced to the tallest tree in the forest and decided to rest in the shade. As they lay under the tree, they spied a large trunk nearby. They were curious, so they decided to explore it. The monkeys opened the trunk and saw that it was full of amazing things! They were so excited and started to play with the objects inside. Suddenly, a large lion emerged from the shadows and roared. The two monkeys ran away, but the lion chased after them. The lion was too fast and soon caught up with the monkeys. He ate them both and they never raced again. The end.
