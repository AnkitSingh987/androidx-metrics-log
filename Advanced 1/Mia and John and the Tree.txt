Title: Mia and John and the Tree

Mia and John were running in the park. The trees were singing and the birds were chirping. Mia and John were laughing and having fun. Suddenly, Mia kicked a tree and bark flew everywhere. John was surprised and he said, "Mia, why did you kick the tree?" Mia answered, "I kicked it because it was so much fun!" John said, "Be careful, Mia. You should use more care!" Mia looked around and saw the birds looking at them from the trees. Then she said, "Okay, I'll be more careful next time." Then she smiled and hugged John. Mia and John played tag and after awhile Mia kicked a stone carelessly and said, "That felt good!" John shook his head, but Mia just laughed and continued playing. Mia looked up at the sky and said, "Wow, what a beautiful day!" And the two of them kept running and laughing until it was time to go home.

Lily was a smart girl who liked to read and write. She had many books and papers in her room. She also liked to send letters to her friends and family. She would write nice words and draw pictures on them. Then she would put them in envelopes and stamps. She would walk to the mailbox and put them inside. One day, Lily wrote a letter to her grandma. She told her how much she loved her and missed her. She also drew a flower and a heart. She was very proud of her letter. She put it in an envelope and a stamp. She walked to the mailbox and put it inside. She smiled and waved to the mailbox. She hoped her grandma would get her letter soon. But Lily did not know that a bad man was watching her. He was a thief who liked to steal mail. He waited until Lily was gone. Then he ran to the mailbox and opened it. He saw Lily's letter and took it out. He did not care about Lily or her grandma. He did not value her words or her pictures. He only wanted to see if there was money or something else he could sell. He ripped the envelope and looked inside. He saw no money, only a paper with words and drawings. He was angry and threw the paper on the ground. He ran away with the envelope and the stamp. Lily's grandma never got her letter. She was sad and wondered why Lily did not write to her. Lily was sad too. She waited and waited for a reply from her grandma, but she never got one. She thought her grandma did not love her or miss her. She did not know that her letter was stolen and thrown away by a bad man. She cried and wished she could see her grandma again. She did not want to write any more letters. She did not want to go to the mailbox anymore. She lost her joy and her hope. The end.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there lived a kind bear. The bear loved to cook. One day, while he was cooking, a little bird came to him. The bird said, "Hello, big bear! What is your name?" The bear smiled and said, "My name is Ben. I like to cook. Do you want to try my food?" The little bird was happy and said, "Yes, please! My name is Lily." Ben and Lily became good friends. They played and cooked together every day. One day, while they were playing, they found a big, shiny stone. They did not know what it was, but they knew it was special. The next day, when they were cooking, they used the shiny stone to stir the food. Suddenly, the food turned into gold! Ben and Lily were so surprised and happy. They knew the shiny stone was magic. From that day on, Ben and Lily used the magic stone to help their friends in the deep, deep forest. They cooked food for everyone and made their friends happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Ben liked to design things with his blocks. He made houses, cars, towers and bridges. He was very good at it. One day, he wanted to design something new. He looked around his room and saw his stuffed mole. He loved his mole. It was soft and brown and had a pink nose. Ben had an idea. He decided to design a home for his mole. He took some blocks and made a big hill. He made a hole in the hill for the mole to go in and out. He put some grass and flowers on the hill to make it pretty. He was very proud of his design. He showed his design to his mom. "Look, mom, I made a home for my mole!" he said. Mom smiled and hugged him. "That's wonderful, Ben. You are very creative. Your mole must be very happy." Ben nodded and ran to his mole. He put it in the hole and said, "This is your new home, mole. Do you like it?" The mole squeaked and snuggled with Ben. Ben felt happy too. He had a good friend and a good design. He was not poor at all. He was rich in love.
