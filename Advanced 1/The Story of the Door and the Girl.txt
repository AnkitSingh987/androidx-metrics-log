Title: The Story of the Door and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. The girl had a cold oat in her hand. She wanted to unlock the door, but it was too hard. The girl asked, "How can I unlock the door?". A kind voice answered, "You need to use your oat. Push it firmly into the keyhole and turn it". The girl was excited. She put the oat in the keyhole and twisted it. And, sure enough, the door unlocked. The girl smiled. She opened the door, stepped inside, and suddenly the room was filled with sunshine.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a jog. She liked to jog in the park. The sun was out, and it was a perfect day. On her way, Sue saw an envelope on the ground. She picked it up and looked inside. There was a pretty picture in the envelope. Sue liked the picture a lot. Sue showed the picture to her friend, Tim. Tim looked at the picture and laughed. "Sue, that is a picture of you!" he said. Sue was surprised and happy. The envelope had her own picture, and it made her day even more perfect.

Mum and Dad were in the kitchen, picking out the best bottle of wine. Dad said, "This looks like a good one!" The bottle was an unusual colour and shape. Mum said, "I'm not sure we can drink this - it looks a bit unusual!" Dad chuckled and said, "Oh, I'm sure it's fine. I've heard it's a great wine!" Mum wasn't convinced though, and shook her head. She said, "I don't think it's a good idea." Dad said, "Why not try a sip? You can't know until you try it!" Mum still didn't want to try it, so Dad said, "Ok, we'll get something else" and put the bottle back. Mum and Dad had a conflict but in the end, Dad listened to Mum and they chose a different bottle of wine.

Once there was a happy hippo. She liked to laugh and have fun. One day she wanted to go for a swim. She hopped in the water and had a wonderful time. Suddenly, she heard a bad sound. She looked up and saw a big alligator! The hippo was so scared she started to giggle. But the alligator was not happy and he yelled "go away!" The hippo was scared and so she screamed. But then she remembered something, she could laugh! So the hippo began to laugh and the alligator was so surprised he couldn't move. The hippo's laughter was so loud and so strong, that it made the alligator feel embarrassed. Soon he swam away and the hippo was happy and laughed all the way back home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sleepy, but he did not want to go to bed. He wanted to play with his toy boat in the bathtub. His mommy said, "Tim, it's time for bed. You can play with your boat tomorrow." Tim did not listen to his mommy. He went to the bathtub and started to play with his boat. He poked the boat to make it go faster. The boat went round and round in the bathtub. Tim was having so much fun, but he was also getting more and more sleepy. Suddenly, Tim fell asleep in the bathtub. The water started to fill up the bathtub more and more. Tim's mommy heard the water and ran to the bathroom. She saw Tim asleep in the bathtub and tried to wake him up, but it was too late. The water had filled up too much, and Tim could not breathe. This story had a bad ending because Tim did not listen to his mommy when she told him to go to bed.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a weird-looking tree. The tree had a face, and it could talk! Sue was very surprised. The tree said, "I can give you a massage if you are tired." Sue thought it was strange, but she was tired from playing. So, she said, "Okay, tree. Give me a massage." The tree's branches gently massaged Sue's back. It felt nice, and Sue became happy. Then, Sue's friend, Tom, came to the park. He saw the tree giving Sue a massage and laughed. "That's so weird!" he said. But Sue told Tom that the tree was kind and helpful. Tom learned that it's important to value kindness, even if it looks weird or different. And that day, both Sue and Tom became friends with the talking tree.
