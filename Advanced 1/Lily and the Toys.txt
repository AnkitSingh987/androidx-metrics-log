Title: Lily and the Toys

Lily was a naughty girl. She liked to play with the faucet in the bathroom. She turned it on and off and made the water splash everywhere. She did not care if she made a mess or wasted water. One day, her mom caught her playing with the faucet. She was very angry. She told Lily to stop and go to her room. Lily did not want to go. She lied to her mom. She said she was not playing with the faucet. She said she was just washing her hands. Her mom did not believe her. She saw the water on the floor and on the walls. She saw the wet towel and the soap bubbles. She knew Lily was lying. She took her by the hand and led her to her room. She said Lily had to stay there until she said sorry. Lily was sad and mad. She did not like her room. She did not like her mom. She did not want to say sorry. She thought it was fun to play with the faucet. She did not think it was naughty. But after a while, she felt lonely and bored. She missed her mom and her toys. She realized she had done something wrong. She decided to say sorry. She called her mom and told her the truth. She said she was sorry for playing with the faucet and lying to her mom. Her mom was happy to hear that. She hugged Lily and forgave her. She said Lily could come out of her room and play with her toys. But she also said Lily had to help her clean the bathroom and not play with the faucet again. Lily agreed. She learned her lesson. She did not want to be naughty anymore. She wanted to be good.

Once upon a time there was an oyster that lived at the bottom of the sea. He was a very humble oyster and kept to himself. He didn't care much for going out and meeting other oysters, he was perfectly content to stay where he was. One day he decided to take a little swim around and meet the other sea creatures. He kept close to the ocean floor so he felt safe. Suddenly, he spotted a wonderful pearl in the sand. He couldn't believe his eyes! He knew it was very valuable and he decided to keep it. He was so excited and he kept his pearl very safe in his shell. Everyone around the sea admired him for his simple humble heart and his newfound treasure. From then on, the oyster kept his pearl carefully and proudly. Whenever he had to go anywhere, he made sure he kept the pearl safe and protected. The end!

One day, a penguin named Pippin felt dizzy. He did not know why. Pippin had a friend named Sally. Sally was a seal. Pippin went to Sally and said, "I feel dizzy. What can I do?" Sally said, "Let's start by sitting down. Maybe that will help." So Pippin and Sally sat down. But Pippin still felt dizzy. Sally thought hard. She said, "Let's ask our friend, Dr. Fish." Dr. Fish was a smart fish. They went to Dr. Fish and Pippin said, "I feel dizzy. Can you help me?" Dr. Fish looked at Pippin and said, "You need to drink more water. That will help you feel better." Pippin drank more water and soon he felt better. Pippin, Sally, and Dr. Fish were happy. They played together all day. Pippin was not dizzy anymore.

Lily and Max were best friends. They liked to play in the park together. One day, they saw a big fountain in the park. It had water that went up and down. It was very pretty. "Wow, look at the fountain!" Lily said. She was enthusiastic. She liked water a lot. "Can we go closer?" Max asked. He was curious. He wanted to see the fountain better. "Okay, let's go!" Lily said. She took Max's hand and they ran to the fountain. They saw many colors in the water. They heard the water splash. "Can we talk to the fountain?" Max asked. He had a funny idea. "Talk to the fountain? How?" Lily asked. She was confused. "Like this!" Max said. He leaned over the fountain and shouted. "Hello, fountain! How are you today?" The fountain did not answer. It just kept spraying water. "Maybe it can't hear us," Lily said. She shouted louder. "Hello, fountain! We are Lily and Max! We like you a lot!" The fountain still did not answer. It just changed colors. "Maybe it can't talk," Max said. He was disappointed. "Maybe it can talk, but in a different way," Lily said. She was optimistic. "How?" Max asked. "Maybe it can talk with water and colors," Lily said. She pointed at the fountain. "Look, it is making a rainbow! Maybe that is its way of saying hello!" Max looked at the fountain. He saw the rainbow. He smiled. "Hello, rainbow!" he said. "You are very pretty!" Lily smiled too. "Hello, rainbow!" she said. "Thank you for talking to us!" The fountain kept making rainbows. Lily and Max kept talking to it. They had a lot of fun. They were happy.
