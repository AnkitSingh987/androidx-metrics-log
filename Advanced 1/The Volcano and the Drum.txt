Title: The Volcano and the Drum

Once upon a time, there was a big volcano. The volcano was very quiet. One day, a small boy named Tim went to the volcano. He wanted to ask the volcano a question. He asked, "Volcano, why are you so quiet?" The volcano did not answer. Tim felt embarrassed. The next day, Tim went to the volcano again. This time, he brought a big drum. He played the drum very loud. He hoped the volcano would answer him. But the volcano stayed quiet. Tim was sad. Suddenly, the volcano started to shake. Tim was scared. But then, something unexpected happened. The volcano did not get loud or angry. Instead, it started to sing! The volcano sang a beautiful song. Tim was so happy. He played his drum and sang with the volcano. From that day on, the volcano was never quiet again.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big hole in the ground. It is a cave. They want to go inside. They hold hands and walk into the cave. It is dark and cold. They hear strange noises. They are scared. They do not understand what is in the cave. Suddenly, they see two bright eyes. They are yellow and angry. It is a wild animal. It is a bear. It growls and shows its teeth. It wants to eat Tom and Sue. Tom and Sue scream and run out of the cave. They are very fast. The bear is slow and big. It cannot catch them. It stays in the cave. Tom and Sue are safe. They hug each other. They are happy. They do not want to go into the cave again. They understand that the cave is not a good place to play. It is a home for wild animals. They go back to the park and play with their toys. They have fun.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park to play. She saw her friend, Tom, there. They liked to play at the park because it was big and had a lot of fun things to do. Lily and Tom liked to be flexible when they played, like bending and stretching. "Let's play catch until the sunset!" said Lily. Tom agreed, and they started to play. They threw the ball back and forth, laughing and having a good time. As the sun began to get lower in the sky, Lily and Tom got tired. They sat down on the grass to rest. Suddenly, they saw a big, colorful bird in the sky. The bird was flying towards them, and it had something in its mouth. "Look, it's a bird!" said Tom. "What does it have?" asked Lily. The bird dropped the thing it had in its mouth, and it landed right in front of them. It was a shiny, golden key! Lily and Tom looked at each other, surprised. They never thought they would find a key at the park! They decided to keep the key and look for what it could open. The sunset was beautiful that day, and the two friends had a new adventure waiting for them.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a nice kitchen. He loved to sleep all day and did not like to play. One hot day, Tom's owner, a little girl named Sue, wanted to make ice cream. She asked Tom, "Do you want to help me make ice cream?" Tom just yawned and went back to sleep. Sue went to the kitchen and started making the ice cream. She put the ice cream in the freezer, but she left the door open by mistake. The ice cream started to melt! Tom woke up and saw the mess. He felt bad for not helping Sue. So, Tom helped Sue clean up the kitchen and they made new ice cream together. From that day on, Tom was not so lazy anymore.

Once upon a time there were two best friends called Tom and Jack. Tom was the wisest and Jack was the funniest. One day, Tom and Jack were walking in the park together and Jack told Tom a new joke. He thought it was so funny and so he started laughing. Suddenly, something really smelly came from under a bush. Jack was so surprised that he fell over and hit his head on the floor! Tom realised that he had to act quickly to help Jack and prevent any more harm. So he put his hand over Jack's mouth so that no toxic fumes could come in. Jack soon recovered and Tom explained to him why he had done what he did. Tom said, "Jokes are fun, but you must be careful when you laugh. Laughter can bring danger if it comes too close to bad-smelling and dangerous things like this." Jack was surprised to learn this but he thanked Tom and promised to always be careful when he laughs. The moral of this story is to always be careful in life, and know when it's ok to joke and when it's not. Laughter can prevent harm, but it can also bring danger.
