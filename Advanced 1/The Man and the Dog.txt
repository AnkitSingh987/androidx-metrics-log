Title: The Man and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a man who had a lab. He had many things in the lab. Some things were dangerous. The man was very careful with these dangerous things. One day, a little dog came into the lab. The little dog was very curious. He looked at all the things in the lab. The man saw the dog and was worried. He did not want the dog to touch the dangerous things. The man tried to catch the dog, but the dog was fast. The dog ran around the lab and did something unexpected. The dog pushed a big button. The button made the dangerous things safe. The man was very surprised and happy. He and the dog became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a selfish city. The people in the city were always looking out for themselves and no one else. One day, in the lonely town, a little girl came to visit. The girl stopped and sat in the town square. She watched all of the people walking around and noticed how selfish they all were. She felt so sad, but she stayed and kept her head high. As the day went on, the girl started to feel very alone. Everyone in the city seemed to be so selfish, nobody wanted to be her friend. She sat there until the sun went down. Finally, she decided it was time to go home. But as she went to leave she noticed the people in the city had gathered around her. They were all selfish and ignored her. She realised there was no one in this city for her. So, she sadly turned and left the selfish city.

Tommy saw a kangaroo on the grass. He ran to his mom and said, "Mommy, look! There's a kangaroo!" She smiled and said, "Yes, it's a kangaroo. What do you want to do?" Tommy thought for a moment then said, "Let's pack up and follow the kangaroo!" His mom agreed and they started to pack. They put on their rain coats since the grass was wet. They followed the kangaroo until it stopped. It looked at them, blinked its eyes and hopped away. They watched until it was out of sight. Tommy said, "Let's go home now, Mommy." His mom smiled and they packed up their things and went home.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a dependable dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toys, especially his ball. One day, Max saw a new toy in his room. It was a surprise from his owner, a game of match. Max was very excited to play the new game. Max started to play the match game. He had to find two cards that looked the same. At first, it was hard for Max. He could not find any matches. Max started to feel sad. He did not want to give up, but he needed help to solve the problem. Max's best friend, a little girl named Lily, saw Max feeling sad. She came over to help him play the match game. Together, they looked at the cards and found the matches. Max was very happy, and he wagged his tail. Max and Lily played the match game all day, and they had a great time. Max knew that with the help of his friend Lily, he could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round ball. The ball was tight and liked to bounce. One day, the ball saw a beautiful sunset in the sky. The colors were red, orange, and pink. The ball wanted to go up to the sky and see the sunset closer. A kind boy saw the ball and wanted to help. The boy held the ball tight and threw it up high. The ball went up, up, up into the sky. The ball was so happy to be close to the sunset. But, the ball started to fall back down. The boy was ready to catch it. He caught the ball and gave it a big hug. The ball was so happy it got to see the sunset. The boy and the ball played together until it was time to go home.
