Title: Sue the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toy car. One day, Sue had a big idea. She wanted to attach her toy car to a big balloon. She thought it would be fun to watch it fly in the sky. Sue found a big balloon and some soap. She used the soap to make the balloon stick to the car. She was very glad it worked. Sue took her toy car outside to show her friends. She let go of the balloon and the car flew up, up, up in the sky. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew the balloon and the car far away. Sue was sad because she lost her toy car. Her friends tried to help her look for it, but they could not find it. The moral of the story is to be careful with your things, and not to let them go too easily.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a modest girl named Lily. She had a special power. When she would stand up tall, she could make flowers grow. Everyone in the town loved her power. One day, Lily went to the park. She saw a sad boy sitting on the grass. He told her that his favorite flower was gone. Lily wanted to help him. She stood up tall and used her power to grow a pretty flower for the boy. The boy was so happy to see the flower. He thanked Lily for her help. Lily felt good that she could use her power to make others happy. From that day on, Lily and the boy became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big piano. A little girl named Lily loved to play it. She would play the piano every day and make pretty music. Her friends liked to listen to her play. One day, Lily was playing the piano when a big cat jumped on it. The cat tried to balance on the piano, but it was very hard. The cat made a big mess, and the piano made funny sounds. Lily and her friends started to laugh. Lily was a little embarrassed, but she had an idea. She played the piano with the cat still on it. The cat danced on the piano, and it made everyone laugh even more. The cat was not just a big mess, it was now part of the music!

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a little bird. The little bird lived in a cage. The cage was not big, but the bird was happy. Every night, the little bird would dream. The little bird would dream about flying free in the sky, high above the trees. One day, a harmless cat came to the cage. The cat said, "Hello, little bird. I can help you. I can open your cage, and you can fly free in the sky like in your dreams." The little bird was happy and said, "Yes, please help me. I want to fly free like in my dreams." The harmless cat opened the cage, and the little bird flew out. The little bird was so happy to be free. But then, the harmless cat was not so harmless. The cat jumped up and caught the little bird. The little bird was sad, and its dreams of flying free were gone.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl named Ella. Every morning, Ella would rub her hands together and look out of the window. Outside, she could see a regular hoop in the garden. She thought it looked like lots of fun! One day, Ella asked her mom if she could go and play with the hoop. Mom said yes, so Ella ran outside to play. Ella had so much fun. She kept rubbing the hoop with her hands and spinning it around. But then she felt something weird in her hand. She looked down and saw a thorn pricking her finger. She started to cry. "It's ok, sweetie," said Mom. She came over and pulled out the thorn. "It's not so bad, but don't touch the hoop again. It's not safe." Ella was sad, and knew she wouldn't be allowed to play with the hoop anymore. Even though she had to give up the fun, she was happy to have avoided getting hurt.
