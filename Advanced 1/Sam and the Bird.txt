Title: Sam and the Bird

Once upon a time, a little squirrel named Sam lived in a big tree. Sam loved to eat peanuts. One day, Sam found a very big peanut. He was very happy and wanted to eat it. But there was a problem. The peanut was inside a hard shell. Sam did not know how to remove the shell. Sam tried to open the shell, but it was too hard. He was very slow and careful, but still could not open it. He was sad and did not know what to do. Then, Sam had an idea. He would ask his friend, the strong bird, for help. Sam went to the strong bird and asked for help. The strong bird was very kind and wanted to help Sam. The strong bird used his beak to remove the hard shell. Sam was very happy and could now eat the peanut. Sam thanked the strong bird for his help. They both shared the peanut and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a musician. He loved to play songs for everyone. He was very good at it. But he was also a bit weak. He could not lift heavy things or run very fast. One day, the musician went to play at a big party. Many people were there. They danced and laughed to his songs. The musician was very happy. But then, it started to rain. The rain made the ground wet and slippery. The musician was afraid he would fall. He did not want to get hurt. So he decided to stay inside where it was safe and dry. The people at the party were sad, but they understood. They knew the musician was weak and needed to be careful. And the musician kept playing his songs, making everyone happy from inside the house.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl called Jane. Jane often woke up early in the morning to enjoy the sunshine. She enjoyed taking long walks in the park, eating ice cream and playing games. One day, on her way to the park, Jane comes across a mysterious cave. She is curious and decides to explore what is inside. As she enters, she hears an old man calling out for help. He tells Jane that he has forgotten the way out and asks for her help. Jane is brave and decides to help the old man. After searching and searching, Jane finally finds a door at the end of the cave. The old man is happy and thanks Jane. As he steps out, he notices a bright sunrise. He looks at Jane and says, "Remember, sunshine will always help you when you need it". Jane smiles and waves goodbye as the old man leaves. A few weeks later, Jane is feeling very scared when she finds herself lost in the woods. Suddenly, she remembers the old man's words, and she has a feeling that the sunshine will help her find her way back home. She takes a deep breath and takes a brave step into the sunshine. Sure enough, she finds her way back home. Lastly, Jane realises that she no longer needs to be scared when she is lost. She knows that sunshine will always help her bravely navigate her way back home.

One day, a boy named Tim had a toy motor. He loved his toy motor because it was perfect. It was fast and fun to play with. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam wanted to play with the toy motor too. Tim said, "Be careful, Sam. The toy motor has a sharp part." Sam did not listen. He wanted to play with the toy motor right away. Tim knew something bad might happen. Sam played with the toy motor. But he touched the sharp part. "Ouch!" Sam cried. Tim was right. The sharp part hurt Sam's hand. Tim got a band-aid for Sam's hand. Sam said, "Thank you, Tim. I should have listened to you." From that day on, Sam always listened to his friend Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a horse named Jack. Jack loved to eat hay. Every day, the kind farmer would give Jack hay to eat. One day, Jack saw a big red ball. Jack thought the ball was dangerous. He was scared. Jack said, "Help! There is a dangerous ball!" The farmer came and saw the ball. The farmer said, "Don't worry, Jack. The ball is not dangerous. It is just a toy." The farmer moved the ball away. Jack was happy. The farmer gave Jack more hay to eat. Jack said, "Thank you for helping me." They both lived happily on the farm.
