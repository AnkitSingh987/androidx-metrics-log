Title: Tim the Boy and the Toy

One day, a boy named Tim found a cheap toy in his room. It was a missile toy. He wanted to play with it outside. So, he ran to the park with the toy. At the park, Tim met a friend named Sue. Sue saw the missile toy and said, "Wow! Can I play too?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play together!" They both got ready to release the missile. Tim showed Sue how to release the missile. They took turns and had a lot of fun. The cheap toy made them very happy. They played until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty bracelet that she loved very much. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. She wore her bracelet to show her friends. While playing, a big boy named Tom saw Lily's bracelet. He wanted it for his sister. Tom went to Lily and said, "Give me your bracelet!" Lily was scared but did not want to give her bracelet away. She said, "No, it's mine!" Tom was angry and demanded, "Give it to me now!" Lily started to cry and ran to her mom. Her mom saw that her hands were empty and asked, "Where is your bracelet?" Lily told her mom about Tom and how he took her bracelet. Lily's mom went to Tom and said, "You must give back the bracelet. It is not nice to take things from others." Tom felt bad and gave the bracelet back to Lily. From that day on, Tom learned to be kind and never took things from others again. Lily was happy to have her bracelet back and continued to play with her friends.

One day, a red bird named Tim went to search for a band. He wanted to sing with his friends. Tim flew up in the sky and looked down at the ground. He saw a big tree with many birds on it. Tim thought, "This must be the band!" Tim flew down to the tree and said, "Hi, I am Tim. Can I sing with you in your band?" The birds looked at him and said, "We are not a band. We are a family of birds. But we know where the band is. Follow us!" They all flew together to a big park. There, they saw a group of animals playing music. Tim was so happy to find the band. He started to sing with them. But then, something unexpected happened. The band stopped playing music and started to dance! Tim was surprised but he liked dancing too. So, he joined them and they all had a fun day dancing and singing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to be organized. She always put her toys in a big basket after she played with them. One day, Sue went to the park with her mom and took her basket with her. At the park, Sue met a boy named Tom. Tom saw Sue's basket and said, "Can I come and play with you?" Sue said, "Yes, but we have to put the toys back in the basket when we are done." Tom agreed, and they played together all afternoon. When it was time to go home, Sue and Tom tried to put the toys back in the basket. But they had played with so many toys that the basket was too full. The toys fell out and made a big mess. Sue and Tom felt sad because they could not keep the basket organized. Sue's mom said they had to leave the park and they couldn't take the toys with them. Sue and Tom learned that they should have been more careful with their toys.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to play all day. One day, while he was playing, he stumbled on a delicate flower. The flower was sad and cried, "Oh no, my pretty petals!" Momo felt very bad and said, "I'm sorry, little flower. I didn't see you there. Let me help you." Momo gently picked up the flower and took it to his mommy. She knew how to make things better. Mommy monkey looked at the flower and said, "Don't worry, Momo. We can fix the flower with love and care." They both took care of the flower, giving it water and sunshine. Soon, the delicate flower was happy and pretty again. Momo and the flower became best friends and played together every day.
