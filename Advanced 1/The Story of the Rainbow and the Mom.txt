Title: The Story of the Rainbow and the Mom

Once upon a time there was a mom and her three year old boy. The mom was sad and was crying. The little boy looked up and said "Mom, why do you weep?". The mom looked up at the sky and said, "I am crying because something important happened and its making me feel really sad". The little boy hugged his mom tight and said, "It's ok mom, I'll take care of you". The little boy was so kind and caring. He wanted to make his mom feel better, so he started to dance around and sing. It was a sunny day and the sky was bright and blue. As the little boy sang and danced, he looked up and saw a rainbow in the sky. He thought to himself that this was a sign of hope, and it made him feel better. He took his mom's hand and said, "Let's look at the rainbow and everything will be alright!”. The little boy was right. Looking at the rainbow, the mom's tears dried and she knew that all was going to be alright. She kissed her little boy and knew that he was so important to her. They smiled together and the mom felt thankful that her little boy cared for her so much.

Katie was a 3 year old girl and she loved sports. She tried lots of different kinds of sports but her favorite was soccer. She always felt so powerful and happy when she was playing soccer. One day, Katie was playing soccer with her brother and he said to her “Wow! You’re really powerful!” Katie stopped and thought for a moment. She really liked what her brother said and it made her feel really happy inside. She smiled and said to him “I think sports are really fun!” Her brother agreed, nodded his head and they both went back to playing. Katie and her brother played soccer for the rest of the afternoon. They laughed, ran, jumped and had lots and lots of fun. By the end of the day, Katie felt really proud and happy that she was a powerful sports player.

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there was a little mole. The mole was very healthy. He loved to dig and play in the dirt all day long. One sunny day, the mole met a big, friendly dog. The dog wagged his tail and said, "Hi, little mole! What are you doing?" The mole looked up and replied, "I am digging a new home." The dog was curious and asked, "Can I help you?" The mole thought for a moment and said, "Yes, you can help me by moving the big rocks." The dog was happy to help the mole. They worked together all day, moving rocks and digging in the dirt. By the end of the day, the mole had a new home. The mole and the dog became good friends. They played and helped each other every day. The garden was a happy place for both of them. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a big leopard with no hair lived in the woods. He was bald and sad because he was different from the other leopards. One day, he entered a cave he had never seen before. Inside the cave, he found a shiny hat. The leopard thought the hat was very pretty. The bald leopard put the hat on his head and went to show his friends. When he got to his friends, they all laughed and said, "Look at the bald leopard with the funny hat!" The leopard was sad, but he knew the hat was special. One day, it rained very hard. All the other leopards got wet and cold. But the bald leopard with the hat stayed dry and warm. His friends stopped laughing and said, "Wow, your hat is amazing! We're sorry for laughing at you." The bald leopard was happy, and from that day on, he and his friends played together and had fun, no matter how different they were.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved daisies. Every day, she would search for them in the field, filled with excitement. She knew that when she found some, she would pick them and squeeze them between her fingers until all of the petals fell off. One day, the little girl found a daisy that looked very cool. She couldn’t wait to take it home and squeeze it. But as she went to pick it, she heard a loud voice from behind her. “Don’t squeeze that daisy!” said the voice. The little girl turned around to see an old man. He had a big, white beard and a gentle smile. “Why not?” she asked. “Well,” replied the old man, “it’s the daisy’s home. If you take it away, it won’t be happy. Let it stay here and enjoy being in its own special place.” The little girl thought about what the old man said. She decided to leave the daisy in the field and watch as it swayed in the cool breeze. Instead of squeezing it, she gave the daisy a gentle hug.
