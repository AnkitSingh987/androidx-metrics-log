Title: Ben and Mia and the Sheep

Ben and Mia like to go to the ranch with Mom and Dad. The ranch has many animals that they can see and touch. They see cows, horses, sheep, pigs, chickens and ducks. Ben and Mia like to feed the animals with corn and hay. They also like to pet the animals and hear their sounds. One day, they see a new animal at the ranch. It is very big and chubby. It has long hair and horns. It is brown and white. Ben and Mia are curious. They ask Mom and Dad what it is. Mom and Dad say it is a bison. A bison is a kind of cow that lives in the wild. They say the bison is a guest at the ranch. He came from another place and he will go back soon. Ben and Mia want to get closer to the bison. They think he looks friendly and soft. They ask Mom and Dad if they can feed him and pet him. Mom and Dad say no. They say the bison is not used to people and he might be scared or angry. They say the bison needs space and respect. They say they can watch him from a distance and say hello. Ben and Mia listen to Mom and Dad. They stay away from the bison. They watch him eat grass and walk around. They say hello to him and wave. The bison looks at them and snorts. He does not seem to mind them. Ben and Mia smile. They think the bison is cool and interesting. They hope he likes the ranch and has a good time. They say goodbye to him when they go home. They wonder if they will see him again.

Once upon a time, Andrew and Megan were playing in the park. Andrew had his toy truck and Megan had her doll. They were having so much fun talking and laughing together. Suddenly, Andrew stumbled and the truck fell on the ground. Megan saw it and said, "Let's try to fix it together!" But Andrew was too stubborn and wouldn't listen. He just kept staring at the truck on the ground. Megan tried to get his attention, but he didn't budge. Megan knew she had to do something. She took out her doll and said, "Look, Andrew! I have a surprise for you!" Andrew stopped staring at the truck and looked at the doll. His face lit up and he smiled. They both picked up the toy truck and put it back together. Andrew and Megan played together all day long. They were both very happy to be together and were definitely not stubborn anymore!

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little dog named Max. Max loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Max found a big, red ball in his yard. Max ran up to the ball and gave it a big kick. The ball went flying through the air and made a loop in the sky. Max was so happy and excited to see the ball make a loop. Max kicked the ball again and again. Each time, the ball made a loop in the sky. Max played with the ball all day long. He was very tired, but he had so much fun.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was in her bedroom. She was getting ready to play with her toys. She had a big box of toys that she loved. Lucy was very excited to play and have fun. As Lucy started to play, something unexpected happened. A toy bear from the box began to talk! The bear said, "Hello, Lucy! I am a magic bear, and I am here to play with you." Lucy was very surprised but happy to have a new friend. They played and played, and then it was time for bed. Lucy was ready to sleep, but the bear was not. He said, "I have a secret to tell you, Lucy. I am not really a magic bear. I am your big brother, Tom, who found a magic toy that turned me into a bear!" Lucy laughed and hugged her brother. They were both happy to be together again.
