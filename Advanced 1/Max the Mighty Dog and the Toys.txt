Title: Max the Mighty Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to pack his toys in a big bag. He took his bag everywhere he went. One day, Max went to a place where he could learn. He wanted to learn math. Max was very excited to learn math because he thought it would make him even more mighty. Max worked very hard to learn math. He used his toys to help him count. Soon, Max became very good at math. Everyone was proud of Max, and he felt mighty and happy.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to play with her friend, Tom. They wanted to have a tea party. They sat down on the grass and put a small table between them. Lucy had a toy tea set. She put cups on the table and poured pretend tea. They were very happy. Tom said, "Lucy, let's kneel and pour more tea!" They both kneeled down and poured more pretend tea into the cups. But, Lucy found it difficult to kneel because her knees hurt. She tried her best to keep playing with Tom. Then, a big dog came running to them. The dog knocked over the small table and broke the toy tea set. Lucy and Tom were very sad. The tea party was over. They went home with their broken toys, feeling sad about their bad day.

Once there was a little bunny who lived in a big house. Every day, he would raise up on his hind legs to look out the window. He saw a wire in front of his house and he wanted to go play with it. But, the wire was very close to a busy street. So, his mommy said he could not go near it. The little bunny was so sad. He wanted to play with the wire. So, he decided to try and figure out a way to get the wire. He was very slow, but he was smart. He thought and thought about the wire, until finally he had an idea. He asked his mommy for some help and she agreed to help. They worked together for days and nights, until finally the bunny and his mommy had safely moved the wire closer to the house. Now, the bunny could play with it safely! He was so happy. He forgot all about the busy street and had lots of fun!

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Lucy. She was only three years old. One day, Lucy was looking at all of the exciting things in her house. She noticed a big, impressive seat in the corner of the room. Lucy thought it would be fun to sit on the seat, so she hopped on it. But, it was so big that her feet couldn't touch the ground. She decided to try to wipe the seat clean before she sat on it. So, Lucy wiped off the seat using her mischievous little hands. The seat was so big that it took Lucy a long time to wipe it. After a while, she had finally wiped the seat clean. Lucy was very pleased with her impressive job. She hopped up onto the seat and enjoyed the view from the top of it. Lucy was so proud of all the hard work she put in to cleaning the seat. The End.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to write. He would write on leaves with his tiny paws. One day, Sam found a big acorn. He was very happy. He wanted to show it to his friend, Tim the bird. "Tim, look at my big acorn!" said Sam. Tim looked at the acorn and said, "Oh no, Sam! That acorn is dead. It won't grow into a tree." Sam was sad. He wanted to see if he could make it grow anyway. Sam dug a hole and put the dead acorn in it. He covered it with dirt and waited. After some time, something unexpected happened. The dead acorn began to grow! Sam and Tim were very surprised. But then, a big wind came and blew the little tree away. Sam and Tim were very sad. They had never seen a tree grow from a dead acorn before, and now it was gone. The wind had taken their special tree away, and they couldn't find it. The forest was quiet and they missed their little tree.
