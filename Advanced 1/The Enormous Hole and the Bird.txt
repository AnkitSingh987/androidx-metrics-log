Title: The Enormous Hole and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was an enormous hole in the roof. The people who lived there were sad because rain would come in and make everything wet. They wanted to improve their home, but they didn't know how. One day, a big, strong bird saw the hole in the roof. The bird wanted to help the people, so it flew away to find something to fix the hole. The bird knew that it had to find the perfect thing to make the house better. The bird came back with a big, flat rock. It put the rock on the roof, and the hole was gone! The people were so happy that their home was improved. They thanked the big, strong bird for helping them. From that day on, the house was cozy and dry, and the people lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Wink. Wink liked to play and keep things. One day, Wink found a big red ball. He thought it was very fun to play with the ball. Wink played with the ball all day long. He would push it, roll it, and jump on it. Wink was very happy. He wanted to keep the ball forever. But one day, the ball rolled away from Wink. Wink chased the ball, but it rolled too fast. The ball went into the street. Wink did not see the big car coming. The car hit the ball and it popped. Wink was very sad. He could not keep his ball anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big town, lived a kind girl named Sally. She liked to help everyone. One day, she saw a little cat. The cat looked sad. Sally asked the cat, "Why are you sad, little cat?" The cat said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it, please?" Sally said, "I will help you, don't worry." They looked all around the town. They found the toy under a big tree. The cat was very happy. Sally said, "Always mind your toys, little cat." The cat said, "Thank you, Sally. I will." And they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Becky who wanted to help people. She wanted to know why some people were not feeling well so she asked a doctor if she could help. He said yes and did a test. He took her to a big lab and asked her to look at the test results. She saw all kinds of colors but the one that caught her eye was a sour one. She told the doctor it tasted sour and he smiled and said she was right. The doctor was very happy with Becky's work and said she could help him with a lot of other tests. Becky was so excited that she could help people, she worked on all the tests in the lab. She was eventually able to help figure out why some people were feeling sick. All the people in the lab said she did a fantastic job. Becky was very proud of herself. She never knew how she could help people with her special skills, but thanks to the doctor's test she knew now and she was so excited to use her skills.

Once upon a time, in a small room, there was a big blackboard. The sun was not shining, and the room was gloomy. On the blackboard, there were many colors. The colors were always happy and played with each other. One day, a new color came to the blackboard. The new color was not nice. He did not want to play with the other colors. The colors tried to be friends with the new color. They would rush to him and ask him to play, but he would say no. The new color made the other colors sad. The blackboard was not a happy place anymore. The colors tried to make the new color happy, but nothing worked. In the end, the new color left the blackboard. The colors were still sad, and the room stayed gloomy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Joey. He was a curious three year old who loved exploring his neighborhood. One day, Joey saw something truly remarkable. It was a black crane! Joey watched the crane as it slowly stepped across the lawn. He wanted to get closer so he took one step towards it. Suddenly, the crane stepped away. He wanted to follow it, so he took two steps closer. The crane stepped further away and Joey was disappointed. He took three steps and that's when the crane spread its wings and flew away. Joey wanted to catch up with the crane but he was too tired. He sat down to take a break. As he looked around, Joey noticed an old woman watching him from a nearby garden. She said, "Why did you want to follow the black crane, little one?" Joey didn't know how to answer, so he just stared at her silently. The old woman smiled and said, "The crane is a magical bird. It grants wishes to those who follow it. You have to be brave and keep on stepping if you want to reach it." Joey was surprised, but he was also excited. He started running so he could catch up with the crane and make his wish. It was hard, but Joey kept on stepping until he reached the crane and made his wish.
