Title: Lily the Girl and the Orange

One day, a girl named Lily and her mom went on a picnic. They put a big orange blanket on the grass. They had yummy food like apples, cookies, and juice. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Lily was very happy. Lily's mom said, "Let's play a game, Lily!" So they played catch with a big orange ball. They laughed and had lots of fun. Suddenly, the ball went into the bushes. Lily went to get it, but she found something else. In the bushes, Lily found a small orange cat. The cat was scared and hungry. Lily said, "Mom, look! Can we keep it, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can take it home and love it." The cat was happy to be with Lily and her mom. They all ate their picnic together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a big horse. This horse was very impressive. Everyone loved to watch the horse run and play. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to see the horse. Lucy watched the horse run in the big field. The horse was very fast and strong. Lucy clapped and laughed as the horse played. Lucy and the horse became best friends. They played together every day. Lucy would watch the horse and the horse would watch Lucy. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little pink ball. It loved to bounce all day long. One day, the pink ball went to the park to play. It saw a pretty dove sitting on a tree. The pink ball wanted to play with the dove, too. The pink ball started to bounce higher and higher. It wanted to reach the dove in the tree. The dove saw the pink ball and thought it looked fun. So, the dove flew down from the tree to play with the pink ball. They played and played, bouncing all around the park. But then, the pink ball bounced too high. It went up and up, and the dove tried to follow it. The pink ball got stuck in a tall tree, and the dove could not get it down. The pink ball and the dove were very sad. They could not play together anymore. The pink ball stayed in the tree, and the dove flew away.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. At the store, she saw a perfect lollipop. It was big and red. Amy asked her mom, "Can I have the lollipop, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but only if you are good." Amy was very good at the store. She helped her mom and did not run away. When they were done, her mom said, "You can have the lollipop now." Amy was so happy. She opened the lollipop and started to lick it. But then, something unexpected happened. A bird flew by and took the lollipop from Amy's hand! Amy was sad and started to cry. Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, we can go back to the store and get another one." So they went back to the store and got another perfect lollipop. This time, Amy held it tight and did not let go. She repeated to herself, "I will not let go of my lollipop!" And she enjoyed her sweet treat all the way home.

Once upon a time, there was a sun. The sun was big and bright. But one day, the sun felt broken. It was sad. All the animals and plants started to complain. They needed the sun to be happy and grow. They wanted the sun to be bright again. So, the animals and plants worked together. They made the sun feel loved. They sang songs and danced. The sun became happy again. It was no longer broken. Everyone was happy and the sun shined brightly again.

Once upon a time, there was an obedient dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toy trumpet. One day, Max and his trumpet went to the park. They had fun in the sun. Max would blow his trumpet and run around. While Max was playing, he saw a big tree. Max wanted to hide his trumpet under the tree. He put the trumpet down and went to play some more. Max ran and jumped and had lots of fun. Then, Max wanted to play with his trumpet again. He rushed back to the big tree. But, the trumpet was gone! Max was sad. He looked around and saw a squirrel. The squirrel had the trumpet in its mouth! The squirrel wanted to play too. Max and the squirrel played together with the trumpet, and they became good friends.
