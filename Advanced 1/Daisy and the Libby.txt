Title: Daisy and the Libby

Once upon a time, there were two best friends. One was called Daisy, and the other was called Libby. Daisy was very excited every day. She loved playing and having fun with Libby. One day, Libby asked Daisy, "Let's give each other a hug!" Daisy was so excited, she jumped up and gave Libby a big hug. Libby smiled and said, "That made me so happy!" They hugged all day long, until it was time to go to bed. Daisy was very sleepy and Libby said, "See you tomorrow!" But the next day, Libby did not come. Daisy asked her mom where Libby was. Her mom said, "We had to move away." Daisy was so upset that Libby was gone. Daisy was never as excited as when she was with Libby. She was so sad that Libby moved away. She never got to give Libby another hug.

Once upon a time, there was a little church in a small town. The church was old and broken. The door was hard to open and the roof had a big hole. People did not like to go inside because it was not nice. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the church. He saw the broken church and said, "I want to help fix the church." Tim tried to push the door, but it was too hard for him. A kind man saw Tim and said, "I can help you, Tim." Together, they opened the door and went inside. They tried to fix the church, but it was too broken. They could not fix the roof or the door. The kind man said, "I am sorry, Tim. We must leave the church like this. It is too broken." Tim was sad because they could not fix the church. The church stayed broken and people still did not like to go inside.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Bob. Bob loved to stretch in the sun. One day, he saw a shiny medal hanging on a tree. He wanted the medal, so he started to climb the tree. As he climbed, he met a bird named Sue. Sue said, "That medal is fake! I saw a dog put it there." Bob didn't care. He still wanted the shiny medal. So, he climbed higher and higher. Finally, Bob reached the top of the tree. He stretched out his paw and grabbed the fake medal. He was so happy! He climbed down and showed Sue. They both laughed and played with the shiny medal all day long.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Grandma. She lived in a small house with a big garden. Grandma loved to grow flowers and play with her cat, Mittens. One day, Grandma was outside when she saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her hands were dirty. Grandma said, "Don't worry, I have soap to help you." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Grandma, I hope the soap will clean my hands." Grandma and Lily went inside the house. They used the soap and washed their hands. Lily's hands were clean and she was happy. They went outside and played with Mittens in the garden. Grandma, Lily, and Mittens had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a weird castle. The castle was big and had many colors. Inside the castle, lived a kind king and queen. They had a little boy named Sam. One sunny day, Sam went to the park. He saw a girl named Lily. Lily was playing with a big red ball. Sam wanted to play too. He walked up to Lily and said, "Hi, my name is Sam. Can we share your ball and play together?" Lily smiled and said, "Sure, Sam! We can share my ball and play together." They played with the ball and had lots of fun. They laughed and ran around the park. After a while, they became tired and sat down under a big tree. Sam told Lily about the weird castle he lived in. Lily wanted to see it too. So, Sam took her to the castle. They played together in the big, colorful castle. They shared their toys and had a great time. From that day on, Sam and Lily became best friends and always shared their toys and played together.
