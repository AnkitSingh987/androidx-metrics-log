Title: Lily the Girl and the Kitten

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her makeup. One day, she found a small, gentle kitten outside her house. The kitten was lost and scared. Lily said to the kitten, "Don't worry, I will rescue you!" She took the kitten inside and gave it some food. But then, Lily had a bad idea. She wanted to put makeup on the kitten to make it pretty. The kitten did not like the makeup. It hissed and tried to run away. Lily got upset and started to cry. The kitten ran out of the house and never came back. Lily was sad that her new friend was gone.

Lily and Ben were bored. They wanted to do something fun. They saw a big bowl and a spoon on the table. They saw some flour, sugar, eggs and milk in the kitchen. They had an idea. "Let's make a cake!" Lily said. "Yay, cake!" Ben said. They put some flour, sugar, eggs and milk in the bowl. They used the spoon to mix them. They mix, mix, mix until the batter was smooth. They poured the batter in a pan. They put the pan in the oven. They set the timer for 20 minutes. They waited for the cake to bake. They smelled something yummy. They heard a ding. The timer was done. They ran to the oven. They opened the door. They saw a big, fluffy, golden cake. "Wow, it looks so good!" Lily said. "Can we eat it now?" Ben said. They took the cake out of the oven. They put it on a plate. They cut a slice for each of them. They took a bite. It was warm and sweet and yummy. "Mmm, this is the best cake ever!" Lily said. "We did it!" Ben said. They smiled and hugged. They ate the whole cake. They were happy. They were not bored anymore.

One day, Jenny and her daddy were walking through the park together. Daddy told Jenny to be careful when they walked by the water. "Be careful Jenny," her daddy said. Jenny nodded and said, "Yes Daddy." Jenny loved playing in the park. She especially liked to go down to the water and watch the fish swim around. But, Daddy was always careful and kept one hand on her back, so she didn't get too close. Jenny saw a big rock nearby and wanted to go over and see it. Daddy replied, "Okay, but don't go too close to the water." Jenny said, "Yes Daddy," and skipped over to the rock. She touched it with one finger trying to be careful. Suddenly, water started flooding around the rock and quickly rose up to Jenny's waist! She was so scared and tried to swim back to Daddy, but the water was too strong and she couldn't get back. Daddy tried to reach her, but it was too late. Jenny was swept away by the water. The story ends with a bad ending. Daddy's warning to be careful foreshadowed the bad outcome, proving that it's important to pay attention to warning signs.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had an elderly friend named Mr. Brown. They liked to talk and play together. One day, Mr. Brown told Tim a secret. He said, "I believe there is a hidden treasure in my garden. But, I need your help to find it." Tim was excited and said, "Yes, I want to help you find the treasure!" They went to the garden and started to dig. They dug and dug, but they could not find anything. Tim started to feel sad. Mr. Brown said, "Don't worry, we will find the truth soon." The next day, they tried again. This time, they found a box. They opened the box, and inside was a beautiful toy car. Tim was so happy! Mr. Brown said, "I knew we would find the treasure. The truth is, I hid this toy car for you because I believe you are a very special friend." Tim hugged Mr. Brown and said, "Thank you for the treasure and for being my friend." They played with the toy car and had a lot of fun together. From that day on, Tim knew that Mr. Brown's belief and the truth were always there to guide him.
