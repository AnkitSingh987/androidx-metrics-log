Title: Tom and Mary and the Scarf

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Tom and Mary. Tom was wearing a thin scarf and Mary wanted to try it on. Tom said to Mary, "No! This is my scarf." Mary said, "Please let me wear it even for just a few minutes!" Tom thought about it and then he said, "Alright, you can wear it, but only for a few minutes." Mary put on the thin scarf and she felt so happy. She went outside to play. Suddenly, a strong wind came and the scarf flew away with the wind. Mary tried to run after it, but she couldn't catch it. She started to cry. Tom came out of the house and saw Mary crying. He hugged her and said, "Don't worry. I'll get you a new scarf soon." Mary smiled and they went inside the house together.

One day, a little boy named Tom found a blue tie on the floor. He picked it up and looked around to see who it belonged to. He saw a man standing by a tree with a sad face. Tom walked up to the man and asked, "Is this your tie?" The man looked at the blue tie and started to cry. He said, "Yes, that is my tie. I lost it and I was so sad. Thank you for finding it." Tom gave the tie back to the man and he stopped crying. Tom and the man became friends. They played games and had fun together. At the end of the day, the man said, "Thank you for helping me and being my friend." Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome. Friends help each other."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play in her yard. One day, she found a rough, brown bag. She opened the bag and saw many oats inside. Sue thought, "I will cover these oats and see what happens." The next day, Sue went to check on the oats. She uncovered the bag and saw that the oats were gone! Instead, there was a big, beautiful flower in the bag. Sue was very happy and surprised. Sue showed the flower to her mom. Her mom said, "Sue, this flower is very special. It can talk!" Sue was even more surprised. The flower said, "Thank you for helping me grow, Sue." From that day on, Sue and the flower became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a large mug. The mug was very special because it could shine. It was the favorite mug of a little girl named Lily. She loved the mug so much and always used it for her drinks. One sunny day, Lily took her shining mug outside to play. She filled it with water and watched the sun make it shine even more. She looked at the mug and smiled, because it was so pretty. As Lily played, her friend Tom came to join her. They had fun playing with the large, shining mug. They pretended it was a magical cup that could make wishes come true. They laughed and played until the sun went down, and the shining mug helped them have a wonderful day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. Lisa had a clean, shiny penny. She loved her penny very much. One day, her friend Tom came over to play. Tom saw Lisa's penny and asked, "Can you lend me your penny, please?" Lisa thought about it and said, "Okay, but you have to give it back." Tom took the penny and said, "Thank you!" Later, Tom's mom called him to go home. Tom forgot to give the penny back to Lisa. Lisa was sad. She thought her penny was gone forever. The next day, Lisa went to Tom's house to ask for her penny back. But when she got there, she saw something unexpected. There was a big, shiny pile of pennies! Tom said, "I'm sorry I forgot to give your penny back. But look, I put it in my piggy bank and it turned into many pennies!" Lisa was so happy. She got her penny back and even more! Now, Lisa and Tom had lots of pennies to share and play with. And they were the best of friends forever.
