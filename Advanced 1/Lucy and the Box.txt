Title: Lucy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a lucky apron that she wore every day. Lucy loved to laugh and play with her friends. One day, Lucy and her friends went to the park. They played fun games and laughed a lot. Then, Lucy saw a big box under a tree. She was excited and called her friends to see what was in the box. When they opened the box, balloons flew out! They were so surprised and laughed even more. Lucy and her friends played with the balloons all day long. They knew it was Lucy's lucky apron that brought them this fun surprise. And from that day on, they always remembered the day the lucky apron brought them the box of balloons.

One day, a little dog named Max found a big piece of meat. Max was very excite. He wanted to take the meat home to share with his old friend, Cat. As Max walked, he met his friend, Bird. "Hi Max, what do you have?" asked Bird. "I have meat for Cat and me," Max said. Bird was happy for Max and Cat. When Max got home, he gave the meat to Cat. Cat was very happy too. They ate the meat together and had a great day. Max and Cat were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a creative girl named Amy. She liked to solve problems and make things. One day, Amy found a whip in her toy box. She thought of a fun way to use it. Amy took the whip outside to play. She saw a big tree with a high branch. Amy wanted to get a toy down from the branch. She had a plan. She would use the whip to solve her problem. Amy swung the whip up at the branch. The whip wrapped around the branch and pulled the toy down. Amy was happy she solved her problem. She knew she was creative and could solve anything.

---

Lisa loved to bake with her mom. They made cakes, cookies, pies and bread. Lisa liked to mix, pour, roll and cut the dough. She also liked to lick the spoon and the bowl. Baking was a luxury for Lisa, because she did not have many toys or books. One day, Lisa and her mom baked a tall cake for Lisa's birthday. It had four layers, chocolate, vanilla, strawberry and lemon. It also had cream, sprinkles and candles on top. Lisa was very happy and proud of the cake. She wanted to show it to her friends. But when Lisa and her mom went to the park, they saw a boy who was crying. He had no cake, no presents, no friends. He was very sad and lonely. Lisa felt sorry for him. She asked her mom if they could share their cake with him. Her mom said yes, because she was kind and generous. Lisa and her mom went to the boy and gave him a big slice of cake. They also gave him a hug and a smile. The boy was surprised and grateful. He thanked them and said he had never tasted such a delicious cake. He also said he had never met such nice people. He asked if he could be their friend. Lisa and her mom said yes, of course. Lisa, her mom and the boy ate the cake together. They also played, laughed and talked. Lisa was very happy and proud of herself. She learned that baking was a luxury, but sharing was a joy. She also learned that being kind and generous was better than being tall and rich. She had the best birthday ever.

One day, a generous cat named Tom went to the store. Tom wanted to provide a gift for his friend, a little bird named Tim. Tom looked around the store and saw a stamp. He thought the stamp was a great gift for Tim. Tom bought the stamp and went to Tim's house. When Tom gave the stamp to Tim, he was very happy. They played with the stamp all day, putting it on paper and making fun pictures. In the end, Tom and Tim had a great day together. They were thankful for the generous gift and the fun times it provided. They knew they would always be best friends.
