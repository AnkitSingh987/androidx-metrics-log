Title: Tim the Bunny and the Squirrel

One day, a little bunny named Tim was playing in the yard. He saw a long wire on the ground. Tim thought it was boring and wanted to play with something else. He looked around and saw his friend, a squirrel named Sally. "Hi Sally!" Tim said. "Do you want to play with me?" Sally looked at Tim and then at the wire. "Okay, Tim! Let's hurry and make this wire fun to play with," she said. Together, Tim and Sally picked up the wire and ran around the yard. They made a big circle with the wire and jumped in and out of it. They had so much fun that they forgot the wire was boring. They played all day and then went home to rest.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to take pictures with his camera. He would take pictures of his toys, his pets, and his family. One day, his mom encouraged him to take a picture of a big tree in the park. Tom went to the park with his camera. He saw the big tree and wanted to take a picture. He saw a squirrel on the tree and said, "Stay still, squirrel. I will take your picture too." The squirrel stayed still so Tom could take the picture. As Tom was taking the picture, he noticed his camera strap was very tight around his neck. It started to hurt him. He tried to loosen the strap, but it was stuck. Tom began to cry. A grown-up saw Tom and tried to help, but they couldn't fix the strap. Tom's camera was broken, and he went home sad with a red mark on his neck.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Toby. Toby loved to explore the world around him. One day, Toby was walking through the park, when he discovered a shiny trumpet. He picked up the trumpet and blew loudly. Toby was very excited when he discovered he could make different sounds with the trumpet. Just then, Toby's mum shouted over to him. Toby felt guilty as he knew he wasn't meant to take things that didn't belong to him. Mum said, "Toby, why did you take the trumpet? You know it wasn't yours!" Toby hung his head and said, "I'm sorry, mum. I just wanted to discover what sound it could make." Mum smiled and said, "That's ok, Toby. I'm glad that you were so curious. Next time, just ask before you take something." Toby nodded and from then on he made sure to always ask before he discovered something new.

Once there was a little girl named Julie. She wanted to go on an adventure so she planned her journey. She needed to find something to take her on her journey but the only thing she could find was a cheap old car. She was determined and excited so she got in the car and started to drive. As she drove along the road, she started to feel tired. She knew she had to keep going, so she started to stretch her legs to keep her awake. She kept going and going, but the old car began to make funny noises. Suddenly, the car broke down and wouldn't start again. Julie was stuck in the middle of nowhere and had no way to get back home. Julie sat there all alone, feeling sad and scared. She started to cry. She wanted to start her adventure but instead she had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hockey with his friends. One day, he saw a perfect spot to play hockey near his home. Tim called his friend Sam and said, "Let's play hockey at the perfect spot I found!" Sam agreed, and they went to the spot with their hockey sticks and ball. They played for hours and had lots of fun. But then, a big dog came and took their ball. Tim and Sam were scared. They knew they had to surrender the ball to the dog. The dog ran away with the ball, and they were sad. The next day, Tim and Sam went to the same spot to play hockey. This time, they brought a new ball. They played and had more fun than before. Tim said to Sam, "We won't let the dog take our ball again!" And they played hockey happily ever after.
