Title: Lily and the Grass

Lily likes to dance. She dances every day in her room. She dances with her dolls, her teddy and her cat. She dances fast and slow, happy and sad, big and small. She likes to try different dances. One day, Lily goes to the park with her mom. She sees some kids dancing on the grass. They have music and costumes. They dance together and make shapes. Lily thinks they look fun. She wants to join them. She asks her mom if she can dance with them. Her mom says yes, but she has to ask them first. Lily goes to the kids and says hello. She tells them she likes to dance. She asks if she can dance with them. The kids are nice. They say yes, they like to dance too. They ask Lily what kind of dance she knows. Lily says she knows many different dances. She shows them some of her dances. The kids clap and smile. They say they like her dances. They tell Lily they are learning a new dance. They say it is a dance from another place. They say it is different from their dances. They ask Lily if she wants to learn it. Lily says yes, she likes to learn new things. They say they will teach her. They say they recommend it. They say it is fun and easy. Lily is happy. She learns the new dance with the kids. She follows their steps and moves. She listens to their music and words. She likes the new dance. She thinks it is different and fun. She dances with the kids on the grass. She makes new friends. She has a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray cat. The cat lived in a big house with a nice family. The family loved the cat very much. The cat liked to play with the family and sleep in the warm sun. One day, the little gray cat saw a big ball of yarn. The cat wanted to play with the yarn. The cat jumped and played with the yarn all day. The yarn was soft on the cat's skin. The cat was very happy. After playing, the little gray cat was tired. The cat wanted to sleep. The cat found a soft and warm bed. The cat curled up and went to sleep. The family saw the cat and smiled. They were happy that the little gray cat was part of their family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that could increase in size. He loved to make his toy big and small. One day, Tim took his toy to the park to play. He saw a big cooler there. He was curious about what was inside. Tim opened the cooler and found many yummy treats inside. He wanted to take some, but he knew it was not nice to take things that were not his. Tim felt a bit ignorant about what to do. He decided to wait and see if someone would come for the cooler. Soon, a girl named Lily came to the park. She saw Tim near her cooler and was upset. She thought Tim was trying to take her treats. Tim told her he was not taking anything, and he was just waiting for the owner. Lily felt bad for thinking Tim was not nice. They both learned not to be ignorant and became friends. Tim showed Lily his toy that could increase, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat spaghetti. One day, his mom made him an interesting plate of spaghetti. It was very long and twisty. Tim was very happy. As Tim started to eat his spaghetti, something unexpected happened. The spaghetti began to part in the middle. Tim was very surprised. He looked closer and saw a tiny door hidden in the spaghetti. He opened the door and found a small toy car inside. Tim showed his mom the toy car he found in his spaghetti. His mom was very surprised too. She told him that she did not know how the car got there. Tim and his mom laughed and thought it was a funny twist. They played with the toy car and ate the spaghetti together.
