Title: The Green Frog and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The frog liked to slide down a big hill. One day, the frog saw a candle on the hill. The frog was happy and wanted to slide with the candle. The frog and the candle started to slide down the hill. They went very fast. Then, something unexpected happened. The candle began to talk. The frog was very surprised. The talking candle said, "I am a magic candle. I will give you a wish." The frog thought and said, "I want to slide faster." The candle made the wish come true. Now, the frog and the candle slide down the hill faster and have more fun.

Once upon a time, there was an old cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house near a long road. Every night, Tom would sleep and dream about big things. One day, Tom met a little bird on the road. The bird said, "Hi Tom! What do you dream about at night?" Tom replied, "I dream about big things, like a big house and a big cake." The little bird liked Tom's dreams. They became best friends and played together every day. They would dream together about the big things they wanted. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little rabbit called Alice. Alice was very alert and liked to explore the forest around her home. One day, as she was hopping around, Alice noticed a funny looking frame in the corner of the clearing. She was curious and decided to take a closer look. Alice hopped over and saw that the frame was filled with lots of different objects. On the bottom she spotted some tiny pebbles and two pretty stones. She wanted to name all the things and was just starting to think up names when she heard a noise. Alice froze and was very alert. “Who’s there?” she asked, starting to feel a little scared. “It’s me, a fox,” answered the voice. Alice let out a sigh of relief. The fox explained that the frame was called a treasure box and the things inside were all the things he had found in his travels. Alice thanked the fox for showing her the frame and the fox thanked Alice for being so alert and keeping him safe. From then on, Alice always named the things she found on her adventures and kept them safe in her own treasure frame.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big box in her room. Lily loved to put her toys in the box. One day, she saw a small cat outside her window. The cat looked sad and cold. Lily wanted to help the cat feel better. Lily said, "Come in, cat! I will make you warm and happy." She opened the door and the cat came inside. Lily took the cat to her room. She opened the big box and took out some toys. Then, she put a soft blanket in the box for the cat to sleep on. The cat jumped in the box and felt warm and cozy. Lily looked at the cat in the box and said, "You look too tight in there. Let me take out more toys." She took out more toys, and the cat had more space. The cat felt very happy and safe in the box. Lily and the cat played together all day. From that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She was very adventurous. Lucy loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Lucy's mommy and daddy told her they were going to a marriage. Lucy was so excite! She put on her pretty dress and her shiny shoes. At the marriage, Lucy danced and laughed with her friends. They played fun games and ate yummy cake. It was a happy day for everyone.

Sara was a little girl who liked to point at things. She pointed at the birds in the sky, the flowers in the garden, and the toys in her room. She liked to ask her mom and dad what they were and learn new words. One day, Sara saw a shiny thing in the corner of her room. She pointed at it and asked, "What is that?" Her mom and dad did not hear her. They were busy in the kitchen. Sara was persistent. She wanted to know what the shiny thing was. She got out of her bed and walked to the corner of her room. The shiny thing was a scissors. Sara did not know what a scissors was. She picked it up and felt the sharp blades. She thought they were pretty. She pointed them at her face and looked at them. She wanted to see how they worked. She opened and closed them. She did not see the danger. The scissors slipped from her hand and cut her eye. Sara screamed in pain and fear. Blood dripped from her face. Her mom and dad heard her and ran to her room. They saw what happened and were very scared and sad. They called for help and took Sara to the hospital. Sara lost her eye and could not see well anymore. She wished she had not pointed at the shiny thing. She learned a hard lesson.
