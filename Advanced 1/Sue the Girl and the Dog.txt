Title: Sue the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a tidy house with her mom, dad, and dog, Max. Sue loved to play with Max in the yard. One day, Sue and Max saw a band playing music in the park. They went to watch the band. The band was happy and waved at Sue and Max. Sue and Max waved back. They danced and clapped to the music. After the band finished playing, Sue and Max went home. They told mom and dad about the fun time they had. Mom and dad were happy for Sue and Max. They all had a big hug and went to bed.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a small house near the woods with her mommy and daddy. Amy loved to play outside, especially near the trees and flowers. One sunny day, Amy met a friendly squirrel in the woods. The squirrel said, "Hi, Amy! I have some special minerals. Would you like some?" Amy was very happy and said, "Yes, please!" The squirrel offered her the minerals, and Amy took them with a big smile. Amy knew her best friend, Tim, would like the minerals too. So she went to his house and said, "Tim, I have a gift for you!" Tim was excited and asked, "What is it?" Amy showed him the minerals and said, "These are special minerals from my squirrel friend! They are available for you too." Tim was very happy and thanked Amy. They played together with the minerals all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a magic toy that could shrink. One day, Lucy found a small space behind her bed. She thought it was a secret place. Lucy said to her toy, "Can you shrink me, please? I want to go into the secret space." The toy nodded and made Lucy very small. She went into the space and found a tiny, honest bug. The bug said, "Hello, Lucy! Thank you for visiting my home." Lucy and the bug talked and became good friends. After a while, Lucy asked her toy to make her big again. She said goodbye to her new friend and went back to her room. From that day on, Lucy and the honest bug would play together in the secret space. They had lots of fun, and Lucy was always happy to share her magic toy with her little friend.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play together at school. Today, they have to study for a test. The test is about animals and their sounds. Sara and Ben sit at a table with their books. Sara points to a picture of a cow. "What does a cow say?" she asks Ben. Ben thinks hard. He remembers the sound. "A cow says moo!" he says. Sara smiles. "Good job, Ben! Now, what does a duck say?" Ben knows this one too. "A duck says quack!" he says. Sara and Ben are happy. They know the sounds of the animals. They think they will do well on the test. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is the rain. The rain is falling hard on the roof. The rain makes a lot of noise. Sara and Ben cannot hear each other. Sara tries to ask Ben another question. But Ben cannot hear her. He says, "What? What did you say?" Sara repeats the question. But Ben still cannot hear her. He says, "I can't hear you, Sara! The rain is too loud!" Sara and Ben are sad. They want to study, but they cannot. They do not like the rain. The rain is ruining their study time. They look out the window. They see the raindrops on the glass. They see the puddles on the ground. They see the clouds in the sky. They wish the rain would stop. They wish the sun would come out. They wish they could have a perfect day. A perfect day to study and play.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She felt gloomy one rainy day. She wanted to play outside, but it was wet and cold. So, Lisa decided to stay inside her house. Lisa had an idea to make her day more fun. She thought, "I will create a new friend!" So, Lisa found some cloth, buttons, and string. She made a doll with a big smile on its face. Lisa named her new friend Daisy. Lisa and Daisy played all day. They had tea parties and played dress-up. Soon, the gloomy day was not gloomy anymore. Lisa was happy she made a new friend. They played together every day, even when the sun came out again.
