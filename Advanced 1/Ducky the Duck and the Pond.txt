Title: Ducky the Duck and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky had a long neck and liked to play with his friends in the pond. They would swim, splash, and have lots of fun together. One day, Ducky's mom warned him to be careful near the edge of the pond. She was serious because she didn't want him to get hurt. Ducky listened to his mom and promised to stay safe. Ducky and his friends played near the middle of the pond, away from the edge. They had a great time and Ducky's long neck helped him reach the yummy plants in the water. At the end of the day, Ducky knew that listening to his mom's warning kept him and his friends safe and happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a cold day. A boy named Tim and a girl named Sue wanted to play outside. They put on their warm clothes and went to the park. At the park, they found a big box. Tim said, "What's in the box?" Sue said, "I don't know. Let's open it!" They opened the box and found fireworks inside. They were very excited and decided to measure the fireworks to see how big they were. As they were measuring the fireworks, a little dog came running up to them. The dog barked and wagged its tail. Suddenly, the dog bumped into the box and the fireworks started to go off! Tim and Sue were surprised and laughed as they watched the beautiful colors light up the sky. The cold day became a fun and special day for them.

One day, a boy named Tim got a new shirt. The new shirt was red and very pretty. Tim was very happy and wanted to show his friend, Sam. When Tim went to Sam's house, Sam saw the new shirt and said, "Wow, Tim! Your new shirt is very pretty!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sam. I like it too!" After playing, Tim and Sam felt tired. They sat under a big tree to relax. Tim looked at his new shirt and said, "I am happy we played today." Sam smiled and said, "Me too, Tim. Let's play again tomorrow!" And they both relaxed under the tree, happy with their day.

Once there was a little girl called Tess. She was twirl-ing around when she noticed something in the corner of the room - a big, shiny mirror. Tess was very curious and wanted to take a closer look. She walked up to the mirror and saw how big and glossy it was. She asked, "who is that?". Suddenly she heard a voice coming from the mirror. It said, "I'm the mirror, Tess! Look at yourself in me and you will see". Tess smiled and insisted on taking a peek. But when she looked at herself, she noticed something strange. It wasn't Tess looking back at her. It was a very serious-looking princess! She was shocked to see the princess, but the princess was very friendly. She said, "Hello Tess, I will help you with your twirl-ing. Let's dance together!" Tess was so excited, she twirled and danced with the princess in the mirror. They had such a great time! Tess thanked the princess when they were done, and the princess smiled and waved goodbye. Tess watched her go in the mirror and waved back. She then twirled off, happy and sparkly.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. One day, he found an interesting tree. It was big and had many branches. Tim wanted to climb the tree. As Tim climbed the tree, he saw a funny skeleton. The skeleton was not scary. It was made of sticks and leaves. Tim thought it was a fun friend to play with. So, he talked to the skeleton and they became friends. Tim and the skeleton played in the tree all day. They climbed up and down, and they laughed a lot. When it was time to go home, Tim said goodbye to his new friend. He knew he would come back to the interesting tree to play again.
