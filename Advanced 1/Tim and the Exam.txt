Title: Tim and the Exam

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a big exam at school. He did not want to take the exam, so he asked his mom, "Please, can I stay home?" His mom said, "No, you must go to school and take the exam." At lunch, Tim saw a big, disgusting bug in his food. He did not want to eat it. He showed it to his friends, and they all said, "Ew, that is so disgusting!" Tim was sad and did not eat his lunch. After lunch, Tim went to take his exam. He felt hungry and tired. He tried his best, but he did not do well on the exam. Tim's mom was not happy when she saw his bad grade. Tim learned that he should always try his best and ask for help when he needs it.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird named Bob. Bob loved to balance on tree branches. He was very good at it. One day, Bob was very hungry. He wanted to find lunch. Bob flew high and low, looking for food. He saw a big, red fruit on a tree. "That's my lunch!" he thought. Bob flew to the tree and tried to balance on a branch. But this branch was different. It was very thin and weak. As Bob tried to balance, the branch broke! Bob fell down, but he was okay. He learned that not all branches are the same. The moral of the story is to be careful and not to judge things only by their looks.

Once upon a time, there was a big slide in the park. Many kids would come and play on it every day. One day, a little girl named Amy went to play on the slide too. She was very excited to go up and down on the slide. As Amy was waiting for her turn, a selfish boy named Tom came to the slide. He didn't want to wait and tried to slip in front of Amy. But Amy didn't let him do that. She told Tom, "We all have to wait our turn to play on the slide." Tom didn't like waiting, but he knew Amy was right. So, he waited with the other kids. When it was finally his turn, he went down the slide very fast. He had so much fun that he forgot about being selfish. From that day on, Tom learned to share and wait his turn, and he became friends with Amy. They played together on the slide every day, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown dog. The dog had a tiny flea friend. They lived in a big yard with lots of flowers. The dog and the flea liked to play together. They were very happy. One day, the dog's family wanted to celebrate. They did not want the flea at the party. The dog's family tried to make the flea go away. They put water on the dog. The flea was scared. The dog did not want to lose his friend. The dog had an idea. He ran to the flowers in the yard. The flea jumped onto a flower. The dog's family did not see the flea anymore. They were happy. The dog and the flea could still play together. The dog and the flea celebrated together in the flowers. They were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to speak with her mother every day. They would sit under the light of the sun and talk about all the fun things they did together. One day, Amy's mother said, "I have a secret for you. When the moon is out and the sky is dark, we will go on a special adventure." Amy was very excited and could not wait for the night to come. When the moon came out, Amy and her mother went outside. They walked and walked until they reached a big tree. Amy's mother said, "Close your eyes and count to ten." Amy did as she was told. When she opened her eyes, she saw a big, bright light in the sky. It was a shooting star! Amy's mother had known it would happen, and they both made a wish together. The unexpected adventure made Amy very happy, and she knew her mother was full of surprises.
