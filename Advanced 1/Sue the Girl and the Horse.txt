Title: Sue the Girl and the Horse

One day, a girl named Sue went to a ranch with her mom. Sue was anxious because it was her first time at a ranch. There were many animals like cows, horses, and pigs. Sue liked the animals and wanted to meet them. Sue's mom said, "Let's open the gate and see the cows." Sue opened the gate and the cows came out. They were big and made loud "moo" sounds. Sue thought the cows were funny. Next, Sue saw a horse. The horse said, "Neigh!" Sue said, "Hello, horse! I like your big teeth." The horse was happy to meet Sue. They played together and had a fun day at the ranch.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a clever driver named Tom. Tom drove a big yellow bus. Every day, he took kids to school and back home. Tom liked his job and always had a big smile on his face. One day, a new kid named Timmy got on the bus. Timmy was scared because he did not know where to sit. Tom saw Timmy and said, "I recommend the seat by the window, Timmy. You can see all the fun things outside." Timmy smiled and sat down. As they drove to school, Tom and Timmy talked and laughed. They saw dogs, cats, and birds outside. When they got to school, Timmy said, "Thank you, Tom! You are a very clever driver." Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Timmy. Have a great day at school!" And they did this every day, becoming good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Lucy. Lucy liked to take a tour around her big yard every day. She would walk and walk, looking at the trees and the flowers. One sunny day, while Lucy was on her tour, she saw a big hole in the ground. She wanted to look inside but she was scared. Lucy decided to remain near the hole and wait to see if anything would come out. Suddenly, a little mouse popped out of the hole! Lucy was very surprised. She thought the mouse would be scared of her, but it wasn't. The mouse said, "Hi, I'm Molly! Want to be friends?" Lucy was so happy to make a new friend. From that day on, Lucy and Molly played together every day during Lucy's tour.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a hurt bird on the ground. The bird had a broken wing and couldn't fly. Sue wanted to help the bird, so she picked it up gently. Sue looked around and saw a big leaf. She thought the leaf could help the bird. She put the leaf on the bird's wing like a band-aid. The bird looked at Sue with happy eyes. Sue's friend, Tim, walked by and saw Sue with the bird. He wanted to play, but Sue didn't want to ignore the hurt bird. So, Tim helped Sue take care of the bird. They both felt good helping their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a lively ostrich named Ollie. Ollie lived in a big, hot place where the sun was always shining. Ollie had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, Ollie and his friends found a big puddle of water. They all jumped in and splashed around. They had so much fun and got all wet. But soon, the sun made the water dry up. Ollie and his friends were sad that the puddle was gone, but they knew they could find more fun things to do. They ran and played in the dry, hot place until the sun went down. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Emily. Emily loved to see the sunrise every morning, so every day she would get out of bed as soon as she could and go outside to watch the sun come up in the sky. One morning, Emily was so excited to see the sunrise, she forgot to put on her shoes! She ran outside, barefoot on the cold grass, and saw a very fair sunrise. "Wow!" said Emily. "That is so pretty!" Suddenly, Emily felt something strange. She reached down and touched the grass. It felt cold and wet! Emily started to laugh. She had forgotten her shoes, but then remembered that she would still get to enjoy the bright and fair sunrise every morning.
