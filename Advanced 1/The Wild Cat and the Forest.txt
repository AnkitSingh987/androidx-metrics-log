Title: The Wild Cat and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a wild cat. The wild cat liked to run and play all day long. He had many friends in the forest, like the big bear and the small rabbit. They all had fun together. One day, the wild cat saw a pretty house. He wanted to go inside and see what was there. But the other animals told him to remain in the forest. They said it was not safe for a wild cat to go into the pretty house. The wild cat listened to his friends and stayed in the forest. He remained wild and free. He continued to play with the big bear and the small rabbit. The wild cat was happy that he stayed with his friends in the distant forest.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. Tim saw a big tree with a light blue bird on it. He wanted to get a better view, so he went closer to the tree. The bird saw Tim and said, "Please don't disturb me, I am trying to sleep." But Tim wanted to see the bird up close. He started to climb the tree, even though the bird asked him not to. This made the bird very sad and upset. The bird flew away to find a new tree to sleep in. As Tim reached the top of the tree, he saw something unexpected. There was a big nest with bright, shiny eggs in it. Tim realized that the bird was trying to protect her eggs. He felt bad for disturbing the bird and her nest. Tim climbed down the tree and went home, promising to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time there was a brave little girl. She was out walking and it was very cold. She kept on walking until she saw something that made her gasp. Above the tree tops was a beautiful the rainbow. The little girl was so excited that she ran up to the rainbow. When she reached the end of the rainbow all she saw was a big pile of gold coins. Then suddenly the gold coins vanished! The little girl was so shocked, she didn't know what to do. Suddenly, a voice spoke out of the sky. "Be brave my child" the voice said. "You were brave enough to come here, so I will reward you with something special." Then the little girl saw something amazing. A beautiful golden elephant appeared in front of her. The little girl was so happy. She thanked the voice with all her heart and hugged the elephant. Then the elephant vanished. The little girl never forgot the special gift she was given.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird named Sunny. Sunny lived in a big tree with his family. Every day, he would fly around and play with his friends. One day, Sunny woke up and saw a thick mist all around the tree. He couldn't see anything, but he wanted to play with his friends. Sunny said to his mom, "Mom, can I go play with my friends in the mist?" His mom replied, "Be careful, Sunny. The mist makes it hard to see. Don't go too far away." Sunny promised to be careful and flew out of the tree. Sunny managed to find his friends, even in the mist. They played games and had fun. But after a while, Sunny couldn't see the big tree anymore. He was scared and didn't know how to get back home. Sunny's friend, a little red bird named Rosie, said, "Don't worry, Sunny. We'll help you find your way back!" All of Sunny's friends flew together, looking for the big tree. They managed to find it and Sunny was so happy to be home. Sunny thanked his friends and gave his mom a big hug. From that day on, Sunny always made sure to listen to his mom and be careful when playing in the mist.

One day, a big, strong horse named Tom and a small, fast rabbit named Sue were talking. Tom said, "I need to eat some hay. I am very hungry." Sue said, "I will help you find hay. I am a reliable friend." They went to look for hay together. They looked in the barn, but they did not find any hay. They looked in the field, but they did not find any hay. They looked near the trees, but they did not find any hay. Tom started to feel sad. Then, Sue had an idea. She said, "Tom, did you forget the big pile of hay in your house?" Tom thought for a moment and then he said, "Oh, I did forget! Thank you, Sue!" They went to Tom's house and found the big pile of hay. Tom was happy and ate the hay. Sue was happy because she could help her friend. They both learned that sometimes, the thing you need is right where you left it.
