Title: Tom the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a blue toy car that he loved very much. He played with his car every day. His car had a light on top that would shine bright when he pushed it. One day, Tom took his blue car outside to play in the sun. The light on the car shone so bright, it made him smile. He raced the car up and down the sidewalk, laughing as he went. As the sun began to set, Tom's mommy called him inside for dinner. He brought his blue car with him and put it on the table. The light on the car was not as bright now, but it still made Tom happy. After dinner, Tom went to bed with his blue car, dreaming of more fun times together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to paint. One day, he found a big strip of paper. He wanted to paint something nice on it. Tim started to paint. He painted a sun, a tree, and a house. Then, a rude bird flew by. The bird did not like Tim's painting. It laughed at him. Tim felt sad. But he did not stop painting. He painted more trees and a big, happy family. The rude bird saw this and felt bad. It said sorry to Tim. They became friends and painted together. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to organize her toys. She had many toys in her room. She started to put her dolls in one place, her cars in another place, and her blocks in a different place. She felt happy because her room was clear and easy to play in. Lucy's mom saw her organizing her toys and said, "Great job, Lucy! But be careful not to touch the poison under the sink. It is not safe for you." Lucy nodded and promised she would not touch it. Later, Lucy's friend Tim came to play. They played with dolls, cars, and blocks. Then, Tim saw a bottle under the sink. He did not know it was poison. Lucy remembered her mom's words and said, "No, Tim! Don't touch that! It's poison and not safe." Tim listened to Lucy and they went back to playing with their toys. They had a fun day and Lucy's mom was proud of her for keeping her friend safe.

One day, a cold wind blew. Tim and Jane went outside to play. They wore warm coats to not shiver. They saw a hose in the yard. They had an idea to play with the hose. Tim took the hose and turned it on. Water came out fast. Jane ran and laughed. The water was cold, but they did not care. They played and got wet. Soon, the sun came out. It was not cold anymore. Tim and Jane stopped playing with the hose. They went inside to change into dry clothes. They were happy they played with the hose on a cold day.
