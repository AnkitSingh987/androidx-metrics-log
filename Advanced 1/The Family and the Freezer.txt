Title: The Family and the Freezer

Once there was a family who liked to talk. Every night they would all sit together at the dinner table and talk. They talked about many interesting things. One day the family needed to buy a new freezer. They didn't have a lot of money, so they had to get a cheap one. So they went to the store and bought a cheap freezer. When they brought it home, they all gathered around it and talked. They talked all about the wonderful dishes they could make in the freezer. Then the family put the food in the freezer and everyone was very excited. They even talked about how good the food would taste when it came out of the freezer. The family was so glad that they were able to get a cheap freezer. They enjoyed talking about how much fun the freezer would bring them.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful bird named Spike. Spike loved to help his friends in the forest. One day, a little bunny was lost and needed help. Spike asked the bunny, "Do you need help?" The bunny said, "Yes, I can't find my way home." So, Spike helped the bunny find the path back to his family. As they walked, they saw a big box. They opened it, and inside was a magic wand! Spike touched the wand, and suddenly, he could talk like a human! Spike and the bunny were very happy. They used the wand to help even more friends, and they all lived happily to the end.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a squirrel named Sam. Sam was a famous squirrel because he could speak to all the animals in the forest. Every day, Sam would run around and play with his friends. One sunny day, Sam found a big nut under a tall tree. He wanted to share it with his best friend, a little bird named Billy. Sam called out to Billy, "Hey, Billy! Look at this big nut I found! Let's share it together!" Billy flew down from the tree and said, "Wow, Sam! That's the biggest nut I've ever seen! Thank you for sharing it with me." They both sat under the tree, and Sam cracked open the nut. They ate the nut and laughed together. From that day on, Sam and Billy became even better friends. They played and shared more nuts with each other. All the animals in the forest loved Sam because he could speak to them and was always kind. And that's how Sam the squirrel became the most famous squirrel in the big green forest.

Lila was a lonely girl. She had no friends to play with at school. She only liked to read books and eat grapes. She did not talk to anyone or share her grapes. One day, a new boy came to her class. His name was Sam. He was shy and quiet. He sat next to Lila. He saw her reading a book and eating grapes. He liked books and grapes too. "Can I read with you?" he asked Lila. Lila looked at him. She did not know what to say. She had never talked to anyone before. "Please?" Sam said. "I like books. And grapes. Can I have one?" Lila thought for a moment. She liked her books and grapes. But she also felt lonely. Maybe Sam could be her friend. "Okay," she said. She gave him a book and a grape. "But you have to be quiet." Sam smiled. He took the book and the grape. He thanked Lila. He read with her and ate the grape. He was quiet and nice. Lila and Sam enjoyed reading and eating grapes together. They found out they had many things in common. They liked the same stories and colors and animals. They started to talk and laugh and play. Lila was not lonely anymore. She had a friend. She learned to share her books and grapes. She was happy. The moral of the story is: Sharing can make you happy and help you make friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a ticket on the floor. It was a filthy ticket, but he picked it up. Tim showed the ticket to his mom. "Look, mom! I found a ticket!" he said. His mom looked at the ticket and said, "That's filthy, Tim. We should throw it away." But Tim didn't want to throw the ticket away. He kept it in his pocket. When they got home, Tim put the ticket under his bed. At night, a big wind came and blew the ticket away. Tim was sad because he lost his filthy ticket.
