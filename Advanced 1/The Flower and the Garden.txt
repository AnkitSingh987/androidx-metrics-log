Title: The Flower and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a shy flower. The flower was too scared to open up and show its pretty colors. It lived near a big tree and some bushes. One day, a little girl named Lily saw the shy flower. She said, "Hello, little flower! Why don't you open up and show your colors?" The flower whispered, "I am too shy. I don't know if I am pretty enough." Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's test it. I will ask my friends to come and see you. If they like you, will you open up?" The flower agreed. Lily brought her friends to see the flower. They all said, "Wow! What a pretty flower!" The shy flower felt happy and finally opened up to show its beautiful colors.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. They saw a big hill with a hole. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's go in there!" Tom said. "OK, but be careful. It looks dark and filthy." Lily said. They crawled into the hole. It was very dark and they could not see much. They felt something hard and cold. They touched it with their hands. "What is this?" Lily asked. "It feels like bones. Maybe it is a skeleton." Tom said. "A skeleton? What is that?" Lily asked. "A skeleton is what is inside you and me. It holds us together. But this one has no skin or hair or eyes. It is dead." Tom said. "Dead? That is scary. I don't like it. Let's go out." Lily said. But when they tried to go out, they could not find the way. The hole was too big and they got lost. They started to cry and shout for help. But no one heard them. They were trapped with the skeleton. They were very hungry and thirsty and cold. They wished they had minded their mom and dad and stayed away from the hole. But it was too late. They never got out. The skeleton was their only friend.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to listen to the ocean. He would sit on the sand and watch the water. The ocean was big and unknown to him. One day, Tom saw a funny fish in the water. The fish was not like the other fish. It was big and had a lot of colors. Tom was very curious. He wanted to know more about the unknown fish. Tom went into the water to play with the fish. But the fish was not a fish! It was a big, friendly whale. Tom was so happy to meet the whale. They played in the ocean and became best friends.

Once upon a time, a big fat dinosaur named Dino lived in a forest. Dino loved to watch the little birds fly in the sky. One day, he saw a tiny bird named Chirpy sitting on a branch. Dino said, "Hi Chirpy! I like to watch you fly. Can I try too?" Chirpy looked at the fat dinosaur and said, "But Dino, you are too big and heavy to fly like me." Dino felt sad, but Chirpy had an idea. "Why don't you watch me fly and I can teach you to jump high?" Dino loved the idea. They played together all day, with Dino jumping high and Chirpy flying in the sky. They became the best of friends and had lots of fun every day.

Once there was a girl called Jack. She was very hungry so she decided to go out and get some pizza. She went to the pizza shop and asked the pizza maker to bring her a pizza. The pizza maker smiled and went to get her a nice, warm pizza. When he brought the pizza to Jack, it was icy! Jack was very angry, it was not what she wanted. She shouted at the pizza maker for bringing a cold pizza. The pizza maker apologized and said to Jack, “I'm sorry. I’ll go and get you a warm pizza. Please wait here”. Jack waited and waited, but the pizza maker never returned. Jack was sad, but she also became really really hungry. She had to go and find something else to eat. The end.
