Title: Nana and the Garden

Nana was very proud of her passport. She used it to go on trips all around the world. All the places she saw were so interesting! One day, Nana was sitting in her garden when it started to rain. She looked up into the sky at the big drops of water and said "Oh no, it's raining." Just then, Nana's mother came outside with an umbrella. She handed it to Nana and said, "Here Nana, take this umbrella so you don't get wet from the rain." Nana was proud of her mother for helping her. She smiled and said, "Thank you Mama!" Then Nana opened the umbella and carefully walked in the rain.

Once upon a time there was a boy who was very happy. He was always playing and laughing. One day he made a promise that he would always be happy and not be ashamed of anything. He celebrated this promise with a big party. Everyone brought special gifts and they all laughed and danced. The boy was so happy that he couldn't contain his joy and he danced until he was out of breath. He thanked everyone for coming and celebrating with him. From that day forward he lived a happy life, never ashamed and always kept his promise. The End.

Once there was a little boy who was feeling lonely. He was walking around in the park when he saw a stranger. The boy smiled because a stranger is so exciting! The stranger noticed him and said "hello". The little boy smiled even more. The stranger said he wanted to make the little boy laugh, so he started to make a joke. The little boy laughed and laughed. He had never heard a joke before and thought it was so funny! The little boy was no longer feeling lonely. He thanked the stranger and the stranger told him to have a nice day. The little boy waved goodbye to the stranger and went home, feeling happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little shape named Circle. Circle was very popular. All of his friends liked to play with him. They would roll and bounce together. Circle was very happy. One day, Circle saw Square waving at him. Square wanted to play too. Circle and Square played together. They made a new game. Circle would roll, and Square would slide. They had lots of fun. Soon, all the other shapes wanted to play with Circle and Square. Triangle, Star, and Heart joined in. They all played and waved to each other. Circle was happy to have so many friends. They all played together and were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a long dog named Max. Max loved to play on the rug in the living room. One day, Max was playing with his ball on the rug when he felt a tickle in his nose. Max tried to hold it in, but he could not. He let out a big sneeze! The ball flew across the room and landed under the table. Max looked around to see where the ball went. Max found the ball under the table and brought it back to the rug. He played and played until he was tired. Then, Max took a nap on the rug, happy and sleepy from all the fun he had.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play games. Today they play a game called quiz. Quiz is when one person asks a question and the other person tries to guess the answer. Lily asks Tom, "What is the name of the animal that has a long neck and spots?" Tom thinks hard. He knows some animals, but not all of them. He says, "Is it a dog?" Lily laughs. "No, silly, a dog has a short neck and fur. Try again." Tom says, "Is it a horse?" Lily shakes her head. "No, a horse has a long face and a mane. One more try." Tom says, "Is it a giraffe?" Lily claps her hands. "Yes, you got it! A giraffe has a long neck and spots. You are good at quiz." Tom smiles. He likes quiz. He says, "Now it is my turn. I will ask you a question. What is the name of the thing that is round and yellow and shines in the sky?" Lily thinks. She knows this one. She says, "Is it a sun?" Tom nods. "Yes, you are right. It is a sun. You are good at quiz too." Lily and Tom hug. They are happy. They say, "Quiz is fun. Let's play more." They play more quiz. They ask each other questions about colors, shapes, numbers, and fruits. They guess and learn and laugh. They have a crazy time. Quiz is their favorite game.
