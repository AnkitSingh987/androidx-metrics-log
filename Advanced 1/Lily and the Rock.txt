Title: Lily and the Rock

Lily liked to collect rocks. She had a big bowl full of rocks of different colors and shapes. She liked to look at them and touch them and sort them. One day, she went to the park with her mom and saw a new rock. It was shiny and smooth and had a rainbow on it. She wanted to have it for her bowl. She ran to the rock and picked it up. "Wow, what a pretty rock!" her mom said. "Where did you find it?" "Over there, by the tree," Lily said, pointing. "Can I keep it, mom?" "Of course, you can, sweetie. You are very clever to find such a special rock," her mom said. Lily smiled and put the rock in her pocket. She was happy. She had a new rock for her bowl. She wondered what other rocks she could find in the park. She loved to collect rocks.

Once upon a time, there was a pink cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play with her ball. One day, the ball rolled away and got stuck under a big tree. Kitty tried to catch the ball with her paws, but she could not reach it. She saw a tool near the tree. The tool was a long stick. Kitty thought, "I can use this stick to get my ball!" Kitty said to herself, "I will use this tool to catch my ball." She put the stick under the tree and pushed the ball out. The ball rolled back to Kitty. Kitty was very happy. She caught the ball and played with it all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little mop. The mop was very sad because no one wanted to play with it. It was all alone in the house. One day, a fierce dog came into the house. The dog wanted to play with the mop. The mop was scared at first but then thought, "Maybe the dog will be my friend." So, the mop said, "Welcome, dog! Let's play together!" The dog and the mop played and played. They had so much fun! The fierce dog was not so fierce anymore. The mop was happy because it finally had a friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy lived in a modern house with her mom, dad, and little brother Tim. They had a big garden with many pretty flowers and trees. One day, Amy saw a new bulb in the garden. She asked her mom, "What is this bulb for?" Her mom replied, "That is a special bulb. It will grow into a big, beautiful flower if we take care of it." Amy was excited and wanted to help the bulb grow. Every day, Amy watered the bulb and watched as it started to grow. She saw the flower stretch up towards the sky. She felt happy and proud. Her little brother Tim saw this and wanted to help too. Together, they took care of the flower and watched it grow even bigger and more beautiful. Soon, all their friends came to see the amazing flower. They all said, "Wow, Amy and Tim, you did a great job taking care of the flower!" Amy and Tim felt very happy and proud. And the moral of the story is: when we work together and care for others, we can create something beautiful and special.

Once upon a time, there lived a boy named Timmy. Timmy was three years old and he was very happy. His mum always said he was the cleverest boy in the world. One day, Timmy found a large head in the garden. It was very big. He decided to join it. He thought maybe it was a game. He climbed on top of the head and he couldn't believe it! It was so clear that he could see for miles and miles. He could see everything! He saw mountains and rivers and forests. He shouted with delight. Suddenly, he heard someone from inside the head say: "Hello Timmy, what are you looking at?" Timmy was so excited that he almost jumped off the head. He looked down and saw a big friendly face smiling up at him. Suddenly, he realised that it wasn't a head at all, but a new friend to join!
