Title: Amy the Girl and the Book

One day, a little girl named Amy went to her big bookcase. She wanted to find a fun book to read. She saw many books, but she didn't know which one to choose. Amy's mom came in and said, "Amy, pick an honest book." Amy asked, "What is honest, Mom?" Her mom said, "Honest means the book tells a true story." Amy looked at the bookcase again. She saw a book about a cat who could talk. She thought it was fun but knew it was not honest. She did not ignore her mom's words. Then, she saw a book about a girl who learned to ride a bike. Amy thought, "This is an honest book." She picked the book and read it with her mom. They both had a good time reading the honest book together. Amy learned that honest stories can be fun too.

Once upon a time, there was a big uncle. He was very nice. He had a perfect red ball. He liked to play with it every day. One day, the uncle went to a big park. He saw a nice spot to lay on the grass. The sun was warm and he was happy. He took out his red ball and began to play. The uncle played with the ball all day long. He threw it up and caught it. He rolled it on the ground. He even gave it a big kick. The uncle was very tired but had lots of fun. And that was his perfect day at the park.

John was very restless. He wanted to explore, but his parents wouldn't let him. He thought there must be something behind the door. So he opened it and ran into the room. A monster was waiting for him. It was huge and angry, and it snapped at John with its giant teeth. John was scared and he tried to get away, but the monster caught him and he screamed. John's parents heard the sound and ran in to help. But it was too late. The monster had taken John away and all they could see was an open door. They felt so despair as they realized that their son was gone and they could do nothing but sit and think about poor John.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to drink juice every day. Her mommy would give her a cup of juice after she finished her lunch. Lily's favorite juice was apple juice because it was sweet and yummy. One day, Lily wanted her juice, but her mommy was busy. So, she decided to get it herself. She went to the kitchen and saw the juice box on the top shelf. Lily used a chair to reach the juice box, but it was locked in a cupboard. She needed a key to unlock it. Lily asked her mommy, "Mommy, can I have the key to unlock the cupboard?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, but be careful, sweetie." Lily took the key and unlocked the cupboard. She got her juice and was very happy. From that day on, Lily felt proud that she could unlock the cupboard and get her juice all by herself.

Once upon a time, on a big round planet, there lived a happy little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sam. They loved to play together and have fun. One day, they wanted to find something new to play with. They looked everywhere they could. As they were looking, they found a big, round ball. It was very bitter and cold to touch. They didn't know what it was, but they thought it was fun to play with. They played catch with it and managed to have a great time. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to talk! It said, "Hello, I am a magic ball from another planet. I can grant you one wish." Tim and Sam looked at each other in surprise. They talked and decided to wish for a big, fun playground for all the kids to enjoy. The magic ball made their wish come true, and everyone was so happy. Tim and Sam had even more fun playing with all their friends on their new playground.
