Title: Ben and the Park

Ben likes to follow his dad when he rides his motorcycle. He has a small bicycle with three wheels that he pedals fast. He wears a helmet and a jacket like his dad. He thinks his dad is very cool and brave. One day, they go to the park together. Ben follows his dad on the road, but he stays on the sidewalk. He sees many cars and buses and people. He hears many noises. He likes to make noises too. He goes "vroom vroom" like his dad's motorcycle. But when they get to the park, his dad tells him to be quiet. He says there are animals in the park that are shy and scared of noises. He says they can see them if they are quiet and careful. Ben nods and tries to be quiet. He follows his dad on a path that goes into the woods. There, they see a deer and a rabbit and a bird. They are brown and white and red. They have big ears and eyes and wings. They look at Ben and his dad, but they do not run away. Ben thinks they are very pretty and cute. He smiles and whispers to his dad. "Dad, can we come back here again?" "Sure, son. We can come back anytime. But remember, we have to be quiet and respectful of the animals. They live here and we are their guests." "Okay, dad. I will be quiet and respectful. I like the animals. And I like you and your motorcycle." "I like you too, son. You are a good follower and a good listener. Now, let's go back to our motorcycle and bicycle. We have to go home soon. It's getting dark." "Okay, dad. Let's go. Vroom vroom!"

Once upon a time, there was a soldier. He was a little confused. He needed to sort his toys. The soldier had many toys, like cars, dolls, and balls. He wanted to make them neat and nice. One day, the soldier started to sort his toys. He put the dolls in one box, the cars in another box, and the balls in a big bag. While he was sorting, he found a strange toy. It was a tiny, shiny box. He had never seen it before. The soldier opened the tiny, shiny box. It was not a toy! It was a magic wand! He waved the wand, and something unexpected happened. All his toys came to life! The dolls danced, the cars raced, and the balls bounced. The soldier was very happy. He played with his new friends all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a reindeer named Remy. Remy lived in a big forest. He had many friends who played with him. Remy was a very happy reindeer. One day, Remy saw an expensive toy in a shop. He wanted the toy very much. But he knew he could not have it. Remy was sad. He told his friends about the toy. His friends had an idea to help Remy get the toy. Remy's friends told him they would prevent anyone from taking the toy. They would watch the toy until Remy could find a way to get it. Remy was happy again. Remy thought and thought. He found a way to get the toy. He did jobs for people in the town. He got money for his work. Finally, Remy had enough money to buy the expensive toy. His friends stopped anyone from taking the toy while Remy was working. Remy got his toy and was very happy. He thanked his friends for helping him.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Bob. Bob liked to travel to far away places. He would go on big trips with his friends. One day, Bob and his friends found a big mattress. They decided to play on it. As they played on the mattress, it started to move. The mattress was magic! It could fly! Bob and his friends were so happy. They flew up in the sky, high above the trees. They saw many fun things while they were up there. But then, something unexpected happened. The magic mattress started to go down very fast. Bob and his friends were scared. They tried to make the mattress go up, but it didn't work. The mattress fell down to the ground. Sadly, Bob and his friends got hurt. They never played on the magic mattress again.
