Title: The Weak Deer and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a weak deer. The deer was so weak that it could not run and play like the other deer. It lived near a big lake where it would go to drink water every day. One day, the weak deer went to the lake to drink water. As it was drinking, it saw a big fish in the water. The fish liked to splash with its tail. The deer thought it would be fun to play with the fish, so it went closer to the water. But the weak deer was not careful. As it went closer to the water, it slipped and fell into the lake. The fish was scared and swam away. The deer tried to swim, but it was too weak. It could not get out of the water and it was too late for anyone to help. The weak deer stayed in the water and did not come out again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pink glove. Lily loved her pink glove very much. It was her favorite thing to wear. One day, Lily saw a tiny bird on the ground. The bird was scared. Lily wanted to help the bird. She put on her pink glove and said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will protect you." Lily picked up the bird with her pink glove and took it to her mom. Lily's mom said, "Good job, Lily! You used your pink glove to protect the bird. Now, let's help the bird find its family." Together, they helped the bird fly back to its nest. The bird was happy, and Lily felt proud that she could protect the bird with her pink glove.

Once upon a time, there was a little car. The car had four regular tires. One day, the car went for a ride in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. The car was very happy. While driving, the car saw a big tire on the ground. The car thought, "I can offer the tire a ride." So, the car went close to the tire and said, "Hi, tire! Do you want to ride with me?" The tire said, "Yes, please!" But, the unexpected happened! The tire was not a regular tire. It was a magic tire! The magic tire could make the car fly. The car and the magic tire flew up into the sky. They had so much fun flying together. From that day on, the car and the magic tire were best friends. They went on many more fun rides and had many more adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Nina. She had a stubborn streak and always wanted to do things her own way. One day, Nina's mom asked her to wear her skirt. But Nina refused. She said, "No! I don't want to wear the skirt". Her mom looked at her firmly and said, "Nina, put it on right now. You have to look nice when we go out." But Nina was still stubborn and said, "No way! I'm not wearing it." Then, Nina's dad stepped in. He knelt down so he was at eye level with Nina and said, "Nina, Mommy and I both think it's important that you wear the skirt. Please put it on." Nina looked at her dad, sighed and said, "Alright, I'll put it on." She got dressed and went out with them wearing the skirt. Her parents smiled and praised her for being so stubborn and clever. Nina was proud of herself for getting her way.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to make and sell jewelry. She made pretty necklaces and bracelets with colorful beads. Her friends liked her jewelry a lot. One day, her friend Emma saw Lily's new necklace. Emma felt envious. She said, "I want a necklace like that too!" Lily smiled and said, "I can make one for you, Emma. We can sell more jewelry together and have fun." Emma and Lily started making and selling jewelry together. They had a great time, and their friends loved their pretty necklaces and bracelets. They learned that working together was more fun than being envious. The moral of the story is: it's better to work together and share than to be envious of what others have.
