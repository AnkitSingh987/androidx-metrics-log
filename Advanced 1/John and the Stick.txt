Title: John and the Stick

John was walking in the park. Suddenly, he saw a big stick on the ground. He was so excited and he wanted to pick it up and show it to his friends. John picked up the stick and started running, but he wasn't looking where he was going. Suddenly, he bumped into an old man. The man asked him seriously: "Why are you running around with a stick? What are you looking for?" John was embarrassed and he said nothing. The old man smiled and said: "I think you are looking for something special. Is it really worth looking for a stick?" John thought about it for a moment and realized the old man is right. He thanked the man and returned the stick to the ground. The old man shook his head and said: "If you are looking for something special, maybe you should look somewhere else. Sometimes if you want something special, you have to do something special." John thanked the old man and walked away, thinking about the old man's words. He realized that looking for something special is not always easy. John was now determined to search for something special and he knew exactly where to look.

One day, True the dog went to the store with his friend, Max. True wanted to buy a big ball to play with. Max wanted to buy a toy car. They both had happy faces as they looked at the toys. True said to Max, "I hope the store is not empty!" Max replied, "Yes, I want to find the best toy car!" They walked and walked, looking for the toys they wanted. They saw many toys, but not the ones they wanted. Then, they saw a big box in the corner. True said, "Let's look inside the box!" They opened the box and found the big ball for True and the toy car for Max. They both smiled and said, "We knew we would find them!" They bought their toys and went home to play together.

Anna likes to hop. She hops in the morning, she hops at noon, she hops at night. She hops on the bed, she hops on the couch, she hops on the floor. She hops when she is happy, she hops when she is sad, she hops when she is bored. One day, Anna hops too much. She hops on the table, she hops on the chair, she hops on the cabinet. The cabinet is high and big. It has many things inside. Anna wants to see what is inside. She hops on the cabinet and opens the door. "Wow!" Anna says. She sees many shiny things. She sees cups and plates and spoons and forks. She sees a big cake with candles. She sees a card with her name. She sees a present with a bow. She thinks it is for her. She is very happy. She hops on the cabinet and reaches for the cake. "Anna, no!" Mommy says. She comes into the kitchen and sees Anna on the cabinet. She is angry and scared. She runs to the cabinet and grabs Anna. She puts her down on the floor. She closes the cabinet door. "Anna, you are naughty!" Mommy says. "You cannot hop on the cabinet. You can fall and hurt yourself. You can break the things inside. You can ruin the cake. The cake is for your birthday. The present is for you too. But you have to wait until the party. You have to be quiet and good." Anna feels sorry. She did not know it was her birthday. She did not know the cake and the present were for her. She did not mean to be naughty. She hugs Mommy and says, "I am sorry, Mommy. I love you. I will not hop on the cabinet again. I will be quiet and good. Can I still have the cake and the present?" Mommy smiles and hugs Anna back. She says, "I love you too, Anna. I am glad you are sorry. You can still have the cake and the present. But you have to wait until the party. The party will start soon. You can hop with your friends and have fun. But you have to be careful and listen to me. Okay?" Anna nods and says, "Okay, Mommy. Thank you. I am excited for the party. I will hop with my friends and have fun. But I will be careful and listen to you. I promise." Mommy kisses Anna and says, "You are welcome, Anna. You are a good girl. Happy birthday, my hoppy bunny."
