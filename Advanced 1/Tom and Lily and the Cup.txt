Title: Tom and Lily and the Cup

Tom and Lily were playing in the kitchen. They had many cups and spoons and pots. They liked to pretend they were making tea and cake. They were very happy. But then, Tom saw a big cup on the shelf. It was shiny and red and had a picture of a dragon on it. Tom wanted to play with it. He was eager to see the dragon. He climbed on a chair and reached for the cup. "Tom, no!" Lily shouted. "That is Mom's cup. She loves it very much. You can't touch it!" But Tom did not listen. He grabbed the cup and pulled it down. But the cup was too heavy for him. It slipped from his hand and fell on the floor. It broke into many pieces. It was destroyed. Tom and Lily were very scared. They heard Mom coming. They did not know what to say. They looked at the broken cup and felt very sorry.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play in the park. One day, he saw a rail near the park. Spot wanted to jump over the rail to get to the other side. Spot ran and tried to jump over the rail. But he was so clumsy that he fell on the rail instead! His friends, the birds, saw him fall and came to help. They picked Spot up and tried to fly him over the rail. But then something unexpected happened. The birds started to applaud Spot! They were clapping their wings and cheering. Spot was confused, but then he saw why they were so happy. He had knocked down the rail, and now all his friends could play together on both sides of the park. Spot felt proud of himself, even though he was clumsy. He learned that sometimes, unexpected things can bring happiness to everyone. And from that day on, all the animals played together in the big, open park.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog named Spot. Spot loved to sleep all day. He did not like to run or play with his friends. One day, Spot's friend, a little bird named Tweet, came to visit him. "Spot, I need your help!" Tweet said. "I want to cross the road, but it is too big and scary for me. Can you help me solve this problem?" Spot did not want to leave his cozy bed, but he knew his friend needed help. So, he got up and walked to the road with Tweet. They looked both ways to make sure no cars were coming. Then, Spot and Tweet crossed the road together. After they crossed, Tweet was very happy and thanked Spot for his help. Spot felt good about helping his friend. He realized that it was more fun to help others than to be lazy all day. From that day on, Spot was not lazy anymore. He loved to play and help his friends whenever they needed him.

Once upon a time, there was a musician who was very sad. He sat in his room and played sad songs. He wanted to make himself feel better, but he couldn't. Then one day he went outside and spotted a flower blooming in the garden. He smiled at how beautiful it was. He sat and watched the flower until the sun went down. The next day, the musician went back outside to look at the flower. It was brighter and it reminded him of all the good things in the world. He felt happy and decided to write a happy song. As he played the song, he thought about all the things that can bloom. He realised there was still beauty and happiness in the world, even when he was sad.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He liked to look at the pretty world around him. He saw a big landscape with many trees, flowers, and a big blue sky. Tim thought the landscape was very attractive. As he walked, he met a girl named Sue. She also liked the pretty world around them. "I wish I could own this landscape," Sue said to Tim. "Me too," Tim agreed. They both wanted to keep the pretty world just for themselves. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the leaves from the trees. The landscape did not look attractive anymore. Tim and Sue were sad. But they learned that they could not own the pretty world. They knew they had to share it with everyone and take good care of it together.
