Title: Spot the Cat and the Tree

One day, a playful cat named Spot went outside to play. Spot loved to stretch in the sun and chase bugs. He saw his friend, a little bird named Tweet. Tweet said, "Spot, I found a big tree we can play by. It's fun to climb and stretch our legs!" Spot and Tweet went to the big tree. They played and stretched all day. Spot climbed up and down the tree, and Tweet flew around him. They laughed and had a great time. As the sun went down, Spot said, "Tweet, look at the sky! It's so pretty when the sun goes down." Tweet smiled and said, "Yes, it is. And it's even better when we can watch it together after a fun day of playing and stretching." Spot and Tweet watched the sun set, happy to have spent the day playing and stretching by the big tree. They knew they would come back to the tree again and again to play and have more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a sad little boy. He liked to peek and explore around outside. One day, he saw something shiny in the bushes. He peeked to see what it was and found a measure. He was so happy, he took it inside to show his mommy. But when his mommy saw the measure, she got very sad. "Where did you get this measure?" she asked. The little boy was scared and he just looked down and didn't say anything. But the mommy figured out that he had taken it from the neighbor's yard. She was disappointed and made him take it back to the neighbor's yard. He was so sad that he had made his mommy sad. He returned the measure to the neighbor and went back inside. He peeked into his mommy's bedroom and apologized for taking the measure. His mommy smiled and gave him a big hug. She said she was not mad anymore, and they could read a story together. The little boy was so happy. He learned not to take things without permission.

One day, a little zebra went for a walk. He saw a huge tree with big, red apples. The little zebra wanted to eat the apples, but they were too high up. He felt sad. A tall giraffe saw the little zebra and asked, "Do you want help?" The little zebra nodded and said, "Yes, please!" The tall giraffe reached up and picked the apples with his long neck. He gave them to the little zebra. The little zebra was very happy. He thanked the tall giraffe and they both ate the apples together. They became good friends and played all day long. The little zebra was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a car named Tommy. Tommy loved to perform tricks for his friends. He would go fast and then stop, making everyone laugh. Tommy needed fuel to do his tricks, so he always made sure he had some. One day, Tommy realized he was low on fuel. He went to the fuel place, but there was no fuel available. Tommy was sad because he couldn't perform tricks for his friends. He didn't know what to do. Then, Tommy saw a big truck with fuel. The truck driver saw Tommy and said, "I can give you some fuel!" Tommy was happy and got the fuel he needed. But when he tried to perform tricks, he found out he could now fly! The fuel was special flying fuel. Tommy and his friends were so surprised and happy. Now, Tommy could perform even better tricks in the sky.

One day, Ben and Lily went to the park with their mom. They liked to play on the swings, the slide and the sandbox. They also liked to look at the sky and the clouds. Sometimes they saw shapes of animals or things in the clouds. But today, the sky was dark and gray. Mom said it was going to rain soon. Ben and Lily wanted to stay and play, but mom said they had to go home. They were sad and grumpy. As they walked home, they heard a loud noise. It was thunder. They saw a bright flash. It was lightning. Ben and Lily were scared. They hugged mom and asked her what was happening. Mom said it was a storm. She said thunder and lightning were like big noises and lights in the sky. She said they were not dangerous if they stayed inside. She said they were part of nature, like the sun and the moon and the stars. Ben and Lily looked at the sky again. They saw the lightning make shapes of animals and things in the clouds. They saw a lion, a car, a flower and a star. They thought it was cool and fun. They started to laugh and smile. Mom said they were brave and smart. She said they could believe in anything they wanted. She said they could make up stories about the thunder and the lightning. She said they could use their imagination. When they got home, mom made them some hot chocolate and cookies. They were yummy. Ben and Lily told mom their stories about the thunder and the lightning. Mom listened and smiled. They had a cozy and happy time. They liked the storm.
