Title: Tom the Smart Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to sing. Every day, he would sit on the bookshelf and sing his favorite songs. One day, while Tom was singing on the bookshelf, a little bird flew in through the window. The bird heard Tom sing and thought it was a beautiful sound. The bird wanted to sing with Tom. So, Tom and the bird sang together on the bookshelf. Their voices sounded so nice together. They became best friends and sang songs every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play at the zoo. They see many animals. They see lions, monkeys, and birds. They have fun. They also see a big hippo. The hippo is purple. Lily and Ben are surprised. They have never seen a purple hippo before. They want to know why the hippo is purple. They ask the zookeeper. The zookeeper says the hippo likes to relax in a pool of water. The water has purple flowers. The flowers make the water and the hippo purple. The hippo is happy. The purple color keeps the hippo cool and calm. Lily and Ben think the hippo is cool too. They want to relax in the pool with the hippo. But the zookeeper says no. The pool is only for the hippo. Lily and Ben are sad. But they understand. They say bye to the hippo. They go to see other animals. They have more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She lived with her family in a small house. Lily loved to match her socks with her dresses. She liked to look nice and neat. One day, Lily went to the park with her family. She saw a big, pretty butterfly. The butterfly had wings that matched Lily's dress. Lily was so happy! She tried to catch the butterfly, but it flew away. Lily followed the butterfly into the woods. She got lost and was very scared. Then, something unexpected happened. The butterfly turned into a fairy! The fairy wasn't shy at all. She said, "I'm here to help you find your family, Lily." The fairy held Lily's hand and led her back to the park. Lily was so surprised and happy. She hugged the fairy and thanked her. From that day on, Lily and the fairy were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was an expensive toy. The toy was a skeleton. The skeleton had a name. It was called Mr. Bones. One day, Mr. Bones set a fun game in the house. He hid in a box. He wanted to surprise his friends. The cat, the dog, and the bird were looking for him. They looked everywhere. Then, the cat found the box. The cat opened the box. But, Mr. Bones was not there! The cat, the dog, and the bird were sad. They looked more. They found Mr. Bones in the kitchen. He was hiding in a pot! They all laughed and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little fish named Fin. Fin loved to number his toys. One day, he found a big scale in his toy box. He had never seen it before. He thought, "I can use this scale to number all my toys!" The next day, Fin played with his friends. He told them about the big scale he found. His friends were excited and wanted to see it too. They all went to Fin's house to look at the big scale. They could not wait to see what would happen. When they got to Fin's house, Fin showed them the big scale. They all took turns putting their toys on it. The scale showed them how big and heavy each toy was. They had so much fun! They played with the scale all day. At the end of the day, they were tired but very happy. The big scale helped them have a great time together.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and pretend she was a big ship captain. One sunny day, she went to the beach with her mom and dad. Lily stood at the edge of the water and watched the waves come in. She put her hands on her hips and said, "I am the captain of this big ship! I will sail across the sea!" Her mom and dad smiled and clapped for her. As Lily played in the water, she saw a big wave coming. She was a little scared, but she knew she was brave. She shouted, "I am not scared! I will ride the wave!" The big wave came and Lily jumped over it. Her mom and dad cheered for her. "Good job, brave Captain Lily!" they said. And Lily felt very proud.
