Title: Jane and the Book

Jane had been impatient all week waiting for her new book to arrive. Every day she had asked when it would be delivered, but no one knew the answer. Finally, one day a large package came in the mail. Jane was so excited; she had to see what was inside! It was her book! She held it in her hands, wanting to open it right away. But first, she had to be patient. Jane finally opened it and as she was reading, she couldn't put the book down. She was completely absorbed in discovering all the exciting things it had to offer. But then time ran out. Jane had stayed up all night and in the morning her mother came in to wake her up. Jane didn't want to close the book, but she had to. She was so sad, but she also knew that she had to stop reading now if she wanted to have time for other things later. Jane felt unhappy when the story had to end. She felt like her life was being cut short before could finish the amazing tales she had been reading. Jane was impatient, wanting the book to last forever, but she knew the story had to end.

Jim shook his head when he heard the loud noise coming from outside. He was feeling scared, but his mom gave him a nod so he knew it was ok. He peeked out the window and saw a big, yellow truck. Jim was still a bit nervous, so he decided to hide behind the curtain. He saw that the truck was shining a light on the house, and he knew he was safe. He watched as the big, yellow truck left, and he felt better knowing the coast was clear. After a while, Jim was feeling brave again. He pulled back the curtain, and saw that the night sky was lit up with stars. He smiled and nodded to himself; he knew he was alright.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One gloomy day, Tim went for a walk. He wanted to meet a new friend. As Tim walked, he saw a newspaper on the ground. The newspaper had a picture of a dog on it. Tim thought the dog was cute and wanted to find it. Tim walked and walked until he found the dog from the newspaper. They played together and became best friends. Even though it was a gloomy day, Tim was very happy to meet his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat chocolate. One day, his mom hid the chocolate in a secret place. Tim wanted to find the chocolate. Tim looked everywhere. He looked under his bed, behind the door, and even in the terrible, dark closet. Finally, he found a big box. Tim opened the box, and it revealed the chocolate! He was so happy. Tim took the chocolate and shared it with his friends. They all ate the chocolate together and had a great time. Even though the hiding place was terrible, Tim was happy he found the chocolate. And his mom was happy he shared it with his friends.

Lily and Tom were playing in the kitchen. They saw a big bowl of dough on the table. Mom was making bread for dinner. "Let's play with the dough!" Lily said. "We can make shapes and animals!" Tom nodded. He liked to play with dough. They took some dough and started to roll it and pinch it. Mom came back to the kitchen. She saw the dough on the table. She saw Lily and Tom playing with it. "Hey, what are you doing?" Mom asked. "That dough is for the bread, not for playing!" Lily and Tom looked at Mom. They felt bad. They did not know they were not supposed to play with the dough. "We're sorry, Mom," Lily said. "We just wanted to have fun." Mom sighed. She was not very angry, but she was disappointed. "I know you wanted to have fun, but you should have asked me first," Mom said. "Now the dough is dirty and sticky. I can't use it for the bread anymore." Lily and Tom felt worse. They liked bread. They did not want to waste the dough. "Is there any more dough, Mom?" Tom asked. Mom shook her head. "No, there is not. I only made enough for one loaf." Lily and Tom thought for a moment. They had an idea. "Mom, can we help you make more dough?" Lily asked. Mom smiled. She saw that Lily and Tom were sorry and wanted to make up for their mistake. "Sure, you can help me," Mom said. "But you have to listen and follow the instructions. Making dough is not a game. It is a skill." Lily and Tom nodded. They washed their hands and put on aprons. Mom showed them how to measure the flour, the water, the yeast, and the salt. She showed them how to mix them and knead them. She showed them how to let the dough rise in a warm place. Lily and Tom learned a lot. They enjoyed helping Mom. They also learned to respect the dough and not play with it. Soon, they had a new batch of dough. Mom put it in a pan and into the oven. She thanked Lily and Tom for their help. Lily and Tom hugged Mom. They said they were sorry again and that they loved her. Mom hugged them back. She said she loved them too and that she was proud of them. They waited for the bread to bake. They smelled the delicious aroma. They were happy. They learned that playing with dough was not fun. They learned that helping Mom was fun. They learned that making bread was fun. They learned that wasting food was not good. They learned that saying sorry and making up was good. They learned a lot.
