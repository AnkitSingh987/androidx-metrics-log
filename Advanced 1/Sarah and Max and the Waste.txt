Title: Sarah and Max and the Waste

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Sarah and Max. They both loved to play together and have fun. One day Max had a great idea. He thought it would be fun to send waste away to help keep the world clean. Sarah thought it was a great idea and so they set to work. Max and Sarah both thought it would be a very successful plan. Max called his friends and asked them to help. Together they filled a big truck with all of the waste. "Let's send it away" declared Max. Max and Sarah smiled and waved as the truck drove away. After that Max and Sarah kept sending waste away and the world got cleaner and its people were happier. Max and Sarah were so successful that everyone wanted them to help keep the world clean. Max and Sarah still loved to play together, but now they knew that it was important to send waste away.

Anna likes to play with her mom's jewelry. She thinks they are shiny and colorful. She likes to put them on her neck, ears, and fingers. She feels like a princess. One day, she finds a new necklace in her mom's drawer. It has a big stone that changes colors. Anna thinks it is magic. She puts it on and runs to the mirror. She sees the stone turn blue, then green, then purple. She is amazed. She wants to show her mom, but her mom is busy in the kitchen. Anna decides to go outside and show her friends. She runs to the door, but she forgets to close the drawer. She also forgets to look both ways before crossing the street. A car is coming fast. The driver sees Anna and honks. Anna is scared and freezes. The car stops just in time. The driver gets out and yells at Anna. He says she is very naughty and careless. He says he could have hit her and hurt her. Anna starts to cry. She drops the necklace on the ground. The stone breaks and loses its colors. Anna feels very sad and sorry. She runs back to the house and hugs her mom. She tells her what happened and says she is sorry. She says she will never play with her mom's jewelry again. Her mom is angry and worried. She scolds Anna for taking her necklace and running outside. She says the necklace was very special and expensive. She says Anna could have been hurt or lost. She says Anna needs to be more careful and listen to her. But her mom also loves Anna very much. She sees that Anna is sorry and sad. She hugs her back and kisses her. She says she is glad that Anna is safe and sound. She says she forgives her and still loves her. She says she will buy her a new necklace that is colorful and safe for her to play with. She says she is proud of her for telling the truth and saying sorry. Anna feels better and happier. She says she loves her mom and thanks her. She says she will be more careful and listen to her. She says she will share her new necklace with her friends and be nice to them. She says she is happy to be her mom's princess.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a distant tree with something shiny on it. Tim wanted to see what it was, so he walked closer to the tree. When he got to the tree, he saw a trophy stuck in the branches. Tim wanted the trophy, so he tried to get it. He said to himself, "I want that trophy. It is so shiny!" Tim found a sharp stick on the ground. He used the stick to poke the trophy. He said, "Come on, trophy! Fall down!" Finally, the trophy fell down. Tim was very happy. He held the trophy and said, "I got the shiny trophy!" He took the trophy home and showed it to his mom. His mom was proud of him. They put the trophy on the shelf, and Tim smiled every time he saw it.
