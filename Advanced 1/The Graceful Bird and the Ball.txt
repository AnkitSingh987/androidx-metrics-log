Title: The Graceful Bird and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird who lived in the sky. She loved to fly high and try new things. One day, she found a big ball in the sky. It was the universe. The bird wanted to see what was inside the universe. She flew around and around, trying to find a way in. Then, something unexpected happened. The universe opened up and the bird went inside. Inside the universe, the bird found many new friends. She met a cat, a dog, and a frog. They all played and had fun together. The bird thought she was in a new place, but then she realized it was her home all along. The universe was where she belonged, with her new friends and her old sky.

Once upon a time there was a girl. She was very small and had a big doll. She was also very loud! One day she found a big crystal in the garden. She thought it was very pretty and she wanted to keep it. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, can I keep this crystal?" Her mom said, "Yes, if you can find a safe place for it. Somewhere it won't break." So the girl found a tall shelf and put the crystal up high so it wouldn't break. At night, the girl was tired from playing in the garden. She cuddled in her bed and said goodnight to her crystal. She fell asleep very quickly and dreamed about her crystal. In the morning, she woke up and found the crystal still up high. She jumped out of bed, ran over to it, and said, "Good morning, crystal!" She gave the crystal a big hug and they both sang a loud song together. Then the girl went outside to play again.

Once upon a time, there was a fluffy sheep named Bob. Bob had a lot of soft wool on his body. He lived on a big farm with his friends. One day, a girl named Sue came to the farm. Sue saw Bob's wool and thought it looked very nice. She wanted to borrow some of his wool to make a warm sweater. But she knew that wool was expensive. Sue asked Bob if she could borrow his wool. Bob was a kind sheep and said yes. Sue took some of Bob's wool and made a pretty sweater. She was very happy and warm. Bob was happy too, because he had helped his friend. Sue said thank you to Bob and promised to give back some wool when she could. They both smiled and played together on the farm.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They went to a big park where Tim could play and have fun. The park was busy with many kids running and laughing. Tim liked to watch all the fun things happening. As they walked, Tim saw a small cave near the park. He wanted to see what was inside, so he asked his mom if they could observe it. His mom said yes, and they went to the cave together. Inside the cave, they found a cute little rabbit. The rabbit was busy hopping around, looking for food. Tim and his mom smiled as they watched the rabbit play. Then, they went back to the busy park to play some more.

Lily was a little girl who loved to play. She had many toys and books and games, but her favorite thing was to pretend. She liked to pretend she was a princess, or a pirate, or a superhero. She had a big imagination and an amazing smile. One day, Lily wanted to play with her friend Max, who lived next door. She asked her mom if she could go, and her mom said yes, but only after she took a nap. Lily did not like naps. She thought they were boring and a waste of time. She wanted to play now, not later. So she had an idea. She told her mom she was going to lie down in her bed, but she did not. She sneaked out of her window and ran to Max's house. Max was happy to see Lily. He had a new toy, a big red truck that made loud noises and had flashing lights. He showed it to Lily and they decided to play with it in the street. They had a lot of fun, zooming and honking and pretending to be drivers. They did not see the car that was coming fast. They did not hear the horn that was warning them. They did not feel the impact that hit them. Lily's mom was worried. She went to check on Lily and saw that her bed was empty. She looked out of the window and saw the police and the ambulance and the crowd. She ran outside and saw the blood and the broken toys and the bodies. She screamed and cried and wished she had made Lily take a nap. She wished she had not lied. She wished she had not played. She wished she had her amazing smile back.
