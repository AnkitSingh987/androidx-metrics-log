Title: Lily Finds the Box

Lily was sick. She had a bad cough and a sore throat. She did not want to eat or play. She only wanted to sleep in her bed. Mommy was worried. She called the doctor on the phone. The doctor said he would send some pills to make Lily feel better. He said Mommy had to be careful and give Lily only one pill every day. The next day, a man came to the door. He had a box with the pills. He gave the box to Mommy and said, "Here are the pills for Lily. Remember, only one pill a day." Mommy thanked the man and took the box. She opened it and saw a bottle with the pills. The pills were small and white. They had a picture of a flower on them. Mommy went to Lily's room. She woke her up and said, "Lily, I have something for you. The doctor sent some pills to make you feel better. They have a flower on them. Do you like flowers?" Lily nodded. She liked flowers. She liked pink and yellow and purple flowers. She opened her mouth and Mommy gave her a pill. She swallowed it with some water. "Good girl," Mommy said. "Now you can go back to sleep. Tomorrow you will take another pill and soon you will be well." Lily smiled. She felt a little better. She hugged Mommy and said, "Thank you, Mommy. I love you." "I love you too, Lily," Mommy said. She kissed her forehead and tucked her in. She hoped the pills would work. She did not know that the flower on the pills was a clue. The pills were not ordinary pills. They were magic pills. They would not only cure Lily's cough and sore throat. They would also give her a special gift. A gift that would change her life forever. But that is another story.

Once upon a time, there was a big chair test in the town. All the kids wanted to show they had the best seat. They were very competitive. One day, a little girl named Sue found a small seat in her house. She thought it was perfect for the test. Sue was excited to show her seat to everyone. On the day of the test, all the kids showed their big and fancy seats. But when Sue showed her small seat, something unexpected happened. The small seat grew bigger and more beautiful than all the other seats. Sue won the test, and everyone was surprised.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a brave little girl named Mia. One day, she went for a walk and found a small box. She thought, "I will bury this box and come back later to see what is inside." The next day, Mia went back to the spot where she buried the box. When she got there, she saw a big crocodile sitting on the ground. The crocodile looked at Mia and said, "I know you buried a box here. Can you help me find it?" Mia was scared, but she wanted to be brave, so she said, "Yes, I will help you." Mia and the crocodile worked together to dig up the box. Inside, they found a shiny gold key. The crocodile smiled and said, "Thank you for helping me, brave Mia. This key opens a secret door in the forest. Let's go find it together!" And so, Mia and the crocodile became good friends, and they went on many more adventures in the big green forest.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a cheap watch that he loved very much. He wore it every day. One day, Tim fell down and hurt his leg. His mom said, "Don't worry, it will heal soon." Tim was sad because he couldn't play with his friends. He sat in his room and looked at his watch. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's watch started to glow! The watch jumped off his wrist and touched his leg. Just like that, his leg was all better! Tim was so happy and surprised. Tim went outside to play with his friends. He knew his watch was special. From that day on, Tim and his magic watch had many fun adventures together.
