Title: Lily the Beautiful Girl and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful girl named Lily. She lived in a small house near the woods. One day, Lily wanted to make soup for her family. Lily went to the woods to gather things for the soup. She found red tomatoes, green peas, and yellow corn. She put them all in a big pot and cooked them together. When the soup was ready, Lily called her family to eat. They all sat around the table and enjoyed the yummy soup. They were very happy, and Lily felt proud of the soup she made.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very anxious. He wanted to play with his scooter, but he was scared. He saw other kids playing with their scooters and having fun. Tim thought, "I want to be brave like them." Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, why don't you try your scooter? You can do it!" Tim felt better and decided to try. He put on his helmet and got on his scooter. At first, Tim was wobbly, but he did not fall. He kept trying and got better. Tim saw a big hill and thought, "I can go down that hill!" He was a little scared, but he did not ignore the hill. He went down the hill very fast and had so much fun. Tim was not anxious anymore. He loved his scooter and played with it every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. One day, Emma's mommy asked her to do a fun test. She said, "Emma, can you put your face on the table?" Emma was confused and asked, "What is a test?" Her mommy laughed and said, "It's really easy. Just put your face on the table and close your eyes." Emma was excited and did as she was told. When she opened her eyes, there was a big, thick felt blanket on the table! "That's my test?" asked Emma. Her mommy smiled and nodded. Emma gave her mommy a big hug and thanked her for the thick, soft blanket. They both laughed and enjoyed the surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a very good dog. He liked to run and play with his friends in the park. One day, Max saw a shiny medal hanging on a tree. He wanted to touch the medal. Max jumped high to touch the medal, but it was too high for him. He tried again and again, but he could not touch it. Then, Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was good at climbing trees. Max asked Lily to help him touch the medal. Lily climbed the tree and got the medal for Max. Max was very happy. He touched the medal and it was very shiny. Max and Lily became good friends. They played together with the medal and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a kind and compassionate boy named Tom. Tom liked to fold warm, soft pastels in his hands. Every day, Tom took pleasure in his folding activity. But one day, a naughty girl named Jenny came along. Jenny saw Tom folding pastels and thought it would be fun to steal them. Jenny grabbed a pile of pastels and ran off. Tom was sad and did not know what to do. But then, Tom remembered how compassionate and caring he was. So Tom decided that instead of being mad at Jenny, he would give her another pastel as a gift. Tom found Jenny and gave her a colourful pastel. Jenny was shocked at Tom's generous gesture and for the first time, she felt guilty about stealing. She thanked Tom and handed back the pastels. Tom and Jenny became friends and both of them enjoyed folding pastels together from then on.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a swing and ran to it. He loved to play on the swing. He sat on the swing and started to swing back and forth. Tim was very happy. But then, Tim saw a big apple on the ground. The apple was rot. Tim felt worried. He did not want to touch the rot apple. He stopped swinging and looked at the apple. Tim's mom saw him and came over. She saw the rot apple too. She picked it up and threw it away. Tim was not worried anymore. He smiled and went back to play on the swing.
