Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time there was a girl named Lucy. She was very lucky. Every day Lucy would take her word and say it out loud. She was very proud of her word. She loved to show her word to her family and friends. One day Lucy went to the park. She saw a cat and wanted to take it home with her. But the cat ran away. Lucy was sad. Then, something wonderful happened. She found a lucky coin on the ground. She was so happy! She decided to take the lucky coin home with her. She kept the coin in a special box and every time she looked at it, she thought of her lucky day.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went on a journey. He wanted to visit his friend, Sam, who lived far away. Tim put on his red hat and said goodbye to his mom and dad. On his way, Tim met a big, dangerous dog. The dog was angry and loud. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. He gave the dog a yummy treat, and the dog became his friend. With his new dog friend, Tim continued his journey. They walked and walked until they reached Sam's house. Sam was happy to see Tim and the dog. They played all day and had lots of fun. In the end, Tim's journey was a great adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a favorite vest that he loved to wear. It was red and soft, and it kept him warm when it was cold outside. Tim was very careful with his vest because he did not want to lose it. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a lot of fun playing on the swings and slide. But when it was time to go home, Tim noticed that his vest was gone. He was sad and worried that he had lost it. Tim and his mom looked everywhere in the park for the vest. They were very careful to check every corner and behind every tree. Then, they found the vest under the slide! Tim was so happy that he did not lose his favorite vest. From that day on, he was even more careful with his things.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Olive. She was very excited to help her mom in the kitchen. Olive wanted to serve food to her family. She loved to see them happy when they ate. One day, Olive's mom asked her to serve a big bowl of fruit. Olive was so excited! She picked up the bowl and walked very slowly. She did not want to drop the fruit. Suddenly, a little bird flew into the kitchen. Olive was so surprised! The bird flew around, and Olive started to laugh. She forgot about the fruit for a moment. Then, the bird landed on the bowl of fruit. Olive was not scared. She knew the bird just wanted to eat too. So, Olive served fruit to her family and the little bird. Everyone was happy and laughed together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to pour water into cups and bowls. One day, she found a big bow in her room. She had an idea to play a game. Lily put the bow on her head and went to the kitchen. She started to pour water into a cup. But Lily was impatient. She poured too fast, and the water spilled everywhere. Her mom came in and saw the mess. Her mom said, "Lily, you need to be patient when you pour water." Lily felt sad but learned her lesson. From that day on, Lily poured water slowly and carefully. She never made a mess again. And she always wore her big bow when she played.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing outside in the mud. She loved the texture of the mud on her hands. She made a big ball of mud and ran to show her mom. "Mom, look at my mud ball!" Sue said. Her mom looked at the mud ball and tried to accept it, but she thought it was terrible. She told Sue to go wash her hands and come inside. Sue felt sad but did as she was told. Later, Sue went back outside to play. She saw a big hole in the ground and thought it would be fun to jump in. She didn't know that the hole was very deep. Sue jumped in, but she couldn't get out. She called for help, but no one could hear her. The hole was too deep, and Sue was stuck inside. The mud ball was a sign that Sue should have stayed away from the hole, but she didn't understand.
