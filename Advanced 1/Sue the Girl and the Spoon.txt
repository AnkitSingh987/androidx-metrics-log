Title: Sue the Girl and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat pasta. One day, she went to the kitchen to make some yummy pasta for lunch. She saw a big pot and a box of pasta on the counter. Sue was very happy. Sue's mom came into the kitchen and saw the open box of pasta. She asked, "Sue, do you want to make pasta for lunch?" Sue said, "Yes, Mom! I love pasta!" So, Sue and her mom started to cook the pasta together. As they were cooking, Sue realized she needed a spoon to stir the pasta. She asked her mom, "Can I have a spoon, please?" Her mom gave her a big spoon, and Sue stirred the pasta. When it was ready, they put the pasta on plates and sat down to eat. Sue and her mom enjoyed their delicious pasta lunch.

One day a girl named Amy wanted to go to the gym. She asked her mom to help her get ready. Mom found her some creative clothes and put her curls in pigtails. Amy and her mom went to the gym. Mom helped Amy learn how to do push-ups, sit-ups, and jump rope. Amy was so proud of herself and even could do more than Mom! Mom went to the desk to sign out a ball. She asked Amy to help her find the right size. Amy grabbed one bigger than Mom expected, but they laughed and decided to give it a try. Amy was so creative! She made up games with the ball that Mom had never seen before. They laughed and cheered as Amy kicked and threw the ball. Amy was very proud of all the fun things she had learned and how much she had helped her mom. They both had a great day at the gym!

Once upon a time, there was a tutor who loved to play with water. He liked to splash in the puddles after the rain. One day, he met a little boy who was sad. "Hi, why are you sad?" the tutor asked the little boy. "I don't know how to splash," said the little boy. The tutor, who was ignorant about many things, thought he could teach the little boy to splash. "Follow me," said the tutor. They went to a big puddle. The tutor showed the little boy how to jump in the puddle and make a big splash. The little boy tried to jump and splash, but he fell in the puddle and got all wet. The little boy cried, and the tutor felt bad. The tutor did not know how to help the little boy. In the end, the little boy was still sad, and the tutor learned that he could not teach everything.

One day, a little girl was playing in the garden. She picked some dandelions and made a funny print with them. When she was done, she showed it to her mum. Her mum was so happy to see it and said that she was going to put it in a frame. The little girl was very excited and said she wanted to go and get some more dandelions. Her mum agreed so the little girl went back to the garden. But when she returned, she was very angry. She found that the garden was empty! All of the dandelions had gone! The little girl was so angry that she started to cry. "What happened to the dandelions?", she said. Her mum told her that the gardener had to pick them all up because they were getting old. The little girl was still very sad and didn't understand why they had to go. Then her mum had a great idea. She said that they could go to the printer and make some pictures of the dandelions. This made the little girl happy again and the two of them went to the printer. They selected their favorite photos, printed them out, and put them in a frame. The little girl was so happy that she had her own dandelion prints forever!

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was very happy. One day, she decided to go for a walk. She put on her nice shoes and walked out of her house. As she walked along, she saw many wonderful things. She saw skylarks soaring in the sky and a big, beautiful hill. She walked and walked until she came across a lake. Sarah stopped and looked around her. "What a nice world!" she said with a smile. Suddenly, Sarah heard a voice. "Come join me!" it said. Sarah looked around, but she couldn't see anyone. Then she looked in the lake and saw a friendly frog. Sarah smiled and said, "Hello, Frog. It's a really nice world, isn't it?" The frog grinned. "It sure is nice! Come for a walk with me and I'll show you more," he said. So Sarah and the friendly frog went for a walk together and had lots of fun. They saw amazing things and Sarah realised how wonderful the world was. When the sun went down, Sarah and the frog said goodbye and Sarah walked home, happy and content.
