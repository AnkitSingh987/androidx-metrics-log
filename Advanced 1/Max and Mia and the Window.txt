Title: Max and Mia and the Window

Once upon a time there were two friends: Max and Mia. One day Max was walking by Mia’s house, when he peeked into the window. He saw Mia’s mom scolding her for being rude to her brother. Max didn’t understand why Mia’s mom was so mad. So Mia explained that it was bad to be rude to others. Max thought about this and decided to put it into practice. A few days later, Max was tailing another friend, Bo. Bo was walking down the street and he didn’t notice anyone following him. He began to get upset thinking someone was following him. Max quickly spoke up and told Bo it was him, Max. Max apologized and told Bo he was only following him because he wanted to learn more about what it meant to be a good friend. Bo was relieved and was happy to teach Max more about being kind. Both Max and Bo learned a valuable lesson: It’s rude to follow someone without asking first. And that being kind will get you much further in life than being rude.

Once upon a time there was a museum. Inside the museum lived a small seed. Every day the seed grew bigger and bigger. One day the seed was so big, it was the size of a tree! It grew so tall it almost touched the ceiling of the museum. Suddenly, the seed began to make a noise. "Hey there," said the seed. "Can you help me get out of this museum?" The museum guard was very surprised. He knew that the seed must be a very special seed. "It looks dangerous," said the guard. "I think you should stay inside the museum." The seed was not happy. "Please, let me go outside," it said. But the guard said no. He knew if the seed went outside it would be too dangerous. So the seed stayed in the museum and kept growing. But it never forgot about the beautiful world outside and the things it could do if it were free.

One day, a big dog named Max went to play in the park. Max loved to run and jump in the park. The sun was hot and made Max very dry. Max saw a water to drink, but a sign said "No dogs allowed". Max didn't know the law, so he went to drink the water. A man saw Max and yelled, "Hey, dog! You can't drink that water!" Max was sad and still dry. Max walked away from the man and the water. A little girl named Lily saw Max and knew he was dry. She had a water bottle with her. Lily gave Max some water to drink. Max was happy and not dry anymore. Max and Lily played in the park all day, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little rocket. The rocket was red and shiny. It was a fearful rocket, always scared to fly high in the sky. One day, the rocket met a big bird. The bird said, "Why are you scared, little rocket? You can taste the clouds if you fly high!" The rocket was not sure, but it wanted to taste the clouds. With a big breath, the rocket tried to fly. It went up, up, up! The rocket was scared, but it kept going. Soon, it was high in the sky with the big bird. The rocket tasted the clouds, and they were sweet. The rocket was not fearful anymore. It was happy to fly high with its new friend, the big bird.

One day, a small shrimp was swimming in the sea. The shrimp saw a big fish. The fish was bitter because it was hungry. The shrimp was scared of the big fish. The shrimp said to the fish, "Please don't eat me! I am too small. I will not fill your tummy." The big fish thought for a moment and said, "Okay, little shrimp, I will not eat you. But you must help me find food." The shrimp and the big fish swam together. They found some yummy plants for the fish to eat. The fish ate the plants and was not bitter anymore. The shrimp was happy it did not get eaten, and the fish was happy it had food. From that day on, the shrimp and the fish were friends. They played together in the sea and helped each other find food. They were no longer scared or bitter, and they lived happily together.
