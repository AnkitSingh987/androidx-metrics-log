Title: The Story of the Dog and the Tree

One day, a big, hairy dog went for a walk. The dog saw his shadow on the ground. He thought the shadow was another dog. The hairy dog wanted to play with the shadow dog. The hairy dog tried to wipe the shadow with his paw. But the shadow did not go away. The hairy dog was sad. He wanted to be friends with the shadow dog. Then, the hairy dog saw a big tree. He thought maybe the shadow dog was hiding behind the tree. The hairy dog ran to the tree, but the shadow dog was not there. The hairy dog was very sad. He never found the shadow dog to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the sun. One day, it was very hot and Spot's mom told him, "Spot, the heat is too much today. Don't forget to drink water." Spot was glad because he liked to drink water. Spot went outside to play with his friend, the cat named Fluffy. They were playing with a ball when Fluffy said, "Spot, remind me to drink water too. The heat is making me thirsty." Spot said, "Okay, Fluffy. Let's drink water together." Just then, something unexpected happened. A big cloud came and covered the sun. The heat went away and it started to rain. Spot and Fluffy looked at each other and laughed. They didn't need to remind each other to drink water anymore. They played in the rain and drank the raindrops, happy and cool.

Tommy was a bunny who loved to hop. He hopped all day long in the meadow, where he found many yummy things to eat. He liked to hop with his friends, too, like Daisy the duck and Sammy the squirrel. They had fun playing games and telling jokes. One day, Tommy saw something shiny in the grass. He hopped closer and saw that it was a coin. He picked it up and looked at it. It had a picture of a man with a big hat and a word he did not know. "What is this?" he asked his friends. "That is a lucky coin," Daisy said. "My mom told me that if you find one, you can make a wish and it will come true." "Really?" Tommy said. He wondered what he could wish for. He had everything he needed: food, friends, and a cozy burrow. But there was one thing he wanted more than anything: a spring. A spring was a toy that Tommy had seen in a shop window once. It was a long coil of metal that could stretch and bounce. Tommy thought it looked like the most fun thing ever. He wished he could hop with a spring. He decided to make that his wish. He closed his eyes and held the coin tight. He said his wish in his mind. Then he opened his eyes and threw the coin in the air. He watched it sparkle and fall. He hoped his wish would come true. The next day, Tommy was hopping in the meadow again. He saw something colorful in the grass. He hopped closer and saw that it was a spring. It was the same one he had seen in the shop window. It was red and blue and shiny. He could not believe his eyes. "Wow!" he said. "This is my wish! This is my lucky spring!" He picked it up and tried to hop with it. He stretched it and let it go. It bounced and made a funny sound. Tommy laughed and hopped some more. He felt so happy and lucky. He called his friends and showed them his spring. They were amazed and happy for him. They took turns hopping with the spring. They had a lot of fun. Tommy thanked the lucky coin for his wish. He decided to keep the spring in his burrow. He knew he would hop with it every day. He was the luckiest bunny in the meadow.

Once upon a time, there lived an old man who was very rich. He had a lot of expensive things in his house. Every day, he would clean and polish his expensive things to keep them shiny and new. One day, he was feeling lonely and sad. He missed the friends who were far away. He wanted to talk to somebody but there was just the silence around him. So he just kept polishing his expensive things. Later that day, something strange happened. As he was polishing one of his things, he heard a loud voice. When he looked around, he noticed a little bird sitting right near him. He smiled, realizing that the bird was trying to talk to him. The old man and the bird became good friends. They would spend hours talking and playing together. The old man was no longer sad and the house was filled with joy and laughter. And with the little bird's help, he would also keep polishing his expensive things.
