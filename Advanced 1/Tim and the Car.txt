Title: Tim and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved Sundays because his mom would allow him to play with his expensive toy car. He would wait all week to play with it. One Sunday, Tim woke up very happy. He knew he could play with his toy car today. He ran to his toy box and took out his expensive car. Tim played with the car all day long, making it go fast and slow, and making it jump over small rocks. But then, something bad happened. Tim's expensive car hit a big rock and broke into pieces. Tim was very sad because he could no longer play with his favorite toy. He cried and cried, knowing that he would have to wait a long time for a new car. This Sunday did not end well for Tim.

Sara and Ben were very happy. They were going to decorate the big tree with their uncle Tom. Uncle Tom was very kind and funny. He brought many shiny things to put on the tree. He also brought some cookies and milk for them. They helped uncle Tom to put the shiny things on the tree. They used red, green, blue, and gold. They also put some stars and bells. The tree looked very beautiful. Sara and Ben clapped their hands and smiled. "Wow, you did a great job, Sara and Ben!" uncle Tom said. "The tree is very beautiful. You are very good helpers. Do you know why we decorate the tree?" Sara and Ben shook their heads. They wanted to know. Uncle Tom sat down with them and gave them some cookies and milk. He said, "We decorate the tree to celebrate a special day. It is called Christmas. On Christmas, we remember that God gave us a very special gift. He gave us his son, Jesus, who was born in a stable. Jesus loves us very much and he wants us to love each other. That is why we give gifts to each other and share our joy. Do you understand?" Sara and Ben nodded. They understood. They said, "Thank you, uncle Tom, for telling us. Thank you, God, for giving us Jesus. Thank you, Jesus, for loving us. We love you, too. And we love uncle Tom and each other." Uncle Tom hugged them and said, "I love you, too, Sara and Ben. You are very sweet and smart. You make me very happy. Now, let's sing some songs and enjoy the beautiful tree." They sang some songs and enjoyed the beautiful tree. They were very happy. They learned that decorating the tree was not only fun, but also meaningful. They learned that Christmas was about love and joy. They learned that they had a lot to be thankful for.

Once upon a time, there was a little pig. The pig was not clean. He was anxious to play with his friends. But his friends did not want to play with a dirty pig. So, the pig had an idea. The pig thought, "I will bathe in the pond. Then I will be clean and my friends will play with me." He went to the pond and had a fun time in the water. The pig was very happy. After his bath, the pig was clean and not anxious anymore. His friends saw him and wanted to play. They all played together and had a great day. The little pig was happy that he bathed and could play with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Penny. She loved to play with her toy car. One day, she saw an unknown bug on the floor. It was a tiny bug with many legs. Penny was not scared, but she was very curious. Penny decided to delay her playtime to watch the bug. She wanted to see where it would go. So, she followed the bug as it crawled across the floor. The bug moved very slowly, but Penny was patient. Finally, the bug reached a small hole in the wall. Penny waved goodbye to the bug as it crawled inside. Then, she went back to playing with her toy car. Penny was happy to have met the unknown bug and learned something new.
