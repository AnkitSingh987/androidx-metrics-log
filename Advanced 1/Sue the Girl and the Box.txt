Title: Sue the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big box that she loved. One day, Sue took her box to play outside. At the park, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim was not polite. He liked to tease Sue about her box. Sue felt sad because of Tim's mean words. Sue's mom saw her feeling sad and told her, "Don't let Tim's words hurt you. Be kind and polite, even when others are not." Sue listened to her mom and kept playing with her box. Soon, other kids joined Sue and they all played together. In the end, Tim saw how much fun Sue and the other kids were having. He felt sorry for teasing Sue. Tim learned that being polite and kind is better than being mean. The moral of the story is to always be polite and kind, even when others are not.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy boy named Tom. He loved to play with his ball. Every day, he would ask his friends to join him. They were all happy players. One sunny day, while playing with the ball, it went very far away. Tom and his friends ran to get it. They saw a big tree and the ball was under it. They were surprised to see a big, green frog sitting on their ball. The frog looked at Tom and said, "I want to join you and be a player too." Tom and his friends were so surprised that the frog could talk! They let the frog join them, and they all played together. The frog was a very good player, and they all had lots of fun.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big dollhouse with many rooms and furniture. They liked to pretend they were a family living in the dollhouse. One day, they decided to play school in the dollhouse. Lily wanted to be the teacher and Ben wanted to be the student. Lily put on a hat and glasses and took a book and a ruler. Ben took a pencil and a paper and sat on a small chair in front of a table. "Good morning, class," Lily said in a loud voice. "Today we will learn about flowers. Flowers are delicate and pretty. They have many colors and shapes. They need water and sun to grow. Do you have any questions?" Ben raised his hand. "Yes, teacher, I have a question," he said. "Can I go to the bathroom?" Lily frowned. "No, you cannot go to the bathroom. You have to seat and listen to my lecture. You are a bad student. You have to pay attention and learn." Ben felt angry. He did not like Lily's lecture. He did not care about flowers. He wanted to play something else. He did not want to seat and listen. He wanted to go to the bathroom. He stood up and threw his pencil and paper on the floor. "I don't want to play school. I want to play cars. You are a mean teacher. You are boring. I'm going to the bathroom." Lily was shocked. She did not like Ben's behavior. He was rude and noisy. He ruined her lecture. He did not respect her. She wanted to teach him a lesson. She grabbed her ruler and ran after him. "Come back here, you naughty student. You have to seat and listen. You have to apologize. You have to obey me." They chased each other around the dollhouse, shouting and arguing. They knocked over the furniture and the toys. They made a big mess. Their mother heard the noise and came to see what was going on. She saw the dollhouse in chaos and the twins fighting. She was not happy. She took the ruler from Lily and the hat and glasses from Ben. She made them seat on the couch and gave them a lecture. She told them they had to share and play nicely. She told them they had to be gentle and polite. She told them they had to clean up the dollhouse and say sorry to each other. Lily and Ben felt sorry. They knew they had done wrong. They hugged each other and said sorry. They went to the dollhouse and cleaned up the mess. They decided to play something else. They decided to play family. They agreed that Lily would be the mother and Ben would be the father. They smiled and had fun.
