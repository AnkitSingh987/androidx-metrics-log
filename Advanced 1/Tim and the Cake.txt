Title: Tim and the Cake

One day, a big bird with many pretty feathers was flying in the sky. The bird saw a small boy named Tim. Tim was eating a bitter apple. The bird wanted to help Tim, so it flew down to the boy. "Hi, Tim! I see you are eating a bitter apple. Let me help you," said the bird. The bird gave Tim a sweet candy. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, bird! This candy is so good. Can we be friends?" "Yes, Tim! We can be best friends," said the bird. They played together every day. One day, the bird saw Tim's mom making a cake. The bird wanted to help, but it did not know how to make a cake. The bird tried to help, but it spoiled the cake by mistake. Tim's mom was sad, but Tim said, "It's okay, bird. We can make a new cake together." The bird was happy, and they all made a new cake. The bird learned how to make a cake, and they had a fun time.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a ride on a messy helicopter. The helicopter was big and had lots of colors. Tim was very happy to ride in the sky. As they went up, up, up, Tim saw a big bird. The bird said, "Hello, Tim! Do you like to ride in the messy helicopter?" Tim said, "Yes, I do! It is so much fun!" Then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to clean the helicopter with its wings. The helicopter became shiny and new. Tim and the bird laughed and played in the sky. They had the best day ever.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a daddy who wanted to go on a picnic. The mommy and daddy said “Let’s go on a picnic!”. So the mommy and daddy got all the picnic things ready. They packed lots of yummy foods and drinks for their picnic. They also brought some silly toys for the picnic. “Come on, everyone! Let’s go to the park for a picnic!” said the mommy. So the family went to the park. The kids were really excited and started running around in the park. They played with the silly toys and ran around like silly silly kids. The mommy and daddy called out to the kids. “Come here, help me spread out the picnic blanket!” said the mommy. The kids all ran over to help set up the blanket. Then the mommy and daddy spread out all the yummy food and drinks on the blanket. “Yay, picnic time!” shouted the kids in joy. They ate the yummy food and played with their silly toys. It was a very fun picnic!

Once upon a time, there was a powerful dog named Max. Max liked to collect sticks. One day, he saw a big billboard. The billboard had a picture of a dog with a stick. Max said to his friend, Tim the cat, "I want to collect sticks like the dog on the billboard!" Tim said, "Let's go to the park and find sticks!" At the park, Max and Tim found many sticks. They collected them in a big pile. Max was very happy. He felt like the dog on the billboard. Tim was happy too, because he helped his friend. They played with the sticks all day long.

Once upon a time there was a boy who wanted something very special. He wished very hard for something but he couldn't just wish it into existence. He had to do something to make his wish come true. The boy decided to attach the wish he made to a colorful beautiful balloon. He was very sure the balloon was strong enough to carry the wish. The boy released the balloon and watched it softly float up into the sky. He wanted his wish to reach the clouds and come true. The boy's mom asked him what he was up to. He told her that he was sending a wish to the sky. His mom said, "Son, I am sure that your wish is going to come true. Just be patient and calm." The boy smiled, and imagined his wish flying high in the sky. He said, "I am sure it will".
