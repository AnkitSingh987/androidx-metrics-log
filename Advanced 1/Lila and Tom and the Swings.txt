Title: Lila and Tom and the Swings

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big cage with a parrot inside. The parrot had bright feathers and a sharp beak. It was making loud noises. "Hello, parrot!" Lila said. She wanted to be friends with the parrot. "Hello, hello!" the parrot said. It copied Lila's voice. Tom was curious. He went closer to the cage. He tried to touch the parrot's feathers. "Ouch!" Tom cried. The parrot bit his finger. It was fierce. "Bad, bad!" the parrot said. It copied Tom's voice. Lila and Tom ran away from the cage. They were scared of the parrot. "Let's go to the swings," Lila said. She wanted to have fun. "OK," Tom said. He wanted to forget the parrot. They turned away from the cage and went to the swings. They did not look at the parrot again.

One day, a little gray bird went out to see the world. It was spring, and the sun was bright in the sky. The bird was very happy to fly and sing. As the bird flew, it saw a big gray rock. The bird thought the rock was a friend, so it went to say hello. But when the bird got close, the rock started to move! The bird was very surprised. The rock was not a rock at all! It was a big gray turtle! The turtle was happy to see the bird too. They became friends and played together all day in the warm spring sun. The bird and the turtle had a lot of fun, and they both learned that new friends can be found in the most unexpected places.

One day, a colorful bird appeared in the sky. It was so pretty and bright. The bird saw a boy playing in the park. The boy was playing with scissors, cutting paper. Suddenly, the wind blew the paper away. The boy was sad. The colorful bird saw this and flew down. The bird picked up the paper with its beak and gave it back to the boy. The boy was so happy. He said, "Thank you, bird!" But then, something unexpected happened. The bird turned into a girl! The girl said, "I am a magic girl, and I was a bird for a day. You were kind to me, so I want to be your friend." The boy was surprised but happy. They played together all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time there was an infant called Tom. Tom was very attractive and he loved to play. Tom's mother always explained things to him carefully, but he never seemed to understand. One day, Tom's mother said, "Tom, you must be very careful when you play outside. Do you understand?" Tom shook his head, but he still didn't understand. So Tom's mother said again, "Tom, if you are not careful, you could get hurt. Do you understand now?" But Tom didn't understand. Tom went outside to play and he had so much fun. He forgot about his mother's warnings and he didn't understand how dangerous it was. Unfortunately, Tom got hurt and his mother had to take him to hospital. Tom never understood his mother's warnings in time, and he never got to play outside again.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Navy. Navy had a special power. He could glow in the dark. One night, Navy went out to play with his friends. Navy said, "Look! I can glow!" His friends were happy and amazed. They played hide and seek in the dark. Navy's glow helped them find each other easily. They had a lot of fun. After playing, Navy's friends said, "Thank you, Navy!" They learned that sharing your special gifts with others can make everyone happy. And that was the moral of the story.

One day, a curious little cat went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to look at it. She walked closer and closer to the tree. When she got to the tree, she looked up and saw a bird. The bird looked at her and said, "Hello, little cat!" The cat lifted her hand to wave at the bird. The cat and the bird became friends. They played and looked at things together. The curious cat was very happy to have a new friend.
