Title: The Princess and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a small princess who liked to wander in the big garden. One day, she saw a tiny door she had never seen before. She thought, "I want to see what is behind this door." The small princess opened the door and walked in. She saw a big, pretty butterfly with many colors. The butterfly said, "Hello, princess! I can give you a wish if you help me find my friend, the caterpillar." The princess felt happy and said, "Yes, I will help you!" They looked and looked for the caterpillar. The princess found a small, green caterpillar under a leaf. The butterfly was so happy to see her friend! But then, something unexpected happened. The caterpillar turned into another beautiful butterfly! The first butterfly said, "Thank you, princess! Now you can have your wish." The small princess wished for more friends to play with, and the two butterflies stayed with her forever.

John was very excited. He was going on a plane for the first time! His mom was taking him to the airport. John saw the airport when they arrived. It looked big with many cars outside. He knew it was the place he was going to the sky on a plane. John and his mom went inside the airport. He saw many people and he wanted to talk to them but his mom said no. She told him to be careful. At the gate, John saw a plane outside the window. He was so excited! He couldn't believe it. John's mom said, “Look, that plane is here for us. We’re going to fly in it!” John was so happy. Suddenly, a man appeared in a careless way. He handed John’s mom some papers and said, “This is your boarding pass.” John's mom thanked the man and they both prepared to get on the plane. John was so excited, he felt like he was going to burst!

Once upon a time, there was a little brown dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, he saw a new friend, a big brown bear named Ben. Spot wanted to invite Ben to play with him and his friends. Spot went to Ben and said, "Hi, Ben! Do you want to play with us?" Ben smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you and your friends!" So, Spot and Ben went to the park together to play with all the other animals. As they played, they saw a beautiful image in the sky. It was a big rainbow with many colors. All the animals looked up and smiled. They were so happy to see the rainbow and to have a new friend in Ben. They played together all day long, and from that day on, Ben was always invited to play with Spot and his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Brown. He had a big house and lots of toys. Mr. Brown loved pasta. He ate it for lunch and dinner every day. His favorite pasta was long and thin like a snake. He liked to eat it with red sauce and cheese. One day, Mr. Brown heard a whisper in his kitchen. He went to see who was there. He saw a little mouse on the floor. The mouse was eating the pasta that Mr. Brown dropped on the floor. The mouse whispered, "Thank you for the pasta, Mr. Brown." Mr. Brown smiled and whispered back, "You're welcome, little mouse." From that day on, Mr. Brown and the little mouse became friends. They shared pasta and played with toys every day. They were very happy together.

One day, a pink bunny and a little boy went to play in the park. They saw a big tree with many roots. The pink bunny said, "Look at the big tree! Let's play here." As they played, the pink bunny tripped on a root. The little boy helped the bunny up and said, "Be careful of the roots." The bunny said, "Thank you, friend." Just then, a big wind came and pushed the tree. The tree started to talk! It said, "I am a magic tree. Thank you for being nice to my roots." The tree gave the boy and the pink bunny a big surprise. They got to fly in the sky and play all day.
