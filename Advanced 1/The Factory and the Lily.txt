Title: The Factory and the Lily

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big factory. In this factory, they made things to help people heal when they got hurt. One day, a little girl named Lily got a small cut on her knee. She was very sad and cried a little. Lily's mom saw her crying and said, "Don't worry, Lily. We can go to the factory to get something to help your knee heal." So, they walked to the factory together. Inside the factory, they met a kind man named Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown saw Lily's knee and said, "Oh no, that looks serious. Let me give you a special band-aid to help you heal." He gave Lily the band-aid and put it on her knee. Lily felt better and smiled. "Thank you, Mr. Brown!" said Lily. She and her mom left the factory, happy that Lily's knee would heal soon. From that day on, Lily knew that the factory was a special place that helped people feel better.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell his friends what to do. He lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. One day, Sue's mom gave her a new toy, a radio. Sue loved her radio and wanted to play with it all day. Tom, the bossy cat, wanted to play with the radio too. But Sue did not permit Tom to touch it. Tom was sad and felt left out. Later, Sue saw that Tom was sad. She thought about it and decided to let Tom play with the radio too. They both danced and listened to fun songs. They laughed and had a great time. In the end, Tom learned not to be so bossy and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. Jane loved summertime because it was very nice. She liked to go outside and play. One of her favorite things to do was spin. Jane liked to spin around and around until she got dizzy. She liked it a lot because it made her giggle. When she was spinning around, the sun shone on her and it made her feel warm. She also liked that her skirt twirled around her as she spun. So each summer, Jane would go outside and spin around as much as she wanted. She would do it in the morning, in the afternoon and sometimes she'd even do it at night. It made her so happy, so she kept spinning and spinning, until it was time for the summer to end. Jane was sad to see the summer go, but all the spinning and fun she had made the summer nice. Jane hoped to get just as many spins in the next summer!

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play hide and seek. One day, they decided to play in the house while their mom was doing laundry. Ben said, "I will count to ten and you hide. No peeking!" Lily nodded and ran to find a good spot. She saw a big basket full of clothes in the hallway. She thought, "This is a perfect place to hide. Ben will never find me here." She climbed into the basket and covered herself with a blanket. She waited for Ben to come and look for her. But Ben did not come. He looked in every room, under every bed, behind every door, but he could not find Lily. He started to feel worried and scared. He called, "Lily, where are you? This is not funny. Come out, please!" But there was no answer. Meanwhile, their mom finished the laundry and saw the basket in the hallway. She did not know that Lily was inside. She thought, "I need to put these clothes in the dryer." She picked up the basket and carried it to the laundry room. She opened the dryer and dumped the clothes inside. She did not see Lily's face under the blanket. Lily felt the basket moving and heard a loud noise. She was confused and frightened. She tried to scream, but the blanket muffled her voice. She felt hot and dizzy. She thought, "What is happening? Where am I? Where is Ben?" Ben heard the dryer running and had an idea. He ran to the laundry room and opened the dryer. He saw a pile of clothes and a blanket. He pulled the blanket and saw Lily's face. She was sweaty and scared. He hugged her and said, "Lily, I found you! Are you okay? I was so worried. Why did you hide in the basket?" Lily cried and said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I thought it was a good place to hide. I didn't know mom would take me to the dryer. It was hot and loud and scary. I'm sorry, Ben." Ben said, "It's okay, Lily. I'm glad you're safe. But next time, don't hide in the laundry. It's dangerous and not fun. Let's hide in ordinary places, like the closet or the sofa. Okay?" Lily nodded and said, "Okay, Ben. I learned my lesson. Let's hide in ordinary places. And let's tell mom we're sorry for making her worry." They hugged each other and went to find their mom. They apologized and told her what happened. Their mom was relieved and angry. She said, "You two are very lucky. You could have been hurt or worse. Don't ever hide in the laundry again. Do you understand?" They said, "Yes, mom. We understand. We're sorry, mom." Their mom said, "I love you, but you have to be careful and smart. Hide and seek is a fun game, but you have to follow the rules and respect the house. Now, go and play somewhere else. And be nice to each other." They said, "We love you, too, mom. We will follow the rules and respect the house. And we will be nice to each other." They kissed their mom and went to play somewhere else. They hid in ordinary places and had fun. They never hid in the laundry again. They learned their lesson.
