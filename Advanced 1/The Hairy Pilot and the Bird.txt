Title: The Hairy Pilot and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a hairy pilot. He was very good at flying. He had a big plane that he loved. Every day, he would fly in the sky and look at the world below. One day, the hairy pilot was flying when he saw something strange. There was a big bird flying next to his plane. The bird was very big and had many colors. The pilot was surprised because he had never seen a bird like that before. The big bird looked at the hairy pilot and said, "Hello, I am a magic bird. I can make wishes come true." The pilot was very happy and asked the bird to make him the best pilot in the world. The magic bird said, "Okay, you are now the best pilot in the world." And just like that, the hairy pilot became the best pilot ever. He was very happy and flew even higher in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a dolphin who lived in the sea. She was a very purple dolphin, and her favourite pastime was to sing. One day, she was happily singing and swimming when suddenly, she saw a big iceberg melting in the sea. She was so amazed that she forgot to sing. "Oh wow – look at that iceberg melting!", she shouted. A nearby fish swam towards her and asked, "What is melting?" "Icebergs melt!", the dolphin said. The fish was very impressed and asked, "Can I watch you sing?" The dolphin smiled and said, "Yes, I'd love that! Let's sing together!" So, the dolphin and the fish sang songs together, and the iceberg melted even more. The dolphin was very happy and knew she had made a great friend.

One day, Tom and Sue went for a drive. They wanted to see the big hill. The sky was gloomy and gray, but they did not care. They were happy to go on a fun trip. On the way, they saw a chain on the road. Tom said, "Sue, look at the chain!" Sue said, "Let's pick it up!" They stopped the car and picked up the chain. They put it in the car and kept driving. Soon, it started to rain. The rain made the road very wet. Tom and Sue felt sad. The car slipped on the wet road and hit a tree. The car was broken, and they could not drive anymore. Tom and Sue had to walk home in the rain, feeling sad and gloomy.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lily. Lily had a big, shiny crown. She loved to sit and wear her crown all day. One day, Lily went to the park with her crown on her head. At the park, Lily saw a dog named Max. Max looked at Lily and said, "Wow! You have a pretty crown!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Max! I like to wear it every day." They played together in the park, and Lily's crown sparkled in the sun. As they played, a big wind came and blew Lily's crown away. Lily and Max were sad. But then, they saw a little bird with the crown on its head. The bird said, "I found this crown! Can I wear it too?" Lily thought about it and said, "Yes, you can wear it too. We can all share the crown and be friends." So, Lily, Max, and the bird played together and shared the crown, and they all had a happy day.

Once upon a time, there was a boring, old hedge. It was not fun to look at. The hedge was in a big yard. The yard had lots of grass and flowers. But the hedge was not pretty like the flowers. One day, a little bug came to the hedge. The bug was very small. It liked to eat plants. The bug saw the hedge and thought, "Yum! This will be a good snack!" The bug started to eat the hedge. The hedge began to rot. Soon, the hedge was not boring anymore. It was a home for the little bug. The bug was happy in its new home. The hedge was not pretty, but it was not boring either. And they both lived happily ever after.
