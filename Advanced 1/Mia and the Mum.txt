Title: Mia and the Mum

Mia and her family had a very special day. Mia wanted to go to the circus. She was so excited. Her mum said, “We will have to weigh it.” Mia wanted it so much she said, “Please Mum, please” in a loud voice. Mia's mum smiled and said, “Let us go.” They went to the circus and Mia had a great time. She saw many animals like elephants and tigers. She also saw a clown and it made her laugh loudly. When Mia got home, she wished she could go back to the circus. She said to her mum, “I wish I could stay at the circus forever.” Her mum said, “I know you would like that, but we will have to weigh our options.” Mia smiled and said, “Yes, I will weigh my wishes.”

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. They were always happy and had amazing fun together. But one day, they started to quarrel about who was the best at running. Their worry grew bigger and bigger, and they decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. They asked a wise owl to watch and say who won. The owl agreed and told them to run to a big tree far away and come back. The cat and dog were so excited that they forgot their quarrel and started the race. As they ran, a big twist happened. A sneaky fox, who was watching them, saw a chance to have some fun. He quickly dug a big hole in the ground while the cat and dog were far away. When the cat and dog came running back, they both fell into the hole! The wise owl saw this but could not help them. The cat and dog were stuck in the hole, and their amazing fun was over. Their worry and quarrel led them to a bad ending.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He had a small metal toy car. It was very important to him. He played with it every day. Tim's mom said, "Tim, keep your toy car safe. It is important." One day, Tim went to the park with his friend, Sam. They played with the toy car together. They had a lot of fun. But then, a big dog came. The dog took the toy car in its mouth and ran away. Tim and Sam were very sad. Tim's mom saw them and asked, "What happened?" Tim said, "A big dog took my important toy car." Tim's mom hugged him and said, "It's okay. We can get a new one." Tim and Sam looked for the dog, but they could not find it. The next day, Tim and Sam went to the park again. They saw the big dog with a little girl. The little girl was very happy with the toy car. Tim thought for a moment and said, "I think she needs it more than me. I can get a new one, but she looks very happy with it." Sam agreed and they both learned to share and care for others. The metal toy car was important, but making others happy was more important.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. He had a little sister named Lily. They liked to play together in their big yard. One day, they saw a baby bird on the ground. The bird could not fly. Tim said, "Lily, let's help the bird. We can raise it and teach it to fly." Lily said, "Yes, Tim! We can be the bird's family." They took the bird home and gave it food and a warm place to sleep. Every day, Tim and Lily took care of the bird. They were very kind to it. One day, something unexpected happened. The bird started to change. It grew bigger and bigger. The bird was not a bird at all! It was a dragon! The dragon was very happy that Tim and Lily helped it. The dragon became their friend and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a small house with a porch. On the porch, a little dog named Bobo lived. Bobo was a very sad and miserable dog. He felt very lonely because he had no friends. Every day, he would sit on the porch all by himself and watch the world go by. One sunny day, Bobo saw a little bird fly down and land on the porch. The bird was lost and had no place to go. Bobo felt bad for the bird and welcomed the bird to stay with him on the porch. The bird was very happy and they became friends. But one day, the bird found out that it could talk! The bird started to tell Bobo stories about faraway places and the friends it had met. Bobo was no longer miserable, he was very happy to have a friend who could talk. They spent all their days together on the porch, chatting and laughing. And from that day on, the porch became the happiest place in the world for Bobo and his new friend.
