Title: The Ball and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little ball. The ball was gold and shiny. It liked to bounce all day long. It lived in a big house with a clear window. The ball could see outside and watch the birds. One day, the ball saw a big tree. The tree had a swing. The ball wanted to bounce on the swing. It bounced and bounced all the way to the tree. The ball was happy. Soon, the little gold ball met a new friend. The friend was a red ball. They both liked to bounce. They bounced together on the swing. They had lots of fun. The two balls became best friends and bounced together every day.

One day, a pale mosquito flew into a house. It was very thirsty and wanted to drink. The mosquito saw a little boy with a big glass of juice. The mosquito thought, "I can drink that!" The mosquito flew closer to the boy and the juice. Just as it was about to drink, the little boy saw the mosquito. The boy was not scared. He smiled and said, "Hello, mosquito! Are you thirsty, too?" The mosquito was very surprised. It did not think that the boy would be nice. The boy shared his juice with the mosquito. They both drank and became friends. And from that day on, the mosquito did not bother the boy or his family anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, he found a phone on the floor. Tim was confused. He did not know what to do with the phone. So, he decided to reverse and go back to his mom. When Tim got to his mom, he showed her the phone. She was surprised. She told Tim that the phone belonged to their neighbor, Mr. Brown. Tim felt happy that his mom knew who the phone belonged to. They went to Mr. Brown's house to give him the phone. Mr. Brown was very happy to see his phone. He thanked Tim and his mom. He gave Tim a big hug. The moral of the story is to always ask for help when you are confused. You might make someone else happy too.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had an ordinary notebook. She liked to draw and write in it every day. She drew her family, her friends, and her pets. She also wrote about her day and her dreams. One sunny day, Lily took her notebook outside to play. She was drawing a big bird when a strong wind blew. The wind made her notebook fly up into the sky. Lily was sad and did not know what to do. She looked up and saw her notebook flying higher and higher. Just then, a nice bird saw Lily and her flying notebook. The bird flew up high and caught the notebook in its beak. The bird brought the notebook back down to Lily. She was so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Lily and the bird were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. He had many different cars in his box. But Tim did not have a track to race his cars on. He wanted a track so much. One day, Tim's mom said, "Let's go to the store and buy something special!" Tim was very excited. He hoped that they would buy a track for his cars. At the store, Tim saw a big track with hills and turns. He asked his mom, "Can we buy this track, please?" His mom said, "Yes, we can buy this track!" Tim was so happy. They took the track home and set it up. Tim raced his different cars on the track all day. He knew that going to the store with his mom would be a special day.

It was a sunny day and Billy was walking through the park with his mom. As they stopped to look at the flowers, Billy saw a little bunny. He ran up to it and the bunny hopped away. Billy's mom just smiled and said, "Isn't he amazing?". She then took out an invitation from her pocket and showed it to him. Billy looked at it. It was an invitation to a birthday party! He was so excited. He jumped up and down, screaming and said, "Can I go?". His mother nodded and said, "Yes, of course you can". Billy was so excited, he wanted to go to the party right away. He thanked his mom and gave her a big hug. She put the invitation away and said, "Let's go get ready". Together they got ready for the party. Billy grabbed his favorite toy and dressed up in his best clothes. Then they left to have an amazing time at the party.
