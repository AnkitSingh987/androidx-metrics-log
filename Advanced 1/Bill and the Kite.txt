Title: Bill and the Kite

One day, Bill was standing outside in the icy cold. His blouse was blowing around in the wind like a kite. Suddenly, Bill watched as a pink blouse dropped from the sky! He looked all around, but there was no one in sight. “Who dropped this?” Bill said. Suddenly, a voice whispered, “Me!” Bill was very surprised. He peeked around the corner and saw a tiny girl with a pink hat. “What’s your name?” asked Bill. The girl looked up, smiled and said, “My name is Bill! Just like you!” Bill laughed. “Why did you drop your blouse?” The girl grinned and said, “I wanted to see if it could fly like a kite!” Bill laughed and said, “It looks like it did!” The girl smiled and said, “Yay! Let’s go get my blouse before it gets too icy!” So, Bill and the little girl went to pick up her blouse. She flew it around like a kite, and Bill laughed at how happy she was. They had a nice, icy time together and Bill never forgot his unusual adventure with a little girl named Bill and her pink blouse.

One day, a girl named Lucy went with her mom and dad to a big purple hotel. They were going to stay there for a few days. Lucy was very happy because she loved the color purple. In the hotel, Lucy saw a big part with lots of kids playing. She wanted to play too. But she was shy and didn't know how to make friends. Lucy felt sad and sat in a corner. Then, a nice boy named Tom came to Lucy. He asked her to play with him and the other kids. Lucy was happy and they all played together. They had lots of fun. In the end, Lucy learned that it's good to be kind and make new friends.

Alice and her mommy had a special day. They went to the park and Alice remembered the fun she had playing on the swing. Mommy asked Alice if she wanted to go home, but Alice said no. She noticed a big network of ropes and logs in the middle of the park and she wanted to explore. Alice started climbing but soon she realized that she was feeling sleepy. She told her mommy that she was feeling very tired and needed a nap. But her mommy just sighed and said no. Alice remembered being told not to climb the network and she started to cry. She wanted to be home cuddled with her mommy, listening to her bed time stories. But her mommy told her she had to stay there, that she was too sleepy to go home. Alice started to feel really scared and she tried to scream, but nobody could hear her. She was stuck, alone, in the middle of the park, tired and scared, until it was dark.

Once upon a time there was a wealthy family. The family had a bright and happy little girl named Rosie. One day a new word arrived at Rosie's house. "What is this?" Rosie asked her mom. "It's a new word," her mom said. "What does it mean?" Rosie asked. Her mom smiled and said, "It means to add something new to your life." Rosie thought about the new word for a moment. She was excited to add something new to her life! She went around the house and asked people if they knew anything she could add. Everyone had different ideas, but Rosie was looking for something special. Then one day, Rosie got an idea. She would add her own drawing to her bedroom wall! She gathered her supplies and made a beautiful picture that she was proud of. Each time Rosie looked at her drawing she smiled, knowing that she had added something special to her life. The end.

Lily wanted to play with her dolls, but her brother Max refused to share. He said they were his dolls and he did not like Lily. Lily was sad and angry. She went to her room and slammed the door. She looked around her room and saw her cupboard. She opened it and found a big box. She did not know what was in the box. She was curious and excited. She opened the box and saw many beautiful things. There were books, puzzles, stickers, crayons, and a teddy bear. She took out the teddy bear and hugged it. It was soft and warm. She felt happy and calm. She named the teddy bear Timmy and decided to read a book with him. She picked a book with pictures of animals and opened it. She heard a knock on the door. It was Max. He said he was sorry for being mean and he wanted to play with her. He asked if he could see what was in the box. Lily thought for a moment. She remembered how Max refused to share his dolls. She felt hurt and angry. She wanted to say no. But then she looked at Timmy and the book. She remembered how happy they made her. She wanted to share her happiness with Max. She thought he might like the puzzles or the stickers. She decided to be kind and forgive him. She said yes and opened the door. She showed him the box and invited him to play with her. Max smiled and thanked her. He said she was a good sister and he loved her. Lily smiled and hugged him. She said she loved him too. They played together and had fun.
