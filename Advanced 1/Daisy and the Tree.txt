Title: Daisy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Daisy. Daisy was dependable and she always did whatever her mom asked her to do. One day, Daisy went for a walk in the forest with her mom. They noticed a big, old tree and Daisy admired its big, grey trunk. Her mom bent down and picked up a twig from the ground. She showed it to Daisy and said: “Look Daisy, this is a twig. Can you imagine how old this tree must be?” Daisy looked at the twig and smiled. Then she turned and looked at the tree and said: “I admire the tree!” Daisy’s mom smiled and they both admired the tree together. Then they went back home, feeling content.

Once upon a time there was a little girl with her mommy. Her mommy was worried because the little girl was always so sad. One day, the little girl and her mommy went outside. As they were walking, they heard a loud sound. To their surprise, they saw a big flame. The little girl was so excited. She said to her mommy: "Mommy, can I add this flame to our house?" Her mommy smiled and said: "No, sweetheart, that's not a good idea. Flames can be dangerous. We need to be careful. Let's just watch it for a bit." The little girl was excited, but also a bit worried about what to do next. Her mommy hugged her and said: "Don't worry, sweetheart. It will be okay. Let's go inside now and have some hot chocolate. That will make you feel better." The little girl held her mommy's hand and they walked back inside. The flame stayed outside and lit up the night sky.

Once upon a time, there was a big, black raven. The raven was unusual because it had a red spot on its head. One day, it flew away from its home to find food. The raven flew and flew until it saw a big tree with a lot of fruit. The raven ate and ate until it was full. It was happy and wanted to return home to its family. But, the raven did not know how to go back. The raven saw a big, yellow sun in the sky. It thought the sun could help it find the way home. So, the raven flew up to the sun. But, the sun was very hot! The raven got too close to the sun and its red spot turned into a big, red burn. The raven could not return home and was lost forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The bug was very alert. He saw a pretty light far away. The bug wanted to go see the light. He started to walk to the light. The bug had to struggle to get to the light. He had to climb over big rocks and swim in deep water. The bug was very tired, but he did not give up. He kept going and going. Finally, the bug got to the light. But the light was not what he thought it would be. It was a big, scary monster! The bug was scared, but he was brave. He said "hello" to the monster. The monster was nice and said "hello" back. The bug and the monster became friends. The moral of the story is to never give up and not to judge someone by their looks.

One day, a girl named Lily was in her room. She saw a table appear. Lily was very surprised. On the table, there was a big, comfortable chair. Lily sat on the chair and said, "Wow, this chair is so nice!" A cat jumped on the table. The cat said, "Hello, Lily! I am a magic cat. I can grant you one wish." Lily thought for a moment and said, "I wish for a big, yummy cake!" The cat waved its tail, and a big cake appeared on the table. Lily was so happy. She started to eat the cake. But then, something unexpected happened. The cake turned into a big pile of fruits! Lily was shocked. The cat laughed and said, "I am a tricky cat! I hope you like fruits too!" Lily smiled and said, "That's okay, I like fruits too. Thank you, magic cat!"
