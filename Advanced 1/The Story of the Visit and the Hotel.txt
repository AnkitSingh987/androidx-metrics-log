Title: The Story of the Visit and the Hotel

Once upon a time, there was a girl who wanted to go on a very fun visit. She asked her mom and dad if they could take her to a big, fancy hotel. Her parents said yes and took her for a spicy visit. When she arrived at the hotel, she was so excited. She saw so many interesting things and it was even more fun than she thought it could be. She jumped and laughed, and even tried some of the spicy food. Finally, it was time to go home. The girl was sad to leave but she knew she would be back to visit the hotel again soon.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, a panda named Pandy lived with his family. Pandy was a regular panda who liked to eat bamboo and play with his friends. One day, Pandy met a new friend named Tiny. Tiny was a small bird who liked to sing. One sunny day, Pandy and Tiny were playing near the river. Suddenly, they heard a shout. They saw a baby panda stuck in a tree. Pandy and Tiny wanted to help the baby panda. Pandy tried to climb the tree, but he was too big. Tiny had an idea. He flew up to the baby panda and helped him get down. After the baby panda was safe, Pandy and Tiny were very happy. They learned that it was good to help others in need. The baby panda thanked Pandy and Tiny, and they all became friends. From that day on, they always helped others and had lots of fun together. And the moral of the story is: no matter how big or small you are, you can always help someone in need.

Milo saw the little kite. It was blue and purple with yellow stripes. He liked it very much. The sunny day was perfect for flying a kite. He ran to the field to fly it. Milo found a spot and started running as fast as he could to make it fly in the air. He ran and ran and finally the kite was up there so high. Milo loved it. Butwind started to blow and the kite got caught in one of the trees. Milo couldn't get it. He was so sad and angry. Then he saw a tall man coming closer. This man was filthy. He offered to help Milo get the kite. Milo said yes and the man climbed the tree and tore the kite out of the branches. Milo was so happy! He thanked the man and ran home with his kite. He never saw the tall, filthy man again.

Once upon a time, there lived a small rabbit. She had white fur and big ears. Every day she hopped around looking for food to eat. One day, the rabbit saw a big red apple near a lake. She was excited and quickly jumped across the lake to get it. But then, bad luck! As soon as she landed, the rabbit broke her toe. She yelped in pain and fell to the ground. Tears started rolling down her face. Then a magical fairy appeared. She waved her wand and said, “I will heal your toe!” The rabbit was so excited and thanked the fairy. But when the healing was done, the rabbit saw that her toe was still broken. The fairy shook her head sadly and said, “I can only do so much. You will have to live with your broken toe forever.” The rabbit was so upset. She couldn’t bear the thought of hopping around with a broken toe. But it was too late - she had to, and all she could do was burst into tears.

Lila and Tom are playing in the park. They see a big band with drums and horns. They like the music and the colors. They want to join the band. "Can we play with them?" Lila asks Tom. "Maybe. Let's go and see," Tom says. They run after the band. They try to copy the steps and the sounds. They turn left and right. They march fast and slow. They make loud and soft noises. The band leader sees them. He smiles and waves. He likes the children. He stops the band and talks to them. "Hello, little ones. Do you like our band?" he asks. "Yes, we do. You are very good," Lila says. "Thank you. Do you want to march with us?" he asks. "Yes, please. Can we?" Tom says. "Of course. Here, take these hats and flags. Follow me and the band. We will march to the clear lake. It is very pretty there," he says. Lila and Tom are very happy. They put on the hats and flags. They follow the band leader and the band. They march to the clear lake. They have fun and make new friends. They love the band.
