Title: Jack and the Car

Jack and his mom went to the store. Jack saw something shiny and said, "Mommy look!". It was a silver car. Jack was so excited to see it. Jack said to Mom, "Can we buy it?". Mom said with a laugh, "No, it is too big and too expensive". Jack was so sad. But then, Jack saw something else. It was a silver tricycle. It was so pretty and shiny. Jack asked, "Mommy, can I have this?". Mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can". Jack was so happy he got his new silver tricycle. He put on his helmet and took it for a ride around the store. He had so much fun and was very spoiled by his Mom.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the yard. He saw a big rope. Tim wanted to pull the rope. He called his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, come and pull the rope with me!" Tim said. Sam came running to the yard. He saw the rope and said, "Wow, this rope is very big! Let's pull it together!" They both grabbed the rope and started to pull. They pulled and pulled, but the rope was very strong. As they pulled, a rich man came out of his big house. He was angry. "Stop pulling my rope!" he yelled. But Tim and Sam did not hear him. They kept pulling the rope. The rich man became very mad. He took the rope away and told them to leave his yard. Tim and Sam were sad and had to go home.

Jacky and John were best friends. They sat together in class every day and shared stories with each other. One day, the teacher put a blackboard in front of the class and asked them to write on it. John was excited and wanted to be the first to write on the blackboard. "Jacky, can I be the first to write on the blackboard?" he asked. Jacky shook his head. "No, I hate blackboards. I never like to write on them," he replied. John was surprised. Blackboards were rare now, and he wanted to write on one. "Why don't you like blackboards, Jacky?" Jacky looked down. "They remind me of when I was little. My parents would make me stand in front of the blackboard and write something. I don't like it," he said. John felt sorry for Jacky and gave him a hug. "It's alright, we can play something else if you don't want to write on the blackboard," he said with a smile on his face. Jacky smiled back and hugged John back. They both knew that rare things like blackboards could be appreciated, despite any bad memories.

One day, a little elephant named Elly went for a walk. She saw a big, terrible bug. The bug was very scary. Elly wanted to go home, but the bug wanted to follow her. "Please, don't follow me!" Elly said to the bug. But the bug did not listen. It kept following Elly. Elly was very sad and scared. She did not know what to do. Then, Elly saw her friend, a little bird named Betty. Elly told Betty about the terrible bug. Betty had an idea. She flew up high and then came down fast. Betty hit the bug and it went away. Elly was so happy. She hugged Betty and they went home together.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth square. The square lived in a big box with other shapes. They all played together and had fun. But one day, the smooth square felt sad. It did not want to play with the other shapes. The smooth square started to suffer. One day, a little girl opened the box and found the smooth square. She saw that it was sad and wanted to help. She took the smooth square and put it with other squares. The smooth square was very happy. It did not expect to find friends like itself. But then, something unexpected happened. The little girl took some paint and painted the smooth square. It was not smooth anymore. It was now a bumpy square. The other squares did not want to play with it. The bumpy square was sad again. But then, the little girl found more bumpy squares, and they all played together. The bumpy square was happy again, and it did not suffer anymore.
