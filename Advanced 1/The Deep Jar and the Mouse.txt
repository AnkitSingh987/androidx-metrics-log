Title: The Deep Jar and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a deep jar. It was all alone in the kitchen. One day, the jar heard a sound. It was a little mouse. The mouse was sad because it could not find food. The jar had an idea. It told the mouse, "I can help you find food!" The mouse was happy. Together, they looked all around the kitchen. They looked high and low. They found a big box of yummy treats. The mouse was very happy. The mouse wanted to thank the jar. It had a big party for the jar. All the mouse friends came to the party. They all saw the deep jar and the yummy treats. They all clapped and were happy. The jar was a hero, and they all applauded.

One day, a little kitten named Fluffy went outside to play. Fluffy saw a big pile of blocks and had an idea. "I will build a house!" said Fluffy. Fluffy started to build with the blocks. A serious dog named Max came to see what Fluffy was doing. Max looked at the blocks and asked, "Can I help you build?" Fluffy smiled and said, "Yes, let's build together!" So, Fluffy and Max started to build the house with the blocks. They worked all day and built a big, beautiful house. Fluffy and Max were very proud of what they had built. They played in the house until the sun went down. At the end of the day, Fluffy and Max were very happy and tired. They went home and slept well, dreaming of the fun they had building the house together.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Max. Max lived in a hot place with his friend, a little bird named Lily. They liked to play together under the big, hot sun. One day, Lily was very sad and began to cry. Max asked, "Why do you cry, Lily?" Lily said, "I am too hot, and I can't find any shade to rest in." Max wanted to help his friend. He thought for a moment and then said, "I have an idea, Lily! Come sit under my big body. I will be your shade so you won't feel the heat anymore." Lily was happy and stopped crying. She sat under Max, and together they enjoyed the day, safe from the heat. From that day on, Max and Lily stayed close, always helping each other when they needed it.

Anna and Ben love to collect rocks. They have a big box full of rocks of different colors, shapes and sizes. They like to sort them, count them, and make patterns with them. One day, they decide to make a band with their rocks. They find some sticks and cans and make drums. They find some strings and boxes and make guitars. They find some bottles and water and make flutes. They are very proud of their instruments. They invite their friends Lily and Sam to join their band. They all wear hats and scarves and sunglasses to look cool. They make up a song about their rocks and sing it loud and clear. They have a lot of fun playing music together. Their mom and dad hear the noise and come to see what they are doing. They smile and clap and take pictures of their adorable band. They tell them they are very talented and creative. They give them cookies and juice as a reward. Anna and Ben are very happy. They thank their mom and dad and their friends. They say they love to collect rocks and make music. They say they are the best band ever.

Sam and Jake were in a hurry. They had a very special surprise. They decided they wanted to welcome the surprise in a unique way. So they hopped on their bikes and hurried down the street. They had to make sure they got to the surprise before it vanished! As they arrived their eyes widened. A big, furry bear was standing there! They were so happy to see the bear and welcomed it with giant hugs and lots of love. It was a very unique welcome. They couldn't believe their surprise. They could tell the bear was happy too with all the cuddles and it was a moment they would never forget.
