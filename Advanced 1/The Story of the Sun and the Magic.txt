Title: The Story of the Sun and the Magic

Once upon a time there was a strong and tough little tooth. He shined and sparkled in the sun. He had a very important job. Every night, he had to get polished by a magical toothbrush. One day, the tough little tooth was feeling particularly sparkly. He asked the toothbrush to polish him extra long and extra hard! The toothbrush worked its magic and made the tooth shine even brighter. The little tooth was so happy with its shiny new look that it wanted to show everyone. He ran around the house, screaming at the top of his voice, “Look at me! Look at my sparkle!” At the end of the day, the little tooth got tucked into bed with a big smile on its face. It was happy that it had been so tough, and it was incredibly proud of its bright smile.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. Her name was Maureen and she loved to play in the rain. One Sunday morning, Maureen woke up and looked out the window. She saw rain coming down and she was so excited. “Mommy, can I go outside and play in the rain?” She asked. Mommy smiled. “Yes, Maureen,” she said. “But remember, not too long so you don't get too wet.” So Maureen put on her raincoat and went outside. She had so much fun splashing in the puddles and getting soaking wet. She had to use a towel when she came back inside, but it was all worth it. The next day, Maureen got dressed and went outside. She saw how the rain had made everything so clean. She looked around her, "Isn't it nice how the rain can make everything so clean again?" Maureen was right. Rain can help clean the world and make it beautiful again. So next time it rains, remember that it can be fun to play in it, but it also helps us keep our environment clean and healthy.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the fair. They were very happy. At the fair, they saw a big wheel to ride. Tom said, "Let's ride the big wheel!" Sue said, "Yes, let's go!" While they were on the big wheel, they saw a snack stand. They were hungry. Tom said, "I want a snack!" Sue said, "Me too!" So they got off the big wheel and went to get a snack. But, when they got to the snack stand, a big bird took all the snacks! Tom and Sue were sad. Then, the big bird said, "I'm sorry, I was hungry too. Let's share the snacks." So Tom, Sue, and the big bird shared the snacks and had fun at the fair.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Maria who loved to draw. She was very creative and her work caught the eye of a bigger, meaner girl in her school. The meaner girl began to tease Maria about her drawings and, no matter how kind Maria was, the girl wouldn’t stop. Whenever Maria drew, the meaner girl would snatch her colors and hide them. Maria was so sad that she couldn’t do what she loved and nothing she said seemed to save her. She decided to come up with a creative plan to get her colors back. Maria smiled to herself as she came up with her plan. She asked her mother to buy some treats and when the meaner girl came to take the colors away again, Maria offered her the treats instead. The meaner girl was astonished and soon enough the treats were gone. As she was leaving with the empty box, she left the colors behind. Maria smiled and ran to pick up the colors. She learned that if she had been kind and saved the situation instead of getting angry, she would get a better outcome.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that could spray water. He loved to play with it outside in the sun. One day, Tim saw a big, expensive car. The car was shiny and pretty. Tim wanted to show his toy to the person in the car. He ran to the car and said, "Look at my toy! It can spray water." The man in the car smiled and said, "That's a fun toy, Tim! Can I see how it works?" Tim was happy to show the man. He sprayed water from his toy onto a flower. The man laughed and said, "Wow, Tim! Your toy is great for the plants. Thank you for showing me." Tim felt proud and waved goodbye to the man as he drove away. Later, Tim's mom called him inside for dinner. She saw his wet clothes and asked, "What happened, Tim?" Tim told her about his toy and the man with the expensive car. His mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you had fun, but now it's time to clean your ears and get ready for bed." Tim cleaned his ears and went to bed. He dreamed of more adventures with his spray toy and the shiny car.
