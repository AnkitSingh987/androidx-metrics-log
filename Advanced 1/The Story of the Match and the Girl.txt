Title: The Story of the Match and the Girl

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very proud. She had a match and wanted to show it to the world. So she kneeled down in the street. Everyone could see her. Then she lit her match and smiled. But a big gust of wind came along and blew out her match. The people only laughed at her. The little girl felt sad and embarrassed. She ran away as fast as she could. And she never tried to show off her match again.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball who loved to roll. One day, the ball found a track in the park. It was a long and fun track. The ball was very happy and wanted to roll on the track all day. The ball met a big, patient dog at the park. The dog said, "Little ball, be careful on the track. The end is not safe." The ball did not listen and just wanted to roll and have fun. The little ball rolled faster and faster on the track. It was so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The track had a big hole at the end. The little ball fell into the hole and could not get out. The patient dog tried to help, but it was too late. The little ball was stuck in the hole forever. If only the ball had listened to the dog's warning.

Once upon a time, there was a cute bunny. It was very helpful and always tried to help its friends. One night, the bunny was thirsty and went out looking for something to drink. It went through the woods and soon spotted a shelter. Then, it saw a pond nearby and excitedly hopped over to get a drink. The bunny said to himself, "I'm so thirsty. I will drink from the pond." As it bent down to drink, it heard another voice behind it. It was a bear! The bear growled. The bunny knew it was in trouble. The bear said, "I'm the owner of this shelter. You cannot drink from my pond without my permission!" The bunny was scared, but it decided to apologize to the bear. It tried to explain that it was just very thirsty. But the bear didn't seem to understand. The bear decided to teach the bunny a lesson and chased after it. The bunny was terrified. It ran as fast as it could, but the bear was faster. In the end, the bear caught the bunny and ate it. The kind bunny was gone forever. It was the last time it was helpful and tried to get a drink.

Once upon a time, there was a princess named Lily. She lived in a big castle with her family. Princess Lily loved to play games with her friends. Her favorite game was "guess the picture." She would draw a picture and her friends had to guess what it was. One sunny day, Princess Lily and her friends were playing "guess the picture" in the garden. Lily drew a picture of a big, hard rock. It was very hard for her friends to guess what it was. They guessed a ball, a house, and a tree, but they were all wrong. Princess Lily felt sad because her friends could not guess her picture. Then, Princess Lily's mom, the queen, came to the garden. She saw that Lily was sad and asked her what was wrong. Lily told her mom about the hard picture she drew. The queen smiled and said, "Sometimes, things are hard, but we should not give up. We should keep trying and help each other." Princess Lily learned that it was okay for things to be hard, and that the important thing was to keep trying and be there for her friends. From that day on, she always remembered the queen's wise words and shared them with her friends.

One day, a little boy named Mark went to the park with his mom. The sun was shining and the ground was dry. Mark loved to play on the swings and slide. He also liked to watch the police drive by in their big, blue car. Mark saw a police officer walking in the park. He was very excited and said, "Look, Mom! It's the police!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mark, they keep us safe." Mark wanted to talk to the police officer. He walked up to him and said, "Hi, I'm Mark!" The police officer smiled and said, "Hello, Mark! I'm Officer Dan." They talked about the park and the dry weather. Officer Dan showed Mark his police car. Mark loved the big, blue car and the shiny lights. They said goodbye and Mark went back to play with his mom. Mark had a great day at the park. He was happy to meet a real police officer and learn about their job. He couldn't wait to tell his friends about his new friend, Officer Dan.
