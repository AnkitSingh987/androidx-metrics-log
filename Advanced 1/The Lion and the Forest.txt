Title: The Lion and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion was powerful and strong. He lived in the forest with his friends. They played all day and had fun. The lion liked to lie down and rest when he was tired. One day, the lion smelled gas. He did not know what it was. The smell was strong and bad. He followed the smell to find out where it came from. His friends did not want to go, but the lion was brave. The lion found a big hole in the ground. The gas came from the hole. He knew it was not good. He told his friends about the hole. They all worked together to cover the hole with dirt and rocks. The powerful lion and his friends saved the forest from the bad gas. They were happy and proud. The forest was safe again. The moral of the story is to work together to solve problems and protect our home.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly reindeer. He lived in a big forest with his reindeer friends. They loved to play and run all day. The elderly reindeer was very wise and kind. He liked to help his friends when they needed him. One day, a big, mean wolf came to the forest. The wolf wanted to eat the reindeer. The reindeer were scared and did not know what to do. The elderly reindeer knew he must resist the wolf to save his friends. He thought of a smart plan. The elderly reindeer told the other reindeer to hide in a cave. Then, he put on a big, scary mask. When the wolf came, he saw the elderly reindeer with the mask and got very scared. The wolf ran away and never came back. The reindeer were safe and thanked the elderly reindeer for being brave. The moral of the story is that sometimes you need to be brave and resist your fears to protect your friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big aeroplane in the sky. He was very happy and wanted to tell his mom. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I saw a big aeroplane in the sky!" His mom smiled and gave him a big hug. The next day, it was a mild day. Tim went outside to play with his friends. They all looked up and saw the aeroplane again. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "Wow, the aeroplane is so big!" They all laughed and played under the aeroplane in the sky. At the end of the day, Tim went home and told his mom about playing with his friends and seeing the aeroplane again. His mom said, "I'm glad you had fun, Tim!" She gave him another big hug before bedtime. Tim was very happy and fell asleep dreaming about the big aeroplane in the sky.

One day, a helpful horse named Sam was walking in a big field. He saw a little rabbit stuck in a hole. The rabbit was sad and scared. Sam wanted to help the rabbit. Sam said, "Don't worry, little rabbit. I will help you." Sam used his strong legs to spring up and down. He was trying to get the rabbit out of the hole. The rabbit was happy that Sam was trying to help him. After a few tries, Sam was able to spring high enough to help the rabbit out of the hole. The rabbit was very happy and said, "Thank you, helpful horse!" Sam and the rabbit became good friends and played together in the big field every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a poor man named Tom. Tom had many stones that he wanted to sell. He thought, "If I sell these stones, I can buy food." One day, Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue saw the stones and said, "Wow! Pretty stones! Can I have one?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, but I need to sell them. Can you give me something for it?" Sue thought for a moment and said, "I have a big apple. I can give you that!" Tom was happy with the apple and gave Sue a pretty stone. They both smiled and went their own way. Tom sold more stones and got more food. He was not so poor anymore. And Sue had a new pretty stone to play with. They both lived happily ever after.
