Title: Lucy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very shy. One day, Lucy and her daddy went to the park to play. At the park, Lucy saw a boy playing with a ball. She wanted to play too, but she was too shy to ask. Her daddy said, "Go ask the boy if you can play. Just repeat after me, 'Can I play with you?'" Lucy listened to her daddy and slowly walked to the boy. Lucy was so shy that she could not say the words her daddy told her. Instead, she just took the ball from the boy. The boy was sad and started to cry. Lucy's daddy came and said, "That was not nice, Lucy. You should have asked him first." Lucy felt bad and gave the ball back to the boy. They did not play together that day. The bad ending made Lucy very sad. The moral of the story is to always ask before taking something that is not yours.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange cat named Tuesday. Tuesday loved to play with his friends outside. One sunny day, he was playing with a big red ball. Tuesday's friend, a small dog named Max, came running over. "Tuesday, I lost my toy," Max said, looking sad. Tuesday wanted to comfort his friend. "Don't worry, Max. We will find your toy," Tuesday said with a smile. They looked high and low, and finally found the toy under a green tree. Max was so happy and hugged Tuesday. "Thank you, Tuesday!" Max said. They played together for the rest of the day, feeling happy and safe.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there lived an adorable little rabbit named Remy. Remy had many friends, but he loved to play with his best friend, Tommy the turtle. They would always play fun games together. One sunny day, Remy had a question for Tommy. He asked, "Tommy, do you dare to race me to the big tree?" Tommy smiled and said, "Yes, Remy, I dare to race you. Let's go!" So, they got ready and started the race. Remy ran fast, but Tommy moved slow. Remy looked back and saw Tommy far behind. He stopped and waited for his friend. When Tommy reached Remy, they walked to the big tree together. They laughed and had a great time. From that day on, Remy and Tommy knew that they would always be best friends, no matter who won the race.

One day, a big enthusiastic dog and a little cat played in the park. The dog had a long tail, and the cat had a short tail. They liked to play and share their toys with each other. They saw a big box near a tree. The dog and the cat were curious. They went to the box and found many tails inside. The dog and the cat were very excited. The dog and the cat tried on different tails. They shared the tails and laughed. Then, a squirrel came and took the box away. The squirrel was the owner of the tails! The dog and the cat were surprised, but they still had their own tails and were happy to share their toys and play together.

One day, a bald man went to the park. He was feeling hungry, so he bought an ice cream cone. The man was happy because he loved ice cream a lot. He sat on a bench to eat his ice cream. A little bird saw the man with the ice cream cone. The bird wanted some ice cream too. So, the bird flew down and tried to take the ice cream cone from the man. The man tried to manage and hold onto his ice cream cone, but the bird was very fast. Just as the bird was about to get the ice cream cone, something unexpected happened. The ice cream fell off the cone and onto the ground! The man and the bird were both sad. But then, they saw a big ice cream truck nearby. The man bought a new ice cream cone for himself and a small one for the bird. They both ate their ice cream and became friends.
