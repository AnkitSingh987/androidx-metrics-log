Title: Tim the Boy and the Uncle

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite uncle who was very funny. One day, his uncle came to visit him. Tim was so happy to see his uncle. They played games and had fun all day. In the evening, Tim's uncle had a surprise for him. He said, "I will reveal something scary, but don't worry, it's just for fun." Tim was excited and a little scared too. His uncle went to the next room and came back with a big box. Tim's uncle opened the box and revealed a scary mask. Tim was scared at first, but then he saw his uncle's smile and started to laugh. They both put on scary masks and had fun scaring each other. At the end of the day, Tim was not scared anymore, and he loved his uncle even more.

Once upon a time, there was a long snake named Spike. Spike liked to play with his friends in the big forest. They loved to play hide and seek. One day, Spike was hiding from his friends. He found a big bush and thought, "I can cover myself with leaves!" So he went under the bush and covered himself with the green leaves. He was very good at hiding. Soon, his friend, a little bird, found him. The bird said, "Spike, I found you!" Spike laughed and said, "You are good at this game!" They played all day and had lots of fun in the big forest.

Once there was a little girl called Lee. She enjoyed playing outside in the garden with her family. One day, Lee's daddy decided to raise a rock. He pushed the rock up from the ground until it was tall. He said to Lee, "Let's make a spicy rock tower." Lee was so excited! Just like that, her daddy and her worked together to raise many rocks until the tower was complete. It was so tall that it almost reached the sky. The rocks had a spicy smell and tasted like pepper. Lee and her daddy were so proud of their tower that they hugged each other tightly. Lee knew she would never forget the day they raised their rock tower.

One day, Tom and Sue plan to build a big tower with their toy blocks. They are very excited and ready to start. They put one block on top of another, making the tower taller and taller. They are happy to see their tower grow. But then, something unexpected happens. A little bird flies through the window and lands on their tower. The tower wobbles and shakes. Tom and Sue watch as their tower starts to fall. Just as the tower is about to fall, the little bird flies away. The tower stops wobbling and stands tall again. Tom and Sue are surprised and happy. They laugh and clap their hands. The little bird helped save their tower, and they keep playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to gather pretty flowers. She had a small basket to put her flowers in. Sue thought her flowers were beautiful. One sunny day, Sue went to the park to gather more flowers. She saw many beautiful flowers there. She picked them and put them in her basket. Sue was very happy. But then, a big wind came. The wind blew Sue's basket away. All of Sue's beautiful flowers were gone. Sue was very sad. She went home without her flowers.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a perfect, big tree. The tree had a big root that stuck out of the ground. Lily wanted to play near the tree. Lily saw a small box near the root. She wanted to see what was inside. She asked her friend, Tom, "Can you help me unlock the box?" Tom said, "Yes, I can help!" They tried to unlock the box, but it was hard. Then, a little bird came and sang a song. The box opened! Inside, they found a key. They looked around and saw a small door on the tree. Lily and Tom used the key to unlock the door. They were so surprised! Inside the tree, they found a room filled with toys and games. They played all day and had lots of fun.
