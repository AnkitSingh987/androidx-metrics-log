Title: Tom the Modest Cat and the Window

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a modest cat named Tom. Tom loved to observe the world around him. Every day, he would sit by the window and watch the birds fly by. He liked the sight of the blue sky and the green grass. One day, Tom saw a little girl named Sue playing outside. Sue was laughing and having fun. Tom wanted to play too, so he went outside to meet her. "Hi, I am Tom!" he said. "Do you want to play with me?" Sue was happy to have a new friend. "Yes, Tom! Let's play!" she said. They played all day long, running and jumping in the warm sun. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, they were both very tired but very happy. From that day on, Tom and Sue were the best of friends. They played together every day, and Tom loved the sights and sounds of the world even more with Sue by his side. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a small, fearful cat named Kitty went for a walk on a trail. She wanted to see what was at the end of the trail. As she walked, she saw a big dog. The dog looked at Kitty and said, "Don't be scared, I will protect you." Kitty and the dog walked together on the trail. They saw many pretty things like flowers and trees. They talked and became good friends. Kitty was not scared anymore because the dog was with her. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog stepped on a big stick and it broke. The loud noise made Kitty jump and she got scared again. But the dog said, "Don't worry, it's just a stick. I will still protect you." Kitty felt better and they kept walking until they reached the end of the trail, where they found a beautiful pond. They played there all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big yellow house, there lived a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the sun. She would run and jump on the soft green grass in her yard. One day, Sue saw a pretty yellow butterfly. She wanted to catch it and show it to her mom. So, she ran after the butterfly. As Sue chased the butterfly, it flew over the fence and into the woods. Sue did not want to go into the woods, but she wanted to catch the butterfly. So, she went in. She walked and walked, but she could not find the butterfly. Sue was sad and wanted to return home. Sue started to walk back, but she saw something strange on the grass. It was a big yellow egg! She picked it up and carried it home. When she showed her mom the egg, it started to crack. Out came a baby yellow bird! The bird looked at Sue and said, "Thank you for finding me!" Sue was so happy. She did not catch the butterfly, but she made a new friend.

Little Timmy was feeling very scared. He was in the hospital waiting room with his mother and all the grown-ups were anxious and worried. He wanted to know what was happening but he was too scared to ask. Suddenly, a nurse came in and asked little Timmy's mother to kneel down. Little Timmy was surprised, why was the nurse asking his mother to kneel? His mother had a sad look on her face as she knelt down. Little Timmy asked nervously, "Mommy, why did the nurse ask you to kneel?" His mother put her arm around him and said softly, "Because it's time for our prayer." The nurse and his mom were kneeling in the middle of the room. They both closed their eyes and the nurse started the prayer. Little Timmy started to feel calmer. He closed his eyes too and he felt a deep peace come over him. After the prayer, the nurse opened her eyes, smiled at Timmy, and said, "God bless you, my dear." Little Timmy felt happy and safe as his mother and the nurse smiled at him. He knew that everything was going to be okay.
