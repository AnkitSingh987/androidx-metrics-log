Title: The Wise Owl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The owl lived in a big tree. The tree was in a dark forest. The owl liked the dark. It was easy for him to see at night. One day, a little girl named Lucy walked into the forest. She was looking for her lost cat. She saw the wise owl and asked, "Mr. Owl, have you seen my cat?" The owl said, "Yes, I saw your cat. It is close to the big tree." Lucy was happy and went to the big tree. But she found a dog, not her cat! The dog was nice and wagged its tail. Lucy said, "You are not my cat, but I like you too." Then, the dog barked and ran away. Lucy was sad again. But then, she heard a meow. She looked up and saw her cat in the tree! The wise owl had been right. The cat was close to the big tree. Lucy climbed the tree and saved her cat. The wise owl smiled and said, "Now you have two friends."

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Lily. One day, Lily found a note on the floor. The note had a big word on it. Lily did not know what the word meant, so she asked her mom. Her mom told her the word was "harsh". Lily asked what it meant, and her mom said it meant something not nice. Lily did not want to be not nice, so she made a promise to her mom. She said, "I will not be harsh." From that day on, Lily was always kind and nice to everyone. She kept her promise to her mom. And everyone loved Lily because she was so sweet and never harsh.

Once upon a time there was a curious little girl named Jane. Jane was a player and liked to go on all kinds of adventures. She loved to explore, so one day she decided to explore her backyard. Jane ran around the garden, pretending to be a pirate and singing all kinds of silly songs. She looked everywhere, from the grass to the trees, and even inside the flower pots. Suddenly, Jane noticed an ant hill. She felt very curious and wanted to look inside, so she laid down in the grass and started to carefully poke at it with a stick. But, before she could get too close, Jane's mother called out to her to remind her to behave. 'Behave yourself, Jane!' her mother said. Jane felt disappointed but knew her mother was right. She had to be careful when exploring and not get too curious. She took one last look at the ant hill and went back inside the house to play.

Once upon a time, there was a little monster. The monster was very hot because it was a sunny day. He wanted to play in the water, but he did not have a toy to play with. The monster went to his friend, a little girl. He asked if he could borrow her toy boat. The little girl said yes, and gave the monster her toy boat. The monster was very happy. The hot monster and the little girl went to the pond. They played with the toy boat in the cool water. They had a lot of fun. After they played, the monster gave the toy boat back to the little girl. They were very good friends.

Tom was out in the park on a foggy day. He was running and jumping, but sometimes he had to stop and take a deep breath. Whenever he did that, he let out a big sigh. Suddenly, Tom heard someone coming up close behind him. He turned and saw a nosy squirrel. Tom said, "Hi there! What are you doing?" The squirrel looked around and said, "I was just looking for something to eat." Tom smiled and said, "We could look for nuts, but the fog is so thick I can barely see anything." The squirrel thought for a moment and then said, "I know! We could make a game!" Tom was very excited. They spent the rest of the day playing and laughing, even though the fog was still thick. Every once in a while, they would both take a deep breath and let out a big sigh.
