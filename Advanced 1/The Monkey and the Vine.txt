Title: The Monkey and the Vine

Once upon a time there was a monkey who loved to eat delicious fruit. One day he stumbled upon a vine growing alongside a tall cliff. The vine had such delicious fruit that the monkey could not resist. He shimmied up the vine and began to pick the yummy fruit. Just as he was reaching for the most delicious looking fruit, an eagle suddenly appeared in the sky. It flew right at the monkey and tried to peck him! The monkey quickly grabbed onto the vine and tried to avoid the eagle's claws. The monkey was in trouble. He was too high up the vine for the eagle to reach, but the vine was becoming weak from the monkey's weight. In a desperate move to get away, the monkey quickly released its grip and jumped off the vine. The monkey's plan worked and he rose high into the sky, high enough to escape the eagle’s claws. As the eagle watched in disbelief, the monkey flew off with the delicious fruit and never looked back.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had soft fur and loved to play. One day, Max saw a big cat. The cat had crazy fur and looked scary. Max started to worry. Max said to the cat, "Why is your fur so crazy?" The cat said, "I was playing and got all messy." Max thought for a moment and said, "Let's be friends and play together." So, Max and the cat played all day. They had so much fun that Max stopped worrying. Max learned that even if someone looks different, they can still be a good friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there was a generous boy named Tim. He liked to imagine things and play with his friends. One day, Tim and his friend, Sam, were playing with their toys. "Let's imagine we are in a big race with our cars!" said Tim. Sam agreed and they started to race their toy cars. But soon, they began to argue about who was winning the race. "No, I won!" said Tim. "My car was faster!" "No way!" said Sam. "I won the race!" Tim didn't want to fight with his friend. He thought for a moment and said, "Let's just be happy that we both had fun racing our cars." Sam agreed and they both played happily together in the big valley. They learned that being generous and kind to each other was better than arguing. And they stayed best friends forever.

Lily loved waffles. She liked to eat them with honey and butter. Sometimes, Mommy made waffles for breakfast. Lily was happy when she smelled the waffles in the kitchen. She ran to the table and saw a big plate of waffles. She saw Mommy and Daddy sitting there too. They smiled at her. "Good morning, Lily," Mommy said. "Do you want a waffle?" "Yes, please," Lily said. She reached for a waffle, but Mommy stopped her. "Wait a minute, Lily," Mommy said. "We have a game for you. Can you guess what it is?" Lily looked at Mommy and Daddy. They had something behind their backs. Lily was curious. "Is it a toy?" Lily asked. "No, it's not a toy," Daddy said. "Is it a book?" Lily asked. "No, it's not a book," Mommy said. "Is it a waffle?" Lily asked. "Yes, it is a waffle!" Daddy said. He showed her a waffle with a smiley face made of berries. Mommy showed her a waffle with a heart made of whipped cream. "These are special waffles for you, Lily," Mommy said. "They are delicate and yummy. We made them with love." Lily clapped her hands. She was happy and surprised. "Thank you, Mommy and Daddy," Lily said. "You are the best. I love you and I love waffles." She hugged them and took a bite of her waffle. It was sweet and soft. She smiled too. It was a good morning.

Billy was very excited. Today he was going to the shop with his mummy. He was wearing his blue shoes and he couldn't wait to go. First they went to the bakery and bought some yummy treats. Then they went to the toy shop and Billy chose a toy car. He was so happy that he gave it a big rub. After that they went to the butcher shop. Mummy bought some sausages for dinner. Then they went to the fish shop and bought some fish for tea. Billy was so excited that he rubbed his hands together. Finally, they went to the sweet shop. Billy couldn't believe his eyes. There were lots of blue sweets in the shop and he wanted to buy them all! He gave them all a rub to check they were real.
