Title: Amy the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to dance on a stage. She had a cat named Max. Max was her best friend. Max was a very reliable friend. He always stayed by Amy's side. One day, Amy had a big dance show on the stage. She was so excited. But when she looked for Max, he was not there. Amy was sad. She did not want to lose Max. She needed her reliable friend with her. Amy and her mom looked everywhere for Max. They looked in the house, in the yard, and by the stage. Finally, they found Max sleeping under the stage. Amy was so happy to see her reliable friend. Now, she could dance on the stage with Max by her side. And they lived happily ever after.

On a cold winter's day, a little bear named Ben woke up from his deep sleep. He was shivering as he stepped outside! He looked around and saw that everything was pure and white, thanks to a thick blanket of snow. Suddenly, he heard a strange noise coming from a nearby switch. When he approached the switch, he saw a little bird perched on top of it. She was shaking, and Ben could see that she was very cold. "Why are you shaking?" asked Ben. "I'm so cold, my feathers are too thin to keep me warm!" replied the little bird. Ben had an idea! He took the bird back to his home to keep her warm and safe. He carefully tucked the bird into a healthy bed of grass and flowers. The bird was so thankful. She snuggled in and soon stopped shivering. Ben smiled with joy as he watched the little bird be safe, warm, and healthy.

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy was a very happy rabbit, but he had one problem. All the other animals in the forest would ignore him. They would not talk or play with Remy, and this made him sad. One day, Remy saw a little bird named Betty. He thought maybe Betty would not ignore him. So, Remy hopped over to Betty and said, "Hi, Betty! Will you play with me?" Betty looked at Remy and smiled. "Yes, Remy! I would love to play with you!" Remy was so happy that he jumped up and down. For a whole week, Remy and Betty played together. They ran, jumped, and laughed in the wild forest. The other animals saw how much fun they were having, and they wanted to play too. Soon, all the animals in the forest were friends, and no one was ignored anymore. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big train was on a track. The train was very clean and shiny. It had many cars for people to ride in. The train loved to go fast and never wanted to stop. The train met a little bird on the track. The bird said, "Please slow down, Mr. Train. I need to cross the track to get to my nest." The train did not want to resist going fast, but it wanted to help the bird. So, the train slowed down and let the bird cross the track. "Thank you, Mr. Train!" said the bird. The train felt happy that it helped the bird. The train continued on its track, still clean and shiny. It learned that sometimes it is good to slow down and help others.

One day, a big, gloomy shape went for a march in the park. It was a very big and dark shape, and it liked to march around. The shape saw many kids playing in the park, but it did not want to play. It just wanted to march all day. As the shape marched, it saw a small, happy shape. The small shape asked the gloomy shape, "Why do you march alone? Come play with me!" But the big shape did not want to play. It just kept marching and said, "No, I like to march alone." The small shape was sad, but it had an idea. It jumped in front of the big shape and said, "Let's march together!" The big shape was surprised, but it agreed. They started to march together, and the small shape was happy. But then, the big shape got tired of marching and stopped. The small shape was sad again because it wanted to keep marching. In the end, the small shape had to march alone, and the big shape stayed in the park, not moving at all.
