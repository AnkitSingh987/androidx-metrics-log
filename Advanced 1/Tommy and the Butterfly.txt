Title: Tommy and the Butterfly

The pit was very deep and dark. Tommy was scared of it and did not want to go in. As he walked around it he spotted a brilliant butterfly perched on the edge of the pit. Tommy crept slowly towards the butterfly but just as he reached out to touch it, the butterfly spread its wings and flew away. "Where did it go?" Tommy asked. Suddenly, Tommy heard a loud rumbling noise coming from behind him. He looked around in surprise and saw a ball of light striking across the sky. "That's a shooting star," Tommy said excitedly. He watched as the bright ball of light struck the dark pit in the ground. Tommy felt a ripple of excitement in his stomach. "It must have been a brilliant wish," he said. "I sure hope it comes true!"

One day, a sweet little girl named Lily went outside to play in the snow. She wore her warm coat and her favorite mittens. Her mom said, "Lily, don't lose your mittens!" While playing, Lily met a little boy named Tommy. Tommy wanted to make a snowman, but his hands were cold. He said, "Lily, can I please use your mittens?" Lily thought about what her mom said, but she wanted to help Tommy. So, she gave him one mitten. They made a big snowman together. When they were done, Lily looked for her mitten. She couldn't find it! She was sad and worried. Tommy saw her looking and said, "Lily, I insist you take my mitten. It's just like yours!" Lily smiled and took Tommy's mitten. They went inside to show their moms the new mitten. Both moms were happy and proud that they shared and helped each other.

One day, a big jug was on the table. A little boy named Tim saw it and wanted to start playing with it. He was very impatient to see what was inside. Tim tried to open the jug, but it was too tight. He pulled and pulled, but it would not open. Then, he had an idea! He asked his big sister, Jane, to help him. Jane was strong and could open the jug. When Jane opened the jug, something unexpected happened! A lot of colorful balls jumped out of the jug. Tim and Jane were very surprised. They laughed and started playing with the balls. They had a lot of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to paint. She painted all day, every day. One day, Sally painted a unique picture. It was a picture of a big, happy sun. She was very proud of her painting. Sally showed her painting to her friend, Tim. Tim looked at the painting and said, "I hate your painting. It's not good." Sally felt very sad. She didn't know what to do. Sally's mom saw her feeling sad and asked, "What's wrong, Sally?" Sally told her mom what Tim said about her painting. Her mom hugged her and said, "Sally, everyone has different likes. Just because Tim doesn't like your painting doesn't mean it's bad. Be proud of your unique painting." Sally felt better and learned a lesson. She learned that not everyone will like what you do, but that's okay. It's important to be proud of your own unique work. And from that day on, Sally kept painting and being proud of her art.

Once upon a time, in a quiet town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy pistol. He loved to play with it all day. One day, Tim went to the park with his toy pistol. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's pistol and wanted to play too. Tim said, "Do you want to play with me and my pistol?" Sue said, "Yes, I do!" They played together and had lots of fun. They felt happy. After playing, they sat under a tree. Tim and Sue became good friends. They went home and promised to play together again.

One day, a little octopus was in the sea. He wanted to play a game. He thought, "I want to search for something fun and easy." The octopus swam and swam. He saw a fish. The fish said, "Hi, octopus! What are you doing?" The octopus said, "I am searching for an easy game to play. Do you want to play with me?" The fish said, "Yes, I want to play too!" They swam together and found a big shell. The octopus said, "Let's play hide and seek! I will hide, and you search for me." The fish agreed. The octopus hid in the shell, and the fish started to search. The fish looked around and soon found the octopus in the shell. They laughed and played hide and seek all day. They had a lot of fun and became good friends. The octopus was happy that he found an easy and fun game to play with his new friend.
