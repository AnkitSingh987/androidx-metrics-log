Title: Mandy and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Mandy. She had a special friend, a blackbird. Every morning Mandy would call out to her friend, “Hello!” and the blackbird would answer back. One day the blackbird was carrying a pine cone in his beak. Mandy was very curious and asked him where he got the pine cone from. The blackbird said, “I borrowed it from the pine tree in the park.” Mandy thought this was very generous and kind of the blackbird. She decided to try it and asked the next day if she could borrow something from the pine tree too. Mandy was so excited when the pine tree said, “Yes!” Mandy found a special black stone on the ground and she thanked the pine tree. She decided to borrow from others too and shared her finds with the other children in the park. Everyone had so much fun that day! Mandy learnt a valuable lesson that day - it’s always nice to borrow things from others, but it’s also important to be kind and generous and to share our gifts with others.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play outside with their dog, Spot. But today is very cold. The wind blows and the snow falls. Lily and Ben are not happy. "Let's go inside, Ben. I'm cold and hungry," Lily says. "But I want to make a snowman, Lily. Look, I have a big ball of snow already," Ben says. He rolls the snow on the ground. Spot barks and runs after him. Lily shakes her head. She goes to the porch. The porch is a place by the door. It has a roof and a bench. Lily sits on the bench. She wraps her arms around herself. She sees her mom in the window. Her mom smiles and waves. "Ben, come on. Mom is making soup for us. It will be warm and yummy," Lily calls. Ben looks at Lily. He looks at his snowball. He looks at Spot. He thinks for a moment. "Okay, Lily. I'm coming. But can we play with the snowman later?" Ben asks. "Sure, Ben. If the snow doesn't melt," Lily says. She gets up from the bench. She and Ben gather their hats and gloves. They leave their snowball on the porch. They run to the door. Spot follows them. Mom opens the door. She hugs them and kisses them. "Welcome back, my little snowflakes. Come in and have some soup. You must be very cold," Mom says. Lily and Ben smile. They are not cold anymore. They are happy. They love their mom and their dog and their porch. And they love their snowman, too.

One day, a boy named Tim went to buy milk. He put on his shoes and walked to the store. Tim was very serious about getting the milk because his mom needed it to make his favorite food. On his way to the store, he saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play with Tim, but Tim was too serious about buying the milk. Suddenly, the dog grabbed the money from Tim's hand and ran away! Tim was very sad and didn't know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog came back with the milk! The dog had used the money to buy the milk for Tim. Tim was so happy and thanked the dog. From that day on, Tim and the dog were best friends and they always went to buy milk together.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to wake up early every day to see the sunrise. The colors in the sky made her happy. One morning, as the sun started to rise, Lily saw a little bird on her window. She said, "Hi, bird! Do you like the sunrise too?" The bird nodded its head and called, "Yes, I do!" From that day on, Lily and the bird would meet each morning to watch the sunrise together. They called the bird Sunny because it loved the sun so much. Lily and Sunny became best friends, and they loved their special time together.
