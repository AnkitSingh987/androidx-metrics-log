Title: Lizzy the Lizard and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a shy lizard named Lizzy. She lived in a small hole under a big tree. Lizzy liked to play with her friends, but she would feel scared to talk to new animals. One day, a happy bird named Benny came to the tree. Benny wanted to be friends with all the animals. He said "hi" to Lizzy, but she felt too shy to say anything back. Benny felt sad, but he didn't give up. Benny tried to make Lizzy feel happy by bringing her yummy bugs to eat. Lizzy liked the bugs and started to feel less shy. She said "thank you" to Benny and they became good friends. The other animals saw how kind Benny was to Lizzy, and they all wanted to be friends too. The moral of the story is that being kind can help others feel less shy and make many friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little baby named Tim. Tim lived in a crib in a cozy room. One day, Tim's big sister, Sue, came into the room to play with him. She saw the crib and wanted to celebrate Tim's birthday. "Tim, let's celebrate your birthday!" Sue said. Tim clapped his hands and giggled. Sue brought in a cake and they sang "Happy Birthday" together. Tim was so happy to celebrate with Sue. But then, Sue accidentally bumped into the crib and it wobbled. Tim felt scared and started to cry. Sue felt sorry for making Tim cry. She said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to scare you." Sue hugged Tim and he stopped crying. They continued to celebrate and had a fun day together.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were ready to play. They went to a track near a pond. The cat said, "Let's run and see who is the fastest." The dog agreed and they started to run. As they ran, the dog saw a big puddle. He thought it would be fun to splash in it. So, he jumped in and water went everywhere. The cat stopped running and laughed. "You are so funny," said the cat. They both laughed and played in the puddle. Then, a frog jumped out from the puddle. The cat and the dog were surprised. The frog said, "Thank you for splashing in my home. You made it clean." The cat and the dog learned that sometimes, when you play and have fun, you can also help others.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish with a shiny scale. The fish lived in a pond. The fish was frightened of the dark. One day, the fish saw a little bug near the pond. The bug was also frightened. The fish and the bug became friends. They played in the sun. When it was time to turn around, the fish would help the bug. The bug would help the fish when it was scared of the dark. They were happy. One day, the bug turned into a beautiful butterfly. The fish was surprised. The butterfly said, "Do not be frightened. I am still your friend." The fish smiled. They played together in the sun and the dark. They were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular shoe. Everyone loved this shoe because it was very pretty and shiny. The shoe lived in a big store with many other shoes, but this shoe was the most special one. One day, a little girl came to the store with her mom. She saw the popular shoe and wanted to try it on. She put her foot inside the shoe, but it was too big. She tried to walk, but she started to slip. Her mom saw her slip and helped her up. The little girl was sad that the popular shoe was too big for her. Her mom told her not to worry because there were many other shoes to try. They found another pretty shoe that fit the little girl just right. She was very happy and danced around the store. The popular shoe was happy too because it knew that one day, someone else would come and it would be just right for them.
