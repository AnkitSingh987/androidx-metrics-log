Title: Anna the Bright Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a bright girl named Anna. Anna was only three years old and she loved discovering new things. One day, Anna went out to search for something special. She walked for a long time until she came to a big, round room. In the center of the room was a big, purple machine. Anna was curious, so she decided to search what it was. "What is this?" Anna asked her mom. "It's an operation machine," Anna's mom said. "Do you want to press the button?" Anna was excited, so she pressed the bright green button. Suddenly, the operation machine lit up and music started to play. Anna's eyes were wide as she explored the room. After searching through the room, Anna found a small red button. When she pressed it, the room turned into a magical place. Anna found many treasures, like candy and toys. She laughed with joy as she discovered new things. Anna had a great day and when it was time to go home, she felt very proud of herself and excited for her next search.

Once upon a time, there was a red crayon who lived in a big box with many other crayons. The red crayon was very sad because he was always being used to draw bad things. One day, a little girl named Amy found the red crayon and wanted to draw a pretty picture. Amy said, "Red crayon, I want to draw a big heart to save for my mommy." The red crayon felt very happy to help Amy draw something nice. They worked together to draw the big heart, and it was the most beautiful heart anyone had ever seen. In the end, the red crayon was no longer used for bad things. He was happy to be saved by Amy and used for good things. The red crayon and Amy became the best of friends, and they drew many more beautiful pictures together.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play together. One day, Lily has an idea. "Let's have a party!" she says to Tom. "We can invite our toys and have cake and juice." Tom thinks this is a good idea. He likes parties. He likes cake and juice. He likes his toys. "OK, let's have a party!" he says to Lily. "But where can we have it?" Lily looks around. She sees the living room. She sees the sofa, the table, the TV, the lamp. She sees her mom's vase with flowers. "Let's have it here!" she says to Tom. "We can use the sofa as a stage, the table as a buffet, the TV as a music box, the lamp as a disco ball. And we can pick some flowers from the vase for decoration." Tom is not sure. He thinks this might be a bad idea. He knows his mom and Lily's mom do not like it when they make a mess. He knows they do not like it when they touch their things. He knows they do not like it when they are loud. "Maybe we should not have it here," he says to Lily. "Maybe we should suggest another place. Like the garden, or the park, or the playroom. They are more fun and less trouble." Lily does not listen. She thinks Tom is silly. She thinks the living room is the best place for a party. She thinks her mom and Lily's mom will not mind. She thinks they will be happy to see them having fun. "Come on, Tom, don't be a baby," she says to Tom. "Let's have it here. It will be the best party ever!" She grabs Tom's hand and pulls him to the sofa. She starts to set up the party. She does not know that her mom and Lily's mom are watching from the kitchen. They do not look happy. They look angry. They look like they are going to stop the party. They look like they are going to give Lily and Tom a bad time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had an amazing toy rabbit named Mr. Bunny. Sue and Mr. Bunny were best friends. They loved to play together all day long. One day, Sue and Mr. Bunny went to the park. They played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. Then, Sue whispered something in Mr. Bunny's ear. "Let's play hide and seek," she said. Mr. Bunny nodded, and they started the game. Sue closed her eyes and began to count. Sue finished counting and opened her eyes. She looked for Mr. Bunny everywhere. At last, she found him hiding behind a big tree. Sue laughed and said, "I found you, Mr. Bunny! This was the best day ever!" They went home, happy and tired from their fun day at the park.
