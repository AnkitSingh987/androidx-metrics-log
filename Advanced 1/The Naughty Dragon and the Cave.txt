Title: The Naughty Dragon and the Cave

Once there was a naughty dragon who lived in a cave. The dragon was very spoiled, it never listened to anyone and always caused trouble. One day the naughty dragon made a fire too close to the edge of the cave and it started to spread quickly. The dragon tried to put out the fire, but he was too scared. He was shivering with fear. Suddenly an old man appeared in the cave. He said to the dragon: "you must learn to listen to the wisdom of others, otherwise you will always be in trouble." He took out a smoldering stick from the fire and asked the dragon to follow him out of the cave. The dragon was still too scared to move and shivered uncontrollably. The old man used the smoldering stick to create a path to safety, and the dragon followed him. Once they were safe, the old man told the dragon: "You must learn to be wise and stop being so spoiled, then you will be safe!" The dragon learnt his lesson and never made the same mistake again of starting fires too close to the edge of the cave. He always remembered to listen to the wisdom of his elders and be more careful. Thus, the dragon stayed safe due to his newfound wisdom.

One day, a nosy cat named Tom went to the station. He saw a big red train. Tom was very excited. He wanted to ride the train. But the train was too big for him. Tom met a nice lady at the station. The lady had a magic wand. She said, "I can help you! I can make you shrink!" "Please shrink me!", Tom said. The lady waved her magic wand and Tom became very small. Now, he could ride the train! But, there was a problem. A big dog named Max saw Tom. Max did not like cats. Max wanted to chase Tom. Tom was scared. He had to find a way to get away from Max. Tom ran and hid under a seat on the train. Max could not find him. Tom was safe. He was happy to ride the train and be small.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pet dog named Max. Sue and Max were best friends. They liked to play together every day. One day, Sue found a long string. She had an idea. She said to Max, "I will attach this string to you, and we can play a new game." Max wagged his tail, happy to play. Sue attached the string to Max's collar, and they played a game of catch. As they played, Max got better and better at the game. Soon, Max was very successful at catching the ball. Sue was so proud of her pet. She knew that practice made perfect. But then, Max got too excited. He ran too fast and the string got caught on a tree. Max was stuck! Sue knew she had to help her friend. She carefully untied the string and set Max free. Sue learned that it's important to be careful and think about the things we do. And Max learned that even when you're successful, you still need your friends to help you sometimes.

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a little figure named Tim. Tim was a small, round creature with big eyes and tiny legs. He loved to run and play with his friends, the birds, and the bugs. One day, Tim met a big bird named Sam. Sam was sad because he could not change his color like the other birds. "I wish I could change colors too," Sam said to Tim. Tim wanted to help his new friend. Tim had an idea. He asked all his friends in the forest to find things that could change Sam's color. They found berries, mud, and leaves. Tim and his friends rubbed the berries, mud, and leaves on Sam's feathers. Sam's feathers changed color! Sam was so happy! He flew around the forest, showing off his new colors to everyone. "Thank you, Tim!" Sam said. Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sam! I'm glad I could help." From that day on, Tim and Sam were best friends, and they played together in the wild forest every day.
