Title: Lost in the glow navigating the crisis of Part Three

Lost in the glow: navigating the crisis of light and wildlife

In doing so, they are reshaping the natural world, without realising the cost. The solution to this crisis is simple, and does not require a massive budget or decades of efforts. It takes only the humility to admit that not everything needs to be seen all the time. The night is not a void to be filled; it is a heritage to be protected.

Preserving its sanctity safeguards human health, while allowing wilderness and wildlife to remain truly wild. Nafin K.S. is a conservation biologist at the Periyar Tiger Conservation Foundation, Kerala Forest Department; and Pramod G. Krishnan is the Chief Wildlife Warden of the Kerala Forest Department.

The views expressed are personal.
