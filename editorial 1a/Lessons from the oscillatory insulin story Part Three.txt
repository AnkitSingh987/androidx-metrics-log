Title: Lessons from the oscillatory insulin story Part Three

Lessons from the oscillatory insulin story

If universities and governments want real breakthroughs, they must reward persistence over performance metrics, value inquiry over output, encourage interdisciplinary exploration, allow failure as part of discovery, and promote deep science over shallow metrics. Real science is a test match and not a twenty-twenty game. And societies that understand this will be the ones that win the future.

By R Krishna Kumar The writer is Institute Professor, Department of Medical Sciences and Technology, IIT Madras
