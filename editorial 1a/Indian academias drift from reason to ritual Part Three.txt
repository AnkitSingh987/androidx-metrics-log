Title: Indian academias drift from reason to ritual Part Three

Indian academia's drift from reason to ritual

That is one traditional link worth defending  -  and perhaps even chanting at a convocation. It is a hymn to intellectual courage and the relentless pursuit of truth, not the hollow verse used to silence and intimidate. Let us reclaim the free spirit of inquiry, ensure our academic landscapes remain true to their secular purpose, and help resurrect an education sector currently in a shambles  -  largely due to doctrinaire rigidity, corruption and misgovernance.

C.P. Rajendran is a geoscientist and a communicator on science, politics, environment and education
