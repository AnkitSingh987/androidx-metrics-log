Title: Green steel can shape Indias climate goals Part Three

Green steel can shape India's climate goals trajectory

India has already demonstrated global leadership in renewable energy deployment, climate diplomacy, and clean-tech scaling. Steel is now the next frontier: a critical test and an unprecedented opportunity. By combining decisive corporate action with a robust, market-aligned policy framework, India can decarbonise steel, secure economic competitiveness, and shape global industrial standards.

Sanjiv Paul is a steel industry veteran and a former Vice-President of Safety, Health and Sustainability at Tata Steel
