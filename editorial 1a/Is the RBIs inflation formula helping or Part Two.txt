Title: Is the RBIs inflation formula helping or Part Two

Is the RBI's inflation formula helping or hurting you?

It fosters macroeconomic stability, providing a stable and predictable environment conducive to investment, employment generation and sustainable economic growth, while providing the flexibility necessary to absorb external shocks and maintain price stability, especially in the wake of increased geopolitical uncertainties. Policy credibility is key FIT framework improved policy credibility and enhanced data oriented independent interest rate decisions. Before 2016, the monetary policy faced frequent adjustments due to an unprofessional and biased approach to target inflation.

Financial stability also improved with reduction in speculation activities, low market volatility, ultimately enhancing trust among investors and business organisations. Studies has indicated that prior to the implementation of FIT (2010 - 2016) inflation volatility reached 2.9%. Inflation volatility dropped significantly to 1.6% because of FIT implementation during 2017 - 2024.

Thus, FIT framework is key for India's macroeconomic stability. Abhinav Banka and Surjith Karthikeyan serves as Deputy Director and Director at Government of India. Views are personal
