Title: Real AI debate isnt about technology Its Part Three

Real AI debate isn't about technology. It's about the choices we make

We can invite experiments that refuse the binary of human vs machine and instead explore their entanglement: Multi-stakeholder labs in Indian universities, hackathons that design AI for relational economies, public art that asks citizens to choreograph their algorithmic feeds. We must remember that the future isn't arriving for us. The future arrives with us.

And in that arrival, our choices matter. Written by Sushant Kumar The writer, formerly a fellow of the Harvard Kennedy School, is assistant professor, Jindal School of Government and Public Policy
