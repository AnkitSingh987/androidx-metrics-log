Title: Ladakh seeks belonging through representation Part Three

Ladakh seeks belonging through representation

That distinction matters. Sri Aurobindo wrote that freedom is the necessary atmosphere for a nation's soul to grow. India's spirit has often been strongest at its edges  -  in places that chose Bharat and defended it through hardship and sacrifice without asking what it cost. The strength of a republic is not measured by how tightly it controls its frontiers, but by how deeply its farthest regions feel they belong.

The voice rising from Ladakh today is not a demand for privilege, but a quiet appeal to be trusted with its own future. Gitanjali J. Angmo Founder of the Himalayan Institute of Alternatives, Ladakh, and an education reformer and public intellectual working to reimagine education, democracy, ecology and Himalayan futures through grassroots leadership and innovation
