Title: The state against its students lessons to Part Three

The state against its students, lessons to remember

No government ever believes the pattern applies to itself. That is precisely how the pattern survives. The young people at Jantar Mantar were not asking the state to surrender. They were asking it to be worthy of the examinations it conducts. On the evidence of a century and a half, the governments that listen to that request are the ones that last.

Shashank Pandey is a lawyer based in New Delhi and former Legislative Assistant to Member of Parliament (LAMP) Fellow
