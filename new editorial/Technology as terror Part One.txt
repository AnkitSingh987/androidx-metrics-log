Title: Technology as terror Part One

Technology as terror

It is a clear possibility that Artificial Intelligence will undermine societies Humans need to be assured of a predictable and secure future to remain productive in every way. Technological innovations so far have by and large promised these. But now, the arrival of Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is forcing us to look at tomorrow's world through a different lens.

What we see there is a society in turmoil. Robots were developed to aid humans in workplaces. But with the help of high-end robotics and AI, they have started replacing humans. In dark factories, work is done by robots with minimal or no human intervention. Predictably, this will lead to job loss: not overnight, but at a pace at which workers will not be able to upskill or find alternative jobs.

The use of high-end robotics and AI will not be limited to dark factories. So, the chances of a recession are high. Recessions have created periods of unemployment, but there was always the hope that soon everything would be back to normal. However, in the wake of AI, a large portion of the displaced workforce may remain unemployed.

Psychological impact Fear of mass unemployment will impact on society's collective psyche, which will in turn affect everyone's body and brain functioning. This may set off a chain of events where people will start distancing themselves from society, hesitating to start a family, and rejecting accepted social norms. One of the results could be a rapid population decline.

Already several countries are facing population decline for reasons like high cost of health care, education and housing. Even government incentives aimed at increasing the population have often failed. When one feels low, there is a high chance of that individual pulling back from society. When this happens across society, it means disaster.

Are we heading in that direction? Children as victims In another scenario, people will be competing with AI instead of peers. In this new norm, children will, from a very young age, be exposed to AI products which may not be emotionally sensitive. Nowadays children are overloaded with data, educational and otherwise, and it is not fair to expect every child to perform on the same scale.

Some may be slow to catch up with others. This does not mean that they are less intelligent, but AI can make them feel inferior in some way or the other. This can lead to low self-esteem and long-lasting psychological problems among them. Another issue is access to AI-based content. This may require payment, which every family may not be able to afford.

So, children from poor families would find it difficult to compete with others. Children who can afford AI-based content will get a head start and this may develop a gap between them. Making affordable AI content and technology available to all is a major challenge. It is time to enact laws and ensure that children are protected from the evils AI may throw at them while they receive the benefits from AI at an affordable cost.

Dealing with the challenge Against this gloomy background, an action plan is necessary. It should be made mandatory for products from dark factories to announce the truth about their origin. The government needs to draw a line separating goods that aid humans and that drastically replace humans. The latter category should be allowed restricted entry or taxed heavily.
