Title: Will VBG Ram G score over MGNREGS Part Three

Will VB-G Ram G score over MGNREGS?

It is not clear how the new framework will be more effective and successful in curbing leakages, as the technology already in place will continue and the ground level agents will be the same. While the efficacy of the uniform digital protocols in reducing arbitrariness etc., over those in the past is an empirical issue, the concerns relating to reduced discretion of field-level officials through standardised digital protocols, exclusion of the vulnerable owing to mandatory digital processes (smartphones, internet connectivity, biometric or app-based attendance), decentralisation, and the core rights-based character of the mission cannot be dismissed. According to the new Act 'all assets created are aggregated into the Viksit Bharat National Rural Infrastructure Stack, ensuring a unified, coordinated national development strategy', argues Bhattacharya.

Planning is decentralised through Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans, which are prepared locally and spatially integrated with national systems such as PM Gati Shakti'. This techno-managerial dressing up in the new Act admittedly dilutes the guarantee of work on demand. Importantly, one other avowed objective is empowering gram panchayats.

It however increases local level dependency on centrally designed software, reduces flexibility in local planning by subjecting the panchayats to top-down clearance cum surveillance system. Many gram panchayats lack in trained staff, reliable electricity, and connectivity while digital mandates add to paperwork and workload, leading to delays and procedural violations. It is apprehended that the technology-driven fund flow system may not eliminate wage delays but instead, may obscure responsibility, making it harder to fix accountability between Centre, States, and local bodies.

By PC Mohanan & Amitabh Kundu Mohanan is former Acting Chairperson of National Statistical Commission and Kundu is Senior Fellow at IORA Ecological Solutions
