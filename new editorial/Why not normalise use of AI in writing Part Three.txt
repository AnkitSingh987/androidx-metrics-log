Title: Why not normalise use of AI in writing Part Three

Why not normalise use of AI in writing?

To conclude, AI writing is a redistribution of who gets these standards, rather than the erosion of standards. The only practical choice left is whether to accept that now, or to keep resisting a novelty to be accepted at a later stage, like everyone before it. It is inevitable that it will become the new normal, and failing to see that is not caution, but a failure of foresight.

In the words of historian E.H. Carr, history can only be written by those who have a sense of direction and an instinct that they are advancing towards an end goal in order to piece evidence together convincingly. And man moves within history himself and indeed progresses. Amit Kumar is an Assistant Professor in the Institute for Development and Communication, Chandigarh
