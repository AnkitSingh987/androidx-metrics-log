Title: The Wrong Train

Category: Intermediate

It was Friday afternoon, and Maya was in a terrible hurry. The university campus was large, and her final biology class had finished twenty minutes late. She was running toward the central train station with a heavy backpack bouncing against her shoulders. She needed to catch the 4:15 PM train to Manchester to visit her best friend, Sarah, for the weekend. They had planned this trip for two months, and Maya did not want to be late.
The streets were crowded with people finishing work and starting their weekend. Maya dodged past a group of slow-moving tourists and checked her watch. It was 4:10 PM. If she ran faster, she could just make it.
She arrived at the station out of breath. The large electronic departure board was flashing brightly. Maya quickly scanned the list of trains. "Platform 9 – 4:15 PM," she read out loud. She didn't read the destination; she just saw the time and the platform number.
She sprinted through the main hall, pushed her ticket through the automatic gates, and ran down the stairs to Platform 9. A long, silver train was waiting with its doors open. The whistle blew, warning passengers that it was about to leave. Maya jumped through the doors just as they began to slide shut. She let out a long breath of relief and leaned against the glass. She had made it.
The train slowly pulled out of the station. Maya found an empty window seat, took off her heavy backpack, and sat down. She was exhausted. She closed her eyes and decided to take a short nap. The journey to Manchester usually took two hours, so she had plenty of time to rest.
When Maya woke up, the train was moving smoothly through the countryside. She looked out the window and frowned. She expected to see the flat, green fields of northern England. Instead, she saw tall, rugged hills covered in thick forests and deep valleys. There was a large river winding beside the tracks. This did not look like the route to Manchester.
A cold feeling started in her stomach. She sat up straight and looked around the carriage. There was a small electronic screen at the front of the car displaying the next stop. The red letters scrolled across the screen: "Next stop: Bristol Temple Meads."
"Bristol?" Maya whispered in shock. Bristol was in the southwest of England. Manchester was in the north. They were in completely opposite directions!
She quickly reached into her pocket and pulled out her train ticket. It clearly said "Destination: Manchester." Then, she remembered the departure board at the station. She had only looked at the time and the platform number. She hadn't read the final destination. She had boarded the wrong train.
Maya felt a wave of panic. What was she going to do? She was traveling hundreds of miles away from her friend. She immediately took out her phone to call Sarah, but there was no signal in the countryside. She was completely disconnected.
Just then, the ticket inspector walked into the carriage. He was an older man with gray hair and a neat blue uniform. "Tickets, please. Have your tickets ready," he called out cheerfully.
Maya nervously held out her ticket. The inspector looked at it, then looked at Maya. He adjusted his glasses and looked at the ticket again.
"Excuse me, miss," he said kindly, but with a confused expression. "This ticket is for a train going to Manchester. We are currently heading towards Bristol. You are on the wrong train."
"I know," Maya said, her voice shaking a little. "I realized just a moment ago. I was late, and I ran to Platform 9 because I saw a 4:15 PM train. I didn't check the destination. What can I do? I need to get to Manchester tonight."
The inspector gave her a sympathetic smile. "Don't panic. These things happen more often than you think. Let's see how we can fix this." He pulled out a small electronic device from his belt and started typing on it.
"Okay," he said after a minute. "We are arriving in Bristol in thirty minutes. You will need to get off there. From Bristol, you can take a direct train north to Birmingham. In Birmingham, you will have to change trains one more time to get to Manchester. It is a long way around, but you will get there."
Maya felt slightly relieved, but still worried. "Do I have to buy a new ticket? I am a student, and I don't have very much money."
The inspector thought for a moment. "Technically, your ticket is not valid for this route. But because it was an honest mistake, I will write a note on your ticket explaining the situation. You won't have to pay a fine, but you might need to buy the connecting ticket from Birmingham. I recommend talking to the customer service desk in Bristol."
"Thank you so much," Maya said gratefully. "You have been very kind."
"No problem. Just make sure you check the boards carefully next time," he said with a wink before moving on to the next passenger.
When the train arrived in Bristol, Maya grabbed her backpack and stepped onto the platform. The station was huge and beautiful, built with old red bricks and a high glass roof. Maya found a quiet corner and finally got a phone signal. She called Sarah.
"Maya! Are you almost here?" Sarah answered happily.
"Sarah, you won't believe this," Maya said, sighing. "I got on the wrong train. I am in Bristol."
Sarah laughed loudly. "Bristol? How did you manage that? That's the opposite end of the country!"
Maya explained the whole story. Sarah was very understanding and promised to wait up for her, no matter how late she arrived.
Maya went to the customer service desk. The woman there read the inspector's note and smiled. "You have had a bit of an adventure today, haven't you? Let's get you to Manchester." She printed out a new itinerary for Maya and even gave her a discount on the final connecting ticket.
Maya had to wait an hour for her train to Birmingham. She decided to go outside and see a tiny bit of Bristol. She bought a hot chocolate from a small cafe near the station and walked along the harbor. It was a beautiful evening, and the water reflected the lights of the city. She watched the boats gently moving in the water and felt her stress disappear. Getting lost wasn't always a bad thing. Sometimes, it forced you to slow down and see places you never intended to visit.
The rest of the journey was long but peaceful. She took the train to Birmingham, read her book, and then caught the final train to Manchester. When she finally walked out of Manchester station, it was past 11:00 PM. Sarah was waiting for her near the entrance, holding a large cup of coffee and smiling brightly.
"Welcome to Manchester, eventually!" Sarah joked, hugging her tightly.
Maya laughed, feeling happy and exhausted. "It took a long time, and a lot of trains, but I made it. Remind me to always read the destination board twice from now on." They walked into the cold night air, ready to finally start their weekend together, knowing this would be a story they would tell for years.
