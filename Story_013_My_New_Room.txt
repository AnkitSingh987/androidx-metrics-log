Title: My New Room
Category: Beginner

Today is an important day. My family is moving to a new apartment. Our old apartment was very small. I did not have my own room. I shared a room with my little brother. But our new apartment is bigger. 
In the new apartment, I have my own room! I am very excited. I am ten years old, and this is my first real room. 

My father drives a big truck to the new apartment building. The truck is full of boxes. The boxes are full of our things. 
My father opens the back of the truck. 
"Okay, family," he says. "Time to work. We must carry these boxes inside."

I see a box with my name on it. It says 'DAVID' in big black letters. 
"Can I carry this box?" I ask my father. 
"Yes, David," he says. "But be careful. It might be heavy."
I lift the box. It is a little heavy, but I am strong. I carry the box into the building. I walk up the stairs. We live on the second floor. 
I go into the new apartment. It smells like fresh paint. 

My mother is in the hall. 
"Mother, where is my room?" I ask. 
She points to a door at the end of the hall. "That is your room, David," she says. 

I walk to the door. I turn the handle. I open the door. I walk inside. 
I look around. The room is empty. But it is beautiful. The walls are painted light blue. My favorite color is blue. The floor is made of wood. It is clean and shiny. There is a big window. The sun shines through the window. It makes the room very bright. 
I put my box on the floor. 

My father brings my bed. It is not in a box. It is in pieces. He puts the pieces together. Now I have a bed. He puts a soft mattress on the bed. 
"There is your bed, David," he says. 
"Thank you, Father," I say. 
Next, he brings a desk and a chair. He puts the desk near the window. I can look outside when I study. 
My mother brings a small bookshelf. She puts it against the blue wall. 

Now the furniture is in the room. It looks like a real bedroom. But it needs my things. 
I open my box. First, I take out my clothes. I have shirts, trousers, and sweaters. There is a closet in my new room. I open the closet doors. I hang my shirts on hangers. I fold my trousers. I put my sweaters on the shelf. The closet is very nice. 

I go back to the truck. I carry another box. This box is very heavy. It is full of my books. 
I take the books out of the box. I have many story books. I love to read. I put the books on the small bookshelf. I arrange them carefully. I put the big books together. I put the small books together. The bookshelf looks great. 

I go back to the truck. I carry one more box. This box is light. It has my special things. 
I open the box. I take out my alarm clock. It is a green clock. I put it on the small table next to my bed. 
I take out my favorite toy car. I put it on my desk. 
Then, I take out a picture. It is a picture of my dog, Max. Max died last year. I miss him. The picture is in a nice wooden frame. 
I want to put the picture on the wall. 
"Father!" I call. "Can you help me?"

My father comes into my room. He has a hammer in his hand. 
"What do you need, David?" he asks. 
"I want to hang this picture on the wall," I say. "Can you put a nail in the wall?"
"Of course," he says. 
He takes a small nail. He holds it against the blue wall. He hits the nail with the hammer. Bang, bang, bang. The nail is in the wall. 
I take the picture of Max. I hang it on the nail. I step back and look at it. It looks perfect. 

My mother comes into the room. She has my blanket and my pillows. 
"Let's make your bed, David," she says. 
We put the white sheets on the mattress. We put the blue blanket on top. We put the soft pillows at the head of the bed. 
"It looks very comfortable," she says. 

I stand in the middle of the room. I look at the closet. I look at the bookshelf. I look at the desk. I look at the picture of Max on the wall. I look at my comfortable bed. 
"How do you like your new room?" my father asks. 
"I love it," I say. "It is the best room in the world."
I am very happy. Tonight, I will sleep in my new room for the first time. I know I will have very good dreams.
