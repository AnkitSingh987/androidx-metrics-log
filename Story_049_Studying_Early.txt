Title: Studying Early
Category: Beginner

It is very dark in the bedroom. The house is completely silent. 
Suddenly, a small alarm clock rings. Beep, beep, beep. 
The time on the clock is six o'clock in the morning. It is very early. 

Leo opens his eyes. He is very tired. He wants to turn off the alarm and go back to sleep. The bed is warm, and the morning air is cold. 
But he remembers what day it is. It is Friday. Today is the day of the big history test. 
Leo reaches out and turns off the alarm. He sits up in his bed. He rubs his eyes. 

"I must get up," he says to himself. "I must review my notes."
He puts on his warm sweater and his slippers. He leaves his bedroom quietly. He does not want to wake up his parents or his little sister. 
He walks down the stairs. The house is dark. He goes into the kitchen and turns on a small light. 

He goes to the fridge and pours a glass of cold water. He drinks the water. The cold water wakes up his brain. 
He takes his history book and his notebook from his school bag. He sits at the kitchen table. The kitchen is very peaceful at six o'clock in the morning. There is no television, no talking, no cars outside. 
It is the perfect time to study. 

Leo opens his history notebook. He studied hard last night. He studied for two hours. He knows the information. But he wants to be very sure. He wants to review everything one more time before the test. 

He reads the first page of his notes. It is about ancient Rome. He reads about the soldiers and the emperors. He reads about the big buildings. 
He covers the page with his hand. He closes his eyes. 
"Who was Julius Caesar?" he asks himself. 
He answers the question in his head. He remembers the answer perfectly. 

He uncovers the page and smiles. He moves to the next page. 
He reads about the food they ate in ancient Rome. He reads about the clothes they wore. 
The quiet kitchen is very helpful. He can concentrate completely. He is not distracted by anything. 
He reads all his notes from the beginning to the end. It takes him thirty minutes. 

At half past six, he closes his notebook. He closes his history book. 
He feels very good. He is not worried about the test anymore. He feels prepared. He knows all the answers in his notebook. He is ready for Mr. Brown's difficult questions. 

He hears a noise upstairs. A door opens. Footsteps come down the stairs. 
His mother walks into the kitchen. She is wearing her pink robe. She looks surprised to see Leo at the table. 
"Good morning, Leo," she says. "You are up very early. It is only half past six."
"Good morning, Mother," Leo answers. "I got up at six o'clock."
"Why did you get up so early?" she asks. "Are you not well?"
"I am very well," Leo says with a smile. "I have a big history test today. I wanted to review my notes one last time."

His mother smiles warmly. "That is very responsible, Leo. I am proud of you. Do you feel ready for the test?"
"Yes, I do," Leo says confidently. "I know everything."
"That is wonderful," she says. "Well, since you are up, do you want some breakfast? I can make you some eggs."
"Yes, please," Leo says. "I am hungry."

His mother turns on the stove. She starts to cook the eggs. The smell of cooking fills the kitchen. The sun begins to rise outside the window. The sky turns from black to blue to pink. The day is starting. 
Leo sits at the table. He is not tired anymore. He feels fresh and awake. 
Studying early in the morning was a very good idea. He is ready for a good breakfast, and he is ready for a great mark on his history test.
