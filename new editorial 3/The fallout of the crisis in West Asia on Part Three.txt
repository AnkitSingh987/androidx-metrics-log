Title: The fallout of the crisis in West Asia on Part Three

The fallout of the crisis in West Asia on India's economy

As on April 9, 2026, the price of the Indian crude basket at $120.28 per barrel has exceeded the baseline by about $50 per barrel. If this margin of increase becomes applicable for the whole year, real GDP growth may fall from baseline estimates by 1 percentage point and inflation may increase by more than 2 percentage points. While these effects would be lower if the crisis gets resolved quickly, much depends upon when true peace will dawn.

By C. Rangarajan (Former Chairman, Prime Minister's Economic Advisory Council and Former Governor, Reserve Bank of India) & D. K. Srivastava (Former Director, Madras School of Economics) Views expressed are personal.
