Title: Is Indias growth rate sustainable Part Four

Is India's 8.2% growth rate sustainable?

It's clear that the future of cricket broadcasting lies in smaller but smarter bids and more focus on profitability over visibility. In short, cricket will remain India's biggest content magnet, but the blank-cheque era is over. Make no mistake: cricket retains its popularity but popularity does not equal profitability.

The Indian consumer has evolved faster than the cricket business model, and unless rights valuations reflect this new reality, exits, renegotiations, and strategic pivots are inevitable.
