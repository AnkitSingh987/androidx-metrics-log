Title: Cooking for My Family
Category: Beginner

My name is Anna. I am twelve years old. Today is Saturday. It is my turn to cook dinner. I like to cook. I watch my mother cook every day. Today, I will cook for my mother, my father, and my little brother, Leo. 

I am in the kitchen. It is five o'clock in the evening. Dinner is at six o'clock. I have one hour. 
I wear a red apron. The apron keeps my clothes clean. I wash my hands with soap and water. My hands are very clean. 

What will I cook? I will cook spaghetti. Everybody likes spaghetti. It is easy and delicious. 
First, I need a big pot. I find a big silver pot in the cupboard. I put it in the sink. I turn on the tap. Cold water goes into the pot. I fill the pot halfway with water. 
The pot is very heavy now. I lift it with two hands. I put it on the stove. 

I turn on the stove. I wait for the water to boil. 
While I wait, I prepare the sauce. I will make a simple tomato sauce. 
I take a small pan. I put it on another part of the stove. I put a little bit of olive oil in the pan. 
I go to the fridge. I take an onion. I take two cloves of garlic. 
I put the onion on a wooden board. I take a knife. I cut the onion very carefully. I cut it into small pieces. I cut the garlic into very small pieces too. 

I put the onion and garlic into the hot oil in the pan. Sizzle, sizzle, sizzle! It smells very good. The smell goes through the house. 
Leo runs into the kitchen. 
"What are you cooking, Anna?" he asks. "It smells great!"
"I am cooking spaghetti," I say. 
"Yay! I love spaghetti!" Leo shouts. He runs back to the living room. 

The onion is soft and yellow. Now I add the tomatoes. I have a can of red tomatoes. I open the can. I pour the tomatoes into the pan. 
I add a little salt. I add a little pepper. I add some green herbs. I stir the sauce with a wooden spoon. I turn the heat down. The sauce will cook slowly. 

I look at the big pot. The water is boiling! There are big bubbles in the water. 
I take a packet of dry spaghetti. The spaghetti is long and yellow. I open the packet. I put the spaghetti into the boiling water. I push it down with a long fork. The spaghetti becomes soft in the hot water. 

Now, I must set the table. 
I walk to the dining room. The table is made of wood. 
I take four white plates. I put one plate for my father, one for my mother, one for Leo, and one for me. 
I take four forks and four knives. I put a fork on the left of each plate. I put a knife on the right. 
I take four glasses. I put a glass near each plate. 
I put a white napkin next to each plate. 
The table looks beautiful. It is ready for dinner. 

I go back to the kitchen. I check the spaghetti. I take one piece of spaghetti from the pot. I bite it. It is soft. It is ready. 
"Mother!" I call. "Can you help me with the hot water?"
My mother comes into the kitchen. The pot of boiling water is very heavy and dangerous for me. 
"Of course, Anna," she says. 
She takes two thick cloths. She holds the handles of the heavy pot. She carries it to the sink. She pours the water and the spaghetti into a big strainer. The hot water goes down the sink. The soft spaghetti stays in the strainer. There is a lot of steam. 

I put the spaghetti into a big glass bowl. 
Then, I take the hot tomato sauce from the small pan. I pour the red sauce over the yellow spaghetti. I mix it all together with two big spoons. It looks red and delicious. 

"Dinner is ready!" I call out loudly. 
My father and Leo come to the dining room. My mother sits down. I carry the big bowl of spaghetti to the table. I put it in the middle. 
"Wow, Anna," my father says. "That looks wonderful."

I use the big spoons to put spaghetti on everyone's plate. 
We all sit down. We start to eat. 
My father takes a big bite. He smiles. "This is very good, Anna," he says. "The sauce is perfect."
My mother tastes it. "Yes, it is delicious," she says. "You are a good cook."
Leo is eating very fast. He has red sauce on his face. "I love it!" he says. 

I feel very proud. I smile. My spaghetti is good. 
We eat and talk. We drink water from our glasses. We laugh at Leo's messy face. 
It is a very nice dinner. Cooking for my family is hard work, but it is very fun. I like to see them happy and full. Next Saturday, I will cook a chicken!
