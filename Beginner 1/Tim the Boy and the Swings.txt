Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Swings

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked to the park to play. Tim was a bit nervous because he had never been to this park before. He held his mom's hand tight as they walked. As they walked, Tim noticed something on a bench. It was an ashtray. Tim did not know what it was, so he asked his mom. His mom told him it was for grown-ups who smoke, but it was not good for them. Tim and his mom kept walking and finally got to the swings. Tim was not nervous anymore. He played on the swings and had lots of fun. He forgot all about the ashtray he saw earlier. At the end of the day, Tim and his mom went home with big smiles on their faces.

Tim and Mia like to play pretend. They have a big box of costumes and toys. Today, they want to be knights and fight a dragon. They put on helmets and capes and take swords and shields. They go to the backyard and look for a dragon. They see a big orange cat sleeping on a rock. They think it is a dragon. They sneak up to the cat and shout, "Charge!" The cat wakes up and sees the children. It is scared and angry. It hisses and scratches at them. It sounds like a roar. Tim and Mia are scared and run away. They drop their swords and shields. "Help, help, the dragon is chasing us!" they cry. They run to the house and slam the door. They hug each other and breathe hard. They hear a knock on the door. It is their mom. She has the cat in her arms. She says, "What are you doing? You scared the poor cat. He is not a dragon, he is Fluffy. He is our friend. You have to be nice to him." Tim and Mia feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, mom. We were playing pretend. We did not mean to hurt Fluffy. Can we pet him and say sorry?" Mom says, "Yes, you can. But be gentle and quiet. He is still scared. And next time, use your imagination, not your swords." Tim and Mia nod and smile. They pet Fluffy and say sorry. Fluffy purrs and licks them. He is not a dragon, he is a cat. And they are not knights, they are children. But they still like to play pretend.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to a big hotel with her mom and dad. The hotel was very tall and had many rooms. In the hotel, there was a black cat. The cat liked to play and jump around. Sue saw the cat and wanted to play with it. The cat jumped up and tried to hang on a tree branch. Sue laughed and said, "Silly cat, you can't hang on the tree!" The cat looked at her and meowed. Sue's mom and dad came over and saw the cat too. They all played with the cat and had a fun time at the hotel. The cat was happy to have new friends. They all said goodbye to the cat when it was time to go home.

One day, a man had an enormous ostrich. He wanted to sell it. So, he took the ostrich to the market. At the market, many people saw the big ostrich. They were very surprised. A little boy saw the ostrich and said, "Wow! That is a big bird! Can I buy it?" The man said, "Yes, you can buy my big ostrich." The little boy was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The big ostrich ran away! It ran so fast that no one could catch it. The little boy was sad, and the man did not sell his big ostrich. In the end, the man went home without his big ostrich, and the little boy went home without a new pet.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a poor spider. The spider was sad because it could not get to its web. The web was high up in a tree. Tim wanted to help the spider. Tim said, "Don't worry, spider! I will help you get back to your web." Tim picked up the spider and put it on a stick. He lifted the stick up to the tree. The spider crawled onto the web. The spider was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim smiled and waved goodbye to the spider. From that day on, Tim and the spider were best friends.
