Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bikes

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Amy. Amy loved to ride her old bike. She rode it every day in the park near her house. One sunny day, Amy met a boy named Tim. Tim was riding a big, red bike. He saw Amy and said, "Hi, do you want to ride bikes together?" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, let's ride!" Amy and Tim rode their bikes all day. They raced each other and laughed. They had so much fun. When the sun went down, they said goodbye and went home. From that day on, Amy and Tim became best friends and they always rode their bikes together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy car. One day, he saw a weak bird on the ground. The bird could not fly because it was stuck in a cable. Tom wanted to help the bird. Tom found a seat near the bird and thought about what to do. He was very careful not to scare the bird. He slowly moved the cable away from the bird. The bird was free! The bird tried to fly, but it was still weak. Tom gave the bird some food and water to make it strong again. After a while, the bird was strong enough to fly. The bird thanked Tom and flew away. Tom was happy that he could help the bird. He learned that it is good to be kind and help others when they are in trouble.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in a room. The cat saw a sack on the floor. The cat went into the sack. The dog did not see the cat go in. The dog was sad. He could not find the cat. The cat was hiding. The room was clear, but the dog could not see the cat. The dog looked and looked. He could not find the cat. Then, the dog heard a sound. It was the cat. The cat was in the sack. The dog was happy. He went to the sack. The dog tried to open the sack. The cat jumped out. The dog was surprised. The cat laughed. The dog laughed too. They played together again. The dog and the cat were happy. They played all day. The room was not clear now. But they did not care. They had fun with the sack and the floor. The cat and the dog were best friends.

Once upon a time, in a nice little house, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia had a big stack of colorful blocks. She loved to play with them every day. One day, Mia's friend Tom came to visit. "Hi, Mia!" said Tom. "Can I play with your blocks?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, of course! Let's build a big tower together!" So they started to play and built a tall tower with the blocks. As they played, Mia's mom called her. "Mia, please come eat your snack!" Mia told Tom, "I have to leave for a little bit, but I'll be back soon." Tom said, "Okay, I'll wait for you!" Mia left the room and Tom waited, looking at the big stack of blocks they had built together. When Mia came back, they continued to play and built an even bigger tower with the blocks. They laughed and had a great time. At the end of the day, Tom said, "Thank you, Mia, for letting me play with your nice blocks. I had so much fun!" Mia smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom! Come back and play again soon!" And so, Mia and Tom became the best of friends, and they played with the big stack of blocks every time they were together.

Once upon a time, there was a wise cat. The cat was very smart and could see very well. The cat lived in a big house with a little girl. One day, the little girl lost her skirt. She looked everywhere but could not find it. She was very sad. The wise cat saw the little girl crying and wanted to help her. The cat used its sharp eyes to look for the skirt. It looked high and low, and finally, the cat saw the skirt under the bed. The little girl was so happy to have her skirt back. She hugged the wise cat and they played together all day.
