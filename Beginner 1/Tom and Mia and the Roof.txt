Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Roof

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the yard. One day, they see a big ladder near the house. Tom has an idea. "Let's climb the ladder and go on the roof!" he says to Mia. Mia is scared. She knows the roof is dangerous. She remembers what her mom told her. "No, Tom, we can't do that. The roof is high and slippery. Mom said we should never go on the roof. It is not safe," she says to Tom. Tom does not listen. He thinks the roof is fun. He wants to see the birds and the clouds. He starts to climb the ladder. "Tom, stop! Come back!" Mia shouts. She tries to pull him down. But Tom is too fast. He reaches the top of the ladder and jumps on the roof. Mia is very worried. She runs to the house and knocks on the door. She hopes her mom is home. She needs to tell her what Tom did. "Mom, mom, please open the door! Tom is on the roof! He is on the roof!" she cries. Her mom hears her voice and opens the door. She sees Mia's face and knows something is wrong. "What did you say, Mia? Tom is on the roof? How did he get there?" she asks. Mia tells her mom everything. She tells her how Tom climbed the ladder and how she tried to stop him. She tells her how scared she is for Tom. Her mom hugs her and says, "Don't worry, Mia. You did the right thing. You are a good friend. You mentioned the roof to me. Now we have to get Tom down. Let's go and call him." They go to the yard and look at the roof. They see Tom sitting on the edge. He is waving his arms and smiling. He does not know he is in danger. "Tom, Tom, can you hear me?" her mom shouts. "You have to come down, Tom. The roof is dangerous. You can fall and hurt yourself. Please, Tom, come down." Tom hears her mom's voice and stops smiling. He realizes he made a mistake. He is sorry. He wants to come down. But he is afraid. He does not know how to climb the ladder again. "Mom, mom, I'm sorry. I'm sorry. I want to come down. But I'm scared. I don't know how to come down. Help me, mom, help me," he cries. Her mom says, "It's okay, Tom. I'm here to help you. Just listen to me. I will tell you what to do. First, you have to move away from the edge. Then, you have to turn around and face the ladder. Then, you have to hold the ladder with both hands and put your feet on the rungs. Then, you have to go down slowly, one step at a time. Can you do that, Tom?" Tom nods his head. He does what his mom says. He moves away from the edge. He turns around and faces the ladder. He holds the ladder with both hands and puts his feet on the rungs. He goes down slowly, one step at a time. Mia and her mom watch him. They are happy. They are proud of Tom. He is brave. He is learning. Tom reaches the ground. He runs to his mom and hugs her. He says, "I'm sorry, mom. I'm sorry. I was silly. I won't go on the roof again. I promise." His mom says, "I'm glad you're safe, Tom. I'm glad you're sorry. I love you, Tom. But you have to be careful. You have to listen to me and Mia. We only want what's best for you." Tom says, "I know, mom. I know. I love you too. And I love Mia. She is a good friend. She mentioned the roof to you. She saved me." Mia smiles. She is happy too. She says, "I'm glad you're okay, Tom. You are a good friend too. But next time, let's play something else. Something less dangerous." Tom agrees. He says, "Okay, Mia. Okay. Let's play something else. Something more fun." They hug each other. They hug their mom. They are friends. They are safe. They are happy.
