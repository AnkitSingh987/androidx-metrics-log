Category: Beginner
Title: The Man with Mustache and the Dog

One day, a man with a big mustache went to the park. He sat under a tree to read a book. He liked to read a lot. The mustache man was impatient because he wanted to know what happened in the story. A little dog came to the park. The dog saw the mustache man and wanted to play. The dog ran to the man and wagged its tail. The mustache man looked up from his book and saw the dog. The mustache man put his book down and played with the dog. They ran and jumped and had fun. After they played, the man went back to read his book. The dog sat next to the man, happy and tired. The mustache man was not impatient anymore. They both enjoyed the day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a calm little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big bag of beans. He loved to use the beans to play games with his friends. Tim's favorite game was to see who could throw the beans the farthest. One sunny day, Tim and his friend Sue went to the park to play with the beans. They stood in a line and took turns throwing the beans. "I can throw my bean very far!" said Tim. "I can throw my bean far too!" said Sue. They laughed and played, having lots of fun. As they played, a small bird flew down from a tree and picked up a bean. The bird carried the bean to its nest. Tim and Sue watched the bird and smiled. They decided to use the rest of the beans to help the bird and its family. They gently placed the beans near the tree for the birds to find. Tim and Sue felt happy and calm, knowing they had shared their fun with their new bird friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little house in the woods. In this house lived a kind rabbit named Remy. Remy was a careful rabbit who always made sure his house was clean and safe. One day, Remy's friend, a bird named Bella, came to visit. "Hi Remy!" said Bella. "Do you want to play outside?" Remy wanted to play, but he knew he had to delay their playtime. "I need to be careful and clean my house first," Remy told Bella. Bella understood and said, "Okay, I'll wait for you." Remy quickly cleaned his house and then went outside to play with Bella. They had a great time playing in the woods. And because Remy was careful and delayed their playtime, they could have fun knowing the little house was clean and safe.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play with toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big truck. The truck is made of metal. It is shiny and strong. It has four wheels and a hook. One day, Tom and Sam find a new toy in the garage. It is a metal box with wires and buttons. They do not know what it is, but it looks fun. They take it to their room and put it on the floor. "Let's play with the box," Tom says. "OK, but what can we do with it?" Sam asks. "Maybe we can attach it to the truck," Tom says. He picks up the box and tries to fit it on the truck. He sees a hole on the box and a hole on the truck. He thinks they can match. He pushes the box and the truck together. He hears a click. He smiles. He thinks he did it right. "Look, Sam, I attached the box to the truck!" Tom says. "Wow, Tom, you are smart!" Sam says. He wants to see the box. He touches a button on the box. He hears a beep. He touches another button. He hears a buzz. Suddenly, the box and the truck start to move. They make loud noises. They shake and smoke. They go fast and hit the wall. They break the lamp and the picture. They make a big mess. Tom and Sam are scared. They run out of the room. They cry for Mom. Mom comes and sees the mess. She is angry. She sees the box and the truck. She knows what they are. They are not toys. They are tools. They are for Dad. They are not for kids. She picks up the box and the truck. She throws them in the trash. She gives Tom and Sam a harsh look. She says, "No more playing with metal things. They are dangerous. You are in trouble." Tom and Sam are sorry. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We did not know. We just wanted to play." Mom sighs. She says, "I know, boys. But you have to be careful. You have to ask before you touch. You have to learn the rules." She hugs Tom and Sam. She says, "I love you, boys. But please, no more metal things."
