Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Smart Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a smart girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house in the woods with her mom and dad. One day, Lucy went outside to play. She found a big box hidden behind a tree. Lucy was excited and wanted to reveal what was inside the box. Lucy called her dad and said, "Dad, I found a big box! Can you help me open it?" Her dad came outside with an axe. He used the axe to break the lock on the box. When the lock was broken, Lucy and her dad opened the box together. Inside the box, they found many toys and games. Lucy was so happy to have new things to play with. She thanked her dad for helping her. From that day on, Lucy and her dad played with the toys and games together, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little bird named Blue. Blue could sing very well. One day, Blue saw some mud on the ground. It was wet and soft. Blue thought it would be fun to play in the mud. Blue jumped into the mud and started to play. While playing, Blue found a sharp rock. Blue said, "Ouch! That rock is sharp!" Blue's friend, Yellow, heard Blue and came to see what happened. Yellow was a happy and helpful bird. Yellow asked, "Are you okay, Blue?" Blue replied, "Yes, I am okay. But be careful, there is a sharp rock in the mud." Yellow helped Blue get out of the mud. They both flew away, leaving the sharp rock behind. Blue and Yellow continued to play and sing together, happy to be safe and sound.

Lily was going to the park with her mom. She wanted to take her small purse with her. She liked her purse. It was pink and had a flower on it. She put her doll and a snack in her purse. "Are you ready, Lily?" her mom asked. "Yes, mom. I have my purse," Lily said. They walked to the park. Lily saw many things on the way. She saw a dog, a bird, a car, and a tree. She pointed at them and said their names. When they got to the park, Lily saw a swing. She loved the swing. She ran to the swing and asked her mom to push her. "Can you hold my purse, mom?" Lily said. "Sure, honey. I'll hold your purse," her mom said. Lily gave her purse to her mom and got on the swing. Her mom pushed her gently. Lily felt happy. She smiled and laughed. Then she saw a girl on the slide. The girl had a big purse. It was purple and had a star on it. Lily wanted to see the girl's purse. She wanted to play with the girl. "Mom, can I go to the slide?" Lily asked. "Of course, Lily. But remember to be polite and ask the girl if you can see her purse," her mom said. "OK, mom. I'll be polite," Lily said. She got off the swing and ran to the slide. She said hello to the girl. "Hi, I'm Lily. What's your name?" Lily asked. "Hi, I'm Mia. Do you want to slide with me?" Mia asked. "Yes, please. But can I see your purse first? It's very big and pretty," Lily said. "Sure, you can see my purse. But can I see your purse too? It's very small and cute," Mia said. They agreed to part with their purses for a while. They showed each other their purses. They looked at the doll and the snack in Lily's purse. They looked at the book and the crayons in Mia's purse. They liked each other's purses. "Can we trade purses for a bit?" Lily asked. "OK, let's trade purses. But only for a bit," Mia said. They traded purses and slid down the slide. They had fun. They made a new friend. They were happy.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a toy on a shelf that he really wanted. Tim asked his mom if he could have the toy, but she said no. Tim was worried that he would never get the toy. One day, Tim's mom went to the store alone. She saw the toy on the shelf and thought of Tim. She wanted to make him happy, so she bought the toy and hid it in a bag. When she got home, she told Tim that she had a surprise for him. He was excited but didn't know what it was. Tim's mom told him to close his eyes and hold out his hands. When she said "release," he opened his eyes and saw the toy from the store! Tim was so happy and couldn't believe his mom got it for him. The worried feeling went away, and Tim and his mom played with the toy all day. The surprise made it even more special.
