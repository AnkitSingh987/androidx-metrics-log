Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Gifted Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Max. Max could do many tricks. He loved to play and have fun with his friends. One day, Max saw his friend, Tom the cat, looking sad. Max asked, "Tom, why are you sad?" Tom replied, "I lost my pants while playing in the park. Now I am cold and sad." Max wanted to help his friend. Max thought for a moment and said, "I can lend you my pants! They might be big, but they will keep you warm." Tom was happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" Max gave Tom his pants, and they both played happily in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a robot. The robot had a name. People call him Robo. Robo was a big and strong robot. He was also a bit uncomfortable. He did not like to sit or lie down because it was hard for him. One day, Robo met a little girl. The girl was sad. She lost her toy. Robo wanted to help the girl. So, they both looked for the toy. They looked under the bed, in the toy box, and behind the door. But they could not find the toy. Then, the little girl had an idea. She called her dog. The dog came running to help. The dog sniffed and sniffed. He found the toy under the couch. The little girl was happy, and so was Robo. Even though Robo was uncomfortable, he was happy to help his new friend. And they all played together for the rest of the day.

One day, a little bird was troubled. He did not have a home. He saw a big bush and thought it would be good for a home. So, the little bird flew to the bush. In the bush, the little bird met a big bug. The big bug was nice. The big bug said, "I have a home in this bush. You can have a home here too." The little bird was happy and they became friends. But one day, the bush started to move! The little bird and the big bug were scared. Then, they saw it was not a bush, but a big green turtle. The turtle said, "I have been carrying this bush on my back." The little bird and the big bug were surprised. They all became friends and lived together on the big green turtle.

One day, a little swan was swimming in a pond. The swan was feeling frustrated. It wanted to play with the other animals, but they were all busy. The swan swam up to a duck and said, "Do you want to play with me?" The duck replied, "I can't, I have to find food for my babies." The swan felt sad and swam away. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the water and landed next to the swan. The fish said, "Hey, I want to play! Let's have fun together!" The swan was very happy and they played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He loved to sleep all day and watch birds out the window. Tom lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. One week, Lily and Tom had a fun idea. They wanted to play a new game every day. On the first day, they played with a ball. On the second day, they played with a toy car. On the third day, they played with a doll. On the fourth day, they played with a train. On the fifth day, they played with a teddy bear. On the sixth day, they played with a puzzle. On the seventh day, they played with a kite. After the week was over, Tom and Lily were very happy. They had so much fun playing with all the toys. Tom was not lazy anymore. He loved to play with Lily and watch her laugh. They were the best of friends and lived happily ever after.

Tom and Sue like to play in the park. They like to run, swing, slide and climb. One day, they see a big truck near a big tree. The truck has a long arm with a sharp thing on the end. The truck is loud and scary. "What is the truck doing?" Sue asks Tom. "I don't know. Maybe it is cutting the tree," Tom says. "Why is it cutting the tree?" Sue asks. "I don't know. Maybe the tree is sick or old or in the way," Tom says. They watch the truck for a while. The sharp thing moves up and down and makes a lot of noise. The tree shakes and drops some leaves. Tom and Sue feel sad for the tree. "Let's go play somewhere else," Tom says. "OK," Sue says. They run to the other side of the park. They find a big hill with a lot of grass and flowers. They roll down the hill and laugh. They have fun. Suddenly, they hear a loud crash. They look back and see the big tree fall down. The tree is heavy and hits the ground hard. It makes a big hole and a big mess. The truck is still loud and scary. "Wow, look at that!" Tom says. "That was the tree they cut," Sue says. They feel sorry for the tree. They wonder what will happen to it. They hope it will be OK. They hug each other and go home.
