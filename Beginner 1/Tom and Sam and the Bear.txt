Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Bear

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the woods. They find a big axe near a tree. Tom wants to use the axe to chop wood. Sam wants to use the axe to make a fort. "Let me have the axe, Tom!" Sam says. "I want to make a fort!" "No, Sam, I want to chop wood!" Tom says. "I want to learn how to use the axe!" They pull the axe from each other. They are angry and loud. They do not see the bear behind them. The bear is hungry and angry too. It growls and shows its teeth. Tom and Sam hear the bear. They are scared. They drop the axe and run away. They hug each other and say sorry. They find their mom and dad. They tell them about the axe and the bear. Mom and dad are glad they are safe. They say they are brave and smart. They say they will teach them how to use the axe when they are older. Tom and Sam are happy. They say they love each other. They say the woods are amazing. They say they want to play again. But not with the bear.

Once upon a time there was a small girl. She was very brave and adventurous. One day she went for a hike in the forest. Suddenly it began to rain and the girl got lost. The girl began to cry but then she saw a big, friendly stitch. It said "Don't worry, I'll help you find your way home. Follow me". The stitch led the girl back to the path and she was very grateful. The girl wanted to thank the stitch so she said, "Can I be your friend?". The stitch said yes, and ever since that day it has been a very good friend. Every time the girl goes hiking, the stitch follows her and protects her. They are the best of friends and the girl is always grateful for the stitch's help.

Once there was a happy little boy. His name was Joe. Joe was always glad and he loved to play. One day, Joe's parents took him to school. He was very excited. The teacher gave Joe a quiz. He answered all the questions correctly. Then the teacher said he had to shoot at a target. Joe was very scared, but he did it. He missed the target. Joe's parents were not glad. They were so angry that they yelled at Joe. They said he was not a good shooter. Joe felt so sad. He cried and cried until he went to bed that night. The next day, he was still sad. He did not want to go to school anymore. He wished he had not missed the target. This was the end of Joe's bad day.

One day, a little cat named Lucy was very tired. She went to her bed and slept. The next day, Lucy did not wake up when the sun came up. She was still sleeping. Her friend, a small bird named Tim, saw that Lucy was still sleeping. Tim flew to Lucy's window and said, "Lucy, wake up! It's time to play!" But Lucy did not wake up. Tim had a big mouth and made a very loud sound. Lucy woke up and said, "Why did you make that loud sound, Tim? You scared me!" Tim felt foolish for scaring Lucy. He said, "I'm sorry, Lucy. I just wanted you to wake up and play with me." Just then, they heard a strange sound outside. They went to see what it was, and they saw a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "I heard Tim's big mouth, and I came to play too!" Lucy and Tim laughed, and they all played together. The day was full of fun and new friends.

One day, a pale cat went for a walk. The cat wore a big red boot on one foot. The cat liked the boot very much. It walked in the sun and saw a dog. The dog was big and had a ball. The dog saw the cat's red boot and wanted it. The cat said, "No, it is my boot." The dog was sad, but the cat warned the dog, "You can not have my boot." The dog looked at the cat and had an idea. The dog ran away and came back with a blue boot. The dog said, "I found this boot for you. Now we can both have a boot." The cat was happy and they both played with their boots. The cat and dog became good friends.
