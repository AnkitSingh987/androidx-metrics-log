Category: Beginner
Title: It and the Window

It was a summer day and Sarah was looking out the window. It was so sunny outside that she couldn't wait to go out and play. Suddenly, she heard a sound. It was rain! Sarah was excited to go outside and play in the rain. She put on her raincoat and boots, and ran outside. In the sky she could see a beautiful parrot flying around. She watched the parrot for a while and noticed how polite he was - he said 'hello' to all the other animals in the sky! The rain stopped and the sun came out again. Sarah was sad since she didn't get to play in the rain, but she was happy she saw the polite parrot!

Once upon a time, there was a lively bunny named Quick. Quick loved to run and play in the big green field. One day, he met a new friend named Slow. Slow was a little turtle who liked to take his time. Quick said to Slow, "Let's have a race! I bet I can beat you." Slow thought about it and said, "Okay, let's do it!" They started the race, and Quick ran very fast. Slow walked slowly, but he did not give up. As the race went on, Quick's speed began to increase. He was so fast that he finished the race before Slow even got halfway. Quick cheered for his friend as he crossed the finish line. Slow was happy that he finished the race too. From that day on, Quick and Slow were the best of friends. They learned that it's not always about being fast, but about having fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play with clay. One day, he made a big clay ball. Tim was very happy with his clay ball. His friend, Sam, saw the clay ball and wanted one too. Sam was envious of Tim's clay ball. Tim wanted to help Sam. So, he showed Sam how to make a clay ball. They took some clay and rolled it in their hands. Then, they both had big clay balls. Now, Sam was not envious anymore. They were both very happy with their clay balls. They wanted to play a game with the clay balls. They decided to strike the clay balls with a stick. Tim and Sam took turns hitting the clay balls. The clay balls went flying through the air. They laughed and had a lot of fun. They played with their clay balls until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big dream. Mia wanted to serve food to her friends. She thought it was important to help others. One day, Mia asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Yes, we can make food for your friends." They made sandwiches, cookies, and juice. Mia was very happy. Mia and her mom took the food to the park. Mia served the food to her friends. They all said, "Thank you, Mia!" Mia learned that helping others is important and it made her feel good. And that was the best day ever.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big orange slide. They want to try it. "Look, Mia, a slide!" Tom says. "Let's go!" "Okay, Tom, but be careful. It is very high," Mia says. They run to the slide. Tom goes first. He sits on the slide and twists his body. He goes down very fast. He laughs and shouts, "Whee!" Mia watches Tom. She is scared. She does not want to twist. She thinks it is too hard. "Come on, Mia, it is fun!" Tom says. "I will teach you how to twist." He climbs up the slide again. He sits next to Mia. He holds her hand. "Look, Mia, you just move your hips like this," he says. He shows her how to twist. "Then you go down like this." He pushes them down the slide. Mia feels the wind in her hair. She feels the slide under her. She feels Tom's hand in hers. She twists with him. She goes down very fast. She laughs and shouts, "Whee!" They reach the bottom of the slide. They hug each other. They are happy. "Thank you, Tom, you are a good teach," Mia says. "You are welcome, Mia, you are a good friend," Tom says. They smile at each other. They run to the slide again. They twist and slide and have fun. They like the big orange slide.
