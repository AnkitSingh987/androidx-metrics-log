Category: Beginner
Title: The Park Where Kids and the Swings

Once upon a time, there was a park where kids liked to play. In this park, there were swings, slides, and a big sandbox. All the kids loved to go there. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to play. He was so happy to be there. But then, a big kid named Max started to tease Tim. Max took Tim's toy and ran away. This made Tim very upset. Tim's mom saw what happened and came to help. She talked to Max and told him that teasing is not nice. Max gave Tim's toy back and said sorry. Tim and Max played together in the park, and they became good friends. From that day on, they always had fun at the park and never teased each other again.

---

Ben and Lily were playing in the park with their mom. They saw a big truck with many animals inside. They wanted to see the animals, so they ran to the truck. Mom followed them and told them to be careful. Inside the truck, they saw a lion, a zebra, a monkey, and a leopard. The leopard was sleeping on a soft mat. Ben and Lily thought the leopard was pretty, with its yellow and black spots. They wanted to touch it, but mom said no. "Leopard is not a pet. Leopard is wild. Leopard can bite and scratch. You can look, but not touch," mom said. But Ben did not listen. He was curious and brave. He reached out his hand and touched the leopard's tail. The leopard woke up and was angry. It turned around and slapped Ben's hand with its paw. Ben cried out in pain and fear. "Ow! Ow! Ow! Mom, help!" Ben shouted. Mom ran to Ben and hugged him. She checked his hand and saw a mild cut. She took out a band-aid and put it on his hand. "I'm sorry, Ben. Leopard hurt you. You should have listened to me. Leopard is not a pet. Leopard is wild. You have to be careful with wild animals. They don't like to be touched," mom said. Ben nodded and said sorry. He learned his lesson. He did not want to touch the leopard again. He hugged mom and said he loved her. Lily watched and felt sorry for Ben. She also learned her lesson. She did not want to touch the leopard either. She hugged Ben and said she loved him. The leopard watched and felt sorry too. It did not mean to hurt Ben. It was just scared and angry. It wished Ben and Lily would leave it alone. It went back to sleep on its soft mat.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw his friend, Sam, playing with his original red ball. Tim wanted to play with Sam, but Sam's ball was very dirty. Tim told Sam, "We need to wash the ball." Sam didn't want to wash the ball. He said, "No, I like it dirty." Tim was sad because he didn't want to play with a dirty ball. He tried to think of a way to make Sam change his mind. Then, Tim had an idea. He told Sam, "If we wash the ball, it will look new and pretty. Everyone will want to play with us." Sam thought about it and agreed. They went to Tim's house and washed the ball. It looked bright and clean. Sam was happy to see the ball so clean. He said, "You were right, Tim. The ball looks nice now." They played together with the clean ball, and more friends joined them. Tim was glad he helped Sam see the sight of a clean ball, and they all had a great time playing together.

One day, a poor little dog named Max was very cold. He saw a coat on the ground and put it on to feel warm. Max was so happy to have a warm coat. He wanted to celebrate with his friends. Max went to his friend, Cat, and said, "I found a coat! Let's celebrate!" Cat looked at Max and said, "That's my coat! I lost it yesterday." Max felt sad and gave the coat back to Cat. They both felt cold now. Just then, a big bird flew down with a big bag. The bird said, "I have coats for everyone!" Max, Cat, and all their friends got new coats. They all felt warm and happy. They all celebrated together, wearing their new coats.
