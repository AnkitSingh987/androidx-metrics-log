Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Glass

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very persistent. He always wanted to see new things. One day, Tim saw a big glass on the table. He wanted to see what was inside the glass. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can I see what is in the glass?" His mom said, "No, Tim. The glass is not for you. It can break and hurt you." But Tim was persistent. He did not listen to his mom. When his mom was not looking, Tim tried to reach the glass. He climbed on the chair and stretched his hand. Suddenly, the glass fell and broke. Tim cried because he was hurt. His mom came and said, "I told you not to touch the glass, Tim." The end.

Tom and Lily went to the park with Mom. They saw a pond with many ducks. The ducks were swimming and quacking. Tom and Lily liked the ducks. They wanted to feed them some bread. Mom gave them some bread and said, "Be nice to the ducks. Don't throw the bread too hard. Don't chase them or scare them. They are living things, like us. They have feelings, too." Tom and Lily nodded and said, "OK, Mom." They broke the bread into small pieces and threw them gently to the ducks. The ducks came closer and ate the bread. They seemed happy and friendly. Tom saw a duck that was smaller and fluffier than the others. It had yellow feathers and a pink beak. Tom thought it was very cute. He wanted to pick it up and hug it. He said to Lily, "Look at that duck. It's so cute. I want to pick it up." Lily said, "No, Tom. Don't do that. Mom said be nice to the ducks. The duck might not like it. It might be scared or hurt. It might bite you or flap its wings. It might miss its mom or dad. Let it be with its friends." Tom said, "But I just want to hold it for a little bit. It won't mind. It will like me." He reached out his hand to grab the duck. The duck saw Tom's hand and got scared. It quacked loudly and tried to swim away. But Tom was faster. He picked up the duck and lifted it in the air. The duck was very unhappy. It felt trapped and scared. It bit Tom's finger and flapped its wings. It quacked louder and louder. Tom felt a sharp pain in his finger. He dropped the duck and cried. He said, "Ow, ow, ow! The duck bit me! It hurt me!" Mom heard Tom's cry and ran to him. She saw his finger bleeding and the duck swimming away. She said, "Oh, Tom. What did you do? Why did you pick up the duck? Didn't I tell you to be nice to the ducks?" Tom said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I wanted to hug the duck. It was so cute. I didn't mean to make it mad." Mom said, "I know, Tom. But you have to respect the ducks. They are not toys. They are living things, like us. They have feelings, too. They don't like to be picked up by strangers. They like to be free and safe. You have to be gentle and kind to them. That's how you show them you care." Tom said, "I understand, Mom. I'm sorry. I won't pick up the ducks again. I will be nice to them. I will feed them and watch them and talk to them. But I won't touch them." Mom said, "That's good, Tom. I'm proud of you. Now let me clean your finger and put a band-aid on it. It will heal soon. And maybe the duck will forgive you, too." Tom said, "Thank you, Mom. I love you. And I love the ducks, too." Mom said, "I love you, too, Tom. And I'm sure the ducks love you, too. As long as you are nice to them."

One day, a little cat named Kitty was playing in the park. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Up, up, up she went, but she got stuck. Kitty was scared and didn't know how to get down. A kind boy named Tim saw Kitty in the tree. He said, "Don't worry, Kitty. I will rescue you!" Tim found a long stick and used it to help Kitty climb down. Kitty was so grateful to Tim for helping her. After the rescue, Tim said, "I have a lunch with me. Would you like to share it with me, Kitty?" Kitty meowed happily and they sat under the tree. They shared a yummy lunch and became good friends. From that day on, Kitty and Tim played together in the park every day.
