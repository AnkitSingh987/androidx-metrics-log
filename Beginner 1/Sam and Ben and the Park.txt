Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Ben and the Park

Sam and Ben are brothers. They like to play outside with their bikes. Sam has a red bike and Ben has a blue bike. They have vests to wear when they ride. The vests are yellow and have lights on them. Mom says the vests are important because they help cars see them. One day, Sam and Ben are riding their bikes on the sidewalk. They see a big truck coming on the road. The truck is loud and fast. Sam and Ben want to cross the road to go to the park. Sam says, "Let's go, Ben. The park is fun." Ben says, "No, Sam. We have to stop. The truck is coming. It is dangerous. We have to wait until the truck passes." Sam says, "But I want to go now. The park is fun. The truck is far away. It will not hit us." Ben says, "No, Sam. You are wrong. The truck is close. It will hit us if we go. We have to stop and look both ways. That is what Mom taught us. It is important to be safe." Sam does not listen to Ben. He pushes his bike and runs to the road. He does not see the truck. He does not hear Ben yelling, "Stop, Sam, stop!" The truck sees Sam. The truck honks its horn. The truck tries to stop. But it is too late. The truck hits Sam and his bike. Sam falls to the ground. His bike is broken. His vest is torn. He is hurt. He cries. Ben runs to Sam. He is scared and sad. He says, "Sam, Sam, are you okay? I told you to stop. Why did you not listen to me? You were not careful. You were not smart. You were not safe." Sam says, "I'm sorry, Ben. I was wrong. I should have listened to you. You were right. I was not careful. I was not smart. I was not safe. I was silly. I was bad." Ben says, "It's okay, Sam. I forgive you. But you have to promise me something. You have to promise me that you will always stop and look both ways before you cross the road. You have to promise me that you will always wear your vest when you ride your bike. You have to promise me that you will always be safe. Because you are my brother. And you are important to me." Sam says, "I promise, Ben. I promise. Thank you for being my brother. And thank you for being important to me."

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Snap. Snap loved to eat toast for breakfast. Every morning, Snap would jump up on the table and wait for his toast. One day, Snap's friend, a little bird, came to visit. The bird saw the toast on the table and wanted to try it too. Snap was happy to share his toast with his friend. They both took small bites and enjoyed the taste. After they finished eating the toast, Snap and the bird played outside in the sun. They ran and jumped and had so much fun. When it was time for the bird to go home, Snap said goodbye and went back inside. He knew that tomorrow, there would be more toast to share with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man. The rich man had a big fridge. The fridge was full of yummy food. One hot day, the rich man wanted to bathe. He filled a tub with water. He played with his toy duck in the tub. After the rich man's bath, he was hungry. He went to his big fridge. He ate a lot of yummy food. Then he went to bed. The rich man was happy.

Once upon a time there lived a little boy. He was 3 years old and he was very happy. One day he wanted to join his mum on an airplane and see the world, so he packed his luggage. He was so excited and he felt warm. He said bye to his mum, who was sad to see him go but smiled. The little boy joined the plane, but when the door closed behind him, he knew something was wrong. He was all alone. The luggage he was carrying was too heavy and he couldn't find his mum. Then the plane took off and he felt very scared. The little boy looked out of the window and saw the ground getting smaller and smaller. He started to cry and he never saw his mum again. He was very sad and he never flew in a plane again.
