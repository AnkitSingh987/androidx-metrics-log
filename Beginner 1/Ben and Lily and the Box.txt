Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Box

Ben and Lily were playing in their room. They had many toys and books, but they wanted to try something new. They saw a big box in the closet. It was from Mom's new TV. "Let's play with the box!" Ben said. "We can make a house or a car or a rocket!" "OK!" Lily said. "But how do we get the box out? It is too big for the door." Ben had an idea. He found Mom's scissors and cut the box into smaller pieces. He gave some to Lily and kept some for himself. They taped the pieces together and made their own shapes. "Look, I made a car!" Ben said. He put wheels and a steering wheel on his shape. He got inside and pretended to drive. "Beep beep!" "Look, I made a rocket!" Lily said. She put wings and a cone on her shape. She got inside and pretended to fly. "Zoom zoom!" They had fun with their shapes. But then they heard Mom coming upstairs. "Uh oh, we have to clean up!" Ben said. "Mom will be mad if she sees the mess." They tried to put the pieces back in the box, but they did not fit. They had cut them too much. "What do we do?" Lily said. "We can't hide them under the bed or in the drawers. They are too big." Ben had another idea. He found Mom's hair dryer and plugged it in. He pointed it at the pieces and turned it on. "Maybe this will shrink them!" he said. But it did not work. The pieces did not shrink. They only got hot and smelly. "Stop, stop!" Lily said. "You are making it worse!" They turned off the hair dryer and looked at the pieces. They were brown and wrinkled and sticky. They heard Mom knock on the door. "Ben, Lily, what are you doing in there?" Mom said. They did not know what to say. They looked at each other and hoped Mom would not be too mad.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. They have a big box of chalk. They can make pictures on the sidewalk with chalk. One day, they find a big orange chalk. It is very bright and pretty. They want to draw a big sun with it. But the orange chalk is too heavy. They cannot lift it. "Let's crawl with it," Lily says. "We can push it with our hands." "OK," Ben says. "But we have to be careful. We don't want to break it." They crawl on the sidewalk with the orange chalk. They push it slowly and gently. They make a long orange line. They are happy. They reach a big empty space. They can draw a big sun there. They stop crawling. They sit up. "Wow, look at our line!" Lily says. "It is so long and orange. It looks like a snake." "It does," Ben says. "But we want a sun, not a snake. How can we make a sun?" They think for a moment. Then they have an idea. "We can make a circle with the orange chalk," Lily says. "And then we can make rays with other colors." "Good idea," Ben says. "Let's do it." They use the orange chalk to make a big circle. Then they use other chalks to make rays. They use yellow, red, pink, purple, blue, and green. They make a beautiful sun. They look at their sun. They smile. They are proud. They hear a voice. "Wow, what a nice sun!" It is Mom. She has come to pick them up. "Thank you, Mom," Lily and Ben say. "We made it with chalk. We crawled with a big orange chalk. It was fun." Mom hugs them. She is proud too. "Let's go home," she says. "You can show me your sun tomorrow. It will still be there." They nod. They take their box of chalk. They wave goodbye to their sun. They go home with Mom. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there lived two bad birds called Bob and Bea. Every day, they would fly up high and steal fuel. They had so much fuel that they did not know what to do with it, so they locked it away in a big and sour cave. One day, Bob and Bea flew over a peaceful town and thought they could try to steal something else. But when they landed, they heard a loud voice shouting: "Get away! Get away!" It was the sheriff. He knew Bob and Bea were up to no good, so he locked them up in a cage. The sheriff then took all the fuel that Bob and Bea had stolen and used it to make a feast for the town. Everyone in the town was so happy and thanked the sheriff for his kindness. Bob and Bea were very sad and vowed never to steal again. From then on, they would help the sheriff protect the town from bad birds, and they are still doing it today.
