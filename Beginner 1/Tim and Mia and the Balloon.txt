Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Mia and the Balloon

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big balloon. The balloon is red and yellow and blue. It is very pretty. "Look, a balloon!" Tim says. "I want to fly with it!" "Me too!" Mia says. "Let's go and get it!" They run to the balloon. A man is holding it. He is smiling. "Hello, kids," he says. "Do you like my balloon?" "Yes, we do!" Tim and Mia say. "Would you like to fly with it?" the man asks. "Yes, please!" Tim and Mia say. "OK, then," the man says. "But you have to make me a promise. You have to do what I say. And you have to come back soon. Can you do that?" "Yes, we can!" Tim and Mia say. They are very excited. "Good," the man says. "Then hold on to this rope. And don't let go. Ready?" "Yes, we are!" Tim and Mia say. The man lets go of the balloon. The balloon goes up in the sky. Tim and Mia go up with it. They feel like they are flying. They laugh and scream. "Wow, this is fun!" Tim says. "Look, we can see everything!" Mia says. They see the park, the trees, the flowers, the birds, the people. They see their moms and dads. They wave to them. "Hi, mom! Hi, dad!" Tim and Mia say. Their moms and dads see them. They are very scared. "Tim! Mia! Come down!" they shout. "That man is bad! He is crazy!" But Tim and Mia don't hear them. They are too high. They don't see the man. He is gone. He has taken their bikes. He has tricked them. He has broken his promise. Tim and Mia are stuck in the balloon. They don't know how to come down. They start to cry. "Help! Help!" they say. But no one can help them. The balloon goes higher and higher. It goes away from the park. It goes to the clouds. It goes to the sun. Tim and Mia are gone. They never come back.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the snow. He saw a hidden igloo and went inside. In the igloo, he found a big toy car. Tim was very happy and wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house and said, "Sam, come see what I found!" Sam followed Tim to the hidden igloo. When they got there, Sam saw the big toy car and wanted to play with it too. But the toy car was too big for both of them to play with at the same time. So, they decided to split the toy car. Tim took one half, and Sam took the other half. They played with their halves of the toy car all day, and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they put the toy car back together and left it in the hidden igloo for other kids to find and play with.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there lived a compassionate jellyfish named Jilly. Jilly was very kind and loved to help her friends. One day, Jilly saw a little fish named Finny, who was sad and crying. "What's wrong, Finny?" Jilly asked. Finny said, "I tried to play with a crab, but he pinched my fin and it hurts." Jilly wanted to help Finny feel better. Jilly had an idea. She took Finny to her friend, Sally the sea star. Sally was good at fixing boo-boos. Sally put a soft bandage on Finny's fin and gave him a big hug. Soon, Finny felt much better. Finny said, "Thank you, Jilly and Sally, for helping me. You both are so kind!" Jilly and Sally smiled, happy they could help their friend. From that day on, Finny, Jilly, and Sally became the best of friends, and they played together in the big sea every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Olive. Olive was a very mild girl. She never yelled or got mad. She always said "please" and "thank you". One day, Olive went to the park with her mom. She saw a big slide and wanted to go on it. "Please, can I go on the slide?" she asked her mom. Her mom said, "Yes, you can go on the slide, Olive." Olive went up the slide and slid down. She had so much fun! She played with her friends and they all said "please" when they wanted a turn. Olive was happy and had a great day at the park.
