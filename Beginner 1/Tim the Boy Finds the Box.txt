Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a brother named Sam. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, they found a big box in their room. They did not know what was inside the box. Tim and Sam wanted to solve the mystery. "What do you think is in the box?" asked Tim. "I don't know," said Sam, "Let's open it and see!" They opened the box and found many colorful balloons inside. They were so happy and started to play with the balloons. As they played, something unexpected happened. The balloons started to lift Tim and Sam up into the air! They were scared at first, but then they started to enjoy flying with the balloons. They saw their house, their friends, and their dog from up high. After a while, the balloons slowly brought them back down to the ground. Tim and Sam were very happy about their fun adventure, and they knew they had a special story to tell their friends.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big cage in the park. They go to look at the cage. Inside the cage, there is a lion. The lion is very big and has a lot of hair. The lion looks sad. Tom and Lily feel sorry for the lion. "Hello, lion," Tom says. "Why are you in the cage?" The lion does not answer. He just lies on the ground and sighs. "Maybe he is hungry," Lily says. "Let's give him some food." Tom and Lily find some bread and apples. They throw them into the cage. The lion does not eat them. He just looks at them and sighs. "Maybe he is lonely," Tom says. "Let's be his friends." Tom and Lily go closer to the cage. They smile and wave at the lion. The lion does not smile or wave back. He just looks at them and sighs. "Maybe he is bored," Lily says. "Let's do something fun." Tom and Lily think of something fun. They decide to do a show for the lion. They sing and dance and tell jokes. They hope the lion will like their show. The lion watches their show. He does not laugh or clap. He just looks at them and sighs. Tom and Lily are sad. They do not know how to make the lion happy. "Maybe he does not like us," Tom says. "Maybe he does not like the cage," Lily says. They look at the cage. The cage is very small and has no toys or plants. The cage is not a good home for the lion. "Maybe he wants to go home," Tom says. "Where is his home?" Lily asks. The lion hears their question. He lifts his head and roars. He points his paw at the sky. He shows them a picture in his mind. The picture is of a big land with many trees and animals. The land is his home. Tom and Lily understand. The lion misses his home. He does not belong in the cage. He belongs in the land. "We are sorry, lion," Tom says. "We wish you could go home." "We are sorry, too," Lily says. "We hope you will be happy someday." Tom and Lily bow to the lion. They say goodbye and leave the cage. They hope someone will help the lion go home. They hope the lion will find his happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a big rhinoceros. He was very clean. Every day, he would sit by the river and play with his friends. They all loved to splash in the water. One day, the rhinoceros saw a dirty rock. He wanted to help make it clean. So, he took the rock to the river and washed it. The rock became clean and shiny. The rhinoceros was very happy. The rhinoceros and his friends sat by the clean rock and played all day. They had so much fun. The clean rock became their new friend. And they all lived happily ever after.

Sam and Mia are best friends. They like to play together in the park. One day, they find a big box near the slide. They wonder what is inside. "Let's peek!" Sam says. He lifts the flap of the box and looks in. He sees some old clothes, some books, and some toys. "Wow, look at all these things!" Mia says. She joins Sam and picks up a pair of pants. They are green and have flowers on them. "These are funny pants!" Sam laughs. He puts the pants on his head like a hat. "I am a creative king!" he says. He finds a stick and pretends it is a sword. Mia giggles. She finds a scarf and wraps it around her neck. "I am a creative queen!" she says. She finds a ball and pretends it is a crown. They play in the box for a long time. They make up stories and act them out. They have a lot of fun. Then they hear a voice. It is Sam's mom. She is looking for them. "Sam, Mia, where are you?" she calls. Sam and Mia peek out of the box. They see Sam's mom walking towards them. They smile and wave. "Hi, mom!" Sam says. "Hi, Mrs. Lee!" Mia says. Sam's mom sees them and laughs. She sees their creative outfits and their happy faces. "What are you doing in there?" she asks. "We are playing with the things in the box!" Sam says. "We are creative kings and queens!" Mia says. Sam's mom nods. She is glad they are having fun. "That's wonderful!" she says. "But it's time to go home now. Come on, let's take off those funny pants and scarf and put them back in the box. Maybe someone else will find them and play with them too." Sam and Mia agree. They take off their creative clothes and put them back in the box. They say goodbye to the box and thank it for the fun. Then they hold hands and follow Sam's mom to the car. They are happy. They had a great day. They are best friends.
