Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Net

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his big net. He would run around and catch bugs with it. One day, he saw a butterfly with spicy red wings. It was so pretty! Tim wanted the butterfly to be his friend. He tried to catch it with his net, but the butterfly was too fast. Tim felt sad. Then, he thought of an idea. He could ask the butterfly to come to him. He said, "Please, butterfly, come to me. I want to be your friend." The butterfly heard Tim and flew to him. It landed on his hand. The butterfly accepted Tim's friendship. They became the best of friends and played together all the time. And that's how Tim and the spicy red butterfly became friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. In the hall, there was a flexible toy snake. The toy snake was very good at bending and twisting. It could move in many ways. The children loved to play with the toy snake. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the hall. She saw the toy snake and wanted to play with it. But when she picked up the toy snake, something unexpected happened. The toy snake started to cry! Lucy was very surprised. Lucy thought the toy snake was sad. She wanted to make it happy again. So, she hugged the toy snake and sang a happy song. Suddenly, the toy snake stopped crying. It was a magic toy snake! It could feel love and happiness. From that day on, Lucy and the magic toy snake became best friends.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and look for bugs and worms. They had a big bucket to put their finds in. "Look, Lily, I found a shiny rock!" Ben said, holding up a smooth pebble. "Wow, Ben, that's pretty!" Lily said. "Can I see?" Ben gave her the rock and she rubbed it with her fingers. She saw something red and shiny under the dirt. "What's that?" she asked, pointing to the red thing. Ben dug some more and pulled out a glass bottle. It had a cork and a label with words they could not read. "Maybe it's wine," Ben said. "Mama and Papa drink wine sometimes. They say it's yummy." Lily nodded. She wanted to try the wine. She pulled the cork with her teeth and spit it out. She smelled the bottle. It smelled sour and funny. "Yuck, this is not wine!" she said, making a face. "This is bad juice!" She gave the bottle to Ben and he smelled it too. He agreed that it was bad juice. He threw the bottle back in the hole. "Let's find something else," he said. They dug some more and found a chubby worm. It was long and wiggly and had many legs. "Wow, Lily, I found a chubby worm!" Ben said, holding it up. "Wow, Ben, that's gross!" Lily said. "Can I touch?" Ben gave her the worm and she felt it with her fingers. It tickled and squirmed. "Ha ha, this is fun!" she said. "Let's put it in the bucket!" They put the worm in the bucket and went to find more things. They had a lot of fun in the garden. They did not care about the bad juice anymore.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play sport in the park. They have a ball and a net. They try to throw the ball in the net. That is their sport. Tom is tall. He can throw the ball high. Lily is not tall. She can throw the ball low. They take turns to throw the ball. They count how many times they can throw the ball in the net. That is their number. One day, a dog comes to the park. The dog likes the ball. The dog runs to the ball and takes it in his mouth. He runs away with the ball. "Hey, that is our ball!" Tom says. He runs after the dog. "Come back, dog!" Lily says. She runs after Tom. The dog is fast. He runs to the other side of the park. He sees a pond. He jumps in the pond with the ball. Tom and Lily stop. They see the dog in the pond. The ball is wet. "Oh no, our ball is wet!" Tom says. "Bad dog!" Lily says. The dog looks at them. He wags his tail. He thinks they want to play with him. Tom and Lily look at each other. They smile. They run to the pond. They splash in the water. They play with the dog and the ball. They have fun. They forget their number. They make a new friend. That is their story.
