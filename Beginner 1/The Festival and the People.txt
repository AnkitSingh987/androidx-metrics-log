Category: Beginner
Title: The Festival and the People

Once upon a time there was a festival. Everyone in the town seemed very excited. Everyone wore pretty clothes and put on big smiles. In the center of the festival was a graceful girl. She had long hair and a big smile. All the people noticed her and smiled at her. She seemed very happy and moved around the festival with grace. She twirled around a few times and hopped around the festival square. All the people clapped and cheered when she moved gracefully. At the end of the festival, the grace girl was given a big prize. All the people said she was the most graceful girl anyone had ever seen. She was very happy and thanked all the people for the prize.

Tim and Sam were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big cloud in the sky. It was white and fluffy. "Look, a cloud!" Tim said. "Let's pretend it is a dragon." "OK!" Sam said. "I will be the knight and you will be the princess." They ran around the park, making noises and having fun. They did not see the other kids watching them. The other kids laughed at Tim and Sam. They thought they were silly and babyish. They called them names and threw stones at them. "Hey, dragon and princess, go home!" one kid shouted. "You are too old to play like that!" Tim and Sam felt ashamed. They stopped playing and looked at the ground. They wanted to go home. But then, the cloud changed. It became dark and angry. It made a loud noise and flashed a bright light. It started to rain hard. The other kids screamed and ran away. They were scared of the cloud. They got wet and cold. Tim and Sam looked at the cloud. They saw a dragon's face in it. It smiled at them and winked. "Thank you, cloud," Tim said. "You are our friend." "Yes, thank you," Sam said. "You are our hero." They hugged each other and smiled. They were not ashamed anymore. They were happy. But then, the cloud opened its mouth and breathed fire. It burned Tim and Sam to ashes. They were gone. The cloud was not their friend. It was a bad cloud. It liked to trick and hurt people. It laughed and flew away. It looked for more victims.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends at the park. One day, Tim's mom made a yummy lunch for him. The lunch had a sandwich, an apple, and a cookie. At the park, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue did not have a lunch. Tim wanted to help Sue. He said, "Sue, do you want to eat lunch with me? My lunch has a big sandwich, and I can share it with you." Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! I would like that. Thank you for sharing your lunch with me." Tim and Sue sat under a big tree and ate the yummy lunch together. They were very happy and had a fun day at the park.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toy cars. They make zoom sounds as they move them on the floor. They pretend they are racing on a big road. "Zoom, zoom, zoom!" Anna says. "My car is faster than yours!" "No, it's not!" Ben says. "My car is stronger and can push yours away!" They start to bump their cars into each other. They laugh and shout. "Stop that noise!" Mom says. She comes into the room. She looks angry. "You are making too much mess and you are not being obedient. You have to clean up your toys and go to bed. It is late." "But Mom, we are having fun!" Anna says. "Can we play a little more? Please?" "No, you can't." Mom says. "You have to listen to me. I am the boss. I do not permit you to play more. You have to go to bed now. Or you will be in trouble." Anna and Ben feel sad. They do not want to go to bed. They want to play more. But they know Mom is right. They have to be obedient. They do not want to be in trouble. "Okay, Mom." They say. "We will clean up and go to bed." They put their toy cars in a box. They say good night to Mom. They go to their room. They brush their teeth and put on their pajamas. They get into their beds. "Good night, Anna." Ben says. "Good night, Ben." Anna says. They close their eyes. They dream of zooming cars. They smile.
