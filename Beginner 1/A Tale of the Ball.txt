Category: Beginner
Title: A Tale of the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree. Under the tree, a boy and a girl sat. They liked to share their toys with each other. They were very happy. One day, they found a box under the tree. The box was from the universe. They opened the box and saw a big ball. The ball was very pretty. They wanted to share the ball and play with it. But when they touched the ball, something unexpected happened. The ball changed into a big, friendly bear. The bear could talk! The boy, the girl, and the bear played together under the thick tree. They shared their toys and had lots of fun. And they all became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big billboard in the middle of the town. The billboard had a picture of a man with a big smile. It made people happy when they saw it. One day, a little boy named Timmy walked by the billboard with his mom. Timmy looked up and saw the smiling man. He asked his mom, "Why is the man on the billboard smiling?" His mom said, "He smiles because he wants to make people happy." Timmy thought it was difficult to smile all the time. He wanted to try it too. So, he smiled at everyone he saw that day. Some people smiled back, and some people didn't. But Timmy kept trying. At the end of the day, Timmy's mom said, "You did a good job making people happy today." Timmy felt happy too, and they both smiled as they walked home.

Lily liked to read books with her mom. They had many books with pictures and stories. Lily had a favorite book. It was about a bunny who had many friends. Lily liked to look at the bunny and his friends on every page. One day, Lily wanted to read her favorite book with her mom. She went to her room and looked for the book. But she could not find it. She looked under her bed, in her closet, and in her toy box. But the book was not there. Lily felt sad and angry. She wondered who took her book. She went to the living room and saw her brother, Ben. Ben was playing with his cars on the floor. Lily saw something on the couch. It was her book! But it was not the same. The book was torn and dirty. Some pages were missing. Some pages had scribbles on them. Lily felt very sad and angry. She ran to Ben and grabbed the book. She yelled at him. "This is my book! You broke it! You are bad!" Ben looked at Lily and the book. He did not understand. He liked the book too. He wanted to see the bunny and his friends. He did not mean to break the book. He was just playing. He felt scared and sorry. He started to weep. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I like the book. I want to read it with you." Lily looked at Ben and the book. She saw his tears and heard his words. She felt sorry too. She knew Ben did not know better. He was just a baby. She hugged him and said, "It's okay, Ben. I'm sorry too. I love you. We can still read the book. We can make up our own stories." They went to their mom and showed her the book. Mom saw the book and smiled. She said, "What a creative book! You made it your own. Let's read it together." She took the book and opened it. She saw an empty page. She said, "This page is empty. What do you think happens here?" Lily and Ben looked at the page and thought. They said, "The bunny and his friends go to the park. They play and have fun. They are happy." Mom nodded and said, "That's a wonderful story. Let's read the next page." They read the book and made up their own stories. They laughed and had fun. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was in a class with many friends. One day, the class went to a park to play. Tim saw a dirty ball on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Look, friends! I found a ball!" His friends looked at the dirty ball and said, "Ew, Tim! That's dirty! You should pray and ask for a clean ball." Tim didn't listen to his friends and played with the dirty ball anyway. He had lots of fun, but he got dirty too. Later, Tim went home and his mom saw him. She said, "Tim, you're so dirty! You should have prayed for a clean ball like your friends said." Tim learned that it's important to listen to his friends and pray for good things. The next time he found a dirty ball, he prayed and found a clean one to play with instead.
