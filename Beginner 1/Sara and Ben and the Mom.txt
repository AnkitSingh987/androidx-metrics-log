Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Mom

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. They have many toys: dolls, cars, blocks, books, and more. But their room is very messy. They never put their toys away. Their mom is not happy. One day, mom says, "Sara and Ben, you have to clean your room. It is too messy. You have to be organized. You have to put your toys in the right places. If you do not, I will take away your toys." Sara and Ben do not want to lose their toys. They say, "OK, mom, we will clean our room." But they do not really want to. They think cleaning is boring. They think playing is fun. They have a plan. They start to clean their room. But they do not really clean. They just move their toys around. They hide some toys under the bed. They put some toys in the closet. They throw some toys in the trash. They think mom will not see. They think they are smart. But mom is smarter. She comes to check their room. She sees the toys under the bed. She sees the toys in the closet. She sees the toys in the trash. She is very angry. She says, "Sara and Ben, you did not clean your room. You made it worse. You lied to me. You are not organized. You are not good. You have to give me your toys. All of them. Now." Sara and Ben are very sad. They cry. They say, "Sorry, mom, we are sorry. We will not do it again. Please, do not take our toys. We love our toys. We will be organized. We will be good. Please, mom, please." Mom says, "No, Sara and Ben, you have to learn your lesson. You have to give me your toys. All of them. Now. You can have them back when you show me you can be organized. You can have them back when you show me you can be good. You can have them back when you show me you can be my staff." Sara and Ben do not know what staff means. They think it is a big word. They think it is a hard word. They think it is a scary word. They do not want to be staff. They want to be kids. They want to play with their toys. They give mom their toys. All of them. They hope mom will change her mind. They hope mom will be nice. They hope mom will love them.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to cook. One day, she wanted to make a sauce. She wanted to make it cheap, so she used what she had at home. Sue asked her mom, "Can I change the sauce? I want it to taste good." Her mom said, "Yes, you can change it. Just use what we have." So Sue mixed and mixed, and the sauce was ready. She tasted it, and it was yummy. She was happy with her cheap sauce. Then, something unexpected happened. A cat jumped on the table and knocked over the sauce! Sue was sad, but her mom said, "It's okay. We can make more sauce together." Sue and her mom made a new sauce. This time, it was even better. They laughed and enjoyed their food. The cat watched from the floor, but it didn't knock over the sauce again.

Jim and his mommy were at the park. Jim saw some other kids playing and he wanted to join them. So, his mommy pointed to the playground. Jim was so excited! He started to run towards the playground and he made it in no time. He found a slide and told his mommy, "Look!" He tried to climb up, but he was too small. His mommy reassured him and said, "Don't worry! Let's find something else to play on." They spotted a swingset and Jim pointed to it. His mommy helped him up and Jim smiled. He had so much fun pushing himself higher and higher. But then his tummy started to hurt and Jim started to cry. His mommy was very sorry and asked, "What's wrong?" Jim told her that he was hungry, so she picked him up and said, "Let's go get lunch. It's time to go home for a break." Jim was still a little sad, but he smiled and agreed to go home.
