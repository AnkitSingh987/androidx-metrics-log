Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Cake

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the garden. One day, they see a big brown cake on the table. It smells good. They want to taste it. They look around. Mom is not here. Dad is not here. They think it is okay to take a bite. They climb on the chairs and reach for the cake. Tim takes a big bite. Sam takes a big bite. They smile. The cake is sweet and soft. But then, they hear a voice. It is Mom. She is coming back. She says, "Who ate my cake? I made it for Grandma's birthday!" Tim and Sam are scared. They know they are in trouble. They hide under the table. They hope Mom will not see them. Mom comes in. She sees the cake. She sees the bites. She sees the crumbs. She is angry. She says, "Where are Tim and Sam? They are naughty boys. They ate my cake. They need to say sorry." She looks around. She sees two feet under the table. She pulls the tablecloth. She sees Tim and Sam. They have brown mouths. They look sad. Mom says, "Tim and Sam, why did you eat my cake? You know you should not do that. You spoiled Grandma's birthday. You need to say sorry to me and to Grandma. And you need to help me make a new cake." Tim and Sam say, "Sorry, Mom. Sorry, Grandma. We were hungry. We wanted to taste the cake. It was yummy. But we were bad. We will help you make a new cake." Mom says, "Thank you for saying sorry. But you still need to learn to ask before you take something. And you need to wait for your snack. Come on, let's go to the kitchen and make a new cake. And then you need to wash your faces. You have brown mouths." Tim and Sam say, "Okay, Mom. We love you. We love Grandma. We love cake." They follow Mom to the kitchen. They hope Grandma will like the new cake. They hope she will not be mad. They hope she will still give them a hug.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and jump all day long. He had a toy whistle that he loved to play with. He would carry the whistle in his mouth and wag his tail. One day, Spot saw a big hill. He wanted to raise the whistle to the top of the hill. So, he started to climb up the hill. It was hard, but Spot was strong and happy. He climbed and climbed until he reached the top. Spot was so excited that he let go of the whistle. The whistle fell down the hill and broke into pieces. Spot was very sad. He looked at the broken whistle and knew he could not play with it anymore. Spot went home with a heavy heart, missing his favorite toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who lived in a tree. She was very anxious and couldn't sit still for even a second. She spent all of her days flying from branch to branch. One day, the little bird was getting very tired. She wanted to take a rest, but she was still feeling so anxious. She saw a nice, strong branch and decided to take a break there. The little bird hopped onto the branch, but then she heard a voice. It was a big, friendly owl! The owl said, "You look a bit anxious, little one. What's wrong?" The little bird replied, "I am so anxious I can't sit still. I just need to take a rest on this branch and get some energy." The owl smiled and said, "Yes, that's a good idea. You can stay on this branch and rest as long as you like." The little bird thanked the owl and felt much better knowing that she could take a nice, peaceful rest on the branch. She fell asleep right away and had the best night's sleep she'd ever had. When she woke up, the little bird felt much more relaxed and happy. She thanked the owl before flying off in search of her next adventure.
