Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Dog

Tom is a dog. He likes to play and run and bark. He lives with Sam and Anna, who are his friends. They give him food and water and hugs. Tom is happy. One day, Tom sees a cat in the yard. The cat is black and white and has a bell on its neck. Tom wants to chase the cat and make it run away. He thinks it is a silly cat. He runs after the cat and barks loudly. The cat is scared of Tom. It runs away from him and climbs a tree. It is very high up in the tree and cannot come down. It meows and shakes. Tom stops barking and looks at the cat. He feels sorry for the cat. He does not want to make it sad. Tom runs to the house and finds Sam and Anna. He barks and wags his tail and tries to tell them about the cat. Sam and Anna follow Tom to the yard. They see the cat in the tree and hear it meow. They get a ladder and a blanket and go to the tree. Sam climbs the ladder and reaches for the cat. He wraps the cat in the blanket and brings it down. Anna holds the cat and pets it. The cat stops shaking and purrs. Tom is happy that Sam and Anna rescue the cat. He sniffs the cat and licks its nose. The cat does not hiss or scratch. It licks Tom's ear. Tom and the cat are friends. Sam and Anna give them some food and water and hugs. They are proud of Tom for being a good dog. Tom is proud of himself too. He thinks he is not a silly dog. He is a brave dog.

One day, Tim saw a big football in the park. He was very happy and cheerful. He wanted to play with it. But Tim did not recognize that the football was not his. He took it and ran to his house. In the house, Tim's mom saw the football. She knew it was not his. She told Tim to take it back to the park. Tim was sad but he knew it was the right thing to do. When Tim went back to the park, he saw a little girl crying. She was looking for her football. Tim gave the football to the girl. She was very happy and said thank you. They played together and became good friends. The girl's football made them both cheerful.

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus was very happy because it loved to help people. Every day, the bus would take people to many places. The bus had a lot of friends who loved it too. One day, the bus saw a little girl who looked very sad. The bus stopped and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I had a terrible day at school. I don't want to go back." The bus wanted to help the little girl. The bus told the girl, "I can take you to a fun park. You will feel better there." The girl smiled and got on the bus. They went to the park and had a great time. The girl was not sad anymore. She said, "Thank you, bus, for making me happy." The bus was happy too because it loved to help people.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Lily. She had a small purse that she loved very much. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, Lily met a boy named Tom. He saw her small purse and said, "What's in your purse, Lily?" Lily smiled and said, "I will show you, Tom." She opened her purse and showed him her favorite toy car. Tom and Lily played together with the toy car. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Lily put her toy car back in her small purse. Tom said, "Bye, Lily! I hope we can play again soon." Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Tom! I would like that very much."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her delicate doll. One day, when she was playing, her doll vanished. She looked everywhere, but could not find it. Lily was very sad. Lily's mommy told her to look near the stair. Lily went to the stair and saw a tiny door. She opened the door and found a secret room. In the room, she saw many toys that had vanished from other kids. Lily was surprised but happy to find her delicate doll. Lily decided to return the toys to the other kids. She knocked on their doors and gave them back their toys. They were all very happy. The moral of the story is that when you help others, good things can happen to you too.
