Category: Beginner
Title: The in Sea and the Fish

Once upon a time, a little shrimp lived in the sea. This shrimp was very humble. He had many friends. One day, he saw a big mark on a rock. The mark was red and round. The shrimp was curious about the mark. The shrimp asked his friends about the mark. They did not know what it was. They all wanted to find out. They swam closer to the mark. They did not see that a big fish was watching them from behind a rock. The big fish saw the shrimp and his friends near the mark. The fish was hungry. He swam very fast towards the shrimp. The shrimp and his friends did not see the fish coming. The big fish ate the shrimp and his friends. The mark was a trap, and the shrimp and his friends did not know.

Jack and his dad went for a walk. They were out in the sunshine, enjoying the bright day. Suddenly, Jack heard a noise. "What is that?" he asked. His dad looked around and told him, "It's an old oil can. Let's go and see what's inside". Jack and his dad opened the can and saw some slippery oil. Jack tried to pick it up, but it was too slippery. "It's oily," his dad said. They put the lid back on the oil can and his dad said, "Let's put it back where we found it and lock it. We don't want animals getting in." Jack looked at the can and thought it looked kind of dry, but his dad said it was still locked. That night Jack dreamed about the old oil can. In his dream strange creatures came out of the can and crept around his house. It was a scary dream! The next day, Jack and his dad went back to the can. When they arrived, they saw that the can was unlocked and the oil was gone. There was no sign of the strange creatures from Jack's dream. Jack and his dad never figured out what happened to the oil. They were sad, especially Jack, who never wanted to have scary dreams like that again.

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play in their room. They had many toys, but they did not like to share them. They always fought over who got to play with what. One day, their mom told them to clean up their room before dinner. She said they had to put all their toys in the big box. Lily and Tom did not want to clean up. They wanted to keep playing. They started to argue about who had to do more work. "I cleaned up the dolls yesterday, so you have to do it today!" Lily said. "No, you didn't! You just threw them in the box. I cleaned up the cars and the blocks. You have to do the dolls and the books!" Tom said. They did not listen to each other. They did not discuss how to solve their problem. They just yelled and pulled and pushed. They made a bigger mess. Their mom heard the noise and came to their room. She saw the toys all over the floor and the bed and the shelves. She saw Lily and Tom fighting and crying. She was not happy. "What are you doing?" she asked. "I told you to clean up your room, not to make it worse. Why are you being so selfish?" Lily and Tom stopped fighting. They looked at their mom. They felt ashamed. They knew they had been bad. "We're sorry, mom," they said. "We didn't want to clean up. We wanted to play with our toys." Their mom sighed. She said, "I know you like your toys, but you have to learn to share them and to take care of them. If you don't, you will lose them. And you will lose each other. Do you want that?" Lily and Tom shook their heads. They did not want to lose their toys or their twin. They loved their toys and their twin. "Then you have to work together and help each other. You have to discuss how to divide the tasks and how to cooperate. You have to be kind and fair. You have to be good twins," their mom said. Lily and Tom nodded. They said, "We will, mom. We will be good twins. We will clean up our room. We will share our toys. We will discuss and not fight." Their mom smiled. She said, "That's good. I'm proud of you. Now, hurry up and finish your work. Then you can have dinner and dessert. But remember, no more playing until your room is tidy." Lily and Tom said, "Okay, mom. Thank you, mom." They hugged their mom and then each other. They felt happy. They went to the big box and started to clean up their room. They discussed how to do it. They shared their toys. They did not fight. They were good twins.
