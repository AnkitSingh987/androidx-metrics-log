Category: Beginner
Title: Max and Kitty and the Dog

One day, a big dog named Max and a little cat named Kitty were playing in the park. They saw a deep hole in the ground. Max said, "Let's see who can jump over the hole the quickest!" Kitty agreed, and they both got ready to jump. Max jumped first and made it across the hole very quick. Now it was Kitty's turn. Kitty was scared but wanted to display her bravery. "I can do it too!" she said. Max cheered her on, and Kitty tried her best to jump. But Kitty was too small, and she fell into the deep hole. Max tried to help her, but he couldn't reach her. They both cried and called for help, but no one came. In the end, Kitty was stuck in the hole, and Max was sad that he couldn't save his friend.

Anna loved to help her mom in the garden. She liked to dig in the dirt, plant seeds, and water the flowers. She also liked to watch the bees and butterflies that came to visit the garden. One day, Anna saw a little bird on the fence. It was brown and had a red chest. It looked sad and scared. Anna wanted to be its friend. She said, "Hello, bird. Do you want to play with me? I have some seeds for you. They are sweet and yummy." The bird did not move. It was too shy. Anna's mom saw her talking to the bird. She smiled and said, "That's a robin, Anna. It's a very nice bird, but it's not used to people. You have to be gentle and patient with it. Maybe you can encourage it to come closer by singing a song." Anna nodded. She loved to sing. She sang a song that she learned at school. It was about the sun and the rain and the rainbow. She sang softly and sweetly. She held out her hand with some seeds. The bird listened to Anna's song. It liked her voice. It saw the seeds in her hand. It smelled the flowers in the garden. It felt curious and hungry. It hopped off the fence and flew to Anna's hand. It pecked at the seeds and chirped happily. Anna was so happy. She made a new friend. She said, "Thank you, mom, for helping me. Thank you, bird, for trusting me. You are a very brave and beautiful bird. I hope you come back to visit me and my garden."

One day, a poor cat named Tom was very sad. He wanted to drive a car like his friend, Ben the dog. Tom asked Ben, "Can I drive your car, please?" Ben said, "No, Tom. You are too small to drive a car." Tom was very sad and walked away. He saw a shelf with a small toy car on it. He thought, "Maybe I can drive this toy car." Tom climbed up the shelf and tried to drive the toy car. But the toy car was too small for Tom to drive, too. Tom fell off the shelf and broke the toy car. Ben saw this and said, "I told you, Tom, you are too small to drive a car." Tom was very sad and felt even more poor. He could not drive a car, and now he broke a toy car, too.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn on a farm. Inside the barn, there were many animals. There were cows, pigs, and chickens. They all lived happily together. One day, a fierce wind blew outside. The wind made the trees dance and the flowers bloom. The animals were scared, but they stayed safe inside the barn. The next day, the sun shone brightly, and the wind was gone. The animals went outside to play. They saw the pretty flowers that bloomed. They were happy and played all day long. The end.

One day, a girl named Sue went to a cold cabin in the woods with her mom and dad. They wanted to have fun and play in the snow. Sue put on her warm coat, hat, and boots. She went outside to play. While Sue was playing, she saw a big bear. The bear looked cold and sad. Sue went closer to the bear and asked, "Why are you sad, bear?" The bear said, "I am cold and I lost my warm cave." Sue wanted to help the bear, so she had an idea. Sue took the bear back to the cold cabin. She asked her mom and dad if the bear could stay with them. They were scared at first, but then they saw the bear was nice. They let the bear come in and shake off the snow. The bear was happy and warm in the cabin. Sue and her family had a new friend.
