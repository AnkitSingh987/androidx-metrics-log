Category: Beginner
Title: John and the Hat

John had a piece of paper that he was very upset about. He was holding it tight and crying. He was very sad. His mom asked him what was wrong. He said, "I can't seem to get the paper to do what I want." His mom smiled, knelt next to him and said, "Let me try. What do you want it to do?" John said, "I want it to turn into a hat." His mom said, "That's not something that paper usually does. But I can help. I can show you how to fold it into the shape of a hat!" John looked excited. He tried to follow the instructions his mom gave him. He folded it carefully, and sure enough, it started looking like a hat. He smiled and wore it proudly. He was no longer upset.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play with her toy car. One day, Sue was playing in her room when she heard a loud "bang!" She looked around and saw that her toy car had broken into two parts. "Oh no!" Sue cried. "My toy car is broken!" She picked up the two parts and tried to put them back together, but they would not stick. Sue felt sad and did not know what to do. Sue's mom heard her crying and came to see what was wrong. "What happened, Sue?" her mom asked. Sue showed her the broken toy car and said, "It had a big fall and now it's in two parts." "Don't worry," her mom said. "We can fix it together." Sue's mom got some glue and helped Sue put the parts back together. They waited for the glue to dry and soon, the toy car was as good as new. Sue was so happy and felt successful because she fixed her car with her mom's help. They played with the toy car all day, and Sue knew that even when things fall apart, they can be fixed with love and teamwork.

Tom and Lily went to the big water with Mom and Dad. They saw many fish and boats and birds. They were happy and excited. They wanted to see a whale, a big, big fish that can make loud sounds. They got on a boat with other people and a man who knew about whales. The man said, "Look, there is a whale! Do you see the water coming out of its head? That is how it breathes. And do you hear the noise it makes? That is how it talks to other whales." Tom and Lily saw the whale and clapped their hands. They tried to make the same noise as the whale, but they could not. They laughed and said, "Hello, whale! We are Tom and Lily. We like you!" The whale heard them and came closer to the boat. It looked at them with its big eye and made a friendly sound. Tom and Lily were amazed and happy. They wanted to touch the whale, but Mom and Dad said, "No, you can't. The whale is too big and too strong. You have to stay on the boat and be careful." But Tom did not listen. He wanted to be friends with the whale. He leaned over the edge of the boat and reached out his hand. He felt the whale's skin. It was wet and smooth and warm. He smiled and said, "Hi, whale! You are nice!" But then the whale moved and splashed the water. The water was cold and salty and wet. It hit Tom's face and made him fall. He lost his balance and fell into the water. He was scared and cold and wet. He cried and cried. He could not see the boat or Mom or Dad or Lily. He only saw the water and the whale. Lily saw Tom fall and screamed. She was scared and sad for Tom. She cried and cried. She wanted Tom to be safe and dry and happy. She said, "Tom, Tom, where are you? I love you, Tom!" Mom and Dad heard Lily scream and saw Tom fall. They were scared and worried for Tom. They cried and cried. They wanted Tom to be back on the boat and in their arms. They said, "Tom, Tom, hold on! We are coming, Tom!" The man who knew about whales saw what happened and called for help. He said, "Don't worry, we will save Tom. The whale will not hurt him. The whale is gentle and smart. He knows Tom is a child and needs help." The whale heard Tom cry and felt sorry for him. He knew Tom was not a fish and could not breathe in the water. He knew Tom was in trouble and needed help. He wanted to be friends with Tom and make him happy. He lifted Tom with his big fin and brought him to the surface. He held Tom close and tight and made a soft sound. He said, "Don't cry, Tom. I am here. I am your friend. I will keep you safe." Tom felt the whale's fin and heard his sound. He was still scared and cold and wet, but he was also glad and thankful. He knew the whale was helping him and not hurting him. He knew the whale was his friend and not his enemy. He stopped crying and hugged the whale. He said, "Thank you, whale. You are nice. You are my friend. I am sorry I was silly. I will not do it again." The boat came near and Mom and Dad and Lily and the man who knew about whales reached out their hands. They pulled Tom back on the boat and wrapped him in a blanket. They hugged him and kissed him and said, "We are so happy you are safe, Tom. We love you, Tom. You are brave, Tom." Tom smiled and said, "I love you, too. I am sorry I scared you. I was brave, but I was also silly. I learned my lesson. I will not lean over the boat again. I will stay with you and listen to you." The whale saw Tom on the boat and was happy for him. He was happy he could help him and be his friend. He made a loud sound and waved his tail. He said, "Goodbye, Tom. Goodbye, Lily. Goodbye, Mom and Dad. Goodbye, man who knows about whales. Thank you for visiting me. Come back soon. I will miss you." Tom and Lily and Mom and Dad and the man who knew about whales waved back and said, "Goodbye, whale. Thank you for saving Tom. Thank you for being his friend. You are amazing. We will never forget you. We will come back soon. We will miss you, too." The end.
