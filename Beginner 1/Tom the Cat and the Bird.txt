Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Bird

One day, a big cat named Tom was very tired. He had a lot of work to do. The work was not easy. Tom had to clean his house, wash his clothes, and find food to eat. He yawned because he was so sleepy. Tom went to his friend, a small bird named Sue. He said, "Sue, can you help me with my work? I am very tired and I yawn a lot." Sue looked at Tom and said, "Yes, I can help you. We can make the work easy if we do it together." Tom and Sue worked together to clean the house, wash the clothes, and find food. They talked and laughed while they worked. The work became easy and fun. At the end of the day, Tom was not tired anymore. He said, "Thank you, Sue, for helping me. You made the work easy and fun." Sue smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom. Friends help each other."

It was a cold day and Sam wanted to play outside. He put on his coat, his hat and his mittens. He opened the door and saw the snow. He smiled and ran to the yard. He saw a big pile of snow and wanted to make a snowman. He rolled a big ball of snow and then a smaller one. He put the smaller ball on top of the big one. He looked for a carrot, a scarf and some buttons to make the snowman's face and clothes. He found them in his mom's basket. He was about to put the carrot on the snowman's nose when he heard a meow. He turned around and saw his cat, Fluffy, sitting on the fence. Fluffy looked at Sam and the snowman. He jumped down and ran to the snowman. He wanted to play with the carrot. "Fluffy, no!" Sam shouted. "That's the snowman's nose, not your toy!" But Fluffy did not listen. He grabbed the carrot with his teeth and ran away. Sam chased him, but Fluffy was too fast. He ran to the street and hid under a car. Sam was sad and angry. He wanted his carrot back. He wanted to finish his snowman. He looked at the car and saw Fluffy's tail sticking out. He walked to the car and tried to reach Fluffy. "Fluffy, come out!" Sam said. "Give me back my carrot!" But Fluffy did not move. He was scared of the car and the noise. He held the carrot tight and curled up under the car. Sam did not know what to do. He wanted to get Fluffy out, but he did not want to scare him more. He thought for a moment. He remembered what his mom told him when Fluffy was hiding under the bed. He took off one of his mittens and held it in his hand. He waved it in front of the car and made a soft sound. "Fluffy, look!" Sam said. "I have a mitten for you. It's soft and warm and fun. Do you want to play with it?" Fluffy heard Sam's voice and saw the mitten. He was curious and hungry. He let go of the carrot and peeked out from under the car. He saw Sam smiling and holding the mitten. He came out slowly and sniffed the mitten. He liked it. He pawed at it and bit it. He purred and rubbed his head against Sam's hand. Sam was happy. He picked up Fluffy and hugged him. He took the carrot from the ground and put it back in his pocket. He put his mitten back on his hand. He walked back to the yard with Fluffy in his arms. He finished his snowman and gave him a scarf and some buttons. He put the carrot on his nose and smiled. He looked at Fluffy and said, "Fluffy, you are a good cat. I'm sorry I yelled at you. Do you like the snowman?" Fluffy looked at the snowman and meowed. He did not care about the snowman. He cared about Sam. He licked Sam's face and snuggled in his coat. Sam laughed and hugged Fluffy. He felt warm and calm. He said, "I love you, Fluffy. You are my best friend."
