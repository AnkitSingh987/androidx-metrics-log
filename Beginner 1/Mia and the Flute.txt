Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and the Flute

Mia loved to play her flute. She made pretty sounds with it and danced around the room. She liked to show her flute to her friends and teach them how to play. But her brother Ben did not like her flute. He thought it was noisy and annoying. He wanted to play with his cars and trucks instead. One day, Mia was playing her flute in the living room. She was happy and smiling. Ben was playing with his cars and trucks on the floor. He was grumpy and frowning. He did not like the sound of Mia's flute. He wished she would stop. "Stop playing your flute!" he shouted at Mia. "You are making too much noise!" "No, I won't stop!" Mia said. "I like playing my flute. It is fun and beautiful!" Ben got angry. He got up and ran towards Mia. He wanted to take her flute away. He reached out his hand and tried to grab it. But Mia was quick. She moved away and held her flute tightly. "Leave me alone!" she said. "This is my flute. You can't have it!" Ben did not listen. He chased Mia around the room. He tried to catch her and take her flute. Mia ran and dodged. She tried to keep her flute safe. But Ben was faster. He caught up with Mia and pushed her hard. Mia fell down and dropped her flute. Her flute hit the floor and broke. It made a loud and ugly sound. Mia cried. She looked at her broken flute. She felt miserable. She loved her flute. It was her favorite thing. Now it was ruined. She could not play it anymore. Ben looked at what he had done. He saw Mia's tears and heard her sobs. He saw the pieces of her flute. He felt sorry. He did not mean to break her flute. He just wanted her to stop playing. He did not know it would break so easily. "I'm sorry, Mia," he said. "I didn't mean to push you. I didn't mean to break your flute. Please don't cry." Mia did not answer. She did not look at him. She hugged her flute and cried. She was very sad. She did not want to talk to Ben. She did not want to play with him. She did not want to forgive him.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with his toy car. One day, he played near a big drain. He was very alert as he knew he should be careful. While playing, Tim made his toy car twist and turn. He laughed and clapped his hands. But then, the toy car twisted too much and fell into the drain. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim's big sister, Sue, saw him crying. She came to help him. Sue found a long stick and used it to get the toy car out of the drain. Tim was so happy and hugged his sister. They played together for the rest of the day, and Tim learned to be more careful near the drain.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy went to visit her grandma. Grandma lived in a small house near a row of pretty trees. Lucy loved to play with her grandma and listen to her stories. One day, Lucy was very impatient. She wanted to visit her grandma right away. She asked her mom, "Can we go now?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's go!" They walked to grandma's house, holding hands and smiling. When they arrived at grandma's house, Lucy saw the row of trees. She ran to them and played hide and seek with her mom. After playing, they went inside to see grandma. Grandma gave Lucy a big hug and they all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Sam. Sam was a successful fireman. He liked to help people and put out fires. One day, Sam was driving his big, red fire truck when he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her cat, Kitty, was stuck in a tree. Sam stopped his fire truck and asked Lily, "What's wrong?" Lily looked up at Sam with tears in her eyes and said, "Please, can you help my Kitty? She's stuck in the tree and can't come down." Sam smiled and said, "Don't worry, Lily. I'll help your Kitty." He put on his fireman hat and climbed the tall ladder on his fire truck. Sam reached Kitty and carefully brought her down to the ground. Lily hugged her Kitty and said, "Thank you, Sam! You're the best fireman ever!"
