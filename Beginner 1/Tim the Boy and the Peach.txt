Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Peach

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a smooth peach on the ground. Tim picked up the peach and said, "Wow, this is a nice peach!" Tim saw his friend, Sue, and called her, "Hey Sue, come look at this peach!" Sue came and looked at the peach. She said, "That's a very smooth peach!" Tim wanted to play, so he put the peach on the ground. He kicked the peach, and it rolled away. Sue laughed and kicked the peach too. They took turns kicking the peach and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Tim and Sue said goodbye. Tim took the peach home and shared it with his family. They all enjoyed the smooth peach and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little penguin named Pippin. Pippin lived in a comfortable, warm house with his family. He loved to play with his friends and have fun. One day, Pippin and his friends were playing near the ice. Pippin was running very fast, and he did not see a big rock in front of him. He stumbled and fell down. It hurt, and he was sad. His friends helped him up and made sure he was okay. Pippin learned that it is important to look where you are going and be careful. He knew that his friends would always be there to help him when he needed it. And from that day on, Pippin always looked where he was going, and he never stumbled again.

Once upon a time, in a sweet little town, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy loved to have fun and play with her friends. One day, she wanted to find a new game to play. Amy asked her friend Tom to recommend a game. Tom said, "Let's play hide and seek. It is so much fun!" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, that is a great idea!" They played hide and seek all day long. Amy and Tom laughed and had lots of fun together. They found sweet spots to hide and counted to ten. At the end of the day, they were happy and tired. They knew they would play this fun game again soon.

One day, a small cat named Tom found a triangle toy. He liked to play with it. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max was eating something disgusting. Tom went to Max and said, "Yuck! Don't eat that, Max!" Max stopped eating and looked at Tom. He saw the triangle toy in Tom's paw. Max wanted to play with it too. They played together and had lots of fun. Then, a big wind came and blew the triangle toy away. Tom and Max were sad. A bird named Sally saw them and flew to get the toy. She brought it back to them. Tom and Max were so happy. They said, "Thank you, Sally!" and bowed to her. Sally smiled and flew away. Tom and Max learned to share and be thankful for their friends.

One day, a hairy dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot liked to gaze at the sky and the birds. He saw a little bird in a tree and said, "Hi, bird! Can you play with me?" The bird looked at Spot and said, "I am busy, Spot. I need to find a razor to cut some leaves for my nest." Spot wanted to help his friend. He went to look for a razor. He found one under a big rock. Spot carried the razor to the bird. "Thank you, Spot!" said the bird. "Now I can cut the leaves for my nest." Spot and the bird were happy. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved gum. He had a big bag of gum. Tim was very happy. One day, Tim saw a rich man. The rich man had a big box of gum. Tim wanted the big box of gum. Tim was sad. He did not know how to get the big box of gum. Tim had an idea. He went to the rich man and asked if he could have some gum. The rich man said yes. Tim realized he did not need the big box of gum. Tim was happy again. He made a new friend and they shared gum.
