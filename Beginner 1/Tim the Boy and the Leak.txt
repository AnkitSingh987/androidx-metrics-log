Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Leak

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a scary dream last night. He dreamt about a big monster that tried to wrap him up. Tim was very scared. One day, Tim saw a leak in his room. He called his mom and said, "Mom, there is a leak in my room!" His mom came to look at the leak. She said, "Oh no! We need to fix it before it gets bigger." Tim and his mom went to the store to buy tape. They wanted to use the tape to cover the leak. But when they came back, the leak was bigger and scarier. The water was all over the floor. Tim's mom tried to wrap the tape around the leak, but it didn't work. The water kept coming out. Tim said, "This is just like my scary dream!" The leak got bigger and bigger, and the water filled the room. Tim and his mom could not fix the leak, and the room was full of water. They were very sad and scared.

One day, a boy named Tim found a thick envelope on the floor. He wanted to know what was inside, so he tried to open it. But it was too hard for him. He pulled and pulled, but the envelope would not open. Tim saw a big slide in the park. He had an idea. He took the envelope to the slide and climbed up. He thought the slide could help him open the envelope. He put the envelope on the slide and let it go. The envelope slid down very fast and hit a tree at the bottom. When the envelope hit the tree, it opened! Inside, Tim found a map. The map showed a secret path in the park. Tim followed the path and found a big box of toys. He was so happy! He played with the toys all day and shared them with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little sailor named Tim. Tim loved to sail on his boat in the big blue sea. One day, Tim saw a big island far away. He wanted to go there and see what was on the island. Tim's mom and dad encouraged him to sail to the island. They knew he was a good sailor. So, Tim got ready for his big trip. He packed food, water, and a map to help him find his way. As Tim sailed to the island, he saw many fish and birds. He was very happy. When he got to the island, he found a big treasure chest. Tim was so excited! He opened the chest and found gold and jewels. He took the treasure back to his mom and dad, and they were all very happy.

Ben and Mia are twins. They like to play with cars and dolls. But today they have to go to grandma's house. Mom says they have to be ready soon. "Ben, Mia, time to take a shower!" Mom calls from the bathroom. "No, no, no!" Ben says. He wants to speed his car on the floor. "I don't want to take a shower!" "Mia, come on, let's go!" Mom says. She picks up Mia and carries her to the bathroom. "OK, Mommy, I want to take a shower!" Mia says. She likes to splash water and soap bubbles. She puts on her pink towel and smiles. "Good girl, Mia!" Mom says. She dries Mia's hair and puts on her dress. "Now you are ready to go!" "Ben, where are you?" Mom says. She looks for Ben in the living room. She sees him hiding under the sofa with his car. "Ben, come out, please!" Mom says. "You have to take a shower, too!" "No, no, no!" Ben says. He does not want to leave his car. He thinks showers are boring and cold. "Ben, if you don't take a shower, you can't go to grandma's house!" Mom says. "And you know what? Grandma has a big surprise for you!" "A surprise?" Ben says. He likes surprises. He wonders what it is. "What is it, Mommy?" "I can't tell you, Ben. You have to go and see for yourself!" Mom says. She smiles and holds out her hand. "But first, you have to take a shower!" "OK, OK, I will take a shower!" Ben says. He puts down his car and takes Mom's hand. He hopes the surprise is something fun. Maybe a new toy, or a cake, or a puppy! "Good boy, Ben!" Mom says. She takes him to the bathroom and turns on the water. "Now you will be ready to go, too!"
