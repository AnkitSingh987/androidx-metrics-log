Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Mia and the Story

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Mia. She loved to write stories and draw pictures. One day, Mia found a flute in her garden. She picked it up and blew into it. The sweet sound came out of the flute. Mia was so excited she ran to get a piece of paper and a pen. She wanted to write a story about the flute and all the music it could make. Mia wrote and wrote until she had filled the thick paper. At dinner that night, Mia showed her story to her mom. "I wrote all about the flute!" Mia said proudly. "That's wonderful!" her mom said. "Maybe you can play the flute so we can hear the music you wrote about!" And so Mia learnt to play the flute, and she played it all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and eat. One day, he saw a little flea. The flea jumped on Tom's back. Tom got an idea. Tom went to his friend, the dog named Max. He asked Max, "Will you help me with the flea?" Max nodded his head. They made a plan to get the flea off Tom's back. Tom and Max played together, running and jumping. The flea held on tight. Then, Max gave Tom a big shake. The flea flew off Tom's back and landed in a puddle. Tom and Max smiled, happy that their plan worked.

---

One day, a little girl named Amy was very excited. She was going to the sea with her mom and dad. The sea was big and blue. Amy loved to play in the water and build sand castles. When they got to the sea, Amy ran to the water. She saw a little fish and said, "Hello, fish! I am Amy." The fish said, "Hi, Amy! I am Fin. Let's play!" They played and splashed in the water for a long time. Then, Amy saw a big wave coming. She started to panic. "Fin, I am scared!" she said. Fin told her, "Don't worry, Amy. Just hold my fin and we will be safe." Amy held Fin's fin and they rode the wave together. They were not scared anymore. They had a fun day at the sea.

---

Tom and his aunt went to the park. Tom liked to play on the swings and the slide. His aunt liked to sit on the bench and read a book. She told Tom to be careful and not go too far. Tom saw a big metal thing with a wheel. He wanted to try it. He ran to it and climbed on. He twisted the wheel and the metal thing started to spin. Tom felt happy and excited. He twisted the wheel faster and faster. His aunt looked up from her book and saw Tom on the metal thing. She felt nervous and scared. She ran to him and shouted, "Tom, stop! That is not a toy! You can get hurt!" But Tom did not hear her. He was too busy twisting the wheel. The metal thing spun so fast that Tom could not hold on. He flew off and landed on the grass. He felt dizzy and sore. He started to cry. His aunt reached him and hugged him. She checked if he was okay. She was angry and sad. She said, "Tom, why did you do that? You did not listen to me. You could have broken your bones. You need to be more careful and obey the rules." Tom felt sorry and ashamed. He said, "I'm sorry, aunt. I just wanted to have fun. I did not know it was dangerous. I will not do it again." His aunt kissed him and said, "I'm glad you are okay. I love you, Tom. But you need to learn from your mistake. Fun is good, but safety is better. Next time, listen to me and play with the toys that are meant for you." Tom nodded and said, "Okay, aunt. I love you too. Can we go back to the swings and the slide?" His aunt smiled and said, "Yes, we can. But only if you promise to be careful and not go too far." Tom promised and they went back to the swings and the slide. Tom had fun, but he also remembered to be safe. He learned his lesson.
