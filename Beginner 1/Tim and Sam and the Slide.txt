Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Slide

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Let's jump on the slide!" Tim says. "OK!" Sam says. They run to the slide. They jump on the slide. They slide down fast. They laugh and have fun. But then they see a cop. The cop is not happy. He has a loud voice. "Stop! You are not allowed to jump on the slide. It is not safe. You can get hurt. You have to wait your turn." The cop says. Tim and Sam are scared. They do not want to get in trouble. They say sorry to the cop. "We are sorry, cop. We did not know. We will not jump on the slide again. We will be patient and wait our turn." They say. The cop nods. He is not angry anymore. He is kind. "Good. I am glad you understand. The slide is for everyone to enjoy. But you have to follow the rules. Be careful and have fun." He says. He walks away. Tim and Sam look at each other. They smile. They are still friends. They go to the swing. They wait for their turn. They are patient. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a tall boy called Tim. He lived in a small house with his mum and dad. One day, Tim's mum said "Tim, you have a test tomorrow and you need to study for it. You have to work hard and do your best". But Tim didn't want to do any work. He insisted that he didn't have to do the test and that he would rather play with his toys. His mum said "No, Tim. You must do the test, it's very important". But Tim just kept insisting that he didn't want to do the test. He said "I don't wanna do it! I don't wanna do it!" His mum was getting frustrated, but then she had an idea. She said "If you do the test, I'll buy you a new toy". Tim smiled. He said "Ok, I'll do the test!" And so, Tim studied hard and did the test. He did very well and was rewarded with a new toy. The end.

Once there was a little boy named Tommy who was very eager. He wanted to explore his house, so he set off. He stumbled through the kitchen, and saw something strange. It was a bottle of wine! Tommy didn't know what it was but he was eager to get it. He stuck his hand in and was very surprised when he felt the glass. He looked at the bottle and even though he couldn't read the label, he knew it was something special. He tried to pick it up, but the bottle was too big. He stumbled around the kitchen some more and found a stool. After climbing up, he could finally reach the bottle. He pulled it off the shelf and felt the weight of it in his hands. He ran excitedly out of the kitchen and brought it to his mom. She was so surprised! Tommy couldn't understand why his mom was so surprised, but he was very eager to find out. His mom explained that the wine was a special drink that grown-ups could enjoy. Tommy was confused and wanted to try it, but he was too young. And so, Tommy put the bottle back where he found it, a little wiser and a little more eager to learn more.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Jack. He was three and he loved to kick things - he'd kick his soccer ball and even his little sister's dolls! One day Jack's mom told him he had to wear an apron. He wasn't too happy about it but he put it on anyway. Jack laughed at himself in the mirror - he looked funny. He decided to have some fun, so he started kicking with the apron on. He kicked his soccer ball, he kicked the air and he kicked his mom's chair. Jack had a lot of fun with his new apron. He kicked until he wore it out!
