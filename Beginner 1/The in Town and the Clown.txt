Category: Beginner
Title: The in Town and the Clown

Once upon a time, a normal clown lived in a little town. He liked to make kids laugh and play. One day, he saw a girl crying. The clown wanted to help her. He said, "Why are you sad? Let me make you laugh." The girl looked at him and said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" The clown nodded and they began to look for the toy. They took many steps, looking high and low. But they could not find the toy. The girl started to cry again. The clown tried to make her laugh, but she was too sad. They never found the toy, and the girl stayed sad. The clown felt bad and went back to his little house.

One day, a little girl named Olive went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a soft spot under it. Olive felt tired, so she decided to take a nap under the tree. She closed her eyes and started to sleep. While Olive was napping, a small unknown animal came by. It saw Olive and wanted to be her friend. The animal said, "Hi, I want to play!" Olive woke up and saw the animal. She was surprised but happy to have a new friend. Olive and the unknown animal played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. When the sun went down, it was time for Olive to go home. She said bye to her new friend and walked home with a big smile on her face. The unknown animal was happy too, because it had made a new friend in Olive.

Sara liked to help her mom with the laundry. She liked to sort the clothes by color and size. She liked to add soap and water to the machine. She liked to watch the clothes spin and splash. One day, Sara saw a delicate sock in the laundry basket. It was pink and had lace and flowers. It was her mom's sock. Sara thought it was very pretty. She wanted to wear it on her foot. She took the sock and put it on. But the sock was too big for Sara. It slipped off her foot and fell on the floor. Sara did not see it. She ran to the laundry room to add more clothes to the machine. She did not know that the sock was on the floor. The sock was on the floor when the dog came in. The dog liked to chew on things. He saw the sock and thought it was a toy. He grabbed the sock and ran away. He bit the sock and tore the lace and flowers. When Sara's mom came home, she looked for her delicate sock. She could not find it in the laundry basket or the machine. She asked Sara if she had seen it. Sara said no. She did not remember the sock. Then they heard the dog barking. They went to see what he was doing. They saw the dog with the sock in his mouth. The sock was ripped and dirty. Sara's mom was sad. She liked her delicate sock. Sara was sorry. She did not mean to lose the sock. She hugged her mom and said she was sorry. She said she would help her mom find a new sock. Sara's mom smiled and said she loved her. She said it was okay. She said they could go to the store and buy a new sock. Sara was happy. She liked to go to the store with her mom. She hoped they would find a pretty sock.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a cold bench near the trees. He wanted to sit on it, but he was scared because it was cold. So, Tim put on his warm coat and sat on the bench. He felt happy and warm. While sitting, Tim saw a cat. The cat was playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play too. He got off the bench and went to the cat. They played together for a while. Tim threw the ball and the cat ran to catch it. They had so much fun. Then, the cat paused and looked at the bench. Tim looked too. He saw his mom coming to sit on the cold bench. Tim and the cat went back to the bench. They all sat together and enjoyed the day at the park.
