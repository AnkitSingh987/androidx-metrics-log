Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Jolly Boy and the Car

One day, a jolly boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a big red ball to play with. Tim loved to throw and catch the ball with his mom. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a fun day. Then, Tim's mom mentioned that they needed to buy a battery for Tim's toy car. "We need a battery for your toy car, Tim," she said. Tim was excited because he loved to play with his toy car. They walked to the store and found the battery. At home, Tim's mom put the battery in the toy car. Tim was so happy. He played with his toy car all day long. It went fast and made Tim laugh. It was a jolly day for Tim and his mom. They had lots of fun together.

Lily loves her kitten, Spot. Spot is small and soft and has black spots on his white fur. Lily likes to hug Spot and play with him. She gives him milk and fish and a ball of yarn. One day, Lily and Spot go to the park with Mom. There are many other children and animals there. Lily sees a big dog with brown fur and a loud bark. She is scared of the dog. She holds Spot tight and runs to Mom. "Mom, Mom, can we go home? I don't like the big dog. He is scary and noisy. He might hurt Spot," Lily says. Mom smiles and hugs Lily and Spot. "Don't worry, sweetie. The big dog is just playing. He won't hurt Spot. He is friendly and gentle. He just wants to say hello. Why don't you try to pet him and see?" Lily looks at the big dog. He is wagging his tail and licking his lips. He does not look mean, but he is still big and loud. Lily is not sure. She shrugs and says, "Maybe later, Mom. I like Spot better. He is small and quiet and fit. He is my best friend." Mom nods and says, "OK, Lily. You can play with Spot. But maybe someday you will make friends with the big dog too. He is not so bad. He is just different." Lily and Spot go to the swings and have fun. They are happy and safe. They love each other very much.

Once upon a time, there was a modern motorcycle. It was shiny and fast. It loved to ride around the town. One day, the motorcycle saw a little boy. The boy was sad and began to cry. The motorcycle said, "Why do you cry, little boy?" The little boy looked at the motorcycle and said, "I lost my toy. I can't find it anywhere." The kind motorcycle wanted to help. "Don't cry, little boy. I will help you find your toy," it said. They looked around the town together. The little boy sat on the motorcycle as they rode. Finally, they found the toy under a big tree. The little boy was so happy, he stopped crying. He hugged the motorcycle and said, "Thank you, motorcycle!" The motorcycle was happy too. From that day on, the little boy and the motorcycle were best friends. They played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing. The cat was very smelly. The dog did not like the smelly cat. The cat said, "I think I found some ink." The dog said, "What is ink?" The cat showed the dog the ink. The ink was black and wet. The dog said, "Let's play with the ink!" The cat and the dog played with the ink. They made a big mess. Soon, the cat and the dog were very dirty. They were covered in ink. The cat said, "I think we should clean up." But the dog did not want to clean. The cat and the dog got into a fight. In the end, they were not friends anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a humble girl named Lily. She had long, pretty hair. Lily was nice and loved to play with her friends. One day, Lily saw a big tree. She began to wonder what was on top of the tree. She wanted to climb, but she was scared. Her friends said, "You can do it, Lily!" Lily was brave and started to climb. Her hair got stuck in the tree, but she did not give up. She kept going up and up. At last, she reached the top and saw a beautiful view. Lily was happy she climbed the tree. She thanked her friends for helping her. They all played together and had a fun day. And Lily was still the humble girl with the pretty hair.
