Category: Beginner
Title: The Bald Man and the Box

One day, a bald man went for a walk. He liked to wander in the park. He saw a little boy looking sad. The bald man asked the boy, "Why are you sad?" The little boy said, "I lost my toy." The bald man wanted to help the boy. They looked for the toy together. They looked under a big tree and found a box. The box was not the boy's toy, but they opened it anyway. Inside the box, they found many toys! The boy was happy and said, "Thank you for helping me!" The bald man was happy too. They gave the toys to other kids in the park. The moral of the story is: when you help others, good things can happen.

Once upon a time, there were two twin bunnies, named Ben and Jen. They lived in a white house in the woods. They loved to play and hop around all day. One day, Ben and Jen found a broken toy car. Ben said, "Let's fix it!" Jen nodded and said, "Yes, let's fix it together!" They worked very hard to make the toy car like new again. When the toy car was fixed, the twins painted it white, just like their house. They played with the car all day, pushing it around and laughing. They were so happy that they could fix the car and play with it together.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant named Andy. Andy was a regular ant, but he had big dreams. He wanted to climb to the top of the tallest tree. Andy thought it would be a fun goal. One sunny day, Andy started to climb the tree. He met a wise old bird named Betty. Betty asked, "Why do you want to climb so high, Andy?" Andy replied, "I want to reach my goal and see the world from the top of the tree." Betty smiled and said, "That's a good goal, but remember, it's not just about reaching the top. It's about the friends you make and the things you learn along the way." Andy kept climbing, and he made many friends. He learned a lot from them, too. At last, Andy reached the top of the tree. He looked around and saw the beautiful world. He was happy, but he knew that the real joy was in the journey. The moral of the story is that reaching your goal is important, but the friends you make and the things you learn along the way are just as valuable.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy magnet. He loved to play with it. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with Sam. Tim went to Sam and said, "Can I play with you?" Sam said, "Sure! Let's pass the ball." They started to pass the ball. But Tim's magnet was persistent. It kept sticking to the ball. This made it hard for them to play. Tim and Sam tried to play again. But the magnet still stuck to the ball. Tim said, "I am sad. The magnet won't let us play." Sam had an idea. He said, "Let's play with the magnet instead!" They both played with the magnet and had lots of fun.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. Sara has a big jeep. Ben has a small car. They race their toys on the floor. "Ready, set, go!" Sara says. She pushes her jeep fast. Ben pushes his car faster. He wants to win. But Sara has a trick. She presses a button on her jeep. The jeep makes a loud noise. It reveals a rocket on the back. The rocket shoots out fire. The jeep zooms ahead of the car. "Wow!" Ben says. "Your jeep has a rocket! That is so cool!" Sara smiles. She is happy. She likes to surprise her friend. She says, "Do you want to try my jeep? It is fun." Ben nods. He is shy. He says, "Thank you, Sara. You are very nice." They swap their toys. They play with the jeep and the car. They have fun. They are good friends.
