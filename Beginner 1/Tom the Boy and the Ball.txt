Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Ball

One day, a boy named Tom went to his classroom. Tom was very excited because he had a secret to tell his friend, Sam. When he saw Sam, he whispered, "I have something to tell you. I saw a big, red ball in the park yesterday." Sam was surprised and wanted to see the big, red ball too. He asked Tom, "Can you show me the big, red ball after school?" Tom agreed and they both planned to go to the park after school. When school was over, Tom and Sam went to the park. But when they got there, they did not see the big, red ball. Tom was embarrassed because he could not show Sam the ball. They looked everywhere, but it was gone. Just then, a big, red dog came running towards them. The dog had eaten the ball! Tom and Sam were very sad and went back home without playing with the big, red ball.

Once upon a time there were two brothers, Tim and Joe. They both liked to play outside. One day Tim had a great idea, he wanted to build a statue out of stones. So he asked Joe if he wanted to help him build it. Joe was very jealous of Tim’s idea and said no. Tim was sad but he still wanted to build the statue on his own, so he said goodbye and started to look for stones. While looking for stones, Tim found a really big one. Suddenly, Joe appeared and said “I want to help you build the statue! Can I please join you?”. Tim was so excited and suggested that they work together so they both could build a great statue. Joe looked very happy with the suggestion and they started to build the statue together. After a few hours, they finished the statue and they were very proud of it. They looked at the statue and smiled. They had built the statue together and it made them very happy.

One day, a fast cat named Tim went for a run. He ran to the west, where the sun sets. Tim liked to run fast because it made him feel free. As he ran, he saw a big tree. In the tree, there was a bell. Tim thought it would be fun to ring the bell. He jumped high and hit the bell with his paw. The bell made a loud "ring" sound. A little bird in the tree heard the bell. The bird flew down and said, "Why did you ring the bell, Tim?" Tim said, "I like the sound it makes." The bird smiled and said, "Me too. Let's ring it together." So Tim and the bird rang the bell and played until the sun went down in the west.

Tom and Lily were friends. They liked to play with toys and run in the park. One day, they found a big stick. It had a sharp point at one end. Tom said, "This is my stick. I can use it to poke things." Lily said, "No, that is not nice. You can hurt someone with the point. You should care about others." Tom did not listen to Lily. He said, "I don't care. I want to have fun. This is a regular stick. It can't do much harm." He ran away with the stick. He poked a bird, a cat, and a dog. They all cried and ran away. Tom laughed. He thought he was funny. Lily followed Tom. She was sad and angry. She said, "Tom, stop it. You are being mean. You are making everyone unhappy. You should say sorry and put the stick away." Tom said, "No, I won't. This is my stick. I can do what I want." He turned around and poked Lily with the point. Lily screamed. The point made a big cut on her arm. It hurt a lot. It bled a lot. Tom was scared. He dropped the stick. He said, "Lily, I'm sorry. I didn't mean to hurt you. Please don't cry." Lily said, "Go away, Tom. You are not my friend. You are a bad boy. You don't care about anyone. You only care about yourself." She ran to her mom. Her mom took her to the doctor. The doctor gave her a bandage and a hug. Tom was alone. He felt bad. He wished he had listened to Lily. He wished he had cared about others. He learned a hard lesson. He learned that a regular stick can do a lot of harm. He learned that a point can hurt someone. He learned that he should be kind and gentle. He learned that he should care.
