Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Red Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little red bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree in the park. Every night, Tim would dream about flying high in the sky with his friends. One day, a big cat came to the tree. The cat was a threat to Tim and his friends. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. He flew down to the cat and said, "Please don't hurt us. We just want to be happy and live in our tree." The cat looked at Tim and said, "I won't hurt you, little red bird. I just wanted to play. Let's be friends." Tim was happy that the cat was not a threat anymore. They played together in the park and became good friends. From that day on, Tim's dreams were full of fun and laughter with his new friend, the big cat.

Tim and Mia are friends who like to play in the park. One day, they see a big oak tree with many brown leaves. They want to climb the tree and see the sky. "Can we climb the tree, Tim?" Mia asks. "Yes, we can, Mia. But we have to be careful. The tree is very old and the branches are weak. We don't want to fall and hurt ourselves." Tim says. They hold hands and start to climb the tree. They find a big branch that is strong and flat. They sit on the branch and look at the sky. They see many birds and clouds. They feel happy and proud. "Look, Tim, there is a pond over there. It looks shiny and blue. Can we go there and play?" Mia says. "I don't know, Mia. The pond might be cold and deep. We don't know how to swim. We might drown and die." Tim says. "But it looks so fun and pretty. I want to touch the water and see the fish. Please, Tim, let's go there and play." Mia says. Mia does not listen to Tim. She jumps off the branch and runs to the pond. Tim follows her and tries to stop her. But Mia is too fast and excited. She reaches the pond and dives into the water. Splash! Mia feels a shock. The water is not shiny and blue. It is icy and dark. She cannot breathe or see. She feels scared and cold. She cries for help. "Help, Tim, help! The water is icy and dark. I cannot breathe or see. I am scared and cold. Help, Tim, help!" Mia screams. Tim hears Mia's scream. He runs to the pond and sees Mia in the water. He is scared and sad. He does not know what to do. He looks around and sees a big stick. He grabs the stick and reaches out to Mia. "Grab the stick, Mia, grab the stick! I will pull you out of the water. Don't let go of the stick. You will be okay." Tim shouts. Mia sees the stick and grabs it. Tim pulls her out of the water. She is wet and shivering. She coughs and cries. Tim hugs her and comforts her. "I am sorry, Tim, I am sorry. I was wrong and foolish. I should have listened to you. You are a good friend. Thank you for saving me." Mia says. "It's okay, Mia, it's okay. I am glad you are safe. You are a good friend too. But next time, please be careful and wise. Don't dive into things you don't know. You might get hurt or worse." Tim says. They walk back to the tree and dry themselves with the leaves. They learn a lesson and promise to be careful and wise. They are still friends who like to play in the park. But they never dive into the pond again. The end.

Jimbo had a big adventure planned for the day. He was going to zip around the neighbourhood, looking for gum! He couldn't wait. He asked his mum if he could go. "Be careful!" she said. Jimbo nodded and started zipping away. As Jimbo started zipping around the neighbourhood, lots of nice people waved to him. He just flashed them a smile! Soon, he came to a street corner. There, he noticed something strange - there were two children arguing over a box of gum! Jimbo decided to help. He zipped right up to the two children and said, "Let's share the gum, so everyone can be happy". The children agreed. They each took a handful of gum and smiled. Jimbo was really pleased he had been successful. They all thanked him and then the three of them happily zipped away in different directions.
