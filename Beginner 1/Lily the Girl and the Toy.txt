Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toy

One day, a girl named Lily found a broken toy. She wanted to repair it. She took it to her dad. Her dad said, "I can fix it for you, Lily." Lily saw her mom's perfume on the table. She felt guilty for touching it, but she wanted to make the toy smell nice. So, she put some perfume on the toy. When her dad finished fixing the toy, Lily was happy. She said, "Thank you, Dad!" She gave the toy a big hug. It smelled like her mom's perfume. She loved her toy even more now.

One day, a dog named Max was walking in the park. He saw a ball roll by and he jumped up to get it. The ball belonged to a little girl who was very happy. Max barked at the girl and she asked, "Do you want to play?" Max nodded and they began passing the ball back and forth. They laughed and played until the girl realized it was time to go home. The little girl thanked Max for the fun and she gave him the ball. Max was so happy to have his new toy. He ran home and showed it to his owner. They both laughed and had a great time. From that day on, Max and the little girl were the best of friends. They passed the ball to each other every day, and both were always happy.

Ben and Mia liked to play with their jeep. It was big and red and had four wheels. They drove it around the yard and made loud noises. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep! One day, they saw a bird in the sky. It was black and white and had a long tail. It flew very fast and high. Ben and Mia wanted to fly too. "Look, Mia, a bird!" Ben said. "Can we fly like that?" "I don't know, Ben. How can we fly?" Mia asked. "Maybe we can use our jeep. It has wheels and a horn. Maybe it can fly too." Ben said. They got into their jeep and drove to the hill. They hoped the hill would make them fly. They pushed the horn and said, "Ready, set, go!" They drove down the hill very fast. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They smiled and laughed. They thought they were flying. But they were not flying. They were rolling. The jeep hit a rock and flipped over. Ben and Mia fell out and landed on the grass. They were not hurt, but they were scared and sad. Their jeep was broken and dirty. "Ben, Mia, are you okay?" their mom said. She ran to them and hugged them. She saw their jeep and said, "Oh, no, what happened?" "We wanted to fly, mom. Like the bird. But our jeep can't fly. It can only roll. And it rolled too hard." Ben said. "Mom, we're sorry. We broke our jeep. We were silly." Mia said. Their mom kissed them and said, "It's okay, my loves. I'm glad you're not hurt. But you're right, your jeep can't fly. It can only drive. And you have to be careful when you drive. The hill is too steep and the rock is too harsh. They can hurt you and your jeep." "Can we fix our jeep, mom?" Ben asked. "Maybe we can, Ben. But first, let's go inside and have some juice and cookies. And maybe we can read a book about birds and how they fly. Would you like that?" their mom asked. "Yes, mom. We would like that. Thank you, mom." Ben and Mia said. They held their mom's hand and walked to the house. They still liked their jeep, but they learned that flying was not for them. They were happy to be on the ground.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there lived a dog named Max. Max loved to walk and play with his friends. One day, Max went for a walk with his best friend, a cat named Bella. As they walked, Max and Bella saw a big box. They were curious and wanted to see what was inside. Max said, "Bella, let's open the box!" Bella agreed, "Yes, Max! Let's see what's inside!" When they opened the box, they got a big shock. Inside the box was a huge pile of colorful balls! Max and Bella were so happy. They jumped into the box and played with the balls all day long. And from that day on, Max and Bella had even more fun on their walks together.
