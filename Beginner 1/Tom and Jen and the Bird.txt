Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Jen and the Bird

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. They run, jump, slide and swing. They have fun. One day, they see a bird on the grass. The bird does not move. It does not sing. It does not fly. Tom and Jen go near the bird. "Look, a bird!" Tom says. "Is it sleeping?" Jen asks. Tom touches the bird. It is cold and hard. Tom shakes his head. "No, it is not sleeping. It is dead," he says. "What is dead?" Jen asks. "Dead is when something stops living. It does not breathe, eat, or feel. It does not do anything. It is end," Tom says. Jen feels sad. She likes birds. She does not want them to be dead. "Can we help the bird?" she asks. Tom thinks. He remembers what his mom told him. "No, we cannot help the bird. It is too late. But we can say goodbye to the bird. We can make a nice place for it. We can find some flowers and stones and make a nest. We can say a prayer or a wish for the bird. We can thank it for being a beautiful bird," he says. Jen nods. She likes Tom's idea. They look for flowers and stones. They make a nest for the bird. They say goodbye to the bird. They thank it for being a beautiful bird. They say a prayer or a wish for the bird. They feel better. They hold hands. They go back to play. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Sam. Sam lived in a little house near a big temple. Every day, Sam would lock the door of his house and go to the temple to play with his friends. One day, when Sam went to the temple, he saw a big box. He was very curious about what was inside the box. He tried to open it, but it was locked. Sam and his friends tried to find the key to open the box, but they could not find it. After a while, a little bird came and told Sam that the key was hidden in his own house. Sam ran back to his house and found the key under his bed. He was so happy! He took the key to the temple and unlocked the big box. To everyone's surprise, inside the box, there were many toys for all the animals to play with. Sam and his friends had a great time playing with the new toys, and they all thanked Sam for being such a dependable friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to collect pretty things. One day, Amy found a shiny gold rock. She was very happy and wanted to find more gold rocks. Amy went to the park and looked everywhere for gold rocks. She looked under trees, in the sandbox, and by the swings. She found many gold rocks and put them in her pocket. Amy was very organized and put the gold rocks in a neat line in her pocket. When Amy went home, she showed her mom and dad her gold rocks. They were very proud of her. Amy put her gold rocks in a special box. She was happy she had collected so many gold rocks, and she kept them organized in her box. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy witch. She lived in a little house in the woods. The witch had a big black hat and a long broom. She liked to fly in the sky with her broom. One day, the witch was flying and she fell off her broom. She was very sad and hurt. A little girl saw the witch fall. The girl went to comfort the witch. She said, "Don't cry, I will help you." The little girl helped the witch back to her house. They became friends. The witch was not mean and scary like people thought. She was just clumsy. The little girl and the witch had fun together and lived happily ever after.

One day, a furry cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a shiny shield on the ground. Tom picked it up and said, "Wow, this is a big, shiny shield!" Tom took the shield home and showed it to his friend, a little bird named Sue. Sue said, "That's a nice shield, Tom! Let's play with it!" They played with the shield all day, having lots of fun. At night, they heard a loud noise. It was a big, scary bear trying to get into their house! Tom and Sue were scared, but Tom had an idea. He used the shield to lock the door. The bear tried and tried, but he couldn't get in. Tom and Sue were safe, and they were happy they found the shield.
