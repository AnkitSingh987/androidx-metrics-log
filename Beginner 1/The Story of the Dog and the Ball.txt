Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big, red dog. The dog loved to play with a ball. One day, the dog got a letter. The letter was from a friend. The friend wanted to play with the dog. The dog went to the friend's house. They played with the ball. They had so much fun. The dog was very hungry. The friend gave the dog a delicious treat. The dog was very happy. But then, the dog saw a toy. The toy was not strong. The dog wanted to play with the toy. The dog played too hard and destroyed the toy. The friend was not mad. They laughed and played with the ball again. The dog and the friend were happy.

One day, a small cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree and a little house. Tim walked to the little house and saw a funny thing. It was an ashtray. He did not know what it was, so he stared at it. A nice lady came out of the house. She saw Tim staring at the ashtray. She said, "Hi, little cat! Do you like my ashtray?" Tim looked at her and meowed. He still did not know what the ashtray was for. The lady laughed and picked up Tim. She took him inside the house and gave him some milk. Tim liked the milk and the nice lady. He forgot about the ashtray and played with the lady all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to spin. He would spin around and around in the yard, feeling very enthusiastic. One day, he found a new thing to spin. It was a top. The top was colorful and shiny. Tim was very happy. Tim took the top to his friend, Sue. Sue also liked to spin things. They both played with the top, spinning it as fast as they could. They laughed and had lots of fun. The top would spin and spin, and they would clap their hands in excitement. But one day, while Tim and Sue were playing with the top, it spun too fast. It flew out of their hands and broke a window. Tim and Sue were sad. They knew they had done something wrong. Their parents were not happy, and they had to clean up the mess. The fun was over, and they learned to be more careful with their things.

Once upon a time, there was a smart squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find nuts and save them for later. One day, Sam saw a big nut under a tree. He was very happy and thought, "I must save this nut for winter." Sam hid the nut in a hole near his tree house. But he did not mind the hole very well. A sneaky bird saw Sam hide the nut. The bird thought, "I will take that nut when Sam is not looking." The next day, Sam went to find his big nut. But it was gone! Sam was very sad. Then, he saw the sneaky bird with his big nut. Sam knew he should have minded the hole better. He learned a lesson and promised to be more careful next time.

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They like the slide. They go up and down, up and down. They laugh and shout. They are noisy. A man is walking his dog. The dog is big and brown. The dog sees Lily and Ben. The dog runs to them. The dog wants to play. The dog barks. He is noisy too. Lily and Ben are scared of the dog. They run away from the slide. They hide behind a tree. They are quiet. The man calls the dog. He says, "Rex, come here. Rex, stop. Rex, bad dog." He is angry. He is noisy too. The dog does not listen. The dog finds Lily and Ben. The dog jumps on them. The dog licks them. The dog is happy. He is noisy too. Lily and Ben are not scared anymore. They see the dog is friendly. They pet the dog. They say, "Hi, Rex. You are a good dog. You are a noisy dog." They are happy. They are noisy too. The man comes to them. He says, "I'm sorry. Rex is a good dog, but he is very excited. He likes to play. He likes children. He likes slides." He is sorry. He is not noisy. Lily and Ben say, "It's okay. We like Rex. We like to play. We like slides too. Can we play with Rex?" The man says, "Yes, you can. But be careful. Rex likes to bury things. He might bury your shoes or your toys. He might bury the slide." Lily and Ben laugh. They say, "That's silly. Rex can't bury the slide. The slide is too big. The slide is too fun. We will play with Rex and the slide." They all go to the slide. They take turns. They go up and down, up and down. They laugh and shout. They are noisy. They are happy. The end.
