Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Squirrel

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to explore the woods near their cabin. One day, they found a big tree with a hole in it. They wanted to see what was inside, so they crawled into the hole. They saw a lot of nuts and berries that looked yummy. They decided to take some and eat them. They did not know that the hole was the home of a squirrel. The squirrel came back and saw the twins chewing his food. He was very angry and scared. He started to make loud noises and scratch them with his claws. "Ow! Ow! Let's go!" Lily cried. "Sorry, sorry, we did not know!" Ben said. They ran out of the hole and back to their cabin. They had scratches and bites on their arms and legs. They told their mom what happened. Mom was not happy. She cleaned their wounds and gave them a hug. She said, "You should not take things that are not yours. That is not nice. The squirrel worked hard to gather his food for the winter. How would you feel if someone took your toys or snacks?" Lily and Ben felt sorry. They said, "We feel bad. We did not think. We are sorry, mom. We are sorry, squirrel." Mom said, "It's okay. You made a mistake, but you learned from it. Next time, you should ask before you take something. And you should respect the animals and their homes. They are our friends, not our food." Lily and Ben nodded. They said, "Yes, mom. We will do that. We will be kind and careful." Mom smiled. She said, "That's good. Now, let's have some soup and bread. They are warm and good for you." Lily and Ben smiled too. They said, "Thank you, mom. We love you." They ate their soup and bread and felt better. They learned a lesson that day. They learned to be respectful and honest. They learned not to chew what was not theirs.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They were running and having fun. The cat saw an empty box and had an idea. She wanted to joke with the dog. So, the cat hid in the box. The dog looked for the cat but could not find her. The cat jumped out of the box and said, "Joke! I was hiding in the box!" The dog laughed and said, "You are funny, cat!" Then, they heard some jazz music. They saw a group of animals playing music. The cat and the dog danced to the jazz. They had a great day in the park, full of jokes and fun.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing in his room. His mom came in and said, "Tim, your room is not clean. Please make it clean." Tim did not want to do this job. He wanted to play with his toys. But his mom said, "If you do not clean your room, you cannot play outside." Tim was sad, but he started to clean his room. He put his toys away and made his bed. As he was cleaning, he found a toy he had lost. He was happy to see it again. He showed it to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found my toy!" His mom was happy too. She said, "Good job, Tim! Now your room is clean, and you can play outside." Tim was very happy. He went outside to play with his friends. The conflict was over, and Tim learned that it was good to have a clean room.

Once upon a time, there was a small, healthy dog named Spot. Spot loved to try new things. One day, he saw a big step in front of him. He wanted to go up the step, but it was too high for him. Spot thought, "I will try my best to go up the step." Spot jumped and tried to go up the step. He tried once, but he could not do it. He tried again, but he still could not do it. Spot was sad, but he did not give up. He knew he had to try one more time. Just then, a friendly bird saw Spot and asked, "What are you trying to do?" Spot said, "I want to go up the step, but it is too high for me." The bird said, "I can help you!" The bird took Spot's paw and helped him up the step. Spot was so happy! He thanked the bird and they became good friends. From that day on, Spot and the bird tried new things together and had lots of fun.
