Category: Beginner
Title: The Pink Caterpillar and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little pink caterpillar. She lived in a big green garden. The pink caterpillar had many friends like the blue bird, the red ant, and the yellow bee. One day, the pink caterpillar saw her friend, the blue bird, jump from a tree. She wanted to jump too. So, she tried to jump, but she could not. She was sad. The red ant and the yellow bee saw her and wanted to help. They had an idea! They found a big leaf and told the pink caterpillar to climb on it. Then, they held the leaf and jumped. The pink caterpillar jumped too! She was so happy. All her friends laughed and played together in the big green garden.

Tim and Kim were twins who liked to play and have fun. One day, their mom made a big pie for them to share. It was a healthy pie with fruits and nuts and honey. Mom said, "This pie is good for you and tasty too. You can have one piece each after lunch." Tim and Kim were happy and said, "Thank you, mom!" They went to play in the yard until lunchtime. But Tim had a sneaky idea. He wanted to have more pie than Kim. He thought, "If I eat my piece fast, I can get another one before Kim finishes hers. Then I will have two pieces and she will have one. That will be more fun for me." So Tim ate his lunch very quickly and ran to the kitchen. He saw the pie on the table and grabbed another piece. He was about to eat it when he heard Kim coming. He hid the piece behind his back and pretended to be full. Kim said, "That was a yummy lunch, mom. Can I have my piece of pie now?" Mom said, "Of course, dear. Here you go." She gave Kim a big piece of pie and smiled. Kim took a bite and said, "This pie is so good, mom. I love it. Thank you for making it for us." She looked at Tim and said, "Do you want to share some of my pie with me, Tim? We can have half and half. That will be more fun for us." Tim felt guilty and ashamed. He knew he had been greedy and selfish. He said, "No, thank you, Kim. I already had my piece. And I have something to tell you. I took another piece when you were not looking. I'm sorry, Kim. I was not fair to you. I did not value your feelings or our friendship. Can you forgive me?" Kim was surprised and hurt. She said, "Why did you do that, Tim? That was not nice. You made me sad. You should not take more than you need. You should value what you have and share with others. That is what mom and dad taught us. That is what makes us happy and healthy." She paused and looked at Tim. She saw that he was sorry and sad too. She said, "I forgive you, Tim. But you have to promise me that you will not do that again. And you have to give back the piece you took. You can give it to mom or dad or someone else who needs it. That will make you feel better and show that you care." Tim nodded and said, "I promise, Kim. And I will give back the piece I took. You are right, Kim. Sharing is better than taking. And valuing is better than wasting. I'm sorry, Kim. I love you. You are my best friend." He hugged Kim and said, "Thank you, Kim. You are very kind and generous. You taught me a good lesson today. I'm glad we have each other." Kim hugged him back and said, "I love you too, Tim. You are my best friend too. And I'm glad we have this pie. It is very good and healthy. Let's enjoy it together." They smiled and ate their pie happily. Mom and dad watched them and were proud of them. They said, "You are good children. You learned a valuable lesson today. We love you very much." They joined them and shared the pie too. They all had a good and healthy time. The end.
