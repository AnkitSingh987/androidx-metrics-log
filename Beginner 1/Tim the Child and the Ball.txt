Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Child and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little child named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the open. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the ball?" His mom said, "Yes, you can, but be careful." Tim started to pass the ball to his new friend, Sam. They laughed and had fun. They passed the ball back and forth. Tim was very happy. After a while, it was time to go home. Tim and Sam said goodbye. Tim told his mom, "I had so much fun today!" His mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you did, Tim." From that day on, Tim and Sam played together at the park, passing the ball in the open. They became best friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus had lots of yummy food inside. The bus went down the street every day to give food to all the kids. One day, the bus driver got lost. He did not know where to go. He looked left and right, but he could not find the way. The kids were sad because they wanted their yummy food. A nice lady saw the lost bus and helped the driver. She showed him the right way to go. The bus driver was happy and said thank you. The bus went to the kids and gave them their yummy food. Everyone was happy and the bus never got lost again.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a little beetle named Bob. Bob was a very happy beetle. He had many friends. They liked to play in the sun all day. One day, Bob saw his friend, the ant, lying on the ground. The ant was not moving. Bob called to his friend, "Ant, wake up! Let's play!" But the ant did not move. Bob was sad. He thought his friend was dead. Bob went to find help. He called to his friend, the bee, "Bee, come help! Ant is not moving!" The bee flew down and looked at the ant. The bee said, "Ant is not dead, he is just sleeping. Let him rest." Bob felt happy again. Bob and the bee left the ant to sleep. They played together and waited for the ant to wake up. When the ant woke up, they all played together. They had a lot of fun. The moral of the story is to be patient and not jump to conclusions. Sometimes, things are not as bad as they seem.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toys, but he always wanted to play on the roof. One day, he asked his mom, "Can I play on the roof, please?" His mom said, "No, Tom. The roof is high, and you must be careful. I recommend you play in the yard instead." Tom listened to his mom and played in the yard. He saw a little bird on the ground. The bird looked sad. Tom asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I want to go on the roof, but I am scared to fly." Tom said, "My mom told me to be careful of the roof. But I will help you, little bird." Tom helped the bird to fly, and it went to the roof safely. The bird was happy. Tom was happy too. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little red ball. The ball loved to bounce all day long. It was very excited to play with the kids in the park. One sunny day, a small boy named Tim came to the park. Tim saw the little red ball and wanted to play with it. He picked up the ball and put it on his shoulder. They played together and the ball was so happy. As they played, Tim bounced the ball high into the sky. The ball went up, up, up, and then came down, down, down. Tim caught the ball on his shoulder with a big smile. They played all day and became best friends. The little red ball was so excited to have a friend like Tim.
