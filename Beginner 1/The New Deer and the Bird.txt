Category: Beginner
Title: The New Deer and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a new little deer. The deer had many friends. They liked to play together every day. One day, the deer went to a new part of the forest and got lost. The deer missed its friends and wanted to go back home. The deer was walking and saw a big tree. The deer thought, "Maybe if I climb the tree, I can see my friends." The deer tried to climb, but it was too hard. The deer was sad and missed its friends even more. Then, a kind bird saw the deer and asked, "Why are you sad?" The deer said, "I miss my friends. I am lost." The kind bird said, "I can help you find your friends." The bird flew high and saw the deer's friends. The bird showed the deer the way back. The deer was very happy to see its friends again. They all played together and never missed each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a big piano. The piano lived in a small house. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the house. Tim was very frightened, but he wanted to see the piano. Tim opened the door and saw the piano. He was scared, but he wanted to play it. Tim started to play the piano. The piano made a loud sound. Tim was so scared that he ran away. Tim decided to jog back to his home. On the way, he saw a big dog. The dog was angry and chased Tim. Tim was very frightened and tried to run faster. But the dog caught him and bit him. Tim cried and wished he never went to the piano house.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to sort his toys by color. One day, he found an ancient toy. It was a ball. Tim had a goal to play with the ball. Tim took the ball outside. He kicked it high and far. The ball went into the woods. Tim was sad but he wanted to find the ball. So, he went into the woods to look for it. The woods were dark and scary. Tim looked and looked but he could not find the ball. He got lost in the woods. Tim was very sad because he could not find his way back home.

Lily was a dancer. She liked to dance with her friends at school. They had a big show soon. Lily wanted to do a good job. But Lily had a problem. She did not have a dress for the show. All her friends had pretty dresses. Lily was worried. She asked her mom for a dress. But her mom said no. They did not have money for a dress. Lily was sad. She looked around her room. She saw her old doll. Her doll had a dress. Lily had an idea. She used scissors to cut the dress from the doll. She put the dress on. It was too small. But Lily did not care. She was happy. She had a dress for the show. She went to school with her dress. She saw her friends. They laughed at her. They said her dress was ugly. They said she looked like a baby. Lily was hurt. She ran away. She cried. She missed the show. She missed her chance to dance. She missed her doll. She was sorry. She wished she had not used the scissors. She wished she had a better dress. She wished she had better friends. She was lonely. She was unhappy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Sam. Sam loved to play with his toy mouse. One day, Sam could not find his toy mouse. He looked under the bed, but it was not there. He looked behind the chair, but it was not there either. Sam was sad. Sam's mom said, "Don't worry, Sam. We will find your toy mouse." She had a big hook to help look for the toy. Sam was excited to use the hook to find his toy. They looked in the kitchen and used the hook to move things around. Finally, they found the toy mouse! It was hiding behind the big pot. Sam was so happy to find his toy mouse with the help of the hook. From that day on, Sam always kept his toy mouse close to him so he would not lose it again.
