Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy the Bird and the Benny

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy was an independent bird who liked to fly alone. He had a happy spirit and loved to sing. One sunny day, Timmy met a big bird named Benny. Benny said, "Hello, Timmy! Do you want to fly with me?" Timmy was not sure if he should trust Benny. But Benny had a kind smile and a gentle voice. Timmy decided to trust Benny, and they flew together in the sky. They played and sang happy songs. Timmy learned that making new friends was fun. From that day on, Timmy and Benny were the best of friends, and they shared many adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly mechanic named Tom. He liked to fix cars and play with his dog, Spot. One day, Tom went to the park with Spot to play ball. At the park, Tom threw the ball far away. Spot ran fast to get it. On the way back, Spot saw a big tear in the grass. Spot showed it to Tom. Tom looked at it and said, "What could have made this tear?" Just then, a little girl came running to them. She said, "I made the tear with my toy car!" Tom and Spot were surprised. They did not know a toy car could make a big tear like that. The little girl showed them her toy car, and they all played together in the park.

Jill was hungry. She wanted lunch. She asked Mom for lunch. Mom said, "Not now, Jill. I am busy. Wait a little bit." Jill did not like to wait. She asked Mom again. And again. And again. She was persistent. She said, "Please, Mom. I want lunch. I want lunch. I want lunch." Mom sighed. She was tired. She had a lot of work to do. She said, "Jill, stop asking. You are making me angry. Go play with your toys. I will make lunch soon." Jill did not listen. She kept asking. She followed Mom around the house. She said, "Lunch, lunch, lunch. I want lunch. I want lunch. I want lunch." Mom snapped. She was very angry. She said, "That's it, Jill. You are being naughty. No lunch for you. Go to your room. And stay there until I say so." Jill cried. She was hungry. She was sad. She was sorry. She went to her room. She had no lunch. She had no toys. She had no fun. She had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very glad because she found a big, shiny needle. She knew she could use the needle to sew her teddy bear's arm back on. Sue loved her teddy bear so much. One day, Sue saw a mean, big cat trying to fight her teddy bear. The cat wanted to take the needle away from Sue. Sue was scared, but she knew she had to protect her teddy bear and the needle. Sue was brave and she yelled, "No, cat! You can't have my needle!" The cat was scared of Sue's big voice and ran away. Sue was so glad that she saved her teddy bear and the needle. They lived happily ever after, and Sue's teddy bear never lost its arm again.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird. The bird had a friend, a kind old man with a cane. They liked to play and sing together. One sunny day, the blue bird saw something new in the garden. It was a big red ball. The bird stared at the ball, wondering what it was. The old man saw the bird and smiled. The old man picked up the ball with his cane and threw it. The blue bird was so happy! It flew and played with the ball all day. The old man and the blue bird were the best of friends, and they had lots of fun together.

One sunny day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big, angry dog. The dog was barking at a tiny cat. Mia wanted to help the cat, but she was scared of the angry dog. Mia had an idea. She found a stick and said, "Hey dog, look at this!" She threw the stick far away. The angry dog ran after the stick. The cat was safe now. Mia walked to the cat and saw that it was hurt. The cat had a hurt toe. Mia said, "I will test if you can walk." The cat tried to walk, but it was hard. Mia took the cat home and her mom helped fix the cat's toe. Now, Mia and the cat are best friends.
