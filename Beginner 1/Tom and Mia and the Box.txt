Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Box

Tom and Mia were friends who liked to play together. One day, they found a big box in the park. They wondered what was inside the box. "Let's open it!" Tom said, enthusiastic. He tried to lift the lid, but it was too heavy. "Maybe we can shake it and hear what it is," Mia suggested. She grabbed one side of the box and Tom grabbed the other. They started to shake the box hard. Suddenly, they heard a loud roar from inside the box. It was a lion! The lion was angry and scared. It broke the lid and jumped out of the box. It saw Tom and Mia and ran towards them. "Help!" Tom and Mia screamed. They dropped the box and tried to run away. But the lion was faster and stronger. It caught them and bit them. Tom and Mia were hurt and sad. They wished they had never found the box.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw her friend Tom playing with a ball. "Can I join?" she asked. Tom said, "Yes! Let's play together!" They played with the ball and had lots of fun. After a while, they got hungry. Amy had a big blueberry in her bag. "I have a blueberry! Let's eat it!" she said. Tom was excited because he loved blueberries. They sat down and shared the big, smooth blueberry. It was very yummy. As they ate the blueberry, they saw a big bird in the sky. The bird looked like it wanted the blueberry too. But Amy and Tom had already eaten it. The bird flew away, and Amy and Tom knew they had to bring more blueberries next time. They smiled at each other and went back to playing with the ball.

One day, a smelly frog named Bob lived near a big pond. He liked to hop and play all day. He had a friend named Sam, who was a little fish. Sam liked to swim fast and make big splashes. One sunny day, Bob saw a kayak near the pond. He thought it looked fun to play with. He called to Sam, "Sam, let's play with the kayak!" Sam swam over and said, "Okay, let's go!" They jumped into the kayak and started to paddle. But then, a big wind came and struck the kayak. It shook and wobbled, but they held on tight. They laughed and had fun, even though it was a little scary. After the wind stopped, they paddled back to the pond. They were tired but happy. They had a great day playing with the kayak. Bob and Sam knew they would have more fun adventures together. From that day on, Bob the smelly frog and Sam the little fish played with the kayak all the time. They loved their new toy and the fun times they had together. And they were always the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small ordinary town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. He had a big blue car and a small red car. Tim would push the cars around the room and make them go fast. He liked to watch the wheels spin as they raced across the floor. One day, Tim saw a big wheel outside his window. It was on a truck, and the truck was carrying a big load. Tim wanted to play with the big wheel, so he asked his mom, "Mom, can I please play with the big wheel outside?" His mom looked outside and saw the big truck. She said, "No, Tim, that wheel is too big for you to play with. But we can go to the park and play with your toy cars instead." Tim thought about it and agreed, "Okay, Mom! Let's go to the park and play with my cars!" So Tim and his mom went to the park, and Tim played with his toy cars all day. He was happy to see the wheels spin as they raced down the slide and across the grass. And even though he couldn't play with the big wheel on the truck, Tim still had a fun day with his mom and his toy cars.
