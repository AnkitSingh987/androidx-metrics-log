Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Garden

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to splash in the puddles after the rain. They wore their boots and coats and hats. They laughed and jumped and splashed. But then they heard a loud noise. It was thunder. It made them scared. They ran to the house and looked for Mom. "Mom, Mom, where are you?" they cried. Mom was in the kitchen. She heard them and came to the door. She hugged them and said, "Don't worry, my loves. Thunder is just a noise. It can't hurt you. It's God's way of saying hello." Lily and Tom felt better. They smiled and said, "Hello, God!" Mom said, "Do you want some cookies and milk?" They nodded and said, "Yes, please!" They followed Mom to the kitchen. But then they smelled something smelly. It was the trash. It was full of old food and dirty diapers. "Mom, what is that?" they asked. Mom said, "That's the trash. It's very smelly. We need to take it out." Lily and Tom said, "Can we help you?" Mom said, "Sure, you can. But first, let's have some cookies and milk. And then we can take the trash out together." They ate their cookies and drank their milk. They felt happy and warm. They forgot about the thunder and the puddles. They only remembered the cookies and the hugs. And they helped Mom take the trash out. They were good kids.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and have fun in the park. One day, he found a big red ball. He thought it would be good to play with it. Spot did not know who the ball belonged to, but he was sure he would find out soon. The next day, Spot and his friend, Kitty, went to the park. They played with the big red ball and had a lot of fun. They threw it, kicked it, and rolled it around. They knew someone must be missing the ball, but they didn't know who. On the third day, Spot and Kitty saw a sad girl in the park. She was looking for something. Spot had a feeling she was looking for the big red ball. He ran to get it and brought it to the girl. The girl was very happy to find her ball. She said, "Thank you, Spot and Kitty!" Now, they all played together and had lots of good fun with the big red ball.

Anna loves to dance. She dances every day in her room. She dances with her dolls, her teddy bear and her scarf. She likes to spin, jump and twirl. On Tuesday, Anna has a dance class. She wears her pink dress and her shiny shoes. She is very happy. She goes to the park with her mom. There she meets her friends, Ben and Lily. They also have dance class. "Hello, Anna!" Ben and Lily say. "Are you ready to dance?" "Yes, I am!" Anna says. "I love to dance!" They walk to the dance studio. There they see their teacher, Miss Rose. She is very nice. She smiles and says, "Welcome, children! Today we are going to learn a new dance. It is called the bunny hop. Are you alert and ready?" "Yes, we are!" Anna, Ben and Lily say. Miss Rose shows them how to do the bunny hop. They have to hop like bunnies, with their ears up and their noses twitching. They have to follow the music and the rhythm. They have to have fun. Anna, Ben and Lily try to do the bunny hop. They hop and hop and hop. They laugh and giggle. They have fun. They dance.

One day, a cat and a dog were friends. They liked to play and talk. The cat was very wise, and the dog was very foolish. They lived in a small house with a mail box in front. One day, the dog said, "I want to send a letter to my friend." The cat said, "You can trust me. I will help you put your letter in the mail box." The dog was happy and gave the cat his letter. But the cat did a silly thing! Instead of putting the letter in the mail box, the cat hid it under a rock. The dog found the letter and said, "Why did you do that?" The cat said, "I was just playing a game." The dog said, "Next time, I will trust you to do the right thing." And they both laughed and played together again.
