Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Ball

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run, jump and slide on the grass and the swings. They saw many things that were green: the grass, the trees, the leaves, the frogs. They liked green. One day, they noticed something unusual. It was a big green ball in the corner of the park. It was shiny and smooth and had no holes or seams. They wondered what it was and how it got there. "Let's go and see the green ball!" Tom said. "OK, but be careful. It might be dangerous." Lily said. They ran to the green ball and touched it. It felt cold and hard. They tried to push it, but it did not move. They tried to bounce it, but it did not bounce. They tried to kick it, but it hurt their feet. "What is this green ball?" Tom asked. "I don't know. Maybe it's a toy. Maybe it's a rock. Maybe it's an alien." Lily said. They heard a loud noise. It was a man with a big truck. He had a crane and a hook. He came to the green ball and hooked it up. He lifted it and put it in his truck. "Hey, what are you doing?" Tom shouted. "I'm taking this green ball away. It's not a toy. It's not a rock. It's not an alien. It's a bomb." The man said. "A bomb?" Tom and Lily gasped. "Yes, a bomb. Someone left it here to hurt people. It's very bad. I'm taking it to a safe place where it can't explode. You are very lucky you did not make it explode. You should never touch things that are unusual or strange. They might be dangerous. You should always tell an adult if you see something like this." The man said. Tom and Lily nodded. They felt scared and sorry. They learned a lesson. They thanked the man and ran back to their parents. They told them what happened and hugged them. They decided to only play with things that were green and safe. They did not like bombs.

Once upon a time there was a sad fish. It had nobody to swim with. Every day it would swim and swim, but it still felt very lonely. Then one day, the fish met a jogger. The jogger was friendly and said "hello". The fish was so happy to make a new friend. The jogger said they could be friends if they went swimming each week. The fish was so excited! Every week, the fish and the jogger would swim and swim together. They had so much fun together and the fish was no longer sad. It had found its best friend. The fish and the jogger were friends forever. They kept swimming every week until one day, the jogger had to leave. The fish was sad, but it was happy to have had a great friend. The end.

Tom and Mia were friends. They liked to play outside in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They were cheerful and laughed a lot. But then, a mean boy came. He was bigger than Tom and Mia. He wanted to play with their snowman. He said, "Give me your snowman. It is mine now." Tom and Mia said, "No, it is our snowman. We made it. Go away." The mean boy did not go away. He was angry. He tried to pull the hat and the scarf from the snowman. Tom and Mia tried to stop him. They pulled back. They shouted, "Stop it! Leave our snowman alone!" But the mean boy was stronger. He pulled harder. He broke the snowman. He threw the hat and the scarf in the air. He laughed. He said, "Ha ha ha! Your snowman is gone. You are silly." Tom and Mia were sad. They cried. They looked at their snowman. It was only a pile of snow. They felt cold and lonely. They wished the mean boy would go away. They wished they could make their snowman again. But they could not. The end.

One day, a big, fast cat named Tom was playing in the sunshine. Tom liked to think he was the fastest cat in the world. He liked to run and jump all day long. One day, while Tom was playing, he saw a small, slow bug. Tom thought he could catch the bug very fast. But the bug was not slow, it was very fast too! The bug ran and ran, and Tom could not catch it. This made Tom very sad. Tom kept trying to catch the fast bug, but he could not. He got very tired and fell into a deep sleep. While Tom was sleeping, the fast bug went away. When Tom woke up, the sunshine was gone, and it was dark. Tom was cold, tired, and still sad because he could not catch the fast bug.
