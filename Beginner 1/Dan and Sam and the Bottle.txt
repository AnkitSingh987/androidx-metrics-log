Category: Beginner
Title: Dan and Sam and the Bottle

Dan and Sam are friends. They like to play with gear. Gear is fun. Gear can move and make noise. Dan and Sam have many gear in their room. One day, Dan and Sam are thirsty. They want to drink. They see a bottle on the table. The bottle is red. The bottle has a picture of a car. Dan and Sam think the bottle has juice. They like juice. Dan and Sam take the bottle. They open the bottle. They smell the bottle. The bottle smells bad. Dan and Sam do not care. They are very thirsty. They drink from the bottle. The bottle does not have juice. The bottle has gas. Gas is not for drinking. Gas is for cars. Gas is rough. Gas hurts Dan and Sam. Dan and Sam cough and cry. They feel sick. They fall on the floor. Mom and Dad hear Dan and Sam. They run to the room. They see the bottle. They see Dan and Sam. They are scared. They call for help. They hug Dan and Sam. They say sorry. They say they love Dan and Sam. But it is too late. Dan and Sam do not wake up. Dan and Sam are gone.

Mia and Ben are friends. They like to play in the farm. They see many animals and plants. They see cows, pigs, chickens, and sheep. They see corn, carrots, apples, and pumpkins. One day, they want to pick some corn for a snack. They walk to the corn field. They see many tall green plants with yellow ears. They pull some ears and peel them. They see the corn kernels. They are yellow and sweet. "Look, Mia, I have a big ear of corn!" Ben says. He shows Mia his corn. He is proud. "Me too, Ben, I have a big ear of corn!" Mia says. She shows Ben her corn. She is happy. They sit under a tree and eat their corn. They munch and crunch. They smile and laugh. They enjoy their snack. But they do not see a chubby pig behind them. The pig is hungry. He smells the corn. He wants some too. He sneaks up to Mia and Ben. He snorts and grunts. He grabs Mia's corn with his mouth. He runs away. "Hey, that's my corn!" Mia shouts. She is angry. She chases the pig. "Stop, pig, stop!" Ben shouts. He is surprised. He chases the pig too. The pig is fast. He runs to the barn. He hides behind a haystack. He eats Mia's corn. He is satisfied. Mia and Ben reach the barn. They look for the pig. They see the haystack. They hear the pig. "There he is!" Mia says. She points to the haystack. "Give back my corn, pig!" Ben says. He shakes his fist. The pig does not mind. He ignores Mia and Ben. He finishes Mia's corn. He burps. Mia and Ben are sad. They have no corn. They walk back to the tree. They see a farmer. He is kind. He sees Mia and Ben. "Hello, kids, what's wrong?" the farmer asks. He smiles. "A pig took our corn!" Mia says. She pouts. "He ran away with it!" Ben says. He frowns. The farmer feels sorry for Mia and Ben. He has an idea. "I have more corn in my truck. Do you want some?" the farmer asks. He winks. "Yes, please!" Mia and Ben say. They nod. The farmer gives Mia and Ben two ears of corn each. They are bigger and sweeter than before. They thank the farmer. They hug him. They sit under the tree again and eat their corn. They munch and crunch. They smile and laugh. They forget the pig. They enjoy their snack. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy penguin named Pippin. Pippin lived in a cold place with lots of ice and snow. He had many friends, but his best friend was a little girl named Lily. One day, Pippin and Lily were playing in the snow. Pippin said, "Lily, can you hold my flipper?" Lily held Pippin's flipper and they walked together. They laughed and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened! A big snowball came rolling down a hill. It was bigger than Pippin and almost as big as Lily! Pippin and Lily were scared, but they held each other's flippers and hands. They jumped out of the way just in time. They were safe and happy. Pippin said, "Thank you, Lily, for holding my flipper. You are my best friend." And they played in the snow all day long.
