Category: Beginner
Title: The Drum and the Rhythm

Once upon a time, in an ancient land, there was a big drum. This drum had a special rhythm. It was a happy rhythm that made everyone dance. One day, a mean man came to the land. He did not like the happy rhythm. He wanted to take the drum away. The people did not want the mean man to take their drum. They loved to dance to the rhythm. The mean man was very strong. He took the drum and ran away. The people were very sad. They could not dance to the happy rhythm anymore. The mean man had won, and the people never danced again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a sleepy lion named Leo. Leo loved to sleep all day and all night. He would find a soft spot under a big tree and lay down to rest. One sunny day, while Leo was sleeping, a little bird named Bella came by. Bella saw Leo and said, "Leo, why do you sleep all day? Don't you want to play with me?" Leo opened one eye and let out a big sigh. "I am so sleepy, Bella. Maybe tomorrow," he said and went back to sleep. The next day, Bella came back to find Leo still sleeping. She had an idea. She flew away and returned with a big red ball. Bella dropped the ball on Leo's head. Leo woke up with a start. "What's that?" he asked. Bella smiled and said, "It's a ball for us to play with!" Leo looked at the ball and then at Bella. He smiled too. From that day on, Leo and Bella played together every day, and Leo wasn't so sleepy anymore.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big box. She wanted to open it, but she had to struggle. It was very hard for her. Sue wished she was strong like her big brother, Tom. Sue asked Tom for help. Tom was nice and helped her open the box. Inside the box, they found many toys. Sue and Tom were very happy. Sue and Tom played with the toys all day. They had a lot of fun. They shared the toys and were nice to each other. Sue was happy her wish came true.

One day, a brown dog named Max was walking in the park. Max saw a little girl with a hurt leg. She was crying. Max went over to the girl and said, "Why are you crying?" The girl said, "I fell down and hurt my leg. It hurts a lot." Max wanted to help the girl, so he looked around for something to make her feel better. Max saw a soft spot under a tree. He told the girl, "Let's settle down under that tree. It will make you feel better." The girl and Max went to the tree and sat down. They started talking and laughing. Suddenly, the girl's mom came running over. She was so happy to see her daughter feeling better. She said, "Thank you, Max, for helping my girl." The girl's mom took her to the doctor, and Max went home. The next day, Max and the girl met in the park again. They played and had fun. Max learned that helping others can make you feel good too. And the girl learned that even when you are hurt, you can still have fun with friends.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the park with his mom. He brought his toy model car with him. It was a mild day, so the sun was not too hot. Tom played with his car on the grass and had a lot of fun. Tom saw his friend, Sue, and said, "Hi Sue, look at my car!" Sue looked at the car and liked it too. They played together with the car and ran around the park. Suddenly, Tom's mom shouted, "Tom, look out!" A big dog came running and took the car in its mouth. Tom and Sue were scared, but the dog just wanted to play. They all played together and had a great day at the park.

Lily and Sam were playing in the park. They saw a big red ball on the grass. Lily wanted to play with the ball, but Sam got there first. He picked up the ball and ran away. "Give me the ball, Sam!" Lily shouted. "It is my turn!" "No, it is mine!" Sam said. "You are too slow!" Lily was angry. She ran after Sam and tried to grab the ball. Sam dodged and laughed. He threw the ball in the air and caught it again. Lily saw a stick on the ground. She picked it up and ran towards Sam. She wanted to poke him with the stick. She thought that would make him give her the ball. But Sam saw her coming. He threw the ball at her face. The ball hit Lily hard and made her cry. She dropped the stick and covered her eyes. Sam felt bad. He ran to Lily and said he was sorry. He gave her the ball and hugged her. "Are you okay, Lily?" Sam asked. Lily opened her eyes and saw Sam. She was still sad, but she forgave him. She hugged him back and said she was sorry too. They decided to share the ball and play together. They had more fun that way. The moral of the story is: Don't be selfish and mean to your friends. Share and be kind.
