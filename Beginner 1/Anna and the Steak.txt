Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Steak

Anna loved steak. It was her favorite food. She liked it when her mom cooked steak for dinner. It smelled good and tasted good. She always ate it fast and asked for more. One day, Anna saw a big steak in the fridge. She wanted to eat it right away. She opened the fridge and reached for the steak. But her mom saw her and said, "No, Anna, you can't have that steak. That's for daddy. He will be home soon. You have to wait until dinner." Anna was sad and angry. She did not want to wait. She wanted the steak now. She said, "But mom, I'm hungry. And I love steak. Please, can I have it? Just a little bit?" Her mom shook her head. She said, "No, Anna, you can't. That's not fair to daddy. He works hard and he deserves a nice steak. You have to be patient and share. You can have some salad and bread for now. And you can have some steak later, if there is any left." Anna felt helpless. She could not have what she wanted. She could not make her mom change her mind. She could not take the steak without her mom seeing. She did not like salad and bread. She only liked steak. She started to cry and scream. She threw a tantrum. Her mom sighed. She said, "Anna, stop that. You're being naughty. You're not allowed to act like that. You have to calm down and listen. If you don't, you will have no steak at all. And you will have to go to your room. Do you understand?" Anna did not understand. She did not care. She only wanted steak. She cried and screamed louder. She kicked and hit. She was not happy. She was not nice. She was not smart. She was not good.

Lia was a shy girl who liked to play with her dolls in her hut. She made the hut from old blankets and sticks in the garden. She set her dolls on a mat and pretended they were having a tea party. One day, a new boy moved in next door. His name was Ben and he had a big dog. Lia saw him from her hut and felt scared. She hid behind her dolls and hoped he would not see her. But Ben was curious and friendly. He saw the hut and wanted to play. He walked over with his dog and said hello. "Hi, I'm Ben. What's your name? Can I play with you?" Lia was too shy to answer. She clutched her dolls and shook her head. She wished he would go away. But Ben did not give up. He smiled and said, "Don't be afraid. My dog is nice. He likes to play fetch. Look, I have a ball. Do you want to try?" He threw the ball and his dog ran after it. Lia watched the dog and felt a little curious. She liked animals. She peeked out of her hut and saw the dog bring back the ball. He wagged his tail and licked Ben's face. Ben laughed and said, "See? He's a good dog. Do you want to pet him?" He held out the ball and the dog to Lia. Lia felt a little brave. She crawled out of her hut and reached for the ball. The dog sniffed her hand and licked it. Lia giggled and threw the ball. The dog ran after it again. Ben clapped and said, "You did it! You're good at this. What's your name?" Lia smiled and said, "I'm Lia. I like your dog. He's fun." Ben smiled back and said, "I'm glad you like him. His name is Spot. Do you want to see my hut? I have some cars and books." Lia nodded and said, "Yes, please. Can I bring my dolls?" Ben said, "Of course. Come on, let's go." He took her hand and they walked to his hut. Spot followed them, wagging his tail. Lia was not shy anymore. She had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat loved to relax in the sun. Every day, the cat would find a high spot and lie down. The cat felt warm and happy. One day, the cat found a jar of jam. The cat had never seen jam before. The cat thought the jam was a new toy. The cat played with the jar and had a lot of fun. Then, the jar of jam fell and broke. The jam went all over the cat. The cat was scared at first, but then the cat tasted the jam. The cat loved the sweet taste of the jam. Now, the cat not only relaxed in the sun but also enjoyed the yummy jam.
