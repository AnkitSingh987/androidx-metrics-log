Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Slide

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They run and jump and laugh. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Me first!" says Anna. She climbs up the ladder. She sits on the slide. She slides down very fast. She is happy. "Me too!" says Ben. He climbs up the ladder. He sits on the slide. He slides down very fast. He is not happy. He slides too fast. He falls off the slide. He hurts his knee. He cries. Anna hears Ben cry. She runs to him. She sees his knee. It is red and wet. She feels sorry for him. "It's okay, Ben," she says. "I will help you." She takes a tissue from her pocket. She wipes his knee. She gives him a hug. She kisses his knee. "There, there, Ben. You are safe. You are brave." Ben stops crying. He smiles at Anna. He hugs her back. He kisses her cheek. "Thank you, Anna. You are kind. You are my friend." They hold hands. They walk to the bench. They sit with their moms. They have a snack. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Sam. He had a big house and many toys. One day, Sam got a new toy boat. He wanted to play with it in the pool. Sam put the toy boat in the pool and said, "Look at my new toy boat, it's so big and fun!" But, the toy boat had a leak. Water went into the boat, and it started to sink. Sam did not like that. Sam tried to fix the leak, but he could not. He got mad and said, "I don't want this boat anymore, it will spoil my fun!" So, Sam threw the boat away. The toy boat was gone, and Sam was sad.

Mama was anxious. Everything was broken in the house and she didn't know how to fix it. She opened the box of tools and found a screwdriver, hammer, and some wires. Mama said to her three year old son "I'm going to try and fix the broken tv. Would you like to help me?" He smiled and said "Yes!" Mama and son worked together to fix the tv. The wires were connected, the screwdriver tightened screws, and the hammer hit the back of the tv. Suddenly, there was a loud shock and the tv came to life. Mama jumped in surprise and said "Wow, we did it! I'm so relieved". The little boy was very happy too and jumped around with joy.

One day, a big powerful owl sat in a tree. He saw a little mouse on the ground. The owl was very hungry. He wanted to chew the mouse for his dinner. The little mouse looked up and saw the owl. He was very scared. The mouse said, "Please, Mr. Owl, don't eat me! I am too small. I will not fill you up." The owl thought about it. He did not want to be hungry all night. So, the owl said, "Okay, little mouse, I will not eat you. I will find something bigger to chew." Just then, a big wind came. The wind blew the owl out of the tree. The owl was not powerful enough to fly back up. The little mouse saw the owl on the ground. He felt bad for the owl. The mouse said, "I will help you, Mr. Owl. I will find a big stick to help you get back up." The mouse found a big stick. He tried to help the owl back up. But the stick was too heavy for the little mouse. The mouse could not lift the stick. The owl could not get back up in the tree. The owl was very sad. He stayed on the ground all night. The mouse was sad too. He could not help his new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom had a long staff that he used to help him walk. One day, Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad and gloomy because she lost her toy. Tom asked Sue, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Tom wanted to help Sue find her toy. So, they walked together, with Tom using his staff to look for the toy. As they walked, Tom saw something under a bush. He used his staff to move the bush and found Sue's toy. Sue was so happy and she smiled. She said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom nodded and smiled too. They became good friends and played together every day.
