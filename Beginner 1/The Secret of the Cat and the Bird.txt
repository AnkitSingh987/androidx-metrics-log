Category: Beginner
Title: The Secret of the Cat and the Bird

One day, a modern cat with a soft paw went for a walk. This cat liked to provide help to its friends. The cat walked and walked, looking for friends to help. The cat found a dog that needed help. The dog was sad because it lost its toy. The cat used its paw to look for the toy. The cat found the toy and gave it to the dog. The dog was very happy and thanked the cat. The modern cat walked more and found a bird that was hungry. The cat wanted to provide food for the bird. The cat used its paw to get some food from a tree. The cat gave the food to the bird. The bird was very happy and thanked the cat. The cat felt good and went home, happy that it could help its friends.

---

One day, a little girl named Sue was sad. She lost her toy. Her compassionate mom saw her cry and wanted to help. She gave Sue a big hug to comfort her. "Let's go find your toy," said Mom. They looked everywhere. In the end, they found it under the bed. Sue was so happy! She hugged her toy tight. Then, Mom had a surprise. She took out a big chocolate. Sue's eyes got wide. She never saw chocolate so big! They laughed and shared the chocolate. It was the best day ever.

---

One day, a boy named Tim went to the ice cream shop with his mom. Tim saw a big cone with many colors. He asked his mom, "Can I have that big cone, please?" His mom said, "No, that is not a cheap cone. We will get a small cone." Tim was sad and he frowned. At the park, Tim saw his friend, Jess. She had a big cone too! Tim was upset. He said, "My mom got me a small cone, but I wanted a big one like yours." Jess looked at her cone and had an idea. She said, "Let's share!" Tim smiled and they both ate the ice cream together. As they ate, a dog ran up to them. The dog jumped and took a big lick of their ice cream! Tim and Jess laughed. They didn't expect the dog to do that. They were happy to share their ice cream, even if it was with a dog.

---

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide on the big slide. It was fun and fast. But today, the slide was wet. It rained last night and the slide was slippery. Tom went first. He climbed up the ladder and sat on the slide. He pushed himself down and zoomed to the bottom. He laughed and shouted, "Whee!" Lily wanted to try too. She followed Tom and sat on the slide. But she did not push herself hard enough. She slid very slowly and got stuck in the middle. She tried to wiggle and kick, but she did not move. Tom saw Lily and ran to help her. He grabbed her hand and pulled. But he pulled too hard and Lily flew off the slide. She landed on the grass with a thud. She was not hurt, but she was dirty and wet. A guard who was watching the park came over. He was wearing a hat and a badge. He looked at Lily and asked, "Are you okay, little girl?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, sir. I'm sorry. I slipped on the slide." The guard smiled and said, "It's okay. It happens to everyone. Just be careful next time. And maybe wait until the slide is dry." Tom and Lily said, "Thank you, sir." They got up and brushed themselves off. They felt embarrassed and silly. They decided to play on the swings instead. They hoped no one saw them slip on the slide.

Tim and Lily were playing in the living room while Mom was in the kitchen. They saw a shiny thing on the table. It was an ashtray. Mom used it to put her cigarettes in. "Look, Lily, it's pretty!" Tim said. He picked up the ashtray and held it in his hands. "Let me see, Tim!" Lily said. She wanted to touch the ashtray too. She reached for it, but Tim did not let her. "No, it's mine!" Tim said. He pulled the ashtray away from Lily. Lily did not like that. She pulled harder. "Give it to me, Tim!" Lily said. She was angry. She and Tim started to struggle. They pulled and pushed and shouted. "Stop it, you two!" Mom said. She heard the noise and came to the living room. She saw Tim and Lily fighting over the ashtray. She was not happy. She took the ashtray from them and put it back on the table. "That's not a toy, Tim and Lily. That's an ashtray. It's dirty and dangerous. You should not touch it. You could get hurt or make a mess. Do you understand?" Mom said. Tim and Lily nodded. They felt sorry. They did not want to make Mom angry or sad. "We're sorry, Mom. We won't touch it again. We'll be obedient." Tim and Lily said. Mom smiled. She hugged them and kissed them. "That's good, Tim and Lily. I love you. Now, why don't you play with your blocks or your dolls? They are much more fun and safe than an ashtray." Mom said. Tim and Lily agreed. They went to their toys and played nicely. They did not fight or struggle anymore. They were obedient and happy.
