Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Park and the Triangle

One day, a little triangle wanted to go on an adventure. It went to the park and saw many friends. They played and had fun together. The triangle liked to go fast and make everyone laugh. While playing, the triangle found a disgusting, smelly pile of mud. It was brown and wet. The triangle thought it was funny and jumped in the mud. It got all dirty and laughed. The other shapes didn't want to play in the mud, but they laughed too. After playing, the triangle went home. It was time for a bath to wash the disgusting mud off. The triangle splashed in the water and got clean. It was happy from the fun day at the park. The triangle could not wait to go play again.

Lily was a gifted girl. She could sing, draw and read very well. She loved to learn new things and share them with her friends. One evening, she wanted to show her friends a new book she had. It was about animals and their homes. She searched for the book in her room, but she could not find it. She searched under her bed, in her closet, on her desk, but the book was not there. She felt sad and worried. Where was her book? Did she lose it? Did someone take it? She asked her mom and dad if they knew where her book was. They said they did not know, but they would help her look for it. They searched in the living room, in the kitchen, in the bathroom, but the book was not there. They asked Lily if she remembered where she last had it. Lily thought hard and said she had it in the car when they came back from the library. They went to the car and searched in the back seat, in the trunk, in the glove box, but the book was not there. They were about to give up, when Lily saw something shiny on the floor. She bent down and picked it up. It was her book! It had fallen out of her backpack and slid under the seat. She was so happy and relieved. She hugged her book and thanked her mom and dad for helping her. She ran back to her room and called her friends. She told them she had found her book and invited them to come over. She wanted to read them the stories and show them the pictures of the animals and their homes. She was a gifted girl, and she loved to share her gifts.

One day, a bird was in a cage. The bird was sad. A cat came and saw the bird. The cat wanted to help the bird. The cat said, "I will help you get out of the cage." The bird was happy. The cat opened the cage. The bird flew out of the cage. The cat and the bird played together. They found a tree with sour fruit. The cat said, "Let's eat the sour fruit." The bird said, "Okay!" They ate the sour fruit. The cat and the bird did not like the sour fruit. The cat said, "I have an idea." The bird asked, "What is it?" The cat said, "I will put you back in the cage so you do not eat the sour fruit." The bird went back in the cage. The cat closed the cage. The cat said, "I helped you. Now you are safe from the sour fruit." The bird was sad again. The cat left the bird in the cage and went away. The bird stayed in the cage and was sad.

One day, a boy named Tom found a cool flag. It had many colors and he liked it a lot. He ran to his friend, Sue, to show her the flag. "Look what I found!" he said. Sue looked at the flag and said, "That's a cool flag, Tom! Let's play a game with it." They played a game where they took turns hiding the flag, and the other had to find it. They had a lot of fun. Then, a big wind came and took the flag away. Tom and Sue tried to catch it, but they could not. They were very sad. "I quit," said Tom. "We can't get the flag back." And just like that, the fun day was over.
