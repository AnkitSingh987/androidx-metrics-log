Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Car

Anna likes to play with her dolls. She has many dolls with long hair. She likes to comb their hair and make them look pretty. She has a pink comb that she uses for her dolls. One day, Anna is playing with her dolls in her room. She hears her mom call her for lunch. She puts her comb on the bed and runs to the kitchen. She is hungry and wants to eat. But on the way, she stumbles on a toy car. She falls down and hurts her knee. She cries and calls for her mom. Her mom comes and picks her up. She hugs her and kisses her knee. "Are you okay, Anna?" her mom asks. "It hurts, mom. I stumbled on a car," Anna says. "I'm sorry, sweetie. Let me see. It's not too bad. Just a little scratch. I'll put a band-aid on it and it will be better soon," her mom says. She takes Anna to the bathroom and cleans her knee. She puts a band-aid with a star on it. Anna smiles and says thank you. "Can I have my comb, mom?" Anna asks. "Your comb? Where is it?" her mom asks. "On the bed. I was combing my dolls' hair," Anna says. "Okay, let's go get it. Then we can have lunch," her mom says. They go back to Anna's room. But when they get there, they see something interesting. Anna's cat, Fluffy, is on the bed. He has the pink comb in his mouth. He is chewing on it and making funny noises. "Fluffy! That's my comb!" Anna says. "Meow!" Fluffy says. He drops the comb and jumps off the bed. He runs away and hides under the table. Anna and her mom laugh. They pick up the comb and see that it is not broken. Just a little wet. "Fluffy likes your comb, Anna. He thinks it's a toy," her mom says. "He can't have it. It's mine. He has his own toys," Anna says. She puts the comb in her drawer and locks it. She doesn't want Fluffy to take it again. "Come on, Anna. Let's have lunch. Maybe Fluffy will come out later and say sorry," her mom says. They go to the kitchen and have lunch. Anna tells her mom about her dolls and their hair. Her mom listens and smiles. She thinks Anna is very creative and cute. She loves her very much.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room with a big, soft carpet. In this room, there lived a happy cat named Tom and a little girl named Sue. They loved to play on the carpet all day long. One day, Tom and Sue decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could jump the highest. Tom jumped first. He jumped high, but Sue wanted to jump higher. She tried her best and jumped even higher than Tom. They both laughed and had so much fun. After they played, Sue's mom came into the room. She was very happy to see the carpet was still tidy. Sue and Tom were proud of themselves for keeping the room nice and clean. To show her thanks, Sue's mom gave them a treat. They enjoyed their treat and promised to always play nicely and keep the room tidy.

Sara and Ben like to play with their toys in their room. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big phone. The phone is modern and can ring and talk. Sara and Ben pretend to call their friends and family with the phone. One day, Sara and Ben hear a loud noise outside. They look out the window and see a big fire. The fire is near their house and it is scary. Sara and Ben are afraid and want to go to their mom and dad. But they cannot find them. They do not know what to do. They remember that their mom and dad told them to call 911 if there is an emergency. They think that the fire is an emergency. They run to their room and grab the phone. They try to dial 911, but the phone does not work. It is not a real phone, it is a toy phone. It can only ring and talk with other toys. Sara and Ben do not know that. They keep trying to call 911, but no one answers. The fire gets closer and closer to their house. The smoke and the heat make it hard to breathe. Sara and Ben start to cough and cry. They hug each other and hope that someone will come and save them. But no one comes. The phone does not ring. Sara and Ben are not safe.
