Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends, but he also liked to go on trips. One day, Spot's family said they were going on a trip. Spot was very happy and excited. Spot knew he had to pack his things to go on the trip. He got his favorite ball, his soft bed, and his yummy treats. He put them all in a big bag. Spot was ready to go. On the trip, Spot met a new friend, a cat named Whiskers. Whiskers had a question for Spot. "Do you want to play with me?" Whiskers asked. Spot said yes, and they played together all day. They had lots of fun, and Spot was very happy he packed his things for the trip.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They liked to run, jump, and have fun together. One day, they saw a big boat called a yacht. The yacht was on the water, but it could not move. Tim and Spot wanted to help the yacht. They thought and thought about how to solve the problem. They had an idea! They would ask their friends, the ducks, for help. The ducks were very good at swimming and could push the yacht. So, Tim and Spot went to the ducks and asked for help. The ducks agreed and swam to the yacht. They pushed and pushed until the yacht could move again. Tim, Spot, and the ducks were very happy that they solved the problem. They all played together and had a great day.

Tom and Amy are friends. They like to play in the park. They run, jump, slide and swing. They have a lot of fun. But one day, they see a big mud puddle. They think it is funny to splash in the mud. They get very dirty. They laugh and laugh. "Look at us!" Tom says. "We are mud monsters!" "Yes, we are!" Amy says. "We are scary and funny!" They do not see that their mom and dad are coming. Their mom and dad are not happy. They are angry. "Tom! Amy! What have you done?" their mom and dad say. "You are so dirty! You need to wash right now!" Tom and Amy are sad. They do not want to wash. They want to play more. "But we were having fun!" Tom says. "It was just a game!" Amy says. Their mom and dad shake their heads. They take them to the bathroom. They make them wash with soap and water. They scrub their hair, their face, their hands and their feet. They rinse them with clean water. They dry them with towels. Tom and Amy are clean. But they are not happy. They are tired. They do not want to play anymore. They sit on the couch. They look at each other. They feel sorry. "We were wrong," Tom says. "We should not have played in the mud." "We made a mess," Amy says. "We should have listened to our mom and dad." They hug each other. They say sorry to their mom and dad. Their mom and dad smile. They hug them back. They say, "We love you, Tom and Amy. We want you to have fun, but we also want you to be clean and safe. Next time, please be careful and follow the rules." Tom and Amy nod. They say, "We will, mom and dad. We love you too." The moral of the story is: Playing in the mud can be fun, but it can also be dirty and dangerous. It is important to respect the rules and to wash when you are dirty.

One day, a little rabbit named Tim saw a distant cliff. He wanted to go there and see what was on top. So, he started to hop, hop, hop towards the cliff. As he got closer, he met a bird named Sue. Sue asked, "Where are you going, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to go to the cliff." Sue said, "I can help you. I can fly and see the best way to go." Sue flew up and saw the path to the cliff. She told Tim, "You need to spring over the rocks to get there." Tim jumped and jumped, and with Sue's help, they made it to the top of the cliff. At the top, they saw a beautiful view of their home. They were so happy they made it. They laughed and played, enjoying the view together. When it was time to go home, Sue helped Tim find the way back. They had a fun day and became the best of friends.
