Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Bird

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a toy. This toy was a fake bird. He loved to play with it. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and his fake bird. At the park, Tim met another boy named Sam. Sam saw Tim's fake bird and wanted it. He said, "Give me your bird!" Tim did not want to give his bird away, but Sam was very big and demanded the toy. Tim was sad and gave Sam his fake bird. Tim's mom saw this and came to help. She told Sam that it was not nice to demand things from others. She showed Sam a beautiful view of the park and said, "Look at this view. The world is big and full of toys. You don't need to take from others." Sam listened and gave the fake bird back to Tim. They both learned that it is better to share and be kind to others.

One day, a big ship was out at sea. It was a very hot day. On the ship, there were two friends, Tom and Sue. They liked to play games and have fun. They saw a small shop on the ship that had cold ice cream. Tom and Sue wanted ice cream to cool down. Tom said, "Let's pay the man and get ice cream!" Sue agreed. They went to the shop and paid the man. He gave them two ice creams. They were very happy. But, they did not know that something was going to happen soon. As they ate their ice cream, they felt the ship shake. They looked around and saw a big wave coming. The wave hit the ship and made it rock back and forth. Tom and Sue held on tight. The ice cream fell, but they were safe. The ship stopped shaking, and they knew they were okay. They laughed and said, "Next time, we will pay for ice cream on a calm day!"

One day, a little boy named Tim found a basketball in the park. He was very happy. He wanted to play with it. Tim was a creative boy, so he thought of a fun game to play with his new basketball. Tim remembered that his friend, Jane, lived close to the park. He went to her house and knocked on the door. Jane came out and saw Tim with the basketball. "Hi, Tim! Can I play with you?" she asked. Tim said, "Yes, let's play a fun game!" They went to the park and played with the basketball. Tim and Jane threw the ball to each other. They laughed and had a good time. They played until they were tired. Then, they sat under a big tree and talked. "Tim, I like your game," Jane said. "You are very creative." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Jane. I am happy we played together." They both remembered that day as a fun time with the basketball.

One day, a small pupil named Tim went to school. Tim had black hair and big eyes. He liked to play with his friends and learn new things. After lunch, it was time for a nap. All the kids lay down on their mats and closed their eyes. As Tim was napping, he heard a sound. It was a little black cat. The cat said, "Hello, Tim! I can talk!" Tim was very surprised. He had never met a talking cat before. "Wow! Can you play with me?" asked Tim. The cat nodded and they started to play. They played and played, but it was time for the nap to end. Tim didn't want the cat to leave. The cat said, "Don't worry, I will come back to play again!" With a wink, the cat ran away. Tim smiled and went back to his friends. He couldn't wait to tell them about the talking cat.

One day, a little alligator named Al went to the river to play. He saw a big, red ball near the water. It looked fun, but Al was not sure if it was safe to play with. He thought, "I should taste it first to see if it is good or bad." Al took a small bite of the ball. "Ouch!" he cried. The ball was hurt and not fun to play with. Al's friend, a bird named Ben, flew down and asked, "What happened, Al?" Al told Ben, "I tried to taste the ball, but it hurt my mouth." Ben laughed and said, "Silly Al, balls are not for eating. They are for playing!" Al felt silly but learned his lesson. From that day on, Al never tried to taste things he was unsure about. He asked his friends for help instead. And they all played happily with the red ball together. The moral of the story is to ask for help when you are unsure about something, and not to taste things that are not food.
