Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Naughty Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a naughty dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with a cane. He would run, jump, and toss the cane in the air. One day, Spot saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Spot wanted to help Sue. He said, "I can find your toy. I see many things when I play with my cane." Spot and Sue looked for the toy together. They walked and talked. Spot used his cane to look under a big tree. He saw something shiny. It was Sue's toy! Sue was so happy to see her toy. She said, "Thank you, Spot! You are not naughty. You are a good dog." Spot wagged his tail and they played with the cane and the toy. They became best friends.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is black and brown. It has sharp teeth and a loud bark. "Look, a dog!" Tom says. "Let's go and pet it." "No, Tom, don't!" Jen says. "The dog might bite. It looks angry." But Tom does not listen. He marches to the dog. He wants to touch its fur. He thinks the dog is cute. The dog does not like Tom. It growls and snaps at him. It bites Tom's hand. Tom screams and cries. He runs back to Jen. His hand is red and hurts. "Tom, are you okay?" Jen asks. She is scared and sad. She hugs Tom. "No, Jen, I'm not okay," Tom says. "The dog bit me. It was not kind. It was mean." Jen helps Tom to find his mom. His mom takes him to the doctor. The doctor gives him a bandage and a shot. Tom is still in pain. The moral of the story is: Do not march to a dog you do not know. It might bite you. It might not be kind. It might be mean. Always listen to your friend. Your friend cares about you. Your friend wants you to be safe.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. She brought her toy fan with her. Sue loved her fan because it made her feel cool when it was hot outside. While they were at the park, Sue saw a big hole in the ground. She thought it would be fun to bury her fan in the hole and dig it up later. Sue put her fan in the hole and covered it with dirt. Then she ran to her mom and said, "Mom, I buried my fan in the hole. Can we come back later to dig it up?" Her mom said, "Sure, Sue. That sounds like fun." Later that day, Sue and her mom went back to the park to dig up the fan. But when they got to the hole, they found that the fan was gone! Sue felt scared and said, "Mom, where is my fan?" Just then, a friendly dog came running up to them with the fan in its mouth. The dog had dug up the fan and wanted to play with Sue. She laughed and played with the dog, happy to have her fan back.

Once upon a time, there was a gray bunny named Bob. Bob liked to hop and play with his friends. One day, Bob saw a big rug. The rug was very pretty with many colors. Bob wanted to take the rug home. Bob asked his friend, Cat, "Can you help me complete my task? I want to take the rug home." Cat said, "Yes, I will help you." So, Bob and Cat worked together to carry the rug. As they carried the rug, they met a bird. The bird said, "You should not take things that are not yours." Bob and Cat listened to the bird and put the rug back where they found it. In the end, Bob and Cat learned that it is wrong to take things that do not belong to them. They thanked the bird for teaching them this lesson and went on to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his friends in the park. They liked to run, jump, and chase each other. One day, Spot and his friends had a small problem. They could not agree on what game to play. Spot wanted to play fetch, but his friend Buddy wanted to play tag. They used the word "disagree" because they could not decide. In the end, Spot and Buddy played both games. First, they played fetch and then they played tag. They had a lot of fun and were very happy. From that day on, they knew that even if they disagreed, they could still find a way to have fun together.
