Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Hill

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with their bikes in the park. Lily had a pink bike with a basket and a bell. Ben had a blue bike with a horn and a light. They rode their bikes very fast and had fun. One day, they saw a big hill in the park. Lily wanted to go up the hill and see what was on the other side. Ben was nervous. He said, "Lily, the hill is too high and steep. We might fall and get hurt. Let's stay here and play with the flowers and the birds." Lily said, "Don't be scared, Ben. It will be fun. Follow me and we will see something new and exciting. Come on, pedal faster!" She rode her bike up the hill and waved at Ben. Ben did not want to lose his friend. He followed her and pedaled as hard as he could. He felt his bike wobble and shake. He wished he had stayed on the flat ground. He tried to catch up with Lily, but she was too far ahead. Lily reached the top of the hill and looked around. She saw a big lake with ducks and swans. She saw a bridge and a boat. She saw a playground and a slide. She smiled and shouted, "Ben, look how beautiful it is! Come and join me!" But Ben did not hear her. He was still on the hill, going down very fast. He lost control of his bike and hit a rock. He flew off his bike and landed on the ground. He felt a sharp pain in his leg and his head. He cried and called for Lily, but she did not come. He was alone and hurt. He wished he had listened to his friend.

Tim and Sue like to play with their toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is the big tank. The tank can move and make noise. They pretend the tank is in a war and they are the soldiers. One day, they play with the tank in the park. They have fun and make loud sounds. They see a dog and a bird and a flower. They forget about the tank and run to see them. An unknown boy sees the tank. He likes the tank. He thinks it is his. He takes the tank and runs away. He does not see Tim and Sue. Tim and Sue come back to the place where they left the tank. They look for the tank. They do not see the tank. They are sad and scared. They cry and call for the tank. "Where is our tank?" Tim asks. "Someone took our tank!" Sue says. They look around and see the unknown boy. He is far away. He has the tank. He smiles and plays with the tank. "Hey, that's our tank!" Tim shouts. "Give it back!" Sue screams. But the unknown boy does not hear them. He does not care. He keeps the tank and runs away. Tim and Sue are very sad. They have no tank. They have no fun. They go home and tell their mom. Their mom hugs them and says sorry. But she cannot find the tank. The tank is gone. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Tom. Tom had a big house and lots of junk. He liked to play with his junk and make new things. One day, Tom met a new friend named Sam. Tom wanted to introduce Sam to his junk. They went to Tom's big house and looked at all the junk. Sam was very happy and they played with the junk all day. Tom and Sam became best friends. They played with the junk and made new things every day. They were very happy and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary family. The family had a mom, a dad, a big sister, and a little brother. They lived in a small house with a big yard. They loved to play in the yard together. One sunny day, the family decided to mark their favorite tree in the yard. They found a big, smooth rock and used it to draw a big heart on the tree. Inside the heart, they wrote their family name. Now, everyone would know that this was their special tree. As the days passed, the family played under their marked tree. They had picnics, played games, and told stories. Even when it rained, they would sit under the tree and watch the raindrops fall. The ordinary family loved their special tree, and it made them very happy.
