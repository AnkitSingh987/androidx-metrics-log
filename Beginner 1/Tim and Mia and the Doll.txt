Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Mia and the Doll

Tim and Mia are playing with their toys in the living room. Tim has a big truck with a loud motor. Mia has a doll with a nice dress. Tim likes to make his truck go fast and make noise. Mia likes to comb her doll's hair and talk to her. "Vroom, vroom, vroom!" Tim says, as he drives his truck around the sofa. "Beep, beep, beep!" He pretends to honk the horn. "Shh, Tim, you are too loud!" Mia says, as she covers her ears. "You are scaring my doll. She is trying to sleep." "I don't care, Mia. My truck is fun. You are boring." Tim says, as he drives his truck closer to Mia and her doll. "Stop, Tim, stop!" Mia says, as she tries to protect her doll. "You are mean. Go away!" Tim does not stop. He laughs and drives his truck over Mia's doll. The doll's dress gets torn and her hair gets tangled. "NO, TIM, NO!" Mia screams, as she sees her doll get hurt. She picks up her doll and hugs her. "You are bad. You broke my doll. I hate you!" Mia's scream is so loud that their mom hears it from the kitchen. She runs to the living room and sees what happened. "Tim, what did you do?" Mom says, as she looks at Mia's doll. "You made Mia cry. You ruined her doll. Why did you do that?" "I don't know, Mom. I was just playing." Tim says, as he shrugs his shoulders. "It was just a doll." "It was not just a doll, Tim. It was Mia's doll. She loves her doll. You hurt her feelings. You need to say sorry." Mom says, as she picks up Tim and puts him in the corner. "You need to think about what you did. You need to be nice to your sister." Tim sits in the corner and feels sad. He looks at Mia and her doll. He sees that Mia is still crying and her doll is still broken. He feels sorry for what he did. He wishes he could make it better. "Mia, I'm sorry." Tim says, as he gets up from the corner. "I was wrong. I should not have hurt your doll. Can I help you fix her?" Mia looks at Tim and sees that he is sorry. She wipes her tears and nods her head. "Okay, Tim. You can help me." Mia says, as she smiles a little. "But you have to be gentle. And you have to be quiet." "Okay, Mia. I will be gentle. And I will be quiet." Tim says, as he walks to Mia and her doll. "Maybe we can find some glue and some tape. And maybe we can find a new dress. And maybe we can comb her hair. And maybe we can make her happy again." Mia and Tim work together to fix the doll. They find some glue and some tape. They find a new dress. They comb her hair. They make her happy again. "Look, Mom, we fixed the doll!" Mia and Tim say, as they show their mom the doll. "She is pretty again. And she is happy again." Mom looks at the doll and smiles. She hugs Mia and Tim and kisses them. "You did a good job, Mia and Tim. I'm proud of you." Mom says, as she praises them. "You fixed the doll. And you fixed your problem. You are nice to each other. You are good siblings." Mia and Tim hug each other and smile. They are happy again. They play with their toys in the living room. Tim plays with his truck. Mia plays with her doll. They share and take turns. They talk and laugh. They are nice to each other. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cute dog named Spot. Spot loved to nap and take walks. Spot was very attractive, and everyone liked to pet him. One day, Spot went for a walk with his friend, Tim. They walked and walked until they were very tired. They saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. Tim said, "Let's take a nap, Spot." Spot and Tim lay down under the tree and closed their eyes. They took a nice nap, and when they woke up, they felt happy and ready to play again. They knew that naps and walks were the best things ever. And they lived happily ever after.
