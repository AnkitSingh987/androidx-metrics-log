Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived with her mom, dad, and her aunt. Sue liked her aunt a lot. She was very kind and made tasty food. One day, Sue's aunt made a big cake. It was very tasty. Sue wanted to eat the cake, but her aunt said, "Not now, Sue. We will eat the cake later." Sue was sad, but she waited. Later, the aunt went out. Sue saw the cake and thought, "I will eat just a little bit." She took a big piece and ate it. Then, she heard her aunt coming back. Sue was scared. She tried to hide the cake, but it fell on the floor. The cake was all gone. Sue's aunt was sad, and Sue felt very bad.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Dizzy. Dizzy loved to sing all day long. One day, Dizzy met a big bird named Biggie. Biggie told Dizzy, "I am going to fly east to find new friends. Would you like to come with me?" Dizzy was very excited and said, "Yes! I will sing while we fly!" As they flew east, Dizzy sang a happy song. Biggie liked Dizzy's song and they became good friends. After a while, Dizzy started to feel dizzy from flying so high. Biggie noticed and said, "Let's take a break, Dizzy. We can rest and you can sing more songs." They found a nice tree to rest in. Dizzy sang a beautiful song that made them both feel happy. After they rested, they continued flying east. Dizzy and Biggie met many new friends and sang songs together. They had a great time and were very happy.

Tim and Sue wanted to play in the sand. They took their spades and buckets and ran to the big hill. The hill had a lot of sand and rocks. It looked like fun. "Let's climb the hill and make a castle!" Tim said. "OK!" Sue said. They started to climb the hill with their spades and buckets. They dug some sand and put it in their buckets. But the hill was very steep and slippery. Tim and Sue had to be careful not to fall. They held on to their spades and buckets. Suddenly, Tim saw a shiny rock. He wanted to have it. He let go of his spade and reached for the rock. "Tim, don't!" Sue shouted. "You will fall!" But Tim did not listen. He grabbed the rock and smiled. He thought he was clever. But then he lost his balance. He slipped and rolled down the hill. He dropped his rock and his bucket. He got sand in his eyes and mouth. He cried. Sue watched him and shook her head. She thought he was foolish. She climbed down the hill and helped him. She gave him some water and a hug. "Tim, you should not let go of your spade. You could have hurt yourself. And the rock was not worth it. It was just a rock." Sue said. "I'm sorry, Sue. You are right. I was foolish. I should have listened to you. Can we still play?" Tim said. "Of course we can. But let's play on the flat ground. It is safer and easier. And we can still make a castle." Sue said. They took their spades and buckets and went to the flat ground. They dug some sand and made a castle. They had fun. They learned their lesson. They were happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big mailbox. He did not know what it was. He asked his mom to explain. His mom said, "We put letters in the mailbox, and they go to our friends." One day, Tim had a fight with his friend, Sam. Tim was sad. His mom said, "Why don't you write a sorry letter to Sam?" Tim thought it was a good idea. He wrote a letter and put it in the mailbox. Sam got the letter and read it. He felt happy and comfortable. He wrote a letter back to Tim. They both learned that saying sorry can make things better. The mailbox helped them be friends again. The moral of the story is to always say sorry when you make a mistake.
