Category: Beginner
Title: The Bell and the Ring

Once upon a time, there was a big bell in a small town. The bell would ring every day. The people in the town loved the sound of the bell. But one day, a bad giant came to the town. He did not like the sound of the bell. He wanted to stop the bell from ringing. The giant took the bell and hid it away. The people in the town were very sad. They missed the sound of the bell. They knew they had to get the bell back from the bad giant. So, they made a plan to take the bell back. The people in the town waited for the giant to take a nap. When the giant was asleep, they took the bell back. They put the bell in its place and made it ring again. The bad giant woke up and heard the bell. He knew he could not win, so he left the town. The people were happy, and the bell rang every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very careful when he walked. One day, Tom went to the park to play. In the park, Tom met a small mouse named Jerry. Jerry was running fast, but Tom was careful not to step on him. Tom said, "Hi, Jerry! Let's play together!" Jerry said, "Yes, let's play!" They played and played until the sun went down. Then, they heard a loud noise. They stopped playing and there was silence. They looked around and saw a big truck arrive. Tom and Jerry were scared, so they ran home together. At home, they were safe and happy. They played more games until it was time for bed. Tom and Jerry were good friends and they had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max was very strong and very fast. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Max found a mask. The mask was big and red. Max put on the mask and felt very special. He ran to the park to show his friends. When he got there, his friends were playing with a ball. They asked Max if he would lend them his mask to play with. Max said yes, because he was a good friend. They all took turns wearing the mask and playing with the ball. They laughed and had a lot of fun. Max was happy he could share his mask with his friends. The mighty dog with the big red mask was the best friend anyone could have.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. She was three years old and loved playing outside on summer days. One day, Jane was at the station with her father waiting for the train when she spotted a strange looking box. She asked her father what it was and he told her it was an ice cream box. Jane's eyes lit up and she asked for one. Her father said, "Sure, as long it isn't sour!" They bought two cones and started walking down the street. The sun was bright and the ice cream started to melt. Jane was laughing as she watched the ice cream drip down her cone. Then they heard a voice say, "Oh no! It looks like someone's ice cream is melting!" It was an old man with a cart full of ice cream. He said, "How about trying a different flavor? I have a special one that isn't sour at all!” He handed Jane a cone of lemon sorbet. She took one lick and smiled. “It’s so sweet!” she said. Then they thanked the old man and continued on their way. Jane finished her sorbet before they even reached the station. She was so happy and her summer day was complete.

One day, a boy named Mark went to the park. He liked to jump and play with his friends. They played a fun game called "Jump and Catch". Mark was very good at it. But then, a big bad dog came to the park. The dog wanted to play too, but he was not good at the game. He would jump and try to catch the ball, but he would miss and fall down. The other kids started to laugh at the dog. The dog felt bad and sad. Mark saw this and decided to help the dog. He taught the dog how to jump and catch the ball. Soon, the big bad dog was not bad at the game anymore. The dog was happy, and Mark and his friends had a new friend to play with.
