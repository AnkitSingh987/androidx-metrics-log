Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Sheep and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a big field, there were many sheep. They were very noisy. They liked to play and run all day. One day, a small sheep named Tim wanted to pass a big rock. The other sheep said, "No, you are too little." Tim felt sad, but he did not give up. He tried again and again to pass the big rock. The other sheep laughed at him. But Tim kept trying. He pushed and pushed until he finally passed the big rock. The other sheep stopped laughing. They saw that Tim was strong and brave. They learned that even a small sheep can do big things. The moral of the story is to never give up, even when others don't believe in you.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make roads and bridges with blocks and books. They have fun. One day, Tom sees a new car in Sam's room. It is big and shiny and red. Tom wants to play with it. He asks Sam, "Can I have your red car?" Sam says, "No, it is mine. You have your own cars. Go away." Tom is angry. He says, "You are mean. I want your red car. Give it to me." Sam says, "No, you can't have it. It is my will. You have to yield." Tom does not listen. He runs to Sam and tries to grab the red car. Sam holds it tight. They pull and push and shout. They do not see the blocks and books on the floor. They trip and fall. The red car flies out of their hands and hits the window. The window breaks. The glass cuts their faces and hands. They cry and bleed. Mom hears the noise and comes to see. She sees the broken window and the blood and the red car. She is very angry and sad. She says, "What have you done? You are bad boys. You have to go to the doctor. And you have to pay for the window. And you have to say sorry. And you have no more cars. And you have no more playtime. And you have no more friends." Tom and Sam are sorry. They wish they had been nice and shared. They wish they had been slow and careful. They wish they had not broken the window and hurt themselves. But it is too late. They have no more fun. They have a bad ending.

Sara and Ben are in the car with Mom and Dad. They are going to the zoo. Sara and Ben love animals. They look out the window and see many things. They see houses, trees, shops, and cars. "Look, Sara, a billboard!" Ben says. He points to a big sign with a picture of a lion. The lion has a fierce face and a big mane. It says: "Come to the zoo and see the king of the jungle!" "I want to see the lion!" Sara says. She twists in her seat and tries to see the billboard better. She likes lions. They are strong and brave. "Me too!" Ben says. He twists in his seat too. He likes lions. They are loud and fast. "Stop twisting, kids!" Mom says. She looks at them in the mirror. "You need to sit still and wear your seatbelts. We will be at the zoo soon." "Okay, Mom," Sara and Ben say. They sit still and wear their seatbelts. They are excited to see the lion and the other animals. They hope the lion will roar for them.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat. The small cat lived in a house with a girl. One day, the girl made muffins. They smelled so good! The small cat wanted a muffin too. The girl saw the small cat and wanted to give it a muffin. She picked up a muffin and walked to the small cat. But, she did not see a toy on the floor. She stepped on the toy and dropped the muffin. The small cat was sad. But, the girl picked up the muffin and gave it to the small cat. The small cat was happy! They both sat and ate their muffins together. The small cat and the girl were best friends.
