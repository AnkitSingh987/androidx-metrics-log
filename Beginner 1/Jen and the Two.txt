Category: Beginner
Title: Jen and the Two

Once upon a time, two best friends were living happily in a small village. Little Jen and Benny always played together and never got into any trouble. One day, Jen’s Momma started to weep. Benny asked Jen, “What’s wrong with your mom?” Jen replied in a sad voice, “She is worried because the village is in danger! A missile is coming!” Benny was scared and said, “What do we do?” Jen looked worried and said, “My Momma said we have to find somewhere safe. We must be careful, the missile is very harsh!” The two friends hugged each other and started to weep. They were scared but Jen knew they had to get away so they could stay safe. The two best friends quickly ran away from the village, never to return.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to swim in the lake near their home. One day, they see a big boat on the water. It makes a lot of noise and smoke. "Look, Ben, a boat!" Lila says. "Let's swim closer and see it!" Ben says. They swim fast and reach the boat. But the water is not clean. It is dirty and oily. It smells bad. It hurts their eyes and skin. "Yuck, this water is gross!" Lila says. "Let's go back to the shore!" Ben says. They swim away from the boat and towards the shore. But the shore is not clean either. It is full of trash and cans and bottles. They are sad and angry. "Who did this to our lake?" Lila says. "I don't know, but it's not fair!" Ben says. They find a spot where the shore is a little cleaner. They dry themselves with their towels and sit on the grass. They look at the lake and the boat. "Maybe we can do something to help our lake," Lila says. "Like what?" Ben says. "Maybe we can tell our parents and teachers and friends. Maybe we can pick up some trash and put it in a bin. Maybe we can make a sign that says 'Keep our lake clean!'" Lila says. "That sounds good, Lila. Let's do that!" Ben says. They smile and hug. They are still friends. They still like to swim in the lake. But they want to make it clean and beautiful again.

Tom and Mia like to play soccer in the park. They have a ball and two goals. They take turns to kick the ball and try to score. One day, they see a big black dog near the goals. The dog is barking and running around. Tom and Mia are scared of the dog. They do not want to play soccer anymore. "Let's go home," Mia says to Tom. "The dog is too loud and mean." But Tom wants to get the ball back. He likes the ball. It is his favorite toy. He says to Mia, "I will get the ball. You wait here." Tom steps slowly towards the ball. He hopes the dog will not see him. He tries to be quiet and brave. But the dog sees Tom. The dog runs to Tom and jumps on him. Tom falls down on the pitch. The dog licks Tom's face and wags his tail. He is not mean. He is friendly. Tom is surprised and happy. He laughs and pets the dog. He says to the dog, "You are a good dog. Do you want to play soccer with us?" The dog barks and nods. He likes to play soccer too. He gives the ball to Tom with his mouth. Tom calls Mia. He says to Mia, "Come here. The dog is nice. He wants to play with us." Mia comes to Tom and the dog. She is not scared anymore. She smiles and hugs the dog. She says to the dog, "You are a cute dog. What is your name?" The dog has a tag on his collar. It says, "Rex". Rex is his name. Tom and Mia say to Rex, "Hello, Rex. We are Tom and Mia. Do you want to be our friend?" Rex barks and wags his tail. He wants to be their friend. Tom and Mia and Rex play soccer together. They have fun. They are happy. They are friends.
