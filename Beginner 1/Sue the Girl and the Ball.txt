Title: Sue the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a wish. She wanted to grab the big, red ball that was stuck in the tree. But Sue was too small to reach it. One day, a loud bird came and sat on a branch near the ball. Sue said, "Please, loud bird, can you help me grab the ball?" The bird looked at Sue and said, "I can help, but first, you must help me find some food." Sue and the bird worked together. They found food for the bird, and then the bird helped Sue grab the ball. They both were very happy. The moral of the story is that when you help others, they will help you too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pet fish named Bob. Today, Sue saw that Bob was not moving. He was dead. Sue was very sad. Sue's mom saw her sad face and knew she had to help. She told Sue that they would change Bob's water to see if it would help him. Sue and her mom changed the water, but Bob still did not move. Sue's mom said they could get a new fish to make Sue happy again. They went to the store and picked a new fish. Sue named him Sam. She was happy to have a new friend. Sue learned that sometimes things change, but it is okay because new things can make you happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. In the room, there was a big screen. The screen showed funny pictures. A little boy named Tim lived in the house with the messy room. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. They liked to tease each other. They laughed and played near the screen. "You are messy like the room!" Sam said to Tim. Tim laughed and said, "You are messy too!" Tim's mom came in and saw the messy room. She said, "You two need to clean up!" Tim and Sam cleaned the room together. They put away their toys and made the room nice. Then they could watch the funny pictures on the screen. They were happy and had a great day.

Once upon a time there was a tired little rabbit called Tim. He had big furry ears and a soft, fluffy tail. Tim was getting very scared because he heard a loud noise outside. He trembled as he hid under a tree. He put his paws over his mouth to keep quiet. The noise stopped and Tim slowly came out of hiding. He was relieved but he was also very tired. He yawned as he hopped up to a nearby bush. He was happy to find a cozy nest to sleep in. He snuggled up in the nest and fell fast asleep. In his dream, he was hopping around in a wide, open field, free and happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim was sad. He wanted to play outside, but it was raining. Tim's mom said, "We can't go out now, it's wet and there is lightning." Tim looked out the window and saw a little dog. The dog was running in the rain. Tim said, "Look, mom! The dog is running in the rain. Can we run too?" Mom said, "No, it's not safe." Just then, the rain stopped and the sun came out. Tim was happy. He ran outside and saw the little dog. The dog was not a real dog, it was a toy dog! Tim laughed and played with his new friend.

Tim and Lily were playing with magnets in their room. They liked to stick them on the fridge, the door, and the toys. They had big magnets and small magnets, red magnets and blue magnets. One day, they found a weird magnet in a box. It was green and round, and it had a button on it. Tim wanted to press the button, but Lily said no. "Don't press it, Tim. It might be bad," Lily said. "Why not? It looks fun," Tim said. He reached for the magnet, but Lily pulled it away. "Give it to me, Lily. I want to see what it does," Tim said. He tried to grab the magnet, but Lily ran away. "Stop, Tim. You don't know what it is," Lily said. She ran to the closet and opened the door. She hid behind the clothes with the magnet. Tim followed her and opened the door too. He looked for Lily and the magnet. He saw them behind a dress. He moved the dress and smiled. "Ha, I found you, Lily. Now give me the magnet," Tim said. He reached for the magnet, but Lily pressed the button. Suddenly, the magnet made a loud noise and a bright light. Tim and Lily felt a strong pull. The magnet pulled them and everything else in the closet to it. They screamed and held on to each other. "Help, help! The magnet is weird!" Tim and Lily shouted.
