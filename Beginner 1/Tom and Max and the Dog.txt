Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Max and the Dog

One day, a silly cat named Tom went to visit his friend, a dog named Max. Tom liked to play and have fun. Max was a big, happy dog who loved to run and jump. When Tom got to Max's house, he asked, "Max, can we play?" Max said, "Yes, let's play!" They played with a big red ball. They threw the ball and chased it. They laughed and had lots of fun. After playing, they were very tired. Tom said, "Max, let's sit and rest." They sat under a big tree and watched the birds fly in the sky. They had a nice visit and were happy to be friends.

The little boy was so excited to go outside and play in the sun. He ran up to the park, but then suddenly he noticed a steak lying on the sidewalk. He had never seen a steak before, so he was very confused. "What is this?" he asked. A man nearby saw him and answered, "That's a steak." The little boy peered at the steak and said, "Can I take it home?" The man shook his head and said, "No, sorry. Someone must have been so upset they left it here." The little boy didn't understand why someone would leave a steak on the sidewalk. He sadly ran home, but then he remembered that he still had the park to play in. So he ran to the park and enjoyed the day.

Once upon a time, there was a flexible snake named Sam. Sam lived in a big tree in a green park. He had a long body and a small lip. One sunny day, Sam met a friendly bird named Bob. Bob asked Sam, "Can you help me supply food for my family?" Sam said, "Yes, I can help you." Together, they went to find food. Sam used his flexible body to reach the high fruits. Bob used his beak to pick up the fruits. They were a great team. In the end, they supplied enough food for Bob's family. They became the best of friends.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their toys. They liked to make sand castles and dig holes. They had a bucket, a shovel, and some cups. "Look, Lily, I made a big castle!" Tom said, showing his work. "It's very nice, Tom. Can I help you?" Lily asked. "Yes, you can. Here, take this cup and fill it with water. We can make a flood around the castle," Tom said. Lily nodded and ran to the fountain. She filled the cup with water and came back. She poured the water around the castle, making a moat. "Wow, that's cool! Our castle is like a rich castle, with a moat and a bridge," Lily said. "Yes, it is. But we have to be careful. The water might remove our castle if it's too much," Tom said. They played some more, adding more water and sand. They had fun, until they heard a voice. "Tom, Lily, it's time to go home!" It was their mom, calling them from the bench. "Okay, mom, we're coming!" Tom and Lily said. They picked up their toys and ran to their mom. They gave her a hug and a kiss. "Did you have fun, my sweeties?" Mom asked. "Yes, mom, we did. We made a rich castle with a flood and a bridge," Tom and Lily said. "That sounds wonderful. But look, you're all wet and dirty. Let's go home and remove your clothes and take a bath," Mom said. "Okay, mom, let's go," Tom and Lily said. They followed their mom to the car, holding hands. They were happy and tired. They had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, red ball. She loved to play with her ball in the park. One day, while playing with her ball, she saw a glass on the ground. The glass was very pretty and shiny. Sue picked up the glass and said, "Hello, pretty glass! Can you speak to me?" To her surprise, the glass spoke! "Yes, I can speak," said the glass. Sue was so happy to have a talking glass. They played together all day long. But one day, Sue's mom told her that the glass was fake. Sue was sad, but then she had an idea. She would pretend that the glass could still speak. Sue and the fake glass played and talked every day, and they were very happy.
