Title: Spot the Dog and the Tree

One day, a little dog named Spot went to play in the park. Spot had four legs and loved to run and jump. He saw a big tree and wanted to play with it. Spot ran to the tree and started to dig with his dirty legs. He dug and dug, making a big hole. Then, a little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Spot and said, "Hi, Spot! What are you doing?" Spot looked up from his hole and said, "I am digging! I want to stand on my back legs and touch the tree with my front legs!" Sue wanted to help Spot. So, she stood by the tree and held Spot's front legs. Spot stood on his back legs and touched the tree with his front legs. They both laughed and played. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the tree. The tree moved and a big, colorful ball fell out of the branches. Spot and Sue were very surprised! They did not know the ball was in the tree. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. Spot forgot about his dirty legs and was happy to have a new friend and a new toy.
