Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bus

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a fearful boy. He was scared of many things. One day, Tim went on a big bus with his mom. He was scared, but his mom held his hand. On the bus, Tim's mom found a seat for them. "Let's sit here, Tim," she said. They sat next to a big window. Tim liked to look out the window. He saw trees, houses, and other cars go by. Tim started to feel less scared. A kind lady sat next to them. She saw that Tim was fearful. "Don't worry, little boy. The bus is safe," she said with a smile. Tim smiled back. He felt better. The bus ride was not so scary after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, his mom asked him to polish the big spoons. Tim was very happy to help. While Tim was polishing the spoons, he saw a white thing on the table. It was salt. He thought, "Maybe the salt will make the spoons shiny." So, he put some salt on the spoons and rubbed them. But the spoons did not get shiny. They got dirty! Tim felt ashamed. He knew he made a mistake. He told his mom what happened. His mom smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. We all make mistakes. Let's clean the spoons together." So, Tim and his mom cleaned the spoons, and they became shiny again. Tim learned that it's important to ask for help when he doesn't know what to do.

One day, a crazy bird found a missile. The bird did not know what it was. He thought it was a big toy. The bird wanted to play with it. He wanted to find a match for his new toy. The bird went to his friend, the cat. The cat said, "I can play with you, but I don't want to play with that toy. It looks scary." The bird did not listen. He wanted to find a match for his missile. Finally, the bird found a big tree. He thought the tree would be a good match for his missile. The bird pushed the missile near the tree. The cat came running and said, "Stop! That toy is not safe. It can hurt us." The bird listened to the cat. He left the missile and played with the cat instead. They had fun playing together. The bird learned that some toys are not safe to play with, and it is important to listen to friends when they try to help.

One day, a little boy named Tim felt dizzy. He did not know why. He told his mom, "Mom, I feel dizzy." His mom knew what to do. She went to the kitchen and got a pill for Tim. Tim did not like pills. He said, "No, Mom! I don't want the pill!" But his mom said, "Tim, you need the pill to feel better." Tim was not happy, but he took the pill. Later, Tim and his mom went to the park. They saw a big truck. The truck was loading dirt. Tim watched and soon, he forgot about the pill. He was not dizzy anymore. Tim and his mom had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little sunflower. The sunflower was nervous because it was not as big as the other flowers in the garden. It wanted to grow tall and strong like its friends. One day, a nice boy came to the garden. He saw the nervous sunflower and wanted to help it. He thought maybe he could replace the old dirt around the sunflower with new dirt. He thought this might help the sunflower grow big and strong. The boy replaced the dirt and gave the sunflower some water. The sunflower started to feel better. It was not as nervous as before. Soon, the sunflower grew tall and strong, just like its friends in the garden. The sunflower and the boy were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his blocks. One day, he found some hidden blocks under his bed. "Wow!" he said, "I didn't know these were here!" Tim played with the hidden blocks all day. Then, his friend Sam came over. "Look at my new blocks!" Tim said. Sam looked at the blocks and said, "Hey, those blocks belong to me! I lost them when I came over to play last week." Tim was sad. He didn't want to give the blocks back. But he knew it was the right thing to do. So, he gave the blocks to Sam. Sam smiled and said, "Thank you! Let's play together with my blocks." Tim smiled too, and they played together all day. The end.
