Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Pastry

One day, a little girl named Mia was very eager to help her mom. Her mom was making a pastry in the kitchen. Mia said, "Mom, can I help you make the pastry?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia. You can help me. First, we need to clean the table." Mia and her mom cleaned the table together. They were happy. But then, Mia's big brother Tom came into the kitchen. Tom was not nice. He took the pastry and ran away. Mia was sad. She said, "Mom, Tom took our pastry!" Her mom said, "Don't worry, Mia. We will make a new pastry together." So they made a new pastry and it was even better than the first one. Tom saw the new pastry and said sorry. Mia, her mom, and Tom all ate the pastry together and they were happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sam. Sam lived in a house near a big tree. They liked to play catch with a red ball. The sun was shining, but the wind was a bit harsh. Tim threw the ball to Sam. Sam tried to catch it, but the wind made the ball fly away. They both laughed and ran after the ball. They were having so much fun. After playing for a while, Tim and Sam went back to Sam's house. They were tired but happy. They had a great visit and couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Wednesday. She was a light dog, not too big and not too small. Wednesday loved to play with her favorite toy, a red ball. One day, Wednesday saw a cat playing with a string. The cat would pull on the string, and the string would move. Wednesday thought it was fun and wanted to try it too. She asked the cat, "Can I play with the string too?" The cat said, "Sure, but be careful. This string is not like your ball." Wednesday started to pull the string, just like the cat. But as she pulled, the string got tighter and tighter. She didn't know what was happening, but she kept pulling. The cat watched and didn't say anything. Suddenly, the string snapped! It hit Wednesday's face and hurt her. She cried and went back to her red ball. The cat knew the string was not a good toy for Wednesday, but didn't stop her. From that day on, Wednesday was more careful with new toys and always remembered to ask for help when trying something new.

One day, a cat saw a big puddle. The cat was very thirsty. It wanted to drink from the puddle. But a dog was lying by the puddle. The dog was big and mean. The cat was scared of the dog. The cat thought it was foolish to go near the dog. But it was so thirsty. So, the cat made a plan. It jumped on a tree branch near the puddle. The cat hoped the dog would not see it. But the dog saw the cat. The dog barked and jumped at the tree. The cat fell into the puddle. The cat was wet and sad. The dog laughed at the cat. The cat should have found another puddle.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy ball. One day, Spot saw a big piece of paper on the floor. He wanted to have fun with it. Spot's friend, Sam, came over and said, "Let's fold the paper and make a toy." Spot barked, "Yes, let's start!" They folded the paper together, making a nice toy for them to play with. But as they played, the paper toy got wet and tore apart. Spot and Sam were sad. Their fun day had a bad ending. They learned that paper toys don't last long.

Sam and Mia were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were cooking and serving food. Sam found a big cup on the shelf. He wanted to fill it with water and give it to Mia. "Look, Mia, I have a cup for you!" he said. Mia looked at the cup. It was shiny and had a handle. But it also had something else. It had a sharp edge. It was a broken cup. "Sam, that cup is bad. It can hurt you. Put it back!" she said. But Sam did not listen. He wanted to play with the cup. He turned on the tap and filled the cup with water. He did not see the sharp edge. He did not feel the water dripping from the crack. He walked to Mia with the cup. "Here, Mia, drink this!" he said. Mia saw the water coming out of the cup. She saw the sharp edge. She was scared. She did not want the cup. "Sam, no! That cup is bad. It can hurt me. Take it away!" she said. But Sam did not listen. He wanted to give the cup to Mia. He pushed the cup to her face. He did not mean to hurt her. He wanted to share. But the cup was bad. It was sharp. It cut Mia's lip. Mia felt pain. She screamed. She dropped the cup. The cup broke into more pieces. The water spilled on the floor. Mia and Sam were both crying. They were both hurt. They were both scared. The kitchen was messy. The cup was bad. Mom heard the noise. She ran to the kitchen. She saw Mia and Sam. She saw the blood. She saw the broken cup. She was angry. She was sad. She was worried. "What did you do?" she asked. "Why did you play with the broken cup? You know it is dangerous. You could have hurt yourself more. You could have hurt each other more. You are very naughty. You are very silly. You are in big trouble." Mom took Mia and Sam to the bathroom. She cleaned their wounds. She put bandages on them. She hugged them. She loved them. But she was still angry. She was still sad. She was still worried. She took them to their room. She put them in their beds. She said they had to stay there. They had to think about what they did. They had to say sorry. They had to be good. Mia and Sam did not like their room. They did not like their beds. They did not like their bandages. They did not like their mom. They did not like the cup. They did not like the water. They did not like the kitchen. They did not like the noise. They did not like the pain. They did not like the trouble. They did not like the story. It was a bad story. It had a bad ending.
