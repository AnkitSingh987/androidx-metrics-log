Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little car with four wheels. One day, one wheel felt terrible. The other wheels saw this and felt sad for their friend. A kind bird saw the sad wheels and flew down. The bird whispered to the wheels, "I can help your friend feel better." The wheels were happy to hear that. The kind bird took the terrible wheel and fixed it. The little car and all the wheels were very happy. They all thanked the kind bird and went on their way, feeling good again.

Tom and Sue like to play with plastic blocks. They have many colors and shapes of blocks. They can make houses, cars, animals and more. They like to add more and more blocks to their creations. One day, they find a weird block in their box. It is white and round and has a hole in the middle. They do not know what it is for. They try to add it to their house, but it does not fit. They try to add it to their car, but it falls off. They try to add it to their animal, but it looks funny. "What is this block?" Tom asks Sue. "I don't know," Sue says. "Maybe it is a donut." "A donut?" Tom says. "But it is not sweet. And it is not soft. And it is not yummy." "Maybe it is a broken donut," Sue says. They decide to throw the weird block away. They do not want it to ruin their play. But when they go to the trash can, they see something amazing. There is a big pile of white and round blocks with holes in the middle. They are all the same as the weird block. And they are all connected by a long string. "What are these?" Tom asks Sue. "I don't know," Sue says. "But they look like fun." They grab the string and pull it out of the trash can. They see that the blocks make a long necklace. They put it around their necks and laugh. They feel like kings and queens. They do not know that the weird block was a bead. And that the necklace was a gift from their mom. And that she was looking for it all day. And that she will be very surprised when she sees them.

Once upon a time, in a wide land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to march all day long. One day, Tim saw a big hill and wanted to march up the hill. As Tim marched up the hill, he saw ice on the ground. The ice was cold and shiny. Tim thought it was pretty. He wanted to march on the ice too. So, Tim stepped on the ice and started to march. But, the ice was very slippery. Tim tried to march on the ice, but he fell down. He got hurt and cried. Tim's march on the ice was not fun. And that was the end of Tim's bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a tall bus. The bus loved to joke and make the kids happy. Every morning, the bus would pick up the kids and take them to school. The kids would laugh and smile because of the bus's jokes. One day, a small car came to the bus stop. The car was not tall like the bus, and it did not know how to joke. The kids did not laugh and smile when they saw the car. The bus saw the car and felt sad for it. The bus decided to teach the car how to joke. They practiced together, and soon the car learned how to make the kids laugh and smile too. The kids were happy with both the tall bus and the small car. The moral of the story is that we can help our friends learn new things and make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. One day, Jack was feeling sad. Jill looked at him and asked, "What's wrong, Jack?" Jack said, "I don't feel good. Can you help me?" Jill smiled. "I know what will make you feel better!" She reached into her bag and pulled out a cup. It was very smooth and beautiful. Jack smiled. "What is it?" he asked. Jill said, "It's a special cup. Hold it in your hands and close your eyes. Feel how smooth it is and imagine you're happy. That will make you feel better." Jack held the cup and closed his eyes. He felt the smooth cup and started to feel better. When he opened his eyes, he was smiling again! Jill said, "See? Now you feel better, right?" Jack smiled and said, "Yes, I feel much better now!"
