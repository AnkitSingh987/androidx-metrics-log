Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Bike

Lila and Ben are adventurous twins. They like to explore new places and play with new things. One day, they find a big bike in the garage. It has two wheels, a seat, and a handle. "Wow, look at this bike!" Lila says. "It is so big and shiny. Can we ride it?" "I don't know," Ben says. "It looks hard to ride. And we don't have helmets." Lila does not care. She wants to try the bike. She climbs on the seat and holds the handle. She tries to move the pedals, but they are too far. She pushes the handle, but nothing happens. "Ben, how do you start this bike?" Lila asks. Ben shrugs. He does not know how to start the bike. He sees a button on the handle. He thinks it might be the start button. He presses it. Vroom! The bike makes a loud noise and moves forward. Lila screams and holds on tight. Ben runs after her. "Lila, stop the bike!" Ben shouts. "You are going too fast!" Lila does not know how to stop the bike. She sees a tree in front of her. She is scared. She pulls the handle hard. Screech! The bike stops suddenly and falls over. Lila and Ben tumble to the ground. They are not hurt, but they are dirty and scared. "Are you okay, Lila?" Ben asks. "I'm okay, Ben," Lila says. "But that was not fun. That was scary. I don't like this bike." "Me neither," Ben says. "Let's go back to the garage and find something else to play with. Something safer and quieter." They leave the bike on the ground and run back to the garage. They decide to play with some balls and blocks instead. They are still adventurous, but they are also careful. They do not want to start another bike again.

Once, there was a nosy cat named Sam. Sam liked to play with a top, a toy that spins. One day, Sam saw a big box near the door. It had a word on it, "mail." Sam did not know what "mail" was, but he was very nosy. Sam played with his top, but he kept looking at the box. He wanted to know what was inside. He pushed the top closer and closer to the box. The top hit the box, and the box opened a little bit. Sam saw something shiny inside. Later, Sam's friend, a dog named Max, came to play. Max saw the box and said, "That's my mail!" Max opened the box and took out a new top. He said, "Thank you for finding my new toy!" Sam and Max played with their tops and had lots of fun.

One day, an ordinary boy named Tom went to play in the park with his ball. He liked to throw the ball high in the air and catch it again. He was very good at catching the ball. He saw a well in the park. It was a big hole in the ground with a wall around it and a bucket on a rope. He wondered what was inside the well. He threw his ball over the wall and into the well. He heard a splash. "Oh no!" he said. "My ball is gone!" He ran to the well and looked down. He saw his ball floating on the water. He tried to reach it with his hand, but it was too far. He pulled the rope and the bucket came up. He put the bucket down and climbed inside. He hoped the bucket would take him to his ball. But the bucket was too heavy for the rope. The rope broke and the bucket fell into the well. Tom screamed. "Help! Help!" he shouted. A girl named Anna heard him. She was walking by the park with her dog. She ran to the well and saw Tom in the bucket. "Don't worry!" she said. "I will help you!" She tied her dog's leash to the rope and threw it down to Tom. Tom grabbed the leash and Anna pulled him up. She also pulled up his ball. "Thank you!" Tom said. "You are very kind!" "You're welcome!" Anna said. "Do you want to play with me and my dog?" "Yes, please!" Tom said. "That would be fun!" They became friends and played with the ball and the dog. They never threw the ball into the well again. They learned to be careful and happy.
