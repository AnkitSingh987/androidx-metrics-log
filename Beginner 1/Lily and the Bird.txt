Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Bird

Lily was hungry. She wanted to eat toast with jam. She asked her mom for toast. Her mom said, "OK, Lily, I will make toast for you. But you have to wait a little bit. The bread is not ready yet." Lily did not want to wait. She saw a loaf of bread on the table. She thought, "I can make toast by myself. I can bite the bread and make it warm and crunchy." She took the bread and bit it hard. But the bread was not soft and sweet. It was hard and bitter. Lily did not like it. She spit it out. She said, "Yuck! This bread is bad. I don't want it." Her mom heard Lily. She came to the table. She saw the bread with Lily's bite marks. She was angry. She said, "Lily, what did you do? You bit the bread. That is not bread for toast. That is bread for the birds. It has seeds and nuts in it. You could have hurt your teeth. And you wasted the bread. Now the birds have nothing to eat." Lily felt sorry. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I was hungry. I wanted toast. I did not know it was bird bread." Her mom said, "I'm sorry, too, Lily. But you have to listen to me. You have to wait for the toast. And you have to ask before you take something. You can't just bite anything you see. That is not nice. And that is not safe." Lily nodded. She said, "OK, mom. I will listen. I will wait. I will ask. Can I have toast now?" Her mom said, "No, Lily. You can't have toast now. The bread is gone. You bit it all. There is no more bread for toast. You have to eat something else. Maybe some cereal or some fruit." Lily was sad. She did not want cereal or fruit. She wanted toast. But she had no toast. She had only bird bread. And she did not like bird bread. She wished she had waited for the toast. She wished she had not bitten the bread. She learned her lesson. She did not bite anything again.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken named Chicky. Chicky liked to imagine things all day long. One day, Chicky imagined a big, dark cloud in the sky. The dark cloud scared Chicky. Chicky did not like the dark. Chicky ran to find his mommy to tell her about the dark cloud. On his way, he saw his friend, Bunny. Bunny asked, "Why are you scared, Chicky?" Chicky told Bunny about the dark cloud he imagined. Bunny said, "Don't worry, Chicky. It's just your imagination!" Chicky felt better and stopped being scared. They both played and had fun all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy dog named Max. Max loved to perform tricks for his friends. One day, he found a secret toy in the yard. He wanted to show it to his friend, Cat. Max said to Cat, "Look! I found a secret toy. Let's play with it." Cat was not happy. She said, "No, that toy is mine! You cannot play with it." Max was sad and did not know what to do. Max thought of a plan. He said, "Let's share the toy! We can both play with it." Cat thought about it and said, "Okay, we can share." So, Max and Cat played with the secret toy together, and they were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a king. The king was big and strong. He liked to prepare food for his friends. One day, he made a big meal for them. They all sat down to eat and talk. While they were eating, a dangerous lion came to the king's house. The lion was hungry and wanted to eat the king's food. The king's friends were scared, but the king was brave. He stood up to face the lion. But then, something unexpected happened. The lion did not want to hurt the king or his friends. The lion just wanted some food. The king gave the lion some food, and they all became friends. The dangerous lion was not so dangerous after all. They all had a great time eating and playing together.
