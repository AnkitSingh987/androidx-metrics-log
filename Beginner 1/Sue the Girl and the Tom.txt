Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in her yard. In her yard, there was a small patch of unknown flowers. Sue wanted to raise them and see what they would become. One day, Sue's friend, Tom, came over to play. Tom saw the patch of unknown flowers and asked Sue, "What are these?" Sue said, "I don't know, but I want to raise them and see what they become." Sue and Tom took care of the flowers every day. They gave them water and love. Soon, the unknown flowers grew big and tall. They turned into beautiful sunflowers. Sue and Tom were so happy to see their hard work pay off. They smiled and played in the sunflower patch all day long.

One day, a dog named Spot went to a big pond. Spot was very hungry. He saw a fish in the water. Spot wanted to catch the fish to eat. Spot said, "I will dive into the water to get the fish." He put on his collar and jumped into the pond. The fish saw Spot and swam away fast. Spot tried to dive again, but the fish was too quick. Spot said, "I am still hungry, but I cannot catch the fish." He went home to eat his dog food.

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. The hall was very safe. In the hall, there were many kids. They liked to play and have fun. One day, a boy named Tom wanted to design. He liked to draw and make things. He asked his friends to help him. They all said yes. They were happy to help. Tom and his friends made many designs. They drew on paper and made things with blocks. The hall was filled with their art. They had a great time. The end.

One night, Tom and Lily went to the park with Mom and Dad. They wanted to see the stars and the moon. They brought a flashlight and a blanket. They heard a sound in the trees. Hoo, hoo, hoo. It was an owl. Tom and Lily were curious. They asked Mom and Dad what the owl was saying. Mom and Dad said the owl was asking who was there. They said they could answer the owl. They said hoo, hoo, hoo back to the owl. The owl heard them and flew out of the trees. It was a big owl. It had brown feathers and yellow eyes. It looked at Tom and Lily and said hoo, hoo, hoo again. Tom and Lily smiled and said hoo, hoo, hoo too. They felt happy and brave. They made a new friend. The owl flew away and said goodbye. Tom and Lily said goodbye too. They thanked Mom and Dad for the fun night. They went home and dreamed of the owl.

Once upon a time, there was a tall man. He lived in a small house with a big gate. Every day, he went out to play with his friends. One day, he saw a little cat. The cat was sad and alone. "Hello, cat," said the tall man. "Why are you sad?" The cat blinked its eyes and looked at the man. "I can't find my way home," said the cat. "Can you help me?" The tall man smiled and said, "Of course! Let's find your home." They walked together, looking for the cat's home. They passed the big gate and went down the street. Soon, they found the cat's home. The cat's family was there, and they were so happy to see the cat. They thanked the tall man for helping their cat find its way home. From that day on, the tall man and the cat were the best of friends. They played together every day, and the cat was never sad again.

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. Toys were everywhere. A little girl named Amy lived there. She liked to play all day. But at night, she did not like the dark. One night, Amy could not sleep. She started to think. "What if I clean my room?" she thought. "Then I will not be scared at night." So, she began to pick up her toys. Amy put her toys in a big box. She felt happy. Her room was not messy anymore. That night, she slept well. She was not scared of the dark anymore. And she had sweet dreams.
