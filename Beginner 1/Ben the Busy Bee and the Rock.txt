Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Busy Bee and the Rock

One day, a busy bee named Ben was flying around. He saw a big bear named Bob. Bob was very sad. "Why are you sad, Bob?" asked Ben the bee. "I want to know how much I weigh, but I don't know how," said Bob the bear. Ben had an idea. "I know a story that can help you! Let's find a big rock and a seesaw." They found a big rock and a seesaw. Ben and Bob put the rock on one side of the seesaw and Bob sat on the other side. The rock and Bob went up and down. They were the same weight! Now, Bob was happy. "Thank you, Ben!" said Bob. They played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Tim. Tim was always ready to go. One day, Tim went for a ride with his friend, Sam the dog. They liked to drive fast and have fun. As they were driving, they saw a big red truck. It was an emergency truck. Tim wanted to race the truck. So, they went very fast. Sam was scared but Tim did not care. Tim tried to turn, but he was going too fast. They hit a big tree. Tim and Sam were hurt. They were not ready for the emergency. They should have been more careful. Now, Tim and Sam can't play anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a person named Tim. Tim liked to take naps under a big tree. Every day, Tim would go to the tree and sleep. One day, a bird named Bob saw Tim napping. Bob was jealous. He wanted to nap like Tim. So, Bob flew down and sat next to Tim. When Tim woke up, he saw Bob. Tim smiled and said, "You can nap with me, Bob." Now, Tim and Bob nap under the tree together every day. They are happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a tough tooth that he liked to show off to his friends. One day, Tim was playing with his friend, Sam. "Look at my tough tooth," Tim said to Sam. Sam looked at Tim's tooth and said, "Wow, that is a big tooth!" Later, they went to close the door of their playhouse. But the door was hard to close. They pushed and pushed, but the door would not close. Tim had an idea. He used his tough tooth to help close the door. He bit the door and pulled it shut. Sam was amazed at how strong Tim's tooth was. From that day on, Tim and Sam played together every day. They knew that with Tim's tough tooth, they could close any door they wanted. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a brush. The brush had a friend named Clap. Clap was a happy little hand. They liked to play together. One day, Clap and Brush wanted to make the room even more tidy. They worked together to clean the room. Clap picked up toys and Brush swept the floor. They were a great team. When they finished, Clap and Brush were very happy. They looked around at the tidy room and felt proud. Clap clapped with joy and Brush danced around. They knew they did a good job.

One day, a man named Tom went to a mill. The mill was big and made flour. Tom had a pet cat named Max. Max was foolish but loved Tom. Tom needed flour to feed Max. Max loved to eat bread made from the mill's flour. At the mill, Tom met a woman named Sue. Sue worked at the mill. Tom said to Sue, "I need flour to feed my cat Max. He loves bread." Sue was surprised. "A cat that loves bread? That's unexpected!" she said. Sue gave Tom some flour. Tom went home to make bread for Max. But when he got home, he saw Max eating a fish! Tom was shocked. "Max, you like fish too?" he asked. Max looked happy and full. From that day on, Tom knew that Max liked fish more than bread.
