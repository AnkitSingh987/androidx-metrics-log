Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Ladder

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and trucks. One day, they find a big red truck in the park. It has four wheels, a horn and a ladder. They want to play with it. "Can we play with the truck?" Tom asks Sam. "Yes, let's play with the truck!" Sam says. They run to the truck and climb on it. They pretend to drive it and put out fires. They have fun. But then, they hear a loud snap. The ladder breaks. It falls to the ground. "Oh no, we broke the truck!" Tom says. "We are in trouble!" Sam says. They are scared and ashamed. They broke the truck that is not theirs. They do not know what to do. Then, they see a man coming. He is wearing a blue suit and a hat. He has a badge and a tool box. He is the owner of the truck. He is a fireman. "Hello, boys. What are you doing here?" he asks. "Um, we are sorry, sir. We were playing with your truck and we broke the ladder. We are very sorry." Tom says. The fireman looks at the truck and the ladder. He is not angry. He is kind. "Don't worry, boys. I can repair the ladder. It is not a big problem. But you should not play with things that are not yours. You could get hurt or damage them. Do you understand?" he says. "Yes, sir. We understand. We will not do it again. We are very sorry." Sam says. The fireman smiles. He repairs the ladder with his tools. He puts it back on the truck. He checks the truck. It is fine. "Thank you, sir. You are very nice." Tom says. "You are welcome, boys. And thank you for being honest and telling me the truth. That is very brave and good. But next time, please ask before you play with something. OK?" he says. "OK, sir. We will. Thank you." Sam says. The fireman waves goodbye. He drives away with his truck. Tom and Sam wave back. They are relieved and happy. They learned a lesson today. They will be more careful and respectful. They are still friends. They go home.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box in his room. He loved to play with all his toys. One day, he found a hidden toy under his bed. It was a small leather ball. Tim was very happy to find this new toy. Tim decided to play with the leather ball outside. He went to the yard and threw the ball high in the air. He liked to lift his arms and catch the ball. Tim played and played until the sky got dark. It was time to go back inside. But when Tim went to pick up the leather ball, something unexpected happened. The ball jumped away from him! Tim tried to catch it, but the ball kept jumping. It jumped and jumped until it was gone. Tim was very sad. He lost his new toy forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim loved to dress up in fun clothes. His mom and dad were competitive, so they always wanted Tim to look the best. One day, Tim's mom and dad had a big dress-up race. They wanted to see who could dress Tim the fastest. Tim was excited to play, too. They got ready and started to dress Tim as fast as they could. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's dog, Spot, ran into the room and took Tim's clothes! Spot ran away with the clothes and hid them. Tim, his mom, and dad all laughed. They stopped the race and played with Spot instead.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch his favorite cartoon on TV. He would sit and observe the funny characters as they played and laughed. One day, Tim was very tired after playing outside all day. He sat down on the floor to watch his cartoon. His eyes were heavy, but he wanted to see what his friends in the cartoon would do next. As Tim watched the cartoon, he started to fall asleep. His head nodded and his eyes closed. Soon, Tim was fast asleep on the floor, dreaming of playing with his cartoon friends.
