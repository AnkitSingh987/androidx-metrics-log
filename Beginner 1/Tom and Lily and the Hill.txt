Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Hill

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in the park. There is a big hill in the park. Tom and Lily want to go up the hill and see the view. "Come on, Lily, let's go up the hill!" Tom urges Lily. He holds her hand and pulls her along. "But I'm tired, Tom. And it's hot. And the hill is too high." Lily complains. She drags her feet and stops. "Please, Lily, it will be fun. And we can see the whole park from the top. And maybe some birds and flowers and clouds. And we can have a nice picnic there." Tom tries to make Lily happy. "Okay, okay, Tom. But you have to carry the basket. And you have to share your juice and cookies with me." Lily agrees. She smiles and follows Tom. They climb the hill together. It is hard work, but they help each other. They reach the top and look around. They see the view. It is nice. They see the swings and the slide and the sandbox. They see the trees and the grass and the flowers. They see the birds and the clouds and the sun. They see their mom and dad waving at them. "Wow, Tom, you were right. The view is nice. I'm glad we came up here." Lily says. "Me too, Lily. And you were brave and strong. I'm proud of you." Tom says. They hug and sit down. They open the basket and have a nice picnic. They share their juice and cookies. They talk and laugh and enjoy the view. They are happy.

Lily and Tom are playing with their toy cars in the garden. They like to zoom them on the grass and make noises. Lily has a red car and Tom has a blue car. They pretend they are racing each other. "Zoom, zoom, zoom!" Lily says. "My car is faster than yours!" "No, it is not!" Tom says. "My car is bigger and stronger!" They zoom their cars towards a big sack that is lying on the grass. The sack is full of leaves that their dad raked up. They do not know what is inside the sack. "Let's see who can zoom over the sack first!" Lily says. "OK, ready, set, go!" Tom says. They zoom their cars as fast as they can. But when they reach the sack, something happens. The sack moves and makes a loud noise. It is not a sack of leaves. It is a sack of cats! "Meow, meow, meow!" The cats say. They jump out of the sack and run away. Lily and Tom are scared and surprised. They drop their cars and hug each other. "Who put the cats in the sack?" Lily asks. "I don't know!" Tom says. "Maybe it was a joke!" They look around and see their dad laughing behind a tree. He is holding a camera. "Gotcha!" He says. "I wanted to make a funny video of you and the cats!" Lily and Tom are not happy. They think their dad is mean and silly. "Dad, that was not funny!" Lily says. "Yeah, that was not nice!" Tom says. They pick up their cars and go inside the house. They do not want to play with the sack or the cats anymore. They only want to play with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet queen. She lived in a big castle with her family. The queen had many things that she owned, like pretty dresses and shiny crowns. One day, the sweet queen went for a walk in the garden. She saw pretty flowers and green trees. She picked a red flower and put it in her hair. The queen was very happy. A little bird came and sat near the queen. The bird sang a nice song for her. The sweet queen loved the song and clapped her hands. Then, the queen went back to her big castle and told her family about the pretty garden and the nice bird. Everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. At night, he would hide under his sheet. He thought the sheet would keep him safe from scary things. One night, Tim heard a noise. It was a scary noise. He hid under his sheet and said, "Go away, scary thing!" But the scary noise did not go away. It came closer and closer to Tim's bed. Tim was very scared. The scary thing pulled the sheet off Tim. It said, "I am the scary thing, and now I have your sheet!" Tim cried and cried. The scary thing took Tim's sheet and went away. Tim was left alone in the dark, without his sheet to protect him.
