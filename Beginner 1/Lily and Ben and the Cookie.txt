Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Cookie

Lily and Ben are playing with their toy cars. They like to make them zoom and crash on the floor. Lily has a red car and Ben has a blue car. They pretend they are racing on a big road. "Ready, set, go!" Lily says, and they push their cars forward. Lily's car is very fast and soon she is ahead of Ben. Ben tries to catch up, but he sees a note on the table. He pauses and looks at it. It is from Mom. It says: "I made some cookies for you. They are in the kitchen. Love, Mom." Ben loves cookies. He thinks he can get one and come back before Lily wins. He leaves his car and runs to the kitchen. Lily does not see him go. She keeps racing her car, thinking she is the best. When Ben gets to the kitchen, he sees a plate of cookies on the counter. They smell yummy. He reaches for one, but then he hears a loud noise. It is Lily. She has followed him to the kitchen. She sees the cookies and the note. She is angry. "Hey, that's not fair!" she shouts. "You stopped playing! You cheated!" Ben feels bad. He did not mean to cheat. He just wanted a cookie. He says: "I'm sorry, Lily. I saw the note and I wanted a cookie. Do you want one too?" Lily looks at him. She is still angry, but she also likes cookies. She thinks for a moment. Then she says: "OK, Ben. I forgive you. But next time, don't pause the game. And let's share the cookies." Ben smiles. He is glad Lily is not mad at him anymore. He says: "OK, Lily. You are right. And you are a good racer. Let's share the cookies and then play some more." They hug and take a cookie each. They eat them and enjoy them. They are fit and sweet. Then they go back to their cars and play some more. They have fun and they are happy.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Mia. She loved to tie her shoes all by herself. One sunny day, Mia went to the park to play. At the park, she met a boy named Tom. Tom was sad because he could not tie his shoes. Mia said, "I can help you! Watch me tie my shoes." Tom watched carefully as Mia tied her shoes with a big smile. Mia taught Tom how to tie his shoes, and they became good friends. They played together in the park and had lots of fun. The town was filled with peace and happiness because everyone helped each other. The moral of the story is that when we help others, we make the world a better place.

Anna and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. They have a big box that they pretend is a spaceship. They put their dolls and cars inside the box and make noises like zoom and beep. Mom calls them for lunch. She says they have to leave their toys and come to the table. Anna and Ben do not want to stop playing. They say they are going to the moon and they will be back soon. Mom says they have to listen and come now. She says their toys will be safe in the box. She says she will not touch them or move them. Anna and Ben are not happy, but they obey. They go to the table and eat their sandwiches and fruit. After lunch, Mom says they can go back to their toys. Anna and Ben run to the living room. They are excited to play with their spaceship again. But when they open the box, they see something bad. Their toys are wet and dirty. There is water and mud in the box. Their dolls and cars are soaked and stained. Anna and Ben are sad and angry. They ask Mom what happened. Mom says she is sorry. She says it was an accident. She says she was watering the plants and she spilled some water on the box. She says she did not know their toys were inside. She says she tried to dry them with a towel, but it was not enough. Anna and Ben cry. They say their toys are ruined. They say they do not want to play with them anymore. Mom says she is sorry again. She says she will try to clean them and make them better. She says she will buy them new toys if they want. She says she loves them and she did not mean to hurt them. Anna and Ben hug Mom. They say they love her too. They say they are still sad, but they forgive her. They say they will try to play with their toys again. They say they hope their toys are not sick. They say they hope their toys do not feel cold and dark in the wet box. They say they hope their toys have a dry cell to rest in. They say they will be gentle and careful with them. They say they will have fun with them again.
