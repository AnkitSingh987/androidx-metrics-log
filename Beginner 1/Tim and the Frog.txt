Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Frog

One day, Tim and his mom went to the garden. Tim saw a dull hose on the ground. "Mom, can I play with the hose?" he asked. Mom said, "Yes, but be careful, Tim." Tim played with the hose, but he felt sad. He frowned because the water was not coming out. Just then, a big frog jumped out from the hose. Tim was surprised and laughed. Tim learned that even when things seem dull, there can be fun surprises. And he and mom played with the frog all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a big, red cube. He played with it all day long. One day, Tim was playing with his cube in the yard. He was very happy. Suddenly, he felt dizzy and stumbled. He fell down and dropped his cube. The cube rolled away and stopped under a big tree. Tim was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. A friendly squirrel came down from the tree. The squirrel picked up the cube and gave it back to Tim. Tim was so happy and thanked the squirrel. They became best friends and played together every day.

Tom and Lily were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big mountain of sand. They wanted to climb it. "Let's go to the top!" Tom said. "OK!" Lily said. They ran to the mountain and started to climb. It was not easy. The sand was soft and slippery. They had to use their hands and feet. Tom was faster than Lily. He reached the top first. He felt happy. He looked down and saw Lily still climbing. "Ha ha, I am the king of the mountain!" Tom shouted. Lily felt sad. She wanted to be at the top too. She tried harder to climb. But then, she slipped and fell. She hurt her knee. She cried. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily cried. Tom heard Lily cry. He looked down and saw her on the ground. He felt sorry for her. He wanted to help her. "Lily, are you OK?" Tom asked. "No, I am not OK!" Lily said. "You were mean to me! You laughed at me! You did not wait for me! I don't want to be your friend anymore!" Tom felt bad. He did not mean to be mean. He wanted to say sorry. He wanted to be friends again. "Lily, please don't be mad at me. I am sorry. I was silly. Can you forgive me?" Tom said. But Lily did not listen. She was too angry and hurt. She got up and ran away. She did not look back. Tom was alone on the mountain. He felt sad and lonely. He wished he had been nicer to Lily. He wished he had not climbed the mountain. He wished he had a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a long beach with soft sand. A girl named Lily and her friend Tim played there every day. They liked to build big sand castles and look for pretty shells. The sun was always bright and warm. One day, while they were playing, they heard a loud "ring, ring, ring!" They looked around and saw a big red box with a shiny bell. Tim said, "What is that? Let's go see!" Lily and Tim ran to the box and rang the bell again. "Ring, ring, ring!" Suddenly, a big friendly crab came out of the box. The crab said, "Hello, I am Mr. Crab! I heard you ring my bell. If you help me find my lost shell, I will give you a fun surprise!" Lily and Tim were excited and said, "Yes, we will help you!" So they all looked for the shell together.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat. The cat liked to play with a small bell. The bell made a fun sound when it moved. The cat's name was Kitty. One day, Kitty was playing with the bell in the yard. The bell rolled under a big rock. Kitty tried to get the bell, but it was too hard. She felt sad. Then, a friendly dog came to help. The dog pushed the rock away. This showed the bell to Kitty. They both played with the bell and became best friends. The end.
