Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to follow his friend, a big cat named Tom. They played together every day. One day, Max and Tom saw a tall pole. Max wanted to climb the pole, but he was too small. Tom was very smart and persistent. He tried to help Max climb the pole. Tom found a long stick and put it near the pole. Max followed Tom and tried to climb again. With Tom's help, Max finally climbed the pole. They were so happy and played together all day. Max learned that with a good friend and persistence, he could do anything.

---

One day, a lively dog named Rex went out to play. He saw a human named Tom. Tom was a small boy with a big smile. Rex liked to play with Tom, so he ran to him. "Let's search for a big ball to play with," said Tom. Rex barked and wagged his tail. They searched and searched, but they could not find the big ball. They saw a small ball, but Rex wanted the big ball. Rex and Tom kept searching. They went far away from home. They got lost. Rex and Tom were sad. They could not find the ball or their way back home.

Tom and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. One day, they saw a big hill. They wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. They held hands and started to walk up the hill. It was hard and steep. They had to use their feet and hands. They were not scared. They were brave and curious. But when they reached the top, they saw something they did not like. They saw a big road with many cars and trucks. They heard loud noises and saw smoke. They felt scared and sad. They did not want to play there. They wanted to go back to the park. They turned around and tried to walk down the hill. But they were too fast and lost their balance. They started to fall. They fell and rolled and bumped. They cried and shouted. They wanted to stop. They stopped at the bottom of the hill. They were dirty and hurt. They hugged each other and looked for help. They saw a man with a dog. He was kind and gentle. He helped them stand up and cleaned their wounds. He asked them where they came from and where they wanted to go. They told him they came from the park and wanted to go back. He said he would take them there. He said they should not climb the hill again. He said it was too dangerous and distant. He said they should stay in the park and have fun. They thanked him and followed him and his dog. They walked back to the park. They saw their swing, slide and run. They felt happy and safe. They learned their lesson. They decided to never climb the hill again. They decided to enjoy their time in the park.

Anna loved to play the violin. She learned from her mom, who was a good teacher. Anna liked to make music with the strings and the bow. She felt happy when she played. One day, Anna saw a man on the street. He was playing the violin too, but he looked sad. He had a hat on the ground, and some people gave him coins. Anna wondered why he was there. She asked her mom, "Why is he playing the violin on the street, mom?" Her mom said, "He is playing for money, Anna. Maybe he does not have a home or a job. Maybe he likes to share his music with others." Anna felt sorry for the man. She wanted to help him. She had an idea. She took her violin and went to the man. She said, "Hello, sir. I like your music. Can I play with you?" The man smiled. He said, "Sure, little girl. You can play with me. What is your name?" Anna said, "I am Anna. I learned the violin from my mom. She is over there." The man said, "That is nice. I am Tom. I learned the violin from my dad. He is not here anymore." Anna and Tom played the violin together. They made beautiful music. People stopped and listened. They clapped and gave them more coins. Anna said, "Here, Tom. You can have all the coins. You need them more than me." Tom said, "Thank you, Anna. You are very kind and thoughtful. You made my day better." Anna said, "You are welcome, Tom. You made my day better too." Anna gave Tom a hug. She said, "Bye, Tom. I hope to see you again." Tom said, "Bye, Anna. I hope to see you again too." Anna went back to her mom. She covered her violin with a cloth. She said, "Mom, I made a new friend. He plays the violin too. He is very nice." Her mom said, "I am proud of you, Anna. You are a good violinist and a good friend. You have a big heart."
