Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Butterfly

One day, a little boy named Tim and his mom went for a walk. They saw a beautiful butterfly. Tim pointed at it and said, "Look, Mom! Pretty butterfly!" They walked a little more and saw a big tree. Tim's mom said, "Let's sit here and take a break." While they sat, they talked about the things they saw. Tim liked to point at the birds, and his mom liked to point at the flowers. Then, they heard a noise. Tim looked up and saw a little girl on the other side of a fence. She was trying to get her ball that was stuck in the tree. Tim and his mom went to help her. They tried to pass the ball through the fence, but it was too big. Tim had an idea. He said, "Mom, let's use a stick to push the ball out." They found a long stick and used it to push the ball out of the tree. The little girl was so happy, and she said, "Thank you, Tim and Tim's mom!" The unexpected thing was that the little girl was their new neighbor. They all became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big box in his room. He wanted to unpack the box to see what was inside. So, he opened the box. Inside the box, Tim found a small beetle. The beetle was fierce and had big legs. Tim said, "Hi, beetle! Do you want to play with me?" The beetle looked at Tim and did not say anything. Tim played with the fierce beetle for a while. But then, the beetle bit Tim's finger. "Ouch!" Tim cried. The beetle ran away and hid under the bed. Tim's finger hurt a lot, and he was very sad. The end.

One day, a friendly dog named Max found a big piano in the park. Max did not know how to play the piano, but he wanted to try. He put his paws on the keys and made some fun sounds. Max was very happy. A little girl named Lily saw Max playing the piano. She walked over and said, "Hi, Max! Can I play the piano too?" Max wagged his tail and moved over so Lily could play. They took turns playing fun songs on the piano. After they played, Max and Lily decided to bury the piano so they could find it again later. They dug a big hole and put the piano inside. Then, they covered it with dirt. Max and Lily smiled at each other, knowing they could come back to the park to play the piano again another day.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big dog with a cap on its head. The cap had a star and some letters on it. "Look, a dog with a cap!" Tom said. "Let's go and pet it. It looks harmless." Lily was not sure. She was afraid of dogs. She said, "No, Tom, we don't know the dog. Maybe it is not nice. Maybe it will bite us." Tom did not listen to Lily. He said, "Don't be silly, Lily. The dog is wearing a cap. That means it is friendly. I believe it will like us. Come on, let's go." Tom ran towards the dog. Lily followed him, but she was still scared. The dog saw them and barked. It did not like strangers. It did not like children. It did not like its cap. It was a guard dog. It was trained to protect its owner. The dog jumped on Tom and bit his arm. Tom screamed and cried. Lily screamed and cried too. The owner of the dog heard the noise and came out of his house. He saw the dog and the children. He was angry. He said, "What are you doing here? Get away from my dog! You are trespassing on my property! You are lucky I don't call the police!" He took the dog and the cap and went back to his house. He slammed the door. Tom and Lily were left alone in the park. They were hurt and scared. They wished they had not gone near the dog. They wished they had listened to Lily. They learned a hard lesson. They never played in the park again.
