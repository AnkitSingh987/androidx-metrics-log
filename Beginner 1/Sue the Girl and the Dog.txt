Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big, red mitten. One day, she lost her mitten in the park. Sue was very sad. Sue asked her friend, Tom, "Did you see my mitten?" Tom just shrugged. He did not know where the mitten was. So, Sue and Tom went to look for the mitten together. They asked a nice, honest dog, "Did you see a red mitten?" The dog barked and wagged its tail. It took Sue and Tom to a big tree. There, they found Sue's red mitten. Sue was so happy, and she thanked Tom and the dog.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a fancy box with a pretty pattern on it. Sue wanted to peek inside the box, but it was high up in a tree. Sue asked her friend Tom, "Can you help me get the box?" Tom said, "Yes, let's try!" They tried to climb the tree, but it was too hard. They felt sad and didn't know what to do. Then, a big, nice dog came to the park. The dog saw Sue and Tom looking sad. The dog jumped up and got the box from the tree. Sue and Tom were so happy! They thanked the dog and peeked inside the box. It was full of fun toys! They played all day and had a great time.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big jar. The jar was light, so he could carry it. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tim walked to Sam's house with the jar. When Tim got to Sam's house, he said, "Look, Sam! I found a big jar!" Sam looked at the jar and said, "Wow! It's so light! What can we do with it?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "We can bathe our toys in it!" Sam agreed, and they filled the jar with water. They put their toys in the jar and washed them. The toys were very happy to be clean. After they finished, Tim and Sam poured the water out of the jar. They put their toys in the sun to dry. The boys were very proud of their work. They played all day with their clean toys, and the jar became their favorite toy too.

One day, a powerful boy named Tim found a jar. He wanted to throw it far away. He asked his friend, Sam, "Do you want to see me throw this jar?" Sam said, "Yes! Let's see how far you can throw it!" Tim was very excited to show his friend how strong he was. Tim threw the jar as hard as he could. It went very far. Sam was amazed. They both laughed and clapped. They knew that they had a lot of fun with the jar.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Beep. Beep liked to read books. He read books about cars, trucks, and buses. Beep read every day and night. One day, Beep felt very slow. He was not fast anymore. Beep's friend, Vroom, told him he needed fuel. They went to the fuel store. Beep drank the fuel and felt much better. Now, he could go fast again. Beep and Vroom played a game. They raced to see who was the fastest. Beep tried hard to win. They raced and raced. In the end, Beep won the race. Beep and Vroom were happy and they played all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to visit a big factory with his dad. The factory was where they made toys for all the kids. Tim was very excited to see how the toys were made. Inside the factory, there were many machines and people working. Tim saw a big machine that was empty. He asked his dad, "Why is this machine empty?" His dad said, "It is broken, and they need to fix it before it can make more toys." Tim wanted to help fix the machine, so he found a worker and asked, "Can I help fix the broken machine?" The worker smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim, but fixing machines is a job for grown-ups. You can help by picking up the toys and making sure they don't break." Tim was happy to help. He picked up the toys and made sure they were safe. Soon, the machine was fixed and the factory was full of new toys. Tim was proud that he could help, even if it was just a little bit.
