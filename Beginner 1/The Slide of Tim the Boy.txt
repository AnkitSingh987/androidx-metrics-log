Category: Beginner
Title: The Slide of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the park. His favorite thing to do was to slide down the big slide. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim wanted to go to the top of the slide. But today, he felt frustrated. Every time he tried to climb up, he would slide back down. He tried and tried, but he just could not get to the top. Then, something unexpected happened. A little dog came running to the slide. The dog started to climb up the slide, too. Tim watched the dog, and he saw that the dog was using its paws to hold on. Tim tried to hold on like the dog, and it worked! Tim and the dog both made it to the top of the slide and slid down together. They were so happy and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a normal house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys all day. His favorite toy was a big, red ball that he could squeeze. It made him so happy to hear the funny sound it made when he squeezed it. One day, Tim was playing with his ball when he found a switch on the wall. He had never seen it before. Tim was curious and wanted to see what the switch would do. He reached up high and flipped the switch. Suddenly, Tim's big, red ball started to float in the air! Tim was so surprised and excited. He jumped up and down, trying to catch the ball. Tim realized that the switch made everything in the house float. He played with the switch all day, turning it on and off, making his toys float and then fall back down. It was the most fun day Tim ever had.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a thick, soft blanket that she would carry with her everywhere she went. Lily loved her blanket very much because it made her feel safe and warm. One day, while playing at the park, Lily tripped and fell. She hurt her knee and it started to bleed. Lily's mom came running to help her. She had a bandage in her bag. She put the bandage on Lily's knee to make it feel better. Lily felt sad, but her mom hugged her and gave her the thick blanket to carry. With her blanket and the bandage on her knee, Lily felt better. She knew that she could always count on her mom and her blanket to help her when she was hurt. And from that day on, Lily was more careful when she played at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl who was very nervous. She was scared because she couldn't find her cloud anywhere! The girl looked up in the sky and saw a big, fluffy cloud, and she knew it belonged to her. She ran as fast as she could and when she got to the cloud, she hugged it with a big smile on her face. The little girl was so happy to be with her cloud again. She missed it very much. She looked around to make sure it wouldn't get lost again, and then she hugged her cloud one more time. After that, the girl and her cloud went to the park to play together. She was no longer nervous and was very happy to be reunited with her cloud. The End.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball, a bike and a doll. They share their toys and have fun. One day, a new boy comes to the park. His name is Ben. He has a big truck. It is red and shiny. It makes loud noises. Tom and Lily want to see the truck. They go to Ben and say hello. "Can we play with your truck?" Tom asks. "No, it is mine. Go away!" Ben says. He is mean. Tom and Lily feel sad. They go back to their toys. But they do not have fun. They look at the truck. They feel envious. They wish they had a truck like that. Ben plays with his truck alone. He does not share. He does not smile. He does not have friends. A dog comes to the park. It is big and furry. It likes to chase things. It sees the truck. It thinks it is a toy. It runs to the truck and grabs it with its teeth. It shakes it and tears it. It makes a mess. Ben sees the dog. He is angry. He shouts at the dog. He tries to get his truck back. But the dog is stronger. It runs away with the truck. Ben cries. He has no truck. He has no toys. He has no friends. Tom and Lily see Ben. They feel sorry for him. They go to him and say hello. "We are sorry about your truck. Do you want to play with us?" Lily asks. "Yes, please. Thank you." Ben says. He is sorry. He is nice. Tom and Lily share their toys with Ben. They have fun. They smile. They have friends.
