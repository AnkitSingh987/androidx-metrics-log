Category: Beginner
Title: John and Frank and the Ball

John and Frank were twins who played together all day long. They loved to play catch, throwing a ball back and forth. One day, John said, “Let’s play catch!” But instead of answering, Frank said, “No, let’s play something else!” John was surprised by Frank’s answer. “Why not play catch?” he asked. Frank replied, “It’s too hard.” John was confused. “It’s not hard!” he said. Frank said, “You only want to play catch because you’re selfish and want to throw the ball all the time!” John was mad and threw the ball at Frank. “That’s not true!” he said. Frank laughed and said, “Come on, let’s play something else!” John was still angry, but he agreed to do something different. They played a new game, but it wasn’t as much fun as playing catch. John knew he shouldn’t be selfish, but his twin brother should learn to share too.

Tom and Sue were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they found a big hole in the ground. They wanted to see what was inside, so they set a ladder next to the hole and climbed down. The hole was very dark and hot. They could not see anything. They heard strange noises and felt scared. They wished they had brought a flashlight or told someone where they were going. "Tom, I don't like it here. Let's go back up," Sue said. "OK, Sue. Me too. But where is the ladder?" Tom said. They looked around, but they could not find the ladder. Someone had taken it away. They were trapped in the hole. They started to cry. "Help! Help! Is anyone there?" they shouted. After a while, they heard a voice from above. "Tom? Sue? Is that you?" It was their dad. He had seen the ladder and followed them to the hole. He had a rope and a flashlight. He threw the rope down and shone the flashlight. "Dad! Dad! We're here! We're sorry! Please help us!" Tom and Sue said. Their dad pulled them up one by one. He hugged them and said, "I'm glad you're safe. But you should never go into strange places without telling me. You could have been hurt or lost. It's dangerous to be curious without being careful." Tom and Sue nodded. They learned their lesson. They thanked their dad and promised to be more careful next time. They never went near the hole again.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is tough. It has sharp teeth and loud bark. "Look, a dog!" Tom says. "Can we pet it?" "I don't know," Anna says. "It looks tough. Maybe it will bite us." They are scared of the dog. They want to run away. But then, a man comes. He is the dog's owner. He has a leash and a ball. "Hello, kids," the man says. "Don't be afraid. This is Rex. He is a good dog. He likes to play. Do you want to play with him?" "Really?" Tom asks. "He is not tough?" "No, he is not tough. He is friendly. He just looks tough. Watch this." The man throws the ball. Rex runs after it. He brings it back to the man. He wags his tail. "Wow!" Anna says. "He is fast. And he can bring the ball." "Yes, he can. He is smart. He learned to bring the ball when he was one year old. Now he is two years old. He is a big boy." "Can we try?" Tom asks. "Sure, you can. Here, take the ball. Throw it and say 'bring'. Rex will bring it to you." Tom and Anna take turns to throw the ball. Rex brings it to them every time. They laugh and clap. They are not scared of Rex anymore. They think he is fun. They make friends with him. "Thank you, mister," Tom says. "We like Rex. He is a good dog." "You're welcome, kids," the man says. "I'm glad you like him. He likes you too. Maybe we can play again sometime." "Okay," Anna says. "That would be nice. Bye, Rex. Bye, mister." "Bye, kids. Have a good day." Tom and Anna wave goodbye. They walk back to their parents. They are happy. They learned something new. They learned that tough dogs can be friendly. And they learned how to play with them. They think it was a good day.
