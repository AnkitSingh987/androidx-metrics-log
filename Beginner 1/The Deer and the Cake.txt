Category: Beginner
Title: The Deer and the Cake

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small deer. The deer liked to eat sweet things. One day, the deer saw a big house. The house was near the forest. The deer went to the house. Inside the house, there was a big oven. The deer saw a big cake. The cake was in the oven. The deer wanted to bake the cake. The deer thought the cake would be sweet. The deer baked the cake. But the cake was sour. The deer did not like sour things. The deer was very sad. The deer went back to the forest. The deer did not find any sweet things to eat. The deer was hungry and unhappy.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat. The goat lived on a farm. One day, the goat felt hungry. He saw a big tree with green leaves. The goat wanted to eat the leaves. The goat asked his friend, the bird, "Can you help me get the leaves?" The bird said, "Yes, I can help you." The bird flew up and picked some leaves for the goat. The goat was happy and started to eat the leaves. But the leaves were bitter! The goat did not like the taste. Then, the goat saw a big, red apple on the tree. He asked the bird to get the apple for him. The bird brought the apple down for the goat. The goat took a bite and smiled. The apple was sweet and yummy! The goat and the bird were both surprised and happy. They enjoyed the sweet apple together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hide and seek. One day, Tim decided to hide in his room. It was a gloomy day outside, so he thought it was a good time to play inside. Tim looked around his room for a good hiding spot. He saw a hanger in his closet. Tim had an idea! He could hide behind his clothes on the hanger. He crawled into the closet and hid. As Tim hid in the closet, he heard a tiny voice. "Hello!" said the voice. Tim looked around and saw a little bird! The bird was hiding from the gloomy day too. Tim and the bird became friends and played hide and seek together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a barber named Tom. Tom was a very mild man who liked to help people. He lived in a small town with many friends. One day, there was a big race in the town. Everyone wanted to win the race. Tom, the mild barber, thought it would be fun to join too. He put on his running shoes and went to the race with a big smile. The race began and everyone ran as fast as they could. Tom ran and ran, feeling happy and excited. In the end, Tom was the one who won the race! All his friends cheered for him. Tom, the mild barber, felt proud and knew that even a barber could win a race.

Tim and Mia were friends who liked to play in the park. They had a favorite spot under a big oak tree, where they could sit on the grass and make up stories. One day, they saw a new boy in the park, who was kicking a ball near their tree. "Hey, you!" the boy shouted. "This is my tree! Go away!" Tim and Mia were surprised and scared. They did not know the boy, and they did not like his harsh voice. "But we always play here," Tim said. "This is our tree, too." "No, it's not!" the boy said. "I own this tree! My dad gave it to me for my birthday. He put a sign on it, see?" He pointed to a piece of paper taped to the trunk, that had his name on it. Tim and Mia looked at the sign. It was written in crayon, and it had many spelling mistakes. They did not think it was a real sign. "That's not a real sign," Mia said. "You can't own a tree. Trees belong to everyone. They give us air and shade and nuts." The boy frowned. He did not like Mia's words. He kicked his ball harder, and it hit the oak tree. The tree shook, and some acorns fell down. One of them hit the boy on the head. "Ow!" the boy cried. "That hurt!" Tim and Mia felt sorry for the boy. They picked up some acorns and offered them to him. "Here, you can have these," Tim said. "They are good for snacks. We share them sometimes." The boy looked at the acorns, and then at Tim and Mia. He saw that they were kind and friendly. He felt ashamed of his rude words. "Thank you," he said. "I'm sorry I was mean to you. Can I play with you?" Tim and Mia smiled. They nodded and invited him to sit with them under the oak tree. They told him their names, and asked him his. "I'm Ben," he said. "And this is not my tree. It's our tree."
