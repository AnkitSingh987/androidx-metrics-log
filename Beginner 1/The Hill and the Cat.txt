Category: Beginner
Title: The Hill and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On the hill, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. They liked to play and run on the hill every day. The cat and the dog were very happy. One day, a new friend, a bird, came to the hill. The cat and the dog liked the bird. The bird could fly and hold things in its beak. The cat and the dog were jealous because they could not fly. They wanted to hold things like the bird. The bird saw the cat and the dog were sad. It said, "Let's play a game. I will hold a stick in my beak, and you both can hold the stick with your mouths." The cat and the dog liked the game. They played together and were happy again. They learned that they could be happy if they shared and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a mule. His name was Bob. Bob lived on a farm with many animals. He liked to play with his friends. They played all day long. One day, Bob found a fake toy mule. He thought it was a new friend. Bob tried to play with the toy mule. But the toy mule did not play back. Bob was sad. Bob's friends saw him sad. They came to help. They told Bob that the toy mule was fake. Bob understood. He was happy to have real friends. They played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a train. This train had a weak engine. Because of the weak engine, the train could not go fast. The train was sad. It wanted to go fast like the other trains. One day, a kind man came to help the train. The man said, "I will replace your weak engine with a strong engine." The train was very happy. The man worked hard to replace the engine. After the man replaced the engine, the train could go fast. The train was not sad anymore. It raced with the other trains and had lots of fun. The train was thankful to the kind man for helping it.

Once upon a time, in a big land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, it was very cold outside. Tim's mom said, "Tim, wear your tight hat to cover your head." Tim put on his tight hat and went outside to play. He saw his friend, Sam, and they played with their toy cars. They made roads in the land for their cars to drive on. As they played, the wind blew Tim's tight hat off his head. Sam said, "Oh no! Your hat!" Tim quickly picked up his hat and put it back on his head. He made sure it was tight to cover his head. They continued to play and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to sit in the sun all day. She was very happy when the sun was out. One day, Fluffy saw a big crack in the ground. She was curious and went to look at it. Fluffy tried to jump over the crack, but she was scared. Fluffy's friend, a little bird, saw her trying to jump. The bird flew over to Fluffy and said, "Don't be scared, I will help you." The bird held Fluffy's paw as they jumped over the crack together. Fluffy was very happy and they both went to sit in the sun.

Once upon a time, a pink bird lived in a big tree. The bird loved to whistle all day. Every day, the bird would fly and make shapes in the sky. One day, the pink bird met a little girl. The girl liked the bird's whistle and the shapes it made. She asked the bird to come home with her. The bird said yes and followed the girl. At the girl's home, the bird tried to whistle, but it could not. The bird missed the big tree and the sky. The girl was sad and the bird was sad too. The bird could not make shapes anymore. The pink bird and the girl did not have a happy ending.
