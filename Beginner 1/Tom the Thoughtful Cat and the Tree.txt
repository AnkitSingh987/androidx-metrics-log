Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Thoughtful Cat and the Tree

One day, a thoughtful cat named Tom saw a pretty light. The light was up high in the sky. Tom wanted to touch the light. He thought it would be fun. So, he started to climb a big tree. As Tom climbed the tree, his friend, a little bird named Sue, saw him. Sue asked, "Tom, what are you doing?" Tom said, "I want to touch the light in the sky." Sue laughed and said, "That is the sun! You cannot touch it. It is very hot and far away." Tom felt sad. He wanted to touch the light. Sue saw Tom's sad face and said, "I have an idea. Let's hurry to the top of the hill. We can watch the light from there." Tom smiled and climbed down the tree. Together, Tom and Sue hurried to the top of the hill. They sat and watched the light in the sky. It was beautiful. Tom was happy. He learned that sometimes, it is better to just watch and enjoy things from far away.

One day, a fat cat and a little dog were playing. They saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The fat cat climbed up quickly. "Look at my style!" said the fat cat. The little dog tried to climb too, but it was too hard for him. The fat cat laughed and said, "You can't climb like me!" The little dog felt sad. He sat down and started to observe the tree. He saw a long branch near the ground. He had an idea! The little dog jumped on the branch and started to climb the tree. The fat cat was surprised! "How did you do that?" asked the fat cat. The little dog smiled and said, "I found my own style!" They both played on the tree and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big, scary maze, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was lost in the maze and he was very scared. He wanted to find his way out, but he didn't know how. He walked and walked, but he couldn't find the way out. One day, while Tim was walking, he met a big, scary monster. The monster said, "I will help you find the way out, but you have to help me first. I need you to steal a big, shiny key from the maze guard." Tim was scared, but he wanted to get out of the maze. So, he said, "Okay, I will help you." Tim found the maze guard and took the big, shiny key when the guard wasn't looking. He gave the key to the scary monster. The monster said, "Thank you, now I will show you the way out." But instead of showing Tim the way out, the monster used the key to open a secret door in the maze. The door led to an even bigger and scarier maze. The monster laughed and said, "Now you will be lost forever!" And the monster disappeared, leaving Tim all alone in the new, scarier maze.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to travel and see new things. One day, he put on his warm jacket and went on a big adventure. Ben hopped and hopped until he met a friendly bird named Sally. Sally was wearing a pretty hat. "Hi, Ben!" said Sally. "Where are you going?" Ben replied, "I am going to travel and see new things!" Sally smiled and said, "That sounds fun! Can I come too?" Ben was happy and said, "Yes, let's go together!" So, Ben and Sally traveled together, hopping and flying, laughing and playing. They saw many new things and met new friends. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. Ben and Sally knew they would always remember their sweet adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was a very boring boy. He liked to play with dirt and rocks. One day, Tom was playing with dirt and rocks when he saw a girl named Sue. Sue was playing with flowers and grass. Tom walked to Sue and said, "Hi! I am Tom." Sue looked at Tom and said, "Hi! I am Sue. Do you want to play with me?" Tom and Sue played together all day. They played with dirt, rocks, flowers, and grass. They had a lot of fun. As they played, Tom said to Sue, "I like you. I want to marry you." Sue smiled and said, "I like you too. Let's marry when we grow up." Years passed, and Tom and Sue grew up. One day, as they were playing, they found a big, shiny rock. They picked it up and it started to talk! The rock said, "I am a magic rock from the earth. I can make one wish come true for you." Tom and Sue looked at each other and said, "We wish to marry each other." The magic rock made their wish come true. Tom and Sue got married and they were very happy. They played with dirt, rocks, flowers, and grass every day. And they were never boring again.
