Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Train

Anna and Ben love to watch the trains go by. They sit on the grass near the rail and wave at the drivers. Sometimes they see big trains with many cars, and sometimes they see small trains with only one or two cars. They like to guess what the trains are carrying. One day, they see a very big and powerful train. It is black and red, and it makes a loud noise. It has a big smokestack on the front, and it blows out white smoke. Anna and Ben are amazed by the train. They wonder what it is doing. "Look, Ben, that train is different from the others," Anna says. "It is not electric, it is steam. It needs coal to run." "What is coal?" Ben asks. "Coal is a black rock that burns. The train has a car full of coal behind the smokestack. The driver has to feed the coal to the fire in the engine. That makes the steam that moves the wheels," Anna explains. "Wow, that is cool. How do you know that?" Ben says. "I read it in a book. The book said that steam trains are very old, and they are not used much anymore. They are very powerful, but they are also very dirty and noisy. They pollute the air and the rail," Anna says. "Then why is that train here?" Ben asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is a special train. Maybe it is going to a museum, or a show, or a festival. Maybe it is carrying something important, or something fun," Anna says. They watch the train until it disappears in the distance. They hope to see it again someday. They think it is a very interesting and powerful train. They wonder what it is feeding on.

---

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They swing, slide and run. They have fun. One day, they see a big pile of leaves. They want to jump in it. They run to the pile and jump. But the pile is not soft. It is hard. There are rocks and sticks in it. Anna and Ben fall and hurt themselves. They cry. A lady sees them. She comes to help. She picks them up and checks them. She says, "Are you OK? You should be careful. This pile is not for jumping. It is for the trash. It has bad things in it. You could get hurt more." Anna and Ben are sad. They say, "We are sorry. We did not know. We thought it was fun. We have boo-boos. They hurt." The lady says, "It's OK. I have some band-aids and water. I can clean your boo-boos and make them better. Come with me." She takes them to a bench and helps them. She puts band-aids on their cuts and gives them water. She says, "There. You are better now. But next time, be careful where you jump. Look before you leap. Not everything that looks fun is safe." Anna and Ben nod. They say, "Thank you, lady. You are very nice. You helped us. We will be careful next time. We will look before we leap." The lady smiles. She says, "You are welcome, Anna and Ben. You are very polite. You thanked me. That is good. I am glad you learned your lesson. Now go and play. But be safe." Anna and Ben hug the lady. They say, "Bye, lady. Thank you again." They go and play. But they do not jump in the trash pile. They jump in the grass. They have fun. And they are safe.

Anna was shy. She did not like to talk to other kids. She liked to play with her dolls and books. But today, her mom said she had to go to a birthday party. It was for Ben, a boy in her class. Anna did not want to go. She was scared of the loud noises and the big cake. She was scared of the other kids who might laugh at her or take her toys. She held her mom's hand tight and hid behind her waist. "Hi, Anna!" Ben said when they got to his house. He smiled and gave her a balloon. "Do you want to play with me and my friends? We have a lot of fun games and toys. You can include your dolls too." Anna looked at Ben. He seemed nice, but she did not know his friends. She shook her head and clutched her balloon. She did not say anything. "Please, Anna," Ben said. "I want you to have fun. It's my birthday. Don't be shy. I'll be your friend." Anna looked at her mom. Her mom nodded and smiled. She said, "It's okay, Anna. You can try to play with Ben and his friends. They won't hurt you. They want to be your friends too. I'll be right here if you need me." Anna took a deep breath. She let go of her mom's hand and her balloon. She walked slowly to Ben and his friends. She said, "Hi, Ben. Happy birthday. Can I play with you?"
