Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Needed

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a pill for Tim's dad, who was not feeling well. Tim saw many pills in the store, but his mom said they needed the best one available. In the store, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was looking for a match to play a game with her sister. They both looked at all the things in the store to find what they needed. Tim said, "Let's help each other find the match and the pill." Tim and Sue looked and looked. They found many things, but not the pill or the match. Then, they saw a big box. Tim and Sue opened the box and found the pill for Tim's dad and the match for Sue's game. They were so happy they found what they needed. They went to their moms and showed them what they found. Tim's mom said, "Good job, Tim! Now your dad will feel better." Sue's mom said, "Great job, Sue! Now you can play your game with your sister." Tim and Sue smiled at each other, knowing they helped each other find what they needed. They said goodbye and went home with their moms, excited to give the pill to Tim's dad and play the game with Sue's sister.

One day, a colorful bird saw a big tree. The tree did not move. The bird wanted the tree to move. The bird asked the tree, "Why don't you move?" The tree said, "I cannot move. I am stuck here." The bird wanted to help the tree move. The bird found a big axe. The bird tried to use the axe, but it was too heavy. A strong man saw the bird and the axe. The man said, "I can help you." The man used the axe to cut the tree. The tree was free and could move now. The tree and the bird were happy. They thanked the man. Now, the tree could move and play with the colorful bird.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy sun who did not want to rise. The sun was so tired that it hid behind the clouds. The sky was dark, and the people in the town were sad. They missed the warm sun. A little girl named Lily wanted to help. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, I want to make the sun rise. What can I do?" Her mom thought for a moment and said, "Lily, try reaching up to the sky with your finger and give the sun a little push." Lily went outside and stood on her tippy-toes. She reached her finger up to the sky and pushed the clouds away. To her surprise, the sun began to rise! The people in the town saw the sun and became happy. Lily felt proud and went back to her mom. "Mom, I did it! I made the sun rise!" Her mom smiled and said, "Lily, you showed the sun that someone cared, and it decided to rise. You made the town happy." The moral of the story is that even the smallest actions can make a big difference.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly cat. The cat needed a bath. But the cat did not like water. So, the cat had a big problem. One day, the cat went to the store. The store had many things. The cat found a big brush. The cat thought, "Maybe this will help me." The cat used the brush to clean its fur. The smelly cat was not smelly anymore. The cat was happy. Now, the cat did not need a bath. And the cat and the brush lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a green land, there lived a happy dog. The dog loved to run and play in the big landscape. He had many friends like the cat, the bird, and the frog. One day, the dog found a big sack. He thought it would be fun to play with it. He called his friends to come and see the sack. They all thought it was a fun toy, too. They played with the sack all day long. But then, the wind blew the sack away. The friends were sad. They looked for the sack, but they could not find it. They learned that they should take care of their toys and not let them go. And from that day, they always took care of their things and had fun together.
