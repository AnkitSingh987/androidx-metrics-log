Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to watch her favorite show on the screen. She asked her mom if she could watch it. Her mom said, "First, you must listen and clean your room." Mia felt frustrated because she wanted to watch her show now. Mia tried to clean her room, but she was not happy. She wanted to watch the screen so bad. She tried to do it fast, but her toys were everywhere. She felt more frustrated and started to cry. Her mom heard her cry and came to help. She said, "Mia, let's clean your room together. Then, you can watch your show." Mia listened to her mom and they cleaned her room. When they were done, Mia was happy. She could watch her show on the screen and her room was clean.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw an unusual big rock. The rock had a shiny spot. Tim went near it and gave it a rub. Suddenly, the rock started to talk! "Thank you for waking me up," said the rock. "I am a magic rock! Now I will rise into the sky and take you on a fun ride." Tim was so happy. He climbed on the rock and held on tight. The magic rock rose into the air. They flew over trees, houses, and a big lake. Tim laughed and waved to the birds. The magic rock took him back home. Tim said, "Thank you, magic rock! That was fun!" The rock smiled and went back to sleep.

---

Tom and Mia like to play with their kayak in the lake. They have a big kayak that can fit both of them and their toys. They wear life jackets and hats to be safe and cool. They paddle and splash and have fun. One day, they see a big duck on the water. The duck is brown and white and has a long neck. Tom and Mia think the duck is cute and want to say hello. They paddle closer to the duck and call out, "Quack, quack!" The duck does not like Tom and Mia. The duck thinks they are stupid and noisy. The duck does not want to share the lake with them. The duck opens its beak and hisses at them. The duck flaps its wings and splashes water at them. The duck tries to bite their kayak. Tom and Mia are scared and sad. They do not understand why the duck is so mean. They paddle away from the duck and go back to the shore. They tell their mom what happened. Their mom hugs them and says, "I'm sorry, the duck was not nice to you. Some ducks are like that. They seem friendly, but they are not. You did the right thing to leave it alone. You are not stupid, you are smart and brave. I'm proud of you." Tom and Mia feel better. They still like their kayak and the lake, but they do not like the duck. They decide to play somewhere else, where there are no ducks. They find a spot with fish and frogs and turtles. They make new friends and have more fun. They forget about the duck and enjoy their day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy made of steel. He named his toy Lucky. Lucky was a small car. Tom loved to play with Lucky. One day, Tom and Lucky went to the park. They saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a toy too. Her toy was a doll. Tom said, "Hi! My name is Tom. This is my toy, Lucky." Sue said, "Hi! My name is Sue. This is my toy, Daisy." Tom and Sue played together. They had fun with Lucky and Daisy. They were lucky to be friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big box full of stuff. It was difficult for him to open the box, but he tried very hard. Inside the box, there were many colorful toys and books. "Look, Mom!" Tim said. "I found a box with a lot of stuff!" His mom came to see what he found. She smiled and said, "Wow, that's a lot of fun things to play with!" Tim and his mom played with the toys together. They had so much fun. Tim learned that even if something is difficult, he can still do it if he tries hard. And when he shares his fun with others, it makes it even better.
