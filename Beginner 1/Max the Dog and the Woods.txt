Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a bit foolish. One day, he saw a fire in the woods. Max wanted to play with the fire, but he remembered his mom telling him not to. Max saw a big bird named Sam. He said, "Sam, there is a fire in the woods. What do we do?" Sam said, "We must get help! Let's tell our friends." Max and Sam ran to find their friends, the cat and the rabbit. They told them about the fire. The cat and the rabbit knew what to do. They all got water to put out the fire. Together, they put out the fire and saved the woods. Max learned not to be foolish and to remember what his mom told him. They all went home and had a big party to celebrate.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a nice police officer named Tom. Tom loved to help people. One day, Tom saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because his toy box was empty. Tom wanted to help Tim. He said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will supply you with toys!" Tom went to the toy store and bought some fun toys for Tim's empty toy box. Tom came back and gave the toys to Tim. Tim was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Tom!" They played together with the new toys all day. From that day on, Tom and Tim were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big tree with lots of fruit on it. Tom wanted to get the fruit, so he tried to climb the tree. But Tom was careless. He did not watch where he put his feet. He slipped and fell down from the tree. Tom felt sad and hurt. He wanted to become big and strong so he could climb the tree and get the fruit. Tom's mom saw him and helped him up. She told him, "Don't be careless, Tom. You need to be careful when you climb." Tom listened to his mom and tried again. This time, he was careful and he became a good climber. He got the fruit from the tree and shared it with his friends. They were all happy and enjoyed the sweet fruit together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the park. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big slide and his eyes lit up with joy. "Mom, look at the slide!" Tim said. "Can I go on it?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Go have fun!" Tim started to jog to the slide. He climbed up the steps and sat down at the top. The slide was very smooth. He pushed off and went down the slide very fast. "Weee!" Tim shouted as he slid down. At the bottom, Tim jumped off and ran back to his mom. "Mom, that was so much fun! Can I go again?" His mom laughed and said, "Yes, Tim. You can go as many times as you want." Tim spent the whole day at the park, going down the smooth slide and jogging back up to do it again. He was very happy and had lots of fun.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were playing in the park. They were running and jumping. The dog was very happy, but the cat was a little scared. The dog said, "Don't be scared, cat. We are just having fun!" Then, the cat saw a bird on a tree. The bird had a long neck. The cat wanted to catch the bird, but the dog said, "No, cat. We must not hurt the bird." The cat did not listen and tried to catch the bird. The bird saw the cat and flew away. The cat was sad and started to yawn. The dog laughed and said, "You were too careless, cat. Now the bird is gone." The cat looked at the dog and said, "You are right, dog. I should have listened to you." Suddenly, the bird came back with a big group of birds. The birds were not happy. They chased the dog and the cat away from the park. The dog and the cat were scared and ran fast. They learned that they should not be careless and should listen to each other.
