Category: Beginner
Title: The White Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big white dog. He lived in a small house with an old lady. The old lady loved the big white dog very much. They were best friends. One day, the big white dog and the old lady went for a walk. They walked and walked until they saw a little boy. The little boy had a red ball. The big white dog wanted to play with the red ball. "Can I play with your ball?" asked the big white dog. The little boy smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with my ball." The little boy and the big white dog played together. They passed the red ball back and forth, having lots of fun. The old lady watched them play and felt happy. After a while, it was time for the big white dog and the old lady to go home. They said goodbye to the little boy and walked back to their small house. That night, the big white dog and the old lady slept well. They had a great day playing with the little boy and the red ball. And they knew they would have many more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dish that lived in a big kitchen. The dish was frightened of the dark. Every day, the dish would do its best to stay in the light. It would hide under the warm sun that came through the window. One day, a big cloud came and covered the sun. The dish was very frightened. It knew that the dark was coming soon. The dish did not want to be in the dark. It wanted to find a way to stay in the light. Just then, the dish saw a big lamp in the corner of the kitchen. The dish had an idea. It would go to the lamp and be in the light. The dish did just that. It went to the lamp and the light was bright and warm. The dish was happy and not frightened anymore. And from that day on, the dish stayed near the lamp and was never scared of the dark again.

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Spot. Spot liked to dig holes in the ground. One day, he found a mine with many shiny rocks. He wanted to show his friend, Cat. "Cat, come see the mine I found!" said Spot. Cat went with Spot to see the mine. They both thought the shiny rocks were pretty. But they did not know how to get the rocks out of the mine. Spot had an idea. "I can solve this!" he said. He started to dig a big hole next to the mine. The rocks fell into the hole. Now, Spot and Cat could take the shiny rocks home. They were very happy and played with their new shiny rocks all day.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball all day. One day, Spot could not find his ball. He looked all around, but it was not there. Spot was very sad. Spot asked his friend, Cat, "Can you please help me find my ball?" Cat said, "Yes, I will help you." They looked under the tree, but it was not there. They looked by the pond, but it was not there. Spot and Cat did not know where to look next. Then, Bird flew down and said, "I know where your ball is." Bird pointed to the top of a big hill. Spot and Cat walked up the hill, and there was the ball. Spot was so happy, and he said, "Thank you, Bird!" Now, Spot, Cat, and Bird played with the ball together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a modern jacket with a zipper. He loved to zip it up and down. One day, Tim saw a little flag on the ground. He had an idea. Tim's dad helped him raise the flag high up on a big stick. Tim was very happy to see the flag fly. He knew something fun was going to happen soon. The next day, Tim's friends came to play. They saw the flag and knew Tim was ready to have fun. They all played games and laughed. Tim's zipper jacket and the flag brought everyone together for a great day.
