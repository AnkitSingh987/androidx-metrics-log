Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Dog

Ben liked to play with his toy horse. He named it Brownie and took it everywhere. He pretended that Brownie was a real horse and he was a cowboy. He made Brownie run and jump and neigh. One day, Ben went to the park with his mom and Brownie. He saw a big slide and wanted to try it. He left Brownie on a bench and climbed up the ladder. He slid down fast and laughed. He wanted to do it again. But when he looked for Brownie, he saw a smelly dog. The dog had Brownie in its mouth and was chewing it. Ben was scared and sad. He ran to the dog and shouted, "No! Give me back my horse!" The dog growled and ran away with Brownie. Ben chased the dog, but it was too fast. He lost sight of it and started to cry. His mom came and hugged him. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. Maybe we can find another toy horse for you." Ben shook his head. He said, "No, I want Brownie. He's my best friend. I miss him." He wished he had not left Brownie on the bench. He wished he had taken him to the slide. He wished he could see him again. The next day, Ben was walking with his mom to the store. He saw a sign on a fence. It said, "Lost dog. Reward." There was a picture of the smelly dog that took Brownie. Ben pointed to the sign and said, "Mom, look! That's the dog that stole my horse!" His mom looked at the sign and said, "You're right, Ben. That's the same dog. Maybe we can call the number and tell them what happened. Maybe they have your horse." Ben felt a spark of hope. He said, "Yes, let's call them. Maybe they can return my horse. Maybe Brownie is okay." He hoped that the dog had not hurt Brownie. He hoped that the dog's owners were nice. He hoped that he could get his horse back. He hoped that he could play with Brownie again.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to swim in the pool. They wear swim suits and goggles. They splash and kick in the water. "Look at me, Tim!" Sam says. He dives under the water. He swims like a fish. He sees a shiny coin at the bottom of the pool. He wants to get it. "Wow, Sam, you are fast!" Tim says. He dives after Sam. He tries to catch him. But Sam is too quick. He grabs the coin and swims up. "Ha ha, I got it!" Sam says. He shows Tim the coin. It is silver and round. He is happy. But the guard is not happy. He is lazy. He does not like to watch the kids. He likes to sit and read. He hears Sam and Tim. He sees them dive. He blows his whistle. "Hey, you two, stop diving!" the guard says. He is angry. "Diving is not safe. You can hurt your head. Give me that coin. It is not yours." Sam and Tim are scared. They swim to the edge of the pool. They give the coin to the guard. They say sorry. The guard takes the coin. He puts it in his pocket. He goes back to his chair. He reads his book. Sam and Tim are sad. They lost the coin. They do not want to dive anymore. They sit on the side of the pool. They wait for their mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy box full of toys. One day, he found a roll of tape in the toy box. Tim was very happy and wanted to play with the tape. Tim went to his mom and asked, "Can I play with the tape, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but don't make a mess." Tim promised to be careful. He played with the tape all day, sticking it to everything he could find. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's dad came home and saw the mess. He was very angry. He said, "Tim, you must clean up this mess or you will be punished." Tim felt sad and scared, but he knew he had to clean up. As he started to clean, he found a secret note under the tape. The note said, "I love you, Tim." It was from his mom. Tim smiled and knew that even if he made a mistake, his mom still loved him.
