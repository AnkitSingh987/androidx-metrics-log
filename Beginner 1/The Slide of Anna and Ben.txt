Category: Beginner
Title: The Slide of Anna and Ben

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. Anna wants to go on the slide, but Ben is scared. "Come on, Ben, it is fun!" Anna says. "No, it is too high. I do not like it." Ben says. Anna goes on the slide by herself. She slides down very fast. She laughs and shouts. Ben watches her. He wants to try, but he is still scared. "Ben, Ben, come here!" Anna says. She sees something on the ground. It is a jar of jelly. Someone left it there. Anna loves jelly. She opens the jar and takes a big spoon. "Look, Ben, jelly! Do you want some?" Anna says. Ben walks to Anna. He likes jelly too. He takes a spoon and dips it in the jar. He puts the jelly in his mouth. It is sweet and soft. He smiles. "Yum, yum, this is nice jelly!" Ben says. Anna and Ben eat more jelly. They do not see the man who owns the jar. He is looking for his jelly. He sees Anna and Ben. He is angry. He runs to them. "Hey, you, stop! That is my jelly! Give it back!" the man says. Anna and Ben hear the man. They are scared. They drop the spoons. They panic. They run away. They leave the jar behind. "Sorry, sorry, we did not know!" Anna and Ben say. They run to the slide. They climb up the ladder. They slide down together. They hold hands. They are still scared, but they are also happy. They laugh and shout. "Wow, that was fun!" Anna says. "Yes, it was. But no more jelly!" Ben says. They run to their mommies. They hug them. They tell them what happened. Their mommies are not happy. They say sorry to the man. They buy him a new jar of jelly. They also buy Anna and Ben some ice cream. They say ice cream is better than jelly. Anna and Ben agree. They eat their ice cream. They are nice and cool. They are not scared anymore. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time there was a smart girl. She had lots of work to do. She wanted to attach her work so she could share it with her friends. So she ran to her father's room and grabbed a stapler and staples. She carefully figured out how to use the stapler and attach her work all together. Once she was done, she smiled and waved her work in the air. She was so proud of how she attached her work so well. The girl's father saw her work and was impressed. He said, "You are so smart! You did such a great job. Well done!" The girl was so happy and ran off to show her friends her attached work. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to go to the shop with his mom. The shop was big and had many things. Tim liked to look at toys and books. One day, Tim saw a big, round ball. It was red and blue. Tim wanted the ball very much. Tim's mom saw that he liked the ball. She said, "Tim, if you are good today, you will receive a surprise." Tim was happy and tried to be very good. He helped his mom at the shop. He carried a small bag and did not run around like crazy. At the end of the day, Tim's mom took him back to the shop. She said, "You were a good boy today, Tim." She gave Tim the big, round ball. Tim was very happy. He played with the ball all day and all night. And that is how Tim received the best gift ever from the shop.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a very important job. Every day, she would take a long cord and use it to tie up the big gate. This gate kept all the animals safe inside their home. One sunny day, Mia went to tie the cord on the gate like always. But, oh no! The cord had a big split in it. Mia was sad and worried. What would she do now? The animals needed to be safe and the gate needed to be closed. Mia thought and thought. Then, she had a great idea! She took the two ends of the split cord and tied them together very tight. The cord was fixed! Mia tied the gate with the fixed cord and the animals were safe again. Mia was very happy and proud of her important job.
