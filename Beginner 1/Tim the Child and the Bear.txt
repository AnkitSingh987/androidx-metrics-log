Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Child and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a child named Tim. Tim was very useful at home. He helped his mom and dad with small jobs. He liked to touch things and learn about them. One day, Tim found a big box in his room. It was a surprise! He wanted to touch it and see what was inside. He opened the box and found a big, soft bear. The bear could talk! The bear said, "Hi, I am a magic bear. Thank you for touching me. I will help you be even more useful at home." Tim was so happy. He and the bear did many useful things together. They became best friends and had lots of fun.

---

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys at school. One day, Tim brings a new bag to school. It is big and blue and has a picture of a dinosaur on it. Mia notices the bag and thinks it is cool. "Wow, Tim, your bag is awesome!" Mia says. "Can I see it?" "Sure, Mia, you can see it," Tim says. He opens his bag and shows Mia his toys. He has a lot of toys. He has cars and trains and blocks and a teddy bear. Mia feels jealous. She wants to have a bag like Tim's. She wants to have a lot of toys like Tim's. She thinks Tim is lucky and she is not. She gets angry and sad. "Tim, can I have your bag?" Mia asks. "Please, please, please?" "No, Mia, you can't have my bag," Tim says. "It is mine. My mom gave it to me. You have your own bag." "But I don't like my bag," Mia says. "It is small and pink and has a picture of a flower on it. It is boring. I want your bag. Give it to me!" Mia tries to grab Tim's bag. Tim pulls it back. They start to fight. They pull and push and shout. The teacher hears them and comes to see what is wrong. "Tim, Mia, what are you doing?" the teacher asks. "Why are you fighting?" "Mia wants to take my bag," Tim says. "She is jealous of my bag and my toys." "That is not nice, Mia," the teacher says. "You can't take Tim's bag. You have to share and be kind. You have to say sorry to Tim." Mia does not want to say sorry. She does not want to share. She does not want to be kind. She wants to have Tim's bag. She stomps her feet and cries. The teacher is not happy. She takes Mia to the time-out corner. She tells Mia to stay there until she calms down and learns her lesson. Mia sits in the corner and feels more angry and sad. She does not have Tim's bag. She does not have any toys. She does not have any friends. She has a bad day.

One day, a curious little bear went for a walk in the woods. He saw many fun things like trees, flowers, and bugs. The bear liked to poke things with his nose. He poked leaves, he poked rocks, and he even poked a big log. As the bear was walking, he saw a big, round thing on the ground. It was soft and squishy. The curious bear poked it with his nose. Suddenly, the big, round thing moved! The bear was very surprised. The big, round thing was not a thing at all. It was a big, sleepy turtle! The turtle opened his eyes and looked at the bear. The bear and the turtle became friends. They played and poked things together in the woods all day long.

Sara loves to play dress-up with her dolls. She has many dresses, hats, shoes and masks for them. She likes to make them talk and act like different people. One day, she decides to play with her new pink mask. It has feathers, glitter and a big smile. She puts the mask on her favorite doll, Lily. She says, "Hello, I am Lily, the princess of the pink land. I am very pretty and kind. Do you want to be my friend?" She makes another doll, Ben, answer, "Yes, I want to be your friend. You are very pretty and kind. Can I see your mask?" Sara says, "No, you cannot see my mask. It is mine and only mine. You have to do what I say if you want to be my friend. I demand you to give me your cookie and your car." She makes Ben say, "But I like my cookie and my car. They are mine and only mine. Why do you demand me to give them to you?" Sara says, "Because I am the princess of the pink land and I can have whatever I want. If you do not give them to me, I will not be your friend and I will make you go away." She makes Ben say, "But that is not nice. That is mean. Friends do not demand things from each other. Friends share and play together. I do not want to be your friend if you are mean." Sara feels angry and sad. She does not want to lose Ben as a friend, but she also does not want to give up her pink mask. She thinks for a moment. She says, "Maybe you are right. Maybe friends do not demand things from each other. Maybe friends share and play together. I am sorry for being mean. Can we still be friends?" She makes Ben say, "Yes, we can still be friends. I am sorry for being rude. Can I see your mask?" Sara says, "Yes, you can see my mask. It is very pretty, but not as pretty as you. You are my best friend." She takes off the mask and shows it to Ben. They hug and smile. They share the cookie and the car. They play dress-up with the other dolls. They have fun. They are happy.
