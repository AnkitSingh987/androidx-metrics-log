Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Thoughtful Girl and the Peach

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Amy. She loved to eat peaches. One day, Amy went to the store with her mom to buy some peaches. At the store, Amy saw a big, round peach that she wanted. She tried to pick it up, but it was too big for her little hands. The peach fell on the ground and rolled away. Amy felt embarrass because everyone was looking at her. Amy's mom saw what happened and picked up the peach for her. She gave it to Amy with a big smile. Amy was happy and said, "Thank you, mommy." They went home and enjoyed the yummy peach together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wise old cat named Mr. Whiskers. Mr. Whiskers did not like ice. He would say, "I hate ice!" every time he saw it. All the other cats in the town loved ice, but not Mr. Whiskers. One hot day, all the cats in the town were playing with ice. They were sliding on it and having fun. Mr. Whiskers did not want to play. He sat under a tree and watched the other cats. Suddenly, the ice started to melt very fast. The cats were sad and did not know what to do. Mr. Whiskers thought for a moment. Then, he had an idea. He told the cats to bring all the ice to the tree. When they did, Mr. Whiskers climbed up the tree and pushed a big cloud over the sun. The cloud made shade, and the ice stopped melting. The other cats were happy and thanked Mr. Whiskers. From that day on, Mr. Whiskers did not hate ice anymore. He learned that sometimes, even things we do not like can help us in unexpected ways.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. It is very high and smooth. "Let's go on the slide!" Sara says. She runs to the ladder. "No, it is too scary!" Ben says. He stays on the ground. "Come on, Ben, it is fun!" Sara urges. She climbs to the top. "Please, Sara, wait for me!" Ben says. He is afraid, but he wants to be with Sara. He follows her slowly. Sara sits on the slide. She smiles at Ben. "Ready, set, go!" She says. She pushes herself down. "Wait, Sara, no!" Ben says. He is not ready. He tries to stop, but he slips. He falls off the ladder. Sara slides down fast. She does not see Ben. She laughs and enjoys the ride. Ben lands on the ground. He hurts his head and his arm. He cries and cries. Sara reaches the end of the slide. She looks for Ben. She does not see him. She hears him cry. She is scared and confused. "Ben, where are you?" She says. She runs to him. She sees him hurt. She feels sorry and sad. "Ben, I am sorry, I did not mean to leave you!" She says. She hugs him. Ben is angry and sad. He pushes her away. He does not want to talk to her. "Go away, Sara, you are a bad friend!" He says. He turns his face. Sara cries too. She does not know what to do. She lost her friend. She wishes she did not go on the slide.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with a porch. The porch was his favorite place to play. One hot day, Tim wanted to go outside and play on the porch with his toys. As Tim played on the hot porch, he saw a big red ball. The ball reminded him of his friend, Sam. Tim and Sam loved to play with the big red ball together. They would throw it, kick it, and roll it all around the yard. Tim decided to take the big red ball and go find Sam. They played together in the hot sun all day long. They laughed and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said goodbye to Sam and went back to his porch. He couldn't wait to play with Sam and the big red ball again.
