Category: Beginner
Title: Henry and the Sun

Henry was walking down the street with his mom. He was so happy. The sun was shining, the birds were singing, and his mom promised they were going to a restaurant. Henry felt a little unsure. He had never been to a restaurant before, and he was scared of how he would behave at a fancy place like that. When they arrived, Henry saw people in nice clothes. He felt embarrassed and started to cry. His mom held him close and whispered in his ear, "It's ok sweetheart. Don't worry, everything will be ok." She hugged him tight and smiled. Henry and his mom were seated at their table. But before long, the waiter asked them to leave. Henry started to cry because he thought he had done something wrong. His mom said softly, "No honey, it's not your fault. You were a perfectly behaved little boy." She went on to explain to Henry that the restaurant no longer allowed children anyway. Henry felt better, but he was still sorry that he had embarrassed his mom with all his crying. Henry and his mom made their way back home. Henry was still a little embarrassed, but his mom said, "It's ok, we will find another place to go. I'm sorry for what happened." Henry smiled and thought that even though it was embarrassing, it was still an adventure.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with dolls and cars. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. Anna goes first. She climbs up the steps. She sits on the slide. She smiles. She likes the slide. She slides down fast. She laughs. She is happy. Ben goes next. He climbs up the steps. He sits on the slide. He smiles. He likes the slide. He slides down fast. He screams. He is scared. He slides too fast. He does not stop at the end. He falls off the slide. He hits his head on the ground. He cries. He is hurt. Anna and Ben's moms run to him. They hug him. They say sorry. They take him to the doctor. The doctor gives him a band-aid. He says he will be okay. Anna and Ben are sad. They do not play with the slide anymore. They do not smile. They are not happy. They wish they did not go to the park. They are not grateful for the tip. The tip was bad. The tip made Ben fall. The tip made Ben cry. The tip made Anna and Ben sad. The end.

One fine day, Tim and his mom went to the park. Tim loved to play with his red ball. He would throw it high and run to catch it. His mom sat on a bench and watched him play. "Mom, look how high I can throw the ball!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "Wow, Tim! That's very high!" Just then, a big dog came running and took Tim's ball in its mouth. The dog ran away with the ball. Tim started to cry. He had lost his ball. But then, the dog came back with the ball. The dog dropped the ball at Tim's feet and wagged its tail. Tim stopped crying and started to laugh. He had a new friend to play with and his ball was back. It was a fine day after all.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big deer with shiny horns. They wanted to touch it, but they were scared. "Hello, deer," Tom said. "We are nice. Can we be your friends?" The deer looked at them with big eyes. It did not run away. It came closer and sniffed them. "Wow, your horns are so pretty," Lily said. "Did you polish them?" The deer nodded. It rubbed its horns on a tree. It liked to make them shiny. Tom and Lily smiled. They reached out their hands and gently touched the horns. They felt smooth and hard. The deer liked them too. It licked their faces with its tongue. It made them giggle. They played with the deer for a long time. They gave it some grass and flowers. They hugged it and thanked it. "Bye, deer," Tom and Lily said. "You are a good friend. We hope to see you again." The deer waved its tail. It watched them leave. It was happy.
