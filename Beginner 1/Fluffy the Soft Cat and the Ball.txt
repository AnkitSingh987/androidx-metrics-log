Category: Beginner
Title: Fluffy the Soft Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a soft little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her ball and chase butterflies. One day, Fluffy saw a big shower outside her house. The rain was falling down, and Fluffy wanted to play in it. Fluffy jumped into the shower and started to dance and spin. She saw a little bug who was also playing in the rain. The bug was trying to pinch the raindrops as they fell. Fluffy thought it was funny and wanted to try too. So, Fluffy and the bug pinched the raindrops together. After the shower stopped, Fluffy and the bug were very wet but happy. They became good friends and played together every day. Fluffy learned that even on rainy days, there can be fun and new friends to make.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, unusual hammer. This hammer was special because it could polish things. Tim loved to polish his toys with the hammer. One day, Tim saw a sad girl named Sue. Sue had a dirty toy car. Tim wanted to help Sue, so he used his unusual hammer to polish her toy car. Sue was very happy! The toy car started to shine and look new. Tim and Sue played with the shiny car and became good friends. The next day, Tim and Sue found a dirty park. They thought, "Why not use the unusual hammer to polish the park?" So they did. The park became clean and shiny. All the kids in the town came to play in the park. Tim and Sue learned that helping others can make them happy too.

Jill and Ben were playing in the garden. They saw a big bunch of grapes on the vine. They liked grapes. They wanted to eat some. "Let's pick some grapes," Jill said. "OK," Ben said. They ran to the vine and reached for the grapes. But the grapes were not ripe. They were green and hard. They did not taste good. "Yuck!" Jill said. "These grapes are sour!" "Maybe we can make them sweet," Ben said. He had an idea. He took a grape and squeezed it hard in his hand. He hoped it would turn into juice. But the grape did not turn into juice. It burst and squirted in his face. He felt a sting in his eye. He dropped the grape and cried. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Ben said. "It hurts!" Jill saw Ben crying. She felt sorry for him. She wanted to help him. She took a grape and squeezed it too. She thought she could wash his eye with the juice. But the grape did not wash his eye. It squirted in his other eye. He felt another sting. He cried louder. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Ben said. "Stop it!" Jill was scared. She did not mean to hurt Ben. She dropped the grape and ran away. Ben was alone. He had two sore eyes. He could not see. He was angry and sad. He did not like grapes. He did not like Jill. He wished he had never played in the garden.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a fun time playing on the swings and slide. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim was very happy. As they played, Tim saw a big, rough rock. He wanted to climb on it. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim. The rock is rough, and you might fall." Tim didn't listen and climbed the rock anyway. He slipped and fell, making his knee hurt. Tim felt embarrassed and started to cry. His mom came over and said, "It's okay, Tim. Everyone falls sometimes. Let's clean up your knee and keep playing." Tim smiled and they had a great time at the park for the rest of the day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to build a big house. He wanted to be rich. He had many blocks to build with. Tim worked hard all day. The sun was hot, but he did not stop. A girl named Sue saw Tim. She said, "Tim, why do you build? You can play with me." Tim said, "No, I want to be rich. I need a big house with a lock." Tim built and built. The house was very big now. He put the lock on the door. But Tim was sad. He was alone in the big house. Sue was gone. Tim was rich, but he had no friends.
