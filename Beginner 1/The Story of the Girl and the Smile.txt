Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Girl and the Smile

One day there was a little girl who really felt sad. She was crying. Tears ran down her face and she felt so alone. But then she remembered something her mum had said one time. She said that when you are sad, you have to look for something to smile about. So the little girl thought really hard. She thought about all the times she had laughed and smiled before. She thought about the things she loved doing and the people who loved her. Finally, the little girl smiled. It was a thoughtful smile and it made her feel so much better. She was still a bit sad, but she knew that if she kept looking for things to smile about, everything would get better.

Jill and Ben are playing with blocks. They like to build towers and houses and cars. Jill has a lot of pretty blocks. She has red and blue and yellow and green blocks. Ben has only brown and black blocks. He wants some of Jill's blocks. "Can I have some of your blocks, Jill?" Ben asks. "No, they are mine. You have your own blocks," Jill says. "But your blocks are pretty. My blocks are ugly. Please share with me," Ben begs. "No, no, no. Go away, Ben. Leave me alone," Jill says. Ben is angry. He does not like Jill. He does not like her blocks. He wants to make her sad. He waits until Jill is not looking. Then he runs to her tower and strikes it with his hand. The tower falls down. The blocks scatter everywhere. Jill sees what Ben did. She screams. "Ben, you are mean! You are bad! You broke my tower! You made a mess!" Jill cries. Jill runs to Ben and hits him. Ben hits her back. They fight and fight. They hurt each other. They cry and cry. Their mom hears them. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees the blocks and the tower and the fight. She is angry. She is sad. "Jill, Ben, what have you done? Why are you fighting? Why did you break the tower? Why did you make a mess?" Mom asks. Jill and Ben try to explain. They blame each other. They say sorry. But Mom does not listen. She gives them an order. "Jill, Ben, you are in trouble. You have to stop fighting. You have to clean up the blocks. You have to go to your rooms. You have no more play time today. You have to think about what you did," Mom says. Jill and Ben are sorry. They are sad. They are scared. They do what Mom says. They clean up the blocks. They go to their rooms. They have no more play time. They have no more fun. They have a bad ending.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big flood near their home. The water was everywhere, and they couldn't play in the park. Tim was sad because it was boring to stay inside all day. Tim said to Spot, "Let's go to the big tree and see if we can play there." So, they went to the big tree. They saw a little girl named Sue crying. Sue said, "I was playing in the water, and now my toy is gone. My mom will punish me because it was her toy when she was little." Tim and Spot wanted to help Sue. They looked around and saw the toy in the water. Spot jumped in the water and got the toy. Sue was very happy and thanked Tim and Spot. They all played together under the big tree until the flood went away.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw a pretty flower and wanted to cut it. Sue got her scissors and started to cut the flower. But, she was not careful and cut her finger. Sue felt weak and started to cry. Sue's mom heard her cry and came to help. She saw the cut on Sue's finger and said, "Don't worry, I will fix it." Mom got a bandage from the house. She put it on Sue's finger and gave her a hug. Now, Sue's finger was not weak anymore. She was happy and went to play again. Sue learned to be careful when she cut things. She played all day and did not get hurt again.
