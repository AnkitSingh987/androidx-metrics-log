Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Potato and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a fair little town, there lived a potato named Tim. Tim was a happy potato, but he had a problem. He would tremble when he met new friends. One day, as Tim was walking down the street, he saw a cat. The cat was so cute and fluffy. Tim wanted to say hi, but he started to tremble. He was scared the cat would not like him. Tim took a deep breath and said, "Hi, I am Tim the potato. Will you be my friend?" The cat looked at Tim and smiled. "Of course, Tim! I am Cat. I like you, even if you tremble." They became best friends and played all day long. From that day on, Tim learned that it was okay to tremble, because true friends will like you just the way you are.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big tree with many green needles. Lily says, "What is this tree?" Tom says, "This is a pine tree. My dad told me. He knows a lot of things." Lily says, "Can we touch it?" Tom says, "Yes, but be careful. The needles are sharp. They can hurt you." They go closer to the pine tree. They touch the needles gently. They smell the pine tree. It smells good. They see a brown cone hanging from a branch. Lily says, "What is this?" Tom says, "This is a pine cone. It has seeds inside. The birds and squirrels like to eat them." Lily says, "Can we take it?" Tom says, "No, we should leave it for the animals. They need it more than we do." They hear a loud noise. They turn around. They see a man running and shouting. He has a red hat and a yellow coat. He looks crazy. He says, "I am the king of the pine trees! Bow to me!" Tom and Lily are scared. They run away from the man. They find their moms. They tell them what they saw. Their moms hug them. They say, "Don't worry. He is just a silly man. He is not the king of anything. You are safe with us." Tom and Lily are glad. They say, "We like pine trees. But we don't like crazy men."

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his football. He would kick the ball in the park every day. The sun was mild and the sky was blue. One day, Tim saw a big door. He wanted to see what was inside. So, he entered the door with his football. Inside, he found a big room with many toys. Tim was very happy and started playing with the toys. But then, the door closed and Tim could not go out. He was sad and scared. He tried to open the door with his football, but it did not work. Tim was stuck in the room forever.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. She saw a big jar of candy. Sue wanted the candy very much. She asked her mom, "Can I have some candy, please?" Her mom said, "No, not today." Sue was sad but she had a plan. She thought she could manage to get the candy by herself. Sue found a useful stick and tried to reach the candy jar. She pushed and pushed, but the jar was too high. Suddenly, the jar fell down and broke. Candy was everywhere, but Sue was scared. Her mom came and saw the mess. Sue knew she was in trouble. She did not get any candy, and she had to clean up the mess.

One day, a pigeon named Zip was in the park. Zip was not a bad pigeon, but he liked to play tricks on his friends. Zip would hide and jump out to scare them. His friends would laugh, but sometimes they got mad. One day, Zip played a trick on a big dog. The dog did not like the trick and chased Zip. Zip was scared and flew away fast. He flew so high that he got lost. Zip was sad and wanted to go home. Zip met a kind bird who showed him the way home. Zip was happy to see his friends again. He learned that playing tricks can be fun, but not to do it too much. Zip promised to be a better friend and everyone was happy.
