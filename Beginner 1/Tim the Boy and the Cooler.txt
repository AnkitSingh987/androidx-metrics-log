Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cooler

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big cooler. He loved to play with the cooler. He would put toys inside and push it around the house. Tim was very happy. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was deaf. She could not hear Tim when he talked. But they both wanted to play together. They decided to unite and find a way to have fun. Tim showed Sue his cooler. Sue smiled and put her toys in the cooler too. They pushed the cooler together, laughing and playing. Even though Sue could not hear, they still had fun. Tim and Sue became best friends. And they learned that when they unite, they can do anything.

Tom and Lily are players. They like to play with toys and games. But sometimes they fight over the toys and games. They want to have the same toy or game. One day, they find a big box of toys and games in the basement. They are very happy. They open the box and see many toys and games. They see cars, dolls, puzzles, books, and more. They want to play with everything. Tom sees a red car. He likes red cars. He takes the red car and says, "This is my car. I want to play with it." Lily sees the red car too. She likes red cars too. She says, "No, this is my car. I saw it first. Give it to me." Tom and Lily fight over the red car. They pull and tug and shout. They do not share. They do not listen. They are angry. The red car is broken. It breaks in two. Tom and Lily are sad. They cry. They look at the broken car. They feel sorry. They say, "I'm sorry. I was mean. I should have shared. Can we be friends?" They hug and make up. They find another toy to play with. They share and take turns. They are happy. They are good players.

Once upon a time, a big, strong man lived in a small house. He was very proud of his big chest filled with shiny things. One day, he heard a knock on his door. A small girl with a big smile arrived at his house. She said, "I am lost. Can I stay with you for a while?" The big, strong man said, "Yes, you can stay with me." The small girl was very happy. They played, laughed, and had fun together. The big, strong man showed the girl his big chest filled with shiny things. The girl's eyes grew wide, and she said, "Wow! You must be very proud!" One day, the big, strong man went to the market. When he came back, the small girl was gone. He looked around and saw that his big chest was open. All his shiny things were gone too! The big, strong man was very sad. He was not proud anymore. The small girl, who he thought was his friend, took all his shiny things and left. The big, strong man learned that not everyone is who they seem to be.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big nose that she liked to keep very clear. She loved to smell the pretty flowers in her yard. One day, a naughty bee came to the yard. It did not want to leave Sue's flowers alone. The bee flew close to Sue's nose, and it made her scared. She did not want the bee to hurt her nose or make it not clear. Sue thought of a plan. She ran inside her house and found a big jar. She went back to the yard and caught the bee in the jar. Then, she took the jar far away from her house and let the bee go. The bee left Sue's flowers, and Sue's nose stayed clear and happy.

One day, a girl named Lily went to play outside. She saw her dad working in the yard. He had a rake in his hand. The rake was big and red. The yard was full of leaves. Lily asked, "Dad, can I help you?" Her dad smiled and said, "Yes, you can have this small rake." Lily was happy. She took the small rake and started to clear the leaves. Lily and her dad worked together. They made big piles of leaves. The yard became clear and clean. They had fun and laughed a lot. When they finished, Lily said, "Look, Dad! The yard is clear now!" Her dad hugged her and said, "Thank you for your help, Lily! You did a great job!" Lily felt proud and happy. She had helped her dad with the rake and made the yard clear. They went inside to have a yummy snack together.
