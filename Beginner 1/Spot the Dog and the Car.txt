Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a very tired dog. He liked to sleep a lot. One day, Spot's family needed to load the car for a trip. They put bags and toys in the car. Spot wanted to help, but he was too tired. Spot's family called for him to get in the car. Spot yawned and got up. He walked to the car and jumped in. Now, Spot was ready for the trip. He lay down in the car and went back to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a park. In the park, there was a big tree. The tree was very old and very tall. All the kids and their moms and dads would come to the park to play and have fun. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw the big tree and he liked it a lot. He said, "Wow, I admire that big tree!" Tim wanted to climb the tree, but his mom said it was too high and too filthy. The tree had lots of dirt and mud on it. Tim was sad, but he still had fun at the park. He played with his friends and they all ran around. They played hide and seek and Tim hid behind the big tree. Even though the tree was filthy, Tim still admired it. And at the end of the day, Tim and his mom went home happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a lovely toy named Mr. Bear. Mr. Bear was very soft and had a nice texture. Amy liked to hold Mr. Bear all the time. One day, Amy went to the park with her mom and Mr. Bear. They played on the swings and the slide. Amy held Mr. Bear tight so he could play too. They were having lots of fun. But then, a big wind came and took Mr. Bear away. Amy was very sad. She looked and looked, but she could not find Mr. Bear. Amy went home without her lovely toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was nervous because she had to choose between two cups. One cup had juice, and the other cup had coffee. Lucy knew she was not allowed to drink coffee, but she wanted to try it. Lucy thought about it for a moment. She knew her mom would be upset if she chose the coffee, but she was curious. So, she took a deep breath and picked up the cup with the coffee in it. As soon as Lucy tasted the coffee, she made a face. It was not what she expected. It was bitter and yucky. She felt bad for not listening to her mom. The coffee made her tummy hurt, and she cried. Her mom came and saw what happened. Lucy was in trouble and felt sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very sleepy one morning. He did not want to go to school. But his mom said, "You have to go, Tim. You have math today." So, Tim went to school. In math class, he felt even more sleepy. He tried to stay awake, but it was hard. He started to lean on his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Don't lean on me, Tim. You need to wake up!" Tim tried his best to stay awake in math class. He counted numbers and added them together. By the end of class, he was not sleepy anymore. He was happy that he went to school and learned new things in math.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a rude bug. The bug was one inch long. Tom said, "Hi, bug!" But the bug did not understand. The bug just looked at Tom. Tom said, "Bug, why are you so rude?" The bug looked at Tom and said, "I am not rude. I just do not understand big words." Tom was surprised. He did not know that bugs could talk. Tom said, "I will use small words, so you can understand me." The bug was happy. They talked and played all day. In the end, the rude bug was not rude anymore. They became good friends.
