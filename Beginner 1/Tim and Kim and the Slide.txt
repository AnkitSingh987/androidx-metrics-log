Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Kim and the Slide

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Let's go on the slide!" Tim says. "OK!" Kim says. They run to the slide. They see a number on the slide. It is a four. "What is that?" Kim asks. "That is a number. It is a four. It means the slide is deep." Tim says. "Deep? What is deep?" Kim asks. "Deep means far from the top to the bottom. Like a pool or a hole." Tim says. "Is deep fun?" Kim asks. "Yes, deep is fun. You go fast and feel the wind." Tim says. "OK, let's try it!" Kim says. They climb to the top of the slide. They sit on the slide. They hold hands. "Ready?" Tim asks. "Ready!" Kim says. They slide down the slide. They go fast and feel the wind. They laugh and scream. They reach the bottom of the slide. They are happy. "That was fun!" Tim says. "Yes, that was fun!" Kim says. They hug each other. They want to go on the slide again. They run to the slide. They see another number on the slide. It is a five. "What is that?" Kim asks. "That is a number. It is a five. It means the slide is deeper." Tim says. "Deeper? What is deeper?" Kim asks. "Deeper means more far from the top to the bottom. Like a lake or a well." Tim says. "Is deeper fun?" Kim asks. "I don't know. Let's find out!" Tim says. They climb to the top of the slide. They sit on the slide. They hold hands. "Ready?" Tim asks. "Ready!" Kim says. They slide down the slide. They go faster and feel more wind. They laugh and scream louder. They reach the bottom of the slide. They are happier. "That was more fun!" Tim says. "Yes, that was more fun!" Kim says. They hug each other. They want to go on the slide again and again. They love the slide. They love the numbers. They love the deep and the deeper. They love each other.

Tom and Lily liked to play in the attic. It was a big and quiet place with many old things. They could find books, toys, clothes and pictures that belonged to their mom and dad when they were little. Sometimes they pretended to be pirates, or astronauts, or teachers. One day, they found a big box with a sign on it. The sign said "Do not open". Tom and Lily were curious. They wanted to see what was inside the box. They looked at each other and nodded. They lifted the lid and peeked inside. They saw a lot of shiny things. There were coins, jewels, medals and keys. They also saw a paper with more signs on it. The signs looked like letters, but they did not know what they meant. They were not the same letters that they learned at school. "Wow, look at all these treasures!" Tom said. He picked up a coin and held it to the light. It sparkled and made a sound. "Maybe this is a secret map!" Lily said. She pointed at the paper. She tried to read the signs, but she could not. She made up some words. "It says... go to the... big... tree... and dig... under the... rock." "Let's do it!" Tom said. He put the coin in his pocket and took the paper. He and Lily ran down the stairs and out of the house. They looked for the big tree in the yard. They did not know that the box and the paper belonged to their grandpa. He was a soldier and a spy when he was young. He had many stories and secrets that he never told anyone. He kept his memories in the attic, where they were safe and peaceful. He did not want anyone to touch them or see them. He did not know that Tom and Lily had found them. He was taking a nap on the couch. He was dreaming of his adventures.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a hot day, so they put on their hats before leaving the house. Tim loved to play at the park and was very excited. At the park, Tim saw his friend, Lily. They both smiled and said, "Hi!" They decided to play a fun game of jump. They took turns jumping as high as they could. Tim jumped high, and Lily jumped even higher. They laughed and clapped for each other. After a while, they got tired and sat under a big tree to rest. Tim and Lily drank some cold water that their moms had brought for them. They talked about how much fun they had jumping at the park on the hot day. Then, it was time to go home. They said bye to each other and promised to play again soon.
