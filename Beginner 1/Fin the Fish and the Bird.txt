Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big pond. The pond was his home. Fin had a friend named Bee. Bee lived in a tree near the pond. They liked to play together. One day, Bee saw a wild bird. The bird had a shiny scale in its beak. Bee did not know what to do. So, Bee went to ask Fin for help. Fin knew what to do. They both went to the bird and asked for the scale back. The wild bird was nice. It gave the scale back to Fin and Bee. They said thank you to the bird. Then they went back to play by the pond. They were happy to have the scale back. And they knew they could always help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play dress-up with her friends. One day, she found a pretty veil in her mom's closet. She put it on her head and felt like a princess. As Sue walked around her room, she saw her cat, Max. Max had big, fierce eyes. Max was a good cat, but sometimes he liked to play rough. Sue wanted to show Max her pretty veil, so she went to him. But as Sue walked to Max, she did not see her toys on the floor. She stumbled on a toy car and fell down. Sue was okay, but her veil was a little dirty. Max came to Sue and licked her hand. Sue knew Max was sorry for her fall. They played together and had a fun day.

One day, a girl named Amy was playing with her toys. She had a doll named Sue. They were good friends. Sue had a big thumb. Amy said, "Sue, why is your thumb so big?" Sue said, "I don't know, but I like it." Just then, a little dog came into the room. The dog saw Sue's big thumb and thought it was a toy. The dog took Sue's thumb in his mouth and ran away. Amy said, "Oh no! Sue's thumb is gone!" But then, the dog came back. He had found a toy that looked like Sue's thumb. The dog gave the toy to Sue, and Sue had a new thumb. Now Sue and the dog were good friends too.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The dog had an owner. The owner loved the dog. One day, the owner and the dog went for a walk. They went to a big park. The park had many trees and flowers. The owner saw a hidden spot behind a big tree. The owner wanted to sit there. The owner said to the dog, "Sit here with me." The owner sat down with the dog. The owner did not attach the dog to a leash. The dog saw a squirrel. The squirrel was running and playing. The dog wanted to play too. The dog ran after the squirrel. The owner called the dog, but the dog did not come back. The owner looked for the dog. The owner called the dog's name. The dog did not hear the owner. The dog was lost. The owner was sad. The dog was sad too. They could not find each other. The owner went home without the dog. The dog stayed in the park. The dog and the owner did not have a happy ending.

One day, a little boy named Tim was walking in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to admire it. He looked up and saw a pretty bird. The bird had a hurt wing. Tim wanted to help the bird. "Hi bird, I see your wing is hurt. Can I help you?" Tim asked. The bird looked at Tim with its big eye and nodded. Tim gently picked up the bird and took it to his mom. Tim's mom knew how to fix hurt wings. Tim's mom fixed the bird's wing. The bird was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to talk! "Thank you, Tim, for helping me. I am a magic bird. I will give you a wish for helping me." Tim was so surprised! He wished for a new friend to play with. The magic bird became a little boy, just like Tim. They played and had fun together all day. And they were best friends forever.
