Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Park

One day, a big bird with many feathers went to the park. The bird was mighty and strong. All the other birds liked to watch the mighty bird fly high in the sky. The mighty bird saw a little bird sitting on a branch. The little bird was sad because it had lost a feather. The mighty bird said, "Why are you sad little bird?" The little bird said, "I lost my feather and now I can't fly high like you." The mighty bird wanted to help the little bird. It looked all around and found the lost feather. The mighty bird said, "Here is your feather, little bird. Now you can fly high too." The little bird was very happy and thanked the mighty bird. Now, the little bird could fly high in the sky again. The two birds became good friends. They would talk and play together every day at the park. The mighty bird and the little bird were very happy.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with their toys, read books and eat ice cream. One day, they went to the park with their moms. They saw a big truck with a sign that said "Ice Cream". They ran to the truck and asked for ice cream. "What flavor do you want?" the man in the truck asked. "I want chocolate!" Lily said. "I want vanilla!" Ben said. The man gave them each a cone with ice cream. They licked their cones and smiled. The ice cream was sweet and cold. "Yummy!" Lily said. "Delicious!" Ben said. They walked to a bench and sat down. They saw a dog running in the grass. The dog was brown and fluffy. It looked friendly. "Can we pet the dog?" Lily asked her mom. "Sure, but be careful. Ask the owner first," her mom said. Lily and Ben got up and walked to the dog. They saw a lady holding a leash. "Hello, can we pet your dog?" Lily asked. "Of course, his name is Rex. He is very nice," the lady said. Lily and Ben reached out their hands and touched Rex. He wagged his tail and licked their faces. "He is so soft and warm!" Lily said. "He is so cute and funny!" Ben said. They laughed and played with Rex. They forgot about their ice cream. Their cones started to melt. The ice cream dripped on the ground. Rex smelled the ice cream. He liked ice cream. He wanted some. He jumped and grabbed Lily's cone. He bit it and ate it. Lily screamed. "Hey, that's mine!" Lily said. She was angry and sad. She loved her ice cream. She wanted it back. She cried. "Bad dog! Bad dog!" she yelled. Rex did not understand. He thought he was good. He liked Lily. He wanted to make her happy. He licked her face. "Stop it! Go away!" Lily said. She pushed Rex away. Rex felt hurt. He whimpered. The lady saw what happened. She was sorry. "Oh, I'm so sorry. Rex didn't mean to. He just loves ice cream. Here, I'll buy you another one," she said. She took some money and went to the truck. She came back with a new cone for Lily. "Here you go, sweetie. I hope this makes you feel better," she said. She gave Lily the cone. Lily took it. She looked at it. She did not want it. She threw it on the ground. "I don't want it! I hate ice cream! I hate dogs! I hate you!" she said. She ran to her mom and hugged her. She sobbed. The lady was shocked. She did not know what to say. She felt bad. "Come on, Rex. Let's go," she said. She took Rex and left. Rex looked back at Lily. He was sad. He wanted to be her friend. He wanted to heal her hurt. He did not know how. He sighed.

One day, a boy named Tim found some rare blocks. They were very pretty and shiny. Tim wanted to play with them and build a big tower. He asked his friend, Sam, to come and play with him. Sam came over and saw the rare blocks. "Wow, those are so cool!" said Sam. "But we need to be careful. We don't want to break them." Tim nodded, and they started to build the tower. They made it very tall and strong. As they were playing, a little bird flew into the room. The bird saw the shiny blocks and wanted to play too. The bird flew down and landed on the tower. The tower began to shake, and the blocks started to fall. Tim and Sam were scared that the rare blocks would break. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird caught the blocks with its wings before they hit the ground. Tim and Sam were so happy! The bird helped them put the blocks back, and they all played together. From that day on, Tim, Sam, and the bird were the best of friends.
