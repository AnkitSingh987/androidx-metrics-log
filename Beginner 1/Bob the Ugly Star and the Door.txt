Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Ugly Star and the Door

Once upon a time, there was an ugly star named Bob. Bob was sad because he was not pretty like the other stars. One day, Bob saw a door in the sky. He wanted to enter and see what was inside. As Bob entered the door, he saw a big room with many colors. In the room, there was a kind star named Lisa. Lisa said, "Hi, I am Lisa. Why are you sad?" Bob said, "I am ugly. I am not pretty like you." Lisa wanted to help Bob. She had an idea. "Let's paint you with pretty colors," she said. Bob liked the idea. They painted him with red, blue, and yellow. Now, Bob was not ugly anymore. He was a beautiful star. Bob and Lisa were happy and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his big, round basketball. Every day, he would go outside and bounce it up and down. He liked the way it felt in his hands. One day, Tim's mom gave him some sweet, colorful stickers. She told him he could decorate anything he wanted with them. Tim thought and thought about what he wanted to make pretty. He looked at his basketball and smiled. Tim took his sweet stickers and started to decorate his basketball. He put red, blue, and yellow stickers all over it. When he was done, his basketball was the most beautiful one in the whole world. Tim was so happy, and he played with his basketball every day.

Ben liked to play with his truck. He made it go vroom vroom on the floor. He liked to pretend it was a big truck that could carry many things. He liked to fill it with his blocks and toys. One day, he saw a new boy in the park. The boy had a truck too. It was red and shiny. Ben wanted to see it. He walked over to the boy and said hello. He wanted to introduce himself and his truck. But the boy did not say hello back. He did not look at Ben. He hugged his truck and turned away. He did not want to share his truck. He did not want to play with Ben. Ben felt sad. He did not understand why the boy was so mean. He thought it was difficult to make a new friend. He wished the boy would be nice and let him see his truck. He decided to try again. He smiled and said, "My name is Ben. This is my truck. It is blue and old. But I like it. Do you want to play with me and my truck?" The boy looked at Ben. He saw his smile and his truck. He felt curious. He said, "My name is Sam. This is my truck. It is red and new. But I am bored. Do you want to play with me and your truck?" Ben was happy. He said, "Yes, I do. Let's play together. We can make our trucks go vroom vroom and carry many things. We can be friends." Sam nodded. He said, "OK. Let's play together. We can share our trucks and have fun. We can be friends." Ben and Sam played with their trucks. They made them go vroom vroom and carry many things. They laughed and talked. They introduced their trucks to each other. They found out they had many things in common. They had a good time. They learned that it was not difficult to make a new friend. All they needed was a smile and a truck.

Tom and Sam were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big goose near the pond. The goose had blue feathers and a long neck. "Look, a blue goose!" Tom said. "Let's go and touch it!" Sam was scared. He said, "No, Tom, don't do that. The goose might bite you or flap its wings. It is not a toy." But Tom did not listen. He ran to the goose and tried to grab its tail. The goose did not like that. It turned around and hissed at Tom. Then it slapped him with its wing. Tom fell down and cried. Sam ran to help Tom. He said, "I told you, Tom, you should not bother the goose. It is angry and hurt you. Are you okay?" Tom was not okay. He had a big bruise on his face and a cut on his lip. He said, "I'm sorry, Sam, I was silly. I wanted to see the blue goose. But it was mean and slapped me. It hurt a lot." Sam said, "It's okay, Tom, I forgive you. But next time, listen to me. And leave the animals alone. They are not for playing. Come on, let's go home and tell your mom. She will make you feel better." They walked away from the pond. The goose watched them and honked. It was glad they were gone. It did not like children who tried to touch it. It wanted to be left alone. It was a blue goose, but not a happy goose.
