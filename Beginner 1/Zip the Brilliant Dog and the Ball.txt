Category: Beginner
Title: Zip the Brilliant Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant little dog named Zip. Zip loved to run and play all day. One day, Zip saw a big red ball in the park. He was so excited to play with it. But he didn't know that the ball had a secret. Zip met a new friend named Sally. Sally was a sweet girl who loved dogs. She said, "Hi, Zip! Do you want to play with the big red ball?" Zip barked happily and wagged his tail. They played with the ball, and it was so much fun! As they played, the big red ball suddenly popped! Out came a tiny, friendly frog. The frog said, "Thank you for playing with the ball. I was stuck inside!" Zip and Sally were so surprised. To say thank you, the frog gave Zip a kiss on his nose. Zip felt so special and he knew that he had made a new friend.

One day, a kind boy named Tim found a big ball. He wanted to play with it. So, he went to his friend, Sue, and asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Sue said, "Yes, let's throw the ball!" They played near a television. They were very careful not to hit it. They threw the ball to each other. They had a lot of fun. But then, the ball went too high. It hit the television! Tim and Sue were sad. They knew they should not play near the television. Next time, they will play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a sheep named Sam. Sam had a lot of wool. His wool was very weird. It was not like the wool of other sheep. It was very soft and had many colors. One day, Sam was going to play with his friends. But his mom said, "Wait, Sam! We need to delay your playtime. We must cut your wool today." Sam was sad, but he knew he had to do it. His mom cut his weird wool and put it in a big bag. After the wool was cut, Sam went to play with his friends. They all looked at his new haircut and said, "Wow, Sam! Your weird wool is gone, but you still look great!" Sam was happy and played with his friends all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin was a pale blue fish who lived in a big pond. He had many friends, but he liked to play with his best friend, Gilly the frog, the most. One day, Fin and Gilly were swimming and jumping in the water. Fin was not feeling very good. He had a hard time taking in air with his lung. Gilly saw that Fin was not happy and asked, "What's wrong, Fin?" Fin told Gilly about his lung and how it was hard to swim and play. Gilly said, "Don't worry, Fin. I will help you. You can trust me." Gilly held Fin close and swam with him to a safe place to rest. Fin felt better after some time. He was very happy to have a friend like Gilly. They played together every day and always trusted each other. And they lived happily ever after in their big pond.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big toy box with lots of toys. One day, Lucy's friend, Tim, came to play with her. They had lots of fun playing with the toys. Tim saw a shiny toy car in the box and wanted to take it home. He asked Lucy, "Can I take this car home? I like it a lot!" Lucy thought for a moment and said, "No, Tim. That would be bad. We cannot take things that are not ours." Tim felt sad, but he knew Lucy was telling the truth. They continued to play with the toys and had a great time. At the end of the day, Tim went home with a big smile on his face. He learned that telling the truth and not taking things is good.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to ride his bike. His bike was in the garage. He went to the garage and found his bike. It was red and shiny. Tim was happy to ride his bike. Tim rode his bike up and down the street. He saw his friends and they all waved at him. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim was having a lot of fun riding his bike. But then, Tim fell off his bike. He felt hurt and started to cry. His mom heard him and came running. She hugged Tim and put a band-aid on his hurt knee. Tim felt better and went back to ride his bike with a big smile on his face.
