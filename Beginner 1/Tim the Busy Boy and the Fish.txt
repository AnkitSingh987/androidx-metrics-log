Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Busy Boy and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a busy little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he found a big net in his yard. He had a great idea. Tim took the net and went to the pond. He saw many fish swimming in the water. He wanted to feed the fish. He put some food in the net and held it in the water. The fish saw the food and swam to the net. They ate the food that Tim gave them. Tim was very happy. He went home and told his mom about the fish. From that day on, Tim loved to feed the fish with his net.

Tom is a dog who likes to play. He likes to run, jump, bark and chew. He does not like to behave. He does not listen to his owner, Sam. Sam tells Tom to sit, stay, and come, but Tom does not do it. Tom thinks it is fun to do what he wants. One day, Sam goes out to work. He leaves Tom at home. He says, "Tom, be a good dog. Do not make a mess. I will be back soon." He gives Tom a newspaper to read. Tom does not like to read. He likes to chew. He chews the newspaper into pieces. He makes a big mess. Then, Tom sees a ball. He likes to play with balls. He runs to the ball. He does not see the window. He crashes into the window. He breaks the glass. He hurts his paw. He cries. He is scared. Sam comes back. He sees the mess. He sees the broken window. He sees Tom. He is angry. He says, "Tom, you are a bad dog. You did not behave. You did not listen. You made a mess. You broke the window. You hurt yourself. You are in trouble." He takes Tom to the vet. He pays a lot of money. He is sad. The moral of the story is: You should behave. You should listen. You should not make a mess. You should not break things. You should not hurt yourself. You should be a good dog.

One day, a queen lived in a big castle. She was a nice queen and had many friends. But there was a rude man who did not like the queen. He wanted to make the queen vanish. The rude man went to the queen and said, "I have a magic trick! Can I show you?" The queen said, "Yes, I love magic tricks!" The rude man showed her a box and said, "Get in this box, and I will make you vanish." The queen got into the box, and the rude man closed it. But, when the rude man tried to make the queen vanish, something unexpected happened. The rude man vanished instead! The queen came out of the box and saw that the rude man was gone. She said, "Oh no! Where did he go?" The queen's friends came and said, "Don't worry, he was rude. Now he can't make you vanish!" They all laughed and lived happily ever after.

Once there was a young boy who had a special friend that he liked to visit. His friend was an unusual old man who lived alone in a cabin in the woods. One day, when the boy went to visit, the old man suggested that they play a game. The old man said, "I know a special game that I think you would like. It is called 'find the cabinet.' All you need to do is find the unusual cabinet I'm hiding in the room. You can look anywhere, but you'll never guess where I'm hiding!" The boy searched all around the room, but he just couldn't find the cabinet. He was starting to get frustrated when the old man said, "Tell you what let's do. I recommend we try one more place and if that doesn't work, then I'll tell you where it is." The boy looked in the last place and suddenly he saw the unusual cabinet tucked away behind the door. He couldn't believe his eyes! He ran to the old man and said, "I found it!" The old man smiled and said, "Good job! I recommend you try that same game at home." The boy thanked the old man and said goodbye, excited to show his family his find. The boy went home and exclaimed, "I'm going to find an unusual cabinet!" His family was so proud of him and couldn't wait to see the surprise he had in store
