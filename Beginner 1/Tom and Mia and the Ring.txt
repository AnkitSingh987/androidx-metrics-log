Title: Tom and Mia and the Ring

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big bin in the park. The bin is dirty and smelly. They wonder what is inside. "Let's open it and see," Tom says. He pulls the lid of the bin. Mia helps him. Inside the bin, they see many things. They see cans, bottles, papers, and clothes. They also see a shiny thing. It is a ring. The ring is silver and has a blue stone. "Wow, look at this!" Mia says. She picks up the ring. It is dusty and dull. "It is pretty, but it needs to be clean." "I know how to clean it," Tom says. He takes a cloth from his pocket. He rubs the ring with the cloth. He polishes it until it sparkles. "Good job, Tom!" Mia says. She puts the ring on her finger. It fits well. She smiles. "It is like a princess ring." Tom smiles too. He is happy to see Mia happy. They play with the ring for a while. They pretend to be a king and a queen. But then, they hear a voice. It is a man. He is angry. He is the owner of the bin. "Hey, you kids! What are you doing with my bin? Give me back my ring!" he shouts. He runs towards them. Tom and Mia are scared. They drop the ring and run away. They leave the bin and the park. They do not look back. They are sad. They liked the ring. They wanted to keep it. But they know they cannot. It is not theirs. It belongs to the man. They go to Mia's house. They hug each other. They say they are sorry. They say they are still friends. They hope the man will be happy with his ring. They hope he will polish it and make it shine. They hope he will not be angry anymore. They hope he will not be sad.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely lady named Sue. Sue wanted to organize a fun party for her friends. She thought, "What can I make for the party? I know, popcorn!" Sue went to the store and got some popcorn. She came back home and started to make the popcorn. She made a big bowl of it. Her friends would be so happy. But she didn't know that her dog, Max, loved popcorn too! The day of the party came. Sue put the popcorn on the table. Max saw the popcorn and his eyes lit up. When Sue went to the kitchen, Max jumped on the table and ate all the popcorn! Sue came back and saw the empty bowl. She was sad, but then she laughed. She knew Max just couldn't resist the popcorn. Sue quickly made more popcorn and the party was a big success.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bob. Bob lived in an oasis with many trees and water. He had a friend named Sam, the squirrel. They played together every day. One day, Bob and Sam saw a new bird in the oasis. The new bird was big and scary. Bob said to Sam, "I don't like the new bird. I hate when new things come to our oasis." Sam nodded and said, "Me too, Bob. Let's stay away from the new bird." But one day, the new bird saw Bob and Sam playing. He came over and said, "Hi, I am Tom. I am new here. Can I play with you?" Bob and Sam looked at each other. They thought Tom was scary, but they decided to let him play. To their surprise, Tom was very nice and fun to play with. Now, Bob, Sam, and Tom are best friends, and they play together every day in the oasis.

Once there was a small ant who lived in the forest. Every day, she would scurry around, searching for food. One day, she saw a bird and decided to tell him her troubles. So the ant asked the bird, "Can you help me find something to eat?" The bird answered, "I'm sorry, I can't help you with that. But I do have some advice. If you ever need anything, just ask. There are many creatures in the forest who will help you, if you just ask them nicely." The small ant thanked the bird and went on her way. She soon came across a bee who was filling up a small jar with honey. The ant asked the bee, "Can I have some honey?" To the ant's surprise, the bee said yes. The ant was happy to have a delicious snack and realized that the bird was right– asking for help was the key. From that day forward, the small ant made sure to always ask for help when she needed it. This story teaches us that it's important to be brave and ask for help from others. Sometimes we need help to do things, and it's ok to ask!
