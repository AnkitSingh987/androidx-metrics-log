Category: Beginner
Title: John and Jane and the Kite

Once upon a time there were two friends, John and Jane. John and Jane liked to play together every day. One day, they decided to fly a kite in their backyard. While they were flying the kite, John started to sing a happy song. Jane thought it was thoughtful of him and smiled. Then suddenly, dark clouds appeared in the sky. Wind began to blow and the kite flew away. Jane was so sad to see it go. John tried to cheer her up by singing a silly song. He sang so loudly and laughed. But Jane couldn't help but worry. She asked, "What if the kite is lost forever?" John shook his head and said, "Don't worry, it will come back". But the kite never returned. Jane was very sad and started to cry. John tried to make her feel better by singing a sad song and giving her a big hug. But nothing could replace the kite. Jane and John never saw the kite again.

Tim and Kim liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. One day, they saw a big dog in the park. The dog was brown and hairy. It barked loud and ran fast. Tim and Kim were scared of the dog. They ran away from the dog. They hid behind a tree. They hugged each other and cried. They wanted their mom and dad. The dog saw them behind the tree. It ran to them and sniffed them. It licked their faces and wagged its tail. It wanted to play with them. Tim and Kim did not like the dog. They thought the dog was disgusting. They did not like its smell, its tongue and its fur. They pushed the dog away and said "No! Go away!" The dog was sad. It did not understand why Tim and Kim did not like it. It walked away with its head down. It looked for another friend. A lady came to the park. She saw the dog and smiled. She called the dog by its name. She said "Rex! There you are! I've been looking for you!" She hugged the dog and gave it a treat. The dog was happy. It licked the lady's face and wagged its tail. It had found its owner. The lady was kind and loved the dog. Tim and Kim saw the lady and the dog. They felt sorry for the dog. They realized the dog was not mean, just lonely. They came out from behind the tree. They said "Sorry, dog. We were scared. You are not disgusting. You are cute." The dog heard them and barked. It ran to them and licked their faces again. It wanted to be their friend. The lady smiled and said "It's okay, kids. Rex is a friendly dog. He likes to play. Do you want to play with him?" Tim and Kim nodded. They petted the dog and threw a ball for it. The dog chased the ball and brought it back. They played together and had fun. The lady, the dog, Tim and Kim became friends. They went to the park often and played. They comforted each other when they were sad. They shared their joy when they were happy. They learned to love and trust each other. They were a happy family.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw his friend, the dog, sitting on the lawn. The dog was very patient and waited for Tim to come over. Tim thought the dog looked hungry, so he decided to feed him. Tim said, "Dog, would you like some food?" The dog barked happily and wagged its tail. Tim went inside his house and found some bread. He gave the bread to the dog, and the dog ate it all up. Tim and the dog played on the lawn for a while. But then, the dog started to feel sick. Tim didn't know what to do. He called for his mom, but she was not home. The dog got sicker and sicker, and finally, the dog lay down and did not get up. Tim was very sad because he didn't know that bread was not good for dogs.
