Category: Beginner
Title: John the Boy and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named John. He wanted to help his family, so he asked his uncle if he could do something. His uncle said, "Yes, you can act like a helpful boy." John smiled and set about doing chores around the house. He helped his mom make the bed, he washed dishes and he even cleaned the windows. He did these with a happy heart. One day, John was gathering leaves from the yard. His uncle was walking by and saw what he was doing. He smiled and said, "That's a good job. You're acting like a helpful boy." John's heart filled with joy and he knew it was a good thing he was doing. The moral of the story is that it always pays to be helpful. You never know when somebody might recognize, and appreciate, your hard work.

Tim and Kim are friends who like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bike with a cool sticker on it. Tim wants to ride the bike, but Kim says no. "That is not our bike. We should not touch it. It belongs to someone else," Kim says. But Tim does not listen. He gets on the bike and speeds away. He feels happy and free. He does not see the herb on the ground. He slips on the herb and falls off the bike. He hurts his knee and cries. Kim runs to help him. She sees the bike is broken. She is sad and angry. "Look what you did! You broke the bike and hurt yourself. You were not careful. You were not nice. You should say sorry to the owner of the bike," Kim says. Tim feels bad. He knows he was wrong. He says sorry to Kim and to the owner of the bike. He learns his lesson. He should not take things that are not his. He should listen to his friend. He should be careful and nice. He hopes the owner of the bike will forgive him. He hopes Kim will still be his friend. He hopes his knee will heal soon.

One day, a little furry cat went for a walk by the sea. The cat saw a big shell on the sand. The cat went closer to look at the shell. "What a pretty shell!" the cat said. A small crab came out of the shell. The crab said, "Hello, furry cat! This is my home. Do you want to play with me?" The cat smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" The cat and the crab played all day. They ran on the sand and squeezed the wet mud between their paws and claws. They had so much fun! When the sun went down, the cat said, "Goodbye, crab. I had a great day with you!" The crab waved its claw and went back to its shell. The furry cat walked home, happy and tired from a fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Spot. Spot liked to play in the park. One day, Spot saw a stand with fine toys. He wanted to play with them. Spot went to the stand and picked up a toy. But, he did not recognize the toy. It was a new kind of toy he had never seen before. Spot was confused about how to play with it. Then, something unexpected happened. The toy changed into a little dog! The little dog said, "Hi, I am a magic dog. I was hiding in the toy. Now we can play together!" Spot was happy and played with the magic dog all day.

Tim and Mia like to play in the garden. They have a small pool with water and toys. They splash and laugh and have fun. One day, Tim has an idea. He says to Mia, "Let's make the pool bigger. We can use the faucet and the hose. Come on!" Mia nods and follows Tim. They find the faucet and the hose near the house. Tim turns the faucet and water comes out. He puts the hose in the pool and says, "Look! The pool is growing!" Mia claps and smiles. She says, "Yes, it is growing! More water, more fun!" But they do not see that the faucet is too open. The water is too much and too loud. It makes a big mess. It floods the garden and the path and the door. Mom hears the noise and comes out. She sees the water and the pool and the hose and the faucet. She says, "Oh no! What are you doing? Turn off the faucet, quick!" Tim and Mia are scared. They did not mean to make a mess. They run to the faucet and try to turn it off. But it is hard and stuck. They push and pull and twist. Mom helps them. She turns the faucet off and stops the water. She says, "You are wet and dirty. And so is the garden. You have to clean up and dry up. And no more playing with the faucet and the hose. Do you understand?" Tim and Mia nod and say, "Yes, Mom. We are sorry. We just wanted to make the pool bigger." Mom sighs and says, "I know. But you have to be careful and ask before you use things. And you have to watch the water and the faucet. Water is good, but too much water is bad. It can make a mess and waste and trouble. Do you understand?" Tim and Mia nod and say, "Yes, Mom. We understand. We will be careful and ask and watch. We will not make a mess again." Mom smiles and says, "Good. Now, let's clean up and dry up. And then, you can play in the pool again. But only with the water that is already there. And not too loud. OK?" Tim and Mia say, "OK, Mom. Thank you, Mom. We love you, Mom." Mom says, "I love you too, Tim and Mia. Now, let's go."
