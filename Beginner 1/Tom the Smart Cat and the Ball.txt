Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Smart Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy ball. One day, Tom lost his ball in the big park. He was very sad and he said, "Oh no! I hope I can find my ball." Tom went to the park and looked for his ball. He heard a sound behind a big tree. It was his friend, the little dog named Tim. Tim said, "Hi Tom! What are you doing?" Tom said, "I lost my ball. Can you help me find it?" Tim was a good friend and wanted to help. They looked and looked for the ball. At last, they found the ball near a small pond. Tom was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a smart dog!" They played with the ball together and had a fun day.

One day, a little girl named Sue felt an urge to look inside a cabinet in her house. She had never opened this cabinet before. Sue thought there might be something rare and fun inside. Sue opened the cabinet and found a big, rare toy. It was a pretty doll with a shiny dress. Sue was very happy to find the doll. She played with the doll all day long. At night, Sue put the doll back in the cabinet. She knew the cabinet was a special place for her rare toy. Sue could not wait to play with the doll again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a toy triangle. He loved to play with his triangle all day long. One day, Tim saw a big kite stuck in a tree. He wanted to get the kite down, but it was too high for him to reach. Tim had an idea. He thought, "I can raise my triangle and use it to get the kite down." So, Tim raised his triangle high in the air. He tried to get the kite down, but he was still too short. Tim felt sad and asked his friend, Sam, for help. Sam said, "Let's work together to get the kite down." They both raised their hands and tried to reach the kite. With their hands raised high, they were able to get the kite down. Tim and Sam were so happy, and they played with the kite and the triangle all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange statue in a park. The statue was of a happy man with a big hat. Every day, the sun would shine on the statue, making it look very pretty. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw the orange statue and wanted to play with it. He ran to the statue and touched the big hat. Suddenly, the statue started to move! The statue took off its big hat and showed Tim a secret toy box hidden inside. Tim was so happy and surprised. He played with the toys and shared them with his friends at the park. From that day on, the orange statue became Tim's favorite place to play, and the park was always full of fun and laughter.

Once upon a time, there was a green tent in the woods. A little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went to play near the tent. They loved the green tent because it was big and fun to play in. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. One day, Tim said, "Let's play a game. We can match the things we find in the woods!" Sue thought it was a great idea. They started to look for things that were the same. Tim found two red flowers and Sue found two blue rocks. They put them next to each other and said, "Look, they match!" They laughed and continued to search for more things to match. After a while, they went back to the green tent to rest. They looked at all the things they matched and felt happy. Tim and Sue loved playing in the woods and matching things together. They knew they would come back to the green tent again soon.
