Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Blouse

Anna loved her red blouse. It was her favorite thing to wear. She liked how it was soft and bright and had a big bow on the front. She wore it every day, even when it was dirty or torn. One day, Anna's mom said, "Anna, you can't wear your red blouse today. It's too old and ragged. We have to throw it away and get you a new one." Anna was sad. She didn't want a new blouse. She wanted her red blouse. She said, "No, mom, please don't throw it away. I love it. It's my best blouse." Mom shook her head. She said, "I'm sorry, Anna, but it's time to say goodbye to your red blouse. It's not good for you to wear it anymore. It has holes and stains and it's too small for you. Look, I bought you a new blouse. It's blue and has flowers on it. It's very pretty and fits you well. Why don't you try it on?" Anna didn't like the blue blouse. She thought it was ugly and boring. She said, "No, mom, I don't want the blue blouse. I want the red blouse. Please, mom, let me keep it. I'll be good. I'll do anything." Mom sighed. She said, "Anna, I know you love your red blouse, but you have to let it go. It's not good for you to be so attached to it. It's just a thing, not a friend. You have to grow up and move on. You have to remember the good times you had with your red blouse, but also be happy for the new things you can have. Trust me, you'll like the blue blouse once you wear it. It's very nice and comfortable. Come on, let's go to the trash can and say goodbye to your red blouse." Anna didn't want to go to the trash can. She wanted to run away with her red blouse. She wanted to hide it somewhere safe and secret. She wanted to keep it forever. But she knew mom was stronger and smarter than her. She knew mom would find her and take her red blouse away. She knew mom was trying to help her and make her happy. She knew mom loved her. So she followed mom to the trash can, holding her red blouse tightly in her arms. She felt tears in her eyes. She felt a lump in her throat. She felt a pain in her heart. Mom said, "Anna, it's time to say goodbye to your red blouse. Do you want to say something to it?" Anna looked at her red blouse. She saw how it was soft and bright and had a big bow on the front. She remembered how it made her feel happy and special and beautiful. She remembered how she wore it on her birthday and on Christmas and on the first day of school. She remembered how she played with it and danced with it and slept with it. She loved her red blouse. She said, "Goodbye, red blouse. Thank you for being my best blouse. I'll always remember you. I'll always love you." She kissed her red blouse and put it in the trash can. She felt a tear roll down her cheek. She felt a sob escape her mouth. She felt a hug from mom. She heard mom say, "I'm proud of you, Anna. You're a brave and big girl. I love you very much. Come on, let's go home and try on your new blouse. I think you'll like it. It's very pretty and fits you well. Maybe you'll make new memories with it. Maybe you'll love it too." Anna nodded. She wiped her eyes. She took mom's hand. She walked away from the trash can. She looked back at her red blouse. She saw how it was old and ragged and too small for her. She felt sad. But she also felt curious. She wondered how the blue blouse would look on her. She wondered how it would feel on her. She wondered if she would like it. She hoped she would. She hoped she would be happy. She hoped mom was right.
