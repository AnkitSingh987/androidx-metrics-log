Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Dog

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They were very happy. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. The cat said, "Look, it is a fine day to play outside!" The dog agreed, "Yes, it is. We can run and have fun!" As they played, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird wanted to play too. The cat and the dog were scared at first, but the bird said, "Don't be scared. I want to play and find peace with you." So, they all played together. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird found a big shiny rock. The cat and the dog looked at the rock and saw that it was very special. They all decided to share the rock and keep it safe. From that day on, they were the best of friends and always played together.

Lily was a big girl. She had a job. Her job was to help Mom with the baby. She liked her job. She liked to feed the baby, sing to the baby, and play with the baby. She loved the baby. One day, Mom said, "Lily, we have to go to the store. Can you watch the baby for a little bit?" Lily said, "Yes, Mom. I can do it. I am a big girl." Mom said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good helper. I will be back soon. Don't open the door for anyone." Lily said, "OK, Mom. Bye-bye." Lily and the baby were happy. They played with toys, read books, and made funny faces. Lily gave the baby a hug. She said, "You are my baby. You belong to me." The baby smiled. Then, Lily heard a knock on the door. She ran to the door. She looked through the peephole. She saw a man. He had a big hat, a big nose, and a big smile. He looked crazy. He said, "Hello, little girl. I have a surprise for you. Do you want to see it?" Lily said, "No, thank you. Go away. I don't know you." The man said, "Oh, come on. Don't be shy. It's a very nice surprise. It's a big, fluffy, pink bunny. Do you like bunnies?" Lily said, "Yes, I like bunnies. But I don't like you. You are a stranger. Mom said not to open the door for anyone." The man said, "But I'm not a stranger. I'm your uncle. Your mom told me to come and visit you. She said you would be happy to see me. And I have a gift for you. A big, fluffy, pink bunny. Don't you want it?" Lily was confused. She liked bunnies. She liked gifts. She liked uncles. But she didn't know this man. He looked crazy. He sounded crazy. He was lying. She said, "No, you are not my uncle. You are a bad man. Go away. Leave me alone. I'm going to call Mom." The man said, "No, no, no. Don't call Mom. She will be mad at you. She will be mad at me. She will take away your baby. She will take away your job. She will take away your big, fluffy, pink bunny. You don't want that, do you?" Lily said, "Yes, I do. I want Mom. I want the baby. I want my job. I don't want you. I don't want your bunny. Go away. Go away. Go away." Lily ran to the phone. She dialed Mom's number. She said, "Mom, Mom, there's a bad man at the door. He's crazy. He's lying. He wants to take me and the baby. He wants to give me a bunny. But I don't want it. I don't want him. Please come back. Please help me." Mom said, "Lily, stay calm. Don't open the door. Don't talk to him. I'm coming back. I'm calling the police. You are a brave girl. You are a smart girl. You are a good helper. I love you. I love the baby. You belong to me." Lily hung up the phone. She ran to the baby. She gave the baby a hug. She said, "Don't worry, baby. Mom is coming. The police are coming. The bad man will go away. We are safe. We are happy. We are together. You are my baby. You belong to me." The baby smiled.
