Category: Beginner
Title: Mr the Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was an elderly cat named Mr. Whiskers. He lived in a small house near a big tree. Mr. Whiskers was very old and loved to play with the kids in the park. One day, Mr. Whiskers was very dirty. He had been playing in the mud with his friends. His fur was all brown and sticky. He needed to clean himself, so he went home to take a bath. Mr. Whiskers filled the bathtub with warm water. He slowly got in and started to soak. The water felt nice on his fur. As he was soaking, a little bird flew in through the window. The bird said, "Hi, Mr. Whiskers! Are you having a nice bath?" Mr. Whiskers replied, "Yes, I am! The water feels good, and I will be clean soon." The bird smiled and flew away. Mr. Whiskers finished his bath and felt happy and clean.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a pretty picture in a frame on her wall. The picture was of a smooth, round ball. Amy loved this picture very much. One day, Amy's little brother, Tim, was playing ball inside the house. He accidentally threw the ball too hard, and it hit the picture frame on the wall. The frame broke, and Amy was very sad. Tim felt bad for breaking the frame. He said, "I'm sorry, Amy. I didn't mean to break it." Amy was sad, but she knew her brother didn't do it on purpose. She decided to forgive him. They hugged and promised to be more careful when playing inside the house.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball in his yard. One day, he saw a new boy next door. The new boy was playing with a ball too. Tim thought he looked ready to play. Tim went to the new boy and said, "Hi! I'm Tim. Do you want to play?" The new boy said, "Yes! I'm Sam. Let's play!" So, Tim and Sam played ball together. They threw the ball back and forth on the pitch. After a while, Tim said, "Sam, do you want to come to my house? I will invite you to play with my toys." Sam was happy and said, "Yes! Thank you, Tim!" So, Tim and Sam went to Tim's house and played with the toys. They had so much fun playing together. They became best friends. From that day on, Tim and Sam always played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, he went to the park to play. He saw a small, helpless bird on the ground. The bird was sad because it lost its shoe. Tim wanted to help the bird find its shoe. He looked under the tree and found the shoe. He picked it up and gave it to the bird. "Here is your shoe!" Tim said. The bird was very happy and put on its shoe. Suddenly, the bird's mom came and saw Tim. She was so happy that Tim helped her baby. She yelled, "Thank you, Tim!" Then, they all played together in the park. The bird and its mom became Tim's good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Mia. Mia loved to sing. Every day, she would sing in the park. One day, Mia found a rope on the ground. She had an idea. She tied the rope to a tree and made a swing. One dark evening, Mia went to the park to sing and swing. She was having fun, but she felt lonely. She wished she had a friend to play with. Suddenly, she heard a noise. It was a little bird. The bird was scared of the dark. Mia felt sorry for the bird. Mia started to sing a sweet song. The bird felt better and started to sing too. They sang and played together all night. The next day, the bird brought more friends. Mia was so happy. She learned that being kind can bring you friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy and a girl who were both very happy. One day, they decided to marry each other. Then, they had the idea to buy a cow. The boy said to the girl, "Let's buy a cow." The girl replied, "OK, let's find one." They started looking for a cow, but it was difficult. Finally, they found one, but it was very expensive. They bought the cow, but soon realised it was sick. The boy said, "It's too late now, we can't return the cow." The girl replied sadly, "What are we going to do?" The boy said, "We could try to treat it, but it's going to be difficult." In the end, they couldn't save the cow and it died. The boy and girl were very sad, but they were still married.
