Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Bug

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bird with big wings. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. It liked to tell stories to all its friends. One day, the bird met a little bug. The bug was sad. The bird asked, "Why are you sad?" The bug said, "I want to fly like you, but I don't have wings." The bird felt bad for the bug. The bird had an idea. It said, "I can help you fly!" The bird held the bug in its wings and flew up into the sky. The bug was so happy. They flew together and the bird told the bug many stories. Now they are best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved colorful things. One day, she went for a walk and saw a colorful pick on the ground. She picked it up and was very happy. Mia walked and walked, but then she drop the colorful pick. She was sad and didn't know what to do. So, she looked and looked for the colorful pick on the ground. Mia saw a big bird with the colorful pick in its beak. The bird saw Mia and dropped the pick. The bird was nice and wanted to help Mia. Mia was happy again and said thank you to the bird. They became friends and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Tom. He lived in a big, pretty house. Tom had a pet dog named Max. Max was very smart and fast. One day, Max saw a little bird outside the window. The bird was trying to escape a big, mean cat. Max wanted to help the bird. Max ran out of the house and barked at the cat. The cat got scared and ran away. The little bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Max! You helped me escape the big, mean cat." Max and the bird became good friends. They played together every day. One day, they saw a race for pets. Max wanted to join the race and win. The bird said, "You can do it, Max! I know you will win!" Max ran very fast in the race. He was faster than all the other pets. At the end of the race, Max won! Everyone clapped and cheered for him. Max was so happy, and the little bird was proud of his friend. They knew that together, they could do anything.

Sam and Ben are brothers. They like to play with tools. They have a big box of tools in their room. They have hammers, screwdrivers, pliers, and more. One day, they want to build a house with their tools. They find some wood and nails in the garage. They ask their mom if they can use them. Their mom says yes, but be careful. They take the wood and nails to the backyard. They start to hammer the wood together. They make a floor, a wall, and a roof. They are very happy with their house. "Look, Sam, we made a house!" Ben says. "Yes, we did, Ben. It is a fine house!" Sam says. They go inside their house. They pretend to cook, to sleep, and to read. They have a lot of fun. But then, they hear a loud noise. They look outside. They see a big truck. The truck is moving their house. They are scared. "Hey, stop! That is our house!" Sam shouts. "Sorry, kids. I have to move this wood. It is for a new fence." The truck driver says. He does not see their tools or their nails. He thinks the wood is just wood. "Please, mister, can we have our house back?" Ben asks. The truck driver looks closer. He sees their house. He sees their tools. He sees their nails. He is surprised. "Oh, I am sorry, kids. I did not know you made a house. You are very clever. Here, take your house and your tools. But be careful with the nails. They are sharp." He says. He puts their house and their tools back in the backyard. He drives away. Sam and Ben are happy. They have their house and their tools again. They thank the truck driver. They hug each other. They go back to their house. They play some more. They are fine.
