Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bear and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a big, rough bear. The bear lived in a deep, dark forest. He had big paws with long fingers. The bear was very hungry. One day, a little girl went into the forest. She saw the big, rough bear with the long fingers. The little girl wanted to help the bear. She thought she could provide food for the bear, so he would not be hungry anymore. The little girl went back to her house to find food for the bear. She brought the bear some bread and honey. But the bear was still very hungry. He ate the bread and honey, but it was not enough. The bear was still hungry and he looked at the little girl. The little girl was scared. In the end, the bear ate the little girl too. It was a bad ending for the little girl.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. Anna has a new toy. It is a red car. It can make noises and move by itself. Ben thinks it is cool. "Can I play with your car, Anna?" Ben asks. "OK, but be careful. It is my favorite toy," Anna says. She gives the car to Ben. Ben plays with the car. He makes it go fast and slow. He makes it turn and stop. He makes it beep and honk. He is happy. But Anna is not happy. She is jealous. She wants her car back. She does not like to share. She thinks Ben is having too much fun. "Give me my car, Ben. It is my turn," Anna says. "No, I am not done. It is still my turn," Ben says. They start to fight. They pull and tug. They shout and cry. They are not friends anymore. A man sees them. He is a park ranger. He wears a hat and a badge. He has a whistle and a tap. He blows the whistle. He walks to them. "What is going on here?" he asks. "They are fighting over my car. It is mine," Anna says. "No, it is mine. She gave it to me," Ben says. The man shakes his head. He is not happy. He takes the car. He turns it off. He puts it on the tap. "Listen, children. Fighting is not nice. Sharing is nice. If you want to play with the car, you have to take turns. Do you understand?" he says. Anna and Ben nod. They are sorry. They do not want to fight. They want to play. "OK, then. I will bring the car back to you. But you have to promise to share and be friends. Can you do that?" he says. Anna and Ben nod. They smile. They hug. They are friends again. "Thank you, sir," they say. The man smiles. He is happy. He brings the car back to them. He turns it on. He gives it to Anna. "Here you go, Anna. You can play with it first. But remember to give it to Ben when you are done. OK?" he says. "OK, sir. Thank you," Anna says. She plays with the car. She makes it go fast and slow. She makes it turn and stop. She makes it beep and honk. She is happy. But she is not jealous. She looks at Ben. She sees him watching. She knows he wants to play too. "Ben, do you want to play with me? We can make the car go together. It will be more fun," she says. Ben smiles. He is happy. He nods. He joins her. They play with the car. They make it go fast and slow. They make it turn and stop. They make it beep and honk. They are happy. They are friends. They share. They are nice.

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to climb the big oak tree. It had many branches and leaves. They felt like birds in the sky. But today, the oak tree was dry. It had no water for a long time. The leaves were brown and the branches were weak. Lila and Tom did not know this. They climbed the oak tree as usual. They were happy and loud. They sang and shouted. "Look at me, Lila! I am the king of the tree!" Tom said. "Be careful, Tom! The branch may break!" Lila said. But Tom did not listen. He jumped from one branch to another. He wanted to show off. He did not see the crack in the branch. Suddenly, the branch snapped. Tom fell down. He hit the ground hard. He cried out in pain. Lila was scared. She climbed down quickly. She ran to Tom. She saw blood on his knee. "Tom, are you okay? I am sorry. I told you to be careful." Lila said. Tom was hurt. He was angry and sad. He blamed Lila. "It is your fault, Lila! You made me fall! You are a bad friend!" Tom said. Lila felt bad. She did not mean to make Tom fall. She wanted to help him. She tried to hug him. "Please, Tom, don't be mad. I am your friend. I love you. Let me get a band-aid for your knee." Lila said. But Tom pushed her away. He did not want her hug. He did not want her help. He did not want her love. He got up and limped away. He left Lila alone. He left the oak tree alone. He left the park alone.
