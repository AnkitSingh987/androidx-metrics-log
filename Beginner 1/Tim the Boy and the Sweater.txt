Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sweater

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a blue sweater that he loved very much. Tim wore his sweater every day, because it kept him warm and cozy. One day, Tim's sweater became weak. It had holes in it and did not keep him warm anymore. Tim was sad and knew he needed a new sweater. Tim's mom saw that he was sad and took him to the store. They found a new, red sweater for Tim to wear. Tim put on his new sweater and felt warm and happy again. From that day on, Tim wore his new red sweater and took good care of it, so it would not become weak like his old blue sweater.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys and draw pictures. One day, they have a test at school. The test is about colors and shapes. Lily and Tom study hard. They want to do well. The next day, the teacher gives them the test. Lily and Tom look at the questions. They know the answers. They write them down. They are happy. They finish the test and give it to the teacher. The teacher looks at the tests. She smiles at Lily and Tom. She says, "You did very well. You know your colors and shapes. I have a surprise for you." She goes to her desk and takes out two boxes. She gives one to Lily and one to Tom. She says, "Open them and see what is inside." Lily and Tom open the boxes. They reveal their surprises. Lily has a doll with a pink dress and a yellow hat. Tom has a car with blue wheels and a red horn. They are very happy. They say, "Thank you, teacher. We love our surprises." They hug the teacher and show their surprises to their friends. They play with them and share them. They are proud of their test and their surprises. They are not sad. They are glad.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play on the farm. They see a mule in the barn. The mule is big and brown. He has long ears and a long tail. "Hi, mule!" Tom says. "Do you want to play with us?" The mule does not answer. He is sleepy. He lies down on the hay. He closes his eyes. "Look, Lily, the mule is sleepy!" Tom says. "He does not want to play. He wants to nap." Lily nods. She has an idea. She picks some flowers from the field. She makes a crown with the flowers. She puts the crown on the mule's head. "Look, Tom, I make the mule a king!" Lily says. "He is the king of the barn. He is a nice king. He lets us play with him." Tom smiles. He likes Lily's idea. He makes a scepter with a stick and a leaf. He gives the scepter to the mule. "Look, Lily, I make the mule a ruler!" Tom says. "He is the ruler of the farm. He is a good ruler. He shares his hay with us." The mule does not mind. He is sleepy. He does not care about the crown or the scepter. He snores softly. Tom and Lily giggle. They are happy. They play with the mule. They are his friends. They are his subjects. They are his guests.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with toys and balls. One day, they find a big box in the park. They open the box and see many things inside. They see books, clothes, shoes, and a string. "Look, a string!" Tom says. He takes the string and pulls it. The string is very long and dirty. It has mud and leaves on it. "What can we do with the string?" Sam asks. He thinks for a moment. He has an idea. "We can make a trap for the cat!" Tom likes the idea. He nods and smiles. They look for a cat in the park. They see a black cat near a tree. The cat is sleeping in the sun. "Shh, be quiet!" Tom whispers. He and Sam walk slowly to the cat. They put the string on the ground near the cat. They make a loop with the string. They hide behind a bush and wait. The cat wakes up and stretches. It sees the string on the ground. It is curious. It walks to the string and sniffs it. It tries to play with the string. It puts its paw in the loop. "Now!" Tom shouts. He and Sam pull the string. The loop tightens around the cat's paw. The cat is trapped. It meows and struggles. "Ha ha, we got you!" Tom and Sam laugh. They run to the cat and try to pick it up. But the cat is not happy. It is angry and scared. It scratches Tom and Sam with its claws. It bites the string and breaks it. It frees itself and runs away. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tom and Sam cry. They have red marks and blood on their hands. They drop the string and run to their moms. Their moms see them and are worried. They ask them what happened. Tom and Sam tell them the story. "That was not nice!" their moms say. "You should not hurt the cat. The cat is a living thing. It has feelings. How would you feel if someone did that to you?" Tom and Sam feel sorry. They know they did a bad thing. They say sorry to their moms and to the cat. They promise to be kind to animals. They hug their moms and go home. They leave the string in the park. They do not want to play with it anymore.
