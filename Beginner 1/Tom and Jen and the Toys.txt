Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Jen and the Toys

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big toy gun in the park. It is black and shiny. They think it is fun. "Let's play shoot, shoot!" Tom says. He picks up the toy gun and points it at Jen. "No, no, don't shoot me!" Jen says. She is scared. She runs away. Tom runs after her. He laughs. He thinks it is a game. He does not know the toy gun is real. Jen hides behind a tree. She sees a big piece of cheese on the ground. She is hungry. She picks up the cheese and bites it. It is dry and hard. She does not like it. Tom finds Jen. He sees the cheese in her hand. He wants some cheese too. He says, "Give me some cheese, or I will shoot you!" Jen says, "No, this is my cheese. Go away!" Tom gets angry. He pulls the trigger. Bang! The toy gun makes a loud noise. Jen falls down. She does not move. She does not breathe. Tom drops the toy gun. He is shocked. He cries. He says, "Jen, Jen, wake up! I'm sorry, I'm sorry!" But Jen does not wake up. She is gone. Tom is alone. He is sad. He is in trouble.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. It was a peaceful place with lots of flowers and trees. In the field, there was a little girl named Lily and her friend, a small dog named Pup. One day, Lily and Pup were playing in the field. They were running and laughing together. Suddenly, Pup stopped and looked at the sky. He saw a big cloud and started to frown. "Pup, why do you frown?" asked Lily. Pup said, "I don't like the big cloud. It might rain, and we can't play outside." Lily looked at the cloud and said, "Don't worry, Pup. We can still have fun inside." So, Lily and Pup left the peaceful field and went to Lily's house. They played with toys and read books together. Even though it rained outside, they were happy and smiled all day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular toy store. Kids loved to go there and play with all the toys. One day, a boy named Timmy went to the store with his mom. He saw a toy missile and really wanted it. He asked his mom, "Can I have the missile, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can keep it if you are good." Timmy was very happy and promised to be good. He played with his missile all day long. He showed it to his friends, and they all loved it too. The missile made Timmy very popular in his town. One day, Timmy and his friends were playing with the missile in the park. They threw it high up in the air and watched it fly. But the missile got stuck in a tree! Timmy was sad, but his friends helped him. They all worked together to get the missile down from the tree. Timmy was happy again and said, "Thank you, friends!" They all played together and had a great day.

One day, a beautiful dog was walking in the park. The dog had a tiny flea on its back. The flea liked to jump and play on the dog's fur. The dog and the flea were best friends. The dog said to the flea, "I have a hurt paw. Can you help me heal it?" The flea jumped onto the dog's paw and looked at it. The flea said, "I will try my best to help you." The flea saw a big leaf nearby. The flea had an idea. It jumped onto the leaf and started to sing a magic song. As the flea sang, the leaf started to glow. The flea put the glowing leaf on the dog's paw. The dog's paw began to heal. The dog was so happy and said, "Thank you, flea. You are the best friend ever!" The flea smiled and said, "You're welcome. I'm happy I could help you." From that day on, the dog and the flea played happily in the park every day. They knew that they could always count on each other to help when they needed it.
