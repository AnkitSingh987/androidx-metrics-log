Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog Finds the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a nervous dog. He did not like the dark. One day, Spot was playing outside when it started to rain. He ran under a big tree to stay dry. As the rain stopped, Spot saw a little cat. The cat looked scared of the dark too. They looked at each other and decided to be friends. They played together and forgot about being nervous. Then, something unexpected happened. The little cat jumped high into the tree and turned on a light. The tree was not a tree at all, but a big lamp! Spot and the cat were not scared of the dark anymore. They played under the lamp and stayed dry and happy.

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They like to slide on the big slide. Lily goes first. She sits on the slide and holds the sides. She says, "Whee!" as she slides down. Ben waits for his turn. Ben is wearing a long scarf. His mom gave it to him. She said it will keep him warm. Ben likes his scarf. He wraps it around his neck and waist. He thinks he looks cool. Ben sits on the slide and lets go of the sides. He wants to slide fast. But his scarf is too long. It gets caught on the slide. Ben feels a tug. He tries to pull his scarf free. He can't. He starts to slip. He says, "Help!" Lily hears Ben. She runs to the slide. She sees Ben's scarf. She grabs it and pulls. She frees Ben from the slide. Ben slides down and lands on the ground. He is scared and sad. He says, "My scarf!" Lily hugs Ben. She says, "It's okay. Your scarf is fine. It's just a little dirty. We can wash it later. Don't be sad. You are brave. You didn't cry. Let's play something else. How about the swings?" Ben smiles. He says, "Okay. Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend. I like the swings. Let's go." He takes his scarf and wraps it around his neck. He makes it shorter. He doesn't want it to get caught again. He follows Lily to the swings. They have fun. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple machine. The machine could pack many things. It lived in a small house with its friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim wanted to pack a box with toys. He asked the purple machine for help. The machine was happy to help Tim, so they started packing the box together. They put a ball, a doll, and a car in the box. The machine was very good at packing, and Tim was very happy. When they were done, Tim and the purple machine closed the box. They carried the box outside to give it to a friend. The friend was very happy to get the box full of toys. Tim and the purple machine went back to their small house, feeling very proud of their work.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very honest boy. He always told the truth and was very nice to everyone. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to his house to play. Sam said, "Tim, let's invite more friends to play with us!" Tim thought it was a great idea. So, they invited their friends, Sue and Max, to come and play with them. They all played a fun game called "Who Can Jump the Highest?" Sue, Max, Sam, and Tim took turns jumping high in the air. They all tried their best to win. In the end, Max jumped the highest and won the game. Everyone clapped and cheered for Max. Tim said, "Good job, Max! You are the best jumper!" Max was very happy and thanked Tim for being honest and kind. They all continued to play and have fun together. And from that day on, they always invited each other to play games and have fun.

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a broken swing. The swing was very sad because no kids would play on it. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the park with his hockey stick and ball. Tim saw the broken swing and said, "Oh no! The swing is broken. I want to help fix it." Tim thought for a moment and then had an idea. He took his hockey stick and used it to hold the swing up. Now the swing was not broken anymore. A little girl named Sue came to the park and saw the swing. She said, "Wow, you fixed the swing with your hockey stick!" Tim and Sue played on the swing together, taking turns pushing each other. They had so much fun. Later, Tim's mom came to the park and saw what Tim had done. She was very proud of him. She said, "Tim, you did a great job fixing the swing and sharing it with Sue. You are a very kind and helpful friend." The moral of the story is that we can use what we have to help others and make things better for everyone.
