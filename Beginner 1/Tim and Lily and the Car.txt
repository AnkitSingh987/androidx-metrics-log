Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Car

Tim and Lily were playing with their vehicles in the garden. Tim had a big red truck and Lily had a small yellow car. They liked to make noises and pretend they were driving on the road. "Beep beep! I am a fast car!" Lily said. "Vroom vroom! I am a strong truck!" Tim said. They had so much fun that they did not hear their mom calling them for lunch. "Tim! Lily! Come and eat!" mom said. But Tim and Lily did not listen. They kept playing with their vehicles. Mom came out of the house and saw them. She was not happy. "Tim! Lily! You have to eat now! It is good for you!" mom said. "But mom, we are not hungry! We want to play!" Tim and Lily said. Mom shook her head. She had an idea. She went back to the house and came back with a plate of sandwiches. She put the plate on the ground near Tim and Lily. "Look, I made you some special sandwiches. They are shaped like vehicles!" mom said. Tim and Lily looked at the plate. They saw sandwiches that looked like trucks, cars, buses, and planes. They were amazed. "Wow! How did you do that, mom?" Tim asked. "I used a cookie cutter. Do you want to try one?" mom asked. Tim and Lily nodded. They picked up a sandwich each and took a bite. They liked the taste. "Mmm, these are yummy!" Lily said. "Thank you, mom! You are so helpful!" Tim said. Mom smiled. She was glad that Tim and Lily ate their lunch. She sat down with them and ate a sandwich too. They talked and laughed and enjoyed their meal. They were a happy family.

Tom and Mia are playing with their toy cars in the living room. They zoom and beep and crash their cars on the carpet. They have a lot of fun, but they also get very tired. "Tom, I need more fuel for my car," Mia says. "It is running low." "Me too," Tom says. "But we don't have any fuel. We only have juice and water and milk." "Maybe we can pretend that juice is fuel," Mia says. "Let's go to the kitchen and fill our cars with juice." They run to the kitchen and find a bottle of juice. They pour some juice into their cars and drink some too. They feel a little better, but they still feel sleepy. "Tom, I want to nap," Mia says. "Playing is difficult when I'm sleepy." "Me too," Tom says. "Let's go to the couch and nap." They go to the couch and cuddle with their cars. They close their eyes and fall asleep. They dream of driving their cars on a big road with lots of fuel. They are happy and rested.

One day, a parent and a kid went to the park. The sun was bright and warm. The kid was very happy to play. The parent had a big bag with them. The kid asked, "What is in the bag?" The parent smiled and said, "We will unpack it soon. It is a surprise for you." The kid was excited and played with the ball and the swing. They knew something yummy was in the bag. After playing, the kid was very hungry. The parent said, "Now, we will unpack the bag." The kid clapped their hands and sat down. The parent took out a yummy cake. The kid was so happy and said, "I knew it was something yummy! Thank you!" They ate the cake and had a great day at the park.

One day, a little squirrel went to play outside. The squirrel liked to jump and run. It saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The squirrel was very happy. As the squirrel went up the tree, it saw a small bird. The bird was sad. The squirrel asked, "What is wrong? Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" The squirrel said, "I can do that. I will help you." The squirrel looked around and saw the toy on the ground. It climbed down and got the toy. The squirrel gave the toy to the bird. The bird was so happy. It said, "Thank you, squirrel. I am sorry I was sad." The squirrel said, "It's okay. We all feel sorry sometimes. Let's play together!" And they played and had fun all day.
