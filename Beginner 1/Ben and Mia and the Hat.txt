Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Hat

Ben and Mia liked to play in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat, a scarf and a carrot nose. They gave him a name: Bob. "Hello, Bob!" Ben said. "You are our friend." "Yes, you are!" Mia said. "You are very cheerful." Bob smiled with his carrot nose. He was happy to have Ben and Mia as his friends. But the sun was shining very bright. The snow started to melt. Bob felt his hat and scarf getting wet. He felt his carrot nose falling off. "Oh no!" Bob said. "I am melting!" Ben and Mia saw Bob melting. They felt sad. They wanted to help him. "Don't worry, Bob!" Ben said. "We have an idea." They ran to the barn. They found a big barrel. They rolled it to Bob. "Get inside, Bob!" Mia said. "The barrel will keep you cold." Bob got inside the barrel. He felt the snow around him. He felt better. "Thank you, Ben and Mia!" Bob said. "You are very kind." Ben and Mia smiled. They put the hat, the scarf and the carrot nose on the barrel. "You are welcome, Bob!" they said. "You are still our friend." They hugged the barrel. Bob hugged them back. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. One day, it was very wet outside. Spot went out to play in the rain. He jumped in the puddles and got all wet. Spot liked the rain, but he started to shiver because he was cold. Spot saw his friend, a big truck named Tom. Tom was big and strong. He carried oil to help cars go fast. Tom saw Spot shivering and said, "Spot, you are wet and cold. Let's go inside to get warm." Spot and Tom went inside a warm house. Spot's mom dried him with a towel. Tom gave Spot some of the oil to help him feel better. They both sat by the fire and got warm. Spot stopped shivering and said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping me get warm." They were happy and played inside until the rain stopped.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and run around in the house. One day, Tom saw a toilet and thought it would be fun to play with it. Tom jumped on the toilet and started to play. He pushed the handle down and watched the water go round and round. Suddenly, Tom slipped and fell into the toilet! He was wet and cold, and he did not like it at all. Tom tried to reverse and get out of the toilet, but he was too chubby. He cried and cried for help. His friend, a little girl named Lily, heard his cries and came to help. She pulled Tom out of the toilet, and they both laughed. Tom learned not to play with the toilet again.

Once upon a time, there was a rude little man. He didn’t like to share and was always grumpy. One morning, he was walking along the beach. He saw something shining in the sun. It was a shiny shell! He picked it up and said, “This will be mine!” He tried to put it in his pocket, but it wouldn’t fit. Suddenly, he heard a voice: “That shell belongs to me!” A kind lady was standing in the sun. The rude little man said, “No, it’s mine!” But the kind lady repeated, “No, it belongs to me.” The rude little man was angry, but the kind lady kept saying it again and again. Finally, the rude little man gave in. He handed over the shell and said, “Okay, you can have it.” The kind lady smiled and thanked the rude little man. Then she walked away in the sun, holding her special shell.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were in a park. The big cat had a sack. The little dog was bored. He wanted to play with the big cat. "Hey, big cat, what do you have in your sack?" asked the little dog. The big cat said, "I have toys that provide fun. But I won't share them with you. They are only for me." The little dog was sad, but then he saw a big tree. He had an idea. He ran and jumped on the tree. The tree shook and a big bird came out. The big bird saw the big cat's sack and wanted it. The big bird took the big cat's sack and flew away. The big cat and the little dog were both sad. They lost their toys and had a boring day. The big bird had a lot of fun with the toys from the sack.
