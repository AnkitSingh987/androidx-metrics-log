Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Ball

Lily and Tom were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball and run after it. Sometimes they missed the ball and it rolled away. They laughed and chased the ball. But then the sky became dark and loud. Lily and Tom heard a big boom. They saw a bright flash in the sky. It was lightning. They were scared. "Let's go home, Lily!" Tom said. "It's going to rain!" But Lily did not want to go home. She wanted to play more. She said, "No, Tom, it's fun! Look, the ball is over there. Let's get it!" She ran to the ball, but Tom stayed behind. He did not like the lightning. He wanted to be safe. Lily reached the ball, but then she heard another big boom. She looked up and saw another bright flash. She felt a drop of water on her nose. It was raining. She picked up the ball and turned around. She saw Tom waiting for her. She wanted to go back to him, but then she saw a big puddle. She liked puddles. She wanted to splash in the puddle. She ran to the puddle and jumped in. She made a big splash. She laughed and said, "Look, Tom, it's fun! Come and splash with me!" But Tom did not want to splash. He wanted to go home. He said, "No, Lily, it's not fun! It's wet and cold and scary! Come on, let's go home!" But Lily did not listen. She splashed more and more. She did not see the lightning. She did not hear the thunder. She only saw the puddle. But then she saw something else. She saw a huge shadow. She looked up and saw a huge dog. The dog was wet and angry. It barked and growled. It wanted the ball. Lily was very scared. She dropped the ball and ran to Tom. She said, "Tom, help me! There's a huge dog! It wants the ball!" Tom saw the dog. He was scared too. But he was brave. He said, "Don't worry, Lily, I'll help you!" He picked up a stick and threw it at the dog. The dog saw the stick and ran after it. It left the ball and the puddle. Lily and Tom ran to their home. They were wet and cold and scared. But they were also happy. They hugged each other and said, "Thank you, Tom! Thank you, Lily! You are a good friend!" They learned a lesson. They learned that playing is fun, but listening is important. They learned that lightning is dangerous, but friendship is strong. They learned that splashing is fun, but sharing is better.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big pile of leaves. They want to jump in the leaves. "Let's gather the leaves and make a big hill!" Lily says. "OK!" Ben says. They run to the pile and start to gather the leaves. They use their hands and their feet. They have fun. But then, they see a cord. The cord is long and red. It is on the ground. They are curious. "What is this?" Ben asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is a snake!" Lily says. They are scared. They drop the leaves and back away. They look at the cord. But then, they hear a laugh. It is a lively laugh. It is from behind a tree. They see a man. The man is holding a remote. The cord is attached to the remote. "Hello, kids!" the man says. "Don't be afraid. This is not a snake. This is a toy car. The cord is to control the car. See?" The man pushes a button. The toy car moves. It makes a beep. It has lights. Lily and Ben are surprised. They are not scared anymore. They are interested. "Wow! That is a cool toy!" Lily says. "Can we play with it?" Ben asks. "Sure!" the man says. "But first, let me help you with the leaves. You worked hard to gather them. Let's make a big hill and jump in it together!" Lily and Ben are happy. They smile. They say thank you. They help the man with the leaves. Then, they play with the toy car. They have a lively time. They make new friends.
