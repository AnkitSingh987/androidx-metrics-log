Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Bikes

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside with their bikes. One day, they see a new bike in the park. It is big and shiny and has a bell. Lily and Ben want to try it. "Can we ride your bike?" Lily asks the boy with the new bike. "No, it is mine. Go away!" the boy says. He is mean and rude. Lily and Ben are sad and jealous. They think the new bike is better than their bikes. They go back to their bikes and ride slowly. Suddenly, they hear a loud noise. It is rain. Big drops of rain fall from the sky. They wet the grass and the trees and the bikes. Lily and Ben are happy. They like rain. They speed up their bikes and splash in the puddles. They laugh and have fun. The boy with the new bike is not happy. He does not like rain. He tries to cover his bike with his jacket, but it is too late. His bike is wet and dirty. He cries and wishes he had friends to play with. Lily and Ben see the boy crying. They feel sorry for him. They stop their bikes and go to him. "Do you want to play with us?" Ben asks the boy. "Really?" the boy says. He is surprised and glad. "Yes, really. We can share our bikes. And you can ring your bell." Lily says. The boy smiles and nods. He gets on his bike and follows Lily and Ben. They ride together in the rain. They are not jealous anymore. They are friends.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars and books. One day, they decided to play marry. They put on their nicest clothes and found some rings and flowers. "Are you ready, Ben?" Lily asked. She held a ring and a flower. "Yes, I am, Lily," Ben said. He held a ring and a flower too. But he felt nervous. He did not know how to play marry. He did not know what to say or do. They walked to the garden, where they had a big rock for a table. They put their rings and flowers on the rock. Then they looked at each other. "Now what?" Ben asked. "We have to say 'I do'," Lily said. "That's what people say when they marry." "Oh, okay," Ben said. He tried to say 'I do', but his voice was very quiet. He felt his face get hot. Lily waited for Ben to say 'I do'. She wondered why he was so quiet. She thought he did not want to play marry with her. She felt sad and angry. "Ben, do you want to play marry with me or not?" she asked. She raised her voice. Ben heard Lily's voice. He felt scared. He did not want to make Lily sad or angry. He wanted to play marry with her. He wanted to be her best friend. "Yes, yes, I do!" he said. He said it very loud. He smiled at Lily. Lily heard Ben's voice. She felt happy. She smiled at Ben too. "Then we are married!" she said. She took his ring and put it on his finger. He took her ring and put it on her finger. They hugged each other. They played marry for a long time. They had a cake and a dance and a kiss. They had fun. They were best friends.

One day, a little bug lay on a big leaf. The bug was not very smart, but it was happy. It liked to play in the sun and dance on the leaf. A bird saw the bug and thought it was stupid. The bird said, "Why do you lay on the leaf all day? You should fly like me." The bug did not care what the bird said. It just wanted to play and have fun. The bird tried to make the bug feel bad, but the bug kept playing. Soon, the bird saw how much fun the bug was having. The bird wanted to play too. So, the bird and the bug played together on the leaf. They had a great day and became good friends.
