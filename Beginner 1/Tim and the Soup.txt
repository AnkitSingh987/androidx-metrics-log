Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Soup

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Tim and Jack. They both lived in a big house and were always very happy. One day, Jack said to Tim "Let's make some soup!" Tim was glad and giggled. They went to the kitchen and started making the soup. First, they cut up all the vegetables and then put them into a big pot with some boiling water. They added some salt and spices and left it to cook. After a while, the soup smelled delicious! Tim and Jack settled down to eat their soup. They felt glad and warm inside. They both agreed that it was the best soup they had ever eaten. The End.

One day, a bald man named Tom was walking in the park. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tom wanted to help Sue find her toy, so he asked her, "What does your toy look like?" Sue said, "My toy is a big, red ball." They looked all around the park, but they could not find the big, red ball. Tom then had an idea. He thought that maybe a bird took the ball. So, they went to ask a bird if it had seen the ball. The bird did not know where the ball was, but it told Tom and Sue that a big cat with a long tail had taken the ball. Tom and Sue looked for the big cat, but they could not find it. They were very tired, so they sat down to rest. Tom started to cough. He had bad lungs and needed to rest. Suddenly, they saw the big cat with the long tail. It had Sue's big, red ball. Tom tried to get the ball back, but the cat was too strong. The cat ran away with the ball, and Tom could not catch it because of his bad lungs. Sue was still sad because she did not get her toy back. Tom was sad too because he could not help Sue. They both went home with heavy hearts, knowing that they had lost the big, red ball forever.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play together in their bedroom. They have many toys and books and pictures. Sometimes they make noise and mess, but they always help to clean up. One night, Lily and Ben hear a loud sound outside. They are scared. They run to their window and look. They see a big truck and some men. The men are taking away their old sofa and table. Mom and Dad told them they are getting new ones. "What are they doing?" Lily asks. "They are taking our things," Ben says. "Why?" Lily says. "Mom and Dad said they are old and broken," Ben says. "But I like them," Lily says. "Me too," Ben says. They feel sad. They hug each other. They go back to their bed. They cuddle with their teddy bears. Mom and Dad come to their bedroom. They see Lily and Ben are awake. "Are you okay, sweeties?" Mom says. "We heard a loud sound," Lily says. "We saw the men take our things," Ben says. Mom and Dad smile. They sit on their bed. They hug them. "Don't worry, my loves," Dad says. "Those things were old and broken," Mom says. "We are getting new ones tomorrow," Dad says. "New ones?" Lily says. "Yes, new ones. A new sofa and a new table. And a surprise for you," Mom says. "A surprise?" Ben says. "Yes, a surprise. Something you will like very much," Dad says. Lily and Ben are curious. They wonder what the surprise is. "Can you tell us?" Lily says. "No, you have to wait until tomorrow," Mom says. "Can you give us a hint?" Ben says. "No, it's a secret," Dad says. They giggle. They like secrets. "Okay, okay, we will wait," Lily says. "Good. Now, it's time to sleep," Mom says. "Okay. Good night, Mom and Dad," Ben says. "Good night, Lily and Ben," Mom and Dad say. They kiss them. They turn off the light. They leave the bedroom. Lily and Ben are happy. They are glad they have Mom and Dad. They are glad they have each other. They are glad they have a surprise. They close their eyes. They dream of new things.
