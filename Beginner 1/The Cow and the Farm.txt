Category: Beginner
Title: The Cow and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cow. She lived on a big farm with her friends. They all liked to play together. One day, the friendly cow had a fun idea. She wanted to decorate her home. She asked her friends to help her. They all said "yes" and were excited to help. They found nice leaves and pretty flowers to decorate the cow's home. They put them all around her home, making it look beautiful. The friendly cow was very happy, and her friends were happy too. They all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a bright little dog named Spot. Spot had a big job to do. He had to rescue his toy ball from the top of a hill. He knew he had to be very brave to get his ball back. One day, Spot took a big turn and started to walk up the hill. He saw a big bird who wanted to take his ball away. Spot knew he had to be fast to save his ball. He barked at the bird and scared it away. Spot kept going up the hill. He was almost there. He saw his ball at the top. With one big jump, Spot grabbed the ball and saved it. Spot was so happy and proud. He ran back down the hill and played with his ball all day long.

---

There was a king who liked to bite. He bit his food, he bit his toys, he bit his clothes. He even bit his people, who were very scared of him. The king thought biting was fun and made him strong. One day, the king saw a big red apple on a tree. He wanted to bite it very much. He climbed the tree and reached for the apple. But the apple was not ready. It was still sour and hard. The king did not know that. He bit the apple with all his teeth. But the apple bit him back. It was so sour that it made his mouth hurt. It was so hard that it broke his teeth. The king cried out in pain and fell from the tree. The people heard the king's cry and came to see what happened. They saw the king on the ground, with a broken tooth and a sour face. They saw the apple on the tree, with a bite mark and a smile. They were not scared of the king anymore. They were happy that the apple taught him a lesson. They picked the apple and thanked it for being brave. They decided to share the apple and be kind to each other. They hoped the king would learn to be kind too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to solve easy puzzles. One day, Lucy found a very big and difficult puzzle. She did not know how to solve it. Lucy asked her mom for help. Her mom gave her a small lecture about trying her best. Lucy listened carefully and felt brave. She was ready to try the difficult puzzle again. Lucy worked on the puzzle all day. It was hard, but she did not give up. Finally, she solved the difficult puzzle. Lucy was very happy and proud of herself. Her mom was proud of her too.

Lily likes to play with her toy box. She has many toys inside. She has dolls, cars, books, and balls. She likes to open the box and take out her toys. She likes to make stories with them. One day, Lily finds a nail in her toy box. She does not know what it is. It is long and shiny and hard. It has a pointy end and a flat end. She thinks it is a toy too. She picks it up and looks at it. She tries to play with the nail. She tries to put it on her doll's head. It does not stay. She tries to roll it with her car. It does not move. She tries to read it like a book. It does not have words. She tries to bounce it like a ball. It does not bounce. She does not like the nail. It is not a good toy. She wants to put the nail back in the toy box. But the box is closed. She tries to open it. She pushes and pulls and bangs. It does not open. She feels sad and mad. She wants her toys. She sees her mom in the kitchen. She runs to her mom. She shows her the nail. She says, "Mom, help me! I have a nail. It is not a toy. I want to put it back. But the box is closed. I can not open it. Please open it for me!" Her mom sees the nail. She is surprised and worried. She says, "Lily, where did you get this nail? This is not a toy. This is dangerous. You can hurt yourself with it. You should not play with it. Give it to me. I will put it away. And I will open the toy box for you. Come with me." She takes the nail from Lily. She puts it in a safe place. She goes to the toy box with Lily. She opens it for her. She says, "Here you go, Lily. Now you can play with your toys. But remember, do not touch the nail. It is not for you. It is for grown-ups. OK?" Lily nods. She says, "OK, mom. Thank you. I will not touch the nail. I will play with my toys. I like my toys. They are fun and nice. I love you, mom." She hugs her mom. She smiles. She takes out her toys. She makes stories with them. She is happy.
