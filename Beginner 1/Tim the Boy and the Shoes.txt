Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He wanted to go on a big journey. He asked his mom, "Can I go on a journey to the unknown park?" His mom said, "You must mind your steps and be careful." Tim put on his shoes and went outside. He walked and walked until he found the unknown park. It was a fun place with many new things. He saw a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. Tim was very happy. Tim played in the unknown park all day. He minded his steps and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, he said goodbye to the park. Tim told his mom all about his journey. His mom was glad that he had a good time and minded his steps.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they found a small shelter made of sticks and leaves in the woods. They wanted to see what was inside, so they crawled in. "Wow, look at this!" Anna said. She saw a soft blanket, a shiny toy car, and a bag of candy. "Someone left these here for us!" Ben was not so sure. He saw a note on the blanket that said, "Do not touch. This is mine." He felt scared. "Anna, I don't think we should take these things. They belong to someone else." Anna did not listen. She was selfish. She grabbed the candy and the toy car and put them in her pocket. "No, they are ours now. We found them first. Come on, let's go." She crawled out of the shelter and ran away. Ben followed her, but he felt bad. He wished he could say sorry to the person who owned the things. But before they could get far, they heard a loud roar. A big bear came out of the bushes and ran towards them. It was angry. It smelled the candy and the toy car. It wanted them back. It was the bear's shelter and the bear's things. Anna and Ben were very surprised. They dropped the candy and the toy car and ran as fast as they could. They screamed for help. They learned a lesson. They should not be selfish and take things that are not theirs.

One day, a little girl named Lisa found a big box in her room. In the box, there were many unusual things. She saw a lot of papers and files. Lisa wanted to sort them. Lisa called her friend Tom to help. "Tom, can you help me sort these files?" she asked. Tom said, "Yes, I can help you, Lisa!" They started to sort the files. Lisa found a file with a picture of a cat. Tom found a file with a picture of a dog. They laughed and had fun. They sorted all the files and put them in the box. When they finished, Lisa and Tom felt happy. They had sorted all the unusual files. They played with the pictures and made a fun game. At the end of the day, they said goodbye and Lisa put the box away.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. Today is a mild day. The sun is warm and the wind is soft. "Look, Tom, a big slide!" Sue says. She runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. "I am going to slide down fast!" Tom follows her. He is a little scared of the slide. It is very high and steep. He does not want to slide down fast. He wants to slide down slow. "Come on, Tom, it is fun!" Sue says. She slides down the slide and laughs. She lands on the grass and rolls over. "Do you want to try?" Tom looks at the slide. He is not sure. He wants to be brave, but he also wants to be safe. He thinks for a moment. Then he has an idea. "OK, Sue, I will try. But can you hold my hand?" he asks. He reaches out his hand to Sue. Sue smiles. She takes his hand and helps him up the ladder. "Sure, Tom, I will hold your hand. We are friends, and friends help each other. That is true." They slide down the slide together, holding hands. Tom feels Sue's hand and he feels less scared. He feels happy and proud. He slides down slow, but he still has fun. They reach the bottom and hug each other. "That was fun, Tom! You did it!" Sue says. "Thank you, Sue! You are a good friend. You are true and kind." Tom says. They go to play some more. They are happy. They are friends. They are true.
