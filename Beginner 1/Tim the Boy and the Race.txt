Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Race

One day, a boy named Tim and his friend, Sam, wanted to race. They both loved to run fast. They agreed to race to the big tree in the park. Sam was very fast, and Tim was a little slower, but they both thought it would be a harmless race. Before the race, Tim found a piece of gum on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to Sam. Sam said, "Yuck! Don't eat that, it's dirty!" Tim laughed and put the gum in his pocket. Then, they got ready to race. On the count of three, they started to run. Tim and Sam were both running very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim tripped and fell. The gum flew out of his pocket and landed on the ground. Tim tried to get up, but he slipped on the gum and fell again. Sam won the race, but Tim was very sad. He had lost the race and got all dirty from the gum.

One day, a big boat was at the port. The boat was very hard and strong. The boat liked to zoom fast in the water. The boat had a friend, a little bird. The bird liked to fly high in the sky. One day, the boat and the bird wanted to have a race. They wanted to see who was faster, the boat that could zoom or the bird that could fly. The boat said, "I will win the race because I am hard and strong." The bird said, "I will win the race because I can fly high." The race began, and the boat zoomed fast in the water. The bird flew high in the sky. They both tried very hard to win. The boat was ahead, but then it saw big rocks in the water. The boat remembered it was hard and strong and could go through the rocks. The boat went through the rocks and won the race. The bird was happy for the boat, and they were still good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. Inside the barn lived a patient cow named Moomoo. Moomoo liked to read books. She read books all day. One day, Moomoo's friend, a little chick named Peep, came to the barn. Peep wanted to play, but Moomoo wanted to read her book. Peep was sad. He wanted Moomoo to play with him. Moomoo saw that Peep was sad. She put down her book and played with Peep. They played in the barn all day. They were both happy. At the end of the day, Moomoo and Peep read a book together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, empty box. He wanted to have fun with the box. He thought very hard about what to do with it. One day, Tim saw his friends playing with their toys. He had an idea. He took his empty box and filled it with all his toys. He went to his friends and said, "Let's have fun and share our toys!" His friends were very happy. They played and shared their toys all day long. Tim learned that sharing can make an empty box full of fun. And that was the day Tim and his friends had the most fun ever. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big toy box. She loved to play with her toys every day. One day, Amy's mom told her that they were going on a trip. Amy was very excited. She knew she had to pack her toys for the trip. Amy opened her toy box and started to pack her toys. She counted the number of toys she had. She had five toys. Amy packed her teddy bear, her doll, her toy car, her ball, and her toy train. She put them all in a bag. On the day of the trip, Amy's mom asked, "Did you pack your toys, Amy?" Amy said, "Yes, Mom. I packed all my toys." But Amy's mom saw that the toy box was still open. She went to close it and found a naughty toy monkey hiding inside. "Look, Amy! You forgot to pack this naughty toy monkey," said Amy's mom. Amy laughed and said, "Silly monkey! You need to come with us too!" She put the naughty toy monkey in the bag with the other toys. Then, Amy and her mom left for their trip, ready for lots of fun and playtime.
