Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play outside. She saw a big, red ball. Mia wanted to catch the ball, so she ran and jumped. But she fell into a pile of soft, white powder. It was flour! Mia's friend, Tom, saw her fall. He laughed and said, "You look like a white ghost!" Mia felt sad and asked, "Can you help me clean up?" Tom was a reliable friend, so he said, "Yes, let's go to the water and wash the powder off." But when they got to the water, a big dog was there. The dog wanted to play, too. It tried to catch the ball, but it got flour all over it. Mia and Tom laughed and said, "Now we all need to clean up!" So they all played in the water together and became friends.

One day, a powerful cat named Tom saw a big bin. He wanted to act like a king. So, he put a crown on his head and stood on top of the bin. He looked at all the other cats and said, "I am King Tom! I am the most powerful cat!" A little cat named Lucy heard Tom. She wanted to act like a queen. So, she put a crown on her head too and stood next to Tom on the bin. She said, "I am Queen Lucy! I am also a powerful cat!" Tom and Lucy acted like king and queen on the bin. They told all the other cats what to do. The other cats listened and did what they were told. They all had a fun day playing together. At the end of the day, Tom and Lucy took off their crowns and went back to being normal cats. They had fun acting like king and queen, but they knew it was just a game.

Ben liked to organize his toys. He had a big box for his cars, a small box for his blocks, and a shelf for his books. He liked to make everything neat and tidy. He did not like when his sister, Mia, messed up his toys. She liked to play with them and leave them everywhere. One day, Ben was organizing his toys when he saw something unknown on the floor. It was hard and shiny and had a long cord. He picked it up and looked at it. He wondered what it was and what it did. He thought it looked like a car, but it had no wheels. He decided to put it in his big box with his cars. Mia came into the room and saw Ben's big box. She wanted to play with his cars. She opened the box and saw the unknown thing. She thought it looked like a phone, but it had no buttons. She decided to try it. She pulled the cord and plugged it into the wall. She heard a hiss and saw some steam. Ben heard the hiss and saw the steam. He ran to the box and saw Mia holding the unknown thing. He knew it was not a toy. He knew it was something hot and dangerous. He shouted, "Mia, no! That's an iron! It can burn you!" He grabbed the iron from Mia and unplugged it. He put it back on the floor and told Mia to stay away from it. Mia was scared and sorry. She did not know it was an iron. She did not know it could burn her. She hugged Ben and said, "Thank you, Ben. You saved me. I'm sorry I messed up your toys." Ben hugged Mia and said, "It's okay, Mia. I'm glad you're okay. But next time, don't touch unknown things. And don't play with my toys without asking." Mia nodded and said, "Okay, Ben. I promise." They smiled and played together with Ben's toys. Ben let Mia use some of his cars. He was happy to share with his sister. He was a good brother.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk in the park with his friend, a cat named Luna. They liked to play together and have fun. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. It was a regular day for them. As they walked, Max heard some news from Luna. She said, "I heard there is a new toy at the store. Let's go see it!" Max was very excited and they quickly went to the store. When they got to the store, they saw the new toy. It was a ball that could shake and make funny sounds. Max said, "Wow, that looks fun! Let's play with it!" Luna agreed, and they had a great time playing with the shaking ball.
