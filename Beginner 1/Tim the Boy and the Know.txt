Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Know

One day, a boy named Tim saw a dark jeep. He wanted to know who it belonged to. He asked his friend, Sam. "Do you know who owns the dark jeep?" Sam did not know. Tim and Sam went to ask their friend, Sue. "Do you know who owns the dark jeep?" Sue said, "I think it belongs to Mr. Brown." But Mr. Brown was not a nice man. Tim, Sam, and Sue went to Mr. Brown's house. They were scared, but they wanted to know for sure. They asked Mr. Brown, "Is the dark jeep yours?" Mr. Brown said, "Yes, it is mine." The kids were sad, but they were brave to find out the truth.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was restless. He did not want to sleep. He wanted to play. But it was bedtime. Tim's mom said, "Remember, we read a book before bed." Tim picked a book about a cat and a dog. The cat and the dog did not like each other. They would fight and chase each other. One day, the cat got stuck in a tree. The dog saw and wanted to help. The dog barked and barked until someone came to help the cat. After the cat was safe, the cat and the dog became friends. They did not fight anymore. Tim liked the story. He felt sleepy. Tim's mom said, "See, the book helped you rest." Tim closed his eyes and went to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He wanted to eat a big snack. Tim thought about what he could eat. Tim saw his toy printer. He had an idea. "Maybe I can print a big snack!" Tim said. He pressed the button on the printer. Out came a picture of a snack. But it was not real. It was just a picture. Tim felt sad. Then, Tim found some ash from the fireplace. He mixed the ash with water. He made a gray snack. Tim showed it to his mom. "Look, I made a snack!" he said. His mom laughed. "That's not a snack, Tim. That's just ash and water!" She gave Tim a real snack. Tim ate it and was not hungry anymore.

One day, a little otter was playing by the river. He saw a dull rock and picked it up. The otter wanted to find a friend to play with, so he looked around. "Hi, rock," said the otter to the dull rock. "Do you want to play with me?" The rock did not reply. The otter felt sad and put the rock down. Just then, a big frog jumped out of the river. "Hi, otter!" said the frog. "Do you want to play with me?" The otter was so happy and replied, "Yes! I would love to play with you!" And they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a selfish man named Tom. He had a lot of gold. He did not like to share his gold with others. Tom always wanted more gold for himself. One day, Tom met a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a big paper heart. She asked Tom, "Can you help me tear this heart into two pieces? I want to give one piece to my friend." Tom said, "No, I won't help you. I am busy looking for more gold." Lucy was sad, but she did not give up. She asked other people in the village to help her tear the heart. They all helped her, and soon, she had many pieces of the heart to share with her friends. When Tom saw how happy Lucy and her friends were, he realized that sharing can make people happy. He decided to share his gold with the village. From that day on, Tom was not selfish anymore. He learned that sharing with others can bring happiness to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play dress-up with her clothes. One day, she heard about a fashion show where kids could enter and show off their best outfits. Lucy was nervous, but she wanted to try. She put on her favorite dress, a pretty hat, and shiny shoes. She looked in the mirror and said, "I can do it!" Then, she went to the fashion show with her mom. When it was her turn, Lucy walked on the stage. She heard people say, "Wow! She looks great!" Lucy smiled and felt happy. After the show, she told her mom, "I was nervous, but I had fun!" Her mom hugged her and said, "I'm proud of you, Lucy!"
