Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Park

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. But today, the park is very busy. There are many people and cars and noises. Lily and Tom have to wear masks. Masks cover their mouths and noses. They help them stay safe from germs. But masks make it hard to speak and breathe. Lily and Tom find a bench to sit on. They want to talk and share their snacks. But a big man comes and sits next to them. He does not wear a mask. He coughs and sneezes and talks very loud. "Go away!" Lily says to the man. "You are not safe. You need a mask." The man does not listen. He laughs and says, "I don't need a mask. Masks are silly. You are silly." Tom gets angry. He stands up and says, "No, you are silly. And mean. And rude. You are making us sick. Leave us alone." The man gets angry too. He stands up and says, "Who are you to tell me what to do? You are just kids. You don't know anything." Lily and Tom are scared. They hold hands and run away. They look for a safer place to play. They hope the man will learn to wear a mask and be nice.

Once upon a time, there was a big ball that could roll. One day, the ball saw a long strip of grass. The ball felt worried because it was very far from home. The ball wanted to go back home, but it did not know how. The ball had an idea. It would roll on the strip of grass to find its way home. The ball rolled and rolled, and the sky became dark. The ball was still worried, but it did not give up. Finally, the ball saw its home. It was so happy! The ball rolled faster and faster. When the ball reached its home, it was not worried anymore. The ball had learned that even when it was worried, it could still find its way home.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a magnet. She played with it all day. She liked how it could stick to things. She showed it to her mom. "Look, Mom! I found a magnet!" she said. Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Lily! Let's see what it can stick to." They put the magnet on the fridge. It stuck! They put it on a can. It stuck again! Lily was so happy. The next day, it was very hot. Lily wanted to play with water. She filled a big tub with water. She put her toys in the water. She saw her magnet and thought, "I wonder if it can soak." She put the magnet in the water. It got all wet, but it still worked! Lily's mom made a delicious snack for her. "Here, Lily, have some yummy fruit," she said. Lily ate the fruit. It was so good! She shared some with her mom. They both enjoyed the delicious snack. Lily and her mom had a fun day with the magnet and the water. They learned that magnets can still work when they are wet. They also enjoyed the delicious fruit. Lily couldn't wait to play with her magnet again tomorrow.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They run, jump, slide and swing. They have fun. But one day, Ben is not in the park. Anna waits and waits, but he does not come. She feels lonely. She does not want to play by herself. She sees a lady with a dog. The dog is big and brown. It has a red collar. The lady says, "Hello, little girl. Do you want to pet my dog? His name is Rex." Anna likes dogs. She nods and smiles. She walks to the lady and the dog. She pauses. She is a little scared. The dog is bigger than her. The lady says, "Don't worry. Rex is very friendly. He likes children. He won't bite. He just wants to say hello." Anna reaches out her hand. The dog sniffs it. He licks it. He wags his tail. Anna giggles. She pets the dog. She feels happy. The lady says, "You are very brave. Do you want to join us for lunch? I have some sandwiches and cookies. Rex has some bones and water." Anna says, "Yes, please. Thank you. Can I call my mom first?" The lady says, "Of course. Where is your mom?" Anna says, "She is at home. She is working. She said I can play in the park until noon." The lady says, "OK. Let's go to that bench. You can use my phone to call your mom. Then we can have lunch together." Anna says, "OK. Thank you. You are very nice. And Rex is very cute." The lady says, "You are very welcome. And you are very sweet. I'm glad we met. Maybe we can see each other again." Anna says, "Yes, maybe. I hope so. Maybe Ben can come too. He is my friend. He likes dogs too." The lady says, "That would be nice. Maybe we can be friends too." Anna says, "Yes, we can. Friends are good." They walk to the bench. They call Anna's mom. They have lunch. They talk and laugh. They are not lonely anymore.
