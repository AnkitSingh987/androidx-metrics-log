Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Mom

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. Tim saw big waves and wanted to surf. He saw other kids surfing and thought it looked fun. He asked his mom, "Can I surf too?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. You can try, but be careful." Tim was very happy. He grabbed a surfboard and went into the water. At first, he fell off the surfboard many times. But Tim did not give up. After trying and trying, Tim started to succeed. He stood up on the surfboard and rode the waves. His mom watched and clapped for him. Tim was very proud and said, "Look, Mom! I can surf!" And they both had a great day at the beach.

One day, a big round ball went for a roll down a hill. On the way, it met a nosy cat. The cat said, "Where are you going, ball?" The ball said, "I am going to find a friend to play with. Do you want to come?" The nosy cat said, "Yes, I want to come too!" So, they both went down the hill together. As they rolled down, the nosy cat saw a big tree. The cat wanted to rub on the tree, but the ball wanted to keep rolling. The cat said, "I want to rub the tree, but you want to roll. We can't do both." The ball said, "I don't want to stop. I want to keep rolling." The nosy cat got mad and said, "Fine, go roll by yourself!" The ball rolled away fast and got lost. The nosy cat stayed by the tree, sad and alone. They both lost their new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big red barrel in his yard. He did not know where it came from. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a red barrel in the yard." His mom said, "I did not mention a red barrel. Let's go see it." When they went to the yard, they saw a cat stuck on top of the barrel. The cat was scared and could not get down. Tim wanted to help the cat, but he did not know how. Just then, a big wind came and blew the barrel over. The cat jumped off and ran away. Tim and his mom were happy the cat was safe. They still did not know where the red barrel came from, but they were glad it helped the cat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a best friend, a small dog named Max. They liked to play together and have fun. One day, they went to the park to play. While they were playing, Tim's mom said, "Remember, Tim, you need to stay with Max and be quiet near the other kids." Tim nodded and said, "Okay, Mom." Tim and Max played quietly and had a great time. Later, Tim and Max found a big tree. They sat under the tree and talked. Tim said to Max, "I like when we play together and stay quiet. It's fun!" Max wagged his tail and barked happily. They played under the tree until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a mask in her room. The mask was very pretty. Sue put the mask on her face. Sue went outside to play. She saw her friend, Tom. Tom was weak because he was sick. Tom looked at Sue and said, "What is that on your face?" Sue replied, "It's a mask!" Tom wanted to try the mask too. Sue gave the mask to Tom. He put it on his face and felt better. They played together all day long. At the end of the day, they went home and shared the mask with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Max. He loved to play with his toy iron. One day, Max heard about a big race for dogs. He wanted to win and make his family happy. On the day of the race, all the dogs lined up. Max was very excited. He looked at his friend, a small dog named Lucy, and said, "I want to win! Let's do our best!" Lucy wagged her tail and said, "Yes! Let's go!" The race started, and all the dogs ran as fast as they could. Max was ahead, but then he saw something unexpected. His toy iron was on the ground! He stopped to pick it up. Lucy saw Max and ran back to help him. Together, they carried the toy iron and crossed the finish line last. But they were both happy because they had been loyal friends and had fun.
