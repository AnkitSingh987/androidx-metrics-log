Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Sue

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big steel ball in his yard. He did not know where it came from. He felt lucky to find it. Tim wanted to play with it, but it was too heavy to pick up. He tried to push it, but it did not move. Tim was sad. Tim's friend, Sue, came to play. She saw the big steel ball and wanted to help Tim. They both tried to push the ball, but it still did not move. Sue did not know what to do, so she just shrugged. Tim and Sue sat down and thought about how to move the ball. Then, Tim had an idea. They could roll the ball! Tim and Sue stood up and tried to roll the big steel ball. It started to move! They were so happy. They rolled the ball around the yard all day. Tim and Sue felt very lucky to play with the big steel ball.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog. The dog liked to talk to his friends. His friends were a cat, a bird, and a fish. They all lived in a big house. One day, they wanted to play a game. They played with a ball. The brown dog would pass the ball to the cat. The cat would pass the ball to the bird. The bird would pass the ball to the fish. They had lots of fun. After they played, they sat down and talked. They talked about the fun they had. The brown dog was happy to have such good friends. They all loved to play and talk together.

Tom and Sue like to help Mom bake cakes. They like to crack eggs, pour milk, and stir flour. But they do not like to wash the mixer. The mixer is filthy. It has sticky batter and crumbs all over it. One day, Mom says, "Tom and Sue, I need to go to the store. Can you mind the mixer for me? Please do not touch it or play with it. It is very dirty and it can hurt you." Tom and Sue say, "Yes, Mom, we will mind the mixer." But as soon as Mom leaves, they forget what she said. They think the mixer looks fun. They want to see what it can do. They plug in the mixer and turn it on. It makes a loud noise and spins very fast. They laugh and clap. They put their toys in the mixer and watch them fly out. They put their hands in the mixer and feel the tickle. They do not see the mess they are making. Suddenly, they hear Mom's car in the driveway. They turn off the mixer and hide under the table. They hope Mom will not see them or the mixer. But Mom sees everything. She sees the filthy mixer and the toys and the batter and the crumbs. She sees Tom and Sue under the table. She is very angry. "Tom and Sue, what did you do?" she says. "Did you not mind the mixer like I told you? You made a big mess and you could have hurt yourself. You are very naughty." Tom and Sue feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We did not mind the mixer. We wanted to have fun. We will not do it again." Mom says, "You need to learn to listen and obey. You need to help me clean up this mess. And you need to say sorry to the mixer. It is not a toy. It is a tool." Tom and Sue say, "We are sorry, Mom. We are sorry, mixer. We will help you clean up. We will mind the mixer next time."

Once upon a time, a little bird lived on the earth. The bird was very thirsty and wanted to drink some water. But the sun was very hot and all the water was gone. The bird was sad. One day, the bird met a big, selfish frog. The frog had a lot of water in a small pond. The bird asked, "Can I please drink some water from your pond?" The frog said, "No, this is my water. You cannot drink it." The little bird was very sad and flew away. But then, it started to rain. The rain filled a new pond with water for the bird to drink. The bird was very happy and shared the water with all its friends. The selfish frog saw this and learned that sharing is better than being selfish.
