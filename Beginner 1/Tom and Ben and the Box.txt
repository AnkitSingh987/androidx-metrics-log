Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Ben and the Box

Tom and Ben are friends. They like to play with cars and balls. One day, they find a big box of cookies in the kitchen. They are very hungry and want to eat them all. "Let's take the cookies and hide in the garden," Tom says. "We can eat them and no one will know." "OK," Ben says. "But we have to be quick and quiet." They take the cookies and run to the garden. They find a wide tree with thick branches. They climb up the tree and sit on a branch. They open the box and start to eat the cookies. They are very sweet and crunchy. "These are the best cookies ever," Tom says. "We are so lucky to find them." "Yes, they are," Ben says. "But maybe we should save some for later. We might get sick if we eat too many." "No, we won't," Tom says. "We can eat them all. They are ours. We don't have to share with anyone." They eat and eat and eat. They eat until the box is empty. They feel very full and happy. They smile at each other and wipe their mouths. "That was fun," Tom says. "Let's do it again tomorrow." "OK," Ben says. "But we have to hide the box. We don't want anyone to find out." They throw the box down the tree and jump down. They run back to the house. They think they are very smart and clever. But they are not. The next day, they wake up with a big problem. They have a bad stomach ache. They feel very sick and unhappy. They can't play with cars and balls. They have to stay in bed and drink water. They cry and moan. "What happened to us?" Tom asks. "Why do we feel so bad?" "Maybe it was the cookies," Ben says. "Maybe we ate too many. Maybe we were greedy and selfish." "Yes, maybe you were," a voice says. It is Mom. She is standing at the door. She looks very angry and sad. She has the empty box in her hand. She knows what they did. "Did you take the cookies from the kitchen?" she asks. "Did you eat them all by yourselves?" Tom and Ben nod. They are very ashamed and sorry. They know they did something wrong. "Those cookies were for your sister's birthday party," Mom says. "They were a special treat for her and her friends. You ruined her surprise. You were very naughty and mean." Tom and Ben feel very bad. They love their sister. They don't want to make her sad. They wish they could take back what they did. "We are sorry, Mom," they say. "We are sorry, sister. We made a mistake. We won't do it again. Please forgive us." Mom sighs. She is not happy, but she loves them. She knows they are young and they need to learn. "I forgive you, boys," she says. "But you have to gain your sister's forgiveness too. You have to do something nice for her. You have to show her that you care. And you have to share with others. Sharing is caring. Do you understand?" Tom and Ben nod. They understand. They want to be good and kind. They want to be better friends. They hug Mom and say thank you. They hug each other and say sorry. They learn their lesson. The end.

Lily and Max were bored. They had played with all their toys and read all their books. They wanted to go outside and have fun, but it was too hot and sunny. "Can we play with the hose, Mom?" Lily asked. Mom was busy in the kitchen. She sighed. "Not now, sweetie. I have to finish cooking. Maybe later, okay?" Lily and Max were sad. They looked out the window and saw the hose lying on the grass. It looked so cool and wet. They wished they could spray each other and make rainbows. "Let's sneak out and play with the hose," Max whispered. "Mom won't notice. She's too busy." Lily nodded. She liked Max's idea. They put on their hats and shoes and tiptoed to the door. They were very quiet. They opened the door and ran to the hose. Max turned on the tap and grabbed the hose. He sprayed Lily with a big jet of water. Lily squealed and laughed. She grabbed the other end of the hose and sprayed Max back. They had a lot of fun. They forgot about the heat and the sun. But they did not forget about Mom. Mom heard the noise and looked out the window. She saw Lily and Max playing with the hose. She was not happy. She turned off the stove and went outside. "Lily and Max, what are you doing?" Mom asked. "You know you're not supposed to play with the hose without asking me. You're wasting water and making a mess." Lily and Max stopped playing. They looked at Mom. They knew they were in trouble. They dropped the hose and hung their heads. "We're sorry, Mom," they said. "We were bored and hot. We just wanted to have fun." Mom sighed again. She was not very angry. She understood how they felt. She remembered when she was a kid and played with the hose too. "It's okay, kids," she said. "But next time, please ask me first. And don't play too long. Water is precious and we have to save it. Now, come inside and dry off. I have a surprise for you." Lily and Max followed Mom inside. They wondered what the surprise was. They hoped it was something good. They smiled at each other. They were still wet and happy.
