Category: Beginner
Title: The Heavy Ball and the Mirror

Once upon a time, there was a heavy ball. It was so big and round. One day, the ball saw its image in a mirror. It looked very funny. The ball wanted to spin like a top. It tried and tried, but it was too heavy. The ball felt sad because it could not spin. Then, a kind wind came and helped the heavy ball. The wind blew and blew, and the ball started to spin. It spun fast and round. The ball was so happy. It saw its image spinning in the mirror and laughed. The end.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a bright light in the sky. She blinked her eyes and saw a big bird. The bird came down and said, "Hi! I'm Bob." Sue and Bob the bird became friends. They played all day. They had a lot of fun. Then, they saw a big box. They did not know what was in it. "Let's wait and see," said Bob. They waited for a while. Then, the box opened. Out came a small cat. The cat was nice and said, "Hi! I'm Max." Sue, Bob, and Max played together and had a great day.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big toy in the store. He wanted to own the toy. He felt anxious because he did not have enough money. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have the toy, please?" His mom said, "No, we cannot buy it today." Tim was sad and thought of a plan. He took the toy when no one was in sight. He ran home with the toy and hid it in his room. He played with the toy all day and felt happy. But the next day, his mom found the toy. She knew Tim did not have money to buy it. She was very angry. Tim had to return the toy to the store and say sorry. He was not allowed to have any toys for a long time.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the well. He was happy because he loved to play there. He liked to throw rocks into the well and listen to the splash. Today, he saw his friend Sue by the well. Sue was not happy. She looked sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my toy in the well. I am not excited to play now." Tom wanted to help Sue. He had an idea. He ran to get a long stick. Tom put the stick into the well. He tried to get Sue's toy. Suddenly, the stick moved. Tom pulled it out. The toy was not there. But, there was a big, shiny rock. Tom and Sue were excited. They found treasure! They were both happy now.

Lily and Ben are friends. They go to the same class. They like to play and learn together. One day, they have a special lesson. They go to the park with their teacher and see many animals. "Look, Lily, a big bird!" Ben says. He points to a swan on the pond. "It has a long neck and white feathers." Lily gazes at the swan. She thinks it is beautiful. She sees something else. "And look, Ben, a baby bird!" She says. She points to a cygnet next to the swan. "It has a short neck and gray feathers." Ben gazes at the cygnet. He thinks it is cute. He sees something else. "And look, Lily, a fish!" He says. He points to a carp in the water. "It has scales and fins." Lily and Ben gaze at the fish. They think it is shiny. They see many other things in the park. They see flowers, trees, bugs, and squirrels. They have fun and learn a lot. When they go back to the class, they tell their teacher what they saw. Their teacher is happy. She gives them a sweet treat. Lily and Ben share the treat. They are happy too. They say thank you to their teacher and hug each other. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to play near the river with her toy boat. One day, she saw a row of ducks swimming in the water. Lily wanted to play with the ducks, so she sent her toy boat to them. But the ducks did not like the toy boat. They quacked loudly and swam away. Lily felt sad because she wanted to be friends with the ducks. She tried to send her toy boat to them again, but the ducks still swam away. Lily had an idea. She found some bread and put it on her toy boat. She sent the boat to the ducks with the bread. The ducks saw the bread and swam to the boat. They ate the bread and quacked happily. Lily was happy too, because she made friends with the ducks. They all played together by the river every day.
