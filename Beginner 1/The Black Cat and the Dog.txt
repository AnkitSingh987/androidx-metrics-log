Category: Beginner
Title: The Black Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat. The cat loved to enjoy playing outside. One sunny day, the cat went outside to play with her friends, a dog and a bird. While they were playing, they found a big jar of jam. The cat, the dog, and the bird all looked at the jam. They wanted to taste it. So they opened the jar and each took a little taste. The black cat, the dog, and the bird all loved the jam. They enjoyed it so much that they ate it all up. After they finished the jam, they played some more. They had a very fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a big jet. The jet was very restless. It wanted to fly high in the sky. One day, the jet met a little bird. "Hi, bird!" said the jet. "I want to fly high. Can you help me?" The bird thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, I can help you. I can offer you some tips." The bird told the jet to go fast and flap its wings. The jet tried very hard, but it could not fly. The jet was sad. The bird felt bad and said, "I am sorry, jet. I tried my best to help you." The jet was still restless and sad. It could not fly high in the sky like it wanted. The jet stayed on the ground, wishing it could fly with the bird. The end.

Tom and Mia are playing in the garden. They have a big bucket of water and some toys. They splash and laugh and have fun. Mia sees a phone on the table. It is Mom's phone. She wants to play with it. She takes the phone and puts it in the bucket. She thinks it is a boat. Tom sees what Mia does. He is shocked. He knows the phone is not a toy. He knows the phone will be wet and broken. He runs to Mia and takes the phone out of the bucket. "Mia, no!" he shouts. "You can't do that! The phone is not a boat. The phone is for talking. Mom needs the phone. The phone is wet and bad now. You are naughty!" Mia does not understand. She looks at Tom and the phone. She is confused. She does not know why Tom is angry. She does not know what the phone is for. She starts to cry. Tom feels sorry for Mia. He knows she is little and does not know better. He tries to explain to Mia. He shows her the phone and the bucket. "Mia, look. The phone is like this." He points to the screen and the buttons. "You can see pictures and words and numbers. You can press the buttons and make sounds. You can talk to Mom and Dad and Grandma and other people. The phone is good and useful." He points to the bucket. "The water is like this. You can splash and play and have fun. You can make bubbles and waves and sounds. The water is good and fun." He points to the phone and the bucket together. "But the phone and the water are not good together. The water makes the phone wet and sick. The phone can't see or talk or make sounds anymore. The phone is bad and useless. Mom will be sad and angry. You can't put the phone in the water. You have to be careful and gentle with the phone. Do you understand?" Mia nods. She understands a little. She feels bad for making the phone wet and sick. She says sorry to Tom and the phone. She hugs Tom and the phone. She says she will not do it again. Tom smiles. He hugs Mia and the phone. He says it is okay. He says he will help her dry the phone and fix it. He says he loves her. He says they can play with other toys in the garden. They go back to the bucket and the toys. They splash and laugh and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big pond. In the pond, there was a small fish named Splash. Splash loved to play and swim with his friends. They would splash each other with water and laugh. One day, a loud noise came from the ground. It was an earthquake! The ground shook and the pond water moved. Splash and his friends were scared. They knew it could be dangerous. The earthquake stopped and everything was quiet. Splash and his friends swam together to make sure everyone was okay. They were happy that the dangerous earthquake was over. They went back to splashing and playing in the pond, thankful for their safe home.
