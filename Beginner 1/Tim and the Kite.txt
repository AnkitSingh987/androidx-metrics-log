Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Kite

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a pretty kite that he loved to fly. One day, Tim took his kite to the park to play with his friends. Tim's mommy warned him, "Be good and don't be naughty, or your kite might fly away." Tim promised to be good and not be naughty. But when he was playing, he saw a big tree and wanted to see if his kite could fly higher than the tree. Tim's friends told him not to do it, but Tim did not listen. Suddenly, a strong wind came and took Tim's kite up, up, up into the sky. The kite flew away and Tim could not catch it. He felt very sad. Tim learned that he should have listened to his mommy and not been naughty. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and not be naughty, or you might lose something you love.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a little bunny. This bunny had a small ugly patch on his back. He was sad because he thought no one would like him with the ugly patch. One day, the bunny saw a big tree with lots of yummy apples. He wanted to eat them, but they were too high. He tried to jump, but he could not reach. He did not manage to get the apples. Then, a tall giraffe came to help the bunny. The giraffe did not care about the ugly patch. He helped the bunny get the apples. They both ate the apples and became good friends. The bunny was happy and learned that true friends do not care about how you look.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to play. She went to the attic where she found her old toys. The attic was cold, but Lily did not mind. She put on a warm sweater and began to play with her toys. She found a toy bathtub and a little doll. Lily said to the doll, "You are dirty. You need a bath!" She took the toy bathtub and filled it with warm water. She put the doll in the water and began to bathe it. She scrubbed the doll with a little sponge until it was clean. When the doll was all clean, Lily took it out of the water and wrapped it in a soft towel. She said, "Now you are clean and warm!" Lily played with her doll in the cold attic all day long. They had a lot of fun together.

Molly was a young girl who loved to help her mom. One morning, Molly and her mom were in the kitchen. Molly picked up the big pot and tried to place it on the stove. "Be careful, Molly," said her mom. But, Molly did not listen. Suddenly, the pot dropped to the floor and made a loud noise. "Oh no!" Molly's mom cried. "Now we have a big mess on the floor." Molly was scared but she knew she must help. They worked together to clean up the mess. In the end, they fixed the kitchen. Molly was so glad they were okay. She learned a lesson that morning - to be more careful.

Ben and Jen are twins. They like to play in the park. They have a big red ball. They like to kick the ball and catch the ball. One day, they see a big tree in the park. The tree has many fruits. They see apples, oranges, bananas, and grapes. They want to eat the fruits. They say, "Let's get some fruits!" They run to the tree. But the tree is too tall. They cannot reach the fruits. They try to jump. They try to climb. They try to throw the ball. But they cannot get the fruits. They are sad. They see a boy and a girl. They are also twins. They have a big blue ball. They also want to eat the fruits. They say, "Hello, we are Sam and Pam. Can we play with you?" Ben and Jen say, "Hello, we are Ben and Jen. We want to play with you. But we also want to eat the fruits. Can you help us?" Sam and Pam say, "Yes, we can help you. We have an idea. Let's unite our balls. Then we can make a big ball. Then we can roll the big ball to the tree. Then we can stand on the big ball. Then we can reach the fruits. It is easy!" Ben and Jen say, "That is a good idea. Let's do it!" They unite their balls. They make a big ball. They roll the big ball to the tree. They stand on the big ball. They reach the fruits. They pick the fruits. They share the fruits. They eat the fruits. They are happy. They say, "Thank you for helping us. You are our friends. We like to play with you. We like to eat fruits with you. Fruits are yummy!" They play and eat and laugh. They have fun in the park. They are happy twins.
