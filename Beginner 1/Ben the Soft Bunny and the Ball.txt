Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Soft Bunny and the Ball

One day, a soft bunny named Ben was playing in the forest. He loved to hop and play with his friends. As they played, Ben saw a big tree. He wanted to shoot a ball into a hole in the tree. Ben said to his friends, "I want to shoot the ball into the hole. Can you help me?" His friends nodded and they all tried to shoot the ball together. They tried and tried, but the ball would not go in. Ben's mom came and saw them playing. She had a napkin in her hand. She said, "Why don't you use this napkin to help you?" Ben and his friends took the napkin and wrapped it around the ball. They tried to shoot the ball again. This time, the ball went into the hole! Ben and his friends were so happy. They thanked Ben's mom for her help. From that day on, they always used a napkin to help them shoot the ball into the tree hole. And they had lots of fun playing together in the forest.

One day, a fish named Fin was swimming very fast in the water. He loved to speed through the blue sea. He was a happy fish, but sometimes he felt guilty for swimming so fast. He was scared that he might bump into his friends. One day, while Fin was speeding through the water, he saw a big fish. The big fish said, "Slow down, Fin! You might hurt someone!" Fin felt guilty and said, "I'm sorry, I will swim slower." The big fish smiled and swam away. Fin tried to swim slower, but it was hard. He loved to speed through the water. Then, something unexpected happened. Fin saw a little fish stuck in a net. The little fish was scared. Fin knew what to do. He used his speed to break the net and save the little fish. The little fish said, "Thank you, Fin!" Fin learned that sometimes it is good to speed, but only when it can help others.

Once upon a time, there was a big plane. The plane was very dirty. It wanted to be clean. The plane had a friend named Tim. Tim was deaf, but he loved to help his friends. One day, the plane asked Tim to help it be clean. Tim said yes. They went to find water and a big towel. Tim and the plane worked together. They cleaned the plane until it was shiny and new. The plane was so happy. It thanked Tim for his help. Tim was happy too. They were the best of friends. And the clean plane flew high in the sky, waving to Tim below.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to wear a tie. One day, he went to the park. The park was peaceful. Tim liked to play there. He saw a big tree and wanted to add his tie to it. So, he put his tie on the tree. But then, a bird came. The bird took Tim's tie. Tim was sad. He wanted his tie back. The bird flew away. Tim did not know what to do. He looked for the bird and his tie. Tim found the bird with his tie. But, the bird did not want to give it back. The bird wanted to add the tie to its nest. Tim thought about it. He decided to let the bird keep his tie. The bird was happy. Tim was happy too. Now, the bird had a pretty nest with a tie. And Tim made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a clean cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and glow in the sun. One day, he saw a quiz on the ground. Tom was very happy and wanted to play with the quiz. Tom took the quiz to his friend, a dog named Max. Max and Tom played with the quiz all day. They had lots of fun. The quiz made Tom and Max glow with joy. They laughed and played until the sun went down. But when it was time to go home, Tom and Max fought over the quiz. They both wanted to keep it. They pulled and pulled, but the quiz tore apart. Tom and Max were very sad. They did not have the quiz anymore, and they were not friends anymore. The end.
