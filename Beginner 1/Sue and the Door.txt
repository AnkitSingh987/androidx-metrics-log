Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Door

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to play. She had a big box of makeup. Sue thought it would be fun to act in a play. She put on the makeup to look scary. Her face was all green and her eyes were big and red. Sue went to her friend Tom's house. She knocked on the door. Tom opened the door and saw Sue's scary face. "Ah! Who are you?" Tom said. "It's me, Sue! I am acting in a play," Sue said. Tom was scared and did not want to play with Sue. Sue was sad. She wanted to play with Tom. She went home and took off her scary makeup. She went back to Tom's house and knocked on the door again. But Tom was still scared. He did not want to play with Sue anymore. Sue was very sad and went home alone.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big tree and thought it would be a nice place to take a nap. So, Max lay down under the tree and closed his eyes. While Max was napping, a pale cat named Lucy came by. Lucy saw Max and said, "Hi, dog! Why are you napping here?" Max woke up in shock and looked at Lucy. "I was just tired, so I decided to nap under this tree," Max replied. Lucy thought it was a good idea and decided to join Max. They both lay down under the tree and took a nice, long nap together. When they woke up, they played and became good friends. From that day on, Max and Lucy would meet under the tree to nap and play together.

Ben and Mia are twins. They like to play in the kitchen. One day, they see a big basket of eggs on the table. They want to touch them. "Look, eggs!" Ben says. "Let's take them." "No, Ben, Mom said no. They are for baking." Mia says. "They can break." But Ben does not listen. He grabs the basket and runs to the living room. Mia follows him. She is scared. "Ben, stop! Put them back!" Mia says. "Mom will be mad." But Ben does not stop. He thinks it is fun. He throws an egg in the air and catches it. He laughs. "See, Mia, it is easy. You try." Ben says. He gives an egg to Mia. Mia does not want to try. She holds the egg in her hand. It is heavy and cold. She feels something wet on her fingers. The egg is cracked. She drops it on the floor. It makes a mess. "Uh-oh." Mia says. Ben sees the mess. He is not happy. He throws another egg at Mia. It hits her on the head. It hurts. Mia screams. "Ben, why did you do that?" Mia screams. "You are mean!" Ben screams back. He throws more eggs at Mia. Mia throws eggs at Ben. They have a big fight. They make a bigger mess. Mom hears the screams. She comes to the living room. She sees the mess. She sees the twins. She is very mad. "Ben! Mia! What have you done?" Mom says. "You have wasted all the eggs! You have ruined the carpet! You have made a terrible mess! You are in big trouble!" Mom takes the twins to their room. She makes them clean up the mess. She makes them say sorry. She makes them go to bed. They have no dinner. They have no dessert. They have no toys. They have no fun. They cry. They wish they had listened to Mom. They wish they had not played with the eggs. They wish they had been good. They have a bad ending.

One day, a big dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a little cat named Luna. Luna was in a tree and could not get down. She was very scared. Max wanted to help Luna, so he tried to climb the tree too. But Max was too big and heavy, so he struggled to climb. Max had an idea. He found a long stick and tried to help Luna with it. But Luna was too scared to hold on to the stick. Max was serious about helping Luna. He did not want to give up. He thought and thought about how to help Luna stay safe. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny bird flew by and saw Luna and Max. The bird went to Luna and told her not to be scared. The bird helped Luna hold on to the stick. Max pulled the stick and Luna came down from the tree. Luna was happy and safe. Max and Luna became good friends, all thanks to the tiny bird.
