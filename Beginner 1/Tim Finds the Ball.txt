Category: Beginner
Title: Tim Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, there were two twins named Tim and Tom. They lived in a peaceful house by a big tree. Tim and Tom loved to play outside with their red ball. One day, their red ball got stuck in the big tree. Tim and Tom wanted to get it back, so they decided to climb the tree. They took turns climbing higher and higher. Finally, Tim reached the red ball and threw it down to Tom. They were so happy they got their ball back. They climbed down the tree and played with their ball all day long. The twins had a fun and peaceful day together.

One day, a pale boy named Tim went to the sea with his dad. They saw a big yacht. Tim wanted to go on the yacht and play. His dad said, "Okay, let's go!" On the yacht, they met a funny man. The man liked to joke a lot. He said, "Why did the chicken cross the sea? To get to the other yacht!" Tim and his dad laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. The funny man took off his hat and he was a chicken! The chicken said, "I am the chicken from the joke!" Tim and his dad were very surprised, but they all laughed and played together on the yacht.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat. The cat did not like to play or run. One day, the lazy cat saw a hook. The hook was shiny and interesting. The cat wanted to touch the hook. A bird saw the cat and the hook. The bird tried to explain to the cat. "No, no, cat! Do not touch the hook!" But the lazy cat did not listen. The cat wanted the hook. The lazy cat touched the hook. The hook hurt the cat's paw. The cat cried and cried. The bird felt sad. The cat learned to listen next time. But the cat's paw still hurt. This was a bad ending for the lazy cat.

Sara and Ben like to play in the tub. They fill it with warm water and bubbles. They have many toys to splash and float. Sara has a duck and Ben has a boat. One day, they play for a long time. They soak in the water and make waves. They laugh and sing. They do not hear their mom call them. "Time to get out, kids!" Mom says. She comes to the bathroom and sees them. "You have been in the tub too long. Look at your fingers and toes. They are all wrinkly like prunes!" Sara and Ben look at their hands and feet. They are surprised. They do not like to be prunes. They want to be smooth and soft. "Sorry, Mom," they say. "We were having fun. We did not hear you." Mom smiles and helps them out. She wraps them in towels and dries them. She gives them a hug and a kiss. "Next time, be more alert," she says. "I don't want you to turn into prunes. Now, let's go have some snack. How about some real prunes? They are sweet and juicy." Sara and Ben nod. They like prunes. They follow Mom to the kitchen. They are happy and hungry. They are not prunes anymore.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a large bird on a tree. The bird has a red head and a yellow beak. "Look, Tom, a large bird!" Lily says. "Wow, it is so pretty!" Tom says. They walk closer to the tree. They want to see the bird better. The bird sees them too. It opens its beak and talks. "Hello, hello, hello!" the bird says. Lily and Tom are surprised. They stop and look at the bird. "Did you hear that, Lily? The bird can talk!" Tom says. "Yes, I did, Tom. How can the bird talk?" Lily says. The bird talks again. "I can talk because I am a parrot. Parrots can talk. What are your names?" the bird says. Lily and Tom are happy. They tell the bird their names. They ask the bird its name. The bird says its name is Polly. They talk to Polly for a long time. They learn many things about parrots. They become friends with Polly. They have fun in the park.
