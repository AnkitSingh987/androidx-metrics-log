Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to sit by the big tree near his house. He would watch the birds fly and the leaves fall. One sunny day, as Tim sat under the tree, a little bird came and sat next to him. "Hello, Tim!" said the bird. "Do you want to race me?" Tim looked at the bird and smiled. "Yes, let's race!" he said. They started the race. Tim ran as fast as he could, but the bird was faster. The bird had so much speed! Tim laughed as the bird won the race. They sat under the tree together, happy and tired. They were friends from that day on.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play with cars and balls. One day, they find a big box in the garage. They think it is a good place to hide and play. "Let's go inside the box," Tom says. "We can pretend it is a spaceship." "OK," Sam says. "But be quiet. We don't want Mom to hear us." They crawl inside the box and close the flap. They make noises like rockets and stars. They have fun. But Mom does hear them. She hears the noises from the garage. She thinks there is a rat in the box. She is scared of rats. She gets a big knife and goes to the garage. She sees the box and thinks the rat is inside. She is angry. She wants to cut the box and kill the rat. She does not know Tom and Sam are inside. She lifts the knife and stabs the box. She hears a scream. She drops the knife and opens the box. She sees Tom and Sam. They are bleeding. They are hurt. She is shocked. She cries. "Mom, why did you cut us?" Tom asks. "We were just playing." "I'm sorry, I'm sorry," Mom says. "I thought you were a rat. I did not know you were so clever. I did not know you were in the box." She calls for help. She hugs Tom and Sam. But it is too late. They are too hurt. They die. Mom is sad. She is sorry. She wishes she did not cut them. She wishes she did not hear them. She wishes they were still playing.

One day, a parent wanted to create a nice room for their child. They wanted the room to be comfortable and fun. The parent worked hard to make the room perfect. They put a soft bed, a big toy box, and pretty pictures on the walls. The child was very happy. But one night, the child heard a loud noise in the room. They were scared and ran to their parent. The parent hugged the child and said, "Don't worry, I will look." The parent went to check the room, but they did not see anything. The next night, the loud noise came back. The parent had an idea. They went to the room and found a big, friendly bear. The bear was playing with the toys and making the noise. The parent said, "Bear, you are welcome to play, but please be quiet at night." The bear agreed, and the child and the bear became friends. The room was comfortable and fun for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom was baking a cake. She put the cake in the oven. Lily thought the oven was very important because it made the cake yummy. Lily said to her mom, "I hope the cake will be good!" Her mom smiled and said, "I hope so too, Lily. We will see when it comes out of the oven." They waited and waited. Finally, the cake was ready. Lily's mom took the cake out of the oven. It smelled so good! They were both very happy. The cake was perfect, and they knew that the oven was very important.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He loved to learn new things. One day, Tim saw his mom print a letter. He asked, "Mom, what are you doing?" "I am printing a letter for Grandma," she said. "Do you want to help?" "Yes, please!" Tim said with a big smile. They worked together to print the letter. Tim felt happy to help his mom. When they finished, Tim and his mom went to the park. They saw a little girl who felt sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I lost my toy." Tim and his mom helped the girl find her toy. They all became friends. The moral of the story is: helping others can make you and them happy.
