Category: Beginner
Title: Snow and the Car

One day, in the month of March, a white cat named Snow went for a walk. Snow liked to play in the park. She saw a small boy with a toy car. The boy was sad because his toy car did not move. Snow went to the boy and said, "I can change your toy car to make it move." The boy was happy and gave Snow the toy car. Snow tapped the toy car with her paw, and it changed into a real car! The boy was very surprised and said, "Wow, thank you, Snow!" They both played with the car in the park. Snow and the boy became good friends, and they had a lot of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a gloomy day. On this day, a little boy named Tim wanted to race. He asked his friend Sam to race with him. They both loved to run fast. "Let's race to the big tree," Tim said to Sam. "Ready, set, go!" They started to run as fast as their legs could go. Tim and Sam were having so much fun in their race. As they ran, the gloomy day started to change. The sun began to shine, and the sky turned blue. Tim and Sam were so happy. They raced and laughed all day long. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to print a picture of a big navy ship. He liked navy ships because they were big and strong. Tim asked his mom, "Can I print a navy ship, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can print one if you can find a fit paper." Tim looked and looked for a fit paper. But all the papers he found were too small or had something on them. Tim was sad because he could not find a fit paper. He went to his sister, Sue, and told her, "I can't find a fit paper to print my navy ship." Sue said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you find a fit paper." They looked together and finally found a big, clean paper. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, Sue!" They printed the navy ship, and Tim and Sue played with the picture all day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play near the sea. One day, he saw a soft, big ball on the sand. Tim was very happy and wanted to play with it. Tim picked up the ball and said, "I will kick the ball into the sea!" He kicked the ball very hard. The ball went high up in the air and then splashed into the sea. Tim ran to the water to get the ball back. As Tim got closer to the sea, a big wave came and took the ball away. Tim was very sad and cried, "Oh no! My ball is gone!" He looked at the sea, but the ball was gone forever.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a man sitting on a bench. He had a wallet in his pocket. Tom had an idea. "Let's steal his wallet!" he whispered to Lily. "We can buy candy and toys with his money." Lily was scared. "No, Tom, that is bad. We can't take what is not ours. He will be sad and angry." Tom did not listen. He was creative. He picked up a stick and threw it at a bird. The bird flew away, making a loud noise. The man looked up. Tom ran to the bench and grabbed the wallet. He ran back to Lily. "Come on, let's go!" he said. But Lily did not go. She stayed where she was. She saw the man looking for his wallet. He looked worried and sad. She felt sorry for him. "Tom, give it back!" she said. "It is not yours. It is his. He needs it." Tom did not want to give it back. He wanted to keep it. He opened the wallet and saw many papers and cards. He did not see any money. "This is no good!" he said. "There is no money here. Only papers and cards. What can we do with them?" Lily had an idea. She was creative too. She took the wallet from Tom and ran to the man. She gave him the wallet and said, "Sorry, mister. We found this on the ground. We thought it was yours." The man was happy and surprised. He thanked Lily and smiled. He gave her a candy and a toy. He did not see Tom hiding behind a tree. Lily ran back to Tom. She gave him the candy and the toy. She said, "See, Tom, it is better to be good than bad. You can't steal what is not yours. You can make people happy with your creativity." Tom felt ashamed and sorry. He learned his lesson. He said, "You are right, Lily. I was wrong. I am sorry. Thank you for being my friend. Can we share the candy and the toy?" Lily nodded and smiled. They hugged and played. They were happy. They were creative. They were good.
