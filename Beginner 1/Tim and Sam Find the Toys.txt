Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam Find the Toys

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, Tim finds a letter in his room. It is from his grandma. She says she loves him and sends him a sticker. Tim is happy. He shows the letter to Sam. Sam likes the sticker. He wants it for himself. He tries to take it from Tim. "Hey, that is mine!" Tim says. "Grandma gave it to me!" "No, I want it!" Sam says. He pulls the letter hard. The letter rips. The sticker falls off. Tim and Sam are sad. They both cry. "Roar!" They hear a loud noise. It is Tim's big brother, Tom. He is angry. He sees the ripped letter on the floor. "What did you do?" Tom says. "That was Tim's letter from grandma! You are bad!" He picks up the letter and the sticker. He gives them to Tim. He hugs him. "I'm sorry, Tim," he says. "I know grandma's letter is special. I can help you fix it." Tim smiles. He feels better. He looks at Sam. Sam looks hurt. He is sorry too. "Can I say sorry to Sam?" Tim asks Tom. "Of course," Tom says. "He is your friend. Friends say sorry when they make a mistake." Tim goes to Sam. He gives him half of the sticker. "Sorry, Sam," he says. "I was mad. You can have some of the sticker. Grandma loves you too." Sam smiles. He takes the sticker. He hugs Tim. "Sorry, Tim," he says. "I was greedy. You are a good friend. Thank you for sharing." Tim and Sam are happy. They play with the sticker and the letter. They make a card for grandma. They say they love her and they are sorry. They draw a big heart and a lion. They roar like lions. They laugh. Tom laughs too. He is proud of them. He helps them mail the card. Grandma will be happy too.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Emma. One day she decided to go on a nice walk in the woods. Everything seemed nice and she was happy to be outside. Emma saw a large vine and decided to climb up. Carefully she grabbed the vine with her hands and started to climb. It was a bit hard to hold the vine, but she kept going and eventually found a seat at the top. Emma was very excited. It was warm up there so she decided to stay for a while and enjoy the view. She looked around at all the trees, the birds and the clouds in the sky. Suddenly Emma heard her mom yell from below. "Emma! Come down now! It's time to go home!" Emma didn't want to leave so she yelled back. "Mom, can I stay on the vine a bit longer? Please!" Mom sweetly replied, "Ok, just be careful and come down soon." Emma smiled and stayed on the vine a little while longer. Finally she decided it was time to go and reluctantly climbed down. She was so happy she had the chance to visit the warm vine and see the beautiful view.

Tom and Anna were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a watch that their dad gave them. The watch could tell time, make sounds, and light up. Tom and Anna took turns wearing the watch and playing with it. One day, they went to the park with their mom. They saw a boy named Ben who was playing with a ball. Ben was nosy and wanted to see what Tom and Anna had. He came over and said, "What is that on your wrist? Can I see it?" Tom and Anna did not like Ben. He was rude and always took their things. They said, "No, this is our watch. It is special and we do not share it." Ben did not listen. He tried to grab the watch from Tom's wrist. Tom pulled away and said, "Stop it! You are mean and you cannot have our watch!" Ben got angry and pushed Tom to the ground. Tom cried and Anna ran to help him. Their mom saw what happened and came over. She scolded Ben and told him to apologize. She also told Tom and Anna to be careful with their watch and not to mention it to strangers. Tom and Anna learned a lesson that day. They learned that some people are nosy and want to take their things. They learned that they should not show off their watch and make others jealous. They learned that they should be kind and share with their friends, but not with bullies. They hugged their mom and thanked her for the watch. They decided to keep it safe and only play with it at home.
