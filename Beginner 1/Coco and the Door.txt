Title: Coco and the Door

Coco was feeling helpless. He had just moved to a new house and he was sad. He had come with his parents, but he didn't know anyone. His parents said he had to stay in the house until he learned the roads and was brave enough to explore. Suddenly Coco saw a pole standing in the front yard. He walked over to the pole thinking he could try and swing around it. But when Coco looked closer, he saw that the pole was too big and too high for him to get around. Even so, Coco was determined. "I'm going to get around this pole," he said confidently. His dad heard him and smiled. Then he told Coco he could make a new friend who could help make it easier. Coco was thrilled. He opened the door and was surprised to see a stuffed animal sheep waiting for him. They had been sent by one of his dad's friends. His dad said the toy had a special way of making it easier for him to get around the pole. And sure enough, Coco began to practice. He slowly increased his speed as he made laps around the pole. Sarah wanted to clean her horse. He was so cute with his brown hair and white spots. But her horse wouldn't stand still. He kept prancing around and shaking his head. Sarah tried to grab hold of him, but he kept moving away from her. She asked her mom to help and her mom said she would. Together, they were able to get ahold of the horse and Sarah began to brush him clean. He still fidgeted, but he stopped shaking his head. After many minutes of brushing, their horse was nice and shiny. "See, wasn't that easy?" said Sarah's mom. "Now your horse is all clean and he looks so cute." Sarah gave the horse a big hug and said, "Thank you for not running away mom! You are the best!"

Once upon a time, there was a sack. The sack was very tired. It sat near a wire all day. The sack wanted to play with the wire, but it was too tired to move. One day, a big wind came. It pushed the tired sack to the wire. The sack was happy. It played with the wire all day. They had lots of fun together. Then, the wind stopped. The sack was not tired anymore. It wanted to stay with the wire. But, the wind came back and took the wire away. The sack was sad. But soon, it found a new friend to play with. The new friend was a ball. The sack and the ball had lots of fun together.

Jen and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They liked to use the swings, the slide and the sandbox. They also liked to share a snack after playing. They always had a banana or a cookie. One day, Jen and Ben saw a big dog in the park. The dog was black and brown and had a loud bark. Jen and Ben were nervous. They did not like big dogs. They did not know if the dog was friendly or mean. "Let's go to the swings," Jen said. "Maybe the dog will not see us there." "OK," Ben said. "But what if the dog comes to the swings?" "Then we can use the slide to run away," Jen said. They ran to the swings and started to swing. They tried to have fun, but they kept looking at the dog. The dog was walking around the park, sniffing and barking. Sometimes, the dog looked at Jen and Ben. Jen and Ben swung faster and faster. They hoped the dog would leave soon. They wanted to have their snack and go home. But the dog did not leave. The dog came closer and closer to the swings. Jen and Ben were very nervous. They stopped swinging and held each other. "Go away, dog!" Jen shouted. "We don't want to play with you!" The dog did not listen. The dog jumped on the swings and started to lick Jen and Ben. Jen and Ben screamed. They were scared. They did not like the dog's tongue. Then, they heard a voice. "Rex, come here, boy!" the voice said. "Sorry, kids, he's just friendly. He likes to play." Jen and Ben looked up and saw a man. The man was the dog's owner. He had a leash and a bag of treats. He smiled and waved at Jen and Ben. "Hi, I'm Tom," the man said. "This is Rex. He's a good dog, but he's very curious. He likes to meet new people and animals. He doesn't mean to scare you. He just wants to say hello." Jen and Ben looked at the man and the dog. They saw that the dog was wagging his tail and panting. He did not look mean. He looked happy. "Hi, I'm Jen," Jen said. "This is Ben. We like to play in the park. We were nervous of your dog. We thought he was mean." "No, he's not mean," Tom said. "He's very gentle and friendly. He likes to play, too. Do you want to pet him?" Jen and Ben nodded. They reached out and touched the dog's fur. It was soft and warm. The dog licked their hands and nuzzled their faces. Jen and Ben giggled. They liked the dog's kisses. "See, he's nice," Tom said. "Do you want to give him a treat?" Jen and Ben nodded again. Tom gave them each a treat from his bag. They held the treat in their hands and offered it to the dog. The dog took the treat gently and ate it. He wagged his tail and barked. "Good boy, Rex," Tom said. "He likes you. You're his new friends." Jen and Ben smiled. They liked the dog, too. They were not nervous anymore. They were happy. "Can we play with him?" Jen asked. "Sure, you can," Tom said. "But only if you want to. And only if you share your snack with him. He loves bananas and cookies." Jen and Ben laughed. They did not mind sharing their snack with the dog. They had enough for everyone. They took out their snack from their backpacks and gave some to the dog. The dog ate the snack and licked his lips. He was happy, too. They played with the dog for a while. They threw a ball and he fetched it. They ran and he chased them. They hugged and he cuddled them. They had fun. They made a new friend in the park. They were not nervous anymore. They were glad.
