Category: Beginner
Title: The Rich Man and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a rich man. The rich man had a big house and many toys. One day, the rich man saw a small boy playing with a ball. The rich man wanted to be nice, so he put a gift in his palm. The small boy looked at the gift and said, "Thank you, rich man!" The rich man smiled and walked away. The small boy opened the gift and found a toy car. He was very happy and played with the toy car all day. One day, the rich man lost all his toys. He was very sad. The small boy saw the rich man and felt bad for him. He went to the rich man and put the toy car in his palm. The rich man was happy and learned that sharing is good. They both played with the toy car and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little black dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his rag toy. He would run and jump and snap the rag with his teeth. One day, Spot saw a big tree. He wanted to play with his rag under the tree. He put the rag down and started to snap at it. He was having so much fun. But then, the wind came and took the rag up into the tree. Spot was sad. He looked up and saw his rag stuck in the tree. He barked and jumped, but he could not get it down. A kind squirrel saw Spot and wanted to help. The squirrel climbed the tree and got the rag for Spot. Spot was so happy to have his rag back. He wagged his tail and thanked the squirrel. From that day on, Spot and the squirrel became best friends. They played with the rag under the big tree every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max lived in a big house. They liked to play hide and seek together. Tom was very good at hiding, but Max was not. Max was too big to fit in small places. One day, they started a new game. Tom ran to hide while Max closed his eyes to count. Tom found a big box and jumped inside. It was a perfect fit! Max started to look for Tom. He looked under the bed, behind the chair, and in the closet, but he couldn't find Tom. Max was sad because he couldn't find his friend. He sat down by the big box and heard a soft meow. Max was surprised! He looked inside the box and found Tom. Tom laughed and said, "You found me! Now it's your turn to hide." Max was happy because he found Tom in a place he didn't expect. They played hide and seek all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to improve his muscle to be mighty and strong. Every day, he would run, jump, and play with his friends. One sunny day, while Tim was playing, a big wind came and blew his ball high up into a tree. Tim tried to reach it, but he was not tall enough. He wished he had mighty muscles to help him get his ball back. Just then, a little bird flew down from the tree. The bird had tiny wings, but it was very strong. It picked up Tim's ball with its beak and dropped it on the ground. Tim was so happy and surprised! He learned that you don't always need mighty muscles to help others. Sometimes, even the smallest friends can do big things.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He had no hair on his head. He lived in a small house. The bald man had a pet bird. The bird was blue and small. The bald man liked his bird a lot. One day, the bird flew away. The bald man was very sad. He looked for the bird everywhere. He asked his friends if they saw the bird. No one saw the bird. The bald man was very sad. The bald man found the bird in a tree. The bird was on a branch. The bald man tried to get the bird. He pinched the bird's neck. The bird did not like that. The bird bit the bald man's hand. The bald man fell down. He did not get the bird. The bald man was very sad.
