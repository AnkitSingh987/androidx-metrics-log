Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Park

One day, a big dog named Max was walking down the street. He saw a sign that said, "No Dogs Allowed." Max was upset. He did not want to leave. He sat down and started to cry. A little girl named Amy saw Max crying. She walked over to him and said, "Why are you upset, Max?" Max told her about the sign. Amy had an idea. She said, "Let's lift the sign and take it away!" Max and Amy worked together to lift the sign. They took it far away and hid it behind a big tree. Then they went back to play in the park. They were both happy and had a lot of fun. But then, a man came running and said, "Have you seen the sign? It was for a dog show today!" Max and Amy were surprised. They ran back to the tree, lifted the sign, and put it back in its place. It turns out, the sign was there to invite dogs to a fun dog show. Max joined the show and won a prize. Amy was very proud of her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben was a good bear who lived in a small house in the woods. He had many friends, like the birds, the squirrels, and the rabbits. Ben loved his friends very much. One day, a sneaky fox came to the woods. The fox saw Ben's house and wanted to steal something from it. The fox went inside and took Ben's favorite toy. When Ben came home, he saw his toy was gone. Ben was sad and asked his friends, "Who took my toy?" The birds, the squirrels, and the rabbits knew it was the sneaky fox. They all said, "The fox took your toy, Ben!" Ben was very upset, but his friends had a plan. They went to the fox's house and asked for the toy back. The fox felt bad for stealing and gave the toy back to Ben. Ben was so happy to have his toy back. He said, "Thank you, friends! I love you all!" They all hugged and went back to Ben's house. From that day on, the sneaky fox learned not to steal and became a good friend to Ben and his friends. Everyone in the woods lived happily ever after.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play outside in the sun. The sun is bright and warm. It shines on their faces and makes them happy. One day, they see a big cow in the field. The cow is brown and has spots. It is eating grass. Tom and Sam want to pet the cow. They run to the fence and call the cow. "Hello, cow! Come here, cow! We want to say hi!" they shout. But the cow does not come. It is too lazy. It keeps eating grass and does not look at them. Tom and Sam are sad. They think the cow is rude. "Maybe the cow is hungry," Tom says. "Maybe it wants some beef." Sam thinks that is a silly idea. He knows that cows do not eat beef. Beef is meat. Cows are meat. Cows do not eat themselves. "No, Tom. Cows do not eat beef. That is yucky. Cows eat grass. That is what they like," Sam says. Tom does not believe him. He thinks that beef is yummy. He likes to eat beef with his mom and dad. He thinks that the cow would like it too. He has an idea. He runs to the house and gets a piece of beef from the fridge. He brings it back to the fence. He holds it out to the cow. "Look, cow! I have beef for you. It is tasty. Try it, cow. You will like it," he says. But the cow does not try it. It does not like it. It sniffs the beef and makes a loud noise. It sounds like "Moo!" It sounds like "No!" The cow turns away from Tom and walks to the other side of the field. It does not want to be near him. It does not want his beef. Tom is confused. He does not understand why the cow does not like his beef. He thinks that the cow is weird. Sam laughs. He thinks that Tom is funny. He knows that the cow is smart. He knows that the cow knows what is good for it. He hugs Tom and says, "Don't worry, Tom. The cow is not mad at you. The cow is just different from you. The cow likes grass. You like beef. That is okay. We can still be friends with the cow." Tom smiles. He thinks that Sam is right. He thinks that the cow is not so bad. He thinks that the cow is just lazy. He says, "Okay, Sam. I understand. The cow is lazy. But it is still cute. Maybe next time, we can bring it some grass. Maybe then, it will come and say hi." Sam nods. He thinks that is a good idea. He thinks that the cow will like that. He thinks that the sun will shine on them and make them happy.
