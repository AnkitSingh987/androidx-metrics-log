Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big ice cream cone. She was very happy. But it was a hot day, and her ice cream started to melt. Sue saw a rabbit hopping by. The rabbit was quick and frightened. "Why are you scared?" Sue asked the rabbit. "I am scared of the big dog," the rabbit said. Sue wanted to help the rabbit. She had an idea. She threw her melting ice cream on the ground. The big dog saw the ice cream and stopped to eat it. The rabbit was safe and thanked Sue. They became good friends and played together every day.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with dolls and cars. One day, they find a big box in the park. The box is light and has many colors. Anna and Ben want to see what is inside the box. "Let's open it!" Anna says. She pulls the box with her hands. Ben helps her. They are very curious. "Maybe there are toys or candy in the box!" Ben says. He pushes the box with his shoulder. The box makes a loud noise. Anna and Ben are scared. "Stop! Don't open it!" a voice says. Anna and Ben look up. They see a man running towards them. The man is angry and has a stick in his hand. "That is my box! You have to pay for it!" the man says. He reaches the box and hits it with his stick. The box breaks and many sparks fly out. The box is not a toy. It is a bomb. Anna and Ben scream and run away. But they are too late. The bomb explodes and hurts them. They fall on the ground and cry. The man runs away too. He is a bad man. He wanted to hurt people with his bomb. Anna and Ben are very sad and in pain. They wish they never saw the box. They wish they listened to the voice. They wish they had a good ending.

Sara and Tom are twins. They like to play outside with their dog, Spot. Spot is a big, black dog with a white spot on his chest. He is adorable and friendly. One day, Sara and Tom find a big puddle of mud in the yard. They think it is fun to jump and splash in the mud. Spot thinks so too. He runs and jumps with them. Soon, they are all very dirty and muddy. "Look at us!" Sara says. "We are brown like chocolate!" "Yes, we are!" Tom says. "But Mom will not like it. She will say we need a wash." They hear Mom calling them from the house. "Sara! Tom! Spot! Come inside! It is time for lunch!" They run to the house. Mom sees them and gasps. "Oh, my! What have you done? You are all covered in mud! You need a wash right now!" She takes them to the bathroom. She fills the tub with warm water and soap. She puts Spot in first. He does not like it. He shakes and splashes. He makes a big mess. Sara and Tom laugh. They think it is funny. "Spot, stop it!" Mom says. "You need to be clean!" She washes Spot with a sponge. She rinses him with a shower. She dries him with a towel. He looks adorable again. He wags his tail and licks Mom's face. "Good boy, Spot!" Mom says. "Now it is your turn, Sara and Tom!" She puts Sara and Tom in the tub. They like it. They play with the bubbles and the toys. They wash each other's hair and faces. They rinse with the shower. They dry with the towels. They look adorable again. They hug Mom and say thank you. "Good girl, Sara! Good boy, Tom!" Mom says. "Now you are all clean and ready for lunch!" She takes them to the kitchen. She makes them sandwiches and juice. They eat and drink. They are happy and full. They say, "We love you, Mom!" Mom says, "I love you too, Sara and Tom! And Spot!" They all smile and cuddle. They are a happy family.
