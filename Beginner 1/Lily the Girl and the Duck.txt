Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Duck

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a clean, blue pond with ducks swimming in it. Lily wanted to play with the ducks, so she went closer to the water. Lily saw a big, yellow duck stuck in some mud. The duck said, "Help me, please!" Lily wanted to help the duck, so she tried to pull it out. She pulled and pulled, but the duck was still stuck. Then, Lily had an idea. She found a long stick and used it to push the mud away from the duck. With one big push, Lily was able to release the duck from the mud. The duck was so happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" Lily and the duck played together in the clean pond all day. The duck was very thankful to have a new friend like Lily. And Lily was happy she could help the duck and make a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very sad. He did not want to play with his toys. He felt miserable. His mom asked him, "Why are you so sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to write to the stars, but I don't know how." That night, Tim's mom had an idea. She got a big piece of paper and some crayons. She told Tim, "Let's write a letter to the stars together." Tim was happy to try. They wrote a nice letter and put it on the window. The next morning, Tim found a surprise. There was a new letter on the window! It was from the stars! The stars wrote, "Thank you, Tim. We are happy to be your friends!" Tim was not miserable anymore. He learned that sometimes, the stars can write back.

Lily and Ben like to play with puzzles. They have many puzzles with animals, cars, and flowers. They like to make the pictures with the pieces. They are very good at puzzles. One day, they find a new puzzle in the toy box. It is very big and bright. It has many colors and shapes. Lily and Ben want to try it. They open the box and see many pieces. They start to put them together on the floor. "Look, Ben, I found a piece with a star!" Lily says. She holds up the piece and smiles. "Wow, that's a nice piece, Lily. Where does it go?" Ben asks. He looks at the box and tries to find the star. "I don't know. Maybe here?" Lily says. She puts the piece next to another piece. But they don't fit. She tries another place. But they don't fit either. "Maybe we need more pieces, Lily. Let's look for more." Ben says. He reaches for the box and pulls out more pieces. He spreads them on the floor. But as he does, he steps on a piece. He feels a sharp pain in his foot. He cries out loud. "Ow, ow, ow! That hurts!" Ben says. He drops the pieces and holds his foot. Lily hears him and runs to him. She sees the piece stuck in his foot. She feels sorry for him. "Oh, Ben, I'm sorry. Are you okay?" Lily says. She hugs him and tries to help him. "It's okay, Lily. It's just a piece. It's not your fault." Ben says. He tries to smile. He pulls out the piece and throws it away. "Maybe we should play with another puzzle, Ben. This one is too hard and too bright. It hurts our eyes and our feet." Lily says. She picks up the pieces and puts them back in the box. "Maybe you're right, Lily. Let's play with the animal puzzle. It's more fun and more soft." Ben says. He gets up and limps to the toy box. They take out the animal puzzle and start to make it. They forget about the bright puzzle. They are happy again.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a toy for Lily's friend, Sam. Lily was excited to pick the perfect toy. The store was very noisy with lots of people talking and laughing. Lily saw a toy with a point on it. It looked fun, but her mom said, "No, that is not safe for Sam." So they kept looking. Then, they found a big, soft ball. Lily thought it was great, so they bought it for Sam. When they got to Sam's house, they gave him the ball. Sam was so happy, but then something unexpected happened. Sam's dog, Max, jumped up and took the ball! He thought it was for him. Everyone laughed because it was a funny twist. Sam and Max played with the ball together, and they all had a great day.
