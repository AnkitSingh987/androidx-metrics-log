Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Sam and the Cat

Ben and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a big box. They want to make a fort. "Let's supply our fort with things," Ben says. "We can find sticks and rocks and leaves." "Good idea," Sam says. "We can also use our suits. They are strong and colorful." They run to their bikes. They have suits in their backpacks. They are superhero suits. Ben has a red suit. Sam has a blue suit. They put on their suits and helmets. They ride their bikes to the park. They find a good spot for their fort. They use the box as a base. They supply the box with sticks and rocks and leaves. They make a door and a window. They put their bikes next to the fort. They go inside the fort. They pretend they are superheroes. They have fun. "Look, Ben," Sam says. "There is a cat in the tree. It is scared. We can help it." "Let's go, Sam," Ben says. "We are strong and brave. We can save the cat." They run to the tree. They climb the tree. They reach the cat. They pet the cat. They bring the cat down. The cat is happy. It purrs. "Good job, Ben and Sam," a lady says. She is the cat's owner. She thanks them. She gives them cookies. Ben and Sam are happy. They share the cookies with the cat. They go back to their fort. They play some more. They are good friends. They are good superheroes.

Once upon a time, there was a wise cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Lucy. They loved to play with a long cord that Lucy found in a box. One sunny day, Lucy and Tom went to the park to play. They brought the cord with them. They played a game where Tom would serve the cord like a ball, and Lucy would try to catch it. They laughed and played for hours. As the sun began to set, Lucy and Tom walked back to their small house. They were happy and tired from their fun day at the park. They knew that they would always have fun together with their cord and their wise cat Tom.

Once upon a time, there was a tall giraffe named Gerry. Gerry was so tall that he could touch the ceiling of his house with his head. He had a lot of friends who liked to play with him. One day, Gerry and his friends were playing a game. They had to hold a big, round ball and pass it to each other. The friends took turns holding the ball and they were having a lot of fun. But then, the ball went too high and got stuck on the ceiling! All of Gerry's friends tried to jump and reach it, but they were not tall enough. Gerry, with his long neck, reached up and held the ball with his mouth. He saved the day and everyone cheered for him. They continued to play and had a great day together.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But sometimes they fight over who gets what. One day, they both want the same puzzle. They pull and tug and shout. "Mine!" Anna says. "No, mine!" Ben says. Mom hears them and comes to their room. She is not happy. She takes the puzzle and puts it on the shelf. "Stop fighting, Anna and Ben. You need to share and be humble. You have many other toys and books. Why don't you relax and read a story together?" Mom says. Anna and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry. They don't want to make Mom sad. They nod and say sorry. "Okay, Mom. We will relax and read a story. We will be humble and share." Anna says. "Me too, Mom. We will be good." Ben says. Mom smiles and hugs them. She gives them a book and a will. A will is a plant that grows in water. It has long green leaves and small white flowers. Mom says it is a gift for them. "This is a will. It is a special plant. It can grow in any place and any weather. It is humble and strong. It can teach you to be humble and strong too. Take good care of it and it will grow with you." Mom says. Anna and Ben thank Mom and take the will. They put it in a vase with water and place it on their table. They sit down and open the book. They relax and read the story. They look at the will and smile. They are happy and humble. They don't fight anymore.
