Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Amy and the Stack

Tom and Amy were playing with blocks. They liked to make big stacks of blocks and knock them down. Tom had a lot of red blocks and Amy had a lot of blue blocks. They decided to make a red stack and a blue stack and see which one was taller. They worked hard and made their stacks as high as they could. Tom's red stack was a little bit taller than Amy's blue stack. He was very happy and said, "Look, my stack is the best! It is taller than yours!" Amy was sad and angry. She did not like that Tom's stack was taller. She wanted her stack to be the best. She pushed Tom's red stack and made it fall down. The blocks crashed and made a loud noise. Tom was shocked and mad. He liked his red stack and did not want Amy to break it. He shouted, "Hey, that's not fair! You broke my stack! You are a meanie!" He picked up a red block and threw it at Amy. It hit her on the head and hurt her. She started to weep and cry. She said, "Ow, ow, ow! That hurt! You are a meanie too!" They both wept and cried and did not want to play with each other anymore. They sat on the floor and looked at the mess of blocks. They felt sorry and lonely. They wished they had not been mean to each other. They wished they had shared their blocks and made a big stack together. They wished they were still friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish who loved to swim. The fish had a best friend, a big crab. They liked to play in the water all day. One day, they saw a scary shark. The shark wanted to play with them, but the fish and crab were scared. The fish thought of a plan. He told the crab to reverse and go back to their home. The fish swam very fast to the scary shark. The shark was surprised and followed the little fish. The fish swam and swam, leading the shark far away from their home. But, the shark was too fast for the little fish. The shark caught the fish and said, "I just wanted to be your friend." The fish was sad because he did not want to be friends with the scary shark. The crab was sad too, because he lost his best friend. And they never played together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to go on walks with his dog, Spot. One day, they went for a walk in the park. As they walked, they saw many pretty things. They saw flowers, trees, and a big pond with ducks. Then, Tim and Spot discovered something weird. It was a big, round rock with a hole in it. Tim thought it was a fun thing to look at. They went closer to the rock and looked through the hole. The view was amazing! They could see the whole park and all the animals playing. Tim and Spot played near the weird rock all day. They had so much fun discovering new things and looking at the beautiful view. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye to the rock and left the park. Tim couldn't wait to come back and play again.

Once upon a time, there was a big sun. The sun would rise every day to say hello to the world. One day, the sun did not rise. The world was dark and sad. People did not know what to do. They could not see to make dinner or play. Everyone was worried. A little girl named Mia knew something was wrong. She wanted to help the big sun rise again. Mia thought and thought. She remembered her mom saying, "When you are sad, a hug can make you feel better." Mia had an idea. Mia climbed the tallest hill in her town. She reached out her arms and gave the big sun a big hug. The sun felt the love from Mia's hug, and it began to rise again. The world was bright and happy once more. Everyone could make dinner and play again. They all thanked Mia for helping the big sun rise.
