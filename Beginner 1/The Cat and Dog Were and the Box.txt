Title: The Cat and Dog Were and the Box

One day, a cat and a dog were in a place. The cat was very glad. It was a sunny day. The cat said, "Dog, let's play!" The dog said, "Yes, cat, let's play!" They played and played. Then, the sky became dark. Rain started to pour. The cat and dog were wet. They were not glad anymore. They needed to find a dry place. They saw a big box. The cat said, "Dog, let's go in the box!" Inside the box, it was dry. They were glad again. Then, they heard a loud noise. The box started to move! They were in a car. The cat and dog were scared, but they held each other tight. They knew they would be okay because they had each other.

Once upon a time, there was a sad little girl named Amy. She had no friends to play with. One day, she saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and wanted to get down. Amy said, "Don't worry, cat. I will help you." She tried to climb the tree, but she could not manage. So, she thought of a new style to save the cat. She found a long stick and used it to help the cat down from the tree. The cat was very happy and said, "Thank you, Amy!" The two became good friends and played together every day. Amy was not sad anymore. The moral of the story is: helping others can make you happy too.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big truck with a cage. Inside the cage, there is a lion. The lion looks sad. "Where is the lion going?" Sara asks. The man who drives the truck says, "He is going to the zoo. He came from the jungle, but he was sick. We have to weigh him and check him." "Can we see him weigh?" Tom asks. The man nods. He stops the truck and opens the cage. He puts a big scale on the ground. He gives the lion some meat. The lion walks out of the cage and eats the meat. He steps on the scale. "Wow, he is very heavy!" Sara says. The man looks at the number on the scale. He writes it on a paper. He says, "He is fine. He is not sick anymore. He just needs some rest and friends. He will be happy at the zoo." "Can we visit him at the zoo?" Tom asks. The man smiles. He says, "Sure, you can. He will like to see you. He is a friendly lion. But be careful, he is still a wild animal. Don't touch him or go too close to him." Sara and Tom nod. They say, "Thank you, mister. Bye, lion. We hope you like the zoo." The lion roars. He sounds happy. He goes back to the cage. The man closes the cage and drives away. Sara and Tom wave to the lion. They go back to the park. They play some more. They are happy too.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a noisy woodcutter named Tom. He had a big, shiny axe. Every day, Tom would go to the forest and chop wood. One day, while Tom was chopping wood, he heard a voice. It was a little bird. The bird asked, "Why are you so noisy, Tom?" Tom replied, "I need to chop wood for my family." The bird listened and then flew away. The next day, Tom found a big, strong tree. He knew it would give lots of wood. But when he started to chop it, the little bird came back. The bird said, "Please don't chop this tree, Tom. My family lives here." Tom remembered the bird's help and decided not to chop the tree. Instead, he found another tree to chop. The bird was happy, and Tom had wood for his family.

One day, a tall tree named Tom was standing in the park. Tom was a very proud tree. He had big arms that reached up to the sky. He loved to stand tall and strong. One sunny day, a little bird named Bob came to sit on Tom's arm. Bob said, "Hi Tom, I love to sit on your arm. You are so tall and strong." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Bob. I am very proud of my big arms." Then, something unexpected happened. A strong wind came and blew Bob off Tom's arm. Tom was sad but he knew what to do. He bent his arm down to the ground, so Bob could climb back up. Bob was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom. You are not only tall and strong, but also very kind." From that day on, Tom and Bob were the best friends. They both learned that being kind was even better than being tall and strong. And they lived happily in the park, proud of their friendship.
