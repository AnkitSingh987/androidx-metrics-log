Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Banana

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a very restless boy who was always curious and wanted to explore. One day, Tom noticed a ripe banana lying on the floor. He went up to it and looked at it with wide eyes. Tom reached out to touch the banana and as he did, the banana started to stretch! Tom was surprised and could not believe what he was seeing, so he jumped back a few steps. Tom was now feeling quite brave and wanted to know why the banana was stretching. He called out to his mom and asked her why the banana was doing that. His mom smiled and said, "The banana is stretching because it's ready to be eaten. It's trying to tell you that it's ripe and sweet." Tom was now excited and reached out his hand to pick the banana. He smiled and took a big bite out of it. Tom was happy and enjoyed the sweet taste of the banana. From that day on, he never stopped stretching his imagination and exploring the wonderful world around him.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bird named Bluey. Bluey lived in a big tree near a small house. In the house, lived a nice girl named Lily. One day, Lily got some mail. She was very happy. She wanted to say "please" and "thank you" to the mailman, but he was gone. So, Lily had an idea. She asked Bluey to help her. Bluey said, "Please, give me the mail." Lily gave the mail to Bluey. Bluey flew to the mailman and said, "Thank you for the mail!" The mailman smiled and said, "You're welcome, pretty bird!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. They are excited. They run to the slide and wait for their turn. "Can I go first?" Sara asks. "OK, you can go first. I will go after you," Ben says. Sara climbs up the ladder. She sits on the slide. She smiles. She slides down fast. She feels happy. She lands on the ground. She laughs. "Wow, that was fun!" Sara says. Ben climbs up the ladder. He sits on the slide. He smiles. He slides down fast. He feels happy. He lands on the ground. He laughs. "That was fun!" Ben says. They hug each other. They hear a tap. They look around. They see a dog. The dog is brown and fluffy. The dog wags its tail. The dog taps the ground with its paw. "Hello, dog!" Sara says. "Hello, dog!" Ben says. The dog barks. The dog runs to them. The dog licks their faces. They giggle. They pet the dog. "Can we play with the dog?" Sara asks. "Let's ask the owner," Ben says. They see a lady. The lady is holding a leash. The lady smiles. "Hi, kids. This is my dog. His name is Spot. Do you want to play with him?" the lady asks. "Yes, please!" Sara and Ben say. "OK, but be gentle. He likes to fetch and chase," the lady says. She gives them a ball. They throw the ball. Spot runs after the ball. He brings it back. They throw it again. Spot runs again. He brings it back. They play with Spot. They have fun. They smile.

Sara was a popular girl in her class. She had many friends who liked to play with her. She also liked to pack her toys in her bag and take them to school. She had a doll, a car, a ball and a book. One day, she was playing with her toys in the playground. She saw a new boy sitting alone on a bench. He looked sad and lonely. Sara wanted to be nice to him, so she went to him and said, "Hi, I'm Sara. Do you want to play with me?" The boy looked at her and said, "No, go away. I don't like you. You have too many toys. You are greedy and selfish." Sara felt hurt and angry. She said, "No, I'm not. I like to share my toys with my friends. You are mean and rude." The boy got up and pushed Sara. Sara fell down and hit her head on a rock. She started to cry and bleed. The other kids saw what happened and ran to help her. They called the teacher and the nurse. Soon, an ambulance came to take Sara to the hospital. The boy watched as Sara was carried away on a stretcher. He felt sorry and scared. He wished he had not pushed her. He wished he had been nice to her. He wished he had a friend like Sara. But it was too late. Sara was gone. And he was alone.
