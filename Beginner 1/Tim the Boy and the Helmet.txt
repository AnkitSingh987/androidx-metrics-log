Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Helmet

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to bounce. He would bounce on his bed, bounce on the couch, and bounce on his big red ball. One day, Tim found a helmet. It was a big, shiny helmet that he wore when he bounced. One sunny day, Tim went outside to bounce on his big red ball. He wore his helmet to keep his head safe. As he was bouncing, he saw his friend, Sue. Sue saw Tim's helmet and said, "Wow, Tim! I like your helmet. Can I try it on?" Tim smiled and said, "Sure, Sue! You can wear it while you bounce with me." So, Sue put on the helmet and they both bounced on the big red ball. They bounced all day long, laughing and having fun. They were so happy to bounce and play together with the big, shiny helmet. At the end of the day, Tim and Sue were tired from all the bouncing. They sat down on the grass and looked at the helmet. Tim said, "I'm glad we got to bounce together today, Sue. It was so much fun!" Sue agreed and said, "Yes, Tim! And your helmet made it even better. Thank you for sharing it with me." They smiled at each other and knew they would always remember their fun day of bouncing with the big, shiny helmet.

Lily was a princess who liked to play with her dolls. She had a big dollhouse with many rooms and furniture. She also had a pink dress and a crown that she wore when she pretended to be a queen. One day, she invited her friend Max to play with her. Max was a boy who liked to play with cars and trucks. He brought his toy car with him and showed it to Lily. "Look, this is my car. It can go very fast and make loud noises. Do you want to try it?" he asked. Lily nodded and took the car from Max. She pushed it on the floor and made it zoom around the dollhouse. She liked the car, but she also wanted to show Max her dolls. "Look, these are my dolls. They are very pretty and have nice clothes. Do you want to play with them?" she asked. Max shook his head and took the car back from Lily. He did not like the dolls, he thought they were boring and silly. He wanted to play with his car more. He pushed it on the floor and made it crash into the dollhouse. He laughed and said, "This is fun. The car can break the dollhouse. Watch this!" Lily did not like what Max was doing. He was ruining her dollhouse and her dolls. She felt angry and sad. She tried to stop Max, but he was stronger than her. She pulled his arm and said, "Stop it, Max. You are being mean. This is my dollhouse and my dolls. You have to be nice to them. Please, give me back my car." Max did not listen to Lily. He pulled his arm away from her and said, "No, this is my car. You can't have it. I don't want to play with your dolls. They are stupid. I want to play with my car. Go away, Lily." Lily felt hurt and cried. She ran to her room and slammed the door. She hugged her dolls and said, "I'm sorry, dolls. Max is a bad boy. He doesn't know how to play. He doesn't want to be my friend. I don't like him anymore." Max heard Lily crying and felt sorry. He realized he was being rude and mean. He liked Lily, she was his friend. He wanted to make her happy. He knocked on her door and said, "Lily, I'm sorry. I was wrong. I shouldn't have broken your dollhouse and your dolls. I should have played with them. Can you forgive me? Can we be friends again?" Lily opened the door and saw Max holding the car and a doll. He smiled and said, "Here, this is for you. You can have my car and I can have your doll. We can share and play together. We can be happy." Lily smiled and took the car and the doll. She hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max. I forgive you. You are a good boy. You are my friend. We can play together. We can be happy." They hugged and went back to the dollhouse. They fixed the broken parts and put the furniture back. They played with the car and the dolls. They had fun and laughed. They were happy.
