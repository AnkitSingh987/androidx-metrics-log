Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Jump

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to improve her jump. She saw a post in the park and thought it would be fun to jump over it. Sue tried very hard, but she could not jump over the post. Sue's friend, Tim, saw her trying to jump. He said, "Why don't we work together to improve our jumps?" They tried to jump over the post together, but it was still too hard. Just when they were about to give up, a big wind blew the post away. Sue and Tim looked at each other and laughed. They realized that they didn't need the post to improve their jumps. They just needed to have fun and try their best.

Once upon a time, there was a big lake. Many people liked the lake. It was a popular place to play. In the lake, there was a magic fish. The fish could heal anyone who was hurt. One day, a little boy named Tim hurt his knee. He was sad and cried. His friend, Sue, said, "Don't cry, Tim! Let's go to the lake. The magic fish can help you." Tim and Sue went to the lake. They saw the magic fish. Tim asked, "Can you heal my knee, please?" The fish said, "Yes, I can help you." The fish touched Tim's knee with its tail. Tim's knee felt better. He was happy and said, "Thank you, magic fish!" Tim and Sue played by the lake all day. They were happy and had fun.

Once upon a time there was a man named Jim. He was walking down the road when he saw a naughty dog playing in the mud. The dog's name was Max and he was a bit rude. Jim said to Max, "Hey, why don't you be nice and have peace?" But Max didn't seem to listen. He kept playing and rolling around in the mud. Jim saw this and said "Max, don't twist! Play nicely" But once again, Max wouldn't listen. Jim was getting annoyed so he decided to try a different approach. He bent down and said to Max, "If you want to play, why don't you try chasing this stick? That way you can have fun and still be peaceful". Max stopped playing in the mud and happily chased the stick. At last, peace was back in the neighbourhood. Jim smiled and walked away.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a green shield on the ground. "Wow!" Tim said, "A green shield! I want to pick it up." So, he picked up the shield and kept walking. As he walked, he met a girl named Sue. "Look, Sue! I found a green shield!" Tim said. Sue looked at the shield and said, "That's cool, Tim! Let's play with it." So, they played with the shield, pretending to be knights. Then, something unexpected happened. The green shield started to talk! It said, "Thank you for finding me, Tim and Sue! I am a magic shield. I can help you be brave and strong." Tim and Sue were surprised but happy. They knew they had found a new friend in the magic green shield.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little girl named Opal. She was three years old and her favourite thing in the world was to go for a walk with her family. On this particular day, Opal had decided to take her favourite teddy, Tubby, with her. Unfortunately, while they were out walking, Opal managed to lose her beloved teddy! She looked high and low, but Tubby was nowhere to be seen. She started to get sad and began to cry. "Don't worry, Opal," said her mum, gently smoothing her hair. "Let's go and look for him together." They searched for what felt like hours, but eventually Tubby was found tucked away behind some celery in the vegetable section of the grocery store! Opal was filled with joy. She gave Tubby a huge hug, and thanked her mum for finding him. She learnt an important lesson that day: to always keep hold of her beloved teddy, no matter what!

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big band in the park. The band has drums, trumpets, and flags. The band makes loud music. Tom and Sue want to join the band. "Can we play with you?" Tom asks the band leader. "No, you are too small. This is a march. You need to know how to march and play music. Go away!" the band leader says. Tom and Sue are sad and angry. They do not like the band leader. He is mean. They complain to each other. "That is not fair. We want to march and play music too. We can do it. Let's show him!" Sue says. "OK, let's do it. We can make our own band. We have toys and pots and pans. We can make noise too!" Tom says. They run to their homes and get their toys and pots and pans. They make drums, trumpets, and flags out of them. They put on hats and scarves. They look like a band. They go back to the park. They march and play music behind the big band. They make more noise than the big band. They have fun. The band leader hears them. He turns around. He sees Tom and Sue. He is surprised and angry. He tries to make them stop. "Hey, you! Stop that! You are ruining our march! You are making too much noise! Go away!" he shouts. But Tom and Sue do not stop. They do not listen to him. They keep marching and playing music. They smile and laugh. They are glad. They are having more fun than the big band. They are the best band in the park.
