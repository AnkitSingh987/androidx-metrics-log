Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toy

One day, a little boy named Tim got a new toy. He was so happy. He wanted to show his new toy to his friend, Sam. Tim did not walk, he had to rush to Sam's house. He wanted Sam to see his new toy fast. When Tim got to Sam's house, Sam was eating at the table. Tim said, "Look at my new toy!" Sam looked up from the table and saw the new toy. He said, "Wow! That is a nice toy. Can I play with it too?" Tim and Sam played with the new toy all day. They had so much fun. They did not want to stop playing. When it was time for Tim to go home, he said, "I will come back tomorrow, and we can play with my new toy again!" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, please come back. I love your new toy!"

Once upon a time, there were two friends. They were called Daisy and Jake. Jake liked to swim and Daisy liked to play the trumpet. They were both very ready to have fun together. So one day, Jake and Daisy went to the beach. Daisy brought her trumpet and Jake had his swimming clothes on. First, Daisy and Jake built a sandcastle. Then Daisy played her trumpet while Jake jumped in the water to swim. Daisy loved the sound of the trumpet echoing off the waves. Jake was so happy swimming in the sea. When they were both ready, they went back home. Daisy was proud that she could play her trumpet and Jake was proud too, because he had swum really well. The end.

Tom and Ann are friends. They go to school together. They like to play and learn. But one day, Tom has a bad idea. He wants to send a funny letter to the teacher. He thinks it will make the teacher laugh. "Look, Ann, I wrote a letter for the teacher. It says 'You are a silly goose. You do not know anything. Go away and leave us alone.' Do you want to help me send it?" Tom asks Ann. Ann looks at the letter. She feels scared. She knows the teacher is nice and smart. She does not want to hurt the teacher's feelings. She does not want to be naughty. She shakes her head. "No, Tom, that is a bad letter. That is not funny. That is mean. The teacher will be sad and angry. We will be in trouble. Do not send it, Tom. Throw it away," Ann says. But Tom does not listen to Ann. He thinks Ann is a coward. He thinks he is clever. He puts the letter in an envelope. He writes the teacher's name on it. He sneaks to the teacher's desk. He puts the letter on the desk. He runs back to his seat. He smiles. The teacher comes in. She sees the letter. She opens it. She reads it. She does not laugh. She frowns. She looks around. She sees Tom's smile. She knows Tom sent the letter. She walks to Tom's seat. She picks up the letter. She shows it to Tom. "Tom, did you send me this letter?" the teacher asks. Tom nods. He thinks the teacher will praise him. He thinks the teacher will think he is funny. "Yes, I did. Do you like it?" Tom says. The teacher does not like it. The teacher is very upset. The teacher scolds Tom. She tells him he is foolish and rude. She tells him he has to go to the principal's office. She tells him he has to say sorry. She tells him he has to stay after school. She tells him he has to write a good letter. Tom feels ashamed. He feels sorry. He wishes he did not send the letter. He wishes he listened to Ann. He cries. He goes to the principal's office. He says sorry. He stays after school. He writes a good letter. He learns his lesson. He never sends a bad letter again.

One day, a big red ball and a small blue triangle were friends. They liked to play in the park. The red ball liked to roll fast, and the blue triangle liked to slide on the grass. They were happy friends. One day, they found a seat in the park. The red ball said, "Let's sit on the seat!" The blue triangle said, "Okay!" But when they tried to sit, they got angry. The red ball rolled off the seat, and the blue triangle slid off too. They could not sit on the seat together. Just then, a nice square came to the park. The square said, "Why are you angry?" The red ball and the blue triangle told the square about the seat. The square had an idea. "Let's all sit on the seat together!" The red ball, the blue triangle, and the nice square sat on the seat. They were not angry anymore. They were happy friends again, and they played in the park all day.
