Category: Beginner
Title: Sunday and the Cat

One Sunday, a beautiful cat was walking. The cat saw a big dog. The dog was not nice. The cat was scared. The cat did not know what to do. The cat started to think. The cat had a plan. The cat saw a tree. The cat ran to the tree. The dog ran after the cat. The cat climbed the tree very fast. The dog could not climb the tree. The dog was mad. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog saw a ball. The dog forgot about the cat. The dog played with the ball. The cat was safe in the tree. The cat was happy. The cat and the dog both had fun that Sunday.

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus liked to help people go to places they wanted to go. The bus was very calm and happy. Every day, the bus would go down the street and pick up people to take them to work, school, or the park. One day, the bus saw a little girl who had a toy. The toy was a soft bear. The little girl loved to rub the bear's tummy. She would rub the bear's tummy to make it feel happy and loved. The little girl took the bear with her on the bus. They sat together, and the girl rubbed the bear's tummy all the way to the park. At the park, the little girl and the bear played together. They went on the swings and down the slide. The bus watched them play and felt happy. The calm bus knew it helped the little girl and her bear have a fun day. When it was time to go home, the little girl and her bear got back on the bus. They waved goodbye to the park and went home, excited to come back another day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a man with a big box. The man was kind and smiled at Tim. He asked, "Do you want to play a game?" Tim nodded and the man opened the box. Inside, there were many colorful matches. The man said, "Let's play a game. When I point to a match, you tell me its color." Tim was happy and wanted to play. The man pointed to a red match and Tim said, "Red!" The man pointed to a blue match and Tim said, "Blue!" They played until Tim knew all the colors. After the game, Tim's mom gave the man a big hug. She was happy that the man was so compassionate and kind to play with Tim. The man smiled and said, "I love to make kids happy. Have a great day!" Tim and his mom waved goodbye and went home, feeling very happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to paint. She put on her gray shirt and went outside. She found a big wall and got her paint and brush. She was happy to make art. Lily met a boy named Jack. Jack said, "I want to paint too!" Lily smiled and gave him a brush. They painted together. The wall was now full of colors and shapes. Lily and Jack painted for an inch more. They were tired but happy. They looked at their art and laughed. Their gray shirts had paint too. They were proud of their work.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They bought many things like apples, cookies, and a box of mints. Tim liked mints because they were a bit spicy. When they got home, Tim's mom let him eat one mint. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam came over to play with Tim. They played with cars, blocks, and balls. Then, Tim decided to joke with Sam. He said, "Sam, do you want a spicy mint? It's very good!" Sam had never tried a spicy mint before, so he said, "Yes, please!" Tim gave Sam the mint. When Sam put it in his mouth, he made a funny face because it was so spicy. They both laughed and laughed. The joke was fun for both of them. But then, something unexpected happened. The spicy mint made Sam's nose tickle, and he sneezed really big! The sneeze was so big that it knocked down their block tower. Tim and Sam looked at each other and laughed even more. The spicy mint joke was the best joke they ever had.
