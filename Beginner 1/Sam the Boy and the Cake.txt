Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Boy and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. Sam was always ready to help out his mom. One day, his mom asked him to take a knife and test out a cake she had just made. Sam was excited to help and, being a very obedient boy, he agreed. So Sam took the knife and started to cut the cake. But he was having trouble making the cut because the knife was too sharp. Suddenly, his mom arrived and saw what was happening. She told him that he needed to be extra careful when using sharp tools. Then she cut the cake herself, using gentle pressure and lots of care. At this, Sam learned an important lesson - even if you're ready to do something, it's important to use the right tools for the job and to do it with care.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his rubber ball. He would run, jump, and fetch the ball all day long. One day, Spot and his ball went to the park. There, they met a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was looking for a place to settle and build a nest. Spot wanted to help Chirpy find a home. Spot and Chirpy looked all around the park. They saw a big tree with strong branches. Chirpy liked the tree and decided to settle there. Spot was happy to help his new friend. Together, Spot, Chirpy, and the rubber ball had lots of fun in the park. They played, laughed, and enjoyed the sunny day. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

Tom and Sam love to play baseball. They have a big red ball and a wooden bat. They go to the park every day and hit the ball. Sometimes they catch the ball, sometimes they miss. They have fun. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is black and furry. He has a long tail and sharp teeth. He runs fast and barks loud. Tom and Sam are scared of the dog. They want to play baseball, but they do not want the dog to chase them. They decide to throw the ball far away, so the dog will run after it. They hope the dog will leave them alone. Tom throws the ball as hard as he can. The ball flies high and far. The dog sees the ball and runs after it. He jumps and catches the ball in his mouth. He runs away with the ball. Tom and Sam are sad. They have lost their ball. They have no more baseball to play. They are miserable. "Hey, come back!" Tom shouts at the dog. "That's our ball!" "Let's go get it!" Sam says. "Maybe the dog will drop it." They run after the dog, but the dog is faster. He disappears behind some trees. Tom and Sam cannot find him. They look everywhere, but they cannot see their ball. They sit on a bench and cry. They have lost their baseball. They are miserable.

One day, a little dog named Max felt an urge to go outside. He wanted to run and play in the sun. Max looked at his owner, Sam, with big, sad eyes. Sam saw Max and knew he wanted to go out. Sam took Max to the park. Max was very happy. He ran and played with the other dogs. But then, Max saw a big, scary dog. Max felt uncomfortable. He did not want to play with the big dog. Max looked at Sam to help him. Sam saw Max and came to him. They walked away from the big dog. Max felt safe with Sam. They went home, and Max was happy to be with his friend.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a cube. The cube is rough and big. They can roll it and throw it. One day, they see a dog in the park. The dog is brown and furry. It looks friendly. Lily and Ben want to pet the dog. They jog to the dog with the cube. But the dog does not like the cube. The dog thinks the cube is a toy. The dog wants to play with the cube. The dog barks and jumps. It grabs the cube with its teeth. "Hey, that's our cube!" Lily says. She tries to pull the cube from the dog. "No, it's mine!" the dog says. It growls and shakes the cube. Ben is scared. He does not want to fight with the dog. He wants to run away. He says to Lily, "Let's go, Lily. The dog can have the cube. We can find another one." But Lily is angry. She likes the cube. She does not want to give it to the dog. She says to Ben, "No, Ben. The cube is ours. We have to get it back." Lily and Ben tug on the cube. The dog tugs on the cube. They make a lot of noise. They do not see the man who owns the dog. The man hears the noise. He sees the children and the dog. He runs to them. He says, "Stop, stop! What are you doing?" He takes the cube from the dog. He says to Lily and Ben, "I'm sorry, kids. This is my dog. He likes to play with things. He did not mean to take your cube." He says to the dog, "Bad dog! You have to share. You have to be nice." He gives the cube back to Lily and Ben. He says, "Here you go. This is your cube. You can play with it. But be careful. The cube is rough. It can hurt you or the dog." Lily and Ben say, "Thank you, sir. We're sorry, too. We did not know the dog was yours. We just wanted to pet him." They say to the dog, "We're sorry, dog. You can play with us. But you have to share. You have to be nice." The dog wags its tail. It licks Lily and Ben. It says, "Woof, woof! I'm sorry, kids. I just wanted to play with you. I like the cube. But I can share. I can be nice." Lily and Ben smile. They pet the dog. They say, "It's okay, dog. We like you. You can play with us. And you can play with the cube. But we have to share. We have to be nice." They all play together in the park. They roll and throw the cube. They jog and run. They have fun. They are friends.
