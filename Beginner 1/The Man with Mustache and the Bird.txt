Category: Beginner
Title: The Man with Mustache and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a man with a big mustache. He was very smart. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. They all liked his mustache. While they were playing, a little bird came to the man. The bird said, "Hello, Mr. Mustache! You are so smart, can you help me find my nest?" The man said, "Of course, little bird. Let's look for your nest together." They looked and looked, but they couldn't find the nest. Then, something unexpected happened. The man's mustache started to move! The mustache lifted the little bird up into the air. The bird saw its nest in a tall tree. The smart man's mustache helped the bird find its home. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mr. Mustache!" The man and his friends were very surprised and happy too.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside in the sunshine. They have a ball and a bike and a doll. They are happy. One day, a big dog comes to their yard. The dog is black and brown and has sharp teeth. The dog barks and runs at them. Anna and Ben are nervous. They are scared of the dog. Anna grabs the doll and runs to the house. Ben tries to get the ball and the bike, but the dog is faster. The dog bites the ball and pops it. The dog bites the bike and breaks it. Ben cries and runs to the house too. Anna and Ben hug each other and tell their mom. Their mom is angry and calls the dog's owner. The dog's owner is sorry and says he will buy a new ball and a new bike for Anna and Ben. He says he will keep the dog on a leash. Anna and Ben are still nervous, but they are glad the dog is gone. They say thank you to the dog's owner and hug their mom. They hope they can play outside in the sunshine again soon. They are friends.

Once upon a time there were two friends, named Dan and Dave. They were both very lazy. One day, they decided to go on a big adventure. Dave wanted to explore the woods and Dan wanted to fly a kite. So they got ready and started walking. But then Dave stopped and said, “Wait! I forgot my license!” “Don’t worry!” Dan offered. “I can get it for you.” So Dan ran back home and got Dave's license. And then they went on their adventure. Dan and Dave had so much fun exploring the woods and flying the kite. It was the best day ever!

Anna loved to play in the woods with her dog, Spot. She liked to run and jump and find new things. One day, she saw a big, shiny rock on the ground. She picked it up and put it in her pocket. She wanted to show it to her mom. "Come on, Spot, let's go home!" she said. But Spot did not follow her. He sniffed the air and barked. He ran to a trail that Anna had never seen before. He looked at her and wagged his tail. "Spot, no!" Anna said. "We have to go home. Mom will be worried." But Spot did not listen. He ran down the trail and disappeared. Anna was scared. She did not want to lose her dog. She ran after him. She did not see the sign that said "Danger. Bears." She followed the trail for a long time. She called for Spot, but he did not answer. She was tired and thirsty and hungry. She wished she had stayed home. She wished she had listened to her mom. She came to a clearing. She saw a big, brown bear. It was eating berries from a bush. It looked up and saw her. It growled and showed its teeth. Anna was very scared. She wanted to run, but she could not move. She held the rock in her hand. She hoped it would help her. Then, she heard a bark. It was Spot. He came out of the bushes. He was not afraid of the bear. He ran to Anna and stood in front of her. He barked and growled and demanded that the bear go away. The bear was surprised. It did not like the noise. It did not want to fight. It turned around and walked away. It was graceful and strong, but it did not want to hurt the girl and the dog. Anna was very happy. She hugged Spot and thanked him. He licked her face and wagged his tail. He was a good dog. He had saved her. He had found the way back home. He had followed the trail. Anna and Spot walked back to the house. Anna's mom was waiting for them. She was worried and angry. She scolded Anna for going to the woods. She told her it was dangerous. She told her to never do it again. Anna was sorry. She said she was sorry. She gave her mom the rock. She said it was a gift. She said it was a lucky rock. She said it had helped her and Spot. Her mom looked at the rock. It was shiny and pretty. She smiled and hugged Anna. She said she was glad she was safe. She said she loved her. She said she loved Spot. She said it was a special rock. She said they would keep it. Anna was happy. She had learned a lesson. She had had an adventure. She had a lucky rock. She had a brave dog. She had a loving mom. She was happy.
