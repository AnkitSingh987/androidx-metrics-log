Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Famous Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Max. Max was famous for helping people collect things. One day, Max met a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her toy. Max said, "Don't worry, I will help you collect your toy." Max and Lily looked everywhere for the toy. They looked under the bed, in the closet, and even in the garden. But they could not find the toy. Max thought very hard and then he had an idea. "Let's ask the bird who lives in the tree," Max said. Max and Lily went to the tree and asked the bird, "Have you seen Lily's toy?" The bird said, "Yes, I have it! I thought it was a license to build my nest. I'm sorry, I didn't know it was your toy." The bird gave the toy back to Lily. Lily was very happy and thanked Max and the bird. From that day on, Max, Lily, and the bird became the best of friends.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom and dad. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is brown and has a long tail. Lily and Ben want to pet the dog, but they are a little scared. The dog's owner is a nice lady. She smiles at Lily and Ben and says, "Hello, do you want to meet my dog? His name is Max. He is very friendly and gentle. You can pet him if you want, but ask him first." Lily and Ben gaze at the dog. He looks happy and calm. He wags his tail and licks his nose. Lily and Ben hear his voice. He makes a soft sound, like "woof". They think he is saying hello. Lily and Ben look at their mom and dad. They nod and say, "It's okay, you can pet the dog. But be careful and gentle. Don't pull his ears or his tail. And don't make loud noises. He might get scared." Lily and Ben walk slowly to the dog. They hold out their hands and say, "Hi, Max. Can we pet you?" Max sniffs their hands and licks them. He likes Lily and Ben. He lets them pet his fur and scratch his ears. He feels warm and soft. Lily and Ben feel his strong body and his big heart. They smile and giggle. They make a new friend.

One day, Ravi was walking in the park. He spotted a perfect post. He ran over to the post, and gave it a big pinch. Suddenly, a voice said, “Hey! That’s not yours!” Ravi looked up to see an old man. He was pointing at the post. Ravi said, “It’s perfect! I just wanted to pinch it.” The old man said, “Well, that’s my post. You can’t just go around pinching people’s posts.” Ravi said, “I’m sorry. I didn’t mean any harm.” The old man looked at him. Then he smiled and said, “Well, I’m sure it wasn’t so bad. But next time, just ask before you pinch.” Ravi said, “I will. I promise.” The old man nodded and gave Ravi a pat on the head. “That’s perfect. Now let’s go find another post.”

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw a big hoop on the ground. Amy picked up the hoop and started to play with it. She spun it around and around. It was so much fun! Then, Amy saw a scary dog. The dog was big and had sharp teeth. Amy was scared, but she did not run away. She said, "Hi, scary dog! Do you want to play with me and my hoop?" The dog stopped and looked at her. It did not bark or growl. The scary dog came closer and sniffed the hoop. It started to wag its tail. Amy and the scary dog played with the hoop together. They had a lot of fun. The scary dog was not so scary after all. It was just lonely and wanted to play. In the end, Amy and the scary dog became good friends. They met at the park every day to play with the hoop. The other kids saw that the dog was not scary, and they played with it too. The moral of the story is: sometimes things are not as scary as they seem, and being brave can lead to new friends.
