Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Frog and the Mosquito

Once upon a time, there was a restless little mosquito. He loved to fly and buzz around all day. He wanted to learn new things, so he went to his friend, the wise old frog, to ask for help. The frog said, "I can teach you how to jump like me." The mosquito was very excited. They practiced jumping every day. The mosquito loved learning from his friend. One day, the mosquito saw a little girl who was sad. She could not jump as high as her friends. The mosquito remembered how the frog taught him. He went to the little girl and said, "I can teach you how to jump high like me." They practiced together every day, and soon the little girl could jump as high as her friends. The mosquito was very happy, and he and the little girl became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house near the woods, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he saw a wild bird in a tree. The bird was very pretty and sang a nice song. Tim wanted to be its friend. Tim tried to climb the tree, but it was too tall. He saw an axe near the tree. Tim thought he could use the axe to make the tree shorter. He began to hit the tree with the axe. The bird saw Tim and started to shout. Tim did not know why the bird was shouting. Suddenly, the tree began to shake. Tim stopped hitting the tree with the axe. The wild bird flew down from the tree and landed on the ground. To Tim's surprise, the bird could talk! The bird said, "Thank you for not cutting down my home. Let's be friends." Tim was very happy and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tom. Tom had an impressive big plane. He loved to fly it high in the sky. One day, Tom had to fly the plane to a far place. He was a little worry because he had never been there before. Before the trip, Tom talked to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Don't worry, Tom. I will help you find the way." Tom felt better. They got in the plane and started to fly. The sky was blue, and the sun was bright. As they flew, Tom saw a big cloud. He was worry again. "What if we get lost?" he asked Sam. Sam smiled and said, "I told you, don't worry. I know the way." They flew around the cloud, and soon, they saw the far place. Tom was happy, and he thanked Sam for his help. They had a fun time at the far place and flew back home safely.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious big cat. The cat liked to sit on the porch of a little house. The house was near a big tree. The cat had a little friend, a cute bird. They played together every day. One day, the cat and the bird were playing near the tree. They saw a big box under the tree. They were very curious. They wanted to see what was inside the box. The cat opened the box with its paw. Inside the box, there was a big bee. The bee was angry because it was trapped in the box. The bee flew out of the box and stung the cat. The cat started to suffer. The bird was sad and scared. The cat felt very bad and could not play anymore. The bird flew away and never came back. The cat stayed on the porch, sad and alone.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big box of screws. The screws are yellow and shiny. Tom and Jen want to make something with the screws. "Look, Jen, we can make a car with the screws!" Tom says. "Yes, Tom, let's do it!" Jen says. They start to put the screws together. They use a hammer and a screwdriver. They make four wheels, a body, and a seat. They are happy with their car. "Wow, our car is so cool!" Tom says. "I want to drive it!" Jen says. They take turns to sit on the seat and push the car. They have fun. But then, a big boy comes. He sees the car. He likes it. He wants it. "Hey, give me that car!" he says. "No, it's ours! We made it!" Tom says. "Too bad, I'm taking it!" he says. He grabs the car and runs away. Tom and Jen are sad and angry. "Hey, come back! That's not fair!" Jen says. They chase the big boy. They catch him. They pull the car. "Let go of our car!" Tom says. "No, it's mine now!" he says. They fight over the car. The car breaks. The screws fall apart. The car is ruined. Tom and Jen cry. The big boy laughs. "Ha ha, your car is broken! You can't play with it anymore!" he says. He walks away. He does not care. Tom and Jen look at the broken car. They feel bad. "We worked so hard to make it!" Jen says. "I know, Jen, it was our car!" Tom says. They hug each other. They are still friends. They pick up the screws. They put them back in the box. "Maybe we can make something else with the screws," Tom says. "Maybe, Tom, but we need to be careful," Jen says. They look around. They see a sign. It says: "Pay here for the screws. One dollar per screw." They gasp. They have no money. They have no idea what to do. They are in trouble.
