Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Picking Flowers and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a girl picking flowers in the meadow. She saw a mysterious plant that she had never seen before and wanted to pick it for her garden. She tried to pull up the plant but it wouldn't come out. She then thought of a clever idea. She went to her house, got some glue and tried to stick the mysterious plant to the ground. To her surprise, it worked! The girl was so happy that she brought the mysterious plant to her garden. The girl learned an important lesson, always respect nature and don't take things that don't belong to you. She soon shared her lesson with her friends who also wanted to learn more about the mysterious plant. The end.

One day, a little girl named Daisy went to the park. She saw a big tree with a flexible branch. Daisy wanted to write her name on the branch. She took a pen and started to write. A boy named Tom saw Daisy and said, "Stop! That's my tree! You can't write on it!" Daisy felt sad and stopped writing. She didn't want to make Tom mad. Daisy said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I didn't know it was your tree." Tom thought for a moment and said, "It's okay, Daisy. You can write your name on the tree, too." So, Daisy and Tom became friends and wrote their names on the tree together. They played in the park and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant. The ant liked to eat tasty food. One day, he found a big, red apple. The ant was very happy. The ant tried to take the apple home. But the apple was too big. The ant did not give up. He pushed and pushed. The ant knew something good would happen soon. At last, the apple began to yield. It rolled and rolled. The ant and his friends had a big, tasty feast. They were all very happy.

One day, a boy and a girl went for a walk. They saw a big, open field. In the field, they saw many pretty flowers. They walked and walked until they found a big tree. The tree had a swing on it. The boy said, "Let's play on the swing." The girl said, "Yes, let's play!" They took turns on the swing. They had a lot of fun. They laughed and laughed. Then, they saw a big, tall hill. They walked up the hill. At the top, they found a pass. The pass led to a beautiful park. The boy and the girl played in the park until the sun went down. They had a great day.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They like to run and jump and slide. One day, they see a big sign. The sign says: "Race for kids. Win a prize. Tomorrow at 10 o'clock." Lily and Ben want to join the race. They want to win the prize. They do not know what the prize is, but it must be interesting. They go home and tell their moms. "Mom, can I join the race tomorrow?" Lily asks. "Mom, can I join the race tomorrow?" Ben asks. Their moms say yes, but they have to promise something. "You have to promise to be good and listen to the rules," Lily's mom says. "You have to promise to have fun and be nice to the other kids," Ben's mom says. Lily and Ben promise. They are happy and excited. The next day, they go to the park. They see many kids. They see a man with a whistle. He is the race leader. He tells the kids to line up. He tells them the race is around the park. He tells them to be ready. Lily and Ben are next to each other. They smile and say good luck. They hear the whistle. They start to run. Lily and Ben are fast. They pass many kids. They are close to the front. They see the finish line. They want to be the first. But then, something happens. Lily trips on a rock. She falls down. She hurts her knee. She cries. Ben hears Lily cry. He stops. He looks back. He sees Lily on the ground. He wants to help her. But he also wants to win the prize. What will Ben do? Will he leave Lily and run to the finish line? Or will he go back and help Lily? What would you do?
