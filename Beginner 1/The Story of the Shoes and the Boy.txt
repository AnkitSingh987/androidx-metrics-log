Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Shoes and the Boy

Once upon a time there was a little boy. He was three years old and he loved to explore. One day he wanted to get something special. So he went to the market and found a pair of shoes. They were crazy! They were blue and had sparkles all over. The little boy was so excited. He said to himself, "I'm gonna get these shoes!" He put on the shoes and was running around, but then he stopped. He felt something wrong with his foot. The shoe was pinching him! He said to his mom, "Mommy, there is something wrong with this shoe!" The mom looked at the shoe, and she turned pale. She took off the shoe and saw that the boy's foot was red and swollen. That's when they knew: the shoe was too small and it was hurting the boy's foot. The little boy was so sad. He had to take off the shoes, and he never got the chance to show off his crazy shoes.

Once upon a time, a chubby cat named Kitty lived in a small house. Kitty loved to play and eat all day. One day, she saw a calendar on the wall. The calendar had a picture of a big, yummy cake on it. Kitty wanted to eat the cake, but she didn't know how to get it. Kitty asked her friend, a little bird named Chirpy, "How can I get the cake from the calendar?" Chirpy said, "You can't eat the cake, Kitty. It's just a picture. But you can make a real cake with me!" Kitty and Chirpy made a big, yummy cake together. They shared the cake with all their friends. Everyone was happy and had fun. Kitty learned that sharing with friends is better than trying to take things that are not real. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a small fish saw a big shark. The shark was not moving. The fish went near the shark and saw that it was dead. The fish felt sad for the shark. A little crab came to see what was going on. The crab saw the dead shark too. The crab told the fish, "Let's hug the shark to make it feel better." So, the fish and the crab hugged the dead shark. After they hugged the shark, a big wave came. The wave took the dead shark far away. The fish and the crab said goodbye to the shark. They swam away and played together.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a swing and ran to it. She sat on the swing and began to play. Mia blinked her eyes as the sun was very bright. A little boy named Tom came to the park too. He saw Mia on the swing and wanted to play. Tom asked Mia, "Can I play on the swing too?" Mia felt guilty because there was only one swing. She did not want to share. Tom was sad and walked away. Mia kept playing on the swing. She did not let Tom play. In the end, Tom went home and cried. Mia felt bad but did not say sorry. The day was not fun for Tom and Mia felt guilty.

One day, a big cat and a small dog were in a park. They did not like each other. They wanted to fight. The cat said, "I am bigger and stronger than you!" The dog said, "But I am faster and smarter!" Nearby, there was a tall tree with big leaves. On one of the leaves, there was a small bug. The bug was careful and did not want to fall. The cat and the dog saw the bug on the leaf. They both wanted to catch it. The cat and the dog started to fight under the tree. They were very loud. The bug was scared and fell from the leaf. It landed on the palm of a little girl. The little girl saw the bug and smiled. She said, "Hello, little bug! You are safe now." The cat and the dog stopped fighting. They looked at the little girl and the bug. They were surprised. They did not want to scare the little girl or the bug. The cat and the dog said, "We are sorry." They became friends and played together in the park.
