Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of Tim the Boy

One day, a boy named Tim found a ball. The ball was big and filthy. Tim wanted to play, so he tried to kick the ball. But the ball was too heavy to kick. Tim felt sad, but he didn't give up. Tim thought of a plan to make the ball light. He asked his friend, Sue, for help. Sue was strong and had lots of power. Sue helped Tim clean the ball. They used water and soap, and the ball became clean and light. Now, Tim and Sue could kick the ball. They played all day and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball changed color! It was a magic ball. Tim and Sue were so happy. They played with the magic ball every day, and it always changed color.

One day, a big, friendly dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a little girl named Sue playing with her toy lock. Spot wanted to play too. Sue said, "Let's play a game. We will match the keys to open the lock." They started to play the game. Spot was very good at matching the keys. He found the right key to open the lock. But, oh no! When they opened the lock, a big wind blew and took all the keys away. Spot and Sue were sad. Spot had an idea. He said, "Let's work together to find the keys." They looked high and low, and found all the keys. They matched the keys to the lock again and played happily. The moral of the story is that working together makes things easier and more fun.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to go to the park. She asked her mom, "Can we go to the park, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can go, but you have to bring your bear. He is your friend and he will keep you safe." Lily was happy and ran to get her bear. He was a brown bear with a red scarf. His name was Ben. Lily loved Ben very much. She hugged him and said, "Let's go to the park, Ben. We will have fun." They went to the park with Lily's mom. There were many things to do. Lily and Ben went on the slide, the swing, and the seesaw. They also played with some other kids and their toys. Lily and Ben had a lot of fun. But then, Lily saw a big dog. The dog was barking and running towards them. Lily was scared and cried, "Mom, help! There is a big dog!" Her mom said, "Don't worry, Lily. The dog is not mean. He just wants to play. But you have to be careful and not touch him. He might bite you." Lily nodded and held Ben tight. She said, "Ben, you are my friend and you will keep me safe. You are a wise bear. You know what to do." Ben said, "Yes, Lily. I am your friend and I will keep you safe. I am a wise bear. I know what to do. We have to stay calm and not run away. The dog will not hurt us if we are nice to him." Lily and Ben did what Ben said. They stayed calm and did not run away. The dog came closer and sniffed them. He saw that they were not afraid and that they were nice. He wagged his tail and licked them. He wanted to be their friend too. Lily and Ben smiled and said, "Hello, dog. You are not mean. You just want to play. We can be your friends too." The dog barked happily and played with them. Lily's mom watched them and smiled too. She said, "Lily, you are a brave girl. And Ben, you are a wise bear. You both did very well. I am proud of you." Lily and Ben hugged her and said, "Thank you, mom. We love you."

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big ball and wanted to play. He ran to the ball and kicked it very hard. The ball flew through the air and landed in a basket. Tim was very happy. A girl named Sue saw Tim kick the ball. She wanted to play too. Sue asked Tim, "Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes! Let's play together!" So, Tim and Sue took turns kicking the ball into the basket. They had a lot of fun. After a while, Tim kicked the ball too hard, and it hit Sue's leg. Sue said, "Ouch! That hurt!" Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Sue." Sue smiled and said, "It's okay. Let's be more careful." They kept playing and had a great day at the park.
