Category: Beginner
Title: The Cool Ant and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a cool ant. The ant lived in a big tree. The tree was in a green park. The ant had many friends. One day, the ant went for a long walk. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. The ant could hear the birds. The ant liked the pretty songs that the birds sang. The ant was very happy. On the way, the ant met a nice cat. The cat was soft and kind. The ant and the cat played together. They had lots of fun. In the end, the ant went back to the big tree. The ant told all its friends about the fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom lived near a creek. He liked to play in the water and catch fish. One day, Tom saw a little bird by the creek. The bird looked sad. Tom asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird answered, "I can't find my mom. Can you help me?" Tom wanted to help the bird. They walked by the creek, looking for the bird's mom. They asked other animals if they had seen her. Finally, they found the bird's mom in a tree. The bird was happy to see her mom. The mom said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping my baby." Tom was happy that he could help his new friend. From that day on, Tom, the bird, and her mom played together by the creek every day.

Mum and Dad had a special surprise for Sarah. One morning, Mum took her to the room with a desk. It looked so big to Sarah even though she was three. The desk was covered in presents. Sarah was so excited that she wanted to open them all at once. "No, Sarah," said Mum calmly. "You can only open one at a time!" Sarah wanted to open all of them, but she remembered what Mum said and tried to be calm. Slowly, She opened each present one by one. It felt like it took forever. In the end, Sarah was so happy with all the surprises she had received. And when Sarah was finished, the desk was covered up with all the presents again.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max saw a well. He thought it would be fun to play near the well. Max threw the ball, but it fell into the well. He was very sad. He could not get the ball back. A little bird saw Max and asked, "Why are you sad?" Max said, "My ball is in the well. I can't get it." The little bird had an idea. She flew down into the well and got the ball. But when she came back, she had two balls! Max was very happy. He now had two balls to play with. The little bird and Max played together all day, and they became the best of friends.

Sam and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. Sam wants to go first. He runs to the slide and climbs up. Mia follows him. "Wait for me, Sam!" Mia says. "I want to slide too!" Sam does not wait. He slides down very fast. He has fun. He laughs. He runs to the slide again. Mia is sad. She wants to slide too. But Sam does not let her. He pushes her away. He says, "No, Mia! This is my slide! Go away!" Mia is angry. She pushes Sam back. She says, "No, Sam! This is our slide! Share with me!" Sam and Mia push each other. They shout. They do not have fun. They make trouble. A lady sees them. She is the park keeper. She is not happy. She walks to them. She says, "Stop, Sam and Mia! You are making trouble! You are not being nice! You have to share the slide! Or you have to leave the park!" Sam and Mia are scared. They do not want to leave the park. They do not want to make trouble. They say, "Sorry, lady. Sorry, Sam. Sorry, Mia." The lady smiles. She says, "It's okay, Sam and Mia. But you have to be nice. You have to share the slide. You have to take turns. Then you can have fun." Sam and Mia nod. They say, "Okay, lady. Okay, Sam. Okay, Mia." They go to the slide. They take turns. They slide down. They have fun. They laugh. They are friends. They are ordinary.
