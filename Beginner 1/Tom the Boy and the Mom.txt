Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to eat. One day, his mom made him a big plate of yummy food. Tom was very happy. Tom ate and ate until his plate was empty. He had food all over his mouth. His mom saw this and smiled. She got a soft cloth to help him. His mom said, "Let me wipe your mouth, Tom." She wiped his mouth gently. Now, Tom's mouth was clean. Tom said, "Thank you, Mom." They both laughed and were happy.

---

One day, a girl named Lily went outside to play. It was an icy cold day, but Lily wore a big coat to keep her warm. She saw her friend Tom, who was making a snowman. Lily smiled and ran over to help him. Tom and Lily rolled a big snowball for the snowman's body. They put a smaller snowball on top for the head. Lily found two sticks and put them on the sides of the snowman's waist to be his arms. Tom said, "Our snowman looks so happy!" Lily and Tom laughed and played in the snow all day. They had so much fun with their snowman friend. When it was time to go inside, they waved goodbye to the snowman and went home to drink hot cocoa. They couldn't wait to play together again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Bob. Bob had a big toy jeep. He loved to play with the jeep every day. It was his favorite toy. One day, Bob saw a big ball. He wanted to play with the ball too. He pushed the ball with his nose and it rolled away. The ball rolled very fast and hit the jeep. The jeep was not strong. Bob looked at the jeep and saw that it was broken. He tried to fix it, but he could not. The jeep was destroyed. Bob was very sad. He missed his jeep and could not play with it anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved summer more than anything. He liked to play outside with his friends and eat ice cream. But one day, a big rain came and made it hard to play outside. Tim was sad because he could not play with his friends. He wanted the rain to go away. His mom told him, "If you believe, the sun will come back." Tim thought about what his mom said and decided to believe. The next day, the sun came out and the rain was gone. Tim was so happy. He played outside with his friends all day long. Tim learned that if he was thoughtful and believed, good things could happen. And they all had the best summer ever.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, round football. It was colorful and harmless. He was very happy to play with it. He put the football on the ground and kicked it high in the sky. As the football flew up in the sky, something strange happened. The football did not come back down. It stayed up in the sky, like a big round balloon. Tim was sad and confused. He wanted his football back. Just then, a friendly bird flew by and saw Tim's sad face. The bird asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "My football is stuck in the sky." The bird smiled and said, "I can help you." The bird flew up high and pushed the football back down to Tim. Tim was so happy and thanked the bird. They played together and had a fun day.

Sam liked to ride his bicycle. He had a red bicycle with two big wheels and a bell. He rode his bicycle every day to the park, where he met his friends. They played games and had fun. One day, Sam saw a new boy at the park. He had a blue bicycle with three wheels and a basket. Sam wanted to join him and play with his bicycle. He said hello and asked his name. The boy said his name was Ben and he was new in town. He said he liked Sam's bicycle too. Sam and Ben became friends. They rode their bicycles together and shared their toys. They went to the slide, the swing, and the sandbox. They had a lot of fun. But then, a big dog came to the park. It was black and hairy and had sharp teeth. It barked and ran after Sam and Ben. They were scared and tried to get away. Sam's bicycle was fast and he could pedal hard. He reached the gate and got out of the park. He was safe. But Ben's bicycle was slow and he could not pedal fast. He was still in the park and the dog was getting closer. He cried and called for help. Sam heard him and turned around. He saw Ben in trouble and felt sorry. He wanted to help him. Sam had an idea. He rode his bicycle back to the park. He rang his bell and shouted at the dog. He made the dog look at him and follow him. He led the dog away from Ben and to the other side of the park. There was a fence and a pond. Sam threw a stick over the fence and into the pond. The dog jumped over the fence and chased the stick. It fell into the water and got wet. It stopped barking and swam back to the shore. Sam rode his bicycle back to Ben. He helped him get out of the park. He gave him a hug and said he was sorry. Ben thanked him and said he was brave. He said Sam's bicycle was useful and he was glad he was his friend. Sam smiled and said Ben's bicycle was cool and he was glad he was his friend too. They rode their bicycles home and told their parents what happened. They were happy and proud. They were good friends.
