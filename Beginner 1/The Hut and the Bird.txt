Category: Beginner
Title: The Hut and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a small hut in the woods. A nice man lived in the hut. He was a very tough man, but he was kind to everyone. One day, a little bird came to the hut. The bird was lost and scared. The nice man said, "Welcome, little bird!" He gave the bird some food and water. The bird was very happy. The tough man and the little bird became good friends. They played together and helped each other. They lived happily in the little hut, and they welcomed many more friends who came to visit them. And they all lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys in Anna's room. Anna has a fancy rug on the floor. It is soft and has many colors. Ben likes the rug too. One day, Anna and Ben are playing with dolls on the rug. Ben sees a shiny button on the rug. He thinks it is a coin. He wants to have it. He picks up the button and puts it in his pocket. "Ben, what are you doing?" Anna asks. She sees Ben take the button. "I found a coin on the rug. It is mine now," Ben says. "No, Ben, that is not a coin. That is a button from my dress. It fell off yesterday. It is mine. Give it back," Anna says. Ben does not want to give it back. He likes the shiny button. He thinks Anna has many things and he has few. He runs to the door. "Stop, Ben, that is not nice. You are taking something that is not yours. That is stealing. You are not a good friend," Anna says. Ben feels bad. He knows Anna is right. He stops at the door. He turns around and walks back to Anna. He takes the button out of his pocket and gives it to Anna. "I am sorry, Anna. You are right. This is your button. I should not have taken it. I was greedy and selfish. Can you forgive me?" Ben says. Anna smiles. She takes the button and hugs Ben. "Thank you, Ben. I forgive you. You are a good friend. You said sorry and gave it back. That is brave and honest. I am sorry too. I should have shared my things with you. Do you want to play with my dolls?" Anna says. Ben nods. He hugs Anna back. "Yes, please. I would love to play with your dolls. And you can play with my cars. We can share our things. That is fun and kind," Ben says. They sit on the rug and play with their toys. They are happy and friendly. They learn to share and not to steal. That is a good lesson.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to climb a tree. He saw a big tree in the park and started to climb. He was happy and excited. As he climbed, he saw a bird in the tree. The bird said, "Hello, Tim! Why are you climbing the tree?" Tim said, "I want to touch the sky!" The bird laughed and said, "You are very brave!" Later, it started to rain. The rain felt like a shower from the sky. Tim and the bird got wet, but they were not sad. They liked the cheap shower from the sky. They laughed and played in the rain. When the rain stopped, Tim climbed down from the tree. He said goodbye to the bird and went home. He told his mom about his fun day. Tim's mom smiled and hugged him.

Once upon a time there was a bear. He was walking home and he wanted to get to his home quickly. As he was walking he started to feel a tickle in his nose. He knew what it meant, and he was ready. He stopped and said "Ah... Ah... Ah... Choo." He had sneezed. He had to sneeze one more time in order to get home. He made it home and he saw his mommy. He said to her, "Mommy, I sneezed!" His mommy hugged him and said, "Oh, I'm so glad you made it home safe and sound. Now you can relax and get ready for a nap." The bear hugged his mommy and said, "Thank you, Mommy. I'm ready for a nap now." His mommy smiled and said, "That's a good bear. You get some rest now." The bear went to bed, feeling happy and content to be back home in his very own bed. He was ready for his nap and to explore his adventures tomorrow.
