Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Star

Anna and Ben were playing with paper and scissors. They liked to cut out shapes and make pictures. Anna cut out a star and a heart. Ben cut out a circle and a square. "Look, I made a star and a heart!" Anna said, showing her paper to Ben. "Those are nice," Ben said, "but mine are better. See, I made a circle and a square. They are perfect." Anna felt sad. She thought Ben was rude. She did not like how he said his shapes were better. She wanted to say something, but she did not know what. She looked at Ben's paper and scissors. She had an idea. She reached out and grabbed his scissors. "Hey, give me back my scissors!" Ben shouted. He tried to take them from Anna, but she held them tight. "No, they are mine now!" Anna said. She was angry. She wanted to make Ben feel bad. She lifted the scissors and cut Ben's circle and square in half. She laughed and said, "Now your shapes are not perfect. They are broken." Ben felt mad. He did not like how Anna cut his shapes. He thought she was mean. He wanted to get his scissors back. He pushed Anna and grabbed the scissors. He pulled them hard. Anna did not let go. They both tugged and tugged. Suddenly, the scissors slipped from their hands. They flew in the air and landed on the floor. They made a loud noise. Anna and Ben stopped and looked at the scissors. They felt scared. They knew scissors were sharp and dangerous. They knew they should not play with them. They looked at each other. They felt sorry. They knew they were wrong. They did not want to fight. They said at the same time, "I'm sorry." They hugged and said, "Let's be friends." They picked up the scissors and put them away. They decided to play with something else. They learned to share and be kind. They had fun.

Once upon a time, in a regular house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved summer days. He liked to play outside with his friends, eat ice cream, and swim in the pool. One sunny summer day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, let's print a picture for your room." Tim was excited. He picked a big, red ball from his toy box and said, "I want to print a big, red ball!" Tim's mom helped him print the big, red ball on a piece of paper. They put it on the wall in Tim's room. Tim was very happy. He showed his friends the big, red ball and said, "Look! I printed a big, red ball for my room!" From that day on, Tim and his mom printed more pictures for his room. They printed a sun, a tree, and a cat. Tim loved his room with all the pictures he printed. He had the best summer ever.

One day, a boy named Tom found a photo on the floor. It was a difficult day for him because he had lost his toy. Tom saw a girl named Sue in the photo. He thought she might have his toy. Tom went to find Sue to ask her about his toy. "Did you find my toy?" Tom asked Sue. Sue said, "No, but I will help you look for it." They looked everywhere, but they could not find the toy. Tom was sad, and Sue felt bad for him. Then, a big dog came running to them with the toy in its mouth. The dog did not want to give the toy back. Tom said, "Please surrender my toy, dog." The dog looked at Tom and Sue, then dropped the toy. Tom and Sue were very happy and played with the toy and the dog all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very impatient. One day, he saw a big, red apple on a tree. He wanted to eat it, but he could not reach it. Tim's mom saw him trying. She picked the apple and gave it to him. Tim was so happy. He wanted to eat it right away. But his mom said, "Wait, it is hot outside. The apple is hot too. Let it cool down." Tim waited and waited. He was impatient, but he listened to his mom. Soon, the apple was not hot anymore. Tim ate the apple. It was so yummy! Tim was happy he waited, and his mom was proud of him.
