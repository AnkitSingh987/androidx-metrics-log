Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a purple dress that she loved very much. One day, Lily wanted to wrap a gift for her best friend, Tim. Lily asked her mom, "What is your opinion about wrapping the gift?" Her mom said, "I think it's a great idea!" So, Lily found some paper and tape, and wrapped the gift with her mom's help. The next day, Lily gave the wrapped gift to Tim. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" They played together all day and had lots of fun. And Lily was happy she could make her friend smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside and eat healthy food. She had a big bed in her room that she liked to jump on. One day, Sue was playing in her room. She was jumping on her bed, pretending she was a frog. She hopped and hopped, laughing and having fun. But then, Sue slipped on her bed and fell down. Sue was sad and her leg hurt a little. She called her mommy to come and help her. Her mommy came in and saw Sue on the floor. She picked Sue up and gave her a hug. Sue felt better and promised to be more careful when playing on her bed.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big box. The box had the word "equipment" on it. Tim did not know what that word meant. But he was excited to see what was inside the box. Tim opened the box and found a lot of toys. He thought it was a gift for him. He played with the toys all day. He did not mention the box to anyone. He thought it was his secret. But he did something stupid. He left the box open. The next day, Tim went back to the park. He saw that the box was empty. All the toys were gone. He was sad. But then he saw his friends playing with the toys. Tim's mom told him that the toys were for everyone in the park. Tim was happy because now he could play with his friends and the toys together. The unexpected thing was that the toys were for everyone, not just for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was a powerful helper. He liked to gather things for his mom and dad. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, can you gather the toys for me?" Tim said, "Yes, mom!" So, Tim went to his room and gathered all the toys. He put them in a big box. Tim's dad saw him and said, "Wow, Tim! You are a powerful helper!" Tim smiled and said, "I love to help!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he saw a fancy shelf in his room. The shelf was new and had many toys on it. Tim wanted to get a toy from the top of the shelf. He tried to reach it, but he was too small. He looked around and found a chair. Tim moved the chair to the shelf and stood on it. Now, he could reach the toy. As Tim grabbed the toy, he heard a loud snap! The shelf broke and fell down. Tim and the toys fell down, too. Tim was sad and hurt. His toys were broken, and the fancy shelf was gone.

Lily was sad. Her doll had a big cut on her arm. She did not know how to heal it. She asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you heal my doll? She is hurt and I love her very much." Mom smiled and hugged Lily. She said, "Of course, sweetie. We can heal your doll together. We need some water, some soap, some cloth and some glue. Come with me to the kitchen." They went to the kitchen and mom filled a pot with water. She put it on the stove and turned it on. She said, "We have to wait for the water to get hot. Then we can wash your doll's arm and make it clean." Lily nodded and held her doll. She saw a smelly cheese on the table. She wrinkled her nose and said, "Mom, what is that? It smells bad." Mom laughed and said, "That is cheese, honey. Some people like to eat it, but I agree, it is smelly. We can put it in the fridge later." The water in the pot started to bubble. Mom turned off the stove and said, "The water is ready. Let's go to the sink and wash your doll's arm." They went to the sink and mom poured some water and soap on a cloth. She gently rubbed the cloth on the doll's arm. She said, "See, we are making it clean. Does it hurt, doll?" Lily pretended that the doll said, "No, it does not hurt. Thank you, mom and Lily. You are very kind." Mom smiled and said, "You are welcome, doll. Now we need to dry your arm and put some glue on the cut. Then we can wrap it with another cloth and make a bandage." They did as mom said and soon the doll's arm was healed. Lily hugged her doll and said, "You are all better now, doll. I am so happy. You are my best friend." Mom hugged Lily and said, "I am happy too, sweetie. You are a good doctor and a good friend. I love you and your doll very much."
