Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Bench

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a new gem in the grass. It is shiny and red. "Wow, look at this!" Tom says. "It is so pretty!" "Can I see?" Mia asks. She holds out her hand. Tom gives her the gem. Mia smiles. She likes the gem too. "Let's set it on the bench," Tom says. "We can make a game with it." "Okay," Mia says. They run to the bench. They put the gem on the wood. "Now, we can take turns to guess where the gem is," Tom says. "I will close my eyes. You can move the gem. Then I will try to find it." "Okay," Mia says. She moves the gem to the other side of the bench. "Ready!" Tom opens his eyes. He looks at the bench. He sees the gem. "I found it!" he says. He picks up the gem. "Now, you close your eyes. I will move the gem." "Okay," Mia says. She closes her eyes. Tom moves the gem to the middle of the bench. "Ready!" Mia opens her eyes. She looks at the bench. She sees the gem. "I found it!" she says. She picks up the gem. "This is fun!" They laugh. They play with the gem. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a harmless bunny named Bob. Bob lived in a big forest with his family. He liked to hop, play, and eat. One day, Bob found a prune while hopping. He had never seen a prune before. Bob took the prune to his friends, a bird and a squirrel. They all looked at the prune and wondered what it was. They decided to taste it. The bird took a little peck, the squirrel had a small bite, and Bob nibbled the prune too. They all liked it! In the end, they shared the prune and played together. They were happy they found a new yummy treat. Bob, the bird, and the squirrel stayed best friends forever.

Jen and Sam were going to the park with Mom. They liked the park. It had swings and slides and a big sandbox. Jen and Sam put on their coats and hats and ran to the car. Mom followed them with a big bag. "Mom, what is in the bag?" Jen asked. "Something fun for the park," Mom said. "But it is a surprise. You will see when we get there." Jen and Sam were curious. They wanted to know what was in the bag. They tried to peek, but Mom closed the bag and put it in the trunk. She told them to get in the car and buckle their seat belts. Jen and Sam did as they were told. They buckled their seat belts and waited for Mom to start the car. But Mom did not start the car. She looked at Jen and Sam and frowned. "Jen, Sam, I have to tell you something," Mom said. "You know how we always have to keep the car clean?" Jen and Sam nodded. They knew Mom liked the car to be clean. She always said it was safer and nicer to drive in a clean car. "Well, I found something in the back seat that was not clean," Mom said. "It was sticky and smelly and made a big mess. Do you know what it was?" Jen and Sam looked at each other. They knew what it was. It was the ice cream they had yesterday. They had not finished it and had hidden it under a blanket. They had forgotten to mention it to Mom. They had hoped she would not find it. They felt guilty and scared. They knew Mom would be angry. They did not know what to say. "Jen, Sam, do you have anything to say?" Mom asked. Jen and Sam looked down. They whispered, "Sorry, Mom." Mom sighed. She was not happy, but she was not too angry. She loved Jen and Sam and knew they made mistakes sometimes. "Sorry is good, but not enough," Mom said. "You have to help me clean the car. And you have to promise not to hide things from me again. And you have to share the surprise in the bag with me. Do you understand?" Jen and Sam nodded. They said, "Yes, Mom. We understand. We promise." Mom smiled. She said, "Good. Now let's go to the park and have some fun. But first, let me show you what is in the bag. It is something you will love. It is a new kite!"
