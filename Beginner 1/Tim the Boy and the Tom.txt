Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim and his elderly brother, Tom. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. Tim loved to play with Tom, but sometimes Tom liked to scare Tim. Tim would laugh and say, "Don't scare me, Tom!" One sunny day, Tim and Tom went to the park to play. They saw a big tree and decided to climb it. Tom went up first, and when Tim was almost at the top, Tom yelled, "Boo!" Tim was scared, but he laughed and said, "You got me, Tom!" Later, they went back home and told their mom and dad about their day. Their mom said, "Tom, be nice to your little brother. Don't scare him too much." Tom promised he would be nice, and they all had a happy dinner together.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went outside to play. They saw an empty tent in their yard. Tim had an idea. "Let's have a picnic, Spot!" he said. Tim and Spot went into the house to get some food. They found bread, cheese, and jam. Tim spread the jam on the bread. He put the cheese on top. They took the food and a big blanket to the tent. Inside the tent, Tim and Spot had a fun picnic. They ate the yummy food and talked. "This is the best day ever, Spot!" said Tim. Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" They played and ate until it was time to go inside for bed.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big dog named Max. Tim and Max loved to play outside. One day, they saw something new. It was a big, dull box. Tim and Max looked at the box and wondered what it was. Tim said to Max, "Let's sit and look at the box." Max wagged his tail and sat next to Tim. They watched the box for a long time. Tim and Max got very tired. They wanted to know what was inside the box. Tim opened the box and saw lots of ice. Max was very happy. He loved to play with ice. Tim took some ice out of the box and gave it to Max. Max played with the ice and had lots of fun. Tim laughed as Max played with the ice. They had a great day playing with the ice and the dull box.

---

Tim and Mia were playing with a ball in the park. They liked to kick the ball to each other and run after it. Sometimes they kicked the ball very hard and it went far away. One time, Tim kicked the ball so hard that it flew over the fence and into the street. He ran to get it, but he did not look for cars. Mia saw a big car coming fast and shouted, "Tim, stop! Car!" But Tim did not hear her. He ran into the street and tried to get the ball. The car could not stop in time and hit Tim. Tim fell down and did not move. Mia screamed and ran to him. She was very scared and sad. The car driver got out and called for help. He was very sorry and said, "I did not see him. It was a surprise. I tried to brake, but it was too late." Mia cried and hugged Tim. She hoped he would wake up and be okay. She wished they had stayed in the park, where it was safe. She wished they had not kicked the ball so hard. She wished it was all a bad dream. But it was not. It was real. And it was bad.

Sara and Tom were friends. They liked to play games. One day, they had a plan. They wanted to scare their big sister, Mia. They found a pin in the kitchen. They hid it in Mia's bed. They waited for Mia to come home. They smiled at each other. Mia came home. She was tired. She went to her room. She did not see Sara and Tom. She pulled the blanket over her. She felt a sharp pain in her foot. She screamed. Sara and Tom ran to her room. They laughed. They said, "We got you! We got you!" Mia was angry. She saw the pin. She said, "That was not funny! That was mean! You could have hurt me!" Sara and Tom stopped laughing. They felt sorry. They said, "We are sorry, Mia. We just wanted to play. We did not want to hurt you." Mia said, "You should not play with pins. They are dangerous. And you should not scare me in the dark. That is scary." Sara and Tom said, "You are right, Mia. We will not do that again. We will find another game to play. A nice game." Mia said, "OK. I forgive you. But you have to be careful. And you have to say sorry to Mom. She heard you scream. She was worried." Sara and Tom said, "OK. We will say sorry to Mom. And we will hug you. We love you, Mia." Mia said, "I love you too, Sara and Tom. But next time, no pins, OK?" Sara and Tom said, "OK. No pins."
