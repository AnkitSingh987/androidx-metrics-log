Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had an incredible toy car. It was shiny, fast, and he loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim's mom told him that they needed to sell some of their toys to buy new ones. Tim was sad, but he knew he had to help. Tim and his mom set up a table outside their house to sell the toys. They put all the toys on the table, but Tim did not want to sell his incredible car. So, he hid it under the table. While they were busy selling the other toys, Tim's car started to move on its own. It wanted to escape! The car rolled out from under the table and started driving down the street. Tim saw the car and yelled, "Come back!" He ran after the car as fast as he could. The car was very fast, but Tim did not give up. Finally, Tim caught the car and hugged it tight. He knew he could not sell his incredible car. Tim's mom saw how much he loved the car and let him keep it. They both smiled and went back to selling the other toys.

Tom and Mia were playing in the park. They liked to climb the big slide and slide down fast. They also liked to swing on the swings and go high in the air. They had a lot of fun. But then they saw a giant. The giant was very tall and had a big beard and a big coat. He looked angry and scary. He came to the park and started to break the slide and the swings. He made a loud noise and scared the other kids. Tom and Mia were afraid of the giant. They ran and hid behind a tree. They hoped the giant would not see them. They held each other's hands and whispered. "What do we do?" Tom asked. "I don't know," Mia said. "Maybe we can talk to him. Maybe he is not so bad." Mia was brave. She came out from behind the tree and walked to the giant. She said in a small voice, "Hello, Mr. Giant. Why are you breaking the park? We like the park. It is fun. Please don't break it." The giant looked at Mia. He was surprised. He did not expect a little girl to talk to him. He bent down and said in a big voice, "Hello, little girl. I am sorry. I am not breaking the park on purpose. I am just looking for my lost hat. It flew away in the wind. I am sad. I like my hat. It is warm and cozy." Mia felt sorry for the giant. She said, "Oh, I see. That is sad. Maybe we can help you find your hat. Where did you last see it?" The giant pointed to the other side of the park. He said, "It was over there, by the pond. But I can't find it. It is too small for me." Mia had an idea. She said, "Maybe Tom and I can find it. We are small. We can look better. Can you wait here? We will be back soon." The giant nodded. He said, "Thank you, little girl. You are very kind. What is your name?" Mia smiled. She said, "My name is Mia. And this is Tom. He is my friend. We will be right back." Mia ran to the tree and told Tom her plan. Tom agreed to help. They both ran to the pond and looked for the hat. They found it on a duck. The duck was wearing the hat and swimming in the water. They asked the duck nicely to give them the hat. The duck agreed. They took the hat and ran back to the giant. They gave the hat to the giant. The giant was very happy. He put the hat on his head and said, "Thank you, Mia and Tom. You are very good friends. You made me glad. I am sorry for breaking the park. I will fix it. Can I play with you?" Mia and Tom said yes. They were glad too. They played with the giant. They taught him how to slide and swing and kneel. They had a lot of fun. The giant was not angry and scary anymore. He was nice and gentle. They became friends. The end.
