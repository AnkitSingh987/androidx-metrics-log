Category: Beginner
Title: The Musician and the Played

Once upon a time, there was a musician. He played music on a white piano. The music was very pretty. The musician liked to play for his friends. One day, a little girl came to listen to the music. She liked the white piano. She had a question for the musician. "How do you play music on the white piano?" she asked. The musician smiled. "I press the keys," he said. He showed the girl how to press the keys. She tried it too. They played music together. The girl was happy. The musician was happy too. They played music for all their friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big case in his room. He was very curious about what was inside. The case was old and dusty. Tim felt a little bit scared, but he wanted to see what was inside. Tim's friend, Sue, came to visit. Sue was a brave girl. She urged Tim to open the case. "Let's open it," Sue said. Tim nodded and said, "Okay, let's do it together." They opened the case and saw a big, fluffy teddy bear inside. Tim and Sue were very happy. The scary case was not scary anymore. They played with the teddy bear all day long. They learned that sometimes, things that seem scary are not scary at all.

Sara was a gifted girl. She could sing, draw, and read very well. She liked to learn new things and show them to her family and friends. But sometimes, she felt very tired and bored. One day, she had to go to school. She did not want to go. She wanted to stay at home and play with her toys. She yawned and rubbed her eyes. She said to her mom, "I don't want to go to school. I want to sleep." Her mom smiled and hugged her. She said, "I know, Sara. But school is good for you. You can learn more things and have fun with your friends. And you can show your teacher how gifted you are. Come on, let's get ready." Sara sighed and got out of bed. She put on her clothes and shoes. She brushed her teeth and hair. She took her backpack and followed her mom to the car. She yawned again and leaned on the window. On the way to school, she saw a big dog. It was brown and fluffy. It was running and jumping in the park. It looked very happy and playful. Sara smiled and pointed at the dog. She said, "Look, mom. A dog. Can we have a dog?" Her mom looked at the dog and said, "Maybe, Sara. But dogs need a lot of care and love. They need food, water, toys, and walks. They also need a vet and a groomer. Are you ready to take care of a dog?" Sara thought for a moment. She said, "Yes, mom. I am ready. I can feed the dog, and play with it, and read to it. I can be a good friend to the dog. Please, mom. Can we have a dog?" Her mom smiled and said, "We'll see, Sara. We'll see. Maybe for your birthday, you can have a dog. But for now, you have to go to school. Look, we're here." Sara looked at the school. She saw her teacher and her friends. They waved at her and said, "Hi, Sara. Come and join us. We have a surprise for you." Sara got out of the car and hugged her mom. She said, "Bye, mom. I love you. Thank you for taking me to school." Her mom said, "Bye, Sara. I love you too. Have a good day. I'll pick you up later." Sara ran to her teacher and her friends. She was curious about the surprise. She forgot about her yawn and her sleep. She was ready to learn and have fun. She was a gifted girl.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw her friend, Tom, playing with his toys. Tom was very organized. He had all his toys in neat rows. Sue liked to watch Tom play. Sue started to stare at Tom's toys. She saw a big red ball that she wanted to play with. But she didn't want to mess up Tom's organized toys. Sue thought of a plan. Sue ran to the other side of the park and found a stick. She threw the stick far away. Tom saw the stick and ran to get it. While Tom was gone, Sue took the big red ball and played with it. When Tom came back, he was surprised to see Sue playing with the ball. They both laughed and played together.
