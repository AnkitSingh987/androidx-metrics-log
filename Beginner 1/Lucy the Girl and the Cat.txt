Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to go on journeys with her dog, Spot. One day, Lucy insisted they go on a journey to find a big, red ball. As they walked, they met a bitter old cat. The cat said, "You should not go on this journey. It's not safe for little girls and dogs." But Lucy didn't listen and kept going. Spot barked, but he followed her too. In the end, Lucy and Spot found the big, red ball, but it was in a deep hole. Lucy fell into the hole and couldn't get out. Spot tried to help, but he couldn't. The bitter old cat came and said, "I told you it was not safe." The moral of the story is to listen to those who try to keep you safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a big box. The box was open. Inside the box, Tim saw a toy car. He wanted to use the toy car for his work. Tim took the toy car to his mom. He said, "Mom, can I use this car for my work?" Mom looked at the toy car and smiled. She said, "Yes, Tim. You can use the toy car for your work. But first, let's put your other toys away." Tim and his mom put all the toys away. Then, Tim used the toy car for his work. He pushed the car around the room. He made the car go fast and slow. Tim was very happy. His mom was happy too. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Mom, thank you for letting me use the toy car for my work. I had so much fun!" Mom hugged Tim and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad you had fun with your work today." They both smiled and went to bed.

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play together. One day, they found a big box in the garage. They thought it was a good place to hide and pretend. "Let's be pirates and sail the seas!" Ben said. "Okay, but I want to be the captain!" Sara said. They climbed into the box and used a broom as a mast. They put a blanket over the box to make a sail. They pretended to see sharks and islands and treasure. "Look, there's a ship! Let's attack it and take their gold!" Ben shouted. "Repeat, repeat, what did you say?" Sara asked. She could not hear him well under the blanket. "I said, there's a ship! Let's attack it and take their gold!" Ben repeated. "Oh, okay. But be careful, they might have cannons!" Sara said. They made loud noises and waved their arms. They had a lot of fun. Soon, their mom came to the garage. She saw the box moving and heard the noises. She lifted the blanket and smiled. "What are you two doing in there?" she asked. "We're pirates, mom! We're sailing the seas and fighting other ships!" Ben said. "That sounds very exciting. But it's time for lunch now. Come out of the box and wash your hands," mom said. "Can we play pirates again after lunch?" Sara asked. "Sure, if you want to. But first, you have to put the box and the broom and the blanket back where you found them. And you have to say thank you to dad for giving you the box. He was very thoughtful to save it for you," mom said. "Okay, mom. Thank you, dad!" Sara and Ben said. They got out of the box and hugged their mom. They ran to the kitchen to eat their lunch. They were happy and hungry. They liked to play pirates, but they also liked to eat.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Amy loved to dream. She had a big screen in her room where she watched her favorite shows. One day, Amy saw a foolish bird outside her window. The bird was trying to fly but kept falling. Amy went outside and said, "Hello, bird! Why are you so foolish? You need to fly up high, not fall down." The bird looked at Amy and said, "I am trying, but I don't know how to fly." Amy wanted to help the bird, so she had an idea. She took the bird inside and put it in front of the screen. They watched a show about birds flying. The bird looked at the screen and started to flap its wings. Suddenly, the bird began to fly! It flew around the room and then back to Amy. "Thank you, Amy! You helped me learn to fly!" said the bird. Amy was so happy that her screen and dreams helped the bird. From that day on, Amy and the bird were best friends.
