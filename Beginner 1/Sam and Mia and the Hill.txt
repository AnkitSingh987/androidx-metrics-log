Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Mia and the Hill

Sam and Mia were playing in the sand. They had a bucket and a sack. They wanted to make a big castle with towers and flags. "Look, I made a tower!" Sam said. He used the bucket to make a round shape. He put it on top of a pile of sand. "That's good, Sam. But we need more sand. Can you fill the sack?" Mia asked. She was making a flag with a stick and a leaf. Sam nodded. He took the sack and went to find more sand. He saw a big hill of sand near the water. He ran to it. He was impatient. He wanted to finish the castle. But when he got to the hill, he saw a big sign. It said: "Do not touch. This is for the workers. They are building a new road." Sam did not understand the sign. He did not care. He opened the sack and started to scoop sand into it. He filled it up quickly. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. He looked up and saw a big truck coming. It was carrying more sand for the hill. The driver saw Sam and shouted. "Hey, kid! What are you doing? That's my sand! Get away from there!" Sam was scared. He dropped the sack and ran back to Mia. He left the bucket on the hill. "Mia, Mia, we have to go!" he said. "There is a big truck and a man. He is angry. He said this is his sand." Mia looked at Sam. She saw his face was white. She saw he had no bucket and no sack. "What? Where is our bucket and sack? Where is the sand?" she asked. Sam pointed at the hill. Mia saw the bucket on top of it. She saw the sack on the ground. She saw the truck and the man. "Oh, no. Sam, you took the wrong sand. That's not for us. That's for the road. We have to say sorry. Come on, let's go." Mia took Sam's hand. They walked to the truck and the man. They were afraid. But they knew they had to be good. "Excuse me, sir. We are sorry. We did not know this was your sand. We wanted to make a castle. Can we have our bucket and sack back?" Mia said. The man looked at them. He was still angry. But he saw they were small and sorry. He sighed. "Okay, okay. You can have your bucket and sack. But don't touch my sand again. Go find another place to play. And be careful. This is a dangerous area." He gave them their bucket and sack. He watched them go. He shook his head. "Kids. They have no idea." he said. He got back in his truck and drove away. Sam and Mia ran to another spot. They found some sand that was not for the road. They started to make their castle again. They were happy. They learned a lesson. They did not touch the wrong sand again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One hot day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb. Tim went up, up, up the tree. As Tim climbed, he saw a shiny hook on a branch. He did not know what it was for, but he thought it was neat. Tim kept climbing higher and higher. He wanted to see the whole park from the top of the tree. When Tim got to the top, he found a big kite stuck in the tree. He thought, "That's what the hook is for!" Tim used the hook to get the kite out of the tree. He climbed back down with the kite in his hands. Tim was so happy that he found the kite. He ran to show his mom and dad. They were proud of him for being brave and smart. Tim played with the kite all day long, and it was his favorite toy from that day on.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max was a happy dog who loved to play. One day, Max went to the park with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They played and ran around all day. As they played, Max said, "Lily, I am hungry." Lily thought about what would be delicious for Max to eat. She remembered that Max loved bones. "I have a bone for you at home," she told Max. Max wagged his tail, excited for the delicious bone. They went back to Lily's house, and Lily gave Max the bone. Max thought the bone was very delicious. He chewed on it happily. The next time they went to the park, Max knew that a delicious bone would be waiting for him when they got home.
