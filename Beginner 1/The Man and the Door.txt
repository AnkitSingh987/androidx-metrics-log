Category: Beginner
Title: The Man and the Door

Once upon a time there was a man who lived in a big city. One day, he was walking down the street when he noticed something sour. It was coming from the jail! The man heard someone crying inside. He knew he had to help! The man went to the jail and knocked on the door. "What do you want?" asked a deep voice. "I want to help," said the man. The door opened and a small boy stepped out. He was only three years old and he was crying. The man gave him a hug and asked what was wrong. The boy said, "I was in jail because I didn't do what my dad said. But now I'm so scared." The man promised the boy that he would help him. He took him home and promised to be his friend. The boy was so happy that he had someone to help him. From that day on, the man helped the boy whenever he needed it. Life became much easier for the boy, and he no longer had to live in fear.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw an old man with a big box. The old man said, "Hello! Would you like to see what's inside my box?" Tim was excited and said, "Yes, please!" The old man opened the box and took out a toy top. The top was very pretty with many colors. The old man showed Tim how to spin the top. Tim tried to spin it too, but it was hard. Tim's mom said, "Don't give up! Try again." Tim tried to spin the top again, and this time it worked! The top spun round and round, and Tim felt a shock of joy. He laughed and clapped his hands. The old man smiled and said, "Good job, Tim!"

Once upon a time, there was a powerful wolf. He lived in the woods. He was big and strong. All the animals were scared of him. One day, the powerful wolf saw a little bunny. The bunny was eating a carrot. The wolf wanted the carrot. He said, "Give me your carrot, bunny." The bunny was scared, but he did not yield. He said, "No, Mr. Wolf, this is my carrot." The powerful wolf was not happy. He tried to take the carrot, but the bunny was fast. The bunny ran away with the carrot. The wolf was sad. He learned that being powerful does not always mean you get what you want.

Tim and Lily were playing in the park with their dog, Spot. They liked to run and chase and throw the ball for Spot. Spot was very fast and happy and always came back with the ball. One day, they saw a big truck on the road. It was loud and scary and made smoke. Spot did not like the truck. He barked and ran towards it. Tim and Lily shouted, "Spot, no! Come back!" But Spot did not hear them. He ran in front of the truck and the truck hit him. Tim and Lily ran to Spot. He was lying on the ground. He did not move or bark or breathe. He was dead. Tim and Lily cried and hugged Spot. They were very sad and scared. A man came out of the truck. He looked at Spot and said, "I'm sorry, kids. I didn't see him. He was too fast. It was an accident." He tried to comfort Tim and Lily, but they did not want to talk to him. They wanted Spot to be alive. Their mom came to the park. She saw Spot and Tim and Lily. She knew what happened. She cried too. She hugged Tim and Lily and said, "I'm sorry, my loves. Spot was a good dog. He loved you very much. He is in a better place now. He is not in pain. He is happy." She comforted Tim and Lily and took them home. She said they would bury Spot in the garden and make a stone with his name. She said they would always remember him and he would always be in their hearts.
