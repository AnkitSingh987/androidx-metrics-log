Category: Beginner
Title: The Cliff and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a big cliff near a small town. On top of the cliff, there was a cute little house. In the house, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom liked to own many things. He had a lot of cheap toys and games. One day, Tom met a little boy named Tim. Tim saw Tom's cheap toys and said, "Wow! You have so many toys! Can I play with them?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with my toys. I own them, but I like to share." So, Tom and Tim played with the toys all day. They had a lot of fun on the cliff by the little house. They became good friends and played together every day. And they always shared their cheap toys with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Bounce. Bounce loved to bounce all day long. One day, Bounce saw a big pot in the yard. The pot was round and shiny. Bounce thought it would be fun to play with the pot. Bounce was persistent. He tried to jump into the pot, but it was too high. Bounce asked his friend, Bird, for help. "Bird, can you help me get into the pot?" Bounce said. Bird was happy to help. Bird picked up Bounce with her beak and dropped him into the pot. Inside the pot, Bounce had lots of fun. He bounced up and down, making loud sounds. Bounce was so happy that he asked Bird to join him. Bird flew into the pot, and they both played together. They laughed and had a great time. Bounce and Bird were very happy friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a blue puppy. The puppy was lost and sad. Tim wanted to help the blue puppy find his home. Tim and the blue puppy walked and walked. They saw a girl named Sue. Sue said, "I lost my puppy! Did you see him?" Tim said, "We found him! He is blue and very sad." Sue was happy to see her blue puppy again. But the blue puppy did not want to go with Sue. He wanted to stay with Tim. Tim and Sue did not know what to do. They both loved the blue puppy. So they decided to watch the puppy and see what he wanted. The puppy walked to Tim and Sue and licked their hands. They knew the puppy wanted to be friends with both of them. From that day on, Tim, Sue, and the blue puppy played together and were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to polish his toys. He made them shiny and clean. Tim had a map on his wall. The map showed a big park near his house. One day, Tim went to the park. He saw a dead tree. The tree had no leaves. Tim felt sad for the tree. He wanted to help it. Tim went home and got his polish. He polished the dead tree. The tree became shiny and pretty. Tim felt happy. The tree was still dead, but now it looked nice. Tim and his friends played near the tree every day.

Sam and Mia were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they found a big cliff near their house. They wanted to see what was on the other side of the cliff. "Let's put some rocks on the edge of the cliff and see how far they fall," Sam said. "OK, but be careful. The cliff is very high and steep," Mia said. They picked up some small rocks and threw them over the cliff. They heard them splash in the water below. "Wow, that's fun!" Sam said. He saw a bigger rock that was very smooth and shiny. He thought it was attractive. "Look, Mia, this rock is so pretty. I want to put it on the cliff too," he said. He lifted the rock and carried it to the edge of the cliff. But the rock was too heavy for him and he lost his balance. He started to fall with the rock. "Sam, no!" Mia shouted. She ran to the cliff and grabbed his hand. She pulled him back to safety. "Sam, are you OK? That was very scary. You could have hurt yourself," she said. "I'm OK, Mia. Thank you for saving me. You are a good friend. I'm sorry I was silly. I don't want to put rocks on the cliff anymore. Let's go home and play with our toys," he said. They hugged each other and walked back to their house. They learned a lesson that day. They learned to be careful and not to play with dangerous things. They also learned to appreciate each other's friendship.
