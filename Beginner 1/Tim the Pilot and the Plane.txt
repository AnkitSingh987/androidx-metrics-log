Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Pilot and the Plane

Once, there was a pilot named Tim. He had a unique job. Tim flew a big plane in the sky. One day, Tim wanted to spread some joy. He thought of a fun way to do it. He put paint on the wings of his plane. Tim flew his plane up in the sky. He made colorful lines with the paint. The kids on the ground looked up and smiled. They loved the fun colors in the sky. Tim was happy to spread joy with his unique plane. He knew he made the kids smile. And that made Tim smile too.

One day, Tom and his mom went to the park. The park was big and modern. There were swings, slides, and a sand area. Tom loved to play at the park. While Tom was playing, he saw a big branch on the ground. He wanted to snap it. He tried and tried, but the branch was too strong. Then, a nice dog came and helped Tom. With the dog's help, they snapped the branch. But when the branch snapped, something unexpected happened. A secret door opened in the park! Tom, his mom, and the dog went inside. They found a room full of toys and ice cream. They played and ate ice cream all day. The park was even more fun than they thought.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to perform tricks for his family. They would clap and cheer when he did a trick. Spot was very happy when he could make his family smile. One day, Spot saw a sausage on the table. It was big and yummy. He wanted to eat it, but he knew it was not his. Spot thought of a useful idea. He decided to perform a trick to get the sausage. Spot barked and jumped high in the air. His family saw him and laughed. They thought he was so good at performing tricks. They gave Spot the sausage as a treat. Spot was very happy, and he knew that being useful made his family happy too.

One day, a big dog named Max saw a big billboard. The billboard had a picture of a cat on it. Max did not like cats. He felt guilty because his friend, a little dog named Sam, liked cats. Max said to Sam, "Why do you like cats? They are not like us." Sam said, "I like them because they are fun to play with." Max yawned and looked at the billboard again. He did not want to play with a cat. One day, Max and Sam met a cat named Lucy. Lucy said, "Hi, do you want to play with me?" Sam said, "Yes!" but Max said, "No!" Sam and Lucy played together, and Max felt left out. Max felt guilty for not playing with Lucy. He saw Sam and Lucy having fun. Max said, "I am sorry, Lucy. Can I play with you too?" Lucy said, "Yes, of course!" They all played together and had a great time. Max learned that it is okay to like different things.

Sam and Mia are playing in the park. They like to pretend they are spies and chase each other. Sam has a fake watch that makes noises. Mia has a fake phone that lights up. "Stop, spy!" Sam shouts, pointing his watch at Mia. "You are under arrest!" Mia laughs and runs away. She hides behind a big tree. She looks at her phone and pretends to call for help. "Hello, taxi?" she says. "I need a ride. I am a spy and someone is after me." Sam hears her and sneaks up behind her. He taps her on the shoulder and says, "Freeze, spy! I got you!" Mia screams and drops her phone. She turns around and sees Sam smiling. She smiles back and says, "You are a good spy, Sam. But I have a trick up my sleeve." She tickles Sam and makes him laugh. They fall to the ground and hug each other. They are happy and have fun. They are not real spies, but they have real friends.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big hole in the ground. It was a pit. They wanted to look inside. "Be careful," Lily said. "The pit is deep and dark. You might fall in." "I'm not scared," Tom said. "I want to see what's in there. Maybe there's a treasure or a dinosaur." He ran to the edge of the pit and leaned over. He saw nothing but dirt and rocks. He was disappointed. He wanted to go closer. "Tom, come back!" Lily shouted. "It's not safe. You could slip and hurt yourself." But Tom did not listen. He took one more step and then he felt the ground move. He lost his balance and fell into the pit. He screamed. Lily was very worried. She ran to the pit and looked down. She saw Tom lying on the ground. He was not moving. She did not know what to do. "Help! Help!" she cried. "My friend is in the pit. He needs help. Please, someone, help!" A man heard her cry and came to see what was wrong. He saw the pit and Tom. He was serious. "This is bad," he said. "We need to call an ambulance. Stay here and don't move. I'll be right back." He ran to his car and got his phone. He called the ambulance and told them what happened. He came back to Lily and tried to calm her down. "Don't worry," he said. "The ambulance is coming. They will help your friend. He will be okay." Lily hoped he was right. She was very scared and sad. She wished she and Tom had never gone to the pit. She wished they had played somewhere else. She wished Tom was with her. She prayed for him to be okay.
