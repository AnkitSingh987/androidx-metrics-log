Category: Beginner
Title: Lily Finds the Bear

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a lot of toys, but her favorite was a big teddy bear. She hugged him and talked to him every day. She called him Ben. One day, Lily's mom made a yummy meal for lunch. She put some soup, bread, cheese and fruit on a tray and brought it to Lily's room. She said, "Lily, it's time to eat. Come and have your meal. You can play with Ben later." But Lily did not want to stop playing. She said, "No, mom. I want to play with Ben. He is hungry too. He needs a meal." She took the tray from her mom and put it on the floor. She gave some soup to Ben and said, "Here, Ben. Drink this. It will make you warm and happy." But Ben was not a real bear. He was a toy. He could not drink the soup. The soup spilled on him and made him wet and sticky. Lily did not notice. She gave him some bread and cheese and said, "Here, Ben. Eat this. It will make you strong and healthy." But Ben could not eat the bread and cheese. He was a toy. The bread and cheese crumbled on him and made him dirty and smelly. Lily did not notice. She gave him some fruit and said, "Here, Ben. Have this. It will make you sweet and juicy." But Ben could not have the fruit. He was a toy. The fruit squished on him and made him soggy and stained. Lily did not notice. She was happy to share her meal with Ben. She said, "See, mom. Ben and I are having a great meal. We are full and satisfied." But Lily's mom was not happy. She saw the mess on the floor and on Ben. She said, "Lily, what have you done? You have wasted your meal and ruined your toy. Ben is not a real bear. He does not need a meal. He needs to be clean and dry. You need to eat your meal and take care of your things." Lily looked at Ben and saw how wet, dirty, smelly, soggy and stained he was. She felt sad and sorry. She said, "Oh, mom. I'm sorry. I did not know. I wanted to be nice to Ben. I love him so much." Lily's mom hugged her and said, "I know, Lily. I love you too. But you have to learn to be sensible and responsible. You have to eat your meal and keep your toys in good shape. You can still love Ben, but you have to treat him like a toy, not like a real bear." Lily nodded and said, "Okay, mom. I understand. I will eat my meal and clean Ben. I will be more careful next time." Lily's mom smiled and said, "That's good, Lily. I'm proud of you. Come on, let's go to the kitchen and get a new tray. And then we can wash Ben and put him in the dryer. He will be fine. He will still be your big teddy bear." Lily and her mom went to the kitchen and got a new tray. They ate their meal and enjoyed it. Then they washed Ben and put him in the dryer. He came out clean and fluffy. Lily hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I love you. You are my best friend." Ben did not say anything. He was a toy. But he felt warm and cozy in Lily's arms. He was happy to be her big teddy bear. The moral of the story is: You should eat your meal and take care of your toys. You can love them, but you have to treat them right.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a long tail that he liked to wag. One day, Spot saw a big box in his house. He wanted to unpack the box and see what was inside. As Spot started to unpack the box, he found a big, disgusting bug. The bug was green and slimy. Spot was scared of the bug, but he wanted to be brave. So, he tried to chase the bug away with his tail. But the disgusting bug jumped on Spot's tail and bit him. Spot cried out in pain and ran away from the bug. He was sad that he could not chase the bug away. In the end, Spot had a sore tail and the bug stayed in the house.
