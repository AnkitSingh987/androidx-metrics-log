Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to build things. He would build big towers and little houses with his toy blocks. One day, Tim wanted to build something new. He wanted to build a store with a staff and an open sign. Tim asked his mom, "Can I build a store with a staff and an open sign?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim! That's a great idea. Let's build it together." So they started to build the store with the toy blocks. They made a counter, shelves, and a door. When they finished, Tim put a sign on the door that said "open". He put his toys on the shelves to be the staff. Tim and his mom played store all day. They had fun and were happy with their new store. And that night, Tim went to bed dreaming of more fun things to build.

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big bird in the sky. The bird is black and white and has a long neck. "Look, Lily, look!" Ben says. He points at the bird. "What is that?" Lily looks at the bird. She does not know what it is. She is confused. She shakes her head. "I don't know, Ben. It is not a duck. It is not a crow. It is not a pigeon. What is it?" They hear a voice behind them. It is their mom. She is smiling. "That is a goose, kids. A goose. It is a kind of bird that can fly and swim. Sometimes they make a loud noise like this: honk, honk!" She makes the noise with her mouth. Lily and Ben laugh. They try to make the noise too. "Honk, honk, honk!" they say. The goose hears them. It looks at them. It flaps its wings. It flies away. "Bye, bye, goose!" Lily and Ben say. They wave their hands. They are happy. They learned a new word today.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big machine in the park. It has many buttons and lights. "Wow, what is this?" Lily asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is a magic machine. Let's try it," Tom says. They push some buttons and pull some levers. The machine makes funny noises and flashes. Lily and Tom laugh and clap. Suddenly, the machine stops. A door opens and a robot comes out. The robot is shiny and has a smile. "Hello, I am Robo. I am a good robot. I can do many things. Do you want to be my friends?" the robot says. "Yes, we want to be your friends. You are cool," Lily and Tom say. "Thank you. You are nice. I like you. Can I play with you?" the robot says. "Of course. We have many toys. You can choose one," Lily and Tom say. They show the robot their toys. The robot likes the ball, the car, the doll and the book. He plays with them and learns new words. Lily and Tom are happy. They have a new friend. The robot is happy too. He has fun and learns new things. They play until it is dark. Then they say goodbye to the robot. "Bye, Robo. We will come back tomorrow. We want to play with you again," Lily and Tom say. "Bye, Lily and Tom. I will wait for you. You are my best friends. I want to play with you again too," the robot says. The robot goes back to the machine. He closes the door. He bows to Lily and Tom. Lily and Tom bow back. They smile and wave. They run to their homes. They tell their moms and dads about the robot. They are very excited. They can't wait for tomorrow.

Lily liked to paint. She had many colors and brushes. She painted flowers, trees, animals and people. She painted what she saw and what she dreamed. One morning, she woke up very early. She looked out the window and saw a beautiful sunrise. The sky was pink and orange and yellow. She wanted to paint it. She ran to her room and got her paints and paper. She went back to the window and started to paint. She was very happy. She mixed the colors and made the shapes. She painted the sun and the clouds and the birds. She smiled and hummed a song. She wanted to show her painting to her mom and dad. She finished her painting and ran to their room. She knocked on the door and opened it. She saw them sleeping in their bed. She said, "Mom, dad, look at my painting!" But they did not wake up. They did not hear her. They did not see her. They did not smell her. They did not smell her painting. They did not smell the disgusting smell of the paint. They did not smell the paint that Lily had used. The paint that was not for painting. The paint that was for the walls. The paint that was toxic. The paint that had made Lily sick. The paint that had made Lily die.
