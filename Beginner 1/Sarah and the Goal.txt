Category: Beginner
Title: Sarah and the Goal

Once upon a time there was a smart little girl called Sarah. She wanted to achieve her goal, so she could be the best she can be. She asked her Mom for help on how to reach her goal. Her Mom said, “Sarah, if you want to make your goal, you need to fill your bucket with lots of little things.” Sarah was confused and asked, “Mommy, what do you mean?” Her Mom said, “Well, every day you can do something small that will help you reach your goal. Fill your bucket with lots of smaller goals and bit by bit they will fill up until you reach your big goal.” Sarah was excited and said, “I'm smart enough to fill my bucket with lots of small goals! I can do it!” And that's just what Sarah did. Slowly but surely, her bucket filled up and she eventually reached her goal. And she was so proud of herself. The end.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. She lived in a small house with a big yard. Sue loved to play in her yard with her cat, Tom. One day, Sue wanted to lay down in the grass. But, she saw many leaves on the ground. She thought, "I need to clean this up first." So, she took her rake and started to clean the leaves. Tom, the cat, watched her work. While Sue was raking, a naughty squirrel came and threw more leaves on the ground. Sue was sad and said, "Oh no! I need to clean this up again." Tom, the cat, saw the squirrel and chased it away. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Then, Sue and Tom lay down on the clean grass and enjoyed the sunny day.

Sam and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog has long hair and sharp teeth. The dog makes a loud sound. It sounds wild. "Look, a dog!" Sam says. "I want to pet it." "No, Sam, don't!" Mia says. "The dog is wild. It can bite you. We should stay away." But Sam does not listen. He runs to the dog. He tries to touch its hair. The dog does not like it. The dog growls and snaps at Sam. It bites his hand. Sam cries out in pain. "Help, help!" Sam shouts. "The dog bit me!" Mia runs to Sam. She sees blood on his hand. She is scared. She calls for help. A man comes. He takes Sam and Mia to a doctor. The doctor gives Sam a bandage and a shot. He says Sam has to be careful with wild animals. Sam is sorry. He learned a lesson. He should have listened to Mia. He should not have touched the wild dog. He says to Mia, "I'm sorry, Mia. You were right. I was wrong. I will not do it again." Mia is glad. She says to Sam, "It's okay, Sam. I'm glad you are okay. I hope you feel better soon. Next time, we will play with nice animals." The moral of the story is: Listen to your friends. They can help you stay safe. Do not touch wild animals. They can hurt you.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They would run, jump, and laugh together all day long. One day, Sam and Sue found a big ball. Sam wanted to teach Sue how to play with the ball. He said, "Sue, watch me." Sam slapped the ball with his paw, and it rolled far away. Sue clapped her hands and laughed. Sue tried to slap the ball, too. At first, she missed, but Sam said, "Try again, Sue!" She tried again and again, and soon she could slap the ball just like Sam. They played with the ball until the sun went down, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man named Max. One day, he went to the park to enjoy the nice day. Max decided to sit down on a bench and relax. Suddenly, there was a loud cracking noise and a small boy came running up to Max. His small toe had struck a rock when he was running and it really hurt. Max looked down at the little boy and said, “Oh my, I can see that your toe is really hurting! Can I help you?” The little boy nodded with tears in his eyes. Max was very gentle, and he was able to kiss away the pain. After a few kisses and some gentle rubs, the boy's toe was feeling better. He smiled and thanked Max for all his help. Max smiled back and said, “Don't forget to be careful next time!” The little boy agreed and then ran off to play. Max was happy to be able to help the little boy. He admired the little boy's spirit and determination. Max was thankful to be an elderly man with lots of experience and wisdom to share.
