Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Jen and the Mug

Tom and Jen were playing in the kitchen. They saw a big mug on the table. It was Mom's mug. She liked it very much. It had flowers and her name on it. "Let's mark the mug!" Tom said. He had a marker in his hand. He wanted to draw on the mug. "No, Tom, don't do that!" Jen said. She knew Mom would be angry. "Mom loves her mug. She will be sad if you mark it." But Tom did not listen. He laughed and drew a big line on the mug. He thought it was funny. He drew a face and a star and a heart. "Look, Jen, I made the mug pretty!" Tom said. He showed Jen his work. Jen gasped. She saw the mug was ruined. She was scared. She tried to wipe off the marker, but it did not come off. "Tom, you are bad!" Jen said. She was mad at Tom. "You marked Mom's mug. She will be very mad at you. She will not love you anymore." Tom looked at the mug. He saw what he had done. He felt ashamed. He wished he had not marked the mug. He wanted Mom to love him. But it was too late. Mom came into the kitchen. She saw the mug. She saw the marker. She saw Tom and Jen. She was very mad. She yelled at Tom. She took away his marker. She sent him to his room. She did not hug him. She did not say she loved him. Tom cried. He was sorry. He was ashamed. He had marked Mom's mug. He had made Mom sad. He had a bad ending.

Ben and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to run on the grass, swing on the swings, and slide on the slide. They liked to see the birds, the flowers, and the trees. They liked the green nature. One day, they saw a big fence around the park. There was a sign that said, "No more park. We are building here." Ben and Lily were sad. They wanted to play in the park. They wanted to see the green nature. They tried to find another park, but they could not. All the parks were gone. They only saw houses, shops, and cars. They only saw gray and brown. They missed the green nature. They asked their mom and dad, "Why are they building so much? Where is the green nature?" Their mom and dad said, "They need more space for people. They don't care about the green nature. They only care about money." Ben and Lily were angry. They did not like the people who built so much. They did not like the people who did not care about the green nature. They wanted to stop them. They wanted to save the green nature. They made a plan. They took some seeds, some water, and some dirt. They waited until night. They sneaked out of their house. They went to the fence around the park. They dug some holes under the fence. They planted the seeds. They watered them. They hoped they would grow. But they did not grow. The next day, the people who built came. They saw the holes under the fence. They saw the seeds. They were angry. They threw away the seeds. They filled the holes. They put more fence. They said, "No more kids. We are building here." Ben and Lily were sad. They cried. They could not save the green nature. They could not play in the park. They could only remain in their house. They could only see gray and brown. They hated the people who built so much. They hated the people who did not care about the green nature. They hated the bad ending.

Once there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a spoiled pup and liked to get into trouble all the time. His parents were getting very frustrated with him and decided to get him a collar. The collar was plain and made of leather. One day, Spot's mom caught him digging in the garden. She scolded him and told him that he should not do that because it was very naughty. Then his mom put the collar on him and warned him not to misbehave again. Spot thought he was too smart for the collar but soon found out he was wrong. Whenever he got into trouble, his mom pulled on the collar and said, "Bad boy! You must be punished!" Spot got scared and started to think twice before doing something naughty. Spot soon learned that it was better to be a good pup than a dirty one. Spot's parents were very proud and kept the collar on him as a reminder. Spot still gets in trouble but now knows better than to do something naughty and get punished again.
