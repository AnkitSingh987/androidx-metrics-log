Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Flower

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They saw a big red flower. It was very pretty. "Can I touch it?" Lily asked Tom. Tom shook his head. "No, Lily. That is a burn flower. It can hurt you." Lily was curious. She wanted to know more. "Why does it burn?" she asked. Tom shrugged. "I don't know. Mama said it is a burn flower. She said we should stay away from it." Lily nodded. She did not want to get hurt. But she still liked the flower. It was very bright and shiny. "Can we look at it?" she asked. Tom smiled. He liked Lily. She was his best friend. She was very adorable. "Okay, we can look at it. But we have to be careful. We have to stay far away from it." Tom and Lily walked slowly to the flower. They did not touch it. They just looked at it. They saw a little bee. It was flying around the flower. It was very busy. "Look, Tom. A bee!" Lily said. Tom looked. He saw the bee. He was not afraid of bees. He liked bees. They made honey. "Hello, bee!" Tom said. The bee heard Tom. It flew to him. It landed on his nose. Tom giggled. He felt the bee tickle his nose. Lily laughed. She thought Tom was very funny. The bee was friendly. It did not sting Tom. It just said hello. Then it flew away. It went back to the flower. Tom and Lily waved to the bee. They said goodbye. They were happy. They had a new friend. They decided to name the flower Bee Flower. They thought it was a better name than Burn Flower. They went back to their toys. They played some more. They had a lot of fun in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl named Lily. She loved to lift up things and see how strong she was. One day, Lily saw a small plant that looked very sad. The plant was not growing well because it did not have enough sun. Lily wanted to help the plant, so she tried to lift it up to the sun. But the plant was too heavy for her. She tried and tried, but she could not lift it. Lily felt sad and didn't know what to do. Then, her friend Tom came by and saw Lily feeling sad. Tom was strong and he wanted to help. Together, they tried to lift the plant again. This time, with Tom's help, they lifted the plant to a sunny spot. The plant was happy and started to grow. Lily and Tom were happy too because they helped the plant.

Lily liked to play with her mom's leather bag. It was big and brown and had many things inside. Lily wanted to see what was in the bag, so she tried to reach it on the shelf. But the shelf was too high for Lily, and she could not reach the bag. Lily had an idea. She saw a chair near the shelf. She pushed the chair to the shelf and climbed on it. Now she was taller and could reach the bag. She pulled the bag down from the shelf. But the bag was heavy and Lily lost her balance. She fell off the chair and the bag fell on the floor. The things inside the bag spilled out. There were keys, a phone, a wallet, a book, and a red lipstick. Lily heard her mom coming. She was scared and hid under the table. Her mom saw the mess on the floor and the chair by the shelf. She knew what Lily had done. She was angry and said, "Lily, what did you do? You know you are not allowed to touch my bag. You could have hurt yourself or broken something. Come out from under the table and say sorry." Lily came out from under the table. She was crying and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to see what was in your bag. I like your bag. It's leather and it smells good. Please don't be mad at me." Her mom sighed and hugged Lily. She said, "I'm sorry, too, Lily. I know you were curious, but you have to ask me before you touch my things. My bag is not a toy. It has important things that I need. Next time, if you want to see something, just ask me and I will show you. OK?" Lily nodded and said, "OK, mom. I love you." Her mom said, "I love you, too, Lily. Now let's clean up this mess and then we can read a book together. How about that?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, mom. That sounds good."
