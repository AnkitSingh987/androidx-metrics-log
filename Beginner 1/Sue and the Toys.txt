Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a rag doll that she loved very much. The rag doll had a big smile and two big eyes. Sue played with her rag doll every day. One day, Sue's mom told her that they needed to clean their house. Sue's room was very messy. There were toys everywhere. Sue's mom asked her to help put the toys away. Sue picked up her rag doll and put it in an empty box. When it was time for bed, Sue could not find her rag doll. She was very sad. Sue asked her mom to help her look for it. They looked all over the house, but could not find the rag doll. Sue's mom told her that they would look again in the morning. The next day, Sue and her mom looked for the rag doll again. They found it in the empty box with the other toys. Sue was so happy! She hugged her rag doll and told it she would never lose it again. From that day on, Sue always made sure her rag doll had a special place in her room.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy named Tom. He liked to play with his ball. One day, he was playing outside in the sun. While playing, Tom tripped on a rock and fell down. He was not hurt, but his ball rolled away. Tom got up and tried to catch the ball, but he was slow. His friends saw him fall and started to laugh. Tom laughed too because it was funny. In the end, Tom got his ball back and everyone had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin named Pip. Pip was a popular penguin. All his friends liked him. He was nice and liked to play games. One day, Pip found a magic stone. The stone could glow. It was very pretty. Pip showed the stone to his friend, Sam. Sam was a seal. Sam said, "Wow, that is a nice stone!" Pip and Sam played with the glowing stone. They had so much fun. They shared the stone with their other friends, too. All the animals in the land liked the glowing stone. And they all liked Pip, the popular penguin.

One day, a hungry boy named Tim found a big spoon. He wanted to eat with it. He saw a big bowl of yummy food on the table. Tim was very happy. Tim took the spoon and tried to load it with food. But the spoon was too big. The food fell off the spoon and onto the floor. Tim was sad. Tim tried again and again. But the food kept falling. His tummy was still hungry. In the end, Tim cried because he could not eat with the big spoon. The food was all gone, and he was still hungry.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily had a big toy box filled with many toys. She loved to play with them all day. But, Lily did not like to put her toys away. Her room was always messy. One day, Lily's mom came into her room and saw the mess. She let out a big sigh. "Lily," she said, "you need to put your toys away. If you don't, you might lose them." Lily did not listen and went outside to play. The next day, Lily wanted to play with her favorite toy, a little red cross. But, she could not find it anywhere. Lily was sad. She looked and looked but could not find her toy. Lily's mom saw her looking and said, "Remember, I told you to put your toys away." Lily felt bad for not listening to her mom. She decided to clean her room and put all her toys away. As she cleaned, she found her little red cross under her bed. Lily was so happy! From that day on, Lily always put her toys away. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and keep your things tidy.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play and share. But they also like cake. Today is their birthday. Mom made a big cake for them. It has chocolate and cream and candles. Mom puts the cake on the table. She says, "This is your birthday cake, Anna and Ben. You can have some after dinner. But don't touch it before. It is very hot and sweet. You have to wait." Anna and Ben nod. They want to be good. They go to their room. They play with their toys. They have a lot of toys. Some are old and some are new. They have a modern train. It can make sounds and move. They like the train a lot. But Ben is hungry. He thinks about the cake. He says, "Anna, I want some cake. It looks so yummy. Can we have a little bit? Mom won't know." Anna shakes her head. She says, "No, Ben. Mom said we have to wait. The cake is not for now. It is for later. We have to protect the cake. It is our birthday cake. We have to be patient." Ben pouts. He says, "But I'm hungry. And I'm curious. I want to see the cake. I want to smell the cake. I want to taste the cake. Just a little bit. Please, Anna." Anna says, "No, Ben. Don't be naughty. Come on, let's play with the train. It is fun. It is modern. It can make sounds and move. Look, it can go fast and slow. It can go left and right. It can go around and around. Let's play with the train, Ben." Ben looks at the train. He likes the train. He smiles. He says, "Okay, Anna. Let's play with the train. It is fun. It is modern. It can make sounds and move. But maybe later we can have some cake. Just a little bit." Anna says, "Maybe later, Ben. But not now. Now we play with the train. And we protect the cake. It is our birthday cake. We love the cake. And we love mom. And we love each other." They hug. They play with the train. They protect the cake. They are happy. They are twins. They are Anna and Ben.
