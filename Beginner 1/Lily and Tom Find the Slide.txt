Title: Lily and Tom Find the Slide

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a new slide. It is big and shiny. They want to try it. "Let's go on the new slide!" Lily says. "OK, but be careful. It looks high." Tom says. They run to the slide. Lily goes first. She climbs up the ladder. She sits on the slide. She slides down fast. She laughs. "Wow, that was fun!" Lily says. Tom climbs up the ladder. He sits on the slide. He slides down fast. He does not laugh. He screams. "Help! Help!" Tom says. Lily looks at Tom. He is not on the ground. He is in a trap. The trap is a big hole. It is under the slide. It is dark and deep. Tom is scared. "Tom, are you OK?" Lily says. "No, I'm not OK. I'm in a trap. I can't get out. I want to go home." Tom says. Lily runs to the trap. She tries to help Tom. She can't reach him. She cries. "Tom, I'm sorry. I don't know what to do. I can't help you." Lily says. She looks around. She does not see anyone. She is alone. She is scared. She wants to go home. She does not know where to go. She is lost.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big toy box in her room. One day, she was playing with her toys and felt dizzy. She sat down by the side of her toy box to rest. Amy's mom came into the room and saw her. "Why are you sitting down, Amy?" her mom asked. Amy said, "I feel dizzy, Mommy." Her mom hugged her and told her to rest for a while. After resting, Amy felt better. She went outside to play with her friend, Tom. While playing, Tom fell down and started to cry. Amy went to his side and said, "Don't cry, Tom. I will help you up." Tom smiled and they continued to play happily.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box. The box was empty. Tim was sad. He wanted to find what was inside the mystery box. He thought and thought about what to do. Tim had an idea. He would split the box into two pieces. He got help from his big sister, Sue. They pulled and pulled until the box was split. But there was still nothing inside. Tim and Sue were tired. They sat down and looked at the empty box. Then, they had a new idea. They used the box pieces to make a fun playhouse. They played and laughed all day. The mystery box was not empty anymore. It was full of fun and love.

One day, a little cat named Tim was walking in the park. He saw a strip of cloth on the ground. Tim felt very curious about the strip. He picked it up and decided to find its owner. As Tim walked, he met a big dog named Max. Max said, "I lost my strip! Thank you for finding it!" Tim felt grateful that he could help Max. They started to play together in the park. But then, a mean bird came and took the strip from them. The bird flew away with the strip. Tim and Max felt sad because they lost the strip. The moral of the story is to be careful with your things, or someone might take them away.

Once upon a time, there was a big cow named Bessie. Bessie lived on a warm farm with her friend, the farmer. The farmer loved to work hard every day. He took care of Bessie and all the other animals on the farm. One day, Bessie gave the farmer some milk. The farmer was very happy. He took the milk and made some cream. The farmer liked cream a lot. He put the cream on his pie and it tasted very good. Bessie and the farmer were very happy on the warm farm. They worked together every day. Bessie gave milk, and the farmer made cream. They were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.
