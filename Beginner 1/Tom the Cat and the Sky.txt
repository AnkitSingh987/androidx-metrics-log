Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Sky

One day, a friendly cat named Tom went outside to play. He looked up at the sky and saw pretty clouds. Tom wanted to print a picture of the sky to show his friends. Tom asked his friend, a bird named Sally, "Can you help me print a picture of the sky?" Sally said, "Yes, I can help you!" They went to find a way to print the picture. Together, Tom and Sally found a big piece of paper and some colors. They used their paws and wings to print the picture of the sky. When they were done, they showed it to all their friends. Everyone loved the picture and they all played together under the pretty sky.

One day, a cat named Tom went to the store. He wanted to buy some beef for dinner. The store was easy to find. It was next to a big tree. Tom walked into the store and saw a bell. He knew if he rang the bell, someone would come to help him. Tom rang the bell. A nice lady came to help him. "What do you need?" she asked. Tom said, "I want beef, please." The nice lady smiled and got the beef for Tom. She put it in a bag. Tom was very happy. He thanked the lady and went home. At home, Tom cooked the beef. It smelled very good. His friend, a dog named Jerry, came over. Jerry could smell the beef from far away. He asked Tom, "Can I have some beef too?" Tom said, "Yes, of course!" They both ate the beef and were very happy. They knew that ringing the bell at the store was the best thing they did that day.

One day, a polite cat named Sam went for a walk. He saw a big space where kids were playing. Sam was happy and wanted to play too. Sam saw a ball and ran to it. He heard a girl say, "Can you help me dry my hands?" Sam saw a towel and gave it to the girl. The girl said, "Thank you, cat!" But then, something unexpected happened! The ball Sam was playing with changed into a big cake! The kids and Sam were all surprised. They laughed and ate the cake together. The girl said, "This is the best day ever!"

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a restless boy named Tim. He loved to sort things all day long. One day, Tim found a big box of toys. He wanted to sort the toys by size and color. Tim started to sort the toys, but he got very tired. He needed some fuel to keep going. Tim went to the kitchen and found a big apple. He ate the apple and felt much better. Now he had the fuel to sort the toys. As Tim was sorting the toys, his little sister, Sue, came into the room. She wanted to play with the toys too. Tim did not want Sue to mess up his sorting. They both started to argue. Their mom heard the noise and came into the room. She told Tim and Sue to share the toys and sort them together. Tim and Sue worked together and sorted all the toys. They had a great time and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pupil named Tim. Tim was a small boy with clear, blue eyes. He liked to play ball with his friends in the park. One day, while playing ball, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. He thought he could save the cat. So, he tried to climb the tree. His friends were scared, but Tim wanted to help the cat. Tim went up and up, but he slipped and fell. He did not save the cat. Tim was hurt, and the cat was still in the tree. His friends were sad, and they all went home with a bad ending to their day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird had pink wings. It lived on a branch in a big tree. Every day, the bird would sing a happy song. All the other animals liked the bird's song. One day, a cat came to the tree. The cat wanted to play with the bird. The bird was scared. It did not want to play with the cat. The cat said, "Please don't be scared. I just want to be your friend." The bird thought about it and said, "Okay, let's be friends." Then, something unexpected happened. The cat helped the bird make the tree even more beautiful. They put pink flowers on the branch. All the animals came to see the pretty tree. The bird and the cat were very happy. They sang and played together every day. And they became the best of friends.
