Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Bird

One day, a dog named Spot was playing in the park. Spot was a tough dog, but he was also very nice. He saw a little bird who was sad. The bird said, "I can't fly! Can you help me, Spot?" Spot wanted to help the bird, but he didn't know how. Then, Spot had an idea. "Trust me, little bird," he said. "I will carry you on my back, and we will run fast. Maybe you can fly then!" The little bird trusted Spot and got on his back. Spot ran very fast, and the bird flapped its wings. Suddenly, the bird started to fly! The bird was so happy and thanked Spot for his help. The other animals saw what happened and learned that it's good to trust and help each other. The little bird and Spot became best friends, and they played together every day. And they all lived happily in the park.

One day, a big oak tree had many yummy fruits. A boy named Tim and a girl named Sue wanted to pick the fruits. They both wanted to load their baskets with the yummy fruits, but they could not reach them. Tim had an idea. He found a long stick and tried to poke the fruits down. But, the stick was too short. Sue tried to climb the tree, but she was too scared. They both were sad, and they could not get the fruits. Then, a big bird came and saw them. The bird wanted to help Tim and Sue. The bird flew up and picked the yummy fruits with its beak. The bird dropped the fruits into Tim and Sue's baskets. They were so happy and thanked the bird. The big bird, Tim, and Sue became friends and shared the yummy fruits.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a long line of kids waiting to play on the slide. Tim felt sad because he wanted to play on the slide too. He stood in the line and waited for his turn. While waiting, Tim saw a girl named Sue who was also sad. Tim asked her, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Tim smiled and said, "Let's look for it together after we play on the slide." Tim and Sue played on the slide and had fun. Then, they looked for Sue's toy but they could not find it. They searched everywhere in the park, but it was lost. Both Tim and Sue felt very sad. They went home without finding the toy, and the day ended with a bad feeling.

One day, Tom and Lily went to the park with their mom. They saw a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. They wanted to play with everything. "Can we go on the slide, mom?" Tom asked. "Yes, but be careful. Don't push or run," mom said. Tom and Lily climbed up the slide and slid down. They laughed and had fun. Then they went to the swing and took turns. They felt like they were flying. "Can we go to the sandbox, mom?" Lily asked. "Yes, but don't throw sand or eat it," mom said. Tom and Lily ran to the sandbox and started to make castles and cakes. They used sticks and stones and leaves to decorate them. They were very proud of their creations. But then, Tom saw something shiny and long in the sand. He picked it up and saw that it was a snake. He was scared and wanted to drop it. But he had a funny idea. "Look, Lily, I found a toy snake!" he said, holding the snake in his hand. "Wow, let me see!" Lily said, reaching for the snake. But the snake was not a toy. It was a real snake. And it did not like being held by Tom. It opened its mouth and bit Tom's finger. Tom screamed and let go of the snake. The snake slithered away into the grass. "Ow, ow, ow! Mom, help!" Tom cried, holding his finger. Mom heard Tom's cry and ran to him. She saw the bite mark and the snake. She was very angry and worried. "Tom, what did you do? That's not a toy snake, that's a real snake! It could be poisonous! Why did you lie to Lily?" she said, taking Tom to the car. "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to make a joke. I thought it was funny," Tom said, sobbing. "It's not funny, Tom. It's dangerous. You could have hurt yourself or Lily. You should never lie or play with wild animals. They are not toys. They have feelings and can hurt you. Do you understand?" mom said, driving to the hospital. "Yes, mom. I understand. I'm sorry. I won't lie or play with snakes again," Tom said, feeling sorry and scared. Mom hugged Tom and said, "I love you, Tom. But you have to be more careful and honest. Lying is bad and can cause trouble. Always tell the truth and respect nature. OK?" "OK, mom. I love you too," Tom said, hoping the snake was not poisonous.
