Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Blanket

One day, a boy named Tim and his mom went to the park for a picnic. They were very excited. Tim was impatient and wanted to eat right away, but his mom said they had to wait for his dad. When dad came, they all sat on the big blanket and ate yummy food. They had sandwiches, fruit, and juice. Tim was very happy. He said, "This is the best picnic ever!" After they ate, Tim wanted to play on the swings. He ran and jumped on the swing, but he fell off and hurt his knee. Tim began to weep. His mom and dad came to help him. Mom hugged Tim and said, "It's okay, Tim. You will feel better soon." Dad put a band-aid on Tim's knee. Tim stopped weeping and smiled. They all went back to the picnic and had a fun day at the park.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big fountain with water. The water goes up and down. It looks fun. "Let's go near the water!" Ben says. "OK, but be careful. Don't get wet!" Mia says. They run to the fountain. They watch the water. Sometimes the water is high. Sometimes the water is low. They try to touch the water. "Look, I can catch the water!" Ben says. He puts his hand in the water. He feels the water on his skin. He laughs. "Me too, me too!" Mia says. She puts her hand in the water. She feels the water on her skin. She laughs. They play with the water. They have fun. They forget the time. Then, something strange happens. The water disappears. The fountain stops. There is no water. "Where did the water go?" Ben asks. He looks around. He is confused. "I don't know. Maybe it is a magic fountain. Maybe the water will come back." Mia says. She looks around. She is curious. They wait for the water. They hope the water will come back. They are sad. Then, they hear a voice. It is a man. He works in the park. He is smart. He knows about the fountain. "Hello, kids. I'm sorry, but the fountain is broken. I have to fix it. The water will come back later. Please don't play here now. It is not safe." The man says. "Oh, I see. Thank you, mister. We will go somewhere else." Ben says. "Yes, thank you, mister. We will come back another day." Mia says. They say goodbye to the man. They leave the fountain. They look for another place to play. They are still friends. They are still happy.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a swing and ran to it. Mia loved to swing high in the sky. She felt like a bird flying. Mia saw a curious cat near the swing. The cat was looking at the sand. Mia got off the swing and touched the sand too. The sand had a funny texture. It was soft and a little wet. Mia and the curious cat played in the sand. They made a big hill together. Then, they went back to the swing and Mia swung high again. The cat watched and Mia laughed. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house near a bridge. The bridge was amazing because it was big and colorful. Lucy loved to play near the bridge every day. One day, Lucy found a big box near the bridge. She wanted to open it. She called her friend, Tom. "Tom, come see this big box!" she said. Tom came running to see the box. "Wow, it's so big!" said Tom. They both wanted to know what was inside. Lucy and Tom tried to open the box. Inside, they found a big, soft blanket. They decided to wrap the blanket around themselves and play near the bridge. They played all day, feeling warm and happy. The amazing bridge and the soft blanket made it the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named True. True loved to play at the park. Her favorite thing to do was swing on the swings. One day, True met a new friend at the park. His name was Tim. Tim was a bit shy, but he liked to swing too. True asked Tim, "Do you want to swing with me?" Tim said, "Yes, I would like that." As they were swinging, a terrible wind came. It blew the leaves and made the swings go very fast. True and Tim held on tight. The wind stopped, and they laughed. They had lots of fun swinging together. From that day on, True and Tim were best friends.
