Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim had a small nest in a big tree. He loved his nest very much. One day, it rained a lot. The rain made Tim's nest wet. Tim was sad. He wanted to make his nest dry and cozy again. So, Tim found a thin leaf from the tree. He used the leaf to wipe his nest. After Tim wiped his nest, it was dry and warm again. He was very happy. Tim learned that he could fix things by himself. And he lived happily in his nest ever after.

---

One day, a small seed was in the ground. It wanted to grow big and tall. The sun was shining, giving the seed light. The seed said, "I want to be a big tree!" The seed started to grow. It grew a little each day. The sky was clear, and the rain helped it grow too. The seed became a small plant. It was very happy. One day, the plant saw a big shadow. It was a big bird! The bird said, "I need a place to rest. Can I stay on you?" The plant was surprised but happy to help. The bird stayed on the plant, and they became friends. The plant was not a tree, but it was still special.

Once upon a time, there was a guard named Ben. Ben was a big and strong guard. He loved his job of guarding the door. One day, he felt very impatient. He wanted to do something fun. Ben saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was playing with her toy blocks. She was putting them in a line. Ben asked, "What are you doing, Lily?" Lily said, "I am making a number line. Do you want to play with me?" Ben was happy to play with Lily. He thought it would be fun. They played with the blocks together. They made a long number line. Ben was not impatient anymore. He had fun playing with Lily. They became good friends and played together every day.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a toy laser. It makes red light and funny sounds. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is black and brown. It has sharp teeth and loud bark. Tim and Sam are scared. They want to run away. But then, they have an idea. They use their toy laser to make the dog go away. They point the laser at the dog's eyes. The dog does not like the red light. It closes its eyes and whines. Tim and Sam skip away from the dog. They are happy and brave. They meet a girl in the park. Her name is Sue. She has a pink dress and a blue bike. She sees their toy laser. She likes it. She asks them if she can play with it. "Can I play with your laser, please?" she says. Tim and Sam are humble. They do not want to be mean. They share their toy laser with Sue. They take turns to make red light and funny sounds. They have fun together. They make a new friend.

One day, a fish named Fin wanted to swim in a wide pond. He was very happy to play with his friends. They liked to jump and have fun in the water. Near the pond, there was a big tree. A bird lived in that tree, and she liked to make dough. She would mix it and roll it. She loved to bake treats for her friends. One day, the bird made a big ball of dough. She wanted to share it with Fin and his friends. But, the dough ball was too big! It fell into the wide pond. The water became thick and sticky. Fin and his friends could not swim anymore. They were very sad and could not play in the pond again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play in the park. One day, she saw a tiny seed on the ground. Sue picked up the seed and put it in her palm. Sue wanted the seed to become a big tree. So, she dug a hole with her hands and put the seed in it. Every day, Sue watered the seed and waited for it to grow. One day, the seed grew into a small plant. Sue was very happy. One day, Sue's ball hit the plant and it fell over. Sue felt sorry for the plant. She picked it up and put it back in the ground. She took care of the plant and it grew bigger and stronger. Soon, the plant became a big tree with many leaves. Sue was very proud of her tree and played under it every day.
