Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rich

One day, a boy named Tim saw a rich man in a big car. The man had a shiny buckle on his belt. Tim wanted to be like the rich man. He thought if he had a buckle, he would be rich too. Tim asked the rich man, "Can I have your buckle?" The rich man said, "No, you cannot have my buckle. It is mine." Tim was sad and angry. He wanted the buckle so much. Tim made a plan to take the buckle. He waited until the rich man was not looking, and took the buckle. The rich man saw Tim and yelled, "Give it back!" Tim ran away, but he did not look where he was going. He fell and hurt his leg. Tim cried, and the rich man got his buckle back. Tim did not get what he wanted, and he was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to play in the yard. One day, Max saw a hose in the yard. The hose was long and green. Max picked up the hose with his mouth. He wanted to pass it to his friend, Sam the cat. Sam was small and white. Max walked over to Sam and dropped the hose at his feet. Sam looked at the hose and smiled. Together, Max and Sam played with the hose. They had a lot of fun. They ran, jumped, and passed the hose to each other. The sun was shining, and they were very happy. At the end of the day, Max and Sam were tired, but they knew they had a great time with the hose.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to dream about the stars and planets. One night, he had a dream that he went to a far away planet. The planet was very dirty. It had mud and dirt all over it. On the dirty planet, Tim met a happy alien. The alien said, "Hello, Tim! Welcome to my planet!" Tim said, "Hello, Mr. Alien! Your planet is very dirty. Can I help you clean it?" The alien was excited and said, "Yes, please! I would love your help." Tim and the alien started to clean the dirty planet. They picked up all the mud and dirt. But then, something unexpected happened. The dirt and mud turned into beautiful flowers and trees! The alien said, "Wow! I did not know my planet could be so pretty!" Tim smiled and said, "Sometimes, all it takes is a little help from a friend." And they lived happily ever after on the clean, pretty planet.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with cars and trucks. They have many colorful cars and trucks. They make a road with blocks and tape. They pretend to drive their cars and trucks on the road. One day, a new boy comes to play. His name is Sam. He has a big red truck. He wants to play with Tom and Mia. He says, "Hello, I am Sam. Can I play with you?" Tom and Mia say, "Yes, you can play with us. Welcome, Sam. We are playing with cars and trucks. Do you like cars and trucks?" Sam says, "Yes, I like cars and trucks. I have a big red truck. It is faster and stronger than your cars and trucks. Look, I can push them away with my truck." Sam drives his big red truck on the road. He pushes Tom and Mia's cars and trucks off the road. He makes a lot of noise. He says, "Beep, beep, move away. My truck is the best. Your cars and trucks are silly." Tom and Mia are sad and angry. They do not like Sam. They say, "Stop, Sam. You are not nice. You are not playing with us. You are ruining our road. You are making a lot of traffic. We do not want to play with you." Sam does not listen. He keeps driving his big red truck. He says, "I do not care. I do what I want. My truck is the best. Your road is boring." Tom and Mia have an idea. They take their cars and trucks and make a new road. They make a bridge with blocks and tape. They drive their cars and trucks on the bridge. They say, "Look, Sam. We have a new road. It is better than your road. It has a bridge. Our cars and trucks are happy. They do not need your truck." Sam sees Tom and Mia's new road. He sees their bridge. He wants to drive his big red truck on the bridge. He says, "Hey, let me play with you. I want to drive on your bridge. It looks fun." Tom and Mia say, "No, Sam. You cannot play with us. You are not nice. You ruined our road. You made a lot of traffic. You have to say sorry first." Sam thinks for a moment. He feels sorry. He says, "I am sorry, Tom and Mia. I was not nice. I ruined your road. I made a lot of traffic. Can I play with you now?" Tom and Mia say, "Yes, Sam. You can play with us now. But you have to be nice. You have to share your truck. You have to take turns on the bridge. Can you do that?" Sam says, "Yes, I can do that. I will be nice. I will share my truck. I will take turns on the bridge. Thank you, Tom and Mia. You are good friends." Tom and Mia say, "You are welcome, Sam. You are a good friend too. Let's play together with our cars and trucks. Let's have fun on our road." They play together with their cars and trucks. They have fun on their road. They are happy. The end.
