Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cat and the Hill

One day, a small cat named Tim went out to play. Tim liked to jump and run. He saw a big hill with a top. He wanted to go to the top and play there. As Tim walked up the hill, he started to feel cold. "Brrr, it's so cold!" he said. The ground began to freeze. Tim was scared, but he kept going. When he reached the top, he saw a big, warm sun. It was shining and not cold. Tim felt happy and not scared anymore. He played all day at the top of the hill with the warm sun.

Once upon a time there were two friends - Tom and Sarah. Tom was a very sharp boy who liked to solve problems. Sarah liked to help him out. One day they were playing in the garden and Tom noticed an attractive job. It was shiny and had many colors - it was an old puzzle. Tom told Sarah, "I bet we can solve this puzzle!" Sarah replied, "I'm sure we can. Let's give it a try!" So the two friends began to try and solve the puzzle. They worked hard and soon their sharp minds began to figure out the tricky parts. After some time, the puzzle was finished and the two friends laughed joyfully. Tom looked at the completed puzzle and said, "We did a great job!" Sarah smiled and said, "Yes, we did. We're so sharp!"

Once upon a time, there were two children named Jack and Jill. They both were walking in the woods when they saw a big lake at the end of the trail. The sky was very gloomy and it was getting darker. Jack and Jill were both very tired and wanted to take a nap. Jill said, "Hey Jack, let's take a nap by the lake." Jack said, "No! I'm scared, it's so gloomy here." Jill said, "Don't worry, it'll be okay. Let's close our eyes and take a nap. It's been a long day and we need some rest." Jack thought about it and agreed. He lay down on the grass, closed his eyes and fell asleep. Jack had the best nap ever. When he woke up, the sky was bright and the lake was shining in the sun. Jack smiled and said, "I'm so glad we napped here! I'm feeling much better now!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved cookies. One day, his mom put a big jar of cookies on the top shelf. Tim saw the jar and wanted to eat a cookie. But it was difficult for him to reach the jar. Tim had a plan. He picked up his toy box and put it under the shelf. He stood on the toy box and still could not reach the jar. Tim was sad. His big sister, Sue, saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" "I want a cookie, but I can't reach the jar," Tim said. Sue smiled and said, "I can help you." Sue picked up a chair and put it next to the toy box. Tim climbed on the chair and reached the jar. He picked a cookie and shared it with Sue. They both enjoyed the cookie and were happy. Tim learned that when things are difficult, it is good to ask for help. And Sue learned that helping others can make you happy too. From that day on, Tim and Sue always helped each other when things were difficult. And they always shared their cookies.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a tall tree. The tall tree was very kind and helpful. It gave shade to everyone who came to rest under it. The tree loved to help others and make them happy. One day, a little boy came to the tall tree. He wanted to own the land where the tree was. He said, "I will own this land and take care of you, tall tree." The tall tree was happy to hear this and agreed. The little boy took care of the tall tree, and the tree kept giving shade to everyone. The boy learned that being kind and helpful was good. The tall tree and the boy became best friends. The moral of the story is to be kind and helpful to others, and you will make many friends.
