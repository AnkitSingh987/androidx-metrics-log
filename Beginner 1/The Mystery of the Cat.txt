Category: Beginner
Title: The Mystery of the Cat

One day, a big cat and a little dog were having a fun play time. They were good friends. The cat had a red ball and the dog had a blue ball. They liked to part and play with their own balls. The cat saw a mug on the ground. It was a big and pretty mug. The cat wanted to play with the mug too. The dog said, "No, that's a rude thing to do. The mug is not a toy." But the cat did not listen and played with the mug. Suddenly, the mug started to shake and jump. The cat and the dog were very surprised. A tiny frog came out of the mug. The frog said, "Thank you for playing with the mug. I was stuck inside, and you helped me get out!" The cat and the dog were happy that they helped the frog. They all became friends and played together with their balls.

One day, a funny cat named Tom went for a walk on the road. He wanted to measure how long the road was. He had a long string that he used to measure things. Tom started at one end of the road and began to walk. As Tom walked, he met a dog named Max. Max asked, "What are you doing with that string?" Tom said, "I am measuring the road to see how long it is." Max thought it was a fun idea and decided to help Tom. They walked and walked, measuring the road. Suddenly, they found a big hole in the road! They did not expect this. Tom and Max looked at each other and laughed. They decided to stop measuring and play near the hole instead. The funny cat and the dog had a great time playing together.

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a pond with many ducks. Tim and Mia want to feed the ducks. They have some bread. "Look, Mia, a big duck!" Tim says. He throws a piece of bread to the duck. The duck swims fast to get the bread. It eats the bread and quacks. "Can I feed the duck, too?" Mia asks. She breaks a piece of bread and throws it to the duck. The duck swims fast again and eats the bread. It quacks louder. Tim and Mia laugh. They throw more bread to the duck and other ducks. The ducks swim fast and eat the bread. They quack and flap their wings. "Wow, the ducks are hungry!" Tim says. He looks at his bread. He only has a small piece left. "This is my last piece. Do you have any more, Mia?" Mia shakes her head. She has no more bread. She looks at the duck. The duck looks at her. It quacks softly. "Sorry, duck, we have no more bread," Mia says. She throws her last piece to the duck. The duck swims fast and catches the bread. It eats the bread and quacks happily. "Bye, duck, we have to go now," Tim says. He waves to the duck. The duck waves back with its wing. Tim and Mia hold hands and walk away. They finish feeding the ducks. They have fun in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom loved his cushion more than anything. He would go to sleep on it every day. One day, Tom saw a big bird outside. He wanted to go and play with the bird, but he was worried about his cushion. So, he hid the cushion under a big chair and went outside. While Tom was playing with the bird, a little mouse named Jerry found the cushion. Jerry thought the cushion was a perfect place to rest. He did not know it was Tom's cushion. When Tom came back inside, he saw Jerry on his cushion. Tom was very upset and told Jerry to go away. Jerry did not want to be bossy, so he said sorry to Tom. Tom thought for a moment and decided to share his cushion with Jerry. From that day on, Tom and Jerry became friends. They would go outside to play together and then come back to share the cushion. And they both learned that sharing is more fun than being bossy.
