Category: Beginner
Title: The Reef and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big reef in the sea. Many fish lived there. One day, a small fish named Tim swam too close to the reef. He was careless and got stuck. A big fish named Sam swam by and saw Tim. Sam said, "You need help. I will use my strong tail to push you out." Tim said, "Thank you, Sam." Sam used his tail to push Tim out of the reef. Tim was free and happy. Tim said, "I will not be careless again. I will stay away from the reef." Sam and Tim swam away together, and they were good friends.

Tommy was walking in the park one day with his mum. He was so excited to see all the things around him. He saw a big pond and some birds in the sky. Then he spotted something. 'Mum, is that an elephant?' he asked his mum. His mum smiled. 'Yes, it is,' she said. 'Look how small it is.' Tommy was amazed. He had never seen an elephant before and he thought it was the most amazing thing he had ever seen. He was so fascinated by it that he ran up to it and tried to pat it. The elephant backed away from Tommy. 'It's scared of you,' his mum said. Tommy was surprised. He realized that he had to be gentle with the elephant if he wanted it to stay close. So he decided to stand quietly and watch it from a distance. As he watched the elephant, he felt proud that he was so smart. He had realized that the elephant was scared, and he was able to show it respect.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a lot of snow falling from the sky. They put on their coats, hats, gloves and boots and ran outside. They made a snowman, threw snowballs and slid on the ice. "Look, Tom, there is a big hill over there. Let's go and see what is on the other side," Lily said. "OK, Lily, but we have to be careful. Mom said not to go too far from the house," Tom said. They walked up the hill, holding hands. They saw a lot of trees and animals in the snow. They heard a strange sound, like a whistle. "What is that, Tom?" Lily asked. "I don't know, Lily. Maybe it is a bird or a train," Tom said. They followed the sound, curious. They wandered away from the hill and the house. They did not see where they were going. They got lost in the snow. "Tom, I'm cold and scared. Where are we?" Lily said. "I don't know, Lily. I can't see the house or the hill. We have to find our way back," Tom said. They tried to walk back, but they could not. The snow was too deep and the wind was too strong. They saw a dark shape in the snow. It looked like a big dog, but it was not. It was a wolf. It had sharp teeth and yellow eyes. It was hungry and angry. "Tom, look, a wolf! Run, run!" Lily screamed. They ran, but they were too slow. The wolf was faster. It caught them and bit them. They cried and bled. They fell in the snow. They did not get up. They were gone. The wolf ate them. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She had a doll with a pretty body. Jane loved her doll very much. She played with it every day. Jane and her doll were best friends. One day, Jane was playing with her doll when she heard a loud "rip" sound. She looked down and saw that her doll's body had a tear. Jane felt sad and frustrated. She didn't know what to do. She wanted her doll to be all better. Jane's mom saw her sad face and asked what was wrong. Jane showed her mom the tear on her doll's body. Her mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, I can fix it." Jane's mom took a needle and some thread and sewed the tear. Soon, the doll's body was all better. Jane was so happy, and she hugged her mom. From then on, Jane and her doll played happily ever after.
