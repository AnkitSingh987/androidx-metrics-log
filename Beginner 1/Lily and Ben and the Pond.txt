Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Pond

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They like to run and slide and swing. They see a big pond with ducks and fish. They want to feed the ducks some bread. They walk to the pond and see something big and gray in the water. It has a big mouth and small ears. It is a hippo! Lily and Ben are scared. They have never seen a hippo before. They think it is terrible. The hippo sees Lily and Ben. It is hungry. It wants to eat the bread. It comes out of the water and walks to them. It makes a loud noise. Lily and Ben scream. They drop the bread and run away. They find their mom and dad. They tell them about the terrible hippo. Mom and dad are surprised. They say there are no hippos in the park. They say it is a fake hippo. It is a toy for the pond. Lily and Ben do not believe them. They think the hippo is real. They do not want to go back to the pond. They want to play somewhere else. They are glad they are safe. They do not like the terrible hippo.

Sara and Tom were playing with their toy trucks in the backyard. They liked to load the trucks with dirt, rocks and leaves and drive them around. Sara had a big red truck and Tom had a small blue truck. "Look, my truck is full of dirt!" Sara said. "I can make a big hill with it." "That's boring," Tom said. "My truck has rocks and leaves. They are more fun. I can make a bridge with them." "No, they are not. Dirt is better. Let me see your truck," Sara said. She reached for Tom's truck, but he pulled it away. "No, this is my truck. You can't have it. Go away," Tom said. He pushed Sara with his hand. "Hey, don't push me. That's mean. Give me your truck," Sara said. She pushed Tom back and tried to grab his truck. They both started to cry and yell. Their mom heard them and came to the backyard. She saw them fighting over the trucks. "Stop it, both of you. What is going on here?" she asked. "Sara wants my truck. She says dirt is better than rocks and leaves. She pushed me," Tom said. "Tom won't let me see his truck. He says rocks and leaves are more fun than dirt. He pushed me," Sara said. Their mom sighed. She was tired of their arguing. She took both trucks and put them on the table. "Listen, you two. You can't play with the trucks if you can't share them. Dirt, rocks and leaves are all good for loading. But you have to be nice to each other and take turns. Do you understand?" she said. Sara and Tom nodded. They felt sorry for fighting. They hugged each other and said sorry. "Can we have our trucks back, please?" they asked. "Okay, but only if you promise to play nicely. And don't make a mess with the dirt, rocks and leaves," their mom said. She gave them their trucks back and watched them play. They loaded their trucks with different things and showed them to each other. They smiled and laughed. They had fun. Their mom smiled too. She was glad they made up. She thought they were cute.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a regular pillow. He loved his pillow very much. He took it everywhere he went. His mom told him, "Tim, you can't take your pillow to school." But Tim did not listen. At school, Tim's friend, Sam, saw his pillow. Sam said, "I don't have a pillow. Can you give me your pillow?" Tim did not want to give his pillow away. He said, "No, this is my pillow." Sam was sad, but he did not say anything. Later, Tim saw Sam sleeping on a hard table. Tim thought about how much he loved his pillow. He knew that Sam needed a pillow too. So, Tim went home and told his mom about Sam. His mom said, "Let's give Sam a pillow." They bought a new pillow for Sam. The next day, Tim gave the new pillow to Sam. Sam was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Tim!" Now, Tim and Sam both had pillows and they were good friends. Tim learned that it is good to share and help others.
