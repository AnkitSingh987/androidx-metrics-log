Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Magic

One day, a small fish named Fin lived in a great tank. He had many friends in the tank. They all played and swam together. They were happy. One day, a new fish named Bubbles came to the tank. Bubbles was shy and didn't talk much. Fin swam to Bubbles and said, "Hi! Do you want to play with us? You belong here too!" Bubbles smiled and said, "Yes, I want to play." They all played together and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Bubbles started to grow bigger and bigger. The other fish were scared. Bubbles said, "Don't be scared! I am a magic fish. I can make the tank bigger too!" And with a splash, the tank grew bigger and bigger. Now, all the fish had more space to play and swim. They were all very happy. Fin and Bubbles became best friends. They knew they all belonged together in their great big tank.

Ben and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But they do not like to sleep. They think sleep is boring and they want to stay up. One night, Mom and Dad say, "It is time for bed, Ben and Mia. You need to sleep. Sleep is good for you. It makes you grow and learn and dream." Ben and Mia say, "No, no, no! We do not want to sleep. We want to play. Play is fun. Sleep is not fun." Mom and Dad say, "You can play tomorrow. But now you need to sleep. Come on, we will read you a story and sing you a song. Then you will feel sleepy." Ben and Mia say, "OK, OK, OK. But only one story and one song. And we want to choose them." Mom and Dad say, "All right. You can choose. But then you have to sleep." Mom and Dad read them a story about a big bear and a little mouse. They sing them a song about a star and a moon. They give them a kiss and a hug and say, "Good night, Ben and Mia. We love you. Sleep tight." Ben and Mia say, "Good night, Mom and Dad. We love you too. But we are not sleepy." Mom and Dad turn off the light and close the door. They think Ben and Mia will sleep soon. But Ben and Mia do not sleep. They whisper and giggle and play with their pillows and blankets. They make a fort and a cave and a spaceship. They pretend they are pirates and astronauts and superheroes. They do not hear the gas. The gas is in the air. The gas is from the stove. The gas is bad. The gas can make you sick. The gas can make you stop breathing. But Mom and Dad hear the gas. They smell the gas. They see the gas. They are scared. They run to Ben and Mia's room. They open the door and shout, "Ben and Mia, wake up! Wake up! There is gas in the house. We have to get out. Now!" Ben and Mia are surprised. They are confused. They are scared. They do not understand. They say, "What? What? What? What is gas? Why do we have to get out? Where do we go?" Mom and Dad say, "Do not worry. Do not ask. Just follow us. We will explain later. We will keep you safe. Come on, hurry!" Mom and Dad take Ben and Mia by the hand. They run to the front door. They open the door and go outside. They see the fire truck and the ambulance and the police car. They see the firefighters and the paramedics and the police officers. They see the neighbors and the friends and the strangers. They hear the sirens and the horns and the voices. They feel the cold and the wind and the rain. They are out of the gas. They are out of the danger. They are out of the house. Mom and Dad hug Ben and Mia. They say, "We are so glad you are OK. We are so sorry you were scared. We are so proud of you. You did great. You are brave." Ben and Mia hug Mom and Dad. They say, "We are OK. We are sorry we did not sleep. We are proud of you too. You are brave." The firefighters and the paramedics and the police officers come to check on them. They say, "You are lucky. You are fine. You are strong. You are smart." They give them blankets and water and cookies. They give them stickers and toys and books. They give them smiles and nods and high-fives. They say, "You can sleep now. You can sleep here. You can sleep in the truck or the ambulance or the car. You can sleep with Mom and Dad or with us or with anyone. You can sleep as long as you want. You can sleep as gentle as you can." Ben and Mia say, "Thank you. Thank you. Thank you. You are nice. You are kind. You are helpful." They look at Mom and Dad. They look at each other. They look at the blankets and the water and the cookies. They look at the stickers and the toys and the books.
