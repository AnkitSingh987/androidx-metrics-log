Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Dog and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Bob. Bob was an obedient dog who lived in a small home with his best friend, Tim. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, Tim told Bob to stay at home while he went to the store. Bob, being an obedient dog, stayed at home. He was a little sad, but he knew Tim would come back soon. As Bob waited, he heard a whisper outside the door. It was a little cat named Lily. Lily asked Bob if he wanted to come out and play. Bob knew he should stay at home, but he really wanted to play with Lily. So, he left the home and played with Lily in the yard. They had a lot of fun, but when Tim came back, he was very sad. He couldn't find Bob anywhere. Bob had not listened to Tim and now they were apart.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big wish. He wanted to scare his friend, Sam. One day, Tim hid behind a tree. When Sam walked by, Tim jumped out and said, "Boo!" Sam looked confused. "Why did you scare me?" Sam asked. Tim said, "I wanted to make my wish come true." Sam smiled and said, "Okay, let's scare more friends together!" So, Tim and Sam went to scare more friends and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to design things. He would draw and make things all day. One day, he found some black rocks called coal. He was very happy and thought of a plan. Tim wanted to show his friends his coal. He put the coal in a bag and went to his friend's house. On his way, he saw a troubled dog. The dog was sad and lost. Tim wanted to help the dog, but he did not know how. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog saw the coal in Tim's bag and got very excited. The dog took the coal from Tim and started to play with it. The dog was not sad anymore. Tim was happy that his coal helped the dog. He went back home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little boy named Tim. He had a big dream. He wanted to be a brave hunter with a long spear. Every night, he would dream about this. One day, Tim found a stick in the woods. He said, "This can be my spear!" He took it home and played with it. He pretended to hunt big animals. Tim went to his friend, Sam, and said, "Look at my spear! Let's play hunters!" Sam agreed, and they played in the woods. They pretended to hunt big animals together. But then, Tim and Sam got lost in the woods. They were scared and didn't know how to get home. The sun went down, and it got dark. They cried and wished they never played with the spear. The boys never found their way home. They stayed lost in the woods, and their dream of being brave hunters was over.

Once upon a time, in a big lab, there was a clever dog named Max. Max liked to play and run all day. But Max had a secret. He could change into anything he wanted. One day, Max saw a little bird that could not fly. Max felt sad for the bird. He thought, "I can change and help the bird." So, Max changed into a big, strong dog. He picked up the little bird and put it on a tree branch. The little bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" Max learned that it is good to help others. He used his clever power to change and help his friends in the lab. All the animals loved Max, and they were happy to have a friend like him. The moral of the story is to always help others when you can.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to help mom in the kitchen. Mom is busy making a pie for dad. She puts apples and sugar in a bowl. She rolls the dough with a big stick. "Can we help, mom?" Sara asks. "Yes, you can," mom says. "You can shake some cinnamon on the apples. It will make the pie yummy." Sara and Ben take turns to shake the brown powder on the apples. They are careful not to spill it. They smell the sweet and spicy cinnamon. "Good job, kids," mom says. "Now I will put the dough on top of the apples and bake the pie in the oven. It will be ready soon." Sara and Ben are happy. They can't wait to eat the pie with dad. They hug mom and say, "Thank you, mom. We love you and we love pie."
