Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Dog

One day, a cat named Tom found a can of tuna. Tom was very hungry. He wanted to eat the tuna. But the can was on a high shelf. Tom tried to stretch to reach the can. He stretched and stretched, but he could not reach it. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Tom asked Max, "Can you help me get the tuna?" Max said, "I will try." Max stretched and stretched, but he could not reach the can either. Then, a big, bad bird came. The bird saw the can of tuna. The bird said, "I will take the tuna." The bird took the can and flew away. Tom and Max were sad. They did not get to eat the tuna. The big, bad bird ate it all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go to the library with his mom. One day, Tim found a big book with lots of tough animals in it. He liked the book so much that he wanted to take it home. On their way home, Tim told his mom, "Mom, I want to release all the tough animals from the book so they can play with me." His mom smiled and said, "That's a fun idea, but remember, they are just pictures in the book." That night, Tim went to bed with the book. As he slept, something unexpected happened! The tough animals from the book came to life and jumped out of the pages. Tim woke up and saw the animals in his room. He was so excited and said, "Wow! You all came out to play with me!" The animals played with Tim all night, and they had so much fun. When the sun came up, the animals knew it was time to go back into the book. Tim hugged them all and said, "Thank you for playing with me. I'll never forget you!" The animals smiled and jumped back into the book. Tim knew he had a special book, and he would always remember the night the tough animals came out to play.

Lila and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with their toys, read books, and sing songs. They always had fun together. But one day, Ben had to move to a new house far away. He said goodbye to Lila and gave her a hug. "I will miss you, Lila," he said. "You are my best friend." "I will miss you too, Ben," she said. "You are my best friend too." Lila felt sad and lonely without Ben. She did not want to play with her toys, read books, or sing songs. She just wanted Ben to come back. She wished she had something to remember him by. One day, she found a gray box under her bed. She opened it and saw a surprise. It was a gift from Ben. It was a stuffed animal that looked like a unicorn, but it had wings and a rainbow tail. It was unusual and beautiful. Lila smiled and hugged it. "Thank you, Ben," she said. "This is the best gift ever. I love it." She felt happy and warm. She knew that Ben still cared about her, even if he was far away. She decided to name the stuffed animal Benny, after her best friend. She played with Benny, read books to him, and sang songs with him. She still missed Ben, but she felt less sad and lonely. She hoped that Ben had a friend like Benny too.

One day, a gifted dog named Spot went on a journey. He walked and walked until he saw a big tree. The tree had a big hole in it. Spot wanted to look inside the hole. Spot met a little bird in the tree. The bird said, "Hello, Spot. This is my home. Please don't tear it." Spot said, "I won't tear it, little bird. I just want to look." But then, a big wind came. It blew and blew. The tree started to tear. Spot and the little bird were scared. They needed to save the tree. Spot and the little bird worked together. They found some sticks and leaves. They fixed the tree. The tree was safe again. In the end, Spot and the little bird became friends. They were happy that they saved the tree. Spot learned that even a gifted dog needs help sometimes.
