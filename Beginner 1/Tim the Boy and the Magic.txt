Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Magic

One day, a boy named Tim and his mom went to the mall. They wanted to buy some healthy food. They walked and looked at all the shops in the mall. Tim saw a big toy store and asked his mom, "Can we go in there, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but just for a little bit. We need to buy our healthy food first." So they went into the toy store and Tim saw a big, funny toy. He wanted to see what it did, so he poked it. When he poked the toy, it started to move and talk! Tim and his mom were very surprised. The toy said, "Thank you for waking me up! I am a magic toy and I will give you one wish!" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I wish for a big bag of healthy food!" The magic toy made a big bag of healthy food appear. Tim and his mom were so happy. They thanked the magic toy and went home with their healthy food.

Once upon a time, two friends, Tom and Sue, went to a big stadium. They wanted to see a fun game. It was a sunny day, and they were very happy. At the stadium, Tom bought a big, disgusting bug. He thought it was funny. "Look, Sue!" he said. "Do you want to share this with me?" Sue made a face and said, "No, thank you. That bug is disgusting!" So, Tom and Sue watched the game and had a great time. They laughed and cheered. Tom shared his bug with a bird, and Sue shared her yummy snack with Tom. They were happy they could share and be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. His ball was a big circle. It was red and blue. Tim had a lot of fun with his circle ball. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw his friend, Sam. Sam was very fast. Tim and Sam played with the circle ball. They ran and laughed. They had a great time. But then, Tim missed the ball. It rolled away very fast. Tim and Sam looked for the circle ball. They looked under the trees and behind the bushes. They were sad because they missed the ball. But then, they found the ball near the swings. Tim and Sam were very happy. They played with the circle ball until it was time to go home.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big box of toys in the park. They are very happy. "Look, a car!" Tom says. He picks up a red car with four wheels. He makes it go vroom vroom. "I want the car!" Sue says. She tries to grab the car from Tom. Tom does not let go. They pull and pull. "Stop!" Tom says. "This is my car. I saw it first." "No, it is mine!" Sue says. "You have to pay me for it." "How much?" Tom asks. "Five cookies!" Sue says. "That is too much!" Tom says. "I only have two cookies." "Then you cannot have the car!" Sue says. She pulls harder. The car breaks. It makes a loud snap. There is a big crack in the middle. Tom and Sue are sad. They look at the broken car. "This is a bad car!" Tom says. "It is old and weak." "Maybe we can fix it," Sue says. "Do you have tape?" "No, I do not," Tom says. "Me neither," Sue says. They sigh. They put the broken car back in the box. They look for another toy to play with. They hope it is not old and weak too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pretty dress that she loved to wear. But one day, she could not find her dress. Sue was sad and missed her dress a lot. She looked everywhere for it but could not find it. Sue was very stubborn and would not give up. Sue asked her mom for help. Her mom looked high and low, but the dress was still missing. They looked in the closet, under the bed, and even in the toy box. Sue was starting to worry that she would never find her dress again. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue's little brother, Tim, came into the room wearing her dress! He had been playing dress-up and did not know it was Sue's special dress. Sue and her mom laughed and were happy to find the missing dress. Sue learned that sometimes, even when you are stubborn, you need help from others to solve a problem.
