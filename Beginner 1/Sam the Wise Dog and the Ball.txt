Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Wise Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a wise old dog named Sam. Sam loved to go on walks with his boy, Tom. They would walk in the park and play with a big red ball. One day, Sam and Tom went on a walk to the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. At the park, Sam saw another dog named Ben. Ben was not very wise. He liked to take things that were not his. Ben saw Tom's big red ball and wanted it. He said, "I want that ball, give it to me!" Sam did not want to give the ball to Ben. He said, "No, this is Tom's ball." Ben got angry and started to chase Sam. Sam ran away, but he did not bring the ball with him. The chase went on and on, but Sam was too fast for Ben. Suddenly, Sam saw a big hole in the ground. He did not see it in time and fell in the hole. Ben looked down at Sam and laughed. He said, "Now I will take the ball!" Ben ran back to Tom and took the big red ball. Tom was sad that Sam fell in the hole and that Ben took the ball. He missed his wise old dog and wished he could have his ball back. The walk in the park did not end well for Sam and Tom.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Sally. Sally loved to sort her toys. She had many toys: dolls, cars, and balls. Every day, she sorted her toys by color or size. It was fun for her. One day, it was very cold outside. Sally could not go out to play. She decided to sort her toys again. This time, she sorted them into two groups: toys for the cold days and toys for the warm days. She put her dolls and cars in the cold group, and her balls in the warm group. Later, Sally's mom made some spicy soup for lunch. Sally and her mom sat down to eat the soup. It warmed them up on the cold day. After lunch, Sally played with her cold day toys and dreamed about the warm days when she could play outside with her balls again.

One day, a boy named Tim played with his football outside. He loved to kick and throw the ball. He had a lot of fun. Tim always had his mom to mind him while he played. One sunny day, Tim's mom gave him a spicy snack. Tim had never eaten anything spicy before. He took a bite and his face turned red. He felt hot and his tongue burned. He didn't like the spicy snack, but he didn't want to hurt his mom's feelings. Tim had an idea. He kicked his football high into the air. As the ball came down, it landed on the spicy snack. The snack stuck to the ball. Tim's mom wasn't looking, so he kicked the ball again. This time, the snack flew off the ball and into a bush. Tim's mom never knew, and Tim was happy to play with his football again.

Ben and Mia were playing in the woods. They liked to explore and find new things. One day, they saw a cabin. It was old and brown and had no windows. "Let's go inside!" Ben said. He was curious and brave. "OK, but be careful!" Mia said. She was careful and smart. They opened the door and went inside. The cabin was empty. There was only dust and spider webs. They looked around and saw a big ruler on the wall. "What is that?" Mia asked. "It's a measure. You can use it to see how big things are." Ben said. "Really? How do you use it?" Mia asked. "Like this. You stand next to it and see where your head is. That's how tall you are." Ben said. He showed her how to do it. "I want to try!" Mia said. She stood next to the measure and looked up. "Wow, you are very tall!" Ben said. He smiled at her. "Thank you! You are very tall too!" Mia said. She smiled back. They measured each other and laughed. They had fun in the empty cabin. They forgot about the time and the dust and the spider webs. They were happy.
