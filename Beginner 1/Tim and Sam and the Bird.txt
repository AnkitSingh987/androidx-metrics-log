Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Bird

Tim and Sam were brothers. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big bird in the grass. It had brown feathers and a red thing on its head. "Look, a turkey!" Tim said. "No, it's not a turkey. It's a duck," Sam said. They ran to the bird and tried to touch it. The bird did not like that. It made a loud noise and ran away. "See, it's a turkey. Ducks don't run like that," Tim said. "Fine, it's a turkey. But why is it here?" Sam asked. They followed the bird to a fence. Behind the fence, there was a farm. They saw many animals: cows, pigs, chickens, and more turkeys. "Maybe it came from the farm," Tim said. "Let's go and see," Sam said. They climbed over the fence and walked to the farm. They saw a man with a hat and a bucket. He was feeding the animals. "Hello, mister. Why do you have turkeys?" Tim asked. "Hello, boys. I have turkeys because I sell them for Thanksgiving. Do you know what Thanksgiving is?" the man asked. "Yes, it's when we eat turkey and say thank you," Sam said. "That's right. But not all turkeys are the same. Some are special. Come, I'll show you," the man said. He took them to a barn. Inside, there was a cage with a green turkey. It had green feathers and a green thing on its head. "Wow, a green turkey!" Tim said. "Yes, this is my green turkey. He's very rare and very expensive. I don't sell him. I keep him as a pet. He's very smart and friendly. Do you want to pet him?" the man asked. "Yes, please," Sam said. They went to the cage and petted the green turkey. The turkey made a happy noise and rubbed his head on their hands. "He likes you. What are your names?" the man asked. "I'm Tim and this is Sam. We're brothers," Tim said. "Nice to meet you, Tim and Sam. I'm Bob. This is Greeny. He's my best friend," the man said. They talked and played with the green turkey for a while. Then they said goodbye and thanked the man. "Thank you, Bob. Thank you, Greeny. You're very nice," Sam said. "You're welcome, boys. Come back anytime. Greeny likes you," Bob said. They climbed over the fence and went back to the park. They were happy. They had made new friends. They had seen a green turkey. They had learned something new. They had managed to have a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a white cat named Snow. They loved to play together in the garden. One sunny day, Lily and Snow were playing when a bird appeared. The bird said, "I have big news! There is a new park in town. It has a big slide and a fun swing. You should go and play there!" Lily was very excited. She said, "Thank you, bird! Snow, let's go to the new park!" Snow meowed happily, and they both ran to the park. At the park, Lily and Snow had so much fun. They played on the slide, the swing, and even made new friends. They were so happy that the bird told them the news. At the end of the day, Lily and Snow went home, tired but happy. They couldn't wait to tell their mom and dad about the fun they had at the new park. And they thanked the bird for sharing the news with them.

Once there was a small boy called Sam. He was only three years old and he loved to help his Mom. "Mom, what can I do to help? Can I carry something?" Sam asked. "Sure Son. Here, carry this tray for me," Mom said. Sam took the tray and held it carefully. He carried it with both hands, not wanting to drop it. He felt so big and important bringing things to his mom. Mom smiled. "Thanks for helping Sam. Now, here's something else for you to do. It's smaller, so it's easy to carry. Can you carry this inside for me?" Sam smiled and nodded. He took the small item and carried it carefully inside. He thought it felt special to help his mom in this way. When he was done, his mom couldn't help but smile at him. "Thanks for helping Sam. I'm so proud of you!" she said, giving him a big hug. Sam felt proud of himself, too. He loved helping his mom and carrying things for her. It was something special he could do to show her how much he cared.
