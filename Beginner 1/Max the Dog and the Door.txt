Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Door

One day, a big dog named Max took a step out of his yard. He saw a little cat named Lily. Lily was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I was playing with my toy, and it went into the jail. I cannot get it back. I feel guilty because I should have been more careful." Max wanted to help Lily. He went to the jail and saw the toy inside. The door was closed, but Max was strong. He pushed the door open and took a step inside. He got the toy and gave it to Lily. Lily was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Max! You are a good friend." Max and Lily played together for the rest of the day. They were both very happy.

Tim and Lily are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars. One day, they go to the park with their moms. There are many other kids in the park. Tim and Lily see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. They run to the slide and wait in line. There is a boy in front of them. He is bigger than Tim and Lily. He has a red shirt and a mean face. He does not look polite. He goes on the slide very fast and pushes the other kids. He does not say sorry. He does not wait for his turn. He goes on the slide again and again. Tim and Lily are sad. They want to go on the slide too. But the boy does not let them. He says, "This is my slide. Go away!" He makes a loud noise and scares them. Tim and Lily look at their moms. Their moms look angry. They say, "That is not nice. You have to share and be polite. You have to wait for your turn. You have to say sorry if you hurt someone." The boy does not listen. He does not care. He thinks he is the boss. He goes on the slide one more time. But this time, he slips and falls. He hurts his knee and his elbow. He cries and cries. He looks for his mom. But his mom is not there. She went to the store. She left him alone. Tim and Lily feel sorry for the boy. They are kind. They go to the boy and help him. They say, "Are you okay? Do you need a band-aid? Do you want to play with us?" The boy is surprised. He does not understand. He says, "Why are you nice to me? I was mean to you. I did not share. I did not say sorry." Tim and Lily say, "Because we are friends. We like to play and have fun. We do not like to fight and be mean. We know how to be polite. We say please and thank you. We wait for our turn. We say sorry if we hurt someone. That is what friends do." The boy feels ashamed. He realizes he was wrong. He says, "I am sorry. I was not polite. I did not share. I did not say sorry. I was not a good friend. Can you forgive me? Can we be friends?" Tim and Lily smile. They say, "Of course. We can be friends. Come on, let's go on the slide together. But this time, we have to wait for our turn and be polite." The boy nods. He says, "Okay. Thank you. You are very nice. You are good friends." He goes on the slide with Tim and Lily. They have fun. They laugh and play. They are polite. They are happy. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a long slide and wanted to play on it. He watched as other kids went down the slide, laughing and having fun. Tim was excited to try it too. As Tim climbed up the slide, he saw a little screw on the ground. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. Then, he slid down the slide and had so much fun. He went up and down many times, always watching for more screws. One time, when Tim was at the top of the slide, he saw a big robot. The robot looked sad and had a hole where a screw should be. Tim took the little screw from his pocket and gave it to the robot. The robot was so happy! It turned out the robot was a magic robot that could make everyone's wishes come true. Tim and the robot became best friends, and they played at the park all day long.
