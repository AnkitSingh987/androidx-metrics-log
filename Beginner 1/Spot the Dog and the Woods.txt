Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and run all day. One day, Spot went for a walk in the woods. In the woods, Spot found a camp. There were kids playing and laughing. Spot was happy and wanted to meet the kids. He wagged his tail and walked to the camp. The kids saw Spot and smiled. They all came to meet him. Spot and the kids played together all day. They had so much fun at the camp. And from that day on, Spot and the kids were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She wanted to discover something new. As she walked, she saw a big tree with a hole in it. Lucy thought it was a thoughtful place to hide something. Lucy looked inside the hole and found a letter. She was very happy. She opened the letter and it said, "Hello, I am a friend. I hid a toy for you to find. Look under the big rock near the tree." Lucy ran to the big rock and said, "I hope I find the toy!" She moved the rock and saw a pretty doll. She was so excited and said, "Thank you, thoughtful friend!" From that day on, Lucy and her new doll were always together. They went on many adventures and discovered new things. And every time they found something, Lucy remembered her thoughtful friend who hid the doll for her.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big red ball that he loved to play with. One day, Tim was playing with his ball in the yard when it bounced into the street. He felt guilty for not being careful and started to weep. Tim's mom saw him weeping and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "My ball is in the street, and I feel bad for not being careful." Tim's mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, we will get your ball back." As they walked to the street, they saw a big truck making a lot of gas come out. Tim's mom said, "Wait here, I will get your ball." She got the ball and gave it back to Tim. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They went back to the yard and played with the ball together. Tim was happy and didn't feel guilty anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a high tree with his family. Yesterday, he saw a shiny red ball in the park. Tim wanted to play with the ball, but it was too big for him. Tim had a friend, a big bear named Ben. Tim went to Ben's house and asked, "Can I borrow your help? I want to play with the red ball, but it's too big for me." Ben smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's go to the park together." Tim and Ben went to the park, and Ben helped Tim play with the ball. They had so much fun! Tim was happy that he asked for help. From that day on, Tim and Ben played together every day, and they became the best of friends.

One day, a pale little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. She put her hand on the tree and started to climb. As she climbed, she saw a small bird on a branch. The bird said, "Hello!" Amy said, "Hi, little bird! Can you help me climb the tree?" The bird said, "Yes, I can help you. Hold my hand and we can climb together." They climbed higher and higher. When they reached the top, they saw a beautiful view. Amy said, "Thank you, bird, for helping me." The bird said, "You're welcome, Amy. Let's be friends forever." And they were the best of friends.

Sam and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big flag on a pole. The flag is red, white and blue. It has stars and stripes. "Look, Mia, a flag!" Sam says. "What is a flag, Sam?" Mia asks. "A flag is a cloth that shows who you are. This flag is for our country. It is called America," Sam explains. "Who made the flag, Sam?" Mia wonders. "Many people made the flag, Mia. They worked hard and used their hands and needles. They wanted to show they are proud of their country," Sam says. "Wow, that is cool, Sam. I want to be proud of my country too. Can we make our own flag?" Mia asks. "Sure, Mia. We can use paper and crayons. We can draw what we like and what makes us happy. Then we can stick our flag on a stick and wave it in the air," Sam says. They run to the bench and find some paper and crayons. They draw flowers and hearts and suns and stars. They use many colors and make their flag bright and beautiful. They find a stick and tape their flag to it. They hold their flag high and run around the park. "Look at our flag, Sam. It is so pretty. It shows who we are. We are friends and we are happy," Mia says. "Yes, Mia. Our flag is wonderful. It shows who we are. We are kind and we are fun," Sam says. They smile and hug each other. They are proud of their flag and of themselves. They grow as friends and as people. They love their flag and their country.
