Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Flowers

Lily and Ben are young. They are brother and sister. They like to play with their toys and books. Sometimes they help their mom and dad with chores. One day, mom says, "Lily and Ben, today we will spend time with grandma and grandpa. They are coming for dinner. Can you help me make the table?" Lily and Ben say, "Yes, mom, we can help. We love grandma and grandpa. They are very nice and funny. They always tell us stories and give us hugs." Lily and Ben help mom make the table. They put plates, forks, spoons, napkins, and cups. They also put a vase with flowers. They want to make the table pretty for grandma and grandpa. Soon, grandma and grandpa arrive. They knock on the door. Lily and Ben run to open it. They say, "Hello, grandma and grandpa. We are happy to see you. Come in, come in. We have a surprise for you." Grandma and grandpa smile and hug Lily and Ben. They say, "Hello, Lily and Ben. We are happy to see you too. You are so sweet and smart. What is the surprise?" Lily and Ben take grandma and grandpa to the dining room. They show them the table. They say, "Look, grandma and grandpa. We helped mom make the table. We put flowers for you. Do you like it?" Grandma and grandpa say, "Oh, Lily and Ben. We love it. You did a great job. Thank you so much. You are very kind and helpful. We are proud of you." Mom says, "Dinner is ready. Let's eat. We have chicken, rice, salad, and cake. I hope you enjoy it." Lily and Ben say, "Yummy, yummy. We are hungry. Let's eat. Thank you, mom, for cooking. Thank you, grandma and grandpa, for coming. We love you." They all sit down and eat. They talk and laugh. They have a good time. They spend time with grandma and grandpa. They are happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sue. Sue had a fair, where many people came to play games and eat yummy food. Tim was very happy to see Sue and all the fun things at the fair. At the fair, there was a funny clown who painted Tim's arm. The clown painted a big, red heart on Tim's arm. Tim laughed and said, "Thank you, Mr. Clown! I love my red heart!" Sue and Tim played games and ate ice cream together. They had a great time at the fair. When it was time to go home, Tim and Sue said goodbye. "I had so much fun today," said Tim. "Me too," said Sue. "Come visit me again soon!"

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying around. Tim saw a wire hanging from a tree. The wire was rough and long. Tim wanted to play with the wire, so he flew closer to it. Tim said, "Hello, wire! Can I play with you?" The wire did not talk, but Tim thought it was okay to play. Tim started to hang on the wire with his little feet. He swung back and forth, having fun. While Tim was playing, his friend, a squirrel named Sam, came by. Sam saw Tim hanging on the wire and said, "Hi, Tim! That looks fun!" Tim replied, "It is fun, Sam! Come join me!" Sam climbed the tree and started to hang on the wire too. They laughed and played together until the sun went down.

---

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. They had a long blue shovel and a long blue bucket. They filled the bucket with dirt and worms and snails. "Look, Ben, I found a big snail!" Lily said, holding up a slimy shell. "Wow, it's so cool!" Ben said, taking the snail from Lily. "Can I keep it?" "OK, but you have to be nice to it," Lily said. "It's my friend." Ben nodded. He put the snail in the bucket and ran to the house. He wanted to show his mom the snail. He opened the door and went inside. He forgot to close the door. Lily followed Ben. She saw the door was open. She was scared. She did not want the snail to run away. She ran to the door and closed it. She saw a big lock on the door. She turned the lock. She heard a click. She locked the door. "Ben, Ben, come back!" Lily shouted. "I locked the door!" Ben heard Lily. He ran to the door. He tried to open it. It did not move. He saw the lock. He did not know how to unlock it. "Lily, Lily, why did you lock the door?" Ben asked. "How do we get out?" "I don't know, Ben, I don't know," Lily said. "I was scared of the snail. I'm sorry." Ben and Lily started to cry. They wanted to get out. They wanted their mom. Mom heard Ben and Lily. She came to the door. She saw the lock. She had a key. She put the key in the lock. She turned the key. She opened the door. "Ben, Lily, what happened?" Mom asked. "Why did you lock the door?" Ben and Lily hugged Mom. They told her about the snail. They said they were sorry. Mom smiled. She was not mad. She was glad they were safe. She took the snail from the bucket. She showed Ben and Lily how to unlock the door. She said they could keep the snail, but they had to be careful with the door. Ben and Lily said thank you. They learned a new word: unlock. They played with the snail in the garden. They did not lock the door again.
