Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Pale Squirrel and the Bird

There was a pale squirrel named Tim. Tim loved to eat nuts. One day, Tim saw a big nut high in a tree. Tim wanted to get the nut, but it was too high. Tim thought, "I can stretch to get the nut!" Tim climbed the tree and stretched his arm out. He reached for the nut, but he still could not get it. Then, Tim's friend, a bird, saw Tim. The bird flew up and got the nut for Tim. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" They both ate the nut and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was restless. She liked to run, jump, and play all day. One day, Sue found a pretty comb. The comb was pink and shiny. Sue went to her friend, Tom. "Look at my comb!" she said. Tom liked the comb too. They decided to play a game. They would take turns and watch each other comb their hair. First, Sue combed her hair while Tom watched. Then, it was Tom's turn. Sue watched as Tom combed his hair. They laughed and played with the comb all day. At the end of the day, they were both very happy and not restless anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max had a big toy yacht. He loved to play with his yacht in the water. One day, Max saw a little bird who was sad. Max asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I can't find my nest." Max wanted to help the bird. He had an idea. Max said, "I will suggest a way to find your nest." Max told the bird to get on his yacht. They went on the water to look for the bird's nest. They saw a tree with many nests. The bird was happy to find her nest. Max and the bird became good friends. And they played with the yacht every day.

One day, a big friendly dog named Sam was playing in the park. He saw a small cat under a tree. Sam went to the cat and said, "Hi, let's play!" The cat did not like dogs. She was scared. So, she slapped Sam with her paw. Sam was sad and said, "Why did you do that? I just wanted to play." Just then, a big wind came and blew the cat up into the tree. The cat was scared and cried for help. Sam saw the cat in trouble and forgot about the slap. He climbed the tree and saved the cat. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, Sam. I am sorry for the slap. Let's be friends." From that day on, Sam and the cat were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an interesting toy car that could reverse. He loved to play with his car all day long. Tim's mom was in the kitchen, putting food in the fridge. One day, while Tim was playing with his car, it started to reverse on its own. The car went under the fridge! Tim was very surprised. He tried to get his car back, but his hand was too big to fit under the fridge. Tim had an idea. He asked his little sister, Lily, to help him. Lily's hand was small enough to reach under the fridge. When Lily pulled the car out, it was a different color! The car was now pink instead of blue. Tim and Lily were so amazed. They played with the interesting car together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a small bird called Sam. He was very hungry so he flew to the park to search for something to feed on. He saw a square shaped pizza on the grass. He flew down and pecked at it. Suddenly, a fast moving mouse came running. It took the pizza and ran away. Sam was so angry. He shouted at the mouse, "Why did you take my food?" The mouse replied, "I am so sorry, but I am very hungry too. Please feed me instead." Sam thought for a moment and then looked around and saw some seeds on the ground. He picked them up and said, "Here, eat these. Maybe next time we can share?". The mouse smiled and said, "Thank you". They shared the seeds and then went their separate ways. From then on, every time Sam went to the park, the mouse was there waiting to share with him.
