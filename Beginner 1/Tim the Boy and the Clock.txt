Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Clock

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a fearful boy who was scared of many things. One day, Tim saw a big clock on the wall. He wanted to touch the clock, but he was too small to reach it. Tim's big sister, Jane, saw him trying to reach the clock. She wanted to help him, but she was scared too. She thought the clock might fall and break. Tim and Jane looked at each other and knew they had to be brave. Together, they found a small chair. Tim stood on the chair and tried to reach the clock again. This time, he could touch it! He was so happy, and Jane was proud of him. They learned that when they work together and face their fears, they can do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Ben. Ben loved to smile and play with his friends. One day, Ben saw a girl in a uniform. She was a little girl who went to school. The little girl in the uniform saw Ben and smiled back. She asked Ben if he wanted to play with her. Ben was very happy and wagged his tail. They played catch and had lots of fun. After playing, the little girl in the uniform gave Ben a hug. Ben smiled and licked her face. They were very happy and became best friends. From that day on, they always played together after school.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to taste new foods. One day, she found a big, shiny buckle on the ground. Amy thought it was pretty and wanted to show her friend, Tom. Amy went to Tom's house and said, "Look what I found, Tom! It's a shiny buckle!" Tom looked at the buckle and said, "Wow, that's cool! Let's taste it!" Amy was confused. She didn't think you could taste a buckle. Tom, being a bit ignorant, licked the buckle. He made a yucky face and said, "Eww, this does not taste good!" Amy laughed and said, "Silly Tom, buckles are not for tasting!" They both laughed and played with the shiny buckle all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby person named Tom. Tom loved to skip and play with his friends in the park. One day, he saw a little bird who looked sad. Tom thought, "I will make the bird happy by skipping with it." The next day, Tom went to the park and found the sad bird again. He started to skip around, hoping the bird would join him. The bird watched Tom, and it looked like it wanted to skip too. Tom could see that the bird was getting ready to fly. Finally, the bird started to fly and skip with Tom. They skipped together, and the bird was happy. Tom felt so good that he had made a new friend. From that day on, Tom and the bird always skipped and played together in the park.

Anna and Ben like to play near the river. They see a big kayak on the shore. It is red and shiny. They want to try it. "Let's go in the kayak, Ben. It looks fun," Anna says. "OK, Anna. But we have to be careful. The river is fast and deep," Ben says. They climb into the kayak and push it into the water. Anna takes a paddle and Ben holds on to the kayak. They start to move with the river. "Wow, this is fun, Anna. You are good at paddling. You lead us," Ben says. "Thank you, Ben. I like to lead. I know where to go. We are safe in the kayak," Anna says. But Anna does not know where to go. She does not see the big rocks ahead. She does not hear the loud noise of the water. She does not feel the kayak tipping over. The kayak hits the rocks and flips over. Anna and Ben fall into the water. They scream and splash. They are scared and cold. They are not safe in the kayak. No one hears them. No one sees them. No one helps them. The river takes them away. They never come back.
