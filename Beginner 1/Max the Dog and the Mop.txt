Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Mop

One day, a little dog named Max found a mop in his house. Max was a good dog who liked to help. He thought he could use the mop to clean the floor. Max's friend, a cat named Lucy, saw Max with the mop and said, "Max, be careful with that mop. It has harsh bristles, and it might scratch the floor." Max didn't listen to Lucy. He wanted to act like a big helper. So, Max started to mop the floor with the harsh mop. He was very happy to help his family. But soon, Max noticed some scratches on the floor. He was sad and worried. Max went to Lucy and told her about the scratches. Lucy said, "Don't worry, Max. We can fix it. I told you the mop was harsh, but now we know for next time." Max and Lucy worked together to fix the scratches. They were happy to help each other. Max learned to listen to his friends and be more careful with the mop.

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly high in the sky. One day, Tim saw a big room with many kids. It was a gym. In the gym, Tim saw a swing. He thought it was a branch. Tim wanted to sit on the swing. But it was not available. A kid was on the swing. Tim waited for the kid to go. When the swing was free, Tim tried to sit. But, oh no! Tim fell. He did not know it was not a branch. The kids helped Tim. They all became friends. Tim now knew a gym was not a tree.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a computer. He loved to spend time on the computer. He played games and watched fun videos. Tim's friends saw the computer and felt envious. They wanted a computer too. One day, Tim's friend, Sara, came over. She said, "Tim, can I play on your computer?" Tim said, "Yes, we can play together!" They spent the day playing games and laughing. Sara was happy to play with Tim. Soon, all of Tim's friends came over to play on the computer. They took turns and shared the fun. They were not envious anymore. They were happy to spend time together, playing on Tim's computer. And they all became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big crab. The big crab had a big shell. The big crab loved to play on the beach. One day, the big crab saw a big hill of sand. The big crab wanted to finish climbing the big hill of sand to see what was on the other side. The big crab started to climb the big hill of sand. It was not easy, but the big crab did not give up. The big crab knew that if it could finish climbing the big hill of sand, it would find something special. Finally, the big crab finished climbing the big hill of sand. On the other side, the big crab found a beautiful treasure chest. The big crab was so happy! It opened the treasure chest and found a new, shiny, big shell. The big crab put on the new big shell and went back to playing on the beach, proud of its new treasure.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. It was icy and cold. Tim saw a cat on the porch. He wanted to be friends with the cat. So, Tim walked to the porch and said, "Hi, cat! Want to play?" The cat looked at Tim and did not say anything. Tim tried again. He said, "Please, cat! I want to play with you!" But the cat still did not say anything. Tim was sad. Then, Tim had an idea. He thought, "Maybe the cat can't hear me." So, he took a big breath and shouted, "Cat, play with me!" The cat jumped and ran away. Tim was very sad. He did not make a new friend that day.

One day, a big shark went to the beach. It wanted to play with the little kids. The kids saw the shark and said, "Wow! A big shark!" They were not scared because the shark had a big smile. The shark wanted to show the kids how it could load lots of sand on its back. The shark found it easy to load sand on its back. It could carry lots of sand to make a big sand castle. The kids were happy and clapped their hands. They all helped the shark to load more sand. But then, something unexpected happened. The shark's big smile turned into a big frown. The kids looked at the shark and asked, "What's wrong?" The shark said, "I am not a shark, I am a dolphin!" The kids were surprised, but they still loved their new friend. They all laughed and played together on the beach.
