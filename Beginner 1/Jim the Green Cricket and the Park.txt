Category: Beginner
Title: Jim the Green Cricket and the Park

One day, a green cricket named Jim was in the park. He liked to think about fun things to do. He saw a big tree and thought, "I want to jump to the top!" As Jim started to jump, a little girl named Sue saw him. She said, "Hi, Mr. Cricket! Can I help you jump to the top?" Jim said, "Yes, please!" So, Sue put Jim on her hand and lifted him up high. Jim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A strong wind blew, and Jim fell off Sue's hand! Luckily, he landed on a soft leaf. Jim and Sue learned that it's good to think before you act. They became good friends and played in the park together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to hold her toy bear and play all day. One day, her mom made a big cake. It was a special day for Amy, as it was her birthday. The cake was so big, with many colors on top. Amy was so happy to see the cake. Amy was a persistent girl. She wanted to hold the cake all by herself. She asked her mom, "Can I hold the cake, please?" Her mom said, "No, Amy. The cake is too big for you to hold. Let me help you." But Amy didn't want help. She tried to hold the cake, but it was too heavy. The cake fell on the floor. Amy felt sad and started to cry. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Amy. We can make another cake together." So, Amy and her mom made a new cake. This time, Amy asked her mom to help her hold the cake. They put the cake on the table and sang "Happy Birthday" to Amy. Amy learned that it's okay to ask for help when things are too big to hold.

Sara and Ben were playing with their dolls. Sara had a big doll with long hair and a nice dress. Ben had a small doll with short hair and a shirt. Sara liked her doll's style, but Ben wanted to change his doll's style. "Can I use your scissors?" Ben asked Sara. "What for?" Sara asked. "I want to cut my doll's hair and make it long like yours," Ben said. "No, you can't do that!" Sara said. "You will ruin your doll's style. It is nice the way it is." "But I don't like it," Ben said. "I want to try something new." Ben took the scissors from Sara and cut his doll's hair. He tried to make it long, but he cut too much. His doll's hair became very short and uneven. It looked bad. "Oh no, what have I done?" Ben cried. "My doll's style is gone. It is not nice anymore." Sara felt sorry for Ben. She saw that he was sad. She had an idea. "Maybe we can restore your doll's style," Sara said. "We can use some glue and yarn and make new hair for your doll." "Really?" Ben said. "Can you help me?" "Yes, I can help you," Sara said. "But you have to promise me something." "What is it?" Ben asked. "You have to promise me that you will not cut your doll's hair again," Sara said. "You have to respect your doll's style. It is part of who your doll is." "OK, I promise," Ben said. "I learned my lesson. I will not cut my doll's hair again. I will love my doll the way it is." Sara and Ben worked together to restore Ben's doll's style. They used glue and yarn and made new hair for the doll. They also gave the doll a new shirt and a hat. The doll looked nice again. "Thank you, Sara," Ben said. "You are a good friend. You helped me restore my doll's style. And you taught me to be happy with what I have." "You are welcome, Ben," Sara said. "You are a good friend too. You said you are sorry and you kept your promise. And you learned to be kind to your doll." Sara and Ben hugged each other and their dolls. They continued to play with their dolls. They had fun and they were happy.
