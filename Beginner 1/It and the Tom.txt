Category: Beginner
Title: It and the Tom

It was a beautiful, sunny day outside. Tom and his mom, Betty, were walking in the park. Betty looked so happy, but Tom was trembling. "What's wrong, Tom?" Betty asked. Tom opened his mouth to talk, but he was so scared, he couldn't say anything. "Are you okay, honey?" Betty asked. Tom shook his head. Betty looked so worried. She took his hand and said, "Let's go home and have a dessert." Tom felt better and nodded his head. When they arrived home, Betty brought out a delicious dessert for them. Tom was so happy! Suddenly, Betty got angry. She was frowning and looked very upset. "What's wrong," Tom asked. Betty said, "I'm mad because I can't find my keys!" Tom felt scared so he trembled again. But Betty smiled at him and said, "It's alright, let's look together and we might be able to find them." Tom was so relieved. He was happy that Betty wasn't angry anymore. They looked for the keys together and eventually, they found them! Tom and Betty shared a hug and enjoyed their dessert in peace.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big, hairy dog. The dog is black and brown. It has a long tail and sharp teeth. "Wow, look at that dog!" Tom says. "It is so big and hairy. I want to pet it." "No, Tom, don't!" Sam says. "The dog might be angry. It might bite you. Stay away from it." But Tom does not listen. He runs to the dog. He tries to touch its fur. The dog does not like that. It growls and barks. It shows its teeth. It tries to bite Tom. "Help, help!" Tom shouts. "The dog is biting me! Sam, help me!" Sam hears Tom shout. He is scared. He does not know what to do. He looks around. He sees a man. The man is wearing a blue shirt and a hat. He has a stick in his hand. He is smoking. Smoke comes out of his mouth. "Help, help!" Sam shouts. "My brother is in trouble! The dog is biting him! Please help us!" The man hears Sam shout. He sees the dog and Tom. He is not happy. He throws his stick away. He runs to the dog. He grabs its collar. He pulls it away from Tom. "Bad dog, bad dog!" the man shouts. "You are not supposed to bite people! Go away, go away!" The dog is scared. It whines and runs away. The man helps Tom stand up. He checks his arm. It is not bleeding. It is only red. "Are you okay, kid?" the man asks. "Did the dog hurt you?" "I'm okay, thank you," Tom says. "The dog did not hurt me. It only scared me. I'm sorry, I should not have petted it." "That's okay, kid," the man says. "You should be careful with strange dogs. They might not be friendly. You should always ask the owner first. Do you understand?" "Yes, I understand," Tom says. "Thank you for saving me. You are very brave." "You're welcome, kid," the man says. "You are very lucky. Now go and play with your brother. And be careful." Tom and Sam hug the man. They say thank you again. They run back to the park. They play with their ball. They do not go near the dog. They are happy. They have learned a lesson.

One day, a boy named Tim found a small motor. He believed it was magic. Tim showed it to his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam! I found a magic motor!" he said. Sam looked at it and said, "Wow! Let's see what it can do!" They went to play with the motor outside. It was a hot day. Tim and Sam put the motor on a toy car. The car started to move very fast. They were so happy. They played all day with the toy car and the motor. They believed it was the best day ever. But then, the motor got very hot. Tim and Sam didn't know what to do. The toy car started to smoke. They got scared. The car caught fire and burned. The magic motor was gone. Tim and Sam were sad. They learned to be more careful with things they didn't understand.
