Category: Beginner
Title: The Ball of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim found a mysterious box in the garage. He did not know what was inside, but he wanted to see. He asked his mom, "Can I open the box?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim opened the box and found a big, red ball. He played with it all day. He threw it high in the air and caught it. He rolled it on the ground and kicked it. But then, Tim threw the ball too hard. It went into the street and a car hit it. The ball popped and was gone. Tim felt regret. He was sad that he broke the ball. His mom saw him crying and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can get a new ball." Tim smiled and hugged his mom. They went to the store and bought a new ball to play with. Tim learned to be more careful when playing near the street.

Once, there was a poor little dog named Tim. Tim had a big horn on his head. He did not like his horn. One day, he met a cat named Sam. "Hi, Sam!" said Tim. "Do you like my horn?" Sam looked at Tim's horn and said, "No, I do not like it. It is too big." Tim felt sad. He wanted to get rid of his horn. He went to a wise old owl for help. "Please, help me," said Tim. "I want to take off my horn." The owl said, "Are you sure? You may regret it." Tim thought for a moment. He did not want to regret anything. But, he still wanted his horn gone. "Yes, I am sure," he said. The owl helped Tim take off his horn. Tim was happy. He went back to Sam and showed him. "Look, Sam! My horn is gone!" said Tim. Sam looked at Tim and said, "Now you are just like me. I am glad." Tim and Sam became good friends. They played together every day. And Tim never regretted the choice he made.

Once upon a time, there was a red ball. The red ball had a best friend named Phone. They liked to play together all day long. They would roll, bounce, and have lots of fun. One day, the red ball and Phone went to the park. They played on the swings and went down the slide. They were having a great time. But then, they saw a big rock. The red ball and Phone wanted to see who could jump over the rock. The red ball went first and jumped over the rock. Then it was Phone's turn. Phone tried to jump over the rock, but it didn't make it. Phone hit the rock and split into two pieces. The red ball was very sad. Now, Phone could not play anymore. The red ball missed its best friend and wished they never jumped over the rock.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big, red ball and wanted to hold it. So, he ran to the ball and picked it up. As Tim held the ball, he got a little shock. It made him feel miserable. He didn't want to hold the ball anymore. So, he put the ball down and started to cry. His mom saw him crying and gave him a big hug. She said, "It's okay, Tim. Sometimes things give us a little shock, but we can be brave and try again." Tim wiped his tears, picked up the ball again, and this time, there was no shock. He smiled and played with the ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was troubled because he had no one to talk to. One day, Tim went for a visit to his friend, the big tree. "Hello, big tree," Tim said. "I am sad. Can we talk?" The big tree did not talk back. Tim was very sad. He sat under the big tree and cried. A little bird flew by and saw Tim crying. The bird felt bad for Tim. "Hello, Tim," said the little bird. "I can talk with you." Tim was happy to have a friend to talk to. They talked and talked all day. But then, the sky got dark. The little bird had to fly away. "Goodbye, Tim," said the little bird. Tim was sad again. He had no one to talk to. The big tree still did not talk. Tim went home, feeling very lonely.
