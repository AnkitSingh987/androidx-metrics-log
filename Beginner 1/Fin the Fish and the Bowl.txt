Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Bowl

One day, a little fish named Fin wanted to swim in a new place. He was bored of swimming in his small fishbowl. He saw a big room called the kitchen and thought it would be fun to swim there. Fin asked his friend, Bee, "Can you help me swim in the kitchen?" Bee said, "Yes, I can help you, Fin!" So, Bee picked up Fin with her little legs and took him to the kitchen. She put Fin in a big bowl of water. Fin was very happy to swim in the big bowl. But then, Mom came into the kitchen and saw Fin. She was upset. "Fin, you should not be in the kitchen!" she said. "You belong in your fishbowl!" So, Mom took Fin back to his fishbowl. Fin was sad, but he knew Mom was right. The kitchen was not a safe place for him to swim. He promised to stay in his fishbowl from now on, and Bee promised to visit him there.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy had long, soft fur that she loved to have pet. One day, Fluffy went for a walk and saw a big tree. She wanted to climb the tree and see how high she could go. As Fluffy climbed the tree, she met a bird. The bird said, "Hi, Fluffy! What are you doing up here?" Fluffy said, "I want to see how high I can go!" The bird smiled and said, "Be careful, Fluffy. It's a long way down." Fluffy kept climbing higher and higher. She looked down and saw her friend, Dog, on the ground. Dog called up to her, "Fluffy, are you okay up there?" Fluffy looked down and said, "Yes, Dog, but it's a long way down." Dog said, "Just be careful, Fluffy!" At last, Fluffy reached the top of the tree. She felt so proud! But when it was time to go down, she felt scared. Dog saw her and said, "Don't worry, Fluffy. I'll help you!" Fluffy began to climb down, and with Dog's help, she made it safely to the ground. Fluffy was happy to be back on the ground. She gave Dog a big hug and said, "Thank you, Dog! You're a good friend!" Dog just gave a little shrug and said, "That's what friends are for, Fluffy." And they walked home together, happy and safe.

Lila and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs and worms. They had a big shovel and a small shovel. Lila had the big shovel and Tom had the small shovel. "Look, Lila, I found a root!" Tom said. He held up a long, brown thing that came out of the ground. "It is very big and hard. What is it for?" Lila looked at the root. She remembered what her mom had told her. "It is for the plant, Tom. The root helps the plant get water and food from the dirt. It is like a straw for the plant." "Oh, I see. Can I pull it out?" Tom asked. "No, Tom, you can't. If you pull out the root, you will hurt the plant. The plant needs the root to live. It is not an ordinary thing. It is very important." Lila said. Tom nodded. He did not want to hurt the plant. He liked the plant. It had pretty flowers and smelled nice. He put the root back in the dirt and covered it with more dirt. He patted it gently. "Sorry, plant. I did not know. I will not pull your root again." Tom said. Lila smiled. She was proud of Tom. He was a good brother. He learned fast. She gave him a hug. "Good job, Tom. You helped the plant. You are very kind." Lila said. They continued to play in the garden. They had fun. They found more bugs and worms. They did not pull any more roots. They helped the plants. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big closet in his room. He liked to hide in the closet when he was bored. One day, Tim heard his mom and dad talking. They said they had a surprise for him. Tim was very excited. The next day, Tim's mom and dad gave him a new toy. It was a funny toy that could talk. The toy liked to tease Tim. It would say, "You can't catch me!" Tim would laugh and chase the toy around the room. But the toy was too fast for Tim. One day, Tim was playing with the toy near the closet. The toy teased, "You're too slow!" Tim got mad and threw the toy into the closet. The closet door closed. Tim tried to open the door, but it was stuck. He could hear the toy laughing inside. Tim cried and cried, but he could not open the door. The toy was stuck in the closet forever, and Tim was sad.
