Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Stick

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big stick on the ground. Tim picked up the stick and started to play with it. He swung it around and pretended it was a sword. Tim felt very happy. A girl named Sue saw Tim and walked up to him. She said, "Hey, that's my stick! Give it back!" Tim looked at her and said, "No, I found it. It's mine now." Sue was not happy. She wanted her stick back. Just then, they both heard a loud noise. They turned around and gazed at a big dog. The dog looked at the stick and barked. Tim and Sue were scared. They both thought the dog was stupid for barking at them. Tim threw the stick, and the dog ran after it. Sue and Tim laughed and became friends. They played together in the park and forgot all about the stick.

Once upon a time, there was a fragile little dog named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with a big bell. Every day, Tim would ring the bell to let his friends know it was time to play. One day, as Tim rang the bell, a big dog named Max came by. Max asked, "Why do you ring the bell, Tim?" Tim said, "I ring the bell to call my friends to play with me." Max thought it was a good idea and wanted to join in. So, Tim and Max played together every day. They would run, jump, and have lots of fun. And every day, Tim would ring the bell, and his friends would come running. The fragile little dog was very happy to have so many friends to play with, and they all loved hearing the bell ring.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in the woods. It was so tall that it could touch the sky. One day, a little bird came to sit on the tip of the tree. The bird looked around and began to wonder. "Hi, tree! Why are you so tall?" the bird asked. The tree did not know. It had always been tall. The bird and the tree became friends. They talked and played every day. The bird liked to fly high and the tree liked to watch. One day, a big wind came. The wind was strong and it shook the tree. The tree was scared. The little bird was scared too. They did not know what to do. The wind pushed and pushed until the tree fell down. The bird was sad. It missed its friend, the tall tree. But then, the bird had an idea. It flew around the woods and talked to the other trees. The bird asked the trees to help its friend. The trees agreed and they all worked together to help the tall tree stand up again. The tall tree was happy and thanked the little bird and the other trees. The moral of the story is that friends help each other when they are in trouble.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a gentle cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to mix things. She would mix colors, toys, and even her food. One day, Mimi found a big hook. She thought, "I can use this hook to mix things even better!" So, she took the hook and went to her friend, a little dog named Dodo. "Look, Dodo! I found a big hook. Let's mix things with it!" Mimi said. Dodo wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's do it!" So, Mimi and Dodo used the hook to mix all sorts of things. They mixed leaves, sticks, and even mud. They had so much fun playing together with the big hook. And from that day on, Mimi and Dodo were the best of friends, always mixing things and having fun.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wanted to travel. He liked to walk and see new things. One day, he found a big pear tree. The tree had many pears, but one was very special. It was the biggest and most yummy-looking pear of all. Tim tried to get the big pear, but it was too high. He jumped and jumped, but he could not reach it. Then, a kind bird saw Tim and wanted to help. The bird flew up and got the big pear for Tim. Tim was very happy and thanked the bird. But when Tim took a bite of the big pear, he found out it was dead inside! It was not yummy at all. Tim was sad, but then he saw the bird had brought him a new pear. This pear was not as big, but it was fresh and yummy. Tim learned that sometimes the best things are not always the biggest.
