Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Plane and the Bird

One day, a little plane named Tim was flying in the sky. Tim was a tough plane, but he felt lonely. He wanted to find a friend to play with. As he flew, he saw a big tree with a little bird on it. The bird was singing a happy song. Tim flew close to the tree and said, "Hi, bird! I am Tim, the plane. Will you come and play with me in the sky?" The bird looked at Tim and said, "Hi, Tim! I am Bob, the bird. I would love to come and play with you." So, Bob the bird flew up and started to play with Tim the plane. They flew high and low, fast and slow. They had so much fun together. Tim was happy to have a friend, and Bob was happy to meet a tough plane like Tim. As the sun began to set, Bob said, "Tim, I need to go back to my tree now. But can we play again tomorrow?" Tim smiled and said, "Of course, Bob! I can't wait to play with you again." From that day on, Tim the tough plane and Bob the bird became best friends. They played in the sky every day and were always happy to be together.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old bear. One day, he was walking through the forest when he saw a happy little bunny. Every time the bear said hello, the bunny smiled. The bear thought this was a brilliant display and grew very fond of the bunny. He asked the bunny why it always smiled. The bunny told him that it smiled to bring joy to others. The bear was so impressed with the bunny's wise answer that he smiled back. From then on, the bear and bunny were the best of friends. Every day, they would meet in the forest and display their friendship with big smiles.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She wanted to play and have fun. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Mia was very happy. At the park, Mia saw a big, loud dog. The dog said, "Woof! Woof!" Mia was a little scared, but she wanted to meet the dog. She walked up to the dog and said, "Hi, I'm Mia. What's your name?" The dog wagged its tail and said, "Woof! My name is Max." Mia and Max played together all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. At the end of the day, Mia said, "Max, you are a good friend. I had fun today." Max gave Mia a big, happy wink. They knew they would be friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Tom. Tom was very good at helping others. One day, Tom heard about an exam at the school. The exam was for little kids who wanted to be smart. Tom wanted to help the kids with their exam. So, he went to the school to see what he could do. When he got there, the kids were very happy to see him. They knew Tom was famous for helping others. Tom helped the kids practice for the exam. He showed them how to do things and helped them learn. The kids did very well on the exam because of Tom's help. They were so happy, and so was Tom. They all thanked Tom for helping them, and he knew he had done a good thing.

Anna and Ben like to play golf with their dad. Dad has a big bag with many sticks. He lets Anna and Ben choose one stick each. Anna picks a short stick and Ben picks a long stick. Dad says they can hit the small ball with the stick and try to make it go in the hole. They go to a big field with many holes. Dad shows them how to hold the stick and swing it. He hits the ball very far and it goes in the hole. Anna and Ben clap and say, "Wow, dad, you are good at golf!" Then it is Anna's turn. She holds the stick and swings it. But the ball does not go far. It rolls on the grass and stops. Anna says, "Oh, no, I am bad at golf!" Dad says, "No, Anna, you are not bad. You just need to practice more. And maybe you need a taller stick. Do you want to try Ben's stick?" Anna says, "Yes, please, dad. Can I try Ben's stick?" Ben says, "OK, Anna, you can try my stick. But you have to guess how tall it is." Anna looks at Ben's stick. It is much taller than her. She says, "Hmm, I guess it is as tall as a tree. Or maybe as tall as a house. Or maybe as tall as a giraffe." Ben laughs and says, "No, Anna, it is not that tall. It is as tall as me. See, it reaches my head. Can you guess how tall I am?" Anna says, "Yes, I can guess. You are as tall as a chair. Or maybe as tall as a table. Or maybe as tall as a dog." Ben says, "No, Anna, I am not that tall. I am as tall as four books. See, I can stack four books on the ground and they reach my head. Can you do that?" Anna says, "Yes, I can do that. I can stack four books on the ground and they reach my head too. Look, we are the same tall!" Ben says, "Yes, we are the same tall. And we are both good at guessing. And we are both good at golf. Let's try to hit the ball with my stick and see how far it goes." Anna says, "OK, let's try. Maybe it will go in the hole. Maybe we will be as good as dad." They take turns to hit the ball with Ben's stick. The ball goes farther than before. They run after it and try to find the hole. They have fun playing golf with Ben's stick. They are happy. Dad is happy too. He says, "You are both good at golf. And you are both good at guessing. And you are both good at being my kids. I love you."
