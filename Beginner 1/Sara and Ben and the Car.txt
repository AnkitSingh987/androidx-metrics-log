Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Car

Sara and Ben were going to the beach with Mom and Dad. They were very happy. They had to pack their bags with toys, clothes and snacks. Sara packed her doll, her hat and her book. Ben packed his ball, his shovel and his bucket. "Are we ready?" Mom asked. "Yes, Mom!" Sara and Ben said. They got in the car and drove to the beach. It was a sunny day. The water was blue and calm. Sara and Ben ran to the sand and started to play. They made a big sandcastle with their shovel and bucket. They decorated it with shells and stones. "Look, Mom, look, Dad!" Sara and Ben shouted. "We made a castle!" Mom and Dad smiled and took a picture. They were proud of Sara and Ben. Sara and Ben wanted to see more shells. They walked along the shore and looked for them. They found many different ones. Some were big, some were small, some were shiny, some were dull. "Wow, look at this one!" Ben said. He picked up a shell that was red and round. It had two claws and two eyes. "What is it?" Sara asked. "I don't know. Maybe it's a crab," Ben said. "A crab? Can I see?" Sara asked. Ben gave the shell to Sara. She held it in her hand and looked at it. It was cute. She liked it. "Can I keep it?" Sara asked. "Sure, why not?" Ben said. Sara put the shell in her pocket. She was happy. She had a new friend. They kept walking and looking for more shells. They did not see that the shell in Sara's pocket was moving. It was not a shell. It was a crab. And it was not happy. It did not like being in Sara's pocket. It wanted to go back to the water. It was angry. It pinched Sara's leg with its claw. Sara screamed. It hurt. She dropped the crab on the sand. The crab ran away. "Ow, ow, ow!" Sara cried. "What happened?" Ben asked. "The crab bit me!" Sara said. "What crab?" Ben asked. "The crab in my pocket!" Sara said. Ben looked at the crab. He saw that it was not a shell. He was scared. He did not like crabs. He ran away. "Help, help!" Ben shouted. Mom and Dad heard Sara and Ben. They ran to them. They saw Sara crying and Ben running. They saw the crab on the sand. They understood what happened. "Oh, no!" Mom said. They picked up Sara and Ben. They checked Sara's leg. It was red and swollen. It needed a bandage. They took Sara and Ben to the car. They left the beach. They went to the doctor. Sara and Ben were sad. They did not have fun at the beach. They did not like crabs. They learned a lesson. They should not pick up things they do not know. They should be careful. They should listen to Mom and Dad.

Once upon a time, there was a panda. The panda was bossy. He liked to tell his friends what to do. One day, he went to play with his friends. The bossy panda told his friends to seat. He said, "Seat here, seat there." His friends did what he said. They sat down and listened to the bossy panda. But then, one little friend said, "Let's play together, not just listen to the bossy panda." The friends liked the idea. They all played together and had lots of fun. The bossy panda learned that it is better to play with friends than to be bossy.

One day, a quiet boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. Tim wanted to write his name on a leaf. He picked up a stick and started to write. Another boy named Sam came to the tree. Sam said, "I want to write my name too!" But there was only one stick. Tim and Sam did not know what to do. They both wanted the stick to write their names on the leaves. Then, a little bird flew down from the tree. The bird had a stick in its mouth. The bird dropped the stick and flew away. Now, Tim and Sam both had a stick to write their names. They were very happy and became good friends.
