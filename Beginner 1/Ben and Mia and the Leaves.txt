Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Leaves

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play outside in the park. They see a big hill with many leaves. They want to jump in the leaves and have fun. They run to the hill and start to jump. The leaves make a crunchy sound. They laugh and throw leaves at each other. They are happy. But then the wind comes. The wind is cool and strong. It blows the leaves away from the hill. Ben and Mia are sad. They want to play with the leaves. "Stop, wind, stop!" Ben shouts. "We want to jump in the leaves!" "Please, wind, please!" Mia begs. "We like the leaves!" But the wind does not listen. It keeps blowing the leaves far away. Ben and Mia are angry. They do not like the wind. They decide to chase the leaves. They run after the wind and the leaves. They hope to catch some leaves and bring them back to the hill. They run and run. They see some leaves on the ground. They try to grab them. But the wind is faster. It lifts the leaves up and carries them away. Ben and Mia are tired. They stop running. They look at the empty hill. They feel sad. They sit on the grass and hug each other. They are still friends. They do not need the leaves to have fun. They look at the sky. They see some clouds. The clouds are white and fluffy. They look like animals. "Look, Ben, look!" Mia says. "That cloud looks like a dog!" "You are right, Mia, you are right!" Ben says. "And that cloud looks like a cat!" They smile and point at the clouds. They make up stories about the clouds. They forget about the wind and the leaves. They are happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a giant. The giant was very dirty. He lived in a big cave. The giant had a small friend, a little bird. They liked to play together. One day, the bird said, "Giant, you are so dirty! Can I help you get clean?" The giant said, "Yes, please! I want to be clean, too." So, the bird tried to help the giant. They went to the river to wash the dirt off. But the giant was too big for the little bird to help. The bird could not see all the dirt on the giant. The giant was still dirty. The bird was sad. The giant was sad, too. They could not make the giant clean. The giant stayed dirty, and they both were unhappy.

Once upon a time, in a normal town, there was a man who would deliver boxes in the evening. He liked his job very much. Every day, he would put the boxes in his big truck and drive around to give them to the people in the town. One day, while he was delivering boxes, he saw a big cat in the road. The cat was not a normal cat. It was very big and had a long tail. The man did not know what to do. He stopped his truck and looked at the cat. The big cat looked at the man and said, "I need your help." The man was very surprised that the cat could talk. The cat told the man that it was lost and needed to find its way home. The man decided to help the cat. They went on a big adventure together and found the cat's home. The man and the cat became good friends, and the man continued to deliver boxes in the evening.

Johnny was so excited that it was a sunny day. He wanted to go outside and have some fun. He ran out to his play area and saw a big tree with lots of branches. "Let's climb it!" Johnny shouted out. He started to climb the tree and soon reached the top. He felt brave and looked around. Suddenly, he saw something to pick up! He quickly got the pick and said, "This is so cool!" He started to climb back down, but he suddenly had a crazy idea - he wanted to climb back up to the top! He thought it was a naughty thing to do, but he climbed up again. He looked around and smiled. At the bottom, his mom saw him and said, "Johnny, you were climbing so high! You're so brave, don't be naughty again!" Johnny said, "Ok Mom!" and ran off full of excitement.
