Category: Beginner
Title: The Thin Crane and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a thin crane. The crane lived near a pond. It loved to catch fish and play with its friends. One day, the crane's friend, a frog, said, "My home is broken. Can you help me replace it?" The crane said, "Yes, I will help you!" The crane and the frog went to find a new home. They found a big rock near the pond. The crane used its long beak to move the rock. The frog said, "Thank you, crane! This is a great new home!" The crane and the frog were very happy. They played together every day. The thin crane was glad that it could help its friend. They lived happily ever after near the pond.

Tim and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big sign in the park. The sign says: "No dogs allowed. This is the law." Tim and Jen love dogs. They have a dog at home. His name is Spot. They want to bring Spot to the park. They think the sign is not fair. They make a plan. They say: "We will bring Spot to the park at night. No one will see us. We will have fun with Spot." That night, they sneak out of their houses. They take Spot with them. They go to the park. They run and play with Spot. They are happy. But then, they hear a voice. The voice says: "Hey, you kids! What are you doing here? Don't you know the law? No dogs allowed in the park!" Tim and Jen look up. They see a man. The man is a guard. He is nosy. He likes to catch people who break the law. Tim and Jen are scared. They say: "Sorry, sir. We didn't know. We just wanted to play with our dog. Please don't be mad." The guard is not happy. He says: "You kids are in trouble. You have to go home now. And take your dog with you. And don't come back. Or I will tell your parents." Tim and Jen nod. They say: "OK, sir. We will go home. We are sorry." They take Spot and run away. They are sad. They think the guard is mean. They wish they could play with Spot in the park. They hope he will not tell their parents. They say: "Maybe we can find another park. A park where dogs are allowed. A park where no one is nosy."

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Tom and Sam. Tom and Sam wanted to have fun. So, Sam had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game!" Tom said, "Yes!" So, Sam got some scissors out. He said, "Whoever wins the scissors game will get a prize!" Tom and Sam took turns holding the scissors. Each time, they tried their best to win. Finally, Tom won the game! Sam said, "Yay! You won the game, Tom! Here's your prize!" Tom smiled. He was so happy. But then he got a little sleepy. So, they decided to take a nap. They cuddled up in a blanket and had the best sleep ever!

One day, a modest bird named Tim went for a soar in the sky. Tim was a small bird with big dreams. He loved to fly high and look at the trees and houses below. While flying, he saw a little boy named Ben sitting on the grass, holding his knee. "Ouch!" Ben cried. "My knee hurts!" Tim heard Ben and flew down to help him. "What happened?" Tim asked. Ben looked surprised to see a talking bird, but he answered, "I fell and hurt my knee." Tim wanted to help Ben, so he had an idea. He told Ben to close his eyes and count to three. Ben did what Tim said, and when he opened his eyes, he saw a big, colorful bandage on his knee. Ben was so happy that his new friend Tim helped him. They played together all day, and Ben forgot all about his hurt knee.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim and his cat, Max, lived in a little house. Tim loved to eat toast for breakfast. One day, Tim wanted to make his toast cool, so he put it in the fridge. Max saw the toast in the fridge and said, "No, Tim! Toast should be warm." But Tim did not listen to Max. He wanted to try cool toast. Max was sad and decided to quit helping Tim. The next morning, Tim ate the cool toast. It did not taste good at all. Tim was sad and missed Max's help. He realized that Max was right all along. From that day on, Tim always ate warm toast, but Max never helped him again.
