Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Horse

One day, a girl named Sue went to a stable. She saw a big horse. The big horse was sad. Sue wanted to make the big horse glad. Sue said, "Can I sit on you, big horse?" The big horse said, "Yes, you can." So, Sue sat on the big horse. The big horse started to feel glad. They went for a walk around the stable. They saw a small cat. The small cat was in a tree. But then, something unexpected happened! The big horse turned into a small horse. The small cat jumped down from the tree. The small cat turned into a big cat! The big cat said, "Thank you for making the big horse glad. Now, I am a big cat and the horse is small." Sue was very surprised, but she was still glad. She said, "I am happy to help. I like making friends glad." The big cat, the small horse, and Sue all played together. They were all very glad.

One day, a cool cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big wreck on the road. It was a mess. Tim wanted to help. Tim started to scatter the broken things away from the road. He worked hard. Soon, the road was clean. Tim felt happy. A little girl saw Tim and said, "Thank you, Tim, for cleaning the wreck!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome. I like to help." They became friends and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max was a good dog, but he liked to play in the mud. One day, Max saw a big, brown ball. He wanted to play with it, so he ran to get it. But the ball was stuck in the mud. Max started to struggle to get it out. While Max was struggling, a big bird saw him. The bird wanted to help Max, so it flew down and tried to pull the ball out, too. They both pulled and pulled, but the ball would not come out. Max and the bird were very tired and sad. Then, a mean man saw Max and the bird. The man did not like animals playing near his house. He took Max and the bird to a small jail. Max and the bird were very sad in the jail. They could not play with the ball anymore. The brown ball stayed in the mud, and Max and the bird were stuck in the jail.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends: Tom and Sally. They were always very happy together. One day, Tom and Sally were playing with their favorite toy, a chess set. They loved zooming the little chess pieces around the board. Soon, it was time for them to go home. They said goodbye, but promised to play chess on the board again soon. They were so happy when they hugged each other goodbye. They zoomed off to their homes, looking forward to the next time they could play chess together again.

One day, a clever cat named Zip went to play outside. Zip liked to jump and run in the sun. Zip saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. So, Zip climbed the tree very fast. When Zip was high in the tree, he saw a bird. The bird said, "Hi, I am Bill. Do you want to play with me?" Zip said, "Yes, I want to play with you. But I am a cat, and I cannot fly." Bill the bird had an idea. "I will call my friend, the bug. The bug can help you fly." Bill called the bug, and the bug came to help. The bug was strong and could carry Zip. Zip held on to the bug, and they flew in the sky. Zip, Bill, and the bug had a fun day playing together. The clever cat, Zip, was happy he made new friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a wide puddle and had an urge to jump in it. So, she took a big leap and splashed in the puddle. Her feet got all wet and muddy. Lily's mom saw her and said, "Lily, you need to wash your feet now. They are very dirty!" Lily nodded and went to the small pool of water nearby. She washed her feet until they were clean again. As Lily played in the park, she saw a little boy who also jumped in the puddle. He got muddy too, so Lily told him, "You should wash your feet like I did." The little boy listened to Lily and washed his feet. They became friends and played together in the park all day.
