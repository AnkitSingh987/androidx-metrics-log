Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Stick

Once upon a time, in a big land, there lived a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play with his friends. They played catch and hide-and-seek. Spot was very happy. One day, Spot and his friends were playing a game. Spot wanted to win. He ran very fast, but he did not look where he was going. Spot fell into a big hole. He felt sad and ashamed. Spot's friends came to help him. They found a long stick and put it into the hole. Spot climbed up the stick and got out. The game had to end, but Spot was happy to be with his friends. He said "thank you" and they all went home. From that day on, Spot always looked where he was going.

One day, a cat and a dog were friends. The cat found a big jug. Inside the jug, there was a delicious drink. The cat and dog wanted to take the jug to their home. But the jug was very heavy. The cat said, "Let's drive the jug home." The dog agreed. They tried to push the jug onto a small car. The cat pushed and the dog pulled. But the jug was too big and it did not fit. The cat and dog were sad. They wanted the delicious drink. Then, a big, strong bear came. The bear saw the cat and dog. He wanted to help. The bear picked up the jug and put it on his back. He walked with the cat and dog to their home. At home, they all shared the delicious drink. The cat, dog, and bear were happy. They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a weird paper on the floor. He picked it up and looked at it. It made him feel happy. The paper had a funny face drawn on it. Tim showed the paper to his friend, Sue. "Look at this!" he said. Sue looked at the paper and laughed. "That is funny!" she said. They both felt happy looking at the funny face on the paper. Tim and Sue decided to draw more funny faces on more papers. They took their crayons and drew big smiles and silly eyes. They felt very happy making their friends laugh with their weird papers.

Ben and Sam are friends. They like to play with blocks. They work hard to make a big tower. They put one block on top of another block. They make a line of blocks. "Look at our tower!" Ben says. "It is so tall and strong!" Sam nods. He is happy. He wants to make the tower taller. He reaches for another block. But he bumps the tower. The blocks fall down. They make a loud noise. "Oh no!" Sam says. "We broke our tower!" Ben is sad. He looks at the blocks. They are rough and messy. He does not want to play anymore. He starts to cry. Sam feels sorry. He does not want Ben to cry. He hugs Ben. He says, "It's okay, Ben. We can work again. We can make a new tower. We can make it better." Ben stops crying. He looks at Sam. He smiles. He says, "Okay, Sam. You are a good friend. Let's work again. Let's make a new tower. Let's make it fun." They pick up the blocks. They work together. They make a new tower. They make a new line of blocks. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Sue found a hard rock in her yard. It was not like the other rocks. This rock was special. Sue took the rock with her everywhere she went. She even gave it a name, "Rocky". Sue and Rocky played all day long. They played in the park, they played in the sand, and they played in the water. At night, Sue would hug Rocky tight when she went to sleep. One day, Sue lost Rocky while playing at the park. She was very sad and missed her hard rock friend. Sue looked and looked for Rocky, but she could not find him. Just when she was about to give up, she found Rocky hiding under a big tree. Sue was so happy that she hugged Rocky and took him home. They were best friends forever.
