Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Nervous

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He was nervous because he was going to play in a big stadium. Tim loved to kick a ball with his friends. Today, they were going to play a game. When they got to the stadium, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Hi, Tim! Are you ready to play?" Tim said, "Yes, but I am nervous." Sam said, "Don't worry, we will have fun!" The game started, and Tim kicked the ball. He ran fast and scored a goal. His friends cheered, and Tim felt happy. They played all day and had a great time. In the end, Tim wasn't nervous anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" Tim started to climb the tree and saw a bird. The bird was singing a pretty song. Tim saw something in the tree and said, "Look, Mom!" He pointed at a ghost hiding in the tree. The ghost was friendly and smiled at Tim. Tim was not scared. He said, "Hi, ghost! Want to play?" The ghost said, "Yes, let's play!" Tim and the ghost played in the park all day. They played with the ball and ran around the tree. When it was time to go home, Tim's mom gave him a yummy apple. Tim shared the yummy apple with the ghost. They both smiled and said, "Yummy!"

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a humble boy named Tom. He had a piece of chalk that he loved very much. One day, Tom went for a walk and found a big wall. He decided to draw a big smiley face on the wall with his chalk. As Tom was drawing, a small bird came up to him and said, "Tom, I am cold and I tremble. Can you help me?" Tom looked at the bird and saw that it was shaking. He felt sad for the bird and wanted to help. Tom picked up the bird and took it to his warm house. He gave the bird some food and made it a soft bed. The bird was very happy and stopped trembling. They became good friends and played together every day. The bird told other animals about Tom's kindness, and they all loved him too. The moral of the story is: always be kind and help others in need, and you will make many friends.

Once upon a time, there was a light little bird named Blue. Blue had a best friend named Red. They loved to play together in the sun. One day, Blue saw Red had a stitch in his wing. Blue wanted to help his friend. Blue reminded Red to be careful when they played. The next day, Red's stitch was gone. Blue and Red played happily in the sun again. They were always there to help each other. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with dolls and cars. But today, they have a fight. Lily wants to play with the pink car, and Tom wants to play with the blue car. They both pull the cars and shout. "Give me the car!" Lily says. "No, it is mine!" Tom says. They are very angry and sad. They do not care about each other. A fat cat sees them. The cat is hungry and wants to eat the cars. The cat sneaks up to them and grabs the cars with its paws. Lily and Tom see the cat and scream. "Help! The cat took our cars!" Lily says. "Go away, cat! Give us back our cars!" Tom says. They run after the cat and try to get their cars. The cat is scared and runs away. Lily and Tom catch up with the cat and take their cars back. They hug their cars and smile. "Are you okay, Lily?" Tom says. "Yes, I am okay, Tom. I am sorry I was mean to you." Lily says. "I am sorry, too. You can play with the pink car if you want." Tom says. "Thank you, Tom. You can play with the blue car if you want." Lily says. They are happy and friendly. They care about each other. They play with the cars and the dolls. The fat cat watches them and wishes it had a friend.
