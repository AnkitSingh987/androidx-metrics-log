Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Rainbow

Once upon a time, there lived a brave little girl named Amy. She was always playing by the sea and making sand castles, sailing in boats and looking for treasures. One day, she and her friends paddled out to the middle of the sea. They laughed and shouted and had so much fun. Suddenly, a huge wave hit their boat! Amy was scared. "What's wrong, Amy?" asked one of her friends. "I didn't listen to my mom and I have been punished," replied Amy sadly. The others paddled back to shore. Then, a perfect rainbow appeared over the sea. "I promise to listen to my mom. No more punishment!" said Amy. She smiled as she and her friends watched the rainbow.

Lily and Ben like to play with their toys. They have many toys in their room. But their room is not tidy. There are toys on the floor, on the bed, on the desk, and in the closet. Mom and Dad are not happy. They say, "Lily and Ben, you have too many toys. You need to make your room tidy. You need to sell some of your toys and give them to other kids who have no toys." Lily and Ben do not want to sell their toys. They love their toys. They say, "No, Mom and Dad, we do not want to sell our toys. They are ours. We need them. They have names. This is Teddy, and this is Bunny, and this is Doll, and this is Car, and this is Ball, and this is Train, and this is Book, and this is Puzzle, and this is..." Mom and Dad say, "Stop, Lily and Ben, you do not need so many toys. You can keep some of them, but you have to choose which ones. You have to sell the rest. It is good to share. It is good to be tidy. It is good to have space." Lily and Ben are sad. They do not know how to choose. They look at their toys. They hug their toys. They say, "We are sorry, toys, we do not want to sell you. But we have to. Mom and Dad say so. We hope you find a good home. We hope you make other kids happy. We will miss you." They put some of their toys in a big box. They write "For Sale" on the box. They take the box to the front yard. They wait for other kids to come and buy their toys. Soon, other kids come and see the box. They say, "Wow, look at all these toys. They are nice. How much are they?" Lily and Ben say, "They are one dollar each. Or you can trade with us. Do you have something we like?" Some kids give Lily and Ben money. Some kids give Lily and Ben other things, like stickers, or crayons, or cookies. Lily and Ben say, "Thank you, kids, we hope you like our toys. They have names. This is Teddy, and this is Bunny, and this is Doll, and this is Car, and this is Ball, and this is Train, and this is Book, and this is Puzzle, and this is..." The other kids say, "Thank you, Lily and Ben, we love your toys. We will take good care of them. We will give them new names. This is Fluffy, and this is Hoppy, and this is Anna, and this is Zoom, and this is Bounce, and this is Choo-Choo, and this is Read, and this is Fun, and this is..." Lily and Ben sell all their toys in the box. They have some money and some new things. They go back to their room. They put their money and their new things in a jar. They look at their room. It is tidy. There are only a few toys left. They say, "We did it, Mom and Dad, we made our room tidy. We sold some of our toys and gave them to other kids. We have some money and some new things. We are happy. But we are also a little sad. We miss our toys. We hope they are happy too." Mom and Dad say, "We are proud of you, Lily and Ben, you did a good thing. You shared your toys. You made your room tidy. You have some money and some new things. You are happy. And you still have some toys. You can play with them. You can love them. You can give them new names. This is..."
