Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Park

Tom and Lily are brother and sister. They like to play in the park with their mom and dad. One day, they see a big fountain in the park. It has water that goes up and down. Tom and Lily want to touch the water. They run to the fountain and put their hands in the water. It is cold and wet. They splash and laugh. Mom and dad watch them and smile. They are happy. But then, Tom sees something shiny in the water. It is a wallet. Someone left it in the fountain. Tom picks it up and shows it to Lily. Lily opens it and sees money and cards. She thinks it is a toy. She says, "Look, Tom, we have a new toy. Let's play with it." Tom says, "OK, let's play with it." They take the money and cards out of the wallet and pretend they are buying things. They say, "I want to buy a yummy ice cream. I want to buy a big balloon. I want to buy a new bike." They have fun with the wallet. But then, mom and dad see them with the wallet. They are surprised and worried. They say, "Tom, Lily, where did you get that wallet? That is not a toy. That is someone's wallet. You have to give it back." Tom and Lily are confused. They say, "But we found it in the fountain. We thought it was a toy. We just wanted to play with it." Mom and dad say, "No, it is not a toy. It is very important. Someone is looking for it. They need it to buy things and to go places. You have to be honest and kind and give it back." Tom and Lily feel sorry. They say, "OK, we will give it back. We are sorry. We did not know." They put the money and cards back in the wallet and give it to mom and dad. Mom and dad say, "Thank you, Tom and Lily. You did the right thing. We are proud of you. You are good children." They hug them and kiss them. They say, "Let's go and find the owner of the wallet. Maybe they are still in the park. Maybe they are sad and worried. Maybe we can make them happy and relieved." They walk around the park and look for the owner of the wallet. They see a man sitting on a bench. He looks sad and worried. He has a bottle of water in his hand. He is thirsty. Mom and dad say, "Excuse me, sir, is this your wallet?" They show him the wallet. The man looks at the wallet and his eyes light up. He says, "Yes, yes, that is my wallet. Thank you, thank you, thank you. You are very kind and honest. You saved my day. I lost it in the fountain. I was looking for it everywhere. I need it to buy things and to go places. Thank you, thank you, thank you." He takes the wallet and hugs mom and dad. He says, "How can I thank you? How can I repay you?" Mom and dad say, "No, no, you don't have to thank us or repay us. We are just happy to help you. We are glad you have your wallet back." The man says, "But I want to do something for you. You have two lovely children. They are very good and honest. They found my wallet and gave it back to you. They are very special. Please, let me buy them something. Let me buy them a yummy ice cream. Let me buy them a big balloon. Let me buy them a new bike." Tom and Lily are surprised and happy. They say, "Really? You want to buy us something? You are very nice and generous. Thank you, thank you, thank you." The man says, "Yes, yes, I want to buy you something. Come with me. Let's go to the ice cream shop. Let's go to the balloon stand. Let's go to the bike store. You can choose whatever you want. You deserve it. You are very good and honest children." Tom and Lily say, "Yay, yay, yay. We are so happy. We are so lucky. We love ice cream. We love balloons. We love bikes. Thank you, thank you, thank you." They go with the man and mom and dad. They buy a yummy ice cream. They buy a big balloon. They buy a new bike. They are very happy. They say, "This is the best day ever. We are so happy. We are so lucky. Thank you, thank you, thank you." They eat the ice cream. They drink the water. They play with the balloons. They ride the bikes. They have fun
