Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy sail. One day, he took his sail to the park with his mom. The park had a big pond. Tim wanted to see his sail zoom on the water. Tim put his sail in the pond. The wind was thick, and it made the sail zoom fast. Tim was so happy to see his sail zoom. He said, "Mom, look! My sail is zooming so fast!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, it is very fast!" Soon, it was time to go home. Tim took his sail out of the water. He couldn't wait to come back to the park and play with his sail again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big tree with pretty leaves. The leaves had a pattern on them. Tim thought it was an ordinary tree, but his mom said, "This tree seems special, Tim." They walked closer to the tree and saw a little door on it. Tim asked, "Mom, can we open the door?" His mom said, "Sure, let's see what's inside." When they opened the door, they found a little room with tiny chairs and a table. Suddenly, they heard a tiny voice say, "Hello, Tim and Mom!" They looked down and saw a little mouse dressed like a person. The mouse said, "I live here with my family. We made the pattern on the leaves!" Tim and his mom were surprised, but they made new friends with the mouse family.

Lily and Tom are playing in the kitchen. They see a big knife on the table. It is shiny and sharp. Lily wants to touch it, but Tom says no. He says it is not for kids. It can hurt them. Lily does not listen. She reaches for the knife. Tom tries to stop her. He pulls her hand away. The knife falls on the floor. It makes a loud noise. Lily and Tom are scared. They run to the living room. Mom hears the noise. She comes to the kitchen. She sees the knife on the floor. She is angry. She asks Lily and Tom what they did. Lily and Tom look at each other. They do not know what to say. Mom picks up the knife. She puts it in a drawer. She tells Lily and Tom to never touch it again. She says it is dangerous. She says they could have cut themselves. She says they are lucky they are not hurt. Lily and Tom feel sorry. They say they are sorry. They hug Mom. Mom hugs them back. She says she loves them. She says they are good kids. She says they should be careful. She smiles at them. Lily and Tom smile back. They see Mom's wide teeth. They laugh. Mom laughs too. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big blue sea. He had many friends, like the big whale and the tiny crab. They all liked to play in the wet water together. One day, Fin saw a new friend. It was a fly, and it was stuck in the water. The fly couldn't swim, and it was very wet. Fin wanted to help the fly, so he swam close and let the fly climb onto his back. Fin swam to the shore, where the fly could dry off and be safe. The fly was very happy and thanked Fin for his help. From that day on, Fin and the fly were best friends. They played together every day, and Fin learned that even the smallest friends can be the most special.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He loved to swim all day with his friends. One day, a big bad shark came to their home. The shark said, "I want to swim with you!" But the fish were scared. They knew the shark was bad and could hurt them. So, they swam away very fast. Fin had an idea. He said to his friends, "Let's swim to the end of the sea and hide there." All the fish swam as fast as they could to the end of the sea. When they reached the end, they saw a big rock. They all hid behind the rock. The bad shark could not find them. He swam away, sad and alone. From that day on, Fin and his friends swam happily together. They knew that they were safe at the end of the sea, far away from the bad shark.
