Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Book

Ben and Mia are hungry. They want to eat food. Mom says they can have food when they finish their homework. Ben and Mia do not like homework. They think it is hard and boring. They look at the clock. It is almost time for food. They try to do their homework faster. But they do not know some words. They ask Mom for help. Mom is busy. She is making food in the kitchen. She says they can use the book or the computer to find the words. Ben and Mia go to the book. The book has many pages. They do not know which page to look at. They go to the computer. The computer has many buttons. They do not know which button to press. They feel sad and angry. They hear a beep. Mom says food is ready. Ben and Mia run to the table. They see food on the plates. The food looks good. It smells good. It is big food. There is pizza, salad, chicken, and cake. Ben and Mia stare at the food. They want to eat it all. Mom says they can eat food if they show her their homework. Ben and Mia look at their homework. Their homework is not done. They have many blanks and scribbles. They feel bad. They know they did not try hard. They say sorry to Mom. Mom says it is okay. She says she loves them. She says they can eat food, but they have to finish their homework later. She says homework is important, but food is important too. She says they can use the book or the computer or ask her for help. She says she is proud of them for trying. Ben and Mia smile. They say thank you to Mom. They eat food. They like food. They think food is yummy. They think Mom is the best. They say they will do their homework better next time. They are happy.

Lily and Tom were playing with their dolls. Lily had a modern doll with long hair and a pink dress. Tom had an old doll with short hair and a blue shirt. Lily liked to cut her doll's hair and make new styles. Tom liked to stitch his doll's shirt and make new patterns. One day, Lily wanted to cut Tom's doll's hair. She said, "Your doll's hair is too short and boring. Let me cut it and make it modern." Tom said, "No, I like my doll's hair. It is neat and tidy. Do not cut it." Lily did not listen. She took scissors and snipped Tom's doll's hair. Tom was very angry. He said, "You are mean. You ruined my doll's hair. Now it is uneven and ugly." He grabbed Lily's doll and cut her dress. Lily was very sad. She said, "You are mean. You ruined my doll's dress. Now it is torn and dirty." They both started to cry. Their mom heard them and came to see what was wrong. She saw their dolls and said, "What did you do? You both cut your dolls and made them look bad. Why did you do that?" Lily and Tom said, "We are sorry, mom. We were playing and we got mad at each other. We did not mean to hurt our dolls." Mom said, "You should not cut your dolls or each other's dolls. You should be kind and respectful. Cutting is not a good way to play. It can hurt and damage things. If you want to change your dolls, you can use other things, like ribbons, stickers, or hats. Do you understand?" Lily and Tom said, "Yes, mom. We understand. We are sorry." Mom said, "It's okay. I forgive you. But you have to fix your dolls. You can use some stitch and glue to mend them. Do you want to do that?" Lily and Tom said, "Yes, mom. We want to do that. Thank you, mom." Mom said, "You're welcome. I love you both. Now, let's go and fix your dolls." They hugged mom and went to fix their dolls. They learned their lesson and did not cut their dolls again. They played nicely and shared their dolls. They made their dolls look modern and happy.
