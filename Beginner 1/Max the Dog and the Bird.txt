Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very excited to play with his friend, a big cat named Sam. Max and Sam loved to play in the park together. One day, Max and Sam were playing when they saw a small bird. The bird looked helpless. It could not fly. Max and Sam wanted to help the bird. They went near the bird and Max said, "Don't worry, little bird. We will help you." Max used his ear to listen to the bird's problem. The bird said, "I hurt my wing. I can't fly." Max and Sam thought about how they could help the bird. They decided to find a soft bed for the bird to rest. Max and Sam found a nice, soft bed for the bird. They brought it to the bird and helped it get comfortable. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max and Sam. You are good friends." Max and Sam were excited that they could help the bird feel better. They played in the park and took care of the bird until it could fly again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved going to the playground. Every day, she and her best friend would play together until it was time to go home. One day, when they were playing, her friend ran right into another little boy. He started to cry and called out to his mom. His mom came over and she was very angry. She glared at the little girl and said, "It's very important to obey the law! You should explain it to your friend." The little girl felt very sorry for her friend and nodded. She said to the little boy, "We have to always follow the law because it helps us stay safe." The little boy looked at her and said, "OK, I will remember. I'm sorry." The little girl was happy that her friend had learned his lesson, and she smiled. She learned a very important lesson that day, which was to always explain the law to the people around us because following the law is important for our safety.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. Lily found a big worm and showed it to Ben. Ben said, "Ew, that is disgusting! Put it back!" Lily said, "No, it is my friend. I will keep it in a jar." She ran inside the house to find a jar. Ben followed her, but he stopped at the door. He saw something on the roof. It was big and black and furry. It had a long tail and sharp teeth. It was a rat. Ben was scared. He said, "Lily, come back! There is a rat on the roof!" But Lily did not hear him. She was in the kitchen, looking for a jar. The rat saw Ben and jumped down from the roof. It landed on Ben's head and bit his ear. Ben screamed and ran away. Lily heard him and came out of the kitchen. She saw the rat on the ground and Ben crying. She said, "What happened? What is that?" Ben said, "It is a rat! It was on the roof and it bit me!" Lily said, "Oh no, that is disgusting! We need to tell mom!" They ran to the house and found mom. Mom saw Ben's ear and said, "What happened to you? You are bleeding!" Ben said, "A rat bit me! It was on the roof!" Mom said, "A rat? On the roof? That is not good. We need to call the pest control." She took Ben to the bathroom and cleaned his ear. She put a band-aid on it and gave him a hug. She said, "You are brave, Ben. I am sorry that happened to you." Lily felt bad for Ben. She said, "I am sorry too, Ben. I was not listening to you. I was busy with my worm." She showed him the jar with the worm inside. Ben said, "That is okay, Lily. I am glad you have a friend. But can you please keep it away from me? I do not like worms. They are disgusting." Lily said, "Okay, Ben. I will keep it in my room. But can I show it to dad when he comes home?" Ben said, "Sure, Lily. But do not let the rat see it. It might want to eat it." They laughed and hugged. They were happy to have each other.
