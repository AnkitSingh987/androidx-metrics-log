Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Graceful Pirate and the Treasure

One day, a graceful pirate named Bob went to the sea. He wanted to find a big treasure. He had a big boat and a map. The map showed him where to go. Bob was very happy. Bob met another pirate named Tom. Tom said, "I want to find the treasure too!" Bob said, "We can look for it together." So, they went on the big boat and looked at the map. They sailed and sailed. At last, they found the treasure on a small island. Bob and Tom were very happy. They shared the treasure with each other. They learned that working together is good. And they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He liked to walk on the road every day. One day, he saw a big rock on the road. The bald man tried to move the rock, but it was too heavy. He sat on the rock and thought. Then, a little bird came to the rock and sat next to the bald man. The bird started to sing, and the rock began to move! The bald man and the bird were surprised. They had found a magic rock that moved when the bird sang. Now, the bald man and the bird were friends, and they went on fun walks together on the road.

Ben likes to help his dad in the garden. His dad has many tools that he uses to dig, cut and plant. Ben wants to use the tools too, but his dad says some of them are sharp and can hurt him. He tells Ben to be careful and only use the tools that are safe for him. One day, Ben sees a big tool that looks like a knife. It has a long handle and a shiny blade. Ben thinks it is a cool tool and he wants to try it. He picks it up and swings it around. He feels strong and proud. He does not see the rose bush behind him. The tool hits the rose bush and makes a loud noise. Ben feels a pain in his hand. He drops the tool and looks at his hand. He sees blood and a thorn. He starts to cry. His dad hears him and runs to him. He sees the tool and the rose bush and the blood. He is angry and scared. He picks up Ben and takes him inside. He washes his hand and puts a bandage on it. He tells Ben that he was very naughty and that he should never touch the sharp tool again. He says that the tool is for grown-ups only and that it can cut things and people. He says that Ben could have hurt himself very badly. Ben feels sorry and sad. He says he is sorry to his dad and that he will not touch the sharp tool again. He says he wanted to help his dad and be like him. His dad hugs him and says he loves him. He says he is proud of Ben for wanting to help, but that he has to be careful and listen to his dad. He says he will show him how to use the safe tools next time. Ben smiles and says he loves his dad too. He says he will be a good helper and a good listener.

One day, a baby was playing with his toys. He had a lot of fun. His mom was in the room too. The baby did something unusual. He threw his toy in the air. The toy hit a cup. The cup fell and made a mess. "Oh no!" said the mom. "Look at the mess!" The baby looked at the mess and felt sad. The mom got a cloth to wipe the mess. The baby wanted to help. He took the cloth from his mom and started to wipe the mess too. As they wiped, they heard a sound. It was the toy that made the sound! The toy could talk! "Thank you for cleaning," said the toy. The baby and mom were very surprised. They never knew the toy could talk. Now, they had a new friend to play with.
