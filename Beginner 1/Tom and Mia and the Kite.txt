Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Kite

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play outside. They have a big kite. The kite is red and blue and has a long tail. They want to make the kite soar in the sky. They run with the kite. The wind is strong. The kite goes up, up, up. They hold the string. They are happy. They see the kite soar high and fast. They laugh and shout. "Look, Tom, the kite is like a bird!" Mia says. "Yes, Mia, it is. It is a funny bird. It has no wings, only paper and sticks." Tom says. They watch the kite for a long time. They have fun. Then, they hear a bell. It is time to go inside. They have to do math. Math is hard. They do not like math. They pull the kite down. They fold it and put it in a bag. They walk to the door. They are sad. They do not want to do math. They want to play with the kite. "Can we play with the kite again tomorrow?" Mia asks. "Sure, Mia, we can. Maybe we can learn some math from the kite. How many colors does it have? How long is the tail? How high does it soar?" Tom says. Mia thinks. She smiles. She likes Tom's idea. Maybe math can be funny, too. Maybe math can help them make the kite soar better. She nods. "Okay, Tom, let's do that. Let's do math and then play with the kite. That sounds fun." Mia says. They go inside. They do math. They learn some new things. They are not sad anymore. They are proud. They are ready to play with the kite again. They are friends. They have a big kite. The kite is red and blue and has a long tail. They make the kite soar in the sky.

Lily likes to cook with her mom. She helps her mom stir the soup, break the eggs and cut the cheese. She wears a big apron and a chef hat. She feels happy and proud. One day, Lily sees a cat outside the window. The cat is flexible. It can jump and twist and curl. Lily wants to play with the cat. She asks her mom, "Can I go outside and see the cat?" Her mom says, "Not now, Lily. We have to finish cooking. The cat will wait for you." Lily says, "But I want to see the cat now. It is so cute and fun. Please, mom, please." Her mom says, "No, Lily. You have to be patient. Cooking is important. We have to make dinner for dad. He will be home soon." Lily is sad and angry. She does not want to cook anymore. She wants to see the cat. She throws her spoon on the floor and runs to the door. She tries to open it, but it is locked. She cries and screams. Her mom is surprised and upset. She says, "Lily, what are you doing? Why are you acting like this? You have to calm down and listen to me. You can see the cat later, but you have to help me cook now. It is not nice to throw things and run away." Lily does not listen. She keeps crying and screaming. She does not care about cooking or dinner or dad. She only cares about the cat. The cat hears Lily's noise. It is curious and friendly. It comes closer to the window and looks at Lily. It meows and rubs its head on the glass. It wants to say hello to Lily. Lily sees the cat. She stops crying and screaming. She smiles and says, "Hi, cat. You are so cute and fun. I want to play with you." The cat meows and nods. It wants to play with Lily too. Lily says, "Mom, look. The cat is here. It likes me. Can I go outside and see the cat now?" Her mom looks at the cat. She sees that it is gentle and sweet. She feels sorry for Lily. She says, "Okay, Lily. You can go outside and see the cat for a little while. But you have to promise me that you will come back and help me cook. And you have to say sorry for throwing your spoon and running away." Lily says, "I promise, mom. And I am sorry. I love you, mom. And I love cooking with you. But I also love the cat." Her mom says, "I love you too, Lily. And I understand. The cat is very flexible and fun. But cooking is also fun and important. You can do both, but you have to be respectful and responsible." Lily says, "Okay, mom. I will be respectful and responsible. Thank you, mom." Her mom says, "You are welcome, Lily. Now go and see the cat. But don't forget to come back soon." Lily says, "I won't forget, mom. Bye, mom." She opens the door and runs to the cat. The cat runs to her. They hug and play. They are happy and friends. The end.
