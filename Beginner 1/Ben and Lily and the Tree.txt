Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Tree

Ben and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to run, swing, slide and climb. They also liked to feed the birds. They brought some bread and seeds with them. One day, they saw a big tree with many branches and leaves. They saw some birds on the tree. They wanted to feed them. They walked to the tree and said hello. "Hello, birds! We have some food for you. Do you want some?" Ben asked. "Tweet, tweet! Yes, please! We are hungry!" the birds answered. Ben and Lily smiled. They broke the bread into small pieces and threw them to the birds. They also scattered some seeds on the ground. The birds flew down and ate the food. They thanked Ben and Lily. "Thank you, Ben and Lily! You are very friendly and kind!" the birds said. "You are welcome, birds! You are very friendly and pretty!" Ben and Lily said. They watched the birds for a while. They saw them sing, flap their wings and hop around. They felt happy. They liked the tree and the birds. They decided to come back again. They said goodbye and went to play some more.

John was a little boy of three. He liked to play at the beach. One day, he was playing in the sand when two of his friends started to tease him. They said he couldn't catch the big ball they were throwing around. John was not scared. He ran and shouted, "I can do it!" John was so fast that he caught the ball easily. His friends were amazed, but they still wanted to tease him. So, they took him to the shore. The waves were wet and they splashed John all over. John was so dizzy. He started to laugh and he couldn't stop. His friends were laughing too. After all the fun, John was very tired. He was happy that he had made his friends laugh and he hadn't let them tease him. He was proud of himself and ran home with a big smile on his face.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big, rough tree. Tim wanted to show the tree to his friend, Sam. But Sam was far away. Tim tried to pull the tree. It was too big. He tried to push the tree. It was too heavy. Tim was sad. He wanted Sam to see the rough tree. Then, a bird came and helped Tim. The bird flew to Sam and told him about the tree. Sam came running to see the rough tree. Tim and Sam were happy. They played near the tree all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a pretty skirt that she loved very much. One day, she wore her pretty skirt to play with her friend, Tom. While they were playing, a fierce dog came near them. The dog was big and loud. Amy and Tom were scared. The dog started to rub its head on Amy's pretty skirt. Amy didn't like that. She said to Tom, "I want the dog to stop!" Tom had an idea. He picked up a toy and threw it far away. The fierce dog saw the toy and ran after it. Amy and Tom were happy. They could play again, and Amy's pretty skirt was safe.

Anna and Lily liked to play dress up. They had many clothes and hats and shoes to try on. They liked to pretend they were fashion stars. One day, they invited their friend Mia to play with them. Mia came to their house with a big smile. She said, "Hello, Anna and Lily. Thank you for inviting me. I like to play dress up too." Anna and Lily welcomed Mia and showed her their clothes. They said, "You can choose anything you like. We have many things to wear." Mia looked at the clothes and picked a pink dress and a purple hat. She put them on and said, "How do I look?" Anna and Lily looked at her and frowned. They said, "You look silly. That dress and hat do not match. You do not know fashion. You should wear something else." Mia felt sad and hurt. She said, "But I like this dress and hat. They are pretty and fun. Why are you so rude to me? I thought you were my friends." Anna and Lily said, "We are not rude. We are honest. You need to learn fashion. You can not play with us if you wear that." Mia started to cry. She took off the dress and hat and ran away. She said, "You are not my friends. You are mean and nasty. I do not want to play with you ever again." Anna and Lily did not care. They said, "Good. Go away. We do not need you. We have each other and our fashion." The end.
