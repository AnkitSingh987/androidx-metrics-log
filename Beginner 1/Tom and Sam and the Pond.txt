Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Pond

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big hedge. The hedge has many holes. Tom and Sam want to see what is behind the hedge. Tom says, "Let's join hands and go through the hedge." Sam says, "OK, but be careful. The hedge is prickly." They join hands and walk to the hedge. They find a hole that is big enough for them. They go through the hedge. Behind the hedge, they see a pond. The pond has ducks and frogs. The ducks are fast. They swim and quack. The frogs are slow. They sit and croak. Tom and Sam are happy. They watch the ducks and frogs. They throw stones and sticks in the pond. They make splashes and ripples. But then they hear a voice. It is Mom. She calls, "Tom, Sam, where are you?" Tom and Sam are scared. They forgot to tell Mom where they went. They run back to the hedge. They go through the hole. They see Mom. She is angry. She says, "Why did you go behind the hedge? You could have got lost or hurt. You should have told me where you were going." Tom and Sam are sorry. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We wanted to see the pond. It was fun. But we won't do it again." Mom hugs them. She says, "I'm glad you are safe. But next time, ask me before you go anywhere. Now let's go home. It's time for lunch." Tom and Sam nod. They hold Mom's hand. They leave the park. They say goodbye to the hedge, the pond, the ducks and the frogs.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play in the park. One sunny day, she saw a big maze in the park. Mia was glad and wanted to play in it. Mia went inside the maze and found a little boy named Tim. Tim was lost and couldn't find his way out. Mia said, "Don't worry, I will help you." They walked and walked, trying to find the way out of the maze. Finally, they saw a man at the end of the maze. The man said, "To get out, you must pay me with a smile." Mia and Tim were glad to pay the man with their biggest smiles. The man showed them the way out of the maze. Mia and Tim were happy to be out and they played in the park all day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys all day. One day, it was very icy outside, and Tim wanted to go out and play. Tim put on his coat and went outside. He built a big snowman and had lots of fun. But then, he saw a big icy hill. Tim wanted to finish his snowman, but the hill looked like more fun. He climbed the hill and started to slide down. As Tim slid down the hill, he went very fast. He tried to stop, but he couldn't. There was silence all around as he went faster and faster. At the bottom of the hill, there was a big tree. Tim hit the tree very hard and fell down. He was hurt and very sad.

One day, a blue bird named Billy flew down to a big tree. He saw a little girl named Sally. Sally had a whip in her hand. She was trying to get her toy down from the tree. Billy asked Sally, "Why do you have a whip?" Sally said, "I want to get my toy. It is stuck in the tree." Billy wanted to help Sally, so he flew up and tried to get the toy with his beak. But the toy was too high for Billy. Billy had an idea. He told Sally, "I will find my big friend, the elephant. He can help us." Billy flew away and came back with the big elephant. The elephant used his long nose to get the toy down from the tree. Sally was very happy and thanked Billy and the elephant. From that day on, they all played together and became the best of friends.
