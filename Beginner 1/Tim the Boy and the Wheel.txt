Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Wheel

One day, a boy named Tim found a big wheel in his yard. He was very happy and played with it all day. He rolled the wheel up and down the street with his friends. They had so much fun. Then, Tim saw a girl named Sue looking sad. She said, "That is my wheel. I lost it yesterday." Tim felt guilty because he knew it was not his wheel. He said, "I am sorry, Sue. I did not know it was yours. Here, you can have it back." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim went home and decided to write a note to Sue. He wrote, "I am sorry for taking your wheel. I hope we can be friends." Sue read the note and they became good friends. They played with the wheel together and shared their toys. The moral of the story is to always be honest and kind to others.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a watch. The watch can tell time and make sounds. Lily and Ben think the watch is great. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is brown and fluffy. It runs and barks. Lily and Ben are scared. They hide behind a tree. "Who is that dog?" Lily asks. "I don't know," Ben says. "Maybe it is lost." They look at the watch. The watch says it is three o'clock. They hear a sound. It is the watch. The watch makes a beep. "What is that?" Lily asks. "It is the watch," Ben says. "It is telling us something." They look at the watch again. The watch has a picture. It is a picture of the dog. The watch says: "This is Max. He is a friendly dog. He likes to play. Do you want to play with him?" Lily and Ben are surprised. They recognize the dog. It is Max. Max is the dog of their teacher. They have seen him before. He is a nice dog. They come out from behind the tree. They call Max. Max hears them. He runs to them. He wags his tail. He licks their faces. "Hi, Max!" Lily and Ben say. "We are happy to see you. Do you want to play with us?" Max barks. He nods. He wants to play. Lily and Ben smile. They take the watch. They play with Max. They have fun. They think the watch is great.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to find a teaspoon. Lily looked and looked, but she could not find it. She saw a small spoon, but it was unknown to her if it was the right one. Lily asked her mom, "Is this the teaspoon?" Her mom smiled and said, "No, but good guess! The teaspoon is a little bigger." Lily looked again and found a bigger spoon. She showed it to her mom and asked, "Is this the teaspoon?" Her mom nodded and said, "Yes, you found it! Good job, Lily!" Lily felt happy that she could help her mom. She learned what a teaspoon looks like. From that day on, Lily always knew which spoon was the teaspoon when her mom needed it. They had lots of fun cooking together in the kitchen.

One day, a dog named Max saw some dry grass. Max was sad because the grass was not good to play on. Max wanted the grass to be green and soft. Max asked his friend, the sun, "Why is the grass dry? Can you help make it green again?" The sun said, "I can try, but I need water too. Let's ask the rain to help us." So Max and the sun asked the rain for help. The rain said, "I will help, but I have been suffering from a cold. I will try my best to make the grass green again." The rain tried to rain, but it was hard because of the cold. Then, something unexpected happened! The rain sneezed really big, and lots of water came down. The grass became green and soft again. Max was happy, and he played on the grass with his friends.
