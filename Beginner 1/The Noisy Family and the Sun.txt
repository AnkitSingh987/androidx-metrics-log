Category: Beginner
Title: The Noisy Family and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a noisy family. They loved to sing and dance all day. They had a little girl named Lily. Lily was very happy and loved to play with her friends. One day, the noisy family decided to go on a holiday. They wanted to join their friends at the beach. Lily was very excited. She could not wait to play in the sand and swim in the sea with her friends. On the day of the holiday, the noisy family and their friends had a big party on the beach. They sang and danced together. Lily played with her friends and had so much fun. At the end of the day, they all watched the sun go down and said goodbye to the beach. They could not wait for their next holiday together.

---

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Ben. Ben loved to play in the park. One day, he saw a tool on the ground. It was a small, red hammer. Ben picked it up with his mouth and took it home. At home, Ben showed the tool to his friend, Tom the cat. Tom thought it was very interesting. They decided to use the tool to build a little house for their toys. They worked together, and soon the house was done. Ben and Tom felt very happy. Every day, Ben and Tom played with the little house they built. They put their toys inside and pretended they were living there. The cute little dog and the cat felt so proud of their work. They knew they had made something special with the tool they found. And they lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a little fish named Finn was swimming in the big blue sea. Finn had a best friend named Sam, who was a small crab. They loved to play together. One of their favorite games was to dive deep into the water and find pretty shells. One sunny day, Finn and Sam were playing near the shore. Finn said, "Let's dive and find the most important shell!" Sam agreed, and they both dove deep into the water. They looked and looked, and finally found a big, shiny shell. "Wow!" said Sam, "This is the most important shell ever!" Finn and Sam were very tired after their big dive. They decided to take a nap on the soft, warm sand. As they lay down, they put the important shell between them. Finn and Sam closed their eyes and went to sleep, dreaming of their next big adventure together.

---

One day, a kind boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. They took a baseball with them. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to observe the birds in the tree. He looked up and saw many birds. Tim said, "Dad, look at the birds! They are so pretty!" His dad smiled and said, "Yes, they are. Let's play with the baseball now." Tim and his dad played catch with the baseball. They had a lot of fun. After they played, they sat under the tree. Tim and his dad shared a snack and talked about the birds they observed. They were happy to spend time together at the park.

Tom and Lily were playing with blocks. They liked to make big buildings with many colors. They had fun stacking and knocking down the blocks. But then, Max came. Max was a mean boy. He did not like to share or play nice. He wanted all the blocks for himself. He said, "These are my blocks. Go away!" Tom and Lily did not want to go away. They said, "No, these are our blocks. We were here first. You go away!" Max got angry. He grabbed some blocks and threw them at Tom and Lily. He hit Tom on the head and Lily on the arm. They cried and said, "Ow! That hurts! Stop it!" Max did not stop. He ran to their building and kicked it. He broke their building. He said, "Ha ha! Your building is ugly. My building is better." Tom and Lily were very sad. They looked at their broken building. They did not like Max. They wanted to make him go away. They had an idea. They whispered to each other. They smiled and nodded. They picked up some blocks and walked to Max. They said, "Hey, Max. We want to see your building. Can we come?" Max was surprised. He thought they were scared of him. He said, "Sure, you can come. But don't touch anything. My building is the best." Tom and Lily followed Max. They saw his building. It was big and tall, but it had only one color. It was boring. They did not like it. They waited for the right moment. When Max was not looking, they pinched him. They pinched him hard. They pinched him on his ears, his nose, his fingers, and his toes. They pinched him all over. Max screamed and said, "Ow! Ow! Ow! What are you doing? Stop it! Stop it!" Tom and Lily did not stop. They said, "No, we will not stop. You are a mean boy. You hurt us and broke our building. You deserve a pinch. A big pinch!" Max ran away. He ran to his mom and cried. He said, "Mom, Tom and Lily pinched me. They pinched me a lot. It hurts!" His mom looked at him. She saw the red marks on his skin. She said, "Why did they pinch you? What did you do?" Max did not want to tell the truth. He said, "I don't know. They just pinched me. They are bad." His mom did not believe him. She said, "I don't think so. I think you did something to make them angry. Did you take their blocks? Did you throw them? Did you break their building?" Max looked down. He said, "Maybe. A little." His mom said, "That was not nice. That was rude. You should say sorry to them. And you should share and play nice. Or else, you will get more pinches." Max did not like that. He said, "But I don't want to say sorry. And I don't want to share. And I don't want to play nice. And I don't want to get pinches." His mom said, "Too bad. You have to. Or you will have no friends. And you will have no fun. And you will have no blocks. And you will have only pinches." Max realized his mom was right. He did not want to have no friends, no fun, no blocks, and only pinches. He said, "Okay, okay. I will say sorry. And I will share. And I will play nice. And I will not get pinches." His mom said, "Good. Now go and make up with Tom and Lily. And be careful with your building. It might fall down. It is not very strong." Max did as his mom said. He went to Tom and Lily. He said, "I'm sorry. I was mean. Can we be friends?" Tom and Lily looked at him. They saw he was sorry. They said, "Okay. We can be friends. But you have to be nice. And you have to share. And you have to play nice. And you have to not pinch." Max said, "I will. I promise." They smiled and hugged. They decided to play together. They used all the blocks. They made a new building. It was bigger and better than before. It had many colors. It was beautiful. They had fun. They did not fight. They did not throw. They did not break. They did not pinch. They were happy.
