Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a far away land, there was a big, red ball. This ball could rise high in the sky. It loved to play with the birds and the clouds. But one day, the ball went too high and got stuck in a tree. The ball felt sad and crazy because it could not play anymore. Then, a small, blue bird came to help the red ball. The bird tried to push the ball out of the tree, but the ball was too big. The bird felt sad too. But the bird had an idea. The bird called all its friends to help the red ball. Many birds came to help the red ball. They all pushed together and the ball started to move. Suddenly, the ball fell out of the tree and started to rise again. The ball was so happy that it went higher and higher. But this time, the ball did not get stuck. The ball learned to be careful and not go too high. The birds and the ball played together in the sky and had lots of fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a tall kid named Tim. Tim loved to write. He would write all day with his big crayon. One day, Tim wanted to write on the wall, but his mom said, "No, Tim! You can't write on the wall." Tim was sad. He wanted to find a way to write and be happy. So, he thought of an idea. He asked his mom if he could write on a big paper instead. His mom said, "Yes, Tim! That's a great idea." Tim got a big paper and wrote with his crayon. He drew a sun, a tree, and a cat. His mom was happy, and Tim was happy too. They hung the big paper on the wall, and Tim could see his art every day. The tall kid learned that it's good to find new ways to do the things he loves.

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big hole in the ground. They want to know what is inside. "Let's try to look in the hole," Sara says. "OK, but be careful," Ben says. They go near the hole and bend down. They see something shiny and round. It is a coin. "Wow, look at this!" Sara says. "It is a nice coin. Maybe we can buy some candy with it." She reaches her hand into the hole to get the coin. But the hole is too deep and her hand gets stuck. "Help, help!" Sara cries. "I can't get my hand out!" Ben tries to pull her hand, but it does not work. He is scared. "Don't worry, Sara, I will get help," he says. He runs to find a grown-up. He sees a man with a hat and a shovel. He is a gardener. He works in the park. "Please, please, help us!" Ben says. "My friend is stuck in a hole. She found a coin and she can't get it out." The gardener follows Ben to the hole. He sees Sara crying and her hand in the hole. "Don't be afraid, little girl, I will help you," he says. He uses his shovel to make the hole bigger. He gently frees Sara's hand. He gives her the coin. "Here you go, you can keep the coin. But be careful next time, OK? Holes can be dangerous," he says. "Thank you, thank you, mister!" Sara says. She hugs Ben and the gardener. They are happy and relieved. They decide to go to the store and buy some candy with the coin. They share it with the gardener. He is nice. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little cat. The cat lived in a small house with a girl named Sue. Sue loved the cat very much. One day, Sue saw an orange on the table. She wanted to share it with the cat. Sue cut the orange into small pieces and put them in a bowl. The cat stared at the orange pieces, not sure what they were. Sue picked up a piece of the orange and gave it to the cat. The cat sniffed it and then took a small bite. The cat liked it! The shy cat and Sue ate the orange together and had a very fun day.
