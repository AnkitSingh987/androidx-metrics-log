Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom and the Box

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, Sara finds a big box in her room. She wants to open it. "Tom, look! I have a big box. Maybe there are toys inside. Can you help me open it?" Sara asks. Tom comes to Sara's room. He sees the big box. He is curious too. "OK, Sara. Let's open it. Maybe we can share the toys." Tom says. They pull and push the box. It is heavy and hard. They cannot open it. They see a word on the box. It says "spell". "What is spell?" Sara asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is a name. Maybe it is a game. Maybe it is a magic word." Tom says. They try to say the word. They say "spell, spell, spell". Nothing happens. They are sad. "Maybe this box is not for us. Maybe it is for someone else. Maybe it is a cheap box. Maybe there are no toys inside." Sara says. They leave the box alone. They go to play with other toys. They forget about the box. But the box is not cheap. The box is not for someone else. The box is for Sara and Tom. The box is a magic box. The word "spell" is a key. When they say it three times, the box will open. And inside the box, there are many wonderful toys. Toys that can talk and sing and fly and dance. Toys that can make Sara and Tom happy and surprised. Toys that can be their new friends. But Sara and Tom do not know that. They do not open the box. They do not see the toys. They do not have the magic. They miss the fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went on an adventurous trip with his family. They wanted to go to a camp near the woods. Tim was very excited because he loved to play outside. When they got to the camp, Tim saw a big tree. He said, "Look, Mom! A big tree!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. It's a great tree to stand under when it's hot." Tim ran to the tree and stood under it. He felt very happy. Later, Tim met a new friend named Sam at the camp. They played together and had lots of fun. Sam said, "Let's go on a walk!" Tim agreed and they went on a walk in the woods. They saw many animals and had a great day. The camp was a fun place for Tim and his family.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house near a big rock. Every day, Tim would go outside and play near the rock. One day, while Tim was playing, he saw a big dog named Max. Max said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play with me?" Tim was scared but said, "Okay, let's play." They played near the big rock. As they were playing, Max found a bottle. Max said, "Look, Tim! I found a bottle." Max didn't know what it was for, so he pressed it and it began to spray water. The water sprayed all over Tim and the big rock. Tim got very wet and cold. He was not happy. Tim said, "Max, you made me wet and cold! I don't want to play with you anymore." Tim went back to his small house, still wet and cold. He was very sad and had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play with her blocks. She had red blocks, blue blocks, and yellow blocks. Mia would build tall towers and small houses with her blocks. One day, Mia was playing with her blocks when her tower fell down. It made her cry. She felt sad because she worked hard to build her tower. Mia's mommy heard her cry and came into the room. Mommy sat down with Mia and gave her a big hug. She helped Mia build a new tower with her blocks. This time, they made it even bigger and better. Mia felt comfortable and happy again. Now, every time Mia plays with her blocks, she remembers how her mommy helped her and she smiles.
