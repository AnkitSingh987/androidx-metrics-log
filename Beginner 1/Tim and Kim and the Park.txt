Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Kim and the Park

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to play in the park. They like to roll down the hill. They laugh and have fun. One day, Mom takes them to the park. She says they can have ice-cream after they play. Tim and Kim are happy. They run to the hill and start to roll. But Tim rolls too fast. He bumps into a big rock. He hurts his head. He cries. Mom runs to him and hugs him. She says they have to go to the doctor. Kim is sad. She wants to roll more. She wants to have ice-cream. She says it is not fair. She says Tim is bad. She is angry. Mom says they have to go now. She says they can have ice-cream another day. She says Kim has to be nice to Tim. She says Tim is hurt. Kim does not listen. She kicks Tim. She says he is stupid. She says he ruined everything. She says she hates him. Mom is shocked. She scolds Kim. She says she is naughty. She says she has to say sorry to Tim. She says she has no ice-cream. Kim does not say sorry. She pouts. She says she does not care. She says she does not want ice-cream. She says she does not want Tim. Mom is sad. She takes Tim and Kim to the car. She drives to the doctor. She hopes Tim will be okay. She hopes Kim will be sorry. She hopes they will be friends again. She hopes they will be happy.

One day, a big octopus named Tom went for a swim. Tom was enormous and had many arms. He liked to play with his friends in the sea. The sun was shining, and the water was warm. As Tom was swimming, he saw a door. The door was close to the bottom of the sea. Tom was curious and wanted to see what was behind the door. He used one of his arms to open the door. When the door opened, Tom saw something unexpected. There was a room full of toys! The toys were for all the sea animals to play with. Tom was very happy and called all his friends to come and play. They all had a great time playing with the new toys.

One day, Mama and Baby were in the garden. Mama had a can in her hands. Baby asked what it was. Mama said it was a spray and it would help the flowers grow. Mama held down the switch on the spray and aimed it at the flowers. Baby watched as the spray made the flowers sparkle. Mama was very glad, and Baby was too. After the spraying was done, Baby asked if he could try. Mama said yes and handed the spray can to Baby. Baby held down the switch and sprayed the flowers. And Mama was glad to see it. Baby was so proud of himself that he gave Mama a big hug. Mama smiled and said that he had done a very good job. Then, they went inside and had a nice snack.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw an enormous bug in his room. Tim was scared of the big bug. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a big bug in my room!" Mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." They went to the room and saw the bug. Tim's mom managed to catch the bug in a cup. "See, it's not so scary now," she said. But then, the bug got out of the cup! Tim and his mom ran to the bathroom. They turned on the shower and the bug went down the drain. Tim was happy, and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They were safe now, and the big bug was gone.

One day, a little girl named Sally went outside to play. She saw a wide puddle from the rain shower last night. Sally jumped in the puddle and splashed water everywhere. She was having a lot of fun. Then, Sally saw her friend Tom. He was standing near the puddle, but he did not want to jump in. "Why don't you jump in the puddle, Tom?" Sally asked. Tom looked at the puddle and said, "I don't want to get wet." Sally realized that not everyone likes to get wet. She had an idea. "Let's play with my big ball instead!" Sally said. Tom smiled and they played with the ball all day. They had lots of fun together, even without jumping in the puddle.
