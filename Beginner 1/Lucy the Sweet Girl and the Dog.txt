Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Sweet Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a sweet girl named Lucy. She loved to wear her favorite skirt that was pink and had flowers on it. Lucy was always kind to her friends and liked to share her toys. One day, Lucy went to the park to play with her friends. They wanted to go on the slide, but a mean boy named Tom wouldn't let them. He said, "I won't let you play on the slide because I don't like your skirt." Lucy and her friends were sad and didn't know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A nice dog came to the park and saw that Lucy was sad. The dog went to Tom and took his ball. The dog then gave the ball to Lucy and her friends. Tom saw how much fun they were having and wanted to play too. Lucy decided to let Tom play with them, even though he was mean before. Tom learned that being nice and sharing is better than being mean. From that day on, Tom was a good friend to Lucy and her friends, and they all played happily together.

One sunny day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They bought some cream and a toy for Tim. Tim was very happy. When they got home, Tim's mom wanted to record Tim playing with his new toy. She said, "Tim, let's record you playing with your toy, so we can show it to dad later!" But Tim was stubborn and didn't want to share his toy. He said, "No, it's mine!" Tim's mom was sad, but she didn't force him. Later, Tim's dad came home and saw the cream. He asked, "Did you record Tim playing with his new toy?" Tim's mom said, "No, Tim didn't want to share his toy." Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I will share my toy now." So, they recorded Tim playing with his toy, and they all watched it together. Tim learned that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a friendly little town, there was a cat named Tom. Tom was a happy cat who loved to play and make friends. One day, Tom met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a bird who lived in a tree near Tom's house. Tom and Sam liked to play together under the big, blue sky. One day, Tom said to Sam, "I love life! We have so much fun." Sam nodded and said, "Yes, life is good when we play together." They both sighed happily, knowing they had each other as friends. As the days went by, Tom and Sam played more and more. They would chase each other around the tree and laugh. The friendly little town was even happier because of Tom and Sam's friendship. And they lived happily ever after, enjoying their simple and fun life together.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big pile of junk near the road. It has old boxes, cans, bottles and wires. "Let's go and see the junk!" Lily says. "Maybe we can find something fun." "No, Lily, that is dangerous!" Ben says. "The junk can hurt us. It can cut us or poke us. And the road has fast cars. They can hit us." But Lily does not listen. She runs to the junk. She sees a big box. She thinks it is a house. She jumps on the box. The box breaks. Lily falls down. She cries. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily says. "My leg hurts!" Ben runs to Lily. He sees blood on her leg. He is scared. He calls for help. "Help, help, help!" Ben says. "Lily is hurt! She jumped on the junk!" A man hears Ben. He comes to see. He sees Lily and Ben. He sees the junk. He sees the road. He is angry. "What are you doing here?" the man says. "This is not a place to play. This is dangerous. You could have been killed. Come with me. I will take you to your parents. And I will call the doctor for Lily." Lily and Ben are sorry. They learned a lesson. They will not play with junk again. They will listen to Ben. He is a good friend. He cares for Lily.
