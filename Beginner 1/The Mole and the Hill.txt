Category: Beginner
Title: The Mole and the Hill

One day, a little mole went out to play. He wanted to observe the world around him. As he walked, he saw a big hill. He wanted to climb the hill and see what was on the other side. As the mole started to climb, he slipped and fell. He felt embarrassed because a bird saw him fall. The bird laughed at the mole. The mole was sad and wanted to hide, but he still wanted to see what was on the other side of the hill. The mole tried again and again to climb the hill. Each time he fell, he felt more embarrassed. But he did not give up. Finally, he made it to the top of the hill. He was so happy! The mole saw a beautiful field with many flowers on the other side. The bird that laughed at him came to say sorry. They became friends and played together in the field.

One day, a big dog and a small cat went for a walk. The big dog saw a tall tree and wanted to stand by it. The small cat was jealous because she could not stand tall like the big dog. The big dog tried to help the small cat stand tall too. As they stood by the tree, they saw a lot of cars and buses on the road. This was traffic. The big dog and small cat did not like the noise from the traffic. They wanted to find a quiet place to play. The big dog and small cat walked away from the traffic and found a nice park. There were no cars or buses here. They played in the park all day and had a lot of fun. The small cat was not jealous anymore because they could both have fun together.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with books. They have many books in their room. Some books have pictures, some books have stories, some books have words. One day, they find a big book on the shelf. It is a dictionary. It has many words, but no pictures or stories. Tom and Mia are curious. They want to know what the words mean. They open the dictionary and look at the pages. They see words like "apple", "ball", "cat", and "dog". They know these words. They point at them and say them out loud. Then they see a word they do not know. It is "slap". They do not know what "slap" means. They look at each other and wonder. "Maybe it is a sound," Tom says. "Like clap or snap." "Maybe it is a thing," Mia says. "Like cap or map." They decide to try it out. Tom puts his hand on the book and says "slap". He makes a loud noise. Mia laughs. She thinks it is funny. Then Mia puts her hand on Tom's face and says "slap". She makes a loud noise too. But Tom does not laugh. He feels pain. He cries. Mia is scared. She did not mean to hurt Tom. She says "sorry" and hugs him. She gives him a kiss. Tom stops crying. He says "it's okay". He hugs her back. He gives her a smile. They are friends again. They are glad. They close the dictionary and put it back on the shelf. They do not want to play with it anymore. They pick another book with pictures and stories. They have fun.

Tom and Anna are brother and sister. They like to play with their toys and books. One morning, Tom wakes up early. He sees the sun shining through the window. He feels cheerful and wants to share his joy with Anna. He runs to her room and knocks on the door. "Anna, Anna, wake up!" he calls. "It is a good day to play!" Anna hears Tom's voice and opens her eyes. She is still sleepy and yawns. "What is it, Tom?" she asks. "Look, Anna, the sun is bright and the sky is blue. Let's go outside and have fun!" Tom says. Anna smiles and gets out of bed. She likes to play with Tom. They put on their clothes and shoes and go downstairs. They see their mom in the kitchen. She is making breakfast. "Good morning, mom!" Tom and Anna say. "Good morning, my sweeties!" mom says. "You are up early today. Are you hungry?" "Yes, mom, we are hungry. Can we have some toast and jam?" Tom and Anna say. "Sure, you can. Here you go. And look, I have something for you." mom says. She gives them each a print of a colorful picture. It is a picture of a happy family and a cute dog. "Wow, mom, this is beautiful! Thank you!" Tom and Anna say. They hug mom and look at their prints. "This is us, Tom. And this is our dog, Spot. See, he has a spot on his eye." Anna says. "I see, Anna. And look, we are all smiling and holding hands. We are a cheerful family." Tom says. "Yes, we are, Tom. And we love each other very much." Anna says. They finish their breakfast and thank mom. Then they take their prints and go outside. They find a nice spot under a tree and stick their prints on the trunk with some tape. They admire their prints and feel happy. "Look, Tom, our prints are like a window. They show us how we feel inside." Anna says. "You are right, Anna. And we feel cheerful and grateful. We have a good day to play and a good family to love." Tom says. They hold hands and run to the playground. They play with their friends and have a lot of fun. They are cheerful children with cheerful prints.
