Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Hill

Lily liked to climb. She climbed on chairs, on tables, on trees, on rocks. She wanted to climb higher and higher, to see more and more. One day, she saw a big hill near her house. She wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. She asked her mom if she could go. Her mom said yes, but she had to be careful and come back soon. Lily put on her shoes and her hat and ran to the hill. She started to climb, step by step, with her hands and feet. She felt happy and strong. But when she reached the top, she saw something that made her sad. She saw a big castle, with a big wall and a big gate. She saw a woman in a long dress and a shiny crown. She was the queen. But she was not smiling. She was crying. She was alone in the castle, with no friends and no fun. Lily felt sorry for the queen. She wanted to help her. She walked to the gate and knocked. She said, "Hello, queen. Why are you sad? Do you want to play with me?" The queen heard the knock and the voice. She looked out of the window and saw Lily. She was surprised and curious. She opened the gate and came out. She said, "Hello, little girl. Who are you? Why are you here?" Lily said, "I am Lily. I live near the hill. I like to climb. I climbed the hill and saw your castle. I wanted to see you. You are sad. Why are you sad?" The queen said, "I am sad because I have no friends. I have no one to talk to, to laugh with, to play with. I am lonely in my castle." Lily said, "That is sad. But you can have friends. I can be your friend. I can talk to you, laugh with you, play with you. Do you want to be my friend?" The queen smiled. She felt a warm feeling in her heart. She said, "Yes, I want to be your friend. Thank you, Lily. You are very kind and brave. You made me happy." Lily smiled too. She said, "You are welcome, queen. You are very pretty and nice. You made me happy too." They hugged each other. They became friends. They played together, in the castle and on the hill. They climbed and ran and jumped and sang. They were happy.

One day, a cop named Jim went to the park. Jim liked to help people. He saw a big box and a small box. He wanted to see if the big box could fit in the small box. Jim tried to push the big box into the small box, but it did not fit. He tried again and again, but it still did not fit. Jim felt sad because he could not make it fit. Then, Jim saw a little girl eating a sour apple. The little girl saw Jim and wanted to help. She said, "Jim, let's try to fit the small box into the big box." They tried together and it fit! Jim and the little girl were very happy.

Once upon a time, on a hot Tuesday, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play outside in the sun. She had a big red ball that she loved to bounce and kick. One day, Sue found a big ice cube on the ground. She picked it up and felt how cold it was. The sun was very hot, so the ice cube started to melt in her hand. Sue watched as the ice cube turned into water. Sue had an idea. She ran inside and got a cup. She put the ice cube in the cup and watched it melt some more. Then, she drank the cold water. It was so refreshing on the hot Tuesday. Sue smiled and went back outside to play with her red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a fat man. He lived in a small house. The man liked to eat a lot. He ate all day and did not play outside. His house was very dirty. One day, the fat man saw a little boy playing outside. The boy was happy and clean. The fat man wanted to be like the little boy. He decided to clean his house. The fat man started to wipe his house. He wiped the floor, the table, and the windows. It was hard work, but the fat man did not give up. Soon, his house was clean and shiny. The fat man felt happy and proud. Now, the fat man plays outside with the little boy. They both have fun and stay clean. The fat man learned that being clean and playing outside makes him happy. The moral of the story is that it is important to be clean and have fun.
