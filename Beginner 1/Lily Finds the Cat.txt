Category: Beginner
Title: Lily Finds the Cat

Lily was very tired. She had a long day at school. She learned many new things, like how to draw a cat and how to count to ten. She also played with her friends, but they had a fight over a toy. Lily was sad and angry. She came home and saw her mom. Her mom smiled and hugged her. "How was your day, Lily?" she asked. "Not good," Lily said. "I want to go to bed." "Okay, sweetie," her mom said. "But first, you need to eat your dinner and take a bath. Then you can relax in your clean sheet." Lily nodded. She ate her dinner, but she did not like it. It was green and mushy. She took a bath, but she did not like it. It was too hot and bubbly. She wanted to go to bed. She put on her pajamas and ran to her room. She saw her bed. It looked so cozy and soft. She jumped on it and pulled the sheet over her head. She closed her eyes and tried to relax. But she could not relax. She heard a noise. It was loud and scary. It sounded like a monster. It was coming from under her bed. Lily was afraid. She screamed. Her mom came running. She turned on the light and lifted the sheet. She saw Lily crying. She also saw something else. It was a toy. It was the toy that Lily and her friends had fought over. Lily had taken it home without asking. "Lily, what is this?" her mom asked. "Did you take this toy from your friend?" Lily nodded. She felt ashamed. She knew she did something bad. "That was not nice, Lily," her mom said. "You need to say sorry to your friend and give it back. How would you feel if someone took your toy?" Lily felt sorry. She knew how she would feel. She hugged her mom and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I'm sorry, friend. I will give it back tomorrow." Her mom hugged her back and said, "I'm glad you're sorry, Lily. That's the first step to make things right. Now, let's put this toy away and try to relax. I will read you a story and sing you a song. Then you can sleep in your clean sheet." Lily smiled. She liked stories and songs. She snuggled with her mom and listened to her voice. She felt calm and happy. She relaxed in her clean sheet and fell asleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to look at the pretty sunset every day. The colors in the sky made her smile. One day, she saw a sad bird sitting on her window. The bird looked very sorry. Lily asked the bird, "Why are you sorry?" The bird said, "I had a dream that I could not fly. I am sorry because I am scared." Lily wanted to help the bird feel better. She told the bird, "Don't be sorry. You can still fly and see the beautiful sunset!" The bird listened to Lily and started to feel happy. It flapped its wings and flew up high in the sky. The bird saw the pretty sunset and was not scared anymore. Lily waved goodbye to the bird and felt happy too. From that day on, they both enjoyed the sunset and were never sorry again.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to draw with her pastel colors. One day, she drew a big, green frog. She wanted to show her friend Sam. Lily went to the mailbox to mail her frog picture to Sam. On the way, she saw her friend Tom. Tom was eating a sandwich. "Hi, Tom!" said Lily. "I am mailing a picture to Sam." Tom looked at his sandwich and said, "This sandwich is disgusting! I don't like it. Can I see your picture?" Lily showed Tom her frog picture. Tom smiled and said, "Wow! That is a great frog!" Lily mailed her picture to Sam and went home. She was very happy to share her frog with her friends. The end.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play with toys and watch TV. One day, they saw a big truck on TV. The truck was slow and loud. It had many things on it. "Wow, look at the truck!" Lily said. "It has a bike and a ball and a doll and a teddy bear!" "I want the bike!" Tom said. "It is red and shiny. I can ride it fast." "Let's wave to the truck!" Lily said. "Maybe it will stop and give us the things." They ran to the window and waved to the truck. The truck did not see them. It kept going slow and loud. "Hey, truck, stop!" Tom shouted. "We want the bike and the ball and the doll and the teddy bear!" He ran out of the door and chased the truck. Lily followed him. They did not look at the road. They did not see the car. The car was fast and quiet. It hit Tom and Lily. They fell down and did not get up. The truck did not stop. The car did not stop. The TV did not stop. The end.
