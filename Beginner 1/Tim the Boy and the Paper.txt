Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Paper

One day, a little boy named Tim found a roll of tape. He was confused about what to do with it. He asked his mom, "What can I do with this tape?" His mom replied, "You can use it to stick things together, like paper or toys." Tim thought that was a great idea. Tim went to his room and found some paper. He used the tape to stick the papers together and made a big paper chain. He was very happy with his work and showed it to his mom. She was proud of him and they hung the paper chain in his room.

One day, a boy named Tim and his friend, Sue, went to the park to play. They liked to throw a ball back and forth. They were very good at it. Tim threw the ball high, and Sue caught it. Sue threw the ball low, and Tim caught it. They were having lots of fun. A bad dog named Max saw them playing. Max wanted to play too, but he didn't know how to ask. So, Max ran up to Tim and Sue and said, "I want to play! Can I throw the ball too?" Tim and Sue were scared, but they said, "Okay, you can play with us." Max threw the ball very hard, and it went far away. Tim and Sue ran to get the ball, but they could not find it. They saw a line on the ground and followed it. The line led them to a big tree. They looked up and saw the ball stuck in the tree! Max said, "I am sorry, I did not mean to throw the ball so hard." Tim and Sue said, "It's okay, Max. We can all help get the ball down." They all worked together and got the ball down. From that day on, Tim, Sue, and Max were the best of friends and played together all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a good little boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk and run with his dog, Max. They had lots of fun together. One day, they went to the park to play. They saw a sign with a big number on it. The sign said "Speed: 5". Tim and Max did not know what the sign meant. They thought it was a game. So, they started to run very fast. They ran faster and faster. They thought they were winning the game. But then, something unexpected happened. A kind man stopped them and told them the sign meant they should not run too fast. Tim and Max learned that they should always follow the signs to be safe and not get hurt. They promised to be more careful and learned that following rules is good.

One sunny day, Tom and Tim were playing outside. Tom had a cool red ball. Tim had a big basket. They wanted to play a game together. Tom said, "Let's pass the ball and put it in the basket!" Tim agreed, and they started to play. They took turns and shared the ball. They laughed and had lots of fun. As they played, a little girl named Sue came by. She felt sad because she had no friends to play with. Tom and Tim asked her to join their game. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you for letting me play!" They all played together, passing the ball and putting it in the basket. They learned that sharing and being kind to others made them happy. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and have fun with his friends. One day, Tim's mom told him they were going to his friend's house. Tim was very excited and ready to play. When they got to the friend's house, Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue was playing with a big piece of paper. "Hi, Tim! Want to play with me?" Sue asked. "Yes, I do!" Tim replied. Sue showed Tim how to fold the paper to make a fun toy. They both laughed and had a great time folding the paper. After they finished playing, Tim's mom said it was time to go home. Tim hugged Sue and said, "Thank you for teaching me how to fold the paper." Sue smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Come play again soon!" Tim went home with his mom, happy and ready for more fun the next day.
