Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Plant

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a small plant in his garden. He loved his plant very much. Every day, he would water the plant and talk to it. One day, Tim saw a big, stupid bug near his plant. The bug wanted to eat the plant. Tim said, "No, bug! You cannot eat my plant!" The bug did not listen to Tim. It still wanted to eat the plant. Then, Tim had an idea. He bowed to the bug and said, "Please, Mr. Bug, do not eat my plant. It is my friend." The bug looked at Tim and then at the plant. The bug decided not to eat the plant. It bowed back to Tim and went away. Tim was very happy, and his plant was safe.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty basket that she loved very much. One day, Lily went to the big tree to pick some apples. Lily saw a boy named Tom at the tree. Tom was selfish and did not want to share the apples. Lily asked Tom to yield, but he said no. Lily was sad but did not give up. Then, Lily had an idea. She gave Tom her basket and asked if they could share the apples. Tom liked the basket and agreed to share. They filled the basket with apples together and became friends.

Lily and Max are best friends. They like to play in the park with their toys. They have a lot of fun together. Sometimes they run, sometimes they swing, sometimes they slide. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is wild. It barks and jumps and chases the birds. Lily and Max are scared. They hide behind a bench and hold each other. "Go away, dog!" Max shouts. "You are bad and mean!" But the dog does not go away. It comes closer and closer. Lily and Max are very scared. They hug each other and close their eyes. Then they hear a voice. It is Lily's mom. She has come to pick them up. She sees the dog and the kids. She runs to them and shoos the dog away. She is brave and strong. "Lily, Max, are you okay?" she asks. "I'm sorry, that dog was wild. It should not be here." Lily and Max open their eyes. They are happy to see Lily's mom. They nod and smile. "Thank you, mom!" Lily says. "You saved us!" "Yes, thank you!" Max says. "You are the best!" Lily's mom hugs them and kisses them. She is proud and glad. "Come on, kids, it's time to go home," she says. "You can play again tomorrow." Lily and Max pick up their toys and follow Lily's mom. They are still best friends. They hold hands and kiss each other goodbye. They are not scared anymore. They are brave and strong, too.

One day, a boy named Tim found a ball. The ball had a handle. He held the handle tight. He liked to swing the ball around. He had fun with it. Tim saw his friend, Sam. He wanted to show Sam his new ball. He said, "Look, Sam! I have a ball with a handle!" Sam looked at the ball and wanted to play too. But Sam's mom said, "Be careful, boys. Don't swing the ball too fast. It can hurt someone." Tim and Sam didn't listen to Sam's mom. They swung the ball fast. They didn't want to prevent a bad thing. They just wanted to have fun. They swung the ball very fast. Then, the ball hit a big glass window. The window broke. Glass was everywhere. Tim and Sam were sad. They knew they should have listened to Sam's mom. Now, they had to clean up the mess. The fun day was over.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a farm with his mom and dad. He was very excited. The farm had many animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. Tim loved to look at them all. His dad said, "Let's fill a bucket with food for the animals!" Tim was happy to help. They filled the bucket with food and went to feed the animals. Tim wondered what would happen when they gave the food to the animals. They went to the cows first. Tim held the bucket and the cows ate the food. Next, they went to the pigs. The pigs ate the food too. Finally, they went to the chickens. The chickens ate the food and then something fun happened! The chickens started to lay eggs! Tim was so happy to see this. His mom said, "We can take some eggs home!" Tim knew that feeding the animals would make something good happen. He felt proud and couldn't wait to go back to the farm again.
