Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Box and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a big house with a tall chimney. Inside the house, there was a small family. The family had a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lily. They all liked to sit by the warm fire under the chimney. One day, Lily heard a snap sound coming from the chimney. "Mom, Dad, what was that?" she asked. "I don't know," said Mom, "let's go look." They all went near the chimney and saw a tight rope tied around a big box. Dad pulled the rope and the box came down from the chimney. "Snap!" went the rope as it broke. Inside the box, there were toys and games for Lily to play with. "Wow! Thank you, Mom and Dad!" said Lily, and they all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Sam. Sam was a confused dog. He did not know how to play with his toys. One day, he found a toy heart. The heart was red and soft. Sam wanted to play with it. Sam tried to snap the heart with his teeth. But the heart did not break. He tried again and again, but the heart stayed whole. Sam was sad. He did not understand why the heart did not break. Then, Sam's friend, a wise cat named Lily, came to help. Lily told Sam that the heart was strong because it was full of love. Sam learned that being kind and loving makes things strong. From that day on, Sam played with the heart gently and shared it with his friends. Sam was happy, and he was no longer confused.

Once upon a time, there was a little mole. He lived in a hole in the ground. The mole had many friends in the forest. One day, the mole saw a big tree. It was very impressive. The mole wanted to climb the tree to see his friends. So, he started to climb up the tree. When the mole reached the top, he saw his friends. They were all playing and having fun. The mole was very happy. He waved to his friends and they waved back. Then, the mole climbed down the tree and played with his friends. They all had a great time together.

Tom and Sam like to play sport. They like to run and kick and throw. They want to be strong and fast. They see a big ball in the park. It is red and shiny. They want to get the ball. "Look, Sam, a big ball!" Tom says. "Let's get it and play with it!" "OK, Tom, let's go!" Sam says. They run to the ball. They do not see the man who owns the ball. He is sitting on a bench. He is reading a book. Tom and Sam get to the ball. They pick it up. They are happy. They start to play with the ball. They kick it and throw it and catch it. They laugh and shout. "Hey, stop that!" the man says. He sees Tom and Sam with his ball. He is angry. He gets up from the bench. He walks to Tom and Sam. He looks big and scary. "Give me back my ball!" the man says. "You are bad boys! You took my ball without asking!" Tom and Sam are scared. They drop the ball. They try to run away. But the man is faster. He grabs Tom and Sam by their arms. He drags them to the bench. He sits them down. He calls their parents on his phone. Tom and Sam cry. They are sorry. They did not mean to be bad. They just wanted to play sport. They just wanted to be strong. But now they are in trouble. They wish they did not get the ball. They wish they played with their own toys. They have a bad ending.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her parent. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Amy loved to play on the swings and slide. She saw a big, impressive tree with many branches. She wanted to climb it. "Can I climb the tree, please?" Amy asked her parent. Her parent looked at the tree and thought for a moment. "Yes, but I will watch you and help you if you need it," her parent said. Amy started to climb the tree. She felt happy and excited. Her parent watched her closely. After a while, it was time to go home. "Let's end our fun day at the park," her parent said with a smile. Amy climbed down from the tree and they went home together.
