Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Fish and the Fast

One day, a little fish named Sam was sad. He could not swim fast like his friends. They would all go and play, but Sam was always left behind. He would watch them and wish he could swim fast too. One day, Sam saw a big print on the sand. It looked like a big foot. He was very curious, so he followed the print. Then, he found a big crab named Carl. Carl said, "Hello, little fish! Why are you sad?" Sam told Carl that he could not swim fast like his friends. Carl had an idea. "I can help you swim fast," he said. Carl told Sam to hold on to his big claw. Then, they swam very fast together. Sam was so happy! He could finally swim fast like his friends. From that day on, Sam and Carl were best friends. They played and swam together every day. And Sam was not sad anymore.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with a ball in the park. One day, they see a big tree with a rope. The rope has a knot at the end. They want to swing on the rope. Tom goes first. He holds the knot and runs. He swings high in the air. He feels happy. He lets go of the knot and lands on the grass. He laughs. Sam goes next. He holds the knot and runs. He swings high in the air. He feels happy. But he does not let go of the knot. He is scared. He holds on tight. He swings back and forth. He cries. Tom hears Sam cry. He wants to help him. He is a reliable friend. He runs to the tree. He climbs up the trunk. He reaches the branch. He grabs the rope. He pulls Sam closer. He tells him to let go of the knot. He says he will catch him. Sam trusts Tom. He lets go of the knot. He falls. Tom catches him. He hugs him. He says he is brave. He says he is his friend. He says he saved him. They go back to the grass. They play with the ball. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was a very curious boy. He loved to play outside and see new sights. One day, Tom went to the park with his mom. The sun was shining, and it was a beautiful day. At the park, Tom saw a cute dog. The dog had a bad leg and could not run fast. Tom wanted to help the dog, so he asked his mom if they could get a toy for the dog. His mom said yes, and they went to the store to buy a toy. Tom picked a soft, colorful ball for the dog. When they went back to the park, Tom threw the ball gently to the dog. The dog was happy and played with the ball. Tom felt good because he helped the dog have fun. From that day on, Tom and the dog were the best of friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The dog was wet from playing in the rain. The dog had a name. His name was Buddy. One day, Buddy met a big dog. The big dog was dry. Buddy said, "Hi, what is your name?" The big dog said, "My name is Max. I do not like to be wet. I do not play in the rain." Buddy said, "I enjoy being wet. It is fun to play in the rain." Max said, "Maybe I will try it too." So, Buddy and Max played in the rain together. They both got very wet and had a lot of fun.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they go to the shop with their mums. The shop has many toys. Lily sees a doll with a pink dress. She likes the doll very much. She asks her mum if she can have it. "Sorry, Lily, we don't have enough money for the doll today. Maybe another time," her mum says. Lily feels sad. She looks at Ben. Ben has a car with big wheels. He likes the car very much. He makes the car go vroom vroom on the floor. "Ben, can I borrow your car for a little bit?" Lily asks. "No, Lily, this is my car. You can't have it. Go find your own toy," Ben says. Lily feels angry. She thinks Ben is mean. She tries to grab the car from Ben. Ben pulls the car back. They start to fight over the car. "Stop it, both of you! You can't fight in the shop. That's not nice," their mums say. They take the car and the doll away from them. They make them say sorry to each other. They tell them to find another toy to play with. Lily and Ben look around the shop. They see many toys, but they all look dull. They don't want to play with them. They miss the doll and the car. They wish they could have them. They look at each other. They feel sorry for fighting. They decide to be friends again. "Lily, do you want to share my car with me?" Ben asks. "Ben, do you want to share my doll with me?" Lily asks. They nod and smile. They give each other a hug. They take turns playing with the car and the doll. They have fun together. They are happy.
