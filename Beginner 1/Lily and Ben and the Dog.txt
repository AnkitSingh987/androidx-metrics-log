Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Dog

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a kite. They have fun. But one day, it is very hot. The sun is bright and makes them sweat. They look for some shade. They see a big tree. They run to the tree. But there is a problem. There is a dog under the tree. The dog is big and black. It has sharp teeth and loud bark. It does not like Lily and Ben. It stares at them. It growls at them. It wants the shade for itself. Lily and Ben are scared. They do not want to play with the dog. They want to play with their ball and kite. They want to be in the shade. They do not know what to do. Then they see a man. The man is nice and kind. He has a hat and a leash. He is the dog's owner. He calls the dog. He says sorry to Lily and Ben. He takes the dog away. Lily and Ben are happy. They thank the man. They go to the tree. They play with their ball and kite. They enjoy the shade. They think the shade is lovely. They are friends. They have fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the jungle with his mom and dad. He thought the jungle was boring. He wanted to play with his toys at home. His mom and dad wanted to show him the fun things in the jungle. In the jungle, they saw a big tree. Tim's dad said, "Let's name the tree Big Tree." Tim did not like the name. He wanted to name it Fun Tree. Tim and his dad did not agree. They were both sad. Tim's mom had an idea. She said, "Why not name it Big Fun Tree?" Tim and his dad liked the name. They were happy. They played near the Big Fun Tree all day. The jungle was not boring anymore.

One day, a zebra was walking in the forest. He saw an angry bird stuck in a tree. The bird was very sad and scared. The zebra wanted to help the bird. The zebra said, "Don't worry, bird. I will save you!" The zebra tried to reach the bird with his long neck, but he couldn't. Then, the zebra had a big idea. He saw a big rock near the tree. The zebra pushed the rock under the tree. He climbed on the rock and tried again. This time, he could reach the bird! The zebra saved the angry bird. The bird was not angry anymore. The bird said, "Thank you, zebra! You are my hero!" The zebra and the bird became best friends. They played and had fun in the forest every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy cat. The cat was ugly, but Lily loved it very much. They played together every day. One day, Lily and her cat went on a bus. Lily was very excited. She found a seat for her and her cat. "Sit here, cat," said Lily. The cat sat on the seat next to her. The bus went fast, and Lily and her ugly cat had fun. They looked out of the window and saw many things. They saw a big tree, a red house, and a blue car. When they got home, they were tired but happy. They had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play with her friends in the park. One sunny day, Mia and her friends were playing when they saw a big ice cream truck. They all wanted some yummy ice cream. Mia asked her friend Tom, "What time is it?" Tom just shrugged his shoulders and said, "I don't know, but it's time for ice cream!" They all laughed and ran to the ice cream truck. The ice cream man gave them each a yummy ice cream cone. They ate their ice cream and played in the park until it was time to go home. It was a fun and yummy day for Mia and her friends.
