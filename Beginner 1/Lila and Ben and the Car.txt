Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Car

Lila and Ben were friends. They liked to play outside in the sun. But one day, the weather was bad. It was cold and wet and gray. Lila and Ben could not go out. "I'm bored," Lila said. "Let's play with your toys." "OK," Ben said. He showed Lila his new toy car. It was red and shiny and fast. "Look, it can make sounds and lights," he said. He pressed a button and the car beeped and flashed. "Wow, that's cool," Lila said. She wanted to play with the car too. "Can I try it?" "Sure," Ben said. He gave the car to Lila. She smiled and thanked him. She pressed the button and the car beeped and flashed. She moved the car on the floor and made it go zoom. But then, something bad happened. The car stopped working. It did not beep or flash or zoom. It was silent and dark and still. Lila tried to press the button again, but nothing happened. She looked at the car and saw a crack on the side. She had broken the car. "Oh no," Lila said. She felt scared and sorry. She gave the car back to Ben. "I'm sorry, Ben. I broke your car. It was an accident." Ben looked at the car and saw the crack. He felt angry and sad. He loved his car. It was his favorite toy. He did not want to share it with Lila. He thought she was careless and mean. He did not believe it was an accident. He snatched the car from Lila and shouted at her. "You're a bad friend, Lila. You broke my car on purpose. You're jealous of my car. You're a liar and a thief. Go away. I don't want to play with you anymore." Lila was shocked and hurt. She did not break the car on purpose. She was not jealous of the car. She was not a liar or a thief. She was a good friend. She wanted to play with Ben. She tried to explain and apologize, but Ben did not listen. He turned his back on her and hugged his car. Lila felt tears in her eyes. She wept. She ran out of Ben's house and went back to her own. She did not want to see Ben again. She did not want to play with his fake car. She did not want to be his friend. She was alone and sad. The weather was bad. The end.

One day, a big elephant with long legs was walking in the forest. He saw a small bug with a long tail. The bug was sad. The elephant asked, "Why are you sad, little bug?" The bug said, "I have a big rock on my home. I can't remove it. I need help." The elephant wanted to help the bug. He used his long legs to remove the big rock. The bug was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! Under the rock, there was a big hole. The bug said, "Oh no! My home is gone!" The elephant felt bad. But he had an idea. "I can help you make a new home," the elephant said. They worked together to make a new home for the bug. The bug was happy again, and they became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to do some exercise. He saw a tidy spot under a big tree. Tim was happy and started to run and jump. But then, a big dog came. The dog wanted to play too. The dog took Tim's ball and ran away. Tim was sad and began to frown. He did not know what to do. Just then, a little girl came to help. She had a whistle. She blew the whistle and the dog came back. The dog gave Tim his ball. Tim was happy again. They all played together and had fun.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to bake a cake. She had a big, rich cake in her mind. She went to the store to buy all the things she needed. She got eggs, milk, and sugar. She was very happy. When she got home, she started to bake the cake. But, her little brother Tom saw her and wanted to help. Sue did not want Tom to help her. She thought he would make a big mess. Tom got very sad and started to cry. Sue thought about it and decided to let Tom help her. Together, they baked the cake and it was very good. Sue let Tom put the wrap on the cake so they could save it for later. They were both happy and shared the cake with their family.
