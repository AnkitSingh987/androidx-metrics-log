Category: Beginner
Title: The Monster and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a big monster. The monster was not hurt, but he was very hungry. So, he went to find some food to eat. The monster found a big red apple. He was happy to eat the apple. It was very yummy. The monster's tummy was not hurt anymore. He was full and happy. After eating the apple, the monster went back to his home. He told his monster friends about the yummy apple. They all went to find more apples to eat. Now, all the monsters were happy and full.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a big porch. On the porch lived a happy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his red ball all day long. One gloomy day, the sky was gray, and the wind was strong. Spot went outside to play on the porch with his red ball. He ran and jumped, and then he would drop the ball and pick it up again. As Spot played, the wind blew harder. Suddenly, the wind took the red ball from Spot's mouth and it rolled off the porch. Spot was sad. He looked at the gloomy sky and wished for his ball back. Just then, the wind stopped, and the ball rolled back to the porch. Spot was so happy! He picked up the ball and continued to play. From that day on, Spot and his red ball had many more fun days on the porch, even on gloomy days.

Lily was playing with her doll in her room. She loved her doll very much. She gave her doll a name, a dress, and a hat. She pretended that her doll was her friend and they had tea parties and adventures together. One day, her brother Sam came into her room. He was bored and wanted to play with Lily. He saw her doll and thought it was weird. He did not like dolls. He liked cars and trucks and robots. He said to Lily, "Your doll is weird. It has no eyes and no hair. It is not fun. Give me your doll. I want to see what it can do." Lily was angry and scared. She did not want to give her doll to Sam. She knew he would break it or throw it away. She said to Sam, "No, you cannot have my doll. It is mine. It is not weird. It is special. It can talk and sing and dance. It is my friend. Go away and play with your own toys." Sam did not listen to Lily. He tried to grab her doll from her. Lily held on to her doll tightly. She tried to run away from Sam. But Sam was bigger and faster. He reached for her doll and pulled it hard. Lily screamed and cried. She felt a snap. Her doll's arm came off. Sam looked at the doll's arm in his hand. He felt bad. He did not mean to hurt Lily's doll. He saw Lily's tears and heard her sobs. He realized he was wrong. He said to Lily, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was mean and rude. I should not have taken your doll. It is your toy and you love it. I will help you fix it. Please forgive me." Lily stopped crying. She looked at Sam and saw his sorry face. She felt sad and angry, but also kind. She said to Sam, "Thank you, Sam. I forgive you. But you have to be careful and gentle with my doll. It is not weird. It is different. And different is good. We can all be friends and play together. But you have to respect my doll and my things." Sam nodded and hugged Lily. He gave her back her doll's arm. He said to Lily, "You're right, Lily. Different is good. And I respect your doll and your things. I'm sorry I hurt your friend. Can we be friends too?" Lily smiled and hugged Sam back. She said to Sam, "Yes, we can be friends too. And maybe you can teach me about your cars and trucks and robots. And I can teach you about my doll and her tea parties and adventures. We can learn from each other and have fun." They both agreed and went to find some glue to fix the doll's arm. They learned to share and care for each other's toys. They learned to appreciate and celebrate their differences. They learned to be good siblings and friends.
