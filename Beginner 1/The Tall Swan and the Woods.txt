Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Swan and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a tall swan. The swan liked to cook. One day, the swan was cooking in the woods. A little boy saw the swan. The boy said, "Hi, swan! What are you doing?" The swan said, "I am cooking. Do you want to help me?" The boy said, "Yes, I want to help you." They cooked together. But the fire was too big. The fire went to the trees. The trees were on fire. The swan and the boy were sad. The woods were gone. The end.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball and run after it. But then it started to rain. The rain was very big and wet. Tom and Lily got wet too. They did not like the rain. "Let's go home!" Lily said. "I am cold and wet!" "Me too!" Tom said. "But where is the exit? I can't see it!" They looked around. The park was very big and green. There were many trees and flowers. But they could not see the exit. The exit was a gate that led to the street. They needed to find the exit to go home. They walked and walked. They saw a pond with ducks. They saw a slide and a swing. They saw a bench and a trash can. But they did not see the exit. They were very sad and scared. They wanted to see their mom and dad. They wanted to be dry and warm. They wanted to stop the rain. "Help!" they cried. "We are lost!" Then they heard a voice. It was a man with a hat and a coat. He was holding an umbrella. "Hello, kids!" he said. "Are you OK? Do you need help?" "Yes, please!" Tom and Lily said. "We can't find the exit! We want to go home!" "Don't worry, I know where the exit is. Follow me!" he said. He smiled and took their hands. He led them to the exit. He showed them the gate and the street. "Here you are!" he said. "The exit is here. Do you see your house?" Tom and Lily looked. They saw their house. It was not far. They saw their mom and dad. They were waiting for them. They looked happy and worried. "Mom! Dad!" Tom and Lily shouted. They ran to them. They hugged them. "Where were you?" their mom asked. "We were so worried! Are you OK?" "We were lost in the park!" Tom said. "We could not find the exit! It was terrible!" "But a nice man helped us!" Lily said. "He showed us the exit! He was very kind!" Their mom and dad thanked the man. They gave him a cookie. They said goodbye. They took Tom and Lily home. They gave them dry clothes and hot soup. They read them a story. They tucked them in bed. Tom and Lily felt better. They were not wet and cold anymore. They were not sad and scared anymore. They were dry and warm. They were happy and safe. They loved their mom and dad. They loved their home. They closed their eyes and slept.

One day, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max were playing in the park. They liked to run, jump, and be flexible. They were good friends. Tom said, "Max, I am hungry. My stomach is empty." Max said, "Me too. Let's find some food." They looked around and saw a big, red apple on a tree. They wanted to get the apple, but it was too high. A little bird saw them and said, "I can help you get the apple. I can fly." Tom and Max were happy. The little bird flew up and pushed the apple down. It fell to the ground. Tom and Max saw the apple and tried to resist eating it. They knew they had to share. But, they were so hungry that they both jumped at the apple at the same time. The apple rolled away and fell into a pond. Tom and Max looked at each other and laughed. They knew they had to be more careful next time. They decided to go home and ask their families for food.
