Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Leaves

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for worms. They found a big worm and put it in a jar. "Look, it is moving!" Lily said. "It is restless. It wants to go back to the dirt." "Let's keep it as a pet," Tom said. "We can give it some leaves and water." They took the jar to the porch and put it on a table. They went to get some leaves and water. But they did not see that the sun was shining on the jar. The jar got very hot. The worm got very hot too. "Ouch, ouch, ouch!" the worm said. "It is burning! Help me, help me!" The worm tried to get out of the jar, but it was too tight. It was very scared and sad. Tom and Lily came back with the leaves and water. They saw the worm in the jar. They saw that it was burning. "Oh no, oh no, oh no!" they said. "We are sorry, worm! We did not mean to hurt you!" They quickly opened the jar and took out the worm. They put it on the grass and poured some water on it. They hoped that it would feel better. "Thank you, thank you, thank you!" the worm said. "You are kind. You saved me." The worm smiled and waved at them. Then it crawled back to the dirt. Tom and Lily felt sorry for the worm. They learned that they should not keep worms in jars. They decided to let the worms be free in the garden. They played with other things instead.

Once upon a time, there was a bright red fox. The fox lived in the big woods. One day, he saw a small bird standing on a branch. The bird looked sad. "Hi, little bird. Why are you sad?" asked the fox. The bird said, "I can't fly high like the other birds. I feel alone." The fox felt bad for the bird and wanted to help. The fox had an idea. "Let's stand together and be friends," said the fox. They played and had fun all day. The bird was happy and felt better. The fox and the bird learned that when you are kind and help others, you make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with a long string. One day, the string was lost. Fluffy was very sad. Fluffy began to look for the string. Fluffy looked in the big room, the small room, and even under the bed. But the string was not there. Fluffy did not give up. Then, Fluffy saw the string outside in the yard. Fluffy had to hurry to get the string. Fluffy ran fast, picked up the string and played all day. Fluffy was so happy to find the string.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a big anchor. One day, the boat met a little fish. The little fish was not comfortable in the deep water. The boat wanted to help the little fish. The boat said, "Little fish, I will show you a safe place. Follow me!" The little fish followed the boat. They went to a nice, shallow place. The little fish felt comfortable there. The boat was happy to help. Later, the little fish saw the boat's anchor. The little fish said, "Thank you, boat, for showing me this safe place. You are a good friend." The boat and the little fish were happy and became best friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a fast rabbit named Bob. Bob had big ears that he liked to wiggle. He lived in a small house in the woods with his family. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, Bob wanted to succeed in a race. He asked his friends, a slow turtle and a tiny bird, to race with him. They all lined up at the start of the race. Bob wiggled his ears, ready to run fast. When the race started, Bob ran very fast. His friends tried to keep up, but Bob was too fast for them. In the end, Bob succeeded in the race and won. His friends were happy for him and they all played together after the race.
