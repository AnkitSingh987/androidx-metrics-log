Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Tree

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars in the garden. They liked to make them zoom and crash and beep. Lily had a pink car and Ben had a blue car. They were having fun. But then Lily saw something shiny in the grass. She got out of her car and picked it up. It was a crystal. It sparkled in the sun. Lily was happy. She showed it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I found a crystal! It is so pretty!" Lily said. Ben looked at the crystal. He wanted it too. He reached out his hand and tried to grab it from Lily. "Give it to me, Lily! It is mine!" Ben said. Lily did not want to give it to him. She held the crystal tight and moved away from Ben. "No, Ben, it is mine! I found it first!" Lily said. Ben got angry. He got in his car and drove towards Lily. He wanted to bump her and make her drop the crystal. Lily saw him coming and got scared. She got in her car and tried to drive away. But she did not know how to reverse. She hit a tree and got stuck. "Help, help!" Lily cried. Ben saw that Lily was in trouble. He felt sorry for her. He did not want to hurt her. He knew how to reverse. He was helpful. He drove his car to Lily and helped her get out of the tree. "Are you okay, Lily?" Ben asked. "Yes, thank you, Ben. You are helpful. You know how to reverse." Lily said. Lily smiled at Ben. Ben smiled back. He did not want the crystal anymore. He wanted to be Lily's friend. "Can we share the crystal, Lily? It is so pretty." Ben said. "Okay, Ben, we can share the crystal. It is more fun to play together." Lily said. They put the crystal in the middle of their cars and drove around it. They made it zoom and crash and beep. They were happy.

One day, a little girl named Mia was having a tea party with her toys. She was very happy and excited. She had a big cup of tea and some yummy cookies. Mia was a bit careless and did not look where she put her cup of tea. "Oh no!" Mia cried when she saw a big mark on her mom's favorite table. The mark was from the hot tea. Mia was very sad and scared. She did not want her mom to be mad at her. Mia's toy bear, named Teddy, said, "Don't worry, Mia. We can clean the mark together." Mia and Teddy tried to clean the mark, but it did not go away. They felt bad. When Mia's mom saw the mark, she was not happy. But she saw that Mia was very sad. Mom said, "Mia, you should be more careful with your tea. But it's okay. We all make mistakes. Let's clean the mark together." Mia and her mom cleaned the mark, and Mia learned to be more careful with her tea.

One day, a cute little train was going to arrive at the town. The train was very happy because it could see all the kids waiting for it. The train went fast, and steam came out of it. As the train got closer, it saw a big rock on the track. The train said, "Oh no! There is a big rock. What should I do?" The train tried to stop, but it was going too fast. The kids saw the rock too and started to worry. In the end, the train could not stop in time. It hit the rock and broke. The kids were sad because they could not ride the cute train. The train was sad too because it could not make the kids happy.

One day, a big dog found a big mine. In the mine, there were many big rocks. The dog was very happy. He wanted to count all the rocks in the mine. He started to count: one, two, three... While the big dog was counting, a little cat came to the mine. The cat saw the big rocks too. The cat wanted the rocks to be her own. She thought, "I will take the rocks while the dog is counting." So, the cat took one, two, three rocks and ran away. The big dog saw the cat run away with the rocks. He was not happy. He stopped counting and chased the cat. But, the cat was too fast. The big dog could not catch the cat. As the dog chased the cat, he found a big pile of toys. The big dog forgot about the rocks and played with the toys. The cat and the dog both had something they liked.
