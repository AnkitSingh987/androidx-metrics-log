Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Car

Ben and Mia are playing with their toy cars in the garden. They make a lot of noise and pretend to race and crash. Mia has a red car and Ben has a blue car. "Look, Mia, I can go faster than you!" Ben says, zooming his car on the grass. "No, you can't, Ben, my car is better!" Mia says, chasing him with her car. They follow each other around the garden, making sounds like engines and horns. They have fun and laugh. But then, Ben sees a big rock and has an idea. He drives his car towards the rock and makes it jump over it. He thinks he is very clever. "Wow, Ben, that was cool!" Mia says, watching him. But when Ben's car lands on the other side of the rock, it hits a stick and flips over. It makes a loud bang and breaks into pieces. Ben's car is a wreck. "Oh no, Ben, your car!" Mia says, running to him. Ben is sad and angry. He picks up the pieces of his car and throws them on the ground. He cries and stomps his feet. "It's not fair, it's not fair!" he says. "My car was real, not fake!" Mia feels sorry for Ben. She goes to him and hugs him. She gives him her car and says: "Here, Ben, you can have my car. It's not fake, either. It's just a toy. We can share and play together." Ben stops crying and looks at Mia. He smiles and says: "Thank you, Mia, you are very nice. I'm sorry I was mean. Can we still be friends?" Mia nods and says: "Of course, Ben, we are always friends. Come on, let's play some more." They hug and take turns playing with the red car. They forget about the wreck and have fun again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue lived on a ranch with her mom and dad. She loved to play with her dog, Max. Max was a big, fierce dog, but he was very nice to Sue. One day, Sue and Max were playing outside. Sue said, "Max, I want to play hide and seek. You hide, and I will find you." Max wagged his tail and ran to hide. Sue closed her eyes and counted to ten. Then she opened her eyes and looked for Max. Sue looked behind a tree, but Max was not there. She looked in the barn, but Max was not there either. Sue started to feel sad. She called out, "Max, where are you? I want to find you!" Max heard Sue and came running to her. Sue was so happy to see Max. They hugged and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a big ostrich named Ollie. Ollie loved to run very fast. He would zoom around the field all day, feeling ready to race anyone who came by. Ollie had a best friend named Timmy, a tiny turtle. Timmy wished he could zoom like Ollie, but he could only walk slow. One day, Ollie and Timmy saw a sign. It said there was a big race for all the animals. Ollie was excited and knew he would win. Timmy wanted to race too, but Ollie told him he was too slow. Timmy felt sad but still went to watch Ollie race. The race began and Ollie zoomed ahead of everyone. But then, something unexpected happened. Ollie tripped on a rock and fell down. All the other animals passed him. Timmy saw this and knew he had to help. He slowly walked to Ollie and helped him get up. Together, they finished the race. Ollie didn't win, but he learned that friends are more important than being the fastest.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Spot lived in a small house. Spot loved to whistle and play all day. He had many friends who liked to play with him too. One day, Spot saw a little bird who was sad. "Why are you sad, little bird?" Spot asked. The little bird said, "I lost my way home, and I don't know how to find it." Spot wanted to be helpful, so he said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you find your home." Spot and the little bird went on a long walk. They looked everywhere for the bird's home. Spot whistled as they walked. Soon, they found the little bird's home. The little bird was so happy! "Thank you, Spot! You are so helpful!" the bird said. Spot felt very good for helping his new friend. They played and whistled together all day. From that day on, Spot and the little bird were the best of friends. The moral of the story is to always be helpful and kind to others. When you help someone, you make a friend and feel good too.
