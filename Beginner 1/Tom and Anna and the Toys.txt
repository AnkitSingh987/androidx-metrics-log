Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Anna and the Toys

Tom and Anna are brother and sister. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books and games. But their room is very messy. Their mom is not happy. "Tom and Anna, you have too many things in your room. You need to sell some of them and make space. Or I will sell them for you." Mom says. Tom and Anna do not want to sell their things. They love their things. They have an idea. They take a large curtain from the closet. They use the curtain to cover their things. They make a big pile of things under the curtain. They think mom will not see their things. "Look, mom, we made space in our room. We sold some of our things." Tom and Anna say. Mom comes to their room. She sees the large curtain. She is not fooled. She lifts the curtain and sees the pile of things. "Tom and Anna, this is not selling. This is hiding. You did not make space. You made a mess. You need to sell some of your things for real. Or I will sell them for you." Mom says. Tom and Anna are sad. They do not want to sell their things. But they do not want mom to sell them either. They decide to sell some of their things. They pick the things they do not play with much. They put them in a box. They go outside. They make a sign that says "Sell". They wait for people to come and buy their things. They hope to keep some of their things. They hope mom will be happy.

Once upon a time, there was an old painter who loved to paint. He had a big dream to fly high in the sky. He would watch the birds fly and wish he could join them. One day, the old painter had an idea. He painted a big, pretty bird on a large piece of paper. He thought, "If I can make a bird, maybe I can fly too!" He worked very hard to make the bird just right. The next day, the old painter took the big bird outside. He held on tight and ran fast. Suddenly, the wind blew hard, and the big bird lifted the old painter off the ground! He was flying! The old painter was so happy, he laughed and laughed. His dream came true, and it was all because of his beautiful painting.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a big teddy bear that she loved to hug. Ben had a wooden hammer and some nails that he liked to bang on the floor. They were having fun until Ben saw Lily's teddy bear. "Can I play with your bear?" Ben asked. "No, it's mine!" Lily said. "You have your hammer and nails." "But I want to hug the bear too!" Ben said. He tried to grab the bear from Lily, but she held on tight. "Let go!" Lily shouted. "You'll hurt my bear!" Ben did not listen. He pulled harder and harder, and the bear ripped in half. Lily gasped and started to cry. Ben saw the stuffing and the button eyes fall out of the bear. He felt bad and scared. "I'm sorry, Lily!" Ben said. "I didn't mean to break your bear!" Lily was very sad and angry. She threw the bear's head at Ben and hit him in the face. Ben cried out and dropped his hammer and nails. He felt a sharp pain in his foot. He looked down and saw a nail sticking out of his sock. He had stepped on one of his nails by accident. He felt very uncomfortable and started to cry louder. Lily heard Ben's cry and saw the nail in his foot. She felt sorry for him and stopped crying. She ran to him and helped him pull out the nail. She threw the nail away and hugged Ben. "I'm sorry, Ben!" Lily said. "I didn't mean to hurt you!" Ben hugged Lily back and said, "I'm sorry, Lily! I didn't mean to hurt your bear!" They both said they were sorry and hugged each other. They decided to share their toys and play nicely. They found some tape and tried to fix the bear. They gave the bear a big hug and said they loved him. They were happy again.
