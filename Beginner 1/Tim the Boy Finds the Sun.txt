Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Sun

One day, a small boy named Tim went out to play. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. Tim saw a big rock and sat on it. He looked around and saw his friend, Bee, flying in the sky. Bee was a persistent little bug who never gave up. "Hi, Bee!" said Tim. "Come play with me!" Bee flew down and said, "Okay, Tim! But it will be night soon. We have to go home before it gets dark." Tim and Bee played in the grass. They ran and laughed. But then, they saw a big puddle of mud. They were scared. They did not want to get dirty. "Let's jump over it!" said Tim. Bee agreed. Tim jumped first, but he fell in the mud. He was sad and dirty. Bee helped Tim get up. "Don't worry, Tim," said Bee. "We can wipe the mud off." They wiped the mud off Tim's clothes and went home. As the night came, they remembered what Bee said about going home before it got dark. They were happy they made it home in time. Tim learned to listen to his friend, and they both had a fun day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. It was a sunny day. Tim played with his ball and had fun. He saw a wet dog. The dog was running and splashing in a puddle. The dog was very happy. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. Tim saw something on the ground. It was a wallet. The wallet was wet too. Tim wanted to find the person who lost the wallet. He knew it was important to help. Tim started to look for the person. Tim asked his mom to help him. They looked for the person together. At last, they found a man. The man was looking for his wallet. Tim gave the wallet to the man. The man was very happy. Tim felt good because he helped. Tim gained a new friend that day.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They were both very eager to go on an adventure. So they decided to explore a nearby forest. The special thing about this forest was a giant bow twisted around the entrance. Jack wanted to untwist the bow and get a closer look, but Jill didn't want him to. She said it was too dangerous. Jack wasn't going to let Jill stop him. He got two sticks and started to untwist the bow. Suddenly, the bow snapped and both Jack and Jill were scared. Jack was too scared to move and Jill tried her best to comfort him. But it was too late. The bow had twisted into a big knot! Jack and Jill were stuck in the forest and had to find a new way out. They never found a new way out. Jack and Jill stayed stuck in the forest until the end of time.

One day, a little dog named Max saw a big pit. Max was scared because he felt helpless. Max did not know how to get around the pit. He wanted to go to his friend's house on the other side. Max thought about what to do. Max saw a big bird named Betty. Max said, "Betty, can you help me? I need to go to my friend's house. There is a big pit in the way. I feel helpless." Betty listened to Max's request. Betty said, "I will help you, Max." Betty picked up Max with her strong wings. She flew Max over the pit. Max was very happy. He thanked Betty for helping him. Max went to his friend's house and played all day.

Once upon a time, a helpful dog named Spot lived in a small house. Spot had a toy with a loop. He loved to play with it all day. One day, Spot went to the park. He saw a cat named Fluffy. Spot wanted to be friends with Fluffy. He said, "Hi, I am Spot. I want to introduce you to my toy loop. We can play together." Fluffy looked at the loop and said, "Okay, let's play." They played with the loop, but then it got stuck in a tree. Spot and Fluffy tried to get it down, but they couldn't. They were sad. The loop stayed in the tree, and they went home without it.
