Category: Beginner
Title: The Ant and the Leaf

Once upon a time, there was a little ant. The ant lived in a small cell in the ground. The ant had many friends in the cell. They all worked together to find food and keep their home clean. One day, the ant went out to find food. The ant saw a big leaf. The leaf was far away from the cell. The ant wanted to take the leaf back to the cell. The ant tried to pick up the leaf, but it was too heavy. The ant started to struggle. The ant pushed and pulled, but the leaf would not move. The ant felt angry. The ant wanted to take the leaf to the cell. The ant did not want to give up. The ant asked for help from its friends. The friends came and helped the ant. Together, they all moved the leaf back to the cell. The ant was happy and not angry anymore. The end.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play knights and dragons. Tom has a sword. It is made of wood. Sam has a shield. It is made of cardboard. One day, they see a big hill. They think it is a castle. They want to join the knights in the castle. They run to the hill with their sword and shield. They pretend to fight the dragons. But the hill is not a castle. It is a pile of dirt. The dirt is dirty. It makes their sword and shield dirty. It makes their clothes dirty. It makes their faces dirty. They hear a voice. It is Mom. She is angry. She says, "What are you doing? You are so dirty! Come here and clean up!" Tom and Sam are sad. They do not want to stop playing. They do not want to clean up. They say, "Sorry, Mom. We were just playing knights and dragons. Can we join you later?" Mom sighs. She says, "Okay, but first you have to wash your sword and shield. And your clothes. And your faces. Then you can join me for a snack." Tom and Sam nod. They wash their sword and shield. They wash their clothes. They wash their faces. They are not dirty anymore. They are happy. They join Mom for a snack. They say, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best."

Anna and Ben were bored. They had no toys to play with. They had no books to read. They had no games to do. They looked out the window and saw a big hill. They wanted to go to the hill and have fun. They asked their mom if they could go to the hill. Their mom said yes, but they had to take a basket with them. She put some bread and cheese and apples in the basket. She said they had to be back before dark. She said they had to be careful and not get lost. Anna and Ben took the basket and went out the door. They started to walk to the hill. But walking was too slow and boring. They decided to march to the hill. They lifted their feet high and swung their arms. They sang a song as they marched. They felt happy and excited. They marched and marched until they reached the hill. They saw a big tree on the top of the hill. They wanted to climb the tree and see the view. They left the basket under the tree and climbed up. They saw the sky and the clouds and the birds. They saw their house and their mom waving at them. They waved back and smiled. They climbed down the tree and opened the basket. They were hungry and thirsty. They ate the bread and cheese and apples. They drank some water. They felt full and satisfied. They thanked their mom for the food. They packed the basket and got ready to go back. They marched and marched until they reached their house. They saw their mom waiting for them. She hugged them and kissed them. She asked them if they had fun. They said yes, they had fun. They said they climbed the tree and saw the view. They said they ate the food and drank the water. They said they marched and sang and smiled. Their mom said she was proud of them. She said they were brave and smart and good. She said they could go to the hill again another day. She said they could take more toys and books and games with them. She said they could have more fun and more adventures. Anna and Ben said they loved their mom. They said they loved the hill. They said they loved the basket. They said they loved to march. They said they were not bored anymore. They said they were happy.
