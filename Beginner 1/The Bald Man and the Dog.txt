Category: Beginner
Title: The Bald Man and the Dog

Once there was a bald man. He was going on a trip. He had to pack his bag. In his bag, he put his wallet. His wallet had all his money. The bald man went to the park. He saw a big tree. He put his bag under the tree. He played with his ball. He had fun. But then, a dog came. The dog took his bag. The bald man tried to catch the dog. But the dog was too fast. The dog ran away with his bag. The bald man was sad. He lost his wallet and all his money.

---

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. Lily wants to go first. She runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. "Be careful, Lily!" Ben says. "The slide is wet and slippery!" Lily does not listen. She sits on the slide and pushes herself down. But she goes too fast and slips off the slide. She falls on the grass and hurts her knee. She cries out loud. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts!" Lily says. Ben runs to Lily and hugs her. He helps her stand up and walks with her to their mom. Their mom is sitting on a bench with a bag. She sees Lily's knee and takes out a band-aid and a pill. "Lily, you have a boo-boo. Let me put this band-aid on your knee. It will make it better," Mom says. She sticks the band-aid on Lily's knee and kisses it. Then she gives Lily a pill. "This is a pill. It will make the pain go away. But you have to be careful with pills. You can only take them when Mom or Dad gives them to you. And you have to swallow them with water. Do you understand?" Mom says. Lily nods. She takes the pill and drinks some water from Mom's bottle. She feels better. "Thank you, Mom. I'm sorry I was not careful. I will listen to Ben next time," Lily says. Mom smiles and hugs Lily and Ben. "That's good, Lily. I'm glad you're okay. And I'm proud of you, Ben, for being a good brother and helping Lily. Now, do you want to play some more in the park?" Mom says. Lily and Ben nod. They hold Mom's hand and go back to the park. They play on the swings and the seesaw. They do not go on the slide again. They have fun and are careful.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. The frog lived in a gloomy pond. The pond had many trees around it. The frog liked to play in the water. One day, the little frog wanted to play with his friends. But he could not find them. The frog felt sad. He did not want to be alone. The frog looked everywhere for his friends. At last, the little frog found his friends. They were playing near the pond. The frog was very happy. He did not lose his friends. They all played together in the gloomy pond. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a small, harmless bug. He loved to play with his friends. One day, a big helicopter came to the park and made a lot of noise. The bug and his friends got scared. The bug said to his friends, "Let's hide! We can bury ourselves in the sand until the noise stops." So, they all dug holes and buried themselves, waiting for the helicopter to leave. After a while, the helicopter went away, and the park was quiet again. The bug and his friends came out of their hiding spots. They learned that it is good to help each other when they are scared. They also learned that sometimes big, noisy things can be harmless.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She had long hair that she liked to play with. As she walked, she saw a big rock in her way. Sue tried to go around the rock, but she did not see a small hole in the ground. She stumbled and fell down. Sue got up and felt angry. Sue wanted to go home but she did not know the way back. She started to cry. A small bird saw Sue and wanted to help her. The bird flew around Sue's head and pulled her hair a little. Sue felt the pull and looked up. The bird wanted Sue to follow it. Sue walked behind the bird and they went back to her house. When they got there, Sue was very happy. She said thank you to the bird and gave it a hug. Sue was not angry anymore. She went inside her house and told her mom the story. From that day on, Sue and the bird were best friends.
