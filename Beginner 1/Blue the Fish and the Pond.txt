Category: Beginner
Title: Blue the Fish and the Pond

One day, a happy fish named Blue lived in a big pond. Blue loved to dive deep into the water and play with his friends. They liked to swim and splash all day long. Blue was very good at diving, and his friends always watched him. One sunny day, Blue and his friends were playing by the pond. Blue wanted to show his friends how high he could dive. He swam back and jumped up high. But something unexpected happened! Blue jumped so high that he landed on a big green leaf outside the pond. Blue was scared and didn't know what to do. His friends saw him and wanted to help. They had an idea! They all swam fast and splashed water on the big green leaf. The leaf began to move closer to the pond. With one big splash, Blue slid off the leaf and back into the pond. He was so happy to be back with his friends. They all laughed and played together, knowing that they could always help each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a street. On this street, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Lily wanted to surprise her friends. She thought and thought about what would make them happy. Then, she had an idea! She would make them all pretty hats to wear. Lily worked hard to make the hats. She used paper, glue, and lots of colors. When the hats were done, she put them in a big box. She walked down the street to where her friends were playing. Lily opened the box and showed her friends the hats. They were all so surprised and happy! They put on the hats and played all day long. Lily felt happy that she could make her friends smile.

Tom and Jen liked to play knights and dragons in the park. Tom had a big stick for a sword and Jen had a lid for a shield. They pretended to fight the scary dragon that lived in the bushes. One day, they saw a new kid in the park. He had a bright orange shirt and a mean face. He came over to Tom and Jen and said, "Give me your sword and shield. I want to play with them." Tom and Jen did not want to give him their sword and shield. They said, "No, these are ours. You can find your own stick and lid." The new kid got angry. He pushed Tom to the ground and grabbed his stick. He hit Jen on the head with it and took her lid. He laughed and said, "Now I have the sword and shield. I am the best knight. You are the weak dragons." Tom and Jen were hurt and sad. They cried and ran away. They did not want to play knights and dragons anymore. They wanted to go home. The new kid did not care. He shrugged and went to the bushes. He said, "I will show the dragon who is the boss." He waved his stick and lid and shouted, "Come out, dragon. I will fight you." But there was no dragon in the bushes. There was a big dog. The dog did not like the new kid. He did not like his orange shirt and his mean face. He did not like his stick and lid. He did not like his noise. The dog growled and jumped out of the bushes. He bit the new kid on the leg and tore his shirt. He made him drop his stick and lid. He chased him out of the park. The new kid was hurt and scared. He cried and ran away. He did not want to play knights and dragons anymore. He wanted to go home. But he had no sword and shield. He had no friends. He had no fun. He had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tom. He had a red cap that he loved very much. Tom wore his cap every day. One sunny day, Tom met a girl named Ann. Ann saw Tom's cap and said, "I like your cap! Can I try it on?" Tom said, "No, it's mine!" They started to fight over the cap. A big wind blew the cap away. Tom and Ann stopped fighting. They looked at each other and said, "Let's find the cap together!" They became friends and found the cap under a tree. They played together all day, taking turns wearing the cap.
