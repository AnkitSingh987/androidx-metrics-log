Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Famous Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot had a big, red ball that he loved to play with. One day, while Spot was playing with his ball, it rolled behind a big tree. Spot went to the tree and tried to peek behind it to find his ball. But he could not see it. He looked left and right, but the ball was still missing. Spot was very sad. Just then, his friend Cat came by. Cat asked, "Spot, why are you sad?" Spot replied, "My ball is gone. I can't find it." Cat said, "Let's look together!" They both peeked behind the tree and found the ball. Spot was so happy, and he thanked Cat for helping him. They played together with the ball and had a great day.

One day, a rat saw an expensive toy car. The rat wanted the toy car very much. The rat tried to go near the toy car, but a big cat was there. The cat did not like the rat. The rat was sad. The rat thought of a plan. The rat saw a ball. The rat pushed the ball to the cat. The cat saw the ball and wanted to play. The cat left the toy car to play with the ball. The cat was happy. The rat was happy too. The rat could go to the expensive toy car now. The rat played with the toy car all day. The cat played with the ball. They both had fun.

Once upon a time there was a smart cat. The cat could do lots of things that other cats couldn't. Every day, the cat liked to bow for people. The cat had a friend. One day, the friend asked the cat why it liked to bow. The cat looked at its friend and said, "one day you will find out". The friend was very curious, so they decided to follow the cat. The cat led the friend to a big house. The cat bowed to the house and suddenly the door opened. The friend was so surprised. The cat smiled and said, "you never know who you might meet when you bow". They went inside and it turns out the house belonged to a princess. She had been watching the cat bow and she was so impressed with how smart it was. The princess invited the cat and its friend to tea. She even gave them a special treat as a reward for being so smart. From then on, the friend understood why the cat liked to bow.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was restless and always looking for fun. One day, he went outside to play. The sky was full of rain, but Tim didn't care. He loved to play in the rain. Tim saw his friend, Sam, hiding behind a tree. Sam loved to joke and make Tim laugh. Tim thought Sam was going to jump out and joke with him. He walked closer to the tree, ready to laugh. But it wasn't Sam behind the tree, it was a big, friendly dog! The dog had a tag that said "Buddy." Tim was so surprised! He laughed and laughed. Buddy joined Tim in playing in the rain. They splashed in puddles and had so much fun. That day, Tim made a new friend and learned that sometimes, the best jokes come from the most unexpected places.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She loved to tell stories to her friends. One day, she found a big iron in her mom's room. She didn't know what it was, so she was fearful. Lisa was scared, but she wanted to learn. She asked her mom, "What is this big iron?" Her mom smiled and said, "It's an iron to make clothes smooth. But be careful, it can be very hot." Lisa listened to her mom and didn't touch the iron. Lisa told her friends about the big iron and how it can be hot. She also told them not to touch it. Her friends learned from Lisa's story. The moral of the story is to always ask and learn about things we don't know, and to share our knowledge with others.
