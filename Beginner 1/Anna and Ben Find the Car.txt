Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben Find the Car

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with dolls and cars. But today, they have a fight. Anna wants to play with the pink car, but Ben says it is his. Ben grabs the car from Anna and runs away. Anna is sad and angry. She cries and shouts at Ben. Ben does not want to share the car. He thinks it is the best car. He puts it on the table and plays with it. He forgets to watch out for the cat. The cat jumps on the table and knocks over the car. The car falls on the floor and breaks. Ben is shocked and sad. He cries and shouts at the cat. Anna hears Ben cry. She runs to see what is wrong. She sees the broken car on the floor and the cat on the table. She feels sorry for Ben. She forgets the fight. She hugs Ben and says, "It's okay. We can fix the car. Or we can play with something else. We are still friends." Ben hugs Anna and says, "Thank you. I'm sorry I was mean. You can play with the pink car. We are still friends." They smile and wipe their tears. They pick up the broken car and the cat. They go to find some glue and some other toys. They are happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree. It was so big that it could wrap its arms around all the other trees. One day, a little, red bird came to sit on the tree. The bird was sad because it could not find a match to play with. Then, a small, green bug came to the tree. The bug said, "I will play with you, little bird." The bird was happy, and they played all day long. But the big, thick tree was not happy. The tree wanted to play, too. So, the tree shook and shook, trying to join in the fun. The little bird and the bug were scared of the tree's shaking. They did not know why the tree was shaking. They thought the tree did not like them. But then, the big, thick tree stopped shaking and said, "I just want to play, too!" The little bird and the bug were surprised. They did not know trees could talk! They all became friends and played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with cars and trucks. Today is Tom's birthday. He has a big cake and many balloons. He invites Mia to his house to celebrate. "Happy birthday, Tom!" Mia says. She gives him a present. It is a new truck. It is red and shiny. Tom likes it very much. He says, "Thank you, Mia! This is a great truck. Let's play with it outside." They go to the backyard. There is a hill of dirt. Tom and Mia put their trucks on the hill. They make them go down fast. They make loud noises. "Vroom, vroom!" They have fun. But then, something bad happens. Tom's truck goes too fast. It hits a rock. It flies in the air. It lands on the fence. It breaks into pieces. It is a wreck. "Oh no!" Tom cries. He runs to his truck. He picks up the pieces. He is sad. His new truck is ruined. Mia feels sorry for Tom. She runs to him. She hugs him. She says, "I'm sorry, Tom. It was an accident. Your truck was very high. It was too high for the fence. Don't be sad. I have an idea." She takes him to her house. She has a box of tools. She says, "We can fix your truck. We can use these tools. We can make it new again. It will be fun." Tom smiles. He says, "Really? You can do that? Thank you, Mia. You are a good friend. Let's fix my truck." They work together. They use the tools. They put the pieces back. They make the truck whole again. It is not as shiny as before. But it still works. It can still go fast. They go back to the hill. They put their trucks on the hill. They make them go down fast. They make loud noises. "Vroom, vroom!" They have fun. They celebrate Tom's birthday. They eat cake and balloons. They are happy. They are friends.
