Category: Beginner
Title: Pinky the Cheerful Cat and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful cat named Pinky. Pinky was pink and loved to play all day. She lived in a small house with her best friend, a little boy named Tim. One sunny day, Pinky and Tim went outside to play in the yard. They ran and jumped, and had lots of fun. After a while, Pinky started to feel tired. She let out a big yawn and said, "Tim, I'm sleepy. Can we go inside and rest?" Tim looked at Pinky and said, "Okay, Pinky. Let's go inside and take a nap." They went inside their small house and laid down on their soft bed. Pinky yawned again, and they both closed their eyes. As they slept, they dreamed of all the fun things they would do the next day. They would play more games, eat yummy food, and laugh together. Pinky and Tim were very happy, and they couldn't wait for another fun day together.

Once there was a little plant. It was very weak. The plant wanted to grow big and strong. But it was hard because it lived next to a drain. The drain was full of dirty water. The little plant did not like the dirty water. One day, a bird came and saw the weak plant. The bird wanted to help the plant grow. The bird thought of a plan. It flew away and came back with some clean water. The bird gave the clean water to the weak plant. The weak plant drank the clean water, and something unexpected happened. The plant started to grow big and strong! The drain was not a bad thing anymore. The plant used the drain to hold on and grow even bigger. Now, the plant was happy and strong, and the bird was happy too.

Ben and Lily liked to play with their toy boat. They put the boat in the tub and made it sail. They pretended they were pirates and looked for treasure. One day, they found a shiny crown in the sand. It was gold and had red and blue stones. They wondered who it belonged to. Maybe it was a king's crown or a queen's crown. They decided to take the crown to their mom and ask her. They thought she would know. They dried the boat and put it in their bag. They ran to their mom and showed her the crown. Mom was surprised and happy. She hugged Ben and Lily and said they were very smart. She told them the crown was a gift from their dad. He was away on a ship and he sent them the crown to make them smile. Ben and Lily smiled and thanked their mom. They put the crown on their boat and made it sail again. They felt like royalty and had fun. They loved their dad and their mom and their boat.

Sam and Ben are brothers. They like to play with cars and blocks. But they do not like to clean up their toys. Their room is very messy. Mom and Dad are not happy. One day, Sam and Ben have a plan. They want to make a big fort with their toys. They take all their toys from their room and put them in the living room. They use blankets and pillows and chairs and books to make a fort. They have fun inside the fort. But Mom and Dad come home from work. They see the living room. They are very angry. They say, "What is this? This is a big mess! You have to clean up right now!" Sam and Ben do not want to clean up. They say, "No, this is our fort. We want to play more." They run back to the fort. But they do not see the toy car on the floor. Sam steps on the car and falls down. He hurts his foot. He feels a lot of pain. He cries. Ben tries to help him, but he trips over a block and hits his head. He feels pain too. He cries. Mom and Dad hear them cry. They come to the living room. They see Sam and Ben on the floor. They are hurt and sad. Mom and Dad feel sorry for them. They say, "We are sorry you are hurt. But you have to listen to us. You have to clean up your toys. They are not safe. They can cause pain. And they make a big mess." Sam and Ben say, "We are sorry, Mom and Dad. We will clean up our toys. We will not make a mess again." They hug Mom and Dad. They clean up their toys. They go to their room. They have no more fun. They have a bad ending.
