Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Jen and the Car

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big billboard. It has a picture of a toy car. The car can spin and make noises. Tom and Jen want the car. "Look, Jen, the car is so cool! Let's go and get it!" Tom says. "OK, Tom, but how? The billboard is very high. We can't reach it." Jen says. Tom has an idea. He is very smart. He sees a ladder near the billboard. He says, "We can use the ladder. Come on, Jen, follow me!" Tom and Jen run to the ladder. They climb up the ladder. They are very happy. They think they can get the car. But they are wrong. The ladder is not strong. It is old and broken. It shakes and falls. Tom and Jen fall too. They hit the ground. They are hurt. They cry and scream. A man sees them. He runs to help them. He calls the doctor. He says, "You are very silly. You should not climb the ladder. You should not touch the billboard. The car is not real. It is only a picture. You are in trouble now." Tom and Jen are very sad. They are sorry. They wish they did not see the billboard. They wish they did not climb the ladder. They wish they did not want the car. They learn a lesson. They will not do it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a suit that he loved very much. He wore it every day. But one day, his suit got bad. It had a big hole in it! Tim was very sad. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, my suit is bad. Can you please fix it?" His mom looked at the suit and said, "Yes, Tim, I will fix your suit. Please give it to me." Tim gave his mom the suit, and she started to fix it. While his mom was fixing the suit, Tim waited and played with his toys. After a while, his mom came back with the suit. It looked like new! Tim was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Mom! I love my suit!" And from that day on, Tim took very good care of his suit.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play together. One day, they find a big box of costumes in the attic. They want to try them on. "Look, Sam, I have a hat and a coat!" Tom says. He puts them on and looks in the mirror. "I look like a detective!" "I have a wig and a dress!" Sam says. He puts them on and looks in the mirror. "I look like a princess!" They laugh and have fun. Then they see a mustache in the box. It is black and curly. "Can I have the mustache?" Tom asks. He thinks it will make him look more like a detective. "OK, you can have it," Sam says. He is kind. He picks the mustache and gives it to Tom. "Thank you, Sam!" Tom says. He sticks the mustache on his face and smiles. "How do I look?" "You look funny!" Sam says. He giggles. They play some more and pretend to solve mysteries and have adventures. They are happy. They like to share and be kind. They are good friends.

Tom is a cat. He likes to balance on things. He can balance on the fence, on the chair, on the book. He is very good at balancing. One day, he sees a big bird on the roof. He wants to catch the bird. He jumps on the fence and walks to the roof. He balances on the edge of the roof. He sees the bird. He gets ready to pounce. But the bird is wise. It sees Tom. It flaps its wings and makes a loud noise. Tom is scared. He loses his balance. He falls off the roof. He lands on the ground. He hurts his leg. He cries. Mia is a girl. She lives in the house. She hears Tom cry. She runs outside. She sees Tom on the ground. She picks him up. She hugs him. She takes him inside. She puts a bandage on his leg. She gives him some milk. She tells him to be careful. Tom is sorry. He learns his lesson. He does not try to catch the bird again. He still likes to balance on things. But he balances on safer things. He balances on the sofa, on the table, on the pillow. He is happy. He is a wise cat.
