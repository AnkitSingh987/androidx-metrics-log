Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a careless boy who always forgot to put his toys away. One day, his mom told him to clean his room and put all his toys in the attic. Tim didn't want to do it, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. Tim took his toys up to the attic. He saw a big box and started putting his toys inside. But he was still being careless. He didn't close the box right, and some of his toys fell out. Tim didn't notice and went back downstairs. Later, Tim's little sister went to the attic to find a toy. She saw the open box and tripped over the toys that fell out. She started to cry because she was hurt. Tim heard her cry and ran to the attic. He saw what happened and felt very sorry. Tim helped his sister and promised to be more careful with his toys. He learned that being careless can hurt others. From that day on, Tim always put his toys away and closed the boxes right. And his sister never got hurt again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Buddy. Mia loved to play outside with Buddy. They would run, jump, and laugh together. One day, while playing, Mia and Buddy noticed a hole in their yard. "Look, Buddy," Mia said, "What do you think is in the hole?" Buddy barked and wagged his tail. Mia thought it was important to find out, so they went closer to look. As they got near the hole, a tiny mouse popped out. "Hello!" said the mouse. "I live in this hole. My name is Tim." Mia and Buddy were excited to make a new friend. They played together every day, and the hole became an important part of their playtime.

Once upon a time, there was a big castle. A king lived in the castle. The king had a big key to lock the castle door. Every night, the king would lock the door to keep everyone safe. One day, a little girl came to the castle. She saw the big key and thought it was a toy. The little girl said, "I want to play with the key!" The king said, "No, it is not a toy. It is the key to lock the castle door." The little girl did not listen. When the king was not looking, she took the key. She tried to lock the door, but it was not easy. She dropped the key and it broke. That night, the king could not lock the castle door. A big wind came and blew the door open. The wind was very strong and it made a big mess in the castle. The king was sad because his castle was not safe anymore. The end.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Missy. She loved to play outside in the sun, and one of her favorite things to do was to slide. She had a little, green slide she loved to ride up and down all day. One day, she was sliding and noticed a boy in the park. "Hi," Missy said to the boy. The boy smiled and asked, "What is your name?" Missy told him her name and that she loved to slide. The boy told her his name was Hal and said, "Let's race each other down the slide!” Missy and Hal lined up at the top of the slide. They looked at each other and smiled before they said, "On your marks, get set, go!" They both slid down the slide. Hal reached the bottom first and cheered for himself. Missy pouted her lip and said, "That's not fair!" Hal looked at her and said, "We can try again if you want." Missy smiled and said, "Okay, let's do it!" This time they both made it to the bottom of the slide at the same time. They smiled and cheered together and enjoyed their fair race. The end.

Ben and Mia are playing with toys in the living room. Ben has a big truck and Mia has a doll. They are having fun and making noises. "Vroom, vroom, my truck is fast and strong!" Ben says. "Look, my doll can dance and sing!" Mia says. But then Ben sees Mia's doll and wants it. He thinks it is more fun than his truck. He grabs the doll from Mia's hands. Mia is sad and angry. "Hey, that is my doll! Give it back!" Mia says. "No, it is mine now! Go away!" Ben says. He is harsh and does not share. Mia starts to cry. She wants her doll back. She tries to grab it from Ben, but he pushes her away. They start to fight and yell. Mom hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees Ben and Mia fighting over the doll. She is not happy. "Ben, Mia, what are you doing? Why are you fighting?" Mom says. "Ben grabbed my doll and he is harsh! He does not share!" Mia says. "Mia grabbed my truck and she is harsh! She does not share!" Ben says. Mom shakes her head. She takes the doll and the truck from Ben and Mia. She puts them on the shelf. "Ben, Mia, you are both harsh and selfish. You need to learn to share and be kind. No toys for you until you say sorry and hug each other." Mom says. Ben and Mia look at each other. They feel sorry and ashamed. They do not want to lose their toys. They do not want to make Mom angry. They do not want to fight anymore. "Sorry, Mia. I should not have grabbed your doll. It is yours and you like it." Ben says. "Sorry, Ben. I should not have grabbed your truck. It is yours and you like it." Mia says. They hug each other and smile. Mom smiles too. She gives them back their toys. "Good, Ben and Mia. I am proud of you. You are sharing and kind. Now you can play nicely together." Mom says. Ben and Mia nod and say thank you. They play with their toys and take turns. They are happy and friendly. Mom is happy too. She watches them and hugs them.
