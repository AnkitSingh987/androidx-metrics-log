Category: Beginner
Title: The New Tent and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a new tent. It was red and blue. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, liked to play in the tent. They had fun in the sun and the grass. One day, Tim and Spot saw a big spring. They jumped on it and went up and down. They laughed and barked, happy to play together. The spring was near the tent, so they could play all day. At the end of the day, Tim and Spot went back to the tent. They were tired but very happy. They had a nice nap in the new tent. The sun went down, and the stars came out. Tim and Spot dreamed of more fun days with the spring and the tent.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that he loved so much. It was made of gold, and he played with it every day. One day, Tim saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play with the gold toy too. But Tim did not want to share his toy. "Go away, dog!" Tim said. "This is my toy, and it is difficult to find another one like it." The dog did not listen and tried to take the toy away from Tim. They both pulled on the toy, and it started to tear. Tim began to cry. He did not want his gold toy to break. A kind man saw Tim and the dog. He walked over and said, "Let's not tear the toy. Why don't you both play together?" Tim thought about it and agreed. From that day on, Tim and the dog became best friends. They played with the gold toy together and had lots of fun. Tim learned that sharing can make playtime even better.

Once upon a time, there was a kind mechanic. He had a big, ugly truck. The truck was very loud and made a lot of noise. The mechanic loved his truck and used it to help people fix their cars. One day, the mechanic went to the park. He saw a little girl with a toy car. The toy car was broken. The little girl was sad. The mechanic wanted to help her, so he took out his tools and fixed the toy car. After fixing the toy car, the mechanic told the little girl, "Now, you can play with your car again. But make sure you shut the door when you're done playing." The little girl was very happy, and she thanked the mechanic. They both smiled and played with the toy car until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, in a small town, a boy named Tim went for a walk on a hot summer day. He saw an empty jar on the ground. Tim picked it up and decided to find something to put in it. Tim walked down the street and saw a line of ants. He thought it would be fun to follow the ants and see where they went. He walked and walked, following the ants all the way to a big tree. Under the tree, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was looking at a big red ball. Tim said, "Hi Sue! I found an empty jar. What can we put in it?" Sue said, "Let's put some of these ants in the jar!" Tim and Sue put some ants in the jar and closed the lid. They were happy to have caught the ants. But then, the ants got very mad. They pushed the lid off the jar and ran out. The ants climbed up Tim and Sue and started to bite them. Tim and Sue cried and ran away. They learned that it was not nice to put ants in a jar.

One day, a helpful cat named Tom went for a walk. He heard a noise coming from a big tree. He looked up and saw a little bird stuck in the tree. The bird was scared and said, "Help me, please!" Tom wanted to help the bird, so he tried to climb the tree. But it was too hard for him. He thought, "I need help to save the bird!" So he went to find his friend, a big dog named Max. Tom told Max about the bird and asked, "Can you help me?" Max was happy to help. They went to the tree and Max lifted Tom up with his nose. Tom reached the bird and helped it come out of the tree. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom and Max!" They all became good friends and played together every day.
