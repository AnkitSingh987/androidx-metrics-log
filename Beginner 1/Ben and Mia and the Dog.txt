Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Dog

Ben and Mia liked to play in the creek near their house. They made boats from sticks and leaves and watched them float on the water. Sometimes they saw fish and frogs and birds in the creek. They had a lot of fun. One day, Ben and Mia went to the creek with their mom. They brought some bread to feed the ducks. They saw a big duck with a green head and a brown duck with a yellow beak. They threw some bread to them and they came closer. Ben and Mia were happy. But then, a mean dog came running to the creek. It barked and growled and chased the ducks away. It tried to bite the bread and the boats. Ben and Mia were scared and upset. They ran to their mom and hugged her. Their mom was angry. She shouted at the dog and told it to go away. She ordered it to leave the creek and the ducks alone. She said it was a bad dog and it had to go home. The dog was afraid and ran away. Ben and Mia were glad. They thanked their mom and went back to the creek. They found their boats and the ducks came back. They fed them more bread and played with them. They forgot about the mean dog and had fun again. They loved the creek.

Jill liked to write in her diary every day. She wrote about her toys, her friends, her dreams and her feelings. She kept her diary in a secret place under her bed, where no one could find it. One day, her brother Jack was looking for his ball in her room. He saw a pink book under her bed and pulled it out. He opened it and saw Jill's writing and drawings. He laughed and said, "What is this? This is so messy and silly! Jill can't write at all!" Jill heard him and ran into her room. She was very angry and sad. She snatched her diary from his hands and shouted, "Give that back! That's mine! That's my diary! You can't read it! You're so mean!" Jack said, "Why do you care? It's just a bunch of scribbles. You should quit writing and do something else. Like playing ball with me." Jill said, "No! I like writing. It makes me happy. You should quit being a bully and leave me alone. And don't touch my diary again!" She hugged her diary and ran out of the room. She went to her mom and told her what happened. Her mom hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Jill. Jack was wrong to do that. Your diary is yours and you can write whatever you want in it. It's not messy or silly. It's beautiful and creative. Don't let anyone make you quit something you love." Jill smiled and said, "Thank you, mom. I love you. And I love my diary." She went to her secret place and wrote some more. She wrote about how she felt and how she stood up to Jack. She felt proud and happy. She didn't quit.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a long tunnel. A little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue liked to play near the tunnel. They loved to throw rocks and watch them roll down the tunnel. One day, they had a big fight. "I can throw rocks better than you!" said Tim. "No, you can't!" said Sue. "Let's see who can throw the farthest!" said Tim. They started to throw rocks into the tunnel. They were not being careful. They threw the rocks very hard. Suddenly, a small cat came out of the tunnel. The cat was scared and ran away. Tim and Sue felt bad. They knew they should have been careful. They said sorry to each other and promised to be more careful next time. They went to find the cat and give it a hug. From that day on, they played together nicely and always remembered to be careful near the tunnel.

Lily and Tom were hungry. They wanted to eat something yummy. Mom said she would make them sausages and eggs for breakfast. Lily and Tom were happy. Mom put the sausages in a pan on the stove. She turned on the fire. The sausages sizzled and popped. They smelled good. Mom cracked some eggs in a bowl. She whisked them with a fork. She added some cheese and salt. "Can I help you, Mom?" Lily asked. She liked to help in the kitchen. "Sure, sweetie. You can help me pour the eggs in another pan. Be careful, it's hot." Mom said. She gave Lily a big spoon. Lily took the spoon and went to the other pan. She tried to pour the eggs slowly and evenly. But she was too excited. She poured too fast. The eggs splashed out of the pan. Some of them landed on the stove. Some of them landed on the floor. Some of them landed on Tom. "Hey!" Tom shouted. He was covered in eggs. He did not like it. He wiped his face with his hands. He looked at Lily. He was angry. "Lily, you are so clumsy! You made a mess! You quit pouring eggs on me!" Tom said. Lily felt bad. She did not mean to pour eggs on Tom. She wanted to help Mom. She wanted to make Tom happy. She quit pouring eggs. She put down the spoon. She looked at Mom. She was sorry. "I'm sorry, Mom. I'm sorry, Tom. I did not want to pour eggs on you. I wanted to help. I wanted to eat sausages and eggs with you." Lily said. Mom smiled. She was not mad. She knew Lily was trying to help. She knew Lily loved Tom. She turned off the fire. She took the sausages out of the pan. They were brown and crispy. She put them on a plate. She took a cloth and wiped the stove and the floor. She took another cloth and wiped Tom's face and clothes. "It's okay, Lily. It was an accident. You don't have to be sorry. You are a good helper. And you are a good sister. Tom, you don't have to be angry. Lily did not do it on purpose. She loves you. And I love you both. Come on, let's eat our sausages and eggs. They are still hot and yummy." Mom said. Lily and Tom hugged Mom. They said they loved her too. They went to the table. They ate their sausages and eggs. They shared some bread and jam. They drank some juice. They were happy. They forgot about the mess. They enjoyed their breakfast. They thanked Mom for making it. They said it was the best breakfast ever.
