Category: Beginner
Title: The Green Tree and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big green tree. The tree was in a park where many kids liked to play. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the park with her mom. Lily saw the green tree and wanted to play near it. Lily's mom said, "Be careful, Lily! Don't go too close to the tree." Lily listened to her mom and played near the tree, but not too close. She saw a sign next to the tree that had a picture of a happy face on it. Lily liked the sign because it made her smile. Lily asked her mom, "What does the sign say?" Her mom read the sign out loud, "It says, 'Play here and have fun!'" Lily was happy and played near the green tree all day. She was careful and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia liked to gather things. She would gather rocks, sticks, and leaves. One day, she found a button. The button was big and shiny. Mia was very happy to find the button. Mia showed the button to her mom. Her mom said, "That is a very nice button, Mia." Mia wanted to keep the button. So, she put the button in a jar with her other things. The jar was on a shelf in her room. Mia had a friend named Tim. Tim was a healthy boy. He liked to run and play with Mia. One day, Tim came to play at Mia's house. Mia showed Tim her button. Tim liked the button too. They played with the button and had a lot of fun.

Sam is a big boy. He likes to play with his ball. He can throw it and catch it. He can kick it and roll it. He can bounce it and spin it. He has fun with his ball. But today, Sam has to go to school. He has an exam. He has to write and read and count. He does not like exams. They are hard and boring. He wants to play with his ball. He puts his ball in his backpack. He goes to the bus stop. He waits for the bus. He sees his friend Lily. Lily is a nice girl. She likes to play with Sam. She also has an exam. She is nervous and scared. Sam has an idea. He takes out his ball from his backpack. He says to Lily, "Let's play catch. It will make us happy and calm." Lily smiles and nods. She likes Sam's idea. They start to play catch. They throw the ball and catch it. They laugh and giggle. They forget about the exam. They have fun with the ball. But they do not see the car. The car is fast and loud. The car is dangerous. The car comes near the bus stop. The car does not stop. Sam throws the ball to Lily. Lily reaches for the ball. She does not see the car. The car hits Lily. Lily falls down. She drops the ball. She does not catch it. Sam screams. He runs to Lily. He sees blood and tears. He is sad and scared. He wishes he did not play catch. He wishes he did not have the ball. He wishes he did not have the exam. He wishes he could help Lily. He wishes he could catch her.

One day, a girl named Sue was very excite. Her family was going to a restaurant for dinner. She put on her pretty dress and shoes. At the restaurant, Sue saw a big dog. The dog was bossy. It barked loud and wanted food. Sue's mom gave the dog a treat. After dinner, Sue and her family went home. Sue was happy. She liked the restaurant and the bossy dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, red ball. One day, she went to play with her friend, Tom. They played catch with the big, red ball. They had a lot of fun. Tom had a pet cat named Max. Max was an unusual cat. He had big, blue eyes and a long, fluffy tail. Lily liked to pet Max. But today, Max was not feeling well. He was sick. Tom said, "Mom, Max is sick. What can we do?" Tom's mom said, "We need to give Max some medicine to make him feel better." Tom and Lily gave Max the medicine. They were very careful. Soon, Max started to feel better. He played with Lily and Tom again. They were all very happy. And from that day on, they always took good care of Max.
