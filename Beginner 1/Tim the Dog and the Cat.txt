Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Tim. Tim loved to play with his pipe toy. One day, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she had no toy to play with. Tim thought he could help her. Tim went to Sue and said, "Sue, I can lend you my pipe toy. We can play together and have fun." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." They played with the pipe toy all day long. They were very happy and had a lot of fun. As they played, a cat came near them. Tim was alert and barked at the cat. The cat ran away, and Sue laughed. She said, "Good job, Tim! You are a brave dog." They continued to play with the pipe toy until it was time to go home. Sue gave the pipe toy back to Tim and said, "Thank you for lending me your toy. We had a great day!" Tim wagged his tail and they said goodbye. From that day on, Tim and Sue were the best of friends.

One day, a kind dog named Spot went to the park. Spot loved to play with his ball. He would run and jump, and enjoy the sun. Spot saw a little bird near the exit of the park. The bird looked sad. Spot went to the bird and asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I lost my toy. I can't find it." Spot wanted to help. He looked around the park for the toy. Soon, he found a small, red toy near a tree. The little bird was so happy! "Thank you, kind dog!" said the bird. Spot and the bird played together. They had a lot of fun. They both enjoyed the day, and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy dog named Dare. Dare loved to play near the sea. One sunny day, Dare saw a big surf. He wanted to play with it. Dare met a friendly crab. The crab said, "Hi Dare, do you want to play with the surf?" Dare was happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with the surf!" So, Dare and the crab went to the surf together. They played and splashed in the water. Dare was not sleepy anymore. They had so much fun. As the sun went down, Dare and the crab went back to the sand. They said goodbye and went to their homes. Dare was very happy and he had a good sleep that night.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch movies with his big sister, Sue. One day, they found a mysterious movie. They had never seen it before. Tim and Sue were very excited to watch it. Before they started the movie, Tim poked Sue and said, "Do you think this movie will be fun?" Sue smiled and said, "I don't know, Tim. Let's watch and find out!" So, they sat down and started the movie. The movie was about a kind cat and a funny dog who went on an adventure. They met many new friends and had lots of fun. Tim and Sue laughed and clapped during the movie. When it was over, Tim poked Sue again and said, "That was a fun movie!" Sue agreed, and they both went to bed with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a little girl named Sue was sad. She had to go to the doctor for an operation. She did not know what it was, but she was scared. "Don't weep, Sue," her mom said. "It will be okay. The doctor will be calm and help you feel better." At the doctor's office, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim was also scared. He had a big red ball, but it had a hole and could not bounce. They talked and became friends. Tim said, "I wish my ball could bounce again." Sue had an idea. She asked the doctor, "Can you fix Tim's ball?" The doctor smiled and said, "I can try." He took the ball into the room where they do operations. When he came back, the ball was fixed! Tim and Sue were so happy. They played with the ball and forgot to be scared. The doctor helped Sue with her operation, and everything was okay.
