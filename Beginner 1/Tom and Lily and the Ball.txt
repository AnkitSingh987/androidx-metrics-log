Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Ball

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and run after it. Sometimes the ball went too far and they had to look for it. One day, the ball rolled down a hill and stopped near a cliff. A cliff is a high place where the ground ends and there is only air. Tom and Lily ran after the ball, but they stopped when they saw the cliff. They were fearful of the cliff. They did not want to fall down and get hurt. "Tom, how can we get the ball?" Lily asked. "I don't know, Lily. The ball is too close to the cliff. Maybe we can find a stick and poke it," Tom said. They looked around and saw a big tree. The tree had many sticks on the ground. They gathered some sticks and went back to the cliff. They tried to poke the ball with the sticks, but the sticks were too short. They needed a longer stick. "Look, Tom, there is a long stick over there!" Lily said, pointing to a stick that was lying on the edge of the cliff. Tom ran to the stick and picked it up. He was happy. He thought he could get the ball with the long stick. He walked to the ball and stretched the stick towards it. But he did not see that the ground under the stick was loose. The stick pushed the ground and the ground moved. Tom lost his balance and fell towards the cliff. He screamed. Lily saw Tom falling and screamed too. She dropped her sticks and ran to Tom. She grabbed his hand and pulled him back. She was very strong. She saved Tom from falling. Tom and Lily hugged each other. They were scared and relieved. They did not care about the ball anymore. They just wanted to go home. They left the sticks and the ball near the cliff and ran to their mom. Their mom was sitting on a bench and reading a book. She saw them running and smiled. "Did you have fun, kids?" she asked. "Yes, mom, but we also had a big scare," Tom said. "We almost lost Tom to the cliff, mom," Lily said. They told their mom what happened. Their mom hugged them and said, "I'm so glad you're safe, kids. You were very brave and smart. But next time, be careful and stay away from the cliff, okay?" "Okay, mom," Tom and Lily said. They learned their lesson. They never played near the cliff again. They found a new place to play with their ball. A place that was safe and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to read. One day, he found a big book with unknown words. Tim wanted to know what those words meant. Tim went to his friend, Sam, and said, "Sam, can you help me read this book? It has unknown words." Sam looked at the book and said, "I can't read those words either. Let's go ask our teacher." On the way to their teacher, Tim and Sam saw a tap. The tap was leaking water. They tried to turn it off, but they couldn't. They decided to tell their teacher about the tap too. When they reached their teacher, they showed her the book with unknown words and told her about the tap. The teacher smiled and said, "I will help you read the book and fix the tap." Tim and Sam were happy. They learned new words from the book and fixed the tap together with their teacher. From that day on, they loved to read even more and help each other with unknown things.

Once upon a time, there was a little house. The house was very proud. It had a red door and a green roof. The house loved to show off its colors. One day, a big wind came. A bird flew by the house. The bird was scared of the wind. The bird said, "Can I stay in your house?" The house said, "Yes, you can stay." The bird was happy. The wind grew stronger. The house shook. The bird was scared, but the house was still proud. The wind stopped. The bird said, "Thank you, house." The house was happy to help.
