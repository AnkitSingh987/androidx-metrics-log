Category: Beginner
Title: Luna the Lovely Cat and the Bird

One day, a lovely cat named Luna went for a walk. She saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his bag of oats. Luna wanted to help and care for Tim, so she said, "Do not be sad, I will help you find your oats." Luna and Tim walked and looked for the bag. They saw a big tree and asked, "Tree, do you know where the oats are?" The tree said, "Yes, I saw a bird take the bag to a big hill." Luna and Tim went to the big hill and found the bird. The bird said, "I am sorry, I took the oats. I did not know they were yours." The bird gave the bag back to Tim. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Luna and bird, for caring and helping me find my oats." They all became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. Spot could do many tricks that made people say "Wow!" One day, Spot saw a big ball in the park. He wanted to own the ball, so he went to the park to get it. Spot saw a little boy with the ball. He said, "Can I have your ball? I will do a trick for you." The little boy said, "Okay, show me your trick." Spot did a flip and the little boy laughed. He gave the ball to Spot. But then, a big dog came and took the ball away. Spot tried to get the ball back, but the big dog was too strong. Spot was sad because he lost the ball. The little boy was sad too, because he gave away his ball for nothing. The big dog was happy because he got a new ball, but Spot and the little boy were not clever enough to keep the ball.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a large tree. Under the tree, there was a red basket. In the basket, there were many yummy fruits. A little boy named Tim saw the basket and wanted to eat the fruits. Tim ran to the basket and said, "Wow, look at all these yummy fruits! I want to eat them all!" He picked up the basket and started to pour the fruits on a big blue mat. He was very happy and excited. His friend, Sue, came running and saw the fruits too. She said, "Can I eat some fruits with you, Tim?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's eat the fruits together!" Tim and Sue sat on the mat and ate the yummy fruits from the large basket. They laughed and talked while they enjoyed their tasty treat.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They were playing in Jill's yard. Jack saw something very smooth in the grass. He said to Jill, "Look! Here it is!" Jill smiled and said, "Let’s pick it up!" They both reached down and their hands touched the smooth object. Suddenly, it started to jump! It was a frog. Jack was scared and he stepped back and fell. Jill gasped and said, "Are you all right?" But Jack smiled and said, "Yes, I'm fine!" Jack and Jill looked at the frog in the grass and Jack said, "We should leave it alone. That’s what it wants to be." Jill thought for a moment and then smiled. She said, "Jack is right. We should leave it alone. That's very kind." She took Jack's hand and together they walked away from the frog. Jack and Jill had learned an important lesson that day. They learned that it is important to respect other creatures and their homes, even in a small yard.

One day, a little bug named Tim was walking on a dull root. He saw his friend, Sam the snail, who was very slow. Tim said, "Hi Sam! Want to play?" Sam said, "Yes, but I am slow. Can we play a game where I can win too?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's play hide and seek. I will hide, and you look for me!" Tim found a good spot to hide, and Sam started to look for him. While Tim was hiding, he saw a big red car. The car went zoom, zoom, zoom! It was so fast! Tim had an idea. He got on the car and went zoom, zoom, zoom too! Sam looked and looked for Tim, but he could not find him. Then, he heard a loud zoom, zoom, zoom! He saw Tim on the big red car. Sam was surprised and said, "Wow, Tim! You are so fast now!" Tim laughed and said, "Let's play another game. This time, we can both go zoom, zoom, zoom!"
