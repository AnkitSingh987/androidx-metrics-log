Category: Beginner
Title: The Pit and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big pit. It was a regular pit, with dirt and rocks. Many people walked by the pit every day. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the pit and had an idea. Tim went home and got his toys. He came back to the pit and started to put his toys in it. He put his cars, his trucks, and his balls in the pit. Tim was very happy with his new play area. One day, a little girl named Sue walked by the pit. She saw Tim playing with his toys. Sue asked, "Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes, you can play with me!" So, Sue and Tim played together in the pit. They had lots of fun, and the pit became their favorite place to play.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play in the park. He had a toy that he liked a lot. It was a big red switch. Spot would rush to the switch and push it with his paw. When he pushed the switch, it made a fun noise. One day, Spot's friend, a little girl named Amy, came to play with him. Amy saw the red switch and wanted to push it too. Spot was happy to share his toy with his friend. They took turns pushing the switch and making the fun noise. As they played, the sky got dark and it started to rain. Spot and Amy knew they needed to go home. They rushed to pick up the red switch and ran back to Amy's house. They were happy they had played with the switch before the rain came. Spot and Amy had lots of fun that day and they knew they would play with the big red switch again soon.

Ben and Sam like to play with jets. They have many jets of different colors and sizes. They pretend to fly them in the air and make zooming sounds. One day, they decide to make a big jet out of cardboard and paper. They cut and glue and paint and stick. They work hard and have fun. They want to complete their jet before lunch. "Look, our jet is almost done!" Ben says. "We just need to add the wings and the tail." "Wow, it looks great!" Sam says. "Can I help you with the wings?" "Yes, please. Here, take this one and glue it here." Ben says. They are busy with their jet when they hear a loud noise. They turn around and see Max, the bad boy from next door. Max has a big stick in his hand. He is smiling in a mean way. "Hello, Ben and Sam. What are you doing?" Max says. "We are making a jet. Go away, Max. You are not nice." Sam says. "I don't care. I don't like your jet. It looks silly. I'm going to break it." Max says. He swings his stick and hits the jet. The jet falls apart. The cardboard and paper tear and crumple. Ben and Sam are sad and angry. They cry and shout. "Max, you are bad! You ruined our jet! We worked hard on it! Go away, Max!" Ben and Sam say. Max laughs and runs away. He doesn't care. He likes to be bad. Ben and Sam hug each other. They are sad, but they are also brave. They decide to make another jet. They pick up the pieces and start again. They complete their jet before lunch. They are happy and proud. They fly their jet in the air and make zooming sounds. They don't let Max spoil their fun. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch his favorite cartoon on TV. He would sit on the floor and laugh at the funny things the characters did. One day, Tim was very hungry. He wanted to eat something while watching his cartoon. Tim went to the kitchen and saw a big box of cereal. He thought, "Yummy! I can eat this while I watch my cartoon." So, Tim got a bowl and poured the cereal into it. He was so excited to eat and watch his favorite show. As Tim was pouring the cereal, his mom walked into the kitchen. She saw Tim and asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim smiled and said, "I'm hungry, Mom! I want to eat cereal and watch my cartoon." His mom laughed and helped him pour some milk into the bowl. Then, Tim and his mom went to watch the cartoon together, eating the yummy cereal.
