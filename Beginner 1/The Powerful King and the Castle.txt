Category: Beginner
Title: The Powerful King and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a powerful king. He lived in a big castle with a beautiful fountain. The king wanted to test his people to see who was the most kind. One day, the king dressed like a poor man. He went to the fountain and asked people for help. Many people walked by and did not help the king. They were too busy or did not care. Finally, a little girl came to the fountain. She saw the king and felt bad for him. She gave him some food and water. The king was very happy. He took off his poor man clothes and showed he was the powerful king. The little girl was surprised. The king said, "You passed my test. You are the kindest person in my kingdom." The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to look for stones of different colors and shapes. Lily found a shiny stone that was red and yellow. She showed it to Tom. "Look, Tom, I found a pretty stone!" Lily said. "Wow, that is a pretty stone!" Tom said. "Can I see it?" Lily gave the stone to Tom. He looked at it closely. He saw something on the stone. It was a picture of a bird. "Look, Lily, there is a bird on the stone!" Tom said. Lily looked at the stone again. She saw the bird too. It had a long tail and a big beak. It looked like a parrot. "Wow, that is a rare stone!" Lily said. "Where did you find it?" Lily and Tom went back to where Lily found the stone. They saw a big hole in the ground. They realized that the stone came from the hole. "Maybe there are more stones like this in the hole!" Tom said. "Let's dig and see!" Lily said. They took their shovels and started to dig. They hoped to find more rare stones with pictures on them. They were very happy and curious.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big box in the park. The box has many things inside. There are hats and shoes and bags and dolls and cars and balls. "Wow, look at all these things!" Lily says. "We are rich!" Ben nods. He picks up a hat and puts it on his head. He looks funny. Lily laughs. She takes a bag and opens it. She finds a book inside. The book has many pictures and words. "Can you read this book?" Ben asks. Lily shakes her head. She can read some words, but not all. She likes the pictures. She sees a picture of a cat and a dog. They are friends. "I like this picture," Lily says. "Do you like cats or dogs?" Ben thinks. He likes both. He remembers his grandma's cat and his uncle's dog. They are nice and soft and fun. "I like both," Ben says. "But I like cats more. They are cute and quiet." Lily nods. She likes cats more too. She remembers her cat, Mimi. Mimi is black and white and likes to sleep on her bed. "I have a cat," Lily says. "Her name is Mimi. She is my best friend." Ben smiles. He likes Lily. She is his best friend too. "Can I see your cat?" Ben asks. Lily nods. She closes the book and puts it back in the bag. She takes Ben's hand and they walk to her house. They are happy. They have many things to play with and many memories to share. They are rich.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very good at keeping things clean. One day, Sue saw a sign near her house. The sign had a big word on it, but Sue didn't know what it meant. She asked her mom, "Mom, can you please bring me the sign?" Her mom said, "Okay, but you must promise to keep it clean." Sue nodded and said, "I promise, mom." Sue played with the sign all day. She kept it very clean. But then, a big wind came and took the sign away. Sue was very sad. She looked and looked, but she couldn't find the sign. The next day, Sue saw the sign again. It was high up in a tree. Sue tried to get the sign, but she fell and hurt her leg. It was a bad ending for Sue. She learned that sometimes, even when you try your best, things don't go the way you want.
