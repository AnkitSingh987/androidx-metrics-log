Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Cat

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing together. They were very good friends. The dog had a thick, soft coat that kept him warm. The cat liked to cuddle with the dog when they were tired. After playing for a long time, the dog let out a big yawn. "I am so tired," said the dog. The cat yawned too. "Me too," said the cat. They decided to take a nap under a big tree. As a result of their fun day, the dog and the cat slept very well. When they woke up, they were happy and ready to play again. They knew they would always be the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to a big store with her mom. The store was full of people and many things. Lily felt restless. She wanted to play, but she had to stay with her mom. In the store, they met a nice lady who worked there. She had a staff badge on her shirt. The lady said, "Welcome to our store! Can I help you find something?" Lily's mom asked for help, and the lady showed them where to find what they needed. But Lily was still restless. She wanted to play with her new toy, but her mom said, "Not yet, we need to pay first." Lily got upset and started to cry. The nice lady came back and gave Lily a small toy to play with while they waited in line. Lily felt better and said "Thank you!" to the nice lady. They paid for their things and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom was sad because he wanted to marry, but no one liked his bald head. One day, Tom met a kind lady named Sue. Sue liked Tom and did not care about his bald head. Tom and Sue were walking near a big wall. They heard a small voice say, "Help me, please!" They saw a little bird stuck in a hole in the wall. Tom and Sue worked together to help the bird get out. The bird said, "Thank you! I will help you too one day." Tom and Sue got married. They were very happy. But one day, a mean man laughed at Tom's bald head. Tom was sad again. Then, the little bird they helped came back. The bird dropped a beautiful hat from the sky. Tom put on the hat and felt better. The mean man stopped laughing. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and they will be kind to you.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly bug named Bob. Bob had a long cord for a tail. He lived in a big tree with his friends. They liked to play and have fun. One day, Bob and his friends decided to march to the park. They lined up and started to walk. "Let's go!" said Bob. They marched, and their feet went up and down. They were so happy. When they got to the park, they saw a big puddle. Bob thought it was fun to splash in the puddle. "Watch me!" he said. His long cord swung around as he jumped. All the friends laughed and joined him. They had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run very fast. He had a lot of speed. One day, Tim was running in the park. He was being careless and not looking where he was going. Tim bumped into a girl named Sue. Sue was sad. "Why are you sad?" Tim asked. Sue said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Tim wanted to help Sue, so they looked for her toy together. As they looked around, they discovered the toy under a big tree. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim learned to be more careful when running. From that day on, Tim and Sue became good friends and played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Mandy and Sam. Every day, they went walking in the park. One day, when they were playing, Mandy wanted to sack the leaves on the ground. "Let's sack the leaves!" she cried. But Sam said, "We can't sack the leaves yet! We need something flexible to do it!" So Mandy went off and found a flexible stick. They both grabbed it and started sacking the leaves. Mandy and Sam had lots of fun while they sacked the leaves. When they had finished, they sat down to rest. Mandy said to Sam, "That was fun! We can come back and sack the leaves again tomorrow." And they did! From then on, Mandy and Sam always had lots of fun when they went to sack the leaves in the park.
