Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a small, weak candle. She loved her candle very much. Every night, she would put her candle on her window and watch the light dance. One day, her friend Tom came to play. Tom saw the weak candle and asked, "Mia, why do you put that candle on your window?" Mia smiled and said, "I put the candle there because it makes me happy. Even if it's weak, it still shines bright in the dark." Tom and Mia played all day. When it was time to go home, Tom said, "I like your candle too. It shows that even small things can make us happy." From that day on, Mia and Tom would put their candles on their windows every night. They would watch the light dance together and remember that even small things can bring joy.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big hill. They wanted to climb it. "Let's go up there!" Tom said. "It looks fun!" Lily was not sure. She saw some rocks and thorns on the hill. She thought it was dangerous. "I don't know, Tom. It looks scary. Maybe we should stay here." But Tom did not listen. He ran to the hill. He started to climb. He looked back at Lily and smiled. "Come on, Lily! Don't be a baby!" Lily felt sad. She did not want Tom to think she was a baby. She wanted to be his friend. She followed him to the hill. She tried to climb. But the hill was too steep and slippery. Lily lost her balance. She fell down. She hurt her knee and her elbow. She cried. Tom heard her cry. He looked down. He saw Lily on the ground. He felt bad. He wanted to help her. He tried to go down. But he was too high and fast. He could not stop. He tripped on a rock. He rolled down. He hit his head on a thorn. He bled. He cried. Lily and Tom were both hurt and scared. They wished they had not climbed the hill. They regretted their step. They learned a lesson. The hill was dangerous.

One day, a deer was in the wide woods. The deer saw a zip bag on the ground. The deer was very curious about the zip bag. It walked up to the bag and looked inside. There was some food in the bag! The deer thought, "Wow! I found a yummy snack!" The deer ate the food from the zip bag. Then, a little boy came into the woods. He saw the deer and the zip bag. The boy said, "Hey, deer! That's my zip bag and my food!" The deer looked at the boy and felt bad. It didn't know the food belonged to someone else. The boy and the deer became friends. They played in the wide woods together. The deer learned to ask before taking things. The boy learned that sharing is good. They both had fun and were happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty boy named Tim. He liked to make a big mess in his room. One day, his mom said, "Tim, you need to clean your room before you watch TV." Tim did not want to clean his room, but he wanted to watch his favorite show on TV. So, he started to clean. As he picked up his toys, he found a big red ball that he had lost a long time ago. Tim was so happy to find it! Just then, Tim's dog, Spot, came into the room. Spot saw the big red ball and wanted to play. He jumped on the ball and it started to bounce all over the room. The ball hit the TV and turned it on! Tim and Spot were so surprised. They stopped cleaning and watched the TV together. And from that day on, Tim learned to keep his room clean so he could always find his toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Mae. Mae had a hat that was very special to her. She played with it every day and would never take it off. One day, Mae decided to take her hat outside and have a picnic. She laid it on the grass and began to eat her food. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise. It was raining! Mae picked up her hat and quickly ran home. When she got inside, she noticed that her hat was filthy. She asked her mum, "Why is my hat so filthy?" Mae's mum said, "Your hat has been outside and it has been raining. That is why it is filthy. If you don't wash it, it will rot". So, Mae took out a bucket of water and some soap and began to wash her hat. She scrubbed it hard and got rid of all the dirt. When she was finished, her hat looked brand new. Mae was so happy. She would never let her hat get filthy again!
