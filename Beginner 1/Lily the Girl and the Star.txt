Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Star

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Lily. She loved to wear her red dress with white dots. One day, she saw a spark in the sky. It was a little star, shining very bright. Lily smiled and said, "Hello, little star!" The little star answered, "Hello, Lily! I like your pretty dress. Can I come down and play with you?" Lily was very happy and said, "Yes, please, little star! I would love to play with you." The little star came down, and they played together all day. They danced, they laughed, and they had a lot of fun. When the sun went down, the little star had to go back to the sky. Lily said, "Goodbye, little star! I hope we can play again soon." The little star twinkled and said, "Goodbye, Lily! I will always remember our fun day together."

One day, a boy named Tim found a big bag. Tim was very happy. He thought the bag was full of rich things. He wanted to tease his friend, Sam, with the bag. Tim ran to Sam's house. He said, "Look what I found! A big bag of rich things!" Sam was very curious. He wanted to see what was inside the bag. They opened the bag together. But, surprise! It was not full of rich things. It was full of funny hats. Tim and Sam laughed and laughed. They both put on the funny hats and played all day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a big lion. The lion liked to roar very loud. All the animals could hear him. They were scared of the big lion. But the lion was not mean, he just liked to roar. One day, the big lion found some ice. He had never seen ice before. He touched the ice with his paw. It was very cold! The big lion thought the ice was fun. He played with the ice all day long. In the forest, there was also an obedient dog. The dog always did what he was told. The dog saw the lion playing with the ice. He wanted to play too. The dog went to the lion and asked if he could play. The lion said yes. They played with the ice together. The big lion and the obedient dog became good friends. From that day on, the lion did not roar so loud, and the animals were not scared anymore.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball, a kite and a bike. They have fun. But then, something bad happens. A big dog comes and takes their ball. It runs away with the ball in its mouth. Tom and Anna are sad. They want their ball back. "Hey, give us our ball!" Tom shouts at the dog. But the dog does not listen. It keeps running. "Let's chase the dog!" Anna says. She gets on her bike and pedals fast. Tom follows her on his kite. They chase the dog for a long time. They go past the pond, the bridge and the hill. But they cannot catch the dog. The dog is too fast. They are very tired and hungry. They look at the sun. It is high in the sky. It is time for lunch. "Let's go home and have lunch," Tom says. "Maybe we can find our ball later." "OK," Anna says. "But I am still sad. I liked our ball." They go home and have lunch. They have sandwiches, apples and milk. They are still sad, but they feel a little better. They hope the dog will bring their ball back. They hope they can play with their ball again. They hope they can have fun in the park again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a long vine. A small bird lived near the vine. The bird was very lucky. Every day, the bird would play and sing on the vine. One day, the bird heard a big sound. The bird was scared. The bird went to see what made the sound. The bird saw a big tree fall near the vine. The vine was safe. The bird was sad. The bird thought the vine was gone. But then, the bird saw the vine was still there! The bird was so happy! The bird played and sang on the vine again. The vine and the bird were very lucky.
