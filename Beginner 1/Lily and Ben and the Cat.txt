Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Cat

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Lily liked to be a cat and Ben liked to be a dog. "Meow, meow, I'm a cat and I'm going to catch you, woof, woof!" Lily said, chasing Ben around the couch. "Arf, arf, I'm a dog and I'm going to run away from you, meow, meow!" Ben said, dodging Lily's paws. They laughed and giggled as they ran and jumped. But then, Lily felt a tickle in her nose. She tried to ignore it, but it got worse and worse. She stopped and rubbed her nose with her hand. "Are you okay, Lily?" Ben asked, noticing that she was not playing anymore. "I'm fine, Ben. I just have a tickle in my nose. Maybe I'm allergic to something," Lily said, sniffing. "Maybe you're allergic to dogs," Ben joked, wagging his tail. "Maybe I'm allergic to cats," Lily joked back, flicking her ears. They smiled at each other and were about to resume their game, when Lily felt a big sneeze coming. She couldn't hold it back. She sneezed so hard that she fell backwards on the floor. "Achoo!" Lily sneezed, making a loud noise. "Wow, that was a big sneeze, Lily!" Ben said, surprised. "Are you okay, Lily?" Ben asked, running to her side. "I'm okay, Ben. That was a weird sneeze. It made my heart beat fast," Lily said, feeling her chest. "Me too, Lily. Your sneeze made my heart beat fast too," Ben said, feeling his chest. They looked at each other and felt something warm and fuzzy in their hearts. They didn't know what it was, but they liked it. They leaned closer and gave each other a hug. "Maybe we're not allergic to each other, Lily. Maybe we're friends," Ben said, smiling. "Maybe we're more than friends, Ben. Maybe we're best friends," Lily said, smiling. They hugged each other tighter and felt their hearts beat in sync. They were happy and they knew it. They didn't need to say anything else. They just felt it.

Lily and Ben are playing in their room. They have many toys, but they are bored. It is very hot today, and they are sweaty. "Can we go outside?" Lily asks Ben. "No, Mom said it is too hot. We have to stay inside," Ben says. They look around the room. They see a fan on the shelf. It is big and white. It has four blades that spin. "Let's play with the fan!" Lily says. She runs to the shelf and takes the fan. She plugs it in the wall. Ben follows her. He watches the fan. It makes a noise and blows air. "Wow, it is cold!" Ben says. He feels the air on his face. He likes it. Lily smiles. She holds the fan in front of her. She pretends it is a microphone. She sings a song. "La la la, I am a star, la la la, I have a fan, la la la, it is so cold, la la la, I am so cool!" Ben laughs. He claps his hands. He joins her song. "La la la, you are a star, la la la, I have a fan, la la la, it is so cold, la la la, we are so cool!" They sing and play with the fan. They forget they are bored. They have fun. Mom hears them and smiles. She is happy they are happy.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and balls. One day, Tom has a toothache. He cries and holds his mouth. His mom says he has to go to the dentist. Tom is fearful. He does not want to go. He thinks the dentist will hurt him. Sam hears Tom crying. He comes to see him. He says, "What is wrong, Tom? Why are you sad?" Tom tells him about the dentist. Sam says, "I went to the dentist last week. He was very nice. He did not hurt me. He just looked at my teeth and gave me a sticker. You should not be fearful, Tom. The dentist will help you." Tom is not sure. He says, "But what if he has a big needle or a loud drill? What if he makes me open my mouth very wide?" Sam says, "He will not do that. He will tell you what he is doing and ask you if you are okay. He will be gentle and patient. You can hold my hand if you want. I will go with you." Tom feels a little better. He says, "Thank you, Sam. You are a good friend. Will you really go with me?" Sam says, "Yes, I will. Come on, let's go. The sooner you go, the sooner you will feel better." Tom nods. He takes Sam's hand and goes with his mom to the dentist. He is still fearful, but he is brave. He trusts Sam and the dentist. He finds out that the dentist is not scary at all. He fixes Tom's tooth and gives him a sticker. Tom smiles and says, "Thank you, dentist. Thank you, Sam. You were right. It was not so bad." The moral of the story is: Don't be afraid of the dentist. He is there to help you. Trust your friends and listen to them. They can support you and make you feel better.
