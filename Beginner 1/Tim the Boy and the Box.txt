Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Box

One day, a little boy named Tim was restless. He could not sit still. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, I am bored. What can I do?" His mom told him, "Tim, you can go outside and play. But first, let us pray for a fun and safe day." They prayed together and Tim felt happy. Then, he went outside to play. While playing, Tim saw a big box on the left side of his yard. He did not know what was inside. He opened the box and found a small, lost puppy! Tim was so surprised and happy. He knew that his prayer had been answered.

---

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big fountain with water and coins. Lily says, "Look, Ben, shiny coins! Can we get them?" Ben says, "No, Lily, we can't. They are not ours. People put them there for a wish. We have to leave them alone." Lily says, "But I want to make a wish, too. I wish for a new doll. Can I throw a coin in the water?" Ben says, "OK, Lily, but only one. You have to be careful. The water is deep and cold. Don't fall in." Lily takes a coin from her pocket. She runs to the edge of the fountain. She jumps and throws the coin in the air. She says, "I wish, I wish, I wish for a new doll!" But Lily jumps too far. She slips and falls in the water. Splash! She is wet and scared. She cries, "Help, help, help! Ben, help me!" Ben runs to the fountain. He sees Lily in the water. He reaches out his hand. He says, "Lily, grab my hand! I'll pull you out!" Lily grabs Ben's hand. Ben pulls her out of the water. He hugs her and says, "Lily, are you OK? You are so silly. Why did you jump so far?" Lily says, "I'm sorry, Ben. I wanted to make a big wish. But I'm OK. I'm just wet and cold. Thank you for saving me." Ben says, "You're welcome, Lily. Come on, let's go home. We can dry off and warm up. Maybe your wish will come true anyway. You are a good friend." Lily says, "You are a good friend, too, Ben. I'm glad you are here. Maybe we can play with my new doll tomorrow." They smile and hold hands. They walk home together. They are fine.

---

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. They saw a big tree with red fruits. They wanted to try them. "Look, Lily, red fruits! Maybe they are sweet and juicy. Let's pick some and eat them," Ben said. "OK, Ben, but be careful. The tree is very high and the fruits are hard to reach. Don't fall and hurt yourself," Lily said. Ben climbed the tree and picked some fruits. He threw one to Lily and kept one for himself. They bit into the fruits and made a face. "Yuck! These fruits are sour and bitter. They make my mouth hurt. They are not good to eat," Lily said. "Me too. These fruits are bad. They make my tummy ache. I don't like them," Ben said. They threw away the fruits and climbed down the tree. But Ben slipped and fell. He landed on the ground and cried. "Ow! Ow! Ow! My leg is broken. It hurts so much. Help me, Lily, help me," Ben said. Lily ran to Ben and hugged him. She tried to comfort him. "I'm sorry, Ben, I'm sorry. You have pain. I will get help. Don't cry, don't cry," Lily said. She looked around and saw a man walking his dog. She waved and shouted. "Help, help! My friend is hurt. He needs a doctor. Please help us," Lily said. The man heard Lily and came closer. He saw Ben lying on the ground with a twisted leg. He frowned and shook his head. "I'm sorry, little girl, but I can't help you. I have to go. My dog is sick and I have to take him to the vet. You have to accept your pain and deal with it yourself. Goodbye," the man said. He walked away with his dog and left Ben and Lily alone. They felt scared and sad. They wished they never ate the sour fruits. They wished they never climbed the tree. They wished someone would help them. But no one came. They cried and cried. The end.
