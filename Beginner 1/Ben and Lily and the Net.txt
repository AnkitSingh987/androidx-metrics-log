Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Net

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big net on the grass. They wanted to catch some bugs with it. "Look, a net!" Ben said. "Let's catch some bugs!" "Okay!" Lily said. "But we have to be careful. The net is not ours. Maybe someone left it here." They ran to the net and picked it up. They saw a lot of bugs flying and crawling around. They tried to catch them with the net. "Got one!" Ben said. He caught a butterfly in the net. He was happy. "Me too!" Lily said. She caught a ladybug in the net. She was happy too. They put the bugs in a jar. They wanted to keep them as pets. But then they heard a voice. It was a boy. He was looking for his net. "Hey, that's my net!" the boy said. "You took my net!" Ben and Lily felt scared. They did not know what to do. "Sorry, we did not know it was yours," Ben said. "We just wanted to catch some bugs." "Well, you can't do that," the boy said. "The net is mine. I left it here for a minute. You have to give it back." Ben and Lily looked at each other. They liked the net and the bugs. But they knew it was wrong to take something that was not theirs. They decided to be honest. They gave the net and the jar back to the boy. "Here, take your net and your bugs," Lily said. "We are sorry we took them." The boy took the net and the jar. He looked at Ben and Lily. He saw that they were sorry. He decided to be kind. "Thank you for being honest," the boy said. "You can keep one bug each. I have enough. Do you want to be my friends?" Ben and Lily smiled. They accepted the boy's offer. They each chose a bug to keep. They thanked the boy. They became friends. They played with the net and the bugs together. They learned to share and to be honest. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch the big clock on his wall. He would sit and watch the clock for hours. He liked to see the hands move and hear the tick-tock sound. One day, Tim was very impatient. He wanted the clock to move faster. He said to the clock, "Clock, please move faster! I want to go play outside with my friends!" But the clock did not listen. It kept moving at the same speed. Tim felt sad. Tim's mom saw him looking at the clock. She said, "Tim, don't worry about the clock. Time will pass, and soon you can go play outside." Tim listened to his mom and decided to be patient. He played with his toys and read a book. Soon, it was time to go outside and play with his friends. Tim was happy and learned that being patient was a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom liked to eat fruit every day. He had a big fruit bowl in his house, filled with apples, bananas, and oranges. One day, Tom ate all the fruit in his bowl. He was sad because he had no more fruit to eat. Tom knew he had to replace the fruit in his bowl. Tom walked to the store and bought more fruit. He replaced the empty bowl with new apples, bananas, and oranges. Now, Tom was happy because he had more fruit to eat. And that is the end of the story about the bald man and his fruit bowl.

Once upon a time, there was a popular book. Many kids liked to read it. One day, a little boy named Tim found the book. He was very happy. Tim loved to read books. Tim started to read the popular book. He saw a question in the book. The question was: "What is your favorite color?" Tim thought and said, "My favorite color is blue!" Then, something unexpected happened. The book started to talk! It said, "Thank you for answering my question, Tim!" Tim was surprised. He did not know that books could talk. From that day on, Tim and the book became good friends. They read and talked together every day.
