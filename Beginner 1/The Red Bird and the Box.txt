Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Bird and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a red bird. The red bird had a nest. In the nest, the bird had a box. The box had jewelry. The red bird loved the jewelry. One day, a big wind came. The wind was strong. The wind took the box out of the nest. The red bird was sad. The bird wanted to find the box. The bird looked and looked but could not find it. A nice dog saw the red bird. The dog wanted to help. The dog tried to provide help. The dog looked for the box too. But they could not find the box. The red bird and the dog were sad. The jewelry was gone.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go outside and play. The weather was not nice. It was rainy and cold. Tim was sad because he wanted to play in the sun. He thought of a plan to improve the weather. Tim went to his naughty friend, Sam. Sam always had ideas to do things they were not supposed to do. Tim asked Sam, "How can we make the weather better?" Sam smiled and said, "We can do a dance and maybe the sun will come out." They started to dance and sing loudly. Tim and Sam were making a lot of noise. They were being very naughty. They hoped their dance would make the sun come out. But the weather did not improve. In fact, it started to rain harder. Tim's mom heard them and came outside. She was not happy. "You boys are being too loud and naughty," she said. "Now you both have to stay inside and clean your rooms." Tim and Sam were sad. They had to stay inside on a bad weather day and clean instead of having fun.

Tom and Lily are playing in the garden. They find a big box with tools inside. Tom likes the hammer. He wants to lift it and hit things with it. "Look, Lily, I have a hammer!" Tom says. He tries to lift the hammer, but it is too heavy for him. He falls down and drops the hammer. "Ouch!" Tom cries. "The hammer is too big for me!" Lily sees Tom and runs to him. She is worried. She picks up the hammer and gives it to Tom. "Are you okay, Tom?" Lily asks. "The hammer is very long and heavy. You need to be careful with it." Tom nods. He rubs his head. He is not hurt, but he is sad. He wants to play with the hammer, but he can't lift it. Lily has an idea. She sees a small stick on the ground. She gives it to Tom. "Here, Tom, you can use this stick as a hammer. It is not long or heavy. You can lift it and hit things with it. It is fun!" Lily says. Tom smiles. He takes the stick and lifts it. He hits the box with it. It makes a loud noise. "Bang, bang, bang!" Tom says. He is happy. He likes the stick. "Good job, Tom!" Lily says. She claps her hands. She finds another stick and joins Tom. They play with the sticks and the box. They have fun. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little dog named Spot. Spot had a favorite seat in the house. It was a big, soft pillow by the window. Spot loved to sit there and watch the birds in the trees. One sunny day, Spot's friend, a little girl named Lily, came over to play. They played with toys and had lots of fun. Then, Lily saw Spot's seat by the window. "What a nice seat!" she said. "Can I sit there too, Spot?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, Lily! Let's sit together!" So, they both sat on the soft pillow by the window. Spot put his head on Lily's lap, and she gently patted him. They looked at the birds and the trees, and they felt very happy. As they sat there, a butterfly flew by the window. Spot and Lily both looked at it, their eyes wide with wonder. The butterfly was very pretty and danced in the air. Spot and Lily smiled and laughed together. After a while, it was time for Lily to go home. She gave Spot a big hug and said, "Thank you for sharing your seat with me, Spot. I had so much fun today!" Spot barked happily and wagged his tail. They waved goodbye, and Lily went home. Spot sat on his seat by the window, feeling happy and loved.
