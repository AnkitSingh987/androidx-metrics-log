Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Sock

Tim and Sam are brothers. They live in a big house with Mom and Dad. Tim is five and Sam is three. They like to play with their toys, but sometimes they fight. One day, Tim finds a smelly sock under his bed. He thinks it is funny. He runs to Sam's room with the sock. He says, "Sam, smell this! It is your sock! You are smelly!" Sam is angry. He does not like to be teased. He says, "No, it is not my sock! It is your sock! You are smelly!" He tries to grab the sock, but Tim is faster. He runs away with the sock. Sam chases Tim around the house. They run up and down the stairs, in and out of the rooms, and knock over some things. Mom and Dad hear the noise. They are not happy. They go to find Tim and Sam. They see them in the kitchen. Tim is holding the sock over Sam's head. Sam is jumping and crying. Mom and Dad say, "Stop! What are you doing?" Tim and Sam stop. They look at Mom and Dad. They feel sorry. They say, "Sorry, Mom. Sorry, Dad." Mom and Dad say, "Why are you teasing Sam with the sock, Tim? That is not nice." Tim says, "I was just playing. I did not mean to hurt Sam." Sam says, "He said I was smelly. That is not true." Mom and Dad say, "You are both not smelly. You are both clean and sweet. But the sock is smelly. It needs to go in the laundry. And you need to say sorry to each other and hug." Tim and Sam say sorry to each other and hug. They give the sock to Mom. They say, "We love you, Mom. We love you, Dad." Mom and Dad say, "We love you, too. Now, let's clean up the mess and play nicely."

One day, a busy bee named Ben found a broken bucket. Ben wanted to help repair the bucket. He flew to his friend, the ant named Amy. "Amy, can you help me fix the bucket?" Ben asked. "Yes, I can help," said Amy. They worked together to fix the bucket. They were happy when the bucket was fixed. They put the bucket near a big tree. Later, a little mouse named Max saw the bucket. He climbed inside, but the bucket was too deep, and he could not get out. "Help, help!" Max cried. Ben and Amy heard him and came to help. "We will save you, Max!" said Ben and Amy. They made a rope with grass and pulled Max out of the bucket. Max was happy and thanked Ben and Amy. They all became good friends and played together every day. The bucket was not just fixed, but it also brought new friends together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived on a farm with his mom, dad, and his pet horse, Max. They had a big red stable where Max slept at night. Tim loved to play with Max in the deep, green grass near the stable. One day, Tim saw that Max did not seem happy. Max did not want to play in the deep grass. Tim was sad and wanted to help Max feel better. He asked his mom and dad what was wrong with Max. They said Max was hungry and needed some food. Tim knew he had to help Max. He went to the barn and found some hay for Max to eat. He carried the hay to the stable and gave it to Max. Max ate the hay and started to feel better. Soon, Max was happy again and ready to play with Tim in the deep grass. Tim was so happy that he could help his friend Max. They played together all day long, and Tim knew that he would always take care of Max.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park with her mom. They took a big bowl of salad to eat. In the park, Sally saw a small door in a tree. She thought it was unusual. Sally said to her mom, "Look, Mom! A door in the tree!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's special, Sally! Why don't you peek inside?" Sally opened the door and peeked inside. She saw a tiny room with a table and chairs. On the table was a little salad, just like the one she had brought. Sally was very happy to find the little room. She and her mom shared their salad with the tiny friends who lived in the tree. They had a fun day at the park.
