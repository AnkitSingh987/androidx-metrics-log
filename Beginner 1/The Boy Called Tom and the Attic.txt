Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy Called Tom and the Attic

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tom. He liked to play in his attic all day long. But one day he noticed that it was very hot in his attic. He started to feel very hot and he started to feel very sad. Tom went to his mom to ask for help. He said, "Mom, the attic is too hot. Please help me." His mom said that she would help him and told him that she would put a fan in the attic. Tom was very happy and he smiled really big. But then he remembered that he didn't have enough money to buy a fan. He started to feel very sad again and then he let out a big sigh. Tom's mom said, "Don't worry, I will find a way to get you a fan." Then she went outside and asked the neighbors if they had an old fan. The neighbors were kind and they gave Tom an old fan. Now Tom's attic was much cooler and he was very happy. He was glad that his mom had helped him and the neighbors were kind enough to give him the fan. From then on, he was able to play in the attic without feeling too hot.

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. He loved to run and zoom around the park. One day, Max saw a small cat stuck in a tall tree. Max wanted to help the cat. He thought hard and had an idea. He would offer his help to the cat. Max barked and said, "Jump on my back, I will catch you." The small cat was scared, but Max's strong back made it feel better. The cat jumped and Max caught it. They were both happy and became friends. And from that day, they played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a dull toy. He did not like the toy. One day, Tim found a long string. He had an idea. Tim tied the string to his toy. He showed his mom. "Look, Mom! I made a new toy!" His mom smiled. "Be careful, Tim," she said. Tim did not know why she said that. He played with his new toy all day. The next day, Tim took his toy outside. He swung it around and around. Suddenly, the string broke! The toy flew far away. Tim felt regret. He wished he had listened to his mom. He learned that sometimes, it is good to be careful.

Once upon a time, there was a small fish named Fin. He had a friend named Sam. Sam was not a regular fish, he was a shark. But Fin and Sam liked to play together in the sea. One day, Fin asked Sam to explain why he was so big. Sam said, "I am a shark, so I am big and strong. But I am also nice and I like to play with you." Fin was happy to hear that. Fin and Sam played together every day. They swam fast and had fun. They were the best of friends. And Fin learned that even though Sam was a shark, he was a good friend to have.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car. It was a very expensive car. He loved to play with it every day. Tim's brother, Sam, did not have a car. Sam wanted one too. One day, Sam saw Tim playing with his car. Sam said, "Can I play with your car, please?" Tim said, "No, you cannot. It is my special car." Tim started to tease Sam. He said, "You don't have a car like mine. It is too expensive for you." Sam was sad. He wanted to have a car too. So, Sam decided to do some work. He helped his mom and dad with chores. They were happy with Sam's work. After some days, Sam's mom and dad bought him a car. It was as expensive as Tim's car. Now, both Tim and Sam had cars to play with. They played together and had lots of fun. They did not tease each other anymore.
