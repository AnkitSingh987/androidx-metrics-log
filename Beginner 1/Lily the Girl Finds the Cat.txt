Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a friend named Tim. They both liked to play with their toy animals. Lily had a toy cat and Tim had a toy dog. They were very flexible toys, so they could bend and twist them in many ways. One day, Lily and Tim decided to feed their toy animals. They got some toy food and put it in little bowls. Lily said to Tim, "Let's pretend to feed our toys!" Tim agreed and said, "Yes, let's do it!" So they both sat down and pretended to feed their toy animals. As they played, they made up a story about their toy animals going on an adventure. The cat and dog were friends too, just like Lily and Tim. They explored a big forest and found a magical tree. The tree gave them more toy food so they could feed their toy animals forever. Lily and Tim laughed and played, feeding their toy animals and having a great time together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. It was a hot day, and the sun was shining bright. Tim loved to play in the sand and splash in the water. "Mom, I want to build a sandcastle!" Tim said. His mom smiled and helped him. They used a bucket and shovel to make a big castle. Tim was very happy with his castle. But then, a big wave came and washed the castle away. Tim was sad. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can reverse it!" They built another sandcastle, even bigger than the first one. This time, they built it far away from the water. Tim and his mom had a great day at the beach.

Lily and Ben are playing with paper and crayons. They like to draw animals and flowers and cars. They have a big folder to keep their drawings. They are very proud of their folder. "Look, I draw a cat!" Lily says, showing Ben her paper. "It has stripes and a tail and whiskers." "Wow, that's a good cat!" Ben says. "I draw a truck. It has wheels and a horn and a big box." They put their drawings in the folder and make more. They have fun and laugh. But then, it starts to rain. The rain is very loud and wet. It comes through the window and makes the floor wet. It also makes the folder wet. "Oh no, our folder!" Lily cries. "Our drawings are wet!" Ben runs to the window and tries to close it. But the rain is too strong and he can't. He gets wet too. "Help, help!" he shouts. "The rain is too much!" Lily grabs the folder and runs to the door. She wants to find Mommy or Daddy. They can help them. They can make the rain stop. They can make the folder dry. But the door is stuck. It won't open. Lily pushes and pulls, but nothing happens. She feels scared and sad. "Ben, the door is stuck!" she says. "We can't get out!" Ben comes to the door and helps Lily. They push and pull together. But the door is still stuck. The rain is still wet. The folder is still wet. They hug each other and cry. They don't know what to do. They wish the rain would stop. They wish the door would open. They wish the folder would dry. The rain increases. It makes more noise and more wet. It makes the folder wetter and wetter. It makes the drawings fade and tear. It makes Lily and Ben sadder and sadder.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a man with a popcorn cart. Tim's mom said, "Let's get some popcorn!" Tim was very happy. The man showed them the popcorn. But it looked bad. Tim's mom said, "No, thank you." The man was sad. Tim and his mom went to play on the swings. Later, they saw a girl with good popcorn. The girl said, "I got this from the store!" Tim and his mom went to the store and got good popcorn. They shared it with the man from the park. He smiled and said, "Thank you!"
