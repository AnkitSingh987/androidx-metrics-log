Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Pale Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a pale boy named Tim. He liked to play with his friends at school. One day, Tim met a new friend, a girl named Sue. Sue was a new pupil in his class. Tim and Sue played together at school. They liked to touch the toys and share them. They had a lot of fun playing with the toys. Tim said, "Sue, I like playing with you!" Sue said, "I like playing with you too, Tim!" One day, the teacher told the class to be gentle with the toys. Tim and Sue remembered to touch the toys softly. They took turns and played nicely. The teacher was happy, and Tim and Sue were happy too. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a mule named Molly. Molly lived on a farm with her friend, a boy named Tim. They liked to play all day. Sometimes, they would strike a ball with a stick. One day, Molly and Tim saw something disgusting. It was a big pile of mud. Tim said, "Eww, Molly, that mud is yucky!" Molly nodded her head, agreeing with Tim. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game. We can strike the mud with our sticks!" Molly and Tim took their sticks and hit the mud. It was messy, but they had a lot of fun.

---

One day, a big cat and a small dog were playing in the park. They liked to mix dirt and water to make mud. They were very happy and calm. The cat said, "I found a horn! Can we mix it with the mud?" The dog said, "Yes, let's try!" So they put the horn in the mud and mixed it all up. Suddenly, the horn made a loud noise! The cat and the dog were scared. They did not know that the horn could make noise. They ran away from the horn and hid behind a tree. After a while, they laughed and went back to play with the horn and the mud.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily went to play outside. She wore her adorable clothes with lots of colors. She saw her friend, Tom, who was playing with a ball. Lily ran to Tom and they started to play together. While they played, the ball went into the bushes. Lily and Tom went to find it. In the bushes, they saw a small cat. The cat was stuck and could not get out. Tom said, "We need to help the cat!" Lily agreed and they both tried to free the cat. As they helped the cat, they heard a loud shout. It was their friend, Max, who was looking for them. Max saw the cat and was so happy. He said, "You found my lost cat! Thank you!" Lily and Tom were surprised. They did not know the cat was Max's. They all went back to play together, with the cat joining them too.

Tom and Anna are twins. They like to go to camp with Mom and Dad. Camp is a place with a big tent, a fire, and many trees. They can play, sing, and eat marshmallows at camp. One night, Tom and Anna are in the tent with Mom and Dad. They are ready to sleep. But Tom is not sleepy. He wants to see the stars. He says to Anna, "Let's go outside and look at the stars. They are shiny and pretty." Anna says, "No, Tom. It is dark outside. Dark is scary. We might get lost. Mom and Dad will be mad. We should stay in the tent and sleep." Tom says, "No, Anna. It is not scary. It is fun. We can take a flashlight and a blanket. We can find a good spot to see the stars. Mom and Dad will not know. Come on, Anna. Please." Anna thinks for a moment. She likes to see the stars too. She says, "Okay, Tom. But we have to be quiet and careful. And we have to come back soon." Tom and Anna get out of the tent. They take a flashlight and a blanket. They walk in the dark. They reach a big rock. They climb on the rock. They spread the blanket. They lie down. They turn on the flashlight. They look at the stars. They see many stars. They are shiny and pretty. They make shapes and pictures. Tom and Anna point and name the shapes and pictures. They say, "Look, Anna. There is a bear. And there is a spoon. And there is a moon." They are happy and amazed. They forget about the time. They forget about the dark. They forget about Mom and Dad. They only see the stars.
