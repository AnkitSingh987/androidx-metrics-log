Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Slide

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big slide. Tom wanted to go on the slide, but Lily was scared. "Come on, Lily, don't be a baby. I dare you to go on the slide with me. It will be fun!" Tom said. "No, Tom, it is too high and too fast. I don't want to go on the slide. It is not safe. We should be careful." Lily said. Tom did not listen to Lily. He ran to the slide and climbed up the ladder. He sat on the top and smiled. He looked down and saw Lily watching him. "Look at me, Lily. I am brave. I am going to slide down. Are you ready?" Tom said. He pushed himself and slid down the slide. But he did not see a file on the slide. Someone had left it there by mistake. The file was sharp and metal. It cut Tom's leg as he slid down. Tom screamed and cried. He felt a lot of pain. Lily ran to Tom and hugged him. She saw the blood on his leg and the file on the slide. She was very sad and scared. "I am sorry, Tom. You are hurt. We need to get help. Let me get a grown-up." Lily said. She ran to find a grown-up. She found a lady who was sitting on a bench. She told her what happened. The lady was kind and helped Tom. She wrapped his leg with a cloth and called his mom. She also took the file and threw it in the trash. Tom's mom came and took him to the doctor. The doctor cleaned his wound and put a bandage on it. He gave Tom a sticker and a lollipop. He told Tom to be more careful next time. Tom learned his lesson. He said sorry to Lily and thanked her for being a good friend. He also said sorry to the lady and his mom. He realized that being brave is not the same as being foolish. He decided to listen to Lily more often and to be more careful in the park. He and Lily played together again, but they avoided the slide. They had fun with other toys and games. They were happy and safe.

Lily was a selfish girl. She did not like to share her toys or her snacks with anyone. She always wanted to have everything for herself. She did not care if other kids were sad or angry. One day, Lily went to sleep with her favorite doll. She hugged it tight and did not let go. She dreamed of having more dolls and more toys. She was happy in her dream. But in the night, something strange happened. The doll came to life and started to talk. It said, "Lily, you are very selfish. You do not love me. You only love yourself. You never play with me or talk to me. You only use me to make yourself feel good. That is not nice. I want to be free. I want to have friends. I want to have fun." Lily woke up and heard the doll. She was scared and confused. She said, "No, no, no. You are my doll. You belong to me. You cannot leave me. You cannot talk. You are mine. Mine. Mine." The doll said, "No, Lily. I am not yours. I am my own. I have a heart and a mind. I have feelings and wishes. I do not want to be with you. You are mean and selfish. You do not know how to love. You need to learn. Goodbye, Lily." The doll jumped out of the bed and ran away. Lily tried to catch it, but it was too fast. It ran out of the door and into the night. Lily cried and cried. She felt alone and sad. She realized that she had been selfish and that she had lost her doll. She wished she had been nicer and kinder. She wished she had a friend.

Tom has a dog named Spot. Spot is big and brown and likes to play. Tom loves Spot very much. One day, Tom and Spot go to the park. They see many other dogs and people there. Spot sees a big pile of mud. He runs to the mud and jumps in it. He rolls and splashes and gets very dirty. Tom sees Spot and shouts, "No, Spot, no! Come back here!" But Spot does not listen. He is having too much fun. Tom runs to Spot and tries to pull him out of the mud. But Spot is too strong and too slippery. He pulls Tom into the mud too. Tom and Spot are both covered in mud. They look disgusting. Some people see Tom and Spot and laugh. They point and say, "Look at those two! They are so messy!" Tom feels sad and angry. He does not like being laughed at. He does not like being dirty. Tom starts to cry. He wants to go home. He wants to take a bath. He wants to hug his mom. He does not want to play with Spot anymore. Spot sees Tom crying and feels sorry. He stops playing and goes to Tom. He licks Tom's face and wags his tail. He tries to make Tom happy. He loves Tom very much. Tom feels Spot's tongue and tail. He looks at Spot's eyes. He sees that Spot is sorry and loves him. He stops crying and smiles. He hugs Spot and says, "It's okay, Spot. I love you too. But next time, please stay away from the mud."
