Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat had a hat. The hat was red. The cat liked to wear the hat. One day, the cat saw a dead bug. The cat felt sad. The cat yawned. It was time to sleep. The cat went to sleep with the hat. When the cat woke up, it was happy. The cat played with the hat. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a small bird named Tim. Every day, Tim would wake up early in the morning to sing. His songs made the other animals happy. One day, Tim found a tie on the ground. It was a pretty tie with many colors. He picked it up and showed it to his friend, the cat. "Look at this tie I found!" said Tim. "Oh, it is very nice!" said the cat. "Can you help me put it on?" asked Tim. The cat helped Tim put the tie around his neck. Tim wore the tie every day while he sang. All the animals loved his new look. They would gather around to listen and watch Tim sing with his pretty tie. Tim was very proud and happy to share his songs and tie with his friends.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to jump on the swings and slide down the slide. They saw a big dog with a red tie around his neck. The dog was smelly and barked loudly. "Hello, doggy!" Ben said. He wanted to pet the dog, but Lily was scared. "No, Ben, don't touch him! He is smelly and mean!" Lily said. She pulled Ben away from the dog. The dog ran after them and tried to bite their legs. Ben and Lily screamed and ran to the other side of the park. They found a bench and climbed on it. "Go away, doggy! You are not nice!" Ben shouted. He waved his arms and kicked his feet. The dog stopped and looked at them. He wagged his tail and licked his lips. He wanted to play with them, but he did not know how. "Maybe he is lonely and wants a friend," Lily said. She felt sorry for the dog. "Maybe, but he is still smelly and scary," Ben said. He did not like the dog. Just then, a man came running to the park. He had a leash in his hand and a whistle in his mouth. He blew the whistle and called the dog. "Rex, come here, boy! I'm sorry, he got loose from his tie. He is friendly, but he doesn't know his own strength," the man said. He put the leash on the dog and apologized to Ben and Lily. Ben and Lily got down from the bench and looked at the dog. He was still smelly, but he seemed happy to see his owner. "Can we pet him?" Lily asked. "Sure, but be careful. He might lick you," the man said. Ben and Lily reached out their hands and touched the dog's fur. He licked their faces and wagged his tail. He was not mean, after all. He was just playful. Ben and Lily smiled and laughed. They made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk and explore the world around him. One day, while walking in the forest, he found a big, shiny arrow on the ground. Tim picked up the arrow and started to play with it. He was very curious about what it could do. He walked around with the arrow, pretending to be a brave hunter. He was having so much fun. But then, Tim tripped on a rock and fell down. The arrow poked him and it hurt a lot. Tim cried and wished he had never found the arrow. He learned that playing with things he didn't know could be dangerous.

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Mark. Mark was a lucky bird. He had a nice nest in a big tree. He liked his nest very much. It was his home. One day, Mark saw a big storm coming. He was scared. He wanted to keep his nest safe. So, Mark tried to cover his nest with leaves and sticks. He worked very hard to protect his home. The storm came and went. Mark was very lucky. His nest was still safe in the tree. He was happy that he could keep his home safe. Mark knew that he was a lucky bird with a strong nest.
