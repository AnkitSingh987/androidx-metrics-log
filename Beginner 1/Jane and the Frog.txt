Category: Beginner
Title: Jane and the Frog

Jane was sitting in the garden, watching the birds. She was feeling very lazy. Suddenly, she heard a noise in the bushes. She jumped up and saw a small frog. The frog looked at Jane and nodded. "Hello!" said the frog. Jane smiled and nodded back. She asked the frog why his leg was so sore. The frog said he had been swimming too much, and was too lazy to jump out of the pond. Jane laughed and offered to help the frog. She carefully picked him up and put him in the shade next to the pond. She said goodbye to the frog and nodded as she walked away. The frog nodded back, smiling as he watched his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball all day long. He would run, jump, and sometimes even bite the ball. Spot was very happy. One day, Spot found a new thing in the bathroom. It was a bar of soap. Spot thought the soap looked like a fun toy. He picked it up with his mouth and started to play with it. He tossed it in the air and caught it again. It was slippery and fun! But then, Spot decided to bite the soap. Oh no! It tasted very bad. Spot didn't like the taste of soap at all. He dropped it and ran to find his ball again. From that day on, Spot only played with his ball and never tried to bite soap again. And Spot stayed a friendly and happy dog.

One day, a little girl named Sue was very glad. She had a new toy pony. Sue loved her toy pony very much. She played with it all day. She named her pony, "Happy." Sue's friend, Tim, came to play. He saw Sue's pony and said, "I like your pony. Can I play with it too?" Sue said, "Yes, you can play with Happy." They played with the pony together and had a lot of fun. Later, Sue and Tim found a box. They put the pony in the box and played mail. They took turns being the mailman. But, when it was Tim's turn, he tripped and the box opened. Happy the pony fell out and landed in a puddle. Sue was sad. Tim said, "I am sorry, Sue." Sue's mom came outside and saw the wet pony. She said, "Don't worry, we can clean Happy." They washed the pony and let it dry in the sun. Soon, Happy was clean and dry. Sue and Tim were glad again. They played with Happy the pony, and had a great day.

Tom and Sam were playing in the park. They liked to climb on the equipment and slide down the slides. But there was a big dog in the park too. The dog was wet and dirty. It barked at the children and ran after them. "Go away, dog!" Tom shouted. "You are not nice!" But the dog did not go away. It chased Tom and Sam to the swings. Tom and Sam jumped on the swings and tried to swing high. The dog jumped and tried to bite their feet. "Help, help!" Sam cried. "The dog wants to bite us!" A lady heard Sam's cry. She was the dog's owner. She ran to the park and called the dog. "Rex, Rex, come here!" she said. "You are a bad dog! You cannot bite the children!" The dog heard the lady and stopped jumping. It ran to the lady and wagged its tail. The lady put a leash on the dog and said sorry to Tom and Sam. "I'm sorry, boys," she said. "Rex is not a bad dog. He just likes to play. But he does not know how to play with children. He only knows how to play with other dogs. I will take him home and teach him to be gentle." Tom and Sam were scared, but they were also brave. They said thank you to the lady and waved goodbye to the dog. They decided to play on the equipment again, but they stayed away from the wet and dirty dog.
