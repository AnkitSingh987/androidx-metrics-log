Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved playing outside with her friends and was always very happy. One day, she saw a pretty pear so she decided to pick it up and take it home. When Lucy got home, she saw her mom with a wounded foot. She knew that if she ate the pear, she could make her mom feel better. So, Lucy stayed by her mom's side and ate the pear. When she was done, she hugged her mom and told her that she hoped it would make her feel better. And it did! Lucy's mom's foot started to heal, and very soon it was as good as new. Lucy and her mom hugged and thanked the sweet pear that had made them both happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia was very clean and loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a big box in her room. Mia was very excited and wanted to see what was inside the box. She tried to unlock it, but she couldn't find the key. Mia asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me unlock the box?" Her mom said, "I don't know where the key is, but we can look for it together." They looked and looked, but they couldn't find the key. Mia was sad, but she didn't give up. She knew she had to find the key to unlock the box. One night, when everyone was in bed, Mia heard a strange noise. She woke up and saw a little key on her pillow. She thought, "This must be the key to the box!" Mia was so happy that she ran to the box and unlocked it. But when she opened the box, all her clean toys turned dirty and messy. Mia was very sad and didn't know what to do. She learned that sometimes, it's better to leave things locked away.

Tom likes to whistle. He can make many sounds with his mouth. He whistles when he plays, when he walks, when he is happy. He thinks whistling is great. One day, he sees a bird on a tree. The bird is whistling too. It has a red head and a yellow belly. Tom thinks the bird is pretty. He wants to talk to the bird. He whistles to the bird. The bird whistles back. Tom smiles. He asks the bird, "What is your name?" The bird whistles again. Tom does not understand. He asks again, "What is your name?" The bird flies down and lands on Tom's shoulder. It whistles in his ear. Tom laughs. He feels the bird's feathers tickle him. He says, "You are a funny bird. I like you. Can I be your friend?" The bird nods and whistles. Tom hugs the bird. He says, "You are my friend. I will call you Whistle. Do you like that name?" The bird whistles happily. Tom and Whistle play together. They have a great time.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. She lived in a small house with her dog, Max. Sue loved to draw with her crayons. One day, Sue drew a big, happy sun with her yellow crayon. She showed her drawing to Max and said, "Look, Max! I made a sun with my crayon!" Max wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" He liked the drawing too. Suddenly, the happy sun came to life! It jumped off the paper and started to dance around the room. Sue and Max couldn't believe their eyes. The sun said, "Hello, Sue! Thank you for drawing me. I am here to comfort you and make you happy." Sue and Max were so surprised, but they were also happy. They played with the sun all day long. The sun comforted Sue when she was sad and made her feel warm and loved. From that day on, Sue knew that her crayons were very special.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and balls. They have a club in the garden. The club is a big box with a door and a window. They put a sign on the box that says "Tom and Sam's Club. No girls allowed." One day, a girl named Lily comes to the garden. She sees the box and the sign. She wants to play with Tom and Sam. She knocks on the door and says "Hello, can I join your club?" Tom and Sam look at each other. They do not like girls. They want to make Lily go away. They think of a plan. They say to Lily "You can join our club, but you have to do a test first. You have to fill this bucket with water from the tap and bring it back to us." Lily agrees. She takes the bucket and goes to the tap. She turns on the tap and fills the bucket with water. The bucket is heavy and wet. She carries it back to the box. She is tired and thirsty. She knocks on the door again and says "I filled the bucket. Can I join your club now?" Tom and Sam open the door and smile. They say "Yes, you can join our club. But first, you have to pour the water on your head." Lily is shocked. She does not want to pour the water on her head. She says "No, that is not fair. That is mean. You are not nice. You are not loyal. I do not want to join your club anymore. I will find other friends who are kind and fun." She puts down the bucket and runs away. She leaves Tom and Sam alone in their box. They look at each other. They feel sorry and sad. They realize they made a mistake. They say to each other "Maybe we should have let Lily join our club. Maybe she would have been a good friend. Maybe we should have been more loyal."
