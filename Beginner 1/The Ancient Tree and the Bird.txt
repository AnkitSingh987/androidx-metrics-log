Title: The Ancient Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tree in a small park. One sunny day, a girl named Mia and her mom went to the park. They sat under the tree and had a yummy cake for a snack. A little bird flew down and stared at the cake. "Can I have some cake, please?" the bird asked. Mia and her mom were surprised that the bird could talk, but they shared their cake with the bird anyway. Then, the bird said, "Thank you for sharing your cake. I am a magic bird, and because you were kind, I will give you a wish." Mia thought for a moment and wished for more cake to share with her friends. The bird made many cakes appear, and Mia and her mom shared them with everyone in the park. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others, because you never know what unexpected things might happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was an obedient dog. He always listened to his mom and dad. Max loved to play with his ball and run around the yard. But Max's favorite thing to do was sleep. One day, Max's mom said, "Max, it's time to go to bed. You need to sleep now." Max was tired, so he went to his bed and lay down. He closed his eyes and started to sleep. In the middle of the night, Max heard a noise. He opened his eyes and saw a little cat outside. The cat said, "Max, come play with me!" Max wanted to play, but he remembered that his mom told him to sleep. So Max said, "No, I need to stay in bed and sleep." The cat was sad, but Max knew he was being obedient. He closed his eyes and went back to sleep. When Max woke up in the morning, he felt happy and rested. He knew he had done the right thing by listening to his mom and staying in bed.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a coin on the ground. It is shiny and round. "Look, a coin!" Tim says. "Let's share it!" "How do we share it?" Sam asks. "It is only one coin." Tim thinks for a moment. He has an idea. He sees a big rock nearby. It is rough and hard. "Let's part the coin!" Tim says. "We can use the rock to break it in two. Then we each have half a coin!" Sam likes the idea. He nods and smiles. They take the coin to the rock. They hit the coin with the rock. But the coin does not break. It only makes a loud noise. "Ouch!" Tim says. "The coin is too strong. The rock hurts my hand." "Me too!" Sam says. "The coin is too hard. The rock hurts my hand." They look at the coin. It is still shiny and round. But it has some scratches from the rock. "Maybe we should not part the coin," Tim says. "Maybe we should keep it whole." "Maybe you are right," Sam says. "Maybe we should keep it together." They decide to keep the coin as it is. They put it in their pocket. They go back to playing in the park. They are still friends. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gray cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to eat her food from a tiny plate. One day, Kitty's plate was lost. She looked everywhere but could not find it. Kitty was very sad and hungry. Kitty asked her friend, Doggy, "Can you help me find my plate?" Doggy wagged his tail and said, "Yes, Kitty, let's solve this together!" They looked under the bed, behind the door, and even in the garden. Finally, they found the plate under a big tree. Kitty was so happy and said, "Thank you, Doggy! You are a good friend." They both learned that when you help each other, you can solve any problem. From that day on, Kitty and Doggy were the best of friends, and they always helped each other.
