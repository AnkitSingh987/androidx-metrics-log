Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom Find the Dog

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big black dog. The dog is cool. He has a shiny coat and a long tail. "Hi, dog!" Lily says. "Can we play with you?" The dog wags his tail. He likes Lily and Tom. He runs to them and licks their faces. "Wow, you are so friendly!" Tom says. "What is your name?" The dog barks. He does not talk like Lily and Tom. But he tries to tell them his name. "Ruff, ruff, ruff!" he says. "Ruff? Is that your name?" Lily asks. The dog nods. He is happy that Lily and Tom understand him. "Ruff, you are a good dog!" Tom says. "We want to be your friends!" Lily and Tom play with Ruff. They throw a ball and he fetches it. They rub his belly and he rolls over. They have a lot of fun. But then, a man comes. He is angry. He shouts at Lily and Tom. "Hey, you kids! Get away from my dog!" he says. "He is not for you to play with!" Lily and Tom are scared. They do not know the man. They do not know why he is angry. "But, sir, we are just playing with Ruff!" Lily says. "He is cool and friendly!" The man does not listen. He grabs Ruff by the collar and pulls him away. "Ruff is not his name! His name is Shadow! And he is not cool and friendly! He is fierce and loyal! He is my guard dog! He does not like strangers!" he says. He drags Ruff to his car. Ruff looks sad. He does not want to leave Lily and Tom. He whines and tries to break free. "Bye, Ruff! We will miss you!" Lily and Tom say. They wave to Ruff. Ruff barks. He says goodbye to Lily and Tom. He hopes to see them again. Lily and Tom walk home. They are sad too. They liked Ruff. They wish the man was nicer. "Maybe the man does not have any friends," Lily says. "Maybe that is why he is angry and lonely." "Maybe," Tom says. "But that is not a good reason to be mean to others." Lily and Tom hug each other. They are glad they have each other. They are glad they are kind and friendly. They learn a lesson. They learn that being cool is not enough. Being good is more important. They learn that talking is not the only way to communicate. Being loving is more powerful. They learn that black is not a bad color. It is a beautiful color.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had an interesting jacket. It was red and had a big pocket. Sue loved her jacket very much. One day, Sue wanted to see if her toy bear could fit in the jacket's big pocket. She put the bear in the pocket, and it fit! Sue was very happy. She took her bear with her everywhere in her interesting jacket. Sue and her bear went to the park, the store, and even to bed. The interesting jacket and the bear were Sue's best friends. They played and had fun every day. And that is the story of Sue, her toy bear, and the interesting jacket.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She liked to skip and sing. Amy saw a big tree with delicious red apples. She wanted to pick one, but they were too high. She tried to reach with her finger, but she could not get it. Amy saw a tall boy named Ben. She asked, "Can you help me get an apple?" Ben said, "I will try." Ben reached up high with his finger, but he could not get the apple too. They were both sad. Then, they saw a long stick. They had an idea. Amy and Ben used the stick to get the apple. They both smiled and said, "We did it!" They shared the delicious apple and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a hairy bug named Bob. Bob was very small and had many friends. One day, Bob saw a big net near his home. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was fun to play with. One sunny day, Bob went to play with the net. He jumped on it and had a lot of fun. But soon, he got stuck in the net. He tried to get out, but it was very hard. Bob began to struggle. He wished his friends were there to help him. Just then, his friends came to find Bob. They saw him stuck in the net and wanted to help. They all worked together and pulled the net off of Bob. He was free! Bob was so happy and thanked his friends. From that day on, Bob knew that the net was not a toy and he never played with it again.
