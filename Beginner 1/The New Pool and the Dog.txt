Category: Beginner
Title: The New Pool and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a new pool in the park. The sun would shine on it every day. It was so pretty. All the kids liked to play in the pool. They would splash and laugh. One day, a big dog came to the park. The dog liked the pool too. But the dog was not nice. It would jump in the pool and scare the kids. The kids were sad. They did not want to play in the pool with the big dog. A little girl had an idea. She went to the store and got a big ball. She took the ball to the park. She threw the ball far away. The big dog saw the ball and ran after it. The dog liked the ball more than the pool. Now the kids could play in the pool again. They were happy and the sun kept shining on their new pool.

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there lived many animals. They all played and had fun together. One day, they needed to choose a leader. They wanted someone wise to help them. A wise old owl lived in the valley too. All the animals thought the owl was very smart. They asked the owl to be their leader. The owl said yes and was happy to help. The wise owl helped the animals find food, water, and good places to live. The animals in the valley were happy and safe. They played together and listened to their wise leader, the owl. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a deaf boy, whose name was Sam. Every day he would take a walk and enjoy the fresh air. On this one day, Sam heard a whistle. He was very happy and he wanted to find out who was making the sound. He looked around, but he couldn't find anyone. Suddenly, he heard the whistle again, but from much further away. Sam followed the sound until he saw a man walking. But the man couldn't hear Sam, because he was deaf. Sam tried to get the man's attention by waving and shouting, but the man couldn't hear him. Sam kept following him until he suddenly turned around and saw Sam. The man was so surprised that he ran away, leaving Sam alone. The next day, Sam still heard the whistle, but he was too fearful to follow it. He was afraid that the man might be waiting for him and do something bad. Sam was so sad. He never heard the whistle again.

John and his mom were planning an outing. They wanted to find a guard to look after their house while they were gone. John's mom said, "John, let's look for a polite guard for our house." John asked, "What does polite mean?" His mom smiled, "Polite means kind and respectful." So they left their house and went to a nearby town. They looked for a very polite guard. John saw a nice guard with a smile. He looked kind and respectful. John ran to his mom and said, "Mommy, I found a nice guard." His mom smiled and said, "That's perfect. Let's ask him to watch our house." John and his mom walked up to the guard and asked him politely if he could look after their house while they went on their outing. The guard said yes, and they thanked him. Once they got back, the house was in perfect shape. John and his mom were glad to have found such a polite guard.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Let's skip to the slide!" Lily says. She holds Ben's hand. They skip and laugh. They get to the slide. Lily goes first. She sits on the start. She slides down. She is happy. "Your turn, Ben!" Lily says. She waits for Ben. Ben is scared. He does not want to slide. He looks at the start. He looks at the end. He shakes his head. "No, Lily. I can't slide. It is too high. It is too fast. I am afraid." Lily sees that Ben is scared. She wants to help him. She is helpful. "Don't be afraid, Ben. I am here. You can do it. It is fun. I will slide with you. Come on, Ben. Trust me." Lily climbs up the slide. She sits on the start. She makes room for Ben. She holds his hand. "Ready, Ben?" Lily says. She smiles at him. Ben nods. He is still scared. But he trusts Lily. He holds her hand. "Ready, Lily!" Ben says. He tries to smile. Lily and Ben slide down together. They go fast. They feel the wind. They scream and laugh. They get to the end. They are happy. They hug each other. "Wow, Ben! You did it! You are brave! How do you feel?" Lily says. "I feel good, Lily! Thank you for helping me! You are a good friend! That was fun!" Ben says. They skip to the slide again. They slide down many times. They have fun. They are friends.
