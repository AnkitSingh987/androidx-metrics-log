Category: Beginner
Title: The Sun of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was upset because he could not choose what to play with. He had a toy car and a toy ball, but he could not decide which one he liked more. One day, Tim went outside to play. The sun was shining brightly in the sky. He thought, "Maybe the sun can help me choose what to play with." So, he asked the sun, "Sun, can you help me choose?" The sun smiled and said, "Tim, you can play with both the toy car and the toy ball. You don't have to choose just one." Tim was so happy. He played with his toy car and his toy ball all day long. And he was never upset again.

Once upon a time, there was a little baby named Tim. Tim lived in a small crib. His crib was blue and had lots of toys. Tim loved to play with his toys all day. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, are you ready to taste some new food?" Tim looked at his mom and said, "Yes, mommy! I am ready!" Tim's mom gave him a small spoon with some yummy orange food on it. Tim tasted the food and his eyes got big. "Yummy!" he said. From that day on, Tim loved to taste new foods. He tried green food, red food, and even yellow food. Tim and his mom had so much fun trying new foods together. And Tim's crib was always full of yummy food and happy memories.

One day, a big, interesting bug went to the forest. The bug liked to steal things. In the forest, he saw a big log. The bug thought the log was fun to play with. So, he tried to steal the log. As the bug tried to steal the log, something unexpected happened. The log started to move! The bug was very surprised. The log was not a log at all. It was a big, friendly animal! The animal said, "I am not a log, I am a friend. I saw you steal things. That is not nice." The bug felt bad. He said sorry to the animal. They became good friends. The bug did not steal anymore. They played and had fun in the forest together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy car. One day, Tim saw a spark in the sky. It was very bright and pretty. Tim wanted to touch the spark, but his mom said no. Tim was sad and didn't understand why his mom said no. Tim's mom tried to explain that the spark was not safe to touch. But Tim was ignorant and didn't listen. He thought his mom was just being mean. Tim insisted that he would touch the spark, no matter what his mom said. He waited for his mom to go inside the house. When his mom was gone, Tim went outside to find the spark. He looked up at the sky and saw the spark again. It was so close! Tim reached out to touch it. But when he touched the spark, it burned his hand. Tim cried and cried because it hurt so much. His mom came running out and saw Tim's burned hand. She felt sad for him, but she knew that Tim had to learn not to touch things that were not safe. Tim learned his lesson, but he had to live with the pain of his burned hand. And that was a bad ending for Tim.

One day, a purple cat named Max went for a walk. He liked to walk in the sun and see the bugs and flowers. Max saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. While he was sitting, he saw a little girl with a toy. "Hi, I'm Sally," said the little girl. "Do you like my toy? It's very special and has a lot of value." Max looked at the toy and saw that it was a pretty doll. Just then, a big wind came and blew the doll away. Max and Sally were sad. But Max had an idea. He jumped and ran to catch the doll. The wind blew the doll high and far, but Max did not give up. He jumped and grabbed the doll with his paw. Max brought the doll back to Sally. She was so happy! "Thank you, Max! You are my hero!" Sally hugged Max, and they became best friends. Now, they always walk together and play with the valuable doll. And Max learned that even a little cat can do big things.
