Category: Beginner
Title: The Yellow Bird and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a warm summer, there was a yellow bird. The bird liked to fly and sing in the sun. The bird had many friends who played with it every day. One day, the bird found some seeds. The bird wanted to share the seeds with its friends. So, the bird took the seeds and scatter them on the ground. The bird's friends came, and they all ate the seeds together. After eating the seeds, the bird and its friends played a fun game. They chased each other around the trees until the sun went down. Then, they said goodbye and went to their homes. The yellow bird was happy because it had a great day with its friends.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big yellow net on the grass. It was for catching butterflies. "Let's play with the net!" Lily said. She ran to the net and picked it up. "No, it's mine!" Ben said. He ran after Lily and tried to grab the net. "Stop it, Ben! You are mean!" Lily said. She ran away from Ben and swung the net in the air. Ben was angry. He wanted the net too. He chased Lily and pushed her. Lily fell down and dropped the net. She started to cry. "Ben, you are bad! You hurt Lily!" a voice said. It was Mom. She saw what happened and came to help. She picked up Lily and hugged her. She checked if she was hurt. She was not hurt, but she was sad. She looked at Ben and frowned. "Ben, you need to say sorry to Lily. You can't push and take things. You need to share and be nice." Ben felt sorry. He knew he was wrong. He walked to Lily and said, "Sorry, Lily. I was bad. Can we be friends?" Lily looked at Ben. She saw he was sorry. She nodded and said, "Okay, Ben. We can be friends. But you have to share the net." Mom smiled and said, "That's good. Now, why don't you both play with the net together? Maybe you can catch some butterflies." Lily and Ben smiled too. They took the net and went to look for butterflies. They played nicely and had fun. They learned to share and be nice.

Once upon a time, there was a big ocean. In the ocean, there was a fish named Fin. Fin was not a fancy fish, but he was happy. He liked to swim with his friends in the ocean. One day, Fin saw a new fish. The new fish was very fancy. He had pretty colors and a long tail. Fin wanted to talk to the new fish. He swam up to him and said, "Hi, I am Fin. What is your name?" The fancy fish smiled and said, "Hello, Fin. I am Finsworth. I am new here. Can you help me recognize where I can find some food?" Fin was happy to help. He said, "Sure, Finsworth! Follow me." Fin and Finsworth swam together. They found a nice place with lots of food. They ate and played together. Now, Fin and Finsworth are best friends. They swim and have fun in the big ocean every day.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful cat named Tom. Tom liked to sit under a big tree and watch the birds. One day, Tom saw a little bird with a frown on its face. The bird looked sad. Tom decided to help the bird. He asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I lost my way home. Can you help me?" Tom nodded and said, "Let's find your home together." As they walked, they met a wise frog. The frog said, "I know a spell to help find the bird's home." The frog did the spell and suddenly, the bird's frown turned into a big smile. The spell made the bird remember the way home! Tom and the bird thanked the wise frog and walked to the bird's home together. In the end, the sad bird was happy again, and Tom made a new friend.
