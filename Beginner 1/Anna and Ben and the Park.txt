Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Park

Anna and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big statue of a man on a horse. The statue is old and has cracks and dirt on it. "Who is that?" Anna asks. "I don't know. Maybe a king or a hero," Ben says. They go closer to the statue and touch it. It feels cold and hard. "Look, he has a sword and a hat," Ben says. "He looks sad and angry," Anna says. They hear a loud noise. It is a big truck coming to the park. The truck has a sign that says "Park Renovation". "Oh no, they are going to take the statue away!" Ben says. "Why?" Anna asks. "Maybe they want to make the park new and pretty. But the statue is old and poor," Ben says. They feel sorry for the statue. They don't want it to go. "Let's save the statue!" Anna says. They rush to the statue and hug it. They shout at the truck. "Stop! Stop! Don't take the statue! He is our friend!" they say. The truck driver sees them and stops. He gets out of the truck and walks to them. "What are you doing, kids?" he asks. "We are saving the statue. He is old and poor, but he is a king or a hero. He belongs to the park. Please don't take him away," Anna says. The truck driver looks at the statue and smiles. "I see. You have a big heart, kids. But don't worry, I'm not going to take the statue away. I'm here to fix it and clean it. He will look new and happy again," he says. "Really?" Ben asks. "Really. Come on, let me show you. I have some tools and paint in the truck. You can help me if you want," he says. Anna and Ben are happy. They let go of the statue and follow the truck driver. They help him fix and clean the statue. They give him a new sword and a new hat. They paint him with bright colors. The statue looks new and happy again. He smiles at Anna and Ben. "Thank you, kids. You are my friends," he says. Anna and Ben smile back. "You are welcome, statue. You are our friend, too," they say.

Once upon a time, there was a big bookshelf in a little room. The bookshelf was full of books. Some books were known, but some were unknown. A little boy named Timmy liked to read the known books. He was scared of the unknown books. One day, Timmy's friend, Sally, came to play. Sally was not scared of the unknown books. She said, "Let's read the unknown books, Timmy!" Timmy was scared, but he wanted to be brave like Sally. So, he said, "Okay, let's read the unknown books." They started to read the unknown books. The first book had a bad story. It was about a mean monster who liked to scare little kids. Timmy and Sally got very scared. They did not want the mean monster to come and scare them. They put the book back on the bookshelf and ran away. They never read the unknown books again.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a pretty picture. The picture was not on the wall. It needed to hang up. A big dog and a little cat lived in the room. They both wanted to hang the picture. They tried, but it was hard. The dog was too big, and the cat was too small. They were not happy. This made a big mess in the tidy room. In the end, they worked together. The dog lifted the cat up. The cat hung the picture on the wall. They were happy with the result. The room was tidy again, and the picture was in its place. The dog and the cat were friends again.

Sara and Ben like to play in the yard. They have a big tree with a swing. They take turns to push each other on the swing. They laugh and have fun. One day, they see a cat on the roof of their house. The cat is black and white and has a bell on its collar. The cat looks lazy and sleepy. It does not see Sara and Ben. Sara and Ben want to say hello to the cat. They run to the house and look for a way to get on the roof. They find a ladder in the garage. They are careful and climb the ladder. They reach the roof and see the cat. The cat is still lazy and sleepy. It does not hear Sara and Ben. Sara and Ben walk slowly to the cat. They want to pet the cat and hear its bell. They are close to the cat. But the cat wakes up and sees Sara and Ben. The cat is scared and angry. It does not like strangers on its roof. It hisses and spits at Sara and Ben. It jumps and runs to the other side of the roof. Sara and Ben are scared and sorry. They did not mean to make the cat mad. They want to get off the roof. But the ladder is too far. They do not know what to do. They see their mom in the yard. She is looking for them. She sees them on the roof. She is shocked and worried. She yells at them to stay still and not move. She runs to the garage and gets the ladder. She puts the ladder on the roof and climbs up. She hugs Sara and Ben and tells them to be careful. She helps them get down the ladder. She is mad and glad at the same time. Sara and Ben say sorry to their mom. They say they just wanted to see the cat. They say they will not do it again. Their mom says she loves them and they are safe. She says they should not go on the roof. She says the roof is for the cat, not for them. She says they can play on the swing, not on the roof. Sara and Ben nod and hug their mom. They go back to the swing. They do not look at the cat. The cat is still on the roof. It is lazy and sleepy again. It does not care about Sara and Ben. It rocks its bell and closes its eyes.
