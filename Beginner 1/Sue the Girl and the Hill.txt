Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Hill

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw her pet dog, Max. Max was a big dog with mild brown fur. Sue loved to play with Max in their big yard. "Max, let's jump and play!" Sue said. They began to jump and run all around. They jumped over rocks, sticks, and even a small hill. They were having so much fun together. Suddenly, they saw something in the bushes. It moved and made a noise. Sue and Max stopped jumping and looked. Out came a tiny kitten! It was lost and scared. Sue and Max were surprised, but they knew they had to help. They took the kitten home and gave it food and love. Now, Sue had two pets to jump and play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little angel. She had pretty wings and a nice smile. The angel liked to help people. She was a good friend to all. One day, the angel found a cheap toy on the ground. It was a small ball. She wanted to give the toy to a child who had no toys. But the toy was stuck in a bush. The angel had to struggle to get it out. After a lot of trying, the angel got the cheap toy out of the bush. She was very happy. She gave the toy to a little boy who had no toys. The boy was so happy too. They played together and became good friends. The angel felt good because she helped someone.

Tom and Mia like to play in the forest. They find many things to use. They use sticks to make a house. They use pine cones to make food. They use leaves to make a bed. One day, they see a big pine tree. It has many branches and needles. Tom and Mia want to climb it. They think it will be fun. "Can we climb the pine tree?" Tom asks Mia. "Yes, let's climb the pine tree!" Mia says. They start to climb the pine tree. They hold on to the branches and needles. They go higher and higher. They see the forest and the sky. But the pine tree is dirty. It has sap and bugs. The sap is sticky and the bugs are icky. Tom and Mia get dirty too. They have sap and bugs on their hands and clothes. "Yuck, the pine tree is dirty!" Tom says. "Yuck, we are dirty too!" Mia says. They want to get down. They climb down the pine tree. They are not happy. They are dirty and itchy. They go to the river. They wash their hands and clothes. They wash off the sap and the bugs. They feel better. They do not want to climb the pine tree again. They go back to their house. They play with their sticks and pine cones and leaves. They are happy. They like to play in the forest.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, hairy dog. The dog loved to play and run around. One day, the dog saw a cat. The cat was up in a tree. The dog wanted to play with the cat. The dog barked and barked at the cat. The cat did not like the dog's loud bark. The cat hissed at the dog. The dog did not stop barking. It wanted the cat to come down and play. The cat was not happy. It jumped down from the tree. The cat found a water bottle. The cat sprayed water at the dog. The dog was sad and wet. The cat did not want to play with the dog. The dog went home, still wet and sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a bit nervous because it was his first time to deliver yogurt to his friend, Sue. Tim put the yogurt in a small bag and walked to Sue's house. When Tim got to Sue's house, he knocked on the door. Sue opened the door and said, "Hi Tim! What do you have in the bag?" Tim said, "I have yogurt for you, Sue!" Sue was very happy and thanked Tim for bringing the yogurt. After Sue got the yogurt, they both sat down and ate the yogurt together. Tim was not nervous anymore because he had fun with his friend. They laughed and played until it was time for Tim to go home. Tim was happy that he could deliver the yogurt to Sue and make her smile.
