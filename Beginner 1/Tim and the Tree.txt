Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Tree

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a spoon. It was his favorite spoon. It was big and shiny. Tim took it everywhere he went. One day, he saw a big tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree. Tim tried to climb the tree. It was hard. His friend, Sue, came by and saw him. "What are you doing?" she asked. "I want to climb the tree," Tim said. "But it is hard." Sue had an idea. "Maybe your spoon can help," she said. Tim took his spoon and dug it into the tree. It helped him climb up. He was so happy. Sue cheered for him. Tim reached the top of the tree and looked around. He could see his house and Sue's house. Then, Tim climbed back down. "Thank you, Sue," he said. "My spoon helped me climb the tree." They smiled and went home to play.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Sam. Sam loved to clean his home. One day, Sam found a gum under his bed. He picked it up and said, "Yuck! I need to throw this away." Sam went to his friend, a dog named Max. Max saw the gum and said, "Oh no! That is dirty. We need to clean it up." So, Sam and Max worked together to clean the gum. After they cleaned the gum, Sam and Max played outside. They were very happy. They knew that if they kept their home clean, they would not find any more gum. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. She saw a big tree with red apples on it. Sue wanted to pick an apple. She asked her mom, "Can I pick an apple, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." As Sue tried to pick an apple, a noisy bird flew by and scared her. She fell down, and her knee hurt. Sue cried, and her mom came to help her. Her mom said, "Don't cry. I will make it better." Then, a stranger came to the park. He saw Sue and her mom. The stranger wanted to pick apples too. But he was too big and too noisy. Sue and her mom did not like the stranger. They told him, "You are too noisy. Please go away." The stranger said, "I'm sorry. I will be quiet." He picked an apple and gave it to Sue. Sue was happy. She said, "Thank you!" The stranger smiled and left. Sue, her mom, and the noisy bird enjoyed the rest of the day at the park.

One day, a frog named Tim saw an empty box. He decided to jump in it. Inside the box, he found a rag. Tim thought the rag was pretty. He picked it up and jumped out of the box. Tim met a cat named Sam. Sam saw the rag and said, "Can I play with the rag too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They took turns jumping on the rag, having lots of fun. After playing, Tim and Sam were tired. They sat on the rag and looked at the sky. The sun was going down. Tim said, "Today was fun. Let's play again tomorrow!" Sam agreed, and they both went home, excited to play more the next day.

Once upon a time, a beautiful animal named Lucy lived in a big forest. Lucy was very big and liked to play with her friends. She had lots of friends like the bird, the cat, and the dog. One day, Lucy found a magic stone. The magic stone could shrink things. Lucy thought it would be fun to play with the stone. She used the stone to shrink her friends. The bird, the cat, and the dog became very small. But then, Lucy lost the magic stone. She looked everywhere, but she could not find it. Her friends stayed small and they were sad. Lucy was sad too. They could not play like before. The magic stone was gone and they never found it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a blue watch that he loved very much. His mom gave it to him on his birthday. Tim wore the watch all the time. He liked to look at it and see what time it was. One day, Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Before he started to climb, he remembered his mom told him to remove his watch when playing. He took off his blue watch and put it on the ground. Tim climbed the tree and had a lot of fun. He looked down and saw a little dog come to the tree. The dog found Tim's blue watch on the ground. The dog picked up the watch in its mouth and ran away. Tim was sad and climbed down the tree. He looked for his blue watch but could not find it. He went home and told his mom what happened. His mom said not to worry and they would find it. The next day, Tim and his mom went back to the park. They saw the little dog with the blue watch in its mouth. The dog gave Tim his watch back. Tim was so happy and thanked the dog. From that day on, Tim always remembered to remove his watch when playing.
