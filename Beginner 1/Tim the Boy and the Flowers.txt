Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Flowers

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a beautiful landscape with many trees and flowers. He wanted to join his friends who were playing near the trees. As he walked, he met a fierce dog. The dog barked at Tim and scared him. Tim didn't know what to do. He wanted to get to his friends, but the dog was in the way. Tim said, "Please let me go to my friends." The dog stopped barking and looked at Tim. The dog was not so fierce after all. The dog wagged its tail and let Tim pass. Tim joined his friends and they all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, rough bear named Ben. Ben liked to play with his toy navy boats. One day, Ben went to the park to play with his friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily said, "Ben, let's complete a race with our navy boats!" Ben agreed, and they started the race. They pushed their boats in the water and watched them go. Ben's boat was winning, but then something unexpected happened! A tiny frog jumped on Ben's boat and made it stop. Lily's boat passed by and won the race. Ben was sad but then he laughed, "That was fun! Let's play again!" They played with their navy boats all day and had a great time.

One day, a dog named Max went to the park. Max saw a big fountain. He saw people wave at him. Max was happy and wagged his tail. He liked the park and the fountain. Max saw a spicy smell. It was a hot dog stand. Max loved hot dogs. He ran to the stand and waited. A nice man gave Max a hot dog. Max was very happy and ate it fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The hot dog was not a hot dog! It was a toy hot dog. Max was sad. The nice man saw Max and laughed. He gave Max a real hot dog. Max was happy again and enjoyed his treat by the fountain.

Tom and Mia were hungry. They wanted to eat some olives. They saw a big jar of olives on the table. But the jar was too high for them to reach. They had an idea. They got a chair and a stool. They put the stool on the chair. Tom climbed on the stool. He reached for the jar. "Be careful, Tom!" Mia said. Tom grabbed the jar. But the jar was too heavy for him. He lost his balance. He fell down with the jar. The jar broke on the floor. The olives rolled everywhere. Tom and Mia were scared. They heard a loud noise. It was their mom. She was angry. "What have you done?" she shouted. "Look at this mess! You could have hurt yourself! You are in big trouble!" Tom and Mia wanted to escape. But they could not. Their mom was blocking the door. She made them clean up the olives and the glass. She gave them a time out. They had no olives to eat. They were sad. They wished they had not been so greedy. They learned their lesson.

One day, a little girl named Sue was helping her mom in the kitchen. Sue liked to be honest and help her mom. She saw the stove was dirty. Sue wanted to wipe the stove clean. Sue got a cloth and started to wipe the stove. While she wiped, she saw a little door on the stove. It was a secret door! Sue opened the door and found a tiny room inside the stove. In the tiny room, Sue saw a small mouse. The mouse was honest and kind. The mouse said, "Thank you for wiping the stove. Now I can live in a clean house." Sue and the mouse became good friends.

Once there was a boy who loved to lift objects. One day, he decided to lift a huge oak tree! He was so excited to try and lift the huge tree. He was determined to do it. So, he grabbed hold of the tree and with all his might he lifted. He lifted and lifted, until he was able to keep the oak tree off the ground. He was so proud of himself! But then he noticed something. There was another boy, and he looked jealous. The jealous boy also wanted to lift the oak tree. He thought he could do it just like the first boy. So, the jealous boy grabbed a hold of the tree branches and started to lift too. After lots of hard work, the second boy managed to lift the tree just like the first boy. He was so excited that he had done it. Now, both boys were happy that they were able to lift the huge oak tree.
