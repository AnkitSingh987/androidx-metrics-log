Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drawing

One day, a little boy named Tim found a piece of paper on the floor. He picked it up and saw that it had a fun drawing on it. Tim wanted to keep the paper, but he was not sure if he should. He decided to ask his mom. "Mom, can I keep this paper?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the paper and said, "Yes, but you must mind your manners and be obedient. Do not make a mess with it." Tim was happy and played with the paper all day. But then, his little sister, Lily, saw the paper and wanted it too. She tried to take it from Tim, but he did not want to give it to her. They both started to cry. Their mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She told them, "You must learn to share and be kind to each other." Tim and Lily listened to their mom and decided to share the paper. They both played with it happily and were obedient kids.

Ben is a friendly boy who likes to learn new things. He likes to play with his toys, read his books, and watch his shows. He also likes to learn about his body and how it works. He knows he has a head, a chest, a belly, two arms, two legs, and ten fingers and toes. One day, he goes to the park with his mom and sees a girl on a swing. She has long hair, a dress, and a bow. Ben thinks she is pretty and wants to say hello. He runs to the swing and says, "Hi, I'm Ben. What's your name?" The girl smiles and says, "Hi, Ben. I'm Mia. Do you want to play with me?" Ben nods and says, "Yes, please. Can I push you on the swing?" Mia says, "Sure, that would be fun. Thank you, Ben. You are very friendly." Ben pushes Mia on the swing and they laugh and talk. He learns that Mia likes to draw, sing, and dance. He tells her that he likes to learn about his body and how it works. He says, "Did you know that your heart beats fast when you swing? And that your lungs breathe air in and out? And that your eyes see colors and shapes?" Mia says, "Wow, that's amazing. I didn't know that. How do you know that?" Ben says, "I read it in my books and watch it on my shows. Do you want to learn more?" Mia says, "Yes, please. Can you teach me more?" Ben says, "Sure, I can. Come, let's sit on the bench and I'll show you." Ben and Mia sit on the bench and Ben shows her his books and his shows on his mom's phone. They learn about their body and how it works. They learn that they have a brain, a spine, a stomach, a liver, a kidney, and many more. They learn that their body is amazing and that they should take care of it. They have so much fun learning together that they don't notice the time. Soon, their moms come to get them and say it's time to go home. Ben and Mia hug and say goodbye. They say, "You are my new friend. I hope to see you again soon." They wave and smile as they leave the park. They are happy that they met each other and that they learned new things. They think that learning is fun and that friendly is good. They look forward to the next day and what they will learn and do.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a little bird named Spot. Spot had fine, pretty wings. He loved to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big bird who could not fly. "Hi, big bird! Why can't you fly?" Spot asked. The big bird looked sad and said, "My wings are hurt. I can't fly and play like you." Spot wanted to help the big bird. He thought and thought. Then he had an idea! "I will find help for you, big bird!" Spot said. He flew away to find his friends. Spot found his friends and told them about the big bird. They all went to help the big bird. They pushed and pushed, and the big bird was able to fly again! The big bird was so happy and thanked Spot and his friends. The moral of the story is: when we help others, we can make their lives better and feel happy too.
