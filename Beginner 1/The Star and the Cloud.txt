Category: Beginner
Title: The Star and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a little star. The star loved to spin in the sky. One day, the star saw a big, filthy cloud. The cloud was sad and dirty. The star said, "Why are you so filthy, cloud?" The cloud replied, "I don't know how to be clean like you, star." The star wanted to help the cloud. The star thought and said, "Spin with me, cloud! The wind will make you clean." The cloud started to spin with the star. As they spun together, the wind blew the dirt away. The cloud became clean and white. They both laughed and kept spinning in the sky.

Once upon a time, in a far away land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside and raise his kite high up in the sky. He liked to watch it fly and dance with the wind. The land was big and green, with many trees and flowers. One day, while Tim was playing with his kite, he saw a big, scary shadow. It made him feel afraid. He did not know what it was, so he decided to go and see. He walked and walked until he found a big, scary tree. Tim was brave, so he walked up to the tree and said, "Hello, scary tree. I am not afraid of you." The tree was surprised and started to laugh. It was not a scary tree after all. It was a kind, friendly tree that just looked scary. Tim and the tree became good friends, and they played together every day.

Tim and Mia like to play knights and dragons. They have a big box with costumes and toys. Tim puts on a helmet and a sword. Mia puts on a dress and a shield. They go to the garden and look for a dragon. "Where is the dragon?" Tim asks. "I don't know. Maybe he is hiding behind the tree," Mia says. They march to the tree and peek behind it. They see a big, green lizard. It is not a dragon, but it looks scary. It has sharp teeth and a long tail. "Roar!" the lizard says. "Ah!" Tim and Mia scream. They run away from the lizard. Mia holds her shield in front of her. Tim swings his sword in the air. "Help! Help!" they shout. Mom hears them and comes to the garden. She sees the lizard and laughs. "Don't be afraid. That is not a dragon. That is Leo. He is our neighbor's pet. He is very friendly and healthy. He likes to sunbathe in the garden," Mom says. She picks up Leo and shows him to Tim and Mia. They touch his scales and his tail. He licks their fingers. "He is not scary. He is cute," Mia says. "Can we play with him?" Tim asks. "Sure, but be gentle. He is a living creature, not a toy. And don't forget to put him back in his cage when you are done," Mom says. Tim and Mia nod. They take Leo to their box and dress him up as a dragon. They play knights and dragons with him. They have a lot of fun. Leo likes to play with them too. He roars and wags his tail. He is a good dragon.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm with his mom. They saw a lot of animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. Tim loved to watch the animals play and run around. He saw a big pile of hay and thought it would be fun to pick some up. Tim went to the pile of hay and started to pick it up with his hands. As he picked up the hay, he heard a tiny voice say, "Hey! That's my home!" Tim looked around and saw a little mouse with a crazy hat on its head. The mouse looked very sad. Tim said, "I'm sorry, little mouse. I didn't know this was your home." The mouse looked at Tim and said, "It's okay. I can help you pick up the hay, and we can make a new home for me." Tim and the mouse worked together to pick up the hay. They made a new home for the mouse, and Tim even made a tiny hat for the mouse to wear. They became good friends, and Tim learned that even the smallest animals can be the best of friends.
