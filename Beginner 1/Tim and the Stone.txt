Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Stone

Once upon a time, a little red bird named Tim lived in a big tree. One day, Tim saw a pretty blue stone on the ground. He wanted to give it to his friend, Sue the squirrel. But Tim was worried. He did not know if Sue would like the blue stone. Tim went to his friend, Max the cat. He asked, "Max, do you think Sue will like my blue stone?" Max said, "I don't know, Tim. You must decide." Tim thought and thought. He wanted to make Sue happy. At last, Tim decided to give Sue the blue stone. He took it to her and said, "Sue, I have a gift for you." When Sue saw the blue stone, her eyes got big. She said, "Oh, Tim! I love it! Thank you!" Tim was not worried anymore. He was happy that Sue liked her gift.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because his mom said they were going to the shop today. He wanted to buy a new toy. At the shop, Tim saw many new toys. He saw cars, dolls, and balls. He couldn't decide which one he wanted. He picked up a toy car and started to play with it. But then, he dropped the car and it made a loud noise. Tim felt embarrass because everyone looked at him. His mom came over and gave him a hug. She said, "It's okay, Tim. Everyone makes mistakes." Tim smiled and picked up the toy car again. He decided that was the new toy he wanted. They went to the counter and bought the car. Tim was happy and couldn't wait to play with his new toy at home.

Lily and Tom are twins. They like to play together. But sometimes they make trouble. One day, they want to help Mom serve lunch. Mom is busy in the kitchen. She tells them to wait in the living room. But Lily and Tom do not listen. They sneak into the kitchen. They see a big pot of soup on the stove. They want to taste it. They grab two spoons and dip them into the pot. But the soup is very hot. They burn their tongues. They drop the spoons and spill the soup. They start to cry. Mom hears the noise and runs into the kitchen. She sees the mess and the twins. She is angry and worried. She says, "Lily and Tom, what have you done? You made a big trouble. You could have hurt yourselves. You should not touch the stove. It is dangerous. You should listen to Mom. Go to your room now. No lunch for you." Lily and Tom feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We wanted to help you. We wanted to taste the soup. We did not know it was hot. Please forgive us. We love you, Mom." Mom hugs them and says, "I love you too, Lily and Tom. But you have to be careful and follow the rules. Now go to your room and take a nap. You are sleepy. Maybe later you can have some soup. But only if you behave." Lily and Tom nod and go to their room. They are sleepy. They lie down on their beds and close their eyes. They dream of soup and Mom. They hope Mom is not mad at them. They hope they can have some soup later. They hope they can play again. But next time, they will not make trouble.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his chubby dog, Spot, went to the park. They saw a girl with a big ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he asked the girl, "Can I play with your ball?" The girl just shrugged and said, "Okay." Tim and Spot played with the ball, but then the ball flew high in the air and got stuck in a tree. They didn't know how to get it down. Tim put his hands on his waist and thought hard. Spot barked at the tree, but the ball didn't move. Just then, a bird flew by and saw the ball in the tree. The bird grabbed the ball with its beak and flew down to Tim and Spot. They didn't expect the bird to help them! Tim and Spot were very happy, and they said "Thank you" to the bird. Then they played with the ball again, making sure it didn't get stuck in the tree.
