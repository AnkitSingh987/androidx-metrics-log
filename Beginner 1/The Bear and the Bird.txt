Category: Beginner
Title: The Bear and the Bird

Once there was a bear. The bear was big and fair. He lived in the woods. He liked to play with his friends. They would run and jump all day. One day, the bear lost his toy. The toy was small and red. He looked for it under the trees. He looked for it by the river. But he could not find it. The bear felt sad. The bear sat down and let out a sigh. His friend, the bird, saw him. The bird asked, "Why are you sad?" The bear told the bird about his lost toy. The bird said, "I will help you find it." They looked together. Soon, they found the toy under a big rock. The bear was happy again. They played together all day.

Tom loves rockets. He has a toy rocket that he plays with every day. He makes it fly in the air and zoom around the room. He pretends it goes to the moon and the stars. One day, Tom goes to the park with his mom. He sees a big rocket on a stand. It is red and white and shiny. It has a sign that says "Space Camp". Tom thinks it is very pretty. He wants to see it up close. He runs to the rocket and touches it. It feels smooth and cold. He looks inside the window. He sees buttons and screens and seats. He wishes he could go inside and fly it. He hears a loud noise. He turns around and sees a man with a microphone. He says "Countdown to launch in 10, 9, 8...". Tom gets scared. He thinks the rocket is going to fly away. He runs back to his mom and hugs her. He watches the rocket soar into the sky. It makes a big trail of smoke and fire. It goes higher and higher until it is a tiny dot. Tom feels amazed and excited. He claps his hands and cheers. He says "Wow, mom, did you see that? That was the best rocket ever!"

Once upon a time, there was a thin king. He lived in a big castle. The castle was very tall and strong. The king liked to play with his friends inside the castle. One day, the king and his friends played a fun game. They ran and jumped all around the castle. They laughed and had a great time. The king was very happy. But soon, the sun went down. It was time to end the game. The king and his friends said good night. They went to sleep in their warm beds. The castle was quiet and peaceful.

One day, a wide frog named Bob saw a shiny crown in the mud. He put it on his head and hopped away. He felt very happy and proud. Bob met a small bug named Tim. Tim asked, "Can I wear the crown too?" Bob said, "No, I found it. It's mine." Tim felt sad and walked away. Later, Bob saw Tim crying. He guessed that Tim was still sad about the crown. Bob thought about it and decided to share the crown with Tim. They both wore it and played together. Now, they were both happy and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat jam on his bread. One day, he saw a big jar of jam on the top shelf in the kitchen. He wanted to eat it, but he was too small to reach it. Tim had an idea. He found a chair and tried to push it to the shelf. He pushed very hard, but the chair was too heavy. He asked his sister, "Can you help me push the chair, please?" His sister said, "No, Tim. You should not eat jam without asking mom." Tim was sad and angry. He pushed the chair with all his strength. The chair moved, but it made a harsh sound. The sound was so loud that it scared Tim. He started to cry. Mom heard the noise and came into the kitchen. She saw Tim crying and the chair near the shelf. She knew what happened. Mom said, "Tim, you should not try to get the jam by yourself. It is not safe." She put the jam away and did not give Tim any. Tim was very sad and learned that he should always ask for help.
