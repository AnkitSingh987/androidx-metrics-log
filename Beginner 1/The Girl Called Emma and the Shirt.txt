Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Emma and the Shirt

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emma. Emma was very clumsy and she was always spilling things on her shirt. Her mum was getting worried, so she took some special polish and told Emma she should polish her shirt so it could be nice and clean again. "But that's scary!" said Emma. She didn't want to touch the polish because it was smelly. "Don't worry," said mummy. "it won't hurt you, I promise. It will just make your shirt look nice and sparkly". So Emma bravely picked up the polish and began to rub it into her shirt. She was surprised at how shiny it looked and her shirt was almost as clean as it was when she first put it on. "See," said mummy, "I told you it would be ok." Emma was glad that her shirt was so shiny. From then on she always remembered to polish her shirt when it got dirty.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emma. She was three years old and loved muffins. One day, her mom put an ugly muffin on the table for her. Emma said: "Mom, that's an ugly muffin!" Her mom just smiled and said: "Yes, it is an ugly muffin, but you can still eat it if you want!" So Emma picked up the ugly muffin and started to eat it. She really liked it, even though it was ugly! From that day on, Emma always wanted an ugly muffin when she was hungry.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a king. The king had a shiny crown. He was a fast king, always running and playing. One day, the king went to play with his friends. He ran so fast that his crown fell off. He did not see it fall. His friends did not see it fall. The crown was lost. A small dog found the crown. The dog put the crown on his head. The dog was now the king. The old king and his friends looked for the crown. They found the dog king. The dog king was nice and wanted to play. So, they all played together and had fun. The old king was happy to have a new friend and let the dog remain the king.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Sam. Sam was walking in the woods one sunny day. He saw a hidden path on the side of the trees. Sam was very curious, so he decided to go down the path. As Sam walked down the path, he met a big bear. The bear said, "Hi! I'm Ben. What's your name?" Sam told him his name and they became friends. They played together all day long. When it was dark, they heard a loud noise that made them both jump. "What was that?" asked Sam. "I don't know," said Ben. They decided to find out what made the noise. They walked together and saw a small owl. The owl said, "I'm sorry I scared you. I just wanted to say hello." Sam, Ben, and the owl became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a broken toy. Tim was sad. He wanted to fix it. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me fix my toy?" Tim said. His mom knelt down beside him. She looked at the broken toy. "I will try my best, Tim," she said. That night, Tim had a dream. In his dream, he met a friendly ghost. The ghost said, "Hi, Tim! I can help you fix your toy." Tim was happy. The ghost waved its hand, and the toy was fixed. Tim woke up the next morning. He looked at his toy. It was still broken. He felt sad. But then, he remembered the ghost in his dream. He smiled and thought, "Maybe I can fix it too." Tim tried to fix his toy. With a little help from his mom, they fixed it together. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" And they played with the fixed toy all day.
