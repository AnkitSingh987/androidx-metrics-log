Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Spot

Ben and Lily were playing in the park with their dog, Spot. They liked to run, jump, and slide on the grass and the swings. Spot liked to chase the birds and the squirrels. "Look, Ben, a big stick!" Lily said, pointing to a twig on the ground. "Let's play fetch with Spot!" "OK, Lily, but be careful. Don't throw it too hard or too far," Ben said. He picked up the twig and waved it in front of Spot. Spot wagged his tail and barked. "Ready, Spot? Go get it!" Ben said, and threw the twig across the field. Spot ran after it as fast as he could. He caught the twig in his mouth and brought it back to Ben and Lily. "Good boy, Spot! You're so smart and fast!" Lily said, and hugged Spot. Spot licked her face and wagged his tail some more. "Let's do it again, Ben!" Lily said, and took the twig from Spot. "OK, Lily, but only one more time. Then we have to go home and nap," Ben said. He knew that Mom and Dad would be waiting for them, and that they would be tired after playing so much. "OK, Ben, one more time," Lily said, and threw the twig again. Spot ran after it again, but this time he did not come back right away. Ben and Lily waited and waited, but they did not see Spot. "Where is Spot, Ben? Did he get lost?" Lily asked, worried. "I don't know, Lily. Maybe he found something else to play with. Let's go look for him," Ben said, and took Lily's hand. They walked across the field, calling Spot's name. They found Spot under a big tree, lying on a pile of leaves. He had the twig in his mouth, but he was not playing with it. He was napping. "Spot, wake up! It's time to go home!" Ben said, and shook Spot gently. Spot opened his eyes and yawned. He saw Ben and Lily and wagged his tail. He got up and stretched. "Sorry, Ben, Lily. I was sleepy. The leaves were soft and warm. And the twig was tasty," Spot said, in his doggy way. "It's OK, Spot. We're sleepy too. You're so adorable when you nap," Lily said, and kissed Spot on the nose. "Come on, Spot. Let's go home and nap some more. Mom and Dad will be happy to see us," Ben said, and took the twig from Spot. "OK, Ben, Lily. Let's go home. I love you," Spot said, and followed them. They walked back to the park gate, holding hands and the twig. They were happy and tired. They had a lot of fun playing with the twig. And they loved their dog, Spot. He was their best friend.

One day, a curious little bunny named Tim went out to play. He hopped and jumped around the green ground. Tim liked to gather pretty flowers and soft leaves. He wanted to make a nice gift for his mom. As he was hopping, Tim saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a tiny ant. The ant looked sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, little ant?" The ant said, "I lost my food. I can't find it." Tim wanted to help the ant. They looked around the ground together. They found the ant's food under a small rock. The ant was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim and the ant became good friends. They played and gathered things together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy phone. One day, he found a box of powder in the kitchen. He thought it would be fun to play with it. Tim took the powder and his toy phone to his room. He pretended to ring his friend, Sam. "Hi Sam, do you want to play with my powder?" Tim asked. Sam, who was very grateful, said, "Yes, Tim! I would love to play with your powder!" But Tim didn't know that the powder was not for playing. It was for cooking. When his mom came into the room, she saw the mess. She was very sad and told Tim, "You should not play with things that are not yours." Tim felt bad for making his mom sad. The moral of the story is: always ask before using things that are not yours.
