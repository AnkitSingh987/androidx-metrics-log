Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Wagon

One day, a little boy named Tim found an old red wagon in his yard. He wanted to play with it. He put his toys in the wagon and pulled it around. The wagon made a loud noise as it moved, but Tim was happy. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and yelled, "Sue, come play with my old wagon!" Sue ran to Tim and they took turns pulling the wagon. They laughed and had a lot of fun. Soon, the sun began to set. Tim's mom called out, "Time to go inside!" Tim and Sue said goodbye. They put the old wagon away and went inside for dinner. Tim couldn't wait to play with the wagon again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Tim was playing in the sand. The sand was rough on his toes, but he didn't mind. As Tim was playing, a big dog came near him. The dog said, "Woof, woof! Can I play with you?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played and had fun in the sand. After a while, Tim's mom came outside. She saw that the dog had disturbed the sand. "Oh no, the sand is all messy!" she said. Tim and the dog looked at each other and laughed. They knew they had a fun day, even if the sand was rough and messy.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw his shadow on the ground. He was very happy and started to clap. His shadow clapped with him. They played together all day. "Hello, shadow!" said Tim. "Do you want to play with me?" The shadow nodded and they played together. But then, the sun went away and the sky became dark. Tim's shadow was gone. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. He missed his shadow and wanted to find it. "Where did my shadow go?" Tim asked his mom. She told him that the shadow would come back when the sun came out again. The next day, the sun was shining bright. Tim went outside and saw his shadow again. He clapped and his shadow clapped too. They were both happy to play together again.

Once upon a time there was a black cat named Coco. Coco loved to take trips to the park. One day, Coco was feeling a bit tired, so he decided to rest by a big tree. He was so tired that he dozed off and stayed there for a little while. When Coco awoke, he was so happy to see a new bird had landed on the tree. The bird was so tiny and black! Coco watched the bird for a few moments and the bird looked back at Coco with a friendly little nod. The bird flew off but left a message for Coco: "Someone will come to restore the park soon". Coco was very excited and knew he had to tell all of his friends. He quickly ran around telling all of his furry friends about the good news. Soon after, a big truck drove up to the park and workers hopped out with a few tools. They started to work and Coco could see the park being restored back to life. It was like a totally new park! Coco was so pleased and began to take trips to the park every day.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big green hill. They want to climb it. "Let's go to the hill!" Tom says. "Maybe we can find something fun." "OK!" Mia says. "But be careful. The hill is very high." They run to the hill. They start to climb. They see many rocks and flowers. They hear many birds and bugs. They have fun. When they reach the top, they see a big bell. The bell is shiny and has a rope. Tom and Mia are curious. "What is this?" Tom asks. "I don't know," Mia says. "Maybe it is a toy." "Let's try it!" Tom says. He pulls the rope. The bell rings loud. It makes a mighty sound. Tom and Mia are happy. They laugh and clap. But then they hear a roar. They look around. They see a big dragon. The dragon is green and has wings and teeth. The dragon is angry. The bell is his. "Who rang my bell?" the dragon says. "Who woke me up?" Tom and Mia are scared. They hide behind the bell. "Sorry, Mr. Dragon," Tom says. "We didn't know it was your bell. We just wanted to play." "Play?" the dragon says. "You can't play with my bell. It is mine. Go away!" The dragon breathes fire. The fire is hot and red. Tom and Mia are very scared. They run down the hill. They don't look back. They reach the park. They are safe. They hug each other. "Let's never go to the hill again," Mia says. "OK," Tom says. "But it was fun." They smile and go home. They don't hear the bell anymore.
