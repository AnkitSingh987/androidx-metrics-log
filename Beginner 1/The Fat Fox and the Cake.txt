Category: Beginner
Title: The Fat Fox and the Cake

One day, a fat fox went for a walk in the woods. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. The fat fox was happy. The fat fox saw a big tree with red apples. He wanted to eat them. He tried to jump, but he was too fat. The fat fox was sad. He walked away to find something else to eat. Then, the fat fox found a small house. He went inside and saw a big cake. He ate the cake and was very happy. But he heard someone coming. It was the owner of the house. The fat fox ran back to the woods. He returned to the big tree with the red apples. He was still too fat to jump, but he was happy because he had eaten the cake.

Once upon a time, there was a playful driver named Tom. Tom loved to drive his big car. He would drive it all day and all night. Tom's friends told him he needed to sleep, but he didn't listen. He just wanted to keep driving and having fun. One sunny day, Tom was driving his car very fast. He saw a big hill and wanted to go over it. As he went up the hill, Tom started to feel very sleepy. He tried to stay awake, but his eyes closed. As Tom's car went over the hill, something unexpected happened. Tom's car started to fly! It went up, up, up into the sky. Tom was still sleeping and didn't know what was happening. The car flew higher and higher until it reached the clouds. Then, it started to fall down, down, down. Tom's car crashed on the ground with a big boom. Tom woke up, but it was too late. The playful driver and his big car were broken. Tom should have listened to his friends and gotten some sleep.

One morning, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The park had a big, modern playground. Tim saw a friend named Sue playing there too. "Hi Sue, let's spin on the round thing!" Tim said. Sue smiled and they both climbed on it. They spun around and around, laughing and having fun. After a while, they stopped spinning and went to play on the swings. Tim and Sue had a great morning at the park, and they couldn't wait to come back and spin again.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and run in the park. One day, they go to church with their mom and dad. They see a big black cat sitting on the roof of the church. The cat looks at them and meows. "Look, a cat!" Sara says. "Can we pet it?" "No, we can't," Ben says. "It is too high. And it is a black cat. Black cats are bad luck." Sara does not believe Ben. She thinks the cat is cute and wants to be friends. She sees a ladder leaning on the wall of the church. She runs to the ladder and starts to climb. "Stop, Sara!" Ben shouts. "It is not safe. Come down!" But Sara does not listen. She wants to reach the cat. She climbs higher and higher. The cat sees her and hisses. It does not want to be bothered. It jumps off the roof and runs away. Sara is sad. She looks down and sees Ben and her mom and dad. They look scared and angry. She tries to climb down, but the ladder is old and weak. It cannot weigh Sara. It breaks and falls. Sara falls too. She hits the ground hard and hurts herself. She cries and bleeds. Mom and dad run to Sara and hug her. They call for help. Ben runs to Sara too and says he is sorry. He wishes he could make her better. He wishes she had listened to him. He wishes they had never seen the black cat.

One day, a big ant named Andy wanted to gather food for his ant friends. He went to a large tree with many leaves. Under the tree, he found a network of tiny bugs that were also looking for food. Andy said to the tiny bugs, "Will you help me gather food for my ant friends?" The tiny bugs agreed to help Andy. Together, they all worked to gather food from the large tree. As they gathered food, they became good friends. Andy and the tiny bugs made a big pile of food for the ant friends. They were happy and proud of their work. In the end, they all shared the food and had a great feast.
