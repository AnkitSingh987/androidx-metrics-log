Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a new toy car that he loved to play with. One day, Tim's mom made him a yummy sandwich for lunch. She told him, "Tim, don't forget to eat your sandwich before you play with your new toy car." Tim was so excited to play with his car that he forgot to eat his sandwich. He played and played until he was very hungry. He looked for his sandwich, but it was gone. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, where is my sandwich?" His mom said, "I told you not to forget to eat it. Now it's too late, and your sandwich is gone." Tim felt sad that he didn't listen to his mom. He learned that it's important to listen and not forget what your parents tell you. The next time his mom made him a sandwich, Tim remembered to eat it before playing. He felt happy and full, and he played even better with his toy car. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and not forget what they tell you.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy toys for Tim's birthday. Tim saw a big, expensive toy car. He asked his mom, "Can I get this car, please?" His mom looked at the price and said, "No, Tim. That car is too expensive. We can get a smaller car for you." Tim was sad, but he picked a small car. When they got home, Tim played with his new car. He pushed the car fast, and it went zoom! But then, something unexpected happened. Smoke came out of the car! Tim's mom saw the smoke and said, "Oh no, Tim! The small car is not good. It makes smoke. We must take it back to the store." They went back to the store, but it was closed. Tim and his mom were sad. They could not get a new car, and the birthday was not fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a long strip of bark hanging down. Tim wanted to poke the strip, so he asked his mom, "Can I poke the strip, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can, but be gentle." Tim poked the strip softly, and it moved a little. He felt happy and laughed. Then, Tim saw a small bug on the strip. He didn't want to poke it, so he just watched it crawl. The bug went into a tiny hole in the tree. Tim looked around and saw an empty cup on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, mom, an empty cup!" His mom said, "Good job, Tim. Let's throw it in the trash so the park stays clean." Tim threw the cup away and felt proud. They played at the park for a while and then went home. Tim told his dad about the strip, the bug, and the empty cup. His dad smiled and said, "You had a fun day at the park!" Tim nodded and said, "Yes, I did!"

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a raft. He liked to play with his raft in the water. One day, Tim went to the river with his raft. As he played, he met a frog. The frog said, "Hi, can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes, but you must be honest. We can play together if you are honest." The frog agreed. They played on the raft together. Suddenly, the frog jumped high and touched the sky. The frog became a big, kind bird. Tim was surprised. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim, for being honest and kind. Now we can fly together." And they flew away, happy and free.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they go to the library with their moms. The library has many books and toys for kids. Lily and Max see a big table with crayons and paper. They want to draw pictures. "Can we draw, Mom?" Lily asks. "Sure, honey. But remember to share with Max and the other kids, okay?" Mom says. Lily nods and runs to the table. She picks up a red crayon and a paper. She starts to draw a flower. Max follows her and sits next to her. He picks up a blue crayon and a paper. He starts to draw a car. Lily and Max are happy. They show each other their pictures. They say, "Wow, that's nice!" and "I like your picture!" They smile and give each other high fives. But then, a boy comes to the table. He sees Lily's red crayon and wants it. He grabs it from Lily's hand and says, "Mine!" Lily is sad and angry. She says, "No, that's mine! Give it back!" The boy says, "No, I want it! Go away!" Lily and Max are upset. They don't like the boy. They say, "That's not nice! You have to share!" The boy says, "No, I don't! I'm independent! I do what I want!" The boy starts to draw with the red crayon. He scribbles over Lily's flower. He laughs and says, "Ha ha, look at your flower now!" Lily cries. She says, "You ruined my picture! You're mean!" Max hugs Lily and says, "Don't cry, Lily. I'll help you. We can draw a new picture together." Max gives Lily his blue crayon and says, "Here, you can have mine. I'll use another color." Lily smiles and says, "Thank you, Max. You're a good friend." Lily and Max draw a new picture. They draw a rainbow with many colors. They share the crayons and the paper. They are happy again. The boy sees their picture and says, "Hey, that's a nice picture. Can I join you?" Lily and Max look at each other. They say, "Maybe, if you say sorry and share with us." The boy thinks for a moment. He says, "Okay, I'm sorry. I was rude. Can I share with you?" Lily and Max nod and say, "Sure, you can. But you have to be nice and respectful." The boy says, "Okay, I will. Thank you." The boy sits with Lily and Max. They give him a crayon and a paper. They draw together. They talk and laugh. They become friends. The moms see them and smile. They say, "Good job, kids. You learned how to share and be independent. We're proud of you."
