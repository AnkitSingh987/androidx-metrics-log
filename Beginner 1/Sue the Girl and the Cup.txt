Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cup

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing with her toy cup. She liked to pretend it was full of tea. She would say, "Care for some tea?" to her stuffed animals. Then, her big brother Tom came into the room. He saw Sue playing and said, "Care for some real tea?" Sue was excited and said, "Yes, please!" Tom went to the kitchen and filled the cup with real tea. He was very careful not to spill. But when Tom came back, he tripped on a hard toy and the tea spilled all over the floor. Sue was sad, but Tom said, "Don't worry, I'll clean it up." After he cleaned the mess, they both laughed and played together with Sue's toy cup.

Sam liked to bounce. He bounced on his bed, on the sofa, on the floor. He bounced with his ball, with his teddy, with his pillow. He bounced all day long. One day, Sam saw a big dish on the table. It was shiny and round and had a lid. Sam wondered what was inside. He climbed on a chair and reached for the dish. He lifted the lid and saw a cake. It was brown and sweet and had nuts on top. Sam wanted to eat the cake. Sam took the dish and bounced down from the chair. He bounced to the sofa and put the dish on the cushion. He bounced to the kitchen and got a fork. He bounced back to the sofa and opened the dish. He cut a big piece of cake and put it on his fork. He bounced the fork to his mouth and ate the cake. It was yummy. But Sam ate too much cake. His tummy felt full and heavy. He could not bounce anymore. He felt sick and sleepy. He lay down on the sofa and closed his eyes. He forgot about the dish and the cake. Mom came home and saw the dish on the sofa. She saw the cake with a big hole. She saw Sam with crumbs on his face. She was angry and sad. She said, "Sam, you are a bad boy. You ate the cake that I made for Dad. You did not ask me. You did not share. You made a mess. You are not fit to bounce." She took the dish and the cake and put them away. She cleaned the sofa and the floor. She picked up Sam and put him in his bed. She said, "You need to rest and say sorry. Maybe then you can bounce again."

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. It looks fun and attractive. They want to go on the slide. "Can we go on the slide, please?" Lily asks her mom. "Sure, but you need to wear the equipment first," her mom says. She gives them helmets and pads. They are for safety. "Thank you, mom," Lily says. She puts on the equipment. Ben does the same. They run to the slide. They take turns to slide down. They laugh and shout. They have a lot of fun. "Can we go again, please?" Ben asks his dad. "Okay, but only one more time," his dad says. He smiles and watches them. Lily and Ben go on the slide again. They are happy. They love the slide and the equipment. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a lot of wheat. The wheat was very pretty and tall. In the field, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play near the wheat. One day, a dependable dog named Max came to the field. Max said, "Hello, Lily! Can I play with you?" Lily was happy and said, "Yes, Max! Let's play together!" So, Lily and Max played near the wheat all day. As the sun went down, Lily said, "Thank you, Max, for playing with me. You are a good friend." Max smiled and said, "You are welcome, Lily. I will always be here to play with you." And from that day on, Lily and Max were the best of friends, always playing near the pretty wheat.
