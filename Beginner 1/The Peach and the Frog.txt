Category: Beginner
Title: The Peach and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a peach. This peach was very big and lived on a tree. The tree was in a boring garden with no friends to play with. One sunny day, the peach fell off the tree. It rolled and rolled until it met a happy frog. The frog liked to dance and play all day long. The peach and the frog became good friends. They danced and played together in the boring garden. Soon, the garden was not boring anymore. The peach and the frog were very happy.

Ben and Mia were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to run and jump and slide and swing. They saw some big dogs with their owners. Ben and Mia liked dogs, but they were careful not to touch them without asking. "Look, mom, that dog looks like a wolf!" Ben said, pointing to a gray and black dog with a bushy tail. "That's not a wolf, Ben, that's a husky. It's a kind of dog that can live in very cold places. Wolves are wild animals that live in the woods or the mountains. They are not pets," mom explained. "What do wolves do, mom?" Mia asked. "Wolves live in packs, which are like big families. They hunt for food together, and they support each other. They make sounds like howls to talk to each other. Sometimes they can be friendly to people, but sometimes they can be dangerous. That's why we should never go near them or feed them," mom said. "Wow, wolves are amazing!" Ben said. "I want to see a wolf, mom!" Mia said. "Maybe someday we can go to a zoo or a sanctuary and see some wolves from a safe distance. But for now, let's enjoy the park and the dogs that are here. They are also amazing, and they love to play with us," mom said. Ben and Mia nodded and ran to the dog area. They asked the owners if they could pet the dogs. They made some new furry friends. They learned their names and their breeds. They had fun with the dogs, but they also remembered what mom said about wolves. They knew they were wild and different, and they respected them.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a funny dog named Whistle. Whistle loved to play with his friends all day. He liked to run, jump, and whistle. Whistle was a very happy dog. One day, Whistle went to school with his friend, a little girl named Lily. At school, they learned about math. Math was counting and adding numbers. Whistle and Lily had fun learning math together. As they played, Whistle and Lily shared their math toys with their friends. They learned that sharing made everyone happy. The moral of the story is that sharing is caring, and it brings joy to everyone.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim wanted to play and pick flowers. He saw a big tree with pretty flowers. He ran to the tree and started to pick the flowers. Tim's mom said, "It's time for tea." She had a nice cup of tea for Tim. But when Tim took a sip, the tea was very cold. Tim did not like cold tea. He wanted his tea to be warm. Tim felt sad. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She wanted to make him happy. She put the tea in a warm cup and gave it back to Tim. Tim took a sip and smiled. The tea was warm now. Tim and his mom had a great day at the park.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys. Ben has a jeep. Mia has a doll. They share their toys and have fun. One day, Ben and Mia go to the park with their moms. They see a big hill. They want to climb the hill. They take their toys with them. Ben puts his jeep in his backpack. Mia holds her doll. They climb the hill. It is hard. They are tired. But they are glad. They see a lot of things. They see birds and flowers and clouds. They see the sun. The sun is bright. It makes everything glow. They reach the top of the hill. They are happy. They sit on the grass. They take out their toys. They play with their toys. They talk to each other. "Look, Mia, my jeep can go fast!" Ben says. He makes his jeep zoom on the grass. "Wow, Ben, your jeep is cool!" Mia says. She makes her doll wave at the jeep. "Look, Ben, my doll can sing!" Mia says. She presses a button on her doll. The doll sings a song. "Wow, Mia, your doll is pretty!" Ben says. He makes his jeep beep at the doll. They laugh. They hug. They are friends. They are glad. They glow.
