Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Door

Ben and Mia are playing with their toy guns in the garden. They like to pretend they are cowboys and robbers. Ben wears a hat and a badge. Mia wears a mask and a scarf. "Stop, robber! You are naughty!" Ben shouts. He points his toy gun at Mia. "No, you stop, cowboy! You are silly!" Mia shouts back. She points her toy gun at Ben. They run around the garden, shooting and dodging. They laugh and have fun. But then, they hear a loud bark. It is Rex, the big dog from next door. He has jumped over the fence and is running towards them. He looks angry and hungry. "Help, help!" Ben and Mia scream. They drop their toy guns and run to the house. Rex chases them, barking and growling. They reach the door and slam it shut. Rex scratches and bites at the door, but he can't get in. "Are you okay, kids?" Mom asks. She hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. "Yes, Mom, we are okay. But Rex is outside. He is scary and mean!" Ben says. "Rex is outside? Oh no, he must have escaped from his yard. I'll call his owner and tell him to come and get him. Don't worry, he won't hurt you." Mom says. She picks up the phone and dials a number. Ben and Mia hug each other and watch Rex through the window. "Mom, can we have a treat?" Mia asks. "We were brave and we ran away from Rex." "Yes, you can have a treat. You were very brave and smart. How about some cookies and milk?" Mom says. "Yay, cookies and milk!" Ben and Mia say. They smile and forget about Rex. They go to the kitchen and enjoy their treat. They decide to play something else later. Maybe they will be pirates and mermaids. Or astronauts and aliens. Or doctors and patients. They have many ideas and they love to play.

Lily liked to sing, but she was shy. She wanted to improve her voice, but she was afraid of what others would say. She only sang when she was alone in her room, with her teddy bear and her doll. One day, her mom told her that there was a talent show at school. She asked Lily if she wanted to join. Lily felt nervous, but she also felt curious. She wondered if she could sing in front of other people. She asked her mom, "Can I try?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, you can try. You have a beautiful voice, and I'm sure you can do well. But you have to practice, and you have to be brave. Do you have a friend who can help you?" Lily thought of her best friend, Ben. He was dependable and kind. He liked to sing, too, and he always encouraged her. She said, "Yes, I have Ben. He can help me." Her mom said, "That's great. Why don't you ask him to come over and practice with you? I'll make some cookies for you." Lily felt happy and excited. She called Ben and told him about the talent show. He said, "That sounds fun. I'll come over and we can sing together. What song do you want to sing?" Lily said, "I like the song about the rainbow. Do you know it?" Ben said, "Yes, I know it. It's a nice song. Let's sing it." They agreed to meet at Lily's house after school. Lily ran to her room and picked up her teddy bear and her doll. She said, "We're going to sing at the talent show. Are you ready?" They nodded and smiled. Lily hugged them and said, "You're my best friends. You always listen to me. Thank you for being here." She put them on her bed and waited for Ben. She hoped he would like her voice. She hoped they would have fun. She hoped they would do well at the talent show. She hoped she would not be shy. She hoped she would improve.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in a big forest. The tree gave shelter to many birds. One day, a little bird named Timmy came to live in the tree. Timmy loved to whistle. He whistled all day long. His bird friends liked his whistle and they all played together. One day, a big bird named Bobby came to the tree. Bobby was new and did not have any friends. Timmy said, "Hi! I am Timmy. Do you want to be my friend?" Bobby smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that." Timmy whistled a happy song, and they all played together under the tall tree. From that day on, the tree was full of happy whistling birds who lived together in the shelter of their tall tree home.
