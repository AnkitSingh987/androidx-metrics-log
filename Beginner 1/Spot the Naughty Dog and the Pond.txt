Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Naughty Dog and the Pond

One day, a naughty dog named Spot found some money on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to the park. At the park, there was a big pond. Spot saw his friend, a duck named Quack. Spot said, "Look, Quack! I found money!" Quack looked at the money and said, "Oh no, Spot! That's not yours. We need to give it back." But Spot didn't want to give it back. He wanted to play with it. They started to argue, and the money fell into the pond. Splash! The money was all wet. Spot and Quack were sad. They knew they had to get the money out of the water. Quack jumped into the pond and swam to the money. He picked it up with his beak and swam back to Spot. They decided to give the money back to its owner. They learned that it's not good to keep things that don't belong to them.

One day, a helpful goat went for a walk. The goat liked to joke and make friends. He saw a big tree and thought, "I want to climb that tree." The goat tried to climb the tree, but it was too hard. A little bird saw the goat and said, "Why are you trying to climb the tree, goat?" The goat said, "I want to see the top." The bird said, "I can help you see the top." The goat and the bird became friends. They played and joked all day. The bird flew high and told the goat what he saw. The goat was happy to have a helpful friend. They laughed and played until the sun went down.

One day, a boy named Tim found a card on the floor. Tim wanted to fit the card into his toy box, but it was too big. Tim asked his friend, Sam, for help. Sam tried to help Tim fit the card into the toy box, but it was still too big. They both felt bad because they could not make the card fit. Tim and Sam had an idea. They tried to cut the card into smaller pieces so it would fit into the toy box. But when they tried to cut the card, it broke into many tiny pieces. Tim and Sam were very sad that they broke the card. At the end of the day, Tim and Sam could not fix the card. They both felt bad because they wanted to keep the card. Tim said, "I wish we didn't break the card." Sam agreed, "Yes, it was a bad idea to cut it." The card was now gone, and Tim and Sam learned a lesson about trying to fit things where they don't belong.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She was a lazy princess. She liked to sleep all day. One day, she woke up late. She was very hungry. She went to the kitchen to find food. But there was no food left! The princess was sad. She had to go to the store to buy food. But it was cold outside. She put on her coat and went out. She started to shiver. The wind was very strong. The princess was scared. She wanted to go back home. But the princess knew she needed food. She was brave. She walked to the store. She bought food and went back home. She was still shivering. But she was happy. She had food now. The princess learned not to be lazy. She woke up early every day.

Lily loved to sing. She sang every day, in her room, in the garden, in the car. She sang to her dolls, her dog, her mom and dad. She sang happy songs, sad songs, silly songs. She wanted to sing for everyone. One day, her mom said, "Lily, you have an amazing voice. Do you want to sing at the school show? There will be a lot of people there. They will love to hear you sing." Lily was very excited. She said, "Yes, mom, I want to sing at the school show. Can I wear my pink dress and my shiny shoes?" "Of course, my dear. You will look beautiful. And you will sing beautifully, too. I am so proud of you." The day of the school show came. Lily was nervous, but also happy. She saw her mom and dad in the audience. They smiled and waved at her. She saw her teacher and her friends. They cheered and clapped for her. She felt brave and ready. She walked on the stage. She held the microphone. She looked at the people. She opened her mouth. She sang her favorite song. She sang with all her heart. She sang loud and clear. She sang like a star. The people listened. They were amazed. They loved her voice. They loved her song. They loved her. When she finished, they all stood up. They all applauded. They all shouted, "Bravo, Lily, bravo!" Lily felt very happy. She smiled and bowed. She thanked the people. She ran to her mom and dad. They hugged her and kissed her. They said, "You did great, Lily. You are amazing. We love you so much." Lily said, "I love you, too. And I love to sing. Can we go home and have some cream? I like cream. It is sweet and yummy." They laughed and said, "Yes, Lily, we can have some cream. You deserve it. You are our sweet and yummy girl."
