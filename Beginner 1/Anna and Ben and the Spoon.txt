Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Spoon

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play together. One day, they are in the kitchen with Mom. Mom is making soup for lunch. She lets Anna and Ben help her. "Can I stir the soup, Mom?" Anna asks. "Sure, honey. Here is a big spoon. Be careful, the soup is hot," Mom says. Anna takes the spoon and stirs the soup slowly. She smells the carrots, the potatoes, and the chicken. She thinks the soup will be yummy and healthy. "Can I stir the soup, too, Mom?" Ben asks. "Of course, sweetie. You can take turns with Anna. Stirring the soup is fun, right?" Mom says. Ben nods and waits for his turn. He watches Anna stir the soup. He sees the bubbles and the steam. He hears the soup make noises. He thinks the soup will be tasty and healthy. Mom smiles and hugs Anna and Ben. She is proud of her helpers. She says, "You are doing a great job, my loves. The soup is almost ready. Let's set the table and wash our hands." Anna and Ben put down the spoon and follow Mom. They are hungry and excited. They can't wait to eat the soup. They know it will make them strong and healthy. After lunch, Anna and Ben are full and happy. They thank Mom for the soup. They say, "We love you, Mom. You make the best soup ever." Mom says, "I love you, too, my darlings. You are the best helpers ever." Anna and Ben give Mom a big kiss. They go to their room and play with their toys. They are tired and sleepy. They decide to take a nap. They climb into their bed and cuddle. They close their eyes and dream of soup. They are warm and healthy.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play with hoops in the park. They roll them, spin them, and jump through them. They have fun with hoops. One day, they see a new hoop in the park. It is big and shiny and has stars on it. Sara and Ben want to play with it. They run to the hoop. "I saw it first!" Sara says. "No, I saw it first!" Ben says. They both grab the hoop and pull. They fight for the hoop. They are angry and loud. "Stop!" a voice says. "That is my hoop!" Sara and Ben look up. They see a boy. He is unknown to them. He has curly hair and a red shirt. He is holding a bag. "Who are you?" Sara asks. "I am Max. I just moved here. This is my hoop. I brought it from my old home. It is special to me." Max says. Sara and Ben feel sorry. They let go of the hoop. They say sorry to Max. "Can we play with you?" Ben asks. "Sure. I like to make new friends. Do you want to try my hoop? It can do cool things." Max says. He shows them how his hoop can light up, make sounds, and change colors. Sara and Ben are amazed. They take turns with the hoop. They play together. They have fun with the hoop. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to learn new things. One day, he wanted to go upstairs to find his toy car. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" As Tim went upstairs, he saw a foolish cat playing with a ball. The cat said, "Meow, do you want to play with me?" Tim thought the cat was funny but he wanted to find his toy car first. Tim found his toy car and played with the cat. They had so much fun together. Tim learned that even a foolish cat can be a good friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big festival in a small town. Everyone was happy and excited. They all wore new clothes and played fun games together. The festival had lots of colors and yummy food for everyone to enjoy. The kids laughed and danced all day long. In the town, there was a kind girl named Lily. She saw a little boy who was sad because his toy was broken. Lily wanted to help him. She had a toy just like his at home. She thought, "I can lend him my toy so he can be happy too." Lily ran home and got her toy. She gave it to the little boy and said, "Here, you can play with my toy." The little boy's face lit up with a big smile. He was so happy and thanked Lily for lending her toy. They played together and became good friends. The festival became even more fun because they shared and cared for each other.
