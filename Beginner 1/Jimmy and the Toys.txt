Category: Beginner
Title: Jimmy and the Toys

One day, Jimmy was playing with his toys on the couch. He was happy, jumping and giggling. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. Jimmy was scared, so he screamed and hugged the couch. He was so ashamed, he began to cry. His mom heard the noise and came to see what was happening. When she saw Jimmy, she knew he had broken her vase. She grabbed Jimmy and put him in his room. Jimmy was so scared he screamed louder. He cried until he fell asleep. That night, Jimmy was still ashamed.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he found a shiny copper car. It was amazing! He wanted to find another car to match it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Do you have a car to match my amazing copper car?" Sam said, "No, but let's look for one together!" They looked and looked but could not find a matching car. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl named Sue came over and said, "I have a car that matches your amazing copper car!" She showed them her car and it was a perfect match. Tim, Sam, and Sue played with their matching cars and had a great time.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. They saw a big dog with a ball in his mouth. The dog was friendly and wagged his tail. Tom and Lily wanted to play with the dog and his ball. "Can we play with you, dog?" Tom asked. The dog barked and dropped the ball. Tom and Lily picked up the ball and threw it. The dog ran after it and brought it back. They played for a long time and had fun. But then a mean boy came. He was bigger than Tom and Lily and had a bad face. He saw the dog and his ball and wanted to take them. He grabbed the ball from the dog's mouth and kicked the dog. The dog yelped and ran away. Tom and Lily were scared and angry. "Hey, that's not nice! Give the ball back to the dog!" Lily shouted. The mean boy laughed and threw the ball far away. He said, "You are stupid and the dog is stupid. I have the ball now and you can't have it. I have more muscle than you and the dog." Tom and Lily felt sad and wanted to help the dog. They looked around and saw a big stick. They had an idea. They ran to the stick and picked it up. They ran back to the mean boy and hit him with the stick. The mean boy dropped the ball and cried. He ran away and said, "Ow, ow, ow! You are mean and the stick is mean. I don't want the ball anymore. You are stronger than me and the stick." Tom and Lily smiled and picked up the ball. They looked for the dog and found him hiding behind a tree. He was shaking and had a hurt leg. Tom and Lily hugged him and said, "We are sorry, dog. We are your friends and we want to play with you. We will rescue you from the mean boy and take you to the vet. You are not stupid and you have more muscle than the mean boy." The dog licked their faces and wagged his tail. He was happy and grateful. Tom and Lily took the dog and his ball and walked to the vet. They helped the dog and played with him. They became best friends and never saw the mean boy again.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Tom. He was three years old and very obedient. He always listened to his mom. One day Tom's mom said to him, "It's time to get ready Tom. Put on your dress." So Tom went to his new dress in his room and put it on. He looked so handsome. Mom then said, "Now it's time to have fun!" She gave Tom his scooter and said, "Let's go to the park." Tom was so excited that he jumped up and down. He said, "Yay! Let's go!" Tom and his mom went to the park. Tom rode his scooter around and around. He had so much fun. After a while, it was time to go home. Tom obeyed and listened to his mom and went home. Tom's mom said, "Good job being so obedient, Tom." Tom smiled, feeling very happy and proud. He knew he was a good boy.
