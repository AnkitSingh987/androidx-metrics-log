Category: Beginner
Title: The Smart Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a smart bird. The bird lived in a cage. The cage was in a big tree. One day, the bird saw a key. The key was far away. The bird wanted to reach the key. The bird thought the key could open the cage. The smart bird used its beak and wings to reach the key. The bird got the key and opened the cage. The bird was happy and flew away.

Tom and Mia are playing with their toys. Tom has a big box of cars and trucks. Mia has a small bag of animals and dolls. Tom likes to match his cars and trucks by color and size. Mia likes to match her animals and dolls by sound and shape. "Look, Mia, I have a red car and a red truck. They match!" Tom says. "Look, Tom, I have a lion and a tiger. They match!" Mia says. They show each other their matches and smile. Then they look for more matches. Tom finds a blue car and a blue truck. Mia finds a monkey and a banana. They match! Tom finds a yellow car and a yellow truck. Mia finds a fish and a scale. They match! Tom finds a green car and a green truck. Mia finds a bird and a feather. They match! But then Tom runs out of cars and trucks. He has no more matches. He looks at Mia's bag. He sees a lot of animals and dolls. He wants to match them too. "Can I play with your animals and dolls, Mia?" Tom asks. "No, Tom, they are mine. You have your cars and trucks. Go play with them." Mia says. "But I have no more matches. Your animals and dolls are more fun. Give me some." Tom says. "No, Tom, go away. You are boring." Mia says. Tom gets angry. He grabs Mia's bag and pulls it. Mia holds on to her bag and screams. They fight over the bag. The bag rips. The animals and dolls fall out. The scale breaks. The feather flies away. The banana squishes. The fish flops. The monkey screeches. The lion roars. The tiger bites. Tom and Mia cry. They have no more matches. They have no more toys. They have no more fun. They have a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a big wide pond, there lived a happy frog named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends. They would jump, swim, and dive all day long. The pond was their favorite place to be. One day, Tim found a pretty wrap near the pond. It was soft and colorful. Tim thought it would be fun to play a game with the wrap. He called his friends to join him. They were all excited to play. Tim and his friends took turns diving into the pond with the wrap. They would wrap it around themselves and see who could swim the fastest. They laughed and splashed as they played their new game. It was a fun day at the wide pond.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to buy a toy. He went to the store with his mom to find the perfect one. He saw a red ball, a blue car, and a big bear. But then, he saw a small, poor bird in a cage. Tim felt sad for the bird and wanted to help it escape. Tim asked his mom if they could buy the bird and set it free. His mom said yes, so they took the bird home. Tim was excited to help the bird escape and find a new home. He opened the cage and the bird flew out. The bird was happy to be free, but then something unexpected happened. The bird flew away and got lost. It could not find its way back to its family. The bird was sad and wished it had stayed in the cage. Tim was sad too, because he thought he was helping the bird, but he made things worse.

Lila and Ben were playing on the beach. They liked to make sand castles and dig holes. They also liked to splash in the water and look for shells. Sometimes they saw big boats and planes in the sky. One day, they saw a big boat with a flag. It was red and white and blue. They heard a loud noise. It was a horn. The boat was saying hello. Lila and Ben wanted to say hello back. They looked for something to wave. Lila found a towel. It was pink and soft. She held it up and waved it. Ben found a fake. It was a plastic snake. He thought it was funny. He held it up and waved it. The boat saw them and waved back. Lila and Ben were happy. They laughed and clapped. They kept waving until the boat was far away. Then they went back to playing on the beach. They had a lot of fun. They liked to wave to boats and planes. They liked to use towels and fakes. They liked to be friends.
