Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bench

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mother. Her mother was very organized. She liked to keep everything clean and tidy. One day, Lily went to the park with her mother. They played on the swings and slides. They had lots of fun. After a while, Lily's mother went to sit on a bench to rest. Lily kept playing. Soon, Lily looked around and could not recognize her mother. She felt scared. But then, she saw her mother's organized bag on the bench. Lily knew the bag belonged to her mother. She ran to the bench and found her mother. They hugged and went home, happy to be together again.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. Tim loved to play and laugh with his friends. One day, they all decided to play a game of tag. As Tim ran around, he tried to catch his friends. He reached out and grabbed his friend Sarah by the waist. "Got you!" he said with a big smile. Sarah laughed and started to chase Tim. While they were playing, Tim saw a big bug. It scared him and he let out a scream. His friends heard him and came to see what was wrong. They all saw the bug and laughed. Tim felt better and they all went back to playing their fun game of tag.

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. It was a long way up. A boy named Tom and a girl named Sue wanted to go up the hill. They wanted to see what was on the other side. Tom said, "Let's take a short way up the hill." Sue said, "No, let's take a long way. It will be more fun." They took the long way. It was hard. They were tired. But they kept going up. As they went up, they saw a big rock. It was in the way. They had to push it. They pushed and pushed. The rock moved. They were happy. They kept going up. At last, they were at the top. They looked down and saw a beautiful lake. They were happy they took the long way.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Ted and Tom. Ted and Tom loved playing together in the park. On one sunny day, Ted and Tom were sitting under a tree. They looked up at the sky and saw a huge cloud in the blue sky. Ted said to Tom, “Wow! That cloud looks very sweet!” Tom smiled and said, “Yes - let’s imagine what it would be like to sit on the cloud!” The pair used their imaginations and tried to imagine what it would feel like to sit on the white, fluffy cloud. They thought it would be so soft and sweet. Ted and Tom looked up at the sky and pretended they were sitting on the cloud. They smiled and laughed. They could imagine that they were free as birds, floating on the sweet cloud in the sky.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his grandpa. His grandpa had a cane to help him walk. Tim was glad to spend time with his grandpa. They walked to a farm where they saw a man. The man was working hard. He was trying to yield fruits from a tree. Tim and his grandpa wanted to help. So, Tim, his grandpa, and the man worked together. They used the cane to reach the fruits. They were all glad to have yummy fruits to eat. They had a fun day at the farm.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big hill. They want to climb it. "Look, Ben, a hill!" Lily says. "Let's go up!" "OK, Lily, but be careful!" Ben says. "The hill is steep and the grass is slippery." They hold hands and start to climb. They take small steps and look for rocks to hold on to. They see flowers and bugs and birds. They are happy. But then, Lily sees something shiny on the hill. She lets go of Ben's hand and runs to it. "Lily, wait!" Ben shouts. "Don't run!" But Lily does not hear him. She runs faster and faster. She wants to see the shiny thing. It is a balloon. A red balloon. She reaches the balloon and grabs it. But the balloon is tied to a stick. The stick is stuck in the ground. Lily pulls hard, but the balloon does not come out. She pulls harder and harder. "Pop!" The balloon bursts. Lily is sad. She drops the stick and the balloon. She looks around. She does not see Ben. She does not see the way down. She is scared. "Ben! Ben!" She cries. "Where are you?" Ben hears Lily's cry. He looks up and sees her. She is far away. He is worried. "Lily! Lily!" He calls. "Don't move! I'm coming!" He climbs up the hill. He takes big steps and looks for Lily. He sees the stick and the balloon. He sees Lily. She is crying. He reaches her and hugs her. He says, "Lily, I'm here. Don't cry. It's OK." Lily hugs him back. She says, "Ben, I'm sorry. I was silly. I wanted the balloon. But it popped. And I lost you. And I was scared." Ben says, "It's OK, Lily. I'm sorry too. I should have stayed with you. But I'm here now. And we're together. And we're safe." They smile and wipe their tears. They hold hands and start to go down. They take small steps and look for rocks to hold on to. They see flowers and bugs and birds. They are happy.
