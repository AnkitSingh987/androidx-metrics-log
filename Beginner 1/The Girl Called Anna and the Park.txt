Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Anna and the Park

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Anna. Every morning, Anna loved to go to the park to play. One morning, when Anna was playing, she heard a loud noise from a nearby van. She went to see what was happening and saw that the van had broken down. She could feel how sad the driver was, he was all alone and the van was empty. Anna was brave and she spoke to the driver. She said, “Hello sir! Can I help you?” The driver replied, “My van has broken down and I can’t fix it on my own. Can you rescue me?” Anna smiled and said, “Yes, I can help you. My dad has a truck and I can call him to come and rescue you.” The driver thanked Anna and soon her dad arrived to help the driver. Dad fixed the van and the driver thanked Anna and her Dad for their kindness. Anna was glad she was able to help the driver and rescue him from being alone in the empty van.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Maya. She really enjoyed playing in her room. She had lots of toys to play with and a special desk all for her own. She sometimes sat at the desk and wrote on paper or drew pictures with crayons. She kept everything in the desk very organized. One day, her mom asked her, "Maya, why do you like the desk so much?" Maya answered, "I just like it because it helps me stay organized when I play. I enjoy playing with all my toys and writing at my special desk!" Maya smiled and her mom smiled back. They hugged and then Maya went back to playing with her toys. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red cap that he wore every day. He loved his cap very much. One day, Tim could not find his cap. He was worried. He looked under his bed, in his toy box, and even in the kitchen. But his cap was not there. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, have you seen my red cap?" His mom said, "Yes, I have. It was dirty, so I washed it. It's hanging outside to dry." Tim went outside and saw his cap hanging on the line. He was so happy. He waited for his cap to dry, and then he put it back on his head. From that day on, Tim always knew where his cap was. He never felt worried about his cap again. And every time his cap got dirty, he would ask his mom to wash it for him. They lived happily ever after.

A boy and his mom went to the park. The boy liked to play on the swings and the slide. He saw a big dog with a ball in his mouth. The boy wanted to play with the dog and the ball. He ran to the dog and tried to take the ball. "Stop, boy, stop!" his mom shouted. "The dog is not yours. He may bite you!" But the boy did not listen. He grabbed the ball from the dog's mouth. The dog did not like that. He growled and barked. He jumped on the boy and bit his arm. The boy dropped the ball and cried. "Help, help!" his mom screamed. She ran to the boy and the dog. She tried to pull the dog away. But the dog was too strong. He bit the mom's leg. She fell to the ground and cried. A man came to help. He was the dog's owner. He was wise. He knew how to calm the dog. He called the dog's name and gave him a treat. The dog let go of the mom and the boy. He wagged his tail and ate the treat. "I'm so sorry," the man said. "My dog is not bad. He just loves his ball. He thought you wanted to take it away from him." The mom and the boy were hurt. They had blood on their clothes. They needed a doctor. The man said he would pay for the doctor. He said he was sorry again. But the mom and the boy were not happy. They were scared and sad. They did not want to play with the dog or the ball. They did not want to go to the park. They wanted to go home. They cried.
