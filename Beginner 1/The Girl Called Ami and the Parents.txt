Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Ami and the Parents

Once upon a time there was a small girl called Ami. She was only three years old, but she was very brave. Today, she was feeling a bit scared of the thunder and lightning outside. Ami's parents heard her crying, so they came in to see her. They gave her a big hug, trying to make her feel better. Ami's dad said, "Don't worry Ami. Let me wipe away your tears." Ami smiled and felt much better. Her dad wiped her tears away with his big soft handkerchief. Ami said a big thank you to her parents. She felt so much safer now that they were around. From that day on, Ami knew she could always depend on her loving parents to make her feel safe and secure.

One day, a wise cat named Tom found some pants. He thought it would be fun to wear them. So, he put on the pants and went for a walk. He met his friend, a bird named Sue. "Hi Sue," said Tom. "Do you like my pants?" Sue looked at Tom and smiled. "Yes, Tom! They are very nice pants." Tom and Sue walked to the park. They saw a big seat. Tom and Sue sat on the seat together. They talked and laughed. They had a great time. At the end of the day, Tom took off his pants. He gave them to Sue. "You can have my pants, Sue. They will keep you warm." Sue was happy. She thanked Tom and flew away. Tom felt good because he helped his friend. They both had a fun day with the pants and the seat.

One day, a little cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big loop on the ground. Tim thought it looked fun to play with. He felt happy and peaceful in the warm sun. Tim nodded to himself, thinking he would come back to play with the loop later. Tim walked more and saw his friend, a dog named Sam. Sam was sad because he lost his toy. Tim wanted to help Sam find his toy. They looked high and low, but they could not find it. Tim then remembered the loop he saw earlier. Tim took Sam to the loop he found. Sam's face lit up with joy when he saw it. The loop was Sam's toy! They played together with the loop all day long. Tim was happy that he could help his friend. From that day on, Tim and Sam became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a tie that he loved very much. It was a gift from his dad. Tim's dad told him the tie was reliable, which means it would always be there for him. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He climbed up a tree, but then he started to fall. As he fell, his tie got caught on a branch. The tie held him up, and he did not get hurt. Tim knew his dad was right, his tie was reliable. Tim's mom and dad came to help him down from the tree. They were happy he was safe, and they were proud of Tim's reliable tie. From that day on, Tim always wore his tie, knowing it would be there for him when he needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a big room. In the room, there were many people. They all worked with a big boss. They were his staff. They all had a job to do. The big boss was nice and happy. One day, the big boss went away. The staff felt sad. They missed him. They had to be alert and do their work. They waited for him to return. They wanted the big boss to be happy with their work. Then, one day, the big boss came back. But, he was not alone. He had a new friend. The new friend was a big, funny dog. The staff was surprised! They did not expect a dog. The dog made everyone laugh and play. The big boss and the staff were happy together with their new funny dog friend. The room was fun and full of joy.
