Category: Beginner
Title: The Family and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a family who celebrated the summertime. They got ready and went outside to enjoy the sun. The father looked up at the sky and said, “Let’s put down this blanket and celebrate!” The mother said, “Yes! Everyone find a spot to sit down.” The little sister said, “Oh, I see a pretty flower!” The little brother said, “I want to show you my new shoes. Look! They have attractive toes!” Everyone was so happy and they celebrated nature and the summer sunshine. They played tag, ran and laughed until it was time to go. They all hugged each other and the father said, “It was great to celebrate with you all!”

Once upon a time, there was a purple cat. This cat was not nice. It was a spoiled cat. The cat liked to play with a ball. One day, the cat saw a boy. The boy was playing with a long string. The cat wanted the string to play. The cat went to the boy and asked, "Can I play with your string?" The boy said, "Yes, but be careful. Do not tie the string too tight." The cat took the string and played. The cat did not listen to the boy. The cat tied the string very tight. The string broke. The cat was sad. The boy was sad too. The cat did not have a string to play with anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big bench under a tree. Tim thought the bench looked fun to sit on. He ran to the bench and sat down. The bench was old and had a terrible smell. Tim didn't like the smell, but he still wanted to sit there. A little girl named Sue came to the park too. She saw Tim on the bench and walked over to him. Sue said, "Hi, I'm Sue. Do you want to play with me?" Tim looked at her and said, "Hi Sue, I'm Tim. I want to play, but this bench smells terrible." Sue had an idea. She called her mom to bring some soap and water. When her mom came, they cleaned the bench together. The terrible smell went away, and Tim was happy. Tim and Sue played on the clean bench all day, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Olive. Olive loved to play with her friends and always smiled. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Olive's mom asked her to help wrap a gift. "Olive, can you help me wrap this?" she said. Olive was excited to help her mom. She took the paper and wrapped the gift carefully. They put a pretty bow on top. Olive and her mom went to a party with the wrapped gift. They saw all their friends there. Olive played games and laughed with her friends. She felt happy and had a great time. In the end, Olive's friend opened the gift and said, "Thank you, Olive!"

Once upon a time, there was a hoop that could walk. The hoop was scared of big dogs. One day, the hoop went for a walk in the park. In the park, the hoop met a little girl. The girl said, "Hi, hoop! Let's play!" The hoop was happy to play with the girl. They played all day long. But then, the hoop saw a big dog. The hoop was very scared. The girl said, "Don't be scared, hoop!" But the hoop was too scared to listen. The hoop ran away from the big dog and the little girl. The hoop got lost and could not find its way back to the park. The hoop was sad and scared, and it never saw the little girl or the big dog again. The end.

Ben and Mia like to play with their toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is the fly. The fly is a small toy that can move in the air. It has wings and a button. When you press the button, the fly goes up and down. One day, Ben and Mia decide to have a game. They want to see who can make the fly go higher and longer. They take turns to press the button and watch the fly. Ben goes first. He presses the button hard and the fly goes up, up, up. He counts to ten and then lets go. The fly falls down and lands on the floor. "Wow, Ben, you are good!" Mia says. "But I can do better. Watch me!" Mia goes next. She presses the button softly and the fly goes up, up, up. She counts to twenty and then lets go. The fly stays in the air for a while and then falls down and lands on the sofa. "Wow, Mia, you are better!" Ben says. "You win the game. You are the best fly player!" Mia smiles and hugs Ben. "Thank you, Ben. You are a good fly player too. And you are a good brother. I love you!" Ben smiles and hugs Mia. "I love you too, Mia. You are a good sister. And you are very organized. You always put the toys back in the box when we are done." Mia nods and says, "Yes, I do. I like to keep things neat and tidy. Come on, let's put the fly back in the box and play something else."
