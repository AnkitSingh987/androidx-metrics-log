Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sue and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went to play at the park. They saw a big tree with many peaches on it. Tim and Sue wanted to eat the peaches, but they were too high to reach. They saw their friend, Mr. Bird, who was very reliable. Tim asked, "Mr. Bird, can you help us get a peach?" Mr. Bird said, "Yes, I will help you." He flew up to the tree and picked two peaches for Tim and Sue. They were so happy and thanked Mr. Bird. They sat down on the grass to eat the peaches. Tim looked at Sue and said, "Let's share our peaches with Mr. Bird, he helped us." Sue agreed, "Yes, we should share with him." So, they called Mr. Bird over and shared their peaches with him. They all enjoyed the sweet peaches together. Tim and Sue learned that sharing is important and that they can always rely on their friend, Mr. Bird.

One day, a hungry bird named Bob was flying in the sky. He was looking for food to eat. He saw a green place with water far away. It was an oasis. Bob was very happy and flew to the oasis. When Bob got to the oasis, he saw a kind monkey named Mike. Mike was there to serve food to all the animals. Bob said, "Hi Mike, I am very hungry. Can you give me some food?" Mike smiled and said, "Sure, Bob. I have some yummy bugs for you." Bob started to eat the bugs. But, surprise! The bugs were not real. They were made of candy. Bob was not happy. He said, "Mike, I cannot eat candy. I need real food." Mike laughed and said, "I am sorry, Bob. I was just having fun. Here are some real bugs for you." Bob ate the real bugs and was not hungry anymore. He and Mike became good friends at the oasis.

One day, a clever dog named Max saw a big van. Max wanted to play in the van. The van had a slide on the side. Max thought it was fun to slide. Max saw a boy near the van. Max said, "Can I play on the slide?" The boy said, "No, you are a dog. Dogs can't slide." Max was sad but he wanted to try. Max went up the slide. He slid down fast. But Max did not know how to stop. Max hit the van hard. The van broke. Max was hurt and the boy was mad. The end.

One day, a cat and a dog wanted to bake a big cake. They put all the things they need in a big bowl. The cat said, "This is difficult, but we can do it!" The dog agreed and said, "Yes, we can!" While they were baking the cake, it started to snow outside. The snow was very pretty and white. The cat and the dog looked out the window and saw their friends playing in the snow. They wanted to play, too! They put the cake in the oven and went outside to play. But, they forgot to set a timer for the cake. When they came back inside, the cake was big and green! The cat and the dog were surprised. They didn't know that the snow made the cake turn green. They laughed and shared the green cake with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to play with his toy saw. One day, Max's mom said, "Max, you need to behave and not make a mess with your saw." Max was a persistent dog. He tried to be good, but he loved his saw so much. He played with it all day long. He would run around the house with his saw, making a mess. One day, Max's friend, a little cat named Luna, came over to play. Luna said, "Max, let's play with your saw together!" Max and Luna played with the saw and had so much fun. They were careful not to make a mess. Max's mom saw them playing nicely and said, "Good job, Max! You are behaving well with your saw." Max was happy that he could play with his saw and still behave. From that day on, Max and Luna played together with the saw. They were always careful to behave and not make a mess. Max learned that being persistent and behaving could make everyone happy.
