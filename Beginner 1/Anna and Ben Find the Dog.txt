Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben Find the Dog

Anna and Ben like to play with their toy bow and arrow. They take turns to shoot the arrow at a big tree in the yard. They try to make the arrow soar high and far. "Wow, you are good, Ben!" Anna says. "Your arrow went very high!" "Thank you, Anna. You are good, too. Your arrow went very far!" Ben says. They laugh and clap for each other. They are having fun. But then, they hear a loud bark. A big dog is running towards them. The dog looks angry and scary. Anna and Ben are afraid. They drop their toy bow and arrow and run to the house. "Help, help!" they cry. "There is a big dog!" Mom hears them and opens the door. She lets them in and hugs them. "Don't worry, Anna and Ben. You are safe now. The dog can't get in. He is just a neighbor's dog. He won't hurt you. He is just curious and playful." Mom says. "Are you sure, Mom?" Anna asks. "Yes, I am sure. He is a friendly dog. He just wants to say hello. Maybe next time, we can go and pet him together. Would you like that?" Mom asks. Anna and Ben nod. They feel better. They love Mom. They look out the window and see the dog. He is sniffing their toy bow and arrow. He picks up the arrow with his mouth and runs away. "Hey, that's our arrow!" Ben says. "Maybe he likes to play with it, too." Anna says. They giggle. They are not afraid of the dog anymore. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy named Mr. Bear. Tim and Mr. Bear loved to play together. They played in the sun and had lots of fun. One day, Tim and Mr. Bear were playing near a big tree. Tim saw a small door on the tree. He said, "Mr. Bear, let's open the door!" Mr. Bear nodded his head. Tim opened the door and they went inside. Inside the tree, they found a room full of toys. Tim picked up a ball. It was hard and had a smiley face on it. Tim threw the ball and it bounced back to his body. Tim and Mr. Bear played with the ball for a while. Then, Tim said, "Mr. Bear, it's time to go home." They walked to the door and Tim closed it. Tim and Mr. Bear went back home and told Mom about their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Bella. She had big wings and loved to fly high in the sky. Bella also liked to display her beautiful feathers for all her friends to see. She would spread her wings wide and everyone would say, "Wow!" One day, Bella saw something shiny on the ground. It was a spike! Bella didn't know what it was, but she thought it was pretty. She picked it up with her beak and took it to her nest. She showed it to her friends, and they all thought it was special too. But then, something unexpected happened. The spike started to glow and changed into a magic wand! Bella was surprised and happy. She waved the wand and found out she could make wishes come true. From that day on, Bella and her friends had lots of fun with the magic wand, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a big toy car. He really wanted it. He asked his mom, "Can I have the toy car, please?" His mom looked at the price. It was too much money. Tim was sad. Later, Tim's mom had an idea. She said, "If you are ready to help me with chores, I will give you money. Then you can buy the toy car." Tim was happy and ready to help. He did his chores every day. After some days, Tim had enough money for the toy car. He went to the store with his mom and bought it. His mom was very proud of him. She said, "Good job, Tim!" and everyone in the store began to applaud. Tim was so happy that he worked hard and got his toy car.
