Category: Beginner
Title: The Motor and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a nice motor. The motor had a big job: it had to fill up all the cars with gas. The motor liked to help the cars go fast and far. One day, a blue car came to the motor. The car was very sad. It could not go fast or far because it had no gas. The nice motor wanted to help the blue car. The motor filled up the blue car with gas. The car was very happy. Now it could go fast and far. The nice motor and the blue car became good friends. They played together every day.

One day, a small cat named Tiny went to visit her good friend, Big Dog. Big Dog lived in a big house with a big yard. Tiny liked to play and rest in the yard with Big Dog. They had lots of fun together. One day, Tiny and Big Dog were playing in the yard when they heard a loud noise. A mean squirrel was yelling at them. "This is my yard! Go away!" said the mean squirrel. Tiny and Big Dog were sad. They did not want to leave the yard, but the mean squirrel would not let them play. Tiny and Big Dog tried to talk to the mean squirrel. "Can we all play together?" asked Tiny. The mean squirrel said, "No! This is my yard!" Tiny and Big Dog were very sad. They could not play in the yard anymore. They went home, and the mean squirrel stayed in the yard. The end.

Tom and Mia like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make roads and bridges with blocks and books. They have fun making noises and racing their cars. One day, Tom and Mia have a big fight. They want to play with the same car, a red one with a shiny hood. They pull and tug and shout. They do not share or take turns. They are not polite. They make their cars crash and bump into each other. They knock down the roads and bridges. They make a big mess. They make a big wreck. Mom hears the noise and comes to see. She is not happy. She sees the wreck and the angry faces. She tells Tom and Mia to stop fighting and clean up. She tells them to say sorry and be nice. Tom and Mia realize they are wrong. They feel bad. They say sorry to each other and to Mom. They hug and make up. They clean up the wreck and fix the roads and bridges. They play with their cars again, but this time they share and take turns. They are polite. They have fun again. They smile and laugh. They are friends. Mom is happy. She gives them a snack and a kiss. She tells them she loves them. They tell her they love her too. They are happy.

One day, an elderly man named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big jet in the sky. The jet was very fast. Tom liked to watch it fly. Tom met a little boy named Tim. Tim had an ice cream cone. The ice cream began to melt because it was a hot day. Tim said, "Oh no, my ice cream is melting!" Tom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can get more ice cream." They went to the ice cream shop and got new ice cream cones. They sat under a tree and watched the jet fly by.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Billy. Billy liked to create things with his friends. One day, Billy met a new friend named Wally. Wally was a big, round ball of wheat. "Hi, Wally!" said Billy. "Do you want to create something with me?" "Yes, Billy!" said Wally. "Let's create a big house for all our friends to play in." So, Billy and Wally worked together to create a big house. They used sticks, leaves, and some of Wally's wheat. The house was so big and fun. All their friends came to play in the house. Billy and Wally were very happy. They had made a great place for all their friends to have fun. And from that day on, Billy and Wally were best friends. They loved to create new things and play with their friends every day.
