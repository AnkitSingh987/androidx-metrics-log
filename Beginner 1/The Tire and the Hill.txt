Category: Beginner
Title: The Tire and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a big tire. It was far away, in a distant land. The tire had a friend named Sam. They loved to play together. Sam would push the tire, and the tire would roll and roll. One sunny day, Sam and the tire were playing near a hill. Sam gave the tire a big push. The tire started to roll down the hill very fast. It was going too fast! Sam yelled, "Tire, stop! Please stop!" But the tire could not hear Sam. The tire rolled into a little house at the bottom of the hill. It broke the house. The people who lived in the house were very sad. Sam ran down the hill and saw what happened. He felt bad. Sam said to the people, "I am sorry. I did not want to break your house. Can you forgive me?" The people were still sad and said, "No, we cannot forgive you. Our house is broken, and we have nowhere to live now." From that day on, Sam was very careful when he played with his tire. He learned that his actions could hurt others. But the people in the broken house never forgave Sam, and he felt sad about it forever.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a lot of gear. They have a ball, a bike, a kite and a teddy. One day, they go to the park with their gear. They see a big, fat dog. The dog is sleeping on the grass. Tom and Sue want to play with the dog. They think the dog is cute. They go near the dog. They say, "Hi, dog. Do you want to play with us?" The dog does not answer. He is still sleeping. Tom and Sue spread their gear on the ground. They try to make the dog wake up. They throw the ball near the dog. They ride the bike around the dog. They fly the kite over the dog. They put the teddy next to the dog. The dog does not move. He is still sleeping. Tom and Sue are sad. They think the dog does not like them. They say, "Bye, dog. We are going to play somewhere else." They pick up their gear. They leave the dog alone. The dog opens one eye. He sees Tom and Sue go away. He smiles. He is not sleeping. He is pretending. He does not want to play with Tom and Sue. He wants to keep their gear. He likes their gear. He is a greedy, fat dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with yarn. One day, she saw a big ball of yarn in her house. It was red and soft. Kitty was very happy. Kitty played with the yarn all day. She rolled it, jumped on it, and chased it. Then, her friend Doggy came to visit. Doggy saw the yarn and said, "Kitty, that's a fake yarn! It's not real." Kitty was sad. She didn't want to play with fake yarn. So, she and Doggy decided to look for real yarn. They looked in the house, in the yard, and under the bed. They found a real yarn that was blue and soft. Kitty and Doggy played with the real yarn all day. They were very happy and tired. When it was time for bed, they said good night to each other and went to sleep. And they dreamed about playing with yarn again the next day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with string. One day, Fluffy found a big ball of string in the living room. Fluffy was very happy and started to play with the string. "Yay! I love string!" said Fluffy. Fluffy played and played, and the string went all around the house. Fluffy did not see that the string was making a big mess. Mom saw the mess and was not happy. "Fluffy, look at this mess!" said Mom. Fluffy looked around and saw the mess. Fluffy felt bad and wanted to make Mom happy. Fluffy had an idea. Fluffy hugged Mom and said, "I am sorry, Mom. I will clean up the mess." Mom smiled and hugged Fluffy back. "Thank you, Fluffy. Let's clean up together." They cleaned up the string together, and the house was nice and clean again. From that day on, Fluffy played with string, but always cleaned up after playing.
