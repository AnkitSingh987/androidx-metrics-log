Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Gun

One day, a boy named Tim went to search for a toy. He wanted a modern toy gun to play with. He asked his mom, "Can we go to the store to find a toy gun?" At the store, Tim and his mom looked at many toys. They saw big toy guns and small toy guns. Tim's mom said, "Choose one toy gun, but remember, we can only buy one." Tim picked a big, modern toy gun that made fun sounds. He was very happy. He played with his new toy gun all day. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Thank you, mom, for taking me to the store. I love my new toy gun!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim found a tiny spark in his room. It was so small but very bright. Tim was so happy that he wanted to show his mom and dad. But first, he wanted to find a box that was a fit for the spark. Tim looked everywhere in his house for a box. He found one in the kitchen, but it was too big. He found another one in his room, but it was too small. Finally, he found a box that was the right fit. It was in his dad's work room. Tim put the spark in the box and closed it. Tim was so excited to show his mom and dad the spark. But, he forget where he put the box! He looked all around the house, but he could not find it. Then, he went back to his dad's work room and there it was! But when he opened the box, the spark was gone. Tim was sad, but then he saw the spark had turned into a beautiful butterfly! It flew around the room and landed on Tim's hand. Tim smiled and knew that the spark had become his new friend.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had many toys: cars, dolls, blocks, animals, and more. They liked to make noise and mess with their toys. But Mom did not like the noise and the mess. She came into the living room and said, "Lily and Ben, you have to organize your toys. Put them in the boxes and on the shelves. It is time to clean up." Lily and Ben did not want to organize their toys. They wanted to keep playing. They said, "No, Mom, we are having fun. We do not want to clean up." Mom was not happy. She said, "If you do not organize your toys, I will take them away. You have to learn to be tidy and respectful. You have five minutes to organize your toys." Lily and Ben felt helpless. They did not know how to organize their toys. There were too many of them. They did not know where to start. They looked at each other and started to cry. Then they saw Spike, their dog. Spike was lying on the couch, watching them. He had a big smile on his face. He liked to play with their toys too. He had a ball in his mouth. Spike saw that Lily and Ben were sad. He wanted to help them. He jumped off the couch and ran to them. He dropped the ball and barked. He wagged his tail and licked their faces. Lily and Ben stopped crying. They smiled at Spike. They said, "Thank you, Spike. You are a good friend. You want to help us organize our toys." Spike nodded and barked. He picked up the ball and put it in a box. He found a car and put it on a shelf. He found a doll and put it in a basket. He helped Lily and Ben organize their toys. Mom came back and saw that Lily and Ben had organized their toys. She was surprised and happy. She said, "Wow, Lily and Ben, you did a great job. You organized your toys very well. How did you do it?" Lily and Ben pointed at Spike. They said, "Spike helped us. He is a smart and helpful dog. He likes to organize our toys." Mom smiled and hugged Lily, Ben, and Spike. She said, "You are right. Spike is a smart and helpful dog. He is also a part of our family. We love him very much. And we love you too. You are good children. You learned to organize your toys. I am proud of you."
