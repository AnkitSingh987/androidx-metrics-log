Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Sun

Lily liked pink very much. She had a pink dress, a pink hat, a pink bike and a pink doll. She wanted everything to be pink. She did not like other colors. One day, she went to the park with her mom. She saw a big slide. She wanted to go on it. But there was a boy on the slide. He had a blue shirt and a green hat. Lily did not like him. She said, "Go away! I want to go on the slide. It is pink. It is mine." The boy was sad. He said, "Please, can we share? The slide is for everyone. It is not yours. It is not pink. It is red. You are wrong." Lily was angry. She said, "No, you are wrong. The slide is pink. I am right. You are bad. Go away!" The boy did not go away. He said, "Please, be nice. We can be friends. We can play together. We can have fun. You are not bad. You are just confused. Look, the sun is shining. It makes the slide look pink. But it is really red. See?" Lily looked. She saw the sun. She saw the slide. She saw the boy. She saw that he was right. The slide was red. She was wrong. She felt sorry. She said, "I am sorry. You are right. The slide is red. I was wrong. I was mean. Please, forgive me. Can we share? Can we be friends? Can we play together? Can we have fun?" The boy smiled. He said, "Yes, we can. I forgive you. You are not mean. You are just silly. Come on, let's go on the slide. It is fun. It does not matter what color it is. What matters is that we are happy and healthy and kind." Lily smiled too. She said, "Yes, you are right. It does not matter what color it is. What matters is that we are happy and healthy and kind. Thank you for teaching me. You are a good friend. I like you." They went on the slide. They had fun. They played together. They were happy and healthy and kind. They learned to share and to respect and to love. They liked all the colors. They liked each other. They liked the park. They liked the sun. They liked the slide. Even if it was red.

Once upon a time, there was a strong bird named Bob. Bob had big, strong wings. He lived on a cliff by the sea. The cliff was very high, and Bob liked to fly and look at the water. One day, Bob saw a little bird that was stuck in a tree. The little bird was scared. Bob wanted to help the little bird. He flew down and used his strong wings to part the branches of the tree. The little bird was free! It flew away and said, "Thank you, Bob!" Bob felt happy that he could help. He flew back to his cliff and watched the sun set over the water.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a toy tube that she loved to play with. She would roll it, throw it, and even use it as a telescope to look at the sky. One day, a rude boy named Tom came to Sally's house to play. He saw Sally's tube and wanted to play with it too. But instead of asking nicely, he just took it from her hands. Sally was very sad and began to cry. Sally's mom heard her cry and came to see what was wrong. She saw Tom with the tube and told him that it was not nice to take things without asking. Tom felt bad and gave the tube back to Sally. They both said sorry to each other and learned to share. From that day on, they played together and became good friends.

Lily was a spoiled girl who always wanted to get her way. She did not like to share her toys, or listen to her mom, or be nice to her friends. She only cared about herself and what she wanted. One day, Lily saw a big shiny reward on the table. It was a gold star that her mom gave to her brother Max for doing his homework. Lily wanted the reward for herself, even though she did not do any homework. She thought of a plan to act like she did. She ran to her room and grabbed a crayon and a paper. She scribbled some lines and circles on the paper and ran back to the table. She showed her mom the paper and said, "Look, mom, I did my homework too! Can I have a reward?" Her mom looked at the paper and shook her head. She knew that Lily was lying and trying to trick her. She said, "No, Lily, this is not your homework. This is just a mess. You did not do any work, you just acted. You cannot have the reward. The reward is for Max, who worked hard and did his homework. You should be proud of him, not jealous of him." Lily was angry and sad. She did not like her mom's answer. She did not want to be proud of Max, she wanted to have the reward. She threw a tantrum and started to cry and scream. She grabbed the reward from the table and ran to her room. She slammed the door and locked it. But as she was running, she did not see the toy car that Max left on the floor. She stepped on it and slipped. She fell down hard and hit her head on the edge of the bed. She felt a sharp pain and saw blood on her forehead. She was hurt and scared. She wanted her mom to help her, but she locked the door. She realized that she made a big mistake. She wished she did not act like a spoiled girl. She wished she did not take the reward. She wished she was nice to her mom and Max. But it was too late. She was alone and in pain. She learned a hard lesson.
