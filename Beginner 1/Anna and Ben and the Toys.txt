Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Toys

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. But sometimes they make a lot of noise. They shout, laugh, and bang their toys on the floor. This makes their mom and dad unhappy. One day, mom and dad tell them to be quiet. They say they need to work on their computer and phone. They say they need to talk to important people. They say they need some peace and quiet. Anna and Ben say okay, but they do not listen. They keep playing and making noise. Mom and dad get very angry. They come to their room and take away their toys. They say they are not nice children. They say they need to learn to be quiet and respectful. They say they need to brush their teeth and go to bed early. Anna and Ben are sad and sorry. They say they are sorry and they love mom and dad. They say they will be quiet and respectful. They say they will brush their teeth and go to bed early. Mom and dad hug them and say they love them too. They say they are proud of them for saying sorry and learning their lesson. They say they can have their toys back tomorrow if they are good. They say they need to be good and kind to each other and to mom and dad. They say they need to be happy and grateful for what they have. Anna and Ben say okay and smile. They brush their teeth and go to bed early. They have sweet dreams and wake up happy. They are good and quiet and respectful. They play with their toys and share them. They make mom and dad happy. They are happy too. The end.

Mila and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bull in the park. The bull is wide and has horns. The bull is angry and runs at them. "Run, Tom, run!" Mila says. "The bull is bad!" They run to the fence and climb over it. The bull cannot climb the fence. He stops and snorts. "Ha, ha, we are safe!" Tom says. "The bull is silly!" They see a man with a hat and a guitar. The man is playing music. The music is nice and happy. "Look, Mila, music!" Tom says. "Let's dance!" They dance to the music. They jump and spin and clap. The man smiles and sings. The bull watches them and feels lonely. "Can we dance with the bull?" Mila asks. "Maybe he is not bad. Maybe he is sad." "I don't know, Mila," Tom says. "The bull is big and scary." They go to the fence and wave at the bull. The bull looks at them and wags his tail. "Hello, bull," Mila says. "Do you want to dance with us?" The bull nods and moos. He likes music too. "OK, bull, we can dance with you," Tom says. "But be careful. Don't hurt us." They open the fence and let the bull out. The bull is gentle and friendly. He dances with them. He lowers his horns and lifts them up. He stomps his hooves and shakes his head. He follows the music and the man. They have fun and laugh. The man, the bull, and the children are friends. They dance in the park. The end.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to ride her pink bike. She loved her bike a lot. It was her favorite toy. But she did not know how to ride it yet. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Don't worry, I will help you." They went outside to the park. Lily sat on her pink bike and her mom held it. They started to ride together. Lily was a little scared at first, but then she felt happy. After a while, Lily's mom let go of the bike. Lily was riding all by herself! She was so proud. Her mom clapped and said, "You did it!" Lily smiled and knew she could ride her pink bike now.

Once upon a time, there was a nice witch. She loved to whistle. Every day, she whistled happy songs. She was very enthusiastic about whistling. She whistled when she worked. She whistled when she played. She whistled all the time. One day, the witch met a little bird. The bird could not whistle. The bird was sad. The witch wanted to help the bird. She thought of a plan. The witch would whistle a magic song to help the bird. The witch whistled the magic song. But then, something unexpected happened! The witch turned into a bird too! Now, the witch and the little bird could whistle together. They were both happy. They whistled and played all day long. And they became the best of friends.
