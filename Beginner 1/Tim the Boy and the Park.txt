Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect sticks. One day, he found a big rod in the park. The rod was very long and strong. Tim was happy and wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look, Sam! I found a big rod. Let's play with it." Sam looked at the rod and said, "Oh no, Tim! That's a bad rod. My dad said we should not play with it." Tim was sad but listened to Sam. They put the bad rod away and played with their toy cars instead. They had a lot of fun and knew that it was better to play with safe toys. In the end, Tim and Sam learned the importance of listening to grown-ups and staying away from bad things.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. The weather was sunny and warm, with a high blue sky. Tim saw a big puddle from the rain the day before. He had an idea to have some fun. He found a long stick and started to mix the water and mud in the puddle. It became very muddy and gooey. Tim laughed as he made the mud thicker and thicker. Tim's friend, Lily, saw what he was doing and joined him. They both mixed the mud and splashed it around, having lots of fun under the high sky. The weather was perfect for their muddy playtime. And that's how Tim and Lily spent their happy day together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big net near the pond. He wanted to catch some fish with the net. Tim asked his mom, "Can I use the net to catch fish?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful near the water." Tim went to the pond with the net. He saw many fish in the clean water. He put the net in the water and caught a small fish. He was very happy. Tim wanted to catch more fish to increase the number of fish he had. Tim's mom saw him catching fish and said, "Tim, it's time to go home now." Tim put the fish back in the water and said goodbye to them. He went home with his mom, thinking about the fun day he had at the park.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. But they do not like to share. One day, they find a big cloth in the closet. It is soft and colorful. They both want it. "Give me the cloth!" Anna says. She pulls one end of the cloth. "No, give me the cloth!" Ben says. He pulls the other end of the cloth. They tug and tug. The cloth rips in half. They both fall down. They look at the torn cloth. They feel miserable. "Look what you did!" Anna says. She cries. "Look what you did!" Ben says. He cries. Mom hears them. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees the ripped cloth and the crying twins. "What happened here?" Mom asks. They both point at each other. They both say, "It is his/her fault!" Mom shakes her head. She picks up the cloth. She says, "This cloth was mine when I was little. It was very special to me. I wanted to give it to you as a gift. But you did not take good care of it. You did not share. You did not listen. You made me sad." Anna and Ben feel sorry. They look at Mom. They look at each other. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We are sorry, Anna/Ben. We were selfish. We were mean. We did not mean to make you sad." Mom smiles. She says, "It is okay. I forgive you. But you have to learn to share and be kind. Sharing is caring. Caring is loving. Loving is happy." She hugs them. She says, "I love you both very much. You are my precious twins. You can still have the cloth. But you have to share it. You can use it to make something nice. You can use it to play together. You can use it to cover up when you are cold. You can use it to make each other happy." Anna and Ben nod. They hug Mom. They hug each other. They say, "We love you, Mom. We love you, Anna/Ben. We will share the cloth. We will make something nice. We will play together. We will cover up when we are cold. We will make each other happy." They take the cloth. They go to their room. They use the cloth to make a tent. They use the cloth to make a cape. They use the cloth to make a blanket. They use the cloth to have fun. They are happy. Mom is happy. The cloth is happy. The end.
