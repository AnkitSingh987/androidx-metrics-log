Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Song

One sunny day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. They took a big sail with them to play with. Tim felt very lucky because he loved playing at the beach. He liked to run and jump in the sand. While they were playing, a big wind came and took the sail up in the air. Tim yelled, "Mom, Dad, look! The sail is flying!" His mom and dad looked up and saw the sail flying high in the sky. They all laughed and clapped their hands. Just then, a big bird saw the sail and thought it was a friend. The bird grabbed the sail and flew away with it. Tim was sad, but then the bird came back and dropped the sail right in front of Tim. He was so lucky! Tim said, "Thank you, bird!" and the bird sang a happy song. From that day on, Tim and the bird were the best of friends.

One day, Tom and Lily went to the park with their mom. They saw a big fountain with many coins in the water. Tom wanted to take some coins and put them in his pocket. "Look, Lily, shiny pennies!" he said. "Let's get some!" "No, Tom, we can't do that!" Lily said. "That's not nice. Mom will be angry. And the pennies are for wishes. People throw them and hope for good things." But Tom did not listen. He ran to the fountain and reached for a penny. He did not see the sign that said "Do not touch the water". Suddenly, a loud voice warned him. "Hey, kid, stop that! You can't take the coins! That's stealing!" Tom was scared. He looked up and saw a man in a blue uniform. He was a guard. He had a big hat and a whistle. "Sorry, sir, I didn't know," Tom said. He dropped the penny and ran back to Lily. The guard followed him. He looked angry. He wanted to tell Tom's mom what he did. But Lily was compassionate. She felt sorry for Tom. She knew he made a mistake. She did not want him to get in trouble. "Please, sir, don't be mad at Tom," she said. "He's only three. He didn't mean to be bad. He just liked the pennies. He won't do it again. I promise." The guard looked at Lily. He saw her big eyes and her sweet smile. He softened a bit. He decided to give Tom a chance. "Okay, okay, I'll let it go this time," he said. "But you have to promise me something. You have to make a wish with the penny you dropped. And you have to wish for something good. Something that will make you a better person. Can you do that?" Tom nodded. He felt ashamed. He did not want to steal anymore. He wanted to be good. "Thank you, sir," he said. "I'm sorry. I'll make a wish." He picked up the penny and threw it back into the fountain. He closed his eyes and wished. "I wish I could be more like Lily," he said. "She's kind and smart and compassionate. She's the best sister ever." Lily hugged him. She was proud of him. She loved him. Mom came back from the bench. She did not know what happened. She saw Tom and Lily smiling. "Did you have fun, kids?" she asked. "Yes, mom, we did," they said. They did not tell her about the pennies. They kept it as a secret. A secret wish.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the garden. They see some bees flying around the flowers. They wonder where the bees go. "Let's follow them!" Lily says. "Maybe they have honey!" "OK!" Ben says. "But be careful. Bees can sting!" They run after the bees. They see a big tree with a hole. The bees go inside the hole. Lily and Ben hear a buzzing sound. "Look! There is honey in the tree!" Lily says. She points to a sticky yellow thing. "Wow! Can we have some?" Ben asks. "I don't know. Maybe the bees will be angry." Lily says. They think for a moment. Then they have an idea. "Let's prepare a gift for the bees!" Lily says. "Maybe they will share their honey with us!" "What kind of gift?" Ben asks. "Something sweet and smooth!" Lily says. "Like jam or peanut butter!" They run back to the house. They find some jam and peanut butter in the kitchen. They put some on a plate. They also find some bread and cut it into small pieces. They put some jam and peanut butter on the bread. "Let's go back to the tree!" Lily says. "Maybe the bees will like our gift!" They carry the plate to the tree. They put it near the hole. They wait for the bees to come out. "Hello, bees!" Lily says. "We have a gift for you! It is sweet and smooth! We hope you like it!" The bees come out of the hole. They see the plate. They smell the jam and peanut butter. They fly around the plate. They taste the bread. They like it! "Thank you, children!" The bees say. "You are very kind! We have a gift for you too! It is honey! It is sweet and smooth too! We hope you like it!" The bees bring some honey from the tree. They put it on another plate. They give it to Lily and Ben. "Thank you, bees!" Lily and Ben say. "You are very generous! We love honey!" They taste the honey. It is sweet and smooth. They smile. Lily and Ben are happy. The bees are happy. They share their gifts. They become friends. They play in the garden.
