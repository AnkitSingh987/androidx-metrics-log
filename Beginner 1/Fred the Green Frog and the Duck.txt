Category: Beginner
Title: Fred the Green Frog and the Duck

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. He loved to jump and play by the pond. One day, he found a mask in the mud. The mask was very big and funny. Fred thought that he could use the mask to make his friends laugh. Fred put on the mask and went to see his friend, the duck. "Quack, quack!" said the duck. "Who are you?" The duck did not know it was Fred. Fred said, "It's me, Fred! I have a funny mask!" The duck laughed and laughed. Fred and the duck went to find more friends to show the mask. They found a turtle and a fish. They all laughed and played together. The green frog was very happy that the mask could supply so much fun for his friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a young girl named Lily. Lily was three years old and loved playing games. One day Lily wanted to play a new game. She wanted to play chess. So her mommy went and bought her a chess set. Every day, Lily would sit and play chess. She never lied or cheated, she always tried to be honest. Soon enough she started to get better at the game. Lily decided to perform in a chess tournament. Everyone was so amazed that a three year old could play chess so well. They couldn't believe how honest she was. Lily won the tournament and everyone cheered for her. She was so happy! From then on she kept practicing and playing chess until she was the best in the whole world!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and eat yummy food. One day, her aunt came to visit. Sue's aunt was very tough, but she loved Sue very much. Sue's aunt brought her a big, red apple. She told Sue, "If you don't eat this apple soon, it will rot." Sue didn't know what "rot" meant, but she guessed it was not good. Sue played with her toys and forgot about the apple. A few days later, Sue saw the apple again. It was brown and yucky. Sue's aunt was right; the apple did rot. Sue was sad she didn't eat the apple when it was good. From that day on, Sue learned to listen to her tough aunt and not let good things go bad.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, he lost his ball in a big case. Spot asked his friend, Cat, "Can you help me find my ball?" Cat said, "Yes, let's look for it!" They looked and looked, but they could not find the ball. Spot and Cat were very sad. They wanted to quit looking for the ball. Then, Spot said, "Let's try one more time." But they still could not find the ball. In the end, Spot and Cat never found the ball, and they were very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a rich kangaroo named Kanga. Kanga had many friends in the forest. She liked to do nice things for them. She would give them yummy food and soft toys to play with. One day, Kanga met a little bird who was sad. The bird said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Kanga wanted to help her new friend. So, they went to look for the toy together. They looked high and low, and soon they found the toy under a big tree. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Kanga! You are a good friend." From that day on, Kanga and the bird were best friends, and they always had fun together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a light in the garage. He walked closer and stared at it. It was his toy car. Inside the garage, Tim found his friend, Sally. She was playing with her doll. "Hi, Tim!" said Sally. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes!" Tim said. They played with the toy car and the doll. They had lots of fun in the light garage. Then, they went outside to play more. The sun was shining, and they were happy.
