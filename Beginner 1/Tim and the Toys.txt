Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy skull. He liked to play with it a lot. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's toy skull and wanted to play with it too. Tim said, "No, it's mine!" Sue felt sad. Then, Sue tried to pinch the toy skull from Tim's hand. Tim thought that was rude. He didn't like it when Sue tried to take his toy. Tim and Sue didn't play together that day. Later, Tim's mom talked to him. She said, "Tim, you should share your toys with your friends." Tim thought about it and decided to share his toy skull with Sue. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bird who lived in a big tree. Every day, the bird would wake up early and sing a pretty song. The bird loved to fly around and play with its friends. One day, the bird saw a shiny thing on the ground. It was an ashtray. The bird didn't know what it was, but it liked how it looked. The bird picked up the ashtray with its beak and brought it to the tree. The bird showed the ashtray to its friends, and they all thought it was very unique. They played with the ashtray all day, taking turns to hold it and look at it. The bird and its friends were very happy with their new toy, and they continued to play with it every day.

Lily liked to mix things. She mixed water and soap to make bubbles. She mixed flour and eggs to make dough. She mixed paint and glue to make slime. She had a lot of fun mixing things in her room. One day, Lily found a bottle of pills in her mom's bag. She wondered what they were. She thought they looked like candy. She wanted to mix them with something. She took the bottle and went to her room. She poured some pills on her table. She saw they had different colors. She mixed them with water and sugar. She made a sweet drink. She tasted it. It was yummy. She drank more and more. She felt sleepy. She lay down on her bed. She closed her eyes. She did not hear her mom calling her name. She did not hear her mom opening her door. She did not hear her mom screaming. Lily did not wake up. She mixed the wrong things. She was not obedient. She made a big mistake. She had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball. One day, Tom went to a fun event with his mom. At the event, there were many games and other animals to play with. Tom saw a big dog named Max. Max liked to tease Tom by taking his ball away. Tom was sad, but he didn't give up. He thought of a plan to get his ball back from Max. Tom found a bigger ball and rolled it towards Max. Max saw the big ball and let go of Tom's ball to chase the big one. Tom got his ball back and was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Max came back with the big ball and wanted to play with Tom. They both played with the big ball and had a great time. In the end, Max and Tom became good friends and didn't tease each other anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little worm named Wiggly. Wiggly lived in a rough, brown dirt home. He had many friends, but his best friend was a big, red bird named Chirpy. One sunny day, Wiggly and Chirpy were playing in the park. Wiggly said, "Chirpy, I hope we can play all day long!" Chirpy smiled and said, "Me too, Wiggly! Let's have fun!" They played hide and seek, and Wiggly hid in the rough dirt. Chirpy looked for him and found him. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Wiggly and Chirpy were very happy friends.

Tim and Sam were playing with their toy cars in the backyard. They liked to pretend they were cops and robbers, chasing each other around the grass. Tim had a blue car that made a siren sound when he pushed a button. Sam had a red car that could glow in the dark. "Stop, robber! You are under arrest!" Tim shouted, as he drove his blue car towards Sam's red car. "No way, cop! You can't catch me!" Sam said, as he drove his red car away from Tim's blue car. They zoomed around the yard, dodging the flowers and the rocks. They were having a lot of fun, until they saw something disgusting on the ground. It was a big pile of dog poop, and Sam's red car drove right into it. "Yuck! Gross! My car is dirty!" Sam cried, as he picked up his red car and saw the brown stuff on it. "Ha ha! You got poop on your car! That's what you get for being a robber!" Tim laughed, as he drove his blue car near Sam. "That's not funny, Tim! That's mean! You made me drive into the poop!" Sam said, as he threw his red car at Tim. "Ow! That hurt! You hit me with your car!" Tim said, as he dropped his blue car and rubbed his head. They both started to cry, and their mom heard them from the house. She came out to see what was wrong. "What's going on here? Why are you crying? And why is there poop on your car?" she asked, as she looked at the mess. "Tim and Sam, you need to say sorry to each other. And you need to clean up your cars. And you need to stay away from the poop. That's disgusting and dangerous. Do you understand?" she said, as she gave them a hug. "Yes, mom. We're sorry. We'll clean up. We'll be nice. We won't play with the poop." they said, as they sniffled and nodded. They took their cars to the hose and washed them off. Then they dried them with a towel and put them away. They decided to play something else, something that didn't involve poop. They learned their lesson, and they were friends again.
