Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Tom and the Toys

Lila and Tom were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Lila had a baby doll that she loved very much. She gave her baby a bottle and a blanket and rocked her to sleep. Tom had a toy car that he liked to zoom around the floor. He made loud vroom-vroom sounds and pretended to be a racer. He saw Lila's baby doll and wanted to play with it. He grabbed the baby from Lila's arms and ran away. "Hey, give me back my baby!" Lila shouted. She chased after Tom and tried to get her baby back. Tom was faster and ran to the other side of the room. He threw the baby in the air and caught it. He laughed and said, "This is not a baby, this is a ball!" Lila was very angry and sad. She cried and said, "No, that is my baby! You are mean, Tom! You hurt my baby!" She ran to her mom and told her what Tom did. Mom was not happy with Tom. She said, "Tom, that is not nice. You have to give Lila back her baby and say sorry. You have to be gentle with babies, they are not balls." Tom felt bad for making Lila cry. He realized he was wrong. He walked to Lila and gave her back her baby. He said, "I'm sorry, Lila. I was silly. Your baby is not a ball. Your baby is very pretty and soft. Can I see your baby?" Lila wiped her tears and smiled. She said, "Thank you, Tom. I forgive you. You can see my baby, but you have to be careful. Do you want to hold her?" She handed her baby to Tom and showed him how to hold her. Tom held the baby gently and looked at her face. He said, "Wow, your baby is impressive. She has big eyes and a cute nose. She looks like you, Lila." Lila and Tom hugged and made up. They decided to play together with their toys. They took turns to feed the baby and drive the car. They had fun and learned to share and be kind. Mom was proud of them and gave them a snack. She said, "You are good kids. I love you both very much."

---

Lily and Tom were twins. They liked to play together, but sometimes they fought. One day, they wanted to go to the park with Mom. Mom said they had to prepare first. They had to brush their teeth, comb their hair, and put on clean shirts. Lily ran to the bathroom and brushed her teeth. Then she combed her hair and put on a pink shirt. She looked in the mirror and smiled. She was ready to go. Tom was slower. He did not want to brush his teeth. He said they were clean enough. He did not want to comb his hair. He said it was too hard. He did not want to put on a clean shirt. He said he liked his old shirt. Lily came to his room and saw him lying on the bed. She said, "Tom, hurry up! Mom is waiting for us. You have to prepare. You have to brush your teeth, comb your hair, and put on a clean shirt." Tom said, "No, I don't. I like my teeth, my hair, and my shirt. They are fine. You are selfish. You always want to do what you want. You don't care about me." Lily was angry. She said, "I am not selfish. You are selfish. You always want to do what you want. You don't care about Mom. She wants us to be clean and nice. She wants us to have fun at the park. You are making her sad." Tom did not listen. He said, "I don't care. I want to stay here. You go to the park. I don't want to go with you." Lily was sad. She said, "Fine. I will go to the park. But you will miss the swings, the slides, and the ducks. You will be lonely and bored. You will be sorry." She left the room and went to Mom. Mom hugged her and said, "Let's go to the park, Lily. Maybe Tom will change his mind and join us later. But if he doesn't, we will still have fun. We love each other, right?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, Mom. We love each other. And I love the park. Let's go!"
