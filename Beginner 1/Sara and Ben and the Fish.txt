Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Fish

Sara and Ben love to swim in the sea. They wear masks and fins to see the fish and the reef. The reef is a big rock with many colors and shapes. Some fish are big and some are small. Some are bright and some are dull. One day, Sara and Ben see a new fish. It is long and thin and has many spots. It swims very fast and makes a noise. Sara and Ben want to number the spots on the fish. They count one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten. The fish has ten spots. But the fish does not like to be numbered. It is shy and scared. It swims away from Sara and Ben. It hides behind the reef. Sara and Ben feel sad. They want to be friends with the fish. They have an idea. They find some mild seaweed. Mild seaweed is soft and green and does not hurt. They make a necklace with the mild seaweed. They put it on the reef near the fish. They hope the fish will like it. They wait and watch. The fish comes out of the reef. It sees the necklace. It is curious and happy. It swims closer and closer. It touches the necklace with its nose. It likes the mild seaweed. It wears the necklace around its neck. It smiles at Sara and Ben. Sara and Ben smile back. They are friends with the fish. They swim together and have fun.

Ben and Sam are best friends. They like to play with cars and balls and blocks. They are loyal to each other and share their toys. One day, Ben finds a funny thing in his dad's drawer. It is black and hairy and sticky. Ben thinks it is a mustache. He puts it on his face and shows Sam. "Look, Sam, I have a mustache like dad!" Ben says. Sam laughs and says, "You look silly, Ben. That is not a mustache. That is tape. Take it off." Ben feels angry and says, "No, it is a mustache. You are jealous. You don't have a mustache." Sam feels hurt and says, "I don't want a mustache. You are mean. I don't want to play with you." Ben and Sam stop talking and turn away from each other. They sit on the floor and play with their own toys. They feel sad and lonely. After a while, Ben's mom comes in and sees them. She sees the tape on Ben's face and says, "Ben, what is that on your face? That is not a mustache. That is tape. It can hurt your skin. Let me take it off." Ben says, "No, mom, it is a mustache. I want to keep it." Ben's mom says, "Ben, it is not a mustache. It is tape. It is not good for you. Please let me take it off." Ben says, "No, mom, it is a mustache. I like it." Ben's mom says, "Ben, why do you want a mustache? You are a little boy. You don't need a mustache." Ben says, "Because dad has a mustache. And Sam doesn't. And Sam laughed at me. And Sam is not my friend anymore." Ben's mom says, "Oh, Ben, I see. You had a fight with Sam. But Sam is your best friend. He is loyal to you. He was not trying to hurt you. He was just joking. He likes you. And you like him. You should say sorry to him. And he will say sorry to you. And you will be friends again. And you will be happy. And you don't need a mustache to be happy. You just need a smile." Ben thinks about what his mom says. He feels sorry for being angry at Sam. He feels sorry for making Sam sad. He wants to be friends with Sam again. He wants to be happy. He says, "OK, mom, you can take off the tape. I don't want a mustache. I want a smile." Ben's mom takes off the tape and gives Ben a hug and a kiss. She says, "That's my good boy. Now go and say sorry to Sam. And give him a hug and a smile. And play nicely together." Ben goes to Sam and says, "Sam, I'm sorry for being mean to you. I don't want a mustache. I want a smile. And I want you to be my friend. You are loyal to me. And I am loyal to you. Can we play together?" Sam smiles and says, "Ben, I'm sorry for laughing at you. I don't care if you have a mustache or not. I want a smile. And I want you to be my friend. You are loyal to me. And I am loyal to you. Yes, we can play together." Ben and Sam hug and smile. They are friends again. They are happy. They play with their cars and balls and blocks. They share their toys. They are loyal to each other. The end.
