Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Cat

Lily loved her furry cat, Max. She liked to play with him and hug him. Sometimes, Max would bite her fingers or toes, but not hard. He was just being playful. One day, Lily found a ball of yarn in her mom's basket. She thought it would be fun to roll it on the floor and watch Max chase it. She threw the yarn and Max ran after it. He caught it with his paws and started to bite it. "Max, stop!" Lily said. "That is not your toy. That is mom's yarn. She needs it to make a sweater." But Max did not listen. He liked the yarn. It was soft and colorful. He bit it harder and harder, until it broke. Then he saw another piece of yarn and ran to get it. Lily tried to stop him, but he was too fast. He bit all the yarn in the basket, making a big mess. Lily was sad and angry. She did not want Max to ruin mom's yarn. "Bad cat!" she shouted. "You are naughty. You should not bite yarn. You should bite your own toys." She picked up Max and put him in his bed. She gave him a stern look and said, "You stay here. You are in time out. No more yarn for you." Max looked at her with his big eyes. He did not understand why she was mad. He thought he was having fun. He meowed softly and licked his paw. He hoped Lily would forgive him soon. He loved her, even when she was cross.

Ben and Lily were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make food with pots and pans. They also liked to use the sink to wash their dishes and hands. "Look, I made soup!" Ben said, holding a pot with water and some toys. "Do you want some?" "Yes, please!" Lily said, taking a spoon. She pretended to taste the soup and smiled. "Mmm, it's very good! What did you put in it?" Ben looked at the pot and tried to remember. "I put some cars, and some blocks, and some crayons, and some..." He stopped when he saw something shiny in the water. He recognized it as his mom's ring. He had taken it from her dresser when she was not looking. He wanted to play with it, but he forgot to put it back. "Oh no!" Ben said, dropping the pot. The water and the toys spilled on the floor. The ring rolled under the fridge. "Mom's ring! I lost it!" Lily gasped. She knew mom's ring was very important to her. She always wore it and said it was a gift from dad. She was not supposed to touch it or play with it. She looked at Ben and saw he was scared and sad. "Maybe we can find it," Lily said, trying to be helpful. "Let's look under the fridge." They got on their knees and tried to reach for the ring. But it was too far and too dark. They could not see it or grab it. They heard mom's footsteps coming to the kitchen. "Kids, are you ready for lunch?" mom asked, opening the door. She saw the mess on the floor and the empty pot. She saw Ben and Lily under the fridge. She saw the water dripping from the sink. She was very angry. "What are you doing?" mom yelled. "What is this? Did you use the sink? Did you make a mess? Where is my ring?" Ben and Lily looked at mom and started to cry. They knew they were in big trouble. They wished they had not played with the sink or the ring. They wished they had listened to mom and been good. But it was too late. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a firework in his dad's garage. He thought it was a toy and wanted to play with it. Tim showed it to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Tim, that's not a toy. It's a firework. It can go boom!" Tim didn't know what to do. He thought it was stupid that he didn't know it was a firework. He asked Sam, "Can we fix it and make it safe to play?" Sam said, "I don't know, but let's ask my big brother, Tom. He knows a lot." They went to Tom and showed him the firework. Tom said, "You can't play with this. It's not safe. But I can help you fix it, so it won't go boom." Tom helped Tim and Sam fix the firework, so it was safe to play with. They were happy that they could play with it now. Tim learned that it was important to ask for help when he didn't know something. And they all had fun playing with the firework together.
