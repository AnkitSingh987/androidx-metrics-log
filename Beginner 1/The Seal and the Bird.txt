Category: Beginner
Title: The Seal and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a nice seal. This seal was very compassionate. It liked to help its friends. One day, the seal saw a little bird who was sad. The bird had lost its way and could not find its mom. The seal wanted to help the bird. It had an idea. The seal and the bird went to a big rock near the water. The seal knew that the bird's mom liked to sit on the rock. They looked at the rock and waited. Soon, the bird's mom came to the rock. She was so happy to see her little bird. The seal felt good for helping its friend. The bird thanked the seal for being compassionate. From that day on, the seal, the bird, and the bird's mom were the best of friends. They played together near the rock every day.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a fat bird on a tree. The bird has a red chest and a black cap. It is a robin. "Look, a robin!" Lily says. "It is so pretty!" Ben nods. He wants to talk to the bird. He tries to whistle like the bird. He makes a high sound with his mouth. But the bird does not answer. It flies away. "Where did it go?" Ben asks. He is sad. Lily hugs him. She has an idea. She points to a bench. There is a man on the bench. He has a whistle in his hand. He blows the whistle. It makes a loud sound. "Maybe he can help us," Lily says. "Let's ask him." They run to the bench. They say hello to the man. He smiles. He sees their faces. They look curious. "Do you like my whistle?" he asks. "Yes, we do!" Lily and Ben say. "Can you show us how to whistle?" Ben asks. "Sure, I can," the man says. "It is easy. You just need to put your lips together and blow. Like this." He blows the whistle again. It makes a loud sound. Lily and Ben try to copy him. They put their lips together and blow. But they only make a soft sound. They try again and again. They do not give up. After a while, they make a louder sound. They are happy. They thank the man. He gives them each a whistle. He says they can keep them. "Thank you, thank you!" Lily and Ben say. They run back to the tree. They blow their whistles. They hope the robin will come back. They want to be friends with the bird. They whistle and whistle. They have fun.

Tim and Sue were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big train set. They liked to make the train go fast and loud on the tracks. One day, their mom gave them a treat. She gave them each a mint candy. The candy was smooth and sweet in their mouths. Tim and Sue said thank you and smiled. But then, Tim had a bad idea. He wanted to see what would happen if he put his mint candy on the train track. He thought it would be funny to make the train crash. He did not tell Sue what he was doing. Sue was busy making the train go around a curve. She did not see Tim's mint candy on the track. The train hit the candy and stopped. The candy got stuck in the wheels and made a mess. Sue was very angry and sad. She yelled at Tim. "Tim, why did you do that? You ruined our train! That was not nice!" Tim felt sorry. He knew he did a bad thing. He said sorry to Sue and tried to help her fix the train. But it was too late. The train was broken and the candy was wasted. Their mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw the train and the candy and the twins. She was very disappointed and mad. She said to Tim. "Tim, you were very naughty. You did not share your candy. You did not respect your toy. You did not listen to your sister. You have to punish for what you did. You have to go to your room and think about your mistake. And you have to give Sue your other candy as a sorry gift." Tim felt very bad. He did not want to punish. He did not want to go to his room. He did not want to give up his other candy. But he knew he had to. He said sorry again to Sue and gave her his candy. He said sorry to his mom and went to his room. Sue felt sorry for Tim too. She knew he was just curious and wanted to have fun. She did not want him to punish too much. She said to her mom. "Mom, can I go to Tim's room and play with him? I forgive him for what he did. And I want to share my candy with him. Maybe we can fix the train together." Mom smiled and hugged Sue. She said to her. "Sue, you are very kind and sweet. You know how to forgive and share. You can go to Tim's room and play with him. But first, let me help you clean up the mess. And maybe we can find a way to fix the train too." The twins learned a lesson that day. They learned that mint candy is not for playing. They learned that toys are for respecting. They learned that sisters and brothers are for loving. They learned that saying sorry and forgiving are important. They learned that mom is always there to help and teach. They learned that playing together is more fun than playing alone.
