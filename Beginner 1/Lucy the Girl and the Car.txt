Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Car

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a lucky triangle. It was shiny and red. She picked it up and showed it to her friend Tom. "Look, Tom! I found a lucky triangle!" Lucy said with a big smile. Tom was very happy for her. They decided to go on an adventure together. They went to the park and saw a small car. They wanted to drive it. Lucy held the lucky triangle tight in her hand. She felt brave. "Let's drive the car!" said Tom. So, they got in the car and started to drive. The car went fast and they laughed. They drove all around the park. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. Lucy knew her lucky triangle made their adventure fun. They couldn't wait for the next adventure.

One day, a big, high tree had a small rag hanging on it. The rag was blue and soft. A little bird saw the rag and wanted it for her nest. The bird flew near the rag and tried to take it. But the rag did not move. The bird tried again, but the rag still did not move. Then, a big wind came and made the rag appear to fly away. The little bird was sad. She wanted the rag for her nest. She looked all around but could not find the rag. She was very sad and went back to her nest. But when the little bird got to her nest, she saw the blue rag was there! The wind had put the rag in her nest. The bird was very happy. She did not know how the rag got there, but she was glad it did. Now, her nest was warm and soft with the blue rag. The bird and her family lived happily in their cozy nest.

One day, a little dog named Spot saw a tasty bone. He wanted to eat it. But, the bone was on a towel. The towel was on a big rock. Spot tried to get the bone, but he could not reach it. He was sad. Spot's friend, a bird named Blue, saw Spot was sad. Blue wanted to help Spot. Blue flew up to the rock and picked up the towel with the bone. Blue told Spot to follow him. Spot followed Blue to a safe place. Blue put the towel and the tasty bone on the ground. Spot was very happy. He ate the bone and wagged his tail. Spot and Blue played together all day. They were very good friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go to the park. He put on his big coat with large pockets. He was very happy because he loved his coat. He asked his mom, "Can I go to the park, please?" His mom said yes, so Tim went to the park. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it, but he was too small. Then, he saw a big dog. The dog was very nice. Tim asked the dog, "Can you help me climb the tree?" The dog said yes, and helped Tim climb the tree. Tim had a lot of fun at the park. He played with the big dog and climbed the tree. When it was time to go home, he put a pretty leaf in his large pocket. He said thank you to the dog and went back home. Tim showed his mom the pretty leaf and told her about his fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big book called a dictionary. He loved to look at the pictures and learn new words. One day, Tim saw a picture of a distant place with lots of snow and ice. He wanted to go there and play. Tim asked his mom, "Can we go to the distant place with snow and ice?" His mom said, "Yes, but we must be careful. If it gets too warm, the snow and ice will melt." They went to the distant place and had fun playing in the snow. They made a snowman and had a snowball fight. But then, the sun came out and it started to get warm. Tim saw the snowman begin to melt. He was sad. Tim's mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can come back when it is cold again, and the snowman will be here." Tim smiled and they went home. He knew he could always visit the distant place with snow and ice again, and his dictionary would help him learn more about it.
