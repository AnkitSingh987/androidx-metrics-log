Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat. The cat loved to play with its toy. One day, the cat saw a cup on the table. It smelled good. The cat wanted to try it. The cat's mom saw the cat near the cup. She said, "I do not recommend you drink that. It is not for you." The cat did not listen. It wanted to see what was in the cup. The cat jumped on the table and looked inside the cup. It saw a brown drink. It was coffee. The cat drank the coffee. It did not like it. The cat felt bad. The cat's mom was right. The cat should not have drunk the coffee. The cat was sad and learned to listen to its mom.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to paint a nice structure. She took her paint and brush and went outside. She saw a big wall and thought it would be the perfect place to paint her nice structure. Lily started to paint. She painted a big house with a nice door and windows. A boy named Tim saw her and came over. "Wow, that's a nice structure!" he said. "Can I help you paint it?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, you can help me." Tim and Lily painted together. They were having fun. But then, a big wind came and knocked over their paint. The paint spilled all over the ground. Lily and Tim were sad. They could not finish their nice structure. The wind had ruined their fun day.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They like to swing on the swings. They swing high and low. They have fun. One day, a new kid comes to the park. His name is Sam. He wants to swing too. But there are only two swings. Anna and Ben do not want to share. They say, "Go away, Sam. These are our swings. You cannot swing with us." Sam feels sad. He looks around. He sees a network of ropes. The ropes make a big spider web. Sam thinks, "Maybe I can swing on the ropes. That looks fun too." He goes to the ropes. He climbs on them. He swings from one rope to another. He laughs. Anna and Ben hear Sam laugh. They stop swinging. They look at Sam. They see him swing on the ropes. They think, "That looks fun too. Maybe we can swing on the ropes with Sam." They get off the swings. They go to Sam. They say, "Hi, Sam. Can we swing on the ropes with you?" Sam smiles. He says, "Sure, you can swing with me. There are many ropes. We can all swing together." Anna and Ben smile too. They climb on the ropes. They swing with Sam. They have fun. The moral of the story is: Sharing is good. You can make new friends and have more fun when you share.

One day, a little boy wanted to put a frog in the pond. He asked his father for help. His father said he needed someone reliable to help him, so he called his grandfather over. The grandfather said yes, and put the frog on his shoulder. The little boy was so excited and followed him with big steps. When they arrived at the pond, the little boy looked at the frog, and said: “Frog, you can jump in now!” The frog looked at them, then jumped into the water. The boy's father smiled and said: “Grandfather, you are so reliable.” The little boy was very proud that his grandfather made the frog jump in the pond. He hugged his grandpa tightly.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He loved to make it go fast across the floor. Tim's mom was wearing her soft bathrobe while she watched him play. She smiled and said, "Tim, you are very good at playing with your car." Tim looked at his mom and said, "Thank you, Mom! I want to race my car with my friends. Can I go outside and play with them?" His mom thought for a moment and said, "Yes, but remember to be kind and share your toys with others." Tim went outside and found his friends. They all played with their toy cars, making them go fast. Tim remembered what his mom said and let his friends play with his car too. They all had fun and played together. At the end of the day, Tim's friends thanked him for sharing his toy car. They all learned that sharing is a good thing and makes everyone happy. Tim felt proud and knew that his mom would be happy too. The moral of the story is to always share with others and be kind, because it brings happiness to everyone.
