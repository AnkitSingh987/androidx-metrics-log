Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Barber and the Danced

One day, a barber named Tom went to a party. At the party, there was a lot of dancing. Tom loved to dance, so he danced and danced. He danced so much that he became dizzy. Tom was so dizzy that he fell down on the floor. At the same time, a little girl named Sue was at the party too. Sue was sad because she did not know how to dance. She saw Tom fall down and felt bad for him. Sue wanted to help Tom, so she went to him and asked if he was okay. Tom said he was fine, but he was still dizzy. Then, Sue had an idea. She asked Tom to teach her how to dance. Tom agreed, and they started dancing together. As they danced, Tom began to feel less dizzy. In the end, they both became good friends and danced the night away.

One day, Tim and his dad went to the shop. They saw a filthy magazine on the floor. Tim's dad picked it up and said, "Look, Tim, this is a joke magazine. It has funny things to make us laugh." They went home and started reading the magazine. Tim's dad read a joke out loud, "Why did the chicken go to the shop? To get some eggs!" They both laughed. Suddenly, the magazine started talking! It said, "I can tell jokes too!" Tim and his dad looked at each other, surprised. They never thought a magazine could talk. They all laughed and read jokes together. It was a fun day with their new talking friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a secret. He liked to play fight with his toys. Every day, he would go to his room and play with his toys, making them fight each other. One day, Tim saw a distant hill. He thought it would be fun to take his toys there and play fight. So, he put his toys in a bag and walked to the hill. It was a big hill, but Tim was strong and brave. When he got to the top of the hill, Tim took his toys out of the bag. He made them fight under the big, blue sky. Tim was so happy. He knew his secret was safe and he could play fight with his toys any time he wanted.

Sara and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the park after school. One day, Sara had a new toy. It was a big, red ball. She wanted to show it to Tom. "Look, Tom, I have a new ball. Do you want to play with me?" Sara asked. "Yes, please. Your ball is very nice. Can I kick it?" Tom said. Sara nodded. She was happy to share her ball with Tom. They took turns kicking the ball in the grass. They had a lot of fun. But then, the sun started to go down. It was evening. The sky was dark and the park was quiet. Sara and Tom felt cold and scared. "Let's go home, Sara. It's too dark and scary here," Tom said. "OK, Tom. But where is my ball? I can't see it," Sara said. They looked around, but they could not find the ball. It was gone. Someone had taken it. "Who took my ball? That's not fair. I liked my ball," Sara said. She started to cry. Tom felt sorry for Sara. He wanted to help her. He had an idea. "Sara, don't cry. I have something for you. It's not a ball, but it's something nice. Do you want to see it?" Tom said. He reached into his backpack and took out a small, yellow teddy bear. He had won it at the fair. He liked it, but he liked Sara more. "Here, Sara. This is for you. I offer you my teddy bear. You can keep it. It's soft and warm and friendly. It will make you feel better," Tom said. He gave the teddy bear to Sara. Sara stopped crying. She smiled. She hugged the teddy bear. "Thank you, Tom. You are very kind. I like your teddy bear. It's very nice. You are my best friend," Sara said. They held hands and walked home. They were not scared anymore. They were happy. They had each other.
