Category: Beginner
Title: Sarah the Girl and the Kite

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah who was very excited. One day she found a kite in her backyard and couldn't believe it. She jumped up and down with joy and then quickly picked up the kite so she could go outside and fly it. As Sarah was running, she stumbled and nearly fell but her mom quickly caught her. "Be careful, Sarah," said her mom. Sarah nodded and then nodded again and grinned widely. Her mom smiled, gave Sarah a big hug, and waved as she ran outside. Once outside, Sarah couldn't stop staring at her kite. She was so excited, she nearly forgot how to fly it. She breathed in, blinked and pulled the kite string. Suddenly, the kite was in the air, soaring high above her head. Sarah watched in amazement and felt the wind on her face. She couldn't believe how lucky she was to have such an exciting kite. She ran and ran in circles, chasing her kite until the sun went down. Her heart was full of happiness as she imagined all the fun she'd have with her new kite.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big post with a sign on it. The sign said "Do not climb". Tom wanted to climb the post. He thought it would be fun. Lily said "No, Tom. The sign says no. It is not safe. You can fall and hurt yourself." But Tom did not listen. He ran to the post and started to climb. He went up and up and up. He reached the top of the post. He felt very proud. He looked around and saw the trees and the birds and the sky. He waved to Lily. Lily waved back. She was worried. Then Tom felt something strange. He felt dizzy. His head was spinning. He lost his balance. He screamed. He fell from the post. He landed on the grass. He was not hurt, but he was scared and sad. He cried. Lily ran to Tom. She hugged him. She said "It's okay, Tom. I'm here. You are brave, but you are also silly. You should not climb the post. You should listen to the sign. And to me. I am your friend. I want you to be safe and happy." Tom nodded. He said "I'm sorry, Lily. You are right. I was silly. I will not climb the post again. I will listen to you. You are my friend. You are smart and kind. Thank you for helping me." They smiled. They hugged. They went back to playing. They had fun. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a purple cat. The cat was very nice. The cat liked to help others. One day, the cat met a dog. The dog was sad. The dog had a hurt paw. The cat wanted to help the dog. The cat said, "I will lend you my toy." The dog was happy. The toy made the dog feel better. The cat and the dog played with the toy. They had a lot of fun. But the dog's paw still hurt. The cat took the dog to a vet. The vet is a person who helps animals feel better. The vet looked at the dog's paw. The vet helped the dog. The dog's paw felt better. The cat and the dog were happy. They went home and played with the toy.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful prince. He liked to play with his friends in the big park. One day, he saw a new friend and wanted to join him. The prince went to the new friend and said, "Hi! Can I join you?" The new friend said, "Yes, you can join me. Let's play with my ball." They played and had a lot of fun together. But then, a big rain came. The prince and his new friend got very wet. They ran to find a dry place, but they could not find one. The prince and his new friend got sick from the rain. They could not play together anymore. The prince was very sad.
