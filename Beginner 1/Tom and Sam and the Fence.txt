Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Fence

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play with cars and blocks. But sometimes they quarrel. They quarrel about who gets the red car or the big block. They quarrel so loud that Mom gets angry. One day, they are playing in the yard. They see a flame in the sky. It is a big, bright flame. It is a rocket. The rocket is going to the moon. Tom and Sam want to see the rocket. They run to the fence. But the fence is too high. They cannot see the rocket. Tom has an idea. He is smart. He says, "Let's get a stool. We can stand on the stool and see the rocket." Sam likes the idea. He is smart, too. He says, "OK. You get the stool. I will wait here." Tom runs to the house. He gets the stool. He brings it to the fence. But when he comes back, he sees Sam on the fence. Sam has climbed the fence. He is looking at the rocket. He says, "Wow! The rocket is so cool!" Tom is mad. He says, "Hey! That's not fair! You said you would wait for me!" Sam says, "Sorry. I wanted to see the rocket. You can see it, too. Just climb the fence." Tom does not want to climb the fence. He wants to stand on the stool. He says, "No. I got the stool. You should get off the fence. We can share the stool." Sam says, "No. I like the fence. You can climb the fence. It's easy." They start to quarrel. They quarrel so loud that Mom hears them. Mom comes to the yard. She sees Tom and Sam quarreling. She sees the stool and the fence. She sees the rocket in the sky. She is not happy. She says, "Stop quarreling, boys. You are both smart. You can both see the rocket. But you have to be nice to each other. You have to take turns. Tom, you can stand on the stool for a minute. Then you can give it to Sam. Sam, you can stay on the fence for a minute. Then you can get off and let Tom climb. OK?" Tom and Sam nod. They say, "OK, Mom." They stop quarreling. They take turns. They see the rocket. They say, "Wow! The rocket is so cool!" They smile at each other. They are happy. Mom is happy, too. She says, "Good boys. I'm proud of you. Now, let's go inside and have some cookies." Tom and Sam say, "Yay! Cookies!" They run to the house. They forget about the stool and the fence. They only remember the rocket and the cookies. And they remember to be nice to each other. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a wild sheep named Sam. Sam had lots of wool. One day, Sam went to the river to pour water on his wool. He wanted to clean his wool because it was dirty. While Sam was pouring water, a big wind came. The wind took Sam's wool and carried it far away. Sam was sad because his wool was gone. He did not know what to do. Then, a bird came and gave Sam a big surprise. The bird had found Sam's wool and brought it back to him! The bird had also made the wool into a warm, soft blanket. Sam was so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Sam and the bird were best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to play outside. She looked up at the big, open sky and saw a big red balloon. She wanted to hold the balloon, so she jumped and jumped, but she could not reach it. A little boy named Tim saw Lily and asked, "Why are you jumping?" Lily said, "I want to hold the red balloon in the sky." Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's find a big stick to help you reach it!" They looked for a big stick, but they could not find one. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky with the red balloon in its beak. The bird dropped the balloon, and Lily was able to hold it. Lily and Tim were so happy. They thanked the bird and played with the red balloon all day long. In the end, they learned that sometimes, help can come from the most unexpected places.
