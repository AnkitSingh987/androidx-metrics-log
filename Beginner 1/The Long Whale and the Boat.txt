Category: Beginner
Title: The Long Whale and the Boat

Once upon a time, in a big blue sea, there was a long whale. The long whale loved to swim and play with his friends. They would splash and jump in the water all day long. One day, the long whale saw a little boat. He wanted to peek at the people in the boat. So, he swam very close and lifted his big head out of the water. The people in the boat were so surprised to see the long whale peek at them. The long whale and the people in the boat became friends. They would wave and smile at each other every time the whale would peek from the water. They all had so much fun together and lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two buttons for eyes. They were very happy. "Look, Lily, I found a mitten!" Ben said. He picked up a red mitten from the ground. It was soft and warm. "Wow, Ben, that's a nice mitten. Where did you find it?" Lily asked. "I found it near the tree. Maybe someone lost it. Can I keep it?" Ben said. "Sure, Ben, you can keep it. But you have to share it with me. It's cold and my hand is freezing," Lily said. "Okay, Lily, we can share it. You can wear it on your left hand and I can wear it on my right hand. Then we can hold hands and be warm," Ben said. They put on the mitten and held hands. They walked around the snowman and sang a song. They were very happy. Then they saw something shiny on the tree. It was a flower. It was yellow and pink and it smelled good. It was a bloom. "Look, Lily, a bloom! How did it grow in the snow?" Ben said. "I don't know, Ben, but it's very pretty. Maybe it's a magic bloom. Maybe it can make a wish come true," Lily said. "Really? What do you wish for, Lily?" Ben asked. "I wish for a tasty snack. I'm hungry and I want something sweet," Lily said. "Me too, Lily. I wish for a tasty snack too. Maybe a cookie or a cake," Ben said. They closed their eyes and made a wish. They opened their eyes and saw a basket on the ground. It was full of cookies and cakes and fruits and juice. It was a tasty snack. "Wow, Lily, our wish came true! The bloom gave us a tasty snack!" Ben said. "Wow, Ben, you're right! The bloom is magic! Thank you, bloom!" Lily said. They ran to the basket and ate the tasty snack. They shared it with the snowman too. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a weird foot. It was not like the other kids' feet. Tim was sad because he didn't understand why his foot was different. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's foot and said, "Hey, your foot looks weird. But that's okay! We can be friends." Tim smiled and they played together all day. They had lots of fun. As they played, other kids saw Tim and Sue having fun. They wanted to play too. Soon, Tim had many friends who didn't care about his weird foot. They all understood that it's okay to be different. The moral of the story is: It's good to be kind and accept others, even if they look different.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to match his socks with his shoes. He thought it made him look attractive. One day, Tim went on a trip to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was wearing a pretty dress with colorful socks. Tim went to Sue and said, "I like your socks. They match your dress." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. I like your shoes and socks too. They look nice together." Tim and Sue became friends and played together in the park. As they played, Tim and Sue learned that it was not just their clothes that matched, but their hearts too. They shared their toys and helped each other when they fell. The moral of the story is that being kind and helpful to others is what truly makes you attractive.
