Category: Beginner
Title: The Fat Cat and the Dog

One day, a fat cat went for a walk. She saw a big bucket. The fat cat wanted to play with the bucket. She pushed the bucket with her nose. The bucket rolled and made a loud noise. A small dog heard the noise. He ran to see what was happening. The small dog saw the fat cat and the bucket. The bucket belonged to the small dog. The small dog was not happy. He wanted the fat cat to stop playing with his bucket. The fat cat saw the small dog. She knew the bucket did not belong to her. The fat cat felt bad. She stopped playing with the bucket. The small dog was happy again. The fat cat and the small dog became friends. They played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a small, humble cat near a drain. The cat looked sad and hungry. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he went to his mom and asked, "Mom, can we feed the cat?" His mom said, "Yes, we can feed the cat. Let's get some food for it." Tim and his mom went to the kitchen and found some food. They took it outside to the cat. Tim kneeled down next to the cat and put the food near it. The cat started to eat the food. Soon, the cat was happy and full. It meowed and rubbed against Tim's leg. Tim smiled and said, "I'm glad we could help the cat. I want to be kind to all animals." His mom smiled too and said, "That's a good thing to do, Tim. I'm proud of you."

One day, a curious little girl named Mia went to play outside. She saw her dad pack a big bag. She asked, "Daddy, what are you packing?" Her dad said, "I am packing oxygen for our trip." Mia didn't know what oxygen was, but she was excited for the trip. On the trip, they went to a big hill. Mia and her dad climbed the hill. When they got to the top, they saw a big, pretty bird. The bird was blue and green. Mia and her dad were very happy to see the bird. But then, something unexpected happened! The bird started to talk! The bird said, "Hello, Mia and Dad! I need your help. Can you give me some oxygen?" Mia and her dad were very surprised. They gave the bird the oxygen from the bag. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you!" Then, the bird flew away, and Mia and her dad went home. They had a great trip and a fun story to tell.

Tim and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They saw some flowers and ran to smell them. They were very pretty and smelled good. But then Tim heard a buzz. He looked and saw a bee. He was scared of bees. He thought they would sting him. He ran away from the flower. He did not see the rock. He tripped and fell. He hurt his knee. He cried. Lily heard Tim cry. She ran to him. She saw his knee. It was red and had a little blood. She felt sorry for him. She said, "Are you okay, Tim? Does it hurt a lot?" Tim nodded. He said, "Yes, it hurts. And I'm scared of the bee. It was so big and loud. I don't like bees." Lily hugged him. She said, "It's okay, Tim. The bee is gone. It was not trying to hurt you. It was just looking for nectar. Bees are good. They make honey. And they help the flowers grow. You don't have to be scared of them." Tim felt a little better. He said, "Really? Bees are good? They make honey? I like honey. But I don't like to fall. I'm so clumsy. I always fall." Lily smiled. She said, "You're not clumsy, Tim. You're just adventurous. You like to explore and have fun. Sometimes you fall, but you always get up. You're brave and strong. And I'm your friend. I'll always help you." Tim smiled too. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You're a good friend. You're kind and smart. And you're not scared of bees. You're amazing." They hugged again. They felt happy. They went to the house. They asked for a band-aid and some honey. They ate the honey and put the band-aid on Tim's knee. They felt better. They went back to the garden. They played some more. They had fun.
