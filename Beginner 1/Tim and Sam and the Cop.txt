Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Cop

Tim and Sam were friends who liked to play with cars. They had many cars of different colors and sizes. They liked to make noises and pretend they were driving fast. One day, they saw a big car parked outside their house. It was a cop car, with a blue light and a loud siren. They wanted to play with it. "Let's go see the cop car!" Tim said. "OK, but be careful. We don't want to get in trouble," Sam said. They ran to the cop car and opened the door. They saw a radio, a gun, and a badge. They thought they were very cool. "Look, I'm a cop!" Tim said. He put on the badge and picked up the gun. "Me too!" Sam said. He grabbed the radio and pressed a button. Suddenly, the siren started to make a loud noise. The boys were scared. They tried to turn it off, but they did not know how. They heard a voice on the radio. "Who is this? What are you doing in my car?" the voice said. The boys did not answer. They wanted to run away, but they were stuck. They saw a man running towards them. It was the cop. He was angry. "Hey, you! Get out of my car right now!" the cop shouted. The boys struggled to get out of the car. They dropped the gun, the radio, and the badge. They ran to their house, but the cop was faster. He caught them and grabbed them by their arms. "You are in big trouble, kids. You could have hurt someone with that gun. You are coming with me to the station," the cop said. The boys cried and begged for mercy. They said they were sorry and they just wanted to play. But the cop did not listen. He put them in the back of his car and drove away. The boys were not successful. They were in big trouble. They wished they never played with the cop car.

Once upon a time, there was a big red jeep. The jeep went on a drive to an unknown place. It was very fun and the sun was shining. The jeep went fast and slow, up and down hills. The people in the jeep were happy. One day, the jeep went to a big wet road. The jeep did not know that the road was slippery. The jeep tried to go fast, but it could not. The people in the jeep were scared. Then the jeep started to slip on the wet road. It went left and right, and the people in the jeep could not make it stop. The jeep slipped off the road and into a big tree. The jeep was broken and the people were sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. He lived in a small house next to a big church. Buddy loved to play in the church yard every day. One sunny day, while Buddy was playing, he saw a little bird. The bird looked sad and lost. Buddy wanted to help the bird and understand why it was sad. So, he asked the bird what was wrong. The bird told Buddy that it had a secret. The secret was that the bird was not really a bird, but a magic friend who could talk! Buddy was very surprised, but now he had a new friend to play with in the church yard. They played together every day and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Lily. Lily was a tiny dancer who lived in an enormous house. She loved to dance all day long. One day, it rained a lot, and Lily's house got wet. Lily said to her friend, "Oh no, my house is wet. I need to dry it!" Her friend, a big bear, came to help her. The bear used a big fan to dry the house. He blew and blew, and the house started to dry. When the house was dry, Lily and the bear danced together. They danced and laughed all day long. The sun was shining, and they were happy. The enormous house was filled with fun and joy.
