Category: Beginner
Title: The Dancer and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a dancer. The dancer was very flexible. She could bend and twist her body in amazing ways. She loved to dance and make people smile. One day, the dancer was dancing in the park. She was dancing so fast that she could not stop. People watched her and clapped their hands. They liked her dancing, but they worried she might get tired. A little boy saw the dancer and wanted to help her. He ran up to her and said, "Stop, dancer, stop!" The dancer heard the boy and slowed down. She finally stopped dancing and smiled at the boy. "Thank you," she said. They became good friends and danced together in the park every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a normal key. The key was very special to a little boy named Tim. Tim and the key were best friends. They played together all day long. One day, Tim wanted to try something new. He took the key and tried to open a big box. The box was heavy and hard to move. Tim tried and tried, but the box would not open. Tim felt sad and started to cry. The key felt bad too. They could not open the box. Tim tried one last time, but still, the box did not open. Tim and the key were very sad. They never found out what was inside the big box. The end.

Once, there was a boy named Jack. He was three years old and loves to play. Everyday, he went to the park and climbed the monkey bars. One day, Jack wanted to lift himself over the top. He tried very hard but he couldn't do it and he got sad. Then, Jack's dad came to help and said, "Let me help you, Jack. I'll lift you. I know you can do it. Close your eyes and try." Jack said, "Can I do it? I'm not sure." Jack's dad said, "Yes, Jack you can do it. Just listen to your heart." And Jack closed his eyes and concentrated really hard. Then, all of a sudden Jack was able to lift himself with his dad's help. He was so happy and proud! But, Jack's dad was feeling a bit selfish. He wished that he could have done it all on his own.

Sam and Tom are brothers. They like to play with their toys and dress up. One day, they find a razor in the bathroom. It is shiny and sharp. "Look, Tom, a razor!" Sam says. "Dad uses it to shave his beard. It is cool!" "Can I see?" Tom asks. He takes the razor from Sam and holds it in his hand. He feels the edge. It is smooth and cold. "Let's wear it!" Sam says. He has an idea. He puts the razor on his head like a hat. He thinks he looks like a superhero. "Wow, Sam, you look cool!" Tom says. He wants to wear it too. He puts the razor on his ear like an earring. He thinks he looks like a pirate. They run to the mirror to see themselves. They laugh and make funny faces. They are having fun. But then, Mom comes in. She sees the razor on their heads. She is scared and angry. "Sam! Tom! What are you doing?" she yells. "That is not a toy! That is a razor! It can hurt you! Give it to me right now!" She takes the razor from them and puts it away. She checks their heads and ears for cuts. She is relieved that they are not hurt. She hugs them and says, "I love you, but you have to be careful. Never play with razors or other sharp things. They are not for wearing. They are for shaving. Do you understand?" Sam and Tom nod. They are sorry. They say, "We understand, Mom. We are sorry. We won't do it again." Mom smiles and says, "Good. Now, go and play with your toys. And next time, wear something safer, like a hat or a scarf." Sam and Tom say, "Okay, Mom." They go to their room and play with their toys. They wear hats and scarves. They are still cool.
