Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Sam and the Ball

Ben and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a bat. They want to play cricket. They find a big field. It is empty. No one is there. They are happy. They put the ball on the ground. Sam hits the ball with the bat. The ball flies far away. "Wow, good hit!" Ben says. He runs after the ball. He wants to catch it. But he does not see a hole. He falls into the hole. He hurts his knee. He cries. "Ben, are you okay?" Sam says. He runs to the hole. He sees Ben. He is sad. "I'm sorry, Ben. I did not see the hole. Do you want to go home?" Sam says. Ben nods. He is in pain. He wants his mom. Sam helps him out of the hole. They pick up the ball and the bat. They walk to the gate. On the way, they see a big book. It is on a bench. It has many pictures and words. It is a book about animals. "Look, Sam. A book. Can we see it?" Ben says. He forgets his knee. He likes books. "Okay, Ben. Let's see it." Sam says. He likes books too. They sit on the bench. They open the book. They see a picture of a cricket. It is a small bug. It has long legs and wings. It makes a loud sound. "Wow, a cricket. Like our game." Ben says. "Yes, a cricket. But not like our game. It is an animal. It lives in the grass. It eats plants. It sings at night." Sam says. He knows a lot. He likes to study. "Really? How do you know?" Ben says. He is curious. "I study. I read books. I watch TV. I ask questions. I learn." Sam says. He is proud. "That's cool, Sam. I want to study too. I want to learn." Ben says. He is eager. They smile. They are friends. They like to play. They like to study. They look at the book. They see more animals. They learn more words. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to polish her shoes. She would make them shine so bright. Lily also loved to eat jelly. Her mom would make her jelly sandwiches for lunch. One day, Lily saw a high tree in her yard. She thought, "I want to climb that tree." So, she put on her shiny shoes and started to climb. As she climbed higher and higher, she found a big jar of jelly on a branch. Lily was so happy to find the jelly. She took it home to show her mom. Her mom was proud of her for climbing so high. They made jelly sandwiches together and ate them for lunch. And Lily's shoes stayed shiny, even after her big adventure.

Sara likes chicken. Chicken is yummy. Sara sees a chicken in the farm. She wants to eat the chicken. She runs to the chicken and tries to catch it. But the chicken is fast. The chicken runs away from Sara. The chicken goes to the fence. The fence has a hole. The chicken goes through the hole. Sara follows the chicken. But the hole is small. Sara gets stuck in the hole. She cannot go through. She cannot go back. She is sad and scared. She cries for help. But no one hears her. The farmer is far away. The other animals are busy. The sun is hot. Sara is thirsty and hungry. She cannot eat the chicken. She cannot eat anything. Sara is in trouble. She wishes she did not chase the chicken. She wishes she was nice to the chicken. But it is too late. The chicken is gone. Sara is alone. The end.

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make noises like vroom and beep when they drive them on the floor. One day, they find a new car in their toy box. It is white and shiny. It has a big number on the side. It is zero. Ben and Sam wonder what zero means. "Can I drive zero?" Ben asks Sam. "No, I want to drive zero!" Sam says. They both grab zero and pull. They start to fight. They yell and cry. They are not quiet. Mom hears them and comes to see what is wrong. She sees Ben and Sam fighting over zero. She is not happy. "Boys, stop fighting!" Mom says. "Zero is not a real car. It is a remote control car. You need a remote to make it move. And you need to share. Fighting is not nice." Mom shows them the remote. It has buttons and a wheel. She turns it on and makes zero move. Ben and Sam are amazed. They see zero go fast and slow, left and right. They hear zero make sounds like honk and screech. "Wow, zero is cool!" Ben says. "Can I try?" Sam asks. Mom gives them the remote. She tells them to take turns and be gentle. Ben and Sam nod. They drive zero together. They have fun. They are quiet.
