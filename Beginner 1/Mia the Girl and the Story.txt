Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to skip and play with her friends. One day, she saw a small, modest house with a shiny tap outside. Mia was very thirsty from all the skipping, so she went to the tap to drink some water. As she drank the water, a kind old lady came out of the house. The old lady saw Mia and smiled. She asked Mia if she was having fun skipping. Mia said yes, and they both laughed. The old lady told Mia that she could always come to her house to drink water from the tap when she was thirsty. Mia was very grateful and thanked the old lady. From that day on, Mia always stopped by the modest house to drink water and visit the kind old lady. She learned that being kind and sharing with others made her happy. The moral value of the story is to always be kind and share with others, because it brings happiness to everyone.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a big toy bear. They call him Bob. Bob is brown and soft. He has a red hat and a blue scarf. One day, Tim and Sam want to make a house for Bob. They find some sticks and leaves. They work hard to build a house. They make a door, a window and a roof. They are happy. They say, "Bob, this is your house. You can sleep here and be warm." But then, they hear a loud roar. They see a real bear. He is big and black. He has sharp teeth and claws. He is mighty. He is angry. He sees the house. He thinks it is his house. He runs to the house. He breaks the door, the window and the roof. He says, "This is my house. Go away!" Tim and Sam are scared. They run to Bob. They hug him. They say, "Bob, help us! The bear is mean. He broke your house. He wants to hurt us!" Bob is brave. He stands up. He looks at the real bear. He says, "No, this is not your house. This is my house. These are my friends. You are bad. You go away!" The real bear is surprised. He sees Bob. He sees his red hat and blue scarf. He thinks, "This is not a real bear. This is a toy bear. He is not mighty. He is silly." He laughs. He says, "You are not a bear. You are a toy. You cannot stop me. I can do what I want." He tries to grab Bob. But then, he hears a loud bark. He sees a dog. He is big and white. He has a collar and a leash. He is Tim's dog. He is Rex. He is mighty. He is angry. He sees the bear. He thinks, "This is a bad bear. He is hurting my friends. He is breaking their house. He is taking their toy. I will stop him." He runs to the bear. He bites his leg. He barks. He says, "Leave them alone! This is their house. This is their toy. You are bad. You go away!" The bear is scared. He feels pain. He hears Rex. He thinks, "This is a real dog. He is mighty. He is not silly. He can stop me. I cannot do what I want." He drops Bob. He runs away. He says, "Sorry, sorry, sorry. I will not come back. I will find another house." Tim and Sam are happy. They thank Rex. They say, "Rex, you are a hero. You saved us. You saved Bob. You scared the bear. You are mighty." They hug him. They give him a bone. They fix the house. They put back the door, the window and the roof. They say, "Bob, this is your house. You can sleep here and be warm. You are brave. You are mighty. You are our friend." They hug him. They play with him. They have fun in the park. They are friends. They are happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a little dog named Spot. They liked to play in the park. One day, while they were playing, a big boy came up to them. He was not nice. The big boy said, "I want to play with your dog, but you can't play with us." This made Lily sad. She didn't want to be left out. So, she raised her hand and said, "No, we all play together or we don't play at all." The big boy thought for a moment and then agreed. They all played together and had lots of fun. Lily even let the big boy put Spot on his shoulder. The boy learned that it was better to be nice and play with everyone.
