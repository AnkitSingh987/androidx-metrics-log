Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Brave

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to be brave. He went to the park with his mom. At the park, there was a big slide. Tim wanted to store his toy in his pocket and go on the slide. Tim climbed up the slide. He was a little scared, but he wanted to be brave. He sat at the top of the slide and looked down. It was very high. But Tim took a deep breath and slid down the slide. As Tim slid down, his toy fell out of his pocket. It broke on the ground. Tim was sad. He was brave, but his toy was gone.

One day, a little girl named Sue was very excited. She found a big chocolate bar in the kitchen. She wanted to taste it. But she knew she should ask her mom first. Sue ran to her mom and asked, "Can I taste the chocolate, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but you must share it with your brother, Tom." Sue and Tom sat down to eat the chocolate. They broke it in half. But, oh no! Tom's half was bigger than Sue's half. Sue was sad and Tom was happy. Then, a little bird flew by and took Tom's chocolate! Tom was sad now too. Sue saw Tom's sad face and decided to share her chocolate with him. They both tasted the chocolate and it was so yummy. They learned that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was very stubborn. Everywhere she went she wanted to do things her way. One day, her mom wanted her to go and watch a film at the cinema. But the little girl didn't want to. She was too stubborn. Her mom tried to talk nicely to her. She said, "Please go and watch the film with me, it will be fun!". But the little girl said no, she wanted to stay at home and play with her toys. Her mom said, "But if you go see the film I will buy you a surprise afterwards". But the stubborn little girl didn't want to go. Finally her mom said, "If you go, I'll buy you an ice cream too!". That was all it took. The little girl agreed to go. She put on her coat and off they went to the cinema. They watched the film and had lots of fun. After the film, they went and bought the ice cream. They laughed and smiled all the way home. The stubborn little girl had enjoyed the film much more than she thought she would. She realised that sometimes it's okay to do what her mom said.

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a big bin on the ground. The bird wanted to see what was inside the bin. So, it flew down and looked inside. There were many things in the bin, like food and toys. The bird was happy to find so many things. The bird saw a big kite in the bin. The bird wanted to fly with the kite. So, it picked up the kite and started to soar high in the sky. The bird and the kite were flying together, and they were having so much fun. Then, the bird saw a little girl on the ground. The girl was sad because she lost her kite. The bird knew that the kite belonged to the girl. So, it flew down and gave the kite back to the girl. The girl was so grateful to the bird. She said, "Thank you, bird, for giving me back my kite!" The bird was happy to help the girl. It learned that it is good to be kind and help others. The bird and the girl became friends, and they played together every day. And they always remembered to be kind and share with others.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big box in the garage. They open the box and see many things inside. They see a ball, a doll, a car, a hat and a bear. Sara likes the bear. She picks it up and hugs it. The bear makes a sound. It says "I love you". Sara is happy. She squeezes the bear and says "I love you too". Tom likes the car. He picks it up and pushes it. The car makes a sound. It says "Beep beep". Tom is happy. He squeezes the start and says "Let's go". Sara and Tom play with the bear and the car. They make sounds and stories. They have fun. They are happy. They squeeze each other and say "You are my friend".
