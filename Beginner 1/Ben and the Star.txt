Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Star

Ben was a sailor who loved to cook. He cooked fish and soup and bread on his big boat. He shared his food with his friends, the other sailors. They liked his food very much. One day, Ben saw a bright light in the sky. It was a star. It was very brilliant. Ben wanted to see it closer. He said to his friends, "I want to sail to the star. Maybe it has more food to cook." His friends said, "No, Ben, you can't sail to the star. It is too far and too hot. You will get lost and burned. Stay with us on the boat." But Ben did not listen. He took a small boat and sailed away. He followed the star. He sailed for a long time. He did not see any land or any other boats. He only saw water and the star. Ben got hungry and thirsty. He had no food to cook. He had no water to drink. He wished he had stayed with his friends. He was sorry he did not listen. He cried, "Help, help, I want to go back to the boat." But no one heard him. He was alone with the star. He was very sad. He missed his friends and his food. He learned a lesson. He learned that sailing to the star was not a good idea. He learned that cooking on the boat was better. He hoped he would see his friends again. He hoped they would forgive him. He hoped they would share their food with him. He hoped he would be happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a family with a big bell. They liked to ring the bell every day. It was fun for them. One day, they were all very tired. They did not want to ring the bell that day. The family went to bed early because they were tired. They forgot to ring the bell. The sun went down and it was dark outside. But something was going to happen. In the night, a big cat came to the family's house. The cat saw the bell and wanted to play. The cat jumped and hit the bell. The bell made a loud ring. The family woke up and saw the cat. They were happy that the cat rang the bell for them. The family and the cat became friends and rang the bell together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big red hat. She loved to wear her hat when she went outside. Sue's mom said, "Be careful with your hat, Sue. Don't lose it." One day, Sue went to the busy park to play. She saw lots of kids playing and having fun. Sue put her hat on a bench and went to play, too. She had so much fun that she didn't notice her hat was gone. Sue looked everywhere for her hat. She was very sad. Then, she saw a dog with her hat in its mouth. The dog was playing with her hat! Sue ran to the dog and said, "That's my hat!" The dog dropped the hat and wagged its tail. Sue was so happy to have her hat back.

Tim and Mia were playing with their toy boats in the bath. They pretended that the bath was a big sea and the edge was a port. They made noises like waves and horns and engines. "Look at my boat, it's faster and stronger than yours!" Tim said. He pushed his boat towards Mia's and bumped it hard. "Hey, stop that! You're being mean!" Mia said. She tried to move her boat away, but Tim followed her and bumped it again. "Ha ha, I'm going to destroy your boat and take over the port!" Tim said. He bumped Mia's boat so hard that it flipped over and sank. Mia started to cry. She was sad and angry that Tim ruined her boat and her game. She grabbed Tim's boat and threw it out of the bath. "Hey, what did you do that for?" Tim said. He was shocked and scared that Mia threw his boat. He heard a loud crash and saw that his boat hit the mirror and broke it. Mom came running into the bathroom. She saw the broken mirror, the wet floor, and the crying children. She was very upset and angry. "What happened here? Who broke the mirror?" Mom said. Tim felt embarrassed and ashamed. He knew he was wrong to be mean to Mia and to break the mirror. He pointed at Mia and said, "She did it!" Mia felt hurt and betrayed. She knew Tim was lying and trying to blame her. She pointed at Tim and said, "He did it!" Mom looked at both of them and sighed. She knew they were both in trouble. She said, "You both did it. You both need to say sorry to each other and to me. And you both need to help me clean up this mess. And no more playing with boats in the bath!"
