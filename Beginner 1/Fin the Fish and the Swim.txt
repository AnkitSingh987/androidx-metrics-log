Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Swim

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in the sea with his family. The sea had big waves called tides. Sometimes, the tides were difficult for Fin to swim in. One day, Fin saw a big fish who could swim very fast in the difficult tides. Fin wanted to learn how to swim like the big fish. So, he asked the big fish to teach him. The big fish said, "Repeat after me, and you will swim fast too." Fin listened to the big fish and repeated his moves. He swam up and down, left and right, just like the big fish. Soon, Fin was able to swim fast in the difficult tides too. He was very happy and thanked the big fish for helping him.

Once upon a time there was a jolly little girl named Mia. One day Mia found a cube and began playing with it. She enjoyed rolling it around and watching it go. Mia's brother, Jack, saw the cube in her hand and wanted it for himself. He asked her for it, but Mia refused. Jack began to get angry. He shouted, “I want that cube! Give it to me!” But Mia held on tight to the cube. She shook her head and said, “No, it's mine.” Suddenly, Mia had an idea. She said, “Let's have a contest! Whoever wins gets to keep the cube!” Jack agreed. They agreed that whoever could roll the cube the farthest would win. Mia and Jack both rolled the cube and kept track of how far it went. In the end, Mia rolled it further than Jack. She was the winner! Mia was happy to keep the cube, but she also learned an important lesson that day. She realized it is more important to be nice and to play together than to compete for something.

Sam and Tom are friends. They like to play with tools. They have hammers, saws, nails and wood. They want to make a house for their toys. "Let's make a mighty house!" Sam says. "We can use all our tools and wood!" "Yes, let's do that!" Tom agrees. "We can hammer and saw and nail!" They start to work. They hammer and saw and nail. They make walls and a roof and a door. They are very happy. "Look, we made a mighty house!" Sam says. "It is big and strong and nice!" "It is the best house ever!" Tom says. "We are very good with tools!" They clap their hands. They are proud of their work. They put their toys inside the house. They play with them. They have fun. They are happy.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big red ball. They want to catch the ball. "Let's catch the ball!" Tom says. "It looks fun!" "OK!" Jen says. "But we have to be careful. The ball is near the road." They run to the ball. They take turns to throw and catch the ball. They laugh and have fun. But then a big car comes. The car is fast and loud. The car does not see the ball. The ball rolls to the road. "Look out!" Jen says. "The ball is in the road!" Tom does not hear Jen. He wants to catch the ball. He runs to the road. He does not see the car. The car hits Tom. Tom falls down. He does not move. He does not laugh. He does not have fun. Jen screams. She runs to Tom. She cries. She does not want to play with the ball. She wants Tom to be OK. The car stops. The driver gets out. He is sorry. He calls for help. He gives Jen a treat. He says it is for Tom. But Jen does not want the treat. She wants Tom to wake up. She wants Tom to be her friend. She wants Tom to catch the ball with her. But Tom does not wake up. Tom is gone. Jen is sad. The ball is red. The end.

Once upon a time, in a bright and big theater, there lived a funny clown named Bobo. Bobo made people laugh and smile every day. He loved his job and was very happy. One day, Bobo did a trick that went wrong. He fell and broke his toy. He felt regret and was very sad. Bobo thought no one would laugh at him anymore. He did not want to go back to the theater. But then, something unexpected happened. When Bobo went back to the theater, all his friends were there. They had fixed his toy and made it even better than before! Bobo was so happy and surprised. He learned that his friends loved him no matter what.
