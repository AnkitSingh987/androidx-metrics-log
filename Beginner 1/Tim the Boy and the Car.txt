Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Car

One day, a little boy named Tim found a small motor. He was very excited. He wanted to use the motor to make his toy car go fast. Tim put the motor in the car and went outside to play. Tim had a big seat in his yard where he liked to sit and watch his car go. He put the car on the ground and turned on the motor. The car went very fast. Tim was so happy. But then, a big dog came into the yard. The dog saw the fast car and started to chase it. Tim was frightened. He picked up his car and ran to the seat. He climbed up and hugged his car tight. The dog could not get him there. Tim and his car were safe on the seat.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim found a rod. He wanted to use the rod to play. Tim had a stupid idea. He thought he could use the rod to poke his dog, Spot. Tim poked Spot with the rod. Spot did not like it. Spot barked at Tim. Tim felt bad. He knew it was not nice to poke Spot with the rod. Tim thought of a better way to use the rod. He tied a toy to the rod. He played with Spot by waving the toy on the rod. Spot was happy, and Tim learned to use the rod in a nice way.

Once upon a time, in a big park, a little boy named Tim saw a clear puddle. The sun was shining bright, and the birds were singing. Tim was very happy to play outside. Tim ran to the puddle and kneeled down. He put his hands in the water and splashed it around. The water felt cool and nice on his hands. He saw his face in the water and smiled. After playing in the puddle, Tim saw his friend, Lily. They played together in the park all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, they went home and told their moms about their great day in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little old house. The house was near the sea. In the house lived a kind man named Tom. Tom had a big hat. One day, a big hurricane came. The wind blew and blew. Tom was scared. He put on his big hat and went outside. He saw a little bird in a tree. The bird was scared too. Tom said to the bird, "Do you want to come inside?" The bird said, "Yes, please!" Tom offered his hand and the bird jumped on. They went back inside the old house. They were safe from the hurricane.

Tom and Sue were going to the farm with their mom and dad. They had to pack their bags with clothes and toys. Tom was very happy. He wanted to see the cows and the pigs and the chickens. Sue was not happy. She did not like the farm. She wanted to stay at home and watch TV. "Tom, hurry up and pack your bag. We have to go soon," mom said. "OK, mom. I am almost done. I want to bring my truck and my ball and my bear," Tom said. "Sue, what are you doing? Why are you not packing your bag?" dad asked. "I don't want to go to the farm. It is boring and dirty and smelly. I want to stay here and watch TV," Sue said. "Sue, don't be foolish. The farm is fun and beautiful and fresh. You will like it when you get there. Now, pack your bag or you will be left behind," dad said. Sue did not listen to dad. She did not pack her bag. She hid under the bed and hoped they would forget her. She thought she was very smart. Mom and dad and Tom got in the car and drove to the farm. They did not see Sue under the bed. They thought she was in the car with them. They did not know she was foolish. Sue was alone in the house. She was happy at first. She turned on the TV and watched her favorite show. She ate some cookies and drank some juice. She played with her dolls and her books. She did not miss the farm at all. But soon, she got bored. She had watched all the shows. She had eaten all the cookies. She had played with all the dolls and books. She had no one to talk to or play with. She felt lonely and sad. She wanted to see mom and dad and Tom. She wanted to go to the farm. She wanted to say sorry for being foolish. She ran to the door and tried to open it. But it was locked. She ran to the window and tried to open it. But it was locked too. She ran to the phone and tried to call mom and dad. But it was dead. She cried and cried. She did not know that a storm was coming. She did not know that the wind was blowing hard. She did not know that the rain was falling fast. She did not know that the lightning was striking near. She did not know that the fire was starting in the kitchen. She did not know that the smoke was filling the house. She did not know that the roof was falling down. She did not know that the root was breaking the wall. She did not know that she was in danger. She did not know that she was foolish. She did not know that she was going to die.
