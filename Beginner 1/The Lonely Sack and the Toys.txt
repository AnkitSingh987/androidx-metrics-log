Category: Beginner
Title: The Lonely Sack and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a lonely sack. The sack was sad because it had no friends. One day, the sack met a small boy. The boy said, "Hi sack, will you be my friend?" The sack was happy to have a friend. The small boy and the sack played all day. They played with toys and ran in the park. But then, the boy saw another friend. The boy left the sack to play with his other friend. The sack was sad again. It wanted to play too. The sack tried to join the boy and his friend. But the friend said, "No, sack, you cannot play with us." The sack was very sad and felt all alone. The lonely sack went away to find a new friend. It looked and looked, but no one wanted to be its friend. The sack became sadder and sadder. In the end, the sack was all alone and never found a friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Spot. Spot loved to go for a jog with his best friend, a boy named Tim. They would run and play together in the park every day. One sunny day, while they were on their jog, Spot saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it, so he asked Tim to lift it up and throw it for him. Tim lifted the ball and threw it far away. Spot ran as fast as he could to catch it. Spot brought the ball back to Tim, and they played with it all day long. They had so much fun on their jog and playing with the ball. When it was time to go home, Tim lifted Spot up and gave him a big hug. They were very happy and couldn't wait to play together again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely garden. It had many pretty flowers and big trees. The birds liked to sing in the trees. The sun always shined on the garden, making it bright and happy. One day, a big storm came. The wind blew hard and the rain fell down. The lovely garden was not happy anymore. The flowers were sad and the trees were hurt. A kind girl named Lily saw the garden. She wanted to help make it lovely again. Lily worked hard to fix the flowers and trees. She helped restore the garden back to its happy self. Soon, the sun shined again and the birds sang their happy songs. The lovely garden was back, all thanks to Lily.

Once upon a time, in a cold land far away, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to pretend she was a queen. She would wear a pretty dress and a shiny crown. She would tell her friends, "I am Queen Mia of the cold land!" One day, while Mia was playing in her pretend land, she met a little boy named Tim. Tim was pretending to be a knight. He had a big sword and a strong shield. Mia said to Tim, "Hello, Knight Tim! Will you help me protect my cold land?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Queen Mia! I will help you keep the land safe." Together, Queen Mia and Knight Tim had many fun adventures in the cold land. They fought dragons and saved the people. At the end of the day, they would sit by the warm fire and share stories. And they became the best of friends in their pretend land.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is black and has sharp teeth. The dog barks at them. "Let's go and pet the dog!" Tom says. He likes dogs. "No, Tom, don't!" Sam says. He is scared of dogs. "The dog is not nice. He can bite you. Mommy always warns us to stay away from strange dogs." But Tom does not listen to Sam. He runs to the dog. The dog growls and snaps at Tom. Tom screams and runs back to Sam. "Are you okay, Tom?" Sam asks. He hugs Tom. "I'm okay, Sam. But the dog hurt me. He bit my hand. It is red and sore." Tom says. He cries. Sam sees a man near the dog. The man is holding a leash. The man is angry. "Hey, you kids! Why did you bother my dog? He is not friendly. He is a guard dog. He protects me from bad people. You are lucky he did not bite you harder." The man says. "Sorry, mister. We did not know. We just wanted to pet the dog. We like dogs." Tom says. "Well, you should not pet dogs you don't know. They can be dangerous. You should listen to your friend. He was smart to warn you. Now go home and tell your mommy what happened. She can help you with your hand." The man says. Tom and Sam nod. They say goodbye to the man and the dog. They run home to their mommy. They tell her everything. Mommy is not happy. She scolds Tom for being careless. She cleans his hand and puts a bandage on it. She hugs him and says she loves him. She is glad he is not badly hurt. She gives him a cookie. She hugs Sam and thanks him for being a good friend. She gives him a cookie too. Tom and Sam are sorry. They promise to be more careful next time. They are glad they have each other. They are glad they have mommy. They are glad they have cookies.
