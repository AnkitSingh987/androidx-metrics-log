Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Max

Lily was a little crab who liked to stay in her shell. She had many colors on her shell, like red, blue and yellow. She thought her shell was very pretty and special. But some other crabs did not like her shell. They said it was too bright and loud. They said she should find a new shell that was more plain and boring. One day, Lily met a new crab named Max. Max had a big shell that was brown and green. He said hello to Lily and asked her if she wanted to play. Lily was happy and said yes. They played in the sand and the water and had fun. Max liked Lily's shell and said it was very popular. He said he had never seen a shell like hers before. He said she should be proud of her shell and not listen to the mean crabs. Lily smiled and thanked Max. She felt happy and confident. She liked Max and his shell too. She said he was very kind and brave. She said she wanted to be his friend. Max agreed and said he wanted to be her friend too. They hugged and decided to stay together. They did not care what the other crabs said. They liked their shells and each other. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play and have fun with his friends. One day, Tim found a pair of sunglasses on the ground. They were so cool! He put them on and felt very special. Tim and his friends were very competitive. They liked to play games and see who could win. They played a game of hide and seek, and Tim was very good at it. He said, "I'm going to win this time, too!" His friends were excited to play and find out who would win. As they played, Tim hid in a really good spot. But when he hid, his sunglasses fell off. He didn't know they were gone. When the game was over, Tim noticed he didn't have his sunglasses. He said, "I miss my sunglasses. Where did they go?" His friends helped him look for the sunglasses, and they found them in the spot where he hid. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you for helping me find my sunglasses!" They all laughed and played more games together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was going to his first day of class. Tim was very serious about learning new things. He put on his favorite shirt and his big boy shoes. In class, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue was sad and began to cry. Tim asked, "Why do you cry, Sue?" Sue said, "I miss my mom and dad." Tim wanted to help Sue. He said, "Don't cry, Sue. We can play and learn together. Our moms and dads will come back soon." Sue stopped crying and smiled at Tim. They played and learned together all day. They were very happy in class.

One day, Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. Tim saw a big hill and wanted to go down fast on his bike. He put on his helmet to keep his head safe. Spot saw Tim's helmet and knew they were going to have fun. The helmet was shiny and strong. Tim and Spot went up the hill. Tim got on his bike and started to go down the hill very fast. Spot ran beside him, wagging his tail. They were having so much fun. But then, Tim saw a big rock in the way. He had to pause and stop his bike. Tim stopped just in time. He did not hit the rock and did not get hurt. Spot was happy that Tim was safe. They went back up the hill and tried again. This time, there were no rocks in the way. Tim and Spot had a great day at the park, and the helmet kept Tim safe.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the grass with his dog, Spot. One day, Tim and Spot were running and laughing in the grass. "Spot," said Tim, "I like playing with you in the grass!" Spot barked and wagged his tail. They were having so much fun. But then, Tim stepped on something sharp in the grass. "Ouch!" cried Tim, as he fell down. He was hurt and sad. Spot barked and tried to help, but he didn't know what to do.
