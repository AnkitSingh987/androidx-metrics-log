Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to play with her pretty veil. One day, she went to the park with her mom. Lily was very impatient to play with her friends. At the park, Lily and her friends played a game. They would pass the veil to each other. They all had fun. But, Lily was not careful. She ran too fast and fell down. The veil was dirty and torn. Lily was sad. Her pretty veil was ruined. Her mom tried to fix it, but it was not the same. Lily learned to be more careful when she plays. But, her veil was never pretty again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite vest. He wore it every day. It was a soft and warm vest. He loved it very much. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, your vest is too small. We need to increase its size." Tim was sad. He didn't want a new vest. He wanted his old vest. But his mom was right. The vest was getting too small. Tim and his mom went to a shop. They found a new vest. It was not like his old vest. It was harsh and not soft. Tim didn't like it. He said, "Mom, I don't want this vest. I want my old vest." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will find a soft vest like your old one." They looked more and found a soft vest. Tim was happy. He wore his new vest and it was just as good as his old one.

Once, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom loved to talk to his friends all day. One day, Tom found a helmet near his house. He thought it was a special helmet, so he put it on his head. Tom went to the park to show his friends his new helmet. When they saw him, they laughed because the helmet was very big. Tom felt sad, but he did not know why they laughed. He still thought his helmet was special. Then, something unexpected happened. The helmet started to talk! It said, "Hello, I am a talking helmet!" Tom's friends were so surprised. They stopped laughing and started to talk to the helmet too. Now, Tom and his friends loved the helmet and had fun talking to it.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they find a big hill. They want to go up the hill. "Let's push our bikes up the hill," Anna says. "OK," Ben says. "But it is hard to push." They push and push. They are tired and sweaty. But they do not give up. They reach the top of the hill. "Wow, look at the view!" Anna says. "We can see the whole park. And the sky is so open and blue." "It is beautiful," Ben says. "But how do we go down?" Anna smiles. She has an idea. "Let's ride our bikes down the hill," she says. "It will be fun and fast." "OK," Ben says. "But be careful." They get on their bikes. They hold the brakes. They let go. They zoom down the hill. They feel the wind in their hair. They laugh and shout. They reach the bottom of the hill. They are happy and proud. "We did it!" Anna says. "We did it!" Ben says. They high-five each other. They are the best friends.

Once upon a time, in a great big forest, there lived a big bear. The bear loved to protect the trees and the animals who lived there. He was a kind and friendly bear. One day, the big bear heard a loud sound. It was the sound of lumber being cut down. The big bear was afraid for his friends, the trees. He ran to see what was happening. But when he got there, he saw something he did not expect. Instead of a mean person cutting down the trees, it was a little girl. She was not cutting down the trees to be mean. She was building a new home for her animal friends. The big bear saw this and felt happy. He decided to help the little girl protect the forest and build homes for all the animals. And they all lived happily ever after.
