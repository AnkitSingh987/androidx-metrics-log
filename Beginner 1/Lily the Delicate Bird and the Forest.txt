Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Delicate Bird and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a delicate bird named Lily. Lily lived in a pretty nest on a big tree. She loved to fly and sing with her friends. One day, while Lily was playing, she got trapped in a net. She tried to escape, but the net was too strong. Lily was very sad and scared. A kind squirrel named Sam saw her and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." Sam worked hard to break the net. Soon, Lily was free! She was so happy to escape. "Thank you, Sam!" said Lily. "You are a good friend." They played together and became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. He would move it all around the house. One day, Tim saw a big bag of powder in the kitchen. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it would be fun to play with. Tim's mom saw him looking at the powder and said, "Be careful, Tim. That powder can make a big mess." Tim didn't listen because he was ignorant. He took the bag and went to his room to play with his toy car. In his room, Tim opened the bag and poured the powder on the floor. He moved his toy car through the powder, making a big mess. Suddenly, his mom came in the room and saw the mess. She was not happy. She said, "Tim, I told you to be careful with the powder. Now we have to clean up this big mess." Tim felt bad and helped his mom clean up the powder. From that day on, he always listened to his mom's advice.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He was three years old and lived with his family in a cozy house. Early one morning, Jack was ready for an adventure. So he put on his cool hat and stepped outside. He walked along and saw a curious rod sticking out of the ground. He looked around, but nobody else was there. So he stepped closer to get a better look. Suddenly, a voice called out from behind him, "Hey! What are you doing there?" Jack jumped back and turned around. It was an old man with a kind smile. "I was just looking at that rod," Jack replied. The old man smiled. "As long as you don't touch it, it's cool," he said. Jack stepped away from the rod and thanked the old man. With that, they both said goodbye and went their separate ways. And Jack continued on his adventure, happy and safe.

Tim and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. Tim has a dog. His name is Spot. Spot is deaf. He cannot hear Tim when he calls him. One day, Tim and Jen go to the park with Spot. Tim does not have a chain for Spot. He says, "Spot will stay with me. He is a good dog." Jen says, "Are you sure? The park is big. There are many cars and people. Spot might get lost." Tim and Jen run and play. Spot follows them. He is happy. He likes to smell the grass and the flowers. He does not see the cars and the people. He does not hear the horns and the shouts. Tim and Jen stop to rest. They sit on a bench. They drink some water. They do not see Spot. Spot sees a squirrel. He likes squirrels. He runs after the squirrel. He does not see Tim and Jen. He does not hear them call him. Tim and Jen look for Spot. They are scared. They cannot find him. They ask the people in the park. They say, "Have you seen a dog? He is brown and white. He is deaf. He does not have a chain." The people shake their heads. They say, "No, we have not seen a dog like that." Tim and Jen are sad. They cry. They think they have lost Spot. They do not know what to do. They wish they had a chain for Spot. They wish Spot could hear them. Spot chases the squirrel. He does not catch it. He is tired. He stops. He looks around. He does not see Tim and Jen. He does not see the park. He sees a street. He sees cars and people. He is scared. He does not know where he is. He does not know how to go back. He wishes he could hear Tim and Jen. He wishes he could see them.
