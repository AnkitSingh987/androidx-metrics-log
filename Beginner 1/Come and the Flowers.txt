Category: Beginner
Title: Come and the Flowers

"Come on Mike, let's go outside and play." said Mom. "Okay, mommy," said Mike. So they went outside and Mike started to grow a garden. He dug some little holes and planted some flowers. Mom said, "This is a big task, but you can do it. Just take it one step at a time." Mike was excited to try, so he went to work. He watered the plants and made sure they got plenty of sun. As time went on, the flowers started to grow and Mike was so happy to see them. Mom said, "See, I knew you could do it! It was easy, wasn't it?" Mike smiled and said, "Yes, it was easy and fun."

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy went to a restaurant with her mom and dad. They sat at a big table with lots of chairs. Lucy felt a little uncomfortable because the chair was too big for her. She tried to sit still, but she kept wiggling around. Lucy's mom and dad looked at the food list. They picked some yummy things to eat. Lucy started to worry because she didn't know what to pick. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Lucy. I will pick something yummy for you too." When the food came, Lucy's mom had picked a big plate of yummy noodles for her. Lucy was so happy and not uncomfortable anymore. She ate all her noodles and didn't worry at all. The restaurant was a fun place for Lucy and her family.

Sam liked to play with his red cap. He wore it every day, even when it was hot or cold. He said his cap made him strong and fast. His mom and dad smiled and said he was a good boy. One day, Sam went to the park with his mom. He saw a big slide and wanted to try it. He ran to the slide and put his cap on his head. He climbed up the ladder and looked down. The slide was very high and long. Sam felt scared. He wanted to go back, but there were other kids behind him. They said, "Hurry up, Sam, we want to slide too!" Sam did not know what to do. He wished his mom was there to help him. He looked at his cap and remembered what he said. His cap made him strong and fast. He decided to be brave and slide. He closed his eyes and pushed himself down. He felt the wind in his face and the sun on his back. He opened his eyes and saw his mom waiting for him at the bottom. She clapped and hugged him. She said, "You did it, Sam, you are a big boy!" Sam felt happy and proud. He said, "Thank you, cap, you are my best friend. You helped me become brave and have fun." He put his cap on his head and smiled. His mom and dad smiled and said he was a reliable boy. They loved him very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab. He was deaf, but he was still very happy. He lived in a big sea with many friends. They would play all day long. One sunny day, the crab and his friends had a party. They had fun games and yummy food. They played hide and seek, and the crab was very good at hiding. His friends would laugh and clap when they found him. At the end of the day, the crab and his friends were very tired. They said goodbye and went to their homes. The little deaf crab had the best day ever. He knew he had the best friends in the whole wide sea.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a distant hill and decided to go there. On the way, Tim found a small bag of powder. He showed it to his mom, and she said, "That's baby powder, Tim. We can use it later." When they reached the top of the hill, Tim wanted to play a game. He said, "Mom, let's play hide and seek!" His mom agreed, and they started to play. Tim hid behind a big tree, and his mom started to look for him. After a while, Tim's mom could not find him. She started to panic. She called out, "Tim, where are you?" Tim heard his mom and came out from behind the tree. He said, "Here I am, Mom!" His mom was so happy to see him. She hugged him tight and said, "Let's go home now, and we'll use the baby powder for your baby sister." They both smiled and walked back home, holding hands.
