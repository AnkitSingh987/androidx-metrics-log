Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom Find the Box

Sara and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to act like pirates and look for gold. They had a map and a shovel and a big hat. "Arrr, matey, where is the gold?" Tom asked Sara. "It is under the big tree, see?" Sara pointed to the map. "We have to dig there." They ran to the big tree and started to dig. They dug and dug until they saw something shiny. "Look, we found it!" Sara shouted. "It is gold!" They pulled out a small box with a lock. They opened it and saw many gold coins and jewels. "Wow, we are rich!" Tom said. "We can buy anything we want!" They hugged and laughed. They were very happy. But then they heard a voice. "Hey, what are you doing with my box?" It was an old man with a beard and a cane. He looked angry. "This is our gold, we found it!" Sara said. "No, it is not, it is mine!" the old man said. "I buried it here long ago. Give it back!" Sara and Tom were scared. They did not want to fight with the old man. "Sorry, sir, we did not know," Tom said. "We were just playing. Here, take your box." They gave the box to the old man and ran away. They felt sad and sorry. They did not mean to take his gold. They went back to the park and sat on a bench. "Maybe we should not act like pirates anymore," Sara said. "Maybe not," Tom agreed. "But we can still play other things." They looked at the sky and saw a rainbow. It was bright and beautiful. They smiled and held hands. They still had each other. And that was more than gold.

One day, a kid was playing with his toy car. He felt happy as he pushed the toy car around the room. The kid's mom was watching him play and smiled. She saw how much fun he was having. The kid's friend came over to play. They started to race their toy cars. The kid's friend was very fast, and the kid felt helpless. He couldn't make his toy car go as fast as his friend's. The kid felt embarrassed and said, "I can't race as fast as you." Then, something unexpected happened. The kid's toy car started to go faster and faster! The kid was surprised and happy. He raced with his friend, and they both had a lot of fun. In the end, it didn't matter who was faster. They were just happy to play together.

Mommy and her little girl were out shopping. The little girl stepped into a restaurant and said "I'm so hungry, Mommy!" Mommy laughed and said, "Let's have a look at the menu." The little girl's eyes lit up when she saw all the different foods on the menu. She felt so proud of herself for being able to read the words all on her own. "I want the burger," she said to Mommy. Mommy smiled, and told her daughter she could order whatever she liked. The little girl stepped up to the counter and said, "I'd like a burger please!" The waiter smiled and asked what kind of toppings she wanted. The little girl's face beamed with joy. She had never felt so proud in her life. She proudly ordered her burger and skipped away with Mommy to their table.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to stir jam. She stirred jam all day long. One day, she wanted to share her jam with her friend, Tom. Mia went to Tom's house and said, "Tom, I made jam. Do you want to try some?" Tom was happy and said, "Yes, Mia! I love jam!" They both sat down to eat the jam. But Mia forgot to bring spoons to eat the jam. Mia and Tom went to find spoons. While they were gone, a cat came into the house. The cat saw the jam and jumped on the table. The cat ate all the jam. Mia and Tom came back and saw the cat. Tom said, "Oh no! The cat ate all the jam!" Mia was sad. They could not eat the jam anymore. The cat ran away, and Mia and Tom were left with no jam. They both felt sad and went back to their homes.
