Category: Beginner
Title: The Light and the Moon

One day, in a dark room, there was a little light. The light had a name. Its name was Shine. Shine liked to make the room bright and happy. Shine had a friend named Moon. Moon was big and round. Moon would come out at night, when it was dark. Shine and Moon liked to play together. One night, Shine and Moon played hide and seek. Shine would hide and Moon would try to find Shine. They had lots of fun. When it was time to go, Shine made the room bright again. Moon said bye and went away. Shine was happy to help make the dark go away.

Once upon a time, there was a busy little bee named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to sort things. One day, Buzzy saw a big print on the ground. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was important. Buzzy went to his friend, a wise old owl named Ollie. "Ollie, I found a big print on the ground. What is it?" asked Buzzy. Ollie smiled and said, "Oh, that print is from a big animal. Be careful, Buzzy." Buzzy was very brave. He wanted to find the big animal. He followed the print all day long. At last, Buzzy found a big bear named Benny. Benny was sad because he lost his favorite toy. Buzzy helped Benny sort through the things in his cave. They found the toy and Benny was happy. Buzzy and Benny became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a wish. He wanted to see a star up close. Every night, he looked at the distant stars in the sky. One day, Tim met a kind old man. The old man said, "I can help you see a star. But first, you must close your eyes and make a wish." Tim closed his eyes and wished very hard. When Tim opened his eyes, he saw a tiny star in the old man's hand. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you! Now, I can see a star up close." The old man smiled and went away. Tim looked at the star and knew his wish had come true.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a pair of pants that he thought were very boring. The pants were all one color and had no pictures or fun things on them. Tim wanted to make his pants more fun. One day, Tim found a pair of scissors. He had an idea to cut shapes into his boring pants. He carefully cut out a circle, a square, and a triangle. Tim was very happy with his new pants. Tim wore his new pants to play with his friends. All his friends liked the shapes he cut into his pants. They thought it was fun and wanted to do the same with their pants. Now, Tim and his friends all had fun pants to wear while they played together.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys in Lily's room. Lily has many toys, but Tom has only one. It is a blue car. Tom loves his car very much. One day, Lily asks Tom, "Can I play with your car, please?" Tom thinks for a moment. He wants to be nice to Lily, but he also worries about his car. He says, "OK, but be careful. It is my only car." Lily takes the car and drives it on the floor. She makes noises like vroom, vroom. She has fun with the car. But then, she sees a big stain on the carpet. It is red and sticky. It is juice. Lily thinks, "Oh no, the car will get dirty. Tom will be sad." Lily stops playing with the car. She looks for a cloth to clean the stain. She finds one in the drawer. She rubs the stain with the cloth. But the stain does not go away. It only gets bigger. Lily feels bad. She says, "Sorry, Tom. I made a mess. I tried to clean it, but I couldn't." Tom sees the stain and the car. He is not angry. He is glad that Lily tried to clean it. He says, "It's OK, Lily. Don't worry. The car is still clean. And the stain is not your fault. It was an accident. We can tell mom and she will help us." Lily and Tom hug each other. They are still friends. They put the car and the cloth away. They wait for mom to come and fix the stain. They hope she will not be mad. They request a snack. They are hungry. They smile. They are happy.
