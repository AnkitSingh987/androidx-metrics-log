Category: Beginner
Title: The Clumsy Bear and the Win

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy bear who wanted to win something. So one day, the bear decided to sign up for a race. On the day of the race, the bear felt excited and nervous. As soon as the race started, the bear ran as fast as it could and tried to win. After the race, the other animals saw the result. The clumsy bear won the race. Everyone was so surprised because they didn't expect the bear to win. Everyone clapped and cheered for the bear. The bear was so happy that it had won something for the first time. The bear knew that with practice, it will be able to win more things in the future. From that day on, the clumsy bear was determined to practice hard and win.

One day, a big, tough dog named Max went for a walk. Max saw a little cat on the left side of the road. The cat looked sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, little cat?" The cat said, "I can't find my way home. I am lost." Max said, "Don't worry, I will help you find your home." They walked together to look for the cat's home. As they walked, Max saw more and more cats. The number of cats increased. Max asked the new cats, "Do you know where this little cat's home is?" One of the new cats said, "Yes, we know! Follow us!" They all walked together, and soon, they found the little cat's home. The little cat was happy and said, "Thank you, Max! You are a good friend." Max was happy to help and went back to his own home, feeling proud.

Sam and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But they do not like to clean their room. They are stubborn. One day, their mom says, "Sam and Mia, you have to clean your room. It is very messy. You have to put your toys and books in the right places. And you have to polish your shoes. They are very dirty." Sam and Mia say, "No, mom. We do not want to clean our room. We want to play. Cleaning is boring. And we do not know how to polish our shoes." Mom says, "You have to learn how to polish your shoes. It is easy. You need a cloth and some polish. You rub the polish on your shoes and then you rub the cloth on your shoes. They will shine. And you have to clean your room. It is good for you. You will feel happy and proud. And you will be ready for school tomorrow." Sam and Mia say, "School? We do not want to go to school. We want to stay home and play. School is hard. We do not like to learn." Mom says, "You have to go to school. School is fun. You will learn many things. You will make friends. You will play games. And you will grow up. Maybe one day, you will go to university. University is a big school. You will learn more things. You will have more friends. You will do many things. You will be happy." Sam and Mia think for a while. They look at their mom. They look at their room. They look at their shoes. They say, "Okay, mom. We will clean our room. And we will polish our shoes. And we will go to school. And maybe one day, we will go to university. But can we play for a little bit more?" Mom smiles. She says, "Yes, you can play for a little bit more. But then you have to clean your room and polish your shoes. And tomorrow, you have to go to school. And maybe one day, you will go to university. I love you, Sam and Mia. You are my smart and sweet twins." Sam and Mia hug their mom. They say, "We love you, mom. You are our kind and good mom." Then they go to play with their toys and books. But they remember to clean their room and polish their shoes. And they are not so stubborn anymore.
