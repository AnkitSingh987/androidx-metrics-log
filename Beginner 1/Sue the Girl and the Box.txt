Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was a glad girl who loved to display her toys. She had a favorite toy car that could zoom very fast. Each day, she would play with her car and make it zoom all around her room. Sue loved her car and was so happy to have it. One day, Sue's toy car went zoom into a big box. Sue looked in the box and saw many more toy cars! She was so happy to see all the cars that she started to display them with her favorite car. Sue thought she had found a secret box of cars to play with. But then, Sue's mom came into the room. She saw Sue with the cars and laughed. "Sue, those are your brother's toy cars!" she said. Sue was surprised but still glad. Now, Sue and her brother could play with the cars together and make them all zoom around the room. They had so much fun and were happy to share their toys.

Sara and Tom were playing with a big red ball in the park. They liked to bounce the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they bounced it very high and it went over the fence. Then they had to run and get it back. One day, when they were bouncing the ball, they felt the ground shake. It was an earthquake. The ball bounced away from them and rolled down the hill. They saw cracks in the road and people screaming. They were scared and held each other. "Where is our ball?" Sara asked. "I don't know. Maybe it is gone," Tom said. They saw an elderly lady sitting on a bench. She looked calm and smiled at them. She had a cane and a hat. "Hello, children. Are you okay?" she asked. "Yes, we are okay. But we lost our ball. It was our favorite ball," Sara said. The lady nodded. She pointed to a tree. "Look, there is your ball. It bounced up and got stuck in the branches. Do you want me to help you get it?" Sara and Tom looked at the tree. They saw their ball. They were happy. "Yes, please. Thank you, lady," Tom said. The lady got up and walked to the tree. She used her cane to poke the ball. It bounced down and landed in Sara's arms. "Here you go, children. Now you can play again. But be careful. The earthquake might come back," she said. Sara and Tom hugged the lady. They thanked her again. They bounced the ball to each other and smiled. They were not scared anymore. They had their ball and a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to go to the store with his mom. One day, they went to the store to buy food. Tim was very happy. At the store, Tim's mom gave him a big cart. Tim was a little anxious because the cart was very big. But he wanted to help his mom. So, he pushed the cart with all his strength. While shopping, they met a nice lady. She could not reach a box on a high shelf. She asked Tim, "Can you please help me?" Tim wanted to help, so he requested his mom to lift him up. His mom lifted him, and Tim gave the box to the lady. The lady said, "Thank you!" Tim felt very proud and happy to help.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the kitchen. They see a big pan on the stove. The pan has a cover. "Let's see what is in the pan," Tom says. "No, we should not touch the pan. It may be hot," Sue says. But Tom does not listen. He takes the cover off the pan. He sees soup. The soup is bubbling. It smells good. "I want to taste the soup," Tom says. He puts his hand in the pan. He feels pain. The soup is very hot. It burns his hand. He screams. Sue runs to him. She sees his hand. It is red and swollen. She feels sorry for him. "Tom, you are silly. You should have listened to me. Now you are hurt," Sue says. She helps him to the sink. She runs cold water on his hand. She wraps a cloth around it. Tom cries. He is sad and sorry. He learns a lesson. He should not touch things that are not his. He should listen to his friends. He should be careful in the kitchen.
