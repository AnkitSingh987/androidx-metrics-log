Category: Beginner
Title: The Bear and the Door

Once upon a time there was a bear who was very adventurous. One day, he wanted to explore a new area and set off on a journey. The bear had been travelling for a while and he started to feel tired and hungry. He looked around and saw a big house. 'I will pray the people in the house will help me,' he thought. So he knocked on the door and when the people opened it, they were so pleased to see him. They gave him some food to eat, and some warm soap to wash with. The people were very kind and loved to hear the bear's stories of his adventures. They all prayed together that he would have a safe journey. The bear said goodbye and continued on his journey feeling much better. He was now ready for more adventures!

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys. Today, they have a new toy. It is a yellow taxi. It has four wheels and a horn. "Beep beep!" Tom says. He makes the taxi drive on the floor. He pretends he is a driver. He picks up Lily and says, "Where do you want to go?" Lily smiles. She says, "I want to go to the park. I want to see the swings and the slide." "OK," Tom says. He drives the taxi to the door. He stops and says, "We are here. That will be two cookies, please." Lily laughs. She gives Tom two cookies from her pocket. She says, "Thank you, driver. Have a nice day." She gets out of the taxi and runs to the park. She is playful. She swings high and slides fast. She has fun. Tom follows her. He brings the taxi with him. He wants to play with Lily. He sees a big dog. The dog is playful, too. It barks and wags its tail. It sees the taxi. It thinks it is a toy. The dog runs to the taxi. It grabs it with its mouth. It shakes it and tosses it in the air. It catches it again. Tom is scared. He says, "No, no, no! That is my taxi! Give it back!" Lily hears Tom. She sees the dog. She says, "Oh, no! The dog has our taxi! We have to get it back!" They run to the dog. They try to get the taxi. The dog thinks they are playing. It runs away from them. It drops the taxi. It picks it up again. Tom and Lily chase the dog. They shout, "Stop, stop, stop! That is our taxi! Drop it, drop it, drop it!" The dog does not listen. It keeps running and playing. It does not drop the taxi. It likes the taxi. It thinks it is its toy.

Once there was a unique bell. It had a big bow on it. The bow was red and pretty. The bell lived in a small town. The town loved the bell because it made them happy. One day, the bell wanted to ring. It wanted to make everyone smile. So, the bell asked a bird to help. The bird was happy to help the bell. The bird flew up and rang the bell. When the bell rang, everyone in the town came to see. They saw the unique bell with the big red bow. They all smiled and clapped. The bell was happy it could make the town smile. The bird was happy too. They all had a fun time together.

Lily and Ben were playing in the living room. They liked to pretend they were grown-ups. They had a sofa, a table, and some toys. They also had an ashtray that Mommy left on the table. It was dirty and smelly, but they thought it looked cool. "Let's have a tea party," Lily said. She put some cups and plates on the table. She gave Ben a toy cake. "Here, this is for you. It's yummy." "Thank you," Ben said. He pretended to eat the cake. He made a funny face. "Mmm, it's so good. Do you want some?" Lily nodded. She took a bite of the cake. She smiled. "It's delicious. You are very creative, Ben. You make the best cakes." Ben felt happy. He liked to make Lily happy. He looked at the ashtray. He had an idea. He picked up the ashtray and put it on a plate. He gave it to Lily. "Here, this is for you too. It's a special cake. It's crunchy and spicy." Lily looked at the ashtray. She did not like it. It was not a cake. It was gross. She did not want to eat it. She made a sad face. "No, thank you, Ben. I don't like this cake. It's not yummy. It's yucky." Ben saw Lily's sad face. He felt sorry. He did not mean to make Lily sad. He wanted to make her laugh. He took the ashtray back. He pretended to eat it. He made a funny noise. "Mmm, it's so good. It's my favorite cake. It's crunchy and spicy and smoky." Lily heard Ben's funny noise. She saw his silly face. She laughed. She knew Ben was joking. He did not really eat the ashtray. He was just being creative. She liked Ben. He was a good friend. She gave him a hug. "You are very funny, Ben. You make me laugh. But don't eat the ashtray. It's not good for you. It's bad." Ben hugged Lily back. He laughed too. He agreed with Lily. He did not want to eat the ashtray. He wanted to eat real cakes. He said, "You are right, Lily. The ashtray is bad. Let's eat more of your cake. It's good." They ate more of Lily's cake. They enjoyed their tea party. They had fun. They were happy. They did not touch the ashtray again. They left it on the table. They hoped Mommy would not be mad.
