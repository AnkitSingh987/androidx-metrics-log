Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Puzzle

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to play with her puzzle. One day, she saw a light near her puzzle. The light was very pretty. Lily was very happy to see the light. Lily wanted to show the light to her mom. She shouted, "Mom, come see the light!" Her mom came to look. But the light was gone. Lily was sad. Her mom said, "It's okay, maybe the light will come back." The next day, Lily saw the light again. This time, she did not shout. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, the light is back. Please come see it." Her mom went with Lily, and they both saw the light. The light was a pretty butterfly! The butterfly flew away, and Lily and her mom smiled. The moral of the story is to always be calm and patient. Good things will come when you are not too loud or too fast.

One day, a big, hairy dog named Max went to the park. Max loved to play with his ball. He saw a bench and sat down. Max was tired from playing all day. A little boy named Tim saw Max on the bench. Tim wanted to play with Max's ball too. Tim asked, "Can you lend me your ball, Max?" Max wagged his tail and gave the ball to Tim. Tim and Max played with the ball together. They had so much fun! After playing, they went back to the bench. Max was happy to have a new friend. They both sat on the bench and watched the sun go down.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play outside in the yard. But they do not like the same things. Anna likes to make snowmen in winter. Ben likes to jump in leaves in fall. They are stubborn and do not want to try new things. One day, their mom says, "It is spring now. The season has changed. Look, there are flowers and birds and bugs. Why don't you go outside and see what you can find?" Anna and Ben go outside. They see a big tree with pink blossoms. Anna says, "Wow, this is pretty. I want to smell the flowers." She runs to the tree and sniffs a blossom. Ben says, "Yuck, flowers are boring. I want to see something cool. Maybe there are worms or spiders under the rocks." He runs to a pile of rocks and lifts one up. But then, something happens. A bee comes out of the blossom and buzzes around Anna's nose. She screams and runs away. A frog comes out of the rock and hops on Ben's hand. He yelps and drops the rock. They run back to their mom and hug her. They are scared and sorry. They say, "Mom, we are sorry. We were stubborn and did not listen to you. We did not know spring had so many surprises. Can we try something else?" Mom smiles and says, "Of course, my loves. Spring has many things to enjoy. How about we go for a walk and see the pond and the ducks and the rainbow?" Anna and Ben nod and say, "Yes, mom. That sounds fun. We want to learn more about spring. Maybe we will like it as much as winter and fall." They hold mom's hand and go for a walk. They see the pond and the ducks and the rainbow. They also see a butterfly and a snail and a kite. They laugh and play and have a good time. They are not stubborn anymore. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a humble pet named Bob. Bob was a little dog who loved to play with his ball. One day, Bob went to the park with his friend, Tom. Tom was a big boy who liked to run and jump. At the park, Bob and Tom saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a big hat and a pretty dress. Sue said, "Hi, Bob and Tom! Do you want to play with me?" Bob and Tom said, "Yes, we want to play with you!" So, they all played together. While they were playing, a big wind came and blew Sue's hat away. Bob, Tom, and Sue were very sad. But then, Bob had an idea. He said, "Tom, if you jump high, you can catch the hat!" Tom jumped up and caught the hat. Sue was so happy that she hugged both Bob and Tom. They all laughed and played together for the rest of the day.
