Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Gas

Tom and Lily were playing with their cars in the yard. They liked to make noises like the cars and pretend they were driving fast. Sometimes they would stop and fill up their cars with gas from a can. They used water for gas, but they did not drink it. One day, a big truck came to their house. It had a sign that said "Gas". Tom and Lily were curious. They ran to the fence and watched the truck. The truck had a long hose that went to a tank in the back of their house. The man who drove the truck got out and opened the tank. "What are you doing?" Tom asked the man. "I'm giving your house some gas," the man replied. "Gas makes your house warm and your stove work. Do you like gas?" Tom and Lily nodded. They liked gas. They liked warm and work. "Can we have some gas for our cars?" Lily asked the man. The man smiled. He was generous. He took a small bottle from his truck and filled it with some gas. He gave it to Lily and said, "Here you go. This is a special gas for your cars. But be careful, don't spill it or drink it. It's not water. It's flammable." "What's flammable?" Tom asked the man. "Flammable means it can catch fire," the man replied. "That's why you have to be careful. Only use a little bit for your cars. And don't play with matches or lighters near it." Tom and Lily thanked the man. They were excited. They took the bottle and went back to their cars. They poured a little bit of gas in each car. They made noises like the cars and pretended they were driving faster. They had fun with their gas. They were careful. They did not spill it or drink it or play with fire. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Jimmy. He wanted to travel and see the world. He waved goodbye to his family and went on his own journey. As he was walking he soon came to a big, strange sign. It was a billboard! Jimmy was surprised. He had never seen one like this before. He wanted to know what it said, so he leaned in close. Suddenly, a harsh voice came from behind: "What are you doing here?!". Jimmy was scared, so he quickly ran away. Back at home, Jimmy told his family all about his journey and the big, strange billboard he saw. But he never mentioned the harsh voice - he was too scared to!

Sam and Ben are young boys who like to play cops and robbers. Sam is the cop and Ben is the robber. They run around the yard and pretend to chase each other. "Stop, robber, stop!" Sam shouts. He has a toy gun and a badge. "No, cop, no!" Ben shouts back. He has a bag of candy and a mask. Sam catches up with Ben and tackles him to the ground. He puts his toy gun to Ben's head and says, "You are under arrest, robber. You have to go to jail." Ben drops his bag of candy and says, "OK, cop, OK. You got me. But can I have one candy before I go to jail?" Sam thinks for a moment and says, "OK, robber, OK. You can have one candy. But only one. And then you have to go to jail." Ben smiles and picks a candy from his bag. He unwraps it and puts it in his mouth. He says, "Thank you, cop, thank you. This is a good candy." Sam says, "You're welcome, robber, you're welcome. But now you have to go to jail." Sam takes Ben by the hand and leads him to the corner of the yard. He says, "This is the jail. You have to stay here until I say you can go." Ben says, "OK, cop, OK. I will stay here. But can I rest for a while? I am tired from running." Sam says, "OK, robber, OK. You can rest for a while. But don't try to escape. I will be watching you." Sam sits on a chair and watches Ben. Ben lies on the grass and closes his eyes. He says, "Good night, cop, good night." Sam says, "Good night, robber, good night."
