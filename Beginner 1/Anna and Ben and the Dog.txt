Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Dog

Anna and Ben are playing in the park. They like to run and jump on the grass. The grass is green and soft. They see some yellow flowers on the grass. They are pretty and smell good. "Look, Anna, yellow flowers!" Ben says. He picks one and gives it to Anna. "For you!" "Thank you, Ben!" Anna says. She smiles and puts the flower in her hair. "You are nice!" They see a big dog running towards them. The dog is black and has sharp teeth. It barks loud and scary. Anna and Ben are afraid. They hold hands and back away. "Go away, dog!" Ben shouts. "You are mean!" The dog does not listen. It jumps on the grass and tries to bite the flowers. It wants to eat them. Anna and Ben love the flowers. They do not want the dog to hurt them. "Stop, dog!" Anna cries. "The flowers are ours!" They run to the flowers and try to protect them. They put their arms around them and hug them tight. The dog is angry and snarls. It comes closer and closer. "Help, help!" Anna and Ben scream. "Someone help us!" A man hears them and comes to the park. He sees the dog and the children. He is the dog's owner. He calls the dog and puts a leash on it. He says sorry to Anna and Ben. "I'm sorry, kids. My dog is naughty. He likes to chase flowers. He did not want to hurt you. Are you okay?" Anna and Ben nod. They are still scared, but they are glad the man came. They look at the flowers. They are still yellow and pretty. They did not get eaten by the dog. "Thank you, mister," Ben says. "You are good." "You're welcome, kids," the man says. "You are brave. You protected the flowers. They are beautiful. Can I have one?" Anna and Ben think. They like the flowers, but they also want to be kind. They give one flower to the man. He smiles and thanks them. He says goodbye and walks away with the dog. Anna and Ben hug each other. They are happy. They still have some flowers. They put them in a vase and take them home. They show them to their mom and dad. They tell them the story of the dog and the man. Their mom and dad are proud of them. They say they are good and brave. They kiss them and hug them. They all have a nice dinner. Anna and Ben go to bed. They dream of yellow flowers and green grass. They are safe and loved. The end.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a chubby cat named Daisy. Daisy liked to play with her toy mouse. One day, she saw a small nail on the floor. She thought it was a toy and started to play with it. Daisy's friend, a bird named Billy, came to visit. He saw Daisy playing with the nail and said, "Daisy, be careful! That's not a toy. It's a nail. It can hurt you." Daisy stopped playing with the nail and thanked Billy for his help. Just then, Daisy's owner came into the room and saw the nail on the floor. He was happy that Daisy didn't get hurt. He picked up the nail and put it away. Then, he gave Daisy a new toy to play with. Daisy and Billy were so happy that they started to applaud. They played together all day, and they knew they would always look out for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb. He would climb trees, chairs, and even his bed. One day, Tim saw a big tree with a high point. He wanted to climb it. Tim started to climb the tree. He climbed up and up. The tree was very tall. Tim felt like he was on top of the world. He could see his house, his yard, and his friends playing. He felt happy and proud. As Tim climbed down, he saw his friends. They wanted to climb too. Tim told them to be fair and take turns. Each friend climbed the tree, one by one. They all reached the high point and felt happy. Tim and his friends had a great day climbing the tree.
