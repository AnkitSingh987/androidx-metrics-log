Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Cat

One day, a dog named Max found a big bone. Max was very happy. He wanted to show his friend, a cat named Luna. Max ran to Luna with the bone in his mouth. When Max found Luna, he said, "Look at my big bone!" Luna looked at the bone and said, "It looks heavy. Can I weigh it?" Max said, "Yes, you can." Luna put the bone on a thing to weigh it. The bone was not heavy at all. Max felt a little stupid. Max and Luna played with the bone all day. They had a lot of fun. Max learned that it is okay to feel stupid sometimes. The next day, Max and Luna found a new toy to play with. They were very happy.

Ben and Mia like to play golf with their dad. They have small clubs and balls that they hit on the grass. Sometimes they hit the ball very far, and sometimes they miss. Dad helps them and says good job. One day, they go to a big golf place with many people. Ben and Mia are anxious. They see other kids who hit the ball very well. They want to do the same, but they are scared. Dad says don't worry, just have fun. Ben goes first. He swings his club and hits the ball. The ball flies in the air and lands on the green. Ben is happy and runs to get his ball. Mia claps and says good job, Ben. Mia goes next. She swings her club and misses the ball. She tries again and misses again. She feels sad and angry. She throws her club on the ground and slaps Ben. Ben cries and says ouch, Mia, why did you do that? Dad sees what happened and runs to them. He picks up Ben and hugs him. He says it's okay, Ben, Mia didn't mean to hurt you. He looks at Mia and says Mia, that was not nice. You don't slap your brother. You say sorry and try again. Mia feels bad and says sorry, Ben. She hugs him and says I love you, Ben. Ben says I love you, Mia. Dad says good, now let's go and play some more golf. They smile and follow dad to the next hole. They have fun and forget about the slap.

One day, a big wild cat and a small dog were playing in the sun. They were running and jumping, having lots of fun. The wild cat was very fast, and the small dog was very funny. "Let's finish our game and find some shade," said the wild cat. "I am very hot!" The small dog agreed and they went to look for a cool place to rest. They found a big tree with lots of shade under it. The wild cat and the small dog lay down and took a long nap. When they woke up, they saw a big surprise! A big wild bird was sitting in the tree. The wild bird looked down at them and said, "Hello, friends! I saw you playing and I wanted to join you. Can I play with you too?" The wild cat and the small dog were very happy to have a new friend. They all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a big town, there was a kind nurse who liked to help people. She was very good at her job. She was also very strong. People would say she was mighty. The nurse had a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom, the nurse. They saw a big tree. Lily looked at the tree and said, "Wow, Mom, that tree is mighty like you!" The nurse smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. I admire how brave you are too." Later, they saw a little boy who was sad. He had fallen and hurt his knee. The nurse went to him and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." She fixed the boy's knee and he was happy again. The boy said, "Thank you, mighty nurse! I admire you!" Lily was proud of her mom. She knew her mom was the best nurse ever. From that day on, Lily wanted to be a nurse too, just like her mom. And one day, she did become a nurse, and everyone admired her just like they admired her mom.
