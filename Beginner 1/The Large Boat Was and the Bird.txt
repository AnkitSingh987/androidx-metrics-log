Category: Beginner
Title: The Large Boat Was and the Bird

One day, a large boat was near the water. The boat was very big and pretty. It was a sunny day, and the boat wanted to look its best. The boat said, "I want to be shiny and clean. I need someone to polish me." A small bird heard the boat and wanted to help. The bird flew down and started to polish the boat with its wings. The boat said, "Thank you, little bird. You are a good friend." The bird was happy to help the boat. They became friends and played together near the water. The large boat was now shiny and clean, thanks to the small bird.

Once upon a time, in a land full of trees, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat fruit. One day, she went to the market with her mom to buy some fruit. At the market, Lily saw many different fruits. She saw red apples, yellow bananas, and green grapes. She wanted to choose the best fruit to take home. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, which fruit should I choose?" Her mom said, "Choose the fruit that you think is the most graceful." Lily thought about it and decided to choose the green grapes. They looked so graceful hanging from their vines. Lily and her mom went home with the green grapes. They washed the grapes and ate them together. The grapes were sweet and juicy. Lily was very happy with her choice. She knew that she had chosen the most graceful fruit.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful cat named Tom. Tom had a friend, a little bird named Tim. Tim was not feeling well. He needed a pill to feel better. But the pill was on a high shelf. Tim could not fly high to get it. Tom wanted to help his friend. He tried to jump high to get the pill. But he could not reach it. Tom felt sad. He did not want Tim to be sick. So, he thought of a plan. He asked a big dog named Max for help. "Please, Max, help me get the pill for Tim," Tom said. Max was a good dog. He wanted to help too. Max stood on his back legs and reached the high shelf. He got the pill for Tom. Tom gave the pill to Tim. Tim took the pill and felt better. Tom and Max were happy they could help their friend. And Tim was happy to have such thoughtful friends.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a fat cat. The cat is black and white. It has a big nose. "Look, a fat cat!" Tom says. "Let's run after it!" "No, Tom, don't!" Sam says. "The cat may be scared. It may scratch you." But Tom does not listen. He runs after the fat cat. The cat sees Tom and runs away. Tom chases the cat. He wants to touch it. The cat runs into a bush. Tom follows the cat. He puts his hand in the bush. But he does not feel the cat. He feels something sharp. It is a thorn. The thorn pokes Tom's hand. Tom cries out. He pulls his hand out of the bush. His hand is red and hurts. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tom says. "My hand is hurt!" Sam runs to Tom. He sees Tom's hand. He feels sorry for Tom. "I told you not to run after the cat, Tom," Sam says. "The cat did not want to play with you. You should be kind to animals. They have feelings too." Tom nods. He is sorry too. He learns his lesson. He says sorry to the cat. He says thank you to Sam. Sam helps Tom to wash his hand. They go back to the park. They play with a ball. They are friends again. The moral of the story is: Be kind to animals. They have feelings too. Listen to your friends. They may know better.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his favorite marble. It was a serious game, and he was trying to win. But suddenly, the marble rolled away and hid under the big chair. Tim was sad. "Where is my marble?" Tim asked his cat, Max. Max looked at the chair and then at Tim. Max wanted to help. So, they both started to search for the marble. They looked under the chair, behind the books, and even in Tim's toy box. But the marble was not there. Then, Max had an idea. He jumped on the chair and looked down. There it was! The marble was stuck in a hole in the chair. Max put his paw in the hole and got the marble out. Tim was so happy, and he hugged Max. "Thank you, Max! You found my marble!" Now, Tim and Max could play together with the marble. And they had the best time ever.
