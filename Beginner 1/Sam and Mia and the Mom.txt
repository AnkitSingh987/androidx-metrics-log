Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Mia and the Mom

Sam and Mia were twins who liked to play in their room. They had many toys and books, but their favorite thing was the lamp. The lamp had a colorful shade with animals and stars on it. When they turned it on, it made their room bright and cozy. One night, their mom said, "It's time for bed, Sam and Mia. Turn off the lamp and brush your teeth." Sam and Mia did not want to go to bed. They wanted to keep playing with the lamp. They said, "Please, mom, can we have five more minutes?" Mom said, "No, you need to sleep. The lamp is not a toy. It is for reading and seeing. If you play with it too much, it might break or get hot. Then you will be sad and hurt. I will allow you to turn it on again tomorrow, but now you must turn it off." Sam and Mia were not happy, but they listened to mom. They turned off the lamp and brushed their teeth. They got into their beds and said good night to mom. Mom kissed them and said, "I love you, Sam and Mia. Sleep well and have sweet dreams." But Sam and Mia were not sleepy. They whispered to each other, "Let's turn on the lamp again when mom is gone. She will not know. It will be fun." They waited until they heard mom close her door. Then they got out of their beds and sneaked to the lamp. They turned it on and smiled. But they did not see that the lamp cord was loose. It made a spark and a pop. The lamp went out and the room was dark. Sam and Mia were scared. They smelled smoke and saw a small fire on the carpet. They screamed, "Mom! Mom! Help!" Mom heard them and ran to their room. She was alert and grabbed a blanket. She threw it on the fire and put it out. She turned on the light and saw Sam and Mia crying. She hugged them and said, "Are you okay? What happened?" Sam and Mia told mom what they did. They said they were sorry and that they were wrong. Mom said, "I'm glad you are safe, but you should have listened to me. The lamp is not a toy. It is dangerous to play with it. You could have burned the house or hurt yourselves. You need to follow the rules and respect the things you have. Do you understand?" Sam and Mia nodded and said, "Yes, mom, we understand. We will not do it again. We will follow the rules and respect the things we have. We love you, mom." Mom said, "I love you too, Sam and Mia. But you need to learn from your mistake. You will not have the lamp for a week. You will use a flashlight instead. And you will help me clean the carpet and buy a new lamp shade. Is that fair?" Sam and Mia said, "Yes, mom, that is fair. We are sorry, mom." Mom said, "It's okay, Sam and Mia. Everyone makes mistakes. But you need to be honest and responsible. And you need to sleep. It's late and you have school tomorrow. Come on, let's go to bed." Mom tucked them in and kissed them. She turned off the light and said, "Good night, Sam and Mia. Sleep well and have sweet dreams." Sam and Mia said, "Good night, mom. Sleep well and have sweet dreams." They closed their eyes and fell asleep. They dreamed of animals and stars, but not of lamps.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the wet grass after it rained. One day, he saw a big, wet frog. The frog was very nice and they became friends. Tim's mom called him inside for lunch. She had made a yummy sandwich. Tim wanted to share his sandwich with the frog. He urged the frog to come inside with him. But the frog said, "No, I like the wet grass." Tim's mom gave him a knife to cut the sandwich in half. Tim was very careful with the knife. He cut the sandwich and took half outside to the frog. They ate lunch together in the wet grass, and it was a very happy day.
