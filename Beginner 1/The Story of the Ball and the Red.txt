Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Red

One day, a small red ball and a big blue ball were friends. They liked to play games together. The red ball said, "Let's have a race to see who is the fastest. The one who gets to the end first will win." The blue ball was excited and agreed to play. They started at the same spot and began to roll. The red ball was fast, but the blue ball was wide and had a hard time rolling. It took him more time to move an inch. Soon, the red ball was far ahead in the race. He looked back and saw the blue ball struggling. The red ball felt bad and decided to wait for his friend. When the blue ball caught up, they rolled together to the end of the race. At the end, the red ball said, "We both win, because we helped each other." The blue ball smiled and agreed. They were happy to be friends and play together.

One day, a dog named Max was walking in the park. It was a wet day, and Max was very wet from the rain. Max saw a big box near a tree. The box had a note on it. The note said, "Please deliver me to Tom's house." Max wanted to help, but he didn't know where Tom's house was. He saw a bird named Sam sitting on a branch. Max asked Sam, "Can you help me deliver this box to Tom's house?" Sam thought Max was stupid for not knowing where Tom's house was. But Sam was a nice bird, so he said, "Yes, I will help you." Sam showed Max the way to Tom's house. They worked together to deliver the box. When they got to Tom's house, Tom was very happy to see his box. He thanked Max and Sam for their help. Max learned that it's okay to ask for help when you don't know something. And Sam learned that it's not nice to think someone is stupid just because they don't know something. The moral value of the story is that we should always be kind and help each other.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a pretty card. The card had a big, red heart on it. She wanted to hang the card on her wall. So, she asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you help me hang this card?" Lily asked. Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, sweetie!" They went to her room and hung the card on the wall. That night, Lily heard a noise outside her window. She felt frightened. She called out, "Mom, I'm scared!" Her mom came in and hugged her. "Don't be frightened, Lily. I'm here." They looked at the pretty card on the wall and felt happy again.

One day, a lively cat named Tom went to the park. He saw a big tree with a toy stuck at the top. Tom wanted to reach the toy, but he was too small. He asked his friend, a tall dog named Max, for help. "Max, can you help me reach the toy in the tree?" Tom asked. Max looked at the tree and said, "I will try, Tom." Max jumped and jumped, but he could not reach the toy. They needed a new idea. A small bird heard Tom and Max and had an opinion. "I can help you reach the toy," said the bird. Tom and Max were surprised. They did not know the bird could help. The bird flew up, grabbed the toy, and brought it down to Tom and Max. They were happy and thanked the bird. The three friends played with the toy all day long.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, open box. Inside the box, there was a shoe. Tim picked up the shoe and saw that it was very old. He felt it tremble in his hand, and he was scared. He ran to his mom and showed her the shoe. "Mom, look at this shoe! It trembles!" Tim said. His mom looked at the shoe and felt it tremble too. She was scared as well. "We should throw it away," she said. But Tim did not want to throw the shoe away. He wanted to keep it. So, he hid it under his bed. That night, as Tim slept, the shoe came to life. It walked around the room and made a big mess. When Tim woke up, he saw the mess and knew it was the shoe. He was very sad that he did not listen to his mom.
