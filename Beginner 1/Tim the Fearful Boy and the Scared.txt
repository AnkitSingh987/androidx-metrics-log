Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Fearful Boy and the Scared

Once upon a time, there was a fearful little boy named Tim. He was scared of the dark. Every night, he would pray to feel safe. He wished for a tool that could help him not be scared. One day, Tim found a tool under his bed. It was a small, shiny light. He was very happy. He thought the light would help him not be scared at night. He could not wait to use it. That night, Tim turned on the shiny light. But the light went out very fast. It did not help him feel safe. Tim was still scared of the dark. He prayed, but the dark stayed. Tim had a bad night and was sad.

Lily and Max were going to the park with Mom. They had a lot of gear to play with. They had a ball, a kite, a bike, and a purple bag. Mom said they had to unpack the gear from the car and carry it to the grass. "Can I ride the bike, Mom?" Max asked. "Yes, but be careful, Max. And stay close to me," Mom said. "Can I fly the kite, Mom?" Lily asked. "Yes, but wait for me, Lily. And don't let go of the string," Mom said. Lily and Max were very happy. They unpacked the gear and ran to the park. Lily took the kite and Max took the bike. They did not listen to Mom. They did not stay close to her. Lily ran with the kite. She saw a big tree. She wanted to fly the kite higher. She let go of the string. The kite flew away. Lily was sad. She lost the kite. Max rode the bike. He saw a big hill. He wanted to go faster. He pedaled hard. He did not see the rock. He hit the rock. He fell off the bike. Max was hurt. He broke the bike. Mom saw Lily and Max. She was angry and worried. She ran to them. She saw the kite was gone and the bike was broken. She saw Lily was crying and Max was bleeding. "What did you do?" Mom asked. "Why didn't you listen to me?" Lily and Max were sorry. They said they were sorry. But Mom was still mad. She said they had to go home. She said they had to leave the gear. She said they had no more fun. Lily and Max were sad. They had no kite, no bike, no ball, and no purple bag. They had no gear. They had no park. They had a bad day.

Lily was a painter. She liked to make pictures with many colors. She had a big box of paints and brushes. She was very happy when she painted. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom. She saw a chubby boy sitting on a bench. He looked sad. He had no toys or friends. Lily wanted to help him. She went to the boy and said, "Hi, I am Lily. Do you want to play with me?" The boy looked up and said, "Hi, I am Ben. I have nothing to play with. My mom is sick and we have no money." Lily felt sorry for Ben. She had an idea. She said, "I have something for you. Wait here." She ran to her mom and asked for her box of paints and brushes. She said, "Mom, can I give these to Ben? He has nothing and he is sad. I want to make him happy." Her mom smiled and said, "That is very kind of you, Lily. You can give them to Ben. But remember, you will not have them anymore. Are you sure you want to do that?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, mom. I have many toys at home. Ben has none. I want to share with him." She took the box and ran back to Ben. She said, "Here, Ben. These are for you. You can make pictures with them. They are very fun." Ben was surprised and happy. He said, "Wow, thank you, Lily. These are amazing. You are very nice. Can I make a picture for you?" Lily said, "Yes, please. I would love that." They sat together and painted. They made pictures of the park, the flowers, the birds, and each other. They laughed and talked. They became friends. Lily and Ben learned that giving and receiving are both good. They learned that sharing can make people happy. They learned that painting can be a way of showing love.
