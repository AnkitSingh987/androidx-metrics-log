Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Dog of Tim and the Toys

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim was very impatient. He wanted to go home and play with his toys. In the store, they saw a big sack. Tim's mom said, "We need to buy coffee." Tim asked, "What is coffee, mom?" She said, "It is a drink for grown-ups. It helps us wake up in the morning." Tim looked at the big sack of coffee and thought it was funny. As they walked home, Tim and his mom saw a dog. The dog was sniffing the big sack of coffee. Tim said, "Hello, dog! Do you like coffee too?" The dog wagged its tail and barked happily. Tim and his mom laughed, and they all walked home together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a very important job to do. She needed to borrow a glove from her friend, Tim. Sue went to Tim's house and asked, "Can I borrow your glove, please?" Tim said, "Yes, you can borrow my glove." Sue was very happy. Sue took the glove and finished her important job. She thanked Tim for letting her borrow the glove. They played together and had lots of fun. Sue and Tim were very good friends.

Once, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with sand. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big sand pit. He was very happy. Tim started to play in the sand. He made a big, ugly sand monster. Tim showed his mom the sand monster. "Look, Mom! I made a big, ugly monster!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim!" While Tim was playing, a mean boy came to the sand pit. The mean boy kicked Tim's ugly sand monster. Tim was very sad. The mean boy's mom saw what he did. She said, "That was not nice. I will punish you." The mean boy was sorry. Tim felt better, and they all played in the sand together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to help his mom and dad. One day, Tom's dad gave him a new toy. It was a big, shiny truck. Tom was very happy. Tom took his new truck outside to play. He saw his friend, Sue. "Look at my new truck!" Tom said to Sue. "Wow, it's so nice!" Sue said. They played together with the truck. They pretended it was a big mine truck, carrying rocks and dirt. As they played, Tom's truck got stuck in a hole. Tom and Sue tried to get it out, but they couldn't. They needed help. So they asked Tom's dad for help. Tom's dad came and helped them get the truck out of the hole. Tom and Sue said, "Thank you!" They went back to playing with the big, shiny truck, and they had a lot of fun.

One day, a calm cat named Tim found a telephone. He wanted to play with it. Tim picked up the telephone and heard a voice. "Hello, I need help. Can you supply me with food?" said the voice. Tim did not know what to do. He wanted to help, but he was just a cat. Tim tried to find his friend, Sam the dog. He wanted Sam to help him supply food. They looked for food together but could not find any. They were both sad. In the end, they could not help the voice on the telephone. The voice was still hungry, and Tim and Sam felt bad. The day had a bad ending, and they wished they could have helped.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very hungry. He walked around and saw a big, yummy bone. Max was so happy. He wanted to eat the bone. Max went to get the bone, but he saw an image of a cat on the wall. The cat said, "You cannot have the bone, Max. It is mine." Max was sad, but he did not want to fight with the cat. So, he walked away. Later, Max saw the cat again. The cat yawned and said, "I am not hungry anymore. You can have the bone, Max." Max was so happy. He took the bone and ate it all up. Max and the cat became good friends. They played together every day.
