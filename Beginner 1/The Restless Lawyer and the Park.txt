Category: Beginner
Title: The Restless Lawyer and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a restless lawyer. He liked to run and jump all day. He was always busy and never sat down. One day, the lawyer found a toy that could shoot little balls. He thought it was fun and wanted to play with it. The lawyer went to the park to play with his new toy. He saw many kids and their moms and dads there. He started to shoot the little balls at the trees and the swings. The kids laughed and clapped their hands. They thought it was a fun game. But then, the lawyer got too excited and did not look where he was shooting. He shot a little ball at a big man who was eating ice cream. The ice cream fell on the ground, and the big man got very mad. He shouted at the lawyer and told him to go away. The lawyer felt sad and went home. He knew he should be more careful when playing with his toy.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the woods. They have a hut there. They made it with sticks and leaves. It is their secret place. One day, they go to the hut. They see a big bear. The bear is angry. It growls and shows its teeth. Tom and Sue are scared. They run away from the hut. They run to their homes. They tell their moms and dads what they saw. Their moms and dads hug them. They say they are brave. They say they are safe. They say they will call the animal people. The animal people will help the bear find a new home. Tom and Sue are happy. They are glad they are safe. They hope the bear will be safe too. They say they will return to the hut when the bear is gone. They say they will play there again. They say they are still friends.

One day, a big red car went on a drive. The car was going very fast. The car was going to an emergency. The people in the car were a little nervous. They saw a cat in a tree. The cat was scared. The people in the car wanted to help the cat. They had a big ladder to reach the cat in the tree. They put the ladder on the tree. They climbed up the ladder. They got the cat. The people in the car were happy. The cat was happy too. They all went home.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He saw a big red ball on a high step. He wanted to touch the ball, but he was worried. Tim was too small to reach the high step. Tim had an idea. He found a small box and put it next to the step. He climbed on the box and then on the step. Now, he could touch the big red ball! Tim was happy and not worried anymore. He played with the big red ball all day. He learned that he could do anything if he tried. And from that day, Tim was never worried about the high step again.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. A little boy named Tim was playing in his room. Tim had a big closet full of toys. He liked to open and close the closet door. One day, while playing, Tim heard a tiny voice. "Hello," said the voice. Tim looked around and saw a little toy bear. The bear was sitting on the floor near the closet. "Hi," said Tim, "I am Tim." "Nice to meet you, Tim," said the bear. "My name is Buddy. I live in your closet with all your toys. Can you please close the closet door? It's too bright for my eyes." Tim smiled and closed the closet door. He and Buddy played all day long. From that day on, Tim always remembered to close the closet door for his new friend, Buddy the bear.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to smell the flowers and look for bugs. Lily saw a rose that was very pretty. It was red and smelled sweet. "Look, Ben, a rose!" Lily said. She picked the rose and held it in her hand. Ben came over and saw the rose. He liked it too. He wanted to hold it and smell it. "Can I have the rose, Lily?" Ben asked. Lily thought for a moment. She liked the rose, but she also liked Ben. She wanted to share with him. "OK, Ben, you can have the rose. But be careful, it has thorns. They can hurt you." Lily said. She passed the rose to Ben. Ben took the rose and smiled. He thanked Lily and smelled the rose. He was careful not to touch the thorns. They both enjoyed the rose. It was an original rose. They had never seen one like it before. They wondered if there were more roses in the garden. They decided to look for them together. They were happy.
