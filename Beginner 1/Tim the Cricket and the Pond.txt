Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cricket and the Pond

One day, a little cricket named Tim had a plan. He wanted to play on the icy pond with his friends. Tim was very excited about his plan. Tim told his friends about the plan. They all went to the icy pond to play. They slid and jumped on the ice. They had a lot of fun together. After a while, they were tired and cold. They went back to their warm homes. Tim was happy that his plan worked. He couldn't wait to play on the icy pond again.

Tom and Sue were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big maze made of hedges. They wanted to try it. "Let's go in the maze," Tom said. "It looks fun." "OK," Sue said. "But we have to stay together." They ran into the maze. They turned left and right and left again. They saw many flowers and birds. They laughed and talked. But soon, they got lost. They could not find the way out. They tried to follow the signs, but they were wrong. They got scared. "Tom, I want to go home," Sue said. "I don't like the maze." "Me too," Tom said. "But I don't know where to go." They walked and walked, but they only saw more hedges. They started to cry. "Help!" Tom shouted. "Help!" But no one heard them. No one came to help them. They were stuck in the maze. The sun went down. It got dark and cold. They hugged each other and shivered. They wished they had never gone into the maze. They wished they had listened to their moms. They wished they could see their moms again. But they never did. They never got out of the maze. They never saw their moms again. They died in the maze. The end.

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play with toys. One day, Anna saw a new toy in the shop. It was a big bear with a red heart on its chest. Anna wanted the bear very much. She asked her mom to buy it for her, but her mom said no. She said they had no money for new toys. Anna was sad and angry. She thought of a plan to get the bear. She went to Ben's house and saw that he had a lot of toys. She saw a black car that Ben liked very much. She asked Ben if she could play with the car. Ben said yes, because he was a good friend. He gave Anna the car and said, "Be careful with it. It is my favorite." Anna took the car and ran away. She did not say thank you or goodbye. She went to the shop and gave the car to the shopkeeper. She said, "I want to trade this car for the bear with the heart." The shopkeeper looked at the car and said, "This is a very nice car. Are you sure you want to trade it?" Anna said yes, because she only wanted the bear. The shopkeeper gave Anna the bear and took the car. Anna hugged the bear and smiled. She thought she was very clever. She went home with the bear and put it on her bed. She did not play with it. She just looked at it. The next day, Ben came to Anna's house. He was sad and angry. He said, "Anna, where is my car? You took it and did not give it back. That was not nice. That was mean." Anna said, "I traded your car for this bear. See? It has a heart. It is better than your car." Ben said, "That was not your car to trade. It was mine. You did not ask me. You did not request. You stole it. That was wrong. You have a black heart. You are not my friend anymore." He turned around and left. Anna felt a pain in her chest. She looked at the bear and saw that its heart was not red anymore. It was black. She realized that she had done a bad thing. She had hurt her friend and herself. She had lost the car and the bear. She had no toys and no friends. She cried and cried. The moral of the story is: Do not take what is not yours. Do not trade what is not yours. Do not hurt your friends. Be honest and kind. Request before you take. Say thank you and goodbye. Then you will have a red heart and happy friends.
