Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Mia found a peach. It was a hard peach. She wanted to eat it, but it was too hard. So, Mia thought of a fun idea. She would dance with the peach until it was soft. Mia started to dance. She danced and danced with the peach in her hand. She danced in the sun and the wind. She danced with a big smile on her face. After a while, her friend Tom saw her dancing. "What are you doing, Mia?" Tom asked. "I am dancing with my peach," Mia said. "I want it to be soft so I can eat it." Tom laughed and said, "That sounds fun! Can I dance too?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, let's dance together!" So, Tom and Mia danced and danced until the peach was soft. Then, they sat down and shared the peach. It was the best peach they ever had.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toy car. One day, she saw a long cord on the floor. It was in the way of her toy car. Lucy felt anxious because she wanted to play. Lucy thought hard about what to do. She decided to remove the cord. She picked it up and put it away. Now, the floor was clear for her toy car. Lucy was happy she could play with her toy car again. She pushed the car fast across the floor. Lucy had fun and was not anxious anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a fast seal named Sam. Sam loved to splash in the water all day. One day, Sam met a little fish named Tim. "Hi, Tim!" said Sam. "Do you want to splash with me?" "Yes, Sam!" said Tim. "But I am small and not fast like you. Can you help me?" Sam and Tim played and splashed together. Sam was kind and helped Tim learn to swim fast. They had so much fun in the water. One day, a big shark came. Sam and Tim swam fast to hide. The shark could not find them. They were safe because they were fast. The moral of the story is to always help your friends and be kind. When you help others, you can stay safe and have fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day long. He had many friends in the water. They would play and swim together. One day, while Fin was swimming, he found something new. It was a hole that went underground. Fin was curious and wanted to see what was inside. He swam into the hole and found a big room with many other fish. Fin started to play with the new fish, but they were not friendly. They played a mean trick on Fin and took his favorite toy. Fin was sad and missed his old friends. But then, something unexpected happened. The mean fish felt bad and gave Fin his toy back. They said they were sorry and wanted to be friends. Fin was happy, and they all played together. From that day on, they were all friendly and had fun swimming together.

One day, a quiet cat named Mark went for a walk. He liked to look at the trees and the birds. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Mark was very happy. Mark saw a big bottle. It was a bottle of wine. He did not know what it was, but he wanted to show his friends. So, he pushed the bottle with his nose. It rolled and rolled until it stopped near a big tree. Suddenly, the bottle opened and out came a magic bird! The bird said, "Thank you, Mark, for setting me free. I will give you a wish." Mark thought and thought, and then he wished to fly like a bird. The magic bird touched Mark with its wing and, just like that, Mark could fly! Mark was so happy. He flew high in the sky and played with his new bird friends. And from that day on, Mark the quiet cat was also known as Mark the flying cat.
