Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. Jane lived with her mom and dad. One day, her mom made some dough for baking a cake. Jane wanted to help so her mom gave her the dough and said, "Roll the dough very carefully, Jane." Jane started to roll the dough, but all of a sudden she reversed and rolled it the other way. She thought it looked more fun that way. But when she showed it to her mom, her mom said, "That's not right, Jane. You need to roll it one way or else the cake won't come out the way it should." Jane didn't want to do it the right way so she continued to roll the dough the wrong way. Her mom got very uncomfortable with this and shouted, "Jane, reverse the dough now!" Jane looked at her mom and knew she had to do it. She slowly began reversing the dough and finally, it was rolled the right way. Her mom smiled and said, "That was much better, Jane." From then on Jane made sure to always roll the dough the right way.

Once upon a time, there was an impressive big animal. He was a big, strong lion. All the animals in the jungle liked him. One day, a little bird came to the lion. The little bird was sad. "Please, Mr. Lion," the little bird said, "I request you to help me. My friend is stuck in a net. We need your big, strong paws to free him." The lion agreed to help the little bird and they went to the place where the bird's friend was stuck. The lion used his big, strong paws to break the net. The bird's friend was free and very happy. All the animals in the jungle were happy too. They thanked the big, strong lion for his help. The moral of the story is to always help your friends when they need you.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. One day, it was very cold. Tim put on his coat and went out to play. Tim saw a rail by the road. He thought it would be fun to grab the rail and swing on it. As he grabbed the rail, he felt how cold it was. "Brrr! This rail is so cold," said Tim. He swung on the rail and had lots of fun. But then, Tim slipped and fell down. He started to cry because he got hurt. "Ouch! I fell and it hurts," cried Tim. His mom heard him and came running out. She saw Tim on the ground and hugged him. Tim's fun day ended with a bad ending because he got hurt.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He was very happy to play on the slide and swings. Before they left, his mom put a buckle on his shoe so it wouldn't fall off. Tim saw his friend, Sue, at the park too. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim said to Sue, "I can go down the slide very fast, watch me!" But when Tim tried to resist going too fast, he fell off the slide. He felt sorry that he couldn't show Sue how fast he could go. Sue came over to help Tim up and said, "It's okay, we can try again." Then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came running over to Tim and Sue. The dog had a buckle on its collar with a note attached. The note said, "I am lost, can you help me find my home?" Tim and Sue were excited to help the dog. They walked around the park with the dog, asking people if they knew where the dog's home was. Finally, they found the dog's owner who was very happy to see the dog again. Tim and Sue felt proud that they could help the dog find its home, and they went back to playing at the park with big smiles on their faces.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big watch on a bench. The watch is bright and shiny. It has many colors and numbers. "Wow, look at the watch!" Sara says. "It is so pretty. Can we play with it?" Tom nods. He likes the watch too. He picks it up and puts it on his wrist. It is too big for him, but he does not care. He feels happy. "Let's pretend we are spies," he says. "We can use the watch to talk to each other. We can whisper secrets and look for clues." Sara agrees. She thinks it is a fun game. She puts her hand to her ear and whispers to Tom. "Do you see the man in the red hat? He is our enemy. He wants to take our watch. We have to hide." Tom whispers back. "I see him. He looks mean. Let's run to the slide. We can slide down and escape." They run to the slide and slide down. They laugh and giggle. They feel brave and smart. They do not see the man in the red hat. He is not their enemy. He is the owner of the watch. He left it on the bench by mistake. He is looking for it. He is worried and sad. He sees Sara and Tom with his watch. He walks to them and smiles. He is not mean. He is nice. "Hello, kids," he says. "I see you found my watch. Thank you for keeping it safe. It is very special to me. It was a gift from my wife. She is in the hospital. She is sick. I use the watch to call her and tell her I love her." Sara and Tom feel sorry for the man. They did not know his story. They did not know his watch was special. They feel bad for playing with it. They give the watch back to the man. They say sorry. They say they hope his wife gets better. The man thanks them. He says they are good kids. He says they can keep the watch for a while. He says he will come back later and get it. He says they can use it to call him and his wife. He says they can be friends. Sara and Tom smile. They say thank you. They say they will call him and his wife. They say they will be friends. They put the watch on the bench. They hold hands. They go back to play. They are happy. They learned a lesson. They learned to be kind and careful. They learned to share and care. They learned to whisper and watch.
