Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Clumsy Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy named Tim. He loved to run and play, but sometimes he would trip and fall. One day, Tim was playing with his toys in his room. He had lots of toys, like cars, dolls, and blocks. He was having so much fun that he didn't see his toys scatter all over the floor. Later, Tim's mom called him for dinner. Tim was so excited that he ran out of his room very fast. But he forgot about the toys on the floor! As he ran, he stepped on a toy car with his toe. "Ouch!" he yelled. His toe hurt a lot, but he was okay. Tim's mom came to see what happened. She saw the toys scattered on the floor and knew that Tim had been clumsy. She helped him clean up the toys and told him to be more careful next time. Tim learned his lesson and tried to be less clumsy. From then on, he always cleaned up his toys before leaving his room.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl called Bella. She was very obedient and always did what her parents asked her. One day, her parents told her she had to go to the port. But Bella hated the port. The water was too loud and the seagulls scared her. "Please don't make me go," pleaded Bella. But her parents said she had to go. So, obediently, she put on her coat, grabbed her bag and headed down to the port. But when she got there, she found she wasn't alone. Her friends were there too, all smiling and excited. "Come on Bella," they shouted. "We're going on a trip!" And just like that, Bella forgot about hating the port. She laughed and smiled and had so much fun that day. From that day on, Bella no longer hated the port. She loved going there - and she was still very obedient to her parents, of course!

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tim. Tim was very useful in his town. He could fix cars, bikes, and even toys. Everyone liked Tim because he was so helpful. One sunny day, a little girl named Sue came to Tim. She whispered, "Please help me, Tim. My bike is broken." Tim smiled and said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will fix your bike." So, Tim worked hard and fixed Sue's bike. Sue was very happy and thanked Tim. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's own car stopped working! Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Sue saw Tim and whispered to her friends, "Let's help Tim." They all worked together and fixed Tim's car. Tim was surprised and happy. He said, "Thank you, friends. You are all very useful, too!" And they all laughed and played together.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run, slide and swing. They saw a big dog with a leather collar. The dog was brown and fluffy. He looked friendly. "Can we pet the dog?" Lily asked Ben. "OK, but be careful. He might bite," Ben said. They walked slowly to the dog. The dog wagged his tail and licked their hands. They giggled and stroked his fur. "Hello, doggy. What is your name?" Lily said. The dog barked and nuzzled her face. He smelled like grass and dirt. "His name is Rex. He is my dog," a voice said. They turned and saw a boy with a cap and a ball. He was the dog's owner. "Hi, I'm Lily and this is Ben. We like your dog. He is very nice," Lily said. "Hi, I'm Sam. Rex likes you too. Do you want to play with us?" Sam said. "Yes, please. Can we throw the ball for Rex?" Ben said. "Sure. He loves to catch the ball. Watch this," Sam said. He threw the ball high in the air. Rex ran after it and jumped. He caught the ball in his mouth and brought it back. He dropped the ball at their feet. "Good boy, Rex. You are very smart," Lily said. She picked up the ball and threw it again. Rex chased it and caught it. He came back and gave the ball to Ben. Ben threw the ball for Rex. Rex ran and caught it. He came back and gave the ball to Sam. They took turns throwing the ball for Rex. They had a lot of fun. Rex was happy and tired. He lay down on the grass and panted. He licked their faces and wagged his tail. They hugged him and thanked him for playing. "Bye, Rex. You are a good dog. We hope to see you again," Lily said. "Bye, Sam. Thank you for letting us play with Rex. He is a great friend," Ben said. "Bye, Lily and Ben. You are welcome. Rex likes you too. He is a great friend," Sam said. They waved goodbye and left the park. They had a good day. They liked the taste of Rex's leather collar. It was dirty but sweet.
