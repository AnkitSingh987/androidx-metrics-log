Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Swing

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a swing and wanted to play on it. But there was a long line of kids waiting to play on the swing too. Mia felt sad because she had to wait. Mia stood in the line with the other kids. She saw a boy named Tim who was not calm. He was mad because he wanted to swing too. Mia had an idea. She told Tim to take deep breaths to feel calm. Tim did what Mia said and started to feel better. When it was finally Mia and Tim's turn to swing, they were both calm and happy. They swung high in the sky and had lots of fun. The other kids in the line saw how calm and happy they were and started to feel better too. In the end, everyone had a great time at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. His name was Jump. Jump lived in a wild pond with many friends. They liked to play and have fun together. Jump was very good at jumping high, and all his friends loved to watch him jump. One sunny day, Jump and his friends were playing near the pond. They were having a great time. Suddenly, a big bird flew down and tried to catch Jump. But Jump jumped so high that the bird missed him. The bird was very surprised and flew away. Jump and his friends were scared at first, but then they laughed. They did not know that Jump could jump so high. Now, they knew that Jump was not just a little frog, but a very special one. Jump became a hero to his friends, and they all played happily ever after.

Jill was a girl who liked to eat. She ate a lot of food every day. She ate more food than her brother Jack and her sister Jane. She ate more food than her mom and dad. She ate more food than her dog Spot. One day, Jill saw a big plate of cookies on the table. She wanted to eat all the cookies. She did not want to share with anyone. She did not listen to her mom, who said, "Jill, leave some cookies for the others. Too many cookies are bad for you." Jill took the plate of cookies and ran to her room. She locked the door and ate all the cookies. She ate them very fast. She did not enjoy them. She only wanted to have them all. Soon, Jill felt sick. Her tummy hurt. She felt hot and cold. She felt dizzy and tired. She wanted to throw up. She hoped she would feel better soon. But she did not. Her mom knocked on the door and asked, "Jill, are you okay?" Jill did not answer. She was too sick. Her mom opened the door with a key and saw Jill on the bed. She saw the empty plate of cookies. She saw Jill's pale face. She was very worried. She called the doctor and said, "Please come quickly. My daughter ate too many cookies and now she is very sick." The doctor came and checked Jill. He said, "She has a fever and a stomach ache. She needs to go to the hospital. She may have to stay there for a long time." Jill was scared. She did not want to go to the hospital. She did not want to leave her mom and dad and her brother and sister and her dog Spot. She wished she had not eaten all the cookies. She wished she had shared with the others. She wished she had listened to her mom. She learned a hard lesson. She learned that being greedy is bad and being kind is good. She learned that too much of anything is not good for you. She learned that she should hope for the right things and not the wrong things.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small room with his toys. He loved to play all day long. One day, Spot could not find his favorite ball. He looked all around the room, but the ball was lost. Spot felt very sad and did not know what to do. Then, Spot's friend, a cat named Whiskers, came to visit. Whiskers saw that Spot was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Spot?" Spot told Whiskers that he lost his ball. Whiskers said, "Don't worry, Spot. I will help you find your ball." They looked all around the room together. At last, they found the ball under the bed. Spot was so happy, and he thanked Whiskers. They played with the ball all day long and had lots of fun.
