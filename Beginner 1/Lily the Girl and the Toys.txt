Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys. Her favorite toy was a big, red button. Lily liked to push the button, and when she did, it made a fun sound. One day, Lily was playing in her room. She saw a tiny bug crawl on the floor. "Hello, bug!" said Lily. "Do you want to play with my button?" The bug crawled closer to the button and pushed it with its little legs. To Lily's surprise, the bug started to talk! "Thank you, Lily! Now I can speak!" said the bug. Lily and the bug became best friends. They played with the button every day, and had lots of fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a journal. He liked to write in his journal every day. He wrote about his friends, his toys, and what he did each day. Tom was always honest when he wrote in his journal. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to play. They played with toys and had a lot of fun. But Sam lost his toy car. Tom found the toy car, but he did not tell Sam. Tom wanted to keep the toy car for himself. He knew it was wrong, but he did not want to be honest. That night, Tom wrote in his journal. He started to write that he had found Sam's toy car. But he remembered that he should be honest. So, Tom decided to give the toy car back to Sam. The next day, Tom told Sam the truth and gave him the toy car. Sam was very happy and thanked Tom. The moral of the story is to always be honest with your friends and yourself.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in the park. She saw a big red ball up in a tree. Sue wanted the ball, but she could not reach it. She tried to jump, but it was too high. A stranger came and saw Sue trying to get the ball. The stranger said, "I can help you reach the ball." Sue looked at him and said, "Thank you!" The stranger got a long stick and pushed the ball out of the tree. Sue was very happy and took the ball. Sue went home and told her mom about the stranger. Her mom looked guilty and said, "Sue, that ball was not yours. We need to take it back to the park." Sue felt bad, but she knew her mom was right. They went back to the park and left the ball for the real owner to find.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with a big sack. One day, Tom found a big hill. He had a fun idea. He wanted to roll the sack down the hill. Tom pushed the sack to the top of the hill. He was very happy. He thought it would be very fun to watch the sack roll down. Tom did not know that his friend, the little mouse, was inside the sack. The sack started to roll down the hill. It went faster and faster. Tom was so excited. But then, the sack hit a big rock. The sack opened and the little mouse came out. The mouse was not happy. He was sad and scared. Tom felt very bad. He did not mean to hurt his friend. The mouse was so upset that he did not want to play with Tom anymore. Tom was sad and alone. He learned that being naughty can have a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a big clock that could talk. The clock was very intelligent. It could tell the time and sing songs. The clock liked to stand tall and proud in the middle of the town. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the clock. He said, "Hello, Mr. Clock! Can you sing a song for me, please?" The clock smiled and sang a happy song for Tim. Tim clapped his hands and danced to the music. But one day, it rained a lot. The clock got wet and cold. It could not sing or tell the time anymore. Tim came to see the clock, but it was sad and quiet. Tim felt sad too, and they could not play together anymore. The end.
