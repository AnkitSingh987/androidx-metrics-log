Category: Beginner
Title: The Butterfly and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a pretty butterfly. The butterfly was old, but it still loved to play. It liked to fly in the sun and visit the flowers. The butterfly had many friends in the garden. One day, the butterfly saw a new flower. It was very pretty. The butterfly wanted to play with the new flower. But it did not say goodbye to its old friends. The old friends were sad. They missed the butterfly. After some time, the butterfly started to feel alone. It missed its old friends too. The butterfly knew it made a mistake. It felt regret. So, the butterfly went back to its old friends. They were happy to see it again. The butterfly promised to always be nice to its friends. And they all played together in the big garden, under the warm sun.

One day, a little girl named Sue and her dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big, white bird. Sue took out her camera to take a photo of the bird. She said, "Stay still, Spot!" Spot wagged his tail and smiled. As Sue was about to take the photo, a boy named Tim ran up to them. He yelled, "Stop!" Sue and Spot looked at him. Tim said, "That bird is my pet, Snowy!" Sue was surprised. She didn't know birds could be pets too. Tim took Snowy and thanked Sue for finding his pet. Sue, Spot, and Tim became good friends. They played together every day and took many photos of their fun times. And Snowy, the white bird, was always with them, making them laugh and smile.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing. The cat said, "Let's play a game for a week. We will see who can slip the least." The dog said, "I am tough, I will win!" The next day, the cat and dog played near a big tree. The cat slipped on a leaf and fell. The dog laughed and said, "I told you I am tough. I will not slip!" Two days later, it rained. The cat and dog went outside to play. The dog slipped on a wet rock and fell in a puddle. The cat laughed and said, "Now you have slipped too!" On the last day of the week, the cat and dog were still tied in their game. They decided to have a race to see who would win. As they ran, they both slipped on a banana peel and fell. They both laughed and said, "We are both tough, and we both slipped. Let's be friends and play together!" From that day on, the cat and dog played together and had fun. They learned that it was not about who was the toughest or who slipped the least, but about having fun with each other.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play with toys. Tim has a new toy gun. It is big and red and makes loud noises. Sam does not have a toy gun. He only has a ball and a car. One day, Tim and Sam go to the park. They see other kids playing. Tim takes out his toy gun and pretends to shoot at the trees and the birds. He says, "Bang! Bang! I am a hero!" Sam watches him and feels jealous. He wants to play with the toy gun too. He says to Tim, "Can I try your gun? It looks fun." Tim says, "No, it is mine. You have your own toys. Go play with them." Sam feels sad and angry. He says, "That is not fair. You are not a good friend. You are mean." He throws his ball at Tim and hits him on the head. Tim cries and drops his toy gun. He says, "Ow! That hurts! Why did you do that?" Sam runs and picks up the toy gun. He says, "Now it is mine. I will show you how to play with it." He points the toy gun at Tim and says, "Bang! Bang! You are not a hero. You are a loser." Tim gets up and chases Sam. He says, "Give me back my gun. It is not yours. You are a thief." They run around the park and fight over the toy gun. They do not enjoy playing anymore. They are both unhappy and angry. A lady sees them and stops them. She says, "What are you doing? Why are you fighting? You are supposed to be friends. Fighting is not nice. Sharing is nice. You should share your toys and play together. That is how you enjoy playing." She takes the toy gun from Sam and gives it back to Tim. She says, "Tim, you have a nice toy gun. But you should not be selfish. You should let Sam try it sometimes. He is your friend. He wants to have fun too." She says to Sam, "Sam, you should not be jealous. You have your own toys. But you should not throw them at Tim. That is not nice. You should ask him nicely if you can play with his toy gun. He might say yes if you are polite." She says to both of them, "Now, say sorry to each other and hug. Then you can play together nicely. You can take turns with the toy gun and the ball and the car. You can pretend to be heroes and have adventures. You can have more fun if you share and cooperate. Do you understand?" Tim and Sam nod and say sorry to each other. They hug and smile. They say, "Thank you, lady. You are right. Sharing is nice. Fighting is not nice. We are friends. We want to enjoy playing." They take their toys and go to play together. They have more fun and forget their fight. They are happy and friendly. The end.
