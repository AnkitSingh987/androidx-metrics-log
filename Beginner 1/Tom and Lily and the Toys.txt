Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Toys

Tom and Lily are playing with their toys in the living room. Tom has a big truck and Lily has a doll. They are having fun, but they want to try something new. "Let's go to the kitchen and see what we can find," Tom says. "OK, but we have to be careful. Mom said not to touch the sharp things," Lily says. They go to the kitchen and look around. They see a lot of things, like pots and pans, spoons and forks, and a big knife. They also see a hook on the wall, where Mom hangs her apron. "Look, a hook! We can use it to make a game," Tom says. He takes the hook and puts it on the floor. He pretends it is a fish and makes a sound like a splash. "Lily, you be the fisherman. You have to catch me with a rope," he says. Lily finds a rope in a drawer and ties one end to a chair. She holds the other end and tries to catch Tom. "Come here, fishy, fishy!" she says. Tom runs around the hook, making it hard for Lily to catch him. He laughs and dodges the rope. But then, he trips over the hook and falls down. He feels a sharp pain in his foot. He looks and sees blood. He starts to cry. "Ow, ow, ow! The hook hurt me!" he says. Lily drops the rope and runs to him. She sees the hook stuck in his foot. She feels scared and sorry. "Tom, Tom, are you OK? I'm sorry, I didn't mean to hurt you," she says. She hugs him and tries to calm him down. "Mom, Mom, help!" she shouts. Mom hears the shout and comes to the kitchen. She sees Tom and Lily and the hook. She feels worried and angry. "What happened here? What were you doing with the hook?" she asks. She takes the hook out of Tom's foot and wraps a bandage around it. She gives him a kiss and a hug. "It's OK, Tom. You'll be fine. But you shouldn't play with the hook. It's dangerous. You could have hurt yourself more," she says. She looks at Lily and sees her tears. "And you, Lily. You should have been more careful too. You should have told me you were playing in the kitchen. You could have helped Tom instead of shouting," she says. She gives her a kiss and a hug too. "It's OK, Lily. I know you didn't mean it. But you have to be more responsible. You're the big sister. You have to look after Tom," she says. She takes them to the living room and gives them some cookies and milk. She tells them to play with their toys and not to go to the kitchen again. Tom and Lily feel sorry and ashamed. They say sorry to Mom and to each other. They promise to be more careful and to listen to Mom. They eat their cookies and milk and play with their toys. They feel fine.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day. One day, Spot saw a big ball of dough in the kitchen. He wanted to play with it, so he took it and ran outside. Spot's friend, a bird named Chirp, saw him with the dough. Chirp said, "Spot, that's not a toy! It's for making bread." But Spot did not care. He played and played with the dough, making it very dirty. Chirp started to complain. "Spot, please stop! The dough is getting dirty!" But Spot just laughed and kept playing. Finally, Chirp flew away to find help. He brought back their friend, Cat. Cat said, "Spot, listen to Chirp. The dough is not a toy." Spot looked at his friends and saw they were upset. He felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know." They all cleaned the dough and made bread together.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his family. He helped them find things, and he helped them clean up. Spot was a very good dog. One day, Spot saw a ball at the top of a stair. His little friend, Tim, wanted to play with the ball. Tim asked, "Spot, can you help me get the ball?" Spot nodded his head to say yes. Spot went up the stair and got the ball. He brought it down to Tim. They played with the ball all day. Tim was happy, and Spot was happy to be helpful. They were very good friends.
