Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree. This tree lived in a pretty forest with many other trees. The big, thick tree had many friends on one side of the forest. But, the big, thick tree did not know the trees on the other side of the forest. One day, the big, thick tree wanted to visit the other side of the forest. It asked its friends to go with it. They all said yes, and they walked to the other side of the forest together. The big, thick tree was very happy to visit the other side. When they got to the other side, they met many new trees. The new trees were nice and friendly. The big, thick tree and its friends played with the new trees all day. They had so much fun! The big, thick tree was very happy to have visited the other side of the forest. From that day on, the big, thick tree and its friends would visit the other side often, and they all became the best of friends.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they go to the toy store with Lily's mom. They see many toys that they want. "Mom, can I have this doll?" Lily asks. She holds a big doll with a pink dress and long hair. "Sorry, Lily, that doll is too cheap. It will break soon. Look, this doll is better. It has a nice hat and shoes." Lily's mom says. She shows Lily a smaller doll with a blue dress and short hair. "But I like the pink dress and the long hair!" Lily says. She is sad. Ben sees a toy car. It is red and shiny. It has four wheels and a horn. He likes it a lot. "Mom, can I have this car?" Ben asks. He holds the toy car and makes a vroom sound. "Sure, Ben, that car is not cheap. It is strong and fun. You can play with it a lot." Ben's mom says. She smiles and gives Ben some money. "Thank you, mom!" Ben says. He is happy. Lily and Ben go to the counter. They give the toys and the money to the lady. The lady puts the toys in a bag and gives them a receipt. "Here you go, kids. Have a nice day!" The lady says. "Thank you, lady!" Lily and Ben say. "Wait, kids, you forgot something." The lady says. She holds out her hand. "What is it, lady?" Lily and Ben ask. "It is a tip. You give a tip to the lady when she helps you. It is a nice thing to do." The lady says. Lily and Ben look at each other. They do not know what a tip is. They do not have any more money. "Sorry, lady, we do not have a tip. We only have toys." Lily and Ben say. The lady is angry. She takes the toys from the bag and puts them back on the shelf. "Then you cannot have the toys. You are cheap kids. Go away!" The lady says. Lily and Ben are shocked. They start to cry. They run to their moms and hug them. "Mom, the lady is mean. She took our toys. She wanted a tip. What is a tip?" Lily and Ben say. Lily's mom and Ben's mom are mad. They go to the lady and talk to her. They tell her that she is wrong. She cannot take the toys from the kids. She cannot ask for a tip from the kids. They are only three years old. They do not understand. The lady is sorry. She gives the toys back to Lily and Ben. She says she is sorry. She says she will not ask for a tip from kids again. Lily and Ben are glad. They say thank you to the lady. They say thank you to their moms. They go home with their toys. They play with their toys. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely shoe. The shoe had no friend to play with. It felt very sad. One day, the shoe met a ball. The ball was round and fun. The shoe and the ball became friends. They liked to play together. The shoe and the ball had a race. They raced to the big tree. The shoe hopped and the ball rolled. The shoe won the race. They both had fun and were happy. The lonely shoe was not lonely anymore.
