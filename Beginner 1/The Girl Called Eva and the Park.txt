Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Eva and the Park

Once there was a little girl called Eva. She was three years old. She wanted to go to the park, but she wasn't sure. So she went out to the front of her house and looked around. She saw a big sign pointing towards the park. She was nervous but excited. She guessed that the sign meant she could go to the park. So she ran down the road to get there. When she arrived at the park, there were lots of people running around. She was still a bit nervous, but she wasn't scared. She smiled and ran off to the swings. It was lots of fun. She played on the swings, the slides and the seesaw. When it was time to go home, Eva guessed that she had to follow the sign back home again. She hugged her new friends goodbye, waved to the park and followed the sign back home. Eva had a great day out and was happy to be home safe with her family.

Once upon a time, in a wide green park, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his friends and imagine big adventures. One day, Tim and his friend Sam were playing with their toy cars. They had a race to see who could reach the tree first. "Ready, set, go!" shouted Tim, and they started to race. But then, Tim's car stopped moving. He tried to push it, but it wouldn't go. Sam's car won the race, and Tim felt sad. He had zero fun when his car didn't work. Tim said, "My car won't go. I don't like this game anymore." Sam thought for a moment and then said, "Let's imagine we are in a big race with real cars. We can run to the tree and back. That way, we both can have fun." Tim smiled and agreed. They stood side by side, ready to race. "Ready, set, go!" they shouted together, and they ran as fast as they could. They laughed and played, and Tim forgot all about his broken toy car. In the end, they both felt like winners.

Once upon a time, there was a popular fish named Bob. Bob loved to swim all day. One day, Bob saw a table in the water. "Who put this table here?" Bob asked his friend, Tim. Tim was a small fish who liked to follow Bob. "I don't know," said Tim. "Let's go see." As they swam closer to the table, they saw a big cake on it. Bob and Tim were very happy. They wanted to eat the cake. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and took the cake away. Bob and Tim were sad. But they kept swimming and playing. They knew they would find more fun things in the water.

Anna liked to eat shrimp. She liked them a lot. She liked them more than any other food. She wanted to own all the shrimp in the world. One day, she went to the store with her mom. She saw a big bag of shrimp in the freezer. She ran to it and grabbed it. She said, "Mine! Mine! Mine!" Her mom said, "No, Anna. You can't have that. That's too much shrimp for you. You have to share with others." Anna did not want to share. She was stubborn. She held the bag tight and said, "No! No! No!" Her mom was angry. She said, "Anna, you are being very rude and greedy. You have to put that back. Now!" Anna did not listen. She tried to run away with the bag. But the bag was too heavy for her. She fell down and the bag ripped open. Shrimp flew everywhere. Anna started to cry. Her mom said, "Look what you did, Anna. You made a big mess. You wasted a lot of shrimp. You have to say sorry and help me clean up." Anna felt bad. She said, "Sorry, mom. I was wrong. I don't need to own all the shrimp. I can share with others." Her mom said, "That's good, Anna. I'm glad you learned your lesson. Now, let's go home and have some shrimp for dinner. But not too much, okay?" Anna said, "Okay, mom. I love you." Her mom said, "I love you too, Anna."
