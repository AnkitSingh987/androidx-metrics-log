Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot loved to jog in the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lucy. They would jog and play all day long. One sunny day, after a long jog, Lucy and Spot saw an ice-cream truck. Lucy got excited and bought ice-cream for both of them. Spot got a yummy bone-shaped ice-cream, and Lucy got a big, pink ice-cream. They sat under a big tree, enjoying their ice-cream and laughing. Spot wagged his tail, happy to share this treat with his best friend. From that day on, after every jog, they would always get ice-cream and have lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and shout all day long. Spot was always happy when he was with his friends. One day, Spot found a big tire in his yard. He thought it would be fun to play with. So, he called his friends over to his house. They all took turns rolling the tire, and they had lots of fun. But, after a while, they got tired. They sat down in the yard and looked at the tire. They were all very happy. Spot knew that the tire was a great toy for him and his friends. They all shouted "Hooray!" and went home to rest.

One day, a pretty bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a little girl. The little girl was sad. She needed help to find her sugar. The pretty bird wanted to help the little girl. They looked for the sugar together. They looked up and down, and all around. They found a big box. The box was red and blue. The little girl and the pretty bird opened the box. They thought they would find sugar. But no! It was full of pretty toys! The little girl was happy. She did not need sugar anymore. She played with the toys and the pretty bird. They had fun and became friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. He was very excited and ran to the slide. Tim climbed up the steps and sat at the top of the slide. He looked down and felt a funny feeling in his stomach. He was a little scared. Tim's mom saw him and said, "It's okay, Tim. Just slide down and I will catch you." Tim took a deep breath and started to slide. He went down fast and his mom caught him at the bottom. Tim was so happy that he did it. He felt very alert and wanted to slide again and again. Tim and his mom played at the park all day. They had fun on the swings, the seesaw, and the slide. When it was time to go home, Tim was tired but happy. He couldn't wait to come back to the park and slide again.

One day, a huge gorilla named Bobo lived in the forest. He liked to play with his friends, the little monkeys. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. Bobo was very happy. One day, Bobo and the monkeys saw a big tree with yummy fruit on top. They all wanted to get the fruit, but the tree was too tall. The little monkeys tried to climb, but they could not succeed. They felt sad and asked Bobo for help. Bobo said, "I will help you get the fruit!" He tried to reach the fruit with his long arms, but he could not. Then, he had an idea. Bobo climbed on top of a big rock and jumped as high as he could. To everyone's surprise, Bobo could fly! He flew up to the fruit and got it for his friends. The little monkeys were so happy and thanked Bobo. From that day on, they knew that their friend Bobo was special, and they played even more fun games together.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park on Sunday. They have a ball and a bike. They have fun. One Sunday, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is black and brown. It has a long tail and sharp teeth. The dog barks at them. Tom and Sue are scared. "Let's move away from the dog," Sue says. "It might bite us." But Tom is not smart. He thinks he is brave. He says, "No, I want to move closer to the dog. I want to touch it. It looks soft." Sue says, "Don't do that, Tom. It is stupid. The dog might not like it. It might hurt you." But Tom does not listen. He moves closer to the dog. He reaches out his hand. The dog growls. The dog snaps. The dog bites Tom's hand. Tom cries. He drops the ball. He runs away. Sue follows him. She helps him. She says, "I'm sorry, Tom. Are you okay?" Tom says, "No, I'm not okay. My hand hurts. The dog was bad. It was mean." Sue says, "The dog was not bad. It was scared. You were bad. You were mean. You did not respect the dog. You did not listen to me. You were stupid." Tom says, "You're right, Sue. I was stupid. I should have listened to you. I should have moved away from the dog. I learned my lesson. I will be more careful next time." Sue says, "That's good, Tom. I'm glad you learned your lesson. I hope your hand gets better soon. Come on, let's go home. Maybe we can play again next Sunday." The end. Moral value: The story teaches the value of listening to others, respecting animals, and being careful.
