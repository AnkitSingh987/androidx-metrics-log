Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Ball

One day, a big bird and a small bird were flying over a valley. They saw a ball stuck in a tree. The big bird said, "Let's pass the ball to each other and have fun." The small bird flew to the tree and got the ball. They started to pass the ball to each other. But the small bird was not very good at catching the ball. The ball fell into the valley. "Oh no! I am sorry," said the small bird. The big bird was not mad. They went down into the valley to find the ball. They looked and looked, but they could not find it. Then, they saw a little girl crying. She said, "I lost my ball. I am very sad." The big bird and the small bird knew that they had to give the ball back to the girl. They were sorry for playing with her ball. The birds found the ball and gave it back to the girl. She was very happy. The big bird and the small bird learned that it is important to be kind and say sorry when you make a mistake.

Tim and Sam are friends. They go to school together. They like to play with cars and blocks. One day, they have a new teacher. Her name is Miss Lee. She is very busy. She has many pupils to look after. Miss Lee gives Tim and Sam some paper and crayons. She tells them to draw something nice. Tim and Sam start to draw. Tim draws a big car. Sam draws a small car. Tim likes Sam's car. He wants it for himself. "Give me your car!" Tim says to Sam. "No, it is mine!" Sam says to Tim. Tim is angry. He grabs Sam's paper and tears it. Sam is sad. He cries. Miss Lee hears Sam cry. She comes to see what is wrong. "What happened here?" Miss Lee asks. "Tim grabbed my car and broke it!" Sam says. "Tim, is that true?" Miss Lee asks. Tim does not answer. He looks down. He knows he did something bad. Miss Lee is not happy. She takes Tim's paper and crayons away. She tells him to sit in the corner. He has to stay there until the end of the day. He cannot play with Sam or the other pupils. He is alone and bored. Tim is sorry. He wishes he did not grab Sam's car. He wishes he was nice to Sam. He wishes he could say sorry to Sam and Miss Lee. But it is too late. He has to wait in the corner. He has no friends. He has no fun. He has a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to put things in her pocket. One day, she saw a frog. The frog was green and slimy. Amy thought it was disgusting, but she wanted to show her friend, Tim. Amy went to Tim's house with the frog in her pocket. She said, "Tim, look what I have!" Tim looked in her pocket and saw the frog. He said, "Ew! That's disgusting!" Amy laughed and said, "I know, but it's funny." Amy and Tim decided to play with the frog. They played catch with it. But then, Amy dropped the frog. The frog jumped away, and they couldn't find it. They were sad, but they laughed. They knew they had a fun time with the disgusting frog.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. The sky was high and blue. Tim saw big, dark clouds coming. He knew it would rain soon. He ran to his friend, Sarah, who was playing with her toys. "Sarah, it will rain soon. Let's go inside!" Tim said. Sarah looked up and saw the clouds too. They both ran inside Tim's house. In the house, Tim's mom was making cookies. She had a big bag of sugar. Tim and Sarah watched as she put the sugar into the cookie mix. They both wanted to taste the cookies. When the cookies were done, Tim's mom gave them each one. They were happy and ate the cookies. Outside, the rain started to fall. Tim and Sarah were glad they went inside. They played and ate cookies while the rain came down.
