Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bathrobe

One day, a boy named Tim found a bathrobe in his room. It was a big, blue bathrobe that was very soft. He liked the bathrobe a lot. Tim wanted to play with it, so he took it outside. Tim saw his friend Sue. Sue had a ball. They decided to play catch with the ball. Tim wanted to use the bathrobe too. So, he put on the bathrobe and tried to throw the ball to Sue. But the bathrobe was too big for him, and he could not throw the ball well. Tim and Sue played for a long time. But then, it started to rain. The rain was very bad. The bathrobe got all wet and dirty. Tim was sad. He knew his mom would be mad at him for getting the bathrobe dirty. When he got home, his mom saw the dirty bathrobe and was very upset. Tim felt bad and knew he should not have played with the bathrobe outside.

One day, a cat and a dog were very hungry. They wanted a meal to eat. The cat said, "Let's find food together!" The dog agreed, and they started to look for food. It was difficult for them to find a meal because they were small and the food was high up. The cat had an idea. She urged the dog to help her climb a tree. The dog said, "I can't climb trees, but I can help you up!" So, the dog pushed the cat up the tree. The cat reached the top and saw a big, yummy meal. But then, something unexpected happened! The meal started to move! It was not a meal, but a big bird! The bird was scared and flew away. The cat and dog were surprised but they laughed. They knew they could find another meal by working together.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named True. True was a very glad dog. He liked to play all day long. One day, True saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it. True went to his friend, Cat. "Cat, will you play with me?" True asked. Cat said, "Yes, I will play with you. Let's finish our game with the big ball." They played and played until they were tired. At the end of the day, True and Cat were very glad. They had so much fun playing with the big ball. They said, "We will play again tomorrow!" And they did. True and Cat became the best of friends and played together every day.

Lily liked to play with her toy cars. She had many cars of different colors and sizes. She liked to make them zoom and beep on the floor. She pretended to be a mechanic and fix them when they broke. One day, her brother Max came into her room. He was tall and older than Lily. He saw her toy cars and picked one up. It was a red car with a shiny hood. "Hey, this is my car!" Lily said. "Give it back!" Max laughed and held the car high in the air. Lily could not reach it. She jumped and stretched, but it was no use. "Look at you, little mechanic!" Max teased. "You can't fix this car. You can't even get it. It's mine now!" Lily felt angry and sad. She wanted her car back. She did not like Max teasing her. She started to cry. Mom heard Lily crying and came into the room. She saw Max holding the car and Lily on the floor. "What's going on here?" Mom asked. Max tried to hide the car behind his back. He looked guilty. "Nothing, Mom," he said. Lily pointed at Max and said, "He took my car! He teased me!" Mom frowned and took the car from Max. She gave it back to Lily and hugged her. "That's not nice, Max," Mom said. "You should not take Lily's toys or tease her. She loves her cars and plays with them nicely. You should apologize to her and share your toys." Max felt sorry for making Lily cry. He realized he was mean to her. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was wrong. Can I play with you and your cars?" Lily wiped her tears and smiled. She liked playing with Max when he was nice. She said, "Okay, Max. You can play with me. But you have to be a good mechanic and help me fix the cars." Max nodded and hugged Lily. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good mechanic and a good sister." Mom smiled and watched them play. She was happy they made up and learned to be kind. She said, "That's better. I'm proud of you both."
