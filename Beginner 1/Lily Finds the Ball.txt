Category: Beginner
Title: Lily Finds the Ball

Lily likes to play soccer with her friends. She has a pink ball that is soft and bouncy. She can kick it very far and fast. She has a style of kicking that makes the ball go high in the air. Her friends think she is good at soccer. One day, Lily and her friends go to the park to play soccer. They see a big boy with a blue ball. He is playing alone. He sees Lily's pink ball and wants it. He runs to Lily and tries to take her ball. Lily does not like that. She says, "No, this is my ball. You have your own ball." The big boy does not listen. He grabs Lily's ball and kicks it away. He laughs and says, "Your ball is silly. It is too soft and pink. My ball is better. It is hard and blue." He kicks his ball at Lily and her friends. They dodge and run away. They are scared and sad. Lily is brave. She runs after her ball. She finds it behind a tree. She picks it up and hugs it. She is happy to have her ball back. She sees the big boy still kicking his ball. She has an idea. She kicks her ball with her style. The ball flies high in the air and lands on the big boy's head. He is surprised and drops his ball. He says, "Ow, that hurt. Your ball is too hard and bouncy." Lily and her friends laugh and cheer. They run to Lily and hug her. They say, "You are awesome, Lily. Your ball is not silly. It is soft and pink and fun. Your style is cool. You can kick the ball very well." They play soccer with Lily's ball and have a great time. The big boy leaves them alone. He learns not to be mean and to share.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely cat named Tom. Tom did not have any friends to play with. He would walk around the park all day, looking for someone to share his secret with. Tom had a secret that he loved very much. One day, Tom met a dog named Max. Max was sad because he had lost his toy. Tom wanted to help Max, so he shared his secret with him. The secret was that Tom knew where to find the best toys in the park. Max was very happy to know the secret. Tom and Max went to the secret place and found many toys to play with. They played together all day and became best friends. From that day on, they were never lonely again. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can bring happiness and create friendships.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a pupil at a fun school. She loved to play with her friends and learn new things. One day, her teacher told the class they would put on a play. Mia was very excited to act in the play. Mia practiced her lines and her moves every day. She wanted to do a great job. All her friends were happy to act in the play too. They worked together to make the play the best it could be. On the day of the play, Mia's mom and dad came to watch. They were so proud of her. Mia acted very well and her friends did too. After the play, everyone clapped and cheered. Mia was grateful for her friends and her teacher. They all had a great time and made a wonderful play together.

Tim and Jen liked to play in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. One day, they saw a big dog in the park. The dog was black and brown and had a shiny collar. The dog looked friendly, so Tim and Jen ran to pet it. But the dog was not friendly. The dog was a guard dog. The dog was trained to protect his owner. The dog did not like strangers. The dog barked and growled at Tim and Jen. He showed his teeth and snapped at them. Tim and Jen were scared. They tried to run away, but the dog was faster. The dog chased them and bit them. Tim and Jen cried and screamed. They wanted the dog to stop, but the dog did not stop. The dog's owner was in the park too. He heard the noise and saw the dog. He was confused. He did not know why the dog was attacking the children. He ran to the dog and shouted his name. He pulled the dog away from Tim and Jen. But it was too late. Tim and Jen were hurt. They had blood and scratches on their arms and legs. They needed a doctor. The dog's owner was sorry. He said he was sorry to Tim and Jen and their parents. He said he did not know the dog would do that. He said he would pay for the doctor. But Tim and Jen did not care. They were angry and sad. They did not like the dog or the owner. They did not want to play in the park anymore. They did not want to pet any dog anymore. They had a bad day. They had a bad ending.
