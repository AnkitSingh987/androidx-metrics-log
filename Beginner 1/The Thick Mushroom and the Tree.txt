Category: Beginner
Title: The Thick Mushroom and the Tree

One day, a thick mushroom went for a walk. It saw a big tree and wanted to play. The tree said, "Hello, thick mushroom! Let's play hide and seek." The mushroom was happy and started to hide. It saw a big puddle and wanted to hide there. The mushroom jumped in the puddle and began to soak. The tree started to look for the mushroom. It looked and looked but could not find the mushroom. The mushroom was sad because it was too wet to play now. The tree said, "Mushroom, you are too wet to play. Go home and dry off." The mushroom went home, still wet and sad. It could not play anymore.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big tree with a high branch. Anna wants to climb the tree and sit on the high branch. Ben is scared of the high branch. He says, "No, Anna, it is too high. You can fall and hurt yourself." Anna says, "Don't be scared, Ben. I can climb the tree. It is fun. Look, there is a symbol on the high branch. It is a star. Maybe it is a magic star. I want to touch it and make a wish." Ben says, "What if the star is not magic? What if it is sharp and cuts you? Or what if someone else put it there and it is a trap? Please, Anna, come down. Let's play something else." Anna does not listen to Ben. She climbs the tree and reaches the high branch. She sees the star. It is shiny and pretty. She wants to touch it. She stretches her hand and grabs the star. But the star is not a star. It is a sticker. And it is sticky. It sticks to Anna's hand and does not come off. Anna tries to pull it off, but it hurts. She cries, "Ow, ow, ow! Help me, Ben! The star is not a star. It is a sticker. And it is sticky. And it hurts." Ben hears Anna cry. He is worried. He climbs the tree and goes to the high branch. He sees Anna with the sticker on her hand. He says, "Oh, Anna, I am sorry. You were right. It was a symbol. But it was a bad symbol. It was a trick. Someone put it there to fool you. Let me help you. Maybe we can accept the sticker and make it our friend. Maybe it will stop hurting you then." Anna says, "Really, Ben? Do you think so? How can we accept the sticker and make it our friend?" Ben says, "Maybe we can give it a name. And talk to it. And tell it we are sorry for taking it. And ask it to be nice to us. And share our snacks with it. And play with it. What do you think?" Anna says, "OK, Ben. That sounds good. Let's try it. What should we name the sticker?" Ben says, "How about... Sparky?" Anna says, "Sparky. I like it. Hello, Sparky. We are Anna and Ben. We are sorry for taking you. We want to be your friends. Will you be nice to us?" Sparky does not say anything. But it feels a little less sticky and a little less hurtful. Anna and Ben smile. They accept Sparky and make it their friend. They share their snacks with Sparky and play with Sparky. They have fun. They forget about the high branch and the bad symbol. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little boy named Tim. He loved to explore and find new things. One day, he saw a big boat. The boat took him to an island. Tim was very excited to see what he would find on the island. As Tim walked around the island, he saw a big box. He wanted to know what was inside. So, he pulled and pulled on the box until it opened. The box revealed a big, scary monster. Tim was very scared, but he could not run away. The monster chased Tim all around the island. Tim was very sad because the monster would not let him go home. In the end, Tim was stuck on the island with the scary monster forever.
