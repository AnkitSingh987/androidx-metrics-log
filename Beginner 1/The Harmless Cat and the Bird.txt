Category: Beginner
Title: The Harmless Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a harmless cat. The cat liked to play with a red brick. One day, the cat found a big pile of bricks. The cat was very happy and wanted to gain more bricks. The cat played with the bricks all day. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bricks away. The cat was sad and didn't know what to do. But then, a nice bird came and helped the cat. The bird showed the cat where the bricks went. The cat was happy again and played with the bricks. And the cat and the bird became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys and visit her aunt. Her aunt lived in a small house near a big tree. Lily liked to play in the tree with her aunt watching her. One day, Lily saw a cat stuck in the tree. She was alert and wanted to help the cat. She called her aunt and pointed to the cat. Her aunt nodded and said, "Let's help the cat, Lily." They both tried to reach the cat, but the cat was too high. Lily had an idea. She asked her aunt to get a long stick. Her aunt nodded and found a stick. They used the stick to help the cat down from the tree. The cat was happy and so were Lily and her aunt. They all played together under the big tree.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lizzy. She was very enthusiastic about rockets. Her mom took her to the park one day and she saw a big, colourful rocket. She was so excited that she asked her mom, "Can I go in it?". Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, sure you can." Lizzy asked, "Does it fly?". Her mom answered, "Not today, but when it's nighttime, it will." Lizzy was so happy and asked, "Can I sleep in it when it's night?". Her mom said, "You can try. Goodnight, sweetheart. Now it's time to sleep". The little girl went to sleep with dreams of flying in the rocket. When she woke up the next morning, she was so happy because she had spent the entire night in her big, colourful rocket. That is how Lizzy became the most enthusiastic rocket kid in town!

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. Tom had a silver car that he liked very much. Lily had a doll that she dressed up in different clothes. They were having fun, but they also made a lot of mess. "Tom, Lily, time to clean up!" their mom called from the kitchen. "We have to go to the park soon." Tom and Lily did not want to stop playing. They wanted to stay with their toys. "Mom, can we play a little more?" Tom asked. "No, you have to clean up now. Look at this mess!" their mom said. "Put your toys in the box, please." Tom and Lily sighed. They started to put their toys away, but they were very slow. Tom saw his silver car and picked it up. He wanted to play with it some more. "Tom, put the car in the box," Lily said. "No, I want to keep it," Tom said. "But you have to clean up," Lily said. "I don't care. It's my car," Tom said. He held the car tightly and ran to the couch. He hid behind the cushions. "Tom, come out of there," their mom said. "Don't be silly. You can play with the car later, but you have to clean up first." Tom shook his head. He did not want to listen. He insisted on keeping the car. "Tom, don't make me come and get you," their mom said. "You are being naughty. If you don't clean up, you can't go to the park." Tom did not like that. He liked the park. He liked the swings and the slides and the sandbox. He did not want to miss the park. He thought for a moment. He looked at his silver car. He liked the car, but he also liked the park. He decided to be a good boy. He came out from behind the couch. He gave the car to his mom. "Sorry, mom. I will clean up now," he said. "Thank you, Tom. That's very nice of you," his mom said. "You can have the car back when we come home from the park." Tom nodded. He smiled. He helped Lily clean up the rest of the mess. They finished quickly. They put on their shoes and coats. They were ready to go to the park. "Good job, Tom and Lily. You are very good children," their mom said. "Let's go to the park and have some fun." Tom and Lily cheered. They held their mom's hand and walked to the door. They were happy. They liked the park. They liked their mom. They liked their toys. They liked their silver car and their doll. They liked everything.
