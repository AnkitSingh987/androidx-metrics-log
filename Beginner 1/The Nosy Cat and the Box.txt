Category: Beginner
Title: The Nosy Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little cat. This cat loved to open things. One day, the cat saw a big gray box. The cat was very curious about what was inside the box. The cat tried to open the gray box, but it was too hard. The cat tried and tried, but the box would not open. The cat felt sad and did not know what to do. Then, a big dog came to help the nosy cat. The dog and the cat worked together to open the gray box. Finally, the box opened, and they found a lot of fun toys inside. The cat and the dog were very happy and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with a ball of thread. One day, Tom saw the thread on the table. He jumped up to get it. As Tom played with the thread, he did not see the cup on the table. He hit the cup and it fell. The cup broke with a loud noise. Tom felt sorry for the broken cup. Tom heard his friend, the dog named Max, coming. Tom paused and hid the thread. Max came into the room and saw the broken cup. Max thought he broke it! Tom came out and showed Max the thread. They both laughed and knew it was just an accident.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his friend, Sue. They were having fun, but they couldn't agree on what game to play next. Tim wanted to play catch, but Sue wanted to play hide and seek. They both got a little upset. Tim said, "Let's play catch, please!" Sue said, "No, I want to play hide and seek!" They kept saying the same thing, and they started to repeat it louder and louder. They were not ready to agree on a game. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's play a new game where we hide and when we find each other, we throw a ball!" Sue thought about it and said, "That sounds fun! I'm ready to play!" They both smiled and played the new game together. Their conflict was over, and they had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird. The bird had big wings. One day, the bird saw a wet lake. The bird wanted to play in the lake. The bird flew down to the lake and splashed in the water. The bird was having fun in the wet lake. The bird saw a frog. The frog was green and small. The frog said, "I can jump high." So the bird and the frog played together. They jumped and splashed in the lake. After playing, the bird was tired. The bird wanted to sleep. The bird flew to a tree and folded its wings. The bird slept in the tree. The frog slept by the lake. When they wake up, they will play again.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a beautiful bird sitting on a tree. The bird had pretty colors on it. Mia wanted to be friends with the bird. Mia called to the bird, "Hi, bird! Come play with me!" The bird looked at Mia and flew down to her. They played together in the park. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. After playing, the bird said, "Thank you, Mia, for being my friend!" Mia smiled and said, "You're welcome, bird! Let's play again soon!" The beautiful bird flew away, and Mia went home happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim had a dog named Max. They played together every day. One day, Tim had a test at school. He was worried. He told Max, "I am scared of the test, Max." Max looked at Tim and wagged his tail. Max wanted to help Tim feel better. After school, Tim came back home. He saw Max chewing on a toy. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Max. Watching you chew your toy makes me feel better about the test." They played together, and Tim was not scared anymore.
