Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy and the Trip

Timmy was eager to go on a trip. He went to his parents and asked them where to choose. His parents were so excited that he wanted to go on a trip that they hurried to plan everything. His mom suggested the beach and his dad suggested the mountains. Timmy was uncertain which one to choose. He wanted to explore them both. So his dad suggested that they could choose both. Timmy was so eager to go on a trip with his parents. They decided to go to the beach one week and to the mountains the next. They packed their bags and went to the beach. They had a lot of fun. They played in the sand, swam in the sea, and ate lots of delicious treats. When their week was over, they packed their bags again and went to the mountains. They hiked up the hills and visited beautiful lakes. They even saw lots of animals and plants. There were so many new things to see. At the end of their trip, they were happy they chose to go to the beach and the mountains. They had so much fun and were eager to plan their next trip.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They had a big bag with toys and snacks. They liked to share and have fun. But then a big dog came. It was orange and loud. It ran to the bag and grabbed it with its teeth. It ran away with the bag. Tom and Lily were scared and sad. They wanted their bag back. They shouted at the dog, but it did not listen. It was too fast and too far. They started to weep. They wept loud and hard. They did not know what to do. A nice lady saw them. She had a blue hat and a green coat. She came to them and asked what was wrong. "We lost our bag!" Tom said. "A big dog took it!" "That's not good!" the lady said. "Do you know where the dog went?" Lily pointed to the woods. "It went there!" "Come with me," the lady said. "Maybe we can find it." She took their hands and walked with them to the woods. She was kind and brave. She helped them look for the dog and the bag. They found them behind a tree. The dog was playing with the toys and eating the snacks. It did not see them. The lady had a plan. She took a stick and threw it. The dog saw the stick and ran after it. It dropped the bag. Tom and Lily ran to the bag. They were happy and relieved. They hugged the bag and thanked the lady. The lady smiled and said, "You're welcome. I'm glad you got your bag back. Now, let's go back to the park and have some more fun."

One day, a little cat with soft fur was walking in the woods. She saw a big dog with rough fur. The little cat began to wonder if the big dog was dangerous. She was scared but wanted to be friends. The little cat said, "Hi, do you want to play?" The big dog looked at her and said, "Yes, I want to play, but I am not dangerous. My fur may be rough, but I am a nice dog." They played together all day long. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew them up into a tree. They were scared, but they helped each other get down. The little cat and the big dog became best friends. They learned that even if someone looks different, they can still be a good friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside. One day, it was very cold, so she put on her coat, hat, and gloves. She went outside to play with her friends. While they were playing, Lily took off her gloves to pick up a pretty rock. But she forgot to put them back on. Her hands started to feel cold. She looked around and saw her gloves on the ground. They were dry and warm. Lily realized she needed to put them back on to keep her hands warm. Lily put her gloves back on, and her hands felt much better. She played with her friends all day long. When it was time to go home, Lily remembered to keep her gloves on. She was happy that her hands stayed warm and dry.
