Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rocket

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim who loved rockets. He had a toy rocket that he played with every day. It was a delicate rocket, so Tim had to be very careful with it. One day, he saw a big rocket on TV and wanted to see one in real life. Tim asked his mom, "Can we go see a big rocket, please?" His mom said, "Maybe one day, Tim. Be patient." Tim was sad, but he kept playing with his toy rocket, hoping that one day he could see a real one. One day, when Tim was playing with his rocket, his mom said, "Tim, we have a surprise for you." She took him to a big place with a huge rocket! Tim was so happy. He demanded, "Can we go inside?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. It's delicate like your toy rocket." Tim went inside the big rocket and had the best day ever.

Lily and Ben like to play in the park. They run and jump and slide and swing. They have a lot of fun. Lily has a funny belt. It is pink and shiny and has a big bow. Ben likes Lily's belt. He wants to play with it. One day, Lily and Ben go to the park with their moms. They see a big hill. They want to roll down the hill. Lily takes off her funny belt and puts it on the grass. She says, "Don't touch my belt, Ben. It is mine." Ben nods. He says, "OK, Lily. I won't touch your belt." Lily and Ben run to the top of the hill. They lie down and roll down the hill. They laugh and scream. They have a lot of fun. But Ben sees Lily's funny belt on the grass. He thinks, "Maybe I can touch it for a little bit. Lily won't know." He runs to the grass and picks up Lily's belt. He puts it around his neck. He pretends he is a superhero. He says, "Look at me, Lily. I am Super Ben!" Lily sees Ben with her funny belt. She gets angry. She says, "Ben, give me back my belt! You said you won't touch it!" She runs to Ben and tries to pull her belt from his neck. Ben says, "No, Lily. It is my belt now. It is fun!" He pulls back. They tug and tug. They don't see the big rock on the grass. They trip over the rock and fall down. Lily's belt gets stuck on the rock. It is tight around Ben's neck. He can't breathe. He turns red. He chokes. He cries. Lily and Ben's moms hear them cry. They run to them. They see Ben with Lily's belt around his neck. They are scared. They try to loosen the belt. But it is too late. Ben is not breathing. He is not moving. He is gone. Lily and Ben's moms hug them and cry. Lily cries. She says, "I'm sorry, Ben. I'm sorry, Mom. I didn't mean to hurt him. I just wanted my belt back." She looks at her funny belt. It is not funny anymore. It is sad. She hates her belt. She wishes she never had it. She wishes Ben was still alive. She wishes they could play again.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play golf. His dad said, "I will explain how to play." Tim was happy and listened to his dad. They went to the golf course with their golf clubs. Tim's dad explained, "To play fair, you have to take turns and not cheat." Tim and his dad took turns hitting the golf ball. Tim tried to hit the ball far, but it did not go far. His dad hit the ball very far. Tim was sad, but his dad said, "It's okay, just keep trying." They played all day and had fun. Tim learned to play fair and not cheat. He also learned that it is okay to not be the best at something, as long as you try your best and have fun. This made Tim feel good and he was happy.
