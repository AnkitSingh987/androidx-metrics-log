Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Magic

Once upon a time, a graceful cat named Lily lived in a big house. She loved to play with her ball and jump high in the air. One day, a new dog named Max arrived at the house. Max was big and loud. Lily did not like Max because he scared her. Lily thought of a plan. She knew a magic spell that could make Max go away. She tried to say the spell, but Max was always too loud. Lily felt sad and wanted to find a way to say the spell without Max hearing her. One day, Lily found a quiet spot in the garden. She said the magic spell and Max became very small. Now, Max was not loud and scary anymore. Lily and Max played together and became friends. They lived happily ever after in their big house.

Billy was playing with his blocks. He loved them and enjoyed making towers. He worked carefully and made nice neat piles. The tidy rows made Billy smile. But then Billy dropped the blocks and felt helpless. He was sad, he did not know how to solve his problem. But then his mommy came and said, "Your blocks are all over! Let me help you." She picked up each block with a gentle touch. As she wrapped them in her arms, Billy was amazed. His mommy's love was like a hug. Billy was no longer helpless and he hugged his mommy back. He couldn't believe that she had wrapped the blocks so perfectly!

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. Bob loved to draw pictures. One day, he wanted to make a huge picture. But Bob did not have a big paper. So, he went to his friend, the rabbit named Ray. Bob asked Ray if he could borrow a big paper. Ray said, "I do not have one now, but I will find one for you tomorrow." Bob was happy and said thank you to his friend. He went back home and waited for the next day. The next day, Ray came to Bob's house with a huge paper. Bob was very excited. He started to draw a big picture of his friend Ray. When he was done, Bob showed the picture to Ray. Ray was so happy to see the beautiful picture. They both smiled and knew that their friendship was very special.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and balls. One day, they see a big truck with many boxes. The truck is near a store. The store is closed. "Look, Sam, maybe there are toys in the boxes!" Tom says. "Let's go and see!" Sam says. They run to the truck and open a box. They find many dolls and games. They are happy. "Wow, these are great! Let's take some!" Tom says. He puts a doll and a game in his bag. Sam does the same. But then, a man comes out of the store. He is angry. He sees Tom and Sam with the boxes. "Hey, you! Stop! What are you doing?" he shouts. He runs to them and grabs their bags. He sees the dolls and games. "These are mine! You are stealing!" he says. He calls the police. The police come and take Tom and Sam. They put them in a car. They drive them to a place. The place is jail. Tom and Sam are scared. They cry. "Please, let us go! We are sorry!" they say. But the police do not listen. They lock them in a room. The room is small and dark. There are no toys, no cars, no balls. Tom and Sam are sad. They wish they did not take the boxes. They wish they did not make the man angry. They wish they listened to their moms and dads. They learn a lesson. They learn that stealing is bad. They learn that they have to accept the rules. They learn that they have to be good.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the backyard. They have a big sandbox, a swing, and a slide. They also have a friendly dog named Spot. Spot likes to run and jump with them. One day, Anna and Ben are playing in the sandbox. They are making a castle with buckets and shovels. Spot is watching them and wagging his tail. He wants to join the fun. "Can Spot help us?" Anna asks Ben. "Sure, he can dig with his paws," Ben says. They call Spot and he comes over. He starts to dig in the sand. He is very happy. He digs so fast that he makes a big hole. He also makes a big mess. He throws sand all over the place. He throws sand on Anna and Ben. He throws sand on the castle. He ruins the castle. "Spot, stop!" Anna and Ben shout. "You are ruining our castle!" Spot does not listen. He keeps digging and throwing sand. He thinks it is a game. He does not know he is making Anna and Ben angry. Anna and Ben are very angry. They do not want to play with Spot anymore. They turn away from him. They go to the swing and the slide. They leave Spot alone in the sandbox. Spot is sad. He does not understand why Anna and Ben are angry. He does not understand why they do not want to play with him. He wants to play with them. He wants to be their friend. He looks at the swing and the slide. He sees Anna and Ben having fun. He wants to have fun too. He decides to try the swing and the slide. He runs to the swing. He jumps on it. He tries to swing. He falls off. He runs to the slide. He climbs on it. He tries to slide. He slides too fast. He bumps his head. Spot is hurt. He cries. He wants Anna and Ben to help him. He wants Anna and Ben to hug him. He wants Anna and Ben to love him. Anna and Ben hear Spot crying. They turn around. They see Spot lying on the ground. They see he is hurt. They feel sorry for him. They run to him. They hug him. They kiss him. They say they are sorry. "Spot, we are sorry. We were angry. We did not mean to hurt you. We love you. You are our friend," Anna and Ben say. Spot stops crying. He wags his tail. He licks their faces. He is happy. He loves them too. He is their friend. They all go back to the sandbox. They make a new castle. They make it bigger and better. They let Spot help them. They have fun. They are happy. They are friends.
