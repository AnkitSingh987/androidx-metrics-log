Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw a big shell far away. Tim wanted to get it. He said, "Mom, look! A big shell!" His mom said, "Yes, Tim! Let's go get it!" They walked to the distant shell and picked it up. It was very pretty. Tim saw some kids playing a game. They were racing to a tree. Tim wanted to win. He asked, "Can I play too?" The kids said, "Yes, come play!" Tim ran as fast as he could. He won the race! The kids clapped and said, "Good job!" Tim was happy. He showed them his big shell. They all liked it. They played together all day at the beach.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big white dog. The dog was wet and dirty. It looked sad and hungry. "Can we pet the dog?" Lily asked Tom. "I don't know. Maybe it is not nice," Tom said. But Lily wanted to pet the dog. She walked to the dog and said, "Hello, dog. You are very white. Do you want to play with us?" The dog did not want to play. The dog was angry and scared. It barked and bit Lily's hand. Lily screamed and cried. Tom ran to help her. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts!" Lily said. "Let go, dog! Let go!" Tom said. But the dog did not let go. It held Lily's hand in its mouth. Lily and Tom suffered a lot. They were scared and hurt. A man came and saw what happened. He shouted at the dog and kicked it. The dog ran away. The man called for help. He took Lily and Tom to the hospital. Lily and Tom had to stay in the hospital for a long time. They had to get shots and medicine. They had to wear bandages and casts. They could not play in the park anymore. They were sad and bored. The dog was never found. It was still wet and dirty. It was still sad and hungry. It was still angry and scared. It suffered a lot, too. It had no friends and no home. The end.

Once upon a time there was a young boy called Jack. Jack lived in a big house and he was very lucky. Every day he would do something naughty. One day, Jack did something very bad so his father decided to punish him. He asked Jack to go outside and pick a skull off the ground. Jack was scared but he went to do it. When he found the skull, Jack put it in his pocket. Then he went back inside the house to show his dad. His dad was very angry and said, "This is your punishment". Jack was very sad because he had been so naughty. He promised himself he would never do something bad again. Then he was allowed to keep the skull as a special reminder. The next day, Jack was very lucky. His dad smiled at him and told him that he was proud. Jack was happy because he had been very good. From that day on, he promised himself to be extra good and never forget his lucky skull.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to read and listen to stories. One day, Tim's mom told him a poem. Tim did not know what a poem was, so he asked his mom, "What is a poem?" His mom said, "A poem is a story with words that sound nice together." Tim felt happy to learn something new. The next day, Tim went to the park to play. Tim saw his friend, Sam, who was kneeling on the ground. Tim asked Sam, "What are you doing?" Sam said, "I am looking for bugs. I like bugs." Tim said, "I learned about poems yesterday. Do you know what a poem is?" Sam looked at Tim and said, "No, I don't. I don't know many things. I am ignorant." Just then, a big bird flew down from the sky and landed next to Tim and Sam. The bird said, "I heard you talking about poems. I know a poem about bugs!" Tim and Sam were surprised that a bird could talk. They listened as the bird told them a poem about bugs. The bird's poem made Tim and Sam laugh and clap. They were happy to learn a new poem and make a new friend.
