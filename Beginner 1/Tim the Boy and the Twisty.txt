Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Twisty

One day, a boy named Tim found an amazing picture in his toy box. It was a picture of a big, twisty tree. Tim loved the tree and wanted to show his mom. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, look at this picture! It's a twisty tree!" His mom looked at the picture and said, "Wow, Tim! That is an amazing twisty tree!" Tim wanted to see the twisty tree for real. He asked his mom, "Can we find this tree, please?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, let's go on an adventure to find the twisty tree!" They went outside and walked for a while. Tim was so excited! After a long walk, they found the twisty tree. Tim looked up at the tree and said, "It's just like the picture! This is the best day ever!"

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the sand. They have a bucket and a shovel. They make a big castle with the sand. They feel happy. "Look at our castle!" Lila says. "It is so big and pretty!" "Yes, it is!" Ben says. "We are good at making castles!" They put a shell on top of the castle. It is shiny and pink. They feel proud. A big wave comes. It is loud and wet. It washes away the castle. The shell is gone. The bucket and the shovel are gone. Lila and Ben feel sad. "Oh no!" Lila says. "Our castle is gone!" "It's okay," Ben says. "We can make another one. We still have each other. We are lucky." They hug. They feel better. They look for a new bucket and a shovel. They find them. They smile. They make a new castle. They feel happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the snow. He liked to make snowmen, snowballs, and snow angels. One day, Tim saw a big snowman in his yard. He wanted to make it more useful. Tim thought about what he could do. He had an idea! He put a hat on the snowman's head. Now, the snowman could keep the hat warm and safe. Tim felt happy because he gave value to the snowman. Tim's friends saw the snowman with the hat. They liked the idea too. They all started to put their hats on the snowman. Now, the snowman was very useful and made everyone happy. Tim learned that even snow can have value when you make it useful.

One day, a little cat named Tim went for a walk. Tim saw a big door with a shiny handle. He wanted to open the door, but he was too small. Tim felt stupid because he could not reach the handle. He was sad and shivered in the cold wind. A big dog named Sam saw Tim shiver. Sam wanted to help Tim. Sam jumped up and pushed the door handle with his nose. The door opened, and they both went inside. It was warm and cozy in there. Tim stopped shivering and felt happy. Inside, they found a room full of toys. Tim and Sam played with the toys and had lots of fun. They learned that helping each other made them feel good. The moral of the story is to always help your friends when they need it, and don't feel stupid if you can't do something by yourself.

Lily and Tom are friends. They love to play games. Today they play a game with cards. Lily has a card with a red heart. Tom has a card with a blue star. "Who will win?" Lily asks. "I don't know. Let's see." Tom says. They show their cards to each other. Lily sees Tom's card. Tom sees Lily's card. "Wow, you have a star. That is cool." Lily says. "Thank you. You have a heart. That is sweet." Tom says. They smile at each other. They are happy. They both win. They hug and say: "We are friends. We love each other."

One day, a girl named Sue went to the shop with her mom. They wanted to buy sweet treats. Sue saw a big jar of sweet candy. She was very happy. "Mom, can we buy the sweet candy?" Sue asked. Her mom said, "Yes, but only one for each of us." Sue picked a red candy and her mom picked a green one. They paid for the candy and left the shop. As they walked home, Sue's candy fell on the ground. She was sad. Her mom said, "Don't cry, we can wipe it and it will be clean." They wiped the candy and Sue ate it. But, the candy tasted like dirt! They both laughed and went back to the shop to get a new sweet candy.
