Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam Find the Car

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys in the living room. Tim has a big truck and Sam has a small car. They make noises and drive their toys on the floor. "Look, Tim, I can go fast!" Sam says, zooming his car past Tim's truck. "Slow down, Sam, you will crash!" Tim says, following his truck with his eyes. But Sam does not listen. He goes faster and faster, until he hits a fat pillow on the sofa. His car flies in the air and lands on the floor. It breaks into two pieces. "Oh no, my car!" Sam cries, picking up the pieces. He feels sad and angry. "I told you to slow down, Sam. Now your car is broken. You should be more careful." Tim says, shaking his head. Sam looks at Tim and his truck. He has an idea. He throws one piece of his car at Tim's truck. It hits the truck and makes a dent. "Hey, what are you doing, Sam? That's not nice!" Tim says, surprised and annoyed. Sam throws the other piece of his car at Tim's truck. It hits the truck and makes another dent. "Stop it, Sam! You are ruining my truck!" Tim says, getting up and grabbing his truck. Sam gets up too and runs away. He laughs and sticks out his tongue. "Ha ha, now your truck is broken too!" Sam says, running to the kitchen. Tim chases him, holding his truck. He is very angry. "Come back here, Sam! You are in big trouble!" Tim says, running after him. Mom hears the noise and comes out of the kitchen. She sees Tim and Sam running and yelling. She sees the broken car and the dented truck on the floor. She is not happy. "Boys, boys, what is going on here? Why are you fighting? And what happened to your toys?" Mom says, stopping them. Tim and Sam stop and look at Mom. They feel guilty and scared. "Sam broke his car and then he broke my truck!" Tim says, pointing at Sam. "Tim was mean and he did not let me play with his truck!" Sam says, pointing at Tim. Mom sighs and shakes her head. She picks up the broken car and the dented truck. She sits on the sofa and puts the boys on her lap. "Boys, boys, you are brothers. You should not fight. You should share and be kind. And you should take care of your toys. They are not for throwing or breaking. They are for playing and having fun. Do you understand?" Mom says, looking at them. Tim and Sam nod and look down. They feel sorry and ashamed. "We are sorry, Mom. We were bad." Tim says, hugging Mom. "We are sorry, Mom. We were bad." Sam says, hugging Mom. Mom smiles and hugs them back. She kisses them on their heads. "It's okay, boys. I love you. But you have to promise me that you will not fight again. And you will try to fix your toys. Can you do that?" Mom says, looking at them. Tim and Sam nod and look up. They smile and hug Mom. "We promise, Mom. We love you." Tim says, kissing Mom. "We promise, Mom. We love you." Sam says, kissing Mom. Mom smiles and kisses them again. She gives them their toys and helps them to fix them. They say thank you and play nicely together. They are happy and Mom is happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a soft, fluffy pillow that she loved very much. Every night, she would hug her pillow and dream of happy things. One day, Lily met a small, angry bird. The bird was upset because he lost his nest. Lily wanted to help the bird. She said, "Don't worry, I will help you find your home." Together, they looked high and low for the bird's nest. Finally, they found it in a big tree. The bird was so happy, he thanked Lily and flew back to his home. That night, Lily hugged her pillow and had a dream. In her dream, she was playing with the happy bird in a beautiful park. They were both very happy, and Lily knew she had made a new friend.
