Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Toys

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play at the gym. The gym has many toys and games. They can run, jump, swing, and slide. They have fun at the gym. One day, Tim and Sam see a new boy at the gym. His name is Ben. Ben has no toys and no games. He has only a ball. He kicks the ball by himself. He looks sad. Tim and Sam want to play with Ben. They go to him and say, "Hi, Ben. Do you want to play with us? We have many toys and games. You can share with us." Ben looks at Tim and Sam. He says, "No, thank you. I have my own toys and games. They are at home. They are better than yours. I don't need to share." Tim and Sam are surprised. They think Ben is lying. They say, "Really? What toys and games do you have at home? Can you tell us?" Ben thinks for a moment. He says, "I have a big car, a big train, a big robot, a big dinosaur, and a big castle. They are all mine. They are all shiny and new. They are all fun." Tim and Sam are more surprised. They think Ben is lying more. They say, "Wow, that sounds amazing. Can we see them? Can we come to your home?" Ben shakes his head. He says, "No, you can't. My home is far away. You can't find it. You can't come. You can't see. You can only play with your toys and games. I can only play with my ball." Tim and Sam are sad. They think Ben is poor. They think he has no toys and games at home. They think he is lying to hide his sadness. They want to help him. They want to be his friends. They say, "Ben, we are sorry. We don't believe you. We think you are lying. We think you are poor. We think you have no toys and games at home. We think you are sad. We want to help you. We want to be your friends. Please, don't lie to us. Please, play with us. Please, share with us. We have enough for all. We can have fun together." Ben looks at Tim and Sam. He sees their kind eyes and their smiling faces. He feels their warm hearts and their gentle words. He feels ashamed of his lies. He feels sorry for his pride. He feels lonely for his ball. He feels happy for their offer. He says, "Tim, Sam, thank you. You are right. I am lying. I am poor. I have no toys and games at home. I am sad. I need your help. I need your friendship. Please, let me play with you. Please, let me share with you. I have only a ball, but I can give it to you. We can have fun together." Tim and Sam are glad. They hug Ben. They say, "Ben, welcome. You are our friend. You don't need to lie to us. You don't need to be poor. You don't need to be sad. You have us. We have you. We have many toys and games. We have a ball. We have fun together." They take Ben to the gym. They show him their toys and games. They play with his ball. They run, jump, swing, and slide. They have fun at the gym. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Tuna. Tuna lived in a big pond with his fish friends. They loved to play and swim all day long. Tuna was a very happy fish, but sometimes he would worry. One day, Tuna saw a big, dead tree in the pond. He was scared and worried. Tuna thought, "What if the tree hurts my friends?" He wanted to make sure they were safe, so he swam around the tree to see if it was okay. Tuna's friends saw him worry and swam over to help. Together, they pushed the dead tree out of the pond. They were all safe again. Tuna was so happy and thankful for his friends. From that day on, they played and swam without any worries.
