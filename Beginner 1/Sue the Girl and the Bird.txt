Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Sue found a needle in her mom's sewing box. Sue wanted to play with the needle, but her mom said, "Be careful, Sue. The needle can pinch you." Sue did not listen to her mom. She took the needle and went to play with her friend, Tom. They played a game where they tried to pinch each other with the needle. "Ouch!" Tom cried. "That hurts!" Sue felt bad for hurting her friend. Just then, a big wind came and blew the needle away. Sue and Tom were sad and scared. They thought they would be in trouble for losing the needle. But then, a kind bird flew down and gave the needle back to them. Sue and Tom were so happy! They learned to be careful and never played with the needle again.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Lily. Lily loved to count and number her toys. She had a big room full of toys, but her favorite toy was her small trophy. One day, Lily's friend Tom came to play. Tom saw the small trophy and said, "Wow, what is that?" Lily smiled and said, "This is my small trophy. I got it for counting to ten all by myself!" Tom wanted a trophy too, so Lily decided to help him. They sat and practiced counting together. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten! Tom was so happy when he could count to ten. Lily's mom saw how helpful Lily was and gave Tom a small trophy too. Tom and Lily played with their small trophies all day, proud of their counting skills. They knew that helping each other was the best way to learn and have fun.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the yard. They have a ball and a bike. They take turns to kick the ball and ride the bike. One day, a new neighbor moves in next door. He is a big boy. He has a big dog. He does not say hello. He looks serious. Tom and Lily are curious. They want to see the new neighbor and his dog. They go to the fence and peek over. The big boy is playing with his dog. He throws a stick and the dog runs to get it. "Hi, what is your name?" Tom asks. The big boy does not answer. He glares at Tom and Lily. He does not like them. "Go away, this is my yard. You are not welcome here." He says. He picks up a rock and throws it at Tom and Lily. It hits the fence and makes a loud noise. Tom and Lily are scared. They run back to their yard. They drop the ball and the bike. "Hey, that is not nice. You are mean." Lily says. She is angry. She wants to stand up to the big boy. She picks up the ball and throws it back over the fence. It hits the big boy on the head. "Ow, that hurts. Stop it." He says. He is surprised. He did not expect Lily to fight back. He throws the stick at Lily. It misses her and hits the bike. It breaks the bike. Tom and Lily are sad. They love their bike. They cry. The big boy is sorry. He did not mean to break the bike. He feels bad. He goes to the fence and says, "I am sorry. I was rude. My name is Sam. Can we be friends?" Tom and Lily are confused. They do not know what to say. They look at each other. They look at Sam. They look at the ball, the bike, and the dog. They think for a moment. They decide to give Sam a chance. They say, "OK, we can be friends. Our names are Tom and Lily. Do you want to play with us?" Sam smiles. He says, "Yes, I do. Thank you. Can I pet your dog?" Tom and Lily nod. They say, "Sure, his name is Spot. He is friendly." They go to the fence and open the gate. They invite Sam and his dog to their yard. They share the ball and the bike. They play together. They have fun. They are happy. They are neighbors. They are friends.
