Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Tops

Ben and Lily were playing with their tops on the floor. They liked to spin them and watch them go round and round. Sometimes they would make them crash into each other and see which one would stop first. "Look, mine is faster than yours!" Ben said, laughing. "No, mine is faster!" Lily said, laughing too. They spun their tops again and again, having fun. But then, their mom came into the room. She looked angry and had a big bag in her hand. "Ben, Lily, what are you doing? You are making a mess! And you are late for school! Hurry up and put your tops away, we have to go now!" she said, in a loud voice. Ben and Lily felt anxious. They did not want to go to school. They wanted to play with their tops. They did not like their mom's angry face and big bag. They wondered what was in it. "Mom, can we play a little more? Please?" Ben asked, in a small voice. "No, you can't! You have wasted enough time! Now, put your tops in this bag, and let's go!" mom said, grabbing their tops and throwing them in the bag. Ben and Lily cried. They did not want to give up their tops. They loved their tops. They tried to get them back, but mom was too strong. She dragged them to the car, with the bag. She did not listen to their pleas. "Mom, please, give us our tops back! We are sorry! We will go to school, we promise!" Lily said, sobbing. "Too late! You should have listened to me! Now, you will have no tops, and no fun, for a long time!" mom said, driving away. Ben and Lily looked at each other, with sad eyes. They did not laugh anymore. They did not play anymore. They did not have their tops anymore. They only had their mom's angry face and big bag. They hated them. They wished they could spin their tops again, and watch them go round and round. But they could not. They had lost their tops, and their happiness, forever.

One day, a little cat named Tom found a box of matches. Tom did not know what matches were, so he played with them. He scratched one match on the box, and it made a fire. Tom was scared. Tom ran to his friend, a deaf dog named Max. Tom said, "Help! I made fire with a match!" Max could not hear Tom, but he saw the fire. Max knew they had to put the fire out. Tom and Max ran to the jail where their friend, a bird named Polly, lived. Polly saw the fire too. She said, "I know! We can use water to put the fire out!" They all worked together and put the fire out. They were happy and safe again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play by the shore. He would run and jump in the water. Spot liked to dig in the sand too. The sand was dirty, but Spot didn't care. He was having fun. One day, Spot was very tired from playing. He wanted to take a nap. Spot found a nice, warm spot on the shore. The sun was shining, and the sand felt soft. Spot lay down and closed his eyes. As Spot napped, a little bird came by. The bird saw Spot and thought he was dirty from playing in the sand. The bird wanted to help. So, the bird splashed water on Spot to clean him. Spot woke up from his nap and saw the bird. He was happy to have a new friend. They played together by the shore all day long.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wanted to play a game. He had a ball and a goal. He asked his friend, Sam, to play with him. Sam said, "Yes, let's play!" They took turns kicking the ball at the goal. Tim was very good at it. Sam was not so good. He felt embarrassed because he kept missing the goal. Tim tried to help him. He said, "Don't worry, Sam. Just keep trying." Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and picked up the ball. The bird did not want to give it back. Tim and Sam did not know what to do. They asked the bird to sign the ball and give it back. The bird thought about it and then signed the ball with its beak. The bird dropped the ball back to Tim and Sam. They were so happy to have their ball back. They played together and Sam got better at kicking the ball. They laughed and had fun. The end.
