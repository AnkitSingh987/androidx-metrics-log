Category: Beginner
Title: Jim and the Door

Jim was feeling ready. He looked around his room and smiled. Everything was neat and tidy. He had put all of his toys away, hung all of his clothes on hangers and made his bed. He was ready to go. He looked at his reflection in the mirror and, with a blink, all his excitement came out. Then, he heard a knock at the door. "Come in," he said. In came his mom. "Are you ready to go, Jim?" she asked. Jim smiled and blinked. "Yes, Mom," he said, "I'm ready!" His mom smiled. She gave him a big hug, then grabbed his hand and said, "Let's go!" Jim and his mom walked out the door, ready to start their day.

One day, a strong boy named Sam went to the park. He saw a big statue. He wanted to guess what it was. He asked his friend, "What do you think it is?" His friend said, "I think it is a big cat." Sam thought for a moment and said, "No, I think it is a big dog." As they were talking, the statue started to move! It was not a statue at all, but a real big dog! The dog was nice and wanted to play. Sam and his friend were very surprised. They played with the dog all day. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye to the dog. Sam and his friend learned that it is good to be kind to everyone, even if they are not what you think they are.

Once upon a time, in a big green jungle, there was a fragile little bird named Tilly. Tilly had soft, pretty wings, but she could not fly well. Tilly was sad because she wanted to play with her friends in the sky. One day, Tilly met a kind monkey named Max. Max saw that Tilly was sad and wanted to help. Max had an idea. He took Tilly to a big tree, where he found a long vine. Max cut the vine into small pieces with his sharp teeth. Max tied the vine pieces to Tilly's wings to make them stronger. Tilly tried to fly again, and this time, she could fly! Tilly was so happy. She thanked Max and flew up into the sky to play with her friends. From that day on, Tilly and Max were the best of friends in the big green jungle.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing with his ball. He loved to jump and catch it. Max was very good at it. But today, Max missed the ball. It went far away. Max was sad. Max looked for the ball. He walked and walked. He felt hungry in his stomach. Max wanted to find his ball and go home. He wanted to be safe and eat food. Max saw a big tree. He found his ball under the tree. Max was happy. He ran home with his ball. Max's home was safe and warm. He ate food and filled his stomach. Max played with his ball and had a good day.

One day, a brown dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big house with a glass door. Max was very happy and wanted to go inside. He thought, "I want to see what is behind the glass door!" Max walked to the glass door and looked inside. He saw a little girl named Lily. Lily saw Max and said, "Hi, dog! Do you want to come in?" Max wagged his tail and went inside the house. Lily and Max played together and had a lot of fun. After playing, Lily and Max were very thirsty. Lily said, "Let's go get some water!" They went to the kitchen and Lily got a glass for her and a bowl for Max. They drank water and felt better. Max was happy that he went inside the big house and made a new friend.

One day, a little gray bug went for a walk. The bug saw a big leaf. The leaf was green and pretty. The little bug wanted to use the leaf as a bed. The bug met a friend, a red ant. The red ant said, "Hi, little gray bug! What are you doing?" The little bug said, "I want to use this leaf as a bed. Can you help me?" The red ant and the little gray bug worked together. They moved the big leaf to a nice spot. The little gray bug was very happy. The bug said, "Thank you, red ant!" The red ant said, "You're welcome, little gray bug! Sleep well on your new leaf bed!"
