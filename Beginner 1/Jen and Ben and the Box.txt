Category: Beginner
Title: Jen and Ben and the Box

Jen and Ben were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big box in the living room. It had a bow and a tag that said "For Jen and Ben, from Mom and Dad". "What is in the box?" Jen asked. "I don't know. Let's open it!" Ben said. They were very nosy and curious. They tried to pull the bow, but it was too tight. They tried to tear the paper, but it was too thick. They tried to shake the box, but it was too heavy. They needed something to help them open the box. They looked around and saw a whip on the wall. It was Dad's whip that he used to ride horses. It was long and thin and had a handle. "Maybe we can use the whip to cut the paper!" Ben said. He took the whip from the wall and swung it in the air. It made a loud sound. Jen covered her ears. "Be careful, Ben!" Jen said. Ben tried to hit the box with the whip, but he missed. He hit the lamp instead. The lamp fell and broke. The whip got tangled in the wires. "Oh no!" Ben said. He dropped the whip and ran to the box. He tried to wrap the paper around the box again, but it was too late. Mom and Dad came into the living room and saw the mess. "What are you doing?" Mom asked. "We wanted to see what was in the box!" Jen said. "It was a surprise for your birthday!" Dad said. Jen and Ben felt sorry. They hugged Mom and Dad and said they were sorry. Mom and Dad smiled and forgave them. They helped them unwrap the box and showed them what was inside. It was a big teddy bear for Jen and a big truck for Ben. Jen and Ben were happy. They thanked Mom and Dad and played with their new toys. They learned not to be too nosy and to wait for surprises.

One day, a polite boy named Tim went to a camp. The camp had many kids and they all played together. Tim made a new friend named Sam. Sam and Tim liked to play with toy cars and run in the park. One day, two other kids at the camp started to fight. Tim and Sam saw this and did not like it. Tim said to Sam, "We should not fight, we should be friends." Sam agreed and said, "Yes, let's tell them to stop." Tim and Sam went to the fighting kids and said, "Please stop fighting. We should all be friends and play together." The kids stopped fighting and they all played together. From that day on, everyone at the camp was polite and had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very dull dog. He did not like to play or run. Spot just liked to sit and watch the world go by. One day, Spot saw a big bird. The bird had some meat in its mouth. Spot wanted the meat, so he followed the bird. The bird flew up, up, up into the sky. Spot could not fly, so he just watched the bird go away. Spot was sad, but he did not give up. He walked and walked until he found a nice place to settle down. He sat and waited for the bird to come back. After a long time, the bird came back with more meat. This time, Spot was ready. He jumped and caught the meat. Spot was very happy and ate the meat. From that day on, Spot was not a dull dog anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an obedient boy who always listened to his mom and dad. One day, Tim's mom and dad told him he could join a camp with his friends. Tim was very happy and excited. At the camp, Tim and his friends played fun games and sang songs. They had a very good time. But one day, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came to the camp. The dog was lost and wanted to play with Tim and his friends. Tim and his friends were scared at first, but then they saw that the dog was nice. They decided to let the dog join them at the camp. They played with the dog and had even more fun. At the end of the camp, the dog's owner came to find him. The owner was so happy to see his dog and thanked Tim and his friends for being kind. Tim and his friends were sad to say goodbye to the dog, but they knew they would always remember their special camp friend.
