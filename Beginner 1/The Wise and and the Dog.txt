Category: Beginner
Title: The Wise and and the Dog

Once there was a wise and intelligent dog. He liked to explore and learn new things. One day, he decided to go on a tour. So, he rushed off to find out new places. The first place he visited was a park. As he was roaming around, he noticed a group of kids playing. He decided to join the fun. He barked loudly to get their attention. One of the kids asked him, “What do you want?” The dog replied, “I want to play with you all.” The kids excitedly rushed towards him. They all had a great time playing and running around. When it was time for the dog to go, the kids thanked him for being so friendly and intelligent. He smiled and waved goodbye before rushing off.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to play in the park with her friends. One sunny day, Lily saw a new swing at the park. She was very happy and excited to try it. Lily ran quick to the swing and sat on it. She started to swing back and forth, higher and higher. Her friends watched her and clapped their hands. They thought the swing was very attractive and fun. Soon, all of Lily's friends took turns on the swing. They laughed and had a great time. They played on the swing all day long. The new swing made their day at the park the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tim. He liked to play outside with his friends. Every day, they would run and jump in the park. Tim was very happy. One day, at noon, Tim and his friends went to the park. They played and had fun. Tim noticed a big tree. He wanted to climb it. His friends said, "No, Tim! It's too high!" But Tim didn't listen. Tim climbed the tree. He went up and up. But then, he fell. Tim got hurt and cried. His friends ran to help him. The strong boy learned that he should listen to his friends.

Anna and Ben are friends. They live in the same nation. The nation is big and has many people and animals. Anna and Ben like to play with the animals. They feed them, pet them, and talk to them. One day, Anna and Ben go to the farm. They see a cow, a pig, a sheep, and a horse. They say hello to them and give them some food. Then they see a duck. The duck is in a pond. The duck is dirty and sad. "Look, Ben, the duck is dirty and sad. Why is he dirty and sad?" Anna asks. "I don't know, Anna. Maybe he is hurt. Maybe he needs a bath. Do you want to help him?" Ben says. "Yes, Ben, I want to help him. Let's give him a bath. Maybe he will be happy then." Anna says. Anna and Ben go to the pond. They are careful not to scare the duck. They talk to him softly and gently. They splash some water on him. They rub his feathers with their hands. They wash away the dirt and the hurt. The duck quacks and flaps his wings. He looks clean and happy. "Look, Anna, the duck is clean and happy. He is not dirty and sad anymore. He likes us. We are his friends." Ben says. "Yes, Ben, the duck is clean and happy. We are his friends. We helped him. We gave him a bath. He likes us." Anna says. The duck swims to them and nuzzles their faces. He quacks and flaps his wings. He thanks them for their help. Anna and Ben hug him and smile. They are happy too. They love the duck. They love the animals. They love the nation.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty man. He lived in a little house in a far away land. One day, the man wanted to go for a walk, but he had nowhere to go. It was winter, and everything was cold and still. But one morning, spring came! The sunny sky brought warm sunshine and the cold ground felt soft and good. The man jumped up in joy, and decided to go for a walk. He stepped out of his house, and was surprised by how different everything was! All the green plants had grown and the birds were singing. Everywhere he looked, he saw beauty and life. The man smiled and enjoyed the spring. He took a deep breath and was happy with the fresh, clean air. He felt the warm sun touch his face and it felt so good. The man thanked the spring for his special walk.
