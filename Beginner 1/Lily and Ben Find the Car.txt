Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Car

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with cars and dolls. Lily has a pink car and Ben has a blue car. They have licenses to drive their cars. Licenses are cards with their names and pictures. One day, they go to the park with their cars. They see other kids playing with cars too. Some kids have big cars and some kids have small cars. Some kids have cars that make noises and some kids have cars that are quiet. Lily and Ben want to play with the other kids. They drive their cars to them and say hello. But the other kids are not nice. They say that Lily and Ben have baby cars. They say that their cars are better and more popular. They say that Lily and Ben's licenses are fake and they can't drive. Lily and Ben feel sad and angry. They don't like the other kids. They say that their cars are fun and their licenses are real. They say that they don't want to play with the other kids. They separate from them and drive away. They find a nice spot under a tree. They play with their cars and dolls. They make their own noises and stories. They are happy and have fun. They don't care about the other kids. They are friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his uncle. They saw a big tree with lots of leaves. Tim's uncle had a toy that could shoot soft balls. He asked Tim if he wanted to play with it. "Tim, do you want to shoot the toy with me?" Uncle asked. Tim said, "Yes, please!" They took turns shooting the soft balls at the tree. It was lots of fun. After a while, Tim's uncle said, "I need to go now, but the toy is available for you to play. Be nice and share with others." Tim played with the toy and made new friends at the park. They all had a great day.

Ben and Lily were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They used pots and pans and spoons and bowls. They also used some things from the cabinet, like salt and sugar and flour. "Look, Lily, I made a cake!" Ben said, holding up a plate with a messy pile of flour and sugar. "Do you want some?" Lily looked at the cake and made a face. She did not think it looked good. She was honest and did not want to lie. "No, thank you, Ben. I don't like your cake. It is too dry and sweet." Ben felt sad. He had worked hard on his cake. He wanted Lily to like it. He put the plate down and started to cry. "You are mean, Lily. You don't like anything I make. You are not my friend." Lily felt sorry. She did not want to hurt Ben's feelings. She was honest, but she was also kind. She went to Ben and hugged him. "I'm sorry, Ben. I don't mean to be mean. I like you. You are my friend." Ben stopped crying and hugged Lily back. He felt better. He smiled and said, "I like you too, Lily. You are my friend. Do you want to play something else?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, let's play something else. How about we play doctor and nurse? We can use the cabinet as a hospital." Ben liked that idea. He said, "OK, let's play doctor and nurse. You can be the doctor and I can be the nurse. We can help the sick toys." They took some toys from the cabinet and put them on the table. They pretended to check their temperature and give them medicine. They were gentle and caring. They made the toys feel better. When they were done, they kissed each toy and put them back in the cabinet. They were happy and proud. They had fun playing together. They kissed each other and said, "You are the best friend ever."

Sara and Ben were in the car with Mom and Dad. They were going to the park to play. Sara and Ben liked the park. They liked the swings and the slide and the sandbox. But the car stopped on the road. Mom looked at the dashboard and said, "Oh no, we have no fuel. We need to find a gas station." Dad said, "Don't worry, I see one over there. I'll push the car there. You stay here and relax." Sara and Ben did not want to relax. They wanted to go to the park. They were bored and hot in the car. They saw a big red button on the dashboard. It looked like a candy. Sara said, "I wonder what this does." Ben said, "Let's push it and see." They pushed the button. It was not a candy. It was a firework. The car made a loud boom and a bright flash. The car was on fire. Sara and Ben screamed. Mom and Dad ran back to the car. They tried to open the doors, but they were locked. They could not save Sara and Ben. Sara and Ben burned in the car. They never went to the park. The end.
