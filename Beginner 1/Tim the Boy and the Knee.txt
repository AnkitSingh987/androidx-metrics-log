Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Knee

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his ball. One day, while playing, he fell down and hurt his knee. It made him very sad. Tim's mom saw him crying and came to help. She said, "Oh no, Tim! Your knee is hurt. Let's learn how to make it feel good again." Tim's mom put a bandage on his knee and gave him a hug. Tim felt much better. The next day, Tim went to play with his friend, Sue. Sue asked, "What happened to your knee, Tim?" Tim said, "I fell down, but my mom taught me how to make it feel good again." Sue smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! Now we can play together and have fun!"

Lily and Ben were playing with a big ball in the park. They liked to kick the ball and run after it. Sometimes they would pass the ball to each other and say, "Catch!" But then a big dog came and took the ball in its mouth. It ran away with the ball and did not stop. Lily and Ben were sad and angry. They wanted their ball back. "Hey, that's our ball!" Ben shouted. He ran after the dog, but the dog was faster. Lily followed Ben, but she was scared of the dog. The dog ran to a tree and dropped the ball. It started to chew on the ball and make holes in it. Ben and Lily saw their ball and felt bad. They wanted to get their ball, but the dog was big and loud. "Go away, dog!" Lily said. She picked up a stick and threw it at the dog. The dog barked and ran after the stick. Ben saw his chance and ran to the ball. He grabbed the ball and ran back to Lily. But the ball had a knot in it. The dog had tied the ball with its teeth. The knot was hard and tight. Ben could not untie the knot. He tried to pull the knot, but it hurt his fingers. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben screamed. He dropped the ball and held his hand. Lily saw Ben and felt sorry for him. She ran to him and hugged him. "It's okay, Ben. It's just a ball. We can get a new one," Lily said. She was smart and kind. She knew how to make Ben feel better. The dog came back and saw the ball. It wanted the ball again. It ran to the ball and picked it up. It wagged its tail and looked at Ben and Lily. "Maybe the dog likes the ball more than us," Ben said. He was not angry anymore. He was curious and brave. He wanted to make friends with the dog. "Maybe we can play with the dog and the ball," Lily said. She was not scared anymore. She was happy and friendly. She wanted to have fun with the dog. Ben and Lily smiled at the dog. The dog smiled back. It dropped the ball and licked their faces. Ben and Lily laughed. They picked up the ball and the stick. They played with the dog and the ball. They had a good time in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big bag. He put all his toys in the bag. Tim loved to talk to his toys. He talked to them every day. One day, Tim felt fearful. He saw a big shadow on the wall. He thought it was a monster. Tim ran to his bag to get his toys. He wanted to talk to them. He thought they could help him. Tim took out his toys and started to talk. He told them about the big shadow. The toys told Tim not to be fearful. They said it was just the sun making the shadow. Tim looked again and saw that they were right. He was not fearful anymore. He was happy to have his toys to talk to.

Alice was three. She was walking outside in her neighborhood when she noticed a number of photostacked on the ground. She curiously picked them up and examined them. They had an interesting pattern on them. Alice went to show her mom. She said, "Mommy, look at the number of photos I found. It's so noisy!" Alice's mom looked at the pictures and said, "Alice, these photos are not allowed to be taken. We need to put them back." Alice sadly put them back where she found them. As Alice began to walk away, two policemen showed up. They asked Alice and her mom what was going on. Alice's mom explained what happened and the police took her away. Alice was so sad. She had wanted to keep the photos but now she had to say goodbye to her mom. Her mom was taken away and Alice never saw her again.
