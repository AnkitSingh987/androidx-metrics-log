Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Park

Once there was a little girl named Jane. She was only three years old. Her dad was taking her to the park one day. She was so excited! Suddenly, her dad pushed the pedal to the floor. The car was going so fast! Jane started to cry. She did not like the speed. Her dad pulled the car over to the side of the road and stopped. He hugged Jane and said everything was okay. He said they would drive slow now. Jane stopped crying and nodded her head. Her dad drove nice and quiet. They soon arrived at the park. Jane smiled and stepped out of the car. She ran to the playground and started to laugh. She had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He loved to play in the sand by the big warm sea. One day, he saw a big mussel by the shore. He wanted to get it, but it was too stuck to the sand. He had an idea and he started to dig and dig and soon he saw the mussel come loose. Jack was so happy, he held it in his hand and shouted: “I did it!” Suddenly, a big seal popped up from the sea and opened its mouth wide. It wanted to take the mussel away! Jack was so scared and he started to tear. The seal laughed and said: “No crying. Let’s be friends. I just want to play with you.” Jack was so surprised, he smiled and said: “Ok!” From that day onwards, Jack and the seal played together near the warm beach every day. They had lots of fun together.

One day, a big, red motorcycle went to the store. The motorcycle wanted to buy food to be healthy. At the store, the motorcycle saw many fruits and vegetables. The motorcycle met a friendly apple. The apple said, "Hi! Let's mix our foods together to make a healthy snack!" The motorcycle liked the idea and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They mixed apples, bananas, and carrots in a big bowl. The motorcycle and the apple ate the healthy snack together. They were very happy and felt strong. From that day on, the motorcycle and the apple were best friends.

Tom and Mia are friends who like to paint. They have many colors and brushes. They paint on paper, on walls, on rocks, and on anything they can find. They have fun making pictures of animals, flowers, cars, and stars. One day, they find a big box of dust in the garage. The dust is gray and soft. They think it is like sand, but finer. They wonder what they can do with it. "I know!" says Tom. "Let's paint with dust! We can make dust shapes on the floor and on the table. It will be like magic!" "OK!" says Mia. "But I bet I can make better dust shapes than you. I am very good at painting. I can make a dust cat, a dust house, a dust sun, and a dust cake!" Tom and Mia are competitive. They want to show each other who is the best painter. They start to paint with dust. They use their fingers, their hands, their feet, and their noses. They make dust shapes of many things. They laugh and giggle. But soon, they make a big mess. The dust is everywhere. It is on their clothes, on their hair, on their faces, and in their noses. They sneeze and cough. They look like dust monsters. "Uh-oh!" says Tom. "We made too much dust. Mom and dad will be angry. How can we clean this up?" "I don't know!" says Mia. "Maybe we can use water. Water can wash away dust. Let's get a bucket and a mop." Tom and Mia run to get water. They try to clean the dust. But they make more mess. The water makes the dust wet and sticky. It makes mud. The mud is brown and dirty. It stains the floor and the table. It drips and splashes. "Oh no!" says Tom. "We made more mess. Water is not good for dust. What can we do now?" "I don't know!" says Mia. "Maybe we can use a broom. A broom can sweep away dust. Let's get a broom and a dustpan." Tom and Mia run to get a broom. They try to sweep the dust. But they make more mess. The broom makes the dust fly and scatter. It makes clouds. The clouds are gray and fuzzy. They cover the air and the light. They make them cough and sneeze. "Help!" says Tom. "We made more mess. A broom is not good for dust. How can we stop this?" "I don't know!" says Mia. "Maybe we can use a vacuum. A vacuum can suck up dust. Let's get a vacuum and a plug." Tom and Mia run to get a vacuum. They try to vacuum the dust. But they make more noise. The vacuum makes a loud and scary sound. It makes them scream and cry. It frightens them and the cat. The cat runs and knocks over a lamp. The lamp breaks and sparks. "Stop!" says Tom. "We made more noise. A vacuum is not good for dust. How can we fix this?" "I don't know!" says Mia. "Maybe we can use a hug. A hug can make us feel better. Let's hug each other and say sorry." Tom and Mia hug each other. They say sorry. They are still messy and noisy. But they are also sorry and sad. They wish they had not painted with dust. They wish they had not been competitive. They wish they had been careful and kind. They hear a car in the driveway. They hear mom and dad coming home. They are scared and worried. What will mom and dad say? What will mom and dad do? They hope mom and dad will hug them too. They hope mom and dad will forgive them. They hope mom and dad will help them. They hope mom and dad will love them. They hope mom and dad will understand.
