Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and Dog Were and the Butter

One day, a cat and a dog were in the kitchen. The cat wanted to eat some butter. The dog said, "No, cat! That butter is for mom and dad." The cat did not listen and tried to open the tight jar. The dog tried to stop the cat. The cat and the dog pulled on the jar. The cat said, "I want the butter!" The dog said, "No, cat! We can't eat it." They pulled and pulled, but the jar was very tight. The cat and the dog were very mad at each other. Suddenly, the jar fell on the floor and it break. The butter was all over the floor. The cat and the dog looked at the mess. They knew they were in trouble. They decided to work together and clean up the mess before mom and dad came home.

Lily likes to play with her neighbor Sam. Sam is clever and has many toys. They play with cars, dolls, blocks and books. They have fun and laugh a lot. One day, Sam has a new toy. It is a big red truck with lights and sounds. Lily wants to play with it, but Sam says no. He says it is his toy and he does not want to share. Lily feels sad and angry. She does not want to play with Sam anymore. She goes to her yard and sees a big cardboard box. She has an idea. She takes the box and some crayons. She draws windows, doors, wheels and a horn on the box. She makes her own truck. She pretends to drive it around and honk the horn. She has fun and laughs a lot. Sam hears Lily and looks over the fence. He sees her truck and thinks it is cool. He wants to play with it, but Lily says no. She says it is her toy and she does not want to share. Sam feels sad and angry. He does not want to play with Lily anymore. He goes to his yard and looks at his truck. He thinks it is boring. He wishes he had a friend to play with. He thinks about Lily and how clever she is. He thinks he was mean to her. He feels sorry and wants to say sorry. He takes his truck and goes to Lily's yard. He knocks on her box and says, "Lily, can I play with you? I am sorry I did not share my truck. You are clever and have a cool truck. Can we be friends again?" Lily hears Sam and opens her box. She sees him and his truck. She smiles and says, "Sam, I am sorry I did not share my box. You are clever and have a nice truck. Can we be friends again?" They hug and say sorry. They decide to share their toys and play together. They have fun and laugh a lot. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little bird named Bob. Bob loved to fly high in the sky and play with the clouds. One day, as Bob was flying, he saw a big cloud with a sad face. Bob flew closer to the cloud and asked, "Why are you sad, Mr. Cloud?" The cloud looked down at Bob and said, "I am sad because I can't raise high in the sky like you, Bob. I am stuck here, and I can't move." Bob wanted to help the cloud, so he thought of a plan. He called all his bird friends and asked them to help him push the cloud up. The birds gathered around the cloud and started to push with all their strength. Suddenly, the cloud began to move! It went up and up, and the cloud became very happy. The cloud said, "Thank you, Bob and friends! Now I can be high in the sky like you!" From that day on, the brave little bird and the cloud became best friends, and they played together in the sky every day. The cloud was never sad again, all thanks to Bob's kind heart and bravery.

Sam was eager to test his new glove. He got it for his birthday from his dad. It was red and shiny and had a big star on it. Sam wanted to play catch with his dad and show him how good he was. He ran outside with his glove and his ball. He saw his dad waiting for him in the yard. He smiled and waved. His dad smiled and waved back. He threw the ball to Sam. Sam caught it with his glove. He felt happy and proud. He threw the ball back to his dad. His dad caught it and threw it again. They did this many times. Sam was having fun. He felt his glove getting warmer and tighter. He thought it was because he was playing so well. But then he felt a sharp pain in his hand. He looked at his glove and saw smoke. His glove was on fire. He screamed and dropped the ball. His dad ran to him and took off his glove. He saw Sam's hand was burned and blistered. He hugged Sam and said he was sorry. He said he did not know the glove was bad. He said he got it from a man who said it was special. He said he wanted to make Sam happy. He said he would take Sam to the doctor and get him a new glove. Sam cried and said he did not want a new glove. He said he wanted his hand to be okay. He said he hated the glove and the man who sold it. He said he wished he never tested it.
