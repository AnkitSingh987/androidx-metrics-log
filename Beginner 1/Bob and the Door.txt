Category: Beginner
Title: Bob and the Door

Bob was a little boy who loved to explore. One day, he spotted a closet in his living room. He liked the closet because it was kind of like a secret. It had a big door that was separate from the rest of the room. At first, Bob thought the closet would be exciting but when he opened the door he found it was very boring. There was nothing special inside except some old blankets. But then, Bob spotted something different. It was a big red box. He pulled it out of the closet and opened it. Inside was a big, fun toy car. He loved playing with the car and raced it around the room. Bob had so much fun that he forgot all about the boring closet. He never knew what other surprises might be hiding inside the closet.

---

Tom and Lily were playing with their blocks in the living room. They liked to build towers and castles and bridges. They had a lot of fun together. But then, Mom came and said, "Kids, it's time to get ready for bed. Put away your blocks and brush your teeth." Tom and Lily did not want to stop playing. They said, "No, Mom, we want to play more. Please, Mom, just five more minutes." Mom said, "No, you have to go to bed now. It's late and you have school tomorrow. Come on, don't delay." Tom and Lily were not happy. They started to whine and cry. They threw their blocks on the floor. They said, "No, no, no!" Mom was not happy either. She said, "That's enough. You are being naughty and rude. You have to listen to me. I'm your mom." She picked up Tom and carried him to the bathroom. Tom kicked and screamed. He tried to get away. He hit Mom's arm with his hand. Mom said, "Ow! That hurt. Why did you do that? You should never hit anyone. That's very bad." Tom felt embarrassed. He knew he did something wrong. He said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I didn't mean to hurt you. I was just angry." Mom said, "I know, but you have to control your anger. You have to use your words, not your hands. Hitting is not nice. It makes people sad and angry." Tom said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I love you. Can you forgive me?" Mom said, "Of course, I love you too. And I forgive you. But you still have to go to bed. And you have to say sorry to Lily too. She was waiting for you." Tom said, "Okay, Mom. I will say sorry to Lily. And I will go to bed. And I will not hit anyone again." Mom said, "That's good. I'm proud of you. Now, let's go and brush your teeth."

Sam and Ben are brothers. They like to play with trains. They have many trains and rails. They make a big loop of rails on the floor. They put their trains on the rails. They push the trains and watch them go. "Choo choo!" Sam says. He likes to make train sounds. "Choo choo!" Ben repeats. He likes to copy his brother. They have fun with their trains. They make them go faster and slower. They make them stop and go. They make them crash and fix them. But then, Mom comes in. She is angry. She sees the big mess on the floor. She sees the trains and rails everywhere. "Boys, what are you doing?" Mom asks. She is not happy. "We are playing with trains, Mom!" Sam says. "We are playing with trains, Mom!" Ben repeats. Mom shakes her head. She does not like the noise and the mess. She tells the boys to clean up. She tells them to put the trains and rails away. She tells them to be quiet. "Clean up, boys!" Mom says. "Clean up, boys!" Ben repeats. Sam does not like to clean up. He likes to play. He does not listen to Mom. He pushes his train harder. He makes it go faster. He makes it go off the rail. He makes it fly in the air. The train hits Mom in the face. Mom screams. She is hurt. She is very angry. She grabs the train from the floor. She throws it in the trash. She tells the boys to go to their room. She tells them they are in trouble. "Go to your room, boys!" Mom says. "Go to your room, boys!" Ben repeats. Sam and Ben are sad. They cry. They lose their train. They lose their fun. They have a bad ending.
