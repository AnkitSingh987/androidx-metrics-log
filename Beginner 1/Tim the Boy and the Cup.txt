Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very thirsty. He went to the kitchen to find something to drink. He saw a big cup on the table. Tim was happy. Tim picked up the cup and took a sip. "Oh no!" he said. "This drink is terrible!" He made a funny face. Tim didn't like the taste of the drink. He wanted to get rid of it. Tim saw a drain near the sink. He poured the terrible drink into the drain. All the bad taste went away. Tim's mom came into the kitchen. She asked, "What happened, Tim?" Tim told her about the terrible drink. They both laughed and found a better drink for Tim to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a name. Its name was "Happy Boat." Happy Boat had a big, thick anchor. The anchor helped the boat stay in one place. One day, the boat saw a little fish. The fish did not have a name. The boat wanted to help the fish. So, the boat named the fish "Tiny." Tiny the fish was very happy to have a name. Now, Tiny and Happy Boat were friends. They played in the water every day. The thick anchor held the boat, and Tiny swam around it. They were happy together. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom are best friends. They like to play in the park with their toys. One day, they find a big ball in the grass. It is red and shiny and bouncy. They want to play with it. "Can I have a turn?" Lily asks Tom. "Okay, but only for a little bit. Then you have to give it back to me. Promise?" Tom says. "I promise," Lily says. She takes the ball and runs with it. She kicks it with her foot. The ball flies high in the air. Lily laughs. But then, a big dog comes. It sees the ball and runs after it. It catches the ball in its mouth. It runs away with it. "Hey, that's our ball!" Tom shouts. He runs after the dog. Lily runs after Tom. They chase the dog across the park. They are fast, but the dog is faster. The dog goes out of the park and into the street. Tom and Lily stop. They are scared. There are many cars and trucks and buses in the street. They are loud and fast and big. "Tom, we can't go there. It's too dangerous," Lily says. "But we have to get our ball back. It's mine. You promised to give it back to me," Tom says. "I'm sorry, Tom. I didn't know the dog would take it. Please don't be mad at me," Lily says. Tom looks at Lily. He sees that she is sad and scared. He feels sorry for her. He remembers that she is his best friend. "It's okay, Lily. I'm not mad at you. You didn't do anything wrong. The dog did. Maybe we can find another ball to play with. Or we can play something else. As long as we play together," Tom says. He hugs Lily. Lily hugs him back. They feel better. "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend. I'm sorry I broke my promise. Can we still be friends?" Lily says. "Of course we can. You are my best friend. I promise," Tom says. They hold hands and walk back to the park. They are happy. They are friends. They keep their promises.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. He had an amazing toy figure named Sam. Tim and Sam loved to play together every day. One sunny day, Tim took Sam outside to play. They found a big red balloon. Tim tied Sam to the balloon and said, "Sam, I will release you now. Up you go!" Tim let go, and the balloon lifted Sam high into the sky. Sam looked down and saw Tim waving. Sam said, "Wow! I can see our house!" As the wind blew, Sam and the balloon floated back down to the ground. Tim caught Sam and said, "That was fun! Let's play again!" And they played together all day long.
