Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam Find the Ball

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a whistle. They take turns to kick the ball and blow the whistle. One day, a big boy comes to the park. He sees Tom and Sam's ball and whistle. He wants them. He runs to Tom and Sam and grabs the ball and the whistle. He says, "These are mine now. Go away!" Tom and Sam are sad and angry. They want their ball and whistle back. They say, "No, they are ours. Give them back!" The big boy laughs. He says, "No, they are not. They are cool. I like them. You are small and silly. You can't have them." Tom and Sam don't know what to do. They are scared of the big boy. They sit on the bench and cry. A lady sees them. She is the big boy's mom. She says, "What's wrong, boys? Why are you crying?" Tom and Sam tell her what happened. They point to the big boy. He is still playing with the ball and the whistle. The lady is angry. She says, "That's not nice. That's not how you share. Come here, now!" She goes to the big boy. She takes the ball and the whistle from him. She says, "You are in trouble. You can't take things that are not yours. You have to say sorry and give them back." The big boy is sorry. He says, "I'm sorry, Tom and Sam. I was wrong. Here are your ball and whistle. Can we be friends?" Tom and Sam are happy. They say, "Thank you, lady. Thank you, big boy. Yes, we can be friends." They all play together. They kick the ball and blow the whistle. They have fun. They are cool.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a tough dog who liked to play outside. One day, Max saw a hose in the yard. Max went to the hose and started to play with it. He bit the hose and it began to tremble. The water came out and Max was very happy. Max played with the hose all day long. He was wet but he had so much fun. In the end, Max was tired and went inside to take a nap.

Lily loved to look at tiny things with her microscope. She had a book with pictures of bugs, plants and crystals that she could find in her garden or her room. She liked to put them on a slide and see how they looked under the microscope. She learned their names and shapes and colors. One day, she wanted to show her microscope to her friend Max, who came to play with her. She took him to her room and showed him her book and her slides. She said, "Look, Max, this is a microscope. It makes things bigger. Do you want to see?" Max did not look interested. He said, "That's boring. I don't like tiny things. I like big things, like cars and trucks and dinosaurs. Let's play with those instead." He grabbed a toy car from Lily's shelf and ran to the living room. Lily felt miserable. She did not understand why Max did not like her microscope. She thought it was the most amazing thing in the world. She stared at her microscope and felt sad. She did not want to play with cars and trucks and dinosaurs. She wanted to share her passion with her friend. She decided to follow Max to the living room and try to talk to him. She said, "Max, why don't you like my microscope? It's fun and cool and you can learn a lot from it. You can see things that you can't see with your eyes. Don't you want to try?" Max looked at her and shrugged. He said, "I don't know. I just don't. I think it's silly and hard and boring. I like things that are easy and fun and exciting. Like cars and trucks and dinosaurs. They make noise and move and have teeth. They are awesome." Lily felt hurt. She said, "But my microscope is awesome too. It shows me a whole new world. A world that is beautiful and amazing and full of wonders. A world that you can't see without it. Don't you want to see that world?" Max thought for a moment. He saw that Lily was sincere and that she really loved her microscope. He felt sorry for being rude and mean to her. He said, "Okay, Lily. I'm sorry. I was wrong. Maybe I was afraid of trying something new. Maybe I was missing out on something cool. Maybe I can learn something from you. Can you show me your microscope again?" Lily smiled. She said, "Sure, Max. I'm glad you changed your mind. Come on, let's go to my room. I have a slide with a dinosaur bone. It's awesome." They went to Lily's room and looked at the dinosaur bone under the microscope. They saw the tiny holes and lines and patterns that made up the bone. They compared it to the picture in the book and learned how it belonged to a big and fierce dinosaur. They were amazed and curious and happy. They learned that day that different things can be awesome in different ways. They learned that they can share their interests and hobbies with each other and discover new things. They learned that they can be friends even if they like different things. They learned that they can stare at the world with wonder and respect.
