Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Says

Lily and Tom are playing in the park. They like to run, jump and slide. They see a big sign with many colors and pictures. They want to know what it says. "Look, Tom, a sign!" Lily says. "What does it say?" "I don't know, Lily. It has many words. Maybe it is a story," Tom says. They go closer to the sign. They see a picture of a lion, a monkey and a bird. They see a word that they know: zoo. "Zoo! It says zoo, Lily!" Tom says. "Do you know what a zoo is?" Lily shakes her head. She does not know what a zoo is. "A zoo is a place where you can see many animals. They live in cages or pens. They are amazing," Tom says. "My mom took me to the zoo once. I saw a giraffe, an elephant and a snake." "Wow, that sounds amazing, Tom. I want to see a zoo too," Lily says. They look at the sign again. They see another word that they know: bus. "Bus! It says bus, Tom!" Lily says. "Do you know what a bus is?" Tom nods. He knows what a bus is. "A bus is a big car that can take many people. It has a driver and a door. It stops at places where you can get on or off. It is fun," Tom says. "My dad took me on a bus once. We went to the library and the museum." "Wow, that sounds fun, Tom. I want to go on a bus too," Lily says. They look at the sign again. They see a number that they know: ten. "Ten! It says ten, Lily!" Tom says. "Do you know what ten is?" Lily nods. She knows what ten is. "Ten is a big number. It is more than nine and less than eleven. It is easy," Lily says. "I can count to ten. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten." "Good job, Lily. You are smart," Tom says. They look at the sign again. They see a word that they do not know: trip. "Trip! It says trip, Tom!" Lily says. "What is a trip?" Tom shrugs. He does not know what a trip is. "Maybe it is something that you do with a zoo and a bus and ten," Tom says. They wonder what a trip is. They want to find out. They see a lady near the sign. She has a hat and a badge. She smiles at them. "Hello, kids. Do you like the sign?" she says. "Yes, we do. It is amazing. But we don't know what a trip is. Can you tell us?" Lily says. "Sure, I can. A trip is an adventure. It is when you go to a new place and see new things. It is fun and exciting," the lady says. "Wow, that sounds amazing. We want to go on a trip. How can we go?" Tom says. "Well, you can go on a trip with me. I am a teacher. I have a class of ten kids. We are going to the zoo on a bus. We have some extra seats. Do you want to join us?" the lady says. Lily and Tom look at each other. They are happy and curious. They want to go on a trip. They nod and smile. "Yes, we do. Thank you, lady. You are nice," Lily says. "You are welcome, kids. You are brave. Come on, let's go. The bus is waiting. It is time for our trip," the lady says. She takes their hands and leads them to the bus. They get on the bus and sit with the other kids. They wave goodbye to the sign. They are ready for their trip. They are ready for their adventure. They are ready to see amazing things.

Once upon a time, there was a new, little cat. The cat was very quiet. She liked to lie down in the sun. One day, she found a big, red ball. The cat was very happy. She played with the red ball all day. She rolled it and jumped on it. The sun was warm, and the cat was tired. So, the cat lied down under a tree. She was quiet, and she slept. When the cat woke up, she saw a new friend. It was a small dog. The dog was also quiet. They played with the red ball together. The cat and the dog were happy. They lied down under the tree and slept.
