Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Park

Lila and Ben were twins who liked to wander in the park. They saw many things: trees, flowers, birds, dogs, and people. They liked to play with their ball, swing on the swings, and slide on the slide. They also liked to talk to each other and make up stories. One day, it was very hot in the park. The sun was bright and the air was warm. Lila and Ben felt thirsty and tired. They looked for a place to rest and drink some water. They saw a big fountain in the middle of the park. It had water that sprayed up and down. It looked cool and fun. "Let's go to the fountain!" Lila said to Ben. "OK!" Ben said. They ran to the fountain and put their hands in the water. It felt nice and cold. They splashed each other and laughed. They drank some water from their bottles. They felt better. They saw a bench near the fountain. It had a shade from a tree. It looked peaceful and cozy. "Let's sit on the bench!" Ben said to Lila. "OK!" Lila said. They walked to the bench and sat down. They leaned on each other and closed their eyes. They listened to the sound of the water and the birds. They felt happy and calm. They fell asleep on the bench. They had a nice dream. They dreamed of wandering in the park again.

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to play with cars and dolls. One day, they find a big balloon in the park. It is red and shiny. They want to take it home. "Let's blow the balloon bigger!" Tim says. He puts his mouth on the balloon and blows. The balloon gets bigger and bigger. "No, stop!" Kim says. She is scared. "The balloon will pop! It has too much gas inside!" Tim does not listen. He likes to blow the balloon. He urges Kim to blow with him. "It will be fun!" he says. He blows harder and harder. Kim does not want to blow. She pulls the balloon away from Tim. "It is mine!" she says. She hugs the balloon. Tim is angry. He wants the balloon too. He tries to grab the balloon from Kim. They tug and pull. The balloon makes a loud noise. It pops! Tim and Kim are sad. They cry. The balloon is gone. They have no gas to play with. Mom comes to the park. She sees Tim and Kim crying. She asks them what is wrong. They tell her about the balloon. Mom hugs them. She says, "I'm sorry, my loves. Balloons are not for blowing too big. They are for holding and looking at. They are pretty, but they are not strong." She takes them to the ice cream truck. She buys them ice cream cones. They are yummy. Tim and Kim lick their ice cream. They smile. They say, "Thank you, Mom. We love you." Mom smiles too. She says, "I love you too, my sweeties. Next time, be careful with balloons. And share your toys. Sharing is caring."

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to draw pictures with crayons. One day, they find a big book with many pictures of animals. They want to draw the same animal, a lion. "I want the yellow crayon!" Anna says. "No, I want it!" Ben says. They both grab the crayon and pull. They fight over the crayon. The crayon breaks in half. They drop the pieces and look at the book. The book has a big tear on the lion page. They feel guilty. "Mommy will be mad at us," Anna says. "Maybe we can fix it," Ben says. They try to tape the book and the crayon. But it does not look good. They hear Mommy coming. They hide the book and the crayon under the sofa. "Hi, kids. What are you doing?" Mommy asks. "Nothing," Anna and Ben say. Mommy sees their guilty faces. She looks under the sofa. She sees the book and the crayon. "Did you do this?" Mommy asks. Anna and Ben nod. They are sorry. Mommy is not happy. She tells them to say sorry to each other and to the book. She tells them to share the crayons and to be careful with the books. She gives them a hug. "Next time, you can draw different animals," Mommy says. "Okay, Mommy," Anna and Ben say. They draw a zebra and a giraffe. They do not fight. They are happy.
