Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Car

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. Anna has a big box of toys. She has dolls, cars, blocks, and books. Ben has a small bag of toys. He has a ball, a train, a bear, and a puzzle. One day, Anna and Ben decide to play with cars. Anna has many cars. She has red cars, blue cars, green cars, and yellow cars. She has big cars, small cars, fast cars, and slow cars. Ben has one car. He has a brown car. It is old and broken. Anna says, "Ben, do you want to play with my cars? I have many cars. You can choose any car you like." Ben says, "Thank you, Anna. But I like my car. It is my favorite car. It is quiet and cozy. It is like my dad's car. He has an automobile. It is also brown and quiet. He takes me to the park in his automobile." Anna says, "But your car is old and broken. It has a dent and a scratch. It does not make any sound. It does not go fast. It is not fun. I recommend you to try my cars. They are new and shiny. They make loud noises. They go very fast. They are very fun." Ben says, "No, thank you, Anna. I do not like loud noises. They hurt my ears. I do not like fast cars. They make me dizzy. I like quiet and cozy cars. They make me happy. I do not want to try your cars. I want to play with my car." Anna and Ben do not agree. They do not play with cars. They play with other toys. Anna plays with dolls. Ben plays with puzzles. They are still friends. They like different things. And that is okay.

One day, a kid named Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sue. They wanted to play a game. Tim said, "Let's start a race!" Sue said, "Yes, let's do it!" They ran and ran. Tim was fast, but Sue was faster. Suddenly, Tim tripped on a rock and fell. Sue stopped and helped Tim up. Tim was sad. Sue said, "I will be honest, you are good too." Just then, a big wind came and blew their hats away. Both kids laughed and ran to catch their hats. They forgot about the race and became best friends.

Lily and Sam were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a lot of snow on the ground. They put on their warm clothes and ran outside. "Let's make a snowman!" Lily said. She rolled a big ball of snow and put it on the grass. "OK!" Sam said. He rolled a smaller ball of snow and put it on top of the big ball. Then he found a carrot, two rocks and a hat for the snowman's face and head. "Look, our snowman is done!" Lily said. She smiled and hugged Sam. They saw a boy walking by. He had a red scarf and a blue coat. He looked shy and sad. "Hi, what's your name?" Lily asked. She waved at the boy. The boy stopped and looked at them. He said in a low voice, "I'm Ben. I'm new here. I don't have any friends." Lily and Sam felt sorry for Ben. They wanted to be his friends. "You can play with us!" Sam said. He encouraged Ben to come closer. "We made a snowman. Do you like snow?" Ben nodded. He liked snow, but he was afraid to join them. He thought they might not like him. "Don't be shy, Ben. We are nice and friendly. We can make another snowman, or have a snowball fight, or make snow angels. What do you want to do?" Lily asked. She held out her hand to Ben. Ben looked at Lily's hand, then at her smile, then at Sam's smile. He felt a warm feeling in his chest. He took Lily's hand and said, "I want to play with you. Thank you for inviting me." Lily and Sam cheered. They welcomed Ben to their snow play. They had a lot of fun together. Ben felt happy and not shy anymore. He made two new friends.
