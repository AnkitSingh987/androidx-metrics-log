Category: Beginner
Title: The Farm and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a mighty cow named Daisy. Daisy liked to play with her friends, the little chicks. One day, Daisy was playing hide and seek with the chicks. She hid behind a big tree, and the chicks started to look for her. They looked under the bushes, and behind the fence, but they could not find Daisy. Daisy decided to peek out from behind the tree. She saw the chicks looking very sad. "Here I am!" she called out to them. The chicks were so happy to find Daisy. They all laughed and played together for the rest of the day.

---

One day, a bald man named Tom went for a walk. He wore a big hat on his head. The hat was red and blue. Tom liked his hat very much. It was a sunny day and the birds were singing. As Tom walked, he saw a cat. The cat was sitting on a rock. Tom said, "Hello, cat! Do you like my hat?" The cat looked at the hat and nodded. Suddenly, the cat sneezed. "Achoo!" The sneeze was so big that it blew the hat off Tom's head! Tom and the cat were very surprised. They looked up and saw the hat flying away. The hat landed on a tree branch. Tom and the cat laughed. Tom said, "Thank you, cat, for making me laugh." The cat smiled and they became good friends. From that day on, Tom and the cat went on walks together, and they always had fun.

---

One day, a little crab went for a walk on the hot sand. The sun was shining, and the crab felt happy. He saw a big book lying on the sand. The crab wanted to read the book, but he didn't know how. A little girl named Sue was playing nearby. She saw the crab looking at the book. Sue walked over to the crab and asked, "Do you want to read the book?" The crab nodded his head. Sue sat down on the hot sand and opened the book. She read the book out loud to the crab. They both laughed at the funny parts and listened closely to the exciting parts. When Sue finished reading, the crab clapped his claws. They both had a great time. Sue and the crab became good friends, and they promised to read more books together on the hot sand.

Tom and Lily were hungry. They wanted a snack. Mom said they could have some celery and cheese. She gave them a plate and a knife. "Be careful with the knife. It is sharp. It can cut you," Mom said. Tom and Lily nodded. They took the knife and the celery. They cut the celery into small pieces. They put cheese on top. They ate their snack. But Tom wanted more cheese. He saw a big piece of cheese on the counter. He reached for it. He did not see the other knife. The other knife was sharp too. It cut his finger. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Tom cried. He dropped the cheese and the knife. He held his finger. It hurt. It bled. Mom heard Tom. She ran to him. She saw his finger. She took a cloth and wrapped it around his finger. "Tom, what did you do? You were not careful. You cut yourself with the knife. You have to listen to me," Mom said. She was grumpy. She was worried. "I'm sorry, Mom. I wanted more cheese. I did not see the knife. It was sharp. It cut me," Tom said. He was sorry. He was sad. Lily saw Tom and Mom. She felt sorry for Tom. She had some celery and cheese left. She gave it to Tom. "Here, Tom. You can have my celery and cheese. It will make you feel better. I'm sorry you cut your finger. It will heal soon," Lily said. She was kind. She was sweet. Tom smiled. He took the celery and cheese. He thanked Lily. He ate the snack. It made him feel better. He hugged Lily. "Thank you, Lily. You are a good sister. I'm sorry I was greedy. I will share with you next time. I love you," Tom said. He was happy. He was grateful. Mom smiled too. She saw Tom and Lily hug. She was proud of them. She kissed them both. "I love you too, Tom and Lily. You are good children. I'm sorry I was grumpy. I was scared. I'm glad you are okay. Now let's clean up and wash your finger," Mom said. She was calm. She was loving. Tom and Lily nodded. They helped Mom clean up. They washed Tom's finger. They put a band-aid on it. They hugged Mom again. They were a happy family. They liked celery and cheese. They learned to be careful with sharp knives. They learned to listen to Mom. They learned to share and care. The end.
