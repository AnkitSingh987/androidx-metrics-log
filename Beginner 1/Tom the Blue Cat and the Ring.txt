Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Blue Cat and the Ring

One day, a blue cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max had a toy that could ring. Tom felt envious because he wanted a toy like that too. Tom said to Max, "Can I play with your toy?" Max smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with it." They played together and had lots of fun. The toy made a loud ring sound when they shook it. After they played, Tom was not envious anymore. He was happy that he had a friend like Max who shared his toy. They went home and told their mommies about their fun day.

The sun was shining brightly, and two little birds were perched on a windowsill. They were looking at the snow-white glass that sparkled in the sunlight. "That looks so pretty," said the smaller bird. "It looks like diamonds are inside!" "It's not real," said the bigger bird. "It's just a fake." The smaller bird blinked in surprise. "What do you mean?" " It's not a real diamond, it's just a glass," the bigger bird explained. "You can find some fake ones in the store, but real ones are much more valuable." The little bird blinked again and said, "I don't understand." The bigger bird smiled. "That's alright. Don't worry about it now. Let's find some food, shall we?" And with that, the two birds flew off into the sky.

One day, a little boy named Tim went on an adventurous trip with his mom. They took a big bus to the park. They had tickets to see a fun show with animals and clowns. Tim was very excited and couldn't wait to get there. When they got to the park, Tim's mom said, "Listen, Tim. We must find our seats before the show starts." They walked and walked, looking for their seats. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew their tickets away! Tim and his mom were sad, but they did not give up. They looked around and saw a kind man who worked at the park. They told him what happened, and he smiled. "Don't worry," he said. "I have extra tickets for you!" Tim and his mom were so happy, and they went to enjoy the show.

Sam and Tom are brothers. They like to watch TV in the living room. But there is a problem. The cable is broken. The TV does not work. "Mom, the TV is broken!" Sam says. "Can you fix it?" Mom comes to the living room. She sees the cable on the floor. It is smelly. It has a big hole. A mouse has bitten it. "Oh no, the cable is smelly and broken!" Mom says. "We need a new cable. But the store is closed today. We have to wait until tomorrow." Sam and Tom are sad. They want to watch TV. They do not know what to do. "Can we play a game?" Tom asks. "Sure, we can play a game," Mom says. "How about we play Simon says?" Sam and Tom nod. They like to play Simon says. Mom is Simon. She tells them what to do. "Simon says, sit on the couch," Mom says. Sam and Tom sit on the couch. "Simon says, touch your nose," Mom says. Sam and Tom touch their nose. "Simon says, stand up," Mom says. Sam and Tom stand up. "Jump," Mom says. Sam and Tom do not jump. They know Mom did not say Simon says. "Good job, you are very smart!" Mom says. "You did not jump. You win!" Sam and Tom smile. They are happy. They forget about the TV. They have fun with Mom. They play Simon says until dinner time.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play. He climbed up and slid down the slide. It was so much fun! Then, Tim saw a girl named Sue holding a big balloon. "Hi Sue, can I play with your balloon?" he asked. Sue said, "Yes, but be careful. My mommy gave it to me for my birthday." They played with the balloon and had a great time. Later, they got hungry and went to Tim's house for a snack. Tim's mommy gave them spicy chips. Sue didn't like spicy food, but she didn't want to be rude. She tried the chips and said, "Thank you, but I can't eat spicy food." Tim's mommy understood and gave her a different snack. The moral of the story is to be honest and kind to others. If you don't like something, it's okay to say so politely.
