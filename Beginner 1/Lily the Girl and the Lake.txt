Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Lake

One day, a girl named Lily went to the lake. She saw an amazing kayak. The kayak was big and red. Lily wanted to touch the kayak. Lily walked to the kayak and touched it. It felt smooth and cool. She was very happy. Lily wanted to play with the kayak in the water. Lily asked her mom if she could play with the kayak. Her mom said yes. Lily and her mom went into the water with the kayak. They had lots of fun in the water. The amazing kayak made the day great.

---

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play together. Today, they are in the park. They have a ball, a kite and a bike. "Let's play with the ball first," Lily says. "I can kick it very far." "OK," Ben says. "But I can catch it very fast." They run and kick and catch the ball. They have fun. They laugh and shout. After a while, they are hungry. They sit on a bench. They have lunch. They have sandwiches, apples and juice. "Yum, this is good," Ben says. "Do you want some of my juice?" "Thank you, Ben," Lily says. "Do you want some of my apple?" They share their food. They are happy. Then, they see a duck. The duck is on the pond. The duck is silly. It quacks and splashes and chases the fish. "Look at the duck," Lily says. "It is so funny." "Let's go and see it," Ben says. "Maybe it wants some bread." They take some bread from their lunch. They go to the pond. They throw some bread to the duck. The duck eats the bread. It quacks and wags its tail. "Hello, duck," Lily says. "You are very cute." "Quack, quack," the duck says. "You are very nice." Lily and Ben smile. They like the duck. They watch it for a long time. They have a good day.

---

One day, a lively cat and a dog played in the park. The cat said, "Let's play hide and look!" The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, let's play!" The cat ran and hid behind a big tree. The dog closed its eyes and counted to ten. Then, the dog opened its eyes and started to look for the cat. The dog looked behind a small bush, but the cat was not there. Next, the dog looked behind a bench, but the cat was not there either. Finally, the dog found the cat behind the big tree. The cat jumped out and said, "You found me! Now it's your turn to hide!" They both laughed and played hide and look all day long.

---

Ben and Lily liked to play guess games. They would take turns hiding something and the other one had to guess what it was. One day, Ben had a very good idea. He found a big, cold box in the kitchen. It was the freezer. He opened it and saw many things inside. He saw ice, milk, meat, peas, and a cake. He took the cake and hid it behind his back. Then he closed the freezer and called Lily. "Lily, Lily, come and play! I have something for you to guess!" he said. Lily ran to the kitchen and saw Ben smiling. She wondered what he had. "Is it big or small?" she asked. "It is big," Ben said. "Is it soft or hard?" she asked. "It is soft," Ben said. "Is it red or blue?" she asked. "It is not red or blue. It has many colors," Ben said. Lily thought hard. She looked around the kitchen and saw the freezer. She had an idea. "Is it from the freezer?" she asked. Ben was surprised. He did not think Lily would guess that. "Yes, it is from the freezer," he said. "Is it the cake?" she asked. Ben was amazed. He did not know how Lily knew that. He showed her the cake behind his back. "Yes, it is the cake! How did you guess?" he said. Lily smiled. She had a secret. "I saw the cake when Mom put it in the freezer this morning. She said it was for Dad's birthday. It looks incredible!" she said. Ben and Lily looked at the cake. It had chocolate, cream, and sprinkles. It looked yummy. "Can we have some?" Ben asked. Lily shook her head. "No, we can't. It is for Dad. We have to put it back in the freezer. But we can have some ice cream instead. Mom said we could," she said. Ben and Lily agreed. They put the cake back in the freezer and took out some ice cream. They ate it with spoons and laughed. They liked to play guess games. They liked to eat ice cream. They liked to be happy.
