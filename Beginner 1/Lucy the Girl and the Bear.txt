Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had an amazing toy named Mr. Bear. Lucy and Mr. Bear were best friends. They played together all day. One day, Lucy and Mr. Bear played hide and seek. Mr. Bear hid under the bed. Lucy looked and looked, but she could not find him. She felt sad and said, "Mr. Bear, where are you?" Mr. Bear heard Lucy and came out from under the bed. Lucy was so happy to see him! She hugged Mr. Bear and said, "Promise me you won't hide too well next time." Mr. Bear promised and they continued to play happily.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy who loved to play. One day, Tim's mom gave him a snack. It was a long green thing called celery. Tim did not like the celery. It was not an easy snack to eat. He wanted to destroy it. So, he stomped on it with his foot. The celery broke into many pieces. Tim's mom saw what he did and was sad. She told him that he should not destroy food. Tim felt bad and said sorry. He promised to try new snacks and not destroy them. From that day on, Tim learned to eat new things, even if they were not easy to eat.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a plan. They want to make a big sand castle. They bring their buckets and shovels. They find a good spot near the swings. They start to dig and pile up the sand. They make the walls and the towers. They use shells and stones to decorate their castle. They are very proud of their work. "Look at our castle!" Lily says. "It is so big and pretty!" "Yes, it is!" Ben agrees. "It has a lot of value. It is the best castle in the park!" They smile and hug each other. They are glad they are friends. They are glad they have a plan. But then, a big dog comes running. It sees the castle and thinks it is a toy. It jumps on it and knocks it down. It makes a big mess. Lily and Ben are sad. They see their castle ruined. They cry and scream. "Bad dog! Bad dog!" Lily shouts. "You broke our castle!" "Go away! Go away!" Ben yells. "You have no value!" The dog's owner hears the noise. He comes and sees what happened. He is sorry. He apologizes to Lily and Ben. He scolds his dog and puts him on a leash. "I'm sorry, kids. My dog didn't know. He was just playing. He didn't mean to hurt your castle." Lily and Ben stop crying. They look at the owner and the dog. They are still sad, but they are not angry. "It's okay, mister. We know he didn't know. He was just a dog." Lily says. "Yeah, it's okay. We can make another castle. We still have our plan." Ben says. They wipe their tears and smile. They are glad they are friends. They are glad they have a plan. They start to dig and pile up the sand again. They make a new castle. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Max. Max liked to help his friends. One day, Max saw his friend, a small bird named Lily. Lily was sad because her nest was broken. Max wanted to help Lily. He found a tool, a small stick. Max used the stick to fix Lily's nest. It was hard work, but Max was happy to help his friend. The nest was fixed and Lily was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The stick Max used was magic! The stick turned the nest into a big, soft bed. Max and Lily were surprised but very happy. Now, they could both sleep in the large, soft bed together.

Once upon a time, there was a tired little boy who loved to play. He was always running around and never wanted to sleep. One night, his mommy put him to bed but he couldn't fall asleep. His mommy told him to take a nap, but he still wouldn't listen. He got out of bed and tried to run away. He went towards the door, but it was locked. The little boy was angry and he didn't know what to do. Then he noticed a buckle on the door - the kind used to lock the door. He tried to open it, but it didn't work. He kept pulling and pulling it, until SNAP! The buckle broke. He opened the door and ran out, but then he felt very tired. He wanted his mommy, but it was too late. He sat down and fell asleep on the doorstep. The little boy slept until morning, when his mommy found him. He was so tired, she decided to let him sleep and not scold him this time. In the end, the little boy didn't get the rest he needed.
