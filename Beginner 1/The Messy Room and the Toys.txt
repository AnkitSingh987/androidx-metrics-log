Category: Beginner
Title: The Messy Room and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. In the room, there was a big box of toys and a magazine. A little boy named Tim lived in the house with the messy room. Every day, Tim played with his toys and read his magazine. But he did not like to clean up the toys and the magazine when he was done. One day, Tim's mom said, "Please, Tim, clean up your messy room." Tim did not want to clean up, so he said, "No, I will not clean up." Tim's mom was sad, but she did not make him clean up. That night, when Tim went to bed, the toys and the magazine were still all over the floor. The next morning, Tim woke up and saw that his toys and the magazine were gone! He looked all over the messy room, but he could not find them. Tim was very sad. He went to his mom and asked, "Where are my toys and my magazine?" His mom said, "The toys and the magazine cleaned up themselves and went to find a new home. They did not want to live in a messy room." Tim learned that he should always clean up his toys and his magazine when he was done playing. From that day on, Tim always cleaned up his room. And one day, when his room was very clean, the toys and the magazine came back to live with him. The moral of the story is to always clean up your things, or they might go away.

Once there was a big bird. The bird liked to soar high in the sky. It felt happy when it flew up and down. One day, the bird saw a row of little ducks. The ducks were swimming in a peaceful pond. The bird wanted to play with the ducks. The big bird flew down and said, "Hello, ducks! Let's play together!" The ducks said, "Yes, let's play!" So the bird and the ducks played all day in the peaceful pond. They were all very happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a big jar of gum. Tim loved gum, so he asked his mom if he could have some. She said yes, but he could only take one. Tim picked a gum that was very big and shiny. He was so happy. He could not wait to eat it. But when he tried to eat the gum, it was very hard. Tim was sad. He thought the gum was fake. Then, the big gum started to move. Tim was surprised! The gum was not fake, it was a little bug! The bug stood up and said, "I am sorry, I was sleeping in the gum jar." Tim laughed and the bug became his new friend.

One sunny day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She was very excited. She saw her friend, Tom, who was playing with a whip. The whip was long and red. Lily asked Tom, "Can I play with the whip too?" Tom said, "Yes, you can!" They took turns playing with the whip. They spread their arms wide and swung the whip around. After playing with the whip, they got hungry. Lily's mom gave them a big sandwich to share. They spread the yummy jam on the bread. They were very happy and had a fun day at the park.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. Sometimes they talk to each other on a screen. The screen is like a big phone. They can see and hear each other. One day, Lila and Ben talk on the screen. They show each other their toys and books. They laugh and have fun. Then, Lila sees a big bird outside her window. She says, "Look, Ben, a big bird! It is blue and yellow. It is pretty." Ben looks at his window. He does not see a big bird. He sees a small bird. It is brown and gray. It is not pretty. He says, "I don't see a big bird, Lila. I see a small bird. It is not pretty. It is boring." Lila says, "No, Ben, you are wrong. The big bird is better than the small bird. It is more pretty and more fun. You are silly." Ben says, "No, Lila, you are wrong. The small bird is better than the big bird. It is more wise and more quiet. You are noisy." Lila and Ben start to argue. They do not listen to each other. They do not have fun. They are sad and angry. Then, a wise voice says, "Stop, Lila and Ben. You are both wrong and right. The big bird and the small bird are different, but they are both good. They are both birds. They can both fly and sing. They can both be your friends. You do not have to like the same things. You can like different things and still be friends. You can learn from each other. You can share and be kind. That is what friends do." Lila and Ben look at the screen. They see a wise face. It is Grandma. Grandma is on the screen too. She heard them argue. She wanted to help them. She says, "I love you, Lila and Ben. You are both my grandchildren. You are both special and smart. You can both be happy and friendly. Do you want to say sorry to each other?" Lila and Ben nod. They say sorry to each other. They say thank you to Grandma. They smile and hug the screen. They are friends again. They are happy. They talk to Grandma and each other. They show each other their birds and other things. They laugh and have fun. They are wise.
