Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play outside with her dog, Max. One day, they went for a walk in the park. They saw a big loop on the ground. Sue thought it was a fun toy, but Max did not like it. Max barked and barked at the loop. Sue did not know why Max did not like the loop. The next day, Sue and Max went back to the park. They saw a big, green, and disgusting bug near the loop. Max barked and barked at the bug. Sue did not like the bug too. It was so big and yucky. Max wanted to keep Sue away from the bug. That is why he barked at the loop the day before. Sue and Max ran away from the bug. They went to another part of the park to play. They found new friends to play with. They met a boy named Tim and his cat, Tia. They all played together and had lots of fun. Sue, Max, Tim, and Tia became good friends. They never went near the loop again, but they had many fun days in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the gym. The gym had many fun things to do. Tim's favorite thing to do was to squeeze a soft, cheap ball. He liked the way it felt in his hands. One day, Tim saw a big box in the gym. The box was closed, but he could see that there was something inside. Tim thought that maybe there was a new toy for the gym. He wanted to see what was in the box. The next day, Tim went back to the gym. He saw that the big box was open. Inside the box, there were many new, cheap balls to squeeze! Tim was so happy. He played with the new balls all day long. He knew that the big box was a sign that something fun was going to happen. And it did!

One day, a little red bird was walking in a march. The bird was very happy. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. The bird liked to rub his wings in the soft grass. Then, the bird met a big blue cat. The cat said, "This is my march. Go away!" The red bird was sad and scared. The bird didn't want to leave his favorite march. The red bird had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game. If I win, I can stay. If you lose, I will go away." The big blue cat agreed. They played a game of hide and seek. The red bird was very fast and good at hiding. The big blue cat could not find the red bird. The cat said, "You win. You can stay in the march." The red bird was happy. The cat and the bird became friends. They played together in the march every day.

Ben and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the sand. They had a big bucket and a small shovel. They made hills and holes and castles. They pretended they were kings of the desert. One day, they saw something shiny in the distance. They ran towards it, hoping it was a treasure. But it was not a treasure. It was an oasis, a place with water and trees and grass. Ben and Sam were very happy. They drank some water and ate some dates. They felt cool and fresh. But then they saw a fat camel lying under a palm tree. The camel looked sleepy and bored. Ben had an idea. He said to Sam, "Let's kick the camel and see what it does. Maybe it will run or spit or make a funny noise." Sam thought it was a bad idea. He said, "No, Ben, don't kick the camel. It is not nice. The camel did not do anything to us. Maybe it will get angry and hurt us." But Ben did not listen. He ran towards the camel and kicked it hard on its side. The camel woke up with a loud roar. It was very angry. It jumped up and chased Ben and Sam. It bit Ben on his arm and Sam on his leg. It made them cry and bleed. Ben and Sam ran away from the oasis, back to the sand. They wished they had never kicked the camel. They wished they had been nice and gentle. They learned a hard lesson. They were not kings of the desert. They were just naughty boys who got punished.
