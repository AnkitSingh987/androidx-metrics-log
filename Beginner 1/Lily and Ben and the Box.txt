Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Box

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. They saw a big box under a tree. It had a red bow on it. "Look, a present!" Lily said. "Who is it for?" "I don't know. Maybe for us!" Ben said. "Let's open it and see." They ran to the box and pulled the bow. They lifted the lid. They were very excited. But inside the box, there was nothing. Only a loud noise. BANG! The noise made them jump. They were scared. They dropped the lid. It fell on Lily's foot. It hurt a lot. She cried. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily said. "My foot is hurt!" Ben felt sorry for Lily. He hugged her. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. It was a bad surprise. Someone was mean to us. They tricked us." Lily sniffed. She said, "It's okay, Ben. You are nice to me. You are my friend." They left the box and went to find their mom. She gave them a hug and a kiss. She said, "What happened, my loves?" They told her about the box and the noise and the hurt. She said, "That was not a nice thing to do. Someone was very naughty. But you are brave and kind. I have a good surprise for you. How about some ice cream?" Lily and Ben smiled. They said, "Yes, please! We love ice cream!" They went to the ice cream truck. They chose their favorite flavors. They licked their cones. They felt better. They said, "This is a good surprise. Thank you, mom!" The end.

Anna likes to dress up in her mom's clothes. She puts on a big hat, a long dress and some shiny shoes. She looks in the mirror and smiles. She thinks she is very pretty. But her brother Ben does not like her dress up. He thinks she is silly and ugly. He wants to play with his cars and trucks. He does not want Anna to bother him. He gets angry and grabs some scissors. He runs to Anna and tries to cut her dress. He says, "You look ugly! I will make you look better!" Anna is scared and screams. She tries to run away, but Ben is faster. He cuts a big hole in her dress. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. She sees Anna crying and Ben holding the scissors. She is very mad and sad. She takes the scissors from Ben and hugs Anna. She says, "Ben, you are very naughty! You should not cut Anna's dress! You should not hurt your sister! You should say sorry!" Ben feels bad and sorry. He sees that he made Anna sad and hurt her dress. He says, "I'm sorry, Anna. I was mean and stupid. I don't think you are ugly. You are pretty and nice. Can we be friends?" Anna stops crying and looks at Ben. She sees that he is sorry and wants to be friends. She says, "It's okay, Ben. I forgive you. You are not mean and stupid. You are smart and funny. We can be friends." Mom smiles and wipes their tears. She says, "I'm glad you are friends. But you still need to learn to share and respect each other. And you need to be careful with scissors. They are not toys. They can cut and hurt." She takes them to the closet and finds some new clothes for them. She helps them dress and says, "Now, let's go and have some cookies and milk. And then we can play together nicely."

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a sister named Emma. Emma was very smart. Lily liked to play with Emma. They were best friends. One day, Lily and Emma went to the park. They saw a big red bike. "Let's ride the bike," said Lily. Emma helped Lily get on the bike. They rode the bike together. They went up and down the hills. They had so much fun. After they finished riding the bike, they went home. Their mom gave them a big hug. "You two are so good at riding the bike," said Mom. Lily and Emma smiled. They were very happy.
