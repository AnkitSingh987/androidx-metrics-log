Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Basket

Every day, Lily liked to help her mom deliver bread to the elderly people in their street. She carried a small basket with a red cloth and smiled at everyone she met. She knew the names of all the old people and their pets. She liked to give them a hug and a slice of bread with jam. One day, when Lily and her mom came to Mr. Lee's house, they saw a note on the door. It said: "Please do not knock. I am having a nap." Lily felt sad. She wanted to say hello to Mr. Lee and his cat, Snowy. She asked her mom if they could leave the bread on the porch. Her mom said yes, but they had to be very quiet. Lily tiptoed to the porch and put the basket down. She saw Snowy sleeping on a chair. She wanted to pet him, but she remembered the note. She whispered: "Good morning, Snowy. I hope you and Mr. Lee have a nice wake." Then she turned to leave. But as she did, she heard a loud meow. Snowy had woken up and seen the bread. He jumped off the chair and ran to the basket. He tried to pull the cloth off, but he knocked the basket over. The bread rolled out and hit the door. The door made a loud bang. Lily and her mom gasped. They heard Mr. Lee's voice from inside. He sounded grumpy. He said: "Who is making all that noise? Can't a man have a nap in peace?" He opened the door and saw Lily, her mom, and Snowy. He looked at the bread on the floor. He frowned. Lily felt scared. She thought Mr. Lee would be angry with her. She said: "I'm sorry, Mr. Lee. We wanted to deliver you some bread, but Snowy woke you up. Please don't be mad at us." Mr. Lee looked at Lily. He saw her big eyes and her small basket. He saw her mom holding her hand. He saw Snowy licking his paws. He felt a warm feeling in his heart. He smiled. He said: "It's okay, Lily. I'm not mad at you. You are very kind to bring me bread. And Snowy is just a silly cat. He likes bread too. Come in, come in. Let's have some tea and jam. And maybe a nap later." He winked at Lily. Lily smiled back. She was happy. She liked Mr. Lee and Snowy. She liked to deliver bread. She said: "Thank you, Mr. Lee. You are very nice. And Snowy is very cute. Let's have some tea and jam. And maybe a nap later." She winked at Mr. Lee.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big, flexible tree. Lily wanted to bring her ball to play near the tree. She had a big smile on her face. Lily played with her ball near the tree. The ball went up and down. Lily was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball went very high and got stuck in the tree! Lily was sad. She did not know what to do. But then, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird went to the tree and got the ball. The bird brought the ball back to Lily. She was so happy! The big bird was her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pink ball that she loved to play with. She liked to be independent and play with the ball on her own. One day, Sue saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to throw the ball over it. Sue threw the ball high up in the air, but it got stuck in the tree! She was very sad and didn't know what to do. She tried to jump and grab it, but she was too small. Sue knew she needed help, so she went to her big brother, Tom. Tom was very tall and strong. He saw Sue's sad face and knew he had to help her. Tom lifted Sue up on his shoulders, and together, they reached the pink ball. Sue was so happy to have her ball back and thanked Tom with a big hug. From that day on, Sue knew that even though she liked to be independent, it was okay to ask for help sometimes.
