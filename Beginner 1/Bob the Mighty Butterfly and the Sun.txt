Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Mighty Butterfly and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a mighty butterfly named Bob. Bob loved to fly high in the sky. He had many friends, but his best friend was a little girl named Sue. Sue and Bob would play all day in the sun. One day, Sue said to Bob, "Let's have a race! I want to see who is the fastest." Bob liked the idea and said, "Okay, let's race to the big tree and back." They both got ready and started to run. Sue was fast, but Bob was faster. He flapped his wings as fast as he could. As they got close to the finish line, Bob and Sue were neck and neck. They both pushed as hard as they could. In the end, Bob won the race by just a little bit. Sue was a little sad, but she was happy for her friend. She said, "Good job, Bob, you are very fast!" Bob said, "Thank you, Sue. You are fast too. Let's race again one day." Sue agreed, and they both went back to playing in the sun. And so, the mighty butterfly and the little girl remained the best of friends, always ready for a new adventure.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in the living room. But today, they have a problem. There is only one bathroom in their house, and they both need to go. "Mom, I need to go to the bathroom!" Anna says. "Me too, me too!" Ben says. "OK, OK, calm down. You can take turns. Anna, you go first. Ben, you wait here." Mom says. Anna runs to the bathroom and closes the door. Ben waits outside. He is grumpy. He does not like to wait. He wants to go to the bathroom now. He knocks on the door. "Anna, hurry up! I need to go!" "Go away, Ben! I'm not done!" Anna says. Ben knocks harder. "Anna, come on! You're taking too long!" "Stop it, Ben! You're being mean!" Anna says. Ben gets angry. He shakes the door. He tries to open it. He yells. "Anna, let me in! Let me in!" Anna gets scared. She screams. "Ben, stop! Stop! You're hurting the door!" Mom hears the noise. She comes to the bathroom. She sees Ben shaking the door. She sees Anna crying inside. She is not happy. She grabs Ben by the arm. She pulls him away from the door. "Ben, what are you doing? You're being very naughty! You can't shake the door like that! You have to wait your turn!" Mom says. "But Mom, I need to go to the bathroom!" Ben says. "Well, you have to be patient. And you have to be nice to your sister. She needs to go to the bathroom too. You have to share." Mom says. She opens the door. She lets Anna out. She hugs her. "Are you OK, Anna? I'm sorry Ben scared you. He was being very rude." "I'm OK, Mom. Thank you." Anna says. She looks at Ben. She is still mad at him. But she is also sorry for him. She knows he needs to go to the bathroom too. She says to him. "Ben, I'm sorry I took so long. You can go to the bathroom now. But please don't shake the door again. It's not nice." Ben looks at Anna. He is still grumpy. But he is also sorry for her. He knows he scared her. He says to her. "Anna, I'm sorry I shook the door. I was being very mean. Thank you for letting me go to the bathroom now. But please don't take so long again. It's not fair." Mom smiles. She is glad they are sorry. She hopes they will be nicer to each other. She says to them. "OK, kids, I'm proud of you for saying sorry. Now, Ben, you go to the bathroom. Anna, you come with me. We'll find something else to play with. And remember, you have to share. And you have to be kind. OK?" "OK, Mom." Anna and Ben say. They hug Mom. They hug each other. They go to the bathroom and to the living room. They play with their toys. They are not grumpy anymore. They are happy.
