Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Toys

Ben likes baseball. He likes to throw the ball and hit the ball with a bat. He likes to run fast on the grass. He has a red hat with a B on it. He wants to play baseball with his friends. But his mom says he has to pay first. He has to pay with his toys. He has to put his toys in a box and give them to his mom. Then he can play baseball. Ben does not like to pay. He likes his toys. He has a lot of toys. He has cars and trucks and trains and planes and dinosaurs and robots and blocks and books and puzzles and balls. He says to his mom, "Mom, why do I have to pay? I want to play baseball. I don't want to give you my toys." His mom says, "Ben, you have to pay because you have too many toys. You don't play with all of them. Some of them are broken or missing pieces. Some of them are old or dirty. You have to pay because you have to share. There are other kids who don't have toys. They would be happy to have your toys. You have to pay because you have to be kind. Kindness is better than toys." Ben thinks for a moment. He loves his toys, but he also loves baseball. He also loves his mom and he wants to be kind. He says, "OK, mom, I will pay. But can I keep some of my toys? Can I keep my red hat and my bat and my ball and my dinosaur and my robot and my book?" His mom smiles and says, "Yes, Ben, you can keep those toys. They are your favorites. You can pay with the rest of your toys. I will take them to the charity shop. They will find new homes with new kids. Then you can play baseball with your friends. I am proud of you, Ben. You are a good boy." Ben hugs his mom and says, "Thank you, mom. I love you. You are a good mom." He puts his toys in a box and gives them to his mom. Then he puts on his red hat and grabs his bat and his ball and his dinosaur and his robot and his book. He runs outside to play baseball with his friends. He is happy. He is fast. He is kind.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot had a plan for the year. He wanted to help his best friend, a little boy named Tim. Spot's plan was to make Tim happy every day. They played ball, went for walks, and shared snacks. Tim laughed and smiled a lot. Spot was a very good dog. As the year went by, Spot and Tim had lots of fun. They were always together. Spot's plan worked, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind queen. She lived in a big castle. She had many friends. One day, a new friend came to the castle. The queen wanted to welcome her new friend. The queen was very happy to see her friend. She said, "Welcome to my castle!" The friend was grateful. She said, "Thank you, queen!" They played together and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, the friend had to go home. The queen was sad, but she knew they would play again soon. The friend was grateful for the fun day. They said goodbye and the queen went back to her castle, waiting for the next time they could play together.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his hoop toy. Every day, he would run and jump through the hoop. One sunny day, a little girl named Sally saw Spot playing with his hoop. She liked the hoop and wanted to play too. Sally went to Spot and said, "Give me the hoop, please." But Spot did not want to share his hoop. He barked and demanded, "No, it's mine!" Sally felt sad and walked away. Spot saw Sally's sad face and felt bad. He picked up the hoop with his mouth and ran to Sally. Spot dropped the hoop in front of her and wagged his tail. Sally smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot!" From that day on, Sally and Spot played with the hoop together. They became the best of friends. Spot was a loyal friend to Sally, and they had lots of fun in the park every day.
