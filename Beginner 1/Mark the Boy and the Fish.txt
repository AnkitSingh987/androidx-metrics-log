Category: Beginner
Title: Mark the Boy and the Fish

Once there was a boy named Mark. He really didn't like the water. He hated swim. Whenever he had to go in the water, he screamed and wailed. Every day his parents took him to the wild beach, hoping that he would learn to love the ocean. But it was no use. Whenever they put him in the water he just got mad. He didn't want to play with the fish, or try to stand up in the waves. One day, his parents had an idea. They decided to give him a swimming lesson. They put some floaties on his arms and taught him how to move in the water. Slowly, he started to enjoy it. He started to like going to the beach. He would play with the other kids, run around on the sand and even try to do some wild swimming. Now whenever he was asked, Mark would always answer: "I love swim!"

Once upon a time, there was a big bear and a little bear. They loved to go on walks in the forest. One day, they saw a fancy hat on the ground. The big bear wanted the hat, but the little bear wanted it too. They decided to have a race to see who could march the fastest. They would march to the big tree and back. The one who won would get the fancy hat. The big bear and the little bear got ready to march. They started to march very fast. The big bear was strong, but the little bear was quick. They marched and marched until they reached the big tree. Then, they turned around and marched back. The little bear won the race and got the fancy hat. The big bear was sad, but then the little bear said they could share the hat. They both were happy and went on a walk together, wearing the fancy hat.

Lily and Max are brother and sister. They like to play outside in the garden. One day, they decide to make a tent with a big sheet and some sticks. They find a good spot under a tree and start to build their tent. "Max, help me with this stick. It is too heavy for me," Lily says. She tries to lift a long stick and put it on the sheet. "OK, Lily. I will help you. But I want to be the boss of the tent. You have to do what I say," Max says. He grabs the stick and throws it on the sheet. The sheet falls down and covers them. "Hey, you made the tent fall down. That is not nice. I don't want to play with you anymore. You are a mean boss," Lily says. She crawls out of the sheet and runs away. "Fine, go away. I don't need you. I can make the tent by myself. I am independent," Max says. He tries to fix the sheet and the sticks, but he can't. He feels sad and lonely. He looks around and sees Lily sitting on a swing. She looks sad and lonely too. He thinks maybe he was too mean to her. He wants to say sorry and play with her again. He gets up and walks to the swing. He says, "Lily, I am sorry I threw the stick and made the tent fall down. I was not a good boss. Can we be friends again and make the tent together?" Lily smiles and says, "Yes, Max. I am sorry I ran away. I was not a good helper. We can be friends again and make the tent together." They hug and go back to the tree. They work together and make a nice tent. They play inside the tent and have fun. They are happy and independent.

One day, a little boy named Tim got a big present. He was very happy. Inside the present was a toy car. Tim played with the car all day long. The car was very reliable and it did not break. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw the car and thought it was fun. Tim and Sue played with the car together. They had a great time. Sue wanted a car just like Tim's. Sue asked her mom for a car like Tim's. Her mom said, "I will think about it." Sue's mom talked to Tim's mom. Tim's mom suggested that Sue's mom buy the same car for Sue. Sue's mom agreed and bought the car for Sue. Now, Tim and Sue both had reliable toy cars and they played together every day.
