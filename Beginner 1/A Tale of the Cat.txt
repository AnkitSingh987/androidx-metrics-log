Category: Beginner
Title: A Tale of the Cat

One day, a big dog and a little cat were friends. They liked to play and have fun. The big dog was very fair and always shared his toys with the little cat. One day, the big dog said to the little cat, "Let's go bathe in the pond." The little cat was scared. "But I don't like water," she said. The big dog smiled and said, "Don't worry, I will be with you. It will be fun!" So, they went to the pond to bathe. As they were playing in the water, the little cat saw a fish in the pond. The fish swam up to them and said, "Hello, friends! I lost my way. Can you help me find my family?" The big dog and the little cat looked at each other and said, "Yes, we will help you." Together, they helped the fish find his family. The fish was very happy and said, "Thank you, friends!" The big dog and the little cat learned that helping others is a good thing to do. They went home feeling happy and proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She had a big book with many words. Anna was careful with her book. She read it every day. One day, Anna found a new word in her book. The word was "pray." She did not know what it meant. So, she asked her mom. Her mom told her that to pray is to talk to God and ask for help. That night, Anna wanted to try praying. She closed her eyes and said, "Dear God, please help me be a good girl and take care of my book. Thank you." Then, she went to sleep. The next day, Anna was happy because she learned a new word and how to pray.

Tom was bald. He did not like his head. He wanted hair like his wife. His wife had long and soft hair. She loved Tom very much. One day, Tom saw a shop. The shop had a sign. The sign said: "We can improve your hair. Come in and try." Tom was curious. He went inside the shop. A man greeted him. The man had a lot of hair. The man said: "Hello, sir. Do you want to improve your hair? We have a special offer. You can choose any color and style you like. It is easy and fast." Tom said: "Yes, please. I want to improve my hair. I want to have hair like my wife. She has brown and curly hair. Can you do that?" The man said: "Of course, sir. No problem. Just sit here and relax. I will use this machine. It will make your hair grow in minutes. Trust me, you will look great." Tom sat on a chair. The man put a helmet on his head. The helmet had wires and buttons. The man turned on the machine. The machine made a loud noise. Tom felt a tickle on his head. After a while, the man turned off the machine. He took off the helmet. He gave Tom a mirror. Tom looked at himself. He was shocked. He had hair, but not like his wife. He had pink and spiky hair. He looked like a clown. Tom said: "What have you done? This is not what I wanted. This is not how my wife's hair looks like. This is awful. I want my money back." The man said: "Sorry, sir. There was a mistake. The machine mixed up the colors and styles. I can fix it. Just give me another chance." Tom said: "No, thank you. I don't trust you. I don't want your hair. I want my bald head back. How can I get rid of this hair?" The man said: "Well, sir. There is only one way. You have to shave it off. Here, take this razor. It is free. Good luck, sir. And have a nice day." Tom took the razor. He ran out of the shop. He went home. He shaved off his hair. He was bald again. He felt better. He hugged his wife. His wife said: "I love you, Tom. You are handsome with or without hair. You don't need to improve anything. You are perfect for me." Tom said: "Thank you, my dear. You are right. I love you, too. You are the best wife in the world. You make me happy with or without hair. You are perfect for me, too."
