Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toast

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the kitchen to make some toast. She put the bread in the toaster and waited. While she waited, she looked around and observed everything in the kitchen. She saw her mom cooking and her dad washing dishes. When the toast popped up, Lily saw that it was a little bit burned. She felt troubled because she didn't want to eat burned toast. So, she asked her mom for help. Her mom showed her how to make the toast just right. Lily tried again and this time, the toast came out perfect. She felt happy and proud of herself. She put some butter on her toast and shared it with her mom and dad. They all enjoyed the yummy toast together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and discover new things. One day, while playing in the park, he found a small leather ball. Spot was very happy and wanted to play with the ball all day. Spot was a stubborn dog and did not want to share the ball with his friends. They tried to take the ball from him, but Spot would not let them. His friends were sad and walked away. Spot felt bad but still wanted to play with the ball. Suddenly, the leather ball started to move on its own. Spot was very surprised! The ball was not a regular ball; it was a magic ball! The magic ball wanted to teach Spot a lesson. It jumped away from Spot and went to his friends. Spot's friends began to play with the magic ball, and they all had fun together. Spot realized that sharing is more fun than being stubborn. From that day on, he shared all his toys with his friends.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a bird on top. Tom had a serious look in his eye. He wanted to catch the bird. He stared at the bird for a long time. Tom thought of a plan. He would jump from a big rock to the tree. He walked to the rock and stared at the tree. He was ready to jump. But, something unexpected happened! A dog named Max saw Tom. Max wanted to play with Tom. He ran to the rock and jumped on it too. The rock moved and Tom fell. The bird flew away. Tom was sad, but then he saw Max. They became best friends and played all day.

Jim and Bob were best friends. One day Jim said to Bob, "Let's play a game. We can study to try to win." Bob was very curious. He asked, "What kind of game?" Jim said, "Let's fly up in the sky." Bob was so excited. They both flew and had lots of fun. But then they started to lose their way. They couldn't find their way down and couldn't even see the ground! The two friends kept flying, but eventually they ran out of air. Jim and Bob had to give up and fall from the sky. When they hit the ground, they were sad. They had lost the game.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big orange slide. It looks fun. "Let's go on the slide!" Lily says. "OK!" Max says. They run to the slide. Lily goes first. She sits on the slide and zips down. She laughs and says, "Whee!" Max goes next. He sits on the slide and zips down. He laughs and says, "Whee!" They do it again and again. They have fun. But then, they see a man. He is not nice. He has a big net. He wants to catch Lily and Max. "Come here, you little bugs!" he says. "I will put you in my cell!" Lily and Max are scared. They run away from the man. They hide behind a tree. "Go away, you bad man!" Lily says. "Leave us alone!" Max says. The man looks for them. He does not see them. He is angry. But then, he hears a bark. It is Rex, the park dog. Rex is big and brave. He does not like the man. He runs to the man and bites his net. He tears it apart. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" the man says. "Stop it, you stupid dog!" He drops his net and runs away. He is scared. Rex barks and wags his tail. He is happy. He likes Lily and Max. He goes to them and licks their faces. "Good boy, Rex!" Lily says. "Thank you, Rex!" Max says. They hug Rex and pet him. They are safe. They go back to the slide. They zip down again. They laugh and say, "Whee!" They have fun.
