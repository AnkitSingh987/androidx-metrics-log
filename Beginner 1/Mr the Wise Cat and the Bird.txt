Category: Beginner
Title: Mr the Wise Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wise old cat named Mr. Whiskers. He was very good at helping his friends. One day, his friend, a little bird named Blue, needed help. Blue had a letter to send to his mom, but he didn't know how to do it. Mr. Whiskers knew just what to do. Mr. Whiskers told Blue to find a big red box called a post. "Please, Blue, go find the big red post," said Mr. Whiskers. Blue looked around the town and saw the big red post. He was very happy and flew towards it. At the post, Blue put the letter inside. He thanked Mr. Whiskers for his wise help. Soon, Blue's mom got the letter and was very happy. She sent a thank you note to Mr. Whiskers and Blue. From that day on, Blue always knew how to send letters, all because of his wise friend, Mr. Whiskers.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird. The blue bird liked to speak. It lived in a big tree. One day, the blue bird found a note. The note was on the ground. The blue bird picked up the note. The note had a picture of a house. The blue bird wanted to find the house. It flew and showed the note to friends. They all helped to find the house. The blue bird was happy.

One day, Sally and her dad went out to their garden. Dad said they were going to do something special - he said they were going to put up a sign. Sally was so excited! They found the sign and dad said it was time to get to work. He grabbed a rake and started working. It was hard work, but every now and then dad would stop and give Sally a big smile. Eventually they were done - the sign looked so nice! Then, dad took Sally's hand and said it was time to read what was written on the sign. Sally was excited to find out what the surprise was. Dad pointed at the sign and said, "Happy Birthday, Sally!" Sally couldn't believe it - it was for her! She smiled a big smile and thanked her dad for being so nice. She was so glad he had planted the sign in their garden.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bob. Bob loved to bring things to his friends. One day, he found a film. The film was shiny and pretty. He thought his friends would like it. Bob showed the film to his friends. They were all very happy. They wanted to see what was on the film. So, they went to see Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl knew a lot about films. Mr. Owl looked at the film. He told them the film was very old. It had a story about being kind and helping others. The friends were excited to watch the film. They learned that being kind made them feel good. But then, something unexpected happened. The film broke! The friends were sad. Bob felt bad because he wanted his friends to be happy. Then, Mr. Owl had an idea. He told the friends to act out the story from the film. The friends acted out the story. They were kind and helped each other. It was more fun than watching the film. The friends learned that being kind was the best thing to do. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a calm cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw his friend, a dog named Max, playing with paint. Tom went to Max and said, "Hi Max! Can I play with paint too?" Max said, "Yes, Tom! We can paint together!" They took the paint and started to make fun pictures. They made a sun, a tree, and a big house. They were very happy. Then, the wind started to blow. Tom and Max started to shiver. Tom said, "Max, I am cold. Let's go to my house and be warm." So, they went to Tom's house and had a nice warm drink. They were happy and warm, and they loved their new paint pictures.
