Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Mouse

One day, a big cat had a deep cut on her leg. She was very sad because it hurt a lot. She wanted to heal fast so she could play with her friends again. The cat went to the open field to find help. There, she met a small mouse. The mouse said, "I can help you heal your cut!" The cat was surprised because she didn't think a tiny mouse could help her. The mouse went to find a big leaf. She put the leaf on the cat's cut and tied it with a small string. In a short time, the cat's cut started to heal. The cat was very happy and thanked the mouse. They became best friends and played together in the open field every day.

Once upon a time, a huge dog named Max lived in a small house. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max saw a pass near his house. He had never seen it before. Max was very excited and wanted to see what was on the other side. Max started to walk down the pass. As he walked, he saw a big tree. Max decided to leave his ball under the tree and keep walking. He thought he would come back to get it later. As Max walked, he saw a cat. The cat was very nice and they played together for a while. After playing with the cat, Max remembered his ball and went back to the tree. But his ball was gone! Max was very sad. Then, he saw the cat holding his ball in its mouth. The cat had taken it! Max was surprised, but he was also happy to see his ball again. The cat gave the ball back to Max, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, two friends named Jack and Jill went for a walk in the park. The day was clear and warm. Jack pointed up to the sky, "Look, Jill! A crane!" Jill smiled, "Oh what a beautiful bird! Let's follow it." So they followed the crane as it flew high into the sky. Suddenly they saw two other crane birds in the sky. The four crane birds started to quarrel with each other. Jack and Jill watched as the crane birds loudly complained. Jill said to Jack, "What are they fighting about?" Jack shrugged, "I don't know. But let's keep watching and find out." They watched for a few more moments and then the birds stopped quarreling. Now the sky was clear and peaceful again. Jill smiled, "Well that was interesting. Now let's go get an ice cream!"

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to sell toys. He sold toys every Wednesday. One Wednesday, he found a big, red ball. Tim thought kids would love the big, red ball. Tim was very happy. He took the big, red ball to sell. But he did not see the small hole in the ball. The ball was not good to sell. Tim did not know this yet. A little girl came to buy the big, red ball. Tim was happy. But then, the ball got smaller and smaller. The girl was sad. Tim was sorry. He gave the girl a new toy to make her happy. From then on, Tim always checked toys before selling them.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big blue hill far away. They want to go there. "Look, Tom, a blue hill! Can we go?" Lily asks. "I don't know, Lily. It is very far. How can we get there?" Tom says. They stare at the blue hill. They think hard. They have an idea. "Let's ask Mom and Dad. Maybe they can take us in the car," Lily says. "Good idea, Lily. Let's go and ask them," Tom says. They run to their parents. They point at the blue hill. They ask nicely. "Mom, Dad, can we go to the blue hill? Please, please, please?" Lily and Tom say. Mom and Dad look at the blue hill. They smile. They say yes. "Okay, Lily and Tom. We can go to the blue hill. But first, we have to pack some snacks and water. And don't forget your hats and sunscreen," Mom and Dad say. Lily and Tom are happy. They hug Mom and Dad. They pack their things. They get in the car. They go to the blue hill. They have a lot of fun. They are glad they asked.
