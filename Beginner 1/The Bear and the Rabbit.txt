Category: Beginner
Title: The Bear and the Rabbit

One day, a big bear went to play outside. It was very cold and icy. He saw a hole in the ground and wanted to look inside. But the bear was too big and heavy to get near the hole. A small rabbit saw the bear trying to look inside the hole. The rabbit was not heavy, so it could get closer. The bear asked the rabbit to help him see what was inside the hole. The rabbit went close to the hole and looked inside. It saw a shiny rock! The rabbit and the bear worked together to get the rock out. They were so happy they could solve the problem together. In the end, the bear and the rabbit became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball that loved to bounce. It lived in a park with many other toys. The big, red ball was very selfish and did not like to share. One sunny day, a small, blue watch came to the park. The watch saw the big, red ball bouncing and wanted to play. The watch said, "Can I bounce with you, big, red ball?" But the big, red ball said, "No, I don't want to share my bounce." The watch felt sad and sat under a tree. A kind, green kite saw the watch and asked, "Why are you sad?" The watch told the kite about the selfish, big, red ball. The green kite said, "Don't worry, we can all play together." So, the green kite, the small, blue watch, and the big, red ball all played together in the park. They learned that sharing and playing together was more fun than being selfish. And from that day on, the big, red ball was never selfish again.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were in a house. The cat was hungry and wanted to eat. The cat saw an oven in the kitchen. The cat thought, "Maybe there is food in the oven." The cat tried to open the oven, but the oven was hot. The little dog saw the cat and wanted to help. The dog used its paw to slap the oven door. The oven door opened, but the dog's paw was hurt. The cat saw the dog's hurt paw and felt sorry. The cat said, "I am sorry, little dog." The dog said, "It's okay, big cat. We are friends." They found some food and ate it together. They were happy and played all day.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big tree with red fruits. They want to pick some fruits and eat them. "Look, Sue, red fruits! Let's pick some!" Tom says. "OK, Tom, but be careful. The tree is high and the fruits are hard to reach." Sue says. Tom and Sue climb the tree. They pick some fruits and put them in a bag. They are happy. They see a cool stream near the tree. They want to drink some water and wash the fruits. "Come on, Sue, let's go to the stream. The water is cool and clear." Tom says. "OK, Tom, but don't forget the bag. We don't want to lose the fruits." Sue says. Tom and Sue go to the stream. They leave the bag under the tree. They drink some water and wash the fruits. They are thirsty and hungry. "Yum, yum, the fruits are sweet and juicy. I like them." Tom says. "Me too, Tom. They are the best fruits ever." Sue says. But then, they hear a loud noise. They look up and see a big bear. The bear is angry and hungry. The bear sees the bag under the tree. The bear runs to the bag and tears it open. The bear eats all the fruits. Tom and Sue are scared and sad. "Hey, bear, stop! Those are our fruits! We picked them!" Tom shouts. "Roar, roar, I don't care! I'm hungry and I like fruits!" The bear growls. Tom and Sue run away from the bear. They weep and cry. They have no fruits and no bag. They are hungry and unhappy. They go home and tell their moms. Their moms hug them and comfort them. But they are still sad. They wish they had their fruits and their bag. They wish they had not seen the bear. They wish they had a good day. But they did not. They had a bad day. The end.
