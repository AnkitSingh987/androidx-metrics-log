Category: Beginner
Title: The Bull and the Cat

Once, there was a big bull. The bull lived on a farm. The bull was very expensive. The farmer liked the bull a lot. One day, the bull was in the field. The bull saw a cat. The cat was stuck in a tree. The bull felt sad for the cat. The bull wanted to help the cat. The bull had an idea. The bull went to the tree. The bull shook the tree with his big head. The cat fell down. But, the cat was not a cat! It was a bird! The bird said "thank you" and flew away. The bull felt happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her garden. She saw an unusual prune on the ground. She picked it up and thought, "I will bury this prune and see what happens." The next day, Sue went back to the garden. She saw a big hole where she had buried the prune. "What happened?" she asked herself. She looked around and saw a big, friendly bear. The bear said, "Hi, Sue! I found your prune and ate it. Thank you, it was yummy!" Sue was surprised and happy. She did not know bears could talk. She and the bear became friends. They played in the garden every day. The unusual prune made something unexpected happen. It brought Sue a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a white frog named Tim. Tim lived in a swamp. Every day, he would wake up and play with his friends. One day, Tim woke up and saw a big, mean bird in the swamp. The bird was trying to take Tim's friends away. Tim was very sad and scared. He did not know what to do. Tim went up to the bird and said, "Please, don't take my friends. We just want to play and be happy." The bird looked at Tim and thought for a moment. Then, the bird said, "Okay, I will not take your friends. We can all play together." So, Tim, his friends, and the big bird played all day in the swamp. They were all very happy, and the swamp was a fun place again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy bear with one arm. He loved his bear very much. Tim believed his bear could do anything, even with just one arm. One day, Tim and his bear went to the park. They saw a small, harmless bug. Tim and his bear played with the bug. The bug liked Tim and his bear too. As the sun went down, Tim and his bear went home. They were happy and tired. They had a fun day with their new bug friend. Tim hugged his one-armed bear and went to sleep with a big smile on his face.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play in the garden. They see a big carrot in the dirt. They want to eat it. "Let's pull the carrot out," Ben says. He grabs the carrot and pulls hard. Lily helps him. "Wow, it's so big and orange!" Lily says. She breaks the carrot in half and gives one part to Ben. They bite the carrot. It is crunchy and sweet. They hear a noise. It is a rabbit. The rabbit is deaf. He cannot hear Ben and Lily. He smells the carrot. He hops to them. "Hello, rabbit," Ben says. He holds out his half of the carrot. "Do you want some?" The rabbit does not talk. He does not hear Ben. He sniffs the carrot and takes a bite. He likes it. "Thank you, Ben," Lily says. She smiles. "You are very kind." Ben smiles too. He likes to share. He, Lily and the rabbit eat the carrot together. They are happy.

One day, a little frog named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a high branch. Tim wanted to jump up and sit on the branch. He thought it would be fun to see the world from up high. Tim met a wise old owl. "How can I get up to the high branch?" he asked. The wise owl said, "You must spring with all your might, but be careful not to hurt your waist." Tim thanked the owl and got ready to jump. He took a deep breath and sprang as high as he could. Tim landed on the branch and looked around. He saw the beautiful world from up high. But, he remembered the wise owl's words and was careful not to hurt his waist. Tim learned that it's important to listen to wise friends and be careful when trying new things. And from that day on, he always listened to the wise owl's advice.
