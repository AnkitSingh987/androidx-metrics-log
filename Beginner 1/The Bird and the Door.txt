Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who lived in a birdcage. The birdcage was safe and warm. The bird liked to play and sing in the birdcage. One day, the bird saw a key. The bird wanted to use the key to open the birdcage. The bird used the key and the birdcage door opened. The bird was happy and flew outside. The bird played outside and met new friends. But soon, the bird missed the safe birdcage. The bird went back and used the key to close the door. The bird was happy to be safe in the birdcage again.

Tim likes to play baseball with his dad. They go to the park and throw the ball to each other. Tim tries to catch the ball with his glove, but sometimes he misses. Dad says, "It's okay, Tim. You are doing great. Just keep your eye on the ball." One day, Tim sees some big kids playing baseball on the field. They have bats and helmets and uniforms. They look very good and fast. Tim wants to play with them, but he is nervous. He thinks they will laugh at him or say he is too small. He walks up to them and says, "Hi, can I play with you?" One of the big kids looks at him and says, "Sure, kid. You can be on my team. What's your name?" Tim says, "I'm Tim. I like baseball." The big kid says, "I'm Jake. I like baseball too. Come on, let's go. You can be the batter." Tim follows Jake to the plate. He picks up a bat and puts on a helmet. He feels very excited and nervous. He sees the pitcher throw the ball. It comes very fast and hard. Tim swings the bat, but he misses. He hears the big kids say, "Strike one!" Tim tries again. He swings the bat, but he misses again. He hears the big kids say, "Strike two!" Tim feels scared. He thinks he will strike out and let Jake down. He thinks the big kids will make fun of him. He wants to run away. He looks at Jake. Jake smiles at him and says, "Don't worry, Tim. You can do it. Just relax and have fun. Remember, keep your eye on the ball." Tim nods. He takes a deep breath. He waits for the ball. He sees the ball. He swings the bat. He hits the ball. He hears a loud crack. He sees the ball fly over the fence. He hears the big kids cheer. He drops the bat and runs around the bases. He feels very happy and proud. He reaches home plate and jumps into Jake's arms. Jake hugs him and says, "You did it, Tim! You hit a home run! You are awesome!" Tim says, "Thank you, Jake! You are awesome too! You are my friend!" Jake says, "You are my friend too, Tim! You are welcome to play with us anytime!" Tim smiles. He thinks he likes baseball very much. He thinks he likes the big kids too. He thinks he is not nervous anymore. He thinks he is brave.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big box in the park. It has a handle on it. They wonder what is inside. "Can we open it?" Lily asks. "Let's try," Tom says. They pull the handle together. The box opens. Inside, there are many balls of different colors. "Wow, look at all the balls!" Lily says. She picks up a red one and throws it in the air. "Me too!" Tom says. He picks up a blue one and throws it too. The balls bounce and roll on the grass. They have fun playing with the balls. They toss them, catch them, and kick them. They laugh and giggle. After a while, a man comes to the park. He sees the box and the balls. He looks angry. "Hey, kids, what are you doing?" he shouts. "Those are my balls! You can't play with them!" Tom and Lily are scared. They drop the balls and run away. "Sorry, mister, we didn't know," Tom says. "We didn't mind to make you mad," Lily says. They run back to their homes. They feel sad. They liked the balls. The man picks up the balls and puts them back in the box. He closes the handle. He is not happy. He thinks the kids are bad. He does not know that Tom and Lily are great friends. They share and care. They just wanted to have fun. They hope to find another toy to play with tomorrow.
