Category: Beginner
Title: The Box and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big old box. In the box, there was a pretty jewel. The jewel was red and shiny. A little girl named Sue found the box in her room. She was very happy to see the jewel. Sue took the jewel and showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Wow, that is a nice jewel! Let's keep it safe in the box." So, Sue put the jewel back in the box and shut the lid tight. Every day, Sue would open the box to look at the jewel. She loved how it sparkled in the sun. The old box and the pretty jewel became her favorite things. Sue and her jewel lived happily ever after.

One day, a little dog named Tim went for a walk. He saw a deep hole in the ground. He wanted to see what was inside, so he jumped in. But the hole was too deep, and Tim could not get out. He started to cry. A big cat named Sam heard Tim's cries. Sam came to the hole and saw Tim. "Why are you crying?" asked Sam. Tim said, "I am stuck in this deep hole and cannot get out. Please help me!" Sam thought for a moment and had an idea. Sam found a long stick and held it out for Tim. "Listen, Tim! Grab this stick, and I will pull you out!" Tim held on to the stick tightly, and Sam pulled him out of the deep hole. Tim was so happy to be out. He thanked Sam for being so kind and helping him. From that day on, they became the best of friends, and Tim learned to be more careful on his walks.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the kitchen. They see a small glass on the table. It is shiny and pretty. "Look, a glass!" Tom says. "Let's play with it!" "OK!" Mia says. "But be careful. It can break." Tom picks up the glass. He sets it on the floor. He rolls it with his hand. It makes a funny sound. "Wow, this is fun!" Tom says. "Can I have a turn?" Mia asks. "Sure!" Tom says. He gives the glass to Mia. Mia sets it on the floor. She rolls it with her hand. It makes a funny sound. But then, the glass hits the wall. It breaks into many pieces. They hear a loud noise. "Oh no!" Tom and Mia say. They are scared. They see the glass on the floor. It is not shiny and pretty anymore. It is sharp and dangerous. They hear footsteps. It is Mom. She comes into the kitchen. She sees the glass on the floor. She sees Tom and Mia. She is angry. "What did you do?" Mom asks. "You broke the glass! You could have hurt yourself! You are very naughty!" Tom and Mia feel bad. They say sorry. But Mom is still angry. She sends them to their rooms. They cannot play anymore. They cry. They wish they did not play with the glass. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a thin leopard. He lived in a big jungle. The leopard had many friends like the monkey, the bird, and the snake. They played all day and had fun. One day, they saw a new flower. It was about to bloom. The friends were very excited. They wanted to see the flower bloom. They waited and waited. They thought it would be a big, pretty flower. But, something unexpected happened. The flower did not bloom like a normal flower. Instead, it changed into a big, colorful butterfly. The friends were surprised and happy. They played with the butterfly and had more fun. The thin leopard and his friends learned that sometimes, things can change in a wonderful way.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play on the swings and slide. His mom was always close by to watch him. She was very dependable and always made sure he was safe. While playing, Tim found a small card on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, mom! What is this?" he asked. His mom looked at the card and realized it was a license. "This is a license, Tim. It tells people that you can do something, like drive a car. But you're too little for that now." Tim thought about it and said, "When I grow up, I want to have a license too, so I can drive a car like you, mom!" His mom smiled and said, "You will, Tim. But for now, let's keep playing and having fun at the park!" So they continued to play and have a great day at the park.
