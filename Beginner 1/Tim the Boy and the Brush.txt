Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Brush

One day, a boy named Tim found a brush. It was a great brush. Tim wanted to paint with it. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, I want to paint with this great brush." His mom looked at the brush and said, "That is a great brush, Tim. But we don't have any paint. Let's go to the store and buy some." At the store, Tim and his mom looked at all the paint. There were so many colors. Tim said, "I want this one!" His mom said, "Okay, let's go home and paint." When they got home, Tim started to paint with his great brush. But then, something unexpected happened. The brush began to talk! The brush said, "I can only paint if you insist on it." Tim looked at his mom and said, "I insist on painting with this great brush!" The brush started to paint by itself. It made a very pretty picture. Tim and his mom were very happy with the talking brush.

One day, a little girl named Amy was very excited. She had a new toy called a microscope. Amy wanted to see tiny things with it. She went to her friend Tom's house to play. At Tom's house, they found a small bug. They put the bug under the microscope to look at it. They were so happy to see the bug up close. Suddenly, the bug started to wave at them. Amy and Tom were surprised. They waved back at the bug, and it waved again. Amy and Tom laughed and laughed. They thought the bug was just a bug, but it was a friendly bug who liked to wave. They played with the waving bug all day long and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty parrot named Polly. Polly liked to serve her friends food, but she always played tricks on them. One day, Polly had an idea for a big trick. Polly invited her friends to her tree for a fun lunch. She told them she would serve them yummy food. Her friends were happy and excited. But Polly had a naughty plan. When her friends came, they sat down to eat. Polly served them food, but she had put yucky things in it! Her friends were sad and didn't want to eat. But then, a smart bird told Polly's friends about her tricks. They all laughed and played a trick on Polly too. In the end, Polly learned not to be naughty and they all had a fun lunch together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friends, the birds and the bugs. One day, while playing with his friends, Sam found a long stick on the ground. The stick was orange, just like the sun. Sam picked up the orange stick and started to shake it. As he shook the stick, his friends watched and laughed. "Look at Sam and his funny stick!" said one of the birds. Sam laughed too and shook the stick even more. As Sam and his friends played with the orange stick, they heard a noise. It was a big bear! The bear was also orange, just like the stick. "What are you doing with my orange stick?" asked the bear. Sam was scared but he said, "I'm sorry, Mr. Bear. I didn't know it was yours." The bear smiled and said, "That's okay. You can play with it, but remember to share with your friends." Sam and his friends played with the orange stick all day and had lots of fun.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play outside. They have bicycles. Ben has a red bicycle. Lily has a white bicycle. One day, they go to the park. They ride their bicycles on the path. They see a big hill. Ben says, "Let's go up the hill. It will be fun." Lily says, "OK. But be careful. The hill is steep." They pedal hard. They go up the hill. They feel the wind in their hair. They are happy. They reach the top of the hill. They see the park below. They see the trees and the flowers and the pond. They say, "Wow. It is beautiful." They get ready to go down the hill. Ben says, "Let's go fast. It will be more fun." Lily says, "OK. But be careful. The hill is steep." They push their bicycles. They go down the hill. They go very fast. They feel the wind in their face. They are excited. But then, something bad happens. Lily's bicycle makes a loud noise. Lily looks down. She sees that her chain has snapped. She says, "Oh no. My chain is broken. I can't stop." Ben hears her. He looks back. He sees that Lily is in trouble. He says, "Lily, use your brakes. Use your brakes." Lily tries to use her brakes. But they don't work. Her bicycle is too fast. She can't control it. She is scared. She sees a big rock on the path. She says, "Help. Help." Ben says, "Lily, steer away. Steer away." Lily tries to steer away. But it is too late. Her bicycle hits the rock. She falls off. She lands on the grass. She hurts her knee. She cries. Ben stops his bicycle. He runs to her. He says, "Lily, are you OK? Are you OK?" Lily looks at him. She sees that he is worried. She says, "I'm OK. But my knee hurts. And my bicycle is broken. Look at my bicycle." She points to her white bicycle. It is bent and scratched. The chain is loose. The wheel is wobbly. She says, "My bicycle is ruined. I can't ride it anymore. I'm sad." Ben looks at her bicycle. He says, "I'm sorry, Lily. I'm sorry your bicycle is broken. It was my fault. I said we should go fast. It was not fun. It was dangerous. I'm sorry." Lily looks at him. She sees that he is sorry. She says, "It's OK, Ben. It was not your fault. It was an accident. Accidents happen. You are my friend. You came to help me. You are nice." Ben smiles. He says, "You are my friend, too. You are brave. You are not angry. You are nice." He hugs her. He says, "Come on. Let's go home. We can tell our moms. Maybe they can fix your bicycle. Or maybe they can buy you a new one. A white one. Or a different color. Whatever you like." Lily smiles. She says, "OK. Let's go home. Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend." She hugs him back. They walk to their bicycles. They hold hands. They go home. They are friends.
