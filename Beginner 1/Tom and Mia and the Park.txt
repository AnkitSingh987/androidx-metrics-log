Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Park

Tom and Mia were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big hill with a lot of mist on top. They wanted to go there and see what was behind the mist. "Let's go, Tom!" Mia said. "Maybe there is a castle or a dragon or a fairy!" "OK, Mia!" Tom said. "But we have to be careful. The mist might be cold or wet or scary." They ran up the hill, holding hands. The mist was thick and white. They could not see very well. They heard strange noises. They felt dizzy. "Tom, I don't like this!" Mia said. "I want to go back!" "Me too, Mia!" Tom said. "But how do we find the way?" They looked around. They could not see the park or the slide or the other kids. They only saw mist. They were lost. They started to cry. They were scared and cold and dizzy. "Help! Help!" they shouted. "We want to go home!" Then they heard a voice. It was their mom. "Tom! Mia! Where are you?" she called. "Mom! Mom! We are here!" they answered. Their mom followed their voices. She found them in the mist. She hugged them and kissed them. "Are you OK, my darlings?" she asked. "Yes, Mom, we are OK," they said. "But we are sorry. We were naughty. We ran away from the park. We wanted to see the mist. But it was not fun. It was scary." "It's OK, my darlings," she said. "I'm glad you are safe. But you have to listen to me. You can't go to places you don't know. You might get lost or hurt. You have to stay with me or with someone who can take care of you. Do you understand?" "Yes, Mom, we understand," they said. "We will not do it again. We love you, Mom." "I love you too, my darlings," she said. "Now let's go back to the park. We can play some more. But first, we have to finish our lunch. Are you hungry?" "Yes, Mom, we are hungry," they said. "And we are thirsty too." They walked down the hill, holding hands. The mist cleared. They saw the sun and the sky and the flowers. They felt happy. They finished their lunch. They played some more. They had fun. They did not go to the mist again.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue loved to dance. She danced all day and all night. Sue was very good at dancing. Her friends were good too, but Sue wanted to be the best. She was competitive. One day, Sue and her friends went to a big dance show. They were all excited. But before the show, Sue felt tired. She decided to take a nap. Sue's friends tried to wake her up, but she was fast asleep. While Sue was napping, her friends danced together. They had so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue's friends won the dance show! When Sue woke up, she was surprised. She learned that being the best was not as important as having fun with her friends. From that day on, Sue danced for fun and not just to win.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who had a friendly crib. He loved it so much that he spent most of his time in it. But one day, he got a boo-boo! He cried and was sad. Suddenly, his Mommy came into the room. She said, "What happened?" Then the little boy told her what had happened. He said, "Mommy, I have a boo-boo!" Mommy said, "Don't worry, my love. I'll sing you a special healing song. It will make you feel better." She sang him a beautiful song, and the little boy smiled. "Mommy, the healing song worked," he said. "I'm so glad it did. Now let's kiss your boo-boo and make it better," Mommy said. She kissed his boo-boo and said, "Now go back to your friendly crib, and you will feel better soon." So the little boy went back to his crib and snuggled in for a nap. When his eyes opened, he felt much better. The healing song had worked!
