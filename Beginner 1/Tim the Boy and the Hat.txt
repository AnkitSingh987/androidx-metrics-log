Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play pretend. One day, he put on a green hat and pretended he was a king. He felt very big and strong. But the hat was uncomfortable. It was too tight on his head. Tim did not like that. Tim went to the park with his green hat. He saw a small cat. The cat was green too! Tim was very surprised. He had never seen a green cat before. The cat looked at Tim and said, "I can see you are a king. But your hat is uncomfortable. I can help you." The green cat jumped on Tim's head and took off the uncomfortable hat. Suddenly, the cat turned into a big, soft, green hat! Tim was so happy. He put on the new green hat and it felt very good. The green cat was really a magic hat all along. Now, Tim could play pretend as a king with his new, comfortable green hat.

Tom and Lily liked to read stories with their mom. They had many books with pictures and words. Mom would point to the words and say them out loud. Tom and Lily would look at the pictures and imagine the stories. One day, they found a new book on the shelf. It had a big, hairy monster on the cover. Tom and Lily were curious and scared. They asked mom to read it to them. Mom said, "This is a scary story. Are you sure you want to hear it?" Tom and Lily nodded. They wanted to be brave. Mom opened the book and started to read. The story was about a hairy monster who lived in the woods. He liked to scare the animals and the people who came near his home. He would roar and chase them away. He was very lonely and angry. Tom and Lily felt sorry for the monster, but they also felt afraid. They hugged mom and each other. As mom read, they heard a loud noise outside. It sounded like a roar. Tom and Lily gasped. They looked at the window and saw a big, hairy shadow. It was the monster from the story! He had come to their house! Tom and Lily screamed. Mom dropped the book and ran to the door. She locked it and said, "Don't worry, kids. It's just a story. The monster can't hurt us. He can't come in." But the monster was not happy. He wanted to see the kids and the book. He wanted to hear the story. He wanted to have friends. He banged on the door and the walls. He shouted, "Let me in! Let me in! I want to hear the story!" Tom and Lily cried. Mom hugged them and said, "Shh, shh, it's okay. He will leave soon. He will get tired and go back to the woods. He can't stay here. He doesn't belong here." The monster heard mom's words. He felt sad and angry. He didn't want to leave. He wanted to stay. He wanted to belong. But he knew mom was right. He didn't fit in. He was too big and too hairy and too scary. He sighed and stopped banging. He walked away from the house. He went back to the woods. He was alone again. He wished he had a story to read. He wished he had a friend to read it with.

One day, a cool cat named Sam went for a walk. He saw a big skull on the ground. Sam was not scared. He wanted to play with it. Sam found a ball and said, "I will shoot the ball into the skull. It will be fun!" So, he kicked the ball hard. The ball went into the skull's mouth. Sam was happy. A little dog saw Sam play. The dog said, "Can I play too?" Sam said, "Yes! Let's shoot the ball together." They played and had a fun day with the cool skull.

Once upon a time, there was a King. He had a big castle and a big sword. He liked to show it off to everyone who visited him. One day, a brave knight came to visit. He asked the King for the sword, but the King refused. He said it was too hot and the knight must wait. But the knight didn't want to wait. He wanted the sword. He begged the King and begged the King, but still the King refused. Eventually, the knight gave up. He did not get the King's sword. But the King remembered how brave the knight had been, and gave him a surprise. He gave the knight a golden sword of his own! The knight was so happy, he hugged the King and thanked him.
