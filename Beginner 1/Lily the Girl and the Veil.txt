Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Veil

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily liked to play dress-up with her friends. One day, she found a cheap veil in her mom's closet. The veil was long and pretty. Lily wanted to wear it and show her friends. Lily put on the veil and went outside. She saw her friends playing and ran to them. As she ran, the veil got caught on a tree. Lily did not want to rip the veil, so she carefully took it off the tree. Lily and her friends played dress-up all day. They took turns wearing the veil. When they finished playing, Lily put the veil back in her mom's closet. She was happy that she found the cheap veil and had a fun day with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a big itch on his back. He tried to reach it, but he could not. He felt very sad. One day, Spot met a big dog named Max. Max saw that Spot was sad. "Why are you sad?" Max asked. Spot said, "I have a big itch, and I can't reach it." Max wanted to help, so he used his paw to scratch Spot's itch. Suddenly, it began to snow. The snow was very cold, and it made everything freeze. Spot and Max felt the cold snow on their fur. Spot noticed that the cold snow made his itch feel better. "Wow, the freeze makes my itch feel good!" said Spot. Max smiled and said, "I'm happy I could help you, Spot!" From that day on, Spot and Max were the best of friends. They played in the snow and helped each other when they had itches. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy went to sell his toy car. It was Tuesday. The boy was sad because he felt helpless. He did not want to sell his toy car, but he needed money to buy food. On the way, he met a girl. The girl said, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I have to sell my toy car, but I don't want to." The girl said, "I will help you. We can sell my toy doll instead." The boy was happy. They went to sell the toy doll. But when they got money, the girl took all the money and ran away. The boy was sad and helpless again. He lost his new friend, his toy car, and did not get any money.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run very fast. One day, he went to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. Just then, he heard a voice. "Listen, Tim," said the voice. "Don't climb the tree. It's not safe." But Tim did not listen. He wanted to climb the tree. Tim climbed the tree fast. He felt happy. But then, the branch broke. Tim fell down and hurt his leg. Tim cried and wished he had listened to the voice.

Once upon a time, a fast dog named Max lived in a small house. One day, Max went to a big hotel with his family. He was very excited to see the big hotel. Max saw a small door and wondered if he could fit inside. In the hotel, Max played with his ball. He ran and jumped all day. He remembered the small door and thought, "I will try to fit in there later." Max had a big smile on his face as he played with his family. At the end of the day, Max went to the small door. He took a deep breath and tried to fit inside. With a little push, Max fit through the door! Inside, he found a big room full of toys and treats. Max was so happy that he could fit in the small door. He played with the toys and ate the treats. It was the best day ever for Max.

One day, a little dog named Max was very hungry. He went to the kitchen and saw a dirty table. On the table, there were some yummy treats. Max jumped on the table and started to chew the treats. Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, came into the kitchen. She saw Max on the table and said, "Max, get off the table! It is dirty!" Max looked at Lily and wagged his tail. He knew he should not be on the table, but the treats were so good. Lily helped Max get off the table. She cleaned the dirty table and gave Max a treat. Max was very happy. He chewed his treat and wagged his tail. From that day on, Max never jumped on the table again.
