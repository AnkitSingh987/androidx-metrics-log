Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Car

Ben and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. Ben had a big red car that made loud noises and had shiny lights. Lily had a small pink doll that could talk and sing when she pressed a button on its back. "Look at my car, Lily. It is so cool and fast. It can go vroom vroom and beep beep. Your doll is so cheap and boring. It only says the same things over and over. Can I play with it?" Ben asked. "No, Ben. This is my doll and I love her. She is not cheap and boring. She is pretty and sweet. She can say I love you and sing a song. You cannot play with her. You have your own toy." Lily said. Ben did not listen to Lily. He grabbed the doll from her hands and ran away. Lily cried and chased him. Ben laughed and pressed the button on the doll's back. But nothing happened. The doll did not talk or sing. Ben pressed the button harder and harder. But still nothing happened. "Hey, what's wrong with this doll? It is broken. It does not work. It is a cheap and bad toy. I don't want it anymore." Ben said. He threw the doll on the floor and went back to his car. Lily picked up the doll and hugged her. She saw that the button on the doll's back was cracked and loose. Ben had broken it when he pressed it too hard. Lily felt sad and angry. She wanted to fix the doll and make her talk and sing again. She ran to the kitchen and looked for something to help her. She saw a roll of tape on the counter. She took it and ran back to the living room. She carefully taped the button on the doll's back. She hoped it would work. She pressed the button gently and waited. She heard a soft voice from the doll. "I love you, Lily. You are my best friend. Do you want to hear a song?" The doll said. Lily smiled and hugged the doll. She was so happy. She had saved her doll. She pressed the button again and sang along with the doll. "Thank you, Lily. You are so kind and smart. You fixed me. You are a good friend." The doll said. Lily looked at Ben and his car. She did not want to play with him anymore. He was mean and rude. He broke her doll. He was a bad friend. She decided to play with her doll in her room. She took her doll and left the living room. She did not say goodbye to Ben. She did not care about him or his car. She only cared about her doll. She loved her doll. Her doll was not cheap and boring. Her doll was precious and fun. Her doll was the best toy ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy horn. One day, Tim's mom told him they were going on a trip. She asked him to pack his toys in a bag. Tim was very excited and packed all his toys, including his horn. On the trip, Tim played his horn very loud. It hurt the ears of his sister, Lily. Lily asked Tim to stop, but he didn't listen. He kept playing the horn louder and louder. Lily started to cry because her ears hurt so much. Mom saw what was happening and took the horn away from Tim. She told him that he should be kind and think about how others feel. Tim felt sad for hurting his sister. He said sorry to Lily and promised to be more careful. From that day on, Tim learned to be kind and think about how his actions affect others. The moral of the story is to always be kind and considerate to others.

One day, a thoughtful cat and a dog went for a walk. They saw a river. The water was blue and pretty. The cat and dog liked the river. The cat said, "Let's discuss how to cross the river." The dog said, "Okay, let's talk about it." They talked and talked. They thought of a plan. The cat and dog found a big log. They put the log over the river. They walked on the log and crossed the river. They were happy and kept walking together.
