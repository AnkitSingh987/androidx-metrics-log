Title: Jim and the Screw

Jim was an engineer. He had a very special job. He needed a screw to help him. But the screw wouldn't fit! Jim was getting very frustrated. He wanted the screw to fit but it just wouldn't go in. So he decided to try something else. He thought he should use a different screw. He got a bigger screw and carefully put it in. He tried to push it in and twist it around. At last the new screw fit! Jim was so happy. He had solved the problem. He carefully tightened the screw and the job was done. Jim was proud that he had been able to find the right screw and make it fit. He was a very talented engineer!

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play together at the park. One day, they see a big slide. Ben wants to go on the slide, but Mia is nervous. She thinks the slide is too high and too fast. "Come on, Mia, let's go on the slide!" Ben says. "It will be fun!" "I don't want to, Ben. I'm scared. The slide is too big for me." Mia says. "Don't be scared, Mia. I will go with you. We can hold hands. It will be easy." Ben says. Mia looks at the slide. She sees other kids going down and laughing. She wants to be brave like Ben, but she feels nervous in her tummy. She shakes her head. "No, Ben. I can't. You go without me. I will wait here." Mia says. Ben is sad. He wants to play with Mia, but he also wants to go on the slide. He thinks for a moment. He has an idea. "Mia, I have something for you. Look!" Ben says. He takes out a small box from his pocket. He opens it and shows Mia. Inside the box, there is a jelly. It is red and shiny and wobbly. Mia loves jelly. Her eyes light up. "Wow, Ben, you have jelly! Where did you get it?" Mia asks. "I got it from my mom. She made it for me. It is my favorite. Do you want some?" Ben asks. "Yes, please, Ben. I love jelly. Thank you!" Mia says. Ben smiles. He gives Mia the box. Mia takes a spoon and eats some jelly. It is sweet and soft and yummy. She feels happy. "Ben, this jelly is delicious. You are a good friend." Mia says. "You're welcome, Mia. You are a good friend, too. Do you feel better now?" Ben asks. Mia nods. She feels better. She looks at the slide again. She sees Ben's face. He looks hopeful. "Mia, do you want to try the slide now? We can share the jelly after. It will be fun." Ben says. Mia thinks. She still feels nervous, but she also feels curious. She wants to try something new. She wants to make Ben happy. She takes a deep breath. "Okay, Ben. I will try the slide. But you have to hold my hand." Mia says. Ben cheers. He takes Mia's hand. They walk to the slide. They climb the steps. They sit on the top. They look down. They feel excited. "Are you ready, Mia?" Ben asks. "Yes, Ben. I'm ready." Mia says. They push off. They slide down. They go fast. They feel the wind. They scream and laugh. They reach the bottom. They are safe. They are proud. "Wow, Mia, that was awesome! You did it! You are brave!" Ben says. "Wow, Ben, that was fun! I did it! I'm brave!" Mia says. They hug. They smile. They run to the bench. They eat the jelly. They share the joy. They are friends.
