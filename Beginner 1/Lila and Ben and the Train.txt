Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Train

Lila and Ben are twins. They like to play with trains. They have a big train set in their room. They make the trains go fast and slow. They make the trains stop and go. One day, they see a real train outside. It is very big and loud. It has steam coming out of it. The steam is white and hot. Lila and Ben want to see the train up close. They run to the window and look. But their mom is there. She says, "No, no, you can't go outside. The train is not safe. It can hurt you. You have to stay inside. You can play with your train set. It is safe and fun." Lila and Ben are sad. They want to see the steam. They want to hear the train. They want to touch the train. But they listen to their mom. They know she loves them. They know she wants them to be safe. They go back to their room. They play with their train set. They pretend their trains have steam. They make the steam with their mouths. They blow and puff. They have fun. They are safe. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little red barn. In the barn, there lived a big cow and a small cat. They were good friends. One sunny day, the cow said, "I want to wander outside and see the world." The cat said, "I want to come too!" So, they both went outside to wander together. They were very independent and happy. As they wandered, they saw a big tree. The cow said, "Let's rest under the tree." The cat agreed, and they both sat down. They talked and laughed, feeling happy and free. After a while, they went back to the little red barn, feeling more independent than ever.

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to learn how to whistle. He asked his mom, "Mom, can you teach me how to whistle?" His mom said, "Yes, I can teach you. But you must remember, whistling is important for us birds to talk to each other." Tim and his mom went outside to practice whistling. They tried and tried, but Tim couldn't whistle. He was sad. "Don't worry," said his mom, "we will try again tomorrow." The next day, they went outside to try again. This time, Tim tried very hard and finally, he could whistle! His mom was so happy and proud. She said, "Great job, Tim! You did it! Now, let's have a treat to celebrate." They went inside and had a yummy treat together.

Tom and Lily were brother and sister. They liked to play in the park with their ball and their bike. One day, they saw a shiny crown on a bench. It was gold and had jewels on it. "Wow, look at that crown!" Tom said. "It is so pretty. I want to wear it." "No, Tom, that is not ours. Someone else left it here. Maybe they will come back for it. We should not touch it." Lily said. She was a good girl and knew how to share. But Tom did not listen. He ran to the bench and grabbed the crown. He put it on his head and said, "I am the king of the park. I can do whatever I want." Lily was sad and angry. She said, "Tom, that is not nice. You are being selfish and greedy. You should give the crown back. It does not belong to you." But Tom did not care. He rode his bike around the park and shouted, "I am the king. Move out of my way. This is my park." Soon, a man came to the park. He was looking for something. He was the owner of the crown. He was a magician who had a show in the park. He had left his crown on the bench by mistake. He was very alert and saw Tom wearing his crown. "Hey, kid, that is my crown. Give it back to me. It is part of my costume. I need it for my show." The man said. Tom was scared. He did not want to give the crown back. He tried to ride away, but the man was faster. He caught Tom and took the crown from him. He was very angry and said, "You are a bad kid. You should not take things that are not yours. You could have ruined my show. You should be ashamed of yourself." Tom felt sorry. He knew he had done wrong. He said, "I am sorry, sir. I was foolish. I thought the crown was cool. I wanted to be the king. But I was not the king. I was a thief. Please forgive me." The man was still mad, but he saw that Tom was sincere. He said, "Well, I accept your apology. But you have to learn your lesson. You have to respect other people's things. You have to be honest and kind. You have to be a good kid. Do you understand?" Tom nodded. He said, "Yes, sir. I understand. I will never do that again. I will be a good kid. Thank you for forgiving me." The man smiled. He said, "You are welcome. Now, go and play with your sister. And maybe you can watch my show later. I will show you some magic tricks. But remember, the real magic is in your heart." Tom ran to Lily. He hugged her and said, "I am sorry, Lily. You were right. I was wrong. I should have listened to you. You are a good sister. Can we still play together?" Lily smiled. She said, "Of course, Tom. I love you. You are a good brother. Come on, let's play. And maybe we can watch the magic show later. It sounds fun." Tom and Lily played happily in the park. They learned their lesson. They learned to be honest, kind, and respectful. They learned to share and care. They learned to be good kids. And they had fun. The end.
