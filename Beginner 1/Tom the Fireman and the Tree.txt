Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Fireman and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tom. Tom liked to help people. He had a big red truck that would go fast when there was a fire. Tom wore a big hat and a long coat to stay safe. One day, the sun was shining very bright. Tom saw a big tree with sour apples. He wanted to pick some for his friends at the fire station. But the apples were too high up in the tree. Tom had an idea. He used his big red truck to help him reach the sour apples. He picked many apples and shared them with his friends. They all smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" The firemen ate the sour apples and felt happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. It was a dry, sunny day. Sue loved to play on the swings and slide. She also liked to watch the birds and the bugs. While Sue was playing, she saw a stranger. The stranger was a big, friendly dog. The dog came up to Sue and said, "Hello, do you want to play with me?" Sue did not understand the dog's words, but she knew the dog wanted to play. Sue asked her mom, "Can I play with the dog?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Sue and the dog played together in the park. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Sue said goodbye to the dog. She hoped to see the dog again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a prince who lived in a big castle. The prince loved to be clean. Every day, he would take a bath and put on his clean clothes. One sunny day, the prince went outside to play. He saw a little girl playing with her ball. She looked happy and was enjoying her game. The prince walked up to her and said, "Hi! Can I play with you?" The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, of course! I would like that." So, the prince and the little girl played with the ball all day. They laughed and enjoyed their time together. At the end of the day, they were both tired but very happy. From that day on, the prince and the little girl became best friends. They always played together and enjoyed their time in the sun. And every night, the prince would take a bath to be clean again, ready for another fun day with his new friend.

Once there lived a lazy bird in a big tree. The bird had big wings, but it rarely flew. Every day the bird slept in the tree and ate waffles. One day, the bird was feeling brave. It spread its wings and flew. It was so happy that it soared high in the sky. It was the highest it had ever gone! Suddenly, the bird saw a giant hawk. The bird was scared, so it tried to fly down. But it was too late. The hawk grabbed the bird in its claws. The bird screamed, "Help me! Please let me go!" But the hawk just flew away with the bird. The lazy bird was never seen again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a lucky boy. He had many toys and a big garden to play in. One day, Tom found a rock in his garden. It was a very special rock. It had a mineral inside. Tom showed the rock to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow! That is a cool rock! Can I have it?" Tom did not want to give his rock away. But he wanted to be a good friend, so he said, "Okay, I will yield it to you." Sam took the rock and said, "Thank you, Tom!" But then, Sam did not play with Tom anymore. He only played with the rock. Tom was sad. He had lost his lucky rock and his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, while playing, he saw a big tree. He walked to the tree and found a map. The map showed a treasure! Tim wanted to find the treasure, so he asked his friend, Sam, to help. "Sam, will you help me find the treasure?" Tim asked. Sam nodded and said, "Yes, I will help you, Tim!" Together, they followed the map. They walked and walked until they found a big hole. Inside the hole, they saw a box. But there was a bad smell coming from the hole. "Ew, what is that bad smell?" Sam asked. Tim didn't know. Tim and Sam held their noses and got the box. They opened it and found the treasure! It was full of shiny gold coins. They were so happy they found the treasure and shared it with their friends. The bad smell went away, and they all played together.
