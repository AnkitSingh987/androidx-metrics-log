Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bug and the Tree

One day, a small bug named Tim wanted to fly high in the sky. He had tight wings, but he tried very hard. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to reach the tip of it. Tim asked his friend, Bee, "Can you help me fly high?" Bee said, "Yes, I can help you. Hold on tight to me, and we will fly together." So, Tim held on tight to Bee, and they flew up to the tip of the tree. Tim was so happy he could fly high. They played together in the sky and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max was a big dog who loved to lead his friends to the stadium to play. All the dogs liked to follow Max because he was a good leader. But there was one dog named Tim who was jealous of Max. Tim wanted to be the leader too. One day, when Max was leading the dogs to the stadium, Tim decided to play a trick. Tim went a different way and called the other dogs to follow him. Some dogs followed Tim, but most dogs stayed with Max. Tim was happy that he had some dogs to lead, but he was still jealous of Max. When they got to the stadium, Max and the dogs that stayed with him had a great time playing. But Tim and the dogs that followed him got lost. Tim was sad and scared. He wished that he had not been jealous of Max and had followed him to the stadium. Tim and the lost dogs never found their way back, and they missed all the fun at the stadium.

Lily liked to read books. She had many books on her bookshelf in her room. She liked to look at the pictures and make up stories in her head. One day, she wanted to read a new book. She saw a big book on the top of the bookshelf. She wanted to get it, but it was too high for her. She got a chair and climbed on it. She reached for the book, but it was heavy and rough. Her mom saw her and said, "Lily, be careful! That book is not for you. It is for grown-ups. It has hard words and no pictures. And it is too heavy for you. You might fall and hurt yourself. Come down from the chair and I will help you find a nice book for you." Lily did not listen to her mom. She wanted the big book. She pulled it hard, but it did not move. She pulled harder, but then the bookshelf shook. The books started to fall. Lily was scared. She let go of the book and tried to get off the chair, but it was too late. The bookshelf tipped over and fell on her and the chair. Lily cried out loud. Her mom ran to her and lifted the bookshelf off her. She hugged her and said, "Lily, are you okay? I told you to be careful. You should have listened to me. I was trying to warn you. You could have been hurt very badly. You are lucky you only have a few scratches. Next time, please do what I say. I love you and I want you to be safe." Lily was sorry. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I was wrong. I should have listened to you. You love me and you know what is best for me. I don't want the big book anymore. I want a nice book for me. Can you help me find one?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily. I'm glad you are okay. Let's clean up this mess and then we can read a nice book together. How about this one? It has cute animals and fun rhymes. Do you like it?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, mom. I like it. Thank you for helping me. I love you, mom." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lily. Now let's read and have some fun."

Once upon a time, there was a helpful cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with string. He would have so much fun with it. He would chase it, jump on it, and carry it around in his mouth. One sunny day, Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because her red balloon had floated away. Tom wanted to help Sue feel happy again. He had an idea! Tom would use his string to get the balloon back. Tom tied one end of the string to his tail and the other end to a big rock. He jumped as high as he could and grabbed the red balloon with his paws. The string helped Tom pull the balloon back down to the ground. Sue was so happy to have her red balloon back. She hugged Tom and thanked him for being so helpful. From that day on, Tom and Sue became the best of friends, and they always had fun playing together with the string.
