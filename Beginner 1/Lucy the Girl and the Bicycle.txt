Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bicycle

One day, a little girl named Lucy got a new bicycle. She was very happy. The bicycle was red and shiny. Lucy wanted to ride her bicycle right away, but it was difficult. She did not know how to ride a bicycle. Lucy's mom and dad helped her. They held the bicycle while Lucy sat on it. She tried to pedal, but it was hard. Lucy did not give up. She tried and tried. Her mom and dad smiled at her. They knew she could do it. After a while, Lucy learned to ride her bicycle. She rode it up and down the street. She was so happy. Her mom and dad clapped and smiled. They were proud of Lucy. Riding a bicycle was not difficult anymore.

One day, a wild cat named Tom went outside to play. The big, round moon was up in the sky. Tom liked to look at the moon. It was very shiny and bright. Tom wished he could go to the moon. Tom saw his friend, a bird named Sue. Tom said to Sue, "I want to go to the moon. Can you help me?" Sue thought for a moment and said, "I can fly, but I cannot fly to the moon. It is too far away." Tom was sad, but he had an idea. He said to Sue, "What if we reverse and go backwards? Maybe we can reach the moon that way." Sue laughed and said, "That is a funny idea, Tom. But I do not think it will work." They tried to reverse and go backwards, but they did not reach the moon. They still had fun playing together. Even though they could not go to the moon, Tom and Sue were happy to be friends and play under the shiny moon.

Tim and Jen are friends. They like to play with toys and draw with crayons. One day, they find a note on the table. The note is black and has big letters. It says: "Do not touch the cake. It is for mom's birthday." Tim and Jen look at the cake. The cake is big and round. It has pink frosting and sprinkles. It smells very good. Tim and Jen want to eat the cake. "Let's have a bite," Tim says. "No one will mind." "No, Tim," Jen says. "The note says do not touch. We have to mind the note. It is for mom's birthday. We have to be good." "But I am hungry," Tim says. "And the cake looks yummy. Just a little bite. Come on, Jen." Tim reaches for the cake. Jen tries to stop him. They tug and pull. The cake falls off the table. It lands on the floor. It breaks into pieces. Tim and Jen are shocked. They hear mom coming. "Oh no," Tim says. "What have we done?" Mom sees the cake on the floor. She sees the note on the table. She sees Tim and Jen with frosting on their faces. She is very sad and angry. "Tim and Jen," mom says. "Why did you not mind the note? Why did you touch the cake? It was for my birthday. You have ruined it. You have been very bad." Tim and Jen feel sorry. They cry and say sorry. They hug mom and say they love her. They say they will never touch the cake again. They say they will mind the note next time. Mom hugs them back. She says she loves them too. She says she forgives them. She says they have to learn to mind the note and be good. She says they can help her make a new cake. She says they can have a slice after dinner. Tim and Jen are happy. They help mom make a new cake. They mind the note and be good. They have a slice of cake after dinner. They wish mom a happy birthday. They learn their lesson.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a zip line. Tim wanted to try it. His mom said, "Hold on tight, Tim!" Tim zipped down the zip line, and it was so fun! He went fast like a bird. When he got to the end, he saw a photo on the ground. It was a photo of a big, delicious cake. Tim showed it to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! Can we make this cake?" They went home and started to make the cake. They mixed and stirred all the things they needed. When the cake was done, they put it on the table. But then, a big wind came through the window and blew the cake away! Tim and his mom were so surprised. They laughed and said, "Let's make another cake, but this time, we'll close the window!"
