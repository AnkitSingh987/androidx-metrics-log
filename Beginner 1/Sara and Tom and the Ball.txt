Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom and the Ball

Sara and Tom like to play catch with their ball. The ball is smooth and red. They throw the ball to each other and catch it with their hands. They laugh and have fun. One day, they play catch in the park. The park is big and green. There are many trees and flowers. There are also many people and animals. Sara and Tom have to be careful not to hit anyone with their ball. They see a big pond in the park. The pond has water and ducks. The ducks swim and quack. Sara and Tom think the ducks are cute. They want to play catch near the pond. They go to the pond and throw the ball to each other. They are not quiet. They shout and giggle. The ducks hear them and get scared. They flap their wings and fly away. Sara and Tom do not see the ducks. They only see the ball. They want to catch the ball. Tom throws the ball to Sara. Sara runs to catch the ball. She does not look where she is going. She trips and falls into the pond. The pond is deep and cold. Sara cannot swim. She screams and splashes. Tom sees Sara in the pond. He is scared and cries. He does not know what to do. No one hears Sara and Tom. No one helps them. Sara and Tom are in trouble. The ball floats on the water. The ball is smooth and red. The end.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big, red button. She did not know what the button did. Sue felt very curious. She wanted to show her friend, Tom. Sue went to Tom's house and said, "Look at this big, red button I found!" Tom looked at the button and said, "Wow! It's so big and red! What does it do?" Sue did not know, but she wanted to find out. Sue and Tom acted silly and pretended the button could do many things. They acted like the button could make them jump high, run fast, and even fly! They laughed and played all day. In the end, they still did not know what the button did, but they had a lot of fun using their imaginations.

Once upon a time, there was a little pony named Tiny. Tiny loved to run and play in the sun. One day, Tiny met a big, hard rock. The rock said, "Hi, Tiny! Do you want to play with me?" Tiny said, "Yes, let's play!" But as Tiny ran around the rock, he tripped and fell. "Oh no!" said Tiny. "I break my leg!" Tiny was sad and scared. Just then, a kind bird flew down from the sky. The bird had a magic gift. The bird touched Tiny's leg and it was not broken anymore! Tiny was so happy. He said, "Thank you, kind bird!" And they all played together in the sun.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her loyal dog, Spot. They loved to play together. At the park, Mia saw a swing. She wanted to swing high in the sky. Spot barked and wagged his tail, happy to see Mia having fun. Mia said, "Spot, watch me swing!" Spot sat down and watched Mia. As Mia was swinging, she saw a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his toy from the shelf at home. He could not find it anywhere. Mia stopped swinging and asked Tim, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "I lost my toy from my shelf. I can't find it." Mia wanted to help Tim. She said, "My dog Spot is good at finding things. He can help you find your toy." Spot barked and wagged his tail, ready to help. Together, Mia, Spot, and Tim went to Tim's house. Spot sniffed around and found the toy under the bed. Tim was so happy, and he thanked Mia and Spot. They all became good friends and went back to the park to play.

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day long. In the evening, when the sun went down, Spot would go home to his family. One day, Spot's mom told him to behave when they went to visit their friends. Spot promised to be a good dog. He wagged his tail and barked happily. At their friend's house, Spot played with the other dogs. They all had so much fun. Spot remembered to behave and was a good dog. In the end, everyone had a great evening.
