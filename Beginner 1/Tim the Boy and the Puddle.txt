Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Puddle

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with water. Every day, he would choose to play in the bathtub. He liked the water because it was wet and fun. One day, Tim went to the bathtub to play. But when he turned on the water, it did not come out. The bathtub was dry! Tim was sad because he could not play with the water. Then, Tim had an idea. He went outside to find a big puddle. He found a big puddle and played in it. The puddle was wet and fun, just like the bathtub. Tim was happy, and he played in the puddle all day.

Lily liked to play with her bear. She called him Brownie. He was soft and cuddly and had a red bow. Lily took him everywhere. She slept with him, ate with him, and read with him. One day, Lily and her mom went to the park. Lily brought Brownie with her. She wanted to show him the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. She thought he would have fun. Mom said to be careful and not lose him. Lily put Brownie on the bench while she went to the slide. She slid down fast and laughed. She ran back to the bench to get Brownie. But he was not there. Lily looked around. She saw a big boy holding Brownie. He was pulling his ears and his bow. He was laughing too, but not in a nice way. Lily felt scared and angry. She ran to the boy and shouted, "Give me back my bear! He is mine!" The boy looked at her and sneered. He said, "No, he is mine now. He is a stupid bear. I will rip him apart." He pulled harder on Brownie's ears. Lily did not know what to do. She wanted to cry. She wished mom was here. She thought the boy was harsh and mean. She thought he would hurt Brownie. She loved Brownie. She had to save him. She saw a stick on the ground. She picked it up and swung it at the boy. She hit him on the arm. He dropped Brownie and yelled. He ran away, rubbing his arm. Lily grabbed Brownie and hugged him. She checked his ears and his bow. They were a little torn, but not too bad. She said, "I'm sorry, Brownie. I'm sorry he was harsh to you. I love you. You are my best friend." She kissed him on the nose. Mom came over and saw what happened. She praised Lily for being brave and smart. She said, "You did the right thing, Lily. You stood up for yourself and your bear. You are a good girl." She fixed Brownie's ears and bow with a safety pin. She said, "He will be fine. He is a strong bear. He loves you too." She hugged Lily and Brownie. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, mom. Can we go home now?" Mom said, "Yes, we can. Let's go home and have some cookies and milk. And maybe read a story with Brownie." Lily said, "Yes, please. That sounds good." She held Brownie tight and walked with mom. She thought she was happy. She thought Brownie was happy too.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a thin stick in her yard. She liked the stick and wanted to attach it to her toy car. Lily tried to push the stick onto the car, but it would not stay. She felt sad and did not know what to do. Lily's big brother, Tom, saw her trying to attach the stick. He wanted to help her. Tom found some tape and put it on the stick. Then, he pressed the stick onto the car with the tape. The stick stayed on the car, and Lily was happy. But then, a big wind came and blew the stick off the car. Lily was sad again. Tom knew they needed something stronger to attach the stick. He found some glue and put it on the stick. This time, when they attached the stick to the car, it stayed. Lily was very happy, and they played with the car and the stick all day.
