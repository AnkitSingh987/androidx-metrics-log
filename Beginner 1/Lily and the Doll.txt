Title: Lily and the Doll

Lily liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls with long hair, short hair, curly hair and straight hair. She liked to comb their hair and make them look pretty. She wanted to be a barber when she grew up. One day, she saw a new doll in the toy store. It had the most beautiful hair she had ever seen. It was shiny and soft and had many colors. Lily wanted that doll very much. She asked her mom if she could buy it, but her mom said no. They had no money for a new doll. Lily was sad. She thought of a bad idea. She decided to steal the doll. She waited until her mom was busy looking at something else. Then she grabbed the doll and ran out of the store. She did not see the man who worked at the store. He saw her and shouted, "Hey, stop! You can't take that doll!" Lily was scared. She ran faster, but the man was faster too. He caught up with her and took the doll from her hands. He was angry and said, "You are a naughty girl. You should not steal things that are not yours. That is wrong. Where is your mom?" Lily started to cry. She felt sorry for what she did. She said, "I'm sorry, mister. I just wanted the doll. It has such nice hair. I want to be a barber and make it look pretty. Please don't be mad at me." The man looked at Lily. He saw that she was a little girl who loved dolls. He felt sorry for her too. He said, "I understand, but you still can't steal. That is not fair to the other kids who want the doll too. And it is not fair to me who works hard to sell the dolls. You have to ask your mom or save your money to buy the doll. Do you understand?" Lily nodded. She said, "Yes, mister. I understand. I'm sorry. Can I say sorry to the doll too?" The man smiled. He was a cheerful man who liked kids. He said, "Sure, you can say sorry to the doll. And you can give it a hug too. But then you have to give it back to me, okay?" Lily said, "Okay." She hugged the doll and said, "I'm sorry, doll. You are very pretty. I hope you find a good home. Maybe I will see you again someday." She gave the doll back to the man. She said, "Thank you, mister. You are very nice. I will not steal again. I will be a good girl." The man said, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm glad you learned your lesson. You are a good girl. And you have a good dream. Maybe you will be a great barber someday. But for now, you have to go back to your mom. She must be worried about you." Lily said, "Yes, mister. I have to go. Bye, doll. Bye, mister." She ran back to the store and found her mom. She hugged her mom and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I did something bad. But I learned my lesson. I love you, mom." Her mom hugged her back and said, "I love you too, Lily. What did you do? Tell me everything." Lily told her mom everything that happened. Her mom was sad and angry and happy and proud. She said, "Lily, you should never steal. That is very bad. But I'm glad you said sorry and learned your lesson. And I'm glad you have a dream. You are a good girl. And I'm proud of you." Lily smiled. She said, "Thank you, mom. You are the best mom. Can we go home now?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can go home now. But first, let's go and say thank you to the man who works at the toy store. He was very kind to you. And maybe, if you are lucky, he will let you play with the doll for a little while." Lily said, "Really? That would be nice. Let's go, mom." They went to the toy store and said thank you to the man. He was happy to see them. He said, "You're welcome, Lily. You are a brave girl. And you have a good mom. And yes, you can play with the doll for a little while. But then you have to give it back to me, okay?" Lily said, "Okay, mister. Thank you, thank you, thank you."
