Category: Beginner
Title: Buddy the Loyal Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. He loved his family very much. Every day, Buddy would play with the kids and make them laugh. They would throw a ball, and Buddy would run and bring it back. The kids were so happy. One day, Buddy heard a noise outside. He went to see what it was. He saw a big cat in the yard. The cat was trying to take the kids' ball. Buddy didn't like that. He barked at the cat and said, "Go away! This ball is for my family." The cat listened to Buddy and ran away. Buddy brought the ball back to the kids. They were so proud of him. They hugged Buddy and said, "We love you, Buddy. You are the best dog ever." Buddy wagged his tail and knew he was loved.

Tom and Sam were friends who liked to play with toys. One day, Tom saw Sam's new truck and wanted it very much. He asked Sam, "Can I have your truck? It is big and red and makes noise." Sam liked his truck and did not want to give it away. He said, "No, this is my truck. You have your own toys. Go and play with them." Tom felt angry and jealous. He thought of a way to get the truck. He said, "How about we trade? You give me your truck and I give you my teddy bear. It is soft and cuddly and you can sleep with it." Sam was not very smart and did not know what trade meant. He thought Tom was being nice and wanted to share. He said, "OK, we can trade. Here is my truck. Give me your teddy bear." Tom smiled and took the truck. He gave Sam his teddy bear, but it was old and dirty and had a hole in it. Sam did not notice and hugged the teddy bear. He said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend." Tom ran away with the truck and played with it. He felt happy and proud. He did not care about Sam or his teddy bear. Soon, it was time for lunch. Tom and Sam went to their homes. Tom's mom made him a sandwich and a glass of milk. She said, "Eat well, Tom. You must be hungry after playing so much." Tom was not hungry. He had eaten a lot of candy before playing. He said, "No, thank you, mom. I am not hungry. I just want to play with my truck." Tom's mom was surprised and worried. She said, "But you need to eat, Tom. Candy is not good for you. It will make you sick and spoil your teeth. Please eat your sandwich and drink your milk." Tom did not listen. He said, "No, mom. I don't want to. Leave me alone." He ran to his room with his truck. Tom's mom was sad and angry. She said, "Fine, Tom. Do what you want. But don't come to me when you feel bad." Tom played with his truck for a long time. He made it go fast and loud. He did not care about anything else. He did not notice that he was feeling tired and sick. He did not notice that his mouth was dry and his stomach was hurting. He did not notice that his teeth were hurting and his gums were bleeding. He only noticed when he could not play anymore. He felt very bad and weak. He wanted to cry and call his mom. He wanted to eat and drink. He wanted to sleep and cuddle. He looked at his truck and did not like it anymore. He looked at his teddy bear and missed it very much. He wished he had not traded it with Sam. He wished he had been a good friend and a good son. He tried to get up and go to his mom, but he could not. He fell on his bed and closed his eyes. He yawned and whispered, "I'm sorry, mom. I'm sorry, Sam. I'm sorry, teddy bear." But no one heard him. No one came to help him. He was alone and sick and sad. The end. Moral: Don't be greedy and selfish. Be kind and honest. Share and care. Eat well and listen to your mom.
