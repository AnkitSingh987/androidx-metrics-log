Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to gather nuts and seeds for his family. He would use his mouth to hold as many nuts as he could find. One sunny day, while Sam was out with his empty bag, he met a small bird named Bella. Bella was sad because she didn't have any food. Sam wanted to help Bella, so he said, "Don't worry, Bella. I will gather some nuts for you too!" Sam and Bella went around the forest, looking for nuts and seeds. They filled their bags and their mouths with yummy food. As they gathered more and more, they became good friends. At the end of the day, Sam and Bella went back to their homes. They shared their food with their families and told them about their new friend. From that day on, Sam and Bella always gathered food together and were never sad or hungry again.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to play outside. He put on his shoes and ran to the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim saw his friend, Sally, and they started to play catch. While they were playing, they felt the ground shake. "What was that?" asked Tim. "I don't know," said Sally. They were scared, but they did not know that it was an earthquake. The ground shook again, and they decided to go home. The next day, Tim and Sally went back to the park. They saw that the earthquake had changed the park. Some trees had fallen, and the grass was not as green. But they were still eager to play. They picked up their ball and started to play catch again, happy to be together.

Once upon a time, there was a woman named Jane. She had an incredible pet cat named Fluffy. They lived in a small house near a big tree. One day, Jane heard a loud scream from outside. She ran out and saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared because a big dog was barking at him. Jane said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." Jane walked up to the big dog and said, "Shoo, dog! Go away!" The big dog stopped barking and went away. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Jane! You are incredible!" Jane and Fluffy smiled, happy to help their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a clean violin. It lived in a big house with a girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with the violin. One day, Sue saw a new toy and started to play with it. She began to ignore the clean violin. The clean violin was sad. It wanted Sue to play with it again. But Sue did not want to play with the clean violin. She liked her new toy more. The clean violin felt very bad. One day, the clean violin was so sad that it broke. Sue did not care. She just played with her new toy. The clean violin was never played again. The end.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to visit their grandma and grandpa. Grandma and grandpa live in a big house with a garden. They have a dog named Spot. Spot is black and white. He has a long strip on his back. One day, Sara and Ben go to visit their grandma and grandpa. They bring a ball and a book. They say hello to Spot. Spot is happy to see them. He wags his tail and licks their faces. He wants to play with the ball. "Can we play with Spot, grandma?" Sara asks. "Of course, dear. But be careful. He is very loud. He barks a lot. He might scare the birds," grandma says. Sara and Ben nod. They take the ball and go to the garden. They throw the ball and Spot runs after it. He catches it and brings it back. He barks and jumps. He is loud. Sara and Ben laugh. They have fun. But then, Spot sees a cat. The cat is on the fence. It is gray and fluffy. It has green eyes. Spot does not like the cat. He drops the ball and runs to the fence. He barks and growls. He is very loud. He tries to catch the cat. "Spot, no!" Sara and Ben shout. They run after him. They want to stop him. But the cat is fast. It jumps from the fence to the roof. It looks down at Spot. It hisses and spits. It is not afraid. Spot is angry. He jumps and scratches the fence. He is very loud. He makes a hole in the fence. He goes through the hole. He chases the cat. "Spot, come back!" Sara and Ben shout. They go through the hole. They follow him. Grandma and grandpa hear the noise. They come out of the house. They see the hole in the fence. They see Sara and Ben running. They see Spot and the cat on the roof. "Oh, no!" grandma and grandpa say. They are worried. They call Sara and Ben. They call Spot. They want them to come back. But Sara and Ben do not hear them. They are too far. They are on the street. They see Spot and the cat on the roof of another house. They see a ladder. They climb the ladder. They want to get Spot and the cat. But the ladder is old. It is weak. It breaks. Sara and Ben fall. They land on a pile of leaves. They are not hurt. But they are scared. They cry. Spot and the cat hear them. They stop fighting. They look at them. They feel sorry. They come down from the roof. They go to them. They lick them. They are not loud. They are quiet. Sara and Ben hug them. They are happy. They are not scared. They stop crying. Grandma and grandpa find them. They are relieved. They hug them. They are glad. They are not angry. They smile. They take them back to the house. They fix the fence. They give them cookies and milk. They read them the book. They are not loud. They are calm. Sara and Ben say thank you. They say sorry. They say goodbye. They love their grandma and grandpa. They love Spot and the cat. They had a good visit. They will come back soon.
