Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park to have some fun. She saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. But there was a problem. A big boy named Tom was holding the ball and would not give it to her. Mia asked Tom, "Can I please play with the ball too?" Tom said, "No, it's mine!" Mia was sad and wanted to find a way to get the ball. She thought of an easy plan. She saw a big tree and decided to climb it. Mia climbed the tree and waited for Tom to come close. When Tom was under the tree, Mia jumped down and squeezed the ball out of his hands. Tom was so surprised! He said, "Wow, that was fun! Let's play together." They both laughed and played with the ball for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Mia had a favorite toy named Mr. Bunny. Mr. Bunny was big and soft. Mia loved to play with Mr. Bunny all the time. One day, Mia saw a pretty vase on a high shelf. It was far away and hard to reach. Mia had an idea. She said to Mr. Bunny, "Let's get the vase!" Mia and Mr. Bunny tried to get the vase, but it was too high. They needed help. Mia's dad saw them trying to reach the vase. He said, "Wait, Mia! I will rescue the vase for you." Mia's dad got a chair and climbed up. He got the vase and gave it to Mia. Mia was so happy. She said, "Thank you, dad!" Mia and Mr. Bunny played with the vase and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to smile and play all day. He was always enthusiastic and full of energy. One sunny day, Spot decided to go outside and do some exercise. He ran around the yard, jumped high in the air, and played with his favorite red ball. Spot was having so much fun, he couldn't stop smiling. After a while, Spot's friend, a little bird named Tweet, saw him playing. Tweet joined in the fun, and together they exercised and played all day long. Both Spot and Tweet were so happy and smiling, they knew they would be best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a good dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his tie. He would run and jump with it in his mouth. Spot's job was to protect his house and his family. One day, Spot heard a noise outside. He saw a big bird trying to take his tie. Spot barked, "No, bird! That is my tie!" The bird flew away, but it dropped the tie on the roof of the house. Spot was sad. He did not know how to get his tie back. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl came to help. She said, "Don't worry, Spot. I will help you get your tie back." The little girl climbed up a tree and got the tie from the roof. She gave it back to Spot. Spot was so happy! He wagged his tail and thanked the little girl. From that day on, Spot and the little girl were best friends. They played together every day and Spot continued to protect his house and his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a toy car. The toy car was very sad because it was dead. It could not move anymore. The owner of the toy car was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved his toy car very much. One day, Tim's mom saw the dead toy car. She wanted to make Tim and the toy car happy again. So, she decided to restore the toy car. She fixed the wheels and painted it with bright colors. When Tim saw his toy car was alive again, he was so happy! He played with the toy car all day long. The toy car was happy too. The moral of the story is that we can bring happiness to others by fixing what is broken and taking care of our things.
