Category: Beginner
Title: The Chair and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little chair that lived in a big room. The chair had a hurt leg, so it couldn't stand up straight. It felt sad because it wanted to help people sit down. One day, a small boy came into the room. He saw the hurt chair and wanted to fix it. The boy urged his mom to come and help him. "Mom, please help me fix the chair," he said. His mom came over and looked at the chair. She saw the hurt leg and knew just what to do. Together, the boy and his mom worked hard to fix the chair's hurt leg. After a while, the chair was all better! The chair was so happy that it could now help people sit down. The boy and his mom were proud of their work, and the chair thanked them with a big smile.

Anna and Ben are playing in the garden. They see a big peach tree with many ripe peaches. They want to pick some and eat them. "Look, Ben, peaches!" Anna says. "They are round and soft and sweet. Let's get some." "OK, Anna," Ben says. "But how do we get them? They are too high." Anna thinks for a moment. She sees a big stick on the ground. She picks it up and says, "I have an idea. I can use this stick to hit the peaches. Then they will fall down and we can get them." Ben nods and says, "Good idea, Anna. Let me help you." They take turns to hit the peaches with the stick. Some peaches fall down, but some stay on the tree. They hit harder and harder. But they hit too hard. The stick breaks the branches and the leaves. The peaches get smashed and bruised. The juice splashes on their clothes and faces. The tree looks sad and hurt. "Oh no, Anna, look what we did!" Ben says. "We destroyed the peach tree. We are bad." Anna looks at the peach tree and feels sorry. She says, "You are right, Ben. We destroyed the peach tree. We are bad. What can we do?" They hear a voice behind them. It is Mom. She is angry. "What are you doing, Anna and Ben?" Mom says. "Why did you destroy the peach tree? That was very naughty. The peaches were for everyone. Now they are ruined. And you are all dirty and sticky. Come here, now." Anna and Ben are scared. They drop the stick and run to Mom. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We wanted to eat some peaches. But we hit them too hard. We didn't mean to destroy the peach tree. Please don't be mad at us." Mom sighs and says, "I am mad at you, Anna and Ben. You should have asked me first. You should have been gentle with the peaches and the tree. They are living things. They feel pain. You should have respected them. Now you have to clean up the mess and apologize to the tree. And you have to go to your room and think about what you did. No more peaches for you today." Anna and Ben nod and say, "We are sorry, Mom. We will clean up and say sorry to the tree. We will go to our room and think. We will be good next time." Mom takes them to the house and gives them a rough towel to wipe their faces and hands. She says, "I hope you learned your lesson, Anna and Ben. Don't destroy things that are not yours. Be kind and careful with nature. And always ask before you take something. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben say, "We understand, Mom. We are sorry. We love you." Mom says, "I love you too, Anna and Ben. But you still have to go to your room. And no more peaches for you today." Anna and Ben go to their room and sit on their bed. They look at each other and say, "We are sorry, peach tree. We are sorry, peaches. We are sorry, Mom. We will be good next time."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a big country with her mom, dad, and dog. Amy loved to perform. She would sing and dance for her family. They would clap and cheer for her. One day, Amy was very impatient. She wanted to perform in front of her friends. So, she called her friends and asked them to come to her house. When her friends came, they all sat down to watch her perform. Amy started to sing and dance. But then, something unexpected happened. Her dog jumped up and started to perform too! The dog was dancing and spinning around. Everyone was surprised and laughed. Amy's dog became a part of her show, and they all had a great time.
