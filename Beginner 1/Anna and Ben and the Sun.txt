Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Sun

Anna and Ben are in school. They like to learn new things and play with their friends. Today, they have a special lesson. The teacher says they will draw on the big blackboard. Anna and Ben are very excited. They love to draw. They want to show their teacher and their friends what they can do. They hurry to the blackboard and pick up some chalk. They start to draw. Anna draws a big sun. She makes it yellow and bright. She says, "Look, this is the sun. It is hot and makes us happy." Ben draws a big tree. He makes it green and brown. He says, "Look, this is the tree. It is tall and gives us shade." The teacher smiles. She says, "Very good, Anna and Ben. You are very creative. I like your drawings." Anna and Ben are happy. They say, "Thank you, teacher. We like to draw." They look at their drawings. They are proud of themselves. They hug each other. They are good friends.

One day, a kind dog named Spot went for a walk in a big field. Spot liked to run and play in the field. The sun was warm, and the grass was soft. Spot was very happy. As Spot ran, he heard a small sound. He stopped and said, "What's that? I need to listen." He heard the sound again. It was a tiny bird. The bird was lost and could not find its mom. "Hi, little bird," said Spot. "I will help you find your mom." The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, kind dog." They walked and listened for the bird's mom. At last, they found the bird's mom. She was so happy to see her baby. "Thank you, kind dog," she said. Spot was happy to help. He waved goodbye and ran back to play in the field.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, a girl named Lily lived with her mom. Her mom loved to knit with yarn. She made hats, socks, and scarfs to keep Lily warm. One day, Lily found a pretty flower in the garden. It was starting to bloom. She was so happy and wanted to show her mom. She ran inside the house with the flower. Lily showed her mom the blooming flower. Her mom smiled and said it was lovely. She took some yarn and made a tiny hat for the flower. They put the hat on the flower, and it looked very cute. They laughed and had a fun day together.

Ben and Lily liked to play near the old mill. The mill had a big wheel that turned in the water. They liked to watch the water splash and the wheel spin. But they knew they had to be careful. The water was deep and fast, and the wheel was strong and sharp. One day, they saw a duck in the water. The duck was swimming near the wheel. Ben and Lily wanted to say hello to the duck. They went closer to the water. But the ground was wet and slippery. Ben lost his balance and slipped. He fell into the water. He was scared and cold. He tried to swim, but the water pushed him to the wheel. He felt helpless. Lily saw Ben fall. She was scared too. She wanted to help Ben. She ran to the mill. She saw a big rope hanging from the roof. She thought maybe she could use the rope to pull Ben out. She grabbed the rope and swung it to Ben. She shouted, "Ben, catch the rope!" Ben saw the rope. He reached for it. He was lucky. He caught the rope. Lily pulled the rope hard. She pulled Ben away from the wheel. She pulled him to the shore. She hugged him. She was happy. Ben was happy too. He thanked Lily. He said, "Lily, you saved me. You are a good friend." They decided to leave the mill. They decided to play somewhere safer. They walked back to their home. They told their mom what happened. Their mom was glad they were okay. She said, "Ben and Lily, you are brave and smart. But you have to be careful. The mill is dangerous. Don't play near the mill again." They nodded. They agreed. They learned their lesson. They never played near the mill again.
