Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat tasty treats. One day, she found a big file in her room. She did not remember where it came from. Lily opened the file and saw many pictures. There were pictures of tasty cakes, cookies, and ice cream. She showed the file to her mom and asked, "Mom, can we make these tasty treats?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Lily. Let's pick one and make it together." They picked a tasty cake and made it. Lily was so happy to eat the tasty cake with her mom. They put the file away, so they could remember to make more treats another day.

Tom and Sam were brothers. They liked to play in the garden. One day, they saw a big red flower. It looked pretty and smelled sweet. "Can we pick it?" Tom asked. "No, we can't," Sam said. "Mom said it is poison. It can make us sick or hurt us. We have to care for it and leave it alone." But Tom did not listen. He wanted to touch the flower. He thought it would be warm and soft. He reached out his hand and pulled the flower. "Ow!" Tom cried. He dropped the flower. His hand was red and itchy. He felt bad. "Tom, what did you do?" Sam asked. He saw the flower and Tom's hand. He was scared. He ran to get Mom. Mom came and saw what happened. She was angry and sad. She took Tom inside and put some cream on his hand. She said he had to stay in bed and drink some water. She said he was lucky it was not worse. "Tom, why did you pick the flower?" Mom asked. "I told you it was poison. You have to listen and obey. You have to care for yourself and the plants. They are alive and they have feelings too." Tom felt sorry. He said he was sorry. He said he would not do it again. He said he would care for the flower and the garden. He hugged Mom and said he loved her. Mom smiled and hugged him back. She said she loved him too. She said he would be fine soon. She said she would care for him and Sam. She kissed him and said good night. She turned off the light and left the room. Tom closed his eyes and tried to sleep. He still felt bad, but he also felt warm. He knew Mom and Sam cared for him. He hoped the flower would forgive him. He dreamed of a better day.

Once upon a time there was a bright little girl. She was so full of energy, she just wouldn't delay! One day she decided to explore her new world. She went outside and ran to the park with her arms out wide. When she arrived she saw a big tree with a special surprise. It was a big yellow slide! She got onto the slide, lifted up her arms and went down, again and again! After playing in the park, she started to get hungry. She wanted to go back home, but then she saw something that made her stop. It was a big yellow bird with a bright yellow beak. She wanted to stay and play but time was ticking and she decided it would have to delay for another day. So, she ran home and soon, dinner was on the table. And after dinner all she wanted was a big warm hug from her mom, and that is just what she got!

One day, a little dog named Max found a stick in the park. He was very happy. Max loved to play with sticks. He picked up the stick with his mouth and started to run. Max saw a big cat named Tom. Tom was sitting under a tree. Max wanted to play with Tom, so he ran to him with the stick. Max said, "Tom, let's play! Look at my stick!" Tom looked at Max and the stick. He said, "That's a fake stick, Max." Max was sad. He didn't want a fake stick. Max dropped the stick and started to walk away. Suddenly, Max saw a real stick on the ground. He was very happy again. Max picked up the real stick and ran back to Tom. He said, "Tom, I found a real stick! Let's play!" Tom smiled and got up. Max and Tom played with the stick all day. They would spring up and run after the stick together. Max and Tom had lots of fun playing with the real stick.
