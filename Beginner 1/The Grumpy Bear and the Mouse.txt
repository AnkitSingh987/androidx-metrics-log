Category: Beginner
Title: The Grumpy Bear and the Mouse

Once upon a time there was a grumpy bear. He had a big, shiny jewel that he wanted to keep safe. One day, he heard some yummy noises from his cave. He went to have a peek, and saw a tiny mouse nibbling on some food. The bear was angry, so he tried to scare the mouse away. The little mouse was scared and ran into a corner. But the bear was too quick! He quickly grabbed the mouse with his big, furry paws. He bared his sharp teeth and tried to bite the mouse. The little mouse was able to escape and run away. The grumpy bear was still angry and he stamped his foot. Then he took a big bite of his lovely jewel, and chomped away until it was all gone. The grumpy bear was very sad. He learned his lesson, and kept his jewel safe after that. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy dog named Max. Max liked to play with his board. He would push the board with his nose and then run to get it. Max and his board were always together. One sunny day, Max was playing with his board in the park. He pushed the board very far away. When Max went to get it, he saw a big tree. Max heard a soft voice say, "Come, come!" Max looked up and saw a little bird in the tree. The bird said, "I need help! My wing hurts and I can't fly." Max wanted to help the bird, so he put the board under the tree. The bird hopped onto the board. Max carried the bird to his home where his mom could help. Max's mom fixed the bird's wing. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max! I have a secret for you. I am a magic bird!" The magic bird gave Max a special gift. Now, Max's board could fly in the air! Max and his flying board had lots of fun playing together.

Ben and Mia are playing in the yard. They have a big box of toys. They want to organize the toys by color. Ben likes red toys and Mia likes blue toys. "Look, I have a red hammer!" Ben says. He holds the hammer in his hand and pretends to hit the box. "No, no, no!" Mia says. "That is not how you organize. You have to put the toys in piles. Red toys here, blue toys there. Like this." She shows Ben how to sort the toys. Ben thinks Mia is very smart. He tries to copy her. But he is not very careful. He drops the hammer on his foot. "Ow, ow, ow!" he cries. He hops on one foot and holds the other. Mia feels sorry for Ben. She puts down her blue doll and runs to him. She gives him a hug. "Are you okay, Ben?" she asks. Ben nods. He smiles at Mia. "I'm okay, Mia. Thank you. You are very kind. And very graceful. You organize so well." Mia smiles back. She is happy that Ben is not hurt. And that he likes her organizing. She picks up the hammer and gives it to him. "Here, you can have the hammer. It is red. And you can organize with me. But be careful, okay?" Ben takes the hammer. He is glad that Mia is his friend. He says, "Okay, Mia. I will be careful. And I will organize with you. You are the best." They hug again and go back to the box. They organize the toys by color. And they have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a sock. The sock was fine and Tim loved it so much. He would rub the sock every day. It made him feel happy. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He took his fine sock with him. Tim played on the swings, and then played in the sand. He put his sock down on the bench while he played. After playing, Tim and his mom went home. Tim forgot his fine sock at the park. He was very sad. His mom said they could go back to look for it, but it was too late. The sock was gone. Tim cried a lot because he missed his fine sock. He could not rub it anymore. He tried to find a new sock, but it was not the same. Tim learned to be more careful with his things. But he never found his fine sock again.
