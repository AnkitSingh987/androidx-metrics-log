Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Lily and her daddy went to the park. Lily saw a big water spray. She was so excited! She asked her daddy if they could play in the spray. Daddy said yes, and they ran to the water. Lily and her daddy played in the water spray. They laughed and splashed each other. Lily was very happy. But then, daddy looked confused. He saw a big red ball near the water spray. He didn't know whose ball it was. Daddy picked up the big red ball. He looked around to find who it belonged to. A little boy came running to them. He said the ball was his. Daddy gave the ball back to the little boy. The little boy said thank you and ran away to play. Lily and her daddy went back to playing in the water spray, happy and smiling.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks. They have many blocks of different colors. Red, blue, yellow, green, and more. One day, they decide to build a big tower with their blocks. They take turns to put one block on top of another. They try to make the tower very tall and strong. "Look, Lily, our tower is so high!" Ben says. He points to the top of the tower. "Yes, Ben, it is very high. And it has many colors. It is so pretty!" Lily says. She smiles and claps her hands. But then, a pale boy comes to their playroom. He is new in the school. He does not know Lily and Ben. He sees their tower and he wants to play with it. He walks to the tower and pushes it with his hand. Crash! The tower falls down. The blocks scatter on the floor. Lily and Ben are sad and angry. They cry and shout. "Hey, why did you do that?" Ben asks the pale boy. He frowns and crosses his arms. "That is not nice. That is our tower. We worked hard to build it." Lily says to the pale boy. She wipes her tears and picks up some blocks. The pale boy looks at Lily and Ben. He feels sorry. He did not mean to make them cry. He did not know they were playing with the tower. He just wanted to join them. "I'm sorry, Lily and Ben. I did not know. I just wanted to play with you. Can I help you build the tower again?" He says to them. He holds out his hand and smiles. Lily and Ben look at each other. They see that the pale boy is not mean. He is just lonely. They decide to forgive him and be his friends. "Okay, you can help us. But you have to be careful. And you have to ask first." Ben says to the pale boy. He takes his hand and shakes it. "Yes, you can help us. And you can choose some colors. What is your name?" Lily says to the pale boy. She gives him some blocks and hugs him. "My name is Sam. Thank you, Lily and Ben. You are very nice. I like your colors. Can I have some green?" Sam says to them. He smiles and hugs them back. They nod and give him some green blocks. Then they start to build the tower again. Together, they make it even higher and stronger and prettier than before. They are happy and they have fun. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a light bunny named Hope. Hope loved to eat pears. One day, Hope went to the pear tree to get her favorite food. When she got there, she saw a big, yummy pear at the very top of the tree. Hope wanted the pear so much, but she was too small to reach it. She tried to jump, but she could not get it. Hope did not give up. She asked her friend, a tall giraffe, to help her. The giraffe reached up with his long neck and got the pear for Hope. She was so happy! They sat down and shared the yummy pear together.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bird in a tree. The bird has many colors and a long tail. It is not an ordinary bird. "Hello, bird!" Lily says. "What is your name?" The bird looks at Lily and Ben. It opens its mouth and makes a sound. The sound is funny. It sounds like a car horn. "Beep beep!" the bird says. Lily and Ben are surprised. They look at each other and laugh. The bird laughs too. It sounds like a baby. "Ha ha!" the bird says. Lily and Ben think the bird is silly. They want to talk to it more. They ask it questions. "Where do you come from, bird?" Ben asks. "Beep beep!" the bird says. "What do you like to eat, bird?" Lily asks. "Ha ha!" the bird says. Lily and Ben do not understand the bird. But they do not mind. They like the bird. They think it is funny and cute. They laugh with the bird. The bird laughs with them. They have fun in the park.
