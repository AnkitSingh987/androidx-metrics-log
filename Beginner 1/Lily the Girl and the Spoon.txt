Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to wash the big spoon. Lily was happy to help. She turned on the water and made sure it was mild, not too hot or too cold. She washed the spoon very well. After washing the spoon, Lily's mom said, "Thank you, Lily! Now we can make dessert." Lily was excited because she loved dessert. They decided to make a yummy fruit salad with lots of sweet fruit. Lily helped her mom cut the fruit and put it in a big bowl. When the fruit salad was ready, Lily and her mom sat down to eat. They enjoyed the mild and sweet taste of the fruit. Lily was proud that she helped her mom make dessert. They both had big smiles on their faces as they ate the yummy fruit salad together.

One day, a little bird was very tired. It wanted to rest on a branch. The bird saw a clear branch on a big tree. It flew to the tree and sat on the branch. A big squirrel came and said, "This is my branch. Go away!" The bird was sad and said, "I just want to rest. Can I stay here for a little while?" The squirrel thought for a moment and said, "Okay, you can stay. But you must help me find nuts later." The bird agreed and they both rested on the branch. They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, wanted to race to the bedroom. They both loved to play and run around the house. Tim's bedroom was smelly because he forgot to clean it. But that did not stop them from having fun. As they started to race, they heard a funny noise coming from the bedroom. They stopped and looked at each other, feeling a little scared. But they wanted to find out what was making the noise, so they went into the smelly bedroom together. When they opened the door, they saw a big pile of toys moving around! The toys were alive and cleaning the bedroom for Tim. Tim and Spot were very surprised but happy. They joined the toys in cleaning up the smelly bedroom, and soon it was clean and nice again. After that, Tim, Spot, and the toys had a big race around the house, laughing and playing together.

One day, a shy crab went for a jog on the beach. The sun was bright and the sand was warm. The crab liked to jog by the water and feel the waves on his feet. As the crab jogged, he saw a big fish. The fish said, "Hello, shy crab! Do you want to play with me?" The crab was scared, but he wanted to make a new friend. So he said, "Yes, I want to play with you." The crab and the fish played together in the water. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish turned into a little girl! She said, "I am a magic girl. Thank you for playing with me, shy crab. Now we are friends forever." The shy crab was so happy to have a new friend, and they played on the beach all day.

Sam and Jen are friends. They like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make a race track with cardboard and tape. "Let's race our cars!" Sam says. "The fastest car wins!" "OK!" Jen says. "But how do we know who is faster?" Sam thinks for a moment. He sees a big clock on the wall. He has an idea. "We can use the clock!" he says. "We can start when the clock says zero, and stop when it says ten. Then we can count how many times our cars go around the track. The car with more times is faster!" Jen nods. She likes Sam's idea. They choose their cars and put them on the track. They wait for the clock to say zero. "Ready, set, go!" Sam shouts. They push their cars with their hands. They make vroom-vroom noises. They watch their cars zoom around the track. Sam's car is red. It is big and shiny. It has a lot of speed. It goes around the track very fast. Jen's car is blue. It is small and cute. It has a lot of stickers. It goes around the track very slow. Sam sees that his car is faster than Jen's car. He smiles. He thinks he will win. Jen sees that her car is slower than Sam's car. She frowns. She thinks she will lose. She looks around. She sees a yellow car on the floor. It is available. It is not on the track. She has an idea. She picks up the yellow car. She adds it to her blue car. She makes a double car. She pushes the double car with both hands. She makes louder vroom-vroom noises. She watches the double car zoom around the track. The double car has more speed than the red car. It goes around the track faster. It catches up with the red car. It passes the red car. Sam sees that the double car is faster than his car. He gasps. He thinks he will lose. Jen sees that the double car is faster than Sam's car. She cheers. She thinks she will win. The clock says ten. They stop pushing their cars. They count how many times their cars go around the track. Sam's car goes around the track four times. Jen's double car goes around the track five times. "Wow!" Sam says. "You win! How did you do that?" "I added a yellow car to my blue car!" Jen says. "It made my car faster!" "That's so cool!" Sam says. "Can I try?" "Sure!" Jen says. "Let's race again!" They swap their cars. They wait for the clock to say zero. They race again. They have fun. They are friends.
