Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Safe

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Mia. She was three years old and had lots of questions. One day Mia asked her mom "What is a question?". Her mom said to her, "A question is when you ask someone something". Mia was curious, so she decided to go explore. She let her mom know she was going for a walk and her mom said it was alright as long as she stayed safe. Mia went outside and noticed a few birds chirping in the trees. She asked them, "What are you saying?" The birds looked at her and flew away. Then Mia saw a bee buzzing around. She asked the bee, "Where are you going?" The bee just flew away too. Mia was disappointed that no one answered her questions. Suddenly, she heard her mom's voice yelling her name. Mia ran back home safe and happy. Her mom was there with a big hug to give her. Mia was so glad to be home and had learned that it's best to stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very alert. He liked to watch birds and play with his toys. He also loved to drink juice. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a big bottle of juice. Tim wanted to own the juice. He asked his mom, "Can I have the juice, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have the juice." Tim was very happy. He took the juice home and drank it. The juice was sweet and tasty. Now, Tim felt even more alert. He played with his toys all day long.

One day, a boy named Tom went for a walk. He saw a dull, long trail in the woods. He wanted to see where it went. So, Tom went on the trail. He was very happy. Tom met a dog on the trail. The dog said, "Do you want to be my friend?" Tom said, "Yes! I accept!" They both walked on the trail together. They knew they would find something fun. As they walked, they saw a big box. Tom and the dog looked at each other. They did not know what was inside. They opened the box, and out came a cat! The cat said, "Thank you for letting me out!" Now, Tom, the dog, and the cat were all friends. They walked on the trail together. They had a very fun day in the woods.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had cars, dolls, blocks and books. They were having fun and making a lot of noise. "Ben, Lily, time for dinner!" Mom called from the kitchen. "Come and wash your hands!" Ben and Lily did not want to stop playing. They liked their toys and they were not hungry. "Can we play a little more, Mom?" Ben asked. "Please, Mom, just five more minutes?" Lily begged. Mom sighed. She knew they were having fun, but they also had to eat. She decided to give them a chance. "Okay, you can play a little more, but only a little. Don't delay, or your food will get cold. And don't make a mess, or you will have to clean it up." Mom said. Ben and Lily smiled and thanked Mom. They promised to come soon and not make a mess. They went back to playing with their toys. But they forgot their promise. They played for a long time and made a big mess. They spilled juice on the carpet, threw blocks on the sofa, and tore pages from the books. They did not hear Mom calling them again and again. Mom came to the living room and saw the mess. She was angry and sad. She had cooked a nice dinner for them, but they did not care. They had delayed too much and made a mess. "Ben, Lily, what have you done?" Mom asked. "Look at this mess! You did not listen to me. You did not come for dinner. You did not respect your toys. You have to clean this up right now!" Ben and Lily looked at Mom and felt sorry. They knew they had done wrong. They had not been nice to Mom or their toys. They had not been good children. "We are sorry, Mom." Ben said. "We are very sorry, Mom." Lily said. They got up and started to clean the mess. They picked up the blocks, the dolls, and the cars. They put them back in their boxes. They taped the pages back to the books. They tried to wipe the juice from the carpet. But the carpet was still wet and sticky. They needed something else to clean it. "Mom, can we use the mop?" Ben asked. "The mop? What for?" Mom asked. "To clean the carpet. It has juice on it." Lily said. Mom shook her head. She did not think the mop was a good idea. The mop was for the floor, not the carpet. The mop was big and heavy, not small and light. The mop would make the carpet more wet and dirty, not dry and clean. "No, you cannot use the mop. The mop is not for the carpet. The mop is for the floor. The mop is too big and heavy for you. The mop will make the carpet worse, not better. You have to use a towel and some soap. That is the right way to clean the carpet." Mom said. Ben and Lily nodded. They understood. They did not want to make the carpet worse. They wanted to make it better. They wanted to make Mom happy. They wanted to be good children. They got a towel and some soap from the bathroom. They went back to the living room. They rubbed the towel and the soap on the carpet. They worked hard and carefully. They cleaned the carpet as best as they could. They finished cleaning the mess. They looked at the living room. It was neat and tidy. They looked at Mom. She was smiling and proud. "Good job, Ben and Lily. You have cleaned the mess. You have learned your lesson. You have been good children. I am proud of you." Mom said. "Thank you, Mom. We love you, Mom." Ben and Lily said. They hugged Mom and kissed her. They felt happy and relieved. They had made a mistake, but they had fixed it. They had delayed, but they had done their duty. They had been bad, but they had become good. They went to the kitchen with Mom. They ate their dinner, which was still warm and delicious. They thanked Mom for the food. They said sorry for the trouble. They promised to be better next time. They went to bed with Mom. They brushed their teeth, put on their pajamas, and cuddled with Mom. They read a story, said a prayer, and said good night. They closed their eyes and fell asleep. They dreamed of their toys, their books, and their Mom. They dreamed of playing, reading, and loving. They dreamed of being good children.
