Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Flexible Snake and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a flexible snake named Sam. He lived in a big box. Sam was very good at bending and twisting his long body. One day, he met a bug named Ben. Ben was sad because his food was starting to rot. "Ben, why are you sad?" Sam asked. "My food is not good. It is rot," Ben said. Sam wanted to help his new friend. Sam thought and thought. He had an idea! "I will find good food for you, Ben!" Sam said. He used his flexible body to twist and turn through the grass. He found fresh food for Ben. Ben was so happy! "Thank you, Sam! You are a success!" Ben said. They both laughed and played. Sam and Ben were now best friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play at the beach. They make sand castles and look for shells. They also joke with each other. They say funny things and laugh. One day, they see a big boat on the water. It has many colorful flags and balloons. Tom and Mia think it is a party boat. They want to go on the boat and have fun. They find a small raft near the shore. It is old and has a hole. Tom says, "Let's use this raft to go to the boat. It will be a joke. The people on the boat will be surprised to see us." Mia says, "But the raft is not good. It can sink. The water is deep and cold. It is not safe." Tom says, "Don't be scared. It will be fine. It will be a joke. Come on, let's go." Tom and Mia get on the raft and push it to the water. They start to paddle with their hands. The raft moves slowly. The boat is far away. But then, the raft starts to leak. Water comes in. The raft sinks. Tom and Mia scream. They are wet and cold. They can't swim. They are scared. The people on the boat hear them. They look at them. They are not surprised. They are angry. They say, "What are you doing here? This is not a party boat. This is a pirate boat. You are not welcome here. Go away." But Tom and Mia can't go away. They are stuck in the water. They are in trouble. They wish they did not joke. They wish they stayed on the beach. They cry. The end.

Sara likes to cook with her mom. They make healthy food, like soup and salad and bread. Sara helps to wash the vegetables, stir the soup and cut the bread. She feels happy and proud. One day, a big storm comes. It is very loud and scary. The wind blows hard and the rain falls fast. Sara and her mom stay inside the house. They hear thunder and see lightning. Sara feels afraid and hugs her mom. Her mom says, "Don't worry, Sara. The storm will go away soon. Let's cook something yummy to cheer us up. How about some cookies?" Sara likes cookies. She nods and smiles. They go to the kitchen and find the ingredients. They need flour, sugar, butter, eggs and chocolate chips. Sara helps to measure, mix and shape the dough. They put the cookies in the oven and wait for them to bake. They smell good. The storm stops. The sun comes out. The cookies are ready. Sara and her mom eat some cookies and drink some milk. They feel warm and cozy. They say, "We love to cook!"

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play pretend. Today they are playing restaurant. Tim is the chef and Sam is the waiter. They have a staff of toys to help them. Tim prepares the food in the kitchen. He makes soup, salad, pizza and cake. He puts them on plates and gives them to Sam. Sam takes the plates to the table where their mom and dad are sitting. "Hello, welcome to our restaurant," Sam says. "What would you like to order?" Mom and dad look at the plates. They see that the soup is water, the salad is grass, the pizza is cardboard and the cake is mud. They think it is weird, but they play along. "I'll have the soup, please," mom says. "I'll have the salad, please," dad says. Sam smiles and gives them the plates. He goes back to the kitchen to get more food. But then he sees that Tim is eating the pizza and the cake. He is very hungry. "Hey, stop that!" Sam says. "That's for the customers!" "No, it's for me!" Tim says. "I made it, I can eat it!" They start to fight over the food. They pull and push and shout. The staff of toys falls on the floor. The plates break. The food spills. Mom and dad hear the noise. They go to the kitchen to see what is wrong. They see the mess and the boys. "What are you doing?" mom asks. "Why are you fighting?" "He ate the food!" Sam says. "He took the food!" Tim says. Mom and dad shake their heads. They are not happy. "Boys, boys, calm down," dad says. "This is not how you run a restaurant. You have to share and cooperate with your staff and your customers. And you have to use real food, not weird things." Tim and Sam look at each other. They feel sorry. They say sorry to mom and dad. They say sorry to their staff. They clean up the kitchen. They hug and make up. They decide to play something else. They play zoo. They have a staff of animals to help them. They prepare the cages and the food. They are careful not to eat the food or fight with the staff. They have fun. Mom and dad are proud.
