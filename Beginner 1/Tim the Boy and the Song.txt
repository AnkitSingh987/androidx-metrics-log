Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Song

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big statue in the middle of the park. Tim looked at the statue and said, "Wow, that's big!" As they walked closer, a little bird sat on top of the statue. The bird began to sing a happy song. Tim smiled and said, "I like that bird, Mom." His mom smiled too and said, "Yes, it's a nice bird." Suddenly, a loud noise made the bird fly away. Tim was scared and screamed, "What was that?" His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, it was just a car. The bird is safe." Tim felt better and they continued to play in the park.

Ben and Lily were going on a trip with their mom and dad. They were very excited to fly on a big plane and see a new place. They packed their bags and went to the airport. When they got on the plane, Ben and Lily looked out the window and saw the clouds and the sky. They felt like they were flying too. They asked their mom and dad many questions about the trip. "Where are we going?" "How long will it take?" "What will we do there?" Mom and dad smiled and answered their questions. They told them they were going to a city with many buildings and people. They said it would take a few hours to get there. They said they would stay in a hotel and visit some parks and museums. Ben and Lily were happy and curious. They wanted to see everything. They played with their toys and read some books on the plane. They also ate some snacks and drank some juice. But then, something bad happened. Lily felt sick in her tummy. She told her mom she needed to go to the bathroom. Mom took her by the hand and walked to the back of the plane. But before they got there, Lily threw up on the floor. She felt very bad and ashamed. She started to cry. Mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. It's not your fault. Sometimes people get sick on planes. It's normal. Don't worry. We will clean it up and you will feel better soon." Lily nodded and wiped her tears. She said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I'm sorry, Ben. I'm sorry, everyone." Mom said, "It's okay, Lily. Everyone understands. Ben is not mad at you. He loves you. And so do I. And so does Dad. And so do the people on the plane. They are nice and kind. They will help us." Lily smiled a little and said, "Thank you, Mom. I love you too. And I love Ben. And I love Dad. And I love the people on the plane. They are nice and kind." Mom and Lily went to the bathroom and cleaned up. Then they went back to their seats. Ben gave Lily a hug and a kiss. He said, "I'm glad you're okay, Lily. You're my best sister. I can't wait to see the hotel with you. It will be fun." Lily said, "Thank you, Ben. You're my best brother. I can't wait to see the hotel with you too. It will be fun." Mom and Dad smiled and said, "We're proud of you, Ben and Lily. You're brave and sweet. We love you very much. We're almost there. Are you ready to see the city?" Ben and Lily said, "Yes, we are. We're ready to see the city. We're ready to have fun. We're ready to fly."

Lily was very happy. She was going to visit her grandma and grandpa. They lived in a big house with a garden and a dog. Lily loved to play with the dog and pick flowers in the garden. She ran to her room and started to pack her bag. She put in her favorite dress, her teddy bear, her book and her toothbrush. She zipped up her bag and dragged it to the door. "Mummy, I'm ready!" she shouted. Mummy came to her room and smiled. "You are very fast, Lily. But did you forget something?" she asked. Lily looked at her bag and thought hard. What did she forget? She had her clothes, her toy, her book and her toothbrush. She shook her head. "No, I have everything, Mummy." Mummy bent down and gave her a hug. "You are a brilliant packer, Lily. But you forgot one thing. You forgot to say goodbye to your room." Lily looked around her room and saw her bed, her toys, her pictures and her lamp. She felt a little sad. She liked her room. It was cozy and warm and full of fun. She ran to her bed and gave it a kiss. She ran to her toys and gave them a hug. She ran to her pictures and gave them a wave. She ran to her lamp and gave it a wink. "Goodbye, room. I will miss you. But I will come back soon. I love you, room." she said. Mummy took her hand and led her to the car. "That was very nice, Lily. Your room loves you too. And so do I. And so do grandma and grandpa. Are you ready to go?" Lily nodded and smiled. She was ready to go. She was excited to see grandma and grandpa and the dog and the garden. She was happy. She climbed into the car and buckled her seat belt. "Let's go, Mummy!" she said.
