Category: Beginner
Title: The Owl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big owl. The owl lived in a tree. The owl was tough. He could fly very high and very fast. He had big eyes that could see in the dark. One day, the owl saw a little bird. The little bird was sad. The owl asked, "Why are you sad?" The little bird said, "I can't find my mom." The owl wanted to help the little bird. So, the owl and the little bird looked for the mom together. They looked up and down, left and right. They looked in the trees and on the ground. The owl and the little bird could not find the mom. They both let out a big sigh. Just then, they heard a sound. It was the mom! She found them! The owl, the little bird, and the mom were all happy. From that day on, they were all good friends.

One day, a funny cat named Zip went on a walk. Zip liked to run fast and jump high. He had a big smile and a fluffy tail. Zip was a happy cat. On his walk, Zip found a toy. It was a red ball with a long string. He had not seen a toy like this yet. Zip played with the toy for a long time. He would pull the string and the ball would bounce. Zip laughed and had so much fun. After a while, Zip got tired. He took the toy home to show his friends. They all wanted to play with the new toy. Zip and his friends played until the sun went down. They all had a very fun day.

One day, a little bird named Tim was worried. He wanted to soar high in the sky like his friends, but he didn't know how. His mom saw him looking sad and asked, "Tim, why are you worried?" Tim said, "I want to soar like my friends, but I don't know how." His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, I will teach you." The next day, Tim's mom started to teach him how to soar. She showed him how to flap his wings and catch the wind. Tim tried and tried, but he was still worried. "I can't do it, Mom," he said. His mom told him, "You can do it, Tim. Just keep trying and believe in yourself." Tim took a deep breath and tried again. This time, he started to soar high in the sky. He was so happy! From that day on, Tim loved to soar with his friends. He was no longer worried because his mom taught him how to fly. And Tim knew that with practice and belief, he could do anything.

One day, a little cat named Kitty went for a walk. She walked to the west, where she saw a big, dirty puddle. Kitty thought it looked fun to jump in, so she did. She got very dirty and wet. Kitty met a dog named Doggy. Doggy saw how dirty Kitty was and said, "Kitty, you should not play in dirty puddles. It can make you sick." But Kitty did not listen and kept playing in the puddle. That night, Kitty went to sleep. She did not feel good. She was cold and her fur was still dirty. In the morning, Kitty was very sick. Her mom said, "Kitty, you should have listened to Doggy. Now you are sick because you played in the dirty puddle." The moral of the story is to listen to good advice from friends and stay away from dirty things that can make you sick.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and pet cat, Whiskers. Lily liked to help her mom and dad with chores. One day, she saw a cobweb in the corner of the room. Lily wanted to move the cobweb, so she got a broom. She asked her mom if she could help. Her mom said yes, so Lily reached up high with the broom. The cobweb stuck to the broom, and Lily moved it away from the corner. After the cobweb was gone, Lily saw Whiskers playing with a toy. She felt happy that her house was clean. She was a thoughtful girl who loved to help her family. And they all lived happily ever after.
