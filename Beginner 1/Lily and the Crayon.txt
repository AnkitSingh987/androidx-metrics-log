Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Crayon

Lily loved to draw with her crayons. She drew flowers, stars, and animals on her paper. Sometimes she drew on the wall, too. She liked how the colors looked on the white wall. But her mom did not like it. She said it was bad to draw on the wall. She said Lily would be punished if she did it again. One day, Lily found a new crayon. It was black and shiny. She wondered how it would look on the wall. She sneaked into the living room and started to draw a big cat on the wall. She made the cat's eyes, nose, and whiskers. She was happy with her drawing. But then she heard her mom's footsteps. She was coming back from the store. Lily felt fearful. She knew her mom would be angry when she saw the cat on the wall. She tried to hide the crayon behind her back. She hoped her mom would not notice. But her mom did notice. She saw the black print on the wall. She saw the crayon in Lily's hand. She was very angry. She said, "Lily, what did you do? You know you are not allowed to draw on the wall. This is very naughty. You will be punished for this. Go to your room and stay there until I say you can come out." Lily started to cry. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to draw a cat. I like cats. Please don't punish me." But her mom did not listen. She took Lily to her room and closed the door. Lily felt sad and scared. She wished she had not drawn on the wall. She wished she had listened to her mom. She learned her lesson. She never drew on the wall again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big log. Tim wanted to complete a task. The task was to make the log fit in a small box. Tim pushed and pushed the log. It did not fit in the box. He was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The log opened and a toy car came out. Tim was happy. The toy car was his favorite. He played with the car all day. The log was not just a log, it was a toy box. Tim had a fun day at the park.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They were having fun. But then Lily saw a big hill. She wanted to climb it. She said to Ben, "Come on, let's go up the hill. It will be fun." Ben was not sure. He said, "But it looks high and steep. What if we fall?" Lily said, "Don't be scared. We can do it. Follow me." She ran to the hill and started to climb. She was fast. Ben followed her, but he was slow. He was scared. Lily reached the top of the hill. She was happy. She looked around and saw the sky and the trees and the birds. She said, "Wow, this is so pretty. Come on, Ben, hurry up." But Ben was not happy. He was still climbing. He was tired and sweaty. He said, "Lily, wait for me. This is hard." He tried to go faster, but he stumbled. He tripped on a rock and fell. He rolled down the hill. He hurt his knee and his elbow. He cried. Lily heard him cry. She looked down and saw him. She felt sorry. She said, "Oh no, Ben, are you okay?" She ran down the hill to help him. She saw his knee and his elbow. They were red and bleeding. She said, "Oh, Ben, you are hurt. I'm sorry. I should have waited for you." She hugged him and kissed his knee and his elbow. She said, "It will be okay. Let me get you a band-aid." She looked around and saw a bench. She saw a lady sitting on the bench. She had a bag. Lily said, "Excuse me, lady, do you have a band-aid? My friend is hurt." The lady smiled and said, "Of course, dear. Here you go." She gave Lily a band-aid. She said, "What happened to your friend?" Lily said, "We were climbing the hill, but he stumbled and fell. He tripped on a rock and an ant." The lady said, "An ant? How did he trip on an ant?" Lily said, "I don't know. Maybe the ant was fast." The lady laughed and said, "Maybe so. Well, I hope he feels better soon." Lily said, "Thank you, lady. You are nice." She took the band-aid and ran back to Ben. She put the band-aid on his knee and his elbow. She said, "There, now you are better. Do you want to play some more?" Ben said, "No, thank you. I want to go home. I don't like the hill. I don't like the rock. I don't like the ant." Lily said, "Okay, let's go home. I'm sorry, Ben. I won't make you climb the hill again. Next time, we can play something else. Something you like." Ben said, "Okay, Lily. Thank you. You are a good friend." They held hands and walked to their mom. They said, "Mom, we are ready to go home." Mom said, "Okay, kids. Did you have fun?" Lily said, "Yes, mom. We had fun. But Ben got hurt. He stumbled and fell. He tripped on a rock and an ant." Mom said, "An ant? How did he trip on an ant?" Lily said, "I don't know. Maybe the ant was fast." Mom said, "Maybe so. Well, I'm glad you are okay, Ben. Let me see your band-aid. Oh, that's a nice band-aid. Who gave it to you?" Lily said, "A nice lady gave it to us. She was sitting on the bench. She had a bag." Mom said, "That was very kind of her. You should say thank you to her." Lily said, "We did, mom. We said thank you. She was nice." Mom said, "That's good. Well, let's go home. You must be hungry and tired." Lily and Ben said, "Yes, mom. We are hungry and tired." They got in the car and went home. They had a snack and a nap. They dreamed of the park and the hill and the rock and the ant. The ant was fast.
