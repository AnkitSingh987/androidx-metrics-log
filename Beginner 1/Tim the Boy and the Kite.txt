Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Kite

One day, a kind boy named Tim had a kite. He loved to play with his kite in the park. The kite was big and red. It could fly high in the sky. Tim was happy when he played with his kite. One day, a bad man saw Tim's kite. He wanted to steal it. When Tim was not looking, the bad man took the kite and ran away. Tim was sad and cried, "My kite is gone! Who took my kite?" A girl named Sue saw what happened. She told Tim, "I saw the bad man take your kite. Let's find him and get your kite back." Tim and Sue looked for the bad man. They found him trying to fly the kite. Tim said, "Give me back my kite!" The bad man felt sorry and gave the kite back to Tim. Tim and Sue played with the kite together, and they became good friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. She saw a big slide and wanted to go on it. But she was scared of the high ladder. She did not know how to climb it. She saw a boy named Max on the slide. He was having fun. He saw Lily and smiled. He said, "Hi, I am Max. Do you want to play with me?" Lily nodded. She said, "Hi, I am Lily. I want to go on the slide, but I am scared." Max was helpful. He said, "Don't be scared. I will help you. You can hold my hand and we can climb together. It is easy and fun." Lily trusted Max. She held his hand and they climbed the ladder. Lily was brave. She felt happy. They reached the top of the slide. They looked at the park. They saw the trees, the flowers, the birds, and the grass. The grass was green and soft. They said, "Wow, the park is beautiful." They smiled at each other. They slid down the slide. They felt the wind in their hair. They laughed. They landed on the grass. They rolled and played. They became friends. They talked and talked. They had a lot of fun. They thanked each other for being helpful. They hugged. They said, "Bye, see you soon." They went to their moms. They told them about their new friend. Their moms were proud of them. They said, "Good job, you are very kind and brave." They gave them a kiss. They went home happy.

Lily and Ben are playing with their toys on the rug. They like to make noises and pretend they are animals. Lily is a lion and Ben is a monkey. They roar and chatter and jump around. "Look at me, I'm a big lion!" Lily shouts. She shows her teeth and claws and chases Ben. "Ooh ooh, I'm a clever monkey!" Ben shouts. He swings from the sofa and throws a pillow at Lily. They laugh and have fun. They are happy. But then, Mom comes in. She is not happy. She sees the mess they have made. The rug is dirty and the pillow is torn. "Stop, stop, stop!" Mom shouts. She is angry. She points at the rug and the pillow. "Look what you have done! You have to clean up this mess!" Lily and Ben are scared. They stop playing. They say sorry to Mom. Mom sighs. She is not angry anymore. She loves Lily and Ben. She hugs them and says, "It's okay. I know you were having fun. But you have to be careful with your things. And you have to help me tidy up. Okay?" Lily and Ben nod. They say okay. They help Mom pick up the toys and the pillow. They wipe the rug with a cloth. They say thank you to Mom. Mom smiles. She is happy. She says, "You are good children. Now, how about a story?" Lily and Ben cheer. They love stories. They sit on the rug with Mom and listen to her read. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside all day. One day, he saw a beautiful sunset in the sky. He wanted to reach it, but he did not know how. Spot tried to jump high to touch the sunset. He jumped and jumped, but he could not reach it. He felt sad and wanted to quit. But then, he saw a big bird in the sky. The big bird saw Spot and asked, "Why are you sad?" Spot told the big bird about the sunset and how he could not reach it. The big bird said, "I can help you!" So, the big bird picked up Spot with its strong feet. They flew up high in the sky, closer to the sunset. Spot was so happy to see the sunset up close. He thanked the big bird for helping him. From that day on, Spot and the big bird became best friends. They played together every day, and Spot never quit trying new things again.
