Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big animal with a long horn. He liked to polish his horn every day. He was very proud of his shiny horn. One day, while he was polishing his horn, a little bird came to him. The bird wanted to help. The big animal was happy and let the bird polish his horn. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird polished the horn so well that it became too shiny. The sun shined on the horn, and the light went into the big animal's eyes. He could not see! The big animal was not proud anymore. He learned that sometimes, too much of a good thing can be bad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to count things all day. One day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, he saw a huge tree with many leaves. Tim wanted to count them all. "Mom, can I count the leaves on the tree?" Tim asked. His mom said yes, and Tim started to count. He counted and counted, but there were too many leaves. So, Tim decided to pick the leaves and put them in a big pile. As Tim picked the leaves, a squirrel came out of the tree. The squirrel was very angry because Tim was taking its home away. It started to yell at Tim, "Stop picking my leaves!" But Tim didn't listen and kept picking. The squirrel got so mad that it called all its friends. Together, they chased Tim and his mom away from the park. Tim was sad because he couldn't finish counting the leaves. From that day on, Tim learned not to pick leaves from trees.

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys in their room. But they have a problem. Their room is very messy. They have cars, blocks, books, and balls everywhere. Mom is not happy. "Boys, you need to clean your room. It is too messy. You can't find your toys. You can't walk. You can't play. You need to be organized," Mom says. Ben and Sam do not want to clean their room. They want to play. They say, "No, Mom. We like our room. We can find our toys. We can walk. We can play. We do not need to be organized." Mom is angry. She says, "If you do not clean your room, you will suffer. You will not get any dessert. You will not watch TV. You will not go to the park. You will stay in your room." Ben and Sam do not like that. They do not want to suffer. They want dessert. They want TV. They want the park. They say, "Sorry, Mom. We will clean our room. We will be organized." They start to clean their room. They put their cars in a box. They put their blocks in a bag. They put their books on a shelf. They put their balls in a basket. They make their bed. They sweep the floor. They are organized. Mom is happy. She says, "Good job, boys. You cleaned your room. You are organized. You do not suffer. You get dessert. You get TV. You get the park. You get a penny." Ben and Sam are happy. They say, "Thank you, Mom. We like our room. We like our toys. We like dessert. We like TV. We like the park. We like the penny." They hug Mom. They are organized. They do not suffer.

One day, a little red car was driving down the road. The car saw a big tree with a small bird on it. The bird looked worried. The car stopped and asked, "Why are you worried, little bird?" The bird said, "I can't find my friend. I waved to her, but she didn't wave back. Now, I don't know where she is." The car wanted to help the bird. So, they went on a trip to find the bird's friend. They drove and drove, but they couldn't find the friend. The bird was very worried. Then, they saw a big green car. The green car said, "I saw your friend! She is by the big lake." The little red car and the worried bird went to the big lake. At the lake, they saw the friend. The bird was so happy! The bird waved to her friend, and her friend waved back. The little red car smiled, happy to help the bird. They all played by the lake and had a fun day.
