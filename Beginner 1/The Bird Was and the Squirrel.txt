Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird Was and the Squirrel

One day, a little bird was in a tree. The bird was very small and helpless. It was learning to fly. The bird tried hard to fly, but it could not. It was sad and wanted to have fun with the other birds. The little bird tried to fly again. This time, it fell out of the tree. The fall was scary. The bird was very scared and cried. A kind squirrel heard the bird cry and came to help. "Are you okay?" the squirrel asked. "Yes," said the bird, "I just want to fly like the other birds." The squirrel wanted to help the bird. It showed the bird how to jump from branch to branch. The bird was happy and thanked the squirrel. They played together and became good friends.

Tim and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bird with a long neck and a big wing. The bird is white and gray. It is a goose. "Look, a goose!" Tim says. "Let's go and say hello." "OK, but be careful," Jen says. "Goose can be mean." Tim and Jen march to the goose. They say hello and try to pet it. The goose does not like that. It hisses and snaps at them. It bites Tim's hand. Tim cries. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tim says. "The goose bit me! It hurts!" "Run, run, run!" Jen says. "The goose is angry!" Tim and Jen run away from the goose. They run to the pond. They want to cross the bridge. But the bridge is wet. It rained last night. Tim slips on the bridge. He falls into the pond. He gets wet and cold. He cries more. "Help, help, help!" Tim says. "I'm wet and cold! I can't swim!" Jen tries to help Tim. She reaches for his hand. But the goose is still chasing them. It flies to the bridge. It flaps its big wing. It pushes Jen into the pond too. Jen gets wet and cold. She cries too. "Help, help, help!" Jen says. "We're wet and cold! We can't swim!" The goose laughs. It swims away. It is happy. It does not like Tim and Jen. It does not want them to play in the park. Tim and Jen are wet and cold. They can't swim. They cry and cry. No one helps them. They drown in the pond. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to organize her toys and eat popcorn. One day, Sue found a hidden box under her bed. She was very excited to see what was inside the box. Sue opened the box, and to her surprise, she found a lot of popcorn inside! She was so happy and decided to eat all the popcorn. But then, something unexpected happened. As she ate the popcorn, it started to grow bigger and bigger inside her tummy. Sue's tummy got so big that she couldn't move. She felt very sad and wished she didn't eat all the popcorn. The moral of the story is not to eat too much of something, even if you love it.

One day, a little boat named Bob wanted to sail on the big sea. But, Bob was scared. He had a tight wire holding him to the dock. He could not sail away with the wire on him. A big, friendly fish named Finn saw Bob was sad. Finn swam up and said, "Why are you sad, Bob?" Bob told Finn about the tight wire. "I want to sail, but I can't with this wire," said Bob. Finn had an idea. He used his strong tail to pull the wire off Bob. "Now you can sail, Bob!" said Finn. Bob was very happy. He sailed away, waving goodbye to his new friend Finn.

Once upon a time there was a boy and a girl who were best friends. One day, the boy and the girl wanted to go and pick some strawberries. They put on their hats and hurried out of their house. As they walked through the woods, the girl saw something shiny and wanted to pick it up. But it was dark and scary, so the boy said “No, we’d better hurry.” But the girl wanted to take the shiny cloth with her, so they picked it up and kept walking. Soon they reached the strawberry patch, but they were sad to find it empty. There was a monster in the patch! It was big, green, and very scary. The girl grabbed the shiny cloth and waved it at the monster. The monster was scared and ran away. The boy and girl were relieved and picked a lot of strawberries. Then they hurried home with their precious cargo!
