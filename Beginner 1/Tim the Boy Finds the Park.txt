Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Park

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. It was a peaceful day. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. At the park, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam had a funny haircut. Tim asked, "Sam, why is your haircut so funny?" Sam said, "I wanted a haircut, and I demanded my mom to do it. But she didn't know how." Tim thought for a moment. He said, "Let's go to my house. My mom can fix your haircut." Sam agreed, and they walked to Tim's house. At Tim's house, Tim's mom gave Sam a new haircut. Sam was very happy. He said, "Thank you!" Tim and Sam played in the peaceful yard all day.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a goal. They want to become big and strong. They run and jump and climb and swing. One day, they see a big tree. It has many sweet fruits. They are red and round and shiny. Lily and Ben want to eat them. But they are too high. They cannot reach them. "Let's try to get them," Lily says. "We can use a stick or a stone or a rope." "OK," Ben says. "But be careful. The tree is very tall and the fruits are very hard." They look for a stick or a stone or a rope. They find a stick. It is long and thin. Ben holds it. He tries to hit the fruits. But the stick is too weak. It breaks. They find a stone. It is big and heavy. Lily throws it. She tries to knock down the fruits. But the stone is too fast. It misses. They find a rope. It is long and strong. They tie it to a branch. They try to swing to the fruits. But the rope is too short. They cannot reach them. They are sad. They sit under the tree. They look at the fruits. They are still hungry. "Maybe we are too small," Lily says. "Maybe we need to grow more." "Maybe," Ben says. "But we are still friends. And we still have our goal. We will become big and strong one day." They hug each other. They smile. They are happy. They have each other. And they have their goal.

One day, a cat named Tom saw a dish on the floor. The dish had yummy food in it. Tom was very hungry, so he walked towards the dish to eat the food. As Tom walked, he did not see a toy on the floor. He stepped on the toy and slipped. "Ouch!" said Tom. "That was stupid of me not to look where I was going." Tom got up and tried to walk again. This time, Tom looked at the floor to make sure he did not slip again. But when he looked up, the dish was gone! A dog named Max had taken the dish and was eating the food. Tom was sad, but he learned to be more careful and to share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty little girl named Lucy. She had a small room with a bed, a chair, and a table. These were her furniture. One day, Lucy saw a tiny mouse in her room. The mouse was scared and started to tremble. Lucy wanted to help the mouse, so she gave it some food. The mouse was happy and stopped trembling. Lucy and the mouse became good friends. They played together every day. The mouse would hide in the furniture and Lucy would find it. They had lots of fun and lived happily ever after.

One day, Mommy said to David, “Let’s go to the shop. I need to buy food.” David was happy. He loved shopping with Mommy. At the shop, David saw a black bag on the floor. He asked Mommy, “Can I lift it?” Mommy smiled. “No,” she said. “It’s too heavy.” So they carried on shopping. Mommy bought lots of food. They put it be in a trolley and then they went to the checkout to pay. When they were done, Mommy lifted the trolley. “Let’s go home,” she said. David smiled. “Yay!” he said, and followed his Mommy out of the shop.
