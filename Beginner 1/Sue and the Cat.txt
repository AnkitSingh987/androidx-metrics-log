Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there was a small house with a red door. In the house, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys, but she was feeling lonely. She wanted to find a friend. One day, Sue saw a little cat outside her door. The cat was looking at her with big, green eyes. "Hello, cat," Sue said. "Do you want to come in and play with me?" The cat meowed and walked into the house. Sue and the cat played with toys all day. They laughed and had fun. Sue was happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Sue and the cat were always together. They played in the quiet little house with the red door, and they were never lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie was different from the other cows. She could understand what people said. Bessie liked to listen to the farmer and his wife talk. One day, Bessie heard the farmer's wife say, "We need more beef for dinner." Bessie did not know what beef was. She asked her friend, the horse, "Do you know what beef is?" The horse said, "I don't know. Let's ask the pig." So, Bessie and the horse went to the pig. The pig said, "Beef is what people eat. It comes from cows." Bessie was scared. She did not want to be beef. Bessie told her friends, "I don't want to be beef. I want to live and be happy." The horse and the pig wanted to help Bessie. They made a plan to hide Bessie so the farmer could not find her. The plan worked. Bessie hid in the woods and the farmer could not find her. The farmer's wife said, "We will have to eat something else for dinner." Bessie was safe and happy. She knew her friends loved her and would always help her.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a doll and Ben had a car. They were having fun until Lily saw Ben's cup on the table. It was blue and shiny and had a picture of a rocket on it. Lily wanted the cup very much. She put down her doll and walked over to the table. She reached for the cup, but Ben saw her and ran to stop her. "No, Lily, that's my cup!" he shouted. "You can't have it!" Lily frowned and pulled the cup towards her. "But I like it!" she said. "It's pretty and cool!" Ben tugged the cup back. "It's mine!" he said. "Mom gave it to me for my birthday!" They both held the cup and tried to take it from each other. They started to yell and cry. Mom heard the noise and came into the living room. She saw Lily and Ben fighting over the cup. She was not happy. "What's going on here?" she asked. "Why are you two fighting?" Lily and Ben stopped crying and looked at Mom. They both wanted to keep the cup, but they also did not want to get in trouble. Lily shrugged and said, "I don't know." Ben shrugged and said, "Me neither." Mom sighed and shook her head. She took the cup from their hands and put it on the shelf. "Listen, you two," she said. "You can't fight over things like this. You have to share and be nice to each other. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nodded. They felt sorry for making Mom upset. Mom smiled and hugged them. "That's good," she said. "Now, why don't you play something else? How about this puzzle? It has a graceful swan on it. Do you like swans?" Lily and Ben looked at the puzzle. It had many pieces and colors. It looked fun and challenging. They nodded again and smiled. They forgot about the cup and went to play with the puzzle. They helped each other and said sorry for fighting. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max went to the park with his mom. He was very glad to go to the park because he could play with his ball there. At the park, Max saw a big dog. The big dog wanted to play with Max's ball too. Max was scared and started to tremble. He did not want to share his ball with the big dog. Max's mom saw him tremble and came to help. She picked up Max and the ball. Max was happy and felt safe with his mom. They went home and Max played with his ball all day. Max was glad that his mom was there to help him.
