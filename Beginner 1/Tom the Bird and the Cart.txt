Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Bird and the Cart

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Tom. Tom loved to fly high in the sky. One day, while flying, Tom saw a cart. It was a big and red cart. Tom wanted to play with the cart. Tom went down and tried to push the cart. But the cart was too big for him. Tom felt a little stupid. He wanted to find a way to play with the cart. Tom thought and thought. Then he had a great idea! Tom called his bird friends to help him. They all came and tried to fly with the cart. They used their strong wings to lift the cart up in the sky. Tom and his friends were very happy. They learned that they could do anything if they worked together.

Tom and Anna were friends who liked to play with toys. One day, Tom saw a shiny red car that Anna had. He wanted it very much. He went to Anna and said, "Give me your car. It is mine now." Anna was shocked and said, "No, Tom. This is my car. You have your own toys. You cannot take mine." Tom was angry and said, "I demand your car. I am bigger and stronger than you. If you do not give me your car, I will hit you." Anna was scared and said, "Please, Tom. Do not be mean. We are friends. We can share our toys. You do not have to hit me." Tom did not listen. He was foolish and greedy. He grabbed the car from Anna and pushed her to the ground. Anna cried and hurt her knee. She felt a pain in her heart. Tom ran away with the car, but he did not see a big rock on the path. He tripped and fell. The car flew out of his hand and broke into pieces. Tom also cried and hurt his head. He felt a pain in his heart too. He looked at the broken car and the crying Anna. He realized he had made a big mistake. He had lost his friend and his toy. He was sorry, but it was too late. Anna did not want to play with him anymore. She went to her mom and hugged her. Tom was alone and sad. He wished he had been kind and not foolish.

Once upon a time, there was a big room full of animals. They all wanted to go outside and play, but the teacher said no. The teacher stood in front of the blackboard and urged the animals to stay inside. They all wanted to play and have fun, but the teacher wanted them to be safe. So, the animals sat quietly in their seats, waiting for the teacher to change her mind. But, instead of playing, the teacher asked them to draw a picture of a peaceful landscape. The animals all grabbed their crayons and chalk, and got to work. They drew beautiful green trees, yellow suns, and blue oceans. When they were all done, the teacher smiled. The animals were so proud of what they had created. They thanked the teacher and finally, they were allowed to go outside and play.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely bird. This bird had big wings, but it did not know how to fly. The bird lived in a big tree with other birds. The other birds would fly, but the lonely bird would just watch. One day, the lonely bird decided to learn how to fly. It asked the other birds for help. The other birds were happy to help. They showed the lonely bird how to flap its wings and jump from the tree. The lonely bird tried very hard. It flapped its wings and jumped from the tree. At first, the lonely bird fell to the ground. But it did not give up. It tried again and again. Soon, the lonely bird was flying high in the sky with the other birds. It was not lonely anymore. The bird had learned to fly and was now happy with its new friends.

Ben and Mia are playing with scissors. They like to cut paper and make shapes. Ben has a big bow on his head. He thinks it is funny. Mia does not like the bow. She thinks it is stupid. "Take off the bow, Ben. It is not for boys. It is for girls," Mia says. "No, I like the bow. It is pretty. You are just jealous," Ben says. Mia gets angry. She grabs the scissors and runs to Ben. She wants to cut off the bow. Ben is scared. He tries to run away. But Mia is faster. She catches him and snips the scissors. But she does not cut the bow. She cuts Ben's ear. Ben screams. Blood comes out. Mia drops the scissors. She is shocked. She did not mean to hurt Ben. Mom hears the scream. She runs to the room. She sees Ben and Mia. She sees the scissors and the blood. She is very mad. She picks up Ben and takes him to the car. She says they have to go to the doctor. She says Mia is in big trouble. She says she will tell Dad. Mia cries. She is sorry. She did not want to cut Ben's ear. She just wanted to cut the bow. She thinks the bow is stupid. She thinks she is stupid. She wishes she did not play with scissors.
