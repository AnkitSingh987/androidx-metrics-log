Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Mia and the Robot

Sam and Mia were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big robot near the swings. It had long arms and legs and a shiny head. "Wow, look at that robot!" Sam said. "Can we play with it?" "I don't know," Mia said. "Maybe it is not a toy. Maybe it is someone's robot." They went closer to the robot. It did not move or make any sound. Sam touched its arm. It felt cold and hard. "Hello, robot," Sam said. "Do you want to play with us?" The robot did not answer. Sam pushed a button on its chest. The robot made a beep and opened its eyes. They were red and bright. "Hello, children," the robot said. "I am Robo. I am here to learn from you." Sam and Mia smiled. They liked the robot. They wanted to teach it how to play. "Come on, Robo," Mia said. "We will show you the swings. They are fun." They took the robot's hands and led it to the swings. The robot tried to follow them, but it was not used to walking on the grass. It stumbled and fell. "Are you okay, Robo?" Sam asked. He helped the robot get up. "I am okay, Sam," the robot said. "Thank you for helping me. I am still learning how to walk on different surfaces." "That's okay, Robo," Mia said. "We will help you. You are our new friend." They hugged the robot and took it to the swings. They taught it how to swing and laugh and have fun. The robot liked the children. It wanted to learn more from them.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max had a plan. He had a task to find his favorite toy. His toy was a small, red ball. Max loved to play with this ball all day long. Max looked for the ball under the table, but it was not there. He looked in the yard, but it was not there either. Max felt dizzy from looking around so much. He sat down and thought about where the ball could be. Then, Max remembered that he left the ball in his dog house. He ran to the dog house and found the ball inside. Max was so happy that he did a little dance. From that day on, Max always put the ball back in his dog house, so he never had to search for it again.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. There is a big slide in the park. Anna likes to slide down fast. Ben is scared of the slide. He thinks it is too high. One day, Anna and Ben go to the park with their moms. Anna runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. She says to Ben, "Come on, Ben, slide with me. It is fun!" Ben shakes his head. He says, "No, Anna, I am scared. The slide is too high. I will fall." Anna says, "No, Ben, you will not fall. I will hold your hand. Trust me. It is not scary. It is fun!" Ben looks at Anna. He sees that she is smiling. He wants to be brave like Anna. He says, "OK, Anna, I will try. But you have to hold my hand." Anna says, "OK, Ben, I will hold your hand. Come on, let's go!" Anna and Ben hold hands and slide down the slide. They feel the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They laugh and scream. They have fun. They reach the bottom of the slide and hug each other. They say, "That was fun! Let's do it again!" Their moms watch them and smile. They are proud of Anna and Ben. They say, "Good job, Anna and Ben! You are brave and kind!" Anna and Ben run to the slide again. They are not scared anymore. They are happy. They slide and slide until it is time to go home. They say goodbye to each other and to the slide. They walk to the gate of the park. They say, "See you tomorrow, slide! See you tomorrow, friend!"
