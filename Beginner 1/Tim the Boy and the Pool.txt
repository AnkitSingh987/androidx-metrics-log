Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pool

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the pool. One day, Tim saw a big ball in the pool. He thought it would be fun to play with the ball. But, it was difficult for Tim to get the ball out of the pool. Tim had an idea. He went to his friend, Sam. He asked, "Sam, can I borrow your net to get the ball from the pool?" Sam said, "Yes, you can borrow my net. It will help you." Tim took the net and went back to the pool. He used the net to get the ball out of the pool. It was not difficult anymore. Tim and Sam played with the ball in the pool. They had a lot of fun. And from that day, they always played together in the pool.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a kind girl named Lily. She had a big brother named Tom. They loved to play together in their backyard. One day, while they were playing, they found a small, lost bird. "Look, Tom!" Lily said, "A bird! Can we help it?" Tom nodded and they took the bird to their room. They made a warm bed for the bird to sleep in. That night, when the moon was up, the bird started to sing. "Shh, bird," Tom whispered, "It's time to sleep. We don't want a war with Mom and Dad because we are too loud." The bird stopped singing and fell asleep. The next day, Lily and Tom found the bird's family and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a purple wagon that he loved very much. One day, he decided to test how fast his wagon could go. Tim went to the park with his purple wagon. He saw a small hill and thought it would be fun to test his wagon there. So, he put the wagon at the top of the hill and gave it a little push. The wagon went down the hill very fast. Tim was so happy! At the bottom of the hill, Tim met a girl named Sue. She saw Tim's purple wagon and said, "Wow, your wagon is so fast! Can I try too?" Tim said, "Yes, of course!" Sue tested the wagon and it went fast for her too. They both had a fun day playing with the purple wagon at the park.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a big hose and a bucket of water. They liked to spray each other and make big splashes. "Spray me, spray me!" Ben shouted, running around the grass. "OK, here I come!" Lily said, aiming the hose at him. She sprayed him with a lot of water and he laughed. But then, they heard a loud noise from the house. It was Mom, and she sounded angry. "What are you doing?" Mom asked, coming out of the door. She had a cake in her hands, and it was messy. It had frosting and sprinkles all over it, and some of it had fallen on the floor. "We were playing, Mom," Lily said, dropping the hose. "Playing? You were supposed to help me with the cake! It was for Grandma's birthday, and now it's ruined!" Mom said, showing them the cake. Lily and Ben felt sorry. They did not mean to make Mom sad. They wanted to make Grandma happy. "We're sorry, Mom," they said together. "Can we help you fix the cake?" Lily asked. Mom sighed. She looked at the cake, and then at the children. They were wet and dirty, but they had sweet faces. "Maybe we can," she said. "But first, you need to dry off and clean up. Then, we can make a new cake together. And no more spraying, OK?" "OK, Mom," they said. They hugged her and ran to the bathroom. They hoped Grandma would like the new cake.

One day, a little frog named Tim saw a tall pole. Tim wanted to jump high like a bird. He thought the pole would help him. So, he got ready to spring. He bent his legs and jumped with all his might. Tim sprang so high that he almost touched the sky. He was so happy. He felt like a bird. He jumped and jumped all day long. The pole helped him jump higher than ever before. As the sun went down, Tim felt hungry. He saw a big, sour apple on the ground. He hopped over, took a bite, and made a funny face. The sour apple made him laugh. Tim had a great day with his new pole and the sour apple.
