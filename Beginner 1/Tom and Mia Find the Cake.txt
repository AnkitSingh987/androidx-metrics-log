Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia Find the Cake

Tom and Mia are twins. They are three years old. Today is their birthday. They are very happy. They wear new clothes and hats. They have a big cake with candles. "Happy birthday, Tom and Mia!" Mom and Dad say. They hug and kiss them. They give them presents. Tom and Mia open them. They see books, toys, and puzzles. They smile and clap. "Thank you, Mom and Dad!" they say. They want to play with their new things. But first, they have to blow the candles. Mom and Dad help them. They count to three. They blow hard. The candles go out. They cheer. "Yay, we did it!" Tom says. He is proud. He jumps up and down. He does not see the chair. He bumps his toe on the chair. He cries. It hurts. "Ow, ow, ow!" he says. He holds his toe. He sits on the floor. Mia sees him. She feels sorry. She runs to him. She hugs him. "It's okay, Tom. I'm here. Does it still hurt?" she says. She kisses his toe. She rubs it gently. She gives him a brilliant smile. She makes him laugh. "It's better, Mia. Thank you. You are a good sister. I love you," he says. He hugs her back. He smiles too. "I love you too, Tom. You are a good brother. Come on, let's eat some cake. It's yummy," she says. She helps him up. They walk to the table. They hold hands. They eat some cake. They share it with Mom and Dad. They celebrate their birthday. They are happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Nana. She lived in a small house with her cat, Whiskers. Nana liked to listen to the birds sing outside her window. One sunny day, Nana's friend, Mr. Brown, came to visit. He brought a bottle of wine for them to share. "Nana, I have wine for us," said Mr. Brown. "Oh, thank you, Mr. Brown!" Nana replied with a smile. Nana and Mr. Brown sat in the garden, listening to the birds sing. They drank the wine and talked about happy times. Whiskers the cat played in the grass nearby. It was a lovely day, and they all enjoyed the sun and each other's company.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to listen to music. Tim had a big, fierce dog named Max. They played together every day. Tim knew that one day, the music would end. One day, Tim and Max went to the park. They saw a big tree. Tim had an idea. He wanted to put a music box in the tree. The music box played Tim's favorite song. Tim and Max were very happy. The next day, Tim and Max went back to the park. They saw that the music box was still in the tree. But the music was not playing. Tim was sad. Max looked at Tim and knew what to do. Max jumped and got the music box from the tree. Tim opened the music box and saw that it needed a new key. He found a new key and wound up the music box. The music started to play again. Tim and Max danced and played in the park. They knew that the music would end one day, but they were happy for now.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an original toy car. It was red and shiny. He loved it very much. One day, Tim was playing with his car in the park. A big boy named Sam saw Tim's car and said, "I want your car. Give it to me!" Tim was scared but didn't want to give his car away. So, he said, "No, this is my car. I love it." Just then, a big wind came and blew the car away. Tim and Sam were sad. They both wanted the car back. They looked and looked for the car, but they couldn't find it. Suddenly, they saw a big wreck of leaves and sticks. They went to look at it. They found the car under the wreck! Tim and Sam were happy. They decided to play with the car together and became friends.
