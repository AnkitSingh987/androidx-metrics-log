Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Large Cat and the Bell

Once upon a time, there was a large cat named Tom. Tom was very big and liked to play with his toys. One day, he found a new toy. It was a small bell. Tom loved the sound the bell made when he shook it. Tom took the bell everywhere he went. He would squeeze the bell with his paw to make it ring. All the other animals liked the sound too. They would dance and play when they heard the bell. One day, Tom was playing with the bell near a big tree. He squeezed the bell too hard, and it broke. The bell didn't make any sound anymore. Tom was very sad. He tried to fix the bell, but he couldn't. The bell was broken forever.

One day, a little red car was driving down the road. The car saw a lot of traffic coming. The red car did not like traffic. The car wanted to go fast, but it could not. The red car saw a big truck next to it. The truck said, "Hello, red car! Why are you sad?" The red car said, "I don't like traffic. I want to go fast, but I can't." The truck said, "I have an idea! Let's race to the end of the traffic!" The red car was happy and said, "Yes, let's race!" So, the red car and the big truck started to race. But racing in traffic was not safe. The red car and the big truck went too fast. They did not see the stop sign. They crashed into each other. The red car and the big truck were sad. They should not have raced in traffic. Now, they were broken and could not go fast anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house with their dog. The man and his wife loved to run fast. Every day, they would run and play with their dog. One day, the man and his wife found a big box. The man said, "Please, wife, let's open it!" The wife said, "Yes, let's open it fast!" They opened the box, and inside was a big, shiny ball. They were very happy. The man and his wife started to play with the big, shiny ball. But, the ball started to grow bigger and bigger! The man and his wife were scared. The dog barked at the ball. Then, the ball stopped growing. It was not a ball, but a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "Please, don't be scared. I am here to play!" The man, his wife, and the dog laughed. They all played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a playful dog named Spot found a big sheet. He liked to pretend it was a cape. Spot would run and jump with the sheet on his back. It was fun to play with the sheet. Spot's friend, a cat named Fluffy, saw him playing. Fluffy wanted to play too. They both put on the sheet and pretended to be superheroes. They ran and jumped together, having lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened! The sheet got caught on a tree branch. Spot and Fluffy tried to pull it free, but it wouldn't come off. They thought their game was over. But then, the tree started to play with them! The tree shook its branch, making the sheet move like a cape again. Spot and Fluffy laughed and played with the tree. Their game was even more fun than before!

Once upon a time, there was a noisy mill near a small village. The mill was very big and made lots of noise when it worked. In the village, there was a girl named Lily. She liked to dress up in pretty clothes and play with her friends. One day, Lily and her friend Tom went to see the noisy mill. They stood outside and listened to the loud sounds it made. "Wow, it's so noisy!" said Tom. "Yes, it is!" agreed Lily. They watched the mill work and saw how it made flour from the wheat. After a while, they went back to the village and told their friends about the noisy mill. They all decided to dress up and have a party near the mill. They danced, sang, and played games. They had a great time and the noisy mill became a fun place for everyone in the village.
