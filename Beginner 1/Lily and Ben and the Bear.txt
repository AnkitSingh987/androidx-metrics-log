Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Bear

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a lot of fog. The fog is white and soft. It covers the trees and the houses. "Wow, look at the fog!" Lily says. "It is like a big blanket." "Let's go and explore the fog!" Ben says. "Maybe we can find something fun." They put on their coats and boots. They hold hands and walk into the fog. They can't see very far. They hear strange noises. "What is that?" Lily asks. "Is it a bird? Is it a car?" "I don't know," Ben says. "It is hard to tell in the fog." They keep walking. They see a big shape in the fog. It is brown and furry. It has a long tail and sharp teeth. It is a fox. "Hello, little ones," the fox says. "What are you doing in the fog?" "We are exploring," Lily says. "Who are you?" "I am a fox," the fox says. "I live in the woods. The fog is my friend. It helps me hide and hunt." The fox smiles. He looks hungry. He thinks Lily and Ben are tasty. "Can we be your friends too?" Ben asks. "You are very pretty." The fox nods. He says, "Of course, you can. Come closer. I will show you something." Lily and Ben are curious. They want to see what the fox has. They take a step towards the fox. But then they hear another voice. It is deep and loud. It is a bear. "Stop!" the bear says. "Don't go near the fox. He is not your friend. He is a trickster. He wants to eat you." Lily and Ben are scared. They look at the bear. He is big and black. He has a round nose and small eyes. He is a wise bear. He knows the fox is bad. "Who are you?" Lily asks. "I am a bear," the bear says. "I live in the woods too. I saw you in the fog. I came to help you. The fog is not safe for you. You should go back home." Lily and Ben trust the bear. They say, "Thank you, bear. You are very kind. How can we go back home? We can't see the way." The bear says, "Follow me. I will guide you. I know the woods well. I can see through the fog." Lily and Ben follow the bear. They leave the fox behind. The fox is angry. He missed his chance. The bear leads Lily and Ben to the edge of the fog. He shows them their house. He says, "Here you are. This is your home. Be careful next time. Don't go into the fog alone. It is full of dangers." Lily and Ben hug the bear. They say, "Thank you, bear. You are very wise. You saved us from the fox. You are our friend." The bear smiles. He says, "You are welcome, little ones. You are brave and sweet. You are my friends too. Goodbye for now. Maybe we will meet again." Lily and Ben wave to the bear. They run to their house. They tell their mom and dad what happened. They are happy and safe. They learned a lesson. They decide to listen to the wise bear. They decide not to go into the fog again.

Tim and Jen are friends. They like to play with toys. Tim has a jet. Jen has a doll. They share their toys and have fun. One day, Tim and Jen go to the park. They see a big sign. The sign says: "No toys on the grass. Only balls and frisbees." Tim and Jen are sad. They want to play with their toys on the grass. Tim says: "Let's play with the jet on the grass. The sign is silly. The jet is not a toy. It is a plane." Jen says: "No, Tim. We have to follow the sign. The sign is not silly. It is a rule. The jet is a toy. It is not a plane." Tim and Jen argue. They are not friends anymore. They go to different sides of the park. They play with their toys alone. They are not happy. A dog comes to the park. The dog sees the jet on the grass. The dog thinks the jet is a bone. The dog runs to the jet. The dog bites the jet. The jet breaks. Tim cries. Jen sees Tim crying. She feels sorry for Tim. She runs to Tim. She gives him a hug. She says: "I'm sorry, Tim. I was wrong. The jet is a plane. It is a cool toy. You can play with my doll. I can play with your ball. We can play together." Tim smiles. He says: "Thank you, Jen. I'm sorry, too. The sign is a rule. We have to follow the rule. You can play with my jet. I can play with your frisbee. We can play together." Tim and Jen are friends again. They play with their toys on the path. They have fun. They are happy.
