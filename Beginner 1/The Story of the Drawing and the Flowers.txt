Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Drawing and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a girl who loved to wake up early. She was very creative and had a big love for drawing. Every morning, she would take her colors and make pretty pictures. She drew trees, flowers, and animals. She was happy. One day, she woke up and saw that her colors were gone. She looked everywhere, but she could not find them. She was very sad. Her love for drawing could not be stopped, so she tried to think of a new way to make her pictures. Then, she had a big idea. She went outside and found things to use instead of her colors. She picked up leaves, flowers, and sticks. The girl used these to make her pictures. They were different, but they were still beautiful. She found out that even without her colors, her love for drawing and her creative mind could still make pretty things. And that made her very happy.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make sounds like vroom and beep when they move the cars. One day, it is very cold outside. The ground is white and hard. It is icy. Tim and Sam want to play with their cars, but they are careful. They do not want to slip and fall. They find a big hill. They think it is fun to roll their cars down the hill. They take turns to choose a car and say its name. "This is Red. He is fast and strong," Tim says. He puts Red on the hill and lets him go. Red zooms down the hill and lands on the grass. "Wow, that was cool!" Sam says. "My turn. This is Blue. He is smart and smooth," Sam says. He puts Blue on the hill and lets him go. Blue slides down the hill and hits a rock. Blue breaks into pieces. He is a wreck. "Oh no, Blue!" Sam cries. "I'm sorry, Sam. I didn't see the rock. Are you okay?" Tim asks. He runs to Sam and hugs him. "I'm okay, Tim. But Blue is not. He was my favorite car. I'm sad," Sam says. He picks up the pieces of Blue and holds them. "Maybe we can fix him. Or maybe we can get a new car. Or maybe we can share my cars. I have many cars. You are my friend. I want you to be happy," Tim says. "Thank you, Tim. You are a good friend. I want you to be happy too. Maybe we can fix Blue. Or maybe we can get a new car. Or maybe we can share your cars. You have many cars. They are all nice. Can I play with Red?" Sam asks. "Of course you can. Red is your friend too. He likes you. Come on, let's play some more. But be careful. It is still icy," Tim says. They smile and hug. They play with their cars. They are careful. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time there was a pale duck. His name was Jack. Every day Jack would go to the bathtub to find some food. One morning his mom asked him, "Jack, what are you doing?" "I am looking for food," Jack replied. Thinking quickly, his mom said, "Why don't we go to the pond instead? There's lots of food there." So they both went to the pond and Jack fed on all the tasty treats. The next day Jack returned to the bathtub to feed. Jack's mom saw him and said, "Why don't you come back to the pond?" Jack was sad and said, "But I like the bathtub!" His mom smiled and said, "Well if you like it, then you can still come to the pond and come back here after you finish feeding. That way, you get to enjoy both places!" Jack was happy and ever since, he went to both the pond and the bathtub to feed.

Once upon a time, there was a small town with a high hill. On top of the hill, there was a rail. Two little birds, Tom and Tim, lived on that hill. One day, Tom and Tim started to fight. They both wanted to sit on the rail. Tom said, "I want to sit on the rail!" Tim said, "No, I want to sit on the rail!" They fought and fought, but no one could sit on the rail. Then, a big wind came and blew the rail down. Tom and Tim stopped fighting and looked at the rail on the ground. They both felt sad that they could not sit on the rail anymore. So, Tom and Tim said sorry to each other and became friends again. They promised to share and not fight over the rail anymore.
