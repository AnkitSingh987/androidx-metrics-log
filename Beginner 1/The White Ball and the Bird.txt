Category: Beginner
Title: The White Ball and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a white ball. This ball had a big circle on it. Many kids came to the field to play with the ball. They liked to pass the ball to each other. They had a lot of fun. One day, a little girl named Lily saw the ball. She wanted to play too. Lily tried to pass the ball to her friend Tom. The ball went up in the air. But something strange happened. The ball did not come back down! The kids looked up and saw the ball stuck in a big tree. They were very sad. They tried to get the ball down, but they could not. Then, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird saw the kids and the ball in the tree. The bird wanted to help. The big bird flew up to the tree and got the ball. It passed the ball back to the kids. They were so happy! The kids said thank you to the bird. From that day on, the bird played with the kids too. They all had fun passing the white ball with the big circle on it.

One day, in a small nation, there lived a foolish cat named Tom. Tom loved to play catch with his friend, a little boy named Tim. They would throw a ball and try to catch it. They laughed and had fun. One sunny day, Tom and Tim were playing catch when they saw a big bird in the sky. The big bird looked at them and said, "Do you want to play catch with me?" Tom and Tim were very surprised, but they said yes. The big bird threw the ball very high in the sky. Tom and Tim tried to catch it, but it was too high. Then, the big bird flew down and caught the ball. Tom and Tim were amazed. They learned that even a foolish cat and a little boy can make new friends and have fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lucy loved to learn new things every day. One day, Lucy found a key on the floor. She asked her mom, "What is this key for?" Her mom said, "That key opens the door to our garden. Would you like to learn how to use it?" Lucy was excited. She took the key and went to the garden door. She put the key in the keyhole and turned it. The door opened, and Lucy saw a beautiful garden with flowers and trees. She felt happy and proud that she learned how to use the key.

Ben likes to follow his big sister Mia. He wants to do everything she does. He thinks she is very smart and brave. Mia likes to play with her clock. She can tell the time and set the alarm. She is proud of her clock. One day, Mia goes to her room to play with her clock. Ben follows her. He wants to see the clock too. He knocks on the door and says, "Mia, can I play with you?" Mia says, "No, Ben. This is my clock. You are too little. You don't know how to use it. You might break it. Go away." Ben feels sad and angry. He thinks Mia is mean. He says, "I am not too little. I can use the clock. I want to play with you. Let me in." Mia says, "No, Ben. Go away. This is my room. You can't come in." Ben does not go away. He pushes the door hard. The door opens. Mia is surprised. She drops the clock. The clock falls on the floor. It makes a loud noise. It breaks. Mia and Ben are both scared. They look at the broken clock. They hear their mom coming. They don't know what to say. They are in trouble.

Once upon a time there were two children: Emily and Bob. Emily was nosy and liked to ask a lot of questions. Bob was tired and asked Emily to leave him alone so he could sleep. But Emily was so nosy that she didn’t want to leave Bob alone and asked him more questions. Bob was so tired that he finally started to shout at Emily. Their mom heard them and told Bob,"You must not shout at Emily like that. Shouting is bad, so you need to be punished." Bob felt bad and said sorry to his mom. His mom said,"That's good. Go have a nap now and when you wake up, you won't be so tired and cranky." Bob was happy and went to have a nap. Emily was glad that Bob was feeling better and she promised her mom that she would never be nosy again. Their mom was very proud and said,"Good job, Emily! Let this be a lesson for both of you." They nodded and hugged their mom.
