Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Kite

One day, a little boy named Tim found a kite in his toy box. He was very happy. He wanted to fly the kite in the park with his friends. So, he took the kite and went to the park. The sun was shining and the wind was just right for flying the kite. Tim ran and ran, trying to make the kite fly. But the kite would not go up in the sky. Tim felt sad. He did not know what to do. Then, his friend, Mary, came over. She was an honest girl and always helped her friends. Mary looked at the kite and realized the string was not tied right. Mary fixed the string on the kite for Tim. They both ran and ran, and the kite went up in the sky. Tim and Mary were very happy. They played with the kite all day. Tim thanked Mary for her help. They were the best of friends, and they loved to play with the kite in the park.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw a light blue butterfly. The butterfly could bring colors to everything it touched. Sue wanted to be friends with the butterfly. "Hi, butterfly! Can you bring color to my toys?" Sue asked. The butterfly nodded and touched her toys. The toys became very colorful. Sue was very happy. Sue and the butterfly played together all day. They made the flowers bright colors and the trees light green. They even made the sky a mix of light blue and pink. Sue had the best day ever with her new butterfly friend.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. They were very busy playing a game. They were trying to win. But it was hard to win. One friend said to the other, "You can't win if you don't try hard." The other friend said, "I can too! I can try harder." The first friend said, "Okay. OK." But when the last game was over, the first friend had won. The second friend was not happy. He started to yell. "It's not fair! I worked hard too!" he yelled. The first friend was surprised. She knew her friend was not happy. She tried to calm him down, but it was too late. The angry friend left the room, leaving the first friend feeling sad. She knew it was the end of their friendship. The first friend had been warned - she should not have played the game. But it was too late. She had been too busy trying to win, and she had lost her best friend.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big field with many flowers and butterflies. They wanted to go there and have fun. "Come on, Ben, let's go to the field!" Lily urged. She ran towards the field with a smile. "But Lily, we can't go there. That's Mr. Brown's field. He doesn't like kids to go there. He says we will ruin his flowers and scare his cows." Ben said. He was afraid of Mr. Brown, who was a grumpy old man. "Mr. Brown is not here. He is in his house. He can't see us. And we won't ruin his flowers or scare his cows. We will just look and touch gently. Please, Ben, come with me. It will be great!" Lily said. She wanted Ben to join her. Ben looked at the field and then at Lily. He liked Lily and he liked flowers and butterflies too. He wanted to go to the field, but he was still scared of Mr. Brown. He didn't know what to do. What do you think Ben did? Did he go to the field with Lily or did he stay away? What happened next? You can use your imagination to finish the story.

One day, a little bunny named Sam went for a hop in the dark woods. He wanted to find a treat for his mom. As he hopped, he saw a big bear. The bear looked sad. "Hi, bear! Why are you sad?" asked Sam. The bear said, "I lost my treat. I can't find it." Sam wanted to help. He said, "I will help you find your treat, bear." They hopped together and looked for the treat. Then, they saw a big, dark cave. Sam was scared, but the bear said, "Don't be scared. I will go in with you." Inside the cave, they found a big pile of treats! The bear was happy, and Sam found a treat for his mom. They hopped home together, and they became best friends.
