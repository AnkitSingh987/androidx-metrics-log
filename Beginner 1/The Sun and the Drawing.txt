Category: Beginner
Title: The Sun and the Drawing

Once upon a time, there was a sun that loved to shine. The sun was friends with a happy and enthusiastic pen. The pen was full of ink, and it loved to draw pretty pictures. One day, the pen said, "Sun, can you help me draw a big picture? Your shine will make it look even better!" The sun agreed and they started to draw together. The sun shined bright, and the pen made beautiful lines with its ink. As they drew, a little boy came over and saw the picture. He wanted to help too, but he didn't ask the sun and the pen. Instead, he started to draw with his own ink, making a mess of the picture. When the sun and the pen saw what the boy did, they were very sad. Their pretty picture was ruined. The boy also felt bad because he didn't ask before drawing on their picture. The moral of the story is, always ask before you join in someone else's work.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a cool grill in the park. Tom was very happy because he loved to cook. He thought, "I will have a fun day with my friends and cook yummy food on the grill!" Tom called his friends, a bird named Tim and a dog named Max. They all came to the park with food. Tim brought corn, Max brought hot dogs, and Tom brought fish. They put the food on the grill and started to cook. The smell of the food made them very hungry. While they were cooking, Tom felt a tickle in his nose. He knew he was going to sneeze soon. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. Tom sneezed very loudly! The sneeze was so big that it blew the food off the grill! Tim, Max, and Tom laughed and laughed. They picked up the food and put it back on the grill. Soon, they had a yummy meal to share with each other.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and make mud pies. They had a big bucket of water to make the mud wet. They were having fun. But then it started to rain. The rain was very heavy and fast. The water in the bucket spilled over and made a flood. The flood washed away their mud pies and filled their holes. Tom and Lily were sad and wet. They ran to the house to get dry and warm. But the door was locked. They could not get in. They knocked and knocked, but no one answered. They were scared and cold. Then they saw a big dog. The dog was brown and fluffy. He had a collar with a tag. The tag said his name was Max. Max saw Tom and Lily and wagged his tail. He was friendly and generous. Max had a key in his mouth. He brought the key to Tom and Lily and dropped it on the ground. Tom and Lily picked up the key and tried it on the door. It worked! The door opened and they went inside. They found a towel and dried themselves and Max. They found some cookies and milk and shared them with Max. They thanked Max for his help and gave him a hug. They were happy and safe.

Jen and Ben are twins. They like to play with toys. They have many toys in a big box. But they want a new toy. Mom says they can choose one toy from the store. They go to the store with Mom. They see many toys. They see cars, dolls, balls, books, and more. They do not know what to choose. They look and look. Then they see a jar. The jar has something inside. It is a cute fish. The fish is orange and white. It swims and flips. Jen and Ben like the fish. They want the fish. They ask Mom if they can have the fish. Mom says yes, but they have to share. They have to take turns to feed the fish and clean the jar. Jen and Ben agree. They choose the fish. They are happy. They take the fish home. They put the jar on a table. They name the fish Fin. They feed Fin and watch him swim. They love Fin. Fin is their new toy and friend.
