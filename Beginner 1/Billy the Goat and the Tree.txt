Category: Beginner
Title: Billy the Goat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a goat named Billy. Billy was a happy goat who lived in a big field with his friends. One day, Billy got hurt while playing with his friends. He fell and hurt his leg. His friends were sad and worried about him. A kind girl named Sue saw Billy and wanted to help him. She walked over to him and said, "Don't worry, Billy. I'll lead you to a safe place so you can feel better." Sue took Billy by the hand and led him to a soft, green spot under a big tree. This was a good place for Billy to rest his hurt leg. As days went by, Sue visited Billy every day to make sure he was okay. She brought him food and water, and she talked to him to cheer him up. Soon, Billy's leg got better, and he was able to play with his friends again. Billy was very thankful for Sue's help and they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a club. He liked to play with his club. One day, Tim saw a sack. The sack was rare. It was big and red. Tim wanted to see what was inside the sack. Tim asked his friend, Sam. "What is in the sack?" Sam did not know. They both wanted to find out. They took the club and hit the sack. The sack did not open. They tried again. This time, the sack opened. Inside the sack, they found many toys. They were so happy. But then, a big dog came. The dog took the sack. Tim and Sam were sad. The dog looked at them and said, "This sack is for all the kids. Share with everyone." Tim and Sam were surprised. They did not know dogs could talk. They agreed to share the toys and played with all their friends.

Once upon a time there was a princess who was brave and strong. She had a pink brush that she wanted to use to comb her long, shiny hair. But the princess was scared and didn’t know how to use it! She didn’t know if she dared. Suddenly, a white fairy appeared in a puff of magic. She said, “Do not be scared, Princess! Just take the pink brush, hold it in your hand, and comb your hair with it.” The princess listened to the fairy and took the brush in her hands. She began to gently brush her hair with the pink brush. It felt so good! She smiled and said to the fairy, “Thank you for helping me. Now I know I can do it on my own.” The fairy smiled and flew away in a twinkle of pink light. The brave princess was ready to start a new adventure with her pink brush!

Lila was a lonely girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had a big purse where she kept all her dolls and their clothes. She loved to dress them up and pretend they were her friends. One day, she saw a new girl in the park. She had curly hair and a red dress. She was playing with a ball and a dog. Lila wanted to talk to her, but she was shy. She watched her from behind a tree. The new girl saw Lila and smiled. She walked over to her and said, "Hi, I'm Ruby. What's your name?" Lila was surprised and scared. She hugged her purse and said, "Lila." Ruby said, "Do you want to play with me? I have a ball and a dog. They are fun." Lila said, "No, thank you. I have my dolls. They are fun too." Ruby said, "Can I see your dolls? I like dolls too." Lila said, "No, they are mine. You can't touch them." Ruby said, "Okay, sorry. Bye." She walked away with her ball and her dog. Lila felt sad. She wanted to play with Ruby, but she was afraid to share her dolls. She opened her purse and took out a doll. She saw that the doll's face was melted. She had left her purse in the sun and the heat had ruined her doll. She cried and cried. She realized that her dolls were not her real friends. They could not talk to her or play with her. They could only melt and break. She wished she had been nicer to Ruby. She wished she had a real friend. She ran after Ruby and said, "Ruby, wait. I'm sorry. Can I play with you?" Ruby turned around and said, "Sure, Lila. I'm glad you changed your mind. Come on, let's play." Lila smiled and followed Ruby. She left her purse behind. She did not need it anymore. She had found a real friend. The moral of the story is: It is better to share and make friends than to be selfish and lonely.
