Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Book

Lily was a thin girl who liked to look at books. She had many books in her room, but her favorite one was about a fairy who lived in a flower. She liked to look at the pictures of the fairy and her friends, and imagine she was with them. One day, Lily's mom said she had to go to the store and buy some food. She told Lily to stay in her room and be good. Lily said okay, and hugged her mom. She watched her mom go out the door, and then went to her bookshelf. She picked up her favorite book and went to the armchair. The armchair was big and soft, and Lily liked to sit on it and read. She opened her book and started to look at the pictures. She saw the fairy flying over a pond, and a frog jumping out of the water. She saw the fairy talking to a bird, and a worm crawling on a leaf. She saw the fairy dancing with a butterfly, and a bee buzzing around a rose. Lily was so happy looking at the pictures, she did not hear the door open. She did not hear her mom come in with a bag of food. She did not hear her mom call her name. She did not hear her mom walk to her room. She only heard her mom when she said, "Lily, what are you doing?" Lily looked up and saw her mom standing by the door. She smiled and said, "I'm looking at my book, mom. It's about a fairy who lives in a flower. Do you want to look with me?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, I do. That sounds like a lovely book." She walked to the armchair and sat next to Lily. She took the book and opened it. She said, "Let's look at the pictures together, and you can tell me the story." Lily nodded and hugged her mom. She said, "Okay, mom. I love you." She pointed to the first picture and said, "This is the fairy. Her name is Flora. She lives in a flower. She has wings and a wand. She can do magic. She is very nice and pretty. She has many friends. Do you want to meet them?" Her mom said, "Yes, I do. Let's meet them." She turned the page and said, "Who is this?" Lily said, "This is the frog. His name is Fribbit. He lives in the pond. He can jump and swim. He likes to play with Flora. He is very funny and green. He has a big mouth. Do you want to hear him?" Her mom said, "Yes, I do. Let's hear him." She made a croaking sound and said, "Ribbit, ribbit, ribbit." Lily laughed and said, "That's right, mom. That's how he sounds. He is very loud and happy. He has a big smile. Do you want to see more?" Her mom said, "Yes, I do. Let's see more." She turned the page and said, "Who is this?" Lily said, "This is the bird. His name is Chirp. He lives in the tree. He can fly and sing. He likes to talk to Flora. He is very smart and blue. He has a beak and feathers. Do you want to hear him?" Her mom said, "Yes, I do. Let's hear him." She made a whistling sound and said, "Chirp, chirp, chirp." Lily clapped and said, "That's right, mom. That's how he sounds. He is very sweet and friendly. He has a nice voice. Do you want to see more?" Her mom said, "Yes, I do. Let's see more." She turned the page and said, "Who is this?" Lily said, "This is the worm. His name is Wriggle. He lives in the ground. He can crawl and dig. He likes to eat leaves. He is very quiet and brown. He has no legs or eyes. Do you want to touch him?" Her mom said, "No, I don't. Let's skip him." She turned the page and said, "Who is this?" Lily said, "This is the butterfly. Her name is Flutter. She lives in the air. She can fly and change colors. She likes to dance with Flora. She is very beautiful and pink. She has wings and antennas. Do you want to see her?" Her mom said, "Yes, I do. Let's see her." She turned the page and said, "Who is this?"
