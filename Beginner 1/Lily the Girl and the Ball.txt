Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Lily. She had a big, red ball. Lily liked to play with her ball in the park. One day, while she was playing, the wind came and took her ball away. Lily was sad and troubled because she loved her ball very much. Lily went to look for her ball. She walked and walked. She turned left and right, but she could not find her ball. Then, she met a kind cat. The cat saw Lily was sad and gave her a little wink. The cat said, "I will help you find your ball." Lily and the cat looked for the ball together. They turned around a big tree and saw a big dog. The dog was playing with Lily's red ball. Lily was scared of the dog, but the kind cat was brave. The cat said, "Let's go get your ball back!" The dog saw Lily and the cat and was not mean at all. The dog said, "I found this ball and thought it was lost. You can have it back!" Lily was very happy and thanked the dog. The cat winked at Lily again, and they all played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to zip around in his yard. One day, the sun was warm, and Tim went outside to play. Tim saw a big ball on the ground. It was as big as the earth! He kicked the ball and it went up, up, up into the sky. Tim was so happy to see it fly. Tim's mom came out to watch him play. She gave him a warm hug. They played with the big ball all day long. Tim loved to zip around the earth with his mom. They had lots of fun together.

Lily and Ben were playing in the yard. They liked to jump in the big pile of leaves that Dad had made with the rake. The rake was a long stick with sharp teeth that could move the leaves. Sometimes, Dad let them help with the rake, but they had to be careful not to hurt themselves. "Let's play hide and seek in the leaves!" Ben said. "You count to ten and I'll hide." "Okay!" Lily said. She closed her eyes and started to count. "One, two, three..." Ben ran to the other side of the yard and looked for a good hiding spot. He saw a big bush with a hole under it. He crawled inside the hole and waited for Lily to find him. "Ten!" Lily shouted. She opened her eyes and looked around. "Where are you, Ben? I'm coming to find you!" She ran to the pile of leaves and kicked them with her feet. She hoped Ben was hiding there, but he was not. She looked behind the tree, under the swing, and in the sandbox, but he was not there either. She started to feel worried. Where could he be? "Ben, where are you?" she called. "This is not funny!" She heard a muffled voice from the bush. "Help! Help! I'm stuck!" She ran to the bush and saw Ben's feet sticking out of the hole. He had crawled too far and could not get out. He looked scared and dirty. "Ben, what are you doing?" she asked. "How did you get in there?" "I was hiding from you, but I got delayed by a mysterious thing," Ben said. "It was dark and cold and it grabbed me and pulled me in. I think it was a monster!" Lily did not believe him. She thought he was making up a story. She grabbed his feet and tried to pull him out. He was too heavy and she could not move him. "Stop lying, Ben! There is no monster. You just got stuck. We need to get Dad to help you." She ran to the house and knocked on the door. Dad opened it and saw her. "What's wrong, Lily?" he asked. "Ben is stuck in the bush! He says there is a monster, but I don't think so. He needs your help!" Dad followed her to the yard and saw Ben in the hole. He shook his head and smiled. "Ben, what are you doing?" he asked. "How did you get in there?" "I was hiding from Lily, but I got delayed by a mysterious thing," Ben repeated. "It was dark and cold and it grabbed me and pulled me in. I think it was a monster!" Dad did not believe him either. He thought he was being silly. He took the rake and pushed it into the hole. He moved it around and made some noise. "Roar! Roar! I'm the monster! Let go of Ben or I'll bite you!" he said. Ben heard the noise and felt the rake. He got scared and let go of the thing that was holding him. He crawled out of the hole and saw Dad with the rake. He realized it was a prank. "Dad, that's not fair! You scared me!" he said. Dad laughed and hugged him. "I'm sorry, Ben. I was just joking. There is no monster. You just got tangled in some roots. Are you okay?" Ben nodded and rubbed his eyes. He was okay, but he felt silly and embarrassed. Lily came over and hugged him too. "I'm glad you're out, Ben. That was a bad hiding spot. Next time, don't go in the hole." Ben agreed and smiled. He was glad to be out too. He learned his lesson. He decided not to hide in the hole again. He also decided not to lie about the monster. He knew it was not real. It was just Dad with the rake.
