Category: Beginner
Title: Joe and the Bill

Once, there were two boys named Joe and Bill. Joe wanted to go and wander. He asked Bill to come. “Let’s go, Bill!” Joe said. “Where?” Bill asked. “Out on a wander. We can find something fun,” Joe said. So, Joe and Bill wandered and soon they came across a dirty old pistol. “Look at this. It’s a pistol,” Joe said, looking excitedly at the gun. “It looks dirty. We should leave it alone,” Bill said. So, Joe and Bill agreed to leave the dirty pistol and kept on wandering. Soon, they found some shiny rocks which were fun to play with. “Let’s take these rocks home and show Mom,” Joe said. So, Joe and Bill went home with the shiny rocks and showed their Mom. She was very happy to see the rocks and thanked Joe and Bill for finding them. The End.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a jolly shop. This shop had many toys and treats for little kids. The shop owner, Mr. Brown, was a very happy man. He loved to see the kids smile when they got a new toy. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the shop with her mom. Lily saw a big, red ball she wanted to play with. She asked her mom if she could have it. Her mom said, "Not today, dear." But Lily kept asking. She insisted, "Please, Mom, I really want the ball!" Lily's mom thought about it for a moment. She knew Lily was a good girl and always helped at home. So, she smiled and said, "Alright, you can have the ball." Lily was so happy! She hugged her mom and thanked her. Mr. Brown, the jolly shop owner, saw how happy Lily was. He felt good knowing his shop made kids happy. From that day on, Lily and her red ball were the best of friends. They played together every day, and Lily always remembered to say thank you to her mom for the special gift.

One day, a little girl named Lucy and her dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw an enormous tree with many leaves. Lucy had an idea. "Let's play hide and seek, Spot!" she said. Spot was very good at hiding. Lucy started to count, "One, two, three..." When she reached ten, she opened her eyes and said, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looked behind the tree, but Spot was not there. She looked under a bush, but Spot was not there either. Lucy started to feel sad. Then, Lucy saw a long, fluffy tail behind a big rock. She had a guess. "I found you, Spot!" she shouted. Spot came out from behind the rock, wagging his tail. Lucy was so happy she found her best friend. They laughed and played under the enormous tree for the rest of the day.

Lily did not like to go to bed. She wanted to play with her toys and watch TV. She hated bed. Every night, her mom and dad told her to brush her teeth and put on her pajamas. But Lily said no. She ran away and hid under the table. "Lily, it's time for bed. You have to sleep, or you will be tired tomorrow," her mom said. "No, I don't want to go to bed. I want to stay up," Lily said. Her dad came and lifted her from under the table. He carried her to her room. He tucked her in and gave her a kiss. "Good night, Lily. We love you. You have to sleep, or you will miss the fun things tomorrow," he said. Lily pouted and turned away. She did not say good night. She closed her eyes, but she did not sleep. She was angry and sad. She heard a noise outside her window. She opened her eyes and saw a big moon. It was bright and round. She saw stars, too. They were shiny and small. She felt curious and happy. She wanted to see more. She got out of her bed and went to the window. She looked at the moon and the stars. She smiled and waved at them. She felt like they were her friends. She heard a voice in her ear. It was the moon. "Hello, Lily. Do you like me and the stars?" the moon said. "Yes, I do. You are beautiful and nice," Lily said. "Thank you, Lily. We like you, too. But you know what? We have to go soon. We only come out at night. When the sun comes, we have to sleep. That's how we stay ready for the next night," the moon said. "Really? You have to sleep, too?" Lily said. "Yes, we do. Everyone has to sleep, Lily. Even you. Sleep is good for you. It makes you grow and learn and dream. It makes you happy and healthy. If you don't sleep, you will miss the fun things tomorrow. You will be tired and grumpy. You will not enjoy the sun and the flowers and the birds. You will not play well with your toys and your friends. You will not listen to your mom and dad. You will not be a good girl," the moon said. Lily thought about what the moon said. She realized that the moon was right. She needed to sleep. She wanted to be ready for the next day. She wanted to be happy and healthy. She wanted to be a good girl. She said goodbye to the moon and the stars. She thanked them for talking to her. She went back to her bed. She snuggled with her teddy bear. She said good night to her mom and dad in her heart. She closed her eyes and fell asleep. She had a nice dream. She dreamed that she was flying with the moon and the stars. They showed her many wonderful things. They told her stories and jokes. They made her laugh and sing. They were her friends. She woke up in the morning. She felt refreshed and cheerful. She saw the sun shining through her window. She heard the birds chirping outside. She smelled the pancakes that her mom was making in the kitchen. She jumped out of her bed and ran to the bathroom. She brushed her teeth and put on her clothes. She was ready for the day. She hugged her mom and dad and said good morning. She ate her pancakes and drank her milk. She played with her toys and watched TV. She had fun with her friends. She listened to her mom and dad. She was a good girl. She learned that bed was not bad. She learned that sleep was good. She learned that the moon and the stars were her friends. She learned that she could be ready for the day. She did not hate bed anymore. She loved bed.
