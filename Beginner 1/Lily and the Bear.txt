Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Bear

Lily liked to play with her furry toys. She had a bear, a cat, a dog and a dove. She liked to make them talk and hug them. One day, she wanted to play a new game. She wanted to match her toys with things in her room. She looked around and saw her bed. It had a white sheet and a white pillow. She thought, "The dove is white. The dove can match the bed." She put the dove on the pillow and smiled. Then she saw her rug. It was brown and soft. She thought, "The bear is brown. The bear can match the rug." She put the bear on the rug and giggled. Next she saw her window. It had blue curtains and a blue sky. She thought, "The cat is blue. The cat can match the window." She put the cat on the window sill and clapped. Finally she saw her door. It had a red knob and a red sign. She thought, "The dog is red. The dog can match the door." She put the dog on the door and cheered. Lily was happy. She had matched all her toys with things in her room. She said, "Good job, toys! You are very smart and pretty. I love you all." She hugged them and kissed them. Then she took them to her bed and cuddled with them. She was sleepy and happy. She closed her eyes and dreamed of more matching games.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. They can make houses, cars, animals and more with their blocks. One day, they decide to make a modern city with their blocks. They lay the blocks on the floor and start to build. They make tall buildings, bridges, roads and parks. They use blue blocks for water and green blocks for trees. They are very creative and have fun. "Look at our city!" Anna says. "It is so big and beautiful!" "Yes, it is!" Ben agrees. "We are good builders!" They play with their city for a long time. They pretend to be people who live and work there. They drive cars, go to shops, visit museums and have picnics. They enjoy their modern city. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is Anna's baby brother, Tom. He crawls into the room and sees the blocks. He likes the blocks. He wants to play with them too. He grabs some blocks and throws them around. He knocks down some buildings and bridges. He makes a mess. "Tom, no!" Anna shouts. "Stop it! You are ruining our city!" "Tom, bad!" Ben says. "Go away! These are our blocks!" But Tom does not listen. He laughs and keeps playing with the blocks. He does not understand why Anna and Ben are angry. He thinks they are funny. Anna and Ben are sad and mad. They worked hard to make their city. They do not like what Tom did. They try to stop him, but he is too fast and strong. They do not know what to do. They wish Tom would leave them alone. They want their city back.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. He lived in a big house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They played all day and had lots of fun together. One day, Tom and Sue were playing outside when they heard a loud noise. It was a big dog barking. The dog was a threat to them because it was not nice. Tom said to Sue, "We should go back to the house where it is safe." Sue agreed and said, "You are a smart cat, Tom. I am happy you are my friend." They went back to the house and told Sue's mom about the big dog. Sue's mom made sure they were safe and they continued to play happily inside.

Once upon a time there was a bear. The bear was big and purple. The bear had a big nail in its paw. The nail was red and shiny. The bear saw a little mouse. The mouse was grey and small. The bear warned the mouse not to go near its paw. But the mouse wanted to see the nail. The mouse scurried towards the bear's paw. The bear warned the mouse again but the mouse kept going. When the mouse got close to the paw, the bear growled. The mouse was frightened and ran away. The bear was happy that it could warn the mouse. And the mouse was happy to be away from the big purple bear.
