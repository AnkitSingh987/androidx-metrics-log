Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Park of Tim and the Toys

One day, a boy named Tim felt very miserable. He did not want to play with his toys or talk to his friends. His mom saw him and said, "Tim, why are you so sad?" Tim said, "I don't know, Mom. I just feel bad." His mom had an idea. She suggested, "Let's go to the park and play. Maybe you will feel better." At the park, Tim saw a small animal with a sharp thing. The animal said, "I found a razor. What should I do with it?" Tim said, "Do not touch it! It can hurt you. Let's give it to my mom." The animal agreed, and they gave the razor to Tim's mom. Tim felt happy because he helped the animal. He was not miserable anymore.

Ben and Mia like to play with their sail. It is a toy boat with a big cloth on a stick. They put the sail in a big tub of water and make it go fast with their hands. They pretend they are pirates and look for treasure. One day, they find a shiny coin in the water. Ben says, "Look, Mia, we are rich! This is our treasure!" Mia says, "Wow, Ben, it is so shiny! Can I hold it?" Ben says, "OK, but be careful. It is wet and slippery." Mia takes the coin and looks at it. She sees a picture of a man with a hat and a beard. She says, "Who is this man, Ben? Is he a pirate too?" Ben says, "I don't know, Mia. Maybe he is the king of the pirates. Maybe he hid this coin for us to find." Mia says, "He is very nice, then. Can we keep the coin, Ben?" Ben says, "Yes, Mia, we can keep it. It is our treasure. But we have to hide it from Mom and Dad. They might take it away." Mia says, "OK, Ben, we will hide it. But where?" Ben says, "Let's hide it in the sail. It is dry and soft. No one will look there." Mia says, "Good idea, Ben. Let's do that." They put the coin in the sail and fold it. They take the sail out of the water and put it in their toy box. They are very happy. They drink some juice and eat some cookies. They think about their treasure and smile. They are the best pirates ever.

One day, a big orange ball was in the park. It was on the tip of the hill. A girl named Sue saw the ball and wanted to play with it. She ran up the hill to get the ball. Sue had a fun idea. She wanted to pass the ball to her friends Tom and Ann. Sue called Tom and Ann to come to the park. They were happy to play with the big orange ball. They played a game where they pass the ball to each other. The ball went up and down, and they laughed a lot. Then, something unexpected happened. When Tom tried to pass the ball to Sue, it did not go to her. The ball went up in the sky! It flew higher and higher. Sue, Tom, and Ann were very surprised. They looked up and saw a big bird in the sky. The bird had the big orange ball in its beak. The bird wanted to play too! The bird passed the ball back to them, and they all played together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a dull knife. He liked to cut things with his knife. One day, he went outside to play in the mud. He took his knife with him. He wanted to cut shapes in the mud. Tim started to cut a big circle in the mud. He was having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A frog jumped out of the mud! Tim was very surprised. The frog looked at Tim and said, "Thank you for cutting me free from the mud!" Tim was amazed that the frog could talk. The frog told Tim that he was a magic frog. He said, "Because you helped me, I will give you one wish." Tim thought about it and wished for a new, sharp knife. The magic frog granted his wish. Tim was happy with his new knife. He promised the frog that he would be careful with it. The frog said goodbye and hopped away. Tim went back to cutting shapes in the mud, but this time, he was more careful. He didn't want to accidentally trap any more magic frogs.
