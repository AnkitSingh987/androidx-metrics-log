Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Cat

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park. She saw a fearful cat stuck in a tree. The cat was making a sad noise. Sally wanted to help the cat, so she went to find her dad. Sally found her dad and said, "Daddy, the cat is stuck! Help!" Her dad listened to her demand and went to the tree. He saw the cat and knew he had to do something. He thought about a way to get the cat down. Dad remembered he had a long stick with a cut at the end. He went to get the stick and brought it back to the tree. Sally watched as her dad used the stick to help the cat climb down. The cat was happy to be on the ground. It looked at Sally and her dad, and made a happy noise. Sally and her dad felt good that they could help the cat. They went home with big smiles on their faces, knowing they had done a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a big red van. The van was very high, and it could go very fast. One sunny day, the van was driving down the road when it saw a little blue car. The little blue car was sad because it was not as high as the big red van. The big red van wanted to help the little blue car feel better. So, the big red van said, "I am sorry you are not high like me. I forgive you for being sad." The little blue car felt better after the big red van said it forgave it. They became good friends and played together all the time. The little blue car learned that being high was not so important after all.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sam, playing with a toy car. Tim thought the toy car was boring. He wanted to find something more fun to play with. Tim said to Sam, "This car is boring. Let's go find something else to play with." Sam did not want to go. He liked his toy car. But Tim went to look for a new toy. While Tim was gone, a big dog came and tried to take Sam's toy car. It made a big wreck. Sam was sad and called for Tim. Tim heard Sam and ran back to help. Tim saw the wreck and said, "I am sorry, Sam. I was wrong. Your car is not boring. It is fun." They picked up the pieces of the car and played together. They were happy to be friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his teaspoon. One day, Tom saw a big dog named Max. Max wanted to play with Tom's teaspoon too. Tom did not like that. He wanted to keep his teaspoon all to himself. "Go away, Max!" said Tom. "This is my teaspoon!" Max was sad but he had an idea. He would send a letter to ask for his own teaspoon. Max found a bird named Sally. "Sally, can you send a letter for me?" Max asked. Sally said, "Yes, I can help!" Sally sent the letter and soon, a new teaspoon came for Max. Max was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. The teaspoon broke in half! Tom saw this and felt bad for Max. Tom said, "Max, we can share my teaspoon." Max smiled and they played together with the teaspoon, now as friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toys in the living room. He had a lot of fun, but he made a big mess. His toys were everywhere, and his hands were dirty. His mom came into the room and saw the mess. She said, "Tim, you need to clean up and wash your hands." Tim did not want to clean up. He said, "No, I don't want to." His mom was not happy. She told him, "If you don't clean up, you can't go upstairs to play with your new toy." Tim looked at his mom and thought about it. He still refused to clean up. His mom went upstairs to get the new toy. When she came back down, she had a surprise. It was a toy cleaning set with a small broom and dustpan! Tim was so excited to see the new toy. He started to clean up his mess with the new toy cleaning set. His mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! Now you can go upstairs and play with your other new toy." Tim cleaned up all his toys and washed his hands. He was happy to go upstairs and play with his new toy.
