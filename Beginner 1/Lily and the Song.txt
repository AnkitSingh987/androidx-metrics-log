Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Song

Lily loves her pink comb. She uses it every day to make her hair look nice. She combs her hair in front of the mirror and sings a song. She likes to see her hair shine and bounce. One day, Lily cannot find her pink comb. She looks everywhere in her room, but it is not there. She feels sad and cries. She does not want to go to school without her pink comb. She asks her mom and dad if they have seen her pink comb. They shake their heads and say no. They help her look for it, but they cannot find it either. They tell her not to worry, they will buy her a new one. Lily goes to school with a different comb. She does not like it. It is not pink and it does not make her hair look nice. She misses her pink comb and hopes it will come back. At the end of the day, Lily comes home and runs to her room. She sees something on her bed. It is her pink comb! She is so happy and hugs it. She wonders how it got there. She sees a note next to it. It says, "I found your pink comb in the garden. I think you dropped it when you played with your friends. I cleaned it and brought it back to you. I hope you like it. Your friend, Max." Lily smiles and thanks Max in her heart. She combs her hair with her pink comb and sings a song. She likes to see her hair shine and bounce. She loves her pink comb and her friend Max.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play with toys and watch TV. One day, Tom comes to Sue's house. He sees a cable on the floor. He thinks it is a snake. He is scared. "Look, Sue, a snake!" he says. Sue laughs. She is not scared. She knows it is a cable. It is for the TV. "That is not a snake, Tom. That is a cable. It helps the TV to show pictures and sounds," she says. Tom does not believe her. He thinks she is deaf. He thinks she cannot hear the snake hiss. "No, Sue, you are deaf. That is a snake. It can bite us. We have to run away," he says. He grabs Sue's hand and pulls her. He runs to the door. But he is not careful. He trips on the cable. He falls down. He hurts his knee. He cries. Sue helps him up. She hugs him. She is sorry for him. "Tom, you are not deaf. You are silly. That is a cable. It cannot bite us. It is not alive. But you have to be careful. You can suffer if you fall on it. It can make you bleed," she says. Tom looks at his knee. He sees blood. He is sad. He is sorry for being silly. "I am sorry, Sue. You are right. That is a cable. It is not a snake. I was silly. I did not listen to you. I suffer because I was not careful," he says. Sue smiles. She is happy. She is still his friend. "It is OK, Tom. I forgive you. You are still my friend. Come, I have a band-aid for your knee. Then we can watch TV. But we have to be careful with the cable. We do not want to suffer again," she says. They go to the bathroom. Sue puts a band-aid on Tom's knee. Then they go to the living room. They watch TV. They are careful with the cable. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big factory. In the factory, they made toys for kids. One day, a little toy car wanted to go fast. It was excited. The toy car asked another toy, "Can you help me go fast?" The other toy said, "Yes, I will help you succeed." They worked together and tried many times. The toy car went faster and faster. They were very happy. At last, the toy car succeeded. It was the fastest toy car in the factory. All the other toys were excited too. They all played together and had fun.
