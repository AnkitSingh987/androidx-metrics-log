Category: Beginner
Title: The Busy Ant and the Tunnel

Once upon a time, there was a busy little ant. The ant lived in a tunnel under the ground. Every day, the ant worked hard to find food and help his friends. One day, the ant found a big piece of food. He wanted to take it back to his tunnel. But the food was too big! The ant tried and tried, but he could not move it. He felt embarrassed because his friends were watching him. Then, a kind ant friend came to help. Together, they were able to move the big piece of food. The busy ants were very happy. They took the food back to their tunnel and shared it with all their friends. The embarrassed ant felt better because his friend helped him, and they all had a big feast.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom had an urge to catch a small bird. The bird lived in a tree outside Tom's home. Tom would watch the bird every day and try to think of a way to catch it. One day, Tom said to his friend, the dog, "I want to catch the bird, but it is too high in the tree." The dog said, "You should not quit! You can climb the tree and get the bird. I will help you." Tom and the dog made a plan to catch the bird. The dog would push Tom up the tree, and Tom would grab the bird. They tried and tried, but Tom could not reach the bird. The bird saw them and was scared. The bird said, "Please stop! I am scared. I do not want to be caught." Tom and the dog did not listen. They kept trying to catch the bird. The bird was very sad. In the end, Tom and the dog did not catch the bird. The bird flew away to a new home far away. Tom and the dog were sad because they lost their friend. They learned that they should have listened to the bird when it asked them to stop.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in her garden with her friends. One day, Lily saw a thick, big bush in the corner of the garden. She thought it was hiding something special. Lily told her friends about the big bush. They all wanted to see what was behind it. They played and played, trying to find a way to get through the thick bush. They knew something fun was waiting for them. Finally, they found a secret path through the big bush. When they got to the other side, they saw a beautiful, colorful place. It was a secret party spot! They decided to celebrate and have a fun party there. They played games, ate yummy treats, and danced all day. The big bush had led them to the best surprise ever!

Ben and Mia were happy. They were going to celebrate their birthday at the station. They loved trains. They had a big cake with candles and a train on top. They had balloons and hats and presents. They got on the bus with Mom and Dad. They sang songs and looked out the window. They saw cars and trees and shops. They were very excited. They could not wait to see the trains. They got off the bus and walked to the station. They saw a big sign that said "Welcome Ben and Mia". They smiled and clapped. They followed Mom and Dad to a special room. There were other kids and grown-ups there. They were their friends and family. They said hello and hugged them. They played games and ate snacks. They listened to stories and sang songs. They had a lot of fun. Then Mom said, "It's time for the cake!" They all went to the table. They saw the cake with the train on top. They blew out the candles and made a wish. They cut the cake and shared it. It was yummy. Then Dad said, "I have a surprise for you!" He took them to the door. He opened it and said, "Look!" They looked and saw a big train. It was red and shiny. It had a whistle and a bell. It had their names on it. They gasped and cheered. They ran to the train. They got on and sat in the seats. They waved to their friends and family. The train started to move. It went around the station. They saw the tracks and the signals and the people. They heard the whistle and the bell. They felt the wind and the sun. They were very happy. They had the best birthday ever.
