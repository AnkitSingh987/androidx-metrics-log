Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and the Book

Sara likes to write. She has a long pencil and a big book. She writes her name and some letters. She is happy. But then, her baby brother comes. He wants to play with Sara. He grabs her pencil and book. He scribbles on the pages. He tears some pages. He laughs. Sara is angry. She cries. She says, "No, no, no! Give me back my pencil and book! You are bad!" Her mother hears Sara and her brother. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees the mess. She is sad. She says, "Oh, no, no, no! What have you done? You are naughty!" She takes the pencil and book from her brother. She gives them to Sara. She says, "I'm sorry, Sara. Your brother is too little. He doesn't know how to write. He only knows how to play." She gives her brother a rag. She says, "Here, you can play with this. It is soft and colorful. You can make shapes with it. But don't tear it. Be gentle." Sara hugs her mother. She says, "Thank you, mama. I love you. And I love my brother, too. But he needs to learn how to write. Can you teach him?" Her mother smiles. She says, "Yes, I can. But not now. Now, you can write some more. And I will read to your brother. Maybe he will learn some words. And maybe he will be nice." Sara nods. She says, "OK, mama. That sounds good. I will write a story. And you can read a story. And we can all be happy."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived with her mother in a small house. Lily was a happy girl who loved to play with her toys. Her mother made sure that Lily always had fun things to do. They would play games, sing songs, and read books together. One day, Lily's mother had to go to the store. She told Lily to stay at home and play with her toys. Lily agreed, but as soon as her mother left, she felt lonely. She wished her mother was available to play with her. So, Lily decided to look for her mother outside. Lily walked and walked, but she could not find her mother. Suddenly, she saw a big, friendly dog. The dog said, "Hello, my name is Max! I am here to help you find your mother." Lily was surprised that the dog could talk, but she was happy to have a new friend. Together, they searched for her mother and found her at the store. Lily's mother was so glad to see Lily and Max, and they all went home together. From that day on, Max lived with Lily and her mother, and they all had lots of fun together.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toy cars in the living room. They liked to make them zoom and crash and honk. Ben had a red car and Lily had a blue car. They pretended they were drivers and raced each other. "Ready, set, go!" Ben shouted and pushed his car forward. Lily did the same and followed him. They made their cars go around the sofa, under the table, and over the rug. They had fun and laughed. But then, Ben saw a big yellow car on the shelf. It was his dad's car and it looked shiny and cool. Ben wanted to play with it. He stopped his red car and reached for the yellow car. "Ben, no!" Lily said. "That's not your car. That's dad's car. He said we can't touch it. It's not a toy." Ben ignored her. He grabbed the yellow car and pulled it down. He pressed a button and the car made a loud noise. Ben smiled and held the car in his hands. "Look, Lily, this car is awesome! It can make sounds and lights. And look, it can release fire from the back!" Ben said and pressed another button. A small flame came out of the car's tail. Lily gasped and backed away. She was scared of the fire. She saw that the fire was close to the rug. The rug was made of wool and it could catch fire easily. "Ben, stop! You're making a terrible mess! The fire can burn the rug and the house! Put the car back!" Lily said and tried to take the car from Ben. Ben didn't want to give up the car. He liked it too much. He pushed Lily away and held the car tighter. He pressed the button again and made more fire. "Leave me alone, Lily! This is my car now! I'm the best driver ever!" Ben said and laughed. But then, they heard a loud voice from the door. It was their dad. He had come home from work and he saw the smoke and the fire in the living room. He was angry and scared. "Ben! Lily! What are you doing? Drop that car right now! You're in big trouble!" Dad said and ran to them. He took the car from Ben and threw it in the sink. He turned on the water and put out the fire. He checked the rug and the sofa and saw that they were not burned. He sighed and looked at Ben and Lily. They were both quiet and sad. They knew they had done something bad. They had disobeyed their dad and played with his car. They had made a terrible mess and almost caused a fire. They were sorry and ashamed. "Dad, we're sorry. We didn't mean to. We just wanted to play." Lily said and hugged dad's leg. "Dad, we're sorry. We were stupid. We won't do it again." Ben said and hung his head. Dad hugged them both and kissed their heads. He was still angry, but he loved them. He knew they were young and curious. He knew they needed to learn and be careful. "I'm glad you're sorry. I'm glad you're safe. But you can't play with my car. It's not a toy. It's dangerous. You could have hurt yourselves or the house. You have to listen to me and respect my things. Do you understand?" Dad said and looked at them. "Yes, dad. We understand. We're sorry." Ben and Lily said together. "Okay. I forgive you. But you have to clean up this mess. And you have to play with your own cars. And you have to be good drivers. No more fire, okay?" Dad said and smiled. "Okay, dad. No more fire. We'll be good drivers." Ben and Lily said and smiled. They cleaned up the mess and played with their red and blue cars. They raced and honked and zoomed. But they didn't touch the yellow car. They left it on the shelf. They knew it was dad's car. And they knew it was not a toy.
