Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Jane and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Jane. She was playing in her garden when she saw a weird-looking plant. She couldn't resist getting closer to the plant to have a better look. Jane walked over to the plant and started to stir it. When she did that, a huge, purple monster jumped out of the ground! The monster said to her in a low and angry voice, "Why did you stir me? Now you have to come with me!" Jane started to cry and said to the monster, "Please, let me go. My mommy will be worried!" But the monster didn't care and said, "No way! You stirred me, so now you must come with me!" So the monster grabbed hold of Jane, and she couldn't escape. She was never seen again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse was wild and lived in the woods. One day, the mouse made a plan to find a new friend. The mouse went to the big tree and saw a bird. The mouse said, "Hi, bird! I am a wild mouse. Do you want to be my friend?" The bird said, "Yes, let's be friends!" The mouse and the bird played all day in the woods. They were very happy. The wild mouse was glad that his plan worked. From that day on, the mouse and the bird were best friends.

One day, a little crab woke up on a dirty beach. He was sad because his home was not clean. He wanted to make it nice for his friends. The crab met a fish in the water. The fish said, "Hello, crab! Why are you sad?" The crab said, "My home is dirty. I want to make it clean for my friends." The fish wanted to help the crab. They worked together to clean the beach. They picked up all the dirty things and put them in a big pile. After they finished, the beach was clean and pretty. The crab was very happy. He said, "Thank you, fish! Now my friends can come and play with me." The fish smiled and said, "You're welcome, crab! Let's be friends and keep the beach clean together." From that day on, the crab and the fish were best friends. They played together every day and kept the beach clean for everyone to enjoy.

Lily and Tom went to a big place with many people and many tables. They sat with Mom and Dad and looked at the pictures on the paper. The paper had many things to eat and drink. "What do you want, Lily?" Mom asked. "I want fish," Lily said. She liked fish. It was yummy and soft. "What do you want, Tom?" Dad asked. "I want fish, too," Tom said. He liked fish, too. But he liked it a different way. The man who worked at the big place came to their table. He had a pen and a paper. "Hello, what can I get for you?" he asked. Mom and Dad told him what they wanted. Then they told him what Lily and Tom wanted. "Lily wants fish with rice and peas," Mom said. "Tom wants fish with fries and ketchup," Dad said. The man nodded and wrote on his paper. "How do you want your fish?" he asked Tom. "Rare," Tom said. "Rare?" the man asked. He looked surprised. "Yes, rare," Tom said. He liked his fish rare. It was pink and juicy. "Are you sure?" the man asked. "Yes, I'm sure," Tom said. "Okay, rare fish for Tom," the man said. He smiled and went away. Lily looked at Tom. She did not like rare fish. She liked her fish well done. It was white and flaky. "Why do you like rare fish?" she asked Tom. "Because it's good," Tom said. "No, it's not," Lily said. "Yes, it is," Tom said. They started to argue. Mom and Dad told them to stop. "Everyone can like different things," Mom said. "That's right," Dad said. "Let's enjoy our food and be nice to each other." Soon, the man came back with their food. He gave them plates and forks and spoons. He gave Tom his rare fish. It was pink and juicy. He gave Lily her well done fish. It was white and flaky. They ate their fish and their sides. They drank their drinks. They said thank you to the man. They were full and happy. They liked fish. But they liked it different ways. And that was okay.
