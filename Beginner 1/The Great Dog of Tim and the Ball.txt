Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Dog of Tim and the Ball

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a silly dog with a big red ball. The dog had a very long nose. Tim wanted to play with the dog and the ball. Tim threw the ball very far. The dog ran fast to get the ball with its long nose. They played and laughed. Tim liked the dog a lot. The dog liked Tim too. Soon, Tim's mom came to the park. She saw Tim and the dog playing. She recognized the dog. It was their neighbor's dog, named Spot. Tim, Spot, and Tim's mom went home together. They had a fun day at the park.

Ben and Sam were brothers. They liked to play with their toys in their room. One day, they saw a big dish on the table. It was shiny and round. They wanted to touch it. "Look, Ben, a dish!" Sam said. "Can we play with it?" "I don't know, Sam. Mom said it is her dish. She said it is very old and hard to find. She said we should not touch it." Ben said. "But it is so pretty, Ben. I want to see it. Maybe it is magic. Maybe it can make wishes come true." Sam said. He did not listen to Ben. He ran to the table and grabbed the dish. "Sam, no! Put it back! Mom will be mad!" Ben said. He followed Sam and tried to take the dish from him. But Sam did not want to give the dish to Ben. He held it tight and pulled it away. He believed the dish was his. He believed the dish was magic. They fought over the dish. They pushed and pulled and shouted. They did not see the edge of the table. They did not see the dish slip from their hands. They did not see the dish fall to the floor. CRASH! The dish broke into many pieces. It was not magic. It was not hard. It was fragile and old. It was Mom's dish. Mom heard the noise. She came to the room. She saw the dish on the floor. She saw Ben and Sam with guilty faces. She was very angry. She was very sad. "Ben! Sam! What have you done? That was my dish! That was my grandmother's dish! That was the only thing I had from her! How could you be so careless? How could you be so selfish?" Mom said. She did not hug them. She did not kiss them. She did not forgive them. She sent them to their room. She did not talk to them. She did not play with them. She did not love them. Ben and Sam felt bad. They felt sorry. They felt lonely. They wished they had listened to Ben. They wished they had not touched the dish. They wished they had not broken the dish. They wished they had not broken Mom's heart. But it was too late. The dish was gone. The magic was gone. The love was gone. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had an owner named Tom. Spot and Tom played all day. One day, Spot saw a pale cat. The cat did not like Spot. Spot wanted to be friends with the cat. Spot tried to play with the cat, but the cat was mad. Spot did not know what to do. He started to think about how he could make the cat happy. Spot had an idea. He found a toy and gave it to the cat. The cat liked the toy. The cat played with the toy and with Spot. Spot and the cat were friends now. Tom, the owner, was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved the color orange. She had an orange ball, an orange shirt, and even an orange cat. One day, Lucy's mom suggested they make orange juice. Lucy and her mom went to the fridge to get oranges. They found a big orange in the fridge. But when they tried to open the fridge, it was stuck! They pulled and pulled, but the fridge would not open. Then, something unexpected happened. The fridge started to shake and wiggle. Lucy and her mom stepped back, and the fridge door opened all by itself! Out came the orange cat, holding the big orange in his mouth. The cat had been hiding in the fridge and was the reason it was stuck. Lucy and her mom laughed and made orange juice together with the big orange.
