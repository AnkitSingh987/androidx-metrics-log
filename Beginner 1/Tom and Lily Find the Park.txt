Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Park

Tom and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. One day, they saw a big truck in the park. The truck had a piano on it. The piano was shiny and black. "Wow, look at that piano!" Tom said. "I wonder who it is for?" "Maybe it is for the park," Lily said. "Maybe someone will play music for us." They ran to the truck and asked the man who was driving it. The man was kind and smiled at them. "Hello, kids. This piano is for the school. They are having a concert tonight. Do you like music?" "Yes, we do!" Tom and Lily said. "Would you like to hear a song?" the man asked. "Yes, please!" Tom and Lily said. The man opened the truck and lifted the piano. He put it on a big cart with wheels. He pushed the cart to a shady spot under a tree. He sat on a small bench and played a song. The song was fast and happy. It made Tom and Lily feel like they could soar in the sky. They clapped and cheered when the man finished. The man bowed and thanked them. "That was amazing!" Tom said. "You are very good at playing the piano." "Thank you, Tom. You are very kind," the man said. "Do you want to try playing the piano?" Tom and Lily nodded. The man helped them climb on the bench. He showed them how to press the keys. Tom and Lily played some notes. They made their own song. It was not very good, but it was fun. The man laughed and clapped for them. He said they had talent. He said they could be musicians someday. Tom and Lily smiled. They liked the man. They liked the piano. They liked music. They had a wonderful day in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big tree with a small hole at the start of the path. Tim said to Spot, "I see something in that hole. Let's look!" As they got closer, they heard a tiny voice. It was a little bird with a terrible hurt wing. The bird said, "Please help me! I can't fly." Tim and Spot wanted to help the bird, so they picked it up and took it home. At home, they took care of the bird and its terrible hurt wing. After some days, the bird felt much better. Tim and Spot were so happy to see the bird fly again. The bird thanked them and said, "You are my heroes!" And from that day on, they all became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly rat. The rat lived in a small house. The house was near a big tree. The elderly rat was very happy. One day, the elderly rat heard a sound. He looked around and saw a bird. The bird could clap its wings. The elderly rat liked the bird. They became friends. The elderly rat and the bird played together. They clapped and danced. They had so much fun. They were happy friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to eat hot food. One day, Mia got an invitation to a big party. She was very happy and excited to go. At the party, there was a lot of hot food to eat. Mia wanted to eat everything. She saw her friend Tom, and he was sad. Tom did not have any food to eat. Mia thought for a moment and decided to share her hot food with him. Tom was very happy and thanked Mia. They both ate the hot food together and had a great time at the party. The other kids at the party saw Mia and Tom sharing and decided to share their food too. Everyone was happy, and they all learned that sharing is good.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a raft by the river. He wanted to play with it. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the raft?" His mom said, "Yes, but stay safe near the water." Tim played with the raft and had fun. He saw a frog near the water. The frog said, "Ribbit! I want to play too!" Tim let the frog on the raft. They played together. Then, a big wind came and pushed the raft far from Tim. The frog was scared. Tim was sad. He did not know what to do. A kind man saw Tim and helped him. The man had a long stick. He used the stick to get the raft back. Tim said, "Thank you!" The man said, "You're welcome. Always be careful and accept help when you need it." Tim learned to be safe and ask for help when he needed it.
