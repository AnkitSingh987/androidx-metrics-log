Category: Beginner
Title: Skip and the Tree

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Skip and Zip. Skip was a humble boy who was always kind and gentle. Zip, on the other hand, was proud and boastful. One day, when the two of them went out for a walk, Skip said to Zip, "Let’s have a race! The first one to the tree at the end of the path wins!" Zip, who was full of himself, quickly agreed. Skip, being the humble one, let Zip take the lead. He didn’t want to show off. Zip began skipping down the path but, as soon as he started, he stumbled and fell. Skip had waited there and said, "Let’s try again and this time, I will go first." He started off, hopping and skipping. When they arrived at the tree, Skip had already won the race. Zip, feeling a bit embarrassed, said to Skip, "Hey! You won because you were humble and didn’t show off." Skip smiled and said, "Showing off isn’t the way to win. You should be humble and kind" Skip and Zip learned an important lesson that day: humility will always be rewarded.

One day, a little girl named Mia went out to play. She wandered far away from her home. The sun went down and it got dark. Mia was lost and scared. She thought about her gentle puppy, Max, and wished he was with her. Mia saw a big tree and sat under it. She started to cry. A little boy named Tim heard Mia and came to help her. "Why are you crying?" Tim asked. Mia said, "I wandered away from my home and now I am lost. I had a nightmare about being alone in the dark." Tim said, "Don't worry, Mia. I will help you find your home." They walked together, hand in hand. Soon, they saw Mia's house. Mia was so happy! She thanked Tim and gave him a big hug. They became good friends and never wandered too far from home again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had her own phone. She loved to play games and talk to her friends on her phone. One day, it was very wet outside, and Sue wanted to go out and play. Sue put on her rain boots and raincoat. She took her phone with her to play outside. She jumped in the puddles and had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Sue dropped her phone in a big puddle. Her phone got very wet and stopped working. Sue was very sad. She went back inside her house and told her mom what happened. Her mom said, "Sue, you should not have taken your phone outside when it was wet." Sue learned that she needs to take care of her things and not use them when it is not a good time. From that day on, Sue never took her phone outside when it was wet. And she learned to care for her things better.

One day, a little brown dog named Max found a spade in the yard. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his best friend, a cat named Lily. Max wanted to play with the spade and dig holes in the dirt. "Hey Lily, look what I found!" Max said. "Let's play and dig holes with this spade!" But Lily wanted to take a nap in the sun and ignore Max. She closed her eyes and lay down on the warm grass. Max felt sad that Lily didn't want to play with him. But then, he saw a little boy named Tim playing in the yard next door. Max ran over to Tim with the spade in his mouth, wagging his tail. Tim saw Max and smiled. "Hi Max!" Tim said. "Do you want to play with me and dig holes with that spade?" Max barked happily and nodded his head. So Max and Tim played together, digging holes and having fun in the sun. And when Lily woke up from her nap, she saw Max and Tim playing and decided to join them.

Lily and Ben like to play with trains. They have a big box of trains and tracks. They make long and short trains. They make loops and bridges. They have fun. One day, they find a new train in the box. It is green and shiny. It has a big funnel on top. It can make steam. Lily and Ben want to play with the green train. "Please, can I have the green train?" Lily asks Ben. "No, I want the green train!" Ben says. They both pull on the green train. They do not share. They are not nice. The green train does not like this. It does not want to be pulled. It wants to be played with nicely. It makes a loud noise. It makes a lot of steam. The steam is hot and wet. It makes Lily and Ben let go of the green train. "Ow! That hurts!" Lily says. "Ouch! That is not fun!" Ben says. They look at the green train. It is still making steam. It looks angry. Lily and Ben feel sorry. "Sorry, green train," Lily says. "Sorry, green train," Ben says. They decide to share the green train. They take turns to make it go. They make it go fast and slow. They make it go up and down. They make it happy. The green train makes less steam. It makes a happy noise. Lily and Ben are happy too. They play with the green train and the other trains. They are nice. They have fun.
