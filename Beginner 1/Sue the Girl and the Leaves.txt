Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Leaves

One day, a girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw pretty, delicate leaves on the ground. It was fall. Sue wanted to scatter the leaves and jump in them. She called her friend Tim to come play too. "Tim, come play with me. We can scatter leaves and jump in them," Sue said. Tim came running to play with Sue. They were both very happy. As they played, they found a big pile of leaves. They decided to scatter the leaves and jump in them together. But when they jumped, something unexpected happened. They fell into a deep hole that was hidden under the leaves. Sue and Tim were stuck and could not get out. They called for help, but no one could hear them. And so, Sue and Tim stayed in the hole with a bad ending to their day.

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to sing. He flew to a big tree and saw many shapes. Tim saw a round shape, a square shape, and a star shape. He liked the star shape best. He sat on the star shape and started to sing. While Tim was singing, the star shape started to feel hot. Tim was surprised. He did not know why the star shape was hot. He kept singing, and the star shape got hotter and hotter. Tim was scared, but he did not want to stop singing. Suddenly, the star shape moved! Tim was very surprised. The hot star shape was not a shape at all! It was a big, friendly dragon. The dragon liked Tim's song so much that he woke up. Tim and the dragon became good friends, and they sang songs together every day.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play with blocks. They make a big castle with blocks. They put a flag on the castle. They are happy. But Ben is not happy. Ben is mean. He wants to play with the blocks too. He does not ask Tim and Sam. He just takes some blocks from the castle. He makes a small castle. He puts a hat on the castle. He says, "My castle is better than your castle. Your castle is stupid." Tim and Sam are sad. They do not like Ben. They say, "Ben, stop! Give us back our blocks. You are not nice. You are stupid." Ben does not listen. He takes more blocks from the big castle. He makes his small castle bigger. He says, "No, I will not give you back your blocks. I will take all your blocks. I will make the best castle ever. You are silly. You are stupid." Tim and Sam are angry. They do not want to play with Ben. They want to play with their big castle. They have an idea. They say, "Ben, look! There is a bird on your hat. It is a funny bird. It is a stupid bird." Ben looks up. He sees a bird on his hat. He says, "What? A bird on my hat? That is not funny. That is stupid. Go away, bird. Go away." But the bird does not go away. The bird pecks Ben's hat. The bird makes a hole in Ben's hat. Ben says, "Ow! Ow! Stop, bird. Stop. You are hurting me. You are stupid." Tim and Sam smile. They see a chance. They run to Ben's castle. They take back their blocks. They pass the blocks to each other. They make their big castle bigger. They put a new flag on the castle. They say, "Thank you, bird. You are smart. You are nice. You are not stupid." Ben is sad. He has no blocks. He has no castle. He has no hat. He has a hole in his hat. He says, "Please, Tim and Sam. Please, let me play with you. Please, let me have some blocks. Please, let me have a castle. Please, let me have a hat. Please, please, please." Tim and Sam think. They say, "Ben, you were mean. You were not nice. You were stupid. But we are not mean. We are not stupid. We are nice. We will let you play with us. We will give you some blocks. We will help you make a castle. We will share our hat. But you have to say sorry. You have to be nice. You have to be smart. You have to be not stupid." Ben says, "Sorry, Tim and Sam. Sorry, bird. I was mean. I was not nice. I was stupid. I will be nice. I will be smart. I will be not stupid. Thank you, Tim and Sam. Thank you, bird. Thank you, thank you, thank you." Tim and Sam say, "You are welcome, Ben. You are welcome, bird. You are welcome, welcome, welcome." They all play with blocks. They all make castles. They all wear hats. They all are happy. They all are friends. They all are not stupid. The end.
