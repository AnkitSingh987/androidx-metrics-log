Category: Beginner
Title: The Swan and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big swan. The swan lived in a pond. The swan was very serious. It did not like to play. It liked to watch the water and the fish. One day, the swan saw a long string in the water. The string was around a fish. The fish could not swim well. The swan wanted to help the fish. The swan used its beak to cut the string. The string fell off the fish. The fish was free. The fish was very happy. The serious swan was happy too. The swan and the fish became friends. They swam together in the pond. The swan learned that helping others can be fun.

Once there was a cute cat. The cat lived in a small house. One day, the cat wanted to pour milk into a bowl. But, the milk was all gone. So, the cat went to find more milk. The cat walked and walked. Then, the cat saw a big jail. The cat thought, "Maybe there is milk in the jail." The cat went inside the jail. The cat looked for milk. In the jail, the cat found a big bowl of milk. The cat was very happy. But, the milk was not for the cat. The milk was for a big dog. The dog was not in the jail. The dog was outside. The dog came back and saw the cat. The dog and the cat became friends. They shared the milk and played together.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big flag on a pole. The flag is red and white and blue. It has stars and stripes. "Look, Anna, a flag!" Ben says. "Let's go and touch it!" Anna shakes her head. "No, Ben, we can't. The flag is not for us. It is for the people who work here. They are very important. We have to listen to them and be good." Ben does not listen to Anna. He thinks he is very intelligent. He runs to the flag and pulls it. He wants to take it home and show his mom. "Stop, Ben, stop!" Anna shouts. "You are making a big mistake!" But Ben does not stop. He pulls harder and harder. The flag comes off the pole. Ben is happy. He holds the flag in his arms. But then, he hears a loud siren. He sees a big car with lights and a badge. He sees a man in a uniform. The man is angry. He runs to Ben and grabs the flag. "Hey, kid, what are you doing?" the man says. "This is not your flag. This is the flag of the park. You are breaking the law. You are in big trouble!" Ben is scared. He drops the flag and cries. He looks for Anna. But Anna is not there. She ran away when she saw the car. She did not want to be in trouble too. Ben is alone. He wishes he listened to Anna. He wishes he did not touch the flag. He wishes he was not so intelligent. He wishes he was at home with his mom.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a colorful house. In this house lived a kind man named Bob. Bob liked to repair things for his friends. One day, Bob saw smoke coming from his friend Sue's house. He ran to Sue and said, "Sue, there is smoke in your house! Let me help you." Sue was scared but happy that Bob was there to help. Together, they put out the smoke and found a broken toy. Bob said, "I can repair this toy for you, Sue." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Bob!" After fixing the toy, they played all day in the colorful house.

Once upon a time, there was a cricket. He was a flexible cricket. He could jump high and far. The cricket lived in a dirty house. He did not like it. So, he decided to clean the house. The cricket started to clean. He cleaned the floor. He cleaned the walls. He cleaned the windows. The house became very clean. The cricket was happy. But one day, while the cricket was cleaning, he fell. He hurt his leg. The cricket could not jump anymore. He could not clean the house. The house became dirty again. The cricket was sad. The end.
