Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Whale

Tom and Mia went to the sea with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and look at the waves. Sometimes they saw big fish jump out of the water and make a loud noise. Mom said they were whales. One day, Tom and Mia saw a very big whale near the shore. It was gray and had no hair on its head. Mom said it was a bald whale. Tom and Mia wanted to see it better, so they ran to the water. But the whale saw them too, and it opened its mouth and splashed them with water. "Wow, that was fun!" Tom said, laughing and wiping his face. "Let's do it again!" Mia said, jumping up and down. But the whale had other plans. It swam away, waving its tail at them. Tom and Mia waved back, and said, "Bye, bald whale! Thank you for the splash!" Mom and Dad came to get them, and gave them towels to dry off. They said they were very brave to play with the whale, and that they had a special friend in the sea. Tom and Mia smiled, and hugged Mom and Dad. They hoped to see the bald whale again.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, Tom has a new toy. It is a rod. He can make bubbles with it. He dips the rod in a bottle of soap and blows. Big bubbles fly in the air. "Wow, that is fun!" Lily says. "Can I try?" "Sure, you can try," Tom says. He gives the rod to Lily. She dips it in the soap and blows. But no bubbles come out. She tries again and again. Nothing happens. "This rod is dull," Lily says. "It does not work." Tom takes the rod back. He dips it in the soap and blows. Bubbles fly in the air again. "See, it works," Tom says. "You have to blow harder." Lily feels sad. She does not like the rod. She likes her doll better. She goes to get her doll from her bag. "Where are you going?" Tom asks. "I am going to play with my doll," Lily says. "She is not dull. She can talk and sing and dance." Tom feels lonely. He does not like to play with the rod by himself. He likes Lily better. "Can I play with your doll too?" Tom asks. Lily thinks for a moment. She likes Tom. He is her friend. "Okay, you can play with my doll too," Lily says. "But you have to invite me to play with your rod again." "Deal," Tom says. He smiles. He invites Lily to play with his rod again. They take turns making bubbles. They also play with the doll. They talk and sing and dance. They have fun. They are happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim was feeling adventurous. He wanted to play a game. He asked his mom, "Can we play hide and seek?" His mom said, "Yes, let's play!" Tim ran to the kitchen and hid behind the freezer. His mom started to count. While he was hiding, Tim heard a strange noise. He looked around and saw a little bird. The bird was stuck in a box. Tim wanted to help the bird. He opened the box and the bird was free. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim's mom found him and the bird. She was surprised to see the bird. She said, "Wow, Tim! You saved the bird!" They all laughed and played together. They were happy to have a new friend. And from that day on, they had many more adventurous games together.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to bounce all day long. His favorite thing to do was to bounce from one color to another in the big meadow. One day, Remy saw a big rainbow in the sky. It had many colors. Remy wanted to bounce on the colors of the rainbow. He thought it would be fun. So, he started to bounce very high to reach the rainbow. But Remy could not reach the rainbow, no matter how high he bounced. He felt sad. Then, a wise old owl saw Remy and said, "Remy, you don't need to bounce on the rainbow to have fun. Just enjoy the colors all around you in the meadow." Remy listened to the wise owl and learned that he didn't need to chase the rainbow to have fun. Remy went back to the meadow and bounced from one color to another. He had so much fun and was very happy. The moral of the story is to enjoy what you have and not chase things that are hard to get.
