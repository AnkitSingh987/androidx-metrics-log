Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a favorite shoe. The shoe was red and fit her foot just right. She loved to wear her shoe everywhere she went. One sunny day, Sue went to the park to play. She played on the swings and in the sandbox. She was so happy. But then, she saw a big dog. The dog was very loud and made Sue scared. Sue let out a scream. Sue's mommy heard her scream and came running to help. She picked up Sue and hugged her tight. Sue felt safe with her mommy. They went home, and Sue put on her favorite shoe. She knew that tomorrow would be a better day.

One day, a bright sun was in the sky. A little squirrel was in the park. The squirrel seemed happy. It was hopping and playing on the grass. A girl named Mia saw the squirrel. She said, "Hi, squirrel! Do you want to play with me?" The squirrel looked at Mia and seemed to say, "Yes, let's play!" Mia and the squirrel played in the park. They ran and jumped together. They had so much fun. After a while, Mia said, "Bye, squirrel! I will come back tomorrow." The squirrel seemed to say, "Bye, Mia! See you soon!"

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little twig. The twig was on the ground, near a tall tree. It wanted to grow tall like the tree. The twig looked up and said, "I want to grow tall like you!" The tall tree heard the twig and said, "You can grow tall like me. Just drink water and get sun. Then, you will grow and grow." The little twig was happy. It drank water from the rain and got sun from the sky. Day by day, the twig started to grow. It grew taller and taller. One day, the twig was not little anymore. It was a tall tree, just like its friend. The two tall trees were very happy. They talked and laughed every day. They were the best friends in the big forest. And so, the tall trees lived happily ever after. They grew together and helped each other, making the big forest a beautiful place to be.

Once upon a time, there was a fat mule named Mimi. Mimi lived on a farm with many other animals. Mimi had a big job to do. She had to carry things from one place to another. One day, Mimi had to load some hay onto her back. The farmer said, "Mimi, it's time to load the hay." Mimi nodded her head and let the farmer put the hay on her back. The other animals watched as Mimi carried the hay. As Mimi walked, she met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, Mimi! You are so strong." Mimi smiled and said, "Thank you, bird. I like to help." The bird flew away, and Mimi kept walking. She was happy to be a helpful, fat mule.

Lily and Ben were at the park with their mom. They saw a big truck with a picture of ice cream on it. Mom said they could have a treat if they were good. Lily and Ben ran to the truck and asked for ice cream. Lily wanted a cone with chocolate and Ben wanted a cone with vanilla. The man in the truck gave them their cones and smiled. Lily and Ben licked their cones and enjoyed the cold and sweet ice cream. They saw a boy on a bike who looked unknown. He had a red hat and a blue shirt. He rode his bike near them and stopped. "Hi, I'm Sam. Do you want to play with me?" he asked. Lily and Ben looked at him and nodded. They liked to make new friends. "OK, let's play tag. You're it!" Sam said and touched Ben's cone. Ben's cone fell from his hand and landed on the ground. Ben felt sad and angry. He liked his cone and it was gone. "Hey, that's not nice! You made me drop my cone!" Ben shouted. Sam laughed and rode away. He thought it was funny to tease Ben. Lily saw Ben's face and felt sorry for him. She wanted to help him. "Ben, don't cry. You can have some of my cone. I have enough for both of us." Lily said and offered her cone to Ben. Ben smiled and hugged Lily. He was glad to have a kind sister. "Thank you, Lily. You're the best." Ben said and took a bite of Lily's cone. Lily and Ben shared the cone and felt happy. They didn't care about Sam anymore. They had each other and ice cream. Mom saw them and smiled too. She was proud of Lily and Ben.
