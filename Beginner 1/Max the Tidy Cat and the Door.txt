Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Tidy Cat and the Door

One day, a tidy cat named Max was in the house. Max liked to sit on the couch. The couch was soft and big. Max saw the door was open. Max knew the door should be close. Max went to his friend, Dog. Max said, "Dog, the door is open. We need to close it." Dog was happy to help Max. They went to the door together. Max and Dog tried to close the door, but it was too big. Then, Max saw a big ball. Max had an idea. Max said, "Dog, let's use the ball to close the door." They pushed the big ball to the door. The ball hit the door and the door closed. Max and Dog were happy. They went back to the couch and sat together. Now, their house was tidy and safe again.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big pond with ducks and boats. Lily wanted to play with the boats. She saw a sailor boat with a red flag. She liked the flag. She ran to the boat and took it. "Hey, that's my boat!" a boy shouted. He was the owner of the sailor boat. He was angry. He ran after Lily. "Give it back!" he said. "It's not yours!" Lily did not listen. She wanted to keep the boat. She ran away from the boy. She tried to hide behind a tree. But the boy saw her. He grabbed the boat from her hands. "Ha! I got it!" he said. He looked at the boat. He saw that the flag was torn. Lily had ripped it when she took it. He was very sad. He loved his flag. Lily felt bad. She had hurt the boy and his boat. She wanted to say sorry. But the boy did not want to talk to her. He walked away with his boat. He did not look at her. Lily and Ben went back to their mom. They told her what happened. Mom was not happy. She said they did a bad thing. She said they had to learn to share and be nice. She said they had to go home and have no ice cream. Lily and Ben were sad. They wished they had not taken the boat. They wished they had played with the ducks instead. They learned a hard lesson. They hoped the boy and his boat would be okay.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a panda named Pandy. Pandy was a bit weird because he liked to wear a big red hat. All the other animals in the forest would look at Pandy and think he was funny. But Pandy did not care, he loved his big red hat. One day, Pandy was walking in the forest when he saw a small bird. The bird was very sad because it had no food. Pandy wanted to help, so he thought of what he could provide. He looked around and saw some yummy leaves on a tall tree. But the bird was too small to reach the leaves. Pandy had an idea! He took off his big red hat and put it on the ground. The small bird jumped on the hat and then onto Pandy's head. Pandy walked close to the tall tree, and the bird could now reach the yummy leaves. The bird was very happy and thanked Pandy for his help. From that day on, all the animals in the forest did not think Pandy was weird anymore. They saw that his big red hat was not just for fun, but it could also help others. And that was the unexpected thing that made Pandy a hero in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a thoughtful girl who loved to listen to the sounds around her. One day, she heard a new sound coming from the garden. Lily went outside to see where the sound was coming from. She saw a small, scared bird. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she thought of a plan. She wanted to introduce the bird to her friend, the cat. Lily brought the cat to the bird. The cat was nice and did not scare the bird. The bird felt happy and safe. They became friends and played in the garden together. Lily felt proud that she could help her new friends.
