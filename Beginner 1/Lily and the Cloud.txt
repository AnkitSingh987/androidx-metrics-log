Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Cloud

Lily liked to observe things. She liked to look at the sky, the trees, the birds, and the bugs. She liked to see how they moved and changed. She liked to ask questions and learn new words. One day, Lily went to the park with her family. She saw a big gray cloud in the sky. She asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "That is a cloud. It is made of water. Sometimes it rains from the cloud." Lily said, "I like rain. It makes puddles. I can splash in them." Her mom said, "Yes, rain can be fun. But sometimes it can be loud and scary. That is called thunder and lightning." Lily said, "I don't like thunder and lightning. They make me hide under my blanket." Her mom said, "It's okay to be afraid. But you don't have to worry. Your family is always with you. We will keep you safe and warm." Lily said, "I love my family. You are the best." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lily. You are the best." They hugged and smiled. Then they observed the cloud some more. They saw different shapes and colors. They made up stories and laughed. They had a good time at the park.

One day, a girl named Sue found a book. She had a plan to read the book to her friend, Tim. She went to Tim's house and said, "I have a book. Let's read it together!" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, let's read the book!" As they started to read the book, they saw a picture of a dead tree. Sue and Tim were sad to see the dead tree. They wished the tree was not dead. They turned the page and saw more pictures of dead trees. They did not like this book. Then, something unexpected happened. The last page had a picture of a big, green tree. Sue and Tim were surprised and happy to see the green tree. They said, "Wow! The tree is not dead anymore!" They closed the book and smiled. The book had a happy ending after all.

Once upon a time, there was a big sack in a room. The sack was very boring. It just sat there and did nothing. One day, a little boy named Tim found the sack. Tim thought the sack could be fun. Tim played with the sack. He jumped on it, and he hid in it. It was so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The boring sack started to move. Tim was surprised. The sack opened up, and out came a big, friendly dog. The dog was inside the sack all along! Tim and the dog played together and had lots of fun. The boring sack was not boring anymore. It was a big surprise and a happy twist for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside. One day, Mia went to the park to play with her ball. The park was full of mist. She could not see very far. Mia felt a little scared, but she wanted to play. Mia threw her ball very hard. The ball went far away into the mist. She could not see where it went. Mia walked and walked to find her ball. She heard a voice from the mist. It said, "Hi! I found your ball." Mia was happy to hear the voice. A little boy named Tim came out from the mist. He had Mia's ball. Tim said, "I am new here. Can I play with you?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes! Let's play together." Mia and Tim played with the ball and became good friends. The mist went away, and they had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. It was full of people who love to watch games. There was a boy named Tim who was deaf. But Tim loved the stadium too, even if he could not hear the cheers. One day, Tim went to the stadium to watch a game with his dad. They were having a good time. Tim could see the players running and kicking the ball. He was happy to be there with his dad. But then, something bad happened. A big storm came and it started to rain very hard. The people in the stadium were scared. They all ran to find a safe place. Tim and his dad tried to run too, but they got lost in the crowd. They could not find each other. Tim was very sad and scared. He could not hear his dad calling for him. He cried and cried, but his dad could not find him. The rain did not stop and the game was over. Tim never saw his dad again.
