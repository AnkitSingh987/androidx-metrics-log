Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Song

One day, a sleepy cat named Tom wanted to print a song. He went to his friend, a bird named Max. Max knew many songs. Tom asked Max, "Can you help me print a song?" Max said, "Yes, I can help you." They looked for a big box to print the song. But they could not find one. Tom and Max started to fight. "You are not helping me!" said Tom. "I am trying my best!" said Max. At last, they found a big box. But it was broken. They could not print the song. Tom and Max were very sad. The sun went down, and they went home without a song.

Lily and Max are twins. They like to play and talk. Sometimes they say the same thing. They repeat each other. One day, Mom makes dinner. She puts green beans, chicken and rice on the plates. She calls Lily and Max to the table. "Come on, kids, dinner is ready!" Mom says. "Dinner is ready!" Lily says. "Dinner is ready!" Max says. They repeat Mom. Mom smiles and gives them their plates. She tells them to eat their green beans. They are good for them. "Eat your green beans!" Mom says. "Eat your green beans!" Lily says. "Eat your green beans!" Max says. They repeat Mom. Mom laughs and says they are funny. She asks them if they like the chicken and rice. "Do you like the chicken and rice?" Mom says. "Do you like the chicken and rice?" Lily says. "Do you like the chicken and rice?" Max says. They repeat Mom. Mom shakes her head and says they are silly. She tells them to enjoy their dinner and stop repeating her. "Enjoy your dinner and stop repeating me!" Mom says. "Enjoy your dinner and stop repeating me!" Lily says. "Enjoy your dinner and stop repeating me!" Max says. They repeat Mom. Mom sighs and says they are cute. She gives them a hug and a kiss. She tells them she loves them. "I love you!" Mom says. "I love you!" Lily says. "I love you!" Max says. They repeat Mom. Mom smiles and says they are sweet. She tells them to finish their dinner and get ready for bed. "Finish your dinner and get ready for bed!" Mom says. "Finish your dinner and get ready for bed!" Lily says. "Finish your dinner and get ready for bed!" Max says. They repeat Mom. Mom nods and says they are good. She tells them to repeat after her one more time. "Good night!" Mom says. "Good night!" Lily says. "Good night!" Max says. They repeat Mom. They go to bed and dream of green beans, chicken and rice. They are happy.

One day, a boy named Tom found a cord in his room. It was a great cord. It was long and strong. Tom thought he could use it to tie things up. So, he took the cord outside to play. Tom met his friend, Sue. Sue saw the cord and said, "Wow, that's a great cord! Can I play with it too?" Tom said, "Yes, but we need to wash it first. It is dirty." So, Tom and Sue went to the river to wash the cord. They washed it until it was clean and shiny. Tom and Sue used the cord to tie their toys together. They had fun, but they did not know that the cord was not safe. The cord broke when they pulled too hard. Their toys fell into the river and floated away. Tom and Sue were sad. They learned that they should be careful with things they find and ask a grown-up if it is safe to use.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play together. They have a calendar on the wall. The calendar has pictures of animals and days. Tom and Anna look at the calendar every day. One day, Tom and Anna see a picture of a bear on the calendar. The bear is brown and big. The bear has a smile. The calendar says it is a special day. Tom and Anna are happy. They want to see the bear. Tom and Anna ask their moms if they can visit the bear. Their moms say yes. They pack some snacks and some toys. They get in the car and drive to the zoo. The zoo is a place with many animals. At the zoo, Tom and Anna find the bear. The bear is in a big cage. The bear is friendly. He waves his paw at Tom and Anna. Tom and Anna wave back. They give the bear some snacks. The bear eats them and says thank you. Tom and Anna give the bear some toys. The bear plays with them and says you are welcome. Tom and Anna have fun with the bear. They talk to the bear and learn his name. His name is Bob. Bob tells them stories about his life. Tom and Anna tell him stories about their life. They laugh and hug. They are friends. Tom and Anna say goodbye to Bob. They promise to visit him again. They thank their moms for taking them to the zoo. They go back to the car and drive home. They look at the calendar and see another picture of an animal. They wonder what animal it is. They are excited for the next special day.
