Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Rock

Tim and Sam were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big rock. It was gray and hard. Tim wanted to climb on the rock. He said, "Look, Sam, a big rock! Let's go up!" Sam was fearful. He did not like high places. He said, "No, Tim, it is too high. I am scared. Let's play with the ball." Tim was sad. He wanted to have fun with Sam. He said, "Please, Sam, it is not scary. It is fun. I will help you. Come on!" Sam shook his head. He did not want to go. He said, "No, Tim, I don't want to. You go. I will wait here." Tim was angry. He did not understand why Sam was so fearful. He said, "Fine, Sam, you are a baby. I will go by myself. You are not my friend." He ran to the rock and climbed on it. He felt happy and proud. He looked down and saw Sam. He shouted, "Look, Sam, I am on the rock! I am brave! You are not!" Sam felt sad and lonely. He wanted to be brave like Tim. He wanted to be his friend. He looked at the rock. He felt a little curious. He said, "Maybe it is not so bad. Maybe I can try." He walked to the rock and touched it. It was cold and rough. He felt a little nervous. He said, "Maybe I can go a little bit up." He put his foot on the rock and lifted himself up. He felt a little excited. He said, "Maybe I can go a little bit more." He climbed higher and higher. He felt a little scared. He said, "Maybe I can go to the top." He reached the top and saw Tim. He felt a lot of happy. He said, "Look, Tim, I am on the rock! I am brave, too!" Tim saw Sam and felt a lot of sorry. He said, "Sam, I am sorry. I was mean to you. You are brave, too. You are my friend." He smiled and hugged Sam. Sam smiled and hugged Tim. They said, "We are friends. We accept each other. We have fun together." They played on the rock and had a lot of fun. They were happy and proud. They were friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a stage. On this stage, many people liked to sing and dance. One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to sing on the stage too. But Mia felt helpless because she did not know how to sing. Mia asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me? I want to sing on the stage, but I don't know how." Her mom said, "Of course, Mia! I will help you learn to sing." So, Mia and her mom practiced singing every day. Mia was happy to have her mom's help. After many days, Mia was ready to sing on the stage. She was not helpless anymore. She went on the stage and sang a beautiful song. Everyone clapped and cheered for her. Mia was very happy and proud. She knew that with help, she could do anything.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a tutor named Mr. Fox. Mr. Fox was incredible at teaching Mia new things. Mia loved to learn. One day, Mia found a big, pretty rock. It was different from other rocks. It could glow! Mia showed the rock to Mr. Fox. She said, "Look, Mr. Fox! My rock can glow!" Mr. Fox looked at the rock and said, "Wow, Mia! That is a very special rock." Later, a mean bird saw the rock. The bird wanted the rock for itself. The bird tried to take the rock from Mia. Mia was sad and scared. She did not want to lose her glowing rock. Mr. Fox saw Mia was in trouble. He thought of a plan. He told Mia, "Let's hide the rock, so the mean bird cannot find it." They hid the rock under a big tree. The mean bird could not find the rock and flew away. Mia was happy that her rock was safe. She thanked Mr. Fox for his help. They went back to learning and playing together. Mia knew that Mr. Fox would always be there to help her.
