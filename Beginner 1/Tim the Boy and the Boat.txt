Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boat

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big pond with ducks. Tim wanted to play with the ducks. He saw a boat with rows. Tim thought it would be good to use the boat. Tim asked his mom, "Can I use the boat, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim was very happy. He got in the boat and started to row. The ducks came near the boat to say hello. Tim rowed the boat and played with the ducks. They all had a good time. After a while, Tim's mom called, "Time to go home, Tim!" Tim rowed back to the shore and said goodbye to the ducks. He had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball. One day, he went outside to roll his ball in the grass. Tom saw a little boy named Sam. Sam said, "Hi, Tom! Can I play with you?" Tom barked happily and let Sam roll the ball. They were having so much fun. Then, the ball rolled too fast and went into a drain. Tom and Sam could not get the ball out. They were sad. Tom and Sam went home without the ball. The end.

Anna and Ben were bored. They had no toys to play with. They had no books to read. They had no friends to visit. They only had each other. "Let's play a game," Ben said. "Let's pretend we are grown-ups. We can have tea and talk." "Okay," Anna said. "But where can we get tea?" Ben looked around. He saw a big pot on the stove. It had steam coming out of it. He thought it was tea. He did not know it was hot water. "I have an idea," he said. "You stay here. I will get the tea." He ran to the kitchen. He climbed on a chair. He reached for the pot. He did not see the sign that said "Do not touch". Anna heard a loud scream. She ran to the kitchen. She saw Ben holding his hand. He was crying. He had touched the pot. It was very hot. It had burned him. "Ben, what did you do?" Anna asked. "I wanted to get tea," Ben said. "But it was not tea. It was hot water. It hurt me." Anna felt sorry for Ben. She hugged him. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. You should not touch things you do not know. It can be dangerous. Come on, let's go to mom. She will make you feel better." They went to mom. Mom saw Ben's hand. She was worried. She put some ice on it. She gave him a kiss. She said, "Ben, you were very brave. But you should be more careful. You should always ask before you touch something. Especially if it is hot. Do you understand?" Ben nodded. He said, "Yes, mom. I understand. I'm sorry. I will not touch things I do not know again." Mom smiled. She said, "That's good, Ben. I'm glad you learned your lesson. Now, how about some real tea? And some cookies? And a story?" Anna and Ben smiled. They said, "Yes, please, mom. That sounds fun." They went to the living room. They had tea and cookies and a story. They were not bored anymore. They were happy.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a phone that could make sounds and send messages. They liked to pretend they were ordering food or talking to their friends. One day, they decided to order pizza for lunch. They used the phone to call the pizza place and said what they wanted. "Hello, we want pizza with cheese and ham, please. And juice, too. Thank you, bye!" They hung up and waited for their pizza. But they did not know that the phone was not a toy, but a real phone that belonged to their dad. Their dad had left it on the table by mistake. He was at work and did not see the message from the pizza place. The pizza place thought it was a real order and made the pizza and juice. They put them in a box and a bag and drove to Ben and Lily's house. When the pizza man knocked on the door, Ben and Lily were very excited. They opened the door and saw the pizza and juice. They thought it was funny that their phone worked. They took the pizza and juice and said thank you. They did not pay the pizza man, because they did not have any money. They closed the door and ran to the kitchen to eat their pizza. The pizza man was very angry. He did not think it was funny that two kids ordered pizza and did not pay. He called their dad and told him what happened. Their dad was very surprised and upset. He had to pay the pizza man a lot of money. He also had to leave work and go home to talk to Ben and Lily. He was not happy with them. He scolded them and took away their phone. He said they could not play with it anymore. He said they had to say sorry to the pizza man and learn to be more careful. Ben and Lily felt very bad. They did not mean to cause trouble. They just wanted to have fun. They said sorry to their dad and the pizza man. They learned their lesson. They never played with the phone again. They only played with their toys.
