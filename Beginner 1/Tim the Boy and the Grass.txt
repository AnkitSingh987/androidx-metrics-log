Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the woods behind his house. One day, while he was playing, he saw a big log. The log was very old and had many bugs living in it. Tim was not scared of the bugs; he liked to watch them as they moved around. As Tim was watching the bugs, he heard a small voice. It was a tiny bug that could speak! The bug looked hurt and sad. "Hello," said the bug. "I am lost and my leg is hurt. Can you help me find my family?" Tim wanted to help the hurt bug, so he picked it up very gently and put it on his hand. Together, they walked through the woods, looking for the bug's family. They looked under rocks, in the grass, and behind trees. Finally, they found the bug's family near the big log. The hurt bug was so happy to be home and thanked Tim for helping it. Tim felt good that he could help his new bug friend. From that day on, Tim and the bug would play together in the woods, and they were the best of friends.

Sam and Mia like to play in the garden. They have a toy car that can zoom very fast. They take turns to push the car on the grass and watch it go. One day, they see a big ant on the ground. The ant is black and shiny. It has six legs and two feelers. It is carrying a green leaf in its mouth. "Look, Mia, an ant!" Sam says. "It is very strong. It can lift a leaf bigger than its head." "Wow, Sam, you are right. It is amazing. Where is it going?" Mia asks. They follow the ant with their eyes. The ant is walking towards a small hole in the dirt. It is the ant's home. There are many other ants there. They all work together to make their home. "Can we zoom the car near the ant's home?" Sam asks. "Maybe they will like it." "I don't know, Sam. Maybe they will be scared. Maybe they will think it is a monster." Mia says. They decide to try. They push the car gently towards the hole. The car zooms on the grass. The ants see the car. They stop what they are doing. They run away from the car. They hide in the hole. "Oh no, Sam, we made them run away. They don't like the car. They are afraid." Mia says. "I'm sorry, Mia. I didn't mean to scare them. I wanted to be their friend." Sam says. They pick up the car and move away from the hole. They wait for a while. They see the ants come out again. They resume their work. They carry more leaves and seeds. "Look, Mia, they are back. They are not afraid anymore. They are busy." Sam says. "Yes, Sam, they are back. They are brave. They are smart." Mia says. They smile at each other. They decide to leave the ants alone. They play with the car somewhere else. They have fun. They respect the ants. They learn from them.

One day, a goose went to the park. The goose saw a big pond. The goose was happy and wanted to play in the water. The goose started to rush to the pond. It was so much fun! In the pond, the goose saw a frog. The frog was green and small. The goose and the frog became friends. They played together in the water. They splashed and laughed all day. After playing, the goose was hungry. The goose found some delicious grass near the pond. The goose ate the grass and felt happy. The goose and the frog had a great day at the park.

Once there was a little boy called Tim who wanted to learn how to play the drum. He asked his mom if he could have one. His mom said yes and she bought one for him. Every day, Tim practiced hard and listened to his mom. He was very obedient so he could manage to make beautiful music with the drum. One day, some friends of Tim asked him to join in their game. But Tim said no, he needed to practice the drum. His friends thought he was weird but Tim did not care, he was determined to keep practicing. Soon, Tim became very good at playing the drum. His friends were so amazed, they all praised him for his hard work and perseverance. The moral of the story is that if you are obedient and manage to do things hard, you will be surprised by the progress you make and how great the results can be.
