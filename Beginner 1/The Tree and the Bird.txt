Category: Beginner
Title: The Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big old tree. The tree had many logs. One day, a small bug found a log to live in. The bug was happy in his new home. The bug had a friend. The friend was a little bird. The bird would blink its eyes when it saw the bug. They liked to play together near the old tree. One day, it rained. The log got wet, but the bug and the bird stayed dry. They played inside the log and had fun. They were happy to have the old tree and the log as their home.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a guard dog. He liked to keep his home safe. Max was very dirty because he played in the mud all day. One day, Max saw a big ball in the yard. He wanted to play with it. Max tried to stretch his legs to reach the ball. He stretched and stretched, but the ball was too far away. Max felt sad. He wanted the ball so much. He had an idea. Max would try to stretch again, but this time, he would run and jump too. The next day, Max tried his plan. He stretched his legs, ran very fast, and jumped high in the air. This time, Max reached the ball! He was so happy. Max played with the ball all day. From that day on, Max knew that if he stretched and tried hard, he could reach anything he wanted.

---

Once upon a time, there was a great big backyard. In the backyard, there lived a dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball all day. One day, Spot's ball went into the woods. Spot was sad. He did not want to go into the woods, but he wanted his ball back. So, Spot went into the woods to find his ball. In the woods, Spot found a big, friendly bear. The bear had Spot's ball! The bear wanted to play too. Spot did not expect this. Spot accepted the bear as his new friend. They played together all day in the great big backyard.

One day, a yellow bird went to the beach. The bird wanted to find a friend. The bird saw a little boy playing in the sand. The yellow bird went to the boy and said, "Will you be my friend?" The little boy looked at the bird and said, "Yes, I will be your friend." They played all day. They built a big sand castle together. The yellow bird was happy to have a friend. But then, a big wave came and washed the sand castle away. The yellow bird tried to save it but did not succeed. The little boy was sad and cried. The yellow bird felt bad and flew away. They were not friends anymore.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She wore her favorite pants. They were different from other pants. They were red and had big yellow stars on them. She loved her pants very much. In the park, Lily saw a boy named Tim. Tim had pants with blue stripes. They started to play together. They played on the swings and went down the slide. Then, Tim said, "My pants are better than yours. Blue stripes are cooler than yellow stars." Lily felt sad and did not want to play anymore. Lily went home and told her mom what happened. Her mom said, "Your pants are special because they are different. It's okay to be different." The next day, Lily went back to the park. She met Tim again. This time, Tim said, "I am sorry. Your pants are cool too. Let's play together." They played and had fun. They learned that being different is good.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with lots of food hanging from it. He felt very lucky to find this tree. Tim wanted to take some food home for his family. But there was a problem. The food was too high for him to reach. Tim saw a tall man near the tree. He asked the man, "Can you help me get the food?" The tall man said, "Sure, little boy." He picked some food from the tree and gave it to Tim. Tim was very happy and thanked the man. But then, a big wind came and started to blow. The wind was so strong that it made everything freeze. Tim and the tall man were scared. They worked together to find a warm place to hide. Soon, the wind stopped, and everything went back to normal. Tim and the tall man were safe and felt lucky to have each other's help. They went home with the food, and Tim shared it with his family.
