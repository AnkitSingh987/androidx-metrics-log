Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toy car. One day, her toy car's gear was broken. Amy was sad. She went to her dad and said, "Dad, my car is broken. Can you fix it?" Her dad looked at the broken gear and said, "Don't worry, Amy. I will fix it for you." Her dad worked hard to fix the gear. He did not quit until it was fixed. Amy was happy when her dad gave her the toy car back. "Thank you, Dad!" Amy said. They played together with the toy car. Now, the car was not broken anymore. They had fun and lived happily ever after.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big train with many cars. They wanted to get closer and see the train. They ran to the rail where the train was. But they did not see the sign that said "Do not cross". "Wow, look at the train!" Ben said. "It is so long and loud!" "Yes, and it has many colors!" Lily said. "Can we touch it?" "No, we cannot touch it," Ben said. "It is too fast and hot. We have to stay away from the rail." But Lily did not listen to Ben. She wanted to touch the train. She stepped over the rail and walked to the train. She reached out her hand to touch the train. "Stop, Lily, stop!" Ben shouted. "The train is dangerous! Come back!" But Lily did not hear Ben. She was too curious. She touched the train. But the train was moving. It pulled Lily with it. Lily screamed. She was scared. She let go of the train. But she fell. She fell into a deep hole next to the rail. She could not get out. "Help, help!" Lily cried. "I am in a deep hole! I cannot see!" Ben heard Lily. He was scared too. He wanted to help Lily. But he did not know how. He looked around. He saw a man. The man was wearing a hat and a badge. He was a train worker. He knew about trains and rails. "Help, help!" Ben ran to the man. "My sister is in a deep hole! She touched the train and fell! Please help her!" The man heard Ben. He saw the hole. He saw Lily. He was worried. He ran to the hole. He reached out his hand to Lily. "Grab my hand, little girl," the man said. "I will pull you out. Do not be afraid. Trust me." Lily saw the man. She saw his hand. She was still scared. But she wanted to get out. She trusted the man. She grabbed his hand. The man pulled her out. He lifted her up. He hugged her. "Are you okay, little girl?" the man asked. "Are you hurt?" Lily shook her head. She was not hurt. She was just dirty and wet. She hugged the man back. "Thank you, thank you," Lily said. "You saved me. You are very nice." The man smiled. He was glad. He was nice. He looked at Ben. Ben was happy too. He hugged Lily. "I am sorry, I am sorry," Lily said to Ben. "I did not listen to you. I was silly. I will not touch the train again. I will stay away from the rail." Ben nodded. He forgave Lily. He loved Lily. He was glad she was okay. "You are my sister," Ben said. "I love you. I will always help you." The man took Ben and Lily to their mom. Their mom was in the park. She was looking for them. She was worried. She saw them. She saw the man. She saw the hole. She saw the train. She understood what happened. She was angry. But she was also relieved. She hugged Ben and Lily. "Thank you, thank you," their mom said to the man. "You saved my children. You are very brave." The man smiled. He was brave. He was happy. He said goodbye to Ben and Lily and their mom. He went back to his work. He watched the train. Ben and Lily learned a lesson. They learned to listen to their mom and their brother. They learned to stay away from the rail and the train. They learned to trust the man who helped them. They learned to be careful and safe. They were happy. They were okay.
