Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Max and the Hill

Lily and Max love to play with their new jet. It is red and shiny and makes a loud noise when they push a button. They like to pretend they are flying to faraway places and having adventures. One day, they take their jet to the park and find a big hill. They think it would be fun to roll their jet down the hill and watch it fly. They run to the top of the hill and get ready to let go of their jet. But then they see a little boy at the bottom of the hill. He is playing with a ball and does not see them. Lily and Max are worried that their jet might hit him and hurt him. "Stop!" Lily shouts. "We have a jet!" The little boy looks up and sees them. He sees the jet, too. He thinks it looks cool and wants to play with it. "Can I see your jet?" he asks. Lily and Max are happy to share their jet, but they warn him to be careful. "Our jet is new and fast and loud," Max says. "You have to hold it tight and not let it go." The little boy nods and takes the jet. He pushes the button and hears the noise. He smiles and thanks them. Lily and Max smile, too. They are glad they did not hurt the little boy. They are also glad they made a new friend. They decide to play together with their jet and their ball. They have a lot of fun at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bird named Bob. Bob liked to poke things with his beak. One day, he found a pin on the ground. He picked it up with his beak and flew away. Bob saw his friend, a big dog named Max. Max was playing with a ball. Bob wanted to play too. He flew down and said, "Max, let's play!" Max wagged his tail and said, "Okay, Bob!" Bob poked the ball with the pin. The ball went "POP!" and air came out. Max looked sad. Bob said, "I'm sorry, Max. I didn't mean to." Max said, "It's okay, Bob. We can find a new ball." So, Bob and Max went to find a new ball to play with, and they had lots of fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim did not want to wake up. It was not a normal day. Today, he had to go to a new teach place. He was scared. He liked his old teach place. But his mom said he had to go. At the new teach place, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was nice. She said, "Do not be scared. I will help you." Tim felt better. They played with toys and had fun. But then, another boy named Sam took Tim's toy. Tim was sad. Sue saw Tim was sad. She said, "Let's talk to Sam." They went to Sam and said, "Can we all play together?" Sam said yes. They all played and had fun. Tim was not scared anymore. He liked the new teach place.

John was a three-year-old boy who loves to draw and color. One day, he asked his Mommy to take him to the store to buy some more crayons. He knew that if he had more crayons, he would be able to make even more colorful drawings. At the store, John was surrounded by boxes and boxes of different crayons and he wanted to buy them all! But, his Mommy said no. She said it would be too much for him to handle. She only got him a box of 16 different colorful crayons and John couldn't have been happier. On the way home, John enjoyed the colorful view outside the bus window. He made a plan for the day; he would relax in his room and draw with all of the new crayons. When John got home, he immediately asked his Mommy if he could go to his room to draw. She said yes and told him to relax and enjoy it. He gave his Mommy a big hug, put the box of crayons under his arm and ran upstairs to his room. John spent the entire day surrounded by his pictures and colorfully-drawn crayons. He was so calm and relaxed, the combination of the artwork and the vibrant colors of the crayons made him feel peaceful. He had never felt so happy and relaxed before.
