Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside. One day, she found a light glove in the yard. Sue wanted to pick flowers for her mom. She put on the light glove and went to the garden. There were red and yellow flowers. Sue was very happy. Sue picked the flowers with her glove. She took them to her mom. Her mom was happy too. They put the flowers in a cup. Sue and her mom had a great day.

Anna and Ben like to dance. They hear music from the big box. The music has rhythm. It makes them move fast and slow. They jump and spin and clap. Anna says, "Let's play lift. You lift me up and I lift you up. It is fun." Ben says, "OK. I lift you first. Hold my hands." Ben lifts Anna up in the air. Anna laughs. She feels like a bird. She says, "Now I lift you. You are not too heavy." Anna tries to lift Ben. But Ben is too heavy for Anna. She cannot hold him. He falls down. He hits his head on the floor. He cries. He has a big bump. Anna is scared. She says, "I'm sorry, Ben. I didn't mean to hurt you. Are you OK?" Ben says, "No, I'm not OK. You are bad. You dropped me. You are not my friend." Anna feels sad. She says, "Please, Ben. Don't be mad. I am your friend. I want to help you." But Ben does not listen. He runs away. He does not want to see Anna. He does not want to dance with her. He does not want to play with her. Anna is alone. She has no friend. She has no fun. She has no rhythm. She stops dancing. She sits on the floor. She cries. The music is still playing. But it sounds wild and loud. It hurts her ears. She wants it to stop. She wants Ben to come back. She wants to say sorry. But it is too late. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a curious little boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn new things and ask many questions. One day, he saw a big sign that said "Test your strength, win a prize!" He was very excited and wanted to try it. Tim went to the man who was selling tickets for the test. The man said, "Hello little boy, do you want to try the test?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" He gave the man some money and got a ticket to play. Tim tried the test and hit the big button with all his strength. The lights went up, and he won a prize! Tim was so happy and proud. He ran to show his mom and dad his new toy. They were very proud of him too. And that's how curious Tim had a fun day at the fair.

One day, a little boy named Tim found an envelope on the table. He was very curious about what was inside. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can I open this envelope?" His mom said, "No, Tim. That is not yours. It is for your dad." Tim was not happy. He wanted to see what was inside. So, he decided to poke a hole in the envelope with his finger. When his dad came home, he saw the hole in the envelope. He was sad and angry. He said, "Tim, you should not touch things that are not yours." In the end, Tim learned that he should always ask before touching other people's things.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom had a big head. He lived in the woods. Tom had a friend named Jerry. Jerry was a small mouse. They liked to play together. One day, Tom and Jerry saw a dangerous snake. The snake said, "I hate cats and mice!" Tom and Jerry were scared. They ran away from the snake. They hid behind a big tree. Tom said to Jerry, "We must be careful. The snake is dangerous." Jerry nodded his head. They made a plan to scare the snake away. They found a big stick and hit the snake. The snake went away, and Tom and Jerry were safe. They were happy and played together again.
