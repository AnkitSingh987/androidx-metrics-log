Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a normal house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a shiny marble. He would roll it around and watch it go. One day, Tim was playing with his marble in the living room. He rolled it too hard, and it went under the couch. Tim tried to reach for it, but it was too far away. He was sad because he could not get his marble back. Then, a friendly cat came into the room. The cat saw the marble and wanted to help Tim. The cat reached under the couch with its paw and pushed the marble out. The marble escaped from under the couch, and Tim was happy again. He thanked the cat and played with his marble all day long.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to make toast. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Okay, let's go to the kitchen." They walked to the kitchen together. Lucy and her mom took out the bread. They put the bread in the toaster. They pushed the button and waited for the toast to be ready. Lucy was very excited. When the toast popped up, Lucy and her mom took it out. They put butter and jam on the toast. They took a bite and it was delicious. Lucy was so happy she made toast with her mom. They enjoyed their yummy toast together.

Jen and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park. They see a big tree with many loops on it. The loops are brown and dull. They are not shiny or bright. "Look, Jen, loops!" Ben says. He runs to the tree and tries to pick a loop. But the loop is too high. He jumps and jumps, but he cannot reach it. "Let me try, Ben," Jen says. She is taller than Ben. She picks a loop from the tree. She holds it in her hand. It is hard and rough. She does not like it. "This loop is not fun," Jen says. She throws the loop away. It lands on the grass. "Let's find something else to play with," Ben says. He agrees with Jen. The loops are not fun. They run to the slide. The slide is shiny and smooth. They slide down and laugh. They have fun. They forget about the loops.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play together. But today, Lily is restless. She does not want to play with Max's cars or dolls. She wants to do something new. "Let's do yoga!" Lily says to Max. "Yoga is fun and good for you. You can stretch and bend and breathe." Max does not know what yoga is. He thinks it sounds strange. He likes to run and jump and kick. He does not want to stretch and bend and breathe. "What is yoga?" Max asks Lily. "Is it a game? Is it a toy? How do you play it?" Lily shows Max her yoga mat. She says it is a soft place to do yoga. She rolls it out on the floor. She tells Max to sit on it with her. "Yoga is not a game or a toy. It is a way to move your body and calm your mind. You can do different poses and follow your breath. It is easy and fun. I will show you." Lily says. Lily starts to do some yoga poses. She does a cat pose, a dog pose, a tree pose, and a butterfly pose. She tells Max to copy her. Max tries to do the same, but he feels silly and bored. He does not like yoga. He wants to play something else. "Yoga is not fun. It is hard and boring. I don't want to do it. Let's play something else." Max says. Lily is sad. She likes yoga. She thinks it is fun and good for her. She wants Max to like it too. She does not want to play something else. "Yoga is fun. It is easy and good for you. You just have to try it. Please, Max, do yoga with me." Lily says. Max and Lily look at each other. They are friends. They do not want to make each other sad. They want to have fun together. They decide to compromise. "OK, Lily, I will do yoga with you for a little bit. But then, you have to play with me something I like. Deal?" Max says. "Deal, Max. Thank you for trying yoga with me. I will play with you something you like. What do you want to play?" Lily says. Max thinks for a moment. He smiles. He has an idea. "Let's play tag! Tag is fun and good for you. You can run and jump and chase. It is easy and fun. I will show you." Max says. Lily agrees. She likes tag. She thinks it is fun and good for her. She wants to play with Max. She rolls up her yoga mat. She gets ready to run. "OK, Max, let's play tag. But first, you have to catch me. I am fast and smart. You can't catch me. Ready, set, go!" Lily says. Max and Lily start to play tag. They run and jump and chase. They laugh and shout. They have fun together. They are not restless anymore. They are happy.
