Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Door

Tom and Lily were playing hide and seek in the house. Tom was the seeker and Lily was the hider. Lily ran to find a good spot to hide. She saw a door that was a little bit open. She thought it was a good place to hide. She opened the door and went inside. Tom counted to ten and then started to look for Lily. He looked under the bed, behind the sofa, and in the closet. He could not find Lily. He heard a noise from the other room. He went to the door and knocked. "Is Lily there?" he asked. "No, go away!" a voice said. Tom was confused. He did not know who the voice was. He opened the door and saw Lily. She was hiding behind a big chair. But she was not alone. There was a man in the room. He was wearing a hat and a coat. He had a bag in his hand. He looked angry. "Who are you?" Tom asked. "I'm a friend of your mom. I came to visit. Now go away!" the man said. Tom did not believe him. He guessed that the man was a bad man. He guessed that the man was trying to take something from the house. He guessed that Lily was in danger. "Help! Help!" Tom shouted. The man tried to run away. But he was clumsy. He tripped over the chair and fell down. He dropped his bag and his hat. Tom saw that the bag was full of shiny things. They were mom's jewels. The man was a thief. Mom and dad heard Tom's shout. They came to the room and saw the man. They called the police. The police came and took the man away. Mom and dad hugged Tom and Lily. They were proud of Tom for being brave and smart. They were happy that Lily was safe. They thanked Tom for saving the day.

Once upon a time, a little gray cat named Kitty lived in a small house. One day, Kitty saw a big tree with a yummy treat at the top. Kitty wanted to reach the treat, but the tree was very tall. Kitty tried to jump, but it was too high. Kitty asked a bird, "Can you help me reach the treat?" The bird said, "No, I cannot. I am too small." Kitty was sad, but she did not give up. She wanted to reach her goal. Kitty tried to climb the tree, but she slipped and fell. A big dog saw Kitty and said, "Do not worry, Kitty. I will help you reach the treat." The dog stood up on his back legs, and Kitty climbed on his head. With the dog's help, Kitty could reach the treat. They both shared the treat and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play games with her friends. One day, Sue and her friends played a guessing game. They had to guess what was in a box. The box was very fragile, so they had to be careful. Sue's friend, Tim, guessed that there was a toy car in the box. Sue's other friend, Lily, guessed that there was a doll in the box. Sue thought hard and guessed that there was a pretty glass cup in the box. They were all excited to see who would win. Sue's mom opened the fragile box very carefully. Inside the box, there was a pretty glass cup! Sue was so happy that she won the guessing game. Her friends clapped for her and they all had a fun day playing together.

One day, a little bird named Tim saw a big lime on a tree. He wanted to eat it, but the lime was too high. Tim was persistent. He did not give up. He thought, "I will dive and get the lime." Tim went up in the sky and dove down. "I will get the lime," he said. But he missed the lime. He tried again and again. He did not stop. A wise old owl saw Tim. The owl said, "Tim, do not give up. Keep trying." Tim listened to the owl. He tried one more time. This time, he got the lime. Tim was happy. The other birds saw Tim and said, "Good job, Tim! You did it!" Tim shared the lime with his friends. They all learned that if you are persistent, you can do anything.
