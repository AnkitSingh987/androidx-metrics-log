Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Grass

One day, a big dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a nice patch of grass and decided to lie down. As he lay there, a little bird named Tim came to sit near him. "Hi, I am Tim," said the bird. Max looked at him and said, "Hi, I am Max. I like this grass. It is soft." Just then, an angry cat came up to them. The cat did not like that Max and Tim were in her spot. She said, "Go away! This is my spot!" Max and Tim did not want to make the cat more angry, so they left. Max and Tim found a new patch of grass to lie on. They were happy and played together all day. The angry cat stayed in her spot, but Max and Tim did not mind. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Next to his house, there was an old neighbor named Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown was nice, but sometimes he would be grumpy. One day, Tim went outside to play. He saw Mr. Brown in his yard. Tim said, "Hi, Mr. Brown!" Mr. Brown looked at Tim and said, "Hello, Tim. Please behave and don't make too much noise." Tim played with his ball and tried to be quiet. But then, the ball went into Mr. Brown's yard. Tim was scared to get it. He didn't want Mr. Brown to be mad. But Mr. Brown saw the ball and said, "It's okay, Tim. You can get your ball. Just remember to behave." Tim got his ball and said, "Thank you, Mr. Brown!" From that day on, Tim always tried to behave and be a good neighbor to Mr. Brown. And Mr. Brown wasn't so grumpy anymore. They both became good friends and had lots of fun together.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play with a camera. They took pictures of their toys, their pets and their faces. They had fun making silly poses and funny noises. They were clever and knew how to use the camera buttons. One day, they found a big camera in the park. It was shiny and had a long lens. They wanted to try it and see what it could do. They did not see the sign that said "Do not touch". They did not know that the camera belonged to a mean man who was hiding behind a tree. They picked up the camera and looked through the lens. They saw a bird, a flower and a cloud. They smiled and pressed the button. The camera made a loud click and a flash. The mean man heard the noise and saw the flash. He was angry and ran towards them. He grabbed the camera from their hands and shouted at them. He said they were naughty and stupid. He said they broke his camera and he would make them pay. He scared them and made them cry. He did not care that they were sorry and did not mean to hurt his camera. He took them to his car and locked them inside. He said he would take them far away and sell them to a bad man. He drove away fast and did not look back. Lily and Ben were sad and scared. They wished they had not touched the camera. They wished they could go back to their homes and their families. They wished they could unite with their friends and be happy again. But they could not. They were trapped and alone. The end.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were friends. They liked to play together. They liked to watch TV too. They saw a cool show on TV. It was about a big cat and a small dog. Tim and Sue wanted to discuss the show. They talked about the big cat and the small dog. They liked how the cat and dog were friends. The cat was very cool and the dog was very funny. They laughed a lot. One day, Tim and Sue decided to act like the cool cat and funny dog. They played together and had a lot of fun. They were happy to have a cool TV show to watch and discuss.
