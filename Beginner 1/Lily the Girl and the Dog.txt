Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily had an amazing flute. She loved to play it all day. One day, she went to a big park to play her flute. While playing, a boy named Tim came up to her. He said, "Hey, can you delay your playing? I want to talk to you." Lily stopped playing and talked with Tim. They became friends. But then, a mean dog came and took Lily's flute. She was very sad. Tim tried to help but the dog ran away. Lily and Tim couldn't find the flute. They went home sad and the amazing flute was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. On the rock, there was a bee. The bee was sad. It sat on the rock and sighed. "Why are you sad?" asked a little girl. "I can't find my way home," said the bee. The girl wanted to help. They walked and walked. They saw a big tree. The bee was happy. "My home is in this tree!" said the bee. But, when they looked up, they saw a big bird. The bird was in the bee's home! The girl had an idea. She gave the bird a yummy treat. The bird flew away. The bee went back to its home. The bee was happy, and the girl was happy too. They were friends forever.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden at night. They liked to look at the stars and the moon. They had a big flashlight and a blanket. "Look, Tom, a comet!" Lily said. She pointed at the sky. A bright ball of fire was flying fast. It had a long tail of light. "Wow, it's so pretty!" Tom said. He smiled and clapped his hands. But then the comet came closer and closer. It made a loud noise like a big plane. It looked bigger and bigger. "Tom, I'm scared!" Lily said. She hugged her brother. "What if it hits us?" Tom was scared too. He did not know what to do. He wished their mom and dad were here. "Shh, Lily, it's okay. Maybe it will go away. Maybe it's just a toy." Tom said. He tried to sound brave. He turned on the flashlight and shone it at the comet. The comet saw the light. It did not like it. It changed its course and flew away from the garden. It went to another place. Lily and Tom saw the comet disappear. They felt relieved and happy. "Tom, you did it! You made the comet go away!" Lily said. She kissed her brother. "You are so smart and brave!" Tom smiled and hugged his sister. "You are too, Lily. You are my best friend. I love you." They snuggled under the blanket and watched the stars and the moon. They were not scared anymore. They were glad they had each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the pond with his dad. They wanted to catch a fish. They took a pole and a net with them. At the pond, they saw a big, heavy fish swimming in the water. Tim said, "Dad, look! That fish is so big!" His dad smiled and said, "Yes, it is. Let's try to catch it!" They tried and tried, but the heavy fish was too fast. Then, Tim had an idea. He put some food in the net and the fish swam in. They caught the fish! Tim's dad was so happy. He said, "Good job, Tim!" They both clapped their hands and laughed. They took the heavy fish home to show mom. She was so proud of them and everyone applauded.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful valley, there lived a little rabbit. He was always busy preparing for his day. One morning, he was getting ready to go out of his burrow when he heard a voice. "Help! Help!" the voice said. The rabbit stepped outside to see who was calling for help and he noticed a very rare white bird. The rabbit said, "What's wrong little bird? Are you stuck?" "Yes," the bird said. "I'm stuck in the tree and I can't get down!" So the rabbit hopped over to the tree and started to carefully prepare a plan to get the bird out. He brought a big ladder and managed to save her. The rabbit said, "There you go, you are safe now!" The bird thanked him and flew away into the sky. The rabbit watched with a smile on his face as the bird flew away. He was happy that he could help the rare bird and that he could make the valley even more beautiful.
