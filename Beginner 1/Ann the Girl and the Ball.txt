Category: Beginner
Title: Ann the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Ann. Ann had an old ball with a pretty pattern on it. She loved to play with her ball every day. One day, she was playing with her ball in the yard. While Ann was playing, a boy named Tom came to her. He said, "Can I play with you? I like your ball with the pretty pattern." Ann said, "Yes, you can play with me. Let's roll the ball to each other!" So, Ann and Tom started to roll the ball to each other. As they were playing, the ball rolled too far and went into the street. Ann ran fast to get her ball. But when she tried to grab it, a big car came and hit the ball. The ball with the pretty pattern broke into many pieces. Ann cried and cried because her old ball was gone. And that was the end of Ann's favorite ball.

One day, a little fish named Tim was eager to explore the big sea. He swam far away from his home. He saw a big blue whale named Bob. Bob was very big and strong. Tim wanted to be friends with Bob. Tim said, "Hi, Bob! Can we be friends?" Bob said, "Yes, but first, you must order the sea to be calm." Tim did not know how to order the sea, but he wanted to try. He wanted to be friends with Bob. Tim swam to the top of the sea and said, "Sea, please be calm." But the sea did not listen. Tim tried again and again, but the sea did not listen. Tim was sad. He thought he could not be friends with Bob. But then, Bob saw how hard Tim tried. Bob said, "Tim, you tried your best. Let's be friends!" Tim was happy, and they swam together in the big sea.

Tom and Sue are playing with their toys in the yard. They have a rocket, a car, a doll and a ball. They pretend that the rocket is a missile that can fly very fast and far. "Look, Sue, I can make the missile go zoom!" Tom says. He throws the rocket in the air and watches it fly. "Wow, Tom, you are so smart!" Sue says. She claps her hands and smiles. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is a big dog that lives next door. The dog sees the rocket and thinks it is a bone. He jumps over the fence and runs after the rocket. "Oh no, the dog wants our missile!" Tom says. He is scared of the dog. He grabs the car and tries to distract the dog. "Hey, dog, look here! I have a car! It is shiny and noisy!" Tom says. He makes the car beep and honk. But the dog does not care. He is faster than the car. He catches the rocket in his mouth and starts to chew it. "Help, Sue, help!" Tom says. He does not know what to do. Sue is brave. She loves the rocket. She does not want the dog to break it. She picks up the doll and the ball. She has an idea. "Dog, dog, look at me!" Sue says. She waves the doll and the ball in the air. "I have a doll and a ball! They are soft and fun!" The dog hears Sue. He is curious. He drops the rocket and looks at Sue. He likes soft and fun things. He runs towards Sue. "Good, dog, good!" Sue says. She throws the ball far away. "Go get the ball!" The dog chases the ball. He forgets about the rocket. He is happy. Sue runs to the rocket. She picks it up. She checks if it is okay. It is a little dirty and wet, but it is not broken. "Yay, Sue, you saved the missile!" Tom says. He runs to Sue and hugs her. "You are so smart and brave!" "Thank you, Tom, you are too!" Sue says. She hugs him back. "Let's play with the missile again!" They go back to their yard. They play with the missile and their other toys. They are happy. They are friends.
