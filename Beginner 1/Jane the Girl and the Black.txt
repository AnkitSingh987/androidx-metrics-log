Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Black

One day, a little girl named Jane found a black pebble on the ground. She picked it up and showed it to her friend, Tom. Tom wanted the black pebble too, so he took it from Jane's hand. Jane was sad. "Give it back!" said Jane. "It's mine!" "No, I want it!" said Tom. They both held the black pebble and did not want to quit. They pulled and pulled, but no one would let go. Jane's big sister, Sue, saw them fighting and came to help. Sue said, "You both want the black pebble, but you can't share it like this. Let's find another black pebble, so you both can have one." Jane and Tom agreed, and they all looked for another black pebble. Soon, they found one. Now, Jane and Tom each had their own black pebble, and they were happy. They played together and forgot about their fight.

One day, Tom and Sue went to play in the park. They saw many twigs on the ground. Tom had an idea. "Let's sort the twigs by size," he said. Sue liked the idea, so they started to sort the twigs. As they sorted the twigs, they found a very big and useful twig. They thought it would be great to make a toy with it. "Look, this twig is so big and useful!" said Sue. "We can make a fun toy with it." Just then, a little bird flew down and landed on the big twig. The bird looked at Tom and Sue and said, "Thank you for finding my home!" Tom and Sue were surprised. They didn't know that the big twig was the bird's home. They gave the twig back to the bird and helped it put its home back in the tree. The bird was very happy, and Tom and Sue were happy too. They learned that sometimes, the most useful thing is to help a friend.

One day, a little girl named Lucy had a red shoe. She loved her red shoe very much. She wore it all the time. One day, she lost her red shoe. She was very sad. Lucy asked her friend, Tom, "Did you see my red shoe?" Tom said, "No, I did not see it. But I will help you look for it." They looked and looked, but they could not find the red shoe. Then, they saw a big bird. The bird had the red shoe in its beak! The bird flew down and gave Lucy her red shoe back. The bird said, "I took your shoe to provide a home for my babies, but now they have a better home." Lucy was happy to get her red shoe back, and she was glad that the bird found a better home for its babies.

Once there was a boy named Tom. Tom lived on a farm with his mom and dad. They had a big field of wheat. Tom loved to play in the wheat. One day, Tom saw a filthy dog. The dog was very dirty and sad. Tom said, "Hi, dog! You are so dirty. I will help you." Tom took a cloth and began to wipe the dog. The dog wagged its tail and was happy. Tom's mom called him, "Tom, come inside! It's time to eat!" Tom said goodbye to the dog and went inside. The next day, Tom went to play in the wheat field again. But the wheat was gone! The filthy dog had eaten all the wheat. Tom's mom and dad were very sad. The farm had no more wheat, and they had no food. Tom was sad too. He wished he had not wiped the filthy dog.

One day, a little girl named Sue found an unusual toy. It was a magic wand. She picked it up with her hand and waved it around. As she waved it, her toy bear vanished! "Where did my bear go?" Sue asked her mom. Her mom was very surprised. "I don't know, Sue. Let's look for it together." As they looked for the bear, Sue waved the wand again. Suddenly, the bear appeared, but it was now big and could talk! "Hello, Sue!" said the bear. Sue and her mom were both very happy and played with the talking bear all day.
