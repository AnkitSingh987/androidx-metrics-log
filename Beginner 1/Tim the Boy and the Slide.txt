Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the park. His favorite thing to do was to slide down the big slide. It made him very happy. One sunny day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was deaf, so he could not hear what Tim said. Tim didn't know that, so he asked Sam, "Do you want to slide with me?" Sam did not answer, but he smiled and nodded his head. They went to the slide and took turns sliding down. After playing on the slide, Tim and Sam saw a door with a handle. Tim said, "Let's open it!" Sam watched Tim's mouth and understood. They held the handle together and opened the door. Inside, they found a room full of toys! They played together all day and became best friends.

Ben and Lily were playing with their cars in the living room. They liked to make noises and pretend they were racing. Sometimes they crashed their cars and made a big wreck. "Watch out, Lily!" Ben shouted. "My car is faster than yours!" He zoomed his car towards Lily's car and bumped it hard. Lily's car flew off the couch and landed on the floor. "Hey, that's not fair!" Lily cried. "You broke my car!" She picked up her car and saw that one of the wheels was loose. She tried to fix it, but it fell off. "Look what you did, Ben!" she said. "You ruined my car!" She threw her car at Ben and hit him on the head. Ben felt a sharp pain and started to cry. "Ow, ow, ow!" he wailed. "That hurt, Lily!" Mom heard the noise and came into the living room. She saw the wreck of the cars and the tears on Ben and Lily's faces. "What's going on here?" she asked. "Why are you two so grumpy?" Ben and Lily both tried to tell Mom what happened, but they talked at the same time and made a lot of noise. "Stop, stop, stop!" Mom said. "I can't understand you when you talk like that. Calm down and take turns." She sat down on the couch and asked Ben to go first. Ben told Mom how he was playing with his car and how he bumped Lily's car and how she threw her car at him. "That was not nice, Ben," Mom said. "You should not crash your car into Lily's car. You should be careful and gentle with your toys and your sister. How do you think Lily felt when you broke her car?" Ben thought for a moment and said, "She felt sad and angry." "That's right," Mom said. "And how do you feel when someone breaks your toy?" Ben said, "I feel sad and angry, too." Mom nodded and said, "So you see, Ben, you should not do to others what you don't want them to do to you. You should say sorry to Lily and help her fix her car." Ben looked at Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to break your car. Can I help you fix it?" Lily looked at Ben and said, "It's okay, Ben. I'm sorry, too. I shouldn't have thrown my car at you. That was mean. Can you help me find the wheel?" Ben and Lily hugged and said, "I love you, brother/sister." Mom smiled and said, "That's better. I love you both, too. Now, let's see if we can find the wheel and open the glue and fix the car."

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tom. Tom was very good at numbers. He loved to play with his toy screen that showed him numbers. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam wanted to play with the screen too, but Tom didn't want to share. They both started to fight. "I want to play with the screen!" said Sam. "No, it's mine!" said Tom. Tom's mom saw the fight and said, "Boys, you need to share the screen and play together." Tom and Sam listened to Tom's mom and started to share the screen. They had fun playing with the numbers together. The boys learned that sharing is important. They became better friends because they shared the screen. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy.
