Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Tom and the Bird

Lila and Tom are twins. They like to play outside in the snow. They make a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They give him a carrot nose and two coal eyes. "Look, Lila, a bird!" Tom says. He points to a small brown bird on a branch. The bird has a nest in the tree. The nest is made of sticks and leaves. "Hello, bird!" Lila says. She waves to the bird. The bird chirps back. It looks cold and hungry. "Can we feed the bird, Tom?" Lila asks. She has some bread in her pocket. She breaks a piece and throws it to the bird. "Sure, Lila, but be careful. Don't go too close to the nest. The bird might be scared," Tom says. He watches as the bird hops down and pecks at the bread. Lila wants to see the nest better. She walks to the tree and reaches for a branch. She pulls on the branch to lift herself up. But the branch is icy and slippery. Lila loses her grip and falls down. She lands on the snow with a thud. "Ouch!" Lila cries. She rubs her head and her arm. They hurt. "Are you okay, Lila?" Tom asks. He runs to her and helps her up. He hugs her and says, "I'm sorry, Lila. You should have listened to me. The branch was too icy." "I'm sorry, too, Tom. I was silly. I wanted to see the nest," Lila says. She sniffles and wipes her nose. The bird flies back to the nest. It looks at Lila and Tom. It chirps again. It sounds like it is saying, "Thank you for the bread. And be careful next time." Lila and Tom smile at the bird. They say, "You're welcome, bird. And we will be careful." They hold hands and walk back to their snowman. They are happy to have a new friend.

Once there was a boy named John. He really admired the pet he had, a small fluffy white puppy! His puppy was very bossy, but John thought it was funny. Every day, John liked to take his pet outside and admire the lovely garden. The pet would run around and sniff all the flowers! Its tail would wag and its eyes sparkle. John also liked to admire his pet inside the house. He would run around, chasing his pet and having lots of fun. The pet was very fast and would always manage to escape! John was so happy to have such a bossy little pet. He didn't mind when it would bark or when it wanted to be fed first. He would just smile at it and admire its cuteness.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big slide. Tom wanted to go on it, but Lily was scared. "Come on, Lily, it's fun!" Tom said. "I recommend you try it. You will like it." "No, Tom, it's too high and fast. I don't want to go on it. I urge you to stop asking me." Lily said. She held on to Tom's hand. Tom did not listen to Lily. He pulled her to the slide. He made her sit with him. He pushed them down the slide. Lily screamed. She felt terrible. She was dizzy and scared. She did not like the slide at all. Tom and Lily reached the bottom of the slide. Lily was angry and sad. She cried. She did not want to play with Tom anymore. "Tom, you are a bad friend. You did not listen to me. You made me do something I did not want to do. You made me feel terrible. I don't like you." Lily said. Tom felt sorry. He did not mean to make Lily feel terrible. He wanted to make her happy. He liked her. "Lily, I'm sorry. I was wrong. I should have listened to you. I should not have made you go on the slide. You did not like it. I like you. Please don't be mad at me." Tom said. Lily looked at Tom. She saw that he was sorry. She felt a little better. She liked him too. "Tom, it's okay. I forgive you. But next time, please listen to me. And don't make me do things I don't want to do. Let's play something else. Something we both like." Lily said. Tom nodded. He hugged Lily. He was happy. They were friends again. They went to play on the swings. They had fun. They listened to each other. They did not feel terrible anymore.
