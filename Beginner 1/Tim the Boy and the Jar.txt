Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Jar

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big jar. The jar was full of pretty rocks. Tim wanted to count the rocks in the jar. He sat down and started to count them one by one. Tim had a dog named Spot. Spot was deaf, so he could not hear Tim when he called. Spot saw the jar and wanted to play with it. He ran to the jar and hit it with his paw. The jar fell and broke. The pretty rocks went all over the floor. Tim was very sad. He could not count the rocks anymore. The jar was gone and the pretty rocks were not in one place.

Ben and his dog Spot like to play in the yard. Spot is a loyal dog. He always stays with Ben and does not run away. Ben has a ball and he throws it for Spot to catch. Spot runs fast and brings the ball back to Ben. One day, Ben and Spot see a big hole in the gate. The gate keeps them safe from the road. Ben is curious and wants to see what is on the other side. He tells Spot to wait and he goes through the hole. Spot does not like this. He barks and whines and tries to follow Ben. Ben sees a lot of cars and people and noise on the road. He is scared and wants to go back. But he cannot find the hole in the gate. He cries and calls for Spot. Spot hears Ben and tears the hole bigger with his teeth. He runs through the gate and finds Ben. He licks Ben's face and wags his tail. Ben hugs Spot and says sorry. He is happy that Spot is loyal and saved him. They go back to the yard and play with the ball. They do not go near the gate again.

One day, a bright sun was in the sky. A dog named Max went for a jog. Max was very happy. He saw a cat named Luna. Max said, "Hi Luna! I am going to a meeting with my dog friends. Want to come?" Luna said, "Yes, Max! I will come with you." Max and Luna jogged to the meeting. They saw many dogs there. They all played and had fun. But then, something big and round fell from the sky. It was a ball! The dogs and Luna looked at the ball. They did not know where it came from. Then, a big bird flew down. The bird said, "I am sorry! That is my ball. I dropped it." The dogs and Luna were surprised. They did not know birds played with balls too. Max said, "Do you want to play with us?" The bird was happy and said, "Yes, thank you!" So, Max, Luna, the dogs, and the bird played with the ball. They all had a great day. And from that day on, they were all friends.

One day, a big, impressive car went for a ride. It liked to speed down the long row of trees. The car went faster and faster, making the trees look like a blur. The car felt happy and strong as it raced down the row. As the car went faster, it saw a small, slow car up ahead. The big car thought it could pass the small car with no problem. It did not think about what could happen if it went too fast. The big car did not know that something was waiting for it. The big car went to pass the small car, but there was a big rock in the road. The big car did not see the rock in time. It hit the rock and broke. The small car passed the big car and kept going. The big car was sad and could not race anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Spot. Spot had a very big ear. One day, Spot was playing in the yard. He was running and jumping. He was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Spot saw a small cat in the yard. The cat was not scared of Spot. The cat was very brave. Spot started to tremble. He was not used to cats being so brave. He thought the cat was stupid for not being scared of him. The cat walked up to Spot and said, "Hello, big dog. I am not scared of you. I want to be your friend." Spot was very surprised. He never thought a cat could be his friend. But he liked the idea. So, Spot and the cat became friends. They played together in the yard every day. Spot learned that the cat was not stupid at all. And the cat learned that Spot was a very good friend.
