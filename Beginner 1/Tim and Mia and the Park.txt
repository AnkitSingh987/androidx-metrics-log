Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Mia and the Park

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big house. It is old and gray. It has no people inside. "Let's go in," Tim says. "Maybe we can find something fun." "No, Tim," Mia says. "That house is scary. It has ghosts. They will make noises and scare us." Tim laughs. "There are no ghosts, Mia. Ghosts are not real. They are only in stories. Come on, let's go in." Tim runs to the house. Mia follows him. She does not want to be alone. They open the door. It is dark and dusty. They hear a creak. "Hello?" Tim says. "Is anyone here?" There is no answer. Tim and Mia walk inside. They see a big room. It has a fireplace and a sofa. They see a picture on the wall. It is a man and a woman. They look sad. "Who are they?" Mia asks. "I don't know," Tim says. "Maybe they lived here a long time ago." They hear another creak. They turn around. They see a ghost. It is gray and thin. It has no eyes. It has a mouth. It opens its mouth and says: "Boo!" Tim and Mia scream. They run to the door. They try to open it. It is locked. They are trapped. "Help!" Tim and Mia shout. "Let us out! Let us out!" The ghost laughs. It floats to them. It says: "Welcome to my house. You are my guests. You can never leave. You will stay with me forever." Tim and Mia cry. They hug each other. They are very scared. They wish they never went in the house. They wish they listened to Mia. They wish they stayed in the park.

One day, a fly saw a big cake. The fly wanted to eat the cake. It was a fake cake, but the fly did not know. The fly went to the cake and tried to take a bite. A girl saw the fly and wanted to help. The girl tried to add a real cake to make the fly happy. She put the real cake next to the fake cake. The fly was very happy to see two cakes now. The fly tried to eat the real cake, but it was too big. The fly was sad and flew away. The girl tried to help, but the fly was gone. The girl was sad too. The fly did not eat the cake and the girl could not make the fly happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his toy gun. He liked to play shoot with it. Tim saw a big cliff near his house. He decided to climb the cliff to see what was on the other side. As he climbed, he met a friendly bird. The bird said, "Hello, Tim! What are you doing?" Tim replied, "I am climbing this cliff to play shoot with my toy gun." The bird laughed and said, "That sounds fun! But be careful, the cliff is very high." When Tim reached the top, he saw a big tree. In the tree, there was a fake fruit. Tim thought it was real and tried to shoot it with his toy gun. But the fruit did not fall. He tried again, and still, the fruit did not fall. The bird flew up to the tree and said, "Tim, that fruit is fake. It will not fall when you shoot it." Tim laughed and said, "Oh, I didn't know! Thank you, bird." They both laughed and played together at the top of the cliff. Then, Tim and the bird went back down the cliff and said goodbye. Tim had a fun day playing with his new friend.

Lizzie and her mom were walking through the park. As they walked, the sun made Lizzie's face light up. She felt so excited to be out in the fresh air. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise coming from the playground nearby. Her mom's face became worried. Lizzie walked over to the playground and saw a huge and powerful lion. She was scared and started to cry. Her mom hugged her tightly and said, "Let's stay away from him, he looks too powerful." Lizzie was sad and didn't want to leave her park. Then she saw something that made her heart race. A brave little mouse was walking up to the lion. He chattered something in a brave voice. The lion seemed surprised, but he stepped away. The lion looked away like he was embarrassed. Lizzie was excited! The little mouse had scared the lion away. She hugged her mom and said, "That was so brave!" Her mom smiled and they continued on their way feeling happy and relieved.
