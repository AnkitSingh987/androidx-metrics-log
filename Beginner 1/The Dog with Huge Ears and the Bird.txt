Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog with Huge Ears and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big dog with huge ears. His ears were so big that they seemed like they could touch the sky. The dog loved to play and run around with his friends. They all liked his big ears because they were so funny. One day, the dog and his friends were playing near a big tree. They heard a tiny sound, but they didn't know what it was. The dog used his big ears to listen very carefully. He heard a little bird stuck high up in the tree. The bird was scared and needed help. The dog's friends were surprised when he told them about the bird. They didn't think his big ears could be helpful. But the dog knew what to do. He stood tall and let the bird jump onto his big ears. Then, he gently lowered his head so the bird could hop off and be safe. The friends learned that even if something seems funny, it can still be helpful. And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong man named Tom. Tom had an important job. He had to pick up heavy steel from a big truck and put it in a special place. Every day, Tom would wait for the truck to come. One day, Tom did not receive the steel. He was sad and worried. He needed the steel to do his important job. Tom looked around and saw a little bird. The bird was holding a note in its beak. The note said, "I have the steel. Follow me." Tom followed the little bird. They went through the woods and over a hill. At last, they found the steel. Tom was happy! He thanked the little bird and took the steel back to the special place. With the help of the bird, Tom could do his important job again.

Once upon a time, there was a baby bear who lived in a cave. His name was Ted, and he loved his fur very much. One day Ted went for a walk in the woods, and he saw a funny twist in the path. He wanted to twist it around to see what it did, so he grabbed it and started to twist. But no matter how hard he tried, he couldn't make it move. Ted felt very frustrated and wanted to cry. Suddenly the air around him started to move and the twist started to turn. Ted couldn't believe it; he was so excited! He found out that if he twisted his fur it made the twist move. Ted had lots of fun playing with the twist and wasn't frustrated anymore!

Once upon a time, there was a bird who could fly very high. One day, the bird saw a hook on a tree. The hook was empty, and the bird was curious. It wanted to know what the hook was for. The next day, the bird saw a big, red balloon near the hook. The bird had an idea. It took the balloon and put it on the hook. The bird was happy to see the balloon on the hook. But something unexpected happened. When the bird tried to fly away, it could not. The balloon had a string, and the string was tied to the bird's leg. The bird was scared, but then it had another idea. The bird flew up, up, up, and the balloon went with it. The bird was now flying higher than ever before, all thanks to the hook and the balloon.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play together. One day, they find a big book in the park. The book has many pictures and words. "Look, Ben, a book!" Lily says. "Let's read a story." "OK, Lily, you start," Ben says. He sits next to her on the grass. Lily opens the book. She sees a picture of a cake. The cake has many colors and candles. She reads the words under the picture. "This story is about a cake. A tasty cake. A cake for a birthday. Do you like cake, Ben?" "Yes, I do, Lily. I like cake very much. Do you?" "Yes, I do, Ben. Cake is yummy. Let's read more." They read more. They learn that the cake is for a boy named Sam. Sam is turning four. He has many friends and toys. He is happy. But then, something bad happens. A big dog sees the cake. The dog is hungry. The dog wants the cake. The dog jumps on the table and grabs the cake. "Oh no, Ben, the dog took the cake!" Lily says. "What will Sam do?" "I don't know, Lily. Maybe he will cry. Maybe he will be angry. Maybe he will chase the dog. Let's read more and find out." They read more. They see that Sam is sad. He has no cake. He has no candles. He has no wish. His friends are sad too. They have no cake. They have no fun. But then, something good happens. Sam's mom sees the dog. She runs after the dog. She catches the dog. She takes the cake back. "Look, Lily, Sam's mom got the cake!" Ben says. "She is brave. She is nice." "Yes, she is, Ben. She is very brave. She is very nice. Let's read more." They read more. They see that Sam's mom fixes the cake. She puts more icing and candles. She gives the cake to Sam. She says sorry for the dog. "Happy birthday, Sam. I love you. Make a wish and blow the candles," she says. Sam smiles. He makes a wish. He blows the candles. His friends clap. They sing. They eat the cake. They have fun. "Wow, Ben, what a good story!" Lily says. "I'm glad Sam got his cake. I'm glad he had a happy birthday. What do you think he wished for?" "I don't know, Lily. Maybe he wished for more cake. Maybe he wished for more toys. Maybe he wished for more friends. What would you wish for?" "I would wish for you, Ben. You are my best friend. You are fun. You are kind." "Thank you, Lily. You are my best friend too. You are smart. You are sweet. I would wish for you too." They hug. They close the book. They put the book back in the park. They hope someone else will find it and read the story. They go home. They are happy. The end.
