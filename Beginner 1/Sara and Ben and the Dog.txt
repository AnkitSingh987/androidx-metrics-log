Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Dog

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big dog with long hair and a spike on its collar. The dog is barking loud, but it does not hear anything. "Look, a spike dog!" Sara says. "Can we pet it?" Ben shakes his head. "No, Sara. The dog is deaf. It can't hear us. It might be scared or angry. We need to stay away." But Sara likes the dog. She thinks it is soft and cute. She walks closer to the dog and reaches out her hand. "Hi, spike dog!" she says. "Do you want to be my friend?" The dog sees Sara and growls. It does not like strangers. It snaps at Sara's hand and bites her finger. "Ow!" Sara cries. "The dog bit me! It hurts!" Ben runs to Sara and hugs her. He sees blood on her finger. He is scared and angry. "Bad dog!" he shouts. "Go away!" He picks up a stick and throws it at the dog. The dog dodges the stick and runs away. Sara and Ben run to their mom. She is sitting on a bench and reading a book. She sees Sara's finger and gasps. "Oh, no! What happened?" she asks. "The spike dog bit me!" Sara says. "It was deaf and mean!" Mom takes Sara's finger and wraps it with a tissue. She kisses it and hugs Sara. "I'm sorry, Sara. You need to be careful with dogs you don't know. Some of them are deaf and can't hear you. They might be scared or angry. You need to ask their owners before you pet them." Sara nods. She feels better. She learns a lesson. "OK, Mom. I'm sorry. I won't pet spike dogs again. Can we go home now?" Mom smiles. She takes Sara and Ben by the hand and walks to the car. "Yes, we can go home now. Let's go get some ice cream on the way. You did a good job, Ben. You helped your sister. You are a good brother." Ben smiles. He feels proud. He likes ice cream. The end.

Tom and Lily like to paint. They have many colors and brushes. They make pictures of animals and flowers and cars. They are happy when they paint. But one day, they have a problem. They only have one big paper left. They both want to paint on it. Tom says, "It is my paper. I saw it first." Lily says, "No, it is my paper. I need it more." They start to fight. They pull the paper and tear it. They yell and cry. Mom hears the noise. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees the paper and the paint. She is not happy. She says, "Tom and Lily, you are not nice. You have to share. You have to be quiet. It is nap time now. No more paint. Go to your beds." Tom and Lily are sad. They do not want to nap. They want to paint. But they have no paper. They have no paint. They have to go to their beds. They are quiet. They are sorry. They wish they did not fight. They wish they could paint.

Once upon a time, there was a little field of wheat. The wheat was very organized. It grew in neat rows. Every day, the sun would shine on the wheat and help it grow. One day, a small bug came to visit the wheat. The bug liked to blink its eyes. It would blink, blink, blink all day long. The wheat liked the bug's company, and they became friends. The bug and the wheat spent their days together. The bug would blink and the wheat would sway in the wind. They were very happy. And so, the little field of wheat and the blinking bug lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a youth who loved to explore the unknown. One day, Tim went for a walk in the woods near his house. As Tim walked, he found a big box. He was very excited to see what was inside. Tim tried to open the box, but it was locked. He looked around and found a small key on the ground. Tim picked up the key and used it to open the box. Inside, he found a toy car and a teddy bear. Tim was so happy to find these treasures in the unknown woods. He took them home and played with them all day long. And that's how Tim found new friends in the woods.
