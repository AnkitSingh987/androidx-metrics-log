Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to make things. One day, she wanted to make a big turn with her toy car. Sue was very careful when she played with her toys. Sue got her toy car and started to make the big turn. She pushed the car very slow and careful. The car went around and around in a big circle. Sue was happy to see her car make the big turn. After the big turn, Sue wanted to make a race. She got her other toy cars and lined them up. Sue was careful not to break them. The cars raced and made turns. Sue had fun playing with her toy cars all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play catch with his friends. One day, they found a shiny, original ball. It was a special ball because it had a flame on it. They thought it was the coolest ball ever. Tim and his friends played catch with the flame ball all day. They laughed and had lots of fun. But then, the ball went too high and got stuck in a tree. Tim and his friends were sad. They didn't know how to get their special ball back. Tim's dad saw them looking sad and asked, "What's wrong, kids?" Tim said, "Our flame ball is stuck in the tree." Tim's dad smiled and said, "Don't worry, I'll help you." He climbed the tree and got the ball back for them. Tim and his friends were so happy. They thanked Tim's dad and continued to play catch with their original flame ball.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to mail letters to her friends. One day, she saw a wild bird outside her house. Sue said, "Hi, wild bird! Do you want to be my friend?" The wild bird nodded and became Sue's friend. They played and had lots of fun together. Sue wanted to mail a letter to her friend, Tim. She asked the wild bird, "Can you help me mail this letter?" The wild bird said, "Yes!" So, they went to the mailbox together and mailed the letter. After that, Sue and the wild bird played more and became best friends. They had a happy day in the village.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big tree and under it, a shiny gold box. Tim said, "Look, Spot! Let's see what's inside!" They opened the box and found a big, delicious cake. They were so happy! Just then, a mean cat named Fluffy came by. She wanted the cake too. Fluffy said, "Give me the cake or I will fight you!" Tim and Spot did not want to fight, but they also did not want to give away their cake. Suddenly, a nice bird flew down from the tree. The bird said, "Don't fight! I have an idea!" The bird took the cake and broke it into three parts. The bird gave one part to Tim, one part to Spot, and one part to Fluffy. They all ate their parts of the cake and felt happy. No one had to fight, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zoom. Zoom was very fast and loved to race. One day, Zoom felt anxious because he had a big race coming up. He was worried that he might not win. Zoom went to his friend, a big truck named Tom. Tom saw Zoom frown and asked, "Why are you sad, Zoom?" Zoom said, "I am anxious about the race. What if I don't win?" Tom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Zoom. Just do your best and have fun!" On the day of the race, Zoom lined up with the other cars. He was still feeling anxious, but he remembered what Tom said. As the race started, Zoom went as fast as he could. He zoomed past the other cars, one by one. The wind blew in his face, and he felt happy. Zoom crossed the finish line and won the race! He was so proud of himself. Tom was there to cheer for him, and they both smiled. From that day on, Zoom knew that if he tried his best, he could do anything. And he never felt anxious again.
