Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Mom

One day, Tom went to the gym with his mom. He saw people stretch and play. Tom wanted to try too. He looked at his mom and asked, "Can I stretch and play too, mom?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tom! Let's stretch together." They stretched their arms and legs. Tom felt happy and strong. After stretching, they went home. Mom made a spicy snack for Tom. Tom took a bite and said, "Wow, mom! This is spicy and yummy!" They laughed and enjoyed their day at the gym and their spicy snack.

One day, a little cricket named Jim was in the grass. He liked to make songs with his wings. Jim was a lucky cricket because he had many friends. Jim had a big friend named Tom. Tom was a cat. Tom liked to listen to Jim's songs. They were good friends and played all day. One day, Jim and Tom were playing near the road. Jim was making a song and did not see the big car coming. Tom tried to save Jim, but it was too late. Jim was not lucky that day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He was very excited to play. He saw a big statue in the middle of the park. The statue was of a man on a horse. Tim had a ball with him. He wanted to roll the ball on the ground. He rolled the ball near the statue. The ball rolled fast and hit the statue's foot. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He rolled the ball again and again. Each time, the ball hit the statue. Tim was very happy. He played at the park until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was restless because he had a big, red envelope. Tim wanted to share the envelope with his friend, Sue. Tim went to Sue's house and said, "Sue, I have a big, red envelope to share with you!" Sue was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! Let's open it together!" They opened the envelope and found a big, green frog inside. The frog jumped out and said, "Hello, Tim and Sue! I am a magic frog. Thank you for sharing the envelope. Now, I will give you each one wish!" Tim and Sue were very surprised but happy. They made their wishes and played with the magic frog all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot's leg was in pain. His mom said, "I believe a massage will help you feel better." Spot was impatient and wanted to play, but he knew he needed to rest. Spot's mom started to give him a massage. She rubbed his leg gently, and he felt a little better. But then, something unexpected happened. Spot felt a tickle in his leg and started to laugh. The tickle made Spot forget about the pain for a moment. The next day, Spot's leg felt much better. He was ready to run and play again. Spot was happy that his mom knew what to do. He learned that sometimes being patient and listening to mom can lead to good things. And from that day on, Spot always remembered to be patient and believe in his mom's help.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk near a cliff. She saw a bird who looked upset. Lily asked the bird, "Why are you upset?" The bird said, "I lost my eggs. I can't find them anywhere." Lily wanted to help the bird. She said, "Let's count your eggs and look for them together." They started to count the eggs. One, two, three, four. They found four eggs. But the bird had five eggs. They looked and looked for the last egg. As they looked, it started to get dark. Lily was close to the cliff. She could not see well. She took a step and fell off the cliff. The bird was very sad. It found the last egg, but it could not bring Lily back. The bird flew away with its eggs, missing its new friend.
