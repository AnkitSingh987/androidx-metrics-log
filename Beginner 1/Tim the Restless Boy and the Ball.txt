Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Restless Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a restless little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play all day. One day, while playing, Tim saw a big red ball. He wanted to kick the ball and have fun. As Tim ran to the ball, he did not see a rock on the ground. He stumble and fall down. Tim felt sad and started to cry. But then, he saw his mommy coming to help him. Mommy said, "It's okay, Tim. This is not an emergency. You will be fine." She gave him a hug and helped him stand up. Tim felt better and was ready to play again. They kicked the big red ball together and had a happy day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big tree in her yard. The tree had many red berries on it. Lily was ready to pick the berries. One day, Lily went to the tree with a basket. She saw a squirrel on the tree too. The squirrel was trying to get a berry. Lily thought it was funny. She watched the squirrel strike the branch to make the berries fall. Lily started to pick the berries. The squirrel helped her, striking the branches and making more berries fall. They both had fun and filled the basket with red berries. Lily and the squirrel were very happy. They became friends and shared the yummy berries.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty red glove. Lily loved her glove so much. Her friend, Max, saw the glove and felt envious. Max wanted a glove just like Lily's. One day, Max tried to take Lily's glove. He pulled on it very hard. Suddenly, the glove began to snap. Lily heard the sound and saw Max with her glove. She started to cry. In the end, Lily's glove was broken and she was very sad. Max felt bad for what he had done. They both learned that being envious and taking things from others can lead to a bad ending.

Ben and Mia were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were knights and princesses. Ben had a stick that he used as a sword. Mia had a yellow scarf that she used as a crown. "Look, Ben, I found a flower!" Mia said. She picked a daisy and put it in her hair. "That's pretty, Mia," Ben said. "But watch out, there is a dragon!" Ben pointed to a big bush that looked like a green monster. He ran towards it with his sword. "Roar!" Ben shouted. "I will save you, Mia!" Mia giggled and followed him. She picked up a pine cone and threw it at the bush. "Take that, dragon!" Mia said. "You can't hurt us!" The bush shook and made a noise. Ben and Mia stopped and looked. It was not a dragon. It was their dog, Rex. He had been hiding in the bush. He wagged his tail and licked their faces. "Rex, you silly dog!" Ben said. "You scared us!" Mia laughed and hugged Rex. She gave him the daisy. "You can be our friend, Rex," Mia said. "You can be a knight, too. Here, you can have my crown." Rex barked and wagged his tail. He liked the yellow crown. Ben, Mia and Rex played together in the garden. They had fun. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a blue shirt that he loved very much. One day, Tim went to play outside with his friends. While playing, Tim saw a tiny bug on the ground. The bug had big eyes that would blink, blink, blink. Tim said to the bug, "Hi, little bug! I like your blinky eyes!" The bug looked at Tim and blinked its eyes even more. Tim picked up the bug and put it on his blue shirt. They played together all day long. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Bye, little bug! See you tomorrow!" The bug blinked its eyes one last time and flew away. Tim smiled and went home, excited to play with his new friend again.
