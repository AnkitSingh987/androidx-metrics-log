Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Bird of Tim and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars on a big board. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with his cars on the board. The sky was clear, and Tim was very happy. As Tim played with his cars, he heard a big noise. He looked up and saw a big bird flying in the sky. The bird was holding something in its beak. To Tim's surprise, the bird dropped the thing it was holding. It fell right on Tim's board! Tim looked at the thing on his board. It was a shiny, new toy car! He couldn't believe it. Tim picked up the car and started to play with it. He was very happy with his new toy. And that's how Tim got a surprise toy car from a big bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the mud. One day, he got very dirty. His clothes, hands, and face were all covered in mud. Tim saw a club where kids were playing and having fun. He wanted to join them, but he was too dirty. One of the kids said, "You can't play with us if you're dirty. Go wash up first!" So, Tim went home and washed his hands, face, and changed his clothes. He felt clean and happy. He went back to the club and asked if he could play now. The kids looked at him and said, "Yes, you can play with us now!" They all played together and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, they all clapped their hands and said, "We had a great time! Let's play again tomorrow!" Tim was very happy that he could play with his new friends, and he promised to stay clean when playing at the club.

---

One day, a little girl named Lilly put on her pink dress. She wanted to visit her friend, Timmy. Lilly walked to Timmy's house and saw him on the porch. She was so happy! "Hi Timmy! Let's play!" said Lilly. Timmy smiled and said, "Okay, let's go to the park!" They held hands and walked to the park. The sun was very bright, but they did not mind. At the park, Lilly and Timmy played on the swings. They went up and down, laughing and having fun. But then, dark clouds came. Lilly looked at the sky and felt scared. "Timmy, we should go home," she said. But Timmy did not want to leave. The rain started to fall, and it was very heavy. Lilly and Timmy got wet and cold. They ran back to Timmy's porch, but it was too late. They both got sick from the rain. Next time, they would listen to each other when they felt scared.

Lily and Tom are playing with their toys in the living room. Lily has a doll and Tom has a ball. Tom likes to throw the ball and catch it. Lily likes to hug the doll and dress it. "Look, Lily, I can throw the ball very high!" Tom says. He throws the ball up in the air and waits for it to come down. "Be careful, Tom!" Lily says. "You might hit something or hurt yourself!" Tom laughs. "I am careful, Lily. I have a good brain. I know how to throw and catch. Do you want to try?" Lily shakes her head. "No, thank you, Tom. I like my doll. She is soft and pretty. She has a good brain too. She knows how to talk and sing." Tom rolls his eyes. "Dolls can't talk or sing, Lily. They are not real. They don't have brains. They have stuffing." Lily frowns. "That's not nice, Tom. My doll is real to me. She has a brain. She has feelings. She is my friend." Tom shrugs. "Whatever, Lily. You can play with your doll. I will play with my ball. It is more fun." He throws the ball again, but this time he throws it too hard. The ball flies across the room and hits a vase on the shelf. The vase falls and breaks with a loud crash. Lily and Tom gasp. They look at the broken vase and then at each other. They are scared. They know their mom will be angry. "Uh-oh," Tom says. "We are in trouble." Lily nods. "Yes, we are. Maybe you should have been more careful, Tom. Maybe you should have listened to me." Tom feels sorry. He wishes he had not thrown the ball so hard. He wishes he had not been mean to Lily and her doll. He hugs Lily and says, "I'm sorry, Lily. You are right. Your doll is real to you. She has a brain. She has feelings. She is your friend. And you are my friend too. Can you forgive me?" Lily smiles. She hugs Tom back and says, "Of course, I can forgive you, Tom. You are my brother. I love you. But we still have to tell mom what we did." Tom nods. He takes Lily's hand and says, "Let's go, Lily. Let's be brave. Let's tell mom the truth." They walk to the kitchen, where their mom is making cookies. They hope she will not be too mad. They hope she will still love them. They hope she will still give them cookies.
