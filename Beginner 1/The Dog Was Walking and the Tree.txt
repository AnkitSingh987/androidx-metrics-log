Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog Was Walking and the Tree

One day, a little dog was walking in the park. He could hear a big storm coming. The little dog was scared and needed to find a shelter to stay dry and safe. The little dog looked around and saw a big tree. He thought, "I am lucky to find this tree. It will be a good shelter for me." But when he went near the tree, a big cat jumped out. The cat did not want to share the shelter with the little dog. The little dog was sad, but he did not give up. He looked around and found a small house. He went inside and found a warm, cozy bed. The little dog was very lucky to find this shelter. The storm came, but the little dog was safe and happy in his new home.

Once upon a time, there was a person named Tom. Tom liked to create things with his hands. He made toys, houses, and even cars with paper and glue. One day, Tom felt worried. He had to create something special for his friend's birthday, but he didn't know what to make. Tom asked his friend, "What do you want for your birthday?" His friend said, "I want a big surprise!" Tom thought and thought, but he was still worried. He didn't know what surprise to create for his friend. Finally, Tom had an idea. He decided to create a big, colorful cake with his friend's name on it. Tom worked hard and made the cake. At the party, his friend was so happy to see the cake. Tom wasn't worried anymore. He knew he had made the perfect surprise for his friend's birthday.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big dream. She wanted to swim in a big pool. Every night, Mia would dream of the pool. She would tell her mom and dad about her dream. They would smile and say, "One day, Mia. One day." One day, Mia's mom and dad took her to a surprise place. Mia was so happy when she saw it. It was a big pool! Mia was finally going to swim. She put on her soft swim suit and jumped in the water. Mia's dream came true. She was so happy. Mia swam in the big pool all day. Her mom and dad watched her and smiled. They were happy that Mia's dream came true. When it was time to go home, Mia was tired but happy. She knew that dreams can come true if you believe in them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to help her mom with work. One day, Sue's mom asked her to pour water into a big cup. Sue was very happy to help. "Can I pour the water, Mom?" asked Sue. "Yes, you can," said her mom with a clear and happy voice. Sue picked up the small jug and started to pour the water into the big cup. The water made a nice sound as it went into the cup. Sue was proud of her work. She showed her mom the big cup full of water. "Look, Mom! I did it!" Sue said with a big smile. "Good job, Sue!" said her mom, giving her a hug. Sue felt very happy that she could help her mom and do a good job.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there lived a little bug. The bug lived on green grass. The bug liked to polish his tiny house. He was a very clean bug. One day, the bug found a tasty leaf. He shared it with his bug friends. They all loved the tasty leaf. It made them very happy. The next day, the bug and his friends played on the grass. They ran and jumped. They had lots of fun. After playing, they went back to their tiny houses and polished them. They were very clean bugs.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wore his big red boot. On his way, he saw a tasty cake on a table. "Yum! I want to eat that cake," Tim said. He took a big bite and smiled. Just then, a sad girl named Sue came by. She saw Tim eating the cake. "That is my cake," Sue said with a sigh. Tim felt bad. He didn't know it was her cake. Tim wanted to make Sue happy. He had an idea. He took off his big red boot and put the rest of the cake inside. "Here, Sue, take my boot with the cake. Now you have a cake and a fun boot!" Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They both laughed and became good friends.
