Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Doll

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia liked to create new things. She made pretty things with paper, glue, and colors. Mia had a lot of fun when she made new things. One day, Mia wanted to make a new dress for her doll. She took a piece of cloth and wrapped it around the doll's waist. But Mia's little brother, Max, wanted to play with the doll too. Max took the doll from Mia and ran away. Mia was sad and cried. Max saw that Mia was sad, so he gave the doll back to her. Mia said, "Thank you, Max." Together, they created a new dress for the doll. Mia and Max played with the doll and had a lot of fun. They were happy again.

Lily was a chubby girl who liked to wear skirts. She had many skirts of different colors and patterns. She liked to twirl and dance in her skirts. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. She wore her favorite skirt, a pink one with flowers. At the park, Lily saw a big slide. She wanted to go on the slide, but there was a long line of kids waiting. She waited and waited, but the line did not move fast. She started to feel bored and tired. She yawned and rubbed her eyes. "Mom, can we go home?" she asked. "Not yet, sweetie. You wanted to go on the slide, remember?" her mom said. "But I'm sleepy. And my skirt is dirty. And the slide is too high." Lily whined. Her mom looked at her skirt and saw that it had some grass stains and mud on it. She sighed and smiled. "Okay, okay. Let's go home. You can take a nap and change your skirt. Maybe we can come back another day when the slide is less busy." she said. Lily nodded and took her mom's hand. Her dad followed them. They walked to the car and drove home. Lily fell asleep on the way, dreaming of her skirts.

Lily and Ben are brother and sister. They like to play with toys in their room. But sometimes they fight over the same toy. Lily wants to play with the red car. Ben wants to play with the red car too. Lily says, "It is my turn with the red car. You had it before." Ben says, "No, it is my turn. You had it too long." They pull the red car from each other. The red car breaks in two. Lily and Ben are sad and angry. Mom hears the noise and comes to their room. She sees the broken red car and the crying children. She says, "What happened here? Why did you break the red car?" Lily and Ben both say, "He/she did it. It is his/her fault." They repeat the same words over and over. Mom says, "Stop. You both did it. You both fought over the red car. You both broke it. You both need to say sorry." Lily and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry for the red car and for each other. They say, "Sorry, brother/sister. I did not mean to break the red car. Can we share it?" They hug each other and mom. Mom says, "That is good. I am glad you said sorry. But you still need to learn to share and take turns. Sharing is not hard. It is fun and kind. Next time, ask before you take a toy. And give it back when your turn is over. Can you do that?" Lily and Ben nod and say, "Yes, mom. We can do that." Mom smiles and says, "Good. Now, let's see if we can fix the red car." They go to the kitchen and look for glue.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small hidden house. In that house, a nice girl named Lily lived with her pet cat, Tippy. They liked to play with a big circle toy. One day, they went for a walk in the forest. Lily said, "I hope we find a new friend to play with us." Tippy agreed. They walked and walked, but they did not find anyone. Just when they were about to go home, they saw a big circle on the ground. It was a big hole! They looked inside and saw a small bird. The bird said, "Help! I am stuck!" Lily and Tippy helped the bird out of the hole. The bird said, "Thank you! I am your new friend!" Lily, Tippy, and their new friend played together all day.
