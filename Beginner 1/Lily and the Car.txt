Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Car

Lily likes to play with her dolls. She has many dolls with different hair. Some have long hair, some have short hair, some have curly hair, some have straight hair. Lily likes to comb their hair and make them tidy. One day, Lily's mom tells her, "Lily, it is time to go to the park. Put your dolls away and get ready." Lily nods and puts her dolls in a box. She picks one doll to take with her. She chooses the doll with long, straight hair. She likes this doll the best. At the park, Lily sees her friend Ben. Ben has a toy car. He likes to zoom it on the grass and make noises. "Vroom, vroom!" he says. Lily says hello to Ben and shows him her doll. "This is my doll. She has long, straight hair. I combed her hair and made her tidy. Do you like her?" she asks. Ben looks at the doll and shakes his head. "No, I don't like her. She is boring. Her hair is too long and too straight. She needs a haircut. Can I cut her hair?" he asks. Lily gasps and hugs her doll. "No, you can't cut her hair! She is my doll and I like her hair. Her hair is not boring, it is pretty. You are mean, Ben. Go away!" she tells him. Ben shrugs and zooms his car away. "Fine, I don't care. Your doll is silly. My car is cool. Vroom, vroom!" he says. Lily feels sad and angry. She doesn't want to play with Ben anymore. She sits on a bench and cuddles her doll. She tells her doll, "Don't worry, I love you. You have the best hair. You are not silly, you are smart. You are my friend." She smiles and kisses her doll. She feels better. She looks around and sees a girl with curly hair. She waves and says, "Hi, do you want to play with me and my doll?" The girl nods and smiles. She comes over and says, "Yes, I do. Your doll is very pretty. She has long, straight hair. I have curly hair. Can I comb your doll's hair and make her tidy?" Lily says yes and shares her comb. They play together and have fun. They are both happy.

Tom and Anna were playing hide and seek in their house. Tom was the seeker and Anna was the hider. Tom counted to ten and then looked for Anna. He looked in the kitchen, the living room, the bathroom, but he could not find her. "Where are you, Anna?" he called out. He heard a giggle from upstairs. He ran up the stairs and saw a door that was slightly open. He peeked inside and saw Anna hiding under the bed. He smiled and said, "I found you!" Anna crawled out from under the bed and said, "You are good at seeking, Tom. How did you find me?" Tom said, "I heard you giggle. You should be quiet when you hide." Anna said, "Sorry, Tom. I could not help it. It was fun to hide." Tom said, "It's okay, Anna. It was fun to seek. Do you want to play again?" Anna said, "Yes, please. But this time, I will be the seeker and you will be the hider." Tom said, "Okay, Anna. You count to ten and I will hide." Anna said, "Okay, Tom. One, two, three..." Tom ran downstairs and looked for a good hiding spot. He saw a closet that was big and dark. He opened the door and went inside. He closed the door behind him and waited for Anna to find him. He heard Anna's footsteps on the stairs. He heard her say, "Where are you, Tom?" He tried to be quiet, but he was a little scared of the dark. He wished he had a flashlight or a toy to play with. He heard Anna open and close doors. He wondered if she would find him. He heard Anna open the closet door. He saw her face and smiled. He said, "You found me, Anna!" Anna looked at him and said, "Tom, why are you in the closet? It is dark and scary in here." Tom said, "I thought it was a good hiding spot. But I was a little scared, too." Anna said, "You are brave, Tom. But I don't like the closet. It is not fun to hide in here." Tom said, "You are right, Anna. The closet is not fun. Let's find a better hiding spot." Anna said, "Okay, Tom. Let's go." They left the closet and went to find a new hiding spot. They were happy to play hide and seek together. They were not scared or confused anymore. They were having fun.
