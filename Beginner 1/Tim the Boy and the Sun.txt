Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sun

One day, a boy named Tim went to play golf with his dad. They went to a big, green field with many holes. Tim was very excited to play. He had a small golf club, perfect for his size. His dad had a big golf club. They both wore hats to keep the sun away. Before they started, Dad said, "Tim, we have to be patient when we play golf. It takes time to learn." Tim nodded and smiled. They took turns hitting the small white ball. Sometimes, the ball went far, and sometimes it didn't go far at all. But Tim and his dad kept trying. As they played, a man came up to them. He asked, "Can I pass and play the next hole?" Tim's dad said, "Of course! Have fun!" Tim and his dad continued to play. They were patient and had a great day playing golf together.

Tom is a bald man who likes to watch birds. He has a big window in his living room where he can see many birds. He has a book with pictures of birds and he tries to find the birds he sees in the book. One day, he sees a new bird he does not know. It is red and black and has a long tail. He looks in his book but he cannot find it. He is very curious and excited. He wants to show the bird to someone. He calls his friend Lily, who is a little girl who lives next door. He says, "Lily, come and see! I have a new bird in my window. It is very pretty and different. Do you want to watch it with me?" Lily likes birds too. She runs to Tom's house and knocks on the door. Tom opens the door and lets her in. He takes her to the window and points to the bird. "Look, Lily, do you see it? It is red and black and has a long tail. Do you know what it is?" Lily looks at the bird and smiles. She knows what it is. She has seen it in her own book. She says, "Yes, Tom, I know what it is. It is a parrot. It is a bird that can talk. It is not from here. It is from a far away place where it is warm and sunny. Someone must have brought it here as a pet and it flew away. Maybe it is looking for a friend." Tom is amazed. He says, "Wow, a parrot! A bird that can talk! That is so cool. I wish I could talk to it. Do you think it would talk to me?" Lily says, "Maybe. You can try. But you have to be nice and gentle. Parrots are smart and shy. They like seeds and fruits. Do you have any?" Tom says, "Yes, I have some. Come, let's get some and see if we can make friends with the parrot." They go to the kitchen and get some seeds and fruits. They come back to the window and hold them out to the parrot. The parrot looks at them and tilts its head. It hops closer and takes a seed from Tom's hand. It says, "Hello. Hello. Who are you?" Tom and Lily are very happy. They say, "Hello, parrot. We are Tom and Lily. We are your friends. We like you. What is your name?" The parrot says, "My name is Coco. Coco. I like you too. You are nice. Can I stay with you?" Tom and Lily look at each other and nod. They say, "Yes, Coco. You can stay with us. We have a big window and a book and many birds. You will like it here. You are our new friend." Coco says, "Thank you. Thank you. You are my new friends too. I am happy. I am happy." They all smile and watch the birds together. They are happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big hat that he loved to wear. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. He was very happy and excited. While playing, Tim met a new friend named Jack. Jack was not nice and acted ignorant. Jack wanted Tim's hat and tried to take it from him. Tim yelled, "No, Jack! This is my hat!" But Jack did not listen and still wanted the hat. Tim and Jack fought over the hat. Tim yelled and yelled, but Jack did not care. In the end, Jack took the hat and ran away. Tim was very sad and cried. The moral of the story is to be careful with whom you play and not to trust ignorant people.
