Category: Beginner
Title: Tim Finds the Park

One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. The sky was big and open. Tim's mom said, "We have fun today, but look at the sky. If it gets dark, we go home." Tim said, "Yes, Mom." They played and had fun. Tim saw his friend, Sam. They played with a ball. The sky got dark. Tim's mom said, "We go home now." Tim was sad, but he knew they had to leave. Sam said, "Bye, Tim!" As they walked home, the wind blew hard. Tim's mom said, "Oh no, a tornado is coming!" They ran to a safe place. They hid until the tornado went away. Tim was scared, but his mom hugged him tight. After the tornado, they went home. The house was still there, but the door was open. Inside, they found a new puppy! The tornado had blown the puppy into their house. Tim was happy and said, "We have a new friend!"

One day, a big dog named Max was in the park. He loved to run and play in the mud. He got very filthy. Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her toy ball was stuck in a tree. Max wanted to help Lily. He said, "I will get your ball." Max jumped high to reach the ball, but he could not get it. He tried to increase his jump, but still could not reach it. He felt bad for Lily. Then, a bird in the tree saw Max and Lily. The bird wanted to help too. It flew up and pushed the ball out of the tree. The ball fell, and Lily caught it. She was so happy! Lily gave Max a big hug and said, "Thank you, Max! Thank you, bird!" Max felt happy and proud, even though he was still filthy.

One day, a girl named Mia was playing in her room. She wore a pretty skirt that her mom had sent her. Mia loved her skirt because it was pink and had flowers on it. She was very happy. Mia's friend, Tom, came over to play. Tom saw Mia's skirt and said, "That skirt is for girls! Boys can't wear skirts!" Mia felt sad. She wanted to show Tom that skirts are harmless and fun to wear. So, she had an idea. Mia took Tom to her mom's room and found a big skirt. She put it on Tom and said, "See? Skirts are fun for everyone!" Tom laughed and started to dance. They both had fun and learned that skirts are for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a tiny flea friend who lived in his fur. The flea was very small, but he was also very brave. Spot and the flea liked to play together all day long. One day, Spot and the flea went to the park to play. They saw a big rope on the ground. Spot had an idea. He wanted to twist the rope with his paws. Spot and the flea started to twist the rope together. They twisted and twisted until they made a big knot. Suddenly, a big wind came and made a loud noise. The flea got frightened and jumped off Spot. Spot was worried and looked for his friend everywhere. Finally, he found the flea hiding under a leaf. Spot was so happy to find his friend. They went back home, and from that day on, they were never apart again.

One day, a goose went for a walk. The sun was mild and the sky was blue. The goose was happy. She saw a big tree and decided to rest under it. Under the tree, a little boy was playing with a toy. The goose wanted to play, too. The boy said, "No, you can't play. You might break my toy." The goose felt sad and walked away. The goose saw a big stick on the ground. She had an idea. She picked up the stick with her beak and walked back to the boy. The goose said, "Look, I have a toy, too. We can play together." The boy smiled and they played all day. They both had fun and became good friends.
