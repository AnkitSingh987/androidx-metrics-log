Category: Beginner
Title: The Stubborn Cloud and the Rain

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn cloud. The cloud did not like to rain. It just wanted to stay up in the sky and play all day. All the other clouds would rain, but not this stubborn cloud. One day, the stubborn cloud met a laser. The laser was very bright and shiny. The laser said, "Hey cloud, let's play a game together! If you rain, I will make a pretty light show for everyone to see." The stubborn cloud thought about it and agreed to play the game. The stubborn cloud started to rain, and the laser made a beautiful light show. The people on the ground were so happy to see the rain and the pretty lights. The stubborn cloud and the laser became best friends and played together every day. From that day on, the stubborn cloud loved to rain and make everyone happy.

Sam and Ben are brothers. They like to play baseball in the park. They have a red ball and a wood bat. They take turns to hit and catch. One day, they see a big car in the park. It is shiny and black. It has four doors and a long nose. A man and a woman get out of the car. They wear fancy clothes. The woman has a long dress and a hat. The man has a suit and a tie. They look like they are going to a party. Sam and Ben are curious. They want to see the car. They run to the car and look inside. They see leather seats and a big wheel. They see buttons and lights. They see a small TV and a phone. "Wow, this car is so cool!" Sam says. "Shh, don't be loud. They might hear us," Ben whispers. They hear the man and the woman talking. They sound happy and rich. They laugh and smile. They say they are going to a wedding. They say they are friends with the bride and the groom. "Who are they?" Sam whispers. "I don't know. Maybe they are famous," Ben whispers. They hear the man and the woman say goodbye. They walk to the car. They see Sam and Ben. "Hello, boys. What are you doing here?" the man asks. Sam and Ben are scared. They drop the ball and the bat. They run away. "Sorry, mister. We were just looking," Sam says. "Bye, lady. You have a nice dress," Ben says. The man and the woman smile. They get in the car. They drive away. Sam and Ben go back to their game. They forget about the car. They have fun with their ball and their bat. They are happy.

Once, there was a big, black dog named Tom. Tom liked to play with his friends in the park. They liked to run, jump, and bark. Tom was a happy dog. One day, Tom saw a big bag of coal. He wanted to play with it, but he knew it was not his. Tom did something bad. He stole the coal. He felt uncomfortable because he knew it was wrong to steal. Tom's friends saw him with the coal and they said, "Tom, that is not yours. You should not steal." Tom knew they were right. He took the coal back and said sorry. Tom learned his lesson and never stole again.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they go to the library with their moms. The library has many books and toys. Lily and Ben want to see them all. They find a big display of cars and trucks. They are shiny and colorful. Lily and Ben love cars and trucks. They take some and play on the floor. They make noises and pretend to drive. "Look, Lily, this truck is big and strong. It can carry many things," Ben says. "Wow, Ben, that truck is cool. And look, this car is fast and pretty. It can go zoom, zoom, zoom," Lily says. They have fun with the cars and trucks. They share and take turns. They are good friends. Then, they see a display of dolls and animals. They are soft and cute. Lily and Ben love dolls and animals. They take some and sit on a seat. They hug and talk to them. "Hello, doll, you are so nice and pretty. Do you want to be my friend?" Lily says. "Hello, animal, you are so fuzzy and funny. Do you want to play with me?" Ben says. They have fun with the dolls and animals. They are gentle and kind. They are good friends. The moms watch Lily and Ben. They are happy and proud. They see that Lily and Ben are helpful and polite. They help each other and say please and thank you. They put the toys and books back on the display. They are good friends.
