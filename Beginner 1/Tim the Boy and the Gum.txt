Title: Tim the Boy and the Gum

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to chew gum all day. One day, Tim found a piece of gum on the ground. He thought it was his, so he picked it up. But he did not know that it was disgusting. Tim's mom saw him with the gum and said, "Tim, where did you get that gum?" Tim said, "I found it on the ground." His mom told him, "You should not chew gum from the ground. It is not clean. It can be disgusting. Let's leave it and get a new one." Tim and his mom went to the store to buy new gum. Tim was happy to have a clean piece of gum to chew. He promised his mom that he would never pick up gum from the ground again. From that day on, Tim only chewed clean gum and always remembered to leave the disgusting gum on the ground.

One day, a poor rat named Tim was walking in the park. He saw a big cat. The cat was mean and wanted to catch Tim. Tim was scared and ran away. "Ha! You can't catch me!" Tim said to the cat. But the cat was fast and chased Tim. Tim ran up a tree and the cat could not get him. The cat was mad and said, "I will get you, rat!" Then, something unexpected happened. A dog came to the park and saw the cat. The dog did not like the cat and wanted to chase it away. The dog barked loud and the cat got scared. The cat quit chasing Tim and ran away from the dog. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, dog!" The dog smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim!" Now, Tim and the dog were friends and played together in the park. The cat never bothered Tim again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a curious cat named Tom. Tom liked to enter every room in the house to see what was inside. Today, Tom saw a new room he had never seen before. Tom said to his friend, the dog named Max, "Max, let's enter the new room today." Max was scared but Tom was very curious. So, they both went to the new room and opened the door. Inside the room, they found a big box. Tom and Max were very surprised! They opened the box and found a lot of fun toys. They played with the toys all day and had a great time. The new room became their favorite place to play.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to climb the big oak tree and pretend they were birds. They had a nest made of sticks and leaves, and they sang songs to each other. One day, they saw a new boy arrive at the park. He had a red hat and a blue bike. He looked sad and lonely. He did not talk to anyone or play with anything. "Who is he?" Anna asked Ben. "I don't know. Maybe he is new here. Maybe he has no friends," Ben said. Anna felt sorry for the boy. She was a compassionate girl. She wanted to make him happy. "Let's go and say hello to him. Maybe he wants to play with us," she said to Ben. Ben nodded. He was a kind boy. He wanted to help Anna. They climbed down from the oak tree and walked to the boy. "Hi, I'm Anna and this is Ben. What's your name?" Anna said with a smile. The boy looked up and saw them. He smiled back. "Hi, I'm Leo. I just moved here. I don't know anyone," he said. "Would you like to play with us? We have a nest in the oak tree. It's very fun," Ben said. "Really? Can I see it?" Leo said. "Sure, come on. We'll show you," Anna said. They took Leo by the hand and led him to the oak tree. They helped him climb up and showed him their nest. They shared their songs and stories with him. Leo felt happy and welcomed. He made two new friends. They played until the sun set and their parents came to pick them up. They hugged and said goodbye. "See you tomorrow, Leo. We'll play again," Anna said. "See you tomorrow, Anna and Ben. Thank you for being so nice to me," Leo said. They waved and smiled. They were happy. They had a good day.
