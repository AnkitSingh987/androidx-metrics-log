Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Gifted Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Spot. He could reach very high places. One day, Spot's family went to the grocery store. They needed food for dinner. Spot stayed home alone. While they were gone, Spot heard a noise. It was a ball stuck in a tree. Spot used his gifted skills to reach the ball. He barked and jumped high. The ball fell down, and Spot was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. When the ball fell, it hit a button. The button opened a secret door in the yard. Spot went inside and found a big box of toys. Spot played with the toys until his family came home. They were all very surprised!

One day, a little girl named Amy went to play outside. It was icy and cold. Amy wore a big coat, but she forgot her hat. Her lips felt cold too. She saw her friend Tim playing with a ball. They played together and had fun. But then, the ball went into the woods. Amy and Tim were scared. The woods were dark and big. They did not want to go in. But they wanted the ball back. So, they held hands and walked into the woods. In the woods, they found a big bear. The bear had the ball. They thought the bear would be mean. But the bear was nice! He gave the ball back to Amy and Tim. They all played together and had fun. Then, they went home and told their moms and dads about their new friend, the bear.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house. In the house, lived a cat and a dog. They were best friends. The cat was very good at keeping the house tidy. The dog loved to protect the house. One day, they heard a strange sound. They went to look. It was a little bird who had hurt its wing. The cat and dog wanted to help the bird. They let the bird stay in their tidy house. The cat and dog took good care of the bird. They gave it food and a warm place to sleep. The dog protected the bird from danger. Soon, the bird's wing got better. The bird said thank you and flew away. The cat and dog were happy they could help. They went back to their tidy house and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy who loved to do fun things. He liked to play with his toys and visit his friends. One day, Tim went to visit his friend, Sam. They played with their toys and had a lot of fun. But then, they saw a little bug on the floor. They thought it was funny and wanted to play with it. They tried to pick up the bug, but it was not nice. The little bug bit Tim's finger and it hurt a lot. Tim cried and went home. He was very sad and did not want to visit Sam again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a youth who loved to play in the wet rain. One day, Tim put on his rain boots and went outside to jump in the puddles. Tim saw his friend, Sue, playing in the rain too. They both loved the wet rain. Tim said, "Let's jump in the big puddle!" Sue said, "No, let's jump in the small puddle!" Tim and Sue did not agree. Then, Tim had a good idea. He said, "Let's jump in both puddles!" Sue liked the idea. They jumped in the big puddle and the small puddle. They laughed and played until the rain stopped. Tim and Sue were happy they found a way to agree.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He took his big, red bucket with him. Tim wanted to play in the sand. In the park, he saw a big, old tree. The tree was very tall and had many leaves. Tim saw a long, green thing wrap around the tree. It was a snake! Tim said, "Oh no! A snake is in the tree!" A girl named Sue was playing near the tree. She heard Tim and looked up. Sue said, "Don't worry, Tim. That snake is my pet. His name is Sam. He is mild and nice. He won't hurt you." Tim was scared at first, but then he saw Sam the snake smile. Tim and Sue played with Sam in the park. They made a sand castle with Tim's red bucket. Then, Sam wrap around the sand castle like a big, green ribbon. They all had lots of fun together.
