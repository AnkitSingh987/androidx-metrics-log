Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a selfish boy. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim found a big box. He thought it was a trap. Tim was scared, but he wanted to see what was inside. Tim opened the box very slowly. To his surprise, there was a big toy inside. It was a toy he had always wanted. Tim was so happy. He wanted to play with the toy all by himself. But then, he saw his friends looking at him. They were sad because they wanted to play too. Tim thought about it and decided to share his new toy with his friends. They all played together and had a great time. Tim learned that sharing was more fun than being selfish. And the big toy made everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little fairy named Lily. She lived in a big tree with her friends. Lily was a good fairy, but she had a friend named Max who was a bad fairy. Max liked to play tricks on the other fairies and make them sad. One day, Max played a trick on Lily. He hid her magic wand, and she did not know where it was. Lily was very sad because she needed her wand to help her friends. She asked Max if he knew where her wand was, but he just laughed and said he did not know. Lily asked her other friends to help her look for her wand. They looked everywhere, and finally, they found it under Max's bed. Lily was happy, and she knew Max had played a trick on her. She told Max that being a bad fairy was not nice, and he should be a good fairy like her. Max felt sorry and promised to be a good fairy from now on.

One day, a tall man named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I want to start a new world, but I don't know how." Tom thought for a moment. He said, "Let's build a new world with blocks!" They found some blocks and started to build. But then, a big wind came. It blew their world down! Sue and Tom were sad, but they did not give up. They built their new world again, this time stronger. They were happy and proud. Sue said, "Thank you, Tom!" And they played together in their new world all day.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lucy. Lucy loved to dance and play all day. One day, Lucy saw a big ball in the yard. She wanted to play with it, but she struggled to push it. A little boy named Tim saw Lucy. He said, "Lucy, do you need help?" Lucy nodded her head. Tim helped Lucy push the ball. They laughed and played together. Later, Lucy hurt her leg while playing. Tim's mom took Lucy to the doctor. The doctor used an x-ray to look at Lucy's leg. It was not broken, just a little hurt. Lucy was happy, and she went home to rest. Soon, she was dancing and playing again with Tim.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big pit with lots of sand in it. Tim saw a sleepy cat lying in the pit. He wanted to play with the cat. "Mom, can I feed the cat?" Tim asked. "Yes, but be careful," his mom said. Tim took some food from his bag and went to the pit. But the cat didn't want to eat. It just wanted to sleep. Tim tried to wake the cat up. "Wake up, cat! Eat the food!" he said. But the cat didn't want to wake up. Tim felt sad. He didn't know what to do. Then, his mom came over and said, "Let's let the cat sleep. We can feed it later." Tim agreed and they left the food near the cat. They went to play on the swings and had a fun day at the park.
