Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Slide

Tom and Lily are playing in the park. They see a big slide that is green. They want to go on the slide, but there is a long line of other kids. "Let's hurry, Lily!" Tom says. "We have to wait our turn." They run to the end of the line and wait. They see the other kids slide down and laugh. They want to slide too. But then they hear a loud noise. It is a big truck. The truck is carrying wood. The wood is for the new playground. The truck stops near the slide. "Look, Tom!" Lily says. "The truck has wood. Maybe we can play with the wood." They leave the line and run to the truck. They see the wood. The wood is brown and long and heavy. They try to lift the wood, but it is too hard. "Hey, kids!" a man says. He is the driver of the truck. He is wearing a yellow hat. "You can't play with the wood. The wood is not a toy. The wood is for building. Go back to the slide." Tom and Lily are sad. They like the wood. They want to play with the wood. "Sorry, mister," Tom says. "We just wanted to see the wood." They go back to the slide. But the line is gone. All the other kids have slid down and left. The slide is empty. "Look, Lily!" Tom says. "Now we can slide. Let's hurry!" They run to the slide and climb up. They sit on the slide and hold hands. They slide down and laugh. They like the slide. The slide is fun.

Once upon a time there was a small bird who loved to eat. She wanted nothing more than to always be eating. But one day she got a very long thread caught in her beak. It was too long and too tricky for her to get out. The bird was feeling very sad and worried. She flew around looking for help. Finally she found a cat that was sleeping in the sun. She asked the cat to help her. The cat opened one eye and said, "Meow!" He was very sleepy. He told the bird he was too tired to help her. The bird felt sad and scared. But she knew she had to get the thread out of her beak. So she used her claws and pulled it out. And that's how the small bird got the thread out of her beak. She was so happy she could eat again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a cat named Tom. They loved to play together all day. One day, Lily and Tom were playing outside when they saw a big dog. The dog looked guilty. It had a sad face. Lily said, "Tom, why is the dog sad?" Tom did not know. They went to the dog and asked, "Dog, why are you sad?" The dog said, "I am sad because I cannot wake my friend. He is sleeping too long." Lily and Tom wanted to help the dog. They tried to wake the dog's friend by shouting, "Wake up, wake up!" But the friend did not wake. Then, Lily had an idea. She said, "Tom, let's try to kiss the friend. Maybe that will wake him up." Tom was scared, but he wanted to help. So, they both kissed the sleeping friend. Suddenly, the friend woke up! The dog was so happy. He thanked Lily and Tom for their help. From that day on, Lily, Tom, the dog, and his friend played together every day. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent bird who liked to work. He had a nest on a cliff, high up in the sky. He loved to fly around and help his friends. One day, while he was working, he saw a little mouse. The mouse was crying. The bird flew down and asked, "Why are you sad, little mouse?" The mouse said, "I lost my way home. Can you help me find it?" The intelligent bird agreed to help. They worked together to find the little mouse's home. The bird flew up high to see the way, and the mouse followed on the ground. After some time, they found the mouse's home. The little mouse was so happy and thanked the bird. The bird was glad that he could help his new friend. From that day on, the intelligent bird and the little mouse worked together to help other animals in need. And they all lived happily ever after.
