Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tray

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a tray that he loved to carry around. One day, he wanted to use his tray to support his toys. He put his toys on the tray and tried to walk with them. But the tray was hard to hold, and Tim dropped his toys. He felt sad. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." She took a big, soft cloth and put it on the tray. Now, the tray was not hard to hold. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Tim walked around with his toys on the tray again. But this time, something unexpected happened. The toys started to talk! They said, "Thank you, Tim, for giving us a nice ride on your tray!" Tim was surprised but happy. Now, he had new friends to play with. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a dead bug on the ground. She picked it up and put it in a small frame to keep it safe. Mia showed the frame to her mom and said, "Look, I found a bug!" Her mom looked at the frame and saw the dead bug. She said, "Oh, it's a little cold out today. The bug might be shivering if it were still alive." Mia thought about the bug and felt sad that it was dead. The next day, Mia found a live bug outside. She let it crawl on her hand and said, "Don't worry, bug. I will keep you warm so you don't shiver like the dead bug." Mia took care of the live bug and they became friends.

One day, a tall boy named Tim found a lamp. He liked the lamp very much. He put the lamp in his room. Tim's room was now very bright. He was happy. Tim had a toy car that could speed. He loved to play with it. The car went very fast. Tim's mom told him to be careful with his fast car. She said it might break something. One day, Tim was playing with his car. It went very fast. The car hit the lamp. The lamp fell and broke. Tim was sad. He remembered his mom's words. Next time, Tim would be more careful with his fast car.

There was a girl who had a pen. She was walking and looking around when she saw a fierce dog. She was scared and stopped. The dog barked and came closer to her. The girl was so afraid and shouted "What do you want?". The dog replied in a very fierce and loud bark. The girl could not understand, but she got an idea and showed the pen to the dog. The dog grabbed the pen and started chewing it. The girl was so angry and shouted again "No! Give it back!". The dog growled and started running away with the pen. The girl felt so sad and looked for the pen. She looked under a tree, between the rocks and behind some bushes, but the pen was nowhere to be found. The girl sighed, feeling unhappier than ever. Suddenly, the dog came back wiggling its tail, and in its mouth the pen! The dog had a big smile on its face and gave the pen back to the girl. The girl was so happy and hugged the dog. From that moment on, they were good friends and the girl never had to be afraid of that fierce dog ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a mailman named Tom. Tom wore a blue uniform. Every day, he would mail letters and packages to people. One day, Tom felt worried. He lost a very important letter. He looked and looked for it, but he could not find it. He was very sad. In the end, Tom never found the important letter. The person waiting for the letter was sad too. Tom was still worried and not happy.

Tom and Sue like to play with a ball in the park. They throw the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they miss and the ball rolls away. They run after it and laugh. One day, a big person comes to the park. He has a mean face and a loud voice. He sees Tom and Sue playing with the ball. He wants the ball for himself. He walks to them and says, "Give me that ball, or I will hurt you!" Tom and Sue are scared. They do not want to give the ball to the big person. They do not want to be hurt. They look at each other and think of a plan. Sue says to the big person, "OK, you can have the ball, but first you have to show us how strong you are. Can you throw the ball very far?" The big person smiles. He thinks he is very strong. He takes the ball from Sue and says, "Watch this!" He throws the ball as hard as he can. But the ball does not go far. It goes up in the air and comes down on the big person's head. It makes a loud noise and hurts him. He falls to the ground and cries. Tom and Sue see what happens. They are happy. They run to the ball and pick it up. They say to the big person, "You are not strong, you are weak! You cannot throw the ball well. You are a bad person. Go away and leave us alone!" They run away from the big person and keep playing with the ball. They are not scared anymore. They are brave and smart. They have fun.
