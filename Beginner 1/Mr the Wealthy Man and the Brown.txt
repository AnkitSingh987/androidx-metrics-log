Category: Beginner
Title: Mr the Wealthy Man and the Brown

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wealthy man named Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown loved to watch the sky. He liked to see the birds fly and the clouds move. One day, Mr. Brown saw a little girl named Lily. She was playing with a toy that could spin. "Look, Mr. Brown!" said Lily. "My toy can spin round and round!" Mr. Brown smiled and said, "That's a fun toy, Lily!" Lily shared her toy with Mr. Brown, and they both took turns making it spin. They laughed and played under the big blue sky. From that day on, Mr. Brown and Lily became good friends. They enjoyed watching the sky and playing with the spinning toy together.

Dan and Sue are husband and wife. They love each other very much. They live in a small house with a big yard. They have a dog named Spot and a cat named Fluffy. One day, Dan goes to work. He works in a big building with many people. He helps them with their computers. Sue stays at home. She cleans the house and makes food. She plays with Spot and Fluffy. She waits for Dan to come back. But Dan does not come back. He does not call Sue. He does not answer his phone. Sue is very worried. She does not know where Dan is. She does not know what happened to him. She calls the police. The police look for Dan. They ask many people. They check many places. They do not find Dan. They do not know where Dan is. They do not know what happened to him. Sue is very sad. She cries a lot. She misses Dan very much. She does not want to live without him. She does not want to be alone. She does not want to be a wife without a husband. She takes some pills. She drinks some water. She lies on the bed. She closes her eyes. She does not wake up. She does not answer anyone. She does not know anything. She is gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the sun. One day, it rained and everything became wet. Tim wanted to play, but he didn't want to get wet. Tim thought of an idea. He found his friend, Sam, and said, "Let's play inside today." Sam agreed and they played with their toys. But Tim still felt guilty because he wanted to play outside. Finally, Tim decided to surrender to the rain. He put on his boots and ran outside. The rain was wet, but he had fun splashing in the puddles. Tim learned that sometimes it's okay to get wet and have fun in the rain.

Ben and Mia liked to swim in the sea. They had masks and fins to see the fish and the reef. The reef was like a big garden under the water, with many colors and shapes. Ben and Mia liked to play with the fish and look for shells and starfish. One day, they saw a big boat near the reef. The boat had a big net that it threw into the water. Ben and Mia were curious and swam closer to see what the boat was doing. They saw that the net was catching many fish and some of the reef. They felt sad and angry. They wanted to help the fish and the reef. They saw a man on the boat who was speaking to another man. They could not hear what he was saying, but they saw that he was pointing at the net and smiling. He looked happy and greedy. Ben and Mia did not like him. They wanted to make him stop. They had an idea. They swam to the net and started to pull and bite it. They made holes in the net and let some of the fish and the reef out. They hoped that the man would see and get scared. They hoped that he would leave the reef alone. But the man did not see. He was too busy speaking and laughing. He did not care about the fish and the reef. He only cared about the money he would get. He did not know that there was something else in the water. Something big and fierce. Something that did not like boats and nets. Something that liked to eat fish and men. It was a shark. A big, fierce shark. It smelled the fish and the blood in the water. It saw the boat and the net. It saw the man who was speaking and laughing. It felt hungry and angry. It wanted to make him stop. It swam fast and hard. It jumped out of the water and bit the net. It tore the net and let all the fish and the reef out. It saw the man who was speaking and laughing. It bit him too. It made him stop. Ben and Mia saw the shark. They were scared and amazed. They swam away from the boat and the net. They swam back to the shore. They were safe and happy. They thanked the shark for saving the fish and the reef. They hoped that the man would learn his lesson. They hoped that he would never come back.
