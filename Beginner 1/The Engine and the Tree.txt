Category: Beginner
Title: The Engine and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little engine. This engine loved to hurry all day long. It would go fast and make loud noises. The engine was very happy when it could hurry. One day, the engine met a big, calm tree. The tree liked to stand still and watch the world go by. The engine asked the tree, "Why are you so calm?" The tree said, "I like to watch and listen. It makes me happy." The little engine thought about what the tree said. It decided to try being calm too. The engine slowed down and stopped by the tree. They watched the world together. The engine felt happy in a new way. From that day on, the engine learned to hurry sometimes and be calm sometimes. And the engine and the tree were the best of friends.

Bob was an impatient boy. He was always looking for something new to do; something exciting. One day, he noticed a sun in the sky. It was big and bright, and it looked very funny to him. He decided to try and shoot it with his slingshot. He carefully placed a smooth stone in the slot, pulled back the elastic and took aim. He let go and the stone flew towards the sun with a whoosh. "What happened? Where did the stone go?" Bob asked in a very curious voice. His dad chuckled and said, "Let's wait and see." They waited and waited but nothing happened. After a few minutes, Bob started to get restless. "Dad, I don't understand. Why isn't anything happening?" he asked in an impatient voice. "Just wait, Bob. You're going to be very surprised," his dad said with a smile. Bob didn't understand, but he did as he was told. A few minutes later, they were both surprised by a loud explosion. The sun had been shot by Bob's stone! He was so excited that he started jumping around in joy. What Bob didn't know was that his dad had set up the scene. His dad had known all along that the stone would never hit the sun, and that what they were really waiting for were the fireworks!

Sam and Mia were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. They saw a big hill with many leaves. They wanted to climb the hill and jump in the leaves. "Let's go to the hill!" Sam said. "We can have fun there!" "OK!" Mia said. "But be careful. The hill is high and steep." They ran to the hill and started to climb. They used their hands and feet to go up. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their skin. They were happy. They reached the top of the hill and looked around. They saw the park, the trees, the sky and the birds. They saw a big pile of leaves at the bottom of the hill. "Look at the leaves!" Sam said. "They are red and yellow and brown. They are soft and crunchy. Let's jump in them!" "OK!" Mia said. "But how do we get down?" Sam had an idea. He raised his arms and shouted, "We can fly!" He jumped off the hill and tried to fly. But he could not fly. He only fell. He fell fast and hard. He landed in the leaves with a thud. He felt pain in his leg and his head. He cried. Mia saw Sam fall. She was scared and sad. She wanted to help him. She was thoughtful. She did not jump. She climbed down the hill slowly and carefully. She reached Sam and hugged him. "Are you OK?" Mia asked. "I'm sorry you fell. You can't fly. You are not a bird." Sam stopped crying. He felt Mia's hug. He felt better. He smiled. "I'm OK," Sam said. "Thank you for being thoughtful. You are a good friend. Maybe we can't fly, but we can still play in the leaves." They played in the leaves. They threw them in the air and made them rain. They laughed and had fun. They forgot about the fall. They were happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He saw a big jar of butter on the table. Tim wanted to eat some butter with his bread. He tried to grab the jar, but it was too high for him. Tim felt confused. Tim's big sister, Sue, saw him. She asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "I want butter, but I can't grab it." Sue smiled and said, "I can help you." Sue tried to grab the butter, but it was too high for her too. They both felt confused. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's ask mom for help." Mom came to the kitchen and saw the problem. She grabbed the butter and put it on the table. Tim and Sue were happy. They ate their bread with butter and thanked their mom for helping them. The problem was solved, and they were not confused anymore.
