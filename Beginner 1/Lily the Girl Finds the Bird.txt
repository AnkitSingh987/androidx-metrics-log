Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to play with her football. One day, she went outside to play with her ball in the park. Lily saw a big tree and had an idea. She found a long string and attached it to her football. She threw the ball over a branch and pulled the string. The football went up and down, up and down. Lily was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird came and sat on the football. The bird thought the football was an egg! Lily smiled and let the bird play with her football. She made a new friend that day, and they played together in the park.

---

One day, a dog named Bow went to the park. Bow loved to play with his friends. They had lots of fun running and playing. Bow was very happy. But then, a big bad cat came to the park. The cat was mean and did not want to play with Bow and his friends. The cat said, "Go away, I don't want to play with you!" Bow felt sad, but he had an idea. He said to the cat, "Let's try to play together and have fun. Maybe you will like it too." The cat thought about it and said, "Okay, let's try." They all played together and had so much fun. The bad cat was not mean anymore. Bow was happy that everyone could play and have fun together.

---

Once upon a time, there was a big gorilla named Jolly. He was very jolly and liked to play with his friends. One day, Jolly saw a little bird who was sad. The bird did not know how to fly. "Hello, little bird," said Jolly. "Why are you sad?" "I do not know how to fly," said the bird. "All my friends can fly, but I can't." Jolly wanted to help the bird. He said, "I know how to help you. Let's ask our friends for help, too." So, Jolly and the bird went to their friends. They all worked together to teach the bird how to fly. After some time, the bird finally learned to fly. The bird was very happy and thanked Jolly and all their friends. From that day on, the bird and Jolly played together every day. The moral of the story is: If you do not know how to do something, do not be sad. Ask your friends for help, and they will be happy to help you.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there lived a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day with his friends. They would play games and have lots of fun. One day, while swimming, Fin saw an unknown fish. This fish was big and looked different. Fin was scared but wanted to be friends. Fin swam closer and said, "Hi, I'm Fin! Do you want to play with us?" The unknown fish looked at Fin and said, "I don't want to fight. I want to be friends too." Fin was happy and introduced the unknown fish to his friends. They all played and swam together, having a great time in the pond.

Ben and Lily were playing knights and princesses in the garden. Ben had a big shield made of cardboard and Lily had a fancy dress and a crown. They pretended to fight dragons and save the castle. But then, Tom came over. Tom was Lily's big brother and he liked to tease them. He saw Ben's shield and laughed. "That's a silly shield," he said. "It's not real. It's just paper. I can break it with my hand." He reached out to grab the shield from Ben. Ben was angry and scared. He did not want Tom to take his shield. He liked his shield. He had painted it with red and blue and yellow. He held the shield tight and stared at Tom. He tried to look brave. "No, you can't," he said. "This is my shield. Go away." Lily saw what was happening and ran to help Ben. She was not afraid of Tom. She was his sister and she knew how to make him stop. She stood in front of Ben and shouted at Tom. "Leave him alone," she said. "You are mean. You can't play with us. We don't like you." Tom was surprised and annoyed. He did not like being told what to do by his little sister. He wanted to make them cry. He pushed Lily aside and tried to snatch the shield from Ben. But Ben was stronger than he thought. He pushed Tom back and hit him with the shield. Tom fell down and hurt his nose. He started to cry. Ben and Lily were happy. They had won the fight. They hugged each other and cheered. They went back to playing knights and princesses. They did not care about Tom. He was a bad dragon. They had a good shield. They were brave.
