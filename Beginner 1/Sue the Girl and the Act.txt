Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Act

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to act like a big girl. One day, she saw a big tree with a sour fruit on it. Sue thought that if she could get the fruit, she would be a big girl. Sue tried to climb the tree. She put her knee on a branch and pulled herself up. But the branch broke, and Sue fell down. She hurt her knee and cried. Her mom came and hugged her. Sue learned that it's okay to be little and that she should not act like a big girl too soon. She knew that she would grow up one day, but for now, she would be happy being a little girl.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They saw an enormous slide that looked like a lot of fun. Anna wanted to go first, but Ben said no. "I saw it first, so I go first," Ben said. "No, you did not. I saw it first, so I go first," Anna said. They both ran to the slide, but there was a problem. There was a chain on the slide that said "Do not use. Broken." "Look, we cannot use the slide. It is broken," Anna said. Ben did not listen. He wanted to use the slide anyway. He tried to pull the chain off, but it was too strong. "Stop, Ben. You will get hurt. The chain is there for a reason," Anna said. Ben did not stop. He pulled harder and harder, until the chain snapped. He fell to the ground with a loud thud. He started to cry. Anna ran to him and hugged him. She was sorry he was hurt, but she was also angry he did not listen. "Why did you not listen to me, Ben? I told you the slide was broken. I told you the chain was there for a reason. Why did you not listen?" Anna said. Ben sniffed and looked at her. He was sorry he did not listen, but he was also sad he could not use the slide. "I wanted to use the slide, Anna. It looked like a lot of fun. I did not mean to break the chain. I did not mean to get hurt. I am sorry," Ben said. Anna sighed and kissed his cheek. She was still angry, but she was also his friend. "It is okay, Ben. I am sorry too. I know you wanted to use the slide. But we have to be careful. We have to follow the rules. We have to listen to each other. Do you understand?" Anna said. Ben nodded and smiled. He understood. He hugged her back. "Yes, I understand, Anna. Thank you for being my friend. Can we play something else?" Ben said. Anna smiled and nodded. She was glad he was okay. She was glad they were friends. "Sure, Ben. We can play something else. How about the swings? They are not broken. They are fun. Do you want to go?" Anna said. Ben nodded and got up. He took her hand and they walked to the swings. They forgot about the slide and the chain. They had fun. They were happy.

One day, a girl named Jane was playing in the kitchen. She saw a small box on the table. It was an ashtray. Jane did not know what it was. She thought it was a toy. She picked it up and looked inside. There was something spicy inside the ashtray. It was red and smelled funny. Jane wanted to find out what the spicy thing was. She took a little bit and put it in her mouth. It was very hot! Jane did not like it. She ran to her mom and cried. Her mom asked her what was wrong. Jane showed her mom the ashtray with the spicy thing inside. Her mom laughed and said, "That is not a toy, Jane. That is an ashtray. And the spicy thing is called pepper." Jane's mom took the ashtray and shut the lid. She put it away where Jane could not reach it. Jane learned that not everything in the kitchen was a toy. From that day on, she was careful with what she touched.
