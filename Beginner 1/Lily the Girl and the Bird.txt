Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily was walking in the park when she saw a scared little bird. The bird was crying because it was lost and didn't know how to get back to its nest. Lily said, "Don't be scared, little bird. I will help you find your home." The bird was happy and thanked Lily with a sweet song from its heart. Together, they walked through the park, looking for the bird's nest. Soon, they found the bird's family, and everyone was happy to see the little bird again. They all decided to celebrate and have a big party. All the birds sang and danced, and Lily joined them too. At the end of the day, Lily went back home with a big smile on her face, knowing she had made a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to play outside. He put on his shoes and ran out the door with a big smile on his face. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and they started to play. They were having so much fun. But then, Tim felt a sneeze coming. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. "Achoo!" he sneezed, and his smile went away. Sue didn't want to get sick, so she said, "I have to go home now." Tim was sad and went home too. He sneezed and sneezed all day. His fun day was over, and he felt bad.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there lived a boy named Sam. Sam loved to play with his toy police car. He would push the car around and make siren sounds, pretending to catch bad guys. One sunny day, Sam took his toy police car outside to play in the park. He zoomed it up and down the slide, and all around the swings. Sam was having so much fun that he didn't notice his toy car was getting dirty. Suddenly, Sam tripped on a rock and dropped his toy police car. The car got a big scratch and Sam felt sad. He picked up the car and looked at it closely. He knew it wasn't perfect anymore, but he still loved it. Sam's mom saw what happened and came over to give him a hug. She told him that even though the toy police car had a scratch, it was still special because it was his favorite. Sam smiled and continued to play with his toy car, knowing that it was still his most-loved toy, even with the scratch. From that day on, Sam learned that it's okay if things aren't perfect, because they can still be special and loved. He continued to play with his ordinary toy police car, and they had many more adventures together.

Ben and Mia are friends who like to play pretend. They have a big box that they use as their lab. They make things with paper, glue, and crayons. They supply their lab with new things every day. One day, they hear a loud noise outside. They look out the window and see a big dog. The dog is barking and running around. Ben and Mia are scared of the dog. They hide in their lab. "Ben, what do we do?" Mia asks. "I don't know, Mia. The dog is scary. Maybe we should wait until it goes away," Ben says. "But what if it doesn't go away? What if it comes to our lab?" Mia says. Ben thinks for a moment. He remembers that his mom told him that dogs are not bad. They just need to be friendly and calm. He decides to be brave. "Mia, I have an idea. Maybe we can make the dog our friend. We can give it some of our snacks. Do you have any snacks in the lab?" Ben says. Mia nods. She has some crackers and cheese in her backpack. She gives them to Ben. "OK, Mia. You stay here. I will go outside and talk to the dog. Don't be afraid. I will be back soon," Ben says. He takes the snacks and opens the door. He sees the dog. The dog sees him. The dog stops barking and wags its tail. Ben smiles. He walks slowly to the dog. He holds out his hand with the snacks. "Hi, dog. We are Ben and Mia. We are not mean. We want to be your friends. Do you want some snacks?" Ben says. The dog sniffs Ben's hand. It licks the snacks. It licks Ben's hand. It makes a happy sound. Ben pets the dog. The dog likes Ben. "See, Mia? The dog is nice. Come and meet him," Ben says. Mia comes out of the lab. She is still a little scared. But she sees that Ben and the dog are getting along. She walks to them. She gives the dog a cracker. The dog licks her hand too. She pets the dog. The dog likes Mia. "Wow, Ben. You are so brave. You made the dog our friend. He is cute. What should we name him?" Mia says. Ben thinks for a moment. He looks at the dog. The dog has brown fur and black spots. "How about Spot? He has spots. Do you like Spot, dog?" Ben says. The dog barks. He wags his tail. He likes Spot. "Spot it is. Welcome to our lab, Spot. You can play with us. We have lots of things to show you," Mia says. Ben, Mia, and Spot go inside the lab. They play with paper, glue, and crayons. They supply their lab with new things every day. They are happy. They are friends.
