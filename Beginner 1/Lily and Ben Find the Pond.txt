Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Pond

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. But today it rains a lot. The park is wet and muddy. There is a big flood in the pond. "Look, Ben, the ducks are swimming in the flood!" Lily says. She points to the pond. "Let's go and feed them some bread." Ben shakes his head. He does not like the flood. He thinks it is scary and cold. He wants to play on the swings. "No, Lily, I don't want to go to the pond. I want to play on the swings. Come with me," he says. He pulls Lily's hand. Lily is bossy. She does not like it when Ben says no. She wants to do what she wants. She pulls back her hand. "No, Ben, I want to go to the pond. You come with me," she says. She stamps her foot. Lily and Ben are angry. They do not want to play together. They decide to go their own way. Lily goes to the pond. Ben goes to the swings. But soon they are sad. They miss each other. They do not have fun alone. They look at each other from far away. They wish they could play together. They decide to say sorry. They run to each other and hug. "I'm sorry, Lily. I was bossy. You can go to the pond if you want," Ben says. "I'm sorry, Ben. I was bossy too. You can play on the swings if you want," Lily says. They smile. They are friends again. They decide to share. They take turns to feed the ducks and play on the swings. They have fun in the park. They do not mind the flood. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a black cat named Bow. Bow was a very kind and friendly cat. He liked to play with his friends and help them when they needed it. One day, Bow found a big pot in his house. It was empty and needed to be filled. Bow had an idea. He decided to fill the pot with toys and share them with his friends. Bow went around the house and found many toys to put in the pot. Bow brought the pot of toys to his friends, and they were very happy. They played with the toys and had a lot of fun. Bow felt good because he shared his toys with his friends. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she had a big, round, orange ball. She liked to throw it and watch it bounce. Lucy was playing with her orange ball near the house. She threw it very high and far. But then, she couldn't find her orange ball. Lucy felt sad because she lost her ball. She looked and looked, but she couldn't find it. Lucy went to her mom and told her about the lost orange ball. Her mom asked if she felt guilty for losing it. Lucy said yes. Her mom told her it's okay to feel guilty, but they would look for the ball together. They found the orange ball under a bush. Lucy was so happy and hugged her mom. They played with the orange ball together and had lots of fun.

Tom and Mia were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Tom had a furry lion toy that he liked very much. Mia had a glue bottle that she found in the drawer. "Look, Tom, I have glue!" Mia said, holding up the bottle. "I can make things stick together!" "Can I see?" Tom asked, reaching for the bottle. "No, it's mine!" Mia said, pulling it away. "You have your lion, I have my glue!" Tom felt sad and angry. He wanted to see the glue too. He tried to grab the bottle from Mia, but she was too fast. She ran away from him, laughing. "Give it back, Mia!" Tom shouted, chasing her. Mia ran into the kitchen, where their mom was making lunch. She did not see the wet floor, where mom had spilled some juice. She slipped and fell, dropping the glue bottle. The bottle broke and the glue spilled all over her and the floor. "Ow, ow, ow!" Mia cried, feeling the glue stick to her skin and hair. "Help, mom, help!" Mom heard Mia's cry and rushed to the kitchen. She saw Mia covered in glue, and the mess on the floor. She was very angry and worried. "Mia, what have you done?" mom yelled. "This is very bad! You could have hurt yourself and Tom! And you made a big mess! How could you be so naughty?" Mom picked up Mia and took her to the bathroom. She tried to wash off the glue, but it was very hard. Some of the glue stayed in Mia's hair, making it look funny. Mia felt sorry and scared. She wished she had shared the glue with Tom. Tom followed mom and Mia to the bathroom. He saw Mia crying and looking sad. He felt sorry for her too. He wanted to help her, but he did not know how. He still had his furry lion toy, but he did not feel like playing anymore. He wished they had not fought over the glue. He wished they had played nicely together.
