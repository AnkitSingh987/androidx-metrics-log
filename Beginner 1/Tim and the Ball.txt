Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Ball

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to his grandma's house. He loved playing with his toys in the attic. One day, while he was playing, he saw a big, red ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball, but it was not fair. Tim tried to reach the ball, but it was too high. He thought of a plan. He would pinch the ball with a long stick. It was not easy, but Tim did not give up. He kept trying to pinch the ball. Finally, Tim pinched the ball and it started to fall. But, oh no! The ball hit a box, and the box fell on the floor. Tim was sad because the box broke. His grandma was not happy, and Tim knew he should not have played with the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. He liked to draw and put his pictures on the wall. One day, he drew a big, pretty picture of a cat. Tom showed the picture to his friend, Sue. "Look at my cat picture!" he said. Sue looked at the picture and smiled. "I like your cat picture, Tom!" she said. "Can you put it on my wall?" Tom and Sue went to her house. They put the cat picture on her wall. Sue was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom! I love the cat picture on my wall." Every day, Tom and Sue would look at the cat picture and smile. They were happy to have such a pretty picture on the wall. And they were happy to be friends who liked the same things.

Lily was a shy girl who liked to play by herself in the garden. She had a lot of toys, but she did not have many friends. She was scared of talking to other kids, because they might laugh at her or be mean to her. One day, she saw a new boy in the house next door. He was playing with a ball and a dog. He looked happy and friendly. Lily wanted to join him, but she did not know how to say hello. She hid behind the hedge and watched him. The boy saw Lily peeking through the hedge. He smiled and waved at her. He said, "Hi, I'm Tom. What's your name?" Lily felt her face get hot. She wanted to say something, but she could not find the words. She ran back to her house and closed the door. Tom was confused. He did not understand why Lily ran away. He thought she looked nice and wanted to be her friend. He decided to try again the next day. The next day, he brought a gift for Lily. It was a teddy bear with a pink bow. He knocked on her door and said, "Hi, Lily. I brought you something. Do you want to play with me and my dog?" Lily opened the door and saw the teddy bear. She liked it very much. She felt a little less shy. She said, "Thank you, Tom. It's very pretty. I'm sorry I ran away yesterday. I was scared." Tom said, "That's okay, Lily. I was scared too when I moved here. But now I'm happy. Do you want to join me in the garden? We can play with the ball and the dog. They are very fun." Lily nodded and smiled. She took the teddy bear and followed Tom to the garden. She joined him in the game and had a lot of fun. She made a new friend and felt less shy. She was happy too.

Ben likes to jog with his dad in the park. They jog fast and slow, and see many things. They see dogs, birds, flowers, and bins. Ben likes to throw things in the bins, like cans and papers. He thinks it is fun. One day, Ben sees a big bin that is far away. He wants to throw something in it. He runs away from his dad, and picks up a stick. He does not see the road. He does not hear the car. He only sees the bin. The car sees Ben, but it is too late. It honks loud, and tries to stop. But it hits Ben, and Ben falls down. He hurts a lot, and bleeds. His dad runs to him, and cries. He calls for help, but the help is distant. Ben closes his eyes, and does not wake up.
