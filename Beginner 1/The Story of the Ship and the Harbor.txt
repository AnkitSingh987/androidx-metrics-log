Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ship and the Harbor

Once upon a time there was a blue harbor with many ships. The ships would come to the harbor and supply food and other goods to the people. One day, a little girl named Joanna was walking along the shore. All of a sudden, she saw a shiny blue ship sailing into the harbor. Joanna ran closer to see the ship. "Hello up there!" she shouted. "Who are you?" The man on the ship smiled and waved. "I'm supplying goods for your village," he said. Joanna was excited and shouted "Thank you, kind sir!" The man smiled as he dropped his anchor in the harbor. Then he hopped down to the shore and he and Joanna smiled and waved as he walked away. The blue harbor was now full of ships bringing goods to the people. Joanna looked on with a smile and thanked the man again for supplying the village.

Tom was a bad boy. He did not listen to his mom and dad. He liked to play with things that were not his. One day, he saw a nurse's bag in the park. He wanted to see what was inside. He ran to the bag and lifted it. "Hey, that's mine!" a voice said. Tom turned and saw a nurse. She looked angry. She was wearing a white coat and a hat with a red cross. She had a stethoscope around her neck. "Give it back, please. I need it for my work. I help sick people," the nurse said. Tom did not care. He wanted to keep the bag. He ran away with it. The nurse chased him. She was faster than Tom. She caught him and took the bag from him. "That was very bad of you. You should not take things that are not yours. You could have hurt someone who needed my help. How would you feel if someone took your toys?" the nurse said. Tom felt ashamed. He realized he was wrong. He looked at the nurse and said, "I'm sorry. I was bad. I won't do it again. Please forgive me." The nurse smiled. She was kind. She said, "I forgive you. But you have to promise me to be good from now on. And you have to say sorry to your mom and dad, too. They must be worried about you." Tom nodded. He said, "I promise. I will say sorry to them. And I will be good. Thank you, nurse." The nurse hugged him. She said, "You're welcome. Now, let's go find your mom and dad. They must be looking for you." Tom and the nurse walked back to the park. They found Tom's mom and dad. Tom ran to them and hugged them. He said, "I'm sorry, mom and dad. I was bad. I took a nurse's bag. But she forgave me. And I learned my lesson. I will be good." Tom's mom and dad were happy. They said, "We're glad you're safe. And we're proud of you for saying sorry and learning your lesson. But don't ever do that again. It's dangerous and wrong." Tom said, "I won't. I love you, mom and dad." They said, "We love you, too, Tom." The nurse waved goodbye. She said, "Bye, Tom. Be good. And thank you for giving me back my bag." Tom waved back. He said, "Bye, nurse. Thank you for being nice. And thank you for helping sick people." The end. Moral value: Do not take things that are not yours. It's bad and it can hurt others. Say sorry when you make a mistake and learn from it. Be good and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was an independent girl who liked to do things by herself. One day, she went to the beach to play in the sand and swim in the water. While she was playing, Lily saw a big fish jump out of the water. She was very surprised! She wanted to catch it, but it was too far away. So, Lily decided to pray and ask for help to catch the fish. As she was praying, a friendly dolphin came to her. The dolphin said, "I heard you pray, and I want to help you catch the fish." Lily was so happy! She rode on the dolphin's back, and they went to catch the fish together. But when they got close, they saw that it was not a fish, but a shiny treasure chest! Lily and the dolphin brought it back to the beach and opened it. Inside, they found beautiful shells and pretty stones. They shared the treasure and became best friends. And from that day on, Lily knew that sometimes it's good to ask for help and not always be independent.
