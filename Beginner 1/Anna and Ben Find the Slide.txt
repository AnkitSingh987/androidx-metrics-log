Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben Find the Slide

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play together. Today, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. "Look, Ben, a slide!" Anna says. She is enthusiastic. She runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. "Wait for me, Anna!" Ben says. He follows her. He is also enthusiastic. He likes the slide. They slide down together. Whee! They enjoy the slide. They laugh and smile. They go up and down again and again. "Let's try the swing!" Ben says. He points to the swing. He wants to swing high. "Okay, Ben!" Anna says. She agrees. She likes the swing too. They run to the swing. Their moms help them. They push them gently. Anna and Ben swing back and forth. They feel the wind in their hair. They enjoy the swing. They giggle and shout. "Let's go to the sandbox!" Anna says. She sees the sandbox. She wants to make a castle. "Okay, Anna!" Ben says. He nods. He likes the sandbox too. They hop off the swing. They go to the sandbox. They find some buckets and shovels. They dig and fill and dump. They enjoy the sandbox. They make a big castle. Anna and Ben are happy. They have fun. They love to play together. They love the park. They hug their moms and say thank you. They are good friends.

Lily and Tom were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They used pots and pans and spoons and bowls. They also used some things from the cupboard, like salt and sugar and pepper. "Let's make a cake!" said Lily. "We need sugar and eggs and flour and milk." "OK!" said Tom. "I will get the sugar and the eggs. You get the flour and the milk." Lily opened the cupboard and saw a big jar of pepper. She thought it looked like chocolate sprinkles. She wanted to put some on the cake. She reached for the jar, but it was too high. She stood on her toes and stretched her arms. She touched the jar, but it was heavy. She pulled it, but it slipped from her fingers. The jar of pepper fell on the floor. It broke into pieces. Pepper flew everywhere. It got in Lily's eyes and nose and mouth. It made her sneeze and cough and cry. "Ah-choo! Ah-choo! Ah-choo!" she sneezed. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" she cried. Tom heard Lily and ran to her. He saw the mess and the pepper and the broken jar. He was scared. He thought Lily was hurt. "Lily, are you OK?" he asked. "What happened?" "Pepper, pepper, pepper!" Lily sobbed. "It fell and it broke and it hurts!" Tom hugged Lily and tried to calm her down. He wiped her eyes and nose and mouth with a towel. He said sorry for the pepper and the jar. He said they would clean up and make a new cake. Lily stopped crying and sneezing. She hugged Tom back and said thank you. She said sorry too. She said they would be more careful next time. They cleaned up the mess and threw away the pepper and the jar. They washed their hands and faces. They got new ingredients and made a new cake. They put sugar and eggs and flour and milk in a bowl. They mixed them well. They did not put any pepper on the cake. They put it in the oven and waited for it to bake. They smelled the cake and it smelled good. They took it out and let it cool. They cut a slice for each of them and ate it. It tasted good. They smiled and said yum. They were happy. They liked to play in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They liked to be together. They were Lily and Tom.

One day, a young boy named Tim went to the park. He was a little anxious because it was his first time going alone. Tim saw a big slide and wanted to try it. He climbed up the ladder and sat at the top, ready to slide down. As he started to slide, he saw a girl at the bottom. "Look out!" he yelled. But it was too late. Tim bumped into the girl and they both fell. The girl started to cry and scream. Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't see you." The girl stopped crying and said, "It's okay. My name is Lily." Tim and Lily became friends and played together in the park all day.
