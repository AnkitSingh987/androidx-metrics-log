Category: Beginner
Title: The Cop and the Box

Once upon a time, in a normal town, there was a cop. The cop was very nice. He liked to help people. One day, the cop had to deliver a big box. He did not know what was in the box. The cop walked and walked with the big box. He was very tired. But he did not stop. He wanted to be helpful. The cop saw a little girl. She was lost and sad. The cop knew he had to help her. The cop opened the big box. Inside the box, there were many balloons. The cop gave the little girl a balloon. She was very happy. The cop and the girl walked together. The girl found her mom. The cop was happy too. The big box helped the cop make the girl happy.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the water and surf on the big waves. One sunny day, Spot went to the beach with his friend, a little girl named Sue. "Spot, let's surf today!" said Sue. Spot barked happily and ran to the water. They both jumped on a big surfboard and rode the waves. They laughed and played all day long. But then, Spot saw a big log that was starting to rot. He was scared and didn't want to surf near it. "Sue, look at the log! It's not good," Spot barked. Sue saw the rotting log and nodded. They moved away from the log and continued to surf and have fun. At the end of the day, they went home with big smiles on their faces.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars. They have a lot of fun together. One day, Ben has to go away with his family. He tells Lily, "I will miss you. You are my best friend." Lily is sad. She says, "I will miss you too. When will you come back?" Ben says, "I don't know. Maybe in ten days. That is a big number. It is more than zero." Lily nods. She knows zero is nothing. She says, "I will count the days until you come back. Then we can play again." Ben hugs Lily. He says, "OK. I will write you a letter. You can write me one too. Do you know how to write?" Lily says, "Yes. I can write my name and some words. But I need help with some letters. They are hard." Ben says, "Me too. But we can try. Maybe we can draw pictures too. I like to draw cars. What do you like to draw?" Lily says, "I like to draw flowers. And stars. And you." Ben smiles. He says, "That is nice. I will draw you too. And the blocks and the cars. And a big heart." Lily says, "I like that. I will draw a big heart too. And I will keep it in my room. It will make me happy." Ben says, "Me too. I will keep your letter and your picture in my room. It will make me happy too." They say goodbye. They wave to each other. They hope to see each other soon. Lily goes back to her room. She looks at her blocks and her cars. She feels lonely. She misses Ben. She decides to tidy her room. She thinks Ben will like it when he comes back. She puts the blocks in a box. She puts the cars in a basket. She makes her bed. She feels a little better. She takes a paper and a crayon. She starts to write a letter to Ben. She writes, "Dear Ben, I miss you. You are my best friend. I hope you have fun. I tidied my room. I drew you a picture. I love you. From Lily." She draws a flower, a star, and a big heart. She puts the letter in an envelope. She asks her mom to help her send it. She hopes Ben will get it soon. She hopes he will write back. She hopes he will come back. She misses him.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to skip and wear her pink dress. One day, she went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree and wanted to sit under it. As she sat down, she saw a little bird with a hurt wing. "Oh no," said Amy. "You need help, little bird." She wanted to help the bird but didn't know how. So, she went to her friend, the wise old owl. "Please help me, Mr. Owl. I found a bird with a hurt wing," Amy said. Mr. Owl looked at Amy and said, "You can help the bird by being kind and gentle. Take it home and give it a safe place to rest." So, Amy took the bird home and cared for it. She gave the bird food and water, and soon the bird felt better. One day, while Amy was playing outside, she tripped and fell. She felt very sad and began to cry. To her surprise, the little bird she had helped flew down and gave her a soft hug. Amy felt happy and smiled. "Thank you, little bird," she said. The moral of the story is that when you are kind to others, they will be kind to you too. And even the smallest of friends can bring you happiness when you least expect it.
