Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Bear and the Woods

One day, a big bear named Bob went into a chamber in the deep woods. The chamber was dark and small. Bob was curious and wanted to see what was inside. As Bob walked in, he saw a little mouse named Molly. Molly was eating something spicy. "Hi Bob!" said Molly. "I am eating spicy food. Do you want some?" Bob said, "Yes, please!" and took a bite. Suddenly, Bob felt a tickle in his nose. "Oh no!" said Bob, "I think I am going to sneeze!" Bob sneezed so hard that the walls of the chamber shook. Then, something unexpected happened. The wall opened up, and they saw a hidden room full of toys and treats! Bob and Molly were so happy, they played and ate treats all day long.

Once upon a time, there were two charming twin bunnies. They lived in a small house in the woods. The twins loved to play and have fun together. One day, they decided to go on an adventure in the woods. As they walked, they saw a big tree. One of the twins said, "Let's climb the tree!" So, they started to climb. They climbed higher and higher. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. They looked down and saw a big dog barking at them. They were scared. The twins knew they had to go home. They started to rush down the tree. But, they were not careful. One of the twins fell and hurt his leg. The other twin said, "Oh no! We should have been more careful." They went back home, sad and hurt. They learned that they should always be careful when having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a gym. In the gym, there was a big, furry ball. A boy named Tim went to the gym to play. He saw the furry ball and wanted to kick it. He thought it would be fun. Tim said to his friend, "Let's kick the furry ball!" His friend said, "Yes, let's do it!" So, they kicked the furry ball. The ball went high up in the air and came back down. They laughed and had fun. But then, the furry ball got stuck in a tree. Tim and his friend tried to get it down, but they could not reach it. They were sad. The furry ball stayed in the tree, and they could not play with it anymore. The end.

Lily and Ben went to the park with Mom. They saw a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. They wanted to play with everything. Mom said they could take turns. Lily went to the slide first. She climbed up the ladder and slid down fast. She laughed and said, "That was fun!" Ben waited for his turn. He was happy for Lily. Then Ben went to the swing. He pushed himself back and forth. He felt like he was flying. He smiled and said, "This is great!" Lily waited for her turn. She was happy for Ben. Then they went to the sandbox. They found some shovels and buckets. They started to make a castle. They worked together and said, "This is cool!" But then a boy came to the sandbox. He was bigger than Lily and Ben. He had a mean face. He said, "This is my sandbox. Go away!" He grabbed their shovels and buckets. He threw sand at them. He was rude. Lily and Ben were scared and sad. They ran to Mom. They told her what happened. Mom was angry. She said, "That is not nice. He should share. Let's go talk to him." Mom went to the sandbox with Lily and Ben. She saw the boy. She said, "Hello. I am Lily and Ben's mom. Why did you take their toys and throw sand at them?" The boy said, "Because I can. This is my sandbox. I don't care about them." Mom said, "That is not how you treat others. You should say sorry and give back their toys. You should play nicely with them. They are good kids. They want to be your friend." The boy said, "No. I don't want to. I don't need friends. I do what I want. You can't tell me what to do." He stuck out his tongue. He was rude. Mom said, "That is not how you talk to adults. You are being very rude. You need to learn some manners. You need to be kind. You need to listen. You need to order a melon." The boy said, "What? Order a melon? What does that mean?" Mom said, "It means you need to do something good for someone else. A melon is a big fruit. It is sweet and juicy. It is good for you. It is good to share. If you order a melon, you can give some to Lily and Ben. You can say sorry and be nice. You can make them happy. You can make yourself happy. You can be a good boy." The boy thought for a moment. He looked at Lily and Ben. They looked sad and scared. He felt bad. He said, "OK. I will order a melon. I will give some to Lily and Ben. I will say sorry and be nice. I will play with them. I will be a good boy." Mom said, "That is good. I am proud of you. You are learning. You are growing. You are ordering a melon." Lily and Ben smiled. They said, "Thank you. We forgive you. We want to be your friend. We want to play with you. We want to share the melon." The boy smiled. He said, "You're welcome. I am sorry. I want to be your friend. I want to play with you. I want to share the melon." They hugged and laughed. They played in the sandbox. They ate the melon. They had fun. They were happy. They ordered a melon.
