Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Paint

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to paint. They had many colors of paint and brushes and paper. They painted flowers and animals and houses and cars. They had fun and shared their paint and brushes. One day, they decided to paint each other's skin. Lily painted Ben's face blue and green and yellow. Ben painted Lily's arms red and purple and orange. They laughed and looked at each other. They looked funny and colorful. But then, Lily's mom came and saw them. She was angry and shouted. "What have you done? You are not supposed to paint your skin! That is bad and messy and can hurt your skin! Go wash it off right now!" Lily and Ben were scared and sad. They did not mean to make Lily's mom angry. They thought they were just having fun. They ran to the bathroom and tried to wash off the paint. But the paint was hard to wash off. It stained their skin and clothes and towels. Lily's mom came and helped them wash off the paint. She was still angry, but she was also sorry. She said, "I'm sorry I shouted at you. I know you were just playing. But you have to be careful with paint. Paint is for paper, not for skin. It can make your skin itch or burn or sick. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nodded and said, "Yes, we understand. We are sorry, too. We won't paint our skin again. We will only paint on paper." Lily's mom hugged them and said, "It's okay. I love you both. You are good and dependable friends. But next time, please ask me before you use paint. Okay?" Lily and Ben said, "Okay." They smiled and hugged Lily's mom. They were still friends who liked to paint. But they learned to be more careful and respectful with paint.

Once upon a time, there was a big, comfortable chair. It was a luxury chair that everyone wanted to sit on. One day, a small boy named Tim decided to sit on the chair. He loved how soft and nice it felt, but he knew he was not allowed to sit on it. Tim's mom saw him sitting on the luxury chair and said, "Tim, you know you cannot sit on that chair. I must punish you." Tim felt sad and got off the chair. He went to his room and sat on his bed. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's mom came into his room with a big smile on her face. She said, "I have a surprise for you!" She showed Tim a new, smaller chair just like the luxury one. Now, Tim had his own comfortable chair to sit on and he was so happy.

Tom and Mia like to play in the park. They have a lot of fun on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. One day, they find a shiny thing in the grass. It is a jewel. It is red and sparkly and has a hole in the middle. "Wow, look at this!" Tom says. He picks up the jewel and holds it up to the sun. It makes a rainbow on his hand. "What is it?" Mia asks. She wants to touch it too. "I don't know. Maybe it is a magic thing. Maybe it can make wishes come true," Tom says. He puts the jewel on his finger and makes a wish. He wishes for a big ice cream cone. "Did it work?" Mia asks. She looks around for an ice cream truck. Tom shakes his head. He does not see any ice cream. He feels sad. He thinks the jewel is not magic. He thinks it is just a normal thing. "Maybe we should give it back," Mia says. She thinks someone might be looking for the jewel. She thinks it might be important to them. "Who?" Tom asks. He does not want to give it back. He likes the jewel. He thinks it is pretty. "I don't know. Maybe someone who lost it. Maybe someone who made it. Maybe someone who needs it," Mia says. She shrugs. She does not know who the jewel belongs to. She just has a feeling. Tom thinks for a moment. He looks at the jewel. He looks at Mia. He looks at the park. He decides to do the right thing. He takes the jewel off his finger and gives it to Mia. "OK, let's give it back," he says. He smiles. He feels good. He thinks he and Mia are good friends. They walk around the park and look for someone who might own the jewel. They ask the people they see. They ask the lady with the dog. They ask the man with the bike. They ask the kids with the ball. No one knows about the jewel. No one claims it. They are about to give up when they see a sign. It says: "Lost: Red jewel with hole. Reward: Big hug. Please call: Grandma." Tom and Mia look at each other. They smile. They have found the owner of the jewel. They run to the phone and call Grandma. They tell her they have her jewel. They tell her they are in the park. They tell her they are happy to help. Grandma is very happy. She thanks them for finding her jewel. She tells them it is a special thing. She tells them it was a gift from her husband. She tells them he is in heaven now. She tells them she loves them. She comes to the park and meets them. She gives them a big hug. She gives them the jewel. She tells them to keep it. She tells them it is a gift from her and her husband. She tells them it is a magic thing. She tells them it can make wishes come true. She tells them to wish for something good. Tom and Mia are very surprised. They are very grateful. They are very happy. They put the jewel on a string and share it. They make a wish. They wish for more hugs. They wish for more friends. They wish for more fun. Their wish comes true. The jewel is magic. It makes them happy. It makes Grandma happy. It makes the park happy. It is not a normal thing. It is a wonderful thing.
