Category: Beginner
Title: The Vendor and the Box

One day, a vendor had a big box. The vendor was fit and strong. He could carry the box with no help. He wanted to give the box to someone. The box was a surprise. A girl saw the vendor and the big box. She wanted to receive the box. She asked, "Can I have the box?" The vendor said, "Yes, but you must be fit to carry it." The girl was small, but she was strong too. The girl tried to carry the box. It was very big, but she could do it. She was happy to receive the surprise. But when she opened the box, she found it was full of more boxes! The girl laughed and said, "Now I can share the surprise with my friends!"

Lily liked to go to the lake with her dad. He was a fisherman and he taught her how to catch fish. Lily loved to see the fish splash and wiggle on the hook. She always let them go back to the water after she looked at them. One day, they went to the lake when the sky was dark and the wind was strong. The water was rough and made big waves. Lily's dad said they had to be careful and stay close to the shore. He gave Lily a small rod and a worm and told her to wait for a fish to bite. Lily waited and waited, but no fish came. She was bored and cold. She saw a big rock near the water and thought it would be fun to climb on it. She left her rod on the ground and ran to the rock. She did not tell her dad. She climbed on the rock and looked at the lake. She saw a big fish jump out of the water. It was shiny and had a long nose. Lily wanted to catch it. She reached out her hand and tried to grab it. But the fish was too fast and too far. Lily lost her balance and fell off the rock. She screamed as she hit the water. It was cold and deep. She could not swim. She felt the fish touch her leg and she screamed again. She was scared and wet. Her dad heard her scream and ran to the water. He saw Lily's head bob up and down. He threw his rod and jumped in. He swam to Lily and grabbed her. He pulled her out of the water and hugged her. He was angry and worried. "Lily, why did you leave your rod and go to the rock? You could have drowned! You have to listen to me and stay safe!" he said. Lily was sorry and sad. She cried and said, "I'm sorry, dad. I wanted to catch the big fish. It was shiny and had a long nose. I did not mean to fall." Her dad looked at the lake and saw the big fish. It was a pike, a fish with sharp teeth. He realized how lucky they were. He said, "Lily, that fish was not a good fish. It was a bad fish. It could have hurt you. You have to be careful and not touch it. You have to be happy with the small fish. They are nice and gentle." Lily nodded and said, "OK, dad. I'm sorry. I love you." Her dad said, "I love you too, Lily. Let's go home and get warm." They packed their things and left the lake. They did not catch any fish that day, but they learned a lesson. They never went to the lake when the sky was dark and the wind was strong. They never left their rods and climbed on rocks. They never tried to catch the big fish with the long nose. They were happy with the small fish that splashed and wiggled. They were happy with each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to watch the stars. It was easy to see them because it was a clear night. Tim loved to look at the stars and make wishes. As Tim was watching, he saw a star that was different from the others. It was bigger and brighter. Suddenly, the star started to move! Tim couldn't believe his eyes. The star came closer and closer to him. As the star got close, it changed! It was not a star at all. It was a big, friendly alien! The alien said, "Hello, I saw you watching me. I wanted to come say hi." Tim was so surprised, but he made a new friend that night.
