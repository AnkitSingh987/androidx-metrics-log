Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Slide

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. Lily was very happy. Lily saw a boy named Tom. Tom had a stamp. He was putting the stamp on a paper. Lily asked, "Can I see your stamp?" Tom said, "Yes, you can." Lily and Tom played together. They went to the slide. Lily slid down first. She was very graceful. Then, Tom slid down too. But, when Tom slid down, he found a big, shiny coin on the slide. They were both surprised and happy. They took the shiny coin and went to buy more stamps to play with.

Sara and Tom are playing on the farm. They like to see the animals and feed them. Sara has a bag of carrots for the horses. Tom has a bucket of corn for the chickens. They see a big brown cow in the field. Sara wants to pet the cow. She runs to the fence and reaches out her hand. The cow comes closer and sniffs her hand. Sara smiles and strokes the cow's nose. Tom is curious. He wants to see what the cow feels like. He follows Sara and puts his hand on the cow's back. The cow has soft fur and warm skin. Tom likes the cow. He moves his hand to the cow's ear. The cow has a big ear and a small earring. Tom wonders what the earring is for. He pulls on the earring. The cow does not like that. The cow feels a pain in her ear. She moos loudly and shakes her head. She pinches Tom's hand with her teeth. Tom feels a pain in his hand. He screams and lets go of the earring. Sara hears Tom scream. She turns around and sees Tom holding his hand. She sees blood on his hand. She sees the cow looking angry. She is scared and angry too. She shouts at Tom. "Tom, you are bad! You hurt the cow! You made her pinch you! You should say sorry to the cow!" Tom is scared and sorry too. He cries and says sorry to Sara and the cow. He wants to go home. He wants his mom to make his hand better. Sara hugs Tom and says it's okay. She says they should go home too. She says they should be nice to the animals. She says they should learn more about them. They walk back to the house. They see their mom waiting for them. She sees their faces and their hands. She asks them what happened. They tell her their story. She listens and nods. She says they did a good thing to say sorry. She says they should be careful next time. She says she will make their hands better. She says she loves them. They say they love her too. They hug and kiss. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a foolish man named Tim. He loved to play with his friends, but he would always break things. One day, he broke his friends' toy network. The network was a set of toy trains and tracks that they all loved to play with. His friends were very sad. Tim wanted to make his friends happy again, so he thought of a plan. He would restore the toy network. He went to his room and found glue, tape, and some tools. He worked very hard to fix the toy trains and tracks. When Tim was done, he showed the fixed network to his friends. They were so happy to see their toy network working again! They all played together, and Tim promised to be more careful. From that day on, Tim was not foolish anymore, and he took better care of his friends' toys.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little bug. This bug liked to watch what other bugs did. One day, the bug saw a big bug feed a little bug some food. The nosy bug wanted to help feed the little bug too. The nosy bug went to find some food. It saw a big leaf with some sweet stuff on it. The bug took some of the sweet stuff and went back to the little bug. The little bug was very happy to get more food. The big bug saw the nosy bug help and said, "Thank you for helping me feed the little bug." The nosy bug felt happy that it could help. From that day on, the nosy bug and the big bug became good friends. They helped each other feed all the little bugs they could find.
