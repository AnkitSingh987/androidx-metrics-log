Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Dancer and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little dancer named Mia. Mia loved to dance all day long. She was a harmless girl who made friends with everyone. One day, Mia met a big bird named Bob. Bob was scared of Mia at first, but Mia said, "Don't worry, I am harmless. I just want to be friends." Bob liked Mia, so they played together and had fun. Later, Mia and Bob were tired from playing. They decided to settle down under a big tree. They talked and laughed until the sun went down. Mia and Bob became best friends and danced together every day.

---

One day, a pretty girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big tree with red and orange leaves. The leaves would fall and dance in the wind. Sue liked to watch them. She thought they were very pretty. As Sue was walking, she saw a little bird on the ground. The bird could not fly. Sue said, "Hi, bird! What is your name?" The bird said, "My name is Bob. I fell from my nest and I can't fly. Can you help me?" Sue wanted to help Bob, but she didn't know how. Then, a big wind came and blew the pretty leaves into the air. The leaves made a soft bed for Bob. Sue put Bob on the leaves and the wind lifted him up. The wind took Bob back to his nest. Sue and Bob were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Emma. Emma loved to bake with her mom. One night, they decided to bake a big cake. Emma was so excited, she could not wait. As they baked, Emma's mom said, "Emma, we must wait for the cake to bake." Emma was impatient. She wanted the cake now. She asked, "Can I see the cake, mom?" Her mom replied, "Yes, but be careful, it's hot." Finally, the cake was ready. Emma and her mom were very happy. They ate the cake together, and it was the best cake ever. From that night on, Emma knew that waiting was worth it.

Tom and Mia like to play pretend. They have a big box that they use as a kayak. They put on hats and jackets and paddle with sticks. They pretend they are on a river and see many things. "Look, Mia, a fish!" Tom says and points to a picture on the wall. "Wow, a big fish!" Mia says and pretends to catch it with her stick. They laugh and paddle some more. They pretend they see a bridge, a bird, and a boat. They have fun and make noises. "Beep beep!" the boat says. "Tweet tweet!" the bird says. "Hello!" the bridge says. Tom and Mia wave and say hello back. They are good friends and like to play pretend. They are happy in their kayak.

Once there was a little girl who was only three years old. She loved to dream. Every night, she would dream about magical things like ponies and fairies. She was very happy. One day, she went to take a bath. Her mom poured the shampoo into her hands, and she started to get worried. She looked at the bottle of shampoo and felt like it was too big and too slippery. She didn't know what to do. Suddenly, her mom smiled at her and said, "Don't worry. I'll help you." Her mom then showed her how to use the shampoo. Together they put the shampoo on her hair and made bubbles. The little girl was so happy that she was no longer worried. She enjoyed the feeling and realized that the shampoo made her hair nice and soft. Finally, she was ready to finish off her bath and get ready for bed so she could dream again.

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play with toys in the shower. They have a big bucket of water and some cups and spoons. They splash and laugh and make bubbles. One day, Ben finds a sharp thing in the shower. It is shiny and has a pointy end. He thinks it is a toy. He picks it up and shows it to Sam. "Look, Sam, I found a new toy!" Ben says. Sam looks at the sharp thing. He knows it is not a toy. It is a razor. It is for shaving hair. He sees it on the shelf sometimes when dad is in the shower. "Ben, that is not a toy!" Sam says. "That is a razor. It is very sharp. It can hurt you. Put it down now!" But Ben does not listen. He likes the shiny thing. He wants to play with it. He pretends it is a sword. He swings it in the air. He says, "I am a knight. I will fight the dragon!" Sam tries to stop him. He reaches for the razor. He says, "Ben, stop! Give me the razor. It is dangerous!" But Ben does not stop. He moves the razor too fast. He does not see Sam's hand. He cuts Sam's finger with the razor. Sam screams. Blood comes out of his finger. It hurts a lot. Ben drops the razor. He sees the blood. He sees Sam's face. He is scared. He says, "Sam, I'm sorry! I didn't mean to hurt you!" But it is too late. Sam is crying. He runs out of the shower. He calls for mom. Mom comes and sees what happened. She is angry and sad. She wraps Sam's finger with a bandage. She takes him to the doctor. She says, "Ben, you are in big trouble. You should never play with sharp things. You could have killed your brother!" Ben is alone in the shower. He feels bad. He wishes he listened to Sam. He wishes he did not play with the razor. He wishes he could say sorry to Sam. He wishes he could make it better. But he can't. He made a big mistake. He has no one to play with. He is not a knight. He is a bad brother.
