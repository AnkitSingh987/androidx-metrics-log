Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Restless Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a restless little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his leather ball. He would run and jump all day long. One day, Spot was playing with his ball, and something unexpected happened. Spot threw the ball really far, and it vanished! Spot looked everywhere for his ball but could not find it. He was very sad and did not know what to do. He missed his ball so much and wanted to play with it again. Just then, a big bird flew down from the sky with Spot's ball in its beak. The bird had found the ball and brought it back to Spot. Spot was so happy and could not believe it! He thanked the bird and continued to play with his leather ball. From that day on, Spot and the bird became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy was a very tough bird. He had strong wings and loved to soar high in the sky. One day, Billy flew over a big mall. He saw many kids playing and having fun. Billy wanted to play with the kids, but he was too high up in the sky. So, he decided to fly down and join them. When he got close, the kids saw him and started to laugh. They said, "Billy, you are a bird. You cannot play with us." Billy felt sad, but he did not give up. He knew he was tough and could do anything he set his mind to. So, he tried to play with the kids again. This time, he showed them how high he could soar. The kids were amazed and started to cheer for Billy. In the end, the kids learned that being tough and never giving up is important. They all became friends with Billy and played together every day. The moral of the story is that no matter who you are or where you come from, if you are tough and never give up, you can achieve anything.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big factory. The factory made toys for kids to play with. Every day, the workers would raise a flag to show they were open. One day, a clumsy worker named Tom came to the factory. Tom was not good at his job. He would drop things and make a mess. The other workers did not like Tom because he made their work harder. One day, Tom was raising the flag, but he tripped and fell. The flagpole broke and the factory had to close. The kids in the town were sad because they could not get their toys anymore. Tom felt very bad and wished he had been more careful.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they go to the park with their moms. There are many things to do in the park. They see swings, slides, sand, and flowers. Tom and Anna run to the swings. They take turns to push each other. They have fun. They laugh and shout. But then, Tom sees a big red ball on the grass. He wants the ball. He runs to the ball and picks it up. "Look, Anna, I found a ball!" Tom says. "Let's play with it!" Anna follows Tom. She sees the ball. But she also sees a boy sitting on a bench. The boy is crying. He has no ball. "Tom, that's not your ball," Anna says. "That's the boy's ball. He lost it. He is sad. Give it back to him." Tom shakes his head. He likes the ball. He does not want to give it back. He refuses. "No, Anna, it's my ball now. I found it. It's mine. Go away!" Tom says. He holds the ball tight. Anna is angry. She does not like Tom's words. She does not like Tom's face. She thinks Tom is mean. She is ashamed of Tom. "Tom, you are wrong. You are bad. You are not my friend. I don't want to play with you," Anna says. She turns away. She walks to the boy. She says sorry to the boy. She asks the boy to play with her. The boy smiles. He says yes. He thanks Anna. He takes his ball. He and Anna play together. They have fun. They laugh and shout. Tom is alone. He has the ball. But he has no friend. He is not happy. He is not fun. He is quiet and sad. He wishes he gave the ball back. He wishes he was nice. He wishes he was Anna's friend. He is ashamed of himself.
