Category: Beginner
Title: The Lovely Farm and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a lovely farm. On this farm, there were many animals and plants. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm. Tim liked the farm a lot. Tim saw a big squash in the garden. He wanted to take the squash home. So, he asked his mom, "Can I take the squash home, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can." Tim was very happy. But when Tim tried to pick up the squash, it started to talk! The squash said, "Please don't take me away from my home!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that squash could talk. Tim decided to leave the squash at the farm. He went home and told his mom about the talking squash. They laughed and had a fun day at the lovely farm.

Once upon a time, there was a pink cat. The cat had a gift. The gift was in a big, pretty box. The cat was very happy. She wanted to show her friend, the dog. The cat went to the dog's house. She said, "Look at my gift!" The dog was so happy for the cat. They both wanted to know what was inside the box. They question, "What could it be?" Just then, the box started to move! The cat and dog were very surprised. They opened the box together. Inside the box, there was a cute bunny! The bunny said, "Hi! I am your new friend!" The cat, the dog, and the bunny played all day. They were so happy to be friends. The pink cat's gift was the best gift ever. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a curious little girl named Mia saw a box. The box had a pretty print on it. She wanted to peek inside the box. But her mom said, "No, Mia. Do not peek. It is not for you." Mia was sad. She wanted to see what was in the box. When her mom went away, Mia thought, "I will just take one little peek." So, she opened the box just a bit and looked inside. Inside the box, there was a big, mean cat. The cat jumped out and scratched Mia's arm. Mia cried out, "Ouch! That hurt!" Her mom came back and saw what happened. She said, "I told you not to peek, Mia. Now you are hurt." Mia felt bad. She knew she should have listened to her mom. Now, she had a hurt arm and a sad heart. She learned that it is important to listen and not be too curious.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and trucks. One day, Tim found a deep hole in his yard. He was scared that his toys would fall in the hole and get lost, so he asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me? There is a deep hole in the yard and I don't want my toys to fall in," Tim said. His mom came outside and saw the hole. She thought it was a good idea to cover it up. Together, Tim and his mom found some big rocks and put them over the hole. Now, Tim could play without worrying about his toys falling in. To thank his mom, Tim decided to make her a special card. He asked his dad to help him print a picture of a heart on the card. "Here, Dad. Can you help me print a heart for Mom?" Tim asked. His dad helped him print the heart and Tim wrote "I love you, Mom" on the card. When Tim gave the card to his mom, she was so happy that she hugged him tight. From that day on, Tim knew that when he needed help, his mom and dad would always be there for him. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. She lived in a small house near a big river. Sue had a big heart and loved to help people. One day, she saw dark clouds in the sky. She knew that when dark clouds came, it meant rain was coming too. She thought the rain might cause a flood. Sue wanted to warn her friends about the flood. She decided to tear a piece of paper and write a note to her friends. She wrote, "Watch out for the flood!" Sue gave the note to her friends, and they all read it. They were thankful for Sue's warning. The rain came, and it rained a lot. The river started to flood, just like Sue thought it would. But because of her note, all her friends were ready. They moved their things to higher places and stayed safe. When the flood was over, they all thanked Sue for her help. Sue was happy she could help her friends, and they all lived happily ever after.
