Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Garden

One day, a little girl named Mia found a small plant in her garden. The plant was special because it was very flexible. Mia liked to watch the plant dance in the wind. Mia took care of the plant every day. She gave it water and made sure it had enough sun. One day, her friend Tom came over to play. Tom saw the flexible plant and wanted to touch it. Mia said, "Be careful, Tom. The plant is very special to me." Tom touched the plant and accidentally broke it. Mia was sad, but Tom had an idea. He said, "I'm sorry, Mia. Let's replace the plant with a new one." So, they went to the store and picked out a new flexible plant together. Mia and Tom planted the new plant in the garden. They took care of it together and watched it grow. The new plant was just as flexible and danced in the wind like the first one. Mia and Tom were happy, and they promised to always take care of their special plant together.

One day, a strong boy named Tim set out to go to the market. He wanted to buy some fruit for his mom. The sun was high and bright in the sky. Tim was very happy. On his way, he met a dog. The dog said, "Woof! I see you are going to the market. Can you buy me a bone?" Tim said, "Yes, I can do that!" He knew his mom would be happy to help the dog, too. At the market, Tim bought apples for his mom and a bone for the dog. He went back to the dog and gave him the bone. The dog was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim felt good that he could help his mom and the dog. The sun set, and Tim went home with a big smile on his face.

Tim and Mia liked to play on the sand. They made castles and tunnels and bridges. They had a big bucket and a small spade. They had fun. But one day, the tide came in. The water got closer and closer to their sand. Tim and Mia did not see it. They were busy with their castles. The water touched their bucket and spade. The water washed them away. Tim and Mia saw their bucket and spade go. They were scared. They ran to get them. But the water was too fast. The water was too deep. They could not reach them. Tim and Mia cried. They wanted their bucket and spade. They wanted to play on the sand. They did not like the water. They did not like the tide. A man saw them. He was kind. He had a big net. He tried to catch their bucket and spade. He threw the net into the water. He pulled the net back. He got their bucket and spade. Tim and Mia were happy. They thanked the man. They hugged their bucket and spade. They went back to the sand. They played with their castles. They watched the tide go out. They were not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round ball. The ball loved to play with his friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue was always careful when she played with the round ball. She did not want the ball to get hurt. One sunny day, Sue and the round ball went to the park. They played and laughed all day. The round ball liked to roll around in the grass. Sue would insist that the ball be careful near the road. She did not want the ball to roll too far away. As the sun went down, it was time for Sue and the round ball to go home. They said goodbye to the park and walked back home. Sue held the round ball tight in her arms, being careful not to drop it. They were both happy and tired after a fun day of play.

One day, a smart cat named Tom went for a walk. He wanted to see a nice view from the top of a hill. On his way, he met a friendly dog named Max. "Hi, Tom!" Max said with a wave. "Do you want to come with me to see the view?" Tom and Max walked up the hill together. They saw many pretty things like flowers, trees, and birds. When they reached the top, they looked out at the view. They saw a big, blue lake and lots of green grass. "Wow, this is so pretty!" said Tom. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big, colorful bird flew down and landed next to them. The bird waved its wings and said, "Hello, friends! I like the view too. Do you want to fly with me?" Tom and Max looked at each other and smiled. They never thought they would fly with a bird, but today they would!
