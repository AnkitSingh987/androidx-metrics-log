Category: Beginner
Title: Lila the Girl and the Dog

Once there was a little girl named Lila. She liked to play with her toys and draw pictures. One day, Lila was walking in the park when she found an exciting looking mug. She picked it up and said to herself, "I'm going to take this home with me!" Lila began walking with the mug in her hand. All of a sudden, a large dog ran up to her and started barking. It looked like it wanted the mug. Lila pulled it close and said, "No, this is mine!" But the dog kept barking. Just then, a man nearby ran over and grabbed the dog's leash. He said, "It's okay, little girl. I'm sorry that my dog scared you. He loves mugs, but this one has to stay with you, okay?" Lila nodded and smiled. She thanked the man and kept walking. The man watched as Lila pulled the little mug close to her and she smiled. He knew that the mug was finally safe.

Lily was a bird who liked to fly high in the sky. She had big and strong wings that helped her soar and glide. She loved to see the sun, the clouds, and the trees from above. She felt happy and free. One day, Lily saw a shiny thing on the ground. She wanted to see what it was, so she flew down to get it. But she did not see the cat that was hiding behind a bush. The cat jumped out and tried to catch Lily. Lily was scared and tried to fly away, but the cat was fast and bit her wing. Lily felt a lot of pain and fell to the ground. Her wing was broken and she could not fly. Lily cried and cried. She did not know what to do. She was far from her nest and her friends. She felt alone and sad. She wished she had not gone after the shiny thing. She wished she had stayed in the sky. Then, she heard a voice. It was a boy who saw Lily on the ground. He came over and picked her up gently. He saw her broken wing and felt sorry for her. He said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you. I will take you to the vet and he will fix your wing. Then you can fly again." Lily felt a little bit of hope. She thanked the boy and let him carry her. The boy was kind and careful. He took Lily to the vet and waited for her. The vet gave Lily some medicine and wrapped her wing. He said, "You will be fine, little bird. But you have to rest and heal. You have to be patient and brave." Lily nodded and smiled. She was grateful to the boy and the vet. She learned a lesson. She learned that flying high is fun, but she has to be careful and smart. She learned that shiny things are not always good, and that sometimes they can be dangerous. She learned that kindness and help can come from unexpected places, and that she should always say thank you. She learned that stand is not only what you do with your feet, but also what you do with your heart.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big wheel in the park. Max wanted to play with the wheel, but it was up high. He jumped and jumped, but he could not reach it. Max saw a tall tree near the wheel. He thought, "I can peek at the wheel from the tree!" Max climbed the tree and saw a little bird on a branch. The bird looked sad. "Why are you sad?" Max asked the bird. The bird said, "I am cold. The wind is bitter today." Max wanted to help the bird feel warm. He had an idea. "Come sit on my back. My fur will keep you warm." The bird flew down and sat on Max's back. They both looked at the wheel together. They were happy to be warm and have a new friend.

Sara and Tom were playing on the beach. They liked to find shells and make piles of them. Sara saw a big orange shell that shone in the sun. She ran to catch it, but Tom saw it too. He ran faster and grabbed the shell before Sara. "Ha ha, I got it!" Tom said. He held the shell up and smiled. "It is mine now. It is the best shell ever." Sara felt sad and angry. She wanted the shell too. She tried to take it from Tom, but he pushed her away. "No, go away! It is mine, mine, mine!" he shouted. They started to fight over the shell. They pulled and tugged and yelled. They did not see the big wave that was coming. The wave crashed over them and knocked them down. The shell flew out of Tom's hand and into the water. It was gone. Sara and Tom cried. They had lost the shell and they were wet and cold. They wished they had not fought. They wished they had shared the shell and played nicely. A kind lady saw them and came to help. She gave them a towel and a hug. She said, "I saw what happened. I am sorry you lost your shell. But you know, shells are not as important as friends. Friends are more precious than anything. You should not fight over things. You should be kind and share. That is how you keep your friends happy and yourself happy too." Sara and Tom nodded. They felt sorry for what they had done. They said sorry to each other and hugged. They decided to be friends again and share their shells. They learned their lesson. They thanked the lady and went back to play. They found more shells, but they did not fight over them. They gave some to each other and made a beautiful pile together. They were happy.
