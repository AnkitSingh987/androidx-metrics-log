Category: Beginner
Title: The Pink Wagon and the Girl

Once upon a time there was a pink wagon. It belonged to a little girl. She promised the wagon would be hers forever. Every day she would take the wagon outside to play. Then one day a big wind picked up the wagon and blew it away. The girl was very sad. She tried to look for the wagon, but it was gone. The girl was so sad she made a promise never to trust a thing again. She was so sad she couldn't even find it in her heart to keep the promise. The pink wagon was gone!

Once upon a time, there was a playful fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day long in the big pond. One day, while swimming, Fin met another fish named Bubbles. "Hi, I am Fin! I like to swim. Do you want to swim with me?" asked Fin. Bubbles nodded and said, "Yes, I like to swim too! Let's swim together." Fin and Bubbles swam in a new style. They swam in circles, zig-zags, and even made big splashes. They had so much fun and became best friends. From that day on, they always swam together, laughing and playing in their big pond.

---

Sara was a big girl who liked to teach her little brother Ben how to do things. She taught him how to tie his shoes, how to brush his teeth, and how to say please and thank you. Ben loved his sister and listened to her very well. One day, Sara had a new idea. She wanted to teach Ben how to hug. She said, "Ben, do you know what a hug is?" Ben shook his head. He did not know. Sara said, "A hug is when you put your arms around someone and squeeze them gently. It means you love them and you are happy to see them. Do you want to try?" Ben nodded. He wanted to try. Sara showed him how to hug her. She put her arms around his neck and squeezed him gently. She said, "This is a hug. It feels nice, doesn't it?" Ben smiled. He liked the hug. He said, "Yes, it feels nice. Can I hug you back?" Sara said, "Of course you can. That's what hugs are for." Ben put his arms around Sara's waist and squeezed her gently. He said, "This is a hug. I love you, Sara. You are the best sister ever." Sara felt warm and happy. She said, "I love you too, Ben. You are the best brother ever." The next day, Sara and Ben went to the park with their mom. They saw their friends playing on the swings and slides. Sara said, "Ben, do you want to hug our friends?" Ben said, "Yes, I want to hug our friends. Hugs are fun." Sara said, "Okay, let's go and hug them. But remember, some people may not like hugs. You have to ask them first if they want a hug. And if they say no, you have to respect that. Okay?" Ben said, "Okay, I understand." He followed Sara to their friends. Sara asked each friend if they wanted a hug. Some said yes, and some said no. Sara hugged the ones who said yes, and waved to the ones who said no. Ben did the same. He asked each friend if they wanted a hug. Most of they said yes, and he hugged them happily. But one of they said no. He was a boy named Max. He said, "No, I don't want a hug. Hugs are for babies. I'm a big boy. I don't need hugs." Ben felt sad. He liked Max. He wanted to hug him. He said, "But hugs are nice. They mean you love someone and you are happy to see them. Don't you love me and are happy to see me?" Max said, "No, I don't. You are silly and unusual. You hug everyone. That's weird. Go away." Ben felt hurt. He did not understand why Max was so mean. He ran to Sara and cried. Sara saw what happened and hugged him. She said, "Don't worry, Ben. Max is wrong. Hugs are not for babies. Hugs are for everyone who wants them. You are not silly and unusual. You are kind and sweet. You hug everyone because you have a big heart. That's wonderful. I'm proud of you." Ben felt better. He hugged Sara back. He said, "Thank you, Sara. You are kind and sweet too. You teach me so many things. You are a great sister." Sara said, "You're welcome, Ben. You are a great brother. And you know what? I have a surprise for you. Come with me." Sara took Ben to a bench where their mom was sitting. She said, "Mom, Ben has learned how to hug. He hugs everyone who wants a hug. He has a big heart. Can you give him a hug too?" Mom smiled. She said, "Of course I can. I'm so proud of both of you. Come here, Ben. Let me give you a big hug." Mom opened her arms and Ben ran into them. She hugged him tightly and kissed his cheek. She said, "I love you, Ben. You are a wonderful son. And I love you, Sara. You are a wonderful daughter. You teach Ben so well. You are both amazing." Sara and Ben hugged their mom back. They said, "We love you too, Mom. You are the best mom ever." They all hugged each other and laughed. They felt happy and loved. They did not care what Max or anyone else thought. They knew that hugs were not unusual. They were the best thing ever.
