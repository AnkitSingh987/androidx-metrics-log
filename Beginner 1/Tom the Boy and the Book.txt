Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Book

One day, a boy named Tom found a thick book. He opened it and saw a picture of a needle. Tom showed the book to his mom. "Mom, what is this?" he asked, pointing at the needle. His mom frowned and said, "That is a needle, Tom. We use it to fix clothes." Tom wanted to help his mom fix clothes. He took the needle and tried to use it. But, the needle was not real! It was just a picture. Tom was sad and his mom saw this. She smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tom. I have a real needle you can use." Tom was happy and started to fix clothes with his mom. They fixed a big hole in a sock. But then, something unexpected happened. The sock came to life and said, "Thank you, Tom and Mom!" Tom and his mom were very surprised, but they laughed and played with the talking sock.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very restless. He liked to run and play all day long. One day, while playing in the park, he saw a girl who needed help. Her name was Sue. Sue's toy was stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help her. Tim said, "I can help you get your toy." He used his hand to reach up high and get the toy from the tree. Sue was very happy to have her toy back. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" After that day, Tim and Sue became good friends. They played together in the park every day. Tim was not as restless as before because he had a friend to play with. They always remembered to say "thank you" when they helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an amazing flag. It was red and blue with stars on it. He loved to wave his flag up and down. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's flag and said, "Wow, I believe your flag is so nice!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue! I like your toy car." They played together all day, waving the flag and driving the toy car. At the end of the day, Tim and Sue were very happy. They had so much fun playing with the flag and the toy car. They said goodbye to each other and went home to their mom and dad. Tim and Sue couldn't wait to play together again the next day.

Sara liked to play in the old house. She did not care that it was dark and dirty. She liked to find new things and make them her toys. She had a doll, a hat, a spoon and a ball. She was happy. One day, she saw a shiny thing in the corner. It was a ring. It was gold and had a red stone. Sara wanted it. She ran to the corner and reached for the ring. But the ring was on a cobweb. A big spider lived on the cobweb. The spider did not like Sara. It was busy making more cobwebs. When Sara touched the ring, the spider felt it. It was angry. It ran to Sara and bit her hand. Sara screamed. The ring twisted on her finger. It was too small. It hurt. Sara tried to pull it off, but it was stuck. She cried. Sara's mom heard her scream. She ran to the old house. She saw Sara in the corner. She saw the spider, the cobweb and the ring. She was scared. She grabbed Sara and ran out of the house. She took her to the doctor. The doctor looked at Sara's hand. He said the spider was poisonous. He said the ring was cursed. He said he could not help Sara. He said she would be sick and die. Sara's mom was sad. She hugged Sara and cried. Sara was sad too. She wished she never saw the ring. She wished she played with her doll, her hat, her spoon and her ball. She wished she was happy.

One day, a funny bus went down the road. The bus was big and yellow. The bus driver was nice. He saw a flower that was about to bloom. He stopped the bus and said, "Look! A pretty flower is about to bloom!" A little girl on the bus looked out the window. She saw the flower and smiled. She said, "Wow! I want to see it bloom!" All the kids on the bus wanted to see the flower bloom too. They got off the bus and stood around the flower. As they watched, something unexpected happened. The flower started to grow bigger and bigger. It was not a flower at all! It was a big, funny balloon! The kids laughed and played with the balloon. They had a great time. The bus driver said, "Time to go home!" The kids got back on the bus, happy and tired. They had a fun day with the funny balloon. The bus drove away, and the kids waved goodbye to the spot where the balloon had been.
