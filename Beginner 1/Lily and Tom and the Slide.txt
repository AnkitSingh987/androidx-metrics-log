Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Slide

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big slide and wanted to try it. Lily went first. She climbed up the steps and slid down fast. She laughed and said, "That was fun!" Tom followed her. He was not afraid of the slide. He wanted to show Lily how brave he was. He climbed up the steps and slid down faster. He shouted, "Look at me!" But Tom slid too fast. He did not stop at the end of the slide. He flew off and landed on the grass. He hurt his arm and cried, "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily ran to him. She saw his arm was red and swollen. She said, "Tom, you need help. I will save you." She helped him stand up and hugged him. She said, "Don't worry, Tom. We will find a grown-up. They will make your arm better." They walked to the bench where their mom was sitting. She saw Tom's arm and said, "Oh, Tom, what happened?" Lily told her what happened. She said, "I saved Tom. He slid too fast and hurt his arm." Mom said, "You are a good friend, Lily. You did the right thing. Tom, you are lucky to have Lily. She has a big heart and a strong muscle." Tom said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend, too. You have a kind heart and an interesting muscle." Mom smiled and said, "You both have good hearts and interesting muscles. But next time, be careful on the slide. It is fun, but it can be dangerous, too." Lily and Tom nodded. They learned their lesson. They said, "We will be careful, Mom. We love you." Mom hugged them and said, "I love you, too. Now let's go to the doctor and fix your arm, Tom." They went to the doctor and Tom's arm was fixed. He felt better and said, "Sorry, Lily. I was silly. I wanted to show you how brave I was. But I was not brave. I was foolish." Lily said, "It's okay, Tom. I forgive you. You are brave, but you don't have to show off. You are my friend, and I like you for who you are." Tom said, "Thank you, Lily. You are my friend, too. And I like you for who you are." They hugged and smiled. They were happy and grateful. They had good hearts and interesting muscles. And they had each other.

One day, a big, hairy dog went for a walk. The sun was very hot, so the dog looked for some shade. He saw a tree and went under it to rest. While he was resting, a small cat came to sit in the shade too. The dog said, "Hi, little cat. It's hot today. Do you want to sit in the shade with me?" The cat said, "Yes, thank you, hairy dog. I am very hot too." They sat in the shade together. But soon, the sun moved and the shade went away. They were hot again. The dog and the cat looked for more shade, but they could not find any. They were sad and hot. In the end, they went home, still feeling very hot and tired.

Once upon a time, there was a team of animals who liked to play games. They were not normal animals, they liked to wear clothes! There was a big bear, a small cat, and a fast dog. They were very good friends. One sunny day, the animals wanted to play a game. The bear said, "Let's play hide and seek!" The cat and the dog agreed. The bear wore a big hat to cover his eyes while he counted. The cat and the dog ran to hide. The bear finished counting and went to find his friends. He found the cat hiding behind a tree. "I see you!" the bear said. The cat laughed and joined the bear to find the dog. They found the dog hiding under a bush. "We found you!" the cat said. The dog laughed and they all went to play again. The team of animals played games all day. They were very happy. When the sun went down, they went home to rest. They knew they would have more fun together the next day.
