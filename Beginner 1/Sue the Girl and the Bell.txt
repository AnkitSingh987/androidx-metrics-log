Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bell

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to design a pretty bell. She had paper, colors, and a big smile on her face. Sue was very alert and ready to make her bell the best one ever. Sue drew a round bell with a long ribbon on top. She used her favorite colors: red, blue, and yellow. The bell looked so nice that her mom put it on the wall for everyone to see. Every time Sue walked by her bell, she felt proud and happy. The bell made a soft sound when the wind blew. Sue's friends liked her bell too. They all wanted to design their own bells to make their homes happy and full of pretty sounds.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a sister named Rose. Both of them loved to play near the water. One sunny day, they decided to sail their toy boats. Lily and Rose went to the pond with their boats. Lily's boat was red, and Rose's boat was blue. They put their boats in the water and watched them sail. The wind blew, and the boats moved fast. Rose said, "My boat is faster!" Lily was stubborn and said, "No, my boat is faster!" They laughed and played with their boats all day long. They had so much fun sailing together. In the end, they didn't care which boat was faster. They loved playing with their boats and being sisters. They went home, happy and tired, ready to play again another day.

One day, there was a little boy. He was 3 years old. He wanted to meet a new friend, so he left his house. He walked and walked until he saw a girl. He thought she looked nice, so he went up to her. He said "Hi! What's your name?" The girl said nothing. She just looked away. The little boy thought she was being silly and stupid. He was sad. He said "Do you want to be my friend?" The girl smiled, and said "Yes!". From then on, they were good friends. They liked to play together. The little boy was happy that he had met a new friend. He was glad he didn't give up and he left his house. He was glad that silly and stupid thing he did worked out in the end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a boy named Tim had a new toy. He loved his new toy. He played with it all day. One day, his mom asked him to eat his oats. Tim did not want to eat his oats. He refused to eat them. His mom was sad. The next day, Tim's toy was gone. He looked everywhere for his toy, but he could not find it. Tim cried and cried. His mom told him to eat his oats, and maybe his toy would come back. Tim still refused to eat his oats. That night, Tim heard a noise in his room. He saw his toy! The toy was eating his oats. Tim was shocked. He decided to eat his oats too. From that day on, Tim ate his oats and played with his new toy. They were both happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn about history. He had many old things in his room. One day, Tim found a broken toy car. He wanted to fix it. Tim asked his mom what to do. His mom said, "I recommend we go to the toy fixer." So, they went to the toy fixer. The toy fixer said he could fix the broken toy car. Tim was happy. The next day, Tim went to get his toy car. It was fixed! The toy fixer told Tim the toy car was very old and had a big history. Tim was very proud. He took the toy car home and played with it all day.

Lily liked to whistle. She whistled when she played, when she ate, and when she slept. She whistled all the time. She thought it was fun and pretty. But not everyone liked Lily's whistling. One day, she was walking with her mom to the park. She whistled a happy song. A lady was walking in front of them. She turned around and looked at Lily. She had a mean face. "Stop that noise!" the lady said. "You are hurting my ears! You are a bad girl!" Lily was scared and sad. She stopped whistling. She hugged her mom. Her mom was angry at the lady. "That was not nice!" her mom said. "Lily is not a bad girl. She is a good girl. She likes to whistle. Whistling is not a noise. It is a sound. A beautiful sound." The lady did not listen. She walked away. She had a broken heart. She did not know how to whistle. She did not know how to be happy. Lily and her mom went to the park. Lily saw her friends. They were playing and whistling. Lily joined them. She whistled again. She was happy. She had a big heart. She knew how to whistle. She knew how to be happy.
