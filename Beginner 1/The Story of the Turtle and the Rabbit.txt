Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Turtle and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a big hall where all the animals would meet. In this hall, they would talk, play, and have fun. One day, a slow turtle named Timmy went to the hall to meet his friends. When Timmy got to the hall, he saw his best friend, a fast rabbit named Ricky. Ricky said, "Hi, Timmy! Let's play a game!" Timmy thought for a moment and said, "I recommend we play hide and seek. I like that game a lot!" So, all the animals in the hall started to play hide and seek. Timmy, being slow, had a hard time finding his friends. But Ricky always helped Timmy find the other animals. They all had a great time playing together in the big hall.

Tim and Anna are friends. They like to play with trucks in the sand. Tim has a big red truck. Anna has a small blue truck. They make roads and bridges with the sand. One day, Tim says to Anna, "I like your truck. Can I play with it?" Anna says, "Yes, you can. But you have to share your truck with me." Tim says, "OK, I will share. You are a good friend." They play with their trucks for a long time. They have fun. Then, Tim says to Anna, "You are not only a good friend. You are also pretty. Do you want to marry me?" Anna says, "What is marry?" Tim says, "Marry is when you love someone and you stay with them forever. And you wear a ring and a dress and a suit. And you have a cake and a party." Anna thinks for a moment. She says, "That sounds nice. But I don't have a ring or a dress or a suit. And I don't have a cake or a party." Tim says, "That's OK. We can use our trucks as rings. And we can wear our hats and coats. And we can have cookies and juice as cake and party." Anna says, "OK, then. Let's marry." They put their trucks on their fingers. They put on their hats and coats. They find some cookies and juice. They say, "I love you" to each other. They hug and kiss. They are happy. They are married.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Amy and her big dog, Max. Amy and Max were best friends. They loved to play in the yard and have fun. One day, Amy found a harmless bug in the yard. She picked it up and showed it to Max. "Look, Max! I found a bug!" Max looked at the bug and frowned. He didn't like bugs very much. Amy knew the truth about the bug. It was a nice bug that wouldn't hurt anyone. She told Max, "Don't worry, this bug is our friend. It won't hurt us." Max felt better and they played with the bug all day.

Sara and Ben are playing with dough in the kitchen. They have many colorful doughs to make shapes. Sara likes to make flowers and Ben likes to make cars. "Look, I made a red flower!" Sara says, showing her dough to Ben. "It is so pretty and soft. How do you feel your dough?" Ben feels his dough and says, "My dough is blue and hard. I made a car with wheels. It is so fast and cool. Do you want to see?" Sara nods and Ben shows her his car. They both smile and admire each other's doughs. Then, they hear a voice from the living room. It is their mom. "Kids, it is time to clean up and have a snack. Put your doughs away and wash your hands." Sara and Ben say, "Okay, mom!" They put their doughs in a box and go to the sink. They wash their hands with soap and water. Then, they go to the living room and see a plate of cookies and a glass of milk on the table. "Yum, cookies!" Sara says, grabbing one. "Thank you, mom!" "You're welcome, sweetie. How was your playtime?" Mom asks. "It was fun! We made colorful shapes with dough. I made a flower and Ben made a car." Sara says. "That sounds wonderful. And how do you feel now?" Mom asks. Sara thinks for a moment and says, "I feel happy and hungry!" Mom laughs and hugs Sara. Ben joins them and says, "Me too!" They all enjoy their snack and talk about their day. They feel happy and loved.
