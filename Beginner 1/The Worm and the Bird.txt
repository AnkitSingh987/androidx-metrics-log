Category: Beginner
Title: The Worm and the Bird

One day, a little worm went out to play. The worm was thick and strong. He liked to dig in the soft dirt. He had many friends who lived in the dirt too. They would play all day and have fun. One morning, the worm heard a loud noise. It was a big bird. The bird was hungry and wanted to eat the worm. The worm was scared and he started to cry. He did not want to be eaten by the big bird. The worm remembered that he was thick and strong. He had an idea. He quickly dug a hole in the dirt and hid inside. The big bird could not find him. The worm was safe. His friends came to help him and they all played together again. The worm was happy and did not cry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little train. The train was sad. It wanted to join the other trains on the rail. But it was too small. One day, the little train saw a big train. The big train was going very fast on the rail. The little train wanted to join, but it was scared. It thought the big train would not like it. But the big train saw the little train and stopped. "Do you want to join me?" asked the big train. The little train was very happy. It joined the big train on the rail. They went fast together. The little train was not sad anymore.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play games together. Today they play hide and seek. Tom is the seeker. He counts to ten. Mia runs and hides behind a big tree. Tom opens his eyes. He looks for Mia. He does not see her. He walks around the yard. He says, "Where are you, Mia? I will find you!" He hears a giggle. He follows the sound. He sees the big tree. He runs to the tree. He finds Mia. "Got you!" he says. He is happy. He has a wide smile. Mia is sad. She has a frown. She says, "You are too good, Tom. You always find me." Tom says, "Don't be sad, Mia. You are good too. Let's play again. You can be the seeker this time." Mia nods. She is happy again. She says, "OK, Tom. You hide. I will count." Tom runs and hides behind a small bush. Mia counts to ten. She opens her eyes. She looks for Tom. She does not see him. She walks around the yard. She says, "Where are you, Tom? I will find you!" She hears a song. She follows the sound. She sees the small bush. She hears Tom sing. "Twinkle, twinkle, little star, how I wonder what you are. Up above the world so high, like a diamond in the sky." Tom sings. He is happy. He likes to sing. He does not know Mia is near. Mia smiles. She has a plan. She sneaks to the bush. She jumps out. She says, "Got you!" Tom is surprised. He stops singing. He says, "Wow, Mia. You found me. You are very smart. You have a victory." Mia is happy. She has a wide smile. She says, "Thank you, Tom. You are very nice. You have a good voice. You sing well." Tom is happy. He says, "Thank you, Mia. You are very kind. You seek well." They hug. They are friends. They like to play games together. They like to sing together. They are happy.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play in the wood near their home. They had a secret spot where they built a fort with sticks and leaves. One day, they went to the wood after lunch, but they saw something strange. There was a big hole in the ground, and a lot of dirt and rocks around it. "Look, Ben, what is that?" Lily asked, pointing at the hole. "I don't know, Lily, maybe someone dug it," Ben said, curious. "Let's go closer and see," Lily said, feeling restless. They walked towards the hole, but they did not notice a sign that said "Danger! Keep out!" They also did not hear a loud noise that came from the other side of the wood. When they reached the hole, they looked inside and saw a shiny metal thing. It looked like a big pot with wires and buttons. They wondered what it was and what it did. "Maybe it's a toy," Ben said, reaching out his hand to touch it. "Wait, Ben, don't touch it, it might be hot or sharp," Lily said, pulling him back. But it was too late. As soon as Ben touched the metal thing, it made a loud beep and a flash of light. Then, a voice came from the thing, saying "Self-destruct sequence activated. Countdown: 10, 9, 8..." Lily and Ben screamed and ran away from the hole, hoping to get out of the wood before the thing exploded. They did not know that the metal thing was a bomb that someone had hidden in the wood, and that the noise they did not hear was the sound of the police coming to stop the bad person who had planted it. They also did not know that the sign they did not see was there to warn them of the danger. They only knew that they had to run fast and get back to their home, where they would be safe.
