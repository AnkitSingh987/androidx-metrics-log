Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to kick a ball in his yard. One day, while playing, he saw a small, sticky thing on the ground. It was gum. "Yuck!" said Tim. "Who put gum here?" He tried to kick the gum away, but it was very persistent. It stuck to his shoe and would not let go. Tim's mom saw him trying to kick the gum off. She said, "Oh no, Tim! Let me help you." Tim's mom got a stick and scraped the gum off his shoe. Tim was happy and went back to kicking his ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a drum that he liked to play all day. One day, he set the drum on the floor and started to play. The sound was loud, but Tim felt calm when he played the drum. A cat named Max lived in the same house as Tim. Max did not like the sound of the drum. It was too loud for him. One day, Max said to Tim, "Please, can you stop playing the drum? It is too loud for me." Tim did not want to stop playing the drum. He liked the sound it made. So, he kept playing the drum all day. Max was very sad. He could not take the loud sound anymore. One day, Max ran away from home. He could not live in the house with the loud drum. Tim was sad when he found out that Max was gone. He wished he had listened to Max and stopped playing the drum. But it was too late, and Max never came back.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl names Sophia. Sophia's mommy mad her a beautiful bedroom, with lots of toys. There was one special toy that always made Sophia giggle and laugh - it was a little round mirror on the wall. One day, Sophia was jumping on the bed, a bit too hard. Suddenly, the bed shook, and the mirror broke into many pieces. Sophia's face dropped in shock. She had broken the mirror! Sophia's Mommy came in the room, and saw the broken mirror on the floor. She told Sophia, "That was dangerous, Sophia! We need to clean up the broken pieces of glass." She showed Sophia how to pick up the pieces of glass and put them in the bin. Sophia was a very good girl, and she listened to her Mommy. She all the pieces away carefully, and her Mommy gave her a big hug and said, "Good job, Sophia! Now let's go and get a new mirror!"

John was a very intelligent three-year-old boy. He always wondered what was on the calendar each day. One morning, he asked his mom what they were doing that day. "Today, we are going to the park," replied his mom. John was so excited and started to wonder what they would see at the park. He thought that maybe they would see some ducks. John and his mom went to the park and there were lots of ducks! John was thrilled to see them and started to chase them around the lake. His mom watched him with a smile on her face. John and his mom went home before it got too late and he laid in bed wonder what new things he would discover tomorrow.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They saw a chubby bird on the fence. It was brown and white and had a red hat. "Look, a bird!" Lily said. "Hello, bird!" Ben said. The bird looked at them and opened its mouth. It made a loud noise. It was a scream. The bird screamed and screamed. Lily and Ben were scared. They did not like the scream. They ran to the house. They wanted their mom. "Mom, mom, there is a bad bird!" Lily said. "Mom, mom, it screams and screams!" Ben said. Mom came out of the house. She saw the bird on the fence. She smiled. "That is not a bad bird, my loves. That is a post bird. It brings letters and cards. It screams to tell us it has something for us. Do you want to see?" Mom said. Lily and Ben nodded. They were curious. They followed Mom to the fence. The bird stopped screaming. It gave Mom a letter. Mom thanked the bird. The bird flew away. "Who is the letter from, Mom?" Lily asked. "It is from Grandma. She says she loves you and misses you. Do you want to write her back?" Mom asked. Lily and Ben smiled. They liked Grandma. They wanted to write her back. They went inside with Mom. They got some paper and crayons. They drew pictures and wrote their names. They gave the letter to Mom. "Can we give it to the post bird, Mom?" Ben asked. "Sure, we can. We just have to wait for it to come back. Maybe it will scream again. But don't be scared. It is a good scream. It means it has something for us or for someone else." Mom said. Lily and Ben nodded. They were not scared anymore. They liked the post bird. They hoped it would come back soon. They wanted to say hello and give it their letter. They waited in the garden. They played and laughed. They listened for the scream.
