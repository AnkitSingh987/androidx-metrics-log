Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Dog

Lily liked to play in the garden with her ball. She would kick it and catch it and throw it high in the air. One day, she threw it too high and it went over the fence. She could not see it anymore. She ran to the fence and tried to look over it. The fence was tall and made of wood. She saw a big hole in the fence. She thought she could go through the hole and get her ball back. She did not know that the hole was made by a dog that lived on the other side. She crawled through the hole and saw her ball. It was on the grass near a big tree. She was happy and ran to get it. But she did not see the dog. The dog saw her and barked. It was a big and loud dog. It scared Lily. Lily wanted to go back to the garden. She turned around and ran to the hole. But she was too fast and did not watch her feet. She slipped on a wet leaf and fell down. She hurt her knee and it bled. She cried. The dog came closer and sniffed her. It was not a mean dog. It was a friendly dog. It licked her face and wagged its tail. It wanted to play with her. Lily stopped crying and smiled. She petted the dog and gave it her ball. The dog was happy and played with the ball. Lily heard her mom calling her name. She looked at the hole and saw her mom's face. Her mom was worried and angry. She asked Lily what she was doing on the other side of the fence. She told her it was dangerous and she could have been hurt. Lily said she was sorry and explained what happened. She said she threw her ball over the fence and went to get it. She said she slipped and fell and met the dog. She said the dog was nice and helped her. Her mom was still angry but also relieved. She hugged Lily and said she was glad she was okay. She said she should never go through the hole again. She said she would talk to the dog's owner and get her ball back. She said she would clean her knee and put a band-aid on it. Lily said okay and thanked the dog. She said goodbye to the dog and crawled back through the hole. She held her mom's hand and walked to the house. She learned her lesson and never threw her ball over the fence again. She also made a new friend. The dog was happy too and waited for her to come back and play.

Tom and Sam were friends who liked to play in the sea. They saw a big whale who was jolly and kind. The whale let them ride on his back and splash in the water. Tom and Sam had a lot of fun with the whale. One day, Tom had an idea. He wanted to borrow the whale and take him home. He told Sam to help him. They found a big net and threw it over the whale. The whale was scared and sad. He tried to break free, but the net was too strong. Tom and Sam dragged the whale to the shore. They wanted to put him in a big truck and drive him away. But the whale was too heavy and too big. They could not lift him or move him. The whale could not breathe or swim. He started to cry and moan. Some people saw what Tom and Sam did. They were angry and shocked. They called the police and the animal helpers. They came and freed the whale from the net. They gave him water and medicine. They put him back in the sea. The whale was happy and grateful. He waved his tail and said goodbye. Tom and Sam were in trouble. The police and the people scolded them and punished them. They had to pay a lot of money and do a lot of work. They had to learn how to be kind and respectful to animals. They lost their friend, the whale. They were sorry and ashamed. They never borrowed anything again.
