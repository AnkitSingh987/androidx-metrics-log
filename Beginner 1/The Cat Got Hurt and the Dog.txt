Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat Got Hurt and the Dog

One day, a small cat got hurt. She did not know how to heal. She was sad. A big dog saw the cat and wanted to help. The dog had an unknown power to heal. "Let me help you," said the dog. The cat was scared, but she said, "Okay." The dog touched the cat and she felt better. The cat was happy and thanked the dog. The cat and the dog became good friends. They played together every day. The cat learned that sometimes help can come from unknown places. The dog learned that it is good to share his power to help others. And they both learned that being kind is the best thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was an old cat named Mr. Whiskers. Mr. Whiskers lived in a small house with a big tree. He liked to play with his ball and climb the tree. One day, Mr. Whiskers saw a little bird on a branch. He wanted to play with the bird. He jumped high and tried to grab the bird. But the bird flew away. Mr. Whiskers felt sad. Then, a kind girl named Lucy saw Mr. Whiskers. Lucy picked him up by his waist and gave him a big hug. She played with him and his ball. Mr. Whiskers was happy again. They became best friends and played together every day.

Sam and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big green ball on the grass. They run to the ball. They want to play with it. "Wow, look at this ball!" Sam says. "It is so green and round!" "Can I kick it?" Mia asks. "I can kick it very fast!" "Okay, but be careful!" Sam says. "Don't kick it too hard!" Mia kicks the ball. The ball flies in the air. It goes over the fence. It lands in the street. "Oh no!" Sam and Mia say. "The ball is gone!" They run to the fence. They look for the ball. They see a car. The car is fast. The car hits the ball. The ball pops. "Uh oh!" Sam and Mia say. "The ball is broken!" They feel sad. They lose the ball. They go back to the park. They see a man. The man has a dog. The dog is small and brown. The dog barks. "Hello, kids!" the man says. "Do you want to play with my dog? His name is Spot. He is very friendly!" Sam and Mia smile. They like dogs. They go to the man and the dog. They pet the dog. The dog licks them. They laugh. "Thank you, mister!" Sam and Mia say. "Your dog is very nice!" "You're welcome, kids!" the man says. "Have fun!" Sam and Mia play with the dog. They throw a stick. The dog runs and brings it back. They gain a new friend. They forget about the ball. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a smart little bird. It had shiny feathers and liked to sing all day. One day, the bird saw a big yacht sailing down the river. It was so amazed that it decided to follow it. The bird kept flying closer and closer to the yacht, but it was getting too close. The yacht sailed too fast and the bird couldn't keep up. Then the bird heard a loud warning. It was coming from the yacht and it said, "Don't fly too close to me!" The bird was very scared, so it flew away quickly. From then on, the bird always remembered the warning it had heard and kept far away from the big yachts that sailed down the river.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Sue. Sue loved to paint. She painted all day long. One day, she painted a big, happy sun on her wall. Sue was very happy. Sue's friend, Tim, saw the sun and wanted to paint too. Tim painted a big, round turn. They both looked at the turn and laughed. They were having so much fun painting together. But then, something unexpected happened. The sun and the turn started to move! The sun jumped off the wall and the turn rolled away. Sue and Tim were very surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They knew they had made magic with their paint.
