Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Hill

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big hill with white snow on it. They want to have fun on the hill. "Let's go to the hill and slide down!" Tom says. "OK, but we need something to slide on," Lily says. They look around and find a big cardboard box. They take the box to the hill and put it under them. They are ready to slide. "Here we go!" Tom says. "Whee!" Lily says. They slide down the hill very fast. They feel the wind and the snow on their faces. They laugh and scream. They have a lot of fun. But they do not see the big tree at the bottom of the hill. The tree is very hard and brown. The box is not strong enough to stop them. They crash into the tree and the box breaks. "Ouch!" Tom says. "Ow!" Lily says. They are hurt and sad. They have tears in their eyes. They have bumps on their heads. "Are you OK?" Tom asks. "I think so," Lily says. They hug each other and say sorry. They walk back to the park and find their moms. Their moms are worried and angry. "What happened to you?" Tom's mom asks. "We had an accident on the hill," Tom says. "We were sliding on a box and hit a tree," Lily says. "That was very dangerous and silly," Lily's mom says. "You could have been hurt very badly," Tom's mom says. They scold them and take them home. They give them ice and kisses. They tell them to be more careful next time. Tom and Lily are sorry and promise to be more careful. They are still friends. They still like to play in the park. But they do not slide on the hill anymore. They have learned their lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. A little boy named Tim wanted to win. He was fearful, but he wanted to try his best. Tim's mom gave him new shoes to wear for the race. The new shoes were fast and shiny. The day of the race came. Tim wore his new shoes and felt brave. The other kids were fast too. They all ran as fast as they could. Tim was in the front, but he felt something was not right. Tim's shoes started to hurt his feet. He was still fearful, but he did not want to stop. The other kids ran faster and faster. In the end, Tim did not win. He was sad, but he knew he tried his best.

Once upon a time, there was a big, noisy dog named Max. Max loved to eat. His favorite thing to eat was white bread. One day, Max went to the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. At the park, Lily played with her ball while Max looked for some white bread to eat. He could smell it! He found a big bag of white bread near a tree. Max was so happy! He started to eat the bread very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The bread started to move! Max was scared. The bread turned into a big, white, friendly bird! The bird said, "Thank you for not eating all of my bread, Max. Now we can be friends!" So Max, Lily, and the big white bird played together at the park all day long.

Once upon a time, in a cold land, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy liked to march in her big boots. One day, she saw a small cat shivering in the cold. "Are you cold, little cat?" Lucy asked. The cat nodded and said, "Yes, I am very cold." Lucy felt bad for the cat. She took off her big scarf and wrapped it around the cat. The cat was so grateful. It purred and said, "Thank you, Lucy." Lucy and the cat became best friends. They marched together in the cold, always helping each other stay warm. They learned that sharing and being kind to others made them both happy. And that was the most important thing.
