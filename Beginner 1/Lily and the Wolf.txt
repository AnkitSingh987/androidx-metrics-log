Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Wolf

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily loved to swing. One day, she went to the park to play on her new swing. She was very happy and excited. As she was swinging, she saw a big wolf near the trees. Lily was scared, but the wolf said, "Don't be scared. I just want to swing too!" So, Lily and the wolf took turns on the new swing, laughing and playing together. After a while, the wolf took off a mask and it was Lily's dad! He wanted to surprise her by dressing up as a wolf. They both laughed and continued to swing, having a great day at the park.

---

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a lot of snow falling from the sky. They put on their coats, hats, and boots and ran out to the yard. They made a big snowman with a carrot nose, a scarf, and a hat. They gave him a name: Bob. "Bob is our new friend," Anna said. "He is very brave and strong." "Yes, he is," Ben agreed. "He can protect us from anything." They played with Bob for a long time, throwing snowballs, making snow angels, and sliding on the ice. They had a lot of fun. But then, a big dog came running towards them. He was black and furry, and he barked loudly. He looked angry and hungry. He saw Bob and ran towards him. "Go away, dog!" Anna shouted. "Leave Bob alone!" But the dog did not listen. He jumped on Bob and bit his carrot nose. He tore his scarf and hat. He knocked him down and made him fall apart. Anna and Ben were very sad. They saw Bob in pieces on the ground. They started to weep. "Bob, Bob, we are sorry," they cried. "We love you, Bob." The dog saw them crying and felt sorry. He stopped barking and wagged his tail. He licked their faces and tried to make them happy. "Maybe he is not so bad," Ben said. "Maybe he just wanted to play." "Maybe," Anna said. "But he hurt Bob. He was our friend." They looked at Bob and wept some more. They wished they could make him again. They wished the snow would not melt. They wished they could be brave like Bob.

One Sunday, a little boy named Tim was sad. He had a terrible day. He lost his toy and his ice cream fell on the ground. He walked home with his head down. On his way home, he met a funny cat. The cat said, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told the cat about his terrible day. The cat had an idea to make Tim feel better. "Let's clap and dance," said the cat. Tim and the cat clapped their hands and paws. They danced and laughed. The terrible day turned into a happy Sunday.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is brown and has a long tail. The dog looks happy and friendly. "Let's follow the dog!" Tom says. "Maybe he will show us something fun." "OK!" Jen says. They run after the dog. The dog runs fast and barks. Tom and Jen laugh and chase him. But the dog does not belong to them. The dog belongs to a man. The man is angry. He sees Tom and Jen following his dog. He shouts at them. "Hey, stop following my dog! He does not like it. He has an itch. He needs to go to the vet. Go away!" Tom and Jen are scared. They stop running. They say sorry to the man. They feel bad. They go back to the park. They sit on a bench. They are quiet. "Tom, I think we did something wrong," Jen says. "Yes, Jen, we did. We should not follow a dog we do not know. He may be sick or hurt. He may not want to play. He may have a owner who loves him. We should be kind and respectful to animals." "You are right, Tom. We should. Next time, we will ask before we follow a dog. Or we will play with our own toys. Or we will make new friends." Tom and Jen hug. They learn a lesson. They are still friends. They are happy.
