Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Basketball

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim loved to play basketball. He had a big dream to become famous one day. Every day, he would play with his ball and try to get better at it. One day, Tim saw a big game on TV. He saw famous basketball players and wanted to be just like them. His mom and dad saw his love for the game and wanted to encourage him. They said, "You can be famous too, Tim! Just keep playing and practicing." Tim played basketball every day with his friends. They would run, jump, and throw the ball into the hoop. His friends encouraged him too, saying, "You can do it, Tim! You'll be famous one day!" And so, Tim kept playing basketball, always believing in his dream. He knew that with hard work and encouragement, he could become a famous basketball player one day. And everyone would cheer for him as he played the game he loved.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved cookies. One day, his mom made a big plate of cookies for him. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to share the cookies with his friend, Sam. Sam was deaf, so he could not hear. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Sam, I have cookies for us." Sam could not hear, but he could see the cookies. His face lit up with a big smile. Tim and Sam sat down to eat the cookies. But, Tim dropped the plate! The cookies scatter all over the floor. Tim and Sam were sad. Tim's mom saw what happened and said, "Don't worry, boys. I will make more cookies for you." Tim and Sam were happy again.

Once there were two friends, Pete and Jane. Pete seemed very happy and kind. Jane, on the other hand, seemed a bit selfish. She was always taking things from Pete without saying thank you. One day, Jane asked Pete if he had found her missing toy. Pete said he had been searching everywhere but he hadn't been able to find it. "Oh," said Jane with a pout. "Maybe you didn't try very hard." Pete was sad. He wanted to help but he also wanted Jane to be nice to him. "Don't worry," said Pete. "I'll search some more tomorrow." He smiled gently at Jane, but she seemed too busy being selfish to notice.

Once upon a time, there was a small boat. The boat was cheap. It lived in a port. The port was by the sea. The boat liked the sea. One day, the boat wanted to add a new friend. It saw a big boat. The big boat was nice. The small boat asked, "Can I be your friend?" The big boat said, "Yes, let's be friends!" Now, the small boat and the big boat are friends. They play in the sea. They have fun. They are happy in the port. The small boat is glad it added a new friend.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. But sometimes they fight over them. One day, they find a big box of screws in the garage. They think they are shiny and fun. "Look, I have a screw!" Lily says. She holds it up and smiles. "I want a screw too!" Ben says. He grabs one from the box and runs away. "Hey, give it back!" Lily says. She chases him around the garage. "Stop, stop!" Mom says. She hears the noise and comes to see. "What are you doing with those screws? They are not toys. They are dangerous. You could hurt yourself or break something. Put them back in the box right now!" "But Mom, they are pretty!" Lily says. "And fun!" Ben says. "No, they are not. They are for fixing things, not playing. You have to behave, or you will get in trouble. Now, be slow and careful, and put them back in the box. Then you can play with your own toys." Mom says. Lily and Ben nod. They are sorry. They do not want to make Mom angry. They are slow and careful, and put the screws back in the box. Then they hug Mom and say sorry. "It's okay, I love you. But you have to listen to me and be safe. Now, go and play nicely with your toys. And share them, okay?" Mom says. "Okay, Mom. We love you too." Lily and Ben say. They go and play nicely with their toys. And they share them. They are happy. Mom is happy too.
