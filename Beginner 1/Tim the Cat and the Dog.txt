Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Tim. Tim was very scared of the big dog that lived next door. Every time the big dog barked, Tim would tremble. One day, Tim found a dry rag in the yard. He thought it was a toy and began to play with it. As Tim played with the dry rag, he saw the big dog coming closer to him. Tim felt brave and thought that maybe the rag would help him not be scared anymore. He held the rag in his mouth and walked towards the big dog. But the big dog barked louder, and Tim started to tremble again. Tim dropped the dry rag and ran away from the big dog. The big dog took the rag and ran after Tim. Tim was very scared and tried to hide, but the big dog found him. The big dog bit the rag and growled at Tim. Tim was very sad and scared, and he knew that the dry rag could not help him. The big dog took the rag and went back to his yard, leaving Tim all alone and still trembling.

Once, there was a big, blue boat. It liked to sail on the water. A man with a red hat was on the boat. He was a selfish man. He did not want to share his hat with anyone. One day, a little girl came to the man. She said, "Can I try your red hat, please?" The man said, "No, it is my hat. I will not share it." The little girl was sad. The boat saw the girl was sad. It wanted to help her. So, the boat stopped sailing. The man could not sail anymore. The man said, "Why did you stop?" The boat said, "You must share your hat." The man did not want the boat to stop sailing. So, he gave the little girl his red hat. The girl was happy. The boat started to sail again. Now, the man and the girl were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little dog named Max. Max loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Max found a big stick in the park. He picked it up with his mouth and showed it to his friends. They all wanted to play with the big stick, but Max wanted to keep it for himself. He trusted that the big stick would help him make new friends. Max was walking with his big stick when he met a big dog named Sam. Max said, "Hi, Sam! Look at my big stick. Do you want to play with me?" Sam looked at Max and said, "No, Max. I don't like big sticks. I like small sticks." Max was sad, but he still trusted his big stick. The next day, Max went to the park again with his big stick. Suddenly, a strong wind blew and the big stick flew out of Max's mouth. Max was scared and sad. But then, Max saw his friends running towards him. They all had small sticks in their mouths. Max's friends said, "Max, we like small sticks too. Let's play together!" Max was so happy and he learned that it's not the big stick that makes friends, but being kind and sharing with others.

Lily liked to draw with her crayons. She had many colors and she made pictures of her family, her friends and her toys. She was proud of her drawings and she showed them to her mom and dad. One day, Lily wanted to improve her drawings. She wanted to make them more pretty and more real. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "You can use a point to make details. A point is a small dot that you can make with your crayon. For example, you can make points for eyes, noses and mouths. You can also make points for flowers, stars and dots." Lily was happy to learn about points. She took her crayons and her paper and she started to make points on her drawings. She made points for eyes, noses and mouths. She made points for flowers, stars and dots. She made her drawings more pretty and more real. But Lily also made a mess. She used too many crayons and she did not put them back in the box. She dropped some crayons on the floor and some on the couch. She also made points on the wall, the table and the lamp. She made her room very dirty. When her mom saw the mess, she was not happy. She said, "Lily, you need to clean up your room. You cannot make points on everything. You can only make points on your paper. You also need to put your crayons back in the box. You cannot leave them everywhere. They can break or get lost. You need to take care of your things." Lily was sorry. She did not mean to make her mom angry. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to improve my drawings. I like to make points. They are fun and pretty." Her mom said, "I know, Lily. I like your drawings too. They are very nice. But you also need to be neat and tidy. You need to clean up your room and apologize for making points on the wall, the table and the lamp. Then we can hang your drawings on the fridge and admire them." Lily nodded. She said, "OK, mom. I will clean up my room and say sorry. Thank you for teaching me about points. I love you, mom." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lily. You are a good girl. Now let's go and clean up."
