Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Book and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy book called a novel. The novel lived on a high shelf in a little boy's room. The little boy, named Tim, loved to hear stories from the novel every night before bed. One day, Tim asked his mom, "Please, can you read the novel to me?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim." She reached up high and took the heavy novel down from the shelf. Tim was very happy. They sat on the soft bed and opened the novel. They read about a funny dog and a fast car. The heavy novel had many stories to tell. Tim and his mom laughed and had a good time. When it was time for bed, Tim said, "Thank you, mom. Please read the novel to me again tomorrow."

Anna and Ben are playing with their dolls in the living room. Anna likes to make her doll talk and act like a teacher. She says, "Hello, class. Today we are going to learn about colors. Repeat after me: red, blue, yellow, green." Ben tries to copy her, but he forgets some of the colors. He says, "Red, blue, green, red." Anna laughs and says, "No, Ben. That's wrong. You have to say yellow, not red again. Yellow is like the sun and the bananas. Try again." Ben feels embarrassed. He does not like when Anna laughs at him. He thinks she is mean. He looks at his doll and sees that it has a spot on its face. He says, "Look, Anna. Your doll has an itch. It has a red spot on its face. Maybe it has chicken pox." Anna looks at her doll and sees the spot. She feels scared. She does not want her doll to be sick. She says, "No, Ben. That's not an itch. That's a sticker. You put a sticker on my doll. That's not nice. Take it off." Ben smiles and says, "No, Anna. That's an itch. You have to scratch it. Scratch, scratch, scratch." He makes his doll scratch Anna's doll's face. Anna gets angry and says, "Stop it, Ben. You are hurting my doll. Give it back to me." They start to pull and tug on the doll. They make a lot of noise. Their mom hears them and comes to the living room. She sees them fighting over the doll. She says, "What's going on here? Why are you two not playing nicely?" Anna and Ben stop and look at their mom. They feel ashamed. They say, "Sorry, mom." They give each other the doll and hug. They say, "We are sorry, Anna. We are sorry, Ben. We are friends." They go back to playing with their dolls. They repeat the colors and the stickers. They have fun.

Lila and Tom like to play in the big park. They have a boat that they can sail on the pond. They take turns to push the boat and watch it go fast on the water. One day, they see a big tree near the pond. The tree has many vines that hang down from the branches. Lila and Tom think the vines look like ropes. They want to swing on them and pretend they are monkeys. Lila goes first. She grabs a vine and runs to the pond. She swings on the vine and lets go. She lands in the boat and laughs. Tom claps and says, "That was fun!" Tom wants to try too. He grabs another vine and runs to the pond. He swings on the vine and lets go. But he misses the boat and falls in the water. He splashes and cries. He is wet and hurt. Lila jumps out of the boat and runs to Tom. She helps him get out of the water. She hugs him and says, "I'm sorry, Tom. Are you okay?" Tom nods and says, "I'm okay, Lila. But I don't like the vines. They are mean." Lila and Tom go back to the boat. They sail it on the pond and forget about the vines. They are happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ann. Ann lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Ann's mom said, "Ann, I have a gift for you." Ann was very happy to receive a gift. Ann opened the gift and found an original toy whip inside. The whip was red and blue, and Ann loved it. She said, "Thank you, Mom! I will play with this whip all day!" Ann went outside and saw her friend, Ben. "Look at my new whip!" Ann said. "Wow!" Ben said, "That is a cool whip!" They played with the whip together, laughing and having fun. And they lived happily ever after.
