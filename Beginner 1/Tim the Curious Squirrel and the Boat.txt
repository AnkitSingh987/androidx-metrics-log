Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Curious Squirrel and the Boat

One day, a curious little squirrel named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big tree with many acorns. Tim was so happy! He knew that he could eat the acorns and save some for later. He started to pick up the acorns one by one. As Tim was picking up acorns, he saw a small piece of paper on the ground. He picked it up and tried to fold it. Tim's friend, a wise old owl named Mr. Hoot, saw him and asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim showed the paper to Mr. Hoot and said, "I found this paper and I want to fold it, but I don't know how." Mr. Hoot smiled and said, "I can teach you how to fold it, but first, let's pick up all the acorns." Tim and Mr. Hoot picked up all the acorns and put them in a big pile. Then, Mr. Hoot showed Tim how to fold the paper into a little boat. Tim was so happy with his new boat! He thanked Mr. Hoot and they both went to a small pond to play with the boat. As they played with the boat, the wind started to blow. The boat floated away and landed on the other side of the pond. Tim was sad, but Mr. Hoot said, "Don't worry, Tim. I knew this would happen. That's why we picked up all the acorns first." Tim smiled and hugged Mr. Hoot. They went back to the big pile of acorns and ate some. Tim knew that he had a wise friend in Mr. Hoot and that he would always be there to help him.

Ben likes to read books. He has many books in his room. He reads them every day. He learns new words and stories. He likes to read with his mom and dad. One day, Ben finds a new book on his bed. It is a big book with many pictures. It is a book about animals. Ben loves animals. He opens the book and starts to read. He sees a lion, a tiger, a bear, and a monkey. He reads their names and sounds. He sees a zebra, a giraffe, an elephant, and a rhino. He reads their names and colors. He sees a bird, a fish, a frog, and a snake. He reads their names and shapes. He is so happy. He wants to read more. He turns the page. He sees a big word. It says "wall". He does not know what it means. He looks at the picture. He sees a wall. It is a wall with many bricks. It is a wall with a hole. He sees something in the hole. It is a mouse. The mouse is small and gray. It has a long tail and big ears. It looks at Ben. It is alert. It is afraid of Ben. It thinks Ben is a cat. It runs away. It hides in the hole. Ben is surprised. He laughs. He says "wall". He says "mouse". He says "hello". He likes the book. He likes the wall. He likes the mouse. He wants to read more. He turns the page. He sees a new word. It says "door". He does not know what it means. He looks at the picture. He sees a door. He wonders what is behind the door. He is curious. He is excited. He reads on.

Once upon a time, in a mild forest, there lived a big rock. The rock had a friend, a small bird. Every day, the bird would fly and play, and the rock would lay on the ground. One day, the bird said, "Rock, let's play a game!" The rock replied, "I can't play, I lay here all day." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea, I can bring you a leaf, and you can lay it on your head!" The next day, the bird brought a leaf and the rock lay it on his head. They both laughed and had fun. From that day on, they played their new game every day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a large house, there was a big bunny with very large ears. The big bunny loved to hold things with his large ears. He would hold toys, food, and even his friends with his ears. One day, the big bunny wanted to play a game with his friends. He told them to hide, and he would find them using his large ears to hold them. His friends were excited and ran to hide. The big bunny closed his eyes and started to look for them. As the big bunny looked, he found a small mouse stuck in a hole. The mouse was scared and asked for help. The big bunny used his large ears to hold the mouse and pull him out of the hole. The mouse was very happy and thanked the big bunny. In the end, the big bunny learned that helping others is more important than playing games.
