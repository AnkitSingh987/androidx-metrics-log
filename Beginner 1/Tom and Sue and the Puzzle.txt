Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sue and the Puzzle

One day, two friends, Tom and Sue, had a puzzle. They both wanted to be the best at putting the puzzle together. They were very competitive. Tom said, "I will be the best at this puzzle!" Sue said, "No, I will be the best!" They started to put the puzzle together. They worked fast and did not hear the doorbell ring. A big dog came in. The dog saw the puzzle and wanted to play too. The dog jumped on the puzzle and broke it into many pieces. Tom and Sue were very sad. They knew that they should have listened and opened the door. Now, the puzzle was broken, and they could not find out who was the best.

One day, a little girl named Sue woke up and felt great. She put on her soft, warm bathrobe and went to the kitchen. Her mom was making breakfast. Sue could smell the yummy food. She was very hungry. "Mom, can I have some food, please?" Sue asked. Mom said, "Yes, but first, you need to reach for a plate on the shelf." Sue stood on her tiptoes and reached high. She got a plate and put it on the table. Mom gave Sue some eggs and toast. Sue ate her breakfast and felt even better. After that, Sue and her mom went outside to play. They had a fun day together.

Ben likes to help his dad deliver pizza. He puts on his red hat and rides in the big car with him. He holds the boxes of pizza and sauce in his lap. He smiles when he sees the people who order pizza. They are happy to see him and his dad. One day, Ben and his dad go to a house with a big dog. The dog is angry. It barks and growls at them. It runs out of the gate and chases them. Ben is scared. He drops the box of sauce. The sauce spills on the ground. The dog stops and licks the sauce. It likes the sauce. Ben and his dad run to the door. They knock and ring the bell. A lady opens the door. She sees the pizza and the sauce. She is angry. She yells at them. She says they are late and they made a mess. She does not want the pizza. She slams the door. Ben and his dad are sad. They go back to the car. They have no money and no sauce. They drive away. Ben cries. He says he is sorry. He says he does not want to deliver pizza anymore. His dad hugs him. He says it is not his fault. He says the dog and the lady were mean. He says he is proud of him for trying to help. He says he loves him. He says they can eat the pizza and get more sauce. He says they can deliver pizza another day. He says they will have fun. Ben smiles. He wipes his tears. He says he loves his dad. He says he likes to deliver pizza. He says he hopes the dog and the lady will be nice next time. He says he wants to share the pizza and the sauce with them. He says he is not angry. He is happy.

Once there was a little boy named Jack. Jack and his mommy were going on a special trip! Jack was so excited and he couldn't wait to get there. When they arrived, Jack's mommy told him to help her unpack the car. After they were done, Jack went inside the house. It was so clean and shiny! Jack ran around exploring the house. He saw a couch and a big TV, and lots of fun things. He even saw a table with some yummy snacks! Then, Jack saw the bathroom. He spotted the big mirror and smiled at his reflection. He said "Hi there!" and he noticed he had a big lip! He decided to make funny faces with it and he laughed at himself. After a while, Jack was tired and he asked his mommy to read him a bedtime story. He had such a fun day and as he snuggled in his cozy bed, he smiled and drifted off to sleep.
