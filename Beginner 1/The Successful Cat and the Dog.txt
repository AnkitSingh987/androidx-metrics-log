Category: Beginner
Title: The Successful Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a successful cat. The cat liked to wear a leather collar. One day, the cat lost its collar in the park. The cat was very sad. The cat went to the park to find its collar. It looked under the trees and near the pond. Then, the cat met a dog. The dog said, "What are you looking for?" The cat said, "I am looking for my leather collar." The dog wanted to help the cat. They looked together for the collar. They looked behind the bushes and on the swings. Finally, they found the collar near the slide. The cat was very happy. The cat and dog became good friends. They both knew that if they worked together, they could succeed.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. The frog lived near a pond. The pond was his home. He had many friends who lived there too. They all liked to play and swim in the pond. One day, the frog saw a big, red ball. It was next to the pond. He did not know where it came from. The frog wanted to show his friends the ball. They all played with the ball and had fun. After playing, the frog and his friends were hungry. They found some delicious bugs to eat. They all sat by the pond and ate their yummy food. They were happy and full. They knew they had a great day at the pond.

Ben and Mia liked to play in the sandbox. They had shovels and buckets and made castles and roads. One day, they decided to dig a big pit in the sand. They worked hard and made the pit very deep and wide. "Look at our pit!" Ben said. "It is the best pit ever!" "Yes, it is!" Mia agreed. "But we have to be careful. We don't want to fall in the pit." They put a stick in the sand next to the pit. The stick had a red cloth tied to it. The cloth was a flag. The flag meant that the pit was there and that they had to be careful. "Let's pretend that the pit is a restaurant," Mia said. "We can serve food to our toys." "OK!" Ben said. "That sounds fun!" They got their toys and put them around the pit. They had a teddy bear, a doll, a truck and a dinosaur. They used leaves and stones and flowers as food. They took turns to serve the food to their toys. "Here you go, teddy bear," Mia said. "This is a leaf salad. It is very yummy." "Thank you, Mia," the teddy bear said. Mia made the teddy bear talk with her voice. "Here you go, dinosaur," Ben said. "This is a stone burger. It is very crunchy." "Thank you, Ben," the dinosaur said. Ben made the dinosaur talk with his voice. They had a lot of fun serving food to their toys. They were careful not to fall in the pit. They were happy and proud of their pit. They played until it was time to go home.

One day, a green cat named Sam went for a walk. Today was a sunny day, and Sam was happy. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But there was a mean dog named Max near the tree. Sam was scared of Max. Sam had a plan. He saw a ball and had an idea. Sam pushed the ball near Max. Max saw the ball and started to play with it. The dog ran away from the tree with the ball. Now, Sam could climb the tree. Sam climbed the tree and saw a big bird. The bird let out a big yawn. Sam knew the bird was sleepy. So, Sam waited for the bird to sleep. When the bird slept, Sam got down from the tree. He was happy and ran back home.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy rod. One day, he went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big rock and wanted to lift it. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim." When Tim tried to lift the rock, he found a small box under it. He opened the box and found a magic rod inside. The magic rod could make things move without touching them. Tim was very happy and started to play with the magic rod. But then, Tim saw a rude dog that barked at him. He used the magic rod to make the dog stop barking. The dog was not rude anymore. It was friendly and played with Tim. They became best friends and had lots of fun together. Tim learned that sometimes, a little magic can turn something rude into a new friend.
