Category: Beginner
Title: Bow the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Bow. Bow had a big, happy face. He liked to play with his ball all day long. Bow had many friends who liked to play with him too. One day, Bow felt anxious. He could not find his ball. Bow looked all around, but the ball was gone. Bow's friends came and saw his sad face. They wanted to help him find the ball. Bow's friends looked high and low for the ball. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew a hat right onto Bow's face! Bow's friends laughed and laughed. Bow laughed too. Under the hat, Bow found his ball! The wind had brought it back to him. Bow and his friends played with the ball and the hat all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He lived in a small house with his dog, Spike. They were best friends. The bald man liked to wear a big hat on his head. One day, the bald man and Spike went for a walk. The bald man wanted to tie his shoes. He asked Spike to sit and wait. Spike sat down and watched the bald man tie his shoes. But then, a big wind came. It blew the bald man's hat away. The bald man and Spike ran after the hat. They tried to catch it, but they could not. The hat went far away, and they could not find it. The bald man was sad because he lost his hat. Spike was sad too, because he could not help his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. In the field, there were many soft and comfortable spots to sit. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the field with her dog, Max. Lily and Max played in the field. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Lily said, "I want to improve my running." So, she and Max raced around the field. They ran faster and faster. Max was very fast, but Lily got better and better. At the end of the day, Lily and Max were tired. They found a comfortable spot in the field. They sat down, and Lily said, "I improved my running today. I am happy." They smiled and went home, ready to play again another day.

Once, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went on a walk. It was a cold, icy day. They saw a big tree with many red apples. Tim wanted to pick some apples, but the tree was too tall. Max barked, as if he had an idea. Max said, "Tim, let's ask our friend, Mr. Bear, for help." Tim and Max went to Mr. Bear's house. They asked him to help with their task. Mr. Bear agreed and they all went to the apple tree. Mr. Bear stood on his back legs and picked the apples for Tim. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. They all paused and looked around. A big, mean wolf came out from behind a bush. The wolf wanted to eat the apples too. Tim said, "Mr. Wolf, please don't take our apples. We can share." The wolf thought for a moment and agreed. They all shared the apples and became friends. The moral of the story is to always share with others, even if they seem mean at first.

Once upon a time, there was a kind nurse. She liked to serve food to people who were sick. She had a big heart and a big smile. One day, a deaf boy came to the nurse. He could not hear, but he could see her smile. The nurse gave the boy some food. The boy was very happy. The nurse and the deaf boy became good friends. They played and laughed together. The nurse helped the boy feel better. They lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with stamps. They have many stamps with animals, cars, flowers and stars. They stick the stamps on paper and make pictures. One day, they have a game. They want to see who can make the best picture with stamps. Lily says, "I will win. I have the most stamps." Ben says, "No, I will win. I have the best stamps." They start to stick the stamps on paper. Sam is a nosy boy. He lives next door. He sees Lily and Ben playing with stamps. He wants to play too. He knocks on the door and says, "Can I play with you?" Lily and Ben say, "No, we are busy. We are having a game." Sam is sad. He goes away. Lily and Ben finish their pictures. They show them to each other. Lily's picture has many animals. Ben's picture has many cars. They both think their pictures are good. They do not know who wins. Sam comes back. He has a big smile. He says, "I have a surprise for you. Look what I have." He shows them a big book. The book has many stamps. The stamps are shiny and colorful. They have letters and numbers and flags and maps. Lily and Ben are amazed. They say, "Wow, where did you get that?" Sam says, "My grandpa gave it to me. He collects stamps. He has many books like this. He said I can share them with you if you want." Lily and Ben are happy. They say, "Yes, please. Can we see them?" Sam says, "Sure. Come with me." They go to Sam's house. They look at the stamps. They learn about different countries and places. They have fun. Lily and Ben forget about their game. They do not care who wins. They are glad they have a new friend. Sam is not nosy anymore. He is nice. They all like stamps. They play with stamps every day.
