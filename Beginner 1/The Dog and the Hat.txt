Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog and the Hat

One day, a dog went for a walk. The dog liked to walk and chew. He chewed a big, red ball. The dog was happy. The sun was high in the sky. The dog saw a bird. The bird had a hat. The hat was high on the bird's head. The dog wanted the hat. The dog jumped high, but could not get the hat. Then, the bird dropped the hat. The dog was happy. He chewed the hat. But the hat was not a hat. It was a big, red ball. The dog was surprised. The dog and the bird played with the ball. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a long cat with soft fur. The cat liked to play and learn new things. One day, the cat went outside to play with her friends. While playing, the cat found a big, red ball. She wanted to learn how to play with the ball. The cat hit the ball with her head and it went up in the sky. The cat was happy and proud. But then, something bad happened. The ball went too high and got stuck in a tree. The cat tried to get the ball, but she could not reach it. The cat was sad because she lost her new toy.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was a daughter. She was bald. She did not have hair. She had a big hat to keep her head warm. One day, she went to the park. She sat on the grass. She saw a butterfly. She said, "I want to imagine I can fly like you." She closed her eyes and thought she was flying. She felt happy. She played with the butterfly all day. When it was time to go home, she said, "Goodbye, butterfly. Thank you for playing with me. I will imagine we can play together again soon."

---

Once there was a duck who was very greedy. He saw a house made of bricks and he wanted it all for himself. So, the duck decided to steal a brick. He flew over to the house and grabbed a brick with his beak. The house belonged to a cat. The cat saw the duck take the brick and said, "Hey! Give me back my brick!" The duck was so scared he dropped the brick and flew away. The cat was very angry. He thought that was so stupid for the duck to think he could just steal a brick. From then on, the cat kept a close eye on his house and never let anyone steal anything from him again.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a pretty flower and wanted to pick it. She looked around and saw a sign that said, "Pick the flower and get a reward." Sue was excited and picked the flower. Sue looked around, confused. She did not see her reward. A big, friendly dog walked up to her. The dog had a note tied to its collar. Sue read the note, "Your reward is a new friend!" Sue was very happy and played with the dog all day. At the end of the day, Sue took the dog home. She showed her mom the note and the flower. Her mom smiled and said, "Sue, the flower was magic. The dog is a prince!" Sue gasped. The dog turned into a prince! Sue and the prince became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia loved to play sports. She liked to run, jump, and throw balls. In the park, she saw a big dog. The dog was very big and Mia felt frightened. She ran behind her mom and pointed at the dog. Mia's mom said, "Don't worry, Mia. The dog is friendly. Let's go talk to the dog's owner." They walked to the dog's owner and said hello. The owner was a nice man named Tom. He told them the dog's name was Max. Tom said, "Max loves to play sports too! Do you want to play with him?" Mia was still a little scared, but she wanted to try. They started to play catch with Max. Mia threw the ball and Max caught it. They played and laughed together. In the end, Mia was not frightened anymore. She made a new friend in Max, and they all had fun playing sports at the park.
