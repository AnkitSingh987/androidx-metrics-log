Category: Beginner
Title: Mark the Boy and the Ball

One day, a boy named Mark went to the park. He saw a big red ball. He wanted to test how high it would bounce. So, he threw the ball up in the air. It went very high and then came back down. Mark was happy and laughed. But then, the sun got very hot. Mark started to feel hot too. He wanted to find a cool place to sit. He saw a big tree and went to sit under it. The tree had lots of shade and Mark felt better. While Mark was sitting, a big wind came. It blew the red ball away. Mark was sad and thought the ball was gone. But then, the wind blew the ball back to him. It was a twist! Mark was happy again and played with the ball all day.

Once there was a frog who lived in a pond. One day, the frog decided he wanted to go explore the world. He said goodbye to his friends and hopped away. But the frog didn't get far. He soon found himself tangled in some grass. He tried to untangle himself, but it was too difficult. Suddenly, a kind little boy came walking by. He saw the poor frog and said, "Oh no! I have to help him!". The boy gently untangled the frog from the grass and gave him a gentle smile. The relieved frog thanked the boy and said, "You have saved me! Now I am free to explore the world like I wanted!". Moral of the story: Kindness is a special superpower that can be used to help others. When someone needs help, always try to do something kind!

Once upon a time, there was a soldier. He was a good and kind soldier. He had a big job to do, but it was not easy. He had to help people and keep them safe. One day, the soldier met a little girl. She was sad and lost. The soldier wanted to help her. He said, "Do not worry. I will find your family." The little girl smiled and said, "Thank you, soldier." The soldier and the little girl walked and walked. They looked for her family. The soldier hoped they would find them soon. At last, they found the little girl's family. They were so happy to see her. The soldier was very happy too. His job was not easy, but he knew that helping people was important.

Tim wanted to jump on the couch. He liked to jump on the couch when mom was not home. He felt happy and free when he jumped on the couch. But today, mom was home. She was working on her laptop on the table. She told Tim not to jump on the couch. She said it was not normal. She said it was bad for the couch and for Tim. Tim was sad. He did not want to stop jumping on the couch. He thought it was normal and fun. He waited for mom to look away. Then he ran to the couch and jumped on it. He jumped high and low. He jumped fast and slow. He laughed and shouted. He felt good. But mom heard him. She turned around and saw him jumping on the couch. She was angry. She yelled at Tim. She told him to get off the couch. She told him he was naughty. She told him he did not succeed. She told him he had to go to his room. Tim was scared. He did not want to go to his room. He did not want to make mom angry. He did not want to be naughty. He did not want to fail. He got off the couch and said sorry to mom. He said he would not jump on the couch again. He said he would be normal. Mom was still angry, but she was also sorry. She knew Tim was just a kid. She knew he liked to jump on the couch. She knew he did not mean to be bad. She hugged Tim and said sorry to him. She said she loved him. She said he could succeed. She said he could be normal and fun. She said he could jump on the couch sometimes, but not when she was working. She said they could jump on the couch together later. Tim was happy. He hugged mom and said he loved her. He said he was sorry. He said he would succeed. He said he would be normal and fun. He said he would wait for mom to finish working. He said he would jump on the couch with her later. He smiled and went to his room. He played with his toys and waited for mom. He was a good boy.
