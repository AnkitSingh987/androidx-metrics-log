Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Leaves

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a messy pile of leaves. He wanted to jump in it. As he got closer, he heard a whisper. He looked around, but saw no one. "Hello," whispered the voice again. Tim looked down and saw a snake in the leaves. The snake was friendly, but shy. "I'm Sam the Snake," it said. "Can you help me clean up these leaves?" Tim nodded and said, "Yes, Sam. Let's clean up together." They worked together to make the park neat and tidy. When they were done, the park looked nice again. Sam the Snake thanked Tim for his help. They became good friends and played together at the park every day. And from that day on, they always made sure to keep the park clean and beautiful.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. They saw a big beetle on a leaf. It was shiny and black. "Wow, look at that beetle!" Lily said. "It is so pretty. Can I hold it?" "OK, but be careful. It might bite you." Tom said. He picked up the beetle and gave it to Lily. Lily was happy. She held the beetle in her hand. She felt its legs tickle her palm. She smiled and said, "Hello, beetle. You are my friend." The beetle did not like being held. It wanted to be free. It was flexible and strong. It wriggled and twisted in Lily's hand. It opened its jaws and bit Lily's finger. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily cried. She dropped the beetle and shook her hand. She felt a sharp pain in her finger. She saw blood on her skin. "Are you OK, Lily?" Tom asked. He ran to her and looked at her finger. He saw a red mark where the beetle bit her. "No, I'm not OK. It hurts. The beetle was mean. It was not my friend." Lily said. She started to cry. Tom hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I should have told you not to hold the beetle. It was not a nice bug. It was a bad bug. Come on, let's go inside and get a band-aid." They left the garden and went inside. The beetle settled on the leaf again. It was glad to be free. It did not care about Lily and Tom. It was not their friend. It was a bad bug.

Once upon a time, there was a car named Zoomy. Zoomy loved to go fast, but he needed fuel to do that. One day, Zoomy's fuel was very low, so he went to the fuel place to get some more. On the way, Zoomy saw a big tree. The tree had many branches that would shake when the wind blew. Zoomy stopped to watch the tree shake and thought it was fun. He wanted to shake too, so he shook his wheels and laughed. Zoomy knew that going too fast was dangerous. He remembered that his mom always told him to be careful. So, after watching the tree shake, Zoomy went to get his fuel and drove home safely, making sure not to be dangerous. And he was happy because he had a fun day.

One day, a sweet cat and a dog went to a dance. The cat wore a pretty hat, and the dog had a big bow. They were very happy to dance together. The music was loud, and they danced fast and slow. The sweet cat said to the dog, "I like to dance with you. You are a good friend." The dog wagged his tail and said, "I like to dance with you too, cat. We have fun." They danced all day and all night. The sun went down, and the moon came up. The sweet cat and the dog were tired, but they were still happy. They said "bye" to each other and went to their homes to sleep.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a long path. On this path, a boy named Tim walked every day. Tim loved to find pretty things on his walks. One day, Tim saw a big, shiny gem on the path. The gem was so pretty, he wanted to take it home. Tim tried to pick up the gem, but it was too heavy. He asked his friend, a big, strong dog, to help him. The dog did not listen to Tim and ignored him. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He thought of a plan to take the gem home. Tim found a long stick and used it to roll the gem all the way home. It took a long time, but Tim was happy. He showed the gem to his mom and dad. They were so proud of him. Tim learned that even when friends ignore him, he can still find a way to get things done.
