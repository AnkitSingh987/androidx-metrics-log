Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. They had a nice neighbor who lived next door. The neighbor had a big tree in his yard. Tim liked to play near the tree. One sunny day, Tim found a hidden toy under the tree. It was a red ball. Tim was very happy and started to play with the ball. He threw the ball up in the air and tried to catch it. While playing, Tim did not see a banana peel on the ground. He stepped on it and slipped. Tim fell down, but he did not cry. The neighbor saw what happened and came to help Tim. He picked up the ball and gave it back to Tim. Tim said "thank you" and they both laughed. Tim and the neighbor played with the ball together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a toy box full of toys. One day, she found a big square in her toy box. The square was new and Sue was very happy. Sue played with the square all day. She liked to poke it with her finger. But then, something strange happened. When Sue poked the square, it changed into a fake bird. The bird could not fly, but it could sing. Sue was so surprised! Sue showed the fake bird to her mom and dad. They could not believe it! They all laughed and clapped as the bird sang. Sue loved her new toy even more. And from that day on, she always poked the square to make the bird sing.

Jazz was a cat who liked to play music. He had a unique drum set made of cans and boxes. He would tap and bang on them with his paws and make loud noises. He thought he was the best drummer in the world. One day, he saw a mouse running across the floor. He wanted to catch it and eat it. He left his drum set and chased the mouse. The mouse was fast and smart. It ran under a sofa and hid. Jazz could not find it. He was angry and hungry. He went back to his drum set and played louder and louder. He wanted to scare the mouse out. He did not care that he was making a lot of noise. He did not care that it was night time and everyone else wanted to sleep. Soon, he heard a voice. It was his owner, a little girl named Lily. She came out of her room and said, "Jazz, please stop! You are making too much noise! I can't sleep! You are a bad cat!" Jazz felt sorry. He did not want to make Lily sad. He loved Lily. She was his friend. She gave him food and water and toys and hugs. He stopped playing and went to her. He rubbed his head against her leg and said, "Meow, meow, sorry, Lily. I was trying to catch a mouse. I did not mean to be bad." Lily smiled and said, "It's okay, Jazz. I forgive you. But you have to be quiet at night. Everyone needs to sleep. Sleep is good for you. It makes you healthy and happy. And you can play your music in the day. Your music is unique and fun. But not too loud, okay?" Jazz nodded and said, "Meow, meow, okay, Lily. I will be quiet at night. I will sleep with you. And I will play my music in the day. But not too loud. Thank you, Lily. You are the best." Lily hugged Jazz and said, "You are welcome, Jazz. You are the best, too. Now let's go to sleep. Good night, Jazz." Jazz followed Lily to her room. He curled up on her bed and closed his eyes. He felt warm and cozy and happy. He dreamed of playing his music and catching mice. He learned his lesson. He learned to be respectful and kind. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because he was going on vacation with his family. They were going to the beach, and Tim loved to play in the sand and water. One day, while playing, Tim met a little girl named Sue. Sue looked confused, and Tim asked, "Why are you confused?" Sue said, "I hear a funny sound, but I don't know where it's coming from." Tim listened and heard the sound too. They both decided to find out what the sound was. Tim and Sue followed the sound and found a little dog stuck in a hole. They helped the dog get out, and the dog was so happy. The funny sound was the dog's cry for help. Tim and Sue felt good that they found the sound and helped the dog. Their vacation was even more fun because they made a new friend.
