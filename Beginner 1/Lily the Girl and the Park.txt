Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty dress with a bow at the waist. Lily loved to play in the park with her friends. One day, Lily saw a big, terrible bug in the park. She was scared, but she wanted to be brave. So, she tried to settle near the bug and look at it. But the bug did not like that. The bug jumped on Lily's dress at the waist. She cried and ran away. Her friends could not help her, and the bug did not leave. In the end, Lily's pretty dress was ruined, and she was very sad.

---

Lily was a little girl who liked to play in the garden. She had a big shovel and a bucket. She liked to dig holes and fill them with dirt and flowers. One day, she saw a small hole in the ground. She wondered what was inside. She put her shovel in the hole and tried to dig. But something pushed back. It was a mole. The mole was angry. He did not like Lily digging in his home. He wanted to be alone. He bit Lily's shovel and tried to pull it away. Lily did not let go. She pulled back. They started to fight. Lily's mom saw them fighting. She ran to the garden and picked up Lily. She said, "Lily, stop fighting with the mole. He is scared and hurt. He lives in the hole. You live in the house. You have to share the garden and be kind." Lily felt sorry for the mole. She said, "I'm sorry, mole. I did not know you lived here. I will not dig in your hole anymore. Can we be friends?" The mole nodded. He was tired and sore. He said, "I'm sorry, too. I was rude and mean. You can play in the garden. But please leave some space for me." Lily's mom smiled. She said, "You are both very wise. You learned to say sorry and make peace. That is better than fighting. Now, let's go inside and have some cookies and milk." Lily and the mole agreed. They followed Lily's mom to the house. They shared the cookies and milk and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big red ball. She loved to play with her ball every day. One day, Sue was playing with her ball and she forgot to put it away. She left it outside in the yard. That night, a big storm came. The wind blew the ball far away. The next day, Sue went outside to play with her ball. But she could not find it. Sue was sad. She looked all around the yard, but the ball was gone. Sue's friend, Tom, saw her looking for the ball. He asked, "Sue, what's the trouble?" Sue said, "I forgot to put my ball away, and now it's gone." Tom wanted to help Sue feel better. He said, "Don't be sad, Sue. We can find your ball together." So, Sue and Tom looked for the ball all day. And they found it! Sue was happy again, and she never forgot to put her ball away ever again.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a new girl on the swings. She has long hair and a pink dress. Lily and Ben want to say hello. "Hi, I am Lily. This is Ben. What is your name?" Lily asks. "Hi, I am Mia. I am new here. I just moved with my mom and dad," Mia says. "Do you want to play with us?" Ben asks. "Yes, please. I like to play," Mia says. They run to the slide and take turns. They have fun. Then they hear a bell. It is snack time. They go to the picnic table. Lily's mom has a big basket. She opens it and takes out some cups and plates. "Who is your new friend, Lily?" Lily's mom asks. "This is Mia. She is new. She likes to play," Lily says. "Hello, Mia. I am Lily's mom. Nice to meet you," Lily's mom says. "Hello, Lily's mom. Nice to meet you, too," Mia says. Lily's mom smiles. She takes out a big mixer. She puts some bananas, strawberries, milk and honey in it. She turns it on. It makes a loud noise. The mixer mixes everything together. It makes a pink drink. "What is that?" Mia asks. "It is a smoothie. It is a healthy drink. It has fruits and milk. Do you want to try some?" Lily's mom asks. "Yes, please. It looks yummy," Mia says. Lily's mom pours some smoothie in each cup. She gives one to Lily, one to Ben and one to Mia. They sip their smoothies. They like them. They say thank you to Lily's mom. "You are welcome. I am glad you like them. You are a very nice girl, Mia. I hope you come to play again," Lily's mom says. "I will. Thank you, Lily's mom. You are very nice, too. And thank you, Lily and Ben, for being my friends," Mia says. They smile at each other. They finish their smoothies. They go back to play. They are happy. They have a new friend.
