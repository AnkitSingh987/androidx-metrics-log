Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Stick

Tim and Sam were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big swing. The swing had a long chain and a round seat. Tim and Sam wanted to try the swing. "Let me go first!" Tim said. He ran to the swing and sat on the seat. He pushed with his feet and made the swing go high. He felt happy and free. He waved to Sam. "Look at me! I am flying!" Sam waited for his turn. He felt bored and angry. He wanted to swing too. He had an idea. He picked up a stick and threw it at the chain. He hoped to make the chain break and Tim fall. The stick hit the chain hard. The chain made a loud noise and snapped. Tim screamed as he flew off the seat. He landed on the ground with a thud. He felt pain in his head and his arm. He cried and cried. Sam ran to Tim. He felt sorry and scared. He did not mean to hurt Tim. He wanted to say sorry, but Tim did not want to talk to him. Tim's mom came and took Tim to the hospital. She was angry and sad. She told Sam to go away and never come back. Sam learned a lesson. He learned that throwing sticks is bad. He learned that hurting friends is worse. He learned that being selfish is not fun. He lost his friend and his swing. He wished he had been nice and waited for his turn. He waved to Tim, but Tim did not wave back.

Once upon a time, there was a big cloud. The cloud was sad because it had a secret. One day, the cloud decided to reveal the secret. It was going to rain for the first time. There was a small tree in a field. The tree was very thirsty. The tree saw the big cloud and hoped it would rain. The cloud came closer and closer. The tree was very excited. Then, the rain started. It was a persistent rain. The tree was so happy to get a drink. But, something unexpected happened. The rain didn't stop. It rained and rained. The tree started to feel scared. But then, the sun came out and the rain stopped. The tree was safe and not thirsty anymore. The cloud was happy too because it shared its secret with the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a small, sweet dog named Max. Max loved to play and try new things. One day, he saw a big, red ball in an open space. Max wanted to play with the ball, so he ran to it. When Max got to the ball, he met a little girl named Lily. Lily was also sweet and liked to play. She said, "Hi Max, do you want to play with me and my big, red ball?" Max wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, I want to play!" Max and Lily played for a long time in the big, open space. They tried to roll the ball, kick the ball, and even jump on the ball. They had so much fun playing together. At the end of the day, Max and Lily were tired but happy. They knew they would always be friends and try new things together.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day. A little girl named Lily went outside to play. She wore a warm hat and a big coat. Lily saw a cat sitting on the grass. The cat looked sad and cold too. Lily asked the cat, "Why are you sad?" The cat did not answer. Lily went inside her house to get some food for the cat. She gave the cat some milk and a soft blanket. The cat drank the milk and sat on the blanket. It started to feel warm and happy. Then, something unexpected happened! The cat began to talk! It said, "Thank you, Lily!" Lily was very surprised. She did not know cats could talk. The cat told Lily that it was a magic cat. It could only talk when someone was kind to it. From that day on, Lily and the magic cat were the best of friends.
