Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Brown Dog and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max liked to wear a red hat. He wore his red hat every day. One day, Max met a cat named Sam. Sam had a blue hat. Max said to Sam, "I like your blue hat. Do you want to trade hats?" Sam thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I would like to trade hats with you." So, Max gave Sam his red hat, and Sam gave Max his blue hat. Now, Max had a blue hat and Sam had a red hat. They were both very happy with their new hats. They played together all day and became good friends. From that day on, Max and Sam always wore their new hats and played together in the park.

One day, a boy named Tim found a rough screw on the ground. He picked it up and looked at it. It was a big screw with a funny shape. Tim showed it to his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam! I found a screw!" Tim said. Sam looked at the screw and thought it was cool. "Let's taste it!" Sam said. Tim thought it was a funny idea, but he wanted to try it too. So, they both licked the rough screw. It tasted yucky! Just then, a small bird flew down from the sky. It landed next to Tim and Sam, and it started to talk! "That's my screw!" the bird said. Tim and Sam were very surprised. They didn't know birds could talk! They gave the screw back to the bird, and it flew away. Tim and Sam laughed and went to play. They never forgot the day they met a talking bird and tasted a rough screw.

One day, a little girl named Amy went outside to play. She had a pet cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was very silly and sometimes did stupid things. Amy loved her pet cat and liked to play with her. Amy and Fluffy played hide and seek in the yard. Fluffy would hide and Amy would count. Then, Amy would try to find Fluffy. Fluffy was good at hiding, but sometimes she would peek out from her hiding spot. When Amy saw Fluffy peek, she would laugh and run to find her. After playing hide and seek, Amy and Fluffy were tired. They sat under a big tree to rest. Fluffy lay down in Amy's lap and soon fell asleep. Amy looked at her pet cat and smiled. She knew that even if Fluffy did stupid things sometimes, she was the best pet in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue who loved to dress up. She had a big box full of pretty clothes. One day, Sue wanted to play with her friend Tim. But Tim was not home. Sue was impatient. She wanted to play now! So, she decided to make a new friend. She saw a bird on the lawn. Sue said, "Hi, bird! Do you want to play dress-up with me?" The bird looked at Sue and said, "Yes, I would love to play dress-up!" So, they played dress-up together. Sue put a small hat on the bird. They had so much fun and became best friends.

One day, a pink rat named Rosie was playing in the park. She loved to run and jump on the green grass. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Rosie was very happy. Suddenly, it started to get cold. The sun went away and the sky turned gray. Rosie felt a chill and said, "Brrr, I'm cold!" Then, everything started to freeze. The grass, the trees, and even the water in the pond turned to ice. A big bear named Ben saw Rosie shivering and said, "Don't worry, Rosie! I can help you." Ben took Rosie to his warm cave. Rosie was surprised but very thankful. Inside the cave, they became good friends and played together until the ice outside melted. The sun came back, and Rosie and Ben went outside to play in the park again.

On Tuesday, Sam and Lily went to the park with Mom. They liked to play on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. The sun was mild and the sky was blue. They had a lot of fun. "Look, Mom, I can go very high!" Sam said, as he pushed the swing with his legs. "Be careful, Sam, don't go too fast!" Mom said, as she watched him with a smile. Lily wanted to try the slide. She climbed up the ladder and sat on the top. She noticed a big bee on the slide. She was scared of bees. She screamed and tried to get away. "Help, Mom, help!" Lily cried, as she slid down the slide. Mom heard Lily and ran to her. She saw the bee on the slide. She tried to shoo it away, but it was too late. The bee stung Lily on her arm. Lily felt a sharp pain and started to cry. "Ow, ow, ow, it hurts, Mom, it hurts!" Lily sobbed, as she hugged Mom. Mom felt sorry for Lily. She took her to the bench and looked at her arm. It was red and swollen. She said, "I'm sorry, Lily, you have a bee sting. We need to go home and put some ice on it. It will make it feel better." Sam stopped swinging and came to them. He saw Lily's arm and felt sad. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily, I didn't see the bee. Are you okay?" Lily shook her head and said, "No, I'm not okay. I don't like bees. I don't like the park. I want to go home." Mom said, "Okay, let's go home. Maybe we can come back another day, when there are no bees." They left the park and walked to the car. Lily still cried and Sam still felt sad. They didn't have fun anymore. It was a bad Tuesday.
