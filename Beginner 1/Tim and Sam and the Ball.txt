Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Ball

Tim and Sam like to play hockey. They have sticks and a ball. They play on the grass near their house. They have fun and laugh. One day, a big boy comes. He has a stick and a ball too. He says he wants to play hockey with them. Tim and Sam say yes. They think he is nice. But the big boy is not nice. He hits the ball very hard. He does not share. He does not let Tim and Sam touch the ball. He says he is the best. He says they are bad. Tim and Sam are sad and angry. They want to play too. They want to have fun. They tell the big boy to stop. They tell him to go away. The big boy does not listen. He laughs at them. He says they are too thin and weak. He says they cannot race him. He says he is faster and stronger. Tim and Sam are tired of the big boy. They decide to race him. They think they can beat him. They say they are ready. They say they will show him. The big boy agrees. He says he will race them to the tree. He says he will win. He says they will lose. They start to race. They run as fast as they can. They try to catch the big boy. But the big boy is too fast. He runs ahead of them. He reaches the tree first. He turns around and smiles. Tim and Sam are close behind. They do not see the big boy. They do not see the stick. They do not see the ball. They only see the tree. The big boy swings his stick. He hits the ball. He aims at Tim and Sam. He wants to hurt them. He wants to make them cry. The ball flies in the air. It hits Tim in the head. It hits Sam in the chest. They fall to the ground. They scream in pain. They bleed and cry. The big boy laughs. He says he won. He says they lost. He says they are stupid and slow. He says they are not good at hockey. He says they are not good at anything. He picks up his stick and his ball. He walks away. He does not help them. He does not say sorry. He does not care. Tim and Sam are alone. They are hurt and scared. They hug each other. They wish the big boy never came. They wish they never raced him. They wish they could play hockey again. They wish they could have fun again.

---

Ben was sick. He had a bad cough and a hot head. He did not want to play or eat. He only wanted to sleep. His mom was worried. She gave him some medicine to make him feel better. "Drink this, Ben. It will help you stop coughing and cool your head," she said. Ben did not like the medicine. It was bitter and sticky. He made a face and spat it out. "Yuck! I don't want it! It's gross!" he said. His mom was patient. She hugged him and spoke in a calm voice. "I know, Ben. It's not yummy. But you need it to get well. If you drink it, you will end your sickness soon. And then you can play and eat and have fun again. Don't you want that?" she said. Ben thought about it. He did want to play and eat and have fun. He did not want to be sick anymore. He nodded his head. "Okay, mom. I will try it. But can I have some juice after?" he said. His mom smiled and kissed his cheek. "Of course, Ben. You can have some juice after. You are a brave boy. I'm proud of you," she said. She gave him the medicine again. This time, Ben swallowed it. He made a face, but he did not spit it out. He drank some juice to wash it down. He felt a little better. He snuggled with his mom and closed his eyes. "Thank you, mom. I love you," he said. "I love you too, Ben. Sleep well. You will be better soon," she said.
