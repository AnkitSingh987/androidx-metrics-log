Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Rock

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She liked to see new things. The sun was shining and it was hot outside. Lily put on her hat and went to the park. In the park, Lily saw a shiny object on the ground. She picked it up and looked at it. It was a pretty, round rock. Lily liked the object and put it in her pocket. Lily went home and showed her mom the object. Her mom said, "Be careful, it might be hot from the sun." Lily touched the rock and it was not too hot. She smiled and put the rock with her other special things.

---

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. They saw a big bush with many small round things on it. They were blue and purple and sweet. "Look, blueberries!" Lily said. "Let's pick some and eat them." They ran to the bush and started to pick the blueberries. They put them in their mouths and smiled. They were juicy and yummy. But then they heard a growl. It was a big dog. It had black fur and sharp teeth. It was angry and hungry. It wanted the blueberries too. "Go away, dog!" Ben shouted. "These are our blueberries!" The dog did not listen. It jumped on the bush and tried to grab the blueberries. Ben and Lily were scared. They ran away from the bush and looked for a place to hide. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. They ran to the tree and crawled into the hole. It was dark and cozy. They hugged each other and hoped the dog would not find them. They heard the dog barking and sniffing outside. It smelled the blueberries on their hands and faces. It wanted to get into the hole and eat them. "Shh, don't make a sound," Lily whispered. "Maybe it will go away." They stayed very quiet and still. They rubbed their eyes and wished they were home. They wondered if their mom and dad were looking for them. They waited and waited. The dog did not go away. It scratched and bit the tree. It made a lot of noise. But then they heard another sound. It was a whistle. It was their dad. He had come to find them. "Ben, Lily, where are you?" he called. "I have a surprise for you." Ben and Lily were happy to hear his voice. They crawled out of the hole and ran to him. They hugged him and told him about the dog and the blueberries. Their dad looked at the dog and shook his head. He said, "That's not a dog, that's a bear. A very naughty bear. He should not be here. He should be in the forest." He took out a big stick and waved it at the bear. He said, "Go away, bear. Leave the blueberries alone. They are for the children, not for you." The bear was afraid of the stick and the dad. He ran away into the woods. He did not come back. Ben and Lily were glad the bear was gone. They thanked their dad for saving them. They asked him what the surprise was. He smiled and said, "I have a big basket of blueberries for you. I picked them from another bush. They are bigger and sweeter than the ones you found. Do you want some?" Ben and Lily nodded and cheered. They followed their dad to the basket and took some blueberries. They ate them and smiled. They were juicy and yummy. They said, "We love blueberries. And we love you, dad." Their dad said, "I love you too, Ben and Lily. You are my brave and clever children. But next time, be careful where you play. And don't go near the bears."

---

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with blocks. They make towers and houses and cars with the blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. One day, Tom sees a big square block that is red. He likes red. He wants the square block for his tower. But Sam is using the square block for his house. Tom asks Sam, "Can I have the square block, please?" Sam says, "No, I need it for my house. You have many other blocks. You can use them for your tower." Tom is angry. He does not want to use other blocks. He wants the square block. He thinks of a plan. He says to Sam, "Look, there is a big wet puddle outside. Do you want to jump in it?" Sam likes to jump in puddles. He says, "Yes, that sounds fun. Let's go!" Tom and Sam go outside. They see the big wet puddle. Sam runs to the puddle and jumps in it. He splashes and laughs. He is happy. Tom does not jump in the puddle. He runs back to the blocks. He takes the square block from Sam's house. He puts it on his tower. He is happy. But then, Sam comes back. He sees his house. He sees the square block on Tom's tower. He is sad. He says to Tom, "Hey, you took my square block. That is not nice. That is not fair. You lied to me." Tom says, "I did not lie. I just wanted the square block. You have many other blocks. You can use them for your house." Sam says, "But the square block was mine. You asked me and I said no. You should not take things that are not yours. You should not trick your friends. That is not how friends play. That is how enemies play." Tom feels bad. He sees that Sam is right. He says, "I am sorry, Sam. You are right. I should not have taken your square block. I should not have tricked you. That was wrong. Can you forgive me?" Sam says, "Yes, I can forgive you. But you have to give me back my square block. And you have to promise to not do that again. And you have to play with me in the puddle. That is how friends play. That is how friends have fun." Tom says, "OK, Sam. I will give you back your square block. I promise to not do that again. And I will play with you in the puddle. Thank you for being my friend." Tom and Sam hug. They give each other their blocks. They go to the puddle. They jump and splash and laugh. They are happy. The end.
