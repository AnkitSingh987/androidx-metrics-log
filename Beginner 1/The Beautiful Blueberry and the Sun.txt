Category: Beginner
Title: The Beautiful Blueberry and the Sun

Once there was a beautiful blueberry in a garden. It grew big and plump in the sun. One day, it started to rain and the blueberry got really wet. All the raindrops soaked into the blueberry until it was full. The blueberry was so heavy that it got stuck on the branch. It was too cold for the blueberry to stay on the branch, so the sun came out and shone slowly. The warmth of the sun slowly helped the blueberry to dry out and soon it was not stuck anymore. The blueberry slowly grew back to its original size and was happy to be free!

Once upon a time, in a lovely little town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside with her friends. They liked to run, jump, and laugh all day. Lily's favorite place to play was near the well in the middle of the town. One sunny day, Lily and her friends were playing by the well. They were having so much fun! They played catch and hide-and-seek. Lily loved the well because it was so pretty. It had lovely flowers and green grass all around it. As the day went on, the sun began to set. Lily and her friends knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye to each other and to the lovely well. Lily could not wait to play by the well again the next day. She knew she would always love the well and the fun times she had with her friends.

Sam and Mia liked to play at the gym. They liked to jump, run, slide and swing. But they did not like to share. They always wanted the same toy or the same spot. One day, they found a big ball at the gym. It was red and shiny and bouncy. Sam and Mia both wanted the ball. They ran to grab it, but they bumped into each other. The ball flew out of their hands and rolled away. "Hey, that's my ball!" Sam shouted. "No, it's mine!" Mia yelled. They chased the ball, but they did not see where it went. They looked everywhere, but they could not find it. They started to cry. "Where is the ball?" Sam asked. "I don't know. Maybe someone took it." Mia said. They felt sad and angry. They blamed each other for losing the ball. Meanwhile, Tom and Lily saw the ball rolling by. They liked the ball too. It was red and shiny and bouncy. They picked it up and played with it. They had fun. They bounced the ball, threw the ball, and caught the ball. They laughed and smiled. But then they saw Sam and Mia crying. They felt sorry for them. They knew they were looking for the ball. "Maybe we should give them the ball." Tom said. "But we like the ball too." Lily said. "Yes, but they had it first. And they are sad. Sharing is nice." Tom said. Lily nodded. She agreed. Sharing was nice. They walked to Sam and Mia and gave them the ball. "Here, this is your ball. We found it." Tom said. "Really? Thank you!" Sam said. "Wow, you are so kind!" Mia said. They were happy. They stopped crying. They smiled. "Would you like to play with us?" Sam asked. "Sure, that would be fun!" Lily said. They played together. They bounced the ball, threw the ball, and caught the ball. They laughed and smiled. They learned to share. Sharing was nice. The end.

One day, a bird named Bob wanted to fly to a new place. Bob was a bald bird, so he felt cold when he flew. Before he left, his friend Sue said, "Bob, take this hat to keep you warm!" Bob smiled and put the hat on his head. As Bob flew, he saw many beautiful things. He saw a big tree, a red house, and a pretty lake. He felt happy and warm with his hat on. But then, a strong wind came and took his hat away. Bob felt sad and cold. He thought he heard Sue's voice in the wind, "Bob, you can do it!" Bob didn't give up. He flew faster and faster. Suddenly, he saw his hat on a small dog's head. The dog was lost and cold too. Bob said, "Hi, little dog! I can help you find your home if you give me my hat back." The dog agreed, and they became friends. Together, they flew to find the dog's home and a new place for Bob.
