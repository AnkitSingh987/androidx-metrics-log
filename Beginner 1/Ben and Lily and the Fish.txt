Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Fish

Ben and Lily went to the park with Mom. They saw a big pond with many fish. Ben wanted to feed the fish. He had some bread in his pocket. "Mom, can I give the bread to the fish?" Ben asked. Mom nodded. "Yes, but be careful. Don't go too close to the water." Ben broke the bread into small pieces and threw them into the pond. The fish came to eat the bread. They made bubbles and splashes. Ben laughed. He liked the fish. Lily wanted to touch the fish. She walked to the edge of the pond. She reached out her hand. She saw a shiny fish with a long tail. "Come here, fish!" Lily said. "I want to pet you." But the fish was scared of Lily. It swam away. Lily was sad. She tried to catch another fish. She leaned over the water. Ben saw Lily. He was worried. He knew the water was deep and cold. He did not want Lily to fall in. "Lily, stop!" Ben urged. "You can't touch the fish. They don't like it. You will scare them. And you will get wet." But Lily did not listen. She wanted to touch the fish. She leaned more and more over the water. Suddenly, a harsh wind blew. It pushed Lily off balance. She lost her grip and fell into the pond. She screamed. The water was cold and dark. She could not see the fish. She could not breathe. Ben ran to Lily. He grabbed her hand and pulled her out of the pond. He hugged her. He was scared and angry. "Lily, why did you do that?" Ben said. "I told you to stop. You could have drowned. You are silly and naughty." Lily cried. She was wet and cold. She was sorry and ashamed. "Ben, I'm sorry," Lily said. "I just wanted to touch the fish. They were so pretty. I didn't mean to fall in. Please don't be mad at me." Ben looked at Lily. He saw her tears and shivers. He felt sorry for her. He loved his sister. "OK, Lily, I'm not mad at you," Ben said. "But you have to listen to me. I'm your big brother. I want to keep you safe. And you have to listen to Mom. She knows what's best for us." Lily nodded. She hugged Ben. She said thank you. Mom came to Ben and Lily. She was worried and relieved. She wrapped them in a big towel. She kissed them and said they were brave and good. She said they had to go home and change their clothes. She said they could come back to the park another day and watch the fish from a safe distance. Ben and Lily agreed. They said goodbye to the fish. They walked with Mom to the car. They learned their lesson. They were happy to be together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a pencil. Tim liked his pencil very much. It was a rich, red pencil. Tim's pencil could draw many things. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue did not have a pencil. She was sad. Tim wanted to help. He said, "Let's fill this paper with our drawings. I will share my pencil with you." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim and Sue drew many things together. They filled the paper with happy pictures. They laughed and had fun. They became good friends. The moral of the story is to share what you have with others. Sharing makes everyone happy.

One day, a rich man named Tom went to his office. He had a big office with many toys. Tom liked to play with his toys when he was not working. In the office next to him, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys too. One day, Tom heard a loud scream. It was Sue. He went to see what was wrong. Sue was sad because her favorite toy was stuck in a high place. She could not reach it. Tom wanted to help Sue, but he was too short too. Tom had an idea. He said, "Sue, let's use my big toy car to reach your toy!" Sue agreed, and they pushed the big toy car to the high place. Tom climbed on the car and got Sue's toy back. Sue was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" From that day on, Tom and Sue became good friends. They played with their toys together and always helped each other when they needed it.
