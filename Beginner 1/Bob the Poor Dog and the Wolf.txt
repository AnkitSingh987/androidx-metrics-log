Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Poor Dog and the Wolf

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a poor dog named Bob. Bob was always hungry and looking for food. One day, he found a tool in the grass. He thought it might be something to chew. Bob took the tool to his friend, Cat. "Can I chew this?" he asked. Cat looked at the tool and said, "No, Bob, that is not for chewing. That is a tool for fixing things." Bob was sad but still very hungry. He went to the forest to look for food. There, he saw a big, mean wolf. The wolf saw the tool in Bob's mouth and wanted it for himself. "Give me that tool," growled the wolf. Bob was scared but didn't want to give up the tool. He tried to run away, but the wolf was too fast. The wolf caught Bob and took the tool away from him. Poor Bob was left alone in the forest, still hungry and sad.

One day, a bird named Tim and a squirrel named Sam were in the park. Tim saw a grill and wanted to play with it. Sam told Tim, "No, it's not safe to play with a grill." Tim did not listen and acted foolish. He tried to rise up and sit on the grill. But the grill was hot! Tim got scared and started to cry. Sam heard Tim cry and ran to help him. Sam said, "I told you not to play with the grill!" Tim said, "I'm sorry, I was foolish." Sam hugged Tim and they went to play with their toys. They learned to always listen to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big temple. It was incredible. Many people went to the temple to hope for good things. One day, a boy named Tim went to the temple with his mom. They wanted to hope for a new toy for Tim. While they were at the temple, a big wind came. It was very strong. The wind blew Tim's hat away. Tim was sad. He did not want to lose his hat. His mom told him to hope for the hat to come back. Tim hoped very hard for his hat to come back. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew harder and harder. The incredible temple started to shake. The people at the temple were scared. They all ran away. Tim and his mom ran too. They left the temple and went home. The incredible temple was not there anymore. The wind had blown it away. Tim was sad because he lost his hat and the temple was gone. It was a bad day for everyone.

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys in the living room. But sometimes they fight over the same toy. One day, they both want to play with the big red pot. The pot is shiny and makes a loud noise when you bang it. Ben says, "The pot is mine! I saw it first!" Sam says, "No, the pot is mine! I like it more!" They pull and tug on the pot. They yell and cry. They do not share. Mom hears the noise. She comes to the living room. She sees Ben and Sam fighting over the pot. She is not happy. She says, "Stop! What are you doing? Why are you fighting over the pot?" Ben and Sam stop. They look at Mom. They do not know what to say. Mom questions them. She says, "Who had the pot first? How can you share the pot? How can you be nice to each other?" Ben and Sam think. They remember that Mom is dependable. She always helps them when they have a problem. They decide to tell the truth. They say, "We both wanted the pot. We did not ask. We did not listen. We are sorry." Mom smiles. She says, "Thank you for being honest. I know you both like the pot. But you can take turns. You can also play with other toys. You can have fun together. You are brothers. You love each other." Ben and Sam nod. They say, "Yes, Mom. You are right. We love each other. We can share the pot. We can play nicely." They hug Mom. They hug each other. They pick up the pot. They take turns banging it. They laugh and smile. They are happy.
