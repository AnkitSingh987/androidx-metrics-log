Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Woods

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. The sky was gloomy and dark. Tim saw a big tree and heard a loud noise. It made him worry. He thought, "What could make such a noise?" Then, a small bird flew out of the tree. The bird said, "Hello, Tim! I did not mean to scare you. I was just playing in the tree." Tim felt better and said, "That's okay, bird. I was just worried about the noise." They both walked in the woods together. They saw pretty flowers and played with the leaves. The gloomy day became fun and bright. Tim and the bird became good friends, and they were not scared or worried anymore.

Ben and Mia are playing with blocks in the living room. They are building a big tower with red, yellow, green and blue blocks. Mia likes the blue blocks the most. She wants to put them all on the top of the tower. "Can I have that blue block, please?" Mia asks Ben, pointing to a block in his hand. "No, this is mine. You have enough blue blocks already," Ben answers. He does not want to share his block with Mia. Mia feels sad and angry. She tries to grab the block from Ben's hand. Ben pulls his hand away and pushes Mia with his arm. Mia falls down and bumps her head on the floor. She starts to cry. "Ow, ow, ow! That hurts!" Mia cries. She holds her head with her hand. Mom hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees Mia crying and Ben looking scared. She picks up Mia and hugs her. "What happened here?" Mom asks. "Mia wanted my blue block and I said no and she tried to take it and I pushed her and she fell and hit her head," Ben answers quickly. Mom looks at Ben and shakes her head. She sees a bump on Mia's head. She gets some ice and puts it on the bump. "That was not nice, Ben. You should share your blocks with Mia. She is your sister and you should be kind to her. And you should not push her or hurt her. That is very bad," Mom says. "I'm sorry, Mom. I'm sorry, Mia. I didn't mean to hurt you. Can I give you my blue block?" Ben says. He feels sorry for Mia and for what he did. Mia stops crying and looks at Ben. She sees the blue block in his hand. She smiles and nods. "Thank you, Ben. That is very nice of you. I'm sorry, too. I should not try to take your block. Can we play together?" Mia says. Ben smiles and nods. He gives Mia the blue block and hugs her. "Yes, we can play together. I love you, Mia. You are my sister and you are my friend," Ben says. Mom smiles and kisses them both. "I love you, too, Ben and Mia. You are my children and you are my friends. Now, let's finish your tower. It looks very pretty with all the colors," Mom says. They all sit on the floor and play with the blocks. They make the tower taller and taller. They use all the red, yellow, green and blue blocks. They are happy and have fun.

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys in their room, but their favorite thing was to read books. They had a big shelf full of books with pictures and stories. One day, their mom said, "It's time to read, kids. You can choose one book each and sit on the armchair with me. I will read to you." Lily and Max ran to the shelf and looked at the books. They had a hard time choosing, because they liked so many books. Lily saw a book about a princess and a dragon. She liked princesses and dragons. She took the book and said, "I choose this one, mom." Max saw a book about a pirate and a treasure. He liked pirates and treasures. He took the book and said, "I choose this one, mom." They both ran to the armchair with their books. But there was a problem. The armchair was not big enough for mom and two kids and two books. They tried to squeeze in, but they bumped into each other and dropped their books. "Oops!" Lily said. "Ouch!" Max said. Mom smiled and said, "Maybe we can take turns. One of you can sit on the armchair with me and read your book, and the other can sit on the floor and look at your book. Then we can switch. How does that sound?" Lily and Max thought about it. They both wanted to sit on the armchair with mom, but they also wanted to be fair. They looked at each other and nodded. "OK, mom," they said. They decided to play rock-paper-scissors to see who would go first. Lily made a rock, and Max made a paper. Paper covers rock, so Max won. "Yay! I go first!" Max said. He climbed on the armchair with mom and his pirate book. Lily sat on the floor with her princess book. She was a little sad, but she knew she would have her turn soon. Mom opened Max's book and started to read. She made funny voices for the pirate and the parrot. Max listened and laughed. He pointed at the pictures and asked questions. Mom answered and explained. Lily looked at her book and tried to read. She knew some of the words, but not all of them. She made up her own story in her head. She imagined she was the princess and the dragon was her friend. She smiled and turned the pages. After a while, mom said, "OK, Max, it's time to switch. Lily, do you want to sit on the armchair with me and read your book?" Max and Lily nodded. They put their books on the floor and switched places. Max sat on the floor with his pirate book, and Lily climbed on the armchair with mom and her princess book. Mom opened Lily's book and started to read. She made sweet voices for the princess and the dragon. Lily listened and smiled. She pointed at the pictures and told mom her story. Mom nodded and praised. Max looked at his book and tried to read. He knew some of the words, but not all of them. He made up his own story in his head. He imagined he was the pirate and the treasure was his. He grinned and turned the pages. Mom, Lily, and Max had a lot of fun reading their books. They learned new words and new things. They used their imagination and their creativity. They shared their stories and their feelings. They were happy and cozy on the armchair and on the floor. They loved reading and they loved each other.
