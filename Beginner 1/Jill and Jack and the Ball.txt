Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and Jack and the Ball

Jill and Jack like to play in the yard. The yard is big and green and has many trees. Some of the trees are very old and have holes in them. Jill and Jack call them ancient trees. One day, Jill and Jack find a big ball in the yard. It is red and shiny and bouncy. They want to play with it. They take turns to throw it and catch it. They laugh and have fun. But then, Jack throws the ball too hard. It flies over the fence and lands in the street. Jill and Jack run to the fence and look for the ball. They see a car coming. The car is fast and loud and scary. "Look out, ball!" Jill shouts. The car hits the ball. The ball pops and makes a loud noise. The car stops and the driver gets out. He is angry and says bad words. "Hey, you kids! What are you doing? This is my ball! You broke it!" he yells. Jill and Jack are scared and sad. They did not know the ball was his. They did not mean to break it. They say sorry to the driver. The driver is still angry. He takes the ball and goes back to his car. He drives away and leaves Jill and Jack alone. Jill and Jack go back to the yard. They do not want to play anymore. They sit under an ancient tree and hug each other. "Maybe we can find another ball," Jack says. "Maybe," Jill says. They look at the sky and see a rainbow. It is bright and beautiful and makes them smile. "Maybe the rainbow is a sign," Jill says. "A sign of what?" Jack asks. "A sign that everything will be okay," Jill says. They hope she is right. They hold hands and watch the rainbow. They feel a little better.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a cap he would always keep on his head. The cap was dark, like the night sky. Tim loved his cap very much. He wore it when he played outside and when he went to bed. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his cap on. While he played, the cap fell off his head. Tim looked and looked, but he could not find his cap. He was sad and went home without it. That night, Tim went to bed without his cap. When he woke up, he found his cap on his head! It turns out, his mom found the cap in his bed. She put it back on his head while he slept. Tim was so happy to have his cap back, and he gave his mom a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Kitty. Kitty had a big bag. One day, Kitty wanted to go on a trip. So, she started to pack her bag. She put her toys and food in the bag. Kitty's friend, Doggy, saw her packing. Doggy asked, "Kitty, where are you going?" Kitty said, "I am going on a trip to find a new place to play." Doggy wanted to go too. So, Kitty waited for Doggy to pack his bag. When they were ready, Kitty and Doggy walked and walked. They saw a big tree on the left. Kitty said, "Let's play here!" They played all day under the big tree. They were so happy they went on the trip together.

One day, a small train was going on a trip. It was a sunny day, and the train was happy. The train went through a big puddle and got all wet. The train was now soaked. The train felt uncomfortable because it was so wet. It said to a bird, "I am wet and uncomfortable. Can you help me?" The bird said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's find some sun to dry you off." The train and the bird found a sunny spot. The train sat in the sun and soon, it was dry. The train felt better. It said, "Thank you, bird, for helping me." The bird said, "You're welcome, train. Have a nice trip!" And the train went on its way, happy and dry.
