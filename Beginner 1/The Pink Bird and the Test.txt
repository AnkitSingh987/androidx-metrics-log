Category: Beginner
Title: The Pink Bird and the Test

Once upon a time there was a pink bird. He flew as high as he could and kept going higher and higher. The bird wanted more than anything to pass the big test. The big test was hard and the bird had to work hard to pass it. He flew all around and asked the other birds for help. Everyone was scared of the big test, but the pink bird didn't give up. At last the test day came. The pink bird flew really fast, because he wanted to pass more than anything. The other birds saw him and said: "You can do it! Just keep flying!" And so he did! He worked hard and at last he passed the test. Everyone was happy and cheered for the pink bird. He had done it! He had achieved success!

Once upon a time, there was an ancient tree. It was very big and old. Near the tree, there was a hanger. In the hanger, there was a big red ball. One day, a little boy came to the hanger. He wanted to play with the big red ball. So, he tried to fill the ball with air. He wanted the ball to be very big and bouncy. But, the ball got too big. It bounced and hit the ancient tree. The tree fell down and broke the hanger. The little boy was sad. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He was very happy because he was going to receive a new toy. At the store, Tim saw a warm, soft teddy bear that he really wanted. But his mom said he could not have it. Tim was sad. Then, Tim saw a big box of soap. He had an idea! He put the soap on the floor and waited. When his mom walked by, she slipped on the soap and fell down. Tim thought she would be mad, but she just laughed. Tim's mom saw how much fun he had with the soap, and she let him receive the warm teddy bear. They both laughed and played with the soap and the teddy bear all day. And in the end, the soap was more fun than the toy Tim wanted.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the color purple. She had a purple dress, purple shoes, and even a purple room. Sue was very happy because today was her birthday. She was going to have a big party with all her friends. Sue's mom and dad worked hard to make the party fun. They had purple balloons, a purple cake, and even purple hats for everyone. Sue's friends came to the party, and they all wore something purple too. They played games, ate cake, and had a lot of fun. But then, something bad happened. Sue was running to get more cake when she slipped on a purple balloon. She fell down and started to cry. Her friends tried to help her, but Sue was very sad. The party was not fun anymore. Everyone went home, and Sue's birthday ended with tears.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk on the road. It was a calm day, and the sun was shining. Tim saw a big, red ball on the road. He wanted to play with the ball. "Can I play with the ball?" Tim asked a girl named Sue who was standing near the ball. "No, it is my ball. You can't play with it," Sue said. Tim was sad. He walked away and found a stick. He played with the stick and had fun. Later, Sue's ball rolled away and got stuck in a tree. Sue was sad and wanted her ball back. Tim saw Sue was sad and said, "I can help you get your ball." He used the stick to get the ball out of the tree. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! Now we can both play with the ball." Tim and Sue played with the ball and had a lot of fun. They became good friends and always played together on the calm road.
