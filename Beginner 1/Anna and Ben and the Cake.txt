Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Cake

Anna and Ben were playing with their toys on the floor. They liked to make noises and pretend they were cars and animals. They did not hear their mom calling them for lunch. "Anna, Ben, come to the kitchen!" mom said again. She was worried. Why were they so quiet? She walked to their room and opened the door. She saw a big mess on the floor. Toys, books, clothes, and papers were everywhere. She also saw Anna and Ben hiding under the bed. They looked scared. "What are you doing?" mom asked. She was angry. "Why did you make such a mess? And why did you not answer me?" Anna and Ben did not say anything. They hoped mom would go away. But she did not. She came closer to the bed and lifted the blanket. She saw something else on the floor. It was a cake. "Is that my cake?" mom asked. She was shocked. "The cake I made for dad's birthday? The cake I told you not to touch?" Anna and Ben nodded. They felt bad. They wanted to surprise dad with the cake. They wanted to put candles and sprinkles on it. But they dropped the cake on the floor. And they spilled the milk and the juice. And they broke the plate and the glass. Mom was very upset. She did not like surprises like this. She did not like when Anna and Ben did not listen to her. She did not like when they lied to her. She took the cake and threw it in the trash. She told Anna and Ben to clean up the mess. She told them they were in big trouble. She told them they could not have any cake or any fun for a long time. Anna and Ben cried. They were sorry. They did not mean to make mom angry. They did not mean to ruin dad's birthday. They did not mean to be bad. But it was too late. Mom did not forgive them. Dad did not see them. They had no cake and no fun. They had a bad day. They had a bad ending.

One day, a little bird wanted to fly high in the sky. But the wind was harsh and cold. The bird's friend, a wise old owl, told the bird, "It is not a good day to fly. The wind is too strong." The little bird said, "I want to fly and see the world. I will wear clothes to keep warm." So the bird put on a warm hat and a scarf. The bird felt warm and happy in the clothes. The bird tried to fly, but the harsh wind blew the clothes away. The bird was cold and sad. The bird flew back to the wise old owl and said, "You were right. It was not a good day to fly." The bird stayed on the ground, cold and sad, and wished it had listened to the wise old owl.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tiny. Tiny was very small and fragile. He lived in a big house with a big yard. Tiny loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tiny's owner put a leash on him and took him to the park. At the park, Tiny saw a swing. He wanted to play on the swing, but he was too small. Tiny's friend, a big dog named Max, came to help him. Max helped Tiny onto the swing. Tiny held onto the leash while Max pushed him. Tiny swung back and forth, laughing and having fun. They played on the swing all day until it was time to go home. Tiny and Max were happy to have had such a fun day at the park.

Once there was a boy who liked to jump. He jumped every day, and it was so fun. One day he thought he would like to jump higher than ever before. He put on his zipper and ran to the park. He saw a tree and decided to jump from it. He jumped and jumped and jumped, but he didn't make it all the way. He got stuck on a branch. It was so sweet, but he couldn't get down. He was stuck for a long time and it was scary. Finally, the firefighters came and were able to help him down from the tree. But he was so tired from all the jumping that he had to go home and rest. So, he never got to jump again.
