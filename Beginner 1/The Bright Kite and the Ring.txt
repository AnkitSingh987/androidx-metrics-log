Category: Beginner
Title: The Bright Kite and the Ring

Once upon a time, there was a bright kite. The kite was red, blue, and yellow. It liked to fly high in the sky. The sun made the kite's colors shine. The kite felt happy when it was up in the air. One day, a little boy named Sam found the bright kite. He wanted to make it fly. Sam took the kite to a big hill. The wind was strong. Sam ran with the kite. The kite went up, up, up. Sam laughed and clapped his hands. As the kite flew, it made a fun sound. It went "ring, ring, ring." The bright kite loved to ring. Sam and the kite played all day. They had a lot of fun together. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to draw with her pastel colors. One day, she wanted to draw a big rainbow on her wall. But she was worried. She did not know how big to make the rainbow. Amy had an idea. She would measure the wall to know how big to make the rainbow. She used her hands to measure from one side to the other. She counted one, two, three, four, five hands across the wall. Now that she knew how big to make her rainbow, Amy was not worried anymore. She took her pastel colors and started to draw. She drew a big, beautiful rainbow on her wall. Amy was very happy with her rainbow, and her room looked very pretty.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the park. They see a big pond with many ducks. They want to feed the ducks some bread. "Look, Sam, a light duck!" Tim says. He points to a duck with white feathers and a yellow beak. "He is so pretty!" "Yes, he is, Tim. But be careful. Ducks can snap with their mouths. They can hurt you," Sam says. He breaks a piece of bread and throws it to the duck. The light duck swims to the bread and eats it. He makes a quack sound. Tim and Sam laugh. They throw more bread to the duck and his friends. Tim wants to touch the light duck. He walks closer to the pond. He reaches out his hand to the duck. The duck sees Tim's hand. He thinks it is food. He snaps with his mouth. "Ow!" Tim cries. He pulls back his hand. He has a red mark on his finger. "The duck bit me!" "Tim, are you okay?" Sam asks. He runs to Tim and hugs him. "I told you to be careful. Ducks can snap with their mouths." "I'm sorry, Sam. I just wanted to touch the duck. He looked so soft and nice," Tim says. He rubs his finger. He feels sad. "It's okay, Tim. The duck didn't mean to hurt you. He was just hungry. Maybe he thought your finger was a worm," Sam says. He smiles at Tim. "Let's go to mom. She can make your finger better." "Okay, Sam. Thank you for being a good brother," Tim says. He smiles back at Sam. They hold hands and walk to mom. They say goodbye to the ducks. The light duck quacks again. He is sorry too.

Once upon a time, there was a long line of ants. They were marching to a big party. At the party, they would play games, dance, and clap for their friends. One little ant named Tim saw a game of chess on the ground. He did not know how to play, but it looked fun. He asked his friend Sam, "Do you know how to play chess?" Sam said, "No, but we can learn together." So, Tim and Sam tried to play chess. They moved the pieces and had fun. When they were done, they clapped for each other. They were happy they tried something new. And they danced at the party with all their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little seed. The little seed wanted to become a big sunflower. It was cold outside, so the seed waited in the ground. One day, the sun came out and it was warm. The seed started to grow. It grew and grew until it was a big sunflower. The sunflower felt happy in the sun. A little girl saw the sunflower and said, "Hello, big sunflower! You are so pretty!" The sunflower felt proud and happy. Now, it waved to the little girl every day. And they were both happy.
