Category: Beginner
Title: The Curious Elephant and the Leaves

One day, a curious little elephant went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. The little elephant wanted to touch the leaves, but he was too small. He felt sad. A kind bird saw the sad little elephant and asked, "Why are you sad? What can I do to help?" The little elephant said, "I want to touch the leaves, but I am too small. Can you help me?" The kind bird had an idea. She told the little elephant to stand on a big rock. The little elephant did as the bird said. Now he could reach the leaves! He was so happy and thanked the kind bird. They both played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play at the park. One day, he went to the park with his mom. It was a cold day, but Tim didn't care. He put on his warm coat and hat, and off they went. At the park, Tim saw a big slide. He was so excited to play on it. He ran to the slide and climbed up the steps. When he got to the top, he looked down and said, "Mom, guess what? I'm going to slide down now!" His mom smiled and said, "Go ahead, Tim. Have fun!" Tim sat down on the slide and slid all the way down. Whee! It was so much fun. He ran back up the steps and slid down again and again. Even though it was cold, Tim had a great time playing on the slide with his mom watching him. They stayed at the park until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect things. He had many toys and games. One day, Tim found a new game to play. It was called golf. Tim was very excited to try golf, but he was also a little nervous. He had never played golf before. Tim practiced golf every day. He got better and better. One day, Tim's mom told him there was a big golf game coming up. Tim was so happy! He wanted to play in the big game. But he was also nervous. He had never played in a big game before. On the day of the big game, Tim was very nervous. He tried his best to play golf. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Tim's golf ball far away. Tim was sad. But then, he saw that the wind had blown his ball into the hole! Tim had won the big golf game! He was so happy and not nervous anymore.

One day, a beautiful dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big tree and a small cat. The cat said, "Hi Spot, do you want to play a game? We can match things." Spot wagged his tail and said, "Yes, I want to play!" They started to play the match game. They matched a red ball with a red car, a blue cup with a blue hat, and a green frog with a green leaf. They were having so much fun. The cat said, "If we match all the things, we will get a treat!" They matched all the things, and they were so happy. They thought they would get a treat. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, mean dog came and took all the treats away. Spot and the cat were sad. Spot said, "We matched all the things, but we did not get a treat." The cat said, "Yes, it is not fair. The big, mean dog took our treats." They went home feeling sad, and they did not get a treat that day. Even though they did not get a treat, Spot and the cat still had fun playing the match game. They knew that they were good at matching things, and they hoped that next time, they would get a treat.

One day, a boy and a girl were playing in the garden. They had a ball and a bat. They liked to hit the ball and run after it. They had fun. But they were careless. They did not look where they hit the ball. They did not see the hedge. The hedge was green and prickly. It had many flowers and bees. The boy hit the ball very hard. The ball flew over the hedge. The girl ran after the ball. She did not see the hedge. She ran into the hedge. She hurt herself. She cried. "Ow, ow, ow!" she said. "The hedge is sharp. The bees are angry. The ball is gone. I want to go home." The boy heard her cry. He felt sorry. He ran to the hedge. He saw the girl. She had scratches and stings. He said, "I am sorry. I was careless. I did not mean to hit the ball over the hedge. Are you okay?" The girl looked at the boy. She was sad and sore. She said, "No, I am not okay. You were careless. You did not look where you hit the ball. You made me run into the hedge. You made me hurt myself. You made me lose the ball. You are not a good friend." The boy was sad too. He wanted to be a good friend. He wanted to make the girl happy. He said, "Please, do not be mad. I will get the ball back. I will be careful. I will look where I hit the ball. I will be a good friend." He looked over the hedge. He saw the ball. It was on the other side. It was near a big house. He said, "I see the ball. It is over there. I will get it for you. Wait here." He climbed over the hedge. He was careful. He did not hurt himself. He did not make the bees angry. He got the ball. He climbed back over the hedge. He gave the ball to the girl. He said, "Here is the ball. I got it for you. I am sorry. I hope you feel better." The girl took the ball. She looked at the boy. She saw that he was sorry. She saw that he was careful. She saw that he was a good friend. She smiled. She said, "Thank you. You got the ball for me. You are sorry. You are careful. You are a good friend." They hugged. They were happy. They played with the ball and the bat. They looked where they hit the ball. They did not hit the ball over the hedge. They did not run into the hedge. They did not hurt themselves. They did not make the bees angry. They had fun.
