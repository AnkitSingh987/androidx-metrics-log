Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Box

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play cops and robbers. Tom is the cop and Lily is the robber. They have a toy gun and a toy handcuff. They also have a big box. The box is the jail. One day, they play in the park. Tom says, "I will catch you, robber. You are bad." Lily says, "No, you will not catch me, cop. I am fast." They run and chase each other. They have fun. But then, Lily trips on a rock. She falls down. Tom catches up to her. He says, "Ha, I got you. You are going to jail." He puts the toy handcuff on her wrist. He picks up the toy gun. He says, "Come on, let's go to the jail." They walk to the big box. The box is near a tree. Tom opens the box. He says, "Get in, robber. This is your jail. You will stay here for a long time." He pushes Lily into the box. He closes the box. He says, "Bye, robber. Be quiet." Lily is in the box. She is sad. She does not like the jail. The jail is dark and small. She says, "Tom, let me out. This is not fun. I am sorry. I will be good." But Tom does not hear her. He is busy. He is preparing a picnic. He has a sandwich and a juice. He sits on the grass. He eats and drinks. He is happy. Lily is still in the box. She is angry. She does not like Tom. He is mean. She says, "Tom, let me out. This is not fair. I am hungry. I want a sandwich and a juice." But Tom does not hear her. He is noisy. He sings a song. He says, "I am the cop. I am the best. I caught the robber. She is in the jail." Lily is still in the box. She is bored. She does not like the jail. The jail is dull and lonely. She says, "Tom, let me out. This is not fun. I am bored. I want to play." But Tom does not hear her. He is sleepy. He lies on the grass. He closes his eyes. He says, "I am the cop. I am the best. I caught the robber. She is in the jail. Zzzz." Lily is still in the box. She is tired. She does not like the jail. The jail is hard and cold. She says, "Tom, let me out. This is not fun. I am tired. I want to go home." But Tom does not hear her. He is asleep. He does not say anything. Lily is still in the box. She is sad, angry, bored and tired. She does not like the jail. She does not like Tom. She does not like the game. She cries. She says, "Tom, let me out. Please, let me out. I want to be your friend." But Tom does not hear her. He does not wake up. He does not open the box. He does not let her out.

There was once a little boy named Andy who wanted to make sausages for his friend. He was so excited and went to get the ingredients from the supermarket. When Andy got home, he quickly started to prepare the sausages. Suddenly, he realised that he had forgotten to buy the sausage filling! Poor Andy started to weep as he had worked so hard and now all his efforts were wasted. Just then, his friend came around. His friend saw how sad Andy was and gave him a big hug. Then he said, “Don’t worry, Andy, let’s figure out something else to do together. We’ll make something amazing!” Andy sniffed and smiled, feeling much better. He and his friend went to the park and came up with a plan to make something amazing with things they found there. At the end, they made a delicious cake with some flowers! Andy was so proud of himself and realised that, even when things don’t turn out the way you planned, friends are there to help you figure out a new way. The moral of the story is: “With friends, even our mistakes can be amazing.”
