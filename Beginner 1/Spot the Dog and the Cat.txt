Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a chubby dog named Spot. Spot loved to count things. One day, Spot found a big chest in the park. The chest was full of colorful balls. Spot was very happy. Spot said to his friend, Cat, "Look at the big chest I found! Let's count the balls together!" Cat came over and they started to count. "One, two, three," they counted together. They counted all the balls in the chest. After they counted all the balls, Spot and Cat played with them. They had so much fun. They threw the balls, and Spot would catch them. The sun was shining, and they were very happy. At the end of the day, Spot and Cat put the balls back in the chest and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a clean cat named Tom. He liked to serve food to his friends. One day, he wanted to have a big party at his home. He was very happy and excited. Before the party, Tom cleaned his home and cooked a lot of yummy food. He put the food on a big table. His friends were coming soon, so he went to the top of the stair to look for them. While Tom was at the top of the stair, his little brother Tim came into the room. He saw the yummy food and thought it was for him. He started to eat all the food on the table. Tom saw this and ran down the stair to stop him. When Tom got to the bottom of the stair, he slipped on a toy and fell. The food was all gone. Tom was sad, but then his friends came in with more food. They all laughed and had a great party. Tom learned to always keep an eye on his little brother.

Once upon a time, there was a prince named Jack. He lived in a big castle with his family. Jack loved to play with his friends in the garden. One day, Jack got very filthy while playing. His clothes were dirty, and his face was messy. Jack's mom saw him and said, "Oh no, Jack! You are so filthy! You need to take a bath right now!" Jack did not want to take a bath. He started to cry. His mom tried to make him feel better. "Don't cry, Jack. After your bath, you can play again." Jack agreed and took a bath. His mom helped him wash his face and hands. After the bath, Jack felt clean and happy. He went back outside to play with his friends. They all had fun together. Later, Jack's friends went home, and it was time for dinner. Jack's mom called him inside. "Come, Jack! It's time to eat." Jack was hungry and ran inside. He ate his dinner with his family, and they all talked about their day. After dinner, Jack went to bed. He was tired from playing all day. His mom kissed him goodnight and said, "Sleep well, Prince Jack." Jack closed his eyes and fell asleep, dreaming of more fun days with his friends.

Tom and Sue are playing near the river. They like to throw stones in the water and watch the ripples. Tom finds a big black stone and shows it to Sue. "Look, Sue, I have a big black stone. It is heavy and hard. I will throw it in the river and make a big splash." Tom says. Sue looks at the stone and wants to touch it. She asks Tom, "Can I hold the stone, Tom? I want to feel it too." Tom nods and gives the stone to Sue. Sue holds the stone with both hands and feels how heavy and hard it is. She likes the stone and wants to keep it. "Tom, can I keep the stone? It is a nice stone. I will put it in my pocket and take it home." Sue says. Tom shakes his head and says, "No, Sue, you cannot keep the stone. It is my stone. I found it first. Give it back to me. I want to throw it in the river." Sue does not want to give the stone back to Tom. She holds the stone tighter and says, "No, Tom, it is my stone now. I like it more than you. You cannot have it." Tom and Sue start to argue and pull the stone. They both want the stone and do not want to share. They do not see the dog that is running towards them. The dog is big and brown and likes to chase things. The dog sees the stone and thinks it is a ball. The dog jumps and barks and tries to grab the stone. Tom and Sue are scared of the dog and let go of the stone. The stone falls from their hands and drops on the ground. The dog picks up the stone and runs away with it. Tom and Sue watch the dog and cry. They have lost the stone and have no more fun. They wish they had shared the stone and played together.
