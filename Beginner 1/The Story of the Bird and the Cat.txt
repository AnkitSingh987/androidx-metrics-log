Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Cat

One day, a cat and a dog were feeling sleepy. They sat under a big tree to rest. The cat had a fan to keep cool. They started to discuss their day. The dog said, "I played with a ball!" The cat said, "I chased a bug!" Suddenly, a loud noise came from the tree. The cat and dog looked up and saw a bird. The bird said, "I am not a bird, I am a magic bird!" The cat and dog were very surprised. The magic bird asked, "Do you want a wish?" The cat and dog thought about it. They both wanted to be less sleepy. So, they asked the magic bird for help. The magic bird waved its wings and said, "You will not be sleepy anymore!" The cat and dog felt wide awake. They thanked the magic bird and played together all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a small orange figure. The figure was funny and could talk. "Hi, I am Zip!" said the figure. Tim was very happy to meet Zip. They played all day. Tim and Zip went to the park. They saw a big tree. "Let's climb!" said Tim. Zip was scared, but Tim helped him. They went up, up, up. At the top, they could see the whole park. Suddenly, Zip started to grow. He became a big orange figure. "Wow, Zip! You are big now!" said Tim. Zip smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you now!" They had more fun at the park. Tim and Zip were best friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big box of colors. He wanted to make a good picture. He took a piece of paper and started to draw. He drew a big sun, a house, and some trees. He was very happy with his picture. Tim showed his picture to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow, that's a good picture! Can you teach me how to draw like that?" Tim smiled and said, "Of course, I can help you." They sat down and started to draw together. Sam tried to draw a house, but it didn't look good. He felt sad and wanted to quit. Tim said, "Don't quit, Sam. Just keep trying. You will get better." Sam listened to Tim and kept trying. Soon, he made a good picture too. They both felt very happy and proud of their pictures. They hung them on the wall for everyone to see.

Once upon a time there was a bear who was very patient. He was always calm and nice, even when things weren't going his way. One day, he decided to take a trip to the woods. As he was walking, he stumbled and broke his leg. He was in so much pain but he was very patient. He laid in the woods for a few hours until help came. He was carried away on a stretcher and he got his leg fixed. Once he was back home, the Bear learned an important lesson. He learned that it is important to think before you do things. If he had been more careful, he wouldn't have broken his leg. He also learned that being patient and kind can help you out in tough times.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily who loved to play in the kitchen. One day, she wanted to make a cake. Lily got a big bowl, some eggs, and milk. She also got some salt. As she mixed everything in the bowl, she felt angry. She didn't know why, but she just did. Lily sighed and kept mixing. She wanted her cake to be the best cake ever. When the cake was done, Lily was so happy. She shared the cake with her mom, dad, and little brother. They all loved it, even with the salt. Lily wasn't angry anymore, and they all lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play together. Anna is very intelligent. She knows many things. Ben is not so intelligent. He likes to copy Anna. One day, they play in the park. Anna sees a big slide. She wants to go on it. She says to Ben, "Let's go on the slide. It looks fun." Ben says, "OK, but how do we go on it?" Anna says, "It's easy. You sit on the top and slide down. I'll show you. Watch me." Anna climbs up the ladder and sits on the top of the slide. She slides down very fast. She laughs and says, "That was fun. Come on, Ben. It's your turn." Ben is scared. He does not want to go on the slide. He says, "No, thank you. I don't like the slide. It's too high." Anna says, "Don't be silly. It's not high. It's fun. You can do it. Just copy me. That's your style." Ben does not want to make Anna angry. He wants to be her friend. He climbs up the ladder and sits on the top of the slide. He looks down and feels dizzy. He says, "Anna, I can't do it. I'm scared. Please help me." Anna says, "Don't be a baby. Just slide down. It's easy. I'll catch you." Ben closes his eyes and slides down. He goes too fast. He does not see Anna. He hits his head on the ground. He cries and says, "Ow, ow, ow. That hurts. Anna, where are you?" Anna is not there. She has run away. She does not want to play with Ben anymore. She thinks he is stupid. She says, "Bye, Ben. You are not intelligent. You are not my friend. I'll find a new friend who can play with me." Ben is alone. He is hurt. He is sad. He wishes he did not copy Anna. He wishes he did not go on the slide. He wishes he had a friend who cared for him. He wants to go home and sleep. He hopes he will feel better tomorrow.
