Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Map

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a map that showed the way to a big park with lots of fun games. Tim was very happy to have this map. One day, Tim's friend, Lily, asked if she could see the map. But Tim was selfish and did not want to share. He said, "No, I refuse to show you my map." Lily was sad and walked away. Later, Tim saw that Lily was playing with other kids in a new game. Tim wanted to join, but Lily said, "You did not share your map with me, so I will not share my game with you." Tim felt bad and said sorry to Lily. He showed her the map, and they all went to the big park together. From that day, Tim learned to share and not be selfish.

Once upon a time, there was a humble book. This book lived on a small shelf in a little room. The book had many friends like a ball, a doll, and a teddy bear. They all played together every day. One day, the book wanted to tell a story to its friends. The ball, the doll, and the teddy bear sat down to listen. The book told a story about a big tree, a funny dog, and a happy sun. The friends loved the story and they all smiled. When the story was at the end, the friends clapped and cheered. The humble book was very happy. It knew that it could make its friends happy with stories. From that day on, the book told a new story every day and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a tough kid. He liked to run, jump, and play all day long. One night, Tom looked up at the sky and saw the moon. It was big and round. Tom wanted to know more about the moon. He asked his mom, "What is the moon?" His mom said, "The moon is a big rock in the sky." Tom liked the moon. He knew it was very far away. He wanted to be friends with the moon. So every night, Tom would wave at the moon and say, "Hi, Moon! I know you are there." The moon would shine bright, and Tom felt happy. He knew that he and the moon were friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big sun. The sun would rise every day to say hello to the world. One day, the sun met a little boy named Tim. Tim was a stubborn boy who did not like to wake up early. One morning, the sun decided to play a game with Tim. The sun found a switch near Tim's bed. When the sun touched the switch, it made Tim's room very bright. Tim woke up and saw the sun shining through his window. He was not happy because he wanted to sleep more. But the sun kept playing with the switch, making the room bright and then dark. Tim got up from his bed and looked at the sun. He decided that he would wake up early every day, so the sun would not play with the switch anymore. From that day on, Tim was not a stubborn boy anymore. He woke up early to see the sun rise and say hello to the world.

Once upon a time, there was a large bear. The bear was very sleepy. He went to sleep in a big cave. One morning, a little bird came to the cave. The bird wanted to play with the bear. The bird tried to wake the bear up. The bird sang a pretty song to urge the bear to wake up. The large bear woke up and saw the little bird. The bear was happy to have a friend. They played all day in the warm sun. And from that day on, they became the best of friends.

Lily and Max were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They had a lot of fun. But then Max saw a big dog. He was scared of dogs. He ran away from the dog and bumped into Lily. Lily fell down and hurt her knee. She cried. "Ow, ow, ow!" she said. "Max, you made me fall! You are mean!" Max felt bad. He did not mean to hurt Lily. He was just afraid of the dog. He said he was sorry. "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't see you. I was running from the dog. I don't like dogs. They are big and loud and scary." Lily looked at Max. She saw that he was scared too. She stopped crying. She said it was okay. "It's okay, Max. I know you didn't mean it. I don't like dogs either. They are big and loud and scary. But we are brave. We can play together and help each other." Max smiled. He was glad that Lily was not mad at him. He said he was sorry again. "I'm sorry, Lily. Thank you for being nice to me. Do you want a hug?" Lily nodded. She said yes. They hugged each other. They felt better. They got up and went to the slide. They slid down and laughed. They were happy. But they were also tired. They had played a lot. They decided to go home and rest. They said goodbye to the park and the dog. They held hands and walked home. They were friends.
