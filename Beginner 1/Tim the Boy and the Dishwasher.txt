Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dishwasher

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very impatient boy. He always wanted things to be done quickly. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her with the dishwasher. Tim didn't want to wait for the dishwasher to finish. He wanted to play outside with his friends. Tim's mom told him, "Tim, we have to wait for the dishwasher to finish. It's important to be patient." But Tim didn't listen. He opened the dishwasher before it was done. All the dishes were still dirty and wet. Tim's mom was sad because now they had to wash the dishes again. Tim learned that being impatient can make more work for everyone. He decided to be more patient from now on. He waited for the dishwasher to finish and helped his mom put the clean dishes away. After that, Tim played outside with his friends. He was happy he learned to be patient and help his mom.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play with toys and eat cookies. One day, they go to Max's house after school. Max's mom gives them two cookies each. They are glad. But then, Lily sees that Max has a cookie with chocolate chips. She likes chocolate chips. She wants Max's cookie. She says, "Give me your cookie, Max. I want it." Max says, "No, Lily. This is my cookie. You have your own cookie. Go away." Lily gets angry. She tries to grab Max's cookie. Max holds it tight. They fight over the cookie. They pull and push and shout. Max's mom hears them. She comes to the room. She sees them fighting. She is not glad. She says, "Stop it, Lily and Max. Fighting is bad. You have to share and be nice. If you don't, you will have no cookies at all." Lily and Max stop fighting. They feel sorry. They say, "Sorry, Max's mom. Sorry, Max. Sorry, Lily." Max's mom says, "It's okay. I'm glad you are sorry. But you have to learn to share. Here, I have more cookies. You can each have one more. But this time, you can choose which one you want." Lily and Max smile. They say, "Thank you, Max's mom. Thank you for the cookies. We will share and be nice." They choose their cookies. They eat them. They are glad. They hug and play with toys. They are friends.

One day, a big red ball arrives in a small town. The ball is very dull and not fun to play with. The kids in the town do not like the ball. They want a new, fun ball to play with. The big red ball rolls to a hill. It rolls up and down the hill. The kids watch the ball and start to laugh. They think it is funny how the ball moves on the hill. The kids run to the hill and start to play with the big red ball. They push the ball up and down the hill. The ball is not dull anymore. It is now fun to play with. The kids are happy and they play with the ball all day.

Ben and Lily were bored. It was a gloomy day and they could not go outside to play. They looked at their toys, but they did not want to play with them. They looked at their books, but they did not want to read them. They looked at each other, but they did not know what to say. Then they heard a knock on the door. They ran to the door and opened it. It was their friend Sam. He had a big smile on his face and a bag in his hand. He said, "Hi, Ben and Lily! Do you want to play with me? I have something fun in my bag!" Ben and Lily said, "Yes, yes, yes!" They let Sam enter their house. They followed him to the living room. He put his bag on the couch and opened it. He took out a big box. He said, "This is a magic box. It can take us to any place we want. All we have to do is close our eyes and say the name of the place." Ben and Lily were curious. They said, "Wow, a magic box! Where can we go?" Sam said, "We can go anywhere. How about the zoo? Do you like animals?" Ben and Lily said, "Yes, we like animals!" Sam said, "OK, then. Let's go to the zoo. Sit on the couch with me and hold the box. Close your eyes and say 'zoo'." Ben and Lily did what Sam said. They sat on the couch with him and held the box. They closed their eyes and said "zoo". They felt a strange sensation. They felt like they were moving very fast. They heard some noises. They smelled some smells. They opened their eyes and saw that they were not in the living room anymore. They were in the zoo! They saw lions and tigers and bears and monkeys and elephants and giraffes and many more animals. They were amazed and excited. They said, "Wow, this is awesome! Thank you, Sam, for bringing us here!" Sam said, "You're welcome, Ben and Lily! I'm glad you like it. Let's have some fun and see all the animals. And when we want to go back, we just have to close our eyes and say 'home'." Ben and Lily said, "OK, let's go!" They got off the couch and ran to see the animals. They forgot about the gloomy day and their boredom. They had a wonderful time with their friend and his magic box.
