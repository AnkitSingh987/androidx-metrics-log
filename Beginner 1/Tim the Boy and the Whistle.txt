Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Whistle

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He was very happy to play. He saw a big tree and heard a whistle sound. Tim looked up and saw a pretty bird. The bird was singing a song. Tim tried to whistle too, but he could not. He felt a little sad. Tim's mom had a surprise for him. She took out a yummy pie from her bag. Tim was very excited to eat the pie. He was anxious because it smelled so good. He took a big bite and his face lit up with a big smile. Just then, the pretty bird flew down and took a piece of pie from Tim's hand. Tim was shocked but then he laughed. The bird started to whistle again. This time, Tim tried to whistle and he did it! The bird and Tim whistled together while they ate the yummy pie.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy, but he liked to keep all his toys to himself. He did not like to share with his friends. His mom told him, "Tim, you should share your toys, or you may not have friends to play with." One day, Tim went to the park with his favorite toy, a red ball. He saw a girl named Sue who did not have a toy yet. Sue asked Tim if she could play with his red ball. Tim said "No, it's mine!" and he kept the ball to himself. Sue was sad and went away. Tim played with his red ball alone. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim was not careful and lost his red ball. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Tim felt sad and went home without his red ball. The bad ending of the story is that Tim lost his red ball because he did not share it with Sue. The moral value of the story is that sharing is important, and when we share, we can have more fun and make more friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, Tim found out his toy car could reverse. He was very happy. Tim played with his toy car all day. He thought it was harmless fun. But then, he saw a big hole in the ground. Tim knew the hole was trouble. He didn't want his toy car to fall in the hole. So, he used the reverse to move the car away from the hole. In the end, Tim learned that it is important to be careful and watch where you are going. If Tim didn't know how to reverse his toy car, it would have fallen into the hole. The moral of the story is to always pay attention and be safe when playing.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys in the playroom. They have many toys: cars, dolls, blocks, and books. They also have a board. The board is big and white. They can draw on the board with markers. One day, Lily and Ben want to draw on the board. They take the markers and go to the board. Lily draws a flower. Ben draws a sun. They are happy. They talk to each other. "Look, I draw a flower. It is pink and pretty," Lily says. "Wow, your flower is nice. I draw a sun. It is yellow and bright," Ben says. They draw more things on the board. They draw a house, a tree, a cat, and a dog. They fill the board with colors. They have fun. But then, they hear a voice. It is Mom. She comes to the playroom. She sees the board and the toys. She is not happy. She talks to Lily and Ben. "Kids, what have you done? The board is messy. The toys are everywhere. You need to tidy up. It is time for lunch," Mom says. Lily and Ben look at the board and the toys. They see the mess. They feel sorry. They talk to Mom. "Sorry, Mom. We were drawing on the board. We forgot to tidy up. We will do it now," Lily and Ben say. They put the markers back in the box. They erase the board with a cloth. They put the toys in the basket. They make the playroom tidy. Mom sees the playroom. She is happy. She talks to Lily and Ben. "Good job, kids. You made the playroom tidy. Now you can have lunch. Come to the kitchen. I made sandwiches and juice for you," Mom says. Lily and Ben smile. They follow Mom to the kitchen. They are hungry. They eat the sandwiches and drink the juice. They thank Mom. They are happy. They love Mom.
