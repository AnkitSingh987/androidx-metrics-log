Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Slide

Ben and Lily are twins. They like to play and laugh. They are jolly children. One day, they go to the park with Mom and Dad. They see a big slide and a swing. They run to the slide and take turns to slide down. Whee! "Look, Ben, a clown!" Lily says. She points to a man with a red nose and a funny hat. He is making jokes and balloons for the children. Ben and Lily go to see him. He gives them each a balloon. Lily gets a pink flower and Ben gets a green frog. "Hello, little ones. Do you like jokes?" the clown asks. He smiles and winks. "Yes, we do!" Ben and Lily say. "OK, then. Here is a joke for you. What do you get when you cross a frog and a flower?" the clown says. Ben and Lily think hard. They shake their heads. "I don't know," Lily says. "Me neither," Ben says. The clown laughs. "You get a hoppy bouquet!" he says. He makes a sound like a frog. "Ribbit, ribbit!" Ben and Lily giggle. They think the joke is funny. They clap their hands. "That's a good joke!" Ben says. "Thank you, clown!" Lily says. "You're welcome, little ones. Enjoy your balloons. And remember, always be jolly!" the clown says. He waves and walks away. Ben and Lily hug their balloons. They are happy. They go back to Mom and Dad. They tell them about the joke and the clown. Mom and Dad smile and hug them. "Are you hungry, Ben and Lily?" Mom asks. "Yes, we are!" Ben and Lily say. "Good. Let's go and eat some sandwiches and cookies. Then we can play some more," Dad says. Ben and Lily nod. They follow Mom and Dad. They hold their balloons. They are jolly children. They like to play and laugh. They like jokes and clowns. They like to eat sandwiches and cookies. They like the park. They like Mom and Dad. They like each other. They are happy. The end.

Lily and Max were playing in the park. They saw a man with a big bag of lollipops. He was giving them to the children who answered his questions. "Can I have a lollipop, please?" Lily asked the man. "Sure, but you have to answer a question first. What is the name of this animal?" The man showed Lily a picture of a dog. "That's easy. It's a dog!" Lily said. "Very good. Here is your lollipop." The man gave Lily a red lollipop. Max wanted a lollipop too. He ran to the man and said, "Me too, me too!" "Okay, what is the name of this animal?" The man showed Max a picture of a cat. Max did not know. He did not like cats. He said, "I don't know. I don't care. Just give me a lollipop!" The man shook his head. "No, you have to answer the question. If you don't know, you can guess." Max was angry. He did not want to guess. He wanted a lollipop now. He tried to grab the bag from the man. "Hey, stop that! That's not nice!" The man said. Lily saw Max being rude. She felt sorry for the man. She said, "Max, don't do that. That's not how you get a lollipop. You have to answer the question. It's a cat. See, it has whiskers and a tail." Max did not listen. He pulled harder on the bag. The bag ripped open. Lollipops fell on the ground. "Oh no! Look what you did!" The man said. Lily and Max looked at the lollipops. They were dirty and sticky. They could not eat them. They felt bad. They said, "We're sorry, mister. We're sorry we broke your bag and wasted your lollipops." The man was sad. He said, "It's okay. I forgive you. But you have to learn to be polite and patient. Lollipops are not more important than being kind." Lily and Max nodded. They said, "We understand. We will be better next time." The man smiled. He said, "That's good. Here, I have one more lollipop left. You can share it." He gave them a blue lollipop. Lily and Max broke it in half. They said, "Thank you, mister. Thank you for being nice to us." They ate their lollipop together. They learned their lesson. They were happy.
