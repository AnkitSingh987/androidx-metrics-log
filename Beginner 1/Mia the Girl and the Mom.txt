Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved the color purple. One day, she found a big purple cloth in her room. She wanted to make a pretty dress for her doll. Mia went to her mom and said, "Mom, can you help me? I want to cut the purple cloth and make a dress for my doll." Her mom agreed and they both started to cut the cloth. As they cut the cloth, Mia's mom taught her how to sew. Mia was so happy to learn. After they finished, Mia put the purple dress on her doll. "Look, Mom! My doll looks so pretty!" Mia said. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia, you did a great job!" From that day on, Mia and her mom made many more dresses for her dolls.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She took her orange ball and her soft blanket. Mia liked to squeeze her blanket when she felt happy. At the park, she played with her ball and squeezed her blanket. The sun was shining, and Mia was very happy. While Mia was playing, a big wind came. It blew her orange ball far away. Mia was sad and squeezed her blanket tight. She looked around and saw a big tree. Her orange ball was on top of the tree! Mia didn't know how to get it down. Just then, a friendly bird flew down from the tree. The bird had Mia's orange ball in its beak. The bird dropped the ball, and Mia caught it. She was so happy! Mia squeezed her blanket and thanked the bird. The bird smiled, and they both played together in the park.

One day, a cool cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big fish on a hook. Tom wanted to eat the fish. He jumped up to get the fish, but the hook was too high. Tom found a long stick. He used the stick to slap the fish off the hook. The fish fell down and Tom was happy. He picked up the fish with his paw. Tom took the fish home to eat. He shared the fish with his friend, a little dog named Tim. They both enjoyed the fish and had a fun day.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a lot of fun on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They also like to feed the ducks in the pond. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They play for a long time. They get tired and hungry. Their moms say it is time to go home. They give them some snacks and water. They sit on a bench to eat. But Anna does not want to go home. She wants to play more. She is upset. She cries and kicks. She says no to her mom. She throws her snack on the ground. Ben sees Anna. He is sad. He does not like to see his friend upset. He wants to help her. He goes to her and gives her a hug. He says, "Anna, it is okay. We can play again tomorrow. We had a lot of fun today. Let's go home and rest. Maybe we can have a story before bed." Anna hears Ben. She stops crying. She looks at him. She sees he is kind. She feels better. She says, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend. I am sorry I was upset. Let's go home and rest. Maybe we can have a story before bed." Their moms smile. They are proud of their kids. They say, "Good job, Anna and Ben. You are good friends. We are happy you had fun. Let's go home and rest. Maybe we can have a story before bed." They hold hands and walk to the car. They wave goodbye to the park and the ducks. They are tired but happy. They can't wait to play again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat. The cat liked to play with its friends. One day, the cat and its friends went to a big park. They had a lot of fun playing together. In the park, they saw a big door. The door had an exit sign. The little cat was curious and wanted to see what was behind the door. The cat's friends said, "Let's part and meet back here after we look behind the door." The little cat went through the exit and found a big, beautiful garden. It was full of flowers and butterflies. The cat was so happy, it played in the garden for a long time. Then, it remembered its friends and went back to the door. The little cat and its friends met again and shared their fun stories. They all had a great day.
