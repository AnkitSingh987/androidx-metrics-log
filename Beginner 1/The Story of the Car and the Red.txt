Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Car and the Red

Once upon a time, there was a little red car with a big motor. The car was very attractive and all the other cars liked it. The little red car loved to drive fast and make loud noises with its motor. One day, the little red car saw a small blue car that was sad. The blue car's motor was not working well. The red car wanted to help the blue car feel better. So, the little red car gave the blue car a gentle kiss on its front. The blue car started to smile and its motor started to work better. The little red car learned that being kind and helpful is more important than being attractive. From that day on, the red car and the blue car became best friends and they always helped each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sue. They wanted to play by the stream. They walked together and talked about fun things they liked to do. When they got to the stream, they saw a scary frog. Sue said, "Oh no! Look at that scary frog!" Tim said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will be brave and shoo it away." Tim went close to the frog and said, "Go away, scary frog!" The frog jumped away and they were not scared anymore. They played by the stream all day and had lots of fun.

Jill and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a big ball and a small bike. Jill can balance on the bike. Ben can kick the ball high. One day, a big boy comes to the park. He is not kind. He takes the ball from Ben. He says, "This is my ball now. You are too small to play with it." Ben is sad. He wants his ball back. Jill sees the big boy. She is not afraid. She rides the bike to him. She says, "Give the ball back to Ben. He is my friend. You are not nice." The big boy laughs. He says, "You are too small to ride the bike. You will fall off." He pushes the bike. Jill falls off. She hurts her knee. Ben runs to Jill. He helps her up. He says, "Are you okay, Jill? You are brave. You tried to help me." Jill smiles. She says, "I am okay, Ben. You are kind. You care about me." They hug. The big boy sees them. He feels bad. He says, "I am sorry, Jill and Ben. I was mean. I should not have taken your ball or pushed your bike. Can I play with you?" Jill and Ben look at him. They think. They say, "Okay, you can play with us. But you have to be nice and share." The big boy nods. He gives the ball back to Ben. He says, "Thank you. You are good friends. Can you teach me how to balance on the bike?" Jill and Ben say, "Yes, we can. Come on, let's play." They all play together. They have fun. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a big world, there was a clever little ant. The ant loved to march with his friends. They would march up and down, all around the world. One day, the clever little ant and his friends found a big hill. They wanted to march to the top. So, they all started to march together. They marched up the hill, one step at a time. When they reached the top of the hill, the clever little ant looked around. He saw the big world all around him. He and his friends were happy. They had marched all the way up the hill. And then, they marched back down, ready for their next adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. She was really excited because today was a special day. She was going to celebrate her first exam. Sally got up bright and early that morning, put on her very best dress and ran off to school. When Sally arrived, everyone cheered and clapped. She got to sit alone at the front of the classroom with her teacher and all her friends. When the exam started, Sally felt a bit scared. Everyone began writing and Sally just couldn't think of anything to write. She was so sorry that she started to cry. But then her teacher took her hand and said to her, "It's ok, Sally. If you don't know the answer, that's ok. Just do your best and if it's not quite right then that's ok too. You can still celebrate how far you have come." Sally felt so much better after that. She finished the exam with a smile on her face and she was very proud of herself. Everyone was so happy for her and celebrated her exam!
