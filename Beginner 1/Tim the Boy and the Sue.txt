Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his baseball. He played with it every day. Tim was very impatient. He always wanted to play right away. One day, Tim saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. Tim wanted to make her happy. So, he gave her his baseball. Sue smiled and said thank you. They played together all day. At the end of the day, Sue gave Tim a kiss on the cheek. Tim blushed and smiled. They became best friends. From that day on, Tim and Sue played with the baseball together, and Tim learned to be more patient.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Lily and Mia. They were always laughing and having fun. One day, Lily had a great idea. She told Mia, "Let's go on a jet ride!" Mia thought it was a great idea and quickly agreed. They arrived at the jet, which was very shiny. Mia asked, "Where are we going?" Lily said, " I don't know, but it will be lots of fun!" The jet took off and flew high into the sky. A few minutes later, Mia saw something in the clouds. She said, "What's that?" Lily smiled and said, "That's a sour cloud. Let's grab it and make a joke!" Mia and Lily each grabbed a piece of the sour cloud and laughed as it tickled their tongues. It was a wonderful adventure. Mia and Lily learned that nothing beats a good joke with your best friend!

Tom and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with trains and cars. One day, they went to the park with their moms. There was a big track for real trains. Tom and Sam wanted to see the trains up close. "Let's go on the track!" Tom said. "It will be fun!" "No, Tom, that's bad!" Sam said. "The track is not for us. It is for trains. Trains are fast and loud. They can hurt us." But Tom did not listen. He ran to the track. He wanted to touch the rails and the stones. He did not see the train coming. "Tom, come back!" Sam shouted. "There is a train! It is coming!" Sam was scared. He wanted to help Tom. But he did not want to go on the track. He knew it was wrong. He resisted the urge to follow Tom. He stayed on the grass. He called for his mom. "Mom, mom, help! Tom is on the track! There is a train!" The moms heard Sam. They saw Tom. They ran to the track. They waved their arms and yelled at Tom. They tried to stop the train. The train saw the moms. It blew its horn. It slowed down. It stopped. It did not hit Tom. Tom was lucky. He was not hurt. But he was ashamed. He knew he did a bad thing. He made his mom and Sam worried. He made the train stop. He was sorry. "Tom, what were you thinking?" his mom said. "You could have been killed! You could have hurt someone else! You were very naughty!" Tom cried. He hugged his mom. He said he was sorry. He said he would never do it again. Sam was relieved. He was glad Tom was safe. He was proud he did not go on the track. He was a good friend. "Tom, I'm happy you are okay," Sam said. "But don't ever do that again. The track is not for us. It is for trains. We can watch them from far away. We can play with our toys. That is more fun." Tom nodded. He agreed with Sam. He learned his lesson. He thanked Sam for being a good friend. He said he was sorry for scaring him. The moms were angry. But they were also happy. They hugged their sons. They said they loved them. They said they were glad they were okay. They left the park. They went home. They had a snack. They played with their trains and cars. They were careful. They were happy. They were friends.

Sara liked to cook with her mom. They made yummy things like soup, cake and pie. Sara wanted to cook a peach pie for her dad. He was dependable and always came home from work with a smile. "Can we cook a peach pie, mom?" Sara asked. "Sure, honey. We have some peaches in the fridge. Let's wash them and cut them," mom said. They washed the peaches and mom gave Sara a knife. Sara tried to cut the peaches, but the knife was sharp and slippery. She cut her finger and it hurt a lot. She cried and dropped the knife. "Oh no, Sara! Are you okay?" mom said. She ran to Sara and wrapped her finger with a cloth. "We need to go to the doctor. Come on, let's get your coat." Sara was scared and sad. She wanted to cook a peach pie, not go to the doctor. She hoped her dad would not be mad at her. They got in the car and drove to the doctor. The doctor was nice and gave Sara a band-aid and a sticker. He said Sara would be fine, but she had to be careful with knives. Sara and mom went back home. They saw dad's car in the driveway. Sara was happy to see him, but also nervous. She ran to him and hugged him. "Hi, Sara. How was your day?" dad said. "I'm sorry, dad. I wanted to cook a peach pie for you, but I cut my finger and we had to go to the doctor," Sara said. Dad looked at Sara's finger and frowned. He was not happy. He was angry. "Sara, you know you are not supposed to use knives. They are dangerous. You could have hurt yourself badly. You are a bad girl. You are grounded. No pie for you," dad said. Sara felt her heart break. She loved her dad, but he was mad at her. He did not want her pie. He did not want her. She ran to her room and cried. She wished she never cooked a peach pie. She wished she had a different dad. A dad who was dependable and always came home with a smile.
