Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and the Park

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mommy. She saw her friend Tom playing with chalk on the ground. Mia wanted to join Tom and play with the chalk too. "Can I join you?" asked Mia. Tom said, "Yes, let's draw together!" They drew a big sun and some flowers. Mia was very happy. A boy named Sam saw them playing and felt envious. He wanted to play with chalk too. So, Sam asked if he could join them. Mia and Tom said, "Yes, come play with us!" Now, all three friends were drawing with chalk and having fun together.

Once upon a time, in a big world, there were two friends, Tom and Jen. They both were gifted. They could run very fast and jump very high. They loved to play together and share their toys with each other. They were always happy when they played. One day, Tom found a big red ball. He wanted to share it with Jen. They took turns playing with the ball. They kicked it, threw it, and bounced it. They were having so much fun. They laughed all day long. But then, a big wind came. It blew the ball away from Tom and Jen. They tried to catch it, but they couldn't. The ball went far, far away. Tom and Jen were very sad. They lost their big red ball. They did not share any toys that day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy car that he loved to play with every day. But one day, his car became dull and slow. Tim was sad because he could not make the car go fast like before. Tim's mom saw him sad and knew she could help. She went to the store and bought some special medicine for the car. When she came back, she put the medicine on the car's wheels. Tim was excited to see if it would work. Tim pushed the car and it started to go fast again! The medicine had fixed the dull car. Tim was so happy that he played with his car all day long. And from that day on, whenever the car became dull, Tim's mom would use the special medicine to make it go fast again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a big box to unpack. In the box, he found a candle. Tim was very happy. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the candle and was rude. She said, "I don't like your candle." Tim was sad. But Tim did not give up. He went home and made the candle better. He put a smiley face on it. The next day, Tim showed Sue the happy candle. Sue said sorry for being rude. They became friends and played together with the happy candle.

Lily and Ben were playing in the sandbox. They had a big bucket and a small shovel. They wanted to make a sandcastle. Lily filled the bucket with sand and Ben packed it down. Then they turned the bucket over and lifted it up. They had a nice sand tower. "Wow, it's so tall!" Lily said. "Let's make more!" Ben said. They made four more sand towers. They put them in a circle. They had a sandcastle. "Look, we did it!" Lily said. They were very happy. They clapped their hands. But then they heard a bark. It was Rex, their dog. Rex was very playful. He liked to run and jump. He saw the sandcastle. He thought it was a toy. He ran towards it. "Rex, no!" Lily and Ben shouted. But Rex did not listen. He was not obedient. He jumped on the sandcastle. He knocked down the sand towers. He made a big mess. Lily and Ben were very sad. They worked hard on their sandcastle. They did not like Rex's surprise. "Bad dog!" Lily said. "Go away!" Ben said. Rex wagged his tail. He did not understand. He thought they were playing. He licked their faces. Lily and Ben sighed. They loved Rex, but he was very naughty. They decided to make another sandcastle. But this time, they put the bucket on Rex's head. He could not see the sandcastle. He could not ruin it. Lily and Ben smiled. They had a new surprise for Rex. They hoped he would like it.
