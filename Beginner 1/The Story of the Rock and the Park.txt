Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Rock and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big, smooth rock in a park. Many kids liked to play on the rock because it was fun. One day, a kind man came to the park. He saw the kids playing on the rock. He wanted to teach them something new. So, he kneeled down on the grass and called the kids to come. The kids stopped playing and went to the man. The man gave a small lecture about the rock. He told them that the rock was very old and came from far away. The kids listened and learned about the rock. They thanked the man and went back to play. Now, they knew more about their favorite rock in the park.

---

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw her friends having a meeting under a big tree. They were all talking and laughing. Amy was very happy to see them and wanted to play with them. Amy was careful as she walked to her friends. She did not want to fall down or get hurt. When she got to the meeting, her friends were talking about their favorite things to do at the park. Amy said, "I love to swing!" So, they all went to the swings and took turns swinging. They swung high and low, and they all had fun. They laughed and played until it was time to go home. They said goodbye and went home with big smiles on their faces.

---

One day, a little girl named Mia woke up. She got out of bed and saw that it was wet outside. It was raining. Mia liked the rain because it made the flowers grow and the grass green. Mia's mom came into her room and asked, "Do you want some tea?" Mia nodded her head and said, "Yes, please!" Her mom went to the kitchen and made tea for them. They sat by the window and watched the rain together. After they finished their tea, Mia put on her rain boots and coat. She went outside to play in the wet rain. Mia loved to splash in the puddles and feel the rain on her face. It was a fun day for Mia.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his red flag. He would run and jump to catch it. His best friend, a little boy named Tim, would throw the flag for Spot to catch. One day, Tim and Spot were playing in the park when the wind blew the flag far away. Spot could not see it anymore. He started to worry. "Oh no! Where is my flag?" Spot said. Tim saw Spot's worry and said, "Don't worry, Spot. We will find your flag." They looked and looked until they found the flag stuck in a tree. Tim climbed the tree and got the flag. He gave it back to Spot, who was very happy. "Thank you, Tim!" Spot said. "You are my best friend." The moral of the story is that friends help each other when they are worried or in need. And with loyal friends, there is no need to worry.

Tom has a dog. His dog is big and brown and friendly. He likes to play with his dog and give him treats. His dog's name is Rex. One day, Tom and Rex go for a walk. Tom puts a leash on Rex. The leash is red and long. It helps Tom keep Rex safe. Tom and Rex walk to the park. They see many things on the way. They see cars and trees and birds and people. At the park, Tom and Rex see another dog. The other dog is small and white and fluffy. He has a blue leash. His name is Spot. Spot's owner is a girl. She is wearing a yellow dress. She smiles at Tom and Rex. "Hi, I'm Lily. Can I play with you and your dog?" she asks. "Hi, I'm Tom. This is Rex. He is friendly. You can play with us," Tom says. Lily and Tom let their dogs sniff each other. Rex and Spot wag their tails. They like each other. Lily and Tom throw a ball for their dogs. The dogs run and catch the ball. They have fun. Tom and Lily become friends. Rex and Spot become friends. They play together at the park until it is time to go home. They say goodbye and hope to see each other again. Tom and Rex walk back home. They are happy. They had a good day.
