Category: Beginner
Title: The Deer and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a little deer. The deer lived in a big forest. The forest was full of trees and pretty flowers. The little deer had many friends. They played games and had fun together. One day, the little deer found a smelly sock. The sock was very stinky. The little deer did not like the smell. The smelly sock remind the little deer of a bad smell from the trash. The little deer showed the smelly sock to his friends. His friends did not like the smell too. They all decided to throw the smelly sock away. They put the sock in the trash. The little deer and his friends were happy again. They played games and had fun in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a bright red ball. The bright red ball had a friend, a small blue bird. They liked to play together in the park. They would rush and run and have fun all day. One day, when they were playing, the bright red ball said, "Blue bird, let's rush to the big tree!" The small blue bird said, "Okay, Red ball. Let's go!" They rushed and rushed to the big tree. But when they got to the big tree, they saw a big, mean cat. The cat was fast and wanted to catch the small blue bird. The small blue bird tried to fly away, but the cat jumped high and caught the bird. The bright red ball was left all alone, and the small blue bird was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. They all wanted to win the game. They had a big goal to reach. While they played, a big dog came to the park. The dog took the ball away. Tim and his friends were sad. They did not know what to do. They all look at each other and got an idea. Tim and his friends got some treats. They gave the treats to the dog. The dog was happy and gave the ball back. Tim and his friends could play again. They all reached their goal and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty dog named Spot. Spot liked to organize his toys in the yard. One day, Spot found a big metal thing. He wanted to play with it and add it to his toys. He picked it up in his mouth and carried it to his yard. As Spot was organizing his toys, his friend, the cat named Fluffy, came over. Fluffy saw the metal thing and got very excited. "Spot, that's not a toy! It's a magic metal!" said Fluffy. Spot didn't know what magic was, but he liked the sound of it. Together, Spot and Fluffy decided to see what the magic metal could do. They touched it and wished for a big pile of treats. Suddenly, the treats appeared! They were so happy and ate all the treats. From that day on, Spot was not naughty anymore, and he and Fluffy used the magic metal to help their friends and have fun.

One day, a friendly cat named Tom found a case. He did not know what was in it. He tried to open it, but it was hard. Tom asked his friend, a dog named Max, to help him. Max said, "I will help you." They tried to open the case together, but they could not manage. They pushed and pulled, but the case did not open. Tom and Max started to fight. They both wanted to open the case first. The fight got bigger and bigger. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took the case away. Tom and Max stopped fighting and looked at the bird. They were sad because they lost the case. The bird flew away and they never saw the case again.

One day, a little fish named Fin wanted to swim far away. He put on his mask to see better. He swam and swam until he saw a big, troubled crab. The crab looked sad. "Hello, Mr. Crab! Why are you sad?" asked Fin. The crab said, "I lost my mask. I can't see well without it. I can't find my way home." Fin wanted to help. He swam around, looking for the crab's mask. He found it under a rock. He gave it to the crab. The crab was very happy. "Thank you, Fin!" said the crab. "Now I can see and go home!" Fin and the crab swam together. They became good friends. They played and swam every day.
