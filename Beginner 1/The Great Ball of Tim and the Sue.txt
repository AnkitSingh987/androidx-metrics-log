Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Sue

One day, a little boy named Tim was walking with his mom. They saw a big building. The building was open. Tim wanted to go inside and play. Inside the building, there were many kids. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was playing with a toy. Tim wanted to play with the toy too. He asked Sue, "Can I play with the toy?" Sue said, "No, it is mine." Tim was sad. His mom said, "Let's find another toy." They looked and found a big ball. Tim and Sue played with the ball together. They had fun and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big machine. It could change things. A boy named Tim liked to play with the machine. He was a persistent boy. One day, Tim put his toy car in the machine. He said, "Change, please!" The machine changed the toy car into a big car. Tim was very happy. Tim's friend, Sara, came to play. She saw the big car and asked, "How did you do that?" Tim said, "My machine can change things!" They played and had fun with the big car all day.

Once upon a time, there was a bright red ball. It was very happy and loved to play with the children in the park. One day, the wind blew the ball far away from the park. The bright red ball was sad and wanted to go back to the children. Nearby, a tall stand of trees saw the bright red ball. The trees wanted to help the ball. They called their friend, a big, strong bird. The bird heard the trees and decided to rescue the ball. The big, strong bird flew down and picked up the bright red ball with its beak. Then, it carried the ball back to the park. The children saw the bird coming and clapped their hands. They were so happy to have their bright red ball back. The ball was happy too, and they all played together again.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play on the porch. The porch is big and has a swing. They swing and talk and laugh. One day, they see a cat on the porch. The cat is black and white and has green eyes. The cat looks at them and meows. Lily and Ben want to pet the cat. They get off the swing and walk to the cat. The cat runs away. Lily and Ben follow the cat. The cat jumps on the fence. Lily and Ben try to reach the cat. The cat jumps off the fence and runs away. Lily and Ben are sad. They want the cat to be their friend. They go back to the porch and sit on the swing. They think of a name for the cat. "I think we should name the cat Spot," Lily says. "He has a black spot on his nose." "I think we should name the cat Fluffy," Ben says. "He has a lot of fur." They argue about the name. They do not agree. They get mad at each other. They stop talking and swinging. The cat comes back. He sees Lily and Ben on the porch. He sees that they are not happy. He walks to them and rubs his head on their legs. He purrs and meows. Lily and Ben look at the cat. They smile and pet the cat. They forget their fight. They hug each other and the cat. They decide to name the cat Spot-Fluffy. They think it is a good name. They think the cat likes it too. They play with the cat on the porch. They are happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a kind kid named Tim. Tim liked to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Tim saw a small bird on the ground. The bird could not fly. Tim went to the bird and asked, "Why do you lie here, little bird?" The bird said, "I am hurt, and I can't fly back to my nest." Tim felt sad for the bird and wanted to help. Tim picked up the bird and put it in a safe place. He asked his friends to help find food for the bird. The bird got better and could fly again. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim, for being so kind." Tim and his friends learned that helping others is a good thing to do.
