Category: Beginner
Title: The Loop and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little loop. The loop liked to play with his friend, the number. They were very careful when they played together. One day, they went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. The loop went up and down, and the number went round and round. They were so happy and careful not to fall. At the end of the day, the loop and the number were tired. They said goodbye and went to their homes. They knew they would play together again soon, always being careful and having fun.

---

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They also like to feed the ducks. The ducks are popular. Many people come to see them and give them food. One day, Ben and Mia go to the park with a bag of fruit. They want to feed the ducks some apples and bananas. They think the ducks will like them. They walk to the pond and see many ducks swimming and quacking. "Hello, ducks!" Ben says. "We have some fruit for you. Do you want some?" "Yes, please!" Mia says. "Fruit is good for you. It makes you strong and healthy." They take out some fruit and throw it to the ducks. The ducks swim to the fruit and eat it. They seem happy. Ben and Mia smile. But then, a big boy comes to the pond. He has a bag of bread. He wants to feed the ducks too. He sees Ben and Mia and their fruit. "Hey, what are you doing?" he says. "You can't feed the ducks fruit. They don't like fruit. They like bread. Bread is better." He takes out some bread and throws it to the ducks. The ducks swim to the bread and eat it. They seem happy. The big boy smiles. Ben and Mia are sad. They don't like the big boy. They think he is mean. They think the ducks like fruit too. They want to feed the ducks more fruit. "No, you can't!" the big boy says. "You have to stop. This is my pond. These are my ducks. I feed them what I want. You go away." He pushes Ben and Mia. He takes their bag of fruit. He throws it in the trash. He laughs. Ben and Mia are angry. They don't like the big boy. They think he is rude. They think the ducks are not his. They want their fruit back. They want to feed the ducks again. They run to the trash. They take their bag of fruit. They run back to the pond. They throw some fruit to the ducks. The ducks swim to the fruit and eat it. They seem happy. Ben and Mia smile. The big boy is mad. He doesn't like Ben and Mia. He thinks they are silly. He thinks the ducks don't like fruit. He wants to stop them. He runs to the pond. He tries to take their bag of fruit. He tries to push them. But then, a lady comes to the pond. She sees the big boy and Ben and Mia. She sees the fruit and the bread. She sees the ducks. She is not happy. "Hey, what are you doing?" she says. "You can't feed the ducks bread. Bread is bad for them. It makes them sick and fat. Fruit is good for them. It makes them happy and healthy." She takes the bag of bread from the big boy. She throws it in the trash. She scolds him. "You should be nice to the ducks. You should be nice to Ben and Mia. They are doing the right thing. You are doing the wrong thing. You should say sorry." The big boy is ashamed. He doesn't like the lady. He thinks she is bossy. He thinks the ducks like bread. He doesn't want to say sorry. But he is scared. He says sorry. "Sorry, ducks. Sorry, Ben. Sorry, Mia." He walks away. He is sad. Ben and Mia are happy. They like the lady. They think she is smart. They think the ducks like fruit. They want to say thank you. They say thank you. "Thank you, lady. Thank you, ducks." They feed the ducks some more fruit. The ducks swim to the fruit and eat it. They seem happy. They quack. "Quack, quack, quack!" The end.
