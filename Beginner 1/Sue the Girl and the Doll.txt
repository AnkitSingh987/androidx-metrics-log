Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Doll

One day, a girl named Sue found a doll. The doll was dull and old. Sue did not like the doll. She wanted a new and pretty one. Sue went to her friend, Tim. She said, "Tim, I do not like my doll. It is dull. Can I have your doll?" Tim did not want to give his doll to Sue. He said, "No, Sue. I like my doll. You can't have it." Sue was mad. She took the dull doll and slap Tim's doll. Tim was sad and cried. Sue did not care. She went away with her dull doll. The next day, Sue did not have any friends to play with. They all heard what Sue did to Tim. Sue was sad and alone with her dull doll.

One morning, a dog and a cat went for a walk. The dog was big and the cat was small. They liked to play together. The sun was up, and they were grateful for the nice day. As they walked, they saw a big ball. The dog and the cat both wanted the ball. They did not want to share. They both tried to get the ball. They did not know what to do. Then, they both paused. They looked at each other and smiled. They decided to share the ball and play together. The dog and the cat were happy and grateful for being friends.

Raji and Mom were walking in the park. They saw lots of kids playing a sport. Raji wanted to join, but Mom said no. "Why can't I?," Raji asked. "It's too hard for you, honey," Mom said kindly. "It's for big kids, not for you." Raji was upset and didn't understand why he couldn't be like the other kids. "But why!," he asked again. "It's too hard for you. You have to be strong and smart to play," explained Mom. But Raji was determined to resist. He wanted to play and he wasn't going to give up without a fight. He tried and tried, but eventually Mom overpowered him. She was stronger and smarter than him, and he was forced to give up. Raji was angry and frustrated. He was so ignorant that he didn't understand why he couldn't play with the bigger kids. Mom just smiled and held him close, understanding his feelings. "One day, honey, you'll be old enough and strong enough to join in the sport," she said. "But for now, we'll just watch and enjoy." Raji smiled and hugged Mom, feeling content. He knew that one day, he would be able to play too.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin named Bob. Bob was a very attractive penguin. He had shiny black feathers and a big white belly. All the other penguins liked to look at him. One day, Bob found a big red ball. The ball could bounce very high. Bob wanted to play with the ball. He pushed the ball with his beak, and the ball went bounce, bounce, bounce! All the other penguins saw Bob and the red ball. They thought it was fun to watch the ball bounce. So, they all came to play with Bob and the red ball. They took turns pushing the ball and making it bounce. They all had a great day playing with the bouncing ball.

Sara and Ben were playing with their toy cars on the floor. They liked to make them zoom and crash and honk. Sara had a red car and Ben had a blue car. They pretended they were racing on a big road. "Vroom, vroom, I'm faster than you!" Sara said. "No, you're not, I'm faster than you!" Ben said. They made their cars go faster and faster, until they reached the end of the rug. Then they had to stop and turn around. But Sara did not stop in time. Her car went over the rug and hit the wall. A lot of dust fell from the shelf above the wall. "Uh-oh, you made a mess!" Ben said. Sara looked at the dust on the floor and on her car. She felt sad. She did not mean to make a mess. She wanted to fix it. "I'm sorry, Ben. I'll clean it up," she said. She tried to pick up the dust with her hands, but it was too small and too many. She did not know what to do. "Maybe we can use the vacuum," Ben said. He pointed to the big machine in the corner. It had a long hose and a switch. He had seen his mom use it to suck up dirt and dust. "Okay, let's try it," Sara said. They dragged the vacuum to the rug. Ben plugged it in and turned it on. It made a loud noise. They held the hose and moved it over the dust. The dust disappeared into the hose. "Wow, this is amazing!" Sara said. They smiled and laughed. They liked the vacuum. They decided to use it on their cars too. They made them go in reverse and put them under the hose. The vacuum cleaned their cars and made them shiny. "Look, our cars are like new!" Ben said. They were happy. They had fun with the vacuum and their cars. They did not mind the noise. They did not see their mom coming into the room. She looked at them and the vacuum and the rug. She was surprised. "What are you doing, kids?" she asked.
