Category: Beginner
Title: Spot and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very obedient dog. He always did what his owner, a girl named Sue, told him to do. One day, Sue and Spot went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. "Spot, let's play a game," Sue said. Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" Sue threw a ball, and Spot ran to catch it. They played the game for a long time. Spot was having so much fun, but he started to feel very tired. Sue saw that Spot was tired and said, "Spot, come rest with me under this big tree." They both sat down under the tree, and Spot put his head on Sue's lap. Sue gently touched Spot's face and said, "You are such a good dog, Spot." They both rested under the tree and enjoyed the beautiful day.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play on the ranch. The ranch has many animals. There are cows, horses, sheep, and chickens. Tom and Lily like to feed them and pet them. One day, they see a big gray cloud in the sky. It looks like rain. Tom and Lily run to the barn. They want to stay dry. They find a cozy spot in the hay. They talk and giggle. "What do you think will happen if it rains?" Tom asks. "Maybe the animals will get wet and cold," Lily says. "Maybe the grass will grow and be green," Tom says. "Maybe we will see a rainbow," Lily says. They look out of the window. They see drops of water falling from the sky. They hear thunder and see lightning. They hug each other. They are a little scared. But soon, the rain stops. The sun comes out. The sky is blue. Tom and Lily go outside. They see the animals. They are happy and clean. They see the grass. It is fresh and bright. They see a rainbow. It is colorful and beautiful. They smile and clap. They are happy too. They run to the animals. They feed them and pet them. They thank them for sharing the ranch. They say goodbye and go home. They had a fun day on the ranch.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with clay. One day, he found a big ball of clay in his yard. Tim was very happy. Tim tried to make a clay man, but the clay was too hard. He felt helpless. He asked his mom, "What can I do to make the clay soft?" His mom said, "Add water and it will yield." Tim added water to the clay. It became soft and easy to use. He made a clay man and a clay house. Tim was not helpless anymore. He learned that asking for help can make things better.

Once upon a time, there was a big stable. In the stable, there were many animals. There was a horse, a cow, and a sheep. They were all friends. One day, the horse said, "Let's play hide and seek!" The cow and the sheep agreed. The horse closed his eyes and started to count. The cow and the sheep ran to hide. The cow found a good spot behind a tree. The sheep was tired, so he hid in the stable. The horse finished counting and went to look for his friends. He found the cow first, and they both went to find the sheep. They looked and looked, but they could not find the sheep. The horse said, "Sheep, where are you?" The tired sheep came out of the stable and said, "Here I am! I was hiding in the stable." All the friends laughed and played more games. They had a fun day together in the stable.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful cat named Tom. Tom had a favorite toy, a long string. He loved to play with the string all day long. One day, Tom saw the string on top of a high table. He wanted to play with it, but he could not reach it. Tom tried to jump, but he could not manage to get it. He felt sad and sat down on the floor. Tom's friend, a little girl named Lucy, saw him. She asked, "Tom, why are you sad?" Tom looked at the string and then at Lucy. Lucy understood and said, "Don't worry, Tom! I will help you." Lucy picked up the string and gave it to Tom. Tom was very happy and thanked Lucy. They played together with the string all day long, and they both had lots of fun.
