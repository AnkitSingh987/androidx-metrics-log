Category: Beginner
Title: The Palace and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In the palace, there was a king and a queen. They had a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to run and play in the palace. One day, Lily was worried. She lost her toy bear. She looked in her room, but it was not there. She asked her mom, the queen, "Mom, have you seen my bear?" Her mom did not know where the bear was. She just shrugged and said, "I don't know, Lily. Maybe ask your dad." Lily went to her dad, the king. She asked him, "Dad, have you seen my bear?" Her dad also shrugged and said, "I don't know, Lily. Let's look for it together." So, Lily and her dad looked all around the palace. At last, they found the toy bear in the garden. Lily was so happy! She hugged her dad and said, "Thank you for helping me!" From that day on, Lily always knew that her mom and dad would help her when she was worried.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to organize her toys. One day, she decided to organize a tea party for her friends, Tim and Sue. At the tea party, Lucy set up a clear cup and a big plate of cookies. She also had a little bowl of salt. Tim and Sue came and sat down at the table. They were excited for the tea party. Lucy said, "I have a clear cup for our tea and cookies to share. But we need to be careful with the salt. It is not sugar!" Tim and Sue nodded and smiled. Tim and Sue tasted the cookies and said, "Yum! These are very good, Lucy!" They all enjoyed the tea party and had lots of fun together. After the tea party, Lucy, Tim, and Sue helped to clean up. They put the clear cup, the plate, and the bowl of salt away. They were happy to have a fun day together.

Lily and Tom were playing with blocks. They wanted to build a big tower. They put one block on top of another block. They tried to make the tower very high. But the tower was not strong. It fell down with a loud noise. Lily and Tom were sad. They looked at the broken blocks. "Let's try again," Tom said. "We can succeed if we work together." They nodded. They picked up the blocks. They looked for the best blocks. They made a new plan. They helped each other. They built a new tower. It was stronger and higher than the old tower. They were very happy. They smiled and clapped their hands. "Look, we did it!" Lily said. "We succeeded!" They hugged each other. They admired their tower. It was peaceful and quiet. They felt proud and good. They learned a lesson. They knew that they could do anything if they tried hard and cooperated.

Once upon a time, there were twin boys named Tim and Tom. They lived in a big, rich house with their mom and dad. Tim and Tom loved to play and have fun together. One sunny day, Tim and Tom went to the park with their mom. They saw a big tree and decided to climb it. When they reached the top, they could see the whole park. They felt so happy and excited, they began to clap their hands. "Look, Mom!" Tim shouted. "We are so high up!" Tom clapped even louder. Their mom smiled and said, "Good job, my brave boys!" As they climbed down, Tim and Tom saw a little girl crying. They went to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy." The twins wanted to help, so they looked around the park. Soon, Tim found the toy under a bush. He gave it to the little girl, who stopped crying and started to clap. She said, "Thank you, Tim and Tom!" The twins felt proud and happy. They all clapped and played together for the rest of the day.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big puddle. It was shiny and wet. "Let's jump in the puddle!" said Lily. She ran to the puddle and jumped. Splash! She was wet and happy. "Me too!" said Tom. He ran to the puddle and jumped. Splash! He was wet and happy too. They jumped and splashed and laughed. They had fun. But then, a big boy came. He was rude. He did not like Lily and Tom. He wanted the puddle for himself. "Go away!" he said. He pushed Lily and Tom. They fell on the grass. They were sad and hurt. The big boy laughed. He jumped in the puddle. Splash! He was wet and mean. But then, a dog came. It was a big dog. It liked puddles too. It ran to the puddle and jumped. Splash! It was wet and friendly. The big boy was scared. He did not like dogs. He ran away. He was wet and silly. The dog wagged its tail. It licked Lily and Tom. They smiled. They liked the dog. They got up and jumped in the puddle again. Splash! They were wet and happy again. The dog jumped with them. Splash! It was wet and happy too. They jumped and splashed and laughed. They had fun. They were friends.
