Category: Beginner
Title: Daisy the Cow and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Daisy. Daisy lived in a big field with her friends. They liked to eat grass and play all day. One day, a big cloud appeared in the sky. It was not easy to see, but Daisy knew it was there. Daisy said to her friend, "Look, a big cloud is up in the sky!" Her friend looked up and said, "Oh no! What do we do?" They were scared because they did not like big clouds. The big cloud made them feel small. Daisy had an idea. "Let's hide under the tree until the big cloud goes away," she said. All her friends agreed. They hid under the tree and waited. After some time, the big cloud went away. Daisy and her friends were happy again. They went back to eating grass and playing in the field.

Once upon a time, there was a wild pirate named Tom. He lived on a big boat. Tom had a pet bird named Polly. They liked to play catch with a red ball. One day, a mean pirate named Sam came to their boat. Sam wanted to take Tom's red ball. Tom said, "No, you can't have my ball!" Sam got angry and tried to catch the ball. Tom and Polly worked together to stop Sam. Polly flew up high and took the ball from Sam. She gave it to Tom. Tom said, "Thank you, Polly!" Sam was sad because he could not catch the ball. Tom said, "We can all play catch together!" Sam smiled and they all played catch. They became good friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a small bird named Tim. Tim was not a big bird like the other birds. He was very small. But Tim had a friend named Carl. Carl was a big crane. Carl and Tim liked to play together. One day, Tim and Carl saw a sharp rock. Tim wanted to play with the rock, but Carl said it was not safe. Tim did not listen to Carl. He flew down and tried to pick up the rock with his beak. But the rock was too sharp, and it hurt Tim's beak. Tim cried because his beak hurt. Carl felt sad for his friend. He used his big crane neck to pick up the rock and put it far away. Tim learned that he should listen to Carl. They played together and had fun, but they stayed away from sharp rocks.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big red ball that she loved to play with. One sunny day, it was very hot outside. Mia sighed and said, "I wish I could play with my ball inside." In her room, Mia saw a long thread hanging from her shirt. She had an idea! She tied the thread to her big red ball. Now she could play with her ball inside without it rolling away. Later, as Mia played with her ball on the thread, she heard a loud noise. Her ball had knocked over a glass of water! The water spilled everywhere. Mia felt sad, but she learned that playing with her ball inside was not a good idea.

One day, a chubby cat named Bob went for a walk. He liked to see new things. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Bob was very happy. As he walked, Bob saw a big, pretty fountain. The water went up and down. It made a nice sound. Bob wanted to play in the water. Bob jumped into the fountain. He splashed and played. He had lots of fun. Then, he went home. Bob had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, there lived a girl named Sue. She had a brush that she loved very much. Sue would use her brush to make her hair nice and pretty every day. One sunny day, Sue went out to play. She took her brush with her. While she played, she met a small bug. The bug was sad. Sue asked the bug, "Why are you sad?" The bug said, "I am lost. I can't find my way home." Sue wanted to help the bug. She had an idea. She used her brush to make a path for the bug. The bug followed the path made by the brush. But then, something unexpected happened! The bug pinched Sue's finger. Sue was surprised. The bug said, "I'm sorry, Sue. I didn't mean to pinch you. I was just so happy to find my way home." Sue smiled and forgave the bug. They became good friends. From that day on, they played together every day, and Sue always had her brush with her.
