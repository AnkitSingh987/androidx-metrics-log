Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Tower and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a tall tower. The tower was in a big park. Many kids came to play there every day. They liked to run, jump, and laugh in the park. The tower was their favorite place to play. One day, a pretty bird came to the tower. The bird was graceful and could fly high. The kids saw the bird and wanted to play with it. They tried to climb the tower to be close to the bird. The bird saw the kids and wanted to play, too. The bird had an idea. It found a big slide near the tower. The bird showed the kids the slide. The kids were happy and took turns to slide down. They laughed and played with the bird all day. The bird and the kids became good friends.

---

One day, Tom the dog saw a dull bird stuck in a tree. The bird was sad and scared. Tom wanted to help the bird. He thought, "I will rescue the bird!" Tom tried to climb the tree, but it was too big. He tried to jump, but it was too high. Then, he saw a box. He pushed the box to the tree and climbed on it. Now, he could reach the bird. Tom said, "Don't be scared, bird. I will rescue you!" The bird was happy. Tom used his spirit and strength to help the bird get free. The bird said, "Thank you, Tom!" They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a baby. The mommy loved the baby very much. They went to the park together every day and played. One day while they were at the park they found a smelly shoe. The baby looked at the shoe and said "Mommy, I want it!" The mommy said "No, baby, we don't need it." But the baby was determined to get the smelly shoe, and so the mommy said "Okay, I'll keep it safe until you remember why you wanted it". The baby couldn't remember why it wanted the smelly shoe, so the mommy and the baby kept going to the park every day. The baby kept asking for the smelly shoe, but the mommy said no. Eventually, one day when the baby was playing at the park, a bird dropped a seed and the seed got stuck in the smelly shoe. The baby smiled and said "Oh yeah! That's why I wanted it!" The mommy and baby laughed, and from then on the smelly shoe was the baby's favorite toy. The End

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They saw a small hive on a tree. They wanted to examine it. "Look, bees!" Lily said. "They make honey. Yum!" "Can we go closer?" Ben asked. "I want to see them." Lily nodded. They walked slowly to the tree. They were careful not to make noise. They saw many bees flying in and out of the hive. They heard a buzzing sound. They saw some honey dripping from the hive. "Wow, so cool!" Ben said. "Can we touch it?" Lily shook her head. "No, no, no. Bees can sting. It hurts. We have to be nice to them." Ben frowned. He wanted to touch the honey. He reached out his hand. "Ben, no!" Lily shouted. "Stop!" But it was too late. A bee saw Ben's hand and flew to him. It stung him on his finger. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben cried. He pulled his hand back. He saw a red bump on his finger. He felt pain. Lily ran to him. She hugged him. She said sorry. "I'm sorry, Ben. I'm sorry. Are you okay?" Ben sobbed. He showed her his finger. "It hurts, Lily. It hurts." Lily felt sad. She said sorry again. "I'm sorry, Ben. I'm sorry. Let's go to mom. She can help you." They ran to the house. They found mom. They told her what happened. Mom looked at Ben's finger. She said it was not bad. She put some ice on it. She gave him a kiss. She said it would be better soon. Ben felt better. He smiled. He thanked mom. He looked at Lily. He said sorry. "I'm sorry, Lily. I'm sorry. I was silly. I should have listened to you." Lily smiled. She hugged him. She said it was okay. "It's okay, Ben. It's okay. You are not silly. You are curious. But you have to be careful. Bees are good, but they can sting." Ben nodded. He said he understood. They said they loved each other. They said they loved mom. They said they loved bees. They went back to the garden. They played with other things. They did not touch the hive. They were happy.
