Category: Beginner
Title: Mark the Graceful Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Mark. Mark loved to play with his ball. One day, Mark heard about a fun game. The prize was a big, yummy fish. Mark went to the game with his friend, Dog. They played and had lots of fun. Mark jumped high and ran fast. Dog barked and wagged his tail. They both wanted to win the prize. At the end of the game, Mark and Dog were the best. They both won the prize. Mark said, "Let's share the fish!" Dog agreed, "Yes, let's share!" So, the graceful cat and his friend, Dog, shared the yummy fish and were very happy.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a card on the floor. The card had a picture of people dancing. Mia was excited. She ran to her mom and showed her the card. "Mom, can I learn to dance like the people on the card?" asked Mia. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia, you can learn to dance." The next day, Mia and her mom went to a dance class. Mia was very excited. She put on her dance shoes and started to dance. She danced and danced until she was tired. At the end of the day, Mia and her mom went home. Mia was happy. She had learned to dance like the people on the card. She couldn't wait to show her friends her new dance moves.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a delicate toy named Bunny. Amy and Bunny were best friends. They liked to play together all day. One day, Bunny got stuck in a tree. A long cord was around Bunny's foot. Amy was sad and wanted to save Bunny. She thought and thought about how to help her friend. Then, Amy had an idea. She found a strong stick and used it to reach Bunny. She pulled the cord with the stick, and Bunny was free. Amy saved her best friend, and they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Mia. She had a team of friends who played together every day. They liked to run, jump, and laugh in the park. One day, Mia fell down and hurt her leg. She cried because it hurt a lot. Her friends stopped playing and came to help her. "Don't cry, Mia," said her friend Tom. "We are here to comfort you." All her friends hugged Mia and helped her stand up. They walked with her back to her house. Mia's mom put a bandage on her leg. Mia felt better and thanked her team of friends. They all smiled and went back to the park to play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird had a feather. The feather was not impressive. The bird wanted to become impressive too. One day, the bird saw a big tree. The tree was very tall. The bird wanted to fly to the top. The bird tried and tried. It was hard, but the bird did not give up. At last, the bird reached the top. The bird looked down and saw all its friends. They were happy for the bird. The bird's feather became impressive. The bird was proud and happy.

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to play with their toys. But they do not like to share. One day, they find a big purple box in the attic. They open it and see many books and papers. "What is this?" Tim asks. "It is a university," Kim says. "Dad and Mom went there when they were young. They learned many things." "I want to learn too," Tim says. He grabs a book and tries to read it. "No, it is mine," Kim says. She pulls the book from Tim's hands. "You cannot read. You are too small." Tim gets angry. He pushes Kim and takes another book. Kim pushes him back and takes another paper. They start to fight and tear the books and papers. "Stop!" a voice says. It is Dad. He comes into the attic and sees the mess. He is very sad and angry. "What have you done?" he asks. "These are my university things. They are very important to me. You have ruined them. They will rot now." Tim and Kim feel sorry. They did not know that the purple box was Dad's. They did not mean to hurt him. "We are sorry, Dad," they say. "We did not know. We just wanted to play." Dad sighs. He hugs them and says, "I know, my loves. But you have to be careful with other people's things. And you have to share with each other. Can you do that?" Tim and Kim nod. They say, "Yes, Dad. We can do that." Dad smiles. He says, "Good. Now let's clean up this mess and find something else to play with. Maybe we can read a story together. How about that?" Tim and Kim smile too. They say, "Yes, Dad. We would like that." They help Dad to clean up the attic. Then they go downstairs and read a story together. They are happy and they do not fight anymore.
