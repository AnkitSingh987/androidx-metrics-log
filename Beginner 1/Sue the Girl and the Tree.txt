Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very independent. She liked to do things all by herself. One day, Sue saw a pear tree in her yard. The pears were big and yummy. Sue wanted to get a pear, so she asked her mom, "Can I pick a pear, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Sue was so happy. She went to the tree and tried to reach for a pear. It was too high, so she got a chair to stand on. Sue picked a big pear and went inside. She wanted to eat it, but first, she needed to wash it. Sue turned on the water and washed her pear. After that, she shut the water off. Sue was proud of herself for being independent. She enjoyed her yummy pear and shared it with her mom.

One day, a small, unique fish named Bob lived in a stream. He had many friends in the stream. Bob had a big urge to play with his friends today. Bob swam to his friend, Sam. "Let's go play near the big rock," Bob said. Sam said, "Okay, let's go!" They swam and played all day. They knew something fun would happen near the big rock. As they played, they found a hidden cave behind the big rock. They went in and found a treasure chest. Bob and Sam were so happy. The fun thing they thought would happen came true. They shared the treasure with all their friends in the stream.

One day, a pretty bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a tire on the ground. The tire was big and round. The bird wanted to play with the tire. So, the pretty bird flew down to the tire. A little girl named Amy saw the pretty bird and the tire. She went to the tire and said, "Hi, bird! Do you want to play with me?" The bird nodded its head. Amy and the bird played with the tire. They rolled it, and they jumped on it. After playing, Amy said, "I like you, bird." She gave the bird a big hug. The bird hugged her back. They were very happy. Amy and the pretty bird became best friends. They played with the tire every day.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bunny named Bob. Bob liked to look for things in the forest. One day, he found a tool. It was a hammer. Bob hopped to his friend, the squirrel named Sam. "Look, Sam!" said Bob. "I found a tool!" Sam looked at the hammer and said, "Wow, Bob! That's a big hammer. What will you do with it?" Bob thought for a moment and said, "I will use it to help my friends!" So, Bob and Sam went around the forest. They used the hammer to fix the homes of their friends. They fixed a bird's nest and a beaver's dam. All the animals were happy and thanked Bob and Sam. They had learned that it is good to help others. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little hippo went to play outside. He saw a warm, soft ball on the ground. He picked it up and started to play with it. He was very happy with his new toy. He met a small bird who said, "Hey, that's my ball! Can I have it back?" The little hippo felt sad. He didn't want to give the ball back, but he knew it was the right thing to do. So, he gave the ball back to the bird. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you! If you need help, I will be there for you." Later, the little hippo found a broken toy car. He tried to fix it, but he couldn't. He remembered the bird's promise and called for help. The bird came and helped the little hippo replace the broken wheel on the toy car. Now, the toy car was as good as new. The little hippo and the bird became best friends. They played together every day, and they always helped each other when they needed it.
