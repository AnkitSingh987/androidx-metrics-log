Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Restless Bunny and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a restless bunny named Bob. Bob liked to hop and play all day. He loved to make new friends in the forest. One day, Bob found a carrot. It was big and orange. He wanted to share it with his friends. So, he hopped and hopped to find them. Bob found his friend, the bird. They ate the carrot together. They were very happy. Then, they played and had fun all day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was weak and could not fly far. One day, a big storm came. Rain fell hard and the wind blew strong. Tim was scared and hid under a tree. A kind squirrel named Sam saw Tim. Sam said, "Come to my home, Tim. We can stay dry there." Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Sam." They walked to Sam's home together. But the storm was too strong. The rain did not stop. Tim and Sam's home began to soak. The water came in and they could not stay dry. In the end, the home was gone and they were wet and cold. Tim and Sam were very sad.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a kind wolf. The wolf had many friends. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, the kind wolf saw a big bird. The bird was sad. The kind wolf asked, "Why are you sad?" The big bird said, "I lost my egg. Can you help me find it?" The kind wolf wanted to help. They looked for the egg together. They looked and looked, but they could not find the egg. The kind wolf saw a big tree and said, "Let's look up there." The kind wolf pointed at the tree. The big bird flew up and found the egg. But when the big bird tried to take the egg, it fell and broke. The big bird was very sad. The kind wolf felt bad too. They could not fix the egg. The kind wolf and the big bird were sad, and they could not play anymore. The forest was not a happy place that day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a dead bird on the ground. Tim looked at the bird with sad eyes. He asked his mom, "Can we bring the bird home?" His mom said, "No, Tim. The bird is dead. We can't bring it home." Tim felt sad and wanted to help the bird. He saw another little boy named Sam at the park. Sam had a toy shovel. Tim asked Sam, "Can you help me with the dead bird?" Sam said, "Yes, we can dig a hole and put the bird in it." So, Tim and Sam dug a hole and put the bird in it. They covered the hole with dirt. Tim felt better because he helped the bird. Tim said, "Thank you, Sam." They played together at the park and became friends.

One day, a little mouse named Tim went to study. He liked to learn new things. Tim was looking for something to eat. He found a big, smooth cheese. Tim was very happy. "Yum! I love cheese!" said Tim. He took a big bite. But the cheese was not real. It was made of rubber. Tim was very surprised. He thought it was funny. Tim went to tell his friend, Cat. "Cat, I found a funny cheese!" he said. Cat laughed, "That's not real cheese, Tim! It's a toy!" They both laughed and played with the rubber cheese. Tim learned that not everything is what it seems.

Anna liked to print. She had many colors and papers. She printed flowers and stars and hearts. She printed her name and her cat's name. She printed a big picture for her mom. "Mom, look what I made for you!" Anna said. She ran to the kitchen with her picture. She was very happy. Mom was making breakfast. She had a pot of oat and a bowl of fruit. She smiled when she saw Anna's picture. It was bright and pretty. "Wow, Anna, this is wonderful! Thank you so much!" Mom said. She hugged Anna and put the picture on the fridge. "You are very good at printing. Do you want some oat and fruit?" "Yes, please!" Anna said. She sat at the table and waited for her oat. She saw her picture on the fridge. She felt proud. But then she heard a noise. It was her cat, Fluffy. Fluffy was on the counter. Fluffy saw the fruit. Fluffy liked fruit. Fluffy jumped on the bowl and knocked it over. The fruit rolled on the floor. Fluffy chased the fruit. "Oh no, Fluffy!" Anna said. She got up from the table and ran to the counter. She tried to stop Fluffy. But Fluffy was too fast. Fluffy ran to the fridge. Fluffy saw Anna's picture. Fluffy liked paper. Fluffy scratched the picture. The picture tore. "Fluffy, no!" Anna said. She grabbed Fluffy and pulled him away. She looked at her picture. It was ruined. She felt sad. Mom heard the commotion. She turned off the stove and came to the counter. She saw the mess. She saw Anna's picture. She saw Anna's face. "Oh, Anna, I'm so sorry. Fluffy was naughty. He didn't mean to ruin your picture. He didn't know it was special." Mom said. She hugged Anna and Fluffy. "But don't worry. We can fix it. We can print another one. We can print anything you want. How about a rainbow?" Anna sniffed. She looked at Mom. She looked at Fluffy. She nodded. "Okay. A rainbow." Anna said. She smiled a little. She still liked to print. She still liked Fluffy. She still liked Mom. She still liked oat and fruit. She was still happy.
