Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Ship

Ben and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to make noises and pretend they were animals. Ben was a lion and Lily was a bird. They roared and chirped and had fun. Then they heard a knock on the door. It was their mom. She came in with a big smile on her face. She said, "Hi, kids. I have a surprise for you. Do you want to see it?" Ben and Lily nodded and ran to the door. They saw a man standing outside. He had a weird face. He had a big nose, a long beard, and a hat with feathers. He looked like a pirate. He said, "Ahoy, mateys. I'm your uncle Jack. I came to visit you." Ben and Lily stared at him. They did not know him. They felt scared. They hid behind their mom. They said, "Who is he? He has a weird face. We don't like him." Their mom hugged them and said, "Don't be afraid. He is my brother. He is your uncle. He lives far away. He came to say hello. He is very nice. He brought you some gifts. Do you want to see them?" Ben and Lily shook their heads. They did not want to see the gifts. They did not want to see the man. They said, "No, thank you. We want to play. We want him to leave." Their mom looked sad. She said, "I'm sorry, kids. He won't stay long. He just wants to meet you. He loves you. Can you be polite and say hi?" Ben and Lily did not want to be rude. They loved their mom. They peeked from behind her and said, "Hi, uncle Jack. Nice to meet you." Uncle Jack smiled and said, "Hi, Ben and Lily. Nice to meet you too. You are very cute. I'm sorry if I scared you. I don't mean to. I just like to dress like a pirate. It's fun. Do you like pirates?" Ben and Lily looked at each other. They liked pirates. They liked to dress up too. They said, "Yes, we like pirates. They are cool. Do you have a ship?" Uncle Jack nodded and said, "Yes, I do. I have a big ship. It's called the Black Pearl. Do you want to see it?" Ben and Lily nodded and said, "Yes, we want to see it. Can we go with you?" Uncle Jack laughed and said, "Sure, you can. But only if your mom says yes. And only if you promise to be brave and have fun." Ben and Lily looked at their mom. She smiled and said, "Yes, you can. But only for a little while. And only if you promise to be careful and listen to your uncle." Ben and Lily agreed and said, "We promise. We promise." They took their uncle's hand and went to see his ship. They forgot about his weird face. They only saw his kind eyes. They had a lot of fun. They learned to say "Arrr" and "Yo-ho-ho". They played with his parrot and his sword. They felt like real pirates. They were glad they did not leave. They were glad they met their uncle. He was not so weird after all. He was very nice. They loved him.

---

Lily and Max were having breakfast with Mom and Dad. They liked to eat muffins with butter and jam. Lily had a blueberry muffin and Max had a chocolate chip muffin. They were very yummy. "Can I have some of your muffin, Lily?" Max asked. He wanted to try the blueberries. "OK, but only a little bit," Lily said. She broke off a small piece and gave it to Max. She was a good sister. Max took a bite and smiled. He liked the blueberries too. He said, "Thank you, Lily. Your muffin is very good." "You're welcome, Max. Your muffin is good too," Lily said. She wanted to try the chocolate chips. They swapped their muffins and tasted each other's. They were both happy. Mom and Dad watched them and smiled. They were proud of their children. They said, "You two are so adorable. We love you very much." Lily and Max hugged Mom and Dad. They said, "We love you too." Then they rubbed their tummies and finished their muffins. They were full and ready for the day.
