Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tape

One day, a little girl named Sue found a smooth tape. She was very happy. She wanted to show it to her friend Tom. Sue ran to Tom's house. "Tom, look what I found!" Sue said. Tom looked at the smooth tape. "Wow, it's so pretty!" Tom said. They both loved the tape very much. They decided to use the tape to make a game. Sue and Tom played with the tape all day. They made a line on the ground with the tape. They jumped over it, and they ran around it. They had so much fun. But then, it was time for Sue to go home. As Sue walked home, she saw her mom. "Mom, look at my smooth tape!" Sue said. Her mom smiled. "I heard you mention it to Tom. It looks like you had fun today," her mom said. Sue nodded and went inside to show her dad the smooth tape too.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, red ball. He liked to play with the ball. He would pass the ball to his friends, and they would pass it back to him. They all had fun playing together. One day, while Tim was playing with the ball, he saw something on the ball. It was a fake thumb! Tim did not know how it got there. He showed it to his friends, and they all laughed. They thought it was funny to see a fake thumb on the ball. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and took the fake thumb off the ball. The bird flew away with the thumb in its beak. Tim and his friends were surprised. They never thought a bird would want a fake thumb. They all laughed again and kept playing with the big, red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus was very spoiled. It did not like to share the road with other cars. It would think, "I am the best, and I should have the road all to myself!" One day, the bus saw a small blue car on the road. The bus thought, "I will make the car go away!" So, the bus drove very fast and close to the small car. The small car was scared and moved to the side of the road. But then, the bus saw a big truck coming down the road. The truck was bigger than the bus! The bus thought, "Oh no, what will I do now?" The bus was scared and moved to the side of the road too. The truck passed by, and the bus learned to share the road with other cars.

Once upon a time, there was a big mill. The mill was very old and made loud sounds. Inside the mill, there lived a confused little mouse named Tim. Tim was always running around and bumping into things. One day, Tim met a sleepy cat named Sam. Sam said, "I am so tired. I want to sleep." Tim wanted to help Sam sleep. They looked for a quiet place in the mill, but it was too loud. Then, something unexpected happened. The wind stopped blowing, and the mill became very quiet. Sam was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Tim! Now I can sleep." Sam and Tim became best friends, and they both slept happily in the quiet mill.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and make friends. One day, Spot found a tiny teaspoon in the park. He picked it up with his mouth and carried it around. Spot saw a big tree that was trembling. He was curious and wanted to know why the tree was shaking. Spot went closer to the tree and saw a small bird sitting on a branch. The bird was trembling too because it was cold. Spot wanted to help the little bird. He put the teaspoon down and used his noisy bark to call his friends. Soon, all the animals came to help the bird. They all huddled around the bird to keep it warm. The bird stopped trembling and thanked Spot and his friends. From that day on, they all became best friends and played together in the park every day.
