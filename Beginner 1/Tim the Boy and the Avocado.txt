Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Avocado

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat avocado. One day, he found a big avocado tree in his yard. He was so happy! Tim wanted to pick an avocado, but a calm bird was sitting on the tree. The bird said, "Stop! This is my tree. You cannot take my avocados." Tim was sad, but he did not want to make the bird angry. Tim had an idea. He said to the bird, "If you give me an avocado, I will give you a toy." The bird liked the idea. So, the bird gave Tim an avocado, and Tim gave the bird a toy. They both were happy and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a bird who loved to whistle. The bird lived in a big tree. The bird had many friends in the tree. They all liked to play together. But one day, a big wind came and made the tree shake. The bird was scared. The wind made the bird fall from the tree. The bird was not happy. The bird wanted to fly back to the tree but could not. The bird's friends saw the bird on the ground. They wanted to help the bird get back to the tree. The bird's friends tried many things to help the bird. They tried to push the bird. They tried to carry the bird. But nothing worked. Then, one friend had a good idea. The friend told the bird to whistle a happy song. The bird whistled the song, and the wind heard it. The wind liked the song and wanted to help the bird. The wind blew the bird back into the tree. The bird was successful! The bird and its friends were so happy. They all played together in the tree again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with bubbles. One day, Amy found a big bubble wand in her toy box. She was very happy and went outside to play. Amy saw her friend Tom. Tom was sad because he had a spoiled ice cream. Amy asked him, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "My ice cream is spoiled, and I can't eat it." Amy had an idea. She said, "Let's play with my bubble wand! It will make you happy." Amy and Tom played with the bubble wand. They made big and small bubbles. They laughed and had fun. Tom forgot about his spoiled ice cream. They played until the sun went down. From that day on, Amy and Tom always played with bubbles when they were sad.

Once upon a time there was a bald man who loved to drive. Every weekend the man would drive his car to the park. He would park his car, then get out with a big tent. The man always brought the tent to the park to sleep in. One day, a little 3 year old girl saw him getting out of his car with the tent. She wondered what he was doing and decided to ask him. The little girl walked up to the bald man and asked, "What are you doing?" The man smiled and said, "I am going camping. I bring my tent here to sleep in. Would you like to come?" The little girl was so excited. She said that she would love to go camping with him. So they got in the man's car and drove to the park. When they arrived, the man set up his tent and the little girl was amazed. They enjoyed the night camping in the woods under the stars. The little girl was so excited that she didn't want to leave when it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a rare toy called Match. Match was a soft, blue bear that could talk. Tim loved Match very much. One day, Tim was sad because he lost his ball. Match saw Tim cry and said, "Don't be sad, Tim. We can find your ball." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Match." They looked everywhere for the ball. They looked in the house and in the yard. At last, they found the ball under a big tree. Tim was happy and hugged Match. Match comforted Tim and said, "See, I told you we would find it." Tim and Match played with the ball all day. They were best friends forever.
