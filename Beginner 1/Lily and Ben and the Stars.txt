Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Stars

Lily and Ben like to play outside. They like to run, jump, and look at the sky. The sky is big and blue and has many things. Sometimes the sky has white clouds. Sometimes the sky has yellow sun. Sometimes the sky has bright stars. One day, Lily and Ben want to see the stars. They ask their mom if they can stay up late. Their mom says yes, but they have to eat dinner and brush their teeth first. Lily and Ben are happy. They eat their dinner and brush their teeth fast. They want to see the stars soon. They go outside with their mom and look at the sky. But the sky is still blue. There are no stars. Lily and Ben are sad. They are impatient. They want to see the stars now. They ask their mom why the sky has no stars. Their mom says the sky is not dark enough. The stars only come out when the sky is dark. They have to wait a little longer. Lily and Ben do not like to wait. They want to see the stars now. They get mad. They get bored. They get tired. They start to cry. Their mom hugs them and says it is okay. She says the stars will come soon. She says they can sing a song while they wait. She starts to sing "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star". Lily and Ben join her. They like the song. They calm down. They look at the sky again. The sky is darker now. They see some stars. They see more and more stars. The sky is full of stars. Lily and Ben are happy. They smile. They point at the stars. They say the stars are beautiful. They say the stars are worth the wait. They thank their mom for letting them stay up late. They hug their mom and say they love her. They go inside and go to bed. They dream of the stars.

One day, a gifted dog named Max was playing in the park. He was very good at running and catching balls. Max saw a girl named Lily. She had a ball and wanted to play with him. Lily threw the ball far away and said, "Max, come!" Max ran very fast to get the ball. He picked it up with his mouth and came back to Lily. Lily was happy and said, "Good boy, Max! Now, heel!" Max sat down by Lily's side and gave her the ball. Lily and Max played all day in the park. They had so much fun together. Max was a very gifted dog and Lily loved playing with him. They became best friends and played every day.

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Spot. Spot loved to dance all day long. He would dance in the morning, in the afternoon, and even at night. His dance made everyone happy. One day, Spot found a big apple on the ground. He wanted to eat it, but it was old and starting to rot. Spot's friend, a little bird named Tweet, told him not to eat the apple because it was not good for him. Spot listened to Tweet and left the apple alone. Instead, they played and danced together all day long. Spot learned that it was better to have fun with his friends than to eat something that could make him sick. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a good boy named Tim. He had a friend named Sam. Tim and Sam liked to play outside. They were very good friends. One day, they found a shield. The shield was big and strong. Tim said, "Wow, look at this shield!" Sam said, "It's so cool! Can I play with it?" Tim said, "Yes, I will lend it to you." Sam played with the shield. He felt like a hero. Tim and Sam took turns with the shield. They had lots of fun. They were happy to share the shield. And they were the best of friends.

Tom and Lily were playing in the field. They liked to run and jump and roll in the grass. They saw some flowers and picked them for their mom. They were happy. But then they heard a loud noise. It was a big dog. It was barking and running towards them. It had sharp teeth and dirty fur. Tom and Lily were scared. They dropped the flowers and ran away. The dog chased them. It was faster than them. It caught up with them and jumped on them. Tom and Lily fell down. They screamed and cried. They trembled with fear. But then they heard another noise. It was their dad. He had heard them and came to help. He shouted at the dog and scared it away. He picked up Tom and Lily and hugged them. He said they were brave and he was proud of them. He took them home and gave them cookies and milk. They were safe and happy.
