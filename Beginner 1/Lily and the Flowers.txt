Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Flowers

Lily liked to grow flowers in her garden. She had a small pot with soil and seeds. Every day, she watered the soil and waited for the seeds to grow. She hoped they would grow into pretty flowers. One day, she heard a loud noise outside. She ran to the window and saw a big truck. The truck was digging up the road near her house. It made a lot of dust and noise. Lily was scared. She covered her ears and hid under her bed. She forgot about her pot with soil and seeds. She did not water it for many days. The soil became dry and hard. The seeds did not grow. They stayed small and brown. Lily's mom saw the pot and felt sad. She knew Lily loved flowers. She took the pot and put it in the kitchen. She watered the soil and put it near the window. She hoped the seeds would grow. After a few days, the truck left. The road was fixed. It was quiet again. Lily came out of her bed. She saw her pot in the kitchen. She saw something green poking out of the soil. She saw tiny buds on the green stems. She smiled. Her seeds were growing. They were growing into flowers.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy bear named Ben. Ben was a bit ugly, but Lily loved him very much. They played together every day and had lots of fun. One day, Lily and her friend Tom had a fight. Tom wanted to play with Ben, but Lily said no. Tom got angry and took Ben away. Lily was very sad and began to weep. She wanted her ugly bear back. Lily's mom saw her weeping and asked what was wrong. Lily told her mom about the fight with Tom. Her mom talked to Tom's mom, and soon Ben was back with Lily. She hugged her ugly bear and stopped weeping. Lily and Tom said sorry to each other and promised to share toys from now on.

Tim liked to play with his pot. He put it on his head and pretended to be a soldier. He banged it with a spoon and made loud noises. He filled it with water and poured it on the grass. He had fun with his pot. But his mom did not like his pot. She said it was for cooking, not for playing. She said he made too much noise and mess. She said he had to quit playing with his pot and put it back in the kitchen. She was angry with Tim. Tim did not want to quit. He liked his pot. He said no to his mom. He ran away with his pot. He hid behind the tree. He thought his mom could not find him. He was naughty. But his mom did find him. She saw his pot shining in the sun. She came behind the tree and grabbed his pot. She said he had to go to his room and take a nap. She said he was tired and needed to rest. She was very angry with Tim. Tim was sad. He lost his pot. He had to go to his room. He had to take a nap. He did not want to rest. He wanted to play. He cried in his bed. He wished he had his pot. He was sorry.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a new red collar. He was very happy with his new collar. He liked to run and play in the park with his friends. One day, Spot was playing with a ball. He ran fast to catch the ball. As he ran, his new collar began to feel loose. Spot did not notice it and kept playing. Suddenly, Spot's collar fell off. It dropped onto the ground. Spot stopped playing and looked at his collar. His friends helped him put the collar back on. Spot was happy to have his new collar back, and they all continued to play together.

Jill and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big dog with a spike on his collar. The dog looked mean and scary, but he was wagging his tail and barking. "Let's go pet him!" Tom said. "He looks friendly." "No, don't!" Jill said. "He might bite us. We don't know him. We should trust our mom and dad. They told us not to touch strange dogs." But Tom did not listen to Jill. He ran towards the dog and tried to touch his spike. The dog did not like that. He growled and snapped at Tom, making him cry. "Help! Help!" Tom shouted. "The dog is hurting me!" Jill ran to get help. She found a man who was the dog's owner. He came and pulled the dog away from Tom. He said he was sorry and that his dog was spoiled. He said he should have kept him on a leash. Jill and Tom were both scared and hurt. They hugged each other and went home. They learned a lesson that day. They learned to trust their parents and to be careful with animals they don't know. They also learned to be kind and not to spoil their pets.
