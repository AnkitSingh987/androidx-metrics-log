Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Sam

One day, Tom went to his friend Sam's house. Tom saw a thin, round thing on the table. It was an ashtray. Tom thought it was a toy, so he asked Sam if he could borrow it. Sam said yes. Tom took the ashtray to his house and played with it. He put small things in it and moved it around. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom's mom saw the ashtray and was not happy. She told Tom that it was not a toy and that it was for grown-ups only. Tom felt sad and went back to Sam's house. He gave the ashtray back to Sam and told him what happened. Sam said he was sorry and didn't know it was not a toy. Then, Sam showed Tom a real toy they could play with. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom was a harmless cat who loved to play and run. One day, while he was playing, Tom fell and hurt his leg. He felt sad because he could not play with his friends. Tom's mom told him not to worry. She said that his leg would heal soon. Tom waited and waited, but his leg still hurt. He started to think that maybe it would never get better. One day, a little bird came to visit Tom. The bird saw Tom's hurt leg and decided to help. The bird flew away and came back with a special leaf. Tom didn't know that this leaf had magic powers. The bird put the leaf on Tom's leg, and something unexpected happened. His leg healed right away! Tom was so happy, he jumped and played with the bird. From that day on, Tom and the bird became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a big dream. He wanted to find a shiny copper coin. Tom looked for the copper coin every day. He looked under his bed, in the yard, and even in his toy box. But he could not find it. One day, Tom went to the park. The sun was light and warm. Tom played with his friends. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Then, Tom saw something shiny on the ground. He picked it up. It was the copper coin! Tom was so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The copper coin began to talk! It said, "Thank you for finding me, Tom. I am a magic coin. I can make your dreams come true." Tom was surprised. He wished to have more fun with his friends. The magic coin made his dream come true. Tom and his friends played and laughed all day long.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Sam and Jane. Sam had a goat and he wanted to take it for a walk. But it was cold outside and Jane didn't want to go. “Please come,” begged Sam. “My goat needs some exercise and fresh air.” Jane kept shaking her head, but Sam kept insisting. “Let's go,” he said. Finally, Jane gave in and she put on her coat. Sam lead the goat by a rope and the two friends walked around the block. At the park, Sam and Jane watched the goat eating grass. Soon, the goat was so full that he couldn't move. Sam and Jane laughed together. Afterwards, they walked back home, with the goat in tow. Even though it was cold, they felt happy and content.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl who lived in a big tree. The owl loved to sleep during the day and stay awake at night. One day, a little bird came to live in the same tree. The little bird liked to make a lot of noise all day, which made it hard for the wise owl to sleep. The wise owl had an idea to delay the little bird's noise. He asked the bird to play a game of hide and seek. The little bird was very happy and agreed to play. The wise owl told the little bird to hide while he counted to ten. This made the little bird stop making noise for a while. The wise owl was very smart. He knew that the little bird would make noise again after the game. So, he taught the little bird a new song to sing. This song was very quiet and pretty. The little bird loved the new song and sang it all day. The wise owl could finally sleep during the day, and they both lived happily in the big tree.
