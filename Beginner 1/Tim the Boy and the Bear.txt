Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bear

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a toy on a high shelf. He wanted the toy very much. But his mom said, "We have to delay buying the toy until your birthday." Tim was sad, but he waited for his birthday. On his birthday, he was glad to see a big box. He thought it was the toy from the shelf. He opened the box, but it was not the toy. It was a big, soft bear. His mom said, "I know you wanted the toy, but I saw this bear and thought you would like it more." Tim hugged the bear and smiled. He was happy his mom got him the bear instead of the toy from the shelf.

Jenny and Tom are friends. They like to play with dolls and cars. One day, they find a new toy in the park. It is a fluffy cat with a bell on its neck. It is adorable. Jenny and Tom both want the cat. They say, "It is mine!" They pull the cat from each other. The cat does not like it. It meows and scratches. Jenny and Tom cry. A lady comes to the park. She sees Jenny and Tom and the cat. She says, "This is my cat. His name is Spot. He ran away from home. I was looking for him. Why are you hurting him?" Jenny and Tom feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry. We did not know. We just wanted to play with him. He is adorable." The lady smiles. She says, "I understand. He is adorable. But he is not a toy. He is a living thing. He needs love and care. Do you want to pet him and say sorry?" Jenny and Tom nod. They pet the cat and say sorry. The cat purrs and rubs his neck on them. He forgives them. Jenny and Tom are happy. They say, "Thank you, lady. Can we be your friends?" The lady says, "Of course. You can be my friends. And you can play with Spot sometimes. But you have to be gentle and kind. Can you do that?" Jenny and Tom say, "Yes, we can. We will be gentle and kind. We love Spot. He is adorable." The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to catch a ball with his friends. They were all very happy to play together. Tim had a clean watch his mom gave to him. The watch was special because it could tell the time. While playing, Tim's ball went far away. Tim ran to get the ball, but he saw a big dog. The dog took the ball in its mouth. Tim was scared and said, "Please give my ball back." The dog did not listen and ran away with the ball. Tim's friends came to help. They all tried to catch the dog but it was too fast. Finally, the dog stopped to drink water. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "Let's give the dog a treat." They gave the dog a treat and it dropped the ball. Tim got his ball back and they all went back to play. Tim looked at his clean watch and said, "It's time to go home." They all said goodbye and went home.

Once upon a time there was a boy who liked to sail. He was very popular and all the other kids wanted to sail with him. One day he decided to go sailing all by himself. He put on his life vest, hopped in the boat, and then set sail. The boy sailed all around the sea. He saw fish and turtles, and he even saw a whale. He had so much fun sailing! When it was time to go home, he realized how late it was. He hurriedly made it back to the dock before the sun set. The next morning the boy woke up very early. He was so excited to go sailing again! He fixed the boat up, put on the life vest, and set sail. He explored and had so much fun, but soon the sun was setting again. He returned to the dock with a big smile on his face, and he knew that tomorrow he would sail again.
