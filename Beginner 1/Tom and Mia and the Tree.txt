Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Tree

Tom and Mia were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and make houses for them. Tom found a big beetle and put it in a jar. He showed it to Mia and said, "Look, I have a beetle. It is shiny and black. Do you want to see?" Mia nodded and came closer. She liked beetles too. She reached for the jar, but Tom pulled it away. He said, "No, it is mine. You can't have it. Find your own beetle." Mia felt sad and angry. She said, "That is not nice. You have to share. Give me the jar, please." Tom shook his head and ran away. He said, "No, no, no. It is mine, mine, mine. You can't catch me." Mia chased after him. She wanted to see the beetle. She wanted to play with Tom. She ran and ran, but Tom was faster. He ran to the big tree and climbed up. He said, "Ha, ha, ha. I am up here. You are down there. You can't get me or my beetle." Mia stopped and looked up. She saw Tom and the jar on a branch. She felt helpless. She couldn't climb the tree. She couldn't reach the jar. She said, "Tom, please come down. Please give me the jar. Please be nice." Tom laughed and said, "No, no, no. I am the king of the tree. You are the queen of the dirt. You can't have anything. You can only cry." Mia felt tears in her eyes. She didn't like Tom anymore. She didn't want to play with him anymore. She turned around and walked away. She said, "Fine. I don't need you or your beetle. I will find a better friend and a better bug." Tom saw Mia leaving. He felt lonely and bored. He looked at the beetle in the jar. It didn't move. It didn't make any sound. It didn't play with him. He said, "Beetle, beetle, beetle. You are not fun. You are not a friend. You are just a bug." He opened the jar and let the beetle go. He hoped it would fly away and leave him alone. But the beetle didn't fly away. It crawled on the branch and came closer to Tom. It opened its wings and made a loud noise. It said, "Roar, roar, roar. I am a beetle. I am shiny and black. I am not afraid of you. You are mean and selfish. You are not a king. You are just a boy." Tom felt scared and sorry. He didn't like the beetle anymore. He didn't want to be on the tree anymore. He wanted to be with Mia. He said, "Beetle, please go away. Please don't roar. Please don't hurt me." He climbed down the tree and ran to Mia. He said, "Mia, Mia, Mia. I am sorry. I was wrong. You can have the jar. You can have the beetle. You can have anything. Please be my friend again." Mia looked at Tom. She saw his face and heard his voice. She felt happy and kind. She said, "Tom, Tom, Tom. I forgive you. I am glad. You can keep the jar. You can keep the beetle. You can keep anything. But you have to share. Please be my friend again." Tom and Mia hugged and smiled. They went back to the garden. They looked for more bugs and made more houses for them. They played and shared and had fun. They were friends again.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Sue and Bob. They were walking along a cold road. Then, they saw a van parked nearby. Sue said, “Let’s go in the van and play.” Bob said, “Ok!” They were about to get inside when Sue noticed a small box. Sue asked, “What’s this?” Bob said, “It’s a box.” Sue said, “Let’s cut it open.” Bob said, “Ok!” So they found a pair of scissors and cut open the box. Inside, they found some cookies and some cards. Bob said, “Let’s eat the cookies.” Sue said, “Let’s play with the cards instead. It’s too cold to eat now.” So they played with the cards and had a lot of fun. The end.
