Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat had soft fur. The cat liked to play with a ball. One day, the cat saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play with the cat. The cat did not like the dog. The cat said, "No, I refuse to play with you." The dog felt sad. The dog went away. The cat played with the ball all alone. The cat did not look where it was going. The cat went into the road. A big car came. The cat did not move. The car hit the cat. The cat was dead. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a nice little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play near the beach. He liked to watch the waves and feel the soft sand on his feet. One sunny day, Tim saw a small crab stuck under a big rock. The crab was sad and wanted to go back to the water. Tim wanted to help the crab. So, he used all his strength to lift the big rock. When the rock was lifted, the happy crab ran back to the water. It waved its little claw to say thank you to Tim. Tim waved back and smiled. He felt good because he helped a new friend. And from that day on, Tim and the crab played together by the waves.

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big slide and run to it. Lily goes first. She sits on the slide and sets her bottle next to her. She slides down fast and laughs. Ben goes after her. He also sits on the slide and sets his bottle next to him. He slides down faster and louder. He shouts "Whee!" A dog sees the bottles on the slide. He is thirsty and curious. He runs to the slide and jumps on it. He knocks down the bottles and they roll down the slide. They make a lot of noise. They go "Clank! Clank! Clank!" Lily and Ben hear the noise and look up. They see the dog and the bottles. They are angry and scared. They say "Hey! Stop! That's our bottles! Go away!" The dog does not listen. He chases the bottles and tries to catch them. He barks and growls. He is noisy and naughty. He goes "Woof! Woof! Woof!" Lily and Ben run to the slide and get their bottles. They hug them and check if they are okay. They are wet and dirty, but not broken. They are happy and relieved. They say "Phew! That was close! Are you okay, bottle?" The dog sees that Lily and Ben are not happy with him. He feels sorry and ashamed. He lowers his head and wags his tail. He wants to say sorry. He goes "Whimper! Whimper! Whimper!" Lily and Ben see that the dog is sorry. They are not angry anymore. They are kind and forgiving. They say "It's okay, dog. We know you didn't mean to. But don't do that again, okay? Be a good dog." The dog nods and licks their hands. He is grateful and happy. He makes a new friend. He goes "Wag! Wag! Wag!"

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Thomas. He was filled with joy and his heart was compassionate. One day, he was playing in the park with his dad when a special string appeared. The string was so sparkly and beautiful! “Wow Dad! What is this shiny string?” cried Thomas. “That, my boy, is a magical string. We can play games with it," replied his father. Thomas had so much fun playing with the magical string. He felt so happy and excited. The next day, Thomas went back to the same spot in the park. He looked around and saw that the string was gone. He was so sad and he started to cry. But then, out of nowhere, the string appeared again! Thomas was so delighted. He ran to grab the string and hug it. He thanked his dad for being so compassionate and understanding. From then on, Thomas and his dad played with the string in the park each day.
