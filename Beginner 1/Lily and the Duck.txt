Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Duck

Lily was a little girl who liked to wander. She liked to see new things and find new friends. One day, she wandered into a big park. She saw many flowers, trees, birds and people. She felt happy and curious. She saw a big pond with many ducks. She wanted to feed them some bread. She had a bag of bread in her hand. She walked to the pond and threw some bread into the water. The ducks came closer and quacked. Lily smiled and threw more bread. "Hello, ducks!" she said. "Do you like bread? I like bread too. You are so cute and fluffy. Can I pet you?" One duck was very brave and friendly. It came out of the water and walked to Lily. It looked at her with its eye. Its eye was wide and shiny. Lily reached out her hand and touched its head. It felt soft and warm. The duck quacked again and nibbled her finger. Lily giggled and said, "You are tickling me, duck!" The duck and Lily became friends. They played and talked for a long time. Lily forgot that she had wandered away from her home. She did not see her mom and dad looking for her. She did not hear them calling her name. She only saw the duck and its eye. She was happy and safe.

Lily and Max are twins. They like to play together. But they do not like to get haircuts. They think haircuts are scary and hurt. One day, Mom says they need haircuts. She says their hair is too long and messy. She says they will look nice and neat after haircuts. Lily and Max do not want to go. They hide under the bed. Mom finds them and says, "Come on, kids. It's time to go to the salon. It will be fun. You can choose your own style and color. And they will serve you juice and cookies." Lily and Max are curious. They like juice and cookies. They like to choose things. They crawl out from under the bed. They follow Mom to the car. At the salon, they meet a nice lady. She says her name is Sue. She says she will give them haircuts. She shows them a book with pictures of different haircuts. Lily and Max look at the book. They see some haircuts they like. Lily says, "I want this one. It has braids and ribbons. It is pretty." Max says, "I want this one. It has spikes and stars. It is cool." Sue says, "OK, let's do it. You can sit on these chairs. They are like rockets. They can go up and down. And they have mirrors. You can see yourself." Lily and Max sit on the chairs. They feel excited. Sue puts a cape around them. She sprays water on their hair. She combs and cuts their hair. She makes braids and ribbons for Lily. She makes spikes and stars for Max. She says, "You are doing great. You are brave and still. Do you want some juice and cookies?" Lily and Max say, "Yes, please." Sue serves them juice and cookies. They drink and eat. They smile and say, "Thank you." Sue says, "You are welcome. You are very polite. And you look amazing. Do you like your haircuts?" Lily and Max look at the mirrors. They see their new haircuts. They like them very much. They say, "We love them. They are pretty and cool. Thank you, Sue." Sue says, "You are very welcome. I'm glad you like them. You are the best twins ever. Come back anytime." Lily and Max hug Sue. They say, "Bye, Sue. See you soon." They go to Mom. Mom says, "Wow, you look wonderful. I'm so proud of you. You were brave and polite. And you have beautiful haircuts. How do you feel?" Lily and Max say, "We feel happy. We like haircuts. They are fun. And we like Sue. She is nice. And we like juice and cookies. They are yummy." Mom says, "I'm happy too. You are the best kids ever. Let's go home and show Dad. He will love your haircuts too." They go to the car. They sing and laugh. They have a good day.
