Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fish

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They took a seat on a bench near the pond. Tim saw many fish in the water. They were swimming and playing together. Tim liked to watch the fish. Tim saw one fish that was different. It was heavy and big. The heavy fish could not swim fast like the other fish. Tim felt sad for the heavy fish. He wanted to help it. Tim asked his mom if they could give the heavy fish some food. They got some fish food and threw it in the water. The heavy fish ate the food and became happy. Tim and his mom smiled. They knew they had helped the heavy fish have a better day.

Once upon a time, there was a driver named Tom. He drove a big bus. The bus was very comfortable. People liked to ride on Tom's bus. One day, Tom saw a cat in the road. He stopped the bus to save the cat. Tom picked up the cat and said, "You are safe now, little cat." The cat meowed and Tom put the cat on the bus. The people on the bus were happy to see the cat. They all wanted to pet the cat. But then, something unexpected happened! The cat could talk! The cat said, "Thank you, Tom, for saving me. I am a magic cat!" Tom and the people were very surprised. The magic cat made everyone's day better with its magic. And they all had a fun ride on Tom's comfortable bus.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tiny. Tiny was very anxious. He did not like loud noises. One day, there was a big noise outside. Tiny was scared and wanted to escape. Tiny saw a big cushion on the floor. He thought, "I can hide behind the cushion!" So, Tiny ran to the cushion and hid behind it. He felt safe and cozy. Soon, the big noise stopped. Tiny peeked out from behind the cushion. He saw that everything was okay. Tiny was happy that he found a safe place to escape. He wagged his tail and played with the cushion. From that day on, Tiny always knew where to go when he felt anxious.

Tom is a bald cat. He likes to eat tuna. He does not like to move. He likes to sleep on the sofa all day. One day, his owner Sam comes home with a big box. He puts the box on the floor and opens it. Out comes a small dog. The dog is brown and fluffy. He has a lot of energy. He runs around the room and barks. "Tom, this is Rex. He is your new friend. He will live with us now. You can play with him and have fun," Sam says. Tom does not like Rex. He does not want a new friend. He does not want to play. He wants to eat tuna and sleep. He hisses at Rex and runs away. Rex follows Tom. He wants to play. He thinks Tom is a toy. He jumps on Tom and tries to bite his tail. Tom is angry. He scratches Rex and growls. "Stop it, Rex! Leave me alone! You are not my friend. You are a bad dog. Go away!" Tom says. "Sorry, Tom. I just want to play. You are so soft and funny. Don't you like me?" Rex says. "No, I don't like you. I like tuna. Go find your own tuna. And don't touch me!" Tom says. Tom runs to the kitchen and jumps on the counter. He sees a can of tuna. He tries to open it with his paws. He can't. He is sad. Rex follows Tom to the kitchen. He sees the can of tuna. He can open it with his teeth. He does. He is happy. "Look, Tom. I opened the tuna for you. You can have some. I will share with you. Maybe you will like me then," Rex says. Tom looks at Rex. He looks at the tuna. He is hungry. He thinks for a moment. He decides to try. "Okay, Rex. Thank you for the tuna. Maybe you are not so bad. Maybe we can be friends. But only if you don't bite my tail," Tom says. Rex wags his tail. He is happy. He licks Tom's face. Tom purrs. He is happy too. He eats some tuna and shares with Rex. They move to the sofa and cuddle. They fall asleep. Sam smiles. He is happy too. He takes a picture of them and puts it on the fridge. He loves his bald cat and his fluffy dog.
