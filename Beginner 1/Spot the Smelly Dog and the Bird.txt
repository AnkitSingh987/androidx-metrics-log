Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Smelly Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a smelly dog named Spot. Spot lived in a big yard with a fence. Spot liked to run and play all day. One day, a little bird came and sat on the fence. Spot saw the bird and wanted to speak to it. Spot said, "Hi bird, my name is Spot. Do you want to be my friend?" The little bird looked at Spot and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend. My name is Blue." Spot and Blue played and talked all day. They were very happy to be friends. At the end of the day, Spot and Blue said goodbye. Blue flew away, and Spot went to sleep. They both knew they would see each other again and play more. Spot and Blue were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a mighty girl named Mia. Mia loved to clap her hands and stomp her boots. One day, she met a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his boot. "Tim, why are you sad?" asked Mia. "I lost my boot," said Tim. Mia wanted to help Tim find his boot. They looked everywhere for the boot. They looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and even in the mud. Finally, they found the boot by the river. Tim was so happy that he started to clap his hands. Mia clapped her hands too. They put on their boots and danced together. From that day on, Mia and Tim became the best of friends. They always clapped and stomped their boots together, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship was on the sea. It had many yummy things to taste. There were fruits, ice cream, and cookies. All the people on the ship were very happy. One day, a big fish saw the ship. The fish was hungry. It wanted to taste the yummy things too. So, the fish jumped onto the ship. The people were scared. They did not know what to do. Then, a little girl saw the fish. She was not scared. She gave the fish a cookie to taste. The fish liked the cookie. It was very yummy. The fish was happy and jumped back into the sea. The people were not scared anymore. They all clapped for the little girl. The ship sailed on, and everyone was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside, but today it was very cold. The wind was bitter, making him shiver. Tim saw his mother in the kitchen. She was making something to eat. He heard a snap, and his mother smiled. She had made a yummy treat for him. His mother gave him a warm cookie. It was not bitter, but sweet. Tim was happy and warm now. He hugged his mother and said, "Thank you!" They sat and ate cookies together, feeling cozy and loved.

Lily and Tom are twins. They like to play with their toys in the bath. They have a blue boat, a yellow duck and a green frog. They make the boat go fast in the water. They make the duck quack and the frog jump. They have fun with the motion of the toys. "Look, Lily, I can make the boat splash!" Tom says. He pushes the boat hard and the water flies up. "Wow, Tom, that's cool!" Lily says. She tries to do the same with the duck. But the duck does not splash. It only floats. "Why does the duck not splash, Tom?" Lily asks. "Because the duck is not a boat, Lily. The duck is a duck. Ducks like water. They do not like to splash." Tom explains. "Oh, I see. Then I will make the frog splash. The frog is not a duck. The frog is a frog. Frogs like to jump." Lily says. She squeezes the frog and it makes a noise. She throws the frog in the air and it lands in the water with a splash. "Good job, Lily! You made the frog splash!" Tom says. He claps his hands and laughs. Lily and Tom are happy. They bathe and play with their toys until their mom comes to dry them. They love their blue boat, their yellow duck and their green frog. They love their bath time.
