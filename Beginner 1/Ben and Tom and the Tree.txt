Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Tom and the Tree

Ben and Tom were brothers who liked to play in the woods. They had a great game where they would count the trees and pretend to chop them with an axe. They made loud noises and laughed a lot. One day, they found a real axe in a shed. It was big and shiny and had a sharp edge. Ben wanted to try it and said to Tom, "Look, a real axe! Let's use it to chop the trees. It will be more fun than our pretend axe." Tom was scared and said, "No, Ben, that's not a good idea. The axe is not for us. It is for grown-ups. It can hurt us or the trees. Let's leave it alone and play with our pretend axe." But Ben did not listen. He grabbed the axe and ran to the nearest tree. He swung the axe hard and hit the tree. He felt a jolt in his arms and heard a loud crack. He smiled and said, "Wow, this is great! I can chop the trees with one hit. Come on, Tom, join me. Let's count how many trees we can chop." Tom shook his head and said, "No, Ben, stop it. You are being bad. You are hurting the trees and making a mess. Put the axe down and come back to me." But Ben did not stop. He ran to another tree and swung the axe again. He did not see the branch that was above his head. The branch fell on him and hit his head. He fell to the ground and did not move. He did not hear Tom scream or run to him. He did not feel Tom hug him or cry for help. He did not know that he was hurt very bad. He did not know that he would never play again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play outside. One day, she saw a small orange on the ground. The orange was alone and needed help. Mia wanted to save the orange. Mia picked up the orange and went to her friend, Tom. She said, "Tom, I found this orange. Can you help me save it?" Tom said, "Yes, Mia. Let's give it some water and sunshine." They put the orange in a mild, sunny spot and gave it water. Soon, the orange grew bigger and happier. Mia and Tom were very proud that they saved the orange. They shared the orange and it was the best orange they ever had.

One day, a small cat named Fluffy was playing near a creek. The creek was a comfortable place for Fluffy to play. While Fluffy was playing, she saw a big dog named Max. Max was not happy. "Stay away from my creek!" Max said to Fluffy. Fluffy was scared but did not want to suffer. She had an idea. "Let's play a game," said Fluffy to Max. "If I win, I can play at the creek too." Max agreed to play the game. The game was to see who could jump the highest. Fluffy and Max started jumping. Fluffy jumped very high, but Max jumped even higher. Fluffy was sad because she thought she would lose. But then, something unexpected happened. Max jumped so high that he got stuck in a tree! Fluffy saw Max in the tree and felt bad. So, she helped Max get down from the tree. Max was thankful to Fluffy and said, "You are a good friend. We can both play at the creek together!"

Once upon a time, in a little hole in the ground, there lived a mole. This mole was not just any mole, he was famous. All the other animals liked him a lot. One day, the mole met a big bear. The bear was sad. The mole urged the bear, "Let's go play!" The bear smiled and said, "Yes, let's go!" So, they went to play together. They played all day long. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. The mole and the bear became very good friends. And that is how the famous mole made the big bear happy.
