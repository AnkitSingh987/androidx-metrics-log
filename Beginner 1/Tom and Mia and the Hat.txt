Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Hat

Tom and Mia were twins who liked to play pretend. One day, they found a big bookcase in the attic. It had many books with pictures of ships and islands and animals. "Let's sail to a new land!" Tom said. He put a sheet over the bookcase and made a mast. He gave Mia a hat and a map. "You are the captain, Mia. I am the sailor." "OK, Tom. Let's go!" Mia said. She looked at the map and pointed to a spot. "There is our island. It has tasty fruits and friendly monkeys. We have to watch out for the sharks and the pirates, though." Tom and Mia climbed on the bookcase and pretended to sail. They made sounds of waves and wind and birds. They saw the island in their minds. They were happy and excited. Suddenly, they heard a voice from downstairs. It was their mom. "Tom, Mia, lunch is ready. Come down, please." Tom and Mia looked at each other. They did not want to stop playing. But they were also hungry. And they knew their mom made tasty sandwiches and juice. "Can we come back later, mom?" Tom asked. "Sure, honey. But don't make a mess in the attic, OK?" Mom said. "OK, mom. Thank you." Mia said. Tom and Mia got off the bookcase and folded the sheet. They put the hat and the map back on the shelf. They kissed the bookcase and said goodbye to their island. "See you soon, island. We will sail again." Tom said. "Yes, we will. And we will have more adventures." Mia said. They ran downstairs to have lunch with their mom. They were still smiling and dreaming.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a distant tree and wanted to play near it. His mom said, "Okay, but don't go too far." Tim was very happy and ran to the tree. While playing, Tim found a glove on the ground. He picked it up and thought, "Maybe someone lost it." He showed the glove to his mom. She said, "We should try to find the person who lost it." They walked around the park and saw a girl crying. Tim realized the girl might be the one who lost the glove. He asked her, "Is this your glove?" The girl looked at the glove and smiled. "Yes, it is! Thank you!" she said. Tim felt good for helping her. The girl and Tim became friends and played together at the park. Tim's mom was proud of him for being kind and helpful.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found an interesting note on the ground. The note had a picture of a big tree and a red ball. Tim wanted to find the big tree and the red ball. He showed the note to his friend, Sally. "Look at this note, Sally!" said Tim. "Let's find the big tree and the red ball!" They walked and walked until they found the big tree. But they could not see the red ball. Tim and Sally felt sad. Then, they saw a long string tied to a branch of the tree. They pulled the string and the red ball fell down! Tim and Sally were very happy. They learned that sometimes, you need to look closer and try new things to find what you are looking for. And they played with the red ball all day long.

One day, a small dog saw a big, bright building. The dog wanted to see what was inside. So, he walked up to the door and pushed it open. Inside the building, the dog met a cat. The cat said, "You don't belong here. This is a place for cats only." The dog didn't listen and walked deeper into the building. He saw many cats playing and having fun. The dog wanted to play too, but the cats didn't want to play with him. They were mean to the dog and pushed him away. The dog was sad and left the building. The bright building was not a happy place for the small dog after all.

Sara and Tom were playing with their toys in the living room. Sara had a fancy doll with a shiny dress and a hat. Tom had a copper car that made noises when he pushed it. Sara liked Tom's car, but Tom did not want to share it. "Can I play with your car, Tom?" Sara asked. "No, it's mine!" Tom said. "You have your doll, go play with her." "But your car is so cool, it makes sounds and it's copper!" Sara said. "Please, Tom, just for a little bit." "No, no, no!" Tom said. He pushed Sara away and ran to the other side of the room. Sara felt sad and angry. She wanted to play with the car, too. She looked at her doll and had an idea. She took off the doll's hat and dress and threw them on the floor. Then she took a tissue and wiped the doll's face and hair. She made the doll look dirty and messy. "Look, Tom, look at my doll!" Sara said. "She's not fancy anymore, she's like your car!" Tom looked at Sara's doll and gasped. He saw that she had ruined her doll's clothes and face. He felt sorry for the doll and for Sara. He realized that he had been mean to Sara and that she only wanted to have fun with him. "Wow, Sara, what did you do to your doll?" Tom said. "I made her like your car, Tom," Sara said. "Do you like her now?" Tom felt a pang in his heart. He knew that Sara had done that to make him happy, but he also knew that she had given up something that she liked. He felt ashamed of himself and wanted to make up with Sara. "Sara, I'm sorry, I was wrong," Tom said. "You don't have to do that to your doll. She was pretty and fancy, and you liked her. Here, you can play with my car, if you want. I'll help you fix your doll, too." Sara smiled and hugged Tom. She was happy that Tom had changed his mind and that he wanted to share his car with her. She also felt relieved that Tom would help her fix her doll. She thanked Tom and took his car. They played together with the car and the doll, and they had a lot of fun. They learned to be nice to each other and to respect their toys.
