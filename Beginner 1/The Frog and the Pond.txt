Category: Beginner
Title: The Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. The frog lived in a pond with clear water. He had many friends who liked to play with him. They would jump, swim, and splash in the clear water all day long. One day, the frog saw a girl sitting by the pond. She had a big book and a pen. The girl was trying to write something, but she was sad. The frog wanted to help her. He hopped over to the girl and asked, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I want to write a story, but I don't know what to write about." The frog thought for a moment and then said, "Why don't you write a story about me and my friends in the pond?" The girl liked the idea. She started to write a story about the frog and his friends playing in the clear water. The girl was happy, and the frog was happy too. They became good friends, and the girl would come to the pond every day to write stories about the frog and his friends.

Ben and Sam were playing with their toy soldiers in the backyard. They had a big box of missiles that they could shoot at each other's bases. Ben liked to make loud noises and pretend that his missiles were real. Sam liked to be more careful and aim his missiles well. "Bang! Bang! Bang!" Ben shouted as he fired three missiles at Sam's base. "I got you, Sam! You lose!" "No, you didn't!" Sam said. "Your missiles missed. Look, they landed over there, by the fence." Ben looked and saw that Sam was right. He felt ashamed that he was not a good shooter. He wanted to win the game and make Sam lose. He had an idea. He grabbed the biggest missile from the box and ran towards the fence. "Ben, what are you doing?" Sam asked. "You can't go there. That's Mr. Jones' yard. He doesn't permit us to go there. He gets very angry if we touch his flowers." "I don't care!" Ben said. "I'm going to shoot this missile at your base and win the game. Watch me!" Ben climbed over the fence and aimed the missile at Sam's base. He pulled the trigger and waited for the explosion. But nothing happened. The missile did not fly. It was broken. Ben tried to fix it, but it was no use. He heard a loud voice behind him. "Hey, you! What are you doing in my yard? Get out of here, you little rascal! And leave that missile alone. That's mine. I made it for my science project. It's very special and expensive. How dare you touch it!" Ben turned around and saw Mr. Jones, the old man who lived next door. He looked very angry and scary. He had a big stick in his hand. He walked towards Ben, waving the stick. Ben was very scared. He dropped the missile and ran back to the fence. He tried to climb over it, but it was too high. He cried for help. "Sam, help me! Mr. Jones is coming! He's going to hit me with his stick!" Sam heard Ben's cry and felt sorry for him. He ran to the fence and helped Ben climb over it. They ran to their backyard and hid behind their bases. "Are you okay, Ben?" Sam asked. "Yes, I'm okay. Thank you, Sam. You're a good friend." Ben said. "I'm sorry, Mr. Jones. I didn't mean to touch your missile. I was just playing a game." Ben said. Mr. Jones heard Ben's apology and calmed down a bit. He picked up his missile and checked it. It was not damaged. He sighed and shook his head. "Kids these days. They don't know how to respect other people's things. They don't know how to play nice. They don't know how to be ashamed." He said. He went back to his house and closed the door. Ben and Sam looked at each other and felt ashamed. They realized that they had done something wrong. They decided to stop playing with their missiles and play something else. Something that would not make anyone angry or sad. Something that would make them happy and proud.
