Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Swings

Tom and Mia are playing in the garden. They see a big tree with many vines. They want to swing on the vines like monkeys. "Look, Mia, a vine!" Tom says. He grabs a vine and pulls it. It is long and strong. "Me too, me too!" Mia says. She grabs another vine and pulls it. It is wet and slippery. Tom swings on his vine. He feels happy and free. He goes up and down, up and down. Mia swings on her vine. She feels scared and cold. She goes too fast and too high. "Help, Tom, help!" Mia cries. She lets go of her vine. She falls to the ground. Tom hears Mia. He stops swinging. He lets go of his vine. He runs to Mia. "Mia, are you okay?" Tom asks. He sees Mia on the ground. She is wet and dirty. She has a bump on her head. "Ow, ow, ow!" Mia says. She rubs her head. She feels pain and sad. Tom hugs Mia. He says, "I'm sorry, Mia. I didn't mean to strike you with my vine. It was an accident." Mia hugs Tom. She says, "It's okay, Tom. I know you didn't mean it. You are my friend." Tom and Mia go inside. They wash their faces and hands. They put a band-aid on Mia's bump. They drink some juice and eat some cookies. They feel better and happy. They say, "We love vines, but we love each other more."

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to create something fun. She found brown paper and glue in her room. Sue thought she could make a nice picture with them. Sue started to glue the brown paper on a big white paper. But, the glue was very sticky. When she tried to put the brown paper down, it stuck to her hand! Sue tried to shake her hand, but the paper did not come off. It was a big surprise! Sue's mom came into the room and saw the sticky glue mess. She laughed and helped Sue wash her hands. The brown paper fell off, and they both laughed. In the end, Sue and her mom made a funny picture with the glue and the brown paper. It was a fun day, even if it was not what Sue planned.

One day, a little boy named Tim got a new toy. It was a big, red aeroplane. Tim was very happy. He played with the aeroplane all day long. He made it fly up and down, and all around the room. The next day, it was Tim's friend's birthday. Tim's mom said they needed to give his friend a gift. Tim thought his friend would like the red aeroplane. So, Tim and his mom wrapped the aeroplane in pretty paper with a big bow. On the day of the birthday party, the sun was out and it was mild. Tim and his mom went to the party. Tim gave his friend the wrapped gift with the red aeroplane inside. His friend was very happy when he opened it. They played with the aeroplane together and had lots of fun.

One day, a restless little dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot was black and loved to play. He saw a big door and wanted to see what was behind it. Spot barked, "I want to go in and play!" A kind girl named Sue heard Spot and came to help. Sue said, "Let's open the door and see what's inside!" They opened the door and saw a big, fun room with lots of toys. Spot and Sue played with the toys and had lots of fun. After playing for a long time, Spot and Sue got tired. Sue said, "It's time to go home now, Spot." They walked out of the room and Sue shut the door. Spot and Sue went home, happy and tired from their fun day.

One day, a bald man named Tom got into his big van. He loved to drive around the town and sing songs. His van was blue and very big. As he drove, he saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad. Tom stopped the van and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Tom wanted to help, so he said, "Yes, let's look for it together." They looked and looked, and then they found the toy under a tree. Lily was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome!" They got into the van and sang a happy song together as they drove away.
