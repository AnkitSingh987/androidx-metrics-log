Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Engine and the Bird

One day, a big engine named Tom was feeling troubled. He was lying on the train tracks, not able to move. Tom was sad because he could not go and play with his friends. A small bird saw Tom and asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I am troubled because I can't move. I am lying here and I can't go play with my friends." The bird wanted to help Tom, so it flew away to find Tom's friends. The bird found them and told them, "Tom is troubled and needs help. He is lying on the tracks and can't move." The friends came quickly to help Tom. Together, they pushed and pulled Tom until he was no longer lying on the tracks. Tom was very happy and thanked his friends. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a pale girl. She loved to watch the sun go up and down every day. Her mom told her that she would find something special if she kept watching. One day, the girl went to a big tree to watch the sun. She saw a small, shiny thing on the ground. The girl picked it up and put it in her pocket. She knew it was the special thing her mom talked about. The next day, the girl showed the shiny thing to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "I knew you would find it! This is a magic stone." The girl was very happy, and from that day on, she always watched the sun with her magic stone.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a friendly bear. The bear loved to play with all the children during the winter. The snow was white and soft, and they all had so much fun together. One day, a new boy came to the village. The bear wanted to play with him, too. The bear said, "Hi, I am a friendly bear. Do you want to play in the snow with me?" The boy was a little scared, but he said yes. They played and laughed all day long. The boy's mom saw them play and told the boy, "You must behave when you play with the friendly bear." The boy promised to behave and they all played happily in the winter snow. The village was full of joy and laughter as they all became friends.

Ben and Lily like to play with their toys. They have cars, dolls, blocks, and books. But their favorite toy is the jazz box. The jazz box is a big box with many buttons and sounds. When they press a button, the box plays jazz music. Jazz music is fun and loud and makes them want to dance. One day, Ben and Lily are playing with the jazz box in their room. They press all the buttons and hear different jazz sounds. They dance and jump and laugh. They are having a lot of fun. But then, their mom comes in. She looks at the jazz box and says, "Ben and Lily, it is time to remove the jazz box. It is too loud and it is nap time. You need to rest and be quiet." Ben and Lily do not want to remove the jazz box. They want to keep playing and listening to jazz. They say, "No, mom, please. We love the jazz box. It is fun and we are not sleepy." Mom shakes her head and says, "Sorry, Ben and Lily, but you have to remove the jazz box. It is not good for your ears and your brain to hear so much noise. You can play with it again later, but now you need to nap. Come on, help me remove the jazz box and put it away." Ben and Lily are sad and angry. They do not want to help mom remove the jazz box. They want to keep it in their room. They think mom is not fair and not fun. They cross their arms and pout. But mom is strong and smart. She knows how to make Ben and Lily happy. She says, "How about this? If you help me remove the jazz box and nap for a while, I will let you watch a jazz show on TV later. You can see real jazz musicians play their instruments and sing. Would you like that?" Ben and Lily think about it. They like to watch TV and they like to see jazz musicians. They think it might be fun and cool. They nod and say, "OK, mom, we will help you remove the jazz box and nap. But only if we can watch the jazz show later." Mom smiles and says, "Deal. Thank you, Ben and Lily, for being good and helpful. Now, let's remove the jazz box and go to bed. I love you both very much." Ben and Lily hug mom and say, "We love you too, mom. You are the best." They help mom remove the jazz box and put it in the closet. Then they go to bed and nap. They dream of jazz music and jazz musicians. They are happy and calm.
