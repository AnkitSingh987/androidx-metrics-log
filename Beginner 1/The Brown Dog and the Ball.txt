Category: Beginner
Title: The Brown Dog and the Ball

One day, a brown dog went to the subway. The dog wanted to show everyone his tricks. He could sit, jump, and even dance! The dog was very happy to display his tricks to all the people. On the subway, the dog met a little girl. The girl was sad because she lost her toy. The brown dog wanted to help the girl find her toy. They looked everywhere in the subway, but they could not find the toy. Then, something unexpected happened! The brown dog found out that he could talk! He asked the girl what her toy looked like. The girl said it was a small, blue ball. The dog looked around and saw the blue ball under a seat. The girl was very happy to get her toy back. In the end, the brown dog and the girl became best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play on the swings and slide. He also liked to run around and play with his friends. But sometimes, Tim was a bit careless. "Remember to be careful, Tim!" said his mom. "And don't forget to hold on tight when you're on the swings!" While playing, Tim saw a big loop on the ground. It was a jump rope that someone had left behind. Tim picked up the loop and started to jump. He was having so much fun! His friend, Sally, came over and asked, "Can I play too?" Tim said, "Yes, let's jump together!" They took turns jumping in the loop and had a great day at the park. And Tim remembered to be careful while playing.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys and draw pictures. One day, they go to a fair with their moms. The fair has many games and rides. Lily and Tom want to try them all. They see a game with many balls and a big basket. The man says, "If you throw a ball in the basket, you win a prize. You can choose a teddy bear, a doll, or a car." Lily and Tom want to play. They each get three balls. Lily throws her first ball. It misses the basket. She throws her second ball. It misses again. She throws her third ball. It goes in the basket. She is happy. She says, "I win! I win! I want the teddy bear, please." The man gives her the teddy bear. It is soft and brown. Lily hugs it. She says, "Look, Tom, I have a teddy bear. It is so cute. Do you like it?" Tom looks at the teddy bear. He feels jealous. He wants a teddy bear too. He says, "I don't like it. It is ugly. I want a car. I hope I win a car." He throws his first ball. It misses the basket. He throws his second ball. It misses too. He throws his third ball. It hits the basket, but it bounces out. He is sad. He says, "I lose. I lose. I have no prize." The man says, "I'm sorry, Tom. You can try again next time. Here, have a sticker. It has a car on it." Tom takes the sticker. He looks at it. He says, "Thank you, but it is not the same. I want a real car." Lily sees Tom is sad. She feels sorry for him. She says, "Tom, don't be sad. You can play with my teddy bear. We can share. We are friends." Tom looks at Lily. He sees she is nice. He says, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend. I'm sorry I was jealous. Can I hug your teddy bear?" Lily says, "Yes, you can. Here, you can hold it. It is soft and warm." Tom hugs the teddy bear. He smiles. He says, "It is nice. I like it. Maybe next time I will win a prize too." Lily says, "Maybe you will. But it doesn't matter. We have fun anyway. We have each other." They hug each other. They are happy. They go to see the other games and rides. They have a good day at the fair.
