Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a toy bear named Ben. Mia loved to hug Ben. She hugged him every day. Ben was her best friend. One day, Mia and Ben went for a walk in the woods. They saw a big tree. Mia was restless. She wanted to climb the tree. So, Mia and Ben climbed the tree together. When they got to the top, they saw a pretty bird. The bird said "hello" to Mia and Ben. Mia and Ben said "hello" back. Then, they all played together. They laughed and had fun. After playing, Mia, Ben, and the bird went back down the tree. Mia hugged Ben and the bird. They were all happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, tough rope in his yard. He wanted to play with it, but it was too long. So, Tim asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you cut this rope for me?" he said. His mom smiled and said, "Sure, Tim. Let's cut it into two pieces." Tim and his mom cut the rope and started to play. They played a game called "tug of war." Tim pulled one side of the rope, and his mom pulled the other side. They both pulled very hard, but no one could win. Then, suddenly, the rope broke into two pieces again! Now, there were zero ropes to play with. Tim felt sad and said, "Mom, I'm sorry I broke the rope." His mom hugged him and said, "That's okay, Tim. We can still have fun with these smaller ropes." They tied the ropes together and made a swing. Tim and his mom had a great time swinging and laughing. The moral of the story is: even when things don't go as planned, you can still find a way to have fun and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a goal. He wanted to find a big, red ball. Max was very serious about his goal. He would request help from his friends to look for the big, red ball. One day, Max asked his friend, the cat, to help him. The cat was not nice, but Max did not know that. The cat said, "Yes, I will help you, Max." Max was very happy. They looked for the ball together. The cat saw the big, red ball near a big road. The cat told Max, "The ball is over there, Max." Max ran to get the ball, but the cat did not tell Max about the big road. Max did not see the big road, and there was a loud sound. Max did not find the ball, and he did not get to go home. The cat was not a good friend to Max.

One day, a big rock sat in the woods. A fine bird came and sat on the rock. The bird saw a little bug on the rock. The bird said, "Hi, bug. I like your home on this rock." The bug said, "Thank you, bird. I like your home in the woods too. But, I want a new home. Can you help me find one?" The bird said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's look for a new home together." So, they looked for a new home for the bug in the woods. They found a nice pile of wood. The bug said, "This wood looks good for my new home." The bird said, "I think so too." The bug moved into the wood pile. The bird flew away to find food. But, the wood pile was not a good home. It was wet and cold. The bug did not like it. The bird came back and saw the bug was not happy. The bird felt sad. They did not find a good home for the bug after all.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful forest, there lived a big bear and a little rabbit. They were good friends and liked to share. They spent their life playing and having fun together. One day, the big bear and the little rabbit found a big, red apple. They both wanted to eat it, but they knew they had to share. So, the big bear broke the apple in half and gave one piece to the little rabbit. They both ate their pieces and smiled. After they ate the apple, they played in the beautiful forest all day long. They were very happy to share and be friends. The big bear and the little rabbit knew that sharing made their life in the beautiful forest even better.
