Category: Beginner
Title: The Green Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The frog was very foolish. He lived in a big pond with many friends. They loved to play and jump all day long. One day, the green frog met a wise old turtle. The turtle said, "You should be careful when you play near the pond. You might fall in and get wet." The green frog laughed and said, "I am a frog! I love to be wet!" The next day, the foolish green frog played near the pond. He jumped too far and fell into the water. His friends saw him and laughed. The green frog felt silly. He remembered what the wise old turtle said. From that day on, the green frog was more careful when he played near the pond. He still loved to jump and be wet, but he was not foolish anymore. The green frog and his friends played happily ever after.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books. But their favorite game is to jump on the bed. One day, they are jumping on the bed. They are laughing and having fun. They are wearing their socks. Their socks are soft and warm. "Look, Lily, I can jump very high!" Ben says. He jumps up and down. He tries to touch the ceiling. "Wow, Ben, you are so good!" Lily says. She jumps too. She tries to catch Ben's feet. But then, something bad happens. Ben jumps too hard. He slips on the bed. He falls off the bed. He hits his head on the floor. He cries out loud. "Ow, ow, ow! That hurts!" Ben says. He holds his head. He feels a bump. It is hard and sore. Lily stops jumping. She is scared. She runs to Ben. She hugs him. She says sorry. "I'm sorry, Ben, are you okay?" Lily says. She looks at his head. She sees the bump. Ben is sad. He is in pain. He wants his mom. He says no. "No, Lily, I'm not okay. I want mom. Mom, mom, mom!" Ben says. He cries louder. Lily hears Ben. She feels bad. She wants to help him. She thinks of an idea. She runs to the closet. She finds a sock. It is blue and big. She takes it. She runs back to Ben. She puts the sock on his head. She says it is a bandage. "Here, Ben, this is for you. It is a sock. It is a bandage. It will make you feel better." Lily says. She smiles. She hopes Ben will stop crying. Ben looks at Lily. He sees the sock. He feels the sock. It is soft and warm. He feels a little better. He stops crying. He says thank you. "Thank you, Lily. You are a good sister. The sock is nice. It is a good bandage." Ben says. He smiles too. He hugs Lily. Lily and Ben are happy. They are still on the floor. They are wearing their socks. One sock is on Ben's head. They laugh and talk. They wait for mom. They love each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a very filthy boy. He loved to play in the mud and get dirty. One day in March, he saw a big tree with many fruits. Tom wanted to get the fruits, but he was too small to reach them. Tom asked his friend, Sam, "Can you help me get the fruits from the tree?" Sam was taller than Tom. Sam said, "Yes, I will help you, but you must promise to share the fruits with me." Tom agreed, and they made a plan. Tom and Sam worked together to shake the tree. The tree began to yield its fruits, and they fell to the ground. Tom and Sam picked up the fruits and shared them with each other. They were very happy and learned that sharing is a good thing. From that day on, Tom and Sam always shared their things with each other and became the best of friends.

One day, a cat went for a quiet jog. The cat liked to run in the park. The sun was up, and the sky was blue. The cat felt very happy. Then, the cat saw a dog. The dog was big, and it liked to call out loud. The cat was scared. It thought the dog would chase it. But something unexpected happened. The dog did not chase the cat. Instead, the dog wanted to jog too! So, the cat and the dog jogged together. They had a lot of fun. Now, they are friends who jog and play in the park.
