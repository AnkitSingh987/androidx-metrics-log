Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bookcase in his room. He loved to read books from the bookcase. One day, he saw a rough, old book on the top shelf. Tim wanted to read the rough book, but he was too small to reach it. Tim had a plan. He would use a chair to stand on and get the book. He pushed the chair to the bookcase and climbed on it. He was so close to the rough book now. But when he tried to get it, the chair shook and Tim was scared. Tim did not give up. He tried again to get the rough book. But this time, the chair fell and Tim fell too. He got hurt and cried. The rough book stayed on the top shelf, and Tim learned not to climb on chairs.

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Tom. Tom loved to ride his bike with a pedal. Every day, he would go outside and ride his bike up and down the street. One sunny day, Tom met a new friend, a dog named Max. Max wanted to learn how to ride a bike too. Tom said, "I can teach you! First, put your foot on the pedal. Then, push down and go!" Max tried to follow Tom's words, but he fell off the bike. They both laughed. Tom and Max tried a number of times. After a while, Max learned how to ride the bike. They had so much fun riding their bikes together. From that day on, Tom and Max became best friends and had many more funny adventures together.

One day, a tall tree and a small bush lived in a park. They were very good friends. Every day, people came to the park for a jog. The tall tree and the small bush liked to watch them run. One day, a little boy came to the park. He wanted to play with the tall tree and the small bush. "Can I climb you?" he asked the tall tree. The tall tree did not want to yield and said, "No, I am too tall for you. You can play with the small bush." The little boy played with the small bush. They had a lot of fun. The tall tree saw them and felt sad. The next day, the little boy came again. This time, the tall tree said, "You can climb me. I will yield and be careful." The little boy climbed the tall tree. They all had fun together. The moral of the story is that it is good to yield and help others, even if you are tall or small.

Once upon a time, there was a big forest with many animals. In the forest, there was a thick tree. A little bird lived in the tree. The bird loved to sing and record her songs. One day, a strong lion came to the tree. He had a long spear. The lion wanted to hurt the little bird with the spear. The bird was scared and didn't know what to do. The bird thought of a plan. She sang her most beautiful song. The lion listened and felt happy. The lion put down the spear and did not hurt the bird. They became friends. The bird learned that being kind can stop bad things.

Once upon a time, there was a huge cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball. One day, Tom saw a line of ants. The ants were carrying food. "Hi, ants!" Tom said. "What are you doing?" The ants said, "We are trying to get food for our family." Tom wanted to help. So, Tom got a big leaf and put the food on it. The ants were very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the leaf up in the air! The ants were sad. But Tom had an idea. He jumped high and caught the leaf. The ants cheered, "Yay, Tom!" And they all became good friends.

One day, a gentle cat went on a holiday. It was a sunny day, and the cat was very happy. The cat played with a ball, ate yummy food, and made new friends. The gentle cat loved the holiday a lot. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat saw a big tree with a ball stuck high up. The gentle cat wanted to get the ball down. The cat had an idea! The cat found a long stick to strike the ball in the tree. The cat struck the tree with the stick. Suddenly, the tree started to shake, and many more balls fell down! The gentle cat was so surprised and happy. Now, the cat and all its new friends had many balls to play with. The holiday became even more fun, and the gentle cat had the best time ever.
