Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Dog

One day, a girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a big house with a deep hole in the yard. Mia was very curious. She walked closer to the hole and looked down. She could not see the bottom. "Wow, this is very deep," she said. A little boy named Tom came out of the house. "Hi, my name is Tom," he said. "Do you want to play with me?" Mia said yes, and they started to play. They played with a ball, and they played with a toy car. They had lots of fun. As they played, the ball rolled close to the deep hole. Tom tried to reach the ball, but it fell in. "Oh no," said Tom. "My ball is gone." But then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog jumped out of the hole with the ball in its mouth. "Wow," said Mia. "I did not know a dog lived in the deep hole!" Tom and Mia played with the dog and had even more fun. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in his yard with his dog, Spot. They would run and jump, and have lots of fun. One day, Tim felt anxious because Spot was not in the yard. He looked around and called, "Spot, where are you?" Tim's mom heard him and came outside. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. Spot is just at the vet. He will be back soon." Tim felt better, but he still missed his friend. He decided to play with his ball until Spot came back. As Tim played, he heard a noise at the gate. He looked and saw Spot running into the yard. Tim was so happy! He hugged Spot and said, "I missed you, buddy!" Spot barked and wagged his tail. Tim threw the ball and Spot ran to catch it. They played their favorite game, repeat fetch, over and over again. Tim and Spot were happy to be together in the yard once more.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a big pile of dust near his house. He thought it would be fun to play with the dust. So, Tim started to play with the dust and make shapes with it. A girl named Sue saw Tim playing with the dust. She did not like the dust because it was dirty. Sue wanted to play with Tim, but she did not want to play with the dust. Sue was sad because she could not play with Tim. Tim saw Sue was sad and wanted to help. He thought of a way they could both play and be proud. Tim found a ball and asked Sue if she wanted to play with the ball instead of the dust. Sue was happy and they played together. In the end, Tim and Sue were both proud and had a fun day playing together.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the gloomy woods. The sky was gray, and the trees were big and tall. Mia was not scared, because she knew her dog, Buddy, was with her. She could trust Buddy to keep her safe. As they walked, Mia found a big box. It was a mystery what was inside. Mia and Buddy opened the box, and they found a pretty, shiny ball. The ball made them feel happy, even on the gloomy day. Mia and Buddy took the ball home and showed it to their friends. They all played with the ball and had lots of fun. The mystery ball made the gloomy day brighter, and Mia knew she could always trust Buddy to be with her on their adventures.

Mama and Rick were walking down the street. Mama looked at Rick and said, "I hate it when we are so slow." Rick puffed out his chest and said, "No, Mama! I'm not slow. I'm fast like the wind!" Mama laughed and said, "That's right! You're fast like the wind." Just then, they reached a red light. Rick stopped and started counting. "One, two, three." He counted to zero and the light changed to green. "Go!" cheered Rick. Mama and Rick started walking, but Rick stopped. He pointed to the park up ahead and said, "I hate that park." "Why do you hate it?" asked Mama. Rick scrunched up his nose and said, "It's too slow. Too slow for me!" Mama laughed and said, "That's ok. We won't go in there. Let's keep walking." Mama and Rick kept walking, but this time, it was a lot faster. They laughed as the wind blew their hair around their faces. Soon, they had forgotten all about that slow park.
