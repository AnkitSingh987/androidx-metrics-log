Category: Beginner
Title: The Fly and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a small fly. The fly was very old and elderly. The fly had a friend, a kind bird. They liked to talk and play together. One day, the bird said to the fly, "I recommend you try to fly up to the big tree." The fly was scared, but the bird said, "Don't worry, I will help you." So, the elderly fly tried to fly up to the tree. The bird helped the fly by holding it gently. The fly was happy to reach the tree, and they both played together all day.

Sam and Mia liked to play with their big white ball. They would bounce it to each other and catch it with their hands. Sometimes they would bounce it on the ground and try to make it go high in the air. One day, they decided to play with their ball in the park. They found a nice spot with green grass and some trees. They started to bounce their ball and have fun. "Look, Mia, I can bounce it really high!" Sam said, and threw the ball with all his strength. "Wow, Sam, that's amazing!" Mia said, and watched the ball fly up and up. But then, something bad happened. The ball hit a branch of a big tree and got stuck. "Oh no, our ball!" Sam and Mia cried. They ran to the tree and tried to get their ball back. But the tree was too tall and the branch was too far. They could not reach their ball. They felt very sad and looked at their ball. It was still white, but now it was not bouncing. It was just sitting on the branch, like a bird. "Maybe we can find someone to help us," Mia said, and looked around. But there was no one in the park. Everyone had left. They felt even sadder and hugged each other. They did not know what to do. But then, they heard a loud bark. They turned and saw a big dog running towards them. It was brown and furry and had a friendly face. It wagged its tail and licked their faces. "Hi, doggy!" Sam and Mia said, and petted the dog. The dog seemed to like them and barked again. Then, the dog looked at the tree and saw their ball. It barked louder and jumped up and down. It wanted to play with their ball. "Maybe the dog can get our ball," Sam said, and pointed at the tree. "Good idea, Sam," Mia said, and clapped her hands. "Come on, doggy, get our ball!" The dog barked and ran to the tree. It jumped as high as it could and caught the ball with its mouth. It pulled the ball down and ran back to Sam and Mia. It dropped the ball at their feet and wagged its tail. "Yay, doggy, you did it!" Sam and Mia cheered, and hugged the dog. They picked up their ball and thanked the dog. The dog barked and licked their faces again. They were very happy and decided to play with their ball and the dog. They bounced their ball and threw it for the dog to catch. The dog was very good at catching and brought the ball back every time. They had a lot of fun and made a new friend. They forgot about their sadness and smiled. They never wanted to leave.

One day, a boy named Tim and his cat, Mimi, went for a walk. They found a big, round rock. Tim said, "Mimi, let's break this rock!" Mimi meowed and nodded her head. Tim and Mimi pushed and pushed the rock until they heard a crack. They stopped and looked at the rock. Inside the crack, they saw something shiny. Tim said, "Wow, what's that?" They pushed the rock more and the crack got bigger. Suddenly, a small, comfortable chair popped out! Tim and Mimi were very surprised. They never thought a chair could be inside a rock. Tim sat on the chair and said, "This is the best chair ever!" Mimi jumped on Tim's lap and they both laughed. From that day on, Tim and Mimi had a special chair to sit on, and they always remembered their fun adventure.
