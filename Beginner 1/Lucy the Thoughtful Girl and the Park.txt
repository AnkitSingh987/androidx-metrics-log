Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Thoughtful Girl and the Park

One day, a thoughtful little girl named Lucy wanted to go on a trip. She asked her mom, "Mom, can we pack our bags and go on a trip?" Her mom said, "Yes, Lucy. Let's pack our bags and go to the park." So, Lucy and her mom packed their bags with toys and food. They put their bags in the car and drove to the park. At the park, they saw lots of kids playing. Lucy and her mom had a fun day at the park. They played with their toys and ate the food they packed. When it was time to go home, Lucy said, "Mom, I had a great trip to the park. Can we do it again soon?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Lucy. We can go on another trip soon."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy was very popular in her town. She had many friends who liked to play with her. They played with toys, ran in the park, and laughed a lot. One day, Daisy wanted to learn how to ride a bike. She tried and tried, but she could not do it. She felt sad. Her friends told her, "Don't give up, Daisy! You can do it!" They helped her and cheered for her. Daisy tried again and again. She wanted to succeed. And one day, she did it! Daisy was so happy that she could ride her bike. Her friends clapped and cheered for her. Now, Daisy could ride her bike with her friends in the park.

---

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He liked to play with his red ball. He threw the ball high and far. Tim was very happy. Then, Tim saw a big dog. The dog had a dull, brown coat. The dog wanted to play too. So, Tim threw the ball and the dog ran to get it. They played together for a while. Tim and the dog were very happy. Tim wanted to take a break, so he sat on the grass. He looked at his knee and saw a small bug. The bug moved and Tim paused to watch it. Suddenly, the bug grew wings and flew away! Tim was surprised. He had never seen a bug like that before. Tim and the dog went home with a fun story to tell.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with cars. Anna has a red car and Ben has a blue car. They drive their cars on the floor and make noises. Vroom, vroom! One day, they see a big map on the wall. The map has many places on it. Anna points to a place with a star. She says, "I want to go there. It looks fun." Ben nods and says, "Me too. Let's drive there." They take their cars and pretend to drive on the map. They go over hills and bridges and rivers. They see animals and trees and houses. They have a lot of fun. But then, they come to a place with a circle. The circle is very big and chubby. It is the sun. The sun is hot and bright. Anna and Ben stop their cars. They look at the sun and say, "Uh-oh. We can't drive there. It is too hot and bright. We will get burned." They turn their cars around and drive back to where they started. They are happy and safe. They hug their cars and each other. They say, "We had a good adventure. But we like our home best."

One day, a fast rabbit and a slow turtle wanted to have a race. They both loved nature and liked to play in the big, ancient tree. The rabbit said, "Let's race to the big, ancient tree and see who gets there first!" The race started and the rabbit ran fast, but the turtle walked slow. The rabbit got tired and decided to rest under a small tree. He thought, "The turtle is so slow, I can take a nap and still win the race." While the rabbit was sleeping, the turtle kept walking. He did not stop, even though he was slow. Finally, the turtle reached the big, ancient tree and won the race. The rabbit woke up and saw the turtle at the finish line. He said, "Wow, you won! Even though I am fast, I learned that I should not be too sure of myself." The rabbit and the turtle became good friends. They played in nature and had many more races, but the rabbit never took a nap during a race again.
