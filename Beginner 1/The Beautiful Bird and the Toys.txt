Category: Beginner
Title: The Beautiful Bird and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bird. This bird had a flute. The bird loved to play the flute. The bird had many friends. The friends liked to listen to the bird play the flute. One day, the bird and the friends wanted to sort their toys. They had many toys. They had big toys and small toys. They had red toys and blue toys. They all worked together to sort the toys. They put the big toys in one place and the small toys in another place. They put the red toys in one place and the blue toys in another place. When they finished sorting the toys, the beautiful bird played the flute. The friends danced and clapped. They were happy. They learned that working together made the job easier and more fun. The bird and the friends were very happy. They learned that helping each other is a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park, there were many kids playing. They played with balls, swings, and slides. They laughed and had fun. One day, a new game came to the park. The game was called "win". All the kids wanted to play the game. They took turns and tried their best. Some kids felt uncomfortable because they didn't know how to play "win" very well. But they kept trying. Soon, the kids learned how to play "win". They felt happy and not uncomfortable anymore. They played "win" in the park every day. They made new friends and had more fun together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They liked to swing, slide and run. One day, they saw a big duck pond in the park. They wanted to feed the ducks. Lily had some bread in her bag. She broke it into small pieces and threw them to the ducks. The ducks came closer and quacked. Lily was happy. She gave some bread to Ben and said, "Here, you feed them too." Ben took the bread and threw it to the ducks. But he threw it too hard. One piece hit a duck on the head. The duck was angry. It flapped its wings and chased Ben. Ben was scared. He ran away from the duck. He dropped the bread and bumped into Lily. Lily fell down and hurt her knee. She cried. Ben felt bad. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to hurt you or the duck. Are you okay?" Lily looked at her knee. It was red and sore. She looked at Ben. He looked sad and sorry. She looked at the duck. It was graceful and calm. It had stopped chasing Ben and was eating the bread. Lily thought for a moment. She said, "It's okay, Ben. I forgive you. You didn't do it on purpose. And the duck is fine too. See, it's happy now." Ben smiled. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You're a good friend. Can I help you up?" Lily nodded. She said, "Yes, please. And can we still play in the park?" Ben said, "Of course. Let's go to the swings. They're fun and safe." Lily and Ben held hands and walked to the swings. They forgot about the duck and the bread. They laughed and played. They were best friends again.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, while playing, he saw a big boy named Sam. Sam could kick the ball very high. Tim wanted to kick like Sam, but he couldn't. He felt jealous. Tim said to Sam, "Can you teach me how to kick the ball high?" Sam smiled and said, "Sure, I will help you." Sam showed Tim how to kick the ball. Tim tried and tried, but he still could not kick the ball high. After a year, Tim and Sam became good friends. They played together every day. Tim practiced a lot and learned how to kick the ball high. He was no longer jealous of Sam. They were both happy and had lots of fun playing together.
