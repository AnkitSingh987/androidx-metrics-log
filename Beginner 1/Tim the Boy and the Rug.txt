Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rug

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sue. Sue had a new rug in her room. The rug was big, pretty, and a little rough. Tim and Sue liked to play on the rug with their toys. They had lots of fun together. "Let's play with the cars," said Tim. Sue agreed, and they raced their toy cars on the rug. They laughed and cheered as the cars went fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The rug started to move! The rug lifted off the ground and began to fly! Tim and Sue held on tight as the rug took them up in the sky. They were scared at first, but then they saw how beautiful the world looked from up high. They flew over their houses and saw their friends playing outside. "Wow, this is so cool!" said Sue. "I never knew my rug could fly!" Tim agreed, and they continued to fly on the magic rug. After a while, they landed back in Sue's room, happy and excited about their adventure. They couldn't wait to tell their friends about the magic flying rug.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tim. Tim loved to dance. One day, he found a big board in his yard. He had an idea. He thought, "I can dance on this board!" Tim put the board on the ground. He started to dance on the board. He danced and danced. His mom saw him and said, "Wow, Tim! You are so good at dancing on that board!" Just then, something unexpected happened. The board started to move! It was a magic board! The board took Tim up into the sky. Tim was scared at first, but then he remembered how much he loved to dance. He danced on the magic board in the sky, and everyone on the ground clapped and cheered.

John was a three year old boy who loved to play with his friends. One day, he was playing in his backyard and he saw something shiny. He ran to it and grabbed it. It was a broken toy car! John was so excited he said, "This looks like so much fun!" He wanted to make it work again. John's dad saw him with the broken toy car and said, "John, you can't just grab things you don't know. You should ask your mom or dad first." John said, "Okay, I'm sorry Dad." John's dad said, "Let's go find some tools and see if we can fix it." John was so excited. They went to the garage and saw a workbench. Dad showed John how to use the tools. They worked together to fix the toy car. When they were finished, John was so excited he said, "We did it! Now it is ready for some fun!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to paint. She had a big box of paints in many colors. Her favorite color was purple. One day, Sue went for a walk in the park. She found a long stick on the ground. Sue had an idea. She would use the stick to paint! Sue went home and got her purple paint. She dipped the stick in the paint. Then, she made big purple lines on a big piece of paper. Sue was very happy with her purple stick painting. She showed it to her mom and dad, and they loved it too.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day. He hopped high and low, fast and slow. One day, Bob hopped to the woods and saw his friend, Tim the turtle. "Hi, Tim!" said Bob. "Do you want to hop with me?" Tim laughed. "I can't hop, Bob. I am too slow. But I have a surprise for you." Bob was excited. He loved surprises. Tim gave Bob a big, red ball. Bob was very happy. He hopped with the ball all day long. At the end of the day, Bob thanked Tim for the surprise. They played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Bob and Tim became the best of friends.
