Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Apple

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big tree with red apples. They want to pick some apples and eat them. "Look, Mia, apples!" Tom says. He runs to the tree and climbs up. He reaches for an apple. "Be careful, Tom!" Mia says. She stays on the ground and watches him. Tom grabs an apple and pulls hard. But the apple is not ripe. It is green and ugly. It has a worm inside. Tom does not see the worm. He bites the apple. "Yuck!" Tom says. He spits out the apple. He sees the worm and makes a face. "This apple is bad. It has a bug." Mia sees the apple and the worm. She feels sorry for Tom. She says, "Poor Tom. You picked a bad apple. Do you want another one?" Tom shakes his head. He says, "No, thank you. I do not want any more apples. They are all ugly and bad." Mia thinks for a moment. She has an idea. She says, "I know how to fix it. Let's go to the store and buy some good apples. They are red and sweet. They do not have bugs." Tom likes Mia's idea. He says, "Yes, let's do that. I like good apples. They are yummy." Tom and Mia climb down from the tree. They hold hands and walk to the store. They buy some good apples and eat them. They are happy. They say, "Good apples are the best!"

One day, a boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He was very nervous because he wanted to win the race. He had a big score to beat. His toy car was fast, but he needed to make it even faster. Tim's dad saw him and said, "Why don't we repair your car to make it faster?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, please!" So, they worked together to fix the car. They put on new wheels and made it shiny and clean. The next day, Tim raced his car against his friends. He was still nervous, but his car was very fast. He won the race and beat the big score! Tim was so happy and thanked his dad for helping him repair the car.

One day, a nosy cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big tree with a lemon on it. The cat wanted to eat the lemon, so it climbed the tree. When the cat got to the top, it saw a big bird. The bird was holding the lemon in its beak. The cat tried to take the lemon from the bird, but the bird did not want to share. They started to fight over the lemon. The bird was very strong, and it pushed the cat out of the tree. The cat fell to the ground and the lemon flew out of the bird's beak. The lemon hit the ground and broke into many pieces. The pieces scattered all around the cat and the tree. The cat was sad because it could not eat the lemon now. But then, the bird came down from the tree and started to eat the lemon pieces with the cat. They became friends and shared the lemon. The cat learned that it is better to share and make friends than to fight.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very fearful of bugs. One day, she saw a big, green squash in her garden. She wanted to cut it and take it to her mom. Mia went to the garden with a small knife to cut the squash. As she tried to cut it, she saw a tiny bug on the squash. Mia was scared, but she wanted to be brave. Mia took a deep breath and cut the squash. The bug flew away, and Mia was happy. She took the squash to her mom, and they made a yummy dinner. Mia learned that being brave can help her do things, even when she is fearful.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived on a big farm called a ranch. Tim loved to play outside with his dog, Spot. One day, Tim and Spot went on an adventure to find something new. They walked and walked until they found a big tree with red round things on it. Tim picked one and tasted it. It was delicious! Tim and Spot took some of the red round things back to the ranch. They shared them with their family. Everyone loved the delicious red round things. They were so happy that Tim and Spot found them.
