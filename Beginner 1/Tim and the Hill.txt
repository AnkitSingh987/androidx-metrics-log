Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, he saw a big hill and wanted to make his toy car go down it. But the hill was far away and his mom told him not to go too far. Tim was not sure if he should trust his mom. Tim decided to go to the hill anyway. He ran to the hill with his toy car, but he tripped and fell. He hurt his knee and it started to bleed. Tim was scared and started to cry. He wished he had trusted his mom and stayed close to home. His mom heard him crying and came running to him. She saw his hurt knee and put a bandage on it. She hugged him and told him that it would be okay. Tim learned that he should trust his mom and not go to distant places without her. From that day on, Tim always stayed close to his mom and they played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a big house with many stairs. He had a toy that he loved very much. One day, his toy fell down the distant stair. Tim was sad and needed his toy back. Tim tried to go down the stairs, but he was scared. He took a big breath and started to go down. As he went down the stairs, he saw a big shadow. Tim was scared, but he needed his toy. The shadow came closer and closer. Tim closed his eyes, but then he heard a soft meow. It was his cat, Fluffy! Fluffy had found Tim's toy and brought it back to him. Tim was so happy and gave Fluffy a big hug. They went back up the stairs together and played all day.

One day, a girl named Sue found a thick towel. She wanted to show her friend, Tom. Sue went to Tom's house and said, "Look at my thick towel!" Tom wanted the thick towel too. He said, "Give me the thick towel, Sue!" But Sue said "No, it's mine!" They were both very upset. Sue and Tom decided to ask their dog, Max, to lead them to a fair person who could help. Max led them to their teacher, Miss Lee. Miss Lee said, "You must share the thick towel." But Sue and Tom did not want to share. They both pulled on the thick towel very hard. The thick towel ripped in half. Now, neither Sue nor Tom had a nice, thick towel.

One sunny day, Tom the tall boy went to play in the park. He loved to look at the big trees and the pretty flowers. He saw a small cat sitting near a tree. The cat looked sad and scared. "Hi, cat! Why are you sad?" Tom asked. The cat looked at Tom and said, "I lost my toy. It is my favorite toy, and I can't find it." Tom wanted to help the cat. He looked around the park for the toy. He looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and by the flowers. Then, he saw something near his foot. It was the cat's toy! "Look, cat! I found your toy!" Tom said with a big smile. The cat jumped up and ran to Tom. "Thank you, Tom! You are a good friend," the cat said. They played together in the park all day, and the cat was happy again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a nice girl named Lucy. Lucy was very honest. She always told the truth. One day, Lucy went to the park to play. At the park, Lucy saw a seat. It was a big, red seat. Lucy asked her mommy, "Can I sit on the seat?" Her mommy said, "Yes, I permit you to sit on the seat." Lucy was very happy. She jumped on the seat and played. After a while, a boy came to the park. He wanted to sit on the seat too. Lucy was honest and said, "You can sit with me." The boy sat on the seat and they played together. They became good friends and had lots of fun.
