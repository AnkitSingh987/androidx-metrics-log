Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Ball

Tom and Mia like to play hockey with their friends. They have sticks and a ball and they run on the grass. They try to hit the ball into the net. Sometimes they score and sometimes they miss. One day, they play hockey near a pond. The pond has water and fish and ducks. It is deep and cold. Tom and Mia are careful not to go too close to the pond. They do not want to fall in. But then, Tom sees a big duck on the pond. The duck has a ball in its mouth. It is their ball! The duck took their ball and swam away. Tom is angry. He wants his ball back. He runs to the pond and shouts at the duck. He waves his stick and tries to scare the duck. But the duck does not listen. It swims faster and farther. Tom follows the duck. He forgets to be careful. Mia sees Tom go too close to the pond. She is scared. She calls Tom to stop. She tells him to come back. But Tom does not hear her. He only sees the duck and the ball. He reaches the edge of the pond and leans over. He tries to grab the ball from the duck. But the duck is clever. It releases the ball and bites Tom's hand. Tom screams and drops his stick. He loses his balance and falls into the pond. Mia runs to help Tom. She sees him splash and cry in the water. She grabs his stick and pulls him out. She hugs him and tells him to calm down. She says he is lucky. The pond is deep and he could have drowned. Tom is wet and cold and sore. He is sorry for being silly. He says thank you to Mia for saving him. He says sorry to the duck for being mean. He does not care about the ball anymore. He just wants to go home and get warm and dry. Mia and Tom walk back to their friends. They tell them what happened. Their friends are glad they are okay. They say they can share their ball and play hockey together. They say they will not go near the pond again. They say they will be careful and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max saw a thick stick on the ground. He picked it up and started to play with it. Max saw a basket near a tree. He thought it would be fun to support the basket with the thick stick. So, he put the stick under the basket and started to push. The basket was very heavy, but Max was strong. Max pushed the basket too hard. The basket fell and broke. Max was sad because the basket was not his. He knew he did something bad. The end.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there was a big hole that led to the underground. Many people were scared of the hole, but a brave boy named Tom was not. One day, Tom saw a little bird near the hole. The bird looked sad and said, "My friend is stuck in the underground. Can you help me?" Tom nodded and said, "I will help you, little bird." Tom and the bird went into the underground. It was dark, but they found the bird's friend. They helped the friend out of the underground and went back to the town. The two birds were very happy and thanked Tom for his help. "You are a good friend, Tom," they said. The people in the town saw what Tom did and were no longer scared of the hole. They learned that it was good to help others, even if it seemed scary at first. And from that day on, everyone in the town became kinder and more helpful. In the end, the ordinary town became a very special place, thanks to Tom and his brave heart. The moral of the story is: don't be scared to help others, because kindness makes the world a better place.
