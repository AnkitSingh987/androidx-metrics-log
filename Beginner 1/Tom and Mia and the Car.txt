Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Car

Tom and Mia are playing with their toys in the living room. Tom has a big case with many cars and trucks. Mia has a small case with many dolls and animals. They like to share their toys and make stories with them. One day, they find a new toy in Tom's case. It is a shiny orange car that looks very fast and cool. Tom and Mia want to play with it, but they don't know who it belongs to. "Wow, look at this car!" Tom says. "It is so orange and shiny. Can I play with it?" "I don't know, Tom. It is not your car. Maybe it is a gift from someone." Mia says. "Maybe it is from Mom or Dad. They always give us nice things. Let's ask them." Tom says. They run to the kitchen, where Mom and Dad are making lunch. They show them the orange car and ask them if it is theirs. "No, sweeties, that is not our car. We don't know where it came from. Maybe it was in the case by mistake." Mom says. "Be careful with it, though. It looks like it is made of something that can melt. Don't put it near the stove or the heater." Dad says. Tom and Mia nod and go back to the living room. They decide to play with the orange car together, taking turns to drive it around the floor. They have a lot of fun with it, until they hear a strange sound. "Uh-oh, what is that?" Mia says. They look at the orange car and see that it is changing shape. It is getting smaller and softer. It is melting! "Oh no, the car is melting!" Tom says. They pick up the car and try to save it, but it is too late. It is a sticky orange mess in their hands. They start to cry and run to Mom and Dad. They show them the melted car and tell them what happened. "I'm sorry, sweeties, that was not a real car. It was a candy car. It was supposed to be a treat for you, but we forgot to tell you. We didn't know it was in the case." Mom says. "It's okay, sweeties, don't cry. We have more candy for you. You can have some after lunch. And you still have your other toys to play with." Dad says. They hug Tom and Mia and wipe their tears. They give them some water and a napkin to clean their hands. They tell them they love them and they are proud of them for sharing and being kind. Tom and Mia feel better and smile. They say thank you and they love them too. They go back to the living room and play with their other toys. They forget about the melted car and have a happy day.

Tim and Jen are brother and sister. They like to play outside in summer. They have a big yard with a slide, a swing and a sandbox. They also have a small store. They make the store with a table, a box and some toys. They pretend to sell things to each other and to their friends. One day, they have a new customer. It is a big dog. The dog comes from the street and runs into their yard. He sees the store and thinks it is fun. He jumps on the table and knocks down the box. He grabs some toys with his mouth and runs away. Tim and Jen are scared and angry. "Hey, stop! That is our store!" Tim shouts. "Give us back our toys!" Jen cries. But the dog does not listen. He is crazy. He thinks it is a game. He runs around the yard with the toys. He chews them and throws them. He makes a big mess. Tim and Jen do not know what to do. They run after the dog and try to get their toys back. But the dog is faster and stronger. He barks and growls at them. He does not want to share. Then, they hear a whistle. It is the dog's owner. He is looking for his dog. He sees the dog in their yard. He sees the store and the toys. He sees Tim and Jen. He is sorry and embarrassed. "Rex, come here! Bad dog! I am so sorry, kids. He escaped from his leash. He is not mean, just crazy. He likes to play, but he does not know the rules. I will pay for the toys he ruined. Are you OK?" Tim and Jen are not hurt, just upset. They nod and say yes. They are glad the dog is gone. They look at their store. It is broken and dirty. They are sad. They worked hard to make it. But then, they have an idea. They can make a new store. They can use different things. They can make it better and bigger. They can have more fun. They smile and hug each other. They are not mad anymore. They are happy. They love summer. They love their store. They do not love crazy dogs.
