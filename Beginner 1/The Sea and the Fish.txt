Category: Beginner
Title: The Sea and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big sea. In the sea, there was a fish. The fish was original because it could spin. It was a very special fish. One day, the fish met a little boat. The boat said, "Hello, fish! Can you spin for me?" The fish said, "Yes, I can spin!" The fish spun around and around. The boat laughed and clapped. The boat and the fish became good friends. Every day, the boat and the fish played in the sea. They had so much fun together. The fish would spin and the boat would laugh. They were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a pilot named Tim went to the beach. Tim saw a big blue sea. He wanted to swim in the sea. So, he put on his swim clothes and jumped in the water. Tim was very happy. In the sea, Tim met a fish named Bob. Bob was a selfish fish. He did not like to share his sea toys with others. Tim wanted to play with Bob, but Bob said "no." Tim felt sad. Then he had an idea. He showed Bob how to be kind and share. They played together and had fun. Now, Bob was not a selfish fish anymore. They became good friends and swam in the sea every day.

One day, a big truck arrived in a small town. It had many toys in it. One of the toys was a ball. It was a perfect ball. It was round and it was red. All the kids in the town wanted to play with the ball. Two friends, Tom and Sam, saw the ball. They both wanted the ball for themselves. They both said, "This ball is mine!" They started to fight over the ball. They were not happy. They were sad. Their mom saw them fighting. She said, "You should share the ball. When you share, you will both be happy." Tom and Sam listened to their mom. They played with the ball together. They shared and had fun. The moral of the story is to share and be kind to your friends.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big truck. The truck has ice in it. The ice is for a famous show. The show has ice dancers and ice animals. "Wow, look at the ice!" Sara says. "It is so shiny and cold. Can we touch it?" "No, we can't," Tom says. "The ice is not for us. It is for the show. If we touch it, it will rot. Then the show will be bad." Sara and Tom watch the truck. They see the ice dancers and ice animals get out. They are very pretty and funny. They wear costumes and make-up. They smile and wave at Sara and Tom. "Hello, kids!" one of the ice dancers says. "Do you want to see our show? We have tickets for you. Come with us!" Sara and Tom are very happy. They thank the ice dancer. They follow him to the show. They sit in the front row. They see the ice dancers and ice animals dance and sing on the ice. They clap and cheer. They have a lot of fun. The ice does not rot. It stays shiny and cold. The show is very good. Sara and Tom are very lucky. They see a famous show. They make new friends. They have a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a team of friends who loved to play. They liked to run, jump, and reach for the sky. One day, they found a big box in the park. They were very excited to see what was inside. The team of friends tried to open the box, but it was too high for them to reach. They thought of a plan. One friend stood on another friend's back so they could reach the box. When they opened it, they found a big cake inside! The friends were so happy and couldn't wait to eat the cake. But when they took a bite, they found out it was a disgusting cake! They didn't like it at all. Then, they saw a note inside the box. The note said, "This cake is not for eating, it's for playing!" The friends laughed and started to play with the disgusting cake. They had so much fun, and it was a day they would never forget.
