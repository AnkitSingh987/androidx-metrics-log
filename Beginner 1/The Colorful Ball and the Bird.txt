Category: Beginner
Title: The Colorful Ball and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a colorful ball. It was red, blue, green, and yellow. The ball liked to roll and bounce all day long. One day, the ball rolled into a big hole. The ball was sad because it could not get out. A nice bird saw the ball in the hole. The bird wanted to help the ball. The bird tried to push the ball out, but the ball was too big. The bird flew away to find help. The bird came back with a big, soft pillow. The bird put the pillow in the hole and the ball bounced up and out! The ball was so happy! But then, the ball looked at the pillow and saw it was the same colors as the ball. The ball and the pillow were friends all along! They laughed and played together, and they never felt alone again.

Once upon a time, there was a long line of ants. They were on a march to find food. They walked and walked until they found a big, red apple. One ant said, "Look! A big apple! Let's take it home!" The other ants were happy and started to carry the apple. They walked and walked, but they were very tired. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew the ants and the apple far away. The ants were scared and didn't know where they were. They decided to sleep for a while. When they woke up, they saw their home was right next to them! They were so happy and carried the apple inside.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to go out and play near the wheat field. One day, he saw a big, ignorant bird sitting on the wheat. The bird was eating the wheat and making a mess. Tim wanted to help the wheat grow big and strong. Tim walked up to the bird and said, "Hey, bird! You should not eat the wheat like that. It is not good for the wheat." The bird looked at Tim and said, "I did not know that. Thank you for telling me!" The bird and Tim became friends. They would go to the wheat field every day and help the wheat grow. The bird stopped eating the wheat and started to eat bugs instead. The wheat grew tall and strong, and Tim and the bird were very happy. They learned that by working together, they could do great things.

Lily and Max liked to play with their kayak in the lake. They had a big yellow kayak that could fit both of them and their toys. They liked to paddle and splash and pretend they were pirates or explorers. One day, they found a hole in their kayak. Water was coming in and making it sink. They were sad and scared. They paddled back to the shore and ran to their dad. "Dad, dad, our kayak is broken!" they cried. "Can you fix it?" Dad looked at the hole and nodded. "I can fix it, but I need some glue and tape. You two can help me. Go to the shed and bring me the glue and tape, please." Lily and Max ran to the shed and looked for the glue and tape. They saw a big jar of glue and a roll of tape on a shelf. They tried to reach them, but they were too high. They saw a stool nearby and had an idea. They dragged the stool to the shelf and climbed on it. They grabbed the glue and tape and smiled. They were happy they could help dad. They jumped off the stool and ran back to dad. But they did not see the sleepy cat that was sleeping on the stool. The cat woke up and saw them running away. It was angry and confused. It hissed and scratched the stool. The stool fell over and knocked down the shelf. The shelf hit the wall and made a loud noise. The noise scared the cat and it ran away. Lily and Max heard the noise and turned around. They saw the mess in the shed and gasped. They dropped the glue and tape and ran to the shed. They hoped they did not break anything else. Dad heard the noise and came to the shed. He saw the mess and sighed. He saw Lily and Max and smiled. He hugged them and said, "Don't worry, kids. Accidents happen. We can fix the shed later. Let's fix the kayak first." They took the glue and tape and went to the kayak. They put glue on the hole and taped it. They waited for it to dry and then tested it. The kayak was fixed and did not leak. They cheered and thanked dad. They put the kayak back in the lake and paddled away. They had fun and forgot about the shed. They did not see the cat that was watching them from a tree. The cat was still angry and wanted revenge. It waited for them to come back. It had a plan.
