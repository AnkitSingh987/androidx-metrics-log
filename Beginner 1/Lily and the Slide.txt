Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Slide

Lily was a brave girl who liked to help her mom with the infant. The infant was her baby brother, Tom. He was very small and cute, but he cried a lot. Lily tried to make him happy by singing to him and giving him toys. One day, Lily and her mom went to the park with Tom. They saw many other kids playing and having fun. Lily wanted to play too, but she did not want to leave Tom alone. She asked her mom if she could take Tom with her to the slide. Her mom said yes, but told her to be careful and watch him. Lily took Tom to the slide and put him on a blanket. She smiled at him and said, "I will be right back, Tom. You stay here and enjoy the sun." She ran to the slide and climbed up. She was about to slide down when she saw a big boy coming towards Tom. The big boy had a mean face and a big bag. He looked at Tom and said, "What a nice baby. I think I will take him and put him in my bag. He will be my new toy." Lily was scared, but she was also brave. She did not want the big boy to steal her brother. She slid down as fast as she could and ran to Tom. She shouted, "No, no, no! He is not your toy. He is my brother. You cannot take him. Go away!" The big boy was surprised and angry. He tried to grab Tom, but Lily held him tight. She kicked the big boy and bit his hand. The big boy cried out and dropped his bag. He ran away, leaving his bag behind. Lily hugged Tom and kissed him. She said, "It's okay, Tom. I am here. I love you. You are safe." Tom stopped crying and smiled at Lily. He reached out and touched her hair. Lily's mom heard the noise and came running. She saw Lily and Tom on the blanket and the big boy's bag on the ground. She was shocked and worried. She asked Lily what happened. Lily told her everything. She said, "Mom, I was brave. I saved Tom from the big boy. He wanted to steal him, but I stopped him." Lily's mom was proud and relieved. She said, "Lily, you are very brave and smart. You did a great job. I am so glad you and Tom are okay. You are the best sister ever." She hugged Lily and Tom and thanked them for being so good. She picked up the big boy's bag and looked inside. She saw many toys that he had stolen from other kids. She said, "We should return these toys to their owners. Maybe they are looking for them. And we should tell the police about the big boy. He should not steal from anyone." Lily agreed. She said, "Yes, mom. Let's do that. But first, can we play on the slide again? Tom likes it too." Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily. Let's play on the slide. You deserve it. You are a brave girl."

Once upon a time, there was a great big tree. It was very tall and strong. The tree had many leaves that were green and pretty. Under the tree, there was ground. The ground was brown and hard. One day, a small boy named Tim came to play near the tree. Tim saw a big fruit on the tree. He wanted to eat the fruit. Tim said, "Tree, can I have your fruit, please?" The tree said, "Yes, Tim, you can have my fruit." Tim took the fruit and ate it. It was very good. But then, Tim saw that the fruit had a bad spot. The bad spot was rot. Tim did not like the rot. He said, "Tree, your fruit has rot. I don't like rot." The tree said, "I'm sorry, Tim. Next time, I will give you a better fruit." Tim said, "Thank you, tree." He played on the ground near the tree for the rest of the day. The tree was happy to have a friend like Tim. And Tim was happy to have a great big tree to play with.
