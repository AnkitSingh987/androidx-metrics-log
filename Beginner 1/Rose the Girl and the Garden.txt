Category: Beginner
Title: Rose the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Rose. Rose was three and loved playing with her toys in the garden. One day Rose was playing in the garden when she noticed that her favourite toy, a fluffy bunny, was missing! Rose began to yell. She yelled so loud that her mom heard her from the kitchen. "What's wrong, Rose?" Mom asked, coming out of the kitchen. Rose told Mom that she could not find her fluffy bunny. Mom smiled and said to Rose she might find it in the garden if she looked carefully. Rose started looking for the bunny in the garden. After a few minutes, she saw it lying behind a bush! She was so happy she yelled with joy. Mom heard Rose and came out with a special treat for her. In her hands was a cup of pink yogurt. Rose liked yogurt and began to eat it. She noticed it was clean and tasted yummy. Rose happily ate the yogurt while playing with her fluffy bunny. She was very happy.

Ben and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and balls. One day, they find a big stick in the park. They both want the stick. They pull and tug and shout. "Give me the stick!" Ben says. "No, it is mine!" Sam says. They are not happy. They are angry. Then, Sam has an idea. He sees a bald man sitting on a bench. The bald man has a cast on his leg. He looks sad. "Hey, Ben, look!" Sam says. "Let's give the stick to the bald man. Maybe he can use it as a cane. Maybe he will smile." Ben thinks for a moment. He likes to make people smile. He nods. "OK, Sam, let's do that. But you have to promise me something." "What?" Sam asks. "You have to promise me that we can share the stick later. We can take turns playing with it. OK?" "OK, Ben, I promise. We can share the stick later." They run to the bald man. They give him the stick. They say hello. The bald man is surprised. He takes the stick. He smiles. "Thank you, boys. You are very kind. This stick will help me walk. Where did you find it?" "We found it in the park. We were playing with it. But we decided to give it to you. We hope you like it." Ben says. "I do like it. It is a nice stick. But you can have it back if you want. I don't want to take your toy." The bald man says. "No, no, you can keep it. We have other toys. We have cars and balls. And we can share the stick later. We promised." Sam says. The bald man is touched. He thanks them again. He stands up. He uses the stick as a cane. He walks away. He waves. Ben and Sam wave back. They are happy. They are proud. They are friends. They keep their promise.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play with his friend, Sam. They wanted to prepare for a big party. Tim put on his red shirt. Sam put on his blue shirt. They were very excited. "Let's make a cake for the party," said Tim. Sam agreed. They went to the kitchen. They tried very hard to make a cake. But it was not easy. The cake was not good. It was hard and not tasty. At the party, everyone tried the cake. They did not like it. The kids said, "This cake is too hard!" Tim and Sam were sad. They learned that making a cake is not easy. They decided to ask for help next time. The party was not fun because of the hard cake.
