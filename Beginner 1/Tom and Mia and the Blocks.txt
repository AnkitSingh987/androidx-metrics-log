Title: Tom and Mia and the Blocks

Tom and Mia are playing with blocks. They like to make different shapes and colors with the blocks. Tom has a lot of black blocks. He likes to make a pattern with them. He puts one black block, then two black blocks, then three black blocks, and so on. Mia sees Tom's pattern. She thinks it is pretty. She wants to make the same pattern. She takes some of Tom's black blocks. She puts them next to her blocks. Tom sees Mia taking his blocks. He is angry. He says, "Mia, stop! Those are my blocks! You can't take them!" Mia says, "But I like your pattern. I want to make it too. Please, can I have some of your blocks?" Tom says, "No, you can't. You have your own blocks. You can make your own pattern. Give me back my blocks!" Mia feels sad. She does not want to make Tom angry. She gives him back his blocks. She says, "I'm sorry, Tom. I didn't mean to make you mad. I just liked your pattern." Tom feels sorry too. He sees that Mia is sad. He says, "It's okay, Mia. I'm sorry I yelled at you. You can have some of my blocks. We can make the pattern together." Mia smiles. She says, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend. I like your pattern. And I like you." Tom smiles too. He says, "You're welcome, Mia. You are a good friend too. I like your blocks. And I like you." They hug. They share the blocks. They make the pattern together. They are happy.

One day, a big, impressive dog named Max went to the park. Max liked to help and serve his friends. Max saw his friend, a small cat named Lily. "Hi Lily," Max said. "Do you need help today?" Lily said, "Yes, Max. I need to give this pill to my friend, Birdy. Can you help me?" Max and Lily went to find Birdy. They looked up, down, and all around. Then, they saw Birdy in a tree. Max had an idea. He put the pill in his mouth and jumped high in the air. Max gave the pill to Birdy. "Thank you, Max," Birdy said. But then, something unexpected happened. Birdy changed into a big, impressive bird. Max, Lily, and the big bird all played together in the park. They had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat meat. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, let's have a picnic today!" Tim was so happy and he helped his mom get everything ready. They were very organized and put all the food in a big basket. At the park, Tim and his mom found a nice spot to sit. They put a blanket on the ground and started to eat. Tim said, "Mom, where is the meat?" His mom looked in the basket and said, "Oh no! We must have lost the meat at home." Tim was sad, but his mom had an idea. She said, "Let's play a game, Tim. We can pretend this cheese is meat!" Tim liked the idea and they both laughed. They had a great time at the picnic, even without the meat. From that day on, Tim and his mom always double-checked their basket to make sure they didn't lose anything again.

Once upon a time, there was a big lime tree. The tree had many green limes. The limes were tight on the tree. A little boy named Tim liked to play near the tree. One day, Tim saw a small bug. The bug had a zip on its back. The bug wanted to eat a lime. Tim wanted to help the bug. He picked a lime from the tree. Tim gave the lime to the bug. The bug was happy. The bug used its zip to open the lime. The bug ate the lime. Tim and the bug became friends. They played together near the lime tree every day.

Once there was a little bear who had a big fridge. He liked to keep it very clean and he was very proud of being a neat bear. One day, a black bird flew by and perched on the fridge. The bird saw the bear admiring his clean fridge and said "What a unique fridge you have! I have never seen one like it before!" The bear grinned and said "Yes, I love to keep it clean. I make sure to clean it every day. Cleaning takes time but it's important." The bird nodded and said "That's a very wise thing to do. Cleaning is important." The little bear smiled and thanked the bird. From then on, he kept the fridge clean every day. He was not only proud of being a neat bear but he was also proud of the wise lessons he had learnt. The moral of the story is that if you keep your things clean, it will give you a feeling of pride. Cleaning things may take time, but it is always worth it.
