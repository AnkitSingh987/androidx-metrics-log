Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Mia and the Dog

Sam and Mia are playing in the park. They see a big cable on the ground. It is long and thick and black. "Let's roll the cable!" Sam says. He likes to roll things. "OK!" Mia says. She likes to play with Sam. They grab the cable and try to roll it. But it is too heavy. They pull and push, but it does not move. "Uh-oh," Sam says. "The cable is too strong. We are too weak." Mia thinks for a moment. She has an idea. "Let's ask for help!" she says. She sees a man walking his dog nearby. "Excuse me, sir. Can you help us roll the cable?" The man smiles. He sees the children and the cable. He is curious. "Sure, I can help you," he says. "But why do you want to roll the cable?" Sam and Mia look at each other. They do not have a reason. They just want to roll it. "Because it is fun!" they say together. The man laughs. He likes children. He is strong. "OK, then. Let's roll the cable!" he says. He lifts one end of the cable and puts it on his shoulder. He tells Sam and Mia to hold the other end. He walks and they follow. The cable rolls behind them. "Yay!" Sam and Mia say. They are happy. They roll the cable with the man and his dog. They have fun.

One day, a dog named Chewy heard some news. The news was about a big, yummy bone. Chewy wanted that bone very much. But, another dog named Fluffy wanted the bone too. They did not know who should get the bone. Chewy said, "I will get the bone!" Fluffy said, "No, I want the bone!" They did not know what to do. They were both ignorant about how to share. They began to fight over the bone. In the end, the bone broke into pieces. Chewy and Fluffy looked at the broken bone. They were both sad. They could not chew the bone now. The news of the big, yummy bone was not good news anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with a nice porch. One day, Sue wanted to play outside. But first, she had to dress. She put on her favorite red shirt and blue pants. Sue went outside and saw her friend Tom. Tom was playing in the dirt. "Hi Tom," said Sue. "Do you want to play on the porch?" Tom looked at his dirty hands and said, "Yes, but I need to wash my hands first." So, Tom washed his hands and they both played on the porch. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go inside, they were both very tired. Sue's mom said, "You two had a fun day. Now it's time to wash up and get ready for bed." Sue and Tom smiled and said, "We love playing on the porch!"

One day, a big ant named Tim was walking in the garden. He saw a cobweb and wanted to play with it. But a small spider named Sally said, "No, this is my cobweb. You can't play with it!" Tim was sad and went away. He thought and thought, and then he had an idea. He decided to bury the cobweb so Sally could not find it. He dug a hole and put the cobweb in it. Then, he covered it with dirt. Sally saw what Tim did and was very sad. She asked Tim, "Why did you bury my cobweb?" Tim said, "I wanted to play with it, but you didn't let me." Sally thought for a moment and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. Let's dig it up and play together!" So they dug up the cobweb and played together. Tim and Sally became good friends and shared the cobweb from that day on. And they both were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very proud of his new suit. The suit was red and blue with a big bow. Tom liked to wear his suit every day. One day, Tom went for a walk in the park. He wanted to show off his new suit to everyone. While walking, he saw a small dog named Max. Max was playing with a ball. Tom wanted to meet Max and play with him. Tom walked up to Max and said, "Hi, I am Tom. I like your ball." Max looked at Tom and said, "Hi, I am Max. I like your suit." They played with the ball and had a lot of fun. They became good friends and were very happy to meet each other.
