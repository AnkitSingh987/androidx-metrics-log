Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Fish and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Bob. Bob was a dependable fish. He lived in a reef with many other fish. Bob loved to drink water and swim with his friends. One day, Bob met a new friend named Tim. Tim asked Bob, "Do you want to play with me?" Bob said, "Yes, let's play!" They played and swam together all day. But Tim was not a good fish. He had a plan to trick Bob. Tim told Bob, "I know a place where there is more water to drink. Follow me." Bob followed Tim, but it was a trap. Tim led Bob away from the reef and into a net. Bob was stuck and could not swim back home. Tim laughed and swam away, leaving Bob all alone.

---

One day, a little cat found a rod. The cat was very happy. She wanted to play with the rod. She picked it up and ran to her friend, the dog. The dog was grateful and wanted to play too. As they played, the rod started to move. The cat and the dog were surprised. They did not know that the rod was magic. The magic rod made a big cake appear. The cat and the dog were very happy. They wanted to eat the cake. But then, the cake started to lie down. The cat and the dog were confused. The cake was not a real cake. It was a pillow! The magic rod had tricked them. The cat and the dog laughed. They were still grateful for the fun day with the magic rod.

---

One day, a pretty girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a pretty insect on a leaf. Lily said, "Hi, pretty insect! I want to pick you up and take you home." The insect said, "Please don't pick me! I have a family here. If you pick me, I will miss them a lot." Lily thought about it and said, "Okay, I will not pick you. I want you to be happy with your family." Then, Lily saw her friend Tom. Tom wanted to pick the pretty insect too. But Lily told him what the insect said. Tom was sad, but he knew it was right to let the insect stay with its family. They both learned that it is important to think about how others feel. And they played in the park, leaving the pretty insect with its family.

---

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big tent. It is green and brown. It has holes and dirt on it. Lily and Ben think it is ugly. They want to look inside the tent. They are curious. They walk to the tent. They hear a noise. It sounds like a growl. They are scared. They notice a big dog. It is black and white. It has sharp teeth and angry eyes. It is guarding the tent. It does not like Lily and Ben. It barks at them. It wants them to go away. Lily and Ben run away from the tent. They are crying. They find their mom. She hugs them. She tells them to stay away from the tent. She says it is not safe. She says it is not a good place to play. Lily and Ben nod. They are sorry. They do not want to see the tent or the dog again. They play with their ball and their dolls. They have fun. They forget about the ugly tent.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing with his toy gun. He liked to run fast and race with his friends. Tim's friend, Sam, came over and said, "Let's race to the big tree!" "OK!" said Tim. They both got ready to race. Tim held his toy gun tight. "One, two, three, go!" They started to run as fast as they could. As they were racing, they saw a big, fast dog coming their way. The dog wanted to play too! The dog raced with Tim and Sam. The dog was very fast and won the race to the big tree. "Wow, that was fun!" said Sam. "The dog is very fast." Tim and Sam laughed and played with the dog and the toy gun. They had a great day racing with their new friend.
