Category: Beginner
Title: The Famous Store and the Magic

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous store. This store had a magic tap. The tap could give out any drink you wanted. People came from far away to see the famous tap. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. Lucy was very excited to see the tap. When they got to the store, Lucy's mom asked her, "What drink do you want from the magic tap?" Lucy thought for a moment and said, "I want apple juice!" Lucy's mom turned the tap, and out came the apple juice. Lucy was very happy. She took a big sip of the juice and smiled. The magic tap had made her wish come true. From that day on, Lucy and her mom would visit the famous store and the magic tap whenever they wanted a special drink.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a lot of dolls and cars and blocks. But she did not like to share them with anyone. When her brother Ben came to her room and asked if he could play with her, she said no. She said they were all hers and he had to go away. Ben felt sad and angry. He wanted to play with Lily and have fun. He did not think it was fair that she kept all the toys for herself. He tried to grab a car from her hand, but she pulled it back. They started to fight and yell. Their mom heard them and came to see what was wrong. She saw Lily and Ben tugging and pushing and shouting. She was not happy. She told them to stop and calm down. She said they had to share their toys and be nice to each other. She said that was the right thing to do. She said if they did not, they would have to go to time out. Lily did not want to go to time out. She liked her room and her toys. But she did not want to share them either. She tried to resist her mom and keep the car. She said it was hers and she did not care. She wrapped her arms around her neck and held on tight. But her mom was stronger and gentler. She pried Lily's arms off her neck and hugged her. She said she loved her and she understood that she liked her toys. But she said she had to learn to share and be kind. She said that was how to make friends and be happy. She said she could play with Ben and have more fun. She asked her to try. Lily looked at her mom and then at Ben. She saw that he was still sad and angry. She felt sorry for him. She thought maybe her mom was right. Maybe sharing was not so bad. Maybe playing with Ben was not so bad. She decided to give it a try. She let go of the car and gave it to Ben. She said he could play with it. She said she was sorry for being mean. Ben smiled and thanked her. He said he was sorry for grabbing the car. He said he wanted to play with her. He asked her if she wanted to play with his blocks. She said yes. They went to his room and played with his blocks. They built a tower and a bridge and a house. They had fun. They were happy. Their mom watched them and smiled. She was proud of them. She said they did a good job. She said they learned to share and be nice. She said that was the best thing to do. She said she loved them both. She gave them a kiss and a hug. They said they loved her too. They were a happy family.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. The sun was light and warm. Tom liked to go outside and play. He saw a big, wet puddle and wanted to jump in it. Tom ran fast and jumped into the wet puddle. But, the puddle was not just water. It was a magic puddle! When Tom jumped in, he became very light and started to float up in the sky. Tom was scared at first, but then he saw other animals floating too. They were all having fun in the sky. Tom made new friends and they all played together. After a while, Tom and his new friends floated back down to the ground. They were all happy and could not wait to play in the magic wet puddle again.
