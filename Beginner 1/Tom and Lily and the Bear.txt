Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Bear

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. But they also liked to poke things with sticks. They poked leaves, rocks and bugs. They had fun. One day, they saw a big pile of mud. It was wet and brown and sticky. They wanted to poke it. They ran to the mud and picked up sticks. They poked the mud hard and fast. They made holes and splashes. They laughed. But then, they heard a loud roar. They looked up and saw a big, ugly bear. It was angry and hungry. It had mud on its fur and face. It did not like to be poked. It growled and showed its teeth. It chased Tom and Lily. Tom and Lily were scared. They dropped their sticks and ran away. They ran to their mom and dad. They hugged them and cried. They told them about the bear. Mom and dad were surprised and worried. They looked at the mud and saw the bear. It was still there, but it was calm. It licked the mud off its fur and face. It looked sad and lonely. It wanted to play. Mom and dad said to Tom and Lily, "That bear is not ugly. It is just different. It lives in the woods and eats honey and fish. It does not know how to play with sticks and mud. Maybe it was curious and wanted to join you. But you poked it and hurt it. That was not nice. You should say sorry to the bear." Tom and Lily felt sorry. They nodded and said, "Okay." They walked to the mud and saw the bear. It looked at them and sniffed. It did not roar or growl. It waited. Tom and Lily said to the bear, "We are sorry, bear. We did not mean to poke you and make you mad. We were playing with the mud. We did not know you were there. We want to be your friend. Can we play with you?" The bear nodded and smiled. It did not mind. It wanted to be their friend. It said to Tom and Lily, "I am sorry, too. I did not mean to scare you and make you cry. I was curious about the mud. I did not know you were playing. I want to play with you, too. Can we share the mud?" Tom and Lily nodded and smiled. They did not mind. They wanted to share the mud. They said to the bear, "Yes, we can share the mud. We can poke it and splash it and make shapes. We can have fun." The bear, Tom and Lily played with the mud. They poked it and splashed it and made shapes. They had fun. They became friends. Mom and dad watched them and smiled. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a naughty boy. He liked to collect rocks and sticks from the forest. One day, Tim went to the forest alone. In the forest, Tim met a big, talking bird. The bird said, "Tim, do you want to see my nest?" Tim said, "Yes, I want to see it!" So, the bird showed Tim the way to the nest. They had to cross a river to get there. When they crossed the river, Tim saw the bird's nest. It was very big and full of shiny things. Tim was naughty and took some shiny things from the nest. The bird got very mad and said, "You are not nice, Tim! You should not take things that are not yours!" Then, the bird flew away and left Tim alone in the forest. Tim was scared and lost. He cried for help, but no one came. The forest became dark and cold. Tim learned that being naughty and taking things that are not yours is wrong. The moral of the story is: always be kind and do not take things that do not belong to you.

Once upon a time, there was a young cat named Kitty. She lived in a small house with her family. Kitty loved to play with her toys and eat yummy stuff. One sunny day, Kitty woke up and stretched her legs. She felt so good! She went outside to play with her friends. They played in the warm sun all day long. As the sun went down, Kitty and her friends went back to their homes. Kitty was so happy she had a fun day. She ate some yummy stuff, stretched again, and went to sleep.
