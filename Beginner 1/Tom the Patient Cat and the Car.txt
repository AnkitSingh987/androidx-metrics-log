Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Patient Cat and the Car

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a patient cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat pastry. One day, he saw a pastry on the table. It looked so yummy. Tom wanted to eat it. Tom jumped on the table to get the pastry. But, he did not see the ball on the floor. When Tom jumped, he kicked the ball. The ball went flying across the room. It hit a toy car, and the car rolled away. The car rolled back to Tom and stopped at his feet. Tom looked at the car and then at the pastry. He decided to play with the car first. After playing, Tom ate the pastry. It was the best day ever.

Lily and Max were playing in their mom's room. They saw a big box of makeup on the dresser. They wanted to try it on. "Let's put some makeup on," Lily said. "It will make us look pretty." "Okay," Max said. "But I want to be a lion." They opened the box and found many colors and brushes. Lily picked a pink lipstick and put it on her lips. She looked in the mirror and smiled. "I look like a princess," she said. Max picked a brown pencil and drew lines on his face. He also found a hairy wig and attached it to his head. He looked in the mirror and roared. "I look like a lion," he said. They heard their mom coming up the stairs. They quickly hid the makeup and the wig under the bed. They pretended to read a book. Their mom opened the door and saw them. She smiled and hugged them. "Hello, my sweeties," she said. "What are you doing?" "Reading," Lily said. "Roaring," Max said. Their mom looked at them and noticed something strange. She saw the pink lipstick on Lily's lips and the brown lines on Max's face. "Did you play with my makeup?" she asked. Lily and Max looked at each other and nodded. They felt sorry. "We're sorry, mom," they said. Their mom was not angry. She laughed and kissed them. "It's okay, my loves," she said. "But next time, ask me first. And don't attach anything to your hair. It might hurt." Lily and Max said okay. They hugged their mom and said they loved her. They learned their lesson. They also had fun.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. It was a hot day, so Tim put on his sunglasses. They felt smooth on his face. Tim and Spot played with a ball and had fun. Then, a girl named Sue came to the park. Sue saw Tim's sunglasses and liked them. "Can I try?" she asked. Tim said, "Yes." Sue put on the sunglasses, but they did not fit. They fell off her face. Sue was sad. Tim had an idea. "Let's ask Mom to help," he suggested. They went to Tim's mom, who was sitting on a bench. She looked at the sunglasses and smiled. "I can fix them," she said. Tim's mom took out a string and tied it to the sunglasses. Now, they fit on Sue's face. Sue was happy, and they all played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was three years old and she loved gum. Every day, she would ask her mom to buy her a pack of gum, and her mom would always say yes. One day, Amy's mom had to go to the store and told Amy to stay at home. "Be a good girl, Amy," said her mom with a kiss on her cheek. Amy decided to stay comfortable on the couch and watch her favorite show. Then she realized she was bored and started to miss her gum. Suddenly, an idea came to her head. She remembered she had a pack of gum that her mom had bought her a few days ago. She ran to her room and looked for the pack in her little bag, and there it was. She was so happy that she did a little dance. Amy grabbed the pack and ran back to the living room. She opened it and used the gum to make bubble, one after another. She laughed and enjoyed her time at home. The end.
