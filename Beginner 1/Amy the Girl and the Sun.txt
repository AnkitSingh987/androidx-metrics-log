Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Amy. She loved to draw with her pencil. She drew all day long. She drew cats, dogs, and trees. Her pencil made her very happy. One day, Amy wanted to add something new to her drawings. She thought and thought. Then, she had an idea! She would add a big sun to her picture. The sun would make her drawings even more happy. Amy picked up her pencil and started to draw a big, round sun. She added lines to make the sun shine. When she finished, she looked at her picture. The sun made her drawings even more happy. Amy smiled and knew she did a great job.

Once upon a time, there was a little seed. The seed wanted to grow big and tall. It was in the ground, under the rough grass. The seed was sad, because it wanted to see the sun and play with the other plants. One day, the rain came. The rain was a big help for the seed. The rain made the ground wet, and the seed could drink the water. The seed felt happy and knew that it would grow soon. The next day, the sun came out. The sun was warm and bright. The seed started to grow. It pushed through the rough grass and saw the sun. The seed was now a little plant. It played with the other plants and was very happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a big swing and ran to it. Sue wanted to swing high in the sky. She sat on the swing and started to move. A boy named Tim came to the swing. He said, "Stop! I want to swing too." Sue did not want to stop. She wanted to keep swinging. Tim was sad and crossed his arms. Sue saw Tim and felt bad. Sue stopped the swing and said, "You can swing now." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you!" He started to swing high and clear. Sue and Tim took turns and played together. They became good friends.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They like to swing on the swings. They swing high and low. They feel happy. One night, Lily and Ben go to the park with their moms. They see the moon in the sky. The moon is big and bright. Lily and Ben want to swing to the moon. "Can we swing to the moon, mom?" Lily asks. "No, sweetie, the moon is too far away. You can only see it, not touch it." Lily's mom says. "But I want to touch the moon. It looks so pretty." Lily says. "Me too. Maybe the moon is fake. Maybe it is a big ball of light." Ben says. "No, the moon is not fake. It is real. It is a big rock that goes around the earth. It makes light at night." Ben's mom says. "How do you know, mom?" Ben asks. "I know because I learned it in school. And because I saw it with a telescope. A telescope is a thing that makes things look bigger." Ben's mom says. "Can we see the moon with a telescope, mom?" Lily asks. "Maybe someday, honey. But for now, let's enjoy the swings. The swings are real and fun." Lily's mom says. Lily and Ben nod. They go back to the swings. They swing high and low. They look at the moon. They feel happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a regular dog who loved to play and wear a red hat. One day, Spot went to the park with his red hat on. At the park, Spot saw a big dog named Max. Max did not wear a hat, and he thought it was funny that Spot did. Max laughed at Spot and said, "You look silly in that hat!" Spot felt sad, but he did not want trouble, so he kept playing. Later, it started to rain. All the dogs ran to find cover, but Spot was not worried. His red hat kept him dry! Max saw this and felt bad for laughing at Spot. He learned that it is not nice to make fun of others, and that sometimes being different can be a good thing. From that day on, Max and Spot became best friends, and they played happily ever after.
