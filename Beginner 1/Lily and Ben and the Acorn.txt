Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Acorn

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big tree with many green leaves. They see something brown and round on the ground. They pick it up. "What is this?" Lily asks. "It is an acorn," Ben says. "My mom told me. The tree makes acorns. The squirrels eat them." "Can we eat them too?" Lily wonders. "I don't know. Let's try," Ben says. They bite the acorn. It is hard and bitter. They spit it out. "Yuck! This is not good," Lily says. "No, it is not. It is funny. The squirrels like it, but we don't," Ben says. They laugh. They learn something new. They throw the acorn away. They run to the slide. They have fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was a very impatient boy. He always wanted to do things fast and never liked to wait. One day, Tim found a pipe in his yard. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, mom! I found a pipe!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! Let's settle down and think of a fun game we can play with it." Tim thought for a moment and said, "I know! We can play a game where we put the pipe on the ground and try to roll a ball through it!" His mom agreed, and they went to get a ball. They played the game together, and Tim learned that sometimes it's good to settle down and enjoy the moment. He became more patient and had lots of fun with his mom and the pipe. And from that day on, Tim was a happier boy.

Tom and Mia liked to play with their boat in the pond. They made the boat from a big leaf and a stick. They pretended the boat was a pirate ship and they were the captains. They were eager to find treasure and have adventures. One day, they saw a big duck swimming near their boat. The duck looked hungry and curious. It opened its beak and moved closer to the boat. Tom and Mia were scared. They did not want the duck to eat their boat. "Go away, duck!" Tom shouted. "This is our boat, not your food!" But the duck did not listen. It stretched its neck and tried to grab the boat. Mia had an idea. She picked up a stone and threw it at the duck. The stone hit the duck on the head. The duck quacked and swam away. "Yay, we did it!" Mia cheered. "We protected our boat from the duck!" Tom hugged Mia and smiled. "You are brave, Mia. You saved our boat. You are the best pirate captain ever!" They put their boat back in the pond and continued to play. They were happy and proud. They did not see the big fish lurking under the water, waiting for a chance to snatch their boat.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a cute kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki had many friends who liked to play with her. They would jump, run, and laugh all day long. But one day, Kiki's favorite toy broke. She was very sad and didn't know what to do. Kiki's friends saw her sad face and asked her what was wrong. She showed them her broken toy. Her friend, Billy the bird, told her that he knew someone who could help. He had seen a wise old owl who could repair things. Kiki's face lit up with hope, and they all went to find the owl. They found the wise old owl in a tall tree. Kiki showed him her broken toy. The owl looked at it carefully and said he could fix it. He worked on the toy and soon, it was as good as new. Kiki was so happy, and she thanked the owl. From that day on, Kiki and her friends knew that if they ever needed help, the wise old owl would be there for them. And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Kitty. She was famous because she could climb very high trees. One day, Kitty saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Kitty started to climb the big tree. She went up, up, up. When she got to the top, she saw a bird. The bird wanted to escape from Kitty. So, the bird flew away very fast. Kitty wanted to go down from the tree. She climbed down slowly and carefully. When she got to the ground, everyone clapped for her. Kitty was happy and proud of her climb.
