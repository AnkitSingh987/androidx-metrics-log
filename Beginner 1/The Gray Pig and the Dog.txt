Category: Beginner
Title: The Gray Pig and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little gray pig. The pig lived on a farm with other animals. The pig liked to play in the mud and eat yummy food. One day, the pig met a big gray dog. The dog said, "Hello, little pig! Do you want to play with me?" The pig was happy and said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" So, the pig and the dog played all day. They ran in the grass and splashed in the mud. They had so much fun together. At the end of the day, the pig said, "Thank you for playing with me, gray dog!" The dog said, "You're welcome, little pig! Let's play again soon!" And they became the best of friends.

One day, a little fish named Tim was swimming in the sea. He saw a big shark named Sam. Sam was grumpy because he had no friends. Tim wanted to help Sam feel better. Tim had an idea. He swam up to Sam and said, "Let's play a game! I will hide, and you can peek to find me." Sam agreed to play. Tim hid behind a rock, and Sam peeked around until he found him. They laughed and played the game many times. As they played, other sea animals saw how much fun they were having. They wanted to be friends with Sam too. Soon, Sam was not grumpy anymore. He had many friends, and they all played together. The moral of the story is that being kind and playing with others can help make friends and feel happy.

One day, a fast dog named Max saw an old man with a cane. The old man walked slow and looked sad. Max wanted to help him, so he walked up to the old man. "Can I lend you a paw?" Max asked. The old man smiled and said, "Thank you, Max. I need help getting to the park." As they walked, a big wind came and blew the old man's cane away. Max ran fast to get it. Just when Max thought he had it, the cane began to fly! Max jumped high and caught the flying cane. The old man laughed and said, "You found my magic cane! Now, we can go to the park fast!" With a wave of the magic cane, Max and the old man flew to the park, happy and fast.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy tank. He loved his tank very much. It was big and green. Tim liked to make it go fast on the floor. He thought it was easy to play with. Tim saw his friend Sue outside. He wanted to show her his tank. He ran to the door with the tank in his hands. But on his way, he did not see a toy car on the floor. Tim stumbled and fell down. His tank broke into two parts. Tim was very sad. Sue heard Tim cry and came in. She saw the broken tank and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can fix it!" Tim and Sue tried to put the tank back together. They worked hard and soon, the tank was fixed. Tim was happy again. He said, "Thank you, Sue. You are a good friend!"

One day, a gray ball went for a roll. It rolled and rolled in a big space. The ball was happy and liked to roll fast. Then, the gray ball saw a big hill. The ball wanted to roll down the hill. It rolled faster and faster. The ball was so happy. But, when the ball got to the bottom of the hill, it saw a big hole. The ball fell in the hole. It was dark and the ball was sad. Then, a big hand picked up the ball. It was a nice boy who helped the ball out of the hole. The gray ball and the boy played together and were happy.

One day, a little bear named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He was looking for some berries to eat. Tim saw a big bush with red berries. He was very happy and started to eat them. But he ate too many berries and felt tired. So, he decided to rest under a tree. While Tim was resting, a silly rabbit named Sam hopped by. Sam saw the berries and wanted to eat them too. But then, something unexpected happened. The bush with the berries started to shake and talk! It said, "Please don't eat all my berries!" Tim and Sam were very surprised. They did not know that the bush could talk. The bush told them that it was a magic bush. It said, "If you promise not to eat all my berries, I will give you a special gift." Tim and Sam agreed, and the bush gave them each a small bag of gold. From that day on, Tim and Sam were good friends. They played together in the woods and shared their berries with the magic bush. And they never felt silly again.
