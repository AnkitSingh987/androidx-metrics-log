Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Sally went for a slow walk in the park. She saw a pretty rose near the path. Sally wanted to pick the rose, but she was too small to reach it. A tall boy named Tom saw Sally and said, "Do you need help?" Sally said, "Yes, I want the rose, but I can't reach it." Tom was kind and wanted to lend a hand. He picked the rose for Sally and gave it to her. Sally was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Later, Sally and Tom saw a small dog stuck in a bush. They went to help the dog. Sally held the rose and Tom used the long stem to gently pull the dog out. The dog was free and wagged its tail. Sally and Tom were happy they could help. The rose that Tom picked for Sally had helped them save the dog.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little duck. The duck had a long neck. It lived near a big pond. The duck loved to play with its friends in the pond. One day, the duck saw a big frog. The frog was sad. The duck asked, "Why are you sad, frog?" The frog said, "I want to make a new game, but I need help." The duck wanted to help the frog. The frog said, "Let's add rocks to the pond and jump on them." The duck used its long neck to add rocks to the pond. The frog jumped on the rocks. The duck and the frog had fun playing their new game. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little bird. This bird had only one wing. He wanted to fly like all the other birds, but he could not. One day, a kind fairy saw the bird. She felt sad for the bird and wanted to help. The fairy used her magic to add another wing to the bird. Now, the adorable little bird had two wings. He was so happy! He flew up into the sky, playing with his friends. They all lived happily ever after. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Mia. She loved to spin around in her pretty dress. One sunny day, Mia went for a walk with her mommy. They walked down the road, holding hands and singing songs. As they walked, Mia saw a big tree with beautiful flowers. She wanted to spin under the tree. Mia asked, "Mommy, can I spin under the tree?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, Mia. You can spin under the tree." Mia ran to the tree and started to spin. She spun round and round, laughing and smiling. Her mommy clapped her hands and cheered for her. They had a great time spinning under the tree, and then walked back home hand in hand, down the lovely road.

One day, a little fish named Fin lived in the sea. He thought the sea was boring. He wanted to have fun and make new friends. So, Fin swam to the top of the sea to see what was there. When he got to the top, he saw a big bird named Sky. Sky was flying high in the sky. Fin said, "Hi Sky! The sea is boring. Can I play with you?" Sky said, "Yes, let's play together!" Fin and Sky played a fun game. They would slap the water with their fins and wings. They laughed and had a great time. Now, the sea was not so boring for Fin. He made a new friend and had lots of fun with Sky.

Sara loves to drive with her dad. They have a big car that goes fast and makes loud noises. Sara sits in the back and looks out the window. She likes to see the trees, the houses, the animals and the people. One day, they drive to the park. Sara is happy because she can play on the swings, the slide and the sandbox. She runs and jumps and laughs with her friends. Her dad watches her and smiles. He is proud of his little girl. But then, the sky gets dark and the wind blows hard. Sara hears a loud boom and sees a bright flash. It is lightning! She is scared and runs to her dad. He hugs her and tells her it is okay. He says lightning is just a big spark in the sky. He says it can be beautiful, too, if you are not afraid. He takes her to the car and they drive home. Sara sits in the back and looks out the window again. She sees the lightning again, but this time she is not scared. She sees the colors and shapes and patterns. She thinks it is beautiful, too. She tells her dad and he agrees. He says he loves her and she says she loves him, too. They drive home and have a cozy night. Sara is happy.
