Category: Beginner
Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to play hide and seek with his friends. But sometimes, Remy was a little selfish and didn't want to share his toys. One sunny day, Remy and his friends decided to do some exercise. They went to the park and played on the swings and slides. After a while, they got tired and wanted to play hide and seek. "Let's play hide and seek!" said Remy's friend, Lily the squirrel. "I'll count, and you all hide." Remy and his friends ran to find the best hiding spots. Remy found a big bush and hid behind it. He could hear Lily counting, "One, two, three..." Soon, Lily finished counting and started to look for her friends. She found everyone except Remy. Remy was so good at hiding that Lily couldn't find him. "Remy, come out! We want to play with your toys now," said Lily. Remy didn't want to be selfish anymore. He came out of his hiding spot and shared his toys with his friends. They all played happily together for the rest of the day.

---

One day, a girl named Mia and a boy named Tom went to the park. They saw a big fountain with water. Mia said, "Let's be careful near the fountain." Tom agreed, "Yes, we should be careful." Mia and Tom walked close to the fountain. They saw a pretty bird near the water. Mia said, "I will pray for the bird to be our friend." Tom prayed too. They both closed their eyes and prayed. When they opened their eyes, the bird was now a big, funny frog! The frog jumped and splashed in the water. Mia and Tom laughed. They were happy to have a new frog friend. They played near the fountain all day.

---

Tim and Mia were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a dove on a tree. The dove was white and pretty. They wanted to touch the dove, but it was too high. "Let's get a ladder and climb up the tree," Tim said. "OK, but be careful," Mia said. They found a ladder and put it against the tree. Tim went first and climbed up the ladder. He reached the dove and tried to grab it. But the dove was scared and flew away. "Come back, dove!" Tim shouted. He leaned over the ladder to follow the dove with his eyes. But he lost his balance and fell down. He hit the ground hard and hurt himself. "Tim, are you OK?" Mia asked. She ran to him and saw that he was bleeding and crying. "No, I'm not OK. It hurts a lot," Tim said. Mia was scared and sad. She did not know what to do. She looked for help, but no one was around. She wished they had not tried to touch the dove. The moral of the story is: Do not try to get what is not yours. You might get hurt or lose what you have.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. But sometimes they fight over them. Lily is bossy. She wants to have all the toys. Ben is sad. He wants to share. One day, they find a new toy in the closet. It is a big ball. It has many colors. It can make sparks. Lily and Ben are happy. They want to play with the ball. But Lily says, "This is my ball. You can't touch it. I found it first." She takes the ball and runs away. Ben follows her. He says, "No, this is our ball. We can play together. It is more fun." Lily and Ben run around the house. They make a lot of noise. They bump into things. They knock over a lamp. The lamp breaks. The sparks fly. The ball rolls away. Mom and Dad hear the noise. They come to see what is wrong. They see the broken lamp and the sparks. They are angry. They say, "What are you doing? You are making a mess. You are not careful. You are not safe." Lily and Ben are scared. They say, "We are sorry. We were playing with the ball. It can make sparks. It is a fun toy." Mom and Dad say, "That is not a toy. That is a firework. It is dangerous. It can hurt you. You should not play with it. You should ask us first." Lily and Ben say, "We did not know. We thought it was a toy. We found it in the closet." Mom and Dad say, "That is our closet. You should not go in there. That is where we keep our things. You should respect our things." Lily and Ben say, "We are sorry. We will not go in there again. We will respect your things." Mom and Dad say, "We are glad you are sorry. We love you. But you need to learn to share. You need to be kind to each other. You need to spend time together. You are twins. You are a team." Lily and Ben say, "We love you too. We will learn to share. We will be kind to each other. We will spend time together. We are twins. We are a team." Mom and Dad hug Lily and Ben. They say, "That is good. Now, let's clean up this mess. And then, we can find a safe toy for you to play with. Maybe a ball that does not make sparks."
