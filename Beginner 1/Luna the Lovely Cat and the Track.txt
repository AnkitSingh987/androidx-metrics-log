Category: Beginner
Title: Luna the Lovely Cat and the Track

One day, a lovely cat named Luna went outside to play. She saw a long track in the dirt. Luna wanted to follow the track to see where it went. She walked and walked until she saw a big puddle of water. Luna met a little dog named Max at the puddle. Max said, "I want to bathe in the water, but I'm scared." Luna wanted to help Max. She thought of a way to make him feel brave. Luna told Max, "We can bathe together! Hold my paw and we'll go in." Max held Luna's paw and they both went into the water. They splashed and played, and Max wasn't scared anymore. They became best friends and went home happy.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big hill near their house. They want to go up the hill and see what is on the other side. "Let's go to the hill, Sue!" Tom says. "It looks fun!" "OK, Tom!" Sue says. "But we should invite Sam, too. He is our friend and he likes to play with us." Tom thinks for a moment. He does not want to invite Sam. He thinks Sam is slow and heavy. He thinks Sam will make them tired and bored. "No, Sue," Tom says. "We don't need Sam. He is too heavy to go up the hill. He will slow us down. He will not have fun. Let's go by ourselves." Sue feels sad. She likes Sam. She thinks he is kind and funny. She thinks he will have fun with them. But she does not want to make Tom angry. She says nothing and follows Tom to the hill. They start to climb the hill. It is hard. The hill is steep and rocky. Tom and Sue get tired and thirsty. They wish they had water and snacks. They wish they had someone to help them and talk to them. They reach the top of the hill. They look around. They see trees and flowers and birds. They see a lake and a bridge and a house. They see Sam. He is sitting on a bench. He has water and snacks. He has a smile. "Hi, Tom and Sue!" Sam says. "I'm glad you made it to the hill. I saw you from the house. My grandma lives here. She invited me to visit her. She gave me water and snacks. She told me to wait for you. She wants to meet you and invite you to her house. Do you want to come?" Tom and Sue feel ashamed. They feel sorry for being mean to Sam. They feel happy to see him. They say yes to Sam. They say sorry to Sam. They say thank you to Sam. They go with Sam to his grandma's house. They have fun with Sam and his grandma. They learn a lesson. They learn to be kind and to invite their friends. They learn that being heavy does not mean being bad. They learn that being friends means being good.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball in the park. One day, Spot saw a boy with a helmet on his head. The boy was riding a bike. Spot wanted to play with the boy, so he ran up to him. The boy stopped and said, "Hi, Spot! Do you want to play?" Spot wagged his tail and barked happily. The boy threw the ball, and Spot ran after it. As Spot was running, he slipped on the wet grass. The boy saw this and helped Spot up. They both laughed and continued to play. Spot and the boy became good friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a brown bunny. The bunny loved to eat carrots. One day, the bunny went to look for more carrots to eat. The bunny saw a big carrot on the ground. The bunny was very happy. But the bunny had to hurry, because a big bird wanted the carrot too. The bunny ran fast and got the carrot. The bird flew away. The bunny ate the carrot and was very happy. The end.
