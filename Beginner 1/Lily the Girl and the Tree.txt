Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a noisy pet bird named Chirpy. They loved to play together. One day, Chirpy flew away and got lost. Lily was very sad. Lily went out to look for Chirpy. She walked and walked until she found a big tree. She heard Chirpy's noisy voice. "Chirpy, where are you?" Lily asked. "I am stuck in the tree!" Chirpy cried. Lily knew she had to rescue her friend. Lily had an idea. She took her comb and used it to reach Chirpy. She climbed the tree and got closer to the noisy bird. "Hold on to the comb, Chirpy!" Lily said. Chirpy held on tight, and Lily pulled him out of the tree. They were so happy to be together again.

Sam and Mia are playing with their toys in the garden. They have a jet, a car, a ball and a doll. Sam likes the jet the most. He makes it fly in the air and makes zooming noises. "Look, Mia, my jet is so fast and loud!" Sam says. Mia looks at the jet and thinks. She has an idea. She picks up the car and says, "My car is faster and louder than your jet. Watch!" She pushes the car on the ground and makes honking noises. The car zooms past the jet and reaches the fence. "Ha, ha, I win!" Mia says. Sam is not happy. He thinks his jet is better than the car. He says, "No, you don't win. My jet can fly higher than your car. Your car can only go on the ground." He throws the jet in the air and makes it soar over the fence. He says, "See, my jet is the best!" Mia is not happy. She thinks her car is better than the jet. She says, "No, your jet is not the best. My car can go in the water. Your jet can only go in the air." She runs to the pond and puts the car in the water. She makes splashing noises. The car floats on the water and moves around. "See, my car is the best!" Mia says. Sam is not happy. He thinks his jet is better than the car. He says, "No, your car is not the best. My jet can go in the sun. Your car can only go in the water." He runs to the sunflower and puts the jet on the flower. He makes shining noises. The jet is warm and bright on the flower. "See, my jet is the best!" Sam says. Mia is not happy. She thinks her car is better than the jet. She says, "No, your jet is not the best. My car can go in the shade. Your jet can only go in the sun." She runs to the tree and puts the car under the tree. She makes cooling noises. The car is cool and dry under the tree. "See, my car is the best!" Mia says. Sam and Mia look at each other. They both think their toy is the best. They both want to play with their toy. They both say, "Mine!" They start to pull and tug at their toys. They start to cry and shout. Mom hears them and comes to the garden. She sees them fighting over their toys. She is not happy. She says, "Stop, stop, stop! What are you doing? Why are you fighting over your toys? You have to share and play nicely. You have to say sorry and hug." Sam and Mia stop fighting. They look at Mom. They look at their toys. They think. They feel sorry. They say, "Sorry, sorry, sorry." They hug each other. They hug Mom. Mom smiles. She says, "That's better. Now, why don't you play together with your toys? You can take turns and have fun. You can make your jet and your car go in the air, on the ground, in the water, in the sun and in the shade. You can make different noises and stories. You can be friends." Sam and Mia smile. They think Mom is right. They say, "Okay, okay, okay." They take turns and play together with their toys. They make different noises and stories. They have fun. They are friends.
