Category: Beginner
Title: The Wolf Was and the Slide

One day, a big wolf was in the park. He was ready to play. He saw a slide and wanted to go down it. The wolf was very happy. The wolf went up the slide, but he was too big. He tried to slide down, but he got stuck. He tried to wiggle and push, but he could not move. The wolf was sad and scared. Then, a little girl came to the park. She saw the stuck wolf and wanted to help. She pushed the wolf and he slid down the slide. The wolf was happy and said thank you. The little girl and the wolf became friends and played together all day.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars in the living room. They liked to make them zoom and crash on the carpet. Lily had a red car and Ben had a blue car. They were having fun. But then, Lily saw a shiny yellow car on the shelf. It was her brother's car, and he did not like to share it. Lily wanted to play with it. She climbed on the couch and reached for the car. She grabbed it and smiled. "Look, Ben, I have a yellow car!" she said. Ben looked up and saw the car. He knew it was not Lily's car. He knew it was her brother's car. He felt angry. "That's not your car, Lily. That's Tom's car. He will be mad if you take it. Give it back!" he said. Lily did not want to give it back. She liked the yellow car. She liked how it sparkled in the light. She liked how it had a handle that she could push to make it go faster. "No, Ben, it's my car now. I like it. Go away!" she said. Ben did not go away. He got up and walked to the couch. He tried to take the car from Lily. Lily held on tight. They pulled and tugged. They shouted and cried. Then, they heard a loud voice. "Hey, what are you doing with my car?" It was Tom, Lily's brother. He had come home from school. He saw Lily and Ben fighting over his car. He felt very angry. He ran to the couch and grabbed his car. He pushed Lily and Ben away. He glared at them. "That's my car, you little brats. How dare you touch it? You broke the handle. Look, it's not working anymore. You ruined it!" he yelled. Lily and Ben felt very sorry. They did not mean to break the car. They did not mean to make Tom angry. They looked at each other. They looked at Tom. They looked at the car. They said in a small voice, "We're sorry, Tom. We're sorry we took your car. We're sorry we broke the handle. We're sorry we made you mad. Please don't be mad at us." Tom looked at them. He looked at his car. He felt very sad. He loved his car. He wished he could fix it. He wished he could make it go fast again. He sighed. He said in a soft voice, "It's okay, Lily and Ben. I'm sorry I yelled at you. I know you didn't mean it. I know you were just playing. But you have to ask me before you take my things. And you have to be careful with them. They are not toys. They are special to me." Lily and Ben nodded. They understood. They hugged Tom. They said, "We're sorry, Tom. We love you. Can we play with you?" Tom smiled. He hugged them back. He said, "Sure, you can play with me. But not with my car. How about we play with your cars? They look fun." Lily and Ben smiled. They ran to get their cars. They said, "Yes, let's play with our cars. They are fun. And we can share them with you." They played with their cars in the living room. They made them zoom and crash on the carpet. They laughed and talked. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to write and draw. One day, she wanted to write a story about a dangerous lion. Lily thought it would be fun to make the lion a nice friend. Lily started to write her story. She wrote about the lion and his new friend, a little bird. They played and had fun in the sun. But then, something unexpected happened. The dangerous lion started to change. He became nice and not dangerous at all. The little girl was happy to see the lion change. The lion and the bird became best friends. They played all day and laughed. Lily learned that even dangerous things can change and be nice. And they all lived happily ever after.
