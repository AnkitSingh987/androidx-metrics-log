Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Furry Dog and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Max. Max loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big slide at the park. Max wanted to try the slide, but he was a little scared. His friend, Tom, said, "Max, you can do it!" The next day, Max saw a big surf at the beach. He remembered the slide and how scared he was. But he also remembered what Tom said. So, Max took a deep breath and jumped into the surf. He had so much fun! Later, Max went back to the park with Tom. He looked at the big slide and thought of the fun he had in the surf. Max knew he could do it. He climbed up and slid down the slide. Max was so happy and proud. Tom cheered and said, "I knew you could do it, Max!"

Tom was a mighty horse who lived on a farm. He liked to run and jump and play with his friends. Every morning, he would wake up early and eat some hay. Hay was his favorite food. He would munch and crunch and feel happy. One day, he woke up and saw that his hay was gone. He looked around and saw a big truck. The truck had taken his hay and was driving away. Tom was angry and sad. He wanted his hay back. He ran after the truck and shouted, "Hey, that's my hay! Give it back!" The truck did not stop. It went faster and faster. Tom tried to catch up, but he could not. He was tired and out of breath. He stopped and felt very sad. He did not know what to do. Then, he heard a voice. It was his friend, Lily. Lily was a cow who lived on the farm. She saw Tom and came to him. She said, "Tom, what's wrong? Why are you so sad?" Tom told her what happened. He said, "The truck took my hay and did not give it back. I ran after it, but I could not catch it. Now I have no hay to eat. I am hungry and unhappy." Lily felt sorry for Tom. She said, "Don't worry, Tom. I have some hay for you. You can share it with me. Come, follow me. I will show you where it is." Tom smiled and thanked Lily. He followed her to a barn. There, he saw a big pile of hay. It was fresh and green and smelled good. Tom and Lily ate some hay together. They felt happy and full. They were good friends. Tom was glad that Lily helped him. He said, "Lily, you are very kind and generous. You are a true friend. Thank you for sharing your hay with me." Lily said, "You're welcome, Tom. You are a mighty and brave horse. You are a true friend, too. I am happy to share my hay with you." Tom and Lily hugged and smiled. They were happy and content. They had hay and they had each other. They were the best of friends.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big pond with ducks and boats. They want to go on a boat. "Can we go on a boat, please?" Tom asks his mom. "OK, but you have to be careful and wear a life jacket," his mom says. She gives them a life jacket and a row. "And don't stir the water too much, or you will scare the ducks." Tom and Sue get on a boat. They are happy. They use the row to move the boat. They see the ducks and the fish. They wave to the other kids. "Look, Sue, there is a big duck with a red beak," Tom says. "Let's go closer." "No, Tom, we have to be nice to the ducks," Sue says. "Mom said not to stir the water too much." But Tom does not listen. He rows faster and faster. He stirs the water a lot. He makes big waves. The big duck with the red beak gets angry. It quacks loudly and splashes water at Tom and Sue. They get wet and scared. "Help, help!" Tom and Sue cry. "The duck is mad at us!" Their mom hears them and comes to help. She pulls the boat to the shore. She is not happy. "What did you do, Tom and Sue?" she asks. "Why did you stir the water so much? You made the duck angry and you made a mess." "I'm sorry, mom," Tom says. "I wanted to see the duck closer. I did not mean to be bad." "I'm sorry, too, mom," Sue says. "I tried to stop Tom, but he did not listen." Their mom hugs them and says, "It's OK, I'm glad you are safe. But you have to learn to respect the animals and the nature. They are not toys. They have feelings and needs. And you have to listen to your friends when they tell you something good. Next time, be more careful and gentle." Tom and Sue nod and say, "Yes, mom, we will. We are sorry." They take off their wet clothes and put on clean ones. They say sorry to the duck and the other kids. They promise to be better. They learn a lesson.
