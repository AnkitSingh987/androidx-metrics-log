Category: Beginner
Title: Mark the Boy and the Bird

One day, a boy named Mark went out to play. It was not dark yet, so he could see well. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Mark was a brave boy, and he loved to climb trees. As Mark climbed the tree, he saw a bird. The bird looked sad. Mark asked, "Why are you sad, bird?" The bird said, "I am deaf. I cannot hear the other birds sing." Mark felt sad for the bird too. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew and the tree shook. Mark and the bird held on tight. When the wind stopped, the bird could hear again! It was a miracle! The bird was so happy, it sang a beautiful song for Mark. They both smiled and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there lived a boy named Will. Will loved to whistle all day long. He would whistle when he woke up, when he played, and even when he ate. One day, Will went for a walk in the park. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb. So, he began to climb the tree, whistling a happy tune. When he got to the top, he saw a small bird with a broken wing. Will wanted to help the bird, so he climbed down the tree with the bird in his hands. He took the bird home and made a soft bed for it to rest. Every day, Will took care of the bird and whistled to make it feel better. Soon, the bird's wing healed and it was time for it to fly away. Will said goodbye to his new friend and watched as it flew up into the sky. The bird whistled back at Will, and they knew they would always be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to jog every day. One day, while Tim was jogging, he saw a big dog. The dog looked fearful. Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the dog. Tim went near the dog and said, "Hi, dog! Why are you fearful?" The dog looked at Tim and said, "I am scared because my toy is torn. I love my toy so much." Tim wanted to help the dog. He took the torn toy and said, "Don't worry, dog! I will help you fix your toy." Tim and the dog went to Tim's house. They found some glue and used it to fix the torn toy. The dog was so happy to have his toy back. He said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim and the dog played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim and the dog became best friends, and they jogged together every day.

One day, a hungry cat named Tom saw a yummy bug on the ceiling. Tom wanted to catch the bug, but it was too high. He jumped and jumped, but he could not reach it. Tom was sad. Tom's friend, a big dog named Max, came to help. Max urged Tom to climb on his back. With a big smile, Tom got on Max's back and tried to catch the bug again. This time, Tom was closer to the ceiling. With one more big jump, Tom caught the bug. He was so happy and thanked Max for his help. They both laughed and played together. Tom was not hungry anymore. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a wide green park, there lived a cat named Tom and a dog named Sam. They were good friends who liked to play together. One day, they decided to play a game called "quiz". They would ask each other questions, and the one who could not answer would yield. Tom asked Sam, "What is big and green and has leaves?" Sam thought for a moment and then said, "A tree!" Tom clapped his paws and said, "Good job, Sam! Now it's your turn to ask me a question." Sam thought hard and asked, "What is small and has a tail and likes to eat cheese?" Tom thought and thought, but he could not find the answer. Tom said, "I give up. I yield. What is the answer?" Sam laughed and said, "It's a mouse, silly!" Tom felt sad because he did not know the answer. They decided to play one more round of the quiz game. This time, Tom asked a very hard question, and Sam could not answer. Sam felt sad, too. They both decided that the quiz game was not fun anymore. In the end, they stopped playing and went home with a bad feeling. They learned that sometimes, games can make friends feel bad if they are too hard or if someone always wins.
