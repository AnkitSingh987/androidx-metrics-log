Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Train

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play together. One day, they went with their mom to the station to see the big trains. They were very excited and ran to the platform. "Look, Ben, that train is red and shiny!" Lily said, pointing at a train that was about to leave. "I want to go on that train!" Ben said, and tried to pull his mom's hand. "No, Ben, we can't go on that train. It's not ours. We have to wait for our train. It will come soon," mom said, holding him back. Ben was angry and did not want to wait. He wanted to go on the red train. He pinched Lily's arm hard and made her cry. "Ow, Ben, that hurts! Why did you pinch me?" Lily said, rubbing her arm. "Because you are weak and silly. I want to go on the red train!" Ben said, and pinched her again. "Stop it, Ben! That's not nice. You are hurting your sister. Say sorry to her," mom said, and took his hand away from Lily. Ben did not say sorry. He pouted and crossed his arms. He did not look at Lily or mom. Lily was sad and hurt. She did not want to play with Ben anymore. She hugged mom and said, "Mom, can we go on our train now?" Mom nodded and smiled. She said, "Yes, Lily, we can. Our train is coming. Look, it's blue and shiny. Do you like it?" Lily looked at the blue train and smiled. She said, "Yes, mom, I like it. It's very pretty. Can we sit by the window?" "Of course, Lily. We can sit by the window and see the trees and the houses and the animals. Maybe we will see a cow or a horse or a bird. Do you want to see them?" mom said, and lifted Lily up. Lily nodded and said, "Yes, mom, I want to see them. Maybe Ben will see them too and be happy." Mom looked at Ben and said, "Ben, do you want to see the cow or the horse or the bird?" Ben looked at the blue train and said, "No, I don't. I want to see the red train." Mom sighed and said, "Ben, the red train is gone. It's too late. We have to go on the blue train. It's our train. It will take us to grandma's house. Don't you want to see grandma?" Ben thought for a moment. He liked grandma. She always gave him cookies and hugs and stories. He said, "Yes, I want to see grandma." Mom smiled and said, "That's good, Ben. Then come on, let's go on the blue train. And say sorry to Lily. She is your sister and she loves you." Ben looked at Lily and saw that she was still sad. He felt sorry for pinching her. He said, "Sorry, Lily. I was mean. I love you." Lily smiled and said, "It's okay, Ben. I love you too." They hugged and made up. Mom was happy and said, "That's my good twins. Now let's go on the blue train and have fun." They went on the blue train and sat by the window. They saw the trees and the houses and the animals. They saw a cow and a horse and a bird. They waved at them and laughed. They had fun. They forgot about the red train. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to imagine all sorts of things. One day, she found a fragile newspaper in her house. It was old and had many pictures on it. Lily looked at the pictures and started to imagine a magical world. In this world, she was a princess with a big, shiny crown. She had a pet cat that could talk and they went on many fun adventures together. One day, Lily and her cat found a big, soft bed made of clouds. They jumped on it and laughed as they bounced up and down. They played all day long, until it was time to go home. Lily felt so happy in her magical world, and she knew that she could always imagine more fun adventures with her talking cat.
