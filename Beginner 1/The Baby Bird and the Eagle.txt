Category: Beginner
Title: The Baby Bird and the Eagle

Once upon a time, there was a baby bird. This bird's wings were small, but it wanted to soar up high. The bird tried and tried again, but it couldn't get off the ground. One day, the bird saw a large eagle flying in the sky. He watched it swoop and soar. The bird's heart was filled with longing. He wished he could fly just like the eagle. The bird asked the eagle, “How can I fly like you?” The eagle said, “If you have deep faith, you can do anything.” The baby bird thought and thought. He believed that if he had deep faith, he could fly. He imagined himself soaring up high. The next day, the bird flapped his wings with all his might. Suddenly, he felt a lift against his wings. With deep faith, he soared into the sky. He flew higher and higher, until the entire world was his. The baby bird realized he could do anything if he believed. The little bird now knew how to soar. To celebrate, he got dressed up in his best feathers and makeup. He was ready to take on the world and be the best bird he could be.

Ben and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. They also liked to splash in the pond. The pond had many fish and ducks. They were lively and fun to watch. One day, it rained a lot. The rain did not stop for a long time. The pond got bigger and bigger. It increased and increased. The water came out of the pond and onto the grass. The grass became wet and muddy. The fish and ducks had more space to swim, but the park was not good for playing. Ben and Lily were sad. They wanted to play in the park, but it was a flood. They could not swing, slide or run. They could not splash in the pond. They could only watch the fish and ducks from far away. They asked their mom and dad when the rain would stop. Their mom and dad said they did not know. They said they had to wait until the sun came out. They said the sun would dry the water and make the park good again. Ben and Lily hoped the sun would come out soon. They missed the park. They missed the fish and ducks. They missed the swing, slide and run. They missed the splash in the pond. They wanted the park to be lively and fun again.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. But they also liked to pretend. They found a long belt on the ground. It was brown and shiny. "Look, a snake!" Lily said. She picked up the belt and waved it in the air. "No, it's not a snake. It's a rope!" Ben said. He grabbed the other end of the belt and pulled it. "Let go! It's mine!" Lily said. She pulled back. "No, it's mine!" Ben said. He pulled harder. They both pulled and pulled. The belt stretched and snapped. It broke in two. "Ow!" Lily said. She dropped her end of the belt. It hit her hand. "Ow!" Ben said. He dropped his end of the belt. It hit his foot. They both started to cry. A lady came over. She was wearing a blue dress and a hat. She had a kind face. "What's wrong, children?" she asked. "We found a belt and we wanted to play with it, but it broke!" Lily said. "It was a snake, but he said it was a rope!" Ben said. The lady smiled. She picked up the two pieces of the belt. "It's not a snake or a rope. It's a belt. It's for holding up your pants. But it's not very useful now. It's broken. Why don't you share something else? Like this ball?" she said. She took out a red ball from her bag and gave it to them. "Thank you, lady!" Lily and Ben said. They stopped crying. They took the ball and started to play with it. They threw it, caught it and rolled it. They had fun. The lady watched them. She was happy. She put the belt in the trash. She walked away. The end.
