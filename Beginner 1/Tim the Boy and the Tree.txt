Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

One day, a boy named Tim and his friend Sue were playing outside. They liked to pretend a lot. Today, they made a jail from big boxes. They took turns being the jail guard and the one in jail. While they were playing, a big wind came and blew the boxes away. Tim and Sue were sad. They thought their jail was dead. But then, they saw a big tree with a hole in it. They thought it looked like a jail, too. They started to play with the tree as their new jail. When Tim was in the tree jail, he found a secret door! The door led to a hidden room with toys and games. Tim and Sue were so happy. They played in their new secret jail all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a long cat named Quick. Quick loved to spread out in the sun and nap. One day, Quick saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb it. Quick jumped and climbed the tree very fast. At the top, Quick saw a nest with three small eggs. Quick knew that the eggs needed to be safe, but the cat was too long to climb down with them. Quick thought of a plan. The cat spread out its long body and made a slide for the eggs. One by one, the eggs slid down Quick's back and landed safely on the ground. When the mother bird came back, she was so happy to see her eggs safe. And Quick had made a new friend.

Tom was a clever boy who liked to make things. He made a whistle out of a stick and a string. He was very proud of his whistle and wanted to show it to his friends. He went to the park and saw his friends playing with a ball. He ran to them and said, "Look what I made! A whistle! Do you want to hear it?" His friends nodded and smiled. Tom put the whistle in his mouth and blew hard. But nothing happened. He blew harder and harder, but still no sound came out. His friends laughed at him and said, "That's not a whistle! That's a stick! You can't make a whistle out of a stick! You are not clever, you are silly!" Tom felt sad and angry. He threw his whistle on the ground and ran away. He did not want to play with his friends anymore. He ran to the road and did not look for cars. He was too upset to be careful. A big car came fast and hit him. Tom fell down and did not get up. He was hurt very bad. The driver of the car stopped and got out. He saw Tom and his whistle on the road. He picked up the whistle and looked at it. He saw that it had a small hole in the middle. He blew into the hole and heard a loud sound. It was a whistle! He said, "This is a clever whistle! Who made it? Where is the boy who made it?" But it was too late. Tom was gone. The moral of the story is: Don't be mean to your friends. They might have something special that you don't see. And always look for cars before you cross the road. They might hurt you very bad.

Little Joe was getting ready for school. He was excited but he couldn't decide what to wear. He thought and thought. "I know, I will wear my dinosaur shirt," he said! He went to his closet and pulled out a bright green shirt with a big dinosaur on it. He put on the shirt and he was so happy! He hugged his mom. "Mommy, look," he said. Mommy smiled. "That shirt looks rough, Joe," she said. But Joe didn't care. He was so excited to wear his dinosaur shirt that nothing else mattered to him. He went off to school with a big grin on his face and feeling very proud of his special shirt.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had very rough hair. One day, his mom said, "Tim, you need a haircut. Let's go to the hair shop." At the hair shop, Tim met a nice lady named Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tim! Let's unpack your bag and find your cape." Tim put on the cape and sat in the big chair. Sue gave Tim a nice haircut. She cut his rough hair so it was soft and smooth. Tim looked in the mirror and said, "Thank you, Sue! I like my new haircut." Tim and his mom went home, and Tim showed his new haircut to all his friends.
