Category: Beginner
Title: Olive the Bossy Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Olive. Olive liked to tell all the other animals what to do. One day, Olive saw a big red ball. She wanted to play with it, but she was scared she would lose it. Olive asked her friend, a small dog, to help her watch the ball. The dog said yes, but he was not very good at it. Olive was mad and told the dog to go away. Olive was sure she could do it all by herself. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the ball away! Olive was very sad that she lost the ball. She learned that she should not be so bossy and should work together with her friends. From that day on, Olive was a kinder and happier cat.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim had a special cloth that could glow in the dark. He loved to play with it at night. One day, a big, fearful dog saw Tim's glowing cloth and wanted it. The dog said, "Give me your glowing cloth!" But Tim did not want to give it away. He was scared of the big dog, but he loved his cloth too much. They both wanted the cloth, so they began to fight. Tim tried to run away, but the dog was too fast. The dog bit Tim and took the glowing cloth. Tim cried and went home without his special cloth. The big, fearful dog had won, and Tim was very sad.

One day, a bird named Bob wanted to soar high in the sky. He saw a jug on the ground. The jug was big and had a delicate flower on it. Bob thought the jug was pretty. Bob had a friend named Tom. Tom was a mean bird who liked to take things from Bob. Tom saw Bob with the jug and wanted it for himself. They both started to fight over the jug. In the end, the delicate jug fell and broke into pieces. Bob and Tom were both sad because the pretty jug was gone. Neither of them got to keep the jug, and they both learned a lesson about fighting.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the beach with her mom and dad. Mia loved the beach because she could play in the sand and swim in the water. She liked to rub her feet in the sand and feel how soft it was. While Mia was playing, she saw a fish in the water. The fish was big and had an ancient look. Mia thought the fish was very special. She ran to her mom and dad and told them about the fish. Mia's mom and dad came to see the ancient fish. They all watched as the fish swam in the water. Mia wanted to be friends with the fish. So, she waved to the fish and the fish waved back with its tail. Mia, her mom, and her dad all laughed and had a great day at the beach.

Once there was a little dog named Max. Max was a happy dog with a big smile. One day, Max saw a big cat. The cat was very mean. Max started to panic. Max said, "I am sorry, cat. I don't want to make you mad." The cat just looked at Max and did not say anything. Max was very scared. He tried to run away, but the cat was too fast. The cat caught Max and said, "You should not panic, little dog. But now it is too late." The cat took Max's smile away. Max was very sad and sorry, but he could not get his smile back.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a cute dog. The dog has brown fur and a black nose. It runs to them and wags its tail. "Hello, doggy!" Anna says. "What is your name?" The dog barks and licks Anna's face. She giggles and hugs the dog. "Can we keep him, Ben?" she asks. Ben shakes his head. "No, Anna. He belongs to someone. See, he has a tag on his chest. It says Max." Anna looks at the tag. She can't read it, but she trusts Ben. She feels sad. She wants the dog to be hers. "Can we play with him, then?" she asks. Ben nods. "Sure, Anna. But we have to be careful. He might run away or bite us." They play with the dog for a while. They throw a ball and he fetches it. They rub his belly and he rolls over. They have fun. But then, something bad happens. A big car comes down the road. It is very fast and loud. The dog sees the car and barks. He runs after the car. He does not see the red light. "Max, no!" Anna and Ben shout. They try to stop him, but they are too late. The car hits the dog. The dog yelps and falls. He does not move. He is hurt. He is bleeding. Anna and Ben run to the dog. They cry and scream. They are scared and sad. "Max, Max, please wake up!" Anna says. "Please be okay!" But the dog does not wake up. He is gone. He is dead. Anna and Ben hug each other. They wish they never saw the dog. They wish they never played with him. They wish he was still alive. They are sorry. They are sorry for Max.
