Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Box

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to visit her aunt. Her aunt had a big house with many rooms. Lily loved to play hide and seek with her aunt. They had so much fun together. One day, Lily felt anxious. She wanted to find a new place to hide. She looked all around the house. She found a big box in a room. Lily thought it was a great place to hide. She went inside the box and waited for her aunt. Her aunt started to look for Lily. She looked in the rooms, under the bed, and behind the chairs. But she could not find Lily. Finally, her aunt found the big box. She opened it and saw Lily inside. They both laughed and hugged each other. They played hide and seek all day long.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big puddle of water. It looked fun. "Let's step in the puddle!" Ben said. "OK!" Lily said. They ran to the puddle and stepped in it. Splash! The water was cold and wet. They laughed and stepped again. Splash! Splash! But then they heard a voice. It was Mom. She was angry. "Ben! Lily! What are you doing? You are making a mess! You are in trouble!" Mom said. Ben and Lily stopped. They looked at Mom. They looked at their shoes and clothes. They were dirty and wet. "Sorry, Mom," Ben said. "We just wanted to play," Lily said. Mom sighed. She was not happy. "Come on, let's go home. You need to change and dry off. And no more stepping in puddles!" Mom said. Ben and Lily followed Mom. They felt sad. They liked the puddle. But they did not like to make Mom angry. They hoped Mom would forgive them. Maybe they could play in the puddle another day. When Mom was not looking.

One day, a little girl named Mia went outside to play. She had a big bottle of bubble mix and a wand to make bubbles. Mia loved to make delicate bubbles and watch them fly in the sky. Mia saw her friend Tom playing in his yard. She called out, "Tom, come play with me! Let's make bubbles together!" Tom came running over, excited to make bubbles too. They took turns dipping the wand in the bubble mix and blowing gently. Delicate bubbles floated through the air, making them both smile and laugh. They tried to catch the bubbles, but they would pop when they touched them. "Wow, Mia! You make the biggest bubbles!" Tom said, clapping his hands. Mia smiled and said, "You make pretty bubbles too, Tom!" They played with the bubbles all day, until the sun began to set. Mia and Tom said goodbye and went inside their homes, happy with their fun day of making delicate bubbles together.

Tim is a young boy who likes to chew. He chews his food, his toys, his clothes and sometimes even his fingers. His mom and dad tell him to stop chewing things that are not food, but he does not listen. He thinks chewing is fun and makes him strong. One day, Tim finds a hammer in the garage. He thinks it is a big and shiny toy. He picks it up and puts it in his mouth. He tries to chew the hammer, but it is hard and hurts his teeth. He makes a loud noise and drops the hammer. His mom hears him and runs to the garage. She sees Tim crying and holding his mouth. She sees the hammer on the floor. She is angry and scared. She picks up Tim and hugs him. She says, "Tim, you should not chew the hammer. It is not a toy. It is a tool. It can break your teeth and hurt you. You are a good boy, but you need to learn what to chew and what not to chew. Do you understand?" Tim nods and says, "I'm sorry, mom. I won't chew the hammer again. I love you." His mom smiles and says, "I love you too, Tim. Now let's go and check your teeth. Maybe we can find you something better to chew, like a carrot or a cookie." Tim agrees and follows his mom. He still likes to chew, but he learns to be more careful.
