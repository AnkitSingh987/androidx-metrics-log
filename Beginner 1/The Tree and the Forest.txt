Category: Beginner
Title: The Tree and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in a forest. The big tree had many branches and leaves. It was very tall and strong. Many little animals lived near the big tree. One day, it started to rain. All the little animals needed a place to stay dry. The big tree was happy to supply shelter to them. The little animals said, "Thank you, big tree!" The rain stopped and the sun came out. The big tree was happy to see the little animals play and have fun. The little animals were happy too, because the big tree had helped them. They all lived happily together.

Tim and Mia like to play with clay. They make animals and people and cars with clay. They have fun and laugh a lot. But sometimes they get tired and need to rest. One day, they decide to make a model of their house with clay. They use brown clay for the walls, red clay for the roof, and green clay for the grass. They also make a door, some windows, and a chimney. They are very proud of their model. "Look, our house is so pretty!" Mia says. "Yes, it is. Let's show it to Mom and Dad!" Tim says. They pick up their model and carry it to the living room. But on the way, they trip over a toy and drop their model. The clay breaks and smears on the floor. Tim and Mia are very sad and cry. "Oh no, our house is ruined!" Mia says. "It's okay, we can make another one." Tim says. But then they see their dog, Spot, coming to the living room. Spot sees the clay on the floor and thinks it is food. He starts to lick and eat the clay. Tim and Mia are shocked and disgusted. "Spot, stop! That's not food, that's clay!" Tim says. "Yuck, Spot, that's disgusting! You'll get sick!" Mia says. They run to Spot and try to pull him away from the clay. But Spot likes the clay and growls at them. He does not want to share. Tim and Mia are scared and call for help. "Mom, Dad, help! Spot is eating our clay house!" they shout. Mom and Dad hear their shouts and come to the living room. They see the mess and the dog and the children. They are surprised and worried. "What happened here?" Mom asks. "We made a model of our house with clay, but we dropped it and Spot ate it!" Tim explains. "Oh dear, that's not good. Spot, spit it out!" Dad says. He grabs Spot by the collar and takes him to the bathroom. He makes him drink some water and spit out the clay. He checks if Spot is okay. He is. He gives him a hug and a treat. Mom helps Tim and Mia clean up the clay from the floor. She throws away the broken pieces and wipes the stains. She gives them a hug and a kiss. "I'm sorry your model got ruined. It was very nice. But you can make another one tomorrow. How about we rest now and watch a movie?" she says. Tim and Mia nod and smile. They are still sad, but they feel better. They love their mom and dad and their dog. They go to the couch and cuddle with them. They watch a movie and rest. They are happy.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They saw a big tree with red apples. They wanted to pick some apples and eat them. They looked for a ladder to reach the apples. They found a ladder near the shed. Ben was brave and climbed the ladder first. He picked an apple and bit it. It was tasty and juicy. He smiled and said, "Anna, come up and try an apple. They are very good." Anna was scared of the ladder. She thought it was too high and shaky. She said, "No, Ben, I can't. I'm afraid I will fall and hurt myself." Ben saw that Anna was scared. He wanted to help her. He said, "Don't be afraid, Anna. I'm here with you. The ladder is strong and safe. You can do it. I will encourage you and hold your hand." Anna heard Ben's words. She felt a little better. She decided to try the ladder. She took a deep breath and climbed the ladder slowly. Ben encouraged her and held her hand. He said, "You're doing great, Anna. You're almost there. You're very brave." Anna reached the top of the ladder. She saw the apples and felt happy. She picked an apple and bit it. It was tasty and juicy. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Ben. You're a good friend. You helped me overcome my fear. The apples are very good." Ben and Anna hugged and shared their apples. They learned that they can do hard things with courage and encouragement. They enjoyed their tasty treat and their friendship.
