Category: Beginner
Title: Max and the Band

Max worked hard every day. He wanted to be in the band. But he was deaf, so he had to practice even harder. He worked and worked until one day he heard something. He was sure it was the band! He followed the sound until he arrived at a big hall. The band was playing and it was beautiful. Max was so excited, he tapped his leg and clapped his hands. Everyone looked at him. The band members called him onto the stage. He was so happy! He showed them he knew the music and he could keep the rhythm. All the band members smiled and said they would love to have Max in the band. Max had worked hard and it had paid off. He was part of the band.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his mask. One day, he met a dog named Sam. Sam was big and strong. Sam saw Tom's mask and wanted to play too. "Can I play with your mask?" asked Sam. Tom said, "Yes, but be careful. It is my favorite." Sam took the mask and ran around with it. Tom was happy to see Sam having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Sam tripped and fell. The mask broke into pieces. Sam had destroyed Tom's mask. Tom was sad and cried. Sam felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Tom. I will get you a new mask." Tom forgave Sam, and they became best friends.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, Tom sees a hairy bear in Lily's room. He wants to play with it. "Can I borrow your bear?" Tom asks Lily. "No, you can't. It is my bear. I love it very much." Lily says. "But I want to play with it. Just for a little bit. Please, Lily." Tom begs. Lily thinks for a moment. She likes to share, but she also likes her bear. She has an idea. "OK, you can borrow my bear. But you have to pass a test first." Lily says. "A test? What test?" Tom asks. Lily points to a pile of books on the floor. "You have to read one of these books to me. If you read it well, you can borrow my bear. If you read it bad, you can't." Tom looks at the books. They have big words and pictures. He does not know how to read them. He feels sad. "I can't read these books, Lily. They are too hard for me." Tom says. Lily smiles. She knows Tom can't read them. She just wants to keep her bear. "Then you can't borrow my bear, Tom. Sorry." Lily says. Tom feels angry. He thinks Lily is not a nice friend. He does not want to play with her anymore. He runs out of her room and goes to his own room. He finds his own toy, a green frog. He hugs it and says: "I don't need your bear, Lily. I have my frog. He is not hairy, but he is soft and fun. He is my best friend."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lindsey who liked to play with her wheel. She liked to push it around and keep it rolling. She was very clever. One day, while out playing in the yard, she noticed a puppy sitting nearby. She asked the puppy, “Hi! What’s your name?” The puppy answered, “My name is Spot. I like to keep my wheel rolling too.” Lindsey was pleased. “Let’s play together!” she said with a smile. Spot and Lindsey ran around together and kept their wheels rolling. They were having lots of fun. Then Spot stopped and said, “I have to go home now. Maybe we can play together again tomorrow!” Lindsey waved goodbye as Spot went off with his wheel. She thought how clever Spot was to keep his wheel rolling. From that day on, Lindsey and Spot were friends and kept their wheels rolling together in the sun.

One day, a big dog named Bob and a small cat named Sam went for a walk. They wanted to find a new park to play in. They took a turn on a path they had never been on before. They were both very confused because they did not know where they were. Bob said, "Let's add more turns to find the park." Sam agreed, and they took another turn. They saw a big tree and a pond. They still did not find the park, but they were happy to see the tree and pond. Finally, they met a bird named Sue. Sue said, "I know where the park is! Follow me!" Bob and Sam followed Sue, and they found the park. They played all day and had a lot of fun. They were not confused anymore, and they knew how to find the park next time.
