Category: Beginner
Title: The Shoe Was Popular and the Play

One day, a shoe was very popular. All the kids loved the shoe. They would play with the shoe and talk about it all the time. One kid said to another, "I like this shoe a lot. Do you like it too?" The other kid said, "Yes, it is my favorite shoe. I want to play with it every day." But one day, the shoe started to rot. It was not nice anymore. The kids were sad and did not want to play with the shoe. They did not know what to do. Then, a new shoe came to the playground. It was shiny and pretty. The kids loved the new shoe and started to play with it. They were happy again and forgot about the old, rotten shoe. In the end, the kids learned that sometimes things change and new things can be just as fun. They were happy to play with the new shoe and made new memories together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He found a big stone near his house. He thought it was fun to kick the stone. But when he kicked it, the stone did not move. Tim tried again, but it still did not move. A small bird came and said, "Why do you want to move the stone?" Tim said, "I want to play with it." The bird said, "I can help you, but I am scared. What if the stone falls on me?" Tim said, "I will be careful. I promise." Together, they tried to move the stone. Tim pushed and the bird pulled with its beak. They tried very hard, but the stone was too heavy. They were tired and sad. Then, a big dog came and said, "I can help you move the stone." With the dog's help, they moved the stone. Tim, the bird, and the dog played together and had a lot of fun. They were very happy and not scared anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He loved the sunshine and how it made everything warm and bright. As he walked, he saw a little bird on the ground. The bird looked frightened. "Hi, bird!" said Tim. "Why are you frightened?" The bird looked up at Tim and said, "I am lost. I can't find my mommy. Can you help me find her?" Tim wanted to help the bird, so he picked it up and they walked together. They looked everywhere for the bird's mommy. Just when they were about to give up, they saw a big shadow in the sky. It was the bird's mommy! The bird's mommy swooped down and said, "Thank you for helping my baby." Then, she did something unexpected. She gave Tim a big hug with her wings. Tim and the bird were so happy. The sunshine seemed to shine even brighter as they all went home together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He had a red ball that he liked to play with. Tim would throw the ball up high and then catch it. He felt very comfortable playing in the park. At one point, Tim saw a cute dog running around. He wanted to play with the dog, so he tried to throw the ball for the dog to catch. But when he threw the ball, it went too far away. The dog ran after the ball, but it did not bring it back. Tim was sad that he had lost his ball. His mom saw him and said, "Don't worry, we will find it." They walked around the park, looking for the red ball. Then, they saw the dog drop the ball near a tree. Tim was so happy to get his ball back. He hugged his mom and they went back to playing in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a high tree. In the tree, there was a big bird. The bird was a guard. The guard bird kept the tree safe. One day, a small cat came to the tree. The cat wanted to play. The guard bird said, "No, you can't play here. This tree is high, and I must keep it safe." The cat sighed. It said, "I just want to play with you. Can we play on the ground?" The guard bird thought for a moment, then said, "Okay, we can play on the ground." So, the guard bird and the cat played on the ground. They had lots of fun together. From that day on, they were best friends.
