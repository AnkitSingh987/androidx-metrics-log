Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Boy and the Sun

One day, a little boy named Ben wanted to go to the park with his mom. He put on his shoes and his hat and ran to the door. But his mom said, "Wait, Ben. We can't go to the park yet. It is too dark and cold outside. We have to wait for the sun to come up." Ben was sad. He did not like to wait. He wanted to play on the swings and the slide and the sandbox. He asked his mom, "When will the sun come up, mom? How long do we have to wait?" His mom smiled and hugged him. She said, "The sun will come up soon, Ben. It is almost morning. Do you want to see how the sun starts the day? Come with me to the window and we can watch together." Ben followed his mom to the window. He saw the sky was dark and full of stars. He liked the stars. They were shiny and pretty. His mom said, "The stars are like the sun's friends. They keep the sky bright at night. But when the sun comes up, they go to sleep. Can you see the sun, Ben? It is hiding behind the hills." Ben looked hard. He saw a tiny bit of orange light peeking over the hills. He said, "There it is, mom! There is the sun! It is starting to come up!" His mom said, "Yes, Ben. You are right. The sun is starting to come up. And look, the sky is changing color. It is not dark anymore. It is blue and pink and purple. And the stars are fading away. They are saying goodbye to the sun and to us. Can you say goodbye to the stars, Ben?" Ben waved his hand and said, "Bye, stars! Thank you for being bright and pretty. See you again at night!" His mom said, "You are very wise, Ben. You know a lot about the sun and the stars. And you are very patient. You waited for the sun to come up. Now we can go to the park and have fun. Are you ready, Ben?" Ben nodded and said, "Yes, mom! I am ready! Let's go to the park and play!" He grabbed his mom's hand and ran to the door. He was happy. He liked the sun and the stars and the park. He liked his mom. He had a good day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a polite girl named Lily. She liked to welcome everyone with a big smile. Lily was very happy and loved to make new friends. One day, Lily went to a place where she could learn history. It was a big room with many books and pictures. She was very excited to learn about the past. Lily found a book with a picture of a big castle. She wanted to know more about it. Lily asked her teacher about the castle. Her teacher told her a fun story about the people who lived there long ago. They were also polite and loved to welcome others. Lily was happy to learn about history and share it with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a great big cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his friends in the park. One day, Tom saw a purse on the ground. He picked it up with his paw and looked inside. It was full of pretty things. Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, came running over. Max saw the purse and said, "Wow, Tom! That's a great purse. What's inside?" Tom showed Max all the pretty things in the purse. Tom and Max decided to play a game with the purse. They took turns sacking the purse by jumping on it and trying to catch it. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Then, they went home to rest and get ready for another fun day.

Lily and Tom were playing with their toys on the rug in the living room. Lily had a doll and Tom had a car. They were having fun, but they also wanted to try each other's toys. "Can I play with your car, Tom?" Lily asked. "OK, but only for a little bit. Then you give it back to me. It is my car. I own it," Tom said. Lily nodded and took the car. She made it zoom and beep on the rug. Tom took the doll and pretended it was talking. "Hello, I am a doll. I like to dance and sing," Tom said in a high voice. Lily and Tom laughed. They were happy to share their toys. But then, Lily was too clumsy. She bumped the car into the table leg and it broke. The wheel came off and the car stopped working. "Oh no! I am sorry, Tom. I broke your car," Lily said. Tom was angry and sad. He looked at his car and then at Lily. "That was my car! You broke my car! You are a bad girl!" Tom shouted. He grabbed the doll from Lily and threw it on the floor. The doll's head cracked and its hair fell out. Lily was shocked and sad. She looked at her doll and then at Tom. "That was my doll! You broke my doll! You are a bad boy!" Lily shouted. She ran to the couch and cried. Tom ran to the door and cried. They both felt sorry for what they did, but they did not know how to say it. They missed playing with each other. Mom came into the living room and saw the broken toys and the crying children. She was not happy. "What happened here? Why did you break your toys and hurt each other?" Mom asked. Lily and Tom tried to explain, but they only blamed each other. "She broke my car! He broke my doll!" they said. Mom shook her head and sighed. She picked up the broken toys and the children. "Come with me. We need to talk," Mom said. She took them to the kitchen and gave them some juice and cookies. She told them that breaking toys and shouting was not nice. She told them that they should be sorry and say sorry. She told them that they should share and be kind. She told them that they should own their mistakes and fix them. Lily and Tom listened to Mom. They felt bad for what they did. They looked at each other and said sorry. "I am sorry, Lily. I should not have thrown your doll. Can we fix it?" Tom said. "I am sorry, Tom. I should not have bumped your car. Can we fix it?" Lily said. Mom smiled and hugged them. She said they were good children. She said they could fix their toys with some glue and tape. She said they could play with their toys on the rug again, but they had to be careful and gentle. Lily and Tom nodded and smiled. They thanked Mom and went to the living room. They fixed their toys and played with them on the rug. They also traded their toys and played with each other's toys. They were having fun, and they were friends again.
