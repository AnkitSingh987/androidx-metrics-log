Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Bunny and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop all day long. One day, Bobo saw a big frame in the woods. The frame was mighty and tall. Bobo wanted to hop over the frame. Bobo tried to hop over the frame, but he was too small. He tried again and again, but he could not do it. Bobo felt sad. Then, a kind bird flew by and saw Bobo. The bird wanted to help Bobo hop over the frame. The bird had a plan. The bird told Bobo to hop on her back. Bobo hopped on the bird's back, and the bird flew over the frame. Bobo was so happy! They did it together! The bird and Bobo became good friends. The unexpected thing that happened was that Bobo learned he could not do everything by himself. The moral value of the story is that sometimes we need help from our friends to do big things.

Tom and Sam were friends. They liked to play with marbles. They had many marbles of different colors and sizes. They liked to roll them, swap them, and make patterns with them. One day, Tom found a very big marble. It was blue and shiny. He was very happy. He showed it to Sam. "Look, Sam, I found a big marble. It is very pretty. Do you want to see it?" Sam looked at the big marble. He liked it too. He wanted to hold it. He asked Tom, "Can I hold your big marble, Tom? Please?" Tom was nice. He said, "Yes, you can hold it, Sam. But be careful. It is heavy and round. Do not drop it." Sam took the big marble from Tom. He felt it in his hand. It was smooth and cool. He rolled it on the floor. It made a soft sound. He smiled. He said, "Thank you, Tom. Your big marble is very nice. I like it a lot." Tom smiled too. He said, "You are welcome, Sam. I am glad you like it. Do you want to swap it for one of your marbles?" Sam thought for a moment. He looked at his marbles. He had a small marble that was red and sparkly. He liked it very much. He said, "Tom, I have a small marble that is red and sparkly. I like it very much. Do you want to swap it for your big marble?" Tom looked at the small marble. He liked it too. He said, "Yes, Sam, I want to swap it for my big marble. Your small marble is very pretty. I like it a lot." They swapped their marbles. They were both happy. They said, "Thank you, thank you, thank you!" They hugged each other. They played with their new marbles. They fit in their hands. They fit in their pockets. They fit in their hearts. They were good friends.

Tom is a big dog who likes to mark his things. He marks his bed, his toys, his bowl and his bone. He thinks that makes him popular with other dogs. He likes to bark and wag his tail when he sees them. One day, Tom sees a new dog in the park. The new dog is small and brown and has a red collar. Tom wants to say hello and mark the new dog. He runs to the new dog and sniffs him. The new dog does not like that. He growls and shows his teeth. "Go away!" the new dog says. "You are rude and mean. You cannot mark me. I am not your thing. I am a dog, like you." Tom is surprised and scared. He does not understand why the new dog is angry. He thinks he is being friendly and nice. He wants to be popular with the new dog. He tries to explain. "I am sorry," Tom says. "I do not want to be rude or mean. I want to be your friend. I like you. You are cute and new. Can we play together?" The new dog thinks for a moment. He sees that Tom is not bad, just silly. He decides to give him a chance. He says: "OK, we can be friends. But you have to be polite and respectful. You cannot mark me or my things. You have to ask before you sniff or touch. And you have to share your things with me, too. Do you understand?" Tom nods and wags his tail. He is happy that the new dog is not mad anymore. He says: "I understand. I will be polite and respectful. I will ask before I sniff or touch. And I will share my things with you, too. Do you want some of my meat? I have a big bone in my bed. It is very tasty and good." The new dog smiles and says: "Thank you. That is very kind of you. I would like some of your meat. Your bone sounds very tasty and good. Can we go to your bed and eat it together?" Tom and the new dog run to Tom's bed and eat the bone. They share the meat and lick each other's faces. They become good friends and play in the park. They are both popular with other dogs. They are happy and have fun.
