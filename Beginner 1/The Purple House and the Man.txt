Category: Beginner
Title: The Purple House and the Man

Once upon a time, in a big land, there was a purple house. In the purple house lived a kind man. The kind man loved to cook. He cooked yummy food every day. People in the land liked his food a lot. One day, the kind man made a big pot of soup. He wanted to share it with his friends. He called all his friends to his purple house. They came and sat around a big table. The kind man cooked more yummy food for them. Everyone ate the food and they were very happy. The kind man felt good because he made his friends happy with his cooking. The friends said, "Thank you!" and went home. The kind man smiled and went to sleep in his purple house.

One day, a boy named Tim found a mysterious frame in his yard. He saw that it was very old and dirty. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Mom, look at this!" he said. "What is it?" His mom looked at the frame and said, "I don't know, Tim. It looks very old. Maybe it's from a long time ago." Tim was excited to find out more about the mysterious frame. He put it in his room and went to bed. The next day, Tim saw that the frame had changed. It was now all brown and yucky. "Mom, the frame is rotting!" he cried. His mom came to see, and she was very surprised. "Maybe it's because it's so old," she said. "We should throw it away." Tim was sad, but he knew his mom was right. So they threw the frame away, and Tim learned that some things are best left in the past.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a magic fridge. The fridge was big and white. Tim was very happy. He opened the fridge and saw many yummy things inside. There were apples, ice cream, and cookies. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the fridge. She said, "Tim, where did this fridge come from?" Tim said, "I found it, Mom. It is a magic fridge!" His mom looked at the fridge and saw a big button. The button said "reverse." Tim's mom pushed the button. Suddenly, the fridge made a loud sound. The fridge turned into a fierce lion. The lion roared loudly. Tim and his mom were scared. But then, the lion spoke. It said, "Do not be scared. I am a friendly lion. I just wanted to play with you." Tim and his mom laughed. They played with the lion all day. The lion was not fierce anymore. It was a fun day for Tim and his mom.

Once there was a little boy. His name was Tim. One day, Tim wanted to explore the upstairs. But it was locked! Tim was very sad and asked his mom for help. His mom smiled and said, "If you are very obedient then I can unlock the door to upstairs." Tim was very excited and ran to his room to be obedient. After he was done being obedient, Tim's mom unlocked the door to the upstairs. Tim couldn't believe it! He was so happy. He raced into the upstairs and explored all of the new and exciting things he found. For the rest of the day, Tim played and enjoyed all the new toys he found upstairs. It was the best day ever!

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big scale that could weigh people. Lily wanted to try it, but Tom was scared. "Come on, Tom, it will be fun!" Lily said. "We can see how much we weigh and compare." Tom felt an urge to run away. He thought the scale was too high and loud. He did not want to stand on it. "No, Lily, I don't want to. It looks scary. What if it falls or makes a noise?" Tom said. Lily did not listen to Tom. She was curious and brave. She climbed on the scale and waited for the number to show. She was a little girl, so she did not weigh much. "Look, Tom, I weigh 30 pounds. That's not a lot. How much do you weigh?" Lily asked. Tom did not answer. He was too afraid to look at the scale. He closed his eyes and hoped Lily would come down. But Lily did not come down. She wanted to see how much more she could weigh. She picked up a big rock and held it in her arms. She thought it would make the number bigger. But she was wrong. The rock was too heavy for her. She lost her balance and fell off the scale. She hurt her knee and cried. Tom heard Lily cry and opened his eyes. He saw her on the ground, bleeding and sad. He felt sorry for her. He ran to her and helped her up. "Lily, are you okay? I'm sorry you fell. That was a bad idea. You should not have done that." Tom said. Lily nodded and hugged Tom. She was sorry too. She realized she was foolish and reckless. She should have listened to Tom and been careful. "Tom, you were right. The scale was scary. I should not have tried to weigh more. That was a silly thing to do. Thank you for being my friend." Lily said. Tom smiled and wiped her tears. He was glad Lily was okay. He learned to be more brave and Lily learned to be more wise. They walked to the nurse and got a band-aid. They decided to play something else in the park. They learned a valuable lesson that day.
