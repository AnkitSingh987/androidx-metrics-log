Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Blanket

One day, a small girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. She brought her favorite blanket with her. The blanket was soft and warm. Lily liked to sit on it and watch the birds in the sky. While they were at the park, Lily saw a little boy crying. She walked up to him and asked, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Lily thought for a moment and had an idea. Lily said, "I will help you look for your toy. Maybe it is hiding under my blanket!" They both looked under the blanket and found the toy. The little boy was so happy. They played together all day and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot loved to walk and play outside. One day, he went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. As Spot walked, he saw a tall stand with pretty balloons. He wanted to get a balloon, but he was too small to reach them. He looked around and saw a nice girl. Spot walked to the girl and wagged his tail. The girl saw Spot and thought he was very cute. She picked him up and helped him get a balloon from the stand. Spot was so happy! They walked together in the park, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a nice day at the park with his mom and dad. They played, laughed, and had a lot of fun together. After playing, Tim felt hungry. He looked at his mom and dad and said, "I want dessert!" His mom smiled and said, "Let's find a dessert for you, Tim." They walked and walked until they found a small shop. Inside the shop, there were many desserts. Tim wondered which one to pick. Tim saw a big, round dessert with red and white on top. He pointed at it and said, "I want that one!" His mom and dad bought the dessert for him. They all sat down and shared the dessert. Tim thought it was the nicest dessert he ever had.

Once there was a boy named Ben. Ben was curious, but he struggled to understand words. One day he saw an old saw in his Grandad's shed. It was a special purple saw! Ben wanted to pick it up but he knew not to. Beside the saw, Ben found a purple hammer with a spiked head. He tried pounding it on the shed wall, but it made a loud noise that made him suffer. He knew he shouldn't make too much noise, so he put it down. Finally, Ben decided to take the purple saw outside. He rolled it along the grass and watched as the bright purple sparkled in the sun. Ben was so happy that he jumped for joy, not suffering at all!

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. Lily has a bike with pedals. Ben has a ball. They share their toys and have fun. One day, a new girl comes to the park. Her name is Mia. She has a bike with pedals too. But her bike is pink and shiny. It has a basket with flowers and a bell that rings. Mia thinks her bike is the best. She does not want to share it with anyone. "Look at my bike!" Mia says to Lily and Ben. "It is so pretty and fast. Your bike is old and boring. And your ball is too small. You can't play with me. I am too popular for you." Lily and Ben feel sad. They do not like Mia. She is mean and rude. They do not want to play with her. They want to play with each other. "Let's go to the swings!" Lily says to Ben. "We can skip and laugh and have fun. We don't need Mia. We have each other." "Okay!" Ben says to Lily. "You are my best friend. I like your bike and your ball. They are fun and nice. You are fun and nice too." Lily and Ben skip to the swings. They swing high and low. They laugh and sing. They are happy. They do not care about Mia. They do not need to be popular. They are friends.
