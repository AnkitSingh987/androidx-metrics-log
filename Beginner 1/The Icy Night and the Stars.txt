Category: Beginner
Title: The Icy Night and the Stars

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an icy night. It was very cold, and all the people stayed inside their warm homes. The trees and the ground were all white with frost. In this town, there was a kind girl named Lily. She had a little brother named Max. Max was scared of the dark night. Lily wanted to help Max, so she had an idea to prevent him from being scared. Lily found some pretty, shiny stars and put them on Max's window. Now, when Max looked outside during the dark, icy night, he saw the pretty stars and was not scared anymore. Lily and Max were both happy, and they slept well every night.

Sam and Tom are brothers. They like to race with their toy cars. Sam has a red car and Tom has a blue car. They race on the floor, on the table, on the sofa. They make loud noises with their mouths. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep! One day, they race on the stairs. Sam is ahead of Tom. He is happy. He thinks he will win. But Tom is not happy. He wants to win. He pushes Sam's car with his hand. Sam's car falls down the stairs. Sam is sad. He cries. "Tom, you are bad! You broke my car!" Sam says. "No, I am not bad! You are slow! I am fast!" Tom says. They start to fight. They hit each other with their fists. They yell and scream. Mom hears them. She is in the kitchen. She has a cell. She talks to Dad. She is deaf. She can't hear well. She uses her eyes to read Dad's words on the cell. She sees Sam and Tom fighting on the cell. She is angry. She runs to the stairs. "Sam, Tom, stop! Stop fighting!" Mom says. She grabs them by their arms. She takes them to their room. She puts them in time out. She says they can't race with their cars for a week. She says they have to say sorry to each other. She says they have to share and be nice. Sam and Tom are sorry. They hug each other. They say sorry to Mom. They say they love each other. They say they love Mom. They say they will not fight again. Mom smiles. She hugs them. She says she loves them. She says they can race with their cars again, but not on the stairs. She says they have to be careful and safe. She says they have to be quiet and respectful. Sam and Tom nod. They say yes, Mom. They go to their room. They race with their cars. They make soft noises with their mouths. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep!

Once upon a time, there was a pigeon named Pete. Pete lived in a big tree in a park. He had many friends. Pete was a perfect pigeon. He was kind and loved to play with his friends. One sunny day, Pete was playing with his friend, a little girl named Sally. Sally had a toy that could spray water. She said, "Pete, let's play with my water toy!" Pete was happy and nodded his head. Sally started to spray water on the grass. Pete loved it! He jumped and danced in the water. They both laughed and played in the sun. It was a perfect day for Pete the pigeon and Sally.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to help her mom at home. One day, Lucy's mom asked her to help with the laundry. Lucy was happy to help. She put the dirty clothes in the big machine and watched them spin around. After the laundry was done, Lucy's mom said they could bake a cake. They mixed the ingredients in a big bowl. Lucy liked to stir the mix with a big spoon. They put the mix in the oven and waited for it to bake. The smell filled the room and made Lucy's tummy rumble. When the cake was ready, Lucy's mom put some expensive fruits on top. Lucy thought the fruits were very pretty. They sat down and ate the cake together. It was the best cake Lucy had ever tasted. She was proud that she helped her mom with the laundry and baking the cake.
