Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Treasure

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig in the yard. One day, he found a big box. It was a treasure chest! Spot was very happy. Spot wanted to show his friends the treasure. He wanted to restore the chest to make it look new. He found paint and a brush. Spot painted the chest with bright colors. It was beautiful. Spot showed his friends the treasure chest. They were excited. But, when they opened it, they found toys inside! The treasure was not gold or jewels, but toys for Spot and his friends to play with. They all laughed and played together. The unexpected treasure made them happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to cook with her mom. They would make cookies, cakes, and pies. Mia's favorite thing to cook was a delicate cake. One day, Mia's mom said, "Mia, let's cook a cake together!" Mia was very happy. She put on her apron and got ready to help. They mixed the flour, sugar, and eggs in a big bowl. Mia made a motion with her hand to stir the batter. Mia's mom said, "Be gentle, Mia. The cake is delicate. We don't want to break it." Mia listened and stirred the batter softly. They poured the batter into a pan and put it in the oven. Mia and her mom waited for the cake to cook. When the cake was ready, they took it out of the oven. It smelled so good! Mia and her mom put icing on the cake and added some pretty decorations. They shared the cake with their family, and everyone loved it. Mia was very proud of her delicate cake.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty little dog named Max. Max loved to play in the mud and get dirty. One day, Max saw a big light in the sky. It was the sun. Max wanted to go to the light, but he did not know how. Max met a big bird named Bill. Bill saw Max looking at the light. Bill said, "Do you want to go to the light?" Max said, "Yes, I want to unite with the light." Bill said, "I can help you, but first we need to clean you up." Max and Bill went to the river. Max jumped in the water and got clean. Then, Bill took Max high up in the sky to unite with the light. Max was happy to be with the light, and he thanked Bill for helping him. From that day on, Max and Bill were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was excited because he had a new picture to show his best friend, Sam. The picture was of a big, red ball. One sunny day, Tim and Sam were playing outside. Tim ran to get his picture, but when he came back, he saw Sam was playing with a real big red ball. Tim was sad because he thought Sam would not want to see his picture now. Tim showed his picture to Sam anyway. Sam looked at the picture and then at the real ball. He said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't know you had a picture of a big red ball. I forgive you for being sad." Then, they both played with the real ball and the picture of the ball together. And they were both happy.

Tom and Sue were playing with a ball in the park. They liked to pass the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they missed and the ball rolled away. Then they ran after it and laughed. But one day, a big dog came to the park. He was nosy and wanted to play too. He saw the ball and ran to get it. He picked it up with his mouth and ran away. Tom and Sue were sad and angry. They shouted at the dog. "Hey, dog, give us our ball back! That's ours, not yours!" But the dog did not listen. He thought it was a game. He ran around the park with the ball, wagging his tail. He did not see the big tree in his way. He bumped into it and dropped the ball. The ball hit a branch and got stuck in a knot. The dog could not reach it. He barked and jumped, but it was too high. Tom and Sue saw what happened. They ran to the tree and looked at the ball. It was in a tight knot and they could not get it out. They tried to pull and push, but it did not move. They were sad and angry. They looked at the dog. "Bad dog, you made us lose our ball! How can we get it back?" But the dog did not understand. He was sorry and wanted to be friends. He licked their faces and wagged his tail. He thought they were playing too. He did not know he had done something wrong. He just wanted to have fun.
