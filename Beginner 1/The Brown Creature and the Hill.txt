Category: Beginner
Title: The Brown Creature and the Hill

Once upon a time there lived a small brown creature in a far away nation. The creature wanted to go on an adventure, so it gathered its courage and began to climb a big hill. Before it knew it, the creature had made it to the top, but the view was so sharp that it made it blink. It saw the nation spread out like a huge blanket of green and brown. The creature smiled and it felt happy knowing that it had been brave enough to climb the hill. Then, it went back down the hill and went home with a big smile on its face. The end.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big pile of junk near the swings. They run to the junk and look for fun things. "Look, Lily, a hat!" Ben says. He puts a hat on his head. The hat is too big and covers his eyes. Lily laughs. "Look, Ben, a wig!" Lily says. She finds a wig in the junk. The wig is long and red. She puts the wig on her head. Ben laughs. They pretend to be different people with the hat and the wig. They make funny voices and jokes. They have a lot of fun. A man walks by the park. He sees Lily and Ben playing with the junk. He is bald. He has no hair on his head. He feels sad. He stops and watches them. He sees how happy they are. He hears how they laugh. He thinks they are cute. He walks to them and says hello. He smiles. "Hello, kids. What are you doing?" he asks. "We are playing with the junk. We found a hat and a wig. Do you want to play with us?" Lily asks. The man thinks for a moment. He says yes. He takes the hat from Ben and puts it on his head. He takes the wig from Lily and puts it on his head. He looks silly. Lily and Ben giggle. They say he looks funny. They give him a hug. They invite him to join their game. The man feels happy. He plays with them. He makes funny voices and jokes. He has a lot of fun. They all smile.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Spot. Spot could find things very well. One day, Spot's friend, a little boy named Tim, lost his toy diamond. Tim was very sad. Spot said to Tim, "Don't worry, I will search for your diamond." So, Spot and Tim went to look for the toy diamond together. They looked in the house, in the yard, and under the trees. Finally, Spot found the toy diamond under a big rock. He barked and showed Tim where it was. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Spot! You are the best dog ever!" And they played with the toy diamond all day long.

Sam and Mia were playing with their blocks. They liked to build tall towers and houses with windows and doors. They had many colors of blocks: red, blue, yellow, green, and more. "Look at my tower, Mia!" Sam said. "It is very high and strong. It has a nice view of the garden." Mia looked at Sam's tower. She felt a little jealous. Her tower was not as high or strong as Sam's. She wanted to have a nice view too. She had a bitter feeling in her heart. She pretended to be happy for Sam. "Wow, Sam, your tower is great!" she said. "Can I see the view?" Sam smiled and nodded. He moved aside to let Mia see the view. But Mia did not really want to see the view. She wanted to break Sam's tower. She pushed it hard with her hand. CRASH! The tower fell down. The blocks scattered everywhere. Sam was shocked and sad. He looked at Mia with angry eyes. "Why did you do that, Mia?" he asked. "You broke my tower! That was not nice!" Mia felt sorry for what she did. She saw Sam's tears and heard his voice. She realized she had hurt his feelings. She wished she had not been bitter and jealous. "I'm sorry, Sam," she said. "I was not nice. I was bitter and jealous. I wanted to have a nice view too. But that was no reason to break your tower. Can you forgive me?" Sam looked at Mia. He saw her tears and heard her voice. He realized she was sorry for what she did. He wished she had not been bitter and jealous. But he also remembered that she was his friend. "It's okay, Mia," he said. "I forgive you. You are my friend. We can share the view. And we can build another tower together. Do you want to do that?" Mia nodded and smiled. She hugged Sam and said, "Thank you, Sam. You are my friend too. Yes, I want to build another tower with you. And I want to share the view with you." They picked up the blocks and started to build again. They used all the colors and made a bigger and better tower. They had a nice view of the garden. And they had a nice feeling in their hearts.
