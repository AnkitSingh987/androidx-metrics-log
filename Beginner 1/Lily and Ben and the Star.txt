Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Star

Lily and Ben were playing with blocks in the living room. They liked to build towers and houses and cars with the blocks. They had many colors and shapes of blocks to choose from. But then Ben saw a block that he wanted very much. It was a big red block with a star on it. He reached for it, but Lily grabbed it first. "No, Ben, this is my block!" Lily said. "I found it first. You have many other blocks to play with." Ben was upset. He wanted the big red block with the star. He tried to take it from Lily, but she held it tight. He pulled and pulled, but she did not let go. "Give it to me, Lily!" Ben said. "It is not fair. You have too many blocks. I want this one." Lily did not listen. She liked the big red block with the star. She did not want to share it with Ben. She pushed him away with her foot. "Go away, Ben!" Lily said. "This is my block. You are being mean. I don't want to play with you." Ben was very upset. He felt a big tear roll down his cheek. He did not like to fight with Lily. He did not like to be pushed with her foot. He did not like to be mean or unfair. He wanted to play with the blocks and have fun. He looked at Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I don't want to make you sad. Can we share the block? Can we play together?" Lily looked at Ben and saw his tear. She felt sorry too. She did not want to make him sad. She did not want to be pushed or mean or unfair. She wanted to play with the blocks and have fun. She smiled and said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I don't want to make you sad. Yes, we can share the block. Yes, we can play together." They hugged and said sorry. They put the big red block with the star in the middle of their tower. They played with the blocks and had fun. They were happy and friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a very attractive rocket. It was red and shiny. It liked to seat in the sky and fly high. The rocket had many friends who liked to watch it go up and up. One day, the rocket went up, up, up in the sky. But then, something unexpected happened. The rocket did not go up anymore. It started to go down, down, down. All its friends were very surprised. The rocket landed in a big tree. It was not hurt, but it could not fly anymore. It was sad. But then, a little bird came and sat on the rocket. The bird said, "Don't be sad! You can still seat in the sky with me!" And the rocket was happy again, because it made a new friend.

Once upon a time there was an old pot. It was hidden in the tall grass at the edge of the woods. One day, a rabbit saw the pot and wanted to explore. He hopped inside. The pot was very old and seemed to know just how to keep the rabbit in. He tried to jump out but the walls of the pot were too high. He was desperate to escape but he could not find a way. Finally, the rabbit thought of a plan. He had heard stories of animals who worked together to get free. So, he called out to the birds and squirrels and asked them to help him. The birds and squirrels listened and rushed to the pot. Working together, they started chipping away at the walls of the pot until there was a big enough gap for the rabbit to escape. The rabbit was so relieved and thanked the birds and squirrels for their help. From that day on, the rabbit remembered the importance of working together and helping one another.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. Tom wants to go on the slide. Sue is scared. "Come on, Sue, it is fun!" Tom says. "You just sit and slide down. It is easy!" "I don't know, Tom. It looks high and fast. What if I fall?" Sue says. "You won't fall, Sue. I will go with you. Just close your eyes and hold my hand. Then you won't be scared." Tom says. Sue thinks for a moment. She wants to be brave like Tom. She nods and takes his hand. They climb up the stairs to the top of the slide. "Are you ready, Sue?" Tom asks. "Yes, Tom. I am ready." Sue says. She closes her eyes and squeezes his hand. "Okay, Sue. Here we go!" Tom says. He pushes them off the slide. They slide down very fast. Sue feels the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. She thinks it is not so bad. She opens her eyes and smiles. But then, something bad happens. There is a big hole in the slide. Tom and Sue fall through the hole. They scream and cry. They land on the hard ground. They are hurt and bleeding. No one is there to help them. They wish they never went on the slide.
