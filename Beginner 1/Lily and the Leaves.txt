Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Leaves

Once upon a time, in a fair sunny day, a little girl named Lily wanted to make a salad for her mom. She went to the garden and picked some big green leaves. Lily also found some red tomatoes and a yellow pepper. She was very happy to find all the colors for her salad. Lily brought all the things to the kitchen. She washed the leaves, tomatoes, and pepper with water. Then, she put them on a big plate to dry. Lily waited for them to get dry so she could make her salad. When the leaves, tomatoes, and pepper were dry, Lily put them in a big bowl. She mixed them all up to make her salad. Lily called her mom to come and see. Her mom was very proud of her. They both sat down and ate the yummy salad together. And they lived happily ever after.

Tim and Sue like to act. They act like animals, like people, like things. They have fun and make noise. One day, they find a big loop in the yard. It is made of rope and hangs from a tree. "Look, a loop!" Tim says. "Let's act like we are in a circus!" "OK!" Sue says. "I will be a lion and you will be a clown!" They run to the loop and take turns. Tim puts his head inside the loop and makes funny faces. Sue roars and pretends to bite him. They laugh and clap. But then, Tim has an idea. He wants to act like he can fly. He grabs the loop and swings on it. He goes high and low, fast and slow. He feels like a bird. "Look at me, Sue!" he shouts. "I can fly!" Sue watches him and smiles. She is glad that he is happy. But she also sees something that he does not. She sees a big rock under the loop. She sees that Tim is going too fast and too high. She sees that he will hit the rock if he does not stop. "Tim, stop!" she yells. "There is a rock! You will get hurt!" But Tim does not hear her. He is too busy flying. He does not see the rock. He does not see the danger. He only sees the sky. He swings one more time. He goes very high. He lets go of the loop. He flies in the air. He feels free. But then, he falls. He falls fast. He falls hard. He falls on the rock. He cries. He cries loud. He cries a lot. Sue runs to him. She hugs him. She says sorry. She says she loves him. Tim stops crying. He hugs her back. He says sorry too. He says he loves her. They go inside. They get a band-aid. They get a kiss. They get a hug. They are still glad. They are still friends. They still like to act. But they do not act like they can fly anymore. They know that flying is not for them. They know that flying is for birds. They know that they are not birds. They are Tim and Sue. And they are happy.

Once there were two friends. One was named Mandy, and the other was named Tony. They were both three years old. They had lots of fun together. One day, Mandy and Tony had an idea. They wanted to explore a space! They started to walk until they found a strange place. The place was a little dirty and filthy. Mandy and Tony were curious and wanted to explore the filthy place. But it was too dark in there to see very much. So, they decided to count how many steps they could take. They were surprised to find that they had taken one hundred steps! The two friends were delighted. The filthy space was now filled with fun and adventure. They both agreed that it was the most exciting place they had ever seen. Mandy and Tony had so much fun. They were now counting all sorts of things in their filthy space. The two friends enjoyed their adventure very much.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a small hill. Many kids loved to play on this hill. They would run, jump, and roll on the soft grass. The hill was a happy place for all the kids in the town. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the hill to play. He saw a ball at the top of the hill. Tim wanted to get the ball, so he started to climb. He put one foot in front of the other, and soon he was at the top. When Tim got the ball, he saw his friend, Sue, at the bottom of the hill. He waved and called her to come up. Together, they played with the ball on the hill. They had so much fun, and the ordinary hill became their favorite place to play.
