Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Tall Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a tall boy named Tim. Tim had a little dog named Spot. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park to play. They saw a big tree, and Tim wanted to climb it. Spot barked, "Woof, woof!" Tim said, "Don't worry, Spot. I will be okay." Tim climbed the tree, but he got stuck. He was scared and cried for help. Spot wanted to comfort Tim, but he was too small to help. Spot ran to find help, and he saw a man with a big, yummy steak. Spot thought, "If I give the steak to Tim, he will be happy and not scared anymore." Spot took the steak and ran back to the tree. He barked, "Woof, woof!" to show Tim the steak. But Tim was still scared and stuck in the tree. He could not get the steak. The man saw Spot with his steak and got very mad. He yelled at Spot and took the steak back. Tim was still stuck in the tree, and now Spot was sad too. They both wished they had never gone to the park that day.

One day, Tom went to play outside. He put on his red cap. The cap was too big. Tom saw a big hill. He wanted to roll down the hill. "This will be fun," he said. Tom started to roll down the hill. His cap fell off. Tom did not see it. A pale dog ran up to the cap. The dog picked up the cap in its mouth. Tom kept rolling down the hill. At the bottom of the hill, Tom stopped rolling. He looked for his cap. He saw the pale dog with his cap. "Hey, that's my cap!" Tom said. The dog wagged its tail and gave the cap back to Tom. They became friends and played together all day.

Once there was a family of four. dad, mom, and two small children. One day, dad took the family to a special surprise. It was in a smelly room, and there were lots of bottles filled with wine. The children were so excited and ran all around the room. Mom smiled at dad and said "What a surprise! I never knew you liked wine!" Dad laughed and told the children not to touch anything. Then he handed each of them a strawberry lolly. They both said "Thank you!" and began to lick their lollies. After a while dad said it was time to go home. The children were sad until dad said "Tonight we will have a special surprise for dinner." Then they clapped and cheered! When they arrived home, the children saw a huge cake on the table. The cake smelled so yummy, and they knew it was a special surprise. They shared the cake with everyone and everyone loved it. The children had a great time and thanked dad for his special surprise. They all agreed that wine is a smelly but wonderful surprise.

One day, a grumpy cat saw a big dog. The dog was happy to see the cat. He wanted to play. The cat did not like the sight of the dog. The cat was scared. The dog said, "Hello, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat did not reply. The cat just ran away. The dog was sad. He wanted a friend. The cat went home. He was still grumpy. The cat saw a big bird. The bird was mean. The bird took the cat's food. The cat was sad. He needed help. The cat went to the dog. The cat said, "Help me, dog. The bird took my food." The dog was still sad. He did not help. The cat was more sad. The moral of the story is: be nice to others. If you are not nice, they will not help you when you need it.

Tom and Lily were playing in the kitchen. They wanted to make a cake for Mom. They had flour, eggs, milk and sugar. They mixed them in a big bowl. They saw a whip on the counter. It looked like a fun toy. "Let's whip the cake!" Tom said. He grabbed the whip and swung it in the air. It made a loud noise. Lily laughed. She took the whip from Tom and whipped the cake too. The cake batter flew everywhere. It splashed on the walls, the floor, the table and their faces. Mom came into the kitchen. She saw the mess. She was not happy. She said, "What are you doing? You made a big mess. You did not help. You made trouble." Tom and Lily felt bad. They did not want to make Mom sad. They said, "We are sorry, Mom. We wanted to make a cake for you. We thought the whip was a toy. We did not know it would make a mess." Mom sighed. She said, "I know you wanted to make a cake for me. That was very sweet. But the whip is not a toy. It is a tool. You use it to make the cake fluffy and smooth. You have to be careful with it. Next time, ask me before you use it." Tom and Lily nodded. They said, "OK, Mom. We will ask you next time. Can we help you clean the mess?" Mom smiled. She said, "Yes, you can help me clean the mess. That would be very nice. Thank you, Tom and Lily. I love you." Tom and Lily hugged Mom. They helped her wipe the batter off the walls, the floor, the table and their faces. They threw away the ruined cake. They made a new cake with Mom. They used the whip the right way. The cake came out fluffy and smooth. They decorated it with icing and sprinkles. They gave it to Mom. Mom was very happy. She said, "This is the best cake ever. You are very good helpers. I am very proud of you." Tom and Lily were happy too. They said, "We are proud of you too, Mom. You are the best mom ever. We love you." They ate the cake together. It was yummy. They had a lot of fun. They learned a new word: whip. They learned a new lesson: ask before you use. They learned a new feeling: proud.
