Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ring

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an old bicycle. He loved to ride it up and down the street. The bicycle was red and had a bell that went "ring ring." One day, Tim asked his mom if he could ride his bicycle to the park. His mom said, "I will permit you to go, but you must be careful." Tim was very happy and put on his helmet. Tim rode his bicycle to the park. He saw his friends and they played together. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim rode his old bicycle back to his house. His mom was happy that he was safe.

---

Lily and Tom were playing in the backyard. They had a big box and some sticks. They wanted to make a raft. They put the sticks on the box and tied them with a rope. They were very proud of their raft. "Let's go to the pond and sail our raft!" Lily said. "Okay!" Tom said. They carried their raft to the pond. They put it on the water and climbed on it. They used a stick to push the raft. They had fun sailing on the pond. But then they saw a big duck. The duck was hungry. It saw the raft and thought it was food. It swam to the raft and bit it. The raft broke and the sticks fell off. The duck ate the sticks. "Hey, stop!" Lily shouted. "That's our raft!" "Quack, quack!" the duck said. It did not care. It was still hungry. Lily and Tom were scared and sad. They jumped off the raft and swam to the shore. They were wet and cold. They ran to their house. "Mom, mom!" they cried. "A duck destroyed our raft!" "Oh, no!" their mom said. "I'm sorry, my dears. Come here, I'll dry you and hug you. Maybe we can make another raft later." Lily and Tom nodded. They hugged their mom. They were still sad, but they felt better. They hoped the duck was not hungry anymore.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a pretty frame. She wanted to put a picture of her family in it. She showed it to her friend, Tom. Tom was deaf, so he could not hear what Mia was saying. Mia knew sign language, so she could talk to Tom. Mia said, "I want to put a picture in this frame. Can you help me?" Tom said, "Yes, I can help you, Mia." They looked for a picture to put in the frame, but they could not find one that was the right size. They tried to make a new picture, but it was not easy. Mia and Tom started to get upset. They wanted the frame to have a picture, but they could not make one. Then, Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's ask our teacher for help." They went to their teacher, and she helped them make a perfect picture for the frame. Mia and Tom were happy, and they put the picture in the frame together.

Ben and Mia were going to play in the snow. They put on their coats, hats, boots and gloves. They were very enthusiastic. They wanted to make a snowman and have a snowball fight. "Come on, Mia, let's go outside!" Ben said. "OK, Ben, let's go!" Mia said. They ran out of the door and into the yard. The snow was white and soft. They rolled big balls of snow and stacked them up. They gave the snowman a carrot nose, a scarf and a hat. They laughed and had fun. Then they decided to have a snowball fight. They hid behind trees and bushes and threw snowballs at each other. They dodged and ducked and giggled. "Got you, Ben!" Mia shouted. "Missed me, Mia!" Ben shouted back. They were having so much fun that they did not notice that one of Mia's gloves had fallen off. It was lying on the ground, covered by snow. After a while, they got cold and tired. They decided to go back inside. "Let's go, Mia, it's time to go in!" Ben said. "OK, Ben, let's go!" Mia said. They ran back to the door and took off their coats, hats, boots and gloves. They hung them on the hooks and went to the kitchen. Their mom had made hot chocolate for them. "Did you have fun, kids?" Mom asked. "Yes, Mom, we had a lot of fun!" Ben and Mia said. They drank their hot chocolate and told Mom about their snowman and their snowball fight. Then Mia noticed something. "Mom, where is my other glove?" Mia asked. She looked at her hands and saw that she only had one glove. "Oh no, I think I left it outside!" Mia said. She felt sad. She liked her gloves. They were pink and warm and had stars on them. "Don't worry, Mia, we can go and look for it later," Mom said. "Maybe the snowman has it," Ben said. He tried to make Mia smile. "Maybe he does," Mia said. She smiled a little. She hoped that her glove was still there. She hoped that the snowman liked it. She hoped that she could find it and wear it again.
