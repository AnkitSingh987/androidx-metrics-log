Category: Beginner
Title: Max and the Park

One day, a chubby dog named Max was playing in the park. He liked to run and play with his friends. Max had a blue collar that his owner put on him every day. He was very happy when he wore his collar. Max saw his friend, a cat named Lily. Lily was sitting under a tree. Max ran to her and said, "Hi, Lily! Do you want to play with me?" Lily looked at Max and said, "Yes, Max! Let's play together!" They started to play a game of pass with a red ball. Max would throw the ball to Lily, and Lily would pass it back to Max. They had so much fun playing together. After a while, they got tired and sat down under the tree. Max and Lily were happy to be friends and play in the park together.

One day, a boy found a box. The box was big and red. He wanted to keep the box, so he took it home. The boy was very happy with his new box. The boy showed the box to his friend. His friend said, "I like your box. Can I keep it too?" The boy did not want to share the box. He said, "No, this is my box. You can't keep it." The boy's friend was sad and went away. The boy played with the box all day. But one day, the box hurt the boy. He cried and cried. The boy's friend heard him cry and came to see what happened. The boy's friend saw the hurt boy and the box. He said, "I told you to share the box. Now it hurt you." The boy was still sad and hurt. He did not have a happy ending because he did not share the box.

Tom and Mia were playing with their toys in the living room. Tom had a red car and Mia had a yellow ball. They were having fun, making noises and rolling their toys on the floor. Suddenly, Mia's ball disappeared. She looked around, but she could not see it. She felt nervous. She asked Tom, "Where is my ball?" Tom shrugged. He did not know. He said, "Maybe it rolled under the sofa. Let's look." They moved the sofa, but the ball was not there. They looked under the table, behind the curtains, and in the closet. They could not find the ball anywhere. Mia started to cry. She said, "I want my ball. It was my favorite object." Tom felt sorry for Mia. He wanted to help her. He thought hard. He remembered that sometimes their dog, Spot, liked to play with balls. He said, "Maybe Spot took your ball. Let's go to the backyard and see." They ran to the backyard, and there they saw Spot. He was lying on the grass, with Mia's ball in his mouth. He wagged his tail when he saw them. Tom said, "Spot, give us the ball. It is Mia's, not yours." Spot dropped the ball and licked their faces. He was happy to see them. Mia picked up the ball and hugged it. She said, "Thank you, Tom. Thank you, Spot. You found my ball. I am not nervous anymore." Tom smiled. He said, "You're welcome, Mia. I am glad you have your ball back. Can we play together again?" Mia nodded. She said, "Yes, let's play. But this time, let's keep an eye on Spot. He might make another object disappear."

Once upon a time, there was a big bull named Bob. Bob was very adventurous. He liked to go to new places and see new things. One day, Bob saw a boat near the water. He thought, "I want to sail on that boat!" Bob went to the boat and met a kind man. The man said, "Hello, bull! My name is Sam. Do you want to sail with me?" Bob was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to sail! Let's go!" So, Bob and Sam got on the boat and started to sail. They sailed to a beautiful island with many trees and flowers. Bob and Sam played on the island all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. When the sun went down, they sailed back home. Bob was very tired but happy. He had a great adventure with his new friend Sam. And from that day on, Bob and Sam sailed together many more times, having lots of fun and making many memories.
