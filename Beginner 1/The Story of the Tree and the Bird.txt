Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, high tree. In the tree, there was a small cage. In the cage, there lived a pretty bird named Blue. Blue was sad because he wanted to fly and play with his friends. One day, a kind girl named Lily saw the cage. She said, "Oh no! Blue, you are in a cage! I will help you get out." Lily tried to reach the cage, but it was too high. She asked her tall friend, Tom, to help her. Tom reached up and opened the cage door. Blue was so happy! He flew out of the cage and said, "Thank you, Lily and Tom!" Then, Blue gave Lily a little kiss on her cheek. Lily giggled and said, "You're welcome, Blue! Go play with your friends now." Blue flew away and played with his friends, while Lily and Tom waved goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was not very old, but he wanted to be big like his dad. His dad was tall and strong, and Tim wished he could be just like him. One day, Tim met an old lady in the park. The old lady looked sad, so Tim asked, "Why are you sad?" The old lady said, "I am old, and I miss being young like you. I want to be young again." Tim thought about it and said, "I want to be old like my dad, and you want to be young like me. Let's trade places!" They agreed, and suddenly, Tim was old and the old lady was young. But they both realized they were not happy with the change. Tim missed playing with his friends, and the old lady missed her family. They decided to trade back and be happy with who they were. Tim learned that it was better to enjoy his youth, and the old lady learned to appreciate her age. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He was very curious and liked to explore his neighborhood. One day Jack went outside to play and he saw a big tree with a strange round fruit on it. He asked his Mom, "What's that fruit called?" His Mom smiled and said, "It's called an apple. Would you like to try it?" Jack nodded and his Mom helped him pick one from the tree. She cautioned him to be careful as he held it, then she gave him a stick to poke the apple. Jack poked gently and the apple cracked open. He pulled the split halves apart and he saw the result inside. It was a beautiful, healthy, red fruit with white juice. Jack's mouth started to water! He took a bite and the sweet flavor filled his mouth. "Tasty!" he exclaimed, smiling from ear to ear. His Mom said with a big smile, "See what healthy foods can do!" Jack was so happy he ate the entire apple!

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys and their dog, Rex. Rex is a big, white dog. He is very friendly and likes to run and jump. One day, Ben and Sam are playing in the yard with Rex. They see their dad coming home from work. He has a bag in his hand. He smiles and says, "Hello, boys. I have a surprise for you. Guess what's in the bag?" Ben and Sam run to their dad and try to look in the bag. They see something red and brown. They smell something good. They say, "What is it, dad?" Dad says, "It's steak. I bought some steak for dinner. Do you like steak?" Ben and Sam nod and say, "Yes, we like steak. Steak is yummy. Can we have some now?" Dad says, "Not now, boys. We have to cook the steak first. We have to raise the fire and put the steak on the grill. Then we have to wait until it's done. Do you want to help me?" Ben and Sam say, "Yes, we want to help. Can we raise the fire, dad?" Dad says, "Sure, you can help me raise the fire. But be careful, boys. Fire is hot. Don't touch the fire. Only touch the stick. The stick is for raising the fire. Can you do that?" Ben and Sam say, "Yes, we can do that. We can raise the fire with the stick. We are big boys. We can help dad." Dad says, "That's right, you are big boys. And you are good boys. I'm proud of you. Come on, let's go to the grill and raise the fire. Then we can cook the steak and eat it with Rex. Rex likes steak, too. Don't you, Rex?" Rex barks and wags his tail. He likes steak, too. He follows dad and the boys to the grill. They raise the fire and cook the steak. They have a nice dinner together. They are happy.
