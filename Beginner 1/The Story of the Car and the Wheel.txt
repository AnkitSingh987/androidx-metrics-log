Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Car and the Wheel

One day, a big red car and a small blue car were on the road. The big red car had a poor wheel. The small blue car saw the big red car and they stopped to talk. The small blue car said, "Let's discuss your poor wheel, big red car. I want to help you." The big red car was happy and they talked about the wheel. As they were talking, a big green truck came by. The truck had a new wheel! The big green truck gave the new wheel to the big red car. The big red car was surprised and said, "Thank you, big green truck!" Now, the big red car, the small blue car, and the big green truck were all happy and they drove away together.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his best friend, a big dog named Max, were playing in the park. They were having a lot of fun running and jumping. Tim had a heavy bag with him. Inside the bag, there was a tape that Tim's mom gave him. The tape had his favorite songs on it. As they played, Tim put the heavy bag down under a tree. They played for a long time and got very tired. When it was time to go home, Tim forgot the heavy bag with the tape under the tree. They went home, and Tim's mom asked, "Where is the tape I gave you?" Tim said, "Oh no! I left it in the park!" Tim and Max ran back to the park to find it. When they got to the park, the heavy bag was not under the tree. Tim was very sad and said, "I miss my tape." Just then, a little girl came up to them with the heavy bag. She said, "I found this under the tree. Is it yours?" Tim was so happy and thanked the little girl. They all became friends and played together. The moral of the story is always to remember your things and be kind to others.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars on the floor. They liked to make them zoom and crash and honk. Lily had a red car and Ben had a blue car. They pretended they were driving on a big road with many other cars. "Look out, Lily!" Ben shouted. "There is a terrible patch on the road. It is brown and sticky and smells bad. You have to reverse your car or you will get stuck." Lily tried to reverse her car, but it was too late. Her car rolled over the patch and got covered in mud. She felt sad and angry. She did not like her car to be dirty. "Ha ha, Lily!" Ben laughed. "Your car is so messy. My car is clean and shiny. I am the best driver." Lily did not like Ben's laugh. She thought he was mean and rude. She wanted to make him stop. She had an idea. She picked up her car and threw it at Ben's car. Her car hit Ben's car hard and made it fly off the floor. Ben's car landed on the sofa and broke a lamp. "Hey, Lily!" Ben cried. "What did you do? You broke my car and the lamp. That was not nice. You are a bad driver and a bad friend." Lily and Ben both started to cry. They heard their mom coming. They knew they were in trouble. They wished they had not played with their cars on the floor. They wished they had been kind and careful. They learned a lesson. They said sorry to each other and hugged. They hoped their mom would not be too mad.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to record his voice. One day, he went to the park. The park had a big tree. Tom liked to climb the tree. The tree had many leaves. The leaves were mild and soft. Tom saw his friend, Sam. Sam was mad. Sam wanted to climb the tree too. But Sam was not good at climbing. Tom and Sam had a fight. They both wanted to be at the top of the tree. Tom climbed up the tree. Sam tried to follow him. But Sam was not careful. Sam had a big fall. Tom was sad. Sam was hurt. The fall made Sam cry. Tom and Sam were not happy.
