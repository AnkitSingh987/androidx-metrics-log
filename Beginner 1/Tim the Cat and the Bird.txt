Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cat and the Bird

One day, a chubby cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree and decided to climb it. When he got to the top, he found a soft spot and took a nap. Tim was very happy. While Tim was sleeping, a bird came and sat on the same branch. The bird saw a napkin stuck in the tree. "Hello, cat!" said the bird. "Did you see the napkin?" Tim woke up and looked at the napkin. He didn't want to disturb the bird, so he said, "Yes, I see it." Just then, a strong wind blew the napkin away. The bird and Tim watched as it flew far away. The bird was sad because it wanted to use the napkin for its nest. But then, Tim had an idea. He told the bird, "I can help you find a new napkin!" The bird was happy, and together they went on a new adventure to find a napkin for the bird's nest.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their ball. They liked to kick the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they missed and the ball rolled away. They had to be careful not to lose the ball or let it go near the road. One time, the ball went too far and landed near a big tree. Lily ran after it, but Ben saw a man with a dog walking on the path. The dog looked big and cross. It barked at Lily and ran towards her. "Stop, Lily!" Ben shouted. "The dog is cross! Come back!" Lily heard Ben and turned around. She saw the dog and felt scared. She dropped the ball and ran back to Ben. The dog picked up the ball in its mouth and ran away with it. "Hey, that's our ball!" Ben yelled. "Give it back!" The man heard Ben and looked at the dog. He was not cross. He smiled and called the dog. "Rex, come here! Drop the ball!" The dog obeyed and dropped the ball. The man picked it up and walked towards Lily and Ben. "I'm sorry, kids," he said. "Rex likes to play with balls. He didn't mean to scare you. Here's your ball back." He gave the ball to Ben and patted Rex on the head. "Thank you, mister," Ben said. "Can we pet your dog?" "Sure, if you want to," the man said. "He's friendly. Just be careful, he might lick you." Lily and Ben smiled and petted Rex. He wagged his tail and licked their faces. They laughed and thanked the man again. They learned that the dog was not cross, he was just playful. They also learned to be careful with their ball and not to judge by looks. They had fun in the park and made a new friend.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with toys and books. But sometimes they quarrel over who gets what. One day, they see a big package in the living room. It has a card that says "For Anna and Ben, from Grandma and Grandpa". "Wow, what is it?" Anna asks. "Let's open it and see!" Ben says. They tear off the paper and find a big box of blocks. There are many colors and shapes. They can build houses, cars, animals, and anything they want. "Look, I made a tower!" Anna says. "I made a bridge!" Ben says. They are very happy and start to play together. But soon they quarrel again. "I want the red block!" Anna says. "No, I want it! It's mine!" Ben says. They pull and tug at the block. It falls to the floor and breaks in half. "Oh no!" they both say. They look at the broken block and feel sad. They realize they were silly to quarrel over a block. They have so many other blocks to share. "I'm sorry, Anna. You can have the red block." Ben says. "I'm sorry, Ben. You can have it. I like the blue block." Anna says. They hug and say they are sorry. They start to build a new tower and a new bridge. They are glad they have each other and the blocks. They thank Grandma and Grandpa for the package. They play happily and do not quarrel anymore.
