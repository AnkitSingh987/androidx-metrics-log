Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Sun

One day, a cute little dog named Max saw a big armchair. The armchair was very soft and nice. Max wanted to sit on it to feel warm and cozy. But there was a problem. A big cat named Tom was sitting on the armchair. Max was sad and didn't know what to do. Max said to Tom, "Please, can I sit on the armchair? I want to feel warm and cozy too." But Tom didn't want to share. Tom said, "No, this is my armchair. You can't sit here." Max felt sad and walked away. Just then, Max saw the sun shining outside. He had an idea. Max went outside and lay down in the warm sunshine. The sun felt good and made Max happy. Tom saw Max in the sun and felt lonely. Tom said, "Max, can I come sit in the sun with you?" Max smiled and said, "Yes, Tom, we can share the sun." So, Max and Tom sat in the sunshine together, feeling warm and happy.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the park. They have a big ball that they can roll and kick. One day, they see a weird thing in the grass. It is brown and long and hard. It looks like a root, but it is not from a plant. "What is that?" Tom asks. "I don't know. Let's roll it with our ball!" Sam says. They run to the weird thing and try to roll it with their ball. But the weird thing does not move. It is too heavy and stuck in the ground. They push and pull, but nothing happens. "Hey, stop that!" a voice says. They look up and see a man. He is angry. He has a hat and a shovel. He is digging near the weird thing. "That's not a root. That's a bone. A dinosaur bone. I'm a scientist. I'm trying to find more bones. You are ruining my work!" the man says. Tom and Sam are scared. They did not know it was a bone. They did not know it was a dinosaur. They did not know it was a scientist. "Sorry, mister. We did not know. We just wanted to play," Tom says. "Yeah, sorry. We won't touch it again," Sam says. They run away with their ball. They find another spot to play. They decide to roll their ball on the slide. It is more fun and less weird.

Mary was so excited. Today was going to be the best day ever! She opened her eyes and smiled at the gray sky. She jumped out of bed and ran outside. In front of the house, the street was full of cars but she wanted to find the special one. Suddenly, she spotted it! It was big and round, and had bright lights! Mary ran closer and saw it was a popcorn truck. She sighed with happiness. She ran to the truck and smelled the delicious popcorn. She asked the nice man if she could have some of the popcorn and he handed her a big bag. Mary carried her popcorn bag to the park. She got to the top of the hill and sat down. She opened the bag and the delicious smell made her even more happy. She ate the popcorn and it was the best thing she ever tasted! Mary was filled with so much joy that she finished the popcorn quickly. She closed her eyes and smiled, knowing that this was the best day ever.

Lily and Tom were playing in their room. They had many toys, but they liked to make noise with pots and pans. They banged them with spoons and forks, and laughed when they heard the loud sounds. But their mom did not like the noise. She was working in the next room, and she needed quiet. She asked them to stop, but they did not listen. They thought it was fun to make noise, and they did not care about their mom. Then, Lily had an idea. She saw a big pot on the shelf, and she wanted to strike it with a spoon. She thought it would make the loudest noise ever. She reached for the pot, but it was too high. She stood on a chair, and stretched her arm. She touched the pot, but it was heavy. It fell from the shelf, and hit the ceiling. Then, it bounced on the floor, and rolled under the bed. The noise was so loud, that their mom came running. She saw the pot, the chair, and the mess. She was very angry. She scolded Lily and Tom, and told them to go to bed. She said they were naughty, and they did not respect her. Lily and Tom felt sorry. They realized they were wrong. They said sorry to their mom, and hugged her. They promised to be more thoughtful, and to play quietly. They learned that noise is not always fun, and that they should think of others.
