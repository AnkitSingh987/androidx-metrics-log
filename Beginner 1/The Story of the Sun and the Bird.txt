Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Sun and the Bird

One day, in a big nation, the sun went away. It was very dark. People were not happy. They wanted the sun to come back. A little girl named Lily asked her mom, "Mom, what can we do to make the sun come back?" Her mom said, "I recommend we all sing a happy song. Maybe the sun will hear us and come back." So, Lily and her mom started to sing a happy song. Soon, all the people in the nation joined them. They sang and sang, but the sun did not come back. Just when they were about to give up, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird said, "I have the sun! I took it because I thought it would make my nest warm. But I see now that you need it more. I will give it back." The big bird put the sun back in the sky. The dark went away, and it was bright again. The people were so happy. They thanked the bird and kept singing their happy song.

One day, a boy named Tim had a zip. He was very proud of his zip. It was on his bag. Tim wanted to serve his friends a snack. He put the snack in his bag and went to the park. At the park, Tim met his friends. He said, "I have a snack to serve you all." His friends were happy. They all sat down to eat the snack. But when Tim opened his bag, the snack was gone! They were all sad. A little bird came and said, "I took the snack. I'm sorry." The bird gave back the snack. Tim and his friends were happy again. They learned to share and forgive. And the bird was proud to serve them too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a messy room. He liked to play with his toys and leave them all over the floor. One day, his mom told him to clean up his room. Tim didn't want to, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. As Tim started picking up his toys, he found a big, red ball. He decided to play with it for a little while. Tim threw the ball up in the air and tried to catch it. But, he missed the ball and it went slap on the floor. The loud noise made Tim jump. Tim's mom heard the slap and came into the room. She saw that Tim was playing instead of cleaning. She told Tim to put the ball away and finish cleaning his room. Tim said sorry and put the ball away. He cleaned up the rest of his toys and soon his room was nice and tidy.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy fox. He was always falling and tripping over things. One day, he was walking in the woods and saw a big tree. The fox thought, "I will try to climb the tree." The fox started to climb the tree, but he slipped and fell. A little bird saw the fox and laughed. "You are so clumsy, fox!" said the bird. The fox felt sad and said, "I will try again and be more careful." The next day, the fox went back to the tree. He remembered what the bird said and was very careful. This time, he climbed all the way to the top. The bird saw the fox and laughed again. "You did it, fox! You are not clumsy anymore!" The fox was so happy and laughed with the bird. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a lawyer. He was a bad man. He liked to slap people when they did not listen to him. Everyone in the town was scared of him. One day, a little girl came to the town. She saw the bad lawyer slap a man. The little girl was not scared. She walked up to the bad lawyer and said, "You should not slap people. It is not nice!" The bad lawyer looked at the little girl and laughed. He raised his hand to slap her, but something unexpected happened. The little girl turned into a big, strong lion! The bad lawyer was very scared now. The lion said, "I am a magic lion. I teach bad people to be nice." The bad lawyer said he was sorry for slapping people. He promised to be nice from now on. The town was happy, and the magic lion went away to help more people.
