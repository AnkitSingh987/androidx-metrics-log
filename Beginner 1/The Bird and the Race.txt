Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Race

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little bird. The bird was very bored. It did not like to just sit in its nest all day. It wanted to have fun and play with its friends. One day, the bird heard about a big race. The race was for all the birds in the town. The bird that could fly the highest and fastest would win a big prize. The little bird was very excited. It wanted to win the prize and show everyone how well it could fly. The day of the race came, and all the birds lined up to start. The little bird was not scared. It knew it could soar high and fast. The race started, and the little bird soared higher and higher. It flew faster than all the other birds. It won the race and got the big prize. The little bird was not bored anymore. It was very happy and had lots of fun with its friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia went with her mom to the store. They needed to buy food for dinner. Mia saw a big bag of rice. She touched the bag and it felt rough. Mia asked her mom, "Can we get this rice?" Her mom said yes, so they took the rice home. Mia wanted to help her mom cook the rice. Her mom showed her how to do it. They put the rice in a pot with water. Mia stirred the rice and it still felt rough. But when it was cooked, the rice was soft and yummy. Mia and her mom ate the rice with chicken and veggies. They were happy they got the rough rice from the store. Mia did a good job helping her mom cook. Now, Mia knew how to make tasty rice for dinner.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play on the farm. They see many animals on the farm. They see cows, pigs, chickens and sheep. One day, Sara and Ben have a plan. They want to make the sheep clean. They think the sheep are dirty and smelly. They want to wash the sheep with water and soap. They find a bucket and a hose. They fill the bucket with water and soap. They look for the sheep. They see the sheep in the field. They run to the sheep with the bucket and the hose. "Hello, sheep!" Sara says. "We have a plan. We want to make you clean. You will look nice and smell good." But the sheep do not like the plan. The sheep do not want to be wet and soapy. The sheep run away from Sara and Ben. They baa and jump. They make a lot of noise. "Stop, sheep!" Ben says. "We want to help you. We want to make you happy. Please, let us wash you." But the sheep do not listen. The sheep run faster and faster. They run to the other side of the field. They hide behind the trees. Sara and Ben are sad. They do not understand why the sheep do not like their plan. They think the sheep are silly and rude. They put down the bucket and the hose. They go back to the farm. "Maybe we can try another plan tomorrow," Sara says. "Maybe we can find another animal to wash," Ben says. They hope the sheep will change their mind. They hope the sheep will be friendly and clean.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Zip. Zip loved to play with his red ball. One day, Zip saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. Zip's friend, a little girl named Sue, came to play too. Sue had a long rope. She said, "I will tie this rope to the tree, and we can play jump rope." Zip barked happily and wagged his tail. They played near the tree all day. But, the sky turned dark and clouds came. Zip and Sue did not see the dark clouds. The rain started to fall. The rope got wet and tight around the tree. Zip and Sue tried to untie the rope, but they could not. They were sad and wet, and they had to leave the rope on the tree.
