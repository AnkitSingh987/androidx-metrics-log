Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Naughty Cat and the Dog

One day, a naughty cat named Max saw a big stand. On the stand, there were sharp things. Max wanted to play with the sharp things, but his mom said, "No, Max! Those are not for play." Max did not listen to his mom. When she was not looking, he jumped on the stand. Max touched the sharp things and they fell down. Max was scared. Just then, a nice dog named Sam came in. He saw Max and the sharp things on the floor. Sam did not want Max to get hurt. He picked up the sharp things with his mouth and put them back on the stand. Max was happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" From that day, Max and Sam were the best of friends.

One day, a curious little cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw a big, soft thing hanging on a door. Kitty did not know what it was, so she went closer to look. It was a bathrobe! Kitty wanted to meet the person who owned the bathrobe. She walked into the house and saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was wearing a pretty dress and had a big smile. Kitty liked her right away. Sue saw Kitty and was happy to meet her. She picked Kitty up and gave her a big hug. Then, Sue showed Kitty her bathrobe and told her it was for wearing after a bath. Kitty and Sue became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, a little boat named True loved to sail on the big blue sea. True was a very serious boat. He always did his best to sail fast and strong. True had many friends in the sea, like fish, dolphins, and even a big whale. One sunny day, True met a new friend while sailing. It was a little bird. The bird asked, "Can I sail with you, True?" True smiled and said, "Yes, little bird, come and sail with me. We can have fun together." They sailed and played all day long. The little bird sang songs while True sailed fast in the sea. They laughed and had a great time. When the sun went down, they said goodbye. True was happy to have a new friend and looked forward to more fun days sailing with the little bird.

One day, a little bird was in its nest. It was a gloomy day. The sky was gray and the wind was cold. The bird felt sad and began to cry. In the nest, there was a small egg. The bird looked at the egg and stopped crying. The bird knew that soon, a baby bird would come out of the egg. The bird felt happy and smiled. Days passed, and the sun came out. The egg began to crack. The little bird was excited. The baby bird came out of the egg. The two birds were very happy, and they sang together in their nest.

John was a worker. Every day he worked on the farm. One day, John needed to do some work, and he had a big job to do! John worked hard all day, but he was covered in dirt. He went to the river and used the soft rub to take away the dirt. He rubbed the dirt away, it felt good and soon John was clean again. When he was done, John turned the cool white rub around in his hand. It sparkled in the sun and it looked so pretty. He watched the sunflowers at the side of the river and thought how nice it was to be clean. John thanked the river for the rub and went back to work.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in his yard. One day, he saw a light green radish in the garden. He wanted the radish to eat. He pulled and pulled, but the radish did not come out. Tim was sad. He wanted help. He asked his dog, Spot, to help him. Spot pulled and pulled too, but the radish still did not come out. Tim and Spot did not know what to do. They wanted the radish so much. Just then, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird saw Tim and Spot trying to get the radish. The bird wanted to help. With one big pull, the bird got the radish out of the ground. But, the bird did not give the radish to Tim and Spot. The bird ate the radish! Tim and Spot were sad, but they laughed. They did not expect the bird to eat the radish.
