Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a cooler under a tree. The cooler was big and blue. Tim was happy to find it. Tim called out to his friend, Sally. "Sally, come here! Look what I found!" Sally ran over to Tim. She saw the cooler, too. "Wow, that's a big cooler!" she said. They decided to play with the cooler. They took the cooler to a distant place in the park. They played with it all day long. They pretended it was a boat, a car, and a house. They had so much fun playing with their new toy. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked on the road near their home. Tim saw a fake bug on the ground and wanted to grab it. "Look, Mom! I want to grab the fake bug!" Tim said. His mom said, "Okay, but be careful." As Tim reached down to grab the fake bug, it started to move! Tim was very surprised. "Mom, the fake bug is moving!" he said. His mom looked closer and saw that it was a real bug, not a fake one. Tim and his mom laughed as the real bug crawled away. They continued their walk, looking for more fun things to find on the road.

Once upon a time there was a little boy. He loved to explore new places and one day he decided to take a walk in the forest. As he walked along, he spotted a big barrel. It looked like a fun toy and he wanted to take it home. The little boy was about to pick up the barrel when he heard something dangerous. He froze in fear but eventually he mustered up the courage to take a peek. Behind the barrel was a big, mean lion! The little boy knew he had to take a different route home, so he slowly backed away from the lion and ran fast. Eventually, he found his way back home safely. He never returned to the forest to take the barrel, but he was very happy that he listened to the dangerous sound.

Tom and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys. They have a big box of toys in their room. They have dolls, cars, balls, books and more. One day, they find a new toy in the box. It is a soft, yellow duck. It has a red strip on its head. It has a hole in its belly. Tom picks up the duck and squeezes it. The duck makes a loud sound. "Quack, quack, quack!" Tom laughs. He likes the duck. "Let me see, let me see!" Mia says. She wants to play with the duck too. She tries to take the duck from Tom. Tom does not want to share. He holds the duck tight. "No, it is mine!" he says. He squeezes the duck again. "Quack, quack, quack!" Mia gets angry. She pulls the duck from Tom. She squeezes the duck hard. "Quack, quack, quack!" The duck sounds funny. Mia laughs. She likes the duck too. "Hey, stop it!" Tom says. He wants the duck back. He pushes Mia. Mia pushes him back. They fight over the duck. They pull and tug and twist the duck. The duck does not like it. The duck feels sad. Suddenly, the duck stops making sound. The duck breaks. The hole in its belly gets bigger. The air comes out. The duck goes flat. Tom and Mia stop fighting. They look at the duck. They feel sorry. They broke the duck. They hear a knock on the door. It is their mom. She comes in the room. She sees the duck. She knows the duck. It is a famous duck. It is her duck. She had it when she was a little girl. She loved it very much. She gave it to Tom and Mia to play with. She is not happy. "Tom, Mia, what did you do?" she asks. She is not angry. She is sad. She picks up the duck. She hugs it. She tries to fix it. She cannot. The duck is broken. Tom and Mia feel bad. They hug their mom. They say sorry. They say they love her. They say they love the duck. They ask her to tell them a story about the duck. She smiles. She tells them a story. She tells them how the duck was her best friend. She tells them how the duck made her laugh. She tells them how the duck was famous. She tells them how the duck was on TV. She tells them how the duck was a star. Tom and Mia listen. They are amazed. They did not know the duck was famous. They did not know the duck was on TV. They did not know the duck was a star. They wish they did not break the duck. They wish they could see the duck on TV. They wish they could make the duck happy. They look at the duck. The duck looks at them. The duck has a secret. The duck is not broken. The duck is smart. The duck played a trick. The duck wanted Tom and Mia to stop fighting. The duck wanted Tom and Mia to love each other. The duck wanted Tom and Mia to love their mom. The duck wanted Tom and Mia to love the duck. The duck squeezes its belly. The duck makes a sound. "Quack, quack, quack!" Tom and Mia are surprised. They are happy. The duck is not broken. The duck is alive. The duck is famous. The duck is a star. The duck is their friend. They hug the duck. They hug their mom. They hug each other. They laugh. They play. They are happy. The duck is happy. The end.
