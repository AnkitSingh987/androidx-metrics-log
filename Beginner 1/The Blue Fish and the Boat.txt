Category: Beginner
Title: The Blue Fish and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a big blue fish. The blue fish lived near the shore. The blue fish had many friends in the sea. They played and swam together every day. One day, the blue fish saw a small boat near the shore. In the boat, there was a little girl. The little girl was sad. She dropped her toy in the water. The blue fish wanted to help the little girl. So, the blue fish took the toy and swam fast to give it back. The little girl was very happy to receive her toy back. She said "Thank you, blue fish!" The blue fish smiled and swam away. All the friends of the blue fish clapped and cheered. The blue fish learned that helping others can make you feel good too.

One day, a big dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was holding a bar of soap. Spot was curious about the soap and wanted to know what it was. "Hi Tim, what is that?" asked Spot. Tim replied, "This is soap, Spot. We use it to get clean." Spot wanted to try it, so he took a bite of the soap. Suddenly, he made a funny face and spit it out. "Yuck! That does not taste good!" said Spot. Tim laughed and said, "Silly Spot, soap is not for eating! It's for washing!" They both laughed and played together, and from that day on, Spot never tried to bite soap again.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a silly boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his round ball. One day, while playing, he saw a big tree. He wanted to strike the ball at the tree. He said to his friend, "Watch me hit the tree with my ball!" His friend said, "No, Tim! Don't do it!" But Tim did not listen. He struck the ball very hard at the tree. The ball bounced back and hit Tim on his head. Tim fell down and started to cry. His friend came to help him, but Tim had a big bump on his head. Tim's mom heard him cry and came running. "What happened, Tim?" his mom asked. Tim told her about the tree and the round ball. His mom said, "That was a silly thing to do, Tim. Now you have a big bump on your head." Tim felt sad and knew he should have listened to his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to wander around the park and play with his friends. One sunny day, Spot saw a little boy with a new haircut. The boy's name was Tim. "Hi, Tim!" said Spot, wagging his tail. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, Spot!" said Tim, smiling. "I love your playful ways. Let's play catch!" Spot and Tim played catch for a long time. They laughed and ran in the warm sun. Tim's mom watched them play and smiled. She was happy that her son had a fun friend like Spot. When it was time to go home, Tim hugged Spot and said, "See you tomorrow, my playful friend!" Spot wagged his tail and watched Tim walk away. He felt happy and couldn't wait to play with Tim again.

One day, a monkey went to the forest to collect food. He saw a big tree with many fruits. The monkey was very happy. He started to collect the fruits in a big basket. While he was collecting, a small bird came to the tree. The bird said, "Hello, monkey! Can I have some fruit too?" The monkey was ignorant and didn't want to share. He said, "No, these are all for me!" The bird was sad and flew away. Later, the monkey tried to carry the heavy basket of fruits. It was too heavy for him. Suddenly, the basket broke, and all the fruits fell into a river. The monkey was very sad. The small bird saw this and felt bad for the monkey. The bird said, "Next time, we can collect fruits together and share." The monkey agreed and learned that sharing is good.
