Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a clean envelope on the floor. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. "Mom, look what I found!" Lucy said. Her mom was busy in the kitchen but told Lucy to put the envelope on the table. Lucy's dog, Max, saw the envelope too. He thought it was a toy to play with. So, Max took the envelope in his mouth and started to chew on it. Lucy saw Max chewing the envelope and got upset. "No, Max! That's not for you!" she yelled. Lucy tried to get the envelope back from Max, but he did not want to give it up. They both pulled on the envelope, but it ripped in half. Lucy was sad, and Max felt bad too. In the end, they both said sorry to each other and promised to share their toys from now on.

One day, a bird lived in a cage. The bird felt sad. It wanted to fly and play with other birds. The bird asked the girl, "Please let me out of the cage. I want to fly and play." The girl thought about it. She said, "I will let you out, but you must remind me to close the cage door when you come back." The bird was happy. It flew out of the cage and played with other birds. The sky was mild and sunny. The bird had a lot of fun. But then, a big wind came. The wind blew the cage away. The bird saw the cage fly away and felt worried. It told the girl, "The wind took the cage! What do we do?" The girl and the bird worked together. They found the cage and brought it back home. The bird reminded the girl to close the cage door. They were both happy and safe.

One day, a man and his wife went to the store. They wanted to buy things for their home. The wife saw a cheap toy she liked. She said, "Look, this toy is cheap! Can we buy it?" The man said, "Yes, it is a nice toy." They bought the toy and went back home. The man put the toy on a high shelf to prevent it from falling. The wife was happy. They went to sleep. In the night, something unexpected happened. The cheap toy came to life! It jumped off the high shelf and started to dance. The man and the wife woke up and saw the toy dancing. They were very surprised. The man said, "Wow! Our cheap toy is magic!" They all danced together and had fun.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a pale dog named Spot. Spot wanted to help his friends. He saw a big tree with a ball stuck in it. All the kids in the village wanted their ball back. Spot tried to get the ball from the tree. He jumped and jumped, but he did not succeed. His friends told him, "Don't give up, Spot!" So, Spot tried again. This time, he ran and jumped as high as he could. He succeeded and got the ball out of the tree. The kids were so happy, they gave Spot a reward. They gave him a big bone to chew. Spot learned that if you try hard and don't give up, you can succeed. And when you succeed, you can get a nice reward. The kids learned that they should always help their friends, just like Spot helped them.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big balloon. It is red and shiny. They want to play with it. They run to the balloon. But a man is there. He is ugly and mean. He says the balloon is his. He does not want to share. He yells at Lila and Tom. They are scared. The man grabs the balloon. He uses a pin to pop it. The balloon makes a loud noise. Lila and Tom cry. The man laughs. He is very bad. The balloon had air inside. The air was good for Lila and Tom. They need air for their lungs. Their lungs help them breathe. But the man does not care. He takes the air away. Lila and Tom feel sick. They cannot breathe well. They fall to the ground. The man walks away. He does not help them. He does not say sorry. He is very ugly and mean. Lila and Tom do not get up. They do not play anymore. They do not see the park. They do not see each other. They are gone. The man is gone too. But the pin is still there. It is small and sharp. It is very bad.
