Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Ball

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play golf with their dads. Golf is a game where you hit a small ball with a stick. You try to make the ball go into a hole in the grass. One day, Tom and Sam go to the golf course with their dads. They each have their own stick and ball. They are very excited. They want to hit the ball far and fast. "Look at me, Sam!" Tom says. He swings his stick hard and hits the ball. The ball flies in the air and lands on the grass. "Wow, Tom, you are good!" Sam says. "Thank you, Sam. Now you try!" Tom says. Sam swings his stick and hits the ball. But the ball does not fly. It rolls on the ground and stops. "Oh, no, Sam, what happened?" Tom says. "I don't know, Tom. My stick is too heavy. I can't swing it well." Sam says. He feels sad. He does not like golf anymore. "Hey, boys, don't worry. Golf is not easy. You need to practice and have fun." Tom's dad says. He comes over and gives Sam a hug. "Why don't you rest for a while and watch me and Tom's dad play? Maybe you can learn something from us." "Okay, dad. Thank you." Sam says. He smiles a little. He sits on a bench and rests. He watches Tom's dad and his dad hit the ball. They are very good. They make the ball go into the hole in a few tries. "Wow, dad, you are amazing!" Tom says. He claps his hands. He is proud of his dad. "Thank you, son. But you know what? You are amazing too. You hit the ball very well for your age. You just need to keep practicing and have fun." Tom's dad says. He gives Tom a high five. "Really, dad? Thank you. I love golf. I want to be like you." Tom says. He hugs his dad. Sam watches Tom and his dad. He feels happy for them. He thinks maybe golf is not so bad after all. He thinks maybe he can try again later. He thinks maybe he can be like Tom and his dad someday. He looks at his stick and ball. He picks them up. He stands up. He walks to the next hole. He says to Tom and his dad, "Can I play with you?" "Of course, Sam. Come on, let's go. We are a team." Tom says. He gives Sam a thumbs up. "Okay, let's go. We are a team." Sam says. He gives Tom a thumbs up. They walk to the next hole. They play golf. They have fun. They are normal. They are happy.

One day, a little train named Tim was in the train yard. Tim had a big engine inside him. He felt very cold because it was a cold winter day. Tim wanted to be warm and help people by taking them to different places. Tim saw his friend, Sally the train. Sally was not cold because she had a warm engine. Tim asked Sally, "How do you feel warm and not cold?" Sally smiled and said, "I have a warm engine because I work hard and help people. When I help people, my engine gets warm and I feel good." Tim wanted to feel warm too. So, he started to work hard and help people. He took people to their homes, to the store, and to see their friends. As Tim worked hard, his engine got warmer and warmer. Tim was no longer cold. He was happy because he was helping people and feeling warm.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there was a high tree. In the tree, there was a bird. The bird liked to fly high in the sky. One day, a little boy came to the farm. He saw the bird in the high tree. He said, "Hello, bird! Can you come down and play with me?" The bird looked at the boy and said, "No, I want to remain high in the tree." The boy was sad, but he understood. He played on the farm with the other animals. The bird watched from the high tree. The boy and the animals had fun on the farm.
