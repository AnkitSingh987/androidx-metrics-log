Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a birdcage with a pretty bird inside. She loved her bird very much. The bird was persistent and would sing every day. One day, Mia was playing with the birdcage. She was swinging it back and forth. But then, she accidentally let go and the birdcage dropped on the floor. The door opened and the bird flew out. Mia was so sad. Mia's mom saw what happened and said, "Don't worry, Mia. We will find your bird." They looked all around the house. Finally, they found the bird hiding under the bed. Mia was so happy. She said, "I will be more careful next time." And she never dropped the birdcage again.

One day, a lively cat named Tom went to the store. He wanted to buy milk for his little sister. On his way, he met his friend, a dog named Max. Max said, "Hi Tom! Where are you going?" Tom said, "I am going to the store to buy milk for my sister." When Tom got to the store, he saw the milk. But, oh no! The door was locked. Tom did not know what to do. He wanted to get the milk for his sister. Then, Max had an idea. He said, "Tom, I can help you. I will go get the key to open the door." Max ran fast to find the key. He came back and unlocked the door. Tom was very happy. Tom got the milk for his sister. They both said thank you to Max. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his arrow toy. He would run around the town, pretending to be a hero. One day, Tim was playing with his arrow when he saw a big dog. He wanted to play with the dog, but the dog didn't like the arrow. The dog barked and barked at Tim. Tim felt embarrass and sad. Tim ran away from the dog, but he tripped and fell. His arrow broke, and he cried. The dog went away, but Tim was still sad. His arrow was gone, and he had no more fun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a lively swan. The swan lived in a big pond with many fish friends. The swan liked to play with the fish every day. They had a lot of fun together. One day, the swan and the fish friends found a big bowl. The bowl was full of colors. The swan had a fun idea. The swan said, "Let's mix the colors and make new ones!" The fish friends liked the idea too. The swan and the fish friends started to mix the colors in the big bowl. They mixed red and blue to make purple. They mixed yellow and blue to make green. They mixed all the colors and made a big rainbow in the pond. The swan and the fish friends were very happy with their colorful pond.

Tim had a pet dog named Spot. Spot was big and brown and liked to play with Tim. They ran and jumped and barked in the yard. Tim loved Spot very much. One day, Tim and Spot went for a walk with Tim's mom. They saw a big park with many trees and flowers. Tim wanted to go and see the park. He asked his mom if he could go. His mom said yes, but told him to stay close and hold Spot's leash. Tim and Spot walked into the park. They saw many things. They saw birds and squirrels and bugs. They saw swings and slides and benches. They saw other people and other pets. Tim and Spot were happy. But then, they saw something unknown. It was big and gray and had a long trunk. It made a loud noise. It was an elephant. Tim had never seen an elephant before. He was scared. He did not know what it was or what it would do. Spot was scared too. He did not like the unknown thing. He did not like the loud noise. He did not like the long trunk. He started to tremble. He pulled on the leash and tried to run away. Tim felt Spot tremble and pull. He was afraid to lose Spot. He held the leash tight and tried to calm Spot. He said, "It's okay, Spot. It's just an elephant. It won't hurt us. Mom is here. We are safe." Tim's mom saw Tim and Spot. She saw the elephant too. She knew Tim and Spot were scared. She ran to them and hugged them. She said, "Don't worry, Tim and Spot. The elephant is not bad. It is just a pet too. It belongs to the circus. The circus is in town. The elephant is here to perform. Do you want to see the circus?" Tim and Spot looked at the elephant. They looked at Tim's mom. They looked at each other. They stopped trembling. They nodded. They wanted to see the circus. They wanted to see the elephant and other animals. They wanted to have fun. Tim's mom smiled. She said, "Let's go then. The circus is this way. Come on, Tim and Spot. Let's go and see the show."
