Category: Beginner
Title: The Toys of Sara and Ben

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. But today, they are not happy. Sara wants to play with the red car. Ben wants to play with the blue car. They do not want to share. They are angry and sad. Mom sees Sara and Ben. She says, "Why are you not playing? You are friends. You should unite and have fun." Sara and Ben do not listen. They turn away and pout. Mom has an idea. She says, "I have juice for you. Do you want some juice?" Sara and Ben love juice. They nod and smile. Mom brings two cups of juice. She says, "Here is your juice. But you have to do something first. You have to give each other a hug and say sorry. Then you can have your juice." Sara and Ben think. They do not want to be angry and sad. They want to be friends and have fun. They want to have juice. They hug each other and say sorry. Mom smiles and gives them their juice. Sara and Ben drink their juice. They feel happy and warm. They say, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best." Mom says, "You are welcome, Sara and Ben. You are the best, too. Now, do you want to play with your toys and books?" Sara and Ben say, "Yes, Mom. We want to play with our toys and books. We want to share and unite. We do not want to be lazy and pout." Mom says, "That is good, Sara and Ben. I am proud of you. Have fun, my loves." Sara and Ben hug Mom and run to their toys and books. They play and laugh. They are happy and united.

Tom and Mia liked to play soccer in the park. They had a ball and two goals. They took turns to kick the ball and try to score. Sometimes they scored, sometimes they missed. They had fun and laughed a lot. One day, a big boy came to the park. He saw Tom and Mia playing soccer. He wanted to play too. He asked them if he could join. Tom and Mia said yes. They were nice and friendly. But the big boy was not nice and friendly. He was rude and mean. He kicked the ball very hard and always scored. He did not let Tom and Mia have a turn. He shouted at them and said they were bad. He made them feel sad and angry. Tom and Mia did not like the big boy. They wanted him to go away. They asked him to stop and share. But the big boy did not listen. He said he was the best and they were the worst. He said he did not care. Then, something happened. The big boy kicked the ball so hard that it flew over the fence. It landed in a huge pond. The big boy could not get it back. He was sad and angry. He cried and screamed. Tom and Mia saw the big boy crying and screaming. They felt sorry for him. They had an idea. They had another ball in their bag. They took it out and gave it to the big boy. They said they could play together and have fun. The big boy was surprised and happy. He said thank you and sorry. He said he was wrong and they were right. He said he would be nice and friendly. He said he would share and care. Tom and Mia smiled and nodded. They played soccer with the big boy. They took turns and helped each other. They scored and missed. They had fun and laughed a lot. The moral of the story is: Be nice and friendly to others. Share and care. Don't be rude and mean. You will have more fun and make more friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big, ugly toy that she did not like. She wanted to remove it from her room, but her mom said, "No, Lily, you must keep it." One day, Lily went to a friend's birthday party. She saw a big, pretty present for her friend. Lily thought, "I want to give my ugly toy away as a present too." Lily went home and wrapped the ugly toy in pretty paper. She gave the present to her friend, Tim. Tim opened the present and said, "Thank you, Lily! I love this toy!" Lily was happy that her friend liked the ugly toy. She learned that what she thought was ugly, someone else might think is nice. The moral of the story is: don't judge things only by how they look.
