Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cookie

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big cookie jar. He knew he should ask his mom before taking a cookie. But he did not want to wait. He felt a little guilty, but he took a cookie anyway. Later, his mom found the cookie jar empty. She was sad because she wanted to share the cookies with everyone. Tim saw his mom's sad face and knew he did something wrong. He started to regret taking the cookie. Tim decided to say sorry to his mom. He told her about the cookie he took. His mom hugged him and said it was okay. They made more cookies together and shared them with everyone. Tim felt happy and learned to always ask before taking something.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Bear. They were best friends. One day, Mr. Bear said, "I heard you mention a ghost. Is it true?" Tim nodded and said, "Yes, it is true. But don't be scared, Mr. Bear. The ghost is a nice ghost." Mr. Bear felt a little jealous. He asked, "Can I meet the ghost?" Tim took Mr. Bear to the ghost. The ghost was happy to see them. "Hello, I am the nice ghost," the ghost said with a smile. Mr. Bear and the ghost became friends too. They all played together and had fun. Tim, Mr. Bear, and the nice ghost were happy to be friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to walk on the trail near her house. One day, she saw a tired bird on the trail. The bird had a cheap, broken wing. Sue wanted to help the bird. "Hi, bird," said Sue. "You need to rest your wing. I will help you." The bird looked at Sue and said, "Thank you, Sue." Sue took the bird to her house. She made a soft bed for the bird to rest in. The bird slept and slept. Sue took care of the bird every day. The bird's wing got better and better. One day, the bird was all better. Sue and the bird went back to the trail. The bird said, "Thank you, Sue. I can fly now." Sue said, "You're welcome, bird. Have fun!" The bird flew away, and Sue went back home. She was happy she could help her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a raft. It was an impressive raft. It was big and strong. Tim was very proud to own it. He would show it to all his friends. They liked the raft too. One day, Tim took his raft to the river. He wanted to play with it. He put the raft on the water and got on it. The raft floated away. Tim was very happy. He saw fish and birds while he was on the raft. He felt like a big boy. But, Tim forgot to tie the raft to a tree. The wind blew and the raft went too far. Tim got scared. He wanted to go back to his mom and dad. But he could not. He cried and cried. The raft floated away and Tim got lost. The moral of the story is to always be careful and listen to your parents when they tell you what to do.

One day, a fast cat and a slow dog unite to play. They want to race to see who has the most speed. The cat is very fast, and the dog is not so fast. They start the race, and the cat runs very fast. The dog tries to run fast too, but he is upset because he cannot catch up. The cat stops and says, "Why are you upset, dog? We are just playing." The dog looks at the cat and says, "I am upset because I cannot run as fast as you. I want to win, but I am too slow." The cat thinks for a moment and says, "We can race again, but this time, I will run slower." The dog agrees, and they start to race again. But the cat forgets to run slower and runs very fast again. The dog gets more upset and stops running. In the end, the dog is too upset to play with the cat. The cat feels bad for making the dog upset. They do not play together anymore, and they go their separate ways.
