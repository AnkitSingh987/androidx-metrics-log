Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Sheep and the Bird

One day, a little sheep named Sam was playing in the icy cold snow. He was having so much fun, but his wool was getting very wet. Sam didn't want to go inside, so he kept playing. Soon, Sam's friend, a little bird named Bella, came to play too. "Sam, your wool is all wet," said Bella. "You should go inside and get dry." Sam looked at his wet wool and said, "Okay, let's go inside." Sam and Bella went inside to get warm and dry. They sat by the warm fire and talked about their fun day in the snow. Sam's wool started to get dry, and he felt much better. "Thank you, Bella, for helping me get dry," said Sam. They both smiled and continued to enjoy their warm, cozy day inside.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely cat named Mia. Mia lived in a big house with her best friend, a little boy named Tim. They played together all day long and had lots of fun. One day, Tim went to school to learn math. Mia was sad because she missed him. She wanted to learn math too. So, she put on her thinking cap and tried to count her toys. Mia counted one, two, three, four, and five toys. When Tim came home, Mia was so happy to see him. She shouted, "Tim, I can do math now! I counted my toys!" Tim smiled and gave Mia a big hug. They played together and counted toys all day long. And from that day on, Mia and Tim did lovely math together.

Once there was a dog named Max. Max liked to perform tricks for his friends. He could sit, roll over, and shake hands. Max had a big head and a big smile. One day, Max saw a cat named Luna. Luna had a bad mood. She did not want to play with Max. Max wanted to make Luna happy. He thought, "I will perform a trick to make her smile!" Max tried to perform a trick, but he fell. His head hurt, but he did not give up. He tried again and again. Finally, Max did the trick right! Luna saw Max and smiled. She was happy now. Max's head felt better, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy bow and arrow. He would pretend to fight with it all day long. Tim's best friend was a girl named Sue. They would play together every day. One sunny day, Tim and Sue were playing in the park. Tim said, "Let's have a fight with my bow and arrow!" Sue agreed, and they started to play. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's arrow hit Sue's leg, and she started to cry. "Ouch! That hurt!" Sue said. Tim felt very bad that he hurt his friend. He said, "I'm sorry, Sue. I didn't mean to hurt you." Sue smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. I know you didn't mean it." They decided to play a different game that didn't involve fighting. From that day on, they played together happily and never had any more hurt feelings.

One day, a little boy named Tim was excited to play. He put his toys in a sack. He put the sack on his shoulder. Tim walked to the park. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "What is in the sack?" Tim said, "My toys! I will show you." They played with the toys together. After playing, Tim and Sue were tired. They put the toys back in the sack. Tim put the sack on his shoulder. They went home with big smiles.

Lily and Ben were playing in the yard. They found a hammer and some nails. They wanted to make a house for their toys. "Let me use the hammer, Lily. I am tough and strong," Ben said. "No, Ben. You are too little. You will hurt yourself. I will use the hammer. You can hold the nails," Lily said. But Ben did not care. He was angry at Lily. He wanted to use the hammer. He waited until Lily was not looking. Then he grabbed the hammer and swung it hard. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Lily cried. The hammer hit her hand. It was red and sore. She dropped the nails and ran to Mom. Mom saw Lily's hand. She was worried. She took Lily to the car. She said they had to go to the doctor. Ben was left alone in the yard. He felt bad. He did not mean to hurt Lily. He wanted to say sorry. But it was too late. Lily was gone. He had no one to play with. He had no house for his toys. He had only a hammer and some nails. And he was not tough and strong. He was sad and sorry.
