Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Flower

One day, a little girl named Amy was getting ready for a party. She had a pretty dress with a flower pattern on it. She was very excited to wear it. Her mom helped her put on the dress and said, "You look so nice, Amy!" At the party, Amy saw her friend, Tim. Tim was wearing a shirt with a pattern too, but it was not the same as Amy's. Tim said, "My pattern is better than yours." Amy was sad and didn't know what to say. Amy's mom saw that she was sad and asked, "What's wrong, Amy?" Amy told her mom what Tim had said. Her mom said, "All patterns are nice. Yours is nice, and Tim's is nice too. You both look ready for the party." Amy felt better and went to play with Tim. They both had fun at the party, and Amy learned that everyone can have nice patterns on their clothes.

One day, a little cat named Kitty went to play outside. She saw a big door that she had never seen before. Kitty felt a little uncomfortable, but she wanted to know what was behind the door. Kitty twisted the door handle and found a big room with lots of toys. She met a new friend, a dog named Buddy. Buddy said, "Hi, Kitty! Come play with me!" Kitty was happy to have a new friend. They played with the toys all day. At the end of the day, Kitty went back to the door. She twisted the handle and went home. She told her mom about her new friend, Buddy. Her mom said, "I knew you would find a friend behind that door!" Kitty was happy and couldn't wait to play with Buddy again.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big hill with a nice view. They wanted to go up and see the sky and the trees. "Let's race to the top!" said Tom. "OK!" said Lily. They ran as fast as they could. Tom was ahead, but Lily was close behind. They did not see a boy coming down the hill on his bike. The boy was going very fast and did not yield. He hit Tom and they both fell down. "Ow!" cried Tom. "That hurt!" "Are you OK?" asked Lily. She ran to help Tom. The boy got up and looked at his bike. It was broken. He was sorry. He said to Tom, "I'm sorry. I did not see you. Are you hurt?" Tom was hurt, but he was not angry. He said, "I'm OK. I'm sorry too. I did not see you either. Is your bike OK?" The boy shook his head. He said, "No, it's broken. I liked my bike." Lily felt sorry for the boy. She said, "Maybe we can fix it. Do you have a tool?" The boy nodded. He said, "Yes, I have one in my bag. Maybe you can help me." Tom and Lily agreed. They went to the boy's bag and got his tool. They tried to fix his bike. They worked together and made it better. The boy was happy. He said, "Thank you. You are nice. What are your names?" Tom and Lily told him their names. They said, "You're welcome. What is your name?" The boy said, "I'm Ben. Do you want to play with me?" Tom and Lily smiled. They said, "Yes, we do. Let's play together." They became friends. They played in the park. They shared their view. They learned to yield. They were not sorry.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big voice and she loved to sing. She was very honest and always told the truth. One day, Sue went outside to play with her friends. While they were playing, Sue found a small bug. She wanted to pick it up, but she did not want to hurt it. So, she tried to pinch it very gently with her fingers. But the bug got scared and jumped away. Sue felt sad, but she knew she needed to be more careful next time. Sue used her big voice to call her friends and show them the bug. They all looked at it and talked about how to be kind to bugs. They promised to be honest and always tell each other if they saw a bug in danger. Sue and her friends learned a lot that day and had lots of fun playing together.
