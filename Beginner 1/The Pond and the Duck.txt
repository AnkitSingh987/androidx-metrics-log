Category: Beginner
Title: The Pond and the Duck

Once upon a time, there was a pond. In the pond, there was a graceful duck. The duck liked to swim and play with his friends. One day, the duck decided to go on a trip. The duck went to his friend, the frog. "I will pack my bag and go on a trip," said the duck. "Do you want to come with me?" The frog thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I want to come too." So, the duck and the frog packed their bags. They put in food and toys. Then, they said goodbye to their friends at the pond. They walked and walked until they found a new pond. There, they made new friends and had a lot of fun.

Susie was very excited to see the brick. It was big and organized. She had never seen a brick so neat! She wanted to take it home with her. So she took the brick, but somebody saw her. It was the brick's owner who yelled, "That brick is mine! Put it back!" Susie was so scared that she dropped the brick on the ground. It broke into lots of small pieces. She quickly ran away and never went back to see the brick.

Once upon a time, in a cool forest, there lived a small tree. The tree wanted to grow big and strong like its friends. One day, a friendly bird came to visit the tree. "Hello, tree! I am on a tour to see all the trees in the forest. Do you want to grow big and strong?" asked the bird. "Yes, I do! How can I grow big and strong?" the tree asked the bird. The bird told the tree to be kind to everyone in the forest and share its shade and fruits. The tree listened to the bird and started to be very kind to all the animals. It shared its shade with them on hot days and gave them tasty fruits to eat. Over time, the tree grew big and strong, just like its friends. All the animals loved the tree, and the tree was very happy. The moral of the story is to be kind and share with others, and you will grow strong and be loved by everyone.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But sometimes they do bad things. They lie to Mom and Dad. They say they are sick or they did not make a mess. Mom and Dad are not happy when they lie. One day, Mom and Dad go out for dinner. They ask Grandma to watch Anna and Ben. Grandma is nice and kind. She makes them soup and bread. She reads them stories. She says they can watch a movie before bed. But Anna and Ben are naughty. They sneak into Mom and Dad's room. They see a bottle of wine on the table. They think it is juice. They open it and drink some. It tastes bad. They make faces and spit it out. They spill some on the bed and the floor. They are scared. They hide the bottle under the bed. They go back to the living room. They lie to Grandma. They say they are tired and want to sleep. Grandma tucks them in and kisses them goodnight. She does not know what they did. Mom and Dad come home. They see the wine stain on the bed and the floor. They see the bottle under the bed. They are angry. They wake up Anna and Ben. They ask them what happened. Anna and Ben are sorry. They cry and tell the truth. They say they are sorry for lying and drinking the wine. Mom and Dad hug them and forgive them. They say they love them, but they have to learn not to lie and not to touch things that are not theirs. They say they have to clean up the mess and go to bed. Anna and Ben say they love Mom and Dad too. They say they will not lie and drink wine again. They help clean up the mess. They go to bed and dream of nice things. The next day, they see a fat cat in the yard. They want to pet it and play with it. They ask Mom and Dad if they can. Mom and Dad say yes, but they have to be gentle and careful. They say the cat belongs to the neighbor. They say they have to ask the neighbor first. Anna and Ben nod and smile. They run to the neighbor's door. They knock and say hello. They ask if they can play with the fat cat. The neighbor says yes, but they have to be back before lunch. Anna and Ben say thank you and run to the fat cat. They pet it and play with it. They are happy and good. They do not lie or drink wine. They have fun with the fat cat.
