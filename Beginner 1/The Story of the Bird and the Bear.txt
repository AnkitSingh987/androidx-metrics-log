Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Bear

Once upon a time, there were two best friends - a bear and a bird. The bear and the bird loved to go out and look at the night sky together. One night they were out looking up at the stars, when suddenly the bear shouted "Look!" "What is it?" asked the bird. The bear pointed up and said "Look, a comet!" The bird squinted his eyes and looked up, and sure enough, there was a bright, clear comet in the sky. The bird said in excitement, "Let's listen and see if it makes a noise!" The pair listened carefully, but the comet was very quiet and just moved silently through the night sky. "That's so magical," said the bird. The bear nodded in agreement, content to just watch the comet pass by.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a tall boy named Tim. Tim loved to play his guitar. He played it every day and made happy songs. One day, he wanted to take his guitar to the park and play for his friends. When Tim got to the park, he saw his friend Sue. Sue was trying to tie her shoe. She was having a hard time. "Can you help me tie my shoe?" Sue asked Tim. Tim was a good friend, so he helped her tie her shoe. After her shoe was tied, Sue wanted to hear Tim play his guitar. Tim played a fun song for Sue and all their friends. They all clapped and danced. Tim was so happy to share his music with everyone. They all had a great day at the park.

Once there was a little bird named Tim. Tim liked to whistle all day. One day, while Tim was whistling, he saw a broken toy in the grass. It was a small car. Tim wanted to find the owner of the broken toy. Tim asked his friend, the squirrel, "Do you know who owns this broken toy car?" The squirrel shook his head and said, "No, but let's ask our friends underground." So, Tim and the squirrel went to the underground home of the ants. When they got to the underground, the ants were very busy. Tim showed the broken toy car to the ants and asked, "Do you know who owns this?" The ants looked at each other and then said, "We do! We found it in the grass and planned to fix it. But, it's too big for us." Tim was surprised. He didn't know ants could fix toys. Tim and the squirrel helped the ants fix the broken toy car. After it was fixed, they all played with it together. They had so much fun and became good friends. And Tim continued to whistle happily every day.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bag on the grass. It is thin and has many colors. "Look, a bag!" Tom says. "What is in it?" "I don't know. Let's see!" Lily says. They run to the bag and open it. Inside the bag, they find many toys. There are balls, cars, dolls, books, and puzzles. Tom and Lily are happy. They take out the toys and play with them. "Wow, this is fun!" Tom says. He throws a ball and catches it. "Yes, it is!" Lily says. She makes a car go vroom vroom. They play for a long time. They do not see a man come to the park. He is looking for something. "Where is my bag?" the man says. He is sad and angry. He lost his bag. He needs it for his work. He is a clown. He uses the toys to make people laugh. He sees Tom and Lily playing with his toys. He walks to them. "Hey, those are my toys!" he says. "Give me back my bag!" Tom and Lily are scared. They do not know the man. They think he is mean. "No, they are ours!" Tom says. He holds the ball. "No, they are not!" the man says. He tries to grab the bag. Tom and Lily run away. They do not want to give the bag. They like the toys. "Stop, stop!" the man says. He runs after them. Tom and Lily are fast. They step on the grass, on the path, on the bridge. They reach the other side of the park. They see their moms. "Mama, mama!" Tom and Lily say. They run to their moms and hug them. "What's wrong, my loves?" their moms say. They see the man coming. "That man wants our bag!" Tom and Lily say. They show the bag. The man stops. He is tired and out of breath. He smiles. "Hello, I'm sorry to scare you," he says. "I'm a clown. This is my bag. I use these toys for my show. I lost it this morning. Can I have it back, please?" Tom and Lily look at the man. He is not mean. He is a clown. He has a red nose, a big hat, and funny clothes. He looks funny. "Oh, you're a clown?" their moms say. They laugh. "Yes, I am," the man says. "Do you want to see a trick?" Tom and Lily nod. They are curious. The man takes out a toy flower. He gives it to Lily. "Smell it," he says. Lily smells the flower. It squirts water on her nose. She giggles. The man takes out a toy horn. He gives it to Tom. "Squeeze it," he says. Tom squeezes the horn. It makes a loud noise. He laughs. The man does more tricks. He makes a balloon animal, he juggles balls, he tells jokes. Tom and Lily clap and cheer. They have fun. "Thank you, thank you," the man says. He bows. "Thank you, clown," Tom and Lily say. They smile. They give the bag back to the man. They are sorry they took it. "I'm sorry, too," the man says. He hugs them. "You are very nice kids. Do you want a souvenir?" He gives them each a toy. Tom gets a car. Lily gets a doll. "Wow, thank you, clown!" Tom and Lily say. They are happy. They say goodbye to the man. He waves and leaves. Tom and Lily show their toys to their moms. They are proud. "Look, mama, a car!" Tom says. "Look, mama, a doll!" Lily says. Their moms hug them. They are proud, too. "What a nice story," their moms say. "You made a new friend today." "Yes, we did," Tom and Lily say. "He is a clown. He is funny." They go home. They play with their toys. They remember the clown. They laugh. They are happy. The end.
