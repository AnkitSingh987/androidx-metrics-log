Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Garden

Anna liked to play in the garden. She liked to smell the flowers and pick them for her mom. She saw a big daisy with many white petals and a yellow middle. She ran to it and pulled it out of the ground. But the daisy was not happy. It felt a sharp pain when Anna tore it from its home. It wanted to stay with its friends, the other flowers and the bees. It felt sad and scared in Anna's hand. Anna did not know the daisy was sad. She thought it was pretty and smelled good. She wanted to show it to her mom. She ran to the house with the daisy in her hand. But she ran too fast and tripped on a stone. She fell down and let go of the daisy. The daisy flew in the air and landed on the ground. It was hurt and broken. Some of its petals were torn off and its stem was bent. It could not stand up or drink water. It felt very weak and cold. It knew it was going to die. Anna got up and saw the daisy on the ground. She felt sorry for the daisy. She picked it up and tried to fix it. But it was too late. The daisy was dead. Anna cried and said sorry to the daisy. She wished she had not torn it from the garden. She buried it under a tree and said a prayer. She learned to be gentle with the flowers and not hurt them. She hoped the daisy would forgive her and be happy in heaven.

Once upon a time, there was a little pig named Pippa. Pippa was a very careful pig. She liked to play in the mud and dream all day. Pippa dreamed of finding a big, red ball to play with. She thought about the ball every day and night. One day, while Pippa was playing in the mud, she saw something shiny under a big tree. Pippa was excited and ran to see what it was. She was very careful not to slip in the mud. As she got closer, she saw that it was a big, red ball just like in her dream! Pippa was so happy to find the ball. She played with it all day long. She rolled it, kicked it, and even jumped on it. Pippa knew that her dream had come true. From that day on, Pippa was always careful with her big, red ball and remembered to dream big.

Christy was going to the park with her mom. She brought a bag to carry all the things she wanted to take with her. Before they left, her mom wanted to explain what was inside the bag. First, she said, was a snack. It was made of crunchy orange carrots. Christy was so excited to eat her snack. Next, her mom pulled out a big orange ball. It was a soccer ball. She told Christy she could kick it with her friends at the park. Lastly, her mom handed Christy a special orange hat. The hat was to keep the sun out of her eyes. Christy was so excited to show her friends her new hat. Now that Christy knew what was in her bag, she was ready to go to the park and have some fun.

Ben and Mia are best friends. They like to play together at the park. They like to swing, slide and run. They also like to guess sounds. One day, they hear a loud sound from the sky. They look up and see a big bird. It has a long neck and big wings. It makes a honk-honk sound. "What is that?" Ben asks. "I know, I know!" Mia says. "It is a goose. A goose is a popular bird. It flies south when it is cold." "Wow, you are good at guessing sounds!" Ben says. "Can you guess this sound?" He makes a bzzz-bzzz sound with his mouth. "Hmm, let me think." Mia says. "Is it a bee? A bee is a small bug. It makes honey and stings." "Yes, you are right!" Ben says. "You are very smart, Mia. I like playing with you." "I like playing with you too, Ben." Mia says. "You are very fun, Ben. Let's guess more sounds!"
