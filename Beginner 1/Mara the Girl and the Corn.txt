Category: Beginner
Title: Mara the Girl and the Corn

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mara. She was three years old and loved eating corn. One day, she saw some corn in the kitchen. She was so excited. She wanted to have it for snack. Mara grabbed the corn, but it was very hot. She jumped back in shock and looked really upset. Mama saw Mara and said, "Don't be upset. That corn has melted." Mara nodded and asked, "How did it melt?" Mama smiled and said, "It got too hot and it melted. Do you want to help me make more?" Mara smiled and said, "Yes! I want some more corn!" Mama laughed and said, "Alright, let's go get some more corn!" So the two of them went to the store and got some more corn. Mara was so happy. She ate her corn and smiled. She enjoyed every single bite.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little girl named Mia. Mia loved to mix colors in her big tube. She had red, blue, and yellow paint. Mia liked to see what new colors she could make. One day, Mia's friend, Tom, came over to play. "What are you doing, Mia?" Tom asked. "I am mixing colors in my tube," Mia said with a big smile. "Do you want to help me?" Tom and Mia mixed red and blue paint in the tube. They made a pretty purple color. Then they mixed yellow and blue paint. They made a bright green color. Mia and Tom were very happy with their new colors. At the end of the day, Mia and Tom showed their mixed colors to their moms. Their moms were so proud of them. Mia and Tom had so much fun mixing colors in the tube. They couldn't wait to do it again!

One day, a cat and a mouse were in the park. The cat was sad because he could not catch the mouse. The mouse was very fast. The cat had an idea. He wanted to make a trap to catch the mouse. The cat and the mouse began to discuss the trap. The cat said, "I will make a trap with cheese. You will come to eat the cheese and then I will catch you." The mouse said, "No! I do not like that plan. I do not want to be caught." The cat made the trap anyway. He put cheese in the trap and waited for the mouse. The mouse saw the trap and was scared. He did not want to be caught by the cat. But the mouse was very hungry and wanted the cheese. The mouse went to the trap and tried to take the cheese. The trap caught the mouse. The cat was happy, but the mouse was very sad. The cat took the mouse away and the mouse never saw his friends again.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play with their dog, Spot. Spot is a big dog. He can run fast and jump high. He can also bow. Ben and Lily taught him to bow. They give him a reward when he bows. They give him a bone or a ball. One day, Ben and Lily want to show Spot to their teacher, Miss Lee. They bring Spot to school. They wait for Miss Lee in the yard. They see other kids and other dogs. The other dogs are noisy. They bark and growl. Spot does not like the noisy dogs. He feels scared. Ben and Lily say to Spot, "Don't be scared, Spot. You are a good dog. You can bow. Miss Lee will like you. We will give you a reward." They try to make Spot calm. They pet him and hug him. Miss Lee comes to the yard. She smiles and says, "Hello, Ben and Lily. Who is this?" She points to Spot. Ben and Lily say, "This is Spot, our dog. He can bow. Watch!" They tell Spot to bow. Spot tries to bow. But he is too scared. He hears the noisy dogs. He sees the other kids. He forgets to bow. He runs away. Ben and Lily are sad. They call Spot. They chase him. But Spot is too fast. He runs out of the yard. He runs into the street. He does not see the car. The car hits him. Spot is hurt. He does not move. Ben and Lily cry. Miss Lee is shocked. She says, "Oh no, Spot. I'm so sorry, Ben and Lily. This is a bad ending."
