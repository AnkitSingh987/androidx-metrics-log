Category: Beginner
Title: The Penny and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little penny. The penny had a friend named Tim. Tim was a small boy with a big smile. They liked to play together. One day, Tim and the penny went to the park. They saw a big tree. Tim said, "Let's sit under the tree and eat our lunch." So, they sat under the tree and had a delicious lunch. Tim had a sandwich and the penny had a tiny cookie. After lunch, Tim and the penny played catch with a ball. They had so much fun. Soon, it was time to go home. Tim put the penny in his pocket and they walked back to their small house. They were happy to have spent a fun day together.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big tree with red leaves. They want to make a picture with the leaves. "Look, Anna, the leaves are so pretty!" Ben says. "Yes, they are. Let's pick some and spread them on the ground. Then we can draw on them with sticks," Anna says. They pick some leaves and spread them on the ground. They find some sticks and start to draw. They make faces, animals, and flowers. "Wow, you are good at drawing, Ben. I like your lion," Anna says. "Thank you, Anna. I like your flower. It is very kind of you to say that," Ben says. They are having fun. They do not see a little child who is watching them. The child is sad. He has no friends. He wants to play with them, but he is shy. "Hello, what are you doing?" the child says. Anna and Ben look up. They see the child. "Hi, we are making pictures with leaves and sticks. Do you want to join us?" Anna says. "Yes, please. That sounds fun. I am Tom. What are your names?" the child says. "I am Anna and this is Ben. We are happy to meet you, Tom. Come, we have many leaves and sticks. You can make your own picture," Ben says. Tom smiles. He comes and sits with them. He picks some leaves and a stick. He starts to draw. He makes a star. "Look, I made a star. I like stars. They are bright and shiny," Tom says. "That is a beautiful star, Tom. You are good at drawing, too. We like stars, too. They are like friends in the sky," Anna says. "Yes, they are. And you are like friends on the ground. Thank you for playing with me. You are very kind," Tom says. They hug each other. They are happy. They have made a new friend. They continue to play and draw. They share their leaves and sticks. They spread their joy.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys. Ben has a cool top. It is red and blue and has a star on it. Mia has a ring. It is shiny and pink and has a flower on it. One day, they go to the park. They find a big hill. Ben says, "Let's play on the hill. I can spin my top and you can roll your ring." Mia says, "OK, that sounds fun." They climb up the hill. Ben puts his top on the ground and gives it a push. The top spins fast and makes a humming sound. Mia puts her ring on the ground and gives it a nudge. The ring rolls down the hill and makes a tinkling sound. They run after their toys. They laugh and shout. They have a good time. They catch their toys at the bottom of the hill. Ben says, "That was cool. Let's do it again." Mia says, "Yes, let's do it again. I like your top and you like my ring. We can share them." They smile and hug. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry. He went to the kitchen to make a sandwich. He took some bread and put peanut butter on it. The peanut butter was very dry. Tim wanted to make it better. Tim saw a knife on the table. He picked it up and said, "I will use this to increase the peanut butter on my sandwich." He spread more peanut butter on the bread. Now, it was not so dry. Tim's mom came into the kitchen and saw Tim with the knife. She said, "Good job, Tim! You are being careful with the knife." Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Mom. I made my sandwich better." Tim ate his sandwich and was very happy.
