Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Farm

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm with his mom. They saw many animals, but Tim liked the big turkey the most. The turkey was incredible, and Tim wanted to be its friend. Tim's mom said, "Don't get too close, the turkey might bite." Tim did not listen and went closer to the turkey. He wanted to touch it, but the turkey was scared. The turkey bit Tim's finger. Tim cried and ran to his mom. She hugged him and said, "I told you the turkey might bite." Tim learned to listen to his mom and be careful with animals.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Let's go on the slide!" Tom says. "OK!" Jen says. They run to the slide. But there is a sign. The sign says: "You must be this tall to go on the slide." There is a line on the sign. Tom and Jen are not tall enough. They are too small. "That's not fair!" Tom says. "I know!" Jen says. They look around. They see a bench. The bench is near the slide. They have an idea. "Let's stand on the bench!" Tom says. "Good idea!" Jen says. They go to the bench. They stand on the bench. Now they are tall enough. They smile. They go to the slide. They wait for their turn. They are happy. But then, a man comes. The man is big. The man wears a hat and a badge. The man is a cop. The cop sees Tom and Jen. The cop is not happy. "Hey, you two!" the cop says. "What are you doing?" Tom and Jen are scared. They look at the cop. "We want to go on the slide," Tom says. "But you are too small," the cop says. "You can't stand on the bench. That's against the law." "The law?" Tom and Jen say. "Yes, the law," the cop says. "The law is a rule. You have to follow the rule. The rule is for your safety. The slide is not safe for you. You could get hurt." Tom and Jen are sad. They don't want to get hurt. They don't want to break the rule. "Sorry, cop," Tom says. "Sorry, cop," Jen says. They get off the bench. They walk away from the slide. They look for another game. The cop is successful. He did his job. He helped Tom and Jen. He smiles. He goes to the next kid. The next kid is too big for the slide. The cop has more work to do.

Once upon a time, there was a mill. The mill was near a big tree. The mill had a big wheel that went round and round. People came to the mill to make flour from wheat. One day, a man came to the mill. He had a serious face. He wanted to show something to the people. He had a big board with pictures. He wanted to display the pictures near the mill. The man put the board near the tree. The people came to see the pictures. They were happy to see the board. The serious man smiled. He liked to make people happy. The mill and the tree became a fun place for everyone.

One day, Tom went to the beach with his mom. He was very excited to play in the water and see the big waves. Tom's mom said, "Before we go in the water, we need to study the waves to make sure it's safe." Tom was a little tired from the drive, but he wanted to play. As they studied the waves, they saw a big fish jump out of the water. The fish said, "Hello, Tom! I am a magic fish. I can make your tired go away!" Tom was surprised and asked, "Really? How?" The fish said, "Just say 'I am not tired' three times, and you will feel better." Tom said, "I am not tired, I am not tired, I am not tired." Suddenly, he felt awake and full of energy. He thanked the magic fish and played in the waves with his mom. They had a great day at the beach, and Tom never felt tired again.
