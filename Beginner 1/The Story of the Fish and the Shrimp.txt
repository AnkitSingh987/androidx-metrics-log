Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Fish and the Shrimp

One day, a little shrimp was swimming in the sea. The shrimp wanted to be brave and strong. It saw a big fish and thought, "I want to be big like that fish." So, the shrimp tried to stretch itself to be big and strong. The shrimp swam up to the big fish and said, "Look at me! I am big and strong now!" The big fish laughed and said, "You are still small, little shrimp. You cannot be big like me." The shrimp was sad, but it did not give up. It wanted to show everyone that it was brave. Then, one day, a net came and caught the big fish. The shrimp saw this and wanted to help. It swam very fast and used its small size to go inside the net. The shrimp found a hole in the net and showed it to the big fish. The big fish said, "Thank you, brave shrimp! You saved me!" The shrimp was happy because it was brave, even if it was small.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat. The cat had soft fur. She liked to play with a ball. One day, the ball went far away. The shy cat went to find the ball. The cat looked and looked, but she could not find the ball. She was sad. She missed her ball. The shy cat walked and walked. She saw a big dog. The dog had short fur. The dog wanted to play with the shy cat. The shy cat was scared of the big dog. She did not want to play. The dog got mad. The shy cat tried to run away. She missed her ball even more. The big dog chased the shy cat. The shy cat could not find her way home. She was lost. The big dog was still mad. The shy cat was very sad. She missed her ball and her home. The shy cat never found her ball or her home again.

Lily and Anna are best friends. They like to play dress up with their dolls. They have many clothes for their dolls. Some are pink, some are blue, some are yellow. They like to make their dolls look pretty. One day, they find a new dress for their dolls. It is red and shiny. It has a big bow on the front. Lily thinks it is the most beautiful dress ever. She wants to put it on her doll. Anna thinks it is the most ugly dress ever. She wants to throw it away. They disagree about the dress. Lily says, "It is the best fashion for my doll. She will look powerful and pretty." Anna says, "It is the worst fashion for your doll. She will look silly and stupid." They start to argue. They raise their voices. They pull the dress from each other. The dress rips in half. They both cry. They are very sad and angry. They do not want to be friends anymore. They take their dolls and go to their own rooms. They do not play dress up together again. They do not have fun. They are lonely.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play on the big pitch near his house. One sunny day, Spot saw a big cat named Fluffy sitting on the edge of the pitch. Spot wanted to play with Fluffy, but Fluffy did not want to play. Spot ran up to Fluffy and barked, "Come play with me!" Fluffy hissed and said, "No, I don't want to play with you, nosy dog!" This made Spot feel sad, but he did not give up. The next day, Spot saw Fluffy again on the pitch. This time, Spot ran around Fluffy in big circles, hoping Fluffy would join in. To Spot's surprise, Fluffy started to run too! They both ran and played together on the pitch, and soon they became good friends. From that day on, nosy Spot and Fluffy had lots of fun playing together.

One day, Tom and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They wanted to play with their ball. But when they got there, it started to rain. Tom said, "Oh no, the rain will delay our play time." Spot barked, as if he agreed. They saw a big tree and ran under it for some space to hide from the rain. Tom said, "Let's wait here until the rain stops." Spot wagged his tail and sat down. While waiting, Tom found a lemon on the ground. He picked it up and took a bite. "Yuck!" he said. "This is sour!" After a while, the rain stopped. Tom and Spot were very happy. They played with their ball and had lots of fun. Tom threw the ball far away and Spot ran to catch it. They played until the sun went down. Then, they went home, tired but happy.
