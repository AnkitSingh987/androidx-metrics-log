Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to spend time with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They liked to play on the road near their house. One day, Max and Lily saw a big box on the road. Max said, "Lily, what is in the box?" Lily said, "I don't know, Max. Let's find out!" They were very excited to see what was inside. When they opened the box, they found a lot of toys. Max and Lily were so happy! But then, a big wind came and blew the toys away. Max and Lily felt frustrated. Max said, "Don't worry, Lily. We will find the toys." Max and Lily looked for the toys all day. At last, they found them under a big tree. Max and Lily were so happy to have their toys back. They played with the toys all day and had a lot of fun. And from that day on, they always kept their toys safe inside their house.

Sara and Tom are going to visit their grandma. They have to pack their bags with clothes and toys. Sara has a big bag and Tom has a small bag. "Tom, why is your bag so small?" Sara asks. "You need more things for grandma's house." Tom says, "I don't need more things. I have my bear and my car. They are my best friends." Sara shakes her head. "You are silly, Tom. You need more clothes and books and snacks. What if you get cold or bored or hungry?" Tom shrugs. "I don't care. I like my bag. It has a hole in it. I can see my bear and my car. They smile at me." Sara rolls her eyes. "You are very silly, Tom. Your bag has a hole in it. That is not good. Your things can fall out. Then you will lose them. And grandma will be sad." Tom frowns. "No, I won't lose them. I will hold my bag tight. And grandma will be happy. She likes my bear and my car. She says they are cute." Sara sighs. "Okay, Tom. But be careful with your bag. Come on, let's go. Mom and dad are waiting for us. They have the tall car. It can fit all our bags and us." Tom nods. He grabs his bag and follows Sara. He is excited to see grandma. He hopes she has cookies and milk for him and his bear and his car. He loves grandma. She is very nice.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big hill with a lot of grass and flowers. They wanted to go to the top and see what was there. "Let's crawl up the hill," Lily said. "It will be fun." "OK," Tom said. "But it looks difficult. The hill is very high and steep." They started to crawl on their hands and knees. They felt the grass tickle their skin and the sun warm their backs. They crawled and crawled, but the top seemed very far away. "Are we there yet?" Tom asked. "No, not yet," Lily said. "But look, I see something." She pointed to a big shape that was moving on the other side of the hill. It was a giant. He was taller than the trees and had a long beard and a big hat. He was carrying a basket full of fruits and vegetables. "Wow, a giant!" Tom said. "He is so big and strong. I wonder what he is doing." "Maybe he is going home," Lily said. "Or maybe he is looking for someone to share his food with." They crawled faster, hoping to see the giant better. But as they got closer, they heard a loud voice. "Who is there?" the giant said. "I hear someone crawling on my hill. Is it a mouse? Or a rabbit? Or a child?" Lily and Tom were scared. They did not want the giant to see them or catch them. They turned around and crawled back down the hill as fast as they could. "Let's go, let's go!" Lily said. "Yes, let's go!" Tom said. They reached the bottom of the hill and ran to their parents, who were sitting on a bench. They told them what they saw and heard. "A giant?" their parents said. "Are you sure?" "Yes, we are sure," Lily and Tom said. "He was very big and loud. And he had a basket full of food." Their parents smiled and hugged them. "Maybe you were dreaming," they said. "Or maybe you have a very good imagination. But we are proud of you for trying to crawl up the hill. It was difficult, but you did it." Lily and Tom smiled too. They were happy and tired. They decided to play something else in the park. They did not want to crawl up the hill again. They did not want to meet the giant again.
