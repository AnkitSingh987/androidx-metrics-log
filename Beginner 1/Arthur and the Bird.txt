Category: Beginner
Title: Arthur and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little bird called Arthur. Arthur had beautiful feathers and he loved to soar high into the sky. One day, Arthur was flying especially high when he saw something that made his heart skip a beat – a glittering gem! He flew closer and closer, desperate to see if it was real. Suddenly, he realized that the gem was attached to a string. He flew nearer and saw that the string was tied to a helpless kitten. The kitten was meowing for help. Arthur flew down and said, “Don’t worry, little one. I will help you.” With one sharp peck, Arthur snapped the string to free the kitten. The kitten was very happy! It purred and thanked Arthur for rescuing it. Arthur smiled and soaring higher, warmed by the knowledge that he had done something right.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the garden. The garden has many flowers and trees. Lily and Tom smell the flowers and touch the leaves. They see a bird and a butterfly. "Look, Tom, a bird!" Lily says. "It is blue and yellow. It is pretty." "Yes, Lily, it is pretty," Tom says. "It sings a sweet song. Do you hear it?" Lily nods. She hears the bird sing. She likes the song. She wants to sing too. "Tom, can we sing with the bird?" Lily asks. She requests to sing. "OK, Lily, we can sing," Tom says. He agrees to sing. Lily and Tom sing with the bird. They make a happy sound. The bird likes their sound. It flies closer to them. It lands on a branch near them. It sings louder. "Hello, bird," Lily and Tom say. They greet the bird. "Hello, Lily and Tom," the bird says. It talks to them. Lily and Tom are surprised. They did not know the bird can talk. They are happy. They make a new friend. They talk and sing with the bird in the garden. They have fun.

One day, a swan was very hungry. She could not find any food in the water. She saw a girl cooking by the lake. The girl was cooking yummy food. The swan wanted some food too. The swan went to the girl and asked for some food. The girl said no. The swan was sad. The girl saw the swan was sad and thought about it. She decided to share her food with the swan. The swan was grateful and happy. The girl and the swan became friends. They shared food and played together. The girl learned to be kind and share with others. The swan learned to say thank you and be a good friend.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. Sue had a red bag with her. When they got to the park, Sue's mom said, "Sue, let's unpack your red bag and see what's inside." Sue opened her red bag and took out a ball, a toy car, and a book. Sue's mom said, "Good job, Sue! You unpacked your bag. Now, let's play." Sue and her mom played with the ball and the toy car. They had a lot of fun. Then, Sue saw a dog. She pointed at the dog and said, "Look, mom! A dog!" Sue's mom looked at the point Sue was showing and said, "Yes, that's a cute dog!" They played at the park all day and had a great time. When it was time to go home, Sue packed her red bag and said, "I love the park, mom!" And her mom smiled and said, "I do too, Sue!"

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a creative boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim's mom said, "We need to hurry to get your haircut." Tim did not want a haircut. He liked his long hair. On the way to the haircut place, Tim thought of a plan. He saw a big tree and said, "Look, mom! A cat is stuck in the tree!" His mom stopped the car to help the cat. While she was busy, Tim ran away to hide. He did not want his haircut. Tim hid for a long time, but his mom found him. She was not happy. They went to the haircut place, but it was closed. Tim's mom was sad. They went home, and Tim still had long hair. The next day, Tim got gum stuck in his hair. It was very bad. Tim had to cut his hair very short. He did not like it.
