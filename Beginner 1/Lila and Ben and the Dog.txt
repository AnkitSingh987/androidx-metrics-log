Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Dog

Lila and Ben were playing in the sandbox. They liked to make castles and roads with the sand and their toys. But then a big dog came and jumped into the sandbox. The dog was filthy. He had mud and dirt all over his fur. He shook his body and made the sand fly everywhere. "Hey, go away!" Lila shouted at the dog. "You are ruining our game!" The dog did not listen. He barked and wagged his tail. He thought the sandbox was fun. He dug holes and knocked down the castles and roads. He grabbed some of the toys in his mouth and ran away. Ben was very angry. He frowns and stomps his feet. He liked his toys. He wanted them back. "Bad dog! Bad dog!" Ben yelled. "Give me back my toys!" Lila and Ben chased the dog. They ran out of the sandbox and into the grass. The dog was faster than them. He ran and ran until he reached a fence. He dropped the toys and jumped over the fence. Lila and Ben stopped. They could not go over the fence. They looked at their toys. They were filthy too. They had mud and dirt and dog spit on them. Lila and Ben were sad. They did not want to play with their filthy toys. They did not want to play in the filthy sandbox. They wanted to go home and take a bath. They walked back to the sandbox. They picked up their filthy toys. They carried them to their mom. "Mom, look what the dog did!" Lila said. "Mom, can we have a bath?" Ben asked. Mom looked at them. She saw their filthy clothes and faces and hands. She saw their filthy toys and the filthy sandbox. She shook her head and sighed. "Of course you can have a bath," she said. "But first, let me take a picture of you. You look like you had a very adventurous day."

One day, a poor cat found a mask. The cat was very hungry. It wanted to eat something. It put on the mask and went to find food. The cat saw a big cake. It wanted to eat the cake. But the mask was magic! When the cat tried to eat, the cake turned into a big fish! The cat was surprised. It tried to eat the fish. But the mask was still magic! The fish turned into a big bowl of milk. The cat was happy. It liked to drink milk. The cat and the mask became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She loved watching movies on her computer. One day, Sarah decided to load a movie. She clicked on the dark movie icon on the computer and it started to load. Sarah was so excited that she started to jump around her room. But then, a scary noise came from the computer. Sarah started to cry and hid behind her bed. Suddenly, Sarah heard a voice. It said, "Hello Sarah! Don't be scared. I'm just loading the movie". Sarah felt relieved. Sarah peeped out from behind the bed to see who was talking to her. It was the computer! She smiled as the movie finished loading. It was finally time to watch the movie! Sarah was so happy that she started clapping her hands. The End.

Once upon a time, in a green park, there lived a little bird named Bob. Bob loved to swing on the twigs of a big tree. One day, when the sun was up, Bob saw a big cat. The cat was hungry and wanted to catch Bob. Bob said, "Please, don't eat me. I am just a small bird. Let's be friends and play together." The cat thought about it and said, "Okay, let's play. I like to swing too." So, they both started to swing on the twigs, having fun. But the cat was not a good friend. While they were swinging, the cat caught Bob with his paw. The cat was happy because he was not hungry anymore. Bob was very sad, and he should have been more careful. The end.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play sport in the park. They have a ball and a net. They part the net in two. One part is for Lily, one part is for Tom. They throw the ball over the net and catch it. "Your part is bigger than mine!" Tom says. He is not happy. He wants a bigger part of the net. "No, it is not!" Lily says. She is happy. She likes her part of the net. "Yes, it is!" Tom says. He is angry. He pulls the net to make his part bigger. "Stop it!" Lily says. She is sad. She pulls the net back to make her part the same. The net breaks. The ball rolls away. Lily and Tom have no sport to play. They have no net and no ball. They have no fun. They look at each other. They feel sorry. They say sorry. "I am sorry, Lily. I was mean. I don't need a bigger part of the net. I just want to play with you." Tom says. "I am sorry, Tom. I was mean too. I don't care about the net. I just want to play with you too." Lily says. They hug. They are friends again. They find the ball. They play a new sport. They kick the ball to each other. They laugh. They have fun. They are happy.
