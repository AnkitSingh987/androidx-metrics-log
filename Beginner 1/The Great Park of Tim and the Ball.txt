Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Park of Tim and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a lucky boy. He had a big red ball that he loved to play with. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his ball. At the park, he met a girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty blue hat. Tim said, "Hi, I like your hat!" Sue smiled and said, "Thank you! I like your ball." They played together all day, and had lots of fun. As the sun started to go down, Tim's mom came to the park to take him home. Sue's mom came too. Tim's mom said, "It's time to go home, Tim." Before they left, Sue said, "I had fun today. Let's play again soon!" Tim smiled and said, "I will remind my mom to bring me back." They waved goodbye and went home, full of wonder and happiness from their fun day at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with yummy fruit. Tim wanted to pick the fruit, but he was too small. His mom said, "Wait, I will help you." Tim's friend, a naughty squirrel, saw the fruit too. The squirrel wanted to take it all for himself. He ran up the tree and started to throw the fruit at Tim and his mom. Tim's mom said, "Oh no, that squirrel is being naughty!" Just then, a big wind came and started to freeze everything. The fruit, the squirrel, and the tree all froze. Tim and his mom felt very cold. Tim said, "Mom, let's go home and get warm." When they got home, they had hot cocoa and talked about their day. Tim said, "I am happy we are home and warm, but I hope the squirrel and the fruit are not frozen forever." His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, they will be fine when the wind goes away."

Once upon a time, there was a popular toy boat. The boat loved to sail on the water. One sunny day, the boat met a big duck. The duck had a funny head. The duck and the boat became friends. "Hi, I am a duck," said the duck. "I have a funny head. Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, I want to play," said the boat. "I love to sail on the water. Let's play together!" So, the duck and the boat played together. They sailed on the water all day. They had lots of fun. The sun was shining, and the water was nice. The duck and the boat were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pretty rose in her garden. Sue loved her rose very much. She would water it and take care of it every day. One day, Sue's friend Tom came to play. Tom saw the rose and said, "Wow, that's a pretty rose! Can I touch it?" Sue said, "Yes, but be easy with it. It is very special to me." Tom touched the rose, but he was not easy. The rose fell and got dirty. Sue was very sad. She tried to wipe the dirt off the rose, but it was too late. The rose was hurt and did not look pretty anymore. Sue and Tom were both sad that they could not fix the rose.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a cat named Tom. Tom was a fine cat with soft, fluffy fur. He loved to play in the sun and nap under the big tree in the park. One day, Tom found a tasty pastry on the ground. It was round and smelled very good. Tom wanted to eat the pastry, but he was not sure if it was okay. He looked around to see if anyone lost it. Tom saw a little girl crying nearby. She was sad because she dropped her pastry. Tom picked up the pastry with his mouth and carried it to the girl. The girl was happy to have her pastry back and thanked Tom. After giving the pastry to the girl, Tom went back to the big tree in the park. He felt good for helping the girl. Tom took a long nap under the tree, dreaming of more fun days in the sun.
