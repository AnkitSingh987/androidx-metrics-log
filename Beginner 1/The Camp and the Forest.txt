Category: Beginner
Title: The Camp and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a pretty forest, there was a camp. In the camp, there were many kids. They liked to play and have fun. One day, they wanted to play a new game. They called it "Who can lie the best?" One kid, named Tim, was very good at lying. He said, "I saw a big bear in the forest!" The other kids were scared. They did not want to play near the bear. But Tim just laughed. He said, "I was only lying! There is no bear." The kids were not happy with Tim. They did not like his lie. They wanted to play and have fun, but they were too scared. They told Tim to stop lying. Tim felt sad. He knew that lying was not good. From that day on, Tim stopped lying. He played with the other kids and had a lot of fun. They all enjoyed the camp and the pretty forest. And they never played "Who can lie the best?" again.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. Lily saw a big spider on a web. She wanted to catch it and put it in a jar. "Look, Ben, a spider!" she said. "Let's get it!" Ben was scared of spiders. He did not want to go near it. He said, "No, Lily, leave it alone. Spiders can bite. They are not toys." Lily did not listen. She ran to the spider and tried to grab it. But the spider was fast and smart. It ran away from her hand and bit her finger. Lily cried out in pain and dropped the jar. "Ow, ow, ow!" she sobbed. "The spider hurt me!" Ben ran to her and hugged her. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Are you okay? Let me see your finger." He looked at her finger and saw a red mark. He said, "We need to tell Mom. She can help you. Come on, let's go inside." They went inside and told Mom what happened. Mom was serious. She said, "Lily, you should not try to catch spiders. They are wild animals. They need to live in their own homes. They do not like to be bothered. You could get sick or hurt from their bites. You need to respect them and leave them alone." She cleaned Lily's finger and put a band-aid on it. She gave Lily a hug and a kiss. She said, "I hope you learned your lesson. Next time, listen to Ben. He was right. He was a good friend to you." Lily nodded. She said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I'm sorry, Ben. I was wrong. I will not catch spiders anymore. I will be nice to them and let them be." She hugged Ben and said, "Thank you for helping me. You are a brave and kind friend. I love you." Ben smiled and said, "I love you too, Lily. And I'm glad you are okay." They hugged and went back to the garden. They played with other bugs and flowers. They did not bother the spider. They were happy and safe.

Ben and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys and their dog, Max. But today, Mom says they have to nap. She says they are too tired and cranky. "No nap!" Ben says. He throws his car on the floor. "I want to play!" "No nap!" Lily says. She kicks her doll. "I want to walk!" Mom sighs. She picks up the toys and puts them away. She takes Ben and Lily to their room. She gives them each a hug and a kiss. She says, "You need to nap, my loves. You will feel better when you wake up. And then we can go for a walk with Max. OK?" Ben and Lily pout. They do not want to nap. They want to have fun. They lie down on their beds. Mom turns off the light and closes the door. But Ben and Lily do not sleep. They whisper to each other. They giggle and make faces. They throw their pillows and blankets. They are very noisy. Max hears them. He barks and scratches the door. He wants to play with them. He is very noisy too. Mom hears them. She opens the door. She is not happy. She says, "Ben and Lily, you are not napping. You are making too much noise. You are disturbing Max and me. You have to be quiet and sleep. Or else, no walk later. Do you understand?" Ben and Lily nod. They are sorry. They do not want to make Mom angry. They do not want to miss the walk. They say, "OK, Mom. We will nap. We will be quiet. We love you." Mom smiles. She says, "I love you too, my loves. Now, sleep well." She closes the door again. Ben and Lily snuggle with their stuffed animals. They close their eyes. They are quiet. They nap.
