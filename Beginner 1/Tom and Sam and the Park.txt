Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Park

One day, a big cat named Tom and a little dog named Sam went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. They saw a shower where people could wash off after playing in the sand. Tom said, "I'm hot. I want to lay under the shower and cool off." Sam agreed and said, "Me too. Let's go slowly so we don't get too tired." They walked slow to the shower and took turns standing under the water. It felt so nice and cool. They laughed and splashed each other. After they were done, Tom and Sam lay under a big tree to rest. They talked about their fun day and how much they liked the shower. They promised to come back to the park and play again soon.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to stay with her aunt. Her aunt lived in a small house with a big yard. Lucy liked to play in the yard with her aunt's dog, Spot. One morning, Lucy and Spot went outside to play. They ran and jumped in the soft grass. Lucy said to Spot, "I like to stay with my aunt. It is fun here!" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Woof! Woof!" Spot was happy too. In the evening, Lucy's mom came to pick her up. Lucy was sad to leave, but her mom said, "Don't worry, you can come stay with your aunt again soon." Lucy hugged her aunt and said, "Thank you for a fine day!" Her aunt smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lucy. Come back soon!"

Tom had a pet fish named Bubbles. He liked to watch Bubbles swim in his bowl and feed him flakes every day. He also liked to fill the bowl with fresh water when it got dirty. One day, Tom went to visit his friend Lily. Lily had a pet dog named Spot. Spot was big and furry and liked to run and play. He barked and wagged his tail when he saw Tom. "Hi, Tom! Do you want to play with Spot?" Lily asked. "Sure!" Tom said. He liked dogs, but he never had one. Tom and Lily threw a ball for Spot and he fetched it. They also rubbed his belly and scratched his ears. Spot licked their faces and made them laugh. Tom had fun, but he also felt something else. He felt envious. He wished he had a pet dog like Spot. He thought Spot was more fun than Bubbles. He wondered if Bubbles was lonely in his bowl. When it was time to go home, Tom said goodbye to Lily and Spot. "Bye, Tom! Come again soon!" Lily said. "Bye, Lily! Bye, Spot!" Tom said. He walked home with his mom. He felt sad and quiet. "What's wrong, Tom?" his mom asked. "I want a pet dog like Lily," Tom said. His mom smiled and hugged him. "I know, Tom. Dogs are nice, but so are fish. You have a special pet that loves you. Bubbles is waiting for you at home. He misses you when you are gone. Why don't you fill his bowl with fresh water and talk to him? He likes to hear your voice." Tom nodded. He felt a little better. He remembered that Bubbles was his friend. He decided to give him some extra flakes and a new toy. When he got home, he ran to his room and saw Bubbles swimming in his bowl. Bubbles saw him and blew some bubbles. He looked happy. "Hi, Bubbles! I'm sorry I was gone. I missed you too. You are a good pet. I love you," Tom said. He filled his bowl with fresh water and gave him some flakes and a toy. He watched him play and swim. He smiled. He was not envious anymore. He was happy. He had a pet fish named Bubbles.

Once upon a time, there was a large, friendly bear. The bear lived in a big, green forest. The bear had a small house with a red door. On the door, there was a lock to keep him safe. One day, the bear saw a boy with a toy that could shoot. The boy wanted to shoot at the birds in the trees. The bear did not like this. He wanted to teach the boy a lesson. The bear took the lock from his door and put it on the toy. The boy could not shoot anymore. The boy was sad, but he learned that it is not nice to hurt others. The bear and the boy became good friends and played together in the big, green forest.
