Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fruit

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big tree with red fruit. Tim wanted to taste the fruit. He picked one and took a bite. "Yum!" he said, "This fruit is very good!" Later, it got dark outside. Tim saw a poor, sad dog under the tree. The dog looked hungry. Tim wanted to help the dog. He gave the dog some of the red fruit. The dog ate it and smiled. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog started to talk! "Thank you, Tim!" said the dog. "I was a person, but a witch made me a dog. The red fruit broke the spell!" Tim and the dog became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a tiny box. Inside the box, there were many small sticks called matches. One day, a little boy found the box. He did not know what the matches were for, but he thought they were fun to play with. The little boy took a match and tried to make it work. He did not know that matches can make fire. When he made the fire, he was scared. The fire started to grow and it was terrible. The little boy knew he had to do something to stop the fire. The little boy got some water and threw it on the fire. The water helped to destroy the fire. The boy was happy that the terrible fire was gone. He learned that playing with matches was not a good idea. From that day on, the little boy never played with matches again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very foolish boy. He loved to play all day and did not like to listen to his mom and dad. One day, Tim found a big cut on his arm. He did not know what to do. He did not want to ask his mom and dad for help. He thought he could fix it himself. Tim tried to hide the cut, but it got worse and worse. It hurt a lot. Finally, he had to ask his mom and dad for help. But it was too late. The cut got very bad and Tim was very sad. If only he had asked for help sooner, he would not have been so sad.

One day, Tom and his mom went to the zoo. They saw many animals. Tom saw a big black bird. He liked the bird a lot. The bird could whistle a pretty song. Tom wanted to whistle like the bird. Tom tried to whistle, but he could not. He asked his mom, "Mom, can you whistle?" His mom said, "Yes, I can." She showed Tom how to whistle. Tom tried again, but he still could not whistle. Then, something unexpected happened. The big black bird flew away. Tom was very sad. He wanted to whistle with the bird. Tom and his mom went home. Tom never saw the big black bird again.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little bird. The bird lived in a big tree near a pond. The bird had many friends who lived in the tree too. One day, a big truck came to the pond. The truck had a lot of oil in it. The truck driver was not careful and the oil spilled into the pond. The oil began to destroy the pond and make it dirty. The cute little bird and its friends were very sad. They wanted to save their pond. So, they all worked together to clean the oil. They used their beaks and wings to remove the oil. After some days, the pond was clean again. The cute little bird and its friends were happy that they saved their home.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Rex, went to the park. They liked to play with a ball. Tim would throw the ball and Rex would run to get it. They were having a fine time. At the park, they met a girl named Sue. She was sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my toy in the park. It is a small red car." Tim and Rex wanted to help Sue find her toy. They looked all around the park, but they could not find the red car. Then, Rex started to dig near a big tree. He dug and dug until he found a big box. Inside the box was Sue's red car and a big, soft bed. Sue was so happy! They all played with the red car, and then they took the bed home to give to Rex. Now, Rex had a fine bed to sleep on after playing at the park.
