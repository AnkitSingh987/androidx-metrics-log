Category: Beginner
Title: The Book and the Bear

Once upon a time there was a book. It seemed special. It was big and strong, like a soldier. One day, a girl saw the book. She asked, "What's that for?". The book did not answer. But then a kind voice said, "That book is full of stories. Would you like to hear one?". The girl said yes. The voice said, "Once upon a time there was a strong bear. The bear could do anything he wanted". The girl seemed happy. The kind voice kept talking. The girl listened and listened. Soon, she was dreaming about the strong bear and his adventures. The End!

Tim and Jen like to play games. They have a lot of games, like cards, puzzles, and blocks. Sometimes they play together, and sometimes they play by themselves. One day, they decide to play a game with dice. They roll the dice and see who gets the bigger number. The one with the bigger number gets a point. The one with the most points at the end wins. Tim rolls the dice and gets a six. Jen rolls the dice and gets a four. Tim gets a point. He is happy. He says, "I have one point. You have zero points. I am going to win." Jen rolls the dice again and gets a five. Tim rolls the dice and gets a three. Jen gets a point. She is happy. She says, "Now we have the same points. One and one. Maybe I will win." They keep rolling the dice and getting points. Sometimes Tim gets a point, and sometimes Jen gets a point. They are both having fun. But then, something happens. Tim rolls the dice and gets a one. Jen rolls the dice and gets a six. Jen gets another point. She says, "I have four points. You have three points. I am going to win." Tim is not happy. He does not like to lose. He says, "No, you are not going to win. I am going to win. You are cheating. You are rolling the dice too hard. You have to roll them softly, like me." Jen says, "I am not cheating. I am rolling the dice the same way as you. You are just being sour. You have to mind your manners. You have to be nice when you play games. You have to say good job when I get a point, and I have to say good job when you get a point. That is how we play games." Tim thinks about what Jen says. He knows she is right. He is being sour. He does not mind his manners. He does not say good job when Jen gets a point. He says sorry to Jen. He says, "You are right. I am sorry. I am being sour. I will mind my manners. I will say good job when you get a point, and you say good job when I get a point. That is how we play games." Jen smiles and says, "Thank you. I am sorry too. I did not mean to make you feel bad. I just like to play games. I do not care who wins. I just want to have fun with you." They hug and say, "I love you." They roll the dice again and keep playing. They say good job to each other when they get points. They do not care who wins. They just have fun. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a gray seal named Sam. Sam lived in the sea with his family. They loved to play and swim all day long. One day, Sam saw a big ball on the beach. He wanted to balance the ball on his nose. But it was very hard for him to do it. He tried and tried, but he could not balance the ball. Sam felt sad and his friends laughed at him. Sam's mom saw him and said, "Don't give up, Sam. Keep trying and you will learn." Sam listened to his mom and tried again. After many tries, Sam finally learned to balance the ball on his nose. His friends stopped laughing and clapped for him. The moral of the story is to never give up and keep trying, even when things are hard.
