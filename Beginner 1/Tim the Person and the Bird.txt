Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Person and the Bird

One day, a person named Tim was very dizzy. He walked and walked but could not find his way home. "Oh no!" he said, "I am so dizzy and lost!" Tim saw a big tree and sat down under it. A friendly bird flew down and asked, "Why are you dizzy and sad?" Tim told the bird he could not find his way home because he was too dizzy. The bird said, "I can help you! First, close your eyes and wipe your face." Tim did what the bird said. When he opened his eyes, he was not dizzy anymore. The bird flew up high and saw Tim's house. "Follow me," said the bird. Tim followed the bird and soon found his house. He was so happy! Tim thanked the bird and went inside to rest. From that day on, Tim and the bird were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to hop and play in the green grass. One day, Remy found a mint plant. The mint plant smelled good. Remy started to worry. He did not know if he could eat the mint. He asked his friend, the big brown bear. The big brown bear did not know if mint was good to eat. Remy and the big brown bear went to ask the wise old owl. The wise old owl told them, "Yes, mint is good to eat." Remy and the big brown bear were happy. They ate the mint and played in the green grass all day long.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little panda named Pandy. Pandy loved to play in the forest with his friends. One sunny day, Pandy and his friends were playing near the river. They were having so much fun, but then the ball they were playing with fell into the river. The ball was now wet. Pandy's friend, Bunny, said, "Oh no! Our ball is wet. What do we do?" Pandy thought for a moment and said, "I know! I will bring a big, soft towel from my home to dry the ball." So, Pandy went to his home and brought back a big, soft towel. Pandy and his friends used the towel to dry the wet ball. They were happy to play again. They said, "Thank you, Pandy, for bringing the towel." Pandy smiled and said, "You're welcome! We should always help our friends when they need us." From that day on, Pandy and his friends always helped each other whenever they needed help. They learned that helping friends is a good thing to do. And they played happily ever after in the big green forest. The moral of the story is: Always help your friends when they need you.

One day, a little girl named Jane found a small plant. The plant was tough and did not grow well. Jane wanted to help the plant. She asked her mom, "How can I help the plant?" Her mom said, "Give it water and sun." So Jane did that. But the plant still did not grow. Jane was sad. She wanted her plant to be big and strong. One day, a big dog came to play. The dog ran and jumped near the plant. Jane was scared. She said, "No, dog! You will hurt my plant!" The dog stopped and looked at Jane. Then, the dog bowed to Jane and walked away. The next day, Jane saw that her plant had grown a little. She was happy. The plant was getting stronger. Jane knew that her love and care helped the plant grow. And the dog learned to be careful too.

One day, a small bird named Tim was playing in the rain. He loved to feel the wet rain on his feathers. Tim saw a big, selfish cat named Tom sitting on a fence. Tom was very grumpy because he did not like the rain. Tim thought it was funny to see the cat so unhappy. "Hi Tom!" Tim said. "Why don't you play in the rain with me?" Tom looked at Tim and said, "No, I don't like the rain. It makes me weigh more, and I don't like that. Go away!" But Tim didn't listen. He kept playing in the rain and splashing water on Tom. The cat got angrier, but Tim just laughed. In the end, Tom decided to try playing in the rain too. He found out that it was fun, and he wasn't so selfish anymore. They both played in the rain and became good friends.
