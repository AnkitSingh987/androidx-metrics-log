Category: Beginner
Title: The Blue Ashtray and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a big blue ashtray. It was sad because it was always dirty. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the blue ashtray and decided to help. Tim said to the ashtray, "Don't worry, I will sort you out and make you clean." He took the ashtray to the sink and washed it with soap and water. The blue ashtray looked shiny and new again. The blue ashtray was very happy and thanked Tim. From that day on, the blue ashtray and Tim became good friends. The moral of the story is that being kind and helpful can make others happy and bring new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his family. They lived in a small house with a big yard. One day, Spot's family was very busy. Mom was cooking, Dad was fixing a chair, and the little boy, Tom, was playing with his toys. Spot wanted to help. So, he went to Mom and helped her by bringing her a spoon. Mom was very happy. Next, Spot went to Dad and helped him by bringing him a tool. Dad was very proud. Finally, Spot went to Tom and helped him by finding his lost toy. Tom was very excited. The family was very thankful for Spot's help and gave him a big hug. They loved their smart dog, Spot, who always helped them.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a great big bush. The bush was full of yummy red berries. Tim wanted to eat the berries, but he was too short to reach them. Tim saw a tall girl named Sue. He asked her, "Can you help me get the berries?" Sue said, "Yes, I can help." She reached up and picked the berries for Tim. They both ate the berries, but soon they started to feel sick. Tim and Sue knew they needed help. They went to find Tim's mom. She was mad at them for eating the berries. Tim's mom said, "You should not eat berries from a bush you don't know." She gave them medicine to make them feel better. Tim and Sue learned not to eat things they didn't know.

Once, there was a boy named Timmy. He was three years old, and he loved playing with his friends. One day, Timmy's friends came to play with him, but he was sad. He was crying and weeping. "Timmy, why are you crying?" his friends asked him. "I don't know," Timmy said. "I spilled my soup and it's all gone." The friends felt sorry for Timmy and got him a bowl of soup. "Here, Timmy," they said. "This soup is for you." Timmy was happy and stopped weeping. He thanked his friends and hugged them. The friends then had a great time together playing with unknown things they found in the garden. They had lots of fun and Timmy stopped feeling sad.

Lila and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But they do not like to share. They fight a lot. One day, Lila finds a new doll in her room. She is very happy. She hugs the doll and brushes its hair. The doll has long, yellow hair and a pretty dress. Lila thinks the doll is her friend. Ben sees the doll and wants it. He thinks the doll is his toy. He runs to Lila and tries to grab the doll. Lila screams and holds the doll tight. She does not want to give it to Ben. They pull and tug on the doll. They are very angry. The doll's hair gets tangled and ripped. The doll's dress gets torn and dirty. The doll's eyes blink and cry. Lila and Ben do not care. They only want the doll. They do not see their mom coming. She is very sad and mad. She sees the doll and how they broke it. She takes the doll away from them. She says they are naughty and mean. She says they cannot play with the doll anymore. She says they have to go to bed without dinner. Lila and Ben are very sorry. They want the doll back. They want their mom to hug them. They want to eat something. They are hungry and thirsty. But their mom does not listen. She leaves them in their room. She closes the door. Lila and Ben cry and hug each other. They are scared and lonely. They do not like the dark. They do not like the silence. They do not like the bitter taste in their mouth. They wish they had shared the doll. They wish they had been nice. They wish they had a good ending.
