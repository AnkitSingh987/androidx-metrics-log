Category: Beginner
Title: The Purple Bird and the Rock

One day, a big purple bird went to visit his friend. The friend was a little red fish. The purple bird liked to fill his friend's pond with pretty rocks. The little red fish loved his purple friend's visits. The purple bird saw a big, shiny rock near the pond. He thought it would be the best rock for his friend. The bird tried to pick up the rock, but it was too heavy. He knew he needed help to fill the pond with the big rock. The purple bird asked his other friends for help. They all worked together to move the big rock. At last, they filled the pond with the big, shiny rock. The little red fish was so happy. The purple bird and his friends had a fun visit.

Lily and Tom were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to pretend they were cops and robbers. Lily had a hat and a badge, and Tom had a mask and a bag. "Stop, robber! You are under arrest!" Lily shouted, as she ran after Tom. "No, you can't catch me! I have the money!" Tom said, as he hid behind the sofa. Lily looked around and saw a big book on the table. She had an idea. She set the book on the floor and waited for Tom to come out. "Ha, I got you!" she said, as she threw the book at Tom. The book was very thick and heavy, and it hit Tom on the head. "Ow, that hurt!" Tom cried, as he dropped the bag and rubbed his head. "I'm sorry, Tom. Are you okay?" Lily asked, as she ran to him and hugged him. "I'm okay, Lily. But that was not fair. You can't use a book as a weapon. That's cheating!" Tom said, as he hugged her back. "I know, I know. I'm sorry. I just wanted to catch you. Can we still be friends?" Lily said, as she looked at him with big eyes. "Of course we can, Lily. You are my best friend. But next time, let's use something softer, okay?" Tom said, as he smiled at her. "Okay, Tom. Let's play something else. How about we pretend we are astronauts and go to the moon?" Lily said, as she picked up a helmet and a rocket. "Okay, Lily. That sounds fun. Let's go to the moon!" Tom said, as he picked up a helmet and a rocket too. They put on their helmets and held their rockets. They counted to three and jumped on the sofa. They pretended they were flying to the moon and having adventures. They had a lot of fun and forgot about the book and the bag. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. The bear lived in a deep, dark forest. One day, the bear wanted to rest. He found a nice, soft spot to lie down. He closed his eyes and started to sleep. In the forest, there was a camp. In the camp, there were kids. The kids were playing and having fun. They saw the bear resting. They wanted to play with the bear, but they were scared. They thought the bear would be mean. The kids went to the bear very slowly. They poked the bear to wake him up. The bear opened his eyes and saw the kids. But the bear was not mean. He was a nice bear! The kids and the bear became friends. They played together all day in the camp. And when they were tired, they all rested together.

Once upon a time, in a big room with lots of furniture, there lived a gentle dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball and run around the room. He had many friends in the room, like the soft chair and the small table. One day, Spot wanted to have a party with all his friends. So, he decided to invite Chair and Table to play with him. He ran to Chair and said, "Chair, come play with me and Table!" Chair was very happy and agreed to come to the party. Spot, Chair, and Table had a great time playing together. They played catch with Spot's ball and danced around the room. They were all very gentle with each other, making sure no one got hurt. After a fun day of playing, they were all very tired and happy. They all agreed to have another party soon, and they lived happily ever after.
