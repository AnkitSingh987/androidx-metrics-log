Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bug and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Ben. Ben loved to crawl all around the garden. One day, while he was crawling, he found a big piece of chocolate on the ground. Ben wanted to share the chocolate with his friend, Sam the snail. He said, "Sam, I found some chocolate. Let's eat it together!" Sam was an honest snail and said, "Ben, we have to ask if it belongs to someone." They crawled around the garden and asked all their friends if the chocolate was theirs. No one said it was. So, Ben and Sam went back to the chocolate and ate it all up. They were happy they had been honest and shared their treat.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the market with his mom. Tim saw a big piece of beef. He asked his mom, "Can we buy the beef?" His mom said, "Yes, we can buy it." They went home and Tim's mom started to cook the beef. But the beef was old and did not smell good. Tim's mom said, "Oh no, this beef is old. We can't eat it." Tim had an idea. He went to his friend, Sam, and said, "I will give you this beef." Sam took the beef and went home. When Sam's mom cooked the beef, it smelled bad too. Sam's mom said, "This beef is old, we can't eat it." Sam was sad and went back to Tim. He said, "The beef you gave me was old. We can't eat it." Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know." Both Tim and Sam were sad because they couldn't eat the beef.

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. It liked to drive fast. One day, the car drove down a long road. It saw a tree with a nest. Inside the nest, there were baby birds. The baby birds were hungry. They wanted food. The big red car wanted to help. It looked for food for the baby birds. It found a dead bug. The car knew the baby birds would like to eat the bug. The big red car brought the dead bug to the nest. The baby birds were happy. They ate the bug. The big red car felt good. It had helped the baby birds. The car drove away, looking for more friends to help.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a smelly kangaroo. He loved to hop and play with his friends. One day, he found a book on the ground. The kangaroo did not know how to read, but he wanted to learn. Soon, he met a wise old owl who could read very well. The owl said, "I will teach you to read, but first, you must take a bath. You are too smelly!" The kangaroo agreed and took a bath in the river. He became clean and not smelly anymore. After his bath, the owl taught the kangaroo how to read. They read the book together. The book was about a hero who saved his friends from danger. The kangaroo was so happy he could read. One day, he saw his friends in trouble. They were stuck in a big hole. The kangaroo used his strong legs to hop and save them, just like the hero in the book. His friends cheered, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a kind dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run in the park. One day, he saw a sign that said, "Stay off the grass." Spot did not understand the sign, so he asked his friend, a little bird named Tweet. "Tweet, what does the sign say?" asked Spot. Tweet looked at the sign and said, "The sign says we must stay off the grass. We should play on the path instead." Spot and Tweet played on the path, being very careful not to step on the grass. They had lots of fun and stayed kind to the grass. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to bed. He had a regular glass of water on his table. He knew he would wake up thirsty in the night. In the middle of the night, Tim woke up. He was very thirsty. He looked at the glass of water on the table. He said, "I knew I would need you, water!" Tim drank the water from the glass. It made him feel much better. He went back to sleep with a smile on his face. In the morning, Tim's mom asked, "Did you sleep well, Tim?" Tim said, "Yes, Mom! I woke up and drank the water I put on my table. I knew I would need it!" Tim's mom smiled and said, "You are so smart, Tim!" Tim was happy that he had the water and that his mom was proud of him.
