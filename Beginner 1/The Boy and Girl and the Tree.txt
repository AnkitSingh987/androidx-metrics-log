Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy and Girl and the Tree

One day, a boy and a girl went to play in the park. They saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. The tree had a hidden secret. It was a tree of wonder. The boy and girl were very happy to find it. The tree had many toys and treats for the kids to play with and eat. The boy and girl were a little fearful at first. They did not know if they should take the toys and treats. But they saw other kids playing and sharing, so they knew it was okay. The boy and girl played with the toys and shared them with each other. They had a lot of fun. They ate the treats and shared them too. They were happy they found the tree of wonder and learned to share with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big bookcase in a room. The bookcase was full of books. It was incredible! Every day, a little boy named Tim would pick a book and read it. He loved his bookcase very much. One day, while Tim was reading, the bookcase started to tremble. It shook and shook! Tim was scared. He did not know what was happening. Then, something unexpected happened. A secret door opened behind the bookcase! Tim was brave and walked through the door. He found a new room full of toys! Tim was so happy. The room had a big sign that said, "For Tim, the best reader!" He played with the toys and had so much fun. The bookcase's secret was a big surprise for Tim.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She loved to design things with her toys. Mia had a big box of blocks, and she was making a tall tower. The sun was going down, and the room was getting dark. Mia's mom came into the room and saw her playing. "Mia, it's getting dark. Are you anxious about the dark?" she asked. "No, mommy, I am not anxious," Mia said. "But I want to keep playing. Can you turn on the light, please?" Her mom smiled and turned on the light. "There you go, now you can see better for your designs," she said. Mia was happy and kept playing with her blocks, making her tower even taller.

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing in the park. The sun was shining and the sky was clear. They were having lots of fun. The dog said, "Let's play hide and seek!" The cat nodded its head and said, "Okay, you hide first and I'll find you." The dog ran to hide behind a big tree. The cat closed its eyes and started counting. When it was done, the cat opened its eyes and looked for the dog. The cat found the dog's head sticking out from behind the tree. "I found you!" the cat said. The dog laughed and said, "Now it's your turn to hide!" The cat ran and hid under a bush. The dog looked for the cat and found it quickly. They played hide and seek all day and had lots of fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. Tim saw a deer in the park. He was so happy. The deer was big and brown. Tim said, "Daddy, look at the deer!" Tim's dad had a toy gun. It could shoot soft balls. He gave it to Tim. Tim wanted to play with the deer. He said, "I will shoot the ball to the deer." Tim shot the ball at the deer. The deer was not scared. It came closer to Tim. They played together. Tim's dad said, "That toy gun is expensive, but it makes you happy." At the end of the day, Tim and his dad went home. Tim said, "I love the deer and my toy gun." His dad smiled and said, "I love you, Tim."

Once upon a time, there was a man who liked to sell things. He had a small shop in the town. People came to his shop every day. The man was very lucky because he always sold a lot of things. One day, a big drum came to his shop. The drum had a special rhythm. When the man played the drum, everyone in the town stopped to listen. They liked the rhythm very much. The man thought he was very lucky to have the drum. But soon, the man wanted to sell the drum. He thought he could get a lot of money for it. So, he put a big price on the drum. But no one wanted to buy it. They did not have enough money. The man was very sad. He kept playing the drum, but no one came to his shop anymore. The man did not feel lucky anymore.
