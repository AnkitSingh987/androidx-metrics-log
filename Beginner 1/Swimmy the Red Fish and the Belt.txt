Category: Beginner
Title: Swimmy the Red Fish and the Belt

Once upon a time, there was a red fish named Swimmy. Swimmy loved to swim all day in the big blue sea. One day, Swimmy found a belt floating in the water. Swimmy thought the belt was very pretty and wanted to show it to his friends. Swimmy swam to his friends and showed them the belt. They all liked it too, but they did not know what it was for. Swimmy had an idea. He asked his friends to help him wear the belt. They all helped, and Swimmy wore the belt around his body. As soon as Swimmy wore the belt, something unexpected happened. Swimmy started to swim very fast. His friends were so surprised! They all wanted to try the belt too. Swimmy shared the belt with his friends, and they all took turns swimming fast. They had so much fun and played together all day long.

Bee and Bear were friends. They liked to play in the forest. One day, Bee found a big jar of honey. He was very happy. He wanted to share it with Bear. He flew to Bear's cave and said, "Bear, Bear, come and see. I have a big jar of honey for you and me." Bear heard Bee and came out of his cave. He saw the jar of honey and smiled. He loved honey. He said, "Thank you, Bee. You are very kind. Let's eat some honey together." But then, they heard a loud roar. It was Lion. He was hungry and angry. He saw the jar of honey and wanted it for himself. He said, "Give me that honey, or I will eat you both." Bee and Bear were scared. They did not want to fight with Lion. He was big and strong. They did not know what to do. Bee said, "Bear, we have to surrender. We have to give him the honey. It is not worth it to get hurt." Bear said, "But Bee, I like honey. And you found it for us. It is not fair that Lion takes it away." Bee said, "I know, Bear. But we can find more honey later. Right now, we have to be safe. And we still have each other. We are friends. That is more important than honey." Bear nodded. He agreed with Bee. He said, "You are right, Bee. We are friends. And friends stick together. Let's give him the honey and go away." They gave the jar of honey to Lion and flew and ran away. Lion was happy. He ate the honey and licked his paws. Bee and Bear were sad. They lost their honey. But they were also happy. They saved their lives. And they had each other. They hugged and said, "We are friends. And friends are sweet."

Once upon a time, there was a nice judge who lived in a big house. He liked to help people and make them happy. One day, he met a little boy who was very sad. The boy said, "I can't sleep at night. I need help." The nice judge thought hard. He wanted to help the boy sleep. He had an idea. He said, "I know! I will sing a song for you. It will make you sleep." That night, the boy and the nice judge sat together. The judge sang a song for the boy. The boy listened and started to feel sleepy. But then, a big wind came and blew the house away. The boy and the nice judge were sad. They had no place to sleep now. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He had a big question. He wanted to know if he could buy a toy. The toy was very expensive. He asked his mom, "Can I buy the toy?" His mom said, "We will see." One day, Tom went to the store with his mom. He saw the toy he wanted. He asked his mom again, "Can I buy the toy?" His mom said, "We will see." Tom was sad. He thought he could not get the toy. But, something unexpected happened! Tom's mom took out a box from her bag. She said, "I have a surprise for you!" Tom opened the box. Inside was the expensive toy! Tom's mom said, "I bought it for you because you have been good." Tom was so happy! His question was answered, and he got the toy he wanted.
