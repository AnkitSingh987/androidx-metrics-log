Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Loyal Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball and run around outside. He lived in a small shelter in the yard. One day, he found a big box near his shelter. Spot was curious about the box. As Spot started to remove the box, he saw something move inside. He was scared at first, but then a little cat came out. The cat looked sad and lost. Spot wanted to help the cat, so he let the cat stay in his shelter. At first, Spot thought the cat was not fun to play with. But one day, when Spot lost his ball, the cat found it for him. From that day on, Spot and the cat were the best of friends. They played together every day and shared the shelter. The cat was not what Spot expected, but they became loyal friends forever.

Lily was cold. She did not like the night. She wanted to see the sun and the flowers. She hugged her blanket. Her blanket was soft and warm. She hoped the night would end soon. She heard a noise. She saw a shadow. She was scared. She hid under her blanket. She hoped the noise would go away. "Hello, little girl. Don't be afraid. I am a friendly owl. I live in the tree. I saw you shiver. Do you need some help?" The owl said. Lily peeked out of her blanket. She saw the owl. The owl had big eyes and a nice voice. She was not scared anymore. She said, "Hello, Mr. Owl. I am cold. I don't like the night. I want to see the sun and the flowers." The owl smiled. He said, "I understand. The night can be dark and cold. But the night can also be beautiful and fun. Look at the stars. They sparkle like diamonds. Listen to the crickets. They sing like a choir. Feel the breeze. It tickles your nose. The night is not so bad. You just need to see the good things." Lily looked at the stars. She listened to the crickets. She felt the breeze. She smiled. She said, "You are right, Mr. Owl. The night is not so bad. You are very kind. Thank you for helping me." The owl said, "You are welcome, little girl. I am glad you are happy. Now, you can sleep well. I will watch over you. Good night, Lily." Lily said, "Good night, Mr. Owl. You are my friend." She closed her eyes. She hugged her blanket. She was not cold anymore. She hoped to see the owl again. The moral value of the story is: Don't be afraid of the unknown. You can find beauty and friendship in unexpected places.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, Lily is sick. She has a cold. She stays at home with her mom. Tom misses Lily. He wants to see her. He asks his mom if he can visit Lily. His mom says yes, but he has to be careful. He has to wear a mask and not touch Lily. Tom puts on his mask and his coat. He walks to Lily's house with his mom. He knocks on the door. Lily's mom opens it. She says hello and thanks them for coming. Tom sees Lily on the couch. She looks pale and tired. She has a blanket and a teddy bear. She smiles when she sees Tom. "Hi, Tom," she says. "I'm happy you're here." "Hi, Lily," Tom says. "I'm sorry you're sick. Do you feel better?" "A little bit," Lily says. "But I'm bored. I can't go out and play." Tom thinks for a moment. He has an idea. He takes out a book from his backpack. It is a book about animals. He likes animals. He thinks Lily likes them too. "Do you want me to read you a story?" he asks. "Yes, please," Lily says. "That would be nice." Tom sits on the floor next to the couch. He opens the book and shows Lily the pictures. He reads the story in a loud and clear voice. He makes funny noises for the animals. Lily laughs and listens. She forgets about her cold for a while. Tom and Lily have fun. They manage to have a good time even when Lily is sick. They are good friends.
