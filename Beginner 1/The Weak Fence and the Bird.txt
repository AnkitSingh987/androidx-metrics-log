Category: Beginner
Title: The Weak Fence and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a weak fence in a big yard. The fence was old and not strong. One day, a small red bird came and sat on the fence. The fence moved a little, but it did not fall. The bird said to the fence, "You are so weak, I am scared to sit on you. What if you break?" The fence said, "I may be weak, but I am still standing. I will try my best to keep you safe." One hot day, a fire started near the fence. The fire was big and hot. It wanted to burn the fence. The small red bird saw the fire and was scared. The bird flew away to find help. The bird found a big strong elephant. The elephant used its long nose to put water on the fire. The fire went away, and the fence was saved. The bird thanked the elephant and went back to sit on the fence. From that day on, the bird and the fence were friends. The bird learned that even weak things can be strong when they try their best. And the fence was happy to have a friend who believed in it.

Anna and Ben are playing with their drums. They like to make loud noises and feel the rhythm in their hands and feet. They pretend they are in a band and sing songs they know. "Let's play faster and faster!" Ben says. "We can make a big finale!" "Okay!" Anna says. "But we have to hurry. Mommy will come soon and tell us to stop." They bang their drums harder and harder, faster and faster. They count to ten and then shout "Hooray!" They laugh and clap their hands. But then they hear a knock on the door. It is Mommy. She looks at them with a smile and a frown. "What are you doing, my little drummers?" she asks. "You are making so much noise!" "We are playing, Mommy!" Anna says. "We are in a band!" Mommy nods. "I see. And what are your drums made of?" Anna and Ben look at their drums. They are not real drums. They are fake drums. They are pots and pans and lids and spoons. "They are fake drums, Mommy!" Ben says. "But they sound good!" Mommy laughs. "They do sound good, but they also sound loud. How about we play something quieter for a while? Maybe we can read a book or draw a picture?" Anna and Ben think for a moment. They like to play with their drums, but they also like to read and draw. And they love Mommy. "Okay, Mommy!" they say. "We can play something quieter. But can we play with our drums again later?" Mommy hugs them. "Of course you can, my little drummers. But only for a little bit. And not too loud, okay?" "Okay, Mommy!" they say. They put away their fake drums and follow Mommy to the living room. They are happy to play something quieter, but they still feel the rhythm in their hearts.

One day, a wet dog named Max went on a tour. He walked with his friends to see new things. Max liked the tour, but he was very wet from the rain. Max saw a big tree and thought it would help him get dry. He shook his body under the tree. His friends laughed and clapped. Max felt better now that he was not so wet. After the tour, Max and his friends went home. They played in the sun and Max got all dry. Max loved his tour and playing with his friends.

One day, a gifted cat named Tom went for a walk. He met a dog named Max. Max was not like other dogs. He liked to pass balls to his friends. Tom and Max became good friends. Tom said, "Max, can you pass the ball to me?" Max was happy to play with Tom. He passed the ball to Tom with his nose. Tom hit the ball back with his paw. They played for a long time. After playing, Tom and Max were tired. They sat under a tree. Tom saw a bird in the tree. The bird had a hurt lung. Tom and Max helped the bird feel better. They became friends with the bird too.
