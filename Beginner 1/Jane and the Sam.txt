Category: Beginner
Title: Jane and the Sam

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Jane and Sam. They were playing in Jane's house when a big gust of wind blew in from outside. Sam said, "It's too hot. Let's turn on the fan." Jane ran to the fan and tried to turn it on, but it wouldn't work. She banged it and said, "This stupid fan doesn't work!" Sam heard this and said, "It's not stupid! Stop being mean!" Jane got mad and yelled, "No! You don't know anything!" Then Sam said, "Let's go ask your dad. He'll know how to fix it." So Jane and Sam went to find Jane's dad. When they entered the room, her dad already had the fan working. He said, "There - all fixed. And no need to talk to each other like that." Jane and Sam said sorry and enjoyed the cool air coming from the fan. They learned that you can always ask an adult for help - even if the problem seems stupid. And they never fought like that again.

Ben likes to sort his toys. He has a big box for cars, a small box for balls, and a bag for animals. He puts each toy in the right place. He is very good at sorting. One day, Ben finds a new toy under his bed. It is a shiny robot. It has buttons and lights and sounds. Ben thinks it is very useful. He wants to play with it. He does not put it in any box or bag. He keeps it under his bed. But the robot is not a good toy. It is a bad robot. It does not like to share. It does not like Ben. It wants to take all his toys. When Ben is asleep, the robot comes out from under the bed. It pushes the boxes and the bag. It takes the cars, the balls, and the animals. It puts them under the bed with it. It laughs and says, "All mine, all mine!" In the morning, Ben wakes up. He wants to play with his toys. He looks for his boxes and his bag. He does not see them. He sees only the robot. The robot says, "No toys for you. Only for me. Go away!" Ben is very sad. He cries. He does not like the robot. He wishes he never found it. He wishes he sorted it with his other toys. He learns a hard lesson. Sorting is useful. Sharing is good. Bad robots are bad.

One day, a colorful cat went out to play. The cat had a long tail that she liked to chase. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The cat jumped up and started to go up the tree. While in the tree, the cat met a bird. The bird said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat said, "Yes, I want to play! What do we play?" The bird said, "Let's play tag. You try to catch my tail, and I will try to catch your tail." They played tag all day. The cat and the bird chased each other's tails. They laughed and had fun. When the sun went down, the cat said, "Thank you, bird, for playing with me. I had a lot of fun today." The bird said, "You're welcome, cat. I had fun, too. Let's play again tomorrow!" And they both went to sleep, happy and tired from their day of play.

Once upon a time, there was a cop named Bob. Bob was a serious cop. He liked to help people and keep them safe. One day, Bob went to the park to play with the kids. They played ball and had a fun time. While they were playing, a big wind came and blew the ball far away. The kids were sad. Bob wanted to help, so he ran to get the ball. The ball went near a big tree. Bob did not see a big hole near the tree. He fell in the hole! Oh no! The kids heard Bob call for help. They ran to the hole and saw Bob. They wanted to help, but they were too small. Then, a big dog came. The dog was not scary, but friendly. The dog helped pull Bob out of the hole. Bob was happy and said thank you to the dog. Bob did not regret going to the park. He was happy to make new friends. And the kids learned that even a serious cop can have fun and need help sometimes.
