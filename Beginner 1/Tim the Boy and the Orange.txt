Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Orange

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a big red ball and wanted it. Tim said, "Mom, I want that red ball!" His mom said, "Okay, but first we need to get some food." They walked to the freezer and saw many cold things. There were cold green peas, cold yellow corn, and cold orange carrots. Tim's mom put some of them in the cart. Tim said, "Mom, I want ice cream too!" His mom said, "Okay, but only if you eat your cold vegetables." At home, Tim ate his cold green peas, cold yellow corn, and cold orange carrots. He was a good boy. His mom said, "Good job, Tim! Now you can have your ice cream." Tim was happy and played with his big red ball after eating his ice cream.

Once upon a time, in a hot land, there was a little boy named Tom. He liked to play outside all day. One day, he saw a big tree with lots of leaves. He wanted to climb it. Tom started to climb the tree. He was very happy. But then, he had a fall. He fell down and hurt his leg. He started to weep. His mom heard him and came running. "Oh no, Tom! What happened?" asked his mom. Tom said, "I had a fall from the tree, and it hurts." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Tom. I will make it better." Mom took Tom inside and put a bandage on his leg. She gave him a big kiss. Tom stopped weeping and smiled. He knew he would be more careful next time he climbed a tree. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a funny cat named Bob went to the fridge. He was very hungry. He saw a big hat on top of the fridge. Bob wanted to wear the hat. Bob said to his friend, a dog named Tim, "I want to wear the big hat on the fridge. Can you help me get it?" Tim said, "Yes, I will help you, Bob." Tim jumped high and got the hat for Bob. Bob was happy and put on the hat. But, the hat was not a hat! It was a big bowl with a hole. The big bowl was stuck on Bob's head. Bob and Tim tried to take it off, but they could not. Bob was sad, and Tim was sorry. They did not know what to do.

One day, a friendly bear went to the woods. He found a long spear. He didn't know what it was, but he liked it. The bear played with the spear, and he was happy. In the woods, the bear met a big deer. The deer saw the spear and got scared. The bear did not want to scare his new friend. So, he put the spear down and said, "Don't worry, I won't hurt you." Then, they heard a loud yell. A little girl was lost in the woods. The bear and the deer went to help her. They found the girl and walked her back to her home. The girl, the bear, and the deer became good friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to fold paper. One day, he made a paper toy called a missile. It was a small paper toy that could fly. He was very proud of his missile. One day, Tim and his friends were playing outside. Tim threw his paper missile, and it flew very far. But, oh no! The missile hit a big man. The man looked angry and guilty. Tim was scared. But then, the man smiled. He said, "I used to fold paper missiles when I was little, too!" The man showed Tim and his friends how to fold even better paper missiles. They all had fun playing together. And that day, Tim made a new friend.

One day, a small girl named Lily found a stamp. It was a very pretty stamp with a picture of a cat on it. Lily wanted to show her friend, Tim, the stamp she found. Lily went to Tim's house and said, "Look at my stamp, Tim!" Tim looked at the stamp and said, "That's a nice stamp, Lily. Can you explain where you found it?" Lily told Tim that she found the stamp in the park. Tim thought it was a cool find. Just then, a rude boy named Max came by. He saw the stamp and took it from Lily. "This is my stamp now!" he said. Lily and Tim were sad. But then, the stamp began to change. The cat on the stamp turned into a big, scary dog! Max got scared and gave the stamp back to Lily. Lily and Tim laughed, and they all learned not to be rude to others.
