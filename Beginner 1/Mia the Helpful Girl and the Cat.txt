Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Helpful Girl and the Cat

One day, a helpful girl named Mia went for a walk. She liked to skip and sing. As she skipped, she saw a little cat with a stitch on its leg. The cat looked sad. Mia said, "Oh no, little cat, what happened?" The cat said, "I hurt my leg and now I can't play. Can you help me?" Mia wanted to help the cat, so she took it home. At home, Mia's mom fixed the cat's stitch. The cat was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to skip and sing too! Mia and the cat became best friends, and they always helped each other. The moral of the story is to always be helpful and kind to others, even if they are different from you.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a house with her mom, dad, and her cat, Tippy. Lily loved to help her mom cook in the kitchen. One day, they cooked a big, delicious cake for a party. At the party, Lily met a new friend, Tim. They played together and had lots of fun. Lily told Tim, "My mom and I cooked a delicious cake for the party. Do you want to try it?" Tim said, "Yes, I love cake!" So they ate the cake and it was very yummy. After the party, Lily was very tired. She went to her room and saw that her mattress was missing. She looked everywhere for it. Finally, she found Tippy, her cat, sleeping on the mattress in the living room. Lily laughed and said, "Silly Tippy, that's my mattress!" She carried the mattress back to her room, tucked Tippy in with her, and they both fell asleep, dreaming of more delicious cakes to cook with mom.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play in the garden. They find many things in the garden. They find bugs, flowers, rocks and herbs. One day, they find a big herb. It has green leaves and purple flowers. It smells nice. Lila says, "This is a nice herb. I want to take it home." Tom says, "No, this is my herb. I found it first. You can have another herb." They start to pull the herb. They pull and pull. They want the herb for themselves. They do not want to part with the herb. They do not want to share. The herb does not like this. It does not like to be pulled. It does not like to be fought over. It wants to grow in the garden. It wants to be happy. The herb has a trick. It makes a loud noise. It says, "Stop! Stop! You are hurting me! You are being mean! You are being boring! Let me go!" Lila and Tom are scared. They let go of the herb. They look at the herb. They look at each other. They feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, herb. We did not know you can talk. We did not mean to hurt you. We did not mean to be mean. We did not mean to be boring." The herb says, "It's okay. I forgive you. You can be my friends. You can visit me in the garden. You can smell me and touch me gently. But you cannot take me home. I belong in the garden. I have other friends here. I have other herbs, bugs, flowers and rocks. They are not boring. They are fun." Lila and Tom nod. They say, "Okay, herb. We understand. We will be your friends. We will visit you in the garden. We will smell you and touch you gently. We will not take you home. We will play with your other friends. They are not boring. They are fun." They hug the herb. They hug each other. They smile. They are happy. They are not boring. They are fun.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with soft toys. They have a big bear, a small cat, and a yellow duck. They hug them and talk to them. One day, Anna's mom says, "Anna, we have to go to a meeting. You can bring one toy with you." Anna thinks for a moment. She loves all her toys, but she picks the yellow duck. She says, "Bye, Ben. Bye, bear. Bye, cat. I will be back soon." Ben is sad. He wants to play with Anna and the duck. He looks at the bear and the cat. They are soft, but they are not fun. He has an idea. He says, "I will go to the meeting too. I will find Anna and the duck. They will be happy to see me." Ben puts on his shoes and his coat. He opens the door and runs outside. He does not see Anna's mom or her car. He does not know where the meeting is. He sees a big street with many cars. He thinks, "Maybe the meeting is on the other side. I will cross the street." Ben does not look left or right. He does not wait for the green light. He runs into the street. He does not see the big truck. The truck does not see him. The truck hits him. Ben falls down. He does not get up. He does not play with Anna and the duck. He does not play with the bear and the cat. He does not play anymore.
