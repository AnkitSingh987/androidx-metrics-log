Category: Beginner
Title: The Vendor and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a noisy town, there lived a vendor. He sold yummy treats like ice cream and candy. Every day, kids came to buy his treats. One day, a big dog came to the vendor. The dog wanted ice cream too! But the vendor said, "No, dogs can't have ice cream." The dog was sad and went away. Later, a cat came to the vendor. The cat wanted candy. The vendor said, "No, cats can't have candy." The cat was sad and went away. But then, the dog and cat met each other. They decided to fight the vendor to get treats. The dog and cat went back to the vendor. They started to fight him. But, the vendor did something unexpected. He gave the dog and cat special treats just for them! The dog and cat were happy and stopped fighting. From that day on, the vendor sold treats for kids, dogs, and cats. Everyone was happy!

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with swords. They have toy swords made of wood. They pretend to be knights and fight bad guys. One day, they go to the park with their swords. They see a big hill. They want to climb the hill and see what is on the other side. They think it will be easy. "Come on, Sam, let's go up the hill!" Tom says. "OK, Tom, let's go!" Sam says. They start to climb the hill. They use their swords to help them. They stick their swords in the ground and pull themselves up. They have fun. But when they get to the top of the hill, they see something scary. They see a big dog. The dog is barking and growling. The dog has sharp teeth and claws. The dog looks angry. "Tom, look, a dog!" Sam says. "Sam, run, a dog!" Tom says. They are afraid of the dog. They do not want to play with the dog. They want to go back to their mommies. They turn around and run down the hill. They forget their swords. They drop their swords on the ground. The dog sees their swords. The dog thinks they are sticks. The dog likes to play with sticks. The dog picks up their swords with his mouth. The dog runs after them. The dog wants to play with them. "Tom, the dog has our swords!" Sam says. "Sam, the dog is coming!" Tom says. They run faster. They run to their mommies. They hug their mommies. They are safe. "Mommy, there was a big dog on the hill!" Tom says. "Mommy, the dog took our swords!" Sam says. Their mommies look at the hill. They see the dog. The dog is wagging his tail. The dog is happy. The dog is not bad. The dog just wants to play. "Boys, that is not a bad dog. That is a friendly dog. He likes your swords. He thinks they are toys. Maybe you can share your swords with him. Maybe you can be friends with him." Their mommies say. Tom and Sam look at the dog. They look at their mommies. They look at each other. They are not afraid anymore. They are curious. "Maybe we can, Mommy." Tom says. "Maybe we can, Mommy." Sam says. They go back to the hill. They go to the dog. They say hello to the dog. The dog says woof to them. They pet the dog. The dog licks them. They laugh. They play with the dog. They play with their swords. They take turns throwing their swords and fetching them. They have fun. They make a new friend. His name is Spot. He is a good dog. He likes to play with swords. He likes to play with Tom and Sam. They are happy.

One day, a little bird was sitting on a tree. The bird was upset. It could not find its friend. The bird looked all around but could not see its friend anywhere. The bird saw a cat and asked, "Cat, did you see my friend?" The cat just shook its head. The bird flew to the next tree and saw a dog. The bird asked, "Dog, did you see my friend?" The dog shook its head too. The bird was very sad and flew to the last tree in the park. On the last tree, the bird saw a big shake. The bird was scared but flew closer. The shake stopped, and the bird's friend came out! The friend said, "I was hiding to surprise you!" The bird was so happy to see its friend. They played together and were not upset anymore.
