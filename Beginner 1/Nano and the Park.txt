Category: Beginner
Title: Nano and the Park

One day, Nano and his mommy were walking in the park. Nano was so happy because they were looking for eggs. He loved Easter egg hunts. They found the first egg nestled in the grass. It was little and white. Mommy laughed and said, “You’re so sharp, Nano! You found it right away.” Nano smiled and they kept looking. After a while, they found another egg. Mommy said, “Look at this one! This one is so nice and little.” Nano was very proud. He had found two eggs already. He made sure to keep looking until he found another one. He spotted another egg in the bushes. He called out, “Mommy! I found another one!” Mommy’s face lit up with a big smile. “You did it! You’re really sharp, Nano!” she said. Nano grinned from ear to ear and held up his 3 eggs. He couldn't wait to take them home.

One day, a little squirrel named Tim found a big peanut. He was very happy. Tim wanted to save the peanut for later. He looked for a safe place to hide it. Tim saw a wet hole in a tree. He thought it was a good place to put the peanut. But a bird named Sam lived in the tree. Sam did not want Tim to put the peanut in his wet home. Tim and Sam did not know what to do. They were both sad. Then, they had an idea. They decided to share the peanut. Tim and Sam became good friends. They learned that sharing is better than fighting.

Sam was a big boy who liked to play with his toys. He had many cars, trains, planes and animals. He liked to make them zoom, chug, fly and roar. He also liked to pretend that he was a giant and his toys were tiny. One day, he found a strange bottle in his dad's closet. It had a label that said "Shrink". Sam was curious and opened the bottle. He smelled something sweet and drank a little bit. He felt funny and looked around. Everything was bigger than before. He had shrunk! He saw his toys on the floor. They were as big as him now. He ran to them and tried to play, but they were too heavy and hard. He could not lift them or move them. He felt scared and sad. He wanted to be big again. He heard his mom call his name. She was looking for him. He tried to answer, but his voice was too small. He saw her feet come into the room. They were huge. He hoped she would see him and help him. She did not see him at first. She looked at his toys and wondered where he was. Then she saw the bottle on the floor. She picked it up and read the label. She gasped and looked around. She saw Sam on the floor, next to his train. He waved at her and cried. She was shocked and worried. She put the bottle down and picked up Sam. She hugged him and said sorry. She told him that the bottle was a bad thing that made people shrink. She said that she would find a way to make him big again. She said that he had to be patient and brave. Sam nodded and hugged her back. He was glad that she found him and loved him. He trusted her and hoped that she would fix him. He missed his youth and his toys. He learned a lesson and never drank from strange bottles again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He was just three years old, and he loved playing in the garden. One day, Jack was playing in the garden, wearing his favourite pants. He was having so much fun that he didn't notice the rain clouds coming. Suddenly, the rain started pouring down. "Oh no!" Jack said. "What am I going to do?" Jack started running to the house to get out of the rain. But the grass was slippery and he fell to the ground. He looked at his pants and saw that they were spoiled. 'Oh dear," he said sadly. Jack's mummy saw Jack and ran to save him. She wrapped him in a towel and said, "Don't worry, Jack. You're safe now." Jack smiled and said thank you. Then he and his mummy went to buy some new pants.
