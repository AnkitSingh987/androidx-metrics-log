Category: Beginner
Title: The Black Engine Was and the Train

One day, a little black engine was in the train yard. It liked to help big trains move. The black engine was very happy when it could help. A big train came to the yard. It needed to go far away. The black engine wanted to help the big train. It went to the big train and said, "Can I help you go far away?" The big train smiled and said, "Yes, little black engine, you can help me go far away. Thank you!" The little black engine was so happy. The little black engine and the big train went far away together. They saw many things on their trip. They saw trees, animals, and big hills. The little black engine was very proud and happy to help the big train go far away.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day long. One sunny day, Fin met a new fish friend named Bubbles. Bubbles was a healthy fish, with big, strong fins. "Hi, I'm Fin! Do you want to swim with me?" Fin asked Bubbles. Bubbles smiled and said, "Yes! I love to swim too!" So, they both start to swim together, splashing in the water. As they swim, they meet more fish friends. They all play and have fun together. Fin and Bubbles are happy to swim and make friends in the big blue sea.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and make things. One day, they find a big box in the park. They wonder what is inside. "Let's open it and see," Lila says. "Maybe it has a surprise for us," Ben says. They pull and push the box until it opens. Inside, they see a lot of wires and buttons and lights. It looks like a thing that can do something. "Wow, what is this?" Lila asks. "I don't know, but it looks fun," Ben says. They touch the wires and buttons and lights. They hear beeps and boops and buzzes. The thing makes noises and flashes. "Look, it's a test thing!" Lila says. "A test thing? What does it test?" Ben asks. "I don't know, maybe it tests how smart we are," Lila says. They keep playing with the test thing. They try to guess what it means and what it wants. They laugh and smile and have fun. But then, something goes wrong. The test thing makes a loud noise and smoke comes out. The wires and buttons and lights stop working. The test thing is broken. "Oh no, what did we do?" Lila says. "We broke the test thing!" Ben says. They feel sad and scared. They don't know who owns the test thing or what it was for. They don't know if they are in trouble or not. "Maybe we should run away," Lila says. "Maybe we should say sorry," Ben says. They look at each other and don't know what to do. They see a man coming towards them. He looks angry and has a badge. He is the owner of the test thing. "Hey, you kids, what did you do to my test thing?" he says. He sees the smoke and the damage. He is very mad. He yells at them and tells them to go away. He says they are bad and stupid and naughty. Lila and Ben feel very bad. They cry and run away. They wish they never found the test thing. They wish they never broke it. They wish they could say sorry. They are poor friends. They are poor testers. They are poor kids.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to cook. She put on her apron and went to the kitchen. She wanted to make a yummy soup for her family. Sue put a big pot on the stove. She turned on the tap to fill the pot with water. She added some vegetables and a little bit of salt. She stirred the soup and waited for it to cook. When the soup was ready, Sue tasted it. It was terrible! She was sad, but she didn't give up. Sue added more salt and some pepper. She tasted the soup again, and this time it was good. Her family loved the soup, and Sue was happy that she didn't give up.
