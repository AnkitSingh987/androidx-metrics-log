Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the shop with her mom. Sue saw a pretty toy she wanted. "Mom, can I have this toy, please?" she asked. Her mom said, "No, Sue. You have many toys at home. You don't need more." Sue was sad but she knew her mom was right. She had so many toys that she was a bit spoiled. They finished shopping and went back home. At home, Sue played with her toys and had lots of fun. She forgot about the toy she saw at the shop. After playing, Sue felt tired. Her mom said, "It's time to take a nap, Sue." Sue went to her bed and closed her eyes. She had a nice nap and dreamed about playing with her toys. When she woke up, she was happy and ready to play again.

One day, a wolf went to play in the woods. The wolf was rare because it had a big, red spot on its back. It loved to run, jump and play with its friends, the birds and the rabbits. As the wolf was playing, it met a little girl. The girl was not scared of the rare wolf. She said, "Hi, wolf! Do you want to play with me?" The wolf wagged its tail and said, "Yes! I want to play!" So, the little girl and the rare wolf played all day. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. The birds and the rabbits joined in, too. They all had so much fun. At the end of the day, they all said, "Bye!" and went back to their homes. The rare wolf had found a new friend to play with.

Zara and Zane love to play with their toy cars. They zoom them on the floor, on the sofa, on the table. They make loud noises like vroom, beep, honk. They have fun together. One day, a new person comes to their house. His name is Zack. He is their cousin. He wants to play with them. But he is rude. He takes their toy cars without asking. He zooms them too fast and crashes them. He breaks one of Zara's cars. He laughs and says, "It's just a toy. Who cares?" Zara and Zane are sad and angry. They tell Zack to stop. They say, "That's not nice. You have to share. You have to be careful. You have to say sorry." But Zack does not listen. He says, "You are babies. I can do what I want. I don't care." Zara and Zane run to their mom. They tell her what Zack did. Their mom is upset. She says, "Zack, you are rude. You have to respect other people's things. You have to apologize. You have to fix the car. Or you can't play with them anymore." But Zack does not care. He says, "Whatever. I don't want to play with them anyway. They are boring. I have better toys at home." He grabs his backpack and runs to the door. He does not see the toy car on the floor. He trips and falls. He hurts his knee. He cries and says, "Ow, ow, ow. Help me. It hurts." Zara and Zane look at him. They feel sorry for him. But they also feel angry. They say, "That's what you get for being rude. You should have been nice. You should have listened. You should have cared." The moral of the story is: Be nice to others and respect their things. Or you might get hurt and lose your friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the station with her mom. The station was very popular. Many people went there to get on big trains. Lucy liked to watch the trains come and go. At the station, Lucy saw a cat. The cat had big eyes that would blink. Lucy said, "Mom, can we take the cat home?" Her mom said, "We have to see if the cat has a family first." Just then, the cat started to talk! The cat said, "I am a magic cat. I can help you find my family." Lucy and her mom were very surprised. They never heard a cat talk before. They all went on a fun trip to find the cat's family.
