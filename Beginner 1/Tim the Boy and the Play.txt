Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Play

One day, a boy named Tim found a flashlight. It was rough and old. He wanted to use it to play. He asked his friend, Sam, to play with him. "Sam, do you want to play with my flashlight?" Tim said. Sam said, "Yes! Let's play hide and seek with it!" So, they went outside to play. Tim used the flashlight to look for Sam. He looked behind trees and under bushes. Suddenly, the flashlight went out. It was very dark. Tim was scared, but he heard a funny sound. He followed the sound and found a little cat. The cat had a shiny collar with a small light. The light was bright enough to use. Tim found Sam with the help of the cat's light. They laughed and played with the cat until it was time to go home.

Jimmy was walking in the park with his Mom. Suddenly he saw a beautiful fountain. He turned to his Mom and asked with wide eyes, "Can we go see it?" His Mom smiled and said, "Of course, sweetheart. Let's go!" So they walked to the fountain. Jimmy looked at the fountain. He felt so excited and he just wanted to run around it but something stopped him. He felt frightened. He turned to his Mom and said, "I don't want to go closer. It's too scary!" His Mom smiled and said, "It's okay sweetheart, I'm here. We can just look at it for now." She put a comforting arm around Jimmy and he felt much better. Jimmy and Mom looked at the fountain together and it was beautiful, even from far away. They laughed and played and then went home with big smiles on their faces.

Lila and Tom are best friends. They like to play hide and seek in the park. Lila is good at hiding, but Tom is good at finding. One day, they play a new game. They call it peek and tell. Lila hides behind a big tree. She waits for Tom to find her. She hears his footsteps coming closer. She peeks from behind the tree. She sees Tom looking around. He does not see her. She smiles. She thinks she will win. But then, Tom stops. He turns around. He looks at the tree. He sees Lila's eyes. He says, "I found you, Lila! You peeked!" Lila comes out from behind the tree. She says, "No, I did not peek! You did not find me!" Tom says, "Yes, you did peek! I saw you! You have to tell the truth!" Lila says, "No, you did not see me! You are lying!" They start to argue. They do not sound like best friends. They sound like enemies. A lady who works in the park sees them. She comes over. She says, "What is the matter, children? Why are you fighting?" Lila and Tom tell her their stories. They both say they are right. They both say the other is wrong. The lady says, "Hmm, this is a tricky situation. How can we know who is telling the truth?" She thinks for a moment. Then she says, "I have an idea. Let's play peek and tell again. But this time, I will be the seeker. You two will be the hiders. And you will have to be reliable. That means you will have to do what you say. If you say you will not peek, you will not peek. If you say you will peek, you will peek. And then you will tell me the truth. Do you agree?" Lila and Tom nod. They agree. They think it is a fair game. They go to hide. The lady counts to ten. Then she says, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looks for them. She finds them. She asks them, "Did you peek?" Lila says, "Yes, I peeked. I'm sorry. I was curious." Tom says, "No, I did not peek. I was patient." The lady says, "Thank you for telling me the truth. You are both reliable. And you are both good at playing peek and tell. But you know what? You are also good at playing hide and seek. And you are better at playing together. So why don't you stop fighting and start being friends again?" Lila and Tom look at each other. They feel sorry. They say, "You are right. We are sorry. We are friends again." They hug. They smile. They say, "Let's play some more!" The lady says, "That's wonderful. Have fun, children. And remember, peek and tell is a fun game, but only if you tell the truth." The end.
