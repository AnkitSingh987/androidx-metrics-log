Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Hill

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They went up a big hill. The hill was icy and cold. Tim and Spot liked to play on the hill. As they played, Tim saw a girl named Sue at the top of the hill. Sue was scared. She said, "Help! I can't get down!" Tim and Spot wanted to help Sue. Tim said, "Don't panic, Sue! Spot and I will help you." Tim and Spot walked up the icy hill very slow. They got to Sue and held her hand. Together, they went down the hill safe and sound.

One day, a tough dog named Max found a big red jeep. He wanted to go for a ride. He jumped in and started the jeep. "Vroom, vroom!" said Max. He drove the jeep down the road, having lots of fun. As Max drove, he saw a little cat named Luna. Luna was scared because she could not escape from a tall tree. "Help me, Max!" said Luna. Max stopped the jeep and thought about how to help his friend. Max had an idea. He drove the jeep under the tree. The jeep was so big and tall that Luna could jump down onto it. "Thank you, Max!" said Luna. They both thought they would drive home, but the jeep started to fly! They were so surprised. Max and Luna had lots of fun flying in their magic jeep.

Ben and Lily are twins. They like to play outside in the sun. They have a camera. It is a toy camera, but they pretend it is real. One day, they go to the park with Mom. Mom sits on a bench and reads a book. Ben and Lily run around and look for things to take pictures of. "Look, Lily, a duck!" Ben says. He points to a pond. "Let's take a picture of it!" "OK, Ben!" Lily says. She holds the camera and presses the button. "Click!" The duck hears the sound and swims away. Ben and Lily laugh. They see more things to take pictures of: a slide, a flower, a dog, a ball. They have fun with the camera. "Ben, Lily, it's time to go home!" Mom calls. She puts the book in her bag. "OK, Mom!" Ben and Lily say. They run to Mom and hug her. "Did you have fun with the camera?" Mom asks. "Yes, Mom!" Ben and Lily say. "We took pictures of many things!" "Can I see them?" Mom asks. Ben and Lily show Mom the camera. It has a screen. On the screen, there are no pictures. Only colors and shapes. Mom smiles. "They are very pretty pictures!" she says. Ben and Lily smile too. They don't mind that the camera is not real. They have good memories in their heads. They go home with Mom. It is a warm day. They are happy.

One day, a little bear was walking in the woods. He was an original bear, not like the others. He liked to say "thank you" all the time. The little bear was very nice to everyone. The little bear met a big bird. The big bird had a red ball. The little bear wanted to play with the red ball. The big bird said, "You can play with my red ball if you say thank you." The little bear said, "Thank you, big bird!" and they played with the red ball. But then, something unexpected happened. The big bird took the red ball and flew away. The little bear was sad. He thought the big bird was his friend. The little bear learned that not everyone is nice, even if you say thank you.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max loved to dig and play all day. One day, Max found a big bone in the yard. He was very happy and wanted to show his friends. Max took the bone to his friend, the cat named Daisy. Daisy was sad because her food bowl was empty. Max felt regret because he could not share his bone with Daisy. So, Max went to find food for Daisy. Max found some food and filled Daisy's bowl. Daisy was happy and they played together. Max learned that sharing with friends is better than having a big bone. And they were happy friends forever.
