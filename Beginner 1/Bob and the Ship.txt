Category: Beginner
Title: Bob and the Ship

Once upon a time, a large dolphin named Bob lived in the sea. Bob loved to build things with his friends. They built big sand castles on the beach. They had a lot of fun. One day, Bob and his friends saw a large ship. They thought it was a fun new thing to build with. They swam to the ship and started to build. They did not know it was not a toy. Then, something unexpected happened. The ship started to sink! Bob and his friends tried to fix it, but they could not. The ship went down, and the people on the ship were not happy. Bob and his friends felt sad. They learned that they should not play with things that are not toys.

Once upon a time, there was a group of animals in the forest. They were all friends and liked to play together. In the group, there was a big bear, a small rabbit, and a funny monkey. One day, the friends found a harmless bug. The big bear said, "I think the bug is cute." The small rabbit said, "I disagree. I think the bug is scary." The funny monkey said, "I think the bug is funny, just like me!" They all talked about the bug and shared their thoughts. In the end, the friends agreed that everyone has different ideas, and that's okay. They continued to play and have fun together in the forest, with their new harmless bug friend.

One day, a little frog named Tim was jumping in the park. He saw a rare, shiny sheet on the ground. Tim jumped on the sheet and it made a fun noise. He was so happy and wanted to show his friend, Sam the squirrel. "Sam, Sam! Look at this rare sheet I found!" Tim said, jumping up and down. Sam came over and saw the sheet. "Wow, that's fun! Can I jump on it too?" Sam asked. Tim said yes, and they both jumped on the sheet together. But then, a big bird named Bob saw the sheet too. He wanted it all for himself. "That's my sheet! Give it back!" Bob said angrily. Tim and Sam were scared but didn't want to give up the sheet. They had an idea. "Let's all jump on the sheet together and share," said Tim. Bob thought about it and agreed. They all jumped on the sheet and had a great time. The rare sheet made everyone happy, and they learned that sharing is the best way to have fun.

Lily and Tom are having lunch with Mom. Mom gives them each a glass of juice. Lily likes juice. It is yummy and sweet. She drinks her juice fast. "Can I have more juice, Mom?" Lily asks. "Sure, honey. There is more juice in the fridge. You can take it yourself. But be careful with the glass. It can break," Mom says. Lily nods and gets up. She goes to the fridge and opens it. She sees the juice. She reaches for it. But she is too small. She cannot take the juice. She tries to stand on her toes. She stretches her arm. She almost touches the juice. But then she loses her balance. She falls down. The glass falls with her. It breaks. The juice spills. Lily cries. "Ow, ow, ow! Mom, help!" Lily shouts. Mom hears Lily. She runs to the kitchen. She sees Lily on the floor. She sees the glass and the juice. She is worried. "Oh, Lily, what happened? Are you hurt?" Mom asks. She picks up Lily and hugs her. She checks her for cuts. She does not see any. She is relieved. "I'm sorry, Mom. I wanted more juice. But I couldn't take it. The glass broke. It was too high. It was too hard," Lily says. "It's okay, Lily. I'm glad you are not hurt. But next time, please ask me for help. I can take the juice for you. Or you can use a plastic cup. It is safer than a glass. Do you understand?" Mom says. Lily nods. She is sorry. She is sad. She likes juice. But she does not like broken glass. "I understand, Mom. I'm sorry. Can I have more juice, please?" Lily asks. "Of course, honey. But first, let's clean up this mess. And then we can have more juice. And maybe a cookie. How does that sound?" Mom says. Lily smiles. She likes cookies. They are yummy and crunchy. She helps Mom clean up. She says sorry to Tom. He says it's okay. He shares his juice with her. They have cookies. They are happy. Mom hugs them. She loves them.
