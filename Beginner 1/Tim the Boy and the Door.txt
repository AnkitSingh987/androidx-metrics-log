Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Door

One day, a little boy named Tim put on his blue bathrobe. He felt so cozy and warm in it. He liked to wear it after his bath. Tim was always careful when he walked around in his bathrobe, so it would not get dirty. Tim saw his friend, Sue, outside. She was playing with her red ball. Tim wanted to play too, so he opened the door and waved to Sue. "Hi, Sue!" he said. Sue saw Tim and waved back. "Come play with me, Tim!" she said. Tim was careful as he ran to Sue. They played with the red ball and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim waved goodbye to Sue. He went back inside, took off his blue bathrobe, and hung it up. It was a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very bored. She did not know what to do. She looked around her room and saw a box. Inside the box, there were clothes to play dress-up. Sue had an idea. Sue decided to act like a queen. She put on a pretty dress and a shiny crown. She walked around her room and told her toys what to do. She felt happy and not bored anymore. Then, Sue's mom came into the room with a plate of cookies. Sue's mom said, "I made these cookies for my little queen." Sue smiled and shared the cookies with her toys. The cookies were yummy, and Sue was not bored anymore. The end.

One day, a big dog named Bob and a little cat named Sam went for a walk. They liked to play in the park. The sun was hot, and they were happy. Bob said, "Let's go to the big tree!" Sam said, "Yes, let's go!" As they walked, they saw a hard spike on the ground. Bob said, "Be careful, Sam! Don't step on the spike." Sam looked at the spike and said, "Okay, Bob. I will watch my step." They played near the big tree. They ran and jumped. Then, Sam saw the spike again. It was close to Bob's foot. Sam said, "Bob, look out!" Bob moved his foot away just in time. They were both safe. Bob said, "Thank you, Sam. You saved me!" Sam smiled and said, "We are friends. Friends help each other." They walked back home, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. The bear lived in a peaceful forest. He had many friends. They liked to play and act in the forest. One day, the bear felt cold. He needed to find a warm place. He asked his friends to help him. They all looked for a warm place together. They found a big cave. The cave was warm and nice. The bear was not cold anymore. They all played and acted in the cave. They were happy and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a normal soldier named Tom. He lived in a small house with his dog, Spot. They were very happy together. One day, Tom and Spot went for a walk in the park. They saw a big, mean cat. The cat was poking a little bird with its paw. The bird was very scared and sad. Tom and Spot wanted to help the bird. Tom went up to the cat and said, "Stop poking the bird!" The cat hissed at Tom, but it stopped poking the bird. The bird flew away, happy and safe. Tom and Spot were proud of what they did. They went home and had a big party to celebrate.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to play. Spot ran to the tree and started to dig. He was having a lot of fun! Then, Spot heard a distant sound. He stopped digging and looked around. "What was that?" he thought. He heard it again. It was a little girl shouting, "Help! Help!" Spot knew he needed to help her. Spot ran to the little girl. She was lost and scared. "Don't worry," Spot said. "I will help you find your way home." The little girl smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot!" Together, they walked back to her home, and she gave Spot a big hug.
