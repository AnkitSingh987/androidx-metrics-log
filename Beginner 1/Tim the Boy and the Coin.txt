Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Coin

One day, a boy named Tim found a coin on the ground. He picked it up and saw that it was a bad coin. It was dirty and had a mean face on it. Tim wanted to get rid of the bad coin. Tim went to his friend, Sam. He said, "Sam, I have a bad coin. Do you want it?" Sam looked at the coin and shook his head. "No, Tim. I don't want the bad coin. You should pass it to someone else." Tim went to his sister, Lily. He said, "Lily, I have a bad coin. Do you want it?" Lily looked at the coin and made a face. "Yuck, Tim. I don't want the bad coin. Pass it to someone else." Tim felt sad. He didn't know what to do with the bad coin. Then, he saw his mom. She said, "Tim, why don't you clean the coin? Maybe it won't be bad anymore." Tim washed the coin with soap and water. The mean face was gone, and the coin was shiny and good. Tim was happy, and he kept the coin in his pocket.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big vine on a tree. The vine is long and green. "Look, a vine!" Anna says. "We can swing on it!" "Let's try!" Ben says. They run to the vine. They are very excite. Anna goes first. She holds the vine with both hands. She jumps and swings in the air. She feels happy and free. "Wow, Anna, you are good!" Ben says. "Thank you, Ben. Your turn!" Anna says. She lets go of the vine and lands on the grass. Ben grabs the vine. He tries to swing like Anna. But he is not strong enough. He falls and hurts his knee. He cries. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben says. Anna hears Ben cry. She runs to him. She is a helpful friend. "Ben, are you okay?" Anna says. "No, Anna, it hurts!" Ben says. "I'm sorry, Ben. Let me help you." Anna says. She finds a leaf and wraps it around Ben's knee. She hugs him and says, "Don't worry, Ben. It will get better soon." "Thank you, Anna. You are a good friend." Ben says. They smile at each other. They are still friends. They go to find another game to play.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a tiny house with a big yard. Every day, he would run and play in the yard. He loved to chase his tail and bark at the birds. One morning, Spot woke up early to see the sunrise. The sky was pink and orange, and it made him very happy. He ran around the yard, jumping and barking with joy. As the sun came up, Spot saw a new friend. It was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was also poor, but he loved to play just like Spot. They played together all day, running and chasing each other. From that day on, Spot and Fluffy were best friends, and they always played together at sunrise.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big oak tree. Tim wanted to pass the tree and play on the other side. But there was a problem. A spoiled cat sat under the oak tree. The cat said, "You can't pass! This is my oak tree." Tim was sad. He wanted to play, but the cat would not let him pass. Tim thought for a moment, and then he had an idea. He picked up a toy and showed it to the cat. The cat saw the toy and wanted to play too. Tim threw the toy far away. The cat ran to get the toy. Now, Tim could pass the oak tree and play on the other side. He was happy, and the cat was happy too. They both had fun playing together.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a gray slide. The gray slide was very fun for all the kids. They loved to play on it every day. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a happy place. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to play on the gray slide. He climbed up the steps and slid down with a big smile on his face. He liked the slide very much. Tim went up and down many times. Near the park, there was a big bell that would ring every hour. When the bell rang, Tim knew it was time to go home. He heard the bell ring and waved goodbye to the gray slide. He couldn't wait to come back and play again tomorrow.
