Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Anna and the Slide

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. Tom wants to go on the slide, but Anna is scared. "Come on, Anna, it is fun!" Tom says. He runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. He sits on the top and smiles. He slides down very fast and laughs. Anna watches Tom. She wants to try, but she is afraid. She thinks the slide is too high and too fast. She suffers in her heart. She does not want to be a baby, but she does not want to be hurt. "Anna, Anna, come here!" Tom calls. He runs to Anna and holds her hand. "You can do it, Anna. I will help you. It is not scary, it is fun. Trust me." Tom leads Anna to the slide. He climbs up the ladder with her. He sits behind her and hugs her. He says, "Are you ready, Anna? We will go together. Just open your eyes and smile. You will see, it is amazing." Anna nods. She opens her eyes and smiles. She feels Tom's hug. She hears his voice. She trusts him. They slide down together. They feel the wind and the sun. They laugh. Anna is happy. She is not scared anymore. She likes the slide. She thanks Tom. They hug. They are friends. They play in the park.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a magazine for Lucy's dad. Lucy liked to point at things she saw in the store. She pointed at a toy, a book, and a ball. Her mom smiled and said, "We need to find the magazine, Lucy." They walked to the place where the magazines were. Lucy saw many different magazines. She wanted to find a reliable one for her dad. She pointed at a magazine with a big car on it. Her mom looked at it and said, "Yes, Lucy, that's a good one!" Lucy was happy that she helped her mom find the magazine. They went to the front of the store to pay for it. After they paid, Lucy held the magazine in her hands. She couldn't wait to give it to her dad. She knew he would love the reliable magazine she picked.

One day, a clever boy named Tim found a big rock. He thought it was a special rock. He took the rock to his friend, Sue. "Look at my rock!" he said. Sue was not happy. She wanted a special rock too. Sue was mad at Tim. She wanted to find a better rock. She looked and looked but did not find one. Sue saw an x-ray picture at the doctor's office. She thought it was a magic picture. "I will use this x-ray to find a great rock!" she said. Sue and Tim had a big fight. They both wanted the best rock. They did not share. They did not help each other. In the end, they lost their special rock and x-ray. They were both sad. The moral of the story is to share and be kind to your friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He loved to nap under a big tree in the park. One sunny day, Tom saw a pretty leaf fall from the tree. He wanted to play with the leaf, but he was too lazy to move. A little girl named Sue was walking by and saw Tom. She said, "Hey, cat! Do you want to play with me?" Tom looked at Sue and then at the leaf. He wanted to play but was still too lazy to move. So, Sue picked up the leaf and threw it in the air. Tom watched the leaf as it flew through the sky. He couldn't help but jump up and try to catch it. Tom and Sue laughed and played with the leaf all day. Tom wasn't lazy anymore. He learned that playing with friends was more fun than napping. The next day, Tom saw a toy car near the tree. He remembered how much fun he had with Sue and the leaf. So, Tom decided to drive the toy car around the park. He had so much fun driving and playing with the car. From that day on, Tom was not a lazy cat anymore. He loved to play with Sue, drive the toy car, and chase leaves in the park. And they all lived happily ever after.
