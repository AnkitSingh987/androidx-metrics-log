Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Gear

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his gear. One day, his mom told him they were going on a trip. Tim was very excited and wanted to pack his gear. "Mom, can I pack my gear?" Tim asked. "Yes, Tim, but don't pack too much. We have to save room for food," his mom said. Tim smiled and ran to his room to pack his gear. But Tim did not listen to his mom. He packed all his gear and left no room for food. On the trip, Tim played with all his gear. He had so much fun. But soon, he got hungry. He went to his mom and asked for food. His mom looked sad and said, "Tim, I told you not to pack too much gear. Now we have no food." Tim felt very spoiled. He learned that it is important to listen to his mom.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big pond with ducks and fish. Lily wanted to swim in the pond, but Tom was afraid of the water. "Come on, Tom, let's swim with the ducks!" Lily said. "The water is mild and nice, not cold and scary." "No, Lily, I don't want to swim. I want to sit on the bench and watch the birds." Tom said. "Swimming is hard and wet, and I might sink or get bitten." Lily did not listen to Tom. She ran to the pond and jumped in. She splashed and laughed and tried to catch the ducks. Tom sat on the bench and watched her. He felt lonely and bored. Soon, Lily got tired and cold. She wanted to get out of the pond, but she could not find the edge. She started to cry and call for help. The ducks and fish swam away from her. Tom heard her and ran to the pond. He saw a big stick and threw it to Lily. Lily grabbed the stick and Tom pulled her out of the pond. "Lily, are you okay?" Tom asked. "You are wet and dirty and sad." "I'm sorry, Tom. I was silly and foolish. I should have listened to you." Lily said. "Swimming is not fun when you are alone and scared. I want to sit with you and watch the birds." Tom hugged Lily and gave her his jacket. They sat on the bench and watched the birds. They learned that playing together is better than playing alone, and that listening to your friends is smarter than doing what you want.

Once upon a time, there lived a lucky bird. He lived in a big, blue sky, and he could soar higher than other birds. One day, he decided to fly down to the ground and see what he could find. He flew to the backyard of a house and noticed a refrigerator standing near the back door. The bird thought it was very strange, so he opened the door and stepped inside. He saw food and drinks inside the fridge. He was so surprised, he decided to stay for a little snack. Just then, a small voice shouted, "Hey! What are you doing in my fridge?" The bird jumped and looked around. The voice belonged to a small girl. She looked up at the bird with a smile on her face. The bird told the girl why he was inside the fridge, and she laughed. She said, "It's ok, you can stay a while and get some snacks. You're lucky, because no other bird has opened a fridge door!" The bird was so glad and thanked the girl. Then, he flew back up to the big, blue sky and soared through the clouds, feeling very lucky.

Once upon a time, there was a big square. The square lived in a small house. One day, the square wanted to go outside and play. But first, it needed to dress up. The square put on a big hat and big shoes. The square went outside to play. It saw many friends in the park. They played with a ball and ran around. The square was very happy. It laughed and danced with its friends. But then, the sky turned dark, and it started to rain. The square's big hat and big shoes got wet. The square was sad and cold. It went back home and took off the wet clothes. The square wished it had stayed home, where it was warm and dry.
