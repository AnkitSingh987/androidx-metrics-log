Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Plants

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy spray bottle. One day, he had a fun idea. He wanted to spray water on his big sister's plants. He thought it would help them grow big and strong. Tim went outside with his spray bottle. He saw his sister's plants looking helpless and dry. He knew they needed water. So, he started to spray them with his toy bottle. The plants began to look happy and not so helpless anymore. But then, his big sister came out and saw what Tim was doing. She was not happy. She told Tim to stop spraying her plants. Tim felt sad, but he knew he had to listen to his sister. Later, his sister saw how much the plants had grown. She realized that Tim's idea was a good one. She thanked him for helping her plants and they both played together, spraying the plants with water.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his cheap toy car. One day, he went to the park with his mom and his toy car. At the park, Tim saw a big maze. He wanted to play in the maze with his toy car. But his mom said, "No, Tim, don't go in there. It's not safe." Tim did not listen to his mom. He went into the maze with his toy car. Tim played in the maze for a long time. He got lost and started to cry. He tried to find his way out but could not. Tim was very scared. He tried to stir some leaves to make a path but it did not help. Tim stayed in the maze and never found his way out.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. Ben wants to go on the slide. Lily is scared. "Come on, Lily, it is fun!" Ben says. He runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. Lily follows him slowly. At the top of the slide, Ben sees a big hole. He thinks it is a game. He tries to put his head in the hole. Lily sees him and shouts. "Ben, no! That is foolish! You can get hurt!" Lily says. She grabs his arm and pulls him back. She prevents him from putting his head in the hole. Ben is angry. He does not like Lily to stop him. He pushes her away. He tries to put his head in the hole again. But then he hears a loud roar. He looks and sees a lion in the hole. The lion is hungry and angry. It tries to bite Ben. Ben is scared. He screams and runs away. Lily runs after him. She helps him get down the slide. They run to the gate and leave the park. Ben is sorry. He hugs Lily and says, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend. You saved me from the lion. I was foolish to put my head in the hole. I will not do that again." Lily smiles and says, "You are welcome, Ben. I am glad you are safe. You are a good friend too. But next time, let us play on the swings. They are more fun and less dangerous."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his red bike. One day, Tim saw a big tree with delicious red apples. He wanted to touch the apples, but they were too high. Tim had an idea. He asked his friend, Sue, to help him. Sue said, "I can help you, Tim. Let's ride your bike to the tree and get the apples." They rode the bike together, and it was fun. When they got to the tree, Sue lifted Tim up. Tim could touch the apples now. He picked the delicious red apples, and they shared them. They were very happy, and they rode the bike back home.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car and make it go fast. One day, he saw a spark in the sky. It was pretty and bright. Tim wanted to see where the spark came from. Tim went outside and saw the spark rise up into the sky. He followed the spark and found a big hill. Tim was very excited. He wanted to climb the hill and see the spark up close. So, he started to climb the hill with his toy car. When Tim reached the top, he saw the spark was a pretty kite. A girl named Sue was flying the kite. Tim and Sue became friends and played together. They flew the kite and raced their toy cars. They had a lot of fun and the naughty little boy learned to share and be nice.
