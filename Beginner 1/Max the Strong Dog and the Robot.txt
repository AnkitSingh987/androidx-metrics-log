Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Strong Dog and the Robot

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max liked to run and play. One day, he saw a big fan. He wanted to see what it was. Max went close to the fan and felt the air. It felt nice. Max wanted to play with the fan, but he did not know how. He tried to push the fan, but it was too big. Then, something unexpected happened. The fan started to move on its own! Max was scared and wanted to escape. Max ran away from the fan, but the fan followed him. It was not a fan, but a friendly robot! The robot just wanted to play with Max. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was an eager dog. He loved to play and run. One day, Spot saw a big bone in the yard. He wanted to have the bone, but it was not his. Spot saw his friend, Max, the dog from next door. Max said, "That is my bone. You cannot have it." Spot was sad, but he wanted to be a good friend. He thought of a way to solve the problem. Spot had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game. If I win, you give me the bone. If you win, you keep the bone." Max agreed, and they played the game. Spot won the game, and Max gave him the bone. They both were happy and played together with the bone.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy machine. This machine could fold clothes. It lived in a big house with a little boy named Tim and his mom. One day, Tim's mom asked the machine to fold all the clothes. But the machine did not want to fold the clothes. It wanted to play with Tim. So, the machine did not fold the clothes. Tim's mom was not happy. She told the machine to fold the clothes. Tim saw the machine was sad. He had an idea. He asked his mom if the machine could play after it folded the clothes. Tim's mom said yes. The machine was happy. It folded all the clothes very fast. Then, Tim and the machine played together. They had so much fun. From that day on, the machine always folded the clothes and then played with Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. The car had lots of gear. The car wanted to show its gear to a small blue car. One day, the big red car went to the deep water. The small blue car was there too. The big red car said, "Look at my gear!" The small blue car looked and liked the gear. The big red car and the small blue car played in the deep water. They showed their gear to each other. They were happy and had fun.

One day, a boy named Tim found a wet lamp in the park. He picked it up and saw that it was very dirty. Tim took the lamp home to clean it. He wanted to make it work again. When Tim got home, he showed the lamp to his mom. "Mom, look at this wet lamp I found," he said. "Can we clean it and make it work again?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Let's clean it and see if it works." After they cleaned the lamp, they tried to turn it on. But the lamp was still wet inside. When they tried to turn it on, there was a loud noise and a flash. The lamp broke and the room went dark. Tim and his mom were sad because they couldn't fix the lamp. They had to close the door and go to bed without any light.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a restless boy who loved to play with his toy car in the garage. One sunny day, Tom went to the garage to play with his toy car. While playing, Tom saw a small fire in the garage. He was scared and called for his mom. "Mom, come fast! The garage has a burn!" Tom shouted. His mom came running to help. The fire was small, but it could grow big and hurt the garage. Tom's mom used water to stop the burn. The garage was safe now. Tom learned that he needed to be careful when playing in the garage. His mom hugged him and said, "Next time, tell me if you see a burn, and we will fix it together." Tom was happy that his mom helped him, and he promised to be more careful.
