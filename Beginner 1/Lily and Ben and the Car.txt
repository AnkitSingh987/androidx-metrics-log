Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Car

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with toys and books. But today is a special day. It is their birthday. Mom and Dad have a big cake and many gifts for them. They sing happy birthday and blow the candles. Then they open the gifts. Lily gets a pink dress, a doll, and a puzzle. Ben gets a blue shirt, a car, and a ball. They are very happy and say thank you. But then Lily sees Ben's car. It is shiny and fast. She wants to play with it. She asks Ben, "Can I have your car?" Ben says, "No, it is mine. You have your doll." Lily frowns. She does not like the doll. She thinks it is boring. She says, "But I like your car. It is better than my doll." Ben says, "No, it is not. My car is for boys. Your doll is for girls." Lily gets angry. She grabs the car from Ben. Ben gets angry too. He grabs the doll from Lily. They start to fight and pull the toys. Mom and Dad hear the noise. They come to the room. They see Lily and Ben fighting. They are not happy. They say, "Stop it! You are twins. You should share and be nice to each other." They take the car and the doll from them. They wrap them in paper. They say, "You cannot play with these toys until you say sorry and hug each other." Lily and Ben look at each other. They feel sad. They do not want to lose their toys. They do not want to make Mom and Dad unhappy. They say sorry and hug each other. Mom and Dad smile. They give them back their toys. They say, "Good. Now you can play together. And remember, it does not matter if the toy is pink or blue. What matters is that you have fun and love each other." Lily and Ben nod. They agree. They say, "We love you, Mom and Dad. And we love each other too." They play with their toys. They share and take turns. They have fun. They are happy. It is a good birthday.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to chew on his toys. One day, Max went to the park with his best friend, Tom. The park had big trees with palm leaves. Max and Tom liked to play under the trees. Tom said, "Max, don't chew on the palm leaves. They may be dull and not fun to chew on." Max listened to Tom and they played with a ball instead. They had a lot of fun at the park. Later, Max saw a palm leaf on the ground. He remembered what Tom said, but he wanted to try it. Max chewed on the dull leaf, but it was not fun. He missed his toys. Max learned that Tom was right. The palm leaves were not fun to chew on. Max and Tom went home and played with Max's toys. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a vendor who sold heavy things. One day, a little boy came to the vendor's shop. The boy wanted to help the vendor. "Can I help you?" the boy asked. The vendor smiled and said, "Yes, you can have this heavy box." The boy tried to lift the heavy box, but it was too heavy for him. He asked his friend, a strong dog, to help him. The dog came and helped the boy lift the heavy box. Together, they carried the box to the vendor's car. The vendor was very happy and thanked the boy and the dog. The boy and the dog were happy too because they could help the vendor. They all had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a monkey named Mo. He loved to slide down trees and play with his friends. One day, Mo wanted to slide down the biggest tree in the jungle. He asked his friends, "Can I slide down this tree?" But his friends said it was too hard. Mo was determined to slide down that tree, so he set about measuring it. He measured and measured, until he found the spot where he felt he could slide. Mo grabbed the tree and started to slide. He was going faster and faster. Suddenly, he felt a strong hand on his shoulder. It was his friend. His friend said, "That's too hard - you shouldn't slide down this tree!" Mo was disappointed, but he knew his friend was right. So, they decided to find an easier tree to slide down. They found one nearby and Mo could slide down that one instead.
