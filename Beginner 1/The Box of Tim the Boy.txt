Category: Beginner
Title: The Box of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a happy boy named Tim. He loved to play and act like a hero. One day, he found a toy arrow in his toy box. He was very excited to play with it. Tim went to the park with his arrow. He saw his friend, Sue. "Look, Sue! I have an arrow!" said Tim. Sue was happy to see the arrow too. "Let's act like heroes and save the day!" she said. They both laughed and played together. They acted like heroes all day long. They saved the trees, the flowers, and the bugs. They were very happy to play together. At the end of the day, Tim and Sue went home. They couldn't wait to play and act like heroes again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a bright little bird. The bird lived in a big tree. One day, the bird saw a twig on the ground. The bird wanted to use the twig to make her nest bigger. The bird asked her friend, the squirrel, "Can you help me get the twig?" The squirrel said, "Yes, I will help you." So, the squirrel picked up the twig with his mouth and carried it up the tree. The bird said, "Thank you, squirrel. Now, please put the twig here." The squirrel put the twig where the bird ordered. They worked together and made the nest bigger and stronger. The bird and the squirrel were very happy and played together every day.

Lily liked to splash in the puddles after the rain. She put on her boots and her coat and ran outside. She saw a big puddle on the porch and jumped in it. She laughed as the water splashed on her legs and the porch. But Lily did not see the fat cat that was sleeping on the porch. The cat did not like the water or the noise. The cat woke up and saw Lily. The cat was angry and scared. The cat hissed and scratched Lily's face. Lily screamed and ran back inside. She had blood and water on her face and her coat. She cried and looked for her mom. Her mom heard her and came to help her. Her mom saw the cat and the puddle and the scratch. Her mom was mad and sad. Her mom hugged Lily and cleaned her face. She put a bandage on the scratch and gave her a kiss. She told Lily to never splash on the porch again. She told Lily to stay away from the fat cat. She told Lily to be careful and quiet. Lily was sorry and hurt. She did not like the fat cat or the porch or the puddle. She did not want to splash anymore. She wanted to stay inside and play with her toys. She learned a hard lesson. She had a bad day.

Ben and Lily are playing with their toy trucks in the yard. They like to load the trucks with sand, rocks and leaves. They make a big pile of sand and pretend it is a mountain. "Look, I can load more sand than you!" Ben says to Lily. He scoops up a lot of sand with his shovel and dumps it on his truck. "No, you can't! I can load more rocks than you!" Lily says to Ben. She picks up some rocks and puts them on her truck. They keep loading their trucks until they are full. Then they drive them around the yard and make noises. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep! They see a big circle on the ground. It is a dirty puddle. It has water and mud in it. "Let's drive our trucks through the circle!" Ben says to Lily. He thinks it will be fun. "Okay, let's go!" Lily says to Ben. She follows him. They drive their trucks through the circle. Splash, splash, splosh, splosh! The water and mud fly everywhere. Their trucks get dirty. Their clothes get dirty. Their faces get dirty. They laugh and giggle. They think it is fun. But then they hear a voice. It is Mom. "Ben, Lily, what are you doing? You are so dirty! Come here right now!" Mom says. She is not happy. Ben and Lily stop laughing. They look at Mom. They look at their trucks. They look at each other. They say, "Uh-oh."
