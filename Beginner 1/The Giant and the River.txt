Category: Beginner
Title: The Giant and the River

Once upon a time, there was a big giant. The giant liked to splash in the water. One day, the giant saw a little boy by the river. The little boy was sad because he did not know how to splash. The giant wanted to help the little boy. He said, "I will show you how to splash." The little boy was scared at first, but the giant was very nice. The giant did not know that the little boy was ignorant about swimming. The giant and the little boy went into the water. The giant showed the little boy how to splash. The little boy tried to splash, but he could not swim. The giant saw the little boy needed help. He quickly picked up the little boy and took him out of the water. The little boy was happy that the giant saved him. They became good friends. From that day on, the giant and the little boy played together by the river. The giant taught the little boy how to swim and splash.

Once upon a time, there was a witch. She had a big black hat. She lived in a little house in the woods. One day, the witch went for a walk. She saw a cat. The cat was black too. The witch and the cat became friends. They played and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The black cat jumped on the witch's hat. The hat fell off, and the witch turned into a beautiful princess! The princess and the cat lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big meeting with her friends. They wanted to think of an original game to play. Lily was excited but also a little scared. She knew she needed to be brave. On the way to the meeting, Lily met a nice cat. The cat said, "Lily, I know you need help. I have an idea for your original game. But first, you must find a red ball." Lily thanked the cat and went to look for the red ball. After looking everywhere, Lily found the red ball. She took it to the meeting with her friends. She said, "I found this red ball. Let's play a new game where we take turns throwing it and catching it!" Her friends loved the idea. They had so much fun playing the original game. Lily was happy she had been brave and found the red ball.

One day, a furry dog named Max went for a walk in the woods. He saw a long stick on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth. Max did not know it, but the stick was really a spear. Max carried the spear to his friend, a big cat named Bob. Bob saw Max and said, "Wow, Max! What a big stick you have!" Max wagged his tail and said, "I found it in the woods. Let's play with it!" As they played, they heard a loud noise. They saw a big bear coming towards them. Max and Bob were scared. But then, Max realized the spear could help them. He held the spear in his mouth and pointed it at the bear. The bear saw the spear and stopped. He looked at Max and Bob, and then he laughed. "I'm not a real bear," he said. "I'm just a man in a bear suit!" Max and Bob were so relieved and they all became friends. They played together with the spear all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a boy named Tim found a fragile jar of jam. It was red and sweet. Tim wanted to eat the jam, but he knew it was not his. Tim asked his mom, "Can I eat the jam, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. That is not our jam. It belongs to our neighbor, Mrs. Brown. We need to give it back." Tim was sad, but he wanted to be a good boy. He picked up the fragile jar of jam to give it back to Mrs. Brown. But as he walked, he tripped and the jar fell. The jam was all over the floor. Tim cried and wished he could reverse what happened. He tried to clean the jam but it was too late. Now, Mrs. Brown's jam was gone and Tim felt very bad.
