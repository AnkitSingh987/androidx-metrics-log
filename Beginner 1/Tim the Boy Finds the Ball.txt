Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his ball in the park when he saw a stranger. The stranger was a big man with a big hat. Tim felt scared. The stranger saw Tim's ball and wanted to change it. He said, "I have a better ball for you." Tim didn't know what to do. He didn't want to change his ball, but the stranger kept asking. Tim was brave and said, "No, thank you." The stranger was sad, but he left Tim alone. Tim felt happy that he didn't change his ball. He played in the park and had a lot of fun.

One day, a grumpy shrimp named Sam lived in the sea. He was very small and always felt sad. All the other sea animals were big and strong, and they did not want to play with Sam. One day, Sam met a fish named Tim. Tim asked, "Do you want to play?" Sam was happy and shouted, "Yes, I want to play!" They played together and had so much fun. As they were playing, a big net came down and caught Tim. Sam was scared, but he wanted to help his friend. He shouted very loud, and a big, strong whale heard him. The whale swam fast and broke the net. Tim was free! The whale said, "You may be small, but you are very brave." Sam was proud and not grumpy anymore. He had new friends and was happy.

---

One day, a cat named Max was very hungry. Max wanted to find some food. He saw a big bowl of red sauce. Max thought it looked very yummy. Max did not know the sauce was spicy. Max moved close to the bowl and took a big lick. "Oh no!" Max said, "This is spicy!" Max's mouth was hot. He needed something to make it feel better. He saw a big cup of water. Max drank the water fast. But the cup had a leak. The water went everywhere. Max was wet and the floor was wet. Max was sad because he was still hungry. But then, Max saw a big fish on a plate. Max was happy again. The spicy sauce and the leak helped Max find a yummy fish.

---

Lily liked to walk with her dog Spot. Spot was a good dog. He did not bark or bite. He liked to play and run. Lily and Spot were happy. One day, Lily saw a big cake on the table. It was for her mom's birthday. Lily wanted some cake. She thought mom would not mind. She took a big piece and gave some to Spot. They ate the cake. It was sweet and yummy. But mom did mind. She came home and saw the cake. She was very angry. She said Lily and Spot spoiled the cake. She said they were bad and naughty. She said they could not walk or play for a week. She said they had to stay in their room. Lily and Spot were sad. They did not mean to spoil the cake. They did not know it was for mom's birthday. They just wanted some cake. They said sorry to mom, but mom did not listen. She shut the door and left them alone. Lily and Spot were poor. They had no cake, no walk, no play. They had no fun. They had no mom. They cried and cried. They wished they never saw the cake. They wished they never spoiled the cake. They wished they were happy again.

---

Lily and Sam were playing with a basketball in the park. They liked to bounce it and throw it to each other. They had a lot of fun. But then, a big dog came running towards them. It was barking and wagging its tail. It wanted to play too. It jumped and grabbed the basketball in its mouth. It ran away with it. "Hey, that's our ball!" Lily shouted. She and Sam ran after the dog. They wanted their ball back. The dog was faster than them. It ran across the street and into a yard. It dropped the ball and started to dig a hole. It buried the ball in the dirt. Lily and Sam reached the yard. They saw the dog and the hole. They saw their ball was dirty and wet. "Oh no, our ball is ruined!" Sam cried. He was sad and angry. Lily was scared. She saw a sign on the fence. It said "Beware of the dog". She saw the dog was big and had sharp teeth. She thought it might bite them. She grabbed Sam's hand. She said "We have to go. The dog is not nice. It might hurt us." She and Sam ran back to the street. They left the ball behind. They panicked. They wanted to go home. They hoped their mom would buy them a new ball.
