Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Ann and the Pool

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Ann. She loved to play in her pool. Every day, Ann would splash in the water and have lots of fun. One day, Ann splashed so much that she started to drift to the left side of the pool. When she looked back, she realised that she had moved too far away from the sides. She felt helpless and started to cry. Just then, her mom rushed to the pool and said, "Don't worry, Ann! I'm here. I'll help you get back to the side." Ann smiled and her mom held her tight as she helped her back to the side. Ann was so happy that she hugged her mom and said, "Thank you!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb. Every day, he would go outside and climb on the big tree in his yard. Tim was very good at climbing. He would climb high up in the tree and look at the world around him. One day, Tim saw a big, red ball stuck in the tree. He wanted to get the ball down. So, he climbed higher and higher. As a result, he reached the ball and grabbed it. Tim was so happy to get the ball down from the tree. Tim climbed down from the tree with the ball in his hands. He showed the ball to his mom and dad. They were very proud of him for climbing so high and getting the ball. Tim played with the big, red ball all day long and had lots of fun.

One day, Tim found a blue folder in his room. He did not know what was inside. He asked his mom, "Mom, what is in this blue folder?" His mom said, "Open it and see, Tim." Tim opened the blue folder and saw many pretty pictures. He liked the pictures a lot. He wanted to show them to his friend, Sam. So, he went to Sam's house. When Tim got to Sam's house, he heard Sam cry. Tim asked, "Sam, why are you sad?" Sam said, "I lost my red ball." Tim said, "Don't worry, Sam. Let's look for the red ball together." Tim and Sam looked and looked. They found the red ball under a big tree. Sam was happy again. They played with the red ball and looked at the pretty pictures in the blue folder. They had a fun day. The moral of the story is: Help your friends when they are sad. It will make them happy, and you will have fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the park. One day, Tim went to the park and saw a little dog. The little dog was running and playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with the little dog too. Tim and the little dog played with the ball. They ran and ran, and they had lots of fun. But then, Tim saw a knife on the ground. He knew it was not safe to play with a knife. Tim was a good boy, so he did not touch the knife. Suddenly, a big bird flew down from the sky. The big bird picked up the knife with its beak. Tim and the little dog watched the big bird fly away with the knife. They were very surprised! The big bird took the knife to a tree and used it to cut some branches. The big bird was making a nest for its babies. Tim and the little dog smiled, happy that the knife was now being used for a good thing. They went back to playing with the ball, and they had a great day at the park.

Once there was a boy. His name was Max, and he was three years old. One day, he was walking with his mom and dad when he heard a noise in the distance. He curiously asked his mom, "What was that noise?" His mom said, "That was a van, Max. It sounds wild, doesn't it?" Max was so excited by the sound of the wild van that he ran to see it. When he reached the street corner, he saw the colorful van with its bright and shiny lights. He thought it was the most amazing thing he had ever seen. Max waved to the driver and jumped for joy. After the van drove away, his mom and dad collected him and said, "Let's go home, Max. It's time for bed." And so Max went home, still smiling at the memory of the wild van.
