Category: Beginner
Title: Rupert and Sally and the Cold

Once upon a time there were two friends, Rupert and Sally, who dared each other to explore the cold winter outdoors, even though it was chilly. Rupert said, "I dare you to run fast in the snow." Sally said, "I'll try, but can you help me?" Rupert smiled and said, "Of course I'll help you. It's going to be a lot of fun!" So Rupert and Sally went to the backyard and began running as fast as they could in the snow. Rupert was very helpful and held Sally's hand when she got cold. They both had a great time. When it was time to go inside, Sally was happy she had accepted Rupert's dare and was thankful for his helpfulness. Together they had conquered the cold winter and made it a fun and exciting adventure!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the park near his house. One day, Tim found a mine. The mine was full of toys and games. Tim was very happy and a little anxious. He wanted to take all the toys home. Tim met a girl named Sue at the park. Sue saw the mine too. She wanted to play with the toys. Tim said, "This is my mine. I found it first." Sue was sad, but she understood. She asked Tim if she could play with just one toy. Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, you can play with one toy." Tim and Sue played with the toys from the mine all day. They had so much fun. Before they left the park, Tim marked the mine with a big stick. He wanted to make sure he could find it again. Tim and Sue went home, excited to play with the toys from the mine the next day.

Once upon a time, there lived a bossy girl called Sophie. She had a best friend named Jack. One day, Sophie found a pile of dust in the garden. She told Jack, “Let’s make something from this dust!” Jack replied, “I don’t want to. Let’s just play on the swing.” But Sophie was bossy, so she said, “No, we will make something from the dust.” So, they got to work. First, they used their hands to mix the dust together. Then, they shaped it into a ball. Sophie said, “Now let’s reverse it.” Jack didn’t know what reverse meant. He asked, “What is reverse?” Sophie explained, “It means to undo our work, and make it different.” So, Jack and Sophie began to undo their work. They made different shapes from the dust. In the end, they had made a lovely dust sculpture. They were both very proud.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a new wrap. The wrap is red and soft. They can use the wrap to make a cape, a tent, or a scarf. One day, they go to the park with the wrap. They see a big swing. Anna wants to swing on it. She says, "Ben, let me swing first. I have the wrap. I can be a superhero." Ben says, "No, Anna. I want to swing too. We can share the wrap. We can take turns." Anna says, "No, Ben. It is my wrap. I got it for my birthday. You can't have it." She runs to the swing with the wrap. She ties the wrap around her neck. She sits on the swing. She pushes herself with her feet. She swings high and fast. She feels like a superhero. But then, the wind blows hard. The wrap flies off her neck. It gets caught on a branch. Anna can't reach it. She cries, "Help! Help! My wrap is stuck!" Ben hears her cry. He runs to her. He says, "Don't worry, Anna. I will help you. I have a stick. I can get your wrap." He finds a long stick. He climbs on the swing. He swings up to the branch. He pokes the wrap with the stick. He frees the wrap. He catches it. He swings down. He gives the wrap to Anna. Anna says, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good friend. You are a superhero." Ben says, "You are welcome, Anna. You are a good friend too. You are a superhero." They hug. They say, "We are sorry. We are sorry. We should share. We should share." They share the wrap. They swing together. They have fun. They learn to be kind and generous. They are happy.
