Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Climb

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb trees. One day, he went for a walk with his mom and dad. They walked and walked until they found a beautiful place with water and trees. This place was called an oasis. Tim saw a big tree near the water. He wanted to climb it. His mom and dad said, "Be careful, Tim!" Tim started to climb the tree. He went up and up. He was very happy. As Tim climbed higher, he saw something in the tree. It was a big, disgusting bug. Tim didn't like the bug, but he kept climbing. When he got to the top, he saw the whole oasis. It was so pretty! Tim waved to his mom and dad, and they waved back. Tim loved the oasis and the tree, even with the disgusting bug.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird. The bird liked to fly high in the sky. One day, the bird wanted to go on a journey. The bird said to its friend, the cat, "I want to fly far away and see new things." The cat said, "I will go with you on your journey." So, the yellow bird and the cat went on a journey together. They saw big trees, pretty flowers, and a big blue pond. The cat liked to watch the fish in the pond. At the end of their journey, the yellow bird and the cat found a big, red barn. Inside the barn, they found new friends like a cow, a pig, and a horse. The yellow bird said, "I am happy we went on this journey." The cat agreed and they all played together in the big, red barn.

Tom and Anna were playing in the yard. They saw a big pile of leaves. They ran to the pile and jumped in. They laughed and threw leaves at each other. They had fun. But then they heard a loud bark. A big dog came out of the bushes. The dog was filthy. He had mud and dirt all over his fur. He looked angry. He ran towards Tom and Anna. Tom and Anna were scared. They got out of the pile and ran to the house. But the dog was faster. He caught up with them and jumped on Tom. He bit Tom's arm. Tom cried out in pain. "Help! Help!" Tom shouted. Anna heard Tom's cry. She loved her brother. She did not want him to get hurt. She picked up a stick and ran to the dog. She hit the dog with the stick. The dog let go of Tom's arm and growled at Anna. "Go away! Bad dog!" Anna yelled. The dog was surprised. He did not like the stick. He ran away from Anna and Tom. He went back to the bushes. Tom and Anna hugged each other. They were happy they were safe. They ran to the house and told their mom what happened. Their mom was proud of them. She hugged them and kissed their faces. She cleaned Tom's arm and put a bandage on it. She gave them cookies and milk. They felt better. They thanked their mom and each other. They learned to be careful of filthy dogs. They still liked to play in the leaves. But they always looked around first.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. She had a little dog named Spot. One day, Sue and Spot went for a walk in the park. They were very happy. As they walked, Sue saw a big tree. She thought it was a good place for a search. She told Spot, "Let's play hide and seek. I will hide, and you will look for me." Spot wagged his tail and barked to answer. Sue hid behind the tree, and Spot began to search. He looked and looked, but he could not find Sue. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and said, "Hello, Spot! I saw Sue go behind the tree. She is hiding there." Spot was surprised but happy. He found Sue, and they laughed and played together. The big bird joined in, and they all had a fun day at the park.
