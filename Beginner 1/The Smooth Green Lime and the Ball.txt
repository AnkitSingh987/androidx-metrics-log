Category: Beginner
Title: The Smooth Green Lime and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a smooth green lime. The lime lived in a big fruit bowl with many other fruits. The lime was very happy, but it wanted a friend who looked just like it. One day, the lime saw a round, green ball. The lime thought, "This ball looks like me! We match!" So, the lime rolled over to the ball and said, "Hello, friend! We are both green and round. We match! Let's play together." The ball was happy to have a new friend and they played all day. They rolled around and had lots of fun. The lime and the ball were very happy because they matched, and they were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a small bird named Timmy. Timmy loved to soar high in the sky. One day, Timmy found a whistle on the ground. "Look, Mom! I found a whistle!" Timmy said. "Can I keep it?" His mom said, "Yes, but you must be careful with it. Do not use it to make others sad." Timmy was so happy. He flew with the whistle in his beak. He saw his friend, Benny the squirrel, on a tree. Timmy blew the whistle, and it made a loud sound. Benny got scared and fell off the branch. Benny said, "Timmy, that was not nice!" Timmy felt sad for scaring his friend. He said, "I am sorry, Benny. I will not do it again." From that day on, Timmy used the whistle to make fun sounds and music with his friends. They all danced and played together. Timmy learned that it is important to be kind and think about how others feel.

Once upon a time, there was a little pony named Fast. Fast was a very fast pony. He loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Fast wanted to win a race. He wanted to show everyone that he could succeed. So, he and his friends decided to have a race. They all lined up and got ready to run. The race began, and Fast ran as fast as he could. His friends were fast, too, but Fast was the fastest. He ran and ran, and soon he was in the lead. He could see the finish line up ahead. Fast knew he could succeed. Fast crossed the finish line first. He had succeeded! All his friends cheered and clapped for him. Fast was very happy. From that day on, everyone knew that Fast was the fastest pony in the land.

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and plant seeds. They had a small patch of corn that they watered every day. They were very proud of their corn. One day, they saw a big bird flying over their corn. It was a crow. The crow was hungry and wanted to eat the corn. It landed on the patch and started to peck at the ears. Tom and Lily were scared and angry. They shouted at the crow. "Go away, crow! This is our corn! You are bad and mean!" they said. But the crow did not listen. It kept pecking and tearing the corn. Tom and Lily felt helpless. They did not know how to stop the crow. They ran to the house and looked for their mom. "Mom, mom, help us! There is a crow in our corn! It is eating our corn!" they cried. Mom heard them and came out. She saw the crow and the damaged corn. She felt sorry for Tom and Lily. She picked up a broom and waved it at the crow. "Shoo, shoo, crow! Leave the corn alone! This is not your food!" she said. The crow saw the broom and got scared. It flew away with a loud caw. Mom hugged Tom and Lily and comforted them. "Don't worry, my loves. The crow is gone now. We can still save some of the corn. Let's wash it and cook it for dinner. It will be yummy and sweet," she said. Tom and Lily smiled and nodded. They helped mom wash the corn and put it in a pot. They were happy to have mom and corn. They thanked mom and said sorry for the crow. Mom kissed them and said it was not their fault. They ate the corn and enjoyed it. They forgot about the crow and the bad day. They had a good night.
