Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Book

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play games. One day, they find a big book of quizzes in the library. They think it is fun to ask each other questions from the book. "Who is the president of our country?" Tom asks Lily. "Lily does not know. She guesses, "Is it Bob?" "No, it is not Bob. It is Joe. You are wrong. You have to give me a coin," Tom says. Lily does not want to give Tom a coin. She says, "That is not fair. The quiz is too hard. I do not know who Joe is." Tom is persistent. He says, "You have to pay. Those are the rules. If you do not pay, I will not play with you." Lily is sad. She does not have many coins. She likes to play with Tom. She thinks of a way to get more coins. She says, "I have an idea. Why don't we sell the quiz book to other kids? We can ask them for coins to answer the questions. Then we can have more coins to play with." Tom likes the idea. He says, "That is a good idea. Let's do that. Maybe we can find some kids who know who Joe is." They take the quiz book and go to the playground. They see many kids playing there. They ask them if they want to buy the quiz book. Some kids say yes. Some kids say no. Some kids answer the questions. Some kids do not. Tom and Lily get many coins. They are happy. They go back to the library. They return the quiz book. They say thank you to the librarian. They go home. They play with their coins. They are still friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a miserable cat named Tom. Tom was sad because he had no friends to play with. Yesterday, he tried to make friends with the little birds, but they flew away. One day, Tom saw a little dog named Max. Max was running around and having fun. Tom wanted to play with Max, but he was scared. He decided to try and stop being scared. Tom walked up to Max and said, "Stop, Max! Can we be friends?" Max looked at Tom and smiled. "Of course, Tom! Let's play together!" From that day on, Tom and Max played together every day, and Tom was not miserable anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a pale dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and run in the yard. One day, he saw a red ball near the door. It was so fun to play with the ball. Spot was very happy. But then, a big cat came to the yard. The cat was not nice. Spot was scared of the cat. The cat liked to bite. Spot tried to hide behind the door, but the cat saw him. The cat came closer and closer to Spot. Spot was very scared. The cat bit the door, and it broke. The cat found Spot and bit him too. Spot was very sad and hurt. The cat took the red ball and went away. The end.

"Where can I find the book?" mom asked, looking around the room. "There it is!" dad answered. He was pointing at a big red book lying on the floor. "I need to read it," mom said. She bent down and grabbed the book. Mom opened the book and started to work. She read slowly but she was deep in thought. Dad watched mom for a while and then he said, "Mom, why are you so quiet?" "I'm trying to figure something out," mom replied without looking away from the book. Dad nodded and went back to his own work. The next morning mom finally had an answer. She smiled, hugged the book and said, "I think I figured it out!" Dad was surprised but curious. He asked, "What did you figure out?" Mom replied with a big smile, "I figured out why it was so important for me to read this book." The book had all the answers mom was looking for. She knew that whatever challenge lay ahead, she was ready to face it.
