Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Garden and the Girl

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was playing in the garden. Suddenly the skies got dark and it began to rain very hard. The little girl was scared and she did not know what to do. She wanted to go inside but she did not know how to get there. She started to cry and then she heard a voice telling her to surrender. She looked up and saw a big mop with eyes! The mop explained to the little girl that she could use it to help her get inside. She grabbed hold of the mop and it flew up in the air, taking her inside the house. Once they were inside, the mop helped the little girl dry off with a towel. She told the mop thank you and that it was a big help. The mop smiled and the little girl hugged it. From that day on, the little girl never forgot to surrender and ask for help when she was in need.

Lily and Tom are playing with blocks in the garden. They build a big tower with many colors. Lily likes the tower. She says, "Look, Tom, our tower is so high and pretty!" Tom nods and smiles. He says, "Yes, Lily, we are good at building. This tower is ours. It is mine and yours." But then, a big dog comes running. The dog is rough and loud. He barks and jumps. He wants to play, too. He knocks down the tower with his paw. The blocks fall and scatter. Lily and Tom are sad and angry. They say, "No, dog, no! Go away! This is not your tower. This is our tower. It is mine and yours. You are bad and mean!" The dog does not understand. He thinks they are playing. He wags his tail and licks their faces. He says, "Woof, woof, I like you. You are fun and nice. Can I play with you?" Lily and Tom do not like the dog. They are scared and upset. They run to their mom. They say, "Mom, mom, help us! There is a big dog in the garden. He is rough and loud. He broke our tower. He is not our friend. He is not mine and yours. He is a pest and a bother!" Mom comes and sees the dog. She knows the dog. He is their neighbor's dog. He is friendly and playful. He does not mean to be rough and loud. He just wants to have fun. Mom says, "Don't worry, Lily and Tom. This is Rex. He is not a bad dog. He is a good dog. He just does not know how to play with blocks. He likes to play with balls and sticks. Let me show you." Mom gets a ball and a stick. She throws the ball and Rex runs after it. He brings it back and drops it. He wags his tail and barks. He says, "Woof, woof, this is fun. Thank you for playing with me. You are kind and sweet. Can I be your friend?" Lily and Tom see that Rex is not so bad. He is just different. They say, "OK, Rex, we can be your friend. But you have to be gentle and quiet. You have to sit and wait. You have to share and care. You have to respect our tower. It is mine and yours. It is not yours and ours." Rex nods and smiles. He says, "OK, Lily and Tom, I can do that. I can be gentle and quiet. I can sit and wait. I can share and care. I can respect your tower. It is mine and yours. It is yours and ours. We are friends and pals." Lily and Tom are happy and glad. They say, "Good, Rex, good. We are happy and glad. We are friends and pals. We can play together. We can have fun. We can build a new tower. It is mine and yours. It is yours and ours. It is ours and his."

Once upon a time there was a wide hut. It belonged to a big family with lots of children. Every morning the children would send their pets outside to play. The pets liked to run around the wide hut and have lots of fun. One day, one of the children had an idea. He thought of a way to send their pets on an adventure. He decided to make a path that led around the hut and put a lovely door at the end of it. The children opened the door and sent their pets off into the jungle. The pets had a wonderful time exploring the jungle, looking for new plants and animals. But, after a while, it was time to come home. So, the children opened the door again and sent their pets back to the wide hut. Everyone was happy to be reunited again. The end.
