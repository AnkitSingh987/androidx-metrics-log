Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Person and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a person named Tom. Tom was not very nice. He was a bad person. He liked to take things from others. One day, Tom set a trap. He wanted to take a toy from a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with her ball in the park. She was very happy. When Sue went to get her ball, she saw the trap. She was smart and did not go near it. A big dog saw the trap too. The dog barked at Tom. Tom got scared and ran away. Sue and the dog played together. They were both happy and safe.

One day, a big girl named Lily went outside to play. The sunshine was warm and bright. Lily wanted to celebrate because it was a beautiful day. She saw a huge tree and decided to have a party under it. Lily invited her friends, a dog named Max and a cat named Daisy. They all sat under the huge tree. Max said, "I am happy to celebrate with you, Lily!" Daisy meowed and wagged her tail, too. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down from the tree and landed right on Lily's head. They all laughed and the bird sang a happy song. Now, they had a new friend to celebrate with under the sunshine.

Lily and Ben were playing with blocks in their room. They liked to build towers and bridges and castles. They used different colors and shapes of blocks. They were very careful not to knock down their creations. Suddenly, they felt the floor shake. They heard a loud noise. They saw their blocks fall down. They were scared. They ran to their mom, who was in the kitchen. "Mom, mom, what is happening?" they asked. Mom hugged them and said, "Don't worry, my loves. It's an earthquake. Sometimes, the ground moves and makes a noise. It will stop soon." "Will our blocks be okay?" Lily asked. Mom smiled and said, "Yes, they will be okay. Blocks are not fragile. They are strong and can be put back together. But we have to be careful. Some things are fragile. They can break easily. Like glass and plates and vases. We have to release them gently and not drop them." "Okay, mom. We understand. We will be careful with fragile things. And we will rebuild our blocks when the earthquake stops." Ben said. Mom kissed them and said, "You are very smart and brave. I love you very much. Now, let's go to a safe place and wait for the earthquake to end."

Tim and Mia are playing in the park. They see a big furry dog. The dog is friendly and wags its tail. Tim and Mia want to pet the dog. "Hello, doggy!" Tim says. "Can we touch you?" The dog barks and licks Tim's face. Mia giggles and strokes the dog's back. The dog is happy and rolls on the grass. The dog has a toy in its mouth. It is a skull. The skull is not real. It is made of rubber. The dog likes to chew the skull. It makes a squeaky sound. "Can I see your toy, doggy?" Mia asks. "It looks fun." The dog growls and shakes its head. It does not want to share the skull. It thinks the skull is its treasure. "Please, doggy, let me have it for a minute," Mia says. "I will give it back to you." The dog snarls and runs away. It hides behind a tree. It does not trust Mia. It thinks Mia will take the skull away. Tim and Mia are sad. They do not want to make the dog angry. They want to be friends with the dog. "Sorry, doggy, we did not mean to upset you," Tim says. "We just wanted to play with you and your toy." The dog peeks from behind the tree. It sees Tim and Mia's faces. They look sorry and kind. The dog feels sorry too. It decides to give them a chance. The dog comes out from behind the tree. It walks slowly to Tim and Mia. It drops the skull on the ground. It wags its tail. "Thank you, doggy, you are very nice," Mia says. "Can we play with you and your toy?" The dog barks and nods. It lets Tim and Mia hold the skull. It does not mind. It knows they will give it back. It is happy to share. Tim and Mia are happy too. They play with the dog and the skull. They throw the skull and the dog fetches it. They make the skull squeak and the dog chews it. They laugh and have fun. The dog, Tim and Mia become friends. They enjoy playing in the park. They learn to share and trust each other. They have a good day.
