Category: Beginner
Title: The Mouse and the Cow

Once there was a mouse who was very independent. He lived alone in a small house. He was always looking for something to eat and one day, he decided he wanted cheese. So he went outside and looked around. He saw some sticks and rocks and he looked and looked, but he didn't find any cheese. He sat down in the grass and thought and thought until he decided to ask a cow for help. He walked up to the cow and said, "Excuse me, do you know where I can find some cheese?" The cow thought for a minute and said, "Yes, I know a place. Follow me and I will take you there." So the mouse followed the cow and pretty soon they came to a cheese factory. The mouse was so excited! He said thank you to the cow, and then he went into the factory. Inside, he found lots and lots of different kinds of cheese. He picked some cheese and decided to go back home. On the way, he felt very proud of himself. He was glad he decided to ask for help and now he had enough cheese to last him a very long time!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a bright red ball. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim had to go to school. He wanted to take his bright red ball with him. At school, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue saw Tim's bright red ball and said, "Wow! I like your ball. Can we play with it?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play!" They played with the ball all day and had lots of fun. But when it was time to go home, Tim could not find his bright red ball. He looked everywhere, but it was gone. Tim started to weep. Sue saw Tim weeping and said, "Don't cry, Tim. We will find your ball." They looked together and found the ball under a big tree. Tim was so happy, and they both went home with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump. The cat saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. The dog said, "Be careful, cat! Don't rush or you will get in trouble." The cat did not listen. He ran fast to get the ball. But he did not see the big hole in the ground. The cat fell into the hole. "Oh no! I am in trouble!" said the cat. The dog came to help the cat. "I told you not to rush," said the dog. The dog found a long stick. He put the stick into the hole. The cat climbed up the stick. It was not easy, but the cat got out of the hole. The cat said, "Thank you, dog! I will not rush next time." The dog and the cat were happy and played with the ball together.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily found a big bowl in her kitchen. The bowl was so big that she could fit her whole head inside it! Lily thought it was a crazy bowl. She wanted to show the bowl to her friend, Tom. Lily went to Tom's house with the big bowl. Tom saw the bowl and said, "Wow! That is a crazy big bowl!" They both laughed and decided to fill the bowl with fruit. They put apples, bananas, and oranges in the bowl. When they were done, Tom's mom came into the room. She saw the big bowl full of fruit and said, "Thank you, Lily and Tom, for filling the bowl with fruit. You both did a great job!" Lily and Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome!" They were happy that they could help Tom's mom and have fun with the crazy big bowl.

One day, a helpful dog named Tom saw a girl with messy hair. The girl looked sad. Tom wanted to make her happy. He thought of a plan. Tom said, "I recommend you let me help you with your hair." The girl nodded and let Tom try. Tom used his paws to brush her hair. It was hard, but Tom did not give up. In the end, Tom's paws got stuck in the girl's hair. They both felt sad. The girl's hair was messier than before. Tom's plan did not work. The bad ending made them both very sad.
