Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Kid and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a kid named Tom. Tom was anxious about going to a new school. He was scared to make new friends. His mom recommended that he should try to talk to the other kids and play with them. On the first day of school, Tom saw a group of kids playing with a ball. He walked over to them and said, "Can I play with you?" The kids looked at him and said, "Sure, you can play with us!" Tom was happy and started to play with the ball. But then, one kid kicked the ball too hard and it went far away. Tom ran to get the ball, but he didn't see a big hole in the ground. He fell into the hole and couldn't get out. The other kids tried to help him, but they couldn't. In the end, Tom had to stay in the hole until his mom came to find him.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. It loved to bounce and play with the kids. One day, on Tuesday, the ball met a little boy named Tom. Tom was very happy to see the big red ball. "Let's play and bounce together!" said Tom. The big red ball and Tom played all day. They bounced high and low, and had lots of fun. Tom's mom gave them a delicious snack to eat. They ate the snack and played some more. But then, a big dog came. The dog saw the big red ball and wanted to play too. The dog bit the ball and it went "pop!" The big red ball could not bounce anymore. Tom was very sad. The big red ball was gone, and they could not play together anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wealthy man. He had a big house and many pretty things. He loved to hold his shiny copper coins in his hands. One day, he met a little girl who had no toys. The wealthy man felt sad for her. He wanted to help her, so he gave her one of his shiny copper coins. The little girl was very happy and thanked him. From that day on, the wealthy man learned that sharing his copper coins made him even happier than holding them all by himself. He started to share his coins with other kids too. The town became a happier place and everyone loved the wealthy man for his kindness.

One day, a little fish named Tim lived in a reef. The reef was very big and had many thick plants. Tim had many friends and they liked to play together. One day, a big bad fish came to the reef. The big bad fish wanted to steal food from the little fish. Tim and his friends were scared, but they had a plan. They swam very fast and hid in the thick plants. The big bad fish could not find them. Tim and his friends were safe. They learned to stick together and not let anyone steal from them.

Sam and Lily were twins. They liked to play with their toys and watch cartoons. One day, they saw their mom put her wallet in her purse. They wanted to see what was inside the wallet. "Let's go to mom's purse and look at her wallet," Sam said to Lily. "Okay, but we have to be quiet. Mom is in the kitchen," Lily said. They tiptoed to the living room and found mom's purse on the sofa. They opened it and took out the wallet. It was shiny and had many cards and papers and coins. "Wow, look at all this!" Sam said. He took out a card with a picture of a store. "This is a store card. Mom uses it to buy things at the store." "Can we use it too?" Lily asked. "Sure, why not? Let's go to the store and buy some candy and toys," Sam said. He put the card in his pocket and gave the wallet to Lily. "You hold the wallet. I'll hold the card. We are so lucky!" They ran out of the house and went to the store. They saw many things they liked. They picked some candy and toys and went to the cashier. "Hello, kids. Do you have money to pay for these?" the cashier asked. "Yes, we have a store card," Sam said. He gave the card to the cashier. The cashier looked at the card and frowned. "This is not a store card. This is a library card. You can't use this to buy things. Where did you get this?" Sam and Lily did not know what to say. They felt scared and confused. They wanted to run away, but the cashier stopped them. "Wait a minute. Where are your parents? How did you get here? What is this?" the cashier said. He took the wallet from Lily and opened it. He saw mom's name and phone number on a paper. He called mom and told her what happened. Mom was very angry and worried. She came to the store and saw Sam and Lily. She took the wallet and the card from the cashier and scolded them. "How could you do this? You took my wallet and my library card without asking. You lied to the cashier. You could have been lost or hurt. You are in big trouble!" mom said. Sam and Lily felt very sorry and ashamed. They cried and said they were sorry. Mom took them home and punished them. They had to return the candy and the toys. They had to stay in their room. They had to read books from the library. They were not lucky at all. They were very unlucky.
