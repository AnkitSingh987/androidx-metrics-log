Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Sally was playing in her room. She saw a shiny razor on the table. Sally knew it was sharp and not for kids. She was worried that her baby brother Tom might find it. Sally called her mom and said, "Mom, there is a razor on the table. I am worried about Tom." Her mom came into the room and saw the razor. She was glad that Sally told her. Mom took the razor and covered it with a big box. She said, "Thank you, Sally, for telling me. Now Tom will be safe." Sally smiled and hugged her mom. They played together and had a fun day.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make noises and pretend to drive them on the floor. One day, Tom has a new car. It is big and red and shiny. It has a button on the side. Tom shows his new car to Sam. "Look, Sam, this is my new car. It is very nice. Do you want to see what it can do?" Tom says. "Yes, please. What can it do?" Sam says. "Watch this. When I press this button, the car increases. It becomes bigger and bigger. See?" Tom says. He presses the button and the car grows in front of their eyes. It is as big as a table now. Tom and Sam are amazed. "Wow, that is cool. How did you get it?" Sam says. "My dad gave it to me. He said it is a special car. He said I have to be careful with it. He said I can only press the button once a day. He said if I press it too much, the car will increase too much and break." Tom says. "Can I try it?" Sam says. "OK, but only once. And then we have to make it small again. Here, you can press the button." Tom says. He gives the car to Sam. Sam holds the car and presses the button. The car increases again. It is as big as a sofa now. Tom and Sam are excited. "That is awesome. Can we make it bigger?" Sam says. "No, no, we can't. We have to make it small again. Remember what my dad said. We have to press the button again to make it small." Tom says. He tries to take the car from Sam. But Sam does not want to give it back. He likes the big car. He wants to make it bigger. "Let go, Sam. Give me the car. We have to make it small." Tom says. "No, I want to make it bigger. It is fun. Let me press the button again." Sam says. He pulls the car away from Tom. He presses the button again. The car increases again. It is as big as a bed now. It fills the whole room. Tom and Sam are scared. "Stop, Sam, stop. You are making the car too big. It will break. It will break the room. It will break the house. Give me the car. Please." Tom says. But Sam does not listen. He presses the button again. The car increases again. It is as big as a house now. It breaks the walls and the windows and the roof. It makes a loud noise. Tom and Sam are in trouble. "Help, help. We are sorry. We are sorry." Tom and Sam say. They hear sirens and see lights. They see people and firemen and policemen. They see Tom's dad and Sam's mom. They are angry and worried. They see the car. It is as big as a mountain now. It is broken and smoking. It is not nice anymore. "Tom, Sam, what have you done? How could you do this? You are very naughty. You are very foolish. You are very lucky. You could have been hurt. You could have hurt others. You have to say sorry. You have to pay for the damage. You have to learn your lesson." Tom's dad and Sam's mom say. Tom and Sam are sad and ashamed. They say sorry. They say they will never do it again. They say they will be good. They say they do not like the car anymore. They say they like their small cars better. They hug their parents. They hug each other. They are still friends. But they are not happy. They are in big trouble.
