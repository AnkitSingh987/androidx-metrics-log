Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Toys

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park with their toys. One day, they saw a new boy sitting on a bench. He had a cane and a sad face. "Hello, what is your name?" Lily asked him. "Tommy," he said. "I can't play with you. I have a bad leg and I need this cane to walk." Lily and Ben felt sorry for Tommy. They wanted to make him happy. "Tommy, you can play with us. We have many toys. Look, this is my adorable doll. Her name is Anna. And this is Ben's car. It makes noises. Vroom, vroom!" Lily said. "Really? Can I see them?" Tommy asked. "Sure, come with us. We will help you walk. We can unite our hands and be a team," Ben said. Lily and Ben each took one of Tommy's hands and helped him stand up. They walked together to the playground and shared their toys. Tommy smiled and laughed. He felt happy and not alone. Lily and Ben were happy too. They made a new friend. They learned that they can unite and help each other. They had fun playing in the park with their toys and their cane.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Lily. She had a favorite toy, a bell. She loved to play with her bell all day long. It made her happy when it made a pretty sound. One sunny day, Lily went outside to play. She saw a big ball and decided to kick it. She kicked the ball so high, it went up, up, up into the sky. "Oh no!" said Lily, "Where did my ball go?" Just then, her friend Tim came to play. "Lily, what's wrong?" he asked. "I kicked my ball, and now it's gone," Lily said with a sad face. Tim looked up and saw the ball stuck in a tree. "Don't worry, Lily. I'll help you get it back," he said. Tim climbed the tree and got the ball for Lily. She was so happy! "Thank you, Tim!" Lily said. They played with the ball and her bell all day long, and they had lots of fun together.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like pizza a lot. Today, Mom makes pizza for them. She puts cheese, sauce, and ham on the pizza. She puts it in the oven. When the pizza is ready, Mom takes it out of the oven. She puts it on the table. She says, "Boys, come and eat your pizza. It is hot and yummy." Tom and Sam run to the table. They see the pizza. They want to eat it. But they see something different on the pizza. There are green things on the pizza. They are small and round. They look like peas. Tom does not like peas. He says, "Mom, what are these green things on the pizza? I do not like them. They are yucky." Mom says, "They are not peas, Tom. They are olives. They are good for you. They make the pizza taste better. You should try them." Sam likes olives. He says, "I like olives, Mom. They are not yucky. They are yummy. Can I have more olives on my pizza?" Mom says, "Yes, Sam. You can have more olives on your pizza. Here, I will lift some olives from my pizza and put them on yours." Tom is angry. He says, "No, Mom. Do not give Sam more olives. Give me more ham. I like ham. Ham is yummy. Olives are yucky." Mom says, "Tom, do not be rude. You have enough ham on your pizza. You should share with your brother. And you should try new things. Maybe you will like olives if you taste them." Tom does not want to taste the olives. He says, "No, Mom. I do not want to taste the olives. I want to eat my pizza without olives. Can I take them off?" Mom says, "No, Tom. You cannot take them off. You have to eat your pizza as it is. It is not nice to waste food. And it is not nice to be picky. You should be grateful for what you have." Tom is sad. He does not want to eat his pizza. He does not want to be grateful. He does not want to be nice. He wants to have his pizza his way. He says, "Mom, you are not fair. You always give Sam what he wants. You never give me what I want. You do not love me." Mom is shocked. She says, "Tom, that is not true. I love you very much. I love you and Sam the same. I want you to be happy and healthy. I want you to eat your pizza and enjoy it. Please, do not say such things." Tom does not listen. He says, "No, Mom. You do not love me. You love Sam more. You love olives more. You do not care about me. I hate you." Tom pushes his plate away. He gets up from the table. He runs to his room. He slams the door. He cries. Mom is sad. She does not know what to do. She looks at Sam. Sam is sad too. He does not like to see Tom cry. He does not like to see Mom cry. He says, "Mom, I am sorry. I did not mean to make Tom angry. I do not care about the olives. I can give them to him. I can eat his pizza. I love him. I love you." Mom says, "Thank you, Sam. You are very sweet. But you do not have to give up your pizza. You can eat what you like. You did nothing wrong. It is Tom who needs to apologize. He needs to learn to be kind and respectful. He needs to learn to appreciate what he has. He needs to learn to love olives."
