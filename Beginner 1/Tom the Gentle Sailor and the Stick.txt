Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Gentle Sailor and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a gentle sailor named Tom. Tom liked to sail on the big blue sea. He had a small boat with a white sail. Tom had a friend named Sam. Sam was a little fish who lived in the sea. They liked to play together. One day, Tom and Sam found a weapon in the water. It was a big stick. Tom said, "What is this?" Sam said, "I don't know. Let's play with it!" They played with the big stick, but it was not fun. They put it back in the water. Suddenly, the big stick started to move! It was not a weapon, but a big, friendly sea snake. The sea snake said, "Thank you for playing with me!" Tom and Sam were surprised but happy. They made a new friend and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird with a colorful wing. The bird was very bossy. It liked to tell other birds what to do. It lived in a tree with many other birds. One day, something strange did happen. The bossy bird woke up and saw that its wing was gone! It could not fly anymore. The other birds looked for the wing, but they could not find it. The bossy bird felt sad. It asked the other birds for help. They all worked together to find the wing. In the end, they found the wing under a bush. The bossy bird said sorry for being bossy and thanked the other birds. From that day, they all became good friends.

Lena and Tom are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they go to the mill with Lena's mom. The mill is a big house with a wheel. The wheel turns and makes flour. "Look, Tom, the wheel is so fast!" Lena says. "Yes, and it makes a lot of noise!" Tom says. They see water coming out of the mill. The water is clear and cold. They run to the water and put their hands in it. "Wow, the water is so nice!" Lena says. "Let's spray each other!" Tom says. They laugh and splash and spray water at each other. They get wet and happy. Lena's mom watches them and smiles. "Are you having fun, kids?" she asks. "Yes, mom, we love the mill!" Lena says. "It's a lively place, isn't it?" she says. "Yes, it is!" Tom says. They play until it is time to go home. They say goodbye to the mill and the water. They hug and thank Lena's mom. They are good friends. They had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a big worry. He was afraid to play with the other dogs because he thought he was terrible at playing. He didn't want to embarrass himself. One day, Spot saw a group of dogs playing in the park. They were having so much fun. Spot wanted to join, but his worry stopped him. He sat under a tree and watched them play. A kind dog named Daisy noticed Spot and came over to talk. Daisy told Spot, "Don't be scared to play with us. We all make mistakes sometimes. It's okay to embarrass ourselves. That's how we learn and get better." Spot listened to Daisy and decided to try playing with the other dogs. He had so much fun and learned that it's okay to make mistakes. The moral of the story is, don't let your worries stop you from trying new things.

Once upon a time, a brown fish named Bob lived in a big pond. Bob was not like the other fish. He could stand on his tail! Bob loved to play with his friends in the pond. They would swim and jump all day long. One day, Bob saw a big box near the pond. He was curious and wanted to see what was inside. Bob stood on his tail and jumped out of the water. He opened the box and saw a shiny, new toy. It was a toy fish that could also stand on its tail! Bob was so happy to see the toy fish. He thought it was a new friend. But then, the toy fish started to move and talk! It was not a toy, but a real fish from a faraway pond. The new fish was named Tim. Tim was also brown and could stand on his tail, just like Bob. Bob and Tim became best friends. They played and stood on their tails together. All the other fish in the pond were surprised and happy to see two brown fish standing on their tails. They all played together and had lots of fun. And that is how Bob found a new friend in the most unexpected way.
