Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia loved to play dress up. She liked to wear pretty clothes and look like a rich princess. One day, Mia found a box in her room. The box had a pretty dress and shoes in it. Mia was very happy. Mia put on the dress and shoes. She went to her friend's house to play. Her friend, Tom, saw Mia and said, "Wow, you look like a rich princess! But you should complete your fashion with a pretty hat." Mia thought Tom was right. So, they both went to find a hat for Mia. They found a hat and Mia put it on. She looked very pretty. But then, Mia saw a girl who had no pretty clothes. Mia felt sad for the girl. She took off her hat, dress, and shoes and gave them to the girl. The girl was very happy. Mia and Tom learned that sharing is more important than looking rich. And they all played happily together.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big box in the yard. They think it is a fun box. "Let's make a house with the box!" Anna says. "OK!" Ben says. They move the box to a shady spot. They cut a door and a window with scissors. They draw flowers and stars with crayons. They are happy. But then, Tom comes. Tom is a lazy boy. He does not like to play with toys. He likes to break things. He sees the box house. He wants to ruin it. "Hey, what are you doing?" Tom says. He kicks the box. The box falls down. Anna and Ben's house is gone. "Stop it!" Anna says. She is angry. She frowns at Tom. "Go away!" Ben says. He is sad. He frowns at Tom too. Tom laughs. He thinks it is funny. He kicks the box again. He does not care. Anna and Ben are not friends with Tom. They think he is mean. They pick up the box. They move it to another spot. They fix their house. They play with their toys. They are happy again.

One day, a big dog named Max saw the mail truck. Max liked to smile and play. The mail truck made him happy. But the mailman was scared of Max. Max said, "Hi! I want to play!" The mailman said, "No, Max. I have to give mail to the houses." Max was sad. He wanted to play with the mailman. He did not want the mailman to be scared. Max had an idea. He got a ball and gave it to the mailman. The mailman smiled and threw the ball. Max ran and caught it. They played for a little bit. Now, the mailman was not scared of Max. They were friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was an icy day, so Tim put on his warm hat and gloves. In the park, Tim saw his friend, Sam, and they decided to spend the day playing together. While they were playing, they saw a big hill. Tim and Sam wanted to see who could run up the hill the fastest. They both said, "Ready, set, go!" and started to run. Tim thought he would win, but Sam was very fast too. As they were running, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came out of nowhere and joined the race. The dog ran up the hill faster than both Tim and Sam. They all laughed and played with the dog for the rest of the day. In the end, they all felt like winners because they made a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He had a big, red kite to play with. The sky was blue, and the sun was warm. Tim was very happy. As Tim played with his kite, he saw a small dog shiver in the cold wind. The dog looked sad and lost. Tim said, "Mom, can we help the shivering dog?" His mom said, "Yes, let's take him home and make him warm." They took the dog home and gave him a warm blanket. The dog stopped shivering and wagged his tail. He was grateful for Tim's help. Tim and the dog became best friends. They played with the kite in the park every day.
