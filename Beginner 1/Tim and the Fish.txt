Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy kayak. He loved to play with it in the water. One day, Tim thought he could improve his kayak. He wanted it to go faster. Tim worked on his kayak. He made it look nice. He was very happy. But when he put it in the water, it did not go faster. It went slower! Tim was ashamed. He did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A magic fish saw Tim and his kayak. The fish touched the kayak with its tail. Suddenly, the kayak went very fast! Tim was so happy. He thanked the magic fish and played with his fast kayak all day.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot saw a cut on his ball. He was sad. Spot went to his friend, Cat. Cat was very smart. "Cat, can you help me fix my ball?" Spot asked. Cat said, "Yes, I can help you. But first, we need to find what made the cut." They looked and looked. At last, they found a sharp rock. "This rock made the cut," said Cat. "We must be careful when we play near it." Cat helped Spot fix the ball. They put a bandage on the cut. Spot was happy. From that day on, they played far away from the sharp rock. They learned that some things don't belong near their play area.

Once upon a time, there was a kid named Tim. Tim was very restless. He did not like to sit still. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw many kids playing. Tim wanted to play with them. Tim and the kids started to play a game. They were running and laughing. Suddenly, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. Tim started to climb the tree, but he was scared. He did not know how to come down. Then, something unexpected happened. A bird came and sat on a branch near Tim. The bird began to sing a song. The song made Tim feel calm. He was not restless anymore. The bird showed Tim how to come down the tree. Tim was happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pink cat. The cat was very nice and liked to play. One day, the cat saw a big missile in the sky. It was going very fast. The cat did not want the missile to hurt anyone. So, the cat decided to fight the missile. The cat jumped high and hit the missile with its paw. The missile went away and did not hurt anyone. Everyone was happy that the pink cat saved the day. They all played together and had fun. The cat was a hero and everyone loved the nice, pink cat.

Ben and Mia love to play soccer in the park. They have a ball and two cones for the goal. Ben is strong and fast, and he likes to kick the ball hard. Mia is smart and quick, and she likes to catch the ball with her hands. One day, they decide to play a game. Ben says, "I will be the kicker, and you will be the goalie. If I score three goals, I win. If you stop three goals, you win." Mia says, "OK, let's play. But you have to be fair and not kick too hard." They start the game. Ben kicks the ball, and Mia runs to catch it. She jumps and grabs the ball in the air. "One stop for me!" she says. Ben kicks the ball again, and Mia slides to catch it. She rolls and holds the ball tight. "Two stops for me!" she says. Ben kicks the ball one more time, and Mia dives to catch it. She lands on the ground and hugs the ball. "Three stops for me! I win!" she says. Ben is sad and angry. He says, "You cheated! You used your hands! That's not fair!" He kicks the ball away and runs to the bench. He sits and pouts. Mia feels sorry for him. She kneels next to him and says, "Ben, I'm sorry. I didn't cheat. That's how you play goalie. You can use your hands to stop the ball. It's OK. You are still strong and fast. Do you want to play again?" Ben looks at her and thinks. He says, "OK, I'm sorry too. I didn't know the rules. You are smart and quick. Do you want to switch? You can be the kicker, and I can be the goalie." Mia smiles and says, "Sure, let's switch. But you have to be fair and not catch too hard." They hug and laugh. They get up and run to the goal. They play again and have fun.
