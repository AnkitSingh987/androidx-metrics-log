Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a job. His job was to rise early and help his mom. He was slow, but he tried his best. One day, Tim woke up and said, "Mom, I will rise and do my job." His mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. Please be careful." Tim went outside to start his work. While Tim was working, a big dog came. The dog was not nice. Tim tried to run, but he was too slow. The dog bit Tim's leg. Tim cried and went back home. His mom hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. We will be more careful next time."

One day, a nice dog named True went for a walk. True liked to play with his friends in the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. True saw his friend, a little cat. They played with a big red ball. They ran and jumped together. True and the cat laughed a lot. They were very happy. After playing, True and the cat were tired. They sat under a tree to rest. They had a fun day. True and the cat went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was adventurous and loved to play outside. One day, he went to the park with his mom. The park had a big log that Tim liked to play on. Tim said to his mom, "I choose to play on the log today." His mom smiled and said, "Okay, Tim. Be careful and have fun." Tim climbed on the log and pretended it was a big ship. He was the captain, and he had to save his friends from the water. He jumped and laughed as he played on the log. His mom watched him and clapped her hands. Tim felt happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty boy named Tim. Tim liked to mark on the walls with his crayons. One day, he looked up and saw the ceiling. He thought, "I want to mark on the ceiling too!" Tim got a chair and stood on it. He reached up high with his crayon and started to mark on the ceiling. His mom came in and saw the marks. She said, "Tim, you are being naughty! You should not mark on the ceiling." Tim did not listen to his mom. He kept marking on the ceiling. His mom got mad and took away his crayons. Tim cried and cried, but he did not get his crayons back. The marks stayed on the ceiling, and Tim was sad.

One day, a cat named Tom found some yarn. Tom loved to play with yarn. He would roll it, jump on it, and even sleep on it. But one day, Tom saw his friend Sue the dog bake a cake. Tom thought, "Why not bake the yarn?" So, Tom asked Sue to help him bake the yarn. They put the yarn in a big bowl, added some eggs, and mixed it all up. Then, they put the mix in the oven. They were very excited to see what would happen. After a while, they opened the oven and saw a big surprise. The yarn had turned into a beautiful, soft, and colorful blanket! Tom and Sue were so happy. They were sorry they had not tried this before. From that day on, Tom and Sue loved to bake yarn and make more blankets for all their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy made of thread. The toy was weak, but Lily loved it so much. She played with it every day. One day, Lily was playing with her toy when it broke. She felt sad and started to cry. Her mom came and saw the broken toy. She said, "Don't worry, I will fix it for you." Her mom took the weak thread and fixed the toy. Lily was so happy to see her toy again. She laughed and hugged her mom. From that day on, Lily played with her toy and always remembered to be gentle with it.
