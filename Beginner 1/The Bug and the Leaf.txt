Category: Beginner
Title: The Bug and the Leaf

One day, a happy bug went for a crawl. The sun was shining and the bug felt good. The bug saw a big tree and decided to crawl up it. As the bug went up the tree, it found a nice leaf to rest on. "Hi, leaf!" said the happy bug. "Can I sit on you?" The leaf moved a little and said, "Yes, bug! You can sit on me." The bug and the leaf became good friends. They talked and laughed together. But, one day, when the happy bug was crawling on the leaf, the leaf started to fall from the tree. The bug was scared! The leaf said, "Don't worry, bug! I will keep you safe." As they fell, a big bird saw them and caught the leaf in its beak. The bird put the leaf and the bug back on the tree. The happy bug and the leaf were safe, and they thanked the bird. From that day on, they had a new friend to share their days with.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. Tim had a red wagon that he loved to play with. He liked to put his toys in the wagon and pull it around the yard. One day, Tim saw a big hill. He thought it would be fun to put his wagon on the hill and watch it spin down. So, he put his wagon at the top of the hill and let it go. The wagon spun very fast down the hill. But, Tim did not see the big rock at the bottom of the hill. The wagon hit the rock and broke into pieces. Tim was very sad. His kind heart felt heavy as he looked at his broken wagon. From that day on, he knew he should be more careful with his toys.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little bird. The bird lived in a tree near a big lime tree. The bird liked to eat the limes. One day, the bird saw a big red ball in the sky. The bird thought the ball was a big lime. The bird said to a friend, "Look at the big lime in the sky! I want to eat it." The friend said, "That is not a lime. That is the sun. It will burn you if you go near it." The bird did not listen. The bird flew up, up, up to the big red ball. But the bird did not get burned. The big red ball was not the sun. It was a big red balloon! The bird and the friend played with the balloon all day. They had so much fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. It was a wet day, and the grass was all wet. Tim saw a big pole in the middle of the park. He wanted to climb the pole and reach the top. Tim's friend, Sam, came to the park too. Sam wanted to help Tim climb the pole. They prepare by putting their hands on the pole. Sam said, "Let's do it together, Tim!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's climb the pole!" They tried to climb the pole, but it was too wet. They slipped and fell on the wet grass. They laughed and had fun, even if they could not climb the pole. Tim and Sam played in the park all day, enjoying the wet grass and the big pole. They promised to come back to the park on a sunny day to try again.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to eat sausage. One day, Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was very good at finding food. Tom had an idea. He asked Jerry, "Can you supply me with sausage?" Jerry thought about it and said, "Okay, but you have to be nice to me." Tom agreed, and they became friends. Jerry would find sausage and give it to Tom. Tom was very happy. One day, Tom was being bossy again. He told Jerry to find more sausage. Jerry was sad because Tom was not being nice. Jerry decided to teach Tom a lesson. He hid the sausage and told Tom there was none left. Tom was sad and asked Jerry for help. Jerry said, "You need to be nice and not bossy." Tom said sorry and promised to be nice. They found the hidden sausage and shared it. Tom and Jerry were happy friends again.
