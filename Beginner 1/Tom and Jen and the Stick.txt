Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Jen and the Stick

Tom and Jen like to play in the snow. They make a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They give him a carrot nose and two coal eyes. They look for something to make his arms. They see an axe in the shed. They think it is a good stick. They run to the shed and take the axe. They do not know it is sharp and heavy. They try to put it on the snowman's side. But it is too hard. The axe falls on the ground. It makes a loud noise. The snowman's head falls off too. Tom and Jen are scared. They hear Dad coming. He is angry. He sees the axe and the snowman. He shouts at them. He tells them to never touch the axe. He says it is dangerous. He says they could get hurt. Tom and Jen are sorry. They cry. They say they did not know. They say they just wanted to make arms. Dad hugs them. He says he loves them. He says he will help them fix the snowman. He says they can use some branches instead. They go to the woods and find some branches. They roll the snowman's head back on. They stick the branches on his sides. They make him smile. They are happy. They say sorry to the snowman. They say he looks nice. They play with him until it is dark. They go inside and have hot cocoa. They say they will never touch the axe again.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the spring. The flowers were pretty and the grass was green. Lily had a friend named Tim. They played together every day. One day, Lily saw Tim looking sad. He was ashamed because he lost his ball. Lily wanted to help him. She requested the other kids in the village to help find Tim's ball. They all said yes and started to search. They looked under rocks, behind trees, and inside bushes. After a long time, they found the ball near the river. Tim was very happy and thanked everyone. He was not ashamed anymore. Lily and Tim played with the ball together. They had lots of fun in the spring sun.

Ben and Mia were friends. They liked to play with their scooters in the park. One day, they saw a big pond with ducks and boats. Ben had an idea. "Let's sail our scooters on the water!" he said. Mia was not sure. She liked her scooter. It was pink and soft and had a bell. She did not want it to get wet. "But what if they sink?" she asked. Ben laughed. He said, "They won't sink. They will float like boats. Come on, it will be fun!" He took his scooter and ran to the edge of the pond. He threw his scooter into the water. It made a big splash. Ben clapped his hands. He said, "Look, it's sailing!" Mia watched. She saw Ben's scooter bob up and down on the water. It looked like it was sailing. She thought maybe it was fun. She wanted to try. She took her scooter and ran to the edge of the pond. She threw her scooter into the water. It made a big splash. But Mia's scooter did not sail. It sank. It went down, down, down to the bottom of the pond. Mia did not clap her hands. She cried. She said, "My scooter! My scooter!" Ben stopped laughing. He saw Mia's scooter disappear under the water. He felt bad. He said, "I'm sorry, Mia. I didn't know your scooter would sink." Mia was very sad. She said, "I want my scooter back. It was my favorite. It was pink and soft and had a bell." Ben did not know what to do. He could not get Mia's scooter back. It was too deep and too dark. He said, "Maybe we can ask someone to help us. Maybe they can fish it out." But no one could help them. The pond was too big and too dirty. No one could see Mia's scooter. No one could reach Mia's scooter. Mia's scooter was gone. Mia and Ben went home. They did not play with their scooters anymore. They did not have fun. They had a bad day. The end.
