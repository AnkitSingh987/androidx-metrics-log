Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Tom and the Castle

Lila and Tom were playing with blocks in the yard. They liked to build towers and bridges and houses. They had many colors of blocks: red, blue, yellow, green, and more. "Look at my fire truck!" Tom said, showing Lila his red and yellow blocks. "It has a ladder and a hose and a siren. It can put out fires and save people." "That's a nice fire truck," Lila said. "But I like my castle better. It has a gate and a tower and a flag. It is very strong and fair. No one can break it." Tom wanted to see if Lila's castle was really strong. He took his fire truck and drove it towards the castle. He pretended that the castle was on fire and he had to put it out. He made a loud noise with his mouth: "Woo-woo-woo!" "Stop!" Lila shouted. "You are ruining my castle! That's not fair! It's not on fire, it's my castle!" But Tom did not stop. He crashed his fire truck into the castle and knocked down some blocks. He sprayed his hose and made the blocks wet. He laughed and said: "Fire out! Fire out!" Lila was very angry. She cried and said: "You are a bad boy! You are not my friend! Go away!" She grabbed some blocks and threw them at Tom. Tom dodged the blocks and threw some back. They started a big fight with blocks. They did not see that their mom was watching them from the window. Mom came out and said: "What are you doing? Why are you fighting? Why are you throwing blocks? That's not nice! That's not safe!" She took away the blocks and the fire truck and the castle. She said: "You have to end this fight right now. You have to say sorry to each other. You have to share your toys and play nicely. Or you will have no toys at all." Lila and Tom felt sorry. They said sorry to each other. They hugged and said: "You are my friend. I like your fire truck. I like your castle. Can we play together?" Mom smiled and said: "That's better. That's fair. You can have your toys back. But be careful and be kind. And don't play with fire."

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to walk to the park and play on the swings. One day, they see a new girl on the slide. She has a pink dress and a big bow in her hair. "Hello, what is your name?" Lily asks. "My name is Anna. I have a lot of style. Do you like my dress?" the new girl says. Lily and Ben look at each other. They do not care about style. They like to wear comfortable clothes and have fun. "Your dress is nice, but we like to walk and run and jump. Do you want to play with us?" Ben says. Anna shakes her head. "No, thank you. I do not want to get dirty or mess up my hair. I like to be calm and pretty. You should try to be more like me." Lily and Ben feel sad and angry. They do not like how Anna talks to them. They think she is mean and snobby. "Come on, Ben. Let's go to the swings. We do not need to be like her. We are happy the way we are." Lily says. They walk away from Anna and go to the swings. They push each other and laugh and have fun. They forget about Anna and her style. They are glad they are friends.

Lila and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Lila had a soft fox that she liked to hug and pet. Ben had a wooden truck that he liked to zoom and crash. "Look at my truck, Lila!" Ben said. "It can go fast and loud!" He pushed his truck hard on the floor and made a vroom sound. The truck hit the wall and broke. One of the wheels came off and rolled away. "Oh no, Ben!" Lila said. "Your truck is broken! Are you sad?" Ben looked at his truck and felt sad. He liked his truck a lot and now it was broken. He started to cry. "It's okay, Ben," Lila said. "Don't cry. I have something for you." She gave him her fox and said, "This is my fox. He is soft and comfortable. You can hug him and he will make you feel better." Ben took the fox and hugged him. He felt the fox's fur and ears and tail. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Lila. Your fox is nice. I like him." Lila smiled and said, "You're welcome, Ben. I like you too. We can share our toys and play together." They hugged each other and played with the fox and the truck. They were happy and had fun.
