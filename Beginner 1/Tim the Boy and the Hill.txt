Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hill

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He was ready to play with his friends. He saw a big hill and wanted to go up. Tim started to walk up the hill. He was very happy. When Tim got to the top, he saw a big jar of salt. He stared at it for a long time. He didn't know why it was there. Tim thought it was strange. He wanted to show his friends. Tim picked up the jar and started to go down the hill. But then, something unexpected happened. The jar started to shake and make funny sounds. Tim was scared. The jar opened, and out came a tiny man. He was the Salt King! He told Tim that he was lost and needed help to find his way home. Tim was surprised but wanted to help. So, Tim and the Salt King became friends and went on an adventure to find the Salt King's home.

Tom is a smart boy. He likes to read books and learn new things. He has many books in his room. He reads them every day. One day, Tom finds a new book on his bed. It is a big book with many pictures. Tom is happy. He opens the book and starts to read. The book is about animals. Tom likes animals. He sees lions, elephants, monkeys, and more. Tom reads and reads. He learns many facts about the animals. He sees how they live, what they eat, and how they play. He is having fun. He does not notice the time. Soon, it is dark outside. Tom's mom comes to his room. She sees Tom reading the book. She smiles. She is proud of Tom. She says, "Tom, it is time for bed. You have read a lot today. You can finish the book tomorrow." Tom looks at his mom. He looks at the book. He sees that he is near the end. He says, "Mom, can I read a little more? I want to see the end of the book. I want to know what happens to the animals." Tom's mom thinks. She sees Tom's face. He looks eager and curious. She says, "Okay, Tom. You can read a little more. But not too much. You need to sleep and rest. You can read more tomorrow." Tom says, "Thank you, mom. You are very nice. I love you." He hugs his mom. He turns the page. He reads the end of the book. He sees a picture of all the animals together. He smiles. He is happy. He closes the book. He puts it on his nightstand. He turns off the light. He lies down. He dreams of the animals. He is a smart boy.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Mia. Mia had a pretty veil that she liked to wear on her head. She wore it every day. One day, while Mia was playing in the park, a naughty boy named Tim came up to her. He wanted to poke her veil. "Can I poke your veil?" Tim asked. Mia said, "No, please don't poke my veil. It is special to me." Tim didn't listen to Mia, and he poked her veil anyway. Mia was sad, and her veil had a hole in it. She started to cry. Tim felt bad for making Mia cry. He said, "I'm sorry, Mia. I didn't mean to make you sad." Mia and Tim went to Mia's mom and told her what happened. Mia's mom said, "It's okay, Mia. We can fix the hole in your veil." Together, they fixed the hole, and Mia's veil was as good as new. Tim learned to listen and be kind to others. And from that day on, Mia and Tim became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young barber. He had a small shop in town. Every day, he would cut hair and make people look nice. One day, a boy came to the barber's shop. The boy said, "I want a haircut, but I don't want you to spoil it." The young barber smiled and said, "Don't worry, I will do my best." As the barber started to cut the boy's hair, a big wind blew into the shop. The wind made a mess of the boy's hair. The barber was very sad because he thought he had spoiled the haircut. But the boy looked in the mirror and laughed. He said, "I love my hair now! It looks so fun and wild!" The young barber was happy that the boy liked his hair, even if it was not what he had planned.
