Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Box

Lily and Tom are hungry. They look for something to eat in the kitchen. They see a big box on the table. It says "cheese" on it. "Let's open it!" Lily says. She and Tom climb on the chairs and pull the box down. They rip the paper and see a yellow block inside. "Wow, cheese!" Tom says. He breaks a piece and puts it in his mouth. He makes a face and spits it out. "Yuck, this cheese is bitter!" Lily laughs. She takes a piece and tries it too. She also spits it out. "You are right, Tom. This cheese is not good. It is too old and hard." They hear a voice behind them. It is Mom. She looks angry. "What are you doing with my cheese?" she asks. "That is not for you. That is for cooking. It is a special cheese." Lily and Tom feel sorry. They say "sorry, Mom" and give her the cheese. Mom sighs and hugs them. "It's okay, my loves. But next time, ask me before you take something. I have some other cheese for you. It is soft and sweet. Do you want some?" Lily and Tom nod. They follow Mom to the fridge. They find some white cheese with holes. Mom cuts some slices and gives them to Lily and Tom. They bite and smile. "This cheese is good!" they say. "Thank you, Mom!" Mom smiles too. She is happy that Lily and Tom like the cheese. She says "You are welcome, my loves. Now, let's have some bread and fruit too. Then we can read a story together. How does that sound?" Lily and Tom say "yes, please!" They love Mom and her cheese. They are not hungry anymore. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a very brave girl. One day, Lily and her mom went to a restaurant. They wanted to eat some yummy food. In the restaurant, Lily saw a big dog. She was not scared. She looked at the dog and said, "Hi, dog! I am brave!" The dog wagged its tail and said, "Woof!" Lily's mom smiled and said, "You are very brave, Lily!" Lily and her mom sat down and ate some yummy food. They were happy. After they finished eating, they went home. Lily knew she was a brave girl and she was proud of herself.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and eat ice cream. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw an ice cream truck and Tim's eyes lit up. "Mom, can I have an ice cream cone, please?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, but you have to be careful not to drop it." Tim nodded and his mom gave him some money to buy the ice cream. He grabbed the cone with both hands and started to eat it happily. While Tim was eating his ice cream, he saw a little girl crying. Her ice cream cone had fallen on the ground and it was broken. Tim felt sad for her and wanted to help. He walked over to the girl and said, "Don't cry, you can have my ice cream cone." The girl stopped crying and smiled. Tim's mom saw what happened and was very proud of him. She hugged Tim and said, "I'm so proud of you for sharing your ice cream with the little girl. That was very kind." Tim felt happy that he could help someone else. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others. When you help someone, it can make both of you feel happy.

Anna and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the hut in the garden. The hut was made of wood and had a door and a window. Anna and Ben enjoyed pretending that the hut was their house. One day, Anna brought a delicate doll to the hut. She said, "This is my baby. She is very pretty and soft. I love her very much." Ben looked at the doll and said, "Can I hold her?" Anna said, "No, you can't. You are too rough. You might break her." Ben felt angry. He wanted to hold the doll too. He said, "That's not fair. You always get to play with your toys. I want to play with them too." Anna said, "No, you don't. They are mine. Go away." Ben said, "No, you go away. This is my hut too." Anna and Ben started to shout and push each other. They forgot to be careful. They knocked over a table and a chair. The doll fell on the floor. Anna and Ben stopped fighting. They looked at the doll. She had a tear in her dress and a crack in her face. Anna and Ben felt sad. They had hurt the doll and each other. Anna said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I was mean to you. I should have let you hold the doll." Ben said, "I'm sorry, Anna. I was rough with you. I should have been gentle with the doll." Anna and Ben hugged each other. They said, "We are still friends. We can share our toys and our hut." They picked up the doll and the furniture. They fixed the doll's dress and face. They said, "We love you, baby. You are still pretty and soft." They enjoyed playing in the hut again.
