Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Wardrobe and the Toy

The girl was very excited. She had a new wardrobe in her bedroom. She liked exploring it and opening the doors to look inside. She was so excited she ran around, throwing things out of it. Suddenly, her favorite toy disappeared. She searched everywhere, but it was nowhere to be seen. She felt so sad and tears rolled down her cheeks. Then her mom came in and said, "Don't worry, let's look together." They searched all around in the new wardrobe, but it was gone. Then mom said, "Maybe it fell behind the wardrobe. Let me move it and check." When mom moved the wardrobe, the girl's toy was there, hiding from her. She was so happy, she hugged her toy, taking care not to let it disappear again.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a spider in his room. The spider was on the floor. Tim was not scared. He wanted to help the spider go outside. Tim said, "Hi, spider! Do you want to go outside?" The spider said, "Yes, please! I want to go outside and play in the sun." Tim thought about how to help the spider. He had an idea. He would lift the spider with a clear cup. Tim got a clear cup from the kitchen. He put the cup on top of the spider. Then, he lifted the cup with the spider inside. Tim walked to the door and put the cup outside. The spider was happy. "Thank you, Tim!" said the spider. "Now I can play outside with my friends." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, spider! Have fun!" The spider went to play in the grass, and Tim went back to his room. They were both happy.

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to explore. One day, they found an ancient house in the woods. It was big and dark and had many holes in the walls. "Let's go inside and see what's there," Tim said. "OK, but be careful," Sam said. They stepped into the house and looked around. They saw old furniture, dusty books and broken toys. They also heard strange noises, like creaks and squeaks and howls. "What's that?" Sam asked, pointing to a door. "Maybe it's a secret room," Tim said. "Let's open it and find out." They stepped to the door and pushed it. It opened with a loud bang. Inside, they saw a big bed with a blanket and a pillow. On the bed, there was a furry animal. It was sleeping. "It's a bear!" Sam shouted. "A bear?" Tim said. "But bears don't live in houses." "This one does," Sam said. "And it looks angry. Run!" They turned around and ran out of the house. The bear woke up and followed them. It roared and chased them. They ran as fast as they could, until they reached their home. They slammed the door and locked it. They were safe. "That was scary," Tim said. "Very scary," Sam said. They hugged each other and took a nap. They dreamed of the ancient house and the bear. They decided not to go there again.

Sara and Ben like to play in the park. They run, jump, swing and slide. They have fun with their friends. One day, they see a big hill with a lot of leaves. They want to slide down the hill. "Let's go, Sara!" Ben says. "It looks like fun!" "OK, Ben!" Sara says. "But be careful. There might be twigs and rocks under the leaves." They climb up the hill and sit on the leaves. They hold hands and slide down. They go very fast and feel the wind in their hair. They laugh and scream. But then, Sara feels something poke her leg. She looks down and sees a twig. It is sharp and hurts. She cries out. "Ow, ow, ow! Ben, I have a twig in my leg!" Ben stops sliding and looks at Sara. He sees the twig and the blood. He feels scared and sorry. "Oh no, Sara! I'm sorry! Are you OK?" He pulls out the twig and throws it away. He hugs Sara and tries to make her feel better. "It's OK, Ben. It's not your fault. It was an accident. But it hurts a lot." "Let's go to mom. She will help you. She has a band-aid and a kiss. She will make it fine." They get up and walk to the bench where mom is sitting. She sees them and smiles. But then she sees Sara's leg and frowns. "What happened, Sara? Ben? Did you slide down the hill?" "Yes, mom. We did. But there was a twig and it poked Sara. She has a boo-boo. Can you help her?" "Of course, sweetie. Come here. Let me see. Oh, poor Sara. It's a small cut, but it's bleeding. Don't worry. I have a band-aid and a kiss. I will make it fine." Mom takes out a band-aid and puts it on Sara's leg. She kisses it and hugs her. She says nice words and makes her smile. "Thank you, mom. You are the best. It feels better now." "You're welcome, Sara. I'm glad. You are very brave. How about you, Ben? Are you OK?" "I'm OK, mom. I'm sorry, Sara. I didn't mean to hurt you." "It's OK, Ben. I know. You are a good brother. I still love you." "I love you too, Sara. You are a good sister. Can we still play?" "Sure, Ben. But let's not slide down the hill again. Let's play something else." "OK, Sara. Let's do that." They hug and kiss mom and run to the playground. They play with their friends. They have fun. They are fine.
