Category: Beginner
Title: Amy and the Box

Once, there was a helpless girl called Amy. She had no money, so she could not buy the things she wanted. One day, Amy saw some money in a box, so she decided to lift it. But just as she was about to lift the box, a big man saw her and shouted! He was angry and he grabbed the box of money away from her. No matter how much she begged, he would not give her any of the money - he kept it all for himself. Amy was very sad, but there was nothing she could do - she was helpless and the man had taken all the money.

Tom was a lazy boy. He did not like to do his work or help his mom. He only liked to play with his toys and watch TV. One day, his mom told him to introduce himself to the new girl next door. Her name was Lily and she had just moved in with her dad. "Mom, I don't want to go. I don't know her and she might be mean," Tom said. "Tom, don't be rude. You should be friendly and kind to new people. Maybe you will make a new friend. Now go and say hello to Lily," his mom said. Tom sighed and went outside. He saw Lily sitting on the porch with a big box of books. She looked sad and lonely. Tom walked up to her and said, "Hi, I'm Tom. I live next door. What's your name?" "Hi, I'm Lily. I just moved here with my dad. We had to leave our old house because it had a big leak and it was wet and moldy," Lily said. "That's too bad. I'm sorry. Do you like books?" Tom asked. "Yes, I love books. They are my favorite thing. Do you like books?" Lily asked. Tom shrugged. He did not read much. He thought books were boring. He preferred to play with his toys and watch TV. He said, "Not really. I like toys and TV. Do you want to see my toys?" Lily nodded. She was curious about Tom's toys. She followed him to his house. Tom showed her his room. He had a lot of toys, but they were messy and broken. He had cars, trucks, robots, dinosaurs, and more. He picked up a car and said, "This is my best toy. It can go fast and make noise. Watch." He pressed a button and the car zoomed across the floor. It hit the wall and fell apart. Tom laughed. Lily did not. She said, "That's not nice. You should take care of your toys. They are not fun if they are broken." Tom did not listen. He picked up another toy and threw it in the air. It landed on his bed and made a hole in his blanket. Tom laughed again. Lily did not. She said, "That's not good. You should respect your things. They are not cheap if they are ruined." Tom did not care. He picked up another toy and smashed it on the floor. It broke into pieces and made a mess. Tom laughed louder. Lily did not. She said, "That's not smart. You should value your stuff. They are not easy if they are wasted." Tom did not stop. He picked up another toy and tossed it out the window. It flew into the street and hit a car. The car honked and the driver yelled. Tom laughed harder. Lily did not. She said, "That's not right. You should be careful with your actions. They are not funny if they are harmful." Tom did not apologize. He picked up another toy and aimed it at Lily. He said, "You are no fun. You are boring and mean. I don't want to be your friend. Go away." He threw the toy at Lily. It hit her in the face and made her cry. Tom did not laugh. He felt bad. He said, "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to hurt you. Please don't cry." Lily did not answer. She ran out of the room and back to her house. She took her books with her. She did not want to see Tom again. Tom felt worse. He looked at his toys and his room. They were all broken and messy. He did not feel happy. He felt lonely and sorry. He realized that he had been lazy and rude. He had not done his work or helped his mom. He had not been friendly or kind to Lily. He had not taken care of his toys or his things. He had not respected his actions or their consequences. He had not made a new friend. He had made a new enemy. He wished he could change what he had done. He wished he could say sorry to Lily and be her friend. He wished he could read her books and learn new things. He wished he could fix his toys and his room. He wished he could do his work and help his mom. He wished he could be a better boy.
