Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a shy boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with his friends. One day, they all decide to play in the park. The park was big and had a lot of trees. It was a fun place to play. That day, there was a lot of mist in the air. It was hard to see far away. Tim and his friends played hide and seek. Tim hid behind a big tree. He was good at hiding because he was shy. Tim's friends could not find him. They looked everywhere. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the mist away. Tim was not behind the tree anymore. He was lost. His friends were sad and could not find him. The end.

One day, a busy bee named Buzz was flying around. He was looking for sweet flowers to get food. Buzz saw a big, red flower. He flew to it and said, "I guess this is a good one!" He got some food from the flower and went back to his home. On his way, Buzz saw a little girl named Lily. She was playing with a toy. Buzz wanted to play too. But he knew he had a job to do. He thought, "I can't play yet. I need to finish my work first." After Buzz finished his work, he went back to find Lily. She was still playing with her toy. Buzz flew close and said, "Now I can play!" Lily saw Buzz and smiled. They played together and had a lot of fun. Buzz was happy that he finished his work and got to play with his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little hut in the woods. In the hut lived a big, enormous bear. The bear loved his hut very much. It was his home, and he was happy there. One day, the enormous bear went for a walk in the woods. He walked and walked, looking at the trees and the flowers. But then, the bear got lost. He could not find his way back to his little hut. The bear was sad and scared. A kind squirrel saw the lost bear and wanted to help. The squirrel knew where the hut was. So, the squirrel led the enormous bear through the woods, back to his little hut. The bear was so happy to be home again. He thanked the kind squirrel and they became friends. From that day on, the bear and the squirrel played together in the woods, and the bear never got lost again.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken named Chick. Chick was not very brave. He was scared of many things. One day, he met a fierce dog. The dog was big and loud. Chick was very scared. Chick's friend, Bunny, saw Chick was scared. Bunny said, "Chick, don't be scared. I will help you. We can be brave together." Bunny wanted to encourage Chick to be brave. Chick and Bunny walked up to the fierce dog. They said, "Hello, dog. We want to be friends." The dog wagged his tail and smiled. The dog was not so scary after all. Chick and Bunny were happy. They learned to be brave together.

Lily and Sam like to play in the snow. They make snowballs and snowmen and snow forts. They have fun and laugh a lot. One day, they find a big brown rope in the shed. They wonder what they can do with it. They have an idea. They tie one end of the rope to a tree and the other end to a sled. They make a hill of snow near the tree. Lily goes first. She sits on the sled and holds the rope. Sam pushes her down the hill. She slides fast and says, "Whee!" She pulls the rope to stop near the tree. She smiles and says, "That was fun!" Sam takes his turn. He sits on the sled and holds the rope. Lily pushes him down the hill. He slides faster and says, "Wow!" He pulls the rope to stop near the tree. He grins and says, "That was awesome!" They take turns again and again. They have more fun and laugh more. They do not notice that it is getting dark and cold. The snow starts to freeze and the rope starts to freeze too. Lily goes one more time. She sits on the sled and holds the rope. Sam pushes her down the hill. She slides very fast and says, "Yay!" She pulls the rope to stop near the tree. But the rope does not move. It is frozen. She slides past the tree and into the woods. She says, "Uh-oh!" Sam sees what happens. He runs after her. He calls her name. He is scared and worried. He hopes she is okay. Lily stops in the woods. She is not hurt. But she is stuck. The sled is stuck in the snow. The rope is stuck to the sled. She is cold and scared. She calls for Sam. She hopes he can find her. Sam finds her. He is happy and relieved. He hugs her and says, "I'm sorry. I'm here. You're safe." He tries to free the rope and the sled. But they are too frozen. He says, "We need help." He sees a light in the distance. It is their house. He sees their mom and dad. They are looking for them. They are waving and shouting. He waves and shouts back. He says, "We're here! We're here! We need help!" Their mom and dad see them. They run to them. They are glad and worried. They hug them and say, "We're sorry. We're here. You're safe." They bring a shovel and a blanket. They dig out the rope and the sled. They wrap them in the blanket. They say, "Let's go home." They go home. They are warm and cozy. They drink hot cocoa and eat cookies. They tell their mom and dad what happened. They say, "We're sorry. We were having fun. We did not mean to freeze the rope and get lost." Their mom and dad say, "We're glad you're okay. We love you. We know you were having fun. But you have to be careful. You have to watch the time and the weather. You have to stay close and safe." Lily and Sam say, "We're sorry. We love you. We will be careful. We will watch the time and the weather. We will stay close and safe." They hug and kiss their mom and dad. They go to bed. They dream of snow and rope and sleds. They are happy.
