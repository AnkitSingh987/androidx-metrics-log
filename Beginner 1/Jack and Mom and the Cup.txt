Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and Mom and the Cup

Jack and Mom were in the kitchen. Mom pointed at a jar on the counter. "What's inside?" asked Jack. Mom said, "That's milk. Would you like some?" Jack said, "Yes please!" Mom opened the jar and pulled out a cup from a drawer. She filled it with milk and handed it to Jack. Jack took the cup in his hands and said, "It's cold!" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, the milk is cold and wet." Mom then took a thin, tight piece of string and tied it around the jar. She said, "This will keep the milk inside the jar and make sure it doesn't spill out." Jack held his cup tightly in both hands and drank his milk. He smiled when he was done and said, "That was yummy!" Mom smiled back and said, "Yes, that's why we always keep milk in the house!"

One day, a little bird named Bob was flying in the sky. He loved to sing. He sang all day long. Bob had a fan named Tim. Tim was a small, persistent ant who loved to hear Bob sing. One day, while Bob was singing, a big, mean bird came by. The mean bird did not like Bob's singing. He said, "Stop singing, or I will make you stop!" Bob was scared, but Tim the ant was brave. He told the mean bird, "No! Bob will keep singing!" The mean bird tried to stop Bob from singing, but Tim was persistent. He would not let the mean bird hurt Bob. Tim climbed up the mean bird's leg and bit him. The mean bird was so surprised that he flew away. Bob and Tim were so happy that they had won. They celebrated by singing and dancing together. From that day on, Bob and Tim were the best of friends.

One day, a girl named Sue went to play outside. She wore a big hat to keep the sun off her face. Sue saw her friend, Tom, in the park. They played together and had fun. Tom had a small teaspoon with him. He used it to dig in the sand. Sue said, "I like your teaspoon, Tom. It's not dull like mine. Can I try it?" Tom said, "Sure, Sue. You can try it." Sue was very happy. She used the teaspoon to make a big sandcastle. It was fun to use Tom's teaspoon. Then, a dog came running to the sandcastle. The dog knocked it down! Sue and Tom were sad. But they learned that it's not the teaspoon that makes things fun, but playing together. They built a new sandcastle together, and it was even better than before.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a foolish boy who loved to play with his shoes. One day, Tim's mom told him to put his shoes away. But Tim did not listen. Tim went outside to play. He saw a big red ball in the sky. He wanted to touch it. So, he took off his shoe and threw it up high. The shoe went up, up, up, and then came down, down, down. It landed in a tree. Tim was sad because his shoe was stuck. Tim asked his dad for help. His dad told him to use a long stick to release the shoe. Tim found a stick and poked the shoe. The shoe fell down and Tim was happy. He learned his lesson and put his shoes away. From that day on, Tim was not foolish with his shoes anymore.

Once there was a very glad girl. She wanted to go on an adventure. So she ran to her big closet, opened the door and she chose the most special outfit. She put on pink shoes and a blue dress! Next, she selected the perfect accessory - a zipper. She pulled up the zipper, and it zipped from top to bottom. She clapped her hands and smiled a big happy smile. She was ready to go! She ran outside into the sunshine and she was so glad that she chose the zipper. She couldn’t wait to explore and go on her adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a stable. In the stable, there was a horse named Tom and a boy named Tim. They were good friends. One day, Tom and Tim had a big job to do. They had to make the stable tidy. Tom said, "Let's finish this job fast!" Tim agreed, and they started to clean. They put hay in the right place, and they swept the floor. But then, they saw a big pile of mud in the corner. "Oh no!" said Tim. "This mud is too big for us to clean!" Tom looked sad. They didn't know what to do. They tried to push the mud, but it didn't move. Just then, their friend Sue came to the stable. She saw the big mud pile and said, "I can help you!" Sue, Tom, and Tim worked together. They pushed the mud out of the stable. At last, the stable was tidy. "Thank you, Sue!" said Tim. Tom neighed happily. They were all happy because they finished their job. The stable was clean, and they could play together. And they all lived happily ever after.
