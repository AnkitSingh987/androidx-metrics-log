Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Fish

Tim and Sam like to go to the park with Mom. They see many things at the park. They see birds, dogs, flowers, and trees. They also see a big pond with water. In the water, they see fish. The fish are different colors. Some are red, some are blue, some are yellow, and some are green. The fish swim fast and slow. They swim up and down. They swim near and far. Tim and Sam like to watch the fish. One day, Tim sees a very big fish. The fish is wide and long. The fish is orange and black. The fish has big teeth. Tim is scared of the fish. He tells Sam to look at the fish. "Sam, look at that fish!" Tim says. "It is so big and wide. It has big teeth. I do not like that fish." Sam looks at the fish. He is not scared of the fish. He thinks the fish is funny. He laughs at the fish. "Tim, that is not a fish," Sam says. "That is a toy. Someone put a toy in the water. It is not real. It cannot hurt you." Tim looks at the fish again. He sees that Sam is right. The fish is a toy. It is not real. It cannot hurt him. He feels silly. He laughs with Sam. "Sam, you are right," Tim says. "That is a toy. It is not a fish. It is funny. I like that toy." Mom hears Tim and Sam laughing. She comes to see what they are laughing at. She sees the toy fish in the water. She smiles. "Did you see the toy fish?" Mom asks. "It is a funny toy. Someone must have put it there as a joke. Do you like the toy fish?" Tim and Sam nod. They like the toy fish. They tell Mom about the toy fish. They mention how big and wide it is. They mention how it has big teeth. They mention how they were scared and then laughed. Mom listens and laughs with them. She is happy that they are having fun. She hugs them and kisses them. They hug and kiss her back. They love Mom. They love the park. They love the fish.

One day, a little boy named Tim found some gum on the ground. He picked it up and thought it looked yummy. Tim wanted to eat the gum, but he knew it was not fair to eat something he found on the ground. Tim went to his friend Sue and asked her what to do with the gum. Sue said, "You should become a gum detective and find who lost the gum." Tim liked the idea and started to look for clues. They asked their friends if they lost gum, but no one did. Tim and Sue were sad. They wanted to solve the problem. Then, they saw a girl named Lily. She was looking for something. Tim asked, "Did you lose gum?" Lily said, "Yes, I did!" Tim gave the gum back to Lily. They all became friends and played together. The day was fair and fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was an obedient boy who always helped his mom. One day, Tim's mom asked him to split a big cookie for his little sister, Sue. Tim went to the kitchen and saw a big mug on the table. He put the cookie on the table and split it into two parts. He put one part in the mug, and the other part on a plate for Sue. Tim was happy that he could help his mom and share with his sister. Tim took the mug and the plate to Sue, who was playing in her room. Sue was very happy to see the cookie. They both sat down and ate their cookie together. Tim and Sue loved sharing and being obedient. They knew it made their mom happy, and it made them happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a humble fisherman named Tom. He lived in a small house by the sea. Every day, Tom went to the sea to catch fish. He loved his job. One sunny day, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue liked to collect pretty shells on the beach. "Hi, Tom!" said Sue. "Do you want to help me collect shells?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to help you." Together, Tom and Sue collected many pretty shells. They laughed and had fun. At the end of the day, they went back to Tom's small house. They were happy and tired. From that day on, Tom and Sue were the best of friends.
