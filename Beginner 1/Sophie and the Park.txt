Category: Beginner
Title: Sophie and the Park

Sophie and her sister, Sarah, were playing in the park. Sophie saw a pink lollipop and wanted it. She asked her mom if she could have the lollipop. "No," Mom said. "We don't take things that don't belong to us." They kept playing in the park. Sarah saw a bright blue lollipop. "Look, Sophie," Sarah said. "I want the lollipop, can I have it?" Sophie shook her head. "Remember what Mom said," she said. "We mustn't take things that don't belong to us." But Sarah was really, really tempted. She wanted the lollipop. Without asking, Sarah grabbed the lollipop, and ran away. Sophie felt scared. She said loudly "Sarah, come back!" But Sarah kept running. Mom saw what happened. She ran after Sarah, but Sarah was already gone. "We must find Sarah!" Mom said. But they couldn't find her. Sophie was very sad. She should have listened to Mom and not taken the pink lollipop.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big bubble. She was very happy. She saw her friend, Tim, and said, "Look at my big bubble!" Tim looked at the bubble and smiled. Then, something strange happened. The bubble started to talk! "Hello, Sue and Tim," said the bubble. Sue and Tim could not believe it. They did not recognize that bubbles could talk. The bubble asked for help. "I am lost. Can you help me find my way home?" Sue and Tim were grateful to help their new friend. They held hands and walked with the bubble, looking for its home. At last, they found a big, blue house. The bubble was happy to be home. It said, "Thank you, Sue and Tim!" and went inside. Sue and Tim smiled and waved goodbye. They were happy to help their new friend, the talking bubble.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play near the sea. One sunny day, he went to the beach with his mom and dad. They all had a fun time. As they played, Tim saw a big wave. The wave was icy and cold. Tim wanted to jump in the wave. He said to his mom and dad, "I want to go in the wave!" Tim's mom and dad said, "Be careful, Tim!" Tim went into the wave and splashed around. He had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim didn't want to leave. He said, "I want to stay and play!" But his mom and dad said, "We must go home now, Tim." Tim was sad, but he knew he could come back another day. He waved goodbye to the icy wave and went home with his mom and dad. Tim couldn't wait to come back to the beach again.

Anna and Ben are at the park with their mom. They see an enormous slide that they want to try. They run to the slide and wait for their turn. "Can I go first?" Anna requests to Ben. "No, I want to go first!" Ben says. They start to argue and push each other. Their mom sees them and calls them. "Anna, Ben, stop fighting! Come here and sit on the bench with me!" she says. They walk to the bench and sit down. Their mom talks to them. "You both want to have fun, right? But you can't have fun if you are mean to each other. You need to share and take turns. How about you go to the slide together and help each other climb up and slide down?" she suggests. Anna and Ben think about it and nod. They say sorry to each other and hug. They go back to the slide and hold hands. They climb up and slide down, laughing and smiling. They have fun and make new friends. Their mom watches them and smiles too.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had a lot of toys, but their favorite was a big, furry sheep. The sheep was soft and white, and it had a lot of wool on its back. Lily and Ben liked to hug the sheep and pretend it was their friend. One day, they decided to play a game with the sheep. They took some scissors and cut some of the wool from the sheep. They wanted to make a hat and a scarf for themselves. They thought the sheep would not mind, because it had so much wool. But the sheep did mind. It did not like to lose its wool. It felt cold and angry. It wanted to get its wool back. It waited for Lily and Ben to finish their game and go to sleep. Then it sneaked into their room and looked for the hat and the scarf. It found them on a chair near the bed. It grabbed them with its teeth and pulled them hard. It tried to put them back on its back. But they did not fit. They were too small and too tight. The sheep felt even more cold and angry. It saw Lily and Ben sleeping in their bed. They looked warm and cozy. They had taken some of the sheep's wool and made themselves happy. The sheep felt jealous and mean. It decided to get revenge. It raised its head and struck the bed with its horns. It made a loud noise and woke up Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben opened their eyes and saw the sheep. They saw the hat and the scarf in its mouth. They saw the holes in its back. They realized what they had done. They felt sorry and scared. They said to the sheep: "Please, sheep, don't be mad. We are sorry we took your wool. We did not know you would be cold and angry. We wanted to play with you and make you happy. Please, sheep, forgive us. We love you, sheep." The sheep heard their words and saw their faces. It felt a little bit of warmth and kindness. It dropped the hat and the scarf and walked to the bed. It licked Lily and Ben's cheeks and snuggled with them. It felt a little bit of love and happiness. It said to Lily and Ben: "It's okay, Lily and Ben. I forgive you. I know you did not mean to hurt me. I love you, too. But please, don't take my wool again. It is mine and it keeps me warm. You can hug me and play with me, but don't cut my wool. Okay?" Lily and Ben nodded and hugged the sheep. They said: "Okay, sheep. We promise. We won't take your wool again. We will take care of you and keep you warm. You are our friend, sheep. The best friend ever." The sheep smiled and closed its eyes. It felt warm and cozy. It fell asleep with Lily and Ben. They all dreamed of happy things.
