Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play in the soil. One day, he dug a deep hole in his garden. He found something shiny in the hole. It was a magic ring! Tim put the ring on his finger. A friendly cat appeared. The cat said, "Hello, Tim! I am a magic cat. The ring lets me talk to you. I can help you with anything you want." Tim was so happy. He asked the cat, "Can you help me plant a big tree in the deep hole?" The cat agreed, and they planted a tree together. The tree grew big and strong. Tim and the magic cat became best friends, and they played in the garden every day.

Once upon a time, a fierce seal was swimming in ocean. It had been looking for a long time for a special kind of fish that it wanted to eat. But it just couldn't find it. The seal swam and swam, but no luck. Then, the seal heard some noise in the distance. It peered through the waves and saw a fisherman in a boat. The seal dived underwater, and quickly swam up to the boat. It peered out of the water, and saw the fisherman had caught the exact kind of fish the seal wanted to eat. The seal was very excited and bobbed in and out of the water. The fisherman could tell the seal wanted the fish and asked "Do you want to win this fish?" The seal eagerly nodded in agreement. So the fisherman said, "Let's see who will win!" The fisherman threw the fish in the water, and the seal chased after it with all of its might. In a couple of seconds the seal used its fierce speed and dives, and won the fish. The fisherman laughed, clapped his hands, and said "Well done! Eat up!" The seal was very happy and swam away quickly, holding the prize fish in its mouth. This was a perfect ending to the seal's search.

One day, a girl named Lucy wanted to design a pretty picture for her mom. She took out her colors and paper. Lucy was very happy to make the best picture ever. But, Lucy's little brother, Tim, was being naughty. He took Lucy's colors and started to draw on the wall. Lucy saw Tim and said, "No, Tim! That is bad!" She took the colors back from Tim. Lucy finished her design and showed it to her mom. Her mom was very happy and said, "Wow, what a nice photo!" But then, they all saw the wall. Mom saw Tim's drawing and said, "Oh no, Tim! That is very naughty!" But then, mom looked closer at the wall. Tim had drawn a big heart with "I love you" in the middle. It was a surprise! Everyone laughed and hugged. Tim was still a little naughty, but his drawing made them all smile.

Tim and Jen are twins. They like to play and have fun. But today, they have to stay in their room. Mom and Dad are busy. They have to talk on the cell. The cell is a thing that makes sounds. Mom and Dad use it to talk to other people. Tim and Jen are bored. They do not want to stay in their room. They want to go outside and see their friends. They want to play with their toys. They want to watch TV. They do not want to listen to Mom and Dad. They have an idea. They sneak out of their room. They go to the living room. They see the cell on the table. They pick it up. They press some buttons. They hear a voice. "Hello? Who is this?" the voice says. Tim and Jen giggle. They say silly things. They make funny noises. They think it is a game. But the voice is not happy. The voice is angry. The voice is loud. The voice is Mom's boss. Mom and Dad hear the voice. They run to the living room. They see Tim and Jen with the cell. They are shocked. They are mad. They take the cell from them. They say sorry to the voice. They hang up. They look at Tim and Jen. They are not smiling. They are frowning. They are scolding. "You are in big trouble!" they say. "You should not touch the cell. You should not bother the voice. You should listen to us. You should stay in your room. You should be quiet." Tim and Jen are sad. They are sorry. They did not know. They did not mean to. They say sorry to Mom and Dad. They hug them. Mom and Dad are still mad. But they are also kind. They love Tim and Jen. They hug them back. They say, "We know you are bored. But you have to be good. You have to follow the rules. You have to wait a little bit. Then we can play and have fun. OK?" Tim and Jen nod. They say, "OK." They go back to their room. They wait a little bit. They listen to Mom and Dad. They are good. They are not bored. They have each other. They are twins. They are happy.
