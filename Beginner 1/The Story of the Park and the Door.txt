Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Park and the Door

There was a happy boy, who enjoyed playing outside. One day, he was playing in the park when he saw an unusual vest. It was bright and colourful, like nothing he had seen before! He ran over to take a closer look. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. He looked up and saw a door slowly shut and lock itself. He wondered who could have done that. The boy shouted: "Who shut the door?" but there was no response. He was starting to get worried. Then, he heard a voice behind him: "It was me. I shut the door. You must be careful in the park." The boy turned to see a kind old woman. She smiled and said: "Come, I will show you something special." The boy followed her eagerly. She walked over to the colourful vest that he had seen earlier. "Look here," she said, "this vest will keep you safe and happy when you are playing out in the park." She fastened the vest on him and watched as his face lit up with joy. The boy thanked her and gave her a big hug. He ran off outside to explore the park, feeling safe and happy in his new vest.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a loyal dog named Max. Max was friends with a little girl named Lily. They played together every day. One day, they found a shiny diamond in the forest. "Wow, look at this!" said Lily. Max barked happily. They decided to give the diamond to Lily's mom as a gift. When they got home, Lily gave the diamond to her mom. "Thank you, Lily and Max!" said her mom with a big smile. They all hugged and were happy to have each other.

Tim and Jen are brother and sister. They like to play with toys, but they do not like to share. One day, they both want the same toy car. They fight over the toy car. They pull and push and yell. Mom hears them fight. She is angry. She tells them to stop and go to their rooms. Tim and Jen do not listen. They keep fighting. They are impatient. They want the toy car now. They fight near the stair. The stair is high and steep. It is not safe to fight there. Mom warns them. She says, "Be careful! You will fall and hurt yourself!" Tim and Jen do not hear her. They are too busy fighting. Then, something bad happens. Tim pulls the toy car hard. Jen loses her grip. She falls back. She falls down the stair. She hits her head. She does not move. She does not make a sound. Tim drops the toy car. He is scared. He runs to Jen. He cries. He says, "I'm sorry! I'm sorry!" Mom runs to Jen. She cries. She says, "Oh no! Oh no!" She calls for help. But it is too late. Jen is gone.

One day, a brown owl was in a big tree. The owl saw a little boy playing with a red ball. The boy was happy and the owl liked to watch him. The boy threw the ball too far and it went into the woods. The boy was sad and wanted his ball back. The brown owl wanted to help, so it flew down and picked up the ball. The owl tried to rush back to the boy, but it was not fast enough. A big dog saw the owl and wanted the ball too. The dog jumped up and took the ball from the owl. The brown owl was sad that it could not help the boy. The dog ran away with the ball and the boy was still sad. The owl went back to the tree and the boy went home without his ball.

One day, a little boy named Tim got a new toy. He loved his new toy so much. He played with it all day. But then, the rain came. Tim was sad because he could not play outside with his new toy. Tim's mom said, "Don't be sad, Tim. We can play inside the house." But Tim did not want to play inside. He wanted to play outside with his new toy. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "I will scare the rain away!" Tim went outside and yelled, "Go away, rain!" But the rain did not go away. Tim's mom came outside and said, "We can't scare the rain away, but we can play with your new toy inside." Tim and his mom went inside and played with the new toy. They had a lot of fun. Tim learned that he could have fun even when it rained.
