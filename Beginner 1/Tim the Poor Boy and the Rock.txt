Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Poor Boy and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a poor boy named Tim. Tim had a toy called a microscope. He loved to look at things with it. One day, Tim went to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a big rock. He wanted to look at the rock with his microscope. He put the microscope on the rock and saw many tiny things on it. Tim was very happy to see these tiny things. As he played, a girl named Sue came up to Tim. She saw the microscope and asked if she could look too. Tim said yes, and they both looked at the rock together. They became good friends and played with the microscope every day.

Once upon a time, there was an otter who lived near the ocean. The otter was having trouble - she weighed much more than any other otter. Every day, even when she ate the same as the other otters, she would grow more and more. One afternoon, the otter looked in the water and saw her reflection. She was surprised to see her thin frame! She gasped and felt the silly otter had to find a way to keep the weight off. The next morning the otter asked her friends, "How can I stay thin like this?" Her friends said, "Eat less and exercise more - that's always a good solution." The otter took her friends advice and started to weigh less and less every day. At the end of a few weeks, the otter was happy to see that she was finally the same size as all of the other otters. She thanked her friends for their help and was glad that she had solved her problem.

Ben and Mia are going to the park with Mom. They put on their coats and shoes. Ben tries to zip his coat, but he can't. He asks Mom for help. "Mom, can you zip my coat?" Ben says. "Sure, honey. Let me see." Mom says. She takes Ben's coat and zips it up. "There you go. All done." "Thank you, Mom." Ben says. He smiles and runs to the door. Mia is still putting on her shoes. She is impatient. She wants to go to the park now. She pushes her feet into her shoes. She does not tie her laces. She does not care. "Mia, wait. You have to tie your laces." Mom says. "No, I don't. I want to go now." Mia says. She runs to the door, too. "Mia, stop. You will trip and hurt yourself." Mom says. She follows Mia to the door. But Mia does not listen. She opens the door and runs outside. She does not see the step. She trips over her laces. She falls down. She cries. "Mia, are you okay?" Mom says. She runs to Mia and picks her up. "Ow, ow, ow. My toe, my toe." Mia says. She points to her toe. It is red and bleeding. "Oh, Mia. I'm sorry. You have a boo-boo. Let me see." Mom says. She takes off Mia's shoe and sock. She kisses Mia's toe. "It will be okay. I have a band-aid for you." "Thank you, Mom." Mia says. She sniffles and hugs Mom. "Next time, will you tie your laces?" Mom says. "Yes, Mom. I'm sorry." Mia says. "It's okay. I love you." Mom says. "I love you, too." Mia says. They go back inside. Mom puts a band-aid on Mia's toe. She ties Mia's laces. She zips Mia's coat. She holds Mia's hand. "Are you ready to go to the park now?" Mom says. "Yes, Mom. But can we wait for Ben?" Mia says. "Of course. He is waiting for us, too." Mom says. They go to the door. Ben is there. He smiles at Mia. "Are you okay, Mia?" Ben says. "Yes, Ben. I'm okay. Thank you." Mia says. They go to the park. They have fun. They are careful. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big wide pond. In the pond, there lived a little fish. The little fish loved to swim all day long. He would swim up and down, left and right, all around the pond. He was a very happy fish. One day, the little fish met a big frog. The big frog liked to relax on a big green leaf. He would sit on the leaf and watch the little fish swim. The frog said, "Little fish, why don't you come and relax with me on this big green leaf?" The little fish thought about it and decided to try. He swam up to the big green leaf and jumped on it. The little fish and the big frog sat on the leaf and relaxed together. They looked at the sky and the trees around the pond. They became very good friends. From that day on, the little fish would swim and play in the wide pond. When he got tired, he would go and relax with the big frog on the big green leaf. They spent many happy days together, just swimming and relaxing in their beautiful pond.
