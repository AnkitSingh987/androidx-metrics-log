Category: Beginner
Title: The Popular Race and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a popular race. A lot of people liked to watch it. The race was in a big park with many trees. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Many animals wanted to race too. In the race, there was a fast dog, a quick cat, and a small bird. They all wanted to win the race. The race started with a loud sound. The dog, the cat, and the bird started to run very fast. The people cheered for their favorite animal. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the small bird in front of the dog and the cat. The bird won the race! Everyone was surprised. They clapped and cheered for the small bird. The bird became the most popular animal in the park.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big box in the living room. It had a tag that said "For Lily and Ben, a reward for being good kids". They were very happy and curious. They wanted to open the box and see what was inside. But their mom said, "No, not yet. You have to wait until your dad comes home. He has a surprise for you. The box is fragile, so be careful. Don't touch it or shake it or break it." Lily and Ben nodded, but they were impatient. They went to their room and played with their other toys, but they kept thinking about the box. They wondered what was in it. Maybe it was a new game, or a book, or a doll, or a car. After a while, Ben said, "I can't wait any longer. I want to see what's in the box. Let's go and peek. Mom won't know. She's busy in the kitchen." Lily said, "No, Ben. That's not nice. Mom told us to wait. We have to listen to her. She will be sad if we disobey. And what if we hurt the box? It's fragile, remember?" Ben said, "Oh, come on, Lily. Don't be a baby. It's just a little peek. We won't hurt the box. We'll be very gentle. And mom won't be sad. She'll be proud that we're curious and smart. Come on, let's go." Lily said, "No, Ben. I don't want to go. You go if you want, but I'll stay here." Ben said, "Fine. I'll go by myself. You're no fun." He left the room and went to the living room. He saw the box on the table. He tiptoed to it and lifted the lid. He saw something shiny and colorful. He reached for it. But then he heard a loud noise. It was a siren. It was coming from the box. It was an alarm. Ben was scared. He dropped the lid and ran back to his room. He hid under his bed. Mom heard the noise and came to the living room. She saw the box and the alarm. She knew what had happened. She was angry and disappointed. She turned off the alarm and went to find Ben. She found him under his bed. She said, "Ben, what did you do? Why did you open the box? You know you were not supposed to. You broke the rules. You made a mess. You ruined the surprise. You made me and dad sad. You have to suffer the consequences. You have to go to time out. And you have to apologize to Lily and dad. And you have to wait until tomorrow to see what's in the box. Maybe then you'll learn to be patient and respectful." Ben said, "I'm sorry, mom. I was curious. I wanted to see what's in the box. I didn't mean to hurt it. I didn't know it had an alarm. Please don't be mad at me. Please don't make me go to time out. Please let me see what's in the box." Mom said, "No, Ben. You have to go to time out. You have to learn from your mistake. You have to suffer the consequences. Maybe then you'll appreciate the reward. Now go to your room and stay there until I say so." Ben said, "Okay, mom. I'm sorry. I love you." He went to his room and sat on his bed. He felt sad and ashamed. He wished he had listened to Lily and mom. He wondered what was in the box. He hoped it was something good. He hoped Lily and dad would forgive him. He hoped mom would still love him. He learned a hard lesson. He learned that curiosity can be good, but it can also be bad. He learned that rules are there for a reason. He learned that fragile things need to be handled with care. He learned that rewards are sweeter when they are earned. He learned that suffering can make you grow. He learned that he had to wait.
