Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Dog

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Come, Sam, let's go on the slide!" Tom says. "OK, Tom, let's go!" Sam says. They run to the slide. They climb up the ladder. They sit on the slide. They slide down fast. They laugh and have fun. But then they see a big dog. The dog is black and brown. The dog has sharp teeth. The dog barks loud. The dog runs to the slide. "Look, Tom, a dog!" Sam says. "Uh-oh, Sam, a dog!" Tom says. They are scared. They do not like the dog. They want to go away. They jump off the slide. They run to the bench. They hide behind the bench. The dog comes to the slide. The dog smells the slide. The dog sees the boys. The dog wants to play. The dog wags its tail. The dog is lively. "Hi, boys, do you want to play?" the dog says. The boys hear the dog. They peek from behind the bench. They see the dog's tail. They see the dog's eyes. The dog looks nice. The dog looks friendly. The boys are not scared anymore. They are shocked. They are surprised. They did not know the dog can talk. "Did you hear that, Tom? The dog can talk!" Sam says. "I heard that, Sam! The dog can talk!" Tom says. They come out from behind the bench. They go to the dog. They pet the dog. They hug the dog. They play with the dog. The dog is happy. The boys are happy. They are friends. They have fun. They laugh and talk. They forget about the slide.

Once upon a time, there was a purple cat named Tom. Tom was not like other cats. He had a big stomach because he loved to eat. Every day, Tom would take food from his bowl and eat and eat. One day, Tom saw a big purple ball. He thought it was food, so he tried to take it. But when he bit the ball, it popped! Tom was very surprised. His stomach felt funny. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom's stomach started to get smaller. He was not a big cat anymore. Tom was happy because now he could play with other cats. And from that day on, he never tried to eat a purple ball again.

One day, a little bunny named Tim went out to play. He found a shiny razor in the grass. Tim didn't know what it was, but he knew it was sharp. He thought he should hide it so no one would get hurt. Tim saw his friend, the squirrel named Sam. Tim said, "Sam, I found a sharp thing. I want to hide it so we stay safe." Sam said, "Good idea, Tim! Let's hide it under the big rock." So they worked together to hide the razor under the big rock. After they hid the razor, Tim and Sam played all day. They felt grateful that they kept their friends safe. They knew they did a good thing by hiding the razor. And from that day on, they always looked out for each other and their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a nice dog named Max. Max liked to sleep a lot. He had a soft bed that he loved to sleep on. Max had a friend, a cat named Sam. One day, Max and Sam wanted to find a new place to play. They found a map on the floor. The map showed them where to go. Max said, "Let's follow the map, Sam!" Sam agreed, "Yes, let's go, Max!" They walked and walked, following the map. They found a big tree with lots of shade. Max and Sam played under the tree all day. When they were tired, they went to sleep under the tree. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to move and play all day. He had a friend named Lulu. Lulu was a little bug. They lived in a big tree. One day, Bobo said, "Lulu, I have a fun game. I will move fast, and you try to catch me!" Lulu said, "Okay, Bobo. But I am small. I can't fly as fast as you." Bobo smiled and said, "Don't worry, Lulu. We will have fun!" They played the game all day. Bobo would move and fly, and Lulu would try to catch him. But Lulu was too slow. She could not catch Bobo. Lulu was sad. Bobo saw Lulu was sad and thought of a new game. Bobo said, "Lulu, let's play a new game. You can move and hide, and I will try to find you." Lulu said, "That sounds fun, Bobo!" So, Lulu moved and hid in the tree. Bobo looked and looked. He could not find Lulu. Then, he saw her hiding in a small hole. Bobo laughed and said, "I found you, Lulu!" Lulu was happy. She and Bobo played the new game all day. They were the best of friends and had lots of fun together.
