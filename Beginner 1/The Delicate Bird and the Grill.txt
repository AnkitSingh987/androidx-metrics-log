Category: Beginner
Title: The Delicate Bird and the Grill

Once upon a time, there was a delicate little bird. The bird lived near a big grill. The grill was where the family cooked their food. The bird liked to watch them cook and play. One day, the sky was full of dark clouds. It started to rain. The rain was heavy and it soaked the bird. The bird was wet and cold. It needed a warm place to go. The family saw the wet bird. They felt sad for it. They took the bird near the grill. The grill was warm and cozy. The bird dried off and felt happy. The family and the bird became friends.

Once upon a time there was a young dove who was feeling very troubled. He was alone and did not know what to do. The dove searched for someone to follow and eventually saw a sparrow. He felt very happy and followed the sparrow around the sky. The sparrow noticed the dove and asked him, "Why are you following me?" The dove replied, "I'm very troubled and I need someone to help me." The kind sparrow said, "I will help you, friend. Follow me to find a nice place to stay." The dove followed the sparrow and soon they found a big tree with lots of bright green leaves. The dove felt happy and was no longer troubled. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a very clean girl. She would always clean her room and wash her hands. Sue had a big wish. She wanted a new toy. One day, Sue decided to pray for her wish. She closed her eyes and said, "Please, please, give me a new toy." She waited and waited, but nothing happened. Sue felt sad. The next day, Sue went to the park. She saw a dirty toy on the ground. It was the toy she wanted! Sue was so happy. She took the toy home and cleaned it. Now, she had her wish come true.

Lily hated the dark. She always had bad dreams when she slept in the dark. She dreamed of big monsters and loud noises and scary things. She woke up crying and shaking. She wanted her mom and dad to come and hug her and make her feel safe. One night, Lily had a very bad nightmare. She dreamed that a huge snake was chasing her and trying to bite her. She ran and ran, but the snake was faster. She screamed and screamed, but no one heard her. She felt the snake's teeth on her leg and she woke up with a jolt. "Mommy! Daddy!" she cried. "Help me! Help me!" Her mom and dad heard her and ran to her room. They turned on the light and saw Lily sobbing on her bed. They rushed to her and hugged her and kissed her. "What's wrong, sweetie?" her mom asked. "Did you have a bad dream?" "Yes, Mommy," Lily said. "A very bad dream. A snake was trying to eat me. It hurt me. It was so scary." "Oh, honey, I'm so sorry," her dad said. "It was just a dream. It wasn't real. The snake can't hurt you. You're safe with us. We love you." Lily felt a little better. She snuggled with her mom and dad and told them about her nightmare. They listened and comforted her. They told her that they had bad dreams sometimes too, but they always went away. They told her that she was brave and strong and that they were proud of her. Lily smiled and thanked them. She felt their love and warmth. She asked them if they could stay with her until she fell asleep. They said yes and cuddled with her. They sang her a lullaby and stroked her hair. Lily closed her eyes and felt peaceful. She didn't hate the dark so much anymore. She knew that her mom and dad were always there for her. She drifted into a sweet sleep, without any nightmares.

Anna and Ben are playing in the sand. Anna has a colorful spade. She likes to dig holes and fill them with shells. Ben has a blue bucket. He likes to make castles and towers. "Look at my castle!" Ben says. "It is big and strong." Anna looks at Ben's castle. She thinks it is boring. She wants to play with her spade. "I don't like your castle," Anna says. "It is not pretty. It is only blue." Ben feels sad. He likes his castle. He thinks it is cool. "I don't like your spade," Ben says. "It is too colorful. It hurts my eyes." Anna feels angry. She likes her spade. She thinks it is fun. "I hate your bucket," Anna says. "It is too small. It can't hold anything." "I hate your spade," Ben says. "It is too big. It can't make anything." Anna and Ben start to fight. They throw sand and shells at each other. They make loud noises. Mom and Dad hear them. They come to the sand. They see Anna and Ben fighting. "Stop it!" Mom says. "Why are you fighting?" "Anna hates my bucket," Ben says. "Ben hates my spade," Anna says. Mom and Dad shake their heads. They take Anna and Ben by the hand. "You don't hate each other," Mom says. "You are friends. You can share your toys and play nicely." "Your bucket and your spade are both good," Dad says. "You can use them to make different things. You can learn from each other." Anna and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry. They say sorry. "Sorry, Anna," Ben says. "Sorry, Ben," Anna says. Mom and Dad smile. They give Anna and Ben a hug. "Good," Mom says. "Now, let's go and have some ice cream." Anna and Ben nod. They hold their toys. They follow Mom and Dad. They are friends again.
