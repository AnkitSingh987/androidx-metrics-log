Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Lion

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play games. Today they play a game with cards. The cards have pictures of animals. Tim has a lion card. Sam has a zebra card. "Who will win?" Tim asks. "I will win!" Sam says. "My zebra is fast and smart. Your lion is slow and lazy." They show their cards. Tim's lion has big teeth and claws. Sam's zebra has stripes and hooves. "Roar!" Tim says. "My lion will catch your zebra and eat it. My lion likes meat. Meat is yummy." "Neigh!" Sam says. "My zebra will run away from your lion and hide. My zebra does not like meat. Meat is yucky." They argue and laugh. They have fun. But then Mom comes. She sees the cards. She is not happy. "Boys, what are you doing?" Mom says. "These are not your cards. These are Dad's cards. He collects them. They are rare and expensive. You should not play with them. You could damage them." Tim and Sam are embarrassed. They did not know. They say sorry to Mom. They give back the cards. They hug Mom. Mom is not angry. She loves them. She says they can play with their own cards. They have cards with letters and numbers. They are fun too. Tim and Sam go to their room. They play with their cards. They learn new words and math. They are happy. They are good brothers.

One day, a group of kids went to play in the park. They were all very happy. They played with a ball, and they ran and laughed. A little boy named Tim joined them. He was new and did not understand the game. He asked, "Can you show me how to play?" The other kids said, "Yes, we will help you." While they were playing, the ball hit Tim's arm. It hurt him. He started to cry. The other kids felt bad. They said, "We are sorry, Tim. We did not want to hurt you." Then, a big dog came to the park. The dog picked up the ball and started to play with the kids. It was a fun surprise! Tim stopped crying, and they all played together. The dog made everyone happy, and Tim felt better.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy dog named Spot. Spot loved to shake his tail and play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big tree with a hole in it. He thought, "I wonder what is in there?" Spot walked up to the tree and put his nose in the hole. "Hello!" he said. "Is anyone home?" A small voice came from the hole. "Go away!" it said. "I am very itchy and do not want to play." But Spot did not listen. He wanted to know who was in the hole. Spot put his paw in the hole to find the itchy friend. But the itchy friend did not like that. He bit Spot's paw very hard. Spot cried out and ran away. He learned that sometimes, it is not good to be nosy.

Once upon a time there was a tall girl named Sally. She loved to surf and had a special twist she loved to do. Every morning she went to the beach to ride the waves. One day, Sally's mother asked her why she liked to twist when she surfed. Sally smiled and said, “I twist to make it fun. It's like a dance on the surf." Sally's mother smiled and said, “That sounds very exciting. You're so brave to try that!” From that day on, Sally kept twisting and dancing on the surf. She loved the way it made her feel and was always excited to share her special twist.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the ocean with her mom. The ocean was big and empty. They saw a little fish in the water. Lucy's mom said, "Lucy, I want to introduce you to this fish." "Hi, fish!" said Lucy. The fish did not say anything. It was very quiet. Lucy and her mom played in the water and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The fish started to talk! "Hello, Lucy!" said the fish. Lucy was very surprised. "I did not know fish could talk!" said Lucy. The fish told Lucy and her mom a secret. "I am not just a fish, I am a magic fish!" The fish showed them magic tricks. They had even more fun at the ocean that day.
