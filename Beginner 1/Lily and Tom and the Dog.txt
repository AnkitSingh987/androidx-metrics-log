Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Dog

Lily and Tom were going to church with Mom and Dad. They liked church because they could sing and pray and see their friends. They also liked to pick flowers from the garden outside the church. They always asked Mom and Dad if they could pick some flowers to take home. One day, as they were walking to church, they saw a big dog on the street. The dog was barking and running towards them. Lily and Tom were scared and held Mom and Dad's hands. Mom and Dad told them not to worry, they would keep them safe. "Shoo, shoo, go away!" Mom said to the dog. "You are not nice, you are scaring the children!" The dog stopped and looked at Mom. Then it wagged its tail and licked Mom's hand. Mom smiled and petted the dog. She said the dog was just lonely and wanted to play. "Can we play with the dog, Mom?" Lily asked. "Only if the dog's owner says it's okay," Mom said. "We don't know if the dog is friendly or not." They looked around and saw a man coming towards them. He was holding a leash and a collar. He said he was sorry, he had lost his dog and was looking for him. He thanked Mom and Dad for stopping the dog and said his name was Max. "Can we play with Max, please?" Tom asked the man. "Sure, you can play with Max for a little while," the man said. "He is very gentle and loves children. But don't pick his fur, he doesn't like that." Lily and Tom nodded and played with Max. They threw a ball and Max fetched it. They rubbed his belly and Max wagged his tail. They had fun with Max and forgot they were scared. "Okay, kids, it's time to go to church," Dad said. "We don't want to be late." "Bye, Max, we'll see you again," Lily and Tom said. They gave Max a hug and a kiss. "Bye, kids, thank you for playing with Max," the man said. He put the leash and the collar on Max and waved goodbye. Lily and Tom went to church with Mom and Dad. They sang and prayed and saw their friends. They also picked some flowers from the garden outside the church. They gave some to Mom and Dad and some to Max's owner. They were happy and safe.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the yard. Sometimes they fight over toys. Mom does not like it when they fight. She tells them to share and be nice. One day, Mom makes a meal for them. She puts it on the table. She calls them to come and eat. Tom and Sam run to the table. They see two plates of food. One has a sandwich and one has a salad. Tom likes sandwiches. Sam likes salads. But they do not know which plate is for whom. They both want the sandwich. They start to fight over it. They pull and tug and yell. "Stop it!" Mom says. "You are being silly. You can both have what you want. I have a hidden plate for each of you. Look under the tablecloth." Tom and Sam look under the tablecloth. They see two more plates of food. One has a sandwich and one has a salad. They are happy. They say sorry to each other. They thank Mom for the meal. They sit down and eat. They do not fight anymore. They share and are nice.

One day, a red dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a sign on it. Max could not read, but he knew the sign was important. He sat under the tree, thinking about what the sign said. A little boy named Tim saw Max and walked over. "Hi, Max! I can read the sign for you," Tim said. Max wagged his tail, happy that Tim could help. Tim read the sign out loud: "Do not sit under this tree. Bees live here." Max and Tim looked up and saw a big bee nest in the tree. Max and Tim ran away from the tree as fast as they could. They did not want the bees to sting their skin. They found a safe spot to rest and play. Max was happy that Tim read the sign, and they both learned to be careful around bees.
