Category: Beginner
Title: The Group of Ducks and the Fish

One day, a group of ducks went to the pond to swim and play. They saw a big fish in the water and wanted to make friends with it. They quacked hello and asked the fish to join them. But the fish was not nice. It said, "Go away, you silly ducks. You are too small and slow for me. I only like big and fast fish like me. You are terrible and boring." The ducks felt sad and hurt by the fish's words. They did not want to swim and play anymore. They lay on the grass and cried. They wished they had a better friend. Then, they heard a soft voice say, "Hello, ducks. I am a turtle. I live in the pond too. I heard what the fish said to you. He was very mean and rude. You are not terrible and boring. You are lovely and fun. I like you. Can I be your friend?" The ducks looked up and saw the turtle smiling at them. They felt happy and surprised. They wiped their tears and said, "Yes, turtle. You can be our friend. You are very kind and sweet. We like you too. Come and swim and play with us." The turtle nodded and followed the ducks to the water. They swam and played together and had a lot of fun. They forgot about the fish and his words. They were glad they found a new friend. They learned that being nice is better than being big and fast.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. They also like to drink milk from their bottles. They have different bottles. Lily's bottle is pink and has a bunny on it. Ben's bottle is blue and has a car on it. One day, they are playing in their room. Lily sees Ben's bottle on the floor. She thinks it is adorable. She wants to try it. She picks it up and puts it in her mouth. She drinks some milk. Ben sees Lily with his bottle. He is angry. He wants his bottle back. He runs to Lily and shouts. "Hey, that is my bottle! Give it back!" Lily is surprised. She does not want to give it back. She likes the car on it. She shakes her head and says. "No, it is my bottle now! It is adorable!" Ben and Lily start to tug on the bottle. They pull and pull. They are both strong. The bottle is not. It breaks. Milk spills everywhere. Lily and Ben are wet and sad. They cry. Mom hears them cry. She comes to their room. She sees the mess. She is not happy. She picks up Lily and Ben. She hugs them and says. "What happened here? Why did you break the bottle?" Lily and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry. They say. "We are sorry, Mom. We both wanted the bottle. It was adorable." Mom smiles. She says. "I know, sweeties. But you have to share and be nice to each other. You are twins. You are adorable too." She takes them to the bathroom. She cleans them up. She gives them new bottles. She says. "Here you go. These are your bottles. Lily, this is your pink bunny bottle. Ben, this is your blue car bottle. They are both adorable. Just like you." Lily and Ben smile. They hug Mom and say. "Thank you, Mom. We love you. We love our bottles. We love each other." They drink their milk. They are happy. They are adorable.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to swing in his yard. He would swing high and low, and feel like he could touch the sky. One day, while he was swinging, he saw a jar far away. It was in a distant place that he had never been before. Tim got off the swing and walked to the jar. He picked it up and saw that it was full of pretty stones. Tim was very happy and wanted to show his mom. But as he turned around, he saw that his swing was gone! Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, he heard a small voice coming from the jar. It was a tiny bug! The bug said, "I took your swing, but I will give it back if you let me keep the pretty stones." Tim agreed, and the bug gave him back his swing. Tim learned that sometimes, you have to give something to get something back.
