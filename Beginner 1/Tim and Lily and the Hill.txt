Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Hill

Tim and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big hill with many rocks. They wanted to climb the hill and see what was on the other side. "Come on, Lily, let's go up the hill!" Tim said. "OK, Tim, but be careful. The rocks are hard and sharp," Lily said. They started to climb the hill. They used their hands and feet to hold on to the rocks. They were having fun. They saw birds and flowers and bugs. "Look, Tim, I found a regular rock. It is round and smooth and gray. Do you want it?" Lily said. She held out the rock to Tim. Tim looked at the rock. He liked it. He reached out to take it. But then he slipped. He lost his grip on the rocks. He started to fall down the hill. "Tim, stop!" Lily shouted. She grabbed his hand. She pulled him up. She hugged him. "Are you OK, Tim?" Lily asked. Tim was scared. He was hurt. He had scratches on his arms and legs. He had tears in his eyes. "I'm OK, Lily. Thank you for saving me. You are a good friend," Tim said. He smiled. He took the regular rock from Lily. He put it in his pocket. "Let's go down the hill, Lily. I don't want to climb anymore. I want to play on the slide," Tim said. "OK, Tim. Me too. The slide is more fun and safe," Lily said. They went down the hill. They played on the slide. They were happy. They still had the regular rock. They shared it. It was their special rock.

Once upon a time, there was a clever boy named Tim. He loved to learn new things. One day, his mom gave him a big clock. It had many numbers and two hands. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to choose where to put the clock. He asked his mom, "Mom, where should I put the clock?" His mom said, "You can put it on the wall in your room, Tim." So, Tim put the clock on the wall. Every day, Tim looked at the clock. He learned when it was time to eat, play, and sleep. Tim's friends saw the clock and thought it was cool. They all wanted to learn how to tell time too. Tim was a clever boy who helped his friends learn something new.

Once there was a large family that loved to joke. They had many children and all of them were very happy. One day they decided to go out. As they were walking down the street, they heard a lot of noise. It was traffic! All the cars and bikes on the street made a lot of noise. The family began to joke about the traffic. All the children thought it was very funny. Soon they were all laughing, even the grown-ups! They had a great time and soon they were back home, safe and sound.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Ben. He was only three years old. One day, he was at the bus stop with his mum. Ben saw a big, yellow bus coming down the road. He got very excited and wanted to get on the bus with his mum. But just then, a big, scary dog ran up to them and barked really loudly. Ben was very scared and started to cry. His mum told him not to worry because it was just being honest and it was only saying hello. After the dog ran away, Ben felt brave again. He and his mum got on the bus and they went on a wonderful adventure. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his best friend, a big dog named Max. Max always tried to support Tim and help him be less shy. One day, Max and Tim went to the park to play. Max said, "Let's play catch! I'll pass the ball to you, and you can pass it back to me." Tim was scared, but Max told him, "Don't worry, I'll support you." They started to play, and Tim was having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky and took the ball away! Max and Tim were both surprised. Tim looked at Max and said, "Let's go get our ball back!" Max was proud of Tim for being brave. Together, they chased the bird and got their ball back. From that day on, Tim was not as shy as before, all thanks to Max's support.
