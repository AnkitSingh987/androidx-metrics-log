Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day in the pond. One day, Fin saw a small fish crying. The small fish was named Sam. Sam was sad because he could not swim fast like the other fish. Fin wanted to help Sam. He had an idea. Fin told Sam to split his swim into two parts. First, Sam should swim slow and then swim fast. Sam thought it was a good idea. Fin and Sam practiced swimming together. The next day, all the fish in the pond had a swim race. Sam remembered what Fin told him. He split his swim into two parts. First, Sam swam slow and then he swam fast. Sam won the race! Sam was glad and thanked Fin for helping him. Now, Sam could swim fast like the other fish.

Sara and Ben were playing in the sand. They had a bucket and a shovel each. They made a big castle with towers and walls. They were very happy. Then a big boy came. He was mean. He kicked their castle and laughed. He took Sara's bucket and threw it far away. Sara and Ben were sad and angry. "Hey, that's not nice!" Ben shouted. "Give us back our bucket!" The big boy did not listen. He ran away with the bucket. Sara and Ben chased him. They wanted their bucket back. They saw a man with a dog. The dog was big and black, but harmless. He liked to play. He saw the bucket flying in the air. He jumped and caught it in his mouth. He ran to the man with the bucket. "Good boy, Rex!" the man said. He saw Sara and Ben running after the big boy. He understood what happened. He gave the bucket to Sara and Ben. He scolded the big boy. "Sorry, kids. He's a bully. He should not throw your bucket. Here, you can have it back. And take this, too." He gave them a bag of cookies. "You are brave and kind. You deserve a treat." Sara and Ben thanked the man and the dog. They hugged Rex. He licked their faces. They smiled. They went back to the sand. They rebuilt their castle. They shared the cookies. They were very happy.

One day, a person wanted to climb a big hill. It was a difficult hill, but the person was strong and brave. They put on their shoes and started to climb. As the person climbed, they saw many fun things. They saw a dog playing with a ball, a bird singing in a tree, and a funny bug. The person smiled and kept climbing the difficult hill. At the top of the hill, the person saw a beautiful view. They saw the sun shining, the trees dancing, and the river flowing. The person was happy they climbed the difficult hill. They sat down, ate a yummy snack, and enjoyed the view.

Lily and Tom are playing in the garden. They see a big sheet on the clothesline. It is white and soft. "Let's play with the sheet!" Lily says. "We can make a tent or a cape or a ghost!" Tom nods. He likes to play with Lily. They run to the sheet and pull it off the clothesline. The sheet falls to the ground. "Oh no!" Lily says. "We made a mess! Mom will be angry!" Tom looks around. He sees a tree with a low branch. He has an idea. "Look, Lily!" he says. "We can hang the sheet on the tree! Then it will dry and Mom will not know!" Lily smiles. She thinks Tom is clever. They pick up the sheet and carry it to the tree. They throw one end of the sheet over the branch. They tie a knot to make it stay. "There!" Tom says. "We made a swing! We can sit on the sheet and swing back and forth!" Lily claps. She likes swings. She sits on the sheet and holds the other end. Tom pushes her gently. She feels the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. "Whee!" she says. "This is fun! Thank you, Tom!" Tom smiles. He is happy to make Lily happy. He waits for his turn to swing on the sheet. He hopes Mom will not mind. He thinks Mom will like the swing too.
