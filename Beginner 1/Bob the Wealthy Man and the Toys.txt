Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Wealthy Man and the Toys

Once, there was a wealthy man named Bob. He had a big house and many toys. One day, Bob wanted to eat his favorite food, so he went to the store. At the store, he saw a little girl named Sally. Sally was looking for sauce to put on her food. Bob asked Sally, "Do you need help?" Sally said, "Yes, I want to find the sauce for my food, but I can't see it." Bob was tall, so he could see the sauce high up on a shelf. He pointed to the sauce and said, "Look, there it is!" Sally looked up and saw the sauce. She was very happy. Bob helped Sally get the sauce. They became good friends and had a fun day at the store. From that day on, they always helped each other find things they needed. And they always enjoyed their food with lots of yummy sauce.

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the room. He saw a big shelf with toys on it. Spot wanted to get a toy from the shelf, but he was too small. He felt weak and sad. Spot's friend, a cat named Whiskers, came into the room. "What's wrong, Spot?" asked Whiskers. Spot said, "I want a toy from the shelf, but I am too small and weak." Whiskers thought for a moment and said, "Don't panic, Spot. I can help you get a toy." Whiskers climbed up the shelf and pushed a toy off for Spot. The toy fell and broke. Spot and Whiskers both panicked. They knew they had to fix the toy before their owner found out. They worked together and fixed the toy just in time. Spot and Whiskers learned that they could do anything if they helped each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a paper on it. Tim noticed the paper had a print of a cat. "Look, Mom! A cat!" said Tim. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, that's a pretty cat print." As they walked, they met a girl named Sue. Sue was very generous and shared her toys with Tim. They played together and had lots of fun. "Thank you for sharing your toys," said Tim. Sue replied, "You're welcome! I like to share." After playing, they saw a man with many cat prints. Tim and Sue loved the prints. The man was generous too and gave them each a cat print. They both said, "Thank you!" and went home with big smiles on their faces.

Tom and Lily like to play on the swings in the park. They swing high and low, and laugh and sing. One day, they see a new thing in the park. It is a big round thing with seats and bars. It can spin very fast. "Let's try it!" says Tom. He runs to the thing and sits on a seat. He holds a bar with his hand. Lily follows him and sits next to him. She holds a bar too. A man comes and pushes a button. The thing starts to spin. Tom and Lily feel the wind in their hair and the sun on their face. They smile and shout. They like to spin. But then, the thing spins faster and faster. Tom and Lily feel dizzy and sick. They want to stop. They look at the man, but he does not see them. He talks to another man. Tom and Lily are nervous. They do not like to spin anymore. "Help!" they cry. "Stop the thing! We want to get off!" The man hears them and looks at them. He sees their face. They are pale and scared. He pushes the button again. The thing slows down and stops. Tom and Lily jump off the thing and run to the grass. They lie down and close their eyes. They feel better. "Are you okay?" the man asks. He is sorry. He did not know they were nervous. "Yes, we are okay," Tom and Lily say. "But we do not want to spin again. We like the swings better." They go back to the swings and swing gently. They do not spin. They are happy.
