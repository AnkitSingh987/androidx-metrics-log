Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Ball

One day, a little dog named Max went out to play. He saw a big red ball in the park. Max was very happy and wanted to play with the ball. He ran fast and tried to grab the ball with his mouth. But the ball was too big for Max. A naughty cat named Tom saw Max trying to grab the ball. Tom wanted to play too, so he jumped on the ball and pushed it away from Max. Max was sad because he could not play with the ball. Tom laughed and ran away. Max did not give up. He tried again and again to grab the ball. Finally, Max was able to grab the ball with his mouth. He was very happy and proud. Max played with the ball all day and had lots of fun.

Ben likes to write stories with his crayons. He has a big book with many white pages. He writes about his friends, his toys, and his adventures. He also draws pictures to make his stories more fun. One day, he writes a story about a dog. He loves dogs, but he does not have one. He writes that the dog is brown and fluffy, and that he likes to play fetch and cuddle. He writes that the dog is his best friend, and that they go everywhere together. He draws a picture of the dog next to him, with a big smile. He shows his story to his mom. She reads it and says, "This is a wonderful story, Ben. You are very creative and smart. I can see that you really want a dog." Ben nods and says, "Yes, mom. I want a dog very much. Can we get one, please?" Mom thinks for a moment and says, "Well, Ben, dogs are a lot of work. They need food, water, exercise, and love. They also make noise and mess. Are you ready to take care of a dog?" Ben says, "Yes, mom. I will feed him, and walk him, and play with him, and love him. I will also clean up after him, and be quiet when he sleeps. Please, mom, can we get a dog?" Mom smiles and says, "OK, Ben. I think you are ready for a dog. Let's go to the shelter and see if we can find a good match for you." Ben is very happy and hugs his mom. He says, "Thank you, mom. You are the best. I can't wait to meet my new friend." They go to the shelter and see many dogs. Some are big, some are small, some are loud, some are quiet. Ben looks at each one and tries to find the one that matches his story. He sees a brown and fluffy dog in a corner. He goes closer and sees that the dog is shy and sad. He says, "Hello, dog. What is your name?" The dog looks at Ben and wags his tail. He says, "Woof, woof. My name is Buddy. I have been here for a long time. No one wants me. I am lonely and scared." Ben feels sorry for Buddy. He says, "Don't be sad, Buddy. I want you. You are just like the dog in my story. You are brown and fluffy, and you like to play fetch and cuddle. You are my best friend. Can I take you home?" Buddy barks happily and licks Ben's face. He says, "Yes, yes, yes. You can take me home. You are my best friend, too. You are kind and gentle. You make me feel comfortable and safe." Ben and Buddy hug each other. Mom sees them and says, "I think you found your match, Ben. Buddy is a sweet and loyal dog. He needs a good home and a good family. Are you sure you want him?" Ben says, "Yes, mom. I want him more than anything. He is my dog, and I am his boy. We belong together." Mom says, "OK, then. Let's fill out the papers and take him home. I am happy for you, Ben. You made a good choice." Ben, Buddy, and mom leave the shelter. They go to their car and drive home. Ben and Buddy sit in the back seat and cuddle. They are both very happy. They have each other. They have a new story to write.
