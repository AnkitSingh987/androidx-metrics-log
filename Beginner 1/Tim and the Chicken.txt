Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Chicken

Tim likes to jog with his dad in the park. They jog fast and slow, and see many things. They see dogs, birds, flowers, and people. Tim likes to jog, but he also likes to eat. One day, they jog past a farm. Tim sees a chicken in a pen. The chicken is brown and fluffy, and it makes a funny sound. Tim thinks the chicken looks yummy. He wants to eat the chicken for lunch. He tells his dad, "Dad, can we have chicken for lunch? I see a chicken there. It looks yummy." His dad says, "No, Tim, we can't have that chicken. That chicken belongs to the farmer. He takes care of the chicken and gives it food and water. We can't take his chicken. That would be wrong." Tim feels sad and angry. He doesn't understand why he can't have the chicken. He thinks his dad is mean. He says, "But I want the chicken! I'm hungry! Please, dad, please!" His dad says, "Tim, stop. You have to be polite and respectful. You can't always have what you want. We have other food at home. We can have sandwiches or soup or salad. They are yummy too. Come on, let's jog some more. Maybe we'll see something else that makes you happy." Tim pouts and whines, but he follows his dad. He still wants the chicken, but he knows he can't have it. He hopes they will jog past something else that is yummy and fun. He hopes his dad will love him and hug him. He hopes he will have a good lunch.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little girl named Mia. One day, Mia went out to play with her friends, a squirrel named Tim and a bunny named Lily. They loved to play on the green pitch near their homes. The pitch was a big open space where they could run, jump, and have fun. As they played, they saw a big scary shadow on the ground. They stopped and looked up at the sky. A big, scary bird was flying over them. They were all scared and ran to hide under a big tree. They started to discuss what to do next. Tim, the squirrel, said, "We must be brave and not let the scary bird scare us away from our pitch." Lily, the bunny, agreed. They decided to ask their other friends in the forest to help them chase the scary bird away. Together, they all stood up to the scary bird, and it flew away. Mia, Tim, and Lily went back to their pitch and continued playing happily.

Jill saw a daisy in the garden. She wanted to pick it. It looked easy so she tried. But she couldn't reach it. She wanted the daisy. So she got a ladder from the garage and climbed up. She easily picked the daisy and carefully brought it inside. Then she went to get a vase. She filled it with water and put the daisy inside. She smiled as she watched it grow. One day, Jill wanted to know if it was possible to make the daisy dry. She struggled and tried to figure out a way. She finally did it. She opened the window to let the sun come in. Then she set the daisy outside and watched it slowly dry. Jill was proud of herself. She had done it. She had made the daisy dry.

Tim and Tom are twins. They like to play with cars and trucks. They have many cars and trucks in their room. But they have only one tie. The tie is red and blue and has cars on it. Tim and Tom both want to wear the tie. One day, they have a big fight. They pull the tie and yell at each other. "Give me the tie! It is mine!" Tim says. "No, it is mine! I saw it first!" Tom says. They tug and tug until the tie rips. They both fall on the floor and cry. Mom hears the noise and comes to their room. She sees the broken tie and the sad twins. "What happened here?" Mom asks. "We broke the tie. We both wanted it. It was cool." Tim says. "We are sorry, Mom. We love the tie. And we love each other." Tom says. Mom hugs them and comforts them. "It's okay, boys. I know you love the tie and each other. But you have to share and be kind. Fighting is not good. How about we fix the tie together? And then you can take turns wearing it." Mom says. Tim and Tom nod and smile. "Thank you, Mom. You are the best." They say. Mom gets a needle and a thread and sews the tie. She makes it look like new. She gives it to Tim and Tom. "Here you go, boys. Now you have a cool tie again. But remember, it is not as important as your love for each other." Mom says. Tim and Tom hug Mom and thank her. They hug each other and say sorry. They decide to wear the tie together. They put it around their necks and make a bow. They look at the mirror and laugh. "We are cool twins!" They say.
