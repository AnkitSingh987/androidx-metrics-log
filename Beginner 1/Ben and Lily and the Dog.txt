Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Dog

Ben and Lily are playing with cards. They have many cards with animals on them. They want to find cards that match. They put the cards face down on the floor and take turns to flip two cards. "Look, I found a match!" Ben says. He flips two cards with a dog on them. He puts them in his hand and smiles. "Good job, Ben!" Lily says. She flips two cards with a cat on them. She puts them in her hand and claps. They keep playing until there are no more cards on the floor. They count how many matches they have in their hands. "I have six matches!" Ben says. "I have five matches!" Lily says. "Wow, you are both very good at this game!" Mom says. She comes to see what they are doing. She sees a card with a bird on the floor. It is the only card left. "Who has the other card with a bird?" Mom asks. Ben and Lily look at their hands. They do not have the card with a bird. "Uh-oh, we are missing a card!" Ben says. "Maybe it is under the sofa?" Lily says. They look under the sofa. They do not see the card. "Maybe it is in the trash?" Ben says. They look in the trash. They do not see the card. "Maybe it is in the poor dog's mouth?" Lily says. They look at the dog. The dog is chewing on something. It is the card with the bird! "Bad dog, bad dog!" Ben and Lily say. They try to get the card from the dog. "Be careful, kids!" Mom says. She helps them get the card from the dog. The card is wet and torn. "Sorry, kids, this card is ruined. You can't match it anymore." Mom says. "That's okay, Mom. We still had fun." Ben says. "Yeah, we still had fun." Lily says. They hug Mom and the dog. They put the cards away and play something else.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She lived with her parents in a white house on the corner of the street. One day, Sue heard a loud knocking on the door. She ran to open it, and saw a big box. The box said “Deliver to Sue”. Her parents smiled and said “It must be for you!” Sue opened the box and inside was a big chest. Sue was so excited. She asked her parents “What is it?” They said “It’s a chest of helpful things for you. It will help you in many ways!” Sue thanked them and hugged her chest. She knew that it was full of helpful things, and she would use them every day.

Once upon a time there was a ship in the sea. It was filled with fun toys. One day the captain said they had to mix all the toys together. He said it was very important. Everyone was so busy mixing. But then something sad happened. All the toys were broken. The captain was so sorry. He said he was sorry for ruining the fun. Then he called to all the shipmates to help fix the toys. So everyone got to work. They put the pieces together and worked hard. They mixed and matched their colors and shapes and soon the toys were like new. Everyone was so happy and excited. The shipmates danced and sang with joy. They were so glad they were able to fix the toys. The captain was so proud of them all. He said they did a wonderful job.

Once upon a time, there was a little purple fish named Bob. Bob lived in a big ocean with his family. He loved to swim and play with his friends all day long. One day, Bob saw a shiny thing up high. He wanted to reach it, but it was too high for him. He tried to jump and jump, but he could not touch it. Bob was sad and asked his friends for help. His friends, a crab and a starfish, tried to help Bob reach the shiny thing. They made a tall pile with rocks and sand. Bob climbed up and finally could touch the shiny thing. It was a beautiful pearl! Bob was so happy and thanked his friends. They all played together, and the ocean was a happy place again.
