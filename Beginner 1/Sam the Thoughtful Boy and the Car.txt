Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Thoughtful Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Sam. Sam liked to visit the temple with his mom. The temple was big and pretty. Sam loved to look at the colorful pictures on the walls. One day, Sam found a toy car near the temple door. He picked it up and said, "Mom, look! I found a toy!" His mom said, "Sam, we should return the toy to the person who lost it. That is the right thing to do." Sam and his mom asked people at the temple if they lost a toy car. Finally, they found a little girl who was sad because she lost her toy. Sam gave the toy car back to the girl. The girl was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam felt good about returning the toy. He learned that being thoughtful and helping others is important. From that day on, Sam always tried to be kind and helpful to everyone he met.

Tom and Jen moved to a new house with Mom and Dad. They had many boxes to unpack. Tom and Jen wanted to help. They looked for their toys and books. They found a big box in the corner of their room. It had a label that said "Tom and Jen's stuff". They opened the box and saw many things they liked. They saw dolls, cars, puzzles, and crayons. "Look, Jen, our old teddy bears!" Tom said. He took out two brown bears with red bows. He gave one to Jen and hugged the other. "I missed them so much!" "Me too, Tom!" Jen said. She hugged her bear and smiled. "Let's put them on our beds. They will be happy here." They carried their bears to their beds. They saw something on the wall. It was gray and dusty and had many thin strings. It looked like a spider's home. "Yuck, what is that?" Tom asked. He pointed at the wall. "That is a cobweb," Jen said. She remembered what Mom told her. "It means a spider lived here before. But it is gone now. We can clean it with a cloth." "Let's do that," Tom said. He found a cloth in the box. He climbed on his bed and reached for the cobweb. He wiped it off the wall. It was easy. "Good job, Tom!" Jen said. She climbed on her bed and did the same. She wiped off another cobweb. It was easy too. "Thank you, Jen!" Tom said. They looked at their clean wall. They felt proud. They put their bears on their pillows. They looked at each other and smiled. "We did it, Jen!" Tom said. "We did it, Tom!" Jen said. They hugged their bears and each other. They liked their new room. They liked their new house. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a seal named Sam. Sam liked to feel the water on his skin when he swam in the sea. He had many friends who also liked to swim and play in the water. One day, Sam and his friends decided to have a race. They were all very competitive and wanted to see who was the fastest swimmer. They lined up at the start and waited for the signal to begin. When they heard the signal, they all swam as fast as they could. Sam tried his best and swam very fast. In the end, Sam won the race and all his friends clapped. They all had fun and felt happy playing together.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy wanted to go outside and play in the snow. She asked her mom, "Can I go play in the snow?" Her mom replied, "Maybe later, it's too cold now." So Lucy stayed inside and played games inside, but she got bored. She thought to herself, "It would be so much fun to play in the snow!" Soon it was warmer outside and the snow began to melt. Lucy asked her mom again, "Can I go playing in the snow now?" Her mom said, "Yes, but don't stay out too long. It's still cold." So Lucy got ready and went out to play in the snow. She made snow angels, had a snowball fight and built snowmen. At last, Lucy was cold and tired, so she returned home. She was glad to be back, but still excited to return outside and play in the snow again.
