Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Swings

One day, a happy boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big sack on the ground. He was very curious. He opened the sack and found some dough inside. Tim thought it was fun to play with the dough. Tim made a dough friend and named him Buddy. They played together in the park. They went on the swings and down the slide. Buddy was a good friend, and they both had a lot of fun. A little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tim and Buddy playing. Sue asked, "Can I play with you and your dough friend?" Tim said, "Yes, let's all play together!" They all played and laughed. Tim, Sue, and Buddy were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a tall lamp. The tall lamp liked to help people see in the dark. It loved to work at night when the sun went to sleep. The tall lamp had a friend named Tim. One day, Tim came home from playing outside. It was getting dark and hard to see. Tim said, "Tall lamp, will you work for me? I want to read my book, but it's too dark." The tall lamp was happy to help. It turned on its light and made the room bright. Tim and the tall lamp read books together every night. They liked to have fun and learn new things. They were the best of friends, and the tall lamp always worked hard to help Tim see.

Once upon a time, there was a green rocket. It lived in a big forest with many trees. The green rocket had a lot of friends. They liked to play together every day. One day, the green rocket and its friends wanted to make a big cake. They all brought different things to mix. The green rocket brought green leaves. The bird brought soft berries. The rabbit brought sweet carrots. They all mixed their things together in a big bowl. The green rocket and its friends were very happy with their big cake. They all sat down and ate it together. The green rocket said, "This is the best cake ever!" All the friends laughed and played, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Sue went to her mom's kitchen. She saw a big, smooth box. It was the oven. Sue asked her mom, "Mom, what is this big box?" Her mom said, "That is the oven, Sue. We cook food in it." One night, Sue saw the oven glow. She was scared. She ran to her mom and said, "Mom, the oven is glowing!" Her mom laughed and said, "Sue, that is just the oven light. It helps us see the food inside." Sue wanted to see the food too. She opened the oven door, but a big cake popped out! Sue and her mom were very surprised. They laughed and enjoyed the cake. The moral of the story is to always ask for help and not be scared of new things.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog. He saw his friend, Sam, by a big tree. Sam looked sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, friend?" Sam said, "My pet fish is dead." Tim felt sad for his friend. They both sat under the tree, thinking of what to do. As they sat, they saw a big frog jump by. The frog said, "I can help your fish." Tim and Sam looked at each other, surprised that the frog could talk! The frog jumped up and down, and then the fish started to move! The fish was not dead! Tim and Sam were so happy. They thanked the frog and played with their fish friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and make funny faces. One hot day, Sue found a pretty stamp in her room. The stamp had a picture of a happy sun on it. Sue wanted to show the stamp to her mom, but her mom was not home. Sue felt sad and didn't know what to do. She sat down and started to pray. She prayed for her mom to come home soon so she could show her the pretty stamp. As Sue prayed, she heard the door open. Her mom was home! Sue ran to her mom and showed her the pretty stamp. Her mom smiled and hugged Sue. They both felt happy and loved. From that day on, Sue knew that praying could help her when she was feeling sad or alone.
