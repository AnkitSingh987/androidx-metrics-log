Category: Beginner
Title: Rex the Once Dog and the Park

There was once a dog named Rex. He liked to play in the park and explore. One day, he heard an interesting sound. He couldn't understand what it was, but he wanted to find out. He began to follow the sound. The sound led Rex to a strange place. It was full of bright lights and some people in white coats. It was a hospital! Rex didn't understand why he was there. One of the people in white coats pointed to a machine and said "Rex, this is an x-ray." The machine was very big and had lots of buttons. Rex was a bit scared, but he was brave and stayed still. The machine made a big noise and he felt a little rough, but it was all over soon. When it was finished, the people said "Rex looks healthy!" He was so happy and relieved. He thanked the people and went home. From that day on, whenever Rex heard the sound of an x-ray machine, he felt brave and knew he was safe.

Sara liked to help her mom in the kitchen. She put on a pink apron and stood on a stool. She wanted to stir the soup with a big spoon. "Can I taste it, mom?" Sara asked. "Not yet, sweetie. It's too hot. You have to wait until it cools down," mom said. Sara was frustrated. She was hungry and curious. She wanted to know how the soup tasted. She saw some carrots and potatoes in the pot. She liked those. She reached for the spoon again. But mom stopped her. "Sara, no. You have to stay away from the stove. It's dangerous. You could burn yourself or spill the soup. Please listen to me," mom said. Sara pouted. She didn't like to stay away. She wanted to help and have fun. She felt bored and sad. She looked around the kitchen. She saw a bowl of flour on the counter. She had an idea. She climbed down from the stool. She took the bowl of flour and poured it on the floor. She made a big mess. She smiled. She thought it was funny. She started to make shapes with the flour. She made a star, a heart, and a cat. She was happy. She forgot about the soup. But mom was not happy. She saw the flour on the floor. She was shocked and angry. "Sara, what did you do? Look at this mess! How could you?" mom yelled. Sara was scared. She saw mom's face. She knew she was in trouble. She ran to mom and hugged her. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I was frustrated. I wanted to help you. I wanted to taste the soup. I didn't mean to make a mess." Mom sighed. She was still angry, but she loved Sara. She hugged her back. She said, "I know, sweetie. I know you wanted to help. But you have to stay away from the stove and the flour. They are not toys. They are for cooking. You have to be careful and follow the rules. Do you understand?" Sara nodded. She said, "I understand, mom. I'm sorry. I love you." Mom said, "I love you too, Sara. But you have to help me clean up this mess. And then you can taste the soup. OK?" Sara said, "OK, mom. Thank you." They smiled. They took off their aprons and got a broom and a dustpan. They cleaned up the flour together. They talked and laughed. They finished cleaning. They went back to the stove. The soup was ready. It smelled good. They served themselves some soup in bowls. They sat at the table. They tasted the soup. They liked it. It was yummy. They said, "Mmm, this is good soup, mom." They said, "Mmm, this is good soup, Sara." They were happy. They ate their soup and enjoyed their time together.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, were playing in the hot sun. They were very thirsty. They walked and walked until they saw a nice place with water and trees. This place was called an oasis. At the oasis, they saw a man with a big truck. The man was loading the truck with water. Tim said, "Hi! Can we have some water? We are very thirsty." The man said, "Yes, you can have some water. But first, help me load the truck." As they were loading the truck, a big wind came. The wind blew and blew, and made the water in the oasis go away. Tim, Max, and the man were sad. They had no water now. The man said, "This is bad. We need to find more water." Just then, a big bird came down from the sky. The bird had a long beak and big wings. The bird said, "I know where there is more water. Follow me!" Tim, Max, and the man followed the bird to a new oasis. They found water and were happy again.
