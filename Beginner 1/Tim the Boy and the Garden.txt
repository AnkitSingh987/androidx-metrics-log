Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like to eat potatoes. He would say "I hate potatoes!" every time his mom tried to give him some. His mom was sad because potatoes are good for you. One day, Tim was playing in the yard. He found a dirty potato in the ground. It was brown and covered in dirt. Tim thought it was a funny-looking potato. He decided to take it home and show his mom. Tim showed the dirty potato to his mom and said, "Look what I found, Mom!" His mom smiled and said, "That's a potato from our garden!" Tim was surprised. He asked his mom if they could clean the potato and eat it for dinner. That night, Tim tried the potato and he liked it. He didn't hate potatoes anymore.

One day, a clumsy cat named Tom was walking in the park. He saw a small girl with a brush in her hand. The girl was trying to brush her doll's hair, but it was not easy for her. Tom went to the girl and said, "Can I help you brush your doll's hair?" The girl smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Tom took the brush from the girl and started to brush the doll's hair. But Tom was so clumsy that he got the brush stuck in the doll's hair. The girl started to complain, "Oh no, you made it worse!" Just then, a friendly dog named Spot came running up to them. Spot saw the problem and said, "I can help!" With his teeth, Spot gently took the brush out of the doll's hair. The girl was so happy and said, "Thank you, Spot!" Tom felt a little sad because he couldn't help. But the girl gave Tom a big hug and said, "It's okay, you tried your best!" And from that day on, Tom, the girl, and Spot became the best of friends.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their dog, Max. Max was a big, hairy dog who liked to run and jump. Lily and Ben loved Max very much and gave him hugs and treats. "Look, Max, a squirrel!" Ben said, pointing to a small, brown animal on a tree. Max barked and ran after the squirrel. He wanted to catch it and play with it. "Max, come back!" Lily called. She was afraid that Max would get lost or hurt. She ran after him, but he was too fast. He followed the squirrel to the other side of the park, where there was a fence and a road. Lily and Ben reached the fence and saw Max on the road. There was a big, loud car coming. The car honked and screeched. Max was scared and ran back to the fence. But he was too late. The car hit him and he fell down. "Max!" Lily and Ben cried. They ran to him and hugged him. He was bleeding and whimpering. He looked at them with sad eyes. "I'm sorry, Max," Ben said. "I didn't mean to make you chase the squirrel. I love you." Lily kissed Max's head. "We love you, Max. You're a good dog. Please be okay." A lady came out of the car. She was sorry too. She said she didn't see Max. She said she would help them. She called a vet on her phone. The vet came and put Max on a stretcher. He said he would take Max to the hospital and make him better. "Can we go with Max?" Lily asked. "Of course," the vet said. "He needs you. You're his family." Lily and Ben got in the vet's car with Max. They held his paws and talked to him. They told him he was brave and strong. They told him he would be safe and happy again. They told him they would never let him bite a squirrel again.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Jack and Jill. They lived in a cozy house with their Mom. One cold day Mom said, “Let’s go and visit the icy lake. You can touch the cold water.” Jack and Jill put on their warm coats and boots, then hopped into the big car. On the way to the lake, Mom said, “The lake will be so icy and cold. But don’t worry, you’ll stay warm in the car.” When they got to the lake, Jack and Jill looked out of the window. They saw iced-over water, and a mist that was so cold. Jack said, “Can we touch the icy water?” Mom said, “No, it’s too cold and too dangerous. Let’s just enjoy looking at it from the car.” So Jack and Jill watched from the car, and marveled at how pretty and icy the lake was. They enjoyed their visit so much, they even asked to go back the next day.
