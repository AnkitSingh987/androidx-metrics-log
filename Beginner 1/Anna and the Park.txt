Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Park

Anna likes to play with her dolls. She has many dolls, but her favorite is Lily. Lily has long hair, a pink dress and shiny shoes. Anna likes to brush Lily's hair and make her talk. One day, Anna wants to play with Lily, but she cannot find her. She looks under her bed, in her closet, and in her toy box. She asks her mom, "Mom, where is Lily? I want to play with her." Mom says, "I don't know, Anna. Maybe you left her somewhere else. Did you take her to the park yesterday?" Anna thinks hard. She remembers that she took Lily to the park with her dad. She had fun on the swings, the slide and the sandbox. She says, "Yes, I took Lily to the park. Maybe I forgot her there." Mom says, "Oh, no, Anna. That's not good. Maybe someone else took her. We have to go to the park and look for her." Anna feels sad and frustrated. She loves Lily and does not want to lose her. She says, "OK, Mom. Let's go to the park. Hurry, hurry!" Mom and Anna get in the car and drive to the park. They look around, but they do not see Lily. Anna starts to cry. She says, "Lily, where are you? Please come back to me." Then, Anna sees something shiny near the sandbox. She runs to it and picks it up. It is Lily's shoe. She says, "Mom, look! I found Lily's shoe. She must be here somewhere." Mom says, "Good job, Anna. Let's keep looking. Maybe she is hiding." Anna and Mom look behind the trees, under the benches, and in the trash cans. They do not find Lily. Anna feels more sad and frustrated. She says, "Lily, where are you? Why are you hiding from me?" Then, Anna hears a soft voice. It says, "Anna, I'm here. I'm sorry. I was scared." Anna looks around and sees Lily lying on the grass. She has dirt on her face, her dress and her other shoe. She also has a scratch on her toe. She says, "Lily, I found you. I'm so happy. Why were you lying on the grass? Are you hurt?" Lily says, "Anna, I'm sorry. I fell off the slide and hurt my toe. It was bleeding and it hurt a lot. I was scared and I did not know what to do. I tried to call you, but you did not hear me. Then, I saw a big dog coming. He looked mean and he wanted to take me. I was more scared and I ran away. I lost my shoe and I hid on the grass. I hoped you would come back for me." Anna hugs Lily and says, "Lily, I'm sorry too. I did not mean to leave you. I forgot about you. I was having too much fun. I was a bad friend. I love you and I will never lose you again. You are my best doll." Mom comes over and says, "Anna, I'm glad you found Lily. She looks like she needs some care. Let's take her home and clean her up. We can put a band-aid on her toe and make her feel better." Anna says, "OK, Mom. Thank you for helping me. Lily, don't worry. You will be OK. I will take good care of you. You are my best doll."

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy shop. He wanted to sell toys to his friends. Tim had many toys like cars, dolls, and balls. All the kids loved Tim's toy shop. One day, a girl named Sue came to the toy shop. She saw a toy with a big nose. The toy was a funny clown. Sue liked the toy with the big nose. She asked Tim, "Can I buy the toy with the big nose?" Tim said, "Yes, you can buy it." Sue was very happy. She played with the toy with the big nose all day. She showed the toy to her mom. Her mom said, "That toy looks like a dead bug with a big nose!" Sue laughed and played with her new toy. She loved the toy with the big nose from Tim's toy shop.
