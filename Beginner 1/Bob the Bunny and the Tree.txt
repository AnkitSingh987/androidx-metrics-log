Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Bunny and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there lived a shy bunny named Bob. Bob had many friends, but his best friend was a big tree. The big tree was very old and wise, and it always helped Bob when he needed it. They spent many days playing together in the sun. One day, a man with an axe came to the forest. The man wanted to cut down the big tree. Bob was very sad and scared. He didn't want to lose his best friend. So, Bob thought of a plan to save the big tree. He decided to talk to the man and ask him to stop. When Bob went to talk to the man, something unexpected happened. The man was a shy man too, just like Bob. The man didn't want to hurt the big tree, but he needed wood to build a house for his family. Bob felt bad for the man and had an idea. He showed the man some other trees that were not as big and strong as his best friend. The man was happy with Bob's idea and cut down the other trees instead. In the end, Bob's best friend, the big tree, was saved, and the shy man got the wood he needed for his family. They all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars. One day, they find a big box of blocks in the playroom. They are happy and start to build a network of roads and bridges. "Look, Mia, I made a long road!" Tom says. "Wow, Tom, that's good! I made a high bridge!" Mia says. They play for a while, but then Tom wants to make his road longer. He sees a block that Mia is using for her bridge. He takes it and puts it on his road. "Hey, Tom, that's my block! Give it back!" Mia says. "No, Mia, I need it for my road! It's mine now!" Tom says. Mia is angry. She wants her block back. She tries to pull it from Tom's road, but Tom holds it tight. They start to tug and shout. "Let go, Mia! It's mine!" "Let go, Tom! It's mine!" They tug harder and harder, until the block flies out of their hands. It hits the wall and breaks in two. They both gasp and stop. "Oh no, the block is broken!" Tom says. "Oh no, the bridge is ruined!" Mia says. They look at each other and feel sad. They both like the block and the network. They don't like to fight and break things. "I'm sorry, Tom. I shouldn't have taken your block," Mia says. "I'm sorry, Mia. I shouldn't have pulled your block," Tom says. They hug and say sorry. They decide to share the blocks and fix the network. They are friends again. They are happy.

Once upon a time there was a kind man who was walking through the woods. As he walked he noticed the sky was clear and the birds were singing happily. Suddenly he saw some ash laying on the ground. He wanted to help the ash so he took some coins out of his pocket and paid the ash a visit. The ash smiled and thanked the man for his kind words. The man smiled back and continued on his way, feeling happy that he had paid the ash a visit.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor named Tom. He liked to raise the sails on his boat. One sunny day, he raised the sail high and went for a ride on the big blue sea. Tom saw a big fish jump out of the water. He waved at the fish, and the fish waved back with its tail. Tom was very happy to have a new friend. But then, the big fish jumped high and landed on Tom's boat! The fish could talk and said, "Hi, I am not a fish, I am a whale!" Tom was so surprised, but they became good friends and had lots of fun on the sea together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his blocks. He would build tall towers and big houses with them. Tim was very smart, and he could make many different shapes with his blocks. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They were having fun with the blocks, but then Sam had to go home. Tim said, "Bye, Sam! I will miss you!" After Sam left, Tim noticed that some of his blocks were missing. He was sad and didn't know where they went. Tim looked everywhere for his blocks. He looked under his bed, in the toy box, and even in the kitchen. Then, he heard a small sound coming from the closet. Tim opened the door and found his little sister, Lily, playing with his blocks! She had taken them to build her own tower. Tim was surprised, but he decided to teach Lily how to build with the blocks. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim didn't miss his blocks anymore because he was happy to share them with his sister.
