Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben Find the Park

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a man sitting on a bench. He looked sad and dirty. He had a sign that said "Please help me". "Look, Ben, he has no money," Anna said. "Maybe we can give him some of our money," Ben said. He had a piggy bank at home with many coins. "OK, let's go get it," Anna said. They ran to their house and asked their mom if they could take some money from the piggy bank. "Sure, but why do you need it?" their mom asked. "We want to help a man in the park. He has no money and he is sad," Anna said. "That's very kind of you, but be careful, OK? Don't talk to strangers," their mom said. Anna and Ben nodded and took some coins from the piggy bank. They ran back to the park and looked for the man. They found him on the same bench. He was still holding the sign. "Hello, sir, we have some money for you," Ben said. He held out his hand with the coins. The man looked at them with surprise and gratitude. He smiled and took the coins. "Thank you, thank you, you are very nice," he said. "You're welcome, sir, we hope you feel better," Anna said. They waved at him and ran back to their mom. They felt happy and proud. The man counted the coins and felt ashamed. He had lied to them. He had money, but he wanted more. He had a bad habit that made him spend all his money. He felt sorry for the kids. He wished he could be like them. He wished he could be happy and proud. He waved at them and walked away. He felt sad and dirty.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the pool with his mom. The pool was big and wet. Tim was very happy to play in the water. He put on his swim suit and jumped in. The pool was very quiet, and Tim liked that. While Tim was playing, a frog came to the pool. The frog said, "Ribbit! Can I play with you?" Tim was surprised but said, "Yes! Let's play together." They played and splashed in the water, having lots of fun. Then, Tim's mom said, "Tim, it's time to go home." Tim was sad, but he had to listen to his mom. He told the frog, "I have to go now. Bye, frog!" The frog said, "Ribbit! Bye, Tim!" Tim and his mom walked away, and the frog shut the pool gate behind them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a perfect new toy in her room. It was a skeleton. Lucy was very happy and excited to play with it. Lucy played with the skeleton all day. They had tea parties and played hide and seek. The skeleton was very good at hiding. Lucy would frown when she could not find it. But when she did, she would laugh and clap. One day, Lucy's friend Tim came to play. He saw the skeleton and said, "Wow! That's a cool toy!" Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, it is my perfect friend. We play together all the time." Tim and Lucy played with the skeleton, and they all had a fun day.

One day, a polite man named Tom went for a walk in the woods. He found a big axe on the ground. He picked it up and thought, "I should lock this in my shed so no one else can find it." Tom took the axe back to his house and locked it in the shed. Later, his friend Jack came over to play. Tom told Jack about the axe he found. Jack said, "Oh no, that is not a good thing to have! We must get rid of it." They went to the shed to get the axe, but when they opened the door, the axe was gone! Instead, they found a big, yummy cake in the shed. Tom and Jack were very surprised, but they laughed and enjoyed the cake together.
