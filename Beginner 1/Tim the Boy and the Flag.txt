Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Flag

One day, a boy named Tim saw a flag on a pole. The flag was old and torn. Tim wanted to replace the flag with a new one. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can we replace the flag?" Tim said. "It looks sad and old." His mom agreed, and they went to buy a new flag. At the store, they met a girl named Sue. Sue was ignorant about flags. She said, "I don't know what a flag is." Tim and Sue started to argue. Sue did not like that Tim knew more about flags than her. Tim's mom stopped the fight. She said, "It's okay to not know something. We can learn together." They all learned about flags and picked out a new one to replace the old one. In the end, Tim and Sue became friends. They put up the new flag together and were happy.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the snow. One day, they see a big hill of snow. They want to climb it. "Let's go, Ben!" Sara says. "It looks fun!" "OK, Sara!" Ben says. "But be careful. It might be slippery." They put on their hats, scarves and gloves. They run to the hill. They start to climb. But the snow is too soft. They sink in. They try to stand up. But they fall down. "Oh no!" Sara says. "We are stuck!" "Don't worry, Sara!" Ben says. "We can crawl out!" They crawl on their hands and knees. They make a path in the snow. They see something shiny. It is ice. It is frozen water. It sparkles in the sun. "Wow!" Sara says. "Look, Ben! Ice!" "Wow!" Ben says. "It is pretty!" They crawl to the ice. They touch it. It is cold and hard. They break a piece. They put it in their mouths. It is cold and wet. It melts. It is delicious. "Mmm!" Sara says. "It is yummy!" "Mmm!" Ben says. "It is sweet!" They smile. They share the ice. They are happy. They crawl out of the snow. They run to their mom. They tell her about their adventure. The end.

Lily and Ben wanted to join the club of big kids. The club had a tree house, a swing and a lot of toys. But the big kids said Lily and Ben were too small and silly to join. They said they had to behave well and do a test. The test was to go to the old house at the end of the street. The old house was dark and scary. The big kids said there was a ghost inside. Lily and Ben were afraid, but they wanted to join the club. They said they would go. They walked to the old house, holding hands. They saw a broken window, a rusty gate and a sign that said "Keep out". They heard a loud noise from inside. They thought it was the ghost. They screamed and ran away. The big kids were hiding behind a bush. They laughed and made the noise with a horn. They said Lily and Ben were foolish and scared. They said they could not join the club. Lily and Ben felt sad and angry. They said the big kids were mean and rude. They said they did not want to join their club. They said they would make their own club, with their own tree house, swing and toys. They said they would invite their friends who were nice and brave. They said they would have more fun. They smiled and hugged. They walked away.

One day, a little bunny named Tim went for a hop. He liked to hop in the wild field near his home. The sun was warm, but the wind was cold. Tim's mom told him not to go too far. "Stay close, Tim," she said. Tim hopped and hopped, having fun. He saw a big rock and wanted to hop on it. But the rock was far away from home. Tim thought, "I will hop there and back fast. Mom will not know." As Tim hopped to the big rock, he met a wild fox. The fox said, "Hello, little bunny. Want to play with me?" Tim knew that foxes were not nice to bunnies, but he wanted to be brave. So, he said, "Okay, let's play." They played near the big rock, but then the fox got hungry. The fox ate Tim, and Tim was gone. The moral of the story is to always listen to your mom and don't go too far from home.
