Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Apple and the Tree

One day, a boy went for a walk. He saw a giant. The giant was very big and tall. The boy was not scared. He said, "Hi, giant!" The giant looked at the boy and said, "Hi, little boy! I am sad. I ate a sour apple and now my tummy hurts." The boy wanted to help the giant. He said, "I know what to do! Let's find a sweet apple for you to eat." So, the boy and the giant went to look for a sweet apple. They looked and looked. Then, they found a big apple tree. The boy said, "Giant, can you pick an apple from the tree?" The giant said, "Yes, I can!" He picked an apple from the tree and gave it to the boy. The boy looked at the apple and said, "Wait! This is not an apple, it's a ball!" The giant laughed and said, "You are right! It's a ball, not an apple!" They both laughed and played with the ball all day. The giant's tummy did not hurt anymore, and they became best friends.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big jar of mints on a bench. They are curious. "Who left these mints here?" Lila asks. "I don't know. Maybe someone forgot them," Tom says. "Can we have some? They look yummy," Lila says. Tom nods. He opens the jar and takes a mint. He gives one to Lila too. They put the mints in their mouths. They are sweet and cool. "Mmm, these are good," Tom says. "Yes, they are," Lila agrees. They take some more mints and put them in their pockets. They want to save them for later. They close the jar and leave it on the bench. They run to the slide and have fun. Soon, a man comes to the bench. He is looking for his jar of mints. He is sad. He needs the mints for his bad breath. He has a meeting with his boss. He looks around and sees the jar. He is happy. He picks it up and opens it. He is shocked. The jar is almost empty. There are only a few mints left. "Hey, who took my mints?" he shouts. Lila and Tom hear him. They feel guilty. They know they did something wrong. They go to the man and say sorry. "We are sorry, mister. We took your mints. We didn't know they were yours. We thought they were for everyone," Lila says. "Yeah, we are sorry. We didn't mean to make you sad. Here, you can have our mints. We have some in our pockets," Tom says. They give the man their mints. The man is surprised. He sees that they are honest and kind. He smiles. "Thank you, kids. That is very helpful of you. You are good friends. I forgive you. You can keep some mints for yourselves. But next time, don't take things that are not yours, okay?" he says. "Okay, mister. We understand. We won't do it again. Thank you for being nice," Lila and Tom say. They hug the man and say goodbye. They go back to the park and share the mints. They are happy. They learned a lesson. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to get a toy from a high shelf. He tried to reach it, but he was not tall enough. Tim felt sad because he really wanted to play with the toy. He thought about who could help him get the toy. Tim saw his big brother, Sam, who was very powerful. He asked Sam, "Can you help me get my toy from the high shelf?" Sam smiled and said, "Of course, little brother. I will try my best to help you." Sam tried to reach the high shelf. He stretched his powerful arms high up and grabbed the toy. Sam gave the toy to Tim. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sam! You are the best big brother!" They played with the toy together and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Emma was playing with her toys. She was very happy. Her daddy was coming home from a long trip. Emma missed him very much. She wanted to welcome him with a big hug. When daddy came home, he had a big surprise for Emma. He brought a new toy for her. It was a cute teddy bear. Emma was so happy and said, "Thank you, daddy!" But her old toy, a small bunny, felt jealous. The bunny didn't want to share Emma's love with the new teddy bear. That night, when Emma was sleeping, the jealous bunny pushed the teddy bear off the bed. The teddy bear landed on the floor with a soft thud. Emma woke up and saw the teddy bear on the floor. She picked it up and said, "It's okay, teddy. I love you both." From that day on, the bunny and the teddy bear became best friends, and they all welcomed daddy home together.
