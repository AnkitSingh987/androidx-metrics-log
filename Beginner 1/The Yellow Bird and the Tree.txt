Category: Beginner
Title: The Yellow Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. It would fly up and down, and all around. The bird was very happy. One day, the bird saw a big fire. The fire was burning a tree. The bird was scared, but it wanted to help. It flew close to the fire and tried to blow it out. But the fire just got bigger. Then, something unexpected happened. A big rain cloud came and started to pour water on the fire. The fire went out and the tree was saved. The little yellow bird was so happy. It flew up in the sky again, knowing that it had a new friend in the rain cloud.

Once upon a time, there was a small pond in a big park. The pond had fish and ducks. It was a normal pond, just like the others. One day, it got very cold. The water in the pond started to freeze. The ducks and fish were not happy. They wanted to swim and play in the water. A little girl named Lily came to the pond. She saw the ducks and fish were sad. Lily said, "Don't worry, ducks and fish. I will help you." She found a stick and broke the ice. The ducks and fish could swim and play again. The ducks and fish said, "Thank you, Lily!" They were happy and played in the pond all day. The pond was normal again, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot. The pilot flew a big plane in the sky. He loved to fly high and look at the clouds. One day, the pilot met a little bird. The bird was impatient. It wanted to fly fast like the plane. The pilot smiled and said, "I can help you." The pilot told the bird to repeat after him. "Flap your wings fast and strong," he said. The bird listened and did as the pilot said. It flapped its wings and started to fly faster. Soon, the bird was flying just as fast as the plane. The pilot and the bird became good friends. They flew in the sky together, happy and free.

One day, a big truck was on the road. It had to load many things. The truck driver was happy. He liked to help people with his truck. On the way, he saw some police. They were looking for something. The truck driver stopped and asked, "Can I help you, police?" The police said, "Yes, we need to find a lost cat. Can you help us look for it?" The truck driver said, "Yes, I will help you." They looked and looked for the cat. Then, they found the cat on top of the big truck! The cat had been there the whole time. The police and the truck driver laughed. They did not expect the cat to be there. The cat was happy to be found. The truck driver and the police became good friends.

One day, a boat went to a port. The boat had a bad mind. It did not like to help others. In the port, there was a little boat. The little boat was sad. The bad boat said, "Why are you sad, little boat?" The little boat said, "I can't go out and play with my friends. I am stuck in the port." The bad boat thought, "I can trick the little boat and make it more sad." The bad boat said, "I will help you, little boat. Just follow me." The little boat was happy and followed the bad boat. But then, something unexpected happened. The bad boat got stuck in the mud. The little boat helped the bad boat get out of the mud. The bad boat said, "Thank you, little boat. I am sorry I wanted to trick you. I will be good now." They both went back to the port and played with their friends. The bad boat was good from then on.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they have a race with their cars. Lily has a red car and Tom has a blue car. "Ready, set, go!" Lily says. They push their cars on the floor. Lily's car is fast. Tom's car is slow. Lily's car gets to the end first. Lily is happy. She jumps and claps. "I win, I win!" she says. Tom is sad. He looks at his car. His car is cute. It has a smiley face and a star. He likes his car. He says, "Your car is fast, but my car is cute. I like my car." Lily looks at Tom's car. She sees the smiley face and the star. She thinks Tom's car is cute too. She says, "You are right, your car is cute. I like your car too. Do you want to play with my car?" Tom smiles. He says, "Yes, I want to play with your car. Do you want to play with my car?" Lily nods. She says, "Yes, I want to play with your car. Let's share our cars. Sharing is fun." They swap their cars. They play with each other's cars. They have fun. They are happy. They are good friends. The moral of the story is: Winning is not everything. Sharing is caring.
