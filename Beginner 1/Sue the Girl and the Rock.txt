Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she found a shiny gold rock near a big tree. Sue was very happy and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! I found gold!" Sue said. Tom looked at the gold rock and said, "Wow, that's not bad! Let's show it to our teacher." So, they went to their teacher, Miss Ann, and showed her the gold rock. Miss Ann looked at the gold rock and said, "This gold is wet. Let's dry it in the sun." They put the gold rock on the ground and waited for it to dry. After a while, the gold rock was dry and shiny. Sue and Tom played with the gold rock all day. They were very happy and had lots of fun. In the end, they shared the gold rock with all their friends, and everyone was happy. And they all learned that sharing is good, even if it's just a shiny gold rock.

Tom and Lily were playing in the living room. They liked to throw balls and pillows at each other. They laughed and had fun. Mom was in the kitchen. She was making coffee for herself. She was tired and needed a drink. She poured the hot coffee into a big mug. She walked to the living room to watch Tom and Lily. But Tom and Lily did not see Mom. They were too busy throwing things. Tom threw a ball at Lily. Lily ducked and the ball flew past her. It hit Mom's big mug. The coffee spilled out of the mug. It burned Mom's hand and face. Mom screamed and dropped the mug. It broke on the floor. Tom and Lily stopped playing. They saw Mom. They saw the coffee. They saw the broken mug. They felt scared and sorry. They ran to Mom and hugged her. Mom was angry and hurt. She pushed Tom and Lily away. She yelled at them. She said they were bad and naughty. She said they had to go to their room. She said they could not play anymore. She said they made a big mess. Tom and Lily cried. They did not mean to hurt Mom. They did not mean to spill the coffee. They did not mean to break the mug. They just wanted to play. They said they were sorry. But Mom did not listen. She sent them to their room. She did not hug them back. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big freezer. It was in a small room. The freezer had a lot of yummy food inside. One day, the freezer wanted to move to a new place. It wanted to be in a room with more available space. The freezer asked a strong friend to help. The friend was happy to help the freezer move. They moved the freezer to a big room. Now, the freezer had more space to be in. The freezer was very happy in the new room. It had more available space to keep food cold. The freezer and its friend had a fun time moving. They were both happy to be in the big room. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a man and his wife were in their house. They heard a knock on the door. The man went to the door and saw an unknown person outside. The person said, "Can you please unlock the door?" The man was not sure if he should do it. He went to his wife and said, "There is a person I don't know outside. He wants me to unlock the door. What should I do?" The wife thought for a moment and said, "It's okay, maybe he needs help." The man went back to the door and unlocked it. The unknown person came in, but he was not nice. He took some things from the house and ran away. The man and his wife were very sad. From that day on, the man and his wife learned to be more careful with unknown people. They never unlocked the door for someone they didn't know again. But they always remembered the day they lost their things, and it made them feel bad.
