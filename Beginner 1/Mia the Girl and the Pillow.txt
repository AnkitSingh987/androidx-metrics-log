Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Pillow

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big, soft pillow. It was a calm day, and Mia was very happy. She loved her new pillow. She played with it all day, and when it was time for bed, she put it on her bed to sleep on. At night, while Mia was sleeping, a small cat came into her room. The cat saw the big, soft pillow and wanted it too. The cat jumped on the bed and tried to take the pillow away from Mia. Mia woke up and saw the cat trying to take her pillow. She was sad and didn't want to lose her new pillow. Mia had an idea. She got up and found a ball of yarn. She threw the ball of yarn and the cat started to follow it. The cat was having fun playing with the yarn and forgot about the pillow. Mia took her pillow back and put it on her bed. The cat was happy playing with the yarn, and Mia was happy with her pillow. They both became friends and played together every day.

One day, a little cricket was hopping in the grass. He was a careless cricket, always jumping and playing without looking where he was going. He liked to sing songs and make all his friends laugh. The cricket saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He started to climb, but he was still careless. He didn't see a long vine hanging from the tree. The cricket jumped onto the vine and it started to wrap around him. His friends saw him and they all came to help. They pulled and tugged at the vine until the cricket was free. The cricket said thank you to his friends and promised to be more careful. They all laughed and played together in the grass, happy to be together again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tom. He had an incredible toy. It was a big, blue ball. He loved to play with it every day. One sunny day, Tom took his ball outside to play. He saw a glass on the grass. The glass was full of water. Tom kicked the ball high into the air. The ball fell down and hit the glass. The water splashed out and soaked the ball. Tom was sad that his ball was wet. But the sun was warm and bright. Soon, the ball dried up, and Tom was happy again. He played with his incredible ball until the sun went down. Then, he took his ball back inside the house and went to bed, excited to play again the next day.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big jug. He thought it was very hard. He wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tim walked to Sam's house with the jug. When Tim got there, Sam was sad. Sam said, "I want a jug too!" Tim did not want to give his jug to Sam. They both got mad at each other. Tim and Sam did not talk to each other. They both walked away feeling sad. The big, hard jug made them not be friends anymore. And that was a bad ending.

Tom is a boy who likes to sing. He sings a song every day. He sings when he wakes up, when he eats, when he plays, and when he goes to bed. He knows many songs. He sings songs about animals, colors, numbers, and friends. One day, Tom goes to the park with his mom. He sees a man with a guitar. The man is singing a song. Tom likes the song. He goes to the man and says, "Hello, what is your name?" The man smiles and says, "My name is Sam. I am a singer. I sing songs for people. Do you like to sing?" Tom nods and says, "Yes, I like to sing. I sing a song every day. Can you teach me your song?" Sam says, "Sure, I can teach you my song. It is a song about being happy. It is a simple song. You can learn it fast. Do you want to try?" Tom says, "Yes, I want to try. Can I use your guitar?" Sam says, "Of course, you can use my guitar. Here, I will show you how to hold it and play it. Then, I will sing the song and you can repeat after me." Sam and Tom start to play and sing. They have fun. They make a nice sound. People in the park stop and listen. They clap and cheer. They give Sam and Tom some money. Sam and Tom are happy. They are rich. They have a new song and a new friend.
