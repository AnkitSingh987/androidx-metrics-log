Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog of Tim the Boy

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his round ball. He loved to kick it high in the sky. He was having so much fun, when suddenly, he saw a sad dog sitting under a tree. The dog looked lonely, so Tim decided to play with it. Tim kicked the round ball to the sad dog, and the dog ran after it. The dog caught the ball and brought it back to Tim. They played together, and the dog was not sad anymore. They were happy and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog kicked the round ball with its paw, and the ball flew up into the tree. Tim and the dog looked at the ball, and they both started to laugh. The dog was not just a sad dog, but a funny dog too. They played together all day, and Tim made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went for a walk in the thick woods with his dog, Spot. They liked to walk on the path in the woods. One day, they met a big bear on the path. The bear said, "Yield, little boy! This is my path." Tim was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. Tim said, "Please, Mr. Bear, let us pass. We just want to walk on the path." The bear thought for a moment, then he said, "Okay, little boy. You and Spot can go." Tim and Spot walked past the bear and continued on their way. They were very happy to be safe and to keep walking on the path in the thick woods.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a screen that she loved to watch. One day, Lucy saw a bird on the screen. The bird was very intelligent and could talk. The bird said, "Hello, Lucy! I am an intelligent bird. I can help you with anything you want." Lucy was very happy and asked the bird to play with her. They played and had lots of fun together. Later, Lucy's mom called her for dinner. Lucy told the bird, "I have to go now. It's time for dinner." The bird started to cry because it would miss Lucy. Lucy felt sad too and said, "Don't cry, bird. We can play again tomorrow!" The bird stopped crying and smiled. They said goodbye and Lucy went to have dinner with her family.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But they do not like to share. One day, they have a big fight over a cute shirt. The shirt has a picture of a cat and a bird on it. Lily says, "This is my shirt. I want to wear it today. It is cute and soft." Ben says, "No, this is my shirt. I want to wear it today. It is cute and cool." They pull and tug on the shirt. They yell and cry. They make a big mess in their room. Mom hears the noise. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees Lily and Ben fighting over the shirt. She is not happy. She says, "Stop it, both of you. This is not how you treat each other. This is not how you treat your clothes. You need to learn to share and be kind." She takes the shirt from them. She says, "No one can wear this shirt today. You need to say sorry to each other and pick another shirt. Then you need to help me clean up this mess." Lily and Ben feel sad and sorry. They hug and say sorry to each other. They pick another shirt to wear. They help Mom clean up their room. Mom says, "Thank you, Lily and Ben. You are good kids. I love you both. Now, let's go have some breakfast. I made you some pancakes and fruit. I will serve them to you on the table." Lily and Ben smile and say, "Thank you, Mom. We love you too. We love pancakes and fruit. You are the best mom." They go to the table and eat their breakfast. They share and be kind. They have a good day.
