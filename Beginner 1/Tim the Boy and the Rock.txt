Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cart. One day, he found a rare, shiny rock while playing outside. Tim was very happy and put the rock in his cart. He went to his friend, Sam, and said, "Look at my rare rock! I enjoy playing with it in my cart." Sam looked at the rock and smiled. "Wow, that's a nice rock, Tim! Can I play with it too?" Tim and Sam played with the cart and the rare rock all day. They took turns pushing the cart and sharing the rock. They enjoyed their playtime and were happy to be friends. At the end of the day, they went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy spear. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and dad. He took his spear with him. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it, but he needed help. "Mom, Dad, I need help!" Tim said. His dad held his hand and helped him climb the tree. Tim felt happy. But then, Tim dropped his spear from the tree. He felt frustrated. "Oh no, my spear!" he cried. His mom picked up the spear and gave it back to him. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They all played together and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was eager to help his dad fix things around the house. One day, Tim's dad needed to polish a big table. He asked Tim to bring him the polish and a cloth. While Tim was looking for the polish, he found a big hammer. He thought it might help his dad too. So, he carried the hammer and the polish to his dad. "Here, dad! I found the polish and a hammer to help you!" Tim said with a big smile. His dad laughed and said, "Thank you, Tim, but we don't need a hammer to polish the table. Let's use it for another job." Just then, they heard a loud crash from the kitchen. They ran to see what happened. Tim's mom had dropped a big pot, and it had a dent in it. Tim's dad used the hammer to fix the pot. The family was happy, and Tim felt proud that he had helped in an unexpected way.

One day, a sheep named Dizzy felt very dizzy. Dizzy was not like other sheep. Her wool was very long and heavy. Dizzy's wool made it hard for her to walk and play with her friends. She wanted to be like the other sheep and have short wool. Dizzy saw a man with a tool that could cut her wool. She went to him and said, "Please cut my wool. I want to play with my friends." The man said, "Okay, Dizzy. I will help you." The man started to cut Dizzy's wool. Soon, her wool was short and light. Dizzy was so happy. She ran to her friends and shouted, "Look at me! I can play now!" Her friends were happy too. They played all day long. Dizzy learned that it is okay to ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside. One day, it was very icy and cold. Sue's mother said, "Be careful, Sue. The ground is icy." Sue went outside to play. She saw a big hill and wanted to get to the top. She tried to climb the hill, but it was too icy. Sue fell down and started to cry. Her mother heard her and came outside. Sue's mother said, "Let's try something different." She got a big rope and tied it to a tree at the top of the hill. Sue held the rope and climbed the hill with her mother's help. They both got to the top and were very happy. Sue learned that it is good to ask for help when things are hard. And her mother was always there to help her. They had lots of fun playing on the icy hill together.
