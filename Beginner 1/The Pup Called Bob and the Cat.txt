Category: Beginner
Title: The Pup Called Bob and the Cat

Once upon a time there was a little pup called Bob who loved to explore. One day, Bob heard a noisy barrel and decided to investigate. He slowly made his way over. When he got closer, he heard sounds of a conversation. Bob paused to listen. He heard one voice saying: “If you don’t give me what I want, you’ll be in big trouble.” Bob was scared but he was also brave. He didn’t want to run away and he decided to see who was in the barrel. He walked closer and peeped inside. It was a big bad cat! The cat had a sharp tooth and it was growling at a tiny bird. The bird was scared and scared. He pleaded with the cat to leave him alone, but the cat refused. Bob knew he had to save the bird but he wasn’t sure how. He thought and he thought until he had an idea. He shouted “Boo!” The cat was so scared it ran away, never to be seen again. The bird was relieved and thanked Bob for saving him. Bob felt very proud of himself and happy he had saved the bird. But then, the bird said something that made Bob sad. He said “Now that the cat is gone, there’s nothing to keep the other animals away. You’d better go home, Bob.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play in the park. They run, slide, swing and laugh. They have fun. One day, they see a bench in the park. It is big and red. It has a sign on it. The sign says: "Do not sit here. Wet paint." Lily and Max want to sit on the bench. They think it looks nice. They forget the sign. They run to the bench and sit down. "Ouch!" Lily says. "The bench is sticky. It hurts my legs." "Me too!" Max says. "The bench is wet. It makes my pants dirty." They look at each other. They see red paint on their clothes and skin. They are sad. They cry. A lady comes to the bench. She sees Lily and Max. She is the painter. She is angry. "Why did you sit on my bench?" she says. "Can't you read the sign? It says wet paint. You are not smart. You are silly." Lily and Max are sorry. They say: "We are sorry, lady. We did not read the sign. We forget. We are not silly. We are smart. But we made a mistake." The lady is not so angry. She sees that Lily and Max are young. She says: "It's okay. Everyone makes mistakes. But you have to be careful. Next time, read the signs. They are important. They help you stay safe and clean." Lily and Max nod. They say: "Thank you, lady. We will read the signs. We will be careful. We will not forget." The lady gives them a cloth. They wipe the paint off their clothes and skin. They say goodbye to the lady. They go to their moms. They tell them what happened. Their moms hug them. They say: "We love you. You are smart. But sometimes you forget. That's okay. Just try to remember."

Tom and Lily were playing in the living room. They had many toys and books and puzzles. They liked to make noise and have fun. But they did not like to clean up. The living room was very messy. "Tom, Lily, it is time to tidy up!" Mom called from the kitchen. "Put your toys in the sack and your books on the shelf. And be careful with the cable. It is for the TV, not for the toys." Tom and Lily did not want to tidy up. They wanted to play more. They ignored Mom and kept playing. They threw the toys in the air and on the floor. They pulled the cable and wrapped it around the toys. They made a big mess. "Tom, Lily, what are you doing?" Mom came to the living room and saw the mess. She was not happy. She looked at Tom and Lily with a stern face. "This is not how you play. This is how you break things. And this is how you hurt yourself. The cable is dangerous. It can shock you or trip you. You have to be careful." Tom and Lily felt sorry. They saw Mom was right. They had made a mess and they had been naughty. They said sorry to Mom and hugged her. They helped Mom to tidy up. They put the toys in the sack and the books on the shelf. They untangled the cable and put it back. They made the living room neat and tidy. Mom smiled and hugged them back. She was proud of them. She said they were good children. She said they could play again, but only if they cleaned up afterwards. Tom and Lily agreed. They learned their lesson. They played nicely and tidied up. They had fun and Mom was happy.
