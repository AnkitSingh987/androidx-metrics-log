Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben Find the Toys

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. But sometimes they make a big mess. They throw their toys on the floor and do not put them back in the box. One day, Mom comes to their room and sees the mess. She is not happy. She says, "Sara and Ben, you have to clean up your room. You cannot waste your toys like this. They will break or get lost. And you have to share your toys with each other. Do you understand?" Sara and Ben nod their heads. They do not want to make Mom sad. They start to pick up their toys and put them in the box. But Sara sees a doll that she likes. It is Ben's doll. She takes it and says, "This is mine now. I want to play with it." Ben sees Sara take his doll. He is angry. He says, "No, that is my doll. Give it back to me. You have your own doll. You are not nice." Sara and Ben start to fight. They pull the doll from each other. They yell and cry. The doll's arm breaks. They both feel bad. Mom hears the noise and comes back to their room. She sees the broken doll and the tears on their faces. She is sad and angry. She says, "Sara and Ben, what have you done? You have broken your toy. You have hurt each other. You have not listened to me. You have to say sorry to each other and hug. And you have to fix the doll. Do you understand?" Sara and Ben nod their heads. They feel sorry. They say sorry to each other and hug. They are pale from crying. They try to fix the doll. They use tape and glue. They make the doll look better. They smile and say, "We love you, doll. We love you, Mom. We love you, Sara. We love you, Ben." Mom sees them and smiles. She says, "I love you, Sara. I love you, Ben. I am proud of you. You have learned your lesson. You have cleaned up your room. You have shared your toys. You have fixed your toy. You have hugged. You are good twins." She hugs them and kisses them. They hug her and kiss her. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to drink juice. Every day, he would go to the park with his mom. In the park, there was a stand that sold juice. Tim would always ask his mom if he could get some juice to drink. One day, Tim saw a new friend at the park. Her name was Sue. Sue was sitting on a comfortable bench. Tim went to Sue and said, "Hi! Do you want to drink juice with me?" Sue said, "Yes! I like juice too!" Tim and Sue went to the juice stand with their moms. They both got a big cup of juice to drink. They sat on the comfortable bench together, drinking their juice and laughing. From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends who loved to drink juice at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy boat. One day, Lily found a small bulb in her garden. She was very enthusiastic and thought it was a treasure. She put the bulb in her pocket and went to show her mom. Her mom told her that the bulb was a special seed. If she planted it and took good care of it, it would grow into a beautiful flower. Lily was excited and planted the bulb in a pot. Every day, she would water it and rock the pot gently, like her toy boat. As the days passed, the bulb started to grow. Lily was so happy to see the little plant grow bigger and bigger. One day, she found a beautiful flower blooming from the plant. The flower was as bright as the sun and smelled very nice. Lily knew that her love and care had made the flower grow, and she felt very proud.
