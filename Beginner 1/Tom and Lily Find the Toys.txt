Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Toys

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big bottle of glue in the kitchen. They think it is fun to use the glue on everything. "Look, I can glue this car to the table!" Tom says. He puts some glue on the car and sticks it to the table. "That is silly!" Lily says. She laughs and picks up a doll. "I can glue this doll to the chair!" She puts some glue on the doll and sticks it to the chair. They glue more things to the furniture. They glue a ball to the lamp, a hat to the sofa, and a book to the wall. They are very happy and make a lot of noise. But then, Mom comes home. She sees the mess in the kitchen. She is very angry. "What have you done?" Mom asks. She looks at the glue and the things. "This is not your glue. This is my glue. I need it for my work. And these are not your things. These are my things. You cannot glue them to anything. You have to take them off." Tom and Lily are scared. They say sorry to Mom. They try to take off the things, but they are stuck. The glue is very strong. They cannot take them off. Mom sighs. She is not happy. She has to call Dad. Dad has to come home and help. He has to use a tool to take off the things. It takes a long time and a lot of work. Tom and Lily learn a lesson. They do not use glue anymore. They do not touch things that are not theirs. They play with their own toys and books. They are not silly anymore. They are good.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big pond with ducks. Tim wanted to play with the ducks. He ran to the pond and started to splash water at them. The ducks were in shock and swam away quickly. Tim's mom said, "Tim, don't splash the ducks! They don't like it." Tim felt sad and stopped splashing. He wanted the ducks to come back. He saw some bread on the ground and picked it up. He threw the bread into the pond. The ducks saw the bread and swam back. Tim's mom said, "That's interesting, the ducks like the bread." Tim was happy to see the ducks come back. He kept throwing bread, and the ducks kept eating. Tim and his mom had fun at the park. They went home and told dad about their day. The ducks were no longer in shock, and Tim learned how to be kind to them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a modern house with his mom and dad. Tim loved to grab and touch everything he saw. One day, he saw a shiny, red ball on the floor. Tim wanted to grab the ball, but his mom said, "Wait, Tim! Don't touch it yet." Tim did not listen and grabbed the ball. Just then, something unexpected happened! The ball began to grow bigger and bigger. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. His mom came and hugged him. She said, "Tim, you must learn to listen and not touch things without asking." Tim nodded and said sorry. The ball went back to its normal size. From that day on, Tim always asked before he touched anything. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Mia went to her friend Tom's house. Tom wanted to introduce Mia to his new pet cat, Fluffy. Fluffy was soft and smooth. Mia liked Fluffy a lot. Mia wanted to sit on the cushion on the floor, but Fluffy was on it. Mia said to Fluffy, "Please move, I want to sit on the cushion." Fluffy did not want to move. Mia was sad. Tom saw Mia was sad and had an idea. He said, "Mia, let's get another cushion for you to sit on." They found another cushion, and both Mia and Fluffy were happy. They played together all day.
