Category: Beginner
Title: Polly the Parrot and the Tree

One day, a parrot named Polly found a big tree. Polly was a very pretty bird with many colors. She liked to sit on the tree and sing songs. The tree was in a park where kids liked to play. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. In the park, there was a sour lemon tree. The lemons were big and yellow. Polly saw the lemons and wanted to taste them. She flew down from the big tree and took a bite of a lemon. "Yuck!" said Polly. The lemon was very sour, and she didn't like it at all. Polly saw a boy named Tim playing near the tree. Tim had a toy that could spring up and down. Polly liked the toy and wanted to play with Tim. So, Polly flew to Tim and they played with the toy together. They had a lot of fun, and Polly forgot about the sour lemon. They played until the sun went down, and Polly went back to her big tree to sleep.

One day, a little boy named Tim was feeling gloomy. He wanted to eat something sweet. He went to his mom and requested, "Mom, can I have some jam, please?" His mom said, "Sure, Tim. I will get you some jam." She got the jam from the kitchen and gave it to Tim. Tim was very happy with the jam. He ate it all up very fast. But then, he started to feel bad. His tummy hurt a lot. He cried and said, "Mom, my tummy hurts!" His mom looked at him and said, "Oh no, Tim! You ate too much jam. Now you have a tummy ache." Tim was sad and cried more because his tummy hurt so much. The day ended with Tim feeling bad and going to bed early.

One day, an elderly man named Tom went for a walk on the street. He saw a small box on the ground. The box had a pretty glow. Tom was very curious. He wanted to know what was inside the box. Tom opened the box and found a tiny bug. The bug could talk! It said, "Hello, Tom! I am a magic bug. I can grant you one wish. What do you want?" Tom thought for a moment. He said, "I want to be young again." The magic bug smiled and waved its little arms. Suddenly, Tom felt a change. He looked down and saw that he had turned into a baby! Tom was surprised and a little scared. He didn't want to be this young! The magic bug laughed and said, "You are young again, just like you wished! Have fun, baby Tom!" And with that, the bug flew away, leaving Tom to figure out what to do next.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play at school. One day, they see a big blue desk in the hall. It is new and shiny. "Wow, look at that desk!" Ben says. "It is so big and blue!" "Can we play with it?" Mia asks. "It looks fun!" They run to the desk and touch it. It is smooth and hard. They see a button on the side. Ben pushes it. The desk makes a noise and slides open. Inside, there are many books and pencils and papers. "Look, there are things inside!" Ben says. "Let's see what they are!" They take out some books and pencils and papers. They look at the pictures and words. They try to draw and write. They have fun. But then, they hear a voice. It is the teacher. She is coming to the hall. "Who is playing with my desk?" she asks. "That is not for you!" Ben and Mia are scared. They quickly put back the books and pencils and papers. They close the desk. They run away. "Sorry, teacher!" they say. "We were just curious!" The teacher sees them. She is not happy. She shakes her head. "That desk is mine!" she says. "It is for my work, not for your play! Next time, ask before you touch!" Ben and Mia nod. They feel sorry. They go back to their classroom. They decide to play with their own toys. They do not touch the blue desk again.
