Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy gun that made a loud noise when he played with it. He loved his gun and played with it every day. One day, Tim saw a little bird stuck in a tree. The bird was scared and needed help. Tim wanted to rescue the bird, but he didn't know how. He thought about using his loud gun to scare the bird, but he knew that it would not be nice. Tim's mom saw him thinking and helped him find a better way to rescue the bird. They used a long stick to gently help the bird out of the tree. The bird was happy and flew away. Tim learned that it is better to help others with kindness and not with loud noises or scary things.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. They saw a big pile of soil. Sue asked her mom, "What is this?" Her mom said, "It's soil, dear. It's used to help plants grow." Sue wanted to play near the soil, but her mom said, "No, it's dirty and unknown. We don't know what's in there." Sue felt sad, but she listened to her mom. They played on the swings and slides instead. Later, a big rain came and changed the park. The soil washed away and left something shiny behind. Sue and her mom went to see what it was. They found a pretty, shiny rock. Sue said, "Wow! The rain changed the soil and gave us a gift!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, sometimes unknown things can bring nice surprises."

One day, a weak bird named Tim could not fly with his friends. He was sad. Tim had a gift, he could sing a beautiful song. He wanted to find a way to fit in with his friends. Tim met a wise old owl. "I can't fly with my friends," Tim said. "But I can sing a song. How can I fit in?" The wise old owl thought for a moment. "You can teach your friends your song," he said. "Then you will all have fun together." So, Tim taught his friends his special song. They all sang the song together and had lots of fun. Now, Tim fit in with his friends. They all loved his song and Tim was happy.

Polly the rabbit was very busy. She had a lot to do! She needed to clean her bed and put away her toys. She had to help her friends too. Polly tried her best and worked very hard, but it was hard for a young bunny like her. She just didn't know what to do. But then something happened! Polly had an idea. She thought hard and worked out a plan. She did this and she did that, hopping from task to task. Finally, she was able to finish all her work and succeed! Polly was so proud of herself for succeeding. Now, she could relax in bed and go to sleep with a smile on her face.

Lily and Ben were playing in the backyard. They found a big pot and some junk. They wanted to make a soup with the junk. They put sticks, leaves, rocks, and dirt in the pot. They added some water from the hose. They took turns to stir the soup with a big spoon. "Yum, yum, this soup smells good," said Lily. "No, it smells bad," said Ben. "Good!" "Bad!" They started to argue. They did not want to share the soup. They pulled the spoon from each other. The pot tipped over. The soup spilled on the ground. "Oh no, our soup is gone," said Lily. "It's okay, it was not a good soup anyway," said Ben. They looked at the mess. They felt sad. They did not have fun. They heard their mom calling them. "Lily, Ben, time for lunch!" They ran to the house. They washed their hands. They sat at the table. They saw a big bowl of soup. It smelled good. It had carrots, potatoes, chicken, and noodles. "Wow, this soup looks good," said Lily. "Yes, it does," said Ben. They tasted the soup. It was delicious. They smiled at each other. "Mom, you are a successful cook," said Lily. "Thank you, sweetie. And you are a successful eater," said mom. "Mom, can we have more soup, please?" said Ben. "Of course, honey. And you are a successful helper," said mom. They ate more soup. They were happy. They forgot about the junk soup. They learned to share and to be nice. They had fun.
