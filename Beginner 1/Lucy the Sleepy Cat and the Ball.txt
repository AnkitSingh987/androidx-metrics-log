Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Sleepy Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Lucy. Lucy loved to play with her ball of yarn. One day, her ball of yarn rolled under a big chair. Lucy tried to get her ball, but she found a needle under the chair too. Lucy did not know what to do with the needle. Lucy asked her friend, a bird named Sam, to help her. Sam was very smart. Sam said, "Lucy, we need to replace the needle with something else. It is not safe for you to play with." Lucy thought it was a good idea. Sam found a small stick and replaced the needle with the stick. Lucy was very happy. She could play with her ball of yarn again. Lucy and Sam played together all day, and they had so much fun.

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, there lived a round, happy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her ball all day long. She would run and jump, and sometimes she would even roll just like her round ball. One day, Fluffy's ball got very dirty. It had mud and dirt all over it. Fluffy did not like her ball dirty, so she decided to clean it. She took a soft cloth and started to wipe the ball. She wiped and wiped until the ball was shiny and clean again. With her round ball clean, Fluffy went back to playing happily. She rolled the ball around and chased it all through the house. Fluffy was very proud of her clean ball, and she knew that if it ever got dirty again, she could just wipe it clean. And so, Fluffy and her round ball lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily were playing with their blocks. They liked to build towers and houses and bridges. They had many colors and shapes of blocks. But then, Max came. Max was a big boy who lived next door. He was not nice. He liked to destroy things. He saw Tom and Lily's blocks and said, "What are you doing? These are my blocks. Give them to me!" Tom and Lily were scared. They said, "No, Max. These are our blocks. We are playing with them. Go away!" Max was angry. He said, "No, you go away. These are mine. I will take them all." He pushed Tom and Lily and grabbed their blocks. He threw them on the floor and stepped on them. He laughed and said, "Ha ha ha! I destroyed your blocks. Now you have nothing." Tom and Lily were sad. They cried and said, "Max, you are mean. You are a fierce boy. You broke our blocks. We worked hard to build them. Why did you do that?" Max did not care. He said, "Because I can. Because I am bigger and stronger than you. Because I like to destroy things. Now go away and leave me alone." Tom and Lily ran away. They looked for their mom. They told her what Max did. Mom was angry. She said, "Max, you are a bad boy. You have to say sorry to Tom and Lily. You have to give them back their blocks. You have to help them rebuild their towers and houses and bridges. You have one minute to do that. Or else, you will be in big trouble." Max was scared. He did not want to be in trouble. He said, "Sorry, Tom and Lily. Sorry, mom. I will give you back your blocks. I will help you rebuild. Please don't be mad at me." Tom and Lily were still sad, but they forgave Max. They said, "Okay, Max. We will play with you. But you have to be nice. You have to share. You have to not destroy things." Max nodded. He said, "Okay, Tom and Lily. I will be nice. I will share. I will not destroy things. I will play with you." They all played with the blocks. They built new towers and houses and bridges. They had fun. They were friends.

One day, a big black bird sat high in a tree. The bird saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. The bird wanted to help Sue. The bird said, "Sue, I will help you find your toy. Repeat after me, 'Toy, come back to me!'" Sue did what the bird said. She said, "Toy, come back to me!" But the toy did not come back. Sue was more sad. The bird told Sue to repeat the words again. Sue said, "Toy, come back to me!" This time, a big wind came. The wind took Sue high in the sky. Sue was scared. The bird tried to help, but it was too late. Sue was gone with the wind.
