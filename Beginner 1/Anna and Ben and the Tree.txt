Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Tree

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the snow. They have a big hill near their house. They have a red sled. They take turns to ride the sled down the hill. One day, they see a big pine tree on the hill. It has green needles and brown cones. Anna says, "Look, Ben, a pine tree! Let's go see it!" Ben says, "OK, Anna, but be careful. The snow is slippery." They walk to the pine tree. They touch the needles and the cones. They smell the pine. It smells good. They are glad. They say, "We like the pine tree. It is pretty and nice." They decide to ride the sled near the pine tree. Anna goes first. She sits on the sled and pushes with her feet. She goes fast. She says, "Whee, this is fun!" But then, she slips. She slides off the sled. She falls into the snow. She says, "Ouch, that hurts!" Ben runs to Anna. He says, "Are you OK, Anna?" He helps her up. He brushes off the snow from her coat and hat. He says, "I'm sorry, Anna. The snow is slippery. You have to hold on to the sled." Anna says, "It's OK, Ben. I'm not hurt. Just a little cold and wet. Thank you for helping me." She hugs Ben. She says, "You are a good brother. I love you." Ben says, "I love you too, Anna. You are a good sister. Let's go home and have some hot cocoa. Then we can play with the sled again." They hold hands and walk home. They are glad. They have each other. They have the pine tree. They have the sled. They have fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big nose. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, the sky was gloomy, and it was going to rain. Tim's mom told him to stay inside and behave. Tim was sad because he could not play outside. He looked out the window and saw his friends playing. He wanted to go out and play too. But he remembered what his mom said. Tim decided to behave and stay inside. He found some toys to play with in his room. Soon, the rain started, and it was very heavy. Tim's friends ran inside their houses. Tim was happy that he listened to his mom. He learned that it is good to behave and listen to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play all day. One day, he went to a big station with many people. Spot was very excited to see the big station. Spot was careless and did not look where he was going. He saw a nice place to lie down and rest. But, Spot did not know that he was lying on the train tracks. The train was coming fast, and Spot was in danger. A kind man saw Spot and quickly picked him up off the tracks. The man told Spot to be more careful and not lie on the tracks. Spot learned to be more careful and always look where he was going. The moral of the story is to always be careful and not be careless, so you can stay safe and have fun.

One day, a big dog and a little cat were near a creek. The dog loved to play in the water, but the cat did not. The cat hated water. The dog said, "Come play with me in the creek!" The cat said, "No, I hate water. It's wet and cold." The dog did not understand why the cat hated water. He said, "I will show you how fun it is!" He jumped into the creek and made a huge splash. The cat got wet and was very mad. "I told you I hate water!" the cat said. The dog felt sad that he made the cat mad. He said, "I'm sorry, I just wanted you to have fun too." They found a way to play together by the creek. The dog played in the water, and the cat played with a stick on the land. They both had fun and were happy.
