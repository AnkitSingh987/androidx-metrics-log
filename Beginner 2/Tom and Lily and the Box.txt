Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Box

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They liked to pretend they were on TV and record their voices with a big microphone. They would say funny things and make silly noises and laugh a lot. One day, they found a letter on the table. It was from their dad, who was away for work. He said he missed them and he loved them and he would be back soon. He also said he sent them a gift in the mail. Tom and Lily were very happy and excited. They wanted to record a message for their dad and say thank you and we love you too. They grabbed the microphone and pressed the red button. "Hi, dad!" Tom said. "We got your letter and we are so happy!" "Hi, dad!" Lily said. "We can't wait to see your gift and hug you!" They talked for a while and then said goodbye. They pressed the red button again and smiled. They put the microphone and the letter in a box and ran to the door. They wanted to give the box to the mailman, who came every day at the same time. They thought he would take the box and send it to their dad. But when they opened the door, they saw a big truck. It was not the regular mailman. It was a man with a hat and a uniform. He had a lot of boxes in the back of the truck. He saw Tom and Lily and smiled. He said, "Hello, kids. Are you waiting for the mail?" Tom and Lily nodded. They showed him the box and said, "This is for our dad. He is away for work. Can you take it and send it to him?" The man looked at the box and said, "Sure, I can do that. But I need to check what's inside first. It's the rule." He took the box and opened it. He saw the microphone and the letter. He said, "Oh, what's this? A microphone? And a letter? How nice. Can I listen to what you recorded?" Tom and Lily said, "No, please. It's for our dad. It's a surprise. Please don't listen. Please give it back." But the man did not listen. He pressed the red button and put the microphone to his ear. He heard Tom and Lily's voices and their message. He laughed and said, "This is so cute. And so funny. I like it. I think I'll keep it. It's a nice souvenir." He put the microphone in his pocket and threw the letter in the trash. He said, "Sorry, kids. I can't send this to your dad. It's mine now. Bye-bye." He got in the truck and drove away. He left Tom and Lily with nothing. They were very sad and angry. They cried and screamed. They wanted their microphone and their letter back. They wanted their dad to hear their message. They wanted their gift. But it was too late. The man was gone. And so was their box. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a great man. He loved to play with kids. One day, he saw some kids playing ball in the park. The great man wanted to join them. He walked up to the kids and said, "Can I join you? I like to play ball too." The kids smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with us!" They were happy to have the great man join their game. They played ball together for a long time. The great man was very good at playing ball. The kids laughed and had fun with him. After they were done playing, the great man said, "Thank you for letting me join you. I had a great time!" The kids said, "Come back and play with us again!" And from that day on, the great man would join the kids at the park to play ball.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a young cat named Kitty. Kitty had soft fur and big, round eyes. She loved to play with her ball and chase her tail. One day, Kitty went outside to play. She saw her friend, the dog named Buddy. Buddy was big and had a waggy tail. They played together in the sun. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. After playing, they were very hungry. Kitty and Buddy went to their kind neighbor, Mrs. Brown. Mrs. Brown liked to give treats to the animals. Today, they received a bowl of cream to share. Kitty and Buddy drank the cream and felt happy. They thanked Mrs. Brown and went home to take a nap.
