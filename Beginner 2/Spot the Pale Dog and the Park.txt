Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Pale Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a pale dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the rain. One day, he saw a big puddle and jumped in it. He got all wet and soaked. Spot was so happy. Spot saw a shiny chain on the ground near the puddle. He picked it up with his mouth and started to play with it. He did not know that the chain was very special. One sunny day, Spot was playing with the chain in the park. Suddenly, the chain started to glow. Spot was so surprised. The glowing chain made a big rainbow in the sky. All the kids in the park saw the rainbow and came to play with Spot. From that day on, Spot and the kids played together in the park every day. Spot was never lonely again. The shiny chain had brought so much joy to Spot and his new friends.

One day, a tired cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw dark clouds in the sky. He knew rain would come soon. Tim met a frog named Fred. Fred was good at making things happen. "Hi Fred," said Tim. "Can you help me? I don't want to get wet." Fred smiled and said, "I can order the rain to stop for a while. But you must go home before it starts again." Tim was happy. Fred ordered the rain to stop. Tim walked and walked, but he was very tired. He saw a big tree and thought, "I can rest here for a little bit." Tim fell asleep under the tree. When he woke up, he saw the dark clouds again. He remembered what Fred said and ran home as fast as he could. Just as Tim got home, the rain began to fall. He was safe and dry inside. Tim thanked Fred in his heart for helping him. From that day on, Tim always listened to his friends' advice.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a jar on the ground. He picked it up and saw it was weak. Tim thought it would break soon. He went to his friend, Sue, and showed her the jar. "Look, Sue! I found a weak jar," Tim said. Sue looked at the jar and said, "Let's wave to make it stronger!" So, they both waved their hands around the jar. As they waved, something unexpected happened. The jar started to glow and shake! Suddenly, a tiny fairy came out of the jar. She said, "Thank you for waving and making me strong again!" Tim and Sue were so happy to meet the fairy. They learned that helping others can make everyone strong and happy.

Once there was a great giant. He was very tall and strong. He wanted to see how tall he was. So he asked the people around him to help him measure himself. They grabbed a tall piece of string and a marker. They stretched the string all the way up to the top of the giant's head. Then they used the marker to make a mark on the string. Once the mark was made, everyone clapped! The giant was so pleased! He was even taller than he thought. The people were amazed that the giant was so great. The giant was happy to know how tall he was. Everyone around him was full of admiration! The giant enjoyed his moment of glory and smiled.

Tom and Sue liked to play in the park. They liked to climb the big hill that looked like a mountain. They liked to slide down the grass and roll on the ground. They liked to find bugs and flowers and rocks. One day, they saw a big pile of leaves near the hill. It was brown and yellow and red. It looked like a fun place to hide and jump. Tom and Sue ran to the pile of leaves and started to dig and toss and laugh. But something was wrong. The leaves smelled bad. They were wet and soft and sticky. They had holes and spots and worms. They were rotting. Tom and Sue felt sick and scared. They wanted to get out of the pile of leaves. They tried to run, but the leaves were too heavy and slippery. They tried to call, but no one heard them. They tried to cry, but no one came. They were stuck in the pile of leaves. Then they heard a bark. It was Rex, their dog. Rex was a good dog. He was big and strong and fast. He was reliable. He always came when they called. He always helped them when they needed. He always loved them. Rex saw Tom and Sue in the pile of leaves. He knew they were in trouble. He ran to the pile of leaves and started to dig and pull and bite. He made a hole in the pile of leaves. He grabbed Tom and Sue by their shirts and dragged them out of the pile of leaves. Tom and Sue hugged Rex and thanked him. They were happy and relieved. They were safe. They decided to never play in the pile of leaves again. They decided to only play on the hill that looked like a mountain. They decided to always stay with Rex, their reliable dog.
