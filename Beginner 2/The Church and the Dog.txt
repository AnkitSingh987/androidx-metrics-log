Category: Beginner
Title: The Church and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big church. The church was very pretty. Many people went there to sing and pray. One day, a bad man wanted to steal from the church. He wanted to take the pretty things inside. A brave dog named Max lived near the church. Max saw the bad man trying to steal. Max wanted to help the church, so he barked very loud. The bad man was scared and ran away. But, plot twist! The bad man was not really bad. He was just hungry and needed help. Max felt bad for the man, so he took him to his family. They gave the man food and helped him find a job. The man was very happy, and he never tried to steal again. The town people were proud of Max for being so brave and helpful.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big wet puddle. Tim was impatient to use his new red boots. He put them on and ran to the puddle. Tim jumped into the wet puddle. He splashed and laughed. The water went up and down. Tim's red boots kept his feet dry. He was happy to use them. After playing in the puddle, Tim went home. His mom saw his wet boots and smiled. She was glad he could use them. Tim took off his boots and went inside to tell his mom all about his fun day.

---

One day, a boy named Tim found a vest in his room. He did not know where it came from. Tim wanted to examine the vest, so he put it on. The vest was very big on him, but he liked it. Tim went to show his mom. "Mom, look at my vest!" said Tim. His mom looked at the vest and said, "Oh no, Tim! That is your dad's vest, and it is bad. It has a hole in it." Tim did not want to wear a bad vest, so he took it off. Tim and his mom went to the store to buy a new vest. But when they got there, they saw a dog wearing the same vest! The dog was a magician and fixed the hole in the vest with magic. Tim was so happy, and he thanked the dog. Now, Tim had a good vest to wear.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy was a wise bird. He lived in a big tree with his family. The tree was in a pretty garden with many flowers. One day, Billy saw a shiny ornament hanging on a bush. It was round and sparkly. Billy wanted the ornament for his nest. He tried to take it, but he got stuck in the bush. Billy was scared, but he knew he needed to escape. He thought hard and had an idea. He called his family for help. They all came and helped him get free from the bush. Billy was happy to be free. He knew it was not wise to take things that were not his. He thanked his family and they all flew back to their big tree. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful snake. This snake lived in a big forest. Every day, the snake would play with his friends, the birds and the bugs. One day, the snake was playing with his friends when he saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with the ball too. So, the snake and his friends ran to the ball. They played with the ball all day long. Then, the snake had to pause. He was very tired. He said to his friends, "I need to rest now." So, the beautiful snake and his friends stopped playing. They all lay down under a big tree and took a nap. When they woke up, they played some more. They were all very happy.

One day, a big truck was driving down the road. The truck was very troubled because it was running out of fuel. The truck driver, named Tom, was worried. He needed to find fuel fast or the truck would stop. Tom saw a little boy named Tim by the road. Tom yelled, "Hey, do you know where I can find fuel?" Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, I know a place. Follow me!" Tom followed Tim to a small house with a fuel can in the yard. But when they got there, they found out the fuel can was empty! Tom was sad and scared. But then, Tim had an idea. He yelled, "Wait! I know another place with fuel!" Tim led Tom to a hidden fuel station behind the trees. Tom was so happy and surprised! He filled up the truck with fuel and thanked Tim for his help.
