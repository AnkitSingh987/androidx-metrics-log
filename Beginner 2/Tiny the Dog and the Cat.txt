Category: Beginner
Title: Tiny the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tiny. Tiny was very ugly, but he had a big smile. He lived in a small house with a girl named Sue. Sue loved Tiny very much and always gave him a big hug. One day, a mean cat came to their house. The cat did not like Tiny's smile. The cat wanted to make Tiny sad. The cat tried to take away Tiny's toys and food. Tiny was sad, but Sue knew what to do. She gave Tiny a big hug and told him not to worry. Sue and Tiny played together and shared their toys with the cat. The cat saw their smiles and felt happy too. In the end, they all became good friends and the cat learned that being kind is better than being mean.

---

One day, a little boy named Tom got a new scooter. He loved to ride it up and down the street. It was red and shiny, and he felt very happy when he rode it. One day, Tom saw a girl named Sue sitting on the sidewalk. She looked very sad. Tom stopped his scooter and asked, "Why are you sad, Sue?" Sue looked down and said, "I fell off my bike and hurt my knee. I suffer and feel ashamed because I can't ride like before." Tom wanted to help Sue feel better. He thought for a moment and then said, "You don't have to be ashamed. I can help you learn to ride again. We can practice together!" Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom." They became friends and had lots of fun riding their bikes and scooters together.

Sam and Mia were playing with their toy cars on the floor. They liked to make them zoom and crash and honk. But then Sam saw something on the window. It was a big bird with black feathers and a yellow beak. It looked angry and hungry. "Look, Mia, a bird!" Sam said, pointing at the window. Mia looked up and gasped. She was scared of the bird. She thought it might break the window and hurt them. "Let's hide, Sam!" Mia said, grabbing her car and running to the couch. Sam followed her, but he did not want to hide. He wanted to see the bird. He thought it was cool and brave. He left his car on the floor and crawled to the window. He wanted to touch the bird. "Sam, come back!" Mia whispered, but Sam did not listen. He reached the window and put his hand on the glass. The bird saw him and squawked. It pecked at the glass, trying to get to Sam. Sam was not scared. He laughed and wiped the glass with his hand, making the bird more angry. "Go away, bird!" Sam said, wiping harder. But the bird did not go away. It pecked harder and harder, until the glass cracked. Sam was surprised and stopped wiping. He saw the crack and felt the wind. He heard the bird flap its wings and fly inside. He was in trouble. "Help, Mia, help!" Sam screamed, as the bird chased him around the room. Mia heard Sam and got up from the couch. She saw the bird and the broken window. She felt helpless. She did not know what to do. She wanted to help Sam, but she was too scared of the bird. She looked around and saw Sam's car on the floor. She had an idea. She picked up the car and threw it at the bird. The car hit the bird on the head and made it fall. The bird was stunned and stopped moving. "Sam, run!" Mia shouted, as she ran to the door. Sam saw Mia and the car and the bird. He realized what Mia did. He was proud of her. He ran to the door and joined her. They opened the door and ran outside. They were safe. They hugged each other and thanked each other. They looked at the window and the bird. They felt sorry for the bird. It was hurt and lost. It did not belong in their land. They hoped it would fly away and find its home. They decided to never play with the window again. They learned their lesson. They went back inside and played with their cars on the floor. They were happy and careful. They were friends.
