Category: Beginner
Title: The Car and the Fast

Once upon a time, there was a little car. The car loved to drive fast. One day, the car felt lucky. It wanted to drive faster than ever before. As the car drove, it saw a big ear on the road. The car did not know what to do. It thought it was a funny thing to see. So, the car kept driving fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The big ear started to move! It was not just an ear, but a big, scary monster. The monster was not happy. It did not like the car driving fast. So, the monster ate the car. And that was the end of the little car's lucky day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a small garden in her backyard. In her garden, she grew many things like flowers, tomatoes, and beans. Amy was very organized. She made sure that each plant had its own spot in the garden. One day, Amy found a big bean in her garden. She was very excited and showed it to her mom. "Look, Mom! I found a big bean!" Amy said. Her mom smiled and said, "Wow, that's a very big bean! I hope it tastes good." Amy wanted to cook the bean for dinner. She asked her mom for help. They washed the bean and put it in a pot with water. They waited for the water to boil. Amy hoped the bean would be yummy. Finally, it was time to eat the big bean. Amy and her mom shared the bean for dinner. They both thought it tasted very good. Amy was proud of her garden and her big bean. She hoped to find more big beans in her garden soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Tim wanted to help his mom make a meal. They decided to make bread. Making bread was difficult for Tim. The dough was very sticky. But Tim did not give up. He kept trying and trying. Finally, the dough was ready. They put it in the oven and waited for it to rise. When the bread was ready, it had risen very big. Tim was so happy! He and his mom ate the bread together. It was the best meal they ever had. From that day on, Tim loved to help his mom cook.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a triangle. The triangle had three sides and it was very happy. It liked to play with its friends, the circle and the square. They played all day long and had lots of fun. One day, the triangle seemed sad. It was not playing with its friends. The circle and the square asked the triangle why it was sad. The triangle said it wanted to be like them, with no pointy sides. It wanted to be round like the circle or have four sides like the square. The circle and the square wanted to help their friend. They tried to make the triangle round and have four sides, but they could not. The triangle was still sad. Then, a big storm came to the quiet town. The wind blew very hard. The triangle, with its pointy sides, got stuck in a tree. The circle and the square could not help their friend. The storm got worse and the triangle was still stuck. The circle and the square were very sad. They could not save their friend, the triangle. The storm ended, but the triangle was lost. The quiet town was not the same without the triangle. The circle and the square missed their friend very much.

One day, a huge snake went to a pond to splash and play. He loved the water and the sun. He swam and splashed around, having a lot of fun. A little bird saw the snake and flew down to say hello. "Hi, snake! Can I play with you?" the bird asked. The snake smiled and said, "Yes, let's splash together!" So, the huge snake and the little bird splashed and played. They laughed and had a great time. After a while, they got tired and sat by the pond to rest. They were very happy to be friends.
