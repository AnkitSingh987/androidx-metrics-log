Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Woods and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was curious and brave. She loved to explore and adventure. One day, she decided to go on a walk in the woods. The girl was walking through the trees when she noticed something strange. It was a weapon covered in leaves. She was a bit fearful but decided to pick it up and look at it. She thought it was so cool and wanted to take it home with her. Suddenly, she heard a voice calling to her. It was an old man who was walking near her. He said, "Please put the weapon down! You might lose it if you take it with you." The little girl was very scared but she listened to the man. She slowly put the weapon down and walked away. She learned about being careful and never losing something that isn't hers.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big tree in the park. The tree has many leaves and branches. It looks cool. "Let's climb the tree!" Tim says. He runs to the tree and grabs a branch. He pulls himself up. "Wait for me!" Sam says. He follows Tim and climbs the tree too. They go higher and higher. They see birds and squirrels and flowers. "Wow, this is fun!" Tim says. He looks around and smiles. "Yes, it is!" Sam says. He looks at Tim and laughs. They sit on a branch and enjoy the view. They feel the wind and the sun. They are happy. They love the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lily. She was three years old and loved to make a mess. One day, she found her mum's purse lying on the floor. It was a big, dark brown purse and it was full of shiny coins. Lily loved playing with coins and her eyes lit up as soon as she saw them. She quickly opened the purse and scattered the coins all around the room. Her mum walked in and saw the mess Lily had made. She was so angry that she yelled at Lily and called her dirty. Lily didn't know what to do and started to cry. Her mum kissed her and told her off for being naughty. She then helped Lily pick up all the coins and put them back in the purse. She kissed Lily again and said she was still a very good girl. Lily was so happy that she hugged her mum tightly and never scatted coins around the room again.

Once upon a time, there was a big party. All the kids were happy and having fun. At the party, there was a lot of food to eat. A nice lady was there to supply the food. She gave each kid a plate with yummy things on it. One kid named Tim was very serious. He did not want to play with the others. He only wanted to eat his food. He sat down with his plate and began to eat. But then, something unexpected happened. A funny dog came to the party. The dog saw Tim's plate and wanted some food too. The dog jumped up and took Tim's plate. Tim was so surprised! He laughed and laughed. Then, he played with the dog and the other kids. The serious boy became a happy boy, and they all had fun at the party.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the park. He saw a big red ball in a tree. He tried to get it, but it was too high. Tim felt sad and said, "I want the ball, but I can't reach it." His friend, an intelligent dog named Max, came to help. Max said, "I will help you get the ball." Max tried to jump, but he couldn't reach it either. They both struggle for a week to get the ball. One day, a big wind came and blew the ball out of the tree. The ball fell on the ground. Tim and Max were very happy. They said, "Thank you, wind!" And they played with the ball all day long.
