Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Lily

Anna loved to design dresses for her dolls. She used paper, scissors, glue and stickers to make them look pretty. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Lily. Lily had long yellow hair and a charming smile. One day, Anna's mom told her that she had to go to the doctor for an exam. Anna was scared. She did not like the doctor. He always made her open her mouth and looked in her ears with a bright light. He also gave her shots that hurt. "Mom, I don't want to go to the doctor. Can I stay home and play with Lily?" Anna asked. "No, sweetie, you have to go. The doctor will check if you are healthy and strong. It will be over soon, I promise. And you can bring Lily with you, if you want," Mom said. Anna thought for a moment. She did not want to leave Lily alone. Maybe Lily could help her be brave. She nodded and hugged Lily. "Okay, Mom. I will go to the doctor. But can I wear the new dress I designed for Lily? It has pink flowers and a bow." "Of course, honey. You and Lily look very beautiful in your dresses. Come on, let's go. The doctor is waiting for us," Mom said. Anna and Lily got in the car and went to the doctor. Anna held Lily tight and hoped it would not hurt too much. She was glad she had her best friend with her.

Sam and Lily were brother and sister. They liked to play at the beach. They liked to build sand castles and look for shells. But sometimes they fought over the toys and the space. One day, they went to the beach with their mom and dad. They saw an enormous sand castle near the water. It had towers and walls and flags. Sam and Lily wanted to play with it. They ran to the sand castle and each grabbed a toy. Sam took a shovel and Lily took a bucket. "Hey, that's mine!" Sam shouted. "No, it's mine!" Lily shouted back. They pulled and tugged at the toys. They made the sand castle fall down. They made a big mess. They made the other kids who built the sand castle cry. Their mom and dad came over. They were not happy. They scolded Sam and Lily for being rude and selfish. They told them to say sorry and to share the toys. They told them to help rebuild the sand castle. Sam and Lily felt bad. They said sorry to the other kids. They gave back the toys. They asked if they could help. The other kids nodded. They smiled. They gave Sam and Lily some tools and some tips. Together, they worked on the sand castle. They used the shovel and the bucket and the molds and the sticks. They made new towers and walls and flags. They made the sand castle bigger and better than before. They had fun. They made friends. Sam and Lily learned a lesson. They learned to unite and not to fight. They learned to be kind and not to mind. They learned to enjoy the beach and not to breach. They hugged each other and their mom and dad. They thanked the other kids for letting them play. They had a great day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a big beach with lots of sand and water. A little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. Tim loved to play in the sand and watch the waves come and go. One day, Tim found a dead fish on the beach. He didn't know what to do with it. So, he called his mom and said, "Mom, look at this dead fish! Can we tie it and take it home?" His mom said, "No, Tim. We can't take the dead fish home. But we can bury it in the sand and let the waves take it back to the sea." So, Tim and his mom dug a small hole in the sand and buried the dead fish. They watched as the waves came and took the fish back to the sea. Tim felt happy that the fish could go back to its home.
