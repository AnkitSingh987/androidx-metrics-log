Category: Beginner
Title: The Clean Lion and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a clean lion. The clean lion had many friends. They played all day. The clean lion liked to keep his fur clean and shiny. One day, a small bird came to the clean lion. The small bird said, "I do not belong here. Can you help me find my family?" The clean lion said, "Yes, I will help you." They walked and walked. They asked other animals if they knew where the small bird's family was. Finally, they found the small bird's family. The small bird was happy. The clean lion and the small bird became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play by the water. One day, he saw a big surf in the sea. He wanted to go and play in the surf. Tim was a bit worried. He had never played in the surf before. He wanted to gain the courage to go in the water. Tim thought of a plan. He would ask his friend, Sam, to go with him. Sam was happy to help Tim. They went to the surf and played in the water together. Tim gained the courage to play in the surf. They had a lot of fun and Tim was not worried anymore.

One day, a nice cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a lot of junk in the park. Tom wanted to help clean the park, so he tried to catch the junk. He ran and jumped, but he could not catch it all. Tom asked his friend, a big dog named Max, to help him. Max barked and wagged his tail. Together, they started to catch the junk. They caught cans, bags, and more. They put the junk in a big box. When the park was clean, Tom and Max were very happy. They played in the nice, clean park all day. They knew they did a good job. And, all the kids who came to play said, "Thank you, Tom and Max!"

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little girl named Mia. She was adventurous and loved to play outside. One sunny day, she saw a cute bunny in the grass. The bunny had a big wink in his eye. Mia smiled and said, "Hello, bunny! Do you want to play with me?" The bunny nodded and they began to play together. They ran around the trees and picked flowers. Mia picked a yellow flower for the bunny and the bunny picked a red flower for Mia. They laughed and had so much fun. As the sun began to set, Mia said, "Bunny, it's time for me to go home. Thank you for playing with me today." The bunny gave Mia a big wink and hopped away. Mia felt happy and waved goodbye to her new friend. She couldn't wait to tell her mom about her adventurous day with the bunny.

Once upon a time, there was a big jug. It was a very nice jug. It was red and blue. The jug could hold a lot of juice. One day, the jug was very full. It had juice inside. A small boy named Tim wanted to drink the juice. He tried to get the jug to work. But the jug was too big for Tim. He was not fit enough to lift it. Tim felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny ant came to help Tim. The ant was strong and fit. The ant helped Tim lift the jug. They both made the jug work. Tim and the ant drank the juice. They became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to chew on things. He would chew on shoes, toys, and even sticks. One day, Spot found a big stick and started to chew on it. He was very happy. But, he was so clumsy that he tripped and the stick got stuck in a fence. The fence was around a jail where people who did bad things were kept. Spot wanted his stick back, so he tried to get it out of the fence. He pulled and pulled, but the stick would not come out. A kind man who worked at the jail saw Spot and helped him get his stick back. Spot was very happy and wagged his tail. From that day on, Spot was more careful when he chewed on things, and he never went near the jail again.
