Category: Beginner
Title: The Baby Girl Called Hannah and the Crib

Once upon a time there was a baby girl called Hannah. She was three-years-old and she loved her purple crib. One day, Hannah was in the kitchen with her family. She started to shout "Crib! Crib!" Her mom asked, “Hannah, what do you want?” Hannah replied, “I want to play in my purple crib.” So her mom said, “Okay, Hannah, let's go and play in your purple crib.” Hannah was so excited, she shouted “Yay!” She ran into her bedroom and jumped into her purple crib. She was so happy playing in her crib that she started to laugh and shout. Her mom smiled at her and said, “You look so happy in your purple crib, Hannah.” Hannah just kept laughing and playing until she fell asleep in her purple crib.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a happy girl who loved to play. One sunny day, Lily went to her garden to find her favorite toy pot. She wanted to have a tea party with her teddy bear, Mr. Snuggles. Lily was very impatient. She wanted to drink her pretend tea right away. She put her pot on the table and filled it with water. Then, she called Mr. Snuggles to sit with her. They both smiled and laughed as they drank their pretend tea. After they finished drinking, Lily and Mr. Snuggles played in the garden all day. They were very happy and tired. At the end of the day, Lily took her pot and Mr. Snuggles back inside the house. They said goodnight to each other and went to sleep, ready for another fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play in the sun. One day, as Tim was playing outside, he saw his shadow on the ground. He thought it was funny how his shadow followed him everywhere. "Hi shadow," Tim said, "Do you want to play with me?" The shadow nodded and played with Tim all day. They played catch and tag. Tim had so much fun with his new friend. Tim wanted to remember this day forever. He asked his mom for her phone. "Mom, can you help me record my shadow?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim." She took her phone and recorded Tim and his shadow playing. Tim was very happy to have a distant memory of his fun day with his shadow. And from that day on, Tim and his shadow were the best of friends.

Ben likes to play with his jet. It is red and shiny and makes a loud noise. He pretends to fly it in the air and zoom around the room. He likes to make his jet do tricks and flips. One day, Ben's mom says they have to go to the store. She tells Ben to put his jet away and get his coat. Ben does not want to go. He wants to play with his jet more. He thinks of a plan. He runs to his room and locks the door. He hides his jet under his bed. Ben's mom knocks on the door. She says, "Ben, open the door. We have to go now." Ben does not answer. He thinks his mom will go away. He plays with his jet quietly. He makes it fly over his pillow and under his blanket. But Ben's mom does not go away. She gets worried. She calls Ben's dad. He comes home from work. He has a key to Ben's room. He unlocks the door and opens it. He sees Ben on the floor with his jet. Ben's dad is angry. He says, "Ben, why did you lock the door? You scared your mom and me. You have to listen to us. We love you and we want you to be safe." He takes Ben's jet and puts it on the shelf. He says, "You can't play with your jet for a week. It is dead. You have to learn your lesson." Ben is sad. He cries. He says, "I'm sorry, dad. I love my jet. I just wanted to play." He hugs his dad and says, "I love you too. I won't lock the door again." He gets his coat and goes to the store with his mom and dad. He hopes his jet will come alive again soon.
