Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a best friend named Tim. They loved to play together. One day, Spot saw Tim playing with a new toy. Spot felt jealous because he wanted to play with the toy too. Spot asked Tim, "Can I play with the toy too?" Tim said, "I will permit you to play if you can catch me!" They started to run and chase each other. Spot was fast, but Tim was faster. Spot did not give up and kept trying to catch Tim. Finally, Spot caught Tim and they both fell down, laughing. Tim gave Spot a wink and handed him the toy. They played together all day long, and Spot was not jealous anymore. They were happy to share and be best friends forever.

Sara and Ben like to play in the park. They run and jump and slide on the grass. They see a big puddle of mud. Sara wants to splash in the mud. Ben does not. "Come on, Ben, let's play in the mud!" Sara says. "It is fun and wet and squishy!" "No, Sara, I don't want to play in the mud," Ben says. "It is dirty and sticky and icky!" Sara does not listen to Ben. She runs to the puddle and jumps in. She laughs and splashes and makes mud pies. She gets mud on her face and her hair and her clothes. She looks like a brown bear. Ben remains on the grass. He watches Sara and shakes his head. He does not want to get mud on him. He sees a ball on the ground. It is orange and round and bouncy. He picks it up and throws it in the air. He catches it and throws it again. He smiles and has fun. Sara sees Ben and the ball. She wants to play with the ball too. She runs out of the puddle and towards Ben. She is still wet and muddy. She reaches for the ball. "Stop, Sara, don't touch the ball!" Ben says. "You will make it dirty and muddy and icky!" But Sara does not stop. She grabs the ball and tries to bounce it. But the ball does not bounce. It slips out of her hand and falls in the mud. It gets covered in mud. It is not orange anymore. It is brown. Sara and Ben look at the ball. They are both sad. They both like the ball. They both want to play with it. But the ball is dirty and muddy and icky. They don't know what to do.

Anna and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. They hear a loud noise and feel the floor shake. They are scared and hug each other. They hear their mom and dad call their names from the kitchen. "Mom, Dad, what is happening?" Anna asks. "It's an earthquake, sweetie. Don't worry, we are safe. Come here, we will go outside together," Mom says. They run to the kitchen and see Mom and Dad holding hands. They also see some things fall from the shelves and break. They are sad and sorry for the things. "Can we help you clean up, Mom?" Ben asks. "That's very compassionate of you, Ben. But right now, we need to get out of the house. The earthquake might not be over yet. We will clean up later, okay?" Dad says. They go outside and see other people and animals. Some are crying, some are helping, some are praying. They see a big crack in the road and some broken wires and pipes. They are amazed and scared. "Look, Mom, the earth is broken!" Anna says. "Yes, honey, the earthquake made the earth move and break. But it will heal, just like a wound. And we will help each other to fix things and make them better. We are lucky that we are not hurt and that we have each other," Mom says. They hug Mom and Dad and feel safe and loved. They hope the earthquake will stop soon and that everyone will be okay. They are brave and compassionate children.

Once upon a time, in a small place, there lived a big, selfish bear. He did not like to share his toys with anyone. One day, a little rabbit came to the bear's place to play. The rabbit saw a red ball and wanted to play with it. But the selfish bear said, "No, you can't play with my ball! It's mine!" The little rabbit felt sad and started to cry. Then, a wise old owl saw what happened and said to the bear, "You should not be selfish. Sharing is good and makes everyone happy." The bear thought about it and decided to share his toys with the rabbit. The bear gave the red ball to the rabbit, and they played together all day. They had so much fun that they both forgot about being selfish. At the end of the day, the bear and the rabbit gave each other a big hug and became best friends. From that day on, the bear learned to share and was not selfish anymore.
