Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Brown Dog and the Frog

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to play with his red ball. He would run and jump all day long. Max had a friend, a little girl named Lily. They were very happy together. One day, Max and Lily were playing near a tap. Max was running and chasing his ball when it went under the tap. Max tried to get it, but his head got stuck. "Oh no!" said Lily. "Don't weep, Max. I will help you." Lily tried to pull Max's head out, but it was too tight. Just then, a small green frog hopped by. "Hi, I am Fred," said the frog. "I can help you." Fred the frog jumped on the tap and turned it. Water came out and made Max's head wet. With a little wiggle, Max's head came out of the tap. Max and Lily were very happy. They thanked Fred the frog and played together all day. From that day on, Max, Lily, and Fred were the best of friends, and they always played near the tap.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play with his friends. One day, he wanted to have a race with them. He asked his mom, "Can I have a race with my friends?" His mom said, "Yes, I permit you to have a race." Tim and his friends were so excited. They went to the park and got ready to race. Tim was very obedient and listened to his mom. He made sure everyone was safe and ready before they started the race. They all ran as fast as they could. Tim was very fast, but his friend Sue was faster. Sue won the race and they all clapped for her. Tim and his friends had so much fun. They all went home happy, and Tim's mom was proud of him for being obedient.

Once upon a time, there was a small hut in the woods. In the hut lived a nice family. There was a mom, a dad, and a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim went outside to play. He saw a big, scary bug. Tim was scared. He ran back to the hut and told his mom and dad about the bug. Mom and dad did not want Tim to be scared. They insisted that Tim should not be scared of the bug. They told Tim that the bug was just a little friend. Tim listened to mom and dad, and he was not scared anymore. Tim went back outside to play, and he even said hello to the bug.

One day, a fast dog named Max was playing in the park. He loved to run and jump. He had a big smile on his face. Max saw a girl named Lily. She had a pretty image on her shirt. It was a picture of a rainbow. Max wanted to say hi to Lily. He ran up to her and barked. "Hi, I'm Max! I like your shirt," he said. Lily looked at Max and smiled. "Thank you, Max! I like your fast running," she said. But then, Max tripped on a ball and fell down. He felt embarrass. Lily saw this and came to help him. "It's okay, Max. We all fall sometimes," she said. Max smiled and felt better. They played together in the park all day.

Once upon a time, a little girl was in her kitchen. She was looking for something but couldn't find it. So, she asked her mom, "Where is it?" Her mom pointed to the corner and said, "It's over there. Step carefully, the floor is dirty!" The little girl stepped slowly and carefully and sure enough, the kitchen was really dirty. As she stepped she looked around, and all of a sudden, she spotted it! "There it is!" said the little girl. She ran towards it and picked it up. "Oh, good, I found it!" she said. She looked around and said, "Now I just need to clean up this kitchen, it's so dirty!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very eager to go to his bedroom every night. He loved to sleep in his soft bed. One night, Tim's mommy said, "It's time for bed, Tim." Tim happily went to his bedroom and put on his pajamas. He jumped into his bed and said, "Goodnight, mommy." While Tim slept, he had a dream. In his dream, he was playing with his favorite toy, a big red ball. He laughed and had so much fun. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. When Tim woke up, he told his mommy all about his dream. She smiled and said, "That sounds like a wonderful dream, Tim." Tim was so happy and couldn't wait to dream again the next night.
