Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Hungry Dog and the Lion

Once there was a hungry dog named Max. Max liked to eat treats. One day, he went to a big park to find some treats to eat. He saw a little girl with a bag. Max thought the bag had treats. Max went to the little girl and asked her, "Can you provide me a treat? I am very hungry." The little girl said, "Yes, Max, I can help you." She gave Max a big treat. Max was so happy and ate the treat fast. But then, something unexpected happened. Max turned into a big, strong lion! The little girl was not scared. She said, "Max, you are now a lion because you asked for help and were kind. The treat was magic. Now, you can help others too." Max learned that it is good to ask for help and be kind. From that day on, Max the lion helped all his friends in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big fire in a small village. All the people were scared and did not know what to do. A kind and brave man named Tom saw the fire and wanted to help. Tom said to the people, "Do not be scared. I will lead you away from the fire to a safe place." The people trusted Tom and followed him. They all walked together, away from the fire, until they reached a big tree. They all felt safe there. A little boy named Tim was very sorry for starting the fire by accident. He said, "I am sorry for the fire. I did not mean to make it." Tom told Tim, "It is okay to be sorry, but we must learn from our mistakes and be more careful next time." The people in the village learned the importance of being careful and not playing with fire. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a purple dog. The dog had a big tooth. One day, the dog went to play in the park. He saw a cat and they became friends. They played with a ball and ran around. They were very happy. Then, the dog's tooth started to hurt. The tooth began to rot. The dog was sad and didn't want to play anymore. The cat was worried about his friend. But the cat had an idea! The cat took the dog to a magic tree. The tree had special powers. The tree touched the dog's tooth and it was fixed! The rot was gone! The dog was happy and could play again. The dog and the cat played together and were best friends forever.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big pond near their house. They wanted to go and see the fish and frogs. "Can we go to the pond, Mom?" Lily asked. Mom nodded and said, "Yes, but be careful and don't go too close to the water. And don't talk to strangers." Lily and Ben nodded and ran to the pond. They saw many fish and frogs and ducks. They threw some bread to the ducks and watched them eat. They were very happy. But then, they saw a man with a big hat and a long coat. He looked nosy and mean. He came to the pond and said, "Hello, little ones. Do you want to see something cool?" Lily and Ben felt scared. They remembered what Mom said. They shook their heads and said, "No, thank you. We have to go home now." But the man did not listen. He said, "Come on, don't be shy. I have a magic trick for you. Look, I have a big bag. Do you want to see what's inside?" He opened his bag and showed them a big snake. The snake hissed and wriggled. Lily and Ben screamed and ran away. They did not like the snake. They did not like the man. They ran back to their house and told Mom what happened. Mom hugged them and said, "You did the right thing. That man was bad. He wanted to hurt you. You were brave and smart. I'm proud of you." Lily and Ben felt safe and happy. They learned a lesson. They never went to the pond alone again. They always listened to Mom. And they never talked to strangers.
