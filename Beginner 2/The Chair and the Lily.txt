Category: Beginner
Title: The Chair and the Lily

Once upon a time, there was a big chair. It was a very nice seat. One day, a little girl named Lily sat on the chair. She loved her seat. She sat there every day. One day, Lily saw a little boy named Tom. He looked very sad. Lily asked him, "Why are you sad?" Tom said, "I don't have a seat." Lily felt bad for Tom. She wanted to give him a hug. Lily had an idea. She said, "You can share my seat!" Tom was happy. They both sat on the big chair. They were not angry anymore. They gave each other a big hug. They were happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play at the park near her house. She liked to slide down the big slide and swing on the swings. One day, Mia saw a new building at the park. It was a little building, just the right size for her. She walked over to the building and opened the door. Inside, she found a toy slide and a toy swing set. Mia was very happy. She played with the toys and said, "I love this little building! It's just for me!" Then, she heard a voice say, "Hello! Can I play too?" Mia looked around and saw a little boy named Tim. Mia said, "Yes, you can play! Let's slide and swing together!" So, Mia and Tim played in the little building all day long. They had so much fun sliding and swinging together. And from that day on, Mia and Tim became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She loved to play in her garden, it was so peaceful. She spent many hours playing and thinking. One day, when the girl was playing, a bad man came into the garden. She screamed, but the bad man did not care. He grabbed her and ran away. The girl's family looked and looked, but they could not find her. They were so sad and worried. The girl was gone, and they would never see her again. The little girl was never found. Her family always thought of her and missed her. It was not a peaceful ending for the little girl, but her family still thought of her.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red pen. He liked to draw with his pen. One day, Tim was drawing and his pen stopped working. He felt sad and hurt. Tim went to his friend, Sue. Sue saw Tim's sad face and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim showed Sue his pen and said, "My pen doesn't work. I can't draw." Sue wanted to help Tim. She offered her blue pen to him. "Here, Tim. You can use my pen to draw," Sue said with a smile. Tim was happy and thanked Sue. Together, Tim and Sue drew pictures with their pens. They had a lot of fun. Tim's hurt feelings went away, and they both enjoyed their day. And from that day on, they always shared their pens and drew pictures together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the open park. He saw a big tree and wanted to know its history. Tim asked his mom to tell him the story of the tree. Mom said, "Long, long ago, a tiny seed fell on the ground. It grew into a small plant, and then into this big tree." Tim was happy to know the tree's history. He loved the tree even more now. As they were talking, a little bird came out of the tree. The bird said, "Hi, I am the tree's helper. I have a secret to tell you. I can change the tree's history!" Tim and his mom were very surprised. The bird touched the tree, and it turned into a beautiful house for them to live in. The tree had a new history, and they all lived happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog named Spot. Spot did not like to run. He liked to sleep all day. But one day, his mom said, "Spot, we need to go to the market." Spot did not want to go, but he went with his mom. At the market, Spot saw a big cat. The cat was not lazy. The cat liked to run. The cat ran fast and took some food from the market. Spot thought the cat was not nice. Spot wanted to stop the cat. So, he started to run too. Spot ran fast like the cat. He caught the cat and got the food back. The cat was sorry and became nice. Spot was not lazy anymore. He liked to run and play with the cat. They became best friends.
