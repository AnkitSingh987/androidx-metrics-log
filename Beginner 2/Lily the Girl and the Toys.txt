Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily wanted to go on a trip with her mom and dad. They had to pack their bags with clothes and toys. Lily wanted to bring her notebook too. She liked to draw pictures in it. Lily's mom helped her pack her bag. They put the clothes in and made them fit tight. Then, they put her toys and the notebook on top. Lily was so excited to go on the trip! On the trip, Lily and her family went to the park and the beach. Lily drew pictures in her notebook every day. She had so much fun! When it was time to go home, they packed their bags again. Lily's notebook was full of happy memories.

Once upon a time, there was a playful monster named Momo. Momo loved to move and play with his friends in the big park. One sunny day, Momo went to the park to play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily said, "Momo, let's move and play tag!" Momo nodded and they began to run and laugh. They moved fast, but Momo always let Lily win because he liked to see her happy. As they played, a little boy named Tim saw them and asked, "Can I play with you and the monster?" Lily said, "Yes, come and play with us!" So, Tim, Lily, and Momo moved and played together all day long, becoming the best of friends.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to play ball with his friends. So, he went to the park where he saw a big pitch. He was very happy and decided to call his friends to play. "Hey, let's play ball on the pitch!" Tim said to his friends. They all came running, excited to play. They all took turns to throw and kick the ball. They laughed and had lots of fun. After playing for a while, they got tired. They decided to sit under a big tree and eat some snacks. They talked about their fun game and how they loved the big pitch. They all agreed to come back and play again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a large tree. This tree was very big and strong. Many animals lived in the tree. One day, it started to rain. The rain was wet and cold. The animals in the tree did not like the rain. They wanted to stay dry and warm. A little bird had an idea. The bird made a sign. The sign said, "No rain, please." The bird put the sign on the tree. The other animals saw the sign and liked it. They all hoped the rain would stop. They waited and waited under the large tree. Soon, the rain stopped. The sun came out and the animals were happy. They played and danced around the tree. The sign had worked! They stayed dry and warm under the large tree. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to run, jump and slide on the grass and the swings. They saw a big dog with brown fur and a long tail. The dog was running after a ball that a man threw for him. "Look, Lily, a dog!" Tom said. "Do you like dogs?" "Yes, I like dogs. They are soft and friendly. But I don't know this dog. Do you know him?" Lily asked. "No, I don't know him. He is not our dog. He is the man's dog. See, the man is calling him. His name is Max." Tom said. The dog heard his name and ran back to the man. He wagged his tail and licked the man's face. The man smiled and rubbed the dog's ears. He looked at Lily and Tom and waved his hand. "Hello, kids. Do you want to pet my dog? He is very nice. He won't bite you." The man said. Lily and Tom looked at each other. They were not sure. They did not recognize the man or the dog. They remembered what their mom and dad told them. They should not talk to strangers or touch strange animals. "Thank you, but no thank you. We have to go now. Bye bye." Lily said politely. She took Tom's arm and they walked away. They found their mom and dad who were sitting on a bench. They told them what they saw and what they did. "Good job, Lily and Tom. You were very smart and safe. You should never go near people or animals you don't recognize. They might be mean or sick or dirty. You should always stay with us or someone you know and trust." Their mom and dad said. They hugged Lily and Tom and gave them a kiss. They were proud of their children. They decided to go home and have some snacks. Lily and Tom were happy. They had fun in the park, but they also learned a lesson. They were not lazy. They were brave and careful.
