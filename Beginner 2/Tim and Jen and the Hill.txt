Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Jen and the Hill

Tim and Jen are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big hill. They want to climb it. "Let's go, Jen!" Tim says. "We can be like soldiers and march to the top!" "OK, Tim!" Jen says. "But be careful. The hill is very steep and rocky." They start to march up the hill. They hold hands and sing a song. They are happy and ready for adventure. But the hill is harder than they think. They get tired and thirsty. The rocks are sharp and slippery. They fall and hurt their knees and elbows. "Tim, I don't like this hill," Jen says. "Let's go back." "No, Jen, we can do it!" Tim says. "We are almost there. Look, I see a flag!" Tim sees a red flag on a stick. He thinks it is a prize. He lets go of Jen's hand and runs to get it. But the flag is not a prize. It is a warning. The flag means danger. The flag means there is a hole. Tim does not see the hole. He steps on it and falls in. He screams and cries. Jen hears him and crawls to the edge. She sees Tim in the dark hole. She is scared and sad. "Tim, Tim, are you OK?" Jen shouts. But Tim does not answer. He is hurt and alone. Jen does not know what to do. She calls for help, but no one comes. She wishes they never climbed the hill. She wishes they stayed in the park. She wishes they listened to their mom.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird named Ollie. Ollie was an ostrich. He was a very enthusiastic bird. He loved to play with his friends in the sunny field. They would run and jump all day long. One day, Ollie was playing with his best friend, a small bunny named Betsy. They were having so much fun. But then, Ollie accidentally stepped on Betsy's foot. Betsy cried and felt sad. Ollie felt very bad for what he did. Ollie said sorry to Betsy. He asked her to forgive him. Betsy knew that Ollie didn't mean to hurt her. So, she forgave him. They hugged each other and continued to play happily in the sunny field.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop and eat cereal. Every day, he would hop to the big tree and eat his yummy cereal. Bob was very happy. One sunny day, Bob found an ancient, big box under the tree. He was very curious. He opened the box and saw lots of cereal inside. Bob was so excited! He decided to eat some of the cereal from the ancient box. But as soon as Bob ate the cereal, something unexpected happened. Bob started to hop very, very high! He could hop higher than the big tree. Bob was scared at first, but then he realized he could see the whole world when he hopped high. Now, Bob hops high every day, and he is happier than ever before.

Once upon a time, there was a family. They lived in an old house. The family liked to play hide and seek. One day, they played hide and seek inside the house. Mom, Dad, and little Tim hid in different places. Tim found a big box to hide in. He sat in the box and waited. After a while, Tim heard a noise. The box started to move! It was not Mom or Dad. The box was magic! It took Tim to a new place. It was a fun room with lots of toys. Tim played there and had a great time. Then, the box took Tim back home. It was a big surprise for the family. They all laughed and played with the magic box together.

One day, Tom the cat was playing with his toy. He saw his friend, Sam the dog, walking by. Tom wanted to play with Sam, so he ran to him. "Let's play!" said Tom. Sam was happy and wagged his tail. But then, Tom's toy got stuck on Sam's thumb. Sam tried to pull it off, but it hurt. "Ouch!" said Sam. "Stop! It hurts my thumb!" Tom was sad and wanted to help his friend. He thought hard and had an idea. Tom was alert and saw a big cup of water. He put Sam's thumb in the water to make it slippery. With a gentle pull, the toy came off. Sam was happy and wagged his tail again. They played together all day, and Tom was careful with his toys.
