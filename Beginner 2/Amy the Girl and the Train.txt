Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Train

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the station with her mom. They were going to a fun party. Amy was glad because she loved parties. Before they left, Amy dressed in her favorite dress. It was red and had pretty flowers on it. At the station, Amy saw a big train. She asked her mom, "Can we ride the big train?" Her mom said, "Yes, Amy. We will ride the train to the party." Amy was very excited. She held her mom's hand and they got on the train. On the train, Amy saw a lady with a big bag. The lady opened the bag and took out a funny hat. Amy remembered her mom told her that there would be a surprise at the party. Was this the surprise? When they got to the party, Amy saw more funny hats. Everyone put on a hat and had a great time. The surprise was a hat party!

Lily and Tom were playing with their toy cars in the garden. They liked to make them go fast and make loud noises. Lily had a red car and Tom had a blue car. They pretended they were racing on a big road. But then Lily saw a shiny feather on the grass. It was white and soft and sparkled in the sun. Lily wanted to touch it and see how it felt. She stopped her car and got out. She ran to the feather and picked it up. Tom saw Lily with the feather and he wanted it too. He thought it was a treasure and he wanted to be rich. He drove his car to Lily and said, "Give me the feather, it's mine!" He tried to grab it from her hand. Lily did not want to give the feather to Tom. She said, "No, it's mine, I found it first!" She held the feather tight and ran away. Tom chased her with his car. He was angry and he wanted to catch her. But Lily was smart and she knew how to trick Tom. She ran to the fence and then she reversed. She went back the way she came and passed Tom's car. Tom was confused and he did not see her. He kept going to the fence and crashed. His car broke and he cried. Lily was happy and she laughed. She had the feather and she felt rich. She put the feather in her hair and said, "I am the queen of the garden and you are my servant. You have to fix your car and say sorry to me." Tom was sad and he said, "Sorry, Lily, you are the queen and I am your servant. Can I have the feather too?" Lily was kind and she said, "Yes, Tom, you can have the feather too. We can share it and be friends. We can both be rich and play with our cars." They hugged and they smiled. They fixed Tom's car and they played with the feather. They had fun and they were happy.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a boy sitting on a bench. The boy had a book. Lucy went to the boy and said, "I want to see your book." The boy did not answer. Lucy said again, "I want to see your book, please." The boy looked at Lucy and pointed to his ears. He was deaf. Lucy knew she needed to find a new way to talk to him. Lucy had an idea. She picked up a stick and drew a picture in the dirt. It was a picture of a book. The boy smiled and showed her the book. They sat and looked at the book together. They became good friends, even though they could not talk the same way. And that was the best day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty kid named Tim. Tim was strong and fast. He liked to run and play in the park with his friends. One day, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. He thought it would be fun. Tim was brave, so he started to climb. His friends watched and admired him. When Tim got to the top, he felt happy. He could see the whole park. He waved to his friends, and they cheered for him. Tim felt proud of himself. He climbed down and played with his friends until it was time to go home.
