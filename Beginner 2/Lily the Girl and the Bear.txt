Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a big box of toys, but her favorite toy was a small, soft teddy bear. Lily would play with her teddy bear every day and take it everywhere she went. One day, Lily's mom gave her a prune to eat. Lily had never seen a prune before. She looked at it and said, "What is this, Mommy?" Her mom replied, "It's a prune, Lily. It's a yummy snack. Try it!" Lily took a bite of the prune and made a frown. "I don't like it, Mommy. It's not yummy," she said. Lily went outside to play with her teddy bear. She saw her friend, Tim, playing with his toy car. Tim saw Lily's frown and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" She told him about the prune and how it was not yummy. Tim said, "I like prunes. Can I have it?" Lily gave Tim the prune, and he was happy. They played together all day, and Lily's frown turned into a big smile.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars on the floor. They liked to make them zoom and crash and honk. Lily had a red car and Ben had a blue car. They pretended they were driving on a big road with lots of other cars. "Look out, Lily!" Ben said. "There is a cross on the road. You have to stop or you will hit it." Lily did not want to stop. She wanted to keep zooming. She said, "No, Ben. I don't have to stop. I can go over the cross. It is just a toy." Ben was angry. He said, "No, Lily. You can't go over the cross. It is not a toy. It is a sign. It means you have to be careful. If you go over the cross, you will break the rules and make a mess." Lily did not listen. She said, "I don't care. I can do what I want. It is my car." She drove her red car over the cross and bumped into Ben's blue car. The cars fell over and made a loud noise. Ben was sad. He said, "Lily, you are mean. You broke my car and the cross. You are not a good driver. You are a bad friend." Lily felt sorry. She said, "Ben, I am sorry. I did not mean to break your car and the cross. I was just having fun. I am not a bad friend. I am a good friend." Ben said, "How can you be a good friend if you don't follow the rules and respect the signs?" Lily said, "I can be a good friend if I say sorry and fix the mess. I can also learn the rules and respect the signs. Can you teach me how to drive better?" Ben said, "Yes, I can teach you how to drive better. But you have to listen and do what I say. You have to stop at the cross and look both ways. You have to reverse if you are in trouble. You have to be careful and polite." Lily said, "OK, Ben. I will listen and do what you say. I will stop at the cross and look both ways. I will reverse if I am in trouble. I will be careful and polite. Can we still be friends?" Ben said, "Yes, Lily. We can still be friends. We can play with our cars and have fun. But we have to be good drivers and good friends. We have to follow the rules and respect the signs. That is what ordinary drivers and friends do."

One sunny day, Tom and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They saw a big, noisy crowd. Tom and Spot went to see what was happening. They saw people watch a big, colorful flag go up in the air. The flag was very pretty, and Tom liked to watch it. Then, something funny happened. A little bird flew down from the sky. The bird tried to take the flag! The people were very surprised. The bird was too small to carry the flag, but it tried very hard. Tom and Spot had an idea. They found a little flag on the ground. They gave it to the bird. The bird was very happy with the little flag. The people clapped and cheered. Tom and Spot saved the day, and everyone was happy.
