Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Clay

Lily and Ben were playing with clay in the kitchen. They had many colors of clay and they made shapes and animals with them. Lily made a big ball of clay and said, "This is my clay. I don't want to share it with you." Ben felt sad and angry. He wanted to play with the clay too. He said, "That's not fair. We should split the clay and share it. You can have half and I can have half." Lily shook her head and said, "No, this is my clay. Go away and find something else to play with." Ben did not go away. He tried to grab the clay from Lily's hands. Lily pulled the clay back and they both tugged and pulled. The clay stretched and stretched until it snapped and split in two. Lily and Ben each held a piece of clay, but they were not happy. They looked at the clay and saw that it was dirty and mixed with other colors. It did not look nice anymore. They heard a loud noise and turned around. They saw that the clay had fallen into the drain of the sink. The water was running and the clay was going down the drain. They ran to the sink and tried to get the clay out, but it was too late. The clay was gone. Lily and Ben felt sorry and sad. They had lost their clay and they had hurt each other's feelings. They said, "I'm sorry" and "I'm sorry too" and hugged each other. They decided to play with something else and share it. They found some books and crayons and sat on the floor. They read and drew and talked and laughed. They felt comfortable and happy. They were friends again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. It was a sunny day, and Tim felt hot. He saw a man selling cold drinks. Tim wanted a drink to help him cool down. He asked the man for an icy drink. The man gave Tim an icy drink, and Tim felt happy. Tim went back to play with his friends. They were all having fun. Tim put his icy drink on the ground while he played. A dog saw the icy drink and wanted to try it. The dog went to the drink and started to drink it. Tim saw the dog drinking his icy drink and laughed. Tim's friends saw the dog and laughed too. The dog finished the icy drink and looked happy. Then, the dog started to do work. The dog picked up Tim's toys and put them in a box. Tim and his friends were surprised. They did not know that the dog could do work. The dog became their new friend and helped them with their work. They all had a fun day at the park.

Tim and his mom were walking in the park when Tim saw something in the grass. It was round and shiny - a green mint. He ran over and picked it up before his mom could stop him. When she asked him what it was he said "It's a mint Mommy - it looks tasty!" His mom smiled and said: "That's right, it looks like a lump of sugar. But you should never try it. It's not a sweet treat. Tim said sorry and put the mint back in the grass. His mom said "That's ok, you have to be careful. Rolling around in the park can be fun, but it can also be dangerous - like this mint. Some things may look tasty, but it's not always a good idea to try them. It's better to be safe. Tim nodded and smiled. He learned an important lesson that day - a tasty treat isn't always the best to try.

Once there was a stubborn boy named Tom. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. They lived in a big country with lots of trees and animals. One day, Tom's mom asked him to separate his toys into two boxes. She said, "Tom, please put your big toys in one box and small toys in the other box." But Tom was stubborn and did not want to do it. He kept playing with his toys. Tom's dad saw him and said, "Tom, you need to listen to your mom. She asked you to separate your toys." Tom looked at his dad and said, "Okay, I will do it now." Tom started to separate his toys into two boxes. He put all the big toys in one box and all the small toys in the other box. When he finished, he showed his mom and dad. They were happy and Tom was proud of himself.
