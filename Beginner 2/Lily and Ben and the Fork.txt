Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Fork

Lily and Ben were having lunch with their mom. They had soup and bread and cheese. Lily liked soup, but Ben did not. He wanted to eat his cheese with a fork. "Ben, you don't need a fork for cheese. You can use your fingers," Mom said. "But I want to use a fork. It is more fun," Ben said. He picked up a large fork from the drawer. It was too big for his hand. Lily saw Ben's fork and laughed. "That fork is too large for you, Ben. You will make a mess. I recommend you use a small fork, like me," she said. She showed him her small fork, which was just right for her hand. Ben did not like Lily's laugh. He felt angry. He did not want to use a small fork. He wanted to use a large fork. He tried to stab his cheese with the large fork, but he missed. The fork slipped from his hand and fell on the floor. The cheese flew across the table and landed on Lily's soup. Lily screamed. She did not like cheese in her soup. She felt sad. She looked at Ben and said, "See, Ben? I told you to use a small fork. Now you ruined my soup. You are a bad brother." Mom heard Lily's scream and saw the mess. She felt sorry for Lily and mad at Ben. She picked up the large fork and the cheese from the floor. She said, "Ben, you should listen to Lily. She was right. The large fork is not for you. It is for grown-ups. You should use a small fork, like Lily. And you should say sorry to Lily for spoiling her soup." Ben looked at Mom and Lily. He felt sorry for Lily and ashamed of himself. He realized he was wrong. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was silly. I should have used a small fork, like you. Can I share your soup with you?" Lily looked at Ben. She felt happy that he said sorry. She said, "It's okay, Ben. I forgive you. You can share my soup with me. But next time, please use a small fork, like me." Mom smiled. She said, "That's very nice of you, Lily. And very good of you, Ben. Now, let's enjoy our lunch together. And remember, use the right fork for the right food."

Once upon a time there was a green frog. He was hopping around in the garden when he noticed a mirrored pond. He hopped nearer to take a closer look at his reflection and gasped with surprise. He saw another green frog looking back at him. His heart began to tremble. “Excuse me,” said the frog to his reflection. “Do you mind moving away? I don’t want to scare you.” The other frog trembled and shook his head. “No,” he said. “I won’t move.” The frog stayed where he was, not wanting to make the other frog sad. He smiled at his reflection. “It’s okay,” he said. “We can stay here together. It’s much more fun when we’re together!” The other frog smiled back and nodded in agreement. Together, the two frogs hopped around the pond, enjoying their reflection in the mirrored water. The end.

One day, a little boy was playing outside with his dad. He was very alert and full of energy. He ran around the garden and then he stopped by the rail. He was wondering what was beyond it. "Daddy, what’s behind the rail?" asked the little boy. "It’s a beautiful garden, my son" replied his dad. "Would you like to share it with me?" The little boy was so excited. He couldn’t wait to go explore the garden. So, they passed under the rail and went into the garden. They had so much fun! The little boy looked around in awe. There were lots of flowers and trees. His dad smiled at him and they both enjoyed the moment. Moments later, they decided it was time to go back home. As they passed through the rail again, the little boy looked back one last time, smiling at the garden. He was so happy to have shared this special moment with his dad.
