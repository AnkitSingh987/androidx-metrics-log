Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Boat

One day, a girl named Sue went to the beach with her new toy boat. She loved her boat and took care of it. Sue put the boat on the water and let it float. She tied a string to an anchor, so the boat would not go too far. Sue's friend, Tom, came to play. "Wow, nice boat!" he said. "Can I play too?" Sue said, "Yes, but be careful with the anchor. If we lose it, the boat will float away." Tom nodded and held the anchor tight. They had fun playing with the boat. But then, Tom let go of the anchor by mistake. The boat started to float away. Sue and Tom ran to catch it. They almost lost the boat, but Sue grabbed the string just in time. They both laughed and played with the boat until the sun went down.

Ben and Mia like to play near the bridge. The bridge is big and has water under it. They can see fish and ducks in the water. Sometimes they throw bread for the ducks. One day, Ben and Mia find a ball near the bridge. It is red and round and bouncy. They want to play with the ball. They take turns to kick the ball to each other. They have fun. But then, a big boy comes. He is mean and rude. He sees the ball and wants it. He runs to the ball and kicks it hard. The ball flies over the bridge and into the water. Ben and Mia are upset. They cry and shout at the big boy. The big boy laughs and runs away. He does not care. He is bad. Ben and Mia are sad. They lost their ball. They go to the bridge and look at the water. They hope to see their ball. But they only see fish and ducks. The ducks swim to the bridge and quack. They are hungry. Ben and Mia have no bread. But they have an idea. They pick some grass and flowers and throw them to the ducks. The ducks like the grass and flowers. They eat them and quack happily. Ben and Mia smile. They are happy. They made new friends. They forget about the ball and the big boy. They play with the ducks near the bridge. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab named Tim. Tim lived near the shore with his family. They lived in a tight home, but they were happy. Every day, Tim played with his friends and had fun. One day, Tim went to play with his friends, but he forgot the way back home. He walked and walked, but he could not find his house. Tim was very sad and scared. He sat down and cried. Then, something unexpected happened. A big fish came and asked Tim why he was crying. Tim told the fish he was lost. The big fish smiled and said, "I know where your home is!" The fish took Tim back to his family. Tim was so happy, and he never forgot the way back home again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, fake ghost toy. He loved to play with the ghost and scare his friends. One day, Tim had an idea. He wanted to seat the ghost on a chair and see if his friends would be scared. Tim invited his friends to play at his house. When they arrived, Tim said, "Look, there is a ghost on the chair!" His friends were scared at first, but then they saw it was just a toy. They all laughed and said, "Tim, you are so funny!" After playing with the ghost, Tim and his friends learned that it is okay to be scared sometimes, but it is also important to remember that not everything is as scary as it seems. They also learned that it is fun to share a laugh with friends, even if it is a little bit scary at first.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play outside. She liked to feel the wind on her face. The wind was always there, like a friend. It was a persistent wind, always blowing and playing with her hair. One day, Amy found a magic rock. The rock had a special power. It could glow! The rock glowed like the sun. It was so pretty. Amy liked to hold the rock and watch it glow. She showed the rock to her friends, and they all loved it too. Every night, before Amy went to bed, she would hold the rock and watch it glow. The wind would blow through her window, and it would make her feel safe. The wind, the glow, and the rock became her best friends. And they all lived happily ever after.
