Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Cheerful Dog and the Ball

One day, a cheerful dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. The ball went near a big building. Max did not know that the building was a hospital. Max followed the ball into the hospital. He saw many people in white clothes. They were talking and moving fast. Max wanted to play with them too. One person saw Max and said, "Oh no, a dog! Dogs can't be in here." Max got scared and ran away. He tried to find the way out, but he got lost. He ran into a room with a little boy. The little boy was sad and wanted to play with Max. But a person in white clothes came in and said, "Sorry, the dog has to leave." Max was taken out of the hospital. He was sad because he could not play with the little boy or the red ball. He went back home and never found the red ball again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run very fast. He liked to race with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Tim's mom gave him a gift. It was a red ball. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mom! Can I go to the park and play with my new ball?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, I permit you to go, but be back before dinner." Tim went to the park and met his friend, Lily. They played with the ball and ran fast. They laughed and had so much fun. After a while, they got tired and sat under a big tree. Lily said, "Tim, your ball is the best gift ever!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I love it so much! Let's play again tomorrow!" They promised to meet at the park and play with the red ball every day. And they did, running fast and laughing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to pretend she was a navy captain. She had a toy boat that she would play with in the bathtub. She would make the boat go fast and slow, and she would pretend to save other toy animals in the water. One day, Sue's mommy took her to a busy park. There were lots of kids running and playing. Sue brought her toy boat to the park to play with her new friends. She showed them how to be navy captains and save the toy animals. Together, Sue and her new friends had a fun day at the park. They pretended to be navy captains and saved many toy animals. When it was time to go home, Sue was happy that she had made new friends who liked to pretend just like her.

One day, Farmer Joe was planting some corn in his field. He was getting impatient, as he wanted to get the job done right away. He was raising the corn seeds in the ground, one by one. As he lifted the last seed, he heard a voice. "Why are you planting so fast, Farmer Joe?" the voice asked. Joe stopped and looked around. He couldn't see anyone. "Who's there?" he asked. An old gnarled tree nearby creaked, and out flew a little bluebird. The bird flew to a nearby branch and said, "It's me, Farmer Joe. I'm watching you plant corn. Don't rush it so much. Patience brings a happier harvest." Joe thought about the bird's words. He smiled, and went back to planting. He planted each seed a little more slowly from then on, and he smiled and chatted with the bird as he worked. Soon enough, he was finished. Months later, Joe's corn grew higher and fuller than any other farmer in the area. People would come to him for the best crops. Joe always remembered the bluebird's words, and was glad he listened. He was patient and raised the best kind of corn!

Jill and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. Jill likes to do ballet. She can spin and jump with her body. She is graceful. Ben likes to do soccer. He can kick and run with his body. He is fast. One day, they go to the park together. Jill says, "Let's do ballet, Ben. It is fun and pretty." Ben says, "No, let's do soccer, Jill. It is fun and cool." They do not agree. They get mad at each other. Jill says, "Fine, I will do ballet by myself. You are not graceful. You are clumsy." Ben says, "Fine, I will do soccer by myself. You are not fast. You are slow." They go to different parts of the park. Jill finds a big tree. She thinks it is a good place to do ballet. She starts to spin and jump around the tree. She does not see the bee hive on the tree. The bees get angry. They fly out of the hive. They sting Jill all over her body. She screams and cries. She falls to the ground. Ben hears Jill scream. He runs to her. He sees the bees and the hive. He feels sorry for Jill. He says, "Jill, I am sorry. I did not mean to be mean. Are you okay?" Jill says, "Ben, I am sorry too. I did not mean to be rude. I am not okay. The bees hurt me. Help me, please." Ben says, "I will help you, Jill. Come on, let's go to the nurse. She will make you feel better." He picks up Jill and carries her to the nurse. He hopes she will be okay. He wishes they did not fight. He wishes they played together. He wishes they were still friends.
