Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had an incredible toy called Mr. Bear. Mr. Bear was big, soft, and loved to play with Mia. They had many fun days together. One sunny day, Mia and Mr. Bear went outside to play. They found a big tree and decided to have a picnic under it. Mia spread a soft blanket on the ground. She put yummy food on the blanket for them to eat. Mr. Bear said, "This is a nice place for a picnic, Mia!" As they ate their food, a small bird flew down from the tree. The bird looked scared. Mia wanted to help the bird feel safe. She said, "Don't worry, little bird. You can join our picnic and be safe with us." The bird happily joined Mia and Mr. Bear. Mia, Mr. Bear, and the little bird had a great time at their picnic. They laughed and played together. From that day on, they became the best of friends. And the little bird always felt safe with Mia and Mr. Bear.

Lily and Ben were playing with sticks and stones in the backyard. They liked to make shapes and sounds with them. Lily found a big, rough stone that was shiny and smooth on one side. She showed it to Ben and said, "Look, this is my treasure. It is pretty and hard." Ben wanted to see the stone, but Lily did not want to share. She said, "No, it is mine. You can't have it." She held the stone close to her chest and turned away from Ben. Ben felt sad and angry. He thought Lily was not nice. He looked around and saw a dry twig on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Fine, I don't need your stone. I have this twig. It is better than your stone. It can make fire." Lily did not believe him. She said, "No, it can't. You are lying. You are silly. You can't make fire with a twig." Ben wanted to prove her wrong. He rubbed the twig on the rough stone, hoping to see a spark. He rubbed harder and harder, but nothing happened. He got frustrated and threw the twig at the stone. The twig bounced off the stone and hit Lily on the head. Lily cried out in pain and dropped the stone. She felt a bump on her head and tears in her eyes. She said, "Ow, ow, ow! That hurt! Why did you do that?" Ben felt sorry and scared. He did not mean to hurt Lily. He said, "I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I'm sorry! I was trying to make fire. I was mad at you. I wanted your stone." Lily and Ben looked at each other and realized they were both wrong. They had been mean and selfish. They had not shared or listened. They had not been good friends. They hugged each other and said, "I'm sorry. I forgive you. Let's be friends again." They picked up the stone and the twig and decided to play together. They learned that sharing and caring are better than fighting and hurting. They learned that fire is not a toy and that they should be careful with sticks and stones. They learned that friendship is more precious than any treasure.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw a huge rock near the water. Tim wanted to examine the rock, so he went closer. As Tim looked at the rock, he saw a small oyster hiding under it. He called his mom and said, "Mom, look! I found an oyster!" His mom came over and looked at it too. Together, they opened the oyster and found a pretty pearl inside. Tim and his mom were so happy. They took the pearl home to remember their fun day at the beach.

Mia and Tom were playing by the pool. It was a modest size pool. They were having lots of fun. Then, Mia saw a bigger pool in the neighbor's garden. She wanted to go and play in the bigger pool. Tom didn't want to go. Mia tried to persuade him, but Tom said he liked their modest pool and didn't want to leave. Mia got frustrated and told Tom that she wanted to go to the bigger pool. Tom wanted to stay and finish playing in their pool. He asked Mia if they could take turns, so that he could stay in their pool and play and Mia could go to the bigger pool too. Mia agreed and they took turns.
