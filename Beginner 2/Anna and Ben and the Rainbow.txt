Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Rainbow

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. But today it is raining. They are sad. "Can we go out?" Anna asks Mom. "No, it is wet and cold. You have to stay inside," Mom says. Anna and Ben look out the window. They see gray clouds and rain. They are bored. "Let's play with toys," Ben says. They play with toys for a while. But they are not happy. They want to go out. "Maybe the rain will stop soon," Anna says. They look out the window again. They see something bright and colorful. It is a rainbow! "Wow, look! A rainbow!" Ben says. They run to the door. They put on their boots and coats. They ask Mom if they can go out. "Yes, you can. But be careful. The ground is still wet," Mom says. Anna and Ben go out. They see the rainbow in the sky. It has many colors. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. "It is so pretty!" Anna says. They run and jump in the puddles. They laugh and have fun. They are happy. The rain has stopped. The rainbow is great. Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was very thoughtful and loved to play outside. One day, she found a pretty leaf in her yard. It was green and shiny. Mia showed the leaf to her friend, Tom. "Look at my leaf!" she said. Tom liked the leaf too. They played with the leaf all day, and had so much fun. They pretended the leaf was a boat, a hat, and a fan. But then, the wind blew the leaf away. Mia and Tom tried to catch it, but they couldn't. Mia started to cry. Tom hugged her and said, "Don't cry, Mia. We can find more leaves to play with." They looked around and found many more leaves. They played happily together and Mia stopped crying.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Jack. He was three years old and really wanted a new desk. His mom told him if he was a good boy, he could receive a new desk. He was so excited! One day, Jack received his new desk - it was green! He was so happy and played around it all day, imagining what he could do with it. Every day, he would sit at the desk and draw pictures, or build towers with all his toys. But, then one day, when he was sitting at his desk he felt a funny feeling in his tummy. He was feeling very sick! He had to go to bed and when he woke up, his green desk was gone! His mom told him that it was taken away to be fixed, but Jack was very sad. He never got to play with it and his mom never received the desk back. The end.

One day, a little girl named Jane wanted to choose a dress to wear. She looked in her closet and saw many dresses. She saw a red dress, a blue dress, and a yellow dress. She thought very hard about which dress she wanted to wear. "Mommy, which dress should I choose?" Jane asked her mother. Her mommy said, "Choose the yellow dress, it is bright and happy like the sun." Jane put on the yellow dress and went outside to play. She saw her friend, Tim, with a big plate of tasty cookies. Jane and Tim shared the cookies, and they were so happy. Suddenly, a bird flew down and took one of the cookies from the plate. Jane and Tim were surprised! They laughed and said, "Silly bird, you like tasty cookies too!" And they continued to eat and play, happy with their new friend the bird.

One day, Tom and Tim were playing with their toys. Tom had a toy car, and Tim had a toy plane. They were having a lot of fun. But then, Tim's toy plane broke. Tim was sad. Tom said, "Don't be sad, Tim. We can fix it with glue." So, they went to their mom and asked for some glue. Mom gave them clear glue. They were happy. Tom urged Tim, "Put the glue on the broken part." Tim did as Tom said. They waited for the glue to dry. Soon, the toy plane was fixed. Tim was happy again, and they both continued playing with their toys.
