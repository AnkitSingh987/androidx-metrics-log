Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy box with many toys. Tim liked to play with his toys all day. One day, he found a new toy. It was a big, open box with a symbol on it. The symbol was a picture of a cat. Tim loved cats, so he was very happy. He wanted to play with the cat in the open box. But the cat did not want to play. The cat would refuse to come out of the box. Tim tried to make the cat come out. He gave the cat some food. He sang a song to the cat. But the cat still refused to come out. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. Finally, Tim had an idea. He put his favorite toy, a small ball, into the open box. The cat saw the ball and started to play with it. The cat came out of the box, and Tim was very happy. From that day on, Tim and the cat played together all the time. They became the best of friends. And the open box with the cat symbol became Tim's favorite toy.

Once upon a time there was an angel. She was very pretty and wore a white dress. The angel was flying up in the sky. She was so close to the sun that she started to feel hot. Suddenly she fell out of the sky. The angel felt scared but she was not hurt. She could not get back up in the sky on her own. Suddenly a friendly voice said, "I'm here to help." The angel looked up and saw a fluffy white cloud. It had a smiling face! The cloud said, "Don't be sorry, I can take you back up in the sky if you want." The angel said yes, so the cloud lifted her up and flew into the sky. The angel smiled and waved goodbye. She was so happy that she had a new friend to help her when she fell.

One day, a small bird with a hurt wing sat on a tree branch. The bird was sad and scared. It could not fly and play with its friends. The bird began to tremble because it felt cold and alone. A big, kind bear saw the small bird and said, "Little bird, why are you so sad?" The bird told the bear about its hurt wing. The bear wanted to help the bird feel comfortable and warm. So, the bear hugged the bird gently and said, "Do not worry, I will keep you safe." As they sat together, the bear's warm hug made the bird's wing feel better. Then, something unexpected happened! The bird's wing was not hurt anymore. The bird could fly again! The bird and the bear were very happy. They learned that being kind and helping others can make good things happen.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had many toys: cars, dolls, blocks, books, and more. They liked to make noises and stories with their toys. But they did not like to clean up their toys. The living room was very messy. Mom came home from work. She was a lawyer. She helped people with problems. She was tired and hungry. She saw the messy living room and was not happy. She said to Lily and Ben, "Look at this mess! You need to clean up your toys right now!" Lily and Ben did not want to clean up. They said, "No, Mom, we are still playing. We will clean up later." They tried to hide behind the sofa. Mom did not like their answer. She said, "No, you will clean up now. It is not fair to leave your toys everywhere. What if someone trips and falls? What if the toys get broken or lost? You need to be responsible and respectful." Lily and Ben still did not want to clean up. They said, "But Mom, we are having fun. We don't like cleaning. It is boring and hard. Can't you do it for us?" Mom was very angry. She said, "No, I will not do it for you. You made the mess, you clean it up. That is the rule. If you don't clean up, you will have no more toys. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben did not want to lose their toys. They said, "Yes, Mom, we understand. We are sorry. We will clean up." They started to pick up their toys and put them in the boxes. Mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily and Ben. I am proud of you. You are good children. When you finish cleaning, we can have dinner and read a story together. How does that sound?" Lily and Ben smiled and said, "That sounds good, Mom. We love you." They hugged Mom and finished cleaning. They learned their lesson. They decided to keep their living room tidy from then on.
