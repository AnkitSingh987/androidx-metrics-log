Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Leaf

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her dad. They saw a dry, brown leaf on the ground. Mia picked it up and said, "Daddy, why is the leaf dry?" Her dad smiled and said, "The leaf is dry because it fell from the tree. When it was on the tree, it had tiny things called cells. These cells had water in them. But now the leaf has no water, so it is dry." Mia felt excited to learn about cells. She wanted to find more dry leaves and learn about their cells too. So, she and her dad went on a fun walk, looking at all the dry leaves they could find. They had a great day learning about the world around them.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that could go very fast. It was smooth and shiny. Tim liked to play with his car all day long. One day, Tim's car did not work. Tim was sad. He asked his dad, "Dad, can you help me fix my car?" His dad said, "Sure, Tim. Let's see what we can do." They opened the toy car and found a shock. A little mouse was inside! The mouse had been making the car not work. Tim and his dad laughed. They let the mouse go outside. Tim's car worked again, and they played happily ever after.

Ben was a little boy who liked to crawl. He crawled under the table, he crawled behind the sofa, he crawled in the closet. He liked to pretend he was a snake or a bug or a spy. One day, he crawled into a new place. It was a big box in the garage. He pushed the flap and went inside. It was dark and quiet and full of soft things. He felt them with his hands. They were white and fluffy and warm. He thought they were pillows or blankets. But then he heard a noise. It was a loud and harsh noise. It sounded like a growl or a roar. He felt something move in the box. It was not a pillow or a blanket. It was a big and furry and angry thing. It was a cat! The cat did not like Ben in his box. He hissed and scratched and bit at him. Ben was scared and hurt. He cried and screamed and tried to get out. But the flap was closed and he was stuck. He wished he had not crawled into the box. He wished he had stayed under the table or behind the sofa or in the closet. He wished he had listened to his mom, who told him not to go in the garage. He wished the cat would go away. He hoped someone would hear him and help him. He hoped his mom or his dad or his sister would come and save him. He hoped he would be okay. He hoped he would never crawl into a big box again.

One day, a little boy named Timmy went to sleep. He had a big smile on his face because he was excited for tomorrow. Timmy wanted to wake up early and play with his toy car. In the morning, Timmy woke up with a big yawn. He looked at his toy car and said, "Car, let's play and be silly today!" The toy car seemed to smile back at Timmy. They played together all day, making funny noises and racing around the room. At the end of the day, Timmy's mom came into the room. She saw the mess they made and said, "Timmy, you and your car had a very silly day!" Timmy smiled and said, "Yes, mom, we did!" They both laughed and cleaned up the room together.

Once upon a time, there was a big giant. He was very popular because he was so nice. He liked to supply food to his animal friends. The giant had a big bag, and every day, he would fill it with food for his friends. One day, the giant saw that his big bag was almost empty. He knew he needed to find more food for his friends. The giant went on a long walk to find food. He saw a big tree with lots of fruit. The giant knew this tree would be perfect to supply food for his friends. The next day, the giant went back to the tree with his big bag. He picked all the fruit and filled his bag. Then, he went to see his animal friends. They were so happy to see the giant and the food he brought. The giant was happy too because he could help his friends. In the end, the giant and his friends had a big party with all the fruit.
