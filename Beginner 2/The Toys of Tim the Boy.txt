Category: Beginner
Title: The Toys of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim found an unknown eraser. It was big and pink. Tim did not know where it came from, but he liked it. He took the eraser to his room and put it with his toys. Tim liked to draw with his crayons. He drew a big sun, a house, and a tree. But, he made a mistake. He wanted to fix it. Tim remembered the unknown eraser he found. He thought it could help him. Tim took the eraser and started to pull it across the paper. The mistake went away! Tim was happy. He fixed his drawing with the unknown eraser. Now, his picture was perfect. Tim loved his new eraser and used it every time he drew.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play with her toy bear, Sam. One day, Sue and Sam were playing a game with matches. They wanted to know who would win the game. Sue said to Sam, "I am fierce! I will win!" Sam did not say anything because he was just a toy. But he looked fierce too. They started to play the match game. They took turns trying to find matches. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The toy bear, Sam, moved! He found a match! Sue was very surprised. She said, "Wow, Sam! I did not know you could play!" They laughed and played the match game together. And they both felt fierce and happy.

Kenny and Ellie were playing a game. Ellie was holding a large yellow balloon in her hand. Kenny wanted to play catch with the balloon, but Ellie said no. She wanted to save it. Kenny said, "Please, can I have a turn?" But Ellie shook her head. "It's my balloon," she said. Kenny was scared to upset Ellie, so he suggested another game. They played hide-and-seek but the game was dull. Kenny wanted to play again with the balloon. "Let's try!" Kenny said. He smiled. But the smile faded away when Ellie said no again. He could feel his heart beating fast. He was starting to worry. Then Ellie spoke up, "I can show you how to make your own balloon. That way, you won't have to worry about playing with mine." Kenny was thrilled! The two friends spent the rest of the day making colorful balloons together. Kenny was very happy that his new balloon was as bright as Ellie's.

Anna and Ben are friends who like to play with cars. Anna has many cars of different colors and sizes. She keeps them in a big box in her room. Ben has only one car, a red one, that he got for his birthday. He always brings it with him when he visits Anna. One day, Anna and Ben are playing with cars on the floor. Anna picks a blue car and says, "This is my favorite car. It is fast and shiny." Ben picks his red car and says, "This is my favorite car too. It is bright and loud." Anna and Ben have fun racing their cars around the room. But then, Anna sees a yellow car in her box. She likes the yellow car too. She wants to play with it. She says to Ben, "Give me your car. I want to play with the yellow car." Ben says, "No, this is my car. You have many cars. Let me play with my car." Anna gets angry. She grabs Ben's car and says, "I don't care. I want your car. It is mine now." Ben gets sad. He says, "That is not nice. That is not fair. Give me back my car." Anna says, "No, go away. I don't want to play with you anymore." Ben runs away crying. He goes to his mom and tells her what happened. His mom hugs him and says, "I'm sorry, Ben. That was not nice of Anna. She should not take your car. She should share and be kind. Maybe she will learn her lesson." Anna plays with the yellow car for a while. But she does not feel happy. She feels guilty. She remembers that Ben is her friend and that he loves his car. She thinks that she was mean and selfish. She decides to give Ben back his car and say sorry. She puts the yellow car back in her box and looks for Ben. But she cannot find Ben. She asks her mom where he is. Her mom says, "He went home with his mom. He was very upset. He told me that you took his car and did not want to play with him anymore." Anna feels ashamed. She says, "Oh no, I made a mistake. I want to give him back his car and say sorry. Can I go to his house?" Her mom says, "Yes, you can. But you have to hurry. He might not want to see you." Anna runs to Ben's house with his car. She knocks on the door. Ben's mom opens it. She sees Anna and says, "Hello, Anna. What are you doing here?" Anna says, "I came to give Ben back his car and say sorry. I was wrong. I should not have taken his car. He is my friend. Can I talk to him?" Ben's mom says, "That is very nice of you, Anna. I'm glad you realized your mistake. Ben is in his room. You can go and see him." Anna goes to Ben's room. She sees him sitting on his bed with a sad face. She says, "Hi, Ben. I'm sorry. I brought your car back. Here it is." She gives him his car. Ben looks at her and says, "Thank you, Anna. I'm happy to have my car back. But why did you take it? You have many cars. You don't need mine." Anna says, "I know. I was silly. I wanted to play with the yellow car. But I did not need to take your car. I should have asked you or played with another car. I learned that it is not good to take things from others. It hurts their feelings. It is better to share and be kind." Ben says, "I agree. Sharing and being kind are good. I forgive you, Anna. Do you want to play with me?" Anna says, "Yes, I do. Can we play with both our cars?" Ben says, "Sure. We can race them or make a parking lot. You have a lot of cars. You are very organized." Anna says, "Thank you, Ben. You are very nice. You are my best friend." Ben says, "You are my best friend too, Anna. Let's play." The end.
