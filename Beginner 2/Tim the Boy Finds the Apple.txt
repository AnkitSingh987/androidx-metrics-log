Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Apple

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, red apple. He was very hungry, so he took a big bite and started to chew it. "Yum, this apple is so good!" he said. As he ate the apple, his stomach felt full and happy. Tim's friend, Sue, came over and saw him eating the apple. "Hey, that's my apple!" she said, looking sad. Tim felt guilty because he didn't know it was Sue's apple. "I'm sorry, Sue. I didn't know it was yours," he said. Tim wanted to make Sue happy, so he gave her the rest of the apple to chew. Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim." They both chewed the apple together, and their stomachs felt full and happy. Tim and Sue played all day, and they learned to always ask before taking something that wasn't theirs.

Jimmy was very jealous of his friends. They had packs full of fun things to do like drawing and playing games. Jimmy wanted to pack a fun thing too. He decided to take his dad’s nail. His dad had a big box of special nails. Jimmy found the biggest nail he could find and put it in his pack. Then he went outside to play with his friends. He was so proud of his nail. All of Jimmy’s friends wanted to see what was in his pack. When Jimmy showed them the nail, they all gasped. It was the biggest nail they had ever seen. Jimmy’s friends all wanted to play with the nail, but Jimmy said no. He was jealous of the nail and wanted to keep it all to himself. So Jimmy kept his nail in his pack and he was very happy. He had the biggest nail in town and nobody could take it away from him.

Once upon a time, there was a little, messy girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her toys. One day, when she was playing in the mud, she found a tiny insect. It was a little green bug with wings. Lily thought the insect was very cute. She wanted to keep it as a friend. So, she put the insect in a jar and took it to her room. The insect was not happy in the jar. It wanted to leave and go back to its home outside. Lily saw that the insect was sad. She knew that she should let it go. So, she took the jar outside and opened the lid. The little insect flew away, happy to be free. Lily felt good that she had done the right thing. From that day on, she promised to be more careful with her new insect friends.

Once there was a mother and daughter. The daughter was only three years old. One day the mother wanted to take her daughter to the museum. But the daughter did not want to go. She wanted to play instead. The mother said, "Please, please come with me to the museum. It's going to be lots of fun!" But the daughter shook her head and said, "No, I won't go! I want to quit!" The mother insisted, but the daughter quit anyway. She crossed her arms and said, "No! I won't go!" The mother was growing very envious. She asked the daughter why she didn't want to go. The daughter smiled and said, "I don't want to quit playing. I want to play all day!" The mother laughed and said, "I see. But why don't you take a break and come with me to the museum. It will be fun!" The daughter thought about it and finally said, "Ok, I'll go with you to the museum!" The mother and daughter went to the museum and played all day. They had lots of fun!

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Bob. Bob was a big and strong man. He liked to help people. One day, he saw a cat in a tree. The cat was scared. Bob thought it would be easy to help the cat. Bob started to climb the tree. A little boy saw Bob and said, "Be careful, fireman!" But Bob did not listen. He kept climbing the tree. He wanted to save the cat. As Bob reached for the cat, he slipped and fell. He hurt his leg. The little boy ran to get help. The cat jumped down from the tree and ran away. Bob was sad that he could not save the cat. He learned that some things are not as easy as they seem.
