Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Bird

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big white bird in the pond. The bird has a long neck and big wings. It is a swan. "Look, Ben, a swan!" Sara says. "It is so graceful. It swims like a dancer." Ben nods. He likes the swan too. He wants to touch it. He runs to the pond and tries to reach the swan. "Ben, no!" Sara shouts. "The swan might bite you. It is not a toy. It is a wild animal." The swan sees Ben coming. It does not like Ben. It thinks Ben is a threat. It hisses and flaps its wings. It stares at Ben with angry eyes. Ben is scared. He stops and backs away. He runs to Sara and hugs her. "I'm sorry, Sara," he says. "I just wanted to see the swan. It is so beautiful." Sara hugs him back. She is not mad at him. She understands. "It's okay, Ben," she says. "The swan is beautiful, but it is also fierce. We have to respect it. We can watch it from here, but we can't touch it." They sit on the grass and watch the swan. The swan calms down and swims away. It is graceful and white. Sara and Ben are happy. They have learned something new.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. She had a dog named Max. Sue and Max loved to play dress-up. Sue would put on pretty clothes and Max would wear hats. They had a lot of fun with their own style. One day, Sue and Max went to the park. They saw their friend, Tim. Tim said, "Hi, Sue! Hi, Max! I like your style." Sue and Max smiled. They played with Tim for a while. Then, they saw a big box. The box had a sign that said, "Free toys for all kids. Take what you like." There were many available toys inside. Sue, Max, and Tim looked at the toys. They saw a big bear with a hat. The bear looked like Max. Sue said, "Look, Max! This bear is like you!" Max barked and wagged his tail. But then, the bear moved! It was not a toy bear. It was a real bear! The bear said, "Hi, I am Ben! I like your style too." Sue, Max, and Tim were surprised. They did not recognize it was a real bear. Now, they had a new friend to play dress-up with. They all laughed and played together.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there lived a powerful king. This king had many things, but he did lose something very important. He lost his special hat. He looked and looked but could not find it. One day, a little girl found the king's hat. She did not know it was his hat, so she wore it. The hat made her feel powerful and strong. The people in the nation saw her and thought she was a new queen. The king saw the little girl with his hat and was happy. Instead of being mad, he let her keep it. Now, the nation had a powerful king and a strong little queen. They worked together to make their nation even better. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a thoughtful cat named Fluffy saw a big, cozy armchair. The armchair was in a warm house. Fluffy wanted to sit on the armchair, but she was scared. She whispered to herself, "I want to sit, but what if the owner of the house does not like me?" Fluffy saw a little mouse named Squeaky near the house. Fluffy was nice to Squeaky and asked, "Can you help me? I want to sit on the armchair, but I am scared." Squeaky nodded and whispered, "I know the owner. They are very kind. I will ask them." Squeaky went inside the house and talked to the owner. The owner smiled and said, "Yes, Fluffy can sit on the armchair. I like nice cats." Squeaky went back to Fluffy and told her the good news. Fluffy was so happy and thanked Squeaky. Fluffy and Squeaky became good friends. They played and shared the armchair every day. They learned that being kind and asking for help is good. And the owner of the house was happy to see them happy too.
