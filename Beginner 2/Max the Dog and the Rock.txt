Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to go for a walk every day. He liked to feel the sun on his fur and sniff the flowers. Max had a lot of fun on his walks. One day, Max saw a big, hard rock on his walk. He wanted to play with it, but he knew he should resist. Max's mom always told him not to play with hard things because he could get hurt. Max tried very hard to resist, but it was not easy. In the end, Max was a good dog and did not play with the hard rock. He went back to his walk and found a soft ball to play with instead. Max learned that it was better to listen to his mom and stay safe. He was happy and had a great walk.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was an attic. In the attic, there was a seat. The seat was very old and dusty. One day, a little girl named Amy went up to the attic. She saw the seat and thought it was very special. She sat on the seat and started to feel a little selfish. She didn't want anyone else to sit on the seat. Amy told her friends about the seat in the attic. They all wanted to see it too. But Amy didn't want to share the seat. She said, "No, it's mine!" Her friends were sad. Amy's mom saw her friends were sad. She told Amy, "You should share the seat with your friends. It's not nice to be selfish." Amy thought about it and said sorry to her friends. She let them all sit on the seat in the attic. They all played together and had fun. And from that day on, Amy learned to share and not be selfish.

One day, a big, hairy dog named Max went to the park. He saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing a game with his ball. Max wanted to play the game too, so he went to Tim. Max said, "Can you tell me how to play your game?" Tim said, "Yes, I can tell you. You just need to catch the ball when I throw it." So, Max and Tim played the game together. They were having lots of fun. But then, another big, hairy dog named Sam came to the park. Sam saw Max and Tim playing the game. Sam wanted the ball for himself. Sam ran very fast and took the ball away from Tim. Max said, "No, Sam! That is not nice!" But Sam did not listen. Sam ran away with the ball. Tim was sad, and Max was sad too. They could not play their game anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a weak rope. The rope lived in a big park. Every day, kids came to the park to play and do fun things. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the park. He saw the weak rope and wanted to play with it. Tim picked up the rope and tried to jump with it. But the rope was too weak to jump. Tim was sad. He wanted to help the rope. So, he tied the weak rope to a strong rope. Now, the weak rope was not weak anymore. Tim and his friends could jump and do fun things with the rope. The rope was happy and so were the kids. They played in the park every day and had a great time.

One day, a big dog named Max and a small cat named Lily were playing in the yard. Max had a long tail and Lily had a short tail. They liked to chase each other's tails and play all day. Max said to Lily, "I dare you to bite my tail!" Lily thought it was a fun game, so she said, "Okay, Max!" Lily ran and bit Max's tail, but not too hard. Max laughed and said, "That was fun, but now it's my turn to bite your tail!" Just as Max was about to bite Lily's tail, a big wind came and blew a big leaf right into Max's mouth! Max tried to bite Lily's tail, but he bit the leaf instead. The leaf tasted bad, and Max made a serious face. Lily laughed and said, "Max, you look so serious!" Max and Lily laughed and played some more, but they decided not to bite each other's tails anymore. They learned that sometimes, unexpected things can happen when you play, and it's important to be careful and have fun.
