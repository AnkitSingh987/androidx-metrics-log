Category: Beginner
Title: The Frog Was Jumping and the Pond

One day, a little frog was jumping near a pond. He saw a shiny bracelet on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and jumped back to his friends. "Look what I found!" said the frog to his friends. "It's so pretty!" His friends looked at the bracelet and thought it was very nice. They all wanted to wear it. One by one, the frog's friends jumped and tried to put on the bracelet. But they were all too tired from jumping all day. They decided to take turns wearing the bracelet. The next day, the little frog and his friends played with the bracelet. They jumped and laughed, and had a great time. They were happy they found the bracelet and could share it with each other.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a different ticket on the ground. It was shiny and had a picture of a fun park on it. She was very happy and wanted to go to the fun park. Lily asked her mom if they could go to the fun park with the ticket. Her mom said yes, and they went to the park together. When they got to the park, Lily saw a big slide and a swing. She was so excited that she started to skip. Lily skipped all around the park, playing on the slide and the swing. Her mom watched and clapped as Lily had fun. When it was time to leave, Lily gave her mom a big hug. They went home, and Lily put the different ticket on her wall to remember the fun day.

One day, a lively cat and a big dog were in a park. They liked to play and run. They were good friends. But today, they had a problem. They found a ball and both wanted to play with it. The cat said, "I saw it first!" The dog said, "No, I saw it first!" They disagree with each other. A little bird was watching them. The bird knew the park's law. The law said, "Share all the toys in the park." The bird told the cat and the dog about the law. The cat and the dog did not know about the law. They were surprised. The cat and the dog decided to follow the law. They played with the ball together. They took turns and had fun. The bird joined them too. They all became better friends. They learned that sharing made them happy.

One day, a little fish wanted to swim far away. It swam and swam in the big water. The fish was looking for a carrot to eat. It liked carrots a lot. The fish asked a big fish, "Do you have a carrot?" The big fish said, "No, I don't have a carrot. I like to eat other fish." The little fish swam away fast. It did not want to be eaten. Then, the little fish found a nice frog. The frog had a carrot. The frog said, "Here, you can have my carrot. I have more carrots to eat." The little fish was very happy. It had a carrot to eat and a new friend.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big pile of junk near the swings. Sara wants to skip over the junk and go to the swings. Ben wants to look at the junk and find something fun. "Come on, Sara, let's skip over the junk and go to the swings!" Ben says. "No, Ben, let's look at the junk and find something fun!" Sara says. They argue and get mad at each other. They do not want to play together anymore. A man comes to the park. He works for the city. He has a big truck and a shovel. He wants to clean up the junk and make the park nice. "Hello, kids, I'm here to clean up the junk. Do you want to be helpful and help me?" he says. Sara and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry for being mad. They want to be helpful and make the park nice. "Yes, sir, we want to be helpful and help you!" they say. They help the man pick up the junk and put it in the truck. They find some toys and books and clothes in the junk. The man says they can keep them if they want. "Thank you, kids, you are very helpful and kind. You can keep these things if you want. Maybe you can share them with each other and play together," he says. Sara and Ben smile and hug each other. They say thank you to the man and take the things. They skip to the swings and play together. They are happy and friends again.
