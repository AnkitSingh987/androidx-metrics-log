Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Tall Boy and the Sun

One day, a tall boy named Tim went to the pool. He liked to swim and play with his friends. The sun was hot, and the water was cool. Tim saw his friend, Sam, by the pool. They were so happy to see each other. "Let's jump in the pool!" said Tim. Sam laughed and agreed. They both ran and jumped into the pool. They splashed and played in the water. They had so much fun. But then, the sky got dark, and it started to rain. Tim and Sam had to leave the pool. They were sad and wet as they walked home. The fun day at the pool was over.

One day, a boy named Tim put on his uniform. He was very happy. He wanted to let his friends see it. So, he went outside to play with them. They all played a game where they tried to jump high. Tim jumped so high that he could touch the sky. His friends were very surprised. They wanted to jump high too. So, they all kept playing and jumping. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Tim away. His friends were sad. They could not play with Tim anymore. The wind took Tim far away, and he never came back. The end.

Ben and Lily were twins who loved to sing. They had gifted voices that made people happy. They sang at home, at school, and at church. They also liked to write notes to each other and to their friends. One day, they found a sharp pencil on the floor. Ben picked it up and said, "Look, Lily, I can write a note with this." He wrote, "I love you, Lily" on a piece of paper and gave it to her. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Ben. I love you too." She wanted to write a note back, but she did not have a pencil. She saw a crayon on the table and grabbed it. She wrote, "You are the best, Ben" on another piece of paper and gave it to him. Ben looked at the note and frowned. He said, "Lily, this is not a good note. You used a crayon, not a pencil. A crayon is not sharp. A pencil is sharp. A sharp note is better than a crayon note." Lily felt sad and hurt. She said, "But, Ben, I did not have a pencil. I only had a crayon. I wanted to write a nice note to you. I did not mean to make a bad note. I thought you would like it." Ben realized he was wrong. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. You are right. It does not matter what you use to write a note. What matters is what you say in the note. And you said something very nice to me. I do like it. I like it a lot. You are the best, Lily." They hugged and said sorry to each other. They learned that being kind and grateful is more important than being sharp and perfect. They sang a song together and shared their notes with their friends. Everyone liked their notes and their voices. They were happy and gifted twins.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a tall crane. The crane was very tall and could reach the sky. It helped people build big houses and lift heavy things. One day, a little girl named Anna saw the tall crane and wanted to see it up close. She walked up to the crane and looked at it. Anna saw a man who worked with the crane. She said, "Hello, can you please show me how the tall crane works?" The man smiled and said, "Of course, I can show you." The man showed Anna how the tall crane could lift things up high. Anna was very happy and said, "Thank you for showing me the tall crane. It is so cool!" The man was happy to help and said, "You're welcome, Anna. Please come back anytime you want to see the tall crane again." And from that day, Anna and the man became good friends, and she would visit the tall crane often.
