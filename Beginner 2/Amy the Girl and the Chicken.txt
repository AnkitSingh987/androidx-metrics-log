Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Chicken

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a new plate with a picture of a cat on it. Today, her mom made her a yummy dinner with chicken, rice, and broccoli. Amy did not like broccoli, but her mom told her it was good for her. Amy's mom said, "Amy, please eat your broccoli. It will make you big and strong." Amy did not want to eat it, but she wanted to be big and strong. She tried to resist eating the broccoli, but she knew her mom was right. Amy took a small bite of the broccoli. It was not as bad as she thought. She ate more and more until it was all gone. Her mom was happy, and Amy felt proud. Now she knew that trying new things could be good.

Once upon a time, there was a gloomy otter. He lived by a big pond. The otter was sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, a little bird flew by and saw the sad otter. "Hi, otter! Why are you so sad?" asked the little bird. The otter looked up and said, "I have no friends to play with. What can I do?" The little bird thought for a moment and said, "Let's play together! We can do fun things by the pond." The otter was happy, and they played all day long. But as the sun went down, the sky turned dark. The little bird had to go home. The otter was sad again, and he cried. His new friend was gone, and he was all alone by the big pond. And so, the gloomy otter stayed sad, with no one to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside. One day, she went to a pretty stream near her house. She liked to watch the fish swim in the water. The stream was very impressive. Mia saw a big rock next to the stream. She wanted to finish her drawing of the stream on the rock. She took out her crayons and started to draw. Mia was very happy with her drawing. But then, something unexpected happened! The big rock started to move! It was not a rock, it was a big turtle! The turtle looked at Mia's drawing and liked it. Mia and the turtle became good friends. They played together by the stream every day.

One day, a boy named Mark went to play. He saw an icy hill. Mark wanted to play on the icy hill. He put on his coat and hat. Mark was happy to play. Mark met a big cat on the icy hill. The big cat said, "I can play too." Mark and the big cat played on the icy hill. They had fun together. They went up and down the hill. Then, the big cat said, "I am not a cat." Mark was surprised. The big cat turned into a dog. The dog said, "I am your new friend." Mark laughed and played with the dog. They had more fun on the icy hill.

Once upon a time, there was a little square. The square was very healthy and strong. It liked to talk to its friends, the circle and the triangle. One day, the square heard that a big storm was coming. It told the circle and the triangle, but they did not believe it. The square felt sad but thought of a plan to help its friends. The square found a big, strong tree and told the circle and the triangle to hide under it. They listened this time. When the storm came, the tree kept them safe. The square, the circle, and the triangle were happy and thanked the square for talking to them about the storm.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a big bucket of water and a hose. They wanted to make a fountain. "Look, Anna, I can make the water go high!" Ben said. He turned on the hose and pointed it at the sky. "Wow, Ben, that's cool!" Anna said. She clapped her hands and laughed. But then, the hose started to make a funny noise. It had a leak. Water was coming out of a hole in the hose. "Oh no, Ben, the hose is broken!" Anna said. She saw the water dripping from the hose. Ben tried to fix the hose. He put his finger on the hole. But the water was too strong. It pushed his finger away. "Ouch, Anna, the water hurts!" Ben said. He dropped the hose and rubbed his finger. Anna picked up the hose. She wanted to help Ben. She wrapped a cloth around the hole. But the cloth was not enough. The water soaked the cloth and kept leaking. "Anna, the cloth is not working!" Ben said. He saw the water wetting the cloth. Anna and Ben looked at each other. They did not know what to do. They felt sad. They wanted to play with the hose, but the hose was broken. They heard a voice from the house. It was their mom. "Anna, Ben, come inside, it's time for lunch!" Mom said. Anna and Ben dropped the hose and ran to the house. They hoped Mom would not be angry. They hoped Mom would fix the hose. They smelled something in the kitchen. It was soup. Mom had made soup for them. "Here you go, Anna, Ben, some warm soup for you!" Mom said. She gave them each a bowl and a spoon. Anna and Ben tasted the soup. It was bitter. They did not like it. "Mom, the soup is yucky!" Anna said. She made a face and pushed the bowl away. "Mom, the soup is bad!" Ben said. He did the same. Mom looked at them. She was surprised. "What do you mean, the soup is bad? I made it with love!" Mom said. Anna and Ben looked at Mom. They felt sorry. They knew Mom had worked hard to make the soup. They did not want to hurt Mom's feelings. They picked up their bowls and spoons. They tried to eat the soup. They struggled to swallow the bitter soup. They hoped Mom would not notice. Mom smiled at them. She was proud of them. "Good job, Anna, Ben, you are eating your soup!" Mom said. "You know, sometimes things are not perfect, but we have to make the best of them. Like the hose. I know it has a leak, but we can fix it later. Or we can find another way to play with the water. The important thing is that we have fun and we love each other." Anna and Ben nodded. They understood. They smiled at Mom. They loved Mom. They finished their soup. They hugged Mom. They thanked Mom. They went back to the garden. They found a new way to play with the water. They were happy.
