Category: Beginner
Title: The Red House and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a big open field, there was a little red house. In this house, there lived a happy boy named Tim and his dog, Spot. They loved to play outside under the warm sun. One day, Tim's mom told him to send a letter to his grandma. Tim wrote a nice letter and drew a picture of the sun. He put the letter in an envelope and went outside with Spot to mail it. Tim and Spot walked to the mailbox, and Tim put the letter inside. They waved goodbye to the letter as it went on its journey to grandma's house. Then, they went back to the open field to play under the sun until it was time for dinner.

One day, Tom and his friend, Sam, went to play golf. They were very excited. The sun was shining bright, and the birds were singing. As they played, a small frightened bird flew by. Tom asked, "What do you do, little bird?" The bird said, "I am scared of the big cat. Can you help me?" Tom and Sam wanted to help the bird, so they made a plan. They took a big golf ball and threw it at the cat. The cat got scared and ran away. The little bird was so happy. Tom and Sam felt good because they helped their new friend. And from that day on, they always played golf with the little bird.

There once was a boy named Tim. Tim had a job. His job was to shake a big tree. The tree had rare red apples. Tim loved his job. One day, Tim went to shake the tree. He shook it very hard. The tree started to shake, but no apples fell. Tim was sad. He wanted the rare red apples. Then, something unexpected happened. The tree talked to Tim! The tree said, "I will give you apples if you dance." Tim danced and danced. The tree laughed and gave Tim the rare red apples. Tim was happy, and he loved his job even more.

One day, a lively cat named Max went to the beach. Max loved to play in the sand and watch the waves. He liked to chase the birds and jump in the water. As Max was playing, he saw an ice cream cone in the sand. Max was very happy because he loved ice cream. He picked up the ice cream cone and started to eat it. But the sun was very hot, and the ice cream started to melt. Suddenly, a big wave came and washed away Max's ice cream cone. Max was sad, but then he saw a new ice cream truck by the beach. Max ran to the truck and got a new ice cream cone. This time, Max ate his ice cream in the shade, so it didn't melt.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there lived a king. The king was very angry because he could not find his drink. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. The king asked his friends, the cat and the dog, to help him find his drink. They looked high and low, but they could not find it either. One day, while they were looking for the drink, they met a big bird. The bird said, "I know where your drink is! Follow me!" So, the king, the cat, and the dog followed the bird. They walked and walked until they reached a small house. When they went inside the house, they saw a little girl. The little girl was holding the king's drink! She said, "I found this drink and I wanted to give it back to you." The king was very happy, and he was not angry anymore. The king, the cat, and the dog thanked the little girl and went back to the castle. From that day on, they all became good friends and had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had big ears. One day, Max went to work with his mom. Max's mom worked at a big store. Max was very happy to go with her. At the store, Max saw a cat. The cat was not nice. The cat said, "You have terrible ears!" Max felt sad. He did not like the mean cat. Max wanted to go home. But then, something unexpected happened. Max's big ears heard a little girl crying. Max went to help her. The little girl was lost. Max used his big ears to listen for her mom. Max found her mom and the little girl was happy. The cat saw this and said, "I'm sorry, Max. Your ears are not terrible. They are great!" Max felt happy again, and he knew his ears were special.
