Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a reliable dog who loved to play with his boy, Tim. They would run and jump and have fun all day long. Spot was a good dog and he always stayed by Tim's side. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park. They played with a ball and threw it back and forth. But then, Spot saw a big cake on a table. It smelled so good! Spot wanted to eat the cake, but he knew it was not his. He did not want to spoil the cake for the people at the park. Spot looked at the cake and then at Tim. He knew he should not eat the cake, so he walked away. He went back to Tim and played with the ball. Spot was a good dog and did not spoil the cake. He knew that being a reliable friend was more important than eating the cake. And at the end of the day, Tim gave Spot a treat to eat. It was a heel from a loaf of bread, and Spot loved it. They were happy and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful cat. She had a long, soft tail. One day, she went for a walk in the park. In the park, she met a little boy. The boy saw the cat's tail and wanted to touch it. He asked the cat, "Can I touch your tail?" The cat said, "Yes, you can touch my tail. But be gentle." The boy was very happy. He touched the cat's tail softly. It felt nice and warm. They became good friends. They played together in the park every day. The boy always touched the cat's beautiful tail, and they both had lots of fun.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play together. One day, they want to visit the farm. The farm has many animals. Lily and Ben like animals. They ask their moms to take them to the farm. Their moms say yes. They get in the car and drive to the farm. They see a big gate. The gate has a sign. The sign says "Welcome to the farm". Lily and Ben are happy. They get out of the car and open the gate. They walk into the farm. They see cows, sheep, pigs, chickens, and horses. They hear the animals make noises. They say hello to the animals. They go to the cow. The cow is big and black and white. The cow says "moo". Lily and Ben touch the cow. The cow is soft and warm. They go to the sheep. The sheep is small and white and fluffy. The sheep says "baa". Lily and Ben hug the sheep. The sheep is woolly and cozy. They go to the pig. The pig is pink and fat and dirty. The pig says "oink". Lily and Ben laugh at the pig. The pig is funny and snorty. They go to the chicken. The chicken is brown and red and feathery. The chicken says "cluck". Lily and Ben feed the chicken. The chicken is hungry and pecky. They go to the horse. The horse is tall and brown and shiny. The horse says "neigh". Lily and Ben stroke the horse. The horse is smooth and strong. The farmer comes and says hello. He asks them if they want to ride the horse. Lily and Ben say yes. They are excited. The farmer helps them get on the horse. He holds the horse's reins. He walks with the horse around the farm. Lily and Ben feel the horse's back move. They see the farm from high up. They wave to the animals. They smile and giggle. They have a fine time at the farm. They thank the farmer and the horse. They say goodbye to the animals. They close the gate. They get in the car and drive back home. They tell their moms about their visit. They say they want to go to the farm again. Their moms say maybe. Lily and Ben are happy. They are friends. They like to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, tough bear named Ben. Ben lived in the woods near a big tree. Ben had a best friend named Bee. Bee lived in a hive in the big tree. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, Ben and Bee found a box. They did not know what was in the box. Ben said, "Let's bury the box and find it later." Bee agreed. They dug a hole and buried the box under a small tree. Days went by, and the small tree grew bigger and bigger. Ben and Bee almost forgot about the box. One day, they saw the big tree and remembered the box they buried. They dug up the box and found a big, sweet honeycomb inside. Ben and Bee shared the honeycomb and were very happy.
