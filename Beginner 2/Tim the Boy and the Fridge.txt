Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fridge

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He was hungry. He saw the fridge and opened it. Inside the fridge, there were many yummy things to eat. Tim took out a big cake. Tim's mom saw him with the cake. She looked at him with a guilty face. She said, "Tim, I was going to offer that cake to our friends tonight. Can you please put it back in the fridge?" Tim felt bad. He said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I didn't know." He put the cake back in the fridge. Then he asked, "Can I have something else to eat?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. How about a sandwich?" She made him a sandwich and they ate together. Tim was happy, and he learned to ask before taking something from the fridge.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little bird named Billy. Billy lived in a big tree with his parent. They were very happy together. One day, Billy's parent told him it was time to learn how to fly. Billy was scared, but he knew he could do it. He spread his wings wide and jumped from the tree. At first, he fell, but then he started to fly! Billy felt so free and happy. As Billy flew, he saw his parent watching him from the tree. The parent was so proud of their cute little bird. They knew that Billy could do anything he set his mind to. And from that day on, Billy and his parent had many more fun adventures together.

One day, a big dog and a little cat lived in a house. They did not like to play together. They were not friends. But one day, they saw an open door. They both wanted to go out and play. The dog and the cat went outside. They saw a hurt bird on the ground. The bird could not fly. The dog and the cat wanted to help the bird. They decided to unite and work together to help the bird. The dog and the cat found a soft bed for the bird to rest. They gave the bird food and water. After some days, the bird felt better. The bird could fly again. The dog and the cat were happy. They learned that when they unite, they can do good things. Now, the dog and the cat are friends.

Tim and Kim are twins who like to play in the park. One day, they see a big flag on a pole. The flag is red, white and blue, and it moves in the wind. "Look, Tim, a flag!" Kim says. "Let's go and touch it!" They run to the flag and try to reach it, but it is too high. They jump and stretch, but they cannot touch it. "Maybe we can climb the pole," Tim says. He wraps his arms around the pole and tries to pull himself up, but he slides down. "It's too slippery," he says. "And too hard." Kim has an idea. She sees a bench near the pole. She runs to the bench and drags it to the pole. She climbs on the bench and stands on her toes. She is very flexible and can bend her body in different ways. "I can touch it!" she says. She reaches out and touches the flag with her fingers. It feels soft and smooth. "Good job, Kim!" Tim says. He climbs on the bench too and touches the flag with his hand. He smiles and says, "It's beautiful." They hear a voice behind them. It is a man in a uniform. He looks angry. "Hey, you two, get down from there!" he says. "You are not supposed to touch the flag. It is a sign of respect. You should kneel and salute it, not climb on it." Tim and Kim are scared. They get down from the bench and kneel on the ground. They do not know how to salute, so they just put their hands on their hearts. "We are sorry, sir," Tim says. "We did not know. We just wanted to see the flag." The man in the uniform softens his voice. He kneels down and looks at them. "I understand, kids," he says. "You are curious and adventurous. That's good. But you also need to learn some rules and manners. The flag is not a toy. It is a symbol of our country and our freedom. It deserves our honor and gratitude. Do you understand?" Tim and Kim nod. They feel sorry for what they did. "Yes, sir," they say. The man in the uniform smiles and stands up. He helps them up and gives them a hug. "It's okay, kids," he says. "You made a mistake, but you learned from it. That's how you grow and become better. Now, let me show you how to salute the flag properly." He teaches them how to stand straight, look at the flag, and raise their right hand to their forehead. He tells them to say the pledge of allegiance with him. Tim and Kim follow his instructions. They feel proud and happy. They salute the flag and say the pledge of allegiance with him. They learn a new way to show respect and love for their country and their flag. They also learn a new word: salute.
