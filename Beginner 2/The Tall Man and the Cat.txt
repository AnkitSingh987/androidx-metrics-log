Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Man and the Cat

One day, a tall man went to the river. He had a big raft. The man wanted to go on the raft and have fun in the water. The sun was hot and the water was cool. The tall man was very happy. As the tall man was on the raft, he heard a ring. It was a bell from a small boat. The boat was coming near the tall man's raft. The tall man was surprised. He did not know who was in the boat. When the boat came close, the tall man saw a little cat. The cat was ringing the bell. The cat said, "Hello, I am a talking cat". The tall man was very shocked. He never saw a talking cat before. The cat and the tall man became friends and played in the water all day.

Sam and Lily are best friends. They love to play together every day. They like to run, jump, sing, and draw. They have a lot of fun. One day, Sam and Lily go to the park with their moms. They see a huge slide. It is very tall and shiny. Sam and Lily want to try it. They climb up the stairs and sit on the slide. They hold hands and smile. "Are you ready?" Sam asks. "Yes!" Lily says. They slide down very fast. They feel the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They laugh and scream. They land on the soft grass. They hug each other. "That was awesome!" Sam says. "I agree!" Lily says. They run back to the stairs and slide again. They do it many times. They have a blast. Their moms watch them and smile. They are happy to see their kids having fun. They love them very much. They stay at the park until it is dark. They say goodbye to the slide and each other. They promise to play again soon. Sam and Lily go home with their moms. They are tired but happy. They had a great day. They love the park. They love the slide. They love their moms. And they love each other. They are best friends.

Sara was a small girl who liked to play dress-up. She had a big box of clothes and hats and shoes that she could try on. One day, she found a white veil in the box. It was long and soft and shiny. She put it on her head and pretended to be a bride. She walked around the room, holding a bunch of flowers. She felt very pretty and happy. She wanted to show her mom how she looked. She ran to the door and opened it. But she did not see the toy car on the floor. She stepped on it and lost her balance. She started to fall. "Mommy, mommy, look at me!" she cried as she fell. Her mom heard her and turned around. She saw Sara falling and ran to catch her. She hugged her and checked if she was hurt. "Are you okay, my sweetie?" she asked. Sara nodded. She was not hurt, but she was scared. She felt tears in her eyes. She looked at her veil. It was torn and dirty. "My veil, my veil, it's ruined!" she sobbed. Her mom kissed her and wiped her tears. She took the veil and smiled. "It's okay, honey. It's just a veil. You can still play with it. Or we can find another one. You are still my beautiful bride. And I love you very much." Sara smiled and hugged her mom. She felt better. She did not care about the veil anymore. She cared about her mom. And her mom cared about her. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the mud. One day, he found a yummy apple on the ground. He picked it up and wanted to eat it. Before he could eat the apple, his mom saw him. She said, "Tim, you need to wash the apple before you eat it." She gave him a little lecture about how washing fruits keeps us from getting sick. Tim listened to his mom and washed the apple. After washing it, he took a big bite. The apple was very yummy! Tim was happy he listened to his mom and learned something new.
