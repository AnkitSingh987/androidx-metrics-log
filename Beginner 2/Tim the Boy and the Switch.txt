Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Switch

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was deaf, but he was very good at feeling things with his hands. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her find a switch in the dark room. Tim and his mom went into the dark room together. Tim felt the walls with his hands, trying to find the switch. He felt something round and smooth. "I found it!" Tim said to his mom. "Good job, Tim!" his mom said with a smile. Tim pushed the switch, and the room was full of light. Tim's mom was very happy and proud of him. They both felt good because they worked together to find the switch. From that day on, Tim knew that even though he was deaf, he could still help his mom and be a big part of their family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a flashlight that he loved to play with. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sam, frown because he lost his toy car. Tim wanted to help Sam feel better. At night, Tim took his flashlight and went to Sam's house. They both looked for the lost toy car together. Tim's flashlight helped them see in the dark. Soon, they found the toy car under a big tree. The car was not clean anymore, but Sam was very happy to have it back. Tim and Sam went inside to clean the toy car. They learned that helping each other makes them feel good. The moral of the story is that when we help our friends, we can make them happy and feel happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat named Tom. Tom liked to eat butter. Every day, he would sit in the kitchen and wait for his mom to give him butter. One day, Tom saw a bird outside the window. The bird liked to talk. The bird said, "Hello, Tom! Do you want to play?" Tom wanted to play, but he also wanted to eat butter. Tom had an idea. He asked the bird, "Do you like butter?" The bird said, "Yes, I like butter too!" So, Tom and the bird shared the butter and talked. They became good friends and played together every day. And they always ate butter together.

Once upon a time, there was a fair prince named Tim. He lived in a big castle with his mom and dad. Tim loved to play with his toys and run in the garden. One day, a little bird came to Tim's window and said, "Come play with me!" Tim was very excite to play with the bird. They played all day in the garden. But soon, a big cat saw them and wanted to catch the bird. The cat jumped and tried to grab the bird. Tim was scared for his new friend. "No, cat! Don't take my friend!" Tim shouted. The cat stopped and looked at Tim. Tim picked up a ball and threw it far away. The cat ran after the ball and left Tim and the bird alone. They were happy and safe again. They played until the sun went down and the bird flew home.

Once upon a time, there was a big, intelligent dog. This dog had very soft fur. The dog liked to help people. He would supply things to them when they needed it. The dog was very happy to help. One day, the dog met a little cat. The cat was lost and scared. The dog wanted to help the cat. So, the dog gave the cat some food. The cat was happy and thanked the dog. They became friends. But, one day, the dog and the cat were playing near the road. They did not see the big car coming. The car hit them and they got hurt. The dog and the cat were not able to play anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Spot. Spot had a hurt heel, so he walked very slow. Spot was sad because he could not play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a little girl named Sue. Sue asked, "Why are you sad, Spot?" Spot said, "My heel hurts, and I can't play with my friends." Sue felt bad for Spot and wanted to help him. Sue decided to find a way to make Spot's heel better. She took Spot to her mom, who put a bandage on his heel. Soon, Spot's heel was all better. Spot and Sue played together every day, and they were both very happy.
