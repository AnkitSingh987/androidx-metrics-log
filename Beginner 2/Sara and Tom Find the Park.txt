Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom Find the Park

Sara and Tom love to play with their toy band. They have drums, trumpets, flutes and bells. They like to march around the house and make loud music. Sometimes they pretend they are in a parade and wave to their mom and dad. One day, they decided to march to the park and play with their band there. They put on their hats and scarves and packed their instruments in a big bag. They asked their mom if they could go. "Sure, you can go, but be careful and stay together," mom said. "And don't forget to come back for lunch. I made you some yummy sandwiches and cookies." "OK, mom, thank you!" Sara and Tom said. They kissed mom goodbye and marched out the door. They had a lot of fun at the park. They played with their band on the grass, on the swings, on the slide and on the bench. They met some other kids who wanted to join their band. They taught them how to play their instruments and how to march. They made new friends and had a great time. Soon, they heard mom calling them from the gate. "Sara, Tom, it's time for lunch. Come on, let's go home." "OK, mom, we're coming!" Sara and Tom said. They said goodbye to their new friends and packed their instruments in the bag. They marched back to mom and gave her a big hug. "Did you have fun with your band?" mom asked. "Yes, mom, we had so much fun!" Sara and Tom said. "And we made new friends too!" "That's wonderful!" mom said. "Now, let's go home and eat your yummy lunch. I'm sure you're hungry after all that marching and playing." "Yes, mom, we're hungry!" Sara and Tom said. They marched home with mom, holding her hands. They couldn't wait to eat their yummy lunch and tell mom all about their band adventure.

Once there was a little girl named Sally. Sally wanted to do something nice, so she decided to wash her mommy's vase. Sally got a bucket of water and a sponge. She started to wash the vase and made lots of bubbles. She scrubbed and scrubbed until the vase was nice and clean. When Sally was finished, she was so happy! Her vase was so shiny and smelled so good. She put it back in its special place and smiled. Sally was so proud of her hard work and so was her mommy. Mommy gave Sally a big hug and said, "Thank you, Sally, for making my vase look so nice and clean." And Sally smiled again.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Max. He loved to play in the wet grass after it rained. One day, Max saw his friend Tom, who was trying to sell his toys. "Hi Tom," said Max. "Why are you selling your toys?" "I want to buy a new toy," Tom said. "So I need to sell these old ones." Max went home and thought about it. He knew he had a lot of toys too, so he decided to help Tom sell his toys. They both set up a table outside and put all their toys on it. They knew someone would come to buy them soon. A nice lady came by and saw the toys. She bought some toys for her little boy. Max and Tom were so happy! They had sold their toys and now they could buy new ones. The nice lady thanked them and went on her way. Max and Tom had learned that working together made things easier and more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who had a big purple ball. One day, he met a little boy named Tim. The man said, "Hi Tim, do you like my big purple ball?" Tim looked at the ball and said, "Yes, I like it. It is very big and purple." The man and Tim played with the purple ball every day. They had a lot of fun together. But one day, the man did not come to play. Tim was sad and missed his friend. He waited and waited for the man to come back. Finally, after many days, the man came back. He said, "I am sorry, Tim. I had to go away for a while. I realize how much I missed playing with you and the purple ball." Tim was very happy to see his friend again. They played with the purple ball all day and had lots of fun together.
