Category: Beginner
Title: The Powerful Ant and the Pot

Once upon a time, there was a powerful ant. The ant loved to help his friends. One day, he saw a pot. He knew he needed the pot. He wanted to put food in it for a big party. The ant tried to lift the pot, but it was too big. He called his friends to help. They all came and tried to lift the pot. But they could not do it. They needed something more powerful. Then, a little girl came by. She saw the ants and the pot. The girl picked up the pot with one hand. The ants were amazed. They didn't know that the girl was more powerful than all of them. The ants and the girl had a big party. They shared the food from the pot. The ants learned that sometimes, help can come from unexpected places. They were very happy and thankful for the girl's help.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. One day, while hopping, he found a big pile of hay. He thought it was the best thing ever! Bob hopped on the hay and played for hours. He felt very happy. But then, a big, stupid cow came and tried to eat the hay. Bob did not want the cow to eat his new toy. So, he hopped very fast around the cow to make it go away. The cow was so confused by Bob's fast hopping that it left the hay alone. Bob was proud of himself for saving his toy. He hopped back on the hay and played until the sun went down. The end.

One day, a cool cat saw a motorcycle. He wanted to ride it, but he did not know how. He asked his friend, the dog, if he knew how to ride it. The dog said, "No, but we can try together!" They both took a step to the motorcycle. The cat tried to ride it, but he fell off. The dog laughed and said, "Let me try!" The dog stepped on the motorcycle and tried to ride it too, but he also fell off. They both laughed and had fun trying to ride the motorcycle. Then, a big bird came and said, "I can help you ride the motorcycle!" The cat and the dog were happy. But when the bird tried to help them, the motorcycle went too fast and they could not stop. They all fell off the motorcycle and got hurt. The cat, the dog, and the bird learned that they should not play with things they do not know how to use.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She loved to take walks with her dog, Spot. They would walk in the park and play with a ball. Spot was a very good dog. One day, Lisa's mom told her to remind her dad to take Spot for a walk. Lisa found her dad and said, "Dad, don't forget to walk Spot!" Dad smiled and said, "Thank you for reminding me, Lisa." They went for a walk and saw a dead tree. The tree had no leaves and looked very old. Lisa and Spot were a little scared, but Dad said, "Don't worry, the tree is just dead. It can't hurt you." They kept walking and had a fun day at the park.

Lila and Ben like to play with their vehicles. They have cars, trucks, buses and bikes. They make noises and pretend to drive them on the floor. One day, they find a big box in the garage. It is empty and has a hole on one side. Lila has an idea. "Let's make a vehicle with the box!" she says to Ben. "We can cut out windows and wheels and paint it." Ben agrees. They get scissors, paper, glue and paint. They work hard and have fun. They cut out four circles for wheels and glue them to the bottom of the box. They cut out two squares for windows and glue them to the sides of the box. They paint the box red and blue and write "Lila and Ben's vehicle" on it. They are very proud of their vehicle. They take turns to sit inside and steer with a paper plate. They make noises and pretend to drive around the garage. "Beep beep! Watch out!" Lila says as she turns left. "Vroom vroom! I'm going fast!" Ben says as he turns right. They are having so much fun that they don't notice their mom coming into the garage. She sees them and smiles. "Wow, what a nice vehicle you made!" she says. "Can I see it?" Lila and Ben are happy to show their mom their vehicle. They tell her how they made it and what they can do with it. "That's very creative and clever!" mom says. "But you have to be careful when you play with scissors and paint. They can be dangerous if you don't use them right." Lila and Ben nod. They know mom is right. They say sorry and promise to be careful next time. Mom hugs them and says she loves them. She says they can keep their vehicle in the garage and play with it whenever they want. Lila and Ben are very happy. They thank mom and give her a kiss. They love their vehicle and their mom. They are the best.
