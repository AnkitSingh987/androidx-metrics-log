Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Rock

One day, a young boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a cool rock on the ground. He picked it up and examined it. It was shiny and smooth. Tim liked the rock a lot. Tim saw another youth named Sam at the park. Sam was playing with a toy car. Tim said, "Hi, Sam! Look at my cool rock!" Sam looked at the rock and said, "Wow! That is a cool rock!" Tim and Sam played with the rock and the toy car. They had a lot of fun. They were happy to have found the cool rock and to have spent time together.

Mama had a special surprise. In the house was an infant. Everyone was very envious. Mama hung the infant in a swing. The swing was gentle and swung back and forth in the sun. The infant giggled and clapped her hands. Mama and the infant played in the swing for a long time. The infant was so happy! But then the infant got sleepy. So mama hung a blanket over the infant's head to keep her warm. The infant snuggled into the blanket and fell asleep. Everyone wasenvious of the infant's cozy bed. But they were happy to see the infant so happy and content.

Once upon a time, in a normal country, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to hear the birds sing every morning. One day, he heard a special bird singing a different song. Tim knew that this bird was not like the others. Tim told his mom about the special bird. His mom said, "Maybe this bird is trying to tell you something. Listen closely to its song." Tim listened to the bird all day and all night. He knew that the bird's song was important. The next day, Tim heard the special bird again. This time, the bird led him to a hidden treasure! Tim and his mom were so happy. They knew that the special bird's song was a clue to find the treasure. And from that day on, Tim always listened to the birds and their songs.

One day, a dog named Max was playing with his ball. He was very happy. Max saw a big tree and the ball went under it. He ran to get it, but his leg felt bad. Max sat down and looked at his leg. A cat named Luna saw Max and asked, "What's wrong, Max? Why are you not playing?" Max said, "My leg feels bad. I can't get my ball." Luna wanted to help Max. She went under the tree and got the ball for him. Max was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. Luna said, "This ball does not belong to you, Max. It belongs to my friend, Tom." Max was sad, but he gave the ball back to Luna. Luna saw that Max was a good dog and said, "Don't worry, Max. Let's find your ball together." Max and Luna became good friends and played together every day.

Tim and Sam are friends. They go to the same school. They wear the same uniform. The uniform is blue and white. It has a shirt, a skirt or pants, and a tie. Tim and Sam think they look smart in their uniform. One day, a new girl comes to their class. Her name is Mia. She does not wear the same uniform. She wears a dress with flowers. It is pink and green. Tim and Sam think she looks different. "Hi, I am Mia. Can I play with you?" Mia asks Tim and Sam at recess. Tim and Sam look at each other. They are not sure. They like their uniform. They think it is better than Mia's dress. "No, you can't. You don't wear our uniform. You don't look smart. You look silly," Tim says. Sam nods. He agrees with Tim. Mia feels sad. She does not understand. She likes her dress. She thinks it is pretty. She wants to make friends. "Please, can I play with you? I can share my toys. I have a ball and a doll. You can choose," Mia says. Tim and Sam shake their heads. They don't want to play with Mia. They don't want to share her toys. They turn away and run to the slide. Mia cries. She sits on a bench. She feels lonely. A teacher sees Mia. She walks to her. She asks her what is wrong. "Mia tells the teacher what happened. The teacher feels sorry for Mia. She hugs her and wipes her tears. "That is not nice. Tim and Sam should not say that. You can wear whatever you want. You look lovely in your dress. You are smart and kind. You can play with anyone. You can include anyone. Do you want me to help you find some friends?" the teacher asks. Mia nods. She smiles. She feels better. The teacher takes Mia by the hand. She leads her to a group of children. They are playing with blocks and cars. They wear different clothes. Some wear uniforms. Some wear dresses. Some wear shirts and jeans. They all look happy. "Hi, everyone. This is Mia. She is new. She wants to play with you. Can you include her?" the teacher asks. The children look at Mia. They smile. They say yes. They welcome her. They ask her what she likes. They share their toys. They make room for her. Mia feels happy. She thanks the teacher. She joins the children. She plays with them. She makes friends. She does not care what they wear. She likes them for who they are. She thinks they are smart and kind. She thinks they are fun. She thinks she is lucky.
