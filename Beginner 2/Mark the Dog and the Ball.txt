Category: Beginner
Title: Mark the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Mark. Mark was a very glad dog. He loved to play and run in the park. One day, Mark saw a big red ball. He was so happy to see the ball. The sight of the ball made him jump and wag his tail. Mark ran to the ball and picked it up with his mouth. He wanted to show his new toy to his best friend, a cat named Sam. Sam was sitting under a tree. Mark ran to Sam and dropped the ball at his feet. Sam looked at the ball and smiled. He was glad to play with Mark and the ball. Together, Mark and Sam played with the ball all day. They rolled it, chased it, and jumped on it. The sun was shining, and they were both very happy. At the end of the day, they were tired but glad to have had so much fun. They knew they would always remember the sight of the big red ball and the day they played together.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play in the pool. They had a big pool in their backyard with a slide and a ball. They wore swimsuits and hats to protect their skin from the sun. One day, they decided to play a funny game. They took some soap and rubbed it on their hands and feet. Then they tried to climb the slide, but they slipped and slid back into the water. They laughed and splashed each other with soap bubbles. "Look, I'm a fish!" Lily said, making a funny face and blowing bubbles. "I'm a shark!" Ben said, pretending to bite her leg. They were having so much fun, they didn't notice that their mom was watching them from the window. She smiled and shook her head. She knew they were making a mess, but she also knew they were having a good time. She decided to let them play a little longer, but she also prepared some towels and a warm bath for them. She opened the door and called them. "Lily, Ben, time to get out of the pool!" They heard her voice and stopped their game. They looked at each other and grinned. They knew they had a surprise for her. They ran to the door, leaving a trail of soap and water behind them. They hugged their mom and rubbed their soapy hands and feet on her clothes. "Surprise, mom!" they shouted. "We made you a soap suit!" Their mom gasped and laughed. She hugged them back and tickled them. "You two are very funny, but also very naughty. Come on, let's go to the bath and wash off all this soap. And then we'll have some cookies and milk, okay?" They nodded and followed her to the bathroom. They still giggled and whispered to each other. They loved their mom and their pool. They had a lot of fun today.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a collar. The collar was red. Spot liked to play and run around. One day, Spot was in the park. It was a quiet day. Spot saw a big ball. He wanted to play with the ball. So, Spot ran to the ball and started to spin around it. As Spot spun around the ball, his collar came off. But Spot did not know. He just kept spinning and spinning. Spot was very happy. And that is the end of the story about Spot and his collar.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat had big eyes. One day, the cat met a little dog. The dog had a piece of paper. The cat and the dog became friends. The cat said, "What is that?" The dog said, "It is paper. I want to fold it." The cat said, "Let me help you." They started to fold the paper together. They made a pretty bird with the paper. Suddenly, the paper bird started to move! The cat and the dog were very surprised. The bird said, "Thank you for making me!" The cat and the dog were happy. They all became friends and played together.
