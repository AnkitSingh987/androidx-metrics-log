Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Car

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play together. One morning, Lily wakes up early. She wants to play with Ben. She goes to his house and knocks on the door. "Ben, Ben, wake up! Let's play!" Lily says. Ben hears Lily and opens his eyes. He is sleepy, but he likes Lily. He gets out of bed and puts on his clothes. He goes to the door and sees Lily. "Hi, Lily. What do you want to play?" Ben asks. Lily smiles and shows Ben her toy car. She likes to drive it around the yard. She thinks it is fast and fun. "I want to play with my car. Do you have a car, too?" Lily asks. Ben nods and runs to his room. He has a car, too. It is red and shiny. He likes to make it go vroom and beep. He brings his car to the door and shows it to Lily. "Look, this is my car. It is red. Do you like it?" Ben asks. Lily nods and claps her hands. She likes Ben's car. She thinks it is pretty and loud. "I like it. It is red. Let's race our cars. Who will be faster?" Lily asks. Ben agrees and they go to the yard. They find a good spot to start. They count to three and then push their cars. Their cars zoom and zoom. They laugh and cheer. They have a lot of fun. Lily and Ben are successful. They both win. They both have fast and fun cars. They hug and say thank you. They are happy. They are friends. They like to play together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a girl named Sue sitting on a bench. Sue was sad. Tim wanted to help her. Tim had a folder with him. Inside the folder, there were pretty pictures. Tim went to Sue and said, "Do you want to see my folder? It has pretty pictures." Sue said, "Yes, please!" They looked at the pictures together. Sue liked the pictures. She asked Tim, "Can I have one?" Tim said, "Yes, you can have one. It's fair to share." Sue was happy. They became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, loud fridge. It was in a small house. A little cat lived in the house too. The cat liked to lie on top of the fridge. It was warm and nice there. The cat would sleep all day on the fridge. One day, the cat heard a loud noise. The fridge was shaking. The cat was scared. It tried to jump off the fridge, but it was too late. The fridge fell on the floor with a big crash. The cat was hurt. It could not move. The fridge was broken too. All the food inside was bad. The little house was not happy anymore. The cat and the fridge were sad and broken.

Once upon a time, there was a chicken called Bob. Bob was so very filthy, because he liked to roll around in the mud. Every morning, he would hop and play in the mud. One day, spring arrived and Bob wanted to keep on playing in the mud. But it was too hot to stay in the sun, so Bob went to look for a shady place to play. He hopped and hopped, until he saw a cool tree. Bob was so happy and he hopped up into the tree, still all filthy from playing in the mud. Suddenly, Bob heard a noise. It was the farmer, who was angry to see his chicken up in the tree and said, "Come down this instant!" Bob had to choose between staying in the tree, or jumping down and facing his punishment. After a few moments of thinking, Bob decided to jump down. He lay still on the ground, waiting for the farmer's angry words. But instead, the farmer patted Bob and said, "Let's go get you washed off, you filthy chicken!" Bob was so relieved and followed the farmer to the barn, where he was finally able to get clean again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to dream. Every night, he would dream about playing basketball. Tim had a big, bouncy basketball that he played with every day. One day, Tim met a wealthy man named Mr. Smith. Mr. Smith saw Tim playing with his basketball. He said, "Hello, Tim! I see you love basketball. Would you like to play with me?" Tim was very happy and said, "Yes, please!" Tim and Mr. Smith played basketball together. They had so much fun! Tim's dreams came true, and he was very happy. From that day on, Tim and Mr. Smith played basketball every day, and they became the best of friends.
