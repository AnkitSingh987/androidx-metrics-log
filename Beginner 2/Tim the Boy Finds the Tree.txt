Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an adventurous boy who loved to play outside. He had a favorite shirt that he always wore when he went on adventures. It was a red shirt with a big, blue "A" on it. One sunny day, Tim went to play in the park. He climbed trees, ran fast, and jumped high. He was having so much fun! But then, he saw a little girl up in a tree. She was scared and didn't know how to get down. Tim knew he had to help her. Tim shouted, "Don't worry, I will help you!" He climbed up the tree and carefully helped the little girl down. She was so happy and thanked Tim for being brave. Tim felt proud and knew that his adventurous shirt helped him save the day.

Once upon a time, in a dry land, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy spear that he loved to play with. One day, he went outside to play with his friends. As they were playing, Tim's friend urged him to throw the spear far away. So, Tim threw the spear as far as he could. But, it landed in a dark and scary cave. Tim was sad, but he was too scared to go inside the cave. Later, Tim's big brother went to the cave to get the spear back. He found the spear and also found a big treasure chest! Tim and his friends were so happy. They learned that sometimes, when you are brave, good things happen.

One day, a weak cat named Tom was cold. He wanted to wear his bathrobe. He looked for it, but it was not in his room. Tom was sad. He asked his friend, a little bird named Sue, if she had seen his bathrobe. Sue said, "Yes, I saw a big dog take it." Tom and Sue went to find the big dog. They saw him wearing the bathrobe. Tom said, "Please give me back my bathrobe. I am cold and weak." The big dog said, "I will give it back if you pay me with food." Tom and Sue knew they had to find food to pay the big dog. They found some fish and brought it to the big dog. The big dog was happy and gave Tom his bathrobe back. Tom was warm and not weak anymore. They all became good friends.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big ring. She was very happy. She wanted to show it to her friends. So, she went to the park where her friends were playing. Amy saw her friends in a network, holding hands and playing a game. Amy went to her friend, Tim, and said, "Look at my big ring!" Tim looked at the ring and was fearful. He said, "Oh no, that ring is scary! It might hurt us!" Amy was sad because she didn't want to scare her friends. Amy thought and thought. She wanted her friends to like her ring too. So, she put the ring on the ground and showed them it was not scary. "See, it's just a big ring," she said. Tim and the others saw that it was not scary. They all played together with the big ring, and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. The frog loved to jump all day. He was very good at it. The frog lived in a small pond with his friends. They all liked to play together. One day, the frog wanted to try something new. He wanted to see what was outside the pond. He tried to escape. He jumped and jumped, but he could not get out. The frog was persistent. He did not give up. He kept trying to jump out of the pond. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird saw the frog and picked him up with its beak. The bird took the frog high up in the sky. The frog was scared at first, but then he saw the whole world below him. He was happy that he could finally escape the pond. The bird put the frog back down in a new place. The frog made new friends and had many new adventures.
