Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Whistle

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a whistle that he loved to play with. One day, he went to the park with his whistle. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim put his whistle in his pocket and climbed the tree. He went very high. Then, he saw a bird nest with an empty egg. Tim wanted to look at it, but he forgot about his whistle in his pocket. When Tim tried to get down, he heard a crack. He fell and his whistle broke. Tim was sad and felt regret. He wished he had not climbed the tree. Now, Tim had no whistle to play with anymore. The end.

One day, a bird wanted to fly. The bird was unusual because it was orange. It lived in a big tree with other birds. The orange bird was sad because it could not fly like the other birds. The orange bird saw a big cat. The cat said, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I cannot fly." The cat said, "I will help you fly. Come to me." The orange bird was happy and went to the cat. The cat tried to help the orange bird fly. But the cat was not nice. The cat wanted to eat the bird. The cat ate the orange bird. The orange bird did not fly. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to eat soft food. One day, her mom made soup for lunch. Lily was happy because she loved soup. She sat at the table and got ready to drink her soup. While Lily was drinking her soup, she saw something in the bowl. It was a small, green frog! The frog jumped out of the soup and onto the table. Lily was very surprised. She did not expect to see a frog in her soup. The frog looked at Lily and said, "Thank you for saving me from the soup!" Lily could not believe the frog could talk. They became friends and played together all day. From that day on, Lily always checked her soup for new friends.

One day, Tim and his friend, Lily, were playing in their room. They had a big desk where they liked to draw and color. The desk was very useful because it had many places to put their toys and books. Tim wanted to draw a big sun, but Lily wanted to draw a big moon. They couldn't decide what to draw together. They started to disagree. "I want to draw the sun!" said Tim. "No, I want to draw the moon!" said Lily. Mom heard them disagree and came into the room. She said, "Why don't you both draw what you like, and then share your drawings with each other?" Tim and Lily thought it was a good idea. So, Tim drew a big sun, and Lily drew a big moon. When they finished, they shared their drawings and smiled. The moral of the story is that it's okay to have different ideas, and we can still be friends and share our work with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a team of five friends. They liked to play in the wild park near their homes. One day, they found a big, wild tree with many branches. They all wanted to climb the tree, but they were scared. The first friend was very brave. He started to climb the tree. When he was high up, he felt a pinch on his hand. "Ouch!" he said. He looked down and saw a small bug. The bug pinched him again. The brave friend was scared of the bug, so he climbed down. The other friends saw this and were scared too. They did not want to be pinched by the bug. But they still wanted to climb the tree. So, they made a plan. They got a big net to catch the bug. They worked together as a team to catch the bug and put it far away from the tree. Now, the friends could climb the tree without getting pinched. They were happy that they worked as a team. They had fun playing in the wild tree all day long. And they learned that when they work together, they can do anything.
