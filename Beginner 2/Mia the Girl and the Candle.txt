Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Candle

One day, a little girl named Mia found a wide candle in her house. She wanted to light the candle but didn't know how. She asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me light the candle?" Her mom said, "Yes, Mia. But first, we need to find a thing called a lighter." They looked all around the house and found a lighter in a drawer. Mia's mom showed her how to squeeze the lighter to make a flame. Mia squeezed the lighter and lit the wide candle. The flame danced and made the room bright. Mia and her mom sat and watched the candle burn. They felt happy and warm together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Reliable. Reliable lived in a harbor with other boats. The harbor was his home. He liked to help the big boats when they needed him. One day, a big boat got lost. The big boat did not know how to get back to the harbor. Reliable wanted to help. So, he went to find the big boat. He asked the big boat, "Do you need help?" The big boat said, "Yes, please show me the way home." Reliable knew the way back to the harbor. He told the big boat to follow him. The big boat was happy to have Reliable's help. They went back to the harbor together. When they got there, the big boat said, "Thank you, Reliable!" Reliable was happy he could help.

Tom and Lily were playing in the living room. They had many toys, but they liked the carpet best. The carpet was soft and colorful, and they could pretend it was a magic carpet that flew them to different places. "Let's go to the zoo!" Lily said. She put her stuffed animals on the carpet and made animal noises. "Okay!" Tom said. He grabbed his toy car and drove it on the carpet. He made car noises. But then, Tom saw a big hole in the carpet. He was curious and put his finger in the hole. He felt something hard and sharp. He pulled out a nail. "Look, Lily, I found a nail!" Tom said. He showed the nail to Lily. "Where did you get that?" Lily asked. She looked at the carpet and saw the hole. She gasped. "Oh no, the carpet is broken! Mom will be mad!" Tom and Lily were scared. They did not want Mom to be mad. They tried to hide the hole with their toys, but it was too big. They heard Mom coming down the stairs. "Tom, Lily, what are you doing?" Mom asked. She saw the toys on the carpet and smiled. She thought they were having fun. But then, she saw the nail in Tom's hand and the hole in the carpet. She frowned. She was not happy. "Tom, Lily, did you do this?" Mom asked. She pointed at the hole and the nail. Tom and Lily looked at each other. They did not know what to say. They knew they were in trouble. They wished they had not played with the carpet.

Lily and Tom were playing in the yard. They saw a big pile of leaves that Dad had raked. They wanted to jump in the leaves and have fun. "Let's go, Lily!" Tom said. "The leaves look so soft and pretty!" But Lily saw something shiny in the pile. It was a rake that Dad had left there. She knew that rakes could be hurt. They had sharp teeth that could cut or poke. "Wait, Tom!" Lily said. "There is a rake in the leaves. We should not jump there. It could be hurt." Tom did not listen to Lily. He ran to the pile and jumped. He felt a pain in his leg. He had landed on the rake. He cried out loud. Lily ran to Tom and helped him get up. She saw blood on his leg. She was scared and sorry. "I'm sorry, Tom," Lily said. "I tried to tell you about the rake. Are you okay?" Tom was hurt and sad. He wished he had listened to Lily. He hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. You were right. I should have listened to you. Thank you for helping me." They went inside to find Mom. Mom cleaned Tom's wound and put a bandage on it. She hugged them both and said, "I'm glad you are okay, Tom. And I'm proud of you, Lily. You were smart and careful. You tried to warn Tom and you helped him when he was hurt. You are a good sister and a good friend." Lily and Tom learned a lesson that day. They learned to be careful and to listen to each other. They learned that rakes are not toys and that leaves can hide hurt things. They learned that playing is fun, but safety is important.
