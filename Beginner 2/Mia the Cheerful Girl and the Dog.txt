Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Cheerful Girl and the Dog

One day, a cheerful girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big dog with a ball. Mia wanted to get the ball for the dog. She walked up to the dog and said, "Hi dog, can I get the ball?" The dog did not hear Mia. He had a big leaf stuck in his ear. Mia saw the leaf and wanted to help. She tried to get the leaf out, but the dog did not like it. The dog barked and ran away from Mia. Mia felt sad. She wanted to help the dog and be friends. She had an idea. Mia found a long stick and called the dog. "Come here, dog! Let's play!" The dog came back, wagging his tail. Mia used the stick to get the leaf out of the dog's ear. The dog was so happy. Mia and the dog played with the ball all day. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark because he would have nightmares. Every night, he would ask his mom to stay with him until he fell asleep. One night, Tim had a nightmare about a big monster. He woke up and ran to his mom. "Mom, I had a bad dream," he said. His mom hugged him and told him to drink some water. Tim drank the water, hoping it would help him feel better. But the next night, the nightmare came back. The big monster was even scarier this time. Tim was so scared that he couldn't sleep anymore. He cried and cried, but the nightmare wouldn't go away. And from that day on, poor little Tim was always scared of the dark.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to go to the park. He asked his mom, "Can we go to the park, please?" His mom said, "Yes, we can take a taxi to the park." Tim was very happy. As they waited for the taxi, Tim saw a big red balloon. He wanted to hold it, so his mom let him. But when the taxi came, Tim accidentally let go of the balloon. He watched as the balloon went up and up into the sky. He was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. The taxi driver saw Tim's sad face and said, "Don't worry, I have another balloon for you!" The taxi driver gave Tim a new balloon, and Tim was happy again. They all went to the park and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a big mill. The mill was very successful. It made lots of good things to eat. People loved the mill. One day, a big wind came. The wind was very strong. It tried to blow the mill away. The mill was scared. The people who worked at the mill started to wonder what they could do to stop the wind. The people worked together. They held hands and made a big circle around the mill. They did not let the wind blow the mill away. They were very happy. The mill was safe. The wind went away, and the mill stayed successful.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a slow walker. He liked to take his time and look at everything around him. One day, Tom and his friends went to the park to play. Tom's friends wanted to play a game of tag, but Tom didn't want to. He refused to play because he was slow. Tom's friends played tag without him, and Tom sat under a tree to watch. He saw a little bird on the ground. The bird was hurt and couldn't fly. Tom wanted to help the bird, so he took it home. He took care of the bird, giving it food and water. Over time, the bird got better and could fly again. When Tom went back to the park with his friends, they asked him to play tag again. This time, Tom agreed to play. He was still slow, but he had learned that being slow could also be a good thing. He had helped the little bird when no one else saw it. The moral of the story is that sometimes being slow can help you see things others might miss.
