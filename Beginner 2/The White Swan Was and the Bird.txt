Category: Beginner
Title: The White Swan Was and the Bird

One day, a white swan was in a pond. The swan liked to do tricks. It would jump and splash, and all the kids would clap. One day, a big wind came. It blew the white swan far away. The swan did not know where it was. It was sad and scared. Then, the swan saw a big white bird in the sky. The big bird was a swan, too! It was the swan's mom. The mom swan took the little swan home. They did tricks together and were happy.

One day, a little cat went outside. The cat was gray. It liked to play in the sun. Today, the sky was gray too. The cat did not like the gray sky. The cat saw a big dog. The dog seemed nice, but the cat was scared. The cat ran away fast. The dog did not chase the cat. The cat was happy. The gray cat went home. It was not scared now. The cat played with its toys. The gray sky went away, and the sun came out. The cat was very happy.

Lily and Ben like to create things with their blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. They can make houses, cars, animals and more. They have fun playing with their blocks on the floor. One day, they decide to create a tower with their blocks. They want to make it very high, as high as the ceiling. They start to stack the blocks on top of each other, carefully and slowly. They use all the blocks they have. "Look, Lily, our tower is so high!" Ben says, proudly. "Yes, Ben, it is amazing!" Lily says, happily. They stand back and admire their tower. They are very proud of their creation. They clap their hands and smile. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is their dog, Spot. Spot runs into the room, wagging his tail. He sees the tower and thinks it is a toy. He jumps and barks at the tower, trying to play with it. "Oh no, Spot, stop!" Lily and Ben shout. But it is too late. Spot knocks down the tower with his paw. The blocks fall and scatter on the floor. The tower is gone. Lily and Ben are sad and angry. They look at Spot, who looks confused and sorry. He licks their faces and wags his tail. "Bad dog, Spot, you ruined our tower!" Lily says, crying. "Go away, Spot, we don't want to play with you!" Ben says, frowning. Spot whines and lowers his head. He feels bad for making them upset. He wants to make them happy again. He looks around and sees the blocks on the floor. He has an idea. He picks up a block with his mouth and brings it to Lily and Ben. He drops it in front of them and wags his tail. He wants to help them create a new tower. He wants to play with them and their blocks. Lily and Ben see the block and Spot's face. They realize that Spot did not mean to break their tower. He was just curious and playful. He is their friend and he loves them. They forgive Spot and hug him. They smile and say, "It's okay, Spot, we are not mad at you. You can help us create a new tower. We love you, too." They take the block and start to stack it with another block. Spot picks up another block and brings it to them. They work together and create a new tower. It is not as high as the first one, but it is still beautiful and fun. They play with their tower and their dog. They are happy and proud of their creation. They clap their hands and wag their tails.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a game. This game was very attractive and all the kids loved to play it. The game was about raising a flag as high as you can. The kids wanted to see who could raise the flag the highest. One sunny day, two friends, Tom and Tim, played the game. They both wanted to win and started to fight. They pulled the flag from each other, trying to raise it higher. They were not happy and stopped being friends. A wise old man saw them fight. He told them that fighting is not good. The old man said, "You should help each other to raise the flag high. Be kind and work together. Then, everyone wins." Tom and Tim listened to the old man. They worked together and raised the flag very high. They became friends again and learned that working together is better than fighting.
