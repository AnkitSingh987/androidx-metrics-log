Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to watch TV. His favorite show was about cars that speed. He loved to see them go fast. One day, it was very cold outside. Tim looked out the window and saw that it was icy. He put on his coat and went outside to play. He pretended that he was a car that could speed on the icy road. As Tim played, he saw a big truck on the TV show in his mind. The truck was sliding on the icy road. Tim knew he had to be careful. He slowed down and did not speed on the ice. Tim had fun playing, and he was safe.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lily. She was three years old and loved to play outside. One day, Lily's mother handed her a delicate bow and said, "Lily, can you pick some flowers for your room?". Lily happily skipped outside. She looked around for the perfect flowers. After a while, she found the most beautiful purple flowers. But she had forgotten her delicate bow to carry them. She looked back at the house and remembered that she needed the bow. She hopped back to the house and asked her mother, "Mummy, where is my delicate bow?". Her mother smiled and pointed to a special box on the shelf. Lily opened the box and gasped. Inside, was the most beautiful and delicate bow she had ever seen. She carefully took it and thanked her mother. With the bow, Lily was able to pick the flowers and bring them to her room. They filled the room with the sweet smell of love.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy had big paws that he loved to play with. One day, Buddy went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Buddy saw a little cat named Tiny. Tiny was sad because her toy was stuck in a tree. "I will help you," said Buddy. He used his big paw to part the tree branches and get the toy down. Tiny was very happy and said, "Thank you, Buddy!" They played together all day and became best friends. From that day on, Buddy and Tiny always played together at the park, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Tip. Tip loved to play with his ball. One day, Tip went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tip saw a big dog with a ball. The big dog did not want to share his ball. Tip was sad. He wanted to play with the big dog and the ball. Tip had an idea. He found a stick and started to play with it. The big dog saw Tip and wanted to play too. They played together and became friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big fountain. Lily liked the fountain because it was pretty. She wanted to play near it. Lily started to skip by the fountain. She heard a loud sound. It was a bird singing a song. The bird was on a tree near the fountain. Lily liked the bird's song. Lily skipped and played near the fountain all day. She had fun with the bird and the water. When it was time to go home, Lily waved goodbye to the bird and the fountain. She couldn't wait to come back and play again.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a furry dog. The dog has a big nose and a long tail. Sara and Ben want to pet the dog. "Hello, doggy!" Sara says. "Can we touch you?" The dog wags its tail and licks Sara's hand. Sara giggles. She pets the dog's nose and ears. The dog is soft and warm. "Me too, me too!" Ben says. He runs to the dog and hugs it. The dog barks and nips Ben's arm. Ben cries. He lets go of the dog. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben says. "The dog bit me!" Sara runs to Ben and hugs him. She looks at his arm. There is a small red mark. "I'm sorry, Ben," Sara says. "The dog didn't like your hug. Maybe it was too tight." Ben sniffles. He rubs his arm. "I hope it's not bad," Ben says. "I hope the dog is not mad at me." Sara smiles. She kisses Ben's arm. "It's not bad," Sara says. "It's just a little ouchie. The dog is not mad at you. It just wanted to play." Ben smiles. He kisses Sara's cheek. "Thank you, Sara," Ben says. "You are a good friend." Sara and Ben hold hands. They wave to the dog. "Bye, doggy!" Sara and Ben say. "We hope to see you again!"
