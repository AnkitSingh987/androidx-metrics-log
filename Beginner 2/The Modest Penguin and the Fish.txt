Category: Beginner
Title: The Modest Penguin and the Fish

Once upon a time there was a modest penguin. He wanted to go swimming but all the pools were too cold. He thought of a great idea! He decided to soak himself in the lake. He was enjoying his bath when, all of a sudden, a giant fish appeared. The fish was so hungry and he began to eat the poor penguin! The poor penguin was scared and he tried to run away. But it was too late! The fish kept eating the penguin until he was gone! The moral of this story is that you should be careful when you go swimming. Never try to soak yourself in a lake, it may put you in great danger!

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. But they also liked to make things with sticks, leaves and flowers. They found a big stick and some long grass. They wanted to make a flag. "Let's tie the grass to the stick," said Tom. "Then we can wave it in the air." "OK," said Lily. "But how do we tie it?" Tom showed Lily how to make a knot. He wrapped the grass around the stick and pulled it tight. He did it again and again until the grass was secure. "Look, we did it!" said Tom. He lifted the stick and waved it. The grass fluttered in the wind. Lily clapped her hands. She took the stick and waved it too. They ran around the park with their flag. They felt happy and proud. A man was sitting on a bench. He was watching the children play. He smiled when he saw Tom and Lily with their flag. He remembered when he was a youth. He also liked to make things with sticks, leaves and flowers. He thought they were very clever and creative. He decided to talk to them. He walked over to them and said, "Hello, I like your flag. It is very unusual and beautiful. How did you make it?" Tom and Lily stopped running. They looked at the man. He was old and kind. They liked him. "Thank you," said Tom. "We tied the grass to the stick. We made a knot. Do you want to see?" "Yes, please," said the man. He bent down and looked at the knot. He nodded and said, "That is a very good knot. You are very good at tying. Can you teach me how to do it?" Tom and Lily smiled. They liked to teach. They showed the man how to tie a knot. He tried to do it. He was not very good at first. He made a mess. But he did not give up. He tried again and again until he did it. "Look, I did it!" said the man. He lifted the stick and waved it. The grass fluttered in the wind. Tom and Lily clapped their hands. They were happy for the man. They gave him a hug. They said, "You did it! You are very good at tying too. You are our friend." The man hugged them back. He said, "Thank you. You are my friends too. You made my day. You are very unusual and wonderful children."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the snow. One day, he made a big, silly snowman with a carrot nose and stick arms. Tim was very happy with his snowman and smiled a big smile. The next day, the sun came out and started to melt the snow. Tim's silly snowman started to get smaller and smaller. Tim felt very sad and started to weep. He did not want his snowman to go away. Tim's mom saw him weeping and gave him a big hug. She told him not to worry, because the snow would come back again. Tim felt a little better and stopped weeping. He knew that he could make another silly snowman when the snow came back.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play on the swings and slide. His mom said, "Let's play together, Tim!" Tim was so happy and excited. As they played, Tim saw a small, quiet dog sitting under a tree. The dog looked uncomfortable and sad. Tim wanted to help the dog, so he told his mom, "Mom, let's help the dog!" His mom agreed, and they went to see the dog. Tim and his mom gave the dog some water and food. The dog wagged its tail and looked happier. Tim felt good for helping the dog. They played with the dog for a while, and then it was time to go home. Tim said goodbye to the dog and left the park with his mom, feeling happy and proud.
