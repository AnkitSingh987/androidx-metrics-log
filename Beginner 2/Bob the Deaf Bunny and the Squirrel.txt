Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Deaf Bunny and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a deaf bunny named Bob. Bob could not hear, but he was very good at observing things. One day, Bob went on an adventure to find new land. As Bob hopped along, he saw a big tree. He observed a squirrel on a branch. The squirrel saw Bob and said, "Hi, I'm Sam! Do you want to play?" Bob could not hear Sam, but he observed Sam's mouth move and waved. Sam understood that Bob was deaf and used his paws to show that he wanted to play. Together, Bob and Sam found a new land with lots of fun things to do. They played in the grass, climbed trees, and chased butterflies. They became best friends and had a great day. And they knew that even though Bob was deaf, they could still have fun and understand each other.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Bow. Bow loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Bow found a hose in the yard. The hose was long and green. Bow had an idea. He picked up the hose with his mouth and started to run. He ran around and around, making the hose wiggle and dance. Bow was having so much fun with the hose. Then, Bow's friend, a big cat named Paws, came to play. Paws saw the hose and joined Bow. They both played with the hose, laughing and running in the sun. Bow and Paws were very happy, and they played with the hose all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a sad octopus named Ollie. Ollie lived in the big, blue sea. He felt sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, Ollie decided to gather shells to make his home pretty. He thought that maybe if his home was pretty, some sea friends would come to play with him. Ollie swam around and picked up red, blue, and yellow shells. Soon, other sea animals saw Ollie's pretty home and wanted to be his friend. They swam over to play with Ollie and his shells. Ollie was not sad anymore. He had many friends and they all played together happily in the big, blue sea.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lily. She loved to play with her red balloon. One day, she went to the park with her balloon. At the park, she met a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his balloon. Lily wanted to help him. She said, "Don't be sad, Tim. We can share my balloon!" Tim smiled and they played together. As they played, Lily's balloon flew away. Tim said, "I know! Let's reverse and find your balloon!" They walked back and found the balloon. They were happy and played together all day.

Lily liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was a pink doll with long hair. She called her doll Rose. Lily liked to brush Rose's hair and dress her in pretty clothes. One day, Lily wanted to show Rose to her friend Mia. She put Rose in a small bag and went to Mia's house. Mia was happy to see Lily and Rose. She had a doll too, a blue doll with short hair. She called her doll Sky. Lily and Mia played with their dolls. They made them talk and hug and dance. They had fun. But then, Mia wanted to try something. She said, "Let's make our dolls walk down the stairs. It will be fun." Lily did not like this idea. She was afraid that Rose would fall and get hurt. She said, "No, no, Mia. I don't want to do that. Rose is too small. She can't walk down the stairs. She will fall and get hurt." Mia did not understand. She said, "Don't be silly, Lily. It's just a game. Sky can walk down the stairs. Look, I'll show you." She took Sky and made him walk down the first step. He did not fall. He looked happy. Lily was still scared. She said, "Please, Mia. Stop. I don't want to play this game. I love Rose. I don't want her to get hurt. Please, Mia. Please." Mia looked at Lily. She saw that Lily was sad and scared. She felt sorry for her. She said, "Okay, Lily. I'm sorry. I didn't mean to make you sad. I understand. You love Rose. I love Sky too. Let's play something else. Something that is safe and fun for our dolls." Lily smiled. She said, "Thank you, Mia. You are a good friend. I'm sorry too. I didn't mean to be silly. I understand. You just wanted to have fun. Let's play something else. Something that is safe and fun for our dolls." Lily and Mia hugged. They took their dolls and played something else. Something that was safe and fun for their dolls. They were happy. They were friends.
