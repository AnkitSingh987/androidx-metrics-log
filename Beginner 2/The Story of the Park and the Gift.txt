Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Park and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to play. She was very playful and often spent her days making up wonderful games. One day, while out playing in the park, she noticed something on the other side of the playground. The little girl was surprised to find a big puppy! She walked over to the puppy and said, "Hi! What's your name?". The puppy looked up, seemed to smile, and said, "My name is Spot. I'm so pleased to meet you!". The little girl was absolutely delighted and asked Spot to join her in a game of tag. Spot seemed very excited and barked with joy. They played their game of tag for hours, running back and forth across the park. As the little girl was about to go home, Spot barked one last time and gave her a big surprise! He had a special gift for her in his mouth - a beautiful and playful little puppy toy. She hugged Spot and thanked him for the surprise. She asked if he would like to come to the park and play again. Spot barked happily and said, "Yes! I can't wait!".

One day, a cat and a dog were playing. The cat saw a high tree and wanted to climb it. The dog said, "Wait, cat! Let's pause and think. You may get stuck up there." The cat did not listen and climbed up the tree. It was fun at first, but then the cat saw a big bird with a pill in its beak. The bird said, "I found this pill on the ground. It can make you big or small. Do you want it?" The cat was curious and took the pill. It ate the pill and something unexpected happened. The cat became very small, like a bug. The dog laughed and said, "Now you have to be careful not to get stepped on!" The cat was scared, but then the bird said, "Don't worry, I can help you. I have another pill that can make you big again." The cat ate the second pill and went back to its normal size. The cat and the dog went back to playing, and the cat learned to listen to the dog's advice.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. The store was very busy. There were many people there. Lily held her mom's hand so she would not get lost. As they walked, Lily saw a big, gray elephant in the store. "Mom, look at the elephant!" Lily said. Her mom looked and laughed. "That's not a real elephant, silly. It's just a big toy." Lily wanted the toy elephant, but her mom said no. They bought the food and went home. That night, there was a knock at the door. When Lily opened the door, she saw the big toy elephant! It had come to play with her. They played all night and had lots of fun.

One day, Tom and Sue went for a walk. They saw a big, weird building. It was very old and had many colors. They wanted to find out what was inside. Tom said, "Let's go in and see what we can find!" Sue was scared but said, "Okay, let's go." They went inside and saw many things. They found a big box in a room. They opened the box and saw many toys. A man came into the room and said, "You found my toys! I lost them a long time ago. Thank you for finding them!" Tom and Sue were happy they could help. The man gave them a toy each as a thank you. The moral of the story is that when you help others, good things can happen to you too. And even if something looks weird, it might have something nice inside.

Tom and Sue were playing in the woods. They liked to find sticks and stones and make things with them. They saw a big furry animal sleeping under a tree. It looked like a big dog, but with a long tail and sharp teeth. "Look, a furry dog!" Sue said. "Can we pet it?" Tom shook his head. "No, Sue. That is not a dog. That is a fox. Foxes can bite. We should mind it and leave it alone." But Sue did not listen. She wanted to touch the furry fox. She crept closer and closer, until she was right next to it. She reached out her hand and stroked its fur. The fox woke up with a start. It saw Sue and growled. It snapped at her hand and scratched her arm. Sue screamed and ran away. Tom ran after her. He saw a man with an axe chopping wood nearby. He called for help. "Help, help! A fox hurt my sister!" The man with the axe heard Tom and ran to them. He saw Sue's bleeding arm and the angry fox. He swung his axe and scared the fox away. "Are you okay, kids?" the man asked. "What were you doing near the fox?" "We were playing, sir," Tom said. "But Sue wanted to pet the fox. She did not mind it and it bit her." The man shook his head. "That was very foolish, Sue. You should never touch wild animals. They can be dangerous. You are lucky I was here with my axe. Come, I will take you to your parents and get you some medicine." He picked up Sue and carried her to his truck. Tom followed him. He felt sorry for Sue, but he also felt angry. He wished she had minded the fox and listened to him. He hoped she would learn her lesson.
