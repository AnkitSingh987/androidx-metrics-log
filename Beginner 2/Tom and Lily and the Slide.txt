Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Slide

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in the park. They see a big swing. They want to try it. "Can we go on the swing, Mom?" Tom asks. "Sure, but be careful. The swing is very high and fast," Mom says. Tom and Lily run to the swing. They see a seat. The seat is deep. It can fit two kids. "Let's sit together," Lily says. "OK, but I sit in the front. I am bigger," Tom says. They climb on the seat. Tom sits in the front. Lily sits behind him. They hold the ropes. Mom pushes them. "Whee!" Tom and Lily shout. They feel the wind in their hair. They go up and down. They have fun. But then, something happens. The seat starts to tilt. Tom is too heavy. He weighs more than Lily. "Uh-oh," Tom says. "Tom, you are making us fall!" Lily says. They scream. Mom sees them. She runs to catch them. She grabs the seat. She stops the swing. "Are you OK?" Mom asks. "Yes, we are OK. But that was scary," Tom says. "Sorry, Lily. I did not mean to make us fall," Tom says. "It's OK, Tom. But next time, let me sit in the front. I weigh less than you," Lily says. They hug. Mom smiles. She helps them get off the seat. "Let's play something else. How about the slide?" Mom says. "OK, let's go to the slide. But this time, we take turns," Tom and Lily say. They run to the slide. They have more fun. They are happy.

One day, a big, thick dog named Max saw a small cube. The cube was on the grass. Max was very happy. He wanted to play with the cube. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his friend, a small cat named Luna. "Look, Luna!" Max said. "I found a cube! Let's play with it." Luna was excited too. They began to play with the cube. They rolled it, tossed it, and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The cube began to talk! "Hello, Max and Luna," said the cube. "I am a magic cube. I can discuss things with you." Max and Luna were very surprised. They had never seen a talking cube before. They talked with the cube and became friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a bad dream, a nightmare. He felt frightened when he woke up. The sun was shining outside, but Tim still felt scared. He did not want to be alone, so he went to find his mom. Tim found his mom in the kitchen. She was making breakfast. Tim told his mom about his nightmare. He mentioned how it made him feel frightened. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, it was just a dream. You are safe now." After breakfast, Tim played with his toys. He felt better and was not frightened anymore. His mom was there to help him feel safe. Tim knew that even if he had another nightmare, his mom would always be there to help him feel better.

One day, a big dog and a small dog were playing outside. They were very competitive and liked to race each other. The big dog was fast, but the small dog was faster. They ran and ran, having so much fun. Suddenly, they saw a big cross on the ground. They were curious and wanted to find out what it was. They started to dig, and as they dug, they scattered dirt all around them. They were making a big mess, but they didn't care. They just wanted to see what was under the cross. As they dug, they began to argue. The big dog wanted to dig deeper, but the small dog was tired. They couldn't agree on what to do. The small dog wanted to stop, but the big dog kept digging. In the end, they decided to take turns digging. They worked together and found a big box of toys. They were so happy and played with their new toys all day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Lily. She was a delicate girl who loved to play with her toys. One day, her mom asked her to go to the store and request soap from the store man. Lily put on her shoes and went to the store. She saw the store man and said, "I need soap, please." The store man gave her a big bar of soap. Lily said "thank you" and went back home. On her way home, Lily saw a big dog. The dog looked at the soap in her hand and wanted it. Lily was scared and ran away. The dog chased her and took the soap. Lily went home sad and told her mom what happened. They had no soap and felt bad.
