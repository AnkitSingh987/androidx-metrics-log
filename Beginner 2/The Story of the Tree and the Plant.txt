Title: The Story of the Tree and the Plant

One day, a big tree and a small plant were talking. The big tree said, "I am so tall and strong. I have many big roots to help me stand. You are so small and weak." The small plant felt sad and said, "I will grow big and strong one day, too." Just then, a strong wind came and slapped the big tree. The wind was so strong that it broke some of the tree's branches. The small plant saw this and felt scared. But then, something unexpected happened. The small plant's roots started to grow bigger and stronger. They grew so big that they could help the big tree stand up again. The big tree said, "Thank you, small plant. I am sorry I was sour to you. We can be friends and help each other now." And they lived happily together, helping each other grow.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a hairy dog named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friends, but sometimes they would quarrel over toys. One day, Sam found a big stone in the park. Sam ran to his friend, a little cat named Lily, and said, "Look! I found a big stone. Let's play with it!" Lily looked at the stone and said, "No, I don't want to play with a stone. It's not fun." Sam felt sad and said, "But I think it's fun. Let's try!" Lily thought for a moment and said, "Okay, let's try." They rolled the stone around the park and had a lot of fun. They didn't quarrel that day, and they learned that playing together was more fun than fighting. From that day on, Sam, Lily, and the big stone were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot was a very good dog who always helped his friends. One day, Spot and his friends went to play in the park. While they were playing, they saw a spark in the sky. The spark was very pretty and made everyone happy. They all watched the spark and clapped their hands. Spot barked and wagged his tail because he liked the spark too. After playing, Spot and his friends were very thirsty. They went to a big bowl to drink some water. Spot drank first and then let his friends drink too. They all felt better after they drank the water. Spot was a very dependable friend, and they all had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was sweet and kind, but she was also very greedy. One day, while walking with her mommy, Jane saw a big, shiny gold coin on the ground. She quickly picked it up and said, "Mommy, look I found a gold coin! Can I have it?" Her mommy smiled and said, "You can keep it, Jane. But remember, greediness can include too much of something and make it bad." Jane said, "Okay, okay!" and put the gold coin in her pocket. She and her mommy kept walking. But then, Jane saw another gold coin lying on the ground and she couldn't resist. She said, "Mommy, look here is another one. Can I have it too?" Her mommy felt sad. She said, "No, Jane, two gold coins is enough. Greediness can become too much and it won't be sweet anymore." Jane nodded, and put the second gold coin back on the ground. She learnt that sometimes it's important to include just the right amount of something, so it's pleasant and sweet.

Tom and Sam are brothers. They like to play pretend. Today they are playing cops and robbers. Tom is the cop and Sam is the robber. Sam has a long scarf that he uses as a mask. "Stop, robber! You are under arrest!" Tom says. He runs after Sam with his toy gun. "No, you can't catch me!" Sam says. He runs away from Tom and hides behind the sofa. Tom looks for Sam. He sees the scarf sticking out from behind the sofa. He sneaks up to the sofa and jumps on Sam. "Got you, robber! You are going to jail!" Tom says. He grabs Sam and drags him to the closet. The closet is the jail. "Let me go, cop! You are mean!" Sam says. He tries to get out of Tom's grip. "No, you have to stay in jail. You are a bad robber!" Tom says. He opens the closet and pushes Sam inside. He closes the door and locks it with a toy key. "Help, help! Let me out!" Sam says. He bangs on the door. Tom laughs. He thinks he is a good cop. He hears a loud roar from the closet. "What was that?" Tom says. He gets scared. He thinks there is a monster in the closet. "Roar! I am a monster! I will eat you, cop!" Sam says. He makes a scary voice. Tom screams. He drops the toy key and runs away from the closet. He thinks the robber turned into a monster. Sam laughs. He thinks he is a smart robber. He picks up the toy key and unlocks the door. He comes out of the closet. "Ha ha, I tricked you, cop! I am not a monster, I am a robber!" Sam says. He runs after Tom with his scarf. Tom and Sam chase each other around the house. They have fun playing pretend. They are brothers.
