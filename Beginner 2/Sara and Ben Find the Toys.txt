Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben Find the Toys

Sara and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. But then their mom came and said, "It's time for your nap, kids. You need to rest and be quiet for a while." Sara and Ben did not want to nap. They wanted to keep playing. They said, "No, mom, no nap! We are not sleepy! We are having fun!" Mom was persistent. She said, "Yes, you are sleepy. Look at your eyes. They are droopy and red. And you are cranky and loud. You need to nap, or you will be unhappy later. Come on, let's go to your room. I have a surprise for you." Sara and Ben followed mom to their room. They hoped the surprise was something good, like a new toy or a snack. But when they got to their room, they saw the surprise was a big white sheet. Mom had covered their bed with the sheet. She said, "This is your nap tent. You can pretend you are camping in the snow. It will be cozy and fun. Now, get under the sheet and close your eyes. I will be back soon." Sara and Ben did not like the nap tent. They thought it was boring and cold. They did not want to pretend they were camping in the snow. They wanted to play with their toys in the living room. They said, "No, mom, no nap tent! We don't like it! We want to go back!" Mom was persistent. She said, "No, you have to stay in the nap tent. It's good for you. It will help you sleep and dream. And when you wake up, you can play with your toys again. Trust me, you will like it. Now, be good and quiet. I love you." Mom left the room and closed the door. Sara and Ben were angry and sad. They did not trust mom. They did not love the nap tent. They wanted to escape. They tried to lift the sheet and crawl out. But the sheet was too heavy and tight. They could not move. They could not breathe. They started to cry and scream. But no one heard them. No one came to help them. They were trapped in the nap tent. They were scared and alone. They wished they had listened to mom. They wished they had taken a nap. But it was too late. The nap tent was their end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a kind daughter who loved to help her mom and dad. One day, Lucy found a dead bird in the yard. She felt sad and wanted to do something. Lucy went to her mom and said, "Mom, I found a dead bird. What can we do?" Her mom hugged her and said, "We can bury the bird and say a nice thing about it." So, Lucy and her mom dug a small hole and put the bird in it. They covered it with dirt and put a pretty rock on top. Lucy said, "Bird, you were pretty and now you can rest." After that, Lucy asked her mom, "Why do sad things happen?" Her mom said, "Sad things happen, but they help us learn to care for others and be strong." From that day on, Lucy learned to be kind and help others when sad things happen. The moral of the story is that even when sad things happen, we can learn to be kind and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted girl named Lily. Lily loved to write and draw pretty pictures. She had a magic jewel that helped her make her pictures even more beautiful. One day, Lily found a sad boy named Tim. Tim had lost his toy. Lily wanted to help Tim. She had an idea! Lily used her magic jewel and wrote a story about a happy toy finding its way back home. Lily showed the story to Tim. Tim smiled and felt happy. He said, "Thank you, Lily!" They looked for the toy together and found it under a big tree. Tim was so happy to have his toy back. Lily learned that helping others is a wonderful thing to do. Her magic jewel made her gifted, but her kind heart made her special. And that is the most important gift of all.
