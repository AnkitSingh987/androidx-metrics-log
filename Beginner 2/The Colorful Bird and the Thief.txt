Category: Beginner
Title: The Colorful Bird and the Thief

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a colorful bird. The bird loved to sing and play with the kids. One day, a new friend came to the town. It was a thief. The thief wanted to take things from the people in the town. The colorful bird saw the thief and wanted to help him. The bird said, "Welcome to our town! You should be nice and play with us." The thief looked at the bird and thought about what it said. After some time, the thief decided to be nice and not take things. The colorful bird and the kids welcomed the thief to play with them. They all became good friends and had a lot of fun together. And the town was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big bookcase in his room. He loved to own many books. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the bookcase and said, "Wow, you have many books!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I love to read." They picked a book and sat down to read together. They read about a cat and a dog who were friends. They laughed and had a fun time. The end.

Once there was a little boy named Jack. Jack was three years old and he was looking for something to do. One day, he realised that he had lost his favourite truck! He was so sad and frightened that he started to cry. His mother came to see what was wrong and heard him say, "I lost my truck." His mother hugged him and took him outside to look for it. They searched every corner of the garden but couldn't find it. Then, they heard a strange noise coming from the bushes. It sounded like something unusual was inside! Cautiously, Jack peeked into the bushes and found his truck. He was overjoyed and thanked his mother for helping him find it. They both laughed and Jack hugged the truck tightly.

Ben and Mia are playing with their toys in the living room. Ben has a big truck and Mia has a doll. Mia wants to put her doll in the truck, but Ben says no. "That is my truck. Your doll does not fit in my truck. Go away!" Ben says. Mia is sad and angry. She wants to play with the truck too. She thinks of a plan. She waits until Ben is not looking and then she removes the wheels from the truck. She puts them under the sofa. "Ha ha, now you cannot play with your truck!" Mia says. Ben hears Mia and turns around. He sees his truck without wheels. He is very upset. He cries and shouts. "Who did this? Who removed my wheels? Give them back!" Ben says. Mia laughs and says, "I did it. I removed your wheels. Now your truck does not fit anywhere. You are mean to me, so I am mean to you." Ben and Mia start to fight. They pull each other's hair and scratch each other's faces. Their mom hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees the mess and the tears. She is not happy. "What is going on here? Why are you fighting? Why did you remove the wheels from the truck?" Mom says. Ben and Mia stop fighting and look at mom. They are scared and ashamed. They do not know what to say. Mom sighs and says, "You need to learn to share and be nice to each other. You are brother and sister. You should love each other, not hurt each other. Now, say sorry and hug each other. Then, put the wheels back on the truck and play nicely." Ben and Mia say sorry and hug each other. They feel sorry for what they did. They put the wheels back on the truck and play nicely. They take turns to put their toys in the truck. They smile and laugh. Mom smiles too. She is happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big race. All the animals wanted to win the race. The dog, the cat, and the bird were all very fast. They were all very excited to run. On the day of the race, the dog saw a filthy ball near the start line. He loved to play with balls. He picked it up and ran with it. The cat and the bird raced ahead, but the dog did not care. He was having fun with the ball. The race went past a jail. The dog saw a little mouse inside the jail. The dog felt sad for the mouse. He stopped racing and went to help the mouse. He used the filthy ball to open the jail door. The mouse was free! The dog, the mouse, and the ball all raced to the finish line together. They did not win the race, but they were all happy to be friends.
