Category: Beginner
Title: Whenever and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was very ignorant. Whenever someone tried to show her something new, she always said she knew it already and waved it away. One day, the little girl's mommy brought her a measure. The measure was very special, as it had the power to restore things to their like-new state. The little girl was not impressed. She pointed out she knew what the measure was and saw no use for it. But her mommy wasn't deterred. She patiently showed the little girl how to measure the wall and the floor to make sure they were even and straight. The little girl muttered in surprise as she saw how the measure could help her mommy in her work. When her mommy was done, everything was perfect and it looked like it was brand new. Her mommy smiled and told her that the same measure of attention to detail should be put into everything she does. The little girl felt ashamed of her ignorance, and she now realized the power of the measure she had shrugged off so carelessly. She promised herself to pay attention to detail from that day on. The moral of the story is that learning and paying attention to the details can help restore and make things better.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a long cloth in her room. She thought it was a fun toy, so she took it outside to play with her friend, Tom. Sue and Tom played with the cloth all day. They tied it to a tree and made a swing. They had so much fun! Sue said, "Tom, let's stay and play with this cloth all day." Tom agreed, "Yes, let's stay and play!" As they played, a big wind came and blew the cloth away. Sue and Tom were sad. They looked for the cloth, but it was gone. They went home, feeling sad. The next day, Sue's mom told her that the long cloth was not a toy. It was a special cloth for a big party. Sue and Tom felt bad, but they did not know. Sue's mom was not mad. She said, "It's okay. We can find another cloth for the party." Sue and Tom learned to ask before using things that were not theirs. They stayed friends and had many more fun days together.

Ben and Jen are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. One day, they find a big box in the hall. It has their names on it. "Look, Jen, a box for us!" Ben says. "Who sent it?" Jen asks. Ben reads the label. "It is from Grandma and Grandpa. They mail it to us." "Wow, they are so nice. Can we open it?" Jen says. Ben nods. He tries to pull the tape, but it is hard. He can't open it. "Help me, Jen. The tape is hard," Ben says. Jen helps him. They pull and pull. The tape breaks. They open the box. Inside, they see many things. They see a teddy bear, a puzzle, a ball, a book, and a card. "Look, Jen, a teddy bear for you!" Ben says. "Look, Ben, a puzzle for you!" Jen says. They hug their gifts. They are happy. They see the card. It has a picture of Grandma and Grandpa. They smile. "Let's read the card," Ben says. He opens the card. He reads the words. "Dear Ben and Jen, we miss you very much. We hope you like the gifts. We love you. Grandma and Grandpa." Ben and Jen smile. They love Grandma and Grandpa too. They run to Mom and Dad. They show them the box and the card. "Look, Mom, Dad, a box from Grandma and Grandpa!" Ben says. "They mail it to us. They are so nice. They love us," Jen says. Mom and Dad hug them. They are proud of them. "Let's call Grandma and Grandpa and say thank you," Mom says. They go to the phone. They call Grandma and Grandpa. They say thank you. They say they love them. Grandma and Grandpa are happy. They say they love them too. They talk for a while. They tell them about their toys and books. They tell them about their side of the world. They say goodbye. They hang up the phone. Ben and Jen go back to the box. They play with their gifts. They are happy. They have a nice family.
