Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Flower

Tim and Sam are noisy youth. They like to play and run and shout. They do not like to sit and be quiet. One day, they go to the park with Mom. Mom says, "You can play, but do not touch the flowers. They are for everyone to see and smell, not to pick and break." Tim and Sam nod, but they do not listen. They see a big, red flower. It looks pretty and soft. They want to touch it. They run to the flower and reach out their hands. "Stop!" Mom says. "Do not touch the flower!" But Tim and Sam do not stop. They touch the flower. They pull it out of the ground. They laugh and hold it up. "Look, Mom, we have a flower!" they say. Mom is not happy. She is angry and sad. She takes the flower from them and puts it back in the ground. She says, "You did a bad thing. You hurt the flower and the park. You did not listen to me. You have to say sorry and go home." Tim and Sam feel bad. They see the flower is broken and wilted. They see Mom is upset. They say, "Sorry, Mom. Sorry, flower. We will not touch the flowers again." Mom hugs them and says, "I love you, but you have to learn to be careful and respectful. The flowers are alive and beautiful. They make the park nice and happy. You can look at them and smell them, but you cannot touch them. Do you understand?" Tim and Sam nod. They understand. They say, "We understand, Mom. We love you and the flowers. We will be quiet and gentle." Mom smiles and says, "That's good. Now, let's go home and have a snack. Maybe we can read a book about flowers and learn more about them." Tim and Sam agree. They hold Mom's hand and walk home. They are not noisy youth anymore. They are quiet and curious. They want to know more about the flowers and the park. They want to be good and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Tom. Tom liked to do yoga every day. He would stretch and bend in many ways. All the other cats wanted to be like Tom. One day, Tom was doing yoga in the park. He saw a big tree with a pretty ball stuck high up. A little girl was sad because she could not reach the ball. Tom wanted to help the girl get her ball back. Tom had an idea. He used his yoga skills to reach the ball. He stretched and pulled his body to move up the tree. The other cats watched in surprise. They did not know Tom could do that! Tom got the ball and gave it to the little girl. She was very happy, and the cats learned that yoga could do more than just make them famous.

Once upon a time, there was a pink cart. The cart belonged to a little girl named Sally. Sally loved her pink cart very much. She would push it around and put her toys in it. One sunny day, Sally went to the park with her pink cart. She saw her friend, Timmy, playing with his ball. Timmy looked at Sally's cart and said, "Wow, I like your pink cart, Sally!" "Thank you, Timmy!" Sally replied, "It belongs to me, and I love it!" They played together all day, taking turns pushing the pink cart and putting their toys in it. As the sun began to set, Sally and Timmy knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye and promised to play together again soon. Sally pushed her pink cart home, happy to have shared her special toy with her friend.

John and his dad went out to the backyard. John saw a big pile of junk there. "What's that, dad?" he asked. His dad said, "It's junk. That means it's things we don't use." John looked at all the rough things. He saw old toys, a broken chair and a rusty car. He was confused. "Why are they all together?" he asked. His dad explained, "We need to separate them, so we can throw away what we don't need and keep the things that still work." John helped his dad sort through the pile. Dad placed the good things in one pile and the junk in another. When they were done, John felt proud of his work. Dad gave him a high five and said, "Thanks for helping me, John!"
