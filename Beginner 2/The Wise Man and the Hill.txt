Category: Beginner
Title: The Wise Man and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a wise man who had a motorcycle. He loved to ride it up and down the big hill. The motorcycle was very fast and made a loud noise. The wise man was always happy when he was on his motorcycle. One day, the wise man thought that he could improve his motorcycle. He wanted it to be even faster and louder. So, he worked on it for many days. He put on new parts and made it look very nice. When he was done, the wise man got on his improved motorcycle and rode up and down the big hill. It was faster and louder than before. He was very happy and all the people in the town clapped and cheered for him. The wise man knew that improving his motorcycle was a good idea.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy rock. It was a special rock because it could change its color. Every day, the rock would have a new color. The rock lived in a park where kids liked to play. One sunny day, the rock had a bright red color. A little girl saw the rock and smiled. She touched the rock and said, "Wow, you are so pretty!" The rock felt happy to make the little girl smile. The next day, the rock had a blue color. A little boy came to the park and saw the rock. He sat on the rock and said, "You are very heavy, but I like your color!" The rock felt proud to be a part of the little boy's fun day at the park. And so, the heavy rock with many colors kept making the kids happy every day.

Tom was a little boy who liked to play outside. He had a red coat, a blue hat and a yellow scarf. He also had a big dog named Rex, who was his best friend and hero. Rex was strong and brave and loved to run and jump with Tom. One day, Tom and Rex went to the park. They saw a big pond with ducks and fish. Tom wanted to feed the ducks, so he took some bread from his pocket. He threw some pieces into the water and watched the ducks come closer. Rex barked and wagged his tail. But then, Tom saw something shiny in the pond. He leaned over the edge to get a better look. It was a coin! Tom thought it was pretty and wanted to have it. He reached out his hand to grab it, but he slipped and fell into the pond. Splash! Tom felt cold and wet. He was scared and started to cry. He tried to swim, but the water was deep and he could not touch the bottom. He called for Rex, who heard him and ran to the pond. Rex saw Tom in the water and jumped in to save him. He swam to Tom and let him hold on to his fur. He pulled him to the shore and licked his face. Tom was happy to see Rex. He hugged him and said, "Thank you, Rex. You are my hero. You saved me." Rex barked and wagged his tail. He was happy to see Tom. He helped him get out of the pond and shook off the water. He snuggled with him and kept him warm. Tom was still cold and wet, but he felt better with Rex by his side. He stopped crying and started to shiver. He said, "Let's go home, Rex. I need a dry coat and a hot drink. And you need a treat. You are a good dog." Rex barked and nodded. He followed Tom to his home, where his mom was waiting for them. She saw them and said, "Oh, Tom, what happened? You are soaked!" She took them inside and gave them towels and blankets. She made them some soup and cocoa. She said, "You are lucky, Tom. Rex is a hero. He saved you from the pond. You should be careful next time. And you should be proud of Rex. He is a good dog." She hugged them and kissed them. Tom and Rex felt warm and dry. They felt comfortable and safe. They ate their soup and cocoa and thanked Tom's mom. They said, "We love you, mom. And we love each other. We are best friends and heroes." They smiled and cuddled on the couch. They fell asleep and dreamed of ducks and coins and ponds.
