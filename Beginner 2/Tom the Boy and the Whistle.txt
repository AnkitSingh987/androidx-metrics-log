Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Whistle

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a whistle that he loved to play with. One day, he took his whistle to the park to play. At the park, he met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tom's whistle and wanted to play with it too. Tom didn't want to share his whistle. They both wanted the whistle and didn't know what to do. So, they decided to split the whistle in half. Tom and Sue pulled the whistle from both ends. With a loud snap, the whistle broke into two pieces. Now, they both had a part of the whistle. They looked at each other and realized that breaking the whistle was not a good idea. They felt sad. Tom said, "I'm sorry, Sue. I should have shared my whistle with you." Sue replied, "I'm sorry too, Tom. I should have asked nicely." They both felt grateful for learning to share and be kind. From that day on, Tom and Sue became good friends. They played together and shared their toys. And they always remembered the broken whistle and the lesson it taught them.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a kite. Lila wants to play with the ball. Tom wants to play with the kite. "I want to play with the ball," Lila says. "No, I want to play with the kite," Tom says. They disagree. They are not happy. They do not share. A human comes to the park. He has a dog. The dog is big and brown. The dog sees the ball and the kite. The dog is ready to play. The dog runs to the ball and the kite. He grabs the ball with his mouth. He jumps on the kite. He breaks the kite. He makes a mess. Lila and Tom are sad. They lose their toys. They look at the dog. The dog wags his tail. He thinks he is funny. The human says, "I'm sorry. My dog is naughty. He likes to play too much. Do you want to play with him?" Lila and Tom look at each other. They nod. They forgive the dog. They play with the dog. They have fun. They are friends again.

One day, a little ant went for a walk. The sun was light and warm. The ant was very happy. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. So, he started to go up the tree. As the ant climbed, he found a nice spot to lie down and rest. He was very tired. But, when he lay down, he heard a strange sound. The ant was scared. He did not know what it was. The sound came from a big bird. The bird was stuck in the tree. The ant wanted to help. He climbed up to the bird and used his strong legs to push the bird free. The bird was so happy! The ant and the bird became best friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange taxi. The taxi loved to drive around the town and help people. One day, it rained a lot, and the taxi got all wet. After the rain stopped, the sun came out. The taxi wanted to dry, so it drove under the sun. As it drove, the taxi saw a girl named Lily. She was sad because she was wet from the rain too. The taxi stopped and said, "Hi, Lily! I can help you get dry. Let's drive under the sun together." Lily smiled and got into the taxi. They drove around town, and soon both the taxi and Lily were dry and happy. From that day on, they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her favorite toy, a soft pillow. One day, Fluffy saw a spicy smell coming from the kitchen. She wanted to find out what the smell was, so she left her pillow and went to the kitchen. In the kitchen, Fluffy found a big, red pepper. She thought it was a toy and started to play with it. But when she bit it, she felt a hot, spicy taste in her mouth. Fluffy did not like it. She struggled to get the spicy taste out of her mouth. She ran around the house, trying to find something to make it go away. Fluffy remembered her soft pillow and ran back to it. She rubbed her face on the pillow, and the spicy taste started to go away. The pillow saved the day! From then on, Fluffy knew to stay away from the spicy smell in the kitchen. And she loved her pillow even more.
