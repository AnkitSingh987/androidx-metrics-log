Category: Beginner
Title: Spot and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play in the park. One sunny day, Spot saw an enormous ball in the park. He was so excited! Spot ran to the ball and tried to push it with his nose. But he was not careful, and he stumbled on a small rock. As a result, he fell down and the ball rolled away. Spot felt sad. A little girl named Lily saw what happened. She walked over to Spot and said, "Don't worry, Spot. Let's try again together." Spot and Lily pushed the big ball and had lots of fun. They played all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very clean dog. Today, he wanted to go on a walk with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. As they walked, Spot saw a big chair. Spot wanted to sit on the chair, so he jumped up and sat down. Sue laughed and clapped her hands, happy to see Spot sit on the chair. But then, something unexpected happened. The chair started to move! It was not a chair, but a big turtle! The turtle did not mind Spot sitting on it. Spot and Sue made a new friend, and they all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play near the creek behind his house. He liked to throw rocks in the water and watch the fish swim. The fish were always happy to see Tim. One day, Tim saw a small, anxious bird near the creek. The bird was hungry and could not find any food. Tim wanted to help the bird. He thought about what he could give the bird to eat. He remembered that he had some bread at home. Tim ran to his house to get the bread. He broke the bread into small pieces and gave them to the bird. The bird was very happy and ate the bread quickly. Tim felt good that he could help the bird. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends, and they played near the creek every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. Inside the barn, there were many animals. There was a little duck who loved to play in the deep pond near the barn. One day, the little duck saw a shiny thing at the bottom of the pond. The little duck wanted the shiny thing very much. So, he went into the deep pond to get it. But the water was too deep for the little duck. He started to struggle. He tried to swim back up, but he could not. The other animals saw the little duck struggle. They tried to help him, but they could not reach him. The little duck could not get out of the deep pond. The animals were very sad. The little duck should not have gone into the deep pond. The moral of the story is to not go into deep water if you cannot swim.

Once upon a time, a bright sun shone in the sky. It was a happy day. In a big green park, a little girl named Mia played with her red ball. She loved to play outside. As she played, Mia saw a cute bird with a blue heart on its chest. The bird could talk! It said, "Hi Mia! I like your red ball." Mia was happy to meet a talking bird. She said, "Hi bird! Do you want to play with me?" The bird with the blue heart was excited. It said, "Yes, I want to play!" So, Mia and the bird played in the park all day. They had lots of fun together. The sun was bright, and their hearts were full of joy. They became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a blue cat. The blue cat had a friend, a big dog. They liked to play together. One day, they went to a park to play. In the park, they saw a man. The man had a box of mints. The man saw the blue cat and the big dog. He said, "Hello, do you want a mint?" The man offered the blue cat and the big dog a mint. The blue cat and the big dog took the mint. They ate the mint. But the mint was not good. It made them feel sick. The blue cat and the big dog went home sad.
