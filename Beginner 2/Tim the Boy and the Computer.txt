Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Computer

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his computer. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with Sam, but he didn't want to shut his computer. Tim's mom saw this and told him, "Be careful, Tim. If you don't shut your computer, it might break." But Tim didn't listen. He kept playing with his computer and the ball at the same time. Soon, Tim and Sam started to fight. They both wanted the ball and the computer. Tim's mom came back and said, "If you both share, you can have fun together." So, Tim shut his computer and played with the ball with Sam. They learned that sharing is important and that being careful with their things is good too.

Once upon a time, in a small nation, there lived a smart boy named Tim. He loved to help people and learn new things. One day, Tim met a sad girl named Lily. She was crying because she lost the key to her toy box. Tim wanted to help her. "Let's try to unlock the toy box without the key," said Tim. "We can use a stick to open it." Tim picked up a stick and carefully put it in the keyhole. He wiggled it around, and the toy box opened! Lily was so happy, and she thanked Tim for his help. From that day on, Tim and Lily became good friends. They played together and helped each other. The people in their nation learned from them, and they all became kind and helpful too. The moral of the story is that when we help others, we can make our world a better place.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect rocks. He had big rocks, small rocks, and rocks in all colors. His friend, Sam, was envious of his rock collection. One day, Tim went to the park to collect more rocks. He saw a big, blue rock under a tree. He picked it up and put it in his bag. Then, he saw a small, red rock by the slide. He picked it up and put it in his bag too. At the park, Tim met a girl named Lily. Lily was looking at her own rock collection. She had a shiny, green rock that looked like a lung. Tim said, "Wow, that's a cool rock! Can I have it?" Lily smiled and said, "Sure, if you give me one of your rocks!" Tim gave Lily the big, blue rock and they became friends. They spent the day collecting rocks together, and Tim's rock collection grew even bigger.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play with their bikes. One day, they see a big truck on the street. The truck has many boxes. The truck driver is careless. He does not look where he goes. He drops some boxes on the way. "Look, Sue, the truck driver is silly. He loses his boxes," Tom says. "Let's help him, Tom. We can deliver the boxes to the houses," Sue says. They pick up the boxes and put them on their bikes. They ride to the houses and knock on the doors. They give the boxes to the people. "Thank you, kids. You are very kind. What are your names?" a lady asks. "I am Tom and this is Sue. We are friends. We saw the truck driver drop the boxes. We wanted to help," Tom says. "You are very helpful, Tom and Sue. The truck driver was careless. He should be more careful. You did a good job. Here, have some cookies," the lady says. Tom and Sue smile. They eat the cookies. They are happy. They delivered the boxes. They made the people happy. They are good friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to visit his uncle. His uncle lived in a big house with a lot of trees. Tim loved to play in his uncle's yard. While Tim was playing, he saw a small dog. The dog looked confused. Tim went close to the dog and said, "Hi dog, do you want to play with me?" The dog wagged its tail and started to play with Tim. As they were playing, the dog suddenly bit Tim's toy. Tim was surprised and said, "No, dog! Don't bite my toy!" The dog stopped and looked sad. Just then, Tim's uncle came out of the house. He laughed and said, "Tim, this dog is a toy too! It's a robot dog that I made. It's okay if it bites your toy, they can play together!" Tim smiled, and he played with both his toy and the robot dog all day long.
