Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Boat

One sunny day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mommy. They saw the big blue sea and the waves. Tim loved to watch the waves go up and down. He liked to jump over them and play in the water. "Mommy, can you bring my toy boat?" Tim asked. His mommy smiled and said, "Yes, it's easy for me to bring it." She went to their bag and took out Tim's toy boat. Tim was very happy. Tim put his toy boat in the water and watched it float on the waves. The boat went up and down, just like the waves. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He played with his boat and the waves all day long. At the end of the day, Tim and his mommy went home, tired but happy.

One day, a big bus driver named Tom was driving down the road. He saw a small car next to him. The driver of the small car looked at Tom's big bus and felt envious. He wished he could drive a big bus too. The small car driver said to Tom, "I like your big bus. I want to drive one too." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, but your small car is nice too. It can go fast and park easily." The small car driver thought about it and said, "You're right. My car is good too. Maybe one day, we can switch and you can drive my small car, and I can drive your big bus." Tom laughed and said, "That sounds like fun!" So, the big bus driver and the small car driver became friends. They were happy with what they had, but also knew they could try new things together. And they lived happily ever after.

Mia and Sam were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to swing, slide and run on the grass. They saw many other kids and some dogs, but they did not talk to them. They only talked to their mom and each other. One day, a stranger came to the park. He had a big hat and a long coat. He smiled at Mia and Sam and said, "Hello, little ones. Do you want to see something fun?" He took out a shiny ball from his pocket and tossed it in the air. It made a funny sound and changed colors. Mia and Sam were curious. They wanted to see the ball. They walked towards the stranger, but their mom saw them and called them back. "Mia, Sam, come here. Don't go to the stranger. He is not our friend. We don't know him." Mia and Sam ran back to their mom. They were scared and confused. They did not understand why the stranger was not nice. Their mom hugged them and said, "It's okay, my loves. The stranger was trying to trick you. He wanted to take you away from me. You should never go to a stranger, even if he has something fun. You should always stay with me or someone you know and trust." Mia and Sam nodded. They felt better. They said sorry to their mom and thanked her for keeping them safe. They decided to play some more, but they stayed close to their mom. They did not look at the stranger anymore. He left the park soon after. Mia and Sam were tired after playing. They were sleepy and hungry. Their mom said, "Let's go home, my loves. We can have some snacks and read a story. Then we can settle in our beds and have a nice nap." Mia and Sam agreed. They liked to nap with their mom. They held her hand and walked home. They were happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a brilliant idea. She wanted to borrow her mom's big hat for the day. She thought it would be fun to wear it outside. Lucy put on the hat and went to the park. She saw her friend, Max. Max wanted to play a game. They played tag and hide-and-seek. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Lucy's hat started to move! It was not just a hat. It was a magic hat! The hat could talk and fly. The hat said, "Hi, Lucy! Let's go for a ride!" Lucy and Max got on the hat, and it took them high up in the sky. They had a new, fun adventure with the magic hat. And that was just the start!
