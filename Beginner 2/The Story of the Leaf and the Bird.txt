Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Leaf and the Bird

One day, a little caterpillar was walking in the garden. He was very hungry. He saw a big leaf and wanted to eat it. But the leaf was on a tall plant. The caterpillar thought, "I will steal the leaf when no one is looking." The caterpillar climbed the tall plant to get the leaf. As he was climbing, he saw a dead bug on the ground. He felt sad for the bug. The caterpillar said to himself, "I hope nothing bad happens to me." When the caterpillar reached the top, he started to eat the leaf. Suddenly, a big bird saw him. The bird wanted to eat the caterpillar. The caterpillar was scared. He remembered the dead bug and thought, "I don't want to end up like that bug." The caterpillar quickly hid under the leaf. The bird couldn't find him. The caterpillar was safe. After the bird left, the caterpillar said, "I will never steal again." From that day on, the caterpillar only ate leaves that were easy to reach. He grew big and strong. One day, he turned into a beautiful butterfly.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy cars in the garden. They liked to make them go fast and make loud noises. Tom had a red car with a big motor. Lily had a blue car with a small motor. "Look at my car, Lily. It has a big motor. It can go faster than yours," Tom said. "No, it can't, Tom. Your car is too heavy. It can't go fast. My car has a small motor. It can go faster than yours," Lily said. They decided to have a race to see who was right. They lined up their cars at the start line and counted to three. "One, two, three, go!" they shouted. They pushed their cars as hard as they could. Tom's car went fast at first, but then it slowed down. Lily's car went fast and passed Tom's car. "Ha, ha, I win, I win!" Lily said. Tom was sad. He looked at his car. He saw that the motor was broken. It had smoke coming out of it. "Oh, no, my car is broken. The motor is broken. It can't go fast anymore," Tom said. Lily saw that Tom was sad. She felt sorry for him. She stopped her car and went to him. "I'm sorry, Tom. Your car is broken. Do you want to play with mine?" she asked. Tom smiled. He liked Lily. She was a good friend. "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend. Can we share your car?" he asked. Lily nodded. She liked Tom. He was a good friend. "Yes, we can share my car. We can take turns. We can have fun together," she said. They hugged and played with the blue car. They had fun together. They didn't care who had the big motor or the small motor. They only cared about being friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ruby. She was three years old and she loved her family’s van. Every day, she liked to take a ride in it with her Mom. One day, when they were out driving, they stopped at a red light. While they waited, a rude man in a truck pulled up behind them. He beeped his horn and shouted for them to “Move it!” Ruby's Mom was very angry and she said, “That was very rude! We are waited for the light to turn green.” Ruby looked out the window and said, “He didn't have to be mean. That made me feel bad.” Ruby’s Mom reached across and held her hand. She said, “It’s okay, sweetheart. He was just impatient. Let’s take a deep breath and enjoy our ride in the van.” Ruby smiled and nodded. They drove away together, with no more rudeness.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. Lila has a hoop. It is light and pink. Ben has a ball. It is heavy and blue. "Look, Ben, I can roll my hoop!" Lila says. She pushes the hoop with her hand. The hoop goes fast on the grass. "Wow, Lila, that is fun!" Ben says. He throws his ball in the air. The ball comes down and bounces. "Can I try your hoop, Lila?" Ben asks. "Sure, Ben, you can try my hoop. But you have to follow me. I will show you how to roll it." Lila says. "OK, Lila, I will follow you." Ben says. He gives his ball to Lila. He takes the hoop from Lila. Lila and Ben run on the grass. Lila holds the ball. Ben holds the hoop. They laugh and smile. They are happy. They are friends.
