Category: Beginner
Title: The Baby and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little baby. The baby was very lucky. He had a big, soft bear. The bear was his best friend. They played together every day. One day, the baby and the bear went to the park. They saw a big tree. The baby wanted to hug the tree. The bear helped the baby. They hugged the tree together. It was fun. Then, it was time to go home. The baby and the bear were tired. They hugged each other. The baby felt happy and lucky to have a friend like the bear. They went to sleep and had sweet dreams.

One day, a cheerful girl named Amy wanted to paint a big picture. She wanted to paint her nation with happy colors. She took her paint and brush and started to paint on a large paper. Amy's little brother, Tim, saw her painting. He wanted to paint too. Tim said, "I want to paint with you!" But he took the paint and made a big mess on Amy's picture. Amy was sad and said, "You ruined my painting!" Tim felt bad and wanted to make Amy happy again. He said, "I'm sorry, let's paint together." They both painted a new picture of their nation. It was full of cheerful colors and they were both happy with their work.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very restless. He loved to play and run all day. One Sunday, Tim was playing with his friend, Max. They were having lots of fun. Tim and Max were playing with a ball. They were kicking it back and forth. Then, Tim kicked the ball too hard. It went into Max's mom's garden and broke a flower. Max was sad and Tim felt bad. Tim said, "I am sorry, Max. I did not mean to break the flower." Max looked at Tim and said, "I forgive you, Tim. We all make mistakes." They hugged and played again. The moral value of this story is to always say sorry and forgive others when they make mistakes.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. Tim saw a big anchor in the sand. He wanted to play with it because it looked fun. But his mom said, "No, Tim. That anchor is dangerous. You could get hurt." Tim didn't listen to his mom. When she wasn't looking, he went to the anchor and started to play. Suddenly, a big crab came out from under the anchor. The crab wanted to fight Tim. Tim was scared and called his mom. "Mom, help! The crab wants to fight me!" His mom came, but she couldn't see the crab. The crab was hiding again. She said, "Tim, I told you not to play with the anchor. Let's go home." Tim was sad and still scared of the crab. They went home, and Tim never played with the anchor again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the garden. He saw a vine with pretty fruit on it. He wanted to pick the fruit, but he did not know if it was good to eat. It was an unknown fruit to him. He thought, "What if it is not yummy?" Tim asked his friend, Sue, to help him. Sue did not know the fruit either. They both felt scared. They did not want to pick the unknown fruit and get sick. They needed help to solve the problem. Then, Tim's mom came to the garden. She knew about the vine and the fruit. She told Tim and Sue that the fruit was safe and yummy. They could pick it and eat it. Tim and Sue felt happy. They picked the fruit, ate it, and shared it with their friends. The unknown fruit was a sweet surprise for them.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a rod. He thought it was a magic rod. He wanted to hide it from his friends so that he could keep it for himself. So, he hid the rod under his bed. Later, his friend Sam came over to play. They were playing with toys when Sam saw the rod under the bed. "What is that?" Sam asked. Tim got angry and said, "It is mine! Give it back!" Sam was sad and went home. The next day, Tim and Sam went to the park. They saw a dog with the same rod in its mouth. The dog was happy and wagging its tail. Tim and Sam looked at each other and laughed. They realized that the rod was not magic, but just a toy for the dog. They played with the dog and forgot about the magic rod. In the end, they learned that sharing is better than hiding things from friends.
