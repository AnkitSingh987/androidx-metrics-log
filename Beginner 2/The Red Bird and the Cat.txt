Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little red bird. The bird lived in a big tree. One day, the red bird found an orange on the ground. The bird picked up the orange with its beak. The red bird saw a small cat under the tree. The cat looked hungry. The bird said, "Do you want this orange?" The cat said, "Yes, please!" So, the red bird gave the orange to the cat. The cat ate the orange and felt happy. The cat said, "Thank you, red bird!" They became good friends. From that day on, they played together every day under the big tree.

Anna and Ben are going to the park with Mom. They want to play with their hoop and ball. Mom says they need to pack some snacks and water in a bag. "Can I pack the cookies, Mom?" Anna asks. She likes the cookies with chocolate chips. They are yummy. "Yes, you can, Anna. But not too many. We need to share with Ben and save some for later," Mom says. She gives Anna a small box of cookies. "Can I pack the bananas, Mom?" Ben asks. He likes the bananas with brown spots. They are yummy too. "Yes, you can, Ben. But not too many. We need to share with Anna and save some for later," Mom says. She gives Ben a bunch of bananas. Anna and Ben put the cookies and the bananas in the bag. They also put some water bottles and napkins. Mom helps them zip the bag and carry it to the car. They drive to the park and find a nice spot under a tree. They put the bag and the hoop and the ball on the grass. They are ready to have fun. "Let's play with the hoop first," Anna says. She picks up the hoop and tries to roll it on the ground. "Let's play with the ball first," Ben says. He picks up the ball and tries to bounce it on the ground. They take turns playing with the hoop and the ball. They laugh and run and jump. They are happy. After a while, they get hungry and thirsty. They go back to the bag and open it. "Let's eat the cookies first," Anna says. She takes out the box of cookies and opens it. "Let's eat the bananas first," Ben says. He takes out a banana and peels it. They take turns eating the cookies and the bananas. They drink some water and wipe their mouths with the napkins. They are full. "Thank you for the yummy snacks, Mom," Anna says. "Thank you for the yummy snacks, Mom," Ben says. "You're welcome, my sweeties," Mom says. She hugs them and kisses them. "Now, let's pack up and go home. It's getting late." They put the empty box and the peels and the bottles and the napkins in the bag. They zip the bag and carry it to the car. They put the hoop and the ball in the trunk. They buckle up and drive home. They had a fun day at the park. They are tired. They are happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy some fruit. Lily saw a big, yellow lemon. She thought it was a ball. She picked it up and said, "Mom, can I have this ball?" Her mom smiled and said, "That's not a ball, Lily. That's a lemon." Lily didn't know what a lemon was, so her mom told her, "A lemon is a fruit. It's very sour, not sweet like other fruits." Lily wanted to try it, so her mom bought the lemon for her. When they got home, Lily took a bite of the lemon. She made a funny face because it was so sour. "This is not a nice fruit, mom!" she said. Lily's mom had an idea. She said, "Let's make lemonade with the lemon. It will be sweet and yummy." They squeezed the lemon and added some sugar and water. They mixed it all up and Lily took a sip. "Wow, this is so good!" she said. Just then, Lily's pet frog jumped up and kissed the lemonade. The lemonade turned into a tough, strong superhero! The superhero said, "Thank you, Lily and frog, for making me. I will protect you and your family." Lily and her mom were so surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend.
