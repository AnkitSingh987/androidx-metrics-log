Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Regular Cat and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a regular cat named Tom. Tom liked to eat. He would eat all day long. One day, Tom met a small bird named Sue. Sue was very nice and they became friends. Tom said to Sue, "I like to eat. Do you like to eat too?" Sue replied, "Yes, I like to eat. We can eat together!" So, Tom and Sue would eat together every day. They ate yummy food like bread, cheese, and fruit. One day, they found a big cake. Tom and Sue were very happy. They said, "Let's eat the cake together!" They ate and ate until the cake was all gone. Tom and Sue were full and happy. They played and laughed all day long. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

One day, a girl named Lily and her sister, Emma, went to the park. They saw a big blue ball near the swings. Lily wanted to play with the ball, but Emma wanted to play tag. "Let's play tag, Lily!" Emma said. But Lily did not want to join. She wanted to play with the blue ball. Emma was sad, but she let Lily play with the ball. Soon, a big boy came to the park. He saw the blue ball and wanted it. "Give me the ball!" he yelled. Lily did not want to give the ball to the big boy. The big boy got mad and took the ball from Lily. Lily cried and ran to Emma. "The big boy took the blue ball!" she said. Emma hugged her sister, but she was still sad. They went home without the blue ball and without playing tag.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had an amazing red mitten. She loved her mitten very much. It was her favorite thing to wear when it was cold outside. One day, Amy went to the park with her mom. They played on the swings and the slide. But then, Amy looked down and saw that her amazing red mitten was gone. She felt sad and started to cry. Amy and her mom decided to rush back through the park to find the mitten. They looked by the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. At last, they found the amazing red mitten under a big tree. Amy was so happy to have her mitten back. She put it on and gave her mom a big hug.

One day, a gentle cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a dog named Sam. Sam was eating some meat. Tim was hungry and wanted to join Sam. Tim walked up to Sam and asked, "Can I join you?" Sam looked at Tim and said, "Yes, you can join me." They both ate the meat together. They were happy. Then, something unexpected happened. A bird flew down and took the meat away. Tim and Sam were surprised. They thought the bird was their friend. But now they had no meat. So, they went to find more meat together.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Spot. Spot lived in a big house with his friend, a kind girl named Lily. They loved to play together in the yard. One day, Spot saw a big truck arrive near their house. A man got out of the truck and opened the back. Spot saw a big box with the word "lab" on it. The man carried the box into the house. Spot ran inside and saw Lily open the box. Inside was a big toy lab for them to play with. Spot barked happily and Lily said, "Let's play together, Spot!" They played all day with the new toy lab, and had lots of fun.

One day, a little kangaroo named Joey was very glad. He wanted to gather all his friends to play. So, he hopped and hopped to find his friends. He saw his friend, the bird, in a tree. "Hi bird! Do you want to gather and play with me?" asked Joey. The bird said, "Yes! I am glad to play with you, Joey!" So, the bird flew down and joined Joey. Next, they found their friend, the cat. "Hi cat! Do you want to gather and play with us?" asked Joey. The cat said, "Yes! I am glad to play with you both!" So, the cat, bird, and Joey hopped, flew, and ran together. They played all day long, and they were very glad to be together. When the sun went down, they all said, "Bye!" and went home. Joey was happy he could gather his friends and have a fun day.
