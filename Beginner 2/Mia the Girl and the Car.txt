Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Car

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her toy car. It was her favorite toy. But, oh no! The car broke. Mia was sad. She wanted to fix her toy car. Mia went to her dad and said, "Daddy, my car broke. Can you help me fix it?" Her dad looked at the car and saw that the wheel was off. He said, "Don't worry, Mia. I will help you fix it." Mia was glad. Her dad got some glue and put it on the wheel. He pressed the wheel onto the car with his thumb. They waited for a little while. Then, Mia tried to play with her car again. The wheel stayed on! Mia was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Daddy!" Her dad smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mia." They played with the toy car together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big universe, there was a mild bunny named Bobo. Bobo liked to hop and play all day. He had many friends like birds, squirrels, and other bunnies. One day, while Bobo was playing, a magic tree appeared. The tree was very big and had many colors. Bobo was happy to see the tree and wanted to show it to his friends. Bobo and his friends played around the magic tree. They had so much fun together. The tree made them all feel happy and safe. And so, in their big universe, Bobo and his friends lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia was in her room. She saw her big bookcase full of books. Mia loved to read. She wanted to get a book from the top shelf. But Mia was too small to reach it. Mia had an idea. She got a chair to stand on. She was so excited to get the book. But then, her mom came in and said, "Mia, be careful! You might fall and get hurt!" Mia didn't listen to her mom. She climbed on the chair to get the book. But the chair was not strong. It broke, and Mia fell down. She cried because she was hurt. Her mom hugged her and said, "Next time, ask for help when you need it." From that day on, Mia always asked for help when she needed it. She never tried to do things that could hurt her again. And her mom always helped her get books from the top shelf of the bookcase.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old cat. The cat liked to pretend to be a lion. One day, the cat saw a big bin. The bin was full of yummy food. The cat wanted to get the food from the bin. The wise cat had a plan. He said to his friend, the dog, "Let's pretend to be lions and scare the birds away. Then, we can eat the food in the bin." The dog liked the idea and said, "Yes, let's do it!" So, the wise cat and the dog pretended to be lions. They roared and scared the birds away. They were very happy. But when they tried to get the food from the bin, they made a big mess. The food fell on the ground and got dirty. The cat and the dog were sad. They could not eat the yummy food anymore. The wise cat learned that pretending to be a lion was not always a good idea.

Once upon a time, there was a big turkey. He was very foolish. One day, he said to his friend, "I want to play a trick on the farmer." His friend, a little mouse, said, "No, don't do that! You will regret it." But the foolish turkey did not listen. He went to the farmer's house and made a big mess. The farmer was very angry. The next day, the farmer found the turkey and said, "You are a bad turkey! You must leave the farm." The turkey was very sad. He said to his friend, the mouse, "You were right. I regret my trick." The mouse told the turkey, "It's okay. We all make mistakes. Just be a good turkey from now on." The turkey promised to be good and the farmer let him stay. They all lived happily ever after.
