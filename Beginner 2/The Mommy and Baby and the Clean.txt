Title: The Mommy and Baby and the Clean

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a baby. The baby loved to play, but often made a big mess. One day, the mommy said, “It’s time to clean up. I will provide you with a mop.” The baby looked scared. “No! I don’t want to mop!” The mommy smiled and said, “It’s okay, you can do it. I will help you.” But the baby was fearful and didn’t want to do it. Finally, the mommy said, “If you help me clean up, I will give you a special treat!” The baby lit up, and said, “Yay! Let’s mop!” So they mopped together, and when they were done, the room was nice and clean. The mommy gave the baby a special treat, and the baby was very happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant bunny. The bunny loved to eat carrots. Every day, he would hop and look for carrots in the forest. One day, the bunny found a big carrot. It was so big that he could not carry it. He tried to turn the carrot, but it was too heavy. The bunny was sad. Then, the bunny had an idea. He called his friends, the squirrel and the bird. They helped him turn the carrot. Together, they took the big carrot to the bunny's home. They all shared the carrot and had a great time.

Once there was a young boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy chess set. One day, Tom's mom told him to clean his room. "Tom, please wipe your table and put your toys away," she said. Tom wiped his table and put his toys away, but he left his chess set out. He wanted to play chess with his friend, Sam, later. Tom knew that if he put his chess set away, he might forget where he put it. Later, Sam came over to play. Tom was happy he left the chess set out. "Look, Sam! I have my chess set ready for us to play," Tom said. Sam smiled and said, "Good job, Tom! Let's play chess!" They played chess and had lots of fun. Tom was glad he listened to his mom and cleaned his room, but he was also happy he left his chess set out for his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a compassionate boy who loved to play at the park. One day, Tim saw a small cat stuck in a tree at the park. He wanted to help the cat, but he was too small to reach it. Tim had an idea and ran to find help. Tim ran to his big sister, Sue. He told her about the cat in the tree. Sue thought for a moment and said, "I know what to do! Let's run back to the park with a long stick!" Tim and Sue ran back to the park with a long stick, hoping to save the cat. When they got to the park, Tim and Sue used the long stick to help the cat get down from the tree. The cat was so happy to be on the ground again. It rubbed against Tim's leg as a thank you. Tim felt proud that he and Sue had saved the cat. From that day on, Tim, Sue, and the cat became best friends and played at the park every day.

One day, a little dog named Spot went to a new place. Spot saw a big tree and a weak, old fence. He saw a little girl named Sue playing in the yard. "Hi, I am Spot!" said the little dog. "Do you want to play with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" Sue had a toy that could spray water. She showed Spot how to use it. They took turns spraying water on the weak, old fence. The fence got wet and shiny. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Spot and Sue became best friends in their new place.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a peanut on the floor. She picked it up and thought it looked yummy. She wanted to eat it, but she didn't know if it was okay. So, she went to ask her mom. "Mom, can I eat this yummy peanut?" Amy asked. Her mom looked at the peanut and said, "Yes, you can eat it. But first, go wash your hands and then seat at the table." Amy ran to wash her hands and then sat down at the table, excited to eat the peanut. As she was about to eat the peanut, something unexpected happened. The peanut jumped out of her hand and started to talk! "Wait! Please don't eat me!" said the peanut. Amy was very surprised. She didn't know peanuts could talk. The peanut told Amy that he had a whole family of peanuts, and they all lived in a big peanut house. Amy decided not to eat the peanut and let it go back to its family. From that day on, Amy and the talking peanut became great friends.
