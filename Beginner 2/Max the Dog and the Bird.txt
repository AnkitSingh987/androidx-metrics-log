Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Bird

One day, a big dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a small cat named Lily. Lily said, "Max, can you help me find gold? I will be very glad if you do." Max said, "Yes, I will help you." They walked and walked, and then they saw a big tree. Max said, "Let's stay here and look for gold." They looked and looked, but they could not find any gold. They were sad. Then, a bird came and said, "I know where the gold is. Follow me." The bird took them to a small box under a bush. They opened the box and found many gold rocks! They were so glad they found the gold. But then, the bird said, "This gold is for everyone to share." Max and Lily agreed and shared the gold with all their friends. They were all very glad and had a big party.

Once upon a time, in a modern house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy yet. The yet was very funny and liked to play with Tim. They would play all day and have lots of fun together. One day, the yet was hungry. It saw a big red apple on the table. The yet wanted to eat the apple, so it took a big bite. But the apple was not yummy. The yet did not like it. Tim saw the yet with the apple and laughed. He said, "Silly yet, apples are not for you!" Tim gave the yet a cookie instead. The yet took a small bite and loved it. They both smiled and continued to play, happy as can be.

Once upon a time, in a small open house, there lived a happy bird named Whistle. Whistle loved to sing and play all day. One day, Whistle found a pretty veil on the ground. It was soft and light, like a cloud. Whistle thought it would be fun to wear the veil. So, Whistle put the veil on and started to dance around. Whistle felt so pretty and happy. The veil made Whistle feel like a beautiful bird. As Whistle danced and played, other birds came to watch. They loved the pretty veil too. Soon, all the birds were dancing and singing with Whistle. They were all having so much fun together. And that is how Whistle and the pretty veil made everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play outside in the mud. When Fluffy played in the mud, she became very dirty. One day, Fluffy saw a big sign that said "Cat Show Today." She wanted to go to the show, but she was so dirty. Fluffy went home and asked her mom, "Can I go to the cat show?" Her mom said, "You can go, but you must be clean." Fluffy knew she had to clean herself up. She took a bath and scrubbed her fur until it was white again. At the cat show, many people and cats were there. Fluffy saw a big stage. She went on the stage and did a dance. The people liked her dance and started to applaud. Fluffy was so happy that she was clean and could make people happy with her dance.

Once upon a time, there was a large tree. In the tree, there was a rope. A boy named Tim and a girl named Sue liked to play with the rope. They would swing on the rope and laugh. They talked to each other about their fun games. One day, Tim and Sue went to the tree. They saw a big bird on the rope. "Hello, big bird!" said Tim. "Can we play with the rope?" The big bird talked back, "Yes, but you must be nice to me." Tim and Sue were very happy. Tim and Sue played with the big bird and the rope. They all laughed and talked. But then, the big bird pulled the rope up high. Tim and Sue were scared. The big bird said, "Don't be scared! I just want to show you how to fly." Tim and Sue held the rope tight, and the big bird helped them fly in the sky. They were not scared anymore. They had a great time flying with the big bird, and they all became good friends.
