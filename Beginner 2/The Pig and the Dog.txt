Category: Beginner
Title: The Pig and the Dog

One day, a pig went for a jog. The pig liked to run and feel the soft grass under his feet. He was very happy when he jogged. But one day, while the pig was jogging, he saw a big dog. The dog was not nice. The dog wanted to chase the pig. The pig was scared. The pig ran fast. The dog ran fast too. The pig found a soft mud puddle and jumped in. The dog did not like the mud. The dog left, and the pig was safe. The pig was happy to jog again.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tim. Tim liked to play with his ball. One day, Tim saw a big dog. The big dog had Tim's ball. "Can I have my ball back?" Tim asked the big dog. The big dog looked at Tim and did not reply. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. Then, to Tim's shock, the big dog picked up the ball and gave it back to Tim. The big dog said, "Let's play together!" Tim was happy and they played all day. The thin cat and big dog became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hockey. He played with his friends every day. Tim was very intelligent. He knew how to play hockey very well. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, said, "I want to quit hockey. It is too hard for me." Tim was sad. He did not want his friend to quit. Tim had an idea. He said, "I will help you. We can play together and get better." So, Tim and Sam played hockey every day. They practiced and practiced. Soon, Sam got better at hockey. He was very happy. He said to Tim, "Thank you for helping me. I am glad I did not quit. We make a great team." From that day on, Tim and Sam played hockey together all the time. They were the best of friends. They learned that when you work together, you can do anything. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big van came to the town. It was a fair day, and the sun was up. The van was full of toys. A kind man with a big smile came out of the van. He said, "I will sort the toys and give them to all the kids in the town." The kids were very happy. They stood in a line to get their toys. The kind man sorted the toys and gave one toy to each kid. The kids played with their toys and had lots of fun. They were very thankful to the kind man. But then, something unexpected happened. The kind man was a magic man! He waved his hands and all the toys came to life. The toys danced and played with the kids. The kids were very surprised and happy. They had never seen toys come to life before. The magic man and the kids had a fun day with the dancing toys.

Once there was a little bird who was learning to sing. She wanted to sing like the other birds, but she didn't know how. But one day, she found a wide, dark tree and decided to try. She climbed up and round the tree, opening her mouth wide and letting out a song. At first, it was a little wobbly but soon it was getting louder and louder and more beautiful. She sang and sang, higher and higher until she couldn't go any further. As she sang, the other birds joined her and they sang together. Then they all flew away in the bright blue sky, singing with joy!

One day, a little boy named Tim found a jug in the kitchen. He wanted to examine it, but his mom was busy. Tim felt impatient. He wanted to show the jug to his mom and talk about it. "Mom, look at this jug!" Tim said. But his mom was still busy. Tim felt sad. He wanted to share his find with her. He tried to wait, but it was hard. Then, Tim had an idea. He decided to be patient and wait for his mom. He played with his toys and soon, his mom was ready to talk. "What a nice jug, Tim!" she said. They examined it together and talked about it. Tim was happy he waited. In the end, Tim learned that being patient is important. When we wait and give others time, we can share and enjoy things together.
