Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom Find the Dog

Lily and Tom were playing in the yard. They liked to run and jump and make noise. They saw a shiny thing on the grass. It was a coin. Lily picked it up and showed it to Tom. "Look, Tom, I found a shiny thing!" Lily said. "Wow, Lily, it is very shiny. Can I see it?" Tom asked. Lily gave the coin to Tom. He looked at it and smiled. He liked shiny things. He put it in his pocket. "Let's go and find more shiny things!" Tom said. They ran around the yard, looking for shiny things. They did not see the big dog that was watching them. The dog did not like children. He did not like noise. He did not like shiny things. He was angry and hungry. He growled and barked. Lily and Tom heard the dog. They were scared. They tried to run away. But the dog was faster. He chased them and bit them. He took the coin from Tom's pocket. He ate it. He was happy. Lily and Tom were hurt and sad. They cried and cried. They wanted their mom and dad. But their mom and dad were not home. They were at work. They did not know what happened. They would be very worried when they came back. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dull truck. It had four big wheels. The truck liked to weigh things. One day, the truck went to the park to play. At the park, the truck saw a small bike. The bike had two little wheels. The truck thought the bike was cute. The truck asked the bike, "Do you want to play with me?" The bike said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" They played together all day. They went up and down hills. Then, something unexpected happened. The bike went very fast down a hill. The truck tried to catch up, but it was too slow. The bike went so fast that it flew into the sky! The truck was so surprised. The little bike was not just a bike; it was a magic bike! The truck and the magic bike became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little butterfly. This butterfly was adventurous. It liked to fly and see new things. One day, the butterfly flew far away from its home. The butterfly met a big bear. The bear was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked the butterfly. The bear said, "I broke my friend's toy. I don't know if my friend will forgive me." The butterfly wanted to help the bear feel better. The butterfly had an idea. It flew to the bear's friend and told the friend what happened. The friend was not mad. The friend said, "I forgive the bear. I can fix the toy." The butterfly flew back to the bear and told the bear the good news. The bear was happy and thanked the butterfly. The butterfly and the bear became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to write and draw. One day, he found a big, filthy box in his room. The box was full of old things. Tim wanted to make something fun with the things in the box. Tim saw a bottle of glue in the box. He had an idea. He took out paper and his crayons. Tim started to write and draw on the paper. He drew a big tree with lots of leaves. Tim wanted to make the tree look real. Tim used the glue to stick the paper tree on the wall. He was very happy. But the glue was old, and the tree fell off the wall. Tim felt sad. His mom saw him and gave him a hug. She said they would buy new glue and try again. Tim smiled and knew his tree would be up soon.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to camp with her family. They had a tent, a fire, and sweet marshmallows to eat. Lily loved to play with her big brother, Jack, and explore the woods around the camp. One day, Jack and Lily were playing hide and seek. Jack hid behind a tree and jumped out to scare Lily. "Boo!" he said. Lily laughed and said, "You didn't scare me, Jack!" That night, as they sat around the fire, Lily told her family about how Jack tried to scare her. They all laughed and enjoyed their sweet marshmallows. They had a lot of fun at camp and couldn't wait to come back next year.
