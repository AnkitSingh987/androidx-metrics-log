Tara was walking through the park when she noticed a group of kids playing. They looked like they were about 3 years old, and Tara walked over to take a closer look. When she got there, the kids were playing math games. Tara was so surprised to see them playing with something so different from the other games that kids their age usually played. She asked one of the little girls, "What kind of game is this?" The girl grinned and said, "Our teacher at school taught us these math games. We love playing them!" Tara noticed that the little girl really enjoyed it. She couldn't believe kids so young could understand math. The little girl had a bright, happy look on her face, and Tara smiled. She said goodbye to the little girl and went on her way, feeling amazed by what she had seen.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat was very rude. He did not like to share his toys with the dog. The dog was sad. The cat said, "I will shut the door and not let you in my house." The dog said, "Please don't be rude, cat. I want to play with you." So, the cat and the dog went to the cat's house. The cat felt bad for being rude. He said, "I am sorry, dog. I will give you a massage to make you feel better." The dog was happy. He liked the massage. The cat and the dog became good friends. They played together every day and shared their toys.

Once there was a boy who was very sad. He had made a big mess and didn't know what to do. Suddenly he saw his mum coming with a cloth. She said, "Don't be stupid, here use this to wipe it up." So the boy used the cloth to wipe up the mess. He was very happy. By the time night came, everything was nice and clean again. But the boy was still sad. He knew he had been very stupid and felt bad. His mum said, "Never be stupid again, OK? Now give me a big hug!" The boy hugged his mum and was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very successful boy. He was good at making new friends. One day, Tim saw a new girl in his class. Her name was Lily. Tim wanted to make her feel welcome. Tim had an idea. He would bring Lily a toy from his house. He knew she would like it. The next day, Tim brought a toy car for Lily. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They played with the toy car together. From that day on, Tim and Lily were best friends. They played and laughed every day. Tim was happy that he could make Lily feel welcome. And Lily was happy to have a friend like Tim. Their friendship was very successful.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play with balls. They have a cool house with a big yard. They can throw the balls very far and catch them. One day, they see a new house next to theirs. It is big and shiny. It has many windows and a red door. Ben and Mia are curious. They want to see the new house. They go to the fence and look at the new house. They see a man and a woman. They are the new neighbors. They are busy. They carry boxes and bags into the house. "Hello!" Ben and Mia say. "We are Ben and Mia. We live next to you. Do you want to play with us?" The man and the woman look at them. They do not smile. They do not say hello. They look angry. "Go away!" the man says. "We do not want to play with you. You are noisy and messy. You will break our windows and our door. Leave us alone!" Ben and Mia are sad and scared. They do not understand. They are not noisy and messy. They do not want to break anything. They just want to be friends. They run back to their house. They still have their balls. They want to play with them. But they are afraid of the new neighbors. They do not want to make them angry. They throw the balls very softly. They do not catch them. They do not have fun. The balls land on the fence. They roll over the fence. They go into the new house's yard. The man and the woman see the balls. They are very angry. They pick up the balls. They throw them very hard. They throw them at Ben and Mia's house. The balls hit the windows and the door. They break the windows and the door. They make a loud noise. Glass and wood fly everywhere. Ben and Mia's house is broken. Ben and Mia are hurt. They cry. They are very scared. The new neighbors are mean. They do not care. They go into their house. They close their door. Ben and Mia are alone. They have no balls. They have no house. They have no friends. The end.
