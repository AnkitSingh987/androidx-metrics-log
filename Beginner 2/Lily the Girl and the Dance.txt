Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Dance

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to start a dance. She loved to dance and wanted all her friends to dance with her. She told her friends, "Let's dance together!" They all got very excited and said, "Yes, let's start!" They all started to dance in a big circle. They danced and danced, and had lots of fun. But Lily's friend, Tim, was not very good at dancing. He felt bad because he thought he looked silly. Lily saw that Tim was sad and stopped dancing. She went to him and said, "Don't be sad, Tim. We can all dance together and have fun, even if we are not all good at it." Tim smiled and they all started to dance again, having a great time together.

One day, a curious cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a mine and wanted to see what was inside. So, he went in the mine and started to look around. Inside the mine, Tom met a mouse named Jerry. Jerry said, "What do you do in my mine?" Tom said, "I am just looking. I am curious." Jerry thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, you can look with me." As Tom and Jerry looked around the mine, they found a big, shiny rock. They were very surprised! They did not know what it was. They decided to take it out of the mine and show it to their friends. When they showed it to their friends, everyone was happy and amazed by the big, shiny rock. And that is how Tom and Jerry became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog. The dog had a big paw. One day, the dog went outside to play. The dog saw a ball. He wanted to bring the ball to his friend, the cat. The dog picked the ball up with his paw and started to walk. He was very happy. But, the ball was not a ball! It was a very round frog! The frog jumped out of the dog's paw and said, "Hello!" The dog was surprised. The frog became the dog's new friend, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a curious kitten named Kitty. Kitty loved to crawl and play all day. One day, she saw a big tree and wanted to see what was on the top. Kitty started to crawl up the tree. She went higher and higher. Then, she saw a bird sitting on a branch. The bird said, "Hi, Kitty! What are you doing up here?" Kitty said, "I am curious. I want to see what is on the top of the tree." The bird laughed and said, "You're almost there! Keep going!" So, Kitty crawled up more and more until she reached the top. At the top of the tree, Kitty saw her house, the park, and many other fun things. She was so happy to see everything from up high. After a while, Kitty crawled back down the tree and went home. She told her mom about her adventure and they both agreed it was a great day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to church with his mom. He was nervous because it was his first time. The church was big and had many people. Tim held his mom's hand tight. Inside the church, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue asked, "Can you guess my favorite color?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Is it red?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, you're right!" They became friends and played together. Later, Tim and Sue saw a big, mean dog near the church. They were scared. The dog barked loud and chased them. Tim and Sue ran away fast, but the dog caught up. The dog bit Tim's leg and it hurt a lot. Tim cried and his mom came to help. The dog ran away, but Tim's day at church was not fun anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He had a cane that he found in his yard. He liked to use it to poke things and play. As he walked, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. "What happen?" Tim asked. Sue said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Tim said, "Yes, I will help you." They looked and looked. Tim used his cane to look under a bush. There was the toy! Sue was happy. They both learned that helping others can make you feel good.
