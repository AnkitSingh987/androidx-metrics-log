Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sunshine. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his red ball. At the park, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue was a little girl who also liked to play outside. They decided to play together. Tim said, "Let's kick the ball!" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" As they played, they noticed the sunshine was not warm. It was a little bitter, but Tim and Sue did not mind. They played and kicked the ball until the sun went down. Then, they said goodbye and went home. They were happy to have a new friend to play with in the sunshine.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a lot of green grass. On the grass, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. They liked to play games together. One day, they decided to play a new game called "match the things". The cat said, "I will find things that match, and you will tell me if I am right." The dog, being an honest friend, agreed. The cat found two sticks that looked the same. The dog said, "Yes, they match!" The cat was happy and continued to find more things that match. Next, the cat found two leaves. One leaf was big and the other leaf was small. The cat asked the dog, "Do these leaves match?" The dog, being an honest friend, said, "No, they don't match. One is big and the other is small." The cat understood and kept looking for things that match. They played the game all day and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved cheese more than anything. She lived in a small hole in a big house. The hole was her home, but the door was broken. Mimi wanted to fix it. One day, Mimi found a useful stick near her hole. She thought, "This stick can help me fix my door!" So, Mimi worked hard with the stick to fix her broken door. After some time, the door was fixed! Mimi was very happy. She went to find some cheese to celebrate. She found a big piece of cheese in the kitchen. Mimi ate the cheese and shared it with her friends. They all had a fun party in her fixed home.

One day, a strong dog named Max found a rubber ball. He loved to play with it. He would throw it and catch it all day long. Max was very happy with his new toy. One day, Max was playing with his rubber ball near the river. He threw the ball very high, but it fell into the water! "Oh no!" said Max, "I need to save my ball!" Max tried to reach the ball, but the river was too big and he was scared. Just then, a little duck swam by. "Can you help me save my ball?" asked Max. The duck said, "Yes, I can help you!" The duck swam to the ball and brought it back to Max. Max was so happy and thanked the duck. They became good friends and played with the rubber ball together.

Jill and her best friend, Bill, were playing cricket in the backyard. They were having a lot of fun, but Jack thought it was unfair that Jill and Bill were enjoying the game without him. He felt left out. Jack said to Jill, "Can I play cricket too?". Jill replied, "No, this is not your game." Jack begged her, "Please? I want to play." Jill felt sorry for Jack, but she was feeling selfish. She wanted to keep having fun without Jack. She said firmly, "No, Jack. You can't play" Jack was very upset. He started to yell and scream, and he recorded his cries on his phone. He sent the recordings to Jill, just to show her how selfish she was being. Jill felt bad, but it was too late. Jack was still upset and didn't want to forgive her. Jill felt disappointed with herself. She wished she hadn't been so selfish.
