Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Rock and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little dog with a wide smile. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, while playing, he found a big rock. The little dog was very happy. "Look at my big rock!" he said to his friends. The other animals came to see. They all wanted to play with the rock too. They took turns rolling the rock with their paws. The little dog watched his friends play and laughed. As they played, the rock rolled down a hill. The little dog ran after it, using his paw to stop it. His friends cheered as he saved the rock from rolling away. They all played together until the sun went down, and then they went home, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big, red ball that he loved to play with. Every day, he would go outside and play with his ball in the sun. One day, while he was playing, Tim kicked the ball very hard, and it went far away. Tim ran to find his ball, but it was gone. He felt very sorry and started to cry. Then, he heard a scream from behind a tree. Tim walked over to see what was going on. Behind the tree, he found a small, scared cat. The cat had been playing with his ball and was now stuck in the tree. Tim helped the cat down from the tree, and they became good friends. They played with the red ball together and had lots of fun. And Tim learned that sharing his toys could bring happiness to others.

One day, a helpful boy named Tom saw a big gate. He wanted to go through it. Tom tried to push the gate, but it was too heavy. He saw a little bird sitting on the gate. "Help me, little bird," Tom said. The bird looked at Tom and said, "I want to help you, but I am too small." Just then, a big dog came running up to the gate. The dog barked and wagged its tail. Tom asked, "Can you help me open the gate, big dog?" The dog barked again and pushed the gate with its nose. The gate opened, and Tom saw a big surprise. Behind the gate was a playground with lots of toys and kids playing. The dog and the bird both wanted to play too. So, they all went inside and played together. The helpful boy, the little bird, and the big dog had a fun day at the playground.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. One day, they find a big box. They think it is a space ship. They go inside and pretend to fly to the moon. But the box is not a space ship. It is a trap. A mean man put it there. He wants to take Sara and Ben away. He locks the box and puts it in his truck. He drives away fast. Sara and Ben are scared. They cry and shout. But no one hears them. The box is dark and dull. It has no windows or holes. It is very cold. Sara and Ben shiver. They hug each other and hope someone will find them. But no one finds them. The mean man takes them to a far place. He sells them to a bad lady. She makes them work hard and gives them little food. She is mean and hits them. Sara and Ben are sad and lonely. They miss their home and their mom and dad. They wish they never went in the box. They wish they could fly to the moon.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia loved to paint. She would paint all day with her big, wide brush. She liked to paint the sky, the sun, and the trees. One day, Mia was painting outside. It was a harsh, windy day. The wind blew her hair and made it hard for her to paint. But Mia did not give up. She held her wide brush tight and kept painting. Mia painted a big, beautiful tree. The tree had many colorful leaves. Mia was so happy with her painting. She showed it to her mom and dad. They were very proud of her. Mia knew that even on a harsh day, she could still make something beautiful.
