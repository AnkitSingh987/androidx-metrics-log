Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Cat

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big swing with a long string. Lily wants to swing on it. She runs to the swing and sits on it. "Push me, Ben!" Lily says. "Push me high!" Ben pushes Lily on the swing. Lily is happy. She feels the wind in her hair. She sees the sky and the trees. But then Ben sees something. He sees a black cat on the fence. Ben likes cats. He wants to pet the cat. He stops pushing Lily and runs to the cat. "Ben, where are you going?" Lily asks. "Don't leave me!" Lily is sad. She wants Ben to push her. She does not like the black cat. She thinks the cat is mean. She tries to swing by herself, but she cannot. She is stuck on the swing. "Ben, come back!" Lily cries. "I need you!" But Ben does not hear her. He is busy with the cat. He tries to touch the cat, but the cat does not like him. The cat hisses and scratches Ben. Ben is hurt. He cries. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben says. "Bad cat, bad cat!" Lily hears Ben. She sees him crying. She forgets about the swing. She jumps off the swing and runs to Ben. She hugs him. "I'm sorry, Ben," Lily says. "Are you okay?" Ben nods. He hugs Lily back. "I'm sorry, Lily," Ben says. "I should not have left you." Lily and Ben are friends again. They forget about the swing and the cat. They go to play with the ball. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to travel with her family. They went to many places together. One day, they went to a big dessert-eating contest. Sue wanted to join the contest because she was very competitive. In the contest, there were many kids. They all wanted to eat the most dessert. Sue tried her best to eat as much dessert as she could. She ate ice cream, cake, and cookies. She wanted to win the contest and show everyone how good she was. But then, Sue started to feel sick. Her tummy hurt a lot. She had eaten too much dessert. Sue did not win the contest. She went home with her family and had to rest in bed. Sue learned that being too competitive can sometimes lead to a bad ending.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. She wanted to meet her friend, Tim. On her way, she saw a shiny crystal on the ground. Lily picked up the crystal and put it in her pocket. Lily met Tim near a big tree. She showed him the crystal. "Look, Tim! I found a pretty crystal!" she said. Tim looked at the crystal and wanted to hold it too. But when he took it from Lily, he dropped it. The crystal broke into small pieces. Both Lily and Tim felt hurt because the crystal was now broken. They were sad and didn't know what to do. Then, a nice old lady saw them and came to help. She took the broken crystal and put it in a small bag. The old lady told them, "Don't worry, little ones. The crystal may be broken, but your friendship is still strong." Lily and Tim smiled and hugged each other. They knew that their friendship was more important than the broken crystal.

One day, a girl named Lucy and a boy named Tom were playing. They had a big box of crayons and paper. They wanted to make a picture together. They both thought it was important to make the best picture ever. Lucy wanted to draw a big sun with a smiley face. Tom wanted to draw a big tree with lots of leaves. They could not decide which one to draw. This made them both sad. Then, Lucy had an idea. "Why don't we draw both?" she said. Tom liked the idea. So, they drew a big sun with a smiley face and a big tree with lots of leaves. They were both happy with their picture. They learned that working together is important.
