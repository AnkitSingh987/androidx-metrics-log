Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Grumpy Cat and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with yarn. She liked to make fun things with it. One day, Sue dared Tom to play with the yarn. "Tom, I dare you to play with the yarn," Sue said. Tom looked at the yarn and thought it was a fun idea. So, Tom played with the yarn. He rolled it, he jumped on it, and he even tried to eat it. Sue giggled as she watched Tom play. As Tom played, he became less grumpy. He started to purr and enjoy the fun. Sue was happy that Tom was not grumpy anymore. They played with the yarn all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tom and Sue played with the yarn every day and they were always happy.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play a sport. He liked to play with a ball. It was a cheap ball, but he loved it very much. Tim went outside to play with his ball. While playing, Tim saw a tree. He wanted to climb it. So, he put his ball down and started to climb. When he was high up, he saw a pretty bird. He wanted to say "hi" to the bird. But then, Tim started to fall. He was scared. He closed his eyes and waited. But, he did not get hurt. His cheap ball was there, and he fell on it. The ball saved him. Tim was happy and hugged his ball. He knew that he had the best ball ever.

Once upon a time, there was a big sea. In the sea, there was a small fish named Fin. Fin was a little ignorant. He did not know much about the world. He liked to play and swim with his friends all day long. One day, while playing, Fin saw a big boat. It made lots of noise. Fin's friend, Sam the seagull, flew down and said, "Fin, we need to save our sea from the big boat. It will make our sea dirty." Fin and Sam talked to all their friends. They made a plan to save the sea. They worked together to tell the people on the boat to stop making the sea dirty. The people listened and stopped. In the end, Fin, Sam, and all their friends saved the sea. They were happy and proud. They learned that working together can make a big change. And Fin was not ignorant anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a humble mechanic named Tom. He fixed cars and trucks every day. People liked him because he was kind and always ready to help. One sunny day, a little girl named Lucy came to Tom's shop. She said, "Please, can you tell me how to fix my toy car?" Tom smiled and said, "Of course, let me see your toy car." Lucy showed him her broken toy car, and Tom knew just what to do. Tom took a small tool and fixed Lucy's toy car. She was so happy, she jumped and clapped her hands. "Thank you, Tom!" she said. Tom just smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lucy. Have fun playing with your car!" And from that day on, Lucy knew that Tom was not only a great mechanic but also a very kind friend.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved collecting things. One day, she found a box of toys at a cheap price. "Yay, I'm going to collect these toys!" the little girl said. The little girl approached the shop owner. "May I please have this box of toys?" she asked. The shop owner smiled. "Sure! It's so cheap, you'll have many more toys to add to your collection!" The little girl happily paid the shop owner and went home with the box of toys. She was so excited to see what was inside the box! The little girl opened the box to find the most amazing toy collection. She was so happy, she danced and sang with joy. The little girl proudly showed her new toys to her parents. From then on, she collected new toys every week.
