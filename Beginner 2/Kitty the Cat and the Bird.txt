Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Bird

One day, a small cat named Kitty was playing in the park. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. She jumped and climbed up the tree. Kitty felt happy and proud. Suddenly, a tiny bird came and sat on a branch near Kitty. The bird looked confused and said, "Kitty, why did you come up here? I lost my friend and I need help." Kitty looked at the bird and said, "I will help you find your friend." They both went down the tree and started looking for the bird's friend. As they searched, they found a little dog with a hurt lip. The dog was crying. Kitty and the bird went to the dog and asked, "What happened to your lip?" The dog said, "I was playing and I fell. Now my lip hurts." Kitty and the bird helped the dog feel better. At that moment, the bird's friend came and said, "Thank you for helping my friend." The bird was happy to see her friend again. Kitty learned that helping others can make you feel good too. And they all became good friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his football. He loved his football very much. It was his favorite toy. He played with it every day in his yard. One day, while Tim was playing with his football, it hit a big rock. The football got a hole and air came out. The football was broken. Tim was very sad and started to cry. He didn't know what to do. Tim's mom saw him crying and asked, "Why are you crying, Tim?" Tim showed her the broken football. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can get a new football for you." Tim stopped crying and smiled. He was happy his mom would get him a new football. They went to the store and got a new football. Tim played with his new football every day and took good care of it.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a bean on the ground. It was a green bean. Tim felt dizzy looking at the bean. He did not recognize the bean. Tim asked his friend, "Do you know this bean?" His friend said, "No, I do not know this bean." They both looked at the bean. They felt dizzy too. Then, a big bird came. The bird said, "This is my bean!" The bird took the bean and flew away. Tim and his friend were not dizzy anymore. They laughed and played in the park. They did not see the bean again.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. In the field, there were many animals who liked to play. One day, a new animal came. It was a small, sad cat. The cat felt miserable and did not want to play. A kind dog saw the sad cat and wanted to help. The dog said, "Come join us, cat. We can play and have fun in the field." But the cat did not want to play. The cat just sat and watched the other animals play. Then, something unexpected happened. The sad cat saw a big box in the field. The cat went to the box and jumped in. The box was full of toys! The cat started to play with the toys and felt happy. The other animals joined the cat in the box, and they all played together. The sad cat was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big hotel. The hotel was very nice, but it was run by a lazy man. The lazy man did not like to work. He did not like to fix things that were broken. One day, a little girl came to the hotel with her mom. They wanted to stay for the night. The little girl saw that the door was broken. She said, "Mom, look! The door is broken. Can we fix it?" Her mom said, "We can try, but we need to ask the man who runs the hotel." The little girl and her mom went to the lazy man and asked, "Can we fix the door?" The lazy man did not want to work, but he did not want to lose his guests. So, he said, "Yes, you can fix the door." The little girl and her mom worked together to fix the door. They were very happy when it was fixed. The lazy man saw how happy the little girl and her mom were. He learned that it is good to fix things and to work. From that day on, the lazy man worked hard to make his hotel the best in the town. And everyone lived happily ever after.
