Category: Beginner
Title: The Ugly Frog and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was an ugly frog. This frog was not happy because he was not pretty like the other frogs. One day, he heard a snap. He jumped and looked around to see what made the sound. He saw a little girl crying. She was trying to reach a toy stuck in a tree. The ugly frog wanted to help the girl. He jumped high up into the tree and snapped the branch with his strong legs. The toy fell down, and the girl was happy. The girl looked at the ugly frog and said, "Thank you, frog!" She gave him a big hug. The frog felt very happy. The girl gave the frog a reward: a shiny gold coin. The ugly frog learned that being helpful and kind was more important than being pretty. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Tom's mom asked him to organize a party for his friends. Tom thought about what he needed for the party. He knew he needed a mixer to make a yummy cake. He asked his friend, the dog, to help him. The dog was very good at using the mixer. They mixed flour, eggs, and sugar to make the cake. The cake was big and sweet. Tom and the dog were very happy. On the day of the party, Tom's friends came to his house. They played games, ate the yummy cake, and had lots of fun. Tom was not lazy that day. He learned that organizing a party can be fun, too. And from that day on, Tom was not so lazy anymore.

Sara and Ben like to play with chalk. They draw on the sidewalk with many colors. They make flowers, stars, and animals. They have fun and laugh. One day, a big boy comes and sees their drawings. He is mean and rude. He says, "Your drawings are ugly and silly. I will end them!" He takes a stick and erases their drawings. He makes them dirty and gone. Sara and Ben are sad and angry. They say, "Stop! You are bad and mean. You can't end our drawings. They are charming and pretty. We worked hard and had fun. Go away!" The big boy laughs and says, "No, I won't go away. I will end more of your drawings. You can't stop me. I am bigger and stronger than you." Sara and Ben are scared and cry. They don't know what to do. They wish someone would help them. Then, a nice girl comes and sees what is happening. She is kind and brave. She says, "Hey, you! Leave them alone. You are bad and mean. You can't end their drawings. They are charming and pretty. They worked hard and had fun. You should be sorry and go away." The big boy is surprised and scared. He says, "Who are you? You can't tell me what to do. You are smaller and weaker than me." The nice girl says, "I am Lily. I am their friend. You can't bully them. You are smaller and weaker than your heart. You should be nice and share. You can play with chalk too. You can make your own drawings. You can have fun and laugh." The big boy thinks and feels ashamed. He says, "I am sorry. I was bad and mean. I don't know why I did that. I was bored and lonely. I wanted to play with chalk too. But I didn't know how to ask. Can I play with you?" Sara and Ben look at Lily. Lily smiles and nods. She says, "Yes, you can play with us. But you have to be nice and share. You have to respect our drawings. You have to have fun and laugh." The big boy says, "OK, I will. Thank you for being kind and brave. You are charming and pretty. You are a good friend." Sara and Ben smile and say, "You are welcome. You can be a good friend too. Come on, let's play with chalk." They all play with chalk. They draw on the sidewalk with many colors. They make flowers, stars, and animals. They have fun and laugh. They are happy and end their day with a hug.
