Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to school. She had a big, pink eraser with her. Lucy liked to draw and write, and she used her eraser to fix her mistakes. She was very gentle with her eraser because she did not want to break it. At school, Lucy heard a loud noise. She looked around and saw a big dog in the room. The dog was wagging its tail and barking. The dog saw Lucy's eraser and thought it was a toy. The dog took the eraser in its mouth and ran away. Lucy was very sad. She did not know what to do. She looked for the dog and her eraser, but she could not find them. Lucy went home and told her mom about the dog and her eraser. Her mom hugged her and said they would buy a new eraser tomorrow. But Lucy knew it would not be the same as her gentle, pink eraser.

Once upon a time, there was a messy boy named Tim. He liked to play outside all day. One night, Tim had a dream. In his dream, he saw a beautiful sunrise. The colors were so pretty. He wanted to show his friend, Sam. The next day, Tim told Sam about his dream. "I saw a pretty sunrise in my dream," said Tim. "I want to see it with you." Sam looked sad. "I don't know how to see a sunrise," said Sam. Tim thought for a moment. "Let's ask my mom," he said. Tim and Sam went to Tim's mom. She told them to wake up early in the morning to see the sunrise. The next day, they woke up early and went outside. They saw the pretty sunrise together. They were so happy they could share the beautiful sight.

Lily was a mean girl. She liked to pinch other kids and make them cry. She did not like to share her toys or say sorry. She thought she was better than everyone. One day, she saw a new boy in her class. His name was Ben. He was bald. He had no hair on his head. Lily thought he looked funny. She wanted to pinch him and laugh at him. She walked up to him and said, "Hi, baldy. Why do you have no hair? Did you lose it in a brush?" She reached out her hand to pinch his head. But Ben was not scared of Lily. He was brave and smart. He knew that he had no hair because he was sick. He had to take medicine that made his hair fall out. He did not mind being bald. He said, "No, I did not lose it in a brush. I lost it in a fight. A fight with a big bad monster. And I won. Do you want to see my scar?" He lifted his shirt and showed Lily a long scar on his chest. He said, "This is where the monster bit me. But I bit him back. And I kicked him and punched him and made him run away. He was so scared of me. He will never come back." Lily was shocked. She did not expect Ben to have a scar. She did not expect him to be so strong and brave. She felt ashamed of herself. She realized that she was mean and stupid. She wanted to say sorry to Ben. But before she could, Ben said, "And you know what? You are the real baldy. You have no hair in your heart. You have no kindness, no love, no friendship. You are the one who needs a brush. A brush to clean your soul." He turned away from Lily and walked to his desk. He did not want to talk to her anymore. He did not want to be her friend. He had other friends who liked him for who he was. Lily felt sad and lonely. She wished she had not pinched Ben. She wished she had been nice to him. She wished she had a hair in her heart. But it was too late. She had lost her chance. She had learned a hard lesson. She had learned that being mean does not make you happy. It only makes you unhappy. And that being bald does not make you weak. It only makes you strong.
