Category: Beginner
Title: John and the Cake

John was working hard on his project. He had a big folder of papers and wanted to finish up all the work. But he was so tired and didn't have enough energy to go on. Suddenly, John heard something from the kitchen. It smelled kind of sour. He got up from his desk and saw that his mom was baking a cake! John smiled and said: "Mom! I am so tired and I can't finish this folder. Can I help you with the cake?" Mom smiled and said: "Sure, just take the sour berries from the basket." John was happy to have something to do. He carefully picked the sour berries one by one and put them into the batter. Soon the cake was ready and it smelled wonderful! John and his mom enjoyed a big slice of the cake together. Soon enough, John was feeling refreshed and ready to finish his project. With a smile on his face, he went back to his folder and finished up all the work.

Tom and Mia liked to play pretend. They had many costumes and toys to make their games fun. One day, they found a big box in the attic. Inside the box, there were many old things. Some things were shiny, some things were soft, and some things were scary. Tom saw a mask that looked like a lion. He put it on his face and roared. Mia saw a mask that looked like a bird. She put it on her face and flapped her arms. They laughed and ran around the attic, pretending to be animals. But then, Mia saw a mask that looked like a monster. It had big teeth, horns, and red eyes. She was afraid of the mask. She did not want to touch it or look at it. She hid behind the box and cried. Tom heard Mia cry and went to see what was wrong. He saw the mask that scared Mia. He thought it was just a silly mask. He wanted to show Mia that it was not real. He picked up the mask and put it on his face. He made a funny noise and wiggled his ears. Mia peeked from behind the box and saw Tom with the mask. She saw that he was not a monster, but her brother. She saw that he was trying to make her laugh. She smiled and came out of her hiding place. Tom took off the mask and gave it to Mia. He said, "It's just a mask. It can't hurt you. You can try it on if you want. I will encourage you." Mia looked at the mask and at Tom. She was still a little afraid, but she was also curious. She took the mask and put it on her face. She made a funny noise and wiggled her ears. Tom clapped and cheered. They laughed and hugged. They played with the mask and the other old things for a while. They wondered who made them and why. They imagined that they were ancient people who lived long ago. They had fun in the attic until their mom called them for lunch. They put the mask and the other things back in the box and went downstairs. They decided to play pretend again another day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a girl with a pale face. She was sad. Tim wanted to help her. Tim said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I lost my pedal. I can't ride my bike now." Tim wanted to find the pedal for her. Tim jogged around the park to look for the pedal. He found it near a big tree. He gave it to the girl. She was very happy. They both played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, a small dog named Max lived in a tiny house. Max loved to go for walks. One sunny day, Max went on a walk with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. As they walked, Max saw a big red ball. Max said, "Lily, can I get the ball?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Max! Let's get the ball together." They ran to the ball and Max picked it up with his mouth. They were very happy. On their walk, Max and Lily saw a small pond. Max wanted to play with the ball in the water. Max said, "Lily, can we play in the water?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, Max! Let's have fun!" They played with the ball in the water until it was time to go home. Max and Lily had a great day on their walk.
