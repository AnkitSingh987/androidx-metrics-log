Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Dolphin

Tom and Lily were excited to go on a sail with their dad. They liked to see the water and the fish and the birds. They put on their hats and their life jackets and climbed on the boat. "Are you ready, kids?" Dad asked. "Yes, Dad!" Tom and Lily shouted. Dad started the boat and they sailed away from the shore. They felt the wind and the sun on their faces. They waved to other boats and people on the beach. "Look, Dad, a dolphin!" Lily pointed. "Wow, that's amazing!" Dad said. "Let's get closer and see if we can hear it." They sailed towards the dolphin and heard it make noises. Tom and Lily laughed and tried to copy the noises. The dolphin seemed to like them and jumped out of the water. "Hello, dolphin!" Tom and Lily said. But then, something bad happened. A big wave came and hit the boat. The boat rocked and tipped over. Tom and Lily screamed and held on to Dad. "Don't worry, kids, I've got you!" Dad said. "We'll be okay!" Dad grabbed the radio and called for help. He told them where they were and what happened. He said they had life jackets and a dolphin friend. "Help is on the way, Dad!" Tom and Lily said. They hugged Dad and the dolphin. They waited for the rescue boat to come. They were scared, but they were also brave. They knew they would be safe soon.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty flower. It wanted to bloom and be attractive. The flower lived near a pitch where kids played ball. One sunny day, the flower bloomed. It was so happy and pretty. A little girl saw the flower and said, "Wow! What a pretty flower! It bloomed so big!" The flower felt proud and happy. More kids came to play on the pitch. They all saw the flower and liked it. The flower had many friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who loved radishes. He had a big box full of radishes. The old man liked to eat them every day. He was very happy with his radishes. One day, the old man went to his box to get a radish. But he saw that the box was shut. The old man tried to open the box, but he could not. He was very sad. A little bird came and saw the old man. The bird wanted to help. The bird tried to open the box, but it could not. Then, a big wind came and blew the box away. The old man and the bird were very sad. They could not eat the radishes anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Zip. Zip was a very fast cat. He loved to run and play with his friends in the park. One day, Zip was playing with his friend, a little dog named Spot. They were playing a fun game of catch. Zip threw the ball to Spot, and Spot caught it with his mouth. "Zip, I don't want to lose the ball!" said Spot. "Let's be careful when we throw it." Zip agreed, and they continued to play. They were having so much fun that they didn't notice the ball was getting dirty. Suddenly, the ball rolled into a big puddle of mud. The ball was lost! "Oh no, we lost the ball!" cried Spot. "Don't worry, Spot," said Zip. "We can find it together." Zip and Spot looked and looked for the ball. They finally found it in the mud. They were so happy! They learned that even famous cats like Zip can lose things, but with the help of friends, they can find them again. And they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big tree in his backyard. One day, he saw a dead branch on the tree. Tim wanted to cut the branch, but he was too small. He asked his dad, "Dad, can you cut the dead branch for me?" His dad said, "Yes, I will cut it for you, but we have to wait for one week." Tim was sad, but he knew he had to wait. After one week, his dad cut the dead branch from the tree. Tim was so happy. He said, "Thank you, Dad!" Now, Tim could play near the tree without worrying about the dead branch.
