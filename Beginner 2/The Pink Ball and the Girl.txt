Category: Beginner
Title: The Pink Ball and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a pink ball that could shine. The ball was very happy and loved to play with kids. Every day, the ball would roll around and shine so bright for all to see. One day, the ball met a new friend, a little girl. The girl loved to touch the ball and make it shine. They played together all the time. The ball would shine, and the girl would laugh and clap her hands. But one day, the little girl lost the ball. She looked and looked, but could not find it. The ball was sad because it could not shine for the girl anymore. The girl was sad too. She missed her shiny pink ball. And they never found each other again.

Lily and Ben went to the shop with Mom. They wanted to buy a chair for their room. Mom said they could choose one chair together. They saw many chairs in the shop. Some were big, some were small. Some were red, some were blue. Some were soft, some were hard. Lily liked a small chair that was pink and fluffy. She said, "I want this chair, Mom. It is so pretty and cozy." Ben liked a small chair that was green and wooden. He said, "I want this chair, Mom. It is so strong and cool." Lily and Ben did not agree. They both wanted their own chair. They started to argue and cry. Mom said, "Stop, Lily and Ben. You can only buy one chair. You have to share. Why don't you try both chairs and see which one is more comfortable?" Lily and Ben tried both chairs. They sat on them and bounced on them. They found out that the pink chair was too soft and the green chair was too hard. They did not like either chair very much. They looked around the shop again. They saw a small chair that was yellow and striped. It was not too soft and not too hard. It was just right. Lily and Ben said, "We like this chair, Mom. It is so bright and happy. Can we buy this chair, please?" Mom said, "Yes, you can buy this chair. It is a good choice. You can both use it and enjoy it." Lily and Ben smiled and hugged Mom. They bought the yellow chair and took it home. They put it in their room and read books on it. They were very happy with their new chair.

One day, a little boy went outside to play. He saw a new bug he had never seen before. The bug was small and green. The boy thought it was so cool. He started to imagine that the bug was his friend. They would go on big adventures together. They would climb trees, jump in puddles, and play in the grass. The boy and the bug would have so much fun. The sun began to set, and it was time for the boy to go home. He said goodbye to his bug friend and went inside. That night, as he lay in bed, he thought about his day with the bug. He smiled and fell asleep, dreaming of more fun days with his new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a mouse named Tim. Tim loved to eat cheese. One day, he found a large piece of cheese near the big tree. Tim was so happy and wanted to share it with his friends. Tim waved to his friends, the cat, the dog, and the bird. They all came to see the large cheese. They all wanted to eat it too. So, they decided to share the cheese with each other. They all sat down and took turns taking bites of the cheese. As they shared the cheese, they all became very good friends. They played together and helped each other. They learned that sharing is a good thing to do. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy and brings friends closer together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big board with many colors. She liked to draw on the board every day. One day, she drew a fine picture of a cat. She was very happy. Amy's friend, Tom, came to her house to play. He saw the fine cat on the board and said, "Wow, Amy! Your cat is very nice. I like it a lot!" Amy smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. I remember how to draw cats because I love them so much." Tom and Amy played and drew more pictures on the board. They had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, they looked at all the pictures they drew. They remembered the fun they had and felt happy.
