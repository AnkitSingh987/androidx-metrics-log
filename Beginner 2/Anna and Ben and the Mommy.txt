Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Mommy

Anna and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. They hear a loud noise from the kitchen. They run to see what it is. They see water coming out of the sink. The sink has a leak. "Oh no!" Anna says. "We need to tell Mommy!" Ben nods. He sees a phone on the counter. He picks it up and pushes a button. He hears Mommy's voice. "Hello?" Mommy says. "Mommy, Mommy!" Ben says. "The sink is leaking! There is water everywhere!" "What?" Mommy says. "I'm at the store. I'll be home soon. Can you turn off the water?" Ben looks at the sink. He sees a knob under it. He tries to turn it, but it is too hard. "I can't, Mommy. It's too hard!" Ben says. "OK, don't worry. I'll call Mr. Lee. He is our neighbor. He is very helpful. He can fix the leak. Stay away from the water, OK?" Mommy says. "OK, Mommy. Hurry home!" Ben says. He hangs up the phone. He and Anna go back to the living room. They wait for Mr. Lee and Mommy. They hope the leak will stop soon. They are glad they have a helpful neighbor and a loving mommy.

One day, a big car went zoom down the road. It was very fast. A small cat was on the road, feeling helpless. The cat was scared of the car. The car did not see the cat. A little boy saw the cat and the zoom car. He wanted to help the cat. He picked up a bottle of water. He ran to the road and sprayed the water at the car. The car stopped. The boy thought the car would be mad. But, the car was happy. The car did not want to hurt the cat. The boy and the car saved the cat together. They all became friends.

One day, a mild cat named Sam went for a walk. Sam liked to look at the trees and the sky. The sun was bright and the grass was green. Sam felt happy. As Sam walked, he saw a big fruit on the ground. It was round and red. Sam wanted to eat the fruit, but it was too big. So, Sam had an idea. He would split the fruit in half. Sam used his paws to split the fruit. It was not easy, but Sam did it. Inside the fruit, there were seeds and sweet juice. Sam ate the fruit and felt even happier. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a new dancer named Lily. Lily loved to dance all day long. She liked to spin, jump, and twirl in her pretty dress. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came to watch her dance. Tim said, "Wow, Lily! You are a great dancer!" Lily was happy to hear this and wanted to show Tim a new dance she had been practicing. But, Lily did not manage to do the new dance well. She fell down and felt sad. Tim said, "It's okay, Lily. You can try again." Lily tried again and again, and finally, she managed to do the new dance. Lily and Tim danced together all day long. They were very happy and had lots of fun. And that is how Lily managed to learn the new dance and become an even better dancer.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a friend named Sam. They liked to play together. One day, they went to the park. They saw a harmless cat. The cat was very dirty. Tim and Sam wanted to help the cat. They decided to bathe the cat. They took the cat to Tim's house. They filled a tub with water. They put the cat in the water. The cat did not like it. The cat jumped out of the tub. It was very scared. It ran away from Tim and Sam. The cat got lost. Tim and Sam were sad. They could not find the cat. The cat was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, deaf bear named Hugo. Hugo was so deaf that he could not hear even the loudest sounds. One day, Hugo met a rabbit in the woods. The rabbit said, "Hello Hugo! I have a poem I want to test on you." Hugo was so excited. Hugo asked, "What kind of test?" The rabbit said, "I'm going to read this poem and you will repeat it back to me. If you can repeat it word for word, then you pass the test!" Hugo nodded. He took a deep breath and listened very carefully to the rabbit. He repeated the poem back to the rabbit. The rabbit smiled and said, "You passed the test! Well done Hugo!" Then he gave a big hug to Hugo. Hugo was so happy. He felt very proud of himself. The two friends hugged for a long time, and then they parted ways with a wave and a smile. Hugo was so happy that he had passed the test.
