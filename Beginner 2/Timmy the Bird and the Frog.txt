Title: Timmy the Bird and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy had an elderly friend named Mr. Frog. Mr. Frog was made of rubber. They loved playing together in the park. One day, Timmy saw other birds fly high in the sky. He wanted to fly too. But Timmy did not know how to fly. He was sad. Mr. Frog saw Timmy and wanted to help him. Mr. Frog told Timmy, "Don't be sad. We can learn to fly together." They tried and tried. With Mr. Frog's help, Timmy learned to fly. They flew high in the sky and had fun. The moral of the story is that with the help of good friends, you can learn new things and have fun together.

One day, a little boy called John was playing in the park. He saw a very special animal. It was a panda! The panda was very polite and he was very curious about it. John said hello to the panda and the panda said hello back. The panda smiled and offered to play with John. The two of them had lots of fun playing tag and running around. After an hour, the sky turned dark. It was time to go home. John asked the panda if he had a home and the panda said yes. But, he said, his home was not very clean. John suggested that he could help the panda clean his home. The panda was very happy with this idea and thanked John. The two of them worked together to tidy up the panda's home. John was so proud that he had been able to help the panda. He waved goodbye and went back home with a happy smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She had dark brown hair and big, blue eyes. One day, the little girl was picking flowers in the garden. Suddenly, a wise old man with a long beard appeared and said, "Little girl, pick the right flower and life will be fast and full of energy". The little girl was not sure what to pick, so she looked around carefully. She found a small, yellow flower and decided to pick it. The old man smiled and said, "You have picked wisely! Now, it is time to go walking and play - so enjoy your life!" The little girl was so happy. She took off running and laughing in the sunshine. She felt as fast as a cheetah! Her life was now full of energy. That one little flower changed her life forever!
