Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a weird tree with red cherries on it. Lily wanted to pick a cherry from the tree. She reached up high, but she was too small. Lily felt sad. Then, Lily met a boy named Tom. He said, "Why are you sad?" Lily said, "I want a cherry, but I can't reach." Tom was taller than Lily. He said, "I can help you!" Tom picked a cherry from the tree for Lily. But the tree started to tremble. The tree was shaking! They were scared, but then they saw a big bird fly out of the tree. The bird had been hiding in the tree. Lily and Tom laughed. They were not scared anymore. They ate the cherries and played in the park all day. The weird tree with the red cherries was now their favorite tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was nervous because he had a big fight with his friend, Sam. Tim wanted to say sorry, but he did not know how. One day, Tim set out to find Sam. He walked and walked until he saw Sam playing with a ball. Tim went up to Sam and said, "I am sorry for our fight. Can we be friends again?" Sam looked at Tim and said, "No, I am still mad." Tim felt very sad and walked away. He saw a big tree and sat under it. He thought about what he could do to make Sam happy again. Tim had an idea. He picked some pretty flowers and took them to Sam. But when Tim gave the flowers to Sam, Sam threw them away. He said, "I do not want your flowers. Go away!" Tim cried and ran back to the tree. He sat there all day, feeling very sad. The moral of the story is that sometimes, even when we try our best to make things right, not everyone will forgive us. We must learn to accept it and move on.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to go to school and enjoy his time in the classroom. One day, a new girl named Sue came to his class. Sue's family was rich, and she had many toys. In the classroom, Tim and Sue did not share well. Tim wanted to play with Sue's toys, but Sue said, "No, these are mine!" They were both sad and did not enjoy their time together. One day, a big wind came and blew all the toys away. Tim and Sue were very sad. But then, they found one toy that the wind did not take. They decided to share the toy and play together. They both enjoyed the toy and became good friends. The wind taught them that sharing was more important than having many toys.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room with a big ladder. A little boy named Tim lived in the house. Tim loved to climb the ladder and seat at the top. One day, Tim's friend, Lily, came to play. She saw the ladder and said, "Wow, can we climb it?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's go up and seat at the top!" So, Tim and Lily climbed the ladder together. When they reached the top, they looked around and saw all the toys in the tidy room. They played with the toys and had lots of fun. After they finished playing, they climbed down the ladder and put all the toys back in their places. The room was tidy again, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went to the park to play. It was a cold day, so he wore his warm coat. At the park, he saw a big tree with a cut on it. Tim went to the tree and touched the cut. "Ouch!" he said. "That must hurt!" A little squirrel came out of the tree. The squirrel looked at Tim and said, "Yes, it does hurt. The wind was very strong, and it broke my tree." Tim wanted to help the squirrel. He ran home and got a big band-aid from his mom. He went back to the park and put the band-aid on the tree's cut. The squirrel was very happy. "Thank you, Tim!" the squirrel said. They played together in the park all day.
