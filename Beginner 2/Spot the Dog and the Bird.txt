Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Bird

One day, a kind dog named Spot went to the park. Spot loved to play with his friends. They liked to run and jump and have fun. Spot was happy when he played with his friends. Spot saw a big tree and ran to it. He found a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird said, "Help me, please!" Spot wanted to help the bird. He thought of a way to make the bird feel better. Spot had an idea. He said to the bird, "I will order a band-aid for you!" The bird was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bird up into the tree. Spot was sad, but he did not give up. He climbed the tree to get the bird. He put the band-aid on the bird's wing. The bird felt better and said, "Thank you, Spot!" They became good friends and played together in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a king. The king was very kind to everyone. One day, it was very icy outside. The king went for a walk in the cold. The king saw a small boy. The boy was trying to pull a big sled. The sled was too heavy for the boy. The king said, "Let me help you pull the sled." The boy was happy. Together, they pulled the big sled. The king and the boy became good friends. They played in the snow all day. The people in the village saw the king and the boy. They learned that it is good to help each other. The village became a happy place where everyone helped their friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Ben and Mia like to act. They have a big box of clothes and hats and toys. They can be anyone they want. Today, they are playing cops and robbers. Ben is the cop and Mia is the robber. Mia has a bag of candy that she stole from the store. Ben has a badge and a whistle. Ben chases Mia around the house. He blows his whistle and shouts, "Stop, robber! Give me the candy!" Mia runs and laughs. She says, "No, no, you can't catch me!" She hides behind the sofa. Ben looks for her. He sees her feet sticking out. He grabs her and says, "I got you! You are under arrest!" He takes her to the basement. He says, "This is the jail. You have to stay here until you say sorry." He puts her in a corner. He finds a big cardboard box. He says, "This is your cell. You can't get out." He puts the box over her. He says, "Bye, robber!" He goes upstairs. Mia is inside the box. It is dark and quiet. She can't see anything. She can't hear anything. She can't move. She feels scared and sad. She says, "Ben, Ben, where are you? Let me out! I'm sorry, I'm sorry!" But Ben doesn't hear her. He is busy playing with his cars. Mia feels helpless. She cries and cries. She wishes Ben would come back and be her friend again.

One day, a gentle cat named Fluffy found a sack. Fluffy did not want to lose the sack, so she carried it in her mouth. She walked and walked, looking for her friend, Bunny. When Fluffy found Bunny, she said, "Look! I have a sack. Let's open it and see what is inside." Bunny was very excited and helped Fluffy open the sack. They were both very happy and could not wait to see what was inside. As they opened the sack, something unexpected happened. A small bird flew out of the sack! The bird said, "Thank you for saving me! I was stuck in the sack and could not get out." Fluffy and Bunny were surprised but glad they could help the bird. They all became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big toy rocket in his room. It was red and shiny. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play with the rocket and show it to his best friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house with the rocket in his hands. When Tim got to Sam's house, he said, "Look, Sam! I found a big rocket! Let's play with it!" Sam looked at the rocket and said, "Wow, it's so cool! But we need to be careful. Rockets can be dangerous." Tim and Sam decided to play with the rocket outside, where it was safe. As they played, Tim's mom came outside. She had a small box in her hand. "Boys, I want to record you playing with the rocket," she said. Tim and Sam smiled and played with the rocket while Tim's mom recorded them. They had so much fun, and now they could watch the video of their fun day with the rocket anytime they wanted.
