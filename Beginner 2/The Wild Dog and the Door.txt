Category: Beginner
Title: The Wild Dog and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a wild dog. He lived in the woods near a small house. In the house, there was a boy and his mom. They were happy and loved their home. One day, the wild dog saw the house. He wanted to go inside and play. He ran to the house and tried to open the door. But the door was shut. So, the wild dog tried to break the door. He pushed and pushed, but the door did not break. The boy heard the wild dog outside. He opened the door and saw the wild dog. The boy was not scared. He knew the wild dog just wanted to play. So, the boy and the wild dog became friends. They played in the yard every day, and the wild dog never tried to break the house again.

Once upon a time there was a bear who was very cold. He put on his scarf and asked his friend, "Where should I go this week?". His friend thought for a moment and said, "Let's go to the beach. The days will be warm and it's close by". The bear smiled and said "That sounds great!". So the bear and his friend came down to the beach on a cold morning. They found a spot near the shore and put down their picnic blanket. The two friends chatted and played games. It was so much fun! The next week the friends came back to the beach. It was so cold but they still had a great time. They ran around and built sand castles. The bear laughed and said, "What a wonderful day!".

Once upon a time there were two friends. They were both happy and wanted to play. First, the friends wanted to go for a walk. They held hands and left the house. On their walk, they saw a big, fat bird flying in the sky. The bird was carrying something that looked fake. When the two friends looked closer, they saw it was a big, white lung! The lung was big, soft and squishy. The two friends were so excited. They wanted to take the lung home to play. But, it was too heavy so they had to leave it. The friends held hands and ran home. On the way, they started to make plans for the next day. They promised to leave at sunrise to find the lung again!

One day, a big red car with four wheels went down the road. A little blue bike with two wheels saw the car and wanted to join. The little bike said, "Can I join you, big red car?" The big red car said, "Yes, you can join me, little blue bike. Let's go fast together!" So, the little blue bike and the big red car went down the road together, laughing and having fun. But, the little blue bike felt guilty. It knew that bikes should not go fast with cars. The little blue bike said to the big red car, "I feel guilty. I should not go fast with you." Just then, something unexpected happened. The big red car hit a big bump in the road and its wheels broke. The little blue bike tried to help, but it was too late. The big red car could not go fast anymore. The little blue bike felt very sad and guilty for joining the big red car.

One day, Mike decided to carry the two big boxes. He could carry the boxes, though they were heavy. When Mike carried the boxes, he started to itch. Mike hated the itch and wished it would go away. Mike's Mom saw him carrying the boxes and said, "Mike, you are so gifted. You can carry all of those boxes, even though they are so heavy!" Mike replied, "Yes, but I've got this itch which isn't going away." Mike's Mom laughed and said, "Well, let's go and find something to help you get rid of the itch." So Mike and his Mom got up and started looking for a solution to the itch. After some time, they found something which helped the itch. Mike was relieved, and he said, "Now I can carry these boxes without feeling any itch!" And then, with a smile on his face, Mike continued to carry the two big boxes.
