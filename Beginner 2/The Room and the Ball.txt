Category: Beginner
Title: The Room and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big room. In the room, there were many toys. A little boy named Tim lived in the house with the room. Tim loved to play with his toys all day long. He had a favorite toy, a red ball. Tim was very proud of his red ball. One day, Tim's mom told him to clean the room. Tim did not want to clean the room. He tried to resist, but his mom said he must do it. So, Tim started to put away his toys. He picked up his red ball last because it was his favorite. When the room was clean, Tim's mom was happy. She told Tim he did a good job. Tim was proud of himself for cleaning the room. He learned that sometimes, even if you don't want to do something, it is good to do it anyway. And after cleaning, Tim played with his red ball again.

---

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big, red button on the ground. She picked it up and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look, I found a button!" Sue said. Tom wanted the button too, so they both decided to put it on their shirts. But there was only one button, so they couldn't both put it on their shirts. Tom said, "I want the button!" Sue said, "No, I found it first!" They started to get fierce with each other. They didn't want to share the button. Then, a big dog came by and saw the red button on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran away. Sue and Tom stopped being fierce and decided to work together to get the button back. They chased the dog until he dropped the button. Sue and Tom picked up the button and decided to share it. They took turns putting the button on their shirts. They learned that sharing is better than being fierce and fighting. And from that day on, Sue and Tom became the best of friends.

Tom and Lily are brother and sister. They like to play together in their room upstairs. They have many toys and books and games. But sometimes they have to go to bed early, and they miss each other. One night, Mom says, "It's time for bed, Tom and Lily. Brush your teeth and put on your pajamas." Tom and Lily do what Mom says, but they are not sleepy. They want to play more. "Can we stay up a little longer, Mom?" Tom asks. "No, you need to sleep, Tom. You have school tomorrow. And so does Lily. Good night, sweeties." Mom kisses them and turns off the light. Tom and Lily lie in their beds, but they are not happy. They miss each other. "Tom, are you awake?" Lily whispers. "Yes, Lily. I'm awake. I miss you." Tom whispers back. "Me too, Tom. I miss you. Do you want to play?" Lily whispers. "Yes, Lily. I want to play. But how? Mom will hear us." Tom whispers. "I have an idea. We can use our walkie-talkies. I have one under my pillow. Do you have yours?" Lily whispers. "Yes, Lily. I have mine. That's a good idea. Let's do it." Tom whispers. Tom and Lily take out their walkie-talkies and turn them on. They talk to each other in low voices. They tell each other stories and jokes and secrets. They laugh and giggle and have fun. They forget about Mom and sleep and school. They are happy. But they don't know that Mom is listening to them from downstairs. She has a walkie-talkie too. She smiles and shakes her head. She knows they miss each other. She knows they are good kids. She lets them play for a little while. Then she says, "Tom, Lily, it's Mom. I hear you. It's time to sleep now. I love you." Tom and Lily are surprised. They say, "Sorry, Mom. We love you too. Good night." They turn off their walkie-talkies and put them away. They hug their pillows and close their eyes. They are sleepy now. They don't miss each other anymore. They know they will see each other in the morning. They are happy.
