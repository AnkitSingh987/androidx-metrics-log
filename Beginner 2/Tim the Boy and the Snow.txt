Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Snow

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He looked out the window and saw the icy snow outside. He wanted to go play in the snow, but his mom said it was too cold. Tim felt sad, but he had an idea. He would bring the snow inside! Tim put on his boots and coat, then went outside. He picked up some icy snow and brought it into his room. He put the snow on his bed and started to play. But soon, the snow began to melt and his bed became wet. "Tim, what did you do?" his mom asked, seeing the wet bed. Tim said, "I wanted to play with the icy snow, so I brought it to my bed." His mom shook her head and said, "Snow belongs outside, not on your bed. Let's clean this up and then you can come play in the snow with me outside." Tim smiled, happy to play in the snow with his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful cat. The cat liked to relax under a big tree. One sunny day, while the cat was relaxing, she heard a loud noise. The cat jumped up and saw a big box. The box had a big bow on it. The cat was very curious. She thought, "What a surprise! What could be inside the box?" The cat opened the box and found a big, soft pillow. She thought it was for her to relax on. But then, the pillow started to move! The cat was so surprised when a little dog jumped out of the pillow. The dog was hiding inside the pillow all along. The cat and the dog became best friends and relaxed together under the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell all the other animals what to do. One day, Tom saw a little bird named Timmy. Timmy was very sad and had a big worry. Tom asked Timmy, "Why are you sad?" Timmy said, "I can't find my way home. I guess I am lost." Tom thought for a moment and said, "I will help you find your home, but you must do what I say." Timmy agreed, and they started to look for his home. Tom was very bossy and told Timmy to go this way and that way. But then, Tom realized that he didn't know where Timmy's home was either. He felt bad for being bossy and not really helping. Tom said, "I'm sorry, Timmy. I don't know where your home is. Let's ask the other animals for help." They asked their friends, and soon, they found Timmy's home. Timmy was so happy and thanked Tom. From that day on, Tom learned that being bossy is not always good. It is better to ask for help and work together with friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there was a soft and fluffy cat. This cat loved to play and jump all day long. One sunny day, the cat was playing with a ball in the house. As the cat was playing, it saw a big puddle of water on the floor. The cat did not want to get wet, so it tried to jump over the puddle. But the cat did not jump far enough, and its back legs started to slip on the wet floor. The cat was scared, but it tried again to jump over the puddle. This time, the cat jumped very high and did not slip. The cat was very happy and went on playing with its ball. From that day on, the cat always looked for puddles before it jumped, so it would not slip again.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big pond. The pond has many ducks and fish. Lily and Ben want to feed the ducks and fish. They find some bread and go to the pond. They sit on a seat near the water. They break the bread and throw it to the ducks and fish. The ducks and fish come to eat the bread. Lily and Ben are happy. "Look, Ben, that duck is very big!" Lily says. "Yes, Lily, and that fish is very small!" Ben says. They see a new duck. The new duck is white and has a red beak. The new duck swims to the seat. The new duck wants some bread too. "Hello, duck, do you want some bread?" Lily asks. "Quack, quack, quack!" the duck says. Lily and Ben laugh. They give some bread to the new duck. The new duck eats the bread and wags its tail. "Thank you, duck, you are very nice!" Ben says. "Quack, quack, quack!" the duck says. Lily and Ben make a new friend. They are happy. They play with the new duck until it is time to go home. They wave goodbye to the new duck and the other ducks and fish. "Bye, duck, see you soon!" Lily says. "Bye, duck, have fun!" Ben says. The new duck quacks and swims away. The pond is deep and blue. Lily and Ben go home with their parents. They tell them about the new duck. They want to meet the new duck again. They like the pond and the seat. They like to play in the park.
