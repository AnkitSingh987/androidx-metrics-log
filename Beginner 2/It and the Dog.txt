Category: Beginner
Title: It and the Dog

It was a sunny day, and Tom and his dad were walking in the park. Tom's dad looked around and saw a furry brown barrel. "Let's play!" said Tom's dad excitedly. Tom and his dad started to roll the barrel around. As they were playing, Tom sneezed really loud! His dad laughed and said, "Bless you, my little one!" Tom smiled and looked around. He saw a hairy dog that was prancing around, and the dog made him sneeze once again. His dad smiled and said, "It looks like you found another friend!"

---

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat steak. One day, Spot found a big steak on a plate. He wanted to eat it all, but it was too big! Spot had a problem. Spot's friend, a little bird named Tweet, saw him. Tweet wanted to help Spot complete the big steak. So, Tweet had an idea. They could break the steak into small pieces and share it. That way, they could eat it all. Spot and Tweet worked together to break the steak into small pieces. They ate and ate until the steak was all gone. They were so happy they could complete the big steak together. From that day on, Spot and Tweet were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a cat named Tom. They liked to play together. One day, Lucy and Tom were playing outside. A big dog came and started to tease Tom. Tom was frightened and ran away. Lucy was frightened too. She said, "Go away, big dog! Leave my cat alone!" The big dog stopped teasing Tom and went away. Lucy and Tom were happy again. They played together all day. At the end of the day, Lucy said, "I love you, Tom. We will always be friends."

One day, a little boy named Tim found a mosquito. The mosquito was very boring. It did not do anything fun. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sam, the mosquito. So, he put it in a jar. Tim took the jar to Sam's house. He said, "Sam, look at my mosquito!" Sam looked at the mosquito and said, "That is a boring mosquito. It does not do anything fun." Tim was sad because Sam did not like his mosquito. Then, the boring mosquito got out of the jar. It bit Tim and Sam. They both cried and went to their moms. Their moms put some cream on their bites, but it still hurt. Tim and Sam did not like the mosquito anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty dress that she loved to wear. One day, Lily went outside to play in the mud. She had so much fun, but her dress got filthy. Lily went back home and showed her mom the filthy dress. Her mom said, "Oh no! We need to wash it right away!" They put the dress in the washing machine and waited for it to be clean again. After the dress was clean, Lily tried to put it on, but it didn't fit anymore. She had grown so much! Her mom said, "Don't worry, Lily. We will find you a new dress that fits just right." They went to the store and found a beautiful new dress that fit Lily perfectly. And from that day, Lily made sure to keep her new dress clean while she played.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside when it rains. They put on their boots and coats and go to the park. There they find many puddles. They jump and splash in the puddles. They laugh and have fun. "Look, Anna, a big puddle!" Ben says. He runs to the puddle and jumps in. He makes a big splash. The water goes everywhere. "Wow, Ben, you are very wet!" Anna says. She goes to the puddle and jumps in too. She makes a bigger splash. The water goes even higher. "Ha, ha, Anna, you are wetter than me!" Ben says. He jumps in the puddle again. He tries to make the biggest splash ever. He sends water to the sky. "Oh, no, Ben, look out!" Anna says. She sees something in the air. It is a bird. A big bird. A big, clean bird. The bird does not like water. The bird does not like Ben. The bird flies to Ben and pecks his head. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben says. He runs away from the puddle and the bird. He cries and holds his head. "Are you okay, Ben?" Anna says. She runs after him and hugs him. She is sorry for him. "I am okay, Anna, but that bird was mean!" Ben says. He wipes his tears and looks at his head. He has a red mark. He is not happy. "Let's go home, Ben. We can get dry and have a snack. Maybe we can play with your toys. Do you want that?" Anna says. She wants to make Ben feel better. "Yes, Anna, I want that. You are a good friend. Thank you." Ben says. He smiles and nods. He takes Anna's hand and they walk home. They leave the puddle and the bird behind. They are still wet, but they are happy.
