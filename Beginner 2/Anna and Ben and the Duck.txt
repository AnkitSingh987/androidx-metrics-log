Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Duck

Anna and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. They have a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Anna has a yellow duck that she likes to squeeze and make quack. Ben has a red car that he likes to zoom and make vroom. Mom is in the kitchen making lunch. She is happy that Anna and Ben are playing nicely. She does not want them to disturb her while she is cooking. She tells them to be quiet and not to make a mess. But Anna and Ben do not listen to Mom. They start to play louder and louder. They throw their toys around and knock over the lamp. They find a stamp on the table and stick it on the wall. They laugh and giggle and do not care about Mom. Mom hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees the lamp on the floor, the stamp on the wall, and the toys everywhere. She is angry and sad. She says to Anna and Ben, "Why did you disturb me? Why did you make such a mess? You are naughty and rude. You have to clean up and say sorry." Anna and Ben feel bad. They see that Mom is upset. They say to Mom, "We are sorry, Mom. We did not mean to disturb you. We did not mean to make a mess. We were just having fun. We will clean up and be good." They hug Mom and help her pick up the lamp, the stamp, and the toys. Mom smiles and forgives them. She says to Anna and Ben, "Thank you, Anna and Ben. I love you. You are good and kind. Now let's have lunch." They go to the kitchen and eat their lunch. They are happy and quiet.

Once upon a time, in a big orange house, there lived a happy cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball all day. Today was a sunny day, so Tim went outside to play. As Tim played, he saw his friend Sue, the dog. Sue was very nice and loved to play too. Tim had an urge to play with Sue today. So, Tim asked Sue if she wanted to play with his ball. Sue was happy to play with Tim. They played and played until the sun went down. Then, they went back to the big orange house to rest. Tim and Sue had so much fun today. They knew they would play together again soon.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. They took Tim's toy car with them. The toy car had a little motor inside and was black. Tim loved to play with his toy car. At the park, Tim played with his toy car while his dad sat on a bench. Suddenly, a big dog ran up to Tim and sniffed his toy car. The dog picked up the black toy car in its mouth and started to run away. Tim was so scared that he screamed for his dad. Tim's dad heard the scream and ran to help. He saw the big dog with the black toy car and called out to the dog. To their surprise, the dog stopped running and dropped the toy car. The dog wagged its tail and looked friendly. It turned out that the dog just wanted to play with Tim and his toy car. Tim, his dad, and the big dog played together in the park, having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim loved to touch things. He touched the big red apples, the soft green leaves, and the cold milk bottles. His mom told him, "Tim, please don't touch everything." In the store, Tim saw a big cup of yogurt. He loved yogurt. It was sweet and yummy. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have the yogurt, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have it if you promise not to touch anything else." Tim was persistent. He wanted the yogurt so much. He held his mom's hand and walked to the cashier. He did not touch anything else. He got the yogurt and was very happy. Tim learned that when he listened to his mom, good things happened.
