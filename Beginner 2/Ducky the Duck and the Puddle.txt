Category: Beginner
Title: Ducky the Duck and the Puddle

One day, a little duck named Ducky was walking near the pond. He saw a big puddle and wanted to bathe in it. Ducky was very alert, always looking around to see if there were any friends to play with. As Ducky splashed in the puddle, a little frog named Froggy hopped over. "Hi Ducky, can I play too?" Froggy asked. Ducky smiled and said, "Yes, come in! The puddle is big enough for both of us." Ducky and Froggy had so much fun splashing and bathing in the puddle. They laughed and played together all day. When the sun started to set, they said goodbye and went to their homes. Ducky and Froggy knew they would play in the puddle again soon.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big, curious box in her room. She did not know what was inside, so she called her friend Tom to help her open it. Tom came over and they both tried to open the box, but it was very hard. They both let out a big sigh. Tom had an idea. He said, "Mia, let's ask my big brother for help." Mia agreed, and they went to find Tom's brother. They asked him very nicely, and he opened the box for them. Inside the box was a big mess. There were toys, clothes, and books everywhere. Mia and Tom were not happy with the result. Mia and Tom decided to clean up the mess together. They put the toys away, folded the clothes, and put the books back on the shelf. When they were done, they felt very proud of their hard work. The moral of the story is that it is important to work together and ask for help when you need it.

Ben was a naughty boy. He did not listen to his mom or his dad. He liked to break things and make a mess. He did not say sorry or thank you. He made his mom and dad very sad and angry. One day, Ben saw a needle on the table. He wanted to play with it. He did not know that the needle was sharp and could hurt him. He did not know that the needle was for his dad's work. He grabbed the needle and ran away. "Ben, stop!" his mom shouted. "That needle is not a toy! Give it back to me!" But Ben did not stop. He laughed and ran to his room. He closed the door and locked it. He thought he was very clever and funny. He looked at the needle and tried to poke his teddy bear with it. But the needle was too hard and the teddy bear was too soft. The needle did not go in. Ben got bored and tried to poke his finger with it. He wanted to see what would happen. "Ow!" Ben cried. The needle hurt his finger. It made a small hole and blood came out. Ben dropped the needle and started to cry. He felt scared and sorry. He wanted his mom and dad to help him. He opened the door and ran to his mom and dad. He showed them his finger and the needle. He said, "I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I'm sorry. The needle hurt me. Please help me." His mom and dad hugged him and cleaned his finger. They put a band-aid on it and kissed it. They said, "Ben, we love you, but you were very naughty. You should not play with needles. They are dangerous and not for children. You could have hurt yourself or someone else. You should listen to us and be a good boy. We have to punish you for what you did. You have to go to your room and think about what you did. You can't play with your toys or watch TV. You have to stay there until dinner time." Ben nodded and said, "I understand. I'm sorry. I won't do it again. I want to be a good boy. I love you, mom and dad." He went to his room and sat on his bed. He felt sad and ashamed. He wished he had not played with the needle. He wished he had been a reliable boy. He learned his lesson and decided to be better. He hoped his mom and dad would forgive him and be happy with him. He hoped his finger would heal soon and not hurt anymore. He hoped his teddy bear was not angry with him. He hugged his teddy bear and said, "I'm sorry, teddy. I love you. You are my best friend."
