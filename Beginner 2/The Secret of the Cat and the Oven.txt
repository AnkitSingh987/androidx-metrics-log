Category: Beginner
Title: The Secret of the Cat and the Oven

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the kitchen. The cat liked to tease the dog by jumping on the dirty oven. The dog would bark and try to catch the cat, but the cat was too fast. They played this game every day, and the cat always won. One day, the cat teased the dog again and jumped on the dirty oven. But this time, the dog had a plan. He ran around the oven and hid behind it. The cat did not see the dog and thought he had won again. The cat laughed and jumped down from the oven. But the dog was waiting for the cat. When the cat jumped down, the dog jumped out and caught the cat. The cat was very surprised and the dog was very happy. They laughed and played together, and from that day on, they became best friends.

Lily wanted to go outside and play with her ball. She put on her red pants and her blue shirt. She ran to the door and tried to open it. But it was locked. "Mommy, can I go outside?" she asked. "Not now, Lily. It is too cold and wet. You will get sick," Mommy said. "But I want to play with my ball," Lily said. She felt sad and angry. Mommy saw her face and felt sorry for her. She was a kind mommy. She had an idea. "Wait here, Lily. I have a surprise for you," she said. She went to the closet and came back with a big box. "What is it, Mommy?" Lily asked. "Open it and see," Mommy said. Lily opened the box and gasped. Inside was a new ball. It was pink and purple and had stars on it. It was the prettiest ball Lily had ever seen. "Wow! Thank you, Mommy!" Lily said. She hugged Mommy and kissed her cheek. "You're welcome, Lily. I know you love balls. This one is special. It can glow in the dark. You can play with it in your room. But you have to be careful. Don't break anything or hurt yourself," Mommy said. "Okay, Mommy. I will be careful. Can I play with it now?" Lily asked. "Yes, you can. I permit you to play with it now. But only for a little while. Then you have to eat your lunch and take a nap," Mommy said. "Okay, Mommy. I love you," Lily said. "I love you too, Lily. Have fun with your ball," Mommy said. Lily took her ball and ran to her room. She turned off the light and threw the ball in the air. It glowed like a star. Lily laughed and clapped. She was happy. She forgot about the cold and wet outside. She had the best ball in the world. And the best mommy too.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant owl. The owl lived in a big tree. The owl had many friends. One day, the owl met a little bird. The little bird was sad because it could not find water to drink. The owl said, "Do not worry, little bird. I will help you find water to drink." So, the owl and the little bird flew to find water. They saw a small pond. The little bird was happy and said, "Thank you, owl! Now I can drink water." But the owl wanted to play a trick on the little bird. The owl said, "Do not drink the water here. I know a better place to drink water." The little bird trusted the owl and followed him. They flew far away, but there was no water to drink. The little bird was very sad and tired. The owl laughed at the little bird. In the end, the little bird could not find water to drink and was very sad. The owl felt bad for playing a trick on the little bird. The moral of the story is: do not play tricks on your friends, because it can hurt their feelings.

One day, a little dog named Lucky was playing in the park. He liked to run and jump. He was very happy. But then, he saw a big cat. The big cat did not like Lucky. The big cat said, "Go away, I do not want to play with you." Lucky was sad but he did not give up. He went to the big cat and said, "Please be my friend. I am nice and we can have fun." The big cat looked at Lucky and saw his big nose. The big cat said, "Okay, I will be your friend. But only if you help me find my toy with your big nose." Lucky was happy and he used his big nose to find the toy. The big cat was happy too. They played together all day. They became best friends. Lucky was a lucky dog to have a new friend.
