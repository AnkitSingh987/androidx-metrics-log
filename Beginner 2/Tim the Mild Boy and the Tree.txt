Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Mild Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a mild little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. They loved to play outside and look at the pretty landscape. The landscape had trees, flowers, and a big blue sky. One day, Tim and his mom went for a walk. They saw a broken tree. Tim said, "Mom, can we restore the tree?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. We can plant a new tree to make the landscape pretty again." So, Tim and his mom went home to get a new tree. They dug a hole and put the tree in it. They watered it and watched it grow. Soon, the landscape was pretty again. Tim and his mom were very happy. They loved to restore the landscape together.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman named Tom. Tom was glad when he caught many fish. One day, Tom went to the river to catch fish. He took his net and put it in the water. After a while, Tom pulled his net out of the water. He was very happy to see a big fish in it. "Wow! What a big fish!" said Tom. But the fish seemed to talk, "Please let me go. I can help you." Tom was shocked, but he let the fish go. The fish said, "Thank you! I will give you a surprise." The fish splashed water on Tom and swam away. The next day, Tom went to the river again. This time, he caught more fish than he ever had before. Tom was very glad and knew that the talking fish had helped him. From then on, Tom and the fish became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dragon. The dragon was bald. He lived in a cave near a small town. The people in the town were scared of the dragon. One day, a bell started to ring. The bald dragon heard the bell and went to see what was happening. The people in the town saw the dragon coming and hid. They thought the dragon would be mean. But the dragon was not mean. He just wanted to play! The bald dragon played with the bell and made it ring. The people saw that the dragon was nice. They came out of hiding and played with the dragon. They all became friends and lived happily together.

One day, a colorful insect went to the park. It saw a big tree and wanted to go up. The insect said, "I pray to go up the tree and see the sky." It started to go up the tree. On the tree, the insect met a bird. The bird said, "You are a colorful insect. Why do you want to go up the tree?" The insect said, "I want to see the sky." The bird said, "I can help you. I can fly." The bird took the insect up to the sky. They saw the sun and clouds. But then, the bird let go of the insect. The insect was scared. It said, "I pray to go back to the tree!" The bird came back and took the insect to the tree. The insect was happy and said, "Thank you, bird." The bird said, "You are welcome. Be careful next time."

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm with his dad. They saw many animals there. They saw cows, pigs, and horses. Tim liked the horses the most. They were big and strong. Tim saw a big pile of hay near the horses. He asked his dad, "What is the hay for?" His dad said, "The hay is important food for the horses. They need it to grow and be strong." Tim wanted to help the horses. He took some hay and gave it to the horses. The horses were happy and ate the hay. Tim felt good because he helped the horses. He knew that the hay was important for them.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to her class. She felt very tired and troubled. She did not want to be in class today. Mia just wanted to go back to her warm, cozy bed. In class, Mia could not help but yawn. Her friend Tom saw her yawn and asked, "Mia, why are you so tired?" Mia said, "I did not sleep well last night. I had a bad dream." Tom wanted to help Mia feel better. He told her a funny joke that made her laugh. Mia felt a little less troubled. They both listened to their teacher and had a good day in class.
