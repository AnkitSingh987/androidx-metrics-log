Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pet cat named Tom. They loved to play together all day. Lily liked to give Tom treats when he was a good and honest cat. Tom always tried to be honest so he could get more treats. One day, Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. Tom found a big treat under a tree. He wanted to eat it all by himself, but he knew that he should share it with Lily. Tom said, "Lily, look! I found a treat. Let's share it." Lily was happy that Tom was honest and shared the treat with her. After they ate the treat, Lily and Tom were very tired. They went back to their small house and got ready for bed. Lily said, "Tom, it's time to sleep." Tom jumped into bed with Lily and they both fell asleep. They had sweet dreams about playing in the garden and finding more treats.

One day, a boy named Tim had a kite. He loved his kite so much. He would run and the kite would fly high in the sky. Tim wanted his kite to be famous. He had a plan. Tim took a pen and wrote his name on the kite. He said, "Now, everyone will know this kite is mine." He showed his kite to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow, your kite has your name on it. That is so cool!" The next day, Tim and Sam went to the park to fly the kite. The wind was very strong. Tim let the kite go up high in the sky. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind took the kite far away. Tim and Sam could not find the kite. They were sad. Just then, a girl named Sue came to them. She said, "Is this your kite? I found it near the trees." Tim saw his name on the kite and said, "Yes, that is my famous kite! Thank you for finding it." They all played together and had a great time.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a clown making funny faces. Tim liked the clown and laughed a lot. The clown had a big red nose and wore a silly hat. Tim felt happy. The clown asked Tim, "Do you remember my name?" Tim felt embarrassed because he did not remember. The clown said, "That's okay. My name is Mr. Giggles." Tim smiled and said, "Hi, Mr. Giggles!" All of a sudden, the clown took off his red nose and silly hat. It was Tim's dad! Tim was very surprised. He did not know his dad was the clown. Tim and his dad laughed together. They had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max was a very filthy dog. He loved to play in the mud and roll in the dirt all day long. One sunny day, Max saw a big gate. The gate was open just a little bit. Max wanted to see what was on the other side of the gate. He decided to rush through the gate as fast as he could. Max ran and ran, and then he was on the other side. He saw a big, green field with lots of flowers and butterflies. Max played in the field for a long time. He was very happy. But when it was time to go home, Max could not find the gate. He looked and looked, but the gate was gone. Max was very sad and he could not go back home.

Once upon a time, there was a barber. This barber was unusual. He had a very big nose. One day, a little boy came to the barber. The boy was sad. The barber wanted to comfort the boy. The barber said, "Don't be sad. I will give you a nice haircut." The boy smiled. The barber started to cut the boy's hair. But the barber's big nose got in the way. The barber tried to cut the boy's hair, but his big nose made it hard. The boy started to cry. The barber could not comfort the boy. At the end, the boy's hair looked bad. The boy was very sad, and the barber was sad too.
