Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Tree

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. Max was an adventurous dog who loved to explore. He walked to the park and saw a big tree. Max wanted to see what was on the other side of the tree. As Max walked around the tree, he saw a new friend. It was a cat named Bella. Max wanted to introduce himself to Bella. He said, "Hi, I am Max. Do you want to play with me?" Bella looked at Max and said, "Yes, I want to play too!" Max and Bella played together all day. They ran and jumped in the park. When they got tired, they sat under the tree. Max gave Bella his paw, and they became best friends. They had a lot of fun and were happy to have met each other.

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big red circle on the ground. It is a hoop. "Look, a hoop!" Lily says. "Let's play with it." They run to the hoop and pick it up. Ben tries to roll it on the ground, but it falls down. Lily tries to throw it in the air, but it lands on her head. "Ouch!" Lily says. "This hoop is hard to play with." "I have an idea," Ben says. "Let's balance it on our heads." They put the hoop on their heads and try to walk without dropping it. They look funny and laugh. "Balancing is fun!" Lily says. They walk around the park, balancing the hoop on their heads. They see a dog, a bird, and a flower. They say hello to them. "Hello, dog!" Ben says. "Hello, bird!" Lily says. "Hello, flower!" they both say. The dog barks, the bird flies, and the flower smiles. They are happy to see Lily and Ben. But then, a gust of wind blows. The hoop falls off their heads and rolls away. "Oh no!" Lily and Ben say. "Our hoop!" They run after the hoop, but it is too fast. It rolls out of the park and into the street. "Stop, hoop!" Lily and Ben shout. But the hoop does not stop. It rolls and rolls and rolls, until it bumps into a car. The car honks, the hoop stops, and Lily and Ben catch up. They are glad they found their hoop, but they are also scared. The street is loud and busy. They hold hands and look for a way to go back to the park. "Are you okay, kids?" the driver of the car asks. He is a nice man. He sees their hoop and picks it up. "Thank you, mister," Lily and Ben say. "Where are your parents?" the man asks. "They are in the park," Lily and Ben say. "Let me take you back to them," the man says. He puts the hoop in his trunk and opens the door for them. Lily and Ben get in the car and buckle up. The man drives them back to the park. He gives them their hoop and waves goodbye. "Bye, mister," Lily and Ben say. They run to their parents and hug them. They tell them about their adventure with the hoop. "That sounds exciting," their parents say. "But be careful next time. Don't chase things into the street." "We will, mommy and daddy," Lily and Ben say. They play with the hoop some more, but this time, they stay in the park. They balance it, roll it, and throw it. They have fun with their big red circle.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Annie. Annie liked to wipe the windows of her house and stack the books in the cupboard. Today she wanted to do something different. She asked her mum, "Can I go outside and play?". Her mum smiled and said, "Yes, go outside and play". So Annie went out and started to wipe the tree and the grass. She even wiped the clouds in the sky. Annie kept wiping until she was tired. Then Annie looked up and saw a stack of hay bales. She decided to make a bed out of the hay bales and lay down. Annie quickly fell asleep. She dreamt of wiping the stars and stacking the moon and sun. When she awoke, she felt refreshed. Annie thanked her mum for letting her go outside and play. Mum said, “Anytime Annie, you can go outside and play whenever you want.” Annie smiled and felt happy.
