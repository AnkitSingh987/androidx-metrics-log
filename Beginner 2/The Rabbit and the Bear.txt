Category: Beginner
Title: The Rabbit and the Bear

One day, a little rabbit went for a walk in the mild sun. The rabbit had big ears and a soft, white coat. He hopped and hopped, and he met a big, brown bear. The bear looked at the rabbit and said, "Hello, little rabbit! You look happy today. Why do you smile?" The rabbit said, "I smile because it is a nice day and I am having fun hopping around." They both walked together and talked. The rabbit and the bear became good friends. They played in the sun and had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The rabbit stopped hopping and the bear stopped walking. They saw a big, round, green thing in front of them. They didn't know what it was. The rabbit and the bear looked at each other and smiled. They both said, "Let's find out what it is!" The rabbit hopped on the big, round, green thing and the bear pushed it with his big paws. It started to roll very fast! The rabbit and the bear laughed and played with the big, round, green thing all day. They found out it was a big ball! The rabbit and the bear had a great day together and they smiled a lot.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Fido. Fido had a vest. The vest was red and pretty. But Fido had a problem. The vest did not fit him. It was too small. Fido felt terrible. One day, Fido went to the park. He saw a big dog there. The big dog had a vest too. The big dog's vest was too big for him. The big dog felt terrible too. Fido and the big dog had an idea. They swapped vests. Now, Fido's vest fit him, and the big dog's vest fit him too. They were very happy. They played in the park all day. They were not terrible anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big dream. He wanted to find gold. He knew that if he found gold, he would be successful. One day, Tim went to ask his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me find gold?" he asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's go look for gold together." So, they went outside to start their search. They looked and looked, but they could not find any gold. Just as they were about to give up, Tim saw something shiny in the dirt. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. It was a small piece of gold! Tim and his mom were so happy. They knew that they were successful in their search for gold. And from that day on, Tim always believed that he could achieve anything he set his mind to.

Once upon a time, there was a big ball. The ball was unknown to many kids. One day, a boy named Tom found the ball. He was very happy. He played with the ball all day. The next day, Tom played with the ball again. But this time, something unexpected happened. The ball split in two! Tom felt pain in his heart. He didn't know what to do. He was very sad. Then, a little girl named Sue came by. She saw the two parts of the ball. She had an idea. Sue took the two parts and put them together. The ball was whole again! Tom and Sue were very happy. They became best friends and played with the ball every day.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk in the snow. The snow was soft and cold under his feet. He walked slow so he would not slip. Tim liked to look at the snow and point at things he saw. As Tim walked, he saw a big snow hill. He thought it would be fun to climb it. Tim pointed at the snow hill and said, "I want to go there!" So he walked slow to the hill and started to climb. When Tim got to the top of the snow hill, he saw something he did not expect. There was a big, friendly snowman! The snowman smiled at Tim and said, "Hello!" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that snowmen could talk. Tim and the snowman became good friends and played in the snow all day.
