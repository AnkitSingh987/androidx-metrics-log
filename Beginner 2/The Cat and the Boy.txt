Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Boy

Once upon a time, in a big school called University, there lived a small cat. The cat was troubled because it could not find its home. It walked around the school, looking for a place to rest. One day, the cat met a kind boy. The boy saw the troubled cat and wanted to help. "Don't worry, little cat," the boy said. "I will help you find your home." The cat was happy and followed the boy. As they walked, they saw a big dog. The dog started to yell at the cat. The cat was scared, but the boy protected it. They kept walking until they found the cat's home. The cat was no longer troubled and thanked the boy. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Emma found a big, spoiled apple on the ground. She picked it up and looked at it. "Yuck!" she said. "This apple is bad. I don't want to eat it." Emma saw her friend, Tom, sitting under a tree. She walked to him and showed him the apple. "Look, Tom! I found a big, spoiled apple!" Tom looked at the apple and said, "Let's not eat it, Emma. It's not good." They both decided to play a game instead. Emma and Tom took turns lying on the ground, making their bodies look like letters. They made an "A", a "B", and a "C". They laughed and had fun. At the end of the day, they threw the spoiled apple away and went home.

One sunny day, a little frog named Tim hopped to the shore. He saw a big pond and wanted to splash in it. Tim felt very comfortable and happy near the water. As Tim was splashing, a small fish named Sam swam up to him. Sam said, "Hi, I'm Sam! Do you want to play with me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's play and splash together!" Tim and Sam had lots of fun splashing in the water. They laughed and played all day. When the sun went down, they said goodbye and went to their homes. They knew they would play together again soon.

Once upon a time, in a big, red school, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim was very nosy. He liked to look at everything and ask lots of questions. One day, Tim saw a big, green box. He wanted to know what was inside. He asked his friend, Sally, "What is in the big, green box?" Sally said, "I don't know, let's start to open it." Tim and Sally opened the big, green box. Inside, they found a small, yellow ball. They were so happy. They played with the ball all day long. They threw it, kicked it, and rolled it. It was the best day ever. At the end of the day, Tim and Sally put the ball back in the big, green box. They said goodbye to each other and went home. Tim was not nosy anymore. He learned that it is fun to find new things, but it is even more fun to play with friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to crawl under the big chair in the living room. One day, while Spot was crawling under the chair, he found a rough ball. "Look, Owner!" Spot said to his owner, Tim. "I found a rough ball under the chair!" Tim smiled and picked up the ball. "Good job, Spot!" he said. Tim and Spot played with the rough ball all day. They had so much fun. When it was time for bed, Spot put the ball under the big chair so he could find it again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, happy bear. The bear loved to eat. One day, he found a tunnel. The tunnel was dark and long. The bear was excited to see what was inside. In the tunnel, the bear met a small, ignorant rabbit. The rabbit did not know much. The bear said, "Hello, rabbit. Do you know what is at the end of this tunnel?" The rabbit said, "No, bear. I do not know. But I want to find out too." So, the bear and the rabbit walked together in the tunnel. They talked and laughed. The bear was not hungry anymore. They found a big room with lots of food. The bear and the rabbit ate and ate. They were very happy. And they became good friends.
