Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to imagine big worlds with his toys. One day, Tim felt a small itch on his arm. The next day, Tim played outside. The sun was very bright and harsh. Tim still felt the itch on his arm. He thought, maybe a bug bit him. Tim went to his mommy and showed her the itch. His mommy said, "Don't worry, it will go away soon." The next day, Tim went to play with his friend, Sam. They played with their toys and imagined a big world together. Tim showed Sam the itch on his arm. Sam said, "I have one too!" They laughed and kept playing. The itch went away, just like Tim's mommy said.

One day, Tom and Sue were sad. It was a gloomy day. They wanted to play outside, but it was too wet. Tom had an idea. "Let's play inside with masks!" he said. Tom found a big box with masks in it. He put on a cat mask. Sue put on a dog mask. They laughed and played. But then, their friend Max looked through the window. Max felt left out. Max knocked on the door. "Can I join?" he asked. Tom and Sue said, "Yes!" Max put on a bird mask. They all played together. The gloomy day turned into a fun day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Jane who lived with her mom and dad by a mill. Every day Jane's mom and dad would load the mill with grain and the mill would grind it. One day, Mommy was carrying bags of grain to load into the mill, when she heard a loud noise coming from behind her. When Mommy turned around she saw Jane standing by an old truck. Mommy smiled and said, "What are you doing, Jane?" "I'm loading the truck," Jane said proudly. Then Mommy noticed that there was a man in the truck. He was old and deaf. Jane had been trying to load the bags of grain into the truck for him. Mommy said, "That's so kind of you, Jane. What an angel you are!" Jane smiled and said, "I'm happy to help!" The old man thanked Jane and waved goodbye as he drove away. Jane and Mommy waved back and smiled. They were glad that Jane had been able to help the old man.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hero named Tim. Tim was a very kind hero. He loved to help people. One day, Tim saw a little girl who was sad. She had lost her toy. Tim wanted to help her. Tim asked the girl a question. "What does your toy look like?" The girl said, "It is small and red." So Tim looked around for the toy. He found it under a big tree. It was easy for Tim to find it because he was tall. Tim gave the toy back to the girl. She was very happy. "Thank you, hero Tim!" she said. Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome. Remember to always ask for help when you need it." The girl nodded and went home. The moral of the story is to not be afraid to ask for help when you need it.

One day, a big healthy dog named Max was playing in the park. Max loved to run and jump, but he did not like to disturb anyone. Max was a very nice dog. At the park, Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was playing with her ball. Max had an opinion that he wanted to play with the ball too. Max ran to Lily and said, "Can I play with your ball?" Lily said, "Yes, you can play with my ball, but you have to be careful not to disturb the ducks in the pond." Max agreed, and they started to play together. Suddenly, the ball went into the pond. The ducks were not disturbed, but a big fish jumped out of the water and caught the ball in its mouth. The fish swam away with the ball, and Max and Lily were very surprised. They never thought a fish would take their ball. From that day on, Max and Lily became best friends and always played together, but they never forgot the day the fish took their ball.
