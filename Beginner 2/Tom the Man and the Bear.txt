Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Man and the Bear

One day, a kind man named Tom went to a store. He wanted to buy a toy for his little girl. In the store, he saw many toys. He saw a big toy truck, a small doll, and a soft teddy bear. Tom wanted to pick the best toy for his little girl, so he asked the store man, "Which toy should I buy?" The store man thought for a moment and said, "I think the teddy bear is the best. It is soft and nice to hug." Tom agreed and said, "I will buy the teddy bear, then." He paid for the toy and left the store, happy with his choice. As Tom walked home, he saw a little boy crying on the street. The boy was lost and could not find his mom. Tom felt bad for the boy and wanted to help. He was a compassionate man. He held the boy's hand and said, "Don't worry, little one. We will find your mom together." Tom and the boy walked and walked, asking people if they knew where the boy's mom was. Finally, they found her. The mom was so happy to see her son. She hugged him tight and thanked Tom. Then, something unexpected happened. The boy's mom gave Tom an order. She said, "Please, take this gift as a thank you." She handed Tom a big box. Tom opened the box and saw a beautiful toy castle. He knew his little girl would love it. In the end, Tom had two toys for his little girl, the teddy bear and the toy castle. And he felt happy that he could help the little boy and his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to climb trees in his big backyard. One day, he saw an incredible tree with a stand high up in the branches. Tim wanted to climb up to the stand, so he asked his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, can you help me climb this incredible tree?" Tim said. Sam nodded and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's climb together!" They both started to climb the tree. It was not easy, but they helped each other. When they reached the stand, they sat on it and looked around. They could see the whole yard from up there! Tim and Sam smiled and laughed. They had so much fun in the incredible tree. They promised to climb it again soon. And they did, many times, making happy memories together.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a key in his yard. It was a wet day, and the key was shiny. Tim showed the key to his friend, Sue. "Look what I found!" Tim said. Sue had an idea. "Let's bury the key and make a treasure map!" So, they dug a hole and put the key in it. They drew a map with a big X to mark the spot. They were very excited to find the treasure later. A few days passed, and the sun came out. Tim and Sue went back to the yard with their map. They dug and dug, but the key was not there. Just then, a little bird flew down with the key in its beak. The bird had taken the key when they buried it! Tim and Sue laughed and thanked the bird for the surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to sign her name with a big heart. She drew hearts everywhere because she wanted everyone to feel love. One day, Sue saw a big tree with no hearts on it. She thought, "I will put a heart on this tree so everyone knows it is available for love." Sue took her crayon and signed her name with a big heart on the tree. Just then, a little bird came and said, "Thank you, Sue! I was looking for a tree to build my nest in, but I couldn't find one that felt like home. Now that I see your heart, I know this tree is full of love and perfect for my family!" Sue smiled, happy that her heart could help the little bird find a home.
