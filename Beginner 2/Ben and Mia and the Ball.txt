Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Ball

Ben and Mia are playing in the park. They like to swing and slide and run. They see a big red ball near the fence. Ben wants to play with the ball. He runs to the ball and takes it. "Hey, that's not your ball!" Mia says. "That's someone else's ball. You should put it back." Ben shakes his head. "No, I like this ball. It's mine now. Come on, let's play with it." Mia is worried. She knows taking things is wrong. She follows Ben to the grass. They kick the ball and roll it and throw it. The ball makes a funny sound when it bounces. Ben and Mia laugh. But then they hear another sound. A loud and angry sound. A man is shouting at them. He is the owner of the ball. He is very mad. He runs to Ben and Mia and grabs the ball. "Give me back my ball, you little thieves!" he yells. "How dare you take my ball! I'm going to tell your parents!" Ben and Mia are scared. They start to cry. They wish they had not taken the ball. They wish they had listened to Mia. They are in big trouble. The man takes them to their parents and tells them what they did. Their parents are not happy. They scold Ben and Mia and make them say sorry. They also make them do chores and take away their toys. Ben and Mia are very sad. They have learned a hard lesson. Taking things is bad.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly little girl named Lily. She loved to go to the zoo with her mom and dad. One day, they went to see the big animals at the zoo. At the zoo, Lily saw a big lion. She wanted to say hi to the lion. Her mom said, "Lily, I must warn you, do not go close to the lion. It is not safe." Lily listened to her mom and stayed away from the lion. Later, Lily saw a nice zoo worker. The worker said, "Hi, Lily! Do you want to meet a friendly animal?" Lily said, "Yes, please!" The worker took her to see a small rabbit. Lily was happy to pet the rabbit. She learned that it is important to listen to warnings and be safe.

Once upon a time, there was a small pond in the middle of a big field. The pond was full of water and life. Every day, Farmer Jack would come to the pond and pour fresh clean water into it. He did this to make sure the pond stayed full and healthy. One day, the sun was shining very brightly and the pond began to dry up. Farmer Jack knew he had to act fast, so he went and grabbed his rake and started to rake the pond bottom. As he did this, the water poured from the rake into the pond and soon it started to look full and clean again. The moral of the story is that if you work hard and show kindness, then good things will happen. Farmer Jack showed kindness by pouring fresh water into the pond and working hard with his rake to make sure it was full and healthy again.

One day, a small, unusual bug named Bob went to the park. He saw a big hill and thought, "I want to zoom down that hill!" So, he climbed up the hill slowly, step by step. On top of the hill, Bob met a friendly bird named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Bob! What are you doing here?" Bob replied, "I want to zoom down this hill!" Sue thought it was a fun idea, so she said, "I'll race you to the bottom, but the loser has to pay a price!" Bob and Sue got ready to race. They counted, "One, two, three, go!" and started to zoom down the hill. Bob was fast, but Sue was faster. They both laughed and had fun as they raced down the hill. At the bottom of the hill, Sue won the race. Bob was a little sad, but he knew he had to pay the price. Sue said, "The price is a big hug!" Bob smiled and gave Sue a big hug. They both laughed and played in the park for the rest of the day. They had so much fun, and they knew they would always remember the day they raced down the hill.
