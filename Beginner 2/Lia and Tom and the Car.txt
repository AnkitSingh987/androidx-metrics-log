Category: Beginner
Title: Lia and Tom and the Car

Lia and Tom are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars. But today they have a problem. They both want the same red car. They pull and push and shout. "Mine!" Lia says. "No, mine!" Tom says. They are not happy. They are angry. They break the car. The wheels fall off. The car is not good anymore. They are sad. They look at the broken car. They look at each other. They are sorry. "Can we fix it?" Lia asks. "Maybe," Tom says. "Let's try." They work together. They find the wheels. They put them back on the car. They use tape and glue. They fix the car. They are happy. They smile. They hug. "Can we share it?" Tom asks. "Yes," Lia says. "We can take turns." They are friends again. They play with the car. They have fun. They are lucky. They have a good car. They have peace. They have each other.

Anna likes to sing. She sings in the morning, she sings in the afternoon, she sings at night. She sings to her toys, she sings to her mom, she sings to her dad. She sings happy songs, she sings sad songs, she sings silly songs. One day, Anna goes to a big place with many people. It is a school. She sees a big screen on the wall. The teacher says they will sing a song together. Anna feels scared. She does not know the song. She does not know the people. She wants to go home. But then, she sees a girl with a red dress. The girl smiles at her. She says her name is Lily. She says she likes to sing too. She says she will help her learn the song. She says they can be friends. Anna feels happy. She says thank you. She says her name is Anna. She says she likes to sing too. They sit next to each other. They look at the screen. They see the words of the song. They hear the music. They start to sing. They sing loud, they sing soft, they sing together. They have fun. They make the teacher happy. They make the other people happy. They make themselves happy. Anna is brave. She tries something new. She makes a friend. She sings a song. She likes the school. She likes the screen. She likes the song. She likes Lily. She likes to sing.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red button. It was a very reliable button. It could talk, and it had many friends. The button lived in a small, happy town where everyone liked to talk and play. One day, the button met a new friend. This friend was a little, green button. The red button and the green button talked and played together all day long. They had so much fun, and they became best friends. But one day, something unexpected happened. The green button was not a real button. It was a little frog who liked to talk and play too! The frog had been pretending to be a button all this time. The red button was surprised, but they still stayed friends and continued to talk and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl. She was so clumsy, but she was very happy too. One day, she was playing with her toys when suddenly an alarm glowed. It was getting so loud that it made her jump. The little girl was so scared, so she went to find her mom. When she saw her, she asked what the alarm was. Mom said, “It’s an alarm clock. It is to tell us when it’s time to wake up”. The little girl was surprised and asked, “Do I have an alarm clock too?” Mom smiled and said, “Yes, you do! You can have your very own alarm clock, right here in your room.” The little girl was so excited and jumped up and down. From then on, the alarm clock glowed every morning to wake her up. She was never late for school and was always so happy when the alarm glowed.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big, hairy dog. Tim liked the dog's sight. He wanted to be friends with the dog. Tim walked up to the dog and asked a question. "Can we be friends, dog?" he said. The dog looked at Tim and said, "Yes, we can be friends." Tim was very happy. He did not know that dogs could talk. They played together all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Tim and the dog had a lot of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog started to change. It was not a dog anymore. It was a man with a big, hairy coat. The man said, "Thank you, Tim, for being my friend. I am a magic man, and I was stuck as a dog. Your friendship helped me turn back into a man." Tim was surprised but happy to have a new friend.
