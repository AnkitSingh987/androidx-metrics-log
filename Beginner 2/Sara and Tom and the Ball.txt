Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom and the Ball

Sara and Tom were on a trip with their mom and dad. They stayed in a hotel with a big pool. Sara and Tom liked to swim and splash in the water. But one day, they had a fight. Sara wanted to play with the ball, and Tom wanted to play with the boat. They both grabbed the toy and pulled hard. "Let go of the ball!" Sara shouted. "No, you let go of the boat!" Tom yelled. They made a lot of noise and splashed water everywhere. Their mom and dad heard them and came to the pool. "Stop fighting, Sara and Tom!" their mom said. "You are making a mess and bothering the other people. This is a peaceful hotel, not a playground. You have to share and be nice." "But he won't let me have the ball!" Sara said. "But she won't let me have the boat!" Tom said. Their dad looked at them and said, "If you can't play together, then you have to quit. No more pool for today. Come on, let's go back to the room." Sara and Tom felt sad and angry. They let go of the toy and followed their mom and dad. They wished they had not fought. They wished they could swim and have fun. They learned that fighting was not a good way to play.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. He loved to play with his toy friends. His toy friends were a dog, a cat, and a bird. They all liked to play together in the big, clear park. One day, a big wind came and took the toys away. Tim was sad. He wanted his toy friends back. He walked and walked to find them. He looked up, down, and all around. Then, Tim saw his toy friends in a tree. He knew he needed help to get them down. So, he called his mom, dad, and big sister. They all came to unite and help Tim. They made a big, tall ladder and climbed up to get the toys. Tim was so happy to have his toy friends back. They all went home and played together again.

Once upon a time, there was a gray jeep. The jeep had big wheels and went very fast. The gray jeep lived in a small town with many friends. One day, the gray jeep's friend, a red car, got stuck in the mud. The red car needed help to get out. The gray jeep heard the red car and went to help. The gray jeep was very strong and could pull the red car out of the mud. The gray jeep pulled and pulled, and the red car was free. The red car was very happy and said "thank you" to the gray jeep. After that, the gray jeep and the red car were best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. He saw a big, powerful wave. Tim wanted to find a pretty shell to take home. He began to jog along the beach, looking for the best shell he could find. Soon, Tim saw a big crab holding a beautiful shell. He said, "Mr. Crab, can I have that shell, please?" The crab looked at Tim and said, "No, this is my shell. You cannot have it." Tim was sad, but he kept jogging and looking for another shell. As Tim jogged, he saw the powerful wave coming closer and closer. He tried to run away, but the wave was too fast. The wave caught Tim and took him into the water. Tim's mom saw what happened and tried to save him, but she was not strong enough. The powerful wave took Tim far away, and he never found a pretty shell to take home.

Lily and Ben were playing in the living room. They liked to build forts with the cushions and pretend they were in a castle. Lily was the queen and Ben was the knight. They had a lot of fun. But then, Max came in. Max was Lily's big brother and he was very mean. He saw the fort and he wanted to destroy it. He said, "This is my living room, not your castle. Move away or I will knock it down!" Lily and Ben were scared and angry. They did not want to lose their fort. They decided to run away from Max and hide behind the sofa. They hoped he would not find them. But Max was not foolish. He saw their feet sticking out. He grabbed a cushion and threw it at them. It hit Lily on the head and made her cry. Ben was very brave and he threw a cushion back at Max. It hit him on the nose and made him sneeze. Max was furious and he ran after them. He tried to catch them and pull their hair. But Lily and Ben were faster and smarter. They ran around the sofa and the table and the chairs. They made Max dizzy and tired. They also made a lot of noise. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was going on. She saw the mess and the fight and she was not happy. She said, "Stop it, right now! What are you doing? You are making a big mess and hurting each other. You should be nice and share the cushions, not fight over them. Go to your rooms and think about what you did!" Lily and Ben felt sorry and they said, "We are sorry, Mom. We were just playing. Max started it. He was mean to us and he ruined our fort." Mom said, "I know, but you should not throw things and hit back. You should tell me or Dad if Max is bothering you. He was wrong, but so were you. Now go to your rooms and calm down." Max felt ashamed and he said, "I am sorry, Mom. I was bored and I wanted to have some fun. I did not mean to hurt Lily and Ben. I was foolish." Mom said, "I know, but you should not be mean and destroy things. You should find something else to do or join them nicely. You are their big brother and you should protect them, not hurt them. Now go to your room and think about what you did." Mom cleaned up the mess and the cushions. She hoped they would all learn their lesson and get along better. She loved them all very much.
