Category: Beginner
Title: Brooke the Girl and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Brooke. She was three years old and always had a big smile on her face. One day, Brooke noticed that her daddy was sitting alone in the living room, looking very sad. He was holding a cup of coffee, and it seemed like he wanted to cry. Brooke was sad too and she wanted to help, so she walked up to him and said, “Daddy, what’s wrong?” He sighed, and said, "I'm having a bad day. Everything looks ugly, and I can't seem to stop feeling down." Brooke smiled and hugged Daddy, saying, "That's ok, Daddy. I'll make it better. I'll give you lots of hugs and kisses, and then I'll make you a cup of yummy coffee!" Daddy smiled, and even though he was still feeling a bit down, he felt better knowing that Brooke was there to help him.

One day, a band of four friends went to the park. They loved to play together. They had a toy car that could zoom very fast. They took turns to play with the car. They were having a lot of fun. Then, a little dog came to the park. The dog saw the car zoom and wanted to play too. The dog ran and took the car in its mouth. The friends were surprised and said, "Hey, give us our car back!" The dog looked guilty and gave the car back to the friends. They saw that the dog wanted to play too. So, they let the dog join their band. Now, they all played together and had even more fun. The dog would zoom with the car, and they would all laugh and cheer.

Once, there was a high cliff by the sea. On top of the cliff, there was a big tree. Many birds liked to be in the tree. The tree was very popular. One day, a small bird came to the cliff. The small bird wanted to be with the other birds in the tree. But the small bird was scared to fly high. The other birds saw the small bird and wanted to help. They told the small bird, "Do not be scared. We will be with you." So, the small bird flew up to the tree with the help of the other birds. Now, the small bird was happy to be in the popular tree on the cliff.

Once upon a time, there was a pink boat. It liked to sail on the blue water. One day, it met a little flea. The flea said, "I want to sail with you, pink boat." The boat said, "Okay, little flea. Jump on!" The flea and the pink boat sailed far away. They saw fish, birds, and big green trees. The flea was very happy. But the flea was also very small. The boat said, "Little flea, be careful! You are so small, you might fall off." One day, the wind blew very hard. The pink boat went very fast. The flea almost fell off! But the pink boat remembered what it said before. It told the flea to hold on tight. The flea held on to the boat with all its might. The wind stopped blowing, and the pink boat slowed down. The flea was safe. The boat and the flea were happy they could sail together. They knew they would always look out for each other. And they sailed happily ever after.

One sunny day, Tim and his little sister, Sue, went to play outside. They saw a big box with lots of colors. Sue was very impatient. She wanted to see what was inside the box. So, Tim and Sue went to the box to take a peek. Inside the box, they found many colorful balls. Tim said, "Look, Sue! We can play with these balls!" Sue was very happy. She picked up a red ball and threw it to Tim. They played catch with the colorful balls. After a while, their mom called them. "Tim, Sue, come inside! It's time for lunch!" Tim and Sue put the colorful balls back in the box. They went inside to eat. They had so much fun playing with the colors and couldn't wait to play again later.
