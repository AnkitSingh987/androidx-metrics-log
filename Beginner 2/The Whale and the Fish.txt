Category: Beginner
Title: The Whale and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big whale. The whale lived in the sea. The whale was very healthy. It liked to swim and play with the fish. One day, the whale found a big pile of food. The food was not good. It was spoiled. The whale did not know the food was bad. It ate the food because it was hungry. After the whale ate the spoiled food, it did not feel healthy anymore. It felt sick. The whale's friends, the fish, helped it find good food to eat. Soon, the whale felt healthy again. It learned not to eat spoiled food. The whale and the fish swam and played happily in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly bunny named Bob. Bob liked to hop all day long. One day, he hopped to a little cabin in the woods. In the cabin, there was a tiny mouse named Tim. Tim saw Bob hopping and wanted to hop too. So, they hopped together. They hopped up, they hopped down, and they hopped all around the cabin. Bob said to Tim, "I love to hop! Do you love to hop too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, hopping is so much fun!" They hopped and hopped until they were both very tired. Then, they went inside the cabin to rest. The elderly bunny and the tiny mouse became best friends. They lived happily in the cabin, hopping and playing together every day. And they always made sure to take a good nap after all their hopping fun.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to prepare a cake. She asked her mom, "Can I make a cake, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but we only have enough for a small, humble cake." Sue was happy and got ready to make the cake. Sue mixed all the things in a big bowl. She put the cake in the oven. She waited and waited. The cake smelled good. Sue's mom said, "It will be a nice surprise for your dad when he comes home." Finally, it was time to take the cake out of the oven. But when Sue opened the oven, the cake was not small and humble. It was big and tall! Sue and her mom were so surprised. They laughed and laughed. When dad came home, he was happy to see the big cake. They all enjoyed the cake together, and it was the best cake they ever had.

One day, a big cat named Tom saw an enormous bow. He wanted to wear it, but it was on top of a high shelf. Tom tried to jump, but he could not reach the bow. He felt sad. Tom's friend, a small dog named Max, saw Tom was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I want the big bow, but I can't get it." Max wanted to help his friend. Max had an idea. He told Tom to sneeze. Tom sneezed very loud. The big sneeze made the bow fall off the shelf! Tom was so happy. He put on the enormous bow and thanked Max. They played together all day.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys. One day, they find a big box in the living room. It has a lot of stickers inside. The stickers have different shapes and colors. Some are stars, some are hearts, some are animals. "Wow, look at all the stickers!" Tom says. "Let's choose some and stick them on our skin!" "Okay, that sounds fun!" Lily says. "But we have to be careful. We don't want to hurt our skin or make it dirty." They open the box and start to choose the stickers they like. Tom likes the stars and the animals. He sticks them on his arms and legs. Lily likes the hearts and the flowers. She sticks them on her face and hands. They look at each other and laugh. They think they look very funny and pretty. "Look at us, we are sticker people!" Tom says. "Yes, we are!" Lily says. "But what will Mom and Dad say when they see us?" They hear the door open. Mom and Dad come home from work. They see Tom and Lily with the stickers on their skin. "Oh, my!" Mom says. "What have you done?" "We played with the stickers!" Tom says. "We chose the ones we like and stuck them on our skin!" Mom and Dad look at each other. They try not to smile. They want to be serious. "That was not a good idea," Dad says. "The stickers are not for your skin. They are for your books and papers. They can hurt your skin or make it dirty. You have to take them off." Tom and Lily feel sad. They like the stickers. They don't want to take them off. "But we like the stickers!" Lily says. "They are pretty and fun!" "I know, but they are not good for your skin," Mom says. "You have to be careful with your skin. It is part of your body. It is important. You have to keep it clean and healthy." Tom and Lily understand. They love their Mom and Dad. They want to make them happy. They start to peel off the stickers from their skin. It hurts a little. They see some red marks on their skin. "Ouch, that hurts!" Tom says. "I'm sorry, honey," Mom says. "It will be better soon. Here, let me help you." Mom and Dad help Tom and Lily to take off the stickers. They hug them and kiss them. They tell them they love them. "We love you too," Tom and Lily say. They learn a lesson. They don't play with the stickers on their skin anymore. They play with them on their books and papers. They still have fun. They still choose the ones they like. They still look funny and pretty. But they also keep their skin clean and healthy. And they make their Mom and Dad happy.
