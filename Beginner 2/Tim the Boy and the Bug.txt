Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bug

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the park. One day, he saw a green bug. The bug was weak. Tim wanted to help the bug. Tim picked up the bug and took it home. He asked his mom, "What should I do?" His mom did not know, so she just shrugged. Tim thought hard. He gave the bug some food and water. The next day, the green bug was strong again! Tim was happy he helped the bug. The bug flew away, but it came back with a friend. Tim learned that if you help others, they will help you too.

Lily liked to look at the sky. She saw many things in the sky. She saw birds and clouds and stars. Sometimes she saw planes. Planes were big and fast and noisy. Lily wanted to fly in a plane one day. One day, Lily and her mom went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. They had a picnic on the grass. Lily saw a plane in the sky. It was very far away. It looked like a tiny dot. Lily pointed at it and said, "Look, mom, a plane!" Mom looked at the sky and smiled. She said, "Yes, that's a plane. Do you know what kind of plane it is?" Lily shook her head. She did not know. Mom said, "It's a jet. A jet is a very fast and powerful plane. It can fly very high and make a loud sound. Do you want to hear it?" Lily nodded. She wanted to hear the jet. She waited and watched the sky. The plane got bigger and bigger. It came closer and closer. Lily saw it was shiny and had wings and a tail. She saw a fire coming out of the back. She heard a loud sound. It was the jet. It was very noisy. It made Lily's ears hurt. She covered her ears with her hands. She said, "Wow, mom, that's a jet!" Mom hugged Lily and said, "Yes, that's a jet. It's amazing, isn't it? Maybe one day you can fly in a jet. But for now, let's enjoy the park. Do you want to play some more?" Lily nodded. She still liked to look at the sky. But she liked to play with mom more. She said, "Yes, mom, let's play!" They played and laughed and had fun. Lily was happy. She liked the park. She liked the jet. She liked mom.

Once upon a time, there lived a flea. He was very happy, but he was always shivering with cold. He wanted to find a way to be warm. He thought of a creative plan. He made a winter coat with his soft fur. He worked very hard every day to make it. Every morning, he trembled with fear as he continued to work on his coat. But he was determined to finish it. Finally, his coat was finished and he wore it with pride. He never felt such warmth and comfort before. He was happy and he smiled. The flea was no longer cold and he was thankful for his creative plan.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play and learn together. They go to school and do work with their teacher. They learn to read, write and count. They also learn to be healthy. They eat good food, drink water and run in the park. One day, Tom and Lily see a big truck on the road. The truck has many candies and toys. Tom and Lily want to have some. They run to the truck and ask the driver. "Can we have some candies and toys, please?" they say. The driver is a bad man. He smiles and says, "Sure, you can have some. But you have to come with me in the truck. I will take you to a place where you can have more." Tom and Lily are happy. They think the driver is nice. They do not listen to their teacher, who tells them to stay away from strangers. They do not think about their parents, who will be worried. They do not remember to be healthy, and eat too many candies. They go with the driver in the truck. The driver is not nice. He does not take them to a place where they can have more. He takes them to a place where they are locked up. He makes them do hard work and gives them no food or water. He sells their toys and candies to other bad people. Tom and Lily are sad and scared. They wish they did not go with the driver. They wish they listened to their teacher. They wish they remembered to be healthy. They wish they could go back to their parents and their school. But they cannot. They are stuck in the bad place. They do not play and learn anymore. They do not smile anymore. They cry everyday. The end.
