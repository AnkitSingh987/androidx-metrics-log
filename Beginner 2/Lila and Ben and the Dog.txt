Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Dog

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were animals. Lila was a lion and Ben was a rhino. They ran and roared and chased each other. But then they saw a big dog. The dog was barking and running towards them. Lila and Ben were scared. They did not want to play with the dog. Lila said, "Let's hide behind the tree." Ben said, "No, let's climb on the slide." They did not agree. They ran in different ways. The dog followed Ben. Ben tried to use his horn to scare the dog. But the dog was not afraid. The dog jumped and bit Ben's horn. Ben cried out. He did not like the dog. Lila heard Ben. She ran to help him. She roared at the dog. The dog let go of Ben's horn. The dog looked at Lila. Lila looked at the dog. They stared at each other. Then the dog wagged his tail. He licked Lila's face. He wanted to be friends. Lila was surprised. She licked the dog back. She wanted to be friends too. Ben saw Lila and the dog. He was happy. He joined them. He licked the dog too. The dog licked Ben. They all became friends. They played together. They were lively. They had fun. They forgot to pretend they were animals. They were just Lila, Ben and the dog. They did not surrender. They found a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big machine that was broken. A man named Tom was eager to fix it. Tom was very good at fixing things. He had a big box of tools to help him. One sunny day, Tom went to the machine with his tools. He was very happy to start fixing it. He looked at the machine and started to work. He used his tools to take out the broken parts and put in new parts. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the machine. Tom did not see the bird. He kept fixing the machine. When he finished, the machine started to work again. But the little bird was still inside. The bird got hurt by the machine. Tom was very sad about the bad ending.

Ben and Lily liked to play with their drums. They made loud noises and had fun. But Mom and Dad did not like the loud noises. They said it was time to settle down and be quiet. Ben and Lily did not want to settle down. They wanted to keep playing. Mom and Dad had an important meeting on the phone. They needed to talk to some people who could help them with their work. They told Ben and Lily to go to their room and play with something else. They gave them some books and puzzles and crayons. Ben and Lily took them and went to their room. But they did not want to play with them. They wanted to play with their drums. They waited until Mom and Dad were on the phone. Then they took their drums and sneaked out of their room. They went to the living room and started to bang on their drums. They made a big rhythm with their drums. They smiled and laughed. They did not hear Mom and Dad shouting at them to stop. But they did hear a loud crash. They turned around and saw that their drums had knocked over a vase. The vase was broken and there were flowers and water everywhere. Mom and Dad came running and saw the mess. They were very angry and sad. The vase was very important to them. It was a gift from Grandma and Grandpa. They loved the vase and the flowers. Ben and Lily felt bad. They did not mean to break the vase. They said they were sorry and hugged Mom and Dad. Mom and Dad hugged them back and said they loved them. But they also said they had to learn to listen and settle down. They said they had to be careful with their things and respect other people's things. They said they had to clean up the mess and put away their drums. Ben and Lily nodded and said they understood. They helped Mom and Dad clean up the mess and put away their drums. They said they would play with their books and puzzles and crayons instead. They said they would be quiet and let Mom and Dad finish their meeting. They said they would be good. Mom and Dad smiled and thanked them. They said they were proud of them. They said they would play with their drums later, when it was not so important to be quiet. They said they liked their rhythm, but they also liked their peace.
