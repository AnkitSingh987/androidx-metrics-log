Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Messy Sheep and the Wool

Once upon a time, there was a messy sheep named Tim. Tim had lots of wool, but he did not behave well. He liked to play in the mud and make his wool dirty. One day, Tim met a nice sheep named Lily. Lily said, "Tim, you should behave better. Clean your wool and be a good sheep." Tim looked at his dirty wool and felt sad. He knew Lily was right. Tim decided to be a better sheep. He washed his wool and it became clean and fluffy. Tim was proud of his clean wool. From that day on, Tim behaved well and played nicely with Lily and the other sheep.

---

Tom and Lily were camping with their dad in the woods. They liked to sleep in a big tent and roast marshmallows on the fire. They were not afraid of the dark, because dad told them stories and sang songs. One night, they heard a loud noise outside the tent. It sounded like a growl and a snort. They were very scared. They asked dad what it was. Dad said it was a bear, and they had to be very quiet and still. He said the bear would go away when the sunrise came. But the bear did not go away. It smelled the marshmallows and the food in the tent. It ripped the tent with its claws and teeth. It saw Tom and Lily and their dad. It was hungry and angry. It attacked them. Tom and Lily screamed and cried. Dad tried to fight the bear, but the bear was too strong and big. It bit dad and hurt him badly. It dragged him away. Tom and Lily were alone and terrified. They wished the sunrise would come, but it was still dark. The bear came back for Tom and Lily. It grabbed them and carried them to its den. It ate them. They were disgusting. The bear was happy. The sunrise came, but no one saw it. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his friend, Tim. Tim was a very mild boy who always played nice with Max. They loved to play fetch in the yard. One day, Max heard an alarm. It was loud and scary. Max did not recognize the sound, so he ran to find Tim. Tim knew what the sound was and told Max not to worry. It was just a car alarm from outside. Max felt better and went back to playing with Tim. They played all day until it was time to go to bed. Max and Tim were happy to be safe and sound in their little house. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a man came to the farm. He had a big truck with many boxes. He said he wanted to fill the boxes with apples from the trees. The farmer said yes, but he did not know the man. He thought the man was unknown and strange. The farmer's son, Tom, liked to play with the apples. He liked to roll them, toss them, and bite them. He saw the man and the truck. He wanted to see the boxes. He ran to the truck and climbed inside. He saw many boxes, but they were not empty. They had toys, books, and clothes inside. Tom was happy. He wanted to play with the things. The man saw Tom in the truck. He was angry. He shouted at Tom. He said, "Get out of my truck! These are not your things! These are for the children who have nothing!" He grabbed Tom and pushed him out of the truck. He slammed the door and drove away. Tom was sad and scared. He ran to his father and told him what happened. The farmer was shocked and sorry. He hugged Tom and said, "I'm sorry, son. I did not know the man was not honest. He did not fill the boxes with apples. He took them from someone else. He was a bad man. You did nothing wrong. You were just curious. I love you, son." Tom felt better. He said, "I love you, too, dad. Can we still play with the apples?" The farmer smiled and said, "Of course, son. Let's go and pick some apples. They are sweet and juicy. And they are ours." They went to the orchard and filled a basket with apples. They ate some and shared some with the animals. They had a good day. They forgot about the unknown man and his truck.
