Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Toys of Tim and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was upset because he had to pack his toys away. He loved playing with his toys all day, but his mom said it was time to clean up. Tim's mom said, "Tim, please pack your toys and put them on the stair." Tim did not want to pack his toys, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. So, he picked up his toys one by one and put them in a box. As Tim put the box on the stair, he saw his friend, the cat. The cat said, "Don't be upset, Tim. You can play with your toys again tomorrow." Tim smiled and felt better. He knew the cat was right, and he could have more fun with his toys the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a big gray dog. The dog could not hear. He was deaf. The dog liked to play in the mud. He liked to get dirty. One day, the dog played in the mud. He got very dirty. His mom saw him. She said, "You need to bathe." The dog did not hear her. He was deaf. The mom got a big tub of water. She put the dog in the water. She helped him bathe. The dog got clean. He was happy. The mom was happy too. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very excited because today was a big day. She had a test at school, and she felt ready. Lily had practiced a lot with her mom and dad, and they were all so happy. At school, Lily's teacher gave her the test. Lily looked at the test and started to weep. Some of the words were hard, and she didn't know them. Her friend, Tom, saw her weep and wanted to help. He whispered, "It's okay, Lily. Just try your best." Lily wiped her tears and remembered what her mom and dad said, "You are ready, and you can do it!" She took a deep breath and tried her best. When the test was done, Lily felt good. She didn't know all the words, but she tried her best. And that is what made her mom, dad, and Tom very proud.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. He did not like to play or spend time with others. He just wanted to sleep all day. Tom lived in a small house with a big yard. One sunny day, Tom was sleeping when he heard a loud noise outside. He got up and looked out the window. He saw a big, colorful bird in his yard. The bird was singing and dancing. Tom was angry because of the noise and decided to go outside to make the bird go away. As Tom walked towards the bird, he saw the bird was not alone. There were many little kittens playing and having fun with the bird. The kittens were laughing and making the noise. Tom was surprised and felt happy to see the kittens. He decided to join them and started to play with them too. From that day on, Tom was not grumpy anymore and loved to spend time with his new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a new dress that she loved very much. It was red and had pretty flowers on it. She wore it all the time because it made her happy. One day, Lily was playing outside when it started to rain. She didn't want her new dress to get wet. So, she found a big leaf and used it to cover her dress. She held it over her head and ran home. When Lily got home, her mom saw the big leaf and asked her what she was doing. Lily told her mom she was using the leaf to cover her new dress so it wouldn't get wet. Her mom smiled and said, "That's very smart, Lily! You took care of your things, and now your dress is still nice and clean." Lily felt proud of herself and learned that taking care of her things was important.
