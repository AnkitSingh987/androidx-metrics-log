Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Garden

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the garden. One day, they find a big bush with many blueberries. They are happy and hungry. "Look, Ben, blueberries!" Lily says. "We can have some for a snack." "Yes, let's have some. They are sweet and juicy," Ben says. They pick some blueberries and eat them. They are very good. But then, they hear a voice. It is Lily's mom. She is calling them for lunch. "Lily, Ben, come inside. It is time for lunch," she says. "Oh no, we have to go," Lily says. "But we still have many blueberries. What can we do?" "I have an idea," Ben says. "We can hide them. Then we can have them later." "Good idea. Where can we hide them?" Lily says. Ben looks around. He sees a big pot with a lid. It is behind the bush. "Here, we can hide them in the pot. No one will see them," he says. He opens the lid and puts the blueberries inside. He closes the lid. "OK, let's go. We can come back later," he says. They run to the house. They have lunch with Lily's mom. They do not tell her about the hidden blueberries. They are a secret. They smile at each other. They are happy. They have a plan.

Once upon a time, in a small blue house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a pet cat named Max. Max was a blue cat, and he loved to play with Tim. One day, Tim found a piece of string on the floor. Tim said to Max, "Look, Max! I have a piece of string. Let's play!" Tim started to raise the string up and down. Max jumped and tried to catch the string. They laughed and played for a long time. After they played, Tim and Max were very tired. They sat down on the floor and shared a big hug. Tim said, "Max, you are my best friend. I love playing with you and your blue fur." Max purred and snuggled close to Tim. They were very happy and loved their time together.

One day, a small cat named Kitty was playing near a big tree. She saw a helpless bird with a hurt arm on the ground. Kitty wanted to help the bird. She ran to her friend, Doggy, and said, "Hurry, come help the bird!" Doggy and Kitty went to the bird. They saw that the bird's arm was hurt. Doggy said, "We need to help the bird. Let's be gentle and kind." They carefully picked up the bird and took it to a safe place. Kitty and Doggy took care of the bird until its arm was better. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you for helping me. You are good friends." Kitty and Doggy learned that it is important to help others when they are in need. They felt proud of themselves for being kind and helpful.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite pair of pants. They were red and very soft. He loved to wear them every day. One day, when Tim was playing outside, a sneaky fox came by. The fox saw Tim's red pants and wanted them for himself. So, when Tim wasn't looking, the fox stole the pants and ran away. Tim was sad when he saw his pants were gone. He looked everywhere for them. Finally, he saw the fox wearing his red pants in the distant woods. Tim went to the fox and asked for his pants back. The fox felt bad and gave the pants back to Tim. They became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a gray bunny named Bob. Bob liked to pack things in his yellow bag. One day, he wanted to go on an adventure. Bob met his friend, a little bird named Tim. "Let's go on an adventure!" said Bob. "Okay!" said Tim. They walked and walked until they found a big, dark cave. Bob and Tim went inside the cave. It was very dark and scary. They heard a loud growl. "What was that?" asked Tim. "I don't know," said Bob. They were very scared. A big, mean bear came out of the dark. "Go away!" said the bear. Bob and Tim tried to run, but the bear caught them. The bear took Bob's yellow bag and all the things he packed. Bob and Tim were very sad. They went back home without the yellow bag and the things they packed. The adventure had a bad ending, and they learned to be more careful next time.
