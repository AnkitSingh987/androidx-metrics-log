Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pears

Once upon a time, in a charming little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat pears. He ate pears every day. One day, he found a magic pear tree in his yard. Tim decided to increase the number of pears on the tree. He watered the tree and sang to it every day. The tree grew big and strong. More and more pears grew on the tree. Tim shared the pears with his friends. They all loved the sweet pears from the charming tree. Tim was happy that he could share his pears with everyone. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a generous man named Tom. Tom loved to help others. He would give food, clothes, and toys to people who needed them. Everyone in the town liked Tom because he was so kind. One day, a little girl named Sally came to Tom's house. She was sad because her cat was stuck in a tree. "Please help me, Tom," she cried. Tom wanted to help Sally, so he went to the tree and tried to climb it. He started to struggle because the tree was very big and tall. Tom did not give up. He tried again and again until he finally reached the top of the tree. He saved Sally's cat and brought it back to her. "Thank you, Tom!" said Sally with a big smile. Tom was happy he could help. From that day on, Sally and Tom became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play outside under a big tree. The tree had a lot of shade, so Sue could play without getting too hot. She loved to change her toys and play different games under the tree. One day, Sue's mom bought her a cheap, new toy. It was a small, red ball. Sue was very happy and excited to play with her new ball. She took it outside to play under her favorite tree. She threw the ball up and down and rolled it around on the ground. As Sue played with her ball, she noticed the shade under the tree was changing. The sun was moving, and the shade was getting smaller. Sue moved her toys to stay in the shade as it changed. She played with her cheap, red ball all day long, enjoying the changing shade under her tree.

One day, a big lion was walking in the forest. He saw a high tree with lots of leaves. The lion wanted to climb the tree to see what was up there. "Hello, tree," said the lion. "Can I climb you?" The tree did not say anything. So, the lion started to climb. He went up, up, up, until he was very high. At the top, the lion found a big bird. The bird was not happy to see the lion. It wanted the lion to go away. So, the bird gave the lion a big slap with its wing. The lion was very surprised. "Ouch!" said the lion. "Why did you slap me?" The bird said, "This is my tree. You cannot be here." The lion was sad, but he understood. He climbed down the tree and went back to his friends in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived near a big oak tree. The oak tree had many fine branches. Tim loved to play near the oak tree every day. One day, Tim saw a big rock. He wanted to lift the rock. Tim tried very hard to lift the rock, but it was too heavy. Tim's friend, Sam, came to help. "Let's lift the rock together," said Sam. Tim and Sam tried to lift the rock, but it was still too heavy. Tim and Sam were very sad. They could not lift the rock. They sat under the oak tree and cried. The rock stayed there, and they could not play near the oak tree anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim's mom asked him to go to the store to buy some food. "Remember to take the key," she said. So, Tim took the key and went to the store. On the way, Tim met a nice dog. The dog was very friendly and wanted to play. Tim was so happy to play with the dog, but he dropped the key by accident. The dog saw the key and picked it up with his mouth. "Thank you, dog," said Tim. Tim and the dog went to the store together. They bought the food and went back home. Tim's mom was very proud of him for doing a good job. She was also happy to see the dog. She said, "We can accept the dog to be our new friend." Tim, his mom, and dad lived happily with their new friend, the dog.
