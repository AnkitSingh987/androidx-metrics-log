Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Noisy Bird and the Tree

One day, a noisy bird named Tim was flying in the sky. Tim saw his friend, Sam the squirrel, on a tree. Sam was trying to spread dust on the leaves. Tim was curious and asked, "Sam, why are you spreading dust on leaves?" Sam said, "I want to make the tree dirty, so no one will come here. Then I can have all the nuts for myself." Tim thought it was a bad idea, but he didn't say anything. As they kept spreading the dust, more and more animals left the tree. Soon, the tree was very dirty and no one wanted to be there. Sam was happy because he had all the nuts. But the tree became sick from all the dust, and it died. All the nuts were gone, and Sam was sad. Tim learned that being selfish can lead to bad things.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was an old lady. She loved to cook. One day, she wanted to make a new soup. She went to her garden to find something new. In the garden, she saw a green plant. "What is this?" she asked her cat. The cat said, "I don't know." The old lady pulled the green plant from the ground. She discovered it was celery! The old lady was happy. She took the celery to her kitchen. She made a tasty soup with it. She shared the soup with her cat. They both loved the celery soup. From that day on, they always had celery in their soup.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He did not like to eat vegetables. Every day, his mom would put vegetables on his plate, but Tim would just scream and not eat them. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big tree with a dead branch. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but his mom said it was not safe. Tim did not listen and climbed the tree anyway. As Tim climbed higher, the dead branch broke. Tim fell down and hit the ground. He was hurt and cried a lot. His mom came and took him home. From that day on, Tim learned to listen to his mom and started eating his vegetables. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and eat your vegetables to be strong and safe.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they see a small car on the floor. It is red and shiny. Lily wants the car. She runs to it and picks it up. "Look, Ben, I have a car!" she says. Ben is sad. He likes the car too. He thinks it is his car. He left it on the floor before. He walks to Lily and tries to take the car. "Give me my car, Lily!" he says. "No, it is my car!" Lily says. She holds the car tight. They pull the car. They are angry. They shout. They do not share. Then, a big dog comes. It sees the car. It thinks it is a bone. It is hungry. It sneaks to Lily and Ben. It steals the car from them. It runs away. Lily and Ben are shocked. They look at the dog. They look at each other. They have no car. They are sad. They cry. They hug. They say sorry. They go to find another toy. They play together. They share. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a thick tree. A little boy named Tim lived near the tree. One day, Tim went for a walk in the forest. He saw a pretty sight. A big, bright, and round rainbow was in the sky. Tim was very happy to see it. Tim wanted to show the rainbow to his mom. But, he did not want the rainbow to go away. So, Tim had a fun idea. He took a long, thin rope from his pocket. He tried to tie the rainbow to the thick tree. He threw the rope up, but the rainbow was too high. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He tried again and again. At last, the rope tied around the rainbow. Tim ran back home to get his mom. When they came back, the rainbow was still there. Tim and his mom looked at the pretty sight together. They were both very happy.
