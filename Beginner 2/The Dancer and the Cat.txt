Category: Beginner
Title: The Dancer and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dancer. She was a very good dancer, but she was deaf. She could not hear the music, but she could feel it. She danced with her heart. One day, while she was dancing in the park, she saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and needed help. The dancer wanted to rescue the cat. She climbed the tree and reached out to the cat. The cat was happy to see the dancer. It jumped into her arms, and she held it tight. She climbed down the tree with the cat. The cat was safe now, thanks to the deaf dancer. They became best friends, and they danced together in the park every day.

Lily and Ben were excited. Today was Waffle Day at school. They loved waffles. They were sweet and soft and yummy. They put on their hats and coats and got ready to go. "Come on, Lily, let's march to school!" Ben said. He liked to march. It made him feel strong and happy. "Okay, Ben, let's march!" Lily said. She liked to march too. It made her feel brave and fun. They marched out of the door and down the street. They sang a song as they marched. "We love waffles, yes we do. We love waffles, how about you?" They saw their friends on the way. They waved and smiled and joined them. They all marched together to school. They were a big waffle army. When they got to school, they smelled the waffles. They were hot and fresh and ready. They lined up and waited for their turn. They said thank you to the teacher who gave them waffles. They put syrup and butter and fruit on their waffles. They sat down and ate their waffles. They were delicious. They shared their waffles with each other. They talked and laughed and enjoyed. They were happy and full and grateful. They loved Waffle Day.

One sunny day, Tom and his cat, Fluffy, were playing in their house. They liked to play chase and hide. Tom would run and Fluffy would follow. Tom felt dizzy from running around so much. Tom said, "Let's play upstairs, Fluffy!" So, they ran upstairs to Tom's room. In the room, there were many toys and a big bed. Tom and Fluffy jumped on the bed, and they laughed as they fell on the soft pillows. After playing on the bed, Tom hugged Fluffy tight. "I love you, Fluffy," Tom said. Fluffy purred and hugged Tom back with her soft paws. They were very happy and tired from playing. Tom and Fluffy took a nap together on the big bed, dreaming of their next playtime.

One morning, Timmy saw something interesting on the news. He thought it looked so fun that he wanted to get involved. But when he tried squeezing his way out of the door to go find it, he found it wouldn’t work. He was stuck! Timmy’s mom saw this and knew it was a foolish idea. She came over to him and said, ‘No Timmy, you can’t do that. What you saw on the news was for grown-ups, not for little kids like you.’ Timmy thought about this and realised it did seem a little silly. He thanked his mom and decided to stay inside and find something else to do. He grabbed some toys and had a lot of fun playing instead.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw his friend, Sam, by the slide. Tim said, "Sam, let's hurry and play on the slide!" Sam was happy and they both ran to the slide. As they played, they saw a dead bug on the ground. Tim looked at Sam and said, "Look, the bug is dead." Sam nodded and they both felt sad for the bug. They decided to give it a tiny funeral and said goodbye to the bug. After that, they went back to play. They played on the swings, the slide, and even in the sand. They had a fun day at the park. Before they left, Tim looked at Sam and said, "We should play here again soon!" Sam agreed, and they both went home with big smiles on their faces.
