Category: Beginner
Title: Buggy the Bug and the Valley

Once upon a time, in a small valley, there lived a tiny bug named Buggy. Buggy loved to crawl all day long. One day, while Buggy was crawling, he met a big, rude bird named Birdy. "Hey, you! You crawl too slow!" said Birdy to Buggy. Buggy felt sad but did not say anything. He knew that one day, his crawling would help him. One sunny day, Birdy got stuck in a small hole. He tried to get out, but he was too big. Birdy saw Buggy crawling by and called out, "Help me, Buggy! Please get me out of this hole!" Buggy wanted to help Birdy. He used his small size and crawled into the hole. Buggy pushed Birdy out of the hole, and they both got out safe. Birdy said, "Thank you, Buggy! I am sorry for being rude to you. You are a good friend." From that day on, Birdy and Buggy became best friends. They played and had fun together in the valley. Birdy learned that being rude was not nice, and Buggy's crawling had saved the day.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play dress up. Lily has a pink dress and a bow in her hair. Tom has a blue shirt and a hat. They pretend to be a princess and a prince. One day, they find a card on the floor. It has a picture of a cake and some words. Lily tries to read the words, but they are hard. She asks Tom for help. "Tom, what does this card say?" she says. Tom looks at the card. He knows some letters, but not all. He tries to sound out the words. "It says... Happy... Birth... Day... to... you," he says slowly. "Wow, it is a birthday card!" Lily says. "But who is it for?" They look around, but they do not see anyone. They see a door with a sign. The sign says "Party Room". "Maybe it is for someone in the party room," Tom says. "Let's go and see," Lily says. They hold the card and walk to the door. They open the door and see a lot of people. They see balloons, streamers, and a big cake. They hear music and laughter. They see a boy sitting on a chair. He has a crown on his head. He looks happy. He is the birthday boy. Lily and Tom walk to the boy. They smile and say "Happy Birthday!" The boy looks at them. He does not know them. He is confused. "Who are you?" he says. "We are the princess and the prince," Lily says. "We have a card for you." She gives him the card. He takes it and says "Thank you." He opens the card and sees the picture of the cake. He likes it. He says "This is a nice card." Lily and Tom feel happy. They made the boy happy. They see the boy's mom. She is wearing a tight dress and a necklace. She looks pretty. She sees Lily and Tom. She smiles and says "Hello, who are you?" "We are the princess and the prince," Tom says. "We came to the party." The mom says "That is very sweet of you. Do you want some cake?" Lily and Tom nod. They love cake. The mom says "Come on, let's join the party." She takes them by the hand and leads them to the table. They see plates, forks, and napkins. They see candles on the cake. The mom says "Let's sing Happy Birthday to the birthday boy." Everyone sings Happy Birthday. The boy blows out the candles. He makes a wish. He cuts the cake and gives a slice to Lily and Tom. They say "Thank you." They eat the cake. It is yummy. They drink some juice. It is cold. They have fun at the party. They play games, dance, and make new friends. They are happy. They say "Goodbye" to the boy and his mom. They say "Thank you for the party." The boy and his mom say "You are welcome. Thank you for the card." Lily and Tom bow and leave. They hold hands and walk home. They are still friends. They still like to play dress up. They still pretend to be a princess and a prince.
