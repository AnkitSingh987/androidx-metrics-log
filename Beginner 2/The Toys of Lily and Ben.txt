Category: Beginner
Title: The Toys of Lily and Ben

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They had a big box of toys that they shared. They had cars, dolls, balls, and books. One day, they decided to play with the cars. They made a road with sticks and stones. They drove their cars along the road and made noises. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep! But then, a big dog came running. The dog was not mean, but he was very curious. He wanted to see what the children were doing. He ran towards them and wagged his tail. Lily and Ben were scared. They dropped their cars and ran away. The dog chased them and jumped on the box of toys. He scattered the toys all over the park. He picked up some toys with his mouth and ran away. Lily and Ben hid behind a tree. They were sad and angry. They had lost their toys. They cried and hugged each other. "Go away, dog! You are bad! You took our toys!" Lily shouted. "Please, dog, give us back our toys! We want to play!" Ben pleaded. But the dog did not listen. He was having fun with the toys. He chewed them and tossed them and buried them. Lily and Ben did not know what to do. They wished they had a big stick to scare the dog away. They wished they had their toys back. They wished they had a dream come true. Then, they heard a whistle. It was the dog's owner. He was looking for his dog. He saw the dog playing with the toys. He saw the children crying behind the tree. He called the dog and put a leash on him. He apologized to Lily and Ben. He said he was sorry for the trouble. He said he would help them find their toys. He walked around the park with the dog and the children. He asked the dog to show him where he buried the toys. He dug up the toys and gave them back to Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben were happy. They thanked the man and the dog. They said they were sorry for being angry. They said they were glad they found their toys. They put their toys back in the box. They hugged the man and the dog. They said goodbye and went home. Lily and Ben felt lucky. They had their toys back. They had a new friend. They had a happy ending. They smiled and laughed. They had a good dream that night.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends. They were called Jack and Sam. One day, Jack wanted to show Sam something special. "I want to mention something," he said. Sam was very curious and waited to hear what Jack wanted to say. "Do you want to see a map?" he asked. Sam nodded excitedly then followed Jack to his room. When they got there, Jack took out an old map from his drawer and showed it to Sam. It was a colourful map with lots of pictures on it. As Jack spread it out on the floor, he became careless and the map started to tear. Sam gasped and said, "Be careful, Jack! You don't want to ruin the map!" Jack quickly put the pieces back together and apologised to his friend. From then on, they never took the special map for granted.

Sara and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to search for bugs and flowers. They had a jar and a pick to put them in. "Look, Sara, I found a big spider!" Tom said. He used the pick to catch the spider and put it in the jar. "Wow, Tom, that's a cool spider!" Sara said. She searched for something else. She saw a pretty flower. It was yellow and red. She used the pick to cut the flower and put it in the jar. But Sara was careless. She did not see the bee on the flower. The bee was angry. It flew out of the jar and stung Sara on the nose. "Ow, ow, ow!" Sara cried. She dropped the jar and the pick. The spider and the flower fell out. "Sorry, Sara, sorry!" Tom said. He ran to get their mom. He felt bad for Sara. Mom came and hugged Sara. She put some ice on her nose. She told Sara and Tom to be careful when they search for bugs and flowers. She said they should not use the pick. She said they should use their eyes and hands. Sara and Tom said sorry to mom. They said they learned their lesson. They said they would not be careless again. They hugged mom and each other. They went inside to have some juice and cookies.
