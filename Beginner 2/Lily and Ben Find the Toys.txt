Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Toys

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys in Lily's room. One day, they have many toys on the floor. Lily's mom says, "It is time to zip up your toys in the bag. Then we can have a snack." Lily says, "OK, mom. I will zip up my toys." She starts to pick up her toys and put them in the bag. She zips the bag and puts it in the corner. Ben says, "I don't want to zip up my toys. I want to play more." He leaves his toys on the floor and runs to the kitchen. Lily's mom says, "Ben, you have to zip up your toys too. It is not nice to leave a mess in Lily's room. She was tidy and you were not." Ben says, "But I don't like to zip up my toys. It is boring." Lily's mom says, "Zipping up your toys is not boring. It is fun and easy. You can make a game of it. You can count how many toys you have, or you can say the name of each toy as you put it in the bag. You can also sing a song while you zip up your toys. Zipping up your toys is a good way to show respect and gratitude to your friend and to your toys." Ben thinks for a moment. He says, "You are right, Lily's mom. I will zip up my toys. I am sorry for being not tidy. I want to be a good friend to Lily and to my toys." He goes back to Lily's room and picks up his toys. He puts them in the bag and zips it. He says the name of each toy and sings a song. He has fun zipping up his toys. Lily and Ben hug each other. They say, "We are both tidy and we are both happy. We love our toys and we love each other." They go to the kitchen and have a snack with Lily's mom. They smile and say, "Thank you, mom, for the snack and for the lesson." The end.

Once upon a time, there was a race. A little girl named Amy was in the race. She was very fast. She wanted to win the race. All her friends were there to watch her. Amy had a diary. She wrote about the race in her diary. She wrote about how she wanted to win. She also wrote about a fake medal. It was a big secret. She wanted to show her friends the fake medal if she didn't win the real one. The day of the race came. Amy ran very fast. But something unexpected happened. Amy tripped and fell. Her friends saw her fall. They ran to help her. They picked her up and hugged her. They didn't care about the race or the fake medal. They just wanted Amy to be okay. And that made Amy very happy.

One day, a little girl named Emma went to the farm with her mom. They saw many animals there. Emma liked the big, pretty horse. The horse had a shiny coat and a long tail. Emma thought the horse was attractive. Emma asked her mom, "Can I give the horse some food?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can give the horse some oats." They took a handful of oats and lay them on the ground near the horse. The horse came to eat the oats. It was very happy. While the horse ate the oats, a small cat came by. The cat was hungry too. Emma said, "Let's give the cat some oats too!" They lay more oats on the ground for the cat. The cat and the horse ate the oats together. Emma and her mom smiled and went home. They had a fun day at the farm.

Walter was a tank. He was out for a drive in the open countryside with his friend Gail. Suddenly, Walter stopped and said, "I feel like I've been squeezed". Gail smiled and said, "It's OK, Walter. It's just a hug from the sunshine." Walter smiled too and said, "I like that! But I need to stay open so I won't get too squeezed". Gail laughed and said, "That's right, just keep yourself open and you won't be too squeezed". Walter and Gail kept driving, but every once in a while Walter would take a deep breath and say "I can still feel the squeeze". Finally, Gail said, "Walter, time to go home!" Walter stopped, opened wide, and let the sunshine squeeze him one last time.
