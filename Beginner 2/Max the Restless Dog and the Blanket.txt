Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Restless Dog and the Blanket

One day, a restless dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a vendor selling treats. The vendor was a nice man with a big smile. Max wagged his tail and looked at the treats. "Hello, little dog! Are you cold?" the vendor asked. Max shivered in the wind. The vendor gave Max a warm blanket. Max felt better and stopped shivering. Max barked, "Thank you!" The vendor smiled and gave Max a treat. Max ate the treat and felt happy. He went home to tell his friends about the kind vendor.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy hockey set. He loved hockey very much. He hit the small ball with his hockey stick and it went very high in the sky. Tim started to wonder where the ball would land. He looked up and saw a big tree. The ball got stuck in the tree! Tim was sad because he could not reach the high ball. Then, a tall man came by and saw Tim looking sad. He asked, "What's wrong, little boy?" Tim told him about his hockey ball stuck in the tree. The tall man helped Tim get the ball down. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you!" They played hockey together and had a lot of fun.

Anna and Ben were playing with their dolls in the garden. They liked to pretend that their dolls were their babies and they took good care of them. They gave them names, clothes, bottles and blankets. One day, Anna noticed that her doll had a dirty rag for a diaper. She said to Ben, "Look, my doll needs a change. Her rag is dirty and smelly. Do you have a clean one?" Ben looked at his doll and saw that he had a nice cloth diaper with a pin. He said to Anna, "No, I don't have a clean rag. But you can use my cloth diaper. It is soft and dry. Here, I will give it to you." Anna was happy and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are very kind and gentle. I will give you my rag and you can wash it later. Then we can swap again." They changed their dolls' diapers and hugged them. They continued to play and have fun. They learned that sharing is caring and that being gentle is good. They were good friends and good parents to their dolls.

Once upon a time, there was a girl with long hair. Her name was Lily. She liked to play outside with her friends. One day, she fell down and hurt her knee. Lily was sad because it hurt a lot. Lily's mom saw her fall and came to help. She had a magic bandage that could heal boo-boos. Lily's mom put the bandage on her knee. Lily felt better and was very happy. Now, Lily could play with her friends again. She was enthusiastic and jumped up and down. Her long hair bounced as she played. Lily and her friends had lots of fun that day.

Once upon a time, in a big blue house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and equipment. He had a big box filled with all kinds of fun things like balls, cars, and blocks. Tim's favorite toy was his blue car. One sunny day, Tim was playing in his room when he heard a loud noise. "Oh no!" he said, starting to panic. "What was that?" Tim looked around and saw that his blue car was missing from his box of equipment. He felt very sad and worried. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, I can't find my blue car!" His mom smiled and said, "Don't panic, Tim. Let's look for your car together." They searched the house and finally found the blue car under Tim's bed. Tim was so happy to have his favorite toy back. He hugged his mom and said, "Thank you, Mom!" From that day on, Tim made sure to keep his equipment safe and always put his toys away when he was done playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to eat. He would eat anything he found. One day, Max found a big cake on the table. He thought it looked very yummy, so he ate it all up. After Max ate the cake, his tummy started to hurt. He knew he was in trouble. Max's mom saw the empty plate and knew what had happened. She told Max that he should not eat things that were not his. Max felt sad and said sorry. Max's mom gave him a hug and said it was okay. She told Max to be calm and rest. She would make him feel better. Max learned his lesson and promised not to eat things that were not his. From that day on, he was a good and calm dog.
