Category: Beginner
Title: The Bull and the Strong

Once upon a time, there was a big bull. The bull was very strong and good. He lived in a big field with his friends. They played all day long. One day, a man came to the field to test the bull. He wanted to see how strong the bull was. The man put a big bag on the bull's back. The bull tried very hard to carry the bag. He wanted to show the man that he was strong. The bull carried the bag all day long. His friends watched and cheered for him. At the end of the day, the man was very happy. He knew the bull was strong and good. The bull learned that it is important to always do your best and be a good friend. And his friends learned that it is good to support and help each other.

---

One day, a little boy named Ben and his big sister Mia went to the park. They liked to play on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. But Ben also liked to run around and chase the birds and the squirrels. Mia told him to be careful and not to go too far, but Ben did not listen. He thought he was very quick and smart. He saw a big black bird on a tree and ran after it. The bird flew away, but Ben did not stop. He followed the bird across the road, without looking for cars. He did not hear Mia calling his name. He did not hear the loud horn of a car. He only heard the bird. Suddenly, he felt a terrible pain in his leg. He fell down and cried. The car had hit him. The driver got out and ran to him. He was very sorry and scared. He called for help. Mia ran to Ben and hugged him. She was very sad and angry. She said, "Ben, why did you not listen to me? You could have been hurt very badly. You have to be careful and look both ways before you cross the road. You have to hear what I say. I love you and I want you to be safe." Ben was sorry too. He said, "I'm sorry, Mia. I was silly and I did not hear you. I wanted to catch the bird, but it was not worth it. I love you too and I don't want to make you sad. I will be careful and listen to you from now on." Ben learned a lesson that day. He learned that he should hear his sister and obey her. He learned that he should be careful and not run after things that are not important. He learned that he should be grateful for his sister and her love. He learned that being quick is not always good, and that being terrible is not fun.

---

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars in the garden. They liked to pretend they were driving on the road and going to different places. Lily had a red car that was modern and shiny. Ben had a blue car that was old and rusty. "Look at my car, it is so fast and cool!" Lily said. "Your car is slow and ugly!" Ben felt sad. He liked his car, even if it was not modern. He tried to make his car go faster, but it only made a loud noise. "Your car is broken!" Lily laughed. "It needs fuel!" Ben looked at his car. He saw a hole in the tank where the fuel was. He did not have any fuel to put in his car. He felt even sadder. He saw a big leaf on the ground. He had an idea. He picked up the leaf and covered the hole in his tank with it. He hoped it would work. He started his car again. It did not make a loud noise anymore. It went faster and faster. "Wow, your car is fast now!" Lily said. "How did you do that?" Ben smiled. He showed her the leaf. "I covered the hole with a leaf. It stopped the fuel from leaking out." Lily was amazed. She realized that Ben's car was not ugly or broken. It was clever and special. "I'm sorry I was mean to you," she said. "Your car is awesome. Can we share our cars and play together?" Ben nodded. He was happy. He gave Lily his car and took hers. They drove their cars on the road and went to different places. They had fun and learned to be kind and respectful to each other.
