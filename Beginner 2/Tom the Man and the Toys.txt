Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a kind man named Tom. Tom was very generous. He loved to share his things with others. One day, Tom saw a little boy who was sad. The boy had no toys to play with. Tom had an idea. He went to his car and began to load it with toys. He put balls, dolls, and toy cars inside. When the car was full, he drove to the park where the little boy was. "Hi little boy," said Tom. "I have something for you!" The boy looked up and saw all the toys in the car. His face lit up with a big smile. "Wow! Thank you, Tom!" said the boy. They played together in the park all day. From that day on, Tom and the little boy became good friends. They would play with the toys in the park every day. And the generous man, Tom, made sure the little boy always had toys to play with. The city was a happier place because of Tom's big heart.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a comfortable bed that she loved to sleep in. One day, she found a big box under her bed. Sue was very surprised! Inside the box, Sue found a violin. She did not know how to play it, but she tried. She made a funny noise with the violin. Sue laughed and said, "This is fun!" Sue's mom heard the noise and came into the room. "What's that sound?" she asked. Sue showed her mom the violin and said, "I found this under my bed!" Her mom smiled and said, "That was a nice surprise! Let's learn how to play it together!" So they did, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little snake. The snake was very fragile. He lived in a big forest with many friends. One day, the snake wanted to go to a party with his friends. But, the snake had to delay going to the party. He had to help his mom with some work. His mom was happy that he helped her. After they finished the work, the snake could go to the party. At the party, the snake had lots of fun with his friends. They played games and ate yummy food. The fragile snake was very careful while playing. In the end, everyone was happy and the snake went home with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely box. It was in a dark room. The box was sad because nothing fit in it. One day, a little boy found the box. "Why are you sad, box?" the boy asked. "I am lonely and nothing fits in me," said the box. The boy had an idea. He went to find things that could fit in the box. The boy tried to put a big toy in the box, but it did not fit. He tried to put a ball in the box, but it did not fit either. The boy was sad, but he did not give up. One day, the boy found a small toy car. He put the car in the box, and it fit! The box was happy, and the boy was happy too. They played together every day. The moral of the story is to never give up, and you will find what fits. Sometimes, what you need is not what you expect.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. The field had long, green grass. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. In the field, there were two friends: a dog named Spot and a cat named Whiskers. One day, Spot said to Whiskers, "Let's play in the field!" Whiskers liked the idea. They ran and jumped in the long grass. They had a lot of fun. Spot found a stick and they played fetch. After a while, Whiskers felt tired. Whiskers said to Spot, "I am tired. Let's rest." Spot thought for a moment and then he had an idea. Spot suggested, "Let's sit under that big tree!" Whiskers agreed and they both went to the tree. They sat under the tree and enjoyed the cool shade.
