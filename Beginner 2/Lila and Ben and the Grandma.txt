Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Grandma

Lila and Ben are twins. They like to play together. Today is a special day. They are going to visit Grandma and Grandpa. Mom says they have to dress nice and be good. Mom helps Lila put on a pink dress and a bow in her hair. Ben wears a blue shirt and pants. Mom says they look very pretty and handsome. She gives them a hug and a kiss. "Are you ready to go?" Mom asks. "Yes, Mom!" Lila and Ben say. They go to the car and buckle up. Mom drives to Grandma and Grandpa's house. It is not far. Lila and Ben look out the window and see trees and flowers and birds. They arrive at Grandma and Grandpa's house. They are very happy to see them. Grandma and Grandpa hug and kiss them and say they missed them. "Come in, come in!" Grandma says. "We have something important to tell you." Lila and Ben follow Grandma and Grandpa to the living room. They sit on the couch. Mom sits next to them. Grandma and Grandpa sit on the chairs. "What is it, Grandma?" Lila asks. Grandma smiles and looks at Grandpa. Grandpa nods and takes out a big envelope from his pocket. He opens it and shows them a picture. "Look, Lila and Ben. This is your new cousin. He was born yesterday. His name is Leo. He is your aunt's baby. You are going to meet him soon. Isn't he cute?" Lila and Ben look at the picture. They see a tiny baby with a hat and a blanket. He has a small nose and a small mouth. He is sleeping. "Wow!" Lila says. "He is so small and cute. I want to hold him." "Me too!" Ben says. "Can we play with him?" Grandma and Grandpa laugh. Mom smiles. "Of course you can. But you have to be gentle and quiet. He is very new and needs a lot of rest. He can't talk or walk yet. He only eats and sleeps and cries. But he will grow and learn soon. And he will love you very much. You are his big cousins. You can help him and teach him and protect him. You have a very important role." Lila and Ben nod. They feel proud and excited. They want to meet Leo and be good cousins. They hug Grandma and Grandpa and Mom and say thank you for the picture. They hear a soft sound from the picture. It is Leo. He is making a noise with his mouth. He is not crying. He is cooing. Lila and Ben listen. They are quiet. They hear Leo's voice. It is a sweet sound. It breaks the silence. It fills their hearts.

One day, a friendly dog named Buddy found a red vest. He put the vest on and went to the park. In the park, there were many kids playing with balls and running around. Buddy wanted to play too, so he ran up to the kids. "Hi, kids!" Buddy called. "Can I play with you?" The kids looked at Buddy and saw his red vest. They thought it was funny that a dog was wearing a vest. They began to laugh and said, "Yes, Buddy! You can play with us!" Buddy played with the kids all day. They threw balls and Buddy would catch them. They all had a lot of fun. When the sun started to go down, the kids had to go home. They said goodbye to Buddy and thanked him for playing with them. Buddy was very happy that he made new friends and went home with his red vest.

Once upon a time, there was a little kid named Tim. He loved to play with his blue ball. Every day, he would go to the park and play with his ball. One day, while Tim was playing at the park, a blue bird came to watch him. The bird liked to see Tim play with his blue ball. The bird would fly around and sing while Tim played. Tim and the blue bird became good friends. They played together every day. Tim would throw the blue ball, and the bird would catch it. They had lots of fun, and everyone who saw them would stop and watch their games. And they all lived happily ever after.
