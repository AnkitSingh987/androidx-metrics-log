Category: Beginner
Title: The Yellow Bird and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird. The bird loved to eat muffins. One day, the bird found a big muffin on the ground. It was very happy. The bird started to eat the muffin, but then the muffin started to disappear! The bird was sad and did not know what to do. It looked around to find where the muffin went. Then, the bird saw a little mouse. The mouse was eating the muffin too! The bird and the mouse became friends. They ate the muffin together and were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a light bug. The bug was very hungry. A boy named Tim saw the bug and wanted to help. Tim had a big heart and liked to help all the little bugs he found. One day, Tim went to feed the light bug. He gave the bug some leaves to eat. The bug ate and ate. Tim was happy to see the bug eat so much. He thought the bug would grow big and strong. But then, something unexpected happened. The light bug started to change. It grew very big and not so light anymore. The bug was not nice anymore. It ate all the leaves off the trees and the other bugs too. Tim was sad because he wanted to help, but he made things worse.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dependable police named Sam. Sam liked to spring and play with kids in the park. One sunny day, while Sam was playing, they saw a big, red ball. Sam and the kids decided to play with the big, red ball. They kicked it, threw it, and had lots of fun. Suddenly, the big, red ball started to move on its own! They all stopped and watched as the ball rolled away. As they followed the ball, they found a little dog. The dog had been moving the ball all along! The kids laughed and played with the little dog. Sam, the dependable police, had a new friend to spring and play with in the park. And they all had a very fun day together.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were spies on a mission. Lily had a zoom toy that made noises and showed pictures. Ben had a pistol toy that made sounds and flashed lights. They were unique toys that their dad made for them. They saw a big dog near the swings. It was brown and furry and had a long tail. Lily and Ben thought it was a bad guy. They decided to sneak up on it and catch it. They hid behind a tree and waited for the right moment. Lily used her zoom toy to look at the dog. She saw that it had a collar with a name tag. It said Rex. She whispered to Ben, "It's not a bad guy, it's a pet. His name is Rex." Ben did not hear her. He was too excited. He jumped out from behind the tree and pointed his pistol toy at the dog. He shouted, "Freeze, bad guy! You're under arrest!" The dog was scared and confused. It barked loudly and ran away. Lily and Ben chased after it. They wanted to say sorry and play with it. But the dog was too fast. It zoomed across the park and out of sight. Lily and Ben stopped and looked at each other. They felt bad. They did not mean to scare the dog. They hoped it was okay. They decided to be more careful next time. They went back to their toys and continued their mission. They still had fun, but they also learned a lesson.

One day, a boy named Tim saw the big, round moon in the sky. It was icy and shiny. Tim wanted to go to the moon. He asked his mom, "Can we go to the moon?" Mom said, "No, Tim. We cannot go to the moon. It is too far away." Tim was sad. He wanted to go to the moon so much. At night, he went to bed and had a dream. In his dream, he was on the moon! He met a nice alien named Al. Al said, "Hello, Tim! Welcome to the moon. Do you like it here?" Tim was happy. He played with Al on the moon. They had so much fun. But then, Al's mom came. She was mad. She said, "Al, you must mind your manners! You cannot play with Tim. He is from Earth." Al was sad. He did not want Tim to go. But Tim had an idea. He said, "Al, we can still be friends. We can talk to each other from far away." Al liked the idea. They said goodbye and Tim woke up. Now, every night, Tim looks at the moon and thinks of his friend Al. They cannot play together, but they are still friends. And Tim is happy.
