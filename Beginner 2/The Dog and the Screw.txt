Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog and the Screw

Once upon a time, there was a good dog. His name was Scout. Every day he wanted to reach something but he couldn't. One day he saw a screw on the floor and he wanted to reach it! He jumped up really high, but it was too far away. So he tried jumping again. Again, he was too short. But then, Scout had a good idea. He ran and jumped onto a table. Then he jumped onto the counter. He was still too short, so he found a stool. Scout reached up with all his might, and this time he got it! He was so pleased to get the screw. He jumped down from the stool and took the screw back to his bed. Every day, he looked at the screw and remembered how he was able to reach it. He was very proud of himself.

One day, a kind girl named Lily went to the park. She liked to watch the birds and play with her friends. Today, she saw a small box under the tree. She did not know what was in the box. Lily and her friends looked at the box. They thought it was a toy. They were very happy and excited. They opened the box and saw a key. They did not know what the key was for, but they knew it was special. They looked around the park and found a small door in the tree. They used the key to open the door. Inside, they found a room full of toys and games. They were very surprised and happy. They played all day and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly high in the sky. One day, he saw his friend, Sam the squirrel, on the ground. Sam was very creative and could make fun games. Tim had an idea and called out to Sam, "Let's have a race!" Sam thought it was a great idea. He said, "Okay, let's race to the big tree over there!" Tim and Sam got ready to start the race. Tim would fly, and Sam would run. They both said, "One, two, three, go!" And off they went, racing to the big tree. Tim flew high, and Sam ran fast. They both wanted to win the race. As they got closer to the big tree, Tim and Sam saw their friends cheering for them. The race was very close, but in the end, Tim won by just a little bit. Tim and Sam were both happy and had lots of fun. Their friends clapped and said, "Good race!" They all played together for the rest of the day.

Lily and Ben were having lunch with Mom and Dad. They liked to eat noodles with cheese and tomato sauce. Mom gave them each a fork to twirl the noodles. Lily was good at using her fork, but Ben was still learning. He dropped his fork on the floor many times. "Oops, sorry, Mom," Ben said every time he dropped his fork. Mom smiled and gave him a new fork. She was not angry, but she seemed a little tired. "Why do you drop your fork, Ben?" Lily asked. She wanted to help her brother. "I don't know, Lily. The fork is slippery and heavy. And the noodles are wiggly and funny. I can't hold them," Ben said. He looked sad. Lily had an idea. She took her fork and gave it to Ben. Then she took Ben's fork and put it on her plate. "Here, Ben. Try my fork. It is smaller and lighter. Maybe it will be easier for you," Lily said. Ben took Lily's fork and tried to twirl the noodles. He was surprised. The fork was not slippery or heavy. And the noodles were not wiggly or funny. He could hold them. "Wow, Lily. Your fork is better. Thank you for sharing with me," Ben said. He smiled and ate his noodles. Lily smiled too. She was happy to see Ben happy. She did not mind using his fork. It was not too big or too hard. She ate her noodles too. Mom and Dad watched Lily and Ben. They were proud of their children. They were kind and helpful to each other. They finished their lunch and said thank you. Then they went to play in the garden. It was a sunny and peaceful day.
