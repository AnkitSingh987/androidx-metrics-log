Category: Beginner
Title: Bella the Busy Bee and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a busy bee named Bella. Bella loved to work all day in the big cherry tree. The cherry tree had many sweet cherries that Bella liked to eat. One day, Bella saw a little bird named Billy in the cherry tree. Bella said, "Hi, Billy! Do you want to work with me and eat cherries?" Billy said, "Yes, Bella! I love cherries too!" So, Bella and Billy worked together in the cherry tree. They ate many cherries and had a lot of fun. They became good friends and played in the cherry tree every day. And they were always busy and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pet cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was adorable and loved to play with Sue. They liked to play with a ball and a bowl in the house. One day, Sue and Fluffy were playing with the ball. Fluffy hit the ball and it went into the kitchen. The ball hit a big bowl on the table. Sue said, "Oh no, Fluffy! We need to be careful!" Sue's mom came into the kitchen and saw the bowl on the floor. She said, "Sue, you and Fluffy need to behave when you play. The bowl could break!" Sue said, "I'm sorry, mom. We will be good." Sue and Fluffy went outside to play. They played with the ball and the bowl in the grass. They had a lot of fun and behaved well. Sue's mom was happy, and they all had a great day.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She loved to play and have fun. She saw a funny-looking bird on a tree. The bird had a big beak and danced on the branch. Sue wanted to be friends with the bird. She said "hello" to the bird and it said "hello" back. Sue was very happy. She wanted the bird to listen to her sing. She sang a song for the bird. The bird liked the song and danced even more. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. The bird flew down from the tree and landed on Sue's shoulder. Sue was very surprised. Then, the bird began to sing. Sue was amazed! The bird sang the same song Sue sang. Sue and the bird sang together. They had a lot of fun. People in the park stopped to look at Sue and the bird singing. Everyone clapped and laughed. Sue and the funny bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear. He lived in a deep cave with his mom and dad. The little bear did not know how to find food. He was sad. One day, the little bear met a big bear. The big bear said, "Do you want to know how to find food?" The little bear said, "Yes, please!" So, the big bear showed the little bear how to find food. But then, another bear came. This bear was mean. He wanted all the food for himself. The little bear and the big bear did not like this. They worked together to stop the mean bear. In the end, they all shared the food and became friends.

Lily and Ben were bored. They wanted to play with something fun. They saw a big box of paints in the closet. They took out the paints and some paper. They went to the kitchen. "Let's mix the paints and make new colors!" Lily said. "OK, but we need water too," Ben said. They turned on the faucet and filled a cup with water. They dipped their brushes in the water and then in the paints. They mixed the paints on the paper. They made purple, green, orange, and brown. "Look, I made a flower!" Lily said, showing her paper. "I made a car!" Ben said, showing his paper. They were happy with their paintings. They wanted to make more. They used up all the paints in the box. They looked for more paints in the closet. They found another box. It had a label that said "Cheap Paints". "Let's use these ones!" Ben said. He opened the box and took out a paint. He squeezed it on the paper. It was very runny and watery. It did not make a nice color. It made a mess. "Yuck, these paints are bad!" Lily said. She tried another paint. It was the same. It was not bright or smooth. It was dull and lumpy. "These paints are cheap. They are not good for mixing," Lily said. She threw the paint in the trash. Ben did the same. "Let's go back to the faucet and wash our hands," Ben said. They went to the faucet and turned it on. They washed their hands and their brushes. They dried them with a towel. "Next time, let's use the good paints," Lily said. "OK, and let's ask Mom first," Ben said. They agreed. They put their paintings on the fridge. They were proud of their work. They hugged each other and smiled.
