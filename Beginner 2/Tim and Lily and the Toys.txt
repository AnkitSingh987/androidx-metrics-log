Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Toys

Tim and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had many toys: cars, dolls, blocks, books and more. They liked to make noises and stories with their toys. But they also made a big mess on the floor. "Tim, Lily, it's time to tidy up!" their mom called from the kitchen. "You have to put your toys away before dinner." Tim and Lily did not want to tidy up. They wanted to keep playing. They pretended they did not hear their mom. "Tim, Lily, I said it's time to tidy up!" their mom called again, louder. "Come on, don't make me come there!" Tim and Lily sighed. They knew their mom was serious. They started to pick up their toys and put them in the toy box. But they did not do it fast. They did it very slow. "Look, Lily, this car is so cool!" Tim said, holding a red car. "It can go vroom vroom and beep beep!" "Look, Tim, this doll is so pretty!" Lily said, holding a pink doll. "She can sing la la la and dance twirl twirl!" They played with their toys a little more, instead of putting them away. They did not see their mom coming to the living room. "Tim, Lily, what are you doing?" their mom asked, with a frown. "You are not tidying up, you are playing more! This is not good. You have to listen to me and do what I say. Tidying up is important. It makes the house nice and clean. And it shows that you respect your things and your space. Do you understand?" Tim and Lily nodded, but they did not look happy. They felt bad for making their mom angry. They also felt tired from playing so much. They yawned. "Mom, we are sorry," Tim said. "We will tidy up now. We will do it fast." "Mom, we are sleepy," Lily said. "Can we go to bed after dinner?" Their mom smiled. She was not angry anymore. She hugged them and kissed them. "It's okay, my loves," she said. "I know you like to play, and that's good. But you also have to learn to tidy up, and that's good too. It's part of growing up. And yes, you can go to bed after dinner. You had a long day. You need to rest and sleep well. Come on, let's finish tidying up together. I will help you." Tim and Lily smiled too. They felt happy and loved. They helped their mom tidy up the living room. They put all their toys in the toy box. They lifted the toy box together. It was heavy. It had a lot of weight. But they did it. They put the toy box in the corner. They looked at the living room. It was nice and clean. They felt proud. "Good job, Tim and Lily!" their mom said. "You did it! The living room is tidy now. You are very good helpers. I am proud of you. Now, let's go to the kitchen. Dinner is ready. It's your favorite: spaghetti and meatballs!" Tim and Lily cheered. They loved spaghetti and meatballs. They followed their mom to the kitchen. They washed their hands and sat at the table. They ate their dinner and enjoyed it. They thanked their mom for the food. They yawned again. "Mom, can we go to bed now?" Tim and Lily asked. "Of course, my loves," their mom said. "You deserve a good night's sleep. Let's go to your room. I will tuck you in and read you a story." Tim and Lily followed their mom to their room. They brushed their teeth and put on their pajamas. They climbed into their bed and snuggled under the covers. Their mom tucked them in and kissed them. She picked up a book and read them a story. It was a funny and sweet story. Tim and Lily listened and laughed. They yawned some more. "Mom, we love you," Tim and Lily said. "I love you too, my loves," their mom said. "You are my precious children. You make me happy. Sleep tight and dream sweet. Good night." She turned off the light and left the room. Tim and Lily closed their eyes and fell asleep. They slept well and peacefully. They had a good day. And they would have another one tomorrow.
