Category: Beginner
Title: The Wolf and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little wolf. This little wolf was very spoiled. He always got what he wanted. One day, he saw a big red ball. He wanted it so much. The little wolf asked his mommy, "Can I have the big red ball?" His mommy said, "Okay, but don't lose it. I will only buy you one." The little wolf was so happy. He played with the big red ball all day. But one day, the little wolf lost his big red ball. He started to panic. He looked everywhere for it. Then he saw a big tree. The big red ball was stuck in the tree. The little wolf was so happy to find it. He learned not to be spoiled and to take care of his things.

Ben and Lily were playing in the kitchen. They wanted to make a cake for Mom. They saw a big jar of sugar on the shelf. They tried to reach it, but it was too high. "Let's get a chair," Ben said. He pushed a chair to the shelf and climbed on it. He grabbed the jar of sugar and tried to get down. But he slipped and dropped the jar. It broke on the floor and sugar spilled everywhere. "Oh no!" Lily cried. "Mom will be mad!" Ben was scared too. He heard Mom coming. He hid under the table with Lily. "Who made this mess?" Mom asked. She saw the broken jar and the sugar. She was not happy. Ben and Lily did not answer. They hoped Mom would not see them. But Mom was smart. She looked under the table and saw Ben and Lily. They looked at her with big eyes. "Ben, Lily, what did you do?" Mom asked. Ben and Lily were sorry. They wanted to make Mom happy, not mad. "We wanted to make a cake for you," Ben said. "But we could not reach the sugar," Lily said. Mom sighed. She was not mad anymore. She was calm. She knew Ben and Lily were trying to be nice. "That was sweet of you, but you should not play with sugar," Mom said. "It is dangerous and messy. Next time, ask me for help, okay?" Ben and Lily nodded. They said sorry to Mom. They hugged her. Mom smiled. She hugged them back. She said, "I love you, Ben and Lily. But now we have to clean up this mess." Ben and Lily helped Mom clean up the sugar. They learned their lesson. They did not play with sugar again. They waited for Mom to make a cake with them.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play hide and seek with his dog, Max. Tim said, "Max, I will hide and you will find me, okay?" Max barked and wagged his tail. Tim looked for a place to hide and saw the big refrigerator. He thought it was a good spot to hide behind it. Max started to look for Tim. He looked in the bedroom, under the table, and behind the couch. But he could not find Tim. Max was sad and sat down. Then, he heard a small sound coming from the refrigerator. Max ran to it and found Tim hiding there. Tim laughed and said, "You found me, Max! Good job!" Tim's mom saw them and said, "Tim, it is important not to hide behind the refrigerator. It can be dangerous." Tim understood and said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I will find a safer place to hide next time." Max barked happily, and they both learned that it is important to play safe.

Once upon a time, two friends, Max and Chris, were walking outside. Chris noticed a tall tree, and pointed up to its branches. He said, "Max, I want to get up that tree!" Max said, "That's impossible, Chris. We don't even have a ladder." But Chris refused to give up. He looked around and found a twig. He said, "I can use this twig to help me climb the tree!" Max was very envious of Chris' quick thinking, so he said, "Go ahead and try, Chris. I'll allow it." Chris used the twig to climb the tree, and even made it to the top! The moral of this story is that with a little bit of effort, you can achieve great success. So don't be afraid to try something new - with a little bit of help, you can make it to the top!
