Category: Beginner
Title: The Man and His Wife and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house with their dog, Spot. The man and his wife were very glad. They liked to play with Spot in their yard. One day, something happened. Spot ran away from the yard. The man and his wife were sad. They looked for Spot everywhere, but they could not find him. The man said, "Let's ask our friend Tom for help." So, they went to Tom's house. Tom was a good friend. They told Tom what happened. Tom said, "I saw Spot near the park." The man, his wife, and Tom went to the park. At the park, they found Spot. Spot was playing with a ball. The man and his wife were so glad. They said, "Thank you, Tom!" They took Spot back home and played together in the yard again.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with many birds. They wanted to see the birds up close. They climbed the tree and found a nest with three eggs. "Wow, look at the eggs! They are so beautiful!" Lily said. "Let's take them home!" Tom said. He reached for the nest. "No, Tom, don't do that! The eggs belong to the birds. They will be sad if we take them away!" Lily said. But Tom did not listen. He grabbed the nest and ran down the tree. Lily followed him. Suddenly, they heard a loud shout. It was the mother bird. She saw Tom with her nest and was very angry. She flew after him and pecked his head. "Ow, ow, ow! Help, help, help!" Tom cried. Lily ran to him and took the nest from his hands. She climbed the tree and put the nest back where it was. She said sorry to the mother bird. The mother bird calmed down and thanked Lily. She said she was glad that her eggs were safe. She said she hoped that Tom learned his lesson. Lily and Tom went back to the ground. Tom had a big bump on his head. He felt sorry for what he did. He said sorry to Lily and the mother bird. Lily hugged him and said it was okay. She said they should never take things that are not theirs. She said they should respect the animals and their homes. Tom agreed. He said he learned his lesson. He said he would never do that again. They went home and told their mom what happened. Their mom was proud of Lily for being brave and kind. She was also glad that Tom was sorry and learned his lesson. She said they should always be good to each other and to nature. She said that was the moral of the story.

One day, Annie set off on a tour. She was excited to see a new place, but when she got there, she started to shiver. It was too cold for her! She tried to find a warm spot, but everywhere felt so chilly. She held her hands close to her chest and started to cry. Just then, a friendly voice said, “hello, why are you crying?” She looked up and saw an old man with a kind face. "I'm cold and I'm hurt,” Annie said. The old man gave her a warm hug and said, “Don't worry, I'll take you to a place where you'll feel better”. Annie followed the man to his home. He gave her some hot soup and blankets to keep her warm. She felt much better after. Annie smiled and thanked the man for his kindness. He smiled back and said, “You're welcome! That's what friends are for!”

Once upon a time there was a boy who lived in a small town. His name was Harry, and he was a very selfish boy. He liked getting things his own way and never wanted to share with anyone else. One morning, Harry decided to go walking through the town. As he was walking, he saw a huge lake. Suddenly, he heard a voice coming from the lake. It said “Help me! I’m stuck!” Harry thought it was a silly thing to do so he just kept walking. The voice asked again, but Harry didn’t respond. As he walked away, he heard the voice saying, “I won’t forget this. You’ll be sorry!” Later that day, Harry was walking down the same street when he saw a large tree. Suddenly, the tree started speaking to him. “Harry, why didn’t you help me? I was stuck in the lake and I could have drowned!” Harry realized that it was the same voice he had heard at the lake earlier. He was very scared and he started to apologize. But the tree was too angry and said, “You were too selfish to help me. Now you’ll have to lie in my branches and never leave!” Harry was forced to lie in the tree’s branches and remain there for the rest of his days
