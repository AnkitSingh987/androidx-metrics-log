Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo and the Mouse

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there was a small, soft bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to play with his friends, the birds, the bugs, and the other bunnies. One day, Bobo found a big, shiny key. He didn't know what it was for, but he thought it was pretty, so he decided to hold it. Bobo showed the key to his friend, Tina the Turtle. Tina said, "Wow, that's a big key! I wonder what it can open." Bobo thought about it for a moment and said, "I don't know, but I will find out!" And so, Bobo and Tina went on an adventure to find what the key could open. They looked everywhere, but they couldn't find anything that the key would fit. Then, they saw a big, old tree with a small door at the bottom. Bobo tried the key, and it worked! The door opened, and out came a tiny, sad mouse. The mouse said, "Thank you for letting me out! I was stuck in there for so long, and I couldn't escape!" Bobo and Tina were happy they could help the mouse. And from that day on, they all became the best of friends.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play and learn together. One day, Anna has a loose tooth. She is happy because she knows the tooth fairy will come and give her a coin. "Look, Ben, my tooth is wiggly!" Anna says, showing her tooth to Ben. "Wow, that's cool!" Ben says. "Can I touch it?" "Okay, but be gentle," Anna says. Ben touches Anna's tooth with his finger. He feels it move a little. He is curious. "How does the tooth come out?" he asks. Anna shrugs. "I don't know. Maybe you have to pull it or wait for it to fall out." Ben thinks for a moment. He has an idea. "Maybe I can teach you how to make it come out faster," he says. Anna is interested. "How?" she asks. Ben smiles. "Follow me," he says. He leads Anna to the swing set in the playground. He tells her to sit on a swing and hold on tight. He pushes her hard and fast. "Are you ready?" he asks. Anna nods. She likes to swing high. Ben counts to three. Then he says, "Now, open your mouth and say 'ahh'!" Anna does what Ben says. She opens her mouth and says "ahh". She feels a breeze on her tongue. She also feels something fly out of her mouth. She looks down and sees her tooth on the ground. She is surprised and happy. "Ben, you did it! You taught me how to make my tooth come out!" she says. Ben is proud and happy too. He runs to pick up Anna's tooth and gives it to her. "Here you go, Anna. Now you can put it under your pillow and wait for the tooth fairy," he says. Anna hugs Ben and thanks him. They are both happy. They are good friends. They learned something new and cool.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sam, who had a big red ball. Sam said, "Look, Tim! I have a surprise for you!" Tim was very excited to see the surprise. Sam threw the ball high into the sky. It went up and up, and then it came down fast. It hit Tim on the head. "Ouch!" Tim cried. "That hurt!" Sam felt bad for hurting his friend. Sam said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to hurt you." Tim said, "It's okay, Sam. Just be careful next time." They both learned that it is important to be careful when playing with friends, so no one gets hurt. They played with the ball all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful boy named Tom. He liked to play outside with his friends. They played games like catch, tag, and hide-and-seek. Tom always had a big smile on his face when he played with his friends. One day, Tom found a toy arrow in his toy box. He thought it would be fun to give the arrow to his best friend, Tim. So, Tom went to Tim's house to show him the arrow and give it to him as a gift. When Tom gave the arrow to Tim, Tim was very happy. They played together with the arrow, pretending to be heroes in a big adventure. They had lots of fun and laughed a lot. And from that day on, Tom and Tim played with the arrow every time they were together.
