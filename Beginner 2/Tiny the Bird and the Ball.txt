Category: Beginner
Title: Tiny the Bird and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a small bird named Tiny. Tiny loved colors. He liked to look at the green leaves, the blue sky, and the yellow sun. One day, Tiny saw a big, red ball stuck in a tree. "Help! Help!" cried the ball. "I am stuck! Can you save me?" Tiny wanted to help the big, red ball. He flew up to the ball and tried to push it. But Tiny was too small. He needed help. So, he asked his friend, Big Bear, to save the ball. Big Bear was strong. He shook the tree, and the big, red ball fell down. The ball was happy to be free. "Thank you for saving me!" said the ball. Tiny, Big Bear, and the big, red ball played together in the forest. They had fun with all the colors around them. And they were happy to be friends.

One day, an elderly man named Bob was driving his car. He was going to the store to buy some food. On the way, he saw a little girl named Lucy. She was crying. Bob stopped the car and asked, "Why are you crying, Lucy?" Lucy said, "I lose my toy. It is my favorite toy." Bob wanted to help Lucy find her toy, so he said, "Let's look for it together." They looked and looked, but could not find the toy. Then, Bob's car started to make a noise. "Oh no," Bob said, "I have no more fuel." They walked to the store and bought some fuel for the car. They also found a new toy for Lucy. Lucy was happy, and they both went home with a smile.

Ben and Lily were playing in the kitchen. They wanted to make a salad for their mom. They found some lettuce, tomatoes, cheese and pepper in the fridge. "Let's include pepper in the salad. Mom likes pepper," Ben said. "Okay, but only a little bit. Pepper is spicy," Lily said. They washed the lettuce and tomatoes and tore them into pieces. They grated some cheese and sprinkled it on top. Then Ben took the pepper and shook it all over the salad. "Ben, stop! That's too much pepper!" Lily shouted. But Ben did not stop. He thought it was funny to make the salad very spicy. He was being naughty. Mom came into the kitchen and saw the salad. She smiled and thanked Ben and Lily for their help. "Wow, this salad looks delicious. Did you include pepper?" she asked. "Yes, we did. A lot of pepper," Ben said, grinning. Mom took a bite of the salad and felt a burning sensation in her mouth. She coughed and reached for a glass of water. "Ben, why did you put so much pepper in the salad? That's not nice. That's naughty," she said. Ben felt sorry for making mom unhappy. He realized he had made a mistake. "I'm sorry, mom. I was just playing. I won't do it again," he said. Lily hugged mom and gave her a kiss. "It's okay, mom. We love you. We can make another salad for you. A salad without pepper," she said. Mom smiled and hugged Ben and Lily back. "I love you too, my sweeties. Thank you for trying to make me a salad. Next time, let's use less pepper, okay?" she said.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a rich man with a big hat. The rich man was holding a fish. Tim asked, "Why do you hold the fish?" The rich man said, "I want to give this fish to a friend. He loves fish, and I want to make him happy." Tim thought that was a good idea. He wanted to help the rich man. As they walked, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took the fish from the rich man's hand! Tim and the rich man were sad. They did not know what to do. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's go to the store and buy a new fish." The rich man liked the idea. They went to the store and bought a new fish. In the end, they gave the new fish to the rich man's friend. He was very happy. The rich man thanked Tim for his help. They all became good friends and went to the park together often.
