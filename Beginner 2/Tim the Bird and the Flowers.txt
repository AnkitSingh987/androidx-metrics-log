Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a shy bird named Tim. He lived near a big cliff with his family. Tim was not like other birds. He was scared to fly high in the sky. One day, Tim's mom told him, "You must learn to fly high and enjoy the sky like other birds." Tim was scared, but he wanted to make his mom happy. So, he tried to fly higher and higher. As Tim flew near the cliff, he started to enjoy it. He saw pretty flowers and other birds playing. Tim was not shy anymore. He was a happy bird who loved to fly high near the cliff.

Lila and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bird in the sky. It has a long neck and big wings. It is a swan. "Wow, look at the swan!" Lila says. "It can travel far and high. I wish I had wings like that." "Me too," Tom says. "But we can travel too. We can run and jump and ride our bikes. We can go to many places." "Yes, but the swan can see more things. It can see the mountains and the sea and the clouds. It is lucky," Lila says. The swan hears them and flies down. It lands near them and says, "Hello, little ones. Why do you want wings like mine?" "Because you can travel far and high and see more things," Lila says. "But you can do many things that I cannot," the swan says. "You can laugh and hug and sing and draw. You can make friends and learn new things. You are lucky too." Lila and Tom think about what the swan says. They smile and say, "You are right. We are lucky too. Thank you for talking to us." The swan nods and says, "You are welcome. I have to go now. Goodbye, little ones. Have fun and be happy." The swan flies away. Lila and Tom wave and say, "Goodbye, swan. Have a good travel." They run and jump and play in the park. They are happy. They are lucky.

Tom has a pet cat named Max. Max is very soft and likes to play with Tom. Sometimes, Max jokes with Tom and hides under the bed or behind the sofa. Tom laughs when he finds Max and gives him a hug. One day, Tom and Max go to visit Tom's grandma. Grandma is very nice and has a big garden. Tom and Max like to run and play in the garden. Grandma has a pet bird named Coco. Coco is very colorful and can talk. Coco says hello to Tom and Max. Max is curious about Coco. He wants to joke with Coco and make him fly. He jumps on the cage and tries to open it. Coco is scared and shouts "Help! Help!" Grandma hears Coco and comes to the garden. She sees Max on the cage and Tom running after him. "Max, no!" Grandma says. "Coco is not a toy. He is my pet and my friend. You have to be polite to him and leave him alone." Tom catches Max and takes him off the cage. He says sorry to Grandma and Coco. He tells Max that he was naughty and that he should not joke with other pets. Max looks sad and meows. He does not want to make Tom and Grandma angry. Grandma smiles and says "It's okay, Tom. Max did not know better. He was just curious and playful. But now he knows that he has to be polite to Coco and other pets. Right, Max?" Max nods and purrs. He goes to Coco and sniffs him. Coco says "Hello, Max. Nice to meet you." Max says "Meow, Coco. Nice to meet you too." Tom, Max and Coco become friends and play nicely in the garden. Grandma is happy and gives them all a treat.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a small bottle of oil. She was troubled because she didn't know what it was for. She decided to ask her friend Tom. Tom didn't know either, so they went to ask their teacher, Miss Sue. "Miss Sue, what is this oil for?" asked Mia. Miss Sue took a peek at the bottle and said, "This is special oil that makes things work better. But you should always ask before using it." Mia and Tom went to the playground and saw a boy named Sam. Sam was sad because his toy car was not moving well. Mia and Tom thought maybe the oil could help. They asked Sam if they could try the oil on his toy car. Sam agreed, and they put a little oil on the car. It started to move better and faster! Sam was so happy and thanked Mia and Tom. They learned that it's important to ask for help and to share with others.
