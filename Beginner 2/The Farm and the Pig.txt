Category: Beginner
Title: The Farm and the Pig

Once upon a time, there was a pretty farm. On this farm, there were many animals. There was a big cow, a little pig, and a nice dog. The animals liked to play together. One day, the little pig was eating and made a mess. The big cow saw this and said, "We should be clean on our pretty farm." So, the nice dog helped the little pig wipe his face. The little pig said, "Thank you, nice dog." From that day on, all the animals on the pretty farm helped each other stay clean. They learned that it was important to be clean and help their friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pale blue train. The train lived in a small town with many people. The train loved to go fast on the tracks. One day, the train was going very fast. A little bird saw the train and wanted to warn the people. The bird flew to the people and said, "The train is going too fast! Be careful!" The people heard the bird's warning and stopped what they were doing. They waited for the train to pass by. The train saw the people and slowed down. The people were safe, and the train learned to go slower. From that day on, the pale blue train always went slow when it was near the people. The little bird and the train became good friends. They were happy to keep the town safe together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was an obedient dog. He always did what his mom and dad told him to do. One day, his mom asked him to prepare a snack for his little sister. Spot was happy to help. Spot went to the kitchen to make a tasty snack. He found some bread and butter. He took the butter and spread it on the bread. But, oh no! The butter was too hard. Spot tried and tried but he could not spread the butter. Spot felt sad. He wanted to be a good helper. Then, he had an idea! He put the butter in the sun to make it soft. Soon, the butter was easy to spread. Spot finished making the snack and gave it to his sister. She loved it! Spot's mom and dad were proud of their obedient little dog.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. The rock was new and very special. It was in a beautiful park. Many children liked to play near the rock. One day, a little girl named Lily found a small, shiny thing by the rock. It was a key! She was so excited. She showed the key to her friends. They all wanted to know what the key could open. Lily and her friends looked around the park. They found a small, hidden door in the rock. Lily used the key to open the door. Inside, they found a room full of toys and soft things to sit on. It was like a luxury room just for them! They played and had fun in their new secret place. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went out to play with his ball. He had a goal to kick the ball into the tight net. He kicked the ball very hard, but it went too far and got lost. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, he met a big cat who said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you find your ball." They looked for the ball together and found it stuck in a tree. The cat climbed up the tree and got the ball for Tim. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, cat!" In the end, Tim and the cat played together and had lots of fun. The cat helped Tim with his goal, and they became good friends. The moral of the story is that if you help others, they will return the help and you can make new friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to play or run. He liked to sleep all day. Tom had a friend named Tim. Tim was a little boy who lived in the same house. One day, Tim wanted to go outside and play. It was cold, so he needed to wear a jacket. Tim could not find his jacket. He asked Tom, "Can you help me find my jacket?" Tom did not want to help. He was too lazy. But Tim was sad, so Tom said, "Okay, I will help you." Tom and Tim looked for the jacket. They looked in the closet, under the bed, and behind the door. At last, they found the jacket on a chair. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, Tom." Tim put on his jacket and went outside to play. He asked Tom to come with him, but Tom was lazy. He just wanted to sleep. Tim played outside all by himself. He was cold and sad. He wanted Tom to play with him. But Tom was inside, being lazy. Tim got too cold and went back inside. He was not happy. In the end, Tom stayed lazy and did not play with Tim. Tim was sad and cold. They did not have fun together. Tom did not learn to be a good friend.
