Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Ball

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. Sara has a big ball. Ben has a small car. "Can I play with your ball?" Ben asks Sara. "OK, but be careful. It is heavy," Sara says. She offers the ball to Ben. Ben tries to lift the ball, but it is too heavy for him. He drops the ball on the ground. The ball rolls away. "Oh no! Where is the ball?" Sara says. They run after the ball. They see a dog. The dog has the ball in its mouth. The dog is happy. It shakes the ball and makes it squeak. "Hey, that is our ball!" Ben says. He tries to get the ball from the dog, but the dog growls. "Be nice, doggy. Please give us our ball," Sara says. She offers the dog a treat. The dog likes the treat. It drops the ball and eats the treat. "Thank you, doggy. You are a good dog," Sara says. She picks up the ball and gives it to Ben. "Thank you, Sara. You are a good friend," Ben says. He gives Sara his car. They play with the ball and the car. They have fun. The dog watches them and wags its tail.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a brown bracelet in her toy box. She put it on her arm and went to the park to play. While at the park, she saw a small bird sitting on a branch. The bird started to sing a pretty song. "Hi, bird!" said Lily. "I like your song. Can I sing with you?" The bird nodded, and they started to sing together. As they sang, something unexpected happened. The brown bracelet on Lily's arm began to glow. Suddenly, the bird grew bigger and turned into a talking bird! "Thank you, Lily!" said the talking bird. "Your brown bracelet had magic, and your singing set me free. Now, I can talk and be your friend forever!" Lily was very happy to have a new friend. They played and sang together all day long. And from that day on, Lily and the talking bird had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to skip and play near the big sea. One day, Tim saw a big sail far away. He wanted to see it up close. Tim ran to his dad and said, "Dad, can we go see the big sail?" His dad smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's go see the big sail together." They walked to the sea and saw a big boat with a big sail. The boat was rich and shiny. Tim and his dad watched the big boat skip on the water. They laughed and clapped. Tim said, "Dad, I want to skip like the big boat." His dad hugged him and said, "You can, Tim. Just keep skipping and playing." And so, Tim and his dad skipped and played by the sea, happy as can be.

Lily liked to watch the birds in the sky. She saw big birds and small birds, brown birds and white birds, loud birds and quiet birds. But her favorite bird was the raven. The raven was black and shiny, and it flew with graceful wings. Sometimes, the raven would land on a branch near Lily and look at her with bright eyes. One day, Lily wanted to talk to the raven. She walked to the tree where the raven was sitting and said, "Hello, raven. You are very pretty. Do you want to be my friend?" The raven tilted its head and said, "Caw, caw, caw." Lily did not know what that meant, but she smiled and said, "I like your voice. It sounds like a snap." The raven nodded and said, "Caw, caw, caw." Then it flew down and landed on Lily's shoulder. Lily giggled and said, "You are very brave. Do you want to play with me?" The raven nodded and said, "Caw, caw, caw." Then it flew up and down, around and around, and made Lily laugh. Lily and the raven became good friends, and they played every day. Lily was happy, and the raven was happy. They liked each other very much.
