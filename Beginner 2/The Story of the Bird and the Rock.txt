Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a big, hard rock. On top of the rock, a soft curtain lay. One sunny day, a little bird came to the rock. The bird saw the curtain and thought it was a cozy place to rest. The little bird said, "Hello, curtain! Can I sit on you?" The curtain did not talk back, but the bird felt the soft curtain was happy to help. So, the little bird sat on the curtain and took a nap. After the nap, the bird flapped its wings and said, "Thank you, curtain! You are so soft and nice!" The bird flew away, and the curtain lay on the hard rock, waiting for another friend to come and rest.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a dirty comet in the sky. It was big and bright. Tim was so happy to see it. He wanted to show it to his friends, but they were not there. Tim had an idea. He would feed the comet to make it stay. He ran inside and got some food. He threw the food up to the comet. But the comet did not eat the food. Tim was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The comet turned into a big, friendly bird. The bird flew down and ate the food. Tim was so surprised and happy. He played with the bird all day and they became best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some butter. Tim liked to help his mom. He felt like a big boy when he did. At the store, Tim saw many kinds of butter. Some were big, some were small. Tim's mom asked, "Which one is the normal butter?" Tim used his finger to point at the normal butter. "That one!" he said. His mom was happy. They took the butter home and made yummy food with it. Tim was proud that he helped his mom. They had a fun day together.

One day, Tom found a magnet. It was a big, strong magnet. He wanted to play with it. So, he took it to his room. He put it near his toys and it pulled them close. Tom was very happy. He liked his new toy. Tom had a friend named Ben. Ben came to play with him. Tom said, "Let's play with my new magnet." Ben was scared. He thought the magnet was scary. But Tom said, "Don't worry, it's fun!" So, they played with the magnet together. They used it to pick up toys and make them dance. After a while, Ben was not scared anymore. He liked the magnet too. They played with it all day long. When it was time for Ben to go home, Tom said, "Let's play with the magnet again tomorrow." Ben agreed. They both had a great day playing with the magnet.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved the color purple. One day, Tim saw a big purple sign near his house. The sign said, "Do not spoil the flowers." Tim did not know what "spoil" meant. He thought it meant to play with the flowers. So, he went to the flowers and started to pick them. He picked many flowers and made a big mess. Tim's mom saw what he did and explained that "spoil" meant to ruin or hurt something. She told him that picking the flowers hurt them and made the garden sad. Tim felt bad and said sorry. He learned that it is important to listen and ask when we do not know something.

One day, a big cat and a little dog went to play at the fair. The big cat was very happy. The little dog was happy too. They played on the swings and ate yummy treats. The big cat said to the little dog, "I am getting tired. Let's go on the big tire ride." The little dog nodded, but he looked a little scared. They went on the big tire ride, and it went up and down, and round and round. At the end of the day, the big cat and the little dog were very tired. The big cat yawned and said, "I had so much fun today!" The little dog yawned too and said, "Me too! I was scared of the tire ride, but it was fun!" They went home and slept very well.
