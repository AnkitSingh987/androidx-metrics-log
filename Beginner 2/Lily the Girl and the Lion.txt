Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Lion

One day, a little girl named Lily went on a trip with her mom and dad. They went to a big house near the beach. When they got there, Lily was very excited. She wanted to unpack her toys and play with them. Her mom said, "Let's unpack your toys and start our fun operation!" Lily and her mom opened the bags and took out the toys. They found a big red ball, a soft teddy bear, and a proud lion toy. Lily said, "I like the lion the most because he is so proud and strong!" Her mom agreed and said, "Yes, he is a very proud lion!" They played with the toys all day. They had a lot of fun. The operation was a big success. At the end of the day, Lily gave her mom a big hug and said, "Thank you for the best day ever!" Her mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, my little helper. I'm proud of you too!"

One day, a little girl named Amy found a picture in her room. The picture showed a big tree with a swing. Amy wanted to find the tree and play on the swing. She asked her mom, "Can I go find the tree?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful, and don't go too far." Amy walked and walked. She saw a big tree that looked like the one in the picture. But there was no swing on the tree. Amy felt sad. She wanted to play on the swing. A little boy named Tim saw Amy feeling sad. He asked her, "Why are you sad?" Amy showed Tim the picture and said, "I can't find the swing on the tree." Tim looked at the picture and said, "I dare you to look again, but this time, close your eyes and count to ten." Amy did what Tim said. She closed her eyes and counted to ten. When she opened her eyes, she saw the swing on the tree! Tim had put the swing up while Amy had her eyes closed. They both played on the swing and had lots of fun.

Lily and Max are twins. They like to play together. One day, they go to the park with Mom. Mom says they can ride the big slide. Lily and Max run to the slide. They wait for their turn. They see a boy with a red hat. He is sitting on a seat near the slide. He has a book in his hand. He does not look at the slide. He looks at the book. Lily is curious. She wants to know what the boy is reading. She walks to the seat. She says hello to the boy. "Hello," the boy says. He smiles at Lily. "What are you reading?" Lily asks. "I am reading a story about dinosaurs," the boy says. He shows Lily the book. It has pictures of big and small dinosaurs. Lily likes dinosaurs. She says, "Can I see your book?" "Sure," the boy says. He gives Lily the book. Lily looks at the pictures. She points at a dinosaur with a long neck. She says, "What is this?" "That is a brontosaurus," the boy says. He tells Lily some facts about the brontosaurus. Lily listens to the boy. She learns new things. She thinks the boy is smart and nice. Max notices Lily and the boy. He is curious too. He walks to the seat. He says hello to the boy. "Hello," the boy says. He smiles at Max. "Can I see your book too?" Max asks. "Sure," the boy says. He shares the book with Max. Max looks at the pictures. He points at a dinosaur with sharp teeth. He says, "What is this?" "That is a tyrannosaurus rex," the boy says. He tells Max some facts about the tyrannosaurus rex. Max listens to the boy. He learns new things. He thinks the boy is smart and nice. Lily, Max, and the boy become friends. They talk about dinosaurs. They laugh and have fun. Mom watches them. She is happy. She thinks they are sweet and polite. She says, "It is time to go home." Lily, Max, and the boy say goodbye. They hug each other. They say, "See you soon." They go home. They tell Mom about their new friend. They say, "He has a red hat. He likes dinosaurs. He is smart and nice. We like him." Mom says, "I am glad you made a new friend. He sounds like a good boy. Maybe you can play with him again." Lily and Max nod. They say, "Yes, we want to play with him again. We like dinosaurs too. We want to learn more." Mom smiles. She says, "That is wonderful. You are curious and kind. You are good twins."
