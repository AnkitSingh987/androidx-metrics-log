Category: Beginner
Title: Emily and the Castle

Once upon a time there was a nice, honest girl named Emily. Emily had a dream to own a castle. She believed that she could make it come true. One day, Emily saved up enough coins. She went to a store and bought some blocks. Then, she carefully placed the blocks together. Soon, she had a big castle in her room. Emily was so excited. She loved her new castle. She believed in herself. She knew she could do anything she set her mind to. Every day, Emily would play in her castle. She would keep it clean and take care of it. Emily was so happy to have an honest castle of her own.

Once upon a time, there was a unique mule named Max. Max was not like other mules. He had a big, colorful spot on his back. All the other animals liked Max because he was so different. One day, Max met a little girl named Lily. Lily saw the colorful spot on Max's back and said, "Wow, Max! Your spot is so pretty! But it looks like you have some dirt on it. Let me remove it for you." Max said, "Thank you, Lily!" Lily gently removed the dirt from Max's colorful spot. Max was very happy. From that day, Max and Lily became best friends. They played together every day, and all the other animals loved to watch them have fun.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play a game. He asked his friend, Sam, "Do you want to play a game with me?" Sam said, "Yes, let's play!" They started to play a game of ball. But then, a big dog came. The dog took their ball! Tim and Sam did not know what to do. They were ignorant about how to get the ball back. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's kneel and call the dog. Maybe the dog will come to us." So, they kneeled and called the dog. "Here, dog! Come here!" The dog came to them! But, the dog did not have their ball. The dog had a new ball! It was a big surprise. Tim and Sam were happy. They played with the new ball and the dog. They had so much fun!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play at the park. He saw a big bell on the playground. He did not know what it was for. He asked his mom, "Mom, what is this bell for?" His mom said, "Tim, I will explain. When you ring the bell, it means you want to play a fast game with your friends. You can run, jump, and have fun!" Tim was excited. He called his friend, Lily. "Lily, let's play a fast game!" Tim rang the bell, and they started to run and jump together. They had so much fun playing their fast game. As they played, more friends came to join Tim and Lily. They all rang the bell and played the fast game together. The park was filled with laughter and fun. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Mom, I love the bell and the fast game. Can we come back to the park tomorrow?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. We can play here every day."

One day, a loud dog named Max came to a little boy's house. The boy saw Max and said, "Come here, dog!" Max wagged his tail and came to the boy. They played and had fun. The boy's mom came out and saw Max. She said, "What a loud dog!" Max barked and the boy laughed. They played more games in the yard. At the end of the day, the boy said, "Goodbye, loud dog!" Max barked and went back to his home. The boy was happy he had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his bell. He would run and jump, and the bell would make a happy sound. One day, Spot saw a big cat. The cat said, "Why do you have a bell? I don't like the sound." Spot thought for a moment and said, "I like my bell. It makes me happy." Spot wanted to show the cat how fun the bell was. He took the lead and started to run. The cat followed Spot, and they both played with the bell. Soon, the cat liked the sound too. They played together all day and became good friends.
