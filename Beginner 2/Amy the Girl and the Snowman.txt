Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Snowman

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a friend named Ben. They loved to play together every day. One day, Amy had a request for Ben. She said, "Ben, can you help me make a big snowman? My body is too small to lift the heavy snow." Ben was happy to help his friend. They started to make the snowman together. As they worked, a boy named Carl came by. He was ignorant and didn't know how to make a snowman. He asked, "What are you doing?" Amy said, "We are making a snowman. Do you want to help?" Carl was excited and said, "Yes, please!" So, Amy, Ben, and Carl made the snowman together. They had so much fun and learned to work as a team. The snowman was big and happy. The three friends played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to go to the grocery store with his mom. They would buy food and things they needed for their home. One day, Tom saw a rare toy at the grocery store. It was a shiny, red car. Tom wanted it very much, but his mom said they did not have enough money for it. Tom was sad, but he knew that his mom was right. Mom told Tom that it is important to value what you have. She said that even if they could not buy the rare toy, they still had each other and a happy home. Tom understood and hugged his mom. They left the grocery store with smiles on their faces, knowing that they had what was most important.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree with a big, red apple at the top. Two friends, Tom and Tim, wanted the apple. But they could not reach it. They were sad. Tom saw a dull ladder near the tree. He said, "Look, Tim! A ladder! We can use it to get the apple." Tim was happy. They put the ladder against the tree and Tom climbed up. He picked the big, red apple. When Tom came down, he said, "We can share the apple, Tim!" They cut the apple in half and each had a piece. They were happy and smiled. The moral of the story is: When we share, we can make everyone happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play with her friend Tim. They liked to play in Tim's big yard. Sue saw a pin on the ground and picked it up. She put it in her pocket to keep it safe. Sue and Tim found a big tree with sour green apples. They tried to eat them, but they were not good. Tim said, "We need to trust that the apples will be sweet when they are red." Sue nodded and they went to play a different game. Later, Sue and Tim wanted to hang a picture on the tree. They needed something to hold it up. Sue remembered the pin in her pocket. She took it out and said, "We can use this to hang the picture!" Tim smiled and they hung the picture up. They were happy they could trust each other and had a fun day.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. They saw a big dog with a red ball. The dog was friendly and wagged his tail. Ben and Lily wanted to play with the dog. "Hello, dog. What is your name?" Ben asked. The dog barked and dropped the ball. He wanted Ben and Lily to throw it for him. "His name is Max. He is my dog. Do you want to play with us?" A girl came over. She was holding a leash. "Yes, please. We like dogs. I am Ben and this is Lily." Ben said. "I am Mia. Max is a good dog. He can do tricks. Watch this." Mia said. She told Max to sit, roll over and shake. Max did what Mia said and got a treat. "Wow, he is so smart. Can we try?" Lily asked. "Sure. Here, you can hold the leash. Tell him to sit." Mia said. Lily held the leash and said "Sit, Max." Max sat and looked at Lily. "Good boy, Max. You are a good listener." Lily said. She gave him a treat. Ben wanted to try too. He said "Roll over, Max." Max rolled over and got another treat. They played with Max for a long time. They threw the ball, ran with him and hugged him. Max was happy and licked their faces. They were having fun, but they also got hungry. Their tummies rumbled. "I think we need some food. Do you want to come to my house? My mom made some sandwiches and juice." Mia said. "Yes, please. We are hungry. Thank you for sharing your dog with us. He is a good friend." Ben said. They walked to Mia's house. Max followed them. He was hungry too. They ate the sandwiches and juice. They talked and laughed. They became friends. They liked to support each other. They had a good spirit. They were not hungry anymore. They were happy.
