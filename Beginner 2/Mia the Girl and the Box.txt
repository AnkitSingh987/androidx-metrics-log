Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to go to the shop with her mom. They would buy food, toys, and pretty clothes. Mia's favorite part of the shop was the deep toy box. She loved to dig in the toy box and find new toys to play with. One day, Mia and her mom went to the shop. Mia ran to the toy box. She dug deep into the box to find a toy. But something unexpected happened. A little cat jumped out of the box! Mia was so surprised, but she loved the little cat. Mia asked her mom if they could take the cat home. Her mom said yes. They named the cat Lucky. Lucky was not just a cat. He was a magic cat! Lucky could make wishes come true. Mia and her mom loved Lucky, and they all lived happily ever after.

Jimmy was playing in the park when he saw something strange. It was a big black head, rolling across the grass. He wanted to see what it was, so he followed it. Soon he found himself in a cave beneath the park. Inside, he saw a giant black head with eyes that sparkled like moonbeams. Jimmy was curious and wanted to know why the head was there. To his surprise, an old man appeared and said that the head was mysterious and magical. The old man said the head had the power to answer any question that was asked. Jimmy asked the head what the secret of the universe was, and the head replied: "the answer lies in the light". Jimmy now knew the secret and was about to leave, when the old man said he would have to pay a price for the knowledge he had gained. The old man held out his hand and asked for Jimmy's head! Jimmy was scared, but then he remembered what the head had said. He looked for the light, and just then a beam of sunlight shone through the window. He was safe!

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play in Tom's room. Tom has a big fan in his room. The fan makes wind and noise. One day, Tom and Jen want to play with the fan. They think it is fun to put things in front of the fan and see them fly. They put a hat, a ball, a book and a doll in front of the fan. The fan blows them away. Tom and Jen laugh. But then, Tom has a stupid idea. He wants to set his hair on the fan. He thinks it will look funny. He goes near the fan and puts his head close to it. Jen says, "No, Tom, don't do that! It is bad!" But Tom does not listen. He sets his hair on the fan. The fan grabs his hair and pulls it hard. Tom screams. He feels pain. He tries to get away, but he can't. The fan is too strong. Jen runs to get Tom's mom. Tom's mom comes and turns off the fan. She helps Tom get his hair out of the fan. She hugs Tom and says, "Are you okay, Tom? That was a very stupid thing to do. You could have hurt yourself. Never do that again, okay?" Tom says, "I'm sorry, mom. I was stupid. I won't do that again. I'm sorry, Jen. I scared you." Jen says, "It's okay, Tom. I'm glad you are okay. But don't be stupid again. Let's play something else." Tom and Jen hug. They play something else. They don't play with the fan anymore. They learned their lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Ben. Ben and his mum were at the park playing chess. Ben loved playing with his mum. He watched intently as she moved the chess pieces around. Suddenly, Ben saw a bird flying in the sky. He was so excited he wanted to get closer. So, he ran and jumped, reaching up his hands to try and catch the bird. The bird flew away, leaving Ben to quickly look back at his mum. She had a big smile on her face. Ben's mum said, "Let's release the bird. She can fly away to have some fun. We can also go home and have some fun, too". Ben nodded and his mum scooped him up and gave him a big hug as they walked off together, away from the dry park.
