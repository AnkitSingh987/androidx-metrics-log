Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bunny and the Gift

One day, a little bunny named Tim was very excited. It was his birthday! He woke up and said, "Yay! It's my birthday!" He was so happy that he could spring very high. Tim had a friend named Sally, who was a weak little bird. Sally wanted to give Tim a birthday gift. She tried to pick up a big nut but it was too heavy for her. Tim saw Sally and asked, "What are you doing?" Sally said, "I want to give you a gift, but I am too weak." Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game instead!" Sally thought it was a great idea. They played and had lots of fun. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew away the nut. But it also blew a big, pretty balloon to them. It had "Happy Birthday" on it. Tim and Sally were so surprised! They laughed and played with the balloon. It was the best birthday ever!

One day, a cow named Beef was in the field. Beef saw a big red ball in the sky. Beef wanted to play with the red ball. So, Beef tried to raise her head high to reach the red ball. But, she could not reach it. Beef saw a bird named Clear flying near her. Beef asked Clear, "Can you help me get the red ball in the sky?" Clear said, "That is not a ball, Beef. That is the sun. It is hot and not for playing." Beef felt sad. But then, Clear had an idea. Clear found a small ball on the ground. Clear raised the ball high in the sky and dropped it. Beef saw the ball falling and caught it with her mouth. Beef and Clear played together all day. They learned that it is better to play with things that are safe and not to try to reach for things that can hurt them. They also learned that friends can help each other find new and fun ways to play.

Paul and Max were very jolly. They loved to wear their funny hats and go on adventures. Max put on his hat and said, "Let's go on a motion!". Paul put on his hat too and they ventured into the woods. The deeper they went, the darker it got. The wind started to howl, and Paul and Max started to worry. They wanted to turn back. They looked for a path but there was none. Then, Paul heard a scary noise. "What was that?" he asked. Max replied, "I don't know, let's just keep walking." But it was too late. An angry bear appeared in front of them. He roared, "Get out of my woods!" Paul and Max were very scared and tried to run, but the bear was too fast. He chased them out of the woods and back home. Paul and Max were very glad to be safe but sad that their jolly adventure was over.

Once upon a time, there was a little spoon. The spoon was very patient. It would wait on the table every day for someone to use it. One day, the sun came out and started to shine very bright. The spoon liked the sun because it made it shine too. The spoon had a friend named Bunny. Bunny hopped over to the spoon and said, "Wow, you are shining so bright today!" The spoon was happy and said, "Thank you, Bunny! I like the sun because it makes me shine." Bunny and the spoon played together all day. They were very happy in the sunshine. At the end of the day, the sun went to sleep and the spoon went back to the table. The spoon was still patient, waiting for someone to use it again. And the spoon knew that the sun would come back tomorrow to make it shine once more.

Once upon a time, there was a little mosquito named Mo. Mo was very foolish. He liked to chew on things he should not chew on. One day, Mo went to the park and saw a big dog named Max. "Hello, Max!" said Mo. Max looked at Mo and said, "Hello, Mo! What are you doing here?" Mo told Max that he wanted to chew on something. Max thought that was not a good idea, but Mo did not listen. Mo tried to chew on Max's tail, but Max said, "No, Mo! That is not good!" Mo felt sad and flew away. He saw a big tree and tried to chew on it, but it was too hard. Then, Mo saw a little girl eating a sandwich. Mo thought it was a good idea to chew on the sandwich, so he flew closer. "Hey, don't chew on my sandwich!" said the little girl. Mo was surprised and said, "I am sorry, I just wanted to chew on something." The little girl smiled and gave Mo a small piece of bread. Mo chewed on it and felt happy. From that day on, Mo learned not to chew on things he should not chew on.
