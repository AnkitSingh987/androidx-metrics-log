Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to wear a big, red bow in her hair. One day, while she was playing, she met a small, lost bird. "Help! Help!" cried the bird. "I can't find my way home!" Lily wanted to help the bird, so she said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you find your way home." As they walked through the wild forest, Lily used her big, red bow to mark the trees they passed. This way, they would not get lost. The little bird was very happy that Lily was helping him. Finally, they found the bird's home, and the bird's family was so grateful. They thanked Lily for her help and kindness. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel good and bring happiness to everyone.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with puzzles. One day, they find a big puzzle in the toy box. It has many pieces and colors. "Let's do this puzzle!" Tom says. "It looks fun!" "OK!" Lily says. "But where do we start?" They look at the puzzle box. It has a picture of a farm. There are animals, trees, and a house. "Maybe we can find the corners first," Tom says. "They have straight sides." "Good idea!" Lily says. "I see a corner with a cow. Do you see another one?" They look for the corners and put them on the floor. Then they try to find the edges and the middle pieces. They work hard and talk to each other. "Look, I found a piece with a pig!" Tom says. "Where does it go?" "Maybe here, next to the fence," Lily says. "Try it!" Tom tries to fit the piece, but it does not match. He turns it around and tries again, but it still does not match. "That's silly!" Lily says. "That's not a pig, that's a sheep!" Tom looks at the piece again. He sees that it has wool and a tail. "Oops!" Tom says. "You're right, that's a sheep! I was confused by the pink color." They laugh and find the right place for the sheep. They keep doing the puzzle until they finish it. They are happy and proud. "Wow, we did it!" Tom says. "The puzzle is beautiful!" "Yes, we did it!" Lily says. "We are good at puzzles!" They hug and smile. They put the puzzle back in the box and look for another one. They love to play with puzzles.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to help her mom cook. One day, Sue's mom was cooking a big meal for the family. Sue wanted to help, but her mom said the kitchen was too busy for her today. Sue felt sad, so she went to her room. She saw her toy lion, who was fierce. Sue decided to play with her lion and have a pretend cookout. She used a towel as a cape and pretended to be a super chef. Sue and her lion cooked a big meal for all their toy friends. When Sue's mom saw how much fun she was having, she asked Sue to help her cook in the kitchen again. Sue was happy and learned that it's okay to be fierce like her lion and try new things. She also learned that helping others and being patient can be fun too.

Once upon a time, there was a generous cat named Tom. Tom liked to help his friends. One day, he went to his friend's house. His friend was a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was sad because he was hungry. Tom wanted to help Jerry. So, they went to the kitchen. Tom saw a stove. He cooked food for Jerry on the stove. Jerry was very happy. He ate the food and felt better. After eating, Jerry wanted to say thank you to Tom. He started to applaud. Tom was happy, too. They both became good friends and played together every day. And they always shared their food with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship was on the icy sea. It was cold and white. The ship had a captain. The captain was a nice man. He had a big hat and a warm coat. One day, the ship got stuck in the ice. The captain said, "Oh no! We need to cut the ice to move the ship." The crew took out their tools. They started to cut the ice. The ice cracked and broke. As they cut the ice, they saw a little penguin. The penguin was lost. The captain said, "Hello, little penguin. Do you need help?" The penguin nodded. The captain and the crew helped the penguin find its family. They all became friends and sailed away on the big ship.
