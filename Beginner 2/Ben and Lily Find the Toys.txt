Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily Find the Toys

Ben and Lily were playing with their toy castle. They had many knights, horses and dragons. They liked to make up stories about the castle and the king. One day, they decided to have a big battle. Ben was the king of the castle and Lily was the queen of the dragons. They made loud noises and moved their toys around. "Roar! I am the queen of the dragons and I want your castle!" Lily said. "No! I am the king of the castle and I will not give it to you!" Ben said. They fought and fought until they knocked over the castle and some of the toys broke. They both felt sad and angry. "Look what you did! You broke my castle and my knights!" Ben said. "Well, you broke my dragons and my horses!" Lily said. They started to cry and shout at each other. Their mom heard them and came to see what was wrong. "Ben, Lily, what is going on here? Why are you crying and shouting?" mom asked. They told mom what happened and how they had a big battle. Mom looked at the mess and the broken toys. "Oh, Ben, Lily, I'm sorry that your toys are broken. But you know, fighting is not a good way to play. You should try to share and settle your problems with words, not with toys. Fighting can hurt you and your toys and your feelings. Do you understand?" mom said. Ben and Lily nodded. They felt sorry for fighting and breaking their toys. They also felt sorry for hurting each other's feelings. "Mom, we're sorry. We didn't mean to fight and break our toys. We just wanted to have fun. But we got too carried away. Can we fix our toys and play nicely?" Ben said. "Mom, we're sorry. We love our toys and we love each other. We don't want to fight anymore. Can we hug and say sorry?" Lily said. Mom smiled and hugged them. She helped them fix their toys and clean up the mess. "That's very good, Ben and Lily. I'm proud of you for saying sorry and making up. You are both very tough and brave, but you are also very kind and gentle. You can play with your castle and your dragons, but remember to respect each other and your toys. And if you have a problem, you can always talk to me or to each other. OK?" mom said. "OK, mom. Thank you, mom. We love you, mom." Ben and Lily said. They hugged mom and then hugged each other. They played with their castle and their dragons, but this time they made up stories where the king and the queen were friends and helped each other. They had a lot of fun and they were happy.

---

Jill and Ben were bored. It was a gloomy day and they could not go outside to play. They looked around the house for something fun to do. They saw the faucet in the kitchen. It was shiny and made funny noises when they turned it on and off. "Let's play with the faucet!" Jill said to Ben. "We can pretend it is a waterfall or a fountain or a hose!" Ben nodded. He liked the idea. They ran to the kitchen and turned on the faucet. They splashed the water with their hands and laughed. They made up stories about the water and the things they saw in it. But they did not notice that the water was spilling over the sink and onto the floor. It made a big puddle that grew bigger and bigger. They did not hear their mom coming down the stairs. She was looking for them to tell them a surprise. She saw the water on the floor and gasped. She ran to the kitchen and turned off the faucet. She was angry and scared. She told Jill and Ben to stop playing with the faucet and to clean up the mess. She told them they were in trouble and they had to go to their rooms. Jill and Ben felt sad and sorry. They did not mean to make a mess. They did not know the faucet could do that. They wanted to hear the surprise their mom had for them. They wondered what it was. They hoped it was something good. They hoped their mom would forgive them. They hoped the gloomy day would end soon.
