Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Duck

One day, a little dog named Spot was very restless. He ran around the house and played with his toys. Spot wanted to go outside and play, but it was raining. His owner, a kind girl named Sue, saw how restless Spot was. "Spot, let's play a game inside," Sue said. She took a big bowl and filled it with water. She put a toy duck in the water and told Spot, "When I say 'release,' you can try to catch the duck." Spot wagged his tail and waited for Sue to say the word. "Release!" Sue said. Spot jumped and tried to catch the duck in the bowl. They played the game many times, and Spot was not restless anymore. They had fun until the rain stopped, and then they went outside to play.

Once, there was a wealthy man who had a big house. He was not feeling well, so he took a pill to feel better. He had a friend named Tom. Tom was a funny guy who liked to play with toys. One day, Tom and the wealthy man played with toys together. They had a lot of fun, but they could not agree on which toy was the best. The wealthy man liked the big red car, but Tom liked the small blue plane. They both said, "No, my toy is the best!" Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the toys away. Tom and the wealthy man were sad, but they stopped disagreeing. They knew that they had to work together to find their toys. So, they looked high and low, and they found all the toys together. In the end, Tom and the wealthy man agreed that all the toys were fun to play with. They learned that it is better to share and be friends than to disagree. And from that day on, they played with all the toys and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside in the sun. He loved to run and jump and touch things with his nose. One day, Spot saw a big post near his house. He wanted to touch the post with his nose. So, he ran up to the post and touched it. It made him very happy. Spot's friend, a little girl named Sue, saw him touch the post. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. She ran over to the post and touched it too. They both had a fun day playing with the post and touching it together. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl named Mr. Hoot. He lived in a big tree in the forest. Mr. Hoot loved to help his friends. One day, his friend, Little Squirrel, came to him with a problem. Little Squirrel said, "Mr. Hoot, I want to make a pretty picture for my mom, but I don't know what to use." Mr. Hoot thought for a moment and said, "I recommend you use paper. It is easy to find and you can draw on it with your colors." Little Squirrel was happy and thanked Mr. Hoot. He found some paper and made a beautiful picture for his mom. His mom loved it and gave him a big hug. The moral of the story is to always ask for help when you don't know what to do. Wise friends like Mr. Hoot can give good advice and help you make the best choices.

Lily loved her pony, Max. He was brown and soft and fast. She liked to brush his hair and feed him carrots. They were best friends. One day, Lily and Max went for a ride in the field. They saw some flowers and butterflies. They were happy. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was a big truck. It was coming towards them. Lily was scared. She tried to turn Max around, but he was too slow. "Help! Help!" Lily shouted. "The truck is coming!" She saw a man on a horse. He was wearing a hat and a badge. He was a sheriff. He heard Lily's cry. He rode his horse to the truck. He waved his hand and blew his whistle. He made the truck stop. "Are you okay, little girl?" the sheriff asked Lily. "Yes, thank you, mister," Lily said. "You saved us. You are mighty." The sheriff smiled. He patted Max's head. He gave Lily a hug. "You're welcome, little girl," he said. "You and your pony are brave. I'm glad you're safe." Lily smiled back. She hugged Max. She thanked the sheriff again. "Can we be friends?" she asked. "Of course we can," the sheriff said. "Friends are the best." They all laughed. They rode together to Lily's home. They had a picnic and played games. They had a lot of fun. Lily and Max were happy. They had a new friend. He was a mighty sheriff. He saved them.
