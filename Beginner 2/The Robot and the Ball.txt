Category: Beginner
Title: The Robot and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little robot. The robot liked to play all day. One day, the robot was playing in the park. It was a sunny day and the robot was very happy. While playing, the robot saw a big ball. The robot wanted to strike the ball with its hand. But when the robot hit the ball, it felt dizzy. The robot fell down and did not know why it felt dizzy. Then, a little girl came to the robot. She said, "I put a magic spell on the ball. When you hit it, you feel dizzy. I am sorry, I did not mean for you to feel bad." The little girl helped the robot up and took the spell off the ball. The robot felt better and they played together. From that day on, the robot and the little girl were best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He had a brown ball to play with. Tim felt happy because he loved the park. He played with the ball and ran around. His mom sat on a bench and watched him have fun. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to see what was behind it. Behind the tree, he found a jar of jam. The jam was red and sweet. Tim was surprised and felt excited. He ran to his mom and showed her the jam. His mom smiled and said, "That is not jam, Tim. It is paint!" Tim looked at the jar and saw that it was true. The red paint looked like jam, but it was not for eating. Tim felt silly, but he was still happy. He and his mom laughed and went home to paint a beautiful picture with the red paint.

Sam was playing with his toy automobile in the park. He loved playing with the car, making it go from one end to the other. It was the most exciting game! "Hey Sam!" his friend Jack called out. "Let's play a game!" Sam was excited. He picked up his car and went to Jack. "Me and you, we put our cars in the middle of the park," Jack said. "Then we take turns: I take one step back, then you take one step back, until one car reaches the end of the park. Whichever car reaches the end first is the winner!" Sam grinned with delight. "That's fair!" he said. They put their cars at the start and took turns stepping back. Jack was winning and Sam was starting to get worried. But then, at the very last move, Sam's car zoomed forward and passed Jack's. Sam let out a loud cheer and tears of joy filled his eyes. He had won! Jack congratulate him with a smile. "You were fair and fast," he said. Sam wiped away his tears and gave his friend a big hug. He was so happy and proud of himself that he ran around the park with his car from joy.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot who lived in a modern house. The parrot loved to talk and play all day. He had many friends who came to visit him. The parrot was always happy and full of fun. One night, the parrot had a dream. In the dream, he could fly high in the sky with his friends. They all played games and laughed together. The parrot felt very happy in his dream. He wished he could stay in the dream forever. But when the parrot woke up, he found out he was still in his cage. He could not fly high in the sky like in his dream. The parrot felt very sad and cried. His friends came to visit, but he did not want to play. The parrot stayed sad and missed his dream.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom with her work. One day, his mom was making a big cake. Tim wanted to help too. "Can I help, Mom?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can wipe the table for me." Tim was happy to help. He took a cloth and wiped the table clean. After they finished the cake, Tim's mom said, "Good work, Tim! You were a big help today." Tim smiled and felt proud. From that day on, Tim always helped his mom with her work. They had fun working together and Tim learned many new things.
