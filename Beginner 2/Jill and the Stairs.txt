Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and the Stairs

Jill was a friendly girl, who loved to play outside in the garden. One day she ran to the stairs and started to climb them. She slipped and quickly fell down the stair. She was very scared and started to cry. Then her mum came and hugged her tight. “Don’t worry,” she said in a very friendly voice. “It’s ok, no one got hurt.” Jill stopped crying and smiled, happy to be safe once more. Mum then pointed to the stairs. “You need to be careful next time you climb the stairs,” she said. “Let’s try it again, shall we?” Jill nodded and slowly climbed the stairs, taking care not to fall. When she reached the top, she beamed with pride. “I did it!” she exclaimed. Mum smiled and said “Well done, Jill! You’re so brave and strong.” Jill smiled and hugged her mum, thankful for being so friendly.

Tom and Sam were friends. They liked to play with toys and sing songs. One day, they found a black microphone in the park. It was big and shiny. They wanted to use it to sing. "Let me have it first!" Tom said. He grabbed the microphone and ran away. "No, it's mine!" Sam said. He chased Tom and tried to get the microphone back. They ran and ran until they came to a big road. There were many cars and buses. They did not see them. They only saw the microphone. "Give it to me!" Sam shouted. He pulled the microphone from Tom's hand. "Ow, you hurt me!" Tom cried. He pushed Sam hard. Sam fell on the road. A big bus was coming fast. It could not stop. It hit Sam with a loud noise. Sam did not move. He was very still. Tom was scared. He dropped the microphone and ran to Sam. He tried to wake him up. He could not. He saw blood on the road. He screamed and cried. People came and saw what happened. They called for help. They took Sam away. They said he was gone. Tom was alone. He was very sad. He wished he did not fight with Sam. He wished he did not spread the microphone. He wished he did not find it. He wished he could sing with Sam again.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful alligator. She lived in a big, blue lake. She liked to swim and play with her friends, the fish and the ducks. One day, a little boy came to the lake. He saw the beautiful alligator and stopped to admire her. The alligator smiled at the boy, and they became friends. They played together every day. The boy would throw sticks, and the alligator would fetch them. The beautiful alligator was very happy, and so was the boy. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, different toy car. It was red and had a crack on it. Tim loved the car so much. He played with it all day. He made the car go fast and slow. Sometimes, he made the car go reverse. Tim showed his car to his friend, Sue. "Look at my car!" said Tim. "It has a crack, but I like it." Sue looked at the car and smiled. "It is different," she said. "Can I try?" Tim said yes, and they played together. Sue made the car go reverse too. They laughed and had fun. The car with the crack was special to them. They played with the car every day. Tim and Sue were very happy with their different car.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They had a big ball that they kicked to each other. They were very happy and playful. But then they saw a big dog. The dog was not friendly. It barked and growled and showed its teeth. It ran towards them very fast. Anna and Ben were scared. They dropped the ball and ran to the house. They reached the door and tried to open it. But the door was locked. They could not find the knob. It was too high for them. They heard the dog coming closer. They trembled and cried. "Help! Help!" they shouted. "Mom! Dad!" They hoped that someone would hear them and come to save them. They hugged each other and prayed. Then they heard a voice. It was Mom. She had come back from the shop. She saw the dog and the children. She was angry and brave. She yelled at the dog and threw a stick at it. The dog was surprised and scared. It ran away. Mom opened the door and let the children in. She hugged them and kissed them. She said they were safe and she loved them. She said she was sorry for leaving the door locked. She said she would call the dog's owner and tell him to keep his dog away. Anna and Ben were relieved and grateful. They thanked Mom and said they loved her too. They said they did not want to play with the ball anymore. They wanted to stay inside and read a book. Mom smiled and agreed. She said they were very brave and smart. She said she was proud of them. They all went to the living room and cuddled on the sofa. They felt happy and peaceful.
