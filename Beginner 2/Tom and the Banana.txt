Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Banana

Tom was hungry. He saw a banana on the table. He wanted to eat it. He ran to the table and grabbed the banana. But Lily was there too. She also wanted the banana. She said, "No, Tom, that's my banana. Give it to me." She tried to take the banana from Tom. Tom did not want to share. He said, "No, Lily, this is my banana. Go away." He held the banana tight. Lily was angry. She said, "Tom, you are mean. You are a bad brother." She slapped Tom on the face. Tom was surprised. He felt pain. He dropped the banana. He cried. Lily picked up the banana. She said, "Ha, ha, now it's mine." She peeled the banana. She saw that it was orange inside. She said, "Yuck, this banana is bad. It is not yellow. It is orange." She threw the banana away. Tom stopped crying. He saw the orange banana. He said, "Lily, you are silly. That is not a banana. That is a plantain. You can't eat it raw. You have to cook it." Lily felt silly. She said, "Oh, I didn't know. I'm sorry, Tom. I'm sorry I slapped you. I'm sorry I took your banana." Tom said, "It's okay, Lily. I'm sorry I didn't share. I'm sorry I was mean. Do you want to hug?" Lily said, "Yes, Tom, I want to hug." They hugged. Mom came in. She saw them hug. She said, "What a nice hug. What are you doing?" Tom said, "We are hugging. We are sorry. We fought over a plantain." Mom said, "A plantain? That's funny. Do you want to try a real banana? I have some in the kitchen. They are yellow and sweet." Tom and Lily said, "Yes, Mom, we want to try a real banana. Thank you, Mom." They went to the kitchen. They ate a real banana. They liked it. They were happy. They did not fight anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toys all day. One day, she had an idea. She wanted to make a dress for her doll. But first, she needed to measure her doll to know how big the dress should be. Amy took a string and wrapped it around her doll. She felt a little uncomfortable because she didn't know if she was doing it right. But she tried her best. After she measured her doll, she cut a piece of cloth to make the dress. She put the dress on her doll and it fit just right! Amy was so happy. She played with her doll all day, showing her new dress to everyone in her family. They all said it looked very nice. And Amy felt very proud of what she had made.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in the garden. One day, they find a mint plant. It smells good and fresh. "Can we eat it?" Lily asks. "I don't know. Maybe we should ask Mom," Tom says. But Lily is curious. She picks a thin leaf and puts it in her mouth. She makes a funny face. "Yuck! It is too strong. It makes my tongue cold and tingly," she says. Tom laughs. He picks a leaf too. He wants to try it. He manages to bite it, but he does not like it either. "Blah! It is not sweet. It is bitter and spicy," he says. They spit out the leaves. They run to the kitchen. They want some water and some cookies. Mom sees them. She smiles. "What did you eat, my darlings?" she asks. "We ate mint from the garden," Tom says. "Mint is good for cooking and tea, but not for eating raw. It is too strong for you," Mom says. She gives them some water and some cookies. She hugs them. "Next time, ask me before you eat something from the garden. There are many plants that are not good for you," she says. Tom and Lily nod. They learned their lesson. They do not eat mint again. They only smell it.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom was always hungry. One day, Tom found a bean in the yard. The bean was green and shiny. Tom thought the bean would make him full. Tom took the bean home and put it in a pot. He waited for the bean to cook. When it was ready, he ate the bean. But Tom was still hungry. He was sad. He wanted more beans. Tom went outside and lay down in the sun. He saw a thin bean plant growing. It had many beans on it. Tom was so happy. Now, he could eat all the beans and not be hungry anymore. And Tom lived happily ever after.
