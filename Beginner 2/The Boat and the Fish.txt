Category: Beginner
Title: The Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a big anchor. The boat liked to sail on the water. One day, the boat saw a hurt fish. The fish was sad. The boat wanted to help the hurt fish. It used its big anchor to try to save the fish. The fish was happy to see the boat's help. The boat and the fish became good friends. But one day, the boat and the fish had to end their fun. A big storm came. The storm was very bad. The boat tried to use its big anchor to stay safe. But the storm was too strong. The boat and the fish could not stay together. They were very sad. The boat and the fish had a bad ending.

---

One day, a happy mule named Max was walking in the forest. Max was a big, strong mule who liked to help others. As he walked, he saw a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was lost and started to tremble. She was very scared. "Hi Lucy," said Max the mule. "Why are you scared?" Lucy looked at Max and said, "I can't find my way home. Can you help me?" Max smiled and said, "Of course! Let's find your home together." As they walked, they found a big, shiny rock. Max and Lucy were very surprised. The rock could talk! The rock said, "I can help you find your home, but first you must help me. I am a magic rock and I am lost too." Max and Lucy looked at each other, and then they looked at the rock. They decided to help the rock find its home. After they helped the rock, the magic rock showed them the way to Lucy's home. Max, Lucy, and the rock were all very happy. They had a fun day and made a new friend.

---

One day, a fisherman went to the sea. He wanted to catch fish. He had a big net to catch many fish. The sun was bright, and the sea was calm. The fisherman was happy. As the fisherman was catching fish, he saw a big fish in his net. The big fish said, "Please let me go. I am sorry for going into your net." The fisherman felt sorry for the big fish. He let the big fish go back into the sea. The fisherman went home with his fish. When he got home, he saw that all his fish were gone. The big fish had eaten them all. The fisherman was sad and sorry that he let the big fish go. The fisherman had no fish to eat or sell.

One day, a little boy named Tim was looking at the sky. He saw a big, shiny star. He wanted to go there. So, he made a plan. Tim went to his friend, Sam. He said, "I want to go to the star. Will you help me?" Sam felt anxious but said, "Yes, let's make a big ladder to reach the star." They worked hard to make a ladder. As they were almost done, a bird flew by and said, "You can't reach the star with a ladder. Stars are very far away." Tim and Sam were sad. But the bird said, "Don't be sad. You can still look at the stars and dream big." Tim and Sam learned that it's good to dream big, but some things are too far away.

One day, a graceful bird named Lily went to the sea. She saw a big ship in the water. Lily wanted to see what was inside the ship, so she flew up high and then down low to take a peek. As Lily peeked into the ship, she saw a little girl named Emma. Emma looked up and saw Lily. She said, "Hi, bird! Do you want to play with me?" Lily nodded her head and flew down to play with Emma. They played together on the ship all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. The sun began to set, and it was time for Lily to go home. Emma waved goodbye to her new friend. Lily flew away, happy to have made a new friend on the big ship.
